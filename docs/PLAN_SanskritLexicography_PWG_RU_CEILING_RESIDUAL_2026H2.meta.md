## Known limitations / caveats

- The residual inventory derives from the four roadmaps' **own** text, their
  metadocs, and `roadmap_handoff_truth.py`. `pwg_ru` work registered on none of
  them is outside its field of view — and there is a great deal of `pwg_ru` work.
- The RESEARCH_CAPABILITY metadoc states its own author read roughly the first
  third of that 30-card document. The gold-set blocker (R5) is drawn from that
  sample. **Other cards may carry other unsurfaced blockers**; R5 removes the one
  blocker known to be shared, not necessarily every blocker.
- N1–N4 are recorded as human acts. That is a statement about authority, not
  difficulty — and none of them blocks a minted unit.
- Wave C is deliberately unminted. Do not read its absence as a claim that C1 or
  C2 phase 2 are cancelled; they are coverage-gated by ruling.

## Intended use / known misuse

- **For:** picking the next `pwg_ru` research unit with its gate, its fence, and
  its claim discipline already stated.
- **Misuse:** treating the "Not mintable" table as a blocker list — it is the
  opposite, a list of things that are *not* blocking. And treating a `PARTIAL` or
  `STAYS PROXY` ceiling verdict as eventually resolvable: C3, C5 and C6 are
  permanent by ruling.
