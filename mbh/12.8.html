Mahābhārata 12.8 (Śāntiparvan)

1vaiśampāyana uvāca|
athārjuna uvācedamadhikṣipta ivākṣamī
|
abhinītataraṃ vākyaṃ dṛḍhavādaparākramaḥ ||1||

2darśayannaindrirātmānamugramugraparākramaḥ
|
smayamāno mahātejāḥ sṛkkiṇī parisaṃlihan ||2||

3arjuna uvāca |
aho duḥkhamaho kṛcchramaho vaiklavyamuttamam
|
yatkṛtvā'mānuṣaṃ karma tyajethāḥ śriyamuttamām ||3||

4śatrūnhatvā mahīṃ labdhvā svadharmeṇopapāditām
|
evaṃvidhaṃ kathaṃ sarvaṃ tyajethā buddhilāghavāt ||4||

5klībasya hi kuto rājyaṃ dīrghasūtrasya vā punaḥ
|
kimarthaṃ ca mahīpālānavadhīḥ krodhamūrchitaḥ ||5||

6yo hyājijīviṣedbhaikṣyaṃ karmaṇā naiva kasyacit
|
samārambhānbubhūṣeta hatasvastirakiñcanaḥ
|
sarvalokeṣu vikhyāto na putrapaśusaṃhitaḥ ||6||

7kāpālīṃ nṛpa pāpiṣṭhāṃ vṛttimāsādya jīvataḥ
|
saṃtyajya rājyamṛddhaṃ te loko'yaṃ kiṃ vadiṣyati ||7||

8sarvārambhān samutsṛjya hatasvastirakiñcanaḥ
|
kasmādāśaṃsase bhaikṣyaṃ kartuṃ prākṛtavatprabho ||8||

9asmin rājakule jāto jitvā kṛtsnāṃ vasundharām
|
dharmārthāvakhilau hitvā vanaṃ mauḍhyātpratiṣṭhase ||9||

10yadīmāni havīṃṣīhaṃ vimathiṣyantyasādhavaḥ
|
bhavatā viprahīṇāni prāptaṃ tvāmeva kilbiṣam ||10||

11ākiñcanyaṃ munīnāṃ ca iti vai nahuṣo'bravīt
|
kṛtvā nṛśaṃsaṃ hyadhane dhigastvadhanatāmiha ||11||

12aśvastanamṛṣīṇāṃ hi vidyate veda tadbhavān
|
yaṃ tvimaṃ dharmamityāhurdhanādeṣa pravartate ||12||

13dharma saṃharate tasya dhanaṃ harati yasya saḥ
|
hrimāṇe dhane rājan vayaṃ kasya kṣamemahi ||13||

14abhiśastaṃ prapaśyanti daridraṃ pārśvataḥ sthitam
|
daridraṃ pātakaṃ loke na tacchaṃsitumarhati ||14||

15patitaḥ śocyate rājannirdhanaścāpi śocyate
|
viśeṣaṃ nādhigacchāmi patitasyādhanasya ca ||15||

16arthebhyo hi vivṛddhebhyaḥ sambhṛtebhyastatastataḥ
|
kriyāḥ sarvāḥ pravartante parvatebhya ivāpagāḥ ||16||

17arthāddharmaśca kāmaśca svargaścaiva narādhipa
|
prāṇayātrāpi lokasya vinā hyarthaṃ na siddhyati ||17||

18arthena hi vihīnasya puruṣasyālpamedhasaḥ
|
vicchidyante kriyāḥ sarvā grīṣme kusarito yathā ||18||

19yasyārthāstasya mitrāṇi yasyārthāstasya bāndhavāḥ
|
yasyārthāḥ sa pumān loke yasyārthāḥ sa ca paṇḍitaḥ ||19||

20adhanenārthakāmena nārthaḥ śakyo vidhitsitum
|
arthairarthāni badhyante gajairiva mahāgajāḥ ||20||

21dharmaḥ kāmaśca svargaśca harṣaḥ krodhaḥ śrutaṃ damaḥ
|
arthādetāni sarvāṇi pravartante narādhipa ||21||

22dhanātkulaṃ prabhavati dhanāddharmaḥ pravardhate
|
nādhanasyāstyayaṃ loko na paraḥ puruṣottama ||22||

23nādhano dharmakṛtyāni yathāvada‌nutiṣṭhati
|
dhanāddhi dharmaḥ sravati śailādabhi nadī yathā ||23||

24yaḥ kuśārthaḥ kṛśagavaḥ kṛśabhṛtyaḥ kṛśātithiḥ
|
sa vai rājan kṛśo nāma na śarīrakṛśaḥ kṛśaḥ ||24||

25avekṣasva yathānyāyaṃ paśya devāsuraṃ yathā
|
rājan kimanyajjātīnāṃ vadhādgṛddhyanti devatāḥ ||25||

26na ceddhartavyamanyasya kathaṃ taddharmamārabhet
|
etāvāneva vedeṣu niścayaḥ kavibhiḥ kṛtaḥ ||26||

27adhyetavyā trayī nityaṃ bhavitavyaṃ vipaścitā
|
sarvathā dhanamāhāryaṃ yaṣṭavyaṃ cāpi yanataḥ ||27||

28drohāddevairavāptāni divi sthānāni sarvaśaḥ
|
drohātkimanyajjñātīnāṃ gṛddhyante yena devatāḥ ||28||

29iti devā vyavasitā vedavādāśca śāśvatāḥ
|
adhīyante'dhyāpayante yajante yājayanti ca ||29||

30kṛtsnaṃ tadeva tacchreyo yadapyādadate'nyataḥ
|
na paśyāmo'napakṛtaṃ dhanaṃ kiñcitkvacidvayam ||30||

31evameva hi rājāno yajanti pṛthivīmimām
|
jitvā mameyaṃ bruvate putrā iva piturdhanam ||31||

32rājarṣayo'pi te svargyo dharmo hyeṣāṃ nirucyate
|
yathaiva pūrṇādudadheḥ syandantyāpo diśo daśa ||32||

33evaṃ rājakulādvittaṃ pṛthivīṃ prati tiṣṭhati
|
āsīdiyaṃ dilīpasya nṛgasya nahuṣasya ca ||33||

34ambarīṣasya māndhātuḥ pṛthivī sā tvayi sthitā
|
sa tvāṃ dravyamayo yajñaḥ samprāptaḥ sarvadakṣiṇaḥ ||34||

35taṃ cenna yajase rājanprāptastvaṃ rājyakilbiṣam
|
yeṣāṃ rājā'śvamedhena yajate dakṣiṇāvatā ||35||

36upetya tasyāvabhṛthe pūtāḥ sarve bhavanti te
|
viśvarūpo mahādevaḥ sarvamedhe mahāmakhe
|
juhāva sarvabhūtāni tathaivātmānamātmanā ||36||

37śāśvato'yaṃ bhūtipatho nāstyantamanuśuśruma
|
mahān dāśarayaḥ panthā mā rājankupathaṃ gamaḥ ||37||
