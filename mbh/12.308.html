Mahābhārata 12.308 (Śāntiparvan)

1vasiṣṭha uvāca |
atha buddhamathābuddhamimaṃ guṇavidhiṃ śṛṇu
|
ātmānaṃ bahudhā kṛtvā tānyeva pravicakṣate||1||

2etadevaṃ vikurvāṇo budhyamāno na budhyate
|
guṇāndhārayate hyeṣa sṛjatyākṣipate tadā||2||

3ajasraṃ tviha krīḍārthaṃ vikaroti janādhipa
|
avyaktabodhanāccaiva budhyamānaṃ vadantyapi||3||

4na tveva budhyate vyaktaṃ saguṇaṃ tāta nirguṇam
|
kadācittveva khalvetadāhurapratibuddhyakam||4||

5budhyate yadi vā vyaktametadvai pañcaviṃśakam
|
budhyamāno bhavatyeva saṅgātmaka iti śrutiḥ
|
anenāpratibuddheti vadantyavyaktamacyutam||5||

6avyaktabodhanāccāpi budhyamānaṃ vadantyuta
|
pañcaviṃśaṃ mahātmānaṃ na cāsāvapi budhyate||6||

7ṣaḍviśaṃ vimalaṃ buddhamaprameyaṃ sanātanam
|
satataṃ pañcaviṃśaṃ ca caturviṃśaṃ ca budhyate||7||

8dṛśyādṛśye hyanugataṃ svabhāvena mahādyute
|
avyaktamatra tadbrahma budhyate tāta kevalam||8||

9kevalaṃ pañcaviṃśaṃ ca caturviṃśaṃ na paśyati
|
budhyamāno yadā''tmānamanyo'hamiti manyate||9||

10tadā prakṛtimāneṣa bhavatyavyaktalocanaḥ
|
budhyate ca parāṃ buddhiṃ vimalāmamalāṃ yadā||10||

11ṣaḍviṃśo rājaśārdūla tathā buddhatvamāvrajet
|
tatastyajati so'vyaktaṃ sargapralayadharmi vai||11||

12nirguṇaḥ prakṛtiṃ veda guṇayuktāmacetanām
|
tataḥ kevaladharmāsau bhavatyavyaktadarśanāt||12||

13kevalena samāgamya vimukto''tmānamāpnuyāt
|
etattu tattvamityāhurnistattvamajarāmaram||13||

14tattvasaṃśrayaṇādetattattvavanna ca mānada
|
pañcaviṃśati tattvāni pravadanti manīṣiṇaḥ||14||

15na caiṣa tattvavāṃstāta nistattvastveṣa buddhimān
|
eṣa muñcati tattvaṃ hi kṣipraṃ buddhatvalakṣaṇam||15||

16ṣaḍviṃśo'hamiti prājño gṛhyamāṇo'jarāmaraḥ
|
kevalena balenaiva samatāṃ yātyasaṃśayam||16||

17ṣaḍviṃśena prabuddhena budhyamāno'pyabuddhimān
|
etannānātvamityuktaṃ sāṃkhyaśrutinidarśanāt||17||

18cetanena sametasya pañcaviṃśatikasya ha
|
ekatvaṃ vai bhavatyasya yadā buddhyā na budhyate||18||

19budhyamāno'prabuddhena samatāṃ yāti maithila
|
saṅgadharmā bhavatyeṣa niḥsaṅgātmā narādhipa||19||

20niḥsaṅgātmānamāsādya ṣaḍviṃśakamajaṃ vibhum
|
vibhustyajati cāvyaktaṃ yadā tvetadvibuddhyate||20||

21caturviṃśamasāraṃ ca ṣaḍviṃśasya prabodhanāt
|
eṣa hyapratibuddhaśca budhyamānaśca te'nagha||21||

22prokto buddhaśca tattvena yathāśruti nidarśanāt
|
nānātvaikatvametāvaddraṣṭavyaṃ śāstradarśanāt||22||

23maśakodumbare yadvadanyatvaṃ tadvadetayoḥ
|
matsyodake yathā tadvadanyatvamupalabhyate||23||

24evamevāvagantavyaṃ nānātvaikatvametayoḥ
|
etaddhi mokṣa ityuktamavyaktajñānasaṃhitam||24||

25pañcaviṃśatikasyāsya yo'yaṃ deheṣu vartate
|
eṣa mokṣayitavyeti prāhuravyaktagocarāt||25||

26so'yamevaṃ vimucyeta nānyatheti viniścayaḥ
|
paraśca paradharmā ca bhavatyeṣa sametya vai||26||

27viśuddhadharmā śuddhena buddhena ca sa buddhimān
|
vimuktadharmā muktena sametya puruṣarṣabha||27||

28viyogadharmiṇā caiva vimuktātmā bhavatyathā
|
vimokṣiṇā vimokṣaśca sametyeha tathā bhavet||28||

29śucikarmā śuciścaiva bhavatyamitadīptimān
|
vimalātmā ca bhavati sametya vimalātmanā||29||

30kevalātmā tathā caiva kevalena sametya vai
|
svatantraśca svatantreṇa svatantratvamavāpnute||30||

31etāvadetatkathitaṃ mayā te tathyaṃ mahārāja yathārthatattvam
|
amatsaratvaṃ parigṛhya cārthaṃ sanātanaṃ brahma viśuddhamādyam||31||

32na vedaniṣṭhasya janasya rājan pradeyametatparamaṃ tvayā bhavet
|
vidhitsamānāya vibodhakāraṇaṃ prabodhahetoḥ praṇatasya śāsanam||32||

33na deyametacca tathā'nṛtātmane śaṭhāya klībāya na jihmabuddhaye
|
na paṇḍitajñānaparopatāpine deyaṃ tu deyaṃ ca nibodha yādṛśe||33||

34śraddhānvitāyātha guṇānvitāya parāpavādādviratāya nityam
|
viśuddhayogāya budhāya nityaṃ kriyāvate ca kṣamiṇe hitāya||34||

35viviktaśīlāya vidhipriyāya vivādahīnāya bahuśrutāya
|
vijānate caiva na cāhitakṣame dame ca śaktāya śame ca deyam||35||

36etairguṇairhīnatame na deyametatparaṃ brahma viśuddhamāhuḥ
|
na śreyasā yokṣyati tādṛśe kṛtaṃ dharmapravaktāramapātradānāt||36||

37pṛthvīmimāṃ yadyapi ratnapūrṇāṃ dadyānna deyaṃ tvidamavratāya
|
jitendriyāyaitadasaṃśayaṃ te bhavetpradeyaṃ paramaṃ narendra||37||

38karāla mā te bhayamastu kiñcidetacchrutaṃ brahma paraṃ tvayā'dya
|
yathāvaduktaṃ paramaṃ pavitraṃ viśokamatyantamanādimadhyam||38||

39agādhajanmāmaraṇaṃ ca rājan nirāmayaṃ vītabhayaṃ śivaṃ ca
|
samīkṣya mohaṃ tyaja vā'dya sarvajñānasya tattvārthamidaṃ viditvā||39||

40vāptametaddhi mayā sanātanāddhiraṇyagarbhādgadato narādhipa
|
prasādya yatnena tamugracetasaṃ sanātanaṃ brahma yathā'dya vai tvayā||40||

41pṛṣṭastvayā cāsmi yathā narendra yathā mayedaṃ tvayi coktamadya
|
tathāvāptaṃ brahmaṇo me narendra mahājñānaṃ mokṣavidāṃ parāyaṇam||41||

42bhīṣma uvāca|
etaduktaṃ paraṃ brahma yasmānnāvartate punaḥ
|
pañcaviṃśo mahārāja paramarṣinidarśanāt||42||

43punarāvṛttimāpnoti paraṃ jñānamavāpya ca
|
nāvabudhyati tattvena budhyamāno'jarāmaram||43||

44etanniḥśreyasakaraṃ jñānaṃ te paramaṃ mayā
|
kathitaṃ tattvatastāta śrutvā devarṣito nṛpa||44||

45hiraṇyagarbhādṛṣiṇā vasiṣṭhena mahātmanā
|
vasiṣṭhādṛṣiśārdūlānnārado'vāptavānidam||45||

46nāradādviditaṃ mahyametadbrahma sanātanam
|
mā śucaḥ kauravendra tvaṃ śrutvaitatparamaṃ padam||46||

47yena kṣarākṣare vitte bhayaṃ tasya na vidyate
|
vidyate tu bhayaṃ tasya yo naitadvetti pārthiva||47||

48avijñānācca mūḍhātmā punaḥ punarupādravat
|
pretya jātisahasrāṇi maraṇāntānyupāśnute||48||

49devalokaṃ tathā tiryaḍmanuṣyamapi cāśnute
|
yadi śudhyati kālena tasmādajñānasāgarāt||49||

50ajñānasāgaro ghoro hyavyakto'gādha ucyate
|
ahanyahani majjanti yatra bhūtāni bhārata||50||

51yasmādagādhādavyaktāduttīrṇastvaṃ sanātanāt
|
tasmāt tvaṃ virajāścaiva vitamaskaśca pārthiva||51||
