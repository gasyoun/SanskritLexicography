Mahābhārata 2.70 (Sabhāparvan)

1vaiśampāyana uvāca|
tathā tu dṛṣṭvā bahu tatra devīṃ rorūyamāṇāṃ kurarīmivārtām
|
nocurvacaḥ sādhvathavā'pyasādhu mahīkṣito dhārtarāṣṭrasya bhītāḥ||1||

2dṛṣṭvā tathā pārthivaputrapautrāṃstūṣṇīṃbhūtāndhṛtarāṣṭrasya putraḥ
|
smayannivedaṃ vacanaṃ babhāṣe pāñcālarājasya sutāṃ tadānīm||2||

3duryodhana uvāca|
tiṣṭhatvayaṃ praśna udārasatve bhīme'rjune sahadeve tathaiva
|
patyau ca te nakule yājñaseni vadantvete vacanaṃ tvatprasūtam||3||

4anīśvaraṃ vibruvantvāryamadhye yudhiṣṭhiraṃ tava pāñcāli hetoḥ
|
kurvantu sarve cānṛtaṃ dharmarājaṃ pāñcāli tvaṃ mokṣyase dāsabhāvāt||4||

5dharme sthito dharmasuto mahātmā svayaṃ cedaṃ kathayatvindrakalpaḥ
|
īśo vā te hyanīśo'thavaiṣa vākyādasya kṣipramekaṃ bhajasva||5||

6sarve hīme kauraveyāḥ sabhāyāṃ duḥkhāntare vartamānāstavaiva
|
na vibruvantyāryasatvā yathāvatpatīṃśca te samavekṣyālpabhāgyān||6||

7vaiśampāyana uvāca|
tataḥ sabhyāḥ kururājasya tasya vākyaṃ sarve praśaśaṃsustathoccaiḥ
|
celāvedhāṃścāpi cakrurnadanto hāhetyāsīdapi caivārtanādaḥ||7||

8śrutvā tuṃ tadvākyamanoharaṃ taddharṣaścāsītkauravāṇāṃ sabhāyām
|
sarve cāsanpārthivāḥ prītimantaḥ kuruśreṣṭhaṃ dhārmikaṃ pūjayantaḥ||8||

9yudhiṣṭhiraṃ ca te sarve samudaikṣanta pārthivāḥ
|
kiṃ nu vakṣyati dharmajña iti sācīkṛtānanāḥ||9||

10kiṃ nu vakṣyati bībhatsurajito yudhi pāṇḍavaḥ
|
bhīmaseno yamau cobhau bhṛśaṃ kautūhalānvitāḥ||10||

11tasminnuparate śabde bhīmaseno'bravīdidam
|
pragṛhya ruciraṃ divyaṃ bhujaṃ candanacarcitam||11||

12bhīmasena uvāca|
yadyeṣa gururasmākaṃ dharmarājo mahāmanāḥ
|
na prabhuḥ syātkulasyāsya na vayaṃ marṣayemahi||12||

13īśo naḥ puṇyatapasāṃ prāṇānāmapi ceśvaraḥ
|
manyate'jitamātmānaṃ yadyeṣa vijitā vayam||13||

14na hi mucyeta me jīvanpadā bhūmimupaspṛśan
|
martyadharmā parāmṛśya pāñcālyā mūrdhajānimān||14||

15paśyadhvaṃ hyāyatau vṛttau bhujau me parighāviva
|
naitayorantaraṃ prāpya mucyetāpi śatakratuḥ||15||

16dharmapāśasitastvevaṃ nādhigacchāmi saṅkaṭam
|
gauraveṇa niruddhaśca nigrahādarjunasya ca||16||

17dharmarājanisṛṣṭastu siṃhaḥ kṣudramṛgāniva
|
dhārtarāṣṭrānimānpāpānniṣpiṣeyaṃ talāsibhiḥ||17||

18vaiśampāyana uvāca|
tamuvāca tadā bhīṣmo droṇo vidura eva ca
|
kṣamyatāmidamityevaṃ sarvaṃ sambhāvyate tvayi||18||
