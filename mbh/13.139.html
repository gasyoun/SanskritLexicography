Mahābhārata 13.139 (Anuśāsanaparvan)

1yudhiṣṭhira uvāca|
pitāmaha mahāprājña sarvaśāstraviśārada
|
āgamairbahubhiḥ sphīto bhavānnaḥ pravare kule ||1||

2tvatto dharmārthasaṃyuktamāyatyāṃ ca sukhodayam
|
āścaryabhūtaṃ lokasya śrotumicchāmyarindama ||2||

3ayaṃ ca kālaḥ saṃprāpto durlabho jñātibāndhavaiḥ
|
śāstā ca na hi naḥ kaścittvāmṛte puruṣarṣabha ||3||

4yadi te'hamanugrāhyo bhrātṛbhiḥ sahito'nagha
|
vaktumarhasi naḥ praśnaṃ yattvāṃ pṛcchāmi pārthiva ||4||

5ayaṃ nārāyaṇaḥ śrīmān sarvapārthivasaṃmataḥ
|
bhavantaṃ bahumānena praśrayeṇa ca sevate ||5||

6asya caiva samakṣaṃ tvaṃ pārthivānāṃ ca sarvaśaḥ
|
bhrātṝṇāṃ ca priyārthaṃ me snehādbhāṣitumarhasi ||6||

7vaiśampāyana uvāca|
tasya tadvacanaṃ śrutvā snehādāgatasaṃbhramaḥ
|
bhīṣmo bhāgīrathīputra idaṃ vacanamabravīt ||7||

8bhīṣma uvāca|
ahaṃ te kathayiṣyāmi kathāmatimanoharām
|
asya viṣṇoḥ purā rājanprabhāvo yo mayā śrutaḥ ||8||

9yaśca govṛṣabhāṅkasya prabhāvastaṃ ca me śṛṇu
|
rudrāṇyāḥ saṃśayo yaśca dampatyostaṃ ca me śṛṇu ||9||

10vrataṃ cacāra dharmātmā kṛṣṇo dvādaśavārṣikam
|
dīkṣitaṃ cāgatau draṣṭumubhau nāradaparvatau ||10||

11kṛṣṇadvaipāyanaścaiva dhaumyaśca japatāṃ varaḥ
|
devalaḥ kāśyapaścaiva hastikāśyapa eva ca ||11||

12apare carṣayaḥ santo dīkṣādamasamanvitāḥ
|
śiṣyairanugatāḥ siddhairdevakalpaistapodhanaiḥ ||12||

13teṣāmatithisatkāramarcanīyaṃ kulocitam
|
devakītanayaḥ pratio devakalpamakalpayat ||13||

14hariteṣu suvarṇeṣu barhiṣkeṣu naveṣu ca
|
upopaviviśuḥ prītā viṣṭareṣu maharṣayaḥ ||14||

15kathāścakrustataste tu madhurā dharmasaṃhitāḥ
|
rājarṣīṇāṃ surāṇāṃ ca ye vasanti tapodhanāḥ ||15||

16tato nārāyaṇaṃ tejo vratacaryendhanotthitam
|
vaktrāniḥsṛtya kṛṣṇasya vahniradbhutakarmaṇaḥ ||16||

17so'gnirdadāha taṃ śailaṃ sadrumaṃ salatākṣupam
|
sapakṣimṛgasaṃghātaṃ saśvāpadasarīsṛpam ||17||

18mṛgaiśca vividhākārairhāhābhūtamacetanam
|
śikharaṃ tasya śailasya mathitaṃ dīnadarśanam ||18||

19sa tu vahnirmahājyālo dagdhvā sarvamaśeṣataḥ
|
viṣṇoḥ samīpa āgamya pādau śiṣyavadaspṛśat ||19||

20tato viṣṇugiriṃ dṛṣṭvā nirdagdhamarikarśanaḥ
|
saumyairdṛṣṭinipātaistaṃ punaḥ prakṛtimānayat ||20||

21tathaiva sa girirbhūyaḥ prapuṣpitalatādrumaḥ
|
sapakṣigaṇasaṃghuṣṭaḥ saśvāpadasarīsṛpaḥ ||21||

22tamadbhutamacintyaṃ ca dṛṣṭvā sunigaṇastadā
|
vismito hṛṣṭaromā ca babhūvāsrāvilekṣaṇaḥ ||22||

23tato nārāyaṇo dṛṣṭvā tānṛṣīnvismayānvitān
|
praśritaṃ madhuraṃ snigdhaṃ papraccha vadatāṃ varaḥ ||23||

24kimarthamṛṣipūgasya tyaktasaṅgasya nityaśaḥ
|
nirmamasyāgamavato vismayaḥ samupāgataḥ ||24||

25etanme saṃśayaṃ sarve yāthātathyamaninditāḥ
|
ṛṣayo vaktumarhanti niścitārthaṃ tapodhanāḥ ||25||

26ṛṣaya ūcuḥ |
bhavānvisṛjate lokān bhavānsaṃharate punaḥ
|
bhavān śītaṃ bhavānuṣṇaṃ bhavāneva ca varṣati ||26||

27pṛthivyāṃ yāni bhūtāni sthāvarāṇi carāṇi ca
|
teṣāṃ pitā tvaṃ mātā tvaṃ prabhuḥ prabhava eva ca ||27||

28evaṃ no vismayakaraṃ saṃśayaṃ madhusūdana
|
tvamevārhasi kalyāṇa vaktuṃ vahnervinirgamam ||28||

29tato vigatasaṃtrāsā vayamapyarikarśana
|
yacchrutaṃ yacca dṛṣṭaṃ nastatpravakṣyāmahe hare ||29||

30vāsudeva uvāca|
etadvai vaiṣṇavaṃ tejo mama vaktrādviniḥsṛtam
|
kṛṣṇavartmā yugāntābho yenāyaṃ mathito giriḥ ||30||

31ṛṣayaścārtimāpannā jitakrodhā jitendriyāḥ
|
bhavanto vyathitāścāsan devakalpāstapodhanāḥ ||31||

32vratacaryāparītasya tapasvivratasevayā
|
mama vahniḥ samudbhūto na vai vyathitumarhatha ||32||

33vrataṃ cartumihāyātastvahaṃ girimimaṃ śubham
|
putraṃ cātmasamaṃ vīrye tapasā labdhumāgataḥ ||33||

34tato mamātmā yo dehe sognirbhūtvā viniḥsṛtaḥ
|
gataśca varadaṃ draṣṭuṃ sarvalokapitāmaham ||34||

35tena cātmānuśiṣṭo me putratve munisattamāḥ
|
tejaso'rdhena putraste bhaviteti vṛṣadhvajaḥ ||35||

36so'yaṃ vahnirupāgamya pādamūle mamāntikam
|
śiṣyavatparicaryārthaṃ śāntaḥ prakṛtimāgataḥ ||36||

37etadeva rahasyaṃ vaḥ padmanābhasya dhīmataḥ
|
mayā proktaṃ samāsena na bhīḥ kāryā tapodhanāḥ ||37||

38sarvatra gatiravyagrā bhavatāṃ dīrghadarśanāt
|
tapasvivratasaṃdīptā jñānavijñānaśobhitāḥ ||38||

39yacchrutaṃ yacca vo dṛṣṭaṃ divi vā yadi vā bhuvi
|
āścaryaṃ paramaṃ kiñcittadbhavanto bruvantu me ||39||

40tasyāmṛtanikāśasya vāṅmadhorasti me spṛhā
|
bhavadbhiḥ kathitasyeha tapovananivāsibhiḥ ||40||

41yadyapyahamadṛṣṭaṃ vo divyamadbhutadarśanam
|
divi vā bhuvi vā kiñcitpaśyāmyamaradarśanāḥ ||41||

42prakṛtiḥ sā mama parā na kvacitpratihanyate
|
na cātmagatamaiśvaryamāścaryaṃ pratibhāti me ||42||

43śraddheyaḥ kathito hyarthaḥ sajjanaśravaṇaṃ gataḥ
|
ciraṃ tiṣṭhati medinyāṃ śaile lekhyamivārpitam ||43||

44tadahaṃ sajjanamukhānnisṛtaṃ tatsamāgame
|
kathayiṣyāmyahamaho buddhidīpakaraṃ nṛṇām ||44||

45tato munigaṇāḥ sarve vismitāḥ kṛṣṇasannidhau
|
netraiḥ padmadalaprakhyairapaśyaṃstaṃ janārdanam ||45||

46vardhayantastathaivānye pūjayantastathā'pare
|
vāgbhirṛgbhūṣitārthābhiḥ stuvanto madhusūdanam ||46||

47tato sunigaṇāḥ sarve nāradaṃ devadarśanam
|
tadā niyojayāmāsurvacane vākyakovidam ||47||

48munaya ūcuḥ |
yadāścaryamacintyaṃ ca girau himavati prabho
|
anubhūtaṃ munigaṇaistīrthayātrāparairmune ||48||

49tadbhavānṛṣisaṃghasya hitārthaṃ sarvamāditaḥ
|
yathā dṛṣṭaṃ hṛṣīkeśe sarvamākhyātumarhasi ||49||

50evamuktaḥ sa munibhirnārado bhagavān muniḥ
|
kathayāmāsa devarṣiḥ pūrvavṛttāmimāṃ kathām ||50||
