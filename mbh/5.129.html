Mahābhārata 5.129 (Udyogaparvan)

1vaiśampāyana uvāca|
kṛṣṇasya tu vacaḥ śrutvā dhṛtarāṣṭro janeśvaraḥ
|
viduraṃ sarvadharmajñaṃ tvaramāṇo'bhyabhāṣata ||1||

2gaccha tāta mahāprājñāṃ gāndhārīṃ dīrghadarśinīm
|
ānayeha tayā sārdhamanuneṣyāmi durmatim ||2||

3yadi sā'pi durātmānaṃ śamayedduṣṭacetasam
|
api kṛṣṇasya suhṛdastiṣṭhema vacane vayam ||3||

4api lobhābhibhūtasya panthānamanudarśayet
|
durbuddherduḥsahāyasya śamārthaṃ bruvatī vacaḥ ||4||

5api no vyasanaṃ ghoraṃ duryodhanakṛtaṃ mahat
|
śamayeccirarātrāya yogakṣemavadavyayam ||5||

6rājñastu vacanaṃ śrutvā viduro dīrghadarśinīm
|
ānayāmāsa gāndhārīṃ dhṛtarāṣṭrasya śāsanāt ||6||

7dhṛtarāṣṭra uvāca|
eṣa gāndhāri putraste durātmā śāsanātigaḥ
|
aiśvaryalobhādaiśvaryaṃ jīvitaṃ ca prahāsyati ||7||

8aśiṣṭavadamaryādaḥ pāpaiḥ saha durātmavān
|
sabhāyā nirgato mūḍho vyatikramya suhṛdvacaḥ ||8||

9vaiśampāyana uvāca|
sā bhartṛvacanaṃ śrutvā rājaputrī yaśasvinī
|
anvicchantī mahacchreyo gāndhārī vākyamabravīt ||9||

10gāndhāryuvāca
|
ānāyaya sutaṃ kṣipraṃ rājyakāmukamāturam
|
na hi rājyamaśiṣṭena śakyaṃ dharmārthalopinā ||10||

11āptumāptaṃ tathāpīdamavinītena sarvathā
|
tvaṃ hyevātra bhṛśaṃ garho dhṛtarāṣṭra sutapriyaḥ ||11||

12yo jānan pāpatāmasya tatprajñāmanuvartase
|
sa eṣa kāmamanyubhyāṃ pralabdho lobhamāsthitaḥ ||12||

13aśakyo'dya tvayā rājanvinivartayituṃ balāt
|
rāṣṭrapradāne mūḍhasya bāliśasya durātmanaḥ ||13||

14duḥsahāyasya lubdhasya dhṛtarāṣṭro'śnute phalam
|
kathaṃ hi svajane bhedamupekṣeta mahīpatiḥ
|
bhinnaṃ hi svajanena tvāṃ prahasiṣyanti śatravaḥ ||14||

15yā hi śakyā mahārāja sāmnā bhedena vā punaḥ
|
nistartumāpadaḥ sveṣu daṇḍaṃ kastatra pātayet ||15||

16vaiśampāyana uvāca|
śāsanāddhṛtarāṣṭrasya duryodhanamamarṣaṇam
|
mātuśca vacanāt kṣattā sabhāṃ prāveśayat punaḥ ||16||

17sa māturvacanākāṅkṣī praviveśa punaḥ sabhām
|
abhitāmrekṣaṇaḥ krodhānniḥśvasanniva pannagaḥ ||17||

18taṃ praviṣṭamabhiprekṣya putramutpathamāsthitam
|
vigarhamāṇā gāndhārī śamārthaṃ vākyamabravīt ||18||

19duryodhana nibodhedaṃ vacanaṃ mama putraka
|
hitaṃ te sānubandhasya tathā'yatyāṃ sukhodayam ||19||

20duryodhana yadāha tvāṃ pitā bharatasattama
|
bhīṣmo droṇaḥ kṛpaḥ kṣattā suhṛdāṃ kuru tadvacaḥ ||20||

21bhīṣmasya tu pituścaiva mama cāpacitiḥ kṛtā
|
bhaveddroṇamukhānāṃ ca suhṛdāṃ śāmyatā tvayā ||21||

22na hi rājyaṃ mahāprājña svena kāmena śakyate
|
avāptuṃ rakṣituṃ vā'pi moktuṃ bharatasattama ||22||

23na hyavaśyendriyo rājyamaśnīyāddīrghamantaram
|
vijitātmā tu medhāvī sa rājyamabhipālayet ||23||

24kāmakrodhau hi puruṣamarthebhyo vyapakarṣataḥ
|
tau tu śatrū vinirjitya rājā vijayate mahīm ||24||

25lokeśvaraprabhutvaṃ hi mahadetaddurātmabhiḥ
|
rājyaṃ nāmepsitaṃ sthānaṃ na śakyamabhirakṣitum ||25||

26indriyāṇi mahatprepsurniyacchedarthadharmayoḥ
|
indriyairniyatairbuddhirvardhate'gnirivendhanaiḥ ||26||

27avidheyāni hīmāni vyāpādayitumapyalam
|
avidheyā ivādāntā hayāḥ pathi kusārathim ||27||

28avijitya ya ātmānamamātyān vijigīṣate
|
amitrān vā'jitāmātyaḥ so'vaśaḥ parihīyate ||28||

29ātmānameva prathamaṃ dveṣyarūpeṇa yojayet
|
tato'mātyānamitrāṃśca na moghaṃ vijigīṣate ||29||

30vaśyendriyaṃ jitāmātyaṃ dhṛtadaṇḍaṃ vikāriṣu
|
parīkṣyakāriṇaṃ dhīramatyarthaṃ śrīrniṣevate ||30||

31kṣudrākṣeṇeva jālena jhaṣāvapihitāvubhau
|
kāmakrodhau śarīrasthau prajñānaṃ tau vilumpataḥ ||31||

32yābhyāṃ hi devāḥ svaryātuḥ svargasya pidadhurmukham
|
bibhyato'nuparāgasya kāmakrodhau sma vardhitau ||32||

33kāmaṃ krodhaṃ ca lomaṃ ca daṃbhaṃ darpaṃ ca bhūmipaḥ
|
samyagvijetuṃ yo veda sa mahīmabhijāyate ||33||

34satataṃ nigrahe yukta indriyāṇāṃ bhavennṛpaḥ
|
īpsannarthaṃ ca dharmaṃ ca dviṣatāṃ ca parābhavam ||34||

35kāmābhibhūtaḥ krodhādvā yo mithyā pratipadyate
|
sveṣu cānyeṣu vā tasya na sahāyā bhavantyuta ||35||

36ekībhūtairmahāprājñaiḥ śūrairarinibarhaṇaiḥ
|
pāṇḍavaiḥ pṛthivīṃ tāta bhokṣyase sahitaḥ sukhī ||36||

37yathā bhīṣmaḥ śāntanavo droṇaścāpi mahārathaḥ
|
āhutastāta tatsatyamajeyau kṛṣṇapāṇḍavau ||37||

38prapadyasva mahābāhuṃ kṛṣṇamakliṣṭakāriṇam
|
prasanno hi sakhāya syādubhayoreva keśavaḥ ||38||

39suhṛdāmarthakāmānāṃ yo na tiṣṭhati śāsane
|
prājñānāṃ kṛtavidyānāṃ sa naraḥ śatrunandanaḥ ||39||

40na yuddhe tāta kalyāṇaṃ na dharmārthau kutaḥ sukham
|
na cāpi vijayo nityaṃ mā yuddhe ceta ādhithāḥ ||40||

41bhīṣmeṇa hi mahāprājña pitrā te bāhlikena ca
|
dattoṃśaḥ pāṇḍuputrāṇāṃ medādbhītairarindama ||41||

42tasya caitatpradānasya phalamadyānupaśyasi
|
yadbhaṅkṣe pṛthivīṃ kṛtsnāṃ śūrairnihatakaṇṭakām ||42||

43prayaccha pāṇḍuputrāṇāṃ yathocitamarindama
|
yadīcchasi sahāmātyo bhoktumardhaṃ pradīyatām ||43||

44alamardhaṃ pṛthivyāste sahāmātyasya jīvitum
|
suhṛdāṃ vacane tiṣṭhan yaśaḥ prāpsyasi bhārata ||44||

45śrīmadbhirātmavadbhistairbuddhimadbhirjitendriyaiḥ
|
pāṇḍavairvigrahastāta bhraṃśayenmahataḥ sukhāt ||45||

46nigṛhya suhṛdāṃ manyuṃ śādhi rājyaṃ yathocitam
|
svamaṃśaṃ pāṇḍuputrebhyaḥ pradāya bharatarṣabha ||46||

47alamaṅga nikāro'yaṃ trayodaśa samāḥ kṛtaḥ
|
śamayainaṃ mahāprājña kāmakrodhasamedhitam ||47||

48na caiṣa śaktaḥ pārthānāṃ yastvamarthamabhīpsasi
|
sūtaputro dṛḍhakrodho bhrātā duḥśāsanaśca te ||48||

49bhīṣme droṇe kṛpe karṇe bhīmasene dhanañjaye
|
dhṛṣṭadyumne ca saṃkruddhe na syuḥ sarvāḥ prajā dhruvam ||49||

50amarṣavaśamāpanno mā kurūṃstāta jīghanaḥ
|
eṣā hi pṛthivī kṛtsnā māgamattvatkṛte vadham ||50||

51yacca tvaṃ manyase mūḍha bhīṣmadroṇakṛpādayaḥ
|
yotsyante sarvaśaktyeti naitadadyopapadyate ||51||

52samaṃ hi rājyaṃ prītiśca sthānaṃ hi viditātmanām
|
pāṇḍaveṣvatha yuṣmāsu dharmastvabhyadhikastataḥ ||52||

53rājapiṇḍabhayādete yadi hāsyanti jīvitam
|
na hi śakṣyanti rājānaṃ yudhiṣṭhiramudīkṣitum ||53||

54na lobhādarthasampattirnarāṇāmiha dṛśyate
|
tadalaṃ tāta lobhena praśāmya bharatarṣabha ||54||
