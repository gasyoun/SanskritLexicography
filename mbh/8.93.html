Mahābhārata 8.93 (Karṇaparvan)

1dhṛtarāṣṭra uvāca|
tasmiṃstu karṇārjunayorvimarde dagdhasya raudre'hani vidrutasya
|
babhūva rūpaṃ kurusṛñjayānāṃ balasya bāṇonmathitasya kīdṛk ||1||

2sañjaya uvāca|
śṛṇu rājannavahito yathā vṛtto mahākṣayaḥ
|
ghoro manuṣyadehānāmājau ca gajavājinām ||2||

3yatra karṇe hate pārthaḥ siṃhanāda‌mathākarot
|
tadā tava sutānrājannāviveśa mahadbhayam ||3||

4na sandhātumanīkāni na caivāśu parākrame
|
āsīdbuddhirhate karṇe tava yodhasya karhicit ||4||

5vaṇijo nāvi bhinnāyāmagādhe viplave yathā
|
apāre pāramicchanto hate dvīpe kirīṭinā ||5||

6sūtaputre hate rājan vitrastāḥ śastravikṣatāḥ
|
anāthā nāthamicchanto mṛgāḥ siṃhairivārditāḥ ||6||

7bhagnaśṛṃgā vṛṣā yadvadbhagnadaṃṣṭrā ivoragāḥ
|
pratyapāyāmasāyānhe nirjitāḥ savyasācinā ||7||

8hatapravīrā vidhvastā nikṛttā niśitaiḥ śaraiḥ
|
sūtaputre hate rājan putrāste dudruvurbhayāt ||8||

9visrastayantrakavacāḥ kāṃdigbhūtā vicetasaḥ
|
anyonyamavamṛdnanto vīkṣamāṇā bhayārditāḥ ||9||

10māmeva nūnaṃ bībhatsurmāmeva ca vṛkodaraḥ
|
abhiyātīti manvānāḥ peturmamluśca saṃbhramāt ||10||

11hayānanye gajānanye rathānanye mahārathāḥ
|
āruhya javasaṃpannāḥ padātīnprajahurbhayāt ||11||

12kuñjaraiḥ syandanāḥ kṣuṇṇāḥ sādinaśca mahārathaiḥ
|
padātisaṃghāścāśvaughaiḥ palāyadbhirbhayārditaiḥ ||12||

13vyālataskarasaṃkīrṇe sārthahīnā yathā vane
|
sūtaputre hate rājaṃstava yodhāstathā'bhavan ||13||

14hatārohā yathā nāgāśchinnahastā yathā narāḥ
|
sarve pārthamayaṃ lokaṃ saṃpaśyanto bhayārditāḥ ||14||

15saṃprekṣya dravataḥ sarvān bhīmasenabhayārditān
|
duryodhano'tha svaṃ sūtaṃ hāhākṛtveda‌mabravīt ||15||

16nātikramecca māṃ pārtho dhanuṣpāṇimavasthitam
|
jaghane sarvasainyānāṃ śanairaśvānpracodaya ||16||

17yudhyamānaṃ hi kaunteyaṃ haniṣyāmi na saṃśayaḥ
|
notsahenmāmatikrāntuṃ velāmiva mahodadhiḥ ||17||

18adyārjunaṃ sagovindaṃ māninaṃ ca vṛkodaram
|
hanyāṃ śiṣṭāṃstathā śatrūn karṇasyānṛṇyamāpnuyām ||18||

19tacchrutvā kururājasya śūrāryasadṛśaṃ vacaḥ
|
sūto hemaparicchannān śanairaśvānacodayat ||19||

20rathāśvanāgahīnāstu pādātāstava māriṣa
|
pañcaviṃśatisāhasrā yuddhāyaiva vyavasthitāḥ ||20||

21tān bhīmasenaḥ saṃkruddho dhṛṣṭadyumnaśca pārṣataḥ
|
balena caturaṅgeṇa saṃvṛtyājaghnatuḥ śaraiḥ ||21||

22pratyayudhyanta samare bhīmasenaṃ sapārṣatam
|
pārthapārṣatayoścānye jagṛhustatra nāmanī ||22||

23akrudhyata raṇe bhīmastaistadā paryavasthitaiḥ
|
so'vatīrya rathāttūrṇaṃ gadāpāṇirayudhyata ||23||

24na tānrathastho bhūmiṣṭhān dharmāpekṣī vṛkodaraḥ
|
yodhayāmāsa kaunteyo bhujavīryavyapāśrayaḥ ||24||

25jātarūpaparicchannāṃ pragṛhya mahatīṃ gadām
|
avadhīttāvakānsarvān daṇḍapāṇirivāntakaḥ ||25||

26padātino'pi saṃtyaktvā priyaṃ jīvitamātmanaḥ
|
bhīmamabhyadravansaṃkhye pataṅgā jvalanaṃ yathā ||26||

27āsādya bhīmasenaṃ tu saṃrabdhā yuddhadurmadāḥ
|
vineśuḥ sahasā dṛṣṭvā bhūtagrāmā ivāntakam ||27||

28śyenavadvicaran bhīmo gadāhasto mahābalaḥ
|
pañcaviṃśatisāhasrāṃstāvakānavapothayat ||28||

29hatvā tatpuruṣānīkaṃ bhīmaḥ satyaparākramaḥ
|
dhṛṣṭadyumnaṃ puraskṛtya tasthau tatra mahābalaḥ ||29||

30dhanañjayo rathānīkamabhyavartata vīryavān
|
mādrīputrau tu śakuniṃ sātyakiśca mahārathaḥ ||30||

31javenābhyapatan hṛṣṭā ghnanto dauryodhanaṃ balam
|
tasyāśvasādīnsubahūṃste nihatya śitaiḥ śaraiḥ ||31||

32samabhyadhāvaṃstvaritāstatra yuddhamabhūnmahat
|
dhanañjayo'pi cābhyetya rathānīkaṃ tava prabho ||32||

33viśrutaṃ triṣu lokeṣu gāṇḍīvaṃ vikṣipan dhanuḥ
|
kṛṣṇasārathimāyāntaṃ dṛṣṭvā śvetahayaṃ ratham ||33||

34arjunaṃ cāpi yoddhāraṃ tvadīyāḥ prādravanbhayāt
|
viprahīṇarathāścaiva śaraiśca parikarṣitāḥ ||34||

35pañcaviṃśatisāhasrāḥ kālamārchan padātayaḥ
|
hatvā tān puruṣavyāghraḥ pañcālānāṃ mahārathaḥ ||35||

36putraḥ pāñcālarājasya dhṛṣṭadyumno mahāmanāḥ
|
bhīmasenaṃ puraskṛtya na cirātpratyadṛśyata ||36||

37mahādhanurdharaḥ śrīmānamitragaṇatāpanaḥ
|
pārāvatasavarṇāśvaṃ kovidāramayadhvajam ||37||

38dhṛṣṭadyumnaṃ raṇe dṛṣṭvā tvadīyāḥ prādravan bhayāt
|
gāndhārarājaṃ śīghrāstramanusṛtya yaśasvinau ||38||

39na cirātpratyadṛśyetāṃ mādrīputrau sasātyakī
|
cekitānaḥ śikhaṇḍī ca draupadeyāśca māriṣa ||39||

40hatvā tvadīyaṃ sumahatsainyaṃ śaṃkhāṃstathā'dhaman
|
te sarve tāvakān prekṣya dravato'pi parāṅmukhān ||40||

41abhyavartanta saṃrabdhān vṛṣāñjitvā yathā vṛṣāḥ
|
senāvaśeṣaṃ taṃ dṛṣṭvā tava sainyasya pāṇḍavaḥ ||41||

42vyavasthitaḥ savyasācī cukrodha balavānnṛpa
|
dhanañjayo rathānīkamabhyavartata vīryavān ||42||

43viśrutaṃ triṣu lokeṣu vyākṣipadgāṇḍivaṃ dhanuḥ
|
tata enāñśaravrātaiḥ sahasā samavākirat ||43||

44tamasā saṃvṛtenātha na sma kiṃcidvyadṛśyata
|
andhakārīkṛte loke rajobhūte mahītale ||44||

45yodhāḥ sarve mahārāja tāvakāḥ prādravanbhayāt
|
saṃbhajyamāne sainye tu kururājo viśāṃpate ||45||

46parānabhimukhāṃścaiva sutaste samupādravat
|
tato duryodhanaḥ sarvānājuhāvātha pāṇḍavān ||46||

47yuddhāya bharataśreṣṭha devāniva purā baliḥ
|
ta enamabhigarjantaḥ sahitāḥ samupādravan ||47||

48nānāśastrabhṛtaḥ kruddhā bhartsayanto muhurmuhuḥ
|
duryodhano'pyasaṃbhrāntastānraṇe niśitaiḥ śaraiḥ ||48||

49tatrāvadhīttataḥ kruddhaḥ śataśo'tha sahasraśaḥ
|
tatsainyaṃ pāṇḍaveyānāṃ yodhayāmāsa sarvataḥ ||49||

50tatrādbhutamapaśyāma tava putrasya pauruṣam
|
yadekaḥ sahitānsarvān raṇe'yudhyata pāṇḍavān ||50||

51tato'paśyanmahātmā sa svasainyaṃ bhṛśaduḥkhitam
|
tato'vasthāpya rājendra kṛtabuddhistavātmajaḥ ||51||

52harṣayanniva tān yodhānidaṃ vacanamabravīt
|
na taṃ deśaṃ prapaśyāmi yatra yātā bhayārditāḥ ||52||

53gatānāṃ yatra vai mokṣaḥ pāṇḍavātkiṃ gatena vaḥ
|
alpaṃ ca balameteṣāṃ kṛṣṇau ca bhṛśavikṣatau ||53||

54adya sarvānhaniṣyāmi dhruvo hi vijayo bhavet
|
viprayātāṃstu vo bhinnān pāṇḍavāḥ kṛtakilbiṣān ||54||

55anusṛtya vadhiṣyanti śreyānnaḥ samare vadhaḥ
|
sukhaṃ sāṃgrāmiko mṛtyuḥ kṣatradharmeṇa yudhyatām ||55||

56mṛto duḥkhaṃ na jānīte pretya cānantyamaśnute
|
śṛṇudhvaṃ kṣatriyāḥ sarve yāvantaḥ stha samāgatāḥ ||56||

57yadā śūraṃ ca bhīruṃ ca mārayatyantako yamaḥ
|
ko na mūḍho na yudhyeta mādṛśaḥ kṣatriyavrataḥ ||57||

58dviṣato bhīmasenasya kruddhasya vaśameṣyatha
|
pitāmahairācaritaṃ na dharmaṃ hātumarhatha ||58||

59na hyadharmo'sti pāpīyān kṣatriyasya palāyanāt
|
na yuddhadharmācchreyo hi panthāḥ svargasya kauravāḥ
|
acireṇa hatā lokān sadyo yodhāḥ samaśnuta ||59||

60sañjaya uvāca|
evaṃ bruvati putre te sainikā bhṛśavikṣatāḥ
|
anavekṣyaiva tadvākyaṃ prādravansarvato diśaḥ ||60||
