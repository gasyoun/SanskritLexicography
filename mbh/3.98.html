Mahābhārata 3.98 (Āraṇyakaparvan)

1lomaśa uvāca| tato jagāma kauravya so'gastyo bhikṣituṃ vasu
|
śrutarvāṇaṃ mahīpālaṃ yaṃ vedābhyadhikaṃ nṛpaiḥ||1||

2sa viditvā tu nṛpatiḥ kumbhayonimupāgatam
|
viṣayānte sahāmātyaḥ pratyagṛhṇātsusatkṛtam||2||

3tasmai cārghyaṃ yathānyāyamānīya pṛthivīpatiḥ
|
prāñjaliḥ prayato bhūtvā papracchāgamane'rthitām||3||

4agastya uvāca| vittārthinamanuprāptaṃ viddhi māṃ pṛthivīpate
|
yathāśaktyavihiṃsyānyānsaṃvibhāgaṃ prayaccha me||4||

5lomaśa uvāca| tata āyavyayau pūrṇau tasmai rājā nyavedayat
|
ato vidvannupādatsva yadatra vasu manyate||5||

6tata āyavyayau dṛṣṭvā samau samamatirdvijaḥ
|
sarvathā prāṇināṃ pīḍāmupādānādamanyata||6||

7sa śrutarvāṇamādāya bradhnaśvamagamattataḥ
|
sa ca tau viṣayasyānte pratyagṛhṇādyathāvidhi||7||

8tayorarghyaṃ ca pādyaṃ ca bradhnaśvaḥ pratyavedayat
|
anujñāpya ca papraccha prayojanamupakrame||8||

9agastya uvāca| vittakāmāviha prāptau viddhyāvāṃ pṛthivīpate
|
yathāśaktyavihiṃsyānyānsaṃvibhāgaṃ prayaccha nau||9||

10lomaśa uvāca| tata āyavyayau pūrṇau tābhyāṃ rājā nyavedayat
|
ato jñātvā tu gṛhṇītaṃ yadatravyatiricyate||10||

11tata āyavyayau dṛṣṭvā samau samamatirdvijaḥ
|
sarvathā prāṇināṃ pīḍāmupādānādamanyata||11||

12paurukutsaṃ tato jagmustrasadasyuṃ mahādhanam
|
agastyaśca śrutarvā ca bradhnaśvaśca mahīpatiḥ||12||

13trasadasyustu tāndṛṣṭvā pratyagṛhṇādyathāvidhi
|
abhigamya mahārāja viṣayānte mahāmanāḥ||13||

14arcayitvā yathānyāyamaikṣvākū rājasattamaḥ
|
samastāṃśca tato'pṛcchatprayojanamupakrame||14||

15agastya uvāca| vittakāmāniha prāptānviddhi naḥ pṛthivīpate
|
yathāśaktyavihiṃsyānyānsaṃvibhāgaṃ prayaccha naḥ||15||

16lomaśa uvāca| tata āyavyayau pūrṇau teṣāṃ rājā nyavedayat
|
etajjñātvā hyupādadhvaṃ yadatra vyatiricyate||16||

17tata āyavyayau dṛṣṭvā samau samamatirdvijaḥ
|
sarvathā prāṇināṃ pīḍāmupādānādamanyata||17||

18tataḥ sarve sametyātha te nṛpāstaṃ mahāmunim
|
idamūcurmahārāja samavekṣya parasparam||18||

19ayaṃ vai dānavo brahmannilvalo vasumānbhuvi
|
tamatikramya sarve'dya vayaṃ cārthāmahe vasu||19||

20lomaśa uvāca| teṣāṃ tadāsīducitamilvalasyaiva bhikṣaṇam
|
tataste sahitā rājannilvalaṃ samupādravan||20||
