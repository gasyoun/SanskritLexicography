Mahābhārata 5.84 (Udyogaparvan)

1vaiśampāyana uvāca|
prayāntaṃ devakīputraṃ paravīrarujo daśa
|
mahārathā mahābāhumanvayuḥ śastrapāṇayaḥ ||1||

2padātīnāṃ sahasraṃ ca sādināṃ ca paraṃtapa
|
bhojyaṃ ca vipulaṃ rājan preṣyāśca śataśo'pare ||2||

3janamejaya uvāca|
kathaṃ prayāto dāśārho mahātmā madhusūdanaḥ
|
kāni vā vrajatastasya nimittāni mahaujasaḥ ||3||

4vaiśampāyana uvāca|
tasya prayāṇe yānyāsannimittāni mahātmanaḥ
|
tāni me śṛṇu sarvāṇi daivānyautpātikāni ca ||4||

5anamre'śaninirghoṣaḥ savidyutsamajāyata
|
anvageva ca parjanyaḥ prāvarṣadvighane bhṛśam ||5||

6pratyagūhurmahānadyaḥ prāṅmukhāḥ sindhusaptamāḥ
|
viparītā diśaḥ sarvā na prājñāyata kiñcana ||6||

7prājvalannagnayo rājan pṛthivī samakampata
|
udapānāśca kuṃbhāśca prāsiñcan śataśo jalam ||7||

8tamaḥsaṃvṛtamapyāsīt sarvaṃ jagadidaṃ tathā
|
na diśo nādiśo rājan prajñāyante sma reṇunā ||8||

9prādurāsīnmahāñchabdaḥ khe śarīraṃ na dṛśyate
|
sarveṣu rājan deśeṣu tadadbhutamivābhavat ||9||

10prāmathnāddhāstinapuraṃ vāto dakṣiṇapaścimaḥ
|
ārujan gaṇaśo vṛkṣān paruṣo'śaniniḥsvanaḥ ||10||

11yatra yatra ca vārṣṇeyo vartate pathi bhārata
|
tatra tatra sukho vāyuḥ sarvaṃ cāsīt pradakṣiṇam ||11||

12vavarṣa puṣpavarṣaṃ ca kamalāni ca bhūriśaḥ
|
samaśca panthā nirduḥkho vyapetakuśakaṇṭakaḥ ||12||

13saṃstuto brāhmaṇairgīrbhistatra tatra sahasraśaḥ
|
arcyate madhuparkaiśca vasubhiśca vasupradaḥ ||13||

14taṃ kiranti mahātmānaṃ vanyaiḥ puṣpaiḥ sugandhibhiḥ
|
striyaḥ pathi samāgamya sarvabhūtahite ratam ||14||

15sa śālibhavanaṃ ramyaṃ sarvasasyasamācitam
|
sukhaṃ paramadharmiṣṭhamabhyagādbharatarṣabha||15||

16paśyan bahupaśūn grāmān ramyān hṛdayatoṣaṇān
|
purāṇi ca vyatikrāman rāṣṭrāṇi vividhāni ca ||16||

17nityaṃ hṛṣṭāḥ sumanaso bhāratairabhirakṣitāḥ
|
nodvignāḥ paracakrāṇāṃ vyasanānāmakovidāḥ ||17||

18upaplavyādathāgamya janāḥ puranivāsinaḥ
|
pathyatiṣṭhanta sahitā viṣvaksenadidṛkṣayā ||18||

19te tu sarve samāyāntamagnimiddhamiva prabhum
|
arcayāmāsurarcārhaṃ deśātithimupasthitam ||19||

20vṛkasthalaṃ samāsādya keśavaḥ paravīrahā
|
prakīrṇaraśmāvāditye vyomni vai lohitāyati ||20||

21avatīrya rathāttūrṇaṃ kṛtvā śaucaṃ yathāvidhi
|
rathamocanamādiśya sandhyāmupaviveśa ha ||21||

22dāruko'pi hayānmuktvā paricarya ca śāstrataḥ
|
mumoca sarvayoktrādi muktvā caitānavāsṛjat ||22||

23abhyatītya tu tatsarvamuvāca madhusūdanaḥ
|
yudhiṣṭhirasya kāryārthamiha vatsyāmahe kṣapām ||23||

24tasya tanmatamājñāya cakrurāvasathaṃ narāḥ
|
kṣaṇena cānnapānāni guṇavanti samārjayan ||24||

25tasmin grāme pradhānāstu ya āsan brāhmaṇā nṛpa
|
āryāḥ kulīnā hrīmanto brāhmīṃ vṛttimanuṣṭhitāḥ ||25||

26te'bhigamya mahātmānaṃ hṛṣīkeśamarindamam
|
pūjāṃ cakruryathānyāyamāśīrmaṅgalasaṃyutām ||26||

27te pūjayitvā dāśārhaṃ sarvalokeṣu pūjitam
|
nyavedayanta veśmāni ratnavanti mahātmane ||27||

28tān prabhuḥ kṛtamityuktvā satkṛtya ca yathārhataḥ
|
abhyetya caiṣāṃ veśmāni punarāyāt sahaiva taiḥ ||28||

29sumṛṣṭaṃ bhojayitvā ca brāhmaṇāṃstatra keśavaḥ
|
bhuktvā ca saha taiḥ sarvairavasattāṃ kṣapāṃ sukham ||29||
