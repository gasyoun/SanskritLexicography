Mahābhārata 6.15 (Bhīṣmaparvan)

1sañjaya uvāca|
tvadyukto'yamanupraśno mahārāja yathārhasi
|
na tu duryodhane doṣamimamāsaṅktumarhasi ||1||

2ya ātmano duścaritādaśubhaṃ prāpnuyānnaraḥ
|
enasā tena nānyaṃ sa upāśaṅkitumarhati ||2||

3mahārāja manuṣyeṣu nindyaṃ yaḥ sarvamācaret
|
sa vadhyaḥ sarvalokasya ninditāni samācaran ||3||

4nikāro nikṛtiprajñaiḥ pāṇḍavaistvatpratīkṣayā
|
anubhūtaḥ sahāmātyaiḥ kṣāntaśca suciraṃ vane ||4||

5hayānāṃ ca gajānāṃ ca rājñāṃ cāmitatejasām
|
pratyakṣaṃ yanmayā dṛṣṭaṃ dṛṣṭaṃ yogabalena ca ||5||

6śṛṇu tatpṛthivīpāla mā ca śoke manaḥ kṛthāḥ
|
diṣṭametatpurā nūnamidameva narādhipa ||6||

7namaskṛtvā pituste'haṃ pārāśaryāya dhīmate
|
yasya prasādāddivyaṃ tatprāptaṃ jñānamanuttamam ||7||

8dṛṣṭiścātīndriyā rājandūrācchravaṇameva ca
|
paracittasya vijñānamatītānāgatasya ca ||8||

9vyutthitotpattivijñānamākāśe ca gatiḥ śubhā
|
astrairasaṅgo yuddheṣu varadānānmahātmanaḥ ||9||

10śrṛṇu me vistareṇedaṃ vicitraṃ paramādbhutam
|
bharatānāmabhūdyuddhaṃ yathā tallomaharṣaṇam ||10||

11teṣvanīkeṣu yatteṣu vyūḍheṣu ca vidhānataḥ
|
duryodhano mahārāja duḥśāsanamathābravīt ||11||

12duḥśāsana rathāstūrṇaṃ yujyantāṃ bhīṣmarakṣiṇaḥ
|
anīkāni ca sarvāṇi śīghraṃ tvamanucodaya ||12||

13ayaṃ sa māmabhiprāpto varṣapūgābhicintitaḥ
|
pāṇḍavānāṃ sasainyānāṃ kurūṇāṃ ca samāgamaḥ ||13||

14nātaḥ kāryatamaṃ manye raṇe bhīṣmasya rakṣaṇāt
|
hanyudgupto hyasau pārthānsomakāṃśca sasṛñjayān ||14||

15abravīcca viśuddhātmā nāhaṃ hanyāṃ śikhaṇḍinām
|
śrūyate strī hyasau pūrvaṃ tasmādvarjyo raṇe mama ||15||

16tasmādbhīṣmo rakṣitavyo viśeṣeṇeti me matiḥ
|
śikhaṇḍino vadhe yattāḥ sarve tiṣṭhantu māmakāḥ ||16||

17tathā prācyāḥ pratīcyāśca dākṣiṇātyottarāpathāḥ
|
sarvathā'streṣu kuśalāste rakṣantu pitāmaham ||17||

18arakṣyamāṇaṃ hi vṛko hanyātsiṃhaṃ mahābalam
|
mā siṃhaṃ jambukeneva ghātayāmaḥ śikhaṇḍinā ||18||

19vāmaṃ cakraṃ yudhāmanyuruttamaujāśca dakṣiṇam
|
goptārau phālgunaṃ prāptau phālgunopi śikhaṇḍinaḥ ||19||

20saṃrakṣyamāṇaḥ pārthena bhīṣmeṇa ca vivarjitaḥ
|
yathā na hanyādgāṅgeyaṃ duḥśāsana tathā kuru ||20||
