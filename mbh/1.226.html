Mahābhārata 1.226 (Ādiparvan)

1vaiśaṃpāyana uvāca|
tau rathābhyāṃ rathaśreṣṭhau dāvasyobhayataḥ sthitau
|
dikṣu sarvāsu bhūtānāṃ cakrāte kadanaṃ mahat||1||

2yatra yatra ca dṛśyante prāṇinaḥ khāṇḍavālayāḥ
|
palāyantaḥ pravīrau tau tatra tatrābhyadhāvatām||2||

3chidraṃ na sma prapaśyanti rathayorāśucāriṇoḥ
|
āviddhāveva dṛśyete rathinau tau rathottamau||3||

4khāṇḍave dahyamāne tu bhūtā: śatasahasraśaḥ
|
utpeturbhairavānnādānvinadantaḥ samantataḥ||4||

5dagdhaikadeśā bahavo niṣṭaptāśca tathā'pare
|
sphuṭitākṣā viśīrṇāśca viplutāśca tathā'pare||5||

6samāliṅgya sutānanye pitṝnbhrātṝnathā'pare
|
tyaktuṃ na śekuḥ snehena tatraiva nidhanaṃ gatāḥ||6||

7sandaṣṭadaśanāścānye samutpeturanekaśaḥ
|
tataste'tīva ghūrṇantaḥ punaragnau prapedire||7||

8dagdhapakṣākṣicaraṇā viceṣṭanto mahītale
|
tatra tatra sma dṛśyante vinaśyantaḥ śarīriṇaḥ||8||

9jalāśayeṣu tapteṣu kvāthyamāneṣu vahninā
|
gatasatvāḥ sma dṛśyante kūrmamatsyāḥ samantataḥ||9||

10śarīrairapare dīptairdehavanta ivāgnayaḥ
|
adṛśyanta vane tatra prāṇinaḥ prāṇisaṃkṣaye||10||

11kāṃścidutpatataḥ pārthaḥ śaraiḥ saṃcchidya khaṇḍaśaḥ
|
pātayāmāsa vihagānpradīpte vasuretasi||11||

12te śarācitasarvāṅgā ninadanto mahāravān
|
ūrdhvamutpatya vegena nipetuḥ khāṇḍave punaḥ||12||

13śarairabhyāhatānāṃ ca saṅghaśaḥ sma vanaukasām
|
virāvaḥ śuśruve ghoraḥ samudrasyeva mathyataḥ||13||

14vahneścāpi pradīptasya khamutpeturmahārciṣaḥ
|
janayāmāsurudvegaṃ sumahāntaṃ divaukasām||14||

15tenārciṣā susantaptā devāḥ sarṣipurogamāḥ
|
tato jagmurmahātmānaḥ sarva eva divaukasaḥ
|
śatakratuṃ sahasrākṣaṃ deveśamasurārdanam||15||

16devā ūcuḥ|
kiṃ nvime mānavāḥ sarve dahyante citrabhānunā
|
kaccinna saṃkṣayaḥ prāpto lokānāmamareśvara||16||

17vaiśaṃpāyana uvāca|
tacchrutvā vṛtrahā tebhyaḥ svayamevānvavekṣya ca
|
khāṇḍavasya vimokṣārthaṃ prayayau harivāhanaḥ||17||

18mahatā rathavṛndena nānārūpeṇa vāsavaḥ
|
ākāśaṃ samavākīrya pravavarṣa sureśvaraḥ||18||

19tato'kṣamātrā vyasṛjandhārāḥ śatasahasraśaḥ
|
coditā devarājena jaladāḥ khāṇḍavaṃ prati||19||

20asaṃprāptāstu tā dhārāstejasā jātavedasaḥ
|
kha eva samaśuṣyanta na kāścitpāvakaṃ gatāḥ||20||

21tato namucihā kruddho bhṛśamarciṣmatastadā
|
punareva mahāmeghairambhāṃsi vyasṛjadbahu||21||

22arcirdhārābhisaṃbaddhaṃ dhūmavidyutsamākulam
|
babhūva tadvanaṃ ghoraṃ stanayitnusamākulam||22||
