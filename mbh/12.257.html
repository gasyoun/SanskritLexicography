Mahābhārata 12.257 (Śāntiparvan)

1sthāṇuruvāca |
pajāsarganimittaṃ me kāryavattāmimāṃ prabho
|
viddhi sṛṣṭāstvayā hīmā mā kupyāsāṃ pitāmaha ||1||

2tava tejo'gninā deva prajā dahyanti sarvaśaḥ
|
tā dṛṣṭvā mama kāruṇyaṃ mā kuṣyāsāṃ jagatprabho ||2||

3prajāpatiruvāca |
na kupye na ca me kāmo na bhaveyuḥ prajā iti
|
lāghavārthaṃ dharaṇyāstu tataḥ saṃhāra iṣyate ||3||

4iyaṃ hi māṃ sadā devī bhārārtā samacodayat
|
saṃhārārthaṃ mahādeva bhāreṇāpsu nimajjati ||4||

5yadāhaṃ nādhigacchāmi buddhyā bahu vicārayan
|
saṃhāramāsāṃ vṛddhānāṃ tato māṃ krodha āviśat ||5||

6sthāṇuruvāca |
saṃhārārthaṃ prasīdasva mā krudho vibudheśvara
|
mā prajāḥ sthāvaraṃ caiva jaṅgamaṃ ca vyanīnaśat ||6||

7palvalāni ca sarvāṇi sarvaṃ caiva tṛṇopalam
|
sthāvaraṃ jaṅgamaṃ caiva bhūtagrāmaṃ caturvidham ||7||

8tadetadbhasmasādbhūtaṃ jagatsarvamupallutam
|
prasīda bhagavansādho vara eṣa vṛto mayā ||8||

9naṣṭā na punareṣyanti prajā hyetāḥ kathaṃcana
|
tasmānnivartatāmetattena svenaiva tejasā ||9||

10upāyamanyaṃ sampaśya bhūtānāṃ hitakāmyayā
|
yathā'mī jantavaḥ sarve na dahyeranpitāmaha ||10||

11abhāvaṃ hi na gaccheyurucchinnaprajanāḥ prajāḥ
|
adhidaive niyukto'smi tvayā lokeśvareśvara ||11||

12tvadbhavaṃ hi jagannātha etatsthāvarajaṅgamam
|
prasādya tvāṃ mahādeva yācāmyāvṛttijāḥ prajāḥ ||12||

13nārada uvāca|
śrutvā tu vacanaṃ devaḥ sthāṇorniyatavāṅmanāḥ
|
tejastatsannijagrāha punarevāntarātmani ||13||

14tato'gnimupasaṃgṛhya bhagavā~llokapūjitaḥ
|
pravṛttiṃ ca nivṛttiṃ ca kalpayāmāsa vai prabhuḥ ||14||

15upasaṃharatastasya tamagniṃ roṣajaṃ tadā
|
prādurbabhūva viśvebhyaḥ khebhyo nārī mahātmanaḥ ||15||

16kṛṣṇaraktāmbaradharā kṛṣṇanetratalāntarā
|
divyakuṇḍalasampannā divyābharaṇabhūṣitā ||16||

17sā viniḥsṛtya vai khebhyo dakṣiṇāmāśritā diśam
|
dadṛśāte ca tāṃ kanyāṃ devau viśveśvarāvubhau ||17||

18tāmāhūya tadā devo lokānāmādirīśvaraḥ
|
mṛtyo iti mahīpāla jahi cemāḥ prajā iti ||18||

19tvaṃ hi saṃhārabuddhyā me cintitā ruṣitena ca
|
tasmātsaṃhara sarvāṃstvaṃ prajāḥ sajaḍapaṇḍitāḥ ||19||

20aviśeṣeṇa caiva tvaṃ prajāḥ saṃhara kāmini
|
mama tvaṃ hi niyogena śreyaḥ paramavāpsyasi ||20||

21evamuktā tu sā devī mṛtyuḥ kamalamālinī
|
pradadhyau duḥkhitā bālā sāśrupātamatīva ca||21||

22pāṇibhyāṃ caiva jagrāha tānyaśrūṇi janeśvaraḥ
|
mānavānāṃ hitārthāya yayāce punareva ha ||22||
