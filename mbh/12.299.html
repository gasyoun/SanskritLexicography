Mahābhārata 12.299 (Śāntiparvan)

1yudhiṣṭhira uvāca|
satyaṃ damaṃ kṣamā prajñā praśaṃsanti pitāmaha
|
vidvāṃso manujā loke kathametanmataṃ tava ||1||

2bhīṣma uvāca|
atra te vartayiṣye'hamitihāsaṃ purātanam
|
sādhyānāmiha saṃvādaṃ haṃsasya ca yudhiṣṭhira ||2||

3haṃso bhūtvā'tha sauvarṇastvajo nityaḥ prajāpatiḥ
|
sa vai paryeti lokāṃstrīnatha sādhyānupāgamat ||3||

4sādhyā ūcuḥ| śakune vayaṃ sma devā vai sādhyāstvāmanuyuṃkṣmahe
|
pṛcchāmastvāṃ mokṣadharmaṃ bhavāṃśca kila mokṣavit ||4||

5śruto'si naḥ paṇḍito dhīravādī sādhuśabdaścarate te patatrin
|
kiṃ manyase śreṣṭhatamaṃ dvija tvaṃ kasminmanaste ramate mahātman ||5||

6tannaḥ kāryaṃ pakṣivara praśādhi yatkāryāṇāṃ manyase śreṣṭhamekam
|
yatkṛtvā vai puruṣaḥ sarvabandhairvimucyate vihagendreha śīghram ||6||

7haṃsa uvāca |
idaṃ kāryamamṛtāśāḥ śṛṇomi tapo damaḥ satyamātmābhiguptiḥ
|
granthīnvimucya hṛdayasya sarvān priyāpriye svaṃ vaśamānayīta ||7||

8nāruntudaḥ syānna nṛśaṃsavādī na hīnataḥ paramabhyādadīta
|
yayā'sya vācā para udvijeta na tāṃ vadeduṣatīṃ pāpalokyām ||8||

9vāksāyakā vadanānniṣpatanti yairāhataḥ śocati rātryahāni
|
parasya nāmarmasu te patanti tānpaṇḍito nāvasṛjetpareṣu ||9||

10paraścedenamativādabāṇairbhṛśaṃ vidhyecchama eveha kāryaḥ
|
saṃroṣyamāṇaḥ pratihaṣyate yaḥ sa ādatte sukṛtaṃ vai parasya ||10||

11kṣepāyamāṇamabhiṣaṅgavyalīkaṃ nigṛhṇāti jvalitaṃ yaśca manyum
|
adṛṣṭacetā mudito'nasūyuḥ sa ādatte sukṛtaṃ vai pareṣām ||11||

12ākruśyamāno na vadāmi kiṃcit kṣamāmyahaṃ tāḍyamānaśca nityam
|
śreṣṭhaṃ hyetadyatkṣamamāhurāryāḥ satyaṃ tathaivārjavamānṛśaṃsyam ||12||

13vedasyopaniṣatsatyaṃ satyasyopaniṣaddamaḥ
|
damasyopaniṣanmokṣa etatsarvānuśāsanam ||13||

14vāco vegaṃ manasaḥ krodhavegaṃ vidhitsāvegamudaropasthavegam
|
etānvegānyo viṣahedudīrṇāṃstaṃ manye'haṃ brāhmaṇaṃ vai muniṃ ca ||14||

15akrodhanaḥ krudhyatāṃ vai viśiṣṭastathā titikṣuratitikṣorviśiṣṭaḥ
|
amānuṣānmānuṣo vai viśiṣṭastathā'jñānājjñānavidvai viśiṣṭaḥ ||15||

16ākruśyamāno nākruśyenmanyurenaṃ titikṣataḥ
|
ākroṣṭāraṃ nirdahati sukṛtaṃ cāsya vindati ||16||

17yo nātyuktaḥ prāha rūkṣaṃ priyaṃ vā yo vā hato na pratihanti dhairyāt
|
pāpaṃ ca yo necchati tasya hantustasyeha devāḥ spṛhayanti nityam ||17||

18pāpīyasaḥ kṣametaiva śreyasaḥ sadṛśasya ca
|
vimānito hatotkruṣṭa evaṃ siddhiṃ gamiṣyati ||18||

19sadā'hamāryānnibhṛto'pyupāse na me vidhitsotsahate na roṣaḥ
|
na vāpyahaṃ lipsamānaḥ paraimi na caiva kiñcidviṣayeṇa yāmi ||19||

20nāhaṃ śaptaḥ pratiśapāmi kaṃciddamaṃ dvāraṃ hyamṛtasyeha vedmi
|
guhyaṃ brahma tadidaṃ bravīmi na mānuṣācchreṣṭhataraṃ hi kiṃcit ||20||

21nirmucyamānaḥ pāpebhyo ghanebhya iva candramāḥ
|
virajāḥ kālamākāṅkṣandhīro dhairyeṇa siddhyati ||21||

22yaḥ sarveṣāṃ bhavati hyarcanīya utsedhanastambha ivābhijātaḥ
|
yasmai vācaṃ suprasannāṃ vadanti sa vai devāngacchati saṃyatātmā ||22||

23na tathā vaktumicchanti kalyāṇānpuruṣe guṇān
|
yathaiṣāṃ vaktamicchanti nairguṇyamanuyuñjakāḥ ||23||

24yasya vāṅmanasī gupte samyakpraṇihite sadā
|
vedāstapaśca tyāgaśca sa idaṃ sarvamāpnuyāt ||24||

25ākrośanavimānābhyāṃ nābudhānbodhayedbudhaḥ
|
tasmānna vardhayedanyaṃ na cātmānaṃ vihiṃsayet ||25||

26amṛtasyeva saṃtṛpyadevamānasya paṇḍitaḥ
|
sukhaṃ hyavamataḥ śete yo'vamantā sa naśyati ||26||

27yatkrodhano yajati yaddadāti yadvā tapastapyati yajjuhoti
|
vaivasvatastaddharate'sya sarvaṃ moghaḥ śramo bhavati hi krodhanasya ||27||

28catvāri yasya dvārāṇi suguptānyamarottamāḥ
|
upasthamudaraṃ hastau vākcaturthī sa dharmavit ||28||

29satyaṃ damaṃ hyārjavamānṛśaṃsyaṃ dhṛtiṃ titikṣāmatisevamānaḥ
|
svādhyāyanityo'spṛhayanpareṣāmekāntaśīlyūrdhvagatirbhavetsaḥ ||29||

30sarvāṃścainānanucaranvatsavaccaturaḥ stanān
|
na pāvanatamaṃ kiṃcitsatyādadhyagamaṃ kvacit ||30||

31ācakṣe'haṃ manuṣyebhyo devebhyaḥ pratisaṃcaran
|
satyaṃ svargasya sopānaṃ pārāvārasya nauriva ||31||

32yādṛśaiḥ sannivasati yādṛśāṃścopasevate
|
yādṛgicchecca bhavituṃ tādṛgbhavati pūruṣaḥ ||32||

33yadi santaṃ sevati yadyasantaṃ tapasvinaṃ yadi vā stenameva
|
vāso yathā raṃgavaśaṃ prayāti tathā sa teṣāṃ vaśamabhyupaiti ||33||

34sadā devāḥ sādhubhiḥ saṃvadante na mānuṣaṃ viṣayaṃ yānti draṣṭum
|
nenduḥ samaḥ syādasamo hi vāyuruccāvacaṃ viṣayaṃ yaḥ sa veda ||34||

35aduṣṭaṃ vartamāne tu hṛdayāntarapūruṣe
|
tenaiva devāḥ prīyante satāṃ mārgasthitena vai ||35||

36śiśnodare ye niratāḥ sadaiva stenā narā vākparuṣāśca nityam
|
apetadoṣānapi tānviditvā dūrāddevāḥ samparivarjayanti ||36||

37na vai devā hīnasattvena toṣyāḥ sarvāśinā duṣkṛtakarmaṇā vā
|
satyavratā ye tu narāḥ kṛtajñā dharme ratāstaiḥ saha sambhajante ||37||

38avyāhṛtaṃ vyāhṛtācchreya āhuḥ satyaṃ vadedvyāhṛtaṃ taddvitīyam
|
dharmaṃ vadedvyāhṛtaṃ tattṛtīyaṃ priyaṃ vadedvyāhṛtaṃ taccaturtham ||38||

39sādhyā ūcuḥ| kenāyamāvṛto lokaḥ kena vā na prakāśate
|
kena tyajati mitrāṇi kena svargaṃ na gacchati ||39||

40haṃsa uvāca |
ajñānenāvṛto loko mātsaryānna prakāśate
|
lobhāttyajati mitrāṇi saṃgātsvargaṃ na gacchati ||40||

41sādhyā ūcuḥ|
kaḥ svideko ramate brāhmaṇānāṃ kaḥ svideko bahubhirjoṣamāste
|
kaḥ svideko balavāndurbalo'pi kaḥ svideṣāṃ kalahaṃ nānvavaiti ||41||

42haṃsa uvāca |
prājña eko ramate brāhmaṇānāṃ prājñaścaiko bahubhirjoṣamāste
|
prājña eko balavāndurbalo'pi prājña eṣāṃ kalahaṃ nānvavaiti ||42||

43sādhyā ūcuḥ|
kiṃ brāhmaṇānāṃ devatvaṃ kiṃ ca sādhutvamucyate
|
asādhutvaṃ ca kiṃ teṣāṃ kimeṣāṃ mānuṣaṃ matam ||43||

44haṃsa uvāca |
svādhyāya eṣāṃ devatvaṃ vrataṃ sādhutvamucyate
|
asādhutvaṃ parīvādo mṛtyurmānuṣyamucyate ||44||

45bhīṣma uvāca|
saṃvāda ityayaṃ śreṣṭhaḥ sādhyānāṃ parikīrtitaḥ
|
kṣetraṃ vai karmaṇāṃ yoniḥ sadbhāvaḥ satyamucyate ||45||
