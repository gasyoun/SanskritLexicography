Mahābhārata 12.305 (Śāntiparvan)

1janaka uvāca |
akṣarakṣarayoreṣa dvayoḥ sambandha iṣyate
|
strīpuṃsorvāpi bhagavansambandhastadvaducyate||1||

2ṛte tu puruṣaṃ neha strī garbhaṃ dhārayatyuta
|
ṛte striyaṃ na puruṣo rūpaṃ nivartayettathā||2||

3anyonyasyābhisambandhādanyonyaguṇasaṃśrayāt
|
rūpaṃ nirvartayatyetadevaṃ sarvāsu yoniṣu||3||

4ratyarthamabhisambandhādanyonyaguṇasaṃśrayāt
|
ṛtau nirvartyate rūpaṃ tadvakṣyāmi nidarśanam||4||

5ye guṇāḥ puruṣasyeha ye ca mātṛguṇāstathā
|
asthi snāyuśca majjā ca jānīma: pitṛto guṇāḥ||5||

6tvaṅmāṃsaṃ śoṇitaṃ ceti mātṛjānyapi śuśruma
|
evametaddvijaśreṣṭha vede śāstre ca paṭhyate||6||

7pramāṇaṃ yatsvavedoktaṃ śāstroktaṃ yacca paṭhyate
|
vedaśāstradvayaṃ caiva pramāṇaṃ tatsanātanam||7||

8anyonyaguṇasaṃrodhādanyonyaguṇasaṃśrayāt
|
evamevābhisambaddhau nityaṃ prakṛtipūruṣau||8||

9paśyāmi bhagavaṃstasmānmokṣadharmo na vidyate
|
athavā'nantarakṛtaṃ kiñcideva nidarśanam
|
tanmamācakṣva tattvena pratyakṣo hyasi sarvadā||9||

10mokṣakāmā vayaṃ cāpi kāṅkṣāmo yadanāmayam
|
adehamajaraṃ nityamatīndriyamanīśvaram||10||

11vasiṣṭha uvāca |
yadetaduktaṃ bhavatā vedaśāstranidarśanam
|
evametadyathā caitannigṛhṇāti tathā bhavān||11||

12dhāryate hi tvayā grantha ubhayorvedaśāstrayoḥ
|
na ca granthasya tattvajño yathātattvaṃ nareśvara||12||

13yo hi vede ca śāstre ca granthadhāraṇatatparaḥ
|
na ca granthārthatattvajñastasya taddhāraṇaṃ vṛthā||13||

14bhāraṃ sa vahate tasya granthasyārthaṃ na vetti yaḥ
|
yastu granthārthatattvajño nāsya granthāgamo vṛthā||14||

15granthasyārthasya pṛṣṭaḥ saṃstādṛśo vaktumarhati
|
yathā tattvābhigamanādarthaṃ tasya sa vindati||15||

16na yaḥ saṃsatsu kathayedgranthārthaṃ sthūlabuddhimān
|
sa kathaṃ mandavijñāno granthaṃ vakṣyati nirṇayāt||16||

17nirṇayaṃ cāpi chidrātmā na taṃ vakṣyati tattvataḥ
|
sopahāsātmatāmeti yasmāccaivātmavānapi||17||

18tasmāttvaṃ śṛṇu rājendra yathaitadanudṛśyate
|
yāthātathye sāṃkhyeṣu yogeṣu ca mahātmasu||18||

19yadeva yogāḥ paśyanti sāṃkhyaistadanugamyate
|
ekaṃ sāṃkhyaṃ ca yogaṃ ca yaḥ paśyati sa buddhimān||19||

20tvaṅmāṃsaṃ rudhiraṃ medaḥ pittaṃ majjā ca snāyu ca
|
atha caindriyakaṃ tāta tadbhavānidamāha mām||20||

21dravyāddravyasya nirvṛttirindriyādindriyaṃ tathā
|
dehāddehamavāpnoti bījādbījaṃ tathaiva ca||21||

22nirindriyasyābījasya nirdravyasyāpyadehinaḥ
|
kathaṃ guṇā bhaviṣyanti nirguṇatvānmahātmanaḥ||22||

23guṇā guṇeṣu jāyante tatraiva niviśanti ca
|
evaṃ guṇā: prakṛtito jāyante niviśanti ca||23||

24tvaṅmāṃsaṃ rudhiraṃ medaḥ pittaṃ majjāsthi snāyu ca
|
aṣṭau tānyatha śukreṇa jānīhi prākṛtāni vai||24||

25pumāṃścaivāpumāṃścaiva trailiṅgyaṃ prākṛtaṃ smṛtam
|
na vāpumānpumāṃścaiva sa liṅgītyabhidhīyate||25||

26aliṅgātprakṛtirliṅgairupālabhyati sātmajaiḥ
|
yathā puṣpaphalairnityamṛtavo mūrtayastathā||26||

27evamapyanumānena hyaliṅgamupalabhyate
|
pañcaviṃśatimastāta liṅgeṣu niyatātmakaḥ||27||

28anādinidhano'nantaḥ sarvadarśī nirāmayaḥ
|
kevalaṃ tvabhimānitvādguṇeṣu guṇa ucyate||28||

29guṇā guṇavataḥ santi nirguṇasya kuto guṇāḥ
|
tasmādevaṃ vijānanti ye janā guṇadarśinaḥ||29||

30yadā tveṣa guṇānetānprākṛtānabhimanyate
|
tadā sa guṇahānyai taṃ paramevānupaśyati||30||

31yattadbuddheḥ paraṃ prāhuḥ sāṃkhyāyogāśca sarvaśaḥ
|
buddhyamānaṃ mahāprājñamabuddhaparivarjanāt||31||

32aprabuddhamathāvyaktamaguṇaṃ prāhurīśvaram
|
nirguṇaṃ ceśvaraṃ nityamadhiṣṭhātārameva ca||32||

33prakṛteśca guṇānāṃ ca pañcaviṃśatikaṃ budhāḥ
|
sāṃkhyayoge ca kuśalā budhyante paramaiṣiṇaḥ||33||

34yadā prabuddhā hyavyaktamavasthājanmabhīravaḥ
|
budhyamānaṃ prabudhyanti gamayanti samaṃ tadā||34||

35etannidarśanaṃ samyagasamyaganidarśanam
|
budhyamānā prabuddhānāṃ pṛthapṛthagarindam||35||

36paraspareṇaitaduktaṃ kṣarākṣaranidarśanam
|
ekatvamakṣaraṃ prāhurnānātvaṃ kṣaramucyate||36||

37pañcaviṃśatiniṣṭho'yaṃ yadā samyak pravartate
|
ekatvaṃ darśanaṃ cāsya nānātvaṃ cāpyadarśanam||37||

38tattvanistattvayoretatpṛthageva nidarśanam
|
pañcaviṃśatisargaṃ tu tattvamāhurmanīṣiṇaḥ||38||

39nistattvaṃ pañcaviṃśasya paramāhurnidarśanam
|
sargasya vargamācāraṃ tattvaṃ tattvātsanātanam||39||
