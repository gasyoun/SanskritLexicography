Mahābhārata 12.146 (Śāntiparvan)

1bhīṣma uvāca|
sa palyā vacanaṃ śrutvā dharmayuktisamanvitam
|
harṣeṇa mahatā yukto vākyaṃ vyākulalocanaḥ ||1||

2taṃ vai śākunikaṃ dṛṣṭvā vidhidṛṣṭena karmaṇā
|
sa pakṣī pūjayāmāsa yatnāttaṃ pakṣijīvinam ||2||

3uvāca svāgataṃ te'dya brūhi kiṃ karavāṇi te
|
saṃtāpaśca na kartavyaḥ svagṛhe vartate bhavān ||3||

4tadbravītu bhavān kṣipraṃ kiṃ karomi kimicchasi
|
praṇayena bravīmi tvāṃ tvaṃ hi naḥ śaraṇāgataḥ ||4||

5arāvapyucitaṃ kāryamātithyaṃ gṛhamāgate
|
chettumapyāgate chāyāṃ nopasaṃharate drumaḥ ||5||

6śaraṇāgatasya kartavyamātithyaṃ hi prayatnataḥ
|
pañcayajñapravṛttena gṛhasthena viśeṣataḥ ||6||

7pañcayajñāṃstu yo mohānna karoti gṛhāśrame
|
tasya nāyaṃ na ca paro loko bhavati dharmataḥ ||7||

8tadbrūhi māṃ suviśrabdho yattvaṃ vācā vadiṣyasi
|
tatkariṣyāmyahaṃ sarvaṃ mā tvaṃ śoke manaḥ kṛthāḥ ||8||

9tasya tadvacanaṃ śrutvā śakunerlubdhako'bravīt
|
bādhate khalu me śītaṃ saṃtrāṇaṃ hi vidhīyatām ||9||

10evamuktastataḥ pakṣī parṇānyāstīrya bhūtale
|
yathāśaktyā hi parṇena jvalanārthaṃ drutaṃ yayau ||10||

11sa gatvā'ṅgārakarmāntaṃ gṛhītvāgnimathāgamat
|
tataḥ śuṣkeṣu parṇeṣu pāvakaṃ so'pyadīpayat ||11||

12sa sandīptaṃ mahatkṛtvā tamāha śaraṇāgatam
|
pratāpaya suviśrabdhaḥ svagātrāṇyakutobhayaḥ ||12||

13sa tathoktastathetyuktvā lubdho gātrāṇyatāpayat
|
agniṃ pratyāgataprāṇastataḥ prāha vihaṅgamam ||13||

14harṣeṇa mahatā''viṣṭo vākyaṃ vyākulalocanaḥ
|
tathemaṃ śakuniṃ dṛṣṭvā vidhidṛṣṭena karmaṇā ||14||

15dattamāhāramicchāmi tvayā kṣudbādhate hi mām
|
sa tadvacaḥ pratiśrutya vākyamāha vihaṅgamaḥ ||15||

16na me'sti vibhavo yena nāśayeyaṃ kṣudhāṃ tava
|
utpannena hi jīvāmo vayaṃ nityaṃ vanaukasaḥ ||16||

17saṃcayo nāsti cāsmākaṃ munīnāmiva bhojane
|
ityuktvā taṃ tadā tatra vivarṇavadano'bhavat ||17||

18kathaṃ na khalu kartavyamiti cintāparastadā
|
babhūva bharataśreṣṭha garhayan vṛttimātmanaḥ ||18||

19muhūrtāllabdhasaṃjñastu sa pakṣī pakṣighātinam
|
uvāca tarpayiṣye tvāṃ muhūrtaṃ pratipālaya ||19||

20ityuktvā śuṣkaparṇaistu samujjvālya hutāśanam
|
harṣeṇa mahatā''viṣṭaḥ sa pakṣī vākyamabravīt ||20||

21ṛṣīṇāṃ devatānāṃ ca pitṝṇāṃ ca mahātmanām
|
śrutaḥ pūrvaṃ mayā dharmo mahānatithipūjane ||21||

22kuruṣvānugrahaṃ saumya satyametadbravīmi te
|
niścitā khalu me buddhiratithipratipūjane ||22||

23tataḥ kṛtapratijño vai sa pakṣī prahasanniva
|
tamagniṃ triḥparikramya praviveśa mahāmatiḥ ||23||

24agnimadhye praviṣṭaṃ tu lubdho dṛṣṭvā tu pakṣiṇam
|
cintayāmāsa manasā kimidaṃ ve mayā kṛtam ||24||

25aho mama nṛśaṃsasya garhitasya svakarmaṇā
|
adharmaḥ sumahān ghoro bhaviṣyati na saṃśayaḥ ||25||

26evaṃ bahuvidhaṃ bhūri vilalāpa sa lubdhakaḥ
|
gārhayansvāni karmāṇi dvijaṃ dṛṣṭvā tathāgatam ||26||
