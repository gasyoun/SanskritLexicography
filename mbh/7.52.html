Mahābhārata 7.52 (Droṇaparvan)

1sañjaya uvāca|
athainaṃ vilapantaṃ taṃ kuntīputraṃ yudhiṣṭhiram
|
kṛṣṇadvaipāyanastatra ājagāma mahānṛṣiḥ ||1||

2arcayitvā yathānyāyamupaviṣṭaṃ yudhiṣṭhiraḥ
|
abravīcchokasantapto bhrātuḥ putravadhena ca ||2||

3adharmayuktairbahubhiḥ parivārya mahārathaiḥ
|
yudhyamāno maheṣvāsaiḥ saubhadro nihato raṇe ||3||

4bālaśca bālabuddhiśca saubhadraḥ paravīrahā
|
anupāyena saṅgrāme yudhyamāno viśeṣataḥ ||4||

5mayā proktaḥ sa saṅgrāme dvāraṃ sañjanayasva naḥ
|
praviṣṭe'bhyantare tasmin saindhavena nivāritāḥ ||5||

6nanu nāma samaṃ yuddhameṣṭavyaṃ yuddhajīvibhiḥ
|
idaṃ caivāsamaṃ yuddhamīdṛśaṃ yat kṛtaṃ paraiḥ ||6||

7tenāsmi bhṛśasantaptaḥ śokabāṣpasamākulaḥ
|
śamaṃ naivādhigacchāmi cintayānaḥ punaḥ punaḥ ||7||

8sañjaya uvāca|
taṃ tathā vilapantaṃ vai śokavyākulamānasam
|
uvāca bhagavān vyāso yudhiṣṭhiramidaṃ vacaḥ ||8||

9vyāsa uvāca| yudhiṣṭhira mahāprājña sarvaśāstraviśārada
|
vyasaneṣu na muhyanti tvādṛśā bharatarṣabha ||9||

10svargameṣa gataḥ śūraḥ śatrūn hatvā bahūn raṇe
|
abālasadṛśaṃ karma kṛtvā vai puruṣottamaḥ ||10||

11anatikramaṇīyo vai vidhireṣa yudhiṣṭhira
|
devadānavagandharvān mṛtyurharati bhārata ||11||

12yudhiṣṭhira uvāca| ime vai pṛthivīpālāḥ śerate pṛthivītale
|
nihatāḥ pṛtanāmadhye mṛtasaṃjñā mahābalāḥ ||12||

13nāgāyutabālāścānye vāyuvegabalāstathā
|
ta ete nihatāḥ saṅkhye tulyarūpā narairnarāḥ ||13||

14naiṣāṃ paśyāmi hantāraṃ prāṇināṃ saṃyuge kvacit
|
vikrameṇopasampannāstapobalasamanvitāḥ ||14||

15jetavyamiti cānyonyaṃ yeṣāṃ nityaṃ hṛdi sthitam
|
atha ceme hatāḥ prājñāḥ śerate vigatāyuṣaḥ ||15||

16mṛtā iti ca śabdo'yaṃ vartate ca tato'rthavat
|
ime mṛtā mahīpālāḥ prāyaśo bhīmavikramāḥ ||16||

17niśceṣṭā nirabhīmānāḥ śūrāḥ śatruvaśaṅgatāḥ
|
rājaputrāśca saṃrabdhā vaiśvānaramukhaṃ gatāḥ ||17||

18atra me saṃśayaḥ prāptaḥ kutaḥ saṃjñā mṛtā iti
|
kasya mṛtyuḥ kuto mṛtyuḥ kathaṃ saṃharate prajāḥ||18||

19haratyamarasaṅkāśa tanme brūhi pitāmaha
|
sañjaya uvāca|
taṃ tathā paripṛcchantaṃ kuntīputraṃ yudhiṣṭhiram
|
āśvāsanamidaṃ vākyamuvāca bhagavānṛṣiḥ ||19||

20vyāsa uvāca| atrāpyudāharantīmamitihāsaṃ purātanam
|
akampanasya kathitaṃ nāradena purā nṛpa ||20||

21sa cāpi rājā rājendra putravyasanamuttamam
|
aprasahyatamaṃ loke prāptavāniti me matiḥ ||21||

22tadahaṃ sampravakṣyāmi mṛtyoḥ prabhavamuttamam
|
tatastvaṃ mokṣyase duḥkhāt snehabandhanasaṃśrayāt ||22||

23samastapāparāśighnaṃ śṛṇu kīrtayato mama
|
dhanyamākhyānamāyuṣyaṃ śokaghnaṃ puṣṭivardhanam ||23||

24pavitramarisaṅghaghnaṃ maṅgalānāṃ ca maṅgalam
|
yathaiva vedādhyayanamupākhyānamidaṃ tathā ||24||

25śravaṇīyaṃ mahārāja prātarnityaṃ nṛpottamaiḥ
|
putrānāyuṣmato rājyamīhamānaiḥ śriyaṃ tathā ||25||

26purā kṛtayuge tāta āsīdrājā hyakampanaḥ
|
sa śatruvaśamāpanno madhye saṅgrāmamūrdhani ||26||

27tasya putro harirnāma nārāyaṇasamo bale
|
śrīmān kṛtāstro medhāvī yudhi śakropamo balī ||27||

28sa śatrubhiḥ parivṛto bahudhā raṇamūrdhani
|
vyasyan bāṇasahasrāṇi yodheṣu ca gajeṣu ca ||28||

29sa karma duṣkaraṃ kṛtvā saṅgrāme śatrutāpanaḥ
|
śatrubhirnihataḥ saṅkhye pṛtanāyāṃ yudhiṣṭhira ||29||

30sa rājā pretakṛtyāni tasya kṛtvā śucānvitaḥ
|
śocannahani rātrau ca nālabhat sukhamātmanaḥ ||30||

31tasya śokaṃ viditvā tu putravyasanasambhavam
|
ājagāmātha devarṣirnārado'sya samīpataḥ ||31||

32sa tu rājā mahābhāgo dṛṣṭvā devarṣisattamam
|
pūjayitvā yathānyāyaṃ kathāmakathayat tadā ||32||

33tasya sarvaṃ samācaṣṭa yathā vṛttaṃ nareśvaraḥ
|
śatrubhirvijayaṃ saṅkhye putrasya ca vadhaṃ tathā ||33||

34mama putro mahāvīrya indraviṣṇusamadyutiḥ
|
śatrubhirbahubhiḥ saṅkhye parākramya hato balī ||34||

35ka eṣa mṛtyurbhagavan kiṃvīryabalapauruṣaḥ
|
etadicchāmi tattvena śrotuṃ matimatāṃ vara ||35||

36tasya tadvacanaṃ śrutvā nārado varadaḥ prabhuḥ
|
ākhyānamidamācaṣṭa putraśokāpahaṃ mahat ||36||

37nārada uvāca| śṛṇu rājan mahābāho ākhyānaṃ bahuvistaram
|
yathā vṛttaṃ śrutaṃ caiva mayā'pi vasudhādhipa ||37||

38prajāḥ sṛṣṭvā tadā brahmā ādisarge pitāmahaḥ
|
asaṃhṛtaṃ mahātejā dṛṣṭvā jagadidaṃ prabhuḥ ||38||

39tasya cintā samutpannā sahāraṃ prati pārthiva
|
cintayanna hyasau veda saṃhāraṃ vasudhādhipa ||39||

40tasya roṣānmahārāja khebhyo'gnirudatiṣṭhata
|
tena sarvā diśo vyāptāḥ sāntardeśā didhakṣatā ||40||

41tato divaṃ bhuvaṃ caiva jvālāmālāsamākulam
|
carācaraṃ jagat sarvaṃ dadāha bhagavān prabhuḥ ||41||

42tato hatāni bhūtāni carāṇi sthāvarāṇi ca
|
mahatā krodhavegena trāsayanniva vīryavān ||42||

43tato rudro jaṭī sthāṇurniśācarapatirharaḥ
|
jagāma śaraṇaṃ devaṃ brahmāṇaṃ parameṣṭhinam ||43||

44tasminnāpatite sthāṇau prajānāṃ hitakāmyayā
|
abravīt paramo devo jvalanniva mahāmuniḥ ||44||

45kiṃ kurma kāmaṃ kāmārha kāmājjāto'si putraka
|
kariṣyāmi priyaṃ sarvaṃ brūhi sthāṇo yadicchasi ||45||
