Mahābhārata 15.4 (Āśramavāsikaparvan)

1vyāsa uvāca|
yudhiṣṭhira mahābāho yathāha kurunandanaḥ
|
dhṛtarāṣṭro mahātejāstatkuruṣvāvicārayan||1||

2ayaṃ hi vṛddho nṛpatirhataputro viśeṣataḥ
|
nedaṃ kṛcchre cirataraṃ sahediti patirmama||2||

3gāndhārī ca mahābhāgā prājñā karuṇavedinī
|
putraśokaṃ mahārāja dhairyeṇodvahate bhṛśam||3||

4ahamapyetadeva tvāṃ bravīmi kuru me vacaḥ
|
anujñāṃ labhatāṃ rājā mā vṛtheha mariṣyati||4||

5rājarṣīṇāṃ purāṇānāmanuyātu gatiṃ nṛpaḥ
|
rājarṣīṇāṃ hi sarveṣāmante vanamupāśrayaḥ||5||

6vaiśampāyana uvāca|
ityuktaḥ sa tadā rājā vyāsenādbhutakarmaṇā
|
pratyuvāca mahātejā dharmarājo mahāmunim||6||

7bhagavāneva no mānyo bhagavāneva no guruḥ
|
bhagavānasya rājyasya kulasya ca parāyaṇam||7||

8ahaṃ tu putro bhagavan pitā rājā guruśca me
|
nideśavartī ca pituḥ putro bhavati dharmataḥ||8||

9vaiśampāyana uvāca|
ityuktaḥ sa tu taṃ prāha vyāso vedavidāṃ varaḥ
|
yudhiṣṭhiraṃ mahātejāḥ punareva mahākaviḥ||9||

10evametanmahābāho yathā vadasi bhārata
|
rājā'yaṃ vṛddhatāṃ prāptaḥ pramāṇe parame sthitaḥ||10||

11so'yaṃ mayābhyanujñātastvayā ca pṛthivīpatiḥ
|
karotu svamabhiprāyaṃ māsya vighnakaro bhava||11||

12eṣa eva paro dharmo rājarṣīṇāṃ yudhiṣṭhira
|
samare vā bhavenmṛtyurvane vā vidhipūrvakam||12||

13pitrā tu tava rājendra pāṇḍunā pṛthivīkṣitā
|
śiṣyavṛttena rājāyaṃ guruvatparyupāsitaḥ||13||

14kratubhirdakṣiṇāvadbhī ratnaparvataśobhitaiḥ
|
mahadbhiriṣṭaṃ gaurbhuktā prajāśca paripālitāḥ||14||

15putrasaṃsthaṃ ca vipulaṃ rājyaṃ viproṣite tvayi
|
trayodaśasamā bhuktaṃ dattaṃ ca vividhaṃ vasu||15||

16tvayā cāyaṃ naravyāghra guruśuśrūṣayā'nagha
|
ārādhitaḥ sabhṛtyena gāndhārī ca yaśasvinī||16||

17anujānīhi pitaraṃ samayo'sya tapovidhau
|
na manyurvidyate cāsya susūkṣmo'pi yudhiṣṭhira||17||

18vaiśampāyana uvāca|
etāvaduktvā vacanamanumānya ca pārthivam
|
tathāstviti ca tenoktaḥ kaunteyena yayau vanam||18||

19gate bhagavati vyāse rājā pāṇḍusutastadā
|
provāca pitaraṃ vṛddhaṃ mandaṃ mandamivānataḥ||19||

20yadāha bhagavānvyāso yaccāpi bhavato matam
|
yathā''ha ca maheṣvāsaḥ kṛpo vidura eva ca||20||

21yuyutsuḥ sañjayaścaiva tatkartāsmyahamañjasā
|
sarva eva hi mānyā me kulasya hi hitaiṣiṇaḥ||21||

22idaṃ tu yāce nṛpate tvāmahaṃ śirasā nataḥ
|
kriyatāṃ tāvadāhārastato gacchāśramaṃ prati||22||
