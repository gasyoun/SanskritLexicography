Mahābhārata 3.236 (Āraṇyakaparvan)

1janamejaya uvāca|
evaṃ vane vartamānā narāgryāḥ śītoṣṇavātātapakarśitāṅgāḥ
|
sarastadāsādya vanaṃ ca puṇyaṃ tataḥ paraṃ kimakurvanta pārthāḥ||1||

2vaiśampāyana uvāca|
sarastadāsādya tu pāṇḍuputrā janaṃ samutsṛjya vidhāya veśam
|
vanāni ramyāṇyatha parvatāṃśca nadīpradeśāṃśca tadā viceruḥ||2||

3tathā vane tān vasataḥ pravīrān svādhyāyavantaśca tapodhanāṃśca
|
abhyāyayurvedavidaḥ purāṇāstān pūjayāmāsuratho narāgryāḥ||3||

4tataḥ kadācit kuśalaḥ kathāsu vipro'bhyagacchadbhuvi kauraveyān
|
sa taiḥ sametyātha yadṛcchayaiva vaicitravīryaṃ nṛpamabhyagacchat||4||

5athopaviṣṭaḥ pratisatkṛtaśca vṛddhena rājñā kurusattamena
|
pracoditaḥ saṅkathayāṃbabhūva dharmānilendraprabhavān yamau ca||5||

6kṛśāṃśca vātātapakarśitāṅgān duḥkhasya cograsya mukhe prapannān
|
tāṃ cāpyanāthāmiva vīranāthāṃ kṛṣṇāṃ parikleśaguṇena yuktām||6||

7tataḥ kathāstasya niśamya rājā vaicitravīryaḥ kṛpayā'bhitaptaḥ
|
vane tathā pārthiva putrapautrān śrutvā tathā duḥkhanadīṃ prapannān||7||

8provāca dainyābhihatāntarātmā niḥśvāsavātopahatastadānīm
|
vācaṃ kathañcit sthiratāmupetya tat sarvamātmaprabhavaṃ vicintya||8||

9kathaṃ nu satyaḥ śucirāryavṛtto jyeṣṭhaḥ sutānāṃ mama dharmarājaḥ
|
ajātaśatruḥ pṛthivītale sma śete purā rāṅkavakūṭaśāyī||9||

10prabodhyate māgadhasūtapūgairnityaṃ stuvadbhiḥ svayamindrakalpaḥ
|
patantrisaṅghaiḥ sa jaghanyarātre prabodhyate nūnamiḍātalasthaḥ||10||

11kathaṃ nu vātātapakarśitāṅgo vṛkodaraḥ kopapariplutāṅgaḥ
|
śete pṛthivyāmatathocitāṅgaḥ kṛṣṇāsamakṣaṃ vasudhātalasthaḥ||11||

12tathā'rjunaḥ sukumāro manasvī vaśe sthito dharmasutasya rājñaḥ
|
vidūyamānairiva sarvagātrairdhruvaṃ na śete vasatīramarṣāt||12||

13yamau ca kṛṣṇāṃ ca yudhiṣṭhiraṃ ca bhīmaṃ ca dṛṣṭvā sukhaviprayuktam
|
viniḥśvasan sarpa ivogratejā dhruvaṃ na śete vasatīramarṣāt||13||

14yathā yamau cāpyasukhau sukhārhau samṛddharūpāvamarau divīva
|
prajāgarasthau dhruvamapraśāntau dharmeṇa satyena ca vāryamāṇau||14||

15samīraṇenātha samo balena samīraṇasyaiva suto balīyān
|
sa dharmapāśena sito'grajena dhruvaṃ viniḥśvasya sahatyamarṣam||15||

16sa cāpi bhūmau parivartamāno vadhaṃ sutānāṃ mama kāṅkṣamāṇaḥ
|
satyena dharmeṇa ca vāryamāṇaḥ kālaṃ pratīkṣatyadhiko raṇe'nyaiḥ||16||

17ajātaśatrau tu jite nikṛtyā duḥśāsano yat paruṣāṇyavocat
|
tāni praviṣṭāni vṛkodarāṅgaṃ dahanti kakṣāgnirivendhanāni||17||

18na pāpakaṃ dhyāsyati dharmaputro dhanañjayaścāpyanuvartsyate tam
|
araṇyavāsena vivardhate tu bhīmasya kopo'gnirivānilena||18||

19sa tena kopena vidahyamānaḥ karaṃ kareṇābhinipīḍya vīraḥ
|
viniḥśvasatyuṣṇamatīva ghoraṃ dahannivemān mama putrapautrān||19||

20gāṇḍīvadhanvā ca vṛkodaraśca saṃrambhiṇāvantakakālakalpau
|
na śeṣayetāṃ yudhi śatrusenāṃ śarān kirantāvaśaniprakāśān||20||

21duryodhanaḥ śakuniḥ sūtaputro duḥśāsanaścāpi sumandacetāḥ
|
madhu prapaśyanti na tu prapātaṃ yaddyūtamālambya haranti rājyam||21||

22śubhāśubhaṃ karma naro hi kṛtvā pratīkṣate tasya phalaṃ sma kartā
|
sa tena muhyatyavaśaḥ phalena mokṣaḥ kathaṃ syāt puruṣasya tasmāt||22||

23kṣetre sukṛṣṭe hyupite ca vīje deve ca varṣatyṛtukālayuktam
|
na syāt phalaṃ tasya kutaḥ prasiddhiranyatraṃ daivāditi cintayāmi||23||

24kṛtaṃ matākṣeṇa yathā na sādhu sādhupravṛttena ca pāṇḍavena
|
mayā ca duṣputravaśānugena yathā kurūṇāmayamantakālaḥ||24||

25dhruvaṃ pravāsvatyasamīrito'pi dhruvaṃ prajāsyatyuta garbhiṇī yā
|
dhruvaṃ dinādau rajanīpraṇāśastathā kṣapādau ca dinapraṇāśaḥ||25||

26kriyeta kasmādapare ca kuryurvittaṃ na dadyuḥ puruṣāḥ kathañcit
|
prāpyārthakālaṃ ca bhavedanarthaḥ kathaṃ nu tat syāditi tat kutaḥ syāt||26||

27kathaṃ nu bhidyeta na ca sraveta na ca prasicyediti rakṣitavyam
|
arakṣyamāṇaṃ śatadhā prakīryet dhruvaṃ na nāśo'sti kṛtasya loke||27||

28gato hyaraṇyādapi śakralokaṃ dhanañjayaḥ paśyata vīryamasya
|
astrāṇi divyāni caturvidhāni jñātvā punarlokamimaṃ prapannaḥ||28||

29svargaṃ hi gatvā saśarīra eva ko mānuṣaḥ punarāgantumicchet
|
anyatra kālopahatānanekān samīkṣamāṇastu kurūn mumūrṣūn||29||

30dhanurgrāhaścārjunaḥ savyasācī dhanuśca tadgāṇḍivaṃ bhīmavegam
|
astrāṇi divyāni ca tāni tasya trayasya tejaḥ prasaheta ko'tra||30||

31niśamya tadvacanaṃ pārthivasya duryodhanaṃ rahite saubalo'tha
|
abodhayat karṇamupetya sarvaṃ sa cāpyahṛṣṭo'bhavadalpacetāḥ||31||
