Mahābhārata 1.155 (Ādiparvan)

1bhīmasena uvāca|
smaranti vairaṃ rakṣāṃsi māyāmāśritya mohinīm
|
hiḍimbe vraja panthānaṃ tvamimaṃ bhrātṛsevitam||1||

2yudhiṣṭhira uvāca|
kruddho'pi puruṣavyāghra bhīma mā sma striyaṃ vadhīḥ
|
śarīragutyabhyadhikaṃ dharma gopāya pāṇḍava||2||

3vadhābhiprāyamāyāṃtamavadhīstvaṃ mahābalam
|
rakṣasastasya bhaginī kiṃ naḥ kruddhā kariṣyati||3||

4vaiśaṃpāyana uvāca|
hiḍimbā tu tataḥ kuntīmabhivādya kṛtāñjaliḥ
|
yudhiṣṭhiraṃ tu kaunteyamidaṃ vacanamabravīt||4||

5ārye jānāsi yadduḥkhamiha strīṇāmanaṅgajam
|
tadidaṃ māmanuprāptaṃ bhīmasenakṛtaṃ śubhe||5||

6soḍhaṃ tatparamaṃ duḥkhaṃ mayā kālapratīkṣayā
|
so'yamabhyāgataḥ kālo bhavitā me sukhodayaḥ||6||

7mayāhyutsṛjya suhṛdaḥ svadharmaṃ svajanaṃ tathā
|
vṛto'yaṃ puruṣavyāghrastava putraḥ patiḥ śubhe||7||

8vīreṇā'haṃ tathānena tvayā cāpi yaśasvini
|
pratyākhyātā na jīvāmi satyametadbravīmi te||8||

9tadarhasi kṛpāṃ kartuṃ mayi tvaṃ varavarṇini
|
matvā mūḍheti tanmāṃ tvaṃ bhaktā vā'nugateti vā ||9||

10bhartrā'nena mahābhāge saṃyojaya sutena ha
|
tamupādāya gaccheyaṃ yatheṣṭaṃ devarūpiṇam
|
punaścaivānayiṣyāmi visrambhaṃ kuru me śubhe||10||

11ahaṃ hi manasā dhyātā sarvānnaṣyāmi vaḥ sadā
|
vṛjināttārayiṣyāmi durgeṣu viṣameṣu ca||11||

12pṛṣṭhena vo vahiṣyāmi śīghraṃ gatimabhīpsataḥ
|
yūyaṃ prasādaṃ kuruta bhīmaseno bhajeta mām||12||

13āpadastaraṇe prāṇāndhārayedhena tena vā
|
sarvamāvṛtya kartavyaṃ taṃ dharmamanuvartatā||13||

14āpatsu yo dhārayati dharmaṃ dharmaviduttamaḥ
|
vyasanaṃ hyeva dharmasya dharmiṇāmāpaducyate||14||

15puṇyaṃ prāṇāndhārayati puṇyaṃ prāṇadamucyate
|
yena yenācareddharmaṃ tasmingarhā na vidyate||15||

16yudhiṣṭhira uvāca|
evametadyathāttha tvaṃ hiḍimbe nātra saṃśayaḥ
|
sthātavyaṃ tu tvayā satye yathā brūyāṃ sumadhyame||16||

17snātaṃ kṛtāhnikaṃ bhadre kṛtakautukamaṅgalam
|
bhīmasenaṃ bhajethāstvaṃ prāgastagamanādraveḥ||17||

18ahaḥsu viharānena yathākāmaṃ manojavā
|
ayaṃ tvānayitavyaste bhīmasenaḥ sadā niśi||18||

19vaiśaṃpāyana uvāca|
tatheti tatpratijñāya bhīmaseno'bravīdidam
|
śṛṇu rākṣasi satyena samayaṃ te vadāmyaham||19||

20yāvatkālena bhavati putrasyotpādanaṃ śubhe
|
tāvatkālaṃ gamiṣyāmi tvayā saha sumadhyame||20||

21vaiśaṃpāyana uvāca| tatheti tatpratijñāya hiḍimbā rākṣasī tadā
|
bhīmasenamupādāya sordhvamācakrame tataḥ||21||

22śailaśṛṅgeṣu ramyeṣu devatāyataneṣu ca
|
mṛgapakṣivighuṣṭeṣu ramaṇīyeṣu sarvadā||22||

23kṛtvā ca rūpaṃ paramaṃ sarvābharaṇabhūṣitā
|
saṃjalpantī sumadhuraṃ ramayāmāsa pāṇḍavam||23||

24tathaiva vanadurgeṣu puṣpitadrumavalliṣu
|
saraḥsu ramaṇīyeṣu padmotpalayuteṣu ca||24||

25nadīdvīpapradeśeṣu vaidūryasikatāsu ca
|
sutīrthavanatoyāsu tathā girinadaṣu ca||25||

26kānaneṣu vicitreṣu puṣpitadrumavalliṣu
|
himavadvirikuñjeṣu guhāsu vividhāsu ca||26||

27praphullaśatapatreṣu saraḥsvamalavāriṣu
|
sāgarasthapradeśeṣu maṇihemaciteṣu ca||27||

28palvaleṣu ca ramyeṣu mahāśālavaneṣu ca
|
devāraṇyaṣu puṇyeṣu tathā parvatasānuṣu||28||

29guhyakānāṃ nivāseṣu tāpasāyataneṣu ca
|
sarvartuphalaramyeṣu mānaseṣu saraḥsu ca||29||

30vibhratī paramaṃ rūpaṃ ramayāmāsa pāṇḍavam
|
ramayantī tathā bhīmaṃ tatra tatra manojavā||30||

31prajajñe rākṣasī putraṃ bhīmasenānmahābalam
|
virūpākṣaṃ mahāvaktraṃ śaṅkukarṇaṃ bibhīṣaṇam||31||

32bhīmanādaṃ sutāmroṣṭhaṃ tīkṣṇadaṃṣṭraṃ mahābalam
|
maheṣvāsaṃ mahāvīryaṃ mahāsattvaṃ mahābhujam||32||

33mahājavaṃ mahākāyaṃ mahāmāyamariṃdamam
|
dīrghaghoṇaṃ mahoraskaṃ vikaṭodvaddhapiṇḍikam||33||

34amānuṣaṃ mānuṣajaṃ bhīmavegaṃ mahābalam
|
yaḥ piśācānatītyānyānbabhūvātīva rākṣasān ||34||

35bālo'pi yauvanaṃ prāpto mānuṣeṣu viśāṃpate
|
sarvāstreṣu paraṃ vīraḥ prakarṣamagamadvalī||35||

36sadyo hi garbhānrākṣasyo labhante prasavanti ca
|
kāmarūpadharāścaiva bhavanti bahurūpikāḥ||36||

37praṇamya vikacaḥ pādāvagṛhṇātsa pitustadā
|
mātuśca parameṣvāsastau ca nāmāsya cakratuḥ||37||

38ghaṭo hārayotkaca iti mātā taṃ pratyabhāṣata
|
abravīttena nāmāsya ghaṭotkaca iti sma ha||38||

39anuraktaśca tānāsītpāṇḍavānsa ghaṭotkacaḥ
|
teṣāṃ ca dayito nityamātmanityo babhūva ha||39||

40saṃvāsasamayo jīrṇa ityābhāṣya tatastu tān
|
hiḍimbā samayaṃ kṛtvā svāṃ gatiṃ pratyapadyata||40||

41ghaṭotkacī mahākāyaḥ pāṇḍavānpṛthayā saha
|
abhivādya yathānyāyamabravīcca prabhāṣyatām||41||

42kiṃ karomyahamāryāṇāṃ niḥśaṅkaṃ vadatānaghāḥ
|
taṃ bruvantaṃ bhaimaseniṃ kuntī vacanamabravīt||42||

43tvaṃ kurūṇāṃ kule jātaḥ sākṣādbhīmasamo hyasi
|
jyeṣṭhaḥ putro'si pañcānāṃ sāhāyyaṃ kuru putraka||43||

44vaiśaṃpāyana uvāca| pṛthayā'pyevamuktastu praṇamyaiva vaco'bravīt
|
yathā hi rāvaṇo loke indrajicca mahābalaḥ
|
varṣmavīryasamo loke viśiṣṭaścābhavaṃ nṛṣu||44||

45kṛtyakāla upasthāsye pitṝniti ghaṭotkacaḥ
|
āmantrya rakṣasāṃ śreṣṭhaḥ pratasthe cottarāṃ diśam||45||

46sa hi sṛṣṭo maghavatā śaktihetormahātmanā
|
karṇasyāprativīryasya pratiyoddhā mahārathaḥ||46||
