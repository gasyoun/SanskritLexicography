Mahābhārata 6.116 (Bhīṣmaparvan)

1sañjaya uvāca|
abhimanyurmahārāja tava putramayodhayat
|
mahatyā senayā yuktaṃ bhīṣmahetoḥ parākramī ||1||

2duryodhano raṇe kārṣṇi navabhirnataparvabhiḥ
|
ājaghānorasi kruddhaḥ punaścainaṃ tribhiḥ śaraiḥ ||2||

3tasya śaktiṃ raṇe kārṣṇirmṛtyorghorāṃ svasāmiva
|
preṣayāmāsa saṃkṛddho duryodhanarathaṃ prati ||3||

4tāmāpatantīṃ sahasā ghorarūpāṃ viśāṃpate
|
dvidhā ciccheda te putraḥ kṣurapreṇa mahārathaḥ ||4||

5tāṃ śaktiṃ patitāṃ dṛṣṭvā kārṣṇiḥ paramakopanaḥ
|
duryodhanaṃ tribhirbāṇairbāhvorurasi cārpayat ||5||

6punaścainaṃ śarairghorairājaghāna stanāntare
|
daśabhirbharataśreṣṭha bharatānāṃ mahārathaḥ ||6||

7tadyuddhamabhavaddhoraṃ citrarūpaṃ ca bhārata
|
indriyaprītijananaṃ sarvapārthivapūjitam ||7||

8bhīṣmasya nidhanārthāya pārthasya vijayāya ca
|
yuyudhāte raṇe vīrau saubhadrakurupuṅgavau ||8||

9sātyakiṃ rabhasaṃ yuddhe drauṇirbrāhmaṇapuṅgavaḥ
|
ājaghānorasi kruddho nārācena paraṃtapaḥ ||9||

10śaineyo'pi guroḥ putraṃ sarvamarmasu bhārata
|
atāḍayadameyātmā navabhiḥ kaṅkavājitaiḥ ||10||

11aśvatthāmā tu samare sātyakiṃ navabhiḥ śaraiḥ
|
triṃśatā ca punastūrṇaṃ bāhvorurasi cārpayat ||11||

12so'tividdho maheṣvāso droṇaputreṇa sātvataḥ
|
droṇaputraṃ tribhirbāṇairājaghāna mahāyaśāḥ ||12||

13pauravo dhṛṣṭaketuṃ ca śarairācchādya saṃyuge
|
bahudhā dārayāñcakre maheṣvāsaṃ mahārathaḥ ||13||

14tathaiva pauravaṃ yuddhe dhṛṣṭaketurmahārathaḥ
|
triṃśatā niśitairbāṇairvivyādhāśu mahābhujaḥ ||14||

15pauravastu dhanuśchitvā dhṛṣṭaketormahārathaḥ
|
nanāda balavannādaṃ vivyādha ca śitaiḥ śaraiḥ ||15||

16so'nyat kārmukamādāya pauravaṃ niśitaiḥ śaraiḥ
|
ājaghāna mahārāja trisaptatyā śilīmukhaiḥ ||16||

17tau tu tatra maheṣvāsau mahāmātrau mahārathau
|
mahatā śaravarṣeṇa parasparamavidhyatām ||17||

18anyonyasya dhanuśchitvā hayānhatvā ca bhārata
|
virathāvasiyuddhāya samīyaturamarṣaṇau ||18||

19ārṣabhe carmaṇī citre śatacandrapuraskṛte
|
tārakāśatacitre ca nistriṃśau sumahāprabhau ||19||

20pragṛhya vimalau rājaṃstāvanyonyamabhidrutau
|
vāsitāsaṃgame yattau siṃhāviva mahāvane ||20||

21maṇḍalāni vicitrāṇi gatapratyāgatāni ca
|
ceraturdarśayantau ca prārthayantau parasparam ||21||

22pauravo dhṛṣṭaketuṃ tu śaṃkhadeśe mahāsinā
|
tāḍayāmāsa saṃkruddhastiṣṭha tiṣṭheti cābravīt ||22||

23cedirājo'pi samare pauravaṃ puruṣarṣabham
|
ājaghāna śitāgreṇa jatrudeśe mahāsinā ||23||

24tāvanyonyaṃ mahārāja samāsādya mahāhave
|
anyonyavegābhihatau nipetaturarindamau ||24||

25tataḥ svarathamāropya pauravaṃ tanayastava
|
jayatseno rathenājāvapovāha raṇājirāt ||25||

26dhṛṣṭaketuṃ tu samare mādrīputraḥ pratāpavān
|
apovāha raṇe kruddhaḥ sahadevaḥ parākramī ||26||

27citrasenaḥ suśarmāṇaṃ viddhvā bahubhirāyasaiḥ
|
punarvivyādha taṃ ṣaṣṭyā punaśca navabhiḥ śaraiḥ ||27||

28suśarmā tu raṇe kruddhastava putraṃ viśāṃpate
|
daśabhirdaśabhiścaiva vivyādha niśitaiḥ śaraiḥ ||28||

29citrasenaśca taṃ rājaṃstriṃśatā nataparvabhiḥ
|
ājaghāna raṇe kruddhaḥ sa ca taṃ pratyavidhyata ||29||

30bhīṣmasya samare rājanyaśo mānaṃ ca vardhayan
|
saubhadro rājaputraṃ tu bṛhadbalamayodhayat ||30||

31pārthahetoḥ parākrānto bhīṣmasyāyodhanaṃ prati
|
ārjuniṃ kosalendrastu viddhvā pañcabhirāyasaiḥ ||31||

32punarvivyādha viṃśatyā śaraiḥ sannataparvabhiḥ
|
saubhadraḥ kosalendraṃ tu vivyādhāṣṭabhirāyasaiḥ ||32||

33nākampayata saṃgrāme vivyādha ca punaḥ śaraiḥ
|
kausalyasya dhanuścāpi punaściccheda phālguniḥ ||33||

34ājaghāna śaraiścāpi triṃśatā kaṅkapatribhiḥ
|
so'nyat kārmukamādāya rājaputro bṛhadbalaḥ ||34||

35phālguniṃ samare kruddho vivyādha bahubhiḥ śaraiḥ
|
tayoryuddhaṃ samabhavadbhīṣmahetoḥ paraṃtapa ||35||

36saṃrabdhayormahārāja samare citrayodhinoḥ
|
yathā devāsure yuddhe balivāsavayorabhūt ||36||

37bhīmaseno gajānīkaṃ yodhayan bahvaśobhata
|
yathā śakro vajrapāṇirdārayan parvatottamān ||37||

38te vadhyamānā bhīmena mātaṅgā girisannibhāḥ
|
nipetururvyāṃ sahitā nādayanto vasundharām ||38||

39girimātrā hi te nāgā bhinnāñjanacayopamāḥ
|
virejurvasudhāṃ prāptā vikīrṇā iva parvatāḥ ||39||

40yudhiṣṭhiro maheṣvāso madrarājānamāhave
|
mahatyā senayā guptaṃ pīḍayāmāsa saṃgatam ||40||

41madreśvaraśca samare dharmaputraṃ mahāratham
|
pīḍayāmāsa saṃrabdho bhīṣmahetoḥ parākramī ||41||

42virāṭaṃ saindhavo rājā viddhvā sannataparvabhiḥ
|
navabhiḥ sāyakaistīkṣṇaistriṃśatā punarārpayat ||42||

43virāṭaśca mahārāja saindhavaṃ vāhinīpatiḥ
|
triṃśadbhirniśitairbāṇairājaghāna stanāntare ||43||

44citrakārmukanistriṃśau citravarmāyudhadhvajau
|
rejatuścitrarūpau tau sagrāme matsyasaindhavau ||44||

45droṇaḥ pāñcālaputreṇa samāgamya mahāraṇe
|
mahāsamudayaṃ cakre śaraiḥ sannataparvabhiḥ ||45||

46tato droṇo mahārāja pārṣatasya mahaddhanuḥ
|
chittvā pañcāśateṣūṇāṃ pārṣataṃ samavidhyata ||46||

47so'nyat kārmukamādāya pārṣataḥ paravīrahā
|
droṇasya miṣato yuddhe preṣayāmāsa sāyakān ||47||

48tāñcharāñcharaghātena ciccheda sa mahārathaḥ
|
droṇo drupadaputrāya prāhiṇot pañca sāyakān ||48||

49tataḥ kruddho mahārāja pārṣataḥ paravīrahā
|
droṇāya cikṣepa gadāṃ yamadaṇḍopamāṃ raṇe ||49||

50tāmāpatantīṃ sahasā hemapaṭṭavibhūṣitām
|
śaraiḥ pañcāśatā droṇo vārayāmāsa saṃyuge ||50||

51sā chinnā bahudhā rājan droṇacāpacyutaiḥ śaraiḥ
|
cūrṇīkṛtā viśīryantī papāta vasudhātale ||51||

52gadāṃ vinihatāṃ dṛṣṭvā pārṣataḥ śatrutāpanaḥ
|
droṇāya śaktiṃ cikṣepa sarvapārasavīṃ śubhām ||52||

53tāṃ droṇo navabhirbāṇaiściccheda yudhi bhārata
|
pārṣataṃ ca maheṣvāsaṃ pīḍayāmāsa saṃyuge ||53||

54evametanmahāyuddhaṃ droṇapārṣatayorabhūt
|
bhīṣmaṃ prati mahārāja ghorarūpaṃ bhayānakam ||54||

55arjunaḥ prāpya gāṅgeyaṃ pīḍayanniśitaiḥ śaraiḥ
|
abhyadravata saṃyatto vane mattamiva dvipam ||55||

56pratyudyayau ca taṃ rājā bhagadattaḥ pratāpavān
|
tridhā bhinnena nāgena madāndhena mahābalaḥ ||56||

57tamāpatantaṃ sahasā mahendragajasannibham
|
paraṃ yatnaṃ samāsthāya bībhatsuḥ pratyapadyata ||57||

58tato gajagato rājā bhagadattaḥ pratāpavān
|
arjunaṃ śaravarṣeṇa vārayāmāsa saṃyuge ||58||

59arjunastu tato nāgamāyāntaṃ rajatopamaiḥ
|
vimalairāyasaistīkṣṇairavidhyata mahāraṇe ||59||

60śikhaṇḍinaṃ ca kaunteyo yāhi yāhītyacodayat
|
bhīṣmaṃ prati mahārāja jahyenamiti cābravīt ||60||

61prāgjyotiṣastato hitvā pāṇḍavaṃ pāṇḍupūrvaja
|
prayayau tvarito rājan drupadasya rathaṃ prati ||61||

62tato'rjuno mahārāja bhīṣmamabhyadravaddrutam
|
śikhaṇḍinaṃ puraskṛtya tato yuddhamavartata ||62||

63tataste tāvakāḥ śūrāḥ pāṇḍavaṃ rabhasaṃ yudhi
|
samabhyadhāvan krośantastada‌dbhutamivābhavat ||63||

64nānāvidhānyanīkāni putrāṇāṃ te janādhipa
|
arjuno vyadhamatkāle divīvābhrāṇi mārutaḥ ||64||

65śikhaṇḍī tu samāsādya bharatānāṃ pitāmaham
|
iṣubhistūrṇamavyagro bahubhiḥ sa samācinot ||65||

66rathāgnyagāraścāpārcirasiśaktigadendhanaḥ
|
śarasaṅghamahājvālaḥ kṣatriyān samare'dahat ||66||

67yathā'gniḥ sumahāniddhaḥ kakṣe carati sānilaḥ
|
tathā jajvāla bhīṣmo'pi divyānyastrāṇyudīrayan ||67||

68somakāṃśca raṇa bhīṣmo jaghne pārthapadānugān
|
nyavārayata tat sainyaṃ pāṇḍavasya mahārathaḥ ||68||

69suvarṇapuṅkhairiṣubhiḥ śitaiḥ sannataparvabhiḥ
|
nādayan sa diśo bhīṣmaḥ pradiśaśca mahāhave ||69||

70pātayan rathino rājan hayāṃśca saha sādibhiḥ
|
muṇḍatālavanānīva cakāra sa rathavrajān ||70||

71nirmanuṣyān rathān rājan gajānaśvāṃśca saṃyuge
|
cakāra samare bhīṣmaḥ sarvaśastrabhṛtāṃ varaḥ ||71||

72tasya jyātalanirghoṣaṃ visphūrjitamivāśaneḥ
|
niśamya sarvato rājansamakampanta sainikāḥ ||72||

73amoghā nyapatan bāṇāḥ pituste manujeśvara
|
nāsajjanta śarīreṣu bhīṣmacāpacyutāḥ śarāḥ||73||

74nirmanuṣyān rathān rājan suyuktān javanairhayaiḥ
|
vātāyamānānadrākṣaṃ hriyamāṇān viśāṃpate ||74||

75cedikāśikarūṣāṇāṃ sahasrāṇi caturdaśa
|
mahārathāḥ samākhyātāḥ kulaputrāstanutyajaḥ ||75||

76aparāvartinaḥ śūrāḥ suvarṇavikṛtadhvajāḥ
|
saṃgrāme bhīṣmamāsādya savājirathakuñjarāḥ ||76||

77jagmuste paralokāya vyāditāsyamivāntakam
|
na tatrāsīdraṇe rājan somakānāṃ mahārathaḥ ||77||

78yaḥ saṃprāpya raṇe bhīṣmaṃ jīvite sma mano dadhe
|
tāṃśca sarvān raṇe yodhān pretarājapuraṃ prati ||78||

79nītānamanyanta janā dṛṣṭvā bhīṣmasya vikramam
|
na kaścidenaṃ samare pratyudyāti mahārathaḥ ||79||

80ṛte pāṇḍusutaṃ vīraṃ śvetāśvaṃ kṛṣṇasārathim
|
śikhaṇḍinaṃ ca samare pāñcālyamamitaujasam ||80||
