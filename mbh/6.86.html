Mahābhārata 6.86 (Bhīṣmaparvan)

1sañjaya uvāca|
virathaṃ taṃ samāsādya citrasenaṃ yaśasvinam
|
rathamāropayāmāsa vikarṇastanayastava ||1||

2tasmiṃstathā vartamāne tumule saṅkule bhṛśam
|
bhīṣmaḥ śāntanavastūrṇaṃ yudhiṣṭhiramupādravat ||2||

3tataḥ sarathanāgāśvāḥ samakampanta sṛñjayāḥ
|
mṛtyorāsyamanuprāptaṃ menire ca yudhiṣṭhiram ||3||

4yudhiṣṭhiro'pi kauravyo yamābhyāṃ sahitaḥ prabhuḥ
|
maheṣvāsaṃ naravyāghraṃ bhīṣmaṃ śāntanavaṃ yayau ||4||

5tataḥ śarasahasrāṇi pramuñcan pāṇḍavo yudhi
|
bhīṣmaṃ saṃchādayāmāsa yathā megho divākaram ||5||

6tena samyak praṇītāni śarajālāni māriṣa
|
pratijagrāha gāṅgeyaḥ śataśo'tha sahasraśaḥ ||6||

7tathaiva śarajālāni bhīṣmeṇāstāni māriṣa
|
ākāśe samadṛśyanta khagamānāṃ vrajā iva ||7||

8nimeṣārdhena kaunteyaṃ bhīṣmaḥ śāntanavo yudhi
|
adṛśyaṃ samare cakre śarajālena bhāgaśaḥ ||8||

9tato yudhiṣṭhiro rājā kauravyasya mahātmanaḥ
|
nārācaṃ preṣayāmāsa kruddha āśīviṣopamam ||9||

10asaṃprāptaṃ tatastaṃ tu kṣurapreṇa mahārathaḥ
|
ciccheda samare rājanbhīṣmastasya dhanuścyutam ||10||

11taṃ tu cchittvā raṇe bhīṣmo nārācaṃ kālasaṃmitam
|
nijaghne kauravendrasya hayān kāñcanabhūṣaṇān ||11||

12hatāśvaṃ tu rathaṃ tyaktvā dharmaputro yudhiṣṭhiraḥ
|
āruroha rathaṃ tūrṇaṃ nakulasya mahātmanaḥ ||12||

13yamāvapi hi saṃkruddhaḥ samāsādya raṇe tadā
|
śaraiḥ saṃchādayāmāsa bhīṣmaḥ parapurañjayaḥ ||13||

14tau tu dṛṣṭvā mahārāja bhīṣmabāṇaprapīḍitau
|
jagāma paramāṃ cintāṃ bhīṣmasya vadhakāṅkṣayā ||14||

15tato yudhiṣṭhiro vaśyān rājñastānsamacodayat
|
bhīṣmaṃ śāntanavaṃ sarve nihateti suhṛdgaṇān ||15||

16tataste pārthivāḥ sarve śrutvā pārthasya bhāṣitam
|
mahatā rathavaṃśena parivavruḥ pitāmaham ||16||

17sa samantāt parivṛtaḥ pitā devavratastava
|
cikrīḍa dhanuṣā rājan pātayāno mahārathān ||17||

18taṃ carantaṃ raṇe pārthā dadṛśuḥ kauravaṃ yudhi
|
mṛgamadhyaṃ praviśyeva yathā siṃhaśiśuṃ vane ||18||

19tarjayānaṃ raṇe vīrāṃstrāsayānaṃ ca sāyakaiḥ
|
dṛṣṭvā tresurmahārāja siṃhaṃ mṛgagaṇā iva ||19||

20raṇe bhāratasiṃhasya dadṛśuḥ kṣatriyā gatim
|
agnervāyusahāyasya yathā kakṣaṃ didhakṣataḥ ||20||

21śirāṃsi rathināṃ bhīṣmaḥ pātayāmāsa saṃyuge
|
tālebhyaḥ paripakvāni phalāni kuśalo naraḥ ||21||

22yatadbhiśca maharāja śirobhirdharaṇītale
|
babhūva tumulaḥ śabdaḥ patatāmaśmanāmiva ||22||

23tasmin sutumule yuddhe vartamāne bhayānake
|
sarveṣāmeva sainyānāmāsīdvyatikaro mahān ||23||

24bhinneṣu teṣu vyūheṣu kṣatriyā itaretaram
|
ekamekaṃ samāhūya yuddhāyaivāvatasthire ||24||

25śikhaṇḍī tu samāsādya bharatānāṃ pitāmaham
|
abhidudrāva vegena tiṣṭha tiṣṭheti cābravīt ||25||

26anādṛtya tato bhīṣmastaṃ śikhaṇḍinamāhave
|
prayayau sṛñjayānkruddhaḥ strītvaṃ cintya śikhaṇḍinaḥ ||26||

27sṛñjayāstu tato dṛṣṭvā hṛṣṭa bhīṣmaṃ mahāraṇe
|
siṃhanādāṃśca vividhāṃścakruḥ śaṅkhavimiśritān ||27||

28tataḥ pravavṛte yuddhaṃ vyatiṣaktarathadvipam
|
paścimāṃ diśamāsādya sthite savitari prabho||28||

29dhṛṣṭadyumno'tha pāñcālyaḥ sātyakiśca mahārathaḥ
|
pīḍayantau bhṛśaṃ sainyaṃ śaktitomaravṛṣṭibhiḥ ||29||

30śastraiśca bahubhī rājan jaghnatustāvakān raṇe
|
te hanyamānāḥ samare tāvakā bharatarṣabha ||30||

31āryāṃ yuddhe matiṃ kṛtvā na tyajanti sma saṃyugam
|
yathotsāhaṃ tu samare nijaghnustāvakā raṇe ||31||

32tatrākrando mahānāsīttāvakānāṃ mahātmanām
|
vadhyatāṃ samare rājan pārṣatena mahātmanā ||32||

33taṃ śrutvā ninadaṃ ghoraṃ tāvakānāṃ mahārathau
|
vindānuvindāvāvantyau pārṣataṃ pratyupasthitau ||33||

34tau tasya turagān hatvā tvaramāṇau mahārathau
|
chādayāmāsaturubhau śaravarṣeṇa pārṣatam ||34||

35avaplutyātha pāñcālyo rathāt tūrṇaṃ mahābalaḥ
|
āruroha rathaṃ tūrṇaṃ sātyakestu mahātmanaḥ ||35||

36tato yudhiṣṭhiro rājā mahatyā senayā vṛtaḥ
|
āvantyau samare kuddhāvabhyayātsa parantapau ||36||

37tathaiva tava putro'pi sarvodyogena māriṣa
|
vindānuvindau samare parivāryāvatasthivān ||37||

38arjunaścāpi saṃkruddhaḥ kṣatriyān kṣatriyarṣabhaḥ
|
ayodhayata saṃgrāme vajrapāṇirivāsurān ||38||

39droṇastu samare kruddhaḥ putrasya priyakṛt tava
|
vyadhamat sarvapañcālāṃstūlarāśimivānalaḥ ||39||

40duryodhanapurogāstu putrāstava viśāṃpate
|
parivārya raṇe bhīṣmaṃ yuyudhuḥ pāṇḍavaiḥ saha ||40||

41tato duryodhano rājā lohitāyati bhāskare
|
abravīt tāvakān sarvāṃstvaradhvamiti bhārata ||41||

42yudhyatāṃ tu tathā teṣāṃ kurvatāṃ karma duṣkaram
|
astaṃ girimathārūḍhe aprakāśati bhāskare ||42||

43prāvartata nadī ghorā śoṇitaughataraṅgiṇī
|
gomāyugaṇasaṃkīrṇā kṣaṇena kṣaṇadāmukhe ||43||

44śivābhiraśivābhiśca ruvadbhirbhairavaṃ ravam
|
ghoramāyodhanaṃ jajñe bhūtasaṅghaiḥ samākulam ||44||

45rākṣasāśca piśācāśca tathānye piśitāśinaḥ
|
samantato vyadṛśyanta śataśo'tha sahasraśaḥ ||45||

46arjunotha suśarmādīn rājñastān sapadānugān
|
vijitya pṛtanāmadhye yayau svaśibiraṃ prati ||46||

47yudhiṣṭhiro'pi kauravyo bhrātṛbhyāṃ sahitastathā
|
yayau svaśibiraṃ rājā niśāyāṃ senayā vṛtaḥ ||47||

48bhīmaseno'pi rājendra duryodhanamukhān rathān
|
avajitya tataḥ saṃkhye yayau svaśibiraṃ prati ||48||

49duryodhano'pi nṛpatiḥ parivārya mahāraṇe
|
bhīṣmaṃ śāntanavaṃ tūrṇaṃ prayātaḥ śibiraṃ prati ||49||

50droṇo drauṇiḥ kṛpaḥ śalyaḥ kṛtavarmā ca sātvataḥ
|
parivārya camūṃ sarvāṃ prayayuḥ śibiraṃ prati ||50||

51tathaiva sātyakī rājan dhṛṣṭadyumnaśca pārṣataḥ
|
parivārya raṇe yodhān yayatuḥ śibiraṃ prati ||51||

52evamete mahārāja tāvakāḥ pāṇḍavaiḥ saha
|
paryavartanta sahitā niśākāle paraṃtapa ||52||

53tataḥ svaśibiraṃ gatvā pāṇḍavāḥ kuravastathā
|
nyavasanta mahārāja pūjayantaḥ parasparam ||53||

54rakṣāṃ kṛtvā tataḥ śūrānyasya gulmānyathāvidhi
|
apanīya ca śalyāni snātvā ca vividhairjalaiḥ ||54||

55kṛtasvastyayanāḥ sarve saṃstūyantaśca bandibhiḥ
|
gītavāditraśabdena vyakrīḍanta yaśasvinaḥ ||55||

56muhūrtādiva tat sarvamabhavatsvargasannibham
|
na hi yuddhakathāṃ kāñcittatrākurvan mahārathāḥ ||56||

57te prasupte bale tatra pariśrāntajane nṛpa
|
hastyaśvabahule rātrau prekṣaṇīye babhūvatuḥ ||57||
