Mahābhārata 8.94 (Karṇaparvan)

1sañjaya uvāca|
dṛṣṭvā tu sainyaṃ parivṛtyamānaṃ putreṇa te madrapatistadānīm
|
saṃtrastarūpaḥ parimūḍhacetā duryodhanaṃ vākyamidaṃ babhāṣe ||1||

2śalya uvāca|
paśyedamugraṃ naravājināgairāyodhanaṃ vīraśataiḥ supūrṇam
|
mahīdharābhaiḥ patitaiśca nāgaiḥ sakṛtprabhinnaiḥ śarabhinnadehaiḥ ||2||

3savihvaladbhiśca gatāsubhiśca pradhvastavarmāyudhacarmakhaḍgaiḥ
|
vajrāpaviddhairiva cācalottamairvibhinnapāṣāṇamahādrumauṣadhaiḥ ||3||

4praviddhaghaṇṭāṅkuśatomaradhvajaiḥ sahemajālai rudhiraughasaṃplutaiḥ
|
śarāvabhinnaiḥ patitaisturaṅgamaiḥ śvasadbhirārtaiḥ kṣatajaṃ vamadbhiḥ ||4||

5dīnaṃ stanadbhiḥ parivṛttanetrairmahīṃ daśadbhiḥ kṛpaṇaṃ nadadbhiḥ
|
tathāpaviddhairgajavājiyodhairbalāpaviddhairatha vīrasaṃghaiḥ ||5||

6mandāsubhiścaiva gatāsubhiśca narāśvanāgaiśca rathaiśca marditaiḥ
|
mandāṃśubhiścaiva mahī mahāhave nūnaṃ yathā vaitaraṇīva bhāti ||6||

7gajairnikṛttairvarahastagātrairudvepamānaiḥ patitaiḥ pṛthivyām
|
viśīrṇadantaiḥ kṣatajaṃ vamadbhiḥ sphuradbhirārtaiḥ karuṇaṃ nadadbhiḥ ||7||

8nikṛttacakreṣuyugaiḥ sayoktṛbhiḥ praviddhatūṇīrapatākaketubhiḥ
|
suvarṇajālāvatatairbhṛśāhatairmahārathaughairjaladairivāvṛtā ||8||

9yaśasvibhirnāgarathāśvayodhibhiḥ padātibhiścābhimukhairhataiḥ paraiḥ
|
viśīrṇavarmābharaṇāmbarāyudhairvṛtā praśāntairiva tāvakairmahī ||9||

10śaraprahārābhihatairmahābalairavekṣyamāṇaiḥ patitaiḥ sahasraśaḥ
|
divaścyutairbhūratidīptimadbhirnaktaṃ grahairdyauramalapradīptaiḥ ||10||

11pranaṣṭasaṃjñaiḥ punarucchvasadbhirmahī babhūvānugatairivārcibhiḥ
|
karṇārjunābhyāṃ śarabhinnagātrairhataiḥ pravīraiḥ kurusṛñjayānām ||11||

12śarāstu karṇārjunabāhumuktā vidārya nāgāśvamanuṣyadehān
|
prāṇānnirasyāśu mahīṃ pratīyurmahoragā vāsamivābhinamrāḥ ||12||

13hatairmanuṣyāśvagajaiśca saṃkhye śarāpaviddhaiśca rathairnarendra
|
dhanañjayasyādhiratheśca mārgaṇairagamyarūpā vasudhā babhūva ||13||

14rathairvareṣūnmathitaiḥ sukalpaiḥ sayodhaśastraiśca varāyudhairdhvajaiḥ
|
viśīrṇayoktrairvinikṛtya bandhanairnikṛttacakrākṣayugatriveṇubhiḥ ||14||

15vimuktaśastraiśca tathā vyupaskarairhatānukarṣairviniṣaṅgabandhanaiḥ
|
prabhagnanīḍairmaṇihemabhūṣitaiḥ stṛtā mahī dyauriva śāradairghanaiḥ ||15||

16vikṛṣyamāṇairjavanaisturaṅgamairhateśvarai rājarathaiḥ sukalpitaiḥ
|
manuṣya-mātaṅga-rathāśvarāśibhirdrutaṃ vrajanto bahudhā vicūrṇitāḥ ||16||

17sahemapaṭṭāḥ parighāḥ paraśvadhāḥ śitāśca śūlā musalāni mudgarāḥ
|
petuśca khaḍgā vimalā vikośā gadāśca jāmbūnadapanaddhāḥ ||17||

18cāpāni rukmāṅgada‌bhūṣaṇāni śarāśca kārtasvaracitrapuṅkhāḥ
|
ṛṣṭyaśca pītā vimalā vikośāḥ prāsāśca daṇḍaiḥ kanakāvabhāsaiḥ ||18||

19chatrāṇi vālavyajanāni śaṅkhāśchinnāpaviddhāśca srajo vicitrāḥ
|
kuthāḥ patākāmbarabhūṣaṇāni kirīṭamālā mukuṭāśca śubhrāḥ ||19||

20prakīrṇakā viprakīrṇāśca rājan pravālamuktātaralāśca hārāḥ
|
āpīḍakeyūravarāṅgadāni graiveyaniṣkāḥ sasuvarṇasūtrāḥ ||20||

21maṇyuttamā vajrasuvarṇamuktā ratnāni coccāvacamaṅgalāni
|
gātrāṇi cātyantasukhocitāni śirāṃsi cendupratimānanāni ||21||

22dehāṃśca bhogāṃśca paricchadāṃśca tyaktvā manojñāni sukhāni caiva
|
svadharmaniṣṭhāṃ mahatīmavāpya vyāpyāśu lokān yaśasā gatāste ||22||

23nivarta duryodhana yāntu sainikā vrajasva rājañśibirāya mānada
|
divākaro'pyeṣa vilambate prabho punastvamevātra narendra kāraṇam ||23||

24ityevamuktvā virarāma śalyo duryodhanaṃ śokaparītacetāḥ
|
hā karṇa hā karṇa iti bruvāṇamārtaṃ visaṃjñaṃ bhṛśamaśrunetram ||24||

25taṃ droṇaputrapramukhā narendrāḥ sarve samāśvāsya muhuḥ prayānti
|
nirīkṣamāṇā muhurarjunasya dhvajaṃ mahāntaṃ yaśasā jvalantam ||25||

26narāśvamātaṅgaśarīrajena raktena siktāṃ ca tathaiva bhūmim
|
raktāmbarasraktapanīyayogānnārīṃ prakāśāmiva sarvagamyām ||26||

27pracchannarūpāṃ rudhireṇa rājanraudre muhūrte'tivirājamāne
|
naivāvatasthuḥ kuravaḥ samīkṣya pravrājitā devalokāya sarve ||27||

28vadhena karṇasya tu duḥkhitāste hā karṇa hā karṇa iti bruvāṇāḥ
|
drutaṃ prayātāḥ śibirāṇi rājandivākaraṃ raktamavekṣamāṇāḥ ||28||

29gāṇḍīvamuktaistu suvarṇapuṅkhaiḥ śilāśitaiḥ śoṇitadigdhavājaiḥ
|
śaraiścitāṅgo yudhi bhāti karṇo hato'pi sansurya ivāṃśumālī ||29||

30karṇasya dehaṃ rudhirāvasiktaṃ bhaktānukampī bhagavānvivasvān
|
spṛṣṭvāṃśubhirlohitaraktarūpaḥ siṣṇāsurabhyeti paraṃ samudram ||30||

31itīva sañcintya surarṣisaṅghāḥ saṃprasthitā yānti yathā niketanam
|
sañcintayitvā janatā visasruryathāsukhaṃ khaṃ ca mahītalaṃ ca ||31||

32tadadbhutaṃ prāṇabhṛtāṃ bhayaṃkaraṃ niśāmya yuddhaṃ kuruvīramukhyayoḥ
|
dhanañjayasyādhiratheśca vismitāḥ praśaṃsamānāḥ prayayustadā janāḥ ||32||

33śarasaṃkṛttavarmāṇaṃ rudhirokṣitavāsasam
|
gatāsumapi rādheyaṃ naiva lakṣmīrvimuñcati ||33||

34taptajāmbūnadanibhaṃ jvalanārkasamaprabham
|
jīvantamiva taṃ śūraṃ sarvabhūtāni menire ||34||

35hatasyāpi mahārāja sūtaputrasya saṃyuge
|
vitresuḥ sarvato yodhāḥ siṃhasyevetare mṛgāḥ ||35||

36hato'pi puruṣavyāghra jīvavāniva lakṣyate
|
nābhavadvikṛtiḥ kāciddhatasyāpi mahātmanaḥ ||36||

37cāruveṣadharaṃ vīraṃ cārumauliśirodharam
|
tanmukhaṃ sūtaputrasya pūrṇacandrasamadyuti ||37||

38nānābharaṇavānrājaṃstaptajāmbūnadāṅgadaḥ
|
hato vaikartanaḥ śete pā‌dapo'ṅkuravāniva ||38||

39kanakottamasaṃkāśo jvalanniva vibhāvasuḥ
|
sa śāntaḥ puruṣavyāghra pārthasāyakavāriṇā ||39||

40yathā hi jvalano dīpto jalamāsādya śāmyati
|
karṇāgniḥ samare tadvatpārthameghena śāmitaḥ ||40||

41āhatya ca yaśo dīptaṃ suyuddhenātmano bhuvi
|
visṛjya śaravarṣāṇi pratāpya ca diśo daśa ||41||

42saputraḥ samare karṇaḥ sa śāntaḥ pārthatejasā
|
pratāpya pāṇḍavānsarvānpañcālāṃścāstratejasā ||42||

43varṣitvā śaravarṣeṇa pratāpya ripuvāhinīm
|
śrīmāniva sahasrāṃśurjagatsarvaṃ pratāpya ca ||43||

44hato vaikartanaḥ karṇaḥ saputraḥ sahavāhanaḥ
|
arthināṃ pakṣisaṃghasya kalpavṛkṣo nipātitaḥ ||44||

45dadānītyeva yo'vocanna nāstītyarthito'rthibhiḥ
|
sadbhiḥ sadā satpuruṣaḥ sa hato dvairathe vṛṣaḥ ||45||

46yasya brāhmaṇasātsarvaṃ vittamāsīnmahātmanaḥ
|
nādeyaṃ brāhmaṇeṣvāsīdyasya svamapi jīvitam ||46||

47sadā strīṇāṃ priyo nityaṃ dātā caiva mahārathaḥ
|
sa vai pārthāstranirdagdho gataḥ paramikāṃ gatim ||47||

48yamāśrityākarodvairaṃ putraste sa gato divam
|
ādāya tava putrāṇāṃ jayāśāṃ śarma varma ca ||48||

49hate karṇe sarito na prasasrurjagāma cāstaṃ savitā divākaraḥ
|
grahaśca tiryakjvalanārkavarṇaḥ somasya putro'bhyudiyāya tiryak ||49||

50nabhaḥ paphāleva nanāda corvī vavuśca vātāḥ paruṣāḥ sughorāḥ
|
diśo babhūvurjvalitā sadhūmā mahārṇavāḥ sasvanuścukṣubhuśca ||50||

51sakānanāścādricayāścakampire pravivyathurbhūtagaṇāśca sarve
|
bṛhaspatiḥ saṃparivārya rohiṇīṃ babhūva candrārkasamo viśāṃpate ||51||

52hate tu karṇe vidiśo'pi jajvalustamovṛtā dyaurvicacāla bhūmiḥ
|
papāta colkā jvalanaprakāśā niśācarāścāpyabhavan prahṛṣṭāḥ ||52||

53śaśiprakāśānanamarjuno yadā kṣureṇa karṇasya śiro nyapātayat
|
tadā'ntarikṣe sahasaiva śabdo babhūva hāheti surairvimuktaḥ ||53||

54sadevagandharvamanuṣyapūjitaṃ nihatya karṇaṃ ripumāhave'rjunaḥ
|
rarāja rājan parameṇa varcesā yathā purā vṛtravadhe śatakratuḥ ||54||

55tato rathenāmbudavṛndanādinā śarannabhomadhyadivākarārciṣā
|
patākinā bhīmaninādaketunā himenduśaṅkhasphaṭikāvabhāsinā ||55||

56mahendravāhapratimena tāvubhau mahendravīryapratimānapauruṣau
|
suvarṇamuktāmaṇivajravidrumairalaṃkṛtāvapratimena raṃhasā ||56||

57narottamau keśavapāṇḍunandanau tadāhitāvagnidivākarāviva
|
raṇājire vītabhayau virejatuḥ samānayānāviva viṣṇuvāsavau ||57||

58tato dhanurjyātalabāṇaniḥsvanaiḥ prasahya kṛtvā ca ripūn hataprabhān
|
saṃchādayitvā tu kurūn śarottamaiḥ kapidhvajaḥ pakṣivaradhvajaśca ||58||

59hṛṣṭau tatastāvamitaprabhāvau manāṃsyarīṇāmavadārayantau
|
suvarṇajālāvatatau mahāsvanau himāvadātau parigṛhya pāṇibhiḥ
|
cucumbatuḥ śaṅkhavarau nṛṇāṃ varau varānanābhyāṃ yugapacca dadhmatuḥ ||59||

60pāñcajanyasya nirghoṣo devadattasya cobhayoḥ
|
pṛthivīṃ cāntarikṣaṃ ca diśaścaivānvanādayat ||60||

61vitrastāścābhavansarve kauravā rājasattama
|
śaṅkhaśabdena tenātha mādhavasyārjunasya ca ||61||

62tau śaṅkhaśabdena ninādayantau vanāni śailānsarito guhāśca
|
vitrāsayantau tava putrasenāṃ yudhiṣṭhiraṃ nandayatāṃ variṣṭhau ||62||

63tataḥ prayātāḥ kuravo javena śrutvaiva śaṅkhasvanamīryamāṇam
|
vihāya madrādhipatiṃ patiṃ ca duryodhanaṃ bhārata bhāratānām ||63||

64mahāhave taṃ bahu rocamānaṃ dhanañjayaṃ bhūtagaṇāḥ sametāḥ
|
tadā'nvamodanta janārdanaṃ ca divākarāvabhyuditau yathaiva ||64||

65samācitau karṇaśaraiḥ parantapāvubhau vyabhātāṃ samare'cyutārjunau
|
tamo nihatyābhyuditau yathāmalau śaśāṅkasūryai divi raśmimālinau ||65||

66vihāya tān bāṇagaṇānathāgatau suhṛdvṛtāvapratimānavikramau
|
sukhaṃ praviṣṭau śibiraṃ svamīśvarau sadasyahūtāviva viṣṇuvāsavau ||66||

67tau deva-gandharva-manuṣya-cāraṇairmaharṣibhiryakṣamahoragairapi
|
jayābhivṛddhyā parayā'bhipūjitau hate tu karṇe paramāhave tadā ||67||

68yathānurūpaṃ pratipūjitāvubhau praśasyamānau svakṛtairguṇaughaiḥ
|
nanandatustau sasuhṛdgaṇau tadā balaṃ niyamyeva sureśakeśavau ||68||
