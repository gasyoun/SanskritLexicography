Mahābhārata 6.12 (Bhīṣmaparvan)

1sañjaya uvāca|
uttareṣu ca kauravya dvīpeṣu śrūyate kathā
|
evaṃ tatra mahārāja bruvataśca nibodha me ||1||

2ghṛtatoyaḥ samudro'tra dadhimaṇḍodako'paraḥ
|
surodaḥ sāgaraścaiva tathānyo jalasāgaraḥ ||2||

3paraspareṇa dviguṇāḥ sarve dvīpā narādhipa
|
parvatāśca mahārāja samudraiḥ parivāritāḥ ||3||

4gaurastu madhyame dvīpe girirmānaḥśilo mahān
|
parvataḥ paścime kṛṣṇo nārāyaṇasakho nṛpa ||4||

5tatra ratnāni divyāni svayaṃ rakṣati keśavaḥ
|
prasannaścābhavattatra prajānāṃ vyadadhatsukham ||5||

6kuśastambaḥ kuśadvīpe madhye janapadaiḥ saha
|
sampūjyate śālmaliśca dvīpe śālmalike nṛpa ||6||

7krauñcadvīpe mahākrauñco girī ratnacayākaraḥ
|
sampūjyate mahārāja cāturvarṇyena nityadā ||7||

8gomantaḥ parvato rājansumahānsarvadhātukaḥ
|
yatra nityaṃ nivasati śrīmānkamalalocanaḥ ||8||

9mokṣibhiḥ saṅgato nityaṃ prabhurnārāyaṇo hariḥ
|
kuśadvīpe tu rājendra parvato vidrumaiścitaḥ ||9||

10sunāmanāmā durdharṣo dvitīyo hemaparvataḥ
|
dyutimānnāma kauravya tṛtīyaḥ kumudo giriḥ ||10||

11caturthaḥ puṣpavānnāma pañcamastu kuśeśayaḥ
|
ṣaṣṭho harigirirnāma ṣaḍete parvatottamāḥ ||11||

12teṣāmantaraviṣkambho dviguṇaḥ sarvabhāgaśaḥ
|
audbhidaṃ prathamaṃ varṣaṃ dvitīyaṃ veṇumaṇḍalam ||12||

13tṛtīyaṃ surathākāraṃ caturthaṃ kambalaṃ smṛtam
|
dhṛtimatpañcamaṃ varṣaṃ ṣaṣṭhaṃ prabhākaram ||13||

14saptamaṃ kāpilaṃ varṣaṃ saptaite varṣalambhakāḥ
|
eteṣu devagandharvāḥ prajāśca jagatīśvara ||14||

15viharante ramante ca na teṣu mriyate janaḥ
|
na teṣu dasyavaḥ santi mlecchajātyopi vā nṛpa ||15||

16gauraprāyo janaḥ sarvaḥ sukumāraśca pārthiva
|
avaśiṣṭeṣu sarveṣu vakṣyāmi manujeśvara ||16||

17yathāśrutaṃ mahārāja tadavyagramanāḥ śrṛṇu
|
krauñcadvīpe mahārāja krauñco nāma mahāgiriḥ ||17||

18krauñcātparo vāmanako vāmanādandhakārakaḥ
|
andhakārātparo rājanmainākaḥ parvatottamaḥ ||18||

19mainākātparato rādangovindo giriruttamaḥ
|
govindātparato rājannibiḍo nāma parvataḥ ||19||

20parastu dviguṇasteṣāṃ viṣkambho vaṃśavardhana
|
deśāṃstatra pravakṣyāmi tanme nigadataḥ śrṛṇu ||20||

21kroñcasya kuśalo deśo vāmanasya manonugaḥ
|
manonugātparaścoṣṇo deśaḥ kurukulodvaha ||21||

22uṣṇātparaḥ prāvarakaḥ prāvārādandhakārakaḥ
|
andhakārakadeśāttu munideśaḥ paraḥ smṛtaḥ ||22||

23munideśātparaścaiva procyate dundubhisvanaḥ
|
siddhacāraṇasaṅkīrṇo gauraprāyo janādhipa ||23||

24ete deśā mahārāja devagandharvasevitāḥ
|
puṣkare puṣkaro nāma parvato maṇiratnavān ||24||

25tatra nityaṃ prabhavati svayaṃ devaḥ prajāpatiḥ
|
taṃ paryupāsate nityaṃ devāḥ sarve maharṣayaḥ ||25||

26vāgbhirmanonukūlābhiḥ pūjayanto janādhipa
|
jambūdvīpātpravartante ratnāni vividhānyuta ||26||

27dvīpeṣu teṣu sarveṣu prajānāṃ kurusattama
|
brahmacaryeṇa satyena prajānāṃ hi damena ca ||27||

28ārogyāyuḥpramāṇābhyāṃ dviguṇaṃ dviguṇaṃ tataḥ
|
eko janapado rājandvīpeṣveteṣu bhārata
|
uktā janapadā yeṣu dharmaścaikaḥ pradṛśyate ||28||

29īśvaro daṇḍamudyamya svayameva prajāpatiḥ
|
dvīpānetānmahārāja rakṣaṃstiṣṭhati nityadā ||29||

30sa rājā sa śivo rājansa pitā pratitāmahaḥ
|
gopāyati naraśreṣṭha prajāḥ sajaḍapaṇḍitāḥ ||30||

31bhojanaṃ cātra kauravya prajāḥ svayamupasthitam
|
siddhameva mahābāho taddhi bhuñjanti nityadā ||31||

32tataḥ paraṃ samā nāma dṛśyate lokasaṃsthitiḥ
|
caturaśraṃ mahārāja trayastriṃśattu maṇḍalam ||32||

33tatra tiṣṭhanti kauravya catvāro lokasammatāḥ
|
diggajā bharataśreṣṭha vāmanairāvatādayaḥ ||33||

34supratīkastadā rājanprabhinnakaraṭāmukhaḥ
|
tasyāhaṃ parimāṇaṃ tu na saṅkhyātumihotsahe ||34||

35asaṅkhyātaḥ sa nityaṃ hi tiryagūrdhvamadhastathā
|
tatra vai vāyavo vānti digbhyaḥ sarvābhya eva hi ||35||

36asambaddhā mahārāja tānnigṛhṇanti te gajāḥ
|
puṣkaraiḥ padmasaṅkāśairvikasadbhirmahāprabhaiḥ ||36||

37śatadhā punarevāśu te tānmuñcanti nityaśaḥ
|
śvasadbhirmucyamānāstu diggajairiha mārutāḥ ||37||

38āgacchanti mahārāja tatastiṣṭhanti vai prajāḥ
|
dhṛtarāṣṭra uvāca|
paro vai vistaro'tyarthaṃ tvayā sañjaya kīrtitaḥ ||38||

39darśitaṃ dvīpasaṃsthānamuttaraṃ brūhi sañjaya
|
sañjaya uvāca|
uktā dvīpā mahārāja grahaṃ vai śṛṇu tattvataḥ ||39||

40svarbhānoḥ kauravaśreṣṭha yāvadeva pramāṇataḥ
|
parimaṇḍalo mahārāja svarbhānuḥ śrūyate grahaḥ ||40||

41yojanānāṃ sahasrāṇi viṣkambho dvādaśāsya vai
|
pariṇāhena ṣaṭtriṃśadvipulatvena cānagha ||41||

42ṣaṣṭimāhuḥ śatānyasya budhāḥ paurāṇikāstathā
|
candramāstu sahasrāṇi rājannekādaśa smṛtaḥ ||42||

43viṣkambheṇa kuruśreṣṭha trayastriṃśattu maṇḍalam || ekonaṣaṣṭiviṣkambhaṃ śītaraśmermahātmanaḥ ||43||

44sūryastvaṣṭau sahasrāṇi dve cānye kurunandana
|
viṣkambheṇa tato rājanmaṇḍalaṃ triṃśatā samam ||44||

45aṣṭapañcāśataṃ rājanvipulatvena cānagha
|
śrūyate paramodāraḥ patago'sau vibhāvasuḥ ||45||

46etatpramāṇamarkasya nirdiṣṭamiha bhārata
|
sa rāhuśchādayatyetau yathākālaṃ mahattayā ||46||

47candrādityau mahārāja saṅkṣepo'yamudāhṛtaḥ
|
ityetatte mahārāja pṛcchataḥ śāstracakṣuṣā ||47||

48sarvamuktaṃ yathātattvaṃ tasmācchamamavāpnuhi
|
yathoddiṣṭaṃ mayā proktaṃ sanirmāṇamidaṃ jagat ||48||

49tasmādāśvasa kauravya putraṃ duryodhanaṃ prati
|
śrutvedaṃ bharataśreṣṭha bhūmiparva manonugam ||49||

50śrīmānbhavati rājanyaḥ siddhārthaḥ sādhusammataḥ
|
āyurbalaṃ ca kīrtiśca tasya tejaśca vardhate ||50||

51yaḥ śṛṇoti mahīpāla parvaṇīdaṃ yatavrataḥ
|
prīyante pitarastasya tathaiva ca pitāmahāḥ ||51||

52idaṃ tu bhārataṃ varṣaṃ yatra vartāmahe vayam
|
pūrvaiḥ pravartitaṃ puṇyaṃ tatsarvaṃ śrutavānasi ||52||
