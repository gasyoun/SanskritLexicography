Mahābhārata 7.116 (Droṇaparvan)

1sañjaya uvāca|
te kirantaḥ śaravrātān sarve yattāḥ prahāriṇaḥ
|
tvaramāṇā mahārāja yuyudhānamayodhayan||1||

2taṃ droṇaḥ sapta saptatyā jaghāna niśitaiḥ śaraiḥ
|
durmarṣaṇo dvādaśabhirduḥsaho daśabhiḥ śaraiḥ||2||

3vikarṇaścāpi niśitaistriṃśadbhiḥ kaṅkapatribhiḥ
|
vivyādha savye pārśve tu stanābhyāmantare tathā||3||

4durmukho daśabhirbāṇaistathā duḥśāsano'ṣṭabhiḥ
|
citrasenaśca śaineyaṃ dvābhyāṃ vivyādha māriṣa||4||

5duryodhanaśca mahatā śaravarṣeṇa mādhavam
|
apīḍayadraṇe rājañśūrāścānye mahārathāḥ||5||

6sarvataḥ pratividdhastu tava putrairmahārathaiḥ
|
tān pratyavidhyadvārṣṇeyaḥ pṛthak pṛthagajihmagaiḥ||6||

7bhāradvājaṃ tribhirbāṇairduḥsahaṃ navabhiḥ śaraiḥ
|
vikarṇaṃ pañcaviṃśatyā citrasenaṃ ca saptabhiḥ||7||

8durmarṣaṇaṃ dvādaśabhiraṣṭābhiśca viviṃśatim
|
satyavrataṃ ca navabhirvijayaṃ daśabhiḥ śaraiḥ||8||

9tato rukmāṃgadaṃ cāpaṃ vidhunvāno mahārathaḥ
|
abhyayāt sātyakistūrṇaṃ putraṃ tava mahāratham||9||

10rājānaṃ sarvalokasya sarvalokamahāratham
|
śarairabhyāhanadgāḍhaṃ tato yuddhamabhūttayoḥ ||10||

11vimuñcantau śarāṃstīkṣṇān sandadhānau ca sāyakān
|
adṛśyaṃ samare'nyonyaṃ cakratustau mahārathau ||11||

12sātyakiḥ kururājena nirviddho bahvaśobhata
|
asravadrudhiraṃ bhūri svarasaṃ canda‌no yathā ||12||

13sātvatena ca bāṇaughairnirviddhastanayastava
|
śātakuṃbhamayāpīḍo babhau yūpa ivocchritaḥ||13||

14mādhavastu raṇe rājan kururājasya dhanvinaḥ
|
dhanuściccheda samare kṣurapreṇa hasanniva ||14||

15athainaṃ chinnadhanvānaṃ śarairbahubhirācinot
|
nirbhinnaśca śaraistena dviṣatā kṣiprakāriṇā ||15||

16nāmṛṣyata raṇe rājā śatrorvijayalakṣaṇam
|
athānyaddhanurādāya hemapṛṣṭhaṃ durāsadam ||16||

17vivyādha sātyakiṃ tūrṇaṃ sāyakānāṃ śatena ha
|
so'tividdho balavatā tava putreṇa dhanvinā ||17||

18amarṣavaśamāpannastava putramapīḍayat
|
pīḍitaṃ nṛpatiṃ dṛṣṭvā tava putrā mahārathāḥ||18||

19sātyakiṃ śaravarṣeṇa chāda‌yāmāsurojasā
|
sa cchādyamāno bahubhistava putrairmahārathaiḥ ||19||

20ekaikaṃ pañcabhirviddhvā punarvivyādha saptabhiḥ
|
duryodhanaṃ ca tvarito vivyādhāṣṭabhirāśugaiḥ ||20||

21prahasaṃścāsya ciccheda kārmukaṃ ripubhīṣaṇam
|
nāgaṃ maṇimayaṃ caiva śarairdhvajamapātayat ||21||

22hatvā tu caturo vāhāṃścaturbhirniśitaiḥ śaraiḥ
|
sārathiṃ pātayāmāsa kṣurapreṇa mahāyaśāḥ||22||

23etasminnantare caiva kururājaṃ mahāratham
|
avākiraccharairhṛṣṭo bahubhirmarmabhedibhiḥ||23||

24sa vadhyamānaḥ samare śaineyasya śarottamaiḥ
|
prādravat sahasā rājan putro duryodhanastava||24||

25āplutaśca tato yānaṃ citrasenasya dhanvinaḥ
|
hāhābhūtaṃ jagaccāsīd dṛṣṭvā rājānamāhave ||25||

26grasyamānaṃ sātyakinā khe somamiva rāhuṇā
|
taṃ tu śabdamatha śrutvā kṛtavarmā mahārathaḥ ||26||

27abhyayāt sahasā tatra yatrāste mādhavaḥ prabhuḥ
|
vidhunvāno dhanuḥ śreṣṭhaṃ codayaṃścaiva vājinaḥ||27||

28bhartsayan‌ sārāthiṃ cāgre yāhi yāhīti satvaram
|
tamāpatantaṃ saṃprekṣya vyāditāsyamivāntakam||28||

29yuyudhāno mahārāja yantāramidamabravīt
|
kṛtavarmā rathenaiṣa drutamāpatate śarī ||29||

30pratyudyāhi rathenainaṃ pravaraṃ sarvadhanvinām
|
tataḥ prajavitāśvena vidhivat kalpitena ca ||30||

31āsasāda raṇe bhojaṃ pratimānaṃ dhanuṣmatām
|
tataḥ paramasaṃkruddhau jvalitāviva pāvakau ||31||

32sameyātāṃ naravyāghrau vyāghrāviva tarasvinau
|
kṛtavarmā tu śaineyaṃ ṣaḍviṃśatyā samārpayat ||32||

33niśitaiḥ sāyakaistīkṣṇairyantāraṃ cāsya pañcabhiḥ
|
caturaścaturo vāhāṃścaturbhiḥ parameṣubhiḥ||33||

34avidhyat sādhudāntān ve saindhavān sātvatasya hi
|
rukmadhvajo rukmapṛṣṭhaṃ mahadvisphārya kārmukam ||34||

35rukmāṅgadī rukmavarmā rukmapuṃkhairavārayat
|
tato'śītiṃ śineḥ pautraḥ sāyakān‌ kṛtavarmaṇe||35||

36prāhiṇottvarayā yuktā draṣṭukāmo dhanañjayam
|
so'tividdho balavatā śatruṇā śatrutāpanaḥ||36||

37samakampata durdharṣaḥ kṣitikaṃpe yathā'calaḥ
|
triṣaṣṭyā caturo'syāśvān saptabhiḥ sārathiṃ tathā ||37||

38vivyādha niśitaistūrṇaṃ sātyakiḥ satyavikramaḥ
|
suvarṇapuṃkhaṃ viśikhaṃ samādhāya ca sātyakiḥ||38||

39vyasṛjattaṃ mahājvālaṃ saṃkruddhamiva pannagam
|
so'vidhyat kṛtavarmāṇaṃ yamadaṇḍopamaḥ śaraḥ ||39||

40jāṃbūnadavicitraṃ ca varma nirbhidya bhānumat
|
abhyagāddharaṇīmugro rudhireṇa samukṣitaḥ||40||

41saṃjātarudhiraścājau sātvateṣubhirarditaḥ
|
saśaraṃ dhanurutsṛjya nyapatat syandanottamāt||41||

42sa siṃhadaṃṣṭro jānubhyāṃ patito'mitavikramaḥ
|
śarārditaḥ sātyakinā rathopasthe nararṣabhaḥ ||42||

43sahasrabāhusadṛśamakṣobhyamiva sāgaram
|
nivārya kṛtavarmāṇaṃ sātyakiḥ prayayau tataḥ||43||

44khaḍgaśaktidhanuḥkīrṇāṃ gajāśvarathasaṃkulām
|
pravartitograrudhirāṃ śataśaḥ kṣatriyarṣabhaiḥ ||44||

45prekṣatāṃ sarvasainyānāṃ madhyena śinipuṅgavaḥ
|
abhyagādvāhinīṃ hitvā vṛtrahevāsurīṃ camūm||45||

46samāśvasya ca hārdikyo gṛhya cānyanmahaddhanuḥ
|
tasthau sa tatra balavānvārayanyudhi pāṇḍavān||46||
