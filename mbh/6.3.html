Mahābhārata 6.3 (Bhīṣmaparvan)

1vyāsa uvāca|
kharā goṣu prajāyante ramante mātṛbhiḥ sutāḥ
|
anārtavaṃ puṣpaphalaṃ darśayanti vanadrumāḥ ||1||

2garbhiṇyo'jātaputrāśca janayanti vibhīṣaṇān
|
kravyādāḥ pakṣibhiścāpi sahāśnanti parasparam ||2||

3triviṣāṇāścaturnetrāḥ pañcapādā dvimehanāḥ
|
dviśīrṣāśca dvipucchāśca daṃṣṭriṇaḥ paśavo'śivāḥ ||3||

4jāyante vivṛtāsyāśca vyāharanto'śivā giraḥ
|
tripadāḥ śikhinastārkṣyāścaturdaṃṣṭrā viṣāṇinaḥ ||4||

5tathaivānyāśca dṛśyante striyo vai brahmavādinām
|
vainateyānmayūrāṃśca janayanti pure tava ||5||

6govatsaṃ vaḍavā sūte śvā sṛgālaṃ mahīpate
|
kukkurānkarabhāścaiva śukāścāśubhavādinaḥ ||6||

7striyaḥ kāścitprajāyante catasraḥ pañca kanyakāḥ
|
jātamātrāśca nṛtyanti gāyanta ca hasanti ca ||7||

8pṛthagjanasya sarvasya kṣudrakāḥ prahasanti ca
|
nṛtyanti parigāyanti vedayanto mahadbhayam ||8||

9pratimāścālikhantyetāḥ saśastrāḥ kālacoditāḥ
|
anyonyamabhidhāvanti śiśavo daṇḍapāṇayaḥ ||9||

10anyonyamabhimṛdnanti nagarāṇi yuyutsavaḥ
|
padmotpalāni vṛkṣeṣu jāyante kumudāni ca ||10||

11viṣvagvātāśca vāntyugro rajo nāpyupaśāmyati
|
abhīkṣṇaṃ vartate bhūmirarkaṃ rāhurupaiti ca ||11||

12śveto grahastathā citrāṃ samatikramya tiṣṭhati
|
abhāvaṃ hi viśeṣeṇa kurūṇāṃ tatra paśyati ||12||

13dhūmaketurmahāghoraḥ puṣyaṃ cākramya tiṣṭhati
|
senayoraśivaṃ ghoraṃ kariṣyati mahāgrahaḥ ||13||

14maghāsvaṅgārako vakraḥ śravaṇe ca bṛhaspatiḥ
|
bhagaṃ nakṣatramākramya sūryaputreṇa pīḍyate ||14||

15śukraḥ proṣṭhapade pūrve samāruhya virocate
|
uttare tu parikramya sahitaḥ samudīkṣate ||15||

16śveto grahaḥ prajvalitaḥ sadhūma iva pāvakaḥ
|
aindraṃ tejasvi nakṣatraṃ jyeṣṭhāmākramya tiṣṭhati ||16||

17dhruvaṃ prajvalito ghoramapasavyaṃ pravartate
|
rohiṇīṃ pīḍayatyevamubhau ca śaśibhāskarau
|
citrāsvātyantare caiva viṣṭhitaḥ paruṣagrahaḥ ||17||

18vakrānuvakraṃ kṛtvā ca śravaṇaṃ pāvakaprabhaḥ
|
brahmarāśiṃ samāvṛtya lohitāṅgo vyavasthitaḥ ||18||

19sarvasasyaparicchannā pṛthivī sasyamālinī
|
pañcaśīrṣā yavāścāpi śataśīrṣāśca śālayaḥ ||19||

20pradhānāḥ sarvalokasya yāsvāyattamidaṃ jagat
|
tā gāvaḥ prasnutā vatsaiḥ śoṇitaṃ prakṣarantyuta ||20||

21niścerurarciṣaścāpātsvaṅgāśca jvalitā bhṛśam
|
vyaktaṃ paśyanti śastrāṇi saṃgaṅrāmaṃ samupasthitam ||21||

22agnivarṇā yathā bhāsaḥ śastrāṇāmudakasya ca
|
kavacānāṃ dhvajānāṃ ca bhaviṣyati mahākṣayaḥ ||22||

23pṛthivī śoṇitāvartā dhvajoḍupasamākulā
|
kurūṇāṃ vaiśase rājanpāṇḍavaiḥ saha bhārata ||23||

24dikṣu prajvalitāsyāśca vyāharanti mṛgadvijāḥ
|
atyāhitaṃ darśayantaḥ kṣatriyāṇāṃ mahadbhayam ||24||

25ekapakṣākṣicaraṇaḥ śakuniḥ svacaro niśi
|
raudraṃ vadanti saṃrabdhaḥ śoṇitaṃ chardayanniva ||25||

26śastrāṇi caiva rājendra prajvalantīva samprati
|
saptarṣīṇāmudārāṇāṃ samavacchādyate prabhā ||26||

27saṃvatsarasthāyinau ca grahau prajvalitāvubhau
|
viśākhāyāḥ samīpasthau bṛhaspatiśanaiścarau ||27||

28candrādityāvubhau grastāvekāhnā hi trayodaśīm
|
aparvami grahaṃ yātau prajāsaṃkṣayamicchataḥ ||28||

29aśobhitā diśaḥ sarvāḥ pāṃsuvarṣaiḥ samantataḥ
|
utpātameghā raudrāśca rātrau varṣanti śoṇitam ||29||

30kṛttikāṃ pīḍayaṃstīkṣṇairnakṣatraṃ pṛthivīpate
|
abhīkṣṇavātā vāyante dhūmaketumavasthitāḥ ||30||

31viṣamaṃ janayantyeta ākrandajananaṃ mahat
|
triṣu sarveṣu nakṣatranakṣatreṣu viśāmpate
|
gṛdhraḥ sampatate śīrṣaṃ janayanbhayamuttamam ||31||

32caturdaśīṃ pañcadaśīṃ bhūtapūrvāṃ ca ṣoḍaśīm
|
imāṃ nābhijāne'hamamāvāsyāṃ trayodaśīm
|
candrasūryāvubhau grastāvekamāsīṃ trayodaśīm ||32||

33aparvaṇi graheṇaitau prajāḥ saṃkṣapayiṣyataḥ
|
māṃsavarṣaṃ punastīvramāsītkṛṣṇacaturdaśīm
|
śoṇitairvaktrasampūrṇā atṛptāstatra rākṣasāḥ ||33||

34pratisroto mahānadyaḥ saritaḥ śoṇitodakāḥ
|
phenāyamānāḥ kūpāśca kūrdanti vṛṣabhā iva ||34||

35patantyulkāḥ sanirghātāḥ śakrāśanisamaprabhāḥ
|
adya caiva niśāṃ vyuṣṭāmanayaṃ samavāpsyatha ||35||

36viniḥsṛtya maholkābhistimiraṃ sarvatodiśam
|
anyonyamupatiṣṭhadbhistatra bhūmiḥ pāsyati śoṇitam ||36||

37bhūmipālasahasrāṇāṃ bhūmiḥ pāsyati śoṇitam
|
phailāsamandarābhyāṃ tu tathā himavatā vibho ||37||

38sahasraśo mahāśabdaḥ śikharāṇi patanti ca
|
mahābhūtā bhūmikampe catvāraḥ sāgarāḥ pṛthak
|
velāmudvartayantīva kṣobhayanto vasundharām ||38||

39vṛkṣānunmathya vāntyugrā vātāḥ śarkarakarṣiṇaḥ
|
ābhagnāḥ sumahāvātairaśanībhiḥ samāhatāḥ ||39||

40vṛkṣāḥ patanti caityāśca grāmeṣu nagareṣu ca
|
nīlalohitapītaśca bhavatyagnirhuto dvijaiḥ ||40||

41vāmārcirduṣṭagandhaśca muñcanvai dāruṇaṃ svanam
|
sparśā gandhā rasāścaiva viparītā mahīpate ||41||

42dhūmaṃ dhvajāḥ pramuñcanti kampamānā muhurmuhuḥ
|
muñcantyaṅgāravarṣaṃ ca bheryaśca paṭahāstathā ||42||

43śikharāṇāṃ samṛddhānāmupariṣṭātsamantataḥ
|
vāyasāśca ruvantyugraṃ vāmaṃ maṇḍalamāśritāḥ ||43||

44pakvāpakveti subhṛśaṃ vāvāśyante vayāṃsi ca
|
nilīyante dhvajāgreṣu kṣayāya pṛthivīkṣitām ||44||

45dhyāyantaḥ prakirantaśca vyālā vepathusaṃyutāḥ
|
dīnāsturaṅgamāḥ sarve vāraṇāḥ salilāśrayāḥ ||45||

46etacchrutvā bhavānatra prāptakālaṃ vyavasyatām
|
yathā lokaḥ samucchedaṃ nāyaṃ gaccheta bhārata ||46||

47vaiśampāyana uvāca|
piturvaco niśamyaitaddhṛtarāṣṭro'bravīdidam
|
diṣṭametatpurā manye bhaviṣyati narakṣayaḥ ||47||

48rājānaḥ kṣatradharmeṇa yadi vadhyanti saṃyuge
|
vīralokaṃ samāsādya sukhaṃ prāpsyanti kevalam ||48||

49iha kīrtiṃ pare loke dīrghakālaṃ mahatsukham
|
prāpsyanti puruṣavyāghrāḥ prāṇāṃstyaktvā mahāhave ||49||

50vaiśampāyana uvāca|
evaṃ munistathetyuktvā kavīndro rājasattama
|
dhṛtarāṣṭreṇa putreṇa dhyānamanvagamatparam ||50||

51sa muhūrtaṃ tathā dhyātvā punarevābravīdvacaḥ
|
asaṃśayaṃ pārthivendra kālaḥ saṃkṣayate jagat ||51||

52sṛjate ca punarlokānneha vidyati śāśvatam
|
jñātīnāṃ vai kurūṇāṃ ca sambandhisuhṛdāṃ tathā ||52||

53dharmyaṃ deśaya panthānaṃ samartho hyasi vāraṇe
|
kṣudraṃ jātivadhaṃ prāhurmā kuruṣva mamāpriyam ||53||

54kālo'yaṃ putrarūpeṇa tava jāto viśāmpate
|
na vadhaḥ pūjyate vede hitaṃ naiva kathañcana ||54||

55hanyātsa enaṃ yo hanyātkuladharmaṃ svikāṃ tanum
|
kālenotpathagantā'si śakye sati yathā''padi ||55||

56kulasyāsya vināśāya tathaiva ca mahīkṣitām
|
anartho rājyarūpeṇa tava jāto viśāmpate ||56||

57luptadharmā pareṇāsi dharmaṃ darśaya vai sutān
|
kiṃ te rājyena durdha rṣayena prāpto'si kilbiṣam ||57||

58yaśo dharmaṃ ca kīrtiṃ ca pālayansvargamāpsyasi
|
labhantāṃ pāṇḍavā rājyaṃ śamaṃ gacchantu kauravāḥ ||58||

59evaṃ bruvati viprendre dhṛtarāṣṭro'mbokāsutaḥ
|
ākṣipya vākyaṃ vākyajño vākyaṃ caivābravīt punaḥ ||59||

60dhṛtarāṣṭra uvāca|
yathā bhavānvetti tathaiva vettā bhāvābhāvau viditau me yathārthau
|
svārthe hi saṃmuhyati tāta loko māṃ cāpi lokātmakameva viddhi ||60||

61prasādaye tvāmatulaprabhāvaṃ tvaṃ no gatirdarśayitā ca dhīraḥ
|
na cāpi te madvaśagā maharṣe na cādharmaṃ kartumarhā hi me matiḥ ||61||

62tvaṃ hi dharmapravṛttiśca yaśaḥ kīrtiśca bhāratī
|
kurūṇāṃ pāṇḍavānāṃ ca mānyaścāpi pitāmahaḥ ||62||

63vyāsa uvāca|
vaicitravīrya nṛpate yatte manasi vartate
|
abhidhatsva yathākāmaṃ chettā'smi tava saṃśayam ||63||

64dhṛtarāṣṭra uvāca|
yāni liṅgāni saṅgrāme bhavanti vijayiṣyatām
|
tāni sarvāṇi bhagavañchrotumicchāmi tattvataḥ ||64||

65vyāsa uvāca|
prasannabhāḥ pāvaka ūrdhvaraśmiḥ pradakṣiṇāvartaśokho vidhūmaḥ
|
puṇyā gandhāśvāhutīnāṃ pravanti jayasyaitadbhāvino rūpamāhuḥ ||65||

66gambhīraghoṣāśca mahāsvanāśca śaṅkhā mṛdaṅgāśca nadanti yatra
|
viśuddharaśmistapanaḥ śaśī ca jayasyetadbhāvino rūpamāhuḥ ||66||

67iṣṭā vācaḥ prasṛtā vāyasānāṃ samprasthitānāṃ ca gamiṣyatāṃ ca
|
ye pṛṣṭhataste tvarayanti rājan ye cāgrataste pratiṣedhayanti ||67||

68kalyāṇavācaḥ śakunā rājahaṃsāḥ śukāḥ krauñcāḥ śatapatrāśca yatra
|
pradakṣiṇāścaiva bhavanti saṅkhye dhruvaṃ jayastatra vadanti viprāḥ ||68||

69alaṅkāraiḥ kavacaiḥ ketubhiśca sukhapraṇādairheṣitairvā hayānām
|
bhrājiṣmatī duṣprativīkṣaṇīyā yeṣāṃ camūste vijayanti śatrūn ||69||

70hṛṣṭā vācastathā satvaṃ yodhānāṃ yatra bhārata
|
na mlāyanti srajaścaiva te taranti raṇodadhim ||70||

71iṣṭā vācaḥ praviṣṭasya dakṣiṇāḥ pravivikṣataḥ
|
paścāt sandhārayantyarthamāgre ca pratiṣedhikāḥ ||71||

72śabdarūparasasparśagandhāścāvikṛtāḥ śubhāḥ
|
sadā harṣaśca yodhānāṃ jayatāmiha lakṣaṇam ||72||

73anugā vāyavo vānti tathā'bhrāṇi vayāṃsi ca
|
anuplavanti meghāśca tathaivendradhanūṃṣi ca ||73||

74etāni jayamānānāṃ lakṣaṇāni viśāmpate
|
bhavanti viparītāni mumūrṣūṇāṃ janādhipa ||74||

75alpāyāṃ vā mahatyāṃ vā senāyāmiti niścayaḥ
|
harṣo yodhagaṇasyaiko jayalakṣaṇamucyate ||75||

76eko dīrṇo dārayati senāṃ sumahatīmapi
|
tāṃ dīrṇāmanudīryante yodhāḥ śūratarā api ||76||

77durnivartyā tadā caiva prabhagnā mahatī camūḥ
|
apāmiva mahāvegāstrastā mṛgagaṇā iva ||77||

78naiva śakyā samādhātuṃ sannipāte mahācamūḥ
|
dīrṇāmityeva dīryante suvidvāṃso'pi bhārata ||78||

79bhītān bhagnāṃśca samprekṣya bhayaṃ bhūyo'bhivardhate
|
prabhagnā sahasā rājandiśo vidravate camūḥ ||79||

80naiva sthāpayituṃ śakyā śūrairapi mahācamūḥ
|
satkṛtya mahatīṃ senāṃ caturaṅgāṃ mahīpatiḥ
|
upāyapūrvaṃ medhāvī yateta satatotthitaḥ ||80||

81upāyavijayaṃ śreṣṭhamāhurbhedena madhyamam
|
jaghanya eṣa vijayo yo yuddhena viśāmpate ||81||

82mahāndoṣaḥ sannipātastasyādyaḥ kṣaya ucyate
|
parasparajñāḥ saṃhṛṣṭā vyavadhūtāḥ suniścitāḥ ||82||

83api pañcāśataṃ śūrā mṛdnānti mahatīṃ camūm
|
api vā pañca ṣaṭ sapta vijayantyanivartinaḥ ||83||

84na vainateyo garuḍaḥ praśaṃsati mahājanam
|
dṛṣṭvā suparṇo'pacitiṃ mahatyā api bhārata ||84||

85na bāhulyena senāyā jayo bhavati nityaśaḥ
|
adhruvo hi jayo nāma daivaṃ cātra parāyaṇam
|
jayavanto hi saṅgrāme kṛtakṛtyā bhavanti hi ||85||
