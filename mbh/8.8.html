Mahābhārata 8.8 (Karṇaparvan)

1janamejaya uvāca|
śrutvā karṇaṃ hataṃ yuddhe putrāṃścaiva nipātitān
|
narendraḥ kiñcidāśvasto dvijaśreṣṭha kimabravīt ||1||

2prāptavānparamaṃ duḥkhaṃ putravyasanajaṃ mahat
|
tasmin yaduktavān kāle tanmamācakṣva pṛcchataḥ ||2||

3vaiśampāyana uvāca|
śrutvā karṇasya nidhanamaśraddheyamivādbhutam
|
bhūtasaṃmohanaṃ bhīmaṃ meroḥ saṃsarpaṇaṃ yathā ||3||

4cittamohamivāyuktaṃ bhārgavasya mahāmateḥ
|
parājayamivendrasya dviṣadbhyo bhīmakarmaṇaḥ ||4||

5divaḥ prapatanaṃ bhānorurvyāmiva mahādyuteḥ
|
saṃśoṣaṇamivācintyaṃ samudrasyākṣayāṃbhasaḥ ||5||

6mahīviyaddigambūnāṃ sarvanāśamivādbhutam
|
karmaṇoriva vaiphalyamubhayoḥ puṇyapāpayoḥ ||6||

7sañcintya nipuṇaṃ buddhyā dhṛtarāṣṭro janeśvaraḥ
|
nedamastīti sañcintya karṇasya samare vadham ||7||

8prāṇināmevamanyeṣāṃ syādapīti vināśanam
|
śokāgninā dahyamāno dhamyamāna ivāśaye ||8||

9visrastāṅgaḥ śvasan dīno hāhetyuktvā suduḥkhitaḥ
|
vilalāpa mahārāja dhṛtarāṣṭro'mbikāsutaḥ ||9||

10dhṛtarāṣṭra uvāca|
sañjayādhirathirvīraḥ siṃhadvirada‌vikramaḥ
|
vṛṣabhapratimaskandho vṛṣabhākṣagatiścaran ||10||

11vṛṣabho vṛṣabhasyeva yo yuddhe na nivartate
|
śatrorapi mahendrasya vajrasaṃhanano yuvā ||11||

12yasya jyātalaśabdena śaravṛṣṭiraveṇa ca
|
rathāśvanaramātaṅgā nāvatiṣṭhanti saṃyuge ||12||

13yamāśritya mahābāhuṃ vidviṣāṃ jayakāṅkṣayā
|
duryodhano'karādvairaṃ pāṇḍuputrairmahārathaiḥ ||13||

14sa kathaṃ rathināṃ śreṣṭhaḥ karṇaḥ pārthena saṃyuge
|
nihataḥ puruṣavyāghraḥ prasahyāsahyavikramaḥ ||14||

15yo nāmanyata vai nityamacyutaṃ ca dhanañjayam
|
na vṛṣṇīnsahitānanyānsvabāhubaladarpitaḥ ||15||

16śārṅgagāṇḍīvadhanvānau sahitāvaparājitau
|
ahaṃ divyādrathādekaḥ pātayiṣyāmi saṃyuge ||16||

17iti yaḥ satataṃ mandamavocallobhamohitam
|
duryodhanamavācīnaṃ rājyakāmukamāturam ||17||

18yo'jayatsarvakāmbojānāvantyān kekayaiḥ saha
|
gāndhārān madra kānmatsyāṃstrigartāṃstaṅgaṇāñśakān ||18||

19pañcālāṃśca videhāṃśca kulindān kāśikosalān
|
suhmānaṅgāṃśca vaṅgāṃśca niṣādān puṇḍracīrakān ||19||

20vatsānkaliṅgāṃstaralānaśmakānṛṣikānapi
|
jitvaitānsamare vīraścakre balibhṛtaḥ purā ||20||

21śaravrātaiḥ suniśitaiḥ sutīkṣṇaiḥ kaṅkapatribhiḥ
|
duryodhanasya vṛddhyarthaṃ rādheyo rathināṃ varaḥ ||21||

22divyāstravinmahātejāḥ karṇo vaikartano vṛṣaḥ
|
senāgopaśca sa kathaṃ śatrubhiḥ paramāstravit ||22||

23ghātitaḥ pāṇḍavaiḥ śūraiḥ samare vīryaśālibhiḥ
|
vṛṣo mahendro deveṣu vṛṣaḥ karṇo vareṣvapi ||23||

24tṛtīyamanyaṃ lokeṣu vṛṣaṃ naivānuśuśruma
|
uccaiḥśravā varo'śvānāṃ rājñāṃ vaiśravaṇo varaḥ ||24||

25varo mahendro devānāṃ karṇaḥ praharatāṃ varaḥ
|
yojitaḥ pārthivaiḥ śūraiḥ samarthairvīryaśālibhiḥ ||25||

26duryodhanasya vṛddhyarthaṃ kṛtsnāmurvīmathājayat
|
yaṃ labdhvā māgadho rājā sāntvamāno'tha sauhṛdaiḥ ||26||

27arautsītpārthivaṃ kṣatramṛte yādavakauravān
|
taṃ śrutvā nihataṃ karṇaṃ dvairathe savyasācinā ||27||

28śokārṇave nimagno'haṃ bhinnā nauriva sāgare
|
taṃ vṛṣaṃ nihataṃ śrutvā dvairathe rathināṃ varam||28||

29śokārṇave nimagno'hamaplavaḥ sāgare yathā
|
īdṛśairyadyahaṃ duḥkhairna vinaśyāmi sañjaya ||29||

30vajrāddṛḍhataraṃ manye hṛdayaṃ mama durbhidam
|
jñātisambandhimitrāṇāmimaṃ śrutvā parābhavam ||30||

31ko madanyaḥ pumā~lloke na jahyātsūta jīvitam
|
viṣamagniṃ prapātaṃ ca parvatāgrādahaṃ vṛṇe
|
na hi śakṣyāmi duḥkhāni soḍhuṃ kaṣṭāni sañjaya ||31||
