Mahābhārata 12.71 (Śāntiparvan)

1yudhiṣṭhira uvāca|
kathaṃ rājā prajā rakṣannādhibandhena yujyate
|
dharme ca nāparādhnoti tanme brūhi pitāmaha||1||

2bhīṣma uvāca|
samāsenaiva te rājandharmānvakṣyāmi śāśvatān
|
vistareṇaiva dharmāṇāṃ na jātvantamavāpnuyāt||2||

3dharmaniṣṭhān śrutavato vedavratasamāhitān
|
arcayitvā yajethāstvaṃ gṛhe guṇavato dvijān||3||

4pratyutthāyopasaṃgṛhya caraṇāvabhivādya ca
|
atha sarvāṇi kurvīthāḥ kāryāṇi sapurohitaḥ||4||

5dharmakāryāṇi nirvartya maṅgalāni prayujya ca
|
brāhmaṇānvācayethāstvamarthasiddhijayāśiṣaḥ||5||

6ārjavena ca sampanno dhṛtyā buddhyā ca bhārata
|
yathārthaṃ pratigṛhṇīyāt kāmakrodhau ca varjayet||6||

7kāmakrodhau puraskṛtya yo'rthaṃ rajā'nutiṣṭhati
|
na sa dharmaṃ na cāpyarthaṃ pratigṛhṇāti bāliśaḥ||7||

8mā sma lubdhāṃśca mūrkhāṃśca ca prayūyujaḥ
|
alubdhānbuddhisampannānsarvakarmasu yojayet||8||

9mūrkho hyadhikṛto'rtheṣu kāryāṇāmaviśāradaḥ
|
prajāḥ kliśnātyayogena kāmakrodhasamanvitaḥ||9||

10baliṣaṣṭhena śuktena daṇḍenāthāparādhinām
|
śāstrānītena lipsethā vetanena dhanāgamam||10||

11dāpayitvā kara dharmyaṃ rāṣṭraṃ nītyā yathāvidhi
|
tathaitaṃ kalpayedrājā yogakṣemamatandritaḥ||11||

12gopāyitāraṃ dātāraṃ dharmanityamatandritam
|
akāmadveṣasaṃyuktamanurajyanti mānavāḥ||12||

13mā smā lobhenādharmeṇa lipsethāstvaṃ dhanāgamam
|
dharmārthāvadhruvau tasya yo na śāstraparo bhavet||13||

14arthaśāstraparo rājā dharmārthānnādhigacchati
|
asthāne cāsya tadvittaṃ sarvameva vinaśyati||14||

15arthamūlo'pi hiṃsāṃ ca kurute svayamātmanaḥ
|
karairaśāstradṛṣṭairhi mohātsampīḍayan prajāḥ||15||

16ūpaśchindyāttu yo dhenvāḥ kṣīrārthī na labhetpayaḥ
|
evaṃ rāṣṭramayogena pīḍitaṃ na vivardhate||16||

17yo hi dogdhrīmupāste ca sa nityaṃ vindate payaḥ
|
evaṃ rāṣṭramupāyena bhuñjāno labhate phalam||17||

18atha rāṣṭramupāyena bhujyamānaṃ surakṣitam
|
janayatyatulāṃ nityaṃ kośavṛddhiṃ yudhiṣṭhira||18||

19dogdhrī dhānyaṃ hiraṇyaṃ ca mahī rājñā surakṣitā
|
nityaṃ svebhyaḥ parebhyaśca tṛptā mātā yathā payaḥ||19||

20mālākāropamo rājanbhava mā''ṅgārikopamaḥ
|
tathāyuktaściraṃ rājyaṃ bhoktuṃ śakṣyasi pālayan||20||

21paracakrābhiyānena yadi te syāddhanakṣayaḥ
|
atha sāmnaiva lipsethā dhanamabrāhmaṇeṣu yat||21||

22mā sma te brāhmaṇaṃ dṛṣṭvā dhanasthaṃ pracalenmanaḥ
|
antyāyāmapyavasthāyāṃ kimu sphītasya bhārata||22||

23dhanāni tebhyo dadyāstvaṃ yathāśakti yathārhataḥ
|
sāntvayanparirakṣaṃśca svargamāpsyasi durjayam||23||

24evaṃ dharmeṇa vṛttena prajāstvaṃ paripālaya
|
svantaṃ puṇyaṃ yaśo nityaṃ prāpsyase kurunandana||24||

25dharmeṇa vyavahāreṇa prajāḥ pālaya pāṇḍava
|
yudhiṣṭhira yathā yukto nādhibandhena yokṣyase||25||

26eṣa eva paro dharmo yadrājā rakṣati prajāḥ
|
bhūtānāṃ hi yathā dharmo rakṣaṇaṃ paramā dayā||26||

27tasmādevaṃ paraṃ dharmaṃ manyante dharmakovidāḥ
|
yo rājā rakṣaṇe yukto bhūteṣu kurute dayām||27||

28yadahnā kurute pāpamarakṣanbhayataḥ prajāḥ
|
rājā varṣasahasreṇa tasyāntamadhigacchati||28||

29yadahnā kurute dharmaṃ prajā dharmeṇa pālayan
|
daśavarṣasahasrāṇi tasya bhuṅkte phalaṃ divi||29||

30sviṣṭiḥ svadhītiḥ sutapā lokāñjayati yāvataḥ
|
kṣaṇena tānavāpnoti prajā dharmeṇa pālayan||30||

31evaṃ dharmaṃ prayatnena kaunteya paripālaya
|
tataḥ puṇyaphalaṃ labdhvā nādhibandhena yokṣyase||31||

32svargaloke sumahatīṃ śriyaṃ prāpsyasi pāṇḍava
|
asambhavaśca dharmāṇāmīdṛśānāmarājasu||32||

33tasmādrājaiva nānyo'sti yo dharmaphalamāpnuyāt
|
sa rājyaṃ dhṛtimānprāpya dharmeṇa paripālaya
|
indraṃ tarpaya somena kāmaiśca suhṛdo janān||33||
