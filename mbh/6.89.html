Mahābhārata 6.89 (Bhīṣmaparvan)

1dhṛtarāṣṭra uvāca|
dṛṣṭvā me nihatānputrānbahūnekena sañjaya
|
bhīṣmo droṇaḥ kṛpaścaiva kimakurvata saṃyuge ||1||

2ahanyahani me putrāḥ kṣayaṃ gacchanti sañjaya
|
manye'haṃ sarvathā sūta daivenopahatā bhṛśam ||2||

3yatra me tanayāḥ sarve jīyante na jayantyuta
|
yatra bhīṣmasya droṇasya kṛpasya ca mahātmanaḥ ||3||

4saumadatteśca vīrasya bhagadattasya cobhayoḥ
|
aśvatthāmnastathā tāta śūrāṇāmanivartinām ||4||

5anyeṣāṃ caiva śūrāṇāṃ madhyagāstanayā mama
|
yadahanyanta saṃgrāme kimanyadbhāgadheyataḥ ||5||

6na hi duryodhano mandaḥ purā proktamabudhyata
|
vāryamāṇo mayā tāta bhīṣmeṇa vidureṇa ca ||6||

7gāndhāryā caiva durmedhāḥ satataṃ hitakāmyayā
|
nābudhyata purā mohāttasya prāptamidaṃ phalam ||7||

8yadbhīmasenaḥ samare putrānmama vicetasaḥ
|
ahanyahani saṃkruddho nayate yamasādanam ||8||

9sañjaya uvāca|
idaṃ tat samanuprāptaṃ kṣatturvacanamuttamam
|
na buddhavānasi vibho procyamānaṃ hitaṃ tadā ||9||

10nivāraya sutāndyūtātpāṇḍavān mā druheti ca
|
suhṛdāṃ hitakāmānāṃ bruvatāṃ tattadeva ca ||10||

11na śuśrūṣasi yadvākyaṃ martyaḥ pathyamivauṣadham
|
tadeva tvāmanuprāptaṃ vacanaṃ sādhubhāṣitam ||11||

12viduradroṇabhīṣmāṇāṃ tathā'nyeṣāṃ hitaiṣiṇām
|
akṛtvā vacanaṃ pathyaṃ kṣayaṃ gacchanti kauravāḥ ||12||

13tadetat samanuprāptaṃ pūrvameva viśāṃpate
|
tasmāt tvaṃ śṛṇu tattvena yathā yuddhamavartata ||13||

14madhyāhne sumahāraudraḥ saṃgrāmaḥ samapadyata
|
lokakṣayakaro rājaṃstanme nigadataḥ śṛṇu ||14||

15tataḥ sarvāṇi sainyāni dharmaputrasya śāsanāt
|
saṃrabdhānyabhyavartanta bhīṣmameva jighāṃsayā ||15||

16dhṛṣṭadyumnaḥ śikhaṇḍī ca sātyakiśca mahārathaḥ
|
yuktānīkā mahārāja bhīṣmameva samabhyayuḥ ||16||

17virāṭo drupadaścaiva sahitāḥ sarvasomakaiḥ
|
abhyadravanta saṃgrāme bhīṣmameva mahāratham ||17||

18kekayā dhṛṣṭaketuśca kuntibhojaśca daṃśitaḥ
|
yuktānīkā mahārāja bhīṣmameva samabhyayuḥ ||18||

19arjuno draupadeyāśca cekitānaśca vīryavān
|
duryodhanasamādiṣṭān rājñaḥ sarvānsamabhyayuḥ ||19||

20abhimanyustathā śūro haiḍimbaśca mahārathaḥ
|
bhīmasenaśca saṃkruddhaste'bhyadhāvanta kauravān ||20||

21tridhābhūtairavadhyanta pāṇḍavaiḥ kauravā yudhi
|
tathaiva kauravai rājannavadhyanta pare raṇe ||21||

22droṇastu rathinaḥ śreṣṭhānsomakānsṛñjayaiḥ saha
|
abhyadhāvata saṃkruddhaḥ preṣayiṣyan yamakṣayam ||22||

23tatrākrando mahānāsīt sṛñjayānāṃ mahātmanām
|
vadhyatāṃ samare rājan bhāradvājena dhanvinā ||23||

24droṇena nihatāstatra kṣatriyā bahavo raṇe
|
viceṣṭanto hyadṛśyanta vyādhikliṣṭā narā iva ||24||

25kūjatāṃ krandatāṃ caiva stanatāṃ caiva bhārata
|
aniśaṃ śuśruve śabdaḥ kṣutkliṣṭānāṃ nṛṇāmiva ||25||

26tathaiva kauraveyāṇāṃ bhīmaseno mahābalaḥ
|
cakāra kadanaṃ ghoraṃ kruddhaḥ kāla ivāparaḥ ||26||

27vadhyatāṃ tatra sainyānāmanyonyena mahāraṇe
|
prāvartata nadī ghorā rudhiraughapravāhinī ||27||

28sa saṃgrāmo mahārāja ghorarūpo'bhavanmahān
|
kurūṇāṃ pāṇḍavānāṃ ca yamarāṣṭravivardhanaḥ ||28||

29tato bhīmo raṇe kruddho rabhasaśca viśeṣataḥ
|
gajānīkaṃ samāsādya preṣayāmāsa mṛtyave ||29||

30tatra bhārata bhīmena nārācābhihatā gajāḥ
|
peturneduśca seduśca diśaśca paribabhramuḥ ||30||

31chinnahastā mahānāgāśchinnagātrāśca māriṣa
|
krauñcavadvyanadan bhītāḥ pṛthivīmadhiśerate ||31||

32nakulaḥ sahadevaśca hayānīkamabhidrutau
|
te hayāḥ kāñcanāpīḍā rukmabhāṃḍaparicchadāḥ ||32||

33vadhyamānā vyadṛśyanta śataśo'tha sahasraśaḥ
|
patadbhisturagai rājan samāstīryata medinī ||33||

34nirjihvaiśca śvasadbhiśca kūjadbhiśca gatāsubhiḥ
|
hayairbabhau naraśreṣṭha nānārūpadharairdharā ||34||

35arjunena hataiḥ saṅkhye tathā bhārata rājabhiḥ
|
prababhau vasudhā ghorā tatra tatra viśāṃpate ||35||

36rathairbhagnairdhvajaiśchinnairnikṛttaiśca mahāyudhaiḥ
|
cāmarairvyajanaiścaiva chatraiśca sumahāprabhaiḥ ||36||

37hārairniṣkaiḥ sakeyūraiḥ śirobhiśca sakuṇḍalaiḥ
|
uṣṇīṣairapaviddhaiśca patākābhiśca sarvaśaḥ ||37||

38anukarṣaiḥ śubhai rājan yoktraiścaiva saraśmibhiḥ
|
saṃkīrṇā vasudhā bhāti vasante kusumairiva ||38||

39evameṣa kṣayo vṛttaḥ pāṇḍūnāmapi bhārata
|
kruddhe śāntanave bhīṣme droṇe ca rathasattame ||39||

40aśvatthāmni kṛpe caiva tathaiva kṛtavarmaṇi
|
tathetareṣu kruddheṣu tāvakānāmapi kṣayaḥ ||40||
