Mahābhārata 8.79 (Karṇaparvan)

1sañjaya uvāca|
arjunastu mahārāja hatvā sainyaṃ caturvidham
|
sūtaputraṃ ca saṃkruddhaṃ dṛṣṭvā caiva mahāraṇe ||1||

2śoṇitodāṃ mahīṃ kṛtvā māṃsamajjāsthipaṅkilām
|
manuṣyaśīrṣapāṣāṇāṃ hastyaśvakṛtarodhasam ||2||

3śūrāsthicayasaṃkīrṇāṃ kākagṛdhrānunāditām
|
chatrahaṃsaplavopetāṃ vīravṛkṣāpahāriṇīm ||3||

4hārapadmākaravatīmuṣṇīṣavaraphenilām
|
dhanuḥśaradhvajopetāṃ narakṣudrakapālinīm ||4||

5carmavarmabhramopetāṃ rathoḍupasamākulām
|
jayaiṣiṇāṃ ca sutarāṃ bhīrūṇāṃ ca sudustarām ||5||

6nadīṃ prāvartayitvā ca bībhatsuḥ paravīrahā
|
vāsudevamidaṃ vākyamabravītpuruṣarṣabhaḥ ||6||

7arjuna uvāca|
eṣa ketū raṇe kṛṣṇa sūtaputrasya dṛśyate
|
bhīmasenādayaścaite yodhayanti mahāratham ||7||

8ete dravanti pañcālāḥ karṇatrastā janārdana
|
eṣa duryodhano rājā śvetacchatreṇa dhāryatā ||8||

9karṇena bhagnānpañcālān drāvayanbahu śobhate
|
kṛpaśca kṛtavarmā ca drauṇiścaiva mahārathaḥ ||9||

10ete rakṣanti rājānaṃ sūtaputreṇa rakṣitāḥ
|
avadhyamānāste'smābhirghātayiṣyanti somakān ||10||

11eṣa śalyo rathopasthe raśmisaṃcārakovidaḥ
|
sūtaputrarathaṃ kṛṣṇa vāhayan bahu śobhate ||11||

12tatra me buddhirutpannā vāhayātra mahāratham
|
nāhatvā samare karṇaṃ nivartiṣye kathañcana ||12||

13rādheyo hyanyathā pārthānsṛñjayāṃśca mahārathān
|
niḥśeṣānsamare kuryātpaśyatāṃ no janārdana ||13||

14tataḥ prāyādrathenāśu keśavastava vāhinīm
|
karṇaṃ prati maheṣvāsaṃ dvairathe savyasācinā ||14||

15prayātaśca mahābāhuḥ pāṇḍavānujñayā hariḥ
|
āśvāsayanrathenaiva pāṇḍusainyāni sarvaśaḥ ||15||

16rathaghoṣaḥ sa saṃgrāme pāṇḍaveyasya saṃbabhau
|
vāsavāśanitulyasya meghaughasyeva māriṣa ||16||

17mahatā rathaghoṣeṇa pāṇḍavaḥ satyavikramaḥ
|
abhyayādaprameyātmā nirjayaṃstava vāhinīm ||17||

18tamāyāntaṃ samīkṣyaiva śvetāśvaṃ kṛṣṇasārathim
|
madrarājo'bravītkarṇaṃ ketuṃ dṛṣṭvā mahātmanaḥ ||18||

19ayaṃ sa ratha āyāti śvetāśvaḥ kṛṣṇasārathiḥ
|
nighnannamitrānsamare yaṃ karṇa paripṛcchasi ||19||

20eṣa tiṣṭhati kaunteyaḥ saṃspṛśangāṇḍivaṃ dhanuḥ
|
taṃ haniṣyasi cedadya tanna śreyo bhaviṣyati ||20||

21dhanurjyā candratārāṅkā patākākiṅkiṇīyutā
|
paśya karṇārjunasyaiṣā saudāmanyambare yathā ||21||

22eṣa dhvajāgre pārthasya prekṣamāṇaḥ samantataḥ
|
dṛśyate vānaro bhīmo vīrāṇāṃ bhayavardhanaḥ ||22||

23pataccakraṃ gadā śaṅkhaḥ śārṅgaṃ kṛṣṇasya ca prabho
|
dṛśyate pāṇḍavarathe vāhayānasya vājinaḥ ||23||

24etatkūjati gāṇḍīvaṃ visṛṣṭaṃ savyasācinā
|
ete hastavatā muktā ghnantyamitrāñśitāḥ śarāḥ ||24||

25viśālāyatatāmrākṣaiḥ pūrṇacandranibhānanaiḥ
|
eṣā bhūḥ kīryate rājñāṃ śirobhirapalāyinām ||25||

26ete parighasaṅkāśāḥ puṇyagandhānulepanāḥ
|
uddhatā raṇaśūrāṇāṃ pātyante sāyudhā bhujāḥ ||26||

27nirastajihvā netrāntā vājinaḥ saha sādibhiḥ
|
patitāḥ pātyamānāśca kṣitau kṣīṇā viśerate ||27||

28ete parvataśṛṅgāṇāṃ tulyā haimavatā gajāḥ
|
saṃchinnakumbhāḥ pārthena prapatantyadrayo yathā ||28||

29gandharvanagarākārā yathā vā te nareśvarāḥ
|
vimānādiva puṇyānte svargiṇo nipatantyamī ||29||

30vyākulīkṛtamatyarthaṃ parasainyaṃ kirīṭinā
|
nānāmṛgasahastrāṇāṃ yūthaṃ kesariṇāṃ yathā ||30||

31tvāmabhiprepsurāyāti karṇa nighnanvarānrathān
|
asahyamāno rādheya taṃ yāhi prati bhārata ||31||

32eṣā vidīryate senā dhārtarāṣṭrī samantataḥ
|
arjunasya bhayāttūrṇaṃ nighnataḥ śātravānbahun ||32||

33varjayansarvasainyāni tvarate hi dhanañjayaḥ
|
tvadarthamiti manye'haṃ yathāsyodīryate vapuḥ ||33||

34na hyavasthāsyate pārtho yuyutsuḥ kenacitsaha
|
tvāmṛte krodhadīpto hi pīḍyamāne vṛkodare ||34||

35virathaṃ dharmarājaṃ tu dṛṣṭvā sudṛḍhavikṣatam
|
śikhaṇḍinaṃ sātyakiṃ ca dhṛṣṭadyumnaṃ ca pārṣatam ||35||

36draupadeyān yudhāmanyumuttamaujasameva ca
|
nakulaṃ sahadevaṃ ca bhrātarau dvau samīkṣya ca ||36||

37sahasaikarathaḥ pārthastvāmabhyeti parantapaḥ
|
krodharaktekṣaṇaḥ kruddho jighāṃsuḥ sarvapārthivān ||37||

38tvarito'bhipatatyasmāṃstyaktvā sainyānyasaṃśayam
|
tvaṃ karṇa pratiyāhyenaṃ nāstyanyo hi dhanurdharaḥ ||38||

39na taṃ paśyāmi loke'smiṃstvatto hyanyaṃ dhanurdharam
|
arjunaṃ samare kruddhaṃ yo velāmiva dhārayet ||39||

40na cāsya rakṣāṃ paśyāmi pārśvato na ca pṛṣṭhataḥ
|
eka evābhiyāti tvāṃ paśya sāphalyamātmanaḥ ||40||

41tvaṃ hi kṛṣṇau raṇe śaktaḥ saṃsādhayitumāhave
|
tavaiva bhāro rādheya pratyudyāhi dhanañjayam ||41||

42samāno hyasi bhīṣmeṇa droṇadrauṇikṛpeṇa ca
|
savyasācinamāyāntaṃ nivāraya mahāraṇe ||42||

43lelihānaṃ yathā sarpaṃ garjantamṛṣabhaṃ yathā
|
vanasthitaṃ yathā vyāghraṃ jahi karṇa dhanañjayam ||43||

44ete dravanti samare dhārtarāṣṭrā mahārathāḥ
|
arjunasya bhayāttūrṇaṃ nirapekṣā janādhipāḥ ||44||

45dravatāmatha teṣāṃ tu nānyo'sti yudhi mānavaḥ
|
bhayahā yo bhavedvīrastvāmṛte sūtanandana
|
ete tvāṃ kuravaḥ sarve dvīpamāsādya saṃyuge ||45||

46dhiṣṭhitāḥ puruṣavyāghra tvattaḥ śaraṇakāṅkṣiṇaḥ
|
vaidehāmbaṣṭhakāmbojāstathā nagnajitastvayā ||46||

47gāndhārāśca yayā dhṛtyā jitāḥ saṃkhye sudurjayāḥ
|
tāṃ dhṛtiṃ kuru rādheya tataḥ pratyehi pāṇḍavam ||47||

48vāsudevaṃ ca vārṣṇeyaṃ prīyamāṇaṃ kirīṭinā
|
pratyudyāhi mahābāho pauruṣe mahati sthitaḥ ||48||

49karṇa uvāca|
prakṛtistho'si me śalya idānīṃ saṃmatastathā
|
pratibhāsi mahābāho mā bhaiṣīstvaṃ dhanañjayāt ||49||

50paśya bāhvorbalaṃ me'dya śikṣitasya ca paśya me
|
eko'dya nihaniṣyāmi pāṇḍavānāṃ mahācamūm ||50||

51kṛṣṇau ca puruṣavyāghra tataḥ satyaṃ bravīmi te
|
nāhatvā yudhi tau vīrau vyapayāsye kathañcana ||51||

52śiśye vā nihatastābhyāmanityo hi raṇe jayaḥ
|
kṛtārtho'dya bhaviṣyāmi hatvā vā'pyatha vā hataḥ ||52||

53śalya uvāca|
ajayyamenaṃ pravadanti yuddhe mahārathāḥ karṇa rathapravīram
|
ekākinaṃ kimu kṛṣṇābhiguptaṃ vijetumenaṃ ka ihotsaheta ||53||

54karṇa uvāca|
naitādṛśo jātu babhūva loke rathottamo yāvadupaśrutaṃ naḥ
|
tamīdṛśaṃ pratiyotsyāmi pārthaṃ mahāhave paśya ca pauruṣaṃ me ||54||

55raṇe caratyeṣa rathapravīraḥ sitairhayaiḥ kauravarājaputraḥ
|
sa vā'dya māṃ neṣyati kṛcchrametatkarṇasyāntādetadantāstu sarve ||55||

56asvedinau rājaputrasya hastāvavepamānau jātakiṇau bṛhantau
|
dṛḍhāyudhaḥ kṛtimān kṣiprahasto na pāṇḍaveyena samo'sti yodhaḥ ||56||

57gṛhṇātyanekānapi kaṅkapatrānekaṃ yathā tānprati yojya cāśu
|
te krośamātre nipatantyamoghāḥ kastena yodho'sti samaḥ pṛthivyām ||57||

58atoṣayatkhāṇḍave yo hutāśaṃ kṛṣṇadvitīyo'tirathastarasvī
|
lebhe cakraṃ yatra kṛṣṇo mahātmā dhanurgāṇḍīvaṃ pāṇḍavaḥ savyasācī ||58||

59śvetāśvayuktaṃ ca sughoṣamugraṃ rathaṃ mahābāhuradīnasattvaḥ
|
maheṣudhī cākṣaye divyarūpe śastrāṇi divyāni ca havyavāhāt ||59||

60tathendraloke nijaghāna daityānasaṃkhyeyān kālakeyāṃśca sarvān
|
lebhe śaṅkhaṃ devadattaṃ sma tatra ko nāma tenābhyadhikaḥ pṛthivyām ||60||

61mahādevaṃ toṣayāmāsa yo'straiḥ sākṣātsuyuddhena mahānubhāvaḥ
|
lebhe tataḥ pāśupataṃ sughoraṃ trailokyasaṃhārakaraṃ mahāstram ||61||

62pṛthakpṛthaglokapālāḥ sametā dadurmahāstrāṇyaprameyāṇi saṃkhye
|
yaistāñjaghānāśu raṇe nṛsiṃhaḥ sakālakeyānasurānsametān ||62||

63tathā virāṭasya pure sametānsarvānasmānekarathena jitvā
|
jahāra tadgodhanamājimadhye vastrāṇi cādatta mahārathebhyaḥ ||63||

64tamīdṛśaṃ vīryaguṇopapannaṃ kṛṣṇadvitīyaṃ paramaṃ nṛpāṇām
|
tamāhvayansāhasamuttamaṃ vai jāne svayaṃ sarvalokasya śalya ||64||

65anantavīryeṇa ca keśavena nārāyaṇenāpratimena guptaḥ
|
varṣāyutairyasya guṇā na śakyā vaktuṃ sametairapi sarvalokaiḥ ||65||

66mahātmanaḥ śaṅkhacakrāsipāṇerviṣṇorjiṣṇorvasudevātmajasya
|
bhayaṃ na me jāyate sādhvasaṃ ca dṛṣṭvā kṛṣṇāvekarathe sametau ||66||

67atīva pārtho yudhi kārmukibhyo nārāyaṇaścāpraticakrayuddhe
|
evaṃvidhau pāṇḍavavāsudevau caletsvadeśāddhimavānna kṛṣṇau ||67||

68ubhau hi śūrau balinau dṛḍhāyudhau mahārathau saṃhananopapannau
|
etādṛśau phālgunavāsudevau ko'nyaḥ pratīyānmadṛte tau tu śalya ||68||

69manoratho yastu mamādya tasya madreśa yuddhaṃ prati pāṇḍavasya
|
naitaccirādāśu bhaviṣyatīdamatyadbhutaṃ citramatulyarūpam ||69||

70etau ca hatvā yudhi pātayiṣye māṃ vāpi kṛṣṇau nihaniṣyato'dya
|
iti bruvañśalyamamitrahantā karṇo raṇe megha ivonnanāda ||70||

71abhyetya putreṇa tavābhinanditaḥ sametya covāca kurupravīram
|
kṛpaṃ ca bhojaṃ ca mahābhujāvubhau tathaiva gāndhārapatiṃ sahānujam ||71||

72guroḥ sutaṃ cāvarajaṃ tathā''tmanaḥ padātino'tha dvipasādinaśca tān
|
nirudhyatābhidravatācyutārjunau śrameṇa saṃyojayatāśu sarvaśaḥ ||72||

73yathā bhavadbhirbhṛśavikṣitāvubhau sukhena hanyāmahamadya bhūmipāḥ
|
tatheti coktvā tvaritāḥ sma te'rjunaṃ jighāṃsavo vīratarāḥ samāyayuḥ ||73||

74śaraiśca jaghnuryudhi taṃ mahārathā dhanañjayaṃ karṇanideśakāriṇaḥ
|
nadīnadaṃ bhūrijalo mahārṇavo yathā tathā tān samare'rjuno'grasat ||74||

75na saṃdadhāno na tathā śarottamān pramuñcamāno ripubhiḥ pradṛśyate
|
dhanañjayāstaistu śarairvidāritā hatā nipeturnara-vāji-kuñjarāḥ ||75||

76śarārciṣaṃ gāṇḍivacārumaṇḍalaṃ yugāntasūryapratimānatejasam
|
na kauravāḥ śekurudīkṣituṃ jayaṃ yathā raviṃ vyādhitacakṣuṣo janāḥ ||76||

77śarottamānsaṃprahitānmahārathaiściccheda pārthaḥ prahasañcharaughaiḥ
|
bhūyaśca tānahanadbāṇasaṅghān gāṇḍīvadhanvāyatapūrṇamaṇḍalam ||77||

78yathograraśmiḥ śuciśukramadhyagaḥ sukhaṃ vivasvān harate jalaughān
|
tathā'rjuno bāṇagaṇānnirasya dadāha senāṃ tava pārthivendra ||78||

79tamabhyadhāvadvisṛjankṛpaḥ śarāṃstathaiva bhojastava cātmajaḥ svayam
|
mahāratho droṇasūtaśca sāyakairavākiraṃstoyadharā yathā'calam ||79||

80jighāṃsubhistān kuśalaḥ śarottamān mahāhave saṃprahitānprayatnataḥ
|
śaraiḥ praciccheda sa pāṇḍavastvaran parābhinadvakṣasi ceṣubhistribhiḥ ||80||

81sa gāṇḍivavyāyatapūrṇamaṇḍalastapan ripūnarjunabhāskaro babhau
|
śarograraśmiḥ śuciśukramadhyago yathaiva sūryaḥ pariveṣavāṃstathā ||81||

82athāgryabāṇairdaśabhirdhanañjayaṃ parābhinaddroṇasuto'cyutaṃ tribhiḥ
|
caturbhiraśvāṃścaturaḥ kapiṃ tataḥ śaraiśca nārācavarairavākirat ||82||

83tathāpi taṃ prasphuradāttakārmukaṃ tribhiḥ śarairyantṛśiraḥ kṣureṇa
|
hayāṃścaturbhiśca punastribhirdhvajaṃ dhanañjayo drauṇirathāda‌pātayat ||83||

84sa roṣapūrṇo maṇivajrahāṭakairalaṅkṛtaṃ takṣakabhogavarcasam
|
mahādhanaṃ kārmukamanyadādade yathā mahāhipravaraṃ girestaṭāt ||84||

85svamāyudhaṃ copanikīrya bhūtale dhanuśca kṛtvā saguṇaṃ guṇādhikaḥ
|
samārdayattāvajitau narottamau śarottamairdrauṇiravidhyadantikāt ||85||

86kṛpaśca bhojaśca tavātmajaśca te śarairanekairyudhi pāṇḍavarṣabham
|
mahārathāḥ saṃyugamūrdhani sthitāstamonudaṃ vāridharā ivāpatan ||86||

87kṛpasya pārthaḥ saśaraṃ śarāsanaṃ hayān dhvajānsārathimeva patribhiḥ
|
samārpayadbāhusahasravikramastathā yathā vajradharaḥ purā baleḥ ||87||

88sa pārthabāṇairvinipātitāyudho dhvajāvamarde ca kṛte mahāhave
|
kṛtaḥ kṛpo bāṇasahasrayantrito yathā''pageyaḥ prathamaṃ kirīṭinā ||88||

89śaraiḥ praciccheda tavātmajasya dhvajaṃ dhanuśca pracakarta nardataḥ
|
jaghāna cāśvānkṛtavarmaṇaḥ śubhān dhvajaṃ ca ciccheda tataḥ pratāpavān ||89||

90savājisuteṣvasanānsaketanān jadhāna nāgāśvarathāṃstvaraṃśca saḥ
|
tataḥ prakīrṇaṃ sumahadbalaṃ tava pradāritaṃ seturivāṃbhasā yathā ||90||

91tato'rjunasyāśu rathena keśavaścakāra śatrūnapasavyamāturān
|
tataḥ prayātaṃ tvaritaṃ dhanañjayaṃ śatakratuṃ vṛtranijaghnuṣaṃ yathā ||91||

92samanvadhāvanpunarutthitairdhvajai rathaiḥ suyuktairapare yuyutsavaḥ
|
athābhisṛtya prativārya tānarīn dhanañjayasyābhimukhaṃ mahārathāḥ ||92||

93śikhaṇḍiśaineyayamāḥ śitaiḥ śarairvidārayanto vyanadansubhairavam
|
tato'bhijaghnuḥ kupitāḥ parasparaṃ śaraistadāñjogatibhiḥ sutejanaiḥ ||93||

94kurupravīrāḥ saha sṛñjayairyathā'surāḥ purā devagaṇaistathā''have
|
jayepsavaḥ svargamanāya cotsukāḥ patanti nāgāśvarathāḥ parantapa ||94||

95jagarjuruccairbalavacca vivyadhuḥ śaraiḥ sumuktairitaretaraṃ pṛthak
|
śarāndhakāre tu mahātmabhiḥ kṛte mahāmṛdhe yodhavaraiḥ parasparam
|
caturdiśo vai vidiśaśca pārthiva prabhā ca sūryasya tamovṛtā'bhavat ||95||
