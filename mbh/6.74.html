Mahābhārata 6.74 (Bhīṣmaparvan)

1sañjaya uvāca|
atha rājan mahābāhuḥ sātyakiryuddhadurmadaḥ
|
vikṛṣya cāpaṃ samare bhārasāhamanuttamam ||1||

2prāmuñcatpuṅkhasaṃyuktān śarānāśīviṣopamān
|
pragāḍhaṃ laghu citraṃ ca darśayan hastalāghavam ||2||

3tasya vikṣipataścāpaṃ śarānanyāṃśca muñcataḥ
|
ādadānasya bhūyaśca saṃdadhānasya cāparān ||3||

4kṣipataśca parāṃstasya raṇe śatrūnvinighnataḥ
|
dadṛśe rūpamatyarthaṃ meghasyeva pravarṣataḥ ||4||

5tamudīryantamālokya rājā duryodhanastataḥ
|
rathānāmayutaṃ tasya preṣayāmāsa bhārata ||5||

6tāṃstu sarvānmaheṣvāsān sātyakiḥ satyavikramaḥ
|
jaghāna parameṣvāso divyenāstreṇa vīryavān ||6||

7sa kṛtvā dāruṇaṃ karma pragṛhītaśarāsanaḥ
|
āsasāda tato vīro bhūriśravasamāhave ||7||

8sa hi saṃdṛśya senāṃ te yuyudhānena pātitām
|
abhyadhāvata saṃkruddhaḥ kurūṇāṃ kīrtivardhanaḥ ||8||

9indrāyudhasavarṇaṃ tu visphārya sumahaddhanuḥ
|
sṛṣṭavān vajrasaṅkāśān śarānāśīviṣopamān ||9||

10sahasraśo mahārāja darśayan pāṇilāghavam
|
śarāṃstānmṛtyusaṃsparśānsātyakeśca padānugāḥ ||10||

11na viṣehustadā rājan dudruvuste samantataḥ
|
vihāya sātyakiṃ rājan samare yuddhadurmadam ||11||

12taṃ dṛṣṭvā yuyudhānasya sutā daśa mahābalāḥ
|
mahārathāḥ samākhyātāścitravarmāyudhadhvajāḥ ||12||

13samāsādya maheṣvāsaṃ bhūriśravasamāhave
|
ūcuḥ sarve susaṃrabdhā yūpaketuṃ mahāraṇe ||13||

14bho bho kauravadāyāda sahāsmābhirmahābala
|
ehi yudhyasva saṃgrāme samastaiḥ pṛthageva vā ||14||

15asmānvā tvaṃ parājitya yaśaḥ prāpnuhi saṃyuge
|
vayaṃ vā tvāṃ parājitya prītiṃ dhāsyāmahe pituḥ ||15||

16evamuktastadā śūraistānuvāca mahābalaḥ
|
vīryaślāghī naraśreṣṭhastān dṛṣṭvā samavasthitān ||16||

17sādhvidaṃ kathyate vīrā yadyevaṃ matiradya vaḥ
|
yudhyadhvaṃ sahitā yattā nihaniṣyāmi vo raṇe ||17||

18evamuktā maheṣvāsāste vīrāḥ kṣiprakāriṇaḥ
|
mahatā śaravarṣeṇa abhyadhāvannarindamam ||18||

19so'parāhṇe mahārāja saṃgrāmastumulo'bhavat
|
ekasya ca bahūnāṃ ca sametānāṃ raṇājire ||19||

20tamekaṃ rathināṃ śreṣṭhaṃ śaraiste samavākiran
|
prāvṛṣīva yathā meruṃ siṣicurjaladā nṛpa ||20||

21taistu muktān śarān ghorān yamadaṇḍāśaniprabhān
|
asaṃprāptānasaṃbhrāntaścicchedāśu mahārathaḥ ||21||

22tatrādbhutamapaśyāma saumada‌tteḥ parākramam
|
yadeko bahubhiryuddhe samasajjada‌bhītavat ||22||

23visṛjya śaravṛṣṭiṃ tāṃ daśa rājanmahārathāḥ
|
parivārya mahābāhuṃ nihantumupacakramuḥ ||23||

24saumadattistataḥ kruddhasteṣāṃ cāpāni bhārata
|
ciccheda samare rājan yudhyamāno mahārathaiḥ ||24||

25athaiṣāṃ chinnadhanuṣāṃ śaraiḥ sannataparvabhiḥ
|
ciccheda samare rājan śirāṃsi bharatarṣabha ||25||

26te hatā nyapatan rājan vajrabhagnā iva drumāḥ
|
tān dṛṣṭvā nihatānvīro raṇe putrānmahābalān ||26||

27vārṣṇeyo vinadan rājan bhūriśravasamabhyayāt
|
rathaṃ rathena samare pīḍayitvā mahābalau ||27||

28tāvanyonyaṃ hi samare nihatya rathavājinaḥ
|
virathāvabhivalgantau sameyātāṃ mahārathau ||28||

29pragṛhītamahākhaḍgau tau carmavaradhāriṇau
|
śuśubhāte naravyāghrau yuddhāya samavasthitau ||29||

30tataḥ sātyakimabhyetya nistriṃśavaradhāriṇam
|
bhīmasenastvaran rājan rathamāropayattadā ||30||

31tavāpi tanayo rājan bhūriśravasamāhave
|
āropayadrathaṃ tūrṇaṃ paśyatāṃ sarvadhanvinām ||31||

32tasmiṃstathā vartamāne raṇe bhīṣmaṃ mahāratham
|
ayodhayanta saṃrabdhāḥ pāṇḍavā bharatarṣabha ||32||

33lohitāyati cāditye tvaramāṇo dhanañjayaḥ
|
pañcaviṃśatisāhasrānnijaghāna mahārathān ||33||

34te hi duryodhanādiṣṭāstadā pārthanibarhaṇe
|
saṃprāpyaiva gatā nāśaṃ śalabhā iva pāvakam ||34||

35tato matsyāḥ kekayāśca dhanurveda‌viśāradāḥ
|
parivavrustadā pārthaṃ sahaputraṃ mahāratham ||35||

36etasminneva kāle tu sūrye'stamupagacchati
|
sarveṣāṃ caiva sainyānāṃ pramohaḥ samajāyata ||36||

37avahāraṃ tataścakre pitā devavratastava
|
sandhyākāle mahārāja sainyānāṃ śrāntavāhanaḥ ||37||

38pāṇḍavānāṃ kurūṇāṃ ca parasparasamāgame
|
te sene bhṛśasaṃvigne yayatuḥ svaṃ niveśanam ||38||

39tataḥ svaśibiraṃ gatvā nyaviśaṃstatra bhārata
|
pāṇḍavāḥ sṛñjayaiḥ sārdhaṃ kuravaśca yathāvidhi ||39||
