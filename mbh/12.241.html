Mahābhārata 12.241 (Śāntiparvan)

1śuka uvāca |
yadidaṃ vedavacanaṃ kuru karma tyajeti ca
|
kāṃ diśaṃ vidyayā yānti kāṃ ca gacchanti karmaṇā ||1||

2etadvai śrotumicchāmi tadbhavānprabravītu me
|
etaccānyonyavarūpye vartete pratikūlataḥ ||2||

3bhīṣma uvāca|
ityuktaḥ pratyuvācedaṃ parāśarasutaḥ sutam
|
karmavidyāmayāvetau vyākhyāsyāmi kṣarākṣarau ||3||

4yāṃ diśaṃ vidyayā yānti yāṃ ca gacchanti karmaṇā
|
śṛṇuṣvaikamanā vatsa gahvaraṃ hyetadantaram ||4||

5asti dharma iti proktaṃ nāstītyatraiva yo vadet
|
tasya pakṣasya sadṛśamidaṃ mama bhavedyathā ||5||

6dvāvimāvatha panthānau yatra vedāḥ pratiṣṭhitāḥ
|
pravṛttilakṣaṇo dharmo nivṛttau ca subhāṣitaḥ ||6||

7karmaṇā baddhyate janturvidyayā tu pramucyate
|
tasmātkarma na kurvanti yatayaḥ pāradarśinaḥ ||7||

8karmaṇā jāyate pretya mūrtimānṣoḍaśātmakaḥ
|
vidyayā jāyate nityamavyaktaṃ hyavyayātmakam ||8||

9karma tveke praśaṃsanti svalpabuddhiratā narāḥ
|
tena te dehajālāni ramayanta upāsate ||9||

10ye sma buddhiṃ parāṃ prāptā dharmanaipuṇyadarśinaḥ
|
na te karma praśaṃsanti kūpaṃ nadyāṃ pibanniva ||10||

11karmaṇaḥ phalamāpnoti sukhaduḥkhe bhavābhavau
|
vidyayā tadavāpnoti yatra gatvā na śocati ||11||

12yatra gatvā na mriyate yatra gatvā na jāyate
|
na punarjāyate yatra yatra gatvā na vartate ||12||

13yatra tadbrahma paramamavyaktamacalaṃ dhruvam
|
avyākṛtamanāyāsamavyaktaṃ cāviyogi ca ||13||

14dvandvairna yatra bādhyante mānasena ca karmaṇā
|
samāḥ sarvatra maitrāśca sarvabhūtahite ratāḥ ||14||

15vidyāmayo'nyaḥ puruṣastāta karmamayo'paraḥ
|
viddhi candramasaṃ darśe sūkṣmayā kalayā sthitam ||15||

16tadetadṛṣiṇā proktaṃ vistareṇānumīyate
|
navajaṃ śaśinaṃ dṛṣṭvā vakratantumivāmbare ||16||

17ekādaśavikārātmā kalāsambhārasambhṛtaḥ
|
mūrtimāniti taṃ viddhi tāta karmaguṇātmakam ||17||

18devo yaḥ saṃśritastasminnabbinduriva puṣkare
|
kṣetramaṃ taṃ vijānīyānnityaṃ yogajitātmakam ||18||

19tamo rajaśca sattvaṃ ca viddhi jīvaguṇātmakam
|
jīvamātmaguṇaṃ vidyādātmānaṃ paramātmanaḥ ||19||

20sacetanaṃ jīvaguṇaṃ vadanti sa ceṣṭate jīvayate ca sarvam
|
tataḥ paraṃ kṣetravido vadanti prākalpayadyo bhuvanāni sapta ||20||
