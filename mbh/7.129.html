Mahābhārata 7.129 (Droṇaparvan)

1dhṛtarāṣṭra uvāca|
ninadantaṃ tathā taṃ tu bhīmasenaṃ mahābalam
|
meghastanitanirghoṣaṃ ke vīrāḥ paryavārayan ||1||

2na hi paśyāmyahaṃ taṃ vai triṣu lokeṣu kañcana
|
kruddhasya bhīmasenasya yastiṣṭhedagrato raṇe ||2||

3gadāṃ yuyutsamānasya kālasyeveha sañjaya
|
na hi paśyāmyahaṃ yuddhe yastiṣṭhedagrataḥ pumān ||3||

4rathaṃ rathena yo hanyāt kuñjaraṃ kuñjareṇa ca
|
kastasya samare sthātā sākṣādapi purandaraḥ ||4||

5kruddhasya bhīmasenasya mama putrān jighāṃsataḥ
|
duryodhanahite yuktāḥ samatiṣṭhanta ke'grataḥ ||5||

6bhīmasenadavāgnestu mama putrāṃstṛṇopamān
|
pradhakṣato raṇamukhe ke'tiṣṭhannagrato narāḥ ||6||

7kālyamānāṃstu putrān me dṛṣṭvā bhīmena saṃyuge
|
kāleneva prajāḥ sarvāḥ ke bhīmaṃ paryavārayan ||7||

8na me'rjunādbhayaṃ tādṛkkṛṣṇānnāpi ca sātvatāt
|
hutabhugjanmano naiva yādṛgbhīmādbhayaṃ mama ||8||

9bhīmavahneḥ pradīptasya mama putrāndidhakṣataḥ
|
ke śūrāḥ paryavatanta tanmamācakṣva sañjaya ||9||

10sañjaya uvāca|
tathā tu nardamānaṃ taṃ bhīmasenaṃ mahābalam
|
tumulenaiva śabdena karṇo'pyabhyadravadbalī ||10||

11vyākṣipan sumahaccāpamatimātramamarṣaṇaḥ
|
karṇaḥ suyuddha‌mākāṃkṣan darśayiṣyanbalaṃ mṛdhe ||11||

12rurodha mārgaṃ bhīmasya vātasyeva mahīruhaḥ
|
bhīmo'pi dṛṣṭvā sāvegaṃ puro vaikartanaṃ sthitam ||12||

13cukopa balavadvīraścikṣepāsya śilāśitān
|
tānpratyagṛhṇātkarṇo'pi pratīpaṃ prāpayaccharān ||13||

14tatastu sarvayodhānāṃ yatatāṃ prekṣatāṃ tadā
|
prāvepanniva gātrāṇi karṇabhīmasamāgame ||14||

15rathināṃ sādināṃ caiva tayoḥ śrutvā talasvanam
|
bhīmasenasya ninadaṃ śrutvā ghoraṃ raṇājire ||15||

16khaṃ ca bhūmiṃ ca saṃruddhāṃ menire kṣatriyarṣabhāḥ
|
punarghoreṇa nādena pāṇḍavasya mahātmanaḥ ||16||

17samare sarvayodhānāṃ dhanūṃṣyabhyapatānkṣitau
|
śastrāṇi nyapatan dorbhyaḥ keṣāṃciccāsavo'dravan ||17||

18vitrastāni ca sarvāṇi śakṛnsūtraṃ prasusruvuḥ
|
vāhanāni ca sarvāṇi babhūvurvimanāṃsi ca ||18||

19prādurāsannimittāni ghorāṇi subahūnyuta
|
gṛdhrakaṅkabalaiścāsīdantarikṣaṃ samāvṛtam ||19||

20tasminsutumule rājan karṇabhīmasamāgame
|
tataḥ karṇastu viṃśatyā śarāṇāṃ bhīmamārdayat ||20||

21vivyādha cāsya tvaritaḥ sūtaṃ pañcabhirāśugaiḥ
|
prahasya bhīmaseno'pi karṇaṃ pratyādravadraṇe ||21||

22sāyakānāṃ catuḥṣaṣṭyā kṣiprakārī mahāyaśāḥ
|
tasya karṇo maheṣvāsaḥ sāyakāṃścaturo'kṣipat ||22||

23asaṃprāptāṃśca tān bhīmaḥ sāyakairnataparvabhiḥ
|
ciccheda bahudhā rājan darśayanpāṇilāghavam ||23||

24taṃ karṇaśchādayāmāsa śaravrātairanekaśaḥ
|
saṃchādyamānaḥ karṇena bahudhā pāṇḍunandanaḥ ||24||

25ciccheda cāpaṃ karṇasya muṣṭideśe mahārathaḥ
|
vivyādha cainaṃ bahubhiḥ sāyakairnataparvabhiḥ ||25||

26athānyaddhanurādāya sajyaṃ kṛtvā ca sūtajaḥ
|
vivyādha samare bhīmaṃ bhīmakarmā mahārathaḥ ||26||

27tasya bhīmo bhṛśaṃ kruddhastrīn śarānnataparvaṇaḥ
|
nicakhānorasi kruddhaḥ sūtaputrasya vegataḥ ||27||

28taiḥ karṇo'rājata śarairuromadhyagataistadā
|
mahīdhara ivodagrastriśṛṃgo bharatarṣebha ||28||

29susrāva cāsya rudhiraṃ viddhasya parameṣubhiḥ
|
dhātuprasyandinaḥ śailādyathā gairikadhātavaḥ ||29||

30kiṃcidvicālitaḥ karṇaḥ suprahārābhipīḍitaḥ
|
ākarṇapūrṇamākṛṣya bhīmaṃ vivyādha sāyakaiḥ ||30||

31cikṣepa ca punarbāṇāñśataśo'tha sahasraśaḥ
|
sa śarairarditastena karṇena dṛḍhadhandhinā
|
dhanurjyāmacchinattūrṇaṃ bhīmastasya kṣureṇa ha ||31||

32sārathiṃ cāsya bhallena rathanīḍāda‌pātayat
|
vāhāṃśca caturastasya vyasūṃścakre mahārathaḥ ||32||

33hatāśvāttu rathātkarṇaḥ samāplutya viśāṃpate
|
syandanaṃ vṛṣasenasya tūrṇamāpupluve bhayāt ||33||

34nirjitya tu raṇe karṇaṃ bhīmasenaḥ pratāpavān
|
nanāda balavānnādaṃ parjanyaninadopamam ||34||

35tasya taṃ ninadaṃ śrutyā prahṛṣṭo'bhūdyudhiṣṭhiraḥ
|
karṇaṃ parājitaṃ matvā bhīmasenena saṃyuge ||35||

36samantācchaṃkhaninadaṃ pāṇḍusenā'karottadā
|
śatrusenādhvaniṃ śrutvā tāvakā hyanadanbhṛśam ||36||

37sa śaṃkhabāṇaninadairharṣādrājā svavāhinīm
|
cakre yudhiṣṭhiraḥ saṃkhye harṣanādaiśca saṃkulām ||37||

38gāṇḍīvaṃ vyākṣipatpārthaḥ kṛṣṇo'pyabjamavādayat
|
tamantardhāya ninadaṃ bhīmasya nadato dhvaniḥ
|
aśrūyata tadā rājan sarvasainyeṣu dāruṇaḥ ||38||

39tato vyāyacchatāmastraiḥ pṛthak pṛthagajihmagaiḥ
|
mṛdupūrvaṃ tu rādheyo dṛḍhapūrvaṃ tu pāṇḍavaḥ ||39||
