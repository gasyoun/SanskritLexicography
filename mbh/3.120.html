Mahābhārata 3.120 (Āraṇyakaparvan)

1sātyakiruvāca|
na rāma kālaḥ paridevanāya yaduttaraṃ tvatra tadeva sarve
|
samācarāmo hyanatītakālaṃ yudhiṣṭhiro yadyapi nāha kiṃcit||1||

2ye nāthavanto'dya bhavanti loke te nātmanā karma samārabhanta
|
teṣāṃ tu kāryeṣu bhavanti nāthāḥ śibyādayo rāma yathā yayāteḥ||2||

3yeṣāṃ tathā rāma samārabhante kāryāṇi nāthāḥ svamatena loke
|
te nāthavantaḥ puruṣapravīrā nānāthavatkṛcchramavāpnuvanti||3||

4tasmādimau rāmajanārdanau ca pradyumnasāmbau ca mayā sametau
|
vasantyaraṇye sahasodarīyaistrailokyanāthānābhigamya pārthāḥ||4||

5niryātu sādhvadya daśārhasenā prabhūtanānāyudhacitravarmā
|
yamakṣayaṃ gacchatu dhārtarāṣṭraḥ sabāndhavo vṛṣṇibalābhibhūtaḥ||5||

6tvaṃ hyeva kopātpṛthivīmapīmāṃ saṃveṣṭayestiṣṭhatu śārṅgadhanvā
|
sadhārtarāṣṭraṃ jahi sānubandhaṃ vṛtraṃ yathā devapatirmahendraḥ||6||

7bhrātā ca me yaḥ sa sakhā guruśca janārdanasyātmasamaśca pārthaḥ
|
yadarthamaicchanmanujāḥ suputraṃ śiṣyaṃ guruṃ cāpratikūlavādam||7||

8yadarthamabhyudyatamuttamaṃ tatkaroti karmāgryamapāraṇīyam
|
tasyāstravarṣāṇyahamuttamāstrairvihatya sarvāṇi raṇe'bhibhūya||8||

9kāyācchiraḥ sarpaviṣāgnikalpaiḥ śarottamairunmathitāsmi rāma
|
khaḍgena cāhaṃ niśitena saṃkhye kāyācchirastasya balātpramathya||9||

10tato'sya sarvānanugāhnaniṣye duryodhanaṃ cāpi kurūṃśca sarvān
|
āttāyudhaṃ māmiha rauhiṇeya paśyantu bhaimā yudhi jātaharṣāḥ||10||

11nighnantamekaṃ kuruyodhamukhyānagniṃ mahākakṣamivāntakāle
|
pradyumnamuktānniśitānna śaktāḥ soḍhuṃ kṛpadroṇavikarṇakarṇāḥ||11||

12jānāmi vīryaṃ ca jayātmajasya kārṣṇirbhavatyeva yathā raṇasthaḥ
|
sāmbaḥ sasūtaṃ sarathaṃ bhujābhyāṃ duḥśāsanaṃ śāstu balātpramathya||12||

13na vidyate jāmbavatīsutasya raṇe viṣahyaṃ hi raṇotkaṭasya
|
etena bālena hi śaṃbarasya daityasya sainyaṃ sahasā praṇunnam||13||

14vṛttoruratyāyatapīnabāhuretena saṃkhye nihato'śvacakraḥ
|
ko nāma sāmbasya mahārathasya raṇe samakṣaṃ rathamabhyudīyāt||14||

15yathā praviśyāntaramantakasya kāle manuṣyo na viniṣkrameta
|
tathā praviśyāntaramasya saṃkhye ko nāma jīvanpunarāvrajecca||15||

16droṇaṃ ca bhīṣmaṃ ca mahārathau tau sutairvṛtaṃ cāpyatha somadattam
|
sarvāṇi sainyāni ca vāsudevaḥ pradhakṣyate sāyakavahnijālaiḥ||16||

17kiṃ nāma lokeṣu viṣahyamasti kṛṣṇasya sarveṣu sadevakeṣu
|
āttāyudhasyottamabāṇapāṇeścakrāyudhasyāpratimasya yuddhe||17||

18tato niruddho'pyasicarmapāṇirmahīmimāṃ dhārtarāṣṭrairvisaṃjñaiḥ
|
hṛtottamāṅgairnihataiḥ karotu kīrṇāṃ kuśairvedimivādhvareṣu||18||

19gadolmukau bāhukabhānunīthāḥ śūraśca saṃkhye niśaṭhaḥ kumāraḥ
|
raṇotkaṭau sāraṇacārudeṣṇau kulocitaṃ viprathayantu karma||19||

20savṛṣṇibhojāndhakayodhamukhyā samāgatā sātvataśūrasenā
|
hatvā raṇe tāndhṛtarāṣṭraputrālloke yaśaḥ sphītamupākarotu||20||

21tato'bhimanyuḥ pṛthivīṃ praśāstu yāvadvrataṃ dharmabhṛtāṃ variṣṭhaḥ
|
yudhiṣṭhiraḥ pārayate mahātmā dyūte yathoktaṃ kurusattamena||21||

22asmatpramuktairviśikhairjitāristato mahīṃ bhokṣyati dharmarājaḥ
|
nirdhārtarāṣṭrāṃ hatasūtaputrāmetaddhi naḥ kṛtyatamaṃ yaśasyam||22||

23vāsudeva uvāca|
asaṃśayaṃ mādhava satyametadgṛhṇīma te vākyamadīnasattva
|
svābhyāṃ bhujābhyāmajitāṃ tu bhūmiṃ necchetkurūṇāmṛṣabhaḥ kathaṃcit||23||

24na hyeṣa kāmānna bhayānna lobhādyudhiṣṭhiro jātu jahyātsvadharmam
|
bhīmārjunau cātirathau yamau ca tathaiva kṛṣṇā drupadātmajeyam||24||

25ubhau hi yuddhe'pratimau pṛthivyāṃ vṛkodaraścaiva dhanaṃjayaśca
|
kasmānna kṛtsnāṃ pṛthivīṃ praśāsenmādrīsutābhyāṃ ca puraskṛto'yam||25||

26yadā tu pañcālapatirmahātmā sakekayaścedipatirvayaṃ ca
|
yudhyema vikramya raṇe sametāstadaiva sarve ripavo hi na syuḥ||26||

27yudhiṣṭhira uvāca|
nedaṃ citraṃ mādhava yadbravīṣi satyaṃ tu me rakṣyatamaṃ na rājyam
|
kṛṣṇastu māṃ veda yathāvadekaḥ kṛṣṇaṃ ca vedāhamatho yathāvat||27||

28yadaiva kālaṃ puruṣapravīro vetsyatyayaṃ mādhava vikramasya
|
tadā raṇe tvaṃ ca śinipravīra suyodhanaṃ jeṣyasi keśavaśca||28||

29pratiprayāntvadya daśārhavīrā dṛṣṭo'smi nāthairnaralokanāthaiḥ
|
dharme'pramādaṃ kurutāprameyā draṣṭāsmi bhūyaḥ sukhinaḥ sametān||29||

30te'nyo'nyamāmantrya tathābhivādya vṛddhānpariṣvajya śiśuṃśca sarvān
|
yadupravīrāḥ svagṛhāṇi jagmuste cāpi tīrthānyanusaṃviceruḥ||30||

31visṛjya kṛṣṇaṃ tvatha dharmarājo vidarbharājopacitāṃ sutīrthām
|
jagāma puṇyāṃ saritaṃ payoṣṇīṃ sabhrātṛbhṛtyaḥ saha lomaśena||31||

32sutena somena vimiśratoyāṃ payaḥ payoṣṇīṃ prati so'dhyuvāsa
|
dvijātimukhyairmuditairmahātmā saṃstūyamānaḥ stutibhirvarābhiḥ||32||
