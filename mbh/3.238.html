Mahābhārata 3.238 (Āraṇyakaparvan)

1vaiśampāyana uvāca|
karṇasya vacanaṃ śrutvā rājā duryodhanastataḥ
|
hṛṣṭo bhūtvā punardīna idaṃ vacanamabravīt||1||

2bravīṣi yadidaṃ karṇa sarvaṃ manasi me sthitam
|
na tvabhyanujñāṃ lapsyāmi gamane yatra pāṇḍavāḥ||2||

3paridevati tān vīrān dhṛtarāṣṭro mahīpatiḥ
|
manyate'bhyadhikāṃścāpi tapoyogena pāṇḍavān||3||

4athavā'pyanubudhyeta nṛpo'smākaṃ cikīrṣitam
|
evamapyāyatiṃ rakṣannābhyanujñātumarhati||4||

5na hi dvaitavane kiñcidvidyate'nyat prayojanam
|
utsādanamṛte teṣāṃ vanasthānāṃ mahādyute||5||

6jānāsi hi yathā kṣattā dyūtakāla upasthite
|
abravīdyacca māṃ tvāṃ ca saubalaṃ vacanaṃ tadā||6||

7tāni sarvāṇi vākyāni yaccānyat paridevitam
|
vicintya nādhigacchāmi gamanāyetarāya vā||7||

8mamāpi hi mahān harṣo yadahaṃ bhīmaphālgunau
|
kliṣṭāvaraṇye paśyeyaṃ kṛṣṇayā sahitāviti||8||

9na tathā hyāpnuyāṃ prītimavāpya vasudhāmimām
|
dṛṣṭvā yathā pāṇḍusutān valkalājinavāsasaḥ||9||

10kiṃ nu syādadhikaṃ tasmādyadahaṃ drupadātmajām
|
draupadīṃ karṇa paśyeyaṃ kāṣāyavasanāṃ vane||10||

11yadi māṃ dharmarājaśca bhīmasenaśca pāṇḍavaḥ
|
yuktaṃ paramayā lakṣmyā paśyetāṃ jīvitaṃ bhavet||11||

12upāyaṃ na tu paśyāmi yena gacchema tadvanam
|
yathā cābhyanujānīyādgacchantaṃ māṃ mahīpatiḥ||12||

13sa saubalena sahitastathā duḥśāsanena ca
|
upāyaṃ paśya nipuṇaṃ yena gacchema tadvanam||13||

14ahamapyadya niścitya gamanāyetarāya ca
|
kalyameva gamiṣyāmi samīpaṃ pārthivasya ha||14||

15mayi tatropaviṣṭe tu bhīṣme ca kurusattame
|
upāyo yo bhavedṝṣṭastaṃ ..yāḥ sahasaubalaḥ||15||

16vaco bhīṣmasya rājñaśca niśamya gamanaṃ prati
|
vyavasāyaṃ kariṣye'hamanunīya pitāmaham||16||

17tathetyuktvā tu te sarve jagmurāvasathān prati
|
vyuṣitāyāṃ rajanyāṃ tu karṇo rājānamabhyayāt||17||

18tato duryodhanaṃ karṇaḥ prahasannidamabravīt
|
upāyaḥ paridṛṣṭo'yaṃ taṃ nibodha janeśvara||18||

19ghoṣā dvaitavane sarve tvatpratīkṣā narādhipa
|
ghoṣayātrāpadeśena gamiṣyāmo na saṃśayaḥ||19||

20ucitaṃ hi sadā gantuṃ ghoṣayātrāṃ viśāmpate
|
evaṃ ca tvāṃ pitā rājan samanujñātumarhati||20||

21tathā kathayamānau tu ghoṣayātrāviniścayam
|
gāndhārarājaḥ śakuniḥ pratyuvāca hasanniva||21||

22upāyo'yaṃ mayā dṛṣṭo gamanāya nirāmayaḥ
|
anujñāsyati no rājā bodhayiṣyati cāpyuta||22||

23ghoṣā dvaitavane sarve tvatpratīkṣā narādhipa
|
ghoṣayātrāpadeśena gamiṣyāmo na saṃśayaḥ||23||

24tataḥ prahasitāḥ sarve te'nyonyasya talān daduḥ
|
tadeva ca viniścitya dadṛśuḥ kurusattamam||24||
