Mahābhārata 8.84 (Karṇaparvan)

1sañjaya uvāca|
duḥśāsane tu nihate tava putrā mahārathāḥ
|
mahākrodhaviṣā vīrāḥ samareṣvapalāyinaḥ ||1||

2daśa rājanmahāvīryā bhīmaṃ prācchādayañśaraiḥ
|
niṣaṅgī kavacī pāśī daṇḍadhāro dhanurdharaḥ ||2||

3alolupaḥ sahaḥ ṣaṇḍho vātavegasuvarcasau
|
ete sametya sahitā bhrātṛvyasanakarśitāḥ ||3||

4bhīmasenaṃ mahābāhuṃ mārgaṇaiḥ samavārayan
|
sa vāryamāṇo viśikhaiḥ samantāttairmahārathaiḥ ||4||

5bhīmaḥ krodhāgniraktākṣaḥ kruddhaḥ kāla ivābabhau
|
tāṃstu bhallairmahāvegairdaśabhirdaśa bhāratān ||5||

6rukmāṅgadān rukmapuṃkhaiḥ pārtho ninye yamakṣayam
|
hateṣu teṣu vīreṣu pradudrāva balaṃ tava ||6||

7paśyataḥ sūtaputrasya pāṇḍavasya bhayārditam
|
tataḥ karṇo mahārāja pravivaśa mahadbhayam ||7||

8dṛṣṭvā bhīmasya vikrāntamantakasya prajāsviva
|
tasya tvākārabhāvajñaḥ śalyaḥ samitiśobhanaḥ ||8||

9uvāca vacanaṃ karṇaṃ prāptakālamarindamam
|
mā vyathāṃ kuru rādheya naivaṃ tvayyupapadyate ||9||

10ete dravanti rājāno bhīmasenabhayārditāḥ
|
duryodhanaśca saṃmūḍho bhrātṛvyasanakarśitaḥ ||10||

11duḥśāsanasya rudhire pīyamāne mahātmanā
|
vyāpannacetasaścaiva śokopahatacetasaḥ ||11||

12duryodhanamupāsante parivārya samantataḥ
|
kṛpaprabhṛtayaścaite hataśeṣāḥ sahodarāḥ ||12||

13pāṇḍavā labdhalakṣāśca dhanañjayapurogamāḥ
|
tvāmevābhimukhāḥ śūrā yuddhāya samupasthitāḥ ||13||

14sa tvaṃ puruṣaśārdūla pauruṣeṇa samāsthitaḥ
|
kṣatra dharmaṃ puraskṛtya pratyudyāhi dhanañjayam ||14||

15bhāro hi dhārtarāṣṭreṇa tvāya sarvaḥ samāhitaḥ
|
tamudvaha mahābāho yathāśakti yathābalam ||15||

16jaye syādvipulā kīrtirdhruvaḥ svargaḥ parājaye
|
vṛṣasenaśca rādheya saṃkruddhastanayastava ||16||

17tvayi mohaṃ samāpanne pāṇḍavānabhidhāvati
|
etacchrutvā tu vacanaṃ śalyasyāmitatejasaḥ
|
hṛdi cāvaśyakaṃ bhāvaṃ cakre yuddhāya susthiram ||17||

18tataḥ kruddho vṛṣaseno'bhyadhāvadavasthitaṃ pramukhe pāṇḍavaṃ tam
|
vṛkodaraṃ kālamivāttadaṇḍaṃ gadāhastaṃ yodhayantaṃ tvadīyān ||18||

19tamabhyadhāvannakulaḥ pravīro roṣāda‌mitraṃ pratudanpṛṣatkaiḥ
|
karṇasya putraṃ samare prahṛṣṭaṃ purā jighāṃsurmaghaveva jambham ||19||

20tato dhvajaṃ sphāṭikacitrakañcukaṃ ciccheda vīro nakulaḥ kṣureṇa
|
karṇātmajasyeṣvasanaṃ ca citraṃ bhallena jāmbūnadacitranaddham ||20||

21athānyadādāya dhanuḥ sa śīghraṃ karṇātmajaḥ pāṇḍavamabhyavidhyat
|
divyairastrairabhyavarṣacca so'pi karṇasya putro nakulaṃ kṛtāstraḥ ||21||

22śarābhighātācca ruṣā ca rājan svayā ca bhāsā'strasamīraṇācca
|
jajvāla karṇasya suto'timātramiddho yathā''jyāhutibhirhutāśaḥ ||22||

23karṇasya putro nakulasya rājan sarvānaśvānakṣiṇoduttamāstraiḥ
|
vanāyujān vai nakulasya śubhrānudagragān hemajālāvanaddhān ||23||

24tato hatāśvādavaruhya yānādādāya carmāmalarukmacandram
|
ākāśasaṃkāśamasiṃ pragṛhya dodhūyamānaḥ khagavañcacāra ||24||

25tato'ntarikṣe ca rathāśvanāgaṃ ciccheda tūrṇaṃ nakulaścitrayodhī
|
te prāpatannasinā gāṃ viśastā yathāśvamedhe paśavaḥ śamitrā ||25||

26dvisāhasrāḥ pātitā yuddhaśauṇḍā nānādeśyāḥ subhṛtāḥ satyasandhāḥ
|
ekena saṃkhye nakulena kṛttā jayepsunā'nuttamacandanāṅgāḥ ||26||

27tamāpatantaṃ nakulaṃ so'bhipatya samantataḥ sāyakaiḥ pratyaviddhyat
|
sa tudyamāno nakulaḥ pṛṣatkairvivyādha vīraṃ sa cukopa viddhaḥ ||27||

28mahābhaye rakṣyamāṇo mahātmā bhrātrā bhīmenākarottatra bhīmam
|
taṃ karṇaputro vyadhamantamekaṃ narāśvamātaṅgarathānanekān ||28||

29krīḍantamaṣṭādaśabhiḥ pṛṣatkairvivyādha vīraṃ nakulaṃ saroṣaḥ
|
sa tena viddho'tibhṛśaṃ tarasvī mahāhave vṛṣasenena rājan ||29||

30kruddhena dhāvansamare jighāṃsuḥ karṇātmajaṃ pāṇḍusuto nṛvīraḥ
|
vitatya pakṣau sahasā patantaṃ śyenaṃ yathaivābhiṣalubdhamājau ||30||

31avākiradvṛṣasenastatastaṃ śitaiḥ śarairnakulamudāravīryam
|
sa tānmoghāṃstasya kurvañśaraughān cacāra mārgānnakulaścitrarūpān ||31||

32athāsya tūrṇaṃ carato narendra khaḍgena citraṃ nakulasya tasya
|
maheṣubhirvyadhamatkarṇaputro mahāhave carmasahasratāram ||32||

33taṃ cāyasaṃ niśitaṃ tīkṣṇadhāraṃ vikośamugraṃ gurubhārasāham
|
dviṣaccharīrāntakaraṃ sughoramādhunvataḥ sarpamivograrūpam ||33||

34kṣipraṃ śaraiḥ ṣaḍbhiramitrasāhaścakarta khaḍgaṃ niśitaiḥ suvegaiḥ
|
punaśca dīptairniśitaiḥ pṛṣatkaiḥ stanāntare gāḍhamathābhyaviddhyat ||34||

35kṛtvā tu tadduṣkaramāryajuṣṭamanyairnaraiḥ karma raṇe mahātmā
|
yayau rathaṃ bhīmasenasya rājan śarābhitapto nakulastvarāvān ||35||

36sa bhīmasenasya rathaṃ hatāśvo mādrīsutaḥ karṇasutābhitaptaḥ
|
āpupluve siṃha ivācalāgraṃ saṃprekṣyamāṇasya dhanañjayasya ||36||

37tataḥ kruddho vṛṣaseno mahātmā vavarṣa tāviṣujālena vīraḥ
|
mahārathāvekarathe sametau śaraiḥ prabhindanniva pāṇḍaveyau ||37||

38tasminrathe nihate pāṇḍavasya kṣipraṃ ca khaḍge viśikhairnikṛtte
|
anye ca saṃhatya kurupravīrāstato nyaghnañśaravarṣairupetya ||38||

39tau pāṇḍaveyau paritaḥ sametān saṃhūyamānāviva havyavāhau
|
bhīmārjunau vṛṣasenāya kruddhau vavarṣatuḥ śaravarṣaṃ sughoram ||39||

40athābravīnmārutiḥ phālgunaṃ ca paśyasvainaṃ nakulaṃ pīḍyamānam
|
ayaṃ ca no bādhate karṇaputrastasmādbhavānpratyupayātu kārṇim ||40||

41sa tanniśamyaiva vacaḥ kirīṭī rathaṃ samāsādya vṛkodarasya
|
athābravīnnakulo vīkṣya vīramupāgataṃ śātaya śīghramenam ||41||

42ityevamuktaḥ sahasā kirīṭī bhrātrā samakṣaṃ nakulena saṃkhye
|
kapidhvajaṃ keśavasaṃgṛhītaṃ praiṣīdudagro vṛṣasenāya vāham ||42||
