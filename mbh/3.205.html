Mahābhārata 3.205 (Āraṇyakaparvan)

1vaiśampāyana uvāca|
tato yudhiṣṭhiro rājā mārkaṇḍeyaṃ mahādyutim
|
papraccha bharataśreṣṭha dharmapraśnaṃ sudurvidam||1||

2śrotumicchāmi bhagavan strīṇāṃ māhātmyamuttamam
|
kathyamānaṃ tvayā vipra sūkṣmaṃ dharmyaṃ ca tattvataḥ||2||

3pratyakṣamiha viprarṣe devā dṛśyanti sattama
|
sūryācandramasau vāyuḥ pṛthivī vahnireva ca||3||

4pitā mātā ca bhagavān gurureva ca sattama
|
yaccānyadeva vihitaṃ taccāpi bhṛgunandana||4||

5mānyā hi guravaḥ sarve ekapatnyastathā striyaḥ
|
pativratānāṃ śuśrūṣā duṣkarā pratibhāti me||5||

6pativratānāṃ māhātmyaṃ vaktumarhasi naḥ prabho
|
niruddhya cendriyagrāmaṃ manaḥ saṃrudhya cānagha||6||

7patiṃ devatavaccāpi cintayantyaḥ sthitā hi yāḥ
|
bhagavan duṣkaraṃ tvetat pratibhāti mama prabho||7||

8mātāpitrośca śuśrūṣā strīṇāṃ bhartari ca dvija
|
strīṇāṃ dharmāt sughorāddhi nānyaṃ paśyāmi duṣkaram||8||

9sādhvācārāḥ striyo brahman yat kurvanti sadādṛtāḥ
|
duṣkaraṃ khalu kurvanti pitaraṃ mātaraṃ ca vai||9||

10ekapatnyaśca yā nāryo yāśca satyaṃ vadantyuta
|
kukṣiṇā daśamāsāṃśca garbhaṃ sandhārayanti yāḥ||10||

11nāryaḥ kālena sambhūya kimadbhutataraṃ tataḥ
|
saṃśayaṃ paramaṃ prāpya vedanāmatulāmapi||11||

12prajāyante sutān nāryo duḥkhena mahatā vibho
|
puṣṇanti cāpi mahatā snehena dvijapuṅgava||12||

13ye ca krūreṣu sarveṣu vartamānā jugupsitāḥ
|
svakarma kurvanti sadā duṣkaraṃ tacca me matam||13||

14kṣatradharmasamācāratatvaṃ vyākhyāhi me dvija
|
dharmaḥ sudurlabho vipra nṛśaṃsena mahātmanām||14||

15etadicchāmi bhagavan praśnaṃ praśnavidāṃ vara
|
śrotuṃ bhṛgukulaśreṣṭha śuśrūṣe tava suvrata||15||

16mārkaṇḍeya uvāca|
hanta te'haṃ samākhyāsye praśnametaṃ sudurvacam
|
tattvena bharataśreṣṭha gadatastannibodha me||16||

17mātṝstu gauravādanye pitṝnanye tu menire
|
duṣkaraṃ kurute mātā vivardhayati yā prajāḥ||17||

18tapasā devatejyābhirvandanena titikṣayā
|
abhicārairupāyaiścāpīhante pitaraḥ sutān||18||

19evaṃ kṛcchreṇa mahatā putraṃ prāpya sudurlabham
|
cintayanti sadā vīra kīdṛśo'yaṃ bhaviṣyati||19||

20āśaṃsate hi putreṣu pitā mātā ca bhārata
|
yaśaḥ kīrtimathaiśvaryaṃ prajā dharmaṃ tathaiva ca||20||

21tayorāśāṃ tu saphalāṃ yaḥ karoti sa dharmavit
|
pitā mātā ca rājendra tuṣyato yasya nityaśaḥ||21||

22iha pretya ca tasyātha kīrtirdharmaśca śāśvataḥ
|
naiva yajñakriyāḥ kāścinna śrāddhaṃ nopavāsakam||22||

23yā tu bhartari śuśrūṣā tayā svargaṃ jayatyuta
|
etat prakaraṇaṃ rājannadhikṛtya yudhiṣṭhira||23||

24pativratānāṃ niyataṃ dharmaṃ cāvahitaḥ śrṛṇu||24||
