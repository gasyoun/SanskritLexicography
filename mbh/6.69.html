Mahābhārata 6.69 (Bhīṣmaparvan)

1sañjaya uvāca|
vyuṣitāyāṃ tu śarvaryāmudite ca divākare
|
ubhe sene mahārāja yuddhāyaiva samīyatuḥ ||1||

2abhyadhāvanta saṃkruddhāḥ parasparajigīṣavaḥ
|
te sarve sahitā yuddhe samālokya parasparam ||2||

3pāṇḍavā dhārtarāṣṭrāśca rājan durmantrite tava
|
vyūhau ca vyūhya saṃrabdhāḥ saṃprahṛṣṭāḥ prahāriṇaḥ ||3||

4arakṣan makaravyūhaṃ bhīṣmo rājan samantataḥ
|
tathaiva pāṇḍavā rājannarakṣan vyūhamātmanaḥ ||4||

5sa niryayau mahārāja pitā devavratastava
|
mahatā rathavaṃśena saṃvṛto rathināṃ varaḥ ||5||

6itaretaramanvīyuryathābhāgamavasthitāḥ
|
rathinaḥ pattayaścaiva dantinaḥ sādinastathā ||6||

7tān dṛṣṭvā'bhyudyatān saṃkhye pāṇḍavā hi yaśasvinaḥ
|
śyenena vyūharājena tenājayyana saṃyuge ||7||

8aśobhata mukhe tasya bhīmaseno mahābalaḥ
|
netre śikhaṇḍī durdharṣo dhṛṣṭadyumnaśca pārṣataḥ ||8||

9śīrṣe tasyābhavadvīraḥ sātyakiḥ satyavikramaḥ
|
vidhunvan gāṇḍivaṃ pārtho grīvāyāmabhavattadā ||9||

10akṣauhiṇyā samaṃ tatra vāmapakṣo'bhavattadā
|
mahātmā drupadaḥ śrīmān saha putreṇa saṃyuge ||10||

11dakṣiṇaścābhavatpakṣaḥ kaikeyo'kṣauhiṇīpatiḥ
|
pṛṣṭhato draupadeyāśca saubhadraścāpi vīryavān ||11||

12pṛṣṭhe samabhavacchrīmān svayaṃ rājā yudhiṣṭhiraḥ
|
bhrātṛbhyāṃ sahito vīro yamābhyāṃ cāruvikramaḥ ||12||

13praviśya tu raṇe bhīmo makaraṃ mukhatastadā
|
bhīṣmamāsādya saṅgrāme cchā‌dayāmāsa sāyakaiḥ ||13||

14tato bhīṣmo mahāstrāṇi pātayāmāsa bhārata
|
mohayan pāṇḍuputrāṇāṃ vyūḍhaṃ sainyaṃ mahāhave ||14||

15saṃmuhyati tadā sainye tvaramāṇo dhanañjayaḥ
|
bhīṣmaṃ śarasahastreṇa vivyādha raṇamūrdhani ||15||

16pratisaṃvārya cāstrāṇi bhīṣmamuktāni saṃyuge
|
svenānīkena hṛṣṭena yuddhāya samupasthitaḥ ||16||

17tato duryodhano rājā bhāradvājamabhāṣata
|
pūrvaṃ dṛṣṭvā vadhaṃ ghoraṃ balasya balināṃ varaḥ ||17||

18bhrātṝṇāṃ ca vadhaṃ yuddhe smaramāṇo mahārathaḥ
|
ācārya satataṃ hi tvaṃ hitakāmo mamānagha ||18||

19vayaṃ hi tvāṃ samāśritya bhīṣmaṃ caiva pitāmaham
|
devānapi raṇe jetuṃ prārthayāmo na saṃśayaḥ ||19||

20kimu pāṇḍusutān yuddhe hīnavīryaparākramān
|
sa tathā kuru bhadraṃ te yathā vadhyanti pāṇḍavāḥ ||20||

21evamuktastasto droṇastava putreṇa māriṣa
|
abhinat pāṇḍavānīkaṃ prekṣamāṇasya sātyakeḥ ||21||

22sātyakistu tato droṇaṃ vārayāmāsa bhārata
|
tayoḥ pravavṛte yuddhaṃ ghorarūpaṃ bhayāvaham ||22||

23śaineyaṃ tu raṇe kruddho bhāradvājaḥ pratāpavān
|
avidhyanniśitairbāṇairjatrudeśe hasanniva ||23||

24bhīmasenastataḥ kruddho bhāradvājamavidhyata
|
saṃrakṣan sātyakiṃ rājan droṇācchastrabhṛtāṃ varāt ||24||

25tato droṇaśca bhīṣmaśca tathā śalyaśca māriṣa
|
bhīmasenaṃ raṇe kruddhāśchādayāñcakrire śaraiḥ ||25||

26tatrābhimanyuḥ saṃkruddho draupadeyāśca māriṣa
|
vivyadhurniśitairbāṇaiḥ sarvāṃstānudyatāyudhān ||26||

27droṇabhīṣmau tu saṃkruddhāvāpatantau mahābalau
|
pratyudyayau śikhaṇḍī tu maheṣvāso mahāhave ||27||

28pragṛhya balavadvīro dhanurjalada‌niḥsvanam
|
abhyavarṣaccharaistūrṇaṃ chādayāno divākaram ||28||

29śikhaṇḍinaṃ samāsādya bharatānāṃ pitāmahaḥ
|
avarjayata saṅgrāmaṃ strītvaṃ tasyānusaṃsmaran ||29||

30tato droṇo mahārāja abhyadravata taṃ raṇe
|
rakṣamāṇastadā bhīṣmaṃ tava putreṇa coditaḥ ||30||

31śikhaṇḍī tu samāsādya droṇaṃ śastrabhṛtāṃ varam
|
avarjayata saṃtrasto yugāntāgnimivolbaṇam ||31||

32tato balena mahatā putrastava viśāṃpate
|
jugopa bhīṣmamāsādya prārthayāno mahadyaśaḥ ||32||

33tathaiva pāṇḍavā rājan puraskṛtya dhanañjayam
|
bhīṣmamevābhyavartanta jaye kṛtvā dṛḍhāṃ matim ||33||

34tadyuddhamabhavaddhoraṃ devānāṃ dānavairiva
|
jayamākāṅkṣātāṃ saṅkhye yaśaśca sumahādbhutam ||34||
