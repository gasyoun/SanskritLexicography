Mahābhārata 12.36 (Śāntiparvan)

1yudhiṣṭhira uvāca|
kiṃ bhakṣyaṃ cāpyabhakṣyaṃ ca kiṃ ca deyaṃ praśasyate
|
kiṃ ca pātramapātraṃ vā tanme brūhi pitāmaha ||1||

2vyāsa uvāca |
atrāpyudāharantīmamitihāsaṃ purātanam
|
siddhānāṃ caiva saṃvādaṃ manoścaiva prajāpateḥ ||2||

3ṛṣayastu vrataparāḥ samāgamya purā vibhum
|
dharmaṃ papracchurāsīnamādikāle prajāpatim ||3||

4kathamannaṃ kathaṃ pātraṃ dānamadhyayanaṃ tapaḥ
|
kāryākāryaṃ ca yatsarvaṃ śaṃsa vai tvaṃ prajāpate ||4||

5tairevamukto bhagavānmanuḥ svāyambhuvo'bravīt
|
śuśrūṣadhvaṃ yathāvṛttaṃ dharmaṃ vyāsasamāsataḥ ||5||

6anādeśe japo homa upavāsastathaiva ca
|
ātmajñānaṃ puṇyanadyo yatra prāyaśca tatparāḥ ||6||

7anādiṣṭaṃ tathaitāni puṇyāni dharaṇībhṛtaḥ
|
suvarṇaprāśanamapi ratnādisnānameva ca ||7||

8devasthānābhigamanamājyaprāśanameva ca
|
etāni medhyaṃ puruṣaṃ kurvantyāśu na saṃśayaḥ ||8||

9na garveṇa bhavetprājñaḥ kadācidapi mānavaḥ
|
dīrghamāyurathecchanhi trirātraṃ coṣṇapo bhavet ||9||

10adattasyānupādānaṃ dānamadhyayanaṃ tapaḥ
|
ahiṃsā satyamakrodha ijyā dharmasya lakṣaṇam ||10||

11sa eva dharmaḥ so'dharmo deśakāle pratiṣṭhitaḥ
|
ādānamanṛtaṃ hiṃsā dharmo hyāvasthikaḥ smṛtaḥ ||11||

12dvividhau cāpyubhāvetau dharmādharmau vijānatām
|
apravṛttiḥ pravṛttiśca dvaividhyaṃ lokavedayoḥ ||12||

13apravṛtteramartyatvaṃ martyatvaṃ karmaṇaḥ phalam
|
aśubhasyāśubhaṃ vidyācchubhasya śubhameva ca
|
etayoścobhayoḥ syātāṃ śubhāśubhatayā tathā ||13||

14daivaṃ ca daivasaṃyuktaṃ prāṇaśca prāṇadaśca ha
|
apekṣāpūrvakaraṇādaśubhānāṃ śubhaṃ phalam ||14||

15ūrdhvaṃ bhavati sandehādiha dṛṣṭārthameva ca
|
apekṣāpūrvakaraṇātprāyaścittaṃ vidhīyate ||15||

16krodhamohakṛte caiva dṛṣṭāntāgamahetubhiḥ
|
śarīrāṇāmupakleśo manasaśca priyāpriye
|
tadauṣadhaiśca mantraiśca prāyaścittaiśca śāmyati ||16||

17upavāsamekarātraṃ daṇḍotsarge narādhipaḥ
|
viśuddhyedātmaśuddhyarthaṃ trirātraṃ tu purohitaḥ ||17||

18kṣayaṃ śokaṃ prakurvāṇo na mriyeta yadā naraḥ
|
śastrādibhirupāviṣṭastrirātraṃ tatra nirdiśet ||18||

19jātiśreṇyadhivāsānāṃ kuladharmāṃśca sarvataḥ
|
varjayanti ca ye dharmaṃ teṣāṃ dharmo na vidyate ||19||

20daśa vā vedaśāstrajñāstrayo vā dharmapāṭhakāḥ
|
yadbrūyuḥ kārya utpanne sa dharmo dharmasaṃśaye ||20||

21anaḍvānmṛttikā caiva tathā kṣudrapipīlikāḥ
|
śleṣmātakastathā viprairabhakṣyaṃ viṣameva ca ||21||

22abhakṣyā brāhmaṇairmatsyāḥ śulkairye vai vivarjitāḥ
|
catuṣpātkacchapādanyo maṇḍūkā jalajāśca ye ||22||

23bhāsā haṃsāḥ suparṇāśca cakravākāḥ plavā bakāḥ
|
kāko madguśca gṛdhraśca ślenolūkastathaiva ca ||23||

24kravyādā daṃṣṭriṇaḥ sarve catuṣpātpakṣiṇaśca ye
|
yeṣāṃ cobhayato dantāścaturdaṃṣṭrāśca sarvaśaḥ ||24||

25eḍakāśvakharoṣṭrīṇāṃ sūtikānāṃ gavāmapi
|
mānuṣīṇāṃ mṛgīṇāṃ ca na pibedbrāhmaṇaḥ payaḥ ||25||

26pretānnaṃ sūtikānnaṃ ca yacca kiñcidanirdaśam
|
abhojyaṃ cāpyapeyaṃ ca dhenordugdhamanirdaśam ||26||

27rājānnaṃ teja ādatte śūdrānnaṃ brahmavarcasam
|
āyuḥ suvarṇakārānnamavīrāyāśca yoṣitaḥ ||27||

28viṣṭhā vārdhuṣikasyānnaṃ gaṇikānnamathendriyam
|
mṛṣyanti ye copapatiṃ strījitānnaṃ ca sarvaśaḥ ||28||

29dīkṣitasya kadaryasya kratuvikrayikasya ca
|
takṣṇaścarmāvakartuśca puṃścalyā rajakasya ca ||29||

30cikitsakasya yaccānnamabhojyaṃ rakṣiṇastathā
|
gaṇagrāmābhiśastānāṃ raṅgastrījīvināṃ tathā ||30||

31parivittīnāṃ puṃsāṃ ca bandidyūtavidāṃ tathā
|
vāmahastāhṛtaṃ cānnaṃ bhaktaṃ paryuṣitaṃ ca yat ||31||

32surānugatamucchiṣṭamabhojyaṃ śeṣitaṃ ca yat
|
piṣṭasya cekṣuśākānāṃ vikārāḥ payasastathā ||32||

33saktudhānākarambhāṇāṃ nopabhogyāścirasthitāḥ
|
pāyasaṃ kṛsaraṃ māṃsamapūpāśca vṛthākṛtāḥ ||33||

34apeyāścāpyabhakṣyāśca brāhmaṇairgṛhamedhibhiḥ
|
devānṛṣīnmanuṣyāṃśca pitṝn gṛhyāśca devatāḥ ||34||

35pūjayitvā tataḥ paścādgṛhastho bhoktumarhati
|
yathā pravrajito bhikṣustathaiva sve gṛhe vaset ||35||

36evaṃvṛttaḥ priyairdāraiḥ saṃvasan dharmamāpnuyāt
|
na dadyādyaśase dānaṃ na bhayānnopakāriṇe ||36||

37na nṛtyagītaśīleṣu hāsakeṣu ca dhārmikaḥ
|
na matte caiva nonmatte na stene na ca kutsake ||37||

38na vāgdhīne vivarṇe vā nāṅgahīne na vāmane
|
na durjane dauṣkule vā vratairyo vā na saṃskṛtaḥ ||38||

39na śrotriyamṛte dānaṃ brāhmaṇe brahmavarjite
|
asamyak caiva yaddattamasamyak ca pratigrahaḥ
|
ubhayaṃ syādanārthāya dāturādātureva ca ||39||

40yathā khadiramālambya śilāṃ vā'pyarṇavaṃ taran
|
majjeta majjatastadvaddātā yaśca pratigrahī ||40||

41kāṣṭhairārdairyathā vahnirupastīrṇo na dīpyate
|
tapaḥsvādhyāyacāritrairevaṃ hīnaḥ pratigrahī ||41||

42kapāle yadvadāpaḥ syuḥ śvadṛtau ca yathā payaḥ
|
āśrayasthānadoṣeṇa vṛttahīne tathā śrutam ||42||

43nirmantro nirvṛto yaḥ syādaśāstrajño'nasūyakaḥ
|
anukrośātpradātavyaṃ hīneṣvavratikeṣu ca ||43||

44na vai deyamanukrośāddīnārtāturakeṣvapi
|
āptācarita ityeva dharma ityeva vā punaḥ ||44||

45niṣkāraṇaṃ smṛtaṃ dattaṃ brāhmaṇe brahmavarjite
|
bhavedapātradoṣeṇa na cātrāsti vicāraṇā ||45||

46yathā dārumayo hastī yathā carmamayo mṛgaḥ
|
brāhmaṇaścānadhīyānastrayaste nāma bibhrati ||46||

47yathā ṣaṇḍho'phalaḥ strīṣu yathā gaurgavi cāphalā
|
śakunirvāpyapakṣaḥ syānnirmantro brāhmaṇastathā ||47||

48grāmadhānyaṃ yathā śūnyaṃ yathā kūpaśca nirjalaḥ
|
yathā hutamanagnau ca tathaiva syānnirākṛtau ||48||

49devatānāṃ pitṝṇāṃ ca havyakavyavināśakaḥ
|
śatrurarthaharo mūrkho na lokānprāptumarhati ||49||

50etatte kathitaṃ sarvaṃ yathāvṛttaṃ yudhiṣṭhira
|
samāsena mahaddhyetacchrotavyaṃ bharatarṣabha ||50||
