Mahābhārata 9.21 (Śalyaparvan)

1sañjaya uvāca|
tasmiṃstu nihate śūre śālve samiti śobhane
|
tavābhajyadbalaṃ vegādvāteneva mahādrumaḥ||1||

2tat prabhagnaṃ balaṃ dṛṣṭvā kṛtavarmā mahārathaḥ
|
dadhāra samare śūraḥ śatrusainyaṃ mahābalaḥ||2||

3sannivṛttāstu te śūrā dṛṣṭvā sātvatamāhave
|
śailopamaṃ sthiraṃ rājan kīryamāṇaṃ śarairyudhi||3||

4tataḥ pravavṛte yuddhaṃ kurūṇāṃ pāṇḍavaiḥ saha
|
nivṛttānāṃ mahārāja mṛtyuṃ kṛtvā nivartanam||4||

5tatrāścaryamabhūdyuddhaṃ sātvatasya paraiḥ saha
|
yadeko vārayāmāsa pāṇḍusenāṃ durāsadām||5||

6teṣāmanyonyasuhṛdāṃ kṛte karmaṇi duṣkare
|
siṃhanādaḥ prahṛṣṭānāṃ divispṛk sumahānabhūt||6||

7tena śabdena vitrastāḥ pañcālān bharatarṣabha
|
śinernaptā mahābāhuranvapadyata sātyakiḥ||7||

8sa samāsādya rājānaṃ kṣemadhūrtiṃ mahābalam
|
saptabhirniśitairbāṇairanayadyamasādanam||8||

9tamāyāntaṃ mahābāhuṃ pravapantaṃ śitāñśarān
|
javenābhyapataddhīmānhārdikyaḥ śinipuṅgavam||9||

10sātvatau ca mahāvīryau dhanvinau rathināṃ varau
|
anyonyamabhidhāvetāṃ śastrapravaradhāriṇau||10||

11pāṇḍavāḥ sahapañcālā yodhāścānye nṛpottamāḥ
|
prekṣakāḥ samapadyanta tayorghore samāgame||11||

12nārācairvatsadantaiśca vṛṣṇyandhakamahārathau
|
abhijaghnaturanyonyaṃ prahṛṣṭāviva kuñjarau||12||

13carantau vividhānmārgān hārdikyaśinipuṅgavau
|
muhurantardadhāte tau bāṇavṛṣṭyā parasparam||13||

14cāpavegabaloddhṛtānmārgaṇān vṛṣṇisiṃhayoḥ
|
ākāśe samapaśyāma pataṅgāniva śīghragān||14||

15tamekaṃ satyakarmāṇamāsādya hṛdikātmajaḥ
|
avidhyanniśitairbāṇaiścaturbhiścaturo hayān||15||

16sa dīrghabāhuḥ saṅkruddhastotrādita iva dvipaḥ
|
aṣṭabhiḥ kṛtavarmāṇamaviddhyatparameṣubhiḥ||16||

17tataḥ pūrṇāyatotsṛṣṭaiḥ kṛtavarmā śilāśitaiḥ
|
sātyakiṃ tribhirāhatya dhanurekena cicchide||17||

18nikṛttaṃ taddhanuḥ śreṣṭhamapāsya śinipuṅgavaḥ
|
anyadādatta vegena śaineyaḥ saśaraṃ dhanuḥ||18||

19tadādāya dhanuḥ śreṣṭhaṃ variṣṭhaḥ sarvadhanvinām
|
ārogya ca dhanuḥ śīghraṃ mahāvīryo mahābalaḥ||19||

20amṛṣyamāṇo dhanuṣaśchedanaṃ kṛtavarmaṇā
|
kupito'tirathaḥ śīghraṃ kṛtavarmāṇamabhyayāt||20||

21tataḥ suniśitairbāṇairdaśabhiḥ śinipuṅgavaḥ
|
jaghāna sūtaṃ cāśvāṃśca dhvajaṃ ca kṛtavarmaṇaḥ||21||

22tato rājanmaheṣvāsaḥ kṛtavarmā mahārathaḥ
|
hatāśvasūtaṃ samprekṣya rathaṃ hemapariṣkṛtam||22||

23roṣeṇa mahatā''viṣṭaḥ śūlamudyamya māriṣa
|
cikṣepa bhujavegena jighāṃsuḥ śinipuṅgavam||23||

24tacchūlaṃ sātvato hyājau nirbhidya niśitaiḥ śaraiḥ
|
cūrṇitaṃ pātayāmāsa mohayanniva mādhavam||24||

25tato'pareṇa bhallena hṛdyenaṃ samatāḍayat
|
sa yuddhe yuyudhānena hatāśvo hatasārathiḥ||25||

26kṛtavarmā kṛtastena dharaṇīmanvapadyata
|
tasminsātyakinā vīre dvairathe virathīkṛte||26||

27samapadyata sarveṣāṃ sainyānāṃ sumahadbhayam
|
putrasya tava cātyarthaṃ viṣādaḥ samajāyata||27||

28hatasūte hatāśve tu virathe kṛtavarmaṇi
|
hatāśvaṃ ca samālakṣya hatasūtamarindama||28||

29abhyadhāvatkṛpo rājañjighāṃsuḥ śinipuṅgavam
|
tamāropya rathopasthe miṣatāṃ sarvadhanvinām||29||

30apovāha mahābāhuṃ tūrṇamāyodhanādapi
|
śaineye'dhiṣṭhite rājanvirathe kṛtavarmaṇi||30||

31duryodhanabalaṃ sarvaṃ punarāsītparāṅmukham
|
tatpare nānvabudhyanta sainyena rajasā vṛtāḥ||31||

32tāvakāḥ pradrutā rājanduryodhanamṛte nṛpam
|
duryodhanastu samprekṣya bhagnaṃ svabalamantikāt||32||

33javenābhyapattūrṇaṃ sarvāṃścaiko nyavārayat
|
pāṇḍūṃśca sarvānsaṅkruddho dhṛṣṭadyumnaṃ ca pārṣatam||33||

34śikhaṇḍinaṃ draupadeyānpañcālānāṃ ca ye gaṇāḥ
|
kekayānsomakāṃścaiva sṛñjayāṃścaiva māriṣa||34||

35asambhramaṃ durādharṣaḥ śitairbāṇairavākirat
|
atiṣṭhadāhave yattaḥ putrastava mahābalaḥ||35||

36yathā yajñe mahānagnirmantrapūtaḥ prakāśavān
|
tathā duryodhano rājā saṅgrāme sarvato'bhavat||36||

37taṃ pare nābhyavartanta martyā mṛtyumivāhave
|
athānyaṃ rathamāsthāya hārdikyaḥ samapadyata||37||
