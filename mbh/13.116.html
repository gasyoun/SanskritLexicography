Mahābhārata 13.116 (Anuśāsanaparvan)

1yudhiṣṭhira uvāca|
ime vai mānavā loke nṛśaṃsā māṃsagṛddhinaḥ
|
visṛjya vividhānbhakṣyānmahārakṣogaṇā iva ||1||

2apūpānvividhākārānśākāni vividhāni ca
|
khāṇḍavānrasayogānna tathecchanti yathā''miṣam ||2||

3tatra me buddhiratraiva viṣaye parimuhyate
|
na manye rasataḥ kiñcinmāṃsato'stīti kiñcana ||3||

4tadicchāmi guṇān śrotuṃ māṃsasyābhakṣaṇe prabho
|
bhakṣaṇe caiva ye doṣāstāṃścaiva puruṣarṣabha ||4||

5sarvaṃ tattvena dharmajña yathāvadiha dharmataḥ
|
kiṃ cābhakṣyamabhakṣyaṃ vā sarvametadvadasva me ||5||

6yathaitadyādṛśaṃ caiva guṇā ye cāsya varjane
|
doṣā bhakṣayato ye'pi tanme brūhi pitāmaha ||6||

7bhīṣma uvāca|
evametanmahābāho yathā vadasi bhārata
|
na māṃsātparamaṃ kiñcidrasato vidyate bhuvi ||7||

8kṣatakṣīṇābhitaptānāṃ grāmyadharmaratātmanām
|
adhvanā karśitānāṃ ca na māṃsādvidyate param ||8||

9sadyo vardhayati prāṇānpuṣṭimagryāṃ dadhāti ca
|
na bhakṣyo'bhyadhikaḥ kaścinmāṃsādasti parantapa ||9||

10vivarjite tu bahavo guṇāḥ kauravanandana
|
ye bhavanti manuṣyāṇāṃ tānme nigadataḥ śṛṇu ||10||

11svamāṃsaṃ paramāṃsena yo vardhayitumicchati
|
nāsti kṣudratarastasmātsa nṛśaṃsataro naraḥ ||11||

12na hi prāṇāt priyataraṃ loke kiñcana vidyate
|
tasmāddayāṃ naraḥ kuryādyathā''tmani tathā pare ||12||

13śukrācca tāta sambhūtirmāṃsasyeha na saṃśayaḥ
|
bhakṣaṇe tu mahān doṣo nivṛttyā puṇyamucyate ||13||

14vidhinā vedadṛṣṭena tadbhuktveha na duṣyati
|
yajñārthe paśavaḥ sṛṣṭā ityapi śrūyate śrutiḥ ||14||

15ato'nyathā pravṛttānāṃ rākṣaso vidhirucyate
|
kṣatriyāṇāṃ tu yo dṛṣṭo vidhistamapi me śṛṇu ||15||

16vīryeṇopārjitaṃ māṃsaṃ yathā bhuñjanna duṣyati
|
āraṇyāḥ sarvadaivatyāḥ sarvaśaḥ prokṣitā mṛgāḥ ||16||

17agastyena purā rājan mṛgayā yena pūjyate
|
nātmānamaparityajya mṛgayā nāma vidyate ||17||

18samatāmupasaṅgamya bhūtaṃ hanyati hanti vā
|
ato rājarṣayaḥ sarve mṛgayāṃ yānti bhārata ||18||

19na hi lipyanti pāpena na caitatpātakaṃ viduḥ
|
na hyataḥ sadṛśaṃ kiñcidiha loke paratra ca ||19||

20yatsarveṣviha bhūteṣu dayā kauravanandana
|
na bhayaṃ vidyate jātu narasyeha dayāvataḥ ||20||

21dayāvatāmime lokāḥ pare cāpi tapasvinām
|
ahiṃsālakṣaṇo dharma iti dharmavido viduḥ ||21||

22yadahiṃsātmakaṃ karma tatkuryādātmavānnaraḥ
|
pitṛdaivatayajñeṣu prokṣitaṃ havirucyate ||22||

23abhayaṃ sarvabhūtebhyo yo dadāti dayāparaḥ
|
abhayaṃ tasya bhūtāni dadatītyanuśuśruma ||23||

24kṣataṃ ca skhalitaṃ caiva patitaṃ kṛṣṭamāhatam
|
sarvabhūtāni rakṣanti sameṣu viṣameṣu ca ||24||

25nainaṃ vyālamṛgā ghnanti na piśācā na rākṣasāḥ
|
mucyate bhayakāleṣu mokṣayedyo bhaye parān ||25||

26prāṇadānāt paraṃ dānaṃ na bhūtaṃ na bhaviṣyati
|
na hyātmanaḥ priyataraṃ kiñcidastīha niścitam ||26||

27aniṣṭaṃ sarvabhūtānāṃ maraṇaṃ nāma bhārata
|
mṛtyukāle hi bhūtānāṃ sadyo jāyati vepathuḥ ||27||

28jātijanmajarāduḥkhairnityaṃ saṃsārasāgare
|
jantavaḥ parivartante maraṇādudvijanti ca ||28||

29garbhavāseṣu pacyante kṣārāmlakaṭukai rasaiḥ
|
mūtrasvedapurīṣāṇāṃ paruṣairbhṛśadāruṇaiḥ ||29||

30jātāścāpyavaśāstatra cchidyamānāḥ punaḥ punaḥ
|
yācyamānāśca dṛśyante vivaśā māṃsagṛddhinaḥ ||30||

31kumbhīpāke ca pacyante tāṃ tāṃ yonimupāgatāḥ
|
ākramya māryamāṇāśca bhrāmyante vai punaḥ punaḥ ||31||

32nātmano'sti priyataraḥ pṛthivīmanusṛtya ha
|
tasmāt prāṇiṣu sarveṣu dayāvānātmavānbhavet ||32||

33sarvamāṃsāni yo rājan yāvajjīvaṃ na bhakṣayet
|
svarge sa vipulaṃ sthānaṃ prāpnuyānnātra saṃśayaḥ ||33||

34ye bhakṣayanti māṃsāni bhūtānāṃ jīvitaiṣiṇām
|
bhakṣyante te'pi bhūtaistairiti me nāsti saṃśayaḥ ||34||

35māṃsaṃ bhakṣayate yasmādbhakṣayiṣye tamapyaham
|
etanmāṃsasya māṃsatvamanubuddhyasva bhārataḥ||35||

36ghātakā vadhyate nityaṃ tathā vadhyati bhakṣitā
|
ākroṣṭā krudhyate rājaṃstathā dveṣyatvamāpnute ||36||

37yena yena śarīreṇa yadyatkarma karoti yaḥ
|
tena tena śarīreṇa tattatphalamupāśnute ||37||

38ahiṃsā paramo dharmastathā'hiṃsā paro damaḥ
|
ahiṃsā paramaṃ dānamahiṃsā paramaṃ tapaḥ ||38||

39ahiṃsā paramo yajñastathā'hiṃsā paraṃ phalam
|
ahiṃsā paramaṃ mitramahiṃsā paramaṃ sukham ||39||

40sarvayajñeṣu vā dānaṃ sarvatīrtheṣu vā''plutam
|
sarvadānaphalaṃ vāpi naitattulyamahiṃsayā ||40||

41ahiṃsasya tapo'kṣayyamahiṃsro yajate sadā
|
ahiṃsraḥ sarvabhūtānāṃ yathā mātā yathā pitā ||41||

42etat phalamahiṃsāyā bhūyaśca kurupuṅgava
|
na hi śakyā guṇā vaktumapi varṣaśatairapi ||42||
