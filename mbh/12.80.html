Mahābhārata 12.80 (Śāntiparvan)

1yudhiṣṭhira uvāca|
yadapyalpataraṃ karma tadapyekena duṣkaram
|
puruṣeṇāsahāyena kimu rājñā pitāmaha||1||

2kiṃśīlaḥ kiṃsamācāro rājño'tha sacivo bhavet
|
kīdṛśe viśvasedrājā kīdṛśe na ca viśvaset||2||

3bhīṣma uvāca|
caturvidhāni mitrāṇi rājñāṃ rājanbhavantyuta
|
sahārtho bhajamānaśca sahajaḥ kṛtrimastathā||3||

4dharmātmā pañcamaścāpi mitraṃ naikasya na dvayoḥ
|
yato dharmastato vā syāddharmastho vā tato bhavet||4||

5yastasyārtho na roceta na taṃ tasya prakāśayet
|
dharmādharmeṇa rājānaścaranti vijigīṣavaḥ||5||

6caturṇāṃ madhyamau śreṣṭhau nityaṃ śaṅkyau tathāparau
|
sarve nityaṃ śaṅkitavyāḥ pratyakṣaṃ kāryamātmanaḥ||6||

7na hi rājñā pramādo vai kartavyo mitrarakṣaṇe
|
pramādinaṃ hi rājānaṃ lokāḥ paribhavantyuta||7||

8asādhuḥ sādhutāmeti sādhurbhavati dāruṇaḥ
|
ariśca mitraṃ bhavati mitraṃ cāpi praduṣyati||8||

9anityacittaḥ puruṣastasminko jātu viśvaset
|
tasmātpradhānaṃ yatkāryaṃ pratyakṣaṃ tatsamācaret||9||

10ekāntena hi viśvāsaḥ kṛtsno dharmārthanāśakaḥ
|
aviśvāsaśca sarvatra mṛtyunā ca viśiṣyate||10||

11akālamṛtyurviśvāso viśvasan hi vipadyate
|
yasminkaroti viśvāsamicchatastasya jīvati||11||

12tasmādviśvasitavyaṃ ca śaṅkitavyaṃ ca keṣucit
|
eṣā nītigatistāta lakṣyā caiva sanātanī||12||

13yaṃ manyeta mamābhāvādimamarthāgamaṃ spṛśet
|
nityaṃ tasmācchaṅkitavyamamitraṃ tadvidurbudhāḥ||13||

14yasya kṣetrādapyudakaṃ kṣetramanyasya gacchati
|
na tatrānicchatastasya bhigheransarvasetavaḥ||14||

15tathaivātyudakādbhītastasya bhedanamicchati
|
yamevaṃ lakṣaṇaṃ vidyāttamamitraṃ vinirdiśet||15||

16yastu vṛddhyā na tṛpyeta kṣaye dīnataro bhavet
|
etaduttamamitrasya nimittamiti cakṣate||16||

17yanmanyeta mamābhāvādasyābhāvo bhavediti
|
tasminkurvīta viśvāsaṃ yathā pitari vai tathā||17||

18taṃ śaktyā vardhamānaśca sarvataḥ paribṛṃhayet
|
nityaṃ kṣatādvārayati yo dharmeṣvapi karmasu||18||

19kṣatādbhītaṃ vijānīyāduttamaṃ mitralakṣaṇam
|
ye tasya kṣatamicchanti te tasya ripavaḥ smṛtāḥ||19||

20vyasanānnityabhīto yaḥ samṛddhyā yo na duṣyati
|
yatsyādevaṃvidhaṃ mitraṃ tadātmasamamucyate||20||

21rūpa-varṇa-svaropetastitikṣuranasūyakaḥ
|
kulīnaḥ kulasampannaḥ sa tasmātpratyanantaraḥ||21||

22medhāvī smṛtimāndakṣaḥ prakṛtyā cānṛśaṃsyavān
|
yo mānito'mānito vā na ca duṣyetkadācana||22||

23ṛtvigvā yadi vā''cāryaḥ sakhā vā'tyantasaṃstutaḥ
|
gṛhe vasedamātyaste sa syātparamapūjitaḥ||23||

24sa te vidyātparaṃ mantraṃ prakṛtiṃ cārthadharmayoḥ
|
viśvāsaste bhavettatra yathā pitari vai tathā||24||

25naiva dvau na trayaḥ kāryā na mṛṣyeran parasparam
|
ekārthe hyeva bhūtānāṃ bhedo bhavati sarvadā||25||

26kīrtipradhāno yastu syādyaśca syātsamaye sthitaḥ
|
samarthān yaśca na dveṣṭi nānarthānkurute ca yaḥ||26||

27yo na kāmādbhayāllobhāt krodhādvā dharmamutsṛjet
|
dakṣaḥ paryāptavacanaḥ sa te syātpratyanantaraḥ||27||

28kulīnaḥ śīlasampannastitikṣuravikatthanaḥ
|
śūraścāryaśca vidvāṃśca pratipattiviśāradaḥ||28||

29ete hyamātyāḥ kartavyāḥ sarvakarmasvavasthitāḥ
|
pūjitāḥ saṃvibhaktāśca susahāyāḥ svanuṣṭhitāḥ||29||

30kṛtsnamete vinikṣiptāḥ pratirūpeṣu karmasu
|
yuktā mahatsu kāryeṣu śreyāṃsyutthāpayantyuta||30||

31ete karmāṇi kurvanti spardhamānā mithaḥ sadā
|
anutiṣṭhanti caivārthamācakṣāṇāḥ parasparam||31||

32jñātibhyaścaiva buddhyethā mṛtyoriva bhayaṃ sadā
|
uparājeva rājardhi jñātirna sahate sadā||32||

33ṛjormṛdorvadānyasya hrīmataḥ satyavādinaḥ
|
nānyo jñātermahābāho vināśamabhinandati||33||

34ajñātino'pi na sukhā nāvajñeyāstataḥ param
|
ajñātimantaṃ puruṣaṃ pare cābhibhavantyuta||34||

35nikṛtasya narairanyairjñātireva parāyaṇam
|
nānyo nikāraṃ sahate jñātirjñāteḥ kathañcana||35||

36ātmānameva jānāti nikṛtaṃ bāndhavairapi
|
teṣu santi guṇāścaiva nairguṇyaṃ caiva lakṣyate||36||

37nājñātiranugṛhṇāti na cājñātirnamasyati
|
ubhayaṃ jñātivargeṣu dṛśyate sādhvasādhu ca||37||

38sammānayetpūjayecca vācā nityaṃ ca karmaṇā
|
kuryācca priyametebhyo nāpriyaṃ kiñcidācaret||38||

39viśvastavadaviśvastasteṣu varteta sarvadā
|
na hi doṣā guṇo veti nirūpyasteṣu dṛśyate||39||

40asyaivaṃ vartamānasya puruṣasyāpramādinaḥ
|
amitrāḥ saṃprasīdanti tathā mitrībhavantyapi||40||

41ya evaṃ vartate nityaṃ jñātisambandhimaṇḍale
|
mitreṣvamitre madhyasthe ciraṃ yaśasi tiṣṭhati||41||
