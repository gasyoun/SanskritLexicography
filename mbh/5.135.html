Mahābhārata 5.135 (Udyogaparvan)

1putra uvāca|
kṛṣṇāyasasyeva ca te saṃhatya hṛdayaṃ kṛtam
|
mama mātastvakaruṇe vīraprajñe hyamarṣaṇe ||1||

2aho kṣatrasamācāro yatra māmitaraṃ yathā
|
niyojayasi yuddhāya paramāteva māṃ tathā ||2||

3īdṛśaṃ vacanaṃ brūyādbhavatī putramekajam
|
kiṃ nu te māmapaśyantyāḥ pṛthivyā api sarvayā ||3||

4kimābharaṇakṛtyena kiṃ bhogairjīvitena vā
|
mayi vā saṅgarahate priyaputre viśeṣataḥ ||4||

5mātovāca
|
sarvāvasthā hi viduṣāṃ tāta dharmārthakāraṇāt
|
tāvevābhisamīkṣyāhaṃ sañjaya tvāmacūcudam ||5||

6sa samīkṣya kramopeto mukhyaḥ kālo'yamāgataḥ
|
asmiṃścedāgate kāle kāryaṃ na pratipadyase ||6||

7asaṃbhāvitarūpastvamānṛśaṃsyaṃ kariṣyasi
|
taṃ tvāmayaśasā spṛṣṭaṃ na brūyāṃ yadi sañjaya ||7||

8kharīvātsalyamāhustanniḥsāmarthyamahetukam
|
sadbhirvigarhitaṃ mārgaṃ tyaja mūrkhaniṣevitam ||8||

9avidyā vai mahatyasti yāmimāṃ saṃśritāḥ prajāḥ
|
tava syādyadi sadvṛttaṃ tena me tvaṃ priyo bhaveḥ ||9||

10dharmārthaguṇayuktena netareṇa kathañcana
|
daivamānuṣayuktena sadbhirācaritena ca ||10||

11yo hyevamavinītena ramate putra naptṛṇā
|
anutthānavatā cāpi durvinītena durdhiyā ||11||

12ramate yastu putreṇa moghaṃ tasya prajāphalam
|
akurvanto hi karmāṇi kurvanto ninditāni ca ||12||

13sukhaṃ naiveha nāmutra labhante puruṣādhamāḥ
|
yuddhāya kṣatriyaḥ sṛṣṭaḥ sañjayeha jayāya ca ||13||

14jayanyā vadhyamāno vā prāpnotīndrasalokatām
|
na śakrabhavane puṇye divi tadvidyate sukham
|
yadamitrān vaśe kṛtvā kṣatriyaḥ sukhamedhate ||14||

15manyunā dahyamānena puruṣeṇa manasvinā
|
nikṛteneha bahuśaḥ śatrūn pratijigīṣayā ||15||

16ātmānaṃ vā parityajya śatruṃ vā vinipātya ca
|
ato'nyena prakāreṇa śāntirasya kuto bhavet ||16||

17iha prājño hi puruṣaḥ svalpamapriyamicchati
|
yasya svalpaṃ priyaṃ loke dhruvaṃ tasyālpamapriyam ||17||

18priyābhāvācca puruṣo naiva prāpnoti śobhanam
|
dhruvaṃ cābhāvamabhyeti gatvā gaṅgeva sāgaram ||18||

19putra uvāca|
neyaṃ matistvayā vācyā mātaḥ putre viśeṣataḥ
|
kāruṇyamevātra paśya bhūtveha jaḍamūkavat ||19||

20mātovāca
|
ato me bhūyasī nandiryadevamanupaśyasi
|
codyaṃ māṃ codayasyetadbhṛśaṃ vai codayāmi te ||20||

21atha tvāṃ pūjayiṣyāmi hatvā vai sarvasaindhavān
|
ahaṃ paśyāmi vijayaṃ kṛcchrabhāvitameva te ||21||

22putra uvāca|
akośasyāsahāyasya kutaḥ siddhirjayo mama
|
ityavasthāṃ viditvaitāmātmanā''tmani dāruṇām ||22||

23rājyādbhāvo nivṛtto me tridivādiva duṣkṛtaḥ
|
īdṛśaṃ bhavatī kañcidupāyamanupaśyati ||23||

24tanme pariṇataprajñe samyakprabrūhi pṛcchate
|
kariṣyāmi hi tatsarvaṃ yathāvadanuśāsanam ||24||

25mātovāca
|
putra nātmā'vamantavyaḥ pūrvābhirasamṛddhibhiḥ
|
abhūtvā hi bhayantyarthā bhūtvā naśyanti cāpare
|
amarṣeṇaiva cāpyarthā nārabdhavyāḥ subāliśaiḥ ||25||

26sarveṣāṃ karmaṇāṃ tāta phale nityamanityatā
|
anityamiti jānanto na bhavanti bhavanti ca ||26||

27atha yenaiva kurvanti naiva jātu bhavanti te
|
ekaguṇyamanīhāyāmabhāvaḥ karmaṇāṃ phalam ||27||

28atha dvaiguṇyamīhāyāṃ phalaṃ bhavati vā na vā
|
yasya prāgeva viditā sarvārthānāmanityatā ||28||

29nudedvṛddhisamṛddhī sa pratikūle nṛpātmaja
|
utthātavyaṃ jāgṛtavyaṃ yoktavyaṃ bhūtikarmasu ||29||

30bhaviṣyatītyeva manaḥ kṛtvā satatamavyathaiḥ
|
maṅgalāni puraskṛtya brāhmaṇāṃśceśvaraiḥ saha ||30||

31prājñasya nṛpaterāśu vṛddhirbhavati putraka
|
abhivartati lakṣmīstaṃ prācīmiva divākaraḥ||31||

32nidarśanānyupāyāṃśca bahūnyuddharṣaṇāni ca
|
anudarśitarūpo'si pakṣyāmi kuru pauruṣam ||32||

33puruṣārthamabhipretaṃ samāhartumihārhasi
|
kruddhān luṣdhān parikṣīṇānavaliptānvimānitān ||33||

34spardhinaścaiva ye kecittānyukta upadhāraya
|
etena tvaṃ prakāreṇa mahato bhetsyase gaṇān ||34||

35mahāvega ivodbhūto mātariśvā balāhakān
|
teṣāmagrapradāyī syāḥ kalpotthāyī priyaṃvadaḥ ||35||

36te tvāṃ priyaṃ kariṣyanti purodhāsyanti ca dhruvam
|
yadaiva śatrurjānīyāt sapatnaṃ tyaktajīvitam
|
tadaivāsmādudvijate sarpādveśmagatādiva ||36||

37taṃ viditvā parākrāntaṃ vaśe na kurute yadi
|
nirvādairnirvadedenamantatastadbhaviṣyati ||37||

38nirvādādāspadaṃ labdhā dhanavṛddhirbhaviṣyati
|
dhanavantaṃ hi mitrāṇi bhajante cāśrayanti ca ||38||

39skhalitārthaṃ punastāni santyajanti ca bāndhavāḥ
|
apyasminnāśvasante ca jugupsante ca tādṛśam ||39||

40śatruṃ kṛtvā yaḥ sahāyaṃ viśvāsamupagacchati
|
ataḥ saṃbhāvyamevaitadyadrājyaṃ prāpnuyāditi ||40||
