Mahābhārata 8.26 (Karṇaparvan)

1sañjaya uvāca|
dhṛṣṭadyumnaṃ kṛpo rājan vārayāmāsa saṃyuge
|
yathā dṛṣṭvā vane siṃhaṃ śarabho vārayedyudhi ||1||

2niruddhaḥ pārṣatastena gautamena balīyasā
|
padātpadaṃ vicalituṃ nāśakattatra bhārata ||2||

3gautamasya rathaṃ dṛṣṭvā dhṛṣṭadyumnarathaṃ prati
|
vitresaḥ sarvabhūtāni kṣayaṃ prāptaṃ ca menire ||3||

4tatrāvocanvimanaso rathinaḥ sādinastathā
|
droṇasya nidhanānnūnaṃ saṃkruddho dvipadāṃ varaḥ ||4||

5śāradvato mahātejā divyāstravidudāradhīḥ
|
api svasti bhavedadya dhṛṣṭadyumnasya gautamāt ||5||

6apīyaṃ vāhinī kṛtsnā mucyeta mahato bhayāt
|
apyayaṃ brāhmaṇaḥ sarvānna no hanyātsamāgatān ||6||

7yādṛśaṃ dṛśyate rūpamantakapratimaṃ bhṛśam
|
gamiṣyatyadya padavīṃ bhāradvājasya gautamaḥ ||7||

8ācāryaḥ kṣiprahastaśca vijayī ca sadā yudhi
|
astravānvīryasaṃpannaḥ krodhena ca samanvitaḥ ||8||

9pārṣataśca mahāyuddhe vimukho'dyābhilakṣyate
|
ityevaṃ vividhā vācastāvakānāṃ paraiḥ saha ||9||

10vyaśrūyanta mahārāja tayostatra samāgame
|
viniḥśvasya tataḥ krodhātkṛpaḥ śāradvato nṛpa ||10||

11pārṣataṃ cārdayāmāsa niśceṣṭaṃ sarvamarmasu
|
sa hanyamānaḥ samare gautamena mahātmanā ||11||

12kartavyaṃ na sma jānāti mohena mahatā''vṛtaḥ
|
tamabravīttato yantā kaccit kṣemaṃ tu pārṣata ||12||

13īdṛśaṃ vyasanaṃ yuddhe na te dṛṣṭaṃ mayā kvacit
|
daivayogāttu te bāṇā nāpatanmarmabhedinaḥ ||13||

14preṣitā dvijamukhyena marmāṇyuddiśya sarvataḥ
|
vyāvartaye rathaṃ tūrṇaṃ nadīvegamivārṇavāt ||14||

15avadhyaṃ brāhmaṇa manye yena te vikramo hataḥ
|
dhṛṣṭadyumnastato rājañśanakairabravīdvacaḥ ||15||

16muhyate me manastāta gātrasvedaśca jāyate
|
vepathuśca śarīre me romaharṣaśca sārathe ||16||

17varjayan brāhmaṇaṃ yuddhe śanairyāhi yato'rjunaḥ
|
arjunaṃ bhīmasenaṃ vā samare prāpya sārathe ||17||

18kṣemamadya bhavedevameṣā me naiṣṭhikī matiḥ
|
tataḥ prāyānmahārāja sārathistvarayan hayān ||18||

19yato bhīmo maheṣvāso yuyudhe tava sainikaiḥ
|
pradrutaṃ ca rathaṃ dṛṣṭvā dhṛṣṭadyumnasya māriṣa ||19||

20kirañśataśatānyeva gautamo'nuyayau tadā
|
śaṅkhaṃ ca pūrayāmāsa muhurmuhurarindamaḥ ||20||

21pārṣataṃ trāsayāmāsa mahendro namuciṃ yathā
|
śikhaṇḍinaṃ tu samare bhīṣmamṛtyuṃ durāsadam ||21||

22hārdikyo vārayāmāsa smayanniva muhurmuhuḥ
|
śikhaṇḍī tu samāsādya hṛdikānāṃ mahāratham ||22||

23pañcabhirniśitairbhallairjatrudeśe samāhanat
|
kṛtavarmā tu saṃkruddho bhittvā ṣaṣṭyā patatribhiḥ ||23||

24dhanurekena ciccheda hasan rājanmahārathaḥ
|
athānyaddhanurādāya drupadasyātmajo balī ||24||

25tiṣṭha tiṣṭheti saṃkruddho hārdikyaṃ pratyabhāṣata
|
tato'sya navatiṃ bāṇān rukmapuṅkhānsutejanān ||25||

26preṣayāmāsa rājendra te'syābhraśyanta varmaṇaḥ
|
vitathāṃstānsamālakṣya patitāṃśca mahītale ||26||

27kṣurapreṇa sutīkṣṇena kārmukaṃ cicchide bhṛśam
|
athainaṃ chinnadhanvānaṃ bhagnaśṛṅgamivarṣabham ||27||

28aśītyā mārgaṇaiḥ kruddho bāhvorurasi cārpayat
|
kṛtavarmā tu saṃkruddho mārgaṇaiḥ kṣatavikṣataḥ ||28||

29vavāma rudhiraṃ gātraiḥ kuṃbhavaktrādivodakam
|
rudhireṇa pariklinnaḥ kṛtavarmā tvarājata ||29||

30varṣeṇa kledito rājan yathā gairikaparvataḥ
|
athānyaddhanurādāya samārgaṇaguṇaṃ prabhuḥ ||30||

31śikhaṇḍinaṃ bāṇagaṇaiḥ skandhadeśe vyatāḍayat
|
skandhadeśasthitairbāṇaiḥ śikhaṇḍī tu vyarājata ||31||

32śākhāpraśākhāvipulaḥ sumahānpādapo yathā
|
tāvanyonyaṃ bhṛśaṃ viddhvā rudhireṇa samukṣitau ||32||

33anyonyaśṛṅgābhihatau rejaturvṛṣabhāviva
|
anyonyasya vadhe yatnaṃ kurvāṇau tau mahārathau ||33||

34rathābhyāṃ ceratustatra maṇḍalāni sahasraśaḥ
|
kṛtavarmā mahārāja pārṣataṃ niśitaiḥ śaraiḥ ||34||

35raṇe vivyādha saptatyā svarṇapuṅkhaiḥ śilāśitaiḥ
|
tato'sya samare bāṇaṃ bhojaḥ praharatāṃ varaḥ ||35||

36jīvitāntakaraṃ ghoraṃ vyajasṛttvarayā'nvitaḥ
|
sa tenābhihato rājan mūrcchāmāśu samāviśat ||36||

37dhvajayaṣṭiṃ ca sahasā śiśriye kaśmalāvṛtaḥ
|
apovāha raṇāttūrṇaṃ sārathī rathināṃ varam ||37||

38hārdikyaśarasaṃtaptaṃ niḥśvasantaṃ punaḥ punaḥ
|
parājite tataḥ śūre drupadasyātmaje prabho
|
vyadravatpāṇḍavī senā vyadhamānā samantataḥ ||38||
