Mahābhārata 12.331 (Śāntiparvan)

1nārada uvāca|
sukhaduḥkhaviparyāso yadā samanupadyate
|
nainaṃ prajñā sunītaṃ vā trāyate nāpi pauruṣam||1||

2svabhāvādyatnamātiṣṭhedyatnavānnāvasīdati
|
jarāmaraṇarogebhyaḥ priyamātmānamuddharet||2||

3rujanti hi śarīrāṇi rogāḥ śārīramānasāḥ
|
sāyakā iva tīkṣṇāgrāḥ prayuktā dṛḍhadhanvibhiḥ||3||

4vyathitasya vidhitsābhistāmyato jīvitaiṣiṇaḥ
|
avaśasya vināśāya śarīramapakṛṣyate||4||

5sravanti na nivartante srotāṃsi saritāmiva
|
āyurādāya martyānāṃ rājyahāni punaḥ punaḥ||5||

6vyatyayo hyayamatyantaṃ pakṣayoḥ śuklakṛṣṇayoḥ
|
jātānmartyāñjarayati nimeṣānnāvatiṣṭhate||6||

7sukhaduḥkhāni bhūtānāmajaro jarayatyasau
|
ādityo hyastamabhyeti punaḥ punarudeti ca||7||

8adṛṣṭapūrvānādāya bhāvānapariśaṅkitān
|
iṣṭāniṣṭānmanuṣyāṇāmastaṃ gacchanti rātrayaḥ||8||

9yo'yamicchedyathākāmaṃ kāmānāṃ tadavāpnuyāt
|
yadi syānna parādhīnaṃ puruṣasya kriyāphalam||9||

10saṃyatāśca hi dakṣāśca matimantaśca mānavāḥ
|
dṛśyante niṣphalāḥ saṃtaḥ prahīṇāḥ sarvakarmabhiḥ||10||

11apare bāliśā: santo nirguṇāḥ puruṣādhamāḥ
|
āśīrbhirapyasaṃyuktā dṛśyante sarvakāminaḥ||11||

12bhūtānāmaparaḥ kaściddhiṃsāyāṃ satatotthitaḥ
|
vañcanāyāṃ ca lokasya sa sukheṣveva jīryate||12||

13aceṣṭamānamāsīnaṃ śrī: kañcidupatiṣṭhate
|
kaścitkarmānusṛtyānyo nāprāpyamadhigacchati||13||

14aparādhaṃ samācakṣva puruṣasya svabhāvataḥ
|
śukramanyatra saṃbhūtaṃ punaranyatra gacchati||14||

15tasya yonau prayuktasya garbho bhavati vā na vā
|
āmrapuṣpopamā yasya nivṛttirupalabhyate||15||

16keṣāñcitputrakāmānāmanusantānamicchatām
|
siddhau prayatamānānāṃ na cāṇḍamupajāyate||16||

17garbhāccodvijamānānāṃ kruddhādāśīviṣādiva
|
āyuṣmāñjāyate putraḥ kathaṃ pretya ivābhavat||17||

18devāniṣṭvā tapastaptvā kṛpaṇaiḥ putragṛddhibhiḥ
|
daśa māsānparidhṛtā jāyante kulapāṃsanāḥ||18||

19apare dhanadhānyāni bhogāṃśca pitṛsañjitān
|
vipulānabhijāyante labdhāstaireva maṅgalaiḥ||19||

20anyonyaṃ samabhipretya maithunasya samāgame
|
upadrava ivāviṣṭo yonigarbhaḥ prapadyate||20||

21śīghraṃ paraśarīrāṇi cchinnabījaṃ śarīriṇam
|
prāṇinaṃ prāṇasaṃrodhe māṃsaśleṣmaviceṣṭitam||21||

22nirdagdhaṃ paradehe'pi paradehaṃ calācalam
|
vinaśyantaṃ vināśānte nāvi nāvamivāhitam||22||

23saṅgatyā jaṭhare nyastaṃ retobindumacetanam
|
kena yatnena jīvantaṃ garbhaṃ tvamiha paśyasi||23||

24annapānāni jīryante yatra bhakṣāśca bhakṣitāḥ
|
tasminnevodare garbha: kiṃ nānnamiva jīryate||24||

25garbhe mūtrapurīṣāṇāṃ svabhāvaniyatā gatiḥ
|
dhāraṇe vā visarge vā na kartā vidyate'vaśaḥ||25||

26sravanti hyudarādgarbhā jāyamānāstathā pare
|
āgamena tathā'nyeṣāṃ vināśa upapadyate||26||

27etasmādyonisambandhādyo bījaṃ parimucyate
|
prajāṃ ca labhate kāñcitpunardvandveṣu sajjati||27||

28sa tasya sahajātasya saptamīṃ navamīṃ daśām
|
prāpnuvanti tataḥ pañca na bhavanti gatāyuṣaḥ||28||

29nābhyutthāne manuṣyāṇāṃ yogāḥ syurnātra saṃśayaḥ
|
vyādhibhiśca vimathyante vyādhaiḥ kṣudramṛgā iva||29||

30vyādhibhirmathyamānānāṃ tyajatāṃ vipulaṃ dhanam
|
vedanāṃ nāpakarṣanti yatamānāścikitsakāḥ||30||

31te cātinipuṇā vaidyāḥ kuśalāḥ saṃbhṛtauṣadhāḥ
|
vyādhibhiḥ parikṛṣyante mṛgā vyādhairivārditāḥ||31||

32te pibantaḥ kaṣāyāṃśca sarpīṃṣi vividhāni ca
|
dṛśyante jarayā bhagnā nagā nāgairivottamaiḥ||32||

33ke vā bhuvi cikitsante rogārtānmṛgapakṣiṇaḥ
|
śvāpadāni daridrāṃśca prāyo nārtā bhavanti te||33||

34ghorānapi durādharṣānnṛpatīnugratejasaḥ
|
ākramyādadate rogāḥ paśūnpaśugaṇā iva||34||

35iti lokamanākrandaṃ mohaśokapariplutam
|
srotasā sahasā''kṣiptaṃ hriyamāṇaṃ balīyasā||35||

36na dhanena na rājyena nogreṇa tapasā tathā
|
svabhāvamativartante ye niyuktāḥ śarīriṇaḥ||36||

37na mriyeranna jīryeransarve syuḥ sarvakāminaḥ
|
nāpriyaṃ prati paśyeyurutthānasya phale sati||37||

38uparyupari lokasya sarvo gantuṃ samīhate
|
yatate ca yathāśakti na ca tadvartate tathā||38||

39aiśvaryamadamattāṃśca mattānmadyamadena ca
|
apramattāḥ śaṭhāḥ śūrā vikrāntāḥ paryupāsate||39||

40kleśāḥ parinivartante keṣāñcidasamīkṣitāḥ
|
svaṃ svaṃ ca punaranyeṣāṃ na kiñcidadhigamyate||40||

41mahacca phalavaiṣamyaṃ dṛśyate karmasandhiṣu
|
vahanti śibikāmanye yāntyanye śibikāgatāḥ||41||

42sarveṣāmṛddhikāmānāmanye rathapuraḥsurāḥ
|
manuṣyāśca gatastrīkāḥ śataśo vividhāḥ striyaḥ||42||

43dvandvārāmeṣu bhūteṣu gacchantyekaikaśo narāḥ
|
idamanyatpadaṃ paśya mā'tra mohaṃ kariṣyasi||43||

44tyaja dharmamadharmaṃ ca ubhe satyānṛte tyaja
|
ubhe satyānṛte tyaktvā yena tyajasi taṃ tyaja||44||

45etatte paramaṃ guhyamākhyātamṛṣisattama
|
yena devāḥ parityajya martyalokaṃ divaṃ gatāḥ||45||

46nāradasya vacaḥ śrutvā śukaḥ paramabuddhimān
|
sañcintya manasā dhīro niścayaṃ nādhyagacchata||46||

47putradārairmahānkleśo vidyāmnāye mahāñcchramaḥ
|
kiṃ nu syācchāśvataṃ sthānamalpakleśaṃ mahodayam||47||

48tato muhūrtaṃ sañcintya niścitāṃ gatimātmanaḥ
|
parāvarajño dharmasya parāṃ naiḥśreyasīṃ gatim||48||

49kathaṃ tvahamasaṃśliṣṭo gaccheyaṃ gatimuttamām
|
nāvarteyaṃ yathā bhūyo yonisaṃkarasāgare||49||

50paraṃ bhāvaṃ hi kāṅkṣāmi yatra nāvartate punaḥ
|
sarvasaṅgānparityajya niścito manasā gatim||50||

51tatra yāsyāmi yatrātmā śamaṃ me'dhigamiṣyati
|
akṣayaścāvyayaścaiva yatra sthāsyāmi śāśvataḥ||51||

52na tu yogamṛte śakyā prāptuṃ sā paramā gatiḥ
|
avabandho hi buddhasya karmabhirnopapadyate||52||

53tasmādyogaṃ samāsthāya tyaktvā gṛhakalevaram
|
vāyubhūtaḥ pravekṣyāmi tejorāśiṃ divākaram||53||

54na hyeṣa kṣayatāṃ yāti somaḥ suragaṇairyathā
|
kampitaḥ patate bhūmiṃ punaścaivādhirohati||54||

55kṣīyate hi sadā somaḥ punaścaivābhipūryate
|
necchāmyevaṃ viditvaite hrāsavṛddhī punaḥ punaḥ||55||

56ravistu santāpayate lokān raśmibhirulbaṇaiḥ
|
sarvatasteja ādatte nityamakṣayamaṇḍalaḥ||56||

57ato me rocate gantumādityaṃ dīptatejasam
|
atra vatsyāmi durdharṣo niḥśaṅkenāntarātmanā||57||

58sūryasya sadane cāhaṃ nikṣipyedaṃ kalevaram
|
ṛṣibhiḥ saha yāsyāmi sauraṃ tejo'tiduḥsaham||58||

59āpṛcchāmi nagānnāgāngirimurvīṃ diśo divam
|
devadānavagandharvānpiśācoragarākṣasān||59||

60lokeṣu sarvabhūtāni pravekṣyāmi na saṃśayaḥ
|
paśyantu yogavīryaṃ me sarve devāḥ saharṣibhiḥ||60||

61athānujñāpya tamṛṣiṃ nāradaṃ lokaviśrutam
|
tasmādanujñāṃ samprāpya jagāma pitaraṃ prati||61||

62so'bhivādya mahātmānaṃ kṛṣṇadvaipāyanaṃ munim
|
śukaḥ pradakṣiṇaṃ kṛtvā kṛṣṇamāpṛṣṭavānmunim||62||

63śrutvā carṣistadvacanaṃ śukasya prīto mahātmā punarāha cainam
|
bho bho putra sthīyatāṃ tāvadadya yāvaccakṣuḥ prīṇayāmi tvadarthe||63||

64nirapekṣaḥ śuko bhūtvā ni:sneho muktasaṃśayaḥ
|
mokṣamevānusaṃcintya gamanāya mano dadhe||64||

65pitaraṃ saṃparityajya jagāma munisattamaḥ
|
kailāsapṛṣṭhaṃ vipulaṃ siddhasaṃghaniṣevitam||65||
