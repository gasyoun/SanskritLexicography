Mahābhārata 3.213 (Āraṇyakaparvan)

1brāhmaṇa uvāca|
pārthivaṃ dhātumāsādya śārīro'gniḥ kathaṃ bhavet
|
avakāśaviśeṣeṇa kathaṃ vartayate'nilaḥ||1||

2mārkaṇḍeya uvāca|
praśnametaṃ samuddiṣṭaṃ brāhmaṇena yudhiṣṭhira
|
vyādhastu kathayāmāsa brāhmaṇāya mahātmane||2||

3vyādha uvāca|
mūrdhānamāśrito vahniḥ śarīraṃ paripālayan
|
prāṇo mūrdhani cāgnau ca vartamāno viceṣṭate||3||

4bhūtaṃ bhavyaṃ bhaviṣyaṃ ca sarvaṃ prāṇe pratiṣṭhitam
|
śreṣṭhaṃ tadeva bhūtānāṃ brahmayonimupāsmahe||4||

5sa jantuḥ sarvabhūtātmā puruṣaḥ sa sanātanaḥ
|
mahān buddhirahaṅkāro bhūtānāṃ viṣayaśca saḥ||5||

6evaṃ tviha sa sarvatra prāṇena paripālyate
|
pṛṣṭhatastu samānena svāṃ svāṃ gatimupāśritaḥ||6||

7bastimūlaṃ gudaṃ caiva pāvakaṃ samupāśritaḥ
|
vahan mūtraṃ purīṣaṃ vā'pyapānaḥ parivartate||7||

8prayatne karmaṇi bale sa eṣa triṣu vartate
|
udānamiti taṃ prāhuradhyātmaviduṣo janāḥ||8||

9sandhau sandhau sanniviṣṭaḥ sarveṣvapi tathā'nilaḥ
|
śarīreṣu manuṣyāṇāṃ vyāna ityupadiśyate||9||

10dhātuṣvagnistu vitataḥ sa tu vāyusamīritaḥ
|
rasān dhātūṃśca doṣāṃśca vartayan paridhāvati||10||

11prāṇānāṃ sannipātāt tu sannipātaḥ prajāyate
|
ūṣmā cāgniriti jñeyo yo'nnaṃ pacati dehinām||11||

12samānodānayormadhye prāṇāpānau samāhitau
|
samarthitastvadhiṣṭhānaṃ samyak pacati pāvakaḥ||12||

13asyāpi pāyuparyantastathā syādgudasaṃjñitaḥ
|
srotāṃsi tasmājjāyante sarvaprāṇeṣu dehinām||13||

14agnivegavahaḥ prāṇo gudānte pratihanyate
|
sa ūrdhvamāgamya punaḥ samutkṣipati pāvakam||14||

15pakvāśayastvadho nābhyāmūrdhvamāmāśayaḥ sthitaḥ
|
nābhimadhye śarīrasya prāṇāḥ sarve pratiṣṭhitāḥ||15||

16pravṛttā hṛdayāt sarve tiryagūrdhvamadhastathā
|
vahantyannarasān nāḍyo daśa prāṇapracoditāḥ||16||

17yogināmeṣa mārgastu yena gacchanti tat param
|
jitaklamāḥ samā dhīrā mūrdhanyātmānamādadhuḥ
|
evaṃ sarveṣu vitatau prāṇāpānau hi dehiṣu||17||

18ekādaśavikārātmā kalāsambhārasambhṛtaḥ
|
mūrtimantaṃ hi taṃ viddhi nityaṃ yogajitātmakam||18||

19tasmin yaḥ saṃsthito hyagnirnityaṃ sthālyāmivāhitaḥ
|
ātmānaṃ taṃ vijānīhi nityaṃ yogajitātmakam||19||

20devo yaḥ saṃsthitastasminnabbinduriva puṣkare
|
kṣetrajñaṃ taṃ vijānīhi nityaṃ yogajitātmakam||20||

21jīvātmakāni jānīhi rajaḥ sattvaṃ tamastathā
|
jīvamātmaguṇaṃ viddhi tathā''tmānaṃ parātmakam||21||

22sacetanaṃ jīvaguṇaṃ vadanti sa ceṣṭate ceṣṭayate ca sarvam
|
tataḥ paraṃ kṣetravido vadanti prākalpayadyo bhuvanāni sapta||22||

23evaṃ sarveṣu bhūteṣu bhūtātmā samprakāśate
|
dṛśyate tvagryayā buddhyā sūkṣmayā jñānavedibhiḥ||23||

24cittasya hi prasādena hanti karma śubhāśubham
|
prasannātmā''tmani sthitvā sukhamānantyamaśnute||24||

25lakṣaṇaṃ tu prasādasya yathā tṛptaḥ sukhaṃ svapet
|
nivāte vā yathā dīpo dīpyet kuśaladīpitaḥ||25||

26pūrvarātre pare caiva yuñjānaḥ satataṃ manaḥ
|
laghvāhāro viśuddhātmā paśyannātmānamātmani||26||

27pradīpteneva dīpena manodīpena paśyati
|
dṛṣṭvā''tmānaṃ nirātmānaṃ sa tadā vipramucyate||27||

28sarvopāyaistu lobhasya krodhasya ca vinigrahaḥ
|
etat pavitraṃ lokānāṃ tapo vai saṅkramo mataḥ||28||

29nityaṃ krodhāt tapo rakṣeddharmaṃ rakṣecca matsarāt
|
vidyāṃ mānāpamānābhyāmātmānaṃ tu pramādataḥ||29||

30ānṛśaṃsyaṃ paro dharmaḥ kṣamā ca paramaṃ balam
|
ātmajñānaṃ paraṃ jñānaṃ satyaṃ vrataparaṃ vratam||30||

31satyasya vacanaṃ śreyaḥ satyaṃ jñānaṃ hitaṃ bhavet
|
yadbhūtahitamatyantaṃ tadvai satyaṃ paraṃ matam||31||

32yasya sarve samārambhā nirāśīrbandhanāḥ sadā
|
tyāge yasya hutaṃ sarvaṃ sa tyāgī sa ca buddhimān||32||

33yato na gururapyenaṃ śrāvayedupapādayet
|
taṃ vidyādbrahmaṇo yogaṃ viyogaṃ yogasaṃjñitam||33||

34na hiṃsyāt sarvabhūtāni metrāyaṇagataścaret
|
nedaṃ jīvitamāsādya vairaṃ kurvīta kenacit||34||

35ākiñcanyaṃ susantoṣo nirāśitvamacāpalam
|
etadeva paraṃ jñānaṃ sadātmajñānamuttamam||35||

36parigrahaṃ parityajya bhavedbuddhyā yatavrataḥ
|
aśokaṃ sthānamāśritya niścalaṃ pretya ceha ca||36||

37taponityena dāntena muninā saṃyatātmanā
|
ajitaṃ jetukāmena bhāvyaṃ saṅgeṣvasaṅginā||37||

38guṇāguṇamanāsaṅgamekakāryamanantaram
|
etat tadbrahmaṇo vṛttamāhurekapadaṃ sukham||38||

39parityajati yo duḥkhaṃ sukhaṃ cāpyubhayaṃ naraḥ
|
brahma prāpnoti so'tyantamasaṅgena ca gacchati||39||

40yathāśrutamidaṃ sarvaṃ samāsena dvijottama
|
etat te sarvamākhyātaṃ kiṃ bhūyaḥ śrotumicchasi||40||
