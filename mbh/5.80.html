Mahābhārata 5.80 (Udyogaparvan)

1nakula uvāca|
uktaṃ bahuvidhaṃ vākyaṃ dharmarājena mādhava
|
dharmajñena vadānyena śrutaṃ caiva hi tattvayā ||1||

2matamājñāya rājñaśca bhīmasenena mādhava
|
saṃśamo bāhuvīryaṃ ca khyāpitaṃ mādhavātmanaḥ ||2||

3tathaiva phālgunenāpi yaduktaṃ tattvayā śrutam
|
ātmanaśca mataṃ vīra kathitaṃ bhavatā sakṛt ||3||

4sarvametadatikramya śrutvā paramataṃ bhavān
|
yatprāptakālaṃ manyethāstatkuryāḥ puruṣottama ||4||

5tasmiṃstasminnimitte hi mataṃ bhavati keśava
|
prāptakālaṃ manuṣyeṇa kṣamaṃ kāryamarindama ||5||

6anyathā cintito hyarthaḥ punarbhavati so'nyathā
|
anityamatayo loke narāḥ puruṣasattama ||6||

7anyathā buddhayā hyāsannasmāsu vanavāsiṣu
|
adṛśyeṣvanyathā kṛṣṇa dṛśyeṣu punaranyathā ||7||

8asmākamapi vārṣṇeya vane vicaratāṃ tadā
|
na tathā praṇayo rājye yathā samprati vartate ||8||

9nivṛttavanavāsānnaḥ śrutvā vīra samāgatāḥ
|
akṣauhiṇyo hi saptemāstvatprasādājjanārdana ||9||

10imān hi puruṣavyāghrānacintyabalapauruṣān
|
āttaśastrān raṇe dṛṣṭvā na vyathediha kaḥ pumān ||10||

11sa bhavān kurumadhye taṃ sāntvapūrvaṃ bhayottaram
|
brūyādvākyaṃ yathā mando na vyatheta suyodhanaḥ ||11||

12yudhiṣṭhiraṃ bhīmasenaṃ bībhatsuṃ cāparājitam
|
sahadevaṃ ca māṃ caiva tvāṃ ca rāmaṃ ca keśava ||12||

13sātyakiṃ ca mahāvīryaṃ virāṭaṃ ca sahātmajam
|
drupadaṃ ca sahāmātyaṃ dhṛṣṭadyumnaṃ ca mādhava ||13||

14kāśirājaṃ ca vikrāntaṃ dhṛṣṭaketuṃ ca cedipam
|
māṃsaśoṇitabhṛnmartyaḥ pratiyudhyeta ko yudhi ||14||

15sa bhavān gamanādeva sādhayiṣyatyasaṃśayam
|
iṣṭamarthaṃ mahābāho dharmarājasya kevalam ||15||

16viduraścaiva bhīṣmaśca droṇaśca sahabāhlikaḥ
|
śreyaḥ samarthā vijñātumucyamānāstvayā'nagha ||16||

17te cainamanuneṣyanti dhṛtarāṣṭraṃ janādhipam
|
taṃ ca pāpasamācāraṃ sahāmātyaṃ suyodhanam ||17||

18śrotā cārthasya vidurastvaṃ ca vaktā janārdana
|
kamivārthaṃ nivartantaṃ sthāpayetāṃ na vartmani ||18||
