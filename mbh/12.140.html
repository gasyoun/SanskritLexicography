Mahābhārata 12.140 (Śāntiparvan)

1yudhiṣṭhira uvāca|
yugakṣayātparikṣīṇe dharme loke ca bhārata
|
dasyubhiḥ pīḍyamāne ca kathaṃ stheyaṃ pitāmaha ||1||

2bhīṣma uvāca|
atra te vartayiṣyāmi nītimāpatsu bhārata
|
utsṛjyāpi ghṛṇāṃ kāle yathā varteta bhūmipaḥ ||2||

3atrāpyudāharantīmamitihāsaṃ purātanam
|
bhāradvājasya saṃvādaṃ rājñaḥ śatrujayasya ca ||3||

4rājā śatrujayo nāma sauvīreṣu mahārathaḥ
|
bhāradvājamupāgamya papracchārthaviniścayam ||4||

5alabdhasya kathaṃ lipsā labdhaṃ kena vivardhate
|
vardhitaṃ pālyate kena pālitaṃ praṇayetkatham ||5||

6tasmai viniścitārthāya paripṛṣṭo'rthaniścayam
|
uvāca brāhmaṇo vākyamidaṃ hetumaduttamam ||6||

7nityamudyatadaṇḍaḥ syānnityaṃ vivṛtapauruṣaḥ
|
acchidraśchidradarśī ca pareṣāṃ vivarānugaḥ ||7||

8nityamudyatadaṇḍasya bhṛśamudvijate naraḥ
|
tasmātsarvāṇi bhūtāni daṇḍenaiva prasādhayet ||8||

9evaṃ daṇḍaṃ praśaṃsanti paṇḍitāstattvadarśinaḥ
|
tasmāccatuṣṭaye tasminpradhāno daṇḍa ucyate ||9||

10chinnamūle tvadhiṣṭhāne sarveṣāṃ jīvanaṃ hatam
|
katha hi śākhāstiṣṭheyuśchinnamūle vanaspatau ||10||

11mūlamevāditaśchindyātparapakṣasya paṇḍitaḥ
|
tataḥ sahāyān pakṣaṃ ca mūlamevānusādhayet ||11||

12sumantritaṃ suvikrāntaṃ suyuddhaṃ supalāyitam
|
āpadāspadakāle tu kurvīta na vicārayet ||12||

13vāṅmātreṇa vinītaḥ syāddhṛdayena yathā kṣuraḥ
|
ślakṣṇapūrvābhibhāṣī ca kāmakrodhau vivarjayet ||13||

14sapatnasahite kārye kṛtvā sandhiṃ na viśvaset
|
apakrāmet tataḥ śīghaṃ kṛtakāryo vicakṣaṇaḥ ||14||

15śatru ca mitrarūpeṇa sāntvenaivābhisāntvayet
|
nityaśaśnodvijettasmādgṛhātsarpayutādiva ||15||

16yasya buddhiḥ paribhavettamatītena sāntvayet
|
anāgatena duṣprajñaṃ pratyutpannena paṇḍitam ||16||

17añjaliṃ śapathaṃ sāntvaṃ praṇamya śirasā vadet
|
aśrupramārjanaṃ caiva kartavyaṃ bhūtimicchatā ||17||

18vahedamitraṃ skandhena yāvatkālasya paryayaḥ
|
prāptakālaṃ tu vijñāya bhindyādghaṭamivāśmani ||18||

19muhūrtamapi rājendra tindukālātavajjvalet
|
na tuṣāgnirivānarcirdhūmāyeta ciraṃ naraḥ ||19||

20nānārthiko'rthasambandhaṃ kṛtaghnena samācaret
|
arthī tu śakyate bhoktuṃ kṛtakāryo'vamanyate
|
tasmātsarvāṇi kāryāṇi sāvaśeṣāṇi kārayet ||20||

21kokilasya varāhasya meroḥ śūnyasya veśmanaḥ
|
naṭasya bhaktimitrasya yacchrecastatsamācaret ||21||

22utthāyotthāya gaccheta nityayukto riporgṛhān
|
kuśalaṃ cāsya pṛccheta yadyapyakuśalaṃ bhavet ||22||

23nālasāḥ prāpnuvantyarthānna klībā nābhimāninaḥ
|
na ca lokaravādbhītā na vai śaśvatpratīkṣiṇaḥ ||23||

24nātmacchidraṃ ripurvidyādvihācchidraṃ parasya tu
|
gūhetkūrma ivāṅgāni rakṣedvivaramātmanaḥ ||24||

25bakavaccintayedarthān siṃhatacca parākramet
|
vṛkavaccāvalumpeta śaravacca viniṣpatet ||25||

26pānamakṣāstathā nāryo mṛgayā gītavāditam
|
etāni yuktyā seveta prasaṃgo hyatra doṣavān ||26||

27kuryāttṛṇamayaṃ cāpaṃ śayīta mṛgaśāyikām
|
andhaḥ syādandhavelāyāṃ bādhiryamapi saṃśrayet ||27||

28deśakālau samāsādya vikrameta vicakṣaṇaḥ
|
deśakālavyatīto hi vikramo niṣphalo bhavet ||28||

29kālākālau sampradhārya balābalamathātmanaḥ
|
parasya ca balaṃ jñātvā tatrātmānaṃ niyojayet ||29||

30daṇḍenopanataṃ śatruṃ yo rājā na niyacchati
|
sa mṛtyumupagṛhṇāti garbhamaśvatarī yathā ||30||

31supuṣpitaḥ syādaphalaḥ phalavān syāddurāruhaḥ
|
āmaḥ syātpakvasaṃkāśo na ca śīryeta kasyacit ||31||

32āśāṃ kālavatīṃ kuryāttāṃ ca vighna yojayet
|
vighnaṃ nimittato brūyānnimittaṃ cāpi hetutaḥ ||32||

33bhītavatsaṃvidhātavyaṃ yāvadbhayamanāgatam
|
āgataṃ tu bhayaṃ dṛṣṭvā prahartavyamabhītavat ||33||

34na saṃśayamanāruhya naro bhadrāṇi paśyati
|
saṃśayaṃ punarāruhya yadi jīvati paśyati ||34||

35anāgataṃ vijānīyādyacchedbhayamupasthitam
|
punarvṛddhibhayātkiṃcidanivṛttaṃ niśāmayet ||35||

36pratyupasthitakālasya sukhasya parivarjanam
|
anāgatasukhāśā ca naiva buddhimatāṃ nayaḥ ||36||

37yo'riṇā saha saṃdhāya sukhaṃ svapiti viśvasan
|
sa vṛkṣāgre prasupto vā patitaḥ pratibuddhyate ||37||

38karmaṇā yena tenaiva mṛdunā dāruṇena ca
|
uddhareddīnamātmānaṃ samartho dharmamācaret ||38||

39ye sapatnāḥ sapatnānāṃ sarvāṃstānupasevayet
|
ātmanaścāpi boddhavyācārā vinihatāḥ paraiḥ ||39||

40cārastvaviditaḥ kārya ātmano'tha parasya ca
|
pāṣaṇḍāṃstāpasādīṃśca pararāṣṭre praveśayet ||40||

41udyāneṣu vihāreṣu prapāsvāvasatheṣu ca
|
pānāgāre praveśeṣu tīrtheṣu ca sabhāsu ca ||41||

42dharmābhicāriṇaḥ pāpāścaurā lokasya kaṇṭakāḥ
|
samāgacchanti tānbuddhvā niyacchecchamayīta ca ||42||

43na viśvasedaviśvaste viśvaste nātiviśvaset
|
viśvāsādbhayamabhyeti nāparīkṣya ca viśvased ||43||

44viśvāsayitvā tu paraṃ tattvabhūtena hetunā
|
athāsya praharetkāle kiṃcidvicalite pade ||44||

45aśaṅkyamapi śaṅketa nityaṃ śaṅketa śaṅkitāt
|
bhayaṃ hyaśaṅkitājjātaṃ samūlamapi kṛntati ||45||

46avadhānena maunena kāṣāyeṇa jaṭājinaiḥ
|
viśvāsayitvā dveṣṭāramavalumpedyathā vṛkaḥ ||46||

47putro vā yadi vā bhrātā pitā vā yadi vā suhṛt
|
arthasya vighnaṃ kurvāṇā hantavyā bhūtimicchatā ||47||

48gurorapyavaliptasya kāryākāryamajānataḥ
|
utpathaṃ pratipannasya daṇḍo bhavati śāsanam ||48||

49abhyutthānābhivādābhyāṃ sampradānena kenacit
|
pratipuṣpakalāghātī tīkṣṇatuṇḍa iva dvijaḥ ||49||

50nācchittvā paramarmāṇi nākṛtvā karma dāruṇam
|
nāhatvā matsyadhātīva prāpnoti mahatīṃ śriyam ||50||

51nāsti jātyā ripurnāma mitraṃ vāpi na vidyate
|
sāmarthyayogājjāyante mitrāṇi ripavastathā ||51||

52amitraṃ naiva muñceta vadantaṃ karuṇānyapi
|
duḥkhaṃ tatra na kartavyaṃ hanyātpūrvāpakāriṇam ||52||

53saṃgrahānugrahe yatnaḥ sadā kāryo'nasūyatā
|
nigrahaścāpi yatnena kartavyo bhūtimicchatā ||53||

54prahariṣyan priyaṃ brūyāt prahṛtyaiva priyottaram
|
asināpi śiraśchittvā śoceta ca rudeta ca ||54||

55nimantrayīta sāntvena sammānena titikṣayā
|
lokārādhanamityetatkartavyaṃ bhūtimicchatā ||55||

56na śuṣkavairaṃ kurvīta bāhubhyāṃ na nadīṃ taret
|
anarthakamanāyuṣyaṃ goviṣāṇasya bhakṣaṇam
|
dantāśca parimṛjyante rasaścāpi na labhyate ||56||

57trivargastrividhā pīḍānubandhāstathaiva ca
|
anubandhaṃ tathā jñātvā pīḍāṃ ca parivarjayet ||57||

58ṛṇaśeṣamāgniśeṣaṃ śatruśeṣaṃ tathaiva ca
|
punaḥ punaḥ pravardhante tasmāccheṣaṃ na dhārayet ||58||

59vardhamānamṛṇaṃ tiṣṭhetparibhūtāśca śatravaḥ
|
janayanti bhayaṃ tīvraṃ vyādhayaścāpyupekṣitāḥ ||59||

60nāsamyakkṛtakārī syādapramattaḥ sadā bhavet
|
kaṇṭako'pi hi duśchinno vikāraṃ kurute ciram ||60||

61vadhena ca manuṣyāṇāṃ mārgāṇāṃ dūṣaṇena ca
|
agārāṇāṃ vināśaiśca pararāṣṭraṃ vināśayet ||61||

62gṛdhradṛṣṭirbakālīnaḥ śvaceṣṭaḥ siṃhavikramaḥ
|
anudvignaḥ kākaśaṅkī bhujaṅgacaritaṃ caret ||62||

63śūramañjalipātena bhīruṃ bhedena bhedayet
|
lubdhamarthapradānena samaṃ tulyena vigrahaḥ ||63||

64śreṇīmukhyopajāyeṣu vallabhānunayeṣu ca
|
amātyān parirakṣeta bhedasaṃghātayorapi ||64||

65mṛdurityavajānanti tīkṣṇa ityudvijanti ca
|
tīkṣṇakāle bhavettīkṣṇo mṛdukāle mṛdurbhavet ||65||

66mṛdunaiva mṛduṃ chindi mṛdunā hanti dāruṇam
|
nāsādhyaṃ mṛdunā kiṃcittasmāttīkṣṇataro mṛduḥ ||66||

67kāle mṛduryo bhavati kāle bhavati dāruṇaḥ
|
prasādhayati kṛtyāni śatruṃ cāpyadhitiṣṭhati ||67||

68paṇḍitena viruddhaḥ san dūrastho'smīti nāśvaset
|
dīrghau buddhimato bāhū yābhyāṃ hiṃsati hiṃsitaḥ ||68||

69na tattaredyasya na pāramuttarennataddharedyatpunarāharetparaḥ
|
na tatkhanedyasya na mūlamuddharenna taṃ hanyādyasya śiro na pātayet ||69||

70itīdamuktaṃ vṛjinābhisaṃhitaṃ na caitadevaṃ puruṣaḥ samācaret
|
paraprayukte na kathaṃ vibhāvayedato mayoktaṃ bhavato hitārthinā ||70||

71yathāvaduktaṃ vacanaṃ hitārthinā niśamya vipreṇa suvīrarāṣṭrapaḥ
|
tathā'karodvākyamadīnacetanaḥ śriyaṃ ca dīptāṃ bubhuje sa bāndhavaḥ ||71||
