Mahābhārata 6.46 (Bhīṣmaparvan)

1sañjaya uvāca|rājan śatasahastrāṇi tatra tatra padātinām
|
nirmaryādaṃ prayuddhāni tatte vakṣyāmi bhārata ||1||

2na putraḥ pitaraṃ jajñe pitā vā putramaurasam
|
na bhrātā bhrātaraṃ tatra svasrīyaṃ na ca mātulaḥ ||2||

3na mātulaṃ ca svasrīyo na sakhāyaṃ sakhā tathā
|
āviṣṭā iva yudhyante pāṇḍavāḥ kurubhiḥ saha ||3||

4rathānīkaṃ naravyāghrāḥ kecidabhyapatan rathaiḥ
|
abhajyanta yugaireva yugāni bharatarṣabha ||4||

5ratheṣāśca ratheṣābhiḥ kūbarā rathakūbaraiḥ
|
saṅgataiḥ sahitāḥ kecitparasparajighāṃsavaḥ ||5||

6na śekuścalituṃ kecitsannipatya rathā rathaiḥ
|
prabhinnāstu mahākāyāḥ sannipatya gajā gajaiḥ ||6||

7bahudhā'dārayan kruddhā viṣāṇairitaretaram
|
satoraṇapatākaiśca vāraṇā varavāraṇaiḥ ||7||

8abhisṛtya mahārāja vegavadbhirmahāgajaiḥ
|
dantairabhihatāstatra cukruśuḥ paramāturāḥ ||8||

9abhinītāśca śikṣābhistotrāṃkuśasamāhatāḥ
|
aprabhinnāḥ prabhinnānāṃ saṃmukhābhimukhā yayuḥ ||9||

10prabhinnairapi saṃsaktāḥ kecittatra mahāgajāḥ
|
krauñcavanninadaṃ kṛtvā dudruvuḥ sarvato diśam ||10||

11samyakpraṇītā nāgāśca prabhinnakaraṭāmukhāḥ
|
ṛṣṭitomaranārācairnirviddhā varavāraṇāḥ ||11||

12praṇedurbhinnamarmāṇo nipetuśca gatāsavaḥ
|
prādravanta diśaḥ kecinnadanto bhairavān ravān ||12||

13gajānāṃ pādarakṣāstu vyūḍhoraskāḥ prahāriṇaḥ
|
ṛṣṭibhiśca dhanurbhiśca vimalaiśca paraśvadhaiḥ ||13||

14gadābhirmusalaiścaiva bhindipālaiḥ satomaraiḥ
|
āyasaiḥ parighaiścaiva nistriṃśairvimalaiḥ śitaiḥ ||14||

15pragṛhītaiḥ susaṃrabdhā dravamāṇāstatastataḥ
|
vyadṛśyanta mahārāja parasparajighāṃsavaḥ ||15||

16rājabhānāśca nistriṃśāḥ saṃsiktā naraśoṇitaiḥ
|
pratyadṛśyanta śūrāṇāmanyonyamabhidhāvatām ||16||

17avakṣiptāvadhūtānāmasīnāṃ vīrabāhubhiḥ
|
saṃjajñe tumulaḥ śabdaḥ patatāṃ paramarmasu ||17||

18gadāmusalarugṇānāṃ bhinnānāṃ ca varāsibhiḥ
|
datidantāvabhinnānāṃ mṛditānāṃ ca dantibhiḥ ||18||

19tatra tatra naraughāṇāṃ krośatāmitaretaram
|
śuśruvurdāruṇā vācaḥ pretānāmiva bhārata ||19||

20hayairapi hayārohāścāmarāpīḍadhāribhiḥ
|
haṃsairiva mahāvegairanyonyamabhividrutāḥ ||20||

21tairvimuktā mahāprāsā jāṃbūnadavibhūṣaṇāḥ
|
āśūgā vimalāstīkṣṇāḥ saṃpeturbhujagopamāḥ||21||

22aśvairagryajavaiḥ kecidāplutya mahato rathān
|
śirāṃsyādadire vīrā rathināmaśvasādinaḥ ||22||

23bahūnapi hayārohān bhallaiḥ sannataparvabhiḥ
|
rathī jaghāna saṃprāpya bāṇagocaramāgatān ||23||

24navameghapratīkāśāścākṣipya turagān gajāḥ
|
pādaireva vimṛdnanti mattāḥ kanakabhūṣaṇāḥ ||24||

25pāṭyamāneṣu kuṃbheṣu pārśveṣvapi ca vāraṇāḥ
|
prāsairvinihatāḥ kecidvineduḥ paramāturāḥ ||25||

26sāśvārohān hayān kāṃścidunmathya varavāraṇāḥ
|
sahasā cikṣipustatra saṃkule bhairave sati ||26||

27sāśvārohān viṣāṇāgrairutkṣipya turagān gajāḥ
|
rathaughānabhimṛdnantaḥ sadhvajānabhicakramuḥ ||27||

28puṃstvādatimadatvācca kecittatra mahāgajāḥ
|
sāśvārohān hayān jaghnuḥ karaiḥ sacaraṇaistathā ||28||

29aśvārohaiśca samare hastisādibhireva ca
|
pratimāneṣu gātreṣu pārśveṣvabhi ca vāraṇān
|
āśugā vimalāstīkṣṇāḥ saṃpeturbhujagopamāḥ ||29||

30narāśvakāyānnirbhidya lauhāni kavacāni ca
|
nipeturvimalāḥ śaktyo vīrabāhubhirarpitāḥ ||30||

31maholkāpratimā ghorāstatra tatra viśāṃpate
|
dvīpicarmāvanaddhaiśca vyāghracarmacchadairapi ||31||

32vikośairvimalaiḥ khaḍgairabhijagmuḥ parān raṇe
|
abhiplutamabhikruddhamekapārśvāvadāritam ||32||

33vidarśayantaḥ saṃpetuḥ khaḍgacarmaparaśvadhaiḥ
|
kecidākṣipya kariṇaḥ sāśvānapi rathān karaiḥ ||33||

34vikarṣanto diśaḥ sarvāḥ saṃpetuḥ sarvaśabdagāḥ
|
śaṃkubhirdāritāḥ kecit saṃbhinnāśca paraśvadhaiḥ ||34||

35hastibhirmuditāḥ kecit kṣuṇṇāścānye turaṃgamaiḥ
|
rathaneminikṛttāśca nikṛttāśca paraśvadhaiḥ ||35||

36vyākrośanta narā rājaṃstatra tatra sma bandhāvān
|
putrānanye pitṝnanye bhrātṝṃśca saha bandhubhiḥ ||36||

37mātulān bhāgineyāṃśca parānapi ca saṃyuge
|
vikīrṇāṃtrāḥ subahavo bhagnasakthāśca bhārata ||37||

38bāhubhiścāpare chinnaiḥ pārśveṣu cca vidāritāḥ
|
krandantaḥ samadṛśyanta tṛṣitā jīvitepsavaḥ ||38||

39tṛṣā parigatāḥ kecidalpasattvā viśāṃpate
|
bhūmau nipatitāḥ saṃkhye mṛgayāṃcakrire jalam ||39||

40rudhiraughapariklinnāḥ kliśyamānāśca bhārata
|
vyanindanbhṛśamātmānaṃ tava putrāṃśca saṃgatān ||40||

41apare kṣatriyāḥ śūrāḥ kṛtavairāḥ parasparam
|
naiva śastraṃ vimuñcanti naiva krandanti māriṣa ||41||

42tarjayanti ca saṃhṛṣṭāstatra tatra parasparam
|
ādaśya daśanaiścāpi krodhātsaradanacchadam ||42||

43bhrukuṭīkuṭilairvaktraiḥ prekṣanti ca parasparam
|
apare kliśyamānāstu śarārtā vraṇapīḍitāḥ ||43||

44niṣkūjāḥ samapadyanta dṛḍhasatvā mahābalāḥ
|
anye ca virathāḥ śūrā rathamanyasya saṃyuge ||44||

45prārthayānā nipatitāḥ saṃkṣuṇṇā varavāraṇaiḥ
|
aśobhanta mahārāja sapuṣpā iva kiṃśukāḥ ||45||

46saṃbabhūvuranekeṣu bahavo bhairavasvanāḥ
|
vartamāne mahābhīme tasmin vīravarakṣaye ||46||

47nijaghāna pitā putraṃ putraśca pitaraṃ raṇe
|
svasrīyo mātulaṃ cāpi svasrīyaṃ cāpi mātulaḥ ||47||

48sakhā sakhāyaṃ ca tathā sambandhī bāndhavaṃ tathā
|
evaṃ yuyudhire tatra kuravaḥ pāṇḍavaiḥ saha ||48||

49vartamāne tathā tasminnirmaryāde bhayānake
|
bhīṣmamāsādya pārthānāṃ vāhinī samakampata ||49||

50ketunā pañcatāreṇa tālena bharatarṣabha
|
rājatena mahābāhurucchritena mahārathe
|
babhau bhīṣmastadā rājaṃścandramā iva meruṇā ||50||
