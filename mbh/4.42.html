Mahābhārata 4.42 (Virāṭaparvan)

1uttara uvāca|bindavo jātarūpasya śataṃ yasmin nipātitāḥ
|
sahasrakoṭisauvarṇāḥ kasyaitaddhanuruttamam ||1||

2vāraṇā yatra sauvarṇāḥ pṛṣṭhe bhāsanti daṃśitāḥ
|
supārśvaṃ sugrahaṃ caiva kasyaitaddhanuruttamam ||2||

3tapanīyasya śuddhasya pṛṣṭhe yasyendragopakāḥ
|
pṛṣṭhe vibhaktāḥ śobhante kasyaitaddhanuruttamam ||3||

4sūryā yatra ca sauvarṇāstrayo bhāsanti daṃśitāḥ
|
tejasā prajvalanto hi kasyaitaddhanuruttamam ||4||

5śalabhā yatra sauvarṇāstapanīyavibhūṣitāḥ
|
suvarṇamaṇicitraṃ ca kasyaitaddhanuruttamam ||5||

6ime ca kasya nārācāḥ sahastrā lomavāhinaḥ
|
samantāt kaladhautāgrā upāsaṅge hiraṇmaye ||6||

7vipāṭhāḥ pṛthavaḥ kasya gārdhrapatrāḥ śilāśitāḥ
|
hāridravarṇāḥ sumukhāḥ pītāḥ sarvāyasāḥ śarāḥ ||7||

8kasyāyamasitaścāpaḥ pañcaśārdūlalakṣaṇaḥ
|
varāhakarṇavyāmiśrān śarān dhārayate daśa ||8||

9kasyeme pṛthavo dīrghāścandrabimbārdhadarśanāḥ
|
śatāni sapta tiṣṭhanti nārācā rudhirāśanāḥ ||9||

10kasyeme śukapatrābhaiḥ pūrvairardhaiḥ suvāsasaḥ
|
uttarairāyasaiḥ pītairhemapuṅkhaiḥ śilāśitaiḥ ||10||

11gurubhārasaho divyaḥ śātravāṇāṃ bhayaṃkaraḥ
|
kasyāyaṃ sāyako dīrghaḥ śilīpṛṣṭhaḥ śilīmukhaḥ ||11||

12vaiyāghrakośe nihito hemacitratsarurmahān
|
suphalaścitrakośaśca kiṅkiṇīsāyako mahān ||12||

13kasya hematsarurdivyaḥ khaḍgaḥ paramanirmalaḥ
|
kasyāyaṃ vimalaḥ khaḍgo gavye kośe samarpitaḥ ||13||

14hematsaruranādhṛṣyo naiṣadhyo bhārasādhanaḥ
|
kasya pāñcanakhe kośe sāyako hemavigrahaḥ ||14||

15pramāṇarūpasaṃpannaḥ pīta ākāśasannibhaḥ
|
kasya hemamaye kośe sutapte pāvakaprabhe ||15||

16nistriṃśo'yaṃ guruḥ pītaḥ sāyakaḥ paranirvraṇaḥ
|
kasyāyamasitaḥ khaḍgo hemabindubhirāvṛtaḥ ||16||

17āśīviṣasamasparśaḥ parakāyaprabhedanaḥ
|
gurubhārasaho divyaḥ sapatnānāṃ bhayapradaḥ ||17||

18nirdiśasva yathātattvaṃ mayā pṛṣṭā bṛhannale
|
vismayo me paro jāto dṛṣṭvā sarvamidaṃ mahat ||18||
