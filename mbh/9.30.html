Mahābhārata 9.30 (Śalyaparvan)

1dhṛtarāṣṭra uvāca|
hateṣu sarvasainyeṣu pāṇḍuputrai raṇājire
|
mama sainyāvaśiṣṭāste kimakurvata sañjaya||1||

2kṛtavarmā kṛpaścaiva droṇaputraśca vīryavān
|
duryodhanaśca mandātmā rājā kimakarottadā||2||

3sañjaya uvāca|
samprādravatsu dāreṣu kṣatriyāṇāṃ mahātmanām
|
vidrute śibire śūnye bhṛśodvignāstrayo rathāḥ||3||

4niśamya pāṇḍuputrāṇāṃ tadā vai jayināṃ svanam
|
vidrutaṃ śibiraṃ dṛṣṭvā sāyāhne rājagṛddhinaḥ||4||

5sthānaṃ nārocayaṃstatra tataste hradamabhyayuḥ
|
yudhiṣṭhiro'pi dharmātmā bhrātṛbhiḥ sahito raṇe||5||

6hṛṣṭaḥ paryacaradrājan duryodhanavadhepsayā
|
mārgamāṇāstu saṅkruddhāstava putraṃ jayaiṣiṇaḥ||6||

7yatnato'nveṣamāṇāste naivāpaśyañjanādhipam
|
sa hi tīvraṇa vegena gadāpāṇirapākramat||7||

8taṃ hradaṃ prāviśaccāpi viṣṭabhyāpaḥ svamāyayā
|
yadā tu pāṇḍavāḥ sarve supariśrāntavāhanāḥ||8||

9tataḥ svaśibiraṃ prāpya vyatiṣṭhanta svasainikāḥ
|
tataḥ kṛpaśca drauṇiśca kṛtavarmā ca sātvataḥ||9||

10sanniviṣṭeṣu pārtheṣu prayātāstaṃ hradaṃ śanaiḥ
|
te taṃ hradaṃ samāsādya yatra śete janādhipaḥ||10||

11abhyabhāṣanta durdharṣaṃ rājānaṃ suptamambhasi
|
rājannuttiṣṭha yuddhyasva sahāsmābhiryudhiṣṭhiram||11||

12jitvā vā pṛthivīṃ bhuḍkṣva hato vā svargamāpnuhi
|
teṣāmapi balaṃ sarvaṃ hataṃ duryodhana tvayā||12||

13pratividdhāśca bhūyiṣṭhaṃ ye śiṣṭāstatra sainikāḥ
|
na te vegaṃ viṣahituṃ śaktāstava viśāmpate||13||

14asmābhirapi guptasya tasmāduttiṣṭha bhārata
|
duryodhana uvāca|
diṣṭyā paśyāmi vo muktānīdṛśātpuruṣakṣayāt||14||

15pāṇḍukauravasammardājjīvamānānnararṣabhān
|
vijeṣyāmo vayaṃ sarve viśrāntā vigatakumāḥ||15||

16bhavantaśca pariśrāntāṃ vayaṃ ca bhṛśavikṣatāḥ
|
udīrṇaṃ ca balaṃ teṣāṃ tena yuddhaṃ na rocaye||16||

17na tvedatadbhutaṃ vīrā yadvo mahadidaṃ manaḥ
|
asmāsu ca parā bhaktirna tu kālaḥ parākrame||17||

18viśramyaikāṃ niśāmadya bhavadbhiḥ sahito raṇe
|
pratiyotsyāmyahaṃ śatrūñśvo na me'styatra saṃśayaḥ||18||

19sañjaya uvāca|
evamukto'bravīddrauṇī rājānaṃ yuddhadurmadam
|
uttiṣṭha rājanbhadraṃ te vijeṣyāmo vayaṃ parān||19||

20iṣṭāpūrtena dānena satyena ca jayena ca
|
śape rājan yathā hyadya nihaniṣyāmi somakān||20||

21mā sma yajñakṛtāṃ prītimāpnuyāṃ sajjanocitām
|
yadīmāṃ rajanīṃ vyuṣṭāṃ na hi hanmi parān raṇe||21||

22nāhatvā sarvapāñcālān vimokṣye kavacaṃ vibho
|
iti satyaṃ bravīmyetattanme śṛṇu janādhipa||22||

23teṣu sambhāṣamāṇeṣu vyādhāstaṃ deśamāyayuḥ
|
māṃsabhārapariśrāntāḥ pānīyārthaṃ yadṛcchayā||23||

24te hi nityaṃ mahārāja bhīmasenasya lubdhakāḥ
|
māṃsabhārānupājahrurbhaktyā paramayā vibho||24||

25te tatra dhiṣṭhitāsteṣāṃ sarvaṃ tadvacanaṃ rahaḥ
|
duryodhanavacaścaiva śuśruvuḥ saṅgatā mithaḥ||25||

26te'pi sarve maheṣvāsā ayuddhārthini kaurave
|
nirbandhaṃ paramaṃ cakrustadā vai yuddhakāṅkṣiṇaḥ||26||

27tāṃstathā samudīkṣyātha kauravāṇāṃ mahārathān
|
ayuddhamanasaṃ caiva rājānaṃ sthitamambhasi||27||

28teṣāṃ śrutvā ca saṃvādaṃ rājñaśca salile sataḥ
|
vyādhābhyajānan rājendra salilasthaṃ suyodhanam||28||

29te pūrvaṃ pāṇḍuputreṇa pṛṣṭā hyāsan sutaṃ tava
|
yadṛcchopagatāstatra rājānaṃ parimārgatā||29||

30tataste pāṇḍuputrasya smṛtvā tadbhāṣitaṃ tadā
|
anyonyamabruvan rājan mṛgavyādhāḥ śanairiva||30||

31duryodhanaṃ khyāpayāmo dhanaṃ dāsyati pāṇḍavaḥ
|
avyaktamiha naḥ khyāto hrade duryodhano nṛpaḥ||31||

32tasmādgacchāmahe sarve yatra rājā yudhiṣṭhiraḥ
|
ākhyātuṃ salile suptaṃ duryodhanamamarṣaṇam||32||

33dhṛtarāṣṭrātmajaṃ tasmai bhīmasenāya dhīmate
|
śayānaṃ salile sarve kathayāmo dhanurbhṛte||33||

34sa no dāsyati suprīto dhanāni bahulānyuta
|
kiṃ no māṃsena śuṣkeṇa parikliṣṭena śoṣiṇā||34||

35evamuktvā tu te vyādhāḥ samprahṛṣṭā dhanārthinaḥ
|
māṃsabhārānupādāya prayayuḥ śibiraṃ prati||35||

36pāṇḍavāpi mahārāja labdhalakṣyāḥ prahāriṇaḥ
|
apaśyamānāḥ samare duryodhanamavasthitam||36||

37nikṛtestasya pāpasya te pāraṃ gamanepsavaḥ
|
cārānsampreṣayāmāsuḥ samantāttadraṇājire||37||

38āgamya tu tataḥ sarve naṣṭaṃ duryodhanaṃ nṛpam
|
nyavedayanta sahitā dharmarājasya sainikāḥ||38||

39teṣāṃ tadvacanaṃ śrutvā cārāṇāṃ bharatarṣabha
|
cintāmabhyagamattīvrāṃ niḥśaśvāsa ca pārthivaḥ||39||

40ata sthitānāṃ pāṇḍūnāṃ dīnānāṃ bharatarṣabha
|
tasmāddeśādapakramya tvaritā lubdhakā vibho||40||

41ājagmuḥ śibiraṃ hṛṣṭā dṛṣṭvā duryodhanaṃ nṛpam
|
vāryamāṇāḥ praviṣṭāśca bhīmasenasya paśyataḥ||41||

42te tu pāṇḍavamāsādya bhīmasenaṃ mahābalam
|
tasmai tatsarvamācakhyuryadvṛttaṃ yacca vai śrutam||42||

43tato vṛkodaro rājan dattvā teṣāṃ dhanaṃ bahu
|
dharmarājāya tatsarvamācacakṣe parantapaḥ||43||

44asau duryodhano rājan vijñāto mama lubdhakaiḥ
|
saṃstabhya salilaṃ śete yasyārthe paritapyase||44||

45tadvaco bhīmasenasya priyaṃ śrutvā viśāmpate
|
ajñātaśatruḥ kaunteyo hṛṣṭo'bhūtsaha sodaraiḥ||45||

46taṃ ca śrutvā maheṣvāsaṃ praviṣṭaṃ salilahrade
|
kṣiprameva tato'gacchanpuraskṛtya janārdanam||46||

47tataḥ kilakilāśabdaḥ prādurāsīdviśāmpate
|
pāṇḍavānāṃ prahṛṣṭānāṃ pañcālānāṃ ca sarvaśaḥ||47||

48siṃhanādāṃstataścakruḥ kṣveḍāśca bharatarṣabha
|
tvaritāḥ kṣatriyā rājañjagmudvaipāyanaṃ hradam||48||

49jñātaḥ pāpo dhārtarāṣṭro dṛṣṭaścetyasakṛdraṇe
|
prākrośan somakāstatra hṛṣṭarūpāḥ samantataḥ||49||

50teṣāmāśu prayātānāṃ sthānāṃ tatra veginām
|
babhūva tumulaḥ śabdo divaspṛk pṛthivīpate||50||

51duryodhanaṃ parīpsantastatra tatra yudhiṣṭhiram
|
anvayustvaritāste vai rājānaṃ śrāntavāhanāḥ||51||

52arjuno bhīmasenaśca mādrīputrau ca pāṇḍavau
|
dhṛṣṭadyumnaśca pāñcālyaḥ śikhaṇḍī cāparājitaḥ||52||

53uttamaujā yudhāmanyuḥ sātyakiśca mahārathaḥ
|
pañcālānāṃ ca ye śiṣṭā draupadeyāśca bhārata||53||

54hayāśca sarve nāgāśca śataśaśca padātayaḥ
|
tataḥ prāpto mahārāja dharmarājaḥ pratāpavān||54||

55dvaipāyanaṃ hradaṃ ghoraṃ yatra duryodhano'bhavat
|
śītāmalajalaṃ hṛdyaṃ dvitīyamiva sāgaram||55||

56māyayā salilaṃ stabhya yatrābhūtte sthitaḥ sutaḥ
|
atyadbhutena vidhinā daivayogena bhārata||56||

57salilāntargataḥ śete durdarśaḥ kasyacitprabho
|
mānuṣyasya manuṣyendra gadāhasto janādhipaḥ||57||

58tato duryodhano rājā salilāntargato vasan
|
śuśruve tumulaṃ śabdaṃ jaladopamaniḥsvanam||58||

59yudhiṣṭhiraśca rājendraṃ taṃ hradaṃ saha sodaraiḥ
|
ājagāma mahārāja tava putravadhāya vai||59||

60mahatā śaṅkhanādena rathanemisvanena ca
|
ūrdhvaṃ dhunvanmahāreṇuṃ kampayaṃścāpi medinīm||60||

61yaudhiṣṭhirasya sainyasya śrutvā śabdaṃ mahārathāḥ
|
kṛtavarmā kṛpo drauṇī rājānamidamabruvan||61||

62ime hyāyānti saṃhṛṣṭāḥ pāṇḍavājitakāśinaḥ
|
apayāsyāmahe tāvadanujānātu no bhavān||62||

63duryodhanastu tacchrutvā teṣāṃ tatra tarasvinām
|
tathetyuktvā hradaṃ taṃ vai māyayāstambhayatprabho||63||

64te tvanujñāpya rājānaṃ bhṛśaṃ śokaparāyaṇāḥ
|
jagmurdūre mahārāja kṛpaprabhṛtayo rathāḥ||64||

65te gatvā dūramadhvānaṃ nyagrodhaṃ prekṣya māriṣa
|
nyaviśanta bhṛśaṃ śrāntāścintayanto nṛpaṃ prati||65||

66viṣṭabhya salilaṃ sūto dhārtarāṣṭro mahābalaḥ
|
pāṇḍavāścāpi samprāptāstaṃ deśaṃ yuddhamīpsavaḥ||66||

67kathaṃ nu yuddhaṃ bhavitā kathaṃ rājā bhaviṣyati
|
kathaṃ nu pāṇḍavā rājan pratipatsyanti kauravam||67||

68ityevaṃ cintayānāstu rathebhyo'śvānvimucya te
|
tatrāsāñcakrire rājan kṛpaprabhṛtayo rathāḥ||68||
