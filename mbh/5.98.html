Mahābhārata 5.98 (Udyogaparvan)

1kaṇva uvāca|
mātalistu vrajanmārge nāradena maharṣiṇā
|
varuṇaṃ gacchatā draṣṭuṃ samāgacchadyadṛcchayā ||1||

2nārado'thābravīdenaṃ kva bhavān gantumudyataḥ
|
svena vā sūta kāryeṇa śāsanādvā śatakratoḥ ||2||

3mātalirnāradenaivaṃ saṃpṛṣṭaḥ pathi gacchatā
|
yathāvatsarvamācaṣṭa svakāryaṃ nāradaṃ prati ||3||

4tamuvācātha sa munirgacchāvaḥ sahitāviti
|
salileśadidṛkṣārthamahamapyudyato divaḥ ||4||

5ahaṃ te sarvamākhyāsye darśayan vasudhātalam
|
dṛṣṭvā tatra varaṃ kañcidrocayiṣyāva mātale ||5||

6avagāhya tu tau bhūmimubhau mātalināradau
|
dadṛśāte mahātmānau lokapālamapāṃ patim ||6||

7tatra devarṣisadṛśīṃ pūjāṃ sa prāpa nāradaḥ
|
mahendrasadṛśīṃ caiva mātaliḥ pratyapadyata ||7||

8tāvubhau pratimanasau kāryavantau nivedya ha
|
varuṇenābhyanujñātau nāgalokaṃ viceratuḥ ||8||

9nāradaḥ sarvabhūtānāmantarbhūminivāsinām
|
jānaṃścakāra vyākhyānaṃ yantuḥ sarvamaśeṣataḥ ||9||

10nārada uvāca|
dṛṣṭaste varuṇaḥ sūta putrapautrasamāvṛtaḥ
|
paśyodakapateḥ sthānaṃ sarvatobhadramṛddhimat ||10||

11eṣa putro mahāprajño varuṇasyeha gopateḥ
|
eṣa vai śīlavṛttena śaucena ca viśiṣyate ||11||

12eṣo'sya putro'bhimataḥ puṣkaraḥ puṣkarekṣaṇaḥ
|
rūpavān darśanīyaśca somaputryā vṛtaḥ patiḥ ||12||

13jyotsnā kālīti yāmāhurdvitīyāṃ rūpataḥ śriyam
|
adityāścaiva yaḥ putro jyeṣṭhaḥ śreṣṭhaḥ kṛtaḥ smṛtaḥ||13||

14bhavanaṃ paśya vāruṇyaṃ yadetatsarvakāñcanam
|
yatprāpya suratāṃ prāptāḥ surāḥ surapateḥ sakhe ||14||

15etāni hṛtarājyānāṃ daiteyānāṃ sma mātale
|
dīpyamānāni dṛśyante sarvapraharaṇānyuta ||15||

16akṣayāṇi kilaitāni vivartante sma mātale
|
anubhāvaprayuktāni surairavajitāni ha ||16||

17atra rākṣasajātyaśca daityajātyaśca mātale
|
divyapraharaṇāścāsan pūrvadaivatanirmitāḥ ||17||

18agnireṣa mahārciṣmān jāgarti vāruṇe hrade
|
vaiṣṇavaṃ cakramāviddhaṃ vidhūmena haviṣmatā ||18||

19eṣa gāṇḍīmayaścāpo lokasaṃhārasaṃbhṛtaḥ
|
rakṣyate daivatairnityaṃ yatastadgāṇḍivaṃ dhanuḥ ||19||

20eṣa kṛtye samutpanne tattaddhārayate balam
|
sahastraśatasaṃkhyena prāṇena satataṃ dhruvaḥ ||20||

21aśāsyānapi śāstyeṣa rakṣobaṃdhuṣu rājasu
|
sṛṣṭaḥ prathamataścaṇḍo brahmaṇā brahmavādinā ||21||

22etacchastraṃ narendrāṇāṃ mahaccakreṇa bhāsitam
|
putrāḥ salilarājasya dhārayanti mahodayam ||22||

23etat salilarājasya chatraṃ chatragṛhe sthitam
|
sarvataḥ salilaṃ śītaṃ jīmūta iva varṣati ||23||

24etacchatrāt paribhraṣṭaṃ salilaṃ somanirmalam
|
tamasā mūrchitaṃ bhāti yena nārchati darśanam ||24||

25bahūnyadbhutarūpāṇi draṣṭavyānīha mātale
|
tava kāryāvarodhastu tasmādgacchāva mā ciram ||25||
