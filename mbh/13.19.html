Mahābhārata 13.19 (Anuśāsanaparvan)

1yudhiṣṭhira uvāca|
yadidaṃ sahadharmeti procyate bharatarṣabha
|
pāṇigrahaṇakāle tu strīṇāmetatkathaṃ smṛtam ||1||

2ārṣa eṣa bhaveddharmaḥ prājāpatyo'thavā''suraḥ
|
yadetatsahadharmeti pūrvamuktaṃ maharṣibhiḥ ||2||

3sandehaḥ sumahāneṣa viruddha iti me matiḥ
|
iha yaḥ sahadharmo vai pretyāyaṃ vihitaḥ kva nu ||3||

4svargo mṛtānāṃ bhavati sahadharmaḥ pitāmaha
|
pūrvamekastu mriyate kva caikastiṣṭhate vada ||4||

5nānādharmaphalopetā nānākarmānavāsitāḥ
|
nānānirayaniṣṭhāntā mānuṣā bahavo yadā ||5||

6anṛtāḥ striya ityevaṃ sūtrakāro vyavasyati
|
yadānṛtāḥ striyastāta sahadharmaḥ kutaḥ smṛtaḥ ||6||

7anṛtāḥ striya ityevaṃ vedeṣvapi hi paṭhyate
|
dharmo'yaṃ pūrvikā saṃjñā upacāraḥ kriyāvidhiḥ ||7||

8gahvaraṃ pratibhātyetanmama cintayato'niśam
|
niḥsandehamidaṃ sarvaṃ pitāmaha yathāśruti ||8||

9yadaitadyādṛśaṃ caitadyathā caitatpravartitam
|
nikhilena mahāprājña bhavānetadbravītu me ||9||

10bhīṣma uvāca|
atrāpyudāharantīmamitihāsaṃ purātanam
|
aṣṭāvakrasya saṃvādaṃ diśayā saha bhārata ||10||

11nirveṣṭukāmastu purā aṣṭāvakro mahātapāḥ
|
ṛṣeratha vadānyasya vavre kanyāṃ mahātmanaḥ ||11||

12suprabhāṃ nāma vai nāmnā rūpeṇāpratimāṃ bhuvi
|
guṇaprabhāvaśīlena cāritreṇa ca śobhanām ||12||

13sā tasya dṛṣṭvaiva mano jahāra śubhalocanā
|
vanarājī yathā citrā vasante kusumācitā ||13||

14ṛṣistamāha deyā me sutā tubhyaṃ hi tacchṛṇu
|
gaccha tāvaddiśaṃ puṇyāmuttarāṃ drakṣyase tataḥ ||14||

15aṣṭāvakra uvāca|
kiṃ draṣṭavyaṃ mayā tatra vaktumarhati me bhavān
|
tathedānīṃ mayā kāryaṃ yathā vakṣyati māṃ bhavān ||15||

16vadānya uvāca|
dhanadaṃ samatikramya himavantaṃ ca parvatam
|
rudrasyāyatanaṃ dṛṣṭvā siddhacāraṇasevitam ||16||

17saṃhṛṣṭaiḥ pārṣadairjuṣṭaṃ nṛtyadbhirvividhānanaiḥ
|
divyāṅgarāgaiḥ paiśācairanyairnānāvidhaiḥ prabhoḥ ||17||

18pāṇitālasutālaiśca śaṃpātālaiḥ samaistathā
|
saṃprahṛṣṭaiḥ pranṛtyadbhiḥ śarvastatra niṣevyate ||18||

19iṣṭaṃ kila girau sthānaṃ taddivyamiti śuśruma
|
nityaṃ sannihito devastathā te pārṣadāḥ smṛtāḥ ||19||

20tatra devyā tapastaptaṃ śaṅkarārthaṃ suduścaram
|
atastadiṣṭaṃ devasya tathomāyā iti śrutiḥ ||20||

21pūrve tatra mahāpārśve devasyottaratastathā
|
ṛtavaḥ kālarātriśca ye divyā ye ca mānuṣāḥ ||21||

22devaṃ copāsate sarve rūpiṇaḥ kila tatra ha
|
tadatikramya bhavanaṃ tvayā yātavyameva hi ||22||

23tato nīlaṃ vanoddeśaṃ drakṣyase meghasannibham
|
ramaṇīyaṃ manogrāhi tatra vai drakṣyase striyam ||23||

24tapasvinīṃ mahābhāgāṃ vṛddhāṃ dīkṣāmanuṣṭhitām
|
draṣṭavyā sā tvayā tatra saṃpūjyā caiva yatnataḥ ||24||

25tāṃ dṛṣṭvā vinivṛttastvaṃ tataḥ pāṇiṃ grahīṣyasi
|
yadyeṣa samayaḥ sarvaḥ sādhyatāṃ tatra gamyatām ||25||

26aṣṭāvakra uvāca|
tathāstu sādhayiṣyāmi tatra yāsyāmyasaṃśayam
|
yatra tvaṃ vadase sādho bhavān bhavatu satyavāk ||26||

27bhīṣma uvāca|
tato'gacchat sa bhagavānuttarāmuttarāṃ diśam
|
himavantaṃ giriśreṣṭhaṃ siddhacāraṇasevitam ||27||

28sa gatvā dvijaśārdūlo himavantaṃ mahāgirim
|
abhyagacchannadīṃ puṇyāṃ bāhudāṃ dharmaśālinīm ||28||

29aśoke vimale tīrthe snātvā vai tarpya devatāḥ
|
tatra vāsāya śayane kauśe sukhamuvāsa ha ||29||

30tato rātryāṃ vyatītāyāṃ prātarutthāya sa dvijaḥ
|
snātvā prāduścakārāgniṃ stutvā cainaṃ pradhānataḥ ||30||

31rudrāṇīṃ rudramāsādya hrade tatra samāśvasat
|
viśrāntaśca samutthāya kailāsamabhito yayau ||31||

32so'paśyatkāñcanadvāraṃ dīpyamānamiva śriyā
|
mandākinīṃ ca nalinīṃ dhanadasya mahātmanaḥ ||32||

33atha te rākṣasāḥ sarve ye'bhirakṣanti padminīm
|
pratyutthitā bhagavantaṃ maṇibhadrapurogamāḥ ||33||

34sa tānpratyarcayāmāsa rākṣasānbhīmavikramān
|
nivedyata māṃ kṣipraṃ dhanadāyeti cābravīt ||34||

35te rākṣasāstathā rājan bhagavantamathābruvan
|
asau vaiśravaṇo rājā svayamāyāti te'ntikam ||35||

36vidito bhagavānasya kāryamāgamanasya yat
|
paśyainaṃ tvaṃ mahābhāgaṃ jvalantamiva tejasā ||36||

37tato vaiśravaṇo'bhyetya aṣṭāvakramaninditam
|
vidhivatkuśalaṃ pṛṣṭvā tato brahmarṣimabravīt ||37||

38sukhaṃ prāpto bhavānkaccitkiṃ vā mattaścikīrṣati
|
brūhi sarvaṃ kariṣyāmi yanmāṃ vakṣyasi vai dvija ||38||

39bhavanaṃ praviśa tvaṃ me yathākāmaṃ dvijottama
|
satkṛtaḥ kṛtakāryaśca bhavān yāsyatyavighnataḥ ||39||

40prāviśadbhavanaṃ svaṃ vai gṛhītvā taṃ dvijottamam
|
āsanaṃ svaṃ dadau caiva pādyamarghyaṃ tathaiva ca ||40||

41athopaviṣṭayostatra maṇibhadrapurogamāḥ
|
niṣedustatra kauberā yakṣagandharvakinnarāḥ ||41||

42tatasteṣāṃ niṣaṇṇānāṃ dhanado vākyamabravīt
|
bhavacchandaṃ samājñāya nṛtyerannapsarogaṇāḥ ||42||

43ātithyaṃ paramaṃ kāryaṃ śuśrūṣā bhavatastathā
|
saṃvartatāmityuvāca munirmadhurayā girā ||43||

44athorvarā miśrakeśī rambhā caivorvaśī tathā
|
alambuṣā ghṛtācī ca citrā citrāṅgadā ruci||44||

45manoharā sukeśī ca sumukhī hāsinī prabhā
|
vidyutā praśamī dāntā vidyotā ratireva ca ||45||

46etāścānyāśca vai bahvyaḥ pranṛttāpsarasaḥ śubhāḥ
|
avādayaṃśca gandharvā vādyāni vividhāni ca ||46||

47atha pravṛtte gāndharve divye ṛṣirupāviśat
|
divyaṃ saṃvatsaraṃ tatrāramataiṣa mahātapāḥ ||47||

48tato vaiśravaṇo rājā bhagavantamuvāca ha
|
sāgraḥ saṃvatsaro jāto vipreha tava paśyataḥ ||48||

49hāryo'yaṃ viṣayo brahman gāndharvo nāma nāmataḥ
|
chandato vartatāṃ vipra yathā vadati vā bhavān ||49||

50atithiḥ pūjanīyastvamidaṃ ca bhavato gṛham
|
sarvamājñāpyatāmāśu paravanto vayaṃ tvayi ||50||

51atha vaiśravaṇaṃ prīto bhagavānpratyabhāṣata
|
arcito'smi yathānyāyaṃ gamiṣyāmi dhaneśvara ||51||

52prīto'smi sadṛśaṃ caiva tava sarvaṃ dhanādhipa
|
tava prasādādbhagavan maharṣeśca mahātmanaḥ ||52||

53niyogādadya yāsyāmi vṛddhimānṛddhimān bhava
|
atha niṣkramya bhagavān prayayāvuttarāmukhaḥ ||53||

54kailāsaṃ mandaraṃ haimaṃ sarvānanucacāra ha
|
tānatītya mahāśailān kairātaṃ sthānamuttamam ||54||

55pradakṣiṇaṃ tathā cakre prayataḥ śirasā nataḥ
|
dharaṇīmavatīryātha pūtātmā'sau tadā'bhavat ||55||

56sa taṃ pradakṣiṇaṃ kṛtvā triḥ śailaṃ cottarāmukhaḥ
|
samena bhūmibhāgena yayau prītipuraskṛtaḥ ||56||

57tato'paraṃ vanoddeśaṃ ramaṇīyamapaśyata
|
sarvartubhirmūlaphalaiḥ pakṣibhiśca samanvitaiḥ ||57||

58ramaṇīyairvanoddeśaistatra tatra vibhūṣitam
|
tatrāśramapadaṃ divyaṃ dadarśa bhagavānatha ||58||

59śailāṃśca vividhākārān kāñcanān ratnabhūṣitān
|
maṇibhūmau niviṣṭāśca puṣkariṇyastathaiva ca ||59||

60anyānyapi suramyāṇi paśyataḥ subahūnyatha
|
bhṛśaṃ tasya mano reme maharṣerbhāvitātmanaḥ ||60||

61sa tatra kāñcanaṃ divyaṃ sarvaratnamayaṃ gṛham
|
dadarśādbhutasaṅkāśaṃ dhanadasya gṛhādvaram ||61||

62mahānto yatra vividhā maṇikāñcanaparvatāḥ
|
vimānāni ca ramyāṇi ratnāni vividhāni ca ||62||

63mandārapuṣpaiḥ saṅkīrṇāṃ tathā mandākinīṃ nadīm
|
svayaṃprabhāśca maṇayo vajrairbhūmiśca bhūṣitā ||63||

64nānāvidhaiśca bhavanairvicitramaṇitoraṇaiḥ
|
muktājālavinikṣiptairmaṇiratnavibhūṣitaiḥ ||64||

65manodṛṣṭiharai ramyaiḥ sarvataḥ saṃvṛtaṃ śubhaiḥ
|
ṛṣibhiścāvṛtaṃ tatra āśramaṃ taṃ manoharam ||65||

66tatastasyābhavaccintā kutra vāso bhavediti
|
atha dvāraṃ samabhito gatvā sthitvā tato'bravīt ||66||

67atithiṃ samanuprāptamabhijānantu ye'tra vai
|
atha kanyāḥ parivṛtā gṛhāttasmādvinirgatāḥ ||67||

68nānārūpāḥ sapta vibho kanyāḥ sarvā manoharāḥ
|
yāṃ yāmapaśyatkanyāṃ vai sā sā tasya mano'harat ||68||

69na ca śakto vārayituṃ mano'syāthāvasīdati
|
tato dhṛtiḥ samutpannā tasya viprasya dhīmataḥ ||69||

70atha taṃ pramadāḥ prāhurbhagavānpraviśatviti
|
sa ca tāsāṃ surūpāṇāṃ tasyaiva bhavanasya hi ||70||

71kautūhalaṃ samāviṣṭaḥ praviveśa gṛhaṃ dvijaḥ
|
tatrāpaśyajjarāyuktāmarajombaradhāriṇīm ||71||

72vṛddhāṃ paryaṅkamāsīnāṃ sarvābharaṇabhūṣitām
|
svastīti tena caivoktā sā strī pratyavadattadā ||72||

73pratyutthāya ca taṃ vipramāsyatāmityuvāca ha
|
aṣṭāvakra uvāca|
sarvāḥ khānālayān yāntu ekā māmupatiṣṭhatu ||73||

74prajñātā yā praśāntā yā śeṣā gacchantu cchandataḥ
|
tataḥ pradakṣiṇīkṛtya kanyāstāstamṛṣiṃ tadā ||74||

75niścakramurgṛhāttasmātsā vṛddhā'tha vyatiṣṭhata
|
atha tāṃ saṃviśan prāha śayane bhāsvare tadā ||75||

76tvayāpi supyatāṃ bhadre rajanī hyativartate
|
saṃlāpāttena vipreṇa tathā sā tatra bhāṣitā ||76||

77dvitīye śayane divye saṃviveśa mahāprabhe
|
atha sā vepamānāṅgī nimittaṃ śītajaṃ tadā ||77||

78vyapadiśya maharṣairvai śayanaṃ vyavarohata
|
svāgatenāgatāṃ tāṃ tu bhagavānabhyabhāṣata ||78||

79sopāgūhadbhujābhyāṃ tu ṛṣiṃ prītyā nararṣabha
|
nirvikāramṛṣiṃ cāpi kāṣṭhakuḍyopamaṃ tadā ||79||

80duḥkhitā prekṣya sañjalpamakārṣīdṛṣiṇā saha
|
brahmanna kāmato'nyāsti strīṇāṃ puruṣato dhṛtiḥ ||80||

81kāmena mohitā cāhaṃ tvāṃ bhajantī bhajasva mām
|
prahṛṣṭo bhava viprarṣe samāgaccha mayā saha ||81||

82upagūha ca māṃ vipra kāmārtā'haṃ bhṛśaṃ tvayi
|
etaddhi tava dharmātmaṃstapasaḥ pūjyate phalam ||82||

83prārthitaṃ darśanādeva bhajamānāṃ bhajasva mām
|
mama cedaṃ dhanaṃ sarvaṃ yaccānyadapi paśyasi ||83||

84prabhustvaṃ bhava sarvatra mayi caiva na saṃśayaḥ
|
sarvānkāmānvidhāsyāmi ramasva sahito mayā ||84||

85ramaṇīye vane vipra sarvakāmaphalaprade
|
tvadvaśāhaṃ bhaviṣyāmi raṃsyase ca mayā saha ||85||

86sarvānkāmānupāśnīmo ye divyā ye ca mānuṣāḥ
|
nātaḥ paraṃ hi nārīṇāṃ vidyate ca kadācana ||86||

87yathā puruṣasaṃsargaḥ parametaddhi naḥ phalam
|
ātmacchandena vartante nāryo manmathacoditāḥ ||87||

88na ca dahyanti gacchantyaḥ sutaptairapi pāṃsubhiḥ
|
aṣṭāvakra uvāca|
paradārānahaṃ bhadre na gaccheyaṃ kathañcana ||88||

89dūṣitaṃ dharmaśāstrajñaiḥ paradārābhimarśanam
|
bhadre nirveṣṭukāmaṃ māṃ viddhi satyena vai śape ||89||

90viṣayeṣvanabhijño'haṃ dharmārthaṃ kila santatiḥ
|
evaṃ lokān gamiṣyāmi putrairiti na saṃśayaḥ ||90||

91bhadre dharmaṃ vijānīhi jñātvā coparamasva ha
|
stryuvāca
|
nānilo'gnirna varuṇo na cānye tridaśā dvija ||91||

92priyāḥ strīṇāṃ yathā kāmo ratiśīlā hi yoṣitaḥ
|
sahastre kila nārīṇāṃ prāpyetaikā kadācana ||92||

93tathā śatasahastreṣu yadi kācitpativratā
|
naitā jānanti pitaraṃ na kulaṃ na ca mātaram ||93||

94na bhrātṝnna ca bhartāraṃ na ca putrānna devarān
|
līlāyantyaḥ kulaṃ ghnanti kūlānīva saridvarāḥ
|
doṣānsarvāṃśca matvā''śu prajāpatirabhāṣata ||94||

95bhīṣma uvāca|
tataḥ sa ṛṣirekāgrastāṃ striyaṃ pratyabhāṣata
|
āsyatāṃ rucitaśchandaḥ kiṃ ca kāryaṃ bravīhi me ||95||

96sā strī provāca bhagavan drakṣyase deśakālataḥ
|
vasa tāvanmahābhāga kṛtakṛtyo bhaviṣyasi ||96||

97brahmarṣistāmathovāca sa tatheti yudhiṣṭhira
|
vatsye'haṃ yāvadutsāho bhavatyā nātra saṃśayaḥ ||97||

98atharṣirabhisaṃprekṣya striyaṃ tāṃ jarayā'rditām
|
cintāṃ paramikāṃ bheje santapta iva cābhavat ||98||

99yadyadaṅgaṃ hi so'paśyattasyā viprarṣabhastadā
|
nāramattatra tatrāsya dṛṣṭī rūpavirāgitā ||99||

100devateyaṃ gṛhasyāsya śāpātkiṃ nu virūpitā
|
asyāśca kāraṇaṃ vettuṃ na yuktaṃ sahasā mayā ||100||

101iti cintā viviktasya tamarthaṃ jñātumicchataḥ
|
vyagacchattadahaḥśeṣaṃ manasā vyākulena tu ||101||

102atha sā strī tathovāca bhagavanpaśya vai raveḥ
|
rūpaṃ sandhyābhrasaṃraktaṃ kimupasthāpyatāṃ tava ||102||

103sa uvāca tatastāṃ strīṃ snānodakamihānaya
|
upāsiṣye tataḥ sandhyāṃ vāgyato niyatendriyaḥ ||103||
