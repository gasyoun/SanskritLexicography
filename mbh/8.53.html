Mahābhārata 8.53 (Karṇaparvan)

1sañjaya uvāca|
vartamāne tathā yuddhe kṣatriyāṇāṃ nimajjane
|
gāṇḍīvasya mahāghoṣaḥ śrūyate yudhi māriṣa ||1||

2saṃśaptakānāṃ kadanamakarodyatra pāṇḍavaḥ
|
kosalānāṃ tathā rājannārāyaṇabalasya ca ||2||

3saṃśaptakāstu samare śaravṛṣṭīḥ samantataḥ
|
apātayanpārthamūrdhni jayagṛddhāḥ pramanyavaḥ ||3||

4tā vṛṣṭīḥ sahasā rājaṃstarasā dhārayanprabhuḥ
|
vyagāhata raṇe pārtho vinighnanrathināṃ varān ||4||

5vigāhya tadrathānīkaṃ kaṅkapatraiḥ śilāśitaiḥ
|
āsasāda tataḥ pārthaḥ suśarmāṇaṃ varāyudham ||5||

6sa tasya śaravarṣāṇi vavarṣa rathināṃ varaḥ
|
tathā saṃśaptakāścaiva pārthaṃ bāṇaiḥ samārpayan ||6||

7suśarmā tu tataḥ pārthaṃ viddhvā daśabhirāśugaiḥ
|
janārdanaṃ tribhirbāṇairahanaddakṣiṇe bhuje ||7||

8tato'pareṇa bhallena ketuṃ vivyādha māriṣa
|
sa vānaravaro rājanviśvakarmakṛto mahān ||8||

9nanāda sumahānādaṃ bhīṣayāṇo jagarja ca
|
kapestu ninadaṃ śrutvā saṃtrastā tava vāhinī ||9||

10bhayaṃ vipulamādhāya niśceṣṭā samapadyata
|
tataḥ sā śuśubhe senā niśceṣṭā'vasthitā nṛpa ||10||

11nānāpuṣpasamākīrṇaṃ yathā caitrarathaṃ vanam
|
pratilabhya tataḥ saṃjñāṃ yodhāste kurusattama ||11||

12arjunaṃ siṣicurbāṇaiḥ parvataṃ jaladā iva
|
parivavrustataḥ sarve pāṇḍavasya mahāratham ||12||

13nigṛhya taṃ pracukruśurvadhyamānāḥ śitaiḥ śaraiḥ
|
te hayān rathacakre ca ratheṣāṃ cāpi māriṣa ||13||

14nigrahītumupākrāman krodhāviṣṭāḥ samantataḥ
|
nigṛhya taṃ rathaṃ tasya yodhāste tu sahasraśaḥ ||14||

15nigṛhya balavatsarve siṃhanā‌damathānadan
|
apare jagṛhuścaiva keśavasya mahābhujau ||15||

16pārthamanye mahārāja rathasthaṃ jagṛhurmudā
|
keśavastu tato bāhū vidhunvan raṇamūrdhani ||16||

17pātayāmāsa tānsarvānduṣṭahastīva hastipān
|
tataḥ kruddho raṇe pārthaḥ saṃvṛtastairmahārathaiḥ ||17||

18nigṛhītaṃ rathaṃ dṛṣṭvā keśavaṃ cāpyabhidrutam
|
rathārūḍhāṃstu subahūnpadātīṃścāpyapātayat ||18||

19āsannāṃśca tathā yodhān śarairāsannayodhibhiḥ
|
chādayāmāsa samare keśavaṃ cedamabravīt ||19||

20paśya kṛṣṇa mahābāho saṃśaptakagaṇānbahūn
|
kurvāṇāndāruṇaṃ karma vadhyamānānsahasraśaḥ ||20||

21rathabandhamimaṃ ghoraṃ pṛthivyāṃ nāsti kaścana
|
yaḥ saheta pumā~lloke madanyo yadupuṅgava ||21||

22ityevamuktvā bībhatsurdevadattamathādhamat
|
pāñcajanyaṃ ca kṛṣṇo'pi pūrayanniva rodasī ||22||

23taṃ tu śaṅkhasvanaṃ śrutvā saṃśaptakavarūthinī
|
sañcacāla mahārāja vitrastā cādravadbhṛśam ||23||

24pādabandhaṃ tataścakre pāṇḍavaḥ paravīrahā
|
nāgamastraṃ mahārāja saṃprakīrya muhurmuhuḥ ||24||

25te baddhāḥ pādabandhena pāṇḍavena mahātmanā
|
niśceṣṭāścābhavan rājannaśmasāramayā iva ||25||

26niśceṣṭāṃstu tato yodhānavadhītpāṇḍunandanaḥ
|
yathendraḥ samare daityāṃstārakasya vadhe purā ||26||

27te vadhyamānāḥ samare mumucustaṃ rathottamam
|
āyudhāni ca sarvāṇi visraṣṭumupacakramuḥ ||27||

28te baddhāḥ pādabandhena na śekuśceṣṭituṃ nṛpa
|
tatastānavadhītpārthaḥ śaraiḥ sannataparvabhiḥ ||28||

29sarvayodhā hi samare bhujagairveṣṭitā bhavan
|
yānuddiśya raṇe pārthaḥ pādabandhaṃ cakāra ha ||29||

30tataḥ suśarmā rājendra gṛhītāṃ vīkṣya vāhinīm
|
sauparṇamastraṃ tvaritaḥ prāduścakre mahārathaḥ ||30||

31tataḥ suparṇāḥ saṃpeturbhakṣayanto bhujaṅgamān
|
te vai vidudruvurnāgā dṛṣṭvā tān khacarānnṛpa ||31||

32babhau balaṃ tadvimuktaṃ pādabandhādviśāṃpate
|
meghavṛndādyathā mukto bhāskarastāpayanprajāḥ ||32||

33vipramuktāstu te yodhāḥ phālgunasya rathaṃ prati
|
sasṛjurbāṇasaṃghāṃśca śastrasaṃghāṃśca māriṣa ||33||

34vividhāni ca śastrāṇi pratyavidhyanta sarvaśaḥ
|
tāṃ mahāstramayīṃ dṛṣṭiṃ saṃchidya śaravṛṣṭibhiḥ ||34||

35nyavadhīcca tato yodhānvāsaviḥ paravīrahā
|
suśarmā tu tato rājanbāṇenānataparvaṇā ||35||

36arjunaṃ hṛdaye viddhvā vivyādhānyaistribhiḥ śaraiḥ
|
sa gāḍhaviddho vyathito rathopastha upāviśat ||36||

37tata uccukruśuḥ sarve hataḥ pārtha iti sma ha
|
tataḥ śaṃkhaninādāśca bherīśabdāśca puṣkalāḥ ||37||

38nānāvāditraninadāḥ siṃhanādāśca jajñire
|
pratilabhya tataḥ saṃjñāṃ śvetāśvaḥ kṛṣṇasārathiḥ ||38||

39aindramastramameyātmā prāduścakre tvarānvitaḥ
|
tato bāṇasahasrāṇi samutpannāni māriṣa ||39||

40sarvadikṣu vyadṛśyanta nighnanti tava vāhinīm
|
hayān rathāṃśca samare śastraiḥ śatasahasraśaḥ ||40||

41vadhyamāne tataḥ sainye bhayaṃ sumahadāviśat
|
saṃśaptakagaṇānāṃ ca gopālānāṃ ca bhārata ||41||

42na hi tatra pumānkaścidyo'rjunaṃ pratyavidhyata
|
paśyatāṃ tatra vīrāṇāmahanyata balaṃ tava ||42||

43hanyamānamapaśyaṃśca niśceṣṭaṃ sma parākrame
|
ayutaṃ tatra yodhānāṃ hatvā pāṇḍusuto raṇe ||43||

44vyabhrājata mahārāja vidhūmo'gniriva jvalan
|
caturdaśa sahasrāṇi yāni śiṣṭāni bhārata ||44||

45rathānāmayutaṃ caiva trisāhasrāśca dantinaḥ
|
tataḥ saṃśaptakā bhūyaḥ parivavrurdhanañjayam ||45||

46martavyamiti niścitya jayaṃ vāpyanivartanam
|
tatra yuddhaṃ mahaccāsīttāvakānāṃ viśāṃpate
|
śūreṇa balinā sārdhaṃ pāṇḍavena kirīṭinā ||46||
