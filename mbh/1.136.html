Mahābhārata 1.136 (Ādiparvan)

1vaiśaṃpāyana uvāca|
datte'vakāśe puruṣairvismayotphullalocanaḥ
|
viveśa raṅgaṃ vistīrṇaṃ karṇaḥ parapurañjayaḥ||1||

2sahajaṃ kavacaṃ bibhratkuṇḍaloddyotitānanaḥ
|
sa dhanurbaddhanistriṃśaḥ pādacārīva parvataḥ||2||

3kanyāgarbhaḥ pṛthuyaśāḥ pṛthāyāḥ pṛthulocanaḥ
|
tīkṣṇāṃśorbhāskarasyāṃśaḥ karṇo'rigaṇasūdanaḥ||3||

4siṃharṣabhagajendrāṇāṃ balavīryaparākramaḥ
|
dīptikāntidyutiguṇaiḥ sūryendujvalanopamaḥ||4||

5prāṃśuḥ kanakatālābhaḥ siṃhasaṃhanano yuvā
|
asaṅkhyeyaguṇaḥ śrīmānbhāskarasyātmasaṃbhavaḥ||5||

6sa nirīkṣya mahābāhuḥ sarvato raṅgamaṇḍalam
|
praṇāmaṃ droṇakṛpayornātyādṛtamivākarot||6||

7sa samājajanaḥ sarvo niścalaḥ sthiralocanaḥ
|
ko'yamityāgatakṣobhaḥ kautūhalaparo'bhavat||7||

8so'bravīnmeghagambhīrasvareṇa vadatāṃ varaḥ
|
bhrātā bhrātaramajñātaṃ sāvitraḥ pākaśāsanim||8||

9pārtha yatte kṛtaṃ karma viśeṣavadahaṃ tataḥ
|
kariṣye paśyatāṃ nṝṇāmātmanā vismayaṃgamaḥ||9||

10asamāpte tatastasya vacane vadatāṃ vara
|
yantrotkṣipta ivottasthau kṣipraṃ vai sarvato janaḥ||10||

11prītiśca manujavyāghra duryodhanamupāviśat
|
hrīśca krodhaśca bībhatsuṃ kṣaṇenānvāviveśa ha||11||

12tato droṇābhyanujñātaḥ karṇaḥ priyaraṇaḥ sadā
|
yatkṛtaṃ tatra pārthena taccakāra mahābalaḥ||12||

13atha duryodhanastatra bhrātṛbhiḥ saha bhārata
|
karṇaṃ pariṣvajya mudā tato vacanamabravīt||13||

14duryodhana uvāca|
svāgataṃ te mahābāho diṣṭyā prāpto'si mānada
|
ahaṃ ca kururājyaṃ ca yatheṣṭamupabhujyatām||14||

15karṇa uvāca|
kṛtaṃ sarvamahaṃ manye sakhitvaṃ ca tvayā vṛṇe
|
dvandvayuddhaṃ ca pārthena kartumicchāmyahaṃ prabho||15||

16duryodhana uvāca|
bhuṅkṣva bhogānmayā sārdhaṃ bandhūnāṃ priyakṛdbhava
|
durhṛdāṃ kuru sarveṣāṃ mūrdhni pādamarindama||16||

17vaiśaṃpāyana uvāca|
tataḥ kṣiptamivātmānaṃ matvā pārtho'bhyabhāṣata
|
karṇaṃ bhrātṛsamūhasya madhye'calamiva sthitam||17||

18arjuna uvāca|
anāhūtopasṛṣṭānāmanāhūtopajalpinām
|
ye lokāstānhataḥ karṇa mayā tvaṃ pratipatsyase||18||

19karṇa uvāca|
raṅgo'yaṃ sarvasāmānyaḥ kimatra tava phālguna
|
vīryaśreṣṭhāśca rājāno balaṃ dharmo'nuvartate||19||

20kiṃ kṣepairdurbalāyāsaiḥ śaraiḥ kathaya bhārata
|
guroḥ samakṣaṃ yāvatte harāmyadya śiraḥ śaraiḥ||20||

21vaiśaṃpāyana uvāca|
tato droṇābhyanujñātaḥ pārthaḥ parapurañjayaḥ
|
bhrātṛbhistvarayāśliṣṭo raṇāyopajagāma tam||21||

22tato duryodhanenāpi sabhrātrā samarodyataḥ
|
pariṣvaktaḥ sthitaḥ karṇaḥ pragṛhya saśaraṃ dhanuḥ||22||

23tataḥ savidyutstanitaiḥ sendrāyudhapurogamaiḥ
|
āvṛtaṃ gaganaṃ meghairbalākāpaṅktihāsibhiḥ||23||

24tataḥ snehāddharihayaṃ dṛṣṭvā raṅgāvalokinam
|
bhāskaro'pyanayannāśaṃ samīpopagatānghanān||24||

25meghacchāyopagūḍhastu tato'dṛśyata phālgunaḥ
|
sūryātapaparikṣiptaḥ karṇo'pi samadṛśyata||25||

26dhārtarāṣṭrā yataḥ karṇastasmindeśe vyavasthitāḥ
|
bhāradvājaḥ kṛpo bhīṣmo yataḥ pārthastato'bhavan||26||

27dvidhā raṅgaḥ samabhavatstrīṇāṃ dvaidhamajāyata
|
kuntibhojasutā mohaṃ vijñātārthā jagāma ha||27||

28tāṃ tathā mohamāpannāṃ viduraḥ sarvadharmavit
|
kuntīmāśvāsayāmāsa preṣyābhiścandanodakaiḥ||28||

29tataḥ pratyāgataprāṇā tāvubhau paridaṃśitau
|
putrau dṛṣṭvā susaṃbhrāntā nānvapadyata kiṃcana||29||

30tāvudyatamahācāpau kṛpaḥ śāradvato'bravīt
|
dvandvayuddhasamācāre kuśalaḥ sarvadharmavit||30||

31ayaṃ pṛthāyāstanayaḥ kanīyānpāṇḍunandanaḥ
|
kauravo bhavatā sārdhaṃ dvandvayuddhaṃ kariṣyati||31||

32tvamapyevaṃ mahābāho mātaraṃ pitaraṃ kulam
|
kathayasva narendrāṇāṃ yeṣāṃ tvaṃ kulabhūṣaṇam||32||

33tato viditvā pārthastvāṃ pratiyotsyati vā na vā
|
vṛthākulasamācārairna yudhyante nṛpātmajāḥ||33||

34vaiśaṃpāyana uvāca|
evamuktasya karṇasya vrīḍāvanatamānanam
|
babhau varṣāmbuviklinnaṃ padmamāgalitaṃ yathā||34||

35duryodhana uvāca|
ācārya trividhāyonī rājñāṃ śāstraviniścaye
|
satkulīnaśca śūraśca yaśca senāṃ prakarṣati||35||

36yadyayaṃ phālguno yuddhe nārājñā yoddhumicchati
|
tasmādeṣo'ṅgaviṣaye mayā rājye'bhiṣicyate||36||

37vaiśaṃpāyana uvāca|
tatastasminkṣaṇe karṇaḥ salājakusumairghaṭaiḥ
|
kāñcanaiḥ kāñcane pīṭhe mantravidbhirmahārathaḥ||37||

38abhiṣikto'ṅgarāje sa śriyā yukto mahābalaḥ
|
sacchatravālavyajano jayaśabdottareṇa ca||38||

39uvāca kauravaṃ rājanvacanaṃ sa vṛṣastadā
|
asya rājyapradānasya sadṛśaṃ kiṃ dadāni te||39||

40prabrūhi rājaśārdūla kartā hyasmi tathā nṛpa
|
atyantaṃ sakhyamicchāmītyāha taṃ sa suyodhanaḥ||40||

41evamuktastataḥ karṇastatheti pratyuvāca tam
|
harṣāccobhau samāśliṣya parāṃ mudamavāpatuḥ||41||
