Mahābhārata 16.1 (Mausalaparvan)

1vaiśampāyana uvāca|
ṣaṭtriṃśe tvatha samprāpte varṣe kauravanandanaḥ
|
dadarśa viparītāni nimittāni yudhiṣṭhiraḥ||1||

2vavurvātāśca nirghātā rūkṣāḥ śarkaravarṣiṇaḥ
|
apasavyāni śakunā maṇḍalāni pracakrire||2||

3pratyagūhurmahānadyo diśo nīhārasaṃvṛtāḥ
|
ulkāścāṅgāravarṣiṇyaḥ prāpatangaganādbhuvi||3||

4ādityo rajasā rājan samavacchannamaṇḍalaḥ
|
viraśmirudaye nityaṃ kabandhaiḥ samadṛśyata||4||

5pariveṣāśca dṛśyante dāruṇāścandrasūryayoḥ
|
trivarṇāḥ śyāmarūkṣāntāstathā bhasmāruṇaprabhāḥ||5||

6ete cānye ca bahava utpātā bhayaśaṃsinaḥ
|
dṛśyante bahavo rājan hradayodvegakārakāḥ||6||

7kasyacittvatha kālasya kururājo yudhiṣṭhiraḥ
|
śuśrāva vṛṣṇicakrasya mausale kadanaṃ kṛtam||7||

8vimuktaṃ vāsudevaṃ ca śrutvā rāmaṃ ca pāṇḍavaḥ
|
samānīyābravīdbhrātṝnkiṃ kariṣyāma ityuta||8||

9parasparaṃ samāsādya brahmadaṇḍabalātkṛtān
|
vṛṣṇīnvinaṣṭāṃste śrutvā vyathitāḥ pāṇḍavābhavan||9||

10nidhanaṃ vāsudevasya samudrasyeva śoṣaṇam
|
vīrā na śraddadhustasya vināśaṃ śārṅgadhanvanaḥ||10||

11mausalaṃ te samāśritya duḥkhaśokasamanvitāḥ
|
viṣaṇṇā hatasaṅkalpāḥ pāṇḍavāḥ samupāviśan||11||

12janamejaya uvāca|
kathaṃ vinaṣṭā bhagavannandhakā vṛṣṇibhiḥ saha
|
paśyato vāsudevasya bhojāścaiva mahārathāḥ||12||

13vaiśampāyana uvāca|
ṣaṭtriṃśe'tha tato varṣe vṛṣṇīnāmanayo mahān
|
anyonyaṃ musalaiste tu nijaghnuḥ kālacoditāḥ||13||

14janamejaya uvāca|
kenānuśaptāste vīrāḥ kṣayaṃ vṛṣṇyandhakā gatāḥ
|
bhojāśca dvijavarya tvaṃ vistareṇa vadasva me||14||

15vaiśampāyana uvāca|
viśvāmitraṃ ca kaṇvaṃ ca nāradaṃ ca tapodhanam
|
sāraṇapramukhā vīrā dadṛśurdvārakāṃ gatān||15||

16te tānsāmbaṃ puraskṛtya bhūṣayitvā striyaṃ yathā
|
abruvannupasaṃ gamya daivadaṇḍanipīḍitāḥ||16||

17iyaṃ strī putrakāmasya babhroramitatejasaḥ
|
ṛṣayaḥ sādhu jānīta kimiyaṃ janayiṣyati||17||

18ityuktāstetadārājanvipralambhapradharṣitāḥ
|
pratyubruvaṃstānmunayo yattacchṛṇu narādhipa||18||

19vṛṣṇyandhakavināśāya musalaṃ ghoramāyasam
|
vāsudevasya dāyādaḥ sāmbo'yaṃ janayiṣyati||19||

20yena yūyaṃ sudurvṛttā nṛśaṃsā jātamanyavaḥ
|
ucchettāraḥ kulaṃ kṛtsnamṛte rāmajanārdanau||20||

21samudraṃ yāsyati śrīmāṃstyaktvā dehaṃ halāyudhaḥ
|
jarā kṛṣṇaṃ mahātmānaṃ śayānaṃ bhuvi bhetsyati||21||

22ityabruvanta te rājanpralabdhāstairdurātmabhiḥ
|
munayaḥ krodharaktākṣāḥ samīkṣyātha parasparam||22||

23tathoktvā munayaste tu tataḥ keśavamabhyayuḥ
|
athābravīttadā vṛṣṇīn śrutvaivaṃ madhusūdanaḥ||23||

24antajño matimāṃstasya bhavitavyaṃ tatheti tān
|
evamuktvā hṛṣīkeśaḥ praviveśa purantadā||24||

25kṛtāntamanyathā naicchatkartuṃ sa jagataḥ prabhuḥ
|
śvobhūte'tha tataḥ sāmbo musalaṃ tadasūta vai||25||

26yena vṛṣṇyandhakakule puruṣā bhasmasātkṛtāḥ
|
vṛṣṇyandhakavināśāya kiṅkarapratimaṃ mahat||26||

27asūta śāpajaṃ ghoraṃ tacca rājñe nyavedayan
|
viṣaṇṇarūpastadrājā sūkṣmaṃ cūrṇamakārayat||27||

28taccūrṇaṃ sāgare cāpi prākṣipanpuruṣā nṛpa
|
aghoṣayaṃśca nagare vacanādāhukasya te||28||

29janārdanasya rāmasya babhroścaiva mahātmanaḥ
|
adyaprabhṛti sarveṣu vṛṣṇyandhakakuleṣviha||29||

30surāsavo na kartavyaḥ sarvairnagaravāsibhiḥ
|
yaśca no viditaṅkuryātpeyaṃ kaścinnaraḥ kvacit||30||

31jīvansa śūlamārohetsvayaṃ kṛtvā sabāndhavaḥ
|
tato rājabhayātsarve niyamaṃ cakrire tadā
|
narāḥ śāsanamājñāya rāmasyākliṣṭakarmaṇaḥ||31||
