Mahābhārata 6.61 (Bhīṣmaparvan)

1sañjaya uvāca|
drauṇirbhūriśravāḥ śalyaścitrasenaśca māriṣaḥ
|
putraḥ sāṃyamaneścaiva saubhadraṃ paryavārayan ||1||

2saṃsaktamatitejobhistamekaṃ dadṛśurjanāḥ
|
pañcabhirmanujavyāghrairgajaiḥ siṃhaśiśuṃ yathā ||2||

3nātilakṣyatayā kaścinna śaurye na parākrame
|
babhūva sadṛśaḥ kārṣṇenāṃstre nāpi ca lāghave ||3||

4tathā tamātmajaṃ yuddhe vikramantamarindamam
|
dṛṣṭvā pārthaḥ susaṃyattaṃ siṃhanāda‌mathānadat ||4||

5pīḍayānaṃ tu tat sainyaṃ pautraṃ tava viśāṃpate
|
dṛṣṭvā tvadīyā rājendra samantāt paryavārayan ||5||

6dhvajinīṃ dhārtarāṣṭrāṇāṃ dīnaśatruradīnavat
|
pratyudyayau sa saubhadrastejasā ca balena ca ||6||

7tasya lāghavamārgasthamādityasadṛśaprabham
|
vyadṛśyata mahaccāpaṃ samare yudhyataḥ paraiḥ ||7||

8sa drauṇimiṣuṇaikena viddhvā śalyaṃ ca pañcabhiḥ
|
dhvajaṃ sāṃyamanaiścaiva so'ṣṭābhiścicchide tataḥ ||8||

9rukmadaṇḍāṃ mahāśaktiṃ preṣitāṃ saumadattinā
|
śitenoragasaṃkāśāṃ patriṇā'pajahāra tām ||9||

10śalyasya ca mahāvegānasyataḥ samare śarān
|
nivāryārjunadāyādo jaghāna caturo hayān ||10||

11bhūriśravāśca śalyaśca drauṇiḥ sāṃyamaniḥ śalaḥ
|
nābhyavartanta saṃrabdhāḥ kārṣṇerbāhubalodayam ||11||

12tatastrigartā rājendra madrāśca saha kekayaiḥ
|
pañcaviṃśatisāhasrāstava putreṇa coditāḥ ||12||

13dhanurveda‌vido mukhyā ajeyāḥ śatrubhiryudhi
|
sahaputraṃ jighāṃsantaṃ parivavruḥ kirīṭinam ||13||

14tau tu tatra pitāputrau parikṣiptau mahārathau
|
dadarśa rājan pāñcālyaḥ senāpatirarindama ||14||

15sa vāraṇarathaughānāṃ sahastrairbahubhirvṛtaḥ
|
vājibhiḥ pattibhiścaiva vṛtaḥ śatasahasraśaḥ ||15||

16dhanurvisphārya saṃkruddho nodayitvā ca vāhinīm
|
yayau taṃ madrakānīkaṃ kekayāṃśca paraṃtapa ||16||

17tena kīrtimatā guptamanīkaṃ dṛḍhadhanvanā
|
saṃrabdharathanāgāśvaṃ yotsyamānamaśobhata ||17||

18so'rjunapramukhe yāntaṃ pāñcālakulavardhanaḥ
|
tribhiḥ śāradvataṃ bāṇairjatrudeśe samārpayat ||18||

19tataḥ sa madrakān hatvā daśaiva daśabhiḥ śaraḥ
|
pṛṣṭharakṣaṃ jaghānāśu bhallena kṛtavarmaṇaḥ ||19||

20damanaṃ cāpi dāyādaṃ pauravasya mahātmanaḥ
|
jaghāna vimalāgreṇa nārācena paraṃtapaḥ ||20||

21tataḥ sāṃyamaneḥ putraḥ pāñcālyaṃ yuddhadurmadam
|
avidhyastriṃśatā bāṇairdaśabhiścāsya sārathim ||21||

22so'tividdho maheṣvāsaḥ sṛkkiṇī parisaṃlihan
|
bhallena bhṛśatīkṣṇena nicakartāsya kārmukam ||22||

23athainaṃ pañcaviṃśatyā kṣiprameva samārpayat
|
aśvāṃścāsyāvadhīdrājannubhau tau pārṣṇisārathī ||23||

24sa hatāśve rathe tiṣṭhan dadarśa bharatarṣabha
|
putraḥ sāṃyamaneḥ putraṃ pāñcālyasya mahātmanaḥ ||24||

25sa pragṛhya mahāghoraṃ nistriṃśavaramāyasam
|
padātistūrṇamānarcchadrathasthaṃ puruṣarṣabhaḥ ||25||

26taṃ mahaughamivāyāntaṃ khāt patantamivoragam
|
bhrāntāvaraṇanistriṃśaṃ kālotsṛṣṭamivāntakam ||26||

27dīpyamānamivādityaṃ mattavāraṇavikramam
|
apaśyan pāṇḍavāstatra dhṛṣṭadyumnaśca pārṣataḥ ||27||

28tasya pāñcāladāyādaḥ pratīpamabhidhāvataḥ
|
śitanistriṃśahastasya śarāvaraṇadhāriṇaḥ ||28||

29bāṇavegamatītasya tathā'bhyāśamupeyuṣaḥ
|
tvaransenāpatiḥ kruddho bibheda gadayā śiraḥ ||29||

30tasya rājan sa nistriṃśaṃ suprabhaṃ ca śarāvaram
|
hatasya patato hastādvegena nyapatadbhuvi ||30||

31taṃ nihatya gadāgreṇa sa lebhe paramāṃ mudam
|
putraḥ pāñcālarājasya mahātmā bhīmavikramaḥ ||31||

32tasmin hate maheṣvāse rājaputre mahārathe
|
hāhākāro mahānāsīttava sainyasya māriṣa ||32||

33tataḥ sāṃyamaniḥ kruddho dṛṣṭvā nihatamātmajam
|
abhidudrāva vegena pāñcālyaṃ yuddhadurmadam ||33||

34tau tatra samare śūrau sametau yuddhadurmadau
|
dadṛśuḥ sarvarājānaḥ kuravaḥ pāṇḍavāstathā ||34||

35tataḥ sāṃyamaniḥ kruddhaḥ pārṣataṃ paravīrahā
|
ājaghāna tribhirbāṇaistotrairiva mahādvipam ||35||

36tathaiva pārṣataṃ śūraṃ śalyaḥ samitiśobhanaḥ
|
ājaghānorasi kruddhastato yuddhamavartata ||36||
