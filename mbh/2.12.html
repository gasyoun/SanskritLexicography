Mahābhārata 2.12 (Sabhāparvan)

1prāyaśo rājalokaste kathito vadatāṃ vara
|
vaivasvatasabhāyāṃ tu yathā vadasi me prabho||1||

2varuṇasya sabhāyāṃ tu nāgāste kathitā vibho
|
daityendrāścāpi bhūyiṣṭhāḥ saritaḥ sāgarāstathā||2||

3tathā dhanapateryakṣā guhyakā rākṣasāstathā
|
gandharvāpsarasaścaiva bhagavāṃśca vṛṣadhvajaḥ||3||

4pitāmahasabhāyāṃ tu kathitāste maharṣabhaḥ
|
sarve devanikāyāśca sarvaśāstrāṇi caiva ha||4||

5śakrasya tu sabhāyāṃ tu devāḥ saṅkīrtitā mune
|
uddeśataśca gandharvā vividhāśca maharṣayaḥ||5||

6eka eva tu rājarṣirhariścandro mahāmune
|
kathitaste sabhāyāṃ vai devendrasya mahātmanaḥ||6||

7kiṃ karma tenācaritaṃ tapo vā niyatavrata
|
yenāsau saha śakreṇa spaddhate sumahāyaśāḥ||7||

8pitṛlokagataścaiva tvayā vipra pitā mama
|
dṛṣṭaḥ pāṇḍurmahābhāgaḥ kathaṃ vā'pi samāgataḥ||8||

9kimuktavāṃśca bhagavaṃstanmamācakṣva suvrata
|
tvattaḥ śrotuṃ sarvamidaṃ paraṃ kautūhalaṃ hi me||9||

10nārada uvāca|
yanmāṃ pṛcchasi rājendra hariścandraṃ prati prabho
|
tatte'haṃ sampravakṣyāmi māhātmyaṃ tasya dhīmataḥ||10||

11sa rājā balavānāsītsamrāṭ sarvamahīkṣitām
|
tasya sarve mahīpālāḥ śāsanāvanatāḥ sthitāḥ||11||

12tenaikaṃ rathamāsthāya jaitraṃ hemavibhūṣitam
|
śastrapratāpena jitā dvīpāḥ sapta janeśvara||12||

13sa nirjitya mahīṃ kṛtsnāṃ saśailavanakānanām
|
ājahāra mahārāja rājasūyaṃ mahākratum||13||

14tasya sarve mahīpālā dhanānyājahrurājñayā
|
dvijānāṃ pariveṣṭārastasminyajñe ca te'bhavan||14||

15prādācca draviṇaṃ prītyā yācakānāṃ nareśvaraḥ
|
yathoktavantaste tasmiṃstataḥ pañcaguṇādhikam||15||

16atarpayacca vividhairvasubhirbrāhmaṇāṃstadā
|
prasarpakāle samprāpte nānādigbhyaḥ samāgatān||16||

17bhakṣyabhojyaiśca vividhairyathākāmapuraskṛtaiḥ
|
ratnaughatarpitaistuṣṭairdvijaiśca samudāhṛtam||17||

18tejasvī ca yaśasvī ca nṛpebhyo'bhyadhiko'bhavat
|
etasmātkāraṇādrājanhariścandro virājate
|
tebhyo rājasahasrebhyastadviddhi bharatarṣabha||18||

19samāpya ca hariścandro mahāyajñaṃ pratāpavān
|
abhiṣiktaśca śuśubhe sāmrājyena narādhipa||19||

20ye cānye ca mahīpālā rājasūyaṃ mahākratum
|
yajante te sahendreṇa modante bharatarṣabha||20||

21ye cāpi ni naṃ prāptāḥ saṅgrāmeṣvapalāyinaḥ
|
te tatsadanamāsādya mondate bharatarṣabha||21||

22tapasā ye ca tīvreṇa tyajantīha kalevaram
|
te tatsthānaṃ samāsādya śrīmanto bhānti nityaśaḥ||22||

23pitā ca tvā''ha kaunteya pāṇḍuḥ kauravanandana
|
hariścandre śriyaṃ dṛṣṭvā nṛpatau jātavismayaḥ||23||

24vijñāya mānuṣaṃ lokamāyāntaṃ māṃ narādhipa
|
provāca praṇato bhūtvā vadethāstvaṃ yudhiṣṭhiram||24||

25samartho'si mahīṃ jetuṃ bhrātaraste sthitā vaśe
|
rājasūyaṃ kratuśreṣṭhamāharasveti bhārata||25||

26tvayīṣṭavati putre'haṃ hariścandravadāśu vai
|
modiṣye bahulāḥ śaśvatsamāḥ śakrasya saṃsadi||26||

27evaṃ bhavatu vakṣye'haṃ tava putraṃ narādhipam
|
bhūrlokaṃ yadi gaccheyamiti pāṇḍumathābruvam||27||

28tasya tvaṃ puruṣavyāghra saṅkalpaṃ kuru pāṇḍava
|
gantāsi tvaṃ mahendrasya pūrvaiḥ saha salokatām||28||

29bahuvighnaśca nṛpate kratureṣa smṛto mahān
|
chidrāṇyasya tu vāñchanti yajñaghnā brahmarākṣasāḥ||29||

30yuddhaṃ ca kṣatraśamanaṃ pṛthivīkṣayakāraṇam
|
kiñcideva nimittaṃ ca bhavatyatra kṣayāvaham||30||

31etatsañcintya rājendra yatkṣamaṃ tatsamācara
|
apramattotthito nityaṃ cāturvarṇyasya rakṣaṇe||31||

32bhava edhasva modasva dhanaistarpaya ca dvijān
|
etatte vistareṇoktaṃ yanmāṃ tvaṃ paripṛcchasi
|
āpṛcche tvāṃ gamiṣyāmi dāśārhanagarīṃ prati||32||

33vaiśampāyana uvāca|
evamākhyāya pārthebhyo nārado janamejaya
|
jagāma tairvṛto rājannṛṣibhiryaiḥ samāgataḥ||33||

34gate tu nārade pārtho bhrātṛbhiḥ saha kauravaḥ
|
rājasūyaṃ kratuśreṣṭhaṃ cintayāmāsa pārthivaḥ||34||
