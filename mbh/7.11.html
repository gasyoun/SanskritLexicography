Mahābhārata 7.11 (Droṇaparvan)

1dhṛtarāṣṭra uvāca|
śṛṇu divyāni karmāṇi vāsudevasya sañjaya
|
kṛtavānyāni govindo yathā nānyaḥ pumān kvacit||1||

2saṃvardhatā gopakule bālenaiva mahātmanā
|
vikhyāpitaṃ balaṃ bāhvostriṣu lokeṣu sañjaya||2||

3uccaiḥśravastulyabalaṃ vāyuvegasamaṃ jave
|
jaghāna hayarājaṃ taṃ yamunāvanavāsinam||3||

4dānavaṃ ghorakarmāṇaṃ gavāṃ mṛtyumivotthitam
|
vṛṣarūpadharaṃ bālye bhujābhyāṃ nijaghāna ha||4||

5pralambaṃ narakaṃ jambhaṃ pīṭhaṃ cāpi mahāsuram
|
muruṃ cāntakasaṅkāśamavadhīt puṣkarekṣaṇaḥ||5||

6tathā kaṃso mahātejā jarāsandhena pālitaḥ
|
vikrameṇaiva kṛṣṇena sagaṇaḥ pātito raṇe||6||

7sunāmā raṇavikrāntaḥ samagrākṣauhiṇīpatiḥ
|
bhojarājasya madhyastho bhrātā kaṃsasya vīryavān ||7||

8baladevadvitīyena kṛṣṇenāmitraghātinā
|
tarasvī samare dagdhaḥ sasainyaḥ śūrasenarāṭ||8||

9durvāsā nāma viprarṣistathā paramakopanaḥ
|
ārādhitaḥ sadāreṇa sa cāsmai pradadau varān||9||

10tathā gāndhārarājasya sutāṃ vīraḥ svayaṃvare
|
nirjitya pṛthivīpālānāvahat puṣkarekṣaṇaḥ||10||

11amṛṣyamāṇā rājāno yasya jātyā hayā iva
|
rathe vaivāhike yuktāḥ pratodena kṛtavraṇāḥ||11||

12jarāsandhaṃ mahābāhumupāyena janārdanaḥ
|
pareṇa ghātayāmāsa samagrākṣauhiṇīpatim||12||

13cedirājaṃ ca vikrāntaṃ rājasenāpatiṃ balī
|
arghe vivadamānaṃ ca jaghāna paśuvat tadā||13||

14saubhaṃ daityapuraṃ svasthaṃ śālvaguptaṃ durāsadam
|
samudrakukṣau vikramya pātayāmāsa mādhavaḥ||14||

15aṅgān vaṅgān kaliṅgāṃśca māgadhān kāśikosalān
|
vātsyagārgyakarūṣāṃśca pauṇḍrāṃścāpyajayadraṇe||15||

16āvantyān dākṣiṇātyāṃśca pārvatīyān daśerakān
|
kāśmīrakānaurasikān piśācāṃśca samudgalān||16||

17kāmbojān vāṭadhānāṃśca colān pāṇḍyāṃśca sañjaya
|
trigartān mālavāṃścaiva daradāṃśca sudurjayān||17||

18nānādigbhyaśca saṃprāptān khaśāṃścaiva śakāṃstathā
|
jitavān puṇḍarīkākṣo yavanaṃ ca sahānugam||18||

19praviśya makarāvāsaṃ yādogaṇaniṣevitam
|
jigāya varuṇaṃ saṃkhye salilāntargataṃ purā||19||

20yudhi pañcajanaṃ hatvā daityaṃ pātālavāsinam
|
pāñcajanyaṃ hṛṣīkeśo divyaṃ śaṅkhamavāptavān||20||

21khāṇḍave pārthasahitastoṣayitvā hutāśanam
|
āgneyamastraṃ durdharṣaṃ cakraṃ lebhe mahābalaḥ||21||

22vainateyaṃ samāruhya trāsayitvā'marāvatīm
|
mahendrabhavanādvīraḥ pārijātamupānayat ||22||

23tacca marṣitavān śakro jānaṃstasya parākramam
|
rājñāṃ cāpyajitaṃ kañcit kṛṣṇeneha na śuśruma||23||

24yacca tanmahadāścaryaṃ sabhāyāṃ mama sañjaya
|
kṛtavān puṇḍarīkākṣaḥ kastadanya ihārhati||24||

25yacca bhaktyā prasanno'hamadrākṣaṃ kṛṣṇamīśvaram
|
tanme suviditaṃ sarve pratyakṣamiva cāgamam||25||

26nānto vikramayuktasya buddhyā yuktasya vā punaḥ
|
karmaṇāṃ śakyate gantuṃ hṛṣīkeśasya sañjaya||26||

27tathā gadaśca sāmbaśca pradyumno'tha vidūrathaḥ
|
agāvaho niruddhaśca cārudeṣṇaḥ sasāraṇaḥ||27||

28ulmuko niśaṭhaścaiva jhillībabhruśca vīryavān
|
pṛthuśca vipṛthuścaiva śamīko'thārimejayaḥ||28||

29ete'nye balavantaśca vṛṣṇivīrāḥ prahāriṇaḥ
|
kathañcit pāṇḍavānīkaṃ śrayeyuḥ samare sthitāḥ||29||

30āhūtā vṛṣṇivīreṇa keśavena mahātmanā
|
tataḥ saṃśayitaṃ sarvaṃ bhavediti matirmama||30||

31nāgāyutabalo vīraḥ kailāsaśikharopamaḥ
|
vanamālī halī rāmastatra yatra janārdanaḥ ||31||

32yamāhuḥ sarvapitaraṃ vāsudevaṃ dvijātayaḥ
|
api vā hyeṣa pāṇḍūnāṃ yotsyate'rthāya sañjaya||32||

33sa yadā tāta saṃnahyet pāṇḍavārthāya sañjaya
|
na tadā pratisaṃyoddhā bhavitā tatra kaścana ||33||

34yadi sma kuravaḥ sarve jayeyurnāma pāṇḍavān
|
vārṣṇeyo'rthāya teṣāṃ vai gṛhṇīyācchastramuttamam||34||

35tataḥ sarvānnaravyāghro hatvā narapatīn raṇe
|
kauravāṃśca mahābāhuḥ kuntyai dadyāt sa medinīm||35||

36yasya yantā hṛṣīkeśo yoddhā yasya dhanañjayaḥ
|
rathasya tasya kaḥ saṃkhye pratyanīko bhavedrathaḥ||36||

37na kenacidupāyena kurūṇāṃ dṛśyate jayaḥ
|
tasmānme sarvamācakṣva yathā yuddhamavartata||37||

38arjunaḥ keśavasyātmā kṛṣṇo'pyātmā kirīṭinaḥ
|
arjune vijayo nityaṃ kṛṣṇe kīrtiśca śāśvatī||38||

39sarveṣvapi ca lokeṣu vībhatsuraparājitaḥ
|
prādhānyenaiva bhūyiṣṭhamameyāḥ keśave guṇāḥ||39||

40mohādduryodhanaḥ kṛṣṇaṃ yo na vettīha keśavam
|
mohito daivayogena mṛtyupāśapuraskṛtaḥ||40||

41na veda kṛṣṇaṃ dāśārhamarjunaṃ caiva pāṇḍavam
|
pūrvadevau mahātmānau naranārāyaṇāvubhau||41||

42ekātmānau dvidhābhūtau dṛśyete mānavairbhuvi
|
manasā'pi hi durdharṣau senāmetāṃ yaśasvinau||42||

43nāśayetāmihecchantau mānuṣatvācca necchataḥ
|
yugasyeva viparyāso lokānāmiva mohanam||43||

44bhīṣmasya ca vadhastāta droṇasya ca mahātmanaḥ
|
na hyeva brahmacaryeṇa na vedādhyayanena ca||44||

45na kriyābhirna cāstreṇa mṛtyoḥ kaścinnivāryate
|
lokasaṃbhāvitau vīrau kṛtāstrau yuddhadurmadau||45||

46bhīṣmadroṇau hatau śrutvā kiṃ nu jīvāmi sañjaya
|
yāṃ tāṃ śriyamasūyāmaḥ purā dṛṣṭvā yudhiṣṭhire||46||

47adya tāmanujānīmo bhīṣmadroṇavadhena ha
|
matkṛte cāpyanuprāptaḥ kurūṇāmeṣa saṃkṣayaḥ||47||

48pakvānāṃ hi vadhe sūta vajrāyante tṛṇānyuta
|
anantamidamaiśvaryaṃ loke prāpto yudhiṣṭhiraḥ||48||

49yasya kopānmahātmānau bhīṣmadroṇau nipātitau
|
prāptaḥ prakṛtito dharmo na dharmo māmakān prati||49||

50krūraḥ sarvavināśāya kālo'sau nātivartate
|
anyathā cintitā hyarthā naraistāta manasvibhiḥ||50||

51anyathaiva prapadyante daivāditi matirmama
|
tasmādaparihārye'rthe saṃprāpte kṛcchra uttame
|
apāraṇīye duścintye yathābhūtaṃ pracakṣva me||51||
