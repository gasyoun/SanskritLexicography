Mahābhārata 12.275 (Śāntiparvan)

1bhīṣma uvāca|
atraivodāharantīmamitihāsaṃ purātanam
|
nāradasya ca saṃvādaṃ devalasyāsitasya ca ||1||

2āsīnaṃ devalaṃ vṛddhaṃ buddhvā buddhimatāṃ varam
|
nāradaḥ paripapraccha bhūtānāṃ prabhavāpyayam ||2||

3nārada uvāca|
kutaḥ sṛṣṭamidaṃ viśvaṃ brahman sthāvarajaṅgamam
|
pralaye ca kamabhyeti tadbhavānprabravītu me ||3||

4asita uvāca |
yebhyaḥ sṛjati bhūtāni kāle bhāvapracoditaḥ
|
mahābhūtāni pañceti tānyāhurbhūtacintakāḥ ||4||

5tebhyaḥ sṛjati bhūtāni kāla ātmapracoditaḥ
|
etebhyo yaḥ paraṃ brūyādasadbrūyādasaṃśayam ||5||

6viddhi nārada pañcaitāśāśvatānacalān dhruvān
|
mahatastejaso rāśīn kālaṣaṣṭhān svabhāvataḥ ||6||

7āpaścaivāntarikṣaṃ ca pṛthivī vāyupāvakau
|
nāsīddhi paramaṃ tebhyo bhūtebhyo muktasaṃśayam ||7||

8nopapattyā na vā yuktyā tvasadbrūyādasaṃśayam
|
vetthaitānabhinirvṛttānṣaḍete yasya rāśayaḥ ||8||

9pañcaiva tāni kālaśca bhāvābhāvau ca kevalau
|
aṣṭau bhūtāni bhūtānāṃ śāśvatāni bhavātyayau ||9||

10abhāvaṃ yānti teṣveva tebhyaśca prabhavantyapi
|
vinaṣṭo'pyanu tānyeva janturbhavati pañcadhā ||10||

11tasya bhūmimayo dehaḥ śrotramākāśasambhavam
|
sūryāccakṣurasuyoradbhyastu khalu śoṇitam ||11||

12cakṣuṣī nāsikākarṇau tvak jihveti ca pañcamī
|
indriyāṇīndriyārthānāṃ jñānāni kavayo viduḥ ||12||

13darśanaṃ śravaṇaṃ ghrāṇaṃ sparśanaṃ rasanaṃ tathā
|
upapattyā guṇānviddhi pañca pañcasu pañcadhā ||13||

14rūpaṃ gandho rasaḥ sparśaḥ śabdaścaivātha tadguṇāḥ
|
indriyairupalabhyante pañcadhā pañca pañcabhiḥ ||14||

15rūpaṃ gandhaṃ rasaṃ sparśaṃ śabdaṃ caivātha tadguṇān
|
indriyāṇi na budhyante kṣetrajñastaistu budhyate ||15||

16cittamindriyasaṃghātātparaṃ tasmātparaṃ manaḥ
|
manasastu parā buddhiḥ kṣetrajño buddhitaḥ paraḥ ||16||

17pūrvaṃ cetayate janturindriyairviṣayān pṛthak
|
vicārya manasā paścādatha buddhyā vyavasyati
|
indriyairupalabdhārthān buddhimāṃstu vyavasyati ||17||

18cittamindriyasaṃghātaṃ mano buddhistathāṣṭamī
|
aṣṭau jñānendriyāṇyāhuretānyadhyātmacintakāḥ ||18||

19pāṇipādaṃ ca pāyuśca mehanaṃ pañcamaṃ mukham
|
iti saṃśabdyamānāni śṛṇu karmendriyāṇyapi ||19||

20jalpanābhyavahārārthaṃ mukhamindriyamucyate
|
gamanendriyaṃ tathā pādau karmaṇaḥ karaṇe karau ||20||

21pāyūpasthaṃ visargārthamindriye tulyakarmaṇī
|
visarge ca purīṣasya visarge cāpi kāmike ||21||

22balaṃ ṣaṣṭhaṃ ṣaḍetāni vācā samyagyathā mama
|
jñānaceṣṭendriyaguṇāḥ sarveṣāṃ śabditā mayā ||22||

23indriyāṇāṃ svakarmebhyaḥ śramāduparamo yadā
|
bhavatīndriyasantyāgādatha svapiti vai naraḥ ||23||

24indriyāṇāṃ vyuparame mano vyuparataṃ yadi
|
sevate viṣayāneva taṃ vidyātsvapnadarśanam ||24||

25sāttvikāścaiva ye bhāvāstathā tāmasarājasāḥ
|
karmayuktānpraśaṃsanti sāttvikānitarāṃstathā ||25||

26ānandaḥ karmaṇāṃ siddhiḥ pratipattiḥ parāgatiḥ
|
sāttvikasya nimittāni bhāvān saṃśrayate smṛtiḥ ||26||

27jantuṣvekatameṣvevaṃ bhāvā ye vidhimāsthitāḥ
|
bhāvayorīpsitaṃ nityaṃ pratyakṣaṃ gamanaṃ tayoḥ ||27||

28indriyāṇi ca bhāvāśca guṇāḥ saptadaśa smṛtāḥ
|
teṣāmaṣṭādaśo dehī yaḥ śarīre sa śāśvataḥ ||28||

29athavā saśarīrāste guṇāḥ sarve śarīriṇām
|
saṃśritāstadviyoge hi saśarīrā na santi te ||29||

30athavā sannipāto'yaṃ śarīraṃ pāñcabhautikam
|
ekaśca daśa cāṣṭau ca guṇāḥ saha śarīriṇā ||30||

31uṣmaṇā saha viṃśo vā saṃghātaḥ pāñcabhautikaḥ
|
mahānsaṃdhārayatyetaccharīraṃ vāyunā saha
|
tasya prabhāvayuktasya nimittaṃ dehabhedane ||31||

32yathaivotpadyate kiṃcitpañcatvaṃ gacchate tathā
|
puṇyapāpavināśānte puṇyapāpasamīritaḥ
|
dehaṃ viśati kālena tato'yaṃ karmasambhavam ||32||

33hitvā hitvā hyayaṃ praiti dehāddehaṃ kṛtāśrayaḥ
|
kālasañcoditaḥ kṣetrī viśīrṇādvā gṛhādgṛham ||33||

34tatra naivānutapyante prājñā niścitaniścayāḥ
|
kṛpaṇāstvanutapyante janāḥ sambandhadarśinaḥ ||34||

35na hyayaṃ kasyacitkaścinnāsya kaścana vidyate
|
bhavatyeko hyayaṃ nityaṃ śarīrasukhaduḥkhabhāk ||35||

36naiva saṃjāyate janturna ca jātu vipadyate
|
yāti dehamayaṃ muktvā kadācitparamāṃ gatim ||36||

37puṇyapāpamayaṃ dehaṃ kṣapayan karmasaṃkṣayāt
|
kṣīṇadehaḥ punardehī brahmatvamupagacchati ||37||

38puṇyapāpakṣayārthaṃ hi sāṃkhyajñānaṃ vidhīyate
|
tatkṣaye hyasya paśyanti brahmabhāve parāṃ gatim ||38||
