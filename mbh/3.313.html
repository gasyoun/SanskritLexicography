Mahābhārata 3.313 (Āraṇyakaparvan)

1vaiśampāyana uvāca|
sa dadarśa hatān bhrātṝn lokapālāniva cyutān
|
yugānte samanuprāpte śakrapratimagauravān||1||

2vinikīrṇadhanurbāṇaṃ dṛṣṭvā nihatamarjunam
|
bhīmasenaṃ yamau caiva nirviceṣṭān gatāyuṣaḥ||2||

3sa dīrghamuṣṇaṃ niḥśvasya śokabāṣpapariplataḥ
|
tān dṛṣṭvā patitān bhrātṝn sarvāṃścintāsamanvitaḥ||3||

4dharmaputro mahābāhurvilalāpa suvistaram
|
nanu tvayā mahābāho pratijñātaṃ vṛkodara||4||

5suyodhanasya bhetsyāmi gadayā sakthinī raṇe
|
vyarthaṃ tadadya me sarvaṃ tvayi vīre nipātite||5||

6mahātmani mahābāho kurūṇāṃ kīrtivardhane
|
manuṣyasambhavā vāco vidharmiṇyaḥ pratiśrutāḥ||6||

7bhavatāṃ divyavācastu tā bhavantu kathaṃ mṛṣā
|
devāścāpi yadā'vocan sūtake tvāṃ dhanañjaya||7||

8sahasrākṣādanavaraḥ kuntiputrastaveti vai
|
uttare pāriyātre ca jagurbhūtāni sarvaśaḥ||8||

9vipranaṣṭāṃ śriyaṃ caiṣāmāhartā punarañjasā
|
nāsya jetā raṇe kaścidajetā naiṣa kasyacit||9||

10so'yaṃ mṛtyuvaśaṃ yātaḥ kathaṃ jiṣṇurmahābalaḥ
|
ayaṃ mamāśāṃ saṃhatya śete bhūmau dhanañjayaḥ||10||

11āśritya yaṃ vayaṃ nāthaṃ duḥkhānyetāni sehima
|
raṇe pramattau vīrau ca sadā śatrunibarhaṇau||11||

12kathaṃ ripuvaśaṃ yātau kuntīputrau mahābalau
|
yau sarvāstrāpratihatau bhīmasenadhanañjayau||12||

13aśmasāramayaṃ nūnaṃ hṛdayaṃ mama durhṛdaḥ
|
yamau yadetau dṛṣṭvā'dya patitau nāvadīryate||13||

14śāstrajñā deśakālajñāstapoyuktāḥ kriyānvitāḥ
|
akṛtvā sadṛśaṃ karma kiṃ śedhvaṃ puruṣarṣabhāḥ||14||

15avikṣataśarīrāścāpyapramṛṣṭaśarāsanāḥ
|
asaṃjñā bhuvi saṅgamya kiṃ śedhvamaparājitāḥ||15||

16sānūnivādreḥ saṃsuptān dṛṣṭvā bhrātṝn mahāmatiḥ
|
sukhaṃ prasuptān prakhinnaḥ khinnaḥ kaṣṭāṃ daśāṃ gataḥ||16||

17evamevedamityuktvā dharmātmā sa nareśvaraḥ
|
śokasāgaramadhyastho dadhyau kāraṇamākulaḥ||17||

18itikartavyatāṃ ceti deśakālavibhāgavit
|
nābhipede mahābāhuścintayāno mahāmatiḥ||18||

19atha saṃstabhya dharmātmā tadā''tmānaṃ tapaḥ sutaḥ
|
evaṃ vilapya bahudhā dharmaputro yudhiṣṭhiraḥ||19||

20buddhyā vicintayāmāsa vīrāḥ kena nipātitāḥ||20||

21naiṣāṃ śastraprahāro'sti padaṃ nehāsti kasyacit
|
bhūtaṃ mahadidaṃ manye bhrātaro yena me hatāḥ||21||

22ekāgraṃ cintayiṣyāmi pītvā vetsyāmi vā jalam
|
syāttu duryodhanenedamupāṃśu vihitaṃ kṛtam||22||

23gāndhārarājaracitaṃ satataṃ jihmabuddhinā
|
yasya kāryamakāryaṃ vā samameva bhavatyuta||23||

24kastasya viśvasedvīro duṣkṛterakṛtātmanaḥ
|
athavā puruṣairgūḍhaiḥ prayogo'yaṃ durātmanaḥ||24||

25bhavediti mahābuddhirbahudhā tadacintayat
|
tasyāsīnna viṣeṇedamudakaṃ dūṣitaṃ yathā||25||

26mṛtānāmapi caiteṣāṃ vikṛtaṃ naiva jāyate
|
mukhavarṇāḥ prasannā me bhrātṝṇāmityacintayat||26||

27ekaikaśaścaughabalānimān puruṣasattamān
|
ko'nyaḥ pratisamāseta kālāntakayamādṛte||27||

28etena vyavasāyena tat toyaṃ vyavagāḍhavān
|
gāhamānaśca tattoyamantarikṣāt sa śuśruve||28||

29yakṣa uvāca|
ahaṃ bakaḥ śaivalamatsyabhakṣo nītā mayā pretavaśaṃ tavānujāḥ
|
tvaṃ pañcamo bhavitā rājaputra na cet praśnān pṛcchato vyākaroṣi||29||

30mā tāta sāhasaṃ kārṣīrmama pūrvaparigrahaḥ
|
praśnānuktvā tu kaunteya tataḥ piba harasva ca||30||

31yudhiṣṭhira uvāca|
rudrāṇāṃ vā vasūnāṃ vā marutāṃ vā pradhānabhāk
|
pṛcchāmi ko bhavān devo naitacchakuninā kṛtam||31||

32himavān pāriyātraśca vindhyo malaya eva ca
|
catvāraḥ parvatāḥ kena pātitā bhūritejasaḥ||32||

33atīva te mahatkarma kṛtaṃ ca balināṃ vara
|
yān na devā na gandharvā nāsurāśca na rākṣasāḥ||33||

34viṣaheran mahāyuddhe kṛtaṃ te tanmahādbhutam
|
na te jānāmi yat kāryaṃ nābhijānāmi kāṅkṣitam||34||

35kautūhalaṃ mahajjātaṃ sādhvasaṃ cāgataṃ mama
|
yenāsmyudvignahṛdayaḥ samutpannaśirojvaraḥ||35||

36pṛcchāmi bhagavaṃstasmāt ko bhavāniha tiṣṭhati
|
yakṣa uvāca|
yakṣo'hamasmi bhadraṃ te nāsmi pakṣī jalecaraḥ||36||

37mayaite nihatāḥ sarve bhrātaraste mahaujasaḥ
|
vaiśampāyana uvāca|
tatastāmaśivāṃ śrutvā vācaṃ sa paruṣākṣarām||37||

38yakṣasya bruvato rājannupakramya tadā sthitaḥ
|
virūpākṣaṃ mahākāyaṃ yakṣaṃ tālusamucchrayam||38||

39jvalanārkapratīkāśamadhṛṣyaṃ parvatopamam
|
vṛkṣamāśritya tiṣṭhantaṃ dadarśa bharatarṣabhaḥ||39||

40meghagambhīranādena tarjayantaṃ mahāsvanam
|
yakṣa uvāca|
ime te bhrātaro rājan vāryamāṇā mayā'sakṛt||40||

41balāttoyaṃ jihīrṣantastato vai mṛditā mayā
|
na peyamudakaṃ rājan prāṇāniha parīpsatā||41||

42pārtha mā sāhasaṃ kārṣīrmama pūrvaparigrahaḥ
|
praśnānuktvā tu kaunteya tataḥ piba harasva ca||42||

43yudhiṣṭhira uvāca|
na cāhaṃ kāmaye yakṣa tava pūrvaparigraham
|
kāmaṃ naitat praśaṃsanti santo hi puruṣāḥ sadā||43||

44yadātmanā svamātmānaṃ praśaṃse puruṣarṣabha
|
yathāprajñaṃ tu te praśnān prativakṣyāmi pṛccha mām||44||

45yakṣa uvāca|
kiṃ svidādityamunnayati ke ca tasyābhitaścarāḥ
|
kaścainamastaṃ nayati kasmiṃśca pratitiṣṭhati||45||

46yudhiṣṭhira uvāca|
brahmādityamunnayati devāstasyābhitaścarāḥ
|
dharmaścāstaṃ nayati ca satye ca pratitiṣṭhati||46||

47yakṣa uvāca|
kenasvicchrotriyo bhavati kenastvidvindate mahat
|
kenasviddvitīyavān bhavati rājan kena ca buddhimān||47||

48yudhiṣṭhira uvāca|
śrutena śrotriyo bhavati tapasā vindate mahat
|
dhṛtyā dvitīyavān bhavati buddhimān vṛddhasevayā||48||

49yakṣa uvāca|
kiṃ brāhmaṇānāṃ devatvaṃ kaśca dharmaḥ satāmiva
|
kaścaiṣāṃ mānuṣo bhāvaḥ kimeṣāmasatāmiva||49||

50yudhiṣṭhira uvāca|
svādhyāya eṣāṃ devatvaṃ tapa eṣāṃ satāmiva
|
maraṇaṃ mānuṣo bhāvaḥ parivādo'satāmiva||50||

51yakṣa uvāca|
kiṃ kṣatriyāṇāṃ devatvaṃ kaśca dharmaḥ satāmiva
|
kaścaiṣāṃ mānuṣo bhāvaḥ kimeṣāmasatāmiva||51||

52yudhiṣṭhira uvāca|
iṣvastrameṣāṃ devatvaṃ yajña eṣāṃ satāmiva
|
bhayaṃ vai mānuṣo bhāvaḥ parityāgo'satāmiva||52||

53yakṣa uvāca|
kimekaṃ yajñiyaṃ sāma kimekaṃ yajñiyaṃ yajuḥ
|
kā caiṣāṃ vṛṇute yajñaṃ kāṃ yajño nātivartate||53||

54yudhiṣṭhira uvāca|
prāṇo vai yajñiyaṃ sāma mano vai yajñiyaṃ yajuḥ
|
ṛgekā vṛṇute yajñaṃ tāṃ yajño nātivartate||54||

55yakṣa uvāca|
kiṃsvidāvapatāṃ śreṣṭhaṃ kiṃsvinnivapatāṃ varam
|
kiṃsvit pratiṣṭhamānānāṃ kiṃsvit prasavatāṃ varam||55||

56yudhiṣṭhira uvāca|
varṣamāvapatāṃ śreṣṭhaṃ bījaṃ nivapatāṃ varam
|
gāvaḥ pratiṣṭhamānānāṃ putraḥ prasavatāṃ varaḥ||56||

57yakṣa uvāca|
indriyārthānanubhavan buddhimān lokapūjitaḥ
|
saṃmataḥ sarvabhūtānāmucchvasan ko na jīvati||57||

58yudhiṣṭhira uvāca|
devatātithibhṛtyānāṃ pitṝṇāmātmanaśca yaḥ
|
na nirvapati pañcānāmucchvasanna sa jīvati||58||

59yakṣa uvāca|
kiṃsvidgurutaraṃ bhūmeḥ kiṃsviduccataraṃ ca khāt
|
kiṃ svicchīghrataraṃ vāyoḥ kiṃsvidbahutaraṃ tṛṇāt||59||

60yudhiṣṭhira uvāca|
mātā gurutarā bhūmeḥ khāt pitoccatarastathā
|
manaḥ śīghrataraṃ vātāccintā bahutarī tṛṇāt||60||

61yakṣa uvāca|
kiṃsvit suptaṃ na nimiṣati kiṃsvijjātaṃ na copati
|
kasyasviddhṛdayaṃ nāsti kiṃsvidvegena vardhate||61||

62yudhiṣṭhira uvāca|
matsyaḥ supto na nimiṣatyaṇḍaṃ jātaṃ na copati
|
aśmano hṛdayaṃ nāsti nadī vegena vardhate||62||

63yakṣa uvāca|
kiṃsvin pravasato mitraṃ kiṃsvinmitraṃ gṛhe sataḥ
|
āturasya ca kiṃ mitraṃ kiṃsvinmitraṃ mariṣyataḥ||63||

64yudhiṣṭhira uvāca|
sārthaḥ pravasato mitraṃ bhāryā mitraṃ gṛhe sataḥ
|
āturasya bhiṣaṅmitraṃ dānaṃ mitraṃ mariṣyataḥ||64||

65yakṣa uvāca|
ko'tithiḥ sarvabhūtānāṃ kiṃsviddharmaṃ sanātanam
|
amṛtaṃ kiṃsvidrājendra kiṃsvit sarvamidaṃ jagat||65||

66yudhiṣṭhira uvāca|
atithiḥ sarvabhūtānāmagniḥ somo gavāmṛtam
|
sanātano'mṛto dharmo vāyuḥ sarvamidaṃ jagat||66||

67yakṣa uvāca|
kiṃsvideko vicarate jātaḥ ko jāyate punaḥ
|
kiṃsviddhimasya bhaiṣajyaṃ kiṃsvidāvapanaṃ mahat||67||

68yudhiṣṭhira uvāca|
sūrya eko vicarate candamā jāyate punaḥ
|
agnirhimasya bhaiṣajyaṃ bhūmirāvapanaṃ mahat||68||

69yakṣa uvāca|
kiṃsvidekapadaṃ dharmyaṃ kiṃsvidekapadaṃ yaśaḥ
|
kiṃsvidekapadaṃ svargyaṃ kiṃsvidekapadaṃ sukham||69||

70yudhiṣṭhira uvāca|
dākṣyamekapadaṃ dharmyaṃ dānamekapadaṃ yaśaḥ
|
satyamekapadaṃ svargyaṃ śīlamekapadaṃ sukham||70||

71yakṣa uvāca|
kiṃsvidātmā manuṣyasya kiṃsviddaivakṛtaḥ sakhā
|
upajīvanaṃ kiṃsvidasya kiṃsvidasya parāyaṇam||71||

72yudhiṣṭhira uvāca|
putra ātmā manuṣyasya bhāryā daivakṛtaḥ sakhā
|
upajīvanaṃ ca parjanyo dānamasya parāyaṇam||72||

73yakṣa uvāca|
dhanyānāmuttamaṃ kiṃsvid dhanānāṃ syāt kimuttamam
|
lābhānāmuttamaṃ kiṃ syāt sukhānāṃ syātkimuttamam||73||

74yudhiṣṭhira uvāca|
dhanyānāmuttamaṃ dākṣyaṃ dhanānāmuttamaṃ śrutam
|
lābhānāṃ śreya ārogyaṃ sukhānāṃ tuṣṭiruttamā||74||

75yakṣa uvāca|
kaśca dharmaḥ paro loke kaśca dharmaḥ sadāphalaḥ
|
kiṃ niyamya na śocanti kaiśca sandhirna jīryate||75||

76yudhiṣṭhira uvāca|
ānṛśaṃsyaṃ paro dharmastrayī dharmaḥ sadāphalaḥ
|
mano yamya na śocanti sandhiḥ sadbhirna jīryate||76||

77yakṣa uvāca|
kiṃ nu hitvā priyo bhavati kiṃ nu hitvā na śocati
|
kiṃ nu hitvā'rthavān bhavati kiṃ nu hitvā sukhī bhavet||77||

78yudhiṣṭhira uvāca|
mānaṃ hitvā priyo bhavati krodhaṃ hitvā na śocati
|
kāmaṃ hitvā'rthavān bhavati lobhaṃ hitvā sukhī bhavet||78||

79yakṣa uvāca|
kimarthaṃ brāhmaṇe dānaṃ kimarthaṃ naṭanartake
|
kimarthaṃ caiva bhṛtyeṣu kimarthaṃ caiva rājasu||79||

80yudhiṣṭhira uvāca|
dharmārthaṃ brāhmaṇe dānaṃ yaśorthaṃ naṭanartake
|
bhṛtyeṣu bharaṇārthaṃ vai bhayārthaṃ caiva rājasu||80||

81yakṣa uvāca|
kena svidāvṛto lokaḥ kena svinna prakāśate
|
kena tyajati mitrāṇi kena svargaṃ na gacchati||81||

82yudhiṣṭhira uvāca|
ajñānenāvṛto lokastamasā na prakāśate
|
lobhāttyajati mitrāṇi saṅgāt svargaṃ na gacchati||82||

83yakṣa uvāca|
mṛtaḥ kathaṃ syāt puruṣaḥ kathaṃ rāṣṭraṃ mṛtaṃ bhavet
|
śrāddhaṃ mṛtaṃ kathaṃ vā syāt kathaṃ yajño mṛto bhavet||83||

84yudhiṣṭhira uvāca|
mṛto daridraḥ puruṣo mṛtaṃ rāṣṭramarājakam
|
mṛtamaśrotriyaṃ śrāddhaṃ mṛto yajñastvadakṣiṇaḥ||84||

85yakṣa uvāca|
kā dikkimudakaṃ proktaṃ kimannaṃ kiṃca vai viṣam
|
śrāddhasya kālamākhyāhi tataḥ piba harasva ca||85||

86yudhiṣṭhira uvāca|
santo digjalamākāśaṃ gaurannaṃ prārthanā viṣam
|
śrāddhasya brāhmaṇaḥ kālaḥ kathaṃ vā yakṣa manyase||86||

87yakṣa uvāca|
tapaḥ kiṃlakṣaṇaṃ proktaṃ ko damaśca prakīrtitaḥ
|
kṣamā ca kā parā proktā kā ca hrīḥ parikīrtitā||87||

88yudhiṣṭhira uvāca|
tapaḥ svadharmavartitvaṃ manaso damanaṃ damaḥ
|
kṣamā dvandvasahiṣṇutvaṃ hrīrakāryanivartanam||88||

89yakṣa uvāca|
kiṃ jñānaṃ procyate rājan kaḥ śamaśca prakīrtitaḥ
|
dayā ca kā parā proktā kiñcārjamudāhṛtam||89||

90yudhiṣṭhira uvāca|
jñānaṃ tattvārthasambodhaḥ śamaścittapraśāntatā
|
dayā sarvasukhaiṣitvamārjavaṃ samacittatā||90||

91yakṣa uvāca|
kaḥ śatrurdurjayaḥ puṃsāṃ kaśca vyādhiranantakaḥ
|
kīdṛśaśca smṛtaḥ sādhurasādhuḥ kīdṛśaḥ smṛtaḥ||91||

92yudhiṣṭhira uvāca|
krodhaḥ sudurjayaḥ śatrurlobho vyādhiranantakaḥ
|
sarvabhūtahitaḥ sādhurasādhurnirdayaḥ smṛtaḥ||92||

93yakṣa uvāca|
ko mohaḥ procyate rājan kaśca mānaḥ prakīrtitaḥ
|
kimālasyaṃ ca vijñeyaṃ kaśca śokaḥ prakīrtitaḥ||93||

94yudhiṣṭhira uvāca|
moho hi dharmamūḍhatvaṃ mānastvātmābhimānitā
|
dharmaniṣkriyatā''lasyaṃ śokastvajñānamucyate||94||

95yakṣa uvāca|
kiṃ sthairyamṛṣibhiḥ proktaṃ kiṃ ca dhairyamudāhṛtam
|
snānaṃ ca kiṃ paraṃ proktaṃ dānaṃ ca kimihocyate||95||

96yudhiṣṭhira uvāca|
svadharme sthiratā sthairyaṃ dhairyamindriyanigrahaḥ
|
snānaṃ manomalatyāgo dānaṃ vai bhūtarakṣaṇam||96||

97yakṣa uvāca|
kaḥ paṇḍitaḥ pumān jñeyo nāstikaḥ kaśca ucyate
|
ko mūrkhaḥ kaśca kāmaḥ syāt ko matsara iti smṛtaḥ||97||

98yudhiṣṭhira uvāca|
dharmajñaḥ paṇḍito jñeyo nāstiko mūrkha ucyate
|
kāmaḥ saṃsārahetuśca hṛttāpo matsaraḥ smṛtaḥ||98||

99yakṣa uvāca|
ko'haṅkāra iti proktaḥ kaśca dambhaḥ prakīrtitaḥ
|
kiṃ taddaivaṃ paraṃ proktaṃ kiṃ tatpaiśunyamucyate||99||

100yudhiṣṭhira uvāca|
mahājñānamahaṅkāro dambho dharmo dhvajocchrayaḥ
|
daivaṃ dānaphalaṃ proktaṃ paiśunyaṃ paradūṣaṇam||100||

101yakṣa uvāca|
dharmaścārthaśca kāmaśca parasparavirodhinaḥ
|
eṣāṃ nityaviruddhānāṃ kathamekatra saṅgamaḥ||101||

102yudhiṣṭhira uvāca|
yadā dharmaśca bhāryā ca parasparavaśānugau
|
tadā dharmārthakāmānāṃ trayāṇāmapi saṅgamaḥ||102||

103yakṣa uvāca|
akṣayo narakaḥ kena prāpyate bharatarṣabha
|
etanme pṛcchataḥ praśnaṃ tacchīghraṃ vaktumarhasi||103||

104yudhiṣṭhira uvāca|
brāhmaṇaṃ svayamāhūya yācamānamakiñcanam
|
paścānnāstīti yo brūyāt so'kṣayaṃ narakaṃ vrajet||104||

105vedeṣu dharmaśāstreṣu mithyā yo vai dvijātiṣu
|
deveṣu pitṛdharmeṣu so'kṣayaṃ narakaṃ vrajet||105||

106vidyamāne dhane lobhāddānabhogavivarjitaḥ
|
paścānnāstīti yo brūyāt so'kṣayaṃ narakaṃ vrajet||106||

107yakṣa uvāca|
rājan kulena vṛttena svādhyāyena śrutena vā
|
brāhmaṇyaṃ kena bhavati prabrūhyetat suniścitam||107||

108yudhiṣṭhira uvāca|
śṛṇu yakṣa kulaṃ tāta na svādhyāyo na ca śrutam
|
kāraṇaṃ hi dvijatve ca vṛttameva na saṃśayaḥ||108||

109vṛttaṃ yatnena saṃrakṣyaṃ brāhmaṇena viśeṣataḥ
|
akṣīṇavṛtto na kṣīṇo vṛttatastu hato hataḥ||109||

110paṭhakāḥ pāṭhakāścaiva ye cānye śāstracintakāḥ
|
sarve vyasanino mūrkhā yaḥ kriyāvān sa paṇḍitaḥ||110||

111caturvedo'pi durvṛttaḥ sa śūdrādatiricyate
|
yo'gnihotraparo dāntaḥ sa brāhmaṇa iti smṛtaḥ||111||

112yakṣa uvāca|
priyavacanavādī kiṃ labhate vimṛśitakāryakaraḥ kiṃ labhate
|
bahumitrakaraḥ kiṃ labhate dharme rataḥ kiṃ labhate kathaya||112||

113yudhiṣṭhira uvāca|
priyavacanavādī priyo bhavati vimṛśitakāryakaro'dhikaṃ jayati
|
bahumitrakaraḥ sukhaṃ vasate yaśca dharmarataḥ sa gatiṃ labhate||113||

114yakṣa uvāca|
ko modate kimāścaryaṃ kaḥ panthāḥ kā ca vārtikā
|
vada me caturaḥ praśnān mṛtā jīvantu bāndhavāḥ||114||

115yudhiṣṭhira uvāca|
pañcame'hani ṣaṣṭhe vā śākaṃ pacati sve gṛhe
|
anṛṇī cāpravāsī ca sa vāricara modate||115||

116ahanyahani bhūtāni gacchantīha yamālayam
|
śeṣāḥ sthāvaramicchanti kimāścaryamataḥ param||116||

117tarko'pratiṣṭhaḥ śrutayo vibhinnā naiko ṛṣiryasya mataṃ pramāṇam
|
dharmasya tattvaṃ nihitaṃ guhāyāṃ mahājano yena gataḥ sa panthāḥ||117||

118asmin mahāmohamaye kaṭāhe sūryāgninā rātridivendhanena
|
māsartudarvīparighaṭṭanena bhūtāni kālaḥ pacatīti vārtā||118||

119yakṣa uvāca|
vyākhyātā me tvayā praśnā yāthātathyaṃ parantapa
|
puruṣaṃ tvidānīṃ vyākhyāhi yaśca sarvadhanī naraḥ||119||

120yudhiṣṭhira uvāca|
divaṃ spṛśati bhūmiṃ ca śabdaḥ puṇyena karmaṇā
|
yāvatsa śabdo bhavati tāvat puruṣa ucyate||120||

121tulye priyāpriye yasya sukhaduḥkhe tathaiva ca
|
atītānāgate cobhe sa vai sarvadhanī naraḥ||121||

122yakṣa uvāca|
vyākhyātaḥ puruṣo rājan yaśca sarvadhanī naraḥ
|
tasmāt tvamekaṃ bhrātṝṇāṃ yamicchasi sa jīvatu||122||

123yudhiṣṭhira uvāca|
śyāmo ya eṣa raktākṣo bṛhacchāla ivotthitaḥ
|
vyūḍhorasko mahābāhurnakulo yakṣa jīvatu||123||

124yakṣa uvāca|
priyaste bhīmaseno'yamarjuno vaḥ parāyaṇam
|
sa kasmānnakulo rājan sāpatnaṃ jīvamicchasi||124||

125yasya nāgasahastreṇa daśasaṅkhyena vai balam
|
tulyaṃ taṃ bhīmamutsṛjya nakulaṃ jīvamicchasi||125||

126tathainaṃ manujāḥ prāhurbhīmasenaṃ priyaṃ tava
|
atha kenānubhāvena sāpatnaṃ jīvamicchasi||126||

127yasya bāhubalaṃ sarve pāṇḍavāḥ samupāsate
|
arjunaṃ tamapāhāya nakulaṃ jīvamicchasi||127||

128yudhiṣṭhira uvāca|
dharma eva hato hanti dharmo rakṣati rakṣitaḥ
|
tasmāddharmaṃ na tyajāmi mā no dharmo hato'vadhīt||128||

129ānṛśaṃsyaṃ paro dharmaḥ paramārthācca me matam
|
ānṛśaṃsyaṃ cikīrṣāmi nakulo yakṣa jīvatu||129||

130dharmaśīlaḥ sadā rājā iti māṃ mānavā viduḥ
|
svadharmānna caliṣyāmi nakulo yakṣa jīvatu||130||

131kuntī caiva tu mādrī ca dve bhārye tu piturmama
|
ubhe saputre syātāṃ vai iti me dhīyane matiḥ||131||

132yathā kuntī tathā mādrī viśeṣo nāsti me tayoḥ
|
mātṛbhyāṃ samamicchāmi nakulo yakṣa jīvatu||132||

133yakṣa uvāca|
tasya te'rthācca kāmācca ānṛśaṃsyaṃ paraṃ matam
|
tasmātte bhrātaraḥ sarve jīvantu bharatarṣabha||133||
