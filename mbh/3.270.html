Mahābhārata 3.270 (Āraṇyakaparvan)

1vaiśampāyana uvāca|
tato ghorataraḥ śabdo vane samabhavat tadā
|
bhīmasenārjunau dṛṣṭvā kṣatriyāṇāmamarṣiṇām||1||

2teṣāṃ dhvajāgrāṇyabhivīkṣya rājā svayaṃ durātmā narapuṅgavānām
|
jayadratho yājñasenīmuvāca rathe sthitāṃ bhānumatīṃ hataujāḥ||2||

3āyāntīme pañca rathā mahānto manye ca kṛṣṇe patayastavete
|
sā jānatī khyāpaya naḥ sukeśi paraṃ paraṃ pāṇḍavānāṃ rathastham||3||

4draupadyuvāca
|
ki te jñātairmūḍha mahādhanurdharairanāyuṣyaṃ karma kṛtvā'tighoram
|
ete vīrāḥ patayo me sametā na vaḥ śeṣaḥ kaścidihāsti yuddhe||4||

5ākhyātavyaṃ tveva sarvaṃ mumūrṣormayā tubhyaṃ pṛṣṭayā dharma eṣaḥ
|
na me vyathā vidyate tvadbhayaṃ vā sampaśyantyāḥ sānujaṃ dharmarājam||5||

6yasya dhvajāgre nadato mṛdaṅgau nandopanandau madhurau yuktarūpau
|
etaṃ svadharmārthaviniścayajñaṃ sadā janāḥ kṛtyavanto'nuyānti||6||

7ya eṣa jāmbūnadaśuddhagauraḥ pracaṇḍaghoṇastanurāyatākṣaḥ
|
etaṃ kuruśreṣṭhatamaṃ vadanti yudhiṣṭhiraṃ dharmasutaṃ patiṃ me||7||

8apyeṣa śatroḥ śaraṇāgatasya dadyāt prāṇān dharmacārī nṛvīraḥ
|
parehyenaṃ mūḍha javena bhūtaye tvamātmanaḥ prāñjalirnyastaśastraḥ||8||

9athāpyenaṃ paśyāsa yaṃ rathasthaṃ mahābhujaṃ śālamiva pravṛddham
|
sandaṣṭauṣṭhaṃ bhṛkuṭīsaṃhatabhruvaṃ vṛkodaro nāma patirmamaiṣaḥ||9||

10ājāneyā balinaḥ sādhu dāntā mahābalāḥ śūramudāvahanti
|
etasya karmāṇyatimānuṣāṇi bhīmeti śabdo'sya gataḥ pṛthivyām||10||

11nāsyāparāddhāḥ śeṣamavāpnuvanti nāyaṃ vairaṃ vismarate kadācit
|
vairasyāntaṃ saṃvidhāyopayāti paścācchāntiṃ na ca gacchatyatīva||11||

12dhanurdharāgryo dhṛtimān yaśasvī jitendriyo vṛddhasevī nṛvīraḥ
|
bhrātā ca śiṣyaśca yudhiṣṭhirasya dhanañjayo nāma patirmamaiṣaḥ||12||

13yo vai na kāmānna bhayānna lobhāt tyajeddharmaṃ na nṛśaṃsaṃ ca kuryāt
|
sa eṣa vaiśvānaratulyatejāḥ kuntīsutaḥ śatrusahaḥ pramāthī||13||

14yaḥ savadharmārthaviniścayajño bhayārtānāṃ bhayahartā manīṣī
|
yasyottamaṃ rūpamāhuḥ pṛthivyāṃ yaṃ pāṇḍavāḥ parirakṣanti sarve||14||

15prāṇairgarīyāṃsamanuvrataṃ vai sa eṣa vīro nakulaḥ patirme
|
yaḥ khaḍgayodhī laghucitrahasto mahāṃśca dhīmān sahadevo'dvitīyaḥ||15||

16yasyādya karma drakṣyase mūḍhasattva śatakratorvā daityasenāsu saṅkhye
|
śūraḥ kṛtāstro matimān manasvī priyaṅkaro dharmasutasya rājñaḥ||16||

17ya eṣa candrārkasamānatejā jaghanyajaḥ pāṇḍavānāṃ priyaśca
|
buddhyā samo yasya naro na vidyate vaktā tathā satsu viniścayajñaḥ||17||

18sa eṣa śūro nityamamarṣaṇaśca dhīmān prājñaḥ sahadevaḥ patirme
|
tyajet prāṇān praviśeddhavyavāhaṃ na tvevaiṣa vyāhareddharmabāhyam||18||

19sadā manasvī kṣatradharme rataśca kuntyāḥ prāṇairiṣṭatamo nṛvīraḥ
|
viśīryantīṃ nāvamivārṇavānte ratnābhipūrṇāṃ makarasya pṛṣṭhe||19||

20senāṃ tavemāṃ hatasarvayodhāṃ vikṣobhitāṃ drakṣyasi pāṇḍuputraiḥ
|
ityete vai kathitāḥ pāṇḍuputrā yāṃstvaṃ mohādavamanya pravṛttaḥ
|
yedyetebhyo mucyase'riṣṭadehaḥ punarjanma prāpsyase jīva eva||20||

21vaiśampāyana uvāca|
tataḥ pārthāḥ pañca pañcendrakalpāstyaktvā trastān prāñjalīṃstān padātīn
|
yathā'nīkaṃ śaravarṣāndhakāraṃ cakruḥ kruddhāḥ sarvataḥ sannigṛhya||21||
