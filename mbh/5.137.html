Mahābhārata 5.137 (Udyogaparvan)

1kuntyuvāca
|
arjunaṃ keśava brūyāstvayi jāte sma sūtake
|
upopaviṣṭā nārībhirāśrame parivāritā ||1||

2athāntarikṣe vāgāsīddivyarūpā manoramā
|
sahastrākṣasamaḥ kunti bhaviṣyatyeṣa te sutaḥ ||2||

3eṣa jeṣyati saṅgrāme kurun sarvān samāgatān
|
bhīmasenadvitīyaśca lokamudvartayiṣyati ||3||

4putraste pṛthivīṃ jetā yaśaścāsya divaṃ spṛśet
|
hatyā kurūṃśca saṅgrāme vāsudevasahāyavān ||4||

5pitryamaṃśaṃ pranaṣṭaṃ ca punarapyuddhariṣyati
|
bhrātṛbhiḥ sahitaḥ śrīmāṃstrīn medhānāhariṣyati ||5||

6sa satyasandho bībhatsuḥ savyasācī yathā'cyuta
|
tathā tvameva jānāsi balavantaṃ durāsadam ||6||

7tathā tadastu dāśārha yathā vāgabhyabhāṣata
|
dharmaścedasti vārṣṇeya tathā satyaṃ bhaviṣyati ||7||

8tvaṃ cāpi tattathā kṛṣṇa sarvaṃ sampādayiṣyasi
|
nāhaṃ tadabhyasūyāmi yathā vāgabhyabhāṣata ||8||

9namo dharmāya mahate dharmo dhārayati prajāḥ
|
etaddhanañjayo vācyo nityodyukto vṛkodaraḥ ||9||

10yadarthaṃ kṣatriyā sūte tasya kālo'yamāgataḥ
|
na hi vairaṃ samāsādya sīdanti puruṣarṣabhāḥ ||10||

11viditā te sadā buddhirbhīmasya na sa śāmyati
|
yāvadantaṃ na kurute śatrūṇāṃ śatrukarśana ||11||

12sarvadharmaviśeṣajñāṃ snuṣāṃ pāṇḍormahātmanaḥ
|
brūyā mādhava kalyāṇīṃ kṛṣṇa kṛṣṇāṃ yaśasvinīm ||12||

13yuktametanmahābhāge kule jāte yaśasvini
|
yanme putreṣu sarveṣu yathāvatvamavartithāḥ ||13||

14mādrīputrau ca vaktavyau kṣatradharmaratāvubhau
|
vikrameṇārjitān bhogān vṛṇītaṃ jīvitādapi ||14||

15vikramādhigatā hyarthāḥ kṣatradharmeṇa jīvataḥ
|
mano manuṣyasya sadā prīṇanti puruṣottama ||15||

16yacca vaḥ prekṣamāṇānāṃ sarvadharmopacāyinām
|
pāñcālī paruṣāṇyuktā ko nu tat kṣantumarhati ||16||

17na rājyaharaṇaṃ duḥkhaṃ dyūte cāpi parājayaḥ
|
pravrājanaṃ sutānāṃ vā na me tadduḥkhakāraṇam ||17||

18yatra sā bṛhatī śyāmā sabhāyāṃ rudatī tadā
|
aśrauṣīt paruṣā vācastanme duḥkhataraṃ mahat ||18||

19strīdharmiṇī varārohā kṣatradharmaratā sadā
|
nādhyagacchattadā nāthaṃ kṛṣṇā nāthavatī satī ||19||

20taṃ vai brūhi mahābāho sarvaśastrabhṛtāṃ varam
|
arjunaṃ puruṣavyāghraṃ draupadyāḥ padavīṃ cara ||20||

21viditaṃ hi tavātyantaṃ kruddhāviva yamāntakau
|
bhīmārjunau nayetāṃ hi devānapi parāṃ gatim ||21||

22tayoścaitadavajñānaṃ yatsā kṛṣṇā sabhāgatā
|
duḥśāsanaśca yadbhīmaṃ kaṭukānyabhyabhāṣata ||22||

23paśyatāṃ kuruvīrāṇāṃ tacca saṃsmārayeḥ punaḥ
|
pāṇḍavān kuśalaṃ pṛccheḥ saputrān kṛṣṇayā saha ||23||

24māṃ ca kuśalinīṃ brūyāsteṣu bhūyo janārdana
|
ariṣṭaṃ gaccha panthānaṃ putrān me pratipālaya ||24||

25vaiśampāyana uvāca|
abhivādyātha tāṃ kṛṣṇaḥ kṛtvā cāpi pradakṣiṇam
|
niścakrāma mahābāhuḥ siṃhakhelagatistataḥ ||25||

26tato visarjayāmāsa bhīṣmādīn kurupuṅgavān
|
āropyātha rathe karṇaṃ prāyāt sātyakinā saha ||26||

27tataḥ prayāte dāśārhe kuravaḥ saṃgatā mithaḥ
|
jajalpurmahadāścaryaṃ keśave paramādbhutam ||27||

28pramūḍhā pṛthiyī sarvā mṛtyupāśavaśīkṛtā
|
duryodhanasya bāliśyānnaitadastīti cābruvan ||28||

29tato niryāya nagarāt prayayau puruṣottamaḥ
|
mantrayāmāsa ca tadā karṇena suciraṃ saha ||29||

30visarjayitvā rādheyaṃ sarvayādavanandanaḥ
|
tato javena mahatā tūrṇamaśvānacodayat ||30||

31te pibanta ivākāśaṃ dārukeṇa pracoditāḥ
|
hayā jagmurmahāvegā manomārutaraṃhasaḥ ||31||

32te vyatītya mahādhvānaṃ kṣipraṃ śyenā ivāśugāḥ
|
ucairjagmurupaplavyaṃ śārṅgadhanvānamāvahan ||32||
