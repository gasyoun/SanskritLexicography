Mahābhārata 14.34 (Āśvamedhikaparvan)

1brāhmaṇyuvāca|
nedamalpātmanā śakyaṃ vedituṃ nākṛtātmanā
|
bahu cālpaṃ ca saṃkṣiptaṃ viplutaṃ ca mataṃ mama||1||

2upāyaṃ taṃ mama brūhi yenaiṣā labhyate matiḥ
|
tanmanye kāraṇaṃ tvatto yata eṣā pravartate||2||

3brāhmaṇa uvāca|
araṇīṃ brāhmaṇīṃ viddhi gururasyottarāraṇiḥ
|
tapa:śrute'bhimanthīto jñānāgnirjāyate tataḥ||3||

4brāhmaṇyuvāca|
yadidaṃ brāhmaṇo liṅgaṃ kṣetrajña iti saṃjñitam
|
grahītuṃ yena yacchakyaṃ lakṣaṇaṃ tasya tat kva nu||4||

5brāhmaṇa uvāca
aliṅgo nirguṇaścaiva kāraṇaṃ nāsya lakṣyate
|
upāyameva vakṣyāmi yena gṛhyeta vā na vā||5||

6samyagupāyo dṛṣṭaśca bhramarairiva lakṣyate
|
karmabuddhirabuddhitvājjñānaliṅgairivāśritam||6||

7idaṃ kāryamidaṃ neti na mokṣeṣūpadiśyate
|
paśyataḥ śṛṇvato buddhirātmano yeṣu jāyate||7||

8yāvanta iha śakyeraṃstāvantoṃśān prakalpayet
|
avyaktān vyaktarūpāṃśca śataśo'tha sahasraśaḥ||8||

9sarvānnānārthayuktāṃśca sarvānpratyakṣahetukān
|
yataḥ paraṃ na vidyeta tato'bhyāse bhaviṣyati||9||

10śrībhagavānuvāca|
tatastu tasyā brāhmaṇyā matiḥ kṣetrajñasaṃkṣaye
|
kṣetrajñānena parataḥ kṣetrajñebhyaḥ pravartate||10||

11arjuna uvāca|
kva nu sā brāhmaṇī kṛṣṇa kva cāsau brāhmaṇarṣabhaḥ
|
yābhyāṃ siddhiriyaṃ prāptā tāvubhau vada me'cyuta||11||

12śrībhagavānuvāca|
mano me brāhmaṇaṃ viddhi buddhiṃ me viddhi brāhmaṇīm
|
kṣetraśra iti yaścoktaḥ so'hameva dhanañjaya||12||
