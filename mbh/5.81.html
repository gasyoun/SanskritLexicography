Mahābhārata 5.81 (Udyogaparvan)

1sahadeva uvāca|
yadetatkathitaṃ rājñā dharma eṣa sanātanaḥ
|
yathā ca yuddhameva syāttathā kāryamarindama ||1||

2yadi praśamamiccheyuḥ kuravaḥ pāṇḍavaiḥ saha
|
tathā'pi yuddhaṃ dāśārha yojayethāḥ sahaiva taiḥ ||2||

3kathaṃ nu dṛṣṭvā pāñcālīṃ tathā kṛṣṇa sabhāgatām
|
avadhena praśāmyeta mama manyuḥ suyodhane ||3||

4yadi bhīmārjunau kṛṣṇa dharmarājaśca dhārmikaḥ
|
dharmamutsṛjya tenāhaṃ yoddhamicchāmi saṃyuge ||4||

5sātyakiruvāca| satyamāha mahābāho sahadevo mahāmatiḥ
|
duryodhanavadhe śāntistasya kopasya me bhavet ||5||

6na jānāsi yathā dṛṣṭvā cīrājinadharān vane
|
tavāpi manyurudbhūto duḥkhitān prekṣya pāṇḍavān ||6||

7tasmānmādrīsutaḥ śūro yadāha raṇakarkaśaḥ
|
vacanaṃ sarvayodhānāṃ tanmataṃ puruṣottama ||7||

8vaiśampāyana uvāca|
evaṃ vadati vākyaṃ tu yuyudhāne mahāmatau
|
subhīmaḥ siṃhanādo'bhūdyodhānāṃ tatra sarvaśaḥ ||8||

9sarve hi sarvaśo vīrāstadvacaḥ pratyapūjayan
|
sādhu sādhviti śaineyaṃ harṣayanto yuyutsavaḥ ||9||
