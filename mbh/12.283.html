Mahābhārata 12.283 (Śāntiparvan)

1yudhiṣṭhira uvāca|
pitāmaha mahāprājña sarvaśāstraviśārada
|
asminvṛtravadhe deva vivakṣā mama jāyate ||1||

2jvareṇa mohito vṛtraḥ kathitaste janādhipa
|
nihato vāsaveneha vajreṇeti tadā'nagha ||2||

3kathameṣa mahāprājña jvaraḥ prādurbabhau kutaḥ
|
jvarotpattiṃ nipuṇataḥ śrotumicchāmyahaṃ prabho ||3||

4bhīṣma uvāca|
śṛṇu rājan jvarasyemaṃ sambhavaṃ lokaviśrutam
|
vistaraṃ cāsya vakṣyāmi yādṛśaścaiva bhārata ||4||

5purā merormahārāja śṛṅgaṃ trailokyapūjitam
|
jyotiṣkaṃ nāma sāvitraṃ sarvaratnavibhūṣitam ||5||

6aprameyamanādhṛṣyaṃ sarvalokeṣu bhārata
|
tatra devo giritaṭe hemadhātuvibhūṣite ||6||

7paryaṅka iva vibhrājannupaviṣṭo babhūva ha
|
śailarājasutā cāsya nityaṃ pārśve sthitā babhau ||7||

8tathā devā mahātmāno vasavaścāmitaujasaḥ
|
tathaiva ca mahātmānāvaśvinau bhiṣajāṃ varau
|
tathā vaiśravaṇo rājā guhyakairabhisaṃvṛtaḥ ||8||

9yakṣāṇāmīśvaraḥ śrīmānkailāsanilayaḥ prabhuḥ
|
upāsanta mahātmānamuśanā ca mahāmuniḥ ||9||

10sanatkumārapramukhāstathaiva ca maharṣayaḥ
|
aṅgiraḥpramukhāścaiva tathā devarṣayo'pare ||10||

11viśvāvasuśca gandharvastathā nāradaparvatau
|
apsarogaṇasaṃghāśca samājagmuranekaśaḥ ||11||

12vavau sukhaḥ śivo vāyurnānāgandhavahaḥ śuciḥ
|
sarvartukusumopetāḥ puṣpavanto drumāstathā ||12||

13tathā vidyādharāścaiva siddhāścaiva tapodhanāḥ
|
mahādevaṃ paśupatiṃ paryupāsanta bhārata ||13||

14bhūtāni ca mahārāja nānārūpadharāṇyatha
|
rākṣasāśca mahāraudrāḥ piśācāśca mahābalāḥ ||14||

15bahurūpadharā hṛṣṭā nānāpraharaṇodyatāḥ
|
devasyānucarāstatra tasthire cānalopamāḥ ||15||

16nandī ca bhagavāṃstatra devasyānumate sthitaḥ
|
pragṛhya jvalitaṃ śūlaṃ dīpyamānaḥ svatejasā ||16||

17gaṅgā ca saritāṃ śreṣṭhā sarvatīrthajalodbhavā
|
paryupāsata taṃ devaṃ rūpiṇī kurunandana ||17||

18sa evaṃ bhagavāṃstatra pūjyamānaḥ surarṣibhiḥ
|
devaiśca sumahātejā mahādevo vyatiṣṭhata ||18||

19kasyacittvatha kālasya dakṣo nāma prajāpatiḥ
|
pūrvoktena vidhānena yakṣyamāṇo'nvapadyata ||19||

20tatastasya makhaṃ devāḥ sarve śakrapurogamāḥ
|
gamanāya samāgamya buddhimāpedire tadā ||20||

21te vimānairmahātmāno jvalanārkasamaprabhaiḥ
|
devasyānumate'gacchangaṅgādvāramiti śrutiḥ ||21||

22prasthitā devatā dṛṣṭvā śailarājasutā tadā
|
uvāca vacanaṃ sādhvī devaṃ paśupatiṃ patim ||22||

23bhagavankva nu yāntyete devāḥ śakrapurogamāḥ
|
brūhi tattvena tattvajña saṃśayo me mahānayam ||23||

24maheśvara uvāca |
dakṣo nāma mahābhāge prajānāṃ patiruttamaḥ
|
hayamedhena yajate tatra yānti divaukasaḥ ||24||

25umovāca |
yajñametaṃ mahādeva kimarthaṃ nādhigacchasi
|
kena vā pratiṣedhena gamanaṃ te na vidyate ||25||

26maheśvara uvāca |
suraireva mahābhāge pūrvametadanuṣṭhitam
|
yajñeṣu sarveṣu mama na bhāga upakalpitaḥ ||26||

27pūrvopāyopapannena mārgeṇa varavarṇini
|
na me surāḥ prayacchanti bhāgaṃ yajñasya dharmataḥ ||27||

28umovāca |
bhagavansarvabhūteṣu prabhāvābhyadhiko guṇaiḥ
|
ajayyaścāpyadhṛṣyaśca tejasā yaśasā śriyā ||28||

29anena te mahābhāga pratiṣedhena bhāgataḥ
|
atīva duḥkhamutpannaṃ vepathuśca mamānagha ||29||

30bhīṣma uvāca|
evamuktvā tu sā devī tadā paśupatiṃ patim
|
tūṣṇīṃ bhūtā'bhavadrājandahyamānena cetasā ||30||

31atha devyā mataṃ jñātvā hṛdgataṃ yaccikīrṣitam
|
sa samājñāpayāmāsa tiṣṭha tvamiti nandinam ||31||

32tato yogabalaṃ kṛtvā sarvayogeśvareśvaraḥ
|
taṃ yajñaṃ sa mahātejā bhīmairanucaraistadā ||32||

33sahasā ghātayāmāsa devadevaḥ pinākadhūk
|
kecinnādānamuñcanta keciddhāsāṃśca cakrire ||33||

34rudhireṇāpare rājaṃstatrāgniṃ samavākiran
|
kecidyūpānsamutpāṭya babhramurvikṛtānanāḥ ||34||

35āsyairanye cāgrasanta tathaiva paricārakān
|
tataḥ sa yajño nṛpate vadhyamānaḥ samantataḥ ||35||

36āsthāya mṛgarūpaṃ vai khamevābhyagamattadā
|
taṃ tu yajñaṃ tathārūpaṃ gacchantamupalabhya saḥ ||36||

37dhanurādāya bāṇena tadā'nvasarata prabhuḥ
|
tatastasya sureśasya krodhādamitatejasaḥ ||37||

38lalāṭātprasṛto ghoraḥ svedabindurbabhūva ha
|
tasminpatitamātre ca svedabindau tadā bhuvi ||38||

39prādurbabhūva sumahānagniḥ kālānalopamaḥ
|
tatra cājāyata tadā puruṣaḥ puruṣarṣabha ||39||

40hrasvo'timātraṃ raktākṣo hariśmaśrurvibhīṣaṇaḥ
|
urdhvakeśo'tiromāṅga śyenolūkastathaiva ca ||40||

41karālakṛṣṇavarṇaśca raktavāsāstathaiva ca
|
taṃ yajñaṃ sumahāsattvo'dahatkakṣamivānalaḥ ||41||

42vyacaratsarvato devānprādravatsa ṛṣīṃstathā
|
devāścāpyādravansarve tato bhītā diśo daśa ||42||

43tena tasminvicaratā puruṣeṇa viśāmpate
|
pṛthivī hyacaladrājannatīva bharatarṣabha ||43||

44hāhābhūtaṃ jagatsarvamupalakṣya tadā prabhuḥ
|
pitāmaho mahādevaṃ darśayanpratyabhāṣata ||44||

45brahmovāca|
bhavato'pi surāḥ sarve bhāgaṃ dāsyanti vai prabho
|
kriyatāṃ pratisaṃhāraḥ sarvadeveśvara tvayā ||45||

46imā hi devatāḥ sarvā ṛṣayaśca paraṃtapa
|
tava krodhānmahādeva na śāntimupalebhire ||46||

47yaścaiṣa puruṣo jātaḥ svedātte vibudhottama
|
jvaro nāmaiṣa dharmajña lokeṣu pracariṣyati ||47||

48ekībhūtasya na tvasya dhāraṇe tejasaḥ prabho
|
samarthā sakalā pṛthvī bahudhā sṛjyatāmayam ||48||

49ityukto brahmaṇā devo bhāge cāpi prakalpite
|
bhagavantaṃ tathetyāha brahmāṇamamitaujasam ||49||

50parāṃ ca prītimagamadutsmayaṃśca pinākadhṛk
|
avāpa ca tadā bhāgaṃ yathoktaṃ brahmaṇā bhavaḥ ||50||

51jvaraṃ ca sarvadharmajño bahudhā vyasṛjattadā
|
śāntyarthaṃ sarvabhūtānāṃ śṛṇu taccāpi putraka ||51||

52śīrṣābhitāpo nāgānāṃ parvatānāṃ śilājatu
|
apāṃ tu nīlikāṃ vidyānnirmokaṃ bhujageṣu ca ||52||

53khorakaḥ saurabheyāṇāmūṣaraṃ pṛthivītale
|
paśūnāmapi dharmajña dṛṣṭipratyavarodhanam ||53||

54randhrāgatamathāśvānāṃ śikhodbhedaśca barhiṇām
|
netrarogaḥ kokilasya jvaraḥ prokto mahātmanā ||54||

55avīnāṃ pittabhedaśca sarveṣāmiti naḥ śrutam
|
śukānāmapi sarveṣāṃ hikkikā procyate jvaraḥ ||55||

56śārdūleṣvatha dharmajña śramo jvara ihocyate
|
mānuṣeṣu tu dharmajña jvaro nāmaiṣa bhārata ||56||

57maraṇe janmani tathā madhye cāviśate naram
|
etanmāheśvaraṃ tejo jvaro nāma sudāruṇaḥ ||57||

58namasyaścaiva mānyaśca sarvaprāṇibhirīśvaraḥ
|
anena hi samāviṣṭo vṛtro dharmabhṛtāṃ varaḥ ||58||

59vyajṛṃbhata tataḥ śakrastasmai vajramavāsṛjat
|
praviśya vajraṃ vṛtraṃ ca dārayāmāsa bhārata ||59||

60dāritaśca sa vajreṇa mahāyogī mahāsuraḥ
|
jagāma paramaṃ sthānaṃ viṣṇoramitatejasaḥ ||60||

61viṣṇubhaktyā hi tenedaṃ jagadvyāptamabhūttadā
|
tasmācca nihato yuddhe viṣṇoḥ sthānamavāptavān ||61||

62ityeṣa vṛtramāśritya jvarasya mahato mayā
|
vistaraḥ kathitaḥ putra kimanyatprabravīmi te ||62||

63imāṃ jvarotpattimadīnamānasaḥ paṭhetsadā yaḥ susamāhito naraḥ
|
vimuktarogaḥ sa sukhī mudā yuto labheta kāmānsa yathā manīṣitān ||63||
