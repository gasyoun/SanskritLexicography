Mahābhārata 5.104 (Udyogaparvan)

1nārada uvāca|
sūto'yaṃ mātalirnāma śakrasya dayitaḥ suhṛt
|
śuciḥ śīlaguṇopetastejasvī vīryavān balī ||1||

2śakrasyāyaṃ sakhā caiva mantrī sārathireva ca
|
alpāntaraprabhāvaśca vāsavena raṇe raṇe ||2||

3ayaṃ harisahasreṇa yuktaṃ jaitraṃ rathottamam
|
devāsureṣu yuddheṣu manasaiva niyacchati ||3||

4anena vijitānaśvairdorbhyāṃ jayati vāsavaḥ
|
anena balabhitpūrvaṃ prahṛte praharatyuta ||4||

5asya kanyā varārohā rūpeṇāsadṛśī bhuvi
|
satyaśīlaguṇopetā guṇakeśīti viśrutā ||5||

6tasyāsya yatnāccaratastrailokyamamaradyute
|
sumukho bhavataḥ pautro rocate duhituḥ patiḥ ||6||

7yadi te rocate samyagbhujagottama mā ciram
|
kriyatāmāryaka kṣipraṃ buddhiḥ kanyāparigrahe ||7||

8yathā viṣṇukule lakṣmīryathā svāhā vibhāvasoḥ
|
kule tava tathaivāstu guṇakeśī sumadhyamā ||8||

9pautrasyārthe bhavāṃstasmādguṇakeśīṃ pratīcchatu
|
sadṛśīṃ pratirūpasya vāsavasya śacīmiva ||9||

10pitṛhīnamapi hyenaṃ guṇato varayāmahe
|
bahumānācca bhavatastathaivairāvatasya ca ||10||

11sumukhaśca guṇaiścaiva śīlaśaucadamādibhiḥ
|
abhigamya svayaṃ kanyāmayaṃ dātuṃ samudyataḥ ||11||

12mātalistasya saṃmānaṃ kartumarho bhavānapi| kaṇva uvāca|
sa tu dīnaḥ prahṛṣṭaśca prāha nārada‌māryakaḥ ||12||

13vriyamāṇe tathā pautre putre ca nidhanaṃ gate
|
kathamicchāmi devarṣe guṇakeśīṃ snuṣāṃ prati ||13||

14āryaka uvāca|
na me naitadbahumataṃ maharṣe vacanaṃ tava
|
sakhā śakrasya saṃyuktaḥ kasyāyaṃ nepsito bhavet ||14||

15kāraṇasya tu daurbalyāccintayāmi mahāmune
|
asya dehakarastāta mama putro mahādyute ||15||

16bhakṣito vainateyena duḥkhārtāstena vai vayam
|
punareva ca tenoktaṃ vainateyena gacchatā || māsenānyena sumukhaṃ bhakṣayiṣya iti prabho ||16||

17dhruvaṃ tathā tadbhavitā jānīmastasya niścayam
|
tena harṣaḥ pranaṣṭo me suparṇavacanena vai ||17||

18kaṇva uvāca|
mātalistvabravīdenaṃ buddhiratra kṛtā mayā
|
jāmātṛbhāvena vṛtaḥ sumukhastava putrajaḥ ||18||

19so'yaṃ mayā ca sahito nāradena ca pannagaḥ
|
trilokeśaṃ surapatiṃ gatvā paśyatu vāsavam ||19||

20śeṣeṇaivāsya kāryeṇa prajñāsyāmyahamāyuṣaḥ
|
suparṇasya vighāte ca prayatiṣyāmi sattama ||20||

21sumukhaśca mayā sārdhaṃ deveśamabhigacchatu
|
kāryasaṃsādhanārthāya svasti te'stu bhujaṅgama ||21||

22tataste sumukhaṃ gṛhya sarva eva mahaujasaḥ
|
dadṛśuḥ śakramāsīnaṃ devarājaṃ mahādyutim ||22||

23saṅgatyā tatra bhagavān viṣṇurāsīccaturbhujaḥ
|
tatastat sarvamācakhyau nārado mātaliṃ prati ||23||

24vaiśampāyana uvāca|
tataḥ purandaraṃ viṣṇuruvāca bhuvaneśvaram
|
amṛtaṃ dīyatāmasmai kriyatāmamaraiḥ samaḥ ||24||

25mātalirnāradaścaiva sumukhaścaiva vāsava
|
labhantāṃ bhavataḥ kāmāt kāmametaṃ yathepsitam ||25||

26purandaro'tha sañcintya vainateyaparākramam
|
viṣṇumevābravīdenaṃ bhavāneva dadātviti ||26||

27viṣṇuruvāca| īśastvaṃ sarvalokānāṃ carāṇāmacarāśca ye
|
tvayā dattamadattaṃ kaḥ kartumutsahate vibho ||27||

28prādācchakrastatastasmai pannagāyāyuruttamam
|
na tvenamamṛtaprāśaṃ cakāra balavṛtrahā ||28||

29labdhvā varaṃ tu sumukhaḥ sumukhaḥ sambabhūva ha
|
kṛtadāro yathā kāmaṃ jagāma ca gṛhān prati ||29||

30nāradastvāryakaścaiva kṛtakāryau mudā yutau
|
abhijagmaturabhyarcya devarājaṃ mahādyutim ||30||
