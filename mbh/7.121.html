Mahābhārata 7.121 (Droṇaparvan)

1dhṛtarāṣṭra uvāca|
saṃpramṛdya mahat sainyaṃ yāntaṃ śaineyamarjunam
|
nirhrīkā mama te putrāḥ kimakurvata sañjaya ||1||

2kathaṃ vaiṣāṃ tadā yuddhe dhṛtirāsīnmumūrṣatām
|
śaineyacaritaṃ dṛṣṭvā yādṛśaṃ savyasācinaḥ ||2||

3kiṃ nu vakṣyanti te kṣātraṃ sainyamadhye parājitāḥ
|
kathaṃ nu sātyakiryuddhe vyatikrānto mahāyaśāḥ ||3||

4kathaṃ ca mama putrāṇāṃ jīvatāṃ tatra sañjaya
|
śaineyo'bhiyayau yuddhe tanmamācakṣva sañjaya ||4||

5atyadbhutamidaṃ tāta tvatsakāśācchṛṇomyaham
|
ekasya bahubhiḥ sārddhaṃ śatrubhistairmahārathaiḥ ||5||

6viparītamahaṃ manye mandabhāgyaṃ sutaṃ prati
|
yatrāvadhyanta samare sātvatena mahārathāḥ ||6||

7ekasya hi na paryāptaṃ yatsainyaṃ tasya sañjaya
|
kruddhasya yuyudhānasya sarve tiṣṭhantu pāṇḍavāḥ ||7||

8nirjitya samare droṇaṃ kṛtinaṃ citrayodhinam
|
yathā paśugaṇān siṃhastadvaddhantā sutān mama ||8||

9kṛtavarmādibhiḥ śūrairyattairbahubhirāhave
|
yuyudhāno na śakito hantuṃ yatpuruṣarṣabhaḥ ||9||

10naitadīdṛśakaṃ yuddhaṃ kṛtavāṃstatra phālgunaḥ
|
yādṛśaṃ kṛtavān yuddhaṃ śinernaptā mahāyaśāḥ ||10||

11sañjaya uvāca|
tava durmantrite rājan duryodhanakṛtena ca
|
śṛṇuṣvāvahito bhūtvā yatte vakṣyāmi bhārata ||11||

12te punaḥ saṃnyavartanta kṛtyā saṃśaptakā mithaḥ
|
parāṃ yuddhe matiṃ krūrāṃ tava putrasya śāsanāt ||12||

13trīṇi sādisahasrāṇi duryodhanapurogamāḥ
|
śakakāmbojabālhīkā yavanāḥ pāradāstathā ||13||

14kulindāstaṃgaṇāmbaṣṭhāḥ paiśācāśca sabarbarāḥ
|
pārvatīyāśca rājendra kruddhāḥ pāṣāṇapāṇayaḥ ||14||

15abhyadravanta śaineyaṃ śalabhāḥ pāvakaṃ yathā
|
yuktāśca pārvatīyānāṃ rathāḥ pāṣāṇayodhinām ||15||

16śūrāḥ pañcāśatā rājan śaineyaṃ samupādravan
|
tato rathasahasreṇa mahārathaśatena ca ||16||

17dviradānāṃ sahasreṇa dvisāhasraiśca vājibhiḥ
|
śaravarṣāṇi muñcanto vividhāni mahārathāḥ ||17||

18abhyadravanta śaineyamasaṃkhyeyāśca pattayaḥ
|
tāṃśca sañcodayan sarvān ghnatainamiti bhārata ||18||

19duḥśāsano mahārāja sātyakiṃ paryavārayat
|
tatrādbhutamapaśyāma śaineyacaritaṃ mahat ||19||

20yadeko bahubhiḥ sārdhamasaṃbhrāntamayudhyata
|
avadhīcca rathānīkaṃ dviradānāṃ ca tadbalam ||20||

21sādinaścaiva tān sarvān dasyūnapi ca sarvaśaḥ
|
tatra cakrairvimathitairbhagnaiśca paramāyudhaiḥ ||21||

22akṣaiśca bahudhā bhagnairīṣādaṇḍakabandhuraiḥ
|
kuñjarairmathitaiścāpi dhvajaiśca vinipātitaiḥ ||22||

23varmabhiśca tathānīkairvyavakīrṇā vasundharā
|
sragbhirābharaṇairvastrairanukarṣaiśca māriṣa ||23||

24saṃcchannā vasudhā tatra dyaurgrahairiva bhārata
|
girirūpadharāścāpi patitāḥ kuñjarottamāḥ ||24||

25añjanasya kule jātā vāmanasya ca bhārata
|
supratīkakule jātā mahāpadmakule tathā ||25||

26airāvatakule caiva tathā'nyeṣu kuleṣu ca
|
jātā dantivarā rājan śerate bahavo hatāḥ ||26||

27vanāyujān pārvatīyān kāmbojān bālhikānapi
|
tathā hayavarān rājannijaghne tatra sātyakiḥ ||27||

28nānādeśasamutthāṃśca nānājātīṃśca dantinaḥ
|
nijaghne tatra śaineyaḥ śataśo'tha sahasraśaḥ ||28||

29teṣu prakālyamāneṣu dasyūn duḥśāsano'bravīt
|
nivartadhyamadharmajñā yudhyadhvaṃ kiṃ sṛtena vaḥ ||29||

30tāṃścātibhagnān saṃprekṣya putro duḥśāsanastava
|
pāṣāṇayodhinaḥ śūrān pārvatīyānacodayat ||30||

31aśmayuddheṣu kuśalā naitajjānāti sātyakiḥ
|
aśmayuddhamajānantaṃ hatainaṃ yuddhakāmukam ||31||

32tathaiva kuravaḥ sarve nāśmayuddhaviśāradāḥ
|
abhidravata mā bhaiṣṭa na vaḥ prāpsyati sātyakiḥ ||32||

33te pārvatīyā rājānaḥ sarve pāṣāṇayodhinaḥ
|
abhyadravanta śaineyaṃ rājānamiva mantriṇaḥ ||33||

34tato gajaśiraḥprakhyairupalaiḥ śailavāsinaḥ
|
udyatairyuyudhānasya puratastasthurāhave ||34||

35kṣepaṇīyaistathā'pyanye sātvatasya vadhaiṣiṇaḥ
|
coditāstava putreṇa sarvato rurudhurdiśaḥ ||35||

36teṣāmāpatatāmeva śilāyuddhaṃ cikīrṣatām
|
sātyakiḥ pratisandhāya niśitān prāhiṇoccharān ||36||

37tāmaśmavṛṣṭiṃ tumulāṃ pārvatīyaiḥ samīritām
|
cicchedoragasaṅkāśairnārācaiḥ śinipuṃgavaḥ ||37||

38tairaśmacūrṇairdīpyadbhiḥ khadyotānāmiva vrajaiḥ
|
prāyaḥ sainyānyahanyanta hāhābhūtāni māriṣa ||38||

39tataḥ pañcaśataṃ śūrāḥ samudyatamahāśilāḥ
|
nikṛttabāhayo rājannipeturdharaṇītale ||39||

40punardaśaśatāścānye śatasāhasriṇastathā
|
sopalairbāhubhiśchinnaiḥ peturaprāpya sātyakim ||40||

41pāṣāṇayodhinaḥ śūrān yatamānānavasthitān
|
nyavadhīdbahusāhasrāṃstada‌dbhutamivābhavat ||41||

42tataḥ punarvyāttamukhāste'śmavṛṣṭīḥ samantataḥ
|
ayohastāḥ śūlahastā daradāstaṃgaṇāḥ khasāḥ ||42||

43laṃpākāśca kuliṃdāśca cikṣipustāṃśca sātyakiḥ
|
nārācaiḥ praticiccheda pratipattiviśāradaḥ ||43||

44adrīṇāṃ bhidyamānānāmantarikṣe śitaiḥ śaraiḥ
|
śabdena prādravan saṃkhye rathāśvagajapattayaḥ ||44||

45aśmacūrṇairavākīrṇā manuṣya-gaja-vājinaḥ
|
nāśaknuvannavasthātuṃ bhramarairiva daṃśitāḥ ||45||

46hataśiṣṭāḥ sarudhirā bhinnamastakapiṇḍikāḥ
|
kuñjarā varjayāmāsuryuyudhānarathaṃ tadā ||46||

47tataḥ śabdaḥ samabhavattava sainyasya māriṣa
|
mādhavenārdyamānasya sāgarasyeva parvaṇi ||47||

48taṃ śabdaṃ tumulaṃ śrutvā droṇo yantāramabravīt
|
eṣa sūta raṇe kruddhaḥ sātvatānāṃ mahārathaḥ ||48||

49dārayan bahudhā sainyaṃ raṇe carati kālavat
|
yatraiṣa śabdastumulastatra sūta rathaṃ naya ||49||

50pāṣāṇayodhibhirnūnaṃ yuyudhānaḥ samāgataḥ
|
tathāhi rathinaḥ sarve hriyante vidrutairhayaiḥ ||50||

51viśastrakavacā rugṇāstatra tatra patanti ca
|
na śaknuvanti yantāraḥ saṃyantuṃ tumule hayān ||51||

52ityetadvacanaṃ śrutvā bhāradvājasya sārathiḥ
|
pratyuvāca tato droṇaṃ sarvaśastrabhṛtāṃ varam ||52||

53sainyaṃ dravati cāyuṣman kauraveyaṃ samantataḥ
|
paśya yodhān raṇe bhagnān dhāvato vai tatastataḥ ||53||

54ime ca saṃhatāḥ śūrāḥ pañcālāḥ pāṇḍavaiḥ saha
|
tvāmeva hi jighāṃsanta ādravanti samantataḥ ||54||

55atra kāryaṃ samādhatsva prāptakālamarindama
|
sthāne vā gamane cāpi dūraṃ yātaśca sātyakiḥ ||55||

56tathaivaṃ vadatastasya bhāradvājasya sāratheḥ
|
pratyadṛśyata śaineyo nighnan bahuvidhān rathān ||56||

57te vadhyamānāḥ samare yuyudhānena tāvakāḥ
|
yuyudhānarathaṃ tyaktvā droṇānīkāya dudruvuḥ ||57||

58yaistu duḥśāsanaḥ sārdhaṃ rathaiḥ pūrvaṃ nyavartata
|
te bhītāstvabhyadhāvanta sarve droṇarathaṃ prati ||58||
