Mahābhārata 13.86 (Anuśāsanaparvan)

1yudhiṣṭhira uvāca|
uktāḥ pitāmaheneha suvarṇasya vidhānataḥ
|
vistareṇa pradānasya ye guṇāḥ śrutilakṣaṇāḥ ||1||

2yattu kāraṇamutpatteḥ suvarṇasya prakīrtitam
|
sa kathaṃ tārakaḥ prāpto nidhanaṃ tadbravīhi me ||2||

3uktaṃ sa daivatānāṃ hi avadhya iti pārthiva
|
kathaṃ tasyābhavanmṛtyurvistareṇa prakīrtaya ||3||

4etadicchāmyahaṃ śrotuṃ tvattaḥ kurukulodvaha
|
kātsnyaina tārakavadhaṃ paraṃ kautūhalaṃ hi me ||4||

5bhīṣma uvāca|
vipannakṛtyā rājendra devatā ṛṣayastathā
|
kṛttikāścodayāmāsurapatyabharaṇāya vai ||5||

6na devatānāṃ kāciddhi samarthā jātavedasaḥ
|
etā hi śaktāstaṃ garbhaṃ sandhārayitumojasā ||6||

7ṣaṇṇāṃ tāsāṃ tataḥ prītaḥ pāvako garbhadhāraṇāt
|
svena tejovisargeṇa vīryeṇa parameṇa ca ||7||

8tāstu ṣaṭkṛttikā garbhaṃ pupuṣurjātarvedasaḥ
|
ṣaṭsu vartmasu tejogneḥ sakalaṃ nihitaṃ prabho ||8||

9tatastā vardhamānasya kumārasya mahātmanaḥ
|
tejasā'bhiparītāṅgyo na kvaciccharma lebhire ||9||

10tatastejaḥparītāṅgyaḥ sarvāḥ kāla upasthite
|
samaṃ garbhaṃ suṣuvire kṛttikāstaṃ nararṣabha ||10||

11tatastaṃ ṣaḍadhiṣṭhānaṃ garbhamekatvamāgatam
|
pṛthivī pratijagrāha kārtasvarasamīpataḥ ||11||

12sa garbho divyasaṃsthāno dīptimānpāvakaprabhaḥ
|
divyaṃ śaravaṇaṃ prāpya vavṛdhe priyadarśanaḥ ||12||

13dadṛśuḥ kṛttikāstaṃ tu bālamarkasamadyutim
|
jātasnehācca sauhārdātpupuṣuḥ stanyavisravaiḥ ||13||

14abhavatkārtikeyaḥ sa trailokye sacarācare
|
skannatvātskandatāṃ prāpto guhāvāsādguhobhavat ||14||

15tato devāstrayastriṃśaddiśaśca sadigīśvarāḥ
|
rudro dhātā ca viṣṇuśca yamaḥ pūṣāryamā bhagaḥ||15||

16aṃśo mitraśca sādhyāśca vāsavo vasavo'śvinau
|
āpo vāyurnabhaścandro nakṣatrāṇi grahā raviḥ ||16||

17pṛthagbhūtāni cānyāni yāni devārpaṇāni vai
|
ājagmuste'dbhutaṃ draṣṭuṃ kumāraṃ jvalanātmajam ||17||

18ṛṣayastuṣṭuvuścaiva gandharvāśca jagustathā
|
ṣaḍānanaṃ kumāraṃ tu dviṣaḍakṣaṃ dvijapriyam ||18||

19pīnāṃsaṃ dvādaśabhujaṃ pāvakādityavarcasam
|
śayānaṃ śaragulmasthaṃ dṛṣṭvā devāḥ saharṣibhiḥ ||19||

20lebhire paramaṃ harṣaṃ menire cāsuraṃ hatam
|
tato devāḥ priyāṇyasya sarva eva samāharan ||20||

21krīḍataḥ krīḍanīyāni daduḥ pakṣigaṇāśca ha
|
suparṇo'sya dadau putraṃ mayūraṃ citrabarhiṇam ||21||

22rākṣasāśca dadustasmai varāhamahiṣāvubhau
|
kukkuṭaṃ cāgnisaṅkāśaṃ pradadāvaruṇaḥ svayam ||22||

23candramāḥ pradadau meṣamādityo rucirāṃ prabhām
|
gavāṃ mātā ca gā devī dadau śatasahasraśaḥ ||23||

24chāgamagnirguṇopetaṃmilā puṣpaphalaṃ bahu
|
sudhanvā śakaṭaṃ caiva rathaṃ cāmitakūbaram ||24||

25varuṇo vāruṇāndivyānsagajānpradadau śubhān
|
siṃhānsurendro vyāghrāṃśca dvipānanyāṃśca pakṣiṇaḥ ||25||

26śvāpadāṃśca bahūn ghorāṃśchatrāṇi vividhāni ca
|
rākṣasāsurasaṃghāśca anujagmustamīśvaram ||26||

27vardhamānaṃ tu taṃ dṛṣṭvā prārthayāmāsa tārakaḥ
|
upāyairbahubhirhantuṃ nāśakaccāpi taṃ vibhum ||27||

28saināpatyena taṃ devāḥ pūjayitvā guhālayam
|
śaśaṃsurviprakāraṃ taṃ tasmai tārakakāritam ||28||

29sa vivṛddho mahāvīryo devasenāpatiḥ prabhuḥ
|
jaghānāmoghayā śaktyā dānavaṃ tārakaṃ guhaḥ ||29||

30tena tasminkumāreṇa krīḍatā nihate'sure
|
surendraḥ sthāpito rājye devānāṃ punarīśvaraḥ ||30||

31sa senāpatirevātha babhau skandaḥ pratāpavān
|
īśo goptā ca devānāṃ priyakṛcchaṅkarasya ca ||31||

32hiraṇyamūrtirbhagavāneṣa eva ca pāvakiḥ
|
sadā kumāro devānāṃ saināpatyamavāptavān ||32||

33tasmātsuvararṇaṃ maṅgalyaṃ ratnamakṣayyamuttamam
|
sahajaṃ kārtikeyasya vahnestejaḥ paraṃ matam ||33||

34evaṃ rāmāya kaukhya vasiṣṭho'kathayatpurā
|
tasmātsuvarṇadānāya prayatasva narādhipa ||34||

35rāmaḥ suvarṇaṃ dattvā hi vimuktaḥ sarvakilbiṣaiḥ
|
triviṣṭape mahatsthānamavāpāsulabhaṃ naraiḥ ||35||
