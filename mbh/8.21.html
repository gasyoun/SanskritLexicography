Mahābhārata 8.21 (Karṇaparvan)

1dhṛtarāṣṭra uvāca|
pāṇḍye hate kimakarodarjuno yudhi sañjaya
|
ekavīreṇa karṇena drāviteṣu pareṣu ca ||1||

2samāptavidyo balavān yukto vīraḥ sa pāṇḍavaḥ
|
sarvabhūteṣvanujñātaḥ śaṅkareṇa mahātmanā ||2||

3tasmānmahadbhayaṃ tīvramamitraghnāddhanañjayāt
|
sa yattatrākarotpārthastanmamācakṣva sañjaya ||3||

4sañjaya uvāca|
hate pāṇḍye'rjunaṃ kṛṣṇastvarannāha vaco hitam
|
paśyāmi nāhaṃ rājānamapayātāṃśca pāṇḍavān ||4||

5nivṛttaiśca punaḥ pārthairbhagnaṃ śatrubalaṃ mahat
|
aśvatthāmnaśca saṅkalpāddhatāḥ karṇena sṛñjayāḥ ||5||

6tathā'śva-ratha-nāgānāṃ kṛtaṃ ca kadanaṃ mahat
|
sarvamākhyātavānvīro vāsudevaḥ kirīṭine ||6||

7etacchrutvā ca dṛṣṭvā ca bhrāturghoraṃ mahadbhayam
|
vāhayāśvān hṛṣīkeśa kṣipramityāha pāṇḍavaḥ ||7||

8tataḥ prāyāddhṛṣīkeśo rathenāpratiyodhinā
|
dāruṇaśca punastatra prādurāsītsamāgamaḥ ||8||

9tataḥ punaḥ samājagmurabhītāḥ kurupāṇḍavāḥ
|
bhīmasenamukhāḥ pārthāḥ sūtaputramukhā vayam ||9||

10tataḥ pravavṛte bhūyaḥ saṅgrāmo rājasattama
|
karṇasya pāṇḍavānāṃ ca yamarāṣṭravivardhanaḥ ||10||

11dhanūṃṣi bāṇānparighānasi-paṭṭiśa-tomarān
|
musalāni bhuśuṇḍīśca saśaktyṛṣṭiparaśvadhān ||11||

12gadāḥ prāsān śitān kuntān bhindipālānmahāṃkuśān
|
pragṛhya kṣipramāpetuḥ parasparajighāṃsayā ||12||

13bāṇajyātalaśabdena dyāṃ diśaḥ pradiśo viyat
|
pṛthivīṃ nemighoṣeṇa nādayanto'bhyayuḥ parān ||13||

14tena śabdena mahatā saṃhṛṣṭāścakrurāhavam
|
vīrā vīrairmahāghoraṃ kalahāntaṃ titīrṣavaḥ ||14||

15jyātalatradhanuḥśabdaḥ kuñjarāṇāṃ ca bṛṃhatām
|
pādātānāṃ ca patatāṃ nṛṇāṃ nādo mahānabhūt ||15||

16tālaśabdāṃśca vividhāñśūrāṇāṃ cābhigarjatām
|
śrutvā tatra bhṛśaṃ tresuḥ peturmamluśca sainikāḥ ||16||

17teṣāṃ ninadatāṃ caiva śastravarṣaṃ ca muñcatām
|
bahūnādhirathirvīraḥ pramamātheṣubhiḥ parān ||17||

18pañca pañcālavīrāṇāṃ rathāndaśa ca pañca ca
|
sāśvasūtadhvajānkarṇaḥ śarairninye yamakṣayam ||18||

19yodhamukhyā mahāvīryāḥ pāṇḍūnāṃ karṇamāhave
|
śīghrāstrāstūrṇamāvṛtya parivavruḥ samantataḥ ||19||

20tataḥ karṇo dviṣatsenāṃ śaravaṣairviloḍayan
|
vijagāhāṇḍajākīrṇāṃ padminīmiva yūthapaḥ ||20||

21dviṣanmadhyamavaskandya rādheyo dhanuruttamam
|
vidhunvānaḥ śitairbāṇaiḥ śirāṃsyunmathya pātayat ||21||

22carmavarmāṇi saṃchinnānyapatanbhuvi dehinām
|
viṣehurnāsya saṃsparśaṃ dvitīyasya patatriṇaḥ ||22||

23varmadehāsumathanairdhanuṣaḥ pracyutaiḥ śaraiḥ
|
maurvyā talatre nyahanatkaśayā vājino yathā ||23||

24pāṇḍusṛñjayapañcālāñśaragocaramāgatān
|
mamarda tarasā karṇaḥ siṃho mṛgagaṇāniva ||24||

25tataḥ pāñcālarājaśca draupadeyāśca māriṣa
|
yamau ca yuyudhānaśca sahitāḥ karṇamabhyayuḥ ||25||

26teṣu vyāyacchamāneṣu kuru-pañcāla-pāṇḍuṣu
|
priyānasūnraṇe tyaktvā yodhā jaghnuḥ parasparam ||26||

27susannaddhāḥ kavacinaḥ saśirastrāṇabhūṣaṇāḥ
|
gadābhirmusalaiścānye parighaiśca mahābalāḥ ||27||

28samabhyadhāvanta bhṛśaṃ kāladaṇḍairivodyataiḥ
|
nardantaścāhvayantaśca pravalgantaśca māriṣa ||28||

29tato nijaghnuranyonyaṃ petuścānyonyatāḍitāḥ
|
vamanto rudhiraṃ gātrairvimastiṣkekṣaṇāyudhāḥ ||29||

30dantapūrṇaiḥ sarudhirairvaktrairdāḍimasannibhaiḥ
|
jīvanta iva cāpyake tasthuḥ śastropabṛṃhitāḥ ||30||

31paraśvadhaiścāpyapare paṭṭiśairasibhistathā
|
śaktibhirbhindi‌pālaiśca nakhara-prāsa-tomaraiḥ ||31||

32tatakṣuścicchiduścānye bibhiduścikṣipustathā
|
sañcakartuśca jaghnuśca kruddhā raṇamahārṇave ||32||

33peturanyonyanihatā vyasavo rudhirokṣitāḥ
|
kṣarantaḥ surasaṃ raktaṃ prakṛttāścandanā iva ||33||

34rathai rathā vinihatā hastibhiścāpi hastinaḥ
|
narairnarā hatāḥ peturaśvāścāśvaiḥ sahasraśaḥ ||34||

35dhvajāḥ śirāṃsi chatrāṇi dvipahastā nṛṇāṃ bhujāḥ
|
kṣurairbhallārdhacandraiśca cchināḥ peturmahītale ||35||

36narāṃśca nāgānsarathān hayānmamṛdurāhave
|
aśvārohairhatāḥ śūrāśchinnahastāśca dantinaḥ ||36||

37sapatākā dhvajāḥ peturviśīrṇā iva parvatāḥ
|
pattibhiśca samāplutya dviradāḥ syandanāstathā ||37||

38hatāśca hanyamānāśca patitāścaiva sarvaśaḥ
|
aśvārohāḥ samāsādya tvaritāḥ pattibhirhatāḥ ||38||

39sādibhiḥ pattisaṃghāśca nihatā yudhi śerate
|
muditānīva padmāni pramlānā iva ca srajaḥ ||39||

40hatānāṃ vadanānyāsan gātrāṇi ca mahāhave
|
rūpāṇyatyarthakāntāni dviradāśvanṛṇāṃ nṛpa
|
samunnānīva vastrāṇi yayurdurdarśatāṃ parām ||40||
