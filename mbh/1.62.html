Mahābhārata 1.62 (Ādiparvan)

1janamejaya uvāca|
kathitaṃ vai samāsena tvayā sarvaṃ dvijottama
|
mahābhāratamākhyānaṃ kurūṇāṃ caritaṃ mahat||1||

2kathāṃ tvanagha citrārthāṃ kathayasva tapodhana
|
vistaraśravaṇe jātaṃ kautūhalamatīva me||2||

3sa bhavānvistareṇemāṃ punarākhyātumarhati
|
na hi tṛpyāmi pūrveṣāṃ śṛṇvānaścaritaṃ mahat||3||

4na tatkāraṇamalpaṃ vai dharmajñā yatra pāṇḍavāḥ
|
avadhyānsarvaśo jaghnuḥ praśasyante ca mānavaiḥ||4||

5kimarthaṃ te naravyāghrāḥ śaktāḥ santo hyanāgasaḥ
|
prayujyamānānsaṃkleśānkṣāntavanto durātmanām||5||

6kathaṃ nāgāyutaprāṇo bāhuśālī vṛkodaraḥ
|
parikliśyannapi krodhaṃ dhṛtavānvai dvijottama||6||

7kathaṃ sā draupadī kṛṣṇā kliśyamānā durātmabhiḥ
|
śaktā satī dhārtarāṣṭrānnādahatkrodhacakṣuṣā||7||

8kathaṃ vyasaninaṃ dyūte pārthau mādrīsutau tadā
|
anvayuste naravyāghrā bādhyamānā durātmabhiḥ||8||

9kathaṃ dharmabhṛtāṃ śreṣṭhaḥ suto dharmasya dharmavit
|
anarhaḥ paramaṃ kleśaṃ soḍhavānsa yudhiṣṭhiraḥ||9||

10kathaṃ ca bahulāḥ senāḥ pāṇḍavaḥ kṛṣṇasārathiḥ
|
asyanneko'nayatsarvāḥ pitṛlokaṃ dhanaṃjayaḥ||10||

11etadācakṣva me sarvaṃ yathāvṛttaṃ tapodhana
|
yadyacca kṛtavantaste tatratatra mahārathāḥ||11||

12vaiśaṃpāyana uvāca|
kṣaṇaṃ kuru mahārāja vipulo'yamanukramaḥ
|
puṇyākhyānasya vaktavyaḥ kṛṣṇadvaipāyaneritaḥ||12||

13maharṣeḥ sarvalokeṣu pūjitasya mahātmanaḥ
|
pravakṣyāmi mataṃ kṛtsnaṃ vyāsasyāmitatejasaḥ||13||

14idaṃ śatasahasraṃ hi ślokānāṃ puṇyakarmaṇām
|
satyavatyātmajeneha vyākhyātamamitaujasā||14||

15ya idaṃ śrāvayedvidvānye cedaṃ śṛṇuyurnarāḥ
|
te brahmaṇaḥ sthānametya prāpnuyurdevatulyatām||15||

16idaṃ hi vedaiḥ samitaṃ pavitramapi cottamam
|
śrāvyāṇāmuttamaṃ cedaṃ purāṇamṛṣisaṃstutam||16||

17asminnarthaśca kāmaśca nikhilenopadekṣyate
|
itihāse mahāpuṇye buddhiśca parinaiṣṭhikī||17||

18akṣudrāndānaśīlāṃśca satyaśīlānanāstikān
|
kārṣṇaṃ vedamimaṃ vidvāñchrāvayitvā'rthamaśnute||18||

19bhrūṇahatyākṛtaṃ cāpi pāpaṃ jahyādasaṃśayam
|
itihāsamimaṃ śrutvā puruṣo'pi sudāruṇaḥ||19||

20mucyate sarvapāpebhyo rāhuṇā candramā yathā
|
jayo nāmetihāso'yaṃ śrotavyo vijigīṣuṇā||20||

21mahīṃ vijayate rājā śatrūṃścāpi parājayet
|
idaṃ puṃsavanaṃ śreṣṭhamidaṃ svastyayanaṃ mahat||21||

22mahiṣī yuvarājābhyāṃ śrotavyaṃ bahuśastathā
|
vīraṃ janayate putraṃ kanyāṃ vā rājyabhāginīm||22||

23dharmaśāstramidaṃ puṇyamarthaśāstramidaṃ param
|
mokṣaśāstramidaṃ proktaṃ vyāsenāmitabuddhinā||23||

24saṃpratyācakṣate cedaṃ tathā śroṣyanti cāpare
|
putrāḥ śuśrūṣavaḥ santi preṣyāśca priyakāriṇaḥ||24||

25śarīreṇa kṛtaṃ pāpaṃ vācā ca manasaiva ca
|
sarvaṃ saṃtyajati kṣipraṃ ya idaṃ śṛṇuyānnaraḥ||25||

26bharatānāṃ mahajjanma śṛṇvatāmanasūyatām
|
nāsti vyādhibhayaṃ teṣāṃ paralokabhayaṃ kutaḥ||26||

27dhanyaṃ yaśasyamāyuṣyaṃ puṇyaṃ svargyaṃ tathaiva ca
|
kṛṣṇadvaipāyanenedaṃ kṛtaṃ puṇyacikīrṣuṇā||27||

28kīrtiṃ prathayatā loke pāṇḍavānāṃ mahātmanām
|
anyeṣāṃ kṣatriyāṇāṃ ca bhūridraviṇatejasām||28||

29sarvavidyāvadātānāṃ loke prathitakarmaṇām
|
ya idaṃ mānavo loke puṇyārthe brāhmaṇāñchucīn||29||

30śrāvayeta mahāpuṇyaṃ tasya dharmaḥ sanātanaḥ
|
kurūṇāṃ prathitaṃ vaṃśaṃ kīrtayansatataṃ śuciḥ||30||

31vaṃśamāpnoti vipulaṃ loke pūjyatamo bhavet
|
yo'dhīte bhārataṃ puṇyaṃ brāhmaṇo niyatavrataḥ||31||

32caturo vārṣikānmāsānsarvapāpaiḥ pramucyate
|
vijñeyaḥ sa ca vedānāṃ pārago bhārataṃ paṭhan||32||

33devā rājarṣayo hyatra puṇyā brahmarṣayastathā
|
kīrtyante dhūtapāpmānaḥ kīrtyate keśavastathā||33||

34bhagavāṃścāpi deveśo yatra devī ca kīrtyate
|
anekajanano yatra kārtikeyasya saṃbhavaḥ||34||

35brāhmaṇānāṃ gavāṃ caiva māhātmyaṃ yatra kīrtyate
|
sarvaśrutisamūho'yaṃ śrotavyo dharmabuddhibhiḥ||35||

36ya idaṃ śrāvayedvidvānbrāhmaṇāniha parvasu
|
dhūtapāpmā jitasvargo brahma gacchati śāśvatam||36||

37śrāvayedbrāhmaṇāñśrāddhe yaścemaṃ pādamantataḥ
|
akṣayyaṃ tasya tacchrāddhamupāvartetpitṝniha||37||

38ahnā yadenaḥ kriyate indriyairmanasā'pi vā
|
jñānādajñānato vāpi prakaroti naraśca yat||38||

39tanmahābhāratākhyānaṃ śrutvaiva pravilīyate
|
bharatānāṃ mahajjanma mahābhāratamucyate||39||

40niruktamasya yo veda sarvapāpaiḥ pramucyate
|
bharatānāṃ mahajjanma mahābhāratamucyate||40||

41mahato hyenaso martyānmocayedanukīrtitaḥ
|
tribhirvarṣairlabdhakāmaḥ kṛṣṇadvaipāyano muniḥ||41||

42nityotthitaḥ śuciḥ śakto mahābhāratamāditaḥ
|
tapo niyamamāsthāya kṛtametanmaharṣiṇā||42||

43tasmānniyamasaṃyuktaiḥ śrotavyaṃ brāhmaṇairidam
|
kṛṣṇaproktāmimāṃ puṇyāṃ bhāratīmuttamāṃ kathām||43||

44śrāvayiṣyanti ye viprā ye ca śroṣyanti mānavāḥ
|
sarvathā vartamānā vai na te śocyāḥ kṛtākṛtaiḥ||44||

45nareṇa dharmakāmena sarvaḥ śrotavya ityapi
|
nikhilenetihāso'yaṃ tataḥ siddhimavāpnuyāt||45||

46na tāṃ svargagatiṃ prāpya tuṣṭiṃ prāpnoti mānavaḥ
|
yāṃ śrutvaivaṃ mahāpuṇyamitihāsamupāśnute||46||

47śṛṇvañśrāddhaḥ puṇyaśīlaḥ śrāvayaṃścedamadbhutam
|
naraḥ phalamavāpnoti rājasūyāśvamedhayoḥ||47||

48yathā samudro bhagavānyathā merurmahāgiriḥ
|
ubhau khyātau ratnanidhī tathā bhāratamucyate||48||

49idaṃ hi vedaiḥ samitaṃ pavitramapi cottamam
|
śravyaṃ śrutisukhaṃ caiva pāvanaṃ śīlavardhanam||49||

50ya idaṃ bhārataṃ rājanvācakāya prayacchati
|
tena sarvā mahī dattā bhavetsāgaramekhalā||50||

51pārikṣitakathāṃ divyāṃ puṇyāya vijayāya ca
|
kathyamānāṃ mayā kṛtsnāṃ śṛṇu harṣakarīmimām||51||

52tribhirvarṣaiḥ sadotthāyī kṛṣṇadvaipāyano muniḥ
|
mahābhāratamākhyānaṃ kṛtavānidamadbhutam||52||

53dharme cārthe ca kāme ca mokṣe ca bharatarṣabha
|
yadihāsti tadanyatra yannehāsti na tatkvacit||53||
