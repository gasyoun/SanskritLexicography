Mahābhārata 5.116 (Udyogaparvan)

1nārada uvāca|
haryaśvastvabravīdrājā vicintya bahudhā tataḥ
|
dīrghamuṣṇaṃ ca niḥśvasya prajāhetornṛpottamaḥ ||1||

2unnateṣūnnatā ṣaṭsu sūkṣmā sūkṣmeṣu saptasu
|
gambhīrā triṣu gambhīreṣviyaṃ raktā ca pañcasu ||2||

3bahudevāsurālokā bahugandharvadarśanā
|
bahulakṣaṇasampannā bahuprasavadhāriṇī ||3||

4samartheyaṃ janayituṃ cakravartinamātmajam
|
brūhi śulkaṃ dvijaśreṣṭha samīkṣya vibhavaṃ mama ||4||

5gālava uvāca|
ekataḥ śyāmakarṇānāṃ śatānyaṣṭau prayaccha me
|
hayānāṃ candraśubhrāṇāṃ deśajānāṃ vapuṣmatām ||5||

6tatastava bhavitrīyaṃ putrāṇāṃ jananī śubhā
|
araṇīva hutāśānāṃ yonirāyatalocanā ||6||

7nārada uvāca|
etacchrutvā vaco rājā haryaśvaḥ kāmamohitaḥ
|
uvāca gālavaṃ dīno rājarṣirṛṣisattamam ||7||

8dve me śate saṃnihite hayānāṃ yadvidhāstava
|
eṣṭavyāḥ śataśastvanye caranti mama vājinaḥ ||8||

9so'hamekamapatyaṃ vai janayiṣyāmi gālava
|
asyāmetaṃ bhavān kāmaṃ saṃpādayatu me varam ||9||

10etacchrutvā tu sā kanyā gālavaṃ vākyamabravīt
|
mama datto varaḥ kaścit kenacidbrahmavādinā ||10||

11prasūtyante prasūtyante kanyaiva tvaṃ bhaviṣyasi
|
sa tvaṃ dadasva māṃ rājñe pratigṛhya hayottamān ||11||

12nṛpebhyo hi caturbhyaste pūrṇānyaṣṭau śatāni me
|
bhaviṣyanti tathā putrā mama catvāra eva ca ||12||

13kriyatāmupasaṃhāro gurvarthaṃ dvijasattama
|
eṣā tāvanmama prajñā yathā vā manyase dvija ||13||

14evamuktastu sa muniḥ kanyayā gālavastadā
|
haryaśvaṃ pṛthivīpālamidaṃ vacanamabravīt ||14||

15iyaṃ kanyā naraśreṣṭha haryaśva pratigṛhyatām
|
caturbhāgena śulkasya janayasvaikamātmajam ||15||

16pratigṛhya sa tāṃ kanyāṃ gālavaṃ pratinandya ca
|
samaye deśakāle ca labdhavān sutamīpsitam ||16||

17tato vasumanā nāma vasubhyo vasumattaraḥ
|
vasuprakhyo narapatiḥ sa babhūva vasupradaḥ ||17||

18atha kāle punardhīmān gālavaḥ pratyupasthitaḥ
|
upasaṅgamya covāca haryaśvaṃ prītamānasam ||18||

19jāto nṛpa sutaste'yaṃ bālo bhāskarasaṃnibhaḥ
|
kālo gantuṃ naraśreṣṭha bhikṣārthamaparaṃ nṛpam ||19||

20haryaśvaḥ satyavacane sthitaḥ sthitvā ca pauruṣe
|
durlabhatvāddhayānāṃ ca pradadau mādhavīṃ punaḥ ||20||

21mādhavī ca punardīptāṃ parityajya nṛpaśriyam
|
kumārī kāmato bhūtvā gālavaṃ pṛṣṭhato'nvayāt ||21||

22tvayyeva tāvattiṣṭhantu hayā ityuktavān dvijaḥ
|
prayayau kanyayā sārdhaṃ divodāsaṃ prajeśvaram ||22||
