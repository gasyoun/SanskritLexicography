Mahābhārata 1.108 (Ādiparvan)

1vaiśaṃpāyana uvāca|
tataḥ sa muniśārdūlastānuvāca tapodhanān
|
doṣataḥ kaṃ gamiṣyāmi na hi me'nyoparādhyati||1||

2taṃ dṛṣṭvā rakṣiṇastatra tathā bahutithe'hani
|
nyavedayaṃstathā rājñe yathāvṛttaṃ narādhipa||2||

3śrutvā ca vacanaṃ teṣāṃ niścitya saha mantribhiḥ
|
prasādayāmāsa tadā śūlasthamṛṣisattamam||3||

4rājovāca
|
yanmayā'pakṛtaṃ mohādajñānādṛṣisattama
|
prasādaye tvāṃ tatrā'haṃ na me tvaṃ kroddhumarhasi||4||

5evamuktastato rājñā prasādamakaronmuniḥ
|
kṛtaprasādaṃ rājā taṃ tataḥ samavatārayat||5||

6avatārya ca śūlāgrāttacchūlaṃ niścakarṣa ha
|
aśaknuvaṃśca niṣkraṣṭuṃ śūlaṃ mūle sa cicchide||6||

7sa tathāntargatenaiva śūlena vyacaranmuniḥ
|
tenātitapasā lokānvijigye durlabhānparaiḥ||7||

8aṇīmāṇḍavya iti ca tato lokeṣu gīyate
|
sa gatvā sadanaṃ vipro dharmasya paramārthavit||8||

9āsanasthaṃ tato dharmaṃ dṛṣṭvopālabhata prabhu:
|
kiṃ nu tadduṣkṛtaṃ karma mayā kṛtamajānatā||9||

10yasyeyaṃ phalanirvṛttirīdṛśyāsāditā mayā
|
śīghramācakṣva me tattvaṃ paśya me tapaso balam||10||

11dharma uvāca|
pataṅgikānāṃ puccheṣu tvayeṣīkā praveśitā
|
karmaṇastasya te prāptaṃ phalametattapodhana||11||

12svalpameva yathā dattaṃ dānaṃ bahuguṇaṃ bhavet
|
adharma evaṃ viprarṣe bahuduḥkhaphalapradaḥ||12||

13aṇīmāṇḍavya uvāca|
kasminkāle mayā tattu kṛtaṃ brūhi yathātatham
|
tenokto dharmarājena bālabhāve tvayā kṛtam||13||

14aṇīmāṇḍavya uvāca|
bālo hi dvādaśādvarṣājjanmato yatkariṣyati
|
na bhaviṣyatyadharmo'tra na prajñāsyati vai diśaḥ||14||

15alpe'parādhe'pi mahānmama daṇḍastvayā dhṛtaḥ
|
garīyānbrāhmaṇavadhaḥ sarvabhūtavadhādapi||15||

16śūdrayonāvato dharma mānuṣaḥ saṃbhaviṣyasi
|
maryādāṃ sthāpayāmyadya loke karmaphalodayām||16||

17ācaturdaśakādvarṣānna bhaviṣyati pātakam
|
parataḥ kurvatāmeva doṣa eva bhaviṣyati||17||

18vaiśaṃpāyana uvāca|
etena tvaparādhena śāpāttasya mahātmanaḥ
|
dharmo vidurarūpeṇa śūdrayonāvajāyata||18||

19dharme cārthe ca kuśalo lobhakrodhavivarjitaḥ
|
dīrghadarśī śamaparaḥ kurūṇāṃ ca hite rataḥ||19||
