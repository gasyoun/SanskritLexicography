Mahābhārata 6.19 (Bhīṣmaparvan)

1dhṛtarāṣṭra uvāca|
akṣauhiṇīṃ daśaikāṃ ca vyūḍhāṃ dṛṣṭvā yudhiṣṭhiraḥ
|
kathamalpena sainyena pratyavyūhata pāṇḍavaḥ ||1||

2yo veda mānuṣaṃ vyūhaṃ daivaṃ gāndharvamāsuram
|
kathaṃ bhīṣmaṃ sa kaunteyaḥ pratyavyūhata sañjaya ||2||

3sañjaya uvāca|
dhārtarāṣṭrāṇyanīkāni dṛṣṭvā vyūḍhāni pāṇḍavaḥ
|
abhyabhāṣata dharmātmā dharmarājo dhanañjayam ||3||

4maharṣervacanāttāta vedayanti bṛhaspateḥ
|
saṃhatānyodhayedalpānkāmaṃ vistārayedbahūn ||4||

5sūcīmukhamanīkaṃ syādalpānāṃ bahubhiḥ saha
|
asmākaṃ ca tathā sainyamalpīyaḥ sutarāṃ paraiḥ ||5||

6etadvacanamājñāya maharṣervyūha pāṇḍava
|
etacchrutvā dharmarājaṃ pratyabhāṣata pāṇḍavaḥ ||6||

7eṣa vyūhāmi te vyūhaṃ rājasattama durjayam
|
acalaṃ nāma vajrākhyaṃ vihitaṃ vajrapāṇinā ||7||

8yaḥ sa vāta ivodbhūtaḥ samare duḥsahaḥ paraiḥ
|
sa naḥ puro yotsayate vai bhīmaḥ praharatāṃ varaḥ ||8||

9tejāṃsi ripusainyānāṃ mṛdananpuruṣasattamaḥ
|
agre'graṇīryotsyati no yuddhopāyavicakṣaṇaḥ ||9||

10yaṃ dṛṣṭvā kuravaḥ sarve duryodhanapurogamāḥ
|
nivartiṣyanti santrastāḥ siṃhaṃ kṣudramṛgā yathā ||10||

11taṃ sarve saṃśrayiṣyāmaḥ prākāramakutobhayāḥ
|
bhīmaṃ praharatāṃ śreṣṭhaṃ devārājamivāmarāḥ ||11||

12na hi so'sti pumā~lloke yaḥ saṅkruddhaṃ vṛkodaram
|
draṣṭumatyugrakarmāṇaṃ viṣaheta nararṣabham ||12||

13evamuktvā mahābāhustathā cakre dhanañjayaḥ
|
vyūhya tāni balānyāśu prayayau phalgunastathā ||13||

14samprayātānkurūndṛṣṭvā pāṇḍavānāṃ mahācamūḥ
|
gaṅgeva pūrṇā stimitā spandamānā vyadṛśyata ||14||

15bhīmaseno'graṇīsteṣāṃ dhṛṣṭadyumnaśca vīryavān
|
nakulaḥ sahadevaśca dhṛṣṭaketuśca pārthivaḥ ||15||

16virāṭaśca tataḥ paścādrājāthākṣauhiṇīvṛtaḥ
|
bhrātṛbhiḥ saha putraiśca so'bhyarakṣata pṛṣṭhataḥ ||16||

17cakrarakṣau tu bhīmasya mādrīputrau mahādyutī
|
draupadeyāḥ sasaubhadrāḥ pṛṣṭhagopāstarasvinaḥ ||17||

18dhṛṣṭadyumnaśca pāñcālyasteṣāṃ goptā mahārathaḥ
|
sahitaḥ pṛtanāśūraiḥ rathamukhyaiḥ prabhadrakaiḥ ||18||

19śikhaṇḍī tu tataḥ paścādarjunenābhirakṣitaḥ
|
yatto bhīṣmavināśāya prayayau bharatarṣabha ||19||

20pṛṣṭhato'pyarjunasyāsīdyuyudhāno mahābalaḥ
|
cakrarakṣau tu pāñcālyau yudhāmanyūttamojasau ||20||

21kaikayo dhṛṣṭaketuśca cekitānaśca vīryavān
|
bhīmaseno gadāṃ bibhradvajrasāramayīṃ dṛḍhām
|
caranvegena mahatā samudramapi śoṣayet ||21||

22ete tiṣṭhanti sāmātyāḥ prekṣantaste janādhipa
|
dhṛtarāṣṭrasya dāyādāniti bībhatsurabravīt ||22||

23bhīmasenaṃ tadā rājandarśayasva mahābalam
|
bruvāṇaṃ tu tathā pārthaṃ sarvasainyāni bhārata ||23||

24apūjayaṃstadā vāgbhiranukūlābhirāhave
|
rājā tu madhyamānīke kuntīputro yudhiṣṭhiraḥ ||24||

25bṛhadbhiḥ kuñjarairmattaiścaladbhiracalairiva
|
akṣauhiṇyātha pāñcālyo yajñaseno mahāmanāḥ || virāṭamanvayātpaścātpāṇḍavārthaṃ parākramī ||25||

26teṣāmādityacandrābhāḥ kanakottamabhūṣaṇāḥ
|
nānācitradharā rājanratheṣvāsanmahādhvajāḥ ||26||

27samutsārya tataḥ paścāddhṛṣṭadyumno mahārathaḥ
|
bhrātṛbhiḥ saha putraiśca so'bhyarakṣadyudhiṣṭhiram ||27||

28tvadīyānāṃ pareṣāṃ ca ratheṣu vipulāndhvajān
|
abhibhūyārjunasyaiko rathe tasthau mahākapiḥ ||28||

29padātāstvagrato'gacchannasiśaktyṛṣṭipāṇayaḥ
|
anekaśatasāhasrā bhīmasenasya rakṣiṇaḥ ||29||

30vāraṇā daśasāhasrāḥ prabhinnakaraṭāmukhāḥ
|
śūrā hemamayairjālairdīpyamānā ivācalāḥ ||30||

31kṣaranta iva jīmūtā mahārhāḥ padmagandhinaḥ
|
rājānamanvayuḥ yaścājjīmūtā iva vārṣikāḥ ||31||

32bhīmaseno gadāṃ bhīmāṃ prakarṣanparighopamām
|
pracakarṣa mahāsainyaṃ durādharṣo mahāmanāḥ ||32||

33tamarkamiva duṣprekṣyaṃ tapantamiva vāhinīm
|
na śekuḥ sarvayodhāste prativīkṣitumantike ||33||

34vajro nāmaiṣa sa vyūho nirbhayaḥ sarvatomukhaḥ
|
cāpavidyuddhvajo ghoro gupto gāṇḍīvadhanvanā ||34||

35yaṃ prativyūhya tiṣṭhanti pāṇḍavāstava vāhinīm
|
ajeyo mānuṣe loke pāṇḍavairabhirakṣitaḥ ||35||

36sandhyāṃ tiṣṭhatsu sainyeṣu sūryasyodayanaṃ prati
|
pravātsapṛṣato vāyurnirabhre stanayitnumān ||36||

37viṣvagvātāśca vivavurnīcaiḥ śarkarakarṣiṇaḥ
|
rajaścoddhūyata mahattama ācchādayajjagat ||37||

38papāta mahatī colkā prāṅmukhī bharatarṣabha
|
udyantaṃ sūryamāhatya vyaśīryata mahāsvanā ||38||

39atha sannahyamāneṣu sainyeṣu bharatarṣabha
|
niṣprabho'bhyudyayau sūryaḥ saghoṣaṃ bhūścacāla ca ||39||

40vyaśīryata sanādā ca bhūstadā bharatarṣabha
|
nirghātā bahavo rājandikṣu sarvāsu cābhavan ||40||

41prādurāsīdrajastīvraṃ na prājñāyata kiñcana
|
dhvajānāṃ dhūyamānānāṃ sahasā mātariśvanā ||41||

42kiṅkiṇījālabaddhānāṃ kāñcanasragvarāmbaraiḥ
|
mahatāṃ sapatākānāmādityasamatejasām ||42||

43sarvaṃ jhaṇajhaṇībhūtamāsīttālavaneṣviva
|
evaṃ te puruṣavyāghrāḥ pāṇḍavā yuddhanandinaḥ ||43||

44vyavasthitāḥ prativyūhya tava putrasya vāhinīm
|
grasanta iva mañjāno yodhānāṃ bharatarṣabha ||44||

45dṛṣṭvāgrato bhīmasenaṃ gadāpāṇimavasthitam ||45||
