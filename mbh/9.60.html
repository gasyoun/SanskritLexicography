Mahābhārata 9.60 (Śalyaparvan)

1dhṛtarāṣṭra uvāca|
adharmeṇa hataṃ dṛṣṭvā rājānaṃ mādhavottamaḥ
|
kimabravīttadā sūta baladevo mahābalaḥ||1||

2gadāyuddhaviśeṣajño gadāyuddhaviśāradaḥ
|
kṛtavān rauhiṇeyo yattanmamācakṣva sañjaya||2||

3sañjaya uvāca|
śirasyabhihataṃ dṛṣṭvā bhīmasenena te sutam
|
rāmaḥ praharatāṃ śreṣṭhaścukrodha balavadbalī||3||

4tato madhye narendrāṇāmūrdhvabāhurhalāyudhaḥ
|
kurvannārtasvaraṃ ghoraṃ dhigbhīmetyuvāca ha||4||

5aho dhigyadadho nābheḥ prahṛtaṃ dharmavigrahe
|
naitaddṛṣṭaṃ gadāyuddhe kṛtavānyadvṛkodaraḥ||5||

6adho nābhyāṃ na hantavyamiti śāstrasya niścayaḥ
|
ayaṃ tvaśāstravinmūḍhaḥ svacchandātsampravartate||6||

7tasya tattadbruvāṇasya roṣaḥ samabhavanmahān
|
tato rājānamālokya roṣasaṃraktalocanaḥ||7||

8baladevo mahārāja tato vacanamabravīt
|
na caiṣa patitaḥ kṛṣṇa kevalaṃ matsamo'samaḥ||8||

9āśritasya tu daurbalyādāśrayaḥ paribhartsyate
|
tato lāṅgalamudyamya bhīmamabhyadravadbalī||9||

10tsyordhvabāhoḥ sadṛśaṃ rūpamāsīnmahātmanaḥ
|
bahudhā tu vicitrasya śvetasyeva mahāgireḥ||10||

11tamutpatantaṃ jagrāha keśavo vinayānvitaḥ
|
bāhubhyāṃ pīnavṛttābhyāṃ prayatnādbalavadvalī||11||

12sitāsitau yaduvarau śuśubhāte'dhikaṃ tadā
|
nabhogatau yathā rājaṃścandrasūryo dinakṣaye||12||

13uvāca cainaṃ saṃrabdhaṃ śamayanniva keśavaḥ
|
ātmavṛddhirmitravṛddhirmitramitrodayastathā||13||

14viparītaṃ dviṣatsvetat ṣaḍvidhā vṛddhirātmanaḥ
|
ātmanyapi ca mitre ca viparītaṃ yadā bhavet||14||

15tadā vidyānmanoglānimāśu śāntikaro bhavet
|
asmākaṃ sahajaṃ mitraṃ pāṇḍavāḥ śuddhapauruṣāḥ||15||

16svakāḥ pitṛṣvasuḥ putrāste parairnikṛtā bhṛśam
|
pratijñāpālanaṃ dharmaḥ kṣatriyasyeha vemyaham||16||

17suyodhanasya gadayā bhaṅktāsmyūrū mahāhave
|
iti pūrvaṃ pratijñātaṃ bhīmena hi sabhātale||17||

18maitraiyeṇābhiśaptaśca pūrvameva maharṣiṇā
|
ūrū te bhetsyate bhīmo gadayeti parantapa||18||

19ato doṣaṃ na paśyāmi mā kruddhyasva pralambahan
|
yaunaḥ svaiḥ sukhahārdaica sambandhaḥ saha pāṇḍavaiḥ||19||

20teṣāṃ vṛddhyā hi vṛddhirno mā krudhaḥ puruṣarṣabha
|
vāsudevavacaḥ śrutvā sīrabhṛtprāha dharmavit||20||

21dharmaḥ sucaritaḥ sadbhiḥ sa ca dvābhyāṃ niyacchati
|
arthaścātyarthalubdhasya kāmaścātiprasaṅgiṇaḥ||21||

22dharmārthau dharmakāmau ca kāmārthau cāpyapīḍayan
|
dharmārthakāmānyo'bhyeti so'tyantaṃ sukhamaśnute||22||

23tadidaṃ vyākulaṃ sarvaṃ kṛtaṃ dharmasya pīḍanāt
|
bhīmasenena govinda kāmaṃ tvaṃ tu yathā''ttha mām||23||

24śrīkṛṣṇa uvāca|
aroṣaṇo hi dharmātmā satataṃ dharmavatsalaḥ
|
bhavānprakhyāyate loke tasmātsaṃśāmya mā krudhaḥ||24||

25prāptaṃ kaliyugaṃ viddhi pratijñāṃ pāṇḍavasya ca
|
ānṛṇyaṃ yātu vairasya pratijñāyāśca pāṇḍavaḥ||25||

26sañjaya uvāca|
dharmacchalamapi śrutvā keśavātsa viśāmpate
|
naiva prītamanā rāmo vacanaṃ prāha saṃsadi||26||

27hatvā'dharmeṇa rājānaṃ dharmātmānaṃ suyodhanam
|
jihmayodhīte loke'smin khyātiṃ yāsyati pāṇḍavaḥ||27||

28duryodhano'pi dharmātmā gatiṃ yāsyati śāśvatīm
|
ṛjuyodhī hato rājā dhārtarāṣṭro narādhipaḥ||28||

29yuddhadīkṣāṃ praviśyājau raṇayajñaṃ vitatya ca
|
hutvā''tmānamamitrāgnau prāpa cāvabhṛthaṃ yaśaḥ||29||

30ityuktvā rathamāsthāya rauhiṇeyaḥ pratāpavān
|
śvetābhraśikharākāraḥ prayayau dvārakāṃ prati||30||

31pañcālāśca savārṣṇeyāḥ pāṇḍavāśca viśāmpate
|
rāme dvārāvatīṃ yāte nātipramanaso'bhavan||31||

32tato yudhiṣṭhiraṃ dīnaṃ cintāparamadhomukham
|
śokopahatasaṅkalpaṃ vāsudevo'bravīdidam||32||

33vāsudeva uvāca|
dharmarāja kimarthaṃ tvamadharmamanumanyase
|
hatabandhoryadetasya patitasya vicetasaḥ||33||

34duryodhanasya bhīmena mṛdyamānaṃ śiraḥ padā
|
upaprekṣasi kasmāttvaṃ dharmajñaḥ sannarādhipa||34||

35yudhiṣṭhira uvāca|
na mamaitatpriyaṃ kṛṣṇa yadrājānaṃ vṛkodaraḥ
|
padā mūrdhnyaspṛśatkrodhānna hṛṣye kulakṣaye||35||

36nikṛtyā nikṛtā nityaṃ dhṛtarāṣṭrasutairvayam
|
bahūni paruṣāṇyuktvā vanaṃ prasthāpitāḥ sma ha||36||

37bhīmasenasya tadduḥkhamatīva hṛdi vartate
|
iti sañcintya vārṣṇeya mayaitatsamupekṣitam||37||

38tasmāddhatvā'kṛtaprajñaṃ lubdhaṃ kāmavaśānugam
|
labhatāṃ pāṇḍavaḥ kāmaṃ dharme'dharme ca vā kṛte||38||

39sañjaya uvāca|
ityukte dharmarājena vāsudevo'bravīdidam
|
kāmamastvetaditi vai kṛcchrādyadukulodvahaḥ||39||

40ityukto vāsudevena bhīmapriyahitaiṣiṇā
|
anvamodata tatsarvaṃ yadbhīmena kṛtaṃ yudhi||40||

41bhīmaseno'pi hatvā''jau tava putramamarṣaṇaḥ
|
abhivādyāgrataḥ sthitvā samprahṛṣṭaḥ kṛtāñjaliḥ||41||

42provāca sumahātejā dharmarājaṃ yudhiṣṭhiram
|
harṣādutphullanayano jitakāśī viśāmpate||42||

43tavādya pṛthivī sarvā kṣemā nihatakaṇṭakā
|
tāṃ praśādhi mahārāja svadharmamanupālaya||43||

44yastu kartā'sya vairasya nikṛtyā nikṛtipriyaḥ
|
so'yaṃ vinihataḥ śete pṛthivyāṃ pṛthivīpate||44||

45duḥśāsanaprabhṛtayaḥ sarve te cogravādinaḥ
|
rādheyaḥ śakuniścaiva hatāśca tava śatravaḥ||45||

46seyaṃ ratnasamākīrṇā mahī savanaparvatā
|
upāvṛttā mahārāja tvāmadya nihatadviṣam||46||

47yudhiṣṭhira uvāca|
gato vairasya nidhanaṃ hato rājā suyodhanaḥ
|
kṛṣṇasya matamāsthāya vijiteyaṃ vasundharā||47||

48diṣṭyā gatastvamānṛṇyaṃ mātuḥ kopasya cobhayoḥ
|
diṣṭyā jayati durdharṣa diṣṭyā śatrurnipātitaḥ||48||
