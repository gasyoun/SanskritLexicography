Mahābhārata 3.168 (Āraṇyakaparvan)

1arjuna uvāca|
tatastāmavasaṃ prīto rajanīṃ tatra bhārata
|
prasādāddevadevasya tryambakasya mahātmanaḥ||1||

2vyuṣito rajanīṃ cāhaṃ kṛtvā paurvāhṇikīḥ kriyāḥ
|
apaśyaṃ taṃ dvijaśreṣṭhaṃ dṛṣṭavānasmi yaṃ purā||2||

3tasmai cā'haṃ yathāvṛttaṃ sarvameva nyavedayam
|
bhagavantaṃ mahādevaṃ sameto'smīti bhārata||3||

4sa māmuvāca rājendra prīyamāṇo dvijottamaḥ
|
dṛṣṭastvayā mahādevo yathā nānyena kenacit||4||

5sametya lokapālaistu sarvairvaivasvatādibhiḥ
|
draṣṭāsyanagha devendraṃ sa ca te'strāṇi dāsyati||5||

6evamuktvā sa māṃ rājannāśliṣya ca punaḥ punaḥ
|
agacchat sa yathākāmaṃ brāhmaṇaḥ sūryasannibhaḥ||6||

7athāparāhṇe tasyāhnaḥ prāvāt puṇyaḥ samīraṇaḥ
|
punarnavamimaṃ lokaṃ kurvanniva sapatnahan||7||

8divyāni caiva mālyāni sugandhīni navāni ca
|
śaiśirasya gireḥ pāde prādurāsan samīpataḥ||8||

9vāditrāṇi ca divyāni sughorāṇi samantataḥ
|
stutayaścendrasaṃyuktā aśrūyanta manoharāḥ||9||

10gaṇāścāpsarasāṃ tatra gandharvāṇāṃ tathaiva ca
|
purastāddevadevasya jagurgītāni sarvaśaḥ||10||

11marutāṃ ca gaṇāstatra devayānairupāgaman
|
mahendrānucarā ye ca ye ca sadmanivāsinaḥ||11||

12tato marutvān haribhiryuktairvāhaiḥ svalaṅkṛtaiḥ
|
śacīsahāyastatrā''yāt saha sarvaistadāmaraiḥ||12||

13etasminneva kāle tu kubero naravāhanaḥ
|
darśayāmāsa māṃ rāja~llakṣmyā paramayā yutaḥ||13||

14dakṣiṇasyāṃ diśi yamaṃ pratyapaśyaṃ vyavasthitam
|
varuṇaṃ devarājaṃ ca yathāsthānamavasthitam||14||

15te māmūcurmahārāja sāntvayitvā nararṣabha
|
savyasācinnirīkṣāsmā~llokapālānavasthitān||15||

16surakāryārthasiddhyarthaṃ dṛṣṭavānasi śaṅkaram
|
asmatto'pi gṛhāṇa tvamastrāṇīti samantataḥ||16||

17tato'haṃ prayato bhūtvā praṇipatya surarṣabhān
|
pratyagṛhṇaṃ tadā'strāṇi mahānti vidhivadvibho||17||

18gṛhītāstrastato devairanujñāto'smi bhārata
|
atha devā yayuḥ sarve yathāgatamarindama||18||

19maghavānapi deveśo rathamāruhya suprabham
|
uvāca bhagavān svargaṃ gantavyaṃ phālguna tvayā||19||

20puraivāgamanādasmādvedāhaṃ tvāṃ dhanañjaya
|
ataḥ paraṃ tvahaṃ vai tvāṃ darśaye bharatarṣabha||20||

21tvayā hi tīrtheṣu purā samāplāvaḥ kṛto'sakṛt
|
tapaścedaṃ mahattaptaṃ svargaṃ gantāsi pāṇḍava||21||

22bhūyaścaiva ca taptavyaṃ tapaścaraṇamuttamam
|
svargaṃ tvavaśyaṃ gantavyaṃ tvayā śatruniṣūdana||22||

23mātalirmanniyogāttvāṃ tridivaṃ prāpayiṣyati
|
viditastvaṃ hi devānāṃ munīnāṃ ca mahātmanām||23||

24tato'hamabruvaṃ śakraṃ prasīda bhagavan mama
|
ācāryaṃ varayeyaṃ tvāmastrārthaṃ tridaśeśvara||24||

25indra uvāca|
krūrakarmāstravittāta bhaviṣyasi parantapa
|
yadarthamastrāṇīpsustvaṃ taṃ kāmaṃ pāṇḍavā''pnuhi||25||

26tato'hamabruvaṃ nā'haṃ divyānyastrāṇi śatruhan
|
mānuṣeṣu prayokṣyāmi vināstrapratighātanāt||26||

27tāni divyāni me'strāṇi prayaccha vibudhādhipa
|
lokāṃścāstrajitān paścāllabheyaṃ surapuṅgava||27||

28indra uvāca|
parīkṣārthaṃ mayaitatte vākyamuktaṃ dhanañjaya
|
mamātmajasya vacanaṃ sūpapannamidaṃ tava||28||

29śikṣa me bhavanaṃ gatvā sarvāṇyastrāṇi bhārata
|
vāyoragnervasubhyo'pi varuṇāt samarudgaṇāt||29||

30sādhyaṃ paitāmahaṃ caiva gandharvoragarakṣasām
|
vaiṣṇavāni ca sarvāṇi nairṛtāni tathaiva ca||30||

31madgatāni ca jānīhi sarvāstrāṇi kurūdvaha
|
evamuktvā tu māṃ śakrastatraivāntaradhīyata||31||

32athā'paśyaṃ hariyujaṃ rathamaindramupasthitam
|
divyaṃ māyāmayaṃ puṇyaṃ yattaṃ mātalinā nṛpa||32||

33lokapāleṣu yāteṣu māmuvācātha mātaliḥ
|
draṣṭumicchati śakrastvāṃ devarājo mahādyute||33||

34saṃsiddhyasva mahābāho kuru kāryamanantaram
|
paśya puṇyakṛtā~llokān saśarīro divaṃ vraja||34||

35devarājaḥ sahastrākṣastvāṃ didṛkṣati bhārata
|
ityukto'haṃ mātalinā girimāmantrya śaiśiram||35||

36pradakṣiṇamupāvṛtya samārohaṃ rathottamam
|
codayāmāsa sa hayān manomārutaraṃhasaḥ||36||

37mātalirhayatattvajño yathāvadbhūridakṣiṇaḥ
|
avaikṣata ca me vaktraṃ sthitasyā'tha sa sārathiḥ||37||

38tathā bhrānte rathe rājan vismitaścedamabravīt
|
atyadbhutamidaṃ tvadya vicitraṃ pratibhāti me||38||

39yadāsthito rathaṃ divyaṃ padānna calitaḥ padam
|
devarājo'pi hi mayā nityamatropalakṣitaḥ||39||

40vicalan prathamotpāte hayānāṃ bharatarṣabha
|
tvaṃ punaḥ sthita evātra rathe bhrānte kurūdvaha||40||

41atiśakramidaṃ sarvaṃ taveti pratibhāti me
|
ityuktvā''kāśamāviśya mātalirvibudhālayān||41||

42darśayāmāsa me rājan vimānāni ca bhārata
|
sa ratho haribhiryukto hyūrdhvamācakrame tataḥ||42||

43ṛpayo devatāścaiva pūjayanti narottama
|
tataḥ kāmagamā~llokānapaśyaṃ vai surarṣiṇām||43||

44gandharvāpsarasāṃ caiva prabhāvamamitaujasām
|
nandanādīni devānāṃ vanānyupavanāni ca||44||

45darśayāmāsa me śīghraṃ mātaliḥ śakrasārathiḥ
|
tataḥ śakrasya bhavanamapaśyamamarāvatīm||45||

46divyaiḥ kāmaphalairvṛkṣai ratnaiśca samalaṅkṛtām
|
na tatra sūryastapati na śītoṣṇe na ca klamaḥ||46||

47na bādhate tatra rajastatrāsti na jarā nṛpa
|
na tatra śoko dainyaṃ vā daurbalyaṃ copalakṣyate||47||

48divaukasāṃ mahārāja na glānirarimardana
|
na krodhalobhau tatrāstāṃ surādīnāṃ viśāmpate||48||

49nityatuṣṭāśca te rājan prāṇinaḥ suraveśmani
|
nityapuṣpaphalāstatra pādapā haritacchadāḥ||49||

50puṣkariṇyaśca vividhāḥ padmasaugandhikāyutāḥ
|
śītastatra vavau vāyuḥ sugandhī jīvanaḥ śuciḥ||50||

51sarvaratnavicitrā ca bhūmiḥ puṣpavibhūṣitā
|
mṛgadvijāśca bahavo rucirā madhurasvarāḥ||51||

52vimānagāminaścātra dṛśyante bahavo'mbare
|
tato'paśyaṃ vasūn rudrān sādhyāṃśca samarudgaṇān||52||

53ādityānaśvinau caiva tān sarvān pratyapūjayam
|
te māṃ vīryeṇa yaśasā tejasā ca balena ca||53||

54astraiścāpyanvajānanta saṅgrāme vijayena ca
|
praviśya tāṃ purīṃ divyāṃ devagandharvapūjitām||54||

55devarājaṃ sahasrākṣamupātiṣṭhaṃ kṛtāñjaliḥ
|
dadāvardhāsanaṃ prītaḥ śakro me dadatāṃ varaḥ||55||

56bahumānācca gātrāṇi pasparśa mama vāsavaḥ
|
tatrāhaṃ devagandharvaiḥ sahito bhūridakṣiṇaiḥ||56||

57astrārthamavasaṃ svarge śikṣāṇo'strāṇi bhārata
|
viśvāvasośca vai putraścitraseno'bhavat sakhā||57||

58sa ca gāndharvamakhilaṃ grāhayāmāsa māṃ nṛpa
|
tatrāhamavasaṃ rājan gṛhītāstraḥ supūjitaḥ||58||

59sukhaṃ śakrasya bhavane sarvakāmasamanvitaḥ
|
śṛṇvan vai gītaśabdaṃ ca tūryaśabdaṃ ca puṣkalam
|
paśyaṃścāpsarasaḥ śreṣṭhā nṛtyantīrbharatarṣabha||59||

60tat sarvamanavajñāya tathyaṃ vijñāya bhārata
|
atyarthaṃ pratigṛhyāhamastreṣveva vyavasthitaḥ||60||

61tato'tuṣyat sahastrākṣastena kāmena me vibhuḥ
|
evaṃ me vasato rājanneṣa kālo'tyagāddivi||61||

62kṛtāstramativiśvastamatha māṃ harivāhanaḥ
|
saṃspṛśya mūrdhni pāṇibhyāmidaṃ vacanamabravīt||62||

63na tvamadya yudhā jetuṃ śakyaḥ suragaṇairapi
|
kiṃ punarmānuṣe loke mānuṣairakṛtātmabhiḥ||63||

64aprameyo'pradhṛṣyaśca yuddheṣvapratimastathā
|
athā'bravīt punardevaḥ samprahṛṣṭatanūruhaḥ||64||

65astrayuddhe samo vīra na te kaścidbhaviṣyati
|
apramattaḥ sadā dakṣaḥ satyavādī jitendriyaḥ||65||

66brahmaṇyaścāstraviccāsi śūraścāsi kurūdvaha
|
astrāṇi samavāptāni tvayā daśa ca pañca ca||66||

67pañcabhirvidhibhiḥ pārtha vidyate na tvayā samaḥ
|
prayogamupasaṃhāramāvṛttiṃ ca dhanañjaya||67||

68prāyaścittaṃ ca vettha tvaṃ pratīghātaṃ ca sarvaśaḥ
|
tato gurvarthakālo'yaṃ samutpannaḥ parantapa||68||

69pratijānīṣva taṃ kartuṃ tato vetsyāmyahaṃ param
|
tato'hamabruvaṃ rājan devarājamidaṃ vacaḥ||69||

70viṣahyaṃ yanmayā kartuṃ kṛtameva nibodha tat
|
tato māmabravīdrājan prahasan balavṛtrahā||70||

71nāviṣahyaṃ tavādyāsti triṣu lokeṣu kiṃcana
|
nivātakavacā nāma dānavā mama śatravaḥ||71||

72samudrakukṣimāśritya durge prativasantyuta
|
tisraḥ koṭyaḥ samākhyātāstulyarūpabalaprabhāḥ||72||

73tāṃstatra jahi kaunteya gurvarthaste bhaviṣyati
|
tato mātalisaṃyuktaṃ mayūrasamaromabhiḥ||73||

74hayairupetaṃ prādānme rathaṃ divyaṃ mahāprabham
|
babandha caiva me mūrdhni kirīṭamida‌muttamam||74||

75svarūpasadṛśaṃ caiva prādādaṅgavibhūṣaṇam
|
abhedyaṃ kavacaṃ cedaṃ sparśarūpavaduttamam||75||

76ajarāṃ jyāmimāṃ cāpi gāṇḍīve samayojayat
|
tataḥ prāyāmahaṃ tena syandanena virājatā||76||

77yenā'jayaddevapatirbaliṃ vairocaniṃ purā
|
tato devāḥ sarva eva tena ghoṣeṇa bodhitāḥ||77||

78manvānā devarājaṃ māṃ samājagmurviśāmpate
|
dṛṣṭvā ca māmapṛcchanta kiṃ kariṣyasi phālguna||78||

79tānabruvaṃ yathābhūtamidaṃ kartā'smi saṃyuge
|
nivātakavacānāṃ tu prasthitaṃ tāṃ vadhaiṣiṇam||79||

80nibodhata mahābhāgāḥ śivaṃ cāśāsta me'naghāḥ
|
tuṣṭuvurmāṃ prasannāste yathā devaṃ purandaram||80||

81rathenānena maghavā jitavān śambaraṃ yudhi
|
namuciṃ balavṛtrau ca prahrādanarakāvapi||81||

82bahūni ca sahasrāṇi prayutānyarbudānyapi
|
rathenānena daityānāṃ jitavān maghavā yudhi||82||

83tvamapyanena kaunteya nivātakavacān raṇe
|
vijetā yudhi vikramya pureva maghavā vaśī||83||

84ayaṃ ca śaṅkhapravaro yena jetā'si dānavān
|
anena vijitā lokāḥ śakreṇāpi mahātmanā||84||

85pradīyamānaṃ devaistaṃ devadattaṃ jalodbhavam
|
pratyagṛhṇaṃ jayāyainaṃ stūyamānastadāmaraiḥ||85||

86sa śaṅkhī kavacī bāṇī pragṛhītaśarāsanaḥ
|
dānavālayamatyugraṃ prayāto'smi yuyutsayā||86||
