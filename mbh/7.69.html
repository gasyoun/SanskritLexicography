Mahābhārata 7.69 (Droṇaparvan)

1nārada uvāca| pṛthuṃ vainyaṃ ca rājānaṃ mṛtaṃ sṛñjaya śuśruma
|
yamabhyaṣiñcan sāmrājye rājasūye maharṣayaḥ ||1||

2yatnataḥ prathitetyūcuḥ sarvānabhibhavan pṛthuḥ
|
kṣatānnastrāsyate sarvānityevaṃ kṣattriyo'bhavat ||2||

3pṛthuṃ vainyaṃ prajā dṛṣṭvā raktāḥ smeti yadabruvan
|
tato rājeti nāmāsya anurāgāda‌jāyata ||3||

4akṛṣṭapacyā pṛthivī āsīdvainyasya kāmadhuk
|
sarvāḥ kāmadughā gāvaḥ puṭake puṭake madhu ||4||

5āsan hiraṇmayā darbhāḥ sukhasparśāḥ sukhāvahāḥ
|
teṣāṃ cīrāṇi saṃvītāḥ prajāsteṣveva śerate ||5||

6phalānyamṛtakalpāni svādūni ca madhūni ca
|
teṣāmāsīttadāhāro nirāhārāśca nābhavan ||6||

7arogāḥ sarvasiddhārthā manuṣyā hyakutobhayāḥ
|
nyavasanta yathākāmaṃ vṛkṣeṣu ca guhāsu ca ||7||

8pravibhāgo na rāṣṭrāṇāṃ purāṇāṃ cābhavattadā
|
yathāsukhaṃ yathākāmaṃ tathaitā muditāḥ prajāḥ ||8||

9tasya saṃstambhitā hyāpaḥ samudramabhiyāsyataḥ
|
parvatāśca dadurmārgaṃ dhvajabhaṅgaśca nābhavat ||9||

10taṃ vanaspatayaḥ śailā devāsuranaroragāḥ
|
saptarṣayaḥ puṇyajanā gandharvāpsaraso'pi ca ||10||

11pitaraśca sukhāsīnamabhigamyedamabruvan
|
samrāḍasikṣatriyo'si rājā goptā pitāsi naḥ ||11||

12dehyasmabhyaṃ mahārāja prabhuḥ sannīpsitān varān
|
yairvayaṃ śāśvatīstṛptīrvartayiṣyāmahe sukham ||12||

13tathetyuktvā pṛthurvainyo gṛhītvā''jagavaṃ dhanuḥ
|
śarāṃścāpratimān ghorāṃścintayitvā'bravīnmahīm ||13||

14ehyehi vasudhe kṣipraṃ kṣaraibhyaḥ kāṃkṣitaṃ payaḥ
|
tato dāsyāmi bhadraṃ te annaṃ yasya yathepsitam ||14||

15vasudhovāca
|
duhitṛtvena māṃ vīra saṅkalpayitumarhasi
|
tathetyuktvā pṛthuḥ sarvaṃ vidhānamakarodvaśī ||15||

16tato bhūtanikāyāstāṃ vasudhāṃ duduhustadā
|
tāṃ vanaspatayaḥ pūrvaṃ samuttasthurdudhukṣavaḥ ||16||

17sā'tiṣṭhadvatsalā vatsaṃ dogdhṛpātrāṇi cecchatī
|
vatso'bhūt puṣpitaḥ śālaḥ plakṣo dogdhā'bhavat tadā ||17||

18chinnaprarohaṇaṃ dugdhaṃ pātramaudumbaraṃ śubham
|
udayaḥ parvato vatso merurdogdhā mahāgiriḥ ||18||

19ratnānyoṣadhayo dugdhaṃ pātramaśmamayaṃ tathā
|
dogdhā cāsīttadā devo dugdhamūrjaskaraṃ priyam ||19||

20asurā duduhurmāyāmāmapātre tu te tadā
|
dogdhā dvimūrdhā tatrāsīdvatsaścāsīdvirocanaḥ ||20||

21kṛṣiṃ ca sasyaṃ ca narā duduhuḥ pṛthivītale
|
svāyaṃbhuvo manurvatsasteṣāṃ dogdhā'bhavat pṛthuḥ ||21||

22alābupātre ca tathā viṣaṃ dugdhā vasundharā
|
dhṛtarāṣṭro'bhavaddogdhā teṣāṃ vatsastu takṣakaḥ ||22||

23saptarṣibhirbrahma dugdhā tathā cākliṣṭakarmabhiḥ
|
dogdhā bṛhaspatiḥ pātraṃ chando vatsaśca somarāṭ ||23||

24antardhānaṃ cāmapātre dugdhā puṇyajanairvirāṭ
|
dogdhā vaiśravaṇasteṣāṃ vatsaścāsīdvṛṣadhvajaḥ ||24||

25puṇyagandhān padmapātre gandharvāpsaraso'duhan
|
vatsaścitrarathasteṣāṃ dogdhā viśvaruciḥ prabhuḥ ||25||

26svadhāṃ rajatapātreṣu duduhuḥ pitaraśca tām
|
vatso vaivasvatasteṣāṃ yamo dogdhā'ntakastadā ||26||

27evaṃ nikāyaistairdugdhā payo'bhīṣṭaṃ hi sā virāṭ
|
yairvartayanti te hyadya pātrairvatsaiśca nityaśaḥ ||27||

28yajñaiśca vividhairiṣṭvā pṛthuvainyaḥ pratāpavān
|
santarpayitvā bhūtāni sarvaiḥ kāmairmanaḥpriyaiḥ ||28||

29hairaṇyānakarodrājā ye kecit pārthivā bhuvi
|
tān brāhmaṇebhyaḥ prāyacchadaśvamedhe mahāmakhe ||29||

30ṣaṣṭināgasahasrāṇi ṣaṣṭināgaśatāni ca
|
sauvarṇānakarodrājā brāhmaṇebhyaśca tān dadau ||30||

31imāṃ ca pṛthivīṃ sarvāṃ maṇiratnavibhūṣitām
|
sauvarṇīmakarodrājā brāhmaṇebhyaśca tāṃ dadau||31||

32sa cenmamāra sṛñjaya caturbhadratarastvayā
|
putrāt puṇyatarastubhyaṃ mā putramanutapyathāḥ ||32||

33ayajvānamadākṣiṇyamabhiśvaityeti vyāharan ||33||
