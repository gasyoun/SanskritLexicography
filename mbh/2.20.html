Mahābhārata 2.20 (Sabhāparvan)

1vāsudeva uvāca|
patitau haṃsaḍibhikau kaṃsaśca sagaṇo hataḥ
|
jarāsandhasya nidhane kālo'yaṃ samupāgataḥ||1||

2na śakyo'sau raṇe jetuṃ sarvairapi surāsuraiḥ
|
prāṇayuddhena jetavyaḥ sa ityupalabhāmahe||2||

3mayi nītirbalaṃ bhīme rakṣitā cāvayorjayaḥ
|
māgadhaṃ sādhayiṣyāma iṣṭiṃ traya ivāgnayaḥ||3||

4tribhirāsādito'smābhirvijane sa narādhipaḥ
|
na sandeho yathā yuddhamekenāpyupayāsyati||4||

5avamānācca lobhācca bāhuvīryācca darpitaḥ
|
bhīmasenena yuddhāya dhruvamapyupayāsyati||5||

6alaṃ tasya mahābāhurbhīmaseno mahābalaḥ
|
lokasya samudīrṇasya nidhanāyāntako yathā||6||

7yadi me hṛdayaṃ vetsi yadi te pratyayo mayi
|
bhīmasenārjunau śīghraṃ nyāsabhūtau prayaccha me||7||

8evamukto bhagavatā pratyuvāca yudhiṣṭhiraḥ
|
bhīmārjunau samālokya samprahṛṣṭamukhau sthitau||8||

9yudhiṣṭhara uvāca|
acyutācyuta māmaivaṃ vyāharāmitrakarśana
|
pāṇḍavānāṃ bhavānnātho bhavantaṃ cāśritā vayam||9||

10yathā vadasi govinda sarvaṃ tadupapadyate
|
na hi tvamagratasteṣāṃ yeṣāṃ lakṣmīḥ parāṅmukhī||10||

11nihataśca jarāsandho mokṣitāśca mahīkṣitaḥ
|
rājasūyaśca me labdho nideśe tava tiṣṭhataḥ||11||

12kṣiprameva yathā tvetatkāryaṃ samupapadyate
|
apramatto jagannātha tathā kuru narottama||12||

13tribhirbhavadbhirhi vinā nāhaṃ jīvitumutsahe
|
dharmakāmārtharahito rogārta iva duḥkhitaḥ||13||

14na śauriṇā vinā pārtho na śauriḥ pāṇḍavaṃ vinā
|
nājeyostyanayorloke kṛṣṇayoriti me matiḥ||14||

15ayaṃ ca balināṃ śreṣṭhaḥ śrīmānapi vṛkodaraḥ
|
yuvābhyāṃ sahito vīra kiṃ na kuryānmahāyaśāḥ||15||

16supraṇīto balaugho hi kurute kāryamuttamam
|
andhaṃ balaṃ jaḍaṃ prāhuḥ praṇetavyaṃ vicakṣaṇaiḥ||16||

17yato hi nimnaṃ bhavati nayanti hi tato jalam
|
yataśchidraṃ tataścāpi nayante dhīvarā jalam||17||

18tasmānnayavidhānajñaṃ puruṣaṃ lokaviśrutam
|
vayamāśritya govindaṃ yatāmaḥ kāryasiddhaye||18||

19evaṃ prajñānayabalaṃ kriyopāyasamanvitam
|
puraskṛrvīta kāryeṣu kṛṣṇaṃ kāryārthasiddhaye||19||

20evameva yaduśreṣṭha yāvatkāryāthisiddhaye
|
arjunaḥ kṛṣṇamanvetu bhīmo'nvetu dhanañjayam
|
nayo jayo balaṃ caiva vikrame siddhimeṣyati||20||

21vaiśampāyana uvāca|
evamuktāstataḥ sarve bhrātaro vipulaujasaḥ
|
vārṣṇeyaḥ pāṇḍaveyau ca pratasthurmāgadhaṃ prati||21||

22varcasvināṃ brāhmaṇānāṃ snātakānāṃ paricchadaiḥ
|
ācchādya suhṛdāṃ vākyairmanojñairabhinanditāḥ||22||

23amarṣādabhitaptānāṃ jñātyarthaṃ mukhyatejasām
|
ravisomāgnivapuṣāṃ dīptamāsīttadā vapuḥ||23||

24itaṃ mene jarāsandhaṃ dṛṣṭvā bhīmapurogamau
|
ekakāryasamudyantau kṛṣṇau yuddhe'parājitau||24||

25īśau hi tau mahātmānau sarvakāryapravartinau
|
dharmakāmārthalokānāṃ kāryāṇāṃ ca pravartakau||25||

26kurubhyaḥ prasthitāste tu madhyena kurujāṅgalam
|
ramyaṃ padmasaro gatvā kālakūḍamatītya ca||26||

27gaṇḍakīṃ ca mahāśoṇaṃ sadānīrāṃ tathaiva ca
|
ekaparvatake nadyaḥ krameṇaityāvrajanta te||27||

28uttīrya sarayūṃ ramyāṃ dṛṣṭvā pūrvāṃśca kosalān
|
atītya jagmurmithilāṃ mālāṃ carmaṇvatīṃ nadīm||28||

29atītya gaṅgāṃ śoṇaṃ ca trayaste prāṅmukhāstadā
|
kuśacīracchadā jagmurmāgadhaṃ kṣetramacyutāḥ||29||

30te śaśvadgodhanākīrṇamambumantaṃ śubhadrumam
|
gorathaṃ girimāsādya dadṛśurmāgadhaṃ puram||30||
