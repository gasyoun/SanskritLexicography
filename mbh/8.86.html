Mahābhārata 8.86 (Karṇaparvan)

1sañjaya uvāca|
tamāyāntamabhiprekṣya velodvṛttamivārṇavam
|
garjantaṃ sumahākāyaṃ durnivāraṃ surairapi ||1||

2arjunaṃ prāha dāśārhaḥ prahasya puruṣarṣabhaḥ
|
ayaṃ saratha āyāti śvetāśvaḥ śalyasārathiḥ ||2||

3yena te saha yoddhavyaṃ sthiro bhava dhanañjaya
|
paśya cainaṃ samāyuktaṃ rathaṃ karṇasya pāṇḍava ||3||

4śvetavājisamāyuktaṃ yuktaṃ rādhāsutena ca
|
nānāpatākākalilaṃ kiṅkiṇījālamālinam ||4||

5uhyamānamivākāśe vimānaṃ pāṇḍurairhayaiḥ
|
dhvajaṃ ca paśya karṇasya nāgakakṣaṃ mahātmanaḥ ||5||

6ākhaṇḍaladhanuḥprakhyamullikhantamivāmbaram
|
paśya karṇaṃ samāyāntaṃ dhārtarāṣṭrapriyaiṣiṇam ||6||

7śaradhārā vimuñcantaṃ dhārāsāramivāmbudam
|
eṣa madreśvaro rājā rathāgre paryavasthitaḥ ||7||

8niyacchati hayānasya rādheyasyāmitaujasaḥ
|
śṛṇu dundubhinirghoṣaṃ śaṅkhaśabdaṃ ca dāruṇam ||8||

9siṃhanādāṃśca vividhāñśṛṇu pāṇḍava sarvataḥ
|
antardhāya mahāśabdān karṇenāmitatejasā ||9||

10dodhūyamānasya bhṛśaṃ dhanuṣaḥ śṛṇu niḥsvanam
|
ete dīryanti sagaṇāḥ pañcālānāṃ mahārathāḥ ||10||

11dṛṣṭvā kesariṇaṃ kruddhaṃ mṛgā iva mahāvane
|
sarvayatnena kaunteya hantumarhasi sūtajam ||11||

12na hi karṇaśarānanyaḥ soḍhumutsahate naraḥ
|
sadevāsuragandharvāṃstrīlloঁkānsacarācarān ||12||

13tvaṃ hi jetuṃ raṇe śaktastathaiva viditaṃ mama
|
bhīmamugraṃ mahātmānaṃ tryakṣaṃ śarvaṃ kapardinam ||13||

14na śaktā draṣṭumīśānaṃ kiṃ punaryodhituṃ prabhum
|
tvayā sākṣānmahādevaḥ sarvabhūtaśivaḥ śivaḥ ||14||

15yuddhenārādhitaḥ sthāṇurdevāśca varadāstava
|
tasya pārtha prasādena devadevasya śūlinaḥ ||15||

16jahi karṇaṃ mahābāho namuciṃ vṛtrahā yathā
|
śreyaste'stu sadā pārtha yuddhe jayamavāpnuhi ||16||

17arjuna uvāca|
dhruva eva jayaḥ kṛṣṇa mama nāstyatra saṃśayaḥ
|
sarvalokagururyastvaṃ tuṣṭo'si madhusūdana ||17||

18codayāśvān hṛṣīkeśa rathaṃ mama mahāratha
|
nāhatvā samare karṇaṃ nivartiṣyati phālgunaḥ ||18||

19adya karṇaṃ hataṃ paśya maccharaiḥ śakalīkṛtam
|
māṃ vā drakṣyasi govinda karṇena nihataṃ śaraiḥ ||19||

20upasthitamidaṃ ghoraṃ yuddhaṃ trailokyamohanam
|
yajjanāḥ kathayiṣyanti yāvadbhūmirdhariṣyati ||20||

21evaṃ bruvaṃstadā pārthaḥ kṛṣṇamakliṣṭakāriṇam
|
pratyudyayau rathenāśu gajaṃ pratigajo yathā ||21||

22punarapyāha tejasvī pārthaḥ kṛṣṇamarindama
|
codayāśvān hṛṣīkeśa kālo'yamativartate
|
evamuktastadā tena pāṇḍavena mahātmanā ||22||

23jayena saṃpūjya sa pāṇḍavaṃ tadā pracodayāmāsa hayānmanojavān
|
sa pāṇḍuputrasya ratho manojavaḥ kṣaṇena karṇasya rathāgrato'bhavat ||23||
