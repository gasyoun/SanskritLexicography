Mahābhārata 7.202 (Droṇaparvan)

1dhṛtarāṣṭra uvāca|
tasminnatirathe droṇe nihate pārṣatena vai
|
māmakāḥ pāṇḍavāścaiva kimakurvannataḥ param||1||

2sañjaya uvāca|
tasminnatirathe droṇe nihate pārṣatena vai
|
kauraveṣu ca bhagneṣu kuntīputro dhanañjayaḥ||2||

3dṛṣṭvā sumahadāścaryamātmano vijayāvaham
|
yadṛcchayā''gataṃ vyāsaṃ papraccha bharatarṣabha||3||

4arjuna uvāca|
saṃgrāme nyahanaṃ śatrūñśaraughairvimalairaham
|
agrato lakṣaye yāntaṃ puruṣaṃ pāvakaprabham||4||

5jvalantaṃ śūlamudyamya yāṃ diśaṃ pratipadyate
|
tasyāṃ diśi vidīryante śatravo me mahāmune||5||

6tena bhagnānarīnsarvānmadbhagnānmanyate janaḥ
|
tena bhagnāni sainyāni pṛṣṭhato'nuvrajāmyaham||6||

7bhagavaṃstanmamācakṣva ko vai sa puruṣottamaḥ
|
śūlapāṇirmayā dṛṣṭastejasā sūryasannibhaḥ||7||

8na padbhyāṃ spṛśate bhūmiṃ na ca śūlaṃ vimuñcati
|
śūlācchūlasahasrāṇi niṣpetustasya tejasā||8||

9vyāsa uvāca|
prajāpatīnāṃ prathamaṃ taijasaṃ puruṣaṃ prabhum
|
bhuvanaṃ bhūrbhuvaṃ devaṃ sarvalokeśvaraṃ prabhum||9||

10īśānaṃ varadaṃ pārtha dṛṣṭavānasi śaṃkaram
|
taṃ gaccha śaraṇaṃ devaṃ varadaṃ bhuvaneśvaram||10||

11mahādevaṃ mahātmānamīśānaṃ jaṭilaṃ vibhum
|
tryakṣaṃ mahābhujaṃ rudraṃ śikhinaṃ cīravāsasam||11||

12mahādevaṃ haraṃ sthāṇuṃ varadaṃ bhuvaneśvaram
|
jagatpradhānamajitaṃ jagatprītimadhīśvaram||12||

13jagadyoniṃ jagadbījaṃ jayinaṃ jagato gatim
|
viśvātmānaṃ viśvasṛjaṃ viśvamūrtiṃ yaśasvinam||13||

14viśveśvaraṃ viśvanaraṃ karmaṇāmīśvaraṃ prabhum
|
śambhuṃ svayaṃbhuṃ bhūteśaṃ bhūtabhavyabhavodbhavam||14||

15yogaṃ yogeśvaraṃ sarvaṃ sarvalokeśvareśvaram
|
sarvaśreṣṭhaṃ jagacchreṣṭhaṃ variṣṭhaṃ parameṣṭhinam||15||

16lokatrayavidhātāramekaṃ lokatrayāśrayam
|
śuddhātmānaṃ bhavaṃ bhīmaṃ śaśāṅkakṛtaśekharam||16||

17śāśvataṃ bhūdharaṃ devaṃ sarvavāgīśvareśvaram
|
sudurjayaṃ jagannāthaṃ janma-mṛtyu-jarātigam||17||

18jñānātmānaṃ jñānagamyaṃ jñānaśreṣṭhaṃ sudurvidam
|
dātāraṃ caiva bhaktānāṃ prasādavihitānvarān||18||

19tasya pāriṣadā divyā rūpairnānāvidhairvibhoḥ
|
vāmanā jaṭilā muṇḍā hrasvagrīvā mahodarāḥ||19||

20mahākāyā mahotsāhā mahākarṇāstathā'pare
|
ānanairvikṛtaiḥ pādaiḥ pārtha veṣaiśca vaikṛtaiḥ||20||

21īdṛśaiḥ sa mahādevaḥ pūjyamāno maheśvaraḥ
|
sa śivastāta tejasvī prasādādyāti te'grataḥ||21||

22tasminghore sadā pārtha saṃgrāme romaharṣaṇe
|
drauṇi-karṇa-kṛpairguptāṃ maheṣvāsaiḥ prahāribhiḥ||22||

23kastāṃ senāṃ tadā pārtha manasā'pi pradharṣayet
|
ṛte devānmaheṣvāsādbahurūpānmaheśvarāt||23||

24sthātumutsahate kaścinna tasminnagrataḥ sthite
|
na hi bhūtaṃ samaṃ tena triṣu lokeṣu vidyate||24||

25gandhenāpi hi saṃgrāme tasya kruddhasya śatravaḥ
|
visaṃjñā hatabhūyiṣṭhā vepanti ca patanti ca||25||

26tasmai namastu kurvanto devāstiṣṭhanti vai divi
|
ye cānye mānavā loke ye ca svargajito narāḥ||26||

27ye bhaktā varadaṃ devaṃ śivaṃ rudramumāpatim
|
ananyabhāvena sadā sarveśaṃ samupāsate||27||

28ihaloke sukhaṃ prāpya te yāṃti paramāṃ gatim
|
namaskuruṣva kaunteya tasmai śāntāya vai sadā||28||

29rudrāya śitikaṇṭhāya kaniṣṭhāya suvarcase
|
kapardine karālāya haryakṣavaradāya ca||29||

30yāmyāyāvyaktakeśāya sadvṛtte śaṃkarāya ca
|
kāmyāya harinetrāya sthāṇave puruṣāya ca||30||

31harikeśāya muṇḍāya kṛśāyottāraṇāya ca
|
bhāskarāya sutīrthāya devadevāya raṃhase||31||

32bahurūpāya sarvāya priyāya priyavāsase
|
upṇīṣiṇe suvaktrāya sahasrākṣāya mīḍhuṣe||32||

33giriśāya praśāntāya pataye cīravāsase
|
hiraṇyabāhave rājannugrāya pataye diśām||33||

34parjanyapataye caiva bhūtānāṃ pataye namaḥ
|
vṛkṣāṇāṃ pataye caiva gavāṃ ca pataye namaḥ||34||

35vṛkṣairāvṛtakāyāya senānye madhyamāya ca
|
sruvahastāya devāya dhanvine bhārgavāya ca||35||

36bahurūpāya viśvasya pataye muñjavāsase
|
sahasraśirase caiva sahasranayanāya ca||36||

37sahasrabāhave caiva sahasracaraṇāya ca
|
śaraṇaṃ gaccha kaunteya varadaṃ bhuvaneśvaram||37||

38umāpatiṃ virūpākṣaṃ dakṣayajñanibarhaṇam
|
prajānāṃ patimavyagraṃ bhūtānāṃ patimavyayam||38||

39kapardinaṃ vṛṣāvartaṃ vṛṣanābhaṃ vṛṣadhvajam
|
vṛṣadarpaṃ vṛṣapatiṃ vṛṣaśṛṅgaṃ vṛṣarṣabham||39||

40vṛṣāṅkaṃ vṛṣabhodāraṃ vṛṣabhaṃ vṛṣabhekṣaṇam
|
vṛṣāyudhaṃ vṛṣaśaraṃ vṛṣabhūtaṃ vṛṣeśvaram||40||

41mahodaraṃ mahākāyaṃ dvīpicarmanivāsinam
|
lokeśaṃ varadaṃ muṇḍaṃ brahmaṇyaṃ brāhmaṇapriyam||41||

42triśūlapāṇiṃ varadaṃ khaḍgacarmadharaṃ prabhum
|
pinākinaṃ khaḍgadharaṃ lokānāṃ patimīśvaram||42||

43prapadye śaraṇaṃ devaṃ śaraṇyaṃ cīravāsasam
|
namastasmai sureśāya yasya vaiśravaṇaḥ sakhā||43||

44suvāsase namastubhyaṃ suvratāya sudhanvine
|
dhanurdharāya devāya priyadhanvāya dhanvine||44||

45dhanvantarāya dhanuṣe dhanvācāryāya te namaḥ
|
ugrāyudhāya devāya namaḥ suravarāya ca||45||

46namo'stu bahurūpāya namo'stu bahudhanvine
|
namo'stu sthāṇave nityaṃ namastasmai tapasvine||46||

47namo'stu tripuraghnāya bhagaghnāya ca vai namaḥ
|
vanaspatīnāṃ pataye narāṇāṃ pataye namaḥ||47||

48mātṝṇāṃ pataye caiva gaṇānāṃ pataye namaḥ
|
gavāṃ ca pataye nityaṃ yajñānāṃ pataye namaḥ||48||

49apāṃ ca pataye nityaṃ devānāṃ pataye namaḥ
|
pūṣṇo dantavināśāya tryakṣāya varadāya ca||49||

50nīlakaṇṭhāya piṅgāya svarṇakeśāya vai namaḥ
|
karmāṇi yāni divyāni mahādevasya dhīmataḥ||50||

51tāni te kīrtayiṣyāmi yathāprajñaṃ yathāśrutam
|
na surā nāsurā loke na gandharvā na rākṣasāḥ||51||

52sukhamedhanti kupite tasminnapi guhāgatāḥ
|
dakṣasya yajamānasya vidhivatsaṃbhṛtaṃ purā||52||

53vivyādha kupito yajñaṃ nirdeyastvabhavattadā
|
dhanuṣā bāṇamutsṛjya saghoṣaṃ vinanāda ca||53||

54te na śarma kutaḥ śāntiṃ lebhire sma surāstadā
|
vidrute sahasā yajñe kupite ca maheśvare||54||

55tena jyātalaghoṣeṇa sarve lokāḥ samākulāḥ
|
babhūvurvaśagāḥ pārtha nipetuśca surāsurāḥ||55||

56āpaścukṣubhire sarvāścakaṃpe ca vasundharā
|
parvatāśca vyaśīryanta diśo nāgāśca mohitāḥ||56||

57andhena tamasā lokā na prākāśanta saṃvṛtāḥ
|
jaghnivānsaha sūryeṇa sarveṣāṃ jyotiṣāṃ prabhāḥ||57||

58cukṣubhurbhayabhītāśca śāntiṃ cakrustathaiva ca
|
ṛṣayaḥ sarvabhūtānāmātmanaśca sukhaiṣiṇaḥ||58||

59pūṣāṇamabhyadravata śaṃkaraḥ prahasanniva
|
puroḍāśaṃ bhakṣayato daśanān vai vyaśātayut||59||

60tato niścakramurdevā vepamānā natāḥ sma te
|
punaśca saṃdadhe dīptāndevānāṃ niśitāñśarān||60||

61sadhūmānsasphuliṅgāṃśca vidyuttoyadasannibhān
|
taṃ dṛṣṭvā tu surāḥ sarve praṇipatya maheśvaram||61||

62rudrasya yajñabhāgaṃ ca viśiṣṭaṃ te tvakalpayan
|
bhayena tridaśā rājañcharaṇaṃ ca prapedire||62||

63tena caivātikopena sa yajñaḥ sandhitastadā
|
bhagnāścāpi surā āsan bhītāścādyāpi taṃ prati||63||

64asurāṇāṃ purāṇyāsaṃstrīṇi vīryavatāṃ divi
|
āyasaṃ rājataṃ caiva sauvarṇaṃ paramaṃ mahat||64||

65sauvarṇaṃ kamalākṣasya tārakākṣasya rājatam
|
tṛtīyaṃ tu puraṃ teṣāṃ vidyunmālina āyasam||65||

66na śaktastāni maghavān bhettuṃ sarvāyudhairapi
|
atha sarve surā rudraṃ jagmuḥ śaraṇamarditāḥ||66||

67te tamūcurmahātmānaṃ sarve devāḥ savāsavāḥ
|
brahmadattavarā hyete ghorāstripuravāsinaḥ||67||

68pīḍayantyadhikaṃ lokaṃ yasmātte varadarpitāḥ
|
tvadṛte devadeveśa nānyaḥ śaktaḥ kathañcana||68||

69hantuṃ daityānmahādeva jahi tāṃstvaṃ suradviṣaḥ
|
rudra raudrā bhaviṣyanti paśavaḥ sarvakarmasu||69||

70nipātayiṣyase caitānasurān bhuvaneśvara
|
sa tathoktastathetyuktvā devānāṃ hitakāmyayā||70||

71gandhamādanavindhyau ca kṛtvā vaṃśadhvajau haraḥ
|
pṛthvīṃ sasāgaravanāṃ rathaṃ kṛtvā tu śaṅkaraḥ||71||

72akṣaṃ kṛtvā tu nāgendraṃ śeṣaṃ nāma trilocanaḥ
|
cakre kṛtvā tu candrārkau devadevaḥ pinākadhṛk||72||

73aṇī kṛtvailapatraṃ ca puṣpadantaṃ ca tryambakaḥ
|
yūpaṃ kṛtvā tu malayamavanāhaṃ ca takṣakam||73||

74yoktrāṃgāni ca sattvāni kṛtvā śarvaḥ pratāpavān
|
vedān kṛtvā'tha caturaścaturaśvānmaheśvaraḥ||74||

75upavedān khalīnāṃśca kṛtvā lokatrayeśvaraḥ
|
gāyatrīṃ pragrahaṃ kṛtvā sāvitrīṃ ca maheśvaraḥ||75||

76kṛtvoṅkāraṃ pratodaṃ ca brahmāṇaṃ caiva sārathim
|
gāṇḍīvaṃ mandaraṃ kṛtvā guṇaṃ kṛtvā tu vāsukim||76||

77viṣṇuṃ śarottamaṃ kṛtvā śalyamagniṃ tathaiva ca
|
vāyuṃ kṛtvā'tha vājābhyāṃ puṅkhe vaivasvataṃ yamam||77||

78vidyutkṛtvā'tha niśrāṇaṃ meruṃ kṛtvātha vai dhvajam
|
āruhya sa rathaṃ divyaṃ sarvadevamayaṃ śivaḥ||78||

79tripurasya vadhārthāya sthāṇuḥ praharatāṃ varaḥ
|
asurāṇāmantakaraḥ śrīmānatulavikramaḥ||79||

80stūyamānaḥ suraiḥ pārtha ṛṣibhiśca tapodhanaiḥ
|
sthānaṃ māheśvaraṃ kṛtvā divyamapratimaṃ prabhuḥ||80||

81atiṣṭhatsthāṇubhūtaḥ sa sahasraṃ parivatsarān
|
yadā trīṇi sametāni antarikṣe purāṇi ca||81||

82triparvaṇā triśalyena tadā tāni bibheda saḥ
|
purāṇi na ca taṃ śekurdānavāḥ prativīkṣitum||82||

83śaraṃ kālāgnisaṃyuktaṃ viṣṇusomasamāyutam
|
purāṇi dagdhavantaṃ taṃ devī yātā pravīkṣitum||83||

84bālamaṅkagataṃ kṛtvā svayaṃ pañcaśikhaṃ punaḥ
|
umā jijñāsamānā vai ko'yamityabravītsurān||84||

85asūyataśca śakrasya vajreṇa prahariṣyataḥ
|
bāhuṃ savajraṃ taṃ tasya kruddhasyāstaṃbhayatprabhuḥ||85||

86prahasya bhagavāṃstūrṇaṃ sarvalokeśvaro vibhuḥ
|
tataḥ sa staṃbhitabhujaḥ śakro devagaṇairvṛtaḥ||86||

87jagāma sasurastūrṇaṃ brahmāṇaṃ prabhumavyayam
|
te taṃ praṇamya śirasā procuḥ prāñjalayastadā||87||

88kimapyaṅkagataṃ brahman pārvatyā bhūtamadbhūtam
|
bālarūpadharaṃ dṛṣṭvā nāsmābhirabhilakṣitaḥ||88||

89tasmāttvāṃ praṣṭumicchāmo nirjitā yena vai vayam
|
ayudhyatā hi bālena līlayā sapuraṃdarāḥ||89||

90teṣāṃ tadvacanaṃ śrutvā brahmā brahmavidāṃ varaḥ
|
dhyātvā sa śaṃbhuṃ bhagavānbālaṃ cāmitatejasam||90||

91uvāca bhagavānbrahmā śakrādīṃśca surottamān
|
carācarasya jagataḥ prabhuḥ sa bhagavānharaḥ||91||

92tasmātparataraṃ nānyatkiṃcidasti maheśvarāt
|
yo dṛṣṭo hyumayā sārdhaṃ yuṣmābhiramitadyutiḥ||92||

93sa pārvatyāḥ kṛte śarvaḥ kṛtavānbālarūpatām
|
te mayā sahitā yūyaṃ prāpayadhvaṃ tameva hi||93||

94sa eṣa bhagavāndevaḥ sarvalokeśvaraḥ prabhuḥ
|
na saṃbubudhire cainaṃ devāstaṃ bhuvaneśvaram||94||

95saprajāpatayaḥ sarve bālārkasadṛśaprabham
|
athābhyetya tato brahmā dṛṣṭvā sa ca maheśvaram||95||

96ayaṃ śreṣṭha iti jñātvā vavande taṃ pitāmahaḥ
|
brahmovāca
|
tvaṃ yajño bhuvanasyāsya tvaṃ gatistvaṃ parāyaṇam||96||

97tvaṃ bhavastvaṃ mahādevastvaṃ dhāma paramaṃ padam
|
tvayā sarvamidaṃ vyāptaṃ jagatsthāvarajaṅgamam||97||

98bhagavanbhūtabhavyeśa lokanātha jagatpate
|
prasādaṃ kuru śakrasya tvayā krodhārditasya vai||98||

99vyāsa uvāca|
padmayonivacaḥ śrutvā tataḥ prīto maheśvaraḥ
|
prasādābhimukho bhūtvā aṭṭahāsamathākarot||99||

100tataḥ prasāda‌yāmāsurumāṃ rudraṃ ca te surāḥ
|
abhavacca punarbāhuryathāprakṛti vajriṇaḥ||100||

101teṣāṃ prasanno bhagavānsapatnīko vṛṣadhvajaḥ
|
devānāṃ tridaśaśreṣṭho dakṣayajñavināśanaḥ||101||

102sa vai rudraḥ sa ca śivaḥ so'gniḥ sarvaśca sarvavit
|
sa cendraścaiva vāyuśca so'śvinau ca sa vidyutaḥ||102||

103sa bhavaḥ sa ca parjanyo mahādevaḥ sanātanaḥ
|
sa caṃdramāḥ sa ceśānaḥ sa sūryo varuṇaśca saḥ||103||

104sa kālaḥ so'ntako mṛtyuḥ sa yamo rātryahāni tu
|
māsārdhamāsā ṛtavaḥ sandhye saṃvatsaraśca saḥ||104||

105dhātā ca sa vidhātā ca viśvātmā viśvakarmakṛt
|
sarvāsāṃ devatānāṃ ca dhārayatyavapurvapuḥ||105||

106sarvadevaiḥ stuto devaḥ saikadhā bahudhā ca saḥ
|
śatadhā sahasradhā caiva bhūyaḥ śatasahasradhā||106||

107dve tanū tasya devasya vedajñā brāhmaṇā viduḥ
|
ghorā cānyā śivā cānyā te tanū bahudhā punaḥ||107||

108ghorā tu yātudhānasya so'gnirviṣṇuḥ sa bhāskaraḥ
|
saumyā tu punarevāsya āpo jyotīṃṣi caṃdramāḥ||108||

109vedāḥ sāṅgopaniṣadaḥ purāṇādhyātmaniścayāḥ
|
yatra paramaṃ guhyaṃ sa vaiḥ devo maheśvaraḥ||109||

110īdṛśaśca mahādevo bhūyāṃśca bhagavānajaḥ
|
na hi sarve mayā śakyā vaktuṃ bhagavato guṇāḥ||110||

111api varṣasahasreṇa satataṃ pāṇḍunandana
|
sarvairgrahairgṛhītānvai sarvapāpasamanvitān||111||

112sa mocayati suprītaḥ śaraṇyaḥ śaraṇāgatān
|
āyurārogyamaiśvaryaṃ vittaṃ kāmāṃśca puṣkalān||112||

113sa dadāti manuṣyebhyaḥ sa caivākṣipate punaḥ
|
sendrādiṣu ca deveṣu tasya caiśvaryamucyate||113||

114sa caiva vyāpṛto loke manuṣyāṇāṃ śubhāśubhe
|
aiśvaryāccaiva kāmānāmīśvaraśca sa ucyate||114||

115maheśvaraśca mahatāṃ bhūtānāmīśvaraśca saḥ
|
bahubhirbahudhā rūpairviśvaṃ vyāpnoti vai jagat||115||

116tasya devasya yadvaktraṃ samudre tadadhiṣṭhitam
|
vaḍavāmukheti vikhyātaṃ pibattoyamayaṃ haviḥ||116||

117eṣa caiva śmaśāneṣu devo vasati nityaśaḥ
|
yajantyenaṃ janāstatra vīrasthāna itīśvaram||117||

118asya dīptāni rūpāṇi ghorāṇi ca bahūni ca
|
loke yānyasya pūjyante manuṣyāḥ pravadanti ca||118||

119nāmadheyāni lokeṣu bahūnyasya yathārthavat
|
nirucyante mahattvācca vibhutvātkarmaṇastathā||119||

120vede cāsya samāmnātaṃ śatarudriyamuttamam
|
nāmnā cānantarudreti hyupasthānaṃ mahātmanaḥ||120||

121sakāmānāṃ prabhurdevo ye divyā ye ca mānuṣāḥ
|
sa vibhuḥ sa prabhurdevo viśvaṃ vyāpnoti vai mahat||121||

122jyeṣṭhaṃ bhūtaṃ vadantyenaṃ brāhmaṇā sunayastathā
|
prathamo hyeṣa devānāṃ sukhādasthānalo'bhavat||122||

123sarvathā yatpaśūnpāti taiśca yadramate punaḥ
|
teṣāmadhipatiryacca tasmātpaśupatiḥ smṛtaḥ||123||

124divyaṃ ca brahmacaryeṇa liṅgamasya yathā sthitam
|
mahayatyeṣa lokāṃśca maheśvara iti smṛtaḥ||124||

125ṛṣayaścaiva devāśca gandharvāpsarasastathā
|
liṅgamasyārcayanti sma taccāpyūrdhvaṃ samāsthitam||125||

126pūjyamāne tatastasminmodate sa maheśvaraḥ
|
sukhī prītaśca bhavati prahṛṣṭaścaiva śaṅkaraḥ||126||

127yadasya bahudhā rūpaṃ bhūtabhavyabhavasthitam
|
sthāvaraṃ jaṅgamaṃ caiva bahurūpastataḥ smṛtaḥ||127||

128ekākṣo jājvalannāste sarvato'kṣimayo'pi vā
|
krodhādyaścāviśallokāṃstasmātsarva iti smṛtaḥ||128||

129dhūmrarūpaṃ ca yattasya dhūrjaṭistena cocyate
|
viśvedevāśca yattasminviśvarūpastataḥ smṛtaḥ||129||

130tisro devīryadā caiva bhajate bhuvaneśvaraḥ
|
dyāmapaḥ pṛthivīṃ caiva tryambakaśca tataḥ smṛtaḥ||130||

131sa medhayati yannityaṃ sarvārthānsarvakarmasu
|
śivamicchanmanuṣyāṇāṃ tasmādeṣa śivaḥ smṛtaḥ||131||

132sahasrākṣo'yutākṣo vā sarvato'kṣimayo'pi vā
|
yacca viśvaṃ mahat pāti mahādevastataḥ smṛtaḥ||132||

133mahatpūrvaṃ sthito yacca prāṇotpattisthitaśca yat
|
sthitaliṅgaśca yannityaṃ tasmātsthāṇuriti smṛtaḥ||133||

134sūryācandramasorloke prakāśante rucaśca yāḥ
|
tāḥ keśasaṃjñitāstryakṣe vyomakeśastataḥ smṛtaḥ||134||

135bhūtaṃ bhavyaṃ bhaviṣyaṃ ca sarvaṃ jagadaśeṣataḥ
|
bhava eva tato yasmādbhūtabhavyabhavodbhavaḥ||135||

136kapiḥ śreṣṭha iti prokto dharmaśca vṛṣa ucyate
|
sa devadevo bhagavānkīrtyate'to vṛṣākapiḥ||136||

137brahmāṇamindraṃ varuṇaṃ yamaṃ dhanadameva ca
|
nigṛhya harate yasmāttasmāddhara iti smṛtaḥ||137||

138nimīlitābhyāṃ netrābhyāṃ balāddevo maheśvaraḥ
|
lalāṭe netramasṛjattena tryakṣaḥ sa ucyate||138||

139viṣamasthaḥ śarīreṣu samaśca prāṇināmiha
|
sa vāyurviṣamastheṣu prāṇopānaḥ śarīriṣu||139||

140pūjayedvigrahaṃ yastu liṅgaṃ cāpi mahātmanaḥ
|
liṅgaṃ pūjayitā nityaṃ mahatīṃ śriyamaśnute||140||

141ūrubhyāmardhamāgneyaṃ somārdhaṃ ca śivā tanuḥ
|
ātmano'rdhaṃ tathā cāgniḥ somo'rdhaṃ punarucyate||141||

142taijasī mahatī dīptā devebhyo'sya śivā tanuḥ
|
bhāsvatī mānuṣeṣvasya tanurghorā'gnirucyate||142||

143brahmacaryaṃ caratyeṣa śivā yā'sya tanustayā
|
yā'sya ghoratarā mūrtiḥ sarvānatti tayeśvaraḥ||143||

144yannirdahati yattīkṣṇo yadugro yatpratāpavān
|
māṃsaśoṇitamajjādo yattato rudra ucyate||144||

145eṣa devo mahādevo yo'sau pārtha tavāgrataḥ
|
saṃgrāme śātravānnighnaṃstvayā dṛṣṭaḥ pinākadhṛk||145||

146sindhurājavadhārthāya pratijñāte tvayā'nagha
|
kṛṣṇena darśitaḥ svapne yastu śailendramūrdhani||146||

147eṣa vai bhagavāndevaḥ saṃgrāme yāti te'grataḥ
|
yena dattāni te'strāṇi yaistvayā dānavā hatāḥ||147||

148dhanyaṃ yaśasyamāyuṣyaṃ puṇyaṃ vedaiśca saṃmitam
|
devadevasya te pārtha vyākhyātaṃ śatarudriyam||148||

149sarvārthasādhanaṃ puṇyaṃ sarvakilviṣanāśanam
|
sarvapāpapraśamanaṃ sarvaduḥkhabhayāpaham||149||

150caturvidhamidaṃ stotraṃ yaḥ śṛṇoti naraḥ sadā
|
vijitya śatrūnsarvānsa rudraloke mahīyate||150||

151caritaṃ mahātmano nityaṃ sāṃgrāmikamidaṃ smṛtam
|
paṭhanvai śatarudrīyaṃ śṛṇvaṃśca satatotthitaḥ||151||

152bhakto viśveśvaraṃ devaṃ mānuṣeṣu ca yaḥ sadā
|
varāṃnkāmānsa labhate prasanne vyambake naraḥ||152||

153gaccha yuddhyasva kaunteya na tavāsti parājayaḥ
|
yasya mantrī ca goptā ca pārśvastho hi janārdanaḥ||153||

154sañjaya uvāca|
evamuktvā'rjunaṃ saṃkhye parāśarasutastadā
|
jagāma bharataśreṣṭha yathāgatamarindama||154||

155yuddhaṃ kṛtvā mahaddhoraṃ pañcāhāni mahābalaḥ
|
brāhmaṇo nihato rājan brahmalokamavāptavān||155||

156svadhīte yatphalaṃ vede tada‌sminnapi parvaṇi
|
kṣatriyāṇāmabhīrūṇāṃ yuktamatra mahadyaśaḥ||156||

157ya idaṃ paṭhate parva śṛṇuyādvāpi nityaśaḥ
|
sa mucyate mahāpāpaiḥ kṛtairghoraiśca karmabhiḥ||157||

158yajñāvāptirbāhmaṇasyeha nityaṃ ghore yuddhe kṣatriyāṇāṃ yaśaśca
|
śeṣau varṇau kāmamiṣṭaṃ labhete putrānpautrānnityamiṣṭāṃstathaiva||158||
