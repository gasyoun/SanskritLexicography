Mahābhārata 5.27 (Udyogaparvan)

1sañjaya uvāca|
dharmanityā pāṇḍava te viceṣṭā loke śrutā dṛśyate cāpi pārtha
|
mahāśrāvaṃ jīvitaṃ cāpyanityaṃ saṃpaśya tvaṃ pāṇḍava mā vyanīnaśaḥ||1||

2na cedbhāgaṃ kuravo'nyatra yuddhāt prayaccheraṃstubhyamajātaśatro
|
bhaikṣacaryāmandhakavṛṣṇirājye śreyo manye na tu yuddhena rājyam ||2||

3alpakālaṃ jīvitaṃ yanmanuṣye mahāsrāvaṃ nityaduḥkhaṃ calaṃ ca
|
bhūyaśca tadyaśaso nānurūpaṃ tasmāt pāpaṃ pāṇḍava mā kṛthāstvam ||3||

4kāmā manuṣyaṃ prasajanta ete dharmasya ye vighnamūlaṃ narendra
|
pūrvaṃ narastān matimān praṇighnan loke praśaṃsāṃ labhate'navadyām ||4||

5nibandhanī hyarthatṛṣṇeha pārtha tāmicchatāṃ bādhyate dharma eva
|
dharmaṃ tu yaḥ pravṛṇīte sa buddhaḥ kāme gṛdhno hīyate'rthānurodhāt ||5||

6dharmaṃ kṛtvā karmaṇāṃ tāta mukhyaṃ mahāpratāpaḥ saviteva bhāti
|
hīno hi dharmeṇa mahīmapīmāṃ labdhvā naraḥ sīdati pāpabuddhiḥ ||6||

7vedo'dhītaścaritaṃ brahmacaryaṃ yajñairiṣṭaṃ brāhmaṇebhyaśca dattam
|
paraṃ sthānaṃ manyamānena bhūya ātmā datto varṣapūgaṃ sukhebhyaḥ ||7||

8sukhapriye sevamāno'tivelaṃ yogābhyāse yo na karoti karma
|
vittakṣaye hīnasukho'tivelaṃ duḥkhaṃ śete kāmavegapraṇunnaḥ||8||

9evaṃ punarbrahmacaryāprasakto hitvā dharmaṃ yaḥ prakarotyadharmam
|
aśraddadhat paralokāya mūḍho hitvā dehaṃ tapyate pretya mandaḥ||9||

10na karmaṇāṃ vipraṇāśo'styamutra puṇyānāṃ vā'pyathavā pāpakānām
|
pūrvaṃ karturgacchati puṇyapāpaṃ paścāttvenamanuyātyeva kartā ||10||

11nyāyopetaṃ brāhmaṇebhyo'tha dattaṃ śraddhāpūtaṃ gandharasopapannam
|
anvāhāryeṣūttamadakṣiṇeṣu tathārūpaṃ karma vikhyāyate te ||11||

12iha kṣetre kriyate pārtha kāryaṃ na vai kiñcit kriyate pretya kāryam
|
kṛtaṃ tvayā pāralaukyaṃ ca karma puṇyaṃ mahatsadbhiratipraśastam ||12||

13jahāti mṛtyuṃ ca jarāṃ bhayaṃ ca na kṣutpipāse manaso'priyāṇi
|
na kartavyaṃ vidyate tatra kiṃcidanyatra vai cendriyaprīṇanāddhi ||13||

14evaṃrūpaṃ karmaphalaṃ narendra mā'trāvahaṃ hṛdayasya priyeṇa
|
sa krodhajaṃ pāṇḍava harṣajaṃ ca lokāvubhau mā prahāsīścirāya ||14||

15antaṃ gatvā karmaṇāṃ mā prajahyāḥ satyaṃ damaṃ cārjavamānṛśaṃsyam
|
aśvamedhaṃ rājasūyaṃ tathejyāḥ pāpasyāntaṃ karmaṇo mā punargāḥ ||15||

16taccedevaṃ dveṣarūpeṇa pārthāḥ kariṣyadhvaṃ karma pāpaṃ cirāya
|
nivasadhvaṃ varṣapūgān vaneṣu duḥkhaṃ vāsaṃ pāṇḍavā dharma eva ||16||

17apravrajye mā sma hitvā purastādātmādhīnaṃ yadbalaṃ hyetadāsīt
|
nityaṃ ca vaśyāḥ sacivāstaveme janārdano yuyudhānaśca vīraḥ ||17||

18matsyo rājā rukmarathaḥ saputraḥ prahāribhiḥ saha putrairvirāṭaḥ
|
rājānaśca ye vijitāḥ purastāt tvāmeva te saṃśrayeyuḥ samastāḥ ||18||

19mahāsahāyaḥ pratapan balasthaḥ puraskṛto vāsudevārjunābhyām
|
varān haniṣyan dviṣato raṅgamadhye vyaneṣyathā dhārtarāṣṭrasya darpam ||19||

20balaṃ kasmādvardhayitvā parasya nijān kasmāt karśayitvā sahāyān
|
niruṣya kasmādvarṣapūgān vaneṣu yuyutsase pāṇḍava hīnakālam||20||

21aprājño vā pāṇḍava yuddhyamāno'dharmajño vā bhūtimatho'bhyupaiti
|
prajñāvān vā budhyamāno'pi dharmaṃ saṃstambhādvā so'pi bhūterapaiti ||21||

22nādharme te dhīyate pārtha buddhirna saṃrambhāt karma cakartha pāpam
|
āttha kiṃ tatkāraṇaṃ yasya hetoḥ prajñāviruddhaṃ karma cikīrṣasīdam ||22||

23avyādhijaṃ kaṭukaṃ śīrṣarogi yaśomuṣaṃ pāpaphalodayaṃ vā
|
satāṃ peyaṃ yanna pibantyasanto manyuṃ mahārāja piba praśāmya||23||

24pāpānubandhaṃ ko nu taṃ kāmayeta kṣamaiva te jyāyasī nota bhogāḥ
|
yatra bhīṣmaḥ śāntanavo hataḥ syādyatra droṇaḥ sahaputro hataḥ syāt ||24||

25kṛpaḥ śalyaḥ saumadattirvikarṇo viviṃśatiḥ karṇaduryodhanau ca
|
etān hatvā kīdṛśaṃ tatsukhaṃ syādyadvindethāstadanu brūhi pārtha ||25||

26labdhvā'pīmāṃ pṛthivīṃ sāgarāntāṃ jarāmṛtyū naiva hi tvaṃ prajahyāḥ
|
priyāpriye sukhaduḥkhe ca rājannevaṃ vidvānnaiva yuddhaṃ kuru tvam ||26||

27amātyānāṃ yadi kāmasya hetorevaṃ yuktaṃ karma cikīrṣasi tvam
|
apakrāmeḥ svaṃ pradāyaiva teṣāṃ mā gāstvaṃ vai devayānāt patho'dya ||27||
