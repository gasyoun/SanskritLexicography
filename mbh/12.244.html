Mahābhārata 12.244 (Śāntiparvan)

1bhīṣma uvāca|
proktā gṛhasthavṛttiste vihitā yā manīṣibhiḥ
|
tadanantaramuktaṃ yattannibodha yudhiṣṭhira ||1||

2kramaśastvavadhūyaināṃ tṛtīyāṃ vṛttimuttamām
|
saṃyogavratakhinnānāṃ vānaprasthāśramaukasām ||2||

3śrūyatāṃ putra bhadraṃ te sarvalokāśramātmanām
|
prekṣāpūrvaṃ pravṛttānāṃ puṇyadeśanivāsinām ||3||

4vyāsa uvāca |
gṛhasthastu yadā paśyedvalīpalitamātmanaḥ
|
apatyasyaiva cāpatyaṃ vanameva tadā śrayet ||4||

5tṛtīyamāyuṣo bhāgaṃ vānaprasthāśrame vaset
|
tānevāgnīnparicaredyajamāno divaukasaḥ ||5||

6niyato niyatāhāraḥ ṣaṣṭhabhukto'pramattavān
|
tadagnihotraṃ tā gāvo yajñāṅgāni ca sarvaśaḥ ||6||

7aphālakṛṣṭaṃ vrīhiyavaṃ nīvāraṃ vighasāni ca
|
havīṃṣi samprayaccheta makheṣvatrāpi pañcasu ||7||

8vānaprasthāśrame'pyetāścatasro vṛttayaḥ smṛtāḥ
|
sadyaḥprakṣālakāḥ kecitkecinmāsikasaṃcayāḥ ||8||

9vārṣikaṃ saṃcayaṃ kecitkeciddvādaśavārṣikam
|
kurvantyatithipūjārthaṃ yajñatantrārthameva vā ||9||

10abhrāvakāśā varṣāsu hemante jalasaṃśrayāḥ
|
grīṣme ca pañca tapasaḥ śaśvacca mitabhojanāḥ ||10||

11bhūmau viparivartante tiṣṭhanti prapadairapi
|
sthānāsanairvartayanti sa vaneṣvabhiṣiñcate ||11||

12dantolūkhalikāḥ kecidaśmakuṭṭāstathā pare
|
śuklapakṣe pibantyeke yavāgūṃ kvathitāṃ sakṛt ||12||

13kṛṣṇapakṣe pibantyanye bhuñjate vā yathāgatam
|
mūlaireke phalaireke yuṣpaireke dṛḍhavratāḥ ||13||

14vartayanti yathānyāyaṃ vaikhānasagatiṃ śritāḥ
|
etāścānyāśca vividhā dīkṣāsteṣāṃ manīṣiṇām ||14||

15caturthaścaupaniṣado dharmaḥ sādhāraṇaḥ smṛtaḥ
|
vānaprasthādgṛhasthācca tato'nyaḥ sampravartate ||15||

16asminneva yuge tāta vipraiḥ sarvārthadarśibhiḥ
|
agastyaḥ sapta ṛṣayo madhucchando'ghamarṣaṇaḥ ||16||

17sāṃkṛtiḥ sudivā taṇḍiryathāvāso kṛtaśramaḥ
|
ahovīryastathā kāvyastāṇḍyo medhātithirbudhaḥ ||17||

18balavānkarṇanirvākaḥ śūnyapālaḥ kṛtaśramaḥ
|
enaṃ dharmaṃ kṛtavantastataḥ svargamupāgaman ||18||

19tāta pratyakṣadharmāṇastathā yāyāvarā gaṇāḥ
|
ṛṣīṇāmugratapasāṃ dharmanaipuṇadarśinām ||19||

20anye cāparimeyāśca brāhmaṇā vanamāśritāḥ
|
vaikhānasā vālakhilyāḥ saikatāśca tathā pare ||20||

21karmabhiste nirānandā dharmanityā jitendriyāḥ
|
gatāḥ pratyakṣadharmāṇaste sarve vanamāśritāḥ ||21||

22anakṣatrāstvanādhṛṣyā dṛśyante jyotiṣāṃ gaṇāḥ
|
jarayā ca paridyūno vyādhinā ca prapīḍitaḥ ||22||

23caturthe cāyuṣaḥ śeṣe vānaprasthāśramaṃ tyajet
|
sadyaskārāṃ nirūpyeṣṭiṃ sarvavedasadakṣiṇām ||23||

24ātmayājī so'tmaratirātmakrīḍātmasaṃśrayaḥ
|
ātmanyagnīnsamāropya tyaktvā sarvaparigrahān ||24||

25sādyaskāṃśca yajedyajñāniṣṭīścaiveha sarvadā
|
yadaiva yājināṃ yajñādātmanījyā pravartate ||25||

26trīṃścaivāgnīnyajetsamyagātmanyevātmamokṣaṇāt
|
prāṇebhyo yajuṣaḥ pañca ṣaṭ prāśnīyādakutsayan ||26||

27keśa-loma-nakhānvāpya vānaprastho munistataḥ
|
āśramādāśramaṃ puṇyaṃ pūto gacchati karmabhiḥ ||27||

28abhayaṃ sarvabhūtebhyo datvā yaḥ pravrajeddvijaḥ
|
lokāstejomayāstasya pretya cānantyamaśnute ||28||

29suśīlavṛtto vyapanītakalmaṣo na ceha nāmutra ca kartumīhate
|
aroṣamoho gatasandhivigraho bhavedudāsīnavadātmavinnaraḥ ||29||

30yameṣu caivānugateṣu na vyathetsvaśāstrasūtrāhutimantravikramaḥ
|
bhavedyatheṣṭāgatirātmavedini na saṃśayo dharmapare jitendriye ||30||

31tataḥ paraṃ śreṣṭhamatīva sadguṇairadhiṣṭhitaṃ trīnadhivṛttimuttamam
|
caturthamuktaṃ paramāśramaṃ śṛṇu prakīrtyamānaṃ paramaṃ parāyaṇam ||31||
