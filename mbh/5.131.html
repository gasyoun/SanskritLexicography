Mahābhārata 5.131 (Udyogaparvan)

1vaiśampāyana uvāca|
vidureṇaivamuktastu keśavaḥ śatrupūgahā
|
duryodhanaṃ dhārtarāṣṭramabhyabhāṣata vīryavān ||1||

2eko'hamiti yanmohānmanyase māṃ suyodhana
|
paribhūya sudurbuddhe grahītuṃ māṃ cikīrṣasi ||2||

3ihaiva pāṇḍavāḥ sarve tathaivāndhakavṛṣṇayaḥ
|
ihādityāśca rudrāśca vasavaśca maharṣibhiḥ ||3||

4evamuktvā jahāsoccai keśavaḥ paravīrahā
|
tasya saṃsmayataḥ śaurervidyudrūpā mahātmanaḥ ||4||

5aṅguṣṭhamātrāstridaśā mumucuḥ pāvakārciṣaḥ
|
asya brahmā lalāṭastho rudro vakṣasi cābhavat ||5||

6lokapālā bhujeṣvāsannagnirāsyādajāyata
|
ādityāścaiva sādhyāśca vasavo'thāśvināvapi||6||

7marutaśca sahendreṇa viśvedevāstathaiva ca
|
babhūvuścaikarūpāṇi yakṣagandharvarakṣasām ||7||

8prādurāstāṃ tathā dorbhyāṃ saṅkarṣaṇadhanañjayau
|
dakṣiṇe'thārjuno dhanvī halī rāmaśca savyataḥ ||8||

9bhīmo yudhiṣṭhiraścaiva mādrīputrau ca pṛṣṭhataḥ
|
andhakā vṛṣṇayaścaiva pradyumnapramukhāstataḥ ||9||

10agre babhūvuḥ kṛṣṇasya samudyatamahāyudhāḥ
|
śaṅkha-cakra-gadā-śakti-śārṅa-lāṅgala-nandakāḥ ||10||

11adṛśyantodyatānyeva sarvapraharaṇāni ca
|
nānābāhuṣu kṛṣṇasya dīpyamānāni sarvaśaḥ ||11||

12netrābhyāṃ nastataścaiva śrotrābhyāṃ ca samantataḥ
|
prādurāsanmahāraudrāḥ sadhūmāḥ pāvakārciṣaḥ ||12||

13romakūpeṣu ca tathā sūryasyeva marīcayaḥ
|
taṃ dṛṣṭvā ghoramātmānaṃ keśavasya mahātmanaḥ ||13||

14nyamīlayanta netrāṇi rājānastrastacetasaḥ
|
ṛte droṇaṃ ca bhīṣmaṃ ca viduraṃ ca mahāmatim ||14||

15sañjayaṃ ca mahābhāgamṛṣīṃścaiva tapodhanān
|
prādātteṣāṃ sa bhagavān divyaṃ cakṣurjanārdanaḥ ||15||

16taddṛṣṭvā mahadāścaryaṃ mādhavasya sabhātale
|
devadundubhayo neduḥ puṣpavarṣaṃ papāta ca ||16||

17dhṛtarāṣṭra uvāca|
tvameva puṇḍarīkākṣa sarvasya jagato hitaḥ
|
tasmāttvaṃ yādavaśreṣṭha prasādaṃ kartumarhasi ||17||

18bhagavan mama netrāṇāmantardhānaṃ vṛṇe punaḥ
|
bhavantaṃ draṣṭumicchāmi nānyaṃ draṣṭumihotsahe ||18||

19tato'bravīnmahābāhurdhṛtarāṣṭraṃ janārdanaḥ
|
adṛśyamāne netre dve bhavetāṃ kurunandana ||19||

20tatrādbhutaṃ mahārāja dhṛtarāṣṭraśca cakṣuṣī
|
labdhavān vāsudevācca viśvarūpadidṛkṣayā ||20||

21labdhacakṣuṣamāsīnaṃ dhṛtarāṣṭraṃ narādhipāḥ
|
vismitā ṛṣibhiḥ sārdhaṃ tuṣṭuvurmadhusūdanam ||21||

22cacāla ca mahī kṛtsnā sāgaraścāpi cukṣubhe
|
vismayaṃ paramaṃ jagmuḥ pārthivā bharatarṣama ||22||

23tataḥ sa puruṣavyāghraḥ saṃjahāra vapuḥ svakam
|
tāṃ divyāmadbhutāṃ citrāmṛddhimattāmarindamaḥ ||23||

24tataḥ sātyakimādāya pāṇau hārdikyameva ca
|
ṛṣibhistairanujñāto niryayau madhusūdanaḥ ||24||

25ṛṣayo'ntarhitā jagmustataste nāradādayaḥ
|
tasmin kolāhale vṛtte tadadbhutamivābhavat ||25||

26taṃ prasthitamabhiprekṣya kauravāḥ saha rājabhiḥ
|
anujagmurnaravyāghraṃ devā iva śatakratum ||26||

27acintayannameyātmā sarvaṃ tadrājamaṇḍalam
|
niścakrāma tataḥ śauriḥ sadhūma iva pāvakaḥ ||27||

28tato rathena śubhreṇa mahatā kiṅkiṇīkinā
|
hemajālavicitreṇa laghunā meghanādinā ||28||

29sūpaskareṇa śubhreṇa vaiyāghreṇa varūthinā
|
śaibyasugrīvayuktena pratyadṛśyata dārukaḥ ||29||

30tathaiva rathamāsthāya kṛtavarmā mahārathaḥ
|
vṛṣṇīnāṃ saṃmato vīro hārdikyaḥ samadṛśyata ||30||

31upasthitarathaṃ śauriṃ prayāsyantamarindamam
|
dhṛtarāṣṭro mahārājaḥ punarevābhyabhāṣata ||31||

32yāvadbalaṃ me putreṣu paśyataste janārdana
|
pratyakṣaṃ te na te kiñcitparokṣaṃ śatrukarśana ||32||

33kurūṇāṃ śamamicchantaṃ yatamānaṃ ca keśava
|
vidityaitāmavasthāṃ me nābhiśaṃṅkitumarhasi ||33||

34na me pāpo'styabhiprāyaḥ pāṇḍavān prati keśava
|
jñātameva hitaṃ vākyaṃ yanmayoktaḥ suyodhanaḥ ||34||

35jānanti kuravaḥ sarve rājānaścaiva pārthivāḥ
|
śame prayatamānaṃ māṃ sarvayatnena mādhava ||35||

36vaiśampāyana uvāca|
tato'bravīnmahābāhurdhṛtarāṣṭraṃ janārdanaḥ
|
droṇaṃ pitāmahaṃ bhīṣmaṃ kṣattāraṃ bāhlikaṃ kṛpam ||36||

37pratyakṣametadbhavatāṃ yadvṛttaṃ kurusaṃsadi
|
yathā cāśiṣṭavanmando roṣādadya samutthitaḥ ||37||

38vadatyanīśamātmānaṃ dhṛtarāṣṭro mahīpatiḥ
|
āpṛcche bhavataḥ sarvān gamiṣyāmi yudhiṣṭhiram ||38||

39āmantrya prasthitaṃ śauriṃ rathasthaṃ puruṣarṣabha
|
anujagmurmaheṣvāsāḥ pravīrā bharatarṣabhāḥ ||39||

40bhīṣmo droṇaḥ kṛpaḥ kṣattā dhṛtarāṣṭro'tha bāhlikaḥ
|
aśvatthāmā vikarṇaśca yuyutsuśca mahārathaḥ ||40||

41tato rathena śubhreṇa mahatā kiṅkiṇīkinā
|
kuruṇāṃ paśyatāṃ draṣṭuṃ svasāraṃ sa pituryayau ||41||
