Mahābhārata 7.99 (Droṇaparvan)

1sañjaya uvāca|
vivartamāne tvāditye tatrāstaśikharaṃ prati
|
rajasā kīryamāṇe ca mandībhūte divākare ||1||

2tiṣṭhatāṃ yudhyamānānāṃ punarāvartatāmapi
|
bhajyatāṃ jayatāṃ caiva jagāma tadahaḥ śanaiḥ ||2||

3tathā teṣu viṣakteṣu sainyeṣu jayagṛddhiṣu
|
arjuno vāsudevaśca saindhavāyaiva jagmatuḥ ||3||

4rathamārgapramāṇaṃ tu kaunteyo niśitaiḥ śaraiḥ
|
cakāra tatra panthānaṃ yayau yena janārdanaḥ ||4||

5yatra yatra ratho yāti pāṇḍavasya mahātmanaḥ
|
tatra tatraiva dīryante senāstava viśāmpate ||5||

6rathaśikṣāṃ tu dāśārho darśayāmāsa vīryavān
|
uttamādhamamadhyāni maṇḍalāni vidarśayan ||6||

7te tu nāmāṅkitāḥ pītāḥ kālajvalanasannibhāḥ
|
snāyunaddhāḥ suparvāṇaḥ pṛthavo dīrghagāminaḥ ||7||

8vaiṇavāścāyasāścogrā grasanto vividhānarīn
|
rudhiraṃ patagaiḥ sārdhaṃ prāṇināṃ papurāhave ||8||

9rathasthito'grataḥ krośaṃ yānasyatyarjunaḥ śarān
|
rathe krośamatikrānte tasya te ghnanti śātravān ||9||

10tārkṣyamārutaraṃhobhirvājibhiḥ sādhuvāhibhiḥ
|
tadāgacchaddhṛṣīkeśaḥ kṛtsnaṃ vismāpayan jagat ||10||

11na tathā gacchati rathastapanasya viśāmpate
|
nendrasya na tu rudrasya nāpi vaiśravaṇasya ca ||11||

12nānyasya samare rājan gatapūrvastathā rathaḥ
|
yathā yayāvarjunasya manobhiprāyaśīghragaḥ ||12||

13praviśya tu raṇe rājan keśavaḥ paravīrahā
|
senāmadhye hayāṃstūrṇaṃ codayāmāsa bhārata ||13||

14tatastasya rathaughasya madhyaṃ prāpya hayottamāḥ
|
kṛcchreṇa rathamūhustaṃ kṣutpipāsāsamanvitāḥ ||14||

15kṣatāśca bahubhiḥ śastrairyuddhaśauṇḍairanekaśaḥ
|
maṇḍalāni vicitrāṇi viceruste muhurmuhuḥ ||15||

16hatānāṃ vājināgānāṃ rathānāṃ ca naraiḥ saha
|
upariṣṭādatikrāntāḥ śailābhānāṃ sahasraśaḥ ||16||

17etasminnantare vīrāvāvantyau bhrātarau nṛpa
|
sahasenau samārcchetāṃ pāṇḍavaṃ klāntavāhanam ||17||

18tāvarjunaṃ catuḥṣaṣṭyā saptatyā ca janārdanam
|
śarāṇāṃ ca śatairaśvānavidhyetāṃ mudānvitau ||18||

19tāvarjuno mahārāja navabhirnataparvabhiḥ
|
ājaghāna raṇe kruddho marmajño marmabhedibhiḥ ||19||

20tatastau tu śaraugheṇa bībhatsuṃ sahakeśavam
|
ācchādayetāṃ saṃrabdhau siṃhanādaṃ ca cakratuḥ ||20||

21tayostu dhanuṣī citre bhallābhyāṃ śvetavāhanaḥ
|
ciccheda samare tūrṇaṃ dhvajau ca kanakojjvalau ||21||

22athānye dhanuṣī rājan pragṛhya samare tadā
|
pāṇḍavaṃ bhṛśasaṅkruddhāvardayāmāsatuḥ śaraiḥ ||22||

23tayostu bhṛśasaṅkruddhaḥ śarābhyāṃ pāṇḍunandanaḥ
|
dhanuṣī cicchide tūrṇaṃ bhūya eva dhanañjayaḥ ||23||

24tathānyairviśikhaistūrṇaṃ rukmapuṅkhaiḥ śilāśitaiḥ
|
jaghānāśvāṃstathā sūtau pārṣṇī ca sapadānugau ||24||

25jyeṣṭhasya ca śiraḥ kāyāt kṣurapreṇānyakṛntata
|
sa papāta hataḥ pṛthvyāṃ vātarugṇa iva drumaḥ ||25||

26vindaṃ tu nihataṃ dṛṣṭvā hyanuvindaḥ pratāpavān
|
hatāśvaṃ rathamutsṛjya gadāṃ gṛhya mahābalaḥ ||26||

27abhyavartata saṅgrāme bhrāturvadhamanusmaran
|
gadayā rathināṃ śreṣṭho nṛtyanniva mahārathaḥ||27||

28anuvindastu gadayā lalāṭe madhusūdanam
|
spṛṣṭvā nākampayat kruddho mainākamiva parvatam ||28||

29tasyārjunaḥ śaraiḥ ṣaḍbhirgrīvāṃ pādau bhujau śiraḥ
|
nicakarta sa sañchinnaḥ papātādricayo yathā ||29||

30tatastau nihatau dṛṣṭvā tayo rājan padānugāḥ
|
abhyadravanta saṅkruddhāḥ kirantaḥ śataśaḥ śarān ||30||

31tānarjunaḥ śaraistūrṇaṃ nihatya bharatarṣabha
|
vyarocata yathā vahnirdāvaṃ dagdhvā himātyaye ||31||

32tayoḥ senāmatikrāmya kṛcchrādiva dhanañjayaḥ
|
vibabhau jaladaṃ hitvā divākara ivoditaḥ ||32||

33taṃ dṛṣṭvā kuravastrastāḥ prahṛṣṭāścābhavan punaḥ
|
abhyavartanta pārthaṃ ca samantādbharatarṣabha ||33||

34śrāntaṃ cainaṃ samālakṣya jñātvā dūre ca saindhavaṃ
|
siṃhanādena mahatā sarvataḥ paryavārayan ||34||

35tāṃstu dṛṣṭvā susaṃrabdhānutsmayan puruṣarṣabhaḥ
|
śanakairiva dāśārhamarjuno vākyamabravīt ||35||

36śarārditāśca glānāśca hayā dūre ca saindhavaḥ
|
kimihānantaraṃ kāryaṃ jyāyiṣṭhaṃ tava rocate ||36||

37brūhi kṛṣṇa yathā tattvaṃ tvaṃ hi prājñatamaḥ sadā
|
bhavannetrā raṇe śatrūn vijeṣyantīha pāṇḍavāḥ ||37||

38mama tvanantaraṃ kṛtyaṃ yadvai tattvaṃ nibodha me
|
hayān vimucya hi sukhaṃ viśalyānkuru mādhava ||38||

39evamuktastu pārthena keśavaḥ pratyuvāca tam
|
mamāpyetanmataṃ pārtha yadidaṃ te prabhāṣitam ||39||

40arjuna uvāca|
ahamāvārayiṣyāmi sarvasainyāni keśava
|
tvamapyatra yathānyāyaṃ kuru kāryamanantaram ||40||

41sañjaya uvāca|
so'vatīrya rathopasthādasambhrānto dhanañjayaḥ
|
gāṇḍīvaṃ dhanurādāya tasthau giririvācalaḥ ||41||

42tamabhyadhāvankrośantaḥ kṣatriyā jayakāṃkṣiṇaḥ
|
idaṃ chidramiti jñātvā dharaṇīsthaṃ dhanañjayam ||42||

43tamekaṃ rathavaṃśena mahatā paryavārayan
|
vikarṣantaśca cāpāni visṛjantaśca sāyakān ||43||

44śastrāṇi ca vicitrāṇi kruddhāstatra vyadarśayan
|
chādayantaḥ śaraiḥ pārthaṃ meghā iva divākaram ||44||

45abhyadravanta vegena kṣatriyāḥ kṣatriyarṣabham
|
narasiṃhaṃ rathodārāḥ siṃhaṃ mattā iva dvipāḥ ||45||

46tatra pārthasya bhujayormahadbalamadṛśyata
|
yatkruddho bahulāḥ senāḥ sarvataḥ samavārayat ||46||

47astrairastrāṇi saṃvārya dviṣatāṃ sarvato vibhuḥ
|
iṣubhirbahubhistūrṇaṃ sarvāneva samāvṛṇot ||47||

48tatrāntarikṣe bāṇānāṃ pragāḍhānāṃ viśāmpate
|
saṅgharṣeṇa mahārciṣmān pāvakaḥ samajāyata ||48||

49tatra tatra maheṣvāsaiḥ śvasadbhiḥ śoṇitokṣitaiḥ
|
hayairnāgaiśca sambhinnairnadadbhiścārikarṣaṇaiḥ ||49||

50saṃrabdhaiścāribhirvīraiḥ prārthayadbhirjayaṃ mṛdhe
|
ekasthairbahubhiḥ kruddhairūṣmeva samajāyata ||50||

51śarormiṇaṃ dhvajāvartaṃ nāganakraṃ duratyayam
|
padātimatsakalilaṃ śaṅkhadundubhiniḥsvanam ||51||

52asaṅkhyeyamapāraṃ ca rathormiṇamatīva ca
|
uṣṇīṣakamaṭhaṃ chatrapatākāphenamālinam ||52||

53raṇasāgaramakṣobhyaṃ mātaṅgāṅgaśilācitam
|
velābhūtastadā pārthaḥ patribhiḥ samavārayat ||53||

54dhṛtarāṣṭra uvāca|
arjune dharaṇīṃ prāpte hayahaste ca keśave
|
etadantaramāsādya kathaṃ pārtho na ghātitaḥ ||54||

55sañjaya uvāca|
sadyaḥ pārthiva pārthena niruddhāḥ sarvapārthivāḥ
|
rathasthā dharaṇīsthena vākyamacchāndasaṃ yathā ||55||

56sa pārthaḥ pārthivān sarvān bhūmistho'pi rathasthitān
|
eko nivārayāmāsa lobhaḥ sarvaguṇāniva ||56||

57tato janārdanaḥ saṅkhye priye puruṣasattamam
|
asambhrānto mahābāhurarjunaṃ vākyamabravīt ||57||

58uda‌pānamihāśvānāṃ nālamasti raṇe'rjuna
|
parīpsante jalaṃ ceme peyaṃ na tvavagāhanam ||58||

59idamastītyasambhrānto bruvannastreṇa medinīm
|
abhihatyārjunaścakre vājipānaṃ saraḥ śubham ||59||

60haṃsakāraṇḍavākīrṇaṃ cakravākopaśobhitam
|
suvistīrṇaṃ prasannāmbhaḥ praphullavarapaṅkajam ||60||

61kūrmamatsyagaṇākīrṇamagādhamṛṣisevitam
|
āgacchannāradamunirdarśanārthaṃ kṛtaṃ kṣaṇāt ||61||

62śaravaṃśaṃ śarasthūṇaṃ śarācchādanamadbhutam
|
śaraveśmākarot pārthastvaṣṭevādbhutakarmakṛt ||62||

63tataḥ prahasya govindaḥ sādhu sādhvityathābravīt
|
śaraveśmani pārthena kṛte tasmin mahātmanā ||63||
