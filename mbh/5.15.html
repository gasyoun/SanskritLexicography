Mahābhārata 5.15 (Udyogaparvan)

1śalya uvāca|
evamuktaḥ sa bhagavān śacyā tāṃ punarabravīt
|
vikramasya na kālo'yaṃ nahuṣo balavattaraḥ ||1||

2vivardhitaśca ṛṣibhirhavyakavyaiśca bhāvini
|
nītimatra vidhāsyāmi devi tāṃ kartumarhasi ||2||

3guhyaṃ caitattvayā kāryaṃ nākhyātavyaṃ śubhe kvacit
|
gatvā nahuṣamekānte bravīhi ca sumadhyame ||3||

4ṛṣiyānena divyena māmupaihi jagatpate
|
evaṃ tava vaśe prītā bhaviṣyāmīti taṃ vada ||4||

5ityuktvā devarājena patnī sā kamalekṣaṇā
|
evamastvityathoktvā tu jagāma nahuṣaṃ prati ||5||

6nahuṣastāṃ tato dṛṣṭvā sasmito vākyamabravīt
|
svāgataṃ te varārohe kiṃ karomi śucismite ||6||

7bhaktaṃ māṃ bhaja kalyāṇi kimicchasi manasvini
|
tava kalyāṇi yat kāryaṃ tat kariṣye sumadhyame ||7||

8na ca vrīḍā tvayā kāryā suśroṇi mayi viśvaseḥ
|
satyena vai śape devi kariṣye vacanaṃ tava ||8||

9indrāṇyuvāca
|
yo me kṛtastvayā kālastamākāṅkṣe jagatpate
|
tatastvameva bhartā me bhaviṣyasi surādhipa ||9||

10kāryaṃ ca hṛdi me yattaddevarājāvadhāraya
|
vakṣyāmi yadi me rājan priyametat kariṣyasi ||10||

11vākyaṃ praṇayasaṃyuktaṃ tataḥ syāṃ vaśagā tava
|
indrasya vājino vāhā hastino'tha rathāstathā ||11||

12icchāmyahamathāpūrvaṃ vāhanaṃ te surādhipa
|
yanna viṣṇorna rudrasya nāsurāṇāṃ na rakṣasām ||12||

13vahantu tvāṃ mahābhāgā ṛṣayaḥ saṅgatā vibho
|
sarve śibikayā rājannetaddhi mama rocate ||13||

14nāsureṣu na deveṣu tulyo bhavitumarhasi
|
sarveṣāṃ teja ādatse svena vīryeṇa darśanāt ||14||

15na te pramukhataḥ sthātuṃ kaścicchaknoti vīryavān| śalya uvāca|
evamuktastu nahuṣaḥ prāhṛṣyata tadā kila
|
uvāca vacanaṃ cāpi surendrastāmaninditām ||15||

16nahuṣa uvāca|
apūrvaṃ vāhanamidaṃ tvayoktaṃ varavarṇini
|
dṛḍhaṃ me ruciraṃ devi tvadvaśo'smi varānane ||16||

17na hyalpavīryo bhavati yo vāhān kurute munīn
|
ahaṃ tapasvī balavān bhūtabhavyabhavatprabhuḥ ||17||

18mayi kruddhe jaganna syānmayi sarvaṃ pratiṣṭhitam
|
devadānavagandharvāḥ kinnaroragarākṣasāḥ ||18||

19na me kruddhasya paryāptāḥ sarve lokāḥ śucismite
|
cakṣuṣā yaṃ prapaśyāmi tasya tejo harāmyaham ||19||

20tasmātte vacanaṃ devi kariṣyāmi na saṃśayaḥ
|
saptarṣayo māṃ vakṣyanti sarve brahmarṣayastathā ||20||

21paśya māhātmyayogaṃ me ṛddhiṃ ca varavarṇini| śalya uvāca|
evamuktvā tu tāṃ devīṃ visṛjya ca varānanām ||21||

22vimāne yojayitvā ca ṛṣīnniyamamāsthitān
|
abrahmaṇyo balopeto matto madabalena ca
|
kāmavṛttaḥ sa duṣṭātmā vāhayāmāsa tānṛṣīn ||22||

23nahuṣeṇa visṛṣṭā ca bṛhaspatimathābravīt
|
samayo'lpāvaśeṣo me nahuṣeṇeha yaḥ kṛtaḥ ||23||

24śakraṃ mṛgaya śīghraṃ tvaṃ bhaktāyāḥ kuru me dayām
|
bāḍhamityeva bhagavān bṛhaspatiruvāca tām ||24||

25na bhetavyaṃ tvayā devi nahuṣādduṣṭacetasaḥ
|
na hyeṣa sthāsyati ciraṃ gata eṣa narādhamaḥ ||25||

26adharmajño maharṣīṇāṃ vāhanācca tataḥ śubhe
|
iṣṭiṃ cāhaṃ kariṣyāmi vināśāyāsya durmateḥ ||26||

27śakraṃ cādhigamiṣyāmi mā bhaistvaṃ bhadramastu te
|
tataḥ prajvālya vidhivajjuhāva paramaṃ haviḥ ||27||

28bṛhaspatirmahātejā devarājopalabdhaye
|
hutvā'gniṃ so'bravīdrājañchakramanviṣyatāmiti ||28||

29tasmācca bhagavān devaḥ svayameva hutāśanaḥ
|
strīveṣamadbhutaṃ kṛtvā tatraivāntaradhīyata||29||

30sa diśaḥ pradiśaścaiva parvatāṃśca vanāni ca
|
pṛthivīṃ cāntarikṣaṃ ca vicintyātha manogatiḥ||30||

31nimeṣāntaramātreṇa bṛhaspatimupāgamat
|
agniruvāca| bṛhaspate na paśyāmi devarājamiha kvacit
|
āpaḥ śeṣāḥ sadā cāpaḥ praveṣṭuṃ notsahāmyaham ||31||

32na me tatra gatirbrahman kimanyat karavāṇi te
|
tamabravīddevagururapo viśa mahādyute ||32||

33agniruvāca| nāpaḥ praveṣṭuṃ śakṣyāmi kṣayo me'tra bhaviṣyati
|
śaraṇaṃ tvāṃ prapanno'smi svasti te'stu mahādyute ||33||

34adbhyo'gnirbrahmataḥ kṣatramaśmano lohamutthitam
|
teṣāṃ sarvatragaṃ tejaḥ svāsu yoniṣu śāmyati ||34||
