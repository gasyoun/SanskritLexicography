Mahābhārata 12.325 (Śāntiparvan)

1bhīṣma uvāca|
sa mokṣamanucintyaiva śukaḥ pitaramabhyagāt
|
prāhābhivādya ca guruṃ śreyo'rthī vinayānvitaḥ||1||

2mokṣadharmeṣu kuśalo bhagavānprabravītu me
|
yathā me manasaḥ śāntiḥ paramā saṃbhavetprabho||2||

3śrutvā putrasya tu vacaḥ paramarṣiruvāca tam
|
adhīṣva putra mokṣaṃ vai dharmāṃśca vividhānapi||3||

4piturniyogājjagrāha śuko dharmabhṛtāṃ varaḥ
|
yogaśāstraṃ ca nikhilaṃ kāpilaṃ caiva bhārata||4||

5sa taṃ brāhmyā śriyā yuktaṃ brahmatulyaparākramam
|
mene putraṃ yadā vyāso mokṣadharmaviśāradam||5||

6uvāca gaccheti tadā janakaṃ mithileśvaram
|
sa te vakṣyati mokṣārthaṃ nikhilaṃ mithileśvaram||6||

7piturniyogamādāya jagāma mithilāṃ nṛpa
|
praṣṭuṃ dharmasya niṣṭhāṃ vai mokṣasya ca parāyaṇam||7||

8uktaśca mānuṣeṇa tvaṃ pathā gacchetyavismitaḥ
|
na prabhāveṇa gantavyamantarikṣacareṇa vai||8||

9ārjaveṇaiva gantavyaṃ na sukhānveṣiṇā tathā
|
nānveṣṭavyā viśeṣāstu viśeṣā hi prasaṅginaḥ||9||

10ahaṃkāro na kartavyo yājye tasminnarādhipe
|
sthātavyaṃ ca vaśe tasya sa te chetsyati saṃśayam||10||

11sa dharmakuśalo rājā mokṣaśāstraviśāradaḥ
|
yājyo mama sa yadbrūyāttatkāryamaviśaṅkayā||11||

12evamuktaḥ sa dharmātmā jagāma mithilāṃ muniḥ
|
padbhyāṃ śakto'ntarikṣeṇa krāntuṃ pṛthvīṃ sasāgarām||12||

13sa girīṃścāpyatikramya nadītīrthasarāṃsi ca
|
bahuvyālamṛgākīrṇā hyaṭavīśca vanāni ca||13||

14merorhareśca dve varṣe varṣaṃ haimavataṃ tataḥ
|
krameṇaivaṃ vyatikramya bhārataṃ varṣamāsadat||14||

15sa deśānvividhānpaśyaṃścīnahūṇaniṣevitān
|
āryāvartamimaṃ deśamājagāma mahāmuniḥ||15||

16piturvacanamājñāya tamevārthaṃ vicintayan
|
adhvānaṃ so'ticakrāma khacaraḥ khecarannivā||16||

17pattanāni ca ramyāṇi sphītāni nagarāṇi ca
|
ratnāni ca vicitrāṇi paśyannapi na paśyati||17||

18udyānāni ca ramyāṇi tathaivāyatanāni ca
|
puṇyāni caiva ratnāni so'tyakrāmadathādhvagaḥ||18||

19so'cireṇaiva kālena vindehānāsasāda ha
|
rakṣitāndharmarājena janakena mahātmanā||19||

20tatra grāmānbahūnpaśyanbahvannarasabhojanān
|
pallīghoṣānsamṛddhāṃśca bahugokulasaṃkulān||20||

21sphītāṃśca śāliyavasairhaṃsasārasasevitān
|
padminībhiśca śataśaḥ śrīmatībhiralaṅkṛtān||21||

22sa videhānatikramya samṛddhajanasevitān
|
mithilopavanaṃ ramyamāsasāda samṛddhimat||22||

23hastyaśvarathasaṃkīrṇaṃ naranārīsamākulam
|
paśyannapaśyanniva tatsamatikrāmadacyutaḥ||23||

24manasā taṃ vahanbhāraṃ tamevārthaṃ vicintayan
|
ātmārāmaḥ prasannātmā mithilāmāsasāda ha||24||

25tasyā dvāraṃ samāsādya niḥśaṅkaḥ praviveśa ha
|
tāpi dvārapālāstamugravācā nyaṣedhayan||25||

26tathaiva ca śukastatra nirmanyuḥ samatiṣṭhata
|
na cātapādhvasantaptaḥ kṣutpipāsāśramānvitaḥ||26||

27pratāmyati glāyati vā nāpaiti ca tathā''tapāt
|
teṣāṃ tu dvārapālānāmekaḥ śokasamanvitaḥ||27||

28madhyaṃ gatamivādityaṃ dṛṣṭvā śukamavasthitam
|
pūjayitvā yathānyāyamabhivādya kṛtāñjaliḥ||28||

29prāveśayattataḥ kakṣyāṃ dvitīyāṃ rājaveśmanaḥ
|
tatrāsīnaḥ śukastāta mokṣamevānvacintayat||29||

30chāyāyāmātape caiva samadarśī samadyutiḥ
|
taṃ muhūrtādivāgamya rājño mantrī kṛtāñjaliḥ||30||

31prāveśayattataḥ kakṣyāṃ tṛtīyāṃ rājaveśmanaḥ
|
tatrāntaḥpurasambaddhaṃ mahaccaitrarathopamam||31||

32suvibhaktajalākrīḍaṃ ramyaṃ puṣpitapādapam
|
śukaṃ prāveśayanmantrī pramadāvanamuttamam||32||

33sa tasyāsanamādiśya niścakrāma tataḥ punaḥ
|
taṃ cāruveṣāḥ suśroṇyastaruṇyaḥ priyadarśanāḥ||33||

34sūkṣmaraktāmbaradharāstaptakāñcanabhūṣaṇāḥ
|
saṃlāpollāpakuśalā nṛtyagītaviśāradāḥ||34||

35smitapūrvābhibhāṣiṇyo rūpeṇāpsarasāṃ samāḥ
|
kāmopacārakuśalā bhāvajñāḥ sarvakovidāḥ||35||

36paraṃ pañcāśataṃ nāryo vāramukhyāḥ samādravan
|
pādyādīni pratigrāhya pūjayā parayā'rcayan||36||

37kālopapannena tadā svādvannenābhyatarpayan
|
tasya bhuktavatastāta tadantaḥpurakānanam||37||

38suramyaṃ darśayāmāsurekaikaśyena bhārata
|
krīḍantyaśca hasantyaśca gāyantyaścāpi tāḥ śubham||38||

39udārasattvaṃ sattvajñāḥ striyaḥ paryacaraṃstathā
|
āraṇeyastu śuddhātmā niḥsaṃdehaḥ svakarmakṛt||39||

40vaśyendriyo jitakrodho na hṛṣyati na kupyati
|
tasmai śayyāsanaṃ divyaṃ devārhaṃ ratnabhūṣitam||40||

41spardhyāstaraṇasaṃkīrṇaṃ dadustā: paramastriyaḥ
|
pādaśaucaṃ tu kṛtvaiva śukaḥ sandhyāmupāsya ca||41||

42niṣasādāsane puṇye tamevārthaṃ vicintayan
|
pūrvarātre tu tatrāsau bhūtvā dhyānaparāyaṇaḥ||42||

43madhyarātre yathānyāyaṃ nidrāmāhārayatprabhuḥ
|
tato muhūrtādutthāya kṛtvā śaucamanantaram||43||

44strībhiḥ parivṛto dhīmān dhyānamevānvapadyata||44||

45anena vidhinā kāṇi stadahaḥśeṣamacyutaḥ
|
tāṃ ca rātriṃ nṛpakule vartayāmāsa bhārata||45||
