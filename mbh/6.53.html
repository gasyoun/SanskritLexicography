Mahābhārata 6.53 (Bhīṣmaparvan)

1dhṛtarāṣṭra uvāca|kathaṃ droṇo maheṣvāsaḥ pāñcālyaścāpi pārṣataḥ
|
ubhau samīyaturyattau tanmamācakṣva sañjaya ||1||

2diṣṭameva paraṃ manye pauruṣāditi me matiḥ
|
yatra śāntanavo bhīṣmo nātaradyudhi pāṇḍavam ||2||

3bhīṣmo hi samare kruddho hanyāllokāṃścarācarān
|
sa kathaṃ pāṇḍavaṃ yuddhe nātarat sañjayaujasā ||3||

4sañjaya uvāca|
śṛṇu rājan sthiro bhūtvā yuddhametatsudāruṇam
|
na śakyāḥ pāṇḍavā jetuṃ devairapi savāsavaiḥ ||4||

5droṇastu niśitairbāṇairdhṛṣṭadyumnamavidhyata
|
sārathiṃ cāsya bhallena rathanīḍādapātayat ||5||

6tathā'sya caturo vāhāṃścaturbhiḥ sāyakottamaiḥ
|
pīḍayāmāsa saṃkruddho dhṛṣṭadyumnasya māriṣa ||6||

7dhṛṣṭadyumnastato droṇaṃ navatyā niśitaiḥ śaraiḥ
|
vivyādha prahasanvīrastiṣṭha tiṣṭheti cābravīt ||7||

8tataḥ punarameyātmā bhāradvājaḥ pratāpavān
|
śaraiḥ pracchā‌dayāmāsa dhṛṣṭadyumnamamarṣaṇam ||8||

9ādade ca śaraṃ ghoraṃ pārṣatāntacikīrṣayā
|
śakrāśīnasamasparśaṃ kāladaṇḍamivāparam ||9||

10hāhākāro mahānāsīt sarvasainyeṣu bhārata
|
tamiṣuṃ sandhitaṃ dṛṣṭvā bhāradvājena saṃyuge ||10||

11tatrādbhutamapaśyāma dhṛṣṭadyumnasya pauruṣam
|
yadekaḥ samare vīrastasthau giririvācalaḥ ||11||

12taṃ ca dīptaṃ śaraṃ ghoramāyāntaṃ mṛtyumātmanaḥ
|
ciccheda śaravṛṣṭiṃ ca bhāradvāje mumoca ha ||12||

13tata uccukruśuḥ sarve pañcālāḥ pāṇḍavaiḥ saha
|
dhṛṣṭadyumnena tatkarma kṛtaṃ dṛṣṭvā suduṣkaram ||13||

14tataḥ śaktiṃ mahāvegāṃ svarṇavaidūryabhūṣitām
|
droṇasya nidhanākāṅkṣī cikṣepa sa parākramī ||14||

15tāmāpatantīṃ sahasā śaktiṃ kanakabhūṣitām
|
tridhā ciccheda samare bhāradvājo hasanniva ||15||

16śaktiṃ vinihatāṃ dṛṣṭvā dhṛṣṭadyumnaḥ pratāpavān
|
vavarṣa śaravarṣāṇi droṇaṃ prati janeśvara ||16||

17śaravarṣaṃ tatastattu sannivārya mahāyaśāḥ
|
droṇo drupadaputrasya madhye ciccheda kārmukam ||17||

18sa cchinnadhanvā samare gadāṃ gurvīṃ mahāyaśāḥ
|
droṇāya preṣayāmāsa girisāramayīṃ balī ||18||

19sā gadā vegavanmuktā prāyāddroṇajighāṃsayā
|
tatrādbhutamapaśyāma bhāradvājasya pauruṣam ||19||

20lāghavādvyasaṃyāmāsa gadāṃ hemavibhūṣitām
|
vyaṃsayitvā gadāṃ tāṃ ca preṣayāmāsa pārṣatam ||20||

21bhallān suniśitān pītān rukmapuṃkhān sudāruṇān
|
te tasya kavacaṃ bhittvā papuḥ śoṇitamāhave ||21||

22athānyaddhanurādāya dhṛṣṭadyumno mahārathaḥ
|
droṇaṃ yudhi parākramya śaraurvivyādha pañcabhiḥ ||22||

23rudhirāktau tatastau tu śuśubhāte nararṣabhau
|
vasantasamaye rājan puṣpitāviva kiṃśukau ||23||

24amarṣitastato rājan parākramya camūmukhe
|
droṇo drupada‌putrasya punaściccheda kārmukam ||24||

25athainaṃ chinnadhanvānaṃ śaraiḥ sannataparvabhiḥ
|
abhyavarṣadameyātmā vṛṣṭyā megha ivācalam ||25||

26sārathiṃ cāsya bhallena rathanīḍā‌dapātayat
|
athāsya caturo vāhāṃścaturbhirniśitaiḥ śaraiḥ ||26||

27pātayāmāsa samare siṃhanādaṃ nanāda ca
|
tato'pareṇa bhallena hastāccāpamathācchinat ||27||

28sa cchinnadhanvā viratho hatāśvo hatasārathiḥ
|
gadāpāṇiravārohat khyāpayan pauruṣaṃ mahat ||28||

29tāmasya viśikhaistūrṇaṃ pātayāmāsa bhārata
|
rathādanavarūḍhasya tada‌dbhutamivābhavat ||29||

30tataḥ sa vipulaṃ carma śatacandraṃ ca bhānumat
|
khaḍgaṃ ca vipulaṃ divyaṃ pragṛhya subhujo balī ||30||

31abhidudrāva vegena droṇasya vadhakāṅkṣayā
|
āmiṣārthī yathā siṃho vane mattamiva dvipam ||31||

32tatrādbhutamapaśyāma bhāradvājasya pauruṣam
|
lāghavaṃ cāstrayogaṃ ca balaṃ bāhvośca bhārata ||32||

33yadenaṃ śaravarṣeṇa vārayāmāsa pārṣatam
|
na śaśāka tato gantuṃ balavānapi saṃyuge ||33||

34nivāritastu droṇena dhṛṣṭadyumno mahārathaḥ
|
nyavārayaccharaughāṃstāṃścarmaṇā kṛtahastavat ||34||

35tato bhīmo mahābāhuḥ sahasā'bhyapatadbalī
|
sāhāyyakārī samare pārṣatasya mahātmanaḥ ||35||

36sa droṇaṃ niśitairbāṇai rājan vivyādha saptabhiḥ
|
pārṣataṃ ca rathaṃ tūrṇaṃ svakamārohayat tadā ||36||

37tato duryodhano rājan bhānumantamacodayat
|
sainyena mahatā yuktaṃ bhāradvājasya rakṣaṇe ||37||

38tataḥ sā mahatī senā kaliṅgānāṃ janeśvara
|
bhīmamabhyudyayau tūrṇaṃ tava putrasya śāsanāt ||38||

39pāñcālyamatha santyajya droṇo'pi rathināṃ varaḥ
|
virāṭadrupadau vṛddhau vārayāmāsa saṃyuge ||39||

40dhṛṣṭadyumno'pi samare dharmarājānamabhyayāt
|
tataḥ pravavṛte yuddhaṃ tumulaṃ lomaharṣaṇam ||40||

41kaliṅgānāṃ ca samare bhīmasya ca mahātmanaḥ
|
jagataḥ prakṣayakaraṃ ghorarūpaṃ bhayāvaham ||41||
