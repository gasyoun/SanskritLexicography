Mahābhārata 5.83 (Udyogaparvan)

1arjuna uvāca|
kurūṇāmadya sarveṣāṃ bhavān suhṛdamuttamaḥ
|
sambandhī dayito nityamubhayoḥ pakṣayorapi ||1||

2pāṇḍavairdhārtarāṣṭrāṇāṃ pratipādyamanāmayam
|
samarthaḥ praśamaṃ caiva kartumarhasi keśava ||2||

3tvamitaḥ puṇḍarīkākṣa suyodhanamamarṣaṇam
|
śāntyarthaṃ bhrātaraṃ brūyā yattadvācyamamitrahan ||3||

4tvayā dharmārthayuktaṃ ceduktaṃ śivamanāmayam
|
hitaṃ nādāsyate bālo diṣṭasya vaśameṣyati ||4||

5śrībhagavānuvāca
|
dharmyamasmaddhitaṃ caiva kurūṇāṃ yadanāmayam
|
eṣa yāsyāmi rājānaṃ dhṛtarāṣṭramabhīpsayā ||5||

6vaiśampāyana uvāca|
tato vyapetatamasi sūrye vimalavadgate
|
maitre muhūrte saṃprāpte mṛdvarciṣi divākare ||6||

7kaumude māsi revatyāṃ śaradante himāgame
|
sphītasasyasukhe kāle kalpaḥ sattvavatāṃ varaḥ ||7||

8maṅgalyāḥ puṇyanirghoṣā vācaḥ śṛṇvaṃśca sūnṛtāḥ
|
brāhmaṇānāṃ pratītānāmṛṣīṇāmiva vāsavaḥ ||8||

9kṛtvā paurvāhṇikaṃ kṛtyaṃ snātaḥ śuciralaṅkṛtaḥ
|
upatasthe vivasvantaṃ pāvakaṃ ca janārdanaḥ ||9||

10ṛṣabhaṃ pṛṣṭha ālabhya brāhmaṇānabhivādya ca
|
agniṃ pradakṣiṇaṃ kṛtvā paśyan kalyāṇamagrataḥ ||10||

11tatpratijñāya vacanaṃ pāṇḍavasya janārdanaḥ
|
śinernaptāramāsīnamabhyabhāṣata sātyakim ||11||

12ratha āropyatāṃ śaṅkhaścakraṃ ca gadayā saha
|
upāsaṅgāśca śaktyaśca sarvapraharaṇāni ca ||12||

13duryodhanaśca duṣṭātmā karṇaśca sahasaubalaḥ
|
na ca śatruravajñeyo durbalo'pi balīyasā ||13||

14tatastanmatamājñāya keśavasya puraḥsarāḥ
|
prasasruryojayiṣyanto rathaṃ cakragadābhṛtaḥ ||14||

15taṃ dīptamiva kālāgnimākāśagamivāśugam
|
sūryacandraprakāśābhyāṃ cakrābhyāṃ samalaṅkṛtam ||15||

16ardhacandraiśca candraiśca matsyaiḥ samṛgapakṣibhiḥ
|
puṣpaiśca vividhaiścitraṃ maṇiratnaiśca sarvaśaḥ ||16||

17taruṇādityasaṅkāśaṃ bṛhantaṃ cārudarśanam
|
maṇihemavicitrāṅgaṃ sudhvajaṃ supatākinam ||17||

18sūpaskaramanādhṛṣyaṃ vaiyāghraparivāraṇam
|
yaśoghnaṃ pratyamitrāṇāṃ yadūnāṃ nandivardhanam ||18||

19vājibhiḥ śaibya-sugrīva-meghapuṣpabalāhakaiḥ
|
snātaiḥ saṃpādayāmāsuḥ saṃpannaiḥ sarvasaṃpadā ||19||

20mahimānaṃ tu kṛṣṇasya bhūya evābhivardhayan
|
sughoṣaḥ patagendreṇa dhvajena yuyuje rathaḥ ||20||

21taṃ meruśikharaprakhyaṃ meghadundubhiniḥsvanam
|
āruroha rathaṃ śaurirvimānamiva kāmagam ||21||

22tataḥ sātyakimāropya prayayau puruṣottamaḥ
|
pṛthivīṃ cāntarikṣaṃ ca rathaghoṣeṇa nādayan ||22||

23vyapoḍhābhrastataḥ kālaḥ kṣaṇena samapadyata
|
śivaścānuvavau vāyuḥ praśāntamabhavadrajaḥ ||23||

24pradakṣiṇānulomāśca maṅgalyā mṛgapakṣiṇaḥ
|
prayāṇe vāsudevasya babhūvuranuyāyinaḥ ||24||

25maṅgalyārthapradaiḥ śabdairanvavartanta sarvaśaḥ
|
sārasāḥ śatapatrāśca haṃsāśca madhusūdanam ||25||

26mantrāhutimahāhomairhūyamānaśca pāvakaḥ
|
pradakṣiṇamukho bhūtvā vidhūmaḥ samapadyata ||26||

27vasiṣṭho vāmadevaśca bhūridyumno gayaḥ krathaḥ
|
śukra-nārada-vālmīkā marutaḥ kuśiko bhṛguḥ ||27||

28devabrahmarṣayaścaiva kṛṣṇaṃ yadusukhāvaham
|
pradakṣiṇamavartanta sahitā vāsavānujam ||28||

29evametairmahābhāgairmaharṣigaṇasādhubhiḥ
|
pūjitaḥ prayayau kṛṣṇaḥ kurūṇāṃ sadanaṃ prati ||29||

30taṃ prayāntamanuprāyāt kuntīputro yudhiṣṭharaḥ
|
bhīmasenārjunau cobhau mādrīputrau ca pāṇḍavau ||30||

31cekitānaśca vikrānto dhṛṣṭaketuśca cedipaḥ
|
drupadaḥ kāśirājaśca śikhaṇḍī ca mahārathaḥ ||31||

32dhṛṣṭadyumnaḥ saputraśca virāṭaḥ kekayaiḥ saha
|
saṃsādhanārthaṃ prayayuḥ kṣatriyāḥ kṣatriyarṣabham ||32||

33tato'nuvrajya govindaṃ dharmarājo yudhiṣṭhiraḥ
|
rājñāṃ sakāśe dyutimānuvācedaṃ vacastadā ||33||

34yo vai na kāmānna bhayānna lobhānnārthakāraṇāt
|
anyāyamanuvarteta sthirabuddhiralolupaḥ ||34||

35dharmajño dhṛtimān prājñaḥ sarvabhūteṣu keśavaḥ
|
īśvaraḥ sarvabhūtānāṃ devadevaḥ sanātanaḥ ||35||

36taṃ sarvaguṇasaṃpannaṃ śrīvatsakṛtalakṣaṇam
|
saṃpariṣvajya kaunteyaḥ saṃdeṣṭumupacakrame ||36||

37yudhiṣṭhira uvāca|
yā sā bālyātprabhṛtyasmān paryavardhayatābalā
|
upavāsatapaḥśīlā sadā svastyayane ratā ||37||

38devatātithipūjāsu guruśuśrūṣaṇe ratā
|
vatsalā priyaputrā ca priyā'smākaṃ janārdana ||38||

39suyodhanabhayādyā no'trāyatāmitrakarśana
|
mahato mṛtyusaṃbādhāduddharennaurivārṇavāt ||39||

40asmatkṛte ca satataṃ yayā duḥkhāni mādhava
|
anubhūtānyaduḥkhārhā tāṃ sma pṛccheranāmayam ||40||

41bhṛśamāśvāsayeścaināṃ putraśokapariplutām
|
abhivādya svajethāstvaṃ pāṇḍavān parikīrtayan ||41||

42ūḍhātprabhṛti duḥkhāni śvaśurāṇāmarindama
|
nikārānatadarhā ca paśyantī duḥkhamaśnute ||42||

43api jātu sa kālaḥ syāt kṛṣṇa duḥkhaviparyayaḥ
|
yadahaṃ mātaraṃ kliṣṭāṃ sukhaṃ dadyāmarindama ||43||

44pravrajanto'nudhāvantīṃ kṛpaṇāṃ putragṛddhinīm
|
rudatīmupahāyaināmagacchāma vayaṃ vanam ||44||

45na nūnaṃ mriyate duḥkhaiḥ sā cejjīvati keśava
|
tathā putrādhibhirgāḍhamārtā hyānartasatkṛta ||45||

46abhivādyātha sā kṛṣṇa tvayā madvacanādvibho
|
dhṛtarāṣṭraśca kauravyo rājānaśca vayodhikāḥ ||46||

47bhīṣmaṃ droṇaṃ kṛpaṃ caiva mahārājaṃ ca bālhikam
|
drauṇiṃ ca somadattaṃ ca sarvāṃśca bharatān prati ||47||

48viduraṃ ca mahāprājñaṃ kurūṇāṃ mantradhāriṇam
|
agādhabuddhiṃ marmajñaṃ svajethā madhusūdana ||48||

49ityuktvā keśavaṃ tatra rājamadhye yudhiṣṭhiraḥ
|
anujñāto nivavṛte kṛṣṇaṃ kṛtvā pradakṣiṇam ||49||

50vrajanneva tu bībhatsuḥ sakhāyaṃ puruṣarṣabham
|
abravīt paravīraghnaṃ dāśārhamaparājitam ||50||

51yadasmākaṃ vibho vṛttaṃ purā vai mantraniścaye
|
ardharājyasya govinda viditaṃ sarvarājasu ||51||

52tacceddadyādasaṅgena satkṛtyānavamanya ca
|
priyaṃ me syānmahābāho mucyeranmahato bhayāt ||52||

53ataścedanyathā kartā dhārtarāṣṭro'nupāyavit
|
antaṃ nūnaṃ kariṣyāmi kṣatriyāṇāṃ janārdana ||53||

54vaiśampāyana uvāca|
evamukte pāṇḍavena samahṛṣyadvṛkodaraḥ
|
muhurmuhuḥ krodhavaśāt prāvepata ca pāṇḍavaḥ ||54||

55vepamānaśca kaunteyaḥ prākrośanmahato ravān
|
dhanañjayavacaḥ śrutvā harṣotsiktamanā bhṛśam ||55||

56tasya taṃ ninadaṃ śrutvā saṃprāvepanta dhanvinaḥ
|
vāhanāni ca sarvāṇi śakṛnmūtre prasusru‌vuḥ ||56||

57ityuktvā keśavaṃ tatra tathā coktvā viniścayam
|
anujñāto nivavṛte pariṣvajya janārdanam ||57||

58teṣu rājasu sarveṣu nivṛtteṣu janārdanaḥ
|
tūrṇamabhyagamaddhṛṣṭaḥ śaibya-sugrīvavāhanaḥ ||58||

59te hayā vāsudevasya dārukeṇa pracoditāḥ
|
panthānamācemuriva grasamānā ivāmbaram ||59||

60athāpaśyanmahābāhurṛṣīnadhvani keśavaḥ
|
brāhmyā śriyā dīpyamānān sthitānubhayataḥ pathi ||60||

61so'vatīrya rathāttūrṇamabhivādya janārdanaḥ
|
yathāvṛttānṛṣīn sarvānabhyabhāṣata pūjayan ||61||

62kaccillokeṣu kuśalaṃ kacciddharmaḥ svanuṣṭhitaḥ
|
brāhmaṇānāṃ trayo varṇāḥ kaccittiṣṭhanti śāsane ||62||

63tebhyaḥ prayujyatāṃ pūjāṃ provāca madhusūdanaḥ
|
bhagavantaḥ kva saṃsiddhāḥ kā vīthī bhavatāmiha ||63||

64kiṃ vā kāryaṃ bhagavatāmahaṃ kiṃ karavāṇi vaḥ
|
kenārthenopasaṃprāptā bhagavanto mahītalam ||64||

65tamabravījjāmadagnya upetya madhusūdanam
|
pariṣyajya ca govindaṃ surāsurapateḥ sakhā ||65||

66devarṣayaḥ puṇyakṛto brāhmaṇāśca bahuśrutāḥ
|
rājarṣayaśca dāśārha mānayantastapasvinaḥ
|
devāsurasya draṣṭāraḥ purāṇasya mahāmate ||66||

67sametaṃ pārthivaṃ kṣatraṃ didṛkṣantaśca sarvataḥ
|
sabhāsadaśca rājānastvāṃ ca satyaṃ janārdanam ||67||

68etanmahatprekṣaṇīyaṃ draṣṭuṃ gacchāma keśava
|
dharmārthasahitā vācaḥ śrotumicchāma mādhava ||68||

69tvayocyamānāḥ kuruṣu rājamadhye paraṃtapa
|
bhīṣmadroṇādayaścaiva viduraśca mahāmatiḥ ||69||

70tvaṃ ca yādavaśārdūla sabhāyāṃ vai sameṣyatha
|
tava vākyāni divyāni tathā teṣāṃ ca mādhava ||70||

71śrotumicchāma govinda satyāni ca hitāni ca
|
āpṛṣṭo'si mahābāho punardrakṣyāmahe vayam ||71||

72yāhyavighnena vai vīra drakṣyāmastvāṃ sabhāgatam
|
āsīnamāsane divye balatejaḥsamāhitam ||72||
