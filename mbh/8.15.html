Mahābhārata 8.15 (Karṇaparvan)

1sañjaya uvāca|
bhīmasenaṃ tato drauṇī rājanvivyādha patriṇā
|
parayā tvarayā yukto darśayannastralāghavam ||1||

2athainaṃ punarājaghne navatyā niśitaiḥ śaraiḥ
|
sarvamarmāṇi saṃprekṣya marmajño laghuhastavat ||2||

3bhīmasenaḥ samākīrṇo drauṇinā niśitaiḥ śaraiḥ
|
rarāja samare rājan raśmivāniva bhāskaraḥ ||3||

4tataḥ śarasahasreṇa suprayuktena pāṇḍavaḥ
|
droṇaputramavacchādya siṃhanādamamuñcata ||4||

5śaraiḥ śarāṃstato drauṇiḥ saṃvārya yudhi pāṇḍavam
|
lalāṭe'bhyāhanadrājannārācena smayanniva ||5||

6lalāṭasthaṃ tato bāṇaṃ dhārayāmāsa pāṇḍavaḥ
|
yathā śrṛṅgaṃ vane dṛptaḥ khaḍgo dhārayate nṛpa ||6||

7tatā drauṇiṃ raṇe bhīmo yatamānaṃ parākramī
|
tribhirvivyādha nārācairlalāṭe vismayanniva ||7||

8lalāṭasthaistato bāṇairbrāhmaṇo'sau vyaśobhata
|
prāvṛṣīva yathā siktastriśṛṅgaḥ parvatottamaḥ ||8||

9tataḥ śaraśatairdrauṇirardayāmāsa pāṇḍavam
|
na cainaṃ kampayāmāsa mātariśveva parvatam ||9||

10tathaiva pāṇḍavo yuddhe drauṇiṃ śaraśataiḥ śitaiḥ
|
nākampayata saṃhṛṣṭo vāryogha iva parvatam ||10||

11tāvanyonyaṃ śarairghoraiśchā‌dayānau mahārathau
|
rathavaryagatau vīrau śuśubhāte balotkaṭau ||11||

12ādityāviva saṃdīptau lokakṣayakarāvubhau
|
svaraśmibhirivānyonyaṃ tāpayantau śarottamaiḥ ||12||

13tataḥ pratikṛte yatnaṃ kurvāṇau tau mahāraṇe
|
kṛtapratikṛte yattau śarasaṅghairabhītavat ||13||

14vyāghrāviva ca saṃgrāme ceratustau narottamau
|
śaradaṃṣṭrau durādharṣau cāpavaktrau bhayaṃkarau ||14||

15abhūtāṃ tāvadṛśyau ca śarajālaiḥ samaṃtataḥ
|
meghajālairiva cchannau gagane candrabhāskarau ||15||

16cakāśete muhūrtena tatastāvapyarinda‌mau
|
vimuktāvabhrajālena aṅgārakabudhāviva ||16||

17atha tatraiva saṃgrāme vartamāne sudāraṇe
|
apasavyaṃ tataścakre drauṇistatra vṛkodaram ||17||

18kirañcharaśatairugrairdhārābhiriva parvatam
|
na tu tanmamṛṣe bhīmaḥ śatrorvijayalakṣaṇam ||18||

19praticakre tato rājan pāṇḍavo'pyapasavyataḥ
|
maṇḍalānāṃ vibhāgeṣu gatapratyāgateṣu ca ||19||

20babhūva tumulaṃ yuddhaṃ tayoḥ puruṣasiṃhayoḥ
|
caritvā vividhānmārgān maṇḍalasthānameva ca ||20||

21śaraiḥ pūrṇāyatotsṛṣṭairanyonyamabhijaghnatuḥ
|
anyonyasya vadhe caiva cakraturyatnamuttamam ||21||

22īṣaturvirathaṃ caiva kartumanyonyamāhave
|
tato drauṇirmahāstrāṇi prāduścakre mahārathaḥ ||22||

23tānyastraireva samare pratijaghne'tha pāṇḍavaḥ
|
tato ghoraṃ mahārāja astrayuddhamavartata ||23||

24grahayuddhaṃ yathā ghoraṃ prajāsaṃharaṇe hyabhūt
|
te bāṇāḥ samasajjanta muktāstābhyāṃ tu bhārata ||24||

25dyotayanto diśaḥ sarvāstava sainyaṃ samaṃtataḥ
|
bāṇasaṃghairvṛtaṃ ghoramākāśaṃ samapadyata ||25||

26ulkāpātāvṛtaṃ yuddhaṃ prajānāṃ saṃkṣaye nṛpa
|
bāṇābhighātātsaṃjajñe tatra bhārata pāvakaḥ ||26||

27savisphuliṅgo dīptārciyaryo'dahadvāhinīdvayam
|
tatra siddhā mahārāja saṃpatanto'bruvanvacaḥ ||27||

28yuddhānāmati sarveṣāṃ yuddhametaditi prabho
|
sarvayuddhāni caitasya kalāṃ nārhanti ṣoḍaśīm ||28||

29nedṛśaṃ ca punaryuddhaṃ bhaviṣyati kadācana
|
aho jñānena saṃpannāvubhau brāhmaṇakṣatriyau ||29||

30aho śauryeṇa saṃpannāvubhau cograparākramau
|
aho bhīmabalo bhīma etasya ca kṛtāstratā ||30||

31aho vīryasya sāratvamaho sauṣṭhavametayoḥ
|
sthitāvetau hi samare kālāntakayamopamau ||31||

32rudrau dvāviva saṃbhūtau yathā dvāviva bhāskarau
|
yamau vā puruṣavyāghrau ghorarūpāvubhau raṇe ||32||

33iti vācaḥ sma śrūyante siddhānāṃ vai muhurmuhuḥ
|
siṃhanādaśca sañjajñe sametānāṃ divaukasām ||33||

34adbhutaṃ cāpyacintyaṃ ca dṛṣṭvā karma tayo raṇe
|
siddhacāraṇasaṃghānāṃ vismayaḥ samapadyata ||34||

35praśaṃsanti tadā devāḥ siddhāśca paramarṣayaḥ
|
sādhu drauṇe mahābāho sādhu bhīmeti cābruvan ||35||

36tau śūrau samare rājan parasparakṛtāgasau
|
parasparamudīkṣetāṃ krodhāduddhṛtya cakṣuṣī ||36||

37krodharaktekṣaṇau tau tu krodhātprasphuritādharau
|
krodhātsaṃdaṣṭadaśanau tathaiva daśanacchadau ||37||

38anyonyaṃ chādayantau sma śaravṛṣṭyā mahārathau
|
śarāmbudhārau samare śastravidyutprakāśinau ||38||

39tāvanyonyaṃ dhvajaṃ viddhvā sārathiṃ ca mahāraṇe
|
anyonyasya hayānviddhvā bibhidāte parasparam ||39||

40tataḥ kruddhau mahārāja bāṇau gṛhya mahāhave
|
ubhau cikṣipatustūrṇamanyonyasya vadhaiṣiṇau ||40||

41tau sāyakau mahārāja dyotamānau camūmukhe
|
ājaghnatuḥ samāsādya vajravegau durāsadau ||41||

42tau parasparavegācca śarābhyāṃ ca bhṛśāhatau
|
nipetaturmahāvīryau rathopasthe tayostadā ||42||

43tatastu sārathirjñātvā droṇaputramacetanam
|
apovāha raṇādrājansarvasainyasya paśyataḥ ||43||

44tathaiva pāṇḍavaṃ rājan vihvalantaṃ muhurmuhuḥ
|
apovāha rathenājau sārathiḥ śatrutāpanam ||44||
