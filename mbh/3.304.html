Mahābhārata 3.304 (Āraṇyakaparvan)

1kuntyuvāca|
brāhmaṇaṃ yantritā rājannupasthāsyāmi pūjayā
|
yathāpratijñaṃ rājendra na ca mithyā bravīmyaham||1||

2eṣa caiva svabhāvo me pūjayeyaṃ dvijāniti
|
tava caiva priyaṃ kāryaṃ śreyaśca paramaṃ mama||2||

3yadyevaiṣyati sāyāhne yadi prātaratho niśi
|
yadyardharātre bhagavānna me kopaṃ kariṣyati||3||

4lābho mamaiṣa rājendra yadvai pūjayatī dvijān
|
ādeśe tava tiṣṭhantī hitaṃ kuryāṃ narottama||4||

5visrabdho bhava rājendra na vyalīkaṃ dvijottamaḥ
|
vasan prāpsyatti te gehe satyametadbravīmi te||5||

6yatpriyaṃ ca dvijarayāsya hitaṃ caiva tavānagha
|
yatiṣyāmi tathā rājan vyetu te mānaso jvaraḥ||6||

7brāhmaṇā hi mahābhāgāḥ pūjitāḥ pṛthivīpate
|
tāraṇāya samarthāḥ syurviparīte vadhāya ca||7||

8sā'hametaddvijānantī toṣayiṣye dvijottamam
|
na matkṛte vyathāṃ rājan prāpsyasi dvijasattamāt||8||

9aparādhe'pi rājendra rājñāmaśreyase dvijāḥ
|
bhavanti cyavano yadvat sukanyāyāḥ kṛte purā||9||

10niyamena pareṇāhamupasthāsye dvijottamam
|
yathā tvayā narendredaṃ bhāṣitaṃ brāhmaṇaṃ prati||10||

11evaṃ bruvantīṃ bahuśaḥ pariṣvajya samarthya ca
|
iti ceti ca kartavyaṃ rājā sarvamathādiśat||11||

12rājovāca|
evametat tvayā bhadre kartavyamaviśaṅkayā
|
maddhitārthaṃ tathā''tmārthaṃ kulārthaṃ cāpyanindite||12||

13evamuktvā tu tāṃ kanyāṃ kuntibhojo mahāyaśāḥ
|
pṛthāṃ paridadau tasmai dvijāya dvijavatsalaḥ||13||

14iyaṃ brahman mama sutā bālā sukhavivardhitā
|
aparādhyeta yat kiñcinna kāryaṃ hṛdi tat tvayā||14||

15dvijātayo mahābhāgā vṛddhabālatapasviṣu
|
bhavantyakrodhanāḥ prāyo hyaparāddheṣu nityadā||15||

16sumahatyaparādhe'pi kṣāntiḥ kāryā dvijātibhiḥ
|
yathāśakti yathotsāhaṃ pūjā grāhyā dvijottama||16||

17tatheti brāhmaṇenokta sa rājā prītamānasaḥ
|
haṃsacandrāṃśusaṅkāśaṃ gṛhamasmai nyavedayat||17||

18tatrāgniśaraṇe klṛptamāsanaṃ tasya bhānumat
|
āhārādi ca sarvaṃ tat tathaiva pratyavedayat||18||

19nikṣipya rājaputrī tu tarndrā mānaṃ tathaiva ca
|
ātasthe paramaṃ yatnaṃ brāhmaṇasyābhirādhane||19||

20tatra sā brāhmaṇaṃ gatvā pṛthā śaucaparā satī
|
vidhivat paricārārhaṃ devavat paryatoṣayat||20||
