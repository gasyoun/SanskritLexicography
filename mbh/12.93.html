Mahābhārata 12.93 (Śāntiparvan)

1vāmadeva uvāca| yatrādharmaṃ praṇayate durbale balavattaraḥ
|
tāṃ vṛttimupajīvanti ye bhavanti tadanvayāḥ||1||

2rājānamanuvartante taṃ pāpābhipravartakam
|
avinītamanuṣyaṃ tatkṣipraṃ rāṣṭraṃ vinaśyati||2||

3yadvṛttamupajīvanti prakṛtisthasya mānavāḥ
|
tadeva viṣamasthasya svajano'pi na mṛṣyate||3||

4sāhasaprakṛtiryatra kiṃcidulbaṇamācaret
|
aśāstralakṣaṇo rājā kṣiprameva vinaśyati||4||

5yo'tyantācaritāṃ vṛttiṃ kṣatriyo nānuvartate
|
jitānāmajitānāṃ ca kṣatradharmādapaiti saḥ||5||

6dviṣanta kṛtakalyāṇaṃ gṛhītvā nṛpatiṃ raṇe
|
yo na mānayate dveṣātkṣatradharmādapaiti saḥ||6||

7śaktaḥ syātsusukho rājā kuryātkaraṇamāpadi
|
priyo bhavati bhūtānāṃ na ca vibhraśyate śriyaḥ||7||

8apriyaṃ yasya kurvīta bhūyastasya priyaṃ caret
|
na cireṇa priyaḥ sa syādyo'priyaḥ priyamācaret||8||

9mṛṣāvādaṃ pariharetkuryātpriyamayācitaḥ
|
na kāmānna ca saṃrambhānna dveṣāddharmamutsṛjet||9||

10nāpatrapeta praśneṣu nāvibhāvyāṃ giraṃ sṛjet
|
na tvareta na cāsūyettathā saṃgṛhyate paraḥ||10||

11priyenātibhṛśaṃ hṛṣyedapriyena ca saṃjvaret
|
na tapyedarthakṛcchreṣu prajāhitamanusmaran||11||

12yaḥ priyaṃ kurute nityaṃ guṇato vasudhādhipaḥ
|
tasya karmāṇi siddhyanti na ca saṃtyajyate śriyā||12||

13nivṛttaṃ pratikūleṣu vartamānamanupriye
|
bhaktaṃ bhajeta nṛpatiḥ sadaiva susamāhitaḥ||13||

14aprakīrṇendriyagrāmamatyantānugataṃ śucim
|
śaktaṃ caivānuraktaṃ ca yuṃjyānmahati karmaṇi||14||

15evametairguṇairyukto yo'nurajyati bhūmipam
|
bharturartheṣvapramattaṃ niyuṃjyādarthakarmaṇi||15||

16mūḍhamaindriyakaṃ lubdhamanāryacaritaṃ śaṭham
|
anatītopadhaṃ hiṃsraṃ durbuddhimabahuśrutam||16||

17tyaktodāttaṃ mādyarataṃ dyūta-strī-mṛgayāparam
|
kārye mahati yuñjāno hīyate nṛpatiḥ śriyā||17||

18rakṣitātmā ca yo rājā rakṣyānyaścānurakṣati
|
prajāśca tasya vardhante dhruvaṃ ca mahadaśnute||18||

19ye kecidbhūmipatayaḥ sarvāṃstānanvavekṣayet
|
suhṛdbhiranabhikhyātaistena rājā'tiricyate||19||

20apakṛtya balasthasya dūrastho'smīti nāśvaset
|
śyenābhipatanairete nipatanti pramādyataḥ||20||

21dṛḍhamūlastvaduṣṭātmā viditvā balamātmanaḥ
|
abalānabhiyuñjīta na tu ye balavattarāḥ||21||

22vikrameṇa mahīṃ labdhvā prajā dharmeṇa pālayet
|
āhave nidhanaṃ kuryādrājā dharmaparāyaṇaḥ||22||

23maraṇāntamidaṃ sarvaṃ neha kiñcidanāmayam
|
tasmāddharme sthito rājā prajā dharmeṇa pālayet||23||

24rakṣādhikaraṇaṃ yuddhaṃ tathā dharmānuśāsanam
|
mantracintāsukhaṃ kāle pañcabhirvardhate mahī||24||

25etāni yasya guptāni sa rājā rājasattama
|
satataṃ vartamāno'tra rājā dhatte mahīmimām||25||

26naitānyekena śakyāni sātatyenānuvīkṣitum
|
teṣu sarvaṃ pratiṣṭhāpya rājā bhuṃkte ciraṃ mahīm||26||

27dātāraṃ saṃvibhaktāraṃ mārdavopagataṃ śucim
|
asantyaktamanuṣyaṃ ca taṃ janāḥ kurvate nṛpam||27||

28yastu niḥśreyasaṃ śrutvā jñānaṃ tatpratipadyate
|
ātmano matamutsṛjya taṃ loko'nuvidhīyate||28||

29yo'rthakāmasya vacanaṃ prātikūlyānna mṛṣyate
|
śṛṇoti pratikūlāni sarvadā vimanā iva||29||

30agrāmyacaritāṃ vṛttiṃ yo na seveta nityadā
|
jitānāmajitānāṃ ca kṣatradharmādapaiti saḥ||30||

31nigṛhītādamātyācca strībhyaścaiva viśeṣataḥ
|
parvatādviṣamāddurgāddhastino'śvātsarīsṛpāt
|
etebhyo nityayuktaḥ san rakṣedātmānameva tu||31||

32mukhyānamātyānyo hitvā nihīnānkurute priyān
|
sa vai vyasanamāsādya gādhamārto na vindati||32||

33yaḥ kalyāṇaguṇān jñātīnpradveṣānno bubhūṣati
|
adṛḍhātmā dṛḍhakrodhaḥ sa mṛtyorvasate'ntike||33||

34atha yo guṇasampannānhṛdayasya priyānapi
|
priyeṇa kurute vaśyāṃściraṃ yaśasi tiṣṭhati||34||

35nākāle praṇayedarthānnāpriye jātu saṃjvaret
|
priye nātibhṛśaṃ tuṣyedyujyetārogyakarmaṇi||35||

36ke vānuraktā rājānaḥ ke bhayātsamupāśritāḥ
|
madhyasthadoṣāḥ ke caiṣāmiti nityaṃ vicintayet||36||

37na jātu balavānbhūtvā durbale viśvasetkvacit
|
bhāruṇḍasadṛśā hyete nipatanti pramādyataḥ||37||

38api sarvaguṇairyuktaṃ bhartāraṃ priyavādinam
|
abhidruhyati pāpātmā na tasmādviśvasejjanāt||38||

39evaṃ rājopaniṣadaṃ yayātiḥ smāha nāhuṣaḥ
|
manuṣyaviṣaye yukto hanti śatrūnanuttamān||39||
