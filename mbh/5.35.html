Mahābhārata 5.35 (Udyogaparvan)

1dhṛtarāṣṭra uvāca|
brūhi bhūyo mahābuddhe dharmārthasahitaṃ vacaḥ
|
śṛṇvato nāsti me tṛptirvicitrāṇīha bhāṣase ||1||

2vidura uvāca|
sarvatīrtheṣu vā snānaṃ sarvabhūteṣu cārjavam
|
ubhe tvete same syātāmārjavaṃ vā viśiṣyate ||2||

3ārjavaṃ pratipadyasva putreṣu satataṃ vibho
|
iha kīrtiṃ parāṃ prāpya pretya svargamavāpsyasi ||3||

4yāvat kīrtirmanuṣyasya puṇyā loke pragīyate
|
tāvatsa puruṣavyāghra svargaloke mahīyate ||4||

5atrāpyudāharantīmamitihāsaṃ purātanam
|
virocanasya saṃvādaṃ keśinyarthe sudhanvanā ||5||

6svayaṃvare sthitā kanyā keśinī nāma nāmataḥ
|
rūpeṇāpratimā rājan viśiṣṭapatikāmyayā||6||

7virocano'tha daiteyastadā tatrājagāma ha
|
prāptumicchaṃstatastatra daityendraṃ prāha keśinī||7||

8keśinyuvāca
|
kiṃ brāhmaṇāḥ svicchreyāṃso ditijāḥ svidvirocana
|
atha kena sma paryaṅkaṃ sudhanvā nādhirohati ||8||

9virocana uvāca|
prājāpatyāstu vai śreṣṭhā vayaṃ keśini sattamāḥ
|
asmākaṃ khalvime lokāḥ ke devāḥ ke dvijātayaḥ ||9||

10keśinyuvāca
|
ihaivāvāṃ pratīkṣāva upasthāne virocana
|
sudhanvā prātarāgantā paśyeyaṃ vāṃ samāgatau ||10||

11virocana uvāca|
tathā bhadre kariṣyāmi yathā tvaṃ bhīru bhāṣase
|
sudhanvānaṃ ca māṃ caiva prātardraṣṭā'si saṅgatau ||11||

12vidura uvāca|
atītāyāṃ ca śarvaryāmudite sūryamaṇḍale
|
athājagāma taṃ deśaṃ sudhanvā rājasattama
|
virocano yatra vibho keśinyā sahitaḥ sthitaḥ
|
sudhanvā ca samāgacchat prāhlādiṃ keśinīṃ tathā||12||

13samāgataṃ dvijaṃ dṛṣṭvā keśinī bharatarṣabha
|
pratyutthāyāsanaṃ tasmai pādyamarghyaṃ dadau punaḥ||13||

14sudhanvovāca
|
anvālabhe hiraṇmayaṃ prāhlāde te varāsanam
|
ekatvamupasampanno na tvāse'haṃ tvayā saha ||14||

15virocana uvāca|
tavārhate tu phalakaṃ kūrcaṃ vā'pyathavā bṛsī
|
sudhanvanna tvamarho'si mayā saha samāsanam ||15||

16sudhanvovāca
|
pitāputrau sahāsītāṃ dvau viprau kṣatriyāvapi
|
vṛddhau vaiśyau ca śūdrau ca na tvanyāviratetaram||16||

17pitā hi te samāsīnamupāsītaiva māmadhaḥ
|
bālaḥ sukhaidhito gehe na tvaṃ kiñcana budhyase ||17||

18virocana uvāca|
hiraṇyaṃ ca gavāśvaṃ ca yadvittamasureṣu naḥ
|
sudhanvan vipaṇetena praśnaṃ pṛcchāva ye viduḥ ||18||

19sudhanvovāca
|
hiraṇyaṃ ca gavāśvaṃ ca tavaivāstu virocana
|
prāṇayostu paṇaṃ kṛtvā praśnaṃ pṛcchāva ye viduḥ ||19||

20virocana uvāca|
āvāṃ kutra gamiṣyāvaḥ prāṇayorvipaṇe kṛte
|
na hi deveṣvahaṃ sthātā na manuṣyeṣu karhicit ||20||

21sudhanvovāca
|
pitaraṃ te gamiṣyāvaḥ prāṇayorvipaṇe kṛte
|
putrasyāpi sa hetorhi prahlādo nānṛtaṃ vadet ||21||

22vidura uvāca|
evaṃ kṛtapaṇau kruddhau tatrābhijagmatustadā
|
virocanasudhanvānau prahlādo yatra tiṣṭhati||22||

23prahlāda uvāca|
imau tau sampradṛśyete yābhyāṃ na caritaṃ saha
|
āśīviṣāviva kruddhāvekamārgavihāgatau ||23||

24kiṃ vai sahaivaṃ caratho na purā carataḥ saha
|
virocanaitat pṛcchāmi kiṃ te sakhyaṃ sudhanvanā ||24||

25virocana uvāca|
na me sudhanvanā sakhyaṃ prāṇayorvipaṇāvahe
|
prahlāda tattvaṃ pṛcchāmi mā praśnamanṛtaṃ vadeḥ ||25||

26prahlāda uvāca|
udakaṃ madhuparkaṃ vā'pyānayantu sudhanvane
|
brahmannabhyarcanīyo'si śvetā gauḥ pīvarīkṛtā ||26||

27sudhanvovāca|
udakaṃ madhuparkaṃ ca pathiṣvevārpitaṃ mama
|
prahlāda tvaṃ tu me tathyaṃ praśnaṃ prabrūhi pṛcchatoḥ|| kiṃ brāhmaṇāḥ svicchreyāṃsa utāhosvidvirocanaḥ||27||

28prahlāda uvāca|
putra eko mama brahmaṃstvaṃ ca sākṣādihāsthitaḥ
|
tayorvivadatoḥ praśnaṃ kathamasmadvidho vadet ||28||

29sudhanvovāca
|
gāṃ pradadyāstvaurasāya yadvā'nyatsyāt priyaṃ dhanam
|
dvayorvivadatostathyaṃ vācyaṃ ca matimaṃstvayā||29||

30prahlāda uvāca|
atha yo naiva prabrūyātsatyaṃ vā yadi vā'nṛtam
|
etatsudhanvan pṛcchāmi durvivaktā sma kiṃ vaset ||30||

31sudhanvovāca
|
yāṃ rātrimadhivinnā strī yāṃ caivākṣaparājitaḥ
|
yāṃ ca bhārābhitaptāṅgo durvivaktā sma tāṃ vaset ||31||

32nagare pratiruddhaḥ san bahirdvāre bubhukṣitaḥ
|
amitrān bhūyasaḥ paśyedyaḥ sākṣyamanṛtaṃ vadet ||32||

33pañca paśvanṛte hanti daśa hanti gavānṛte
|
śatamaśvānṛte hanti sahasraṃ puruṣānṛte ||33||

34hanti jātānajātāṃśca hiraṇyārthe'nṛtaṃ vadan
|
sarvaṃ bhūmyanṛte hanti mā sma bhūmyanṛtaṃ vadeḥ ||34||

35prahlāda uvāca|
mattaḥ śreyānaṅgirā vai sudhanvā tvadvirocana
|
mātā'sya śreyasī mātustasmāttvaṃ tena vai jitaḥ ||35||

36virocana sudhanvā'yaṃ prāṇānāmīśvarastava
|
sudhanvan punaricchāmi tvayā dattaṃ virocanam ||36||

37sudhanvovāca
|
yaddharmamavṛṇīthāstvaṃ na kāmādanṛtaṃ vadīḥ
|
punardadāmi te putraṃ tasmāt prahlāda durlabham ||37||

38eṣa prahlāda putraste mayā datto virocanaḥ
|
pādaprakṣālanaṃ kuryāt kumāryāḥ saṃnidhau mama ||38||

39vidura uvāca|
tasmādrājendra bhūmyarthe nānṛtaṃ vaktumarhasi
|
mā gamaḥ sasutāmātyo nāśaṃ putrārthamabruvan ||39||

40na devā yaṣṭimādāya rakṣanti paśupālavat
|
yaṃ tu rakṣitumicchanti buddhyā saṃvibhajanti tam ||40||

41yathā yathā hi puruṣaḥ kalyāṇe kurute manaḥ
|
tathā tathā'sya sarvārthāḥ siddhyante nātra saṃśayaḥ ||41||

42nainaṃ chandāṃsi vṛjināttārayanti māyāvinaṃ māyayā vartamānam
|
nīḍaṃ śakuntā iva jātapakṣāśchandāṃsyenaṃ prajahatyantakāle ||42||

43madyapānaṃ kalahaṃ pūgavairaṃ bhāryāpatyorantaraṃ jñātibhedam
|
rājadviṣṭaṃ strīpumāṃsorvivādaṃ varjyānyāhuryaśca panthāḥ praduṣṭaḥ ||43||

44sāmudrikaṃ vaṇijaṃ corapūrvaṃ śalākadhūrtaṃ ca cikitsakaṃ ca
|
ariṃ ca mitraṃ ca kuśīlavaṃ ca naitān sākṣye tvadhikurvīta sapta ||44||

45mānāgnihotramuta mānamaunaṃ mānenādhītamuta mānayajñaḥ
|
etāni catvāryabhayaṅkarāṇi bhayaṃ prayacchantyayathākṛtāni ||45||

46agāradāhī garadaḥ kuṇḍāśī somavikrayī
|
parvakāraśca sūcī ca mitradhruk pāradārikaḥ ||46||

47bhrūṇahā gurutalpī ca yaśca syātpānapo dvijaḥ
|
atitīkṣṇaśca kākaśca nāstiko vedanindakaḥ ||47||

48sruvapragrahaṇo vrātyaḥ kīnāśaścātmavānapi
|
rakṣetyuktaśca yo hiṃsyāt sarve brahmahabhiḥ samāḥ ||48||

49tṛṇolkayā jñāyate jātarūpaṃ vṛttena bhadro vyavahāreṇa sādhuḥ
|
śūro bhayeṣvarthakṛcchreṣu dhīraḥ kṛcchreṣvāpatsu suhṛdaścārayaśca ||49||

50jarā rūpaṃ harati hi dhairyamāśā mṛtyuḥ prāṇān dharmacaryāmasūyā
|
krodhaḥ śriyaṃ śīlamanāryasevā hriyaṃ kāmaḥ sarvamevābhimānaḥ ||50||

51śrīrmaṅgalāt prabhavati prāgalbhyāt saṃpravardhate
|
dākṣyāttu kurute mūlaṃ saṃyamāt pratitiṣṭhati ||51||

52aṣṭau guṇāḥ puruṣaṃ dīpayanti prajñā ca kaulyaṃ ca damaḥ śrutaṃ ca
|
parākramaścābahubhāṣitā ca dānaṃ yathāśakti kṛtajñatā ca ||52||

53etān guṇāṃstāta mahānubhāvā neko guṇaḥ saṃśrayate prasahya
|
rājā yadā satkurute manuṣyaṃ sarvān guṇāneṣa guṇo vibhāti ||53||

54aṣṭau nṛpemāni manuṣyaloke svargasya lokasya nidarśanāni
|
catvāryeṣāmanvavetāni sadbhiścatvāri caiṣāmanuyānti santaḥ ||54||

55yajño dānamadhyayanaṃ tapaśca catvāryetānyanvavetāni sadbhiḥ
|
damaḥ satyamārjavamānṛśaṃsyaṃ catvāryetānyanuyānti santaḥ ||55||

56ijyādhyayanadanāni tapaḥ satyaṃ kṣamā ghṛṇā
|
alobha iti mārgo'yaṃ dharmasyāṣṭavidhaḥ smṛtaḥ||56||

57tatra pūrvacaturvargo dambhārthamapi sevyate
|
uttaraśca caturvargo nāmahātmasu tiṣṭhati||57||

58na sā sabhā yatra na santi vṛddhā na te vṛddhā ye na vadanti dharmam
|
nāsau dharmo yatra na satyamasti na tat satyaṃ yacchalenābhyupetam ||58||

59satyaṃ rūpaṃ śrutaṃ vidyā kaulyaṃ śīlaṃ balaṃ dhanam
|
śauryaṃ ca citrabhāṣyaṃ ca daśeme svargayonayaḥ ||59||

60pāpaṃ kurvan pāpakīrtiḥ pāpamevāśnute phalam
|
puṇyaṃ kurvan puṇyakīrtiḥ puṇyamatyantamaśnute ||60||

61tasmāt pāpaṃ na kurvīta puruṣaḥ śaṃsitavrataḥ
|
pāpaṃ prajñāṃ nāśayati kriyamāṇaṃ punaḥ punaḥ||61||

62naṣṭaprajñaḥ pāpameva nityamārabhate naraḥ
|
puṇyaṃ prajñāṃ vardhayati kriyamāṇaṃ punaḥ punaḥ||62||

63vṛddhaprajñaḥ puṇyameva nityamārabhate naraḥ
|
puṇyaṃ kurvan puṇyakīrtiḥ puṇyaṃ sthānaṃ sma gacchati
|
tasmāt puṇyaṃ niṣeveta puruṣaḥ susamāhitaḥ||63||

64asūyako dandaśūko niṣṭhuro vairakṛcchaṭhaḥ
|
sa kṛcchraṃ mahadāpnoti na cirāt pāpamācaran ||64||

65anasūyaḥ kṛtaprajñaḥ śobhanānyācaran sadā
|
na kṛcchraṃ mahadāpnoti sarvatra ca virocate ||65||

66prajñāmevāgamayati yaḥ prājñebhyaḥ sa paṇḍitaḥ
|
prājño hyavāpya dharmārthau śaknoti sukhamedhitum ||66||

67divasenaiva tatkuryādyena rātrau sukhaṃ vaset
|
aṣṭamāsena tatkuryādyena varṣāḥ sukhaṃ vaset ||67||

68pūrve vayasi tatkuryādyena vṛddhaḥ sukhaṃ vaset
|
yāvajjīvena tatkuryādyena pretya sukhaṃ vaset ||68||

69jīrṇamannaṃ praśaṃsanti bhāryāṃ ca gatayauvanām
|
śūraṃ vigatasaṅgrāmaṃ gatapāraṃ tapasvinam ||69||

70dhanenādharmalabdhena yacchidramapidhīyate
|
asaṃvṛtaṃ tadbhavati tato'nyadavadīryate ||70||

71gururātmavatāṃ śāstā śāstā rājā durātmanām
|
atha pracchannapāpānāṃ śāstā vaivasvato yamaḥ ||71||

72ṛṣīṇāṃ ca nadīnāṃ ca kulānāṃ ca mahātmanām
|
prabhavo nādhigantavyaḥ strīṇāṃ duścaritasya ca ||72||

73dvijātipūjābhirato dātā jñātiṣu cārjavī
|
kṣatriyaḥ śīlabhāgrājaṃściraṃ pālayate mahīm ||73||

74suvarṇapuṣpāṃ pṛthivīṃ cinvanti puruṣāstrayaḥ
|
śūraśca kṛtavidyaśca yaśca jānāti sevitum ||74||

75buddhiśreṣṭhāni karmāṇi bāhumadhyāni bhārata
|
tāni jaṅghājaghanyāni bhārapratyavarāṇi ca ||75||

76duryodhane'tha śakunau mūḍhe duḥśāsane tathā
|
karṇe caiśvaryamādhāya kathaṃ tvaṃ bhūtimicchasi ||76||

77sarvairguṇairupetāstu pāṇḍavā bharatarṣabha
|
pitṛvattvayi vartante teṣu vartasva putravat ||77||
