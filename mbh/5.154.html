Mahābhārata 5.154 (Udyogaparvan)

1vaiśampāyana uvāca|
vāsudevasya tadvākyamanusmṛtya yudhiṣṭhiraḥ
|
punaḥ papraccha vārṣṇeyaṃ kathaṃ mando'bravīdidam ||1||

2asminnabhyāgate kāle kiṃ ca naḥ kṣamamacyuta
|
kathaṃ ca vartamānā vai svadharmānna cyavemahi ||2||

3duryodhanasya karṇasya śakuneḥ saubalasya ca
|
vāsudevamatajño'si mama sabhrātṛkasya ca ||3||

4vidurasyāpi tadvākyaṃ śrutaṃ bhīṣmasya cobhayoḥ
|
kuntyāśca vipulaprajña prajñā kārtsnyena te śrutā ||4||

5sarvametadatikramya vicārya ca punaḥpunaḥ
|
kṣamaṃ yanno mahābāho tadbravīhyavicārayan ||5||

6śrutvaitaddharmarājasya dharmārthasahitaṃ vacaḥ
|
meghadundubhinirghoṣaḥ kṛṣṇo vākyamathābravīt ||6||

7kṛṣṇa uvāca|
uktavānasmi yadvākyaṃ dharmārthasahitaṃ hitam
|
na tu tannikṛtiprajñe kauravye pratitiṣṭhati ||7||

8na ca bhīṣmasya durmedhāḥ śṛṇoti vidurasya vā
|
mama vā bhāṣitaṃ kiñcitsarvamevātivartate ||8||

9naiṣa kāmayate dharmaṃ naiṣa kāmayate yaśaḥ
|
jitaṃ sa manyate sarvaṃ durātmā karṇamāśritaḥ ||9||

10bandhamājñāpayāmāsa mama cāpi suyodhanaḥ
|
na ca taṃ labdhavān kāmaṃ durātmā pāpaniścayaḥ ||10||

11na ca bhīṣmo na ca droṇo yuktaṃ tatrāhaturvacaḥ
|
sarve tamanuvartante ṛte viduramacyuta ||11||

12śakuniḥ saubalaścaiva karṇaduḥśāsanāvapi
|
tvayyayuktānyabhāṣanta mūḍhā mūḍhamamarṣaṇam ||12||

13kiṃ ca tena mayoktena yānyabhāṣata kauravaḥ
|
saṃkṣepeṇa durātmā'sau na yuktaṃ tvayi vartate ||13||

14pārthiveṣu na sarveṣu ya ime tava sainikāḥ
|
yat pāpaṃ yanna kalyāṇaṃ sarvaṃ tasmin pratiṣṭhitam ||14||

15na cāpi vayamatyarthaṃ parityāgena karhicit
|
kauravaiḥ śamamicchāmastatra yuddhamanantaram ||15||

16vaiśampāyana uvāca|
tacchrutvā pārthivāḥ sarve vāsudevasya bhāṣitam
|
abruvanto mukhaṃ rājñaḥ samudaikṣanta bhārata ||16||

17yudhiṣṭhirastvabhiprāyamabhilakṣya mahīkṣitām
|
yogamājñāpayāmāsa bhīmārjunayamaiḥ saha ||17||

18tataḥ kilakilābhūtamanīkaṃ pāṇḍavasya ha
|
ājñāpite tadā yoge samahṛṣyanta sainikāḥ ||18||

19avadhyānāṃ vadhaṃ paśyan dharmarājo yudhiṣṭhiraḥ
|
niḥśvasan bhīmasenaṃ ca vijayaṃ cedamabravīt ||19||

20yadarthaṃ vanavāsaśca prāptaṃ duḥkhaṃ ca yanmayā
|
so'yamasmānupaityeva paro'narthaḥ prayatnataḥ ||20||

21tasmin yatnaḥ kṛto'smābhiḥ sa no hīnaḥ prayatnataḥ
|
akṛte tu prayatne'smānupāvṛttaḥ kalirmahān ||21||

22kathaṃ hyavadhyaiḥ saṅgrāmaḥ kāryaḥ saha bhaviṣyati
|
kathaṃ hatvā gurūn vṛddhān vijayo no bhaviṣyati ||22||

23tacchrutvā dharmarājasya savyasācī parantapaḥ
|
yaduktaṃ vāsudevena śrāvayāmāsa tadvacaḥ ||23||

24uktavān devakīputraḥ kuntyāśca vidurasya ca
|
vacanaṃ tattvayā rājannikhilenāvadhāritam ||24||

25na ca tau vakṣyato'dharmamiti me naiṣṭhikī matiḥ
|
nāpi yuktaṃ ca kaunteya nivartitumayudhyataḥ ||25||

26tacchrutvā vāsudevo'pi savyasācivacastadā
|
smayamāno'bravīdvākyaṃ pārthamevamiti bruvan ||26||

27tataste dhṛtasaṅkalpā yuddhāya sahasainikāḥ
|
pāṇḍaveyā mahārāja tāṃ rātriṃ sukhamāvasan ||27||
