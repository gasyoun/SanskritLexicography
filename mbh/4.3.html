Mahābhārata 4.3 (Virāṭaparvan)

1vaiśampāyana uvāca|ityevamuktvā puruṣapravīrastathā'rjuno dharmabhṛtāṃ variṣṭhaḥ
|
vākyaṃ tathā'sau virarāma bhūyo nṛpo'paraṃ bhrātaramābabhāṣe ||1||

2yudhiṣṭhira uvāca|kiṃ tvaṃ nakula kurvāṇastatra tāta cariṣyasi
|
karma tat tvaṃ samācakṣva rājye tasya mahīpateḥ
|
sukumāraśca śūraśca darśanīyaḥ sukhocitaḥ ||2||

3nakula uvāca|aśvabandho bhaviṣyāmi virāṭanṛpateraham
|
sarvathā jñānasampannaḥ kuśalaḥ parirakṣaṇe ||3||

4granthiko nāma nāmnā'haṃ karmaitat supriyaṃ mama
|
kuśalo'smyaśvaśikṣāyāṃ tathaivāśvacikitsane
|
priyāśca satataṃ me'śvāḥ kururāja yathā tava ||4||

5ye māmāmantrayiṣyanti virāṭanagare janāḥ
|
tebhya evaṃ pravakṣyāmi vihariṣyāmyahaṃ yathā ||5||

6pāṇḍavena purā tāta aśveṣvadhikṛtaḥ purā
|
virāṭanagare channaścariṣyāmi mahīpate ||6||

7yudhiṣṭhira uvāca|sahadeva kathaṃ tasya samīpe vihariṣyasi
|
kiṃ vā tvaṃ karma kurvāṇaḥ pracchanno vihariṣyasi ||7||

8sahadeva uvāca|gosaṅkhyātā bhaviṣyāmi virāṭasya mahīpateḥ
|
pratiṣeddhā ca dogdhā ca saṅkhyāne kuśalo gavām ||8||

9tantipāla iti khyāto nāmnā'haṃ viditastvatha
|
nipuṇaṃ ca cariṣyāmi vyetu te mānaso jvaraḥ ||9||

10ahaṃ hi satataṃ goṣu bhavatā prahitaḥ purā
|
tatra me kauśalaṃ sarvamavabuddhaṃ viśāmpate ||10||

11lakṣaṇaṃ cārataṃ cāpi gavāṃ yaccāpi maṅgalam
|
tat sarvaṃ me suviditamanyaccāpi mahīpate ||11||

12vṛṣabhānapi jānāmi rājan pūjitalakṣaṇān
|
yeṣāṃ mūtramupāghrāya api vandhyā prasūyate ||12||

13so'hamevaṃ cariṣyāmi pratiratra hi me sadā
|
na ca māṃ vetsyate kaścit toṣayiṣye ca pārthivam ||13||

14yudhiṣṭhira uvāca|iyaṃ hi naḥ priyā bhāryā prāṇebhyo'pi garīyasī
|
māteva paripālyā ca pūjyā jyeṣṭheva ca svasā ||14||

15kena sma draupadī kṛṣṇā karmaṇā vicariṣyati
|
na hi kiñcidvijānāti karma kartuṃ yathā striyaḥ ||15||

16sukumārī ca bālā ca rājaputrī yaśasvinī
|
pativratā mahābhāgā kathaṃ nu vicariṣyati ||16||

17mālyagandhānalaṅkārān vastrāṇi vividhāni ca
|
etānyevābhijānāti yato jātā hi bhāminī ||17||

18draupadyuvāca
|
sairandhryo rakṣitā lokaṃ bhujiṣyāḥ santi bhārata
|
naivamanyāḥ striyo yānti iti lokasya niścayaḥ ||18||

19sā'haṃ bruvāṇā sairandhrī kuśalā keśakarmaṇi
|
yudhiṣṭhirasya gehe vai draupadyāḥ paricārikā ||19||

20uṣitā'smīti vakṣyāmi pṛṣṭā rājñā ca bhārata
|
ātmaguptā cariṣyāmi yanmāṃ tvaṃ paripṛcchasi ||20||

21sudeṣṇāṃ pratyupasthāsye rājabhāryāṃ yaśasvinīm
|
sā rakṣiṣyati māṃ prāptāṃ mā bhūt te duḥkhamīdṛśam ||21||

22yudhiṣṭhira uvāca|kalyāṇaṃ bhāṣale kṛṣṇe kule jātā'si bhāmini
|
na pāpamabhijānāsi sādhvī sādhuvrate sthitā ||22||

23yathā na durhṛdaḥ pāpā bhavanti sukhinaḥ punaḥ
|
kuryāstat tvaṃ hi kalyāṇi lakṣayeyurna te tathā ||23||
