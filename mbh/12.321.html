Mahābhārata 12.321 (Śāntiparvan)

1yudhiṣṭhira uvāca|
kathaṃ nirvedamāpannaḥ śuko vaiyāsakiḥ purā
|
etadicchāmyahaṃ śrotuṃ paraṃ kautūhalaṃ hi me||1||

2avyaktavyaktatattvānāṃ niścayaṃ buddhiniścayam
|
vaktumarhasi kauravya devasyājasya yā kṛtiḥ||2||

3bhīṣma uvāca|
prākṛtena suvṛttena carantamakutobhayam
|
adhyāpya kṛstnaṃ svādhyāyamanvaśādvai pitā sutam||3||

4vyāsa uvāca |
dharmaṃ putra niṣevasva sutīkṣṇau ca himātapau
|
kṣutpipāse ca vāyuṃ ca jaya nityaṃ jitendriyaḥ||4||

5satyamārjavamakrodhamanasūyāṃ damaṃ tapaḥ
|
ahiṃsāṃ cānṛśaṃsyaṃ ca vidhivatparipālaya||5||

6satye tiṣṭha rato dharme hitvā sarvamanārjavam
|
devatātithiśeṣeṇa mātrāṃ prāṇasya saṃliha||6||

7phenamātropamo dehe jīve śakunivatsthite
|
anitye priyasaṃvāse kathaṃ svapiṣi putraka||7||

8apramatteṣu jāgratsu nityayukteṣu śatruṣu
|
antaraṃ lipsamāneṣu bālastvaṃ nāvabudhyase||8||

9ahaḥsu gaṇyamāneṣu kṣīyamāṇe tathā''yuṣi
|
jīvite likhyamāne ca kimutthāya na dhāvasi||9||

10aihalaukikamīhante māṃsaśoṇitavardhanam
|
pāralaukikakāryeṣu prasuptā bhṛśanāstikāḥ||10||

11dharmāya ye'bhyasūyanti buddhimohānvitā narāḥ
|
apathā gacchatāṃ teṣāmanuyātā'pi pīḍyate||11||

12ye tu tuṣṭāḥ śrutiparā mahātmāno mahābalāḥ
|
dharmyaṃ panthānamārūḍhāstānupāssva ca pṛccha ca||12||

13upadhārya mataṃ teṣāṃ budhānāṃ dharmadarśinām
|
niyaccha parayā buddhyā cittamutpathagāmi vai||13||

14ādyakālikayā buddhyā dūre śva iti nirbhayāḥ
|
sarvabhakṣyā na paśyanti karmabhūmimacetasaḥ||14||

15dharmaṃ niḥśreṇimāsthāya kiñcitkiñcatsamāruha
|
koṣakāravadātmānaṃ veṣṭayannānubudhyase||15||

16nāstikaṃ bhinnamaryādaṃ kūlapātamiva sthitam
|
vāmataḥ kuru visrabyo naraṃ veṇumivoddhṛtam||16||

17kāmaṃ krodhaṃ ca mṛtyuṃ ca pañcendriyajalā nadīm
|
nāvaṃ dhṛtimayīṃ kṛtvā janmadurgāṇi santara||17||

18mṛtyunā'bhyāhate loke jarayā paripīḍite
|
amoghāsu patantīṣu dharmapotena saṃtara||18||

19tiṣṭhantaṃ ca śayānaṃ ca mṛtyuranveṣate yadā
|
nirvṛttiṃ labhate kasmādakasmānmṛtyunāśitaḥ||19||

20sañcinvānakamevainaṃ kāmānāmavitṛptakam
|
vṛkīvoraṇamāsādya mṛtyurādāya gacchati||20||

21kramaśaḥ sañcitaśikho dharmabuddhimayo mahān
|
andhakāre praveṣṭavyaṃ dīpo yatnena dhāryatām||21||

22sampatandehajālāni kadācidiha mānuṣe
|
brāhmaṇyaṃ labhate jantustatputra paripālaya||22||

23brāhmaṇasya tu deho'yaṃ na kāmārthāya jāyate
|
iha kleśāya tapase pretya tvanupamaṃ sukham||23||

24brāhmaṇyaṃ bahubhiravāpyate tapobhistallabdhvā na ratipareṇa helitavyam
|
svādhyāye tapasi dame ca nityayuktaḥ kṣemārthī kuśalapara: sadā yatasva||24||

25avyaktaprakṛtirayaṃ kalāśarīraḥ sūkṣmātmā kṣaṇatruṭiśo nimeṣaromā
|
saṃtvāsyaḥ samabalaśuklakṛṣṇanetro māsāṅgo dravati vayohayo narāṇām||25||

26taṃ dṛṣṭvā prasṛtamajasramugravegaṃ gacchantaṃ satatamihānvavekṣamāṇam
|
cakṣuste yadi na parapraṇetṛneyaṃ dharme te bhavatu manaḥ paraṃ niśāmya||26||

27ye cātra pracalitadharmakāmavṛttāḥ krośantaḥ satatamaniṣṭasaṃprayogāḥ
|
kliśyantaḥ parigatavedanāśarīrā bahvībhiḥ subhṛśamadharmakāraṇābhiḥ||27||

28rājā sadā dharmapara: śubhāśubhasya goptā samīkṣya sukṛtināṃ dadhāti lokān
|
bahuvidhamapi carati praviśati sukhamanupagataṃ niravadyam||28||

29śvāno bhīṣaṇakāyā ayomukhāni vayāṃsi balagṛdhra(kurara)pakṣiṇāṃ ca saṃghāḥ
|
narakadane rudhirapā guruvacananudamuparataṃ viśantyasanta||29||

30maryādā niyatāḥ svayambhuvā ya ihemā: prabhinatti daśa guṇā mano'nugatvāt
|
nivasati bhṛśamasukhaṃ pitṛviṣayavipinamavagāhya sa pāpaḥ||30||

31yo lubdhaḥ subhṛśaṃ priyānṛtaśca manuṣya: satatanikṛtivañcanābhirati: syāt
|
upanidhibhirasukhakṛtsa paramanirayago bhṛśamasukhamanubhavati duṣkṛtakarmā||31||

32uṣṇāṃ vaitaraṇīṃ mahānadīmavagāḍho'si patravanabhinnagātraḥ
|
paraśuvanaśayo nipatito vasati ca mahāniraye bhṛśārtaḥ||32||

33mahāpadāni katthase na cāpyavekṣase param
|
cirasya mṛtyukārikāmanāgatāṃ na budhyase||33||

34prayāyatāṃ kimāsyate samutthitaṃ mahadbhayam
|
atipramāthi dāruṇaṃ sukhasya saṃvidhīyatām||34||

35purā mṛta: praṇīyate yamasya rājaśāsanāt
|
tvamantakāya dāruṇaiḥ prayatnamārjave kuru||35||

36purā samūlabāndhavaṃ prabhurharatyaduḥkhavit
|
taveha jīvitaṃ yamo na cāsti tasya vārakaḥ||36||

37purā'bhivāti māruto yamasya yaḥ puraḥsaraḥ
|
puraika eva nīyase kuruṣva sāṃparāyikam||37||

38purā sa hi kva eva te pravāti māruto'ntakaḥ
|
purā ca vibhramanti te diśo mahābhayāgame||38||

39śrutiśca sannirudhyate purā tveha putraka
|
samākulasya gacchataḥ samādhimuttamaṃ kuru||39||

40śubhāśubhe purā kṛte pramādakarmaviplute
|
smaranpurā na tapyase nidhatsva kevalaṃ nidhim||40||

41purā jarā kalevaraṃ vijarjarī karoti te
|
balāṅgarūpahāriṇī nidhatsva kevalaṃ nidhim||41||

42purā śarīramantako bhinatti rogasārathiḥ
|
prasahya jīvitakṣaye tapo mahatsamācara||42||

43purā vṛkā bhayaṃkarā manuṣyadehagocarāḥ
|
abhidravanti sarvato yatasva puṇyaśīlane||43||

44purāndhakāramekako'nupaśyasi tvarasva vai
|
purā hiraṇyamayānnagānnirīkṣase'drimūrdhani||44||

45purā kusaṅgattāni te suhṛnmukhāśca śatravaḥ
|
vicālayanti darśanādghaṭasva putra yatparam||45||

46dhanasya yasya rājato bhayaṃ na cāsti corataḥ
|
mṛtaṃ ca yanna muñcati samarjayasva taddhanam||46||

47na tatra saṃviyujyate svakarmabhiḥ parasparam
|
yadeva yasya yautakaṃ tadeva tatra so'śnute||47||

48paratra yena jīvyate tadeva putra dīyatām
|
dhanaṃ yadakṣaraṃ dhruvaṃ samarjayasva tatsvayam||48||

49na yāvadeva pacyate mahājanasya yāvakam
|
apakva eva yāvake purā pralīyase tvaram||49||

50na mātṛputrabāndhavā na saṃstutaḥ priyo janaḥ
|
anuvrajanti saṃkaṭe vrajantamekapātinam||50||

51yadeva karma kevalaṃ purā kṛtaṃ śubhāśubham
|
tadeva putra sārthikaṃ bhavatyamutra gacchataḥ||51||

52hiraṇyaratnasañcayāḥ śubhāśubhena saṃñcitāḥ
|
na tasya dehasaṃkṣaye bhavanti kāryasādhakāḥ||52||

53paratragāmikasya te kṛtākṛtasya karmaṇaḥ
|
na sākṣi ātmanā samo nṛṇāmihāsti kaścana||53||

54manuṣyadehaśūnyakaṃ bhavatyamutra gacchataḥ
|
praviśya buddhicakṣuṣā pradṛśyate hi sarvaśaḥ||54||

55ihāgnisūryavāyavaḥ śarīramāśritāstrayaḥ
|
ta eva tasya sākṣiṇo bhavanti dharmadarśinaḥ||55||

56aharniśeṣu sarvataḥ spṛśatsu sarvacāriṣu
|
prakāśagūḍhavṛttiṣu svadharmameva pālaya||56||

57anekapāripanthike virūparaudramakṣike
|
svameva karma rakṣyatāṃ svakarma tatra gacchati||57||

58na tatra saṃvibhajyate svakarmaṇā parasparam
|
tathā kṛtaṃ svakarmajaṃ tadeva bhujyate phalam||58||

59yathāpsarogaṇāḥ phalaṃ sukhaṃ maharṣibhiḥ saha
|
tathā''pnuvanti karmajaṃ vimānakāmagāminaḥ||59||

60yatheha yatkṛtaṃ śubhaṃ vipāpmabhiḥ kṛtātmabhiḥ
|
tadā''pnuvanti mānavāstathā viśuddhayonayaḥ||60||

61prajāpateḥ salokatāṃ bṛhaspateḥ śatakratoḥ
|
vrajanti te parāṃ gatiṃ gṛhasthadharmasetubhiḥ||61||

62sahasraśo'pyanekaśaḥ pravaktumutsahāma te
|
abuddhimohanaṃ punaḥ prabhurnināya pāvakaḥ||62||

63gatā triraṣṭavarṣatā dhruvo'si pañcaviṃśakaḥ
|
kuruṣva dharmasañcayaṃ vayo hi te'tivartate||63||

64purā karoti so'ntakaḥ pramādagomukhāṃ camūm
|
yathā gṛhītamutthitastvarasva dharmapālane||64||

65yathā tvameva pṛṣṭhatastvamagrato gamiṣyasi
|
tathā gatiṃ gamiṣyataḥ kimātmanā pareṇa vā||65||

66yadekapātinā satāṃ bhavatyamutra gacchatām
|
bhayeṣu sāmparāyikaṃ nidhatsva kevalaṃ nidhim||66||

67sakūlamūlabāndhavaṃ prabhurharatyasaṅgavān
|
na santi yasya vārakāḥ kuruṣva dharmasaṃnidhim||67||

68idaṃ nidarśanaṃ mayā taveha putra sāṃpratam
|
svadarśanānumānataḥ pravarṇitaṃ kuruṣva tat||68||

69dadhāti yaḥ svakarmaṇā dadāti yasya kasyacit
|
abuddhimohajairguṇaiḥ sa eka eva yujyate||69||

70śrutaṃ samastamaśnute prakurvataḥ śubhāḥ kriyāḥ
|
tadetadarthadarśanaṃ kṛtajñamarthasaṃhitam||70||

71vibandhanī rajjureṣā yā grāme vasato ratiḥ
|
chittvaitāṃ sukṛto yānti naināṃ chindanti duṣkṛtaḥ||71||

72kiṃ te dhanena kiṃ bandhubhiste kiṃ te putraiḥ putraka yo mariṣyasi
|
ātmānamanviccha guhāṃ praviṣṭaṃ pitāmahāste kva gatāśca sarve||72||

73śvaḥ kāryamadya kurvīta pūrvāhṇe cāparāhṇikam
|
na hi pratīkṣate mṛtyuḥ kṛtaṃ vāsya na vā'kṛtam||73||

74anugamya vināśānte nivartante ha bāndhavāḥ
|
agnau prakṣipya puruṣaṃ jñātayaḥ suhṛdastathā||74||

75nāstikānniranukrośānnarānpāpamate sthitān
|
vāmataḥ kuru visrabdhaṃ paraṃ prepsuratandritaḥ||75||

76evamabhyāhate loke kālenopanipīḍite
|
sumahaddhairyamālaṃbya dharmaṃ sarvātmanā kuru||76||

77athemaṃ darśanopāyaṃ samyagyo vetti mānavaḥ
|
samyak svadharmaṃ kṛtveha paratra sukhamaśnute||77||

78na dehabhede maraṇaṃ vijānatāṃ na ca praṇāśaḥ svanupālite pathi
|
dharmaṃ hi yo vardhayate sa paṇḍito ya eva dharmāccyavate sa muhyati||78||

79prayuktayoḥ karmapathi svakarmaṇo: phalaṃ prayoktā labhate yathākṛtam
|
nihīnakarmā nirayaṃ prapadyate triviṣṭapaṃ gacchati dharmapāragaḥ||79||

80sopānabhūtaṃ svargasya mānuṣyaṃ prāpya durlabham
|
tathā''tmānaṃ samādadhyādbhraśyate na punaryathā||80||

81yasya nokrāmati mati: svargamārgānusāriṇī
|
tamāhuḥ puṇyakarmāṇamaśocyaṃ putrabāndhavaiḥ||81||

82yasya nopahatā vuddhirniścaye hyavalambate
|
svarge kṛtāvakāśasya nāsti tasya mahadbhayam||82||

83tapovaneṣu ye jātāstatraiva nidhanaṃ gatāḥ
|
teṣāmalpataro dharmaḥ kāmabhogānajānatām||83||

84yastu bhogānparityajya śarīreṇa tapaścaret
|
na tena kiñcinna prāptaṃ tanme bahu mataṃ phalam||84||

85mātāpitṛsahasrāṇi putradāraśatāni ca
|
anāgatānyatītāni kasya te kasya vā vayam||85||

86ahameko na me kaścinnāhamanyasya kasyacit
|
na taṃ paśyāmi yasyāhaṃ tanna paśyāmi yo mama||86||

87na teṣāṃ bhavatā kāryaṃ na kāryaṃ tava tairapi
|
svakṛtaistāni jātāni bhavāṃścaiva gamiṣyati||87||

88iha loke hi dhanināṃ svajana: svajanāyate
|
svajanastu daridrāṇāṃ jīvatāmapi naśyati||88||

89saṃcinotyaśubhaṃ karma kalatrāpekṣayā naraḥ
|
tata: kleśamavāpnoti paratreha tathaiva ca||89||

90paśyati cchinnabhūtaṃ hi jīvalokaṃ svakarmaṇā
|
tatkuruṣva tathā putra kṛtsnaṃ yatsamudāhṛtam||90||

91tadetatsaṃpradṛśyaiva karmabhūmiṃ prapaśyataḥ
|
śubhānyācaritavyāni paralokamabhīpsatā||91||

92māsartusaṃjñāparivartakeṇa sūryāgninā rātridivendhanena
|
svakarmaniṣṭhā phalasākṣikeṇa bhūtāni kālaḥ pacati prasahya||92||

93dhanena kiṃ yanna dadāti nāśnute balena kiṃ yena ripuṃ na bādhate
|
śrutena kiṃ yena na dharmamācaretkimātmanā yo na jitendriyo vaśī||93||

94bhīṣma uvāca|
idaṃ dvaipāyanavaco hitamuktaṃ niśamya tu
|
śuko gataḥ parityajya pitaraṃ mokṣadaiśikam||94||
