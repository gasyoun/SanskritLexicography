Mahābhārata 1.102 (Ādiparvan)

1vaiśaṃpāyana uvāca|
hate citrāṅgade bhīṣmo bāle bhrātari kaurava
|
pālayāmāsa tadrājyaṃ satyavatyā mate sthitaḥ||1||

2saṃprāptayauvanaṃ dṛṣṭvā bhrātaraṃ dhīmatāṃ varaḥ
|
bhīṣmo vicitravīryasya vivāhāyākaronmatim||2||

3atha kāśipaterbhīṣmaḥ kanyāstisro'psaropamāḥ
|
śuśrāva sahitā rājanvṛṇvānā vai svayaṃvaram||3||

4tataḥ sa rathināṃ śreṣṭho rathenaikena śatrujit
|
jagāmānumate mātuḥ purīṃ vārāṇasīṃ prabhuḥ||4||

5tatra rājñaḥ samuditānsarvataḥ samupāgatān
|
dadarśa kanyāstāśvaiva bhīṣmaḥ śāntanunandanaḥ||5||

6kīrtyamāneṣu rājñāṃ tu tadā nāmasu sarvaśaḥ
|
ekākinaṃ tadā bhīṣmaṃ vṛddhaṃ śāntanunandanam||6||

7sodvegā iva taṃ dṛṣṭvā kanyāḥ paramaśobhanāḥ
|
apākrāmanta tāḥ sarvā vṛddha ityeva cintayā||7||

8vṛddhaḥ paramadharmātmā valīpalitadhāraṇaḥ
|
kiṃ kāraṇamihāyāto nirlajjo bharatarṣabhaḥ||8||

9mithyāpratijño lokeṣu kiṃ vadiṣyati bhārata
|
brahmacārīti bhīṣmo hi vṛthaiva prathito bhuvi||9||

10ityevaṃ prabuvantaste hasanti sma nṛpādhamāḥ
|
vaiśaṃpāyana uvāca|
kṣatriyāṇāṃ vacaḥ śrutvā bhīṣmaścukrodha bhārata||10||

11bhīṣmastadā svayaṃ kanyā varayāmāsa tāḥ prabhuḥ
|
uvāca ca mahīpālānrājañjaladaniḥsvanaḥ||11||

12rathamāropya tāḥ kanyā bhīṣmaḥ praharatāṃ varaḥ
|
āhūya dānaṃ kanyānāṃ guṇavadbhyaḥ smṛtaṃ budhaiḥ||12||

13alaṅkṛtya yathāśakti pradāya ca dhanānyapi
|
prayacchantyapare kanyā mithunena gavāmapi||13||

14vittena kathitenānye balenānye'numānya ca
|
pramattāmupayantyanye svayamanye ca vindate||14||

15ārṣaṃ vidhiṃ puraskṛtya dārānvindanti cāpare
|
aṣṭamaṃ tamatho vitta vivāhaṃ kavibhirvṛtam||15||

16svayaṃvaraṃ tu rājanyāḥ praśaṃsantyupayānti ca
|
pramathya tu hṛtāmāhurjyāyasīṃ dharmavādinaḥ||16||

17tā imāḥ pṛthivīpālā jihīrṣāmi balāditaḥ
|
te yatadhvaṃ paraṃ śaktyā vijayāyetarāya vā||17||

18sthito'haṃ pṛthivīpālā yuddhāya kṛtaniścayaḥ
|
evamuktvā mahīpālānkāśirājaṃ ca vīryavān||18||

19sarvāḥ kanyāḥ sa kauravyo rathamāropya ca svakam
|
āmantrya ca sa tānprāyācchīghraṃ kanyāḥ pragṛhya tāḥ||19||

20tataste pārthivāḥ sarve samutpeturamarṣitāḥ
|
saṃspṛśantaḥ svakānbāhūndaśanto daśanacchadān||20||

21teṣāmābharaṇānyāśu tvaritānāṃ vimuñcatām
|
āmuñcatāṃ ca varmāṇi saṃbhramaḥ sumahānabhūt||21||

22tārāṇāmiva saṃpāto babhūva janamejaya
|
bhūṣaṇānāṃ ca sarveṣāṃ kavacānāṃ ca sarvaśaḥ||22||

23sa varmabhirbhūṣaṇaiśca prakīryadbiritastataḥ
|
sakrodhāmarṣajihmabhrū kaṣāyīkṛtalocanāḥ||23||

24sūtopaklṛptān rucirānsadaśvairupakalpitān
|
rathānāsthāya te vīrāḥ sarvapraharaṇānvitāḥ||24||

25prayāntamatha kauravyamanusasrurudāyudhāḥ
|
tataḥ samabhavadyuddhaṃ teṣāṃ tasya ca bhārata
|
ekasya ca bahūnāṃ ca tumulaṃ romaharṣaṇam||25||

26te tvindaśasāhasrāṃstasminyugapadākṣipan
|
aprāptāṃścaiva tānāśu bhīṣmaḥ sarvāṃstathā'ntarā||26||

27acchinaccharavarṣeṇa mahatā lomavāhinā
|
tataste pārthivāḥ sarve sarvataḥ parivārya tam||27||

28vavṛṣuḥ śaravarṣeṇa varṣeṇevādrimambudāḥ
|
sa taṃ bāṇamayaṃ varṣaṃ śarairāvārya sarvataḥ||28||

29tataḥ sarvānmahīpālānparyavidhyattribhistribhiḥ
|
ekaikastu tato bhīṣmaṃ rājanvivyādha pañcabhiḥ||29||

30sa ca tānprativivyādha dvābhyāṃ dvābhyāṃ parākraman
|
tadyuddhamāsīttumulaṃ ghoraṃ devāsuropamam||30||

31paśyatāṃ lokavīrāṇāṃ śaraśaktisamākulam
|
sa dhanūṃṣi dhvajāgrāṇi varmāṇi ca śirāṃsi ca||31||

32ciccheda samare bhīṣmaḥ śataśotha sahasraśaḥ
|
tasyātipuruṣānanyāঁllāghavaṃ rathacāriṇaḥ||32||

33rakṣaṇaṃ cātmanaḥ saṃkhye śatravo'pyabhyapūjayan
|
tānvinirjitya tu raṇe sarvaśastrabhṛtāṃ varaḥ||33||

34kanyābhiḥ sahitaḥ prāyādbhārato bhāratānprati
|
tatastaṃ pṛṣṭhato rājañcchālvarājo mahārathaḥ||34||

35abhyagacchadameyātmā bhīṣmaṃ śāntanavaṃ raṇe
|
vāraṇaṃ jaghane bhindandantābhyāmaparo yathā||35||

36vāsitāmanusaṃprāpto yūthapo balināṃ varaḥ
|
strīkāmastiṣṭha tiṣṭheti bhīṣmamāha sa pārthivaḥ||36||

37sālvarājo mahābāhuramarṣeṇa pracoditaḥ
|
tataḥ sa puruṣavyāghro bhīṣmaḥ parabalārdanaḥ||37||

38tadvākyākulitaḥ krodhādvidhūmogniriva jvalan
|
vitateṣu dhanuṣpāṇirvikuñcitalalāṭabhṛt||38||

39kṣatradharmaṃ samāsthāya vyapetabhayasaṃbhramaḥ
|
nivartayāmāsa rathaṃ sālvaṃ prati mahārathaḥ||39||

40nivartamānaṃ taṃ dṛṣṭvā rājānaḥ sarva eva te
|
prekṣakāḥ samapadyanta bhīṣmasālvasamāgame||40||

41tau vṛṣāviva nardantau balinau vāsitāntare
|
anyonyamabhivartetāṃ balavikramaśālinau||41||

42tato bhīṣmaṃ śāntanavaṃ śaraiḥ śatasahasraśaḥ
|
sālvarājo naraśreṣṭhaḥ samavākiradāśugaiḥ||42||

43pūrvamabhyarditaṃ dṛṣṭvā bhīṣmaṃ sālvena te nṛpāḥ
|
vismitāḥ samapadyanta sādhusādhviti cābruvan||43||

44lāghavaṃ tasya te dṛṣṭvā samare sarvapārthivāḥ
|
apūjayanta saṃhṛṣṭā vāgbhiḥ sālvaṃ narādhipam||44||

45kṣatriyāṇāṃ tato vācaḥ śrutvā parapurañjayaḥ
|
kruddhaḥ śāntanavo bhīṣmastiṣṭha tiṣṭhetyabhāṣata||45||

46sārathiṃ cābravītkruddho yāhi yatraiṣa pārthivaḥ
|
yāvadenaṃ nihanmyadya bhujaṅgamiva pakṣirāṭ||46||

47tato'straṃ vāruṇaṃ samyagyojayāmāsa kauravaḥ
|
tenāśvāṃścaturo'mṛdgācchālvarājasya bhūpate||47||

48astrairastrāṇi saṃvārya sālvarājasya kauravaḥ
|
bhīṣmo nṛpatiśārdūla nyavadhīttasya sārathim||48||

49astreṇa cāsyāthaindreṇa nyavadhītturagottamān
|
kanyāhetornaraśreṣṭha bhīṣmaḥ śāntanavastadā||49||

50jitvā visarjayāmāsa jīvantaṃ nṛpasattamam
|
tataḥ sālvaḥ svanagaraṃ prayayau bharatarṣabha||50||

51svarājyamanvaśāccaiva dharmeṇa nṛpatistadā
|
rājāno ye ca tatrāsansvayaṃvaradidṛkṣavaḥ||51||

52svānyeva te'pi rāṣṭrāṇi jagmuḥ parapurañjayāḥ
|
evaṃ vijitya tāḥ kanyā bhīṣmaḥ praharatāṃ varaḥ||52||

53prayayau hāstinapuraṃ yatra rājā sa kauravaḥ
|
vicitravīryo dharmātmā praśāsti vasudhāmimām||53||

54yathā pitāsya kauravyaḥ śāntanurnṛpasattamaḥ
|
so'cireṇaiva kālena atyakrāmannarādhipa||54||

55vanāni saritaścaiva śailāṃśca vividhāndrumān
|
akṣataḥ kṣapayitvā'rīnsaṅkhye'saṅkhyeyavikramaḥ||55||

56ānayāmāsa kāśyasya sutāḥ sāgaragāsutaḥ
|
snuṣā iva sa dharmātmā bhaginīriva cānujāḥ||56||

57yathā duhitaścaiva parigṛhya yayau kurūn
|
āninye sa mahābāhurbhrātuḥ priyacikīrṣayā||57||

58tāḥ sarvaguṇasaṃpannā bhrātā bhrātre yavīyase
|
bhīṣmo vicitravīryāya pradadau vikramāhṛtāḥ||58||

59evaṃ dharmeṇa dharmajñaḥ kṛtvā karmātimānuṣam
|
bhrāturvicitravīryasya vivāhāyopacakrame||59||

60satyavatyā saha mithaḥ kṛtvā niścayamātmavān
|
vivāhaṃ kārayiṣyantaṃ bhīṣmaṃ kāśipateḥ sutā
|
jyeṣṭhā tāsāmidaṃ vākyamabravīddhasatī tadā||60||

61mayā'sau bhūpatiḥ pūrvaṃ manasā hi vṛtaḥ patiḥ
|
tena cāsmi vṛtā pūrvameṣa kāmaśca me pituḥ||61||

62mayā varayitavyo'bhūtchālvastasminsvayaṃvare
|
etadvijñāya dharmajña dharmatattvaṃ samācara||62||

63evamuktastayā bhīṣmaḥ kanyayā viprasaṃsadi
|
cintāmabhyagamadvīro yuktāṃ tasyaiva karmaṇaḥ||63||

64viniścitya sa dharmajño brāhmaṇairvedapāragaiḥ
|
anujajñe tadā jyeṣṭhāmambāṃ kāśipateḥ sutām||64||

65ambikāmbālike bhārye prādādbhrātre yavīyase
|
bhīṣmo vicitravīryāya vidhidṛṣṭena karmaṇā||65||

66tayoḥ pāṇī gṛhītvā tu rūpayauvanadarpitaḥ
|
vicitravīryo dharmātmā kāmātmā samapadyata||66||

67te cāpi bṛhatī śyāme nīlakuñcitamūrdhaje
|
raktatuṅganakhopete pīnaśroṇipayodhare||67||

68ātmanaḥ pratirūpo'sau labdhaḥ patiriti sthite
|
vicittravīryaṃ kalyāṇyaupūjayāmāsatuḥ śubhe||68||

69sa cāśvirupasadṛśo devatulyaparākramaḥ
|
sarvāsāmeva nārīṇāṃ cittapramathano rahaḥ||69||

70tābhyāṃ saha samāḥ sapta viharanpṛthivīpatiḥ
|
vicitravīryastaruṇo yakṣmaṇā samagṛhyata||70||

71suhṛdāṃ yatamānānāmāptaiḥ saha cikitsakaiḥ
|
jagāmā'stamivādityaḥ kauravyo yamasādanam||71||

72dharmātmā sa tu gāṅgeyaścintāśokaparāyaṇaḥ
|
pretakāryāṇi sarvāṇi tasya samyagakārāyat ||72||

73rājño vicittravīryasya satyavatyā mate sthitaḥ
|
ṛtvigbhiḥ sahito bhīṣmaḥ sarvaiśca kurupuṅgavaiḥ||73||
