Mahābhārata 5.184 (Udyogaparvan)

1bhīṣma uvāca|
tato rātrau vyatītāyāṃ pratibuddho'smi bhārata
|
tataḥ sañcintya vai svapnamavāpaṃ harṣamuttamam ||1||

2tataḥ samabhavadyuddhaṃ mama tasya ca bhārata
|
tumulaṃ sarvabhūtānāṃ lomaharṣaṇamadbhutam ||2||

3tato bāṇamayaṃ varṣaṃ vavarṣa mayi bhārgavaḥ
|
nyavārayamahaṃ tacca śarajālena bhārata ||3||

4tataḥ paramasaṃkruddhaḥ punareva mahātapāḥ
|
hyastanena ca kopena śaktiṃ vai prāhiṇonmayi ||4||

5indrāśanisamasparśāṃ yamadaṇḍasamaprabhām
|
jvalantīmagnivatsaṃkhye lelihānāṃ samantataḥ ||5||

6tato bharataśārdūla dhiṣṇyamākāśagaṃ yathā
|
sa māmabhyavadhīttūrṇaṃ jatrudeśe kurūdvaha ||6||

7athāstramasrava‌dghoraṃ girergairikadhātuvat
|
rāmeṇa sumahabāho kṣatasya kṣatajekṣaṇa ||7||

8tato'haṃ jāmadagnyāya bhṛśaṃ krodhasamanvitaḥ
|
cikṣepa mṛtyusaṃkāśaṃ bāṇaṃ sarpaviṣopamam ||8||

9sa tenābhihato vīro lalāṭe dvijasattamaḥ
|
aśobhata mahārāja saśṛṃga iva parvataḥ ||9||

10sa saṃrabdhaḥ samāvṛtya śaraṃ kālāntakopamam
|
sandadhe balavatkṛṣya ghoraṃ śatrunibarhaṇam ||10||

11sa vakṣasi papātograḥ śaro vyāla iva śvasan
|
mahīṃ rājaṃstataścāhamagamaṃ rudhirāvilaḥ ||11||

12saṃprāpya tu punaḥ saṃjñāṃ jāmadagnyāya dhīmate
|
prāhiṇvaṃ vimalāṃ śaktiṃ jvalantamaśanīmiva ||12||

13sā tasya dvijamukhyasya nipapāta bhujāntare
|
vihvalaścābhavadrājan vepathuścainamāviśat ||13||

14tata enaṃ pariṣvajya sakhā vipro mahātapāḥ
|
akṛtavraṇaḥ śubhairvākyairāśvāsayadanekadhā ||14||

15samāśvastastato rāmaḥ krodhāmarṣasamanvitaḥ
|
prāduścakre tadā brāhmaṃ paramāstraṃ mahāvrataḥ ||15||

16tatastatpratighātārthaṃ brāhmamevāstramuttamam
|
mayā prayuktaṃ jajvāla yugāntamiva darśayat ||16||

17tayorbrahmāstrayorāsīdantarā vai samāgamaḥ
|
asamprāpyaiva rāmaṃ ca māṃ ca bhāratasattama ||17||

18tato vyomni prādurabhūtteja eva hi kevalam
|
bhūtāni caiva sarvāṇi jagmurārtiṃ viśāṃpate ||18||

19ṛṣayaśca sagandharvā devatāścaiva bhārata
|
santāpaṃ paramaṃ jagmurasratejobhipīḍitāḥ ||19||

20tataścacāla pṛthivī saparvatavanadrumā
|
santaptāni ca bhūtāni viṣādaṃ jagmuruttamam ||20||

21prajajvāla nabho rājan dhūmāyante diśo daśa
|
na sthātumantarikṣe ca śekurākāśagāstadā ||21||

22tato hāhākṛte loke sadevāsurarākṣase
|
idamantaramityevaṃ moktukāmo'smi bhārata ||22||

23prasvāpamastraṃ tvarito vacanādbrahmavādinām
|
vicitraṃ ca tadasraṃ me manasi pratyabhāttadā ||23||
