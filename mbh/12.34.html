Mahābhārata 12.34 (Śāntiparvan)

1yudhiṣṭhira uvāca|
kāni kṛtveha karmāṇi prāyaścittīyate naraḥ
|
kiṃ kṛtvā mucyate tatra tanme brūhi pitāmaha ||1||

2vyāsa uvāca |
akurvanvihitaṃ karma pratiṣiddhāni cācaran
|
prāyaścittīyate hyevaṃ naro mithyānuvartayan ||2||

3sūryeṇābhyudito yaśca brahmacārī bhavatyuta
|
tathā sūryābhinirmuktaḥ kunakhī śyāvadannapi ||3||

4parivittiḥ parivettā brahmaghno yaśca kutsakaḥ
|
didhiṣūpapatiryaḥ syādagredidhiṣureva ca ||4||

5avakīrṇī bhavedyaśca dvijātivadhakastathā
|
atīrthe brāhmaṇastyāgī tīrthe cāpratipādakaḥ ||5||

6grāmaghātī ca kaunteya māṃsasya parivikrayī
|
yaścāgnīnapavidhyeta tathaiva brahmavikrayī ||6||

7gurustrīvadhako yaśca pūrvaḥ pūrvastu garhitaḥ
|
vṛthāpaśusamālambhī gṛhadāhasya kārakaḥ ||7||

8anṛtenopavartī ca pratiroddhā gurostathā
|
etānyenāṃsi sarvāṇi vyutkrāntasamayaśca yaḥ ||8||

9akāryāṇi tu vakṣyāmi yāni tāni nibodha me
|
lokavedaviruddhāni tānyekāgramamanāḥ śṛṇu ||9||

10svadharmasya parityāgaḥ paradharmasya ca kriyā
|
ayājyayājanaṃ caiva tathā'bhakṣyasya bhakṣaṇam ||10||

11śaraṇāgatasaṃtyāgo bhṛtyasyābharaṇaṃ tathā
|
rasānāṃ vikrayaścāpi tiryagyonivadhastathā ||11||

12ādhānādīni karmāṇi śaktimānna karoti yaḥ
|
aprayacchaṃśca sarvāṇi nityadeyāni bhārata ||12||

13dakṣiṇānāmadānaṃ ca brāhmaṇasvābhimarśanam
|
sarvāṇyetānyakāryāṇi prāhurdharmavido janāḥ ||13||

14pitrā vivadate putro yaśca syādgurutalpagaḥ
|
aprajāyannaravyāghra bhavatyadhārmiko naraḥ ||14||

15uktānyetāni karmāṇi vistareṇetareṇa ca
|
yāni kurvannakurvaṃśca prāyaścittīyate naraḥ ||15||

16etānyeva tu karmāṇi kriyamāṇāni mānavāḥ
|
yeṣu yeṣu nimitteṣu na lipyante'tha tān śṛṇu ||16||

17pragṛhya śastramāyāntamapi vedāntagaṃ raṇe
|
jighāṃsantaṃ jighāṃsīyānna tena brahmahā bhavet ||17||

18iti cāpyatra kaunteya mantro vedeṣu paṭhyate
|
vedapramāṇavihitaṃ dharmaṃ ca prabravīmi te ||18||

19apetaṃ brāhmaṇaṃ vṛttādyo hanyādātatāyinam
|
na tena brahmahā sa syānmanyustanmanyumṛcchati ||19||

20prāṇātyaye tathā'jñānādācaranmadirāmapi
|
ādeśito dharmaparaiḥ punaḥ saṃskāramarhati ||20||

21etat te sarvamākhyātaṃ kaunteyābhakṣyabhakṣaṇam
|
prāyaścittavidhānena sarvametena śuddhyati ||21||

22gurutalpaṃ hi gurvarthaṃ na dūṣayati mānavam
|
uddālakaḥ śvetaketuṃ janayāmāsa śiṣyataḥ ||22||

23steyaṃ kurvaṃśca gurvarthamāpatsu na niṣidhyate
|
bahuśaḥ kāmakāreṇa na cedyaḥ sampravartate ||23||

24anyatra brāhmaṇasvebhya ādadāno na duṣyati
|
svayamaprāśitā yaśca na sa pāpena lipyate ||24||

25prāṇatrāṇe'nṛtaṃ vācyamātmano vā parasya ca
|
gurvarthe strīṣu caiva syādvivāhakaraṇeṣu ca ||25||

26nāvartate vrataṃ svapne śukramokṣe kathaṃcana
|
ājyahomaḥ samiddhe'gnau prāyaścittaṃ vidhīyate ||26||

27pārivittyaṃ tu patite nāsti pravrajite tathā
|
bhikṣiteḥ pāradāryaṃ ca taddharmasya na dūṣakam ||27||

28vṛthā paśusamālambhaṃ naiva kuryānna kārayet
|
anugrahaḥ paśūnāṃ hi saṃskāro vidhinoditaḥ ||28||

29anarhe brāhmaṇe dattamajñānāttanna dūṣakam
|
satkārāṇāṃ tathā tīrthe nityaṃ vā pratipādanam ||29||

30striyāstathā'pacāriṇyā niṣkṛtiḥ syādadūṣikā
|
api sā pūyate tena na tu bhartā praduṣyati ||30||

31tattvaṃ jñātvā tu somasya vikrayaḥ syādadoṣavān
|
asamarthasya bhṛtyasya visargaḥ syādadoṣavān
|
vanadāho gavāmarthe kriyamāṇo na dūṣakaḥ ||31||

32uktānyetāni karmāṇi yāni kurvanna duṣyati
|
prāyaścittāni vakṣyāmi vistareṇaiva bhārata ||32||
