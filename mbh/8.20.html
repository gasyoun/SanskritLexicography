Mahābhārata 8.20 (Karṇaparvan)

1dhṛtarāṣṭra uvāca|
proktastvayā pūrvameva pravīro lokaviśrutaḥ
|
na tvasya karma saṃgrāme tvayā sañjaya kīrtitam ||1||

2tasya vistaraśo brūhi pravīrasyādya vikramam
|
śikṣāṃ prabhāvaṃ vīryaṃ ca pramāṇaṃ darpameva ca ||2||

3sañjaya uvāca|
bhīṣma-droṇa-kṛpa-drauṇi-karṇārjuna-janārdanān
|
samāptavidyāndhanuṣi śreṣṭhānyānmanyase rathān ||3||

4yo hyākṣipati vīryeṇa sarvānetānmahārathān
|
na mene cātmanā tulyaṃ kañcideva nareśvaram ||4||

5tulyatāṃ droṇabhīṣmābhyāmātmano yo na mṛṣyate
|
vāsudevārjunābhyāṃ ca nyūnatāṃ naicchatātmani ||5||

6sa pāṇḍyo nṛpatiḥ śreṣṭhaḥ sarvaśastrabhṛtāṃ varaḥ
|
karṇasyānīkamahanat parābhūta ivāntakaḥ ||6||

7tadudīrṇarathāśvaṃ ca pattipravarasaṃkulam
|
kulālacakravadbhrāntaṃ pāṇḍyenābhyāhataṃ balāt ||7||

8vyaśvasūtadhvajarathān vipraviddhāyudhadvipān
|
samyagastaiḥ śaraiḥ pāṇḍyo vāyurmeghānivākṣipat ||8||

9dviradān dviradārohānvipatākāyudhadhvajān
|
sapādarakṣānahanadvajreṇādrīnivādrihā ||9||

10saśaktiprāsatūṇīrānaśvārohān hayānapi
|
pulinda-khasa-bāhlīka-niṣādāndhraka-kuntalān ||10||

11dākṣiṇātyāṃśca bhojāṃśca śūrān saṃgrāmakarkaśān
|
viśastrakavacānbāṇaiḥ kṛtvā caivākarodvyasūn ||11||

12caturaṅgaṃ balaṃ bāṇairnighnantaṃ pāṇḍyamāhave
|
dṛṣṭvā drauṇirasaṃbhrāntamasaṃbhrāntastato'bhyayāt ||12||

13ābhāṣya cainaṃ madhuramabhītaṃ tamabhītavat
|
prāha praharatāṃ śreṣṭhaḥ smitapūrvaṃ samāhvayat ||13||

14rājan kamalapatrākṣa viśiṣṭābhijanaśruta
|
vajrasaṃhananaprakhya prakhyātabalapauruṣa ||14||

15muṣṭiśliṣṭāyatajyaṃ ca vyāyatābhyāṃ mahaddhanuḥ
|
dorbhyāṃ visphārayan bhāsi mahājaladavadbhṛśam ||15||

16śaravarṣairmahāvegairamitrānabhivarṣataḥ
|
madanyaṃ nānupaśyāmi prativīraṃ tavāhave ||16||

17ratha-dvirada-pattyaśvānekaḥ pramathase bahūn
|
mṛgasaṅghānivāraṇye bibhīrbhīmabalo hariḥ ||17||

18mahatā rathaghoṣeṇa divaṃ bhūmiṃ ca nādayan
|
varṣānte sasyahā megho bhāsi hrādīva pārthiva ||18||

19saṃspṛśānaḥ śarāṃstīkṣṇāṃstūṇādāśīviṣopamān
|
mayaivaikena yudhyasva tryambakenāndhako yathā ||19||

20evamuktastathetyuktvā prahareti ca tāḍitaḥ
|
karṇinā droṇatanayaṃ vivyādha malayadhvajaḥ ||20||

21marmabhedibhiratyugrairbāṇairagniśikhopamaiḥ
|
smayannabhyahanaddrauṇiḥ pāṇḍyamācāryasattamaḥ ||21||

22tato'parānsutīkṣṇāgrānnārācānmarmabhedinaḥ
|
gatyā daśamyā saṃyuktānaśvatthāmā'pyavāsṛjat ||22||

23tāñśarānacchinat pāṇḍyo navabhirniśitaiḥ śaraiḥ
|
caturbhirardayaccāśvānāśu te vyasavo'bhavan ||23||

24atha droṇasutasyeṣūṃstāñchittvā niśitaiḥ śaraiḥ
|
dhanurjyāṃ vitatāṃ pāṇḍyaścicchedādityatejasaḥ ||24||

25divyaṃ dhanurathādhijyaṃ kṛtvā drauṇiramitrahā
|
prekṣya cānu rathe yuktānnarairanyānhayottamān ||25||

26tataḥ śarasahasrāṇi preṣayāmāsa vai dvijaḥ
|
iṣusaṃbādhamākāśamakaroddiśa eva ca ||26||

27tatastānasyataḥ sarvān drauṇerbāṇānmahātmanaḥ
|
jānāno'pyakṣayānpāṇḍyo'śātayatpuruṣarṣabhaḥ ||27||

28prayuktāṃstānprayatnena chittvā drauṇeriṣūnariḥ
|
cakrarakṣau raṇe tasya prāṇu‌danniśitaiḥ śaraiḥ ||28||

29athārerlāghavaṃ dṛṣṭvā maṇḍalīkṛtakārmukaḥ
|
prāsyaddroṇasuto bāṇān vṛṣṭiṃ pūṣānujo yathā ||29||

30aṣṭāvaṣṭagavānyūhuḥ śakaṭāni yadāyudham
|
ahnastadaṣṭabhāgena drauṇiścikṣepa māriṣa ||30||

31tamantakamiva kruddhamantakasyāntakopamam
|
ye ye dadṛśire tatra visaṃjñāḥ prāyaśo'bhavan ||31||

32parjanya iva gharmānte vṛṣṭyā sādridrumāṃ mahīm
|
ācāryaputrastāṃ senāṃ bāṇavṛṣṭyā vyavīvṛṣat ||32||

33drauṇiparjanyamuktāṃ tāṃ bāṇavṛṣṭiṃ suduḥsahām
|
vāyavyāstreṇa saṃkṣipya sudā pāṇḍyānilo'nudat ||33||

34tasya nānadataḥ ketuṃ candanāgururūṣitam
|
malayapratimaṃ drauṇiśchittvā'śvāṃścaturo'hanat ||34||

35sūtamekeṣuṇā hatvā mahājalada‌niḥsvanam
|
dhanuśchittvā'rdhacandreṇa tilaśo vyadhamadratham ||35||

36astrairasrāṇi saṃvārya chittvā sarvāyudhāni ca
|
prāptamapyahitaṃ drauṇirna jaghāna raṇepsayā ||36||

37etasminnantare karṇo gajānīkamupādravat
|
drāvayāmāsa sa tadā pāṇḍavānāṃ mahadbalam ||37||

38virathān rathinaścakre gajānaśvāṃśca bhārata
|
gajānbahubhirānarchaccharaiḥ sannataparvabhiḥ ||38||

39atha drauṇirmaheṣvāsaḥ pāṇḍyaṃ śatrunibarhaṇam
|
virathaṃ rathināṃ śreṣṭhaṃ nāhanadyuddhakāṃkṣayā ||39||

40hateśvaro dantivaraḥ sukalpitastvarābhisṛṣṭaḥ pratiśabda‌go balī
|
tamādravaddrauṇiśarāhatastvaran javena kṛtvā pratihastigarjitam ||40||

41taṃ vāraṇaṃ vāraṇayuddhakovido dvipottamaṃ parvatasānusannibham
|
samabhyatiṣṭhanmalayadhvajastvaranyathādriśṛṅgaṃ harirunnadaṃstathā ||41||

42sa tomaraṃ bhāskararaśmivarcasaṃ balāstrasargottamayatnamanyubhiḥ
|
sasarja śīghraṃ paripīḍayan gajaṃ guroḥ sutāyādripatīśvaro nadan ||42||

43maṇipravekottamavajrahāṭakairalaṅkṛtaṃ cāṃśukamālyamauktikaiḥ
|
hato hato'sītyasakṛnmudā nadan parāhanaddrauṇivarāṅgabhūṣaṇam ||43||

44tadarkacandragrahapāvakatviṣaṃ bhṛśātipātātpatitaṃ vicūrṇitam
|
mahendravajrābhihataṃ mahāsvanaṃ yathādriśṛṅgaṃ dharaṇītale tathā ||44||

45tataḥ prajajvāla pareṇa manyunā pādāhato nāgapatiryathā tathā
|
samādade cāntakadaṇḍasannibhāniṣūnamitrārtikarāṃścaturdaśa ||45||

46dvipasya pādāgrakarānsa pañcabhinṛpasya bāhū ca śiro'tha ca tribhiḥ
|
jaghāna śaḍbhiḥ ṣaḍanuttamatviṣaḥ sa pāṇḍyarājā'nucarānmahārathān ||46||

47sudīrghavṛttau varacandanokṣitau suvarṇamuktāmaṇivajrabhūṣaṇau
|
bhujau dharāyāṃ patitau nṛpasya tau viceṣṭatustārkṣyahatāvivoragau ||47||

48śiraśca tatpūrṇaśaśiprabhānanaṃ saroṣatāmrāyatanetramunnasam
|
kṣitāvapi bhrājati tatsakuṇḍalaṃ viśākhayormadhyagataḥ śaśī yathā ||48||

49sa tu dvipaḥ pañcabhiruttameṣubhiḥ kṛtaḥ ṣaḍaṃśaścaturo nṛpastribhiḥ
|
kṛto daśāṃśaḥ kuśalena yudhyatā yathā havistaddaśadaivataṃ tathā ||49||

50sa pādaśo rākṣasabhojanānbahūn pradāya pāṇḍyo'śva-manuṣya-kuñjarān
|
svadhāmivāpya jvalanaḥ pitṛpriyastataḥ praśāntaḥ salilapravāhataḥ ||50||

51samāptavidyaṃ tu guroḥ sutaṃ nṛpaḥ samāptakarmāṇamupetya te sutaḥ
|
suhṛdvṛto'tyarthamapūjayanmudā jite balau viṣṇumivāmareśvaraḥ ||51||
