Mahābhārata 7.188 (Droṇaparvan)

1sañjaya uvāca|
tato duḥśāsanaḥ kruddhaḥ sahadevamupādravat
|
rathavegena tīvreṇa kampayanniva medinīm||1||

2tasyāpatata evāśu bhallenāmitrakarśanaḥ
|
mādrīputraḥ śiro yantuḥ saśirastrāṇamacchinat||2||

3nainaṃ duḥśāsanaḥ sūtaṃ nāpi kaścana sainikaḥ
|
kṛttottamāṅgamāśutvātsahadevena buddhavān||3||

4yadā tvasaṃgṛhītatvātprayāntyaśvā yathāsukham
|
tato duḥśāsanaḥ sūtaṃ bubudhe gatacetasam||4||

5sa hayānsannigṛhyājau svayaṃ hayaviśāradaḥ
|
yuyudhe rathināṃ śreṣṭho laghucitraṃ ca suṣṭhu ca||5||

6tadasyāpūjayan karma sve pare cāpi saṃyuge
|
hatasutarathenājau vyacaradyadabhītavat||6||

7sahadevastu tānaśvāṃstīkṣṇairbāṇairavākirat
|
pīḍyamānāḥ śaraiścāśu prādravaṃste tatastataḥ||7||

8sa raśmiṣu viṣaktatvādutsasarja śarāsanam
|
dhanuṣā karma kurvaṃstu raśmīṃśca punarutsṛjat||8||

9chidreṣveteṣu taṃ bāṇairmādrīputro'bhyavākirat
|
parīpsaṃstvatsutaṃ karṇastadantaramavāpa tat||9||

10vṛkodarastataḥ karṇaṃ tribhirbhallaiḥ samāhitaḥ
|
ākarṇapūrṇairabhyaghnanbāhvorurasi cānadat||10||

11sa nivṛttastataḥ karṇaḥ saṃghaṭṭita ivoragaḥ
|
bhīmamāvārayāmāsa vikiranniśitāñcharān||11||

12tato'bhūttumulaṃ yuddhaṃ bhīmarādheyayostadā
|
tau vṛṣāviva nardantau vivṛttanayanāvubhau||12||

13vegena mahatā'nyonyaṃ saṃrabdhāvabhipetatuḥ
|
abhisaṃśliṣṭayostatra tayorāhavaśauṇḍayoḥ||13||

14vicchinnaśarapātatvādgadāyuddhamavartata
|
gadayā bhīmasenastu karṇasya rathakūbaram||14||

15vibheda śatadhā rājaṃstada‌dbhutamivābhavat
|
tato bhīmasya rādheyo gadāmāvidhya vīryavān||15||

16avāsṛjadrathe tāṃ tu bibheda gadayā gadām
|
tato bhīmaḥ punargurvīṃ cikṣepādhirathergadām||16||

17tāṃ gadāṃ bahubhiḥ karṇaḥ supuṅkhaiḥ supravejitaiḥ
|
pratyavidhyatpunaścānyaiḥ sā bhīmaṃ punarāvrajat||17||

18vyālīva mantrābhihatā karṇabāṇairabhidrutā
|
tasyāḥ pratinipātena bhīmasya vipulo dhvajaḥ||18||

19papāta sārathiścāsya mumoha ca gadāhataḥ
|
sa karṇaṃ sāyakānaṣṭau vyasṛjatkrodhamūrcchitaḥ||19||

20taistasya niśitaistīkṣṇairbhīmaseno mahābalaḥ
|
ciccheda paravīraghnaḥ prahasanniva bhārata||20||

21dhvajaṃ śarāsanaṃ caiva śarāvāpañca bhārata
|
karṇo'pyanyaddhanurgṛhya hemapṛṣṭhaṃ durāsadam||21||

22tataḥ punastu rādheyo hayānasya ratheṣubhiḥ
|
ṛkṣavarṇāñjaghānāśu tathobhau pārṣṇisārathī||22||

23sa vipannaratho bhīmo nakulasyāpluto ratham
|
hariryathā gireḥ śṛṅgaṃ samākrāmadarindamaḥ||23||

24tathā droṇārjunau citramayudhyetāṃ mahārathau
|
ācāryaśiṣyau rājendra kṛtapraharaṇau yudhi||24||

25laghusandhānayogābhyāṃ rathayośca raṇena ca
|
mohayantau manuṣyāṇāṃ cakṣūṃṣi ca manāṃsi ca||25||

26upāramanta te sarve yodhā bharatasattama
|
adṛṣṭapūrvaṃ paśyantastadyuddhaṃ guruśiṣyayoḥ||26||

27vicitrānpṛtanāmadhye rathamārgānudīrya tau
|
anyonyamapasavyaṃ ca kartuṃ vīrau tadeṣatuḥ||27||

28parākramaṃ tayoryodhā dadṛśuste suvismitāḥ
|
tayoḥ samabhavadyuddhaṃ droṇapāṇḍavayormahat||28||

29āmiṣārthe mahārāja gagane śyenayoriva
|
yadyaccakāra droṇastu kuntīputrājagīṣayā||29||

30tattapratijaghānāśu prahasaṃstasya pāṇḍavaḥ
|
yadā droṇo na śaknoti pāṇḍavaṃ sma viśeṣutum||30||

31tataḥ prāduścakārāstramastramārgaviśāradaḥ
|
aindraṃ pāśapataṃ tvāṣṭraṃ vāyavyamatha vāruṇam||31||

32muktaṃ muktaṃ droṇacāpāttajjaghāna dhanañjayaḥ
|
astrāṇyastrairyadā tasya vidhivaddhanti pāṇḍavaḥ||32||

33tato‍'staiḥ paramairdivyairdoṇaḥ pārthamavākirat
|
yadyadastraṃ sa pārthāya prayuṅkte vijigīṣayā||33||

34tasya tasya vighātāya tattaddhi kurute'rjunaḥ
|
sa vadhyamāneṣvastreṣu divyeṣvapi yathāvidhi||34||

35arjunenārjunaṃ droṇo manasaivābhyapūjayat
|
mene cātmānamadhikaṃ pṛthivyāmadhi bhārata||35||

36tena śiṣyeṇa sarvebhyaḥ śastravidbhyaḥ paraṃtapaḥ
|
vāryamāṇastu pārthena tathā madhye mahātmanām||36||

37yatamāno'rjunaṃ prītyā pratyavārayadutsmayan
|
tato'ntarikṣe devāśca gandharvāśca sahasraśaḥ||37||

38ṛṣayaḥ siddhasaṅghāśca vyatiṣṭhanta didṛkṣayā
|
tadapsarobhirākīrṇaṃ yakṣa-gandharvasaṃkulam||38||

39śrīmadākāśamabhavadbhayo meghākulaṃ yathā
|
tatra smāntarhitā vāco vyacaranta punaḥ punaḥ||39||

40droṇa-pārthastavopetā vyaśrūyanta narādhipa
|
visṛjyamāneṣvastreṣu jvālayatsu diśo daśa||40||

41abruvaṃstatra siddhāśca ṛṣayaśca samāgatāḥ
|
naivedaṃ mānuṣaṃ yuddhaṃ nāsuraṃ na ca rākṣasam||41||

42na daivaṃ na ca gāndharvaṃ brāhmaṃ dhruvamidaṃ param
|
vicitramidamāścaryaṃ na no dṛṣṭaṃ na ca śrutam||42||

43ati pāṇḍavamācāryo droṇaṃ cāpyati pāṃḍavaḥ
|
nānayorantaraṃ śakyaṃ draṣṭumanyena kenacit||43||

44yadi rudro dvidhā kṛtya yudhyetātmānamātmanā
|
tatra śakyopamā kartumanyatra tu na vidyate||44||

45jñānamekasthamācārye, jñānaṃ yogaśca pāṇḍave
|
śauryamekasthamācārye, balaṃ śauryaṃ ca pāṇḍave||45||

46nemau śakyau maheṣvāsau yuddhe kṣapayituṃ paraiḥ
|
icchamānau punarimau hanyetāṃ sāmaraṃ jagat||46||

47ityabruvanmahārāja dṛṣṭvā tau puruṣarṣabhau
|
antarhitāni bhūtāni prakāśāni ca sarvaśaḥ||47||

48tato droṇo brāhmamastraṃ prāduścakre mahāmatiḥ
|
saṃtāpayan raṇe pārthaṃ bhūtānyantarhitāni ca||48||

49tataścacāla pṛthivī saparvata-vana-drumā
|
vavau ca viṣamo vāyuḥ sāgarāścāpi cukṣubhuḥ||49||

50tatastrāso mahānāsītkurupāṇḍavasenayoḥ
|
sarveṣāṃ caiva bhūtānāmudyate'stre mahātmanā||50||

51tataḥ pārtho'pyasaṃbhrāntastadastraṃ pratijaghnivān
|
brahmāstreṇaiva rājendra tataḥ sarvamaśīśamat||51||

52yadā na gamyate pāraṃ tayoranyatarasya vā
|
tataḥ saṃkulayuddhena tadyuddhaṃ vyākulīkṛtam||52||

53nājñāyata tataḥ kiñcitpunareva viśāṃpate
|
pravṛtte tumule yuddhe droṇapāṇḍavayormṛdhe||53||

54śarajālaiḥ samākīrṇe meghajālairivāmbare
|
nāpatacca tataḥ kaścidantarikṣacarastadā||54||
