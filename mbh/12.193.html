Mahābhārata 12.193 (Śāntiparvan)

1yudhiṣṭhira uvāca|
ācārasya vidhiṃ tāta procyamānaṃ tvayānagha
|
śrotumicchāmi dharmajña sarvajño hyasi me mataḥ ||1||

2bhīṣma uvāca|
durācārā durviceṣṭā duṣprajñāḥ priyasāhasāḥ
|
asantastviti vikhyātāḥ saṃtaścācāralakṣaṇāḥ ||2||

3purīṣaṃ yadi vā mūtraṃ ye na kurvanti mānavāḥ
|
rājamārge gavāṃ madhye dhānyamadhye ca te śubhāḥ ||3||

4śaucamāvaśyakaṃ kṛtvā devatānāṃ ca tarpaṇam
|
dharmamāhurmanuṣyāṇāmupaspṛśya nadīṃ taret ||4||

5sūryaṃ sadopatiṣṭheta na ca sūryodaye svapet
|
sāyaṃ prātarjapetsandhyāṃ tiṣṭhan pūrvāṃ tathetarām ||5||

6pañcārdro bhojanaṃ bhuñjyātprāṅmukho maunamāsthitaḥ
|
na nindyādannabhakṣyāṃśca svādvasvādu ca bhakṣayet ||6||

7ārdrapāṇiḥ samuttiṣṭhennārdrapādaḥ svapenniśi
|
devarṣirnāradaḥ prāha etadācāralakṣaṇam ||7||

8śuciṃ deśamanaḍvāhaṃ devagoṣṭhaṃ catuṣpatham
|
brāhmaṇaṃ dhārmikaṃ caityaṃ nityaṃ kuryātpradākṣaṇam ||8||

9atithīnāṃ ca sarveṣāṃ preṣyāṇāṃ svajanasya ca
|
sāmānyaṃ bhojanaṃ bhṛtyaiḥ puruṣasya praśasyate ||9||

10sāyaṃ prātarmanuṣyāṇāmaśanaṃ vedanirmitam
|
nāntarā bhojanaṃ dṛṣṭamupavāsī tathā bhavet ||10||

11homakāle tathā juhvannṛtukāle tathā vrajan
|
ananyastrījanaḥ prājño brahmacārī tathā bhavet ||11||

12amṛtaṃ brāhmaṇocchiṣṭaṃ jananyā hṛdayaṃ kṛtam
|
tajjanāḥ paryupāsante satyaṃ santaḥ samāsate ||12||

13loṣṭamardī tṛṇacchedī nakhavādī tu yo naraḥ
|
nityocchiṣṭaḥ śaṃkuśuko nehāyurvindate mahat ||13||

14yajuṣā saṃskṛtaṃ māṃsaṃ nivṛttā māṃsabhakṣaṇāt
|
na bhakṣayedvṛthāmāṃsaṃ pṛṣṭhamāṃsa ca varjayet ||14||

15svadeśe paradeśe vā atithiṃ nopavāsayet
|
kāmyakarmaphalaṃ labdhvā gurūṇāmupapādayet ||15||

16gurubhya āsanaṃ deyaṃ kartavyaṃ cābhivādanam
|
gurūnabhyarcya yujyante āyuṣā yaśasā śriyā ||16||

17nekṣetādityamudyantaṃ na ca nagnāṃ parastriyam
|
maithunaṃ satataṃ dharmyaṃ guhye caiva samācaret ||17||

18tīrthānāṃ hṛdayaṃ tīrthaṃ śucānāṃ hṛdayaṃ śuciḥ
|
sarvamāryakṛtaṃ caukṣyaṃ vālasaṃsparśanāni ca ||18||

19darśane darśane nityaṃ sukhapraśnamudāharet
|
sāyaṃ prātaśca viprāṇāṃ pradiṣṭamabhivādanam ||19||

20devāgāre gavāṃ madhye brāhmaṇānāṃ kriyāpathe
|
svādhyāye bhojane caiva dakṣiṇaṃ pāṇimuddharet ||20||

21sāyaṃ prātaśca viprāṇāṃ pūjanaṃ ca yathāvidhi
|
paṇyānāṃ śobhate paṇyaṃ kṛṣīṇāṃ bādyate kṛṣiḥ ||21||

22bahukāraṃ ca sasyānāṃ bāhye vāho gavāṃ tathā
|
sampannaṃ bhojane nityaṃ pānīye tarpaṇaṃ tathā
|
suśrutaṃ pāyase brūyādyavafvā kṛsare tathā||22||

23śmaśrukarmaṇi samprāpte kṣute snāne'tha bhojane
|
vyādhitānāṃ ca sarveṣāmāyuṣyamabhinandanam ||23||

24pratyādityaṃ na meheta na paśyedātmanaḥ śakṛt
|
saha striyā'tha śayanaṃ saha bhojyaṃ ca varjayet ||24||

25tvaṃkāraṃ nāmadheyaṃ ca jyeṣṭhānāṃ parivarjayet
|
avarāṇāṃ samānānāmubhayeṣāṃ na duṣyati ||25||

26hṛdayaṃ pāpavṛttānāṃ pāpamākhyāti vaikṛtam
|
jñānapūrvaṃ vinaśyanti gūhamānā mahājane ||26||

27jñānapūrvakṛtaṃ pāpaṃ chādayatyabahuśrutaḥ
|
nainaṃ manuṣyāḥ paśyanti paśyantyeva divaukasaḥ ||27||

28pāpenāpihitaṃ pāpaṃ pāpamevānuvartate
|
dharmeṇāpihito dharmo dharmamevānuvartate
|
dhārmikeṇa kṛto dharmo dharmamevānuvartate ||28||

29pāpaṃ kṛtaṃ na smaratīha mūḍho vivartamānasya tadeti kartuḥ
|
rāhuryathā candramupaiti cāpi tathā'budhaṃ pāpamupaiti karma ||29||

30āśayā sañcitaṃ dravyaṃ duḥkhenaivopabhujyate
|
tadbudhā na praśaṃsanti maraṇaṃ na pratīkṣate ||30||

31mānasaṃ sarvabhūtānāṃ dharmamāhurmanīṣiṇaḥ
|
tasmātsarveṣu bhūteṣu manasā śivamācaret ||31||

32eka eva careddharmaṃ nāsti dharme sahāyatā
|
kevalaṃ vidhimāsādya sahāyaḥ kiṃ kariṣyati ||32||

33dharmo yonirmanuṣyāṇāṃ devānāmamṛtaṃ divi
|
pretyabhāve sukhaṃ dharmācchaśvattairupabhujyate ||33||
