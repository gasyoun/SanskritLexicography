Mahābhārata 6.81 (Bhīṣmaparvan)

1sañjaya uvāca|
athātmajaṃ tava punargāṅgeyo dhyānamāsthitam
|
abravīdbharataśreṣṭhaḥ saṃpraharṣakaraṃ vacaḥ ||1||

2ahaṃ droṇaśca śalyaśca kṛtavarmā ca sātvataḥ
|
aśvatthāmā vikarṇaśca bhagada‌tto'tha saubalaḥ ||2||

3vindānuvindāvāvantyau bāhlīkaḥ saha bāhlikaiḥ
|
trigartarājo balavān māgadhaśca sudurjayaḥ ||3||

4bṛhadbalaśca kausalyaścitraseno viviṃśatiḥ
|
rathāśca bahusāhasrāḥ śobhanāśca mahādhvajāḥ ||4||

5deśajāśca hayā rājan svārūḍhā haya sādibhiḥ
|
gajendrāśca madodvṛttāḥ prabhinnakaraṭāmukhāḥ ||5||

6pādātāśca tathā śūrā nānāpraharaṇadhvajāḥ
|
nānādeśasamutpannāstvadarthe yoddhumudyatāḥ ||6||

7ete cānye ca bahavastvadarthe tyaktajīvitāḥ
|
devānapi raṇe jetuṃ samarthā iti me matiḥ ||7||

8avaśyaṃ hi mayā rājaṃstava vācyaṃ hitaṃ sadā
|
aśakyāḥ pāṇḍavā jetuṃ devairapi savāsavaiḥ ||8||

9vāsudevasahāyāśca mahendrasamavikramāḥ
|
sarvathā'haṃ tu rājendra kariṣye vacanaṃ tava ||9||

10pāṇḍavāṃśca raṇe jeṣye māṃ vā jeṣyanti pāṇḍavāḥ
|
evamuktvā dadāvasmai viśalyakaraṇīṃ śubhām ||10||

11oṣadhīṃ vīryasampannāṃ viśalyaścābhavattadā
|
tataḥ prabhāte vimale svena sainyena vīryavān ||11||

12avyūhata svayaṃ vyūhaṃ bhīṣmo vyūhaviśāradaḥ
|
maṇḍalaṃ manujaśreṣṭho nānāśastrasamākulam ||12||

13saṃpūrṇaṃ yodhamukhyaiśca tathā dantipadātibhiḥ
|
rathairanekasāhastraiḥ samantāt parivāritam ||13||

14aśvavṛndairmahadbhiśca ṛṣṭitomaradhāribhiḥ
|
nāge nāge sthāḥ sapta sapta cāśvā rathe rathe ||14||

15anvaśvaṃ daśa dhānuṣkā dhānuṣke daśa carmiṇaḥ
|
evaṃ vyūḍhaṃ mahārāja tava sainyaṃ mahārathaiḥ ||15||

16sthitaṃ raṇāya mahate bhīṣmeṇa yudhi pālitam
|
daśāśvānāṃ sahasrāṇi dantināṃ ca tathaiva ca ||16||

17rathānāmayutaṃ cāpi putrāśca tava daṃśitāḥ
|
citrasenādayaḥ śūrā abhyarakṣan pitāmaham ||17||

18rakṣyamāṇaḥ sa taiḥ śūrairgopyamānāśca tena te
|
sannaddhāḥ samadṛśyanta rājānaśca mahābalāḥ ||18||

19duryodhanastu samare daṃśito rathamāsthitaḥ
|
vyarājata śriyā juṣṭo yathā śakrastriviṣṭape ||19||

20tataḥ śabdo mahānāsīt putrāṇāṃ tava bhārata
|
rathaghoṣaśca vipulo vāditrāṇāṃ ca niḥsvanaḥ ||20||

21bhīṣmeṇa dhārtarāṣṭrāṇāṃ vyūḍhaḥ pratyaṅmukho yudhi
|
maṇḍalaḥ sa mahāvyūho durbhedyo'mitraghātanaḥ ||21||

22sarvataḥ śuśubhe rājan raṇe'rīṇāṃ durāsadaḥ
|
maṇḍalaṃ tu samālokya vyūhaṃ paramadurjayam ||22||

23svayaṃ yudhiṣṭhiro rājā vajraṃ vyūhamathākarot
|
tathā vyūḍheṣvanīkeṣu yathāsthānamavasthitāḥ ||23||

24rathinaḥ sādinaḥ sarve siṃhanādamathānadan
|
bibhitsavastato vyūhaṃ niryayuryuddhakāṅkṣiṇaḥ ||24||

25itaretarataḥ śūrāḥ sahasainyāḥ prahāriṇaḥ
|
bhāradvājo yayau matsyaṃ drauṇiścāpi śikhaṇḍinam ||25||

26svayaṃ duryodhano rājā pārṣataṃ samupādravat
|
nakulaḥ sahadevaśca madrarājānamīyatuḥ ||26||

27vindānuvindāvāvantyāvirāvantamabhidrutau
|
sarve nṛpāstu samare dhanañjayamayodhayan ||27||

28bhīmaseno raṇe yāntaṃ hārdikyaṃ samavārayat
|
citrasenaṃ vikarṇaṃ ca tathā durmarṣaṇaṃ vibhuḥ ||28||

29ārjuniḥ samare rājaṃstava putrānayodhayat
|
prāgjyotiṣo maheṣvāso haiḍimbaṃ rākṣasottamam ||29||

30abhidudrāva vegena matto mattamiva dvipam
|
alambuṣastadā rājan sātyakiṃ yuddhadurmadam ||30||

31sasainyaṃ samare kruddho rākṣasaḥ samupādravat
|
bhūriśravā raṇe yatto dhṛṣṭaketumayodhayat ||31||

32śrutāyuṣaṃ ca rājānaṃ dharmaputro yudhiṣṭhiraḥ
|
cekitānaśca samare kṛpamevānvayodhayat ||32||

33śeṣāḥ pratiyayuryattā bhīṣmameva mahāratham
|
tato rājasamūhāste parivavrurdhanañjayam ||33||

34śakti-tomara-nārāca-gadā-parighapāṇayaḥ
|
arjuno'tha bhṛśaṃ kruddho vārṣṇeyamida‌mabravīt ||34||

35paśya mādhava sainyāni dhārtarāṣṭrasya saṃyuge
|
vyūḍhāni vyūhaviduṣā gāṅgeyena mahātmanā ||35||

36yuddhābhikāmān śūrāṃśca paśya mādhava daṃśitān
|
trigartarājaṃ sahitaṃ bhrātṛbhiḥ paśya keśava ||36||

37adyaitānnāśayiṣyāmi paśyataste janārdana
|
ya ime māṃ yaduśreṣṭha yoddhukāmā raṇājire ||37||

38etaduktvā tu kaunteyo dhanurjyāmavamṛjya ca
|
vavarṣa śaravarṣāṇi narādhipagaṇān prati ||38||

39te'pi taṃ parameṣvāsāḥ śaravarṣairapūrayan
|
taḍāgaṃ vāridhārābhiryathā prāvṛṣi toyadāḥ ||39||

40hāhākāro mahānāsīt tava sainye viśāṃpate
|
chādyamānau raṇe kṛṣṇau śarairdṛṣṭvā mahāraṇe ||40||

41devā devarṣayaścaiva gandharvāśca sahoragaiḥ
|
vismayaṃ paramaṃ jagmurdṛṣṭvā kṛṣṇau tathāgatau ||41||

42tataḥ kruddho'rjuno rājannaindramastramudairayat
|
tatrādbhutamapaśyāma vijayasya parākramam ||42||

43śastravṛṣṭiṃ parairmuktāṃ śaraughairyadavārayat
|
na ca tatrāpyanirbhinnaḥ kaścidāsīdviśāṃpate ||43||

44teṣāṃ rājasahasrāṇāṃ hayānāṃ dantināṃ tathā
|
dvābhyāṃ tribhiḥ śaraiścānyānpārtho vivyādha māriṣa ||44||

45te hanyamānāḥ pārthena bhīṣmaṃ śāntanavaṃ yayuḥ
|
agādhe majjamānānāṃ bhīṣmaḥ poto'bhavattadā ||45||

46āpatadbhistu taistatra prabhagnaṃ tāvakaṃ balam
|
sañcukṣubhe mahārāja vātairiva mahārṇavaḥ ||46||
