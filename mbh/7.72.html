Mahābhārata 7.72 (Droṇaparvan)

1sañjaya uvāca|
tasminnahani nirvṛtte ghore prāṇabhṛtāṃ kṣaye
|
āditye'staṃ gate śrīmān saṃdhyākāla upasthite ||1||

2vyapayāteṣu vāsāya sarveṣu bharatarṣabha
|
hatvā saṃśaptakavrātān divyairastraiḥ kapidhvajaḥ ||2||

3prāyātsa śibiraṃ jiṣṇurjaitramāsthāya taṃ ratham
|
gacchanneva ca govindaṃ sāśrukaṇṭho'bhyabhāṣata ||3||

4kiṃ nu me hṛdayaṃ trastaṃ vākca sajjati keśava
|
syandanti cāpyaniṣṭāni gātraṃ sīdati cāpyuta ||4||

5aniṣṭaṃ caiva me śliṣṭaṃ hṛda‌yānnāpasarpati
|
bhuvi ye dikṣu cātyugrā utpātāstrāsayanti mām ||5||

6bahuprakārā dṛśyante sarva evāghaśaṃsinaḥ
|
api svasti bhavedrājñaḥ sāmātyasya gurormama ||6||

7vāsudeva uvāca| vyaktaṃ śivaṃ tava bhrātuḥ sāmātyasya bhaviṣyati
|
mā śucaḥ kiñcidevānyat tatrāniṣṭaṃ bhaviṣyati ||7||

8sañjaya uvāca|
tataḥ sandhyāmupāsyaiva vīrau vīrāvasādane
|
kathayantau raṇe vṛttaṃ prayātau rathamāsthitau ||8||

9tataḥ svaśibiraṃ prāptau hatānandaṃ hatatviṣam
|
vāsudevo'rjunaścaiva kṛtvā karma suduṣkaram ||9||

10dhvastākāraṃ samālakṣya śibiraṃ paravīrahā
|
bībhatsurabravīt kṛṣṇamasvasthahṛdayastataḥ ||10||

11nadanti nādya tūryāṇi maṃgalyāni janārdana
|
miśrā dundubhinirghoṣaiḥ śaṅkhāścāḍambaraiḥ saha ||11||

12vīṇā naivādya vādyante śamyātālasvanaiḥ saha
|
maṅgalyāni ca gītāni na gāyanti paṭhanti ca ||12||

13stutiyuktāni ramyāṇi mamānīkeṣu bandinaḥ
|
yodhāścāpi hi māṃ dṛṣṭvā nivartante hyadhomukhāḥ ||13||

14karmāṇi ca yathāpūrvaṃ kṛtvā nābhivadanti mām
|
api svasti bhavedadya bhrātṛbhyo mama mādhava ||14||

15na hi śuddhyati me bhāvo dṛṣṭvā svajanamākulam
|
api pāñcālarājasya virāṭasya ca mānada ||15||

16sarveṣāṃ caiva yodhānāṃ sāmagryaṃ syānmamācyuta
|
na ca māmadya saubhadraḥ prahṛṣṭo bhrātṛbhiḥ saha
|
raṇādāyāntamucitaṃ pratyudyāti hasanniva ||16||

17sañjaya uvāca|
evaṃ saṃkathayantau tau praviṣṭau śibiraṃ svakam
|
dadṛśāte bhṛśāsvasthān pāṇḍavānnaṣṭacetasaḥ ||17||

18dṛṣṭvā bhrātṝṃśca putrāṃśca vimanā vānaradhvajaḥ
|
apaśyaṃścaiva saubhadramidaṃ vacanamabravīt ||18||

19mukhavarṇo'prasanno vaḥ sarveṣāmeva lakṣyate
|
na cābhimanyuṃ paśyāmi na ca māṃ pratinandatha ||19||

20mayā śrutaśca droṇena cakravyūho vinirmitaḥ
|
na ca vastasya bhettā'sti vinā saubhadramarbhakam ||20||

21na copadiṣṭastasyāsīnmayānīkādvinirgamaḥ
|
kaccinna bālo yuṣmābhiḥ parānīkaṃ praveśitaḥ ||21||

22bhittvā'nīkaṃ maheṣvāsaḥ pareṣāṃ bahuśo yudhi
|
kaccinna nihataḥ saṃkhye saubhadraḥ paravīrahā ||22||

23lohitākṣaṃ mahābāhuṃ jātaṃ siṃhamivādriṣu
|
upendrasadṛśaṃ brūta kathamāyodhane hataḥ ||23||

24sukumāraṃ maheṣvāsaṃ vāsavasyātmajātmajam
|
sadā mama priyaṃ būta kathamāyodhane hataḥ ||24||

25subhadrāyāḥ priyaṃ putraṃ draupadyāḥ keśavasya ca
|
ambāyāśca priyaṃ nityaṃ ko'vadhīt kālamohitaḥ ||25||

26sadṛśo vṛṣṇivīrasya keśavasya mahātmanaḥ
|
vikramaśrutamāhātmyaiḥ kathamāyodhane hataḥ ||26||

27vārṣṇeyīdayitaṃ śūraṃ mayā satatalālitam
|
yadi putraṃ na paśyāmi yāsyāmi yamasādanam ||27||

28mṛdukuñcitakeśāntaṃ bālaṃ bālamṛgekṣaṇam
|
mattadviradavikrāntaṃ siṃhapotamivodgatam ||28||

29smitābhibhāṣiṇaṃ dāntaṃ guruvākyakaraṃ sadā
|
bālye'pyatulakarmāṇaṃ priyavākyamamatsaram ||29||

30mahotsāhaṃ mahābāhuṃ dīrgharājīvalocanam
|
bhaktānukampinaṃ dāntaṃ na ca nīcānusāriṇam ||30||

31kṛtajñaṃ jñānasampannaṃ kṛtāstramanivartinam
|
yuddhābhinandinaṃ nityaṃ dviṣatāṃ bhayavardhanam ||31||

32sveṣāṃ priyahite yuktaṃ pitṝṇāṃ jayagṛddhinam
|
na ca pūrvaṃ prahartāraṃ saṃgrāme naṣṭasaṃbhramam ||32||

33yadi putraṃ na paśyāmi yāsyāmi yamasādanam
|
ratheṣu gaṇyamāneṣu gaṇitaṃ taṃ mahāratham ||33||

34mayādhyardhaguṇaṃ saṃkhye taruṇaṃ bāhuśālinam
|
pradyumnasya priyaṃ nityaṃ keśavasya mamaiva ca ||34||

35yadi putraṃ na paśyāmi yāsyāmi yamasādanam
|
sunasaṃ sulalāṭāntaṃ svakṣibhrūdaśanacchadam ||35||

36apaśyatastadvada‌naṃ kā śāntirhṛdayasya me
|
tantrīsvanasukhaṃ ramyaṃ puṃskokilasamadhvanim ||36||

37aśṛṇvataḥ svanaṃ tasya kā śāntirhṛdayasya me
|
rūpaṃ cāpratimaṃ tasya tridaśaiścāpi durlabham ||37||

38apaśyato hi vīrasya kā śāntirhṛdayasya me
|
abhivādanadakṣaṃ taṃ pitṝṇāṃ vacane ratam ||38||

39nādyāhaṃ yadi paśyāmi kā śāntirhṛdayasya me
|
sukumāraḥ sadā vīro mahārhaśayanocitaḥ ||39||

40bhūmāvanāthavacchete nūnaṃ nāthavatāṃ varaḥ
|
śayānaṃ samupāsanti yaṃ purā paramastriyaḥ ||40||

41tamadya vipraviddhāṅgamupāsantyaśivāḥ śivāḥ
|
yaḥ purā bodhyate suptaḥ suta-māgadha-bandibhiḥ ||41||

42bodhayantyadya taṃ nūnaṃ śvāpadā vikṛtaiḥ svanaiḥ
|
chatracchāyāsamucitaṃ tasya tadvadanaṃ śumam ||42||

43nūnamadya rajodhvastaṃ raṇareṇuḥ kariṣyati
|
hā putrakāvitṛptasya satataṃ putradarśane ||43||

44bhāgyahīnasya kālena yathā me nīyase balāt
|
sā ca saṃyamanī nūnaṃ sadā sukṛtināṃ gatiḥ ||44||

45svabhābhirmohitā ramyā tvayā'tyarthaṃ virājate
|
nūnaṃ vaivasvataśca tvāṃ varuṇaśca priyātithim ||45||

46śatakraturdhaneśaśca prāptamarcantyabhīrukam
|
evaṃ vilapya bahudhā bhinnapoto vaṇigyathā ||46||

47duḥkhena mahatā'viṣṭo yudhiṣṭhiramapṛcchata
|
kaccitsa kadanaṃ kṛtvā pareṣāṃ kurunandana ||47||

48svargato'bhimukhaḥ saṃkhye yudhyamāno nararṣabhaiḥ
|
sa nūnaṃ bahubhiryattairyudhyamāno nararṣabhaiḥ ||48||

49asahāyaḥ sahāyārthī māmanudhyātavān dhruvam
|
pīḍyamānaḥ śaraistīkṣṇaiḥ karṇadroṇakṛpādibhiḥ ||49||

50nānāliṅgaiḥ sudhautāgrairmama putro'lpacetanaḥ
|
iha me syāt paritrāṇaṃ piteti saḥ punaḥ punaḥ ||50||

51ityevaṃ vilapanmanye nṛśaṃsairbhuvi pātitaḥ
|
athavā matprasūtaḥ sa svasrīyo mādhavasya ca ||51||

52subhadrāyāṃ ca saṃbhūto na caivaṃ vaktumarhati
|
vajrasāramayaṃ nūnaṃ hṛdayaṃ sudṛḍhaṃ mama ||52||

53apaśyato dīrghabāhuṃ raktākṣaṃ yanna dīryate
|
kathaṃ bāle maheṣvāsā nṛśaṃsā marmabhedinaḥ ||53||

54svasrīye vāsudevasya mama putre'kṣipan śarān
|
yo māṃ nityamadīnātmā pratyudgamyābhinandati ||54||

55upāyāntaṃ ripūn hatvā so'dya māṃ kiṃ na paśyati
|
nūnaṃ sa pātitaḥ śete dharaṇyāṃ rudhirokṣitaḥ ||55||

56śobhayanmedinīṃ gātrairāditya iva pātitaḥ
|
subhadrāmanuśocāmi yā putramapalāyinam ||56||

57raṇe vinihataṃ śrutvā śokārtā vai vinaṃkṣyati
|
subhadrā vakṣyate kiṃ māmabhimanyumapaśyatī ||57||

58draupadī caiva duḥkhārte te ca vakṣyāmi kiṃ tvaham
|
vajrasāramayaṃ nūnaṃ hṛdayaṃ yanna yāsyati ||58||

59sahasradhā vadhūṃ dṛṣṭvā ruda‌tīṃ śokakarśitām
|
dṛptānāṃ dhārtarāṣṭrāṇāṃ siṃhanādo mayā śrutaḥ ||59||

60yuyutsuścāpi kṛṣṇena śruto vīrānupālabhan
|
aśaknuvanto bībhatsuṃ bālaṃ hatvā mahārathāḥ ||60||

61kiṃ modadhvamadharmajñāḥ pāṇḍavaṃ dṛśyatāṃ balam
|
kiṃ tayorvipriyaṃ kṛtvā keśavārjunayormṛdhe ||61||

62siṃhavannadatha prītāḥ śokakāla upasthite
|
āgamiṣyati vaḥ kṣipraṃ phalaṃ pāpasya karmaṇaḥ ||62||

63adharmo hi kṛtastīvraḥ kathaṃ syādaphalaściram
|
iti tān paribhāṣan vai vaiśyāputro mahāmatiḥ ||63||

64apāyācchatramutsṛjya kopaduḥkhasamanvitaḥ
|
kimarthametannākhyātaṃ tvayā kṛṣṇa raṇe mama ||64||

65adhākṣaṃ tānahaṃ krūrāṃstadā sarvān mahārathān
|
sañjaya uvāca|
putraśokārditaṃ pārthaṃ dhyāyantaṃ sāśrulocanam ||65||

66nigṛhya vāsudevastaṃ putrādhibhirabhiplutam
|
maivamityabravīt kṛṣṇastīvraśokasamanvitam ||66||

67sarveṣāmeṣa vai panthāḥ śūrāṇāmanivartinām
|
kṣatriyāṇāṃ viśeṣeṇa yeṣāṃ yuddhena jīvikā ||67||

68eṣā vai yudhyamānānāṃ śūrāṇāmanivartinām
|
vihitā sarvaśāstrajñairgatirmatimatāṃ vara ||68||

69dhruvaṃ hi yuddhe maraṇaṃ śūrāṇāmanivartinām
|
gataḥ puṇyakṛtāṃ lokānabhimanyurna saṃśayaḥ ||69||

70etacca sarvavīrāṇāṃ kāṃkṣitaṃ bharatarṣabha
|
saṃgrāme'bhimukho mṛtyuṃ prāpnuyāditi mānada ||70||

71sa ca vīrān raṇe hatvā rājaputrān mahābalān
|
vīrairākāṃkṣitaṃ mṛtyuṃ saṃprāpto'bhimukhaṃ raṇe ||71||

72mā śucaḥ puruṣavyāghra pūrvaireṣa sanātanaḥ
|
dharmakṛdbhiḥ kṛto dharmaḥ kṣatriyāṇāṃ raṇe kṣayaḥ ||72||

73ime te bhrātaraḥ sarve dīnā bharatasattama
|
tvayi śokasamāviṣṭe nṛpāśca suhṛdastava ||73||

74etāṃśca vacasā sāmnā samāśvāsaya mānada
|
viditaṃ veditavyaṃ te na śokaṃ kartumarhasi ||74||

75evamāśvāsitaḥ pārthaḥ kṛṣṇenādbhutakarmaṇā
|
tato'bravīttadā bhrātṝnsarvān pārthaḥ sagadgadān ||75||

76sa dīrghabāhuḥ pṛthvaṃso dīrgharājīvalocanaḥ
|
abhimanyuryathāvṛttaḥ śrotumicchāmyahaṃ tathā ||76||

77sanāgasyandanahayān drakṣyadhvaṃ nihatān mayā
|
saṃgrāme sānubandhāṃstān mama putrasya vairiṇaḥ ||77||

78kathaṃ ca vaḥ kṛtāstrāṇāṃ sarveṣāṃ śastrapāṇinām
|
saubhadro nidhanaṃ gacchedvajriṇā'pi samāgataḥ ||78||

79yadyevamahamajñāsyamaśaktān rakṣaṇe mama
|
putrasya pāṇḍupañcālān mayā gupto bhavet tataḥ ||79||

80kathaṃ ca vo rathasthānāṃ śaravarṣāṇi muñcatām
|
nīto'bhimanyurnidhanaṃ kadarthīkṛtya vaḥ paraiḥ ||80||

81aho vaḥ pauruṣaṃ nāsti na ca vo'sti parākramaḥ
|
yatrābhimanyuḥ samare paśyatāṃ vo nipātitaḥ ||81||

82ātmānameva garheyaṃ yadahaṃ vai sudurbalān
|
yuṣmānājñāya niryāto bhīrūnakṛtaniścayān ||82||

83āhosvidbhūṣaṇārthāya varma śastrāyudhāni vaḥ
|
vācastu vaktuṃ saṃsatsu mama putramarakṣatām‌ ||83||

84evamuktvā tato vākyaṃ tiṣṭhaṃścāpavarāsimān
|
na smāśakyata bībhatsuḥ kenacitprasamīkṣitum ||84||

85tamantakamiva kruddhaṃ niḥśvasantaṃ muhurmuhuḥ
|
putraśokābhisantaptamaśrupūrṇamukhaṃ tadā ||85||

86na bhāṣituṃ śaknuvanti draṣṭuṃ vā suhṛdo'rjunam
|
anyatra vāsudevādvā jyeṣṭhādvā pāṇḍunandanāt ||86||

87sarvāsvavasthāsu hitāvarjunasya manonugau
|
bahumānāt priyatvācca tāvenaṃ vaktumarhataḥ ||87||

88tatastaṃ putraśokena bhṛśaṃ pīḍitamānasam
|
rājīvalocanaṃ kuddhaṃ rājā vacanamabravīt ||88||
