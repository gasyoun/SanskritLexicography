Mahābhārata 7.106 (Droṇaparvan)

1dhṛtarāṣṭra uvāca|
arjune saindhavaṃ prāpte bhāradvājena saṃvṛtāḥ
|
pañcālāḥ kurubhiḥ sārdhaṃ kimakurvata sañjaya ||1||

2sañjaya uvāca|
aparāhṇe mahārāja saṃgrāme lomaharṣaṇe
|
pañcālānāṃ kurūṇāṃ ca droṇadyūtamavartata ||2||

3pañcālā hi jighāṃsanto droṇaṃ saṃhṛṣṭacetasaḥ
|
abhyamuñcanta garjantaḥ śaravarṣāṇi māriṣa ||3||

4tatastu tumulasteṣāṃ saṃgrāmo'vartatādbhutaḥ
|
pañcālānāṃ kurūṇāṃ ca ghoro devāsuropamaḥ ||4||

5sarve droṇarathaṃ prāpya pañcālāḥ pāṇḍavaiḥ saha
|
tadanīkaṃ bibhitsanto mahāstrāṇi vyadarśayan ||5||

6droṇasya rathaparyantaṃ rathino rathamāsthitāḥ
|
kampayanto'bhyavartanta vegamāsyāya madhyamam || 6||

7tamabhyayādbṛhatkṣatraḥ kekayānāṃ mahārathaḥ
|
pravapanniśitān bāṇānmahendrāśanisannibhān ||7||

8taṃ tu pratyudyayau śīghraṃ kṣemadhūrtirmahāyaśāḥ
|
vimuñcanniśitānbāṇān śataśo'tha sahasraśaḥ ||8||

9dhṛṣṭaketuśca cedīnāmṛṣabho'tibaloditaḥ
|
tvarito'bhyadravadroṇaṃ mahendra iva śambaram ||9||

10tamāpatantaṃ sahasā vyāditāsyamivāntakam
|
vīradhanvā maheṣvāsastvaramāṇaḥ samabhyayāt ||10||

11yudhiṣṭhiraṃ mahārājaṃ jigīṣuṃ samavasthitam
|
sahānīka tato droṇo nyavārayata vīryavān ||11||

12nakulaṃ kuśalaṃ yuddhe parākrāntaṃ parākramī
|
abhyagacchat samāyāntaṃ vikarṇaste sutaḥ prabho ||12||

13sahadevaṃ tathā yāntaṃ durmukhaḥ śatrukarṣaṇaḥ
|
śarairanekasāhasraiḥ samavākiradāśugaiḥ ||13||

14sātyakiṃ tu naravyāghraṃ vyāghradattastvavārayat
|
śaraiḥ suniśitaistīkṣṇaiḥ kampayan vai muhurmuhuḥ ||14||

15draupadeyānnaravyāghrān muñcataḥ sāyakottamān
|
saṃrabdhān rathinaḥ śreṣṭhān saumadattiravārayat ||15||

16bhīmasenaṃ tadā kuddhaṃ bhīmarūpo bhayānakaḥ
|
pratyavārayadāyāntamārṣyaśṛṅgirmahārathaḥ ||16||

17tayoḥ samabhavadyuddhaṃ nararākṣasayormṛdhe
|
yādṛgeva purā vṛttaṃ rāmarāvaṇayornṛpa ||17||

18tato yudhiṣṭhiro droṇaṃ navatyā nataparvaṇām
|
ājaghne bharataśreṣṭhaḥ sarvamarmasu bhārata ||18||

19taṃ droṇaṃ pañcaviṃśatyā nijaghāna stanāntare
|
roṣito bharataśreṣṭha kaunteyena yaśasvinā ||19||

20bhūya eva tu viṃśatyā sāyakānāṃ samācinot
|
sāśvasūtadhvajaṃ droṇaḥ paśyatāṃ sarvadhanvinām ||20||

21tāñśarān droṇamuktāṃstu śaravarṣeṇa pāṇḍavaḥ
|
avārayata dharmātmā darśayan pāṇilāghavam ||21||

22tato droṇo bhṛśaṃ kruddho dharmarājasya saṃyuge
|
ciccheda samare dhanvī dhanustasya mahātmanaḥ ||22||

23athainaṃ chinnadhanvānaṃ tvaramāṇo mahārathaḥ
|
śarairanekasāhasraiḥ pūrayāmāsa sarvataḥ ||23||

24adṛśyaṃ vīkṣya rājānaṃ bhāradvājasya sāyakaiḥ
|
sarvabhūtānyamanyanta hatameva yudhiṣṭhiram ||24||

25keciccainamamanyanta tathaiva vimukhīkṛtam
|
hato rājeti rājendra brāhmaṇena mahātmanā ||25||

26sa kṛcchraṃ paramaṃ prāpto dharmarājo yudhiṣṭhiraḥ
|
tyaktvā tatkārmukaṃ chinnaṃ bhāradvājena saṃyuge ||26||

27ādade'nyaddhanurdivyaṃ bhāsvaraṃ vegavattaram
|
tatastān sāyakāṃstatra droṇanunnān sahasraśaḥ ||27||

28ciccheda samare vīrastadadbhutamivābhavat
|
chitvā tu tāñśarān rājankrodhasaṃraktalocanaḥ ||28||

29śaktiṃ jagrāha samare girīṇāmapi dāriṇīm
|
svarṇadaṇḍāṃ mahāghorāmaṣṭaghaṇṭāṃ bhayāvahām ||29||

30samutkṣipya ca tāṃ hṛṣṭo nanāda balavadbalī
|
nādena sarvabhūtāni trāsayanniva bhārata ||30||

31śaktiṃ samudyatāṃ dṛṣṭvā dharmarājena saṃyuge
|
svasti droṇāya sahasā sarvabhūtānyathābruvan ||31||

32sā rājabhujanirmuktā nirmuktoragasannibhā
|
prajvālayantī gaganaṃ diśaḥ sapradiśastathā ||32||

33droṇāntikamanuprāptā dīptāsyā pannagī yathā
|
tāmāpatantīṃ sahasā dṛṣṭvā droṇo viśāṃpate ||33||

34prāduścakre tato brāhmamastramastravidāṃ varaḥ
|
tadastraṃ bhasmasāt kṛtvā tāṃ śaktiṃ ghoradarśanām ||34||

35jagāma syandanaṃ tūrṇaṃ pāṇḍavasya yaśasvinaḥ
|
tato yudhiṣṭhiro rājā droṇāstraṃ tatsamudyatam ||35||

36aśāmayanmahāprājño brahmāstreṇaiva māriṣa
|
viddhvā taṃ ca raṇe droṇaṃ pañcabhirnataparvabhiḥ ||36||

37kṣurapreṇa sutīkṣṇena cicchedāsya mahaddhanuḥ
|
tadapāsya dhanuśchinnaṃ droṇaḥ kṣatriyamardanaḥ ||37||

38gadāṃ cikṣepa sahasā dharmaputrāya māriṣa
|
tāmāpatantīṃ sahasā gadāṃ dṛṣṭvā yudhiṣṭhiraḥ ||38||

39gadāmevāgrahīt kruddhaścikṣepa ca parantapa
|
te gade sahasā mukte samāsādya parasparam ||39||

40saṅgharṣāt pāvakaṃ muktvā sameyātāṃ mahītale
|
tato droṇo bhṛśaṃ kruddho dharmarājasya māriṣa ||40||

41caturbhirniśitaistīkṣṇairhayān jaghne śarottamaiḥ
|
cicchedaikena bhallena dhanuścendradhvajopamam ||41||

42ketumekena ciccheda pāṇḍavaṃ cādayantribhiḥ
|
hatāśvāttu rathāttūrṇamavaplutya yudhiṣṭhiraḥ ||42||

43tasthāvūrdhvabhujo rājā vyāyudho bharatarṣabha
|
virathaṃ taṃ samālokya vyāyudhaṃ ca viśeṣataḥ ||43||

44droṇo vyamohayacchatrūnsarvasainyāni vā vibho
|
muñcaṃśceṣugaṇāṃstīkṣṇān laghuhasto dṛḍhavrataḥ ||44||

45abhidudrāva rājānaṃ siṃho mṛgamivolbaṇaḥ
|
tamabhidrutamālokya droṇenāmitraghātinā ||45||

46hāheti sahasā śabdaḥ pāṇḍūnāṃ samajāyata
|
hato rājā hato rājā bhāradvājena māriṣa ||46||

47ityāsīt sumahāñchandaḥ pāṇḍusainyasya bhārata
|
tatastvaritamāruhya sahadevarathaṃ nṛpaḥ
|
apāyājjavanairaśvaiḥ kuntīputro yudhiṣṭhiraḥ ||47||
