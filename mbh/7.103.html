Mahābhārata 7.103 (Droṇaparvan)

1sañjaya uvāca|
evamuktvā'rjunaṃ rājā tribhirmarmātigaiḥ śaraiḥ
|
abhyavidhyanmahāvegaiścaturbhiścaturo hayān ||1||

2vāsudevaṃ ca daśabhiḥ pratyavidhyat stanāntare
|
pratodaṃ cāsya bhallena chittvā bhūmāvapātayat ||2||

3taṃ caturdaśabhiḥ pārthaścitrapuṃkhaiḥ śilāśitaiḥ
|
avidhyattūrṇamavyagraste cābhraśyanta varmaṇi ||3||

4teṣāṃ naiṣphalyamālokya punarnava ca pañca ca
|
prāhiṇonniśitānbāṇāṃste cābhraśyanta varmaṇaḥ ||4||

5aṣṭāviṃśāṃstu tān bāṇānastān viprekṣya niṣphalān
|
abravītparavīraghnaḥ kṛṣṇo'rjunamidaṃ vacaḥ ||5||

6adṛṣṭapūrvaṃ paśyāmi śilānāmiva sarpaṇam
|
tvayā saṃpreṣitāḥ pārtha nārthaṃ kurvanti patriṇaḥ ||6||

7kaccidgāṇḍīvajaḥ prāṇastathaiva bharatarṣabha
|
muṣṭiśca te yathāpūrvaṃ bhujayośca balaṃ tava ||7||

8na vā kaccidayaṃ kālaḥ prāptaḥ syādadya paścimaḥ
|
tava caivāsya śatrośca tanmamācakṣya pṛcchataḥ ||8||

9vismayo me mahānpārtha tava dṛṣṭvā śarānimān
|
vyarthānnipatitānsaṃkhye duryodhanarathaṃ prati ||9||

10vajrāśanisamā ghorāḥ parakāyāvabhedinaḥ
|
śarāḥ kurvanti te nārthaṃ pārtha kā'dya viḍambanā ||10||

11arjuna uvāca|
droṇenaiṣā matiḥ kṛṣṇa dhārtarāṣṭra niveśitā
|
abhedyā hi mamāstrāṇāmeṣā kavacadhāraṇā ||11||

12asminnantarhitaṃ kṛṣṇa trailokyamapi varmaṇi
|
eko droṇo hi vedaitadahaṃ tasmācca sattamāt ||12||

13na śakyametatkavacaṃ bāṇairbhettuṃ kathañcana
|
api vajreṇa govinda svayaṃ maghavatā yudhi ||13||

14jānaṃstvamapi vai kṛṣṇa māṃ vimohayase katham
|
yadvṛttaṃ triṣu lokeṣu yacca keśava vartate ||14||

15tathā bhaviṣyadyaccaiva tatsarvaṃ viditaṃ tava
|
na tvidaṃ veda vai kaścidyathā tvaṃ madhusūdana ||15||

16eṣa duryodhanaḥ kṛṣṇa droṇena vihitāmimām
|
tiṣṭhatyabhītavatsaṃkhye bibhratkavacadhāraṇām ||16||

17yattvatra vihitaṃ kāryaṃ naiṣa tadvetti mādhava
|
strīvadeṣa bibhartyetāṃ yuktāṃ kavacadhāraṇām ||17||

18paśya bāhvośca me vīryaṃ dhanuṣaśca janārdana
|
parājayiṣye kauravyaṃ kavacenāpi rakṣitam ||18||

19ida‌maṅgirase prādāddeveśo varma bhāsvaram
|
tasmādbṛhaspatiḥ prāpa tataḥ prāpa purandaraḥ ||19||

20punardadau surapatirmahyaṃ varma sasaṃgraham
|
daivaṃ yadyasya varmaitadbrahmaṇā vā svayaṃ kṛtam ||20||

21nainaṃ gopsyati durbuddhimadya bāṇahataṃ mayā
|
sañjaya uvāca|
evamuktvā'rjuno bāṇānabhimantrya vyakarṣayat ||21||

22mānavāstreṇa gānārhastīkṣṇāvaraṇabhedinā
|
vikṛṣyamāṇāṃstenaiva dhanurmadhyagatāñcharān ||22||

23tānasyāstreṇa ciccheda drauṇiḥ sarvāstraghātinā
|
tānnikṛttāniṣūn dṛṣṭvā dūrato brahmavādinā ||23||

24nyavedayat keśavāya vismitaḥ śvetavāhanaḥ
|
naitadastraṃ mayā śakyaṃ dviḥ prayoktuṃ janārdana ||24||

25astraṃ māmeva hanyāddhi hanyāccāpi balaṃ mama
|
tato duryodhanaḥ kṛṣṇau navabhirnavabhiḥ śaraiḥ ||25||

26avidhyata raṇe rājañcharairāśīviṣopamaiḥ
|
bhūya evābhyavarṣacca samare kṛṣṇapāṇḍavau ||26||

27śaravarṣeṇa mahatā tato'hṛṣyanta tāvakāḥ
|
cakrurvāditraninadān siṃhanādaravāṃstathā ||27||

28tataḥ kruddho raṇe pārthaḥ sṛkkiṇī parisaṃlihan
|
nāpaśyacca tato'syāṃgaṃ yanna syāddharmarakṣitam ||28||

29tato'sya niśitairbāṇaiḥ sumuktairantakopamaiḥ
|
hayāṃścakāra nirdehānubhau ca pārṣṇisārathī ||29||

30dhanurasyācchinattūrṇaṃ hastāvāpaṃ ca vīryavān
|
rathaṃ ca śakalīkartuṃ savyasācī pracakrame ||30||

31duryodhanaṃ ca bāṇābhyāṃ tīkṣṇābhyāṃ virathīkṛtam
|
āviddhyaddhastatalayorubhayorarjunastadā ||31||

32prayatnajño hi kaunteyo nakhamāṃsāntareṣubhiḥ
|
sa vedanābhirāvignaḥ palāyanaparāyaṇaḥ ||32||

33taṃ kṛcchrāmāpadaṃ prāptaṃ dṛṣṭvā paramadhanvinaḥ
|
samāpetuḥ parīpsanto dhanañjayaśarārditam ||33||

34taṃ rathairbahusāhasraiḥ kalpitaiḥ kuñjarairhayaiḥ
|
padātyoghaiśca saṃrabdhaiḥ parivavrurdhanañjayam ||34||

35atha nārjunagovindau na ratho vā vyadṛśyata
|
astravarṣeṇa mahatā janaughaiścāpi saṃvṛtau ||35||

36tato'rjuno'stravīryeṇa nijaghne tāṃ varūthinīm
|
tatra vyaṅgīkṛtāḥ petuḥ śataśo'tha rathadvipāḥ ||36||

37te hatā hanyamānāśca nyagṛhṇāṃstaṃ rathottamam
|
sa rathastaṃmitastasthau krośamātre samantataḥ ||37||

38tato'rjunaṃ vṛṣṇivīrastvarito vākyamabravīt
|
dhanurvisphārayātyarthamahaṃ dhmāsyāmi cāṃbujam ||38||

39tato visphārya balavadgāṃḍīvaṃ jaghnivān ripūn
|
mahatā śaravarṣeṇa talaśabdena cārjunaḥ ||39||

40pāñcajanyaṃ ca balavān dadhmau tāreṇa keśavaḥ
|
rajasā dhvastapakṣmāntaḥ prasvinnavadano bhṛśam ||40||

41tasya śaṃkhasya nādena dhanuṣo niḥsvanena ca
|
niḥsattvāśca sasattvāśca kṣitau petustadā janāḥ ||41||

42tairvimukto ratho reje vāyvīrita ivāmbudaḥ
|
jayadrathasya goptārastataḥ kṣubdhāḥ sahānugāḥ ||42||

43te dṛṣṭvā sahasā pārthaṃ goptāraḥ saindhavasya tu
|
cakrurnādān maheṣvāsāḥ kaṃpayanto vasundharām ||43||

44bāṇaśabdaravāṃścogrānvimiśrāñśaṃkhaniḥsvanaiḥ
|
prāduścakrurmahātmānaḥ siṃhanādaravānapi ||44||

45taṃ śrutvā ninadaṃ ghoraṃ tāvakānāṃ samutthitam
|
pradadhmatuḥ śaṃkhavarau vāsudevadhanañjayau ||45||

46tena śabdena mahatā pūriteyaṃ vasundharā
|
saśailā sārṇavadvīpā sapātālā viśāṃpate ||46||

47sa śabdo bharataśreṣṭha vyāpya sarvā diśo daśa
|
pratisasvāna tatraiva kurupāṇḍavayorbale ||47||

48tāvakā rathinastatra dṛṣṭvā kṛṣṇadhanañjayau
|
saṃbhramaṃ paramaṃ prāptāstvaramāṇā mahārathāḥ ||48||

49atha kṛṣṇau mahābhāgau tāvakā vīkṣya daṃśitau
|
abhyadravanta saṃkruddhāstadadbhutamivābhavat ||49||
