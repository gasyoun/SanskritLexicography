Mahābhārata 12.187 (Śāntiparvan)

1bhṛguruvāca |
na praṇāśo'sti jīvasya dattasya ca kṛtasya ca
|
yāti dehāntaraṃ prāṇī śarīraṃ tu viśīryate ||1||

2na śarīrāśrito jīvastasminnaṣṭe praṇaśyati
|
samidhāmiva dagdhānāṃ yathā'gnirdṛśyate tathā ||2||

3bharadvāja uvāca |
agneryathā tathā tasya yadi nāśo na vidyate
|
indhanasyopayogānte sa cāgnirnopalabhyate ||3||

4naśyatītyeva jānāmi śāntamagnimanindhanam
|
gatiryasya pramāṇaṃ vā saṃsthānaṃ vā na vidyate ||4||

5bhṛguruvāca |
samidhāmupayogānte yathāgnirnopalabhyate
|
ākāśānugatatvāddhi durgrāhyo hi nirāśrayaḥ ||5||

6tathā śarīrasantyāge jīvo hyākāśavatsthitaḥ
|
na gṛhyate tu sūkṣmatvādyathā jyotirna saṃśayaḥ ||6||

7prāṇān dhārayate hyagniḥ sa jīva upadhāryatām
|
vāyusandhāraṇo hyagnirnaśyatyucchvāsanigrahāt ||7||

8tasminnaṣṭe śarīrāgnau tato dehamacetanam
|
patitaṃ yāti bhūmitvamayanaṃ tasya hi kṣitiḥ ||8||

9jaṅgamānāṃ hi sarveṣāṃ sthāvarāṇāṃ tathaiva ca
|
ākāśaṃ pavano'nveti jyotistamanugacchati
|
teṣāṃ trayāṇāmekatvāddvayaṃ bhūmau pratiṣṭhitam ||9||

10yatra khaṃ tatra pavanastatrāgniryatra mārutaḥ
|
amūrtayaste vijñeyā mūrtimantaḥ śarīriṇām ||10||

11bharadvāja uvāca |
yadyāgnimārutau bhūmiḥ khamāpaśca śarīriṣu
|
jīvaḥ kiṃlakṣaṇastatretyetadācakṣva me'nagha ||11||

12pañcātmake pañcaratau pañcavijñānacetane
|
śarīre prāṇināṃ jīvaṃ vettumicchāmi yādṛśam ||12||

13māṃsaśoṇitasaṃghāte medaḥ snāyvasthisaṃcaye
|
bhidyamāne śarīre ta jīvo naivopalabhyate ||13||

14yadyajīvaṃ śarīraṃ tu pañcabhūtasamanvitam
|
śarīre mānase duḥkhe kastāṃ vedayate rujam ||14||

15śṛṇoti kathitaṃ jīvaḥ karṇābhyāṃ na śṛṇoti tat
|
maharṣe manasi vyagre tasmājjīvo nirarthakaḥ ||15||

16sarvaṃ paśyati yaddṛśyaṃ manoyuktena cakṣuṣā
|
manasi vyākule cakṣuḥ paśyannapi na paśyati ||16||

17na paśyati na cāghrāti na śṛṇoti na bhāṣate
|
na ca sparśarasau vetti nidrāvaśagataḥ punaḥ ||17||

18haṣyati kruddhyate ko'tra śocatyudvijate ca kaḥ
|
icchati dhyāyati dveṣṭi vācamīrayate ca kaḥ ||18||

19bhṛguruvāca| na pañcasādhāraṇamatra kiñciccharīrameko bahate'ntarātmā
|
sa vetti gandhāṃśca rasāśrutīśca sparśaṃ ca rūpaṃ ca guṇāṃśca ye'nye ||19||

20pañcātmake pañcaguṇapradarśīṃ sa sarvagātrānugato'ntarātmā
|
sa vetti duḥkhāni sukhāni cātra tadviprayogāttu na vetti dehaḥ ||20||

21yadā na rūpaṃ na sparśo noṣmabhāvaśca pavake
|
tadā śānte śarīrāgnau dehatyāgena naśyati ||21||

22āpomayamidaṃ sarvamāpo mūrtiḥ śarīriṇām
|
tatrātmā mānaso brahmā sarvabhūteṣu lokakṛt ||22||

23ātmā kṣetrajña ityuktaḥ saṃyuktaḥ prākṛtairguṇaiḥ
|
taireva tu vinirmuktaḥ paramātmetyudāhṛtaḥ ||23||

24ātmānaṃ taṃ vijānīhi sarvalokahitātmakam
|
tasmin yaḥ saṃśrito dehe hyabbinduriva puṣkare ||24||

25kṣetrajñaṃ taṃ vijānīhi nityaṃ lokahitātmakam
|
tamo rajaśca sattvaṃ ca viddhi jīvaguṇānimān ||25||

26sacetanaṃ jīvaguṇaṃ vadanti sa ceṣṭate ceṣṭayate ca sarvam
|
ataḥ paraṃ kṣetravido vadanti prāvartayadyo bhuvanāni sapta ||26||

27na jīvanāśo'sti hi dehabhede mithyaitadāhurmata ityabuddhāḥ
|
jīvastu dehāntaritaḥ prayāti daśārdhataivāsya śarīrabhedaḥ ||27||

28evaṃ sarveṣu bhūteṣu gūḍhaścarati saṃvṛtaḥ
|
dṛśyate tvagryayā buddhyā sūkṣmayā tattvadarśibhiḥ ||28||

29taṃ pūrvāpararātreṣu yuñjānaḥ satataṃ budhaḥ
|
laghvāhāro viśuddhātmā paśyatyātmānamātmani ||29||

30cittasya hi prasādena hitvā karma śubhāśubham
|
prasannātmā''tmani sthitvā sukhamānantyamaśnute ||30||

31mānaso'gniḥ śarīreṣu jīva ityabhidhīyate
|
sṛṣṭiḥ prajāpatereṣā bhūtādhyātmaviniścaye ||31||
