Mahābhārata 4.72 (Virāṭaparvan)

1virāṭa uvāca|kimarthaṃ pāṇḍavaśreṣṭha bhāryāṃ duhitaraṃ mama
|
pratigrahītuṃ nemāṃ tvaṃ mayā dattāmihecchasi ||1||

2arjuna uvāca|antaḥpure'hamuṣitaḥ sadā paśyan sutāṃ tava
|
rahasyaṃ ca prakāśaṃ ca viśvastāṃ pitṛvanmayi ||2||

3priyo bahutamaścāsaṃ nartako gītakovidaḥ
|
ācāryavacca māṃ nityaṃ manyate duhitā tava ||3||

4vayasthayā tayā rājan saha saṃvatsaroṣitaḥ
|
atiśaṅkā bhavet sthāne tava lokasya vā vibho ||4||

5tasmānnimantraye'yaṃ te duhitāṃ manujādhipa
|
śuddho jitendriyo dāntastasyāḥ śuddhiḥ kṛtā mayā ||5||

6snuṣāyāṃ duhiturvāpi putre cātmani vā punaḥ
|
atra śaṅkāṃ na paśyāmi tena śuddhirbhaviṣyati ||6||

7abhiśāpādahaṃ bhīto mithyāvādāt parantapa
|
snuṣārthamuttarāṃ rājan pratigṛhṇāmi te sutām ||7||

8svastrīyo vāsudevasya sākṣāddevaśiśuryathā
|
dayitaścakrahastasya sarvāstreṣu ca kovidaḥ ||8||

9abhimanyurmahābāhuḥ putro mama viśāmpate
|
jāmātā tava yukto vai bhartā ca duhitustava ||9||

10virāṭa uvāca|upapannaṃ kuruśreṣṭhe kuntīputre dhanañjaye
|
ya evaṃ dharmanityaśca jātajñānaśca pāṇḍavaḥ ||10||

11yatkṛtyaṃ manyase pārtha kriyatāṃ tadanantaram
|
sarve kāmāḥ samṛddhā me sambandhī yasya me'rjunaḥ ||11||

12vaiśampāyana uvāca|evaṃ bruvati rājendre kuntīputro yudhiṣṭhiraḥ
|
anvaśāsat sa saṃyogaṃ samaye matsyapārthayoḥ ||12||

13tato mitreṣu sarveṣu vāsudevaṃ ca bhārata
|
preṣayāmāsa kaunteyo virāṭaśca mahīpatiḥ ||13||

14tatastrayodaśe varṣe nivṛtte pañca pāṇḍavāḥ
|
upaplavyaṃ virāṭasya samapadyanta sarvaśaḥ ||14||

15abhimanyuṃ ca bībhatsurānināya janārdanam
|
ānartebhyo'pi dāśārhānānayāmāsa pāṇḍavaḥ ||15||

16kāśirājaśca śaibyaśca prīyamāṇau yudhiṣṭhire
|
akṣauhiṇībhyāṃ sahitāvāgatau pṛthivīpatī ||16||

17akṣauhiṇyā ca sahito yajñaseno mahābalaḥ
|
draupadyāśca sutā vīrāḥ śikhaṇḍī cāparājitaḥ ||17||

18dhṛṣṭadyumnaśca durdharṣaḥ sarvaśastrabhṛtāṃ varaḥ
|
samastākṣauhiṇīpālā yajvāno bhūridakṣiṇāḥ
|
vedāvabhṛthasaṃpannāḥ sarve śūrāstanutyajaḥ ||18||

19tānāgatānabhiprekṣya matsyo dharmabhṛtāṃ varaḥ
|
pūjayāmāsa vidhivat sabhṛtyabalavāhanān ||19||

20prīto'bhavadduhitaraṃ dattvā tāmabhimanyave
|
tataḥ pratyupayāteṣu pārthiveṣu tatastataḥ ||20||

21tatrāgamadvāsudevo vanamālī halāyudhaḥ
|
kṛtavarmā ca hārdikyo yuyudhānaśca sātyakiḥ ||21||

22anādhṛṣṭistathā'krūraḥ sāmbo niśaṭha eva ca
|
abhimanyumupādāya saha mātrā parantapāḥ ||22||

23indrasenādayaścaiva rathaistaiḥ susamāhitaiḥ
|
āyayuḥ sahitāḥ sarve parisaṃvatsaroṣitāḥ ||23||

24daśanāgasahasrāṇi hayānāṃ ca daśāyutam
|
rathānāmarbudaṃ pūrṇaṃ nikharvaṃ ca padātinām ||24||

25vṛṣṇyandhakāśca bahavo bhojāśca paramaujasaḥ
|
anvayurvṛṣṇiśārdūlaṃ vāsudevaṃ mahādyutim ||25||

26pāribarhaṃ dadau kṛṣṇaḥ pāṇḍavānāṃ mahātmanām
|
striyo ratnāni vāsāṃsi pṛthak pṛthaganekaśaḥ
|
tato vivāho vidhivadvavṛdhe matsyapārthayoḥ ||26||

27tataḥ śaṅkhāśca bhairyaśca gomukhāḍambarāstathā
|
pārthaiḥ saṃyujyamānasya nedurmatsyasya veśmani ||27||

28uccāvacānmṛgān jaghnurmedhyāṃśca śataśaḥ paśun
|
surāmaireyapānāni prabhūtānyabhyahārayan ||28||

29gāyanākhyānaśīlāśca naṭavaitālikāstathā
|
stuvantastānupātiṣṭhan sutāśca saha māgadhaiḥ ||29||

30sudeṣṇāṃ ca puraskṛtya matsyānāṃ ca varastriyaḥ
|
ājagmuścārusarvāṅgyaḥ sumṛṣṭamaṇikuṇḍalāḥ ||30||

31varṇopapannāstā nāryo rūpavatyaḥ svalaṅkṛtāḥ
|
sarvāścābhyabhavan kṛṣṇā rūpeṇa yaśasā śriyā ||31||

32parivāryottarāṃ tāstu rājaputrīmalaṅkṛtām
|
sutāmiva mahendrasya puraskṛtyopatasthire ||32||

33tāṃ pratyagṛhṇāt kaunteyaḥ sutasyārthe dhanañjayaḥ
|
saubhadrasyānavadyāṅgīṃ virāṭatanayāṃ tadā ||33||

34tatrātiṣṭhanmahārājo rūpamindrasya dhārayan
|
snuṣāṃ tāṃ pratijagrāha kuntīputro yudhiṣṭhiraḥ ||34||

35pratigṛhya ca tāṃ pārthaḥ puraskṛtya janārdanam
|
vivāhaṃ kārayāmāsa saubhadrasya mahātmanaḥ ||35||

36tasmai sapta sahasrāṇi hayānāṃ vātaraṃhasām
|
dve ca nāgaśate mukhye prādādbahudhanaṃ tadā ||36||

37hutvā samyaksamiddhāgnimarcayitvā dvijanmanaḥ
|
rājyaṃ balaṃ ca kośaṃ ca sarvamātmānameva ca ||37||

38kṛte vivāhe tu tadā dharmaputro yudhiṣṭhiraḥ
|
brāhmaṇebhyo dadau vittaṃ yadupāharadacyutaḥ ||38||

39gosahasrāṇi ratnāni vastrāṇi vividhāni ca
|
bhūṣaṇāni ca mukhyāni yānāni śayanāni ca ||39||

40bhojanāni ca hṛdyāni pānāni vividhāni ca
|
tanmahotsavasaṅkāśaṃ hṛṣṭapuṣṭajanāyutam
|
nagaraṃ matsyarājasya śuśubhe bharatarṣabha ||40||
