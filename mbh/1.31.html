Mahābhārata 1.31 (Ādiparvan)

1śaunaka uvāca|
ko'parādho mahendrasya kaḥ pramādaśca sūtaja
|
tapasā vālakhilyānāṃ saṃbhūto garuḍaḥ katham||1||

2kaśyapasya dvijāteśca kathaṃ vai pakṣirāṭ sutaḥ
|
adhṛṣyaḥ sarvabhūtānāmavadhyaścābhavatkatham||2||

3kathaṃ ca kāmacārī sa kāmavīryaśca khecaraḥ
|
etadicchāmyahaṃ śrotuṃ purāṇe yadi paṭhyate||3||

4sautiruvāca|
viṣayo'yaṃ purāṇasya yanmāṃ tvaṃ paripṛcchasi
|
śṛṇu me vadataḥ sarvametatsaṃkṣepatoṃ dvija||4||

5yajataḥ putrakāmasya kaśyapasya prajāpateḥ
|
sāhāyyamṛṣayo devā gandharvāśca daduḥ kila||5||

6tatredhmānayane śakro niyuktaḥ kaśyapena ha
|
munayo vālakhilyāśca ye cānye devatāgaṇāḥ||6||

7śakrastu vīryasadṛśamidhyabhāraṃ giriprabham
|
samudyamyānayāmāsa nātikṛcchrādiva prabhuḥ||7||

8athāpaśyadṛṣīnhrasvānaṅguṣṭhodaravarṣmaṇaḥ
|
palāśavartikāmekāṃ vahataḥ saṃhatānpathi||8||

9pralīnānsveṣvivāṅgeṣu nirāhārāṃstapodhanān
|
kliśyamānānmandabalāngoṣpade saṃplutodake||9||

10tānsarvānvismayāviṣṭo vīryonmattaḥ purandaraḥ
|
apahāsyābhyagācchīvraṃ lambayitvā'vamanya ca||10||

11te'tha roṣasamāviṣṭāḥ subhṛśaṃ jātamanyavaḥ
|
ārebhire mahatkarma yadā śakrabhayaṃkaram||11||

12juhuvuste sutapaso vidhivajjātavedasam
|
mantrairuccāvacairviprā yena kāmena tacchṛṇu||12||

13kāmavīryaḥ kāmagamo devarājabhayapradaḥ
|
indro'nyaḥ sarvadevānāṃ bhavediti yatavratāḥ||13||

14indrācchataguṇaḥ śaurye vīrye caiva manojavaḥ
|
tapaso naḥ phalenādya dāruṇaḥ saṃbhavitviti||14||

15tadbuddhvā bhṛśasaṃtapto devarājaḥ śatakratuḥ
|
jagāma śaraṇaṃ tatra kaśyapaṃ saṃśitavratam||15||

16tacchrutvā devarājasya kaśyapo'tha prajāpatiḥ
|
vālakhilyānupāgamya karmasiddhimapṛcchata||16||

17evamastviti taṃ cāpi pratyūcuḥ satyavādinaḥ
|
tānkaśyapa uvācedaṃ sāntvapūrvaṃ prajāpatiḥ||17||

18ayamindrastribhuvane niyogādbrahmaṇaḥ kṛtaḥ
|
indrārthe ca bhavanto'pi yatnavantastapodhanāḥ||18||

19na mithyā brahmaṇo vākyaṃ kartumarhatha sattamāḥ
|
bhavatāṃ hi na mithyā'yaṃ saṃkalpo vai cikīrṣitaḥ||19||

20bhavatveṣa patattrīṇāmindro'tibalasattvavān
|
prasādaḥ kriyatāmasya devarājasya yācataḥ||20||

21evamuktāḥ kaśyapena vālakhilyāstapodhanāḥ
|
pratyūcurabhisaṃpūjya muniśreṣṭhaṃ prajāpatim||21||

22vālakhilyā ūcuḥ|
indrārtho'yaṃ samārambhaḥ sarveṣāṃ naḥ prajāpate
|
apatyārthaṃ samārambho bhavataścāyamīpsitaḥ||22||

23tadidaṃ saphalaṃ karma tvayaiva pratigṛhyatām
|
tathā caivaṃ vidhatsvātra yathā śreyo'nupaśyasi||23||

24sautiruvāca|
etasminneva kāle tu devī dākṣāyaṇī śubhā
|
vinatā nāma kalyāṇī putrakāmā yaśasvinī||24||

25tapastaptvā vrataparā snātā puṃsavane śuciḥ
|
upacakrāma bhartāraṃ tāmuvācātha kaśyapaḥ||25||

26ārambhaḥ saphalo devi bhavitā yastvayepsitaḥ
|
janayiṣyasi putrau dvau vīrau tribhuvaneśvarau||26||

27tapasā vālakhilyānāṃ mama saṃkalpajau tathā
|
bhaviṣyato mahābhāgau putrau trailokyapūjitau||27||

28uvāca caināṃ bhagavānkaśyapaḥ punareva ha
|
dhāryatāmapramādena garbho'yaṃ sumahodayaḥ||28||

29etau sarvapatatrīṇāmindratvaṃ kārayiṣyataḥ
|
lokasaṃbhāvito vīrau kāmarūpau vihaṅgamau||29||

30śatakratumathovāca prīyamāṇaḥ prajāpatiḥ
|
tvatsahāyau mahāvīryau bhrātarau te bhaviṣyataḥ||30||

31naitābhyāṃ bhavitā doṣaḥ sakāśātte puraṃdara
|
vyetu te śakrasaṃtāpastvamevendro bhaviṣyasi||31||

32na cāpyevaṃ tvayā bhūyaḥ kṣeptavyā brahmavādinaḥ
|
na cāvamānyā darpātte vāgvajrā bhṛśakopanāḥ||32||

33evamukto jagāmendro nirviśaṅkastriviṣṭapam
|
vinatā cāpi siddhārthā babhūva muditā tathā||33||

34janayāmāsa putrau dvāvaruṇaṃ garuḍaṃ tathā
|
vikalāṅgo'ruṇastatra bhāskarasya purassaraḥ||34||

35patattrīṇāṃ ca garuḍamindratvenābhyaṣiñcata
|
tasyaitatkarma sumahacchrūyatāṃ bhṛgunandana||35||
