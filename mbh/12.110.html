Mahābhārata 12.110 (Śāntiparvan)

1yudhiṣṭhira uvāca|
kliśyamāneṣu bhūteṣu taistairbhāvaistatastataḥ
|
durgāṇyatitaredyena tanme brūhi pitāmaha||1||

2bhīṣma uvāca|
āśrameṣu yathokteṣu yathoktaṃ ye dvijātayaḥ
|
vartante saṃyatātmāno durgāṇyatitaranti te||2||

3ye dambhānnācaranti sma yeṣāṃ vṛttiśca saṃyatā
|
viṣayāṃśca nigṛhṇanti durgāṇyataranti te||3||

4pratyāhurnocyamānā ye na hiṃsanti ca hiṃsitāḥ
|
prayacchanti na yācante durgāṇyatitaranti te||4||

5vāsayantyatithīnnityaṃ nityaṃ ye cānasūyakāḥ
|
nityaṃ svādhyāyaśīlāśca durgāṇyatitaranti te||5||

6mātrāpitrośca ye vṛttiṃ vartante dharmakovidāḥ
|
varjayanti divā svapnaṃ durgāṇyatitaranti te||6||

7ye vā pāpaṃ na kurvanti karmaṇā, manasā, girā
|
nikṣiptadaṇḍā bhūteṣu durgāṇyatitaranti te||7||

8ye na lobhānnayantyarthān rājāno rajasā'nvitāḥ
|
viṣayān parirakṣanti durgāṇyatitaranti te||8||

9sveṣu dāreṣu vartante nyāyavṛttimṛtāvṛtau
|
agnihotraparāḥ santo durgāṇyatitaranti te||9||

10āhaveṣu ca ye śūrāstyaktvā maraṇajaṃ bhayam
|
dharmeṇa jayamicchanti durgāṇyatitaranti te||10||

11ye vadantīha satyāni prāṇatyāge'pyupasthite
|
pramāṇabhūtā bhūtānāṃ durgāṇyatitaranti te||11||

12karmāṇyakuhakārthāni yeṣāṃ vācaśca sūnṛtāḥ
|
yeṣāmāśca sambaddhā durgāṇyatitaranti te||12||

13anadhyāyeṣu ye viprāḥ svādhyāyaṃ neha kurvate
|
taponiṣṭhāḥ sutapaso durgāṇyatitaranti te||13||

14ye tapaśca tapasyanti kaumārabrahmacāriṇaḥ
|
vidyāvedavratasnātā durgāṇyatitaranti te||14||

15ye ca saṃśāntarajasaḥ saṃśāntatamasaśca ye
|
sattve sthitā mahātmāno durgāṇyatitaranti te||15||

16yeṣāṃ na kaścittrasati na trasanti hi kasyacit
|
yeṣāmātmasamo loko durgāṇyatitaranti te||16||

17paraśriyā na tapyanti ye santaḥ puruṣarṣabhāḥ
|
grāmyādarthānivṛttāśca durgāṇyatitaranti te||17||

18sarvāndevānnamasyanti sarvadharmāṃśca śṛṇvate
|
ye śraddadhānāḥ śāntāśca durgāṇyatitaranti te||18||

19ye na mānitvamicchanti mānayanti ca ye parān
|
mānyamānānnamasyanti durgāṇyatitaranti te||19||

20ye ca śrāddhāni kurvanti tithyāṃ tithyāṃ prajārthinaḥ
|
suviśuddhena manasā durgāṇyatitaranti te||20||

21ye krodhaṃ saṃniyacchanti kruddhānsaṃśamayanti ca
|
na ca kupyanti bhūtānāṃ durgāṇyatitaranti te||21||

22madhu māṃsaṃ ca ye nityaṃ varjayantīha mānavāḥ
|
janmaprabhṛti madyaṃ ca durgāṇyatitaranti te||22||

23yātrārthaṃ bhojanaṃ yeṣāṃ saṃtānārthaṃ ca maithunam
|
vāk satyavacanārthāya durgāṇyatitaranti te||23||

24īśvaraṃ sarvabhūtānāṃ jagataḥ prabhavāpyayam
|
bhaktā nārāyaṇaṃ devaṃ durgāṇyatitaranti te||24||

25ya eṣa padmaraktākṣaḥ pītavāsā mahābhujaḥ
|
suhṛdbhrātā ca mitraṃ ca sambandhī ca tathā'cyutaḥ||25||

26ya imānsakalā~llokāṃścarmavatpariveṣṭayet
|
icchanprabhuracintyātmā govindaḥ puruṣottamaḥ||26||

27sthitaḥ priyahite jiṣṇoḥ sa eṣa puruṣottamaḥ
|
rājaṃstava ca durdharṣo vaikuṇṭhaḥ puruṣarṣabha||27||

28ya enaṃ saṃśrayantīha bhaktā nārāyaṇaṃ harim
|
te tarantīha durgāṇi na cātrāsti vicāraṇā||28||

29durgātitaraṇaṃ ye ca paṭhanti śrāvayanti ca
|
kathayanti ca viprebhyo durgāṇyatitaranti te||29||

30iti kṛtyasamuddeśaḥ kīrtitaste mayā'nagha
|
tarante yena durgāṇi paratreha ca mānavāḥ||30||
