Mahābhārata 12.356 (Śāntiparvan)

1brāhmaṇa uvāca|
atibhāro'tha tasyaiva bhārāvataraṇaṃ mahat
|
parāśvāsakaraṃ vākyamidaṃ me bhavataḥ śrutam||1||

2adhvaklāntasya śayanaṃ sthānaklāntasya cāsanam
|
tṛṣitasya ca pānīyaṃ kṣudhārtasya ca bhojanam||2||

3īpsitasyeva saṃprāptirannasya samaye'titheḥ
|
eṣitasyātmanaḥ kāle vṛddhasyaiva suto yathā||3||

4manasā cintitasyeva prītisnigdhasya darśanam
|
prahlādayati māṃ vākyaṃ bhavatā yadudīritam||4||

5dattacakṣurivākāśe paśyāmi vimṛśāmi ca
|
prajñānavacanādyo'yamupadeśo hi me kṛtaḥ||5||

6bāḍhamevaṃ kariṣyāmi yathā me bhāṣate bhavān
|
imāṃ hi rajanīṃ sādho nivasasva mayā saha||6||

7prabhāte yāsyati bhavān paryāśvastaḥ sukhoṣitaḥ
|
asau hi bhagavānsūryo mandaraśmiravāṅmukhaḥ||7||

8bhīṣma uvāca|
tatastena kṛtātithyaḥ so'tithiḥ śatrusūdana
|
uvāsa kila tāṃ rātriṃ saha tena dvijena vai||8||

9caturthadharmasaṃyuktaṃ tayoḥ kathayatostadā
|
vyatītā sā niśā kṛtsnā sukhena divasopamā||9||

10tataḥ prabhātasamaye so'tithistena pūjitaḥ
|
brāhmaṇena yathāśaktyā svakāryamabhikāṅkṣatā||10||

11tataḥ sa vipraḥ kṛtakarmaniścayaḥ kṛtābhyanujñaḥ svajanena dharmakṛt
|
yathopadiṣṭaṃ bhujagendrasaṃśrayaṃ jagāma kāle sukṛtaikaniścayaḥ||11||
