Mahābhārata 13.129 (Anuśāsanaparvan)

1lomaśa uvāca|
paradāreṣu ye saktā akṛtvā dārasaṃgraham
|
nirāśāḥ pitarasteṣāṃ śrāddhakāle bhavanti vai ||1||

2paradāraratiryaśca yaśca vandhyāmupāsate
|
brahmasvaṃ harate yaśca samadoṣā bhavanti te ||2||

3asaṃbhāṣyā bhavantyete pitṝṇāṃ nātra saṃśayaḥ
|
devatāḥ pitaraścaiṣāṃ nābhinandanti taddhaviḥ||3||

4tasmātparasya vai dārāṃstyajedvandhyāṃ ca yoṣitam
|
brahmasvaṃ hi na hartavyamātmano hitamicchatā||4||

5śrūyatāṃ cāparaṃ guhyaṃ rahasyaṃ dharmasaṃhitam
|
śraddadhānena kartavyaṃ gurūṇāṃ vacanaṃ sadā ||5||

6dvādaśyāṃ paurṇamāsyāṃ ca māsi māsi ghṛtākṣatam
|
brāhmaṇebhyaḥ prayaccheta tasya puṇyaṃ nibodhata ||6||

7somaśca vardhate tena samudraśca mahodadhiḥ
|
aśvamedhacaturbhāgaṃ phalaṃ sṛjati vāsavaḥ ||7||

8dānenaitena tejasvī vīryavāṃśca bhavennaraḥ
|
prītaśca bhagavān soma iṣṭān kāmān prayacchati||8||

9śrūyatāṃ cāparo dharmaḥ sarahasyo mahāphalaḥ
|
idaṃ kaliyugaṃ prāpya manuṣyāṇāṃ sukhāvahaḥ ||9||

10kalyamutthāya yo martyaḥ snātaḥ śuklena vāsasā
|
tilapātraṃ prayaccheta brāhmaṇebhyaḥ samāhitaḥ ||10||

11tilodakaṃ ca yo dadyātpitṝṇāṃ madhunā saha
|
dīpakaṃ kṛsaraṃ caiva śrūyatāṃ tasya yatphalam ||11||

12tilapātre phalaṃ prāha bhagavān pākaśāsanaḥ
|
gopradānaṃ ca yaḥ kuryādbhūmidānaṃ ca śāśvatam||12||

13agniṣṭomaṃ ca yo yajñaṃ yajeta bahuda‌kṣiṇam
|
tilapātraṃ sahaitena samaṃ manyanti devatāḥ ||13||

14tilodakaṃ sadā śrāddhe manyante pitaro'kṣayam
|
dīpe ca kṛsare caiva tuṣyante'sya pitāmahāḥ ||14||

15svarge ca pitṛloke ca pitṛdevābhipūjitam
|
evametanmayoddiṣṭamṛṣidṛṣṭaṃ purātanam ||15||
