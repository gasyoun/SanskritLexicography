Mahābhārata 12.66 (Śāntiparvan)

1yudhiṣṭhira uvāca|
śrutā me kathatāḥ pūrve catvāro mānavāśramāḥ
|
vyākhyānayitvā vyākhyānameṣāmācakṣva pṛcchataḥ||1||

2bhīṣma uvāca|
viditāḥ sarva eveha dharmāstava yudhiṣṭhira
|
yathā mama mahābāho viditāḥ sādhusammatāḥ||2||

3yattu liṅgāntaragataṃ pṛcchase māṃ yudhiṣṭhira
|
dharmaṃ dharmabhṛtāṃ śreṣṭha tannibodha narādhipa||3||

4sarvāṇyetāni kaunteya vidyante manujarṣabha
|
sādhvācārapravṛttānāṃ cāturāśramyakāriṇām||4||

5akāmadveṣayuktasya daṇḍanītyā yudhiṣṭhira
|
samadarśinaśca bhūteṣu bhaikṣyāśramapadaṃ bhavet||5||

6vetti jñānavisargaṃ ca nigrahānugrahaṃ tathā
|
yathoktavṛtterdhīrasya kṣemāśramapadaṃ bhavet||6||

7arhānpūjayato nityaṃ saṃvibhāgena pāṇḍava
|
sarvatastasya kaunteya bhaikṣyāśramapadaṃ bhavet||7||

8jñātisambandhimitrāṇi vyāpannāni yudhiṣṭhira
|
samabhyuddharamāṇasya dīkṣāśramapadaṃ bhavet||8||

9lokamukhyeṣu satkāraṃ liṅgimukhyeṣu cāsakṛt
|
kurvatastasya kaunteya vanyāśramapadaṃ bhavet||9||

10āhnikaṃ pitṛyajñāṃśca bhūtayajñānsamānuṣān
|
kurvataḥ pārtha vipulānvanyāśramapadaṃ bhavet||10||

11saṃvibhāgena bhūtānāmatithīnāṃ tathārcanāt
|
devayajñaiśca rājendra vanyāśramapadaṃ bhavet||11||

12mardanaṃ pararāṣṭrāṇāṃ śiṣṭārthaṃ satyavikrama
|
kurvataḥ puruṣavyāghra vanyāśramapadaṃ bhavet||12||

13pālanātsarvabhūtānāṃ svarāṣṭraparipālanāt
|
dīkṣā bahuvidhā rājansatyāśramapadaṃ bhavet||13||

14vedādhyayananityatvaṃ kṣamā'thācāryapūjanam
|
athopādhyāyaśuśrūṣā brahmāśramapadaṃ bhavet||14||

15āhnikaṃ japamānasya devānpūjayataḥ sadā
|
dharmeṇa puruṣavyāghra dharmāśramapadaṃ bhavet||15||

16mṛtyurvā rakṣaṇaṃ veti yasya rājño viniścayaḥ
|
prāṇadyūte tatastasya brahmāśramapadaṃ bhavet||16||

17ajihmamaśaṭhaṃ mārgaṃ vartamānasya bhārata
|
sarvadā sarvabhūteṣu brahmāśramapadaṃ bhavet||17||

18vānaprastheṣu vipreṣu traividyeṣu ca bhārata
|
prayacchato'rthānvipulānvanyāśramapadaṃ bhavet||18||

19sarvabhūteṣvanukrośaṃ kurvatastasya bhārata
|
ānṛśaṃsyapravṛttasya sarvāvasthaṃ padaṃ bhavet||19||

20bālavṛddheṣu kaunteya sarvāvasthaṃ yudhiṣṭhira
|
anukrośakriyā pārtha sarvāvasthaṃ padaṃ bhavet||20||

21balātkṛteṣu bhūteṣu paritrāṇaṃ kurūdvaha
|
śaraṇāgateṣu kauravya kurvangārhasthyamāvaset||21||

22carācarāṇāṃ bhūtānāṃ rakṣaṇaṃ cāpi sarvaśaḥ
|
yathārhapūjāṃ ca tathā kurvangārhasthyamāvaset||22||

23jyeṣṭhānujyeṣṭhapatnīnāṃ bhrātṝṇāṃ putranaptṝṇām
|
nigrahānugrahau pārtha gārhasthyamiti tattapaḥ||23||

24sādhūnāmarcanīyānāṃ pūjāsu viditātmanām
|
pālanaṃ puruṣavyāghra gṛhāśramapadaṃ bhavet||24||

25āśramasthāni bhūtāni yastu veśmani bhārata
|
ādadīteha bhojyena tadgārhasthyaṃ yudhiṣṭhira||25||

26yaḥ sthitaḥ puruṣo dharme dhātrā sṛṣṭe yathārthavat
|
āśramāṇāṃ hi sarveṣāṃ phalaṃ prāpnotyanāmayam||26||

27yasminna naśyanti guṇāḥ kaunteya puruṣe sadā
|
āśramasthaṃ tamapyāhurnaraśreṣṭhaṃ yudhiṣṭhira||27||

28sthānamānaṃ kule mānaṃ vayomānaṃ tathaiva ca
|
kurvanvasati sarveṣu hyāśrameṣu yudhiṣṭhira||28||

29deśadharmāṃśca kaunteya kuladharmāṃstathaiva ca
|
pālayanpuruṣavyāghra rājā sarvāśramī bhavet||29||

30kāle vibhūtiṃ bhūtānāmupahārāṃstathaiva ca
|
arhayanpuruṣavyāghra sādhūnāmāśrame vaset||30||

31daśadharmagataścāpi yo dharmaṃ pratyavekṣate
|
sarvalokasya kaunteya rājā bhavati so'śramī||31||

32ye dharmakuśalā loke dharmaṃ kurvanti bhārata
|
pālitā yasya viṣaye dharmāṃśastasya bhūpateḥ||32||

33dharmārāmāndharmaparānye na rakṣanti mānavān
|
pārthivāḥ puruṣavyāghra teṣāṃ pāpaṃ haranti te||33||

34ye cāpyatra sahāyāḥ syuḥ pārthivānāṃ yudhiṣṭhira
|
te caivāṃśaharāḥ sarve dharme parakṛte'nagha||34||

35sarvāśramapade'pyāhurgārhasthyaṃ dīptanirṇayam
|
pāvanaṃ puruṣavyāghra yaṃ dharmaṃ paryupāsmahe||35||

36ātmopamastu bhūteṣu yo vai bhavati mānavaḥ
|
nyastadaṇḍo jitakrodhaḥ pretyeha labhate sukham||36||

37dharme sthitā sattvavīryā dharmasetuvaṭārakā
|
tyāgavātādhvagā śīghrā naustaṃ saṃtārayiṣyati||37||

38yadā nivṛttaḥ sarvasmāt kāmo yo'sya hṛdi sthitaḥ
|
tadā bhavati sattvasthastato brahma samaśnute||38||

39suprasannastu bhāvena yogena ca narādhipa
|
dharmaṃ puruṣaśārdūla prāpsyate pālane rataḥ||39||

40vedādhyayanaśīlānāṃ viprāṇāṃ sādhukarmaṇām
|
pālane yatnamātiṣṭha sarvalokasya caiva ha||40||

41vane caranti ye dharmamāśrameṣu ca bhārata
|
rakṣaṇāttacchataguṇaṃ dharmaṃ prāpnoti pārthivaḥ||41||

42eṣa te vividho dharmaḥ pāṇḍavaśreṣṭha kīrtitaḥ
|
anutiṣṭha tvamenaṃ vai pūrvadṛṣṭaṃ sanātanam||42||

43cāturāśramyamaikāgryaṃ cāturvaryaṃ ca pāṇḍava
|
dharmaṃ puruṣaśārdūla prāpsyase pālane rataḥ||43||
