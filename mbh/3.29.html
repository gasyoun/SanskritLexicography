Mahābhārata 3.29 (Āraṇyakaparvan)

1yudhiṣṭhira uvāca|
krodho hantā manuṣyāṇāṃ krodho bhāvayitā punaḥ
|
iti viddhi mahāprājñe krodhamūlau bhavābhavau||1||

2yo hi saṃharate krodhaṃ bhavastasya suśobhane
|
yaḥ punaḥ puruṣaḥ krodhaṃ nityaṃ na sahate śubhe
|
tasyābhāvāya bhavati krodhaḥ paramadāruṇaḥ||2||

3krodhamūlo vināśo hi prajānāmiha dṛśyate
|
tatkathaṃ mādṛśaḥ krodhamutsṛjellokanāśanam||3||

4kruddhaḥ pāpaṃ naraḥ kuryātkruddho hanyādgurūnapi
|
kruddhaḥ paruṣayā vācā śreyaso'pyavamanyate||4||

5vācyāvācye hi kupito na prajānāti karhicit
|
nākāryamasti kruddhasya nāvācyaṃ vidyate tathā||5||

6hiṃsyātkrodhādavadhyāṃstu vadhyānsaṃpūjayīta ca
|
ātmānamapi ca kruddhaḥ preṣayedyamasādanam||6||

7etāndoṣānprapaśyadbhirjitaḥ krodho manīṣibhiḥ
|
icchadbhiḥ paramaṃ śreya iha cāmutra cottamam||7||

8taṃ krodhaṃ varjitaṃ dhīraiḥ kathamasmadvidhaścaret
|
etaddraupadi sandhāya na me manyuḥ pravardhate||8||

9ātmānaṃ ca parāṃścaiva trāyate mahato bhayāt
|
krudhyantamapratikrudhyandvayoreṣa cikitsakaḥ||9||

10mūḍho yadi kliśyamānaḥ kliśyate'śaktimānnaraḥ
|
balīyasāṃ manuṣyāṇāṃ tyajatyātmānamātmanā||10||

11tasyātmānaṃ santyajato lokā naśyantyanātmanaḥ
|
tasmāddraupadyaśaktasya manyorniyamanaṃ smṛtam||11||

12vidvāṃstathaiva yaḥ śaktaḥ kliśyamāno na kupyati
|
anāśayitvā kleṣṭāraṃ paraloke ca nandati||12||

13tasmādbalavatā caiva durbalena ca nityadā
|
kṣantavyaṃ puruṣeṇāhurāpatsvapi vijānatā||13||

14manyorhi vijayaṃ kṛṣṇe praśaṃsantīha sādhavaḥ
|
kṣamāvato jayo nityaṃ sādhoriha satāṃ matam||14||

15satyaṃ cānṛtataḥ śreyo nṛśaṃsāccānṛśaṃsatā
|
tamevaṃ bahudoṣaṃ tu krodhaṃ sādhuvivarjitam||15||

16mādṛśaḥ prasṛjetkasmātsuyodhanavadhādapi
|
tejasvīti yamāhurvai paṇḍitā dīrghadarśinaḥ||16||

17na krodho'bhyantarastasya bhavatīti viniścitam
|
yastu krodhaṃ samutpannaṃ prajñayā pratibādhate||17||

18tejasvinaṃ taṃ vidvāṃso manyante tattvadarśinaḥ
|
kruddho hi kāryaṃ suśroṇi na yathāvatprapaśyati
|
nākāryaṃ na ca maryādāṃ naraḥ kruddho'nupaśyati||18||

19hantyavadhyānapi kruddho gurūnkruddhastudatyapi
|
tasmāttejasi kartavyaḥ krodho dūre pratiṣṭhitaḥ||19||

20dākṣyaṃ hyamarṣaḥ śauryaṃ ca śīghratvamiti tejasaḥ
|
guṇāḥ krodhābhibhūtena na śakyāḥ prāptumañjasā||20||

21krodhaṃ tyaktvā tu puruṣaḥ samyaktejo'bhipadyate
|
kālayuktaṃ mahāprājñe kruddhaistejaḥ suduḥsaham||21||

22krodhastu paṇḍitaiḥ śaśvatteja ityabhiniścitam
|
rajastu lokanāśāya vihitaṃ mānuṣaṃ prati||22||

23tasmācchaśvattyajetkrodhaṃ puruṣaḥ samyagācaran
|
śreyānsvadharmānapago na kruddha iti niścitam||23||

24yadi sarvamabuddhīnāmatikrāntamacetasām
|
atikramo madvidhasya kathaṃ svitsyādanindite||24||

25yadi na syurmanuṣyeṣu kṣamiṇaḥ pṛthivīsamāḥ
|
na syātsandhirmanuṣyāṇāṃ krodhamūlo hi vigrahaḥ||25||

26abhiṣakto hyabhiṣajedāhanyādguruṇā hataḥ
|
evaṃ vināśo bhūtānāmadharmaḥ prathito bhavet||26||

27ākruṣṭaḥ puruṣaḥ sarvaṃ pratyākrośedanantaram
|
pratihanyāddhataścaiva tathā hiṃsyācca hiṃsitaḥ||27||

28hanyurhi pitaraḥ putrānputrāścāpi tathā pitṝn
|
hanyuśca patayo bhāryāḥ patīnbhāryāstathaiva ca||28||

29evaṃ saṅkupite loke janma kṛṣṇe na vidyate
|
prajānāṃ sandhimūlaṃ hi janmaviddhi śubhānane||29||

30tāḥ kṣiperanprajāḥ sarvāḥ kṣipraṃ draupadi tādṛśe
|
tasmānmanyurvināśāya prajānāmabhavāya ca||30||

31yasmāttu loke dṛśyante kṣamiṇaḥ pṛthivīsamāḥ
|
tasmājjanma ca bhūtānāṃ bhavaśca pratipadyate||31||

32kṣantavyaṃ puruṣeṇeha sarvāpatsu suśobhane
|
kṣamāvato hi bhūtānāṃ janma caiva prakīrtitam||32||

33ākruṣṭastāḍitaḥ kruddhaḥ kṣamate yo balīyasā
|
yaśca nityaṃ jitakrodho vidvānuttamapūruṣaḥ||33||

34prabhāvavānapi narastasya lokāḥ sanātanāḥ
|
krodhanastvalpavijñānaḥ pretya ceha ca naśyati||34||

35atrāpyudāharantīmā gāthā nityaṃ kṣamāvatām
|
gītāḥ kṣamāvatāṃ kṛṣṇe kāśyapena mahātmanā||35||

36kṣamā dharmaḥ kṣamā yajñaḥ kṣamā vedāḥ kṣamā śrutam
|
ya etadevaṃ jānāti sa sarvaṃ kṣantumarhati||36||

37kṣamā brahma kṣamā satyaṃ kṣamā bhūtaṃ ca bhāvi ca
|
kṣamā tapaḥ kṣamā śaucaṃ kṣamayedaṃ dhṛtaṃ jagat||37||

38atiyajñavidāṃ lokānkṣamiṇaḥ prāpnuvanti ca
|
atibrahmavidāṃ lokānati cāpi tapasvinām||38||

39anye vai yajuṣāṃ lokāḥ karmiṇāmapare tathā
|
kṣamāvatāṃ brahmaloke lokāḥ paramapūjitāḥ||39||

40kṣamā tejasvināṃ tejaḥ kṣamā brahma tapasvinām
|
kṣamā satyaṃ satyavatāṃ kṣamā yajñaḥ kṣamā śamaḥ||40||

41tāṃ kṣamāṃ tādṛśīṃ kṛṣṇe kathamasmadvidhastyajet
|
yasyāṃ brahma ca satyaṃ ca yajñā lokāśca dhiṣṭhitāḥ||41||

42kṣantavyameva satataṃ puruṣeṇa vijānatā
|
yadā hi kṣamate sarvaṃ brahma sampadyate tadā||42||

43kṣamāvatāmayaṃ lokaḥ paraścaiva kṣamāvatām
|
iha sanmānamṛcchanti paratra ca śubhāṃ gatim||43||

44yeṣāṃ manyurmanuṣyāṇāṃ kṣamayābhihataḥ sadā
|
teṣāṃ paratare lokāstasmātkṣāntiḥ parā matā||44||

45iti gītāḥ kāśyapena gāthā nityaṃ kṣamāvatām
|
śrutvā gāthāḥ kṣamāyāstvaṃ tuṣya draupadi mā krudhaḥ||45||

46pitāmahaḥ śāntanavaḥ śamaṃ sampūjayiṣyati
|
kṛṣṇaśca devakīputraḥ śamaṃ saṃpūjayiṣyati||46||

47ācāryo viduraḥ kṣattā kṣamameva vadiṣyataḥ
|
kṛpaśca sañjayaścaiva śamameva vadiṣyataḥ||47||

48somadatto yuyutsuśca droṇaputrastathaiva ca
|
pitāmahaśca no vyāsaḥ śamaṃ vadati nityaśaḥ||48||

49etairhi rājā niyataṃ codyamānaḥ śamaṃ prati
|
rājyaṃ dāteti me buddhirna cellobhānnaśiṣyati||49||

50kālo'yaṃ dāruṇaḥ prāpto bharatānāmabhūtaye
|
niścitaṃ me sadaivaitatpurastādapi bhāvini||50||

51suyodhano nārhatīti kṣamāmevaṃ na vindati
|
arhastatrāhamityevaṃ tasmānmāṃ vindate kṣamā||51||

52etadātmavatāṃ vṛttameṣa dharmaḥ sanātanaḥ
|
kṣamā caivānṛśaṃsyaṃ ca tatkartāsmyahamañjasā||52||
