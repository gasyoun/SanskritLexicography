Mahābhārata 1.17 (Ādiparvan)

1sautiruvāca|
etasminneva kāle tu bhaginyau te tapodhana
|
apaśyatāṃ samāyāte uccaiḥśravasamantikāt||1||

2yaṃ tu devagaṇāḥ sarve hṛṣṭarūpamapūjayan
|
mathyamāne'mṛte jātamaśvaratnamanuttamam||2||

3amoghabalamaśvānāmuttamaṃ javināṃ varam
|
śrīmantamajaraṃ divyaṃ sarvalakṣaṇapūjitam||3||

4śaunaka uvāca|
kathaṃ tadamṛtaṃ devairmathitaṃ kva ca śaṃsa me
|
yatra jajñe mahāvīryaḥ so'śvarājo mahādyutiḥ||4||

5sautiruvāca|
jvalantamacalaṃ meruṃ tejorāśimanuttamam
|
ākṣipantaṃ prabhāṃ bhānoḥsvaśṛṅgaiḥkāñcanojjvalaiḥ||5||

6kanakābharaṇaṃ citraṃ devagandharvasevitam
|
aprameyamanādhṛṣyamadharmabahulairjanaiḥ||6||

7vyālairāvāritaṃ ghorairdivyauṣadhividīpitam
|
nākamāvṛtya tiṣṭhantamucchrayeṇa mahāgirim||7||

8agamyaṃ manasāpyanyairnadīvṛkṣasamanvitam
|
nānāpatagasaṅghaiśca nāditaṃ sumanoharaiḥ||8||

9tasya śṛṅgamupāruhya bahuratnācitaṃ śubham
|
anantakalpamadvaddhaṃ surāḥ sarve mahaujasaḥ||9||

10te mantrayitumārabdhāstatrāsīnā divaukasaḥ
|
amṛtāya samāgamya taponiyamasaṃyutāḥ||10||

11tatra nārāyaṇo devo brahmāṇamidamabravīt
|
cintayatsu sureṣvevaṃ mantrayatsu ca sarvaśaḥ||11||

12devairasurasaṅghaiśca mathyatāṃ kalaśodadhiḥ
|
bhaviṣyatyamṛtaṃ tatra mathyamāne mahodadhau||12||

13sarvauṣadhīḥ samāvāpya sarvaratnāni caiva ha
|
manthadhvayudadhiṃ devā vetsyadhvamamṛtaṃ tataḥ||13||
