Mahābhārata 3.58 (Āraṇyakaparvan)

1bṛhadaśva uvāca| vṛte tu naiṣadhe bhaimyā lokapālā mahaujasaḥ
|
yānto dadṛśurāyāntaṃ dvāparaṃ kalinā saha||1||

2athābravītkaliṃ śakraḥ saṃprekṣya balavṛtrahā
|
dvāpareṇa sahāyena kale brūhi kva yāsyasi||2||

3tato'bravītkaliḥ śakraṃ damayantyāḥ svayaṃvaram
|
gatvā hi varayiṣye tāṃ mano hi mama tāṃ gatam||3||

4tamabravītprahasyendro nirvṛttaḥ sa svayaṃvaraḥ
|
vṛtastayā nalo rājā patirasmatsamīpataḥ||4||

5evamuktastu śakreṇa kaliḥ kopasamanvitaḥ
|
devānāmantrya tānsarvānuvācedaṃ vacastadā||5||

6devānāṃ mānuṣaṃ madhye yatsā patimavindata
|
nanu tasyā bhavennyāyyaṃ vipulaṃ daṇḍadhāraṇam||6||

7evamukte tu kalinā pratyūcuste divaukasaḥ
|
asmābhiḥ samanujñāte damayantyā nalo vṛtaḥ||7||

8kā hi sarvaguṇopetaṃ nāśrayeta nalaṃ nṛpam
|
yo veda dharmānakhilānyathāvaccaritavrataḥ||8||

9yo'dhīte caturo vedānsarvānākhyānapañcamān
|
nityaṃ tṛptā gṛhe yasya devā yajñeṣu dharmataḥ
|
ahiṃsānirato yaśca satyavādī dṛḍhavrataḥ||9||

10yasmindākṣyaṃ dhṛtirjñānaṃ tapaḥ śaucaṃ damaḥ śamaḥ
|
dhruvāṇi puruṣavyāghre lokapālasame nṛpe||10||

11evaṃrūpaṃ nalaṃ yo vai kāmayecchapituṃ kale
|
ātmānaṃ sa śapenmūḍho hanyādātmānamātmanā||11||

12evaṃguṇaṃ nalaṃ yo vai kāmayecchapituṃ kale
|
kṛcchre sa narake majjedagādhe vipule hrade
|
evamuktvā kaliṃ devā dvāparaṃ ca divaṃ yayuḥ||12||

13tato gateṣu deveṣu kalirdvāparamabravīt
|
saṃhartuṃ notsahe kopaṃ nale vatsyāmi dvāpara||13||

14bhraṃśayiṣyāmi taṃ rājyānna bhaimyā saha raṃsyate
|
tvamapyakṣānsamāviśya sāhāyyaṃ kartumarhasi||14||
