Mahābhārata 12.316 (Śāntiparvan)

1yājñavaklya uvāca |
sāṃkhyajñānaṃ mayā proktaṃ yogajñānaṃ nibodha me
|
yathāśrutaṃ yathādṛṣṭaṃ tattvena nṛpasattama||1||

2nāsti sāṃkhyasamaṃ jñānaṃ nāsti yogasamaṃ balam
|
tāvubhāvekacaryau tāvubhāvanidhanau smṛtau||2||

3pṛthak pṛthak prapaśyanti ye'pyabuddhiratā narāḥ
|
vayaṃ tu rājan paśyāma ekameva tu niścayāt||3||

4yadeva yogāḥ paśyanti tatsāṃkhyairapi dṛśyate
|
ekaṃ sāṃkhyaṃ ca yogaṃ ca yaḥ paśyati sa tattvavit||4||

5rudrapradhānānaparānviddhi yogānarindam
|
tenaiva cātha dehena vicaranti diśo daśa||5||

6yāvaddhi pralayastāta sūkṣmeṇāṣṭaguṇena ha
|
yogena lokānvicaransukhaṃ saṃnyasya cānagha||6||

7vedeṣu cāṣṭaguṇinaṃ yogamāhurmanīṣiṇaḥ
|
sūkṣmamaṣṭaguṇaṃ prāhurnetaraṃ nṛpasattama||7||

8dviguṇaṃ yogakṛtyaṃ tu yogānāṃ prāhuruttamam
|
saguṇaṃ nirguṇaṃ caiva yathā śāstranidarśanam||8||

9dhāraṇaṃ caiva manasaḥ prāṇāyāmaśca pārthiva
|
ekāgratā ca manasaḥ prāṇāyāmastathaiva ca||9||

10prāṇāyāmo hi saguṇo nirguṇaṃ dhārayenmanaḥ
|
yadyadṛśyati muñcanvai prāṇānmaithilasattama
|
vātādhikyaṃ bhavatyeva tasmāttaṃ na samācaret||10||

11niśāyāḥ prathame yāme codanā dvādaśa smṛtāḥ
|
madhye svapnātpare yāme dvādaśaiva tu codanāḥ||11||

12tadevamupaśāntena dāntenaikāntaśīlinā
|
ātmārāmeṇa buddhena yoktavyo''tmā na saṃśayaḥ||12||

13pañcānāmindriyāṇāṃ tu doṣānākṣipya pañcadhā
|
śabdaṃ rūpaṃ tathā sparśaṃ rasaṃ gandhaṃ tathaiva ca||13||

14pratibhāmapavargaṃ ca pratisaṃhṛtya maithila
|
indriyagrāmamakhilaṃ manasyabhiniveśya ha||14||

15manastathaivāhaṃkāre pratiṣṭhāpya narādhipa
|
ahaṃkāra tathā buddhau buddhiṃ ca prakṛtāvapi||15||

16evaṃ hi parisaṃkhyāya tato dhyāyanti kevalam
|
virajaskamalaṃ nityamanantaṃ śuddhamavraṇam||16||

17tasthuṣaṃ puruṣaṃ nityamabhedyamajarāmaram
|
śāśvataṃ cāvyayaṃ caiva īśānaṃ brahma cāvyayam||17||

18yuktasya tu mahārāja lakṣaṇānyupadhāraya
|
lakṣaṇaṃ tu prasādasya yathā tṛptaḥ sukhaṃ svapet||18||

19nirvāte tu yathā dīpo jvaletsnehasamanvitaḥ
|
niścalo'rdhvaśikhastadvadyuktamāhurmanīṣiṇaḥ||19||

20pāṣāṇa iva meghotthairyathā bindubhirāhataḥ
|
nālaṃ cālayituṃ śakyastathā yuktasya lakṣaṇam||20||

21śaṅkhadundubhinirghoṣairvividhairgītavāditaiḥ
|
kriyamāṇairna kampeta muktasyaitannidarśanam||21||

22tailapātraṃ yathā pūrṇaṃ karābhyāṃ gṛhya pūruṣaḥ
|
sopānamāruhedbhītastarjyamāno'sipāṇibhiḥ||22||

23saṃyatātmā bhayātteṣāṃ na pātrādbindumutsṛjet
|
tathaivottaramāgamya ekāgramanasastathā||23||

24sthiratvādindriyāṇāṃ tu niścalatvāttathaiva ca
|
evaṃ yuktasya tu munerlakṣaṇānyupalakṣayet||24||

25svayuktaḥ paśyate brahma yattatparamamavyayam
|
mahatastamaso madhye sthitaṃ jvalanasannibham||25||

26etena kevalaṃ yāti tyaktvā dehamasākṣikam
|
kālena mahatā rājanśrutireṣā sanātanī||26||

27etaddhi yogaṃ yogānāṃ kimanyadyogalakṣaṇam
|
vijñāya taddhi manyante kṛtakṛtyā manīṣiṇaḥ||27||
