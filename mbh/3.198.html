Mahābhārata 3.198 (Āraṇyakaparvan)

1vaiśampāyana uvāca|
bhūya eva mahābhāgyaṃ kathyatāmityabravīt pāṇḍavo mārkaṇḍeyam
|
athācaṣṭa mārkaṇḍeyaḥ
|
aṣṭakasya vaiśvāmitreraśvamedhe sarve rājānaḥ prāgacchan||1||

2bhrātaraścāsya pratardano vasumanāḥ śibirauśīnara iti
|
sa ca samāptayajño bhrātṛbhiḥ saha rathena prāyāt te ca nāradamāgacchantamabhivādyārohatu bhavān rathamityabruvan||2||

3tāṃstathetyuktvā rathamāruroha
|
atha teṣāmekaḥ surarṣiṃ nāradamabravīt
|
prasādya bhagavantaṃ kiñcidiccheyaṃ praṣṭumiti||3||

4pṛcchetyabravīdṛṣiḥ
|
so'bravīdāyuṣmantaḥ sarvaguṇapramuditāḥ
|
athāyuṣmantaṃ svargasthānaṃ caturbhiryātavyaṃ syāt ko'vataret
|
ayamaṣṭako'vataredityabravīdṛṣiḥ||4||

5kiṃ kāraṇamityapṛcchat
|
athācaṣṭāṣṭakasya gṛhe mayā uṣitaṃ sa māṃ rathenānuprāvaha‌thāpaśyamanekāni gosahasrāṇi varṇaśo viviktāni tamahamapṛcchaṃ kasyemā gāva iti so'bravīt
|
mayā nissṛṣṭā ityetāstenaiva svayaṃ ślāghati kathitena
|
eṣo'vataredatha tribhiryātavyaṃ sāmprataṃ ko'vataret||5||

6pratardana ityabravīdṛṣiḥ
|
tatra kiṃ kāraṇaṃ pratardanasyāpi gṛhe mayoṣitaṃ sa māṃ rathenānuprāvahat||6||

7athainaṃ brāhmaṇo'bhikṣetāśvaṃ me dadātu bhavānnivṛtto dāsyāmītyabravīdbrāhmaṇaṃ tvaritameva dīyatāmityabravīdbrāhmaṇastvaritameva sa brāhmaṇasyaivamuktvā dakṣiṇaṃ pārśvamadadat||7||

8athānyo'pyaśvārthī brāhmaṇa āgacchat
|
tathaiva cainamuktvā vāmapārṣṇimabhyadādatha prāyāt punarapi cānyo'pyaśvārthī brāhmaṇa āgacchat tvarito'tha tasmai apanahya vāmaṃ dhuryamadadat||8||

9atha prāyāt punaranya āgacchadaśvārthī brāhmaṇastamabravīdatiyāto dāsyāmi tvaritameva me dīyatāmityabravīdbrāhmaṇastasmai datvā'śvaṃ rathadhuraṃ gṛhṇatā vyāhṛtaṃ brāhmaṇānāṃ sāmprataṃ nāsti kiñciditi||9||

10ya eṣa dadāti cāsūyati ca tena vyāhṛtena tathā'vataret
|
atha dvābhyāṃ yātavyamiti ko'vataret||10||

11vasumanā avataredityabravīdṛṣiḥ||11||

12kiṃ kāraṇamityapṛcchadathācaṣṭa nāradaḥ
|
ahaṃ paribhraman vasumanaso gṛhamupasthitaḥ||12||

13svastivacanamāsīt puṣparathasya prayojanena tamahamanvagacchaṃ svastivāciteṣu brāhmaṇeṣu ratho brāhmaṇānāṃ darśitaḥ||13||

14tamahaṃ rathaṃ prāśaṃsamatha rājā'bravīdbhagavatā rathaḥ praśastaḥ
|
eṣa bhagavato bhagavato ratha iti||14||

15atha kadācit punarapyahamupasthitaḥ punareva ca rathaprayojanamāsīt
|
samyagayameṣa bhagavata ityevaṃrājā'bravīditi punareva tṛtīyaṃ svastivācanaṃ samabhāvayamatha rājā brāhmaṇānāṃ darśayan māmabhiprekṣyābravīt
|
atho bhagavatā puṣparathasya svastivācanāni suṣṭhu sambhāvitāni etena drohavacanenāvataret||15||

16athaikena yātavyaṃ syāt ko'vataret punarnārada āha
|
śibiryāyādahamavatareyamatra kiṃ kāraṇamityabravīt
|
asāvahaṃ śibinā samo nāsmi yato brāhmaṇaḥ kaścidenamabravīt||16||

17śibe annārthyasmīti tamabravīcchibiḥ kiṃ kriyatāmājñāpayatu bhavāniti||17||

18athainaṃ brāhmaṇo'bravīt ya eṣa te putro bṛhadgarbho nāma eṣa pramātavya iti tamenaṃ saṃskuru annaṃ copapādaya tato'haṃ pratīkṣya iti
|
tataḥ putraṃ pramāthya saṃskṛtya vidhinā sādhayitvā pātryāmarpayitvā śirasā pratigṛhya brāhmaṇamamṛgayat||18||

19athāsya mṛgayamāṇasya kaścidācaṣṭa eṣa te brāhmaṇo nagaraṃ praviśya dahati te gṛhaṃ kośāgāramāyudhāgāraṃ stryagāramaśvaśālāṃ hastiśālāṃ ca kruddha iti||19||

20atha śibistathaivāvikṛtamukhavarṇo nagaraṃ praviśya brāhmaṇaṃ tamabravīt siddhaṃ bhagavannannamiti brāhmaṇo na kiñcidvyājahāra vismayāda‌dhomukhaścāsīt||20||

21tataḥ prāsādayadbrāhmaṇaṃ bhagavan bhujyatāmiti
|
muhūrtādudvīkṣya śibimabravīt||21||

22tvamevaitadaśāneti tatrāha tatheti śibistathaivāvimanā mahitvā kapālamabhyuddhārya bhoktumaicchat||22||

23athāsya brāhmaṇo hastamagṛhṇāt
|
abravīccainaṃ jitakrodho'si na te kiñcidaparityājyaṃ brāhmaṇyārthe brāhmaṇo'pi taṃ mahābhāgaṃ sabhājayat||23||

24sa hyudvīkṣamāṇaḥ putramapaśyadagre tiṣṭhantaṃ devakumāramiva puṇyagandhānvitamalaṅkṛtaṃ sarvaṃ ca tamarthaṃ vidhāya brāhmaṇo'ntaradhīyata||24||

25tasya rājarṣervidhātā tenaiva veṣeṇa parīkṣārthamāgata iti tasminnantarhite amātyā rājānamūcuḥ
|
kiṃ prepsunā bhavatā idamevaṃ jānatā kṛtamiti||25||

26śibiruvāca| naivāhametadyaśase dadāni na cārthahetorna ca bhogatṛṣṇayā
|
pāpairanāsevita eṣa mārga ityevametat sakalaṃ karomi||26||

27sadbhiḥ sadā'dhyāsitaṃ tu praśastaṃ tasmāt praśastaṃ śrayate matirme
|
etanmahābhāgyavaraṃ śibestu tasmādahaṃ veda yathāvadetat||27||
