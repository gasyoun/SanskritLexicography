Mahābhārata 4.17 (Virāṭaparvan)

1vaiśampāyana uvāca|sā hatā sūtaputreṇa rājapatnī yaśasvinī
|
vadhaṃ kṛṣṇā parīpsantī senāvāhasya bhāminī ||1||

2jagāmāvāsamevātha sā tadā drupadātmajā
|
kṛtvā śaucaṃ yathānyāyaṃ kṛṣṇā sā tanumadhyamā ||2||

3gātrāṇi vāsasī caiva prakṣālya salilena sā
|
cintayāmāsa rudatī tasya duḥkhasya nirṇayam ||3||

4kiṃ karomi kva gacchāmi kathaṃ kāryaṃ bhavenmama
|
ityevaṃ cintayitvā sā bhīmaṃ vai manasā'gamat ||4||

5nānyaḥ kartā ṛte bhīmānmamādya manasaḥ priyam
|
tata utthāya rātrau sā vihāya śayanaṃ svakam ||5||

6prādravannāthamicchantī kṛṣṇā nāthavatī satī
|
bhavanaṃ bhīmasenasya kṣipramāyatalocanā ||6||

7duḥkhena mahatā yuktā mānasena manasvinī
|
sairandhyuvāca
|
tasmin jīvati pāpiṣṭhe senāvāha mama dviṣi ||7||

8tat karma kṛtavānadya kathaṃ nidrāṃ niṣevase
|
vaiśampāyana uvāca|evamuktvā'tha tāṃ śālāṃ praviveśa manasvinī ||8||

9yasyāṃ bhīmastathā śete mṛgarāja iva śvasan
|
tasyā rūpeṇa sā śālā bhīmasya ca mahātmanaḥ ||9||

10saṃmūrchiteva kauravya prajajvāla ca tejasā
|
sā vai mahānasaṃ prāpya bhīmasenaṃ śucismitā ||10||

11sarvaśveteva māheyī vane jātā trihāyaṇī
|
upātiṣṭhata pāñcālī vāsiteva vararṣabham ||11||

12sā lateva mahāśālaṃ phullaṃ gomatitīrajam
|
pariṣvajata pāñcālī madhyamaṃ pāṇḍunandanam ||12||

13bāhubhyāṃ parirabhyainaṃ prābodhayadaninditā
|
siṃhaṃ suptaṃ vane durge mṛgarājavadhūriva ||13||

14bhīmasenamupāśliṣyaddhastinīva mahāgajam
|
vīṇeva madhurālāpā gāndhāraṃ sādhu mūrchatī
|
abhyabhāṣata pāñcālī bhīmasenamaninditā ||14||

15uttiṣṭhottiṣṭha kiṃ śeṣe bhīmasena yathā mṛtaḥ
|
nāmṛtasya hi pāpīyān bhāryāmālabhya jīvati ||15||

16sa saṃprahāya śayanaṃ rājaputryā prabodhitaḥ
|
upātiṣṭhata meghābhaḥ paryaṅke sopasaṃgrahe ||16||

17athābravīdrājaputrīṃ kauravyo mahiṣīṃ priyām
|
kenāsyathana saṃprāptā tvariteva mamāntikam ||17||

18na te prakṛtimān varṇaḥ kṛśā pāṇḍuśca lakṣyase
|
ācakṣva pariṣeśeṇa sarvaṃ vidyāmahaṃ yathā ||18||

19sukhaṃ vā yadi vā duḥkhaṃ dveṣyaṃ vā yadi vā priyam
|
yathāvat sarvamācakṣva śrutvā jñāsyāmi yat kṣamam ||19||

20ahameva hi te kṛṣṇe viśvāsyaḥ sarvakarmasu
|
ahamāpatsu cāpi tvāṃ mokṣayāmi punaḥ punaḥ ||20||

21śīghramuktvā yathākāmaṃ yatte kāryaṃ vivakṣitam
|
gaccha vai śayanāyaiva purā nānyena budhyate ||21||
