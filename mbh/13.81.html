Mahābhārata 13.81 (Anuśāsanaparvan)

1yudhiṣṭhira uvāca|
pavitrāṇāṃ pavitraṃ yacchiṣṭaṃ loke ca yadbhavet
|
pāvanaṃ paramaṃ caiva tanme brūhi pitāmaha ||1||

2bhaṣmi uvāca|
gāvo mahārthāḥ puṇyāśca tārayanti ca mānavān
|
dhārayanti prajāścemā haviṣā payasā tathā ||2||

3na hi puṇyatamaṃ kiñcidgobhyo bharatasattama
|
etāḥ puṇyāḥ pavitrāśca triṣu lokeṣu sattamāḥ ||3||

4devānāmupariṣṭācca gāvaḥ prativasanti vai
|
datvā caitāstārayate yānti svargaṃ manīṣiṇaḥ ||4||

5māṃdhātā yauvanāśvaśca yayātirnahuṣastathā
|
gā vai dadantaḥ satataṃ sahasraśatasammitāḥ ||5||

6gatāḥ paramakaṃ sthānaṃ devairapi sudurlabham
|
api cātra purāgītāṃ kathayiṣyāmi te'nagha ||6||

7ṛṣīṇāmuttamaṃ dhīmānkṛṣṇadvaipāyanaṃ śukaḥ
|
abhivādyāhnikakṛtaḥ śuciḥ prayatamānasaḥ||7||

8pitaraṃ paripapraccha dṛṣṭalokaparāvaram
|
ko yajñaḥ sarvayajñānāṃ variṣṭho'bhyupalakṣyate ||8||

9kiṃ ca kṛtvā paraṃ sthānaṃ prāpnuvanti manīṣiṇaḥ
|
kena devāḥ pavitreṇa svargamaśnanti vā vibho ||9||

10kiṃ ca yajñasya yajñatvaṃ kva ca yajñaḥ pratiṣṭhitaḥ
|
devānāmuttamaṃ kiṃ ca kiṃ ca satramitaḥ param ||10||

11pavitrāṇāṃ pavitraṃ ca yattadbrūhi pitarmama
|
etacchrutvā tu vacanaṃ vyāsaḥ paramadharmavit
|
putrāyākathayatsarvaṃ tattvena bharatarṣabha ||11||

12vyāsa uvāca|
gāvaḥ pratiṣṭhā bhūtānāṃ tathā gāvaḥ parāyaṇam
|
gāvaḥ puṇyāḥ pavitrāśca godhanaṃ pāvanaṃ tathā ||12||

13pūrvamāsannaśṛṅgā vai gāva ityanuśuśruma
|
śṛṅgārthe samupāsanta tāḥ kila prabhumavyayam ||13||

14tato brahmā tu gāḥ prāyamupaviṣṭāḥ samīkṣya ha
|
īpsitaṃ pradadau tābhyo gobhyaḥ pratyekaśaḥ prabhuḥ||14||

15tāsāṃ śṛṅgāṇyajāyanta yasyā yādṛṅmanogatam
|
nānāvarṇāḥ śṛṅgavantyastā vyarocanta putraka ||15||

16brahmaṇā varadattāstā havyakavyapradāḥ śubhāḥ
|
puṇyāḥ pavitrāḥ subhagā divyasaṃsthānalakṣaṇāḥ||16||

17gāvastejo mahaddivyaṃ gavāṃ dānaṃ praśasyate
|
ye caitāḥ samprayacchanti sādhavo vītamatsarāḥ ||17||

18te vai sukṛtinaḥ proktāḥ sarvadānapradāśca te
|
gavāṃ lokaṃ tathā puṇyamāpnuvanti ca te'nagha ||18||

19yatra vṛkṣā madhuphalā divyapuṣpaphalopagāḥ
|
puṣpāṇi ca sugandhīni divyāni dvijasattama ||19||

20sarvā maṇimayī bhūmiḥ sarvakāñcanavālukāḥ
|
sarvartusukhasaṃsparśā nippaṅkā nirajāḥ śubhāḥ ||20||

21raktotpalavanaiścaiva maṇikhaṇḍairhiraṇmayaiḥ
|
taruṇādityasaṅkāśairbhānti tatra jalāśayāḥ ||21||

22mahārhamaṇipatraiśca kāñcanaprabhakesaraiḥ
|
nīlotpalavimiśraiśca sarobhirbahupaṅkajaiḥ ||22||

23karavīravanaiḥ phullaiḥ sahasrāvartasaṃvṛtaiḥ
|
santānakavanaiḥ phullaivṛkṣaiśca samalaṅkṛtāḥ ||23||

24nirmalābhiśca muktābhirmaṇibhiśca mahāprabhaiḥ
|
udbhutapulināstatra jātarūpaiśca nimnagāḥ ||24||

25sarvaratnamayaiścitrairavagāḍhā drumottamaiḥ
|
jātarūpamayaiścānyairhutāśanasamaprabhaiḥ ||25||

26sauvarṇā girayastatra maṇiratnaśiloccayāḥ
|
sarvaratnamayairbhānti śṛṃgaiścārubhirucchrritaiḥ ||26||

27nityapuṣpaphalāstatra nagāḥ patrarathākulāḥ
|
divyagandharasaiḥ puṣpaiḥ phalaiśca bharatarṣama ||27||

28ramante puṇyakarmāṇastatra nityaṃ yudhiṣṭhira
|
sarvakāmasamṛddhārthā niḥśokā gatamanyavaḥ ||28||

29vimāneṣu vicitreṣu ramaṇīyeṣu bhārata
|
modante puṇyakarmāṇo viharanto yaśasvinaḥ ||29||

30upakrīḍanti tān rājan śubhāścāpsarasāṃ gaṇāḥ
|
etānlokānavāpnoti gāṃ datvā vai yudhiṣṭhira ||30||

31yeṣāmadhipatiḥ pūṣā māruto balavānbalī
|
aiśvarye varuṇo rājā nāmamātraṃ yugandharaḥ ||31||

32surūpā bahurūpāśca viśvarūpāśca mātaraḥ
|
prājāpatyamiti brahman japennityaṃ yatavrataḥ ||32||

33gāśca śuśrūṣate yaśca samanveti ca sarvaśaḥ
|
tasmai tuṣṭāḥ prayacchanti varānapi sudurlabhān ||33||

34druhyenna manasā vāpi goṣu nityaṃ sukhapradaḥ
|
arcayeta sadā caiva namaskāraiśca pūjayet ||34||

35dāntaḥ prītamanā nityaṃ gavāṃ vyuṣṭiṃ tathā'śnute
|
tryahamuṣṇaṃ pibenmūtraṃ tryahamuṣṇaṃ pibetpayaḥ ||35||

36gavāmuṣṇaṃ payaḥ pītvā tryahamuṣṇaṃ ghṛtaṃ pibet
|
tryahamuṣṇaṃ ghṛtaṃ pītvā vāyubhakṣo bhavet tryaham ||36||

37yena devāḥ pavitreṇa bhuñjate lokamuttamam
|
yatpavitraṃ pavitrāṇāṃ tadghṛtaṃ śirasā vahet ||37||

38ghṛtena juhuyādagniṃ ghṛtena svasti vācayet
|
ghṛtaṃ prāśedghṛtaṃ dadyādgavāṃ puṣṭiṃ tathā'śnute ||38||

39nirhataiśca yavairgobhirmāsaṃ praśritayāvakaḥ
|
brahmahatyāsamaṃ pāpaṃ sarvametena śudhyate ||39||

40parābhavācca daityānāṃ devaiḥ śaucamidaṃ kṛtam
|
te devatvamapi prāptāḥ saṃsiddhāśca mahābalāḥ ||40||

41gāvaḥ pavitrāḥ puṇyāśca pāvanaṃ paramaṃ mahat
|
tāśca dattvā dvijātibhyo naraḥ svargamupāśnute ||41||

42gavāṃ madhye śucirbhūtvā gomatīṃ manasā japet
|
pūtābhiradbhirācamya śucirbhavati nirmalaḥ ||42||

43agnimadhye gavāṃ madhye brāhmaṇānāṃ ca saṃsadi
|
vidyāvedavratasnātā brāhmaṇāḥ puṇyakarmiṇaḥ ||43||

44adhyāpayeran śiṣyānvai gomatīṃ yajñasaṃmitām
|
trirātropoṣito bhūtvā gomatīṃ labhate varam ||44||

45putrakāmaśca labhate putraṃ dhanamathāpi vā
|
patikāmā ca bhartāraṃ sarvakāmāṃśca mānavaḥ ||45||

46gāvastuṣṭāḥ prayacchanti sevitā vai na saṃśayaḥ
|
evametā mahābhāgā yajñiyāḥ sarvakāmadāḥ ||46||

47rohiṇya iti jānīhi naitābhyo vidyate param
|
ityuktaḥ sa mahātejāḥ śukaḥ pitrā mahātmanā
|
pūjayāmāsa gāṃ nityaṃ tasmāttvamapi pūjaya ||47||
