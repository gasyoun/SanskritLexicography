Mahābhārata 7.107 (Droṇaparvan)

1sañjaya uvāca|
bṛhatkṣatramathāyāntaṃ kaikeyaṃ dṛḍhavikramam
|
kṣemadhūrtirmahārāja vivyādhorasi mārgaṇaiḥ ||1||

2bṛhatkṣatrastu taṃ rājā navatyā nataparvaṇām
|
ājaghne tvarito rājan droṇānīkabibhitsayā ||2||

3kṣemadhūrtistu saṃkruddhaḥ kaikeyasya mahātmanaḥ
|
dhanuściccheda bhallena pītena niśitena ha ||3||

4athainaṃ chinnadhanvānaṃ śareṇānataparvaṇā
|
vivyādha samare tūrṇaṃ pravaraṃ sarvadhanvinām ||4||

5athānyaddhanurādāya bṛhatkṣatro hasanniva
|
vyaśvasūtarathaṃ cakre kṣemadhūrtiṃ mahāratham ||5||

6tato'pareṇa bhallena pītena niśitena ca
|
jahāra nṛpateḥ kāyācchiro jvalitakuṇḍalam ||6||

7tacchinnaṃ sahasā tasya śiraḥ kuñcitamūrdhajam
|
sakirīṭaṃ mahīṃ prāpya babhau jyotirivāmbarāt ||7||

8taṃ nihatya raṇe dṛṣṭo bṛhatkṣatro mahārathaḥ
|
sahasā'bhyapatat sainyaṃ tāvakaṃ pārthakāraṇāt ||8||

9dhṛṣṭaketuṃ tathā''yāntaṃ droṇahetoḥ parākramī
|
vīradhanvā maheṣvāso vārayāmāsa bhārata ||9||

10tau parasparamāsādya śaradaṃṣṭrau tarasvinau
|
śarairanekasāhasrairanyonyamabhijaghnatuḥ ||10||

11tāvubhau naraśārdūlau yuyudhāte parasparam
|
mahāvane tīvramadau vāraṇāviva yūthapau ||11||

12girigahvaramāsādya śārdūlāviva roṣitau
|
yuyudhāte mahāvīryau parasparajighāṃsayā ||12||

13tadyuddhamāsīttumulaṃ prekṣaṇīyaṃ viśāṃpate
|
siddhacāraṇasaṃghānāṃ vismayādbhutadarśanam ||13||

14vīradhanvā tataḥ kuddho dhṛṣṭaketoḥ śarāsanam
|
dvidhā ciccheda bhallena prahasanniva bhārata ||14||

15tadutsṛjya dhanuśchinnaṃ cedirājo mahārathaḥ
|
śaktiṃ jagrāha vipulāṃ hemadaṇḍāmayasmayīm ||15||

16tāṃ tu śaktiṃ mahāvīryāṃ dorbhyāmāyamya bhārata
|
cikṣepa sahasā yatto vīradhanvarathaṃ prati ||16||

17tayā tu vīraghātinyā śaktyā tvabhihato bhṛśaṃ
|
nirbhinnahṛdayastūrṇaṃ nipapāta rathānmahīm ||17||

18tasminvinihate vīre traigartānāṃ mahārathe
|
balaṃ te'bhajyata vibho pāṇḍaveyaiḥ samantataḥ ||18||

19sahadeve tataḥ ṣaṣṭiṃ sāyakān durmukho'kṣipat
|
nanāda va mahānādaṃ tarjayan pāṇḍavaṃ raṇe ||19||

20mādreyastu tataḥ kuddho durmukhaṃ ca śitaiḥ śaraiḥ
|
bhrātā bhrātaramāyāntaṃ vivyādha prahasanniva ||20||

21taṃ raṇe rabhasaṃ dṛṣṭvā sahadevaṃ mahābalam
|
durmukho navabhirbāṇaistāḍayāmāsa bhārata ||21||

22durmukhasya tu bhallena chittvā ketuṃ mahābalaḥ
|
jaghāna caturo bāhāṃścaturbhirniśitaiḥ śaraiḥ ||22||

23athāpareṇa bhallena pītena niśitena ha
|
ciccheda sāratheḥ kāyācchiro jvalitakuṃḍalam ||23||

24kṣurapreṇa ca tīkṣṇena kauravyasya mahaddhanuḥ
|
sahadevo raṇe chittvā taṃ ca vivyādha pañcabhiḥ ||24||

25hatāśvaṃ tu rathaṃ tyaktvā durmukho vimanāstadā
|
āruroha rathaṃ rājanniramitrasya bhārata ||25||

26sahadevastataḥ kruddho niramitraṃ mahāhave
|
jaghāna pūtanāmadhye bhallena paravīrahā ||26||

27sa papāta rathopasthānniramitro janeśvaraḥ
|
trigartarājasya suto vyathayaṃstava vāhinīm ||27||

28taṃ tu hatvā mahābāhuḥ sahadevo vyarocata
|
yathā dāśarathī rāmaḥ kharaṃ hatvā mahābalam ||28||

29hāhākāro mahānāsīttrigartānāṃ janeśvara
|
rājaputraṃ hataṃ dṛṣṭvā niramitraṃ mahāratham ||29||

30nakulaste sutaṃ rājan vikarṇaṃ pṛthulocanam
|
muhūrtājjitavān loke tada‌dbhutamivābhavat ||30||

31sātyakiṃ vyāghrada‌ttastu śaraiḥ sannataparvabhiḥ
|
cakre'dṛśyaṃ sāśvasūtaṃ sadhvajaṃ pṛtanāntare ||31||

32tānnivārya śarān śūraḥ śaineyaḥ kṛtahastavat
|
sāśvasūtadhvajaṃ bāṇairvyāghradattamapātayat ||32||

33kumāre nihate tasmin māgadhasya sute prabho
|
māgadhāḥ sarvato yattā yuyudhānamupādravan ||33||

34visṛjantaḥ śarāṃścaiva tomarāṃśca sahasraśaḥ
|
bhindipālāṃstathā prāsān mudgarānmusalānapi ||34||

35ayodhayan raṇe śūrāḥ sātvataṃ yuddhadurmadam
|
tāṃstu sarvān sa balavān sātyakiryuddhadurmadaḥ ||35||

36nātikṛcchrāddhasanneva vijigye puruṣarṣabhaḥ
|
māgadhān dravato dṛṣṭvā hataśeṣān samantataḥ ||36||

37balaṃ te'bhajyata vibho yuyudhānaśarārditam
|
nāśayitvā raṇe sainyaṃ tvadīyaṃ mādhavottamaḥ ||37||

38vidhunvāno dhanuḥśreṣṭhaṃ vyabhrājata mahāyaśāḥ
|
bhajyamānaṃ balaṃ rājan sātvatena mahātmanā ||38||

39nābhyavartata yuddhāya trāsitaṃ dīrghavāhunā
|
tato droṇo bhṛśaṃ kruddhaḥ sahasodvṛtya cakṣuṣī
|
sātyakiṃ satyakarmāṇaṃ svayamevābhidudruve ||39||
