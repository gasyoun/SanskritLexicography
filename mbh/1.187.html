Mahābhārata 1.187 (Ādiparvan)

1vaiśaṃpāyana uvāca|
te'laṅkṛtāḥ kuṇḍalino yuvānaḥ parasparaṃ spardhamānā narendrāḥ
|
astraṃ balaṃ cātmani manyamānāḥsarveṃ samutpeturudāyudhāste||1||

2rūpeṇa vīryeṇa kule caiva śīlena vittena ca yauvanena
|
samiddhadarpā madavegabhinnā mattā yathā haimavatā gajendrāḥ||2||

3parasparaṃ spardhayā prekṣamāṇāḥ saṅkalpajenābhipariplutāṅgāḥ
|
kṛṣṇā mamaivetyabhibhāṣamāṇā nṛpāsanebhyaḥ sahasodatiṣṭhan ||3||

4te kṣatriyā raṅgagatāḥ sametā jigīṣamāṇā drupadātmajāṃ tām
|
cakāśire parvatarājakanyāmumāṃ yathā devagaṇāḥ sametāḥ||4||

5kandarpabāṇābhinipīḍitāṅgāḥ kṛṣṇāgataiste hṛdayairnarendrāḥ
|
raṅgāvatīrṇā drupadātmajārthaṃ dveṣaṃ pracakruḥ suhṛdo'pi tatra||5||

6athāyayurdevagaṇā vimānai rudrādityā vasavo'thāśvinau ca
|
sādhyāśca sarve marutastathaiva yamaṃ puraskṛtya dhaneśvaraṃ ca||6||

7daityāḥ suparṇāśca mahoragāśca devarṣayo guhyakāścāraṇāśca
|
viśvāvasurnāradaparvatau ca gandharvamukhyāḥ sahasāpsarobhiḥ||7||

8halāyudhastatra janārdanaśca vṛṣṇyandhakāścaiva yatāpradhānam
|
prekṣāṃ sma cakruryadupuṅgavāste sthitāśca kṛṣṇasya mate mahāntaḥ||8||

9dṛṣṭvā tu tānmattagajendrarūpān pañcābhipadmāniva vāraṇendrān
|
bhasmāvṛtāṅgāniva havyavāhān kṛṣṇaḥ pradadhyau yaduvīramukhyaḥ||9||

10śaśaṃsa rāmāya yudhiṣṭhiraṃ sa bhīmaṃ sajiṣṇuṃ ca yamau ca vīrau
|
śanaiḥśanaistānprasamīkṣya rāmo janārdanaṃ prītamanā dadarśa ha||10||

11anye tu vīrā nṛpaputrapautrāḥ kṛṣṇāgatairnetramanaḥsvabhāvaiḥ
|
vyāyacchamānā dadṛśurna tānvai sandaṣṭadantacchadatāmranetrāḥ||11||

12tathaiva pārthāḥ pṛthubāhavaste vīrau yamau caiva mahānubhāvau
|
tāṃ draupadīṃ prekṣya tadā sma sarve kandarpabāṇābhihatā babhūvuḥ||12||

13devarṣigandharvasamākulaṃ tat suparṇanāgāsurasiddhajuṣṭam
|
divyena gandhena samākulaṃ ca divyaiśca puṣpairavakīryamāṇam||13||

14mahāsvanairdundubhināditaiśca babhūva tatsaṅkulamantarikṣam
|
vimānasaṃbādhamabhūtsamantāt saveṇuvīṇāpaṇavānunādam||14||

15tatastu te rājagaṇāḥ krameṇa kṛṣṇānimittaṃ kṛtavikramāśca
|
sakarṇaduryodhanaśālvaśalyadrauṇāyanikrāthasunīthavaktrāḥ||15||

16kaliṅgavaṅgādhipapāṇḍyapauṇḍrā videharājo yavanādhipaśca
|
anye ca nānānṛpaputrapautrā rāṣṭrādhipāḥ paṅkajapatranetrāḥ||16||

17kirīṭahārāṅgadacakravālairvibhūṣitāṅgāḥ pṛthubāhavaste
|
anukramaṃ vikramasatvayuktā balena vīryeṇa ca nardamānāḥ||17||

18tatkārmukaṃ saṃhananopapannaṃ sajyaṃ na śekurmanasā'pi kartum
|
te vikramantaḥ sphuratā dṛḍhena vikṣipyamāṇā dhanuṣā narendrāḥ||18||

19viceṣṭamānā dharaṇītalasthā yathābalaṃ śaikṣyaguṇakramāśca
|
gataujasaḥ snastakirīṭahārā viniḥśvasantaḥ śamayāṃbabhūvuḥ||19||

20hahākṛtaṃ taddhanuṣā dṛḍhena visrastahārāṅgadacakravālam
|
kṛṣṇānimittaṃ vinivṛttakāmaṃ rājñāṃ tadā maṇḍalamārtamāsīt||20||

21sarvānnṛpāṃstānprasamīkṣya karṇo dhanurdharāṇāṃ pravaro jagāma
|
uddhṛtya tūrṇaṃ dhanurudyataṃ tat sajyaṃ cakārāśu yuyoja bāṇān||21||

22dṛṣṭvā sūtaṃ menire pāṇḍuputrā bhitvā nītaṃ lakṣyavaraṃ dharāyām
|
dhanurdharā rāgakṛtapratijñamatyagnisomārkamathārkaputram||22||

23dṛṣṭvā tu taṃ draupadī vākyamuccairjagāda nāhaṃ varayāmi sūtam
|
sāmarṣahāsaṃ prasamusya sūryaṃ tatyāja karṇaḥ sphuritaṃ dhanustat||23||

24evaṃ teṣu nivṛtteṣu kṣatriyeṣu samantataḥ
|
cedīnāmadhipo vīro balavānantakopamaḥ||24||

25damaghoṣasuto dhīraḥ śiśupālo mahāmatiḥ
|
dhanurādāyamānastu jānubhyāmagamanmahīm||25||

26tato rājā mahāvīryo jarāsandho mahābalaḥ
|
dhanuṣo'bhyāśamāgatya tasthau giririvācalaḥ||26||

27dhanuṣā pīḍyamānastu jānubhyāmagamanmahīm
|
tata utthāya rājā sa svarāṣṭraṇyabhijagmivān||27||

28tataḥ śalyo mahāvīro madrarājo mahābalaḥ
|
tadapyāropyamāṇastu jānubhyāmagamanmahīm||28||

29tasmiṃstu saṃbhrāntajane samāje nikṣiptavādeṣu janādhipeṣu
|
kuntīsuto jiṣṇuriyeṣa kartuṃ sajyaṃ dhanustatsaśaraṃ pravīraḥ||29||
