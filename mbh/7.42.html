Mahābhārata 7.42 (Droṇaparvan)

1dhṛtarāṣṭra uvāca|
bālamatyantasukhinaṃ svabāhubala‌rpitam
|
yuddheṣu kuśalaṃ vīraṃ kulaputraṃ tanutyajam||1||

2gāha‌mānamanīkāni sadaśvaiśca trihāyakaiḥ
|
api yaudhiṣṭhirāt sainyāt kaścidanvapatadbalī||2||

3sañjaya uvāca|
yudhiṣṭhiro bhīmasenaḥ śikhaṇḍī sātyakiryamau
|
dhṛṣṭadyumno virāṭaśca drupadaśca sakekayaḥ||3||

4dhṛṣṭhaketuśca saṃrabdho mātsyāścābhyapatan raṇe
|
tenaiva tu pathā yāntaḥ pitaro mātulaiḥ saha||4||

5abhyadravan parīpsanto vyūḍhānīkāḥ prahāriṇaḥ
|
tān dṛṣṭvā dravataḥ śūrāṃstvadīyā vimukhābhavan||5||

6tatastadvimukhaṃ dṛṣṭvā tava sūnormahadbalam
|
jāmātā tava tejasvī saṃstaṃbhayiṣurādravat||6||

7saindhavasya mahārāja putro rājā jayadrathaḥ
|
sa putragṛddhinaḥ pārthān saha sainyānavārayat||7||

8ugradhanvā maheṣvāso divyamastramudīrayan
|
vārdhakṣatrirupāsedhat pravaṇādiva kuñjaraḥ||8||

9dhṛtarāṣṭra uvāca|
atibhāramahaṃ manye saindhave sañjayāhitam
|
yadekaḥ pāṇḍavān kruddhān putraprepsūnavārayat||9||

10atyadbhutamahaṃ manye balaṃ śauryaṃ ca saindhave
|
tasya prabrūhi me vīryaṃ karma cāgryaṃ mahātmanaḥ||10||

11kiṃ dattaṃ hutamiṣṭaṃ vā kiṃ sutaptamatho tapaḥ
|
sindhurājo hi yenaikaḥ pāṇḍavān samavārayat||11||

12sañjaya uvāca|
draupadīharaṇe yattadbhīmasanena nirjitaḥ
|
mānāt sa taptavān rājā varārthī sumahat tapaḥ||12||

13indriyāṇīndriyārthebhyaḥ priyebhyaḥ sannivartya saḥ
|
kṣutpipāsātapasahaḥ kṛśo dhamanisantataḥ||13||

14devamārādhayaccharvaṃ gṛṇan brahma sanātanam
|
bhaktānukampī bhagavāṃstasya cakre tato dayām||14||

15svapnānte'pyatha caivāha haraḥ sindhupateḥ sutam
|
varaṃ vṛṇīṣva prīto'smi jayadratha kimicchasi||15||

16evamuktastu śarveṇa sindhurājo jayadrathaḥ
|
uvāca praṇato rudraṃ prāñjalirniyatātmavān||16||

17pāṇḍaveyānahaṃ saṃkhye bhīmavīryaparākramān
|
vārayeyaṃ rathenaikaḥ samastāniti bhārata||17||

18evamuktastu deveśo jayadrathamathābravīt
|
dadāmi te varaṃ saumya vinā pārthaṃ dhanañjayam||18||

19vārayiṣyasi saṃgrāme caturaḥ pāṇḍunandanān
|
evamastviti deveśamuktvā'buddhyata pārthivaḥ||19||

20sa tena varadānena divyenāstrabalena ca
|
ekaḥ saṃvārayāmāsa pāṇḍavānāmanīkinīm||20||

21tasya jyātalaghoṣeṇa kṣatriyān bhayamāviśat
|
parāṃstu tava sainyasya harṣaḥ paramako'bhavat||21||

22dṛṣṭvā tu kṣatriyā bhāraṃ saindhave sarvamāhitam
|
utkruśyābhyadravan rājanyena yaudhiṣṭhiraṃ balam||22||
