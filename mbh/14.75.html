Mahābhārata 14.75 (Āśvamedhikaparvan)

1vaiśampāyana uvāca|
prāgjyotiṣamathābhyetya vyacaratsa hayottamaḥ
|
bhagadattātmajastatra niryayau raṇakarkaśaḥ||1||

2sa hayaṃ pāṇḍuputrasya viṣayāntamupāgatam
|
yuyudhe bharataśreṣṭha vajradatto mahīpatiḥ||2||

3so'bhiniryāya nagarādbhagadattasuto nṛpaḥ
|
aśvamāyāntamunmathya nagarābhimukho yayau||3||

4tamālakṣya mahābāhuḥ kurūṇāmṛṣabhastadā
|
gāṇḍīvaṃ vikṣipaṃstūrṇaṃ sahasā samupādravat||4||

5tato gāṇḍīvanirmuktairiṣubhirmohito nṛpaḥ
|
hayamutsṛjya taṃ vīrastataḥ pārthamupādravat||5||

6punaḥ praviśya nagaraṃ daṃśitaḥ sa nṛpottamaḥ
|
āruhya nāgapravaraṃ niryayau raṇakarkaśaḥ||6||

7pāṇḍureṇātapatreṇa dhriyamāṇena mūrdhani
|
dodhūyatā cāmareṇa śvetena ca mahārathaḥ||7||

8tataḥ pārthaṃ samāsādya pāṇḍavānāṃ mahāratham
|
āhvayāmāsa bībhatsuṃ bālyānmohācca saṃyuge||8||

9sa vāraṇaṃ nagaprakhyaṃ prabhinnakaraṭāmukham
|
preṣayāmāsa saṅkruddhaḥ śvetāśvaṃ prati pārthivaḥ||9||

10vikṣarantaṃ mahāmedhaṃ paravāraṇavāraṇam
|
śāstravatkalpitaṃ saṅkhye vivaśaṃ yuddhadurmadam||10||

11pracodyamānaḥ sa gajastena rājñā mahābalaḥ
|
tadāṅkaḍuśena vibabhāvutpatiṣyannivāmbaram||11||

12tamāpatantaṃ samprekṣya kruddho rājan dhanañjayaḥ
|
bhūmiṣṭho vāraṇagataṃ yodhayāmāsa bhārata||12||

13vajradattastataḥ kruddho mumocāśu dhanañjaye
|
tomarānagnisaṅkāśān śalabhāniva vegitān||13||

14arjunastānasamprāptān gāṇḍīvaprabhavaiḥ śaraiḥ
|
dvidhā tridhā ca ciccheda kha eva khagabhaistadā||14||

15sa tān dṛṣṭvā tathā chinnāṃstomarānbhagadattajaḥ
|
iṣūnasaktāṃstvaritaḥ prāhiṇotpāṇḍavaṃ prati||15||

16tato'rjunastūrṇataraṃ rukmapuṅkhānajihmagān
|
preṣyāmāsa saṅkruddho bhagadattātmajaṃ prati||16||

17sa tairviddho mahātejā vajradatto mahāmṛdhe
|
bhṛśāhataḥ papātorvyāṃ na tvenamajahātsmṛtiḥ||17||

18tataḥ sa punarutsṛjya vāraṇapravaraṃ raṇe
|
avyagraḥ preṣayāmāsa jayārthī vijayaṃ prati||18||

19tasmai bāṇāṃstato jiṣṇurnirmuktāśīviṣopamān
|
preṣayāmāsa saṅkruddho jvalitajvalanopamān||19||

20sa tairviddho mahānāgo visravan rudhiraṃ babhau
|
gaurikāktamivāmbho'drirbahuprasravaṇaṃ tadā||20||
