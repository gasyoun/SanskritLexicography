Mahābhārata 2.4 (Sabhāparvan)

1vaiśampāyana uvāca|
tataḥ praveśanaṃ tasyāṃ cakre rājā yudhiṣṭhiraḥ
|
ayutaṃ bhojayitvā tu brāhmaṇānāṃ narādhipaḥ||1||

2sājyena pāyasenaiva madhunā miśritena ca
|
bhakṣyairmūlaiḥ phalaiścaiva māṃsairvārāhahāriṇaiḥ
|
kṛsareṇātha jīvantyā haviṣyeṇa ca sarvaśaḥ||2||

3māṃsaprakārairvividhaiḥ khādyaiścāpi tathā nṛpa
|
coṣyaiśca vividhai rājanpeyaiśca bahuvistaraiḥ||3||

4ahataiścaiva vāsobhirmālyairuccāvacairapi
|
tarpayāmāsa viprendrānnānādigbhyaḥ samāgatān||4||

5dadau tebhyaḥ sahasrāṇi gavāṃ pratyekaśaḥ punaḥ
|
puṇyāhaghoṣastatrāsīddivaspṛgiva bhārata||5||

6vāditrairvividhairdivyairgandhairuccāvacairapi
|
pūjayitvā kuruśreṣṭho devatāni niveśya ca||6||

7tatra mallā naṭā jhallāḥ sūtā vaitālikāstathā
|
upatasthurmahātmānaṃ dharmaputraṃ yudhiṣṭhiram||7||

8tathā sa kṛtvā pūjāṃ tāṃ bhrātṛbhiḥ saha pāṇḍavaḥ
|
tasyāṃ sabhāyāṃ ramyāyāṃ reme śakro yathā divi||8||

9sabhāyāmṛṣayastasyāṃ pāṇḍavaiḥ saha āsate
|
āsāñcakrurnarendrāśca nānādeśasamāgatāḥ||9||

10asito devalaḥ satyaḥ sarpirmālī mahāśirāḥ
|
arvāvasuḥ sumitraśca maitreyaḥ śunako baliḥ||10||

11bako dālbhyaḥ sthūlaśirāḥ kṛṣṇadvaipāyanaḥ śukaḥ
|
sumanturjaiminiḥ pailo vyāsaśiṣyāstathā vayam ||11||

12tittiriryājñavalkyaśca sasuto romaharṣaṇaḥ
|
apsuhomyaśca dhaumyaśca aṇīmāṇḍavyakauśikau||12||

13dāmoṣṇīṣastraibaliśca parṇādo ghaṭajānukaḥ
|
mauñjāyano vāyubhakṣaḥ pārāśaryaśca sārikaḥ||13||

14balivākaḥ sinīvākaḥ saptapālaḥ kṛtaśramaḥ
|
jātūkarṇaḥ śikhāvāṃśca ālambaḥ pārijātakaḥ||14||

15parvataśca mahābhāgo mārkaṇḍeyo mahāmuniḥ
|
pavitrapāṇiḥ sāvarṇo bhālukirgālavastathā||15||

16jaṅghābandhuśca raibhyaśca kopavegastathā bhṛguḥ
|
haribabhruśca kauṇḍinyo babhrumālī sanātanaḥ||16||

17kākṣīvānauśijaścaiva nāciketotha gautamaḥ
|
paiṅgyo varāhaḥ śunakaḥ śāṇḍilyaśca mahātapāḥ||17||

18kukkuro veṇujaṅghotha kālāpaḥ kaṭha eva ca
|
munayo dharmavidvāṃso dhṛtātmāno jitendriyāḥ||18||

19ete cānye ca bahavo vedavedāṅgapāragāḥ
|
upāsate mahātmānaṃ sabhāyāmṛṣisattamāḥ||19||

20kathayantaḥ kathāḥ puṇyā dharmajñāḥ śucayo'malāḥ
|
tathaiva kṣatriyaśreṣṭhā dharmarājamupāsate||20||

21śrīmānmahātmā dharmātmā muñjaketurvivardhanaḥ
|
saṅgrāmajiddurmukhaśca ugrasenaśca vīryavān||21||

22kakṣasenaḥ kṣitipatiḥ kṣemakaścāparājitaḥ
|
kambojarājaḥ kamaṭhaḥ kampanaśca mahābalaḥ||22||

23satataṃ kampayāmāsa yavanāneka eva yaḥ
|
balapauruṣasampannānkṛtāstrāmamitaujasaḥ
|
yathā'surānkālakeyāndevo vajradharastathā||23||

24jaṭāsuro madrakānāṃ ca rājā kuntiḥ pulindaśca kirātarājaḥ
|
tathāṅgavāṅgau saha puṇḍrakeṇa pāṇḍyoḍrarājau ca sahāndhrakeṇa ||24||

25aṅgo vaṅgaḥ sumitraśca śaibyaścāmitrakarśanaḥ
|
kirātarājaḥ sumanā yavanādhipatistathā||25||

26cāṇūro devarātaśca bhojo bhīmarathaśca yaḥ
|
śrutāyudhaśca kāliṅgo jayasenaśca māgadhaḥ||26||

27sukarmā cekitānaśca puruścāmitrakarśanaḥ
|
ketumānvasudānaśca vaideho'tha kṛtakṣaṇaḥ||27||

28sudharmā cāniruddhaśca śrutāyuśca mahābalaḥ
|
anūparājo durdharṣaḥ kramajicca sudarśanaḥ||28||

29śiśupālaḥ sahasutaḥ karūṣādhipatistathā
|
vṛṣṇīnāṃ caiva durdharṣāḥ kumārā devarūpiṇaḥ||29||

30āhuko vipṛthuścaiva gadaḥ sāraṇa eva ca
|
akrūraḥ kṛtavarmā ca satyakaśca śineḥ sutaḥ||30||

31bhīṣmakothākṛtiścaiva dyumatsenaśca vīryavān
|
kekayāśca maheṣvāsā yajñasenaśca somakiḥ||31||

32ketumānvasumāṃścaiva kṛtāstraśca mahābalaḥ
|
ete cānye ca bahavaḥ kṣatriyā mukhyasaṃmatāḥ||32||

33upāsate sabhāyāṃ sma kuntīputraṃ yudhiṣṭhiram
|
arjunaṃ ye va saṃśritya rājaputrā mahābalāḥ||33||

34aśikṣanta dhanurvedaṃ rauravājinavāsasaḥ
|
tatraiva śikṣitā rājankumārā vṛṣṇinandanāḥ||34||

35raukmiṇeyaśca sāmbaśca yuyudhānaśca sātyakiḥ
|
sudharmā cāniruddhaśca śaibyaśca narapuṅgavaḥ||35||

36ete cānye ca bahavo rājānaḥ pṛthivīpate
|
dhanañjayasakhā cātra nityamāste sma tumburuḥ||36||

37upāsate mahātmānamāsīnaṃ saptaviṃśatiḥ
|
citrasenaḥ sahāmātyo gandharvāpsarasastathā||37||

38gītavāditrakuśalāḥ sāmyatālaviśāradāḥ
|
pramāṇo'tha laye sthāne kinnarāḥ kṛtaniśramāḥ||38||

39sañcoditāstumburuṇā gandharvasahitāstadā
|
gāyanti divyatānaiste yathānyāyaṃ manasvinaḥ||39||

40pāṇḍuputrānṛṣīṃścaiva ramayanta upāsate
|
tasyāṃ sabhāyāmāsīnāḥ suvratāḥ satyasaṅgarāḥ||40||

41divīva devā brahmāṇaṃ yudhiṣṭhiramupāsate||41||
