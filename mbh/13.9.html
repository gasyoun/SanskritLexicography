Mahābhārata 13.9 (Anuśāsanaparvan)

1yudhiṣṭhira uvāca|
brāhmaṇānāṃ tu ye lokāḥ pratiśrutya pitāmaha
|
na prayacchanti mohātte ke bhavanti mahādyute ||1||

2etanme tattvato brūhi dharmaṃ dharmabhṛtāṃ vara
|
pratiśrutya durātmāno na prayacchanti ye narāḥ ||2||

3bhīṣma uvāca|
yo na dadyātpratiśrutya svalpaṃ vā yadi vā bahu
|
āśāstasya hatāḥ sarvāḥ klībasyeva prajāphalam ||3||

4yāṃ rātriṃ jāyate jīvo yāṃ rātriṃ ca vinaśyati
|
etasminnantare yadyat sukṛtaṃ tasya bhārata ||4||

5yacca tasya hutaṃ kiñciddattaṃ vā bharatarṣabha
|
tapastaptamatho vāpi sarvaṃ tasyopahanyate ||5||

6athaitadvacanaṃ prāhurdharmaśāstravido janāḥ
|
niśamya bharataśreṣṭha buddhyā paramayuktayā ||6||

7api codāharantīmaṃ dharmaśāstravido janāḥ
|
aśvānāṃ śyāmakarṇānāṃ sahasreṇa samucyate ||7||

8atraivodārahantīmamitihāsaṃ purātanam
|
śṛgālasya ca saṃvādaṃ vānarasya ca bhārata ||8||

9tau sakhāyau purā hyāstāṃ mānuṣatve parantapa
|
anyāṃ yoniṃ samāpannau śārgālīṃ vānarīṃ tathā ||9||

10tataḥ parāsūn khādantaṃ śṛgālaṃ vānaro'bravīt
|
śmaśānamadhye saṃprekṣya pūrvajātimanusmaran ||10||

11kiṃ tvayā pāpakaṃ pūrvaṃ kṛtaṃ karma sudāruṇam
|
yastvaṃ śmaśāne mṛtakān pūtikānatsi kutsitān ||11||

12evamuktaḥ pratyuvāca śṛgālo vānaraṃ tadā
|
brāhmaṇasya pratiśrutya na mayā tadupāhṛtam ||12||

13tatkṛte pāpakīṃ yonimāpanno'smi plavaṅgama
|
tasmādevaṃvidhaṃ bhakṣyaṃ bhakṣayāmi bubhukṣitaḥ ||13||

14bhīṣma uvāca|
śṛgālo vānaraṃ prāha punareva narottama
|
kiṃ tvayā pātakaṃ karma kṛtaṃ yenāsi vānaraḥ ||14||

15vānara uvāca|
sadā cāhaṃ phalāhāro brāhmaṇānāṃ plavaṅgamaḥ
|
tasmānna brāhmaṇasvaṃ tu hartavyaṃ viduṣā sadā
|
samaṃ vivādo moktavyo dātavyaṃ ca pratiśrutam ||15||

16bhīṣma uvāca|
ityetadbruvato rājanbrāhmaṇasya mayā śrutam
|
kathāṃ kathayataḥ puṇyāṃ dharmajñasya purātanīm ||16||

17śrutaścāpi mayā bhūyaḥ kṛṣṇasyāpi viśāmpate
|
kathāṃ kathayataḥ pūrvaṃ brāhmaṇaṃ prati pāṇḍava ||17||

18na hartavyaṃ vipradhanaṃ kṣantavyaṃ teṣu nityaśaḥ
|
bālāśca nāvamantavyā daridrāḥ kṛpaṇā api ||18||

19evameva ca māṃ nityaṃ brāhmaṇāḥ sandiśanti vai
|
pratiśrutya bhaveddeyaṃ nāśā kāryā dvijottame ||19||

20brāhmaṇo hyāśayā pūrvaṃ kṛtayā pṛthivīpate
|
susamiddho yathā dīptaḥ pāvakastadvidhaḥ smṛtaḥ ||20||

21yaṃ nirīkṣeta saṅkruddha āśayā pūrvajātayā
|
pradahecca hi taṃ rājan kakṣamakṣayyabhugyathā ||21||

22sa eva hi yadā tuṣṭo vacasā pratinandati
|
bhavatyagadasaṅkāśo viṣaye tasya bhārata ||22||

23putrān pautrān paśuṃścaiva bāndhavān sacivāṃstathā
|
puraṃ janapadaṃ caiva śāntiriṣṭena poṣayet ||23||

24etaddhi paramaṃ tejo brāhmaṇasyeha dṛśyate
|
sahasrakiraṇasyeva saviturdharaṇītale ||24||

25tasmāddātavyameveha pratiśrutya yudhiṣṭhira
|
yadīcchecchobhanāṃ jātiṃ prāptuṃ bharatasattama ||25||

26brāhmaṇasya hi dattena dhruvaṃ svargo hyanuttamaḥ
|
śakyaḥ prāptuṃ viśeṣeṇa dānaṃ hi mahatī kriyā ||26||

27ito dattena jīvanti devatāḥ pitarastathā
|
tasmāddānāni deyāni brāhmaṇebhyo vijānatā ||27||

28mahaddhi bharataśreṣṭha brāhmaṇastīrthamucyate
|
velāyāṃ na tu kasyāñcidgacchedvipro hyapūjitaḥ ||28||
