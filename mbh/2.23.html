Mahābhārata 2.23 (Sabhāparvan)

1vaiśampāyana uvāca|
tatastaṃ niścitātmānaṃ yuddhāya yadunandanaḥ
|
uvāca vāgmī rājānaṃ jarāsandhamadhokṣajaḥ||1||

2kṛṣṇa uvāca|
trayāṇāṃ kena te rājanyoddhumutsahate manaḥ
|
asmadanyatameneha sajjībhavatu ko yudhi||2||

3evamuktaḥ sa nṛpatiryuddhaṃ vavre mahādyutiḥ
|
jarāsandhastato rājā bhīmasenena māgadhaḥ||3||

4ādāya rocanāṃ mālyaṃ maṅgalyānyaparāṇi ca
|
dhārayannagadānmukhyānnirvṛtīrvedanāni ca
|
upatasthe jarāsandhaṃ yuyutsuṃ vai purohitaḥ||4||

5kṛtasvastyayano rājā brāhmaṇena yaśasvinā
|
samanahyajjarāsandhaḥ kṣātraṃ dharmamanusmaran||5||

6avamucya kirīṭaṃ sa keśānsamanugṛhya ca
|
udatiṣṭhajjarāsandho velātiga ivārṇavaḥ||6||

7uvāca matimānrājā bhīmaṃ bhīmaparākramaḥ
|
bhīma yotsye tvayā sārdhaṃ śreyasā nirjitaṃ varam||7||

8evamuktvā jarāsandho bhīmasenamarindamaḥ
|
pratyudyayau mahātejāḥ śakraṃ bala ivāsuraḥ||8||

9tataḥ saṃmantrya kṛṣṇena kṛtasvastyayano balī
|
bhīmaseno jarāsandhamāsasāda yuyutsayā||9||

10tatastau naraśārdūlau bāhuśastrau samīyatuḥ
|
vīrau paramasaṃhṛṣṭāvanyonyajayakāṅkṣiṇau||10||

11karagrahaṇapūrvaṃ tu kṛtvā pādābhivandanam
|
kakṣaiḥ kakṣāṃ vidhunvānāvāsphoṭaṃ tatra cakratuḥ||11||

12skandhe dorbhyāṃ samāhatya nihatya ca muhurmuhuḥ
|
aṅgamaṅgaiḥ samāśliṣya punarāsphālanaṃ vibhoḥ||12||

13citrahastādikaṃ kṛtvā kakṣābandhaṃ ca cakratuḥ
|
galagaṇḍābhighātena sasphuliṅgena cāśanim||13||

14bāhupāśādikaṃ kṛtvā pādāhataśirāvubhau
|
urohastaṃ tataścakre pūrṇakumbhau prayujya tau||14||

15karasampīḍanaṃ kṛtvā garjantau vāraṇāviva
|
nardantau meghasaṅkāśau bāhupraharaṇāvubhau||15||

16talenāhanyamānau tu anyonyaṃ kṛtavīkṣaṇau
|
siṃhāviva susaṃṅkruddhāvākṛṣyākṛṣya yudhyatām||16||

17aṅgenāṅgaṃ samāpīḍya bāhubhyāmubhayorapi
|
āvṛtya bāhubhiścāpi udaraṃ ca pracakratuḥ||17||

18ubhau kaṭyāṃ supārśve tu takṣavantau ca śikṣitau
|
adho hastaṃ svakaṇṭhe tūdarasyorasi cākṣipat||18||

19sarvātikrāntamaryādaṃ pṛṣṭhabhaṅgaṃ ca cakratuḥ
|
sampūrṇamūrcchāṃ bāhubhyāṃ pūrṇakumbhaṃ pracakratuḥ||19||

20tṛṇapīḍaṃ yathākāmaṃ pūrṇayogaṃ samuṣṭikam
|
evamādīni yuddhāni prakurvantau parasparam||20||

21tayoryuddhaṃ tato draṣṭuṃ sametāḥ puravāsinaḥ
|
brāhmaṇā vaṇijaścaiva kṣatriyāśca sahasraśaḥ||21||

22śūdrāśca naraśārdūla striyo vṛddhāśca sarvaśaḥ
|
nirantaramabhūttatra janaughairabhisaṃvṛtam||22||

23tayoratha bhujāghātānnigrahapragrahāttathā
|
āsītsubhīmasampāto vajraparvatayoriva||23||

24ubhau paramasaṃhṛṣṭau balena balināṃ varau
|
anyonyasyāntaraṃ prepsū parasparajayaiṣiṇau||24||

25tadbhīmamutsārya janaṃ yuddhamāsīdupaplave
|
balinoḥ saṃyuge rājanvṛtravāsavayoriva||25||

26prakarṣaṇākarṣaṇābhyāmanukarṣavikarṣaṇaiḥ
|
ācakarṣaturanyonyaṃ jānubhiścāvajaghnatuḥ||26||

27tataḥ śabdena mahatā bhartsayantau parasparam
|
pāṣāṇasaṅghātanibhaiḥ prahārairabhijaghnatuḥ||27||

28vyūḍhoraskau dīrghabhujau niyuddhakuśalāvubhau
|
bāhubhiḥ samasajjotāmāyasaiḥ parighairiva||28||

29kārtikasya tu māsasya pravṛttaṃ prathame'hani
|
anāhāraṃ divārātramaviśrāntamavartata||29||

30tadvṛttaṃ tu trayodaśyāṃ samavetaṃ mahātmanoḥ
|
caturdaśyāṃ niśāyāṃ tu nivṛtto māgadhaḥ klamāt||30||

31taṃ rājānaṃ tathā klāntaṃ dṛṣṭvā rājañjanārdanaḥ
|
uvāca bhīmakarmāṇaṃ bhīmaṃ sambodhayanniva||31||

32klāntaḥ śatrurna kaunteya labhyaḥpīḍayituṃ raṇe
|
pīḍyamāno hi kārtsnyena jahyājjīvitamātmanaḥ||32||

33tasmāttenaiva kaunteya pīḍanīyo janādhipaḥ
|
samametena yudhyasva bāhubhyāṃ bharatarṣabha||33||

34evamuktaḥsa kṛṣṇena pāṇḍavaḥ paravīrahā
|
jarāsandhasya tadrūpaṃ jñātvā cakre matiṃ vadhe||34||

35tatastamajitaṃ jetuṃ jarāsandhaṃ vṛkodaraḥ
|
saṃrambhaṃ balināṃ śreṣṭho jagrāha kurunandanaḥ||35||
