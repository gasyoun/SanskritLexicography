Mahābhārata 6.70 (Bhīṣmaparvan)

1sañjaya uvāca|
akarottumulaṃ yuddhaṃ bhīṣmaḥ śāntanavastadā
|
bhīmasenabhayādicchan putrāṃstārayituṃ tava ||1||

2pūrvāhṇe tanmahāraudraṃ rājñāṃ yuddhamavartata
|
kurūṇāṃ pāṇḍavānāṃ ca mukhyaśūravināśanam ||2||

3tasminnākulasaṅgrāme vartamāne mahābhaye
|
abhavattumulaḥ śabdaḥ saṃspṛśan gaganaṃ mahat ||3||

4nadadbhiśca mahānāgairheṣamāṇaiśca vājibhiḥ
|
bherīśaṅkhaninādaiśca tumulaṃ samapadyata ||4||

5yuyutsavaste vikrāntā vijayāya mahābalāḥ
|
anyonyamabhigarjanto goṣṭheṣviva maharṣabhāḥ ||5||

6śirasāṃ pātyamānānāṃ samare niśitaiḥ śaraiḥ
|
aśmavṛṣṭirivākāśe babhūva bharatarṣabha ||6||

7kuṇḍaloṣṇīṣadhārīṇi jātarūpojjvalāni ca
|
patitāni sma dṛśyante śirāṃsi bharatarṣabha ||7||

8viśikhonmathitairgātrairbāhubhiśca sakārmukaiḥ
|
sahastābharaṇaiścānyairabhavacchāditā mahī ||8||

9kavacopahitairgātrairhastaiśca samalaṅkṛtaiḥ
|
mukhaiśca candrasaṃkāśai raktāntanayanaiḥ śubhaiḥ ||9||

10gajavājimanuṣyāṇāṃ sarvagātraiśca bhūpate
|
āsīt sarvā samāstīrṇā muhūrtena vasuṃdharā ||10||

11rajomeghaiśca tumulaiḥ śastravidyutprakāśibhiḥ
|
āyudhānāṃ ca nirghoṣaḥ stanayitnusamo'bhavat ||11||

12sa saṃprahārastumulaḥ kaṭukaḥ śoṇitodakaḥ
|
prāvartata kurūṇāṃ ca pāṇḍavānāṃ ca bhārata ||12||

13tasmin mahābhaye ghore tumule lomaharṣaṇe
|
vavṛṣuḥ śaravarṣāṇi kṣatriyā yuddhadurmadāḥ ||13||

14ākrośan kuñjarāstatra śaravarṣapratāpitāḥ
|
tāvakānāṃ pareṣāṃ ca saṃyuge bharatarṣabha ||14||

15saṃrabdhānāṃ ca vīrāṇāṃ dhīrāṇāmamitaujasām
|
dhanurjyātalaśabdena na prājñāyata kiṃcana ||15||

16utthiteṣu kabandheṣu sarvataḥ śoṇitoda‌ke
|
samare paryadhāvanta nṛpā ripuvadhodyatāḥ ||16||

17śaraśaktigadābhiste khaḍgaiścāmitatejasaḥ
|
nijaghnuḥ samare'nyonyaṃ śūrāḥ parighabāhavaḥ ||17||

18babhramuḥ kuñjarāścātra śarairviddhā niraṃkuśāḥ
|
aśvāśca paryadhāvanta hatārohā diśo daśa ||18||

19utpatya nipatantyanye śaraghātaprapīḍitāḥ
|
tāvakānāṃ pareṣāṃ ca yodhā bharatasattama ||19||

20vāhānāmuttamāṅgānāṃ kārmukāṇāṃ ca bhārata
|
gadānāṃ paridhāṇāṃ ca hastānāṃ corubhiḥ saha ||20||

21pādānāṃ bhūṣaṇānāṃ ca keyūrāṇāṃ ca saṅghaśaḥ
|
rāśayastatra dṛśyante bhīṣmabhīmasamāgame ||21||

22aśvānāṃ kuñjarāṇāṃ ca rathānāṃ cānivartinām
|
saṅghātāḥ sma pradṛśyante tatra tatra viśāṃpate ||22||

23gadābhirasibhiḥ prāsairbāṇaiśca nataparvabhiḥ
|
jaghnuḥ parasparaṃ tatra kṣatriyāḥ kāla āgate ||23||

24apare bāhubhirvīrā niyuddhakuśalā yudhi
|
bahudhā samasajjanta āyasaiḥ parighairivaḥ ||24||

25muṣṭibhirjānubhiścaiva talaiścaiva viśāṃpate
|
anyonyaṃ jaghnire vīrāstāvakāḥ pāṇḍavaiḥ saha ||25||

26patitaiḥ pātyamānaiśca viceṣṭadbhiśca bhūtale
|
ghoramāyodhanaṃ jajñe tatra tatra janeśvara ||26||

27virathā rathinaścātra nistriṃśavaradhāriṇaḥ
|
anyonyamabhidhāvantaḥ parasparavadhaiṣiṇaḥ ||27||

28tato duryodhano rājā kaliṅgairbahubhirvṛtaḥ
|
puraskṛtya raṇe bhīṣmaṃ pāṇḍavānabhyavartata ||28||

29tathaiva pāṇḍavāḥ sarve parivārya vṛkodaram
|
bhīṣmamabhyadravan kruddhāstato yuddhamavartata ||29||
