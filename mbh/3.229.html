Mahābhārata 3.229 (Āraṇyakaparvan)

1mārkaṇḍeya uvāca|
upaviṣṭaṃ tu taṃ skandaṃ hiraṇyakavacasrajam
|
hiraṇyacūḍamukuṭaṃ hiraṇyākṣaṃ mahāprabham||1||

2lohitāmbarasaṃvītaṃ tīkṣṇadaṃṣṭraṃ manoramam
|
sarvalakṣaṇasampannaṃ trailokyasyāpi supriyam||2||

3tatastaṃ varadaṃ śūraṃ yuvānaṃ mṛṣṭakuṇḍalam
|
abhajat padmarūpā śrīḥ svayameva śarīriṇī||3||

4śriyā juṣṭaḥ pṛthuyaśāḥ sa kumāravarastadā
|
niṣaṇṇo dṛśyate bhūtaiḥ paurṇamāsyāṃ yathā śaśī||4||

5apūjayanmahātmāno brāhmaṇāstaṃ mahābalam
|
idamāhustadā caiva skandaṃ tatra maharṣayaḥ||5||

6ṛṣaya ūcuḥ| hiraṇyagarbhaṃ bhadraṃ te lokānāṃ śaṅkaro bhava
|
tvayā ṣaḍrātrajātena sarve lokā vaśīkṛtāḥ||6||

7abhayaṃ ca punardattaṃ tvayaivaiṣāṃ surottama
|
tasmādindro bhavānastu trailokyasyābhayaṅkaraḥ||7||

8skanda uvāca|
kimindraḥ sarvalokānāṃ karotīha tapodhanāḥ
|
kathaṃ devagaṇāṃścaiva pāti nityaṃ sureśvaraḥ||8||

9ṛṣaya ūcuḥ| indro dadhāti bhūtānāṃ balaṃ tejaḥ prajā sukham
|
tuṣṭaḥ prayacchati tathā sarvān kāmān sureśvaraḥ||9||

10durvṛttānāṃ saṃharati vratasthānāṃ prayacchati
|
anuśāsti ca bhūtāni kāryeṣu balasūdanaḥ||10||

11asūrye ca bhavet sūryastathā'candre ca candramāḥ
|
bhavatyagniśca vāyuśca pṛthivyāpaśca kāraṇaiḥ||11||

12etadindreṇa kartavyamindre hi vipulaṃ balam
|
tvaṃ ca vīra balī śreṣṭhastasmādindro bhavasva naḥ||12||

13śakra uvāca|
bhavasvendro mahābāho sarveṣāṃ naḥ sukhāvahaḥ
|
abhiṣicyasva caivādya prāptarūpo'si sattama||13||

14skanda uvāca|
śādhi tvameva trailokyamavyagro vijaye rataḥ
|
ahaṃ te kiṅkaraḥ śakra na mamendratvamīpsitam||14||

15śakra uvāca|
balaṃ tavādbhutaṃ vīra tvaṃ devānāmarīn jahi
|
avajñāsyanti māṃ lokā vīryeṇa tava vismitāḥ||15||

16indratve tu sthitaṃ vīra balahīnaṃ parājitam
|
āvayośca mitho bhede prayatiṣyantyatandritāḥ||16||

17bhedite ca tvayi vibho loko dvaidhamupeṣyati
|
dvidhābhūteṣu lokeṣu niściteṣvāvayostathā||17||

18vigrahaḥ sampravarteta bhūtabhedānmahābala
|
tatra tvaṃ māṃ raṇe tāta yathāśraddhaṃ vijeṣyasi||18||

19tasmādindro bhavāneva bhavitā mā vicāraya
|
skanda uvāca|
tvameva rājā bhadraṃ te trailokyasya mamaiva ca||19||

20karomi kiñca te śakra śāsanaṃ tadbravīhi me
|
indra uvāca|
ahamindro bhaviṣyāmi tava vākyānmahābala||20||

21yadi satyamidaṃ vākyaṃ niścayādbhāṣitaṃ tvayā
|
yadi vā śāsanaṃ skanda kartumicchasi me śṛṇu||21||

22abhiṣicyasva devānāṃ saināpatye mahābala
|
skanda uvāca|
dānavānāṃ vināśāya devānāmarthasiddhaye||22||

23gobrāhmaṇahitārthāya saināpatye'bhiṣiñca mām
|
mārkaṇḍeya uvāca|
so'bhiṣikto maghavatā sarvairdevagaṇaiḥ saha||23||

24atīva śuśubhe tatra pūjyamāno maharṣibhiḥ
|
tatra tat kāñcanaṃ chatraṃ dhriyamāṇaṃ vyarocata||24||

25yathaiva susamiddhasya pāvakasyātmamaṇḍalam
|
viśvakarmakṛtā cāsya divyā mālā hiraṇmayī||25||

26ābaddhā tripuraghnena svayameva yaśasvinā
|
āgamya manujavyāghra saha devyā parantapa||26||

27arcayāmāsa suprīto bhagavān govṛṣadhvaja
|
rudramagniṃ dvijāḥ prāhū rudrasūnustatastu saḥ||27||

28rudreṇa śukramutsṛṣṭaṃ tacchvetaḥ parvato'bhavat
|
pāvakasyendriyaṃ śvete kṛttikābhiḥ kṛtaṃ nage||28||

29pūjyamānaṃ tu rudreṇa dṛṣṭvā sarve divaukasaḥ
|
rudrasūnaṃ tataḥ prāhurguhaṃ guṇavatāṃ varam||29||

30anupraviśya rudreṇa vahniṃ jāto hyayaṃ śiśuḥ
|
tatra jātastataḥ skando rudrasūnustato'bhavat||30||

31rudrasya vahneḥ svāhāyāḥ ṣaṇṇāṃ strīṇāṃ ca bhārata
|
jātaḥ skandaḥ suraśreṣṭho rudrasūnustato'bhavat||31||

32araje vāsasī rakte vasānaḥ pāvakātmajaḥ
|
bhāti dīptavapuḥ śrīmān raktābhrābhyāmivāṃśumān||32||

33kukkuṭaścāgninā dattastasya keturalaṅkṛtaḥ
|
rathe samucchrito bhāti kālāgniriva lohitaḥ||33||

34yā ceṣṭā sarvabhūtānāṃ prabhā śāntirbalaṃ tathā
|
agratastasya sā śaktirdevānāṃ jayavardhinī||34||

35viveśa kavacaṃ cāsya śarīre sahajaṃ tathā
|
yudhyamānasya devasya prādurbhavati tat sadā||35||

36śaktirdharmo balaṃ tejaḥ kāntatvaṃ satyamunnatiḥ
|
brahmaṇyatvamasammoho bhaktānāṃ parirakṣaṇam||36||

37nikṛntanaṃ ca śatrūṇāṃ lokānāṃ cābhirakṣaṇam
|
skandena saha jātāni sarvāṇyeva janādhipa||37||

38evaṃ devagaṇaiḥ sarvaiḥ so'bhiṣiktaḥ svalaṅkṛtaḥ
|
babhau pratītaḥ sumanāḥ paripūrṇendumaṇḍalaḥ||38||

39iṣṭaiḥ svādhyāyaghoṣaiśca devatūryavarairapi
|
devagandharvagītaiśca sarvairapsarasāṃ gaṇaiḥ||39||

40etaiścānyaiśca bahubhistuṣṭairhṛṣṭaiḥ svalaṅkṛtaiḥ
|
susaṃvṛtaḥ piśācānāṃ gaṇairdevagaṇaistathā||40||

41krīḍan bhāti tadā devairabhiṣiktaśca pāvakiḥ
|
abhiṣiktaṃ mahāsenamapaśyanta divaukasaḥ||41||

42vinihatya tamaḥ sūryaṃ yathehābhyuditaṃ tathā
|
athainamabhyayuḥ sarvā devasenāḥ sahasraśaḥ||42||

43asmākaṃ tvaṃ patiriti bruvāṇāḥ sarvato diśaḥ
|
tāḥ samāsādya bhagavān sarvabhūtagaṇairvṛtaḥ||43||

44arcitastu stutaścaiva sāntvayāmāsa tā api
|
śatakratuścābhiṣicya skandaṃ senāpatiṃ tadā||44||

45sasmāra tāṃ devasenāṃ yā sā tena vimokṣitā
|
ayaṃ tasyāḥ patirnūnaṃ vihito brahmaṇā svayam||45||

46iti cintyānayāmāsa devasenāṃ hyalaṅkṛtām
|
skandaṃ provāca balabhidiyaṃ kanyā surottama||46||

47ajāte tvayi nirdiṣṭā tava patnī svayambhuvā
|
tasmāt tvamasyā vidhivat pāṇiṃ mantrapuraskṛtam||47||

48gṛhāṇa dakṣiṇaṃ devyāḥ pāṇinā padmavarcasā
|
evamuktaḥ sa jagrāha tasyāḥ pāṇiṃ yathāvidhi||48||

49bṛhaspatirmantraviddhi jajāpa ca juhāva ca
|
evaṃ skandasya mahiṣīṃ devasenāṃ vidurjanāḥ||49||

50ṣaṣṭhīṃ yāṃ brāhmaṇāḥ prāhurlakṣmīmāśāṃ sukhapradām
|
sinīvālīṃ kuhūṃ caiva sadvṛttimaparājitām||50||

51yadā skandaḥ patirlabdhaḥ śāśvato devasenayā
|
tadā tamāśrayallakṣmīḥ svayaṃ devī śarīriṇī||51||

52śrījuṣṭaḥ pañcamīṃ skandastasmācchrī pañcamī smṛtā
|
ṣaṣṭhyāṃ kṛtārtho'bhūdyasmāttasmāt ṣaṣṭhī mahātithiḥ||52||
