Mahābhārata 8.87 (Karṇaparvan)

1sañjaya uvāca|
vṛṣasenaṃ hataṃ dṛṣṭvā śokāmarṣasamanvitaḥ
|
putraśokodbhavaṃ vāri netrābhyāṃ samavāsṛjat ||1||

2rathena karṇastejasvī jagāmābhimukho ripum
|
yuddhāyāmarṣatāmrākṣaḥ samāhūya dhanañjayam ||2||

3tau rathau sūryasaṅkāśau vaiyāghraparivāritau
|
sametau dadṛśustatra dvāvivārkau samudgatau ||3||

4śvetāśvau puruṣau divyāvāsthitāvarimardanau
|
śuśubhāte mahātmānau candrādityau yathā divi ||4||

5tau dṛṣṭvā vismayaṃ jagmuḥ sarvasainyāni māriṣa
|
trailokyavijaye yattāvindravairocanāviva ||5||

6rathajyātalanirhrādairbāṇasiṃharavaistathā
|
tau rathāvabhyadhāvantau samālokya mahīkṣitām ||6||

7dhvajau ca dṛṣṭvā saṃsaktau vismayaḥ samapadyata
|
hastikakṣaṃ ca karṇasya vānaraṃ ca kirīṭinaḥ ||7||

8tau rathau saṃprasaktau tu dṛṣṭvā bhārata pārthivāḥ
|
siṃhanādaravāṃścakruḥ sādhuvādāṃśca puṣkalān ||8||

9dṛṣṭvā ca dvairathaṃ tābhyāṃ tatra yodhāḥ sahasraśaḥ
|
cakrurbāhusvanāṃścaiva tathā caivāvadhūnanam ||9||

10ājaghnuḥ kuravastatra vāditrāṇi samantataḥ
|
karṇaṃ praharṣayiṣyantaḥ śaṅkhān dadhmuśca sarvaśaḥ ||10||

11tathaiva pāṇḍavāḥ sarve harṣayanto dhanañjayam
|
tūryaśaṃkhaninādena diśaḥ sarvā vyanādayan ||11||

12kṣveḍitāsphoṭitotkṛṣṭaistumulaṃ sarvato'bhavat
|
bāhuśabdaiśca śūrāṇāṃ karṇārjunasamāgame ||12||

|

14varmiṇau baddhanistriṃśau śvetāśvī śaṃkhaśobhitau
|
tūṇīravarasaṃpannau dvāvapyetau sudarśanau ||14||

15raktacandanadigdhāṅgau samadau govṛṣāviva

|

17siṃhaskandhau dīrghabhujau raktākṣau hemamālinau

|

|
devagarbhau devabalau devatulyau ca rūpataḥ ||20||

21yadṛcchayā samāyātau sūryācandramasau yathā
|
balinau samare dṛptau nānāśastradharau yudhi ||21||

22tau dṛṣṭvā puruṣavyāghrau śārdūlāviva dhiṣṭhitau
|
babhūva paramo harṣastāvakānāṃ viśāṃpate ||22||

23saṃśayaḥ sarvabhūtānāṃ vijaye samapadyata
|
sametau puruṣavyāghrau prekṣya karṇadhanañjayau ||23||

24ubhau varāyudhadharāvubhau raṇakṛtaśramau
|
ubhau ca bāhuśabdena nādayantau nabhastalam ||24||

25ubhau viśrutakarmāṇau pauruṣeṇa balena ca
|
ubhau ca sadṛśau yuddhe śambarāmararājayoḥ ||25||

26kārtavīryasamau cobhau tathā dāśaratheḥ samau
|
viṣṇuvīryasamau cobhau tathā bhavasamau yudhi ||26||

27ubhau śvetahayau rājan rathapravaravāhinau
|
sārathī pravarau caiva tayorāstāṃ mahāraṇe ||27||

28tato dṛṣṭvā mahārāja rājamānau mahārathau
|
siddhacāraṇasaṃghānāṃ vismayaḥ samapadyata ||28||

29tava putrāstataḥ karṇaṃ sabalā bharatarṣabha
|
parivavrurmahātmānaṃ kṣipramāhavaśobhinam ||29||

30tathaiva pāṇḍavā hṛṣṭā dhṛṣṭadyumnapurogamāḥ
|
parivavrurmahātmānaṃ pārthamapratimaṃ yudhi ||30||

31tāvakānāṃ raṇe karṇau glaho hyāsīdviśāṃpate
|
tathaiva pāṇḍaveyānāṃ glahaḥ pārtho'bhavattadā ||31||

32ta eva sabhyāstatrāsan prekṣakāścābhavan sma te
|
tatraiṣāṃ glahamānānāṃ dhruvau jayaparājayau ||32||

33tābhyāṃ dyūtaṃ samāsaktaṃ vijayāyetarāya ca
|
asmākaṃ pāṇḍavānāṃ ca sthitānāṃ raṇamūrdhani ||33||

34tau tu sthitau mahārāja samare yuddhaśālinau
|
anyonyaṃ pratisaṃrabdhāvanyonyavadhakāṃkṣiṇau ||34||

35tāvubhau prajihīrṣantāvindravṛtrāviva prabho
|
bhīmarūpadharāvāstāṃ mahādhūmāviva grahau ||35||

36tato'ntarikṣe sākṣepā vivādā bharatarṣabha
|
mitho bhedāśca bhūtānāmāsankarṇārjunāntare ||36||

37vyaśrūyanta mitho bhinnāḥ sarvalokāstu māriṣa
|
deva-dānava-gandharvāḥ piśācoragarākṣasāḥ ||37||

38pratipakṣagrahaṃ cakruḥ karṇārjunasamāgame
|
dyaurāsītsūtaputrasya pakṣe māteva dhiṣṭhitā ||38||

39bhūmirdhanañjayasyāsīnmāteva jayakāṅkiṇī
|
girayaḥ sāgarāścaiva nadyaśca sajalāstathā ||39||

40vṛkṣāścauṣadhayaścaiva vyāśrayanta parasparam
|
asurā yātudhānāśca guhyakāśca parantapa ||40||

41te karṇaṃ samapadyanta hṛṣṭarūpāḥ samantataḥ
|
munayaścāraṇāḥ siddhā vainateyā vayāṃsi ca ||41||

42ratnāni nidhayaḥ sarve vedāścākhyānapañcamāḥ
|
sopavedopaniṣadaḥ sarahasyāḥ sasaṃgrahāḥ ||42||

43vāsukiścitrasenaśca takṣako maṇikastathā
|
sarpāścaiva tathā sarve kādraveyāśca sānvayāḥ ||43||

44viṣavanto mahārāja nāgāścārjunato'bhavan
|
airāvatāḥ saurabheyā vaiśāleyāśca bhoginaḥ ||44||

45ete'bhavannarjunataḥ kṣudrasarpāśca karṇataḥ
|
īhāmṛgā vyālamṛgā māṃgalyāśca mṛgadvijāḥ ||45||

46pārthasya vijaye rājan sarva evābhisaṃsṛtāḥ
|
vasavo marutaḥ sādhyā rudrā viśve'śvinau tathā ||46||

47agnirindraśca somaśca pavano'tha diśo daśa
|
dhanañjayasya te pakṣe ādityāḥ karṇato'bhavan ||47||

48viśaḥ śūdrāśca sūtāśca ye ca saṃkarajātayaḥ
|
sarvaśaste mahārāja rādheyamabhajaṃstadā ||48||

49devāstu pitṛbhiḥ sārdhaṃ sagaṇāḥ sapadānugāḥ
|
yamo vaiśravaṇaścaiva varuṇaśca yato'rjunaḥ ||49||

50brahmakṣatraṃ ca yajñāśca dakṣiṇāścārjunaṃ śritāḥ
|
pretāścaiva piśācāśca kravyādāśca mṛgāṇḍajāḥ ||50||

51rājasāḥ saha yādobhiḥ śvasṛgālāśca karṇataḥ
|
devabrahmanṛparṣīṇāṃ gaṇāḥ pāṇḍavato'bhavan ||51||

52tumburupramukhā rājan gandharvāśca yato'rjunaḥ
|
prāleyāḥ sahamauneyā gandharvāpsarasāṃ gaṇāḥ ||52||

53īhāmṛgāḥ pakṣigaṇā dvipāśvarathapattibhiḥ
|
uhyamānāstathā meghairvāyunā ca manīṣiṇaḥ ||53||

54didṛkṣavaḥ samājagmuḥ karṇārjunasamāgamam
|
devadānavagandharvā nāgayakṣāḥ patatriṇaḥ ||54||

55maharṣayo vedavidaḥ pitaraśca svadhābhujaḥ
|
tapovidyāstathauṣadhyo nānārūpabalānvitāḥ ||55||

56antarikṣe mahārāja vinadanto'vatasthire
|
brahmā brahmarṣibhiḥ sārdhaṃ prajāpatibhireva ca ||56||

57bhavaścaiva sthito yāne divyaṃ taṃ deśamāgamat
|
sametau tau mahātmānau dṛṣṭvā karṇadhanañjayau ||57||

58arjuno jayatāṃ karṇamiti śakro'bravīttadā
|
jayatāmarjunaṃ karṇa iti sūryo'bhyabhāṣata ||58||

59hatvā'rjunaṃ mama sutaḥ karṇo jayatu saṃyuge
|
hatvā karṇaṃ jayatvadya mama putro dhanañjayaḥ ||59||

|
pakṣasaṃsthitayostatra tayorvibudhasiṃhayoḥ
|

|
dṛṣṭvā prajāpatiṃ devāḥ svayaṃbhuvamacodayan ||63||
