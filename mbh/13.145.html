Mahābhārata 13.145 (Anuśāsanaparvan)

1umovāca
|
kiṃśīlaḥ kiṃsamācāraḥ puruṣaḥ kaiśca karmabhiḥ
|
svargaṃ samabhipadyeta saṃpradānena kena vā ||1||

2maheśvara uvāca|
dātā brāhmaṇasatkartā dīnārtakṛpaṇādiṣu
|
bhakṣyabhojyānnapānānāṃ vāsasāṃ ca pradāyakaḥ ||2||

3pratiśrayānsabhāḥ kūpānprapāḥ puṣkariṇīstathā
|
naityakāni ca sarvāṇi kimicchakamatīva ca ||3||

4āsanaṃ śayanaṃ yānaṃ gṛhaṃ ratnaṃ dhanaṃ tathā
|
sasyajātāni sarvāṇi gāḥ kṣetrāṇyatha yoṣitaḥ ||4||

5supratītamanā nityaṃ yaḥ prayacchati mānavaḥ
|
evaṃbhūto naro devi devaloke'bhijāyate ||5||

6tatroṣya suciraṃ kālaṃ bhuktvā bhogānanuttamān
|
sahāpsarobhirmudito ramate nandanādiṣu ||6||

7tasmātsvargāccyuto lokānmānuṣeṣu prajāyate
|
mahābhogakule devi dhanadhānyasamanvitaḥ ||7||

8tatra kāmaguṇaiḥ sarvaiḥ samupeto mudā yutaḥ
|
mahābhogo mahākośo dhanī bhavati mānavaḥ ||8||

9ete devi mahābhāgāḥ prāṇino dānaśīlinaḥ
|
brahmaṇā vai purā proktāḥ sarvasya priyadarśanāḥ ||9||

10apare mānavā devi pradānakṛpaṇā dvijaiḥ
|
yācitā na prayacchanti vidyamāne'pyabuddhayaḥ ||10||

11dīnāndhakṛpaṇāndṛṣṭvā bhikṣukānatithīnapi
|
yācyamānā nivartante jihvālobhasamanvitāḥ ||11||

12na dhanāni na vāsāṃsi na bhogānna ca kāñcanam
|
na gāvo nānnavikṛtiṃ prayacchanti kadācana ||12||

13apravṛttāśca ye lubdhā nāstikā dānavarjitāḥ
|
evaṃbhūtā narā devi nirayaṃ yāntyabuddhayaḥ ||13||

14te vai manuṣyatāṃ yānti yadā kālasya paryayāt
|
dhanarikte kule janma labhante svalpabuddhayaḥ ||14||

15kṣutpipāsāparītāśca sarvalokabahiṣkṛtāḥ
|
nirāśāḥ sarvabhogebhyo jīvantyadharmajīvikām ||15||

16alpabhogakule jātā alpabhogaratā narāḥ
|
anena karmaṇā devi bhavantyadhanino narāḥ ||16||

17apare staṃbhino nityaṃ māninaḥ pāpato ratāḥ
|
āsanārhasya ye pīṭhaṃ na prayacchantyacetasaḥ ||17||

18mārgārhasya ca ye mārgaṃ na yacchantyalpabuddhayaḥ
|
pādyārhasya ca ye pādyaṃ na dadatyalpabuddhayaḥ ||18||

19arghārhānna ca satkārairarcayanti yathāvidhi
|
arghyamācamanīyaṃ vā na yacchantyalpabuddhayaḥ ||19||

20guruṃ cābhigataṃ premṇā guruvanna bubhūṣate
|
abhimānapravṛttena lobhena samavasthitāḥ ||20||

21saṃmānyāṃścāvamanyante vṛddhānparibhavanti ca
|
evaṃvidhā narā devi sarve nirayagāminaḥ ||21||

22te vai yadi narāstasmānnirayāduttaranti vai
|
varṣapūgaistato janma labhante kutsite kule ||22||

23śvapākapulkasādīnāṃ kutsitānāmacetasām
|
kuleṣu teṣu jāyante guruvṛddhāpacāyinaḥ ||23||

24na stambhī na ca mānī yo devatādvijapūjakaḥ
|
lokapūjyo namaskartā praśrito madhuraṃ vacaḥ ||24||

25sarvavarṇapriyakaraḥ sarvabhūtahitaḥ sadā
|
adveṣī susukhaḥ ślakṣṇaḥ snigdhavāṇīpradaḥ sadā ||25||

26svāgatenaiva sarveṣāṃ bhūtānāmavihiṃsakaḥ
|
yathārhasatkriyāpūrvamarcayannavatiṣṭhati ||26||

27mārgārhāya dadanmārgaṃ guruṃ guruvadarcayan
|
atithipragraharatastathā'bhyāgatapūjakaḥ ||27||

28evaṃbhūto naro devi svargatiṃ pratipadyate
|
tato mānuṣatāṃ prāpya viśiṣṭakulajo bhavet ||28||

29tatrāsau vipulairbhogaiḥ sarvaratnasamāyutaḥ
|
yathārhadātā cārheṣu dharmacaryāparo bhavet ||29||

30saṃmataḥ sarvabhūtānāṃ sarvalokanamaskṛtaḥ
|
svakarmaphalamāpnoti svayameva naraḥ sadā ||30||

31udāttakulajātīya udāttābhijanaḥ sadā
|
eṣa dharmo mayā prokto vidhātrā svayamīritaḥ ||31||

32yastu raudrasamācāraḥ sarvasattvabhayaṅkaraḥ
|
hastābhyāṃ yadi vā padbhyāṃ rajjvā daṇḍena vā punaḥ ||32||

33loṣṭaiḥ stambhairāyudhairvā jantūnbādhati śobhane
|
hiṃsārthaṃ nikṛtiprajñaḥ prodvejayati caiva ha ||33||

34upakrāmati jantūṃśca udvegajananaḥ sadā
|
evaṃśīlasamācāro nirayaṃ pratipadyate ||34||

35sa vai manuṣyatāṃ gacchedyadi kālasya paryayāt
|
bahvābādhaparikliṣṭe jāyate so'dhame kule ||35||

36lokadveṣyo'dhamaḥ puṃsāṃ svayaṃ karmaphalaiḥ kṛtaiḥ
|
eṣa devi manuṣyeṣu boddhavyo jñātibandhuṣu ||36||

37aparaḥ sarvabhūtāni dayāvānanupaśyati
|
maitradṛṣṭiḥ pitṛsamo nirvairo niyatendriyaḥ ||37||

38nodvejayati bhūtāni na vighātayate tathā
|
hastapādaiḥ suniyatairviśvāsyaḥ sarvajantuṣu ||38||

39na rajjvā na ca daṇḍena na loṣṭairnāyudhena ca
|
udvejayati bhūtāni lakṣṇakarmā dayāparaḥ ||39||

40evaṃśīlasamācāraḥ svarge samupajāyate
|
tatrāsau bhavane divye mudā vasati devavat ||40||

41sa cetkarmakṣayānmartyo manuṣyeṣūpajāyate
|
alpābādho nirāntakaḥ sa jātaḥ sukhamedhate ||41||

42sukhabhāgī nirāyāso nirudvegaḥ sadā naraḥ
|
eṣa devi satāṃ mārgo bādhā yatra na vidyate ||42||

43umovāca
|
ime manuṣyā dṛśyante ūhāpohaviśāradāḥ
|
jñānavijñānasampannāḥ prajñāvanto'rthakovidāḥ||43||

44duṣprajñāścāpare deva jñānavijñānavarjitāḥ
|
kena karmaviśeṣeṇa prajñāvānpuruṣo bhavet ||44||

45alpaprajño virūpākṣa kathaṃ bhavati mānavaḥ
|
etanme saṃśayaṃ chindhi sarvadharmavidāṃ vara ||45||

46jātyandhāścāpare deva rogārtāścāpare tathā
|
narāḥ klībāśca dṛśyante kāraṇaṃ brūhi tatra vai ||46||

47maheśvara uvāca|
brāhmaṇān vedaviduṣaḥ siddhān dharmavidastathā
|
paripṛcchantyaharahaḥ kuśalāḥ kuśalaṃ tathā ||47||

48varjayanto'śubhaṃ karma sevamānāḥ śubhaṃ tathā
|
labhante svargatiṃ nityamihaloke tathā sukham ||48||

49sa cenmānuṣatāṃ yāti medhāvī tatra jāyate
|
śrutaṃ prajñātugaṃ yasya kalyāṇamupajāyate ||49||

50paradāreṣu ye cāpi cakṣurduṣṭaṃ prayuñjate
|
tena duṣṭasvabhāvena jātyandhāste bhavanti ha ||50||

51manasā tu praduṣṭena nagnāṃ paśyanti ye striyam
|
rogārtāste bhavantīha narā duṣkṛtakarmiṇaḥ ||51||

52ye tu mūḍhā durācārā viyonau maithune ratāḥ
|
puruṣeṣu suduṣpraśāḥ klībatvamupayānti te ||52||

53paśūṃśca ye ghātayanti ye caiva gurutalpagāḥ
|
prakīrṇamaithunā ye ca klībā jāyanti te narāḥ ||53||

54umovāca
|
sāvadyaṃ kintu vai karma niravadyaṃ tathaiva ca
|
śreyaḥ kurvannavāpnoti mānavo devasattama ||54||

55maheśvara uvāca|
śreyāṃsaṃ mārgamanvicchan sadā yaḥ pṛcchati dvijān
|
dharmānveṣī guṇākāṃkṣī sa svargaṃ samupāśnute ||55||

56yadi mānuṣatāṃ devi kadācitsa nigacchati
|
medhāvī dhāraṇāyuktaḥ prāyastatrābhijāyate ||56||

57eṣa devi satāṃ dharmo mantavyo bhūtikārakaḥ
|
nṛṇāṃ hitārthāya mayā tava vai samudāhṛtaḥ ||57||

58umovāca
|
apare svalpavijñānā dharmavidveṣiṇo narāḥ
|
brāhmaṇān veda‌viduṣo necchanti parisarpitum ||58||

59vratavanto narāḥ kecicchraddhādharmaparāyaṇāḥ
|
avratā bhraṣṭaniyamāstathā'nye rākṣasopamāḥ ||59||

60yajvānaśca tathaivānye nirhomāśca tathā'pare
|
kena karmavipākena bhavantīha vadasva me ||60||

61maheśvara uvāca|
āgamā lokadharmāṇāṃ maryādāḥ sarvanirmitāḥ
|
prāmāṇyenānuvartante dṛśyante ca dṛḍhavratāḥ ||61||

62adharmaṃ dharmamityāhurye ca mohavaśaṃ gatāḥ
|
avratā naṣṭamaryādāste proktā brahmarākṣasāḥ ||62||

63te cetkālakṛtodyogātsambhavantīha mānuṣāḥ
|
nirhomā nirvaṣaṭkārāste bhavanti narādhamāḥ ||63||

64eṣa devi mayā sarvaḥ saṃśayacchedanāya te
|
kuśalākuśalo nṝṇāṃ vyākhyāto dharmasāgaraḥ ||64||
