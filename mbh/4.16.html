Mahābhārata 4.16 (Virāṭaparvan)

1kīcaka uvāca|svāgataṃ te sukeśānte suvyuṣṭā rajanī mama
|
svāminī tvamanuprāptā prakuruṣva mama priyam ||1||

2suvarṇamālāḥ kambūśca kuṇḍale parihāṭake
|
nānāpattanaje śubhre maṇiratnaṃ ca śobhanam ||2||

3āharantu ca vastrāṇi kauśikānyajināni ca
|
asti me śayanaṃ divyaṃ tvadarthamupakalpitam
|
ehi tatra mayā sārddhaṃ pibasva madhumādhavīm ||3||

4draupadyuvāca
|
apraiṣīdrājaputrī māṃ surāhārīṃ tavāntikam
|
pānamāhara me kṣipraṃ pipāsā me'ti cābravīt ||4||

5kīcaka uvāca|anyā bhadre nayiṣyanti rājaputryāḥ pratiśrutam
|
ityetāṃ dakṣiṇe pāṇau sūtaputraḥ parāmṛśat ||5||

6draupadyuvāca
|
yathaivāhaṃ nābhicare kadācit patīn madādvai manasā'pi jātu
|
tenaiva satyena vaśīkṛtaṃ tvāṃ draṣṭā'smi pāpaṃ parikṛṣyamāṇam ||6||

7vaiśampāyana uvāca|sa tāmabhiprekṣya viśālanetrāṃ jighṛkṣamāṇaḥ paribhartsayantīm
|
jagrāha tāmuttaravastradeśe sa kīcakastāṃ sahasā''kṣipantīm ||7||

8pragṛhyamāṇā tu mahājavena muhurviniḥśvasya ca rājaputrī
|
tayā samākṣiptatanuḥ sa pāpaḥ papāta śākhīva nikṛttamūlaḥ ||8||

9sā gṛhītā vidhunvānā bhūmāvākṣipya kīcakam
|
sabhāṃ śaraṇamāgacchadyatra rājā yudhiṣṭhiraḥ ||9||

10tāṃ kīcakaḥ pradhāvantīṃ keśapāśe parāmṛśat
|
athaināṃ paśyato rājñaḥ pātayitvā padā'vadhīt ||10||

11tasya yo'sau tadā'rkeṇa rākṣasaḥ saṃniyojitaḥ
|
sa kīcakamapovāha vātavegena bhārata ||11||

12sa papāta tadā bhūmau rakṣobalasamāhataḥ
|
vighūrṇamāno niśceṣṭaśchinnamūla iva drumaḥ ||12||

13tāṃ cāsīnau dadṛśaturbhīmasenayudhiṣṭhirau
|
amṛṣyamāṇau kṛṣṇāyāḥ kīcakena parābhavam ||13||

14tasya bhīmo vadhaṃ prepsuḥ kīcakasya durātmanaḥ
|
dantairdantāṃstadā roṣānniṣpipeṣa mahāmanāḥ ||14||

15dhūmacchāyā hyabhajatāṃ netre cochritapakṣmaṇī
|
sasvedā bhṛkuṭī cogrā lalāṭe samavartata ||15||

16hastena mamṛde caiva lalāṭaṃ paravīrahā
|
bhūyaśca tvaritaḥ kruddhaḥ sahasotthātumaicchata ||16||

17athāvamṛ‌dnādaṅguṣṭhamaṅguṣṭhena yudhiṣṭhiraḥ
|
prabodhanabhayādrājā bhīmaṃ taṃ pratyaṣedhayat ||17||

18taṃ mattamiva mātaṅgaṃ vīkṣamāṇaṃ vanaspatim
|
sa tamāvārayāmāsa bhīmasenaṃ yudhiṣṭhiraḥ ||18||

19ālokayasi kiṃ vṛkṣaṃ sūda dārukṛtena vai
|
yadi te dārubhiḥ kṛtyaṃ bahirvṛkṣānnigṛhyatām ||19||

20sā sabhādvāramāsādya rudatī matsyamabravīt
|
avekṣamāṇā suśroṇī patīṃstān dīnacetasaḥ ||20||

21ākāramabhirakṣantī pratijñā dharmasaṃhitā
|
dahyamāneva raudreṇa cakṣuṣā drupadātmajā ||21||

22draupadyuvāca
|
yeṣāṃ vairī na svapiti ṣaṣṭhe'pi viṣaye vasan
|
teṣāṃ māṃ māninīṃ bhāryāṃ sūtaputraḥ padā'vadhīt ||22||

23ye dadyurne ca yāceyurbrahmaṇyāḥ satyavādinaḥ
|
teṣāṃ māṃ māninīṃ bhāryāṃ sūtaputraḥ padā'vadhīt ||23||

24yeṣāṃ dundubhinirghoṣo jyāghoṣaḥ śrūyate'niśam
|
teṣāṃ māṃ māninīṃ bhāryāṃ sūtaputraḥ padā'vadhīt ||24||

25ye ca tejasvino dāntā balavanto'timāninaḥ
|
teṣāṃ māṃ māninīṃ bhāryāṃ sūtaputraḥ padā'vadhīt ||25||

26sarvalokamimaṃ hanyurdharmapāśasitāstu ye
|
teṣāṃ māṃ māninīṃ bhāryāṃ sūtaputraḥ padā'vadhīt ||26||

27śaraṇaṃ ye prapannānāṃ bhavanti śaraṇārthinām
|
caranti loke pracchannāḥ kva nu te'dya mahārathāḥ ||27||

28kathaṃ te sūtaputreṇa vadhyamānāṃ priyāṃ satīm
|
marṣayanti yathā klībā balavanto'mitaujasaḥ ||28||

29kva nu teṣāmamarṣaśca vīryaṃ tejaśca vartate
|
na parīpsanti ye bhāryāṃ vadhyamānāṃ durātmanā ||29||

30mayā'tra śakyaṃ kiṃ kartuṃ virāṭe dharmadūṣake
|
yaḥ paśyanmāṃ marṣayati vadhyamānāmanāgasam ||30||

31na rājā rājavatkiñcit samācarati kīcake
|
dasyūnāmiva dharmaste na hi saṃsadi śobhate ||31||

32nāhametena yuktaṃ vai hantuṃ matsya tavāntike
|
sabhāsado'tra paśyantu kīcakasya vyatikramam ||32||

33kīcako na ca dharmajño na ca matsyaḥ kathaṃcana
|
sabhāsado'pyadharmajñā ya enaṃ paryupāsate ||33||

34vaiśampāyana uvāca|evaṃvidhairvacobhiḥ sā tadā kṛṣṇā'śrulocanā
|
upālabhata rājānaṃ matsyānāṃ varavarṇinī ||34||

35virāṭa uvāca|parokṣaṃ nābhijānāmi vigrahaṃ yuvayoraham
|
arthatattvamavijñāya kiṃ nu syāt kauśalaṃ mama ||35||

36vaiśampāyana uvāca|tatastu sabhyā vijñāya kṛṣṇāṃ bhūyo'bhyapūjayan
|
sādhu sādhviti cāpyāhuḥ kīcakaṃ ca vyagarhayan ||36||

37sabhyā ūcuḥ | yasyeyaṃ cārusarvāṅgī bhāryā syādāyatekṣaṇā
|
paro lābhastu tasya syānna ca śocet kathaṃcana ||37||

38na hīdṛśī manuṣyeṣu sulabhā varavarṇinī
|
nārī sarvānavadyāṅgī devīṃ manyāmahe vayam ||38||

39vaiśampāyana uvāca|evaṃ saṃpūjayantaste kṛpṇāṃ prekṣya sabhāsadaḥ
|
yudhiṣṭhirasya kopāttu lalāṭe sveda āgamat ||39||

40athābravīdrājaputrīṃ kauravyo mahiṣīṃ priyām
|
gaccha sairandhri mā'trasthāḥ sudeṣṇāyā niveśanam ||40||

41bhartāramanurundhantyaḥ kliśyante vīrapatnayaḥ
|
śuśrūṣayā kliśyamānāḥ patilokaṃ jayantyuta ||41||

42manye na kālaṃ krodhasya paśyanti patayastava
|
tena tvāṃ nābhidhāvanti gandharvāḥ sūryavarcasaḥ ||42||

43akālajñā'si sairandhri śailūṣīva virodiṣi
|
vighnaṃ karoṣi matsyānāṃ dīvyatāṃ rājasaṃsadi ||43||

44gaccha sairandhri gandharvāḥ kariṣyanti tava priyam
|
vyapaneṣyantiṃ te duḥkhaṃ yena te vipriyaṃ kṛtam ||44||

45sairandhyuvāca
|
atīva teṣāṃ ghṛṇināmarthe'haṃ dharmacāriṇī
|
tasya tasyaiva te vadhyā yeṣāṃ jyeṣṭho'kṣadevitā ||45||

46vaiśampāyana uvāca|ityuktvā prādravat kṛṣṇā sudeṣṇāyā niveśanam
|
keśān muktvā ca suśroṇī saṃrambhāllohitekṣaṇā ||46||

47śuśubhe vadanaṃ tasyā rudatyāḥ suciraṃ tadā
|
meghalekhāvinirmuktaṃ divīva śaśimaṇḍalam ||47||

48sudeṣṇovāca
|
kastvā'vadhīdvarārohe kasmādrodiṣi śobhane
|
kasyādya na sukhaṃ bhadre kena te vipriyaṃ kṛtam ||48||

49draupadyuvāca
|
kīcako mā'vadhīttatra surāhārīṃ gatāṃ tava
|
sabhāyāṃ paśyato rājño yathaiva vijane vane ||49||

50sudeṣṇovāca
|
ghātayāmi sukeśānte kīcakaṃ yadi manyase
|
yo'sau tvāṃ kāmasaṃmatto durlabhāmavamanyate ||50||

51sairandhyuvāca
|
anye cainaṃ vadhiṣyanti yeṣāmāgaḥ karoti saḥ
|
manye caivādya suvyaktaṃ yamalokaṃ gamiṣyati ||51||
