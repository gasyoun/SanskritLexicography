Mahābhārata 5.65 (Udyogaparvan)

1dhṛtarāṣṭra uvāca|
duryodhana vijānīhi yattvāṃ vakṣyāmi putraka
|
utpathaṃ manyase mārgamanabhijña ivādhvagaḥ ||1||

2pañcānāṃ pāṇḍuputrāṇāṃ yattejaḥ prajihīrṣasi
|
pañcānāmiva bhūtānāṃ mahatāṃ lokadhāriṇām ||2||

3yudhiṣṭhiraṃ hi kaunteyaṃ paraṃ dharmamihāsthitam
|
parāṃ gatimasaṃpretya na tvaṃ jetumihārhasi ||3||

4bhīmasenaṃ ca kaunteyaṃ yasya nāsti samo bale
|
raṇāntakaṃ tarjayase mahāvātamiva drumaḥ ||4||

5sarvaśastrabhṛtāṃ śreṣṭhaṃ meruṃ śikhariṇāmiva
|
yudhi gāṇḍīvadhanvānaṃ ko nu yudhyeta buddhimān ||5||

6dhuṣṭadyumnaśca pāñcālyaḥ kamivādya na śātayet
|
śatrumadhye śarān muñcan devarāḍaśanīmiva ||6||

7sātyakiścāpi durdharṣaḥ saṃmato'ndhakavṛṣṇiṣu
|
dhvaṃsayiṣyati te senāṃ pāṇḍaveyahite rataḥ ||7||

8yaḥ punaḥ pratimānena trīn lokānatiricyate
|
taṃ kṛṣṇaṃ puṇḍarīkākṣaṃ ko nu yuddhyeta buddhimān ||8||

9ekato hyasya dārāśca jñātayaśca sabāndhavāḥ
|
ātmā ca pṛthivī ceyamekataśca dhanañjayaḥ ||9||

10vāsudevo'pi durdharṣo yatātmā yatra pāṇḍavaḥ
|
aviṣahyaṃ pṛthivyā'pi tadbalaṃ yatra keśavaḥ ||10||

11tiṣṭha tāta satāṃ vākye suhṛdāmarthavādinām
|
vṛddhaṃ śāntanavaṃ bhīṣmaṃ titikṣasva pitāmaham ||11||

12māṃ ca bruvāṇaṃ śuśrūṣa kurūṇāmarthadarśinam
|
droṇaṃ kṛpaṃ vikarṇaṃ ca mahārājaṃ ca bāhlikam ||12||

13ete hyapi yathaivāhaṃ maṃtumarhasi tāṃstathā
|
sarve dharmavido hyete tulyasnehāśca bhārata ||13||

14yattadvirāṭanagare saha bhrātṛ‌bhiragrataḥ
|
utsṛjya gāḥ susaṃtrastaṃ balaṃ te samaśīryata ||14||

15yaccaiva nagare tasmin śrūyate mahada‌dbhutam
|
ekasya ca bahūnāṃ ca paryāptaṃ tannidarśanam ||15||

16arjunastattathā'kārṣīt kiṃ punaḥ sarva eva te
|
sabhrātṝnabhijānīhi vṛttyā taṃ pratipādaya ||16||
