Mahābhārata 8.90 (Karṇaparvan)

1sañjaya uvāca|
tataḥ prayātāḥ śarapātamātramavasthitāḥ kuravo bhinnasenāḥ
|
vidyutprakāśaṃ dadṛśuḥ samantāddhanañjayāstraṃ samudīryamāṇam ||1||

2tadarjunāstraṃ grasati sma karṇo viyadgataṃ ghorataraiḥ śaraistat
|
kruddhena pārthena bhṛśābhisṛṣṭaṃ vadhāya karṇasya mahāvimarde ||2||

3udīryamāṇaṃ sma kurūndahantaṃ suvarṇapuṅkhairviśikhairmamarda
|
karṇastvamogheṣvasanaṃ dṛḍhajyaṃ visphārayitvā visṛjañcharaughān ||3||

4rāmādupāttena mahāmahimnā hyātharvaṇenārivināśanena
|
tadarjunāstraṃ vyadhamaddahantaṃ karṇastu bāṇairniśitairmahātmā ||4||

5tato vimardaḥ sumahān babhūva tatrārjunasyādhiratheśca rājan
|
anyonyamāsādayatoḥ pṛṣatkairviṣāṇaghātairdvipayorivograiḥ ||5||

6tatrāstrasaṃghātasamāvṛtaṃ tadā babhūva rājaṃstumulaṃ sma sarvataḥ
|
tatkarṇapārtho śaravṛṣṭisaṃdhairnirantaraṃ cakraturambaraṃ tadā ||6||

7tato jālaṃ bāṇamayaṃ mahāntaṃ sarve'drākṣuḥ kuravaḥ somakāśca
|
nānyaṃ ca bhūtaṃ dadṛśustadā te bāṇāndhakāre tumule'tha kiṃcit ||7||

8tau sandadhānāvaniśaṃ ca rājan samasyantau cāpi śarānanekān
|
sandarśayetāṃ yudhi mārgānvicitrān dhanurdharau tau vividhaiḥ kṛtāstraiḥ ||8||

9tayorevaṃ yuddhyatorājimadhye sūtātmajo'bhūdadhikaḥ kadācit
|
pārthaḥ kadācittvādhikaḥ kirīṭī vīryāstramāyābalapauruṣeṇa ||9||

10dṛṣṭvā tayostaṃ yudhi saṃprahāraṃ parasparasyāntaramīkṣamāṇayoḥ
|
ghoraṃ tayordurviṣahaṃ raṇe'nyairyodhāḥ sarve vismayamabhyagacchan ||10||

11tato bhūtānyantarikṣasthitāni tau karṇapārthau praśaśaṃsurnarendra
|
bhoḥ karṇa sādhvarjuna sādhu ceti viyatsu vāṇī śrūyate sarvato'pi ||11||

12tasminvimarde rathavājināgaistadābhighātairdalite hi bhūtale
|
tatastu pātālatale śayāno nāgo'śvasenaḥ kṛtavairo'rjunena ||12||

13rājaṃstadā khāṇḍavadāhamukto viveśa kopādvasudhātale yaḥ
|
athotpapātordhvagatirjavena saṃdṛśya karṇārjunayorvimardam ||13||

14ayaṃ hi kālo'sya durātmano vai pārthasya vairapratiyātanāya
|
sañcintya tūṇaṃ praviveśa caiva karṇasya rājañśararūpadhārī ||14||

15tato'strasaṃghātasamākulaṃ tadā babhūva janyaṃ vitatāṃśujālam
|
tatkarṇapārthau śarasaṅghavṛṣṭibhirnirantaraṃ cakraturambaraṃ tadā ||15||

16tadbāṇajālaikamayaṃ mahāntaṃ sarve'trasankuravaḥ somakāśca
|
nānyatkiñci‌ddadṛśuḥ saṃpatadvai bāṇāndhakāre tumule'timātram ||16||

17tatastau puruṣavyāghrau sarvalokadhanurdharau
|
tyaktaprāṇau raṇe vīrau yuddhaśramamupāgatau
|
samutkṣepairvīkṣamāṇau siktau candanavāriṇā ||17||

18savālavyajanairdivyairdivisthairapsarogaṇaiḥ
|
śakrasūryakarābjābhyāṃ pramārjitamukhāvubhau ||18||

19karṇo'tha pārthaṃ na viśeṣayadyadā bhṛśaṃ ca pārthena śarābhitaptaḥ
|
tatastu vīraḥ śaravikṣatāṅgo dadhre mano hyekaśayasya tasya ||19||

20tato ripughnaṃ samadhatta karṇaḥ susañcitaṃ sarpamukhaṃ jvalantam
|
raudraṃ śaraṃ sannatamugradhautaṃ pārthārthamatyarthacirābhiguptam ||20||

21sadārcitaṃ candanacūrṇaśāyitaṃ suvarṇatūṇīraśayaṃ mahārciṣam
|
ākarṇapūrṇaṃ ca vikṛṣya karṇaḥ pārthonmukhaḥ sandadhe cottamaujāḥ ||21||

22pradīptamairāvatavaṃśasaṃbhavaṃ śiro jihīrṣuryudhi savyasācinaḥ
|
tataḥ prajajvāla diśo nabhaśca ulkāśca ghorāḥ śataśaḥ prapetuḥ ||22||

23tasmiṃstu nāge dhanuṣi prayukte hāhākṛtā lokapālāḥ saśakrāḥ
|
na cāpi taṃ bubudhe sūtaputro bāṇe praviṣṭaṃ yogabalena nāgam ||23||

24daśaśatanayano'rhi dṛśya bāṇe praviṣṭaṃ nihata iti suto me srastagātro babhūva
|
jalajakusumayoniḥ śreṣṭhabhāvo jitātmā tridaśapatimavocanmā vyathiṣṭhā jaye śrīḥ ||24||

25tato'bravīnmadrarājo mahātmā dṛṣṭvā karṇaṃ prahiteṣuṃ tamugram
|
na karṇagrīvāmiṣureṣa lapsyate samīkṣya sandhatsva śaraṃ śirodhram ||25||

26athābravītkrodhasaṃraktanetro madrādhipaṃ sūtaputrastarasvī
|
na saṃdhatte dviḥ śaraṃ śalya karṇo na mādṛśā jihmayuddhā bhavanti ||26||

27itīdamuktvā visasarja taṃ śaraṃ prayatnato varṣagaṇābhipūjitam
|
hato'si vai phālguna ityadhikṣipannuvāca coccairgiramūrjitāṃ vṛṣaḥ ||27||

28sa sāyakaḥ karṇabhujaprasṛṣṭo hutāśanārkapratimaḥ sughoraḥ
|
guṇacyutaḥ karṇadhanuḥpramukto viyadgataḥ prājvaladantarikṣe ||28||

29taṃ prekṣya dīptaṃ yudhi mādhavastu tvarānvitaṃ satvarayaiva līlayā
|
padā viniṣpiṣya rathottamaṃ sa prāveśayatpṛthivīṃ kiñcideva ||29||

30kṣitiṃ gatā jānubhiste'tha vāhā hemacchannāścandramarīcivarṇāḥ
|
tato'ntarikṣe sumahānninādaḥ saṃpūjanārthaṃ madhusūdanasya ||30||

31divyāśca vācaḥ sahasā babhuvurdivyāni puṣpāṇyatha siṃhanādāḥ
|
tasmiṃstathā vai dharaṇīṃ nimagne rathe prayatnānmadhusūdanasya ||31||

32tataḥ śaraḥ so'bhyahanat kirīṭaṃ tasyendradattaṃ sudṛḍhaṃ ca dhīmataḥ
|
athārjunasyottamagātrabhūṣaṇaṃ dharāviya‌ddyosalileṣu viśrutam ||32||

33vyālāstrasargottamayatnamanyubhiḥ śareṇa mūrdhnaḥ prajahāra sūtajaḥ
|
divākarendujvalanaprabhatviṣaṃ suvarṇamuktāmaṇivajrabhūṣitam ||33||

34purandarārthaṃ tapasā prayatnataḥ svayaṃ kṛtaṃ yadvibhunā svayaṃbhuvā
|
mahārharūpaṃ dviṣatāṃ bhayaṃkaraṃ bibharturatyarthasukhaṃ sugandhinam ||34||

35jighāṃsate devaripūnsureśvaraḥ svayaṃ dadau yatsumanaḥ kirīṭine
|
harāmbupākhaṇḍalavittagoptṛbhiḥ pinākapāśāśanisāyakottamaiḥ ||35||

36surottamairapyaviṣahyamardituṃ prasahya nāgena jahāra yadvṛṣaḥ
|
sa duṣṭabhāvo vitathapratijñaḥ kirīṭamatyadbhutamarjunasya ||36||

37nāgo mahārhaṃ tapanīyacitraṃ pārthottamāṅgātpraharattarasvī
|
taddhemajālāvatataṃ sughoṣaṃ jājvalyamānaṃ nipapāta bhūmau ||37||

38taduttameṣūnmathitaṃ viṣāgninā pradīptamarciṣmadatho kṣitau priyam
|
papāta pārthasya kirīṭamuttamaṃ divākaro'stādiva raktamaṇḍalaḥ ||38||

39sa vai kirīṭaṃ bahuratnabhūṣitaṃ jahāra nāgo'rjunamūrdhato balāt
|
gireḥ sujātāṅkurapuṣpitadrumaṃ mahendravajraḥ śikharottamaṃ yathā ||39||

40mahī viyat dyauḥ salilaṃ ca vāyunā prasahyamugraṃ vinighūrṇitaṃ yathā
|
atīva śabdo bhuvaneṣu vai tadā janā'dhyavasyanvyathitāśca caskhaluḥ ||40||

41vinā kirīṭaṃ śuśubhe sa pārthaḥ śyāmo yuvā nīla ivoccaśṛṅgaḥ
|
tataḥ samudgrathya sitena vāsasā svamūrdhajānavyathitastadā'rjunaḥ
|
vibhāsitaḥ sūryamarīcinā dṛḍhaṃ śirogatenodayaparvato yathā ||41||

42gokarṇā sumukhī kṛtena iṣuṇā goputrasaṃpreṣitā
|
gośabdātmajabhūṣaṇaṃ suvihitaṃ suvyaktago'suprabham
|
dṛṣṭvā gogatakaṃ jahāra mukuṭaṃ gośabdagopūri vai
|
gokarṇāsanamardanaśca na yayāvaprāpya mṛtyorvaśam ||42||

43sa sāyakaḥ karṇabhujaprasṛṣṭo hutāśanārkapratimo mahārhaḥ
|
mahoragaḥ kṛtavairo'rjunena kirīṭamāhatya tato vyatīyāt ||43||

44taṃ cāpi dagdhvā tapanīyacitraṃ kirīṭamākṛṣya tadarjunasya
|
iyeṣa gantuṃ punareva tūṇaṃ dṛṣṭaśca karṇena tato'bravīttam ||44||

45muktastvayā'haṃ tvasamīkṣya karṇa śiro hṛtaṃ yanna mayā'rjunasya
|
samīkṣya māṃ muñca raṇe tvamāśu hantā'smi śatruṃ tava cātmanaśca ||45||

46sa evamukto yudhi sūtaputrastamabravītko bhavānugrarūpaḥ
|
nāgo'bravīdviddhi kṛtāgasaṃ māṃ pārthena māturvadhajātavairam ||46||

47yadi svayaṃ vajradharo'sya goptā tathā'pi yātā pitṛrājaveśmani
|
karṇa uvāca|
na nāga karṇo'dya raṇe parasya balaṃ samāsthāya jayaṃ bubhūṣet ||47||

48na sandadhyāṃ dviḥ śaraṃ caiva nāga yadyarjunānāṃ śatameva hanyām
|
tamāha karṇaḥ punareva nāgaṃ tadā''jimadhye ravisūnusattamaḥ ||48||

49vyālāstrasargottamayatnamanyubhirhantāsmi pārthaṃ susukhī vraja tvam
|
ityevamukto yudhi nāgarājaḥ karṇena roṣādasahaṃstasya vākyam ||49||

50svayaṃ prāyātpārthavadhāya rājan kṛtvā svarūpaṃ vijighāṃsurugraḥ
|
tataḥ kṛṣṇaḥ pārthamuvāca saṃkhye mahoragaṃ kṛtavairaṃ jahi tvam ||50||

51sa evamukto madhusūdanena gāṇḍīvadhanvā ripuvīryasāhaḥ
|
uvāca ko hyeṣa mamādya nāgaḥ svayaṃ ya āyādgaruḍasya vaktram ||51||

52kṛṣṇa uvāca|
yo'sau tvayā khāṇḍave citrabhānuṃ santarpayāṇena dhanurdhareṇa
|
viyadgato jananīguptadeho matvaikarūpaṃ nihatā'sya mātā ||52||

53sa eṣa tadvairamanusmaranvai tvāṃ prārthayatyātmavadhāya nūnam
|
nabhaścyutāṃ prajvalitāmivolkāṃ paśyainamāyāntamamitrasāha ||53||

54sañjaya uvāca|
tataḥ sa jiṣṇuḥ parivṛtya roṣācciccheda paḍbhirniśitaiḥ sudhāraiḥ
|
nāgaṃ viyattiryagivotpatantaṃ sa cchinnagātro nipapāta bhūmau ||54||

55gate ca tasmin bhujage kirīṭinā svayaṃ vibhuḥ pārthivabhūtalādatha
|
samujjahārāśu punaḥ patantaṃ rathaṃ bhujābhyāṃ puruṣottamastataḥ ||55||

56tasminmuhūrte daśabhiḥ pṛṣatkaiḥ śilāśitairbarhiṇabarhavājitaiḥ
|
vivyādha karṇaḥ puruṣapravīro dhanañjayaṃ tiryagavekṣamāṇaḥ ||56||

57tato'rjuno dvādaśabhiḥ sumuktairvarāhakarṇairniśitaiḥ samarpya
|
nārācamāśīviṣatulyavegamākarṇapūrṇāyatamutsasarja ||57||

58sa citravarmeṣuvaro vidārya prāṇānnirasyanniva sādhu muktaḥ
|
karṇasya pītvā rudhiraṃ viveśa vasundharāṃ śoṇitadigdhavājaḥ ||58||

59tato vṛṣo bāṇanipātakopito mahorago daṇḍavighaṭṭito yathā
|
tadāśukārī vyasṛjaccharottamān mahāviṣaḥ sarpa ivottamaṃ viṣam ||59||

60janārdanaṃ dvādaśabhiḥ parābhinannavairnavatyā ca śaraistathā'rjunam
|
śareṇa ghoreṇa punaśca pāṇḍavaṃ vidārya karṇo vyanadajjahāsa ca ||60||

61tamasya harṣaṃ mamṛṣe na pāṇḍavo bibheda marmāṇi tato'sya marmavit
|
paraḥśataiḥ patribhirindravikramastathā yathendro balamojasā raṇe ||61||

62tataḥ śarāṇāṃ navatiṃ tadā'rjunaḥ sasarja karṇe'ntakadaṇḍasannibhām
|
taiḥ patribhirviddhatanuḥ sa vivyathe tathā yathā vajravidārito'calaḥ ||62||

63maṇipravekottamavajrahāṭakairalaṃkṛtaṃ cāsya varāṅgabhūṣaṇam
|
praviddhamurvyāṃ nipapāta patribhirdhanañjayenottamakuṇḍale'pi ca ||63||

64mahādhanaṃ śilpivaraiḥ prayatnataḥ kṛtaṃ yadasyottamavarma bhāsvaram
|
sudīrghakālena tato'sya pāṇḍavaḥ kṣaṇena bāṇairbahudhā vyaśātayat ||64||

65sa taṃ vivarmāṇamathottameṣubhiḥ śitaiścaturbhiḥ kupitaḥ parābhinat
|
sa vivyathe'tyarthamaripratāḍito yathā''turaḥ pittakaphānilajvaraiḥ ||65||

66mahādhanurmaṇḍalaniḥsṛtaiḥ śitaiḥ kriyāprayatnaprahitairbalena ca
|
tatakṣa karṇaṃ bahubhiḥ śarottamairbibheda marmasvapi cārjunastvaran ||66||

67dṛḍhāhataḥ patribhirugravegaiḥ pārthena karṇo vividhaiḥ śitāgraiḥ
|
babhau girirgairikadhāturaktaḥ kṣaranprapātairiva raktamaṃbhaḥ ||67||

68tato'rjunaḥ karṇamavakragairnavaiḥ suvarṇapuṃkhaiḥ sudṛḍhairayasmayaiḥ
|
yamāgnidaṇḍapratimaiḥ stanāntare parābhinatkrauñcamivādrimagnijaḥ ||68||

69tataḥ śarāvāpamapāsya sūtajo dhanuśca tacchakraśarāsanopamam
|
tato rathasthaḥ sa mumoha ca skhalan praśīrṇamuṣṭiḥ subhṛśāhataḥ prabho ||69||

70na cārjunastaṃ vyasane tadeṣivānnihantumāryaḥ puruṣavrate sthitaḥ
|
tatastamindrāvarajaḥ susaṃbhramāduvāca kiṃ pāṇḍava he pramādyase ||70||

71naivāhitānāṃ satataṃ vipaścitaḥ kṣaṇaṃ pratīkṣantyapi durbalīyasām
|
viśeṣato'rīnvyasaneṣu paṇḍito nihatya dharmaṃ ca yaśaśca vindate ||71||

72tadekavīraṃ tava cāhitaṃ sadā tvarasva karṇaṃ sahasā'bhimarditum
|
purā samarthaḥ samupaiti sūtajo bhindhi tvamenaṃ namuciṃ yathā hariḥ ||72||

73tatastadevetyabhipūjya satvaraṃ janārdanaṃ karṇamavidhyadarjunaḥ
|
śarottamaiḥ sarvakurūttamastvaraṃstathā yathā śambarahā purā balim ||73||

74sāśvaṃ tu karṇaṃ sarathaṃ kirīṭī samācinodbhārata vatsadantaiḥ
|
pracchādayāmāsa diśaśca bāṇaiḥ sarvaprayatnāttapanīyapuṃkhaiḥ ||74||

75sa vatsadantaiḥ pṛthupīnavakṣāḥ samācitaḥ so'dhirathirvibhāti
|
supuṣpitāśokapalāśaśālmaliryathā'calaścandanakānanāyutaḥ ||75||

76śaraiḥ śarīre bahubhiḥ samarpitairvibhāti karṇaḥ samare viśāṃpate
|
mahīruhairācitasānukandaro yathā girīndraḥ sphuṭakarṇikāravān ||76||

77sa bāṇasaṅghān bahudhā vyavāsṛjan vibhāti karṇaḥ śarajālaraśmivān
|
sa lohito raktagabhastimaṇḍalo divākaro'stābhimukho yathā tathā ||77||

78bāhvantarādādhirathervimuktān bāṇānmahāhīniva dīpyamānān
|
vyadhvaṃsayannarjunabāhumuktāḥ śarāḥ samāsādya diśaḥ śitāgrāḥ ||78||

79tataḥ sa karṇaḥ samavāpya dhairyaṃ bāṇānvimuñcan kupitāhikalpān
|
vivyādha pārtha daśabhiḥ pṛṣatkaiḥ kṛṣṇaṃ ca ṣaḍbhiḥ kupitāhikalpaiḥ ||79||

80tataḥ kirīṭī bhṛśamugraniḥsvanaṃ mahāśaraṃ sarpaviṣānalopamam
|
ayasmayaṃ raudramahāstrasaṃbhṛtaṃ mahāhave kṣeptumanā mahāmatiḥ ||80||

81kālo hyadṛśyo nṛpa viprakopānnidarśayankarṇavadhaṃ bruvāṇaḥ
|
bhūmistu cakraṃ grasatītyavocatkarṇasya tasminvadhakāla āgate ||81||

82tatastadastraṃ manasaḥ pranaṣṭaṃ yadbhārgavo'smai pradadau mahātmā
|
cakraṃ ca vāmaṃ grasate bhūmirasya prāpte tasminvadhakāle nṛvīra ||82||

83tato ratho ghūrṇitavānnarendra śāpāttadā brāhmaṇasattamasya
|
tataścakramapatattasya bhūmau sa vihvalaḥ samare sūtaputraḥ ||83||

84savedikaścaitya ivātimātraḥ supuṣpito bhūmitale nimagnaḥ
|
ghūrṇe rathe brāhmaṇasyābhiśāpādrāmādupātte tvavibhāti cāstre ||84||

85chinne śare sarpamukhe ca ghore pārthena tasminviṣasāda karṇaḥ
|
amṛṣyamāṇo vyasanāni tāni hastau vidhunvansa vigarhamāṇaḥ ||85||

86dharmapradhānaṃ kila pāti dharma ityabruvan dharmavidaḥ sadaiva
|
vayaṃ ca dharme prayatāma nityaṃ cartuṃ yathāśakti yathāśrutaṃ ca ||86||

87sa cāpi nighnāti na pāti bhaktān manye na nityaṃ paripāti dharmaḥ
|
evaṃ bruvanpraskhalitāśvasūto vicālyamāno'rjunabāṇapātaiḥ
|
marmābhighātācchithilaḥ kriyāsu punaḥ punardharmamasau jagarha ||87||

88tataḥ śarairbhīmatarairavidhyattribhirāhave
|
haste kṛṣṇaṃ tathā pārthamabhyavidhyacca saptabhiḥ ||88||

89tato'rjunaḥ saptadaśa tigmavegānajihmagān
|
indrāśanisamān ghorānasṛjatpāvakopamān ||89||

90nirbhidya te bhīmavegā hyapatan pṛthivītale
|
kampitātmā tataḥ karṇaḥ śaktyā ceṣṭāmadarśayat ||90||

91balenātha sa saṃstabhya brahmāstraṃ samudairayat
|
aindraṃ tato'rjunaścāpi taṃ dṛṣṭvā'bhyupamantrayat ||91||

92gāṇḍīvaṃ jyāṃ ca bāṇāṃśca so'numantrya parantapaḥ
|
vyasṛjaccharavarṣāṇi varṣāṇīva purandaraḥ ||92||

93tatastejomayā bāṇā rathātpārthasya niḥsṛtāḥ
|
prādurāsanmahāvīryāḥ karṇasya rathamantikāt ||93||

94tān karṇastvagrato nyastān moghāṃścakre mahārathaḥ
|
tato'vavrīdvṛṣṇivīrastasminnastre vināśite ||94||

95visṛjāstraṃ paraṃ pārtha rādheyo grasate śarān
|
tato brahmāstramatyugraṃ saṃmantrya samayojayat ||95||

96chā‌dayitvā tato bāṇaiḥ karṇaṃ pratyasyadarjunaḥ
|
tataḥ karṇaḥ śitairbāṇairjyāṃ ciccheda sutejanaiḥ ||96||

97dvitīyāṃ ca tṛtīyāṃ ca caturthīṃ pañcamīṃ tathā
|
ṣaṣṭhīmathāsya ciccheda saptamīṃ ca tathā'ṣṭamīm ||97||

98navamīṃ daśamīṃ cāsya tathā caikādaśīṃ vṛṣaḥ
|
jyāśataṃ śatasandhānaḥ sa karṇo nāvabudhyate ||98||

99tato jyāṃ vinidhāyānyāmabhimantrya ca pāṇḍavaḥ
|
śarairavākiratkarṇaṃ dīpyamānairivāhibhiḥ ||99||

100tasya jyācchedanaṃ karṇo jyāvadhānaṃ ca saṃyuge
|
nānvabudhyata śīghratvāttada‌dbhutamivābhavat ||100||

101astrairastrāṇi saṃvārya pranighnansaṃvyasācinaḥ
|
cakre cāpyadhikaṃ pārthāt svavīryamatidarśayan ||101||

102tataḥ kṛṣṇo'rjunaṃ dṛṣṭvā karṇāstreṇa ca pīḍitam
|
abhyasetyabravītpārthamātiṣṭhāstraṃ vrajeti ca ||102||

103tato'gnisadṛśaṃ ghoraṃ śaraṃ sarpaviṣopamam
|
aśmasāramayaṃ divyamabhimantrya parantapaḥ ||103||

104raudramastraṃ samādhāya kṣeptukāmaḥ kirīṭavān
|
tato'grasanmahī cakraṃ rādheyasya tadā nṛpa ||104||

105tato'vatīrya rādheyo rathādāśu samudyataḥ
|
cakraṃ bhujābhyāmālambya samutkṣeptumiyeṣa saḥ ||105||

106saptadvīpā vasumatī saśailavanakānanā
|
gīrṇacakrā samutkṣiptā karṇena caturaṅgulam ||106||

107grastacakrastu rādheyaḥ krodhādaśrūṇyavartayat
|
arjunaṃ vīkṣya saṃrabdhamidaṃ vacanamabravīt ||107||

108bho bho pārtha maheṣvāsa muhūrtaṃ paripālaya
|
yāvaccakramidaṃ grastamuddharāmi mahītalāt ||108||

109savyaṃ cakraṃ mahīgrastaṃ dṛṣṭvā daivādidaṃ mama
|
pārtha kāpuruṣācīrṇamabhisandhiṃ visarjaya ||109||

110na tvaṃ kāpuruṣācīrṇaṃ mārgamāsthātumarhasi
|
khyātastvamasi kaunteya viśiṣṭo raṇakarmasu ||110||

111viśiṣṭatarameva tvaṃ kartumarhasi pāṇḍava
|
prakīrṇakeśe vimukhe brāhmaṇe'tha kṛtāñjalau ||111||

112śaraṇāgate nyastaśastre yācamāne tathā'rjuna
|
abāṇe bhraṣṭakavace bhraṣṭabhagnāyudhe tathā ||112||

113na vimuñcanti śastrāṇi śūrāḥ sādhuvrate sthitāḥ
|
tvaṃ ca śūratamo loke sādhuvṛttaśca pāṇḍava ||113||

114abhijño yuddhadharmāṇāṃ vedāntāvabhṛthāplutaḥ
|
divyāstravidameyātmā kārtavīryasamo yudhi ||114||

115yāvaccakramidaṃ grastamuddharāmi mahābhuja
|
na māṃ rathastho bhūmiṣṭhaṃ vikalaṃ hantumarhasi ||115||

116na vāsudevāttvatto vā pāṇḍaveya bibhemyaham
|
tvaṃ hi kṣatriyadāyādo mahākulavivardhanaḥ
|
atastvāṃ prabravīmyeṣa muhūrtaṃ kṣama pāṇḍava ||116||
