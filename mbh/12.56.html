Mahābhārata 12.56 (Śāntiparvan)

1vaiśampāyana uvāca|
praṇipatya hṛṣīkeśamabhivādya pitāmaham
|
anumānya gurūnsarvānparyapṛcchadyudhiṣṭhiraḥ||1||

2yudhiṣṭhira uvāca|
rājñāṃ vai paramo dharma iti dharmavido viduḥ
|
mahāntametaṃ bhāraṃ ca manye tadbrūhi pārthiva||2||

3rājadharmānviśeṣeṇa kathayasva pitāmaha
|
sarvasya jīvalokasya rājadharmaḥ parāyaṇam||3||

4trivargo hi samāsakto rājadharmeṣu kaurava
|
mokṣadharmaśca vispaṣṭaḥ sakalo'tra samāhitaḥ||4||

5yathā hi raśmayo'śvasya dviradasyāṃkuśo yathā
|
narendradharmo lokasya tathā pragrahaṇaṃ smṛtam||5||

6tatra cetsampramuhyeta dharme rājarṣisevite
|
lokasya saṃsthā na bhavetsarvaṃ ca vyākulībhavet||6||

7udayan hi yathā sūryo nāśayatyaśubhaṃ tamaḥ
|
rājadharmāstathālokyāṃ nikṣipantyaśubhāṃ gatim||7||

8tadane rājadharmān hi madarthe tvaṃ pitāmaha
|
prabrūhi bharataśreṣṭha tvaṃ hi dharmabhṛtāṃ varaḥ||8||

9āgamaśca parastvattaḥ sarveṣāṃ paraṃtapa
|
bhavantaṃ hi paraṃ buddhau vāsudevo'bhimanyate||9||

10bhīṣma uvāca|
namo dharmāya mahate namaḥ kṛṣṇāya vedhase
|
brāhmaṇebhyo namaskṛtya dharmānvakṣyāmi śāśvatān||10||

11śṛṇu kārtsnyena mattastvaṃ rājadharmānyudhiṣṭhira
|
nirucyamānānniyato yaccānyadapi vāñchasi||11||

12ādāveva kuruśreṣṭha rājñā rañjanakāmyayā
|
devatānāṃ dvijānāṃ ca vartitavyaṃ yathāvidhi||12||

13daivatānyarcayitvā hi brāhmaṇāṃśca kurūdvaha
|
ānṛṇyaṃ yāti dharmasya lokena ca samarcyate||13||

14utthānena sadā putra prayatethā yudhiṣṭhira
|
na hyusthānamṛte daivaṃ rājñāmarthaṃ prasādayet||14||

15sādhāraṇaṃ dvayaṃ hyetad daivamutthānameva ca
|
pauruṣaṃ hi paraṃ manye daivaṃ niścitamucyate||15||

16vipanne ca samārambhe saṃtāpaṃ mā sma vai kṛthāḥ
|
ghaṭasvaiva sadā''tmānaṃ rājñāmeṣa paro nayaḥ||16||

17na hi satyādṛte kiṃcidrājñāṃ vai siddhikārakam
|
satye hi rājā nirataḥ pretya ceha na nandati||17||

18ṛṣīṇāmapi rājendra satyameva paraṃ dhanam
|
tathā rājñāṃ paraṃ satyānnānyadviśvāsakāraṇam||18||

19guṇavāñśīlavāndānto mṛdurdharmyo jitendriyaḥ
|
sudarśaḥ sthūlalakṣyaśca na bhraśyeta sadā śriyaḥ||19||

20ārjavaṃ sarvakāryeṣu śrayethāḥ kurunandana
|
punarnayavicāreṇa trayīsaṃvaraṇena ca||20||

21mṛdurhi rājā satataṃ laṃghyo bhavati sarvaśaḥ
|
tīkṣṇāccodvijate lokastasmādubhayamāśraya||21||

22adaṇḍyāścaiva te putra viprāśca dadatāṃ vara
|
bhūtametatparaṃ loke brāhmaṇo nāma pāṇḍava||22||

23manunā caiva rājendra gītau ślokau mahātmanā
|
dharmeṣu sveṣu kauravya hṛdi tau kartumarhasi||23||

24adbhyo'gnirbrahmataḥ kṣatramaśmano lohamutthitam
|
teṣāṃ sarvatragaṃ tejaḥ svāsu yoniṣu śāmyati||24||

25ayo hanti yadāśmānamagninā vāri hanyate
|
brahma ca kṣatriyo dveṣṭi tadā sīdanti te trayaḥ||25||

26evaṃ kṛtvā mahārāja namasyā eva te dvijāḥ
|
bhaumaṃ brahma dvijaśreṣṭhā dhārayanti samarcitāḥ||26||

27evaṃ caiva naravyāghra lokatrayavidhātakāḥ
|
nigrāhyā eva satataṃ bāhubhyāṃ ye syurīdṛśāḥ||27||

28ślokau cośanasā gītau purā tāta maharṣiṇā
|
tau nibodha mahārāja tvamekāgramanā nṛpa||28||

29udyamya śastramāyāntamapi vedāntagaṃ raṇe
|
nigṛhṇīyātsvadharmeṇa dharmāpekṣī narādhipaḥ||29||

30vinaśyamānaṃ dharmaṃ hi yo'bhirakṣetsa dharmavit
|
na tena dharmahā sa syānmanyustanmanyumṛcchati||30||

31evaṃ caiva naraśreṣṭha rakṣyā eva dvijātayaḥ
|
sāparādhānapi hi tānviṣayānte samutsṛjet||31||

32abhiśastamapi hyeṣāṃ kṛpāyīta viśāmpate
|
brahmaghne gurutalpe ca bhrūṇahatye tathaiva ca||32||

33rājadviṣṭe ca viprasya viṣayānte visarjanam
|
vidhīyate na śārīraṃ daṇḍameṣāṃ kadācana||33||

34dayitāśca narāste syurbhaktimanto dvijeṣu ye
|
na kośaḥ paramo'nyo'sti rājñāṃ puruṣasaṃcayāt||34||

35durgeṣu ca mahārāja ṣaṭsu ye śāstraniścitāḥ
|
sarvadurgeṣu manyante naradurgaṃ sudustaram||35||

36tasmānnityaṃ dayā kāryā cāturvarṇye vipaścitā
|
dharmātmā satyavāk caiva rājā rañjayati prajāḥ||36||

37na ca kṣāntena te nityaṃ bhāvyaṃ putra samantataḥ
|
adharmo hi mṛdū rājā kṣamāvāniva kuñjaraḥ||37||

38bārhaspatye ca śāstre ca śloko nigaditaḥ purā
|
asminnarthe mahārāja tanme nigadataḥ śṛṇu||38||

39kṣamamāṇaṃ nṛpaṃ nityaṃ nīcaḥ paribhavejjanaḥ
|
hastiyantā gajasyaiva śira evārurukṣati||39||

40tasmānnaiva mṛdurnityaṃ tīkṣṇo naiva bhavennṛpaḥ
|
vāsantārka iva śrīmān na śīto na ca gharmadaḥ||40||

41pratyakṣeṇānumānena tathaupamyāgamairapi
|
parīkṣyāste mahārāja sve pare caiva nityaśaḥ||41||

42vyasanāni ca sarvāṇi tyajethā bhūridakṣiṇa
|
na caiva na prayuñjīta saṃkīrṇaṃ parivarjayet||42||

43lokasya vyasanī nityaṃ paribhūto bhavatyuta
|
udvejayati lokaṃ ca yo'tidveṣī mahīpatiḥ||43||

44bhavitavyaṃ sadā rājñā garbhiṇīsahadharmiṇā
|
kāraṇaṃ ca mahārāja śṛṇu yenedamiṣyate||44||

45yathā hi garbhiṇī hitvā svaṃ priyaṃ manaso'nugam
|
garbhasya hitamādhatte tathā rājñāpyasaṃśayam||45||

46vartitavyaṃ kuruśreṣṭha sadā dharmānuvartinā
|
svaṃ priyaṃ tu parityajya yadyallokahitaṃ bhavet||46||

47na santyājyaṃ ca te dhairyaṃ kadācidapi pāṇḍava
|
dhīrasya spaṣṭadaṇḍasya na bhayaṃ vidyate kvacit||47||

48parihāsaśca bhṛtyaiste nātyarthaṃ vadatāṃ vara
|
kartavyo rājaśārdūla doṣamatra hi me śṛṇu||48||

49avamanyanti bhartāraṃ saṃgharṣādupajīvinaḥ
|
sve sthāne na ca tiṣṭhanti laṅghayanti ca tadvacaḥ||49||

50preṣyamāṇā vikalpante guhyaṃ cāpyanuyuñjate
|
ayācyaṃ caiva yācante bhojyānyāhārayanti ca||50||

51kruśyanti paridīpyanti bhūmipāyādhitiṣṭhate
|
utkocairvañcanābhiśca kāryāṇyanuvihanti ca||51||

52jarjaraṃ cāsya viṣayaṃ kurvanti pratirūpakaiḥ
|
strīrakṣibhiśca sajjante tulyaveṣā bhavanti ca||52||

53vāntaṃ niṣṭhīvanaṃ caiva kurvate cāsya saṃnidhau
|
nirlajjā rājaśārdūla vyāharanti ca tadvacaḥ||53||

54hayaṃ vā dantinaṃ vāpi rathaṃ vā nṛpasattama
|
abhirohantyanādṛtya harṣule pārthive mṛdau||54||

55idaṃ te duṣkaraṃ rājannidaṃ te duṣṭaceṣṭitam
|
ityevaṃ suhṛdo vācaṃ vadante pariṣadgatāḥ||55||

56kruddhe cāsminhasantyeva na ca hṛṣyanti pūjitāḥ
|
saṃharṣaśīlāśca tadā bhavantyanyonyakāraṇāt||56||

57vistraṃsayanti mantraṃ ca vivṛṇvanti ca duṣkṛtam
|
līlayā caiva kurvanti sāvajñāstasya śāsanam||57||

58alaṃkāre ca bhojye ca tathā snānānulepane
|
helanāni naravyāghra svasthāstasyopaśṛṇvataḥ||58||

59nindante svānadhīkārān santyajante ca bhārata
|
na vṛttyā parituṣyanti rājadeyaṃ haranti ca||59||

60krīḍituṃ tena cecchanti sasūtreṇeva pakṣiṇā
|
asmatpraṇeyo rājeti lokāṃścaiva vadantyuta||60||

61ete caivāpare caiva doṣāḥ prādurbhavantyuta
|
nṛpatau mārdavopeta harṣule ca yudhiṣṭhira||61||
