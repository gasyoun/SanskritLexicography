Mahābhārata 12.186 (Śāntiparvan)

1bharadvāja uvāca |
yadi prāṇayate vāyurvāyureva viceṣṭate
|
śvasityābhāṣate caiva tasmājjīvo nirarthakaḥ ||1||

2yadūṣmabhāva āgneyo vahninā pacyate yadi
|
agnirjarayate caitattasmājjīvo nirarthakaḥ ||2||

3jantoḥ pramīyamāṇasya jīvo naivopalabhyate
|
vāyureva jahātyenamūṣmabhāvaśca naśyati ||3||

4yadi vāyumayo jīvaḥ saṃśleṣo yadi vāyunā
|
vāyumaṇḍalavaddṛśyo gacchetsaha marudgaṇaiḥ ||4||

5saṃśleṣo yadi vā tena yadi tasmātpraṇaśyati
|
mahārṇavavimuktatvādanyatsalilabhājanam ||5||

6kūpe vā salilaṃ dadyātpradīpaṃ vā hutāśane
|
kṣipraṃ praviśya naśyeta yathā naśyatyasau tathā ||6||

7pañcadhāraṇake hyasmin śarīre jīvitaṃ kutaḥ
|
teṣāmanyatarābhāvāccaturṇāṃ nāsti saṃśayaḥ ||7||

8naśyantyāpo hyanāhārādvāyurucchvāsanigrahāt
|
naśyate koṣṭhabhedātkhamagnirnaśyatyabhojanāt ||8||

9vyādhivarṇaparikleśairmedinī caiva śīryate
|
pīḍite'nyatare hyeṣāṃ saṃghāto yāti pañcadhā ||9||

10tasminpañcatvamāpanne jīvaḥ kimanudhāvati
|
kiṃ vedayati vā jīvaḥ kiṃ śṛṇoti bravīti ca ||10||

11eṣā gauḥ paralokasthaṃ tārayiṣyati māmiti
|
yo dattvā mriyate jantuḥ sā gauḥ kaṃ tārayiṣyati ||11||

12gauśca pratigrahītā ca dātā caiva samaṃ yadā
|
ihaiva vilayaṃ yānti kutasteṣāṃ samāgamaḥ ||12||

13vihagairupabhuktasya śailāgrātpatitasya ca
|
agninā copayuktasya kutaḥ sañjīvanaṃ punaḥ ||13||

14chinnasya yadi vṛkṣasya na mūlaṃ pratirohati
|
bījānyasya pravartante mṛtaḥ kva punareṣyati ||14||

15bījamātraṃ purā sṛṣṭaṃ yadetatparivartate
|
mṛtāmṛtāḥ praṇaśyanti bījādbījaṃ pravartate ||15||
