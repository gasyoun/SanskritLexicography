Mahābhārata 5.170 (Udyogaparvan)

1bhīṣma uvāca|
draupadeyā mahārāja sarve pañca mahārathāḥ
|
vairāṭiruttaraścaiva rathodāro mato mama ||1||

2abhimanyurmahābāhū rathayūthapayūthapaḥ
|
samaḥ pārthena samare vāsudevena cārihā ||2||

3labdhāstraścitrayodhī ca manasvī ca dṛḍhavrataḥ
|
saṃsmaran vai parikleśaṃ svapiturvikramiṣyati ||3||

4sātyakirmādhavaḥ śūro rathayūthapayūthapaḥ
|
eṣa vṛṣṇipravīrāṇāmamarṣī jitasādhvasaḥ ||4||

5uttamaujāstathā rājan rathodāro mato mama
|
yudhāmanyuśca vikrānto rathodāro mato mama ||5||

6eteṣāṃ bahusāhasrā rathā nāgā hayāstathā
|
yotsyante te tanūṃstyaktvā kuntīputrapriyepsayā ||6||

7pāṇḍavaiḥ saha rājendra tava senāsu bhārata
|
agnimārutavadrājannāhvayantaḥ parasparam ||7||

8ajeyau samare vṛddhau virāṭadrupadau tathā
|
mahārathau mahāvīryau matau me puruṣarṣabhau ||8||

9vayovṛddhāvapi hi tau kṣatradharmaparāyaṇau
|
yatiṣyete paraṃ śaktyā sthitau vīragate pathi ||9||

10sambandhakena rājendra tau tu vīryabalānvayāt
|
āryavṛttau maheṣvāsau snehavīryasitāvubhau ||10||

11kāraṇaṃ prāpya tu narāḥ sarva eva mahābhujāḥ
|
śūrā vā kātarā vā'pi bhavanti kurupuṅgava ||11||

12ekāyanagatāvetau pārthivau dṛḍhadhanvinau
|
prāṇāṃstyaktvā paraṃ śaktyā ghaṭṭitārau paraṃtapa ||12||

13pṛthagakṣauhiṇībhyāṃ tāvubhau saṃyati dāruṇau
|
saṃbandhibhāvaṃ rakṣantau mahat karma kariṣyataḥ ||13||

14lokavīrau maheṣvāsau tyaktātmānau ca bhārata
|
pratyayaṃ parirakṣantau mahat karma kariṣyataḥ ||14||
