Mahābhārata 12.17 (Śāntiparvan)

1yudhiṣṭhira uvāca|
asantoṣaḥ pramādaśca mado rāgo'praśāntatā
|
balaṃ moho'bhimānaścāpyudvegaścaiva sarvaśaḥ ||1||

2ebhiḥ pāpmabhirāviṣṭo rājyaṃ tvamabhikṣakāṅkṣase
|
nirābhiṣo vinirmuktaḥ praśāntaḥ susukhī bhava ||2||

3ya imāmakhilāṃ bhūmiṃ śiṣyādeko mahīpatiḥ
|
tasyāpyudaramekaṃ vai kimidaṃ tvaṃ praśaṃsasi ||3||

4nāhnā pūrayituṃ śakyāṃ na māsairbharatarṣabha
|
apūryāṃ pūrayannicchāmāyuṣāpi na śaknuyāt ||4||

5yatheddhaḥ prajvalatyagnirasamiddhaḥ praśāmyati
|
alpāhāratayā tvagniṃ śamayaudaryamutthitam ||5||

6ātmodarakṛte prājñaḥ karoti vivasaṃ bahu
|
jayodaraṃ pṛthivyā te śreyo nirjitayā jitam ||6||

7mānuṣāṅkāmabhogāṃstvamaiśvaryaṃ ca praśaṃsasi
|
abhogino'balāścaiva yānti sthānamanuttamam ||7||

8yogaḥ kṣemaśca rāṣṭrasya dharmādharmau tvayi sthitau
|
mucyasva mahato bhārāttyāgamevābhisaṃśraya ||8||

9ekodarakṛte vyāghraḥ karoti vighasaṃ bahu
|
tamanye'pyupajīvanti mandā lobhavaśā mṛgāḥ ||9||

10viṣayānpratisaṅgṛhya saṃnyāsaṃ kurvate yadi
|
na ca tuṣyanti rājānaḥ paśya buddhyantaraṃ yathā ||10||

11patrāhārairaśmakuṭṭairdantolūkhalikaistathā
|
abbhakṣairvāyubhakṣaiśca tairayaṃ narako jitaḥ ||11||

12yastvimāṃ vasudhāṃ kṛtsnāṃ praśāseda‌khilāṃ nṛpaḥ
|
tulyāśmakāñcano yaśca sakṛtārtho na pārthivaḥ ||12||

13saṅkalpeṣu nirārambho nirāśo nirmamo bhava
|
aśokaṃ sthānamātiṣṭha iha cāmutra cāvyayam ||13||

14nirāmiṣā na śocanti śocasi tvaṃ kimāmiṣam
|
parityajyāmiṣaṃ sarvaṃ mṛṣāvādātpramokṣyase ||14||

15panthānau pitṛyānaśca devayānaśca viśrutau
|
ījānāḥ pitṛyānena devayānena mokṣiṇaḥ ||15||

16tapasā brahmacaryeṇa svādhyāyena maharṣayaḥ
|
vimucya dehāṃste yānti mṛtyoraviṣayaṃ gatāḥ ||16||

17āmiṣaṃ bandhanaṃ loke karmehoktaṃ tathāmiṣam
|
tābhyāṃ vimuktaḥ pāpābhyāṃ padamāpnoti tatparam ||17||

18api gāthāṃ purā gītāṃ janakena vadantyuta
|
nirddhanddhena vimuktena mokṣaṃ samanupaśyatā ||18||

19anantaṃ bata me vittaṃ yasya me nāsti kiñcana
|
mithilāyāṃ pradīptāyāṃ na me dahyati kiñcana ||19||

20prajñāprāsādamāruhya aśocyāṃ śocato janān
|
jagatīsthānivādristho mandabuddhirna cekṣate ||20||

21dṛśyaṃ paśyati yaḥ paśyan sa cakṣuṣmānsa buddhimān
|
ājñātānāṃ ca vijñānāt sambodhādbuddhirucyate ||21||

22yastu vācaṃ vijānāti bahumānamiyātsa vai
|
brahmabhāvaprapannānāṃ vaidyānāṃ bhāvitātmanām ||22||

23yadā bhūtapṛthagbhāvamekasthamanupaśyati
|
tata eva ca vistāraṃ brahma sampadyate tadā ||23||

24te janāstāṃ gatiṃ yānti nāvidvāṃso'lpatejasaḥ
|
nābuddhayo nātapasaḥ sarvaṃ buddhau pratiṣṭhitam ||24||
