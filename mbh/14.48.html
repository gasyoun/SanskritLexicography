Mahābhārata 14.48 (Āśvamedhikaparvan)

1brahmovāca|
kecidbrahmamayaṃ vṛkṣaṃ kecidbrahmavanaṃ mahat
|
kecittu brahma cāvyaktaṃ kecitparamanāmayam
|
manyante sarvamapyetadavyaktaprabhavāvyayam||1||

2ucchvāsamātramapi cedyo'ntakāle samo bhavet
|
ātmānamupasaṅgamya so'mṛtatvāya kalpate||2||

3nimeṣamātramapi cetsaṃyamyātmānamātmani
|
gacchatyātmaprasādena viduṣāṃ prāptimavyayām||3||

4prāṇāyāmairatha prāṇānsaṃyamya sa punaḥ punaḥ
|
daśadvādaśabhirvāpi caturviṃśātparaṃ tataḥ||4||

5evaṃ pūrvaṃ prasannātmā labhate yadyadicchati
|
avyaktātsattvamudriktamamṛtatvāya kalpate||5||

6sattvātparataraṃ nānyatpraśaṃsantīha tadvidaḥ
|
anumānādvijānīmaḥ puruṣaṃ sattvasaṃśrayam
|
na śakyamanyathā gantuṃ puruṣaṃ dvijasattamāḥ||6||

7kṣamā dhṛtirahiṃsā ca samatā satyamārjavam
|
jñānaṃ tyāgo'tha saṃnyāsaḥ sāttvikaṃ vṛttamiṣyate||7||

8etenaivānumānena manyante vai manīṣiṇaḥ
|
sattvaṃ ca puruṣaścaiva tatra nāsti vicāraṇā||8||

9āhureke ca vidvāṃso ye jñānapariniṣṭhitāḥ
|
kṣetrajñasattvayoraikyamityetannopapadyate||9||

10pṛthagbhūtaṃ tataḥ sattvamityetadavicāritam
|
pṛthagbhāvaśca vijñeyaḥ sahajaścāpi tattvataḥ||10||

11tathaivaikatvanānātvamiṣyate viduṣāṃ nayaḥ
|
maśakodumbare caikyaṃ pṛthaktvamapi dṛśyate||11||

12matsyo yathānyaḥ syādapsu samprayogastathā tayoḥ
|
sambandhastoyabindūnāṃ parṇe kokanadasya ca||12||

13gururuvāca|
ityuktavantaste viprāstadā lokapitāmaham
|
punaḥ saṃśayamāpanāḥ papracchurmunisattamāḥ||13||
