Mahābhārata 8.69 (Karṇaparvan)

1sañjaya uvāca|
yudhiṣṭhireṇaivamuktaḥ kaunteyaḥ śvetavāhanaḥ
|
asiṃ jagrāha saṃkruddho jighāṃsurbharatarṣabham ||1||

2tasya kopaṃ samudvīkṣya cittajñaḥ keśavastadā
|
uvāca kimidaṃ pārtha gṛhītaḥ khaḍga ityuta ||2||

3na hi paśyāmi yoddhavyaṃ tvayā kiñciddhanañjaya
|
te grastā dhārtarāṣṭrā hi bhīmasenena dhīmatā ||3||

4apayāto'si kaunteya rājā draṣṭavya ityapi
|
sa rājā bhavatā dṛṣṭaḥ kuśalī ca yudhiṣṭhiraḥ ||4||

5sa dṛṣṭvā nṛpaśārdūlaṃ śārdūlasamavikramam
|
harṣakāle ca saṃprāpte kimidaṃ mohakāritam ||5||

6na taṃ paśyāmi kaunteya yaste vadhyo bhaviṣyati
|
prahartumicchase kasmātkiṃ vā te cittavibhramaḥ ||6||

7kasmādbhavānmahākhaḍgaṃ parigṛhṇāti satvaraḥ
|
tattvāṃ pṛcchāmi kaunteya kimidaṃ te cikīrṣitam ||7||

8parāmṛśasi yatkruddhaḥ khaḍgamadbhutavikrama
|
evamuktastu kṛṣṇena prekṣamāṇo yudhiṣṭhiram ||8||

9arjunaḥ prāha govindaṃ kruddhaḥ sarpa iva śvasan
|
anyasmai dehi gāṇḍīvamiti māṃ yo'bhicodayet ||9||

10bhindyāmahaṃ tasya śira ityupāṃśuvrataṃ mama
|
taduktaṃ mama cānena rājñā'mitaparākrama ||10||

11samakṣaṃ tava govinda na tatkṣantumihotsahe
|
tasmādenaṃ vadhiṣyāmi rājānaṃ dharmabhīrukam ||11||

12pratijñāṃ pālayiṣyāmi hatvainaṃ narasattamam
|
etadarthaṃ mayā khaḍgo gṛhīto yadunandana ||12||

13so'haṃ yudhiṣṭhiraṃ hatvā satyasyānṛṇyatāṃ gataḥ
|
viśoko vijvaraścāpi bhaviṣyāmi janārdana ||13||

14kiṃ vā tvaṃ manyase prāptamasminkāla upasthite
|
tvamasya jagatastāta vettha sarvaṃ gatāgatam ||14||

15tattathā prakariṣyāmi yathā māṃ vakṣyate bhavān
|
sañjaya uvāca|
dhigdhigityeva govindaḥ pārthamuktvābravītpunaḥ ||15||

16kṛṣṇa uvāca|
'idānīṃ pārtha jānāmi na vṛddhāḥ sevitāstvayā
|
kāle na puruṣavyāghra saṃraṃbhaṃ yadbhavānagāt ||16||

17na hi dharmavibhāgajñaḥ kuryādevaṃ dhanañjaya
|
yathā tvaṃ pāṇḍavādyeha dharmabhīrurapaṇḍitaḥ ||17||

18akāryāṇāṃ kriyāṇāṃ ca saṃyogaṃ yaḥ karoti vai
|
kāryāṇāmakriyāṇāṃ ca sa pārtha puruṣādhamaḥ ||18||

19anusṛtya tu ye dharmaṃ kathayeyurupasthitāḥ
|
samāsavistaravidāṃ na teṣāṃ vetsi niścayam ||19||

20aniścayajño hi naraḥ kāryākāryaviniścaye
|
avaśo muhyate pārtha yathā tvaṃ mūḍha eva tu ||20||

21na hi kāryamakāryaṃ vā sukhaṃ jñātuṃ kathaṃcana
|
śrutena jñāyate sarvaṃ tacca tvaṃ nāvabudhyase ||21||

22avijñānādbhavānyacca dharmaṃ rakṣati dharmavit
|
prāṇināṃ tvaṃ vadhaṃ pārtha dhārmiko nāvabudhyase ||22||

23prāṇināmavadhastāta sarvajyāyānmato mama
|
anṛtāṃ vā vadedvācaṃ na tu hiṃsyātkathañcana ||23||

24sa kathaṃ bhrātaraṃ jyeṣṭhaṃ rājānaṃ dharmakovidam
|
hanyādbhavānnaraśreṣṭha prākṛto'nyaḥ pumāniva ||24||

25ayudhyamānasya vadhastathā'śatrośca mānada
|
parāṅmukhasya dravataḥ śaraṇaṃ cāpi gacchataḥ ||25||

26kṛtāñjaleḥ prapannasya pramattasya tathaiva ca
|
na vadhaḥ pūjyate sadbhistacca sarvaṃ gurau tava ||26||

27tvayā caivaṃ vrataṃ pārtha bāleneva kṛtaṃ purā
|
tasmādadharmasaṃyuktaṃ maurkhyātkarma vyavasyasi ||27||

28sa guruṃ pārtha kasmāttvaṃ hantukāmo'bhidhāvasi
|
asaṃpradhārya dharmāṇāṃ gatiṃ sūkṣmāṃ duratyayām ||28||

29idaṃ dharmarahasyaṃ ca tava vakṣyāmi pāṇḍava
|
yadbrūyāttava bhīṣmo hi pāṇḍavo vā yudhiṣṭhiraḥ ||29||

30viduro vā tathā kṣattā kuntī vā'pi yaśasvinī
|
tat te vakṣyāmi tattvena nibodhaitaddhanañjaya ||30||

31satyasya vaditā sādhurna satyādvidyate param
|
tattvenaiva sudurjñeyaṃ paśya satyamanuṣṭhitam ||31||

32bhavetsatyamavaktavyaṃ vaktavyamanṛtaṃ bhavet
|
yatrānṛtaṃ bhavetsatyaṃ satyaṃ cāpyanṛtaṃ bhavet ||32||

33vivāhakāle ratisaṃprayoge prāṇātyaye sarvadhanāpahāre
|
viprasya cārthe hyanṛtaṃ vadeta pañcānṛtānyāhurapātakāni ||33||

34sarvasvasyāpahāre tu vaktavyamanṛtaṃ bhavet
|
tatrānṛtaṃ bhavetsatyaṃ satyaṃ cāpyanṛtaṃ bhavet
|
tādṛśaṃ paśyate bālo yasya satyamanuṣṭhitam ||34||

35bhavetsatyamavaktavyaṃ na vaktavyamanuṣṭhitam
|
satyānṛte viniścitya tato bhavati dharmavit ||35||

36kimāścaryaṃ kṛtaprajñaḥ puruṣo'pi sudāruṇaḥ
|
sumahatprāpnuyātpuṇyaṃ balāko'ndhavadhādiva ||36||

37kimāścaryaṃ punarmūḍho dharmakāmo hyapaṇḍitaḥ
|
sumahatprāpnuyātpāpamāpagāsviva kauśikaḥ' ||37||

38arjuna uvāca|
ācakṣva bhagavannetadyathā vindāmyahaṃ tathā
|
balākasyānusaṃbandhaṃ nadīnāṃ kauśikasya ca ||38||

39vāsudeva uvāca|
'purā vyādho'bhavatkaścidbalāko nāma bhārata
|
yātrārthaṃ putradārasya mṛgān hanti na kāmataḥ ||39||

40vṛddhau ca mātāpitarau bibhartyanyāṃśca saṃśritān
|
svadharmanirato nityaṃ satyavāganasūyakaḥ ||40||

41sa kadācinmṛgaṃ lipsurnābhyavindanmṛgaṃ kvacit
|
apaḥ pibantaṃ dadṛśe śvāpadaṃ ghrāṇacakṣuṣam ||41||

42adṛṣṭapūrvamapi tatsattvaṃ tena hataṃ tadā
|
andhe hate tato vyomnaḥ puṣpavarṣaṃ papāta ca ||42||

43apsarogītavāditrairnāditaṃ ca manoramam
|
vimānamagamatsvargānmṛgavyādhaninīṣayā ||43||

44tadbhūtaṃ sarvabhūtānāmabhāvāya kilārjuna
|
tapastaptvā varaṃ prāptaṃ kṛtamandhaṃ svayaṃbhuvā ||44||

45taddhatvā sarvabhūtānāmabhāvakṛtaniścayam
|
tato balākaḥ svaragādevaṃ dharmaḥ sudurvidaḥ ||45||

46kauśiko'pyabhavadviprastapasvīno bahuśrutaḥ
|
nadīnāṃ saṅgame grāmādadūrātsa kilāvasat ||46||

47satyaṃ mayā sadā vācyamiti tasyābhavadvratam
|
satyavādīti vikhyātaḥ sa tadā'sīddhanañjaya ||47||

48atha dasyubhayātkecittadā tadvanamāviśan
|
tatrāpi dasyavaḥ kruddhāstānamārganta yatnataḥ ||48||

49atha kauśikamabhyetya prāhuste satyavādinam
|
katamena pathā yātā bhagavanbahavo janāḥ ||49||

50satyena pṛṣṭaḥ prabrūhi yadi tān vettha śaṃsa naḥ
|
sa pṛṣṭaḥ kauśikaḥ satyaṃ vacanaṃ tānuvāca ha ||50||

51bahuvṛkṣalatāgulmametadvanamupāśritāḥ
|
iti tān khyāpayāmāsa tebhyastattvaṃ sa kauśikaḥ ||51||

52tataste tānsamāsādya krūrā jaghnuriti śrutiḥ
|
tenādharmeṇa mahatā vāgduruktena kauśikaḥ ||52||

53gataḥ sa kaṣṭaṃ narakaṃ sūkṣmadharmeṣvakovidaḥ
|
yathā cālpaśruto mūḍho dharmāṇāmavibhāgavit ||53||

54vṛddhānapṛṣṭvā sandehaṃ mahachvabhramivārhati
|
tatra te lakṣaṇoddeśaḥ kaścidevaṃ bhaviṣyati ||54||

55duṣkaraṃ paramaṃ jñānaṃ tarkeṇānuvyavasyati
|
śruterdharma iti hyeke vadanti bahavo janāḥ ||55||

56tatte na pratyasūyāmi na ca sarvaṃ vidhīyate
|
prabhavārthāya bhūtānāṃ dharmapravacanaṃ kṛtam ||56||

57yatsyādahiṃsāsaṃyuktaṃ sa dharma iti niścayaḥ
|
ahiṃsārthāya hiṃstrāṇāṃ dharmapravacanaṃ kṛtam ||57||

58dhāraṇāddharmamityāhurdharmo dhārayate prajāḥ
|
yatsyāddhāraṇasaṃyuktaṃ sa dharma iti niścayaḥ ||58||

59ye'nyāyena jihīrṣanto dharmamicchanti karhicit
|
akūjanena mokṣaṃ vā nānukūjetkathañcana ||59||

60avaśyaṃ kūjitavye vā śaṃkerannapyakūjataḥ
|
śreyastatrānṛtaṃ vaktuṃ tatsatyamavicāritam ||60||

61yaḥ kāryebhyo vrataṃ kṛtvā tasya nānopapādayet
|
na tatphalamavāpnoti evamāhurmanīṣiṇaḥ ||61||

62prāṇātyaye vivāhe vā sarvajñātivadhātyaye
|
narmaṇyabhipravṛtte vā na ca proktaṃ mṛṣā bhavet ||62||

63adharmaṃ nātra paśyanti dharmatattvārthadarśinaḥ
|
yaḥ stenaiḥ saha saṃbaṃdhānmucyate śapathairapi ||63||

64śreyastatrānṛtaṃ vaktuṃ tatsatyamavicāritam
|
na ca tebhyo dhanaṃ deyaṃ śakye sati kathañcana ||64||

65pāpebhyo hi dhanaṃ dattaṃ dātāramapi pīḍayet
|
tasmāddharmārthamanṛtamuktvā nānṛtabhāgbhavet ||65||

66eṣa te lakṣaṇoddeśo mayoddiṣṭo yathāvidhi
|
yathādharmaṃ yathābuddhi mayādya vai hitārthinā ||66||

67etacchrutvā brūhi pārtha yadi vadhyo yudhiṣṭhiraḥ
|
arjuna uvāca|
yathā brūyānmahāprājño yathā brūyānmahāmatiḥ ||67||

68hitaṃ caiva yathā'smākaṃ tathaitadvacanaṃ tava
|
bhavānmātṛsamo'smākaṃ tathā pitṛsamo'pi ca ||68||

69gatiśca paramā kṛṣṇa tvameva ca parāyaṇam
|
na hi te triṣu lokeṣu vidyate'viditaṃ kvacit ||69||

70tasmādbhavānparaṃ dharmaṃ veda sarvaṃ yathātatham
|
avadhyaṃ pāṇḍavaṃ manye dharmarājaṃ yudhiṣṭhiram ||70||

71asmiṃstu mama saṃkalpe brūhi kiṃcidanugraham
|
idaṃ vā paramatraiva śṛṇu hṛtsthaṃ vivakṣitam ||71||

72jānāsi dāśārha mama vrataṃ tvaṃ yo māṃ brūyātkaścana mānuṣeṣu
|
anyasmai tvaṃ gāṇḍivaṃ dehi pārtha tvatto'strairvā vīryato vā viśiṣṭaḥ ||72||

73hanyāmahaṃ keśava taṃ prasahya bhīmo hanyāttūbaraketi coktaḥ
|
tanme rājā proktavāṃste samakṣaṃ dhanurdehītyasakṛdvṛṣṇivīra ||73||

74taṃ hanyāṃ cetkeśava jīvaloke sthātā nāhaṃ kālamapyalpamātram
|
dhyātvā nūnaṃ hyenasā cāpi mukto vadhaṃ rājño bhraṣṭavīryo vicetāḥ ||74||

75yathā pratijñā mama lokabuddhau bhavetsatyā dharmabhṛtāṃ variṣṭha
|
yathā jīvet pāṇḍavo'haṃ ca kṛṣṇa tathā buddhiṃ dātumapyarhasi tvam ||75||

76vāsudeva uvāca|
rājā śrānto vikṣato duḥkhitaśca karṇena saṃkhye niśitairbāṇasaṅghaiḥ
|
yaścāniśaṃ sūtaputreṇa vīra śarairbhṛśaṃ tāḍito yudhyamānaḥ ||76||

77atastvametena saroṣamukto duḥkhānvitenedamayukta rūpam
|
akopito hyeṣa yadi sma saṃkhye karṇaṃ na hanyāditi cābravītsaḥ ||77||

78jānāti taṃ pāṇḍava eṣa cāpi pāpaṃ loke karṇamasahyamanyaiḥ
|
tatastvamukto bhṛśaroṣitena rājñā samakṣaṃ paruṣāṇi pārtha ||78||

79nityodyukte satataṃ cāprasahye karṇe dyūtaṃ hyadya raṇe nibaddham
|
tasmin hate kuravo nirjitāḥ syurevaṃ buddhiḥ pārthive dharmaputre ||79||

80tato vadhaṃ nārhati dharmaputrastvayā pratijñā'rjuna pālanīyā
|
jīvannayaṃ yena mṛto bhaveddhi tanme nibodheha tavānurūpam ||80||

81yadā mānaṃ labhate mānanārhastadā sa vai jīvati jīvaloke
|
yadā'vamānaṃ labhate mahāntaṃ tadā jīvanmṛta ityucyate saḥ ||81||

82saṃmānitaḥ pārthivo'yaṃ sadaiva tvayā ca bhīmena tathā yamābhyām
|
vṛddhaiśca loke puruṣaiśca śūraistasyāpamānaṃ kalayā prayuṅkṣva ||82||

83tvamityatrabhavantaṃ hi brūhi pārtha yudhiṣṭhiram
|
tvamityukto hi nihato gururbhavati bhārata ||83||

84evamācara kaunteya dharmarāje yudhiṣṭhire
|
adharmayuktaṃ saṃyogaṃ kuruṣvainaṃ kurūdvaha ||84||

85atharvāṅgirasī hyeṣā śrutīnāmuttamā śrutiḥ
|
avicāryaiva kāryaiṣā śreyaskāmairnaraiḥ sadā ||85||

86avadhena vadhaḥ prokto yadgurustvamiti prabhuḥ
|
tadbrūhi tvaṃ yanmayoktaṃ dharmarājasya dharmavit ||86||

87vadhaṃ hyayaṃ pāṇḍava dharmarājastvatto'yuktaṃ vetsyate caivameṣaḥ
|
tato'sya pādāvabhivādya paścātsamaṃ brūyāḥ sāntvayitvā ca pārtham ||87||

88bhrātā prājñastava kopaṃ na jātu kuryādrājā dharmamavekṣya cāpi
|
mukto'nṛtādbhrātṛvadhācca pārtha hṛṣṭaḥ karṇaṃ tvaṃ jahi sūtaputram ||88||
