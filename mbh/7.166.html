Mahābhārata 7.166 (Droṇaparvan)

1sañjaya uvāca|
bhūristu samare rājañśaineyaṃ rathināṃ varam
|
āpatantamapāsedhatprayāṇādiva kuñjaram||1||

2athainaṃ sātyakiḥ kruddhaḥ pañcabhirniśitaiḥ śaraiḥ
|
vivyādha hṛdaye tasya prāstravattasya śoṇitam||2||

3tathaiva kauravo yuddhe śaineyaṃ yuddhadurmadam
|
daśabhirniśitaistīkṣṇairavidhyata bhujāntare||3||

4tāvanyonyaṃ mahārāja tatakṣāte śarairbhṛśam
|
krodhasaṃraktanayanau krodhādvisphārya kārmuke||4||

5tayorāsīnmahārāja śastravṛṣṭiḥ sudāruṇā
|
kruddhayoḥ sāyakamucoryamāntakanikāśayoḥ||5||

6tāvanyonyaṃ śarai rājansaṃchādya samavasthitau
|
muhūrtaṃ caiva tadyuddhaṃ samarūpamivābhavat||6||

7tataḥ kruddho mahārāja śaineyaḥ prahasanniva
|
dhanuściccheda samare kauravyasya mahātmanaḥ||7||

8athainaṃ chinnadhanvānaṃ navabhirniśitaiḥ śaraiḥ
|
vivyādha hṛdaye tūrṇaṃ tiṣṭha tiṣṭheti cābravīt||8||

9so'tividdho balavatā śatruṇā śatrutāpanaḥ
|
dhanuranyatsamādāya sātvataṃ pratyavidhyata||9||

10sa viddhvā sātvataṃ bāṇaistribhireva viśāṃpate
|
dhanuściccheda bhallena sutīkṣṇena hasanniva||10||

11chinnadhanvā mahārāja sātyakiḥ krodhamūrcchitaḥ
|
prajahāra mahāvegāṃ śaktiṃ tasya mahorasi||11||

12sa tu śaktyā vibhinnāṅgo nipapāta rathottamāt
|
lohitāṅga ivākāśāddīptaraśmiryadṛcchayā||12||

13taṃ tu dṛṣṭvā hataṃ śūramaśvatthāmā mahārathaḥ
|
abhyadhāvata vegena śaineyaṃ prati saṃyuge||13||

14tiṣṭha tiṣṭheti cābhāṣya śaineyaṃ sa narādhipa
|
abhyavarṣaccharaugheṇa meruṃ vṛṣṭyā yathāmbudaḥ||14||

15tamāpatantaṃ saṃrabdhaṃ śaineyasya rathaṃ prati
|
ghaṭotkaco'bravīdrājannādaṃ muktvā mahārathaḥ||15||

16tiṣṭha tiṣṭha na me jīvandroṇaputra gamiṣyasi
|
eṣa tvāṃ nihaniṣyāmi mahiṣaṃ ṣaṇmukho yathā||16||

17yuddhaśraddhāmahaṃ te'dya vineṣyāmi raṇājire
|
ityuktvā krodhatāmrākṣo rākṣasaḥ paravīrahā||17||

18drauṇimabhyadravatkruddho gajendramiva kesarī
|
rathākṣamātrairiṣubhirabhyavarṣaddhaṭotkacaḥ||18||

19rathināmṛṣabhaṃ drauṇiṃ dhārābhiriva toyadaḥ
|
śaravṛṣṭiṃ tu tāṃ prāptāṃ śarairāśīviṣopamaiḥ||19||

20śātayāmāsa samare tarasā drauṇirutsmayan
|
tataḥ śaraśataistīkṣṇairmarmabhedibhirāśugaiḥ||20||

21samācinodrākṣasendraṃ ghaṭotkacamarindamam
|
sa śarairācitastena rākṣaso raṇamūrdhani||21||

22vyakāśata mahārāja śvāvicchalalato yathā
|
tataḥ krodhasamāviṣṭo bhaimaseniḥ pratāpavān||22||

23śarairavacakartograirdrauṇiṃ vajrāśaniprabhaiḥ
|
kṣuraprairardhacandraiśca nārācaiḥ saśilīmukhaiḥ||23||

24varāhakarṇairnālīkairvikarṇaiścābhyavīvṛṣat
|
tāṃ śastravṛṣṭimatulāṃ vajrāśanisamasvanām||24||

25patantīmupari kruddhā drauṇiravyathitendriyaḥ
|
suduḥsahāṃ śarairghorairdivyāstrapratimantritaiḥ||25||

26vyadhamatsumahātejā mahābhrāṇīva mārutaḥ
|
tatontarikṣe bāṇānāṃ saṃgrāmo'nya ivābhavat||26||

27ghorarūpo mahārāja yodhānāṃ harṣavardhanaḥ
|
tato'strasaṃgharṣakṛtairvisphuliṅgaiḥ samantataḥ||27||

28babhau niśāmukhe vyoma khadyotairiva saṃvṛtam
|
sa mārgaṇagaṇairdroṇirdiśaḥ pracchādya sarvataḥ||28||

29priyārthaṃ tava putrāṇāṃ rākṣasaṃ samavākirat
|
tataḥ pravavṛte yuddhaṃ drauṇirākṣasayormṛdhe||29||

30vigāḍhe rajanīmadhye śakraprahlādayoriva
|
tato ghaṭotkaco bāṇairdaśabhirdrauṇimāhave||30||

31jaghānorasi saṃkruddhaḥ kālajvalanasannibhaiḥ
|
sa tairabhyāyatairviddho rākṣasena mahābalaḥ||31||

32cacāla samare drauṇirvātanunna iva drumaḥ
|
sa mohamanusaṃprāpto dhvajayaṣṭiṃ samāśritaḥ||32||

33tato hāhākṛtaṃ sainyaṃ tava sarvaṃ janādhipa
|
hataṃ sma menire sarve tāvakāstaṃ viśāṃpate||33||

34taṃ tu dṛṣṭvā tathāvasthamaśvatthāmānamāhave
|
pañcālāḥ sṛñjayāścaiva siṃhanādaṃ pracakrire||34||

35pratilabhya tataḥ saṃjñāmaśvatthāmā mahābalaḥ
|
dhanuḥ prapīḍya vāmena kareṇāmitrakarśanaḥ||35||

36mumocākarṇapūrṇena dhanuṣā śaramuttamam
|
yamadaṇḍopamaṃ ghoramuddiśyāśu ghaṭotkacam||36||

37sa bhittvā hṛdayaṃ tasya rākṣasasya śarottamaḥ
|
viveśa vasudhāmugraḥ sapuṃkhaḥ pṛthivīpate||37||

38so'tividdho mahārāja rathopastha upāviśat
|
rākṣasendraḥ subalavāndrauṇinā raṇaśālinā||38||

39dṛṣṭvā vimūḍhaṃ haiḍimbaṃ sārathistu raṇājirāt
|
drauṇeḥ sakāśātsaṃbhrāntastvapaninye tvarānvitaḥ||39||

40tathā tu samare viddhvā rākṣasendraṃ ghaṭotkacam
|
nanāda sumahānādaṃ droṇaputro mahārathaḥ||40||

41pūjitastava putraiśca sarvayodhaiśca bhārata
|
vapuṣā'tiprajajvāla madhyāhna iva bhāskaraḥ||41||

42bhīmasenaṃ tu yudhyantaṃ bhāradvājarathaṃ prati
|
svayaṃ duryodhano rājā pratyavidhyacchitaiḥ śaraiḥ||42||

43taṃ bhīmaseno daśabhiḥ śarairvivyādha māriṣa
|
duryodhano'pi viṃśatyā śarāṇāṃ pratyavidhyata||43||

44tau sāyakairavacchinnāvadṛśyetāṃ raṇājire
|
meghajālasamācchannau nabhasīvendubhāskarau||44||

45atha duryodhano rājā bhīmaṃ vivyādha patribhiḥ
|
pañcabhirbharataśreṣṭha tiṣṭhatiṣṭheti cābravīt||45||

46tasya bhīmo dhanuśchittvā dhvajaṃ ca daśabhiḥ śaraiḥ
|
vivyādha kauravaśreṣṭhaṃ navatyā nataparvaṇām||46||

47tato duryodhanaḥ kruddho dhanuranyanmahattaram
|
gṛhītvā bharataśreṣṭho bhīmasenaṃ śitaiḥ śaraiḥ
|
apīḍayadraṇamukhe paśyatāṃ sarvadhanvinām||47||

48tānnihatya śarānbhīmo duryodhanadhanuścyutān
|
kauravaṃ pañcaviṃśatyā kṣudrakāṇāṃ samārpayat||48||

49duryodhanastu saṃkruddho bhīmasenasya māriṣa
|
kṣurapreṇa dhanuśchittvā daśabhiḥ pratyavidhyata||49||

50athānyaddhanurādāya bhīmaseno mahābalaḥ
|
vivyādha nṛpatiṃ tūrṇaṃ saptabhirniśitaiḥ śaraiḥ||50||

51tadapyasya dhanuḥ kṣipraṃ ciccheda laghuhastavat
|
dvitīyaṃ ca tṛtīyaṃ ca caturthaṃ pañcamaṃ tathā||51||

52āttamāttaṃ mahārāja bhīmasya dhanurācchinat
|
tava putro mahārāja jitakāśī madotkaṭaḥ||52||

53sa tathā bhidyamāneṣu kārmukeṣu punaḥ punaḥ
|
śaktiṃ cikṣepa samare sarvapārasavīṃ śubhām||53||

54mṛtyoriva svasāraṃ hi dīptāṃ vahniśikhāmiva
|
sīmantamiva kurvantīṃ nabhaso'gnisamaprabhām||54||

55aprāptāmeva tāṃ śaktiṃ tridhā ciccheda kauravaḥ
|
paśyataḥ sarvalokasya bhīmasya ca mahātmanaḥ||55||

56tato bhīmo mahārāja gadāṃ gurvīṃ mahāprabhām
|
cikṣepāvidhya vegena duryodhanarathaṃ prati||56||

57tataḥ sā sahasā vāhāṃstava putrasya saṃyuge
|
sārathiṃ ca gadā gurvī mamardāsya rathaṃ punaḥ||57||

58putrastu tava rājendra bhīmādbhītaḥ praṇaśya ca
|
āruroha rathaṃ cānyaṃ nandakasya mahātmanaḥ||58||

59tato bhīmo hataṃ matvā tava putraṃ mahāratham
|
siṃhanādaṃ mahaccakre tarjayanniśi kauravān||59||

60tāvakāḥ sainikāścāpi menire nihataṃ nṛpam
|
tato'ticukruśuḥ sarve te hāheti samantataḥ||60||

61teṣāṃ tu ninadaṃ śrutvā trastānāṃ sarvayodhinām
|
bhīmasenasya nādaṃ ca śrutvā rājan mahātmanaḥ||61||

62tato yudhiṣṭhiro rājā hataṃ matvā suyodhanam
|
abhyavartata vegena yatra pārtho vṛkodaraḥ||62||

63pañcālāḥ kekayā matsyāḥ sṛñjayāśca viśāṃpate
|
sarvodyogenābhijagmurdroṇameva yuyutsayā||63||

64tatrāsītsumahadyuddhaṃ droṇasyātha paraiḥ saha
|
ghore tamasi magnānāṃ nighnatāmitaretaram||64||
