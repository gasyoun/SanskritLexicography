Mahābhārata 4.32 (Virāṭaparvan)

1vaiśampāyana uvāca|niryāya nagarācchūrā vyūḍhānīkāḥ prahāriṇaḥ
|
trigartānaspṛśan matsyāḥ sūrye pariṇate sati ||1||

2te trigartāśca matsyāśca saṃrabdhā yuddhadurmadāḥ
|
anyonyamabhigarjanto goṣu gṛddhā mahābalāḥ ||2||

3bhīmāśca mattamātaṅgāstomarāṅkuśanoditāḥ
|
grāmaṇīyaiḥ samārūḍhāḥ kuśalairhastisādibhiḥ ||3||

4teṣāṃ samāgamo ghorastumulo lomaharṣaṇaḥ
|
ghnatāṃ parasparaṃ rājan yamarāṣṭravivardhanaḥ ||4||

5devāsurasamo rājannāsīt sūrye'valambati
|
padātirathanāgendrahayārohabalaughavān ||5||

6anyonyamabhyāpatatāṃ nighnatāṃ cetaretaram
|
udatiṣṭhadrajo bhaumaṃ na prājñāyata kiñcana ||6||

7pakṣiṇaścāpatan bhūmau sainyena rajasā''vṛtāḥ
|
iṣubhirvyatisarpadbhirādityo'ntaradhīyata ||7||

8khadyotairiva saṃyuktamantarikṣaṃ vyarājata
|
rukmapṛṣṭhāni cāpāni vyatiṣiktāni dhanvinām ||8||

9patatāṃ lokavīrāṇāṃ savyadakṣiṇamasyatām
|
rathā rathaiḥ samājagmuḥ pādātaiśca padātayaḥ ||9||

10sādinaḥ sādibhiścaiva gajaiścāpi mahāgajāḥ
|
asibhiḥ paṭṭiśaiḥ prāsaiḥ śaktibhistomarairapi ||10||

11saṃrabdhāḥ samare rājannijaghnuritaretaram
|
nighnantaḥ samare'nyonyaṃ śūrāḥ parighabāhavaḥ ||11||

12na śekurabhisaṃrabdhāḥ śūrān kartuṃ parāṅmukhān
|
kṛttottaroṣṭhaṃ sunasaṃ kṛttakeśamalaṅkṛtam ||12||

13adṛśyata śiraśchinnaṃ rajodhvastaṃ sakuṇḍalam
|
adṛśyaṃstatra gātrāṇi śaraiśchinnāni bhāgaśaḥ ||13||

14śālaskandhanikāśāni kṣatriyāṇāṃ mahāmṛdhe
|
nāgabhoganikāśaiśca bāhubhiścanda‌nokṣitaiḥ ||14||

15āstīrṇā vasudhā bhāti śirobhiśca sakuṇḍalaiḥ
|
rathināṃ rathibhiścātra saṃprahāro'bhyavartata ||15||

16sādibhiḥ sādināṃ cāpi padātīnāṃ padātibhiḥ
|
upāśāmyadrajo bhaumaṃ rudhireṇa prasarpatā ||16||

17kaśmalaṃ cāviśaddhoraṃ nirmaryādamavartata
|
upāviśan garutmantaḥ śarairgāḍhaṃ pravejitāḥ
|
antarikṣe gatiryeṣāṃ darśanaṃ cāpyarudhyata ||17||

18te ghnantaḥ samare'nyonyaṃ śūrāḥ parighabāhavaḥ
|
na śekurabhisaṃrabdhāḥ śūrān kartuṃ parāṅmukhān ||18||

19śatānīkaḥ śataṃ hatvā viśālākṣaścatuḥśatam
|
praviṣṭau mahatīṃ senāṃ trigartānāṃ mahārathau ||19||

20tau praviṣṭau mahāsenāṃ balavantau manasvinau
|
ārcchetāṃ bāhusaṃrabdhau keśākeśi rathārathi ||20||

21lakṣayitvā trigartānāṃ tau praviṣṭau rathavrajam
|
agrataḥ sūryadattaśca madirākṣaśca pṛṣṭhataḥ ||21||

22virāṭastatra saṃgrāme hatvā pañcaśatān rathān
|
hayānāṃ ca śatānyaṣṭau hatvā pañca mahārathān ||22||

23caran sa vividhān mārgān rathena rathasattamaḥ
|
trigartānāṃ suśarmāṇamārcchadrukmarathaṃ raṇe ||23||

24tau vyavāharatāṃ tatra mahātmānau mahābalau
|
anyonyamabhigarjantau goṣṭheṣu vṛṣabhāviva ||24||

25tato rājā trigartānāṃ suśarmā yuddhadurmadaḥ
|
matsyaṃ samīyādrājānaṃ dvairathena nararṣabhaḥ ||25||

26tato rathābhyāṃ rathinau vyatīyaturamarṣaṇau
|
śarān vyasṛjatāṃ śīghraṃ toyadhārā ghanā iva ||26||

27anyonyaṃ cāpi saṃrabdhau viceraturamarṣaṇau
|
kṛtāstrau niśitairbāṇairasiśaktigadābhṛtau ||27||

28tato rājā suśarmāṇaṃ vivyādha daśabhiḥ śaraiḥ
|
pañcabhiḥ pañcabhiścāsya vivyādha caturo hayān ||28||

29tathaiva matsyarājānaṃ suśarmā yuddhadurmadaḥ
|
pañcāśadbhiḥ śitairbāṇairvivyādha paramāstravit ||29||

30tataḥ sainyaṃ mahārāja matsyarājasuśarmaṇoḥ
|
nābhyajānattadā'nyonyaṃ sainyena rajasā''vṛtam ||30||
