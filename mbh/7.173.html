Mahābhārata 7.173 (Droṇaparvan)

1sañjaya uvāca|
tataḥ karṇo raṇe dṛṣṭvā pārṣataṃ paravīrahā
|
ājaghānorasi śarairdaśabhirmarmabhedibhiḥ||1||

2prativivyādha taṃ tūrṇaṃ dhṛṣṭadyumno'pi māriṣa
|
daśabhiḥ sāyakairhṛṣṭastiṣṭha tiṣṭheti cābravīt||2||

3tāvanyonyaṃ śaraiḥ saṃkhye saṃchādya sumahārathaiḥ
|
punaḥ pūrṇāyatotsṛṣṭairvivyadhāte parasparam||3||

4tataḥ pañcālamukhyasya dhṛṣṭadyumnasya saṃyuge
|
sārathiṃ caturaścāśvānkarṇo vivyādha sāyakaiḥ||4||

5kārmukapravaraṃ cāpi praciccheda śitaiḥ śaraiḥ
|
sārathiṃ cāsya bhallena rathanīḍādapātayat||5||

6dhṛṣṭadyumnastu viratho hatāśvo hatasārathiḥ
|
gṛhītvā parighaṃ ghoraṃ karṇasyāśvānapīpibat||6||

7viddhaśca bahubhistena śarairāśīviṣopamaiḥ
|
tato yudhiṣṭhirānīkaṃ padbhyāmevānvapadyata||7||

8āruroha rathaṃ cāpi sahadevasya māriṣa
|
prayātukāmaḥ karṇāya vārito dharmasūnunā||8||

9karṇastu sumahātejāḥ siṃhanādavimiśritam
|
dhanuḥśabdaṃ mahaccakre dadhmau tāreṇa cāṃbujam||9||

10dṛṣṭvā vinirjitaṃ yuddhe pārṣataṃ te mahārathāḥ
|
amarṣavaśamāpannāḥ pañcālāḥ sahasomakāḥ||10||

11sūtaputravadhārthāya śastrāṇyādāya sarvaśaḥ
|
prayayuḥ karṇamuddiśya mṛtyuṃ kṛtvā nivartanam||11||

12karṇasyāpi rathe vāhānanyān suto'bhyayojayat
|
śaṅkhavarṇānmahāvegān saindhavānsādhuvāhinaḥ||12||

13labdhalakṣastu rādheyaḥ pañcālānāṃ mahārathān
|
abhyapīḍayadāyastaḥ śarairmegha ivācalam||13||

14sā pīḍyamānā karṇena pañcālānāṃ mahācamūḥ
|
saṃprādravatsusaṃtrastā siṃhenevārditā mṛgī||14||

15patitāsturagebhyaśca gajebhyaśca mahītale
|
rathebhyaśca narāstūrṇamadṛśyanta tatastataḥ||15||

16dhāvamānasya yodhasya kṣurapraiḥ sa mahāmṛdhe
|
bāhū ciccheda vai karṇaḥ śiraścaiva sakuṇḍalam||16||

17ūrū ciccheda cānyasya gajasthasya viśāṃpate
|
vājipṛṣṭhagatasyāpi bhūmiṣṭhasya ca māriṣa||17||

18nājñāsiṣurdhāvamānā bahavaśca mahārathāḥ
|
saṃchinnānyātmagātrāṇi vāhanāni ca saṃyuge||18||

19te vadhyamānāḥ samare pañcālāḥ sṛñjayaiḥ saha
|
tṛṇapraspandanāccāpi sūtaputraṃ sma monare||19||

20api svaṃ samare yodhaṃ dhāvamānaṃ vicetasam
|
karṇamevābhyamanyanta tato bhītā dravanti te||20||

21tānyanīkāni bhagnāni dravamāṇāni bhārata
|
abhyadravaddrutaṃ karṇaḥ pṛṣṭhato vikirañcharān||21||

22avekṣyamāṇāstvanyonyaṃ susaṃmūḍhā vicetasaḥ
|
nāśaknuvannavasthātuṃ kālyamānā mahātmanā||22||

23karṇenābhyāhatā rājanpañcālāḥ parameṣubhiḥ
|
droṇena ca diśaḥ sarvā vīkṣyamāṇāḥ pradudruvuḥ||23||

24tato yudhiṣṭiro rājā svasainyaṃ prekṣya vidrutam
|
apayāne manaḥ kṛtvā phālgunaṃ vākyamabravīt||24||

25paśya karṇaṃ maheṣvāsaṃ dhanuṣpāṇimavasthitam
|
niśīthe dāruṇe kāle tapantamiva bhāskaram||25||

26karṇasāyakanunnānāṃ krośatāmeṣa niḥsvanaḥ
|
aniśaṃ śrūyate pārtha tvadbandhūnāmanāthavat||26||

27yathā visṛjataścāsya saṃdadhānasya cāśugān
|
paśyāmi nāntaraṃ pārtha kṣapayiṣyati no dhruvam||27||

28yadatrānantaraṃ kāryaṃ prāptakālaṃ ca paśyasi
|
karṇasya vadhasaṃyuktaṃ tatkuruṣva dhanañjaya||28||

29evamukto mahārāja pārthaḥ kṛṣṇamathābravīt
|
bhītaḥ kuntīsuto rājā rādheyasyādya vikramāt||29||

30evaṃgate prāptakālaṃ karṇānīke punaḥ punaḥ
|
bhavān vyavasyatu kṣipraṃ dravate hi varūthinī||30||

31droṇasāyakanunnānāṃ bhagnānāṃ madhusūdana
|
karṇena trāsyamānānāmavasthānaṃ na vidyate||31||

32paśyāmi ca tathā karṇaṃ vicarantamabhītavat
|
dravamāṇān rathodārānkirantaṃ niśitaiḥ śaraiḥ||32||

33nainaṃ śakṣyāmi saṃsoḍhuṃ carantaṃ raṇamūrdhani
|
pratyakṣaṃ vṛṣṇiśārdūla pādasparśamivoragaḥ||33||

34sa bhavāṃstatra yātvāśu yatra karṇo mahārathaḥ
|
ahamenaṃ haniṣyāmi māṃ vaiṣa madhusūdana||34||

35śrīvāsudeva uvāca|
paśyāmi karṇaṃ kaunteya devarājamivāhave
|
vicarantaṃ naravyāghramatimānuṣavikramam||35||

36naitasyānyo'sti saṃgrāme pratyudyātā dhanañjaya
|
ṛte tvāṃ puruṣavyāghra rākṣasādvā ghaṭotkacāt||36||

37na tu tāvadahaṃ manye prāptakālaṃ tavānagha
|
samāgamaṃ mahābāho sūtaputreṇa saṃyuge||37||

38dīpyamānā maholkeva tiṣṭhatyasya hi vāsavī
|
tvadarthaṃ hi mahābāho sūtaputreṇa saṃyuge||38||

39rakṣyate śaktireṣā hi raudraṃ rūpaṃ bibharti ca
|
ghaṭotkacastu rādheyaṃ pratyudyātu mahābalaḥ||39||

40sa hi bhīmena balinā jātaḥ suraparākramaḥ
|
tasminnastrāṇi divyāni rākṣasānyāsurāṇi ca||40||

41satataṃ cānurakto vo hitaiṣī ca ghaṭotkacaḥ
|
vijeṣyati raṇe karṇamiti me nātra saṃśayaḥ||41||

42evamukto mahābāhuḥ pārthaḥ puṣkaralocanaḥ
|
ājuhāvātha tadrakṣastaccāsītprāduragrataḥ||42||

43kavacī saśaraḥ khaḍgī sadhanvā ca viśāṃpate
|
abhivādya tataḥ kṛṣṇaṃ pāṇḍavaṃ ca dhanañjayam
|
abravīcca tadā kṛṣṇamayamasmyanuśādhi mām||43||

44tatastaṃ meghasaṃkāśaṃ dīptāsyaṃ dīptakuṇḍalam
|
abhyabhāṣata haiḍimbiṃ dāśārhaḥ prahasanniva||44||

45śrīvāsudeva uvāca|
ghaṭotkaca vijānīhi yattvāṃ vakṣyāmi putraka
|
prāpto vikramakālo'yaṃ tava nānyasya kasyacit||45||

46sa bhavānmajjamānānāṃ bandhūnāṃ tvaṃ plavo bhava
|
vividhāni tavāstrāṇi santi māyā ca rākṣasī||46||

47paśya karṇena haiḍiṃbe pāṇḍavānāmanīkinī
|
kālyamānā yathā gāvaḥ pālena raṇamūrdhani||47||

48eṣa karṇo maheṣvāso matimān dṛḍhavikramaḥ
|
pāṇḍavānāmanīkeṣu nihanti kṣatriyarṣabhān||48||

49kirantaḥ śaravarṣāṇi mahānti dṛḍhadhanvinaḥ
|
na śaknuvantyavasthātuṃ pīḍyamānāḥ śarārciṣā||49||

50niśīthe sūtaputreṇa śaravarṣeṇa pīḍitāḥ
|
ete dravanti pañcālāḥ siṃhenevārditā mṛgāḥ||50||

51etasyaivaṃ pravṛddhasya sūtaputrasya saṃyuge
|
niṣeddhā vidyate nānyastvāmṛte bhīmavikrama||51||

52sa tvaṃ kuru mahābāho karma yuktamihātmanaḥ
|
mātulānāṃ pitṝṇāṃ ca tejaso'strabalasya ca||52||

53etadarthe hi haiḍiṃbe putrānicchanti mānavāḥ
|
kathaṃ nastārayedduḥkhātsa tvaṃ tāraya bāndhavān||53||

54icchanti pitaraḥ putrān svārthahetorghaṭotkaca
|
ihalokātpare loke tārayiṣyanti ye hitāḥ||54||

55tava hyatra balaṃ bhīmaṃ māyāśca tava dustarāḥ
|
saṃgrāme yudhyamānasya satataṃ bhīmanandana||55||

56pāṇḍavānāṃ prabhagnānāṃ karṇena niśi sāyakaiḥ
|
majjatāṃ dhārtarāṣṭreṣu bhava pāraṃ paraṃtapa||56||

57rātrau hi rākṣasā bhūyo bhavantyamitavikramāḥ
|
balavantaḥ sudurdharṣāḥ śūrā vikrāntacāriṇaḥ||57||

58jahi karṇaṃ maheṣvāsaṃ niśīthe māyayā raṇe
|
pārthā droṇaṃ vadhiṣyanti dhṛṣṭadyumnapurogamāḥ||58||

59sañjaya uvāca|
keśavasya vacaḥ śrutvā bībhatsurapi rākṣasam
|
abhyabhāṣataṃ kauravya ghaṭotkacamarindamam||59||

60ghaṭotkaca bhavāṃścaiva dīrghabāhuśca sātyakiḥ
|
matau me sarvasainyeṣu bhīmasenaśca pāṇḍavaḥ||60||

61tadbhavānyātu karṇena dvairathaṃ yudhyatāṃ niśi
|
sātyakiḥ pṛṣṭhagopaste bhaviṣyati mahārathaḥ||61||

62jahi karṇaṃ raṇe śūraṃ sātvatena sahāyavān
|
yathendrastārakaṃ pūrvaṃ skandena saha jaghnivān||62||

63ghaṭotkaca uvāca|
alamevāsmi karṇāya droṇāyālaṃ ca bhārata
|
anyeṣāṃ kṣatriyāṇāṃ ca kṛtāstrāṇāṃ mahātmanām||63||

64adya dāsyāmi saṃgrāmaṃ sūtaputrāya taṃ niśi
|
yaṃ janāḥ saṃpravakṣyanti yāvadbhūmirdhariṣyati||64||

65na cātra śūrānmokṣyāmi na bhītānna kṛtāñjalīm
|
sarvāneva vadhiṣyāmi rākṣasaṃ dharmamāsthitaḥ||65||

66sañjaya uvāca|
evamuktvā mahābāhurhaiḍimbirvaravīrahā
|
abhyayāttamule karṇaṃ tava sainyaṃ vibhīṣayan||66||

67tamāpatantaṃ saṃkruddhaṃ dīptāsyaṃ dīptamūrdhajam
|
prahasanpuruṣavyāghraḥ pratijagrāha sūtajaḥ||67||

68tayoḥ samabhavadyuddhaṃ karṇarākṣasayormṛdhe
|
garjato rājaśārdūla śakraprahrāda‌yoriva||68||
