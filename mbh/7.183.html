Mahābhārata 7.183 (Droṇaparvan)

1dhṛtarāṣṭra uvāca|
karṇaduryodhanādīnāṃ śakuneḥ saubalasya ca
|
apanītaṃ mahattāta tava caiva viśeṣataḥ||1||

2yadi jānītha tāṃ śaktimekaghnīṃ satataṃ raṇe
|
anivāryāmasahyāṃ ca devairapi savāsavaiḥ||2||

3sā kimarthaṃ tu karṇena pravṛtte samare purā
|
na devakīsute muktā phālgune vā'pi sañjaya||3||

4sañjaya uvāca|
saṃgrāmādvinivṛttānāṃ sarveṣāṃ no viśāṃpate
|
rātrau kurukulaśreṣṭha mantro'yaṃ samajāyata||4||

5prabhātamātre śvobhūte keśavāyārjunāya vā
|
śaktireṣā hi moktavyā karṇa karṇeti nityaśaḥ||5||

6tataḥ prabhātasamaye rājankarṇasya daivataiḥ
|
anyeṣāṃ caiva yodhānāṃ sā buddhirnāśyate punaḥ||6||

7daivameva paraṃ manye yatkarṇo hastasaṃsthayā
|
na jaghāna raṇe pārthaṃ kṛṣṇaṃ vā devakīsutam||7||

8tasya hastasthitā śaktiḥ kālarātririvodyatā
|
daivopahatabuddhitvānna tāṃ karṇo vimuktavān||8||

9kṛṣṇe vā devakīputre mohito devamāyayā
|
pārthe vā śakrakalpe vai vadhārthaṃ vāsavīṃ prabhā||9||

10dhṛtarāṣṭra uvāca|
daivenopahatā yūyaṃ svabuddhyā keśavasya ca
|
gatā hi vāsavī hatvā tṛṇabhūtaṃ ghaṭotkacam||10||

11karṇaśca mama putrāśca sarve cānye ca pārthivāḥ
|
tena vai duṣpraṇītena gatā vaivasvatakṣayam||11||

12bhūya eva tu me śaṃsa yathā yuddhamavartata
|
kurūṇāṃ pāṇḍavānāṃ ca haiḍimbe nihate tadā||12||

13ye ca te'bhyadravandroṇaṃ vyūḍhānīkāḥ prahāriṇaḥ
|
sṛñjayāḥ saha pañcālaiste'pyakurvankathaṃ raṇam||13||

14saumadattervadhāddroṇamāyāntaṃ saindhavasya ca
|
amarṣājjīvitaṃ tyaktvā gāhamānaṃ varūthinīm||14||

15jṛmbhamāṇamiva vyāghraṃ vyāttānanamivāntakam
|
kathaṃ pratyudyayau droṇamasyantaṃ pāṇḍusṛñjayāḥ||15||

16ācāryaṃ ye ca te'rakṣan duryodhanapurogamāḥ
|
drauṇi-karṇa-kṛpāstāta te vā'kurvankimāhave||16||

17bhāradvājaṃ jighāṃsantau savyasācivṛkodarau
|
samārcchanmāmakā yuddhe kathaṃ sañjaya śaṃsa me||17||

18sindhurājavadheneme ghaṭotkacavadhena te
|
amarpitaḥ susaṃkruddhā raṇaṃ cakruḥ kathaṃ niśi||18||

19sañjaya uvāca|
hate ghaṭotkace rājankarṇena niśi rākṣase
|
praṇadatsu ca hṛṣṭeṣu tāvakeṣu yuyutsuṣu||19||

20āpatatsu ca vegena vadhyamāne bale'pi ca
|
vigāḍhāyāṃ rajanyāṃ ca rājā dainyaṃ paraṃ gataḥ||20||

21abravīcca mahābāhurbhīmasenamidaṃ vacaḥ
|
āvāraya mahābāho dhārtarāṣṭrasya vāhinīm||21||

22haiḍiṃbeścaiva ghātena moho māmāviśanmahān
|
evaṃ bhīmaṃ samādiśya svarathe samupāviśat||22||

23aśrupūrṇamukho rājā niḥśvasaṃśca punaḥ punaḥ
|
kaśmalaṃ prāviśaddhoraṃ dṛṣṭvā karṇasya vikramam||23||

24taṃ tathā vyathitaṃ dṛṣṭvā kṛṣṇo vacanamabravīt
|
mā vyathāṃ kuru kaunteya naitattvayyupapadyate||24||

25vaiklavyaṃ bharataśreṣṭha yathā prākṛtapūruṣe
|
uttiṣṭha rājanyuddhyasva vaha gurvīṃ dhuraṃ vibho||25||

26tvayi vaiklavyamāpanne saṃśayo vijaye bhavet
|
śrutvā kṛṣṇasya vacanaṃ dharmarājo yudhiṣṭhiraḥ||26||

27vimṛjya netre pāṇibhyāṃ kṛṣṇaṃ vacanamabravīt
|
viditā me mahābāho dharmāṇāṃ paramā gatiḥ||27||

28brahmahatyā phalaṃ tasya yaḥ kṛtaṃ nā'vabudhyate
|
asmākaṃ hi vanasthānāṃ haiḍimbena mahātmanā||28||

29bālenāpi satā tena kṛtaṃ sāhyaṃ janārdana
|
astrahetorgataṃ jñātvā pāṇḍavaṃ śvetavāhanam||29||

30asau kṛṣṇa maheṣvāsaḥ kāmyake māmupasthitaḥ
|
uṣitaśca sahāsmābhiryāvannāsīddhanañjayaḥ||30||

31gandhamādanayātrāyāṃ durgebhyaśca sma tāritāḥ
|
pāñcālī ca pariśrāntā pṛṣṭhenoḍhā mahātmanā||31||

32ārambhāccaiva yuddhānāṃ yadeṣa kṛtavān prabho
|
madarthe duṣkaraṃ karma kṛtaṃ tena mahāhave||32||

33svabhāvādyā ca me prītiḥ sahadeve janārdana
|
saiva me paramā pratiḥ rākṣasendre ghaṭotkace||33||

34bhaktaśca me mahābāhuḥ priyo'syāhaṃ priyaśca me
|
tena vindāmi vārṣṇeya kaśmalaṃ śokatāpitaḥ||34||

35paśya sainyāni vārṣṇeya drāvyamāṇāni kauravaiḥ
|
droṇakarṇau tu saṃyattau paśya yuddhe mahārathau||35||

36niśīthe pāṇḍavaṃ sainyametatsainyapramarditam
|
gajābhyāmiva mattābhyāṃ yathā nalavanaṃ mahat||36||

37anādṛtya balaṃ bāhvorbhīmasenasya mādhava
|
citrāstratāṃ ca pārthasya vikramanti sma kauravāḥ||37||

38eṣa droṇaśca karṇaśca rājā caiva suyodhanaḥ
|
nihatya rākṣasaṃ yuddhe hṛṣṭāḥ nardanti saṃyuge||38||

39kathaṃ vā'smāsu jīvatsu tvayi caiva janārdana
|
haiḍimbiḥ prāptavānmṛtyuṃ sūtaputreṇa saṅgataḥ||39||

40kadarthī kṛtya naḥ sarvānpaśyataḥ savyasācinaḥ
|
nihato rākṣasaḥ kṛṣṇa bhaimasenirmahābalaḥ||40||

41yadā'bhimanyurnihato dhārtarāṣṭrairdurātmabhiḥ
|
nāsīttatra raṇe kṛṣṇa savyasācī mahārathaḥ||41||

42niruddhāśca vayaṃ sarve saindhavena durātmanā
|
nimittamabhavaddroṇaḥ sa putrastatra karmaṇi||42||

43upadiṣṭo vadhopāyaḥ karṇasya guruṇā svayam
|
vyāyacchataśca khaḍgena dvidhā khaḍgaṃ cakāra ha||43||

44vyasane vartamānasya kṛtavarmā nṛśaṃsavat
|
aśvāñjaghāna sahasā tathobhau pārṣṇisārathī||44||

45tathetare maheṣvāsāḥ saubhadraṃ yuddhyapātayan
|
alpe ca kāraṇe kṛṣṇa hato gāṇḍīvadhanvanā||45||

46saindhavo yādadvaśreṣṭha tacca nātipriyaṃ mama
|
yadi śatruvadho nyāyyo bhavetkartuṃ hi pāṃḍavaiḥ||46||

47karṇadroṇau raṇe pūrvaṃ hantavyāviti me matiḥ
|
etau hi mūlaṃ duḥkhānāmasmākaṃ puruṣarṣabha||47||

48etau raṇe samāsādya samāśvastaḥ suyodhanaḥ
|
yatra vadhyo bhaveddroṇaḥ sūtaputraśca sānugaḥ||48||

49tatrāvadhīnmahābāhuḥ saindhavaṃ dūravāsinam
|
avaśyaṃ tu mayā kāryaḥ sūtaputrasya nigrahaḥ||49||

50tato yāsyāmyahaṃ vīraḥ svayaṃ karṇajighāṃsayā
|
bhīmaseno mahābāhurdroṇānīkena saṅgataḥ||50||

51evamuktvā yayau tūrṇaṃ tvaramāṇo yudhiṣṭhiraḥ
|
sa visphārya mahaccāpaṃ śaṃkhaṃ pradhmāpya bhairavam||51||

52tato rathasahasreṇa gajānāṃ ca śataistribhiḥ
|
vājibhiḥ pañcasāhastraiḥ pañcālaiḥ saprabhadrakaiḥ||52||

53vṛtaḥ śikhaṇḍī tvarito rājānaṃ pṛṣṭhato'nvayāt
|
tato bherīḥ samājaghnuḥ śaṃkhāndadhmuśca daṃśitāḥ||53||

54pañcālāḥ pāṇḍavāścaiva yudhiṣṭhirapurogamāḥ
|
tato'bravīnmahābāhurvāsudevo dhanañjayam||54||

55eṣa prayāti tvaritaḥ krodhāviṣṭo yudhiṣṭhiraḥ
|
jighāṃsuḥ sūtaputrasya tasyopekṣā na yujyate||55||

56evamuktvā hṛṣīkeśaḥ śīghramaśvānacodayat
|
dūraṃ prayāntaṃ rājānamanvagacchajjanārdanaḥ||56||

57taṃ dṛṣṭvā sahasā yāntaṃ sūtaputrajighāṃsayā
|
śokopahatasaṃkalpaṃ dahyamānamivāgninā||57||

58abhigamyābravīdvyāso dharmaputraṃ yudhiṣṭhiram
|
vyāsa uvāca|
karṇamāsādya saṃgrāme diṣṭyā jīvati phālgunaḥ||58||

59savyasācivadhākāṃkṣī śaktiṃ rakṣitavān hi saḥ
|
na cāgāddvairathaṃ jiṣṇurdiṣṭyā tena mahāraṇe||59||

60sṛjetāṃ spardhināvetau divyānyastrāṇi sarvaśaḥ
|
vadhyamāneṣu cāstreṣu pīḍitaḥ sūtanandanaḥ||60||

61vāsavīṃ samare śaktiṃ dhruvaṃ muñcedyudhiṣṭhira
|
tato bhavette vyasanaṃ ghoraṃ bharatasattama||61||

62diṣṭyā rakṣo hataṃ yuddhe sūtaputreṇa mānada
|
vāsavīṃ kāraṇaṃ kṛtvā kālenopahato hyasau||62||

63tavaiva kāraṇādrakṣo nihataṃ tāta saṃyuge
|
mā krudho bharataśreṣṭha mā ca śoke manaḥ kṛthāḥ||63||

64prāṇināmiha sarveṣāmeṣā niṣṭhā yudhiṣṭhira
|
bhrātṛbhiḥ sahitaḥ sarvaiḥ pārthivaiśca mahātmabhiḥ||64||

65kauravānsamare rājanpratiyudhyasva bhārata
|
pañcame divase tāta pṛthivī te bhaviṣyati||65||

66nityaṃ ca puruṣavyāghra dharmamevānucintaya
|
ānṛśaṃsyaṃ tapo dānaṃ kṣamāṃ satyaṃ ca pāṇḍava||66||

67sevethāḥ paramaprīto 'yato dharmastato jayaḥ'
|
ityuktvā pāṇḍavaṃ vyāsastatraivāntaradhīyata||67||
