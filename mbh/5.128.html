Mahābhārata 5.128 (Udyogaparvan)

1vaiśampāyana uvāca|
tataḥ praśamya dāśārhaḥ krodhaparyākulekṣaṇaḥ
|
duryodhanamidaṃ vākyamabravīt kurusaṃsadi ||1||

2lapsyase vīraśayanaṃ kāmametadavāpsyasi
|
sthiro bhava sahāmātyo vimardo bhavitā mahān ||2||

3yaccaivaṃ manyase mūḍha na me kaścidvyatikramaḥ
|
pāṇḍaveṣviti tatsarvaṃ nibodhata narādhipāḥ ||3||

4śriyā santapyamānena pāṇḍavānāṃ mahātmanām
|
tvayā durmantritaṃ dyūtaṃ saubalena ca bhārata ||4||

5kathaṃ ca jñātayastāta śreyāṃsaḥ sādhusaṃmatāḥ
|
athānyāyyamupasthātuṃ jihmenājihmacāriṇaḥ ||5||

6akṣadyūtaṃ mahāprājña satāṃ mativināśanam
|
asatāṃ tatra jāyante bhedāśca vyasanāni ca ||6||

7tadidaṃ vyasanaṃ ghoraṃ tvayā dyūtamukhaṃ kṛtam
|
asamīkṣya sadācāraiḥ sārdhaṃ pāpānubandhanaiḥ ||7||

8kaścānyo bhrātṛbhāryāṃ vai viprakartuṃ tathā'rhati
|
ānīya ca sabhāṃ vyaktaṃ yathoktā draupadī tvayā ||8||

9kulīnā śīlasampannā prāṇebhyo'pi garīyasī
|
mahiṣī pāṇḍuputrāṇāṃ tathā vinikṛtā tvayā ||9||

10jānanti kuravaḥ sarve yathoktāḥ kurusaṃsadi
|
duḥśāsanena kauntayāḥ pravrajantaḥ paraṃtapāḥ ||10||

11samyagvṛtteṣvalubdheṣu satataṃ dharmacāriṣu
|
sveṣu bandhuṣu kaḥ sādhuścaredevamasāmpratam ||11||

12nṛśaṃsānāmanāryāṇāṃ puruṣāṇāṃ ca bhāṣaṇam
|
karṇaduḥśāsanābhyāṃ ca tvayā ca bahuśaḥ kṛtam ||12||

13saha mātrā pradagdhuṃ tān bālakān vāraṇāvate
|
āsthitaḥ paramaṃ yatnaṃ na samṛddhaṃ ca tattava ||13||

14ūṣuśca suciraṃ kālaṃ pracchannāḥ pāṇḍavāstadā
|
mātrā sahaikacakrāyāṃ brāhmaṇasya niveśane ||14||

15viṣeṇa sarvabandhaiśca yatitāḥ pāṇḍavāstvayā
|
sarvopāyairvināśāya na samṛddhaṃ ca tattava ||15||

16evaṃbuddhiḥ pāṇḍaveṣu mithyāvṛttiḥ sadā bhavān
|
kathaṃ te nāparādho'sti pāṇḍaveṣu mahātmasu ||16||

17yaccebhyo yācamātebhyaḥ pitryamaṃśaṃ na ditsasi
|
tañca pāpa pradātā'si bhraṣṭaiśvaryo nipātitaḥ ||17||

18kṛtvā bahūnyakāryāṇi pāṇḍaveṣu nṛśaṃsavat
|
mithyāvṛttiranāryaḥ sannadya vipratipadyase ||18||

19mātāpitṛbhyāṃ bhīṣmeṇa droṇena vidureṇa ca
|
śāmyeti muhurukto'si na ca śāmyasi pārthiva ||19||

20śame hi sumahā~llābhastava pārthasya cobhayoḥ
|
na ca rocayase rājan kimanyadbuddhilāghavāt ||20||

21na śarma prāpsyase rājannutkramya suhṛdāṃ vacaḥ
|
adharmyamayaśasyaṃ ca kriyate pārthiva tvayā ||21||

22vaiśampāyana uvāca|
evaṃ bruvati dāśārhe duryodhanamamarṣaṇam
|
duḥśāsana idaṃ vākyamabravīt kurusaṃsadi ||22||

23na cetsandhāsyase rājan svena kāmena pāṇḍavaiḥ
|
baddhvā kila tyāṃ dāsyanti kuntīputrāya kauravāḥ ||23||

24vaikartanaṃ tvāṃ ca māṃ ca trīnetān manujarṣabha
|
pāṇḍavebhyaḥ pradāsyanti bhīṣmo droṇaḥ pitā ca te ||24||

25bhrāturetadvacaḥ śrutvā dhārtarāṣṭraḥ suyodhanaḥ
|
kruddhaḥ prātiṣṭhatotthāya mahānāga iva śvasan ||25||

26viduraṃ dhṛtarāṣṭraṃ ca mahārājaṃ ca bāhlikam
|
kṛpaṃ ca somadattaṃ ca bhīṣmaṃ droṇaṃ janārdanam ||26||

27sarvānetānanādṛtya durmatirnirapatrapaḥ
|
aśiṣṭavadamaryādo mānī mānyāvamānitā ||27||

28taṃ prasthitamabhiprekṣya bhrātaro manujarṣabham
|
anujagmuḥ sahāmātyā rājānaścāpi sarvaśaḥ ||28||

29sabhāyāmutthitaṃ kruddhaṃ prasthitaṃ bhrātṛbhiḥ saha
|
duryodhanamabhiprekṣya bhīṣmaḥ śāntanavo'bravīt ||29||

30dharmārthāvabhisantyajya saṃrambhaṃ yo'numanyate
|
hasanti vyasane tasya durhṛdo na cirādiva ||30||

31durātmā rājaputro'yaṃ dhārtarāṣṭro'nupāyakṛt
|
mithyābhimānī rājyasya krodhalobhavaśānugaḥ ||31||

32kālapakvamidaṃ manye sarvaṃ kṣatraṃ janārdana
|
sarve hyanusṛtā mohāt pārthivāḥ saha mantribhiḥ ||32||

33bhīṣmasyātha vacaḥ śrutvā dāśārhaḥ puṣkarekṣaṇaḥ
|
bhīṣmadroṇamukhān sarvānabhyabhāṣata vīryavān ||33||

34sarveṣāṃ kuruvṛddhānāṃ mahānayamatikramaḥ
|
prasahya mandamaiśvarye na niyacchata yannṛpam ||34||

35tatra kāryamahaṃ manye kālaprāptamarindamāḥ
|
kriyamāṇe bhavecchreyastat sarvaṃ śṛṇutānaghāḥ ||35||

36pratyakṣametadbhavatāṃ yadvakṣyāmi hitaṃ vacaḥ
|
bhavatāmānukūlyena yadi roceta bhāratāḥ ||36||

37bhojarājasya vṛddhasya durācāro hyanātmavān
|
jīyataḥ pituraiśvaryaṃ hṛtvā mṛtyuvaśaṃ gataḥ ||37||

38ugrasenasutaḥ kaṃsaḥ parityaktaḥ sa bāndhavaiḥ
|
jñātīnāṃ hitakāmena mayā śasto mahāmṛdhe ||38||

39āhukaḥ punarasmābhirjñātibhiścāpi satkṛtaḥ
|
ugrasenaḥ kṛto rājā bhojarājanyavardhanaḥ ||39||

40kaṃsamekaṃ parityajya kulārthe sarvayādavāḥ
|
sambhūya sukhamedhante bhāratāndhakavṛṣṇayaḥ ||40||

41api cāpyavadadrājan parameṣṭhī prajāpatiḥ
|
vyūḍhe devāsure yuddhe'bhyudyateṣvāyudheṣu ca ||41||

42dvaidhībhūteṣu lokeṣu vinaśyatsu ca bhārata
|
abravīt sṛṣṭimān devo bhagavā~llokabhāvanaḥ ||42||

43parābhaviṣyantyasurā daiteyā dānavaiḥ saha
|
ādityā vasavo rudrā bhaviṣyanti divaukasaḥ ||43||

44devāsuramanuṣyāśca gandharvoragarākṣasāḥ
|
asmin yuddhe susaṃkruddhā haniṣyanti parasparam ||44||

45iti matvā'bravīddharmaṃ parameṣṭhī prajāpatiḥ
|
varuṇāya prayacchaitān baddhvā daiteyadānavān ||45||

46evamuktastato dharmo niyogāt parameṣṭhinaḥ
|
varuṇāya dadau sarvān baddhvā daiteyadānavān ||46||

47tān baddhvā dharmapāśaiśca svaiśca pāśairjaleśvaraḥ
|
varuṇaḥ sāgare yatto nityaṃ rakṣati dānavān ||47||

48tathā duryodhanaṃ karṇaṃ śakuniṃ cāpi saubalam
|
baddhvā duḥśāsanaṃ cāpi pāṇḍavebhyaḥ prayacchatha ||48||

49tyajet kulārthe puruṣaṃ grāmasyārthe kulaṃ tyajet
|
grāmaṃ janapadasyārthe ātmārthe pṛthivīṃ tyajet ||49||

50rājan duryodhanaṃ baddhvā tataḥ saṃśāmya pāṇḍavaiḥ
|
tvatkṛte na vinaśyeyuḥ kṣatriyāḥ kṣatriyarṣabha ||50||
