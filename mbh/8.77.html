Mahābhārata 8.77 (Karṇaparvan)

1sañjaya uvāca|
śrutvā tu rathanirghoṣaṃ siṃhanādaṃ ca saṃyuge
|
arjunaḥ prāha govindaṃ śīghraṃ nodaya vājinaḥ ||1||

2arjunasya vacaḥ śrutvā goviṃdo'rjunamabravīt
|
eṣa gacchāmi sukṣipraṃ yatra bhīmo vyavasthitaḥ ||2||

3taṃ yāntamaśvairhimaśaṅkhavarṇaiḥ suvarṇamuktāmaṇijālanaddhaiḥ
|
jambhaṃ jighāṃsuṃ pragṛhītavajraṃ jayāya devendramivogramanyum ||3||

4rathāśvamātaṅgapadātisaṃghā bāṇasvanairnemikhurasvanaiśca
|
saṃnādayanto vasudhāṃ diśaśca kruddhā nṛsiṃhā jayamabhyudīyuḥ ||4||

5teṣāṃ ca pārthasya ca māriṣāsīddehāsupāpakṣapaṇaṃ suyuddham
|
trailokyahetorasurairyathāsīddevasya viṣṇorjayatāṃ varasya ||5||

6tairastamuccāvacamāyudhaṃ tadekaḥ praciccheda kirīṭamālī
|
kṣurārdhacandrairniśitaiśca bhallaiḥ śirāṃsi teṣāṃ bahudhā ca bāhūn ||6||

7chatrāṇi vālavyajanāni ketūnaśvānrathānpattigaṇāndvipāṃśca
|
te petururvyāṃ bahudhā virūpā vātapraṇunnāni yathā vanāni ||7||

8suvarṇajālāvatatā mahāgajāḥ savaijayantīdhvajayodhakalpitāḥ
|
suvarṇapuṅkhairiṣubhiḥ samācitāścakāśire prajvalitā yathā'calāḥ ||8||

9vidārya nāgāśvarathān dhanañjayaḥ śarottamairvāsavavajrasannibhaiḥ
|
drutaṃ yayau karṇajighāṃsayā tathā yathā marutvān balabhedane purā ||9||

10tataḥ sa puruṣavyāghrastava sainyamariṃdamaḥ
|
praviveśa mahābāhurmakaraḥ sāgaraṃ yathā ||10||

11taṃ hṛṣṭāstāvakā rājanrathapattisamanvitāḥ
|
gajāśvasādibahulāḥ pāṇḍavaṃ samupādravan ||11||

12teṣāmāpatatāṃ pārthamārāvaḥ sumahānabhūt
|
sāgarasyeva kṣubdhasya yathā syātsalilasvanaḥ ||12||

13te tu taṃ puruṣavyāghraṃ vyāghrā iva mahārathāḥ
|
abhyadravanta saṃgrāme tyaktvā prāṇakṛtaṃ bhayam ||13||

14teṣāmāpatatāṃ tatra śaravarṣāṇi muñcatām
|
arjuno vyadhamatsainyaṃ mahāvāto ghanāniva ||14||

15te'rjunaṃ sahitā bhūtvā rathavaṃśaiḥ prahāriṇaḥ
|
abhiyāya maheṣvāsā vivyadhurniśitaiḥ śaraiḥ ||15||

16tato'rjunaḥ sahasrāṇi rathavāraṇavājinām
|
preṣayāmāsa viśikhairyamasya sadanaṃ prati ||16||

17te vadhyamānāḥ samare pārthacāpacyutaiḥ śaraiḥ
|
tatra tatra sma līyante bhaye jāte mahārathāḥ ||17||

18teṣāṃ catuḥśatānvīrānyatamānānmahārathān
|
arjuno niśitairbāṇairanayadyamasādanam ||18||

19te vadhyamānāḥ samare nānāliṅgaiḥ śitaiḥ śaraiḥ
|
arjunaṃ samabhityajya dudruvurvai diśo daśa ||19||

20teṣāṃ śabdo mahānāsīddravatāṃ vāhinīmukhe
|
mahaughasyeva jaladhergirimāsādya dīryataḥ ||20||

21tāṃ tu senāṃ bhṛśaṃ viddhvā drāvayitvā'rjunaḥ śaraiḥ
|
prāyādabhimukhaḥ pārthaḥ sūtānīkaṃ hi māriṣa ||21||

22tasya śabdo mahānāsītparānabhimukhasya vai
|
garuḍasyeva patataḥ pannagārthe yathā purā ||22||

23taṃ tu śabdamabhiśrutya bhīmaseno mahābalaḥ
|
babhūva paramaprītaḥ pārthadarśanalālasaḥ ||23||

24śrutvaiva pārthamāyāntaṃ bhīmasenaḥ pratāpavān
|
tyaktvā prāṇānmahārāja senāṃ tava mamarda ha ||24||

25sa vāyuvīryapratimo vāyuvegasamo jave
|
vāyuvadvyacaradbhīmo vāyuputraḥ pratāpavān ||25||

26tenārdyamānā rājendra senā tava viśāṃpate
|
vyabhraśyata mahārāja bhinnā nauriva sāgare ||26||

27tāṃ tu senāṃ tadā bhīmo darśayan pāṇilāghavam
|
śarairavacakartograiḥ preṣayiṣyan yamakṣayam ||27||

28tatra bhārata bhīmasya balaṃ dṛṣṭvā'timānuṣam
|
vyabhramanta raṇe yodhāḥ kālasyeva yugakṣaye ||28||

29tathā'rditānbhīmabalān bhīmasenena bhārata
|
dṛṣṭvā duryodhano rājā idaṃ vacanamabravīt ||29||

30sainikāṃśca maheṣvāsān yodhāṃśca bharatarṣabha
|
samādiśanraṇe sarvānhata bhīmamiti sma ha ||30||

31tasminhate hataṃ manye pāṇḍusainyamaśeṣataḥ
|
pratigṛhya ca tāmājñāṃ tava putrasya pārthivāḥ ||31||

32bhīmaṃ pracchā‌dayāmāsuḥ śaravarṣaiḥ samantataḥ
|
gajāśca bahulā rājannarāśca jayagṛddhinaḥ ||32||

33rathe sthitāśca rājendra parivavrurvṛkodaram
|
sa taiḥ parivṛtaḥ śūraiḥ śūro rājansamantataḥ ||33||

34śuśubhe bharataśreṣṭho nakṣatrairiva candramāḥ
|
pariveṣī yathā somaḥ paripūrṇo virājate ||34||

35sa rarāja tathā saṃkhye darśanīyo narottamaḥ
|
nirviśeṣo mahārāja yathā hi vijayastathā ||35||

36tasya te pārthivāḥ sarve śaravṛṣṭiṃ samāsṛjan
|
krodharaktekṣaṇāḥ śūrā hantukāmā vṛkodram ||36||

37tāṃ vidārya mahāsenāṃ śaraiḥ sannataparvabhiḥ
|
niścakrāma raṇādbhīmo matsyojālādivāṃbhasi ||37||

38hatvā daśasahasrāṇi gajānāmanivartinām
|
nṛṇāṃ śatasahastre dve dve śate caiva bhārata ||38||

39pañca cāśvasahasrāṇi rathānāṃ śatameva ca
|
hatvā prāsyandayadbhīmo nadīṃ śoṇitavāhinīm ||39||

40śoṇitodāṃ rathāvartāṃ hastigrāhasamākulām
|
naramīnāśvanakrāntāṃ keśaśaivalaśādvalām ||40||

41saṃcchinnabhujanāgendrāṃ bahuratnāpahāriṇīm
|
ūrugrāhāṃ majjapaṅkāṃ śīrṣopalasamāvṛtām ||41||

42dhanuḥkāśāṃ śarāvāpāṃ gadāparighaketanām
|
haṃsachatradhvajopetāmuṣṇīṣavaraphenilām ||42||

43hārapadmākarāṃ caiva bhūmireṇūrmimālinīm
|
āryavṛttavatīṃ saṃkhye sutarāṃ bhīrudustarām ||43||

44yodhagrāhavatīṃ saṃkhye vahantīṃ pitṛsādanam
|
kṣaṇena puruṣavyāghraḥ prāvartayata nimnagām ||44||

45yathā vaitaraṇīmugrāṃ dustarāmakṛtātmabhiḥ
|
tathā dustaraṇīṃ ghorāṃ bhīrūṇāṃ bhayavardhanīm ||45||

46yato yataḥ pāṇḍaveyaḥ praviṣṭo rathasattamaḥ
|
tatastato ghātayata yodhāñśatasahasraśaḥ ||46||

47evaṃ dṛṣṭvā kṛtaṃ karma bhīmasenena saṃyuge
|
duryodhano mahārāja śakuniṃ vākyamabravīt ||47||

48jahi mātula saṃgrāme bhīmasenaṃ mahābalam
|
asmiñjite jitaṃ manye pāṇḍaveyaṃ mahābalam ||48||

49tataḥ prāyānmahārāja saubaleyaḥ pratāpavān
|
raṇāya mahate yukto bhrātṛbhiḥ parivāritaḥ ||49||

50sa samāsādya saṃgrāme bhīmaṃ bhīmaparākramam
|
vārayāmāsa taṃ vīro veleva makarālayam ||50||

51saṃnyavartata taṃ bhīmo vāryamāṇaḥ śitaiḥ śaraiḥ
|
śakunistasya rājendra vāmapārśve stanāntare ||51||

52preṣayāmāsa nārācān rukmapuṅkhāñśilāśitān
|
varma bhittvā tu te ghorāḥ pāṇḍavasya mahātmanaḥ ||52||

53nyamajjanta mahārāja kaṅkabarhiṇavāsasaḥ
|
so'tividdho raṇe bhīmaḥ śaraṃ rukmavibhūṣitam ||53||

54preṣayāmāsa sa ruṣā saubalaṃ prati bhārata
|
tamāyāntaṃ śaraṃ ghoraṃ śakuniḥ śatrutāpanaḥ ||54||

55ciccheda saptadhā rājan kṛtahasto mahābalaḥ
|
tasminnipatite bhūmau bhīmaḥ kruddho viśāṃpate ||55||

56dhanuśchiccheda bhallena saubalasya hasanniva
|
tadapāsya dhanuśchinnaṃ saubaleyaḥ pratāpavān ||56||

57anyadādāya vegena dhanurbhallāṃśca ṣoḍaśa
|
taistasya tu mahārāja bhallaiḥ sannataparvabhiḥ ||57||

58dvābhyāṃ sa sārathiṃ hyārcchadbhīmaṃ saptabhireva ca
|
dhvajamekena ciccheda dvābhyāṃ chatraṃ viśāṃpate ||58||

59caturbhiścaturo vāhānvivyādha subalātmajaḥ
|
tataḥ kruddho mahārāja bhīmasenaḥ pratāpavān ||59||

60śaktiṃ cikṣepa samare rukmadaṇḍāmayasmayīm
|
sā bhīmabhujanirmuktā nāgajihveva cañcalā ||60||

61nipapāta raṇe tūrṇaṃ saubalasya mahātmanaḥ
|
tatastāmeva saṃgṛhya śaktiṃ kanakabhūṣaṇām ||61||

62bhīmasenāya cikṣepa kruddharūpo viśāṃpate
|
sā nirbhidya bhujaṃ savyaṃ pāṇḍavasya mahātmanaḥ ||62||

63nipapāta tadā bhūmau yathā vidyunnabhaścyutā
|
athotkruṣṭaṃ mahārāja dhārtarāṣṭraiḥ samantataḥ ||63||

64na tu taṃ mamṛṣe bhīmaḥ siṃhanādaṃ tarasvinām
|
anyadgṛhya dhanuḥ sajyaṃ tvaramāṇo mahābalaḥ ||64||

65muhūrtādiva rājendra cchādayāmāsa sāyakaiḥ
|
saubalasya balaṃ saṃkhye tyaktvā''mānaṃ mahābalaḥ ||65||

66tasyāśvāṃścaturo hatvā sūtaṃ caiva viśāṃpate
|
dhvajaṃ ciccheda bhallena tvaramāṇaḥ parākramī ||66||

67hatāśvaṃ rathamutsṛjya tvaramāṇo narottamaḥ
|
tasthau visphārayaṃścāpaṃ krodharaktekṣaṇaḥ śvasan ||67||

68śaraiśca bahudhā rājan bhīmamārcchatsamantataḥ
|
pratihatya tu vegena bhamisenaḥ pratāpavān ||68||

69dhanuściccheda saṃkruddho vivyādha ca śitaiḥ śaraiḥ
|
so'tividdho balavatā śatruṇā śatrukarśanaḥ ||69||

70nipapāta tadā bhūmau kiñcitprāṇo narādhipaḥ
|
tatastaṃ vihvalaṃ jñātvā putrastava viśāṃpate ||70||

71apovāha rathenājau bhīmasenasya paśyataḥ
|
rathasthe tu naravyāghre dhārtarāṣṭrāḥ parāṅmukhāḥ ||71||

72pradudruvurdiśo bhītā bhīmājjāte mahābhaye
|
saubale nirjite rājanbhīmasenena dhanvinā ||72||

73bhayena mahatā''viṣṭaḥ putro duryodhanastava
|
apāyājjavanairaśvaiḥ sāpekṣo mātulaṃ prati ||73||

74parāṅmukhaṃ tu rājānaṃ dṛṣṭvā sainyāni bhārata
|
viprajagmuḥ samutsṛjya dvairathāni samantataḥ ||74||

75tān dṛṣṭvā vidrutānsarvān dhārtarāṣṭrānparāṅmukhān
|
javenābhyāpatadbhīmaḥ kirañśaraśatānbahūn ||75||

76te vadhyamānā bhīmena dhārtarāṣṭrāḥ parāṅmukhāḥ
|
karṇamāsādya samare sthitā rājan samantataḥ ||76||

77sa hi teṣāṃ mahāvīryo dvīpo'bhūtsumahābalaḥ
|
bhinnanaukā yathā rājandvīpamāsādya nirvṛtāḥ ||77||

78bhavanti puruṣavyāghra nāvikāḥ kālaparyaye
|
tathā karṇaṃ samāsādya tāvakāḥ puruṣarṣabha ||78||

79samāśvastāḥ sthitā rājansaṃprahṛṣṭāḥ parasparam
|
samājagmuśca yuddhāya mṛtyuṃ kṛtvā nivartanam ||79||
