Mahābhārata 2.44 (Sabhāparvan)

1bhīṣma uvāca|
naiṣā cedipaterbuddhiryayā tvāhvayate'cyutam
|
nūnameva jagadbhartuḥ kṛṣṇasyaiva viniścayaḥ||1||

2ko hi māṃ bhīmasenādya kṣitāvarhati pārthivaḥ
|
kṣeptuṃ kālaparītātmā yathaiṣa kulapāṃsanaḥ||2||

3eṣa hyasya mahābāhustejoṃśaśca harerdhruvam
|
tameva punarādātumicchatyuta tathā bibhuḥ||3||

4yenaiṣa kuruśārdūla śārdūla iva cedirāṭ
|
garjatyatīva durbuddhiḥ sarvānasmānacintayan||4||

5vaiśampāyana uvāca|
tato na mamṛṣe caidyastadbhīṣmavacanaṃ tadā
|
uvāca caina saṅkruddhaḥ punarbhīṣmamathottaram||5||

6śiśupāla uvāca|
dviṣatāṃ no'stu bhīṣmaiṣa prabhāvaḥ keśavasya yaḥ
|
yasya saṃstavavaktā tvaṃ bandivatsatatotthitaḥ||6||

7saṃstave ca mano bhīṣma pareṣāṃ ramate yadi
|
tadā saṃstauṣi rājñastvamimaṃ hitvā janārdanam||7||

8daradaṃ stuhi bāhlīkamimaṃ pārthivasattamam
|
jāyamānena yeneyamabhavaddāritā mahī||8||

9vaṅgāṅgaviṣayādhyakṣaṃ sahasrākṣasamaṃ bale
|
stuhi karṇamimaṃ bhīṣma mahācāpavikarṣaṇam||9||

10yasyeme kuṇḍale divye sahaje devanirmite
|
kavacaṃ ca mahābāho bālārkasadṛśaprabham||10||

11vāsavapratimo yena jarāsandho'tidurjayaḥ
|
vijito bāhuyuddhena dehabhedaṃ ca lambhitaḥ||11||

12droṇaṃ drauṇiṃ ca sādhu tvaṃ pitāputrau mahārathau
|
stuhi stutyāvubhau bhīṣma satataṃ dvijasattamau||12||

13yayoranyataro bhīṣma saṅkruddhaḥ sacarācarām
|
imāṃ vasumatīṃ kuryānniḥśeṣāmiti me matiḥ||13||

14droṇasya hi samaṃ yuddhe na paśyāmi narādhipam
|
nāśvatthāmnaḥ samaṃ bhīṣma na ca tau stotumicchasi||14||

15pṛthivyāṃ sāgarāntāyāṃ yo vai pratisamo bhavet
|
duryodhanaṃ tvaṃ rājendramatikramya mahābhujam||15||

16jayadrathaṃ ca rājānaṃ kṛtāstraṃ dṛḍhavikramam
|
drumaṃ kiṃ puruṣācāryaṃ loke prathitavikramam
|
atikramya mahāvīryaṃ kiṃ praśaṃsasi keśavam||16||

17vṛddhaṃ ca bharatācāryaṃ tathā śāradvataṃ kṛpam
|
atikramya mahāvīryaṃ kiṃ praśaṃsasi keśavam||17||

18dhanurdharāṇāṃ pravaraṃ rukmiṇaṃ puruṣottamam
|
atikramya mahāvīryaṃ kiṃ praśaṃsasi keśavam||18||

19bhīṣmakaṃ ca mahāvīryaṃ dantavaktraṃ ca bhūmipam
|
bhagadattaṃ yūpaketuṃ jayatsenaṃ ca māgadham||19||

20virāṭadrupadau cobhau śakuniṃ ca bṛhadbalam
|
vindānuvindāvāvantyau pāṇḍyaṃ śvetamathottamam||20||

21śaṅkhaṃ ca sumahābhāgaṃ vṛṣasenaṃ ca māninam
|
ekalavyaṃ ca vikrāntaṃ kāliṅgaṃ ca mahāratham||21||

22atikramya mahāvīryaṃ kiṃ praśaṃsati keśavam
|
śalyādīnapi kasmāttvaṃ na stauṣi vasudhādhipān
|
stavāya yadi te buddhirvartate bhīṣma sarvadā||22||

23kiṃ hi śakyaṃ mayā kartuṃ yadvṛddhānāṃ tvayā nṛpa
|
purā kathayatāṃ nūnaṃ na śrutaṃ dharmavādinām||23||

24ātmanindātmapūjā ca paranindā parastavaḥ
|
anācaritamāryāṇāṃ vṛttametaccaturvidham||24||

25yadastavyamimaṃ śaśvanmohātsaṃstauṣi bhaktitaḥ
|
keśavaṃ tacca te bhīṣma na kaścidanumanyate||25||

26kathaṃ bhojasya puruṣe vargapāle durātmani
|
samāveṣayase sarvaṃ jagatkevalakāmyayā||26||

27atha caiṣā na te buddhiḥ prakṛtiṃ yāti bhārata
|
mayaiva kathitaṃ pūrvaṃ bhūliṅgaśakuniryathā||27||

28bhūliṅgaśakunirnāma pārśve himavataḥ pare
|
bhīṣma tasyāḥ sadā vācaḥ śrūyante'rthavigarhitāḥ||28||

29mā sāhasamitīdaṃ sā satataṃ vāśate kila
|
sāhasaṃ cātmanātīva carantī nāvabudhyate||29||

30sā hi māṃsārgalaṃ bhīṣma mukhātsiṃhasya khādataḥ
|
dantāntaravilagnaṃ yattadādatte'lpacetanā||30||

31icchataḥ sā hi siṃhasya bhīṣma jīvatyasaṃśayam
|
tadvattvamapyadharmiṣṭha sadā vācaḥ prabhāṣase||31||

32icchatāṃ bhūmipālānāṃ bhīṣma jīvasya saṃśayam
|
lokavidviṣṭakarmā hi nānyo'sti bhavatā samaḥ||32||

33vaiśampāyana uvāca|
tataścedipateḥ śrutvā bhīṣmaḥ sa kaṭukaṃ vacaḥ
|
uvācedaṃ vaco rājaṃścedirājasya śṛṇvataḥ||33||

34icchatāṃ kila nāmāhaṃ jīvāmyeṣāṃ mahīkṣitām
|
so'haṃ na gaṇayāmyetāṃstṛṇenāpi narādhipān||34||

35evamukte tu bhīṣmeṇa tataḥ sañcukruśurnṛpāḥ
|
kecijjahṛṣire tatra kecidbhīṣmaṃ jagarhire||35||

36kecidūcurmaheṣvāsāḥ śrutvā bhīṣmasya yadvacaḥ
|
pāpo'valipto vṛddhaśca nāyaṃ bhīṣmo'rhati kṣamām||36||

37hanyatāṃ durmatirbhīṣmaḥ paśuvatsādhvayaṃ nṛpāḥ
|
sarvaiḥ sametya saṃrabdhairdahyatāṃ vā kaṭāgninā||37||

38iti teṣāṃ vacaḥ śrutvā tataḥ kurupitāmahaḥ
|
uvāca matimānbhīṣmastāneva vasudhādhipān||38||

39uktasyoktasya nehāntamahaṃ samupalakṣaye
|
yattu vakṣyāmi tatsarvaṃ śṛṇudhvaṃ vasudhādhipāḥ||39||

40paśuvadghātanaṃ vā me dahanaṃ vā kaṭāgninā
|
kriyatāṃ mūrdhni vo nyastaṃ mayedaṃ sakalaṃ padam||40||

41eṣa tiṣṭhati govindaḥ pūjito'smābhiracyutaḥ
|
yasya vastvarate buddhirmaraṇāya sa mādhavam||41||

42kṛṣṇamāhvayatāmadya yuddhe cakragadādharam
|
yādavasyaiva devasya dehaṃ viśatu pātitaḥ||42||
