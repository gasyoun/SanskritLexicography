Mahābhārata 12.115 (Śāntiparvan)

1yudhiṣṭhira uvāca|
pitāmaha mahāprājña saṃśayo me mahānayam
|
saṃchettavyastvayā rājanbhavānkulakaro hi naḥ||1||

2puruṣāṇāmayaṃ tāta durvṛttānāṃ durātmanām
|
kathito vākyasaṃcārastato vijñāpayāmi te||2||

3yaddhitaṃ rājyatantrasya kulasya ca sukhodayam
|
āyatyāṃ ca tadātve ca kṣemavṛddhikaraṃ ca yat||3||

4putrapautrābhirāmaṃ ca rāṣṭravṛddhikaraṃ ca yat
|
annapāne śarīre ca hitaṃ yattadbravīhi me||4||

5abhiṣikto hi yo rājā rāṣṭrastho mitrasaṃvṛtaḥ
|
sasuhṛtsamupeto vā sa kathaṃ rañjayetprajāḥ||5||

6yo hyasatpragraharatiḥ sneharāgabalātkṛtaḥ
|
indriyāṇāmanīśatvādasajjanabubhūṣakaḥ||6||

7tasya bhṛtyā viguṇatāṃ yānti sarve kulodgatāḥ
|
na ca bhṛtyaphalairarthaiḥ sa rājā samprayujyate||7||

8etanme saṃśayasyāsya rājadharmānsudurvidān
|
bṛhaspatisamo buddhyā bhavānśaṃsitumarhati||8||

9śaṃsitā puruṣavyāghra tvannaḥ kulahite rataḥ
|
kṣattā caiko mahāprājño yo naḥ śaṃsati sarvadā||9||

10tvattaḥ kulahitaṃ vākyaṃ śrutvā rājyahitodayam
|
amṛtasyāvyayasyeva tṛptaḥ svapsyāmyahaṃ sukham||10||

11kīdṛśāḥ saṃnikarṣasthā bhṛtyāḥ sarvaguṇānvitāḥ
|
kīdṛśaiḥ kiṃ kulīnairvā saha yātrā vidhīyate||11||

12na hyeko bhṛtyarahito rājā bhavati rakṣitā
|
rājyaṃ cedaṃ janaḥ sarvastatkulīno'bhikāṅkṣati||12||

13bhīṣma uvāca|
na ca praśāstuṃ rājyaṃ hi śakyamekena bhārata
|
asahāyavatā tāta naivārthāḥ kecidapyuta||13||

14labdhuṃ labdhā hyapi sadā rakṣituṃ bharatarṣabha
|
yasya bhṛtyajanaḥ sarvo jñānavijñānakovidaḥ||14||

15hitaiṣī kulajaḥ snigdhaḥ sa rājyaphalamaśnute||15||

16mantriṇo yasya kulajā asaṃhāryāḥ sahoṣitāḥ
|
nṛpatermatidāḥ santaḥ sambandhajñānakovidāḥ||16||

17anāgatavidhātāraḥ kālajñānaviśāradāḥ
|
atikrāntamaśocantaḥ sa rājyaphalamaśnute||17||

18samaduḥkhasukhā yasya sahāyāḥ priyakāriṇaḥ
|
arthacintāparāḥ satyāḥ sa rājyaphalamaśnute||18||

19yasya nārto janapadaḥ saṃnikarṣagataḥ sadā
|
akṣudraḥ satpathālambī sa rājā rājyabhāgbhavet||19||

20kośākhyapaṭalaṃ yasya kośavṛddhikarairnaraiḥ
|
āptaistuṣṭaiśca satataṃ cīyate sa nṛpottamaḥ||20||

21koṣṭhāgāramasaṃhāryairāptaiḥ saṃcayatatparaiḥ
|
pātrabhūtairalubdhaiśca pālyamānaṃ guṇī bhavet||21||

22vyavahāraśca nagare yasya karmaphalodayaḥ
|
dṛśyate śaṃkhalikhitaḥ svadharmaphalabhāṅnṛpaḥ||22||

23saṃgṛhītamanuṣyaśca yo rājā rājadharmavit
|
ṣaḍvargaṃ pratigṛhṇāti sa dharmaphalamaśnute||23||
