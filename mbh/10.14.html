Mahābhārata 10.14 (Sauptikaparvan)

1vaiśampāyana uvāca|
iṅgitenaiva dāśārhastamabhiprāyamāditaḥ
|
drauṇerbuddhvā mahābāhurarjunaṃ pratyabhāṣata||1||

2arjunārjuna yaddivyamastraṃ te hṛdi vartate
|
droṇopadiṣṭaṃ tasyāyaṃ kālaḥ samprati pāṇḍava||2||

3bhrātṝṇāmātmanaścaiva paritrāṇāya bhārata
|
visṛjaitattvamapyājāvastramastranivāraṇam||3||

4keśavenaivamukto'tha pāṇḍavaḥ paravīrahā
|
avātaradrathāttūrṇaṃ pragṛhya saśaraṃ dhanuḥ||4||

5pūrvamācāryaputrāya tato'nantaramātmane
|
bhrātubhyaścaiva sarvebhyaḥ svastītyuktvā parantapaḥ||5||

6devatābhyo namaskṛtya gurubhyaścaiva sarvaśaḥ
|
utsasarja śivaṃ dhyāyannastramastreṇa śāmyatām||6||

7tatastadastraṃ sahasā sṛṣṭaṃ gāṇḍīvadhanvanā
|
prajajvāla mahārciṣmadyugāntānalasannibham||7||

8tathaiva droṇaputrasya tadastraṃ tigmatejasaḥ
|
prajajvāla mahājvālaṃ tejomaṇḍalasaṃvṛtam||8||

9nirghātā bahavaścāsanpeturulkāḥ sahasraśaḥ
|
mahadbhayaṃ ca bhūtānāṃ sarveṣāṃ samajāyata||9||

10saśabdamabhavavdyoma jvālāmālākulaṃ bhṛśam
|
cacāla ca mahī kṛtsnā saparvatavanadrumā||10||

11te tvastratejasī lokāṃstāpayantī vyavasthite
|
maharṣī sahitau tatra darśayāmāsatustadā||11||

12nāradaḥ sarvabhūtātmā bharatānāṃ pitāmahaḥ
|
ubhau śamayituṃ vīrau bhāradvājadhanañjayau||12||

13tau munī sarvadharmajñau sarvabhūtahitaiṣiṇau
|
dīptayorastrayormadhye sthitau paramatejasau||13||

14tadantaramathādhṛṣyāvupagamya yaśasvinau
|
āstāmṛṣivarau tatra jvalitāviva pāvakau||14||

15prāṇabhṛdbhiranādhṛṣyau devadānavasaṅgatau
|
astratejaḥ śamayituṃ lokānāṃ hitakāmyayā||15||

16ṛṣī ūcatuḥ|
nānāśastravidaḥ pūrve ye'pyatītā mahārathāḥ
|
naitadastraṃ manuṣyeṣu taiḥ prayuktaṃ kathañcana
|
kimidaṃ sāhasaṃ vīrau kṛtavantau mahātyayam||16||
