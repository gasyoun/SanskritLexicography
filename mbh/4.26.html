Mahābhārata 4.26 (Virāṭaparvan)

1vaiśampāyana uvāca|tato duryodhano rājā jñātvā teṣāṃ vacastadā
|
ciramantarmanā bhūtvā pratyuvāca sabhāsadaḥ ||1||

2suduḥkhā khalu kāryāṇāṃ gatirvijñātumantataḥ
|
tasmāt sarve nirīkṣadhvaṃ kka nu te pāṇḍavā gatāḥ ||2||

3alpāvaśiṣṭaṃ kālasya gatabhūyiṣṭhamantataḥ
|
teṣāmajñātacaryāyāmasmin varṣe trayodaśe ||3||

4asya varṣasya śeṣaṃ cedvyatīyurihaḥ pāṇḍavāḥ
|
nivṛttasamayāste hi satyavrataparāyaṇāḥ ||4||

5kṣaranta iva nāgendrāḥ sarve hyāśīviṣopamāḥ
|
duḥkhā bhaveyuḥ saṃrabdhāḥ kauravān prati te dhruvam ||5||

6sarve kālasya vettāraḥ kṛcchrarūpadharāḥ sthitāḥ
|
praviśeyurjitakrodhāstāvadeva punarvanam ||6||

7tasmāt kṣipraṃ bubhūṣadhvaṃ yathā te'tyantamavyayam
|
rājyaṃ nirdvandvamavyagraṃ niḥsapatnaṃ ciraṃ bhavet ||7||

8athābravīttataḥ karṇaḥ kṣipraṃ gacchantu bhārata
|
anye dhūrtā narā dakṣā nibhṛtāḥ sādhukāriṇaḥ ||8||

9carantu deśān saṃvītāḥ sphītāñjanapadākulān
|
tatra goṣṭhīṣu ramyāsu siddhapravrajiteṣu ca ||9||

10paricāreṣu tīrtheṣu vividheṣvākareṣu ca
|
vijñātavyā manuṣyaistaistarkayā suvinītayā ||10||

11vividhaistatparaiḥ samyak tajjñairnipuṇasaṃvṛtaiḥ
|
anveṣṭavyāḥ sunipuṇaiḥ pāṇḍavāśchannavāsinaḥ ||11||

12āśrameṣu ca ramyeṣu parvateṣu guhāsu ca
|
nadīkuñjeṣu tīrtheṣu grāmeṣu nagareṣu ca ||12||

13athāgrajānantarajaḥ pāpabhāvānurāgavān
|
jyeṣṭhaṃ duḥśāsanastatra bhrātā bhrātaramabravīt ||13||

14yeṣu naḥ pratyayo rājaṃścāreṣu manujādhipa
|
te yāntu dattadeyā ve bhūyastān parimārgitum ||14||

15etacca karṇo yatprāha sarvamīhāmahe tathā
|
yathoddiṣṭaṃ carāḥ sarve mṛgayantu yatastataḥ ||15||

16ete cānye ca bhūyāṃso deśāddeśaṃ yathāvidhi
|
na tu teṣāṃ gatirvāsaḥ pravṛttiścopalabhyate ||16||

17atyantaṃ vā nigūḍhāste pāraṃ cormimato gatāḥ
|
vyālaiścāpi mahāraṇye bhakṣitāḥ śūramāninaḥ ||17||

18athavā viṣamaṃ prāpya vinaṣṭāḥ śāśvatīḥ samāḥ
|
tasmānmānasamavyagraṃ kṛtvā tvaṃ kurunandana
|
kuru kāryaṃ mahotsāhaṃ manyase yannarādhipa ||18||
