Mahābhārata 3.119 (Āraṇyakaparvan)

1janamejaya uvāca|
prabhāsatīrthamāsādya pāṇḍavā vṛṣṇayastathā
|
kimakurvankathāścaiṣāṃ kāstatrāsaṃstapodhana||1||

2te hi sarve mahātmānaḥ sarvaśāstraviśāradāḥ
|
vṛṣṇayaḥ pāṇḍavāścaiva suhṛdaśca parasparam||2||

3vaiśaṃpāyana uvāca|
prabhāsatīrthaṃ saṃprāpya puṇyaṃ tīrthaṃ mahodadheḥ
|
vṛṣṇayaḥ pāṇḍavānvīrāḥ parivāryopatasthire||3||

4tato gokṣīrakundendumṛṇālarajataprabhaḥ
|
vanamālī halī rāmo babhāṣe puṣkarekṣaṇam||4||

5baladeva uvāca|
na kṛṣṇa dharmaścarito bhavāya jantoradharmaśca parābhavāya
|
yudhiṣṭhiro yatra jaṭī mahātmā vanāśrayaḥ kliśyati cīravāsāḥ||5||

6duryodhanaścāpi mahīṃ praśāsti na cāsya bhūmirvivaraṃ dadāti
|
dharmādadharmaścarito varīyānitīva manyeta naro'lpabuddhiḥ||6||

7duryodhane cāpi vivardhamāne yudhiṣṭhire cāsukhamāttarājye
|
kiṃ tvatra kartavyamiti prajābhiḥ śaṅkā mithaḥ saṃjanitā narāṇām||7||

8ayaṃ sa dharmaprabhavo narendro dharme dhṛtaḥ satyadhṛtiḥ pradātā
|
caleddhi rājyācca sukhācca pārtho dharmādapetastu kathaṃ vivardhet||8||

9kathaṃ nu bhīṣmaśca kṛpaśca vipro droṇaśca rājā ca kulasya vṛddhaḥ
|
pravrājya pārthānsukhamāpnuvanti dhikpāpabuddhīnbharatapradhānān||9||

10kiṃ nāma vakṣyatyavanipradhānaḥ pitṝnsamāgamya paratra pāpaḥ
|
putreṣu samyakcaritaṃ mayeti putrānapāpānvyaparopya rājyāt||10||

11nāsau dhiyā saṃprati paśyati sma kiṃ nāma kṛtvāhamacakṣurevam
|
jātaḥ pṛthivyāmiti pārthiveṣu pravrājya kaunteyamiti sma rājyāt||11||

12nūnaṃ samṛddhānpitṛlokabhūmau cāmīkarābhānkṣitijānpraphullān
|
vicitravīryasya sutaḥ saputraḥ kṛtvā nṛśaṃsaṃ bata paśyati sma||12||

13vyūḍhottarāṃsānpṛthulohitākṣānimānsma pṛcchansa śṛṇāti nūnam
|
prāsthāpayadyatsa vanaṃ saśaṅko yudhiṣṭhiraṃ sānujamāttaśastram||13||

14yo'yaṃ pareṣāṃ pṛtanāṃ samṛddhāṃ nirāyudho dīrghabhujo nihanyāt
|
śrutvaiva śabdaṃ hi vṛkodarasya muñcanti sainyāni śakṛtsamūtram||14||

15sa kṣutpipāsādhvakṛśastarasvī sametya nānāyudhabāṇapāṇiḥ
|
vane smaranvāsamimaṃ sughoraṃ śeṣaṃ na kuryāditi niścitaṃ me||15||

16na hyasya vīryeṇa balena kaścitsamaḥ pṛthivyāmapi vidyate'nyaḥ
|
sa śītavātātapakarśitāṅgo na śeṣamājāvasuhṛtsa kuryāt||16||

17prācyāṃ nṛpānekarathena jitvā vṛkodaraḥ sānucarānraṇeṣu
|
svastyāgamadyo'tirathastarasvī so'yaṃ vane kliśyati cīravāsāḥ||17||

18yaḥ sindhukūle vyajayannṛdevānsamāgatāndākṣiṇātyānmahīpān
|
taṃ paśyatemaṃ sahadevamadya tarasvinaṃ tāpasaveṣarūpam||18||

19yaḥ pārthivānekarathena jigye diśaṃ pratīcīṃ prati yuddhaśauṇḍaḥ
|
so'yaṃ vane mūlaphalena jīvañjaṭī caratyadya malācitāṅgaḥ||19||

20satre samṛddhe'tirathasya rājño vedītalādutpatitā sutā yā
|
seyaṃ vane vāsamimaṃ suduḥkhaṃ kathaṃ sahatyadya satī sukhārhā||20||

21trivargamukhyasya samīraṇasya deveśvarasyāpyathavāśvinośca
|
eṣāṃ surāṇāṃ tanayāḥ kathaṃ nu vane'caranhyastasukhāḥ sukhārhāḥ||21||

22jite hi dharmasya sute sabhārye sabhrātṛke sānucare niraste
|
duryodhane cāpi vivardhamāne kathaṃ na sīdatyavaniḥ saśailā||22||
