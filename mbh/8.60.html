Mahābhārata 8.60 (Karṇaparvan)

1sañjaya uvāca|
etasminnantare kṛṣṇaḥ pārthaṃ vacanamabravīt
|
darśayanniva kaunteyaṃ dharmarājaṃ yudhiṣṭhiram ||1||

2eṣa pāṇḍava te bhrātā dhārtarāṣṭrairmahābalaiḥ
|
jighāṃsubhirmaheṣvāsairdrutaṃ pārtho'nusāryate ||2||

3taṃ cānuyānti saṃrabdhāḥ pañcālā yuddhadurmadāḥ
|
yudhiṣṭhiraṃ mahātmānaṃ parīpsanto mahābalāḥ ||3||

4eṣa duryodhanaḥ pārtha rathānīkena daṃśitaḥ
|
rājā sarvasya lokasya rājānamanudhāvati ||4||

5jighāṃsuḥ puruṣavyāghra bhrātṛbhiḥ sahito balī
|
āśīviṣasamasparśaiḥ sarvayuddhaviśāradaiḥ ||5||

6ete jighṛkṣavo yānti dvipāśvarathapattayaḥ
|
yudhiṣṭhiraṃ dhārtarāṣṭrā narottamamivārthinaḥ ||6||

7paśya sātvatabhīmābhyāṃ niruddhādhiṣṭhitāḥ punaḥ
|
jihīrṣavo'mṛtaṃ daityāḥ śakrāgnibhyāmivāsakṛt ||7||

8ete bahutvāttvaritāḥ punargacchanti pāṇḍavam
|
samudramiva vāryoghāḥ prāvṛṭ‌kāle mahārathāḥ ||8||

9nadantaḥ siṃhanādāṃśca dhamantaścāpi vārijān
|
balavanto maheṣvāsā vidhunvanto dhanūṃṣi ca ||9||

10mṛtyormukhagataṃ manye kuntīputraṃ yudhiṣṭhiram
|
hutamagnau ca kaunteyaṃ duryodhanavaśaṃ gatam ||10||

11yathāvidhamanīkaṃ tu dhārtarāṣṭrasya pāṇḍava
|
nāsya śakro'pi mucyeta saṃprāpto bāṇagocaram ||11||

12duryodhanasya vīrasya śaraughān śīghramasyataḥ
|
saṃkruddhasyāntakasyeva ko vegaṃ saṃsahedraṇe ||12||

13duryodhanasya vīrasya drauṇeḥ śāradvatasya ca
|
karṇasya ceṣuvego vai parvatānapi śātayet ||13||

14karṇena ca kṛto rājā vimukhaḥ śatrutāpanaḥ
|
balavā~llaghuhastaśca kṛtī yuddhaviśāradaḥ ||14||

15rādheyaḥ pāṇḍavaśreṣṭhaṃ śaktaḥ pīḍayituṃ raṇe
|
sahito dhṛtarāṣṭrasya putraiḥ śūrairmahābalaiḥ ||15||

16tasyaibhiryudhyamānasya saṃgrāme saṃśitātmanaḥ
|
anyairapi ca pārthasya kṛtaṃ karma mahārathaiḥ ||16||

17upavāsakṛśo rājā bhṛśaṃ bharatasattamaḥ
|
brāhme bale sthito hyeṣa na kṣātre hi bale vibhuḥ||17||

18karṇena cābhiyukto'yaṃ bhūpatiḥ śatrutāpanaḥ
|
saṃśayaṃ samanuprāptaḥ pāṇḍavo vai yudhiṣṭhiraḥ ||18||

19na jīvati mahārājo manye pārtha yudhiṣṭhiraḥ
|
yadbhīmasenaḥ sahate siṃhanādamamarṣaṇaḥ ||19||

20nardatāṃ dhārtarāṣṭrāṇāṃ punaḥ punararindamaḥ
|
dhamatāṃ ca mahāśaṃkhānsaṃgrāme jitakāśinām ||20||

21yudhiṣṭhiraṃ pāṇḍaveyaṃ hateti bharatarṣabha
|
saṃcodayatyasau karṇo dhārtarāṣṭrānmahābalān ||21||

22sthūṇākarṇendrajālena pārthapāśupatena ca
|
pracchādayanti rājānaṃ śastrajālairmahārathāḥ ||22||

23āturo hi kṛto rājā sanniṣevyaśca bhārata
|
yathainamanuvartante pañcālāḥ saha pāṇḍavaiḥ ||23||

24tvaramāṇāstvarākāle sarvaśastrabhṛtāṃ varāḥ
|
majjantamiva pātāle balino'pyujjihīrṣavaḥ ||24||

25na keturdṛśyate rājñaḥ karṇena nihataḥ śaraiḥ
|
paśyatoryamayoḥ pārtha sātyakeśca śikhaṇḍinaḥ ||25||

26dhṛṣṭadyumnasya bhīmasya śatānīkasya vā vibho
|
pañcālānāṃ ca sarveṣāṃ cedīnāṃ caiva bhārata ||26||

27eṣa karṇo raṇe pārtha pāṇḍavānāmanīkinīm
|
śarairvidhvaṃsayati vai nalinīmiva kuñjaraḥ ||27||

28ete dravanti rathinastvadīyāḥ pāṇḍunandana
|
paśya paśya yathā pārtha gacchantyete mahārathāḥ ||28||

29ete bhārata mātaṅgāḥ karṇenābhihatāḥ śaraiḥ
|
ārtanādān vikurvāṇā vidravanti diśo daśa ||29||

30rathānāṃ dravate vṛndametaccaiva samantataḥ
|
drāvyamāṇaṃ raṇe pārtha karṇenāmitrakarṣiṇā ||30||

31hastikakṣyāṃ raṇe paśya carantīṃ tatra tatra ha
|
rathasthaṃ sūtaputrasya ketuṃ ketumatāṃ vara ||31||

32asau dhāvati rādheyo bhīmasenarathaṃ prati
|
kirañśaraśatānyeva vinighnaṃstava vāhinīm ||32||

33etān paśya ca pañcālān drāvyamāṇān mahārathān
|
śakreṇeva yathā daityān hanyamānānmahāhave ||33||

34eṣa karṇo raṇe jitvā pañcālānpāṇḍusṛñjayān
|
diśo viprekṣate sarvāstvadarthamiti me matiḥ ||34||

35paśya pārtha dhanuḥ śreṣṭhaṃ vikarṣansādhu śobhate
|
śatruṃ jitvā yathā śakro devasaṃghaiḥ samāvṛtaḥ ||35||

36ete nardanti kauravyā dṛṣṭvā karṇasya vikramam
|
trāsayanto raṇe pāṇḍūn sṛñjayāṃśca samantataḥ ||36||

37eṣa sarvātmanā pāṇḍūṃstrāsayitvā mahāraṇe
|
abhibhāṣati rādheyaḥ sarvasainyāni mānada ||37||

38abhidravata bhadraṃ vo drutaṃ dravata kauravāḥ
|
yathā jīvanna vaḥ kaścinmucyeta yudhi sṛñjayaḥ ||38||

39tathā kuruta saṃyattā vayaṃ yāsyāma pṛṣṭhataḥ
|
evamuktvā gato hyeṣa pṛṣṭhato vikirañcharān ||39||

40paśya karṇaṃ raṇe pārtha śvetacchatravirājitam
|
udayaṃ parvataṃ yadvacchaśāṅkenābhiśobhitam ||40||

41pūrṇacandranikāśena mūrdhni cchatreṇa bhārata
|
dhriyamāṇena samare śrīmacchataśalākinā ||41||

42eṣa tvāṃ prekṣate karṇaḥ sakaṭākṣaṃ viśāṃpate
|
uttamaṃ javamāsthāya dhruvameṣyati saṃyuge ||42||

43paśya hyenaṃ mahābāho vidhunvānaṃ mahaddhanuḥ
|
śarāṃścāśīviṣākārānvisṛjantaṃ mahāraṇe ||43||

44asau nivṛtto rādheyo dṛṣṭvā te vānaradhvajam
|
prārthayansamare pārtha tvayā saha paraṃtapa ||44||

45vadhāya cātmano'bhyeti dīptāsyaṃ śalabho yathā
|
karṇamekākinaṃ dṛṣṭvā rathānīkena bhārata ||45||

46rirakṣiṣuḥ susaṃvṛtto dhārtarāṣṭro nivartate
|
sarvaiḥ sahaibhirduṣṭātmā vadhyatāṃ ca prayatnataḥ ||46||

47tvayā yaśaśca rājyaṃ ca sukhaṃ cottamamicchatā
|
adīnayorviśrutayoryuvayoryotsyamānayoḥ ||47||

48devāsure pārthamṛdhe devadānavayoriva
|
paśyantu kauravāḥ sarve tava pārtha parākramam ||48||

49tvāṃ ca dṛṣṭvā'tisaṃrabdhaṃ karṇaṃ ca bharatarṣabha
|
asau duryodhanaḥ kruddho nottaraṃ pratipadyate ||49||

50ātmānaṃ ca kṛtātmānaṃ samīkṣya bharatarṣabha
|
kṛtāgasaṃ ca rādheyaṃ dharmātmani yudhiṣṭhire
|
pratipadyasva kaunteya prāptakālamanantaram ||50||

51āryāṃ yuddhe matiṃ kṛtvā pratyehi rathayūthapam
|
pañca hyetāni mukhyāni rathānāṃ rathasattama ||51||

52śatānyāyānti samare balināṃ tigmatejasām
|
pañca nāgasahasrāṇi dviguṇā vājinastathā ||52||

53abhisaṃhatya kaunteya padātiprayutāni ca
|
anyonyarakṣitaṃ vīra balaṃ tvāmabhivartate ||53||

54droṇaputraṃ puraskṛtya tacchīghraṃ sanniṣūdaya
|
nikṛtyaitadrathānīkaṃ balinaṃ lokaviśrutam ||54||

55sūtaputraṃ maheṣvāsaṃ darśayātmānamātmanā
|
uttamaṃ javamāsthāya pratyehi bharatarṣabha ||55||

56asau karṇaḥ susaṃrabdhaḥ pañcālānabhidhāvati
|
ketumasya hi paśyāmi dhṛṣṭadyumnarathaṃ prati ||56||

57samupaiṣyati pañcālāniti manye paraṃtapa
|
ācacakṣe priyaṃ pārtha tavedaṃ bharatarṣabha ||57||

58rājā'sau kuśalī śrīmān dharmaputro yudhiṣṭhiraḥ
|
asau bhīmo mahābāhuḥ sannivṛttaścamūmukhe ||58||

59vṛtaḥ sṛñjayasainyena śaineyena ca bhārata
|
vadhyanta ete samare kauravā niśitaiḥ śaraiḥ ||59||

60bhīmasenena kaunteya pañcālaiśca mahātmabhiḥ
|
senā hi dhārtarāṣṭrasya vimukhā vikṣaradvraṇā ||60||

61vipradhāvati vegena bhīmasyābhihatā śaraiḥ
|
vipannasasyeva mahī rudhireṇa samukṣitā ||61||

62bhāratī bharataśreṣṭha senā kṛpaṇadarśanā
|
nivṛttaṃ paśya kaunteya bhīmasenaṃ yudhāṃ patim ||62||

63āśīviṣamiva kruddhaṃ drāvayantaṃ varūthinīm
|
pītaraktāsitasitāstārācandrārkamaṇḍitāḥ ||63||

64patākā viprakīryante cchatrāṇyetāni cārjuna
|
sauvarṇā rājatāścaiva taijasāśca pṛthagvidhāḥ ||64||

65ketavo'bhinipātyante hastyaśvaṃ ca prakīryate
|
rathebhyaḥ prapatantyete rathino vigatāsavaḥ ||65||

66nānāvarṇairhatā bāṇaiḥ pañcālairapalāyibhiḥ
|
nirmanuṣyān gajānaśvānrathāṃścaiva dhanañjaya ||66||

67samādravanti pañcālā dhārtarāṣṭrāṃstarasvinaḥ
|
vimṛdnanti naravyāghrā bhīmasenabalāśrayāt ||67||

68balaṃ pareṣāṃ durdharṣāstyaktvā prāṇānarindama
|
ete nardanti pañcālā dhmāpayanti ca vārijān ||68||

69abhidravanti ca raṇe mṛdnantaḥ sāyakaiḥ parān
|
paśyasvaiṣāṃ ca māhātmyaṃ pañcālā hi parākramāt ||69||

70dhārtarāṣṭrānvinighnanti kruddhāḥ siṃhā iva dvipān
|
śastramācchidya śatrūṇāṃ sāyudhānāṃ nirāyudhāḥ ||70||

71tenaivaitānamoghāstrā nighnanti ca nadanti ca
|
śirāṃsyetāni pātyante śatrūṇāṃ bāhavo'pi ca ||71||

72ratha-nāga-hayā vīrā yaśasyāḥ sarva eva ca
|
sarvataścābhipannaiṣā dhārtarāṣṭrī mahācamūḥ ||72||

73pañcālairmānasādetya haṃsairgaṅgeva vegitaiḥ
|
subhṛśaṃ ca parākrāntāḥ pañcālānāṃ nivāraṇe ||73||

74kṛpakarṇāda‌yo vīrā ṛṣabhāṇāmivarṣabhāḥ
|
bhīmāstreṇa sunirbhagnān dhārtarāṣṭrānmahārathān ||74||

75dhṛṣṭadyumnamukhā vīrā ghnanti śatrūn sahasraśaḥ
|
pañcāleṣvabhibhūteṣu dviṣadbhirapabhīrnadan ||75||

76śatrupakṣamavaskandya śarānasyati mārutiḥ
|
viṣaṇṇabhūyiṣṭhatarā dhārtarāṣṭrī mahācamūḥ ||76||

77rathāścaite suvitrastā bhīmasenabhayārditāḥ
|
paśya bhīmena nārācairbhinnā nāgāḥ patantyamī ||77||

78vajrivajrahatānīva śikharāṇi dharābhṛtām
|
bhīmasenasya nirviddhā bāṇaiḥ sannataparvabhiḥ ||78||

79svānyanīkāni mṛdnanto dravantyete mahāgajāḥ
|
abhijānīhi bhīmasya siṃhanādaṃ suduḥsaham ||79||

80nadato'rjuna saṃgrāme vīrasya jitakāśinaḥ
|
eṣa naiṣādirabhyeti dvipamukhyena pāṇḍavam ||80||

81jighāṃsustomaraiḥ kruddho daṇḍapāṇirivāntakaḥ
|
satomarāvasya bhujau chinnau bhīmena garjataḥ ||81||

82tīkṣṇairagniraviprakhyairnārācairdaśabhirhataḥ
|
hatvainaṃ punarāyāti nāgānanyānprahāriṇaḥ ||82||

83paśya nīlāmbudanibhānmahāmātrairadhiṣṭhitān
|
śaktitomarasaṅghātairvinighnantaṃ vṛkodaram ||83||

84sapta sapta ca nāgāṃstānvaijayantīśca sadhvajāḥ
|
nihatya niśitairbāṇaiśchinnāḥ pārthāgrajena te ||84||

85daśabhirdaśabhiścaiko nārācairnihato gajaḥ
|
na cāsau dhārtarāṣṭrāṇāṃ śrūyate ninadastathā ||85||

86purandarasame kruddhe nivṛtte bharatarṣabha
|
akṣauhiṇyastathā tisro dhārtarāṣṭrasya saṃhatāḥ
|
kruddhena bhīmasenena narasiṃhena vāritāḥ ||86||

87na śaknuvanti vai pārthaṃ pārthivāḥ samudīkṣitum
|
madhyaṃdinagataṃ sūryaṃ yathā durbalacakṣuṣaḥ ||87||

88ete bhīmasya saṃtrastāḥ siṃhasyevetare mṛgāḥ
|
śaraiḥ saṃtrāsitāḥ saṃkhye na labhante sukhaṃ kvacit ||88||

89sañjaya uvāca|
etacchrutvā mahābāhurvāsudevāddhanañjayaḥ
|
bhīmasenena tat karma kṛtaṃ dṛṣṭvā suduṣkaram ||89||

90arjuno vyadhamacchiṣṭānahitānniśitaiḥ śaraiḥ
|
te vadhyamānāḥ samare saṃśaptakagaṇāḥ prabho ||90||

91prabhagnāḥ samare bhītā diśo daśa mahābalāḥ
|
śakrasyātithitāṃ gatvā viśokā hyabhavaṃstadā ||91||

92pārthaśca puruṣavyāghraḥ śaraiḥ sannataparvabhiḥ
|
jaghāna dhārtarāṣṭrasya caturvidhabalāṃ camūm ||92||
