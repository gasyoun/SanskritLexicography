Mahābhārata 8.61 (Karṇaparvan)

1dhṛtarāṣṭra uvāca|
nivṛtte bhīmasene ca pāṇḍave ca yudhiṣṭhire
|
vadhyamāne bale cāpi māmake pāṇḍusṛñjayaiḥ ||1||

2dravamāṇe balaughe ca nirānande muhurmuhuḥ
|
kimakurvanta kuravastanmamācakṣva sañjaya ||2||

3sañjaya uvāca|
dṛṣṭvā bhīmaṃ mahābāhuṃ sūtaputraḥ pratāpavān
|
krodharaktekṣaṇo rājanbhīmasenamupādravat ||3||

4tāvakaṃ tu balaṃ dṛṣṭvā bhīmasenātparāṅmukham
|
yatnena mahatā rājanparyavasthāpayadbalī ||4||

5vyavasthāpya mahābāhustava putrasya vāhinīm
|
pratyudyayau tadā karṇaḥ pāṇḍavān yuddhadurmadān ||5||

6pratyudyayustu rādheyaṃ pāṇḍavānāṃ mahārathāḥ
|
dhunvānāḥ kārmukāṇyājau vikṣipantaśca sāyakān ||6||

7bhīmasenaḥ śinernaptā śikhaṇḍī janamejayaḥ
|
dhṛṣṭadyumnaśca balavān sarve cāpi prabhadrakāḥ ||7||

8jighāṃsanto naravyāghrāḥ samantāt tava vāhinīm
|
abhyadravanta saṃkruddhāḥ samare jitakāśinaḥ ||8||

9tathaiva tāvakā rājanpāṇḍavānāmanīkinīm
|
abhyadravanta tvaritā jighāṃsanto mahārathāḥ ||9||

10rathanāgāśvakalilaṃ pattidhvajasamākulam
|
babhūva puruṣavyāghra sainyamadbhutadarśanam ||10||

11śikhaṇḍī ca yayau karṇaṃ dhṛṣṭadyumnaḥ sutaṃ tava
|
duḥśāsanaṃ mahārāja mahatyā senayā vṛtam ||11||

12nakulo vṛṣasenaṃ tu citrasenaṃ yudhiṣṭhiraḥ
|
ulūkaṃ samare rājan sahadevaḥ samabhyayāt ||12||

13sātyakiḥ śakuniṃ cāpi draupadeyāśca kauravān
|
arjunaṃ ca raṇe yatto droṇaputro mahārathaḥ ||13||

14yudhāmanyuṃ maheṣvāsaṃ gautamo'bhyapatadraṇe
|
kṛtavarmā ca balavānuttamaujasamādravat ||14||

15bhīmasenaḥ kurūn sarvān putrāṃśca tava māriṣa
|
sahānīkānmahābāhureka eva nyavārayat ||15||

16śikhaṇḍī tu tataḥ karṇaṃ vicarantamabhītavat
|
bhīṣmahantā mahārāja vārayāmāsa patribhiḥ ||16||

17pratiruddhastataḥ karṇo roṣāt prasphuritādharaḥ
|
śikhaṇḍinaṃ tribhirbāṇairbhruvormadhye'bhyatāḍayat ||17||

18dhārayaṃstu sa tānbāṇāñśikhaṇḍī bahvaśobhata
|
rājataḥ parvato yadvattribhiḥ śṛṅgairivotthitaiḥ ||18||

19so'tividdho maheṣvāsaḥ sūtaputreṇa saṃyuge
|
karṇaṃ vivyādha samare navatyā niśitaiḥ śaraiḥ ||19||

20tasya karṇo hayān hatvā sārathiṃ ca tribhiḥ śaraiḥ
|
unmamātha dhvajaṃ cāsya kṣurapreṇa mahārathaḥ ||20||

21hatāśvāttu tato yānādavaplutya mahārathaḥ
|
śaktiṃ cikṣepa karṇāya saṃkruddhaḥ śatrutāpanaḥ ||21||

22tāṃ chittvā samare karṇastribhirbhārata sāyakaiḥ
|
śikhaṇḍinamathāvidhyannavabhirniśitaiḥ śaraiḥ ||22||

23karṇacāpacyutānbāṇānvarjayaṃstu narottamaḥ
|
apayātastatastūrṇaṃ śikhaṇḍī bhṛśavikṣataḥ ||23||

24tataḥ karṇo mahārāja pāṇḍusainyānyaśātayat
|
tūlarāśiṃ samāsādya yathā vāyurmahābalaḥ ||24||

25dhṛṣṭadyumno mahārāja tava putreṇa pīḍitaḥ
|
duḥśāsanaṃ tribhirbāṇaiḥ pratyavidhyatstanāntare ||25||

26tasya duḥśāsano bāhuṃ savyaṃ vivyādha māriṣa
|
sa tena rukmapuṅkhena bhallenānataparvaṇā ||26||

27dhṛṣṭadyumnastu nirviddhaḥ śaraṃ ghoramamarṣaṇaḥ
|
duḥśāsanāya saṃkruddhaḥ preṣayāmāsa bhārata ||27||

28āpatantaṃ mahāvegaṃ dhṛṣṭadyumnasamīritam
|
śaraiściccheda putraste tribhireva viśāṃpate ||28||

29athāparaiḥ saptadaśairbhallaiḥ kanakabhūṣaṇaiḥ
|
dhṛṣṭadyumnaṃ samāsādya bāhvorurasi cārpayat ||29||

30tataḥ sa pārṣataḥ kruddho dhanuściccheda māriṣa
|
kṣurapreṇa sutīkṣṇena tata uccukruśurjanāḥ ||30||

31athānyaddhanurādāya putraste prahasanniva
|
dhṛṣṭadyumnaṃ śaravrātaiḥ samantātparyavārayat ||31||

32tava putrasya te dṛṣṭvā vikramaṃ sumahātmanaḥ
|
vyasmayanta raṇe yodhāḥ siddhāścāpsarasāṃ gaṇāḥ ||32||

33dhṛṣṭadyumnaṃ na paśyāma ghaṭamānaṃ mahābalam
|
duḥśāsanena saṃruddhaṃ siṃheneva mahāgajam ||33||

34tataḥ sarathanāgāśvāḥ pañcālāḥ pāṇḍupūrvaja
|
senāpatiṃ parīpsanto rurudhustanayaṃ tava ||34||

35tataḥ pravavṛte yuddhaṃ tāvakānāṃ paraiḥ saha
|
ghoraṃ prāṇabhṛtāṃ kāle bhīmarūpaṃ parantapa ||35||

36nakulaṃ vṛṣasenastu bhittvā pañcabhirāyasaiḥ
|
pituḥ samīpe tiṣṭhanvai tribhiranyairavidhyata ||36||

37nakulastu tataḥ śūro vṛṣasenaṃ hasanniva
|
nārācena sutīkṣṇena vivyādha hṛdaye bhṛśam ||37||

38so'tividdho balavatā śatruṇā śatrukarṣaṇa
|
śatruṃ vivyādha viṃśatyā sa ca taṃ pañcabhiḥ śaraiḥ ||38||

39tataḥ śarasahasreṇa tāvubhau puruṣarṣabhau
|
anyonyamācchādayatāmatho'bhajyata vāhinī ||39||

40sa dṛṣṭvā pradrutāṃ senāṃ dhārtarāṣṭrasya sūtajaḥ
|
nivārayāmāsa balāda‌nusṛtya viśāṃpate ||40||

41nivṛtte tu tataḥ karṇe nakulaḥ kauravān yayau
|
karṇaputrastu samare hitvā nakulameva tu ||41||

42jugopa cakraṃ tvarito rādheyasyaiva māriṣa
|
ulūkastu raṇe kruddhaḥ sahadevena vāritaḥ ||42||

43tasyāśvāṃścaturo hatvā sahadevaḥ pratāpavān
|
sārathiṃ preṣayāmāsa yamasya sadanaṃ prati ||43||

44ulūkastu tato yānādavaplutya viśāṃpate
|
trigartānāṃ balaṃ tūrṇaṃ jagāma pitṛnandanaḥ ||44||

45sātyakiḥ śakuniṃ viddhvā viṃśatyā niśitaiḥ śaraiḥ
|
dhvajaṃ ciccheda bhallena saubalasya hasanniva ||45||

46saubalastasya samare kruddho rājan pratāpavān
|
vidārya kavacaṃ bhūyo dhvajaṃ ciccheda kāñcanam ||46||

47tathainaṃ niśitairbāṇaiḥ sātyakiḥ pratyavidhyata
|
sārathiṃ ca mahārāja tribhireva samārpayat ||47||

48athāsya vāhāṃstvaritaḥ śarairninye yamakṣayam
|
tato'vaplutya sahasā śakunirbharatarṣabha ||48||

49āruroha rathaṃ tūrṇamulūkasya mahātmanaḥ
|
apovāhātha śīghraṃ sa śaineyādyuddhaśālinaḥ ||49||

50sātyakistu raṇe rājaṃstāvakānāmanīkinīm
|
abhidudrāva vegena tato'nīkamabhajyata ||50||

51śaineyaśarasaṃchannaṃ tava sainyaṃ viśāṃpate
|
bheje daśa diśastūrṇaṃ nyapatacca gatāsuvat ||51||

52bhīmasenaṃ tava suto vārayāmāsa saṃyuge
|
taṃ tu bhīmo muhūrtena vyaśvasūtarathadhvajam ||52||

53cakre lokeśvaraṃ tatra tenātuṣyanta vai janāḥ
|
tato'pāyānnṛpastatra bhīmasenasya gocarāt ||53||

54kurusainyaṃ tataḥ sarvaṃ bhīmasenamupādravat
|
tatra nādo mahānāsīdbhīmasenaṃ jighāṃsatām ||54||

55yudhāmanyuḥ kṛpaṃ viddhvā dhanurasyāśu cicchide
|
athānyaddhanurādāya kṛpaḥ śastrabhṛtāṃ varaḥ ||55||

56yudhāmanyordhvajaṃ sūtaṃ chatraṃ cāpātayatkṣitau
|
tato'pāyādrathenaiva yudhāmanyurmahārathaḥ ||56||

57uttamaujāśca hārdikyaṃ bhīmaṃ bhīmaparākramam
|
chādayāmāsa sahasā megho vṛṣṭyeva parvatam ||57||

58tadyuddhamāsītsumahaddhorarūpaṃ parantapa
|
yādṛśaṃ na mayā yuddhaṃ dṛṣṭapūrvaṃ viśāṃpate ||58||

59kṛtavarmā tato rājannuttamaujasamāhave
|
hṛdi vivyādha sahasā rathopastha upāviśat ||59||

60sārathistamapovāha rathena rathināṃ varam
|
kurusainyaṃ tataḥ sarvaṃ bhīmasenamupādravat ||60||

61duḥśāsanaḥ saubalaśca gajānīkena pāṇḍavam
|
mahatā parivāryaiva kṣudrakairabhyatāḍayat ||61||

62tato bhīmaḥ śaraśatairduryodhanamamarṣaṇam
|
vimukhīkṛtya tarasā gajānīkamupādravat ||62||

63tamāpatantaṃ sahasā gajānīkaṃ vṛkodaraḥ
|
dṛṣṭvaiva subhṛśaṃ kruddho divyamastramudairayat ||63||

64gajairgajānabhyahanadvajreṇendra ivāsurān
|
tato'ntarikṣaṃ bāṇaughai śalabhairiva pādapam ||64||

65chādayāmāsa samare gajānnighnan vṛkodaraḥ
|
tataḥ kuñjarayūthāni sametāni sahasraśaḥ ||65||

66vyadhamattarasā bhīmo meghasaṅghānivānilaḥ
|
suvarṇajālāpihitā maṇijālaiśca kuñjarāḥ ||66||

67rejurabhyadhikaṃ saṃkhye vidyutvanta ivāmbudāḥ
|
te vadhyamānā bhīmena gajā rājanvidudruvuḥ ||67||

68kecidvibhinnahṛdayāḥ kuñjarā nyapatan bhuvi
|
patitairnipatadbhiśca gajairhemavibhūṣitaiḥ ||68||

69aśobhata mahī tatra viśīrṇairiva parvataiḥ
|
dīptābhai ratnavadbhiśca patitairgajayodhibhiḥ ||69||

70rarāja bhūmiḥ patitaiḥ kṣīṇapuṇyairiva grahaiḥ
|
tato bhinnakaṭā nāgā bhinnakumbhakarāstathā ||70||

71dudruvuḥ śataśaḥ saṃkhye bhīmasenaśarāhatāḥ
|
kecidvamanto rudhiraṃ bhayārtāḥ parvatopamāḥ ||71||

72vyadravañcharaviddhāṅgā dhātucitrā ivācalāḥ
|
mahābhujagasaṅkāśau candanāgururūṣitau ||72||

73apaśyaṃ bhīmasenasya dhāturvikṣipato bhujau
|
tasya jyātalanirghoṣaṃ śrutvā'śanisamasvanam ||73||

74vimuñcantaḥ śakṛnmūtraṃ gajāḥ prādudruvurbhṛśam
|
bhīmasenasya tat karma rājannekasya dhīmataḥ
|
nighnataḥ sarvabhūtāni rudrasyeva ca nirbabhau ||74||
