Mahābhārata 3.14 (Āraṇyakaparvan)

1yudhiṣṭhira uvāca|
asānnidhyaṃ kathaṃ kṛṣṇa tavāsīdvṛṣṇinandana
|
kva cāsīdvipravāsaste kiṃ cākārṣīḥ pravāsakaḥ||1||

2kṛṣṇa uvāca|
śālvasya nagaraṃ saubhaṃ gato'haṃ bharatarṣabha
|
nihantuṃ kauravaśreṣṭha tatra me śṛṇu kāraṇam||2||

3mahātejā mahābāhuryaḥ sa rājā mahāyaśāḥ
|
damaghoṣātmajo vīraḥ śiśupālo mayā hataḥ||3||

4yajñe te bharataśreṣṭha rājasūye'rhaṇāṃ prati
|
saroṣavaśamāpanno nāmṛṣyata durātmavān||4||

5śrutvā taṃ nihataṃ śālvastīvraroṣasamanvitaḥ
|
upāyāddvārakāṃ śūnyāmihasthe mayi bhārata||5||

6sa tatra yodhito rājankumārairvṛṣṇipuṅgavaiḥ
|
āgataḥ kāmagaṃ saubhamāhatyaivaṃ nṛśaṃsavat||6||

7tato vṛṣṇipravīrāṃstānbālānhatvā bahūṃstadā
|
purodyānāni sarvāṇi bhedayāmāsa durmatiḥ||7||

8uktavāṃśca mahābāho kvāsau vṛṣṇikulādhamaḥ
|
vāsudevaḥ sa mandātmā vasudevasuto gataḥ||8||

9tasya yuddhārthino darpaṃ yuddhe nāśayitāsmyaham
|
ānartāḥ satyamākhyāta tatra gantāsmi yatra saḥ||9||

10taṃ hatvā vinivartiṣye kaṃsakeśiniṣūdanam
|
ahatvā na nivartiṣye satyenāyudhamālabhe||10||

11kvāsau kvāsāviti punastatra tatra pradhāvati
|
mayā kila raṇe yoddhuṃ kāṅkṣamāṇaḥ sa saubharāṭ||11||

12adya taṃ pāpakarmāṇaṃ kṣudraṃ viśvāsaghātinam
|
śiśupālavadhāmarṣādgamayiṣye yamakṣayam||12||

13mama pāpasvabhāvena bhrātā yena nipātitaḥ
|
śiśupālo mahīpālastaṃ vadhiṣye mahīpate||13||

14bhrātā bālaśca rājā ca na ca saṅgrāmamūrdhani
|
pramattaśca hato vīrastaṃ haniṣye janārdanam||14||

15evamādi mahārāja vilaṣya divamāsthitaḥ
|
kāmagena sa saubhena kṣiptvā māṃ kurunandana||15||

16tamaśrauṣamahaṃ gatvā yathāvṛttaḥ sa durmatiḥ
|
mayi kauravya duṣṭātmā mārttikāvatako nṛpaḥ||16||

17tato'hamapi kauravya roṣavyākulamānasaḥ
|
niścitya manasā rājanvadhāyāsya mano dadhe||17||

18ānarteṣu vimardaṃ ca kṣepaṃ cātmani kaurava
|
pravṛddhamavalepaṃ ca tasya duṣkṛtakarmaṇaḥ||18||

19tataḥ saubhavadhāyāhaṃ pratasthe pṛthivīpate
|
sa mayā sāgarāvarte dṛṣṭa āsītparīpsatā||19||

20tataḥ pradhmāpya jalajaṃ pāñcajanyamahaṃ nṛpa
|
āhūya śālvaṃ samare yuddhāya samavasthitaḥ||20||

21tanmuhūrtamabhūdyuddhaṃ tatra me dānavaiḥ saha
|
vaśībhūtāśca me sarve bhūtale ca nipātitāḥ||21||

22etatkāryaṃ mahābāho yenāhaṃ nāgamaṃ tadā
|
śrutvaiva hāstinapuraṃ dyūtaṃ cāviniyotthitam
|
drutamāgatavānyuṣmāndraṣṭukāmaḥ suduḥkhitān||22||
