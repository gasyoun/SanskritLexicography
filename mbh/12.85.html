Mahābhārata 12.85 (Śāntiparvan)

1yudhiṣṭhira uvāca|
kathaṃ svidiha rājendra pālayanpārthivaḥ prajāḥ
|
prītiṃ dharmaviśeṣeṇa kīrtimāpnoti śāśvatīm||1||

2bhīṣma uvāca|
vyavahāreṇa śuddhena prajāpālanatatparaḥ
|
prāpya dharmaṃ ca kīrtiṃ ca lokānāpnotyubhau śuciḥ||2||

3yudhiṣṭhira uvāca|
kīdṛśairvyavahāraistu kaiśca vyavaharennṛpaḥ
|
etatpṛṣṭo mahāprājña yathāvadvaktumarhasi||3||

4ye caiva pūrvaṃ kathitā guṇāste puruṣaṃ prati
|
naikasminpuruṣe hyete vidyanta iti me matiḥ||4||

5bhīṣma uvāca|
evametanmahāprājña yathā vadasi buddhiman
|
durlabhaḥ puruṣaḥ kaścidebhiryukto guṇaiḥ śubhaiḥ||5||

6kiṃtu saṃkṣepataḥ śīlaṃ prayatneneha durlabham
|
vakṣyāmi tu yathā'mātyān yādṛśāṃśca kariṣyasi||6||

7caturo brāhmaṇān vaidyān pragalbhān snātakāñśucīn
|
kṣatriyāṃśca tathā cāṣṭau balinaḥ śastrapāṇinaḥ||7||

8vaiśyānvittena sampannānekaviṃśatisaṃkhyayā
|
trīṃśca śūdrānvinītāṃśca śucīnkarmaṇi pūrvake||8||

9aṣṭābhiśca guṇairyuktaṃ sūtaṃ paurāṇikaṃ tathā
|
pañcāśadvarṣavayasaṃ pragalbhamanasūyakam||9||

10śruti-smṛtisamāyuktaṃ vinītaṃ samadarśinam
|
kārye vivadamānānāṃ śaktamartheṣvalolupam||10||

11varjitaṃ caiva vyasanaiḥ sughoraiḥ saptabhi śam
|
aṣṭānāṃ mantriṇāṃ madhye mantraṃ rājopadhārayet||11||

12tataḥ sampreṣayedrāṣṭre rāṣṭrīyāya ca darśayet
|
anena vyavahāreṇa draṣṭavyāste prajā: sadā||12||

13na cāpi gūḍhaṃ dravyaṃ te grāhyaṃ kāryopaghātakam
|
kārye khalu vipanne tvāṃ so'dharmastāṃśca pīdayet||13||

14vidraveccaiva rāṣṭraṃ te śyenātpakṣigaṇā iva
|
parisnavecca satataṃ naurviśīrṇeva sāgare||14||

15prajāḥ pālayato sabhyagadharmeṇeha bhūpateḥ
|
hārdaṃ bhayaṃ sambhavati svargaścāsya viruddhyate||15||

16atha yo dharmataḥ pāti rājāmātyo'tha vā''tmajaḥ
|
dharmāsane sanniyukto dharmamūle nararṣabha||16||

17kāryeṣvadhikṛtāḥ samyagakurvanto nṛpānugāḥ
|
ātmānaṃ purataḥ kṛtvā yāntyadhaḥ sahapārthivāḥ||17||

18balātkṛtānāṃ balibhiḥ kṛpaṇaṃ bahu jalpatām
|
nātho vai bhūmipo nityamanāthānāṃ nṛṇāṃ bhavet||18||

19tataḥ sākṣibalaṃ sādhu dvaidhavādakṛtaṃ bhavet
|
asākṣikamanāthaṃ vā parīkṣyaṃ tadviśeṣataḥ||19||

20aparādhānurūpaṃ ca daṇḍaṃ pāpeṣu dhārayet
|
viyojayeddhanairṛdānadhanānatha bandhanaiḥ||20||

21vinayeccāpi durvṛttānprahārairapi pārthivaḥ
|
sāntvenopapradānena śiṣṭāṃśca paripālayet||21||

22rājño vadhaṃ cikīrṣedyastasya citro vadho bhavet
|
ādīpakasya stenasya varṇasaṃkarikasya ca||22||

23samyak praṇayato daṇḍaṃ bhūmipasya viśāmpate
|
yuktasya vā nāstyadharmo dharma eva hi śāśvataḥ||23||

24kāmakāreṇa daṇḍaṃ tu yaḥ kuryādavicakṣaṇaḥ
|
sa ihākīrtisaṃyukto mṛto narakamṛcchati||24||

25na parasya pravādena pareṣāṃ daṇḍamarpayet
|
āgamānugamaṃ kṛtvā badhnīyānmokṣayīta vā||25||

26na tu hanyānnṛpo jātu dūtaṃ kasyāñcidāpadi
|
dūtasya hantā nirayamāviśetsacivaiḥ saha||26||

27yathoktavādinaṃ dūtaṃ kṣatradharmarato nṛpaḥ
|
yo hanyātpitarastasya bhrūṇahatyāmavāpnuyuḥ||27||

28kulīnaḥ śīlasampanno vāgmī dakṣaḥ priyaṃvadaḥ
|
yathoktavādī smṛtimāndūtaḥ syātsaptabhirguṇaiḥ||28||

29etaireva guṇairyuktaḥ pratihāro'sya rakṣitā
|
śirorakṣaśca bhavati guṇairetaiḥ samanvitaḥ||29||

30dharmaśāstrārthatattvajñaḥ sāṃdhivigrahiko bhavet
|
matimāndhṛtimān hrīmān rahasyavinigūhitā||30||

31kulīnaḥ sattvasampannaḥ śuklo'mātyaḥ praśasyate
|
etaireva guṇairyuktastathā senāpatirbhavet||31||

32vyūhayantrāyudhānāṃ ca tattvajño vikramānvitaḥ
|
varṣaśītoṣṇavātānāṃ sahiṣṇuḥ pararandhravit||32||

33viśvāsayetparāṃścaiva viśvasecca na kasyacit
|
putreṣvapi hi rājendra viśvāso na praśasyate||33||

34etacchāstrārthatattvaṃ tu mayā''khyātaṃ tavānagha
|
aviśvāso narendrāṇāṃ guhyaṃ paramamucyate||34||
