Mahābhārata 6.33 (Bhīṣmaparvan)

1śrībhagavānuvāca —
idaṃ tu te guhyatamaṃ pravakṣyāmyanasūyave
|
jñānaṃ vijñānasahitaṃ yajjñātvā mokṣyase'śubhāt ||1||

2rājavidyā rājaguhyaṃ pavitramidamuttamam
|
pratyakṣāvagamaṃ dharmyaṃ susukhaṃ kartumavyayam ||2||

3aśraddadhānāḥ puruṣā dharmasyāsya parantapa
|
aprāpya māṃ nivartante mṛtyusaṃsāravartmani ||3||

4mayā tatamidaṃ sarvaṃ jagatadavyaktamūrtinā
|
matsthāni sarvabhūtāni na cāhaṃ teṣvavasthitaḥ ||4||

5na ca matsthāni bhūtāni paśya me yogamaiśvaram
|
bhūtabhṛnna ca bhūtastho mamātmā bhūtabhāvanaḥ ||5||

6yathākāśasthito nityaṃ vāyuḥ sarvatrago mahān
|
tathā sarvāṇi bhūtāni matsthānītyupadhāraya ||6||

7sarvabhūtāni kaunteya prakṛtiṃ yānti māmikām
|
kalpakṣaye punastāni kalpādau visṛjāmyaham ||7||

8prakṛtiṃ svāmavaṣṭabhya visṛjāmi punaḥ punaḥ
|
bhūtagrāmamimaṃ kṛtsnamavaśaṃ prakṛtervaśāt ||8||

9na ca māṃ tāni karmāṇi nibadhnanti dhanañjaya
|
udāsīnavadāsīnamasaktaṃ teṣu karmasu ||9||

10mayādhyakṣeṇa prakṛtiḥ sūyate sacarācaram
|
hetunānena kaunteya jagadviparivartate ||10||

11avajānanti māṃ mūḍhā mānuṣīṃ tanumāśritam
|
paraṃ bhāvamajānanto mama bhūtamaheśvaram ||11||

12moghāśā moghakarmāṇo moghajñānā vicetasaḥ
|
rākṣasīmāsurīṃ caiva prakṛtiṃ mohinīṃ śritāḥ ||12||

13mahātmānastu māṃ pārtha daivīṃ prakṛtimāśritāḥ
|
bhajantyananyamanaso jñātvā bhūtādimavyayam ||13||

14satataṃ kīrtayanto māṃ yatantaśca dṛḍhavratāḥ
|
namasyantaśca māṃ bhaktyā nityayuktā upāsate ||14||

15jñānayajñena cāpyanye yajanto māmupāsate
|
ekatvena pṛthaktvena bahudhā viśvatomukham ||15||

16ahaṃ kraturahaṃ yajñaḥ svadhāhamahamauṣadham
|
mantro'hamahamevājyamahamagnirahaṃ hutam ||16||

17pitāhamasya jagato mātā dhātā pitāmahaḥ
|
vedyaṃ pavitramoṅkāra ṛksāma yajureva ca ||17||

18gatirbhartā prabhuḥ sākṣī nivāsaḥ śaraṇaṃ suhṛt
|
prabhavaḥ pralayaḥ sthānaṃ nidhānaṃ bījamavyayam ||18||

19tapāmyahamahaṃ varṣaṃ nigṛhṇāmyutsṛjāmi ca
|
amṛtaṃ caiva mṛtyuśca sadasaccāhamarjuna ||19||

20traividyā māṃ somapāḥ pūtapāpā yajñairiṣṭvā svargatiṃ prārthayante
|
te puṇyamāsādya surendralokamaśnanti divyāndivi devabhogān ||20||

21te taṃ bhuktvā svargalokaṃ viśālaṃ kṣīṇe puṇye martyalokaṃ viśanti
|
evaṃ trayīdharmamanuprapannā gatāgataṃ kāmakāmā labhante ||21||

22ananyāścintayanto māṃ ye janāḥ paryupāsate
|
teṣāṃ nityābhiyuktānāṃ yogakṣemaṃ vahāmyaham ||22||

23ye'pyanyadevatābhaktā yajante śraddhayānvitāḥ
|
te'pi māmeva kaunteya yajantyavidhipūrvakam ||23||

24ahaṃ hi sarvayajñānāṃ bhoktā ca prabhureva ca
|
na tu māmabhijānanti tattvenātaścyavanti te ||24||

25yānti devavratā devānpitṝnyānti pitṛvratāḥ
|
bhūtāni yānti bhūtejyā yānti madyājino'pi mām ||25||

26patraṃ puṣpaṃ phalaṃ toyaṃ yo me bhaktyā prayacchati
|
tadahaṃ bhaktyupahṛtamaśnāmi prayatātmanaḥ ||26||

27yatkaroṣi yadaśnāsi yajjuhoṣi dadāsi yat
|
yattapasyasi kaunteya tatkuruṣva madarpaṇam ||27||

28śubhāśubhaphalairevaṃ mokṣyase karmabandhanaiḥ
|
saṃnyāsayogayuktātmā vimukto māmupaiṣyasi ||28||

29samo'haṃ sarvabhūteṣu na me dveṣyo'sti na priyaḥ
|
ye bhajanti tu māṃ bhaktyā mayi te teṣu cāpyaham ||29||

30api cetsudurācāro bhajate māmananyabhāk
|
sādhureva sa mantavyaḥ samyagvyavasito hi saḥ ||30||

31kṣipraṃ bhavati dharmātmā śaśvacchāntiṃ nigacchati
|
kaunteya pratijānīhi na me bhaktaḥ praṇaśyati ||31||

32māṃ hi pārtha vyapāśritya ye'pi syuḥ pāpayonayaḥ
|
striyo vaiśyāstathā śūdrāste'pi yānti parāṃ gatim ||32||

33kiṃ punarbrāhmaṇāḥ puṇyā bhaktā rājarṣayastathā
|
anityamasukhaṃ lokamimaṃ prāpya bhajasva mām ||33||

34manmanā bhava madbhakto madyājī māṃ namaskuru
|
māmevaiṣyasi yuktvaivamātmānaṃ matparāyaṇaḥ ||34||
