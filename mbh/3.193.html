Mahābhārata 3.193 (Āraṇyakaparvan)

1vaiśampāyana uvāca|
mārkaṇḍeyamṛṣayo brāhmaṇā yudhiṣṭhiraśca paryapṛcchannṛṣiḥ kena dīrghāyurāsīdbako mārkaṇḍeyastu tān sarvānuvāca||1||

2mahātapā dīrghāyuśca bako rājarṣirnātra kāryā vicāraṇā||2||

3etacchrutvā tu kaunteyo bhrātṛbhiḥ saha bhārata
|
mārkaṇḍeyaṃ paryapṛcchaddharmarājo yudhiṣṭhiraḥ||3||

4bakadālbhyau mahātmānau śrūyete cirajīvinau
|
sakhāyau devarājasya tāvṛṣī lokasammatau||4||

5etadicchāmi bhagavan bakaśakrasamāgamam
|
sukhaduḥkhasamāyuktaṃ tattvena kathayasva me||5||

6mārkaṇḍeya uvāca|
vṛtte devāsure rājan saṅgrāme lomaharṣaṇe
|
trayāṇāmapi lokānāmindro lokādhipo'bhavat||6||

7samyagvarṣati parjanye sasyasampada uttamāḥ
|
nirāmayāḥ sudharmiṣṭhāḥ prajā dharmaparāyaṇāḥ||7||

8muditaśca janaḥ sarvaḥ svadharmeṣu vyavasthitaḥ
|
tāḥ prajā muditāḥ sarvā dṛṣṭvā balaniṣūdanaḥ||8||

9tatastu mudito rājan devarājaḥ śatakratuḥ
|
airāvataṃ samāsthāya tāḥ paśyan muditāḥ prajāḥ||9||

10āśramāṃśca vicitrāṃśca nadīśca vividhāḥ śubhāḥ
|
nagarāṇi samṛddhāni kheṭān janapadāṃstathā||10||

11prajāpālanadakṣāṃśca narendrān dharmacāriṇaḥ
|
udapānaṃ prapā vāpī taḍāgāni sarāṃsi ca||11||

12nānābrahmasamācāraiḥ sevitāni dvijottamaiḥ
|
tato'vatīrya ramyāyāṃ pṛthvyāṃ rājañchatakratuḥ||12||

13tatra ramye śive deśe bahuvṛkṣasamākule
|
pūrvasyāṃ diśi ramyāyāṃ samudrābhyāśato nṛpa||13||

14tatrāśramapadaṃ ramyaṃ mṛgadvijaniṣevitam
|
tatrāśramapade ramye bakaṃ paśyati devarāṭ||14||

15bakastu dṛṣṭvā devendraṃ dṛḍhaṃ prītamanā'bhavat
|
pādyāsanārghadānena phalamūlairathārcayat||15||

16sukhopaviṣṭo varadastatastu balasūdanaḥ
|
tataḥ praśnaṃ bakaṃ deva uvāca tridaśeśvaraḥ||16||

17śataṃ varṣasahasrāṇi mune jātasya te'nagha
|
samākhyāhi mama brahman kiṃ duḥkhaṃ cirajīvinām||17||

18baka uvāca|
apriyaiḥ saha saṃvāsaḥ priyaiścāpi vinābhavaḥ
|
asadbhiḥ samprayogaśca tadduḥkhaṃ cirajīvinām||18||

19putradāravināśo'tra jñātīnāṃ suhṛdāmapi
|
pareṣvāyattatā kṛcchraṃ kiṃ nu duḥkhataraṃ tataḥ||19||

20nānyadduḥkhataraṃ kiñcillokeṣu pratibhāti me
|
athairvihīnaḥ puruṣaḥ paraiḥ samparibhūyate||20||

21akulānāṃ kule bhāvaṃ kulīnānāṃ kulakṣayam
|
saṃyogaṃ viprayogaṃ ca paśyanti cirajīvinaḥ||21||

22api pratyakṣamevaitat tava deva śatakrato
|
akulānāṃ samṛddhānāṃ kathaṃ kulaviparyayaḥ||22||

23deva-dānava-gandharva-manuṣyoraga-rākṣasāḥ
|
prāpnuvanti viparyāsaṃ kiṃ nu duḥkhataraṃ tataḥ||23||

24kule jātāśca kliśyante dauṣkuleyavaśānugāḥ
|
āḍhyairdaridrā'vamatāḥ kiṃ nu duḥkhataraṃ tataḥ||24||

25loke vaidharmyametat tu dṛśyate bahuvistaram
|
hīnajñānāśca dṛśyante kliśyante prājñakovidāḥ||25||

26bahuduḥkhaparikleśaṃ mānuṣyamiha dṛśyate
|
indra uvāca|
punareva mahābhāga devarṣigaṇasevita||26||

27samākhyāhi mama brahman kiṃ sukhaṃ cirajīvinām
|
baka uvāca|
aṣṭame dvādaśe vā'pi śākaṃ yaḥ pacate gṛhe||27||

28kubhitrāṇyanapāśritya kiṃ vai sukhataraṃ tataḥ
|
yatrāhāni na gaṇyante nainamāhurmahāśanam||28||

29api śākaṃ pacānasya sukhaṃ vai maghavan gṛhe
|
arjitaṃ svena vīryeṇa nāpyapāśritya kañcana||29||

30phalaśākamapi śreyo bhoktuṃ hyakṛpaṇaṃ gṛhe
|
parasya tu gṛhe bhoktuḥ paribhūtasya nityaśaḥ||30||

31sumṛṣṭamapi na śreyo vikalpo'yamataḥ satām
|
śvavat kīlālapo yastu parānnaṃ bhoktumicchati||31||

32dhigastu tasya tadbhuktaṃ kṛpaṇasya durātmanaḥ
|
yo datvā'tithibhūtebhyaḥ pitṛbhyaśca dvijottamaḥ||32||

33śiṣṭānyannāni yo bhuṅkte kiṃ vai sukhataraṃ tataḥ
|
ato mṛṣṭataraṃ nānyat pūtaṃ kiñcicchatakrato||33||

34datvā yastvatithibhyo vai bhuṅkte tenaiva nityaśaḥ
|
yāvato hyandhasaḥ piṇḍānaśnāti satataṃ dvijaḥ||34||

35tāvatāṃ gosahastrāṇāṃ phalaṃ prāpnoti dāyakaḥ
|
yadeno yauvanakṛtaṃ tat sarvaṃ naśyate dhruvam||35||

36sadakṣiṇasya bhuktasya dvijasya tu kare gatam
|
yadvāri vāriṇā siñcet taddhyenastarate kṣaṇāt||36||

37etāścānyāśca vai bahvīḥ kathayitvā kathāḥ śubhāḥ
|
bakena saha devendra āpṛcchya tridivaṃ gataḥ||37||
