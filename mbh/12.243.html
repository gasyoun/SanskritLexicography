Mahābhārata 12.243 (Śāntiparvan)

1vyāsa uvāca |
dvitīyamāyuṣo bhāgaṃ gṛhamedhī gṛhe vaset
|
dharmalabdhairyuto dārairagnīnāhṛtya suvrataḥ ||1||

2gṛhasthavṛttayaścaiva catasraḥ kavibhiḥ smṛtāḥ
|
kusūladhānyaḥ prathamaḥ kumbhadhānyastvanantaram ||2||

3aśvastano'tha kāpotīmāśrito vṛttimāharet
|
teṣāṃ paraḥ paro jyāyāndharmato dharmajittamaḥ ||3||

4ṣaṭkarmā vartayatyekastribhiranyaḥ pravartate
|
dvābhyāmekaścaturthastu brahmasatre vyavasthitaḥ ||4||

5gṛhamedhivratānyatra mahāntīha pracakṣate
|
nātmārthe pācayedannaṃ na vṛthā ghātayetpaśūn ||5||

6prāṇī vā yadi vā'prāṇī saṃskāraṃ yajuṣārhati
|
na divā prasvapejjātu na pūrvāpararātriṣu ||6||

7na bhuñjītāntarā kāle nānṛtāvāhvayet striyam
|
nāsyānaśnan gṛhe vipre vasetkaścidapūjitaḥ ||7||

8tathā'syātithayaḥ pūjyā havyakavyavahāḥ sadā
|
vedavidyāvratasnātāḥ śrotriyā vedapāragāḥ ||8||

9svadharmajīvino dāntāḥ kriyāvantastapasvinaḥ
|
teṣāṃ havyaṃ ca kavyaṃ cāpyarhaṇārthaṃ vidhīyate ||9||

10nakharaiḥ samprayātasya svadharmajñāpakasya ca
|
apaviddhāgnihotrasya gurorvālīkakāriṇaḥ ||10||

11saṃvibhāgo'tra bhūtānāṃ sarveṣāmeva śiṣyate
|
tathaivāpacamānebhyaḥ pradeyaṃ gṛhamedhinā ||11||

12vighasāśī bhavennityaṃ nityaṃ cāmṛtabhojanaḥ
|
amṛtaṃ yajñaśeṣaṃ syādbhojanaṃ haviṣā samam ||12||

13bhṛtyaśeṣaṃ tu yo'śnāti tamāhurvighasāśinam
|
vighasaṃ bhṛtyaśeṣaṃ tu yajñaśeṣamathāmṛtam ||13||

14svadāranirato dānto hyanasūyurjitendriyaḥ
|
ṛtvikpurohitācāryairmātulātithisaṃśritaiḥ ||14||

15vṛddhabālāturairvedyairjñātisambandhibāndhavaiḥ
|
mātāpitṛbhyāṃ jāmībhirbhrātrā putreṇa bhāryayā ||15||

16duhitrā dāsavargeṇa vivādaṃ na samācaret
|
etānvimucya saṃvādānsarvapāpairvimucyate ||16||

17etairjitastu jayati sarvā~llokān na saṃśayaḥ
|
ācāryo brahmalokeśaḥ prājāpatye pitā prabhuḥ ||17||

18atithistvindralokasya devalokasya cartvijaḥ
|
jāmayo'psarasāṃ loke vaiśvadeve tu jñātayaḥ ||18||

19sambandhibāndhavā dikṣu pṛthivyāṃ mātṛmātulau
|
vṛddhabālāturakṛśāstvākāśe prabhaviṣṇavaḥ ||19||

20bhrātā jyeṣṭhaḥ samaḥ pitrā bhāryā putraḥ svakā tanuḥ
|
chāyā svā dāsavargaśca duhitā kṛpaṇaṃ param ||20||

21tasmādetairadhikṣiptaḥ sahennityamasaṃjvaraḥ
|
gṛhadharmaparo vidvān dharmaśīlo jitaklamaḥ ||21||

22na cārthabaddhaḥ karmāṇi dharmavānkaścidācaret
|
gṛhasthavṛttayastisrastāsāṃ niḥśreyasaṃ param ||22||

23paraṃ paraṃ tathaivāhuścāturāśramyameva tat
|
yathoktā niyamāsteṣāṃ sarvaṃ kāryaṃ bubhūṣatā ||23||

24kumbhadhānyairuñchaśilaiḥ kāpotīṃ cāsthitāstathā
|
yasmiṃścaite vasantyarhāstadrāṣṭramabhivardhate ||24||

25pūrvān daśa daśa parān punāti ca pitāmahān
|
gṛhasthavṛttīścāpyetā vartayedyo gatavyathaḥ ||25||

26sa cakradharalokānāṃ sadṛśīmāpnuyādgatim
|
jitendriyāṇāmathavā gatireṣā vidhīyate ||26||

27svargaloko gṛhasthānāmudāramanasāṃ hitaḥ
|
svargo vimānasaṃyukto vedadṛṣṭaḥ supuṣpitaḥ ||27||

28svargaloko gṛhasthānāṃ pratiṣṭhā niyatātmanām
|
brahmaṇā vihitā yonireṣā yasmādvidhīyate
|
dvitīyaṃ kramaśaḥ prāpya svargaloke mahīyate ||28||

29ataḥ paraṃ paramamudāramāśramaṃ tṛtīyamāhustyajatāṃ kalevaram
|
vanaukasāṃ gṛhapatināmanuttamaṃ śṛṇuṣva saṃśliṣṭaśarīrakāriṇām ||29||
