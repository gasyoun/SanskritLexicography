Mahābhārata 4.2 (Virāṭaparvan)

1bhīmasena uvāca|paurogavo bruvāṇo'haṃ ballavo nāma bhārata
|
upasthāsyāmi rājānaṃ virāṭamiti me matiḥ ||1||

2sūpānasya kariṣyāmi kuśalo'smi mahānase
|
kṛtapūrvāṇi yānyasya vyañjanāni suśikṣitaiḥ ||2||

3tānyapyabhibhāvaṣyāmi prītiṃ sañjanayannaham
|
āhariṣyāmi dārūṇāṃ nicayān mahato'pi ca ||3||

4yat prekṣya vipulaṃ karma rājā saṃyokṣyate sa mām
|
amānuṣāṇi kurvāṇastāni karmāṇi bhārata ||4||

5rājñastasya pare preṣyā maṃsyante māṃ yathā nṛpam
|
bhakṣyānnarasapānānāṃ bhaviṣyāmi tatheśvaraḥ ||5||

6dvipā vā balino rājan vṛṣabhā vā mahābalāḥ
|
vinigrāhyā yadi mayā nigrahīṣyāmi tānapi ||6||

7ye ca kecinniyotsyanti samājeṣu niyodhakāḥ
|
tānahaṃ hi niyotsyāmi rati tasya vivardhayan ||7||

8na tvetān yuddhyamānān vai haniṣyāmi kathañcana
|
tathaitān pātayiṣyāmi yathā yāsyanti na kṣayam ||8||

9ārāliko govikartā sūpakartā niyodhakaḥ
|
āsaṃ yudhiṣṭhirasyāhamiti vakṣyāmi pṛcchataḥ ||9||

10ātmānamātmanā rakṣaṃścariṣyāmi viśāmpate
|
ityetat pratijānāmi vihariṣyāmyahaṃ yathā ||10||

11yudhiṣṭhira uvāca|yamagnirbrāhmaṇo bhūtvā samāgacchannṛṇāṃ varam
|
didhakṣuḥ khāṇḍavaṃ dāvaṃ dāśārhasahitaṃ purā ||11||

12mahābalaṃ mahābāhumajitaṃ kurunandanam
|
so'yaṃ kiṃ karma kaunteyaḥ kariṣyati dhanañjayaḥ ||12||

13yo'yamāsādya taṃ dāvaṃ tarpayāmāsa pāvakam
|
vijityaikarathenendraṃ hatvā pannagarākṣasān ||13||

14vāsukeḥ sarparājasya svasāraṃ hṛtavāṃśca yaḥ
|
śreṣṭho yaḥ pratiyodhānāṃ so'rjunaḥ kiṃ kariṣyati ||14||

15sūryaḥ pratapatāṃ śreṣṭho dvipadāṃ brāhmaṇo varaḥ
|
āśīviṣaśca sarpāṇāmagnistejasvināṃ varaḥ ||15||

16āyudhānāṃ varaṃ vajra kakudmī ca gavāṃ varaḥ
|
hradānāmudadhiḥ śreṣṭhaḥ parjanyo varṣatāṃ varaḥ ||16||

17dhṛtarāṣṭraśca nāgānāṃ hastiṣvairāvaṇo varaḥ
|
putraḥ priyāṇāmadhiko bhāryā ca suhṛdāṃ varā ||17||

18yathaitāni viśiṣṭāni jātyāṃ jātyāṃ vṛkodara
|
evaṃ yuvā guḍākeśaḥ śreṣṭhaḥ sarvadhanuṣmatām ||18||

19so'yamindrādanavaro vāsudevānmahādyutiḥ
|
gāṇḍīvadhanvā bībhatsuḥ śvetāśvaḥ kiṃ kariṣyati ||19||

20uṣitvā pañca varṣāṇi sahasrākṣasya veśmani
|
astrayogaṃ samāsādya svavīryānmānuṣādbhutam
|
divyānyastrāṇi cāptāni devarūpeṇa bhāsvatā ||20||

21yaṃ manye dvādaśaṃ rudramādityānāṃ trayodaśam
|
vasūnāṃ navamaṃ manye grahāṇāṃ daśamaṃ tathā ||21||

22yasya bāhū samau dīrghau jyāghātakaṭhinatvacau
|
dakṣiṇe caiva savye ca gavāmiva vahaḥ kṛtaḥ ||22||

23himavāniva śailānāṃ samudraḥ saritāmiva
|
tridaśānāṃ yathā śakro vasūnāmiva havyavāṭ ||23||

24mṛgāṇāmiva śārdūlo garuḍaḥ patatāmiva
|
varaḥ sannahyamānānāṃ so'rjunaḥ kiṃ kariṣyati ||24||

25arjuna uvāca|pratijñāṃ ṣaṇḍhako'smīti kariṣyāmi mahīpate
|
jyāghātau hi mahāntau me saṃvartuṃ nṛpa duṣkarau ||25||

26valayaiśchādayiṣyāmitabāhū kiṇakṛtāvimau
|
karṇayoḥ pratimucyāhaṃ kuṇḍale jvalanaprabhe ||26||

27pinaddhakambuḥ pāṇibhyāṃ tṛtīyāṃ prakṛtiṃ gataḥ
|
veṇīkṛtaśirā rājan nāmnā caiva bṛhannalā ||27||

28paṭhannākhyāyikāścaiva strībhāvena punaḥ punaḥ
|
ramayiṣye mahīpālamanyāṃścāntaḥpure janān ||28||

29gītaṃ nṛtyaṃ vicitraṃ ca vāditraṃ vividhaṃ tathā
|
śikṣayiṣyāmyahaṃ rājan virāṭasya purastriyaḥ ||29||

30prajānāṃ samudācāraṃ bahukarma kṛtaṃ vadan
|
chāda‌yiṣyāmi kaunteya māyayā''tmānamātmanā ||30||

31yudhiṣṭhirasya gehe vai draupadyāḥ paricārikā
|
uṣitā'smīti vakṣyāmi pṛṣṭo rājñā ca pāṇḍava ||31||

32etena vidhinā channaḥ kṛtakena yathā nalaḥ
|
vihariṣyāmi rājendra virāṭabhavane sukham ||32||
