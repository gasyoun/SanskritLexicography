Mahābhārata 1.43 (Ādiparvan)

1takṣaka uvāca|
yadi daṣṭaṃ mayeha tvaṃ śaktaḥ kiṃciccikitsitum
|
tato vṛkṣaṃ mayā daṣṭamimaṃ jīvaya kāśyapa||1||

2paraṃ mantrabalaṃ yatte taddarśaya yatasva ca
|
nyagrodhamenaṃ dhakṣyāmi paśyataste dvijottama||2||

3kāśyapa uvāca|
daśa nāgendra vṛkṣaṃ tvaṃ yadyetadabhimanyase
|
ahamenaṃ tvayā daṣṭaṃ jīvayiṣye bhujaṃgama||3||

4sautiruvāca|
evamuktaḥ sa nāgendraḥ kāśyapena mahātmanā
|
adaśadvṛkṣamabhyetya nyagrodhaṃ pannagottamaḥ||4||

5sa vṛkṣastena daṣṭastu pannagena mahātmanā
|
āśīviṣaviṣopetaḥ prajajvāla samantataḥ||5||

6taṃ dagdhvā sa nagaṃ nāgaḥ kāśyapaṃ punarabravīt
|
kuru yatnaṃ dvijaśreṣṭha jīvayaiva vanaspatim||6||

7sautiruvāca|
bhasmībhūtaṃ tato vṛkṣaṃ pannagendrasya tejasā
|
bhasma sarvaṃ samāhṛtya kāśyapo vākyamabravīt||7||

8vidyābalaṃ pannagendra paśya me'dya vanaspatau
|
ahaṃ saṃjīvayāmyenaṃ paśyataste bhujaṃgama||8||

9tataḥ sa bhagavānvidvānkāśyapo dvijasattamaḥ
|
bhasmarāśīkṛtaṃ vṛkṣaṃ vidyayā samajīvayat||9||

10aṅkuraṃ kṛtavāṃstatra tataḥ parṇadvayānvitam
|
palāśinaṃ śākhinaṃ ca tathā viṭapinaṃ punaḥ||10||

11taṃ dṛṣṭvā jīvitaṃ vṛkṣaṃ kāśyapena mahātmanā
|
uvāca takṣako brahmannaitadatyadbhutaṃ tvayi||11||

12dvijendra yadviṣaṃ hanyā mama vā madvidhasya vā
|
kaṃ tvamarthamabhiprepsuryāsi tatra tapodhana||12||

13yatte'bhilaṣitaṃ prāptaṃ phalaṃ tasmānnṛpottamāt
|
ahameva pradāsyāmi tatte yadyapi durlabham||13||

14vipraśāpābhibhūte ca kṣīṇāyuṣi narādhipe
|
ghaṭamānasya te vipra siddhiḥ saṃśayitā bhavet||14||

15tato yaśaḥ pradīptaṃ te triṣu lokeṣu viśrutam
|
niraṃśuriva gharmāṃśurantardhānamito vrajet||15||

16kāśyapa uvāca|
dhanārthī yāmyahaṃ tatra tanme dehi bhujaṃgama
|
tato'haṃ vinivartiṣye svāpateyaṃ pragṛhya vai||16||

17takṣaka uvāca|
yāvaddhanaṃ prārthayase tasmādrājñastato'dhikam
|
ahameva pradāsyāmi nivartasva dvijottama||17||

18sautiruvāca|
takṣakasya vacaḥ śrutvā kāśyapo dvijasattamaḥ
|
pradadhyau sumahātejā rājānaṃ prati buddhimān||18||

19divyajñānaḥ sa tejasvī jñātvā taṃ nṛpatiṃ tadā
|
kṣīṇāyuṣaṃ pāṇḍaveyamapāvartata kāśyapaḥ||19||

20labdhvā vittaṃ munivarastakṣakādyāvadīpsitam
|
nivṛtte kāśyape tasminsamayena mahātmani||20||

21jagāma takṣakastūrṇaṃ nagaraṃ nāgasāhvayam
|
atha śuśrāva gacchansa takṣako jagatīpatim||21||

22mantrairviṣaharairdivyai rakṣyamāṇaṃ prayatnataḥ
|
sautiruvāca|
sa cintayāmāsa tadā māyāyogena pārthivaḥ||22||

23mayā vañcayitavyo'sau ka upāyo bhavediti
|
tatastāpasarūpeṇa prāhiṇotsa bhujaṃgamān||23||

24phalapatrodakaṃ gṛhya rājñe nāgo'tha takṣakaḥ
|
takṣaka uvāca|
gacchadhvaṃ yūyamavyagrā rājānaṃ kāryavattayā||24||

25phalapatrodakaṃ nāgāḥ pratigrāhayituṃ nṛpam
|
sautiruvāca|
te takṣakasamādiṣṭāstathā cakrurbhujaṅgamāḥ||25||

26upaninyustathā rājñe darbhānambhaḥ phalāni ca
|
tacca sarvaṃ sa rājendraḥ pratijagrāha vīryavān||26||

27kṛtvā teṣāṃ ca kāryāṇi gamyatāmityuvāca tān
|
gateṣu teṣu nāgeṣu tāpasacchadmarūpiṣu||27||

28amātyānsuhṛdaścaiva provāca sa narādhipaḥ
|
bhakṣayantu bhavanto vai svādūnīmāni sarvaśaḥ||28||

29tāpasairupanītāni phalāni sahitā mayā
|
tato rājā sasacivaḥ phalānyādātumaicchata||29||

30vidhinā saṃprayukto vai ṛṣivākyena tena tu
|
yasminneva phale nāgastamevābhakṣayatsvayam||30||

31tato bhakṣayatastasya phalātkṛmirabhūdaṇuḥ
|
hrasvakaḥ kṛṣṇanayanastāmravarṇo'tha śaunaka||31||

32sa taṃ gṛhya nṛpaśreṣṭhaḥ sacivānidamabravīt
|
astamabhyeti savitā viṣādadya na me bhayam||32||

33satyavāgastu sa muniḥ kṛmirmāṃ daśatāmayam
|
takṣako nāma bhūtvā vai tathā parihṛtaṃ bhavet||33||

34te cainamanvavartanta mantriṇaḥ kālacoditāḥ
|
evamuktvā sa rājendro grīvāyāṃ sanniveśya ha||34||

35kṛmikaṃ prāhasattūrṇaṃ mumūrṣurnaṣṭacetanaḥ
|
prahasanneva bhogena takṣakeṇābhiveṣṭitaḥ||35||

36tasmātphalādviniṣkramya yattadrājñe niveditam
|
veṣṭayitvā ca bhogena vinadya ca mahāsvanam
|
adaśatpṛthivīpālaṃ takṣakaḥ pannageśvaraḥ||36||
