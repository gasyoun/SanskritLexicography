Mahābhārata 13.104 (Anuśāsanaparvan)

1yudhiṣṭhira uvāca|
śatāyuruktaḥ puruṣaḥ śatavīryaśca jāyate
|
kasmānmriyante puruṣā bālā api pitāmaha ||1||

2āyuṣmānkena bhavati alpāyurvā'pi mānavaḥ
|
kena vā labhate kīrtiṃ kena vā labhate śriyam ||2||

3tapasā brahmacaryeṇa japahomaistathauṣadhaiḥ
|
karmaṇā manasā vācā tanme brūhi pitāmaha ||3||

4bhīṣma uvāca|
atra te'haṃ pravakṣyāmi yanmāṃ tvamanupṛcchasi
|
alpāyuryena bhavati dīrghāyurvā'pi mānavaḥ ||4||

5yena vā labhate kīrtiṃ yena vā labhate śriyam
|
yathā vartayanpuruṣaḥ śreyasā saṃprayujyate ||5||

6ācārāllabhate hyāyurācārāllabhate śriyam
|
ācārātkīrtimāpnoti puruṣaḥ pretya ceha ca ||6||

7durācāro hi puruṣo nehāyurvindate mahat
|
trasanti yasmādbhūtāni tathā paribhavanti ca ||7||

8tasmātkuryādihācāraṃ yadicchedbhūtimātmanaḥ
|
api pāpaśarīrasya ācāro hantyalakṣaṇam ||8||

9ācāralakṣaṇo dharmaḥ santaścāritralakṣaṇāḥ
|
sādhūnāṃ ca yathāvṛttametadācāralakṣaṇam ||9||

10apyadṛṣṭaṃ śravādeva puruṣaṃ dharmacāriṇam
|
bhūtikarmāṇi kurvāṇaṃ taṃ janāḥ kurvate priyam ||10||

11ye nāstikā niṣkriyāśca guruśāstrābhilaṃdhinaḥ
|
adharmajñā durācārāste bhavanti gatāyuṣaḥ ||11||

12viśīlā bhinnamaryādā nityaṃ saṃkīrṇamaithunāḥ
|
alpāyuṣo bhavantīha narā nirayagāminaḥ ||12||

13sarvalakṣaṇahīnopi samudācāravānnaraḥ
|
śraddadhāno'nasūyuśca śataṃ varṣāṇi jīvati ||13||

14akrodhanaḥ satyavādī bhūtānāmavihiṃsakaḥ
|
anasūyurajihmaśca śataṃ varṣāṇi jīvati ||14||

15loṣṭhamardī tṛṇacchedī nakhakhādī ca yo naraḥ
|
nityocchiṣṭaḥ saṃkusuko nehāyurvindate mahat ||15||

16brāhme muhūrte budhyeta dharmārthau cānucintayet
|
utthāyācamya tiṣṭheta pūrvāṃ sandhyāṃ kṛtāñjaliḥ ||16||

17evamevāparāṃ sandhyāṃ samupāsīta vāgyataḥ
|
nekṣetādityamudyantaṃ nāstaṃ yāntaṃ kadācana ||17||

18nopasṛṣṭaṃ na vāristhaṃ na madhyaṃ nabhaso gatam
|
ṛṣayo nityasandhyatvāddīrghamāyuravāpnuvan ||18||

19tasmāttiṣṭhetsadā pūrvāṃ paścimāṃ caiva vāgyataḥ
|
ye na pūrvāmupāsante dvijāḥ sandhyāṃ na paścimām ||19||

20sarvāṃstān dhārmiko rājā śūdrakarmāṇi kārayet
|
paradārā na gantavyā sarvavarṇeṣu karhicit ||20||

21na hīdṛśamanāyuṣyaṃ loke kiñcana vidyate
|
yādṛśaṃ puruṣasyeha paradāropasevanam ||21||

22yāvanto romakūpāḥ syuḥstrīṇāṃ gātreṣu nirmitāḥ
|
tāvadvarṣasahasrāṇi narakaṃ paryupāsate ||22||

23prasādhanaṃ ca keśānāmaṃ janaṃ dantadhāvanam
|
pūrvāhṇa eva kāryāṇi devatānāṃ ca pūjanam ||23||

24purīṣamūtre nodīkṣennādhitiṣṭhatkadācana
|
nātikalyaṃ nātisāyaṃ na ca madhyaṃdine sthite ||24||

25nājñātaiḥ saha gaccheta naiko na vṛṣalaiḥ saha
|
panthā deyo brāhmaṇāya gobhyo rājabhya eva ca ||25||

26vṛddhāya bhārataptāya garbhiṇyai durbalāya ca
|
pradakṣiṇaṃ ca kurvīta parijñātānvanaspatīn ||26||

27catuṣpathānprakurvīta sarvāneva pradakṣiṇān
|
madhyandine niśākāle ardharātre ca sarvadā ||27||

28catuṣpathaṃ na seveta ubhe saṃdhye tathaiva ca
|
upānahau ca vastraṃ ca dhṛtamanyairna dhārayet
|
brahmacārī ca nityaṃ syātpādaṃ pādena nākramet ||28||

29amāvāsyāṃ paurṇamāsyāṃ caturdaśyāṃ ca sarvaśaḥ
|
aṣṭamyāṃ sarvapakṣāṇāṃ brahmacārī sadā bhavet ||29||

30vṛthāmāṃsaṃ na khādeta pṛṣṭhamāṃsaṃ tathaiva ca
|
ākrośaṃ parivādaṃ ca paiśunyaṃ ca vivarjayet ||30||

31nāruntudaḥ syānna nṛśaṃsavādī na hīnataḥ paramabhyādadīta
|
yayā'sya vācā para udvijeta na tāṃ vadedruśatīṃ pāpalokyām ||31||

32vāksāyakā vadanānniṣpatanti yairāhataḥ śocati rājyahāni
|
parasya vā marmasu ye patanti tānpaṇḍito nāvasṛjetpareṣu ||32||

33rohate sāyakairviddhaṃ vanaṃ paraśunā hatam
|
vācā duruktaṃ bībhatsaṃ na saṃrohati vākkṣatam ||33||

34karṇinālīkanārācānnirharanti śarīrataḥ
|
vākraśalyastu na nirhartuṃ śakyo hṛdiśayo hi saḥ ||34||

35hīnāṅgānatiriktāṅgānvidyāhīnānvigarhitān
|
rūpadraviṇahīnāṃśca satyahīnāṃśca nākṣipet ||35||

36nāstikyaṃ vedanindāṃ ca devatānāṃ ca kutsanam
|
dveṣastambhobhimānaṃ ca taikṣṇyaṃ ca parivarjayet ||36||

37parasya daṇḍaṃ nodyacchetkruddho nainaṃ nipātayet
|
anyatra putrācchiṣyācca śikṣārthaṃ tāḍanaṃ smṛtam ||37||

38na brāhmaṇānparivadennakṣatrāṇi na nirdiśet
|
tithiṃ pakṣasya na brūyāttathāsyāyurna riṣyate ||38||

39kṛtvā mūtrapurīṣe tu rathyāmākramya vā punaḥ
|
pādaprakṣālanaṃ kuryātsvādhyāye bhojane tathā ||39||

40trīṇi devāḥ pavitrāṇi brāhmaṇānāmakalpayan
|
adṛṣṭamadbhirnirṇiktaṃ yacca vācā praśasyate ||40||

41saṃyāvaṃ kṛsaraṃ māṃsaṃ śaṣkulīṃ pāyasaṃ tathā
|
ātmārthaṃ na prakartavyaṃ devārthaṃ tu prakalpayet ||41||

42nityamagniṃ paricaredbhikṣāṃ dadyācca nityadā
|
vāgyato dantakāṣṭhaṃ ca nityameva samācaret ||42||

43nacābhyuditaśāyī syātprāyaścittī tathā bhavet
|
mātāpitaramutthāya pūrvamevābhivādayet ||43||

44ācāryamathavāpyanyaṃ tathāyurvindate mahat
|
varjayeddantakāṣṭhāni varjanīyāni nityaśaḥ ||44||

45bhakṣayecchāstradṛṣṭāni parvasvapi vivarjayet
|
uda‌ṅmukhaśca satataṃ śaucaṃ kuryātsamāhitaḥ ||45||

46akṛtvā devapūjāṃ ca nācareddantadhāvanam
|
akṛtvā devapūjāṃ ca nābhigacchetkadācana
|
anyatra tu guruṃ vṛddhaṃ dhārmikaṃ vā vicakṣaṇam ||46||

47avalokyo na cādarśo malino buddhimattaraiḥ
|
na cājñātāṃ striyaṃ gacchedgarbhiṇīṃ vā kadācana ||47||

48u‌dakśirā na svapeta tathā pratyakśirā na ca
|
prākśirāstu svapedvidvānathavā dakṣiṇāśirāḥ ||48||

49na bhagne nāvaśīrṇe ca śayane prasvapīta ca
|
nāntardhāne na saṃyukte na ca tiryakkadācana ||49||

50na cāpi gacchetkāryeṇa samayādvāpi nāstikaiḥ
|
āsanaṃ tu padā''kṛṣya na prasajjettathā naraḥ ||50||

51na nagnaḥ karhicitsnāyānna niśāyāṃ kadācana
|
snātvā ca nāvamṛjyeta gātrāṇi suvicakṣaṇaḥ ||51||

52na cānuliṃpedasnātvā snātvā vāso na nirdhunet
|
na caivārdrāṇi vāsāṃsi nityaṃ seveta mānavaḥ ||52||

53srajaśca nāvakṛṣyeta na vahirdhārayīta ca
|
udakyayā ca sambhāṣāṃ na kurvīta kadācana ||53||

54notsṛjeta purīṣaṃ ca kṣetre grāmasya cāntike
|
ubhe mūtrapurīṣe tu nāpsu kuryātkadācana ||54||

55annaṃ bubhukṣamāṇastu trirmukhena spṛśedapaḥ
|
bhuktvā cānaṃ tathaiva trirdvi punaḥ parimārjayet ||55||

56prāṅmukho nityamaśnīyādvāgyatonnamakutsayan
|
praskandayecca manasā bhuktvā cāgnimupaspṛśet ||56||

57āyuṣyaṃ prāṅmukho bhuṅkte yaśasyaṃ dakṣiṇāmukhaḥ
|
dhanyaṃ paścānmukho bhuṅk‌te ṛtaṃ bhuṃkte udaṅ‌mukhaḥ ||57||

58agnimālabhya toyena sarvānprāṇānupaspṛśet
|
gātrāṇi caiva sarvāṇi nābhiṃ pāṇitale tathā ||58||

59nādhitiṣṭhettuṣaṃ jātu keśabhasmakapālikāḥ
|
anyasya cāpyavasnātaṃ dūrataḥ parivarjayet ||59||

60śāntihomāṃśca kurvīta sāvitrāṇi ca dhārayet
|
niṣaṇṇaścāpi khādeta na tu gacchankadācana ||60||

61mūtraṃ nottiṣṭhatā kāryaṃ na bhasmani na govraje
|
ārdrapādastu bhuñjīta nārdrapādastu saṃviśet ||61||

62ārdrapādastu bhuñjāno varṣāṇāṃ jīvate śatam
|
trīṇi tejāṃsi nocchiṣṭa ālabheta kadācana ||62||

63agniṃ gāṃ brāhmaṇaṃ caiva tathā hyāyurna riṣyate
|
trīṇi tejāṃsi nocchiṣṭa udīkṣeta kadācana ||63||

64sūryācandramasau caiva nakṣatrāṇi ca sarvaśaḥ
|
ūrdhvaṃ prāṇāhyutkrāmanti yūnaḥ sthavira āyati ||64||

65pratyutthānābhivādābhyāṃ punastānpratipadyate
|
abhivādayīta vṛddhāṃśca dadyāccaivāsanaṃ svayam ||65||

66kṛtāñjalirupāsīta gacchantaṃ pṛṣṭhato'nviyāt
|
na cāsītāsane bhinne bhinnakāṃsyaṃ ca varjayeta ||66||

67naikavastreṇa bhoktavyaṃ na nagnaḥ snātumarhati
|
svaptavyaṃ naiva nagnena na cocchiṣṭo'pi saṃviśet ||67||

68ucchiṣṭo na spṛśecchīrṣaṃ sarve prāṇāstadāśrayāḥ
|
keśagrahaṃ prahārāṃśca śirasyetānvivarjayet ||68||

69na saṃhatābhyāṃ pāṇibhyāṃ kaṃḍūyedāttmanaḥ śiraḥ
|
na cābhīkṣṇaṃ śiraḥ snāyāttathāsyāyurna riṣyate ||69||

70śirasnātastu tailaiśca nāṅgaṃ kiñcidapi spṛśet
|
tilasṛṣṭaṃ na cāśnīyāttathāsyāyurna riṣyate ||70||

71nādhyāpayettathocchiṣṭo nādhīyīta kadācana
|
vāte ca pūtigandhe ca manasā'pi na cintayet ||71||

72atra gāthā yamodgītāḥ kīrtayanti purāvidaḥ
|
āyurasya nikṛntāmi prajāstasyādade tathā ||72||

73ucchiṣṭo yaḥ prādravati svādhyāyaṃ cādhigacchati
|
yaścānadhyāyakāle'pi mohādabhyasyati dvijaḥ ||73||

74tasya vedaḥ praṇaśyeta āyuśca parihīyate
|
tasmādyukto hyanadhyāye nādhīyīta kadācana ||74||

75pratyādityaṃ pratyanalaṃ prati gāṃ ca prati dvijān
|
ye mehanti ca panthānaṃ te bhavanti gatāyuṣaḥ ||75||

76ubhe mūtrapurīṣe tu divā kuryādudaṅmukhaḥ
|
dakṣiṇābhimukho rātrau tathā hyāyurna riṣyate ||76||

77trīnkṛśānnāvajānīyāddīrghamāyurjijīviṣuḥ
|
brāhmaṇaṃ kṣatriyaṃ sarpaṃ sarve hyāśīviṣāstrayaḥ ||77||

78dahatyāśīviṣaḥ kruddho yāvatpaśyati cakṣuṣā
|
kṣatriyopi dahetkruddho yāvatspṛśati tejasā ||78||

79brāhmaṇastu kulaṃ hanyāddhyānenāvekṣitena ca
|
tasmādetattrayaṃ yatnādupaseveta paṇḍitaḥ ||79||

80guruṇā caiva nirbandho na kartavyaḥ kadācana
|
anumānyaḥ prasādyaśca guruḥ kruddho yudhiṣṭhira ||80||

81samyaṅmithyā pravṛtte'pi vartitavyaṃ gurāviha
|
gurunindā dahatyāyurmanuṣyāṇāṃ na saṃśayaḥ ||81||

82dūrādāvasathānmūtraṃ dūrātpādāvasecanam
|
ucchiṣṭotsarjanaṃ caiva dūre kāryaṃ hitaiṣiṇā ||82||

83raktamālyaṃ na dhāryaṃ syācchuklaṃ dhāryaṃ tu paṇḍitaiḥ
|
varjayitvā tu kamalaṃ tathā kuvalayaṃ prabho ||83||

84raktaṃ śirasi dhāryaṃ tu tathā vāneyamityapi
|
kāñcanīyā'pi mālā yā na sā duṣyati karhicit ||84||

85snātasya varṇakaṃ nityamārdraṃ dadyādviśāṃpate
|
viparyayaṃ na kurvīta vāsaso buddhimānnaraḥ ||85||

86tathā nānyadhṛtaṃ dhāryaṃ na cāpadaśameva ca
|
anyadeva bhavedvāsaḥ śayanīye narottama ||86||

87anyadrathyāsu devānāmarcāyāmanyadeva hi
|
priyaṅgucandanābhyāṃ ca bilvena tagareṇa ca ||87||

88pṛthagevānulimpeta kesareṇa ca buddhimān
|
upavāsaṃ ca kurvīta snātaḥ śuciralaṃkṛtaḥ ||88||

89parvakāleṣu sarveṣu brahmacārī sadā bhavet
|
samānamekapātre tu bhuñjennānnaṃ janeśvara ||89||

90nālīḍhayā parihataṃ bhakṣayīta kadācana
|
tathā noddhṛtasārāṇi prekṣate nāpradāya ca ||90||

91na saṃnikṛṣṭe medhāvī nāśucerna ca satsu ca
|
pratiṣiddhānna dharmeṣu bhakṣyānbhuñjīta pṛṣṭhataḥ ||91||

92pippalaṃ ca vaṭaṃ caiva śaṇaśākaṃ tathaiva ca
|
udumbaraṃ na khādecca bhavārthī puruṣottamaḥ ||92||

93ājaṃ gavyaṃ tathā māṃsaṃ māyūraṃ caiva varjayet
|
varjayecchuṣkamāṃsaṃ ca tathā paryuṣitaṃ ca yat ||93||

94na pāṇau lavaṇaṃ vidvānprāśnīyānna ca rātriṣu
|
dadhisaktūnna bhuñjīta vṛthāmāṃsaṃ ca varjayet ||94||

95sāyaṃprātaśca bhuñjīta nāntarāle samāhitaḥ
|
vālena tu na bhuñjīta paraśrāddhaṃ tathaiva ca ||95||

96vāgyato naikavastraśca nāsaṃviṣṭaḥ kadācana
|
bhūmau sadaiva nāśnīyānnānāsīno na śabdadvat ||96||

97toyapūrvaṃ pradāyānnamatithibhyo viśāṃpate
|
paścādbhuñjīta medhāvī na cāpyanyamanā naraḥ ||97||

98samānamekapaṅktyāṃ tu bhojyamannaṃ nareśvara
|
viṣaṃ hālāhalaṃ bhuṅkte yo'pradāya suhṛjjane ||98||

99pānīyaṃ pāyasaṃ saktūndadhisarpirmadhūnyapi
|
nirasya śeṣamanyeṣāṃ na pradeyaṃ tu kasyacit ||99||

100bhuñjāno manujavyāghra naiva śaṅkāṃ samācaret
|
dadhi cāpyanupānaṃ vai na kartavyaṃ bhavārthinā ||100||

101ācamya caikahastena pariplāvyaṃ tathodakam
|
aṅguṣṭhaṃ caraṇasyātha dakṣiṇasyāvasecayet ||101||

102pāṇiṃ mūrdhni samādhāya spṛṣṭvā cāgniṃ samāhitaḥ
|
jñātiśreṣṭhyamavāpnoti prayogakuśalo naraḥ ||102||

103adbhiḥ prāṇānsamālabhya nābhiṃ pāṇitale tathā
|
spṛśaṃścaiva pratiṣṭheta na cāpyārdreṇa pāṇinā ||103||

104aṅguṣṭhasyāntarāle ca brāhmaṃ tīrthamudāhṛtam
|
kaniṣṭhikāyāḥ paścāttu devatīrthamihocyate ||104||

105aṅguṣṭhasya ca yanmadhyaṃ pradeśinyāśca bhārata
|
tena pitryāṇi kurvīta spṛṣṭvāpo nyāyataḥ sadā ||105||

106parāpavādaṃ na brūyānnāpriyaṃ ca kadācana
|
na manyuḥ kaścidutpādyaḥ puruṣeṇa bhavārthinā ||106||

107patitaistu kathāṃ neccheddarśanaṃ ca vivarjayet
|
saṃsargaṃ ca na gaccheta tathā''yurvindate mahat ||107||

108na divā maithunaṃ gacchenna kanyāṃ na ca bandhakīm
|
na cāsnātāṃ striyaṃ gacchettathā''yurvindate mahat ||108||

109sve sve tīrthe samācamya kārye samupakalpite
|
triḥpīttvā''po dviḥ pramṛjya kṛtaśauco bhavennaraḥ ||109||

110indriyāṇi sakṛtspṛśya trirabhyukṣya ca mānavaḥ
|
kurvīta pitryaṃ daivaṃ ca vedadṛṣṭena karmaṇā ||110||

111brāhmaṇārthe ca yacchaucaṃ tacca me śṛṇu kaurava
|
pavitraṃ ca hitaṃ caiva bhojanādyaṃ tayostathā ||111||

112sarvaśauceṣu brāhmaṇa tīrthena samupaspṛśet
|
niṣṭhīvya tu tathā śrutvā spṛśyāpo hi śucirbhavet ||112||

113vṛddho jñātistathā mitraṃ daridro yo bhavedapi
|
gṛhe vāsayitavyāste dhanyamāyuṣyameva ca ||113||

114gṛhe pārāvatā dhanyāḥ śukāśca sahasārikāḥ
|
gṛheṣvete na pāpāya tathā vai tailapāyikāḥ
|
uddīpakāśca gṛdhrāśca kapotā bhramarāstathā ||114||

115niviśeyuryadā tatra śāntimeva tadā''caret
|
amaṅgalyāni caitāni tathā'krośo mahātmanām ||115||

116mahātmanotiguhyāni na vaktavyāni karhicit
|
agamyāśca na gaccheta rājñaḥ patnīṃ sakhīstathā ||116||

117vaidyānāṃ bālavṛddhānāṃ bhṛtyānāṃ ca yudhiṣṭhira
|
bandhūnāṃ brāhmaṇānāṃ ca tathā śārāṇakasya ca ||117||

118saṃbandhināṃ ca rājendra tathā''yurvindate mahat
|
brāhmaṇasthapatibhyāṃ ca nirmitaṃ yanniveśanam ||118||

119tadāvasetsadā prāśo bhavārthī manujeśvara
|
sandhyāyāṃ na svapedrājanvidyāṃ na ca samācaret ||119||

120na bhuñjīta ca medhāvī tathā''yurvindate mahat
|
naktaṃ na kuryātpitryāṇi bhuktvā caiva prasādhanam ||120||

121pānīyasya kriyā naktaṃ na kāryā bhūtimicchatā
|
varjanīyāścaiva nityaṃ saktavo niśi bhārata ||121||

122śeṣāṇi caiva pānāni pānīyaṃ cāpi bhojane
|
sauhityaṃ na ca kartavyaṃ rātrau na ca samācaret ||122||

123dvijacchedaṃ na kurvīta bhuktvā na ca samācaret
|
mahākule prasūtāṃ ca praśastāṃ lakṣaṇaistathā ||123||

124vayasthāṃ ca mahāprājñaḥ kanyāmāvoḍhumarhati
|
apatyamutpādya tataḥ pratiṣṭhāpya kulaṃ tathā ||124||

125putrāḥ pradeyā jñāneṣu kuladharmeṣu bhārata
|
kanyā cotpādya dātavyā kulaputrāya dhīmate ||125||

126putrā niveśyāśca kulādbhṛtyā labhyāśca bhārata
|
śirasnāto'tha kurvīta daivaṃ pitryamathāpi ca ||126||

127nakṣatre na ca kurvīta yasmin jāto bhavennaraḥ
|
na proṣṭhapada‌yoḥ kāryaṃ tathā''gneye ca bhārata ||127||

128dāruṇeṣu ca sarveṣu pratyariṃ ca vivarjayet
|
jyotiṣe yāni coktāni tāni sarvāṇi varjayet ||128||

129prāṅmukhaḥ śmaśrukarmāṇi kārayetsusamāhitaḥ
|
uda‌ṅmukho vā rājendra tathā''yurvindate mahat ||129||

130parivādaṃ na ca brūyātpareṣāmātmanastathā
|
parivādo hyadharmāya procyate bharatarṣabha ||130||

131varjayedvyaṃginīṃ nārīṃ tathā kanyāṃ narottama
|
samārṣāṃ vyaṃgitāṃ caiva mātuḥ svakulajāṃ tathā ||131||

132vṛddhāṃ pravrajitāṃ caiva tathaiva ca pativratām
|
tathā nikṛṣṭavarṇāṃ ca varṇotkṛṣṭāṃ ca varjayet ||132||

133ayoniṃ ca viyoniṃ ca na gaccheta vicakṣaṇaḥ
|
piṅgalāṃ kuṣṭhinīṃ nārīṃ na tvamudvoḍhumarhasi ||133||

134apasmārikule jātāṃ nihīnāṃ cāpi varjayet
|
śvitriṇāṃ ca kule jātāṃ kṣayiṇāṃ manujeśvara ||134||

135lakṣaṇairanvitā yā ca praśastā yā ca lakṣaṇaiḥ
|
manojñāṃ darśanīyāṃ ca tāṃ bhavānvoḍhumarhati ||135||

136mahākule niveṣṭavyaṃ sadṛśe vā yudhiṣṭhira
|
avarā patitā caiva na grāhyā bhūtimicchatā ||136||

137agnīnutpādya yatnena kriyāḥ suvihitāśca yāḥ
|
vede ca brāhmaṇaiḥ proktāstāśca sarvāḥ samācaret ||137||

138na cerṣyā strīṣu kartavyā rakṣyā dārāśca sarvaśaḥ
|
anāyuṣyā bhavedīrṣyā tasmādīrṣyāṃ vivarjayet ||138||

139anāyuṣyaṃ divā svapnaṃ tathābhyuditaśāyitā
|
prage niśāmāśu tathā naivocchiṣṭāḥ svapanti vai ||139||

140pāradāryamanāyuṣyaṃ nāpitocchiṣṭatā tathā
|
yatnato vai na kartavyamabhyāsaścaiva bhārata ||140||

141sandhyāyāṃ ca na bhuñjīta na snāyenna tathā paṭhet
|
prayataśca bhavettasyāṃ na ca kiñcitsamācaret ||141||

142brāhmaṇānpūjayeccāpi tathā snātvā narādhipa
|
devāṃśca praṇametsnāto gurūṃścāpyabhivādayet ||142||

143animantrito na gaccheta yajñaṃ gaccheta darśakaḥ
|
anarcite hyanāyuṣyaṃ gamanaṃ tatra bhārata ||143||

144na caikena parivrajyaṃ na gantavyaṃ tathā niśi
|
anāgatāyāṃ sandhyāyāṃ paścimāyāṃ gṛhe vaset ||144||

145mātuḥ piturgurūṇāṃ ca kāryamevānuśāsanam
|
hitaṃ cāpyahitaṃ cāpi na vicāryaṃ nararṣabha ||145||

146dhanurvede ca vede ca yatnaḥ kāryo narādhipa
|
hastipṛṣṭhe'śvapṛṣṭhe ca rathacaryāsu caiva ha ||146||

147yatnavānbhava rājendra yatnavānsukhamedhate
|
apradhṛṣyaśca śatrūṇāṃ bhṛtyānāṃ svajanasya ca ||147||

148prajāpālanayuktaśca na kṣatiṃ labhate kvacit
|
yuktiśāstraṃ ca te jñeyaṃ śabda‌śāstraṃ ca bhārata ||148||

149gāndharvaśāstraṃ ca kalāḥ parijñeyā narādhipa
|
purāṇamitihāsāśca tathā''khyānāni yāni ca ||149||

150mahātmanāṃ ca caritaṃ śrotavyaṃ nityameva te
|
patnī rajasvalā yā ca nābhigacchenna cāhvayet ||150||

151snātāṃ caturthe divase rātrau gacchedvicakṣaṇaḥ
|
pañcame divase nārī ṣaṣṭhe'hani pumānbhavet ||151||

152etena vidhinā patnīmupagaccheta paṇḍitaḥ
|
jñātisambandhibhitrāṇi pūjanīyāni sarvaśaḥ ||152||

153yaṣṭavyaṃ ca yathāśakti yajñairvividhadakṣiṇaiḥ
|
ata ūrdhvamaraṇyaṃ ca sevitavyaṃ narādhipa ||153||

154eṣa te lakṣaṇoddeśa āyuṣyāṇāṃ prakīrtitaḥ
|
śeṣastraividyavṛddhebhyaḥ pratyāhāryo yudhiṣṭhira||154||

155ācāro bhūtijanana ācāraḥ kīrtivardhanaḥ
|
ācārādvardhate hyāyurācāro hantyalakṣaṇam ||155||

156āgamānāṃ hi sarveṣāmācāraḥ śreṣṭha ucyate
|
ācāraprabhavo dharmo dharmādāyurvivardhate ||156||

157etadyaśasyamāyuṣyaṃ svargyaṃ svastyayanaṃ mahat
|
anukaṃpya sarvavarṇān brahmaṇā samudāhṛtam ||157||
