Mahābhārata 3.167 (Āraṇyakaparvan)

1vaiśampāyana uvāca|
yathāgataṃ gate śakre bhrātṛbhiḥ saha saṅgataḥ
|
kṛṣṇayā caiva bībhatsurdharmaputramapūjayat||1||

2abhivādayamānaṃ taṃ mūrdhnyupāghrāya pāṇḍavam
|
harṣagadgadayā vācā prahṛṣṭo'rjunamabravīt||2||

3kathamarjuna kālo'yaṃ svarge vyatigatastava
|
kathaṃ cāstrāṇyavāptāni devarājaśca toṣitaḥ||3||

4samyagvā te gṛhītāni kaccidastrāṇi pāṇḍava
|
kaccit surādhipaḥ prīto rudro vā'strāṇyadāttava||4||

5yathā dṛṣṭaśca te śakro bhagavān vā pinākadhṛk
|
yathaivāstrāṇyavāptāni yathaivārādhitaśca te||5||

6yathoktavāṃstvāṃ bhagavān śatakraturarindama
|
kṛtapriyastvayā'smīti tasya te kiṃ priyaṃ kṛtam||6||

7etadicchāmyahaṃ śrotuṃ vistareṇa mahādyute
|
yathā tuṣṭo mahādevo devarājastathā'nagha||7||

8yaccāpi vajrapāṇestu priyaṃ kṛtamarindama
|
etadākhyāhi me sarvamakhilena dhanañjaya||8||

9arjuna uvāca|
śṛṇu hanta mahārāja vidhinā yena dṛṣṭavān
|
śatakratumahaṃ devaṃ bhagavantaṃ ca śaṅkaram||9||

10vidyāmadhītya tāṃ rājaṃstvayoktāmarimardana
|
bhavatā ca samādiṣṭastapase prasthito vanam||10||

11bhṛgutuṅgamatho gatvā kāmyakādāsthitastapaḥ
|
ekarātroṣitaḥ kañcidapaśyaṃ brāhmaṇaṃ pathi||11||

12sa māmapṛcchat kaunteya kvāsi gantā bravīhi me
|
tasmā avitathaṃ sarvamabruvaṃ kurunandana||12||

13sa tathyaṃ mama tacchrutvā brāhmaṇo rājasattama
|
apūjayata māṃ rājan prītimāṃścā'bhavanmayi||13||

14tato māmabravīt prītastapa ātiṣṭha bhārata
|
tapasvī na cireṇa tvaṃ drakṣyase vibudhādhipam||14||

15tato'haṃ vacanāttasya girimāruhya śaiśiram
|
tapo'tapyaṃ mahārāja māsaṃ mūlaphalāśanaḥ||15||

16dvitīyaścāpi me māso jalaṃ bhakṣayato gataḥ
|
nirāhārastṛtīye'tha māse pāṇḍavanandana||16||

17ūrdhvabāhuścaturthaṃ tu māsamasmi sthitastadā
|
na ca me hīyate prāṇastada‌dbhutamivābhavat||17||

18pañcame tvatha samprāpte prathame divase gate
|
varāhasaṃsthitaṃ bhūtaṃ matsamīpaṃ samāgamat||18||

19nighnan prothena pṛthivīṃ vilikhaṃścaraṇairapi
|
sammārjañjaṭhareṇorvīṃ vivartaṃśca muhurmuhuḥ||19||

20anu tasyāparaṃ bhūtaṃ mahat kairātasaṃsthitam
|
dhanurbāṇāsimat prāptaṃ strīgaṇānugataṃ tadā||20||

21tato'haṃ dhanurādāya tathā'kṣayye maheṣudhī
|
atāḍayaṃ śareṇātha tadbhutaṃ lomaharṣaṇam||21||

22yugapattaṃ kirātastu vikṛṣya balavaddhanuḥ
|
abhyājaghne dṛḍhataraṃ kampayanniva me manaḥ||22||

23sa tu māmabravīdrājan mama pūrvaparigrahaḥ
|
mṛgayādharmamutsṛjya kimarthaṃ tāḍitastvayā||23||

24eṣa te niśitairbāṇairdarpaṃ hanmi sthiro bhava
|
sa dhanuṣmān mahākāyastato māmabhyabhāṣata||24||

25tato girimivātyarthamāvṛṇonmāṃ mahāśaraiḥ
|
taṃ cāhaṃ śaravarṣeṇa mahatā samavākiram||25||

26tataḥ śarairdīptamukhairyantritairanumantritaiḥ
|
pratyavidhyamahaṃ taṃ tu vajrairiva śiloccayam||26||

27tasya tacchatadhā rūpamabhavacca sahasradhā
|
tāni cāsya śarīrāṇi śarairahamatāḍayam||27||

28punastāni śarīrāṇi ekībhūtāni bhārata
|
adṛśyanta mahārāja tānyahaṃ vyadhamaṃ punaḥ||28||

29aṇurbṛhacchirā bhūtvā bṛhaccāṇuśirāḥ punaḥ
|
ekībhūtastadā rājan so'bhyavartata māṃ yudhi||29||

30yadā'bhibhavituṃ bāṇairna ca śaknomi taṃ raṇe
|
tato mahāstramātiṣṭhaṃ vāyavyaṃ bharatarṣabha||30||

31na cainamaśakaṃ hantuṃ tada‌dbhutamivā'bhavat
|
tasmin pratihate cāstre vismayo me mahānabhūt||31||

32bhūya eva mahārāja saviśeṣamahaṃ tataḥ
|
astrapūgena mahatā raṇe bhūtamavākiram||32||

33sthūṇākarṇamatho jālaṃ śaravarṣamatholbaṇam
|
śalabhāstramaśmavarṣaṃ samāsthāyāhamabhyayām||33||

34jagrāsa prasabhaṃ tāni sarvāṇyastrāṇi me nṛpa
|
teṣu sarveṣu jagdheṣu brahmāstraṃ mahadādiśam||34||

35tataḥ prajvalitairbāṇaiḥ sarvataḥ sopacīyate
|
upacīyamānaśca mayā mahāstreṇa vyavardhata||35||

36tataḥ santāpitā lokā matprasūtena tejasā
|
kṣaṇena hi diśaḥ khaṃ ca sarvato hi vidīpitam||36||

37tadapyastraṃ mahātejāḥ kṣaṇenaiva vyaśātayat
|
brahmāstre tu hate rājan bhayaṃ māṃ mahadāviśat||37||

38tato'haṃ dhanurādāya tathā'kṣayye maheṣudhī
|
sahasā'bhyahanaṃ bhūtaṃ tānyapyastrāṇyabhakṣayat||38||

39hateṣvastreṣu sarveṣu bhakṣiteṣvāyudheṣu ca
|
mama tasya ca bhūtasya bāhuyuddhamavartata||39||

40vyāyāmaṃ muṣṭibhiḥ kṛtvā talairapi samāgataiḥ
|
apārayaṃśca tadbhūtaṃ niśceṣṭamagamaṃ mahīm||40||

41tataḥ prahasya tadbhutaṃ tatraivāntaradhīyata
|
saha strībhirmahārāja paśyato me'dbhutopamam||41||

42evaṃ kṛtvā sa bhagavāṃstato'nyadrūpamāsthitaḥ
|
divyameva mahārāja vasāno'dbhutamambaram||42||

43hitvā kirātarūpaṃ ca bhagavāṃstridaśeśvaraḥ
|
svarūpaṃ divyamāsthāya tasthau tatra maheśvaraḥ||43||

44adṛśyata tataḥ sākṣādbhagavān govṛṣadhvajaḥ
|
umāsahāyo vyāladhṛgbahurūpaḥ pinākadhṛk||44||

45sa māmabhyetya samare tathaivābhimukhaṃ sthitam
|
śūlapāṇirathovāca tuṣṭo'smīti parantapa||45||

46tatastaddhanurādāya tūṇau cākṣayyasāyakau
|
prādānmamaiva bhagavān varayasveti cābravīt||46||

47tuṣṭo'smi tava kaunteya brūhi kiṃ karavāṇi te
|
manogataṃ vīra yatte tadbrūhi vitarāmyaham||47||

48amaratvamapāhāya brūhi yatte manogatam
|
tataḥ prāñjalirevāhamastreṣu gatamānasaḥ||48||

49praṇamya manasā śarvaṃ tato vacanamādade
|
bhagavān me prasannaścedīpsito'yaṃ varo mama||49||

50astrāṇīcchāmyahaṃ jñātuṃ yāni deveṣu kānicit
|
dadānītyeva bhagavānabravīt tryambakaśca mām||50||

51raudramastraṃ madīyaṃ tvāmupasthāsyati pāṇḍava
|
pradadau ca mama prītaḥ so'straṃ pāśupataṃ mahat||51||

52uvāca ca mahādevo datvā me'straṃ sanātanam
|
na prayojyaṃ bhavedetanmānuṣeṣu kathañcana||52||

53jagadvinirdahedevamalpatejasi pātitam
|
pīḍyamānena balavat prayojyaṃ syāddhanañjaya||53||

54astrāṇāṃ pratighāte ca sarvathaiva prayojayet
|
tadapratihataṃ divyaṃ sarvāstrapratiṣedhanam||54||

55mūrtimanme sthitaṃ pārśve prasanne govṛṣadhvaje
|
utsādanamamitrāṇāṃ parasenānikartanam||55||

56durāsadaṃ duṣprasahaṃ suradānavarākṣasaiḥ
|
anujñātastvahaṃ tena tatraiva samupāviśam||56||

57prekṣataścaiva me devastatraivāntaradhīyata||57||
