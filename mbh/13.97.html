Mahābhārata 13.97 (Anuśāsanaparvan)

1yudhiṣṭhira uvāca|
gārhasthyaṃ dharmamakhilaṃ prabrūhi bharatarṣabha
|
ṛddhimāpnoti kiṃ kṛtvā manuṣya iha pārthiva ||1||

2bhīṣma uvāca|
atra te vartayiṣyāmi purāvṛttaṃ janādhipa
|
vāsudevasya saṃvādaṃ pṛthivyāścaiva bhārata ||2||

3saṃstutya pṛthivīṃ devīṃ vāsudevaḥ pratāpavān
|
papraccha bharataśreṣṭha māṃ tvaṃ yatpṛcchase'dya vai ||3||

4vāsudeva uvāca|
gārhasthyaṃ dharmamāśritya mayā vā madvidhena vā
|
kimavaśyaṃ dhare kāryaṃ kiṃ vā kṛtvā kṛtaṃ bhavet ||4||

5pṛthivyuvāca
|
ṛṣayaḥ pitaro devā manuṣyāścaiva mādhava
|
ijyāścaivārcanīyāśca yathā caiva nibodha me ||5||

6sadā yajñena devāśca sadā''tithyena mānuṣāḥ
|
chaṃdataśca yathā nityamarhān bhuñjīta nityaśaḥ ||6||

7tena hyṛṣigaṇāḥ prītā bhavanti madhusūdana
|
nityamagniṃ paricaredabhuktvā balikarma ca ||7||

8kuryāttathaiva devā vai prīyante madhusūdana
|
kuryādaharahaḥ śrāddhamannādyenoda‌kena ca ||8||

9payomūlaphalairvāpi pitṝṇāṃ prītimāharan
|
siddhānnādvaiśvadevaṃ vai kuryādagnau yathāvidhi ||9||

10agnīṣomaṃ vaiśvadevaṃ dhānvantaryamanantaram
|
prajānāṃ pataye caiva pṛthaghghomo vidhīyate ||10||

11tathaiva cānupūrvyeṇa balikarma prayojayet
|
dakṣiṇāyāṃ yamāyeti pratīcyāṃ varuṇāya ca ||11||

12somāya cāpyudīcyāṃ vai vāstumadhye prajāpateḥ
|
dhanvantareḥ prāgudīcyāṃ prācyāṃ śakrāya mādhava ||12||

13manuṣyebhya iti prāhurbaliṃ dvāri gṛhasya vai
|
marudbhyo devatebhyaśca balimantarguhe haret ||13||

14tathaiva viśvedevebhyo balimākāśato haret
|
niśācarebhyo bhūtebhyo baliṃ naktaṃ tathā haret ||14||

15evaṃ kṛtvā baliṃ samyagdadyādbhikṣāṃ dvijāya vai
|
alābhe brāhmaṇasyāgnāvagramuddhṛtya nikṣipet ||15||

16yadā śrāddhaṃ pitṛbhyo'pi dātumiccheta mānavaḥ
|
tadā paścātprakurvīta nivṛtte śrāddhakarmaṇi ||16||

17pitṝnsantarpayitvā tu baliṃ kuryādvidhānataḥ
|
vaiśvadevaṃ tataḥ kuryātpaścādbrāhmaṇavācanam ||17||

18tato'nnena viśeṣeṇa bhojayedatithīnapi
|
arcāpūrvaṃ mahārāja tataḥ prīṇāti mānavān ||18||

19anityaṃ hi sthito yasmāttasmāda‌tithirucyate
|
ācāryasya pituścaiva sakhyurāptasya cātitheḥ ||19||

20idamasti gṛhe mahyamiti nityaṃ nivedayet
|
te yadvadeyustatkuryāditi dharmo vidhīyate ||20||

21gṛhasthaḥ puruṣaḥ kṛṣṇa śiṣṭāśī ca sadā bhavet
|
rājartvijaṃ snātakaṃ ca guruṃ śvaśurameva ca ||21||

22arcayenmadhuparkeṇa parisaṃvatsaroṣitān
|
śvabhyaśca śvapacebhyaśca vayobhyaścāvapedbhuvi ||22||

23vaiśvadevaṃ hi nāmaitatsāyaṃprātarvidhīyate
|
etāṃstu dharmān gārhasthyānyaḥ kuryādanasūyakaḥ
|
sa iharṣivarānprāpya pretya loke mahīyate ||23||

24bhīṣma uvāca|
iti bhūmervacaḥ śrutvā vāsudevaḥ pratāpavān
|
tathā cakāra satataṃ tvamapyevaṃ sadā''cara||24||

25etadgṛhasthadharmaṃ tvaṃ ceṣṭamāno janādhipa
|
ihaloke yaśaḥ prāpya pretya svargamavāpsyasi ||25||
