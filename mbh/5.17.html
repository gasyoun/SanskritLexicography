Mahābhārata 5.17 (Udyogaparvan)

1śalya uvāca|
atha saṃcintayānasya devarājasya dhīmataḥ
|
nahuṣasya vadhopāyaṃ lokapālaiḥ sadaivataiḥ ||1||

2tapasvī tatra bhagavānagastyaḥ pratyadṛśyata
|
so'bravīdarcya devendraṃ diṣṭyā vai vardhate bhavān ||2||

3viśvarūpavināśena vṛtrāsuravadhena ca
|
diṣṭyā'dya nahuṣo bhraṣṭo devarājyāt purandara ||3||

4diṣṭyā hatāriṃ paśyāmi bhavantaṃ balasūdana| indra uvāca|
svāgataṃ te maharṣe'stu prīto'haṃ darśanāt tava
|
pādyamācamanīyaṃ ca gāmarghyaṃ ca pratīccha me ||4||

5śalya uvāca|
pūjitaṃ copaviṣṭaṃ tamāsena munisattamam
|
paryapṛcchata deveśaḥ prahṛṣṭo brāhmaṇarṣabham ||5||

6etadicchāmi bhagavan kathyamānaṃ dvijottama
|
paribhraṣṭaḥ kathaṃ svargānnahuṣaḥ pāpaniścayaḥ ||6||

7agastya uvāca|
śrṛṇu śakra priyaṃ vākyaṃ yathā rājā durātmavān
|
svargādbhraṣṭo durācāro nahuṣo baladarpitaḥ ||7||

8śramārtāśca vahantastaṃ nahuṣaṃ pāpakāriṇam
|
devarṣayo mahābhāgāstathā brahmarṣayo'malāḥ ||8||

9papracchurnahuṣaṃ devaṃ saṃśayaṃ jayatāṃ vara
|
ya ime brahmaṇā proktā mantrā vai prokṣaṇe gavām ||9||

10ete pramāṇaṃ bhavata utāho neti vāsava
|
nahuṣo neti tānāha tamasā mūḍhacetanaḥ ||10||

11ṛṣaya ūcuḥ| adharme saṃpravṛttastvaṃ dharmaṃ na pratipadyase
|
pramāṇametadasmākaṃ pūrvaṃ proktaṃ maharṣibhiḥ ||11||

12agastya uvāca|
tato vivadamānaḥ sa munibhiḥ saha vāsava
|
atha māmaspṛśan mūrdhni pādenādharmapīḍitaḥ ||12||

13tenābhūddhatatejāśca niḥśrīkaśca mahīpatiḥ
|
tatastaṃ tamasā''vignamavocaṃ bhṛśapīḍitam ||13||

14yasmāt pūrvaiḥ kṛtaṃ rājan brahmarṣibhiranuṣṭhitam
|
adṛṣṭaṃ dūṣayasi me yacca mūrdhnyaspṛśaḥ padā ||14||

15yaccāpi tvamṛṣīn mūḍha brahmakalpān durāsadān ||15||

16vāhān kṛtvā vāhayasi tena svargāddhataprabhaḥ
|
dhvaṃsa pāpaparibhraṣṭaḥ kṣīṇapuṇyo mahītale ||16||

17daśavarṣasahasrāṇi sarparūpadharo mahān
|
vicariṣyasi pūrṇeṣu punaḥ svargamavāpsyasi ||17||

18evaṃ bhraṣṭo durātmā sa devarājyādarindama
|
diṣṭyā vardhāmahe śakra hato brahmaṇakaṇṭakaḥ||18||

19triviṣṭapaṃ prapadyasva pāhi lokān śacīpate
|
jitendriyo jitāmitraḥ stūyamāno maharṣibhiḥ ||19||

20śalya uvāca|
tato devā bhṛśaṃ tuṣṭā maharṣigaṇasaṃvṛtāḥ
|
pitaraścaiva yakṣāśca bhujagā rākṣasāstathā ||20||

21gandharvā devakanyāśca sarve cāpsarasāṃ gaṇāḥ
|
sarāṃsi saritaḥ śailāḥ sāgarāśca viśāṃpate ||21||

22upāgamyābruvan sarve diṣṭyā vardhasi śatruhan
|
hataśca nahuṣaḥ pāpo diṣṭyā'gastyena dhīmatā
|
diṣṭyā pāpasamācāraḥ kṛtaḥ sarpo mahītale ||22||
