Mahābhārata 7.152 (Droṇaparvan)

1sañjaya uvāca|
tato duryodhano rājā droṇenaivaṃ pracoditaḥ
|
amarṣavaśamāpanno yuddhāyaiva mano dadhe||1||

2abravīcca tadā karṇaṃ putro duryodhanastava
|
paśya kṛṣṇasahāyena pāṇḍavena kirīṭinā||2||

3ācāryavihitaṃ vyūhaṃ bhittvā devaiḥ sudurbhidam
|
tava vyāyacchamānasya droṇasya ca mahātmanaḥ||3||

4miṣatāṃ yodhamukhyānāṃ saindhavo vinipātitaḥ
|
paśya rādheya pṛthvīśāḥ pṛthivyāṃ pravarā yudhi||4||

5pārthenaikena nihatāḥ siṃhenevetare mṛgāḥ
|
mama vyāyacchamānasya droṇasya ca mahātmanaḥ||5||

6alpāvaśeṣaṃ sainyaṃ me kṛtaṃ śakrātmajena ha
|
kathaṃ niyacchamānasya droṇasya yudhi phālgunaḥ||6||

7bhindyātsudurbhidaṃ vyūhaṃ yatamāno'pi saṃyuge
|
pratijñāyā gataḥ pāraṃ hatvā saindhavamarjunaḥ||7||

8paśya rādheya pṛthvīśān pṛthivyāṃ pātitānbahūn
|
pārthena nihatānsaṃkhye mahendropamavikramān||8||

9anicchataḥ kathaṃ vīra droṇasya yudhi pāṇḍavaḥ
|
bhindyātsudurbhidaṃ vyūhaṃ yatamānasya śuṣmiṇaḥ||9||

10dayitaḥ phālguno nityamācāryasya mahātmanaḥ
|
tato'sya dattavāndvāramayuddhenaiva śatruhan||10||

11abhayaṃ sindhurājāya dattvā droṇaḥ paraṃtapaḥ
|
prādātkirīṭine dvāraṃ paśya nirguṇatāṃ mayi||11||

12yadyadāsyadanujñāṃ vai pūrvameva gṛhānprati
|
prasthātuṃ sindhurājasya nābhaviṣyajjanakṣayaḥ||12||

13jayadratho jīvitārthī gacchamāno gṛhānprati
|
mayā'nāryeṇa saṃruddho droṇātprāpyābhayaṃ sakhe||13||

14adya me bhrātaraḥ kṣīṇāścitrasenāda‌yo raṇe
|
bhīmasenaṃ samāsādya paśyatāṃ no durātmanām||14||

15karṇa uvāca|
ācāryaṃ mā vigarhasva śaktyā'sau yuddhyate dvijaḥ
|
yathābalaṃ yathotsāhaṃ tyaktvā jīvitamātmanaḥ||15||

16yadyenaṃ samatikramya praviṣṭaḥ śvetavāhanaḥ
|
nātra sūkṣmo'pi doṣaḥ syādācāryasya kathaṃcana||16||

17kṛtī dakṣo yuvā śūraḥ kṛtāstro laghuvikramaḥ
|
divyāstrayuktamāsthāya rathaṃ vānaralakṣaṇam||17||

18kṛṣṇena ca gṛhītāśvamabhedyakavacāvṛtaḥ
|
gāṇḍīvamajaraṃ divyaṃ dhanurādāya vīryavān||18||

19pravarṣanniśitānbāṇān bāhudraviṇadarpitaḥ
|
yadarjuno'bhyayāddroṇamupapannaṃ hi tasya tat||19||

20ācāryaḥ sthaviro rājan śīghrayāne tathā'kṣamaḥ
|
bāhuvyāyāmaceṣṭāyāmaśaktastu narādhipa||20||

21tenaivamabhyatikrāntaḥ śvetāśvaḥ kṛṣṇasārathiḥ
|
tasya doṣaṃ na paśyāmi droṇasyānena hetunā||21||

22ajayyānpāṇḍavānmanye droṇenāstravidā mṛghe
|
tathā hyenamatikramya praviṣṭaḥ śvetavāhanaḥ||22||

23daivādiṣṭo'nyathābhāvo na manye vidyate kvacit
|
yato no yudhyamānānāṃ paraṃ śaktyā suyodhana||23||

24saindhavo nihato yuddhe daivamatra paraṃ smṛtam
|
paraṃ yatnaṃ kurvatāṃ ca tvayā sārdhaṃ raṇājire||24||

25hatvā'smākaṃ pauruṣaṃ vai devaṃ paścātkarotinaḥ
|
satataṃ ceṣṭamānānāṃ nikṛtyā vikrameṇa ca||25||

26daivopasṛṣṭaḥ puruṣo yatkarma kurute kvacit
|
kṛtaṃ kṛtaṃ hi tatkarma daivena vinipātyate||26||

27yatkartavyaṃ manuṣyeṇa vyavasāyavatā sadā
|
tatkāryamaviśaṅkena siddhirdaive pratiṣṭhitā||27||

28nikṛtyā vañcitāḥ pārthā viṣayogaiśca bhārata
|
dagdhā jatugṛhe cāpi dyūtena ca parājitāḥ||28||

29rājanītiṃ vyapāśritya prahitāścaiva kānanam
|
yatnena ca kṛtaṃ tattaddaivena vinipātitam||29||

30yudhyasva yatnamāsthāya daivaṃ kṛtvā nirarthakam
|
yatatastava teṣāṃ ca daivaṃ mārgeṇa yāsyati||30||

31na teṣāṃ matipūrvaṃ hi sukṛtaṃ dṛśyate kvacit
|
duṣkṛtaṃ tava vā vīra buddhyā hīnaṃ kurudvaha||31||

32daivaṃ pramāṇaṃ sarvasya sukṛtasyetarasya vā
|
ananyakarma daivaṃ hi jāgarti svapatāmapi||32||

33bahūni tava sainyāni yodhāśca bahavastava
|
na tathā pāṇḍuputrāṇāmevaṃ yuddhamavartata||33||

34tairalpairbahavo yūyaṃ kṣayaṃ nītāḥ prahāriṇaḥ
|
śaṅke devasya tattkarma pauruṣaṃ yena nāśitam||34||

35sañjaya uvāca|
evaṃ saṃbhāṣamāṇānāṃ bahu tattajjanādhipa
|
pāṇḍavānāmanīkāni samadṛśyanta saṃyuge||35||

36tataḥ pravavṛte yuddha vyatiṣaktaratha-dvipam
|
tāvakānāṃ paraiḥ sārdhaṃ rājan durmantrite tava||36||
