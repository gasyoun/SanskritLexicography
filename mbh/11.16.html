Mahābhārata 11.16 (Strīparvan)

1vaiśampāyana uvāca|evamuktvā tu gāndhārī kurūṇāmavakartanam
|
apaśyattatra tiṣṭhantī sarvaṃ divyena cakṣuṣā||1||

2pativratā mahābhāgā samānavratacāriṇī
|
ugreṇa tapasā yuktā satataṃ satyavādinī||2||

3varadānena kṛṣṇasya maharṣeḥ puṇyakarmaṇaḥ
|
divyajñānabalopetā vividhaṃ paryadevayat||3||

4dadarśa sā buddhimatī dūrādapi yathā'ntike
|
raṇājiraṃ nṛvīrāṇāmadbhutaṃ romaharṣaṇam||4||

5asthikeśavasākīrṇaṃ śoṇitaughapariplutam
|
śarīrairbahusāhasrairvinikīrṇaṃ samantataḥ||5||

6gajāśvarathayodhānāmāvṛtaṃ rudhirāvilaiḥ
|
śarīrairaśiraskaiśca videhaiśca śirogaṇaiḥ||6||

7gajāśvanaranārīṇāṃ niḥsanairabhisaṃvṛtam
|
sṛgālabalakākolakaṅkakākaniṣevitam||7||

8rakṣasāṃ puruṣādānāṃ modanaṃ kurarākulam
|
aśivābhiḥ śivābhiśca nāditaṃ gṛdhrasevitam||8||

9tato vyāsābhyanujñāto dhṛtarāṣṭro mahīpatiḥ
|
pāṇḍuputrāśca te sarve yudhiṣṭhirapurogamāḥ||9||

10vāsudevaṃ puraskṛtya hatabandhuṃ ca pārthivam
|
kurustriyaḥ samādāya jagmurāyodhanaṃ prati||10||

11samāsādya kurukṣetraṃ tāḥ striyo nihateśvarāḥ
|
apaśyanta hatāṃstatra putrānbhrātṝnpitṝnpatīn||11||

12kravyādairbhakṣyamāṇānvai gomāyubalavāyasaiḥ
|
bhūtaiḥ piśācai rakṣobhirvividhaiśca niśācaraiḥ||12||

13rudrākrīḍanibhaṃ dṛṣṭvā tadā viśasanaṃ striyaḥ
|
mahārhebhyo'tha yānebhyo vikrośantyo nipetire||13||

14adṛṣṭapūrvaṃ paśantyo duḥkhārtā bharatastriyaḥ
|
śarīreṣvaskhalannanyāḥ nyapantyaścāparā bhuvi||14||

15śrāntānāṃ cāpyanāthānāṃ nāsītkācana cetanā
|
pāñcālakuruyoṣāṇāṃ kṛpaṇaṃ tadabhūnmahat||15||

16duḥkhopahatacittābhiḥ samantādanunāditam
|
dṛṣṭvā yodhanamatyugraṃ dharmajñā subalātmajā||16||

17tataḥ sā puṇḍarīkākṣamāmantrya puruṣottamam
|
kurūṇāṃ vaiśamaṃ dṛṣṭvā idaṃ vacanamabravīt||17||

18paśyaitāḥ puṇḍarīkākṣa snuṣā me nihateśvarāḥ
|
prakīrṇakeśāḥ krośantīḥ kururīriva mādhava||18||

19amūstvabhisamāgamya smarantyo bhartṛjān guṇān
|
pṛthagevābhyadhāvantyaḥ putrānbhrātṝnpitṝnpatīn||19||

20vīrasūbhirmahābāho hataputrābhirāvṛtam
|
kvacicca vīrapatnībhirhatavīrābhirāvṛtam||20||

21śobhitaṃ puruṣavyāghraiḥ karṇabhīṣmābhimanyubhiḥ
|
droṇadrupadaśalyaiśca jvaladbhiriva pāvakaiḥ||21||

22kāñcanaiḥ kavacairniṣkairmaṇibhiśca mahādhanaiḥ
|
adbhutairhastakeyūraiḥ sragbhiśca samalaṅkṛtam||22||

23vīrabāhuvisṛṣṭābhiḥ śaktibhiḥ parighairapi
|
khaḍgaiśca vividhaistīkṣṇaiḥ saśaraiśca śarāsanaiḥ||23||

24kravyādasaṅghairmuditaistiṣṭhadbhiḥ sahitaiḥ kvacit
|
kvacidākrīḍamānaiśca śayānaiścāparaiḥ kvacit||24||

25etadevaṃvidhaṃ vīra sampaśyāyodhanaṃ vibho
|
paśyamanā hi dahyāmi śokenāhaṃ janārdana||25||

26pāñcālānāṃ kurūṇāṃ ca vināśe madhusūdana
|
pañcānāmapi bhūtānāmahaṃ vadhamacintayam||26||

27tānsuparṇāśca gṛdhrāśca karṣayantyasṛgukṣitāḥ
|
vigṛhya caraṇairgṛdhrā bhakṣayanti sahasraśaḥ||27||

28jayadrathasya karṇasya tathaiva droṇabhīṣmayoḥ
|
abhimanyorvināśaṃ ca kaścintayitumarhati||28||

29avadhyakalpānnihatāngatasatvānacetasaḥ
|
gṛdhrakaṅkavaṭaśyenaśvasṛgālasamāvṛtān||29||

30amarṣavaśamāpannānduryodhanavaśe sthitān
|
paśyemānpuruṣavyāghrānsaṃśāntānpāvakāniva||30||

31śayanā ye purā sarve mṛdūni śayanāni ca
|
vipannāste'dya vasudhāṃ vivṛtāmadhiśerate||31||

32bandibhiḥ satataṃ kāle stuvadbhirabhinanditāḥ
|
śivānāmaśivā ghorāḥ śṛṇvanti vividhā giraḥ||32||

33ye purā śerate vīrāḥ śayaneṣu yaśasvinaḥ
|
candanāgurudigdhāṅgāste'dya pāṃsuṣu śerate||33||

34teṣāmābharaṇānyete gṛghragomāyuvāyasāḥ
|
ākṣipanti śivā ghorā vinadantyaḥ punaḥ punaḥ||34||

35bāṇānviniśitānpītānnistriṃśānvimalā gadāḥ
|
yuddhābhimāninaḥ sarve jīvanta iva bibhrati||35||

36surūpavarṇā bahavaḥ kravyādairavaghaṭṭitāḥ
|
ṛṣabhapratirūpākṣāḥ śerate haritasrajaḥ||36||

37apare punarāliṅgya gadāḥ parighabāhavaḥ
|
śerate'bhimukhāḥ śūrā dayitā iva yoṣitaḥ||37||

38bibhrataḥ kavacānyanye vimalānyāyudhāni ca
|
na dharṣayanti kravyādā jīvantīti janārdana||38||

39kravyādaiḥ kṛṣyamāṇānāmapareṣāṃ mahātmanām
|
śātakaumbhyaḥ srajaścitrā viprakīrṇāḥ samantataḥ||39||

40ete gomāyavo bhīmā nihatānāṃ yaśasvinām
|
kaṇṭhāntaragatānhārānākṣipanti sahasraśaḥ||40||

41sarveṣvapararātreṣu yānavandanta bandinaḥ
|
stutibhiśca parārdhyābhirupacāraiśca śikṣitāḥ||41||

42tānimāḥ paridevanti duḥkhartāḥ paramāṅganāḥ
|
kṛpaṇaṃ vṛṣṇiśārdūla duḥkhaśokārditā bhṛśam||42||

43raktotpalavanānīva vibhānti rucirāṇi ca
|
mukhāni paramastrīṇāṃ pariśuṣkāṇi keśava||43||

44ruditādviratā hyetā dhyāyantyaḥ saparicchadāḥ
|
kurustriyo'bhigacchanti tena tenaiva duḥkhitāḥ||44||

45etānyādityavarṇāni tapanīyanibhāni ca
|
roṣarodanatābhrāṇi vaktrāṇi kuruyoṣitām||45||

46śyāmānāṃ varavarṇānāṃ gaurīṇāmekavāsasām
|
duryodhanavarastrīṇāṃ paśya vṛndāni keśava||46||

47āsāmaparipūrṇārthaṃ niśamya paridevitam
|
itarerasaṅkrandānna vijānanti yoṣitaḥ||47||

48etā dīrghamivocchvasya vikruśya ca vilapya ca
|
vispandamānā duḥkhena vīrā jahati jīvitam||48||

49bahvyo dṛṣṭvā śarīrāṇi krośanti vilapanti ca
|
pāṇibhiścāparā ghnanti śirāṃsi mṛdupāṇayaḥ||49||

50śirobhiḥ patitairhastaiḥ sarvāṅgairyūthaśaḥ kṛtaiḥ
|
itaretasampṛktairākīrṇā bhāti medinī||50||

51viśiraskānatho kāyāndṛṣṭvā hyetānaninditān
|
muhyantyanugatā nāryo videhāni śirāṃsi ca||51||

52śiraḥ kāyena sandhāya prekṣamāṇā vicetasaḥ
|
apaśyantyo paraṃ tatra nedamasye'ti duḥkhitāḥ||52||

53bāhūrucaraṇānanyānviśikhonmathitānpṛthak
|
sandadhatyo'sukhāviṣṭā mūrchantyetāḥ punaḥ punaḥ||53||

54utkṛtyaśirasaścānyānvijagdhānmṛgapakṣibhiḥ
|
dṛṣṭvā kāścinna jānanti bhartṝnbharatayoṣitaḥ||54||

55pāṇibhiścāparā ghnanti śirāṃsi madhusūdana
|
prekṣya bhrātṝnpitṝnputrānpatīṃśca nihatānparaiḥ||55||

56bāhubhiśca sakhaḍgaiśca śirobhiśca sakuṇḍalaiḥ
|
agamyakalpā pṛthivī māṃsaśoṇitakardamā||56||

57babhūva bharataśreṣṭha prāṇibhirgatajīvitaiḥ
|
na duḥkheṣūcitāḥ pūrvaṃ duḥkhaṃ gāhantyaninditāḥ||57||

58bhrātṛbhiḥ patibhiḥ putrairupākīrṇāṃ vasundharām
|
yūthānīva kiśorīṇāṃ sukeśīnāṃ janārdana||58||

59snuṣāṇāṃ dhṛtarāṣṭrasya paśya vṛndānyanekaśaḥ
|
ito duḥkhataraṃ kinnu keśava pratibhāti me||59||

60yadimāḥ kurvate sarvā ravamuccāvacaṃ striyaḥ
|
nūnamācaritaṃ pāpaṃ mayā pūrveṣu janmasu||60||

61yā paśyāmi hatānputrānpautrānbhrātṝṃśca mādhava
|
evamārtā vilapatī samābhāṣya janārdanam
|
gāndhārī putraśokārtā dadarśa nihataṃ sutam||61||
