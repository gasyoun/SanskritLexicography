Mahābhārata 3.249 (Āraṇyakaparvan)

1duryodhana uvāca|
citrasenaṃ samāgamya prahasannarjunastadā
|
idaṃ vacanamaklībamabravīt paravīrahā||1||

2bhrātṝnarhasi me vīra moktuṃ gandharvasattama
|
anarhadharṣaṇā hīme jīvamāneṣu pāṇḍuṣu||2||

3evamuktastu gandharvaḥ pāṇḍavena mahātmanā
|
uvāca yat karṇa vayaṃ mantrayanto vinirgatāḥ||3||

4draṣṭāraḥ sma sukhāddhīnān sadārān pāṇḍavāniti
|
tasminnuccāryamāṇe tu gandharveṇa vacastathā||4||

5bhūmervivaramanvaicchaṃ praveṣṭuṃ vrīḍayā'nvitaḥ
|
yudhiṣṭhiramathāgamya gandharvāḥ saha pāṇḍavaiḥ||5||

6asmaddurmantritaṃ tasmai baddhāṃścāsmānnyavedayan
|
strīsamakṣamahaṃ dīno baddhaḥ śatruvaśaṃ gataḥ||6||

7yudhiṣṭhirasyopahṛtaḥ kiṃ nu duḥkhamataḥ param
|
ye me nirākṛtā nityaṃ ripuryeṣāmahaṃ sadā||7||

8tairmokṣito'haṃ durbuddhirdantaṃ taireva jīvitam
|
prāptaḥ syāṃ yadyahaṃ vīra vadhaṃ tasmin mahāraṇe||8||

9śreyastadbhavitā mahyaṃ naivaṃbhūtasya jīvitam
|
abhūdyaśaḥ pṛthivyāṃ me khyātaṃ gandharvato vadhāt||9||

10prāptāśca puṇyalokāḥ syurmahendrasadane'kṣayāḥ
|
yattvadya me vyavasitaṃ tacchṛṇudhvaṃ nararṣabhāḥ||10||

11iha prāyamupāsiṣye yūyaṃ vrajata vai gṛhān
|
bhrātaraścaiva me sarve yāntvadya svapuraṃ prati||11||

12karṇaprabhṛtayaścaiva suhṛdo bāndhavāśca ye
|
duḥśāsanaṃ puraskṛtya prayāntvadya puraṃ prati||12||

13nahyahaṃ samprayāsyāmi puraṃ śatrunirākṛtaḥ
|
śatrumānāpaho bhūtvā suhṛdāṃ mānakṛt tathā||13||

14sa suhṛcchokado jātaḥ śatrūṇāṃ harṣavardhanaḥ
|
vāraṇāhvayamāsādya kiṃ vakṣyāmi janādhipam||14||

15bhīṣmadroṇau kṛpadrauṇī viduraḥ sañjayastathā
|
bāhlīkaḥ saumadattiśca ye cānye vṛddhasammatāḥ||15||

16brāhmaṇāḥ śreṇimukhyāśca tathodāsīnavṛttayaḥ
|
kiṃ māṃ vakṣyanti kiñcāpi prativakṣyāmi tānaham||16||

17ripūṇāṃ śirasi sthitvā tathā vikramya corasi
|
ātmadoṣāt paribhraṣṭaḥ kathaṃ vakṣyāmi tānaham||17||

18durvinītāḥ śriyaṃ prāpya vidyāmaiśvaryameva ca
|
tiṣṭhanti na ciraṃ bhadre yathāhaṃ madagarvitaḥ||18||

19aho nārhamidaṃ karma kaṣṭaṃ duścaritaṃ kṛtam
|
svayaṃ durbuddhinā mohādyena prāpto'smi saṃśayam||19||

20tasmāt prāyamupāsiṣye na hi śakṣyāmi jīvitum
|
cetayāno hi ko jīvet kṛcchrācchatrubhiruddhṛtaḥ||20||

21śatrubhiścāvahasito mānī pauruṣavarjitaḥ
|
pāṇḍavairvikramāḍhyaiśca sāvamānamavekṣitaḥ||21||

22vaiśampāyana uvāca|
evaṃ cintāparigato duḥśāsanamathābravīt
|
duḥśāsana nibodhedaṃ vacanaṃ mama bhārata||22||

23pratīccha tvaṃ mayā dattamabhiṣekaṃ nṛpo bhava
|
praśādhi pṛthivīṃ sphītāṃ karṇasaubalapālitām||23||

24bhrātṝn pālaya visrabdhaṃ maruto vṛtrahā yathā
|
bāndhavāścopajīvantu devā iva śatakratum||24||

25brāhmaṇeṣu sadā vṛttiṃ kurvīthāścāpramādataḥ
|
bandhūnāṃ suhṛdāṃ caiva bhavethāstvaṃ gatiḥ sadā||25||

26jñātīṃścāpyanupaśyethā viṣṇurdevagaṇān yathā
|
guravaḥ pālanīyāste gaccha pālaya medinīm||26||

27nandayan suhṛdaḥ sarvān śātravāṃścāvabhartsayan
|
kaṇṭhe cainaṃ pariṣvajya gamyatāmityuvāca ha||27||

28tasya tadvacanaṃ śrutvā dīno duḥśāsano'bravīt
|
aśrukaṇṭhaḥ suduḥkhārtaḥ prāñjaliḥ praṇipatya ca||28||

29sagadgadamidaṃ vākyaṃ bhrātaraṃ jyeṣṭhamātmanaḥ
|
prasīdetyapatadbhūmau dūyamānena cetasā||29||

30duḥkhitaḥ pādayostasya netrajaṃ jalamutsṛjan
|
uktavāṃśca nakhyāghro naitadevaṃ bhaviṣyati||30||

31vidīryet sakalā bhūmirdyauścāpi śakalī bhavet
|
ravirātmaprabhāṃ jahyāt somaḥ śītāṃśutāṃ tyajet||31||

32vāyuḥ śaighryamatho jahyāddhimavāṃśca parivrajet
|
śuṣyet toyaṃ samudreṣu vahnirapyuṣṇatāṃ tyajet||32||

33na cāhaṃ tvadṛte rājan praśāseyaṃ vasundharām
|
punaḥ punaḥ prasīdeti vākyaṃ cedamuvāca ha||33||

34tvameva naḥ kule rājā bhaviṣyasi śataṃ samāḥ
|
evamuktvā sa rājānaṃ susvaraṃ praruroda ha||34||

35pādau saṃspṛśya mānārhau bhrāturjyeṣṭhasya bhārata
|
tathā tau duḥkhitī dṛṣṭvā duḥśāsanasuyodhanau||35||

36adhigamya vyathāviṣṭaḥ karṇastau pratyabhāṣata
|
viṣīdathaḥ kiṃ kauravyau bāliśyāt prākṛtāviva||36||

37na śokaḥ śocamānasya vinivarteta karhicit
|
yadā ca śocataḥ śoko vyasanaṃ nāpakarṣati||37||

38sāmarthya kiṃ tataḥ śoke śocamānau prapaśyathaḥ
|
dhṛtiṃ gṛhṇītaṃ mā śatrūn śocantau nandayiṣyathaḥ||38||

39kartavyaṃ hi kṛtaṃ rājan pāṇḍavaistava mokṣaṇam
|
nityameva priyaṃ kāryaṃ rājño viṣayavāsibhiḥ||39||

40pālyamānāstvayā te hi nivasanti gatajvarāḥ
|
nārhasyevaṃgate manyuṃ kartuṃ prākṛtavadyathā||40||

41viṣaṇṇāstava sodaryāstvayi prāyaṃ samāsthite
|
uttiṣṭha vraja bhadraṃ te samāśvāsaya sodarān||41||
