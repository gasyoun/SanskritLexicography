Mahābhārata 6.21 (Bhīṣmaparvan)

1sañjaya uvāca|
bṛhatīṃ dhārtarāṣṭrasya senāṃ dṛṣṭvā samudyatām
|
viṣādamagamadrājā kuntīputro yudhiṣṭhiraḥ ||1||

2vyūhaṃ bhīṣmeṇa cābhedyaṃ kalpitaṃ prekṣya pāṇḍavaḥ
|
abhedyamiva saṅprekṣya vivarṇo'rjunamabravīt ||2||

3dhanañjaya kathaṃ śakyamasmābhiryoddhumāhave
|
dhārtarāṣṭrairmahābāho yeṣāṃ yoddhā pitāmahaḥ ||3||

4akṣobhyo'yamabhedyaśca bhīṣmeṇāmitrakarṣiṇā
|
kalpitaḥ śāstradṛṣṭena vidhinā bhūrivarcasā ||4||

5te vayaṃ saṃśayaṃ prāptāḥ sasainyāḥ śatrukarṣaṇa
|
kathamasmānmahāvyūhādutthānaṃ no bhaviṣyati ||5||

6athārjuno'bravītpārthaṃ yudhiṣṭhiramamitrahā
|
viṣaṇṇamiva samprekṣya tava rājannanīkinīm ||6||

7prajñayābhyadhikāñśūrānguṇayuktānbahūnapi
|
jayantyalpatarā yena tannibodha viśāmpate ||7||

8tatra te kāraṇaṃ rājanpravakṣyāmyanasūyave
|
nāradastamṛṣirveda bhīṣmadroṇau ca pāṇḍava ||8||

9enamevārthamāśritya yuddhe devāsure'bravīt
|
pitāmahaḥ kila purā mahendrādīndivaukasaḥ ||9||

10na tathā balavīryābhyāṃ jayanti vijigīṣavaḥ
|
yathā satyānṛśaṃsyābhyāṃ dharmeṇaivodyamena ca ||10||

11jñātvā dharmamadharmaṃ ca lobhaṃ cottamamāsthitāḥ
|
yuddhyadhvamatahaṅkārā yato dharmastato jayaḥ ||11||

12evaṃ rājanvijānīhi dhruvo'smākaṃ raṇe jayaḥ
|
yathā tu nāradaḥ prāha yataḥ kṛṣṇastato jayaḥ ||12||

13guṇabhūto jayaḥ kṛṣṇe pṛṣṭhato'bhyeti mādhavam
|
tadyathā vijayaścāsya sannatiścāparo guṇaḥ ||13||

14anantatejā govindaḥ śatrupūgeṣu nirvyathaḥ
|
puruṣaḥ sanātanamayo yataḥ kṛṣṇastato jayaḥ ||14||

15purā hyeṣa harirbhūtvā vikuṇṭho'kuṇṭhasāyakaḥ
|
surāsurānavasphūrjannabravītke jayantviti ||15||

16kathaṃ kṛṣṇa jayemeti yairuktaṃ tatra tairjitam
|
tatprasādāddhi trailokyaṃ prāptaṃ śakrādibhiḥ suraiḥ ||16||

17tasya te na vyathāṃ kāñcidiha paśyāmi bhārata
|
yasya te yajamāśāste viśvabhuk tridiveśvaraḥ ||17||
