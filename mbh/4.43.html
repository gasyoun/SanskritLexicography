Mahābhārata 4.43 (Virāṭaparvan)

1bṛhannalovāca
|
yanmāṃ pūrvamihāpṛcchaḥ śatrusenāpahāriṇam
|
gāṇḍīvametat pārthasya lokeṣu viditaṃ dhanuḥ ||1||

2sarvāyudhamahāmātraṃ śātakumbhapariṣkṛtam
|
etattada‌rjunasyāsīdgāṇḍīvaṃ paramāyudham ||2||

3yattacchatasahastreṇa saṃmitaṃ rāṣṭravarddhanam
|
yena devānmanuṣyāṃśca pārtho vijayate mṛdhe ||3||

4citramuccāvacairvaṇaiḥ ślakṣṇamāyatamavraṇam
|
devadānavagandharvaiḥ pūjitaṃ śāśvatīḥ samāḥ ||4||

5etadvarṣasahasraṃ tu brahmā pūrvamadhārayat
|
tato'nantaramevātha prajāpatiradhārayat ||5||

6trīṇi pañcaśataṃ caiva śakro'śīti ca pañca ca
|
somaḥ phañcaśataṃ rājā tathaiva varuṇaḥ śatam
|
pārthaḥ pañca ca ṣaṣṭiṃ ca varṣāṇi śvetavāhanaḥ ||6||

7mahāvīryaṃ mahādivyametat taddhanuruttamam
|
etat pārthamanuprāptaṃ varuṇāccārudarśanam ||7||

8pūjitaṃ suramartyeṣu bibharti paramaṃ vapuḥ
|
supārśvaṃ bhīmasenasya jātarūpagrahaṃ dhanuḥ
|
yena pārtho'jayat‌ kṛtsnāṃ diśaṃ prācīṃ paraṃtapaḥ ||8||

9indragopakacitraṃ ca yadetaccārudarśanam
|
rājño yudhiṣṭhirasyaitadvairāṭe dhanuruttamam ||9||

10sūryā yasmiṃstu sauvarṇāḥ prakāśante prakāśinaḥ
|
tejasā prajvalanto vai nakulasyaitadāyudham ||10||

11śalabhā yatra sauvarṇāstapanīyavicitritāḥ
|
etanmādrīsutasyāpi sahadevasya kārmukam ||11||

12ye tvime kṣurasa‌ṅkāśāḥ sahasrā lomavāhinaḥ
|
ete'rjunasya vairāṭe śarāḥ sarpaviṣopamāḥ ||12||

13ete jvalantaḥ sa‌ṅgrāme tejasā śīghragāminaḥ
|
bhavanti vīrasyākṣayyā vyūhataḥ samare ripūn ||13||

14ye ceme pṛthavo dīrghāścandrabimbārdhadarśanāḥ
|
ete bhīmasya niśitā ripukṣayakarāḥ śarāḥ ||14||

15hāridravarṇā ye tvete hemapuṅkhāḥ śilāśitāḥ
|
nakulasya kalāpo'yaṃ pañcaśārdūlalakṣaṇaḥ ||15||

16yenāsau vyajayatkṛtsnāṃ pratīcīṃ diśamāhave
|
kalāpo hyeṣa tasyāsīnmādrīputrasya dhīmataḥ ||16||

17ye tvime bhāskarākārāḥ sarvapārasavāḥ śarāḥ
|
ete citrakriyopetāḥ sahadevasya dhīmataḥ ||17||

18ye tvime niśitāḥ pītāḥ pṛthavo dīrghavāsasaḥ
|
hemapuṅkhāstriparvāṇo rājña ete mahāśarāḥ ||18||

19yastvayaṃ sāyako dīrghaḥ śilīpṛṣṭhaḥ śilīmukhaḥ
|
arjunasyaiṣa saṅgrāme gurubhārasaho dṛḍhaḥ ||19||

20vaiyāghrakośaḥ sumahān bhīmasenasya sāyakaḥ
|
gurubhārasaho divyaḥ śātravāṇāṃ bhayaṅkaraḥ ||20||

21suphalaścitrakośaśca hematsaruranuttamaḥ
|
nistriṃśaḥ kauravasyaiṣa dharmarājasya dhīmataḥ ||21||

22yastu pāñcanakhe kośo nihitaścitrayodhane
|
nakulasyaiṣa nistriṃśo gurubhārasaho dṛḍhaḥ ||22||

23yastvayaṃ vipulaḥ khaḍgo gavye kośe samarpitaḥ
|
sahadevasya viddhyenaṃ sarvabhārasahaṃ dṛḍham ||23||
