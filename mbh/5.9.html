Mahābhārata 5.9 (Udyogaparvan)

1yudhiṣṭhira uvāca|
kathamindreṇa rājendra sabhāryeṇa mahātmanā
|
duḥkhaṃ prāptaṃ paraṃ ghorametadicchāmi veditum ||1||

2śalya uvāca|
śṛṇu rājan purāvṛttamitihāsaṃ purātanam
|
sabhāryeṇa yathā prāptaṃ duḥkhamindreṇa bhārata ||2||

3tvaṣṭā prajāpatirhyāsīddevaśreṣṭho mahātapāḥ
|
sa putraṃ vai triśirasamindradrohāt kilāsṛjat ||3||

4aindraṃ sa prārthayat sthānaṃ viśvarūpo mahādyutiḥ
|
taistribhirvadanairghoraiḥ sūryendujvalanopamaiḥ ||4||

5vedānekena so'dhīte surāmekena cāpibat
|
ekena ca diśaḥ sarvāḥ pibanniva nirīkṣate ||5||

6sa tapasvī mudurdānto dharme tapasi codyataḥ
|
tapastasya mahattīvraṃ suduścaramarindama ||6||

7tasya dṛṣṭvā tapovīryaṃ satyaṃ cāmitatejasaḥ
|
viṣādamagamacchakra indro'yaṃ mā bhavediti ||7||

8kathaṃ sajjecca bhogeṣu na ca tapyenmahattapaḥ
|
vivardhamānastriśirāḥ sarvaṃ hi bhuvanaṃ graset ||8||

9iti saṃcintya bahudhā buddhimān bharatarṣabha
|
ājñāpayat so'psarasastvaṣṭṛputrapralobhane ||9||

10yathā sa sajjentriśirāḥ kāmabhogeṣu vai bhṛśam
|
kṣipraṃ kuruta gacchadhvaṃ pralobhayata mā ciram ||10||

11śṛṅgāraveṣāḥ suśroṇyo hārairyuktā manoharaiḥ
|
hāvabhāvasamāyuktāḥ sarvāḥ saundaryaśobhitāḥ ||11||

12pralobhayata bhadraṃ vaḥ śamayadhvaṃ bhayaṃ mama
|
asvasthaṃ hyātmanā''tmānaṃ lakṣayāmi varāṅganāḥ ||12||

13bhayaṃ tanme mahāghoraṃ kṣipraṃ nāśayatābalāḥ
|
apsarasa ūcuḥ| tathā yatnaṃ kariṣyāmaḥ śakra tasya pralobhane
|
yathā nāvāpsyasi bhayaṃ tasmādbalaniṣūdana ||13||

14nirdahanniva cakṣurbhyāṃ yo'sāvāste taponidhiḥ
|
taṃ pralobhayituṃ deva gacchāmaḥ sahitā vayam ||14||

15yatiṣyāmo vaśe kartuṃ vyapanetuṃ ca te bhayam| śalya uvāca|
indreṇa tāstvanujñātā jagmustriśiraso'ntikam
|
tatra tā vividhairbhāvairlobhayantyo varāṅganāḥ ||15||

16nityaṃ saṃdarśayāmāsustathaivāṅgeṣu sauṣṭhavam
|
nābhyagacchat praharṣaṃ tāḥ sa paśyan sumahātapāḥ ||16||

17indriyāṇi vaśe kṛtvā pūrvasāgarasannibhaḥ
|
tāstu yatnaṃ paraṃ kṛtvā punaḥ śakramupasthitāḥ ||17||

18kṛtāñjalipuṭāḥ sarvā devarājamathābruvan
|
na sa śakyaḥ sudurdharṣo dhairyāccālayituṃ prabho ||18||

19yatte kāryaṃ mahābhāga kriyatāṃ tadanantaram
|
saṃpūjyāpsarasaḥ śakro visṛjya ca mahāmatiḥ ||19||

20cintayāmāsa tasyaiva vadhopāyaṃ yudhiṣṭhira
|
sa tūṣṇīṃ cintayan vīro devarājaḥ pratāpavān ||20||

21viniścitamatirdhīmān vadhe triśiraso'bhavat
|
vajramasya kṣipāmyadya sa kṣipraṃ nabhaviṣyati ||21||

22śatruḥ pravṛddho nopekṣyo durbalo'pi balīyasā
|
śāstrabuddhyā viniścitya kṛtvā buddhiṃ vadhe dṛḍhām ||22||

23atha vaiśvānaranibhaṃ ghorarūpaṃ bhayāvaham
|
mumoca vajraṃ saṅkruddhaḥ śakrastriśirasaṃ prati ||23||

24sa papāta hatastena vajreṇa dṛḍhamāhataḥ
|
parvatasyeva śikharaṃ praṇunnaṃ medinītale ||24||

25taṃ tu vajrahataṃ dṛṣṭvā śayānamacalopamam
|
na śarma lebhe devendro dīpitastasya tejasā ||25||

26hato'pi dīptatejāḥ sa jīvanniva hi dṛśyate
|
ghātitasya śirāṃsyājau jīvantīvādbhutāni vai ||26||

27tato'tibhītagātrastu śakra āste vicārayan
|
athājagāma paraśuṃ skandhenādāya vardhakiḥ ||27||

28tadaraṇyaṃ mahārāja yatrāste'sau nipātitaḥ
|
sa bhītastatra takṣāṇaṃ ghaṭamānaṃ śacīpatiḥ ||28||

29apaśyadabravīccainaṃ satvaraṃ pākaśāsanaḥ
|
kṣipraṃ chindhi śirāṃsyasya kuruṣva vacanaṃ mama ||29||

30takṣovāca
|
mahāskandho bhṛśaṃ hyeṣa paraśurnabhaviṣyati
|
kartuṃ cāhaṃ na śakṣyāmi karma sadbhirvigarhitam ||30||

31indra uvāca|
mā bhaistvaṃ śīghrametadvai kuruṣva vacanaṃ mama
|
matprasādāddhi te śastraṃ vajrakalpaṃ bhaviṣyati ||31||

32takṣovāca
|
kaṃ bhavantamahaṃ vidyāṃ ghorakarmāṇamadya vai
|
etadicchāmyahaṃ śrotuṃ tattvena kathayasva me ||32||

33indra uvāca|
ahamindro devarājastakṣan viditamastu te
|
kuruṣvaitadyathoktaṃ me takṣan mā'tra vicāraya ||33||

34takṣovāca
|
krūreṇa nāpatrapase kathaṃ śakreha karmaṇā
|
ṛṣiputramimaṃ hatvā brahmahatyābhayaṃ na te ||34||

35śakra uvāca|
paścāddharmaṃ cariṣyāmi pāvanārthaṃ suduścaram
|
śatrureṣa mahāvīryo vajreṇa nihato mayā ||35||

36adyāpi cāhamudvignastakṣannasmādbibhemi vai
|
kṣipraṃ chindhi śirāṃsi tvaṃ kariṣye'nugrahaṃ tava ||36||

37śiraḥ paśoste dāsyanti bhāgaṃ yajñeṣu mānavāḥ
|
eṣa te'nugrahastakṣan kṣipraṃ kuru mama priyam ||37||

38śalya uvāca|
etacchrutvā tu takṣā sa mahendravacanāttadā
|
śirāṃsyatha triśirasaḥ kuṭhāreṇācchinat tadā||38||

39nikṛtteṣu tatasteṣu niṣkrāmannaṇḍajāstvatha
|
kapiñjalāstittirāśca kalaviṅkāśca sarvaśaḥ ||39||

40yena vedānadhīte sma pibate somameva ca
|
tasmādvakrārdviniśceruḥ kṣipraṃ tasya kapiñjalāḥ ||40||

41yena sarvā diśo rājan pibanniva nīrīkṣate
|
tasmādvaktrādviniścerustittirāstasya pāṇḍava ||41||

42yatsurāpaṃ tu tasyāsīdvaktraṃ triśirasastadā
|
kalaviṅkāḥ samutpetuḥ śyenāśca bharatarṣabha ||42||

43tatasteṣu nikṛtteṣu vijvaro maghavānatha
|
jagāma tridivaṃ hṛṣṭastakṣā'pi svagṛhān yayau||43||

44mene kṛtārthamātmānaṃ hatvā śatruṃ surārihā
|
tvaṣṭā prajāpatiḥ śrutvā śakreṇātha hataṃ sutam||44||

45krodhasaṃraktanayana idaṃ vacanamabravīt
|
tvaṣṭovāca
|
tapyamānaṃ tapo nityaṃ kṣāntaṃ dāntaṃ jitendriyam
|
vinā'parādhena yataḥ putraṃ hiṃsitavān mama ||45||

46tasmācchakravināśāya vṛtramutpādayāmyaham
|
lokāḥ paśyantu me vīryaṃ tapasaśca balaṃ maham ||46||

47sa ca paśyatu devendro durātmā pāpacetanaḥ
|
upaspṛśya tataḥ kruddhastapasvī sumahāyaśāḥ||47||

48agnau hutvā samutpādya ghoraṃ vṛtramuvāca ha
|
indraśatro vivardhasva prabhāvāttapaso mama||48||

49so'varghata divaṃ stabdhvā sūryavaiśvānaropamaḥ
|
kiṃ karomīti covāca kālasūrya ivoditaḥ||49||

50śakraṃ jahīti cāpyukto jagāma tridivaṃ tataḥ
|
tato yuddhaṃ samabhavadvṛtravāsavayormahat||50||

51saṃkruddhayormahāghoraṃ prasaktaṃ kurusattama
|
tato jagrāha devendraṃ vṛtro vīraḥ śatakratum ||51||

52apāvṛtyākṣipadvaktre śakraṃ kopasamanvitaḥ
|
graste vṛtreṇa śakre tu saṃbhrāntāstridiveśvarāḥ||52||

53asṛjaṃste mahāsattvā jṛmbhikāṃ vṛtranāśinīm
|
vijṛmbhamāṇasya tato vṛtrasyāsyādapāvṛtāt||53||

54svānyaṅgānyabhisaṅkṣipya niṣkrānto balanāśanaḥ
|
tataḥ prabhṛti lokasya jṛmbhikā prāṇisaṃśritā||54||

55jahṛṣuśca surāḥ sarve śakraṃ dṛṣṭvā viniḥsṛtam
|
tataḥ pravavṛte yuddhaṃ vṛtravāsavayoḥ punaḥ ||55||

56saṃrabdhayostadā ghoraṃ suciraṃ bharatarṣabha
|
yadā vyavardhata raṇe vṛtro balasamanvitaḥ||56||

57tvaṣṭustejobalāviddhastadā śakro nyavartata
|
nivṛtte ca tadā devā viṣādamagaman param ||57||

58sametya saha śakreṇa tvaṣṭustejovimohitāḥ
|
amantrayanta te sarve munibhiḥ saha bhārata||58||

59kiṃ kāryamiti vai rājan vicintya bhayamohitāḥ
|
jagmuḥ sarve mahātmānaṃ manobhirviṣṇumavyayam
|
upaviṣṭā mandarāgre sarve vṛtravadhepsavaḥ ||59||
