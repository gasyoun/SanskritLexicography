Mahābhārata 7.29 (Droṇaparvan)

1dhṛtarāṣṭra uvāca| tathā kruddhaḥ kimakarodbhagadattasya pāṇḍavaḥ
|
prāgjyotiṣo vā pārthasya tanme śaṃsa yathātatham||1||

2sañjaya uvāca|
prāgjyotiṣeṇa saṃsaktāvubhau dāśārhapāṇḍavau
|
mṛtyudaṃṣṭrāntikaṃ prāptau sarvabhūtāni menire||2||

3tathā tu śaravarṣāṇi pātayatyaniśaṃ prabho
|
gajaskandhānmahārāja kṛṣṇayoḥ syandanasthayoḥ||3||

4atha kārṣṇāyasairbāṇaiḥ pūrṇakārmukaniḥsṛtaiḥ
|
avidhyaddevakīputraṃ hemapuṅkhaiḥ śilāśitaiḥ||4||

5agnisparśasamāstīkṣṇā bhagattena coditāḥ
|
nirbhidya devakīputraṃ kṣitiṃ jagmuḥ suvāsasaḥ||5||

6tasya pārtho dhanuśchittvā parivāraṃ nihatya ca
|
lālayanniva rājānaṃ bhagadattamayodhayat||6||

7so'rkaraśminibhāṃstīkṣṇāṃstomarān vai caturdaśa
|
apreṣayat savyasācī dvidhaikaikamathācchinat||7||

8tato nāgasya taddharma vyadhamat pākaśāsaniḥ
|
śarajālena mahatā tadvyaśīryata bhūtale||8||

9śīrṇavarmā sa tu gajaḥ śaraiḥ subhṛśamarditaḥ
|
babhau dhārānipātākto vyabhraḥ parvatarāḍiva||9||

10tataḥ prāgjyotiṣaḥ śaktiṃ hemadaṇḍāmayasmayīm
|
vyasṛjadvāsudevāya dvidhā tāmarjuno'cchinat||10||

11tataśchatraṃ dhvajaṃ caiva chittvā rājño'rjunaḥ śaraiḥ
|
vivyādha daśabhistūrṇamutsmayan parvateśvaram||11||

12so'tividdho'rjunaśaraiḥ supuṅkhaiḥ kaṅkapatribhiḥ
|
bhagadattastataḥ kruddhaḥ pāṇḍavasya janādhipaḥ||12||

13vyasṛjattomarān mūrdhni śvetāśvasyonnanāda ca
|
tairarjunasya samare kirīṭaṃ parivartitam||13||

14parivṛttaṃ kirīṭaṃ tadyamayanneva pāṇḍavaḥ
|
sudṛṣṭaḥ kriyatāṃ loka iti rājānamabravīt||14||

15evamuktastu saṃkruddhaḥ śaravarṣeṇa pāṇḍavam
|
abhyavarṣat sa govindaṃ dhanurādāya bhāsvaram||15||

16tasya pārtho dhanuśchittvā tūṇīrān sannikṛtya ca
|
tvaramāṇo dvisaptatyā sarvamarmasvatāḍayat||16||

17viddhastato'tivyathito vaiṣṇavāstramudīrayan
|
abhimantryāṃkuśaṃ kruddho vyasṛjat pāṇḍavorasi||17||

18visṛṣṭaṃ bhagadattena tadastraṃ sarvaghāti vai
|
urasā pratijagrāha pārthaṃ saṃcchādya keśavaḥ||18||

19vaijayantyabhavanmālā tadastraṃ keśavorasi
|
padmakośavicitrāḍhyā sarvartukusumotkaṭā||19||

20jvalanārkenduvarṇābhā pāvakojjvalapallavā
|
tayā padmapalāśinyā vātakampitapatrayā||20||

21śuśubhe'bhyadhikaṃ śauriratasīpuṣpasannibhaḥ
|
tato'rjunaḥ klāntamanāḥ keśavaṃ pratyabhāṣata||21||

22ayudhyamānasturagān saṃyantāsmīti cānagha
|
ityuktvā puṇḍarīkākṣa pratijñāṃ svāṃ na rakṣasi||22||

23yadyahaṃ vyasanī vā syāmaśakto vā nivāraṇe
|
tatastvayaivaṃ kāryaṃ syānna tatkāryaṃ mayi sthite||23||

24sabāṇaḥ sadhanuścāhaṃ sasurāsuramānuṣān
|
śakto lokānimān jetuṃ taccāpi viditaṃ tava||24||

25tato'rjunaṃ vāsudevaḥ pratyuvācārthavadvacaḥ
|
śṛṇu guhyamidaṃ pārtha purā vṛttaṃ yathā'nagha||25||

26caturmūrtirahaṃ śaśvallokatrāṇārthamudyataḥ
|
ātmānaṃ pravibhajyeha lokānāṃ hitamādadhe||26||

27ekā mūrtistapaścaryāṃ kurute me bhuvi sthitā
|
aparā paśyati jagat kurvāṇaṃ sādhvasādhunī||27||

28aparā kurute karma mānuṣaṃ lokamāśritā
|
śete caturthī tvaparā nidrāṃ varṣasahastrikam||28||

29yā'sau varṣasahasrānte mūrtiruttiṣṭhate mama
|
varārhebhyo varān śreṣṭhāṃstasmin kāle dadāti sā||29||

30taṃ tu kālamanuprāptaṃ viditvā pṛthivī tadā
|
ayācata varaṃ yanmāṃ narakārthāya tacchṛṇu||30||

31devānāṃ dānavānāṃ ca avadhyastanayo'stu me
|
upeto vaiṣṇavāstreṇa tanme tvaṃ dātumarhasi||31||

32evaṃ varamahaṃ śrutvā jagatyāstanaye tadā
|
amoghamastraṃ prāyacchaṃ vaiṣṇavaṃ paramaṃ purā||32||

33avocaṃ caitadastraṃ vai hyamoghaṃ bhavatu kṣame
|
narakasyābhirakṣārthaṃ nainaṃ kaścidvadhiṣyati||33||

34anenāstreṇa te guptaḥ sutaḥ parabalārdanaḥ
|
bhaviṣyati durādharṣaḥ sarvalokeṣu sarvadā||34||

35tathetyuktvā gatā devī kṛtakāmā manasvinī
|
sa cāpyāsīddurādharṣo narakaḥ śatrutāpanaḥ||35||

36tasmāt prāgjyotiṣaṃ prāptaṃ tadastraṃ pārtha māmakam
|
nāsyāvadhyo'sti lokeṣu sendrarudreṣu māriṣa||36||

37tanmayā tvatkṛte caitadanyathā vyapanāmitam
|
vimuktaṃ paramāstreṇa jahi pārtha mahāsuram||37||

38vairiṇaṃ jahi durdharṣaṃ bhagadattaṃ suradviṣam
|
yathā'haṃ jaghnivān pūrvaṃ hitārthaṃ narakaṃ tathā||38||

39evamuktastadā pārthaḥ keśavena mahātmanā
|
bhagadattaṃ śitairbāṇaiḥ sahasā samavākirat||39||

40tataḥ pārtho mahābāhurasaṃbhrānto mahāmanāḥ
|
kumbhayorantare nāgaṃ nārācena samārpayat||40||

41sa samāsādya taṃ nāgaṃ bāṇo vajra ivācalam
|
abhyagāt saha puṅkhena valmīkamiva pannagaḥ||41||

42sa karī bhagadattena preryamāṇo muhurmuhuḥ
|
na karoti vacastasya daridrasyeva yoṣitā||42||

43sa tu viṣṭabhya gātrāṇi dantābhyāmavaniṃ yayau
|
na‌dannārtasvanaṃ prāṇānutsasarja mahādvipaḥ||43||

44tato gāṇḍīvadhanvānamabhyabhāṣata keśavaḥ
|
ayaṃ mahattaraḥ pārtha palitena samāvṛtaḥ||44||

45valīsaṃchannanayanaḥ śūraḥ paramadurjayaḥ
|
akṣṇorunmīlanārthāya baddhapaṭṭo hyasau nṛpaḥ||45||

46devavākyāt praciccheda śareṇa bhṛśamarjunaḥ
|
chinnamātreṃ'śuke tasmin ruddhanetro babhūva saḥ||46||

47tamomayaṃ jaganmene bhagadattaḥ pratāpavān
|
tataścandrārdhabimbena bāṇena nataparvaṇā||47||

48bibheda hṛdayaṃ rājño bhagadattasya pāṇḍavaḥ
|
sa bhinnahṛda‌yo rājā bhagadattaḥ kirīṭinā||48||

49śarāsanaṃ śarāṃścaiva gatāsuḥ pramumoca ha
|
śirasastasya vibhraṣṭaṃ papāta ca varāṃśukam
|
nālatāḍanavibhraṣṭaṃ palāśaṃ nalinādiva||49||

50sa hamamālī tapanīyabhāṇḍāt papāta nāgādgirisannikāśāt
|
supuṣpito mārutavegarugṇo mahīdharāgrādiva karṇikāraḥ||50||

51nihatya taṃ narapatimindravikramaṃ sakhāyamindrasya tadaindrirāhave
|
tato'parāṃstava jayakāṅkṣiṇo narān babhañja vāyurbalavān drumāniva||51||
