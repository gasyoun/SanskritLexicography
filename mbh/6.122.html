Mahābhārata 6.122 (Bhīṣmaparvan)

1sañjaya uvāca|tataste pārthivāḥ sarve jagmuḥ svānālayān punaḥ
|
tūṣṇīṃbhūte mahārāja bhīṣme śantanunandane ||1||

2śrutvā tu nihataṃ bhīṣmaṃ rādheyaḥ puruṣarṣabhaḥ
|
īṣadāgatasaṃtrāsastvarayopajagāma ha ||2||

3sa dadarśa mahātmānaṃ śaratalpagataṃ tadā
|
janmaśayyāgataṃ vīraṃ kārtikeyamiva prabhum ||3||

4nimīlitākṣaṃ taṃ vīraṃ sāśrukaṇṭhastadā vṛṣaḥ
|
bhīṣma bhīṣma mahābāho ityuvāca mahādyutiḥ ||4||

5rādheyo'haṃ kuruśreṣṭha nityamakṣigatastava
|
dveṣyo'haṃ tava sarvatra iti cainamuvāca ha ||5||

6tacchrutvā kuruvṛddho hi balī saṃvṛtalocanaḥ
|
śanairudvīkṣya sasnehamidaṃ vacanamabravīt ||6||

7rahitaṃ dhiṣṇyamālokya samutsārya ca rakṣiṇaḥ
|
piteva putraṃ gāṅgeyaḥ parirabhyaikapāṇinā ||7||

8ehyehi me vipratīpa spardhase tvaṃ mayā saha
|
yadi māṃ nādhigacchethā na te śreyo dhruvaṃ bhavet ||8||

9kaunteyastvaṃ na rādheyo na tavādhirathaḥ pitā
|
sūryajastvaṃ mahābāho vidito nāradānmayā ||9||

10kṛṣṇadvaipāyanāccaiva tacca satyaṃ na saṃśayaḥ
|
na ca dveṣo'sti me tāta tvayi satyaṃ bravīmi te ||10||

11tejovadhanimittaṃ tu paruṣaṃ tvā'hamabruvam
|
akasmāt pāṇḍavān sarvānavākṣipasi suvrata||11||

12yenāsi bahuśo rājñā coditaḥ sūtanandana
|
jāto'si dharmalopena tataste buddhirīdṛśī ||12||

13nīcāśrayānmatsareṇa dveṣiṇī gumināmapi
|
tenāsi bahuśo rūkṣaṃ śrāvitaḥ kurusaṃsadi ||13||

14jānāmi samare vīryaṃ śatrubhirduḥsahaṃ bhuvi
|
brahmaṇyatāṃ ca śauryaṃ ca dāne ca paramāṃ sthitim ||14||

15na tvayā sadṛśaḥ kaścitpuruṣeṣvamaropama
|
kulabhedabhayāccāhaṃ sadā paruṣamuktavān ||15||

16iṣvastre cāstrasaṃdhāne lāghave'strabale tathā
|
sadṛśaḥ phalgunenāsi kṛṣṇena ca mahātmanā ||16||

17karṇa kāśipuraṃ gatvā tvayaikena dhanuṣmatā
|
kanyārthe kururājasya rājāno mṛditā yudhi ||17||

18tathā ca balavān rājā jarāsandho durāsadaḥ
|
samare samaraślāghinna tvayā sadṛśo'bhavat||18||

19brahmaṇyaḥ sattvayodhī ca tejasā ca balena ca
|
devagarbhasamaḥ saṅkhye manuṣyairadhiko yudhi ||19||

20vyapanīto'dya manyurme yastvāṃ prati purā kṛtaḥ
|
daivaṃ puruṣakāreṇa na śakyamativartitum ||20||

21sodaryāḥ pāṇḍavā vīrā bhrātaraste'risūdana
|
saṃgaccha tairmahābāho mama cedicchasi priyam ||21||

22mayā bhavatu nirvṛttaṃ vairamādityanandana
|
pṛthivyāṃ sarvarājāno bhavantvadya nirāmayāḥ ||22||

23karṇa uvāca|jānāmyeva mahābāho sarvametanna saṃśayaḥ
|
yathā vadasi me bhīṣma kaunteyo'haṃ na sūtajaḥ ||23||

24avakīrṇastvahaṃ kuntyā sūtena ca vivardhitaḥ
|
bhuktvā duryodhanaiśvaryaṃ na mithyākartumutsahe ||24||

25vasudevasuto yadvat pāṇḍavāya dṛḍhavrataḥ
|
vasu caiva śarīraṃ ca putradāraṃ tathā yaśaḥ ||25||

26sarvaṃ duryodhanasyārthe tyaktaṃ me bhūridakṣiṇa
|
mā caitadvyādhimaraṇaṃ kṣatraṃ syāditi kaurava ||26||

27kopitāḥ pāṇḍavā nityaṃ samāśritya suyodhanam
|
avaśyabhāvī hyartho'yaṃ yo na śakyo nivartitum ||27||

28daivaṃ puruṣakāreṇa ko nivartitumutsahet
|
pṛthivīkṣayasaṃśīni nimittāni pitāmaha ||28||

29bhavadbhirupalabdhāni kathitāni ca saṃsadi
|
pāṇḍavā vāsudevaśca viditā mama sarvaśaḥ||29||

30ajeyāḥ puruṣairanyairiti taṃścotsahāmahe
|
vijayiṣye raṇe pāṇḍūniti me niścitaḥ manaḥ ||30||

31na ca śakyamavasraṣṭuṃ vairametat sudāruṇam
|
dhanañjayena yotsye'haṃ svadharmaprītamānasaḥ ||31||

32anujānīṣva māṃ tāta yuddhāya kṛtaniścayam
|
anujñātastvayā vīra yuddhyeyamiti me matiḥ ||32||

33duruktaṃ vipratīpaṃ vā rabhasāccāpalāt tathā
|
yanmayeha kṛtaṃ kiñcittanme tvaṃ kṣantumarhasi ||33||

34bhīṣma uvāca|na cecchakyamavasraṣṭuṃ vairametat sudāruṇam
|
anujānāmi karṇa tvāṃ yuddhyasva svargakāmyayā ||34||

35nirmanyurgatasaṃrambhaḥ kṛtakarmā raṇe sma ha
|
yathāśakti yathotsāhaṃ satāṃ vṛtteṣu vṛttavān ||35||

36ahaṃ tvāmanujānāmi yadicchasi tadāpnuhi
|
kṣatradharmajitā~llokānavāpsyasi dhanañjayāt ||36||

37yudhyasva nirahaṅkāro balavīryavyapāśrayaḥ
|
dharmyāddhi yuddhācchreyo'nyat kṣatriyasya na vidyate ||37||

38praśame hi kṛto yatnaḥ sumahān suciraṃ mayā
|
na caiva śakitaḥ kartuṃ karṇa satyaṃ bravīmi te ||38||

39sañjaya uvāca|
ityuktavati gāṅgeye abhivādyopamantrya ca
|
rādheyo rathamāruhya prāyāt tava sutaṃ prati ||39||
