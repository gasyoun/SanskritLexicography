Mahābhārata 13.37 (Anuśāsanaparvan)

1yudhiṣṭhira uvāca|
apūrvaśca bhavetpātramathavāpi ciroṣitaḥ
|
dūrādabhyāgataṃ cāpi kiṃ pātraṃ syātpitāmaha ||1||

2bhīṣma uvāca|
kriyā bhavati keṣāṃcidupāṃśu vratamuttamam
|
yo yo yāceta yatkiñcitsarvaṃ dadyāma ityapi ||2||

3apīḍayan bhṛtyavargamityevamanuśuśruma
|
pīḍayan bhṛtyavargaṃ hi ātmānamapakarṣati ||3||

4apūrvaṃ bhāvayetpātraṃ yaccāpi syācciroṣitam
|
dūrādabhyāgataṃ cāpi tatpātraṃ ca vidurbudhāḥ ||4||

5yudhiṣṭhira uvāca|
apīḍayā ca bhūtānāṃ dharmasyāhiṃsayā tathā
|
pātraṃ vidyāttu tattvena yasmai dattaṃ na santapet ||5||

6bhīṣma uvāca|
ṛtvik purohitācāryāḥ śiṣyasaṃbandhibāndhavāḥ
|
sarve pūjyāśca mānyāśca śrutavanto'nasūyakāḥ ||6||

7ato'nyathā vartamānāḥ sarve nārhanti satkriyām
|
tasmānnityaṃ parīkṣeta puruṣānpraṇidhāya vai ||7||

8akrodhaḥ satyavacanamahiṃsā dama ārjavam
|
adroho'nabhimānaśca hrīstitikṣā damaḥ śamaḥ ||8||

9yasminnetāni dṛśyante na cākāryāṇi bhārata
|
svabhāvato niviṣṭāni tatpātraṃ mānamarhati ||9||

10tathā ciroṣitaṃ cāpi saṃpratyāgatameva ca
|
apūrvaṃ caiva pūrvaṃ ca tatpātraṃ mānamarhati ||10||

11aprāmāṇyaṃ ca vedānāṃ śāstrāṇāṃ cābhilaṅghanam
|
avyavasthā ca sarvatra etannāśanamātmanaḥ ||11||

12bhavetpaṇḍitamānī yo brāhmaṇo vedanindakaḥ
|
ānvīkṣikīṃ tarkavidyāmanurakto nirarthikām ||12||

13hetuvādānbruvansatsu vijetā'hetuvādikaḥ
|
ākroṣṭā cātivaktā ca brāhmaṇānāṃ sadaiva hi ||13||

14sarvābhiśaṅkī mūḍhaśca bālaḥ kaṭukavāgapi
|
boddhavyastādṛśastāta naraṃ śvānaṃ hi taṃ viduḥ ||14||

15yathā śvā bhaṣituṃ caiva hantuṃ caitrāvasajjate
|
evaṃ saṃbhāṣaṇārthāya sarvaśāstravadhāya ca ||15||

16lokayātrā ca draṣṭavyā dharmaścātmahitāni ca
|
evaṃ naro vartamānaḥ śāśvatīrvardhate samāḥ ||16||

17ṛṇamunmucya devānāmṛṣīṇāṃ ca tathaiva ca
|
pitṝṇāmatha viprāṇāmatithīnāṃ ca pañcamam ||17||

18paryāyeṇa viśuddhena suvinītena karmaṇā
|
evaṃ gṛhasthaḥ karmāṇi kurvandharmānna hīyate ||18||
