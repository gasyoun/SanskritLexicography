Mahābhārata 6.55 (Bhīṣmaparvan)

1sañjaya uvāca|gatapūrvāhṇabhūyiṣṭhe tasminnahani bhārata
|
rathanāgāśvapattīnāṃ sādināṃ ca mahākṣaye ||1||

2droṇaputreṇa śalyena kṛpeṇa ca mahātmanā
|
samasajjata pāñcālyastribhiretairmahārathaiḥ ||2||

3sa lokaviditānaśvānnijaghāna mahābalaḥ
|
drauṇeḥ pāñcāladāyādaḥ śitairdaśabhirāśugaiḥ ||3||

4tataḥ śalyarathaṃ tūrṇamāsthāya hatavāhanaḥ
|
drauṇiḥ pāñcāladāyādamabhyavarṣadatheṣubhiḥ ||4||

5dhṛṣṭadyumnaṃ tu saṃyuktaṃ drauṇinā vīkṣya bhārata
|
saubhadro'bhyapatattūrṇaṃ vikiranniṃśitān śarān ||5||

6sa śalyaṃ pañcaviṃśatyā kṛpaṃ ca navabhiḥ śaraiḥ
|
aśvatthāmānamaṣṭābhirvivyādha puruṣarṣabhaḥ ||6||

7ārjuniṃ tu tatastūrṇaṃ drauṇirvivyādhaṃ patriṇā
|
śalyo'tha daśabhiścaiva kṛpaśca niśitaistribhiḥ ||7||

8lakṣmaṇastava pautrastu saubhadraṃ samavasthitam
|
abhyavartata saṃhṛṣṭastato yuddhamavartata ||8||

9dauryodhaniḥ susaṃkruddhaḥ saubhadraṃ paravīrahā
|
vivyādha samare rājaṃstada‌dbhutamivābhavat ||9||

10abhimanyuḥ susaṃkruddho bhrātaraṃ bharatarṣabha
|
śaraiḥ pañcāśatai rājan kṣiprahasto'bhyavidhyata ||10||

11lakṣmaṇo'pi punastasya dhanuściccheda patriṇā
|
muṣṭideśe mahārāja tataste cukruśurjanāḥ ||11||

12tadvihāya dhanuśchinnaṃ saubhadraḥ paravīrahā
|
anyadāda‌ttavāṃścitraṃ kārmukaṃ vegavattaram ||12||

13tau tatra samare yuktau kṛtapratikṛtaiṣiṇau
|
anyonyaṃ viśikhaistīkṣṇairjaghnatuḥ puruṣarṣabhau ||13||

14tato duryodhano rājā dṛṣṭvā putraṃ mahāratham
|
pīḍitaṃ tava pautreṇa prāyāt tatra prajeśvaraḥ ||14||

15sannivṛtte tava sute sarva eva janādhipāḥ
|
ārjuniṃ rathavaṃśena samantāt paryavārayan ||15||

16sa taiḥ parivṛtaḥ śūraiḥ śūro yudhi sudurjayaiḥ
|
na sma pravyathate rājan kṛṣṇatulyaprarākramaḥ ||16||

17saumadramatha saṃsaktaṃ dṛṣṭvā tatra dhanañjayaḥ
|
abhidudrāva vegena trātukāmaḥ svamātmajam ||17||

18tataḥ sarathanāgāśvā bhīṣmadroṇapurogamāḥ
|
abhyavartanta rājānaḥ sahitāḥ savyasācinam ||18||

19udbhūtaṃ sahasā bhaumaṃ nāgāśvarathapattibhiḥ
|
divākararathaṃ prāpya rajastīvramadṛśyata ||19||

20tāni nāgasahasrāṇi bhūmipālaśatāni ca
|
tasya bāṇapathaṃ prāpya nābhyavartanta sarvaśaḥ ||20||

21praṇeduḥ sarvabhūtāni babhūvustimirā diśaḥ
|
kurūṇāṃ cānayastīvraḥ samadṛśyata dāruṇaḥ ||21||

22nāpyantarikṣaṃ na diśo na bhūmirna ca bhāskaraḥ
|
prajajñe bharataśreṣṭha śastrasaṅghaiḥ kirīṭinaḥ ||22||

23sāditā rathanāgāśca hatāśvā rathino raṇe
|
vipradrutarathāḥ keciddṛśyante rathayūthapāḥ ||23||

24virathā rathinaścānye dhāvamānāḥ samantataḥ
|
tatra tatraiva dṛśyante sāyudhāḥ saṅgadairbhujaiḥ ||24||

25hayārohā hayāṃstyaktvā gajārohāśca dantinaḥ
|
arjunasya bhayādrājan samantādvipradudruvuḥ ||25||

26rathebhyaśca gajebhyaśca hayebhyaśca narādhipāḥ
|
patitāḥ pātyamānāśca dṛśyante'rjunasāyakaiḥ ||26||

27sagadānudyatān bāhūn sakhaḍgāṃśca viśāṃpate
|
saprāsāṃśca satūṇīrān saśarān saśarāsanān ||27||

28sāṅkuśān sapatākāṃśca tatra tatrārjuno nṛṇām
|
nicakarta śarairugrai raudraṃ vapuradhārayat ||28||

29parighāṇāṃ pradīptānāṃ mudgarāṇāṃ ca māriṣa
|
prāsānāṃ bhindipālānāṃ nistriṃśānāṃ ca saṃyuge ||29||

30paraśvadhānāṃ tīkṣṇānāṃ tomarāṇāṃ ca bhārata
|
varmaṇāṃ cāpaviddhānāṃ kāñcanānāṃ ca bhūmipa ||30||

31dhvajānāṃ carmaṇāṃ caiva vyajanānāṃ ca sarvaśaḥ
|
chatrāṇāṃ hemadaṇḍānāṃ tomarāṇāṃ ca bhārata ||31||

32pratodānāṃ ca yoktrāṇāṃ kaśānāṃ caiva māriṣa
|
rāśayaḥ smātra dṛśyante vinikīrṇā raṇakṣitau ||32||

33nāsīttatra pumān kaścittava sainyasya bhārata
|
yo'rjunaṃ samare śūraṃ pratyudyāyāt kathañcana ||33||

34yo yo hi samare pārthaṃ pratyudyāti viśāṃpate
|
sa saṃkhye viśikhaistīkṣṇaiḥ paralokāya nīyate ||34||

35teṣu vidravamāṇeṣu tava yodheṣu sarvaśaḥ
|
arjuno vāsudevaśca dadhmaturvārijottamau ||35||

36tat prabhagnaṃ balaṃ dṛṣṭvā pitā devavratastava
|
abravīt samare śūraṃ bhāradvājaṃ smayanniva ||36||

37eṣa pāṇḍusuto vīra kṛṣṇena sahito balī
|
tathā karoti sainyāni yathā kuryāddhanañjayaḥ ||37||

38na hyeṣa samare śakyo vijetuṃ hi kathañcana
|
yathā'sya dṛśyate rūpaṃ kālāntakayamopamam ||38||

39na nivartayituṃ cāpi śakyeyaṃ mahatī camūḥ
|
anyonyaprekṣayā paśya dravatīyaṃ varūthinī ||39||

40eṣa cāstaṃ giriśreṣṭhaṃ bhānumān pratipadyate
|
cakṣūṃṣi sarvalokasya saṃharanniva sarvathā ||40||

41tatrāvahāraṃ saṃprāptaṃ manye'haṃ puruṣarṣabha
|
śrāntā bhītāśca no yodhā na yotsyanti kathañcana ||41||

42evamuktvā tato bhīṣmo droṇamācāryasattamam
|
avahāramatho cakre tāvakānāṃ mahārathaḥ ||42||

43tato'vahāraḥ sainyānāṃ tava teṣāṃ ca bhārata
|
astaṃ gacchati sūrye'bhūt sandhyākāle ca vartati ||43||
