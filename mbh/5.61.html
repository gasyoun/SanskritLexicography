Mahābhārata 5.61 (Udyogaparvan)

1vaiśampāyana uvāca|
pituretadvacaḥ śrutvā dhārtarāṣṭro'tyamarṣaṇaḥ
|
ādhāya vipulaṃ krodhaṃ punarevedamabravīt ||1||

2aśakyā devasacivāḥ pārthāḥ syuriti yadbhavān
|
manyate tadbhayaṃ vyetu bhavato rājasattama ||2||

3akāmadveṣasaṃyogāllobhāddrohācca bhārata
|
upekṣayā ca bhāvānāṃ devā devatvamāpnuvan ||3||

4iti dvaipāyano vyāso nāradaśca mahātapāḥ
|
jāmadagnyaśca rāmo naḥ kathāmakathayat purā ||4||

5naiva mānuṣavaddevāḥ pravartante kadācana
|
kāmāt krodhāttathā lobhāddveṣācca bharatarṣabha ||5||

6yadā hyagniśca vāyuśca dharma indro'śvināvapi
|
kāmayogāt pravarteranna pārthā duḥkhamāpnuyuḥ ||6||

7tasmānna bhavatā cintā kāryaiṣā syāt kathañcana
|
daiveṣvapekṣakā hyete śaśvadbhāveṣu bhārata ||7||

8atha cetkāmasaṃyogāddveṣo lobhaśca lakṣyate
|
deveṣu daivaprāmāṇyānnaiṣāṃ tadvikramiṣyati ||8||

9mayā'bhimantritaḥ śaśvajjātavedāḥ praśāmyati
|
didhakṣuḥ sakalāঁllokān parikṣipya samantataḥ ||9||

10yadvā paramakaṃ tejo yena yuktā divaukasaḥ
|
mamāpyanupamaṃ bhūyo devebhyo viddhi bhārata ||10||

11vidīryamāṇāṃ vasudhāṃ girīṇāṃ śikharāṇi ca
|
lokasya paśyato rājan sthāpayāmyabhimantraṇāt ||11||

12cetanācetanasyāsya jaṅgamasthāvarasya ca
|
vināśāya samutpannamahaṃ ghoraṃ mahāsvanam ||12||

13aśmavarṣaṃ ca vāyuṃ ca śamayāmīha nityaśaḥ
|
jagataḥ paśyato'bhīkṣṇaṃ bhūtānāmanukampayā ||13||

14stambhitāsvapsu gacchanti mayā rathapadātayaḥ
|
devāsurāṇāṃ bhāvānāmahamekaḥ pravartitā ||14||

15akṣauhiṇībhiryān deśān yāmi kāryeṇa kenacit
|
tatrāśvā me pravartante yatra yatrābhikāmaye ||15||

16bhayānakāni viṣaye vyālādīni na santi me
|
mantraguptāni bhūtāni na hiṃsanti bhayaṅkarāḥ ||16||

17nikāmavarṣī parjanyo rājan viṣayavāsinām
|
dharmiṣṭhāśca prajāḥ sarvā ītayaśca na santi me ||17||

18aśvināvatha vāyvagnī marudbhiḥ saha vṛtrahā
|
dharmaścaiva mayā dviṣṭānnotsahante'bhirakṣitum ||18||

19yadi hyete samarthāḥ syurmaddviṣastrātumañjasā
|
na sma trayodaśa samāḥ pārthā duḥkhamavāpnuyuḥ ||19||

20naiva devā na gandharvā nāsurā na ca rākṣasāḥ
|
śaktāstrātuṃ mayā dviṣṭaṃ satyametadbravīmi te ||20||

21yadabhidhyāmyahaṃ śaśvacchubhaṃ vā yadi vā'śubham
|
naitadvipannapūrvaṃ me miveṣvariṣu cobhayoḥ ||21||

22bhaviṣyatīdamiti vā yadbravīmi parantapa
|
nānyathā bhūtapūrvaṃ ca satyavāgiti māṃ viduḥ ||22||

23lokasākṣikametanme māhātmyaṃ dikṣu viśrutam
|
āśvāsanārthaṃ bhavataḥ proktaṃ na ślāghayā nṛpa ||23||

24na hyahaṃ ślāghano rājan bhūtapūrvaḥ kadācana
|
asadācaritaṃ hyetadyadātmānaṃ praśaṃsati ||24||

25pāṇḍavāṃścaiva matsyāṃśca pāñcālān kekayaiḥ saha
|
sātyakiṃ vāsudevaṃ ca śrotā'si vijitān mayā ||25||

26saritaḥ sāgaraṃ prāpya yathā naśyanti sarvaśaḥ
|
tathaiva te vinaṃkṣyanti māmāsādya sahānvayāḥ ||26||

27parā buddhiḥ paraṃ tejo vīryaṃ ca paramaṃ mama
|
parā vidyā paro yogo mama tebhyo viśiṣyate ||27||

28pitāmahaśca droṇaśca kṛpaḥ śalyaḥ śalastathā
|
astreṣu yat prajānanti sarvaṃ tanmayi vidyate ||28||

29ityuktvā sañjayaṃ bhūyaḥ paryapṛcchata bhārataḥ
|
jñātvā yuyutsoḥ kāryāṇi prāptakālamarindamaḥ ||29||
