Mahābhārata 8.35 (Karṇaparvan)

1duryodhana uvāca|
evaṃ sa bhagavān devaḥ sarvalokapitāmahaḥ
|
sārathyamakarottatra brahmā rudro'bhavadrathī ||1||

2rathino'bhyadhiko vīra kartavyo rathasārathiḥ
|
tasmāttvaṃ puruṣavyāghra niyaccha turagān yudhi ||2||

3yathā devagaṇaistatra vṛto yatnātpitāmahaḥ
|
tathāsmābhirbhavān yatnātkarṇādabhyadhiko vṛttaḥ ||3||

4yathā devairmahārāja īśvarādadhiko vṛtaḥ
|
tathā bhavānapi kṣipraṃ rudrasyeva pitāmahaḥ ||4||

5niyaccha turagān yuddhe rādheyasya mahādyute
|
śalya uvāca|
mayā'pyetannaraśreṣṭha bahuśo narasiṃhayoḥ ||5||

6kathyamānaṃ śrutaṃ divyamākhyānamatimānuṣam
|
yathā ca cakre sārathyaṃ bhavasya prapitāmahaḥ ||6||

7yathā'surāśca nihatā iṣuṇaikena bhārata
|
kṛṣṇasya cāpi viditaṃ sarvametatpurā hyabhūt ||7||

8yathā pitāmaho jajñe bhagavānsārathistadā
|
anāgatamatikrāntaṃ veda kṛṣṇo'pi tattvataḥ ||8||

9etadarthaṃ viditvāpi sārathyamupajagmivān
|
svayaṃbhūriva rudrasya kṛṣṇaḥ pārthasya bhārata ||9||

10yadi hanyācca kaunteyaṃ sūtaputraḥ kathañcana
|
dṛṣṭvā pārthaṃ hi nihataṃ svayaṃ yotsyati keśavaḥ||10||

11śaṅkha-cakra-gadāpāṇirdhakṣyate tava vāhinīm
|
na cāpi tasya kruddhasya vārṣṇeyasya mahātmanaḥ ||11||

12sthāsyate pratyanīkeṣu kaścidatra nṛpastava
|
sañjaya uvāca|
taṃ tathā bhāṣamāṇaṃ tu madrarājamarindamaḥ ||12||

13pratyuvāca mahābāhuradīnātmā sutastava
|
mā'vamaṃsthā mahābāho karṇaṃ vaikartanaṃ raṇe ||13||

14sarvaśastrabhṛtāṃ śreṣṭhaṃ sarvaśāstrārthapāragam
|
yasya jyātalanirghoṣaṃ śrutvā bhayakaraṃ mahat ||14||

15pāṇḍaveyāni sainyāni vidravanti diśo daśa
|
pratyakṣaṃ te mahābāho yathā rātrau ghaṭotkacaḥ ||15||

16māyāśatāni kurvāṇo hato māyāpuraskṛtaḥ
|
na cātiṣṭhata bībhatsuḥ pratyanīke kathañcana ||16||

17etāṃśca divasānsarvān bhayena mahatā vṛtaḥ
|
bhīmasenaśca balavān dhanuṣkoṭyā'bhicoditaḥ ||17||

18uktaśca saṃjñayā rājan mūḍha audariko yathā
|
mādrīputrau tathā śūrau yena jitvā mahāraṇe ||18||

19kamapyarthaṃ puraskṛtya na hatau yudhi māriṣa
|
yena vṛṣṇipravīrastu sātyakiḥ sātvatāṃ varaḥ ||19||

20nirjitya samare śūro virathaśca balātkṛtaḥ
|
sṛñjayāścetare sarve dhṛṣṭadyumnapurogamāḥ ||20||

21asakṛnnirjitāḥ saṃkhye smayamānena saṃyuge
|
taṃ kathaṃ pāṇḍavā yuddhe vijeṣyanti mahāratham ||21||

22yo hanyātsamare kruddho vajrahastaṃ purandaram
|
tvaṃ ca sarvāstravidvīraḥ sarvavidyāstrapāragaḥ ||22||

23bāhuvīryeṇa te tulyaḥ pṛthivyāṃ nāsti kaścana
|
tvaṃ śalyabhūtaḥ śatrūṇāmaviṣahyaḥ parākrame ||23||

24tatastvamucyase rājañśalya ityarisūdana
|
tava bāhubalaṃ prāpya na śekuḥ sarvasāttvatāḥ ||24||

25tava bāhubalādrājan kiṃ tu kṛṣṇo balādhikaḥ
|
yathā hi kṛṣṇena balaṃ dhāryaṃ vai phālgune hate ||25||

26tathā karṇātyayībhāve tvayā dhāryaṃ mahadbalam
|
kimarthaṃ samare sainyaṃ vāsudevo nyavārayat ||26||

27kimarthaṃ ca bhavān sainyaṃ na haniṣyati māriṣa
|
tvatkṛte padavīṃ gantumiccheyaṃ yudhi māriṣa
|
sodarāṇāṃ ca vīrāṇāṃ sarveṣāṃ ca mahīkṣitām ||27||

28śalya uvāca|
yanmāṃ bravīṣi gāndhāre agre sainyasya mānada
|
viśiṣṭaṃ devakīputrāt prītimānasmyahaṃ tvayi ||28||

29eṣa sārathyamātiṣṭhe rādheyasya yaśasvinaḥ
|
yudhyataḥ pāṇḍavāgryeṇa yathā tvaṃ vīra manyase ||29||

30samayaśca hi me vīra kaścidvaikartanaṃ prati
|
utsṛjeyaṃ yathāśraddhamahaṃ vāco'sya sannidhau ||30||

31sañjaya uvāca|
tatheti rājanputraste saha karṇena māriṣa
|
abravīnmadrarājānaṃ sarvakṣatrasya sannidhau ||31||

32sārathyasyābhyupagamācchalyenāśvāsitastadā
|
duryodhanastadā hṛṣṭaḥ karṇaṃ tamabhiṣasvaje ||32||

33abravīcca punaḥ karṇaṃ stūyamānaḥ sutastava
|
jahi pārthānraṇe sarvānmahendro dānavāniva ||33||

34sa śalyenābhyupagate hayānāṃ sanniyacchane
|
karṇo hṛṣṭamanā bhūyo duryodhanamabhāṣata ||34||

35nātihṛṣṭamanā hyeṣa madrarājo'bhibhāṣate
|
rājanmadhurayā vācā punarenaṃ bravīhi vai ||35||

36tato rājā mahāprājñaḥ sarvāstrakuśalo balī
|
duryodhano'bravīcchalyaṃ madrarājaṃ mahīpatim ||36||

37pūrayanniva ghoṣeṇa meghagambhīrayā girā
|
śalya karṇo'rjunenādya yoddhavyamiti manyate ||37||

38tasya tvaṃ puruṣavyāghra niyaccha turagān yudhi
|
karṇo hatvetarānsarvān phālgunaṃ hantumicchati ||38||

39tasyābhīṣugrahe rājan prayāce tvāṃ punaḥ punaḥ
|
pārthasya sacivaḥ kṛṣṇo yathābhīṣugraho varaḥ
|
tathā tvamapi rādheyaṃ sarvataḥ paripālaya ||39||

40sañjaya uvāca|
tataḥ śalyaḥ pariṣvajya sutaṃ te vākyamabravīt
|
duryodhanamamitraghnaṃ prīto madrādhipastadā ||40||

41śalya uvāca|
evaṃ cenmanyase rājan gāndhāre priyadarśana
|
tasmātte yatpriyaṃ kiñcittatsarvaṃ karavāṇyaham ||41||

42yatrāsmi bharataśreṣṭha yogyaḥ karmaṇi karhicit
|
tatra sarvātmanā yukto vakṣye kāryaṃ parantapa ||42||

43yattu karṇamahaṃ brūyāṃ hitakāmaḥ priyāpriye
|
mama tat kṣamatāṃ sarvaṃ bhavānkarṇaśca sarvaśaḥ ||43||

44karṇa uvāca|
īśānasya yathā brahmā yathā pārthasya keśavaḥ
|
tathā nityaṃ hite yukto madrarāja bhavasva naḥ ||44||

45śalya uvāca|
ātmanindātmapūjā ca paranindā parastavaḥ
|
anācaritamāryāṇāṃ vṛttametaccaturvidham ||45||

46yattu vidvanpravakṣyāmi pratyayārthamahaṃ tava
|
ātmanastava saṃyuktaṃ tannibodha yathātatham ||46||

47ahaṃ śakrasya sārathye yogyo mātalivatprabho
|
apramādātprayogācca jñānavidyācikitsanaiḥ ||47||

48tataḥ pārthena saṃgrāme yudhyamānasya te'nagha
|
vāhayiṣyāmi turagān ‌vijvaro bhava sūtaja ||48||
