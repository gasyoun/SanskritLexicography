Mahābhārata 6.91 (Bhīṣmaparvan)

1dhṛtarāṣṭra uvāca|
irāvantaṃ tu nihataṃ dṛṣṭvā pārthā mahārathāḥ
|
saṃgrāme kimakurvanta tanmamācakṣva sañjaya ||1||

2sañjaya uvāca|irāvantaṃ tu nihataṃ saṃgrāme vīkṣya rākṣasaḥ
|
vyanadat sumahānādaṃ bhaimasenirghaṭotkacaḥ ||2||

3nadatastasya śabdena pṛthivī sāgarāmbarā
|
saparvatavanā rājaṃścacāla subhṛśaṃ tadā ||3||

4antarikṣaṃ diśaścaiva sarvāśca pradiśastathā
|
taṃ śrutvā sumahānādaṃ tava sainyasya bhārata ||4||

5ūrustaṃbhaḥ samabhavadvepathuḥ kheda eva ca
|
sarva eva mahārāja tāvakā dīnacetasaḥ ||5||

6sarvataḥ samaceṣṭanta siṃhabhītā gajā iva
|
narditvā sumahānādaṃ nirghātamiva rākṣasaḥ ||6||

7jvalitaṃ śūlamudyamya rūpaṃ kṛtvā vibhīṣaṇam
|
nānārūpapraharaṇairvṛto rākṣasapuṅgavaiḥ ||7||

8ājaghāna susaṃkruddhaḥ kālāntakayamopamaḥ
|
tamāpatantaṃ saṃprekṣya saṅkruddhaṃ bhīmadarśanam ||8||

9svabalaṃ ca bhayāttasya prāyaśo vimukhīkṛtam
|
tato duryodhano rājā ghaṭotkacamupādravat ||9||

10pragṛhya vipulaṃ cāpaṃ siṃhavadvinadanmuhuḥ
|
pṛṣṭhato'nuyayau cainaṃ sravadbhiḥ parvatopamaiḥ ||10||

11kuñjarairdaśasāhasrairvaṅgānāmadhipaḥ svayam
|
tamāpatantaṃ saṃprekṣya gajānīkena saṃvṛtam ||11||

12putraṃ tava mahārāja cukopa sa niśācaraḥ
|
tataḥ pravavṛte yuddhaṃ tumulaṃ lomaharṣaṇam ||12||

13rākṣasānāṃ ca rājendra duryodhanabalasya ca
|
gajānīkaṃ ca saṃprekṣya meghavṛndamivoditam ||13||

14abhyadhāvanta saṃkruddhā rākṣasāḥ śastrapāṇayaḥ
|
nadanto vividhānnādānmeghā iva savidyutaḥ ||14||

15śaraśaktyṛṣṭinārācairnighnanto gajayodhinaḥ
|
bhindipālaistathā śūlairmudgaraiḥ saparaśvadhaiḥ ||15||

16parvatāgraiśca vṛkṣaiśca nijaghnuste mahāgajān
|
bhinnakuṃbhānvirudhirānbhinnagātrāṃśca vāraṇān ||16||

17apaśyāma mahārāja vadhyamānānniśācaraiḥ
|
teṣu prakṣīyamāṇeṣu bhagneṣu gajayodhiṣu ||17||

18duryodhano mahārāja rākṣasān samupādravat
|
amarṣavaśamāpannastyaktvā jīvitamātmanaḥ||18||

19mumoca niśitān bāṇān rākṣaseṣu paraṃtapa
|
jaghāna ca maheṣvāsaḥ pradhānāṃstatra rākṣasān ||19||

20saṃkuddho bharataśreṣṭha putro duryodhanastava
|
vegavantaṃ mahāraudraṃ vidyujjihvaṃ pramāthinam ||20||

21śaraiścaturbhiścaturo nijaghāna mahābalaḥ
|
tataḥ punarameyātmā śaravarṣaṃ durāsadam ||21||

22mumoca bharataśreṣṭho niśācarabalaṃ prati
|
tattu dṛṣṭvā mahatkarma putrasya tava māriṣa ||22||

23krodhenābhiprajajvāla bhaimasenirmahābalaḥ
|
sa visphārya mahaccāpamindrāśanisamaprabham ||23||

24abhidudrāva vegena duryodhanamarindamam
|
tamāpatantamudvīkṣya kālasṛṣṭamivāntakam ||24||

25na vivyathe mahārāja putro duryodhanastava
|
athainamabravīt kruddhaḥ krūraḥ saṃraktalocanaḥ ||25||

26adyānṛṇyaṃ gamiṣyāmi pitṝṇāṃ mātureva ca
|
ye tvayā sunṛśaṃsena dīrghakālaṃ pravāsitāḥ ||26||

27yacca te pāṇḍavā rājaṃśchalahūte parājitāḥ
|
yaccaiva draupadī kṛṣṇā ekavastrā rajasvalā ||27||

28sabhāmānīya durbuddhe bahudhā kleśitā tvayā
|
tava ca priyakāmena āśramasthā durātmanā ||28||

29saindhavena parāmṛṣṭā paribhūya pitṝn mama
|
eteṣāmapamānānāmanyeṣāṃ ca kulādhama ||29||

30antamadya gamiṣyāmi yadi notsṛjase raṇam
|
evamuktvā tu haiḍimbo mahadvisphārya kārmukam.||30||

31saṃdaśya daśanairoṣṭhaṃ sṛkkiṇī parisaṃlihan
|
śaravarṣeṇa mahatā duryodhanamavākirat
|
parvataṃ vāridhārābhiḥ prāvṛṣīva balāhakaḥ ||31||
