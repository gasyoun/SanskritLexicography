Mahābhārata 12.98 (Śāntiparvan)

1yudhiṣṭhira uvāca|
ke lokā yudhyamānānāṃ śūrāṇāmanivartinām
|
bhavanti nidhanaṃ prāpya tanme brūhi pitāmaha||1||

2bhīṣma uvāca|
atrāpyudāharantīmamitihāsaṃ purātanam
|
ambarīṣasya saṃvādamindrasya ca yudhiṣṭhira||2||

3ambarīṣo hi nābhāgiḥ svargaṃ gatvā sudurlabham
|
dadarśa suralokasthaṃ śakreṇa sacivaṃ saha||3||

4sarvatejomayaṃ divyaṃ vimānavaramāsthitam
|
uparyupari gacchantaṃ svaṃ vai senāpatiṃ prabhum||4||

5sa dṛṣṭvopari gacchantaṃ senāpatimudāradhīḥ
|
ṛddhiṃ dṛṣṭvā sudevasya vismitaḥ prāha vāsavam||5||

6ambarīṣa uvāca| sāgarāntāṃ mahīṃ kṛtsnāmanuśāsya yathāvidhi
|
cāturvarṇye yathāśāstraṃ pravṛtto dharmakāmyayā||6||

7brahmacaryeṇa ghoreṇa gurvācāreṇa sevayā
|
vedānadhītya dharmeṇa rājaśāstraṃ ca kevalam||7||

8atithīnannapānena pitṝṃśca svadhayā tathā
|
ṛṣīnsvādhyāyadīkṣābhirdevānyajñairanuttamaiḥ||8||

9kṣatradharme sthito bhūtvā yathāśāstraṃ yathāvidhi
|
udīkṣamāṇaḥ pṛtanāṃ jayāmi yudhi vāsava||9||

10devarāja sudevo'yaṃ mama senāpatiḥ purā
|
āsīdyodhaḥ praśāntātmā so'yaṃ kasmādatīva mām||10||

11anena kratubhirmukhyairneṣṭaṃ nāpi dvijātayaḥ
|
tarpitā vidhivacchakra so'yaṃ kasmādatīva mām||11||

12indra uvāca| etasya vitatastāta sudevasya babhūva ha
|
saṃgrāmayajñaḥ sumahānyaścānyo yuddhyate naraḥ||12||

13sannaddho dīkṣitaḥ sarvo yodhaḥ prāpya camūmukham
|
yuddhayajñādhikārastho bhavatīti viniścayaḥ||13||

14ambarīṣa uvāca| kāni yajñe havīṃṣyasmin kimājyaṃ kā ca dakṣiṇā
|
ṛtvijaścātra ke proktāstanme brūhi śatakrato||14||

15indra uvāca| ṛtvijaḥ kuñjarāstatra vājino'dhvaryavastathā
|
havīṃṣi paramāṃsāni rudhiraṃ tvājyamucyate||15||

16śṛgāla-gṛdhra-kākolā: sadasyāstatra patriṇaḥ
|
ājyaśeṣaṃ pibantyete haviḥ prāśnanti cādhvare||16||

17prāsa tomarasaṃghātāḥ khaḍga-śakti-paraśvadhāḥ
|
jvalanto niśitāḥ pītāḥ srucastasyātha satriṇaḥ||17||

18cāpavegāyatastīkṣṇaḥ parakāyāvabhedanaḥ
|
ṛjuḥ suniśitaḥ pītaḥ sāyakaśca sruvo mahān||18||

19dvīpīcarmāvanaddhaśca nāgadantakṛtatsaruḥ
|
hastihastaharaḥ khaḍgaḥ sphigbhavettasya saṃyuge||19||

20jvalitairniśitaiḥ prāsaśaktyṛṣṭisaparaśvadhaiḥ
|
śaikyāyasamayaistīkṣṇairabhighāto bhavedvasu||20||

21saṃkhyāsamayavistīrṇamabhijātodbhavaṃ bahu
|
āvegādyacca rudhiraṃ saṃgrāme sravate bhuvi||21||

22sā'sya pūrṇāhutirhome samṛddhā sarvakāmadhuk
|
chindhi bhindhīti yaḥ śabdaḥ śrūyate vāhinīmukhe||22||

23sāmāni sāmagāstasya gāyanti yamasādane
|
havirdhānaṃ tu tasyāhuḥ pareṣāṃ vāhinīmukham||23||

24kuñjarāṇāṃ hayānāṃ ca varmiṇāṃ ca samuccayaḥ
|
agniḥ śyenacito nāma sa ca yajñe vidhīyate||24||

25uttiṣṭhate kabandho'tra sahasre nihate tu yaḥ
|
sa yūpastasya śūrasya khādiro'ṣṭāstrirucyate||25||

26iḍopahūtāḥ krośanti kuñjarāstvaṃkuśeritāḥ
|
vyāghuṣṭatalanādeva vaṣaṭkāreṇa pārthiva||26||

27udgātā tatra saṃgrāme trisāmā dundubhirnṛpa
|
brahmasve hriyamāṇe tu tyaktvā yuddhe priyāṃ tanum||27||

28ātmānaṃ yūpamutsṛjya sa yajño'nantadakṣiṇaḥ
|
bharturarthe ca yaḥ śūro vikramedvāhinīmukhe||28||

29na bhayādvinivarteta tasya lokā yathā mama
|
nīlacarmāvṛtaiḥ khaḍgairbāhubhiḥ parighopamaiḥ||29||

30yasya vedirupastīrṇā tasya lokā yathā mama
|
yastu nāpekṣate kaṃcitsahāyaṃ vijaye sthitaḥ||30||

31vigāhya vāhinīmadhyaṃ tasya lokā yathā mama
|
yasya śoṇitasaṃghātā bherīmaṇḍūkakacchapā||31||

32vīrāsthiśarkarā durgā māṃsaśoṇitakardamā
|
asicarmaplavā ghorā keśaśaivalaśādvalā||32||

33aśva-nāga-rathaiścaiva saṃcchinnaiḥ kṛtasaṃkramā
|
patākādhvajavānīrā hatavāraṇavāhinī||33||

34śoṇitodā susampūrṇā dustarā pāragairnaraiḥ
|
hatanāgamahānakā paralokavahāśivā||34||

35ṛṣṭi-khaḍgamahānaukā gṛdhra-kaṅkabalaplavā
|
puruṣādānucaritā bhīrūṇāṃ kaśmalāvahā||35||

36nadī yodhasya saṃgrāme tadasyāvabhṛthaṃ smṛtam
|
vediryasya tvamitrāṇāṃ śirobhyaśca prakīryate||36||

37aśvaskandhairgajaskandhaistasya lokā yathā mama
|
patnīśālā kṛtā yasya pareṣāṃ vāhinīmukham||37||

38havirdhānaṃ svavāhinyāstadasyāhurmanīṣiṇaḥ
|
sadasyā dakṣiṇā yodhā āgnīdhraścottarāṃ diśam||38||

39śatrusenākalatrasya sarvalokānadūrataḥ
|
yadā tūbhayato vyūhe bhavatyākāśamagrataḥ||39||

40sā'sya vedistadā yajñairnityaṃ vedāstrayo'gnayaḥ
|
yastu yodhaḥ parāvṛttaḥ saṃtrasto hanyate paraiḥ||40||

41apratiṣṭhaḥ sa narakaṃ yāti nāstyatra saṃśayaḥ
|
yasya śoṇitavegena vediḥ syātsampariplutā||41||

42keśamāṃsāsthisampūrṇā sa gacchetparamāṃ gatim
|
yastu senāpatiṃ hatvā tadyānamadhirohati||42||

43sa viṣṇuvikramakrāmī bṛhaspatisamaḥ prabhuḥ
|
nāyakaṃ tatkumāraṃ vā yo vā syāttatra pūjitaḥ||43||

44jīvagrāhaṃ pragṛhṇāti tasya lokā yathā mama
|
āhave tu hataṃ śūraṃ na śoceta kathaṃcana||44||

45aśocyo hi hataḥ śūraḥ svargaloke mahīyate
|
na hyannaṃ nodakaṃ tasya na snānaṃ nāpyaśaucakam||45||

46hatasya kartumicchanti tasya lokānśṛṇuṣva me
|
varāpsaraḥsahasrāṇi śūramāyodhane hatam||46||

47tvaramāṇā'bhidhāvanti mama bhartā bhavediti
|
etattapaśca puṇyaṃ ca dharmaścaiva sanātanaḥ||47||

48catvāraścāśramāstasya yo yuddhamanupālayet
|
vṛddhabālau na hantavyau na ca strī naiva pṛṣṭhataḥ||48||

49tṛṇapūrṇamukhaścaiva tavāsmīti ca yo vadet
|
jambhaṃ vṛtraṃ balaṃ pākaṃ śatamāyaṃ virocanam||49||

50durvāryaṃ caiva namuciṃ naikamāyaṃ ca śambaram
|
vipracittiṃ ca daiteyaṃ danoḥ putrāṃśca sarvaśaḥ
|
prahlādaṃ ca nihatyājau tato devādhipo'bhavam||50||

51bhīṣma uvāca|
ityetacchakravacanaṃ niśamya pratigṛhya ca
|
yodhānāmātmanaḥ siddhimambarīṣo'bhipannavān||51||
