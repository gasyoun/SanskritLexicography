Mahābhārata 12.164 (Śāntiparvan)

1yudhiṣṭhira uvāca|
ānṛśaṃsyaṃ vijānāmi darśanena satāṃ sadā
|
nṛśaṃsānna vijānāmi teṣāṃ karma ca bhārata ||1||

2kaṇṭakānkūpamagniṃ ca varjayanti yathā narāḥ
|
tathā nṛśaṃsakarmāṇaṃ varjayanti narā naram ||2||

3nṛśaṃso hi dahedvyaktaṃ pretya ceha ca bhārata
|
tasmāttvaṃ brūhi kauravya tasya dharmaviniścayam ||3||

4bhīṣma uvāca|
spṛhā syādgarhitā caiva vidhitsā caiva karmaṇām
|
ākroṣṭā kruśyate caiva vañcito buddhyate sa ca ||4||

5dattānukīrtirviṣamaḥ kṣudro naikṛtikaḥ śaṭhaḥ
|
asaṃvibhāgī mānī ca tathā saṅgī vikatthanaḥ ||5||

6sarvātiśaṅkī puruṣo balīśaḥ kṛpaṇo'thavā
|
vargapraśaṃsī satatamāśramadveṣasaṃkarī ||6||

7hiṃsāvihāraḥ satatamaviśeṣaguṇāguṇaḥ
|
bahvalīko manasvī ca lubdho'tyarthaṃ nṛśaṃsakṛt ||7||

8dharmaśīlaṃ guṇopetaṃ pāpamityavagacchati
|
ātmaśīlapramāṇena na viśvasiti kasyacit ||8||

9pareṣāṃ yatra doṣaḥ syāttadguhyaṃ samprakāśayet
|
samāneṣveva doṣeṣu vṛttyarthamupaghātayet ||9||

10tathopakāriṇaṃ caiva manyate vañcitaṃ param
|
dattvā'pi ca dhanaṃ kāle santapatyupakāriṇe ||10||

11bhakṣyaṃ peyamathālehyaṃ yaccānyatsādhu bhojanam
|
prekṣamāṇeṣu yo'śnīyānnṛśaṃsamiti taṃ vadet ||11||

12brāhmaṇebhyaḥ pradāyāgraṃ yaḥ suhṛdbhiḥ sahāśnute
|
sa pretya labhate svargamiha cānantyamaśnute ||12||

13eṣa te bharataśreṣṭha nṛśaṃsaḥ parikīrtitaḥ
|
sadā vivarjanīyo hi puruṣeṇa vijānatā ||13||
