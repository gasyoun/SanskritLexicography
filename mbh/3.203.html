Mahābhārata 3.203 (Āraṇyakaparvan)

1mārkaṇḍeya uvāca|
sa evamukto rājarṣiruttaṅkenāparājitaḥ
|
uttaṅkaṃ kauravaśreṣṭha kṛtāñjalirathābravīt||1||

2na te'bhigamanaṃ brahman moghametadbhaviṣyati
|
putro mamāyaṃ bhagavan kuvalāśva iti smṛtaḥ||2||

3dhṛtimān kṣiprakārī ca vīryeṇāpratimo bhuvi
|
priyaṃ ca te sarvametat kariṣyati na saṃśayaḥ||3||

4putraiḥ parivṛtaḥ sarvaiḥ śūraiḥ parighabāhubhiḥ
|
visarjayasva māṃ brahman nyastaśastro'smi sāmpratam||4||

5tathā'stviti ca tenokto muninā'mitatejasā
|
sa tamādiśya tanayamuttaṅkāya mahātmane||5||

6kriyatāmiti rājarṣirjagāma vanamuttamam
|
yudhiṣṭhira uvāca|
ka eṣa bhagavan daityo mahāvīryastapodhana||6||

7tasya putro'tha naptā vā etadicchāmi veditum
|
evaṃ mahābalo daityo na śrṛto me tapodhana||7||

8etadicchāmi bhagavan yāthātathyena veditum
|
sarvameva mahāprājña vistareṇa tapodhana||8||

9mārkaṇḍeya uvāca|
śrṛṇu rājannidaṃ sarvaṃ yathāvṛttaṃ narādhipa
|
kathyamānaṃ mahāprājña vistareṇa yathātatham||9||

10ekārṇave tadā loke naṣṭe sthāvarajaṅgame
|
pranaṣṭeṣu ca bhūteṣu sarveṣu bharatarṣabha||10||

11prabhavaṃ lokakartāraṃ viṣṇuṃ śāśvatamavyayam
|
yamāhurmunayaḥ siddhāḥ sarvalokamaheśvaram||11||

12suṣvāpa bhagavān viṣṇurapsu yogata eva saḥ
|
nāgasya bhoge mahati śeṣasyāmitatejasaḥ||12||

13lokakartā mahābhāga bhagavānacyuto hariḥ
|
nāgabhogena mahatā parirabhya mahīmimām||13||

14svapatastasya devasya padmaṃ sūryasamaprabham
|
nābhyāṃ viniḥsṛtaṃ divyaṃ tatrotpannaḥ pitāmahaḥ||14||

15sākṣāllokagururbrahmā padme sūryasamaprabhaḥ
|
caturvedaścaturmūrtistathaiva ca caturmukhaḥ||15||

16svaprabhāvāddurādharṣo mahābalaparākramaḥ
|
kasyacit tvatha kālasya dānavau vīryavattamau||16||

17madhuśca kaiṭabhaścaiva dṛṣṭavantau hariṃ prabhum
|
śayānaṃ śayane divye nāgabhoge mahādyutim||17||

18bahuyojanavistīrṇe bahuyojanamāyate
|
kirīṭakaustubhadharaṃ pītakauśeyavāsasam||18||

19dīpyamānaṃ śriyā rājaṃstejasā vapuṣā tathā
|
sahasrasūryapratimamadbhutopamadarśanam||19||

20vismayaḥ sumahānāsīnmadhukaiṭabhayostathā
|
dṛṣṭvā pitāmahaṃ cāpi padme padmanibhekṣaṇam||20||

21vitrāsayetāmatha tau brahmāṇamamitaujasam
|
vitrasyamāno bahuśo brahmā tābhyāṃ mahāyaśāḥ||21||

22akampayat padmanālaṃ tato'budhyata keśavaḥ
|
athāpaśyata govindo dānavau vīryavattarau||22||

23dṛṣṭvā tāvabravīddevaḥ svāgataṃ vāṃ mahābalau
|
dadāmi vāṃ varaṃ śreṣṭhaṃ prītirhi mama jāyate||23||

24tau prahasya hṛṣīkeśaṃ mahādarpau mahābalau
|
pratyabrūtāṃ mahārāja sahitau madhusūdanam||24||

25āvāṃ varaya deva tvaṃ varadau svaḥ surottama
|
dātārau svo varaṃ tubhyaṃ tadbravīhyavicārayan||25||

26bhagavānuvāca|
pratigṛhṇe varaṃ vīrāvīpsitaśca varo mama
|
yuvāṃ hi vīryasampannau na vāmasti samaḥ pumān||26||

27vadhyatvamupagacchetāṃ mama satyaparākramau
|
etadicchāmyahaṃ kāmaṃ prāptuṃ lokahitāya vai||27||

28madhukaiṭabhāvūcatuḥ|anṛtaṃ noktapūrvaṃ nau svaireṣvapi kuto'nyathā
|
satye dharme ca niratau vidhdyāvāṃ puruṣottama||28||

29bale rūpe ca śaurye ca śame na ca samo'sti nau
|
dharme tapasi dāne ca śīlasattvadameṣu ca||29||

30upaplavo mahānasmānupāvartata keśava
|
uktaṃ pratikuruṣva tvaṃ kālo hi duratikramaḥ||30||

31āvāmicchāvahe deva kṛtamekaṃ tvayā vibho
|
anāvṛte'sminnākāśe vadhaṃ suravarottama||31||

32putratvamadhigacchāva tava cāpi sulocana
|
vara eṣa vṛto deva tadviddhi surasattama||32||

33anṛtaṃ mā bhaveddeva yaddhi nau saṃśrutaṃ tadā
|
bhagavānuvāca|
bāḍhamevaṃ kariṣyāmi sarvametadbhaviṣyati||33||

34sa vicintyātha govindo nāpaśyadyadanāvṛtam
|
avakāśaṃ pṛthivyāṃ vā divi vā madhusūdanaḥ||34||

35svakāvanāvṛtāvūrū dṛṣṭvā devavarastadā
|
madhukaiṭabhayo rājan śirasī madhusūdanaḥ
|
cakreṇa śitadhāreṇa nyakṛntata mahāyaśāḥ||35||
