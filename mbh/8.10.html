Mahābhārata 8.10 (Karṇaparvan)

1sañjaya uvāca|
hate droṇe maheṣvāse tasminnahani bhārata
|
kṛte ca moghasaṅkalpe droṇaputre mahārathe ||1||

2dravamāṇe mahārāja kauravāṇāṃ balārṇave
|
vyūhya pārthaḥ svakaṃ sainyamatiṣṭhadbhrātṛbhirvṛtaḥ ||2||

3tamavasthitamājñāya putraste bharatarṣabha
|
vidrutaṃ svabalaṃ dṛṣṭvā pauruṣeṇa nyavārayat ||3||

4svamanīkamavasthāpya bāhuvīryamupāśritaḥ
|
yuddhvā ca suciraṃ kālaṃ pāṇḍavaiḥ saha bhārata ||4||

5labdhalakṣaiḥ parairhaṣṭairvyāyacchadbhiściraṃ tadā
|
saṃdhyākālaṃ samāsādya pratyahāramakārayat ||5||

6kṛtvā'vahāraṃ sainyānāṃ praviśya śibiraṃ svakam
|
kuravaḥ suhitaṃ mantraṃ mantrayāñcakrire mithaḥ ||6||

7paryaṅgeṣu parārdhyeṣu spardhyāstaraṇavatsu ca
|
varāsaneṣūpaviṣṭāḥ sukhaśayyāsvivāmarāḥ ||7||

8tato duryodhano rājā sāmnā paramavalgunā
|
tānābhāṣya maheṣvāsān prāptakālamabhāṣata ||8||

9mataṃ matimatāṃ śreṣṭhāḥ sarve prabrūta mā ciram
|
evaṃ gate tu kiṃ kāryaṃ kiṃ ca kāryataraṃ nṛpāḥ ||9||

10sañjaya uvāca|
evamukte narendreṇa narasiṃhā yuyutsavaḥ
|
cakrurnānāvidhāśceṣṭāḥ siṃhāsanagatāstadā ||10||

11teṣāṃ niśāmyeṅgitāni yuddhe prāṇāñjuhūṣatām
|
samudvīkṣya mukhaṃ rājño bālārkasamavarcasam ||11||

12ācāryaputro medhāvī vākyajño vākyamādade
|
rāgo yogastathā dākṣyaṃ nayaścetyarthasādhakāḥ ||12||

13upāyāḥ paṇḍitaiḥ proktāste tu daivamupāśritāḥ
|
lokapravīrā ye'smākaṃ devakalpā mahārathāḥ ||13||

14nītimantastathā yuktā dakṣā raktāśca te hatāḥ
|
na tveva kāryaṃ nairāśyamasmābhirvijayaṃ prati ||14||

15sunītairiha sarvārthairdevamapyanulomyate
|
te vayaṃ pravaraṃ nṝṇāṃ sarvairguṇagaṇairyutam ||15||

16karṇamevābhiṣekṣyāmaḥ saināpatyena bhārata
|
karṇaṃ senāpatiṃ kṛtvā pramathiṣyāmahe ripūn ||16||

17eṣa hyatibalaḥ śūraḥ kṛtāstro yuddhadurmadaḥ
|
vaivasvata ivāsahyaḥ śakto jetuṃ raṇe ripūn ||17||

18etadācāryatanayācchrutvā rājaṃstavātmajaḥ
|
āśāṃ bahumatīṃ cakre karṇaṃ prati sa vai tadā ||18||

19hate bhīṣme ca droṇe ca karṇo jeṣyati pāṇḍavān
|
tāmāśāṃ hṛdaye kṛtvā samāśvasya ca bhārata ||19||

20tato duryodhanaḥ prītaḥ priyaṃ śrutvā'sya tadvacaḥ
|
prītisatkārasaṃyuktaṃ tathyamātmahitaṃ śubham ||20||

21svaṃ manaḥ samavasthāpya bāhuvīryamupāśritaḥ
|
duryodhano mahārāja rādheyamidamabravīt ||21||

22karṇa jānāmi te vīryaṃ sauhṛdaṃ paramaṃ mayi
|
tathāpi tvāṃ mahābāho pravakṣyāmi hitaṃ vacaḥ ||22||

23śrutvā yatheṣṭaṃ ca kuru vīra yattava rocate
|
bhavānprājñatamo nityaṃ mama caiva parā gatiḥ ||23||

24bhīṣmadroṇāvatirathau hatau senāpatī mama
|
senāpatirbhavānastu tābhyāṃ draviṇavattaraḥ ||24||

25vṛddhau ca tau maheṣvāsau sāpekṣau ca dhanañjaye
|
mānitau ca mayā vīrau rādheya vacanāttava ||25||

26pitāmahatvaṃ saṃprekṣya pāṇḍuputrā mahāraṇe
|
rakṣitāstāta bhīṣmeṇa divasāni daśaiva tu ||26||

27nyastaśastre ca bhavati hato bhīṣmaḥ pitāmahaḥ
|
śikhaṇḍinaṃ puraskṛtya phālgunena mahāhave ||27||

28hate tasminmaheṣvāse śaratalpagate tathā
|
tvayokte puruṣavyāghra droṇo hyāsītpuraḥsaraḥ ||28||

29tenāpi rakṣitāḥ pārthāḥ śiṣyatvāditi me matiḥ
|
sa cāpi nihato vṛddho dhṛṣṭadyumnena satvaram ||29||

30nihatābhyāṃ pradhānābhyāṃ tābhyāṃ namitavikrama
|
tvatsamaṃ samare yodhaṃ nānyaṃ paśyāmi cintayan ||30||

31bhavāneva tu naḥ śakto vijayāya na saṃśayaḥ
|
pūrvaṃ madhye ca paścācca tathaiva vihitaṃ hitam ||31||

32sa bhavān dhuryavatsaṃkhye dhuramudvoḍhumahati
|
abhiṣecaya sainānye svayamātmānamātmanā ||32||

33devatānāṃ yathā skandaḥ senānīḥ prabhuravyayaḥ
|
tathā bhavānimāṃ senāṃ dhārtarāṣṭrīṃ bibhartu vai ||33||

34jahi śatrugaṇānsarvānmahendro dānavāniva
|
avasthitaṃ raṇe dṛṣṭvā pāṇḍavāstvāṃ mahārathāḥ ||34||

35draviṣyanti ca pañcālā viṣṇuṃ dṛṣṭveva dānavāḥ
|
tasmāttvaṃ puruṣavyāghra prakarṣaitāṃ mahācamūm ||35||

36bhavatyavasthite yatte pāṇḍavā mandacetasaḥ
|
draviṣyanti sahāmātyāḥ pañcālāḥ sṛñjayāśca ha ||36||

37yathā hyabhyuditaḥ sūryaḥ pratapan svena tejasā
|
vyapohati tamastīvraṃ tathā śatrūnpratāpaya ||37||

38sañjaya uvāca|
āśā balavatī rājanputrasya tava yā'bhavat
|
hate bhīṣme ca droṇe ca karṇo jeṣyati pāṇḍavān ||38||

39tāmāśāṃ hṛdaye kṛtvā karṇamevaṃ tadā'bravīt
|
sūtaputra na te pārthaḥ sthitvā'gre saṃyuyutsati||39||

40karṇa uvāca|
uktametanmayā pūrvaṃ gāndhāre tava sannidhau
|
jeṣyāmi pāṇḍavānsarvān saputrān sajanārdanān ||40||

41senāpatirbhaviṣyāmi tavāhaṃ nātra saṃśayaḥ
|
sthiro bhava mahārāja jitān viddhi ca pāṇḍavān ||41||

42sañjaya uvāca|
evamukto mahārāja tato duryodhano nṛpaḥ
|
uttasthau rājabhiḥ sārdhaṃ devairiva śatakratuḥ ||42||

43saināpatyena satkartuṃ karṇaṃ skanda‌mivāmarāḥ
|
tato'bhiṣiṣicuḥ karṇaṃ vidhidṛṣṭena karmaṇā ||43||

44duryodhanamukhā rājan rājāno vijayaiṣiṇaḥ
|
śātakumbhamayaiḥ kumbhairmāheyaiścābhimantritaiḥ ||44||

45toyapūrṇaviṣāṇaiśca dvipakhaḍgamaharṣabhaiḥ
|
maṇimuktāyutaiścānyaiḥ puṇyagandhaistathauṣadhaiḥ ||45||

46audumbare sukhāsīnamāsane kṣaumasaṃvṛte
|
śāstradṛṣṭena vidhinā saṃbhāraiśca susaṃbhṛtaiḥ ||46||

47brāhmaṇāḥ kṣatriyā vaiśyāstathā śūdrāśca saṃmatāḥ
|
tuṣṭuvustaṃ mahātmānamabhiṣiktaṃ varāsane ||47||

48tato'bhiṣikte rājendra niṣkairgobhirdhanena ca
|
vācayāmāsa viprāgryān rādheyaḥ paravīrahā ||48||

49jaya pārthānsagovindānsānugāṃstānmahāmṛdhe
|
iti taṃ bandinaḥ prāhurdvijāśca puruṣarṣabham ||49||

50jahi pārthānsapāñcālānrādheya vijayāya naḥ
|
udyanniva sadā bhānustamāṃsyugrairgabhastibhiḥ ||50||

51na hyalaṃ tvadvisṛṣṭānāṃ śarāṇāṃ vai sakeśavāḥ
|
ulūkāḥ sūryaraśmīnāṃ jvalatāmiva darśane ||51||

52na hi pārthāḥ sapāñcālāḥ sthātuṃ śaktāstvāgrataḥ
|
āttaśastrasya samare mahendrasyeva dānavāḥ ||52||

53abhiṣiktastu rādheyaḥ prabhayā so'mitaprabhaḥ
|
atyaricyata rūpeṇa divākara ivāparaḥ ||53||

54saināpatye tu rādheyamabhiṣicya sutastava
|
amanyata tadā''tmānaṃ kṛtārthaṃ kālacoditaḥ ||54||

55karṇo'pi rājansaṃprāpya saināpatyamarindamaḥ
|
yogamājñāpayāmāsa sūryasyodayanaṃ prati ||55||

56tava putrairvṛtaḥ karṇaḥ śuśubhe tatra bhārata
|
devairiva yathā skandaḥ saṃgrāme tārakāmaye ||56||
