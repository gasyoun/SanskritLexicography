Mahābhārata 5.139 (Udyogaparvan)

1vaiśampāyana uvāca|
evamuktastu vimanāstiryagdṛṣṭiradhomukhaḥ
|
saṃhatya ca bhruvormadhyaṃ na kiṃcidvyājahāra ha ||1||

2taṃ vai vimanasaṃ dṛṣṭvā saṃprekṣyānyonyamantikāt
|
punarevottaraṃ vākyamuktavantau nararṣabhau ||2||

3bhīṣma uvāca|
śuśrūṣumanasūyaṃ ca brahmaṇyaṃ satyavādinam
|
pratiyotsyāmahe pārthamato duḥkhataraṃ nu kim ||3||

4droṇa uvāca|
aśvatthāmni yathā putre bhūyo mama dhanañjaye
|
bahumānaḥ paro rājan saṃnatiśca kapidhvaje ||4||

5taṃ ca putrāt priyatamaṃ pratiyotsye dhanañjayam
|
kṣātraṃ dharmamanuṣṭhāya dhigastu kṣatrajīvikām ||5||

6yasya loke samo nāsti kaścidanyo dhanurdharaḥ
|
matprasādāt sa bībhatsuḥ śreyānanyairdhanurdharaiḥ ||6||

7mitradhrugduṣṭabhāvaśca nāstiko'thānṛjuḥ śaṭhaḥ
|
na satsu labhate pūjāṃ yajñe mūrkha ivāgataḥ ||7||

8vāryamāṇo'pi pāpebhyaḥ pāpātmā pāpamicchati
|
codyamāno'pi pāpena śubhātmā śubhamicchati ||8||

9mithyopacaritā hyete vartamānā hyanupriye
|
ahitatvāya kalpante doṣā bharatasattama ||9||

10ttvamuktaḥ kuruvṛddhena mayā ca vidureṇa ca
|
vāsudevena ca tathā śreyo naivābhimanyase ||10||

11asti me balamityeva sahasā tvaṃ titīrṣasi
|
sagrāhanakramakaraṃ gaṅgāvegamivorṣṇage ||11||

12vāsasaiva yathā hi tvaṃ prāvṛṇvāno'bhimanyase
|
srajaṃ tyaktāmiva prāpya lobhādyaudhiṣṭhirīṃ śriyam ||12||

13draupadīsahitaṃ pārthaṃ sāyudhairbhrātṛbhirvṛtam
|
vanasthamapi rājyasthaḥ pāṇḍavaṃ ko vijeṣyati ||13||

14nideśe yasya rājānaḥ sarve tiṣṭhanti kiṅkarāḥ
|
tamailavilamāsādya dharmarājo vyarājata ||14||

15kuberasadanaṃ prāpya tato ratnānyavāpya ca
|
sphītamākramya te rāṣṭraṃ rājyamicchanti pāṇḍavāḥ ||15||

16dattaṃ hutamadhītaṃ ca brāhmagāstarpitā dhanaiḥ
|
āvayorgatamāyuśca kṛtakṛtyau ca viddhi nau ||16||

17tvaṃ tu hitvā sukhaṃ rājyaṃ mitrāṇi ca dhanāni ca
|
vigrahaṃ pāṇḍavaiḥ kṛtvā mahadvyasanamāpsyasi ||17||

18draupadī yasya cāśāste vijayaṃ satyavādinī
|
tapoghoravratā devī kathaṃ jeṣyasi pāṇḍavam ||18||

19mantrī janārdano yasya bhrātā yasya dhanañjayaḥ
|
sarvaśastrabhṛtāṃ śreṣṭhaḥ kathaṃ jeṣyasi pāṇḍavam ||19||

20sahāyā brāhmaṇā yasya dhṛtimanto jitendriyāḥ
|
tamugratapasaṃ vīraṃ kathaṃ jeṣyasi pāṇḍavam ||20||

21punaruktaṃ ca vakṣyāmi yatkāryaṃ bhūtimicchatā
|
suhṛdā majjamāneṣu suhṛtsu vyasanārṇave ||21||

22alaṃ yuddhena tairvīraiḥ śāmya tvaṃ kuruvṛddhaye
|
mā gamaḥ sasutāmātyaḥ sabalaśca parābhavam ||22||
