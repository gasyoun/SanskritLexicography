Mahābhārata 5.95 (Udyogaparvan)

1vaiśampāyana uvāca|
teṣvāsīneṣu sarveṣu tūṣṇīṃbhūteṣu rājasu
|
vākyamabhyādade kṛṣṇaḥ sudaṃṣṭro dundubhisvanaḥ ||1||

2jīmūta iva dharmānte sarvāṃ saṃśrāvayan sabhām
|
dhṛtarāṣṭramabhiprekṣya samabhāṣata mādhavaḥ ||2||

3śrībhagavānuvāca
|
kurūṇāṃ pāṇḍavānāṃ ca śamaḥ syāditi bhārata
|
apraṇāśena vīrāṇāmetadyācitumāgataḥ ||3||

4rājannānyat pravaktavyaṃ tava naiḥśreyasaṃ vacaḥ
|
viditaṃ hyeva te sarvaṃ veditavyamarindama ||4||

5idaṃ hyadya kulaṃ śreṣṭhaṃ sarvarājasu pārthiva
|
śrutavṛttopasampannaṃ sarvaiḥ samuditaṃ guṇaiḥ ||5||

6kṛpā'nukampā kāruṇyamānṛśaṃsyaṃ ca bhārata
|
tathā''rjavaṃ kṣamā satyaṃ kuruṣvetadviśiṣyate ||6||

7tasminnevaṃvidhe rājan kule mahati tiṣṭhati
|
tvannimittaṃ viśeṣeṇa neha yuktamasāmpratam ||7||

8tvaṃ hi dhārayitā śreṣṭhaḥ kurūṇāṃ kurusattama
|
mithyā pracaratāṃ tāta bāhyeṣvābhyantareṣu ca ||8||

9te putrāstava kauravya duryodhanapurogamāḥ
|
dharmārthaiḥ pṛṣṭhataḥ kṛtvā pracaranti nṛśaṃsavat ||9||

10aśiṣṭā gatamaryādā lobhena hṛtacetasaḥ
|
sveṣu bandhuṣu mukhyeṣu tadvettha puruṣarṣabha ||10||

11seyamāpanmahāghorā kuruṣveva samutthitā
|
upekṣyamāṇā kauravya pṛthivīṃ ghātayiṣyati ||11||

12śakyā ceyaṃ śamayituṃ na cedditsasi bhārata
|
na duṣkaro hyatra śamo mato me bharatarṣabha ||12||

13tvayyadhīnaḥ śamo rājan mayi caiva viśāṃpate
|
putrān sthāpaya kauravya sthāpayiṣyāmyahaṃ parān ||13||

14ājñā tava hi rājendra kāryā putraiḥ sahānvayaiḥ
|
hitaṃ balavadapyeṣāṃ tiṣṭhatāṃ tava śāsane ||14||

15tava caiva hitaṃ rājan pāṇḍavānāmatho hitam
|
śame prayatamānasya mama śāsanakāṅkṣiṇaḥ ||15||

16svayaṃ niṣphalamālakṣya saṃvidhatsva viśāmpate
|
sahāyabhūtā bharatāstavaiva syurjaneśvara ||16||

17dharmārthayostiṣṭha rājan pāṇḍavairabhirakṣitaḥ
|
na hi śakyāstathābhūtā yatnādapi narādhipa ||17||

18na hi tvāṃ pāṇḍavairjetuṃ rakṣyamāṇaṃ mahātmabhiḥ
|
indro'pi devaiḥ sahitaḥ prasaheta kuto nṛpaḥ ||18||

19yatra bhīṣmaśca droṇaśca kṛpaḥ karṇo viviṃśatiḥ
|
aśvatthāmā vikarṇaśca somada‌tto'tha bāhlikaḥ ||19||

20saindhavaśca kaliṅgaśca kāmbojaśca suda‌kṣiṇaḥ
|
yudhiṣṭhiro bhīmasenaḥ savyasācī yamau tathā ||20||

21sātyakiśca mahātejā yuyutsuśca mahārathaḥ
|
ko nu tānviparītātmā yuddhyeta bharatarṣabha ||21||

22lokasyeśvaratāṃ bhūyaḥ śatrubhiścāpyadhṛṣyatām
|
prāpsyasi tvamamitraghna sahitaḥ kurupāṇḍavaiḥ ||22||

23tasya te pṛthivīpālāstvatsamāḥ pṛthivīpate
|
śreyāṃsaścaiva rājānaḥ sandhāsyante parantapa ||23||

24sa tvaṃ putraiśca pautraiśca pitṛbhirbhrātṛbhistathā
|
suhṛdbhiḥ sarvato guptaḥ sukhaṃ śakṣyasi jīvitum ||24||

25etāneva purodhāya satkṛtya ca yathā purā
|
akhilāṃ bhokṣyase sarvāṃ pṛthivīṃ pṛthivīpate ||25||

26etairhi sahitaḥ sarvaiḥ pāṇḍavaiḥ svaiśca bhārata
|
anyānvijeṣyase śatrūneṣa svārthastavākhilaḥ ||26||

27tairevopārjitāṃ bhūmiṃ bhokṣyase ca parantapa
|
yadi sampatsyase putraiḥ sahāmātyairnarādhipa ||27||

28saṃyuge vai mahārāja dṛśyate sumahān kṣayaḥ
|
kṣaye cobhayato rājan kaṃ dharmamanupaśyasi ||28||

29pāṇḍavairnihataiḥ saṅkhye putrairvā'pi mahābalaiḥ
|
yadvindethāḥ sukhaṃ rājaṃstadbrūhi bharatarṣabha ||29||

30śūrāśca hi kṛtāstrāśca sarve yuddhābhikāṅkṣiṇaḥ
|
pāṇḍavāstāvakāścaiva tānrakṣa mahato bhayāt ||30||

31na paśyema kurūn sarvān pāṇḍavāṃścaiva saṃyuge
|
kṣīṇānubhayataḥ śūrānrathino rathibhirhatān ||31||

32samavetāḥ pṛthivyāṃ hi rājāno rājasattama
|
amarṣavaśamāpannā nāśayeyurimāḥ prajāḥ ||32||

33trāhi rājannimaṃ lokaṃ na naśyeyurimāḥ prajāḥ
|
tvayi prakṛtimāpanne śeṣaḥ syāt kurunandana ||33||

34śuklā vadānyā hrīmanta āryāḥ puṇyābhijātayaḥ
|
anyonyasacivā rājaṃstān pāhi mahato bhayāt ||34||

35śiveneme bhūmipālāḥ samāgamya parasparam
|
saha bhuktvā ca pītvā ca pratiyāntu yathāgṛham ||35||

36suvāsasaḥ sragviṇaśca satkṛtā bharatarṣabha
|
amarṣaṃ ca nirākṛtya vairāṇi ca paraṃtapa ||36||

37hārdaṃ yat pāṇḍaveṣvāsīt prāpte'sminnāyuṣaḥ kṣaye
|
tadeva te bhavatvadya sandhatsva bharatarṣabha ||37||

38bālā vihīnāḥ pitrā te tvayaiva parivardhitāḥ
|
tān pālaya yathānyāyaṃ putrāṃśca bharatarṣabha ||38||

39bhavataiva hi rakṣyāste vyasaneṣu viśeṣataḥ
|
mā te dharmastathaivārtho naśyeta bharatarṣabha ||39||

40āhustvāṃ pāṇḍavā rājannabhivādya prasādya ca
|
bhavataḥ śāsanādduḥkhamanubhūtaṃ sahānugaiḥ ||40||

41dvādaśemāni varṣāṇi vane nirvyuṣitāni naḥ
|
trayodaśaṃ tathā'jñātaiḥ sajane parivatsaram ||41||

42sthātā naḥ samaye tasmin piteti kṛtaniścayāḥ
|
nāhāsma samayaṃ tāta tacca no brāhmaṇā viduḥ ||42||

43tasminnaḥ samaye tiṣṭha sthitānāṃ bharatarṣabha
|
nityaṃ saṅkleśitā rājan svarājyāṃśaṃ labhemahi ||43||

44tvaṃ dharmamarthaṃ sañjānan samyaṅnastrātumarhasi
|
gurutvaṃ bhavati prekṣya bahūn kleśāṃstitikṣmahe ||44||

45sa bhavān mātṛpitṛvadasmāsu pratipadyatām
|
gurorgarīyasī vṛttiryā ca śiṣyasya bhārata ||45||

46vartāmahe tvayi ca tāṃ tvaṃ ca vartasva nastathā
|
pitrā sthāpayitavyā hi vayamutpathamāsthitāḥ ||46||

47saṃsthāpaya pathiṣvasmāṃstiṣṭha dharme suvartmani
|
āhuścemāṃ pariṣadaṃ putrāste bharatarṣabha ||47||

48dharmajñeṣu sabhāsatsu neha yuktamasāmpratam
|
yatra dharmo hyadharmeṇa satyaṃ yatrānṛtena ca ||48||

49hanyate prekṣamāṇānāṃ hatāstatra sabhāsadaḥ
|
viddho dharmo hyadharmeṇa sabhāṃ yatra prapadyate ||49||

50na cāsya śalyaṃ kṛntanti viddhāstatra sabhāsadaḥ
|
dharma etānārujati yathā nadyanukūlajān ||50||

51ye dharmamanupaśyantastūṣṇīṃ dhyāyanta āsate
|
te satyamāhurdharmyaṃ ca nyāyyaṃ ca bharatarṣabha ||51||

52śakyaṃ kimanyadvaktuṃ te dānādanyajjaneśvara
|
bruvantu te mahīpālāḥ sabhāyāṃ ye samāsate ||52||

53dharmārthau saṃpradhāryaiva yadi satyaṃ bravīmyaham
|
pramuñcemān mṛtyupāśāt kṣatriyān puruṣarṣabha ||53||

54praśāmya bharataśreṣṭha mā manyuvaśamanvagāḥ
|
pitryaṃ tebhyaḥ pradāyāṃśaṃ pāṇḍavebhyo yathocitam ||‌54||

55tataḥ saputraḥ siddhārtho bhuṅkṣva bhogān paraṃtapa
|
ajātaśatruṃ jānīṣe sthitaṃ dharme satāṃ sadā ||55||

56saputre tvayi vṛttiṃ ca vartate yāṃ narādhipa
|
dāhitaśca nirastaśca tvāmevopāśritaḥ punaḥ ||56||

57indraprasthaṃ tvayaivāsau saputreṇa vivāsitaḥ
|
sa tatra vivasan sarvān vaśamānīya pārthivān ||57||

58tvanmukhānakarodrājanna ca tvāmatyavartata
|
tasyaivaṃ vartamānasya saubalena jihīrṣatā ||58||

59rāṣṭrāṇi dhanadhānyaṃ ca prayuktaḥ paramopadhiḥ
|
sa tāmavasthāṃ saṃprāpya kṛṣṇāṃ prekṣya sabhāgatām ||59||

60kṣatradharmādameyātmā nākampata yudhiṣṭhiraḥ
|
ahaṃ tu tava teṣāṃ ca śreya icchāmi bhārata ||60||

61dharmādarthāt sukhāccaiva mā rājannīnaśaḥ prajāḥ
|
anarthamarthaṃ manvāno'pyarthaṃ cānarthamātmanaḥ ||61||

62lobhe'tiprasṛtān putrānnigṛhṇīṣva viśāṃpate
|
sthitāḥ śuśrūṣituṃ pārthāḥ sthitā yoddhumarindamāḥ|| yatte pathyatamaṃ rājaṃstasmiṃstiṣṭha paraṃtapa ||62||

63vaiśampāyana uvāca|
tadvākyaṃ pārthivāḥ sarve hṛdayaiḥ samapūjayan
|
na tatra kaścidvaktuṃ hi vācaṃ prākrāmadagrataḥ ||63||
