Mahābhārata 5.3 (Udyogaparvan)

1sātyakiruvāca| yādṛśaḥ puruṣasyātmā tādṛśaṃ saṃprabhāṣate
|
yathārūpo'ntarātmā te tathārūpaṃ prabhāṣate ||1||

2santi vai puruṣāḥ śūrāḥ santi kā puruṣāstathā
|
ubhāvetau dṛḍhau pakṣau dṛśyete puruṣānprati ||2||

3ekasminneva jāyete kule klībamahābalau
|
phalāphalavatī śākhe yathaikasminvanaspatau ||3||

4nābhyasūyāmi te vākyaṃ bruvato lāṅgaladhvaja
|
ye tu śṛṇvanti te vākyaṃ tānasūyāmi mādhava ||4||

5kathaṃ hi dharmarājasya doṣamalpamapi bruvan
|
labhate pariṣanmadhye vyāhartumakutobhayaḥ||5||

6samāhūya mahātmānaṃ jitavanto'kṣakovidāḥ
|
anakṣajñaṃ yathāśraddhaṃ teṣu dharmajayaḥ kutaḥ
|
yadi kuntīsutaṃ gehe krīḍantaṃ bhrātṛbhiḥ saha ||6||

7abhigamya jayeyuste tatteṣāṃ dharmato bhavet
|
samāhūya tu rājānaṃ kṣatradharmarataṃ sadā||7||

8nikṛtyā jitavantaste kiṃ nu teṣāṃ paraṃ śubham
|
kathaṃ praṇipateccāyamiha kṛtvā paṇaṃ param||8||

9vanavāsādvimuktastu prāptaḥ paitāmahaṃ padam
|
yadyayaṃ paravittāni kāmayeta yudhiṣṭhiraḥ||9||

10evamapyayamatyantaṃ parānnārhati yācitum
|
kathaṃ ca dharmayuktāste na ca rājyaṃ jihīrṣavaḥ||10||

11nivṛttavāsān kaunteyān ya āhurviditā iti
|
anunītā hi bhīṣmeṇa droṇena vidureṇa ca||11||

12na vyavasyanti pāṇḍūnāṃ pradātuṃ paitṛkaṃ vasu
|
ahaṃ tu tān śitairbāṇairanunīya raṇe balāt||12||

13pādayoḥ pātayiṣyāmi kaunteyasya mahātmanaḥ
|
atha te na vyavasyanti praṇipātāya dhīmataḥ||13||

14gamiṣyanti sahāmātyā yamasya sadanaṃ prati
|
na hi te yuyudhānasya saṃrabdhasya yuyutsataḥ||14||

15vegaṃ samarthāḥ saṃsoḍhuṃ vajrasyeva mahīdharāḥ
|
ko hi gāṇḍīvadhanvānaṃ kaśca cakrāyudhaṃ yudhi||15||

16māṃ cāpi viṣahetkruddhaṃ kaśca bhīmaṃ durāsadam
|
yamau ca dṛḍhadhanvānau yamakālopamadyutī
|
virāṭadrupadau vīrau yamakālopamadyutī||16||

17ko jijīviṣurāsādeddhṛṣṭadyumnaṃ ca pārṣatam
|
pañcaitān pāṇḍaveyāṃstu draupadyāḥ kīrtivardhanān ||17||

18samapramāṇānpāṇḍūnāṃ samavīryānmadotkaṭān
|
saubhadraṃ ca maheṣvāsamamarairapi duḥsaham ||18||

19gadapradyumnasāmbāṃśca kālasūryānalopamān
|
te vayaṃ dhṛtarāṣṭrasya putraṃ śakuninā saha ||19||

20karṇaṃ caiva nihatyājāvabhiṣekṣyāma pāṇḍavam
|
nādharmo vidyate kaścicchatrūnhatvā''tatāyinaḥ ||20||

21adharmyamayaśasyaṃ ca śātravāṇāṃ prayācanam
|
hṛdgatastasya yaḥ kāmastaṃ kurudhvamatandritāḥ ||21||

22nisṛṣṭaṃ dhārtarāṣṭreṇa rājyaṃ prāpnotu pāṇḍavaḥ
|
adya pāṇḍusuto rājyaṃ labhatāṃ vā yudhiṣṭhiraḥ||22||

23nihatā vā raṇe sarve svapsyanti vasudhātale ||23||
