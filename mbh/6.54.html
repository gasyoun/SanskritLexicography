Mahābhārata 6.54 (Bhīṣmaparvan)

1dhṛtarāṣṭra uvāca|tathā pratisamādiṣṭaḥ kāliṅgo vāhinīpatiḥ
|
kathamadbhutakarmāṇaṃ bhīmasenaṃ mahābalam ||1||

2carantaṃ gadayā vīraṃ daṇḍahastamivāntakam
|
yodhayāmāsa samare kāliṅgaḥ saha senayā ||2||

3sañjaya uvāca|putreṇa tava rājendra sa tathokto mahābalaḥ
|
mahatyā senayā guptaḥ prāyādbhīmarathaṃ prati ||3||

4tāmāpatantīṃ mahatīṃ kaliṅgānāṃ mahācamūm
|
rathāśvanāgakalilāṃ pragṛhītamahāyudhām ||4||

5bhīmasenaḥ kaliṅgānāmārcchadbhārata vāhinīm
|
ketumantaṃ ca naiṣādimāyāntaṃ saha cedibhiḥ ||5||

6tataḥ śrutāyuḥ saṃkruddho rājñā ketumatā saha
|
āsasāda raṇe bhīmaṃ vyūḍhānīkeṣu cediṣu ||6||

7rathairanekasāhasraiḥ kaliṅgānāṃ narādhipa
|
ayutena gajānāṃ ca niṣādaiḥ saha ketumān ||7||

8bhīmasenaṃ raṇe rājan samantāt paryavārayat
|
cedimatsyakarūṣāśca bhīmasenapadānugāḥ ||8||

9abhyadhāvanta samare niṣādān saha rājabhiḥ
|
tataḥ pravavṛte yuddhaṃ ghorarūpaṃ bhayāvaham ||9||

10na prājānanta yodhāḥ svān parasparajighāṃsayā
|
ghoramāsīttato yuddhaṃ bhīmasya sahasā paraiḥ ||10||

11yathendrasya mahārāja mahatyā daityasenayā
|
tasya sainyasya saṅgrāme yudhyamānasya bhārata ||11||

12babhūva sumahān śabdaḥ sāgarasyeva garjataḥ
|
anyonyaṃ sma tadā yodhā vikarṣanto viśāṃpate ||12||

13mahīṃ cakruścitāṃ sarvāṃ śaśalohitasannibhām
|
yodhāṃśca svān parānvāpi nābhyajānan jighāṃsayā ||13||

14svānapyādadate svāśca śūrāḥ paramadurjayāḥ
|
vimardaḥ sumahānāsīdalpānāṃ bahubhiḥ saha ||14||

15kaliṅgaiḥ saha cedīnāṃ niṣādaiśca viśāṃpate
|
kṛtvā puruṣakāraṃ tu yathāśakti mahābalāḥ ||15||

16bhīmasenaṃ parityajya saṃnyavartanta cedayaḥ
|
sarvaiḥ kaliṅgairāsannaḥ sannivṛtteṣu cediṣu ||16||

17svabāhubalamāsthāya saṃnyavartata pāṇḍavaḥ
|
na cacāla rathopasthādbhīmaseno mahābalaḥ ||17||

18śitairavākiradbāṇaiḥ kaliṅgānāṃ varūthinīm
|
kāliṅgastu maheṣvāsaḥ putraścāsya mahārathaḥ ||18||

19śakradeva iti khyāto jaghnatuḥ pāṇḍavaṃ śaraiḥ
|
tato bhīmo mahābāhurvidhunvan ruciraṃ dhanuḥ ||19||

20yodhayāmāsa kāliṅgaṃ svabāhubalamāśritaḥ
|
śakradevastu samare visṛjan sāyakān bahūn ||20||

21aśvān jaghāna samare bhīmasenasya sāyakaiḥ
|
taṃ dṛṣṭvā virathaṃ tatra bhīmasenamarindamam ||21||

22śakradevo'bhidudrāva śarairavakiran śitaiḥ
|
bhīmasyopari rājendra śakradevo mahābalaḥ ||22||

23vavarṣa śaravarṣāṇi tapānte jalado yathā
|
hatāśve tu rathe tiṣṭhan bhīmaseno mahābalaḥ ||23||

24śakradevāya cikṣepa sarvaśaikyāyasīṃ gadām
|
sa tayā nihato rājan kāliṅgatanayo rathāt ||24||

25virathaḥ saha sutena jagāma dharaṇītalam
|
hatamātmasutaṃ dṛṣṭvā kaliṅgānāṃ janādhipaḥ ||25||

26rathairanekasāhastrairbhīmasyāvārayaddiśaḥ
|
tato bhīmo mahāvegāṃ tyaktvā gurvīṃ mahāgadām ||26||

27nistriṃśamādade ghoraṃ cikīrṣuḥ karma dāruṇam
|
carma cāpratimaṃ rājannārṣabhaṃ puruṣarṣabha ||27||

28nakṣatrairardhacandraiśca śātakumbhamayaiścitam
|
kāliṅgastu tataḥ kruddho dhanurjyāmavamṛjya ca ||28||

29pragṛhya ca śaraṃ ghoramekaṃ sarpaviṣopamam
|
prāhiṇodbhīmasenāya vadhākāṃkṣī janeśvaraḥ ||29||

30tamāpatantaṃ vegena preritaṃ niśitaṃ śaram
|
bhīmaseno dvidhā rājaṃściccheda vipulāsinā ||30||

31udakrośacca saṃhṛṣṭastrāsayāno varūthinīm
|
kāliṅgo'tha tataḥ kruddho bhīmasenāya saṃyuge ||31||

32tomarān prāhiṇocchīghraṃ caturdaśa śilāśitān
|
tānaprāptān mahābāhuḥ khagatāneva pāṇḍavaḥ ||32||

33ciccheda sahasā rājannasaṃbhrānto varāsinā
|
nikṛtya tu raṇe bhīmastomarānvai caturdaśa ||33||

34bhānunmataṃ tato bhīmaḥ prādravatpuruṣarṣabhaḥ
|
bhānumāṃstu tato bhīmaṃ śaravarṣeṇa cchādayan ||34||

35nanāda balavannādaṃ nādayāno nabhastalam
|
na ca taṃ mamṛṣe bhīmaḥ siṃhanādaṃ mahāhave ||35||

36tataḥ śabdena mahatā vinanāda mahāsvanaḥ
|
tena nādena vitrastā kaliṃgānāṃ varūthinī ||36||

37na bhīmaṃ samare mene mānuṣaṃ bharatarṣabha
|
tato bhīmo mahābāhurnarditvā vipulaṃ khanam ||37||

38sāsirvegavadāplutya dantābhyāṃ vāraṇottamam
|
āruroha tato madhyaṃ nāgarājasya māriṣa ||38||

39tato mumoca kāliṅgaḥ śaktiṃ tāmakaroddvidhā
|
khaḍgena pṛthunā madhye bhānumantamathācchinat ||39||

40so'ntarāyudhinaṃ hatvā rājaputramarindamaḥ
|
guruṃ bhārasahaṃ skandhe nāgasyāsimapātayat ||40||

41chinnaskandhaḥ sa vinadan papāta gajayūthapaḥ
|
ārugṇaḥ sindhuvegena sānumāniva parvataḥ ||41||

42tatastasmādavaplutya gajādbhārata bhārataḥ
|
khaḍgapāṇiradīnātmā tasthau bhūmau sudaṃśitaḥ ||42||

43sa cacāra bahūnmārgānabhitaḥ pātayan gajān
|
agnicakramivāviddhaṃ sarvataḥ pratyadṛśyata ||43||

44aśvavṛndeṣu nāgeṣu rathānīkeṣu cābhibhūḥ
|
padātīnāṃ ca saṃdheṣu vinighnan śoṇitokṣitaḥ ||44||

45śyenavadvyacaradbhīmo raṇe'riṣu balotkaṭaḥ
|
chindaṃsteṣāṃ śarīrāṇi śirāṃsi ca mahābalaḥ ||45||

46khaḍgena śitadhāreṇa saṃyuge gajayodhinām
|
padātirekaḥ saṃkruddhaḥ śatrūṇāṃ bhayavardhanaḥ ||46||

47saṃmohayāmāsa sa tān kālāntakayamopamaḥ
|
mūḍhāśca te tamevājau vinadantaḥ samādravan ||47||

48sāsimuttamavegena vicarantaṃ mahāraṇe
|
nikṛtya rathināṃ cājau ratheṣāśca yugāni ca ||48||

49jaghāna rathinaścāpi balavān ripumardanaḥ
|
bhīmasenaścaran mārgān subahūn pratyadṛśyata ||49||

50bhrāntamāviddhamudbhrāntamāplutaṃ prasṛtaṃ plutam
|
saṃpātaṃ samudīrṇaṃ ca darśayāmāsa pāṇḍavaḥ ||50||

51kecidagrāsinā chinnāḥ pāṇḍavena mahātmanā
|
vinedurbhinnamarmāṇo nipetuśca gatāsavaḥ ||51||

52chinnadantāgrahastāśca bhinnakuṃbhāstathā pare
|
viyodhāḥ svānyanīkāni jaghnurbhārata vāraṇāḥ ||52||

53nipetururvyāṃ ca tathā vinadanto mahāravān
|
chinnāṃśca tomarān rājan mahāmātraśirāṃsi ca ||53||

54paristomānvicitrāṃśca kakṣyāśca kanakojjvalāḥ
|
graiveyāṇyatha śaktīśca patākāḥ kaṇapāṃstathā ||54||

55tūṇīrānatha yantrāṇi vicitrāṇi dhanūṃṣi ca
|
bhindipālāni śubhrāṇi totrāṇi cāṅkuśaiḥ saha ||55||

56ghaṇṭāśca vividhā rājan hemagarbhāntsarūnapi
|
patataḥ pātitāṃścaiva paśyāmaḥ saha sādibhiḥ ||56||

57chinnagātrāvarakarairnihataiścāpi vāraṇaiḥ
|
āsīdbhūmiḥ samāstīrṇā patitairbhūdharairiva ||57||

58vimṛdyaivaṃ mahānāgān mamardānyānmahābalaḥ
|
aśvārohavarāṃścaiva pātayāmāsa saṃyuge ||58||

59taddhoramabhavayuddhaṃ tasya teṣāṃ ca bhārata
|
khalīnānyatha yoktrāṇi kakṣyāśca kanakojjvalāḥ ||59||

60paristomāśca prāsāśca ṛṣṭayaśca mahādhanāḥ
|
kavacānyatha carmāṇi citrāṇyāstaraṇāni ca ||60||

61tatra tatrāpaviddhāni vyadṛśyanta mahāhave
|
prāsairyaṃtrairvicitraiśca śastraiśca vimalaistathā ||61||

62sa cakre vasudhāṃ kīrṇāṃ śabalaiḥ kusumairiva
|
āplutya rathinaḥ kāṃścitparāmṛśya mahābalaḥ ||62||

63pātayāmāsa khaḍgena sadhvajānapi pāṇḍavaḥ
|
muhurutpatato dikṣu dhāvataśca yaśasvinaḥ ||63||

64mārgāṃśca carataścitraṃ vyasmayanta raṇe janāḥ
|
sa jaghāna padā kāṃścidvyākṣipyānyānapothayat ||64||

65khaḍgenānyāṃśca ciccheda nādenānyāṃśca bhīṣayan
|
ūruvegena cāpyanyān pātayāmāsa bhūtale ||65||

66apare cainamālokya bhayāt pañcatvamāgatāḥ
|
evaṃ sā bahulā senā kaliṅgānāṃ tarasvinām ||66||

67parivārya raṇe bhīṣmaṃ bhīmasenamupādravat
|
tataḥ kāliṅgasainyānāṃ pramukhe bharatarṣabha ||67||

68śrutāyuṣamabhiprekṣya bhīmasenaḥ samabhyayāt
|
tamāyāntamabhiprekṣya kāliṅgo navabhiḥ śaraiḥ ||68||

69bhīmasenamameyātmā pratyavidhyat stanāntare
|
kāliṅgabāṇābhihatastotrārdita iva dvipaḥ ||69||

70bhīmasenaḥ prajajvāla krodhenāgnirivaidhitaḥ
|
athāśokaḥ samādāya rathaṃ hemapariṣkṛtam ||70||

71bhīmaṃ saṃpādayāmāsa rathena rathasārathiḥ
|
tamāruhya rathaṃ tūrṇaṃ kaunteyaḥ śatrusūdanaḥ ||71||

72kāliṅgamabhidudrāva tiṣṭha tiṣṭheti cābravīt
|
tataḥ śrutāyurbalavān bhīmāya niśitān śarān ||72||

73preṣayāmāsa saṃkruddho darśayan pāṇilāghavam
|
sa kārmukavarotsṛṣṭairnavābhirniśitaiḥ śaraiḥ ||73||

74samāhato mahārāja kāliṅgena mahātmanā
|
saṃcukruśe bhṛśaṃ bhīmo daṇḍāhata ivoragaḥ ||74||

75kruddhaśca cāpamāyamya balavadbalināṃ varaḥ
|
kāliṅgamavadhītpārtho bhīmaḥ saptabhirāyasaiḥ ||75||

76kṣurābhyāṃ cakrarakṣau ca kāliṃgasya mahābalau
|
satyadevaṃ ca satyaṃ ca prāhiṇodyamasādanam ||76||

77tataḥ punarameyātmā nārācairniśitaistribhiḥ
|
ketumantaṃ raṇe bhīmo'gamayadyamasādanam ||77||

78tataḥ kaliṅgāḥ saṃnnaddhā bhīmasenamamarṣaṇam
|
anīkairbahusāhastraiḥ kṣatriyāḥ samavārayan ||78||

79tataḥ śaktigadākhaḍgatomararṣṭiparaśvadhaiḥ
|
kaliṅgāśca tato rājan bhīmasenamavākiran ||79||

80saṃnivārya sa tāṃ ghorāṃ śaravṛṣṭiṃ samutthitām
|
gadāmādāya tarasā saṃnipatya mahābalaḥ ||80||

81bhīmaḥ sapta śatān vīrānanayadyamasādanam
|
punaścaiva dvisāhasrān kaliṅgānarimardanaḥ ||81||

82prāhiṇonmṛtyulokāya tadadbhutamivābhavat
|
evaṃ sa tānyanīkāni kaliṅgānāṃ punaḥ punaḥ ||82||

83bibheda samare tūrṇaṃ prekṣya bhīṣmaṃ mahāratham
|
hatārohāśca mātaṅgāḥ pāṇḍavena kṛtā raṇe ||83||

84viprajagmuranīkeṣu meghā vātahatā iva
|
mṛdnantaḥ svānyanīkāni vinadantaḥ śarāturāḥ ||84||

85tato bhīmo mahābāhuḥ khaḍgahasto mahābhujaḥ
|
saṃprahṛṣṭo mahāghoṣaṃ śaṅkhaṃ prādhmāpayadbalī ||85||

86sarvakāliṅgasainyānāṃ manāṃsi samakaṃpayat
|
mohaścāpi kaliṅgānāmāviveśa paraṃtapa ||86||

87prākaṃpanta ca sainyāni vāhanāni ca sarvaśaḥ
|
bhīmena samare rājan gajendreṇeva sarvaśaḥ ||87||

88mārgān bahūn vicaratā dhāvatā ca tatastataḥ
|
muhurutpatatā caiva saṃmohaḥ samapadyata ||88||

89bhīmasenabhayatrastaṃ sainyaṃ ca samakampata
|
kṣobhyamāṇamasaṃbādhaṃ grāheṇeva mahatsaraḥ ||89||

90trāsiteṣu ca sarveṣu bhīmenādbhutakarmaṇā
|
punarāvartamāneṣu vidravatsu ca saṅghaśaḥ ||90||

91sarvakāliṅgayodheṣu pāṇḍūnāṃ dhvajinīpatiḥ
|
abravītsvānyanīkāni yudhyadhvamiti pārṣataḥ ||91||

92senāpativacaḥ śrutvā śikhaṇḍipramukhā gaṇāḥ
|
bhīmamevābhyavartanta rathānīkaiḥ prahāribhiḥ ||92||

93dharmarājaśca tān sarvānupajagrāha pāṇḍavaḥ
|
mahatā meghavarṇena nāgānīkena pṛṣṭhataḥ ||93||

94evaṃ saṃnodya sarvāṇi svānyanīkāni pārṣataḥ
|
bhīmasenasya jagrāha pārṣṇiṃ satpuruṣairvṛtaḥ ||94||

95na hi pañcālarājasya loke kaścana vidyate
|
bhīmasātyakayoranyaḥ prāṇebhyaḥ priyakṛttamaḥ ||95||

96so'paśyacca kaliṅgeṣu carantamarisūdanaḥ
|
bhīmasenaṃ mahābāhuṃ pārṣataḥ paravīrahā ||96||

97nanarda bahudhā rājan hṛṣṭaścāsīt paraṃtapaḥ
|
śaṅkhaṃ dadhmau ca samare siṃhanādaṃ nanāda ca ||97||

98sa ca pārāvatāśvasya rathe hemapariṣkṛte
|
kovidāradhvajaṃ dṛṣṭvā bhīmasenaḥ samāśvasat ||98||

99dhṛṣṭadyumnastu taṃ dṛṣṭvā kaliṅgaiḥ samabhidrutam
|
bhīmasenamameyātmā trāṇāyājau samabhyayāt ||99||

100tau dūrāt sātyakiṃ dṛṣṭvā dhṛṣṭadyumnavṛkodarau
|
kaliṅgān samare vīrau yodhayetāṃ manasvinau ||100||

101sa tatra gatvā śaineyo javena jayatāṃ varaḥ
|
pārthapārṣatayoḥ pārṣṇiṃ jagrāha puruṣarṣabhaḥ ||101||

102sa kṛtvā dāruṇaṃ karma pragṛhītaśarāsanaḥ
|
āsthito raudramātmānaṃ kaliṅgānanvavaikṣata ||102||

103kaliṅgaprabhavāṃ caiva māṃsaśoṇitakardamām
|
rudhirasyandinīṃ tatra bhīmaḥ prāvartayannadīm ||103||

104antareṇa kaliṅgānāṃ pāṇḍavānāṃ ca vāhinīm
|
tāṃ santatāra dustārāṃ bhīmaseno mahābalaḥ ||104||

105bhīmasenaṃ tathā dṛṣṭvā prākrośaṃstāvakā nṛpa
|
kālo'yaṃ bhīmarūpeṇa kaliṅgaiḥ saha yudhyate ||105||

106tataḥ śāntanavo bhīṣmaḥ śrutvā taṃ ninadaṃ raṇe
|
abhyayāttvarito bhīmaṃ vyūḍhānīkaḥ samantataḥ ||106||

107taṃ sātyakirbhīmaseno dhṛṣṭadyumnaśca pārṣataḥ
|
abhyadravanta bhīṣmasya rathaṃ hemapariṣkṛtam ||107||

108parivārya tu te sarve gāṅgeyaṃ tarasā raṇe
|
tribhistribhiḥ śarairghorairbhīṣmamānarchurojasā ||108||

109pratyavidhyata tān sarvān pitā devavratastava
|
yatamānān maheṣvāsāṃstribhistribhirajihmagaiḥ ||109||

110tataḥ śarasahastreṇa sannivārya mahārathān
|
hayān kāñcanasannāhān bhīmasya nyahanaccharaiḥ ||110||

111hatāśve sa rathe tiṣṭhan bhīmasenaḥ pratāpavān
|
śaktiṃ cikṣepa tarasā gāṅgeyasya rathaṃ prati ||111||

112aprāptāmatha tāṃ śaktiṃ pitā devavratastava
|
tridhā ciccheda samare sā pṛthivyāmaśīryata ||112||

113tataḥ śaikyāyasīṃ gurvīṃ pragṛhya balavān gadām
|
bhīmasenastatastūrṇaṃ pupluve manujarṣabha ||113||

114sātyako'pi tatastūrṇaṃ bhīmasya priyakāmyayā
|
gāṅgeyasārathiṃ tūrṇaṃ pātayāmāsa sāyakaiḥ ||114||

115bhīṣmastu nihate tasmin sārathau rathināṃ varaḥ
|
vātāyamānaistairaśvairapanīto raṇājirāt ||115||

116bhīmasenastato rājannapayāte mahāvrate
|
prajajvāla yathā vahnirdahan kakṣamivaidhitaḥ ||116||

117sa hatvā sarvakāliṅgān senāmadhye vyatiṣṭhata
|
nainamabhyutsahan kecittāvakā bharatarṣabha ||117||

118dhṛṣṭadyumnastamāropya svarathe rathināṃ varaḥ
|
paśyatāṃ sarvasainyānāmapovāha yaśasvinam ||118||

119saṃpūjyamānaḥ pāñcālyairmatsyaiśca bharatarṣabha
|
dhṛṣṭadyumnaṃ pariṣvajya sameyādatha sātyakim ||119||

120athābravīdbhīmasenaṃ sātyakiḥ satyavikramaḥ
|
praharṣayan yaduvyāghro dhṛṣṭadyumnasya paśyataḥ ||120||

121diṣṭyā kaliṅgarājaśca rājaputraśca ketumān
|
śakradevaśca kāliṃgaḥ kaliṃgāśca mṛdhe hatāḥ ||121||

122svabāhubalavīryeṇa nāgāśvarathasaṃkulaḥ
|
mahāpuruṣabhūyiṣṭho dhīrayodhaniṣevitaḥ ||122||

123mahāvyūhaḥ kaliṅgānāmekena mṛditastvayā
|
evamuktvā śinernaptā dīrghabāhurarinda‌ma ||123||

124rathādrathamabhidrutya paryaṣvajata pāṇḍavam
|
tataḥ svarathamāsthāya punareva mahārathaḥ
|
tāvakānavadhīt kruddho bhīmasya balamādadhat ||124||
