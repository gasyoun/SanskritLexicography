Mahābhārata 13.15 (Anuśāsanaparvan)

1kṛṣṇa uvāca|
mūrdhnā nipatya niyatastejaḥ sannicaye tataḥ
|
paramaṃ harṣamāgatya bhagavantamathābruvam ||1||

2dharme dṛḍhatvaṃ yudhi śatrughātaṃ yaśastathā'gryaṃ paramaṃ balaṃ ca
|
yogapriyatvaṃ tava sannirṣaṃ vṛṇe sutānāṃ ca śataṃ śatāni ||2||

3evamastviti tadvākyaṃ mayoktaḥ prāha śaṅkaraḥ
|
tato māṃ jagato mātā dhāriṇī sarvapāvanī ||3||

4uvācomā praṇihitā śarvāṇī tapasāṃ nidhiḥ
|
datto bhagavatā putraḥ sāmbo nāma tavānagha ||4||

5mattopyaṣṭau varāniṣṭān gṛhāṇa tvaṃ dadāmi te
|
praṇamya śirasā sā ca mayoktā pāṇḍunandana ||5||

6dvijeṣavakopaṃ pitṛtaḥ prasādaṃ śataṃ sutānāṃ paramaṃ ca bhogam
|
kule prītiṃ mātṛtaśca prasādaṃ śamaprāptiṃ pravṛṇe cāpi dākṣyam ||6||

7umovāca
|
evaṃ bhaviṣyatyamaraprabhāva nāhaṃ mṛṣā jātu vade kadācit
|
bhāryāsahasrāṇi ca ṣoḍaśaiva tāsu priyatvaṃ ca tathā'kṣayaṃ ca ||7||

8prītiṃ cāgryāṃ bāndhavānāṃ sakāśāddadāmi te'haṃ vapuṣaḥ kāmyatāṃ ca
|
bhokṣyante vai saptatiṃ vai śatāni gṛhe tubhyamatithīnāṃ ca nityam ||8||

9vāsudeva uvāca|
evaṃ dattvā varān devo mama devī ca bhārata
|
antarhitaḥ kṣaṇe tasmin sagaṇo bhīmapūrvaja ||9||

10etadatyadbhutaṃ pūrvaṃ brāhmaṇāyātitejase
|
upamanyave mayā kṛtsraṃ vyākhyātaṃ pārthivottama
|
namaskṛtvā tu sa prāha devadevāya suvrata ||10||

11upamanyuruvāca|
nāsti śarvasamo devo nāsti śarvasamā gatiḥ
|
nāsti śarvasamo dāne nāsti śarvasamo raṇe ||11||
