Mahābhārata 1.203 (Ādiparvan)

1bhīṣma uvāca|
na rocate vigraho me pāṇḍuputraiḥ kathaṃcana
|
yathaiva dhṛtarāṣṭro me tathā pāṇḍurasaṃśayam||1||

2gāndhāryāśca yathā putrāstathā kuntīsutā mama
|
yathā ca mama te rakṣyā dhṛtarāṣṭra tathā tava||2||

3yathā ca mama rājñaśca tathā duryodhanasya te
|
tathā kurūṇāṃ sarveṣāmanyeṣāmapi pārthiva||3||

4evaṃ gate vigrahaṃ tairna roce
sandhāya vīrairdīyatāmardhabhūmiḥ
|
teṣāmapīdaṃ prapitāmahānāṃ
rājyaṃ pituścaiva kurūttamānām||4||

5duryodhana yathā rājyaṃ tvamidaṃ tāta paśyasi
|
mama paitṛkamityevaṃ te'pi paśyanti pāṇḍavāḥ||5||

6yadi rājyaṃ na te prāptāḥ pāṇḍaveyā yaśasvinaḥ
|
kuta eva tavāpīdaṃ bhāratasyāpi kasyacit||6||

7adharmeṇa ca rājyaṃ tvaṃ prāptavānbharatarṣabha
|
te'pi rājyamanuprāptāḥ pūrvameveti me matiḥ||7||

8madhureṇaiva rājyasya teṣāmardhaṃ pradīyatām
|
etaddhi puruṣavyāghra hitaṃ sarvajanasya ca||8||

9ato'nyathā cetkriyate na hitaṃ no bhaviṣyati
|
tavāpyakīrtiḥ sakalā bhaviṣyati na saṃśayaḥ||9||

10kīrtirakṣaṇamātiṣṭha kīrtirhi paramaṃ balam
|
naṣṭakīrtermanuṣyasya jīvitaṃ hyaphalaṃ smṛtam||10||

11yāvatkīrtirmanuṣyasya na praṇaśyati kaurava
|
tāvajjīvati gāndhare naṣṭakīrtistu naśyati||11||

12tamimaṃ samupātiṣṭha dharmaṃ kurukulocitam
|
anurūpaṃ mahābāho pūrveṣāmātmanaḥ kuru||12||

13diṣṭyā dhriyante pārthā hi diṣṭyā jīvati sā pṛthā
|
diṣṭyā purocanaḥ pāpo na sakāmo'tyayaṃ gataḥ||13||

14yadā prabhṛti dagdhāste kuntibhojasutāsutāḥ
|
tadā prabhṛti gāndhāre na śaknomyabhivīkṣitum||14||

15loke prāṇabhṛtāṃ kiṃcicchrutvā kuntīṃ tathāgatām
|
na cāpi doṣeṇa tathā loko manyetpurocanam
|
yathā tvāṃ puruṣavyāghra loko doṣeṇa gacchati||15||

16tadidaṃ jīvitaṃ teṣāṃ tava kilbiṣanāśanam
|
saṃmantavyaṃ mahārāja pāṇḍavānāṃ ca darśanam||16||

17na cāpi teṣāṃ vīrāṇāṃ jīvatāṃ kurunandana
|
pitryoṃśaḥ śakya ādātumapi vajrabhṛtā svayam||17||

18te sarve'vasthitā dharme sarve caivaikacetasaḥ
|
adharmeṇa nirastāśca tulye rājye viśeṣataḥ||18||

19yadi dharmastvayā kāryo yadi kāryaṃ priyaṃ ca me
|
kṣemaṃ ca yadi kartavyaṃ teṣāmardhaṃ pradīyatām||19||
