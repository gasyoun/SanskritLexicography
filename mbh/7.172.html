Mahābhārata 7.172 (Droṇaparvan)

1sañjaya uvāca|
vidrutaṃ svabalaṃ dṛṣṭvā vadhyamānaṃ mahātmabhiḥ
|
krodhena mahatā''viṣṭaḥ putrastava viśāṃpate||1||

2abhyetya sahasā karṇaṃ droṇaṃ ca jayatāṃ varam
|
amarṣavaśamāpanno vākyajño vākyamabravīt||2||

3bhavadbhyāmiha saṃgrāmaḥ kruddhābhyāṃ saṃpravartitaḥ
|
āhave nihataṃ dṛṣṭvā saindhavaṃ savyasācinā||3||

4nihanyamānāṃ pāṇḍūnāṃ balena mama vāhinīm
|
bhūtvā tadvijaye śaktāvaśaktāviva paśyataḥ||4||

5yadyahaṃ bhavatostyājyo na vācyo'smi tadaiva hi
|
āvāṃ pāṇḍusutānsaṃkhye jeṣyāva iti mānadau||5||

6tadaivāhaṃ vacaḥ śrutvā bhavadbhyāmanusaṃmatam
|
nākariṣyamidaṃ pārthairvairaṃ yodhavināśanam||6||

7yadi nāhaṃ parityājyo bhavadbhyāṃ puruṣarṣabhau
|
yudhyatāmanurūpeṇa vikrameṇa suvikramau||7||

8vākpratodena tau vīrau praṇunnau tanayena te
|
prāvartayetāṃ saṃgrāmaṃ ghaṭṭitāviva pannagau||8||

9tatastau rathināṃ śreṣṭhau sarvalokadhanurdharau
|
śaineyapramukhānpārthānabhidudruvat raṇe||9||

10tathaiva sahitāḥ pārthāḥ sarvasainyena saṃvṛtāḥ
|
abhyavartanta tau vīrau nardamānau muhurmuhuḥ||10||

11atha droṇo maheṣvāso daśabhiḥ śinipuṅgavam
|
avidhyattvaritaṃ kruddhaḥ sarvaśastrabhṛtāṃ varaḥ||11||

12karṇaśca daśabhirbāṇaiḥ putraśca tava saptabhiḥ
|
daśabhirvṛṣasenaśca saubalaścāpi saptabhiḥ||12||

13ete kaurava saṃkrande śaineyaṃ paryavākiran
|
dṛṣṭvā ca samare droṇaṃ nighnantaṃ pāṇḍavīṃ camūm||13||

14vivyadhuḥ somakāstūrṇaṃ samantāccharavṛṣṭibhiḥ
|
tatra droṇo'haratprāṇānkṣatriyāṇāṃ viśāṃpate||14||

15raśmibhirbhāskaro rājaṃstamāṃsīva samantataḥ
|
droṇena vadhyamānānāṃ pañcālānāṃ viśāṃpate||15||

16śuśruve tumulaḥ śabdaḥ krośatāmitaretaram
|
putrānanye pitṝnanye bhrātṝnanye ca mātulān||16||

17bhāgineyān vayasyāṃśca tathā sambandhibāndhavān
|
utsṛjyotsṛjya gacchanti tvaritā jīvitepsavaḥ||17||

18apare mohitā mohāttamevābhimukhā yayuḥ
|
pāṇḍavānāṃ raṇe yodhāḥ paralokaṃ gatāḥ pare||18||

19sā tathā pāṇḍavī senā pīḍyamānā mahātmanā
|
niśi saṃprādravadrājannutsṛjyolkāḥ sahasraśaḥ||19||

20paśyato bhīmasenasya vijayasyācyutasya ca
|
yamayordharmaputrasya pārṣatasya ca paśyataḥ||20||

21tamasā saṃvṛte loke na prājñāyata kiṃcana
|
kauravāṇāṃ prakāśena dṛśyante vidrutāḥ pare||21||

22dravamāṇaṃ tu tatsainyaṃ droṇakarṇau mahārathau
|
jaghnatuḥ pṛṣṭhato rājankirantau sāyakānbahūn||22||

23pañcāleṣu prabhagneṣu kṣīyamāṇeṣu sarvataḥ
|
janārdano dīnamanāḥ pratyabhāṣata phālgunam ||23||

24droṇakarṇau maheṣvāsāvetau pārṣatasātyakī
|
pañcālāṃścaiva sahitau jaghnatuḥ sāyakairbhṛśam
|
||24||

25etayoḥ śaravarṣeṇa prabhagnā no mahārathāḥ
|
vāryamāṇā'pi kaunteya pṛtanā nāvatiṣṭhate||25||

26tāṃ tu vidravatīṃ dṛṣṭvā ūcatuḥ keśavārjunau
|
mā vidravata vitrastā bhayaṃ tyajata pāṇḍavāḥ||26||

27tāvāvāṃ sarvasainyaiśca vyūhaiḥ samyagudāyudhaiḥ
|
droṇaṃ ca sūtaputraṃ ca prayatāvaḥ prabādhitum ||27||

28etau hi balinau śūrau kṛtāstrau jitakāśinau
|
upekṣitau tava balairnāśayetāṃ niśāmimām ||28||

29tayoḥ saṃvada‌torevaṃ bhīmakarmā mahābalaḥ
|
āyādvṛkodaraḥ śīghraṃ punarāvartya vāhinīm||29||

30vṛkodaramathāyāntaṃ dṛṣṭvā tatra janārdanaḥ
|
punarevābravīdrājanharṣayanniva pāṇḍavam||30||

31eṣa bhīmo raṇaślāghī vṛtaḥ somakapāṇḍavaiḥ
|
abhyavartata vegena droṇakarṇau mahārathau||31||

32etena sahito yuddhya pañcālaiśca mahārathaiḥ
|
āśvāsanārthaṃ sainyānāṃ sarveṣāṃ pāṇḍunandana||32||

33tatastau puruṣavyāghrāvubhau mādhavapāṇḍavau
|
droṇakarṇau samāsādya dhiṣṭhitau raṇamūrdhani||33||

34sañjaya uvāca|
tatastatpunarāvṛttaṃ yudhiṣṭhirabalaṃ mahat
|
tato droṇaśca karṇaśca parānmamṛdaturyudhi||34||

35sa saṃprahārastumulo niśi pratyabhavanmahān
|
yathā sāgarayo rājaṃścandrodayavivṛddhayoḥ||35||

36tata utsṛjya pāṇibhyāṃ pradīpāṃstava vāhinī
|
yuyudhe pāṇḍavaiḥ sārdhamunmattavadasaṃkulā||36||

37rajasā tamasā caiva saṃvṛte bhṛśadāruṇe
|
kevalaṃ nāmagotreṇa prāyudhyanta jayaiṣiṇaḥ||37||

38aśrūyanta hi nāmāni śrāvyamāṇāni pārthivaiḥ
|
praharadbhirmahārāja svayaṃvara ivāhave||38||

39niḥśabda‌māsītsahasā punaḥ śabdo mahānabhūt
|
kruddhānāṃ yudhyamānānāṃ jīyatāṃ jayatāmapi||39||

40yatra yatra sma dṛśyante pradīpāḥ kurusattama
|
tatra tatra sma śūrāste nipatanti pataṅgavat||40||

41tathā saṃyudhyamānānāṃ vigāḍhāsīnmahāniśā
|
pāṇḍavānāṃ ca rājendra kauravāṇāṃ ca sarvaśaḥ||41||
