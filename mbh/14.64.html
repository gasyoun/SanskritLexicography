Mahābhārata 14.64 (Āśvamedhikaparvan)

1vaiśampāyana uvāca|
tataste prayayurhaṣṭāḥ prahṛṣṭanaravāhanāḥ
|
rathaghoṣeṇa mahatā pūrayanto vasundharām||1||

2saṃstūyamānāḥ stutibhiḥ sūtamāgadhavandibhiḥ
|
svena sainyena saṃvītā yathādityāḥ svaraśmibhiḥ||2||

3pāṇḍureṇātapatreṇa dhriyamāṇena mūrdhani
|
babhau yudhiṣṭhirastatra paurṇamāsyāmivoḍurāṭ||3||

4jayāśiṣaḥ prahṛṣṭānāṃ narāṇāṃ pathi pāṇḍavaḥ
|
pratyagṛhādyathānyāyaṃ yathāvatpuruṣarṣabhaḥ||4||

5tathaiva sainikā rājanrājānamanuyānti ye
|
teṣāṃ halahalāśabdo divaṃ stabdhvā vyatiṣṭhata||5||

6sarāṃsi saritaścaiva vanānyupavanāni ca
|
atyakrāmanmahārājo giriṃ cāpyanvapadyata||6||

7tasmindeśe ca rājendra yatra taddravyamuttamam
|
cakre niveśanaṃ rājā pāṇḍavaḥ saha sainikaiḥ
|
śive deśe same caiva tadā bharatasattama||7||

8agrato brāhmaṇānkṛtvā tapovidyādamānvitān
|
purohitaṃ ca kauravya vedavedāṅgapāragam
|
āgniveśyaṃ ca rājāno brāhmaṇāḥ sapurodhasaḥ||8||

9kṛtvā śāntiṃ yathānyāyaṃ sarvaśaḥ paryavārayan
|
kṛtvā tu madhye rājānamamātyāṃśca yathāvidhi||9||

10ṣaṭpadaṃ navasaṅkhyānaṃ niveśaṃ cakrire dvijāḥ
|
mattānāṃ vāraṇendrāṇāṃ niveśaṃ ca yathāvidhi||10||

11kārayitvā sa rājendro brāhmaṇanidamabravīt
|
asminkārye dvijaśreṣṭhā nakṣatre divase śubhe||11||

12yathā bhavanto manyante kartumarhanti tattathā
|
na naḥ kālātyayo vai syādihaiva parilambatām||12||

13iti niścitya viprendrāḥ kriyatāṃ yadanantaram
|
śrutvaitadvacanaṃ rājño brāhmaṇāḥ sapurodhasaḥ
|
idamūcurvaco hṛṣṭā dharmarājapriyepsavaḥ||13||

14adyaiva nakṣatramahaśca puṇyaṃ yatāmahe śreṣṭhatamakriyāsu
|
ambhobhiradyeha vasāma rājannupoṣyatāṃ cāpi bhavadbhiradya||14||

15śrutvā tu teṣāṃ dvijaśattamānāṃ kṛtopavāsā rajanīṃ narendraḥ
|
ūṣuḥ pratītāḥ kuśasaṃstareṣu yathādhvare prajvalitā hutāśāḥ||15||

16tato niśā sā vyagamanmahātmanāṃ saṃśṛṇvatāṃ viprasamīritā giraḥ
|
tataḥ prabhāte vimale dvijarṣabhā vaco'bruvandharmasutaṃ narādhipam||16||
