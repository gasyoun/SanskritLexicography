Mahābhārata 7.12 (Droṇaparvan)

1sañjaya uvāca|
hanta te kathayiṣyāmi sarva pratyakṣadarśivān
|
yathā sa nyapataddroṇaḥ sūditaḥ pāṇḍusṛñjayai||1||

2senāpatitvaṃ saṃprāpya bhāradvājo mahārathaḥ
|
madhye sarvasya sainyasya putraṃ te vākyamabravīt||2||

3yat kauravāṇāmṛṣabhādāpageyādanantaram
|
saināpatyena yadrājan māmadya kṛtavānasi||3||

4sadṛśaṃ karmaṇastasya phalaṃ prāpnuhi bhārata
|
karomi kāmaṃ kaṃ te'dya pravṛṇīṣva yamicchasi||4||

5tato duryodhano rājā karṇaduḥśāsanādibhiḥ
|
saṃmantryovāca durdharṣamācāryaṃ jayatāṃ varam||5||

6dadāsi cedvaraṃ mahyaṃ jīvagrāhaṃ yudhiṣṭhiram
|
gṛhītvā rathināṃ śreṣṭhaṃ matsamīpamihānaya||6||

7tataḥ kurūṇāmācāryaḥ śrutvā putrasya te vacaḥ
|
senāṃ praharṣayan sarvāmidaṃ vacanamabravīt||7||

8dhanyaḥ kuntīsuto rājan yasya grahaṇamicchasi
|
na vadhārthaṃ sudurdharṣaṃ varamadya prayācase||8||

9kimarthaṃ ca naravyāghra na vadhaṃ tasya kāṃkṣase
|
nāśaṃsasi kriyāmetāṃ matto duryodhana dhruvam||9||

10āhosvit dharmarājasya dveṣṭā tasya na vidyate
|
yadīcchasi tvaṃ jīvantaṃ kulaṃ rakṣasi cātmanaḥ||10||

11athavā bharataśreṣṭha nirjitya yudhi pāṇḍavān
|
rājyaṃ saṃprati dattvā ca saubhrātraṃ kartumicchasi||11||

12dhanyaḥ kuntīsuto rājā sujātaṃ cāsya dhīmataḥ
|
ajātaśatrutā satyā tasya yatsnihyate bhavān||12||

13droṇena caivamuktasya tava putrasya bhārata
|
sahasā niḥsṛto bhāvo yo'sya nityaṃ hṛdi sthitaḥ||13||

14nākāro gūhituṃ śakyo bṛhaspatisamairapi
|
tasmāttava suto rājan prahṛṣṭo vākyamabravīt||14||

15vadhe kuntisutasyājau nācārya vijayo mama
|
hate yudhiṣṭhire pārthā hanyuḥ sarvān hi no dhruvam||15||

16na ca śakyā raṇe sarve nihantumamarairapi
|
ya eva teṣāṃ śeṣaḥ syātsa evāsmānna śeṣayet||16||

17satyapratijñe tvānīte punardyūtena nirjite
|
punaryāsyantyaraṇyāya pāṇḍavāstamanuvratāḥ||17||

18so'yaṃ mama jayo vyaktaṃ dīrghakālaṃ bhaviṣyati
|
ato na vadhamicchāmi dharmarājasya karhicit||18||

19tasya jihmamabhiprāyaṃ jñātvā droṇorthatattvavit
|
taṃ varaṃ sāntaraṃ tasmai dadau sañcintya buddhimān||19||

20droṇa uvāca|
na cedyudhiṣṭhiraṃ vīraḥ pālayatyarjuno yudhi
|
manyasva pāṇḍavaśreṣṭhamānītaṃ vaśamātmanaḥ||20||

21na hi śakyo raṇe pāṃthaḥ sendrairdevāsurairapi
|
pratyudyātumatastāta naitadāmarṣayāmyaham||21||

22asaṃśayaṃ sa me śiṣyo matpūrvaścāstrakarmaṇi
|
taruṇaḥ sukṛtairyukta ekāyanagataśca ha||22||

23astrāṇīndrācca rudrācca bhūyaḥ sa samavāptavān
|
amarṣitaśca te rājaṃstato nāmarṣayāmyaham||23||

24sa cāpakramyatāṃ yuddhādyenopāyena śakyate
|
apanīte tataḥ pārthe dharmarājo jitastvayā||24||

25grahaṇe hi jayastasya na vadhe puruṣarṣabha
|
etena cāpyupāyena grahaṇaṃ samupaiṣyasi||25||

26ahaṃ gṛhītvā rājānaṃ satyadharmaparāyaṇam
|
ānayiṣyāmi te rājan vaśamadya na saṃśayaḥ||26||

27yadi sthāsyati saṃgrāme muhūrtamapi me'grataḥ
|
apanīte naravyāghre kuntīputre dhanañjaye||27||

28phālgunasya samīpe tu na hi śakyo yudhiṣṭhiraḥ
|
grahītuṃ samare rājan sendrairapi surāsuraiḥ||28||

29sañjaya uvāca|
sāntaraṃ tu pratijñāte rājño droṇena nigrahe
|
gṛhītaṃ tamamanyanta tava putrāḥ subāliśāḥ||29||

30pāṇḍaveyeṣu sākṣepaṃ droṇaṃ jānāti te sutaḥ
|
tataḥ pratijñāsthairyārthaṃ sa mantro bahulīkṛtaḥ||30||

31tato duryodhanenāpi grahaṇaṃ pāṇḍavasya tat
|
sainyasthāneṣu sarveṣu sughoṣitamarindama||31||
