Mahābhārata 8.95 (Karṇaparvan)

1sañjaya uvāca|
hate vaikartane rājan kuravo bhayapīḍitāḥ
|
vīkṣamāṇā diśaḥ sarvāḥ paryāpetuḥ sahasraśaḥ ||1||

2karṇaṃ tu nihataṃ dṛṣṭvā śatrubhiḥ paramāhave
|
bhītā diśo vyakīryanta tāvakāḥ kṣatavikṣatāḥ ||2||

3tato'vahāraṃ cakruste yodhāḥ sarve samantataḥ
|
nivāryamāṇāścodvignāstāvakā bhṛśaduḥkhitāḥ ||3||

4teṣāṃ tanmatamājñāya putro duryodhanastava
|
avahāraṃ tataścakre śalyasyānumate nṛpa ||4||

5kṛtavarmā rathaistūrṇaṃ vṛto bhārata tāvakaiḥ
|
nārāyaṇāvaśeṣaiśca śibirāyaiva dudruve ||5||

6gāndhārāṇāṃ sahastreṇa śakuniḥ parivāritaḥ
|
hatamādhirathiṃ dṛṣṭvā śibirāyaiva dudruve ||6||

7kṛpaḥ śāradvato rājannāgānīkena bhārata
|
mahāmeghanibhenānu śibirāyaiva dudruve ||7||

8aśvatthāmā tataḥ śūro viniḥśvasya punaḥ punaḥ
|
pāṇḍavānāṃ jayaṃ dṛṣṭvā śibirāyaiva dudruve ||8||

9saṃśaptakāvaśiṣṭena balena mahatā vṛtaḥ
|
suśarmāpi yayau rājan vīkṣamāṇo bhayārditaḥ ||9||

10duryodhano'pi nṛpatirhatasarvasvabāndhavaḥ
|
yayau śokasamāviṣṭaścintayanvimanā bahu ||10||

11chinnadhvajena śalyastu rathena rathināṃ varaḥ
|
prayayau śibirāyaiva vīkṣamāṇo diśo daśa ||11||

12tato'pare subahavo bhāratānāṃ mahārathāḥ
|
prādravanta bhayatrastā hriyāviṣṭā vicetasaḥ ||12||

13asṛk kṣarantaḥ sodvignā vepamānāstathāturāḥ
|
kuravo dudruvuḥ sarve dṛṣṭvā karṇaṃ nipātitam ||13||

14praśaṃsanto'rjunaṃ kecitkecitkarṇaṃ mahārathāḥ
|
vyadravanta diśo bhītāḥ kuravaḥ kurusattama ||14||

15teṣāṃ yodhasahasrāṇāṃ tāvakānāṃ mahāmṛdhe
|
nāsīttatra pumān kaścidyo yuddhāya mano dadhe ||15||

16hate karṇe mahārāja nirāśāḥ kuravo'bhavan
|
jīviteṣvapi rājyeṣu dāreṣu ca dhaneṣu ca ||16||

17tānsamānīya putraste yatnena mahatā vibhuḥ
|
niveśāya mano dadhre duḥkhaśokasamanvitaḥ ||17||

18tasyājñāṃ śirasā yodhāḥ parigṛhya viśāṃpate
|
vivarṇavadanā rājannyaviśanta mahārathāḥ ||18||
