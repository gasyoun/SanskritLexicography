Mahābhārata 13.82 (Anuśāsanaparvan)

1yudhiṣṭhira uvāca|
mayā gavāṃ purīṣaṃ vai śriyā juṣṭamiti śrutam
|
etadicchāmyahaṃ śrotuṃ saṃśayo'tra pitāmaha ||1||

2bhīṣma uvāca|
atrāpyudāharantīmamitihāsaṃ purātanam
|
gobhirnṛpeha saṃvādaṃ śriyā bharatasattama ||2||

3śrīḥ kṛtveha vapuḥ kāntaṃ gomadhyeṣu viveśa ha
|
gāvo'tha vismitāstasyā dṛṣṭvā rūpasya saṃpadam ||3||

4gāva ūcuḥ |
kā'si devi kuto vā tvaṃ rūpeṇāpratimā bhuvi
|
vismitāḥ sma mahābhāge tava rūpasya sampadā ||4||

5icchāma tvāṃ vayaṃ jñātuṃ kā tvaṃ kva ca gamiṣyasi
|
tattvena varavarṇā me sarvametadbravīhi naḥ ||5||

6śrīruvāca|
lokakāntāsmi bhadraṃ vaḥ śrīrnāmā'haṃ pariśrutā
|
mayā daityāḥ parityaktā vinaṣṭāḥ śāśvatīḥ samāḥ ||6||

7mayā'bhipannā devāśca modante śāśvatīḥ samāḥ
|
indro vivasvānsomaśca viṣṇurāpo'gnireva ca ||7||

8mayābhipannāḥ sidhyante ṛṣayo devatāstathā
|
yānnāviśāmyahaṃ gāvaste vinaśyanti sarvaśaḥ ||8||

9dharmaścārthaśca kāmaśca mayā juṣṭāḥ sukhānvitāḥ
|
evaṃprabhāvāṃ māṃ gāvo vijānīta sukhapradāḥ ||9||

10icchāmi cāpi yuṣmāsu vastuṃ sarvāsu nityadā
|
āgatya prārthaye yuṣmāñchrījuṣṭā bhavatā'tha vai ||10||

11gāva ūcuḥ |
adhruvā capalā ca tvaṃ sāmānyā bahubhiḥ saha
|
na tvāmicchāma bhadraṃ te gamyatāṃ yatra raṃsyase ||11||

12vapuṣmantyo vayaṃ sarvāḥ kimasmākaṃ tvayādya vai
|
yatheṣṭaṃ gamyatāṃ tatra kṛtakāryā vayaṃ tvayā ||12||

13śrīruvāca|
kimetadvaḥ kṣamaṃ gāvo yanmāṃ nehābhinandatha
|
na māṃ saṃprati gṛhṇīdhvaṃ kasmādvai durlabhāṃ satīm ||13||

14satyaṃ ca lokavādo'yaṃ loke carati suvratāḥ
|
svayaṃ prāpte paribhavo bhavatīti viniścayaḥ ||14||

15mahadugraṃ tapaḥ kṛtvā māṃ niṣevanti mānavāḥ
|
devadānavagandharvāḥ piśācoragarākṣasāḥ ||15||

16prabhāva eṣa vo gāvaḥ pratigṛhṇīta māmiha
|
nāvamanyā hyahaṃ saumyāstrailokye sacarācare ||16||

17gāva ūcuḥ |
nāvamanyāmahe devi na tvāṃ paribhavāmahe
|
adhruvā calacittā'si tatastvāṃ varjayāmahe ||17||

18bahunā ca kimuktena gamyatāṃ yatra vāṃcchasi
|
vapuṣmantyo vayaṃ sarvāḥ kimasmākaṃ tvayā'naghe ||18||

19śrīruvāca|
avajñātā bhaviṣyāmi sarvalokasya mānadāḥ
|
pratyākhyānena yuṣmākaṃ prasādaḥ kriyatāṃ mama ||19||

20mahābhāgā bhavatyo vai śaraṇyāḥ śaraṇāgatām
|
paritrāyantu māṃ nityaṃ bhajamānāmaninditām ||20||

21mānanāmahamicchāmi bhavatyaḥ satataṃ śivāḥ
|
apyekāṅgeṣvadho vastumicchāmi ca sukutsite ||21||

22na vo'sti kutsitaṃ kiñcidaṅgeṣvālakṣyatenaghāḥ
|
puṇyāḥ pavitrāḥ subhagā mamādeśaṃ prayacchatha ||22||

23vaseyaṃ yatra vo dehe tanme vyākhyātumarhatha
|
evamuktāstato gāvaḥ śubhāḥ karuṇavatsalāḥ
|
saṃmantrya sahitāḥ sarvāḥ śriyamūcurnarādhipa ||23||

24avaśyaṃ mānanā kāryā tavāsmābhiryaśasvini
|
śakṛnmūtre nivasa tvaṃ puṇyametaddhi naḥ śubhe ||24||

25śrīruvāca|
diṣṭyā prasādo yuṣmābhiḥ kṛto me'nugrahātmakaḥ
|
evaṃ bhavatu bhadraṃ vaḥ pūjitāsmi sukhapradāḥ ||25||

26evaṃ kṛtvā tu samayaṃ śrīrgobhiḥ saha bhārata
|
paśyantīnāṃ tatastāsāṃ tatraivāntaradhīyata ||26||

27evaṃ gośakṛtaḥ putra māhātmyaṃ te'nuvarṇitam
|
māhātmyaṃ ca gavāṃ bhūyaḥ śrūyatāṃ gadato mama ||27||
