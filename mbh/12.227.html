Mahābhārata 12.227 (Śāntiparvan)

1yudhiṣṭhira uvāca|
magnasya vyasane kṛcchre kiṃ śreyaḥ puruṣasya hi
|
bandhunāśe mahīpāla rājyanāśe'thavā punaḥ ||1||

2tvaṃ hi naḥ paramo vaktā loke'smin bharatarṣabha
|
etadbhavantaṃ pṛcchāmi tanme tvaṃ vaktumarhasi ||2||

3bhīṣma uvāca|
putradāraiḥ sukhaiścaiva viyuktasya dhanena vā
|
magnasya vyasane kṛcchre dhṛtiḥ śreyaskarī nṛpa ||3||

4dhairyeṇa yuktasya sataḥ śarīraṃ na viśīryate
|
viśokatā sukhaṃ dhatte dhatte cārogyamuttamam ||4||

5ārogyācca śarīrasya sa punarvindate śriyam
|
yacca prājño narastāta sāttvikīṃ vṛttimāsthitaḥ ||5||

6tasyaiśvaryaṃ ca dhairyaṃ ca vyavasāyaśca karmasu
|
atraivodāharantīmamitihāsaṃ purātanam ||6||

7balivāsavasaṃvādaṃ punareva yudhiṣṭhira
|
vṛtte devāsure yuddhe daityadānavasaṃkṣaye ||7||

8viṣṇukrānteṣu lokeṣu devarāje śatakratau
|
ijyamāneṣu deveṣu cāturvarṇye vyavasthite ||8||

9samṛddhamātre trailokye prītiyukte svayambhuvi
|
rudrairvasubhirādityairaśvibhyāmapi carṣibhiḥ ||9||

10gandharvairbhujagendraiśca siddhaiścānyairvṛtaḥ prabhuḥ
|
caturdantaṃ sudāntaṃ ca vāraṇendraṃ śriyā vṛtam
|
āruhyairāvataṃ śakrastrailokyamanusaṃyayau ||10||

11sa kadācitsamudrānte kasmiṃścidgirigahvare
|
baliṃ vairocaniṃ vajrī dadarśopasasarpa ca ||11||

12tamairāvatamūrdhasthaṃ prekṣya devagaṇairvṛtam
|
surendramindraṃ daityendro na śuśoca na vivyathe ||12||

13dṛṣṭvā tamavikārasthaṃ tiṣṭhantaṃ nirbhayaṃ balim
|
adhirūḍho dvipaśreṣṭhamityuvāca śatakratuḥ ||13||

14daitya na vyathase śauryādathavā vṛddhasevayā
|
tapasā bhāvitatvādvā sarvathaitatsuduṣkaram ||14||

15śatrubhirvaśamānīto hīnasthānādanuttamāt
|
vairocane kimāśritya śocitavye na śocasi ||15||

16śraiṣṭhyaṃ prāpya svajātīnāṃ mahābhogānanuttamān
|
hatasvaratnarājyastvaṃ brūhi kasmānna śocasi ||16||

17īśvaro hi purā bhūtvā pitṛpaitāmahe pade
|
tattvamadya hṛtaṃ dṛṣṭvā sapatnaiḥ kiṃ na śocasi ||17||

18baddhaśca vāruṇaiḥ pāśairvajreṇa ca samāhataḥ
|
hṛtadāro hṛtadhano brūhi kasmānna śocasi ||18||

19naṣṭaśrīrvibhavabhraṣṭo yanna śocasi duṣkaram
|
trailokyarājyanāśe hi ko'nyo jīvitumutsahet ||19||

20etaccānyacca paruṣaṃ bruvantaṃ paribhūya tam
|
śrutvā sukhamasambhrānto balirvairocano'bravīt ||20||

21baliruvāca |
nigṛhīte mayi bhṛśaṃ śakra kiṃ katthitena te
|
vajramudyamya tiṣṭhantaṃ paśyāmi tvāṃ purandara ||21||

22aśaktaḥ pūrvamāsīstvaṃ kathañcicchaktatāṃ gataḥ
|
kastvadanya imāṃ vācaṃ sukrūrāṃ vaktumarhati ||22||

23yastu śatrorvaśasthasya śakto'pi kurute dayām
|
hastaprāptasya vīrasya taṃ caiva puruṣaṃ viduḥ ||23||

24aniścayo hi yuddheṣu dvayorvivadamānayoḥ
|
ekaḥ prāpnoti vijayamekaścaiva parājayam ||24||

25mā ca te'bhūtsvabhāvo'yamiti te devapuṅgava
|
īśvaraḥ sarvabhūtānāṃ vikrameṇa jito balāt ||25||

26naitadasmatkṛtaṃ śakra naitacchakra kṛtaṃ tvayā
|
yattvamevaṃgato vajrinyadvā'pyevaṃgatā vayam ||26||

27ahamāsaṃ yathā'dya tvaṃ bhavitā tvaṃ yathā vayam
|
mā'vamaṃsthā mayā karma duṣkṛtaṃ kṛtamityuta ||27||

28sukhaduḥkhe hi puruṣaḥ paryāyeṇādhigacchati
|
paryāyeṇāsi śakratvaṃ prāptaḥ śakra na karmaṇā ||28||

29kālaḥ kāle nayati māṃ tvāṃ ca kālo nayatyayam
|
tenāhaṃ tvaṃ yathā nādyaṃ tvaṃ cāpi na yathā vayam ||29||

30na mātṛpitṛśuśrūṣā na ca daivatapūjanam
|
nānyo guṇasamācāraḥ puruṣasya sukhāvahaḥ ||30||

31na vidyā na tapo dānaṃ na mitrāṇi na bāndhavāḥ
|
śaknuvanti paritrātuṃ naraṃ kālena pīḍitam ||31||

32nāgāminamanarthaṃ hi pratighātaśatairapi
|
śaknuvanti prativyoḍhumṛte buddhibalānnarāḥ ||32||

33paryāyairhanyamānānāṃ paritrātā na vidyate
|
idaṃ tu duḥkhaṃ yacchakra kartāhamiti manyase ||33||

34yadi kartā bhavetkartā na kriyeta kadācana
|
yasmāttu kriyate kartā tasmātkartāpyanīśvaraḥ ||34||

35kālenāhaṃ tvāmajayaṃ kālenāhaṃ jitastvayā
|
gantā gatimatāṃ kālaḥ kālaḥ kalayati prajāḥ ||35||

36indra prākṛtayā buddhyā pralayaṃ nāvabuddhyase
|
kecittvāṃ bahu manyante śreṣṭhyaṃ prāptaṃ svakarmaṇā ||36||

37kathamasmadvidho nāma jānan lokapravṛttayaḥ
|
kālenābhyāhataḥ śocenmuhyedvā'pyatha vibhramet ||37||

38nityaṃ kālaparītasya mama vā madvidhasya vā
|
buddhirvyasanamāsādya bhinnā nauriva sīdati ||38||

39ahaṃ ca tvaṃ ca ye cānye bhaviṣyanti surādhipāḥ
|
te sarve śaka yāsyanti mārgamindraśatairgatam ||39||

40tvāmapyevaṃ sudurdharṣaṃ jvalantaṃ parayā śriyā
|
kāle pariṇate kālaḥ kālayiṣyati māmiva ||40||

41bahūnīndrasahasrāṇi daivatānāṃ yuge yuge
|
abhyatītāni kālena kālo hi duratikramaḥ ||41||

42idaṃ tu labdhvā saṃsthānamātmānaṃ bahu manyase
|
sarvabhūtabhavaṃ devaṃ brahmāṇamiva śāśvatam ||42||

43na cedamacalaṃ sthānamanantaṃ vā'pi kasyacit
|
tvaṃ tu bāliśayā buddhyā mamedamiti manyase ||43||

44aviśvaste viśvasiṣi manyase vā'dhruve dhruvam
|
nityaṃ kālaparītātmā bhavatyevaṃ sureśvara ||44||

45mameyamiti mohāttvaṃ rājaśriyamabhīpsasi
|
neyaṃ tava na cāsmākaṃ na cānyeṣāṃ sthirā sadā ||45||

46atikramya bahūnanyāṃstvayi tāvadiyaṃ gatā
|
kaṃcitkālamiyaṃ sthitvā tvayi vāsava cañcalā ||46||

47gaurnipānamivotsṛjya punaranyaṃ gamiṣyati
|
rājalokā hyatikrāntā yānna saṃkhyātumutsahe ||47||

48tvatto bahutarāścānye bhaviṣyanti purandara
|
savṛkṣauṣadhiratneyaṃ sahasattvavanākarā ||48||

49tānidānīṃ ca paśyāmi yairbhukteyaṃ purā mahī
|
pṛthurailo mayo bhīmo narakaḥ śambarastathā ||49||

50aśvagrīvaḥ pulomā ca svarbhānuramitadhvajaḥ
|
prahlādo namucirdakṣo vipracittirvirocanaḥ ||50||

51hrīniṣevaḥ suhotraśca bhūrihā puṣpavān vṛṣaḥ
|
satyeṣurṛṣabho bāhuḥ kapilāśvo virūpakaḥ ||51||

52bāṇaḥ kārtasvaro vahniviśvadaṃṣṭro'tha nairṛtiḥ
|
saṃkoco'tha varītākṣo varāhāśvo ruciprabhaḥ ||52||

53viśvajitpratirūpaśca vṛṣāṇḍo viṣkaro madhuḥ
|
hiraṇyakaśipuścaiva kaiṭabhaścaiva dānavaḥ ||53||

54daiteyā dānavāścaiva sarve te nairṛtaiḥ saha
|
ete cānye ca bahava pūrve pūrvatarāśca ye ||54||

55daityendrā dānavendrāśca yāṃścānyānanuśuśruma
|
bahavaḥ pūrvadaityendrāḥ saṃtyajya pṛthivīṃ gatāḥ ||55||

56kālenābhyāhatāḥ sarve kālo hi balavattaraḥ
|
sarvaiḥ kratuśatairiṣṭaṃ na tvamekaḥ śatakratuḥ ||56||

57sarve dharmaparāścāsan sarve satatasatriṇaḥ
|
antarikṣacarāḥ sarve sarve'bhimukhayodhinaḥ ||57||

58sarve saṃhananopetāḥ sarve parighabāhavaḥ
|
sarve māyāśatadharāḥ sarve te kāmarūpiṇaḥ ||58||

59sarve samaramāsādya na śrūyante parājitāḥ
|
sarve satyavrataparāḥ sarve kāmavihāriṇaḥ ||59||

60sarve vedavrataparāḥ sarve caiva bahuśrutāḥ
|
sarve sammatamaiśvaryamīśvarāḥ pratipedire ||60||

61na caiśvaryamadasteṣāṃ bhūtapūrvo mahātmanām
|
sarve yathārhadātāraḥ sarve vigatamatsarāḥ ||61||

62sarve sarveṣu bhūteṣu yathāvatpratipedire
|
sarve dākṣāyaṇīputrāḥ prājāpatyā mahābalāḥ ||62||

63jvalantaḥ pratapantaśca kālena pratisaṃhṛtāḥ
|
tvaṃ caivemāṃ yadā bhuktvā pṛthivīṃ tyakṣase punaḥ ||63||

64na śakṣyasi tadā śakra niyantuṃ śokamātmanaḥ
|
muñceñchāṃ kāmabhogeṣu muñcemaṃ śrībhavaṃ madam ||64||

65evaṃ svarājyanāśe tvaṃ śokaṃ saṃprasahiṣyasi
|
śokakāle śuco mā tvaṃ harṣakāle ca mā hṛṣaḥ ||65||

66atītānāgataṃ hitvā pratyutpannena vartaya
|
māṃ cedabhyāgataḥ kālaḥ sadā yuktamatandritaḥ ||66||

67kṣamasva nacirādindra tvāmapyupagamiṣyati
|
trāsayanniva devendra vāgbhistakṣasi māmiha ||67||

68saṃyate mayi nūnaṃ tvamātmānaṃ bahū manyase
|
kālaḥ prathamamāyānmāṃ paścāttvāmanudhāvati ||68||

69tena garjasi devendra pūrvaṃ kālahate mayi
|
ko hi sthātumalaṃ loke mama kruddhasya saṃyuge ||69||

70kālastu balavānprāptastena tiṣṭhasi vāsava
|
yattadvarṣasahasrāntaṃ pūrṇaṃ bhavitumarhati ||70||

71yathā me sarvagātrāṇi na susthāni mahaujasaḥ
|
ahamaindrāccyutaḥ sthānāttvamindraḥ prakṛto divi ||71||

72sucitre jīvaloke'sminnupāsyaḥ kālaparyayāt
|
kiṃ hi kṛtvā tvamindro'dya kiṃ vā kṛtvā vayaṃ cyutāḥ ||72||

73kālaḥ kartā vikartā ca sarvamanyadakāraṇam
|
nāśaṃ vināśamaiśvaryaṃ sukhaṃ duḥkhaṃ bhavābhavau ||73||

74vidvānprāpyaivamatyarthaṃ na prahṛṣyenna ca vyathet
|
tvameva hīndra vetthāsmānvedāhaṃ tvāṃ ca vāsava ||74||

75kiṃ katthase māṃ kiṃ ca tvaṃ kālena nirapatrapa
|
tvameva hi purā vettha yattadā pauruṣaṃ mama ||75||

76samareṣu ca vikrāntaṃ paryāptaṃ tannidarśanam
|
ādityāścaiva rudrāśca sādhyāśca vasubhiḥ saha ||76||

77mayā vinirjitāḥ pūrvaṃ marutaśca śacīpate
|
tvameva śakra jānāsi devāsurasamāgame ||77||

78sametā vibudhā bhagnāstarasā samare mayā
|
parvatāścāsakṛtkṣiptāḥ savanāḥ savanaukasaḥ ||78||

79saṭaṅkaśikharā bhagnāḥ samare mūrdhni te mayā
|
kiṃ nu śakyaṃ mayā kartuṃ kālo hi duratikramaḥ ||79||

80na hi tvāṃ notsahe hantuṃ savajramapi muṣṭinā
|
na ta vikramakālo'yaṃ kṣamākālo'yamāgataḥ ||80||

81tena tvāṃ marṣaye śakra durmarṣaṇatarastvayā
|
taṃ māṃ pariṇate kāle parītaṃ kālavahninā ||81||

82niyataṃ kālapāśena baddhaṃ śakra vikatthase
|
ayaṃ sa puruṣaḥ śyāmo lokasya duratikramaḥ ||82||

83baddhvā tiṣṭhati māṃ raudraḥ paśuṃ raśanayā yathā
|
lābhālābhau sukhaṃ duḥkhaṃ kāmakrodhau bhavābhavau ||83||

84vadhabandhapramokṣaṃ ca sarvaṃ kālena labhyate
|
nāhaṃ kartā na kartā tvaṃ kartā yastu sadā prabhuḥ ||84||

85so'yaṃ pacati kālo māṃ vṛkṣe phalamivāgatam
|
yānyeva puruṣaḥ kurvan sukhaiḥ kālena yujyate ||85||

86punastānyeva kurvāṇo duḥkhai kālena yujyate
|
na ca kālena kālajñaḥ spṛṣṭaḥ śocitumarhati ||86||

87tena śakra na śocāmi nāsti śoke sahāyatā
|
yadā hi śocataḥ śoko vyasanaṃ nāpakarṣati ||87||

88sāmarthyaṃ śocato nāstītyato'haṃ nādya śocimi
|
evamuktaḥ sahasrākṣo bhagavānpākaśāsanaḥ ||88||

89pratisaṃhṛtya saṃrambhamityuvāca śatakratuḥ
|
savajramudyataṃ bāhuṃ dṛṣṭvā pāśāṃśca vāruṇān ||89||

90kasyeha na vyathedbuddhirmṛtyorapi jighāṃsataḥ
|
sā te na vyathate buddhiracalā tattvadarśinī ||90||

91dhruvaṃ na vyathase'dya tvaṃ dhairyātsatyaparākrama
|
ko hi viśvāsamartheṣu śarīre vā śarīrabhṛt ||91||

92kartumutsahate loke dṛṣṭvā samprasthitaṃ jagat
|
ahamapyevamevainaṃ lokaṃ jānāmyaśāśvatam ||92||

93kālāgnāvāhitaṃ ghore guhye satatage'kṣare
|
na cātra parihāro'sti kālaspṛṣṭasya kasyacit ||93||

94sūkṣmāṇāṃ mahatāṃ caiva bhūtānāṃ paripacyatām
|
anīśasyāpramattasya bhūtāni pacataḥ sadā ||94||

95anivṛttasya kālasya kṣayaṃ prāpto na mucyate
|
apramattaḥ pramatteṣu kālo jāgarti dehiṣu ||95||

96prayatnenāpyapakrānto dṛṣṭapūrvo na kenacit
|
purāṇaḥ śāśvato dharmaḥ sarvaprāṇabhṛtāṃ samaḥ ||96||

97kālo na parihāryaśca na cāsyāsti vyatikramaḥ
|
ahorātrāṃśca māsāṃśca kṣaṇānkāṣṭhā lavānkalāḥ ||97||

98sampīḍayati yaḥ kālo vṛddhiṃ vārdhuṣiko yathā
|
idamadya kariṣyāmi śvaḥ kartāsmīti vādinam ||98||

99kālo harati samprāpto nadīvega iva dumam
|
idānīṃ tāvadevāsau mayā dṛṣṭaḥ kathaṃ mṛtaḥ ||99||

100iti kālena hriyatāṃ pralāpaḥ śrūyate nṛṇām
|
naśyantyarthāstathā bhogāḥ sthānamaiśvaryameva ca ||100||

101jīvitaṃ jīvalokasya kālenāgamya nīyate
|
ucchrāyā vinipātāntā bhāvo'bhāvaḥ sa eva ca ||101||

102anityamadhruvaṃ sarvaṃ vyavasāyo hi duṣkaraḥ
|
sā te na vyathate buddhiracalā tattvadarśinī ||102||

103ahamāsaṃ purā ceti manasāpi na buddhyate
|
kālenākramya loke'smin pacyamāne balīyasā ||103||

104ajyeṣṭhamakaniṣṭhaṃ ca kṣipyamāṇo na buddhyate
|
īrṣyābhimānalobheṣu kāmakrodhabhayeṣu ca ||104||

105spṛhāmohābhimāneṣu lokaḥ sakto vimuhyati
|
bhavāṃstu bhāvatattvajño vidvān jñānatapo'nvitaḥ ||105||

106kālaṃ paśyati suvyaktaṃ pāṇāvāmalakaṃ yathā
|
kālacāritratattvajñaḥ sarvaśāstraviśāradaḥ ||106||

107vivecane kṛtātmāsi spṛhaṇīyo vijānatām
|
sarvaloko hyayaṃ manye buddhyā parigatastvayā ||107||

108viharansarvato mukto na kvacitpariṣajjate
|
rajaśca hi tamaśca tvāṃ spṛśate na jitendriyam ||108||

109niṣprītiṃ naṣṭasantāpamātmānaṃ tvamupāsase
|
suhṛdaṃ sarvabhūtānāṃ nirvairaṃ śāntamānasam ||109||

110dṛṣṭvā tvāṃ mama saṃjātā tvayyanukrośinī matiḥ
|
nāhametādṛśaṃ buddhaṃ hantumicchāmi bandhane ||110||

111ānṛśaṃsyaṃ paro dharmo hyanukrośaśca me tvayi
|
mokṣyante vāruṇāḥ pāśāstaveme kālaparyayāt ||111||

112prajānāmupacāreṇa svasti te'stu mahāsura
|
yadā śvaśrūṃ snuṣā vṛddhāṃ paricāreṇa yokṣyate ||112||

113putraśca pitaraṃ mohātpreṣayiṣyati karmasu
|
brāhmaṇaiḥ kārayiṣyanti vṛṣalāḥ pādadhāvanam ||113||

114śūdrāśca brāhmaṇīṃ bhāryāmupayāsyanti nirbhayāḥ
|
viyoniṣu vimokṣyanti bījāni puruṣā tadā ||114||

115saṃkaraṃ kāṃsyabhāṇḍaiśca baliṃ caiva kupātrakaiḥ
|
cāturvarṇyaṃ yadā kṛtsnamamaryādaṃ bhaviṣyati ||115||

116ekaikaste tadā pāśaḥ kramaśaḥ parimokṣyate
|
asmattaste bhayaṃ nāsti samayaṃ pratipālaya
|
sukhī bhava nirābādhaḥ svasthacetā nirāmayaḥ ||116||

117tamevamuktvā bhagavāñchatakratuḥ pratiprayāto gajarājavāhanaḥ
|
vijitya sarvānasurānsurādhipo nananda harṣeṇa babhūva caikarāṭ ||117||

118maharṣayastuṣṭuvurañjasā ca taṃ vṛṣākapiṃ sarvacarācareśvaram
|
himāpaho havyamuvāha cādhvare tathā'mṛtaṃ cārpitamīśvaro'pi hi ||118||

119dvijottamaiḥ sarvagatairabhiṣṭuto vidīptatejā gatamanyurīśvaraḥ
|
praśāntacetā muditaḥ svamālayaṃ triviṣṭapaṃ prāpya mumoda vāsavaḥ ||119||
