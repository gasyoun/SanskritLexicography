Mahābhārata 3.302 (Āraṇyakaparvan)

1karṇa uvāca|
bhagavantamahaṃ bhakto yathā māṃ vettha gopate
|
tathā paramatigmāṃśo nāstyadeyaṃ kathañcana||1||

2na me dārā na me putrā na cātmā suhṛdo na ca
|
tatheṣṭhā vai sadā bhaktyā yathā tvaṃ gopate mama||2||

3iṣṭānāṃ ca mahātmāno bhaktānāṃ ca na saṃśayaḥ
|
kurvanti bhaktimiṣṭāṃ ca jānīṣe tvaṃ ca bhāskara||3||

4iṣṭo bhaktaśca me karṇo na cānyaddaivataṃ divi
|
jānīta iti vai kṛtvā bhagavānāha maddhitam||4||

5bhūyaśca śirasā yāce prasādya ca punaḥ punaḥ
|
iti bravīmi tigmāṃśo tvaṃ tu me kṣantumarhasi||5||

6bibhemi na tathā mṛtyoryathā bibhye'nṛtādaham
|
viśeṣeṇa dvijātīnāṃ sarveṣāṃ sarvadā satām||6||

7pradāne jīvitasyāpi na me'trāsti vicāraṇā
|
yacca māmāttha deva tvaṃ pāṇḍavaṃ phālgunaṃ prati||7||

8vyetu santāpajaṃ duḥkhaṃ tava bhāskara mānasam
|
arjunaṃ prati māṃ caiva vijeṣyāmi raṇe'rjunam||8||

9tavāpi viditaṃ deva mamāpyastrabalaṃ mahat
|
jāmadagnyādupāttaṃ yat tathā droṇānmahātmanaḥ||9||

10idaṃ tvamanujānīhi suraśreṣṭha vrataṃ mama
|
bhikṣate vājriṇe dadyāmapi jīvitamātmanaḥ||10||

11sūrya uvāca|
yadi tāta dadāsyete vajriṇe kuṇḍale śubhe
|
tvamapyenamatho brūyā vijayārthaṃ mahābalam||11||

12niyamena pradadyāṃ te kuṇḍale vai śatakrato
|
avadhyo hyasi bhūtānāṃ kuṇḍalābhyāṃ samanvitaḥ||12||

13arjunena vināśaṃ hi tava dānavasūdanaḥ
|
prārthayāno raṇe vatsa kuṇḍale te jihīrṣati||13||

14sa tvamapyenamārādhya sūnṛtābhiḥ punaḥ punaḥ
|
abhyarthayethā deveśamamoghārthaṃ purandaram||14||

15amoghāṃ dehi me śaktimamitravinibarhiṇīm
|
dāsyāmi te sahasrākṣa kuṇḍale varma cottamam||15||

16ityeva niyamena tvaṃ dadyāḥ śakrāya kuṇḍale
|
tayā tvaṃ karṇa saṅgrāme haniṣyāsa raṇe ripūn||16||

17nāhatvā hi mahābāho śatrūneti karaṃ punaḥ
|
sā śaktirdevarājasya śataśo'tha sahasraśaḥ||17||

18vaiśampāyana uvāca|
evamuktvā sahastrāṃśuḥ sahasā'ntaradhīyata
|
tataḥ sūryāya japyānte karṇaḥ svapnaṃ nyavedayat||18||

19yathā dṛṣṭaṃ yathātatvaṃ yathoktamubhayorniśi
|
tatsarvamānupūrvyeṇa śaśaṃsāsmai vṛṣastadā||19||

20tacchrutvā bhagavān devo bhānuḥ svarbhānusūdanaḥ
|
uvāca taṃ tathetyeva karṇaṃ sūryaḥ smayanniva||20||

21tatastattvamiti jñātvā rādheyaḥ paravīrahā
|
śaktimevābhikāṅkṣan vai vāsavaṃ pratyapālayat||21||
