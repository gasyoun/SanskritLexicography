Mahābhārata 15.36 (Āśramavāsikaparvan)

1janamejaya uvāca|
dṛṣṭvā putrāṃstathā pautrān sānubandhānjanādhipaḥ
|
dhṛtarāṣṭraḥ kimakarodrājā caiva yudhiṣṭhiraḥ||1||

2vaiśampāyana uvāca|
taddṛṣṭvā mahadāścaryaṃ putrāṇāṃ darśanaṃ nṛpa
|
vītaśokaḥ sa rājarṣiḥ punarāśramamāgamat||2||

3itarastu janaḥ sarvaste caiva paramarṣayaḥ
|
pratijagmuryathākāmaṃ dhṛtarāṣṭrābhyanujñayā||3||

4pāṇḍavāstu mahātmāno laghubhūyiṣṭhasainikāḥ
|
punarjagmurmahātmānaṃ sadārāstaṃ mahīpatim||4||

5tatrāśramapadaṃ dhīmān brahmarṣirlokapūjitaḥ
|
muniḥ satyavatīputro dhṛtarāṣṭramabhāṣata||5||

6dhṛtarāṣṭra mahābāho śṛṇu kauravanandana
|
śrutaṃ te jñānavṛddhānāmṛṣīṇāṃ puṇyakarmaṇām||6||

7śraddhābhijanavṛddhānāṃ vedavedāṅgavedinām
|
dharmajñānāṃ purāṇānāṃ vadatāṃ vividhāḥ kathāḥ||7||

8mā sma śoke manaḥ kārṣīrdiṣṭena vyathate budhaḥ
|
śrutaṃ devarahasyaṃ te nāradāddevadarśanāt||8||

9gatāste kṣatradharmeṇa śastrapūtāṃ gatiṃ śubhām
|
yathā dṛṣṭāstvayā putrāstathā kāmavihāriṇaḥ||9||

10yudhiṣṭhiraḥ svayaṃ dhīmān bhavantamanurudhyate
|
sahito bhrātṛbhiḥ sarvaiḥ sadāraḥ sasuhṛjjanaḥ||10||

11visarjayainaṃ yātveṣa svarājyamanuśāsatām
|
māsaḥ samadhikasteṣāmatīto vasatāṃ vane||11||

12etaddhi nityaṃ yatnena padaṃ rakṣyaṃ narādhipa
|
bahupratyarthikaṃ hyetadrājyaṃ nāma kurudvaha||12||

13ityuktaḥ kauravo rājā vyāsenātulatejasā
|
yudhiṣṭhiramathāhūya vāgmī vacanamabravīt||13||

14ajātaśatro bhadraṃ te śṛṇu me bhrātṛbhiḥ saha
|
tvatprasādānmahīpāla śoko nāsmānprabādhate||14||

15rame cāhaṃ tvayā putra pureva gajasāhvaye
|
nāthenānugato vidvanpriyeṣu parivartinā||15||

16prāptaṃ putraphalaṃ tvattaḥ prītirme paramā tvayi
|
na me manyurmahābāho gamyatāṃ putra mā ciram||16||

17bhavantaṃ ceha samprekṣya tapo me parihīyate
|
tapoyuktaṃ śarīraṃ ca tvāṃ dṛṣṭvā dhāritaṃ punaḥ||17||

18mātarau te tathaiveme śīrṇaparṇakṛtāśane
|
mama tulyavrate putra na ciraṃ vartayiṣyataḥ||18||

19duryodhanaprabhṛtayo dṛṣṭā lokāntaraṃ gatāḥ
|
vyāsasya tapaso vīryādbhavataśca samāgamāt||19||

20prayojanaṃ ca nirvṛttaṃ jīvitasya mamānagha
|
ugraṃ tapaḥ samāsthāsye tvamanujñātumarhasi||20||

21tvayyadya piṇḍaḥ kīrtiśca kulaṃ cedaṃ pratiṣṭhitam
|
śvo vādya vā mahābāho gamyatāṃ putra mā ciram||21||

22rājanītiḥ subahuśaḥ śrutā te bharatarṣabha
|
sandeṣṭavyaṃ ne paśyāmi kṛtaṃ me bhavatā vibho||22||

23vaiśampāyana uvāca|
ityuktavacanaṃ taṃ tu nṛpo rājānamabravīt
|
na māmarhasi dharmajña parityaktumanāgasam||23||

24kāmaṃ gacchantu me sarve bhrātaro'nucarāstathā
|
bhavantamahamanviṣye mātarau ca yatavrataḥ||24||

25tamuvācātha gāndhārī maivaṃ putra śṛṇuṣva ca
|
tvayyadhīnaṃ kurukulaṃ piṇḍaśca śvaśurasya me||25||

26gamyatāṃ putra paryāptametāvatpūjitā vayam
|
rājā yadāha tatkāryaṃ tvayā putra piturvacaḥ||26||

27vaiśampāyana uvāca|
ityuktaḥ sa tu gāndhāryā kuntīmidamabhāṣata
|
snehabāṣpākule netre pramṛjya rudatīṃ vacaḥ||27||

28visarjayati māṃ rājā gāndhārī ca yaśasvinī
|
bhavatyāṃ baddhacittastu kathaṃ yāsyāmi duḥkhitaḥ||28||

29na cotsahe tapovighnaṃ kartuṃ te dharmacāriṇi
|
tapaso hi paraṃ nāsti tapasā vindate mahat||29||

30mamāpi na tathā rājñi rājye buddhiryathā purā
|
tapasyevānuraktaṃ me manaḥ sarvātmanā tathā||30||

31śūnyeyaṃ ca mahī kṛtsnā na me prītikarī śubhe
|
bāndhavā naḥ parikṣīṇā balaṃ no na yathā purā||31||

32pañcālāḥ subhṛśaṃ kṣīṇāḥ kathāmātrāvaśeṣitāḥ
|
na teṣāṃ kulakartāraṃ kañcitpaśyāmyahaṃ śubhe||32||

33sarve hi bhasmasānnītāste droṇena raṇājire
|
avaśiṣṭāśca nihatā droṇaputreṇa vai niśi||33||

34cedayaścaiva matsyāśca dṛṣṭapūrvāstathaiva naḥ
|
kevalaṃ vṛṣṇicakraṃ ca vāsudevaparigrahāt||34||

35yaddṛṣṭvā sthātumicchāmi dharmārthaṃ nārthahetutaḥ
|
śivena paśya naḥ sarvān durlabhaṃ tava darśanam||35||

36aviṣahyaṃ ca rājā hi tīvraṃ cārapsyate tapaḥ
|
etacchrutvā mahābāhuḥ sahadevo yudhāṃ patiḥ||36||

37yudhiṣṭhiramuvācedaṃ bāṣpavyākulalocanaḥ
|
notsahe'haṃ parityaktuṃ mātaraṃ bharatarṣabha||37||

38pratiyātu bhavān kṣipraṃ tapastapsyāmyahaṃ vibho
|
ihaiva śoṣayiṣyāmi tapasedaṃ kalevaram||38||

39pādaśuśrūṣaṇe rakto rājño mātrostathānayoḥ
|
tamuvāca tataḥ kuntī pariṣvajya mahābhujam||39||

40gammatāṃ putra maivaṃ tvaṃ vocaḥ kuruḥ vaco mama
|
āgamā vaḥ śivāḥ santu svasthā bhavata putrakāḥ||40||

41uparodho bhavedevamasmākaṃ tapasaḥ kṛte
|
tvatsnehapāśabaddhā ca hīyeyaṃ tapasaḥ parāt||41||

42tasmātputraka gaccha tvaṃ śiṣṭamalpaṃ ca naḥ prabho
|
evaṃ saṃstambhitaṃ vākyaiḥ kuntyā bahuvidhairmanaḥ||42||

43sahadevasya rājendra rājñaścaiva viśeṣataḥ
|
te mātrā samanujñātā rājñā ca kurupuṅgavāḥ||43||

44abhivādya kuruśreṣṭhamāmantrayitumārabhan
|
yudhiṣṭhira uvāca|
rājyaṃ pratigamiṣyāmaḥ śivena pratinanditāḥ||44||

45anujñātāstvayā rājan gamiṣyāmo vikalmaṣāḥ
|
evamuktaḥ sa rājarṣirdharmarājñā mahātmanā||45||

46anujajñe sa kauravyamabhinandya yudhiṣṭhiram
|
bhīmaṃ ca balināṃ śreṣṭhaṃ santvayāmāsa pārthivaḥ||46||

47sa cāsya samyaṅmedhāvī pratyapadyata vīryavān
|
arjunaṃ ca samāśliṣya yamau ca puruṣarṣabhau||47||

48anujajñe sa kauravyaḥ pariṣvajyābhinandya ca
|
gāndhāryā cābhyanujñātāḥ kṛtapādābhivādanāḥ||48||

49jananyā samupāghrātāḥ pariṣvaktāśca te nṛpam
|
cakruḥ pradakṣiṇaṃ sarve vatsā iva nivāraṇe||49||

50punaḥ punarnirīkṣantaḥ pracakruste pradakṣiṇam
|
draupadīpramukhāścaiva sarvāḥ kauravayoṣitaḥ||50||

51nyāyataḥ śvaśure vṛttiṃ prayujya prayayustataḥ
|
śvaśrūbhyāṃ samanujñātāḥ pariṣvajyābhinanditāḥ||51||

52sandiṣṭāśceti kartavyaṃ prayayurbhartṛbhiḥ saha
|
tataḥ prajajñe ninadaḥ sūtānāṃ yujyatāmiti||52||

53uṣṭrāṇāṃ krośatāṃ cāpi hayānāṃ heṣatāmapi
|
tato yudhiṣṭhiro rājā sadāraḥ sahasainikaḥ
|
nagaraṃ hāstinapuraṃ punarāyātsabāndhavaḥ||53||
