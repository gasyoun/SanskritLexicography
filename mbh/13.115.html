Mahābhārata 13.115 (Anuśāsanaparvan)

1yudhiṣṭhira uvāca|
ahiṃsā paramo dharma ityuktaṃ bahuśastvayā
|
śrāddheṣu ca bhavānāha pitṝnāmiṣakāṅkṣiṇaḥ ||1||

2māṃsairbahuvidhaiḥ proktastvayā śrāddhavidhiḥ purā
|
ahatvā ca kuto māṃsamevametadvirudhyate ||2||

3jāto naḥ saṃśayo dharme māṃsasya parivarjane
|
doṣo bhakṣayataḥ kaḥ syātkaścābhakṣayato guṇaḥ ||3||

4hatvā bhakṣayato vāpi pareṇopahṛtasya vā
|
hanyādvā yaḥ parasyārthe krītvā vā bhakṣayennaraḥ ||4||

5etadicchāmi tattvena kathyamānaṃ tvayā'nagha
|
niścayena cikīrṣāmi dharmametaṃ sanātanam ||5||

6kathamāyuravāpnoti kathaṃ bhavati sattvavān
|
kathamavyaṅgatāmeti lakṣaṇyo jāyate katham ||6||

7bhīṣma uvāca|
māṃsasyābhakṣaṇādrājanyo dharmaḥ kurunandana
|
tanme śṛṇu yathātattvaṃ yathā'sya vidhiruttamaḥ ||7||

8rūpamavyaṅgatāmāyurbuddhiṃ sattvaṃ balaṃ smṛtim
|
prāptukāmairnarairhiṃsā varjitā vai mahātmabhiḥ ||8||

9ṛṣīṇāmatra saṃvādo bahuśaḥ kurunandana
|
babhūva teṣāṃ tu mataṃ yattacchṛṇu yudhiṣṭhira ||9||

10yo yajetāśvamedhena māsi māsi yatavrataḥ
|
varjayenmadhumāṃsaṃ ca samametadyudhiṣṭhira ||10||

11saptarṣayo vālakhilyāstathaiva ca marīcipāḥ
|
amāṃsabhakṣaṇaṃ rājanpraśaṃsanti manīṣiṇaḥ ||11||

12na bhakṣayati yo māṃsaṃ na ca hanyānna ghātayet
|
tanmitraṃ sarvabhūtānāṃ manuḥ svāyambhuvo'bravīt ||12||

13adhṛṣyaḥ sarvabhūtānāṃ viśvāsyaḥ sarvajantuṣu
|
sādhūnāṃ sammato nityaṃ bhavenmāṃsaṃ vivarjayan ||13||

14svamāṃsaṃ paramāṃsena yo vardhayitumicchati
|
nāradaḥ prāha dharmātmā niyataṃ so'vasīdati ||14||

15dadāti yajate cāpi tapasvī ca bhavatyapi
|
madhumāṃsanivṛttyeti prāha caivaṃ bṛhaspatiḥ ||15||

16māsi māsyaśvamedhena yo yajeta śataṃ samāḥ
|
na khādati ca yo māṃsaṃ samametanmataṃ mama ||16||

17sadā yajati satreṇa sadā dānaṃ prayacchati
|
sadā tapasvī bhavati madhumāṃsavivarjanāt ||17||

18sarve vedā na tatkuryuḥ sarve yajñāśca bhārata
|
yo bhakṣayitvā māṃsāni paścādapi nivartate ||18||

19duṣkaraṃ ca rasajñāne māṃsasya parivarjanam
|
cartuṃ vratamidaṃ śreṣṭhaṃ sarvaprāṇyabhayapradam ||19||

20sarvabhūteṣu yo vidvān dadātyabhayadakṣiṇām
|
dātā bhavati loke sa prāṇānāṃ nātra saṃśayaḥ ||20||

21evaṃ vai paramaṃ dharmaṃ praśaṃsanti manīṣiṇaḥ
|
prāṇā yathā''tmano'bhīṣṭā bhūtānāmapi vai tathā ||21||

22ātmaupamyena mantavyaṃ buddhimadbhiḥ kṛtātmabhiḥ
|
mṛtyuto bhayamastīti viduṣāṃ bhūtimicchatām ||22||

23kiṃ punarhanyamānānāṃ tarasā jīvitārthinām
|
arogāṇāmapāpānāṃ pāpairmāṃsopajīvibhiḥ ||23||

24tasmādviddhi mahārāja māṃsasya parivarjanam
|
dharmasyāyatanaṃ śreṣṭhaṃ svargasya ca sukhasya ca ||24||

25ahiṃsā paramo dharmastathā'hiṃsā paraṃ tapaḥ
|
ahiṃsā paramaṃ satyaṃ yato dharmaḥ pravartate ||25||

26na hi māṃsaṃ tṛṇāt kāṣṭhādupalādvāpi jāyate
|
hatvā jantuṃ tato māṃsaṃ tasmāddoṣastu bhakṣaṇe||26||

27svāhāsvadhāmṛtabhujo devāḥ satyārjavapriyāḥ
|
kravyādān rākṣasānviddhi jihmānṛtaparāyaṇān ||27||

28kāntāreṣvatha ghoreṣu durgeṣu gahaneṣu ca
|
rātrāvahani sandhyāsu catvareṣu sabhāsu ca ||28||

29udyateṣu ca śastreṣu mṛgavyālahateṣu ca
|
amāṃsabhakṣaṇe rājanbhayamanyairna gacchati ||29||

30śaraṇyaḥ sarvabhūtānāṃ viśvāsyaḥ sarvajantuṣu
|
anudvegakaro loke na cāpyudvijate sadā ||30||

31yadi cetkhāda‌ko na syānna tadā ghātako bhavet
|
ghātakaḥ khādakārthāya ta‌dghātayati vai naraḥ ||31||

32abhakṣyametaditi vai iti hiṃsā nivartate
|
khāda‌kārthamato hiṃsā mṛgādīnāṃ pravartate ||32||

33yasmādgrasati caivāyurhiṃsakānāṃ mahādyute
|
tasmādvivarjayenmāṃsaṃ ya icchedbhūtimātmanaḥ ||33||

34trātāraṃ nādhigacchanti raudrāḥ prāṇivihiṃsakāḥ
|
udvejanīyā bhūtānāṃ yathā vyālamṛ‌gāstathā ||34||

35lobhādvā buddhimohādvā balavīryārthameva ca
|
saṃsargādatha pāpānāmadharmarucitā nṛṇām ||35||

36svamāṃsaṃ paramāṃsena yo vardhayitumicchati
|
udvignavāso vasati yatra yatrābhijāyate ||36||

37dhanyaṃ yaśasyamāyuṣyaṃ svargyaṃ svastyayanaṃ mahat
|
māṃsasyābhakṣaṇaṃ prāhurniyatāḥ paramarṣayaḥ ||37||

38idaṃ tu khalu kaunteya śrutamāsītpurā mayā
|
mārkaṇḍeyasya vadato ye doṣā māṃsabhakṣaṇe ||38||

39yo hi khādati māṃsāni prāṇināṃ jīvitaiṣiṇām
|
hatānāṃ vā mṛtānāṃ vā yathā hantā tathaiva saḥ ||39||

40dhanena krayiko hanti khāda‌kaścopabhogataḥ
|
ghātako vadhabandhābhyāmityeṣa trividho vadhaḥ ||40||

41akhādannanumodaṃśca bhāvadoṣeṇa mānavaḥ
|
yo'numodati hanyantaṃ so'pi doṣeṇa lipyate ||41||

42adhṛṣyaḥ sarvabhūtānāmāyuṣmānnirujaḥ sadā
|
bhavatyabhakṣayan māṃsaṃ dayāvān prāṇināmiha ||42||

43hiraṇyadānairgodānairbhūmidānaiśca sarvaśaḥ
|
māṃsasyābhakṣaṇe dharmo viśiṣṭa iti naḥ śrutiḥ ||43||

44aprokṣitaṃ vṛthāmāṃsaṃ vidhihīnaṃ na bhakṣayet
|
bhakṣayannirayaṃ yāti naro nāstyatra saṃśayaḥ ||44||

45prokṣitābhyukṣitaṃ māṃsaṃ tathā brāhmaṇakāmyayā
|
alpadoṣamiha jñeyaṃ viparīte tu lipyate ||45||

46khāda‌kasya kṛte jantūn yo hanyāt puruṣādhamaḥ
|
mahādoṣatarastatra ghātako na tu khādakaḥ ||46||

47ijyāyajñaśrutikṛtairyo mārgairabudho'dhamaḥ
|
hanyājjantūn māṃsagṛdhnuḥ sa vai narakabhāṅnaraḥ ||47||

48bhakṣayitvāpi yo māṃsaṃ paścādapi nivartate
|
tasyāpi sumahān dharmo yaḥ pāpādvinivartate ||48||

49āhartā cānumantā ca viśastā krayavikrayī
|
saṃskartā copabhoktā ca khādakāḥ sarvaeva te ||49||

50idamanyattu vakṣyāmi pramāṇaṃ vidhinirmitam
|
purāṇasṛṣibhirjuṣṭaṃ vedeṣu pariniṣṭhitam ||50||

51pravṛttilakṣaṇo dharmaḥ prajārthibhirudāhṛtaḥ
|
yathoktaṃ rājaśārdūla na tu tanmokṣakāṅkṣiṇām ||51||

52haviryatsaṃskṛtaṃ mantraiḥ prokṣitābhyukṣitaṃ śuci
|
vedoktena pramāṇena pitṝṇāṃ prakriyāsu ca ||52||

53ato'nyathā vṛthāmāṃsamabhakṣyaṃ manurabravīt
|
asvargyamayaśasyaṃ ca rakṣovadbharatarṣabha ||53||

54vidhihīnaṃ naraḥ pūrvaṃ māṃsaṃ rājanna bhakṣayet
|
aprokṣitaṃ vṛthā māṃsaṃ vidhihīnaṃ na bhakṣayet ||54||

55ya icchetpuruṣotyantamātmānaṃ nirupadravam
|
sa varjayeta māṃsāni prāṇināmiha sarvaśaḥ ||55||

56śrūyate hi purā kalpe nṛṇāṃ vrīhimayaḥ paśuḥ
|
yenāyajanta yajvānaḥ puṇyalokaparāyaṇāḥ ||56||

57ṛṣibhiḥ saṃśayaṃ pṛṣṭo vasuścedipatiḥ purā
|
abhakṣyamapi māṃsaṃ yaḥ prāha bhakṣyamiti prabho ||57||

58ākāśādaniṃ prāptastataḥ sa pṛthivīpatiḥ
|
etadeva punaścoktvā viveśa dharaṇītalam ||58||

59prajānāṃ hitakāmena tvagastyena mahātmanā
|
āraṇyāḥ sarvadaivatyāḥ prokṣitāstapasā mṛgāḥ ||59||

60kriyā hyevaṃ na hīyante pitṛdaivatasaṃśritāḥ
|
prīyante pitaraścaiva nyāyato māṃsatarpitāḥ ||60||

61idaṃ tu śṛṇu rājendra kīrtyamānaṃ mayā'nagha
|
abhakṣaṇe sarvasukhaṃ māṃsasya manujādhipa ||61||

62yastu varṣaśataṃ pūrṇaṃ tapastapyetsudāruṇam
|
yaścaiva varjayenmāṃsaṃ samametanmataṃ mama ||62||

63kaumude tu viśeṣeṇa śuklapakṣe narādhipa
|
varjayenmadhumāṃsāni dharmo yatra vidhīyate ||63||

64caturo vārṣikānmāsānyo māṃsaṃ parivarjayet
|
catvāri bhadrāṇyāpnoti kīrtimāyuryaśo balam ||64||

65athavā māsamekaṃ vai sarvamāṃsānyabhakṣayan
|
atītya sarvaduḥkhāni sukhaṃ jīvannirāmayaḥ ||65||

66varjayanti hi māṃsāni māsaśaḥ pakṣaśo'pi vā
|
teṣāṃ hiṃsānivṛttānāṃ brahmaloko vidhīyate ||66||

67māṃsaṃ tu kaumudaṃ pakṣaṃ varjitaṃ pārtha rājabhiḥ
|
sarvabhūtātmabhūtasthairviditārthaparāvaraiḥ ||67||

68nābhāgenāmbarīṣeṇa gayena ca mahātmanā
|
āyunāthānaraṇyena dilīparaghupūrubhiḥ ||68||

69kārtavīryāniruddhābhyāṃ nahuṣeṇa yayātinā
|
nṛgeṇa viṣvagaśvena tathaiva śaśabindunā ||69||

70yuvanāśvena ca tathā śibinauśīnareṇa ca
|
mucukundena māndhātrā hariścandreṇa vā vibho ||70||

71satyaṃ vadata mā'satyaṃ satyaṃ dharmaḥ sanātanaḥ
|
hariścandraścarati vai divi satyena candravat ||71||

72śyenacitreṇa rājendra somakena vṛkeṇa ca
|
raivate rantidevena vasunā sṛñjayena ca ||72||

73etaiścānyaiśca rājendra kṛpeṇa bharatena ca
|
duṣyantena karūṣeṇa rāmālarkanaraistathā
|
virūpāśvena niminā janakena ca dhīmatā ||73||

74ailena pṛthunā caiva vīrasenena caiva ha
|
ikṣvākuṇā śambhunā ca śvetena sagareṇa ca ||74||

75ajena dhundhunā caiva tathaiva ca subāhunā
|
haryaśvena ca rājendra kṣupeṇa bharatena ca ||75||

76etaiścānyaiśca rājendra purā māṃsaṃ na bhakṣitam
|
śāradaṃ kaumudaṃ māsaṃ tataste svargamāpnuvan ||76||

77brahmaloke ca tiṣṭhanti jvalamānāḥśriyānvitāḥ
|
upāsyamānā gandharvaiḥ strīsahasrasamanvitāḥ ||77||

78tadetaduttamaṃ dharmamahiṃsādharmalakṣaṇam
|
ye caranti mahātmāno nākapṛṣṭhe vasanti te ||78||

79madhumāṃsaṃ ca ye nityaṃ varjayantīha dhārmikāḥ
|
janmaprabhṛti madyaṃ ca sarve te munayaḥ smṛtāḥ ||79||

80imaṃ dharmamamāṃsādaṃ yaścarecchrāvayīta vā
|
api cetsudurācāro na jātu nirayaṃ vrajet ||80||

81paṭhedvā ya idaṃ rājañchṛṇuyādvāpyabhīkṣṇaśaḥ
|
amāṃsabhakṣaṇavidhiṃ pavitramṛṣipūjitam ||81||

82vimuktaḥ sarvapāpebhyaḥ sarvakāmairmahīyate
|
viśiṣṭatāṃ jñātiṣu ca labhate nātra saṃśayaḥ ||82||

83āpannaścāpado mucyedbaddho mucyeta bandhanāt
|
mucyettathā''turo rogādduḥkhānmucyeta duḥkhitaḥ ||83||

84tiryagyoniṃ na gaccheta rūpavāṃśca bhavennaraḥ
|
ṛddhimānvai kuruśreṣṭha prāpnuyācca mahadyaśaḥ ||84||

85etatte kathitaṃ rājanmāṃsasya parivarjane
|
pravṛttau ca nivṛttau ca vidhānamṛṣinirmitam ||85||
