Mahābhārata 12.245 (Śāntiparvan)

1śuka uvāca |
vartamānasthaivātra vānaprasthāśrame yathā
|
yoktavyo''tmā kathaṃ śaktyā vedyaṃ vai kāṃkṣatā param ||1||

2vyāsa uvāca |
prāpya saṃskārametābhyāmāśramābhyāṃ tataḥ param
|
yatkāryaṃ paramārthaṃ tu tadihaikamanāḥ śṛṇu ||2||

3kaṣāyaṃ pācayitvāśu śreṇisthāneṣu ca triṣu
|
pravrajecca paraṃ sthānaṃ pārivrājamanuttamam ||3||

4tadbhavānevamabhyasya vartatāṃ śrūyatāṃ tathā
|
eka eva careddharmaṃ siddhyarthamasahāyavān ||4||

5ekaścarati yaḥ paśyan na jahāti na hīyate
|
anagniraniketaśca grāmamannārthamāśrayet ||5||

6aśvastanavidhātā syānmunirbhāvasamāhitaḥ
|
laghvāśī niyatāhāraḥ sakṛdannaniṣevitā ||6||

7kapālaṃ vṛkṣamūlāni kucailamasahāyatā
|
upekṣā sarvabhūtānāmetāvadbhikṣulakṣaṇam ||7||

8yasminvācaḥ prāviśanti kūpe trastā dvipā iva
|
na vaktāraṃ punaryānti kaivalyāśrame vaset ||8||

9naiva paśyenna śṛṇuyādavācyaṃ jātu kasyacit
|
brāhmaṇānāṃ viśeṣeṇa naiva brūyātkathaṃcana ||9||

10yadbrāhmaṇasya kuśalaṃ tadeva satataṃ vadet
|
tūṣṇīmāsīta nindāyāṃ kurvan bhaiṣajyamātmanaḥ ||10||

11yena pūrṇamivākāśaṃ bhavatyekena sarvadā
|
śūnyaṃ yena janākīrṇaṃ taṃ devā brāhmaṇaṃ viduḥ ||11||

12yena kenacidācchanno yena kenacidāśitaḥ
|
yatra kvacana śāyī ca taṃ devā brāhmaṇaṃ viduḥ ||12||

13aheriva gaṇādbhītaḥ sauhityānarakādiva
|
kuṇapādiva ca strībhyastaṃ devā brāhmaṇaṃ viduḥ ||13||

14na kruddhyenna prahṛṣyecca mānito'mānitaśca yaḥ
|
sarvabhūteṣvabhayadastaṃ devā brāhmaṇaṃ viduḥ ||14||

15nābhinandeta maraṇaṃ nābhinandeta jīvitam
|
kālamegha pratīkṣate nideśaṃ bhṛtako yathā ||15||

16anabhyāhatacittaḥ syādanabhyāhatavāgbhavet
|
nirmuktaḥ sarvapāpebhyo niramitrasya kiṃ bhayam ||16||

17abhayaṃ sarvabhūtebhyo bhūtānāmabhayaṃ tataḥ
|
tasya mohādvimuktasya bhayaṃ nāsti kutaścana ||17||

18yathā nāgapade'nyāni padāni padagāminām
|
sarvāṇyevāpidhīyante padajātāni kauñjare ||18||

19evaṃ sarvamahiṃsāyāṃ dharmārthamapi dhīyate
|
amṛtaḥ sa nityaṃ vasati yo'hiṃsāṃ na prapadyate ||19||

20ahiṃsakaḥ samaḥ satyo dhṛtimānniyatendriyaḥ
|
śaraṇyaḥ sarvabhūtānāṃ gatimāpnotyanuttamām ||20||

21evaṃ prajñānatṛptasya nirbhayasya nirāśiṣaḥ
|
na mṛtyuratigo bhāvaḥ sa mṛtyumadhigacchati ||21||

22vimuktaṃ sarvasaṅgebhyo munimākāśavatsthitam
|
asvamekacaraṃ śāntaṃ taṃ devā brāhmaṇaṃ viduḥ ||22||

23jīvitaṃ yasya dharmārthaṃ dharmo haryarthameva ca
|
ahorātrāśca puṇyārthaṃ taṃ devā brāhmaṇaṃ viduḥ ||23||

24nirāśiṣamanārambhaṃ nirnamaskāramastutim
|
nirmuktaṃ bandhanaiḥ sarvaistaṃ devā brāhmaṇaṃ viduḥ ||24||

25sarvāṇi bhūtāni sukhe ramante sarvāṇi duḥkhasya bhṛśaṃ trasante
|
teṣāṃ bhayotpādanajātakhedaḥ kuryānna karmāṇi hi śraddadhānaḥ ||25||

26dānaṃ hi bhūtābhayadakṣiṇāyāḥ sarvāṇi dānānyadhitiṣṭhatīha
|
tīkṣṇāṃ tanuṃ yaḥ prathamaṃ jahāti so'nantyamāpnotyabhayaṃ prajābhyaḥ ||26||

27uttāna āsyena havirjuhoti lokasya nābhirjagataḥ pratiṣṭhā
|
tasyāṅgamaṅgāni kṛtākṛtaṃ ca vaiśvānaraḥ sarvamidaṃ prapede ||27||

28prādeśamāne hṛdi niḥsṛtaṃ yattasminprāṇānātmayājī juhoti
|
tasyāgnihotraṃ hutamātmasaṃsthaṃ sarveṣu lokeṣu sadevakeṣu ||28||

29devaṃ tridhātuṃ trivṛtaṃ suparṇaṃ ye vidyuragryāṃ paramātmatāṃ ca
|
te sarvalokeṣu mahīyamānā devāḥ samartyāḥ sukṛtaṃ vadanti ||29||

30vedāṃśca vedyaṃ tu vidhiṃ ca kṛtsnamatho niruktaṃ paramārthatāṃ ca
|
sarvaṃ śarīrātmani yaḥ praveda tasyaiva devāḥ spṛhayanti nityam ||30||

31bhūmāvasaktaṃ divi cāprameyaṃ hiraṇmayaṃ yo'ṇḍajamaṇḍamadhye
|
patatriṇaṃ pakṣiṇamantarikṣa yo veda bhogyātmani raśmidīptaḥ ||31||

32āvartamānamajaraṃ vivartanaṃ ṣaṇṇābhikaṃ dvādaśāraṃ suparva
|
yasyedamāsyopari yāti viśvaṃ tatkālacakraṃ nihitaṃ guhāyām ||32||

33yaḥ samprasādo jagataḥ śarīraṃ sarvānsa lokānadhigacchatīha
|
tasminhi taṃ tarpayatīha devāṃste vai tṛptāstarpayantyāsyamasya ||33||

34tejomayo nityamayaḥ purāṇo lokānanantānabhayānupaiti
|
bhūtāni yasmānna vasante kadācitsa bhūtānāṃ na trasate kadācit ||34||

35agarhaṇīyo na ca garhate'nyānsa vai vipraḥ paramātmānamīkṣet
|
vinītamoho vyapanītakalmaṣo na ceha nāmutra ca so'nnamarcchati ||35||

36aroṣamohaḥ samaloṣṭakāñcanaḥ prahīṇakośo gatasandhivigrahaḥ
|
apetanindāstutirapriyāpriyaścarannudāsīnavadeṣa bhikṣukaḥ ||36||
