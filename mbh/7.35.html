Mahābhārata 7.35 (Droṇaparvan)

1sañjaya uvāca|
tadanīkamanādhṛṣyaṃ bhāradvājena rakṣitam
|
pārthāḥ samabhyavartanta bhīmasenapurogamāḥ||1||

2sātyakiścekitānaśca dhṛṣṭadyumnaśca pārṣataḥ
|
kuntibhojaśca vikrānto drupadaśca mahārathaḥ||2||

3ārjuniḥ kṣatradharmā ca bṛhatkṣatraśca vīryavān
|
cedipo dhṛṣṭaketuśca mādrīputrau ghaṭotkacaḥ||3||

4yudhāmanyuśca vikrāntaḥ śikhaṇḍī cāparājitaḥ
|
uttamaujāśca durdharṣo virāṭaśca mahārathaḥ||4||

5draupadeyāśca saṃrabdhāḥ śaiśupāliśca vīryavān
|
kekayāśca mahāvīryāḥ sṛñjayāśca sahasraśaḥ||5||

6ete cānye ca sagaṇāḥ kṛtāstrā yuddhadurmadāḥ
|
samabhyadhāvan sahasā bhāradvājaṃ yuyutsavaḥ||6||

7samīpe vartamānāṃstān bhāradvājo'tivīryavān
|
asaṃbhrāntaḥ śaraugheṇa mahatā samavārayat||7||

8mahaughaḥ salilasyeva girimāsādya durbhidam
|
droṇaṃ te nābhyavartanta velāmiva jalāśayāḥ||8||

9pīḍyamānāḥ śarai rājandroṇacāpaviniḥsṛtaiḥ
|
na śekuḥ pramukhe sthātuṃ bhāradvājasya pāṇḍavāḥ||9||

10tada‌dbhutamapaśyāma droṇasya bhujayorbalam
|
yadenaṃ nābhyavartanta pañcālāḥ sṛñjayaiḥ saha||10||

11tamāyāntamabhikruddhaṃ droṇaṃ dṛṣṭvā yudhiṣṭhiraḥ
|
bahudhā cintayāmāsa droṇasya prativāraṇam||11||

12aśakyaṃ tu tamanyena droṇaṃ matvā yudhiṣṭhiraḥ
|
aviṣahyaṃ guruṃ bhāraṃ saubhadre samavāsṛjat||12||

13vāsudevādanavaraṃ phālgunāccāmitaujasam
|
abravīt paravīraghnamabhimanyumidaṃ vacaḥ||13||

14etya no nārjuno garhedyathā tāta tathā kuru
|
cakravyūhasya na vayaṃ vidmo bhedaṃ kathañcana||14||

15tvaṃ vā'rjuno vā kṛṣṇo vā bhindyāt pradyumna eva vā
|
cakravyūhaṃ mahābāho pañcamare nopapadyate||15||

16abhimanyo varaṃ tāta yācatāṃ dātumarhasi
|
pitṝṇāṃ mātulānāṃ ca sainyānāṃ caiva sarvaśaḥ||16||

17dhanañjayo hi nastāta garhayedetya saṃyugāt
|
kṣipramastraṃ samādāya droṇānīkaṃ viśātaya||17||

18abhimanyuruvāca| droṇasya dṛḍhamatyugramanīkapravaraṃ yudhi
|
pitṝṇāṃ jayamākāṃkṣannavagāhe'vilambitam||18||

19upadiṣṭo hi me pitrā yogo'nīkaviśātane
|
notsahe hi vinirgantumahaṃ kasyāṃcidāpadi||19||

20yudhiṣṭhira uvāca| bhindhyanīkaṃ yudhāṃ śreṣṭha dvāraṃ sañjanayasva naḥ
|
vayaṃ tvānugamiṣyāmo yena tvaṃ tāta yāsyasi||20||

21dhanañjayasamaṃ yuddhe tvāṃ vayaṃ tāta saṃyuge
|
praṇidhāyānuyāsyāmo rakṣantaḥ sarvatomukhāḥ||21||

22bhīma uvāca| ahaṃ tvā'nugamiṣyāmi dhṛṣṭadyumno'tha sātyakiḥ
|
pañcālāḥ kekayā matsyāstathā sarve prabhadrakāḥ||22||

23sakṛdbhinnaṃ tvayā vyūhaṃ tatra tatra punaḥ punaḥ
|
vayaṃ pradhvaṃsayiṣyāmo nighnamānā varān‌ varān||23||

24abhimanyuruvāca| ahametat pravekṣyāmi droṇānīkaṃ durāsadam
|
pataṅga iva saṃkruddho jvalitaṃ jātavedasam||24||

25tatkarmādya kariṣyāmi hitaṃ yadvaṃśayordvayoḥ
|
mātulasya ca yatprītiṃ kariṣyati pituśca me||25||

26śiśunaikena saṃgrāme kālyamānāni saṃghaśaḥ
|
drakṣyanti sarvabhūtāni dviṣatsainyāni vai mayā||26||

27nāhaṃ pārthena jātaḥ syāṃ na ca jātaḥ subhadrayā
|
yadi me saṃyuge kaścijjīvito nādya mucyate||27||

28yadi caikarathenāhaṃ samagraṃ kṣatramaṇḍalam
|
na karomyaṣṭadhā yuddhe na bhavāmyarjunātmajaḥ||28||

29yudhiṣṭhira uvāca| evaṃ te bhāṣamāṇasya balaṃ saubhadra vardhatām
|
yatsamutsahase bhettuṃ droṇānīka durāsadam||29||

30rakṣitaṃ puruṣavyāghrairmaheṣvāsairmahābalaiḥ
|
sādhyarudramaruttulyairvasvagnyādityavikramaiḥ||30||

31sañjaya uvāca|
tasya tadvacanaṃ śrutvā sa yantāramacodayat
|
sumitrāśvān raṇe kṣipraṃ droṇānīkāya codaya||31||
