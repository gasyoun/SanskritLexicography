Mahābhārata 7.33 (Droṇaparvan)

1sañjaya uvāca|
pūrvamasmāsu bhagneṣu phālgunenāmitaujasā
|
droṇe ca moghasaṃkalpe rakṣite ca yudhiṣṭhire||1||

2sarve vidhvastakavacāstāvakā yudhi nirjitāḥ
|
rajasvalā bhṛśodvignā vīkṣamāṇā diśo daśa||2||

3avahāraṃ tataḥ kṛtvā bhāradvājasya saṃmate
|
labdhalakṣaiḥ śarairbhinnā bhṛśāvahasitā raṇe||3||

4ślāghamāneṣu bhūteṣu phālgunasyāmitān guṇān
|
keśavasya ca sauhārde kīrtyamāne'rjunaṃ prati||4||

5abhiśastā ivābhūvan dhyānamūkatvamāsthitāḥ
|
tataḥ prabhātasamaye droṇaṃ duryodhano'bravīt||5||

6praṇayādabhimānācca dviṣadvṛddhyā ca durmanāḥ
|
śṛṇvatāṃ sarvayodhānāṃ saṃrabdho vākyakovidaḥ||6||

7nūnaṃ vayaṃ vadhyapakṣe bhavato dvijasattama
|
tathā hi nāgrahīḥ prāptaṃ samīpe'dya yudhiṣṭhiram||7||

8icchatastena mucyeta cakṣuḥprāpto raṇe ripuḥ
|
jighṛkṣato rakṣyamāṇaḥ sāmarairapi pāṇḍavaiḥ||8||

9varaṃ dattvā mama prītaḥ paścādvikṛtavānasi
|
āśābhaṅgaṃ na kurvanti bhaktasyāryāḥ kathañcana||9||

10tato'prītastathoktaḥ san bhāradvājo'bravīnnṛpam
|
nārhase māṃ tathā jñātuṃ ghaṭamānaṃ tava priye||10||

11sasurāsuragandharvāḥ sayakṣoragarākṣasāḥ
|
nālaṃ lokā raṇe jetuṃ pālyamānaṃ kirīṭinā||11||

12viśvasṛgyatra govindaḥ pṛtanānīstathā'rjunaḥ
|
tatra kasya balaṃ krāmedanyatra tryaṃbakāt prabhoḥ||12||

13satyaṃ tāta bravīmyadya naitajjātvanyathā bhavet
|
adyaikaṃ pravaraṃ kañcit pātayiṣye mahāratham||13||

14taṃ ca vyūhaṃ vidhāsyāmi yo'bhedyastridaśairapi
|
yogena kenacidrājannarjunastvapanīyatām||14||

15na hyajñātamasādhyaṃ vā tasya saṃkhye'sti kiñcana
|
tena hyupāttaṃ sakalaṃ sarvajñānamitastataḥ||15||

16droṇena vyāhṛte tvevaṃ saṃśaptakagaṇāḥ punaḥ
|
āhvayannarjunaṃ saṃkhye dakṣiṇāmabhito diśam||16||

17tato'rjunasyātha paraiḥ sārdhaṃ samabhavadraṇaḥ
|
tādṛśo yādṛśo nānyaḥ śruto dṛṣṭo'pi vā kvacit||17||

18tatra droṇena vihito vyūho rājan vyarocata
|
caranmadhyaṃdine sūryaḥ pratapanniva durdṛśaḥ||18||

19taṃ cābhimanyurvacanāt piturjyeṣṭhasya bhārata
|
bibheda durbhidaṃ saṃkhye cakravyūhamanekadhā||19||

20sa kṛtvā duṣkaraṃ karma hatvā vīrān sahasraśaḥ
|
ṣaṭsu vīreṣu saṃsakto dauḥśāsanivaśaṃ gataḥ||20||

21saubhadraḥ pṛthivīpāla jahau prāṇān parantapaḥ
|
vayaṃ paramasaṃhṛṣṭāḥ pāṇḍavāḥ śokakarśitāḥ
|
saubhadre nihate rājannavahāramakurmahi||21||

22dhṛtarāṣṭra uvāca|
putraṃ puruṣasiṃhasya sañjayāprāptayauvanam
|
raṇe vinihataṃ śrutvā bhṛśaṃ meṃ dīryate manaḥ||22||

23dāruṇaḥ kṣatradharmo'yaṃ vihito dharmakartṛbhiḥ
|
yatra rājyepsavaḥ śūrā bāle śastramapātayan||23||

24bālamatyantasukhinaṃ vicarantamabhītavat
|
kṛtāstrā bahavo jaghnurbrūhi gāvalgaṇe katham||24||

25bibhitsatā rathānīkaṃ saubhadreṇāmitaujasā
|
vikrīḍitaṃ yathā saṃkhye tanmamācakṣva sañjaya||25||

26sañjaya uvāca|
yanmāṃ pṛcchasi rājendra saubhadrasya nipātanam
|
tat te kārtsnyena vakṣyāmi śṛṇu rājan samāhitaḥ||26||

27vikrīḍitaṃ kumāreṇa yathānīkaṃ bibhitsatā
|
ārugṇāśca yathā vīrā duḥsādhyāścāpi viplave||27||

28dāvāgnyabhiparītānāṃ bhūrigulmatṛṇadrume
|
vanaukasāmivāraṇye tvadīyānāmabhūdbhayam||28||
