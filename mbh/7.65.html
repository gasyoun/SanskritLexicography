Mahābhārata 7.65 (Droṇaparvan)

1nārada uvāca| śaśibinduṃ ca rājānaṃ mṛtaṃ sṛñjaya śuśruma
|
īje sa vividhairyajñaiḥ śrīmān satyaparākramaḥ ||1||

2tasya bhāryāsahasrāṇāṃ śatamāsīnmahātmanaḥ
|
ekaikasyāṃ ca bhāryāyāṃ sahasraṃ tanayā'bhavan ||2||

3te kumārāḥ parākrantāḥ sarve niyutayājinaḥ
|
rājānaḥ kratubhirmukhyairījānā vedapāragāḥ ||3||

4hiraṇyakavacāḥ sarve sarve cottamadhanvinaḥ
|
sarve'śvamedhairījānāḥ kumārāḥ śaśibindavaḥ ||4||

5tānaśvamedhe rājendro brāhmaṇebhyo'dadat pitā
|
śataṃ śataṃ rathagajā ekaikaṃ pṛṣṭhato'nvayuḥ ||5||

6rājaputraṃ tadā kanyāstapanīyasvalaṅkṛtāḥ
|
kanyāṃ kanyāṃ śataṃ nāgā nāge nāge śataṃ rathāḥ ||6||

7rathe rathe śataṃ cāśvā balino hemamālinaḥ
|
aśve aśve gosahasraṃ gavāṃ pañcāśadāvikāḥ ||7||

8etaddhanamaparyāptamaśvamedhe mahāmakhe
|
śaśabindurmahābhāgo brāhmaṇebhyo hyamanyata ||8||

9vārkṣāśca yūpā yāvanta aśvamedhe mahāmakhe
|
te tathaiva punaścānye tāvantaḥ kāñcanā'bhavan ||9||

10bhakṣyānnapānanicayāḥ parvatāḥ krośamucchritāḥ
|
tasyāśvamedhe nirvṛtte rājñaḥ śiṣṭāstrayodaśa ||10||

11tuṣṭapuṣṭajanākīrṇāṃ śāntavighnāmanāmayām
|
śaśibindurimāṃ bhūmiṃ ciraṃ bhuktvā divaṃ gataḥ ||11||

12sa cenmamāra sṛñjaya caturbhadratarastvayā
|
putrāt puṇyatarastubhyaṃ mā putramanutapyathāḥ ||12||

13ayajvānamadākṣiṇyamabhiśvaityeti vyāharan ||13||
