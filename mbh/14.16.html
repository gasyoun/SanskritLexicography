Mahābhārata 14.16 (Āśvamedhikaparvan)

1janamejaya uvāca|
sabhāyāṃ vasatostatra nihatyārīnmahātmanoḥ
|
keśavārjunayoḥ kā nu kathā samabhavad dvija||1||

2vaiśampāyana uvāca|
kṛṣṇena sahitaḥ pārthaḥ svaṃ rājyaṃ prāpya kevalam
|
tasyāṃ sabhāyāṃ divyāyāṃ vijahāra mudā yutaḥ||2||

3tatra kañcit sabhoddeśaṃ svargoddeśasamaṃ nṛpa
|
yadṛcchayā tau muditau jagmatuḥ svajanāvṛtau||3||

4tataḥ pratītaḥ kṛṣṇena sahitaḥ pāṇḍavo'rjunaḥ
|
nirīkṣya tāṃ sabhāṃ ramyāmidaṃ vacanamabravīt||4||

5viditaṃ me mahābāho saṅgrāme samupasthite
|
māhātmyaṃ devakīmātastacca te rūpamaiśvaram||5||

6yattadbhagavatā proktaṃ purā keśava sauhṛdāt
|
tatsarvaṃ puruṣavyāghra naṣṭaṃ me bhraṣṭacetasaḥ||6||

7mama kautūhalaṃ tvasti teṣvartheṣu punaḥ punaḥ
|
bhavāṃstu dvārakāṃ gantā nacirādiva mādhava||7||

8vaiśampāyana uvāca|
evamuktastu taṃ kṛṣṇaḥ phālgunaṃ pratyabhāṣata
|
pariṣvajya mahātejā vacanaṃ vadatāṃ varaḥ||8||

9vāsudeva uvāca|
śrāvitastvaṃ mayā guhyaṃ jñāpitaśca sanātanam
|
dharmaṃ svarūpiṇaṃ pārtha sarvalokāṃśca śāśvatān||9||

10abuddhyā nāgrahīryastvaṃ tanme sumahadapriyam
|
na ca sādya punarbhūyaḥ smṛtirme sambhaviṣyati||10||

11nūnamaśraddadhāno'si durmedhā hyasi pāṇḍava
|
na ca śakyaṃ punarvaktumaśeṣeṇa dhanañjaya||11||

12sa hi dharmaḥ suparyāpto brahmaṇaḥ padavedane
|
na śakyaṃ tanmayā bhūyastathā vaktumaśeṣataḥ||12||

13paraṃ hi brahma kathitaṃ yogayuktena tanmayā
|
itihāsaṃ tu vakṣyāmi tasminnarthe purātanam||13||

14yathā tāṃ buddhimāsthāya gatimagryāṃ gamiṣyasi
|
śṛṇu dharmabhṛtāṃ śreṣṭha gaditaṃ sarvameva me||14||

15āgacchad brāhmaṇaḥ kaścit svargalokādarindama
|
brahmalokācca durdharṣaḥ so'smābhiḥ pūjito'bhavat||15||

16asmābhiḥ paripṛṣṭaśca yadāha bharatarṣabha
|
divyena vidhinā pārtha nacchṛṇuṣvāvicārayan||16||

17brāhmaṇa uvāca|
mokṣadharmaṃ samāśritya kṛṣṇa yanmāmapṛcchathāḥ
|
bhūtānāmanukampārthaṃ yanmohacchedanaṃ vibho||17||

18tatte'haṃ sampravakṣyāmi yathāvanmadhusūdana
|
śṛṇuṣvāvahito bhūtvā gadato mama mādhava||18||

19kaścid viprastapoyuktaḥ kāśyapo dharmavittamaḥ
|
āsasāda dvijaṃ kañciddharmāṇāmāgatāgamam||19||

20gatāgate subahuśo jñānavijñānapāragam
|
lokatattvārthakuśalaṃ jñātārthaṃ sukhaduḥkhayoḥ||20||

21jātīmaraṇatattvajñaṃ kovidaṃ pāpapuṇyayauḥ
|
draṣṭāramuccanīcānāṃ karmabhirdehināṃ gatim||21||

22carantaṃ muktavatsiddhaṃ praśāntaṃ saṃyatendriyam
|
dīpyamānaṃ śriyā brāhmyā kramamāṇaṃ ca sarvaśaḥ||22||

23antardhānagatijñaṃ ca śrutvā tattvena kāśyapaḥ
|
tathaivāntarhitaiḥ siddhairyāntaṃ cakradharaiḥ saha||23||

24sambhāṣamāṇamekānte samāsīnaṃ ca taiḥ saha
|
yadṛcchayā ca gacchantamasaktaṃ pavanaṃ yathā||24||

25taṃ samāsādya medhāvī sa tadā dvijasattamaḥ
|
caraṇau dharmakāmo'sya tapasvī susamāhitaḥ
|
pratipede yathānyāyaṃ dṛṣṭvā tanmahadadbhutam||25||

26vismitaścādbhutaṃ dṛṣṭvā kāśyapastad dvijottamam
|
paricāreṇa mahattā guruṃ taṃ paryatoṣayat||26||

27upapannaṃ ca tatsarvaṃ śrutacāritrasaṃyutam
|
bhāvenātoṣayaccainaṃ guruvṛttyā parantapaḥ||27||

28tasmai tuṣṭaḥ sa śiṣyāya prasanno vākyamabravīt
|
siddhiṃ parāmabhiprekṣya śṛṇu matto janārdana||28||

29siddha uvāca|
vividhaiḥ karmabhistāta puṇyayogaiśca kevalaiḥ
|
gacchantīha gatiṃ martyā devaloke ca saṃsthitim||29||

30na kvacit sukhamatyantaṃ na kvacicchāśvatī sthitiḥ
|
sthānācca mahato bhraṃśo duḥkhalabdhāt punaḥ punaḥ||30||

31aśubhā gatayaḥ prāptāḥ kaṣṭā me pāpasevanāt
|
kāmamanyuparītena tṛṣṇayā mohitena ca||31||

32punaḥ punaśca maraṇaṃ janma caiva punaḥ punaḥ
|
āhārā vividhā bhuktāḥ pītā nānāvidhāḥ stanāḥ||32||

33mātaro vividhā dṛṣṭāḥ pitaraśca pṛthagvidhāḥ
|
sukhāni ca vicitrāṇi duḥkhāni ca mayānagha||33||

34priyairvivāso bahuśaḥ saṃvāsaścāpriyaiḥ saha
|
dhananāśaśca samprāpto ladhvā duḥkhena tad dhanam||34||

35avamānāḥ sukaṣṭāśca rājataḥ svajanāt tathā
|
śārīrā mānasā vāpi vedanā bhṛśadāruṇāḥ||35||

36prāptā vimānanāśogrā vadhavandhāśca dāruṇāḥ
|
patanaṃ niraye caiva yātanāśca yamakṣaye||36||

37jarā rogāśca satataṃ vyasanāni ca bhūriśaḥ
|
loke'sminnanubhūtāni dvandvajāni bhṛśaṃ mayā||37||

38tataḥ kadācinnirvedānnirākārāśritena ca
|
lokatantraṃ parityaktaṃ duḥkhārtena bhṛśaṃ mayā||38||

39loke'sminnanubhūyāhamimaṃ mārgamanuṣṭhitaḥ
|
tataḥ siddhiriyaṃ prāptā prasādādātmano mayā||39||

40nāhaṃ punarihāgantā lokānālokayāmyaham
|
āsiddherāprajāsargādātmano'pi gatāḥ śubhāḥ||40||

41upalabdhā dvijaśreṣṭha tatheyaṃ siddhiruttamāḥ
|
itaḥ paraṃ gamiṣyāmi tataḥ parataraṃ punaḥ||41||

42brahmaṇaḥ padamavyaktaṃ mā te'bhūdatra saṃśayaḥ
|
nāhaṃ punarihāgantā martyalokaṃ parantapa||42||

43prīto'smi te mahāprājña brūhi kiṃ karavāṇi te
|
yadīpsurupapannastvaṃ tasya kālo'yamāgataḥ||43||

44abhijāne ca tadahaṃ yadarthaṃ māmupāgataḥ
|
acirāttu gamiṣyāmi tenāhaṃ tvāmacūcudam||44||

45bhṛśaṃ prīto'smi bhavataścāritreṇa vicakṣaṇa
|
paripṛcchasva kuśalaṃ bhāṣeyaṃ yattavepsitam||45||

46bahu manye ca te buddhiṃ bhṛśaṃ sampūjayāmi ca
|
yenāhaṃ bhavatā buddho medhāvī hyasi kāśyapa||46||
