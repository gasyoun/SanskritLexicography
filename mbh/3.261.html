Mahābhārata 3.261 (Āraṇyakaparvan)

1devadūta uvāca|
maharṣe āryabuddhistvaṃ yaḥ svargasukhamuttamam
|
samprāptaṃ bahu mantavyaṃ vimṛśasyabudho yathā||1||

2upariṣṭācca svarloko yo'yaṃ svariti saṃjñitaḥ
|
ūrdhvagaḥ satpathaḥ śaśvaddevayānacaro mune||2||

3nātaptatapasaḥ puṃso nāmahāyajñayājinaḥ
|
nānṛtā nāstikāścaiva tatra gacchanti mudgala||3||

4dharmātmāno jitātmānaḥ śāntā dāntā vimatsarāḥ
|
dānadharmaratāḥ puṃsaḥ śūrāścāhavalakṣaṇāḥ||4||

5tatra gacchanti dharmāgryaṃ kṛtvā śamadamātmakam
|
lokān puṇyakṛtāṃ brahman sadbhirācaritānnṛbhiḥ||5||

6devāḥ sādhyāstathā viśve tathaiva ca maharṣayaḥ
|
yāmā dhāmāśca maudgalya gandharvāpsarasastathā||6||

7eṣāṃ devanikāyānāṃ pṛthak pṛthaganekaśaḥ
|
bhāsvantaḥ kāmasampannā lokāstejomayāḥ śubhāḥ||7||

8trayastriṃśatsahasrāṇi yojanāni hiraṇmayaḥ
|
meruḥ parvatarāḍyatra devodyānāni mudgala||8||

9nandanādīni puṇyāni vihārāḥ puṇyakarmaṇām
|
na kṣutpipāse na glānirna śītoṣṇe bhayaṃ tathā||9||

10bībhatsamaśubhaṃ vāpi tatra kiñcinna vidyate
|
manojñāḥ sarvato gandhāḥ sukhasparśāśca sarvaśaḥ||10||

11śabdāḥ śrutimanogrāhyāḥ sarvatastatra vai mune
|
na śoko na jarā tatra nāyāsaparidevane||11||

12īdṛśaḥ sa mune lokaḥ svakarmaphalahetukaḥ
|
sukṛtaistatra puruṣāḥ sambhavantyātmakarmabhiḥ||12||

13taijasāni śarīrāṇi bhavantyatropapadyatām
|
karmajānyeva maudgalya na mātṛpitṛjānyuta||13||

14na saṃsvedo na daurgandhyaṃ purīṣaṃ mūtrameva ca
|
teṣāṃ na ca rajo vastraṃ bādhate tatra vai mune||14||

15na mlāyanti srajasteṣāṃ divyagandhā manoramāḥ
|
saṃyujyante vimānaiśca brahmannaivaṃvidhaiśca te||15||

16īrṣyāśokaklamāpetā mohamātsaryavarjitāḥ
|
sukhaṃ svargajitastatra vartayante mahāmune||16||

17teṣāṃ tathāvidhānāṃ tu lokānāṃ munipuṅgava
|
uparyupari lokasya lokā divyā guṇānvitāḥ||17||

18purastādbrāhmaṇāstatra lokāstejomayāḥ śubhāḥ
|
yatra yānyṛṣayo brahān pūtāḥ svaiḥ karmabhiḥ śubhaiḥ||18||

19ṛbhavo nāma tatrānye devānāmapi devatāḥ
|
teṣāṃ lokāt paratare yān yajantīha devatāḥ||19||

20svayaṃprabhāste bhāsvanto lokāḥ kāmadughāḥ pare
|
na teṣāṃ strīkṛtastāpo na lokaiśvaryamatsaraḥ||20||

21na vartayantyāhutibhistenāpyamṛtabhojanāḥ
|
tathā divyaśarīrāste na ca vigrahamūrtayaḥ||21||

22na sukhe sukhakāmāste devadevāḥ sanātanāḥ
|
na kalpaparivarteṣu parivartanti te tathā||22||

23jarā mṛtyuḥ kutasteṣāṃ harṣaḥ prītiḥ sukhaṃ na ca
|
na duḥkhaṃ na sukhaṃ cāpi rāgadveṣau kuto mune||23||

24devānāmapi maudgalya kāṅkṣitā sā gatiḥ parā
|
duṣprāpā paramā siddhiragamyā kāmagocaraiḥ||24||

25trayastriṃśadime devā yeṣāṃ lokā manīṣibhiḥ
|
gamyante niyamaiḥ śreṣṭhairdānairvā vidhipūrvakaiḥ||25||

26seyaṃ dānakṛtā vyuṣṭiranuprāptā sukhaṃ tvayā
|
tāṃ bhuṅkṣva sukṛtairlabdhān tapasā dyotitaprabhaḥ||26||

27etat svargasukhaṃ vipra lokā nānāvidhāstathā
|
guṇāḥ svargasya proktāste doṣānapi nibodha me||27||

28kṛtasya karmaṇastatra bhujyate yat phalaṃ divi
|
na cānyat kriyate karma mūlacchedena bhujyate||28||

29so'tra doṣo mama matastasyānte patanaṃ ca yat
|
sukhavyāptamanaskānāṃ patanaṃ yacca mudgala||29||

30asantoṣaḥ parītāpo dṛṣṭvā dīptatarāḥ śriyaḥ
|
yadbhavatyavare sthāne sthitānāṃ tat suduṣkaram||30||

31saṃjñāmohaśca patatāṃ rajasā ca pradharṣaṇam
|
pramlāneṣu ca mālyeṣu tataḥ pipatiṣorbhayam||31||

32ābrahmabhavanādete doṣā maudgalya dāruṇāḥ
|
nākaloke sukṛtināṃ guṇāstvayutaśo nṛṇām||32||

33ayaṃ tvanyo guṇaḥ śreṣṭhaścyutānāṃ svargato mune
|
śubhānuśayayogena manuṣyeṣūpajāyate||33||

34tatrāpi sa mahābhāgaḥ sukhabhāgabhijāyate
|
na cet sambudhyate tatra gacchatyadhamatāṃ tataḥ||34||

35iha yat kriyate karma tat paratropabhujyate
|
karmabhūmiriyaṃ brahman phalabhūmirasau matā||35||

36mudgala uvāca|
mahāṃtastu amī doṣāstvayā svargasya kīrtitāḥ
|
nirdoṣa eva yastvanyo lokaṃ taṃ pravadasva me||36||

37devadūta uvāca|
brahmaṇaḥ sadanādūrdhvaṃ tadviṣṇoḥ paramaṃ padam
|
śuddhaṃ sanātanaṃ jyotiḥ paraṃ brahmeti yadviduḥ||37||

38na tatra vipra gacchanti puruyā viṣayātmakāḥ
|
dambhalobhamahākrodhamohadrohairabhidrutāḥ||38||

39nirmamā nirahaṅkārā nirdvandvāḥ saṃyatendriyāḥ
|
dhyānayogaparāścaiva tatra gacchanti mānavāḥ||39||

40etat te sarvamākhyātaṃ yanmāṃ pṛcchasi mudgala
|
tavānukampayā sādho sādhu gacchāma mā ciram||40||

41vyāsa uvāca|
etacchrutvā tu maudgalyo vākyaṃ vimamṛśe dhiyā
|
vimṛśya ca muniśreṣṭho devadūtamuvāca ha||41||

42devadūta namaste'stu gaccha tāta yathāsukham
|
mahādoṣeṇa me kāryaṃ na svargeṇa sukhena vā||42||

43patanānte mahāduḥkhaṃ paritāpaḥ sudāruṇaḥ
|
svargabhājaścarantīha tasmāt svargaṃ na kāmaye||43||

44yatra gatvā na śocanti na vyathanti calanti vā
|
tadahaṃ sthānamatyantaṃ mārgayiṣyāmi kevalam||44||

45ityuktvā sa munirvākyaṃ devadūtaṃ visṛjya tam
|
śiloñchavṛttirdharmātmā śamamātiṣṭhaduttamam||45||

46tulyanindāstutirbhūtvā samaloṣṭhāśmakāñcanaḥ
|
jñānayogena śuddhena dhyānanityo babhūva ha||46||

47dhyānayogādbalaṃ labdhvā prāpya buddhimanuttamām
|
jagāma śāśvatīṃ siddhiṃ parāṃ nirvāṇalakṣaṇām||47||

48tasmāt tvamapi kaunteya na śokaṃ kartumarhasi
|
rājyāt sphītāt paribhraṣṭastapasā tadavāpsyasi||48||

49sukhasyānantaraṃ duḥkhaṃ duḥkhasyānantaraṃ sukham
|
paryāyeṇopasarpante naraṃ nemimarā iva||49||

50pitṛpaitāmahaṃ rājyaṃ prāpsyasyamitavikrama
|
varṣāttrayodaśādūrdhvaṃ vyetu te mānaso jvaraḥ||50||

51vaiśampāyana uvāca|
sa evamuktvā bhagavān vyāsaḥ pāṇḍavanandanam
|
jagāma tapase dhīmān punarevāśramaṃ prati||51||
