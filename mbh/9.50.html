Mahābhārata 9.50 (Śalyaparvan)

1vaiśampāyana uvāca|
tasminneva ta dharmātmā vasati sma tapodhanaḥ
|
gārhasthyaṃ dharmamāsthāya hyasito devalaḥ purā||1||

2dharmanityaḥ śucirdānto nyastadaṇḍo mahātapāḥ
|
karmaṇā manasā vācā samaḥ sarveṣu jantuṣu||2||

3akrodhano mahārāja tulyanindātmasaṃstutiḥ
|
priyāpriye tulyavṛttiryamavatsamadarśanaḥ||3||

4kāñcane loṣṭhabhāve ca samadarśī mahātapāḥ
|
devānapūjayannityamatithīṃśca dvijaiḥ saha||4||

5brahmacaryarato nityaṃ sadā dharmaparāyaṇaḥ
|
tato'bhyetya mahābhāga yogamāsthāya bhikṣukaḥ||5||

6jaigīṣavyo munirdhīmāṃstasmiṃstīrthe samāhitaḥ
|
devalasyāśrame rājannyavasatsa mahādyutiḥ||6||

7yoganityo mahārāja siddhiṃ prāpto mahātapāḥ
|
taṃ tatra vasamānaṃ tu jaigīṣavyaṃ mahāmunim||7||

8devalo darśayanneva naivāyuñjata dharmataḥ
|
evaṃ tayormahārāja dīrghakālo vyatikramat||8||

9jaigīṣavyaṃ munivaraṃ va dadarśātha devalaḥ
|
āhārakāle matimānparivrāḍjanamejaya||9||

10upātiṣṭhata dharmajño bhaikṣakāle sa devalam
|
sa dṛṣṭvā bhikṣurūpeṇa prāptaṃ tatra mahāmunim||10||

11gauravaṃ paramaṃ cakre prītiṃ ca vipulāṃ tathā
|
devalastu yathāśakti pūjayāmāsa bhārata||11||

12ṛṣidṛṣṭena vidhinā samā bahvīḥ samāhitaḥ
|
kadācittasya nṛpate devalasya mahātmanaḥ||12||

13cintā sumahatī jātā muniṃ dṛṣṭvā mahādyutim
|
samāstu samatikrāntā bahvyaḥ pūjayato mama||13||

14na cāyamalaso bhikṣurabhyabhāṣata kiñcana
|
evaṃ vigaṇayanneva sa jagāma mahodadhim||14||

15antarikṣacaraḥ śrīmān kalaśaṃ gṛhya devalaḥ
|
gacchanneva sa dharmātmā samudraṃ saritāṃ patim||15||

16jaigīṣavyaṃ tato'paśyadgataṃ prāgeva bhārata
|
tataḥ savismayaścintāṃ jagāmāthāmitaprabhaḥ||16||

17kathaṃ bhikṣurayaṃ prāptaḥ samudre snāta eva ca
|
ityevaṃ cintayāmāsa maharṣirasitastadā||17||

18snātvā samudre vidhivacchucirjapyaṃ jajāpa saḥ
|
kṛtajapyāhnikaḥ śrīmānāśramaṃ ca jagāma ha||18||

19kalaśaṃ jalapūrṇaṃ vai gṛhītvā janamejaya
|
tataḥ sa praviśanneva svamāśramapadaṃ muniḥ||19||

20āsīnamāśrame tatra jaigīṣavyamapaśyata
|
na vyāharati caivainaṃ jaigīṣavyaḥ kathañcana||20||

21kāṣṭhabhūto''śramapade vasati sma mahātapāḥ
|
taṃ dṛṣṭvā cāplutaṃ toye sāgare sāgaropamam||21||

22praviṣṭamāśramaṃ cāpi pūrvameva dadarśa saḥ
|
asito devalo rājaṃścintayāmāsa buddhimān||22||

23dṛṣṭvā prabhāvaṃ tapaso jaigīṣavyasya yogajam
|
cintayāmāsa rājendra tadā sa munisattamaḥ||23||

24mayā dṛṣṭaḥ samudre ca āśrame ca kathaṃ tvayam
|
evaṃ vigaṇayanneva sa munirmantrapāragaḥ||24||

25utpapātāśramāttasmādantarikṣaṃ viśāmpate
|
jijñāsārthaṃ tadā bhikṣorjaigīṣavyasya devalaḥ||25||

26so'ntarikṣacarān siddhān samapaśyatsamāhitān
|
jaigīṣavyaṃ ca taiḥ siddhaiḥ pūjyamānamapaśyata||26||

27tato'sitaḥ susaṃrabdho vyavasāyī dṛḍhavrataḥ
|
apaśyadvai divaṃ yāntaṃ jaigīṣavyaṃ sa devalaḥ||27||

28tasmāttu pitṛlokaṃ taṃ vrajantaṃ so'nvapaśyata
|
pitṛlokācca taṃ yāntaṃ yāmyaṃ lokamapaśyata||28||

29tasmādapi samutpatya somalokamabhiplutam
|
vrajantamanvapaśyatsa jaigīṣavyaṃ mahāmunim||29||

30lokānsamutpatantaṃ tu śubhānekāntayājinām
|
tato'gnihotriṇāṃ lokāṃstataścāpyutpapāta ha||30||

31darśaṃ ca paurṇamāsaṃ ca ye yajanti tapodhanāḥ
|
tebhyaḥ sa dadṛśe dhīmā~llokebhyaḥ paśuyājinām||31||

32vrajantaṃ lokamamalamapaśyaddevapūjitam
|
cāturmāsyairbahuvidhairyajante ye tapodhanāḥ||32||

33teṣāṃ sthānaṃ tato yātaṃ tathāgniṣṭomayājinām
|
agniṣṭutena ca tathā ye yajanti tapodhanāḥ||33||

34tatsthānamanusamprāptamanvapaśyata devalaḥ
|
vājapeyaṃ kratuvaraṃ tathā bahusuvarṇakam||34||

35āharanti mahāprājñāsteṣāṃ lokeṣvapaśyata
|
yajante rājasūyena puṇḍarīkeṇa caiva ye||35||

36teṣāṃ lokeṣvapaśyacca jaigīṣavyaṃ sa devalaḥ
|
aśvamedhaṃ kratuvaraṃ naramedhaṃ tathaiva ca||36||

37āharanti naraśreṣṭhāsteṣāṃ lokeṣvapaśyata
|
sarvamedhaṃ ca duṣprāpaṃ tathā sautrāmaṇiṃ ca ye||37||

38teṣāṃ lokeṣvapaśyacca jaigīṣavyaṃ sa devalaḥ
|
dvādaśāhaiśca satraiśca yajante vividhairnṛpa||38||

39teṣāṃ lokeṣvapaśyacca jaigīṣavyaṃ sa devalaḥ
|
maitrāvaruṇayorlokānādityānāṃ tathaiva ca||39||

40salokatāmanuprāptamapaśyata tato'sitaḥ
|
rudrāṇāṃ ca vasūnāṃ ca sthānaṃ yacca bṛhaspateḥ||40||

41tāni sarvāṇyatītāni samapaśyattato'sitaḥ
|
āruhya ca gavāṃ lokaṃ prayāto brahmasatriṇām||41||

42lokānapaśyadgacchantaṃ jaigīṣavyaṃ tato'sitaḥ
|
trī~llokānaparān vipramutpatantaṃ svatejasā||42||

43pativratānāṃ lokāṃśca vrajantaṃ so'nvapaśyata
|
tato munivaraṃ bhūyo jaigīṣavyamathāsitaḥ||43||

44nānvapaśyata lokasthamantarhitamarindama
|
so'cintayanmahābhāgo jaigīṣavyasya devalaḥ||44||

45prabhāvaṃ suvratatvaṃ ca siddhiṃ yogasya cātulām
|
asito'pṛcchata sadā siddhā~llokeṣu sattamān||45||

46prayataḥ prāñjalirbhūtvā dhīrastānbrahmasatriṇaḥ
|
jaigīṣavyaṃ na paśyāmi taṃ śaṃsadhvaṃ mahaujasam||46||

47etadicchāmyahaṃ śrotuṃ paraṃ kautūhalaṃ hi me
|
siddhā ucuḥ|
śṛṇu devala bhūtārthaṃ śaṃsatā no dṛḍhavrata||47||

48jaigīṣavyaḥ sa vai lokaṃ śāśvataṃ brahmaṇo gataḥ
|
vaiśampāyana uvāca|
sa śrutvā vacanaṃ teṣāṃ siddhānāṃ brahmasatriṇām||48||

49asito devalastūrṇamutpapāta papāta ca
|
tataḥ siddhāsta ūcurhi devalaṃ punareva ha||49||

50na devalagatistatra tava gantuṃ tapodhana
|
brahmaṇaḥ sadane vipra jaigīṣavyo yadāptavān||50||

51vaiśampāyana uvāca|
teṣāṃ tadvacanaṃ śrutvā siddhānāṃ devalaḥ punaḥ
|
ānupūrvyeṇa lokāṃstānsarvānavatatāra ha||51||

52svamāśramapadaṃ puṇyamājagāma patatrivat
|
praviśanneva cāpaśyajjaigīṣavyaṃ sa devalaḥ||52||

53tato buddhyā vyagaṇayaddevalo dharmayuktayā
|
dṛṣṭvā prabhāvaṃ tapaso jaigīṣavyasya yogajam||53||

54tato'bravīnmahātmānaṃ jaigīṣavyaṃ sa devalaḥ
|
vinayāvanato rājannupasarpya mahāmunim||54||

55mokṣadharmaṃ samāsthātumiccheyaṃ bhagavannaham
|
tasya tadvacanaṃ śrutvā upadeśaṃ cakāra saḥ||55||

56vidhiṃ ca yogasya paraṃ kāryākāryasya śāstrataḥ
|
saṃnyāsakṛtabuddhiṃ taṃ tato dṛṣṭvā mahātapāḥ||56||

57sarvāścāsya kriyāścakre vidhidṛṣṭena karmaṇā
|
saṃnyāsakṛtabuddhiṃ taṃ bhūtāni pitṛbhiḥ saha||57||

58tato dṛṣṭvā praruruduḥ ko'smānsaṃvibhajiṣyati
|
devalastu vacaḥ śrutvā bhūtānāṃ karuṇaṃ tathā||58||

59diśo daśa vyāharatāṃ mokṣaṃ tyaktuṃ mano dadhe
|
tatastu phalamūlāni pavitrāṇi ca bhārata||59||

60puṣpāṇyoṣadhayaścaiva rorūyanti sahasraśaḥ
|
punarno devalaḥ kṣudro nūnaṃ chetsyati durmatiḥ||60||

61abhayaṃ sarvabhūtebhyo yo dattvā nāvabudhyate
|
tato bhūyo vyagaṇayatsvabuddhyā munisattamaḥ||61||

62mokṣe gārhasthyadharme vā kiṃ nu śreyaskaraṃ bhavet
|
iti niścitya manasā devalo rājasattama||62||

63tyaktvā gārhasthyadharmaṃ sa mokṣadharmamarocayat
|
evamādīni sañcintya devalo niścayāttataḥ||63||

64prāptavānparamāṃ siddhiṃ paraṃ yogaṃ ca bhārata
|
tato devāḥ samāgamya bṛhaspatipurogamāḥ||64||

65jaigīṣavye tapaścāsya praśaṃsanti tapasvinaḥ
|
athābravīdṛṣivaro devānvai nāradastathā||65||

66jaigīṣavye tapo nāsti vismāpayati yo'sitam
|
tamevaṃvādinaṃ dhīraṃ pratyūcuste divaukasaḥ||66||

67naivamityeva śaṃsanto jaigīṣavyaṃ mahāmunim
|
nātaḥ parataraṃ kiñcittulyamasti prabhāvataḥ||67||

68tejasastapasaścāsya yogasya ca mahātmanaḥ
|
evaṃ prabhāvo dharmātmā jaigīṣavyastathāsitaḥ
|
tayoridaṃ sthānavaraṃ tīrthaṃ caiva mahātmanoḥ||68||

69tatrāpyupaspṛśya tato mahātmā dattvā ca vittaṃ halabhṛddvijebhyaḥ
|
avāpya dharmaṃ paramārthakarmā jagāma somasya mahatsutīrtham||69||
