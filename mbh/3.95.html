Mahābhārata 3.95 (Āraṇyakaparvan)

1vaiśaṃpāyana uvāca| te tathā sahitā vīrā vasantastatra tatra ha
|
krameṇa pṛthivīpāla naimiṣāraṇyamāgatāḥ||1||

2tatastīrtheṣu puṇyeṣu gomatyāḥ pāṇḍavā nṛpa
|
kṛtābhiṣekāḥ pradadurgāśca vittaṃ ca bhārata||2||

3tatra devānpitṝnviprāṃstarpayitvā punaḥpunaḥ
|
kanyātīrthe'śvatīrthe ca gavāṃ tīrthe ca bhārata
|
kālakoṭyāṃ viṣaprasthe girāvuṣya ca kauravāḥ||3||

4bāhudāyāṃ mahīpāla cakruḥ sarvebhiṣecanam
|
prayāge devayajane devānāṃ pṛthivīpate||4||

5ūṣurāplutya gātrāṇi tapaścātasthuruttamam
|
gaṅgāyamunayoścaiva saṃgame satyasaṃgarāḥ||5||

6vipāpmāno mahātmāno viprebhyaḥ pradadurvasu
|
tapasvijanajuṣṭāṃ ca tato vedīṃ prajāpateḥ||6||

7jagmuḥ pāṇḍusutā rājanbrāhmaṇaiḥ saha bhārata
|
tatra te nyavasanvīrāstapaścātasthuruttamam||7||

8saṃtarpayantaḥ satataṃ vanyena haviṣā dvijān
|
tato mahīdharaṃ jagmurdharmajñenābhisatkṛtam||8||

9rājarṣiṇā puṇyakṛtā gayenānupamadyute
|
nago gayaśiro yatra puṇyā caiva mahānadī||9||

10vānīramālinī ramyā nadī pulinaśobhitā
|
divyaṃ pavitrakūṭaṃ ca pavitraṃ dharaṇīdharam||10||

11ṛṣijuṣṭaṃ supuṇyaṃ tattīrthaṃ brahmasarottamam
|
agastyo bhagavānyatra gato vaivasvataṃ prati||11||

12uvāsa ca svayaṃ tatra dharmarājaḥ sanātanaḥ
|
sarvāsāṃ saritāṃ caiva samudbhedo viśāṃpate||12||

13yatra saṃnihito nityaṃ mahādevaḥ pinākadhṛk
|
tatra te pāṇḍavā vīrāścāturmāsyaistadejire||13||

14ṛṣiyajñena mahatā yatrākṣayavaṭo mahān
|
akṣaye devayajane akṣayaṃ yatra vai phalam||14||

15ye tu tatropavāsāṃstu cakrurniścitamānasāḥ
|
brāhmaṇāstatra śataśaḥ samājagmustapodhanāḥ||15||

16cāturmāsyenāyajanta ārṣeṇa vidhinā tadā
|
tatra vidyātapovṛddhā brāhmaṇā vedapāragāḥ
|
kathāṃ pracakrire puṇyāṃ sadasisthā mahātmanām||16||

17tatra vidyāvratasnātaḥ kaumāraṃ vratamāsthitaḥ
|
śamaṭhokathayadrājannāmūrtarayasaṃ gayam||17||

18śamaṭha uvāca| āmūrtarayasaḥ putro gayo rājarṣisattamaḥ
|
puṇyāni yasya karmāṇi tāni me śṛṇu bhārata||18||

19yasya yajño babhūveha bahvanno bahudakṣiṇaḥ
|
yatrānnaparvatā rājañśataśo'tha sahasraśaḥ||19||

20ghṛtakulyāśca dadhnaśca nadyo bahuśatāstathā
|
vyaṃjanānāṃ pravāhāśca mahārhāṇāṃ sahasraśaḥ||20||

21ahanyahani cāpyevaṃ yācatāṃ saṃpradīyate
|
anye ca brāhmaṇā rājanbhuñjate'nnaṃ susaṃskṛtam||21||

22tatra vai dakṣiṇākāle brahmaghoṣo divaṃ gataḥ
|
na ca prajñāyate kiṃcidbrahmaśabdena bhārata||22||

23puṇyena caratā rājanbhūrdiśaḥ khaṃ nabhastathā
|
āpūrṇamāsīcchabdena tadapyāsīnmahādbhutam||23||

24yatra sma gāthā gāyanti manuṣyā bharatarṣabha
|
annapānaiḥ śubhaistṛptā deśe deśe suvarcasaḥ||24||

25gayasya yajñe ke tvadya prāṇino bhoktumīpsavaḥ
|
tatra bhojanaśiṣṭasya parvatāḥ pañcaviṃśatiḥ||25||

26na tatpūrve janāścakrurna kariṣyanti cāpare
|
gayo yadakarodyajñe rājarṣiramitadyutiḥ||26||

27kathaṃ tu devā haviṣā gayena paritarpitāḥ
|
punaḥ śakṣyantyupādātumanyairdattāni kānicit||27||

28sikatā vā yathā loke yathā vā divi tārakāḥ
|
yathā vā varṣato dhārā asaṃkhyeyāḥ sma kenacit
|
tathā gaṇayituṃ śakyā gayayajñena dakṣiṇāḥ||28||

29evaṃvidhāḥ subahavastasya yajñā mahīpateḥ
|
babhūvurasya sarasaḥ samīpe kurunandana||29||
