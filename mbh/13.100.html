Mahābhārata 13.100 (Anuśāsanaparvan)

1yudhiṣṭhira uvāca|
kathaṃ vai sa vipannaśca kathaṃ vai pātito bhuvi
|
kathaṃ cānindratāṃ prāptastadbhavānvaktumarhati ||1||

2bhīṣma uvāca|
evaṃ tayoḥ saṃvadatoḥ kriyāstasya mahātmanaḥ
|
sarvā eva pravartante yā divyā yāśca mānuṣīḥ ||2||

3tathaiva dīpadānāni sarvopakaraṇāni vai
|
balikarma ca yaccānyadvatsakāśca pṛthagvidhāḥ ||3||

4sarve tasya samutpannā devendrasya mahātmanaḥ
|
devaloke nṛloke ca sadācārā budhāḥ smṛtāḥ ||4||

5te cedbhavanti rājendra ṛddhyante gṛhamedhinaḥ
|
dhūpapradānairdīpaiśca namaskāraistathaiva ca ||5||

6yathā siddhasya cānnasya grahāyāgraṃ pradīyate
|
balayaśca gṛhoddeśe ataḥ prīyanti devatāḥ ||6||

7yathā ca gṛhiṇastoṣo bhavedvai balikarmaṇi
|
tathā śataguṇā prītirdevatānāṃ prajāyate ||7||

8evaṃ dhūpapradānaṃ ca dīpadānaṃ ca sādhavaḥ
|
prayacchanti namaskārairyuktamātmaguṇāvaham ||8||

9snānenādbhiśca yatkarma kriyate vai vipaścitā
|
namaskāraprayuktena tena prīyanti devatāḥ ||9||

10pitaraśca mahābhāgā ṛṣayaśca tapodhanāḥ
|
gṛhyāśca devatāḥ sarvāḥ prīyante vidhinārcitāḥ ||10||

11ityetāṃ buddhimāsthāya nahuṣaḥ sa nareśvaraḥ
|
surendratvaṃ mahatprāpya kṛtavānetadadbhutam ||11||

12kasyacittvatha kālasya bhāgyakṣaya upasthite
|
sarvametadavajñāya kṛtavānida‌mīdṛśam ||12||

13tataḥ sa parihīṇo'bhūtsurendro baladarpataḥ
|
dhūpadīpodakavidhiṃ na yathāvaccakāra ha ||13||

14tato'sya yajñaviṣayo rakṣobhiḥ paryabadhyata
|
athāgastyamṛṣiśreṣṭhaṃ vāhanāyājuhāva ha ||14||

15drutaṃ sarasvatīkūlāt smayanniva mahābalaḥ
|
tato bhṛgurmahātejā maitrāvaruṇimabravīt ||15||

16nimīlaya svanayane jaṭāṃ yāvadviśāmi te
|
sthāṇubhūtasya tasyātha jaṭāṃ prāviśadacyutaḥ ||16||

17bhṛguḥ sa sumahātejāḥ pātanāya nṛpasya ca
|
tataḥ sa devarāṭ prāptastamṛṣiṃ vāhanāya vai ||17||

18tato'gastyaḥ surapatiṃ vākyamāha viśāṃpate
|
yojayasveti māṃ kṣipraṃ kaṃ ca deśaṃ vahāmi te ||18||

19yatra vakṣyasi tatra tvāṃ nayiṣyāmi surādhipa
|
ityukto nahuṣastena yojayāmāsa taṃ munim ||19||

20bhṛgustasya jaṭāntastho babhūva hṛṣito bhṛśam
|
na cāpi darśanaṃ tasya cakāra sa bhṛgustadā ||20||

21varadānaprabhāvajño nahuṣasya mahātmanaḥ
|
na cukopa tadā'gastyo yukto'pi nahuṣeṇa vai ||21||

22taṃ tu rājā pratodena coyāmāsa bhārata
|
na cukopa sa dharmātmā tataḥ pādena devarāṭ ||22||

23agastyasya tadā kruddho vāmenābhyahanacchiraḥ
|
tasmin śirasyabhihate sa jaṭāntargato bhṛguḥ ||23||

24śaśāpa balavatkruddho nahuṣaṃ pāpacetasam
|
yasmātpadā'hataḥ krodhācchirasīmaṃ mahāmunim ||24||

25tasmādāśu mahīṃ gaccha sarpo bhūtvā sudurmate
|
ityuktaḥ sa tadā tena sarpo bhūtvā papāta ha ||25||

26adṛṣṭenātha bhṛguṇā bhūtale bharatarṣabha
|
bhṛguṃ hi yadi so'drakṣyannahuṣaḥ pṛthivīpate ||26||

27na ca śakto'bhaviṣyadvai pātane tasya tejasā
|
sa tu taistaiḥ pradānaiśca tapobhirniyamaistathā ||27||

28patito'pi mahārāja bhūtale smṛtimānabhūt
|
prasā‌dayāmāsa bhṛguṃ śāpānto me bhavediti ||28||

29tato'gastyaḥ kṛpāviṣṭaḥ prāsādayata taṃ bhṛgum
|
śāpāntārthaṃ mahārāja sa ca prādātkṛpānvitaḥ ||29||

30bhṛguruvāca|
rājā yudhiṣṭhiro nāma bhaviṣyati kulodvahaḥ
|
sa tvāṃ mokṣayitā śāpādityuktvā'ntaradhīyata ||30||

31agastyo'pi mahātejāḥ kṛtvā kāryaṃ śatakratoḥ
|
svamāśramapadaṃ prāyātpūjyamāno dvijātibhiḥ ||31||

32nahuṣopi tvayā rājaṃstasmācchāpātsamuddhṛtaḥ
|
jagāma brahmabhavanaṃ paśyataste janādhipa ||32||

33tadā sa pātayitvā taṃ nahuṣaṃ bhūtale bhṛguḥ
|
jagāma brahmabhavanaṃ brahmaṇe ca nyavedayat ||33||

34tataḥ śakraṃ samānāyya devānāha pitāmahaḥ
|
varadānānmama surā nahuṣo rājyamāptavān ||34||

35sa cāgastyena kruddhena bhraṃśito bhūtalaṃ gataḥ
|
na ca śakyaṃ vinā rājñā surā vartayituṃ kvacit ||35||

36tasmādayaṃ punaḥ śakro devarājye'bhiṣicyatām
|
evaṃ saṃbhāṣamāṇaṃ tu devāḥ pārtha pitāmaham ||36||

37evamastviti saṃhṛṣṭāḥ pratyūcustaṃ narādhipa
|
so'bhiṣikto bhagavatā devarājye ca vāsavaḥ ||37||

38brahmaṇā rājaśārdūla yathāpūrvaṃ vyarocata
|
evametatpurāvṛttaṃ nahuṣasya vyatikramāt ||38||

39sa ca taireva saṃsiddho nahuṣaḥ karmabhiḥ punaḥ
|
tasmāddīpāḥ pradātavyāḥ sāyaṃ vai gṛhamedhibhiḥ ||39||

40divyaṃ cakṣuravāpnoti pretya dīpasya dāyakaḥ
|
pūrṇacandrapratīkāśā dīpadāśca bhavantyuta ||40||

41yāvadakṣinimeṣāṇi jvalante tāvatīḥ samāḥ
|
rūpavānbalavāṃścāpi naro bhavati dīpadaḥ ||41||
