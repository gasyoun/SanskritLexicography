Mahābhārata 15.38 (Āśramavāsikaparvan)

1yudhiṣṭhira uvāca|
tathā mahātmanastasya tapasyugre ca vartataḥ
|
anāthasyeva vacane tiṣṭhatsvāsmāsu bandhuṣu||1||

2durvijñeyā gatirbrahmanpuruṣāṇāṃ matirmama
|
yatra vaicitravīryo'sau dagdha evaṃ vanāgninā||2||

3yasya putraśataṃ śrīmadabhavadbāhuśālinaḥ
|
nāgāyutabalo rājā sa dagdho hi davāgninā||3||

4yaṃ purā paryavījanta tālavṛntairvarastriyaḥ
|
taṃ gṛdhrāḥ paryavījanta dāvāgniparikālitam||4||

5sūtamāgadhasaṅgheśca śayāno yaḥ prabodhyate
|
dharaṇyāṃ sa nṛpaḥ śete pāpasya mama karmabhiḥ||5||

6na ca śocāmi gāndhārīṃ hataputrāṃ yaśasvinīm
|
patilokamanuprāptāṃ tathā bhartṛvrate sthitām||6||

7pṛthāmeva ca śocāmi yā putraiśvaryamṛddhimat
|
utsṛjya sumahaddīptaṃ vanavāsamarocayat||7||

8dhigrājyamidamasmākaṃ dhigbalaṃ dhikparākramam
|
kṣatradharmaṃ ca dhigyasmānmṛtā jīvāmahe vayam||8||

9susūkṣmā kila kālasya gatirdvijavarottama
|
yatsamutsṛhya rājyaṃ sā vanavāsamarocayat||9||

10yudhiṣṭhirasya jananī bhīmasya vijayasya ca
|
anāthavatkathaṃ dagdha iti muhyāmi cintayan||10||

11vṛthā santarpito vahniḥ khāṇḍave savyasācinā
|
upakāramajānansa kṛtaghna iti me matiḥ||11||

12yatrādahatsa bhagavān mātaraṃ savyasācinaḥ
|
kṛtvā yo brāhmaṇacchadmā bhikṣārthī samupāgataḥ||12||

13dhigagniṃ dhikca pārthasya viśrutāṃ satyasandhatām
|
idaṃ kaṣṭataraṃ cānyadbhagavanpratibhāti me||13||

14vṛthāgninā samāyogo yadabhūtpṛthivīpateḥ
|
tathā tapasvinastasya rājarṣeḥ kauravasya ha||14||

15kathamevaṃvidho mṛtyuḥ praśāsya pṛthivīmimām
|
tiṣṭhatsu mantrapūteṣū tasyāgniṣu mahāvane||15||

16vṛthāgninā samāyukto niṣṭhāṃ prāptaḥ pitā mama
|
manye pṛthā vepamānā kṛśā dhamanisantatā||16||

17hā tāta dharmarājeti samākrandanmahābhaye
|
bhīma paryāpnuhi bhayāditi caivābhivāśatī||17||

18samantataḥ parikṣiptā mātābhūnme davāgninā
|
sahadevaḥ priyastasyāḥ putrebhyo'dhika eva tu||18||

19na caināṃ mokṣayāmāsa vīro mādravatīsutaḥ
|
tacchutvā ruruduḥ sarve samāliṅya parasparam||19||

20pāṇḍavāḥ pañca duḥkhārtā bhūtānīva yugakṣaye
|
teṣāṃ tu puruṣendrāṇāṃ rudatāṃ ruditasvanaḥ||20||

21prāsādābhogasaṃruddhe anvarautsītsa rodasī||21||
