Mahābhārata 1.79 (Ādiparvan)

1śukra uvāca|
yaḥ pareṣāṃ naro nityamativādāṃstitikṣate
|
devayāni vijānīhi tena sarvamidaṃ jitam||1||

2yaḥ samutpatitaṃ krodhaṃ nigṛhṇāti hayaṃ yathā
|
sa yantetyucyate sadbhirna yo raśmiṣu lambate||2||

3yaḥ samutpatitaṃ krodhamakrodhena nirasyati
|
devayāni vijānīhi tena sarvamidaṃ jitam||3||

4yaḥ samutpatitaṃ krodhaṃ kṣamayeha nirasyati
|
yathoragastvacaṃ jīrṇāṃ sa vai puruṣa ucyate||4||

5yaḥ saṃdhārayate manyuṃ yo'tivādāṃstitikṣate
|
yaśca tapto na tapati dṛḍhaṃ so'rthasya bhājanam||5||

6yo yajedapariśrānto māsi māsi śataṃ samāḥ
|
na kruddhyedyaśca sarvasya tayorakrodhano'dhikaḥ||6||

7yatkumārāḥ kumāryaśca vairaṃ kuryuracetasaḥ
|
na tatprājño'nukurvīta na viduste balābalam||7||

8devayānyuvāca|
vedāhaṃ tāta bālā'pi dharmāṇāṃ yadihāntaram
|
akrodhe cāpi vāde ca veda cāpi balābalam||8||

9śiṣyasyāśiṣyavṛttestu na kṣantavyaṃ bubhūṣatā
|
tasmātsaṃkīrṇavṛtteṣu vāso mama na rocate||9||

10pumāṃso ye hi nindanti vṛttenābhijanena ca
|
na teṣu nivasetprājñaḥ śreyo'rthī pāpabuddhiṣu||10||

11ye tvenamabhijānanti vṛttenābhijanena vā
|
teṣu sādhuṣu vastavyaṃ sa vāsaḥ śreṣṭha ucyate||11||

12vāgduruktaṃ mahāghoraṃ duhiturvṛṣaparvaṇaḥ
|
mama mathnāti hṛdayamagnikāma ivāraṇim||12||

13na hyato duṣkarataraṃ manye lokeṣvapi triṣu
|
yaḥ sapatnaśriyaṃ dīptāṃ hīnaśrīḥ paryupāsate
|
maraṇaṃ śobhanaṃ tasya iti vidvajjanā viduḥ||13||
