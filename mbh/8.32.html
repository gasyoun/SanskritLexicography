Mahābhārata 8.32 (Karṇaparvan)

1sañjaya uvāca|
putrastava mahārāja madrarājaṃ mahāratham
|
vinayenopasaṃgamya praṇayādvākyamabravīt ||1||

2satyavrata mahābhāga dviṣatāṃ tāpavardhana
|
madreśvara raṇe śūra parasainyabhayaṅkara ||2||

3śrutavānasi karṇasya bruvato vadatāṃ vara
|
yathā nṛpatisiṃhānāṃ madhye tvāṃ varaye svayam ||3||

4tattvāmaprativīryādya śatrupakṣakṣayāvaha
|
madreśvara prayāce'haṃ śirasā vinayena ca ||4||

5tasmātpārthavināśārthaṃ hitārthaṃ mama caiva hi
|
sārathyaṃ rathināṃ śreṣṭha praṇayātkartumarhasi ||5||

6tvayi yantari rādheyo vidviṣo me vijeṣyate
|
abhīṣūṇāṃ hi karṇasya grahītā'nyo na vidyate ||6||

7ṛte hi tvāṃ mahābhāga vāsudevasamaṃ yudhi
|
sa pāhi sarvathā karṇaṃ yathā brahmā maheśvaram ||7||

8yathā ca sarvathā''patsu vārṣṇeyaḥ pāti pāṇḍavam
|
tathā madreśvarādya tvaṃ rādheyaṃ pratipālaya ||8||

9bhīṣmo droṇaḥ kṛpaḥ karṇo bhavān bhojaśca vīryavān
|
śakuniḥ saubalo drauṇirahameva ca no balam ||9||

10evameṣa kṛto bhāgo navadhā pṛthivīpate
|
na ca bhāgotra bhīṣmasya droṇasya ca mahātmanaḥ ||10||

11tābhyāmatītya tau bhāgau nihatā mama śatravaḥ
|
vṛddhau hi tau maheṣvāsau chalena nihatau yudhi ||11||

12kṛtvā nasukaraṃ karma gatau svargamito'nagha
|
tathā'nye puruṣavyāghrāḥ parairvinihatā yudhi ||12||

13asmadīyāśca bahavaḥ svargāyopagatā raṇe
|
tyaktvā prāṇān yathāśakti ceṣṭāṃ kṛtvā ca puṣkalām ||13||

14tadidaṃ hatabhūyiṣṭhaṃ balaṃ mama narādhipa
|
pūrvamapyalpakaiḥ pārthairhataṃ kimuta sāṃpratam ||14||

15balavanto mahātmānaḥ kaunteyāḥ satyavikramāḥ
|
balaṃ śeṣaṃ na hanyurme yathā tatkuru pārthiva ||15||

16hatavīramidaṃ sainyaṃ pāṇḍavaiḥ samare vibho
|
karṇo hyeko mahābāhurasmatpriyahite rataḥ ||16||

17bhavāṃśca puruṣavyāghra sarvalokamahārathaḥ
|
śalyaṃ karṇo'rjunenādya yoddhumicchati saṃyuge ||17||

18tasmiñjayāśā vipulā madrarāja narādhipa
|
tasyābhīṣugrahavaro nānyo'sti bhuvi kaścana ||18||

19pārthasya lamare kṛṣṇo yathābhīṣugraho varaḥ
|
tathā tvamapi karṇasya rathe'bhīṣugraho bhava ||19||

20tena yukto raṇe pārtho rakṣyamāṇaśca pārthiva
|
yāni karmāṇi kurute pratyakṣāṇi tathaiva tat ||20||

21pūrvaṃ naḥ samare hyevamavadhīdarjuno ripūn
|
idānīṃ vikramo hyasya kṛṣṇena sahitasya ca ||21||

22kṛṣṇena sahitaḥ pārtho dhārtarāṣṭrīṃ mahācamūm
|
ahanyahani madreśa drāvayan dṛśyate yudhi ||22||

23bhāgo'vaśiṣṭaḥ karṇasya tava caiva mahādyute
|
taṃ bhāgaṃ saha karṇena yugapannāśayādya hi ||23||

24aruṇena yathā sārdhaṃ tamaḥ sūryo vyapohati
|
tathā karṇena sahito jahi pārthaṃ mahāhave ||24||

25udyantau ca yathā sūryau bālasūryasamaprabhau
|
karṇaśalyau raṇe dṛṣṭvā vidravantu mahārathāḥ ||25||

26sūryāruṇau yathā dṛṣṭvā tamo naśyati māriṣa
|
tathā naśyantu kaunteyāḥ sapañcālāḥ sasuñjayāḥ ||26||

27rathināṃ pravaraḥ karṇo yantṝṇāṃ pravaro bhavān
|
saṃyogo yuvayorloke nābhūnna ca bhaviṣyati ||27||

28yathā sarvāsvavasthāsu vārṣṇeyaḥ pāti pāṇḍavam
|
tathā bhavānparitrātu karṇaṃ vaikartanaṃ raṇe ||28||

29tvayā sārathinā hyeṣa apradhṛṣyo bhaviṣyati
|
devatānāmapi raṇe saśakrāṇāṃ mahīpate
|
kiṃ punaḥ pāṇḍaveyānāṃ mā viśaṃkīrvaco mama ||29||

30sañjaya uvāca|
duryodhanavacaḥ śrutvā śalyaḥ krodhasamanvitaḥ
|
triśikhāṃ bhrukuṭiṃ kṛtvā dhunvan hastau punaḥ punaḥ ||30||

31krodharakte mahānetre parivṛtya mahābhujaḥ
|
kulaiśvaryaśrutabalairdṛptaḥ śalyo'bravīdidam ||31||

32śalya uvāca|
avamanyasi gāndhāre dhruvaṃ ca pariśaṃkase
|
yanmāṃ bravīṣi viśrabdhaṃ sārathyaṃ kriyatāmiti ||32||

33asmatto'bhyadhikaṃ karṇaṃ manyamānaḥ praśaṃsasi
|
na cāhaṃ yudhi rādheyaṃ gaṇaye tulyamātmanaḥ ||33||

34ādiśyatāmabhyadhiko mamāṃśaḥ pṛthivīpate
|
tamahaṃ samare jitvā gamiṣyāmi yathāgatam ||34||

35athavā'pyeka evāhaṃ yotsyāmi kurunandana
|
paśya vīryaṃ mamādya tvaṃ saṃgrāme dahato ripūn ||35||

36yathābhimānaṃ kauravya nidhāya hṛdaye pumān
|
asmadvidhaḥ pravarteta mā māṃ tvamabhiśaṃkithāḥ ||36||

37yudhi vā'pyavamāno me na kartavyaḥ kathañcana
|
paśya pīnau mama bhujau vajrasaṃhananopamau ||37||

38dhanuḥ paśya ca me citraṃ śarāṃścāśīviṣopamān
|
rathaṃ paśya ca me klṛptaṃ sadaśvairvātavegitaiḥ ||38||

39gadāṃ ca paśya gāndhāre hemapaṭṭavibhūṣitām
|
dārayeyaṃ mahīṃ kṛtsnāṃ vikireyaṃ ca parvatān ||39||

40śoṣayeyaṃ samudrāṃśca tejasā svena pārthiva
|
taṃ māmevaṃvidhaṃ rājan samarthamarinigrahe ||40||

41kasmādyunaṅkṣi sārathye nīcasyādhirathe raṇe
|
na māmadhuri rājendra niyoktuṃ tvamihārhasi ||41||

42na hi pāpīyasaḥ śreyān bhūtvā preṣyatvamutsahe
|
yo hyabhyupagataṃ prītyā garīyāṃsaṃ vaśe sthitam ||42||

43vaśe pāpīyaso dhatte tatpāpamadharottaram
|
brahmaṇā brāhmaṇāḥ sṛṣṭā mukhāt kṣatraṃ ca bāhutaḥ ||43||

44ūrubhyāmasṛjadvaiśyān śūdrānpadbhyāmiti śrutiḥ
|
tebhyo varṇaviśeṣāśca pratilomānulomajāḥ ||44||

45athānyonyasya saṃyogāccāturvarṇyasya bhārata
|
goptāraḥ saṃgṛhītāro dātāraḥ kṣatriyāḥ smṛtāḥ ||45||

46yājanādhyāpanairviprā viśuddhaiśca pratigrahaiḥ
|
lokasyānugrahārthāya sthāpitā brāhmaṇā bhuvi ||46||

47kṛṣiśca pāśupālyaṃ ca viśāṃ dānaṃ ca dharmataḥ
|
brahma-kṣatra-viśāṃ śūdrā vihitāḥ paricārakāḥ ||47||

48brahmakṣatrasya vihitāḥ sutā vai paricārakāḥ
|
na kṣatriyo vai sūtānāṃ śṛṇuyācca kathañcana ||48||

49ahaṃ mūrdhābhiṣikto hi rājarṣikulajo nṛpaḥ
|
mahārathaḥ samākhyātaḥ sevyaḥ stutyaśca bandinām ||49||

50so'hametādṛśo bhūtvā nehāribalasūdanaḥ
|
sūtaputrasya saṃgrāme sārathyaṃ kartumutsahe ||50||

51avamānamahaṃ prāpya na yotsyāmi kathañcana
|
āpṛcche tvā'dya gāndhāre gamiṣyāmi gṛhāya vai ||51||

52sañjaya uvāca|
evamuktvā mahārāja śalyaḥ samitiśobhanaḥ
|
utthāya prayayau tūrṇaṃ rājamadhyādamarṣitaḥ ||52||

53praṇayādbahumānācca tannigṛhya sutastava
|
abravīnmadhuraṃ vākyaṃ sāmnā sarvārthasādhakam ||53||

54yathā śalya vijānīṣe evametadasaṃśayam
|
abhiprāyastu me kaścittannibodha janeśvara ||54||

55na karṇo'bhyadhikastvatto na śaṃke tvāṃ ca pārthiva
|
na hi madreśvaro rājā kuryādyadanṛtaṃ bhavet ||55||

56ṛtameva hi pūrvāste vadanti puruṣottamāḥ
|
tasmādārtāyaniḥ prokto bhavāniti matirmama ||56||

57śalyabhūtastu śatraṇāṃ yasmāttvaṃ yudhi mānada
|
tasmācchalyo hi te nāma kathyate pṛthivītale ||57||

58yadetadvyāhṛtaṃ pūrvaṃ bhavatā bhūridakṣiṇa
|
tadeva kuru dharmajña madarthaṃ yadyaducyate ||58||

59na ca tvatto hi rādheyo na cāhamapi vīryavān
|
vṛṇe'haṃ tvāṃ hayāgryāṇāṃ yantāramiha saṃyuge ||59||

60manye cābhyadhikaṃ śalya guṇaiḥ karṇaṃ dhanañjayāt
|
bhavantaṃ vāsudevācca loko'yamiti manyate ||60||

61karṇo hyabhyadhikaḥ pārthādastraireva nararṣabha
|
bhavānabhyadhikaḥ kṛṣṇā‌śvajñāne bale tathā ||61||

62yathā'śvahṛdayaṃ veda vāsudevo mahāmanāḥ
|
dviguṇaṃ tvaṃ tathā vetsi madrarājeśvarātmaja ||62||

63śalya uvāca|
yanmāṃ bravīṣi gāndhāre madhye sainyasya kaurava
|
viśiṣṭaṃ devakīputrātprītimānasmyahaṃ tvayi ||63||

64eṣa sārathyamātiṣṭhe rādheyasya yaśasvinaḥ
|
yudhyataḥ pāṇḍavāgryeṇa yathā tvaṃ vīra manyase ||64||

65samayaśca hi me vīra kaścidvaikartanaṃ prati
|
utsṛjeyaṃ yathāśraddhamahaṃ vāco'sya sannidhau ||65||

66sañjaya uvāca|
tatheti rājanputraste saha karṇena bhārata
|
abravīnmadrarājasya mataṃ bharatasattama ||66||
