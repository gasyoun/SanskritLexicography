Mahābhārata 3.142 (Āraṇyakaparvan)

1lomaśa uvāca|
draṣṭāraḥ parvatāḥ sarve nadyaḥ sapurakānanāḥ
|
tīrthāni caiva śrīmanti spṛṣṭaṃ ca salilaṃ karaiḥ||1||

2parvataṃ mandaraṃ divyameṣa panthāḥ prayāsyati
|
samāhitā nirudvignāḥ sarve bhavata pāṇḍavāḥ||2||

3ayaṃ devanivāso vai gantavyo vo bhaviṣyati
|
ṛṣīṇāṃ caiva divyānāṃ nivāsaḥ puṇyakarmaṇām||3||

4eṣā śivajalā puṇyā yāti saumya mahānadī
|
badarīprabhavā rājandevarṣigaṇasevitā||4||

5eṣā vaihāyasairnityaṃ vālakhilyairmahātmabhiḥ
|
arcitā copayātā ca gandharvaiśca mahātmabhiḥ||5||

6atra sāma sma gāyanti sāmagāḥ puṇyaniḥsvanāḥ
|
marīciḥ pulahaścaiva bhṛguścaivāṅgirāstathā||6||

7atrāhnikaṃ suraśreṣṭho japate samarudgaṇaḥ
|
sādhyāścaivāśvinau caiva paridhāvanti taṃ tadā||7||

8candramāḥ saha sūryeṇa jyotīṃṣi ca grahaiḥ saha
|
ahorātravibhāgena nadīmenāmanuvrajan||8||

9etasyāḥ salilaṃ mūrdhni vṛṣāṅkaḥ paryadhārayat
|
gaṅgādvāre mahābhāga yena lokasthitirbhavet||9||

10etāṃ bhagavatīṃ devīṃ bhavantaḥ sarva eva hi
|
prayatenātmanā tāta pratigamyābhivādata||10||

11tasya tadvacanaṃ śrutvā lomaśasya mahātmanaḥ
|
ākāśagaṅgāṃ prayatāḥ pāṇḍavāste'bhyavādayan||11||

12abhivādya ca te sarve pāṇḍavā dharmacāriṇaḥ
|
punaḥ prayātāḥ saṃhṛṣṭāḥ sarvairṛṣigaṇaiḥ saha||12||

13tato dūrātprakāśantaṃ pāṇḍuraṃ merusaṃnibham
|
dadṛśuste naraśreṣṭhā vikīrṇaṃ sarvato diśam||13||

14tānpraṣṭukāmānvijñāya pāṇḍavānsa tu lomaśaḥ
|
uvāca vākyaṃ vākyajñaḥ śṛṇudhvaṃ pāṇḍunandanāḥ||14||

15etadvikīrṇaṃ suśrīmatkailāsaśikharopamam
|
yatpaśyasi naraśreṣṭha parvatapratimaṃ sthitam||15||

16etānyasthīni daityasya narakasya mahātmanaḥ
|
parvatapratimaṃ bhāti parvataprastarāśritam||16||

17purātanena devena viṣṇunā paramātmanā
|
daityo vinihatastena surarājahitaiṣiṇā||17||

18daśavarṣasahasrāṇi tapastapyanmahāmanāḥ
|
aindraṃ prārthayate sthānaṃ tapaḥsvādhyāyavikramāt||18||

19tapobalena mahatā bāhuvegabalena ca
|
nityameva durādharṣo dharṣayansa diteḥ sutaḥ||19||

20sa tu tasya balaṃ jñātvā dharme ca caritavratam
|
bhayābhibhūtaḥ saṃvignaḥ śakra āsīttadānagha||20||

21tena saṃcintito devo manasā viṣṇuravyayaḥ
|
sarvatragaḥ prabhuḥ śrīmānāgataśca sthito babhau||21||

22ṛṣayaścāpi taṃ sarve tuṣṭuvuśca divaukasaḥ
|
taṃ dṛṣṭvā jvalamānaśrīrbhagavānhavyavāhanaḥ||22||

23naṣṭatejāḥ samabhavattasya tejobhibhartsitaḥ
|
taṃ dṛṣṭvā varadaṃ devaṃ viṣṇuṃ devagaṇeśvaram||23||

24prāñjaliḥ praṇato bhūtvā namaskṛtya ca vajrabhṛt
|
prāha vākyaṃ tatastattvaṃ yatastasya bhayaṃ bhavet||24||

25viṣṇuruvāca|
jānāmi te bhayaṃ śakra daityendrānnarakāttataḥ
|
aindraṃ prārthayate sthānaṃ tapaḥ siddhena karmaṇā||25||

26so'hamenaṃ tava prītyā tapaḥsiddhamapi dhruvam
|
viyunajmi dehāddevendra muhūrtaṃ pratipālaya||26||

27tasya viṣṇurmahātejāḥ pāṇinā cetanāṃ harat
|
sa papāta tato bhūmau girirāja ivāhataḥ||27||

28tasyaitadasthisaṃghātaṃ māyāvinihatasya vai
|
idaṃ dvitīyamaparaṃ viṣṇoḥ karma prakāśate||28||

29naṣṭā vasumatī kṛtsnā pātāle caiva majjitā
|
punaruddharitā tena vārāheṇaikaśṛṅgiṇā||29||

30yudhiṣṭhira uvāca|
bhagavanvistareṇemāṃ kathāṃ kathaya tattvataḥ
|
kathaṃ tena sureśena naṣṭā vasumatī tadā||30||

31yojanānāṃ śataṃ brahmanpunaruddharitā tadā
|
kena caiva prakāreṇa jagato dharaṇī dhruvā||31||

32śivā devī mahābhāgā sarvasasyaprarohiṇī
|
kasya caiva prabhāvāddhi yojanānāṃ śataṃ gatā||32||

33kena tadvīryasarvasvaṃ darśitaṃ paramātmanaḥ
|
etatsarvaṃ yathātattvamicchāmi dvijasattama
|
śrotuṃ vistaraśaḥ sarvaṃ tvaṃ hi tasya pratiśrayaḥ||33||

34lomaśa uvāca|
yatte'haṃ paripṛṣṭo'smi kathāmetāṃ yudhiṣṭhira
|
tatsarvamakhileneha śrūyatāṃ mama bhāṣataḥ||34||

35purā kṛtayuge tāta vartamāne bhayaṃkare
|
yamatvaṃ kārayāmāsa ādidevaḥ purātanaḥ||35||

36yamatvaṃ kurvatastasya devadevasya dhīmataḥ
|
na tatra mriyate kaścijñāyate vā tathācyuta||36||

37vardhante pakṣisaṃghāśca tathā paśugaveḍakam
|
gavāśvaṃ ca mṛgāścaiva sarve te piśitāśanāḥ||37||

38tathā puruṣaśārdula mānuṣāśca paraṃtapa
|
sahasraśo hyayutaśo vardhante salilaṃ yathā||38||

39etasminsaṃkule tāta vartamāne bhayaṃkare
|
atibhārādvasumatī yojanānāṃ śataṃ gatā||39||

40sā vai vyathitasarvāṅgī bhāreṇākrāntacetanā
|
nārāyaṇaṃ varaṃ devaṃ prapannā śaraṇaṃ gatā||40||

41pṛthivyuvāca|
bhagavaṃstvatprasādāddhi tiṣṭheyaṃ suciraṃ tviha
|
bhāreṇāsmi samākrāntā na śaknomi sma vartitum||41||

42mamemaṃ bhagavanbhāraṃ vyapanetuṃ tvamarhasi
|
śaraṇāgatāsmi te deva prasādaṃ kuru me vibho||42||

43tasyāstadvacanaṃ śrutvā bhagavānakṣaraḥ prabhuḥ
|
provāca vacanaṃ hṛṣṭaḥ śravyākṣarasamīritam||43||

44viṣṇuruvāca|
na te mahi bhayaṃ kāryaṃ bhārārte vasudhāriṇi
|
ayamevaṃ tathā kurmi yathā laghvī bhaviṣyasi||44||

45lomaśa uvāca|
sa tāṃ visarjayitvā tu vasudhāṃ śailakuṇḍalām
|
tato varāhaḥ saṃvṛtta ekaśṛṅgo mahādyutiḥ||45||

46raktābhyāṃ nayanābhyāṃ tu bhayamutpādayanniva
|
dhūmaṃ ca jvalaya~llakṣmyā tatra deśe vyavardhata||46||

47sa gṛhītvā vasumatīṃ śṛṅgeṇaikena bhāsvatā
|
yojanānāṃ śataṃ vīra samuddharati so'kṣaraḥ||47||

48tasyāṃ coddhārtamāṇāyāṃ saṃkṣobhaḥ samajāyata
|
devāḥ saṃkṣubhitāḥ sarve ṛṣayaśca tapodhanāḥ||48||

49hāhābhūtamabhūtsarvaṃ tridivaṃ vyoma bhūstathā
|
na paryavasthitaḥ kaściddevo vā mānuṣo'pi vā||49||

50tato brahmāṇamāsīnaṃ jvalamānamiva śriyā
|
devāḥ sarṣigaṇāścaiva upatasthuranekaśaḥ||50||

51upasarpya ca deveśaṃ brahmāṇaṃ lokasākṣikam
|
bhūtvā prāñjalayaḥ sarve vākyamuccārayaṃstadā||51||

52lokāḥ saṃkṣubhitāḥ sarve vyākulaṃ ca carācaram
|
samudrāṇāṃ ca saṃkṣobhastridaśeśa prakāśate||52||

53saiṣā vasumatī kṛtsnā yojanānāṃ śataṃ gatā
|
kimetatkiṃ prabhāveṇa yenedaṃ vyākulaṃ jagat
|
ākhyātu no bhavāñśīghraṃ visaṃjñāḥ smeha sarvaśaḥ||53||

54brahmovāca|
asurebhyo bhayaṃ nāsti yuṣmākaṃ kutracitkvacit
|
śrūyatāṃ yatkṛteṣveṣa saṃkṣobho jāyate'marāḥ||54||

55yo'sau sarvatragaḥ śrīmānakṣarātmā vyavasthitaḥ
|
tasya prabhāvātsaṃkṣobhastridivasya prakāśate||55||

56yeṣā vasumatī kṛtsnā yojanānāṃ śataṃ gatā
|
samuddhṛtā punastena viṣṇunā paramātmanā||56||

57tasyāmuddhāryamāṇāyāṃ saṃkṣobhaḥ samajāyata
|
evaṃ bhavanto jānantu chidyatāṃ saṃśayaśca vaḥ||57||

58devā ūcuḥ| kva tadbhūtaṃ vasumatīṃ samuddharati hṛṣṭavat
|
taṃ deśaṃ bhagavanbrūhi tatra yāsyāmahe vayam||58||

59brahmovāca|
hanta gacchata bhadraṃ vo nandane paśyata sthitam
|
eṣo'tra bhagavāñśrīmānsuparṇaḥ saṃprakāśate||59||

60vārāheṇaiva rūpeṇa bhagavā~llokabhāvanaḥ
|
kālānala ivābhāti pṛthivītalamuddharan||60||

61etasyorasi suvyaktaṃ śrīvatsamabhirājate
|
paśyadhvaṃ vibudhāḥ sarve bhūtametadanāmayam||61||

62lomaśa uvāca|
tato dṛṣṭvā mahātmānaṃ śrutvā cāmantrya cāmarāḥ
|
pitāmahaṃ puraskṛtya jagmurdevā yathāgatam||62||

63vaiśaṃpāyana uvāca|
śrutvā tu tāṃ kathāṃ sarve pāṇḍavā janamejaya
|
lomaśādeśitenāśu pathā jagmuḥ prahṛṣṭavat||63||
