Mahābhārata 7.157 (Droṇaparvan)

1sañjaya uvāca|
drupadasyātmajān dṛṣṭvā kuntibhojasutāṃstathā
|
droṇaputreṇa nihatān rākṣasāṃśca sahasraśaḥ||1||

2yudhiṣṭhiro bhīmaseno dhṛṣṭadyumnaśca pārṣataḥ
|
yuyudhānaśca saṃyattā yuddhāyaiva mano dadhuḥ||2||

3somadattaḥ punaḥ kruddho dṛṣṭvā sātyakimāhave
|
mahatā śaravarṣeṇa cchādayāmāsa bhārata||3||

4tataḥ samabhavadyuddhamatīva bhayavardhanam
|
tvadīyānāṃ pareṣāṃ ca ghoraṃ vijayakāṃkṣiṇām||4||

5taṃ dṛṣṭvā samupāyāntaṃ rukmapuṅkhaiḥ śilāśitaiḥ
|
daśabhiḥsātvatasyārthe bhīmo vivyādha sāyakaiḥ||5||

6somada‌tto'pi taṃ vīraṃ śatena pratyavidhyata
|
sātvatastvabhisaṃkruddhaḥ putrādhibhirabhiplutam||6||

7vṛddhaṃ vṛddhaguṇairyuktaṃ yayātimiva nāhuṣam
|
vivyādha daśabhistīkṣṇaiḥ śarairvajranipātanaiḥ||7||

8śaktyā caina vinirbhidya punarvivyādha saptabhiḥ
|
tatastu sātyakerarthe bhīmaseno navaṃ dṛḍham||8||

9mumoca parighaṃ ghoraṃ somadattasya mūrdhani
|
sāttvato'pyagnisaṃkāśaṃ mumoca śaramuttamam||9||

10somadattorasi kruddhaḥ supatraṃ niśitaṃ yudhi
|
yugapatpetaturvīre ghorau parighamārgaṇau||10||

11śarīre somadattasya sa papāta mahārathaḥ
|
vyāmohite tu tanaye bālhīkastamupādravat||11||

12visṛjañcharavarṣāṇi kālavarṣīva toyadaḥ
|
bhīmo'tha sātvatasyārthe bālhīkaṃ navabhiḥśaraiḥ||12||

13prapīḍayanmahātmānaṃ vivyādha raṇamūrdhani
|
prātipeyastu saṃkruddhaḥ śaktiṃ bhīmasya vakṣasi||13||

14nicakhāna mahābāhuḥ purandara ivāśanim
|
sa tathā'bhihato bhīmaścakaṃpe ca mumoha ca||14||

15prāpya cetaśca balavān gadāmasmai sasarja ha
|
sā pāṃḍavena prahitā bālhīkasya śiro'harat||15||

16sa papāta hataḥ pṛthvyāṃ vajrāhata ivādrirāṭ
|
tasminvinihate vīre bālhīke puruṣarṣabha||16||

17putrāste'bhyardayan bhīmaṃ daśa dāśaratheḥ samāḥ
|
nāgada‌tto dṛḍharatho mahābāhurayobhujaḥ||17||

18dṛḍhaḥ suhasto virajāḥ pramāthyugro'nuyāyyapi
|
tāndṛṣṭvā cukrudhe bhīmo jagṛhe bhārasādhanān||18||

19ekamekaṃ samuddiśya pātayāmāsa marmasu
|
te viddhā vyasavaḥ petuḥ syandanebhyo hataujasaḥ||19||

20caṇḍavātaprabhagnāstu parvatāgrānmahīruhāḥ
|
nārācaidarśabhirbhīmastāṃ nihatya tavātmajān||20||

21karṇasya dayitaṃ putraṃ vṛṣasenamavākirat
|
tato vṛkaratho nāma bhrātā karṇasya viśrutaḥ||21||

22jaghāna bhīmaṃ nārācaistamapyabhyadravadbalī
|
tataḥ sapta rathānvīraḥ syālānāṃ tava bhārata||22||

23nihatya bhīmo nārācaiḥ śatacandramapothayat
|
amarṣayanto nihataṃ śatacandraṃ mahāratham||23||

24śakunerbhrātaro vīrā gavākṣaḥ śarabho vibhuḥ
|
subhago bhānudattaśca śūrāḥ pañca mahārathāḥ||24||

25abhidrutya śaraistīkṣṇairbhīmasenamatāḍayan
|
sa tāḍyamāno nārācairvṛṣṭivegairivācalaḥ||25||

26jaghāna pañcabhirbāṇaiḥ pañcaivātirathānbalī
|
tān dṛṣṭvā nihatānvīrānvicelurnṛpasattamāḥ||26||

27tato yudhiṣṭhiraḥ kruddhastavānīkamaśātayat
|
miṣataḥ kuṃbhayonestu putrāṇāṃ tava cānagha||27||

28ambaṣṭhānmālavāñchūrāṃstrigartānsa śibīnapi
|
prāhiṇonmṛtyulokāya kruddho yuddhe yudhiṣṭhiraḥ||28||

29abhīṣāhāñchūrasenān bāhlīkān savasātikān
|
nikṛtya pṛthivīṃ rājā cakre śoṇitakardamām||29||

30yaudheyānmālavān rājanmadrakāṇāṃ gaṇā yudhi
|
prāhiṇonmṛtyulokāya śūrānbāṇairyudhiṣṭhiraḥ||30||

31hatāharata gṛhṇīta vidhyata vyavakṛntata
|
ityāsīttumulaḥ śabdo yudhiṣṭhirarathaṃ prati||31||

32sainyāni drāvayantaṃ taṃ droṇo dṛṣṭvā yudhiṣṭhiram
|
coditastava putreṇa sāyakairabhyavākirat||32||

33droṇastu paramakruddho vāyavyāstreṇa pārthivam
|
vivyādha so'pi taddivyamastramastreṇa jaghnivān||33||

34tasminvinihate cāstre bhāradvājo yudhiṣṭhire
|
vāruṇaṃ yāmyamāgneyaṃ tvāṣṭraṃ sāvitrameva ca||34||

35cikṣepa paramakruddho jighāṃsuḥ pāṇḍunandanam
|
kṣiptāni kṣipyamāṇāni tāni cāstrāṇi dharmajaḥ||35||

36jaghānāstrairmahābāhuḥ kumbhayoneravitrasan
|
satyāṃ cikīrṣamāṇastu pratijñāṃ kumbhasaṃbhavaḥ||36||

37prāduścakre'stramaindraṃ vai prājāpatyaṃ ca bhārata
|
jighāṃsurdharmatanayaṃ tava putrahite rataḥ||37||

38patiḥ kurūṇāṃ gajasiṃhagāmī viśālavakṣāḥ pṛthulohitākṣaḥ
|
prāduścakārāstramahīnatejā māhendramanyatsa jaghāna tena||38||

39vihanyamāneṣvastreṣu droṇaḥ krodhasamanvitaḥ
|
yudhiṣṭhiravadhaṃ prepsurbrāhmamastramudairayat||39||

40tato nājñāsiṣaṃ kiñcidghoreṇa tamasā''vṛte
|
sarvabhūtāni ca paraṃ trāsaṃ jagmurmahīpate||40||

41brahmāstramudyataṃ dṛṣṭvā kuntīputro yudhiṣṭhiraḥ
|
brahmāstreṇaiva rājendra tadastraṃ pratyavārayat||41||

42tataḥ sainikamukhyāste praśaśaṃsurnararṣabhau
|
droṇapārthau maheṣvāsau sarvayuddhaviśāradau||42||

43tataḥ pramucya kaunteyaṃ droṇo drupadavāhinīm
|
vyadhamatkrodhatāmrākṣo vāyavyāstreṇa bhārata||43||

44te hanyamānā droṇena pañcālāḥ prādravanbhayāt
|
paśyato bhīmasenasya pārthasya ca mahātmanaḥ||44||

45tataḥ kirīṭī bhīmaśca sahasā saṃnyavartatām
|
mahadbhyāṃ rathavaṃśābhyāṃ parigṛhya balaṃ tadā||45||

46bībhatsurdakṣiṇaṃ pārśvamuttaraṃ ca vṛkodraraḥ
|
bhāradvājaṃ śaraughābhyāṃ mahadbhyāmabhyavarṣatām||46||

47kekayāḥ sṛñjayāścaiva pañcālāśca mahaujasaḥ
|
anvagacchanmahārāja matsyāśca saha sātvataiḥ||47||

48tataḥ sā bhāratī senā vadhyamānā kirīṭinā
|
tamasā nidrayā caiva punareva vyadīryata||48||

49droṇena vāryamāṇāste svayaṃ tava sutena ca
|
nāśakyanta mahārāja yodhā vārayituṃ tadā||49||
