Mahābhārata 8.25 (Karṇaparvan)

1sañjaya uvāca|
yuyutsuṃ tava putrasya drāvayantaṃ balaṃ mahat
|
ulūko nyapatattūrṇaṃ tiṣṭha tiṣṭheti cābravīt ||1||

2yuyutsuśca tato rājañśitadhāreṇa patriṇā
|
ulūkaṃ tāḍayāmāsa vajreṇeva mahābalam ||2||

3ulūkastu tataḥ kruddhastava putrasya saṃyuge
|
kṣurapreṇa dhanuśchittvā tāḍayāmāsa karṇinā ||3||

4tadapāsya dhanuśchinnaṃ yuyutsurvegavattaram
|
anyadādatta sumahaccāpaṃ saṃraktalocanaḥ ||4||

5śākuniṃ tu tataḥ ṣaṣṭyā vivyādha bharatarṣabha
|
sārathiṃ tribhirānarcchattaṃ ca bhūyo vyavidhyata ||5||

6ulūkastaṃtu viṃśatyā viddhyā svarṇavibhūṣitaiḥ
|
athāsya samare kruddho dhvajaṃ ciccheda kāñcanam ||6||

7sa cchinnayaṣṭiḥ sumahāñśīryamāṇo mahādhvajaḥ
|
papāta pramukhe rājan yuyutsoḥ kāñcanadhvajaḥ ||7||

8dhvajamunmathitaṃ dṛṣṭvā yuyutsuḥ krodhamūrcchitaḥ
|
ulūkaṃ pañcabhirbāṇairājadhāna stanāntare ||8||

9ulūkastasya same tailadhautena māriṣa
|
śiraściccheda bhallena yanturbharatasattama ||9||

10tacchinnamapatadbhūmau yuyutsoḥ sārathestadā
|
tārārūpaṃ yathā citraṃ nipapāta mahītale ||10||

11jaghāna caturośvāṃśca taṃ ca vivyādha pañcabhiḥ
|
so'tividdho balavatā pratyapāyādrathāntaram ||11||

12taṃ nirjitya raṇe rājannulūkastvarito yayau
|
pāñcālānsṛñjayāṃścaiva vinighnanniśitaiḥ śaraiḥ ||12||

13śatānīkaṃ mahārāja śrutakarmā sutastava
|
vyaśvasūtarathaṃ cakre nimeṣārdhādasaṃbhramaḥ ||13||

14hatāśve tu rathe tiṣṭhañśatānīko mahārathaḥ
|
gadāṃ cikṣepa saṃkruddhastava putrasya māriṣa ||14||

15sā kṛtvā syandanaṃ bhasma hayāṃścaiva sasārathīn
|
papāta dharaṇīṃ tūrṇaṃ dārayantīva bhārata ||15||

16tāvubhau virathau vīrau kurūṇāṃ kīrtivardhanau
|
vyapākrametāṃ yuddhāttu prekṣamāṇau parasparam ||16||

17putrastu tava saṃbhrānto viviṃśo rathamāruhat
|
śatānīko'pi tvaritaḥ prativindhyarathaṃ gataḥ ||17||

18sutasomaṃ tu śakunirviddhvā tu niśitaiḥ śaraiḥ
|
nākampayata saṃkruddho vāryaugha iva parvatam ||18||

19sutasomastu taṃ dṛṣṭvā pituratyantavairiṇam
|
śarairanekasāhasaiśchā‌dyāmāsa bhārata ||19||

20tāñśarāñśakunistūrṇaṃ cicchedānyaiḥ patatribhiḥ
|
ladhvastraścitrayodhī ca jitakāśī ca saṃyuge ||20||

21nivārya samare cāpi śarāṃstānniśitaiḥ śaraiḥ
|
ājaghāna susaṃkruddhaḥ sutasomaṃ tribhiḥ śaraiḥ ||21||

22tasyāśvān ketanaṃ sūtaṃ tilaśo vyadhamaccharaiḥ
|
syālastava mahārāja tataḥ uccukruśurjanāḥ ||22||

23hatāśvo virathaścaiva chinnaketuśca māriṣa
|
dhanvī dhanurvaraṃ gṛhya rathādbhūmāvatiṣṭhata ||23||

24vyasṛjatsāyakāṃścaiva svarṇapuṃkhāñśilāśitān
|
chādayāmāsa samare tava syālasya taṃ ratham ||24||

25śalabhānāmiva vrātāñśāravrātānmahārathaḥ
|
rathopagānsamīkṣyaivaṃ vivyathe naiva saubalaḥ ||25||

26pramamātha śarāṃstasya śaravrātairmahāyaśāḥ
|
tatrātuṣyanta yodhāśca siddhāścāpi divi sthitāḥ ||26||

27sutasomasya tat karma dṛṣṭvā śraddheyamadbhutam
|
rathasthaṃ śakuniṃ yastu padātiḥ samayodhayat ||27||

28tasya tīkṣṇairmahāvegairbhallaiḥsannataparvabhiḥ
|
vyahanatkārmukaṃ rājaṃstūṇīrāṃścaiva sarvaśaḥ ||28||

29sa cchinnadhanvā virathaḥ khaḍgamudyamya cānadat
|
vaidūryotpalavarṇābhaṃ dantidantamayatsarum ||29||

30bhrāmyamāṇaṃ tatastaṃ tu vimalāmbaravarcasam
|
kāladaṇḍopamaṃ mene sutasomasya dhīmataḥ ||30||

31so'caratsahasā khaḍgī maṇḍalāni sahasraśaḥ
|
caturdaśa mahārāja śikṣābalasamanvitaḥ ||31||

32bhrāntamudbhrāntamāviddhamāplutaṃ viplutaṃ sṛtam
|
saṃpātasamudīrṇe ca darśayāmāsa saṃyuge ||32||

33saubalastu tatastasya śarāṃścikṣepa vīryavān
|
tānāpatata evāśu ciccheda paramāsinā ||33||

34tataḥ kruddho mahārāja saubalaḥ paravīrahā
|
prāhiṇotsutasomāya śarānāśīviṣopamān ||34||

35ciccheda tāṃstu khaḍgena śikṣayā ca balena ca
|
darśayaঁllāghavaṃ yuddhe tārkṣyatulyaparākramaḥ ||35||

36tasya sañcarato rājan maṇḍalāvartane tadā
|
kṣurapreṇa sutīkṣṇena khaḍgaṃ ciccheda suprabham ||36||

37sa cchinnaḥ sahasā bhūmau nipapāta mahānasiḥ
|
ardhamasya sthitaṃ haste sutsarostatra bhārata ||37||

38chinnamājñāya nistriṃśamavaplutya padāni ṣaṭ
|
prāvidhyata tataḥ śeṣaṃ sutasomo mahārathaḥ ||38||

39tacchittvā saguṇaṃ cāpaṃ raṇe tasya mahātmanaḥ
|
papāta dharaṇīṃ tūrṇaṃ svarṇavajravibhūṣitam ||39||

40sutasomastato'gacchrutakīrtermahāratham
|
saubalo'pi dhanurguhya ghoramanyatsudurjayam ||40||

41abhyayātpāṇḍavānīkaṃ nighnañśatrugaṇān bahūn
|
tatra nādo mahānāsītpāṇḍavānāṃ viśāṃpate ||41||

42sauvalaṃ samare dṛṣṭvā vicarantamabhītavat
|
tānyanīkāni dṛptāni śastravanti mahānti ca ||42||

43drāvyamāṇānyadṛśyanta saubalena mahātmanā
|
yathā daityacamūṃ rājan devarājo mamarda ha
|
tathaiva pāṇḍavīṃ senāṃ saubaleyo vyanāśayat ||43||
