Mahābhārata 9.58 (Śalyaparvan)

1sañjaya uvāca|
samudīrṇaṃ tato dṛṣṭvā saṅgrāmaṃ kurumukhyayoḥ
|
athābravīdarjunastu vāsudevaṃ yaśasvinam||1||

2anayorvīrayoryuddhe ko jyāyān bhavato mataḥ
|
kasya vā ko guṇo bhūyānetadvada janārdana||2||

3vāsudeva uvāca|
upadeśo'nayostulyo bhīmastu balavattaraḥ
|
kṛtī yatnaparastveṣa dhārtarāṣṭro vṛkodarāt||3||

4bhīmasenastu dharmeṇa yuddhyamāno na jeṣyati
|
anyāyena tu yudhyanvai hanyādeva suyodhanam||4||

5māyayā nirjitā devairasurā iti naḥ śrutam
|
virocanastu śakreṇa māyayā nirjitaḥ sa vai||5||

6māyayā cākṣipattejo vṛtrasya balasūdanaḥ
|
tasmānmāyāmayaṃ bhīma ātiṣṭhatu parākramam||6||

7pratijñātaṃ ca bhīmena dyūtakāle dhanañjaya
|
ūrū bhetsyāmi te saṅkhye gadayeti suyodhanam||7||

8so'yaṃ pratijñāṃ tāṃ cāpi pālayatvarikarṣaṇaḥ
|
māyāvinaṃ tu rājānaṃ māyayaiva nikṛntatu||8||

9yadyeṣa balamāsthāya nyāyena prahariṣyati
|
viṣamasthastato rājā bhaviṣyati yudhiṣṭhiraḥ||9||

10punareva tu vakṣyāmi pāṇḍaveya nibodha me
|
dharmarājāparādhena bhayaṃ naḥ punarāgatam||10||

11kṛtvā hi sumahatkarma hatvā bhīṣmamukhānkurūn
|
jayaḥ prāpto yaśaḥ prāgryaṃ vairaṃ ca pratiyātitam||11||

12tadevaṃ vijayaḥ prāptaḥ punaḥ saṃśayitaḥ kṛtaḥ
|
abuddhireṣā mahatī dharmarājasya pāṇḍava||12||

13yadekavijaye yuddhaṃ pāṇitaṃ ghoramīddaśam
|
suyodhanaḥ kṛtī vīra ekāyanagatastathā||13||

14api cośanasā gītaḥ śrūyate'yaṃ purātanaḥ
|
ślokastattvārthasahitastanme nigadataḥ śṛṇu||14||

15punarāvartamānānāṃ bhagnānāṃ jīvitaiṣiṇām
|
bhetavyamariśeṣāṇāmekāyanagatā hi te||15||

16sāhasotpatitānāṃ ca nirāśānāṃ ca jīvite
|
na śakyamagrataḥ sthātuṃ śakreṇāpi dhanañjaya||16||

17suyodhanamimaṃ bhagnaṃ hatasainyaṃ hradaṃ gatam
|
parājitaṃ vanaprepsuṃ nirāśaṃ rājyalambhane||17||

18ko nveṣa saṃyuge prājñaḥ punardvandve samāhvayet
|
api no nirjitaṃ rājyaṃ na hareta suyodhanaḥ||18||

19yastrayodaśavarṣāṇi gadayā kṛtaniśramaḥ
|
caratyūrdhvaṃ ca tiryakca bhīmasenajighāṃsayā||19||

20enaṃ cenna mahābāhuranyāyena haniṣyati
|
eṣa vaḥ kauravo rājā dhārtarāṣṭro bhaviṣyati||20||

21dhanañjayastu śrutvaitatkeśavasya mahātmanaḥ
|
prekṣato bhīmasenasya savyamūrumatāḍayat||21||

22gṛhya saṃjñāṃ tato bhīmo gadayā vyacaradraṇe
|
maṇḍalāni vicitrāṇi yamakānītarāṇi ca||22||

23dakṣiṇaṃ maṇḍalaṃ savyaṃ gomūtrakamathāpi ca
|
vyacaratpāṇḍavo rājannariṃ sammohayanniva||23||

24tathaiva tava putro'pi gadāmārgaviśāradaḥ
|
vyacarallaghu citraṃ ca bhīmasenajighāṃsayā||24||

25ādhunvantau gade ghore candanāgarurūṣite
|
vairasyāntaṃ parīpsantau raṇe kruddhāvivāntakau||25||

26anyonyaṃ tau jighāṃsantau pravīrau puruṣarṣabhau
|
yuyudhāte garutmantau tathā nāgāmiṣaiṣiṇau||26||

27maṇḍalāni vicitrāṇi caratornṛpabhīmayoḥ
|
gadāsampātajāstatra prajajñuḥ pāvakārciṣaḥ||27||

28samaṃ praharatostatra śūrayorbalinormadhe
|
kṣubdhayorvāyunā rājan dvayoriva samudrayoḥ||28||

29tayoḥ praharatostulyaṃ mattakuñjarayoriva
|
gadānirghātasaṃhrādaḥ prahārāṇāmajāyata||29||

30tasmiṃstadā samprahāre dāruṇe saṅkule bhṛśam
|
ubhāvapi pariśrāntau yudhyamānāvariṃdamau||30||

31tau muhūrtaṃ samāśvasya punareva parantapa
|
abhyahārayatāṃ kruddhau pragṛhya mahatī gade||31||

32tayoḥ samabhavadyuddhaṃ ghorarūpamasaṃvṛtam
|
gadānipātai rājendra takṣatorvai parasparam||32||

33samare pradrutau tau tu vṛṣabhākṣau tarasvinau
|
anyonyaṃ jaghnaturvīrau paṅkasthau mahiṣāviva||33||

34jarjarīkṛtasarvāṅgau rudhireṇābhisamplutau
|
daddaśāte himavati puṣpitāviva kiṃśukau||34||

35duryodhanastu pārthena vivare sampradarśite
|
īṣadunmiṣamāṇastu sahasā prasasāra ha||35||

36tamabhyāśagataṃ prājño raṇe prekṣya vṛkodaraḥ
|
avākṣipadgadāṃ tasminvegena mahatā balī||36||

37ākṣipantaṃ tu taṃ dṛṣṭvā putrastava viśāmpate
|
avāsarpattataḥ sthānātsā moghā nyapatadbhuvi||37||

38mokṣayitvā prahāraṃ taṃ sutastava susambhramāt
|
bhīmasenaṃ ta gadayā prāharatkurusattama||38||

39tasya visyandamānena rudhireṇāmitaujasaḥ
|
prahāragurupātācca mūrcheva samajāyata||39||

40duryodhano na taṃ veda pīḍitaṃ pāṇḍavaṃ raṇe
|
dhārayāmāsa bhīmo'pi śarīramatipīḍitam||40||

41amanyata sthitaṃ hyenaṃ prahariṣyantamāhave
|
ato na prāharattasmai punareva tavātmajaḥ||41||

42tato muhūrtamāśvasya duryodhanamupasthitam
|
vegenābhyapadrājan bhīmasenaḥ pratāpavān||42||

43tamāpatantaṃ samprekṣya saṃrabdhamamitaujasam
|
moghamasya prahāraṃ taṃ cikīrṣurbharatarṣabha||43||

44avasthāne matiṃ kṛtvā putrastava mahāmanāḥ
|
iyeṣotpatituṃ rājañchalayiṣyan vṛkodaram||44||

45abuddhyadbhīmasenastu rājñastasya cikīrṣitam
|
athāsya samabhidrutya samutkruśya ca siṃhavat||45||

46sṛtyā vañcayato rājanpunarevotpatiṣyataḥ
|
ūrubhyāṃ prāhiṇodrājan gadāṃ vegena pāṇḍavaḥ||46||

47sā vajraniṣpeṣasamā prahitā bhīmakarmaṇā
|
ūrū duryodhanasyātha babhañja priyadarśanau||47||

48sa papāta naravyāghro vasudhāmanunādayan
|
bhagnorurbhīmasenena putrastava mahīpate||48||

49vavurvātāḥ sanirghātāḥ pāṃśuvarṣaṃ papāta ca
|
cacāla pṛthivī cāpi savṛkṣakṣupaparvatā||49||

50tasminnipatite vīre patyau sarvamahīkṣitām
|
mahāsvanā punardīptā sanirghātā bhayaṅkarī||50||

51papāta colkā mahatī patite pṛthivīpatau
|
tathā śoṇitavarṣaṃ ca pāṃśuvarṣaṃ ca bhārata||51||

52vavarṣa maghavāṃstatra tava putre nipātite
|
yakṣāṇāṃ rākṣasānāṃ ca piśācānāṃ tathaiva ca||52||

53antarikṣe mahānādaḥ śrūyate bhatararṣabha
|
tena śabdena ghoreṇa mṛgāṇāmatha pakṣiṇām||53||

54jajñe ghorataraḥ śabdo bahūnāṃ sarvatodiśam
|
ye tatra vājinaḥ śeṣā gajāśca manujaiḥ saha||54||

55mumucuste mahānādaṃ tava putre nipātite
|
bherīśaṅkhamṛdaṅgānāmabhavacca svano mahān||55||

56antarbhūmigataścaiva tava putre nipātite
|
bahupādairbahubhujaiḥ kabandhairghoradarśanaiḥ||56||

57nṛtyadbhirbhayadairvyāptā diśastatrābhavannṛpa
|
dhvajavanto'stravantaśca śastravantastathaiva ca||57||

58prākampanta tato rājaṃstava putre nipātite
|
hradāḥ kūpāśca rudhiramudvemurnṛpasattama||58||

59nadyaśca sumahāvegāḥ pratisrotovahā'bhavan
|
pulliṅgā iva nāryastu strīliṅgāḥ puruṣābhavan||59||

60duryodhane tadā rājanpatite tanaye tava
|
dṛṣṭvā tānadbhutotpātānpañcālāḥ pāṇḍavaiḥ saha||60||

61āvignamanasaḥ sarve babhūvurbharatarṣabha
|
yayurdevā yathākāmaṃ gandharvāpsarasastathā||61||

62kathayanto'dbhutaṃ yuddhaṃ sutayostava bhārata
|
tathaiva siddhā rājendra tathā vātikacāraṇāḥ
|
narasiṃhau praśaṃsantau viprajagmuryathāgatam||62||
