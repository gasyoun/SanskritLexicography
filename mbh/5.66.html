Mahābhārata 5.66 (Udyogaparvan)

1vaiśampāyana uvāca|
evamuktvā mahāprājño dhṛtarāṣṭraḥ suyodhanam
|
punareva mahābhāgaḥ sañjayaṃ paryapṛcchata ||1||

2brūhi sañjaya yaccheṣaṃ vāsudevādanantaram
|
yadarjuna uvāca tvāṃ paraṃ kautūhalaṃ hi me ||2||

3sañjaya uvāca|
vāsudevavacaḥ śrutvā kuntīputro dhanañjayaḥ
|
uvāca kāle durdharṣo vāsudevasya śṛṇvataḥ ||3||

4pitāmahaṃ śāntanavaṃ dhṛtarāṣṭraṃ ca sañjaya
|
droṇaṃ kṛpaṃ ca karṇaṃ ca mahārājaṃ ca bāhnikam ||4||

5drauṇiṃ ca somadattaṃ ca śakuniṃ cāpi saubalam
|
duḥśāsanaṃ śalaṃ caiva purumitraṃ viviṃśatim ||5||

6vikarṇaṃ citrasenaṃ ca jayatsenaṃ ca pārthivam
|
vindānuvindāvāvantyau durmukhaṃ cāpi kauravam ||6||

7saindhavaṃ duḥsahaṃ caiva bhūriśravasameva ca
|
bhagadattaṃ ca rājānaṃ jalasandhaṃ ca pārthivam ||7||

8ye cāpyanye pārthivāstatra yoddhuṃ samāgatā kauravāṇāṃ priyārtham
|
mumūrṣavaḥ pāṇḍavāgnau pradīpte samānītā dhārtarāṣṭreṇa hotum ||8||

9yathānyāyaṃ kauśalaṃ vandanaṃ ca samāgatā madvacanena vācyāḥ
|
idaṃ brūyāḥ sañjaya rājamadhye suyodhanaṃ pāpakṛtāṃ nidhānam ||9||

10amarṣaṇaṃ durmatiṃ rājaputraṃ pāpātmānaṃ dhārtarāṣṭraṃ sulubdham
|
sarvaṃ mamaitadvacanaṃ samagraṃ sahāmātyaṃ sañjaya śrāvayethāḥ ||10||

11evaṃ pratiṣṭhāpya dhanañjayo māṃ tato'rthavaddharmavaccāpi vākyam
|
provācedaṃ vāsudevaṃ samīkṣya pārtho dhīmā~llohitāntāyatākṣaḥ ||11||

12yathā śrutaṃ te vadato mahātmano madhu pravīrasya vacaḥ samāhitam
|
tathaiva vācyaṃ bhavatā hi madvacaḥ samāgateṣu kṣitipeṣu sarvaśaḥ ||12||

13śarāgnidhūme rathaneminādite dhanuḥ sruveṇāstrabalaprasāriṇā
|
yathā na homaḥ kriyate mahāmṛdhe sametya sarve prayatadhvamādṛtāḥ ||13||

14na cetprayacchadhvamamitraghātino yudhiṣṭhirasyāṃśamabhīpsitaṃ svakam
|
na yāmi vaḥ sāśvapadātikuñjarān diśaṃ pitṝṇāmaśivāṃ śitaiḥ śaraiḥ ||14||

15tato'hamāmantrya tadā dhanañjayaṃ caturbhujaṃ caiva namasya satvaraḥ
|
javena saṃprāpta ihāmaradyute tavāntikaṃ prāpayituṃ vaco mahat ||15||
