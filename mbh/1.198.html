Mahābhārata 1.198 (Ādiparvan)

1drupada uvāca|
aśrutvaivaṃ vacanaṃ te maharṣe mayā pūrvaṃ yatitaṃ saṃvidhātum
|
na vai śakyaṃ vihitasyāpayānaṃ tadevedamupapannaṃ vidhānam||1||

2diṣṭasya granthiranivartanīyaḥ svakarmaṇā vihitaṃ neha kiṃcit
|
kṛtaṃ nimittamiha naikahetostadevedamupapannaṃ vidhānam||2||

3yathaiva kṛṣṇoktavatī purastānnaikānpatīnme bhagavāndadātu
|
sa cāpyevaṃ varamityabravīttāṃ devo hi vettā paramaṃ yadatra||3||

4yadi caivaṃ vihitaḥ śaṅkareṇa dharmo'dharmo vā nātra mamāparādhaḥ
|
gṛhṇantvime vidhivatpāṇimasyā yathopajoṣaṃ vihitaiṣāṃ hi kṛṣṇā||4||

5vaiśaṃpāyana uvāca|
tato'bravīdbhagavāndharmarājamadyaiva puṇyāhamuta vaḥ pāṇḍaveyāḥ
|
adya poṣyaṃ yogamupaiti candramāḥ pāṇiṃ kṛṣṇāyāstvaṃ gṛhāṇādya pūrvam||5||

6tato'rājā yajñasenaḥ saputro janyārthamuktaṃ bahu tattadagryam
|
samānayāmāsa sutāṃ ca kṛṣṇāmāplāvya ratnairbahubhirvibhūṣya||6||

7tatastu sarve suhṛdo nṛpasya samājagmuḥ sahitā mantriṇaśca
|
draṣṭuṃ vivāhaṃ paramapratītā dvijāśca paurāśca yathā pradhānāḥ||7||

8tato'sya veśmāgryajanopaśobhitaṃ vistīrṇapadmotpalabhūṣitājiram
|
balaugharatnaughavicitramābabhau nabho yathā nirmalatārakānvitam||8||

9tatastu te kauravarājaputrā vibhūṣitāḥ kuṇḍalino yuvānaḥ
|
mahārhavastrāmbaracandanokṣitāḥ kṛtābhiṣekāḥ kṛtamaṅgalakriyāḥ||9||

10purohitenāgnisamānavarcasā sahaiva dhaumyena yatāvidhi prabho
|
krameṇa sarve viviśustataḥ sado maharṣabhāgoṣṭhamivābhinandinaḥ||10||

11tataḥ samādhāya sa vedaparāgo juhāva mantrairjvalitaṃ hutāśanam
|
yudhiṣṭhiraṃ cāpyupanīya mantravinniyojayāmāsa sahaiva kṛṣṇayā||11||

12pradakṣiṇaṃ tau pragṛhītapāṇī pariṇāyyayāmāsa sa vedaparāgaḥ
|
tato'bhyanujñāya tamājiśobhinaṃ purohito rājagṛhādviniryayau||12||

13krameṇa cānena narādhipātmajā varastriyaste jagṛhustadā karam
|
ahanyahanyuttamarūpadhāriṇo mahārathāḥ kauravavaṃśavardhanāḥ||13||

14idaṃ ca tatrādbhutarūpamuttamaṃ jagāda devarṣiratītamānuṣam
|
mahānubhāvā kila sā sumadhyamā babhūva kanyaiva gate gate'hani||14||

15kṛte vivāhe drupado dhanaṃ dadau mahārathebhyo bahurūpamuttamam
|
śataṃ rathānāṃ varahemamālināṃ caturyujāṃ hemakhalīnamālinām||15||

16śataṃ gajānāmapi padmināṃ tathā śataṃ giriṇāmiva hemaśṛṅgiṇām
|
tathaiva dāsīśatamagryayauvanaṃ mahārhaveṣābharaṇāmbarasrajam||16||

17pṛthakpṛthak divyadṛśāṃ punardadau tadā dhanaṃ saumakiragnisākṣikam
|
tathaiva vastrāṇi vibhūṣaṇāni prabhāvayuktāni mahānubhāvaḥ||17||

18kṛte vivāhe ca tatastu pāṇḍavāḥ prabhūtaratnāmupalabhya tāṃ śriyam
|
vijahrurindrapratimā mahābalāḥ pure tu pāñcālanṛpasya tasya ha||18||
