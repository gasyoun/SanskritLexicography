Mahābhārata 12.341 (Śāntiparvan)

1janamejaya uvāca|
astauṣīdyairimaṃ vyāsaḥ saśiṣyo madhusūdanam
|
nāmabhirvividhaireṣāṃ niruktaṃ bhagavanmama||1||

2vaktumarhasi śuśrūṣoḥ prajāpatipaterhareḥ
|
śrutvā bhaveyaṃ yatpūtaḥ śaraccandra ivāmalaḥ||2||

3vaiśampāyana uvāca|
śṛṇu rājanyathācaṣṭa phālgunasya hariḥ prabhuḥ
|
prasannātmā''tmano nāmnāṃ niruktaṃ guṇakarmajam||3||

4nāmabhiḥ kīrtitaistasya keśavasya mahātmanaḥ
|
pṛṣṭavānkeśavaṃ rājanphālgunaḥ paravīrahā||4||

5arjuna uvāca |
bhagavanbhūtabhavyeśa sarvabhūtasṛgavyaya
|
lokadhāma jagannātha lokānāmabhayaprada||5||

6yāni nāmāni te deva kīrtitāni maharṣibhiḥ
|
vedeṣu sapurāṇeṣu yāni guhyāni karmabhiḥ||6||

7teṣāṃ niruktaṃ tvatto'haṃ śrotumicchāmi keśava
|
na hyanyo varṇa yennāmnāṃ niruktaṃ tvāmṛte prabho||7||

8śrībhagavānuvāca |
ṛgvede sayajurvede tathaivātharvasāmasu
|
purāṇe sopaniṣade tathaiva jyautiṣe'rjuna||8||

9sāṃkhye ca yogaśāstre ca āyurvede tathaiva ca
|
bahūni mama nāmāni kīrtitāni maharṣibhiḥ||9||

10gauṇāni tatra nāmāni karmajāni ca kānicit
|
niruktaṃ karmajānāṃ tvaṃ śṛṇuṣva prayato'nagha||10||

11kathyamānaṃ mayā tāta tvaṃ hi me'rdhaṃ smṛtaḥ purā
|
namo'tiyaśase tasmai dehināṃ paramātmane||11||

12nārāyaṇāya viśvāya nirguṇāya guṇātmane
|
yasya prasādajo brahmā rudraśca krodhasambhavaḥ||12||

13yo'sau yonirhi sarvasya sthāvarasya carasya ca
|
aṣṭādaśaguṇaṃ yattatsattvaṃ sattvavatāṃ vara||13||

14prakṛtiḥ sā parā mahyaṃ rodasī yogadhāriṇī
|
ṛtāsatyā'marājayyālokānāmātmasaṃjñitā||14||

15tasmātsarvāḥ pravartante sargapralayavikriyāḥ
|
tapo yajñaśca yaṣṭā ca purāṇaḥ puruṣo virāṭ||15||

16aniruddha iti prokto lokānāṃ prabhavāpyayaḥ
|
brāhme rātrikṣaye prāpte tasya hyamitatejasaḥ||16||

17prasādātprādurabhavatpadmaṃ padmanibhekṣaṇa
|
tato brahmā samabhavatsa tasyaiva prasādajaḥ||17||

18ahnaḥ kṣaye lalāṭācca suto devasya vai tathā
|
krodhāviṣṭasya saṃjajñe rudraḥ saṃhārakārakaḥ||18||

19etau dvau vibudhaśreṣṭhau prasādakrodhajāvubhau
|
tadādeśitapanthānau sṛṣṭisaṃhārakārakau||19||

20nimittamātraṃ tāvatra sarvaprāṇivarapradau
|
kapardī jaṭilā muṇḍaḥ śmaśānagṛhasevakaḥ||20||

21ugravratacaro rudro yogī paramadāruṇaḥ
|
dakṣakratuharaścaiva bhaganetraharastathā||21||

22nārāyaṇātmako jñeyaḥ pāṇḍaveya yuge yuge
|
tasminhi pūjyamāne vai devadeve maheśvare||22||

23saṃpūjito bhavetpārtha devo nārāyaṇaḥ prabhuḥ
|
ahamātmā hi lokānāṃ viśveṣāṃ pāṇḍunandana||23||

24tasmādātmānamevāgre rudraṃ sampūjayāmyaham
|
yadyahaṃ nārcayeyaṃ vai īśānaṃ varadaṃ śivam||24||

25ātmānaṃ nārcayetkaściditi me bhāvitātmanaḥ
|
mayā pramāṇaṃ hi kṛtaṃ lokaḥ samanuvartate||25||

26pramāṇāni hi pūjyāni tatastaṃ pūjayāmyaham
|
yastaṃ vetti sa māṃ vetti yo nu taṃ sa hi māmanu||26||

27rudro nārāyaṇaścaiva sattvamekaṃ dvidhākṛtam
|
loke carati kaunteya vyaktisthaṃ sarvakarmasu||27||

28na hi me kenaciddeyo varaḥ pāṇḍavanandana
|
iti sañcintya manasā purāṇaṃ rudramīśvaram||28||

29putrārthamārādhitavānahamātmānamātmanā
|
na hi viṣṇuḥ praṇamati kasmaicidvibudhāya ca||29||

30ṛte ātmānameveti tato rudraṃ bhajāmyaham
|
sabrahmakāḥ sarudrāśca sendrā devāḥ saharṣibhiḥ||30||

31arcayanti suraśreṣṭhaṃ devaṃ nārāyaṇaṃ harim
|
bhaviṣyatāṃ vartatāṃ ca bhūtānāṃ caiva bhārata||31||

32sarveṣāmagraṇīrviṣṇuḥ sevyaḥ pūjyaśca nityaśaḥ
|
namasva havyadaṃ viṣṇuṃ tathā śaraṇadaṃ namaḥ||32||

33varadaṃ namasva kaunteya havyakavyabhujaṃ nama
|
caturvidhā mana janā bhaktā eva hi me śrutam||33||

34teṣāmekāntinaḥ śreṣṭhā ye caivānanyadevatāḥ
|
ahameva gatisteṣāṃ nirāśī: karmakāriṇām||34||

35ye ca śiṣṭāstrayo bhaktāḥ phalakāmā hi te matāḥ
|
sarve cyavanadharmāste pratibuddhastu śreṣṭhabhāk||35||

36brahmāṇaṃ śitikaṇṭhaṃ ca yāścānyā devatāḥ smṛtāḥ
|
prabuddhacaryāḥ sevanto māmevaiṣyanti yatparam||36||

37bhaktaṃ prati viśeṣaste eṣa pārthānukīrtitaḥ
|
tvaṃ caivāhaṃ ca kaunteya naranārāyaṇau smṛtau||37||

38bhārāvataraṇārthaṃ tu praviṣṭau mānuṣīṃ tanum
|
jānāmyadhyātmayogāṃśca yo'haṃ yasmācca bhārata||38||

39nivṛttilakṣaṇo dharmastathā''bhyudayiko'pi ca
|
narāṇāmayanaṃ khyātamahamekaḥ sanātanaḥ||39||

40āpo nārā iti proktā āpo vai narasūnavaḥ
|
ayanaṃ mama tatpūrvamato nārāyaṇo hyaham||40||

41chādayāmi jagadviśvaṃ bhūtvā sūrya ivāṃśubhiḥ
|
sarvabhūtādhivāsaśca vāsudevastato hyaham||41||

42gatiśca sarvabhūtānāṃ prajanaścāpi bhārata
|
vyāptā me rodasī pārtha kāntiścābhyadhikā mama||42||

43adhibhūtāni cānteṣu tadicchaṃścāsmi bhārata
|
kramaṇāccāpyahaṃ pārtha viṣṇurityabhisaṃjñitaḥ||43||

44damātsiddhiṃ parīpsanto māṃ janāḥ kāmayanti ha
|
divaṃ corvīṃ ca madhyaṃ ca tasmāddāmodaro hyaham||44||

45pṛśnirityucyate cānnaṃ veda āpo'mṛtaṃ tathā
|
mamaitāni sadā garbhaḥ pṛśnigarbhastato hyaham||45||

46ṛṣayaḥ prāhurevaṃ māṃ tritaṃ kūpanipātitam
|
pṛśnigarbha tritaṃ pāhītyekatadvitapātitam||46||

47tataḥ sa brahmaṇaḥ putra ādyo hṛṣivarastritaḥ
|
uttatārodapānādvai pṛśnigarbhānukīrtanāt||47||

48sūryasya tapato lokānagneḥ somasya cāpyuta
|
aṃśavo yatprakāśante mamaite keśasaṃjñitāḥ||48||

49sarvajñāḥ keśavaṃ tasmānmāmāhurdvijasattamāḥ
|
svapatnyāmāhito garbha utathyena mahātmanā||49||

50utathye'ntarhite caiva kadāciddevamāyayā
|
bṛhaspatirathāvindatpatnīṃ tasya mahātmanaḥ||50||

51tatau vai tamṛṣiśreṣṭhaṃ maithunopagataṃ tathā
|
uvāca garbha: kaunteya pañcabhūtasamanvitaḥ||51||

52pūrvāgato'haṃ narada nārhasyambāṃ prabādhitum
|
etadbṛhaspatiḥ śrutvā cukrodha ca śaśāpa ca||52||

53maithunāyāgato yasmāttvayā'haṃ vinivāritaḥ
|
tasmādandho yāsyasi tvaṃ macchāpānnātra saṃśayaḥ||53||

54sa śāpadṛṣimukhyasya dīrghaṃ tama upeyivān
|
sa hi dīrghatamā nāma nāmnā hyāsīdṛṣiḥ purā ||54||

55vedānavāpya caturaḥ sāṃgopāṃgānsanātanān
|
prayojayāmāsa tadā nāma guhyamidaṃ mama||55||

56ānupūrvyeṇa vidhinā keśaveti punaḥ punaḥ
|
sa cakṣuṣmānsamabhavadgotamaścābhavatpunaḥ||56||

57evaṃ hi varadaṃ nāma keśaveti mamārjuna
|
devānāmatha sarveṣāmṛṣīṇāṃ ca mahātmanām||57||

58agni: somena saṃyukta ekayonitvamāgataḥ
|
agnīṣomamayaṃ tasmājjagatkṛtsnaṃ carācaram||58||

59parasparamarhanto lokān dhārayanta iti||59||
