Mahābhārata 12.249 (Śāntiparvan)

1vyāsa uvāca |
sṛjate tu guṇān sattvaṃ kṣetrajñastvadhitiṣṭhati
|
guṇān vikriyataḥ sarvānudāsīnavadīśvaraḥ ||1||

2svabhāvayuktaṃ sarvaṃ yadimān sṛjate guṇān
|
urṇanābhiryathā sūtraṃ sṛjate tadguṇāṃstathā ||2||

3pradhvastā na nivartante pravṛttirnopalabhyate
|
evameke vyavasyanti nivṛttiriti cāpare ||3||

4ubhayaṃ sampradhāryaitadadhyavasyedyathāmati
|
anenaiva vidhānena bhavedgarbhaśayo mahān ||4||

5anādinidhano hyātmā taṃ buddhvā vicarennaraḥ
|
akruradhyannaprahṛṣyaṃśca nityaṃ vigatamatsaraḥ ||5||

6ityevaṃ hṛdayagranthiṃ buddhicintāmayaṃ dṛḍham
|
anityaṃ sukhamāsīta aśocaṃśchinnasaṃśayaḥ ||6||

7tāmyeyuḥ pracyutāḥ pṛthvyā yathā pūrṇā nadīṃ narāḥ
|
avagāḍhā hyavidvāṃso viddhi lokamimaṃ tathā ||7||

8na tu tāmyati vai vidvān sthale carati tattvavit
|
evaṃ yo vindate''tmānaṃ kevalaṃ jñānamātmanaḥ ||8||

9evaṃ buddhvā naraḥ sarvaṃ bhūtānāmāgatiṃ gatim
|
samavekṣya ca vaiṣamyaṃ labhate śamamuttamam ||9||

10etadvai janmasāmarthyaṃ brāhmaṇasya viśeṣataḥ
|
ātmajñānaṃ śamaścaiva paryāptaṃ tatparāyaṇam ||10||

11etadbuddhvā bhavecchuddhaḥ kimanyadbuddhalakṣaṇam
|
vijñāyaitadvimucyante kṛtakṛtyā manīṣiṇaḥ ||11||

12na bhavati viduṣāṃ mahadbhayaṃ yadaviduṣā samuhadbhayaṃ paratra
|
na hi gatiradhikā'sti kasyacidbhavati hi yā viduṣaḥ sanātanī ||12||

13lokamāturamasūyate janastattadeva ca nirīkṣya śocate
|
tatra paśya kuśalānaśocato ye vidustadubhayaṃ kṛtākṛtam ||13||

14yatkarotyanabhisandhipūrvakaṃ tacca nirgudati tatpurākṛtam
|
na priyaṃ tadubhayaṃ na cāpriyaṃ tasya tajjanayatīha kurvataḥ ||14||
