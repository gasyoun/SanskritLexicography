Mahābhārata 6.48 (Bhīṣmaparvan)

1dhṛtarāṣṭra uvāca|evaṃ śvete maheṣvāse prāpte śalyarathaṃ prati
|
kuravaḥ pāṇḍaveyāśca kimakurvata sañjaya ||1||

2bhīṣmaḥ śāntanavaḥ kiṃ vā tanmamācakṣva pṛcchataḥ
|
sañjaya uvāca|rājan śatasahasrāṇi tataḥ kṣatriyapuṅgavāḥ ||2||

3śvetaṃ senāpatiṃ śūraṃ puraskṛtya mahārathāḥ
|
rājño balaṃ darśayantastava putrasya bhārata ||3||

4śikhaṇḍinaṃ puraskṛtya trātumaicchanmahārathāḥ
|
abhyavartanta bhīṣmasya rathaṃ hemapariṣkṛtam ||4||

5jighāṃsantaṃ yudhāṃ śreṣṭhaṃ tadā''sīttumulaṃ mahat
|
tatte'haṃ saṃpravakṣyāmi mahāvaiśasamacyuta ||5||

6tāvakānāṃ pareṣāṃ ca yathā yuddhamavartata
|
tatrākarodrathopasthān śūnyān śāntanavo bahūn ||6||

7tatrādbhutaṃ mahaccakre śarerārchadrathottamān
|
samāvṛṇoccharairarkamarkatulyapratāpavān ||7||

8nudan samantāt samare ravirudyan yathā tamaḥ
|
tenājo preṣitā rājan śarāḥ śatasahasraśaḥ ||8||

9kṣatriyāntakarāḥ saṃkhye mahāvegā mahābalāḥ
|
śirāṃsi pātayāmāsurvīrāṇāṃ śataśo raṇe ||9||

10gajān kaṇṭakasannāhān vajreṇeva śiloccayān
|
rathā ratheṣu saṃsaktā vyadṛśyanta viśāṃpate ||10||

11eke rathaṃ paryavahaṃsturagāḥ saturaṅgamam
|
yuvānaṃ nihataṃ vīraṃ lambamānaṃ sakārmukam ||11||

12udīrṇāśca hayā rājan vahantastatra tatra ha
|
baddhakhaḍganiṣa‌ṅgāśca vidhvastaśiraso hatāḥ ||12||

13śataśaḥ patitā bhūmau vīraśayyāsu śerate
|
paraspareṇa dhāvantaḥ patitāḥ punarutthitāḥ ||13||

14utthāya ca pradhāvanto dvandvayuddhamavāpnuvan
|
pīḍitāḥ punaranyonyaṃ luṭhanto raṇamūrdhani ||14||

15sacāpāḥ saniṣaṅgāśca jātarūpapariṣkṛtāḥ
|
visrabdhahatavīrāśca śataśaḥ paripīḍitāḥ ||15||

16tena tenābhyadhāvanta visṛjantaśca bhārata
|
matto gajaḥ paryavartaddhayāṃśca hatasādinaḥ ||16||

17sarathā rathinaścāpi vimṛdnantaḥ samantataḥ
|
syandanādapatatkaścinnihato'nyena sāyakaiḥ ||17||

18hatasārathirapyuccaiḥ papāta kāṣṭhavadrathaḥ
|
yudhyamānasya saṃgrāme vyūḍhe rajasi cotthite ||18||

19dhanuḥkūjitavijñānaṃ tatrāsīt pratiyuddhyataḥ
|
gātrasparśena yodhānāṃ vyajñāsta paripanthinam ||19||

20yuddhyamānaṃ śarai rājan siñjinīdhvajinīravāt
|
anyonyaṃ vīrasaṃśabdo nāśrūyata bhaṭaiḥ kṛtaḥ ||20||

21śabdāyamāne saṃgrāme paṭahe karṇadāriṇi
|
yuddhyamānasya saṃgrāme kurvataḥ pauruṣaṃ svakam ||21||

22nāśrauṣaṃ nāma gotrāṇi kīrtanaṃ ca parasparam
|
bhīṣmacāpacyutairbāṇairārtānāṃ yudhyatāṃ mṛdhe ||22||

23paraspareṣāṃ vīrāṇāṃ manāṃsi samakampayan
|
tasminnatyākule yuddhe dāruṇe lomaharṣaṇe ||23||

24pitā putraṃ ca samare nābhijānāti kaścana
|
cakre bhagne yuge cchinne ekadhurye haye hataḥ ||24||

25ākṣiptaḥ syandanādvīraḥ sasārathirajihmagaiḥ
|
evaṃ ca samare sarve vīrāśca virathīkṛtāḥ ||25||

26tena tena sma dṛśyante dhāvamānāḥ samantataḥ
|
gajo hataḥ śiracchinnaṃ marma bhinnaṃ hayo hataḥ ||26||

27ahataḥ ko'pi naivāsīdbhīṣme nighnati śātravān
|
śvetaḥ kurūṇāmakarot kṣayaṃ tasmin mahāhave ||27||

28rājaputrān rathodārānavadhīcchatasaṃghaśaḥ
|
ciccheda rathināṃ bāṇaiḥ śirāṃsi bharatarṣabha ||28||

29sāṅgadā bāhavaścaiva dhanūṃṣi ca samantataḥ
|
ratheṣāṃ rathacakrāṇi tūṇīrāṇi yugāni ca ||29||

30chatrāṇi ca mahārhāṇi patākāśca viśāṃpate
|
hayaughāśca rathaughāśca naraughāścaiva bhārata ||30||

31vāraṇāḥ śataśaścaiva hatāḥ śvetena bhārata
|
vayaṃ śvetabhayādbhītā vihāya rathasattamam ||31||

32apayātāstathā paścādvibhuṃ paśyāma dhṛṣṇavaḥ
|
śarapātamatikramya kuravaḥ kurunandana ||32||

33bhīṣmaṃ śāntanavaṃ yuddhe sthitāḥ paśyāma sarvaśaḥ
|
adīno dīnasamaye bhīṣmo'smākaṃ mahāhave ||33||

34ekastasthau naravyāghro girirmerurivācalaḥ
|
ādadāna iva prāṇān savitā śiśirātyaye ||34||

35gabhastibhirivādityastasthau śaramarīcimān
|
sa mumoca maheṣvāsaḥ śarasaṅghānanekaśaḥ ||35||

36nighnannamitrān samare vajrapāṇirivāsurān
|
te vadhyamānā bhīṣmeṇa prajahustaṃ mahābalam ||36||

37svayūthādiva te yūthānmuktaṃ bhūmiṣu dāruṇam
|
tamevamupalakṣyaiko hṛṣṭaḥ puṣṭaḥ paraṃtapa ||37||

38duryodhanapriye yuktaḥ pāṇḍavān pariśocayan
|
jīvitaṃ dustyajaṃ tyaktvā bhayaṃ ca sumahāhave ||38||

39pātayāmāsa sainyāni pāṇḍavānāṃ viśāṃpate
|
praharantamanīkāni pitā devavratastava ||39||

40dṛṣṭvā senāpatiṃ bhīṣmastvaritaḥ śvetamabhyayāt
|
sa bhīṣmaṃ śarajālena mahatā samavākirat ||40||

41śvetaṃ cāpi tathā bhīṣmaḥ śaroghaiḥ samavākirat
|
tau vṛṣāviva nardantau mattāviva mahādvipau ||41||

42vyāghrāviva susaṃrabdhāvanyonyamabhijaghnatuḥ
|
astrairasrāṇi saṃvārya tatastau puruṣarṣabhau ||42||

43bhīṣmaḥ śvetaśca yuyudhe parasparavadhaiṣiṇau
|
ekāhnā nirdahedbhīṣmaḥ pāṇḍavānāmanīkinīm ||43||

44śaraiḥ paramasaṃkruddho yadi śvetāṃ na pālayet
|
pitāmahaṃ tato dṛṣṭvā śvetena vimukhīkṛtam ||44||

45praharṣaṃ pāṇḍavā jagmuḥ putraste vimanā'bhavat
|
tato duryodhanaḥ kruddhaḥ pārthivaiḥ parivāritaḥ ||45||

46sasainyaḥ pāṇḍavānīkamabhyadravata saṃyuge
|
durmukhaḥ kṛtavarmā ca kṛpaḥ śalyo viśāṃpatiḥ ||46||

47bhīṣmaṃ jugupurāsādya tava putreṇa noditāḥ
|
dṛṣṭvā tu pārthivaiḥ sarvaiduryodhanapurogamaiḥ ||47||

48pāṇḍavānāmanīkāni vadhyamānāni saṃyuge
|
śveto gāṅgeyamutsṛjya tava putrasya vāhinīm ||48||

49nāśayāmāsa vegena vāyurvṛkṣānivaujasā
|
drāvayitvā camūṃ rājan vairāṭiḥ krodhamūrcchitaḥ ||49||

50āpatatsahasā bhūyo yatra bhīṣmo vyavasthitaḥ
|
tau tatropagatau rājan śaradīptau mahābalau ||50||

51ayudhyetāṃ mahātmānau yathobhau vṛtravāsavau
|
anyonyaṃ tu mahārāja parasparavadhaiṣiṇau ||51||

52nigṛhya kārmukaṃ śveto bhīṣmaṃ vivyādha saptabhiḥ
|
parākramaṃ tatastasya parākramya parākramī ||52||

53tarasā vārayāmāsa matto mattamiva dvipam
|
śvetaḥ śāntanavaṃ bhūyaḥ śaraiḥ sannataparvabhiḥ ||53||

54vivyādha pañcaviṃśatyā tada‌dbhutamivābhavat
|
taṃ pratyavidhyaddaśabhirbhīṣmaḥ śāntanavastadā ||54||

55sa viddhastena balavānnākampata yathā'calaḥ
|
vairāṭiḥ samare kruddho bhṛśamāyamya kārmukam ||55||

56ājaghāna tato bhīṣmaṃ śvetaḥ kṣatriyanandanaḥ
|
saṃprahasya tataḥ śvetaḥ sṛkkiṇī parisaṃlihan ||56||

57dhanuściccheda bhīṣmasya navabhirdaśadhā śaraiḥ
|
saṃdhāya viśikhaṃ caiva śaraṃ lomapravāhinam ||57||

58unmamātha tatastālaṃ dhvajaśīrṣaṃ mahātmanaḥ
|
ketuṃ nipatitaṃ dṛṣṭvā bhīṣmasya tanayāstava ||58||

59hataṃ bhīṣmamamanyanta śvetasya vaśamāgatam
|
pāṇḍavāścāpi saṃhṛṣṭā dadhmuḥ śaṅkhānmudā yutāḥ ||59||

60bhīṣmasya patitaṃ ketuṃ dṛṣṭvā tālaṃ mahātmanaḥ
|
tato duryodhanaḥ krodhāt svamanīkamanodayat ||60||

61yattā bhīṣmaṃ parīpsadhvaṃ rakṣamāṇāḥ samantataḥ
|
mā naḥ prapaśyamānānāṃ śvetānmṛtyumavāpsyati ||61||

62bhīṣmaḥ śāntanavaḥ śūrastathā satyaṃ bravīmi vaḥ
|
rājñastu vacanaṃ śrutvā tvaramāṇā mahārathāḥ ||62||

63balena caturaṅgeṇa gāṅgeyamanvapālayan
|
bāhlīkaḥ kṛtavarmā ca śalaḥ śalyaśca bhārata ||63||

64jalasandho vikarṇaśca citraseno viviṃśatiḥ
|
tvaramāṇāstvarākāle parivārya samantataḥ ||64||

65śastravṛṣṭiṃ sutumulāṃ śvetasyoparyapātayan
|
tān kruddho niśitairbāṇaistvaramāṇo mahārathaḥ ||65||

66avārayadameyātmā darśayan pāṇilāghavam
|
sa nivārya tu tān sarvān kesarī kuñjarāniva ||66||

67mahatā śaravarṣeṇa bhīṣmasya dhanurācchinat
|
tato'nyaddhanurādāya bhīṣmaḥ śāntanavo yudhi ||67||

68śvetaṃ vivyādha rājendra kaṅkapatraiḥ śitaiḥ śaraiḥ
|
tataḥ senāpatiḥ kruddho bhīṣmaṃ bahubhirāyasaiḥ ||68||

69vivyādha samare rājan sarvalokasya paśyataḥ
|
tataḥ pravyathito rājā bhīṣmaṃ dṛṣṭvā nivāritam ||69||

70pravīraṃ sarvalokasya śvetena yudhi vai tadā
|
niṣṭhānakaśca sumahāṃstava sainyasya cābhavat ||70||

71taṃ vīraṃ vāritaṃ dṛṣṭvā śvetena śaravikṣatam
|
hataṃ śvetena manyante śvetasya vaśamāgatam ||71||

72tataḥ krodhavaśaṃ prāptaḥ pitā devavratastava
|
dhvajamunmathitaṃ dṛṣṭvā tāṃ ca senāṃ nivāritām ||72||

73śvetaṃ prati mahārāja vyasṛjat sāyakān bahun
|
tānāvārya raṇe śveto bhīṣmasya rathināṃ varaḥ ||73||

74dhanuściccheda bhallena punareva pitustava
|
utsṛjya kārmukaṃ rājan gāṅgeyaḥ krodhamūrcchitaḥ ||74||

75anyat kārmukamādāya vipulaṃ balavattaram
|
tatra sandhāya vipulān bhallān sapta śilāśitān ||75||

76caturbhiśca jaghānāśvān śvetasya pṛtanāpateḥ
|
dhvajaṃ dvābhyāṃ tu ciccheda saptamena ca sāratheḥ ||76||

77śiraściccheda bhallena saṅkruddho laghuvikramaḥ
|
hatāśvasūtāt sa rathādavaplutya mahābalaḥ ||77||

78amarṣavaśamāpanno vyākulaḥ samapadyata
|
virathaṃ rathināṃ śreṣṭhaṃ śvetaṃ dṛṣṭvā pitāmahaḥ ||78||

79tāḍayāmāsa niśitaiḥ śarasaṅghaiḥ samantataḥ
|
sa tāḍyamānaḥ samare bhīṣmacāpacyutaiḥ śaraiḥ||79||

80svarathe dhanurutsṛjya śaktiṃ jagrāha kāñcanīm
|
tataḥ śaktiṃ raṇe śveto jagrāhogrāṃ mahābhayām ||80||

81kāladaṇḍopamāṃ ghorāṃ mṛtyorjihvāmiva śvasan
|
abravīcca tadā śveto bhīṣmaṃ śāntanavaṃ raṇe ||81||

82tiṣṭhedānīṃ susaṃrabdhaḥ paśya māṃ puruṣo bhava
|
evamuktvā maheṣvāso bhīṣmaṃ yudhi parākramī ||82||

83tataḥ śaktimameyātmā cikṣepa bhujagopamām
|
pāṇḍavārthe parākrāntastavānarthaṃ cikīrṣukaḥ ||83||

84hāhākāro mahānāsīt putrāṇāṃ te viśāṃpate
|
dṛṣṭvā śaktiṃ mahāghorāṃ mṛtyordaṇḍasamaprabhām ||84||

85śvetasya karanirmuktāṃ nirmuktoragasannibhām
|
apatat sahasā rājan maholkeva nabhastalāt ||85||

86jvalantīmantarikṣe tāṃ jvālābhiriva saṃvṛtām
|
asaṃbhrāntastadā rājan pitā devavratastava ||86||

87aṣṭabhirnavabhirbhīṣmaḥ śaktiṃ ciccheda patribhiḥ
|
utkṛṣṭahemavikṛtāṃ nikṛtāṃ niśitaiḥ śaraiḥ ||87||

88uccukruśustataḥ sarve tāvakā bharatarṣabha
|
śaktiṃ vinihatāṃ dṛṣṭvā vairāṭiḥ krodhamūrcchitaḥ ||88||

89kālopahatacetāstu kartavyaṃ nābhyajānata
|
krodhasaṃmūrcchito rājan vairāṭiḥ prahasanniva ||89||

90gadāṃ jagrāha saṃhṛṣṭo bhīṣmasya nidhanaṃ prati
|
krodhena raktanayano daṇḍapāṇirivāntakaḥ ||90||

91bhīṣmaṃ samabhidudrāva jalaugha iva parvatam
|
tasya vegamasaṃvāryaṃ matvā bhīṣmaḥ pratāpavān ||91||

92prahāravipramokṣārthaṃ sahasā dharaṇīṃ gataḥ
|
śvetaḥ krodhasamāviṣṭo bhrāmayitvā tu tāṃ gadām ||92||

93rathe bhīṣmasya cikṣepa yathā devo dhaneśvaraḥ
|
tayā bhīṣmanipātinyā sa ratho bhasmasātkṛtaḥ ||93||

94sadhvajaḥ saha sūtena sāśvaḥ sayugabandhuraḥ
|
virathaṃ rathināṃ śreṣṭhaṃ bhīṣmaṃ dṛṣṭvā rathottamāḥ ||94||

95abhyadhāvanta sahitāḥ śalyaprabhṛtayo rathāḥ
|
tato'nyaṃ rathamāsthāya dhanurvisphārya durmanāḥ ||95||

96śanakairabhyayācchvetaṃ gāṅgeyaḥ prahasanniva
|
etasminnantare bhīṣmaḥ śuśrāva vipulāṃ giram ||96||

97ākāśādīritāṃ divyāmātmano hitasaṃbhavām
|
bhīṣma bhīṣma mahābāho śīghraṃ yatnaṃ kuruṣva vai ||97||

98eṣa hyasya jaye kālo nirdiṣṭo viśvayoninā
|
etacchrutvā tu vacanaṃ devadūtena bhāṣitam ||98||

99saṃprahṛṣṭamanā bhūtvā vadhe tasya mano dadhe
|
virathaṃ rathināṃ śreṣṭhaṃ śvetaṃ dṛṣṭvā padātinam ||99||

100sahitāstvabhyavartanta parīpsanto mahārathāḥ
|
sātyakirbhīmasenaśca dhṛṣṭadyumnaśca pārṣataḥ ||100||

101kaikeyo dhṛṣṭaketuśca abhimanyuśca vīryavān
|
etānāpatataḥ sarvān droṇaśalyakṛpaiḥ saha ||101||

102avārayadameyātmā vārivegānivācalaḥ
|
sa niruddheṣu sarveṣu pāṇḍaveṣu mahātmasu ||102||

103śvetaḥ khaḍgamathākṛṣya bhīṣmasya dhanurācchinat
|
tadapāsya dhanuśchinnaṃ tvaramāṇaḥ pitāmahaḥ ||103||

104devadūtavacaḥ śrutvā vadhe tasya mano dadhe
|
tataḥ pracaramāṇastu pitā devavratastava ||104||

105anyat kārmukamādāya tvaramāṇo mahārathaḥ
|
kṣaṇena sajyamakarocchakracāpasamaprabham ||105||

106pitā te bharataśreṣṭha śvetaṃ dṛṣṭvā mahārathaiḥ
|
vṛtaṃ taṃ manujavyāghrairbhīmasenapurogamaiḥ ||106||

107abhyavartata gāṅgeyaḥ śvetaṃ senāpatiṃ drutam
|
āpatantaṃ tato bhīṣmo bhīmasenaṃ pratāpavān ||107||

108ājaghne viśikhaiḥ ṣaṣṭyā senānyaṃ sa mahārathaḥ
|
abhimanyuṃ ca samare pitā devavratastava ||108||

109ājaghne bharataśreṣṭhastribhiḥ sannataparvabhiḥ
|
sātyakiṃ ca śatenājau bharatānāṃ pitāmahaḥ ||109||

110dhṛṣṭadyumnaṃ ca viṃśatyā kaikeyaṃ cāpi pañcabhiḥ
|
tāṃśca sarvān maheṣvāsān pitā devavratastava ||110||

111vārayitvā śarairghoraiḥ śvetamevābhidudruve
|
tataḥ śaraṃ mṛtyusamaṃ bhārasādhanamuttamam ||111||

112vikṛṣya balavān bhīṣmaḥ samādhatta durāsadam
|
brahmāstreṇa susaṃyuktaṃ taṃ śaraṃ lomavāhinam ||112||

113dadṛśurdevagandharvāḥ piśācoragarākṣasāḥ
|
sa tasya kavacaṃ bhittvā hṛdayaṃ cāmitaujasaḥ ||113||

114jagāma dharaṇīṃ bāṇo mahāśaniriva jvalan
|
astaṃ gacchan yathādityaḥ prabhāmādāya satvaraḥ ||114||

115evaṃ jīvitamādāya śvetadehājjagāma ha
|
taṃ bhīṣmeṇa naravyāghraṃ tathā vinihataṃ yudhi ||115||

116prapatantamapaśyāma gireḥ śṛṅgamiva cyutam
|
aśocan pāṇḍavāstatra kṣatriyāśca mahārathāḥ ||116||

117prahṛṣṭāśca sutāstubhyaṃ kuravaścāpi sarvaśaḥ
|
tato duḥśāsano rājan śvetaṃ dṛṣṭvā nipātitam ||117||

118vāditraninadairghorairnṛtyati sma samantataḥ
|
tasmin hate maheṣvāse bhīṣmeṇāhavaśobhinā ||118||

119prāvepanta maheṣvāsāḥ śikhaṇḍipramukhā rathāḥ
|
tato dhanañjayo rājan vārṣṇeyaścāpi sarvaśaḥ ||119||

120avahāraṃ śanaiścakrurnihate vāhinīpatau
|
tato'vahāraḥ sainyānāṃ tava teṣāṃ ca bhārata ||120||

121tāvakānāṃ pareṣāṃ ca nardatāṃ ca muhurmuhuḥ
|
pārthā vimanaso bhūtvā nyavartanta mahārathāḥ
|
cintayanto vadhaṃ ghoraṃ dvairathena paraṃtapāḥ ||121||
