Mahābhārata 7.165 (Droṇaparvan)

1sañjaya uvāca|
vartamāne tadā raudre rātriyuddhe viśāṃpate
|
sarvabhūtakṣayakare dharmaputro yudhiṣṭhiraḥ||1||

2abravītpāṇḍavāṃścaiva pañcālāṃścaiva somakān
|
abhidravata saṃyāta droṇameva jighāṃsayā||2||

3rājñaste vacanādrājanpañcālāḥ sṛñjayāstathā
|
droṇamevābhyavartanta nadanto bhairavān ravān||3||

4taṃ tu te pratigarjantaḥ pratyudyātāstvamarṣitāḥ
|
yathāśakti yathotsāhaṃ yathāsattvaṃ ca saṃyuge||4||

5kṛtavarmā tu hārdikyo yudhiṣṭhiramupādravat
|
droṇaṃ prati samāyāntaṃ matto mattamiva dvipam||5||

6śaineyaṃ śaravarṣāṇi vikirantaṃ samantataḥ
|
abhyayātkauravo rājan bhūriḥ saṃgrāmamūrdhani||6||

7sahadevamathāyāntaṃ droṇaprepsuṃ mahāratham
|
karṇo vaikartano rājanvārayāmāsa pāṇḍavam||7||

8bhīmasenamathāyāntaṃ vyāditāsyamivāntakam
|
svayaṃ duryodhano rājā pratīpaṃ mṛtyumāvrajat||8||

9nakulaṃ ca yudhāṃ śreṣṭhaṃ sarvayuddhaviśāradam
|
śakuniḥ saubalo rājanvārayāmāsa satvaraḥ||9||

10śikhaṇḍinamathāyāntaṃ rathena rathināṃ varam
|
kṛpaḥ śāradvato rājanvārayāmāsa saṃyuge||10||

11prativindhyamathāyāntaṃ mayūrasadṛśairhayaḥ
|
duḥśāsano mahārāja yatto yattamavārayat||11||

12bhaimasenimathāyāntaṃ māyāśataviśāradam
|
aśvatthāmā mahārāja rākṣasaṃ pratyaṣedhayat||12||

13drupadaṃ vṛṣasenastu sasainyaṃ sapadānugam
|
vārayāmāsa samare droṇaprepsuṃ mahāratham||13||

14virāṭaṃ drutamāyāntaṃ droṇasya nidhanaṃ prati
|
madrarājaḥ susaṃkruddho vārayāmāsa bhārata||14||

15śatānīkamathāyāntaṃ nākuliṃ rabhasaṃ raṇe
|
citraseno rurodhāśu śarairdroṇaparīpsayā||15||

16arjunaṃ ca yudhāṃ śreṣṭhaṃ prādravantaṃ mahāratham
|
alaṃbuṣo mahārāja rākṣasendro nyavārayat||16||

17tathā droṇaṃ maheṣvāsaṃ nighnantaṃ śātravān raṇe
|
dhṛṣṭadyumno'tha pāñcālyo hṛṣṭarūpamavārayat||17||

18tathānyānpāṇḍuputrāṇāṃ samāyātānmahārathān
|
tāvakā rathino rājanvārayāmāsurojasā||18||

19gajārohā gajaistūrṇaṃ sannipatya mahāmṛdhe
|
yodhayantaśca mṛdmantaḥ śataśo'tha sahasraśaḥ||19||

20niśīthe turagā rājandrāvayantaḥ parasparam
|
samadṛśyanta vegena pakṣavanto yathā'drayaḥ||20||

21sādinaḥ sādibhiḥ sārdhaṃ prāsaśaktyṛṣṭipāṇayaḥ
|
samāgacchanmahārāja vinadantaḥ pṛthak pṛthak||21||

22narāstu bahavastatra samājagmuḥ parasparam
|
gadābhirmusalaiścaiva nānāśastraiśca saṃyuge||22||

23kṛtavarmā tu hārdikyo dharmaputraṃ yudhiṣṭhiram
|
vārayāmāsa saṃkruddho velevoddhṛtamarṇavam||23||

24yudhiṣṭhirastu hārdikyaṃ viddhvā pañcabhirāśugaiḥ
|
punarvivyādha viṃśatyā tiṣṭha tiṣṭheti cābravīt||24||

25kṛtavarmā tu saṃkruddho dharmaputrasya māriṣa
|
dhanuściccheda bhallena taṃ ca vivyādha saptabhiḥ||25||

26athānyaddhanurādāya dharmaputro mahārathaḥ
|
hārdikyaṃ daśabhirbāṇairbāhvorurasi cārpayat||26||

27mādhavastu raṇe viddho dharmaputreṇa māriṣa
|
prākampata ca roṣeṇa saptabhiścārdayaccharaiḥ||27||

28tasya pārtho dhanuśchittvā hastāvāpaṃ nikṛtya ca
|
prāhiṇonniśitānbāṇānpañca rājacchilāśitān||28||

29te tasya kavacaṃ bhittvā hemacitraṃ mahādhanam
|
prāviśandharaṇīṃ bhittvā valmīkamiva pannagāḥ||29||

30akṣṇornimeṣamātreṇa so'nyadādāya kārmukam
|
vivyādha pāṇḍavaṃ ṣaṣṭyā sutaṃ ca navabhiḥ śaraiḥ||30||

31tasya śaktimameyātmā pāṇḍavo bhujagopamām
|
cikṣepa bharataśreṣṭha rathe nyasya mahaddhanuḥ||31||

32sā hemacitrā mahatī pāṇḍavena praveritā
|
nirbhidya dakṣiṇaṃ bāhuṃ prāviśaddharaṇītalam||32||

33etasminneva kāle tu gṛhya pārthaḥ punardhanuḥ
|
hārdikyaṃ chādayāmāsa śaraiḥ sannataparvabhiḥ||33||

34tatastu samare śūro vṛṣṇīnāṃ pravaro rathī
|
vyaśvasūtarathaṃ cakre nimeṣārdhādyudhiṣṭhiram||34||

35tatastu pāṇḍavo jyeṣṭhaḥ khaḍgaṃ carma samādade
|
tadasya niśitairbāṇairvyadhamanmādhavo raṇe||35||

36tomaraṃ tu tato gṛhya svarṇadaṇḍaṃ durāsadam
|
apraiṣītsamare tūrṇaṃ hārdikyasya yudhiṣṭhiraḥ||36||

37tamāpatantaṃ sahasā dharmarājabhujacyutam
|
dvidhā ciccheda hārdikyaḥ kṛtahastaḥ smayanniva||37||

38tataḥ śaraśatenājau dharmaputramavākirat
|
kavacaṃ cāsya saṃkruddhaḥ śaraistīkṣṇairadārayat||38||

39hārdikyaṃ śarasaṃchannaṃ kavacaṃ tanmahādhanam
|
vyaśīryata raṇe rājaṃstārājālamivāmbarāt||39||

40sa cchinnadhanvā virathaḥ śarṇivarmā śarārditaḥ
|
apāyāsīdraṇāttūrṇaṃ dharmaputro yudhiṣṭhiraḥ||40||

41kṛtavarmā tu nirjitya dharmātmānaṃ yudhiṣṭhiram
|
punardroṇasya jugupe cakrameva mahātmanaḥ||41||
