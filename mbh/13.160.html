Mahābhārata 13.160 (Anuśāsanaparvan)

1yudhiṣṭhira uvāca|
durvāsasaḥ prasādātte yattadā madhusūdana
|
avāptamiha vijñānaṃ tanme vyākhyātumarhasi ||1||

2mahābhāgyaṃ ca yattasya nāmāni ca mahātmanaḥ
|
tattvatto jñātumicchāmi sarvaṃ matimatāṃ vara ||2||

3vāsudeva uvāca|
hanta te kīrtayiṣyāmi namaskṛtya kapardine
|
yadavāptaṃ mayā rājañchreyo yaccārjitaṃ yaśaḥ ||3||

4prayataḥ prātarutthāya yadadhīye viśāṃ pate
|
prāñjaliḥ śatarudrīyaṃ tanme nigadataḥ śṛṇu ||4||

5prajāpatistatsasṛje tapaso'nte mahātapāḥ
|
śaṅkarastvasṛjattāta prajāḥ sthāvarajaṅgamāḥ ||5||

6nāsti kiñcitparaṃ bhūtaṃ mahādevādviśāṃ pate
|
iha triṣvapi lokeṣu bhūtānāṃ prabhavo hi saḥ ||6||

7na caivotsahate sthātuṃ kaścidagre mahātmanaḥ
|
na hi bhūtaṃ samaṃ tena triṣu lokeṣu vidyate ||7||

8gandhenāpi hi saṃgrāme tasya kruddhasya śatravaḥ
|
visaṃjñā hatabhūyiṣṭhā vepante ca patanti ca ||8||

9ghoraṃ ca ninadaṃ tasya parjanyaninadopamam
|
śrutvā viśīryeddhṛdayaṃ devānāmapi saṃyuge ||9||

10yāṃśca ghoreṇa rūpeṇa paśyetkruddhaḥ pinākadhṛt
|
na surā nāsurā loke na gandharvā na pannagāḥ ||10||

11kupite sukhamedhante tasminnapi guhāgatāḥ
|
prajāpateśca dakṣasya yajato vitate kratau ||11||

12vivyādha kupito yajñaṃ nirbhayastu bhavastadā
|
dhanuṣā bāṇamutsṛjya saghoṣaṃ vinanāda ca ||12||

13te na śarma kutaḥ śāntiṃ viṣādaṃ lebhire surāḥ
|
viddhe ca sahasā yajñe kupite ca maheśvare ||13||

14tena jyātalaghoṣeṇa sarve lokāḥ samākulāḥ
|
babhūvuravaśāḥ pārtha viṣeduśca surāsurāḥ||14||

15āpaścukṣubhire caiva cakampe ca vasundharā
|
vyadravan girayaścāpi dyauḥ paphāla ca sarvaśaḥ ||15||

16andhena tamasā lokāḥ prāvṛtā na cakāśire
|
pranaṣṭā jyotiṣāṃ bhāśca saha sūryeṇa bhārata ||16||

17bhṛśaṃ bhītāstataḥ śāntiṃ cakruḥ svastyayanāni ca
|
ṛṣayaḥ sarvabhūtānāmātmanaśca hitaiṣiṇaḥ ||17||

18tataḥ so'bhyadravaddevānrudro raudraparākramaḥ
|
bhagasya nayane kruddhaḥ prahāreṇa vyaśātayat||18||

19pūṣaṇaṃ cābhidudrāva pādena ca ruṣānvitaḥ
|
puroḍāśaṃ bhakṣayato daśanānvai vyaśātayat ||19||

20tataḥ praṇemurdevāste vepamānāḥ sma śaṅkaram
|
punaśca sandadhe rudro dīptaṃ suniśitaṃ śaram ||20||

21rudrasya vikramaṃ dṛṣṭvā bhītā devāḥ saharṣibhiḥ
|
tataḥ prasādayāmāsuḥ śarvaṃ te vibudhottamāḥ ||21||

22jepuśca śatarudrīyaṃ devāḥ kṛtvā'ñjaliṃ tadā
|
saṃstūyamānastridaśaiḥ prasāda maheśvaraḥ ||22||

23rudrasya bhāgaṃ yajñe ca viśiṣṭaṃ te tvakalpayan
|
bhayena tridaśā rājañcharaṇaṃ ca prapedire ||23||

24tena caiva hi tuṣṭena sa yajñaḥ sandhito'bhavat
|
yadyaccāpahṛtaṃ tatra tattathaivānvajīvayat ||24||

25asurāṇāṃ purāṇyāsaṃstrīṇi vīryavatāṃ divi
|
āyasaṃ rājataṃ caiva sauvarṇamapi cāparam ||25||

26nāśakattāni maghavā jetuṃ sarvāyudhairapi
|
atha sarve'marā rudraṃ jagmuḥ śaraṇamarditāḥ||26||

27tata ūcurmahātmāno devāḥ sarve samāgatāḥ
|
rudra raudrā bhaviṣyanti paśavaḥ sarvakarmasu ||27||

28jahi daityānsaha purairlokāṃstrāyasva mānada
|
sa tathoktastathetyuktvā kṛtvā viṣṇuṃ śarottamam ||28||

29śalyamagniṃ tathā kṛtvā puṅkhaṃ vaivasvataṃ yamam
|
vedān kṛtvā dhanuḥ sarvānjyāṃ ca sāvitrimuttamām ||29||

30brahmāṇaṃ sārathiṃ kṛtvā viniyujya ca sarvaśaḥ
|
triparvaṇā triśalyena tena tāni bibheda saḥ ||30||

31śareṇādityavarṇena kālāgnisamatejasā
|
te surāḥ sapurāstatra dagdhā rudreṇa bhārata ||31||

32taṃ caivāṅkagataṃ dṛṣṭvā bālaṃ pañcaśikhaṃ punaḥ
|
umā jijñāsamānā vai ko'yamityabravīttadā ||32||

33asūyataśca śakrasya vajreṇa prahariṣyataḥ
|
sa vajraṃ stambhayāmāsa taṃ bāhuṃ parighopamam ||33||

34na saṃbubudhire caiva devāstaṃ bhuvaneśvaram
|
saprajāpatayaḥ sarve tasminmumuhurīśvare ||34||

35tato dhyātvā ca bhagavānbrahmā tamamitaujasam
|
ayaṃ śreṣṭha iti jñātvā vavande tamumāpatim ||35||

36tataḥ prasādayāmāsurumāṃ rudraṃ ca te surāḥ
|
babhūva sa tadā bāhurbalahantairyathā purā ||36||

37sa cāpi brāhmaṇo bhūtvā durvāsā nāma vīryavān
|
dvāravatyāṃ mama gṛhe ciraṃ kālamupāvasan ||37||

38viprakārān prayuṅkte sma subahūnmama veśmani
|
tānudāratayā cāhaṃ cakṣame cātiduḥsahān ||38||

39sa vai rudraḥ sa ca śivaḥ so'gniḥ sarvaḥ sa sarvajit
|
sa caivendraśca vāyuścaso'śvinau sa ca vidyutaḥ ||39||

40sa candramāḥ sa ceśānaḥ sa sūryo varuṇaśca saḥ
|
sa kālaḥ sontako mṛtyuḥ sa yamo rātryahāni ca ||40||

41māsārdhamāsā ṛtavaḥ saṃdhye saṃvatsaraśca saḥ
|
sa dhātā sa vidhātā ca viśvakarmā sa sarvavit ||41||

42nakṣatrāṇi gṛhāścaiva diśo'tha pradiśastathā
|
viśvamūrtirameyātmā bhagavānparamadyutiḥ ||42||

43ekadhā ca dvidhā caiva bahudhā ca sa eva hi
|
śatadhā sahasradhā caiva tathā śatasahasradhā ||43||

44īdṛśaḥ sa mahādevo bhūyaśca bhagavānataḥ
|
na hi śakyā guṇā vaktumapi varṣaśatairapi ||44||
