Mahābhārata 13.45 (Anuśāsanaparvan)

1yudhiṣṭhira uvāca|
kanyāyāḥ prāptaśulkāyāḥ patiścennāsti kaścana
|
tatra kā pratipattiḥ syāttanme brūhi pitāmaha||1||

2bhīṣma uvāca|
yā'putrakasya ṛddhasya pratipālyā tadā bhavet
|
atha cennāharecchulkaṃ krītā śulkapradasya sā||2||

3tasyārthe'patyamīheta yena nyāyena śaknuyāt
|
na tasmānmantravatkāryaṃ kaścitkurvīta kiñcana||3||

4svayaṃvṛtena sā''jñaptā pitrā vai pratyapadyata
|
tattasyānye praśaṃsanti dharmajñā netare dvijāḥ||4||

5etattu nāpare cakrurapare jātu sādhavaḥ
|
sādhūnāṃ punarācāro garīyāndharmalakṣaṇaḥ ||5||

6asminneva prakaraṇe sukraturvākyamabravīt
|
naptā videharājasya janakasya mahātmanaḥ ||6||

7asadācarite mārge kathaṃ syāda‌nukīrtanam
|
atra praśnaḥ saṃśayo vā satāmevamupālabhet ||7||

8asadeva hi dharmasya pradānaṃ dharma āsuraḥ
|
nānuśuśruma jātvetāmimāṃ pūrveṣu karmasu ||8||

9bhāryāpatyorhi sambandhaḥ strīpuṃsoḥ svalpa eva tu
|
ratiḥ sādhāraṇo dharma iti cāha sa pārthivaḥ||9||

10yudhiṣṭhira uvāca|
atha kena pramāṇena puṃsāmādīyate dhanam
|
putravaddhi pitustasya kanyā bhavitumarhati ||10||

11bhīṣma uvāca|
yathaivātmā tathā putraḥ putreṇa duhitā samā
|
tasyāmātmani tiṣṭhantyāṃ kathamanyo dhanaṃ haret||11||

12mātuśca yautakaṃ yatsyātkumārībhāga eva saḥ
|
dauhitra eva tadrikthamaputrasya piturharet ||12||

13dadāti hi sapiṇḍānvai piturmātāmahasya ca
|
putradauhitrayoreva viśeṣo nāsti dharmataḥ ||13||

14anyatra jāmayā sārdhaṃ prajānāṃ putra īhate
|
duhitā'nyatra jātena putreṇāpi viśiṣyate ||14||

15dauhitrakeṇa dharmeṇa nātra paśyāmi kāraṇam
|
vikrītāsu hi ye putrā bhavanti pitureva te ||15||

16asūyavastvadharmiṣṭhāḥ parasvādāyinaḥ śaṭhāḥ
|
āsurādadhisambhūtā dharmādviṣamavṛttayaḥ ||16||

17atra gāthā yamodgītāḥ kīrtayanti purāvidaḥ
|
dharmajñā dharmaśāstreṣu nibaddhā dharmasetuṣu ||17||

18yo manuṣyaḥ svakaṃ putraṃ vikrīya dhanamicchati
|
kanyāṃ vā jīvitārthāya yaḥ śulkena prayacchati||18||

19saptāvare mahāghore niraye kālasāhvaye
|
svedaṃ mūtraṃ purīṣaṃ ca tasminmūḍhaḥ samaśrute ||19||

20ārṣe gomithunaṃ śulkaṃ kecidāhurmṛṣaiva tat
|
alpo vā bahu vā rājan vikrayastāvadeva saḥ||20||

21yadyapyācaritaḥ kaiścinnaiṣa dharmaḥ sanātanaḥ
|
anyeṣāmapi dṛśyante lokataḥ saṃpravṛttayaḥ ||21||

22vaśyāṃ kumārīṃ balato ye tāṃ samupabhuñjate
|
ete pāpasya kartārastamasyandhe ca śerate ||22||

23anyo'pyatha na vikreyo manuṣyaḥ kiṃ punaḥ prajāḥ
|
adharmamūlairhi dhanaistairna dharmo'tha kaścana ||23||
