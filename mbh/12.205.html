Mahābhārata 12.205 (Śāntiparvan)

1manuruvāca |
duḥkhopaghāte śārīre mānase cāpyupasthite
|
yasminna śakyate kartuṃ yatnastaṃ nānucintayet ||1||

2bhaiṣajyametadduḥkhasya yadetannānucintayet
|
cintyamānaṃ hi cābhyeti bhūyaścāpi pravartate ||2||

3prajñayā mānasaṃ duḥkhaṃ hanyācchārīramauṣadhaiḥ
|
etadvijñānasāmarthyaṃ na bālaiḥ samatāmiyāt ||3||

4anityaṃ yauvanaṃ rūpaṃ jīvitaṃ dravyasaṃcayaḥ
|
ārogyaṃ priyasaṃvāso gṛdhyettatra na paṇḍitaḥ ||4||

5na jānapadikaṃ duḥkhamekaḥ śocitumarhati
|
aśocan pratikurvīta yadi paśyedupakramam ||5||

6sukhādbahutaraṃ duḥkhaṃ jīvite nāsti saṃśayaḥ
|
snigdhasya cendriyārtheṣu mohānmaraṇamapriyam ||6||

7parityajati yo duḥkhaṃ sukhaṃ vāpyubhayaṃ naraḥ
|
abhyeti brahma so'tyantaṃ na te śocanti paṇḍitāḥ ||7||

8duḥkhamarthā hi yujyante pālanena ca te sukham
|
duḥkhena cādhigamyante nāśameṣāṃ na cintayet ||8||

9jñānaṃ jñeyābhinirvṛttaṃ viddhi jñānaguṇaṃ manaḥ
|
prajñākaraṇasaṃyuktaṃ tato buddhiḥ pravartate ||9||

10yadā karmaguṇairhīnā buddhirmanasi vartate
|
tadā prajñāyate brahma dhyānayogasamādhinā ||10||

11seyaṃ guṇavatī buddhirguṇeṣvevābhivartate
|
aparādabhiniḥsṛtya gireḥ śṛṅgādivodakam ||11||

12yadā nirguṇamāpnoti dhyānaṃ manasi pūrvajam
|
tadā prajñāyate brahma nikaṣaṃ nikaṣe yathā ||12||

13manastvapahṛtaṃ pūrvamindriyārthanidarśakam
|
na samakṣaguṇāpekṣi nirguṇasya nidarśakam ||13||

14sarvāṇyetāni saṃvārya dvārāṇi manasi sthitaḥ
|
manasyekāgratāṃ kṛtvā tatparaṃ pratipadyate ||14||

15yathā mahānti bhūtāni nivartante guṇakṣaye
|
tathendriyāṇyupādāya buddhirmanasi vartate ||15||

16yadā manasi sā buddhirvartate'ntaracāriṇī
|
vyavasāyaguṇopetā tadā sampadyate manaḥ ||16||

17guṇavadbhirguṇopetaṃ yadā dhyānaguṇaṃ manaḥ
|
sadā sarvān guṇān hitvā nirguṇaṃ pratipadyate ||17||

18avyaktasyeha vijñāne nāsti tulyaṃ nidarśanam
|
yatra nāsti padanyāsaḥ kastaṃ viṣayamāpnuyāt ||18||

19tapasā cānumānena guṇairjātyā śrutena ca
|
ninīṣetparamaṃ brahma viśuddhenāntarātmanā ||19||

20guṇahīno hi taṃ māga bahiḥ samanuvartate
|
guṇābhāvātprakṛtyā vā nistarkyaṃ jñeyasammitam ||20||

21nairguṇyādbrahma cāpnoti saguṇatvānnivartate
|
guṇapracāriṇī buddhirhutāśana ivendhane ||21||

22yathā pañca vimuktāni indriyāṇi svakarmabhiḥ
|
tathā hi paramaṃ brahma vimuktaṃ prakṛteḥ param ||22||

23evaṃ prakṛtitaḥ sarve pravartante śarīriṇaḥ
|
nivartante nivṛttau ca svargaṃ caivopayānti ca ||23||

24puruṣaḥ prakṛtirbuddhirviṣayāścendriyāṇi ca
|
ahaṃkāro'bhimānaśca samūho bhūtasaṃjñakaḥ ||24||

25etasyādyā pravṛttistu pradhānātsampravartate
|
dvitīyā mithunavyaktimaviśeṣānniyacchati ||25||

26dharmādutkṛṣyate śreyastathā'śreyo'pyadharmataḥ
|
rāgavānprakṛtiṃ hyeti virakto jñānavān bhavet ||26||
