Mahābhārata 7.94 (Droṇaparvan)

1sañjaya uvāca|
tataḥ praviṣṭe kaunteye sindhurājajighāṃsayā
|
droṇānīkaṃ vinirbhidya bhojānīkaṃ ca dustaram ||1||

2kāmbojasya ca dāyāde hate rājan sudakṣiṇe
|
śrutāyudhe ca vikrānte nihate savyasācinā ||2||

3vipradruteṣvanīkeṣu vidhvasteṣu samantataḥ
|
prabhagnaṃ svabalaṃ dṛṣṭvā putraste droṇamabhyayāt ||3||

4tvarannekarathenaiva sametya droṇamabravīt
|
gataḥ sa puruṣavyāghraḥ pramathyaitāṃ mahācamūm ||4||

5atha buddhyā samīkṣasva kinnu kāryamanantaram
|
arjunasya vighātāya dāruṇe'smin janakṣaye ||5||

6yathā sa puruṣavyāghro na hanyeta jayadrathaḥ
|
tathā vidhatsva bhadraṃ te tvaṃ hi naḥ paramā gatiḥ ||6||

7asau dhanañjayāgnirhi kopamārutacoditaḥ
|
senākakṣaṃ dahati me vahniḥ kakṣamivotthitaḥ ||7||

8atikrānte hi kaunteye bhittvā sainyaṃ parantapa
|
jayadrathasya goptāraḥ saṃśayaṃ paramaṃ gatāḥ ||8||

9sthirā buddhirnarendrāṇāmāsīdbrahmavidāṃ vara
|
nātikramiṣyati droṇaṃ jātu jīvaṃ dhanañjayaḥ ||9||

10yo'sau pārtho vyatikrānto miṣataste mahādyute
|
sarvaṃ hyadyāturaṃ manye nedamasti balaṃ mama ||10||

11jānāmi tvāṃ mahābhāga pāṇḍavānāṃ hite ratam
|
tathā muhyāmi ca brahman kāryavattāṃ vicintayan ||11||

12yathāśakti ca te brahman vartaye vṛttimuttamām
|
prīṇāmi ca yathāśakti tacca tvaṃ nāvabudhyase ||12||

13asmānna tvaṃ sadā bhaktānicchasyamitavikrama
|
pāṇḍavān satataṃ prīṇāsyasmākaṃ vipriye ratān ||13||

14asmānevopajīvaṃstvamasmākaṃ vipriye rataḥ
|
na hyahaṃ tvāṃ vijānāmi madhudigdhamiva kṣuram ||14||

15nādāsyaccedvaraṃ mahyaṃ bhavān pāṇḍavanigrahe
|
nāvārayiṣyaṃ gacchantamahaṃ sindhupatiṃ gṛhān ||15||

16mayā tvāśaṃsamānena tvattastrāṇamabuddhinā
|
āśvāsitaḥ sindhupatirmohāddattaśca mṛtyave ||16||

17yamadaṃṣṭrāntaraṃ prāpto mucyetāpi hi mānavaḥ
|
nārjunasya vaśaṃ prāpto mucyetājau jayadrathaḥ ||17||

18sa tathā kuru śoṇāśva yathā mucyeta saindhavaḥ
|
mama cārtapralāpānāṃ mā krudhaḥ pāhi saindhavam ||18||

19droṇa uvāca|
nābhyasūyāmi te vākyamaśvatthāmnā'si me samaḥ
|
satyaṃ tu te pravakṣyāmi tajjuṣasva viśāmpate ||19||

20sārathiḥ pravaraḥ kṛṣṇaḥ śīghrāścāsya hayottamāḥ
|
alpaṃ ca vivaraṃ kṛtvā tūrṇaṃ yāti dhanañjayaḥ ||20||

21kiṃ na paśyasi bāṇaughān krośamātre kirīṭinaḥ
|
paścādrathasya patitān kṣiptāñśīghraṃ hi gacchataḥ ||21||

22na cāhaṃ śīghrayāne'dya samartho vayasā'nvitaḥ
|
senāmukhe ca pārthānāmetadbalamupasthitam ||22||

23yudhiṣṭhiraśca me grāhyo miṣatāṃ sarvadhanvinām
|
evaṃ mayā pratijñātaṃ kṣatramadhye mahābhuja ||23||

24dhanañjayena cotsṛṣṭo vartate pramukhe nṛpa
|
tasmādvyūhamukhaṃhitvā nāhaṃ yotsyāmi phālgunam ||24||

25tulyābhijanakarmāṇaṃ śatrumekaṃ sahāyavān
|
gatvā yodhaya mā bhaistvaṃ tvaṃ hyasya jagataḥ patiḥ ||25||

26rājā śūraḥ kṛtī dakṣo netuṃ parapurañjayaḥ
|
vīraḥ svayaṃ prayāhyatra yatra pārtho dhanañjayaḥ ||26||

27duryodhana uvāca|
kathaṃ tvāmapyatikrāntaḥ sarvaśastrabhṛtāṃ varam
|
dhanañjayo mayā śakya ācārya pratibādhitum ||27||

28api śakyo raṇe jetuṃ vajrahastaḥ purandaraḥ
|
nārjunaḥ samare śakyo jetuṃ parapurañjayaḥ ||28||

29yena bhojaśca hārdikyo bhavāṃśca tridaśopamaḥ
|
astrapratāpena jitau śrutāyuśca nibarhitaḥ ||29||

30sudakṣiṇaśca nihataḥ sa ca rājā śrutāyudhaḥ
|
śrutāyuścācyutāyuśca mlecchāścāyutaśo hatāḥ ||30||

31taṃ kathaṃ pāṇḍavaṃ yuddhe dahantamiva pāvakam
|
pratiyotsyāmi durdharṣaṃ tamahaṃ śastrakovidam ||31||

32kṣamaṃ ca manyase yuddhaṃ mama tenādya saṃyuge
|
paravānasmi bhavati preṣyavadrakṣa madyaśaḥ ||32||

33droṇa uvāca|
satyaṃ vadasi kauravya durādharṣo dhanañjayaḥ
|
ahaṃ tu tatkariṣyāmi yathainaṃ prasahiṣyasi ||33||

34adbhutaṃ cādya paśyantu loke sarvadhanurdharāḥ
|
viṣaktaṃ tvayi kaunteyaṃ vāsudevasya paśyataḥ ||34||

35eṣa te kavacaṃ rājaṃstathā badhnāmi kāñcanam
|
yathā na bāṇā nāstrāṇi prahariṣyanti te raṇe ||35||

36yadi tvāṃ sāsurasurāḥ sayakṣoragarākṣasāḥ
|
yodhayanti trayo lokāḥ sanarā nāsti te bhayaṃ ||36||

37na kṛṣṇo na ca kaunteyo na cānyaḥ śastrabhṛdraṇe
|
śarānarpayituṃ kaścit kavace tava śakṣyati ||37||

38sa tvaṃ kavacamāsthāya kruddhamadya raṇe'rjunam
|
tvaramāṇaḥ svayaṃ yāhi natvā'sau visahiṣyati ||38||

39sañjaya uvāca|
evamuktvā tvarandroṇaḥ spṛṣṭvāmbho varma bhāsvaram
|
ābabandhādbhutatamaṃ japanmantraṃ yathāvidhi ||39||

40raṇe tasmin sumahati vijayasya sutasya te
|
visismāpayiṣurlokān vidyayā brahmavittamaḥ ||40||

41droṇa uvāca|
karotu svasti te brahma brahmā cāpi dvijātayaḥ
|
sarīsṛpāśca ye śreṣṭhāstebhyaste svasti bhārata ||41||

42yayātirnāhuṣaścaiva dhundhumāro bhagīrathaḥ
|
tubhyaṃ rājarṣayaḥ sarve svasti kurvantu te sadā ||42||

43svasti te'stvekapādebhyo bahupādebhya eva ca
|
svastyastvapādakebhyaśca nityaṃ tava mahāraṇe ||43||

44svāhā svadhā śacī caiva svasti kurvantu te sadā
|
lakṣmīrarundhatī caiva kurutāṃ svasti te'nagha ||44||

45asito devalaścaiva viśvāmitrastathāṅgirāḥ
|
vasiṣṭhaḥ kaśyapaścaiva svasti kurvantu te nṛpa ||45||

46dhātā vidhātā lokeśo diśaśca sadigīśvarāḥ
|
svasti te'dya prayacchantu kārtikeyaśca ṣaṇmukhaḥ ||46||

47vivasvān bhagavān svasti karotu tava sarvaśaḥ
|
diggajāścaiva catvāraḥ kṣitiśca gaganaṃ grahāḥ ||47||

48adhastāddharaṇīṃ yo'sau sadā dhārayate nṛpa
|
śeṣaśca pannagaśreṣṭhaḥ svasti tubhyaṃ prayacchatu ||48||

49gāndhāre yudhi vikramya nirjitāḥ surasattamāḥ
|
purā vṛtreṇa daityena bhinnadehāḥ sahasraśaḥ ||49||

50hṛtatejobalāḥ sarve tadā sendrā divaukasaḥ
|
brahmāṇaṃ śaraṇaṃ jagmurvṛtrādbhītā mahāsurāt ||50||

51devā ūcuḥ| pramarditānāṃ vṛtreṇa devānāṃ devasattama
|
gatirbhava suraśreṣṭha trāhi no mahato bhayāt ||51||

52atha pārśve sthitaṃ viṣṇuṃ śakrādīṃśca surottamān
|
prāha tathyamidaṃ vākyaṃ viṣaṇṇān surasattamān ||52||

53rakṣyā me satataṃ devāḥ sahendrāḥ sadvijātayaḥ
|
tvaṣṭuḥ sudurdharaṃ tejo yena vṛtro vinirmitaḥ ||53||

54tvaṣṭrā purā tapastaptvā varṣāyutaśataṃ tadā
|
vṛtro vinirmito devāḥ prāpyānujñāṃ maheśvarāt ||54||

55sa tasyaiva prasādādvo hanyādeva ripurbalī
|
nāgatvā śaṅkarasthānaṃ bhagavān dṛśyate haraḥ ||55||

56dṛṣṭvā jeṣyatha vṛtraṃ taṃ kṣipraṃ gacchata mandaram
|
yatrāste tapasāṃ yonirdakṣayajñavināśanaḥ ||56||

57pinākī sarvabhūteśo bhaganetranipātanaḥ
|
te gatvā sahitā devā brahmaṇā saha mandaram ||57||

58apaśyaṃstejasāṃ rāśiṃ sūryakoṭisamaprabham
|
so'bravīt svāgataṃ devā brūta kiṃ karavāṇyaham ||58||

59amoghaṃ darśanaṃ mahyaṃ kāmaprāptirato'stu vaḥ
|
evamuktāstu te sarve pratyūcustaṃ divaukasaḥ ||59||

60tejo hṛtaṃ no vṛtreṇa gatirbhava divaukasām
|
mūrtīrīkṣasva no deva prahārairjarjarīkṛtāḥ
|
śaraṇaṃ tvāṃ prapannāḥ sma gatirbhava maheśvara ||60||

61śarva uvāca|
viditaṃ vo yathā devāḥ kṛtyeyaṃ sumahābalā
|
tvaṣṭustejobhavā ghorā durnivāryā'kṛtātmabhiḥ ||61||

62avaśyaṃ tu mayā kāryaṃ sāhyaṃ sarvadivaukasām
|
mamedaṃ gātrajaṃ śakra kavacaṃ gṛhya bhāsvaram ||62||

63badhānānena mantreṇa mānasena sureśvara
|
vadhāyāsuramukhyasya vṛtrasya suraghātinaḥ ||63||

64droṇa uvāca|
ityuktvā varadaḥ prādādvarma tanmantrameva ca
|
sa tena varmaṇā guptaḥ prāyādvṛtracamūṃ prati ||64||

65nānāvidhaiśca śastraughaiḥ pātyamānairmahāraṇe
|
na sandhiḥ śakyate bhettuṃ varmabandhasya tasya tu ||65||

66tato jaghāna samare vṛtraṃ devapatiḥ svayam
|
taṃ ca mantramayaṃ bandhaṃ varma cāṅgirase dadau ||66||

67aṅgirāḥ prāha putrasya mantrajñasya bṛhaspateḥ
|
bṛhaspatirathovāca agniveśyāya dhīmate ||67||

68agniveśyo mama prādāttena badhnāmi varma te
|
tavādya deharakṣārthaṃ mantreṇa nṛpasattama ||68||

69sañjaya uvāca|
evamuktvā tato droṇastava putraṃ mahādyutim
|
punareva vacaḥ prāha śanairācāryapuṅgavaḥ ||69||

70brahmasūtreṇa badhnāmi kavacaṃ tava bhārata
|
hiraṇyagarbheṇa yathā baddhaṃ viṣṇoḥ purā raṇe ||70||

71yathā ca brahmaṇā baddhaṃ saṅgrāme tārakāmaye
|
śakrasya kavacaṃ divyaṃ tathā badhnāmyahaṃ tava ||71||

72baddhvā tu kavacaṃ tasya mantreṇa vidhipūrvakam
|
preṣayāmāsa rājānaṃ yuddhāya mahate dvijaḥ ||72||

73sa sannaddho mahābāhurācāryeṇa mahātmanā
|
rathānāṃ ca sahasreṇa trigartānāṃ prahāriṇām ||73||

74tathā dantisahastreṇa mattānāṃ vīryaśālinām
|
aśvānāṃ niyutenaiva tathā'nyaiśca mahārathaiḥ ||74||

75vṛtaḥ prāyānmahābāhurarjunasya rathaṃ prati
|
nānāvāditraghoṣeṇa yathā vairocanistathā ||75||

76tataḥ śabdo mahānāsīt sainyānāṃ tava bhārata
|
agādhaṃ prasthitaṃ dṛṣṭvā samudramiva kauravam ||76||
