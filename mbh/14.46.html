Mahābhārata 14.46 (Āśvamedhikaparvan)

1brahmovāca|
evametena mārgeṇa pūrvoktena yathāvidhi
|
adhītavān yathāśakti tathaiva brahmacaryavān||1||

2svadharmanirato vidvān sarvendriyayato muniḥ
|
guroḥ priyahite yuktaḥ satyadharmaparaḥ śuciḥ||2||

3guruṇā samanujñāto bhuñjītānnamakutsayan
|
haviṣyabhaikṣyabhuk cāpi sthānāsanavihāravān||3||

4dvikālamagniṃ juhvānaḥ śucirbhūtvā samāhitaḥ
|
dhārayīta sadā daṇḍaṃ bailvaṃ pālāśameva vā||4||

5kṣaumaṃ kārpāsikaṃ cāpi mṛgājinamathāpi vā
|
sarvaṃ kāṣāyaraktaṃ vā vāso vāpi dvijasya ha||5||

6mekhalā ca bhavenmauñjī jaṭī nityodakastathā
|
yajñopavītī svādhyāyī alubdho niyatavrataḥ||6||

7pūtābhiśca tathaivādbhiḥ sadā daivatatarpaṇam
|
bhāvena niyataḥ kurvan brahmacārī praśasyate||7||

8evaṃ yukto jayellokān vānaprastho jitendriyaḥ
|
na saṃsarati jātīṣu paramaṃ sthānamāśritaḥ||8||

9saṃskṛta: sarvasaṃskāraistathaiva brahmacaryavān
|
grāmānniṣkramya cāraṇye muniḥ pravrajito vaset||9||

10carmavalkalasaṃvāsī sāyaṃ prātarupaspṛśet
|
araṇyagocaro nityaṃ na grāmaṃ praviśet punaḥ||10||

11arcayannatithīnkāle dadyāccāpi pratiśrayam
|
phalapatrāvarairmūlaiḥ śyāmākena ca vartayan||11||

12pravṛttamudakaṃ vāyuṃ sarvaṃ vāneyamāśrayet
|
prāśnīyādānupūrveṇa yathādīkṣamatandritaḥ||12||

13samūlaphalabhikṣābhirarcedatithimāgatam
|
yadbhakṣyaṃ syāttato dadyādbhikṣāṃ nityamatandritaḥ||13||

14devatātithipūrvaṃ ca sadā prāśnīta vāgyataḥ
|
aspardhitamanāścaiva laghvāśī devatāśrayaḥ||14||

15dānto maitraḥ kṣamāyuktaḥ keśānśmaśru ca dhārayan
|
juhvansvādhyāyaśīlaśca satyadharmaparāyaṇaḥ||15||

16śucidehaḥ sadā dakṣo vananityaḥ samāhitaḥ
|
evaṃ yukto jayetsvargaṃ vānaprastho jitendriyaḥ||16||

17gṛhastho brahmacārī ca vānaprastho'tha vā punaḥ
|
ca icchenmokṣamāsthātumuttamāṃ vṛttimāśrayet||17||

18abhayaṃ sarvabhūtebhyo dattvā naiṣkarmyamācaret
|
sarvabhūtasukho maitraḥ sarvendriyayato muniḥ||18||

19ayācitamasaṅklṛptamupapannaṃ yadṛcchayā
|
kṛtvā prāhne caredbhaikṣyaṃ vidhūme bhuktavajjane||19||

20vṛtte śarāvasampāte bhakṣyaṃ lipseta mokṣavit
|
lābhena ca na hṛṣyeta nālābhe vimanā bhavet
|
na cātibhikṣāṃ bhikṣeta kevalaṃ prāṇayātrikaḥ||20||

21yātrārthī kālapākāṅkṣaścaredbhaikṣyaṃ samāhitaḥ
|
lābhaṃ sādhāraṇaṃ necchenna bhuñjītābhipūjitaḥ||21||

22abhipūjitalābhāddhi vijugupseta bhikṣukaḥ
|
bhuktānyannāni tiktāni kaṣāyakaṭukāni ca||22||

23nāsvādayīta bhuñjāno rasāṃśca madhurāṃstathā
|
yātrāmātraṃ ca bhuñjīta kevalaṃ prāṇadhāraṇam||23||

24asaṃrodhena bhūtānāṃ vṛttiṃ lipseta mokṣavit
|
na cānyamannaṃ lipseta bhikṣamāṇaḥ kathañcana||24||

25na sannikāśayeddharmaṃ vivikte cārajāścaret
|
śūnyāgāramaraṇyaṃ vā vṛkṣamūlaṃ nadī tathā||25||

26pratiśrayārthaṃ seveta pārvatīṃ vā punarguhām
|
grāmaikarātriko grīṣme varṣāsvekatra vā vaset||26||

27adhvā sūryeṇa nirdiṣṭaḥ kīṭavacca carenmahīm
|
dayārthaṃ caiva bhūtānāṃ samīkṣya pṛthivīṃ caret||27||

28sañcayāṃśca na kurvīta snehavāsaṃ ca varjayet
|
pūtābhiradbhirnityaṃ vai kāryaṃ kurvīta mokṣavit||28||

29upaspṛśedudbhūtābhiraddhiśca puruṣaḥ sadā
|
ahiṃsā brahmacaryaṃ ca satyamārjavameva ca||29||

30akrodhaścānasūyā ca damo nityamapaiśunam
|
aṣṭasveteṣu yuktaḥ syādvrateṣu niyatendriyaḥ||30||

31apāpamaśaṭhaṃ vṛttamajihyaṃ nityamācaret
|
joṣayeta sadā bhojyaṃ grāsamāgatamaspṛhaḥ||31||

32yātrāmātraṃ ca bhuñjīta kevalaṃ prāṇayātrikam
|
dharmalabdhamathāśnīyānna kāmamanuvartayet||32||

33grāsādācchādanādanyanna gṛhṇīyātkathañcana
|
yāvadāhārayettāvatpratigṛhṇīta nādhikam||33||

34parebhyo na pratigrāhyaṃ na ca deyaṃ kadācana
|
dainyabhāvācca bhūtānāṃ saṃvibhajya sadā budhaḥ||34||

35nādadīta parasvāni na gṛhṇīyādayācitaḥ
|
na kiñcidviviṣayaṃ bhuktvā spṛhayettasya vai punaḥ||35||

36mṛdamāpastathānnāni patrapuṣpaphalāni ca
|
asaṃvṛtāni gṛhṇīyātpravṛttāni ca kāryavān||36||

37na śilpajīvikāṃ jīveddhiraṇyaṃ nota kāmayet
|
na dveṣṭā nopadeṣṭā ca bhavecca nirupaskṛtaḥ||37||

38śraddhāpūtāni bhuñjīta nimittāni ca varjayet
|
sudhāvṛttirasaktaśca sarvabhūtairasaṃvidam||38||

39āśīryuktāni sarvāṇi hiṃsāyuktāni yāni ca
|
lokasaṅgrahadharmaṃ ca naiva kuryānna kārayet||39||

40sarvabhāvānatikramya laghumātraḥ parivrajet
|
samaḥ sarveṣu bhūteṣu sthāvareṣu careṣu ca||40||

41paraṃ nodvejayetkācinna ca kasyacidudvijet
|
viśvāsyaḥ sarvabhūtānāmagryo mokṣaviducyate||41||

42anāgataṃ ca na dhyāyennātītamanucintayet
|
vartamānamupekṣeta kālākākṣī samāhitaḥ||42||

43na cakṣuṣā na manasā na vācā dūṣayetkvacit
|
na pratyakṣaṃ parokṣaṃ vā kiñcidduṣṭaṃ samācaret||43||

44indriyāṇyupasaṃhṛtya kūrmo'ṅgānīva sarvaśaḥ
|
kṣīṇendriyamanobuddhirnirīhaḥ sarvatattvavit||44||

45nirdvandvo nirnamaskāro niḥsvāhākāra eva ca
|
nirmamo nirahaṅkāro niryogakṣema ātmavān||45||

46nirāśīrnirguṇaḥ śānto nirāsakto nirāśrayaḥ
|
ātmasaṅgī ca tattvajño mucyate nātra saṃśayaḥ||46||

47apādapāṇipṛṣṭhaṃ tadaśiraskamanūdaram
|
prahīṇaguṇakarmāṇaṃ kevalaṃ vimalaṃ sthiram||47||

48agandhamarasasparśamarūpāśabdameva ca
|
anugamyamanāsaktamamāṃsamapi caiva yat||48||

49niścintamavyayaṃ divyaṃ gṛhasthamapi sarvadā
|
sarvabhūtasthamātmānaṃ ye paśyanti na te mṛtāḥ||49||

50na tatra kramate buddhirnendriyāṇi na devatāḥ
|
vedā yajñāśca lokāśca na tapo na vratāni ca||50||

51yatra jñānavatāṃ prāptiraliṅgagrahaṇā smṛtā
|
tasmādaliṅgadharmajño dharmatattvamupācaret||51||

52gūḍhadharmāśrito vidvānvijñānacaritaṃ caret
|
amūḍho mūḍharūpeṇa careddharmamadūṣayan||52||

53yathainamavamanyeranpare satatameva hi
|
yathāvṛttaścarecchāntaḥ satāṃ dharmānakutsayan||53||

54ya evaṃ vṛttasampannaḥ sa muniḥ śreṣṭha ucyate
|
indriyāṇīndriyārthāṃśca mahābhūtāni pañca ca||54||

55mano buddhirahaṅkāramavyaktaṃ puruṣaṃ tathā
|
etatsarvaṃ prasaṅkhyāya yathāvattattvaniścayāt||55||

56tataḥ svargamavāpnoti vimuktaḥ sarvabandhanaiḥ
|
etāvadantavelāyāṃ parisaṅkhyāya tattvavit||56||

57dhyāyedekāntamāsthāya mucyate'tha nirāśrayaḥ
|
nirmuktaḥ sarvasaṅgebhyo vāyurākāśago yathā||57||

58kṣīṇakeśo nirātaṅgastathedaṃ prāpnuyātparam||58||
