Mahābhārata 12.238 (Śāntiparvan)

1vyāsa uvāca |
eṣā pūrvatarā vṛttiāhmaṇasya vidhīyate
|
jñānavāneva karmāṇi kurvan sarvatra siddhyati ||1||

2tatra cenna bhavedevaṃ saṃśayaḥ karmasiddhaye
|
ki tu karma svabhāvo'yaṃ jñānaṃ karmeti vā punaḥ ||2||

3tatra vedavidhiḥ sa syājjñānaṃ cetpuruṣaṃ prati
|
upapattyupalabdhibhyāṃ varṇayiṣyāmi tacchṛṇu ||3||

4pauruṣaṃ kāraṇaṃ kecidāhuḥ karmasu mānavāḥ
|
daivameke praśaṃsanti svabhāvamapare janāḥ ||4||

5pauruṣaṃ karma daivaṃ ca kālavṛttisvabhāvataḥ
|
trayametatpṛthagbhūtamavivekaṃ tu kecana ||5||

6etadevaṃ ca naivaṃ ca na cobhe nānubhe tathā
|
karmasthā viṣayaṃ brūyuḥ sattvasthāḥ samadarśinaḥ ||6||

7tretāyāṃ dvāpare caiva kalijāśca sasaṃśayāḥ
|
tapasvinaḥ praśāntāśca sattvasthāśca kṛte yuge ||7||

8apṛthagdarśanāḥ sarve ṛksāmasu yajuḥṣu ca
|
kāmadveṣau pṛthak kṛtvā tapaḥ kṛta upāsate ||8||

9tapodharmeṇa saṃyuktastaponityaḥ susaṃśitaḥ
|
tena sarvānavāpnoti kāmānyānmanasecchati ||9||

10tapasā tadavāpnoti yadbhūtvā sṛjate jagat
|
tadbhūtaśca tataḥ sarvabhūtānāṃ bhavati prabhuḥ ||10||

11taduktaṃ vedavādeṣu gahanaṃ vedadarśibhiḥ
|
vedānteṣu punarvyaktaṃ karmayogena lakṣyate ||11||

12ālambhayajñāḥ kṣatrāśca haviryajñā viśaḥ smṛtāḥ
|
paricārayajñāḥ śūdrāśca japayajñā dvijātayaḥ ||12||

13pariniṣṭhitakāryo hi svādhyāyena dvijo bhavet
|
kuryādanyanna vā kuryānmaitro brāhmaṇa ucyate ||13||

14tretādau kevalā vedā yajñā varṇāśramāstathā
|
saṃrodhādāyuṣastvete vyasyante dvāpare yuge ||14||

15dvāpare viplavaṃ yānti vedāḥ kaliyuge tathā
|
dṛśyante nāpi dṛśyante kalerante punaḥ kila ||15||

16utsīdanti svadharmāśca tatrādharmeṇa pīḍitāḥ
|
gavāṃ bhūmeśca ye cāpāmoṣadhīnāṃ ca ye rasāḥ ||16||

17adharmāntarhitā vedā vedadharmāstathā''śramāḥ
|
vikriyante svadharmasthāḥ sthāvarāṇi carāṇi ca ||17||

18yathā sarvāṇi bhūtāni vṛṣṭirbhaumāni varṣati
|
sṛjate sarvato'ṅgāni tathā vedā yuge yuge ||18||

19niścitaṃ kālanānātvamanādinidhanaṃ ca yat
|
kīrtitaṃ yatpurastānme sūte yaccātti ca prajāḥ ||19||

20yaccedaṃ prabhavaḥ sthānaṃ bhūtānāṃ saṃyamo yamaḥ
|
svabhāvenaiva vartante dvandvasṛṣṭāni bhūriśaḥ ||20||

21sargaḥ kālo dhṛtirvedāḥ kartā kāryaṃ kriyāphalam
|
etatte kathitaṃ tāta yanmāṃ tvaṃ paripṛcchasi ||21||
