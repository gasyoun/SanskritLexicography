Mahābhārata 12.131 (Śāntiparvan)

1yudhiṣṭhira uvāca|
kṣīṇasya dīrghasūtrasya sānukrośasya bandhuṣu
|
pariśaṅkitavṛttasya śrutamantrasya bhārata ||1||

2vibhaktapurarāṣṭrasya nirdravyanicayasya ca
|
asambhāvitamitrasya bhinnāmātyasya sarvaśaḥ ||2||

3paracakrābhijātasya durbalasya balīyasā
|
āpannacetaso brūhi kiṃ kāryamavaśiṣyate ||3||

4bhīṣma uvāca|
bāhyaścedvijigīṣuḥ syāddharmārthakuśalaḥ śuciḥ
|
javena sandhiṃ kurvīta purvabhuktānvimocayet ||4||

5yo'dharmavijigīṣuḥ syādbalavānpāpaniścayaḥ
|
ātmanaḥ sannirodhena sandhiṃ tenāpi rocayet ||5||

6apāsya rājadhānīṃ vā tareddravyeṇa cāpadam
|
tadbhāvayukto dravyāṇi jīvanpunarupārjayet ||6||

7yāstu kośabalatyāgācchakyāstaritumāpadaḥ
|
kastatrādhikamātmānaṃ santyajedarthadharmavit ||7||

8avarodhān jugupseta kā sapatnadhane dayā
|
na tvevātmā pradātavyaḥ śakye sati kathaṃcana ||8||

9yudhiṣṭhira uvāca|
ābhyantare prakupite bāhye copanipīḍite
|
kṣīṇe kośe śrute mantre kiṃ kāryamavaśiṣyate ||9||

10bhīṣma uvāca|
kṣipraṃ vā sandhikāmaḥ syāt kṣipraṃ vā tīkṣṇavikramaḥ
|
tadāpanayanaṃ kṣiprametāvatsāmparāyikam ||10||

11anuraktena ceṣṭena hṛṣṭena jagatīpatiḥ
|
alpenāpi hi sainyena mahīṃ jayati bhūmipaḥ ||11||

12hato vā divamāroheddhatvā vā kṣitimāvaset
|
yuddhe hi santyajanprāṇān śakrasyaiti salokatām ||12||

13sarvalokāgamaṃ kṛtvā mṛdutvaṃ gantumeva ca
|
viśvāsādvinayaṃ kuryādviśvaseccāpyupāyataḥ ||13||

14apacikramiṣuḥ kṣipraṃ sāmnā vā parisāntvayan
|
vilaṃghayitvā mantreṇa tataḥ svayamupakramet ||14||
