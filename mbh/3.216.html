Mahābhārata 3.216 (Āraṇyakaparvan)

1vyādha uvāca|
evaṃ śapto'hamṛṣiṇā tadā dvijavarottama
|
abhiprasādayamṛṣiṃ girā trāhīti māṃ tadā||1||

2ajānatā mayā'kāryamidamadya kṛtaṃ mune
|
kṣantumarhasi tat sarvaṃ prasīda bhagavanniti||2||

3ṛṣiruvāca| nānyathā bhavitā śāpa evametadasaṃśayam
|
ānṛśaṃsyāt tvahaṃ kiñcit kartā'nugrahamadya te||3||

4śūdrayonyāṃ vartamāno dharmajño hi bhaviṣyasi
|
mātāpitrośca śuśrūṣāṃ kariṣyasi na saṃśayaḥ||4||

5tayā śuśrūṣayā siddhiṃ mahattvaṃ samavāpsyasi
|
jātismaraśca bhavitā svargaṃ caiva gamiṣyasi||5||

6śāpakṣaye tu nirvṛtte bhavitā'si punardvijaḥ
|
evaṃ śaptaḥ purā tena ṛṣiṇā'smyugratejasā||6||

7prasādaśca kṛtastena mamaiva dvipadāṃ vara
|
śaraṃ coddhṛtavānasmi tasya vai dvijasattama||7||

8āśramaṃ ca mayā nīto na ca prāṇairvyayujyata
|
etat te sarvamākhyātaṃ yathā mama purā'bhavat||8||

9abhitaścāpi gantavyaṃ mayā svargaṃ dvijottama||9||

10brāhmaṇa uvāca|
evametāni puruṣā duḥkhāni ca sukhāni ca
|
āpnuvanti mahābuddhe notkaṇṭhāṃ kartumarhasi||10||

11duṣkaraṃ hi kṛtaṃ karma jānatā jātimātmanaḥ
|
lokavṛttāntatattvajña nityaṃ dharmaparāyaṇa||11||

12karmadoṣaśca vai vidvannātmajātikṛtena vai
|
kañcit kālamuṣyatāṃ vai tato'si bhavitā dvijaḥ||12||

13sāmprataṃ ca mato me'si brāhmaṇo nātra saṃśayaḥ
|
brāhmaṇaḥ patanīyeṣu vartamāno vikarmasu||13||

14dāmbhiko duṣkṛtaḥ prājñaḥ śūdreṇa sadṛśo bhavet
|
yastu śūdro dame satye dharme ca satatotthitaḥ||14||

15taṃ brāhmaṇamahaṃ manye vṛttena hi bhaveddvijaḥ
|
karmadoṣeṇa viṣamāṃ gatimāpnoti dāruṇām||15||

16kṣīṇadoṣamahaṃ manye cābhitastvāṃ narottama
|
kartumarhasi notkaṇṭhāṃ tvadvidhā hyaviṣādinaḥ
|
lokavṛttānuvṛttajñā nityaṃ dharmaparāyaṇāḥ||16||

17vyādha uvāca|
prajñayā mānasaṃ duḥkhaṃ hanyācchārīramauṣadhaiḥ
|
etadvijñānasāmarthyaṃ na bālaiḥ samatāmiyāt||17||

18aniṣṭasamprayogācca viprayogāt priyasya ca
|
manuṣyā mānasairduḥkhairyujyante cālpabuddhayaḥ||18||

19guṇairbhūtāni yujyante viyujyante tathaiva ca
|
sarvāṇi naitadekasya śokasthānaṃ hi vidyate||19||

20aniṣṭaṃ cānvitaṃ paśyaṃstathā kṣipraṃ virajyate
|
tataśca pratikurvanti yadi paśyantyupakramāt||20||

21śocato na bhavet kiñcit kevalaṃ paritapyate
|
parityajanti ye duḥkhaṃ sukhaṃ vā'pyubhayaṃ narāḥ||21||

22ta eva sukhamedhante jñānatṛptā manīṣiṇaḥ
|
asantoṣaparā mūḍhāḥ santoṣaṃ yānti paṇḍitāḥ||22||

23asantoṣasya nāstyantastuṣṭistu paramaṃ sukham
|
na śocanti gatādhvānaḥ paśyantaḥ paramāṃ gatim||23||

24na viṣāde manaḥ kāryaṃ viṣādo viṣamuttamam
|
mārayatyakṛtaprajñaṃ bālaṃ kruddha ivoragaḥ||24||

25yaṃ viṣādo'bhibhavati vikrame samupasthite
|
tejasā tasya hīnasya puruṣārtho na vidyate||25||

26avaśyaṃ kriyamāṇasya karmaṇo dṛśyate phalam
|
na hi nirvedamāgamya kiñcit prāpnoti śobhanam||26||

27athāpyupāyaṃ paśyeta duḥkhasya parimokṣaṇe
|
aśocannārabhetaivaṃ muktaścāvyasanī bhavet||27||

28bhūteṣvabhāvaṃ sañcintya ye tu buddheḥ paraṃ gatāḥ
|
na śocanti kṛtaprajñāḥ paśyantaḥ paramāṃ gatim||28||

29na śocāmi ca vai vidvan kālākāṅkṣī sthito hyaham
|
etairnidarśanairbrahmannavasīdāmi sattama||29||

30brāhmaṇa uvāca|
kṛtaprajño'si medhāvī buddhirhi vipulā tava
|
nāhaṃ bhavantaṃ śocāmi jñānatṛpto'si dharmavit||30||

31āpṛcche tvāṃ svasti te'stu dharmastvāṃ parirakṣatu
|
apramādastu kartavyo dharme dharmabhṛtāṃ vara||31||

32mārkaṇḍeya uvāca|
bāḍhamityeva taṃ vyādhaḥ kṛtāñjaliruvāca ha
|
pradakṣiṇamatho kṛtvā prasthito dvijasattamaḥ||32||

33sa tu gatvā dvijaḥ sarvāṃ śuśrūṣāṃ kṛtavāṃstadā
|
mātāpitṛbhyāṃ vṛddhābhyāṃ yathānyāyaṃ susaṃśitaḥ||33||

34etat te sarvamākhyātaṃ nikhilena yudhiṣṭhira
|
pṛṣṭavānāsi yaṃ tāta dharmaṃ dharmabhṛtāṃ vara||34||

35pativratāyā māhātmyaṃ brāhāṇasya ca sattama
|
mātāpitrośca śuśrūṣā dharmavyādhena kīrtitā||35||

36yudhiṣṭhira uvāca|
atyadbhutamidaṃ brahman dharmākhyānamanuttamam
|
sarvadharmavidāṃ śreṣṭha kathitaṃ munisattama||36||

37sukhaśravyatayā vidvan muhūrta iva me gataḥ
|
na hi tṛpto'smi bhagavan śṛṇvāno dharmamuttamam||37||
