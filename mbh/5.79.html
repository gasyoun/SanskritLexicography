Mahābhārata 5.79 (Udyogaparvan)

1śrībhagavānuvāca
|
evametanmahābāho yathā vadasi pāṇḍava
|
pāṇḍavānāṃ kurūṇāṃ ca pratipatsye nirāmayam ||1||

2sarvaṃ tvidaṃ mamāyattaṃ bībhatso karmaṇordvayoḥ
|
kṣetraṃ hi rasavacchuddhaṃ karmaṇaivopapāditam ||2||

3ṛte varṣānna kaunteya jātu nirvartayet phalam
|
tatra vai pauruṣaṃ brūyurāsekaṃ yatra kāritam ||3||

4tatra cāpi dhruvaṃ paśyecchoṣaṇaṃ daivakāritam
|
tadidaṃ niścitaṃ buddhyā pūrvairapi mahātmabhiḥ ||4||

5daive ca mānuṣe caiva saṃyuktaṃ lokakāraṇam
|
ahaṃ hi tatkariṣyāmi paraṃ puruṣakārataḥ ||5||

6daivaṃ tu na mayā śakyaṃ karma kartuṃ kathañcana
|
sa hi dharmaṃ ca lokaṃ ca tyaktvā carati durmatiḥ||6||

7na hi santapyate tena tathārūpeṇa karmaṇā
|
tathā'pi buddhiṃ pāpiṣṭhāṃ vardhayantyasya mantriṇaḥ ||7||

8śakuniḥ sūtaputraśca bhrātā duḥśāsanastathā
|
sa hi tyāgena rājyasya na śamaṃ samupaiṣyati ||8||

9antareṇa vadhaṃ pārtha sānubandhaḥ suyodhanaḥ
|
na cāpi praṇipātena tyaktumicchati dharmarāṭ
|
yācyamānaśca rājyaṃ sa na pradāsyati durmatiḥ ||9||

10na tu manye sa tadvācyo yadyudhiṣṭhiraśāsanam
|
uktaṃ prayojanaṃ yattu dharmarājena bhārata ||10||

11tathā pāpastu tatsarvaṃ na kariṣyati kauravaḥ
|
tasmiṃścākriyamāṇe'sau loke vadhyo bhaviṣyati ||11||

12mama cāpi sa vadhyo hi jagataścāpi bhārata
|
yena kaumārake yūyaṃ sarve viprakṛtāḥ sadā ||12||

13vipraluptaṃ ca vo rājyaṃ nṛśaṃsena durātmanā
|
na copaśāmyate pāpaḥ śriyaṃ dṛṣṭvā yudhiṣṭhire ||13||

14asakṛccāpyahaṃ tena tvatkṛte pārtha bheditaḥ
|
na mayā tadgṛhītaṃ ca pāpaṃ tasya cikīrṣitam ||14||

15jānāsi hi mahābāho tvamapyasya paraṃ matam
|
priyaṃ cikīrṣamāṇaṃ ca dharmarājasya māmapi ||15||

16sañjānaṃstasya cātmānaṃ mama caiva paraṃ matam
|
ajānanniva māṃ kasmādarjunādyābhiśaṅkase ||16||

17yaccāpi paramaṃ divyaṃ taccāpyanugataṃ tvayā
|
vidhānaṃ vihitaṃ pārtha kathaṃ śarma bhavet paraiḥ ||17||

18yattu vācā mayā śakyaṃ karmaṇā vā'pi pāṇḍava
|
kariṣye tadahaṃ pārtha na tvāśaṃse śamaṃ paraiḥ ||18||

19kathaṃ goharaṇe hyukto naitaccharma tathā hitam
|
yācyamāno hi bhīṣmeṇa saṃvatsaragate'dhvani ||19||

20tadaiva te parābhūtā yadā saṃkalpitāstvayā
|
lavaśaḥ kṣaṇaśaścāpi na ca tuṣṭaḥ suyodhanaḥ ||20||

21sarvathā tu mayā kāryaṃ dharmarājasya śāsanam
|
vibhāvyaṃ tasya bhūyaśca karma pāpaṃ durātmanaḥ ||21||
