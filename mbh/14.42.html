Mahābhārata 14.42 (Āśvamedhikaparvan)

1brahmovāca|
ahaṅkārātprasūtāni mahābhūtāni pañca vai
|
pṛthivī vāyurākāśamāpo jyotiśca pañcamam||1||

2teṣu bhūtāni muhyanti mahābhūteṣu pañcasu
|
śabdasparśanarūpeṣu rasagandhakriyāsu ca||2||

3mahābhūtavināśānte pralaye pratyupasthite
|
sarvaprāṇabhṛtāṃ dhīrā mahadabhyudyate bhayam||3||

4yadyasmājjāyate bhūtaṃ tatra tatpravilīyate
|
līyante pratilomāni jāyante cottarottaram||4||

5tataḥ pralīne sarvasmin bhūte sthāvarajaṅgame
|
smṛtimantastadā dhīrā na līyante kadācana||5||

6śabdaḥ sparśastathā rūpaṃ raso gandhaśca pañcamaḥ
|
kriyāḥ karaṇanityāḥ syuranityā mohasaṃjñitāḥ||6||

7lobhaprajanasambhūtā nirviśeṣā hyakiñcanāḥ
|
māṃsaśoṇitasaṅghātā anyonyaspopajīvinaḥ||7||

8bahirātmāna ityete dīnāḥ kṛpaṇajīvanaḥ
|
prāṇāpānāvudānaśca samāno vyāna eva ca||8||

9antarātmani cāpyete niyatāḥ pañca vāyavaḥ
|
vāḍmanobuddhibhiḥ sārddhamidamaṣṭātmakaṃ jagat||9||

10tvakghrāṇaśrotracakṣūṃṣi rasanā vākca saṃyatāḥ
|
viśuddhaṃ ca mano yasya buddhiścāvyabhicāriṇī||10||

11aṣṭau yasyāgnayo hyete na dahante manaḥ sadā
|
sa tadbrahma śubhaṃ yāti tasmād bhūyo na vidyate||11||

12ekādaśa ca yānyāhurindriyāṇi viśeṣataḥ
|
ahaṅkārātprasūtāni tāni vakṣyāmyahaṃ dvijāḥ||12||

13śrotraṃ tvakcakṣuṣī jihvā nāsikā caiva pañcamī
|
pādau pāyurupasthaśca hastau vāgdaśamī bhavet||13||

14indriyagrāma ityeṣa mana ekādaśaṃ bhavet
|
etaṃ grāmaṃ jayetpūrvaṃ tato brahma prakāśate||14||

15buddhīndriyāṇi pañcāhuḥ pañcakarmendriyāṇi ca
|
śrotrādīnyapi pañcāhurbuddhiyuktāni tattvataḥ||15||

16aviśeṣāṇi cānyāni karmayuktāni yāni tu
|
ubhayatra mano jñeyaṃ buddhistu dvādaśī bhavet||16||

17ityuktānīndriyāṇyetānyekādaśa yathākramam
|
manyante kṛtamityevaṃ viditvā tāni paṇḍitāḥ||17||

18ataḥ paraṃ pravakṣyāmi sarvaṃ vividhamindriyam
|
ākāśaṃ prathamaṃ bhūtaṃ śrotramadhyātmamucyate||18||

19adhibhūtaṃ tathā śabdo diśastatrādhidaivatam
|
dvitīyaṃ māruto bhūtaṃ tvagadhyātmaṃ ca viśrutā||19||

20spaṣṭavyamadhibhūtaṃ ca vidyuttatrādhidaivatam
|
tṛtīyaṃ jyotirityāhuścakṣuradhyātmamucyate||20||

21adhibhūtaṃ tato rūpaṃ sūryastatrādhidaivatam
|
caturthamāpo vijñeyaṃ jihvā cādhyātmamucyate||21||

22adhibhūtaṃ rasaścātra somastatrādhidaivatam
|
pṛthivī pañcamaṃ bhūtaṃ ghrāṇaścādhyātmamucyate||22||

23adhibhūtaṃ tathā gandho vāyustatrādhidaivatam
|
eṣu pañcasu bhūteṣu triṣu yaśca vidhiḥ smṛtaḥ||23||

24ataḥ paraṃ pravakṣyāmi sarvaṃ vividhamindriyam
|
pādāvadhyātmamityāhurbrāhmaṇāstattvadarśinaḥ||24||

25adhibhūtaṃ tu gantavyaṃ viṣṇustatrādhidaivatam
|
avāggatirapānaśca pāyuradhyātmamucyate||25||

26adhibhūtaṃ visargaśca mitrastatrādhidaivatam
|
prajanaḥ sarvabhūtānāmupastho'dhyātmamucyate||26||

27adhibhūtaṃ tathā śukraṃ daivataṃ ca prajāpatiḥ
|
hastāvadhyātmamityāhuradhyātmaviduṣo janāḥ||27||

28adhibhūtaṃ ca karmāṇi śakrastatrādhidaivatam
|
vaiśvadevī tataḥ pūrvā vāgadhyātmamihocyate||28||

29vaktavyamadhibhūtaṃ ca vahnistatrādhidaivatam
|
adhyātmaṃ mana ityāhuḥ pañcabhūtātmacārakam||29||

30adhibhūtaṃ ca saṅkalpaścandramāścādhidaivatam
|
ahaṅkārastathādhyātmaṃ sarvasaṃsārakārakam||30||

31abhimāno'dhibhūtaṃ ca rudrastatrādhidaivatam
|
adhyātmaṃ buddhirityāhuḥ ṣaḍindriyavicāriṇī||31||

32adhibhūtaṃ tu mantavyaṃ brahmā tatrādhidaivatam
|
trīṇi sthānāni bhūtānāṃ caturthaṃ nopapadyate||32||

33sthalamāstathā''kāśaṃ janma cāpi caturvidham
|
aṇḍajodbhijjasaṃsvedajarāyujamathāpi ca||33||

34caturdhā janma ityetadbhūtagrāmasya lakṣyate
|
aparāṇyatha bhūtāni khecarāṇi tathaiva ca||34||

35aṇḍajāni vijānīyātsarvāścaiva sarīsṛpān
|
svedajāḥ kṛmayaḥ proktā jantavaśca yathākramam||35||

36janma dvitīyamityetajjaghanyataramucyate
|
bhittvā tu pṛthivīṃ yāni jāyante kālaparyayāt||36||

37udbhijjāni ca tānyāhurbhūtāni dvijasattamāḥ
|
dvipādabahupādāni tiryaggatimatīni ca||37||

38jarāyujāni bhūtāni vikṛtānyapi sattamāḥ
|
dvividhā khalu vijñeyā brahmayoniḥ sanātanī||38||

39tapaḥ karma ca yatpuṇyamityeṣa viduṣāṃ nayaḥ
|
vividhaṃ karma vijñeyamijyā dānaṃ ca tanmakhe||39||

40jātasyādhyayanaṃ puṇyamiti vṛddhānuśāsanam
|
etadyo vetti vidhivadyuktaḥ sa syāddvijarṣabhāḥ||40||

41vimuktaḥ sarvapāpebhya iti caiva nibodhata
|
ākāśaṃ prathamaṃ bhūtaṃ śrotramadhyātmamucyate||41||

42adhibhūtaṃ tathā śabdo diśaścātrādhidaivatam
|
dvitīyaṃ mārutaṃ bhūtaṃ tvagadhyātmaṃ ca viśrutam||42||

43spraṣṭavyamadhibhūtaṃ tu vidyuttatrādhidaivatam
|
tṛtīyaṃ jyotirityāhuścakṣuradhyātmamiṣyate||43||

44adhibhūtaṃ tato rūpaṃ sūryastatrādhidaivatam
|
caturthamāpo vijñeyaṃ jihvā cādhyātmamiṣyate||44||

45candro'dhibhūtaṃ vijñeyamāpastatrādhidaivatam
|
yathāvadadhyātmavidhireṣa vaḥ kīrtito mayā||45||

46jñānamasya hi dharmajñāḥ prāptaṃ jñānavatāmiha
|
indriyāṇīndriyārthāśca mahābhūtāni pañca ca
|
sarvāṇyetāni sandhāya manasā sampradhārayet||46||

47kṣīṇe manasi sarvasmin na janmasukhamiṣyate
|
jñānasampannasattvānāṃ tatsukhaṃ viduṣāṃ matam||47||

48ataḥ paraṃ pravakṣyāmi sūkṣmabhāvakarīṃ śivām
|
nivṛttiṃ sarvabhūteṣu mṛdunā dāruṇena ca||48||

49guṇāguṇamanāsaṅgamekacaryamanantaram
|
etadbrahmamayaṃ vṛttamāhurekapadaṃ sukham||49||

50vidvānkūrma ivāṅgāni kāmānsaṃhatya sarvaśaḥ
|
virajāḥ sarvato mukto yo naraḥ sa sukhī sadā||50||

51kāmānātmani saṃyamya kṣīṇatṛṣṇaḥ samāhitaḥ
|
sarvabhūtasuhṛnmitro brahmabhūyāya kalpate||51||

52indriyāṇāṃ nirodhena sarveṣāṃ viṣayaiṣiṇām
|
munerjanapadatyāgādadhyātmāgniḥ samidhyate||52||

53yathāgnirindhanairiddho mahājyotiḥ prakāśate
|
tathendriyanirodhena mahānātmā prakāśate||53||

54yadā paśyati bhūtāni prasannātmā''tmano hṛdi
|
svayañjyotistadā sūkṣmātsūkṣmaṃ pranpotyanuttamam||54||

55agnī rūpaṃ payaḥ sroto vāyuḥ sparśanapeva ca
|
mahī paṅkadharaṃ ghoramākāśaśravaṇaṃ tathā||55||

56rogaśokasamāviṣṭaṃ pañcasrotaḥsamāvṛtam
|
pañcabhūtasamāyuktaṃ navadvāraṃ dvidaivatam||56||

57rajasvalamathādṛśyaṃ triguṇaṃ ca tridhātukam
|
saṃsargābhirataṃ mūḍhaṃ śarīramiti dhāraṇā||57||

58duścaraṃ sarvaloke'smin sattvaṃ prati samāśritam
|
etadeva hi loke'smin kālacakraṃ pravartate||58||

59etanmahārṇavaṃ ghoramagādhaṃ mohasaṃjñitam
|
vikṣipetsaṃkṣipeccaiva bodhayetsāmaraṃ jagat||59||

60kāmaṃ krodhaṃ bhayaṃ lobhamabhidrohamathānṛtam
|
indriyāṇāṃ nirodhena satastyajati dustyajān||60||

61yasyaite nirjitā loke triguṇāḥ pañcadhātavaḥ
|
vyomni tasya paraṃ sthānamānantyamatha labhyate||61||

62pañcendriyamahākūlāṃ manovegamahodakām
|
nadīṃ mohahradāṃ tīrtvā kāmakrodhāvubhau jayet||62||

63sa sarvadoṣanirmuktastataḥ paśyati tatparam
|
mano manasi sandhāya paśyannātmānamātmani||63||

64sarvavitsarvabhūteṣu vindatyātmānamātmani
|
ekadhā bahudhā caiva vikurvāṇastatastataḥ||64||

65dhruvaṃ paśyati rūpāṇi dīpāddīpaśataṃ yathā
|
sa vai viṣṇuśca mitraśca varuṇo'gniḥ prajāpatiḥ||65||

66sa hi dhātā vidhātā ca sa prabhuḥ sarvatomukhaḥ
|
hṛdayaṃ sarvabhūtānāṃ mahānātmā prakāśate||66||

67taṃ viprasaṅghāśca surāsurāśca yakṣāḥ piśācāḥ pitaro vayāṃsi
|
rakṣogaṇā bhūtagaṇāśca sarve maharṣayaścaiva sadā stuvanti||67||
