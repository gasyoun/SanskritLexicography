Mahābhārata 5.133 (Udyogaparvan)

1kuntyuvāca
|
atrāpyudāharantīmamitihāsaṃ purātanam
|
vidulāyāśca saṃvādaṃ putrasya ca paraṃtapa ||1||

2tataḥ śreyaśca bhūyaśca yathāvadvaktumarhasi
|
yaśasvinī manyumatī kule jātā vibhāvarī ||2||

3kṣatradharmaratā dāntā vidulā dīrghadarśinī
|
viśrutā rājasaṃsatsu śrutavākyā bahuśrutā ||3||

4vidulā nāma rājanyā jagarhe putramaurasam
|
nirjitaṃ sindhurājena śayānaṃ dīnacetasam ||4||

5vidulovāca
|
anandana mayā jāta dviṣatāṃ harṣavardhana ||5||

6na mayā tvaṃ na pitrā ca jātaḥ kvābhyāgato hyasi
|
nirmanyuścāpyasaṃkhyeyaḥ puruṣaḥ klībasādhanaḥ
|
yāvajjīvaṃ nirāśo'si kalyāṇāya dhuraṃ vaha ||6||

7mā''tmānamavamanyasva mainamalpena bībharaḥ
|
manaḥ kṛtvā sukalyāṇaṃ mā bhaistvaṃ pratisaṃhara ||7||

8uttiṣṭha he kāpuruṣa mā śeṣvaivaṃ parājitaḥ
|
amitrānnandayan sarvānnirmāno bandhuśokadaḥ ||8||

9supūrā vai kunadikā supūro mūṣikāñjaliḥ
|
susaṃtoṣaḥ kāpuruṣaḥ svalpakenaiva tuṣyati ||9||

10apyaherārujan daṃṣṭrāmāśveva nidhanaṃ vraja
|
api vā saṃśayaṃ prāpya jīvite'pi parākrameḥ ||10||

11apyareḥ śyenavacchidraṃ paśyestvaṃ viparikraman
|
vivadan vā'thavā tūṣṇīṃ vyomnīvāpariśaṅkitaḥ ||11||

12tvamevaṃ pretavaccheṣe kasmādvajrahato yathā
|
uttiṣṭha he kāpuruṣa mā svāpsīḥ śatrunirjitaḥ ||12||

13mā'staṃgamastvaṃ kṛpaṇo viśrūyasva svakarmaṇā
|
mā madhye mā jaghanye tvaṃ mā'gho bhūstiṣṭha garjitaḥ ||13||

14alātaṃ tindukasyeva muhūrtamapi hi jvala
|
mā tuṣāgnirivānarcirdhūmāyasva jijīviṣuḥ ||14||

15muhūrtaṃ jvalitaṃ śreyo na ca dhūmāyitaṃ ciram
|
mā ha sma kasyacidgehe jani rājñaḥ kharo mṛduḥ ||15||

16kṛtvā mānuṣyakaṃ karma sṛtvā''jiṃ yāvaduttamam
|
dharmasyānṛṇyamāpnoti na cātmānaṃ vigarhate ||16||

17alabdhyā yadi vā labdhyā nānuśocati paṇḍitaḥ
|
ānantaryaṃ cārabhate na prāṇānāṃ dhanāyate ||17||

18udbhāvayasva vīryaṃ vā tāṃ vā gaccha dhruvāṃ gatim
|
dharmaṃ putrāgrataḥ kṛtvā kiṃ nimittaṃ hi jīvasi ||18||

19iṣṭāpūrtaṃ hi te klība kīrtiśca sakalā hatā
|
vicchinnaṃ bhogamūlaṃ te kiṃnimittaṃ hi jīvasi ||19||

20śatrurnimajjatā grāhyo jaṅghāyāṃ prapatiṣyatā
|
viparicchinnamūlo'pi na viṣīdet kathañcana ||20||

21udyamya dhuramutkarṣedājāneyakṛtaṃ smaran
|
kuru satyaṃ ca mānaṃ ca viddhi pauruṣamātmanaḥ ||21||

22udbhāvaya kulaṃ magnaṃ tvatkṛte svayameva hi
|
yasya vṛttaṃ na jalpanti mānavā mahadadbhutam ||22||

23rāśivardhanamātraṃ sa naiva strī na punaḥ pumān
|
dāne tapasi satye ca yasya noccaritaṃ yaśaḥ ||23||

24vidyāyāmarthalābhe yā māturuccāra eva saḥ
|
śrutena tapasā vā'pi śriyā vā vikrameṇa vā ||24||

25janānyo'bhibhavatyanyān karmaṇā hi sa vai pumān
|
na tveva jālmīṃ kāpālīṃ vṛttimeṣitumarhasi ||25||

26nṛśaṃsyāmayaśasyāṃ ca duḥkhāṃ kāpuruṣocitām
|
yamenamabhinandeyuramitrāḥ puruṣaṃ kṛśam ||26||

27lokasya samavajñātaṃ nihīnāsanavāsasam
|
aho lābhakaraṃ hīnamalpajīvanamalpakam ||27||

28nedṛśaṃ bandhumāsādya bāndhavaḥ sukhamedhate
|
avṛttyaiva vipatsyāmo vayaṃ rāṣṭrāt pravāsitāḥ ||28||

29sarvakāmarasairhīnāḥ sthānabhraṣṭā akiñcanāḥ
|
avalgukāriṇaṃ satsu kulavaṃśasya nāśanam ||29||

30kaliṃ putrapravādena sañjaya tvāmajījanam
|
niramarṣaṃ nirutsāhaṃ nirvīryamarinandanam ||30||

31mā sma sīmantinī kācijjanayet putramīdṛśam
|
mā dhūmāya jvalātyantamākramya jahi śātravān ||31||

32jvala mūrdhanyamitrāṇāṃ muhūrtamapi vā kṣaṇam
|
etāvāneva puruṣo yadamarṣī yadakṣamī ||32||

33kṣamāvānniramarṣaśca naiva strī na punaḥ pumān
|
santoṣo vai śriyaṃ hanti tathā'nukrośa eva ca ||33||

34anutthānabhaye cobhe nirīho nāśnute mahat
|
ebhyo nikṛtipāpebhyaḥ pramuñcātmānamātmanā ||34||

35āyasaṃ hṛdayaṃ kṛtvā mṛgayasva punaḥ svakam
|
paraṃ viṣahate yasmāttasmāt puruṣa ucyate ||35||

36tamāhurvyarthanāmānaṃ strīvadya iha jīvati
|
śūrasyorjitasattvasya siṃhavikrāntacāriṇaḥ ||36||

37diṣṭabhāvaṃ gatasyāpi viṣaye modate prajā
|
ya ātmanaḥ priyasukhe hitvā mṛgayate śriyam ||37||

38amātyānāmatho harṣamādhātyacireṇa saḥ ||38||

39putra uvāca|
kiṃ nu te māmapaśyantyāḥ pṛthivyā api sarvayā
|
kimābharaṇakṛtyaṃ te kiṃ bhogairjīvitena vā ||39||

40mātovāca
|
kimadyakānāṃ ye lokā dviṣantastānavāpnuyuḥ
|
ye tvādṛtātmanāṃ lokāḥ suhṛdastān vrajantu naḥ ||40||

41bhṛtyairvihīyamānānāṃ parapiṇḍopajīvinām
|
kṛpaṇānāmasattvānāṃ mā vṛttimanuvartithāḥ ||41||

42anu tvāṃ tāta jīvantu brāhmaṇāḥ suhṛdastathā
|
parjanyamiva bhūtāni devā iva śatakratum ||42||

43yamājīvanti puruṣaṃ sarvabhūtāni sañjaya
|
pakvaṃ drumamivāsādya tasya jīvitamarthavat ||43||

44yasya śūrasya vikrāntairedhante bāndhavāḥ sukham
|
tridaśā iva śakrasya sādhu tasyeha jīvitam ||44||

45svabāhubalamāśritya yo'bhyujjīvati mānavaḥ
|
sa loke labhate kīrtiṃ paratra ca śubhāṃ gatim ||45||
