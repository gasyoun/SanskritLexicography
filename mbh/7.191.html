Mahābhārata 7.191 (Droṇaparvan)

1sañjaya uvāca|
taṃ dṛṣṭvā paramodvignaṃ śokopahatacetasam
|
pañcālarājasya suto dhṛṣṭadyumnaḥ samādravat||1||

2ya iṣṭvā manujendreṇa drupadena mahāmakhe
|
labdho droṇavināśāya samiddhāddhavyavāhanāt||2||

3sa dhanurjaitramādāya ghoraṃ jaladaniḥsvanam
|
dṛḍhajyamajaraṃ divyaṃ śaraṃ cāśīviṣopamam||3||

4sandadhe kārmuke tasmiṃstatastamanalopanam
|
droṇaṃ jighaṃsuḥ pāñcālyo mahājvālamivānalam||4||

5tasya rūpaṃ śarasyāsīddhanurjyāmaṇḍalāntare
|
dyotato bhāskarasyeva ghanānte pariveṣiṇaḥ||5||

6pārṣatena parāmṛṣṭaṃ jvalantamiva taddhanuḥ
|
antakālamanuprāptaṃ menire vīkṣya sainikāḥ||6||

7tamiṣuṃ saṃhatam tena bhāradvājaḥ pratāpavān
|
dṛṣṭvā'manyata dehasya kālaparyāyamāgatam||7||

8tataḥ prayatnamātiṣṭhadācāryastasya vāraṇe
|
na cāsyāstrāṇi rājendra prādurāsanmahātmanaḥ||8||

9tasya tvahāni catvāri kṣapā caikā'syato gatā
|
tasya cāhnastribhāgena kṣayaṃ jagmuḥ patattriṇaḥ||9||

10sa śarakṣayamāsādya putraśokena cārditaḥ
|
vividhānāṃ ca divyānāmastrāṇāmaprasādataḥ||10||

11utsraṣṭukāmaḥ śastrāṇi ṛṣivākyapracoditaḥ
|
tejasā pūryamāṇaśca yuyudhe na yathā purā||11||

12bhūyaścānyatsamādāya divyamāṅgirasaṃ dhanuḥ
|
śarāṃśca brahmadaṇḍābhān dhṛṣṭadyumnamayodhayat||12||

13tatastaṃ śaravarṣeṇa mahatā samavākirat
|
vyaśātayacca saṃkruddho dhṛṣṭadyumnamamarṣaṇam||13||

14śarāṃśca śatadhā tasya droṇaściccheda sāyakaiḥ
|
dhvajaṃ dhanuśca niśitaiḥ sārathiṃ cāpyapātayat||14||

15dhṛṣṭadyumnaḥ prahasyānyatpunarādāya kārmukam
|
śitena cainaṃ bāṇena pratyavidhyatstanāntare||15||

16so'tividdho maheṣvāso'saṃbhrānta iva saṃyuge
|
bhallena śitadhāreṇa cicchedāsya punardhanuḥ||16||

17yaccāsya bāṇavikṛtaṃ dhanūṃṣi ca viśāṃpate
|
sarvaṃ ciccheda durdharṣo gadāṃ khaḍgaṃ ca varjayan||17||

18dhṛṣṭadyumnnaṃ ca vivyādha navabhirniśitaiḥ śaraiḥ
|
jīvitāntakaraḥ kruddhaḥ kruddharūpaḥ paraṃtapaḥ||18||

19dhṛṣṭadyumno'tha tasyāśvān svarathāśvairmahārathaḥ
|
vyāmiśrayada‌meyātmā brāhmamastramudīrayan||19||

20te miśrā bahvaśobhanta javanā vātaraṃhasaḥ
|
pārāvatasavarṇāśca śoṇāśca bharatarṣabha||20||

21yathā savidyuto meghā nadanto jaladāgame
|
tathā rejurmahārāja miśritā raṇamūrdhani||21||

22īṣābandhaṃ cakrabandhaṃ rathabandhaṃ tathaiva ca
|
prāṇāśayada‌meyātmā dhṛṣṭadyumnasya sa dvijaḥ||22||

23sa chinnadhanvā pāñcālyo nikṛttadhvajasārathiḥ
|
uttamāmāpadaṃ prāpya gadāṃ vīraḥ parāmṛśat||23||

24tāmasya viśikhaistīkṣṇaiḥ kṣipyamāṇāṃ mahārathaḥ
|
nijaghāna śarairdoṇaḥ kruddhaḥ satyaparākramaḥ||24||

25tāṃ tu dṛṣṭvā naravyāghro droṇena nihatāṃ śaraiḥ
|
vimalaṃ khaḍgamādatta śatacandraṃ ca bhānumat||25||

26asaṃśayaṃ tathābhūtaḥ pāñcālyaḥ sādhvamanyata
|
vadhamācāryamukhyasya prāptakālaṃ mahātmanaḥ||26||

27tataḥ sa rathanīḍasthaṃ svarathasya ratheṣayā
|
agacchadasimudyamya śatacandraṃ ca bhānumat||27||

28cikīrṣurduṣkaraṃ karma dhṛṣṭadyumno mahārathaḥ
|
iyeṣa vakṣo bhettuṃ sa bhāradvājasya saṃyuge||28||

29so'tiṣṭhadyugamadhye vai yugasannahaneṣu ca
|
jaghanārdheṣu cāśvānāṃ tatsainyāḥ samapūjayan||29||

30tiṣṭhato yugapālīṣu śoṇānapyadhitiṣṭhataḥ
|
nāpaśyadantaraṃ droṇastadadbhutamivābhavat||30||

31kṣipraṃ śyenasya carato yathaivāmiṣagṛddhinaḥ
|
tadvadāsī‌dabhīsāro droṇapārṣatayo raṇe||31||

32tasya pārāvatānaśvān rathaśaktyā parābhinat
|
sarvānekaikaśo droṇo raktānaśvānvivarjayan||32||

33te hatā nyapatan bhūmau dhṛṣṭadyumnasya vājinaḥ
|
śoṇāstu paryamucyanta rathabandhādviśāṃpate||33||

34tānhayānnihatān dṛṣṭvā dvijāgryeṇa sa pārṣataḥ
|
nāmṛṣyata yudhāṃ śreṣṭho yājñasenirmahārathaḥ||34||

35virathaḥ sa gṛhītvā tu khaḍgaṃ khaḍgabhṛtāṃ varaḥ
|
droṇamabhyapatadrājan vainateya ivoragam||35||

36tasya rūpaṃ babhau rājan bhāradvājaṃ jighāṃsataḥ
|
yathā rūpaṃ purā viṣṇorhiraṇyakaśiporvedhe||36||

37sa tadā vividhānmārgānpravarāṃścaikaviṃśatim
|
darśayāmāsa kauravya pārṣato vicaran raṇe||37||

38bhrāntamudbhrāntamāviddhamāplutaṃ prasṛtaṃ sṛtam
|
parivṛttaṃ nivṛttaṃ ca khaḍgaṃ carma ca dhārayan||38||

39saṃpātaṃ samudīrṇaṃ ca darśayāmāsa pārṣataḥ
|
bhārataṃ kauśikaṃ caiva sātvataṃ caiva śikṣayā||39||

40darśayan vyacaradyuddhe droṇasyāntacikīrṣayā
|
caratastasya tān mārgān vicitrān khaḍgacarmiṇaḥ||40||

41vyasmayanta raṇe yodhā devatāśca samāgatāḥ
|
tataḥ śarasahastreṇa śatacandramapātayat||41||

42carma khaḍgaṃ ca saṃbādhe dhṛṣṭadyumnasya sa dvijaḥ
|
ye tu vaitastikā nāma śarā āsannayodhinaḥ||42||

43nikṛṣṭayuddhe droṇasya nānyeṣāṃ santi te śarāḥ
|
ṛte śāradvatātpārthāddrauṇervaikartanāttathā||43||

44pradyumnayuyudhānābhyāmabhimanyośca bhārata
|
athāsyeṣu samādhatta dṛḍhaṃ paramasaṃmatam||44||

45antevāsinamācāryo jighāṃsuḥ putrasaṃmitam
|
taṃ śarairdaśabhistīkṣṇaiściccheda śinipuṅgavaḥ||45||

46paśyatastava putrasya karṇasya ca mahātmanaḥ
|
grastamācāryamukhyena dhṛṣṭadyumnamamocayat||46||

47carantaṃ rathamārgeṣu sātyakiṃ satyavikramam
|
droṇakarṇāntaragataṃ kṛpasyāpi ca bhārata||47||

48apaśyetāṃ mahātmānau viṣvaksenadhanañjayau
|
apūjayetāṃ vārṣṇeyaṃ bruvāṇau sādhu sādhviti||48||

49divyānyastrāṇi sarveṣāṃ yudhi nighnantamacyutam
|
abhipatya tataḥ senāṃ viṣvaksenadhanañjayau||49||

50dhanañjayastataḥ kṛṣṇamabravītpaśya keśava
|
ācāryarathamukhyānāṃ madhye krīḍanmadhūdvahaḥ||50||

51ānaṃdayati māṃ bhūyaḥ sātyakiḥ paravīrahā
|
mādrīputrau ca bhīmaṃ ca rājānaṃ ca yudhiṣṭhiram||51||

52yacchikṣayā'nuddhataḥ sanraṇe carati sātyakiḥ
|
mahārathānupakrīḍan vṛṣṇīnāṃ kīrtivardhanaḥ||52||

53tamete pratinandanti siddhāḥ sainyāśca vismitāḥ
|
ajayyaṃ samare dṛṣṭvā sādhu sādhviti sātyakim
|
yodhāścobhayataḥ sarve karmabhiḥ samapūjayan||53||
