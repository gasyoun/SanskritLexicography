Mahābhārata 5.5 (Udyogaparvan)

1vāsudeva uvāca|
upapannamidaṃ vākyaṃ somakānāṃ dhuraṃdhare
|
arthasiddhikaraṃ rājñaḥ pāṇḍavasyāmitaujasaḥ ||1||

2etacca pūrvaṃ kāryaṃ naḥ sunītimabhikāṅkṣatām
|
anyathā hyācarankarma puruṣaḥ syātsubāliśaḥ ||2||

3kiṃ tu saṃbandhakaṃ tulyamasmākaṃ kurupāṇḍuṣu
|
yatheṣṭaṃ vartamāneṣu pāṇḍaveṣu ca teṣu ca ||3||

4te vivāhārthamānītā vayaṃ sarve tathā bhavān
|
kṛte vivāhe muditā gamiṣyāmo gṛhānprati ||4||

5bhavān vṛddhatamo rājñāṃ vayasā ca śrutena ca
|
śiṣyavat te vayaṃ sarve bhavāmeha na saṃśayaḥ ||5||

6bhavantaṃ dhṛtarāṣṭraśca satataṃ bahu manyate
|
ācāryayoḥ sakhā cāsi droṇasya ca kṛpasya ca ||6||

7sa bhavān preṣayatvadya pāṇḍavārthakaraṃ vacaḥ
|
sarveṣāṃ niścitaṃ tannaḥ preṣayiṣyati yadbhavān ||7||

8yadi tāvacchamaṃ kuryannyāyena kurupuṅgavaḥ
|
na bhavet kurupāṇḍūnāṃ saubhrātreṇa mahān kṣayaḥ ||8||

9atha darpānvito mohānna kuryāddhṛtarāṣṭrajaḥ
|
anyeṣāṃ preṣayitvā ca paścādasmān samāhvayeḥ ||9||

10tato duryodhano mandaḥ sahāmātyaḥ sabāndhavaḥ
|
niṣṭhāmāpatsyate mūḍhaḥ kruddhe gāṇḍīvadhanvani ||10||

11vaiśampāyana uvāca|
tataḥ satkṛtya vārṣṇeyaṃ virāṭaḥ pṛthivīpatiḥ
|
gṛhān prasthāpayāmāsa sagaṇaṃ sahabāndhavam ||11||

12dvārakāṃ tu gate kṛṣṇe yudhiṣṭhirapurogamāḥ
|
cakruḥ sāṃgrāmikaṃ sarvaṃ virāṭaśca mahīpatiḥ ||12||

13tataḥ saṃpreṣayāmāsa virāṭaḥ saha bāndhavaiḥ
|
sarveṣāṃ bhūmipālānāṃ drupadaśca mahīpatiḥ ||13||

14vacanāt kurusiṃhānāṃ matsyapāñcālayośca te
|
samājagmurmahīpālāḥ saṃprahṛṣṭā mahābalāḥ ||14||

15tacchrutvā pāṇḍuputrāṇāṃ samāgacchanmahadbalam
|
dhṛtarāṣṭrasutāścāpi samāninyurmahīpatīn ||15||

16samākulā mahī rājankurupāṇḍavakāraṇāt
|
tadā samabhavat kṛtsnā saṃprayāṇe mahīkṣitām ||16||

17saṃkulā ca tadā bhūmiścaturaṅgabalānvitā
|
balāni teṣāṃ vīrāṇāmāgacchanti tatastataḥ ||17||

18cālayantīva gāṃ devīṃ saparvatavanāmimām
|
tataḥ prajñāvayovṛddhaṃ pāñcālyaḥ svapurohitam|| kurubhyaḥ preṣayāmāsa yudhiṣṭhiramate sthitaḥ ||18||
