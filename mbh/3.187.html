Mahābhārata 3.187 (Āraṇyakaparvan)

1vaiśampāyana uvāca|
tataḥ sa pāṇḍavo vipraṃ mārkaṇḍeyamuvāca ha
|
kathayasveti caritaṃ manorvaivasvatasya ca||1||

2mārkaṇḍeya uvāca|
vivasvataḥ suto rājan maharṣiḥ supratāpavān
|
babhūva naraśārdūla prajāpatisamadyutiḥ||2||

3ojasā tejasā lakṣmyā tapasā ca viśeṣataḥ
|
aticakrāma pitaraṃ manuḥ svaṃ ca pitāmaham||3||

4ūrdhvabāhurviśālāyāṃ badaryāṃ sa narādhipa
|
ekapādasthitastīvraṃ cakāra sumahattapaḥ||4||

5avākśirāstathācāpi netrairanimiṣairdṛḍham
|
so'tapyata tapo ghoraṃ varṣāṇāmayutaṃ tadā||5||

6taṃ kadācittapasyantamārdracīrajaṭādharam
|
cīriṇītīramāgamya matsyo vacanamabravīt||6||

7bhagavan kṣudramatsyo'smi balavadbhyo bhayaṃ mama
|
matsyebhyo hi tato māṃ tvaṃ trātumarhasi suvrata||7||

8durbalaṃ balavanto hi matsyā matsyaṃ viśeṣataḥ
|
āsvadanti sadā vṛttirvihitā naḥ sanātanī||8||

9tasmādbhayaughānmahato majjantaṃ māṃ viśeṣataḥ
|
trātumarhāsa kartā'smi kṛte pratikṛtaṃ tava||9||

10sa matsyavacanaṃ śrutvā kṛpayā'bhipariplutaḥ
|
manurvaivasvato gṛhṇāttaṃ matsyaṃ pāṇinā svayam||10||

11udakāntamupānīya matsyaṃ vaivasvato manuḥ
|
aliñjare prākṣipattaṃ candrāṃśusadṛśaprabhe||11||

12sa tatra vavṛdhe rājan matsyaḥ paramasatkṛtaḥ
|
putravat svīkarot tasmai manurbhāvaṃ viśeṣataḥ||12||

13atha kālena mahatā sa matsyaḥ sumahānabhūt
|
aliñjare yathā caiva nāsau samabhavat kila||13||

14atha matsyo manuṃ dṛṣṭvā punarevā'bhyabhāṣata
|
bhagavan sādhu me'dyānyat sthānaṃ sampratipādaya||14||

15uddhṛtyāliñjarāttasmāt tataḥ sa bhagavān manuḥ
|
taṃ matsyamanayadvāpīṃ mahatīṃ sa manustadā||15||

16tatra taṃ prākṣipaccāpi manuḥ parapurañjaya
|
athāvardhata matsyaḥ sa punarvarṣagaṇān bahūn||16||

17dviyojanāyatā vāpī vistṛtā cāpi yojanam
|
tasyāṃ nāsau samabhavanmatsyo rājīvalocana||17||

18viceṣṭituṃ ca kaunteya matsyo vāpyāṃ viśāmpate
|
manuṃ matsyastato dṛṣṭvā punarevābhyabhāṣata||18||

19naya māṃ bhagavan sādho samudramahiṣīṃ priyām
|
gaṅgāṃ tatra nivatsyāmi yathā vā tāta manyase||19||

20nideśe hi mayā tubhyaṃ sthātavyamanasūyatā
|
vṛddhirhiparamā prāptā tvatkṛte hi mayā'nagha||20||

21evamukto manurmatsyamanayadbhagavān vaśī
|
nadīṃ gaṅgāṃ tatra cainaṃ svayaṃ prākṣipadacyutaḥ||21||

22sa tatra vavṛdhe matsyaḥ kiñcitkālamarindama
|
tataḥ punarmanuṃ dṛṣṭvā matsyo vacanamabravīt||22||

23gaṅgāyāṃ hi na śaknomi bṛhattvācceṣṭituṃ prabho
|
samudraṃ naya māmāśu prasīda bhagavanniti||23||

24uddhṛtya gaṅgāsalilāt tato matsyaṃ manuḥ svayam
|
samudramanayat pārtha tatra cainamavāsṛjat||24||

25sumahānapi matsyastu sa manornayatastadā
|
āsīdyatheṣṭahāryaśca sparśagandhasukhasya vai||25||

26yadā samudre prakṣiptaḥ sa matsyo manunā tadā
|
tata enamidaṃ vākyaṃ smayamāna ivābravīt||26||

27bhagavan hi kṛtā rakṣā tvayā sarvā viśeṣataḥ
|
prāptakālaṃ tu yat kāryaṃ tvayā tacchūyatāṃ mama||27||

28acirādbhagavan bhaumamidaṃ sthāvarajaṅgamam
|
sarvameva mahābhāga pralayaṃ vai gamiṣyati||28||

29samprakṣālanakālo'yaṃ lokānāṃ samupasthitaḥ
|
tasmāt tvāṃ bodhayāmyadya yatte hitamanuttamam||29||

30trasānāṃ sthāvarāṇāṃ ca yacceṅgaṃ yacca neṅgati
|
tasya sarvasya samprāptaḥ kālaḥ paramadāruṇaḥ||30||

31nauśca kārayitavyā te dṛḍhā yuktavaṭārakā
|
tatra saptarṣibhiḥ sārdhamāruhethā mahāmune||31||

32bījāni caiva sarvāṇi yathoktāni dvijaiḥ purā
|
tasyāmārohayernāvi susaṅguptāni bhāgaśaḥ||32||

33nausthaśca māṃ pratīkṣethāstato munijanapriya
|
āgamiṣyāmyahaṃ śṛṅgī vijñeyastena tāpasa||33||

34evametat tvayā kāryamāpṛṣṭo'si vrajāmyaham
|
tā na śakyā mahatyo ve āpastartuṃ mayā vinā||34||

35nābhiśaṅkyamidaṃ cāpi vacanaṃ me tvayā vibho
|
evaṃ kariṣya iti taṃ sa matsyaṃ pratyabhāṣata||35||

36jagmatuśca yathākāmamanujñāpya parasparam
|
tato manurmahārāja yathoktaṃ matsyakena ha||36||

37bījānyādāya sarvāṇi sāgaraṃ puplave tadā
|
naukayā śubhayā vīra mahormiṇamarindama||37||

38cintayāmāsa ca manustaṃ matsyaṃ pṛthivīpate
|
sa ca taccintitaṃ jñātvā matsyaḥ parapurañjaya||38||

39śṛṅgī tatrājagāmāśu tadā bharatasattama
|
taṃ dṛṣṭvā manujavyāghra manurmatsyaṃ jalārṇave||39||

40śṛṅgiṇaṃ taṃ yathoktena rūpeṇādrimivocchritam
|
vaṭārakamayaṃ pāśamatha matsyasya mūrdhani||40||

41manurmanujaśārdula tasmin śṛṅge nyaveśayat
|
saṃyatastena pāśena matsyaḥ parapurañjaya||41||

42vegena mahatā nāvaṃ prākarṣallavaṇāmbhasi
|
sa ca tāṃstārayannāvā samudraṃ manujeśvara||42||

43nṛtyamānamivormībhirgarjamānamivāmbhasā
|
kṣobhyamāṇā mahāvātaiḥ sā naustasmin mahodadhau||43||

44dhūrṇate capaleva strī mattā parapurañjaya
|
naiva bhūmirna ca diśaḥ pradiśo vā cakāśire||44||

45sarvamāmbhasamevāsīt khaṃ dyauśca narapuṅgava
|
evaṃbhūte tadā loke saṅkule bharatarṣabha||45||

46adṛśyanta saptarṣayo manurmatsyastathaiva ca
|
evaṃ bahūn varṣagaṇāṃstāṃ nāvaṃ so'tha matsyakaḥ||46||

47cakarṣātandrito rājaṃstasmin salilasañcaye
|
tato himavataḥ śṛṅgaṃ yatparaṃ bharatarṣabha||47||

48tatrākarṣattato nāvaṃ sa matsyaḥ kurunandana
|
athābravīttadā matsyastānṛṣīn prahasan śanaiḥ||48||

49asmin himavataḥ śṛṅge nāvaṃ baghnīta mā ciram
|
sā baddhā tatra taistūrṇamṛṣibhirbharatarṣabha||49||

50naurmatsyasya vacaḥ śrutvā śṛṅge himavatastadā
|
tacca naubandhanaṃ nāma śṛṅgaṃ himavataḥ param||50||

51khyātamadyāpi kaunteya tadviddhi bharatarṣabha
|
athābravīda‌nimiṣastānṛṣīn sahitastadā||51||

52ahaṃ prajāpatirbrahmā matparaṃ nādhigamyate
|
matsyarūpeṇa yūyaṃ ca mayā'smānmokṣitā bhayāt||52||

53manunā ca prajāḥ sarvāḥ sadevāsuramānuṣāḥ
|
sraṣṭavyāḥ sarvalokāśca yacceṅgaṃ yacca neṅgati||53||

54tapasā cāpi tīvreṇa pratibhā'sya bhaviṣyati
|
matprasādāt prajāsarge na ca mohaṃ gamiṣyati||54||

55ityuktvā vacanaṃ matsyaḥ kṣaṇenādarśanaṃ gataḥ
|
sraṣṭukāmaḥ prajāścāpi manurvaivasvataḥ svayam||55||

56pramūḍho'bhūt prajāsarge tapastepe mahat tataḥ
|
tapasā mahatā yuktaḥ so'tha sraṣṭuṃ pracakrame||56||

57sarvāḥ prajā manuḥ sākṣādyathāvadbharatarṣabha
|
ityetanmātsyakaṃ nāma purāṇaṃ parikīrtitam||57||

58ākhyānamidamākhyātaṃ sarvapāpaharaṃ mayā
|
ya idaṃ śṛṇuyānnityaṃ manoścaritamāditaḥ
|
sa sukhī sarvapūrṇārthaḥ sarvalokamiyānnaraḥ||58||
