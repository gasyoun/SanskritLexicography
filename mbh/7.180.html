Mahābhārata 7.180 (Droṇaparvan)

1sañjaya uvāca|
haiḍimbiṃ nihataṃ dṛṣṭvā viśīrṇabhiva parvatam
|
babhūvuḥ pāṇḍavāḥ sarve śokabāṣpākulekṣaṇāḥ||1||

2vāsudevastu harṣeṇa mahatā'bhipariplutaḥ
|
nanāda siṃhanādaṃ vai paryaṣvajata phālgunam||2||

3sa vinadya mahānādamabhīṣūnsanniyamya ca
|
nanarta harṣasaṃvīto vātoddhūta iva drumaḥ||3||

4tataḥ pariṣvajya punaḥ pārthamāsphoṭya cāsakṛt
|
rathopasthagato dhīmānprāṇadatpunaracyutaḥ||4||

5prahṛṣṭamanasaṃ jñātvā vāsudevaṃ mahābalaḥ
|
arjuno'thābravīdrājannātidṛṣṭamanā iva||5||

6atiharṣo'yamasthāne tavādya madhusūdana
|
śokasthāne tu saṃprāpte haiḍimbasya vadhena tu||6||

7vimukhānīha sainyāni hataṃ dṛṣṭvā ghaṭotkacam
|
vayaṃ ca bhṛśamudvignā haiḍimbestu nipātanāt||7||

8naitatkāraṇamalpaṃ hi bhaviṣyati janārdana
|
tadadya śaṃsa me pṛṣṭaḥ satyaṃ satyavatāṃ vara||8||

9yadyetanna rahasyaṃ te vaktumarhasyarindama
|
dhairyasya vaikṛtaṃ brūhi tvamadya madhusūdana||9||

10samudrasyeva saṃśoṣaṃ meroriva visarpaṇam
|
tathaitadadya manye'haṃ tava karma janārdana||10||

11śrīvāsudeva uvāca|
atiharṣamimaṃ prāptaṃ śṛṇu me tvaṃ dhanañjaya
|
atīva manasaḥ sadyaḥ prasādakaramuttamam||11||

12śaktiṃ ghaṭotkacenemāṃ vyaṃsayitvā mahādyute
|
karṇaṃ nihatamevājau viddhi sadyo dhanañjaya||12||

13śaktihastaṃ punaḥ karṇaṃ ko loke'sti pumāniha
|
ya enamabhitastiṣṭhetkārtikeyamivāhave||13||

14diṣṭyā'panītakavaco diṣṭyā'pahṛtakuṇḍalaḥ
|
diṣṭyā sā vyaṃsitā śaktiramoghā'sya ghaṭotkace||14||

15yadi hi syātsakavacastathaiva syātsakuṇḍalaḥ
|
sāmarānapi lokāṃstrīnekaḥ karṇo jayedraṇe||15||

16vāsavo vā kubero vā varuṇo vā jaleśvaraḥ
|
yamo vā notsahetkarṇaṃ raṇe pratisamāsitum||16||

17gāṇḍīvamudyamya bhavāṃścakraṃ cāhaṃ sudarśanam
|
na śaktau svo raṇe jetuṃ tathāyuktaṃ nararṣabham||17||

18tvaddhitārthaṃ tu śakreṇa māyāpahṛtakuṇḍalaḥ
|
vihīnakavacaścāyaṃ kṛtaḥ parapurañjayaḥ||18||

19utkṛtya kavacaṃ yasmātkuṇḍale vimale ca te
|
prādācchakrāya karṇo vai tena vaikartanaḥ smṛtaḥ||19||

20āśīviṣa iva kruddho jṛṃbhito mantratejasā
|
tathā'dya bhāti karṇo me śāntajvāla ivānalaḥ||20||

21yadāprabhṛti karṇāya śaktirdattā mahātmanā
|
vāsavena mahābāho kṣiptā yā'sau ghaṭotkace||21||

22kuṇḍalābhyāṃ nimāyātha divyena kavacena ca
|
tāṃ prāpyāmanyata vṛṣaḥ satataṃ tvāṃ hataṃ raṇe||22||

23evaṃ gato'pi śakyo'yaṃ hantuṃ nānyena kenacit
|
ṛte tvāṃ puruṣavyāghra śape satyena cānagha||23||

24brahmaṇyaḥ satyavādī ca tapasvī niyatavrataḥ
|
ripuṣvapi dayāvāṃśca tasmātkarṇo vṛṣaḥ smṛtaḥ||24||

25yuddhaśauṇḍo mahābāhurnityodyataśarāsanaḥ
|
kesarīva vane nardanmātaṅga iva yūthapān||25||

26vimadān rathaśārdūlānkurute raṇamūrdhani
|
madhyaṃgata ivādityo yo na śakyo nirīkṣitum||26||

27tvadīyaiḥ puruṣavyāghra yodhamukhyairmahātmabhiḥ
|
śarajālasahastrāṃśuḥ śaradīva divākaraḥ||27||

28tapānte jalado yadvaccharadhārāḥ kṣaranmuhuḥ
|
divyāstrajaladaḥ karṇaḥ parjanya iva vṛṣṭimān||28||

29trida‌śairapi cāsyadbhiḥ śaravarṣaṃ samantataḥ
|
aśakyastadayaṃ jetuṃ stravadbhirmāṃsaśoṇitam||29||

30kavacena vihīnaśca kuṇḍalābhyāṃ ca pāṇḍava
|
so'dya mānuṣatāṃ prāpto vimuktaḥ śakradattayā||30||

31eko hi yogo'sya bhavedvadhāyacchidre hyenaṃ svapramattaḥ pramattam
|
kṛcchraṃ prāptaṃ rathacakre vimagne hanyāḥ pūrvaṃ tvaṃ tu saṃjñāṃ vicārya||31||

32na hyudyatāstraṃ yudhi hanyādajayyamapyekavīro balabhitsavajraḥ
|
jarāsandhaścedirājo mahātmā mahābāhuścaikalavyo niṣādaḥ||32||

33ekaikaśo nihatāḥ sarva ete yogaistaistaistvaddhitārthaṃ mayaiva
|
athāpare nihatā rākṣasendrā hiḍimba-kirmīra-bakapradhānāḥ
|
alāyudhaḥ paracakrāvamardīghaṭotkacaścograkarmā tarasvī||33||
