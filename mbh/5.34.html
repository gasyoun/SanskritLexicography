Mahābhārata 5.34 (Udyogaparvan)

1dhṛtarāṣṭra uvāca|
jāgrato dahyamānasya yatkāryamanupaśyasi
|
tadbrūhi tvaṃ hi nastāta dharmārthakuśako hyasi ||1||

2tvaṃ māṃ yathāvadvidura praśādhi prajñāpūrvaṃ sarvamajātaśatroḥ
|
yanmanyase pathyamadīnasattva śreyaskaraṃ brūhi tadvai kurūṇām ||2||

3pāpāśaṅkī pāpamevānupaśyan pṛcchāmi tvāṃ vyākulenātmanā'ham
|
kave tanme brūhi sarvaṃ yathāvanmanīṣitaṃ sarvamajātaśatroḥ ||3||

4vidura uvāca|
śubhaṃ vā yadi vā pāpaṃ dveṣyaṃ vā yadi vā priyam
|
apṛṣṭastasya tadbrūyādyasya necchet parābhavam ||4||

5tasmādvakṣyāmi te rājan hitaṃ yatsyāt kurūnprati
|
vacaḥ śreyaskaraṃ dharmyaṃ bruvatastannibodha me ||5||

6mithyopetāni karmāṇi siddhyeyuryāni bhārata
|
anupāyaprayuktāni mā sma teṣu manaḥ kṛthāḥ ||6||

7tathaiva yogavihitaṃ yattu karma na siddhyati
|
upāyayuktaṃ medhāvī na tatra glapayenmanaḥ ||7||

8anubandhānavekṣeta sānubandheṣu karmasu
|
saṃpradhārya ca kurvīta na vegena samācaret ||8||

9anubandhaṃ ca saṃprekṣya vipākaṃ caiva karmaṇām
|
utthānamātmanaścaiva dhīraḥ kurvīta vā na vā ||9||

10yaḥ pramāṇaṃ na jānāti sthāne vṛddhau tathā kṣaye
|
kośe janapade daṇḍe na sa rājye'vatiṣṭhate ||10||

11yastvetāni pramāṇāni yathoktānyanupaśyati
|
yukto dharmārthayorjñāne sa rājyamadhigacchati ||11||

12na rājyaṃ prāptamityeva vartitavyamasāṃpratam
|
śriyaṃ hyavinayo hanti jarā rūpamivottamam ||12||

13bhakṣyottamapraticchannaṃ matsyo baḍiśamāyasam
|
lobhābhipātī grasate nānubandhamavekṣate ||13||

14yacchakyaṃ grasituṃ grasyaṃ grastaṃ pariṇamecca yat
|
hitaṃ ca pariṇāme yattadādyaṃ bhūtimicchatā ||14||

15vanaspaterapakvāni phalāni pracinoti yaḥ
|
sa nāpnoti rasaṃ tebhyo bījaṃ cāsya vinaśyati ||15||

16yastu pakvamupādatte kāle pariṇataṃ phalam
|
phalādrasaṃ sa labhate bījāccaiva phalaṃ punaḥ ||16||

17yathā madhu samādatte rakṣan puṣpāṇi ṣaṭpadaḥ
|
tadvadarthānmanuṣyebhya ādadyādavihiṃsayā ||17||

18puṣpaṃ puṣpaṃ vicinvīta mūlacchedaṃ na kārayet
|
mālākāra ivārāme na yathā'ṅgārakārakaḥ ||18||

19kiṃnnu me syādidaṃ kṛtvā kinnu me syādakurvataḥ
|
iti karmāṇi sañcintya kuryādvā puruṣo na vā ||19||

20anārabhyā bhavantyarthāḥ kecinnityaṃ tathā'gatāḥ
|
kṛtaḥ puruṣakāro'hi bhavedyeṣu nirarthakaḥ ||20||

21prasādo niṣphalo yasya krodhaścāpi nirarthakaḥ
|
na taṃ bhartāramicchanti ṣaṇḍhaṃ patimiva striyaḥ ||21||

22kāṃścidarthānnaraḥ prājño laghumūlānmahāphalān
|
kṣipramārabhate kartuṃ na vighnayati tādṛśān ||22||

23ṛju paśyati yaḥ sarvaṃ cakṣuṣā'nupibanniva
|
āsīnamapi tūṣṇīkamanurajyanti taṃ prajāḥ ||23||

24supuṣpitaḥ syādaphalaḥ phalitaḥ syāddurāruhaḥ
|
apakvaḥ pakvasaṅkāśo na tu śīryeta karhicit ||24||

25cakṣuṣā manasā vācā karmaṇā ca caturvidham
|
prasādayati yo lokaṃ taṃ loko'nuprasīdati ||25||

26yasmāt trasyanti bhūtāni mṛgavyādhānmṛgā iva
|
sāgarāntāmapi mahīṃ labdhvā sa parihīyate ||26||

27pitṛpaitāmahaṃ rājyaṃ prāptavān svena karmaṇā
|
vāyurabhramivāsādya bhraṃśayatyanaye sthitaḥ ||27||

28dharmamācarato rājñaḥ sadbhiścaritamāditaḥ
|
vasudhā vasusaṃpūrṇā vardhate bhūtivardhanī ||28||

29atha saṃtyajato dharmamadharmaṃ cānutiṣṭhataḥ
|
pratisaṃveṣṭate bhūmiragnau carmāhitaṃ yathā||29||

30ya eva yatnaḥ kriyate pararāṣṭravimardane
|
sa eva yatnaḥ kartavyaḥ svarāṣṭraparipālane ||30||

31dharmeṇa rājyaṃ vindeta dharmeṇa paripālayet
|
dharmamūlāṃ śriyaṃ prāpya na jahāti na hīyate ||31||

32apyunmattāt pralapato bālācca parijalpataḥ
|
sarvataḥ sāramādadyādaśmabhya iva kāñcanam ||32||

33suvyāhṛtāni sūktāni sukṛtāni tatastataḥ
|
sañcinvan dhīra āsīta śilāhārī śilaṃ yathā ||33||

34gandhena gāvaḥ paśyanti vedaiḥ paśyanti brāhmaṇāḥ
|
cāraiḥ paśyanti rājānaścakṣurbhyāmitare janāḥ ||34||

35bhūyāṃsaṃ labhate kleśaṃ yā gaurbhavati durduhā
|
atha yā suduhā rājannaiva tāṃ vitudantyapi ||35||

36yadataptaṃ praṇamati na tat santāpayantyapi
|
yacca svayaṃ nataṃ dāru na tatsantāpayantyapi ||36||

37etayopamayā dhīraḥ sannameta balīyase
|
indrāya sa praṇamate namate yo balīyase ||37||

38parjanyanāthāḥ paśavo rājāno mantribāndhavāḥ
|
patayo bāndhavāḥ strīṇāṃ brāhmaṇā vedabāndhavāḥ ||38||

39satyena rakṣyate dharmo vidyā yogena rakṣyate
|
mṛjayā rakṣyate rūpaṃ kulaṃ vṛttena rakṣyate ||39||

40mānena rakṣyate dhānyamaśvān rakṣatyanukramaḥ
|
abhīkṣṇadarśanaṃ gāśca striyo rakṣyāḥ kucelataḥ ||40||

41na kulaṃ vṛttahīnasya pramāṇamiti me matiḥ
|
anteṣvapi hi jātānāṃ vṛttameva viśiṣyate ||41||

42ya īrṣuḥ paravitteṣu rūpe vīrye kulānvaye
|
sukhasaubhāgyasatkāre tasya vyādhiranantakaḥ ||42||

43akāryakaraṇādbhītaḥ kāryāṇāṃ ca vivarjanāt
|
akāle mantrabhedācca yena mādyenna tatpibet ||43||

44vidyāmado dhanamadastṛtīyo'bhijano madaḥ
|
madā ete'valiptānāmeta eva satāṃ damāḥ ||44||

45asanto'bhyarthitāḥ sadbhiḥ kvacitkārye kadācana
|
tāvanna tasya sukṛtaṃ kiñcitkāryaṃ kadācana
|
manyante santamātmānamasantamapi viśrutam ||45||

46gatirātmavatāṃ santaḥ santa eva satāṃ gatiḥ
|
asatāṃ ca gatiḥ santo na tvasantaḥ satāṃ gatiḥ ||46||

47jitā sabhā vastravatā miṣṭāśā gomatā jitā
|
adhvā jito yānavatā sarvaṃ śīlavatā jitam ||47||

48śīlaṃ pradhānaṃ puruṣe tadyasyeha praṇaśyati
|
na tasya jīvitenārtho na dhanena na bandhubhiḥ ||48||

49āḍhyānāṃ māṃsaparamaṃ madhyānāṃ gorasottaram
|
tailottaraṃ daridrāṇāṃ bhojanaṃ bharatarṣabha ||49||

50saṃpannataramevānnaṃ daridrā bhuñjate sadā
|
kṣutsvādutāṃ janayati sā cāḍhyeṣu sudurlabhā ||50||

51prāyeṇa śrīmatāṃ loke bhoktuṃ śaktirna vidyate
|
jīryantyapi hi kāṣṭhāni daridrāṇāṃ mahīpate ||51||

52avṛttirbhayamantyānāṃ madhyānāṃ maraṇādbhayam
|
uttamānāṃ tu martyānāmavamānāt paraṃ bhayam ||52||

53aiśvaryamadapāpiṣṭhā madāḥ pānamadādayaḥ
|
aiśvaryamadamatto hi nāpatitvā vibudhyate ||53||

54indriyaurindriyārtheṣu vartamānairanigrahaiḥ
|
tairayaṃ tāpyate loko nakṣatrāṇi grahairiva ||54||

55yo jitaḥ pañcavargeṇa sahajenātmakarṣiṇā
|
āpadastasya vardhante śuklapakṣa ivoḍurāṭ ||55||

56avijitya yathā''tmānamamātyān vijigīṣate
|
amitrān vā jitāmātyaḥ so'vaśaḥ parihīyate ||56||

57ātmānameva prathamaṃ dveṣyarūpeṇa yojayet
|
tato'mātyānamitrāṃśca na moghaṃ vijigīṣate ||57||

58vaśyendriyaṃ jitātmānaṃ dhṛtadaṇḍaṃ vikāriṣu
|
parīkṣyakāriṇaṃ dhīramatyantaṃ śrīrniṣevate ||58||

59rathaḥ śarīraṃ puruṣasya rājannātmā niyantendriyāṇyasya cāśvāḥ
|
tairapramattaḥ kuśalī sadaśvairdāntaiḥ sukhaṃ yāti rathīva dhīraḥ ||59||

60etānyanigṛhītāni vyāpādayitumapyalam
|
avidheyā ivādāntāḥ hayāḥ pathi kusārathim ||60||

61anarthamarthataḥ paśyannarthaṃ caivāpyanarthataḥ
|
indriyairajitairbālaḥ suduḥkhaṃ manyate sukham ||61||

62dharmārthau yaḥ parityajya syādindriyavaśānugaḥ
|
śrīprāṇadhanadārebhyaḥ kṣipraṃ sa parihīyate ||62||

63arthānāmīśvaro yaḥ syādindriyāṇāmanīśvaraḥ
|
indriyāṇāmanaiśvaryādaiśvāryādbhraśyate hi saḥ ||63||

64ātmanā'tmānamanvicchenmanobuddhīndriyairyataiḥ
|
ātmā hyevātmano bandhurātmaiva ripurātmanaḥ ||64||

65bandhurātmātmanastasya yenaivātmā'tmanā jitaḥ
|
sa eva niyato bandhuḥ sa eva niyato ripuḥ ||65||

66kṣudrākṣeṇeva jālena jhaṣāvapihitāvurū
|
kāmaśca rājan krodhaśca tau prajñānaṃ vilumpataḥ ||66||

67samavekṣyeha dharmārthau saṃbhārān yo'dhigacchati
|
sa vai saṃbhṛtasaṃbhāraḥ satataṃ sukhamedhate ||67||

68yaḥ pañcābhyantarān śatrūnavijitya manomayān
|
jigīṣati ripūnanyān ripavo'bhibhavanti tam ||68||

69dṛśyante hi mahātmāno badhyamānāḥ svakarmabhiḥ
|
indriyāṇāmanīśatvādrājāno rājyavibhramaiḥ ||69||

70asantyāgāt pāpakṛtāmapāpāṃstulyo daṇḍaḥ spṛśate miśrabhāvāt
|
śuṣkeṇārdraṃ dahyate miśrabhāvāttasmāt pāpaiḥ saha sandhiṃ na kuryāt ||70||

71nijānutpatataḥ śatrūna pañca pañca prayojanān
|
yo mohānna nigṛhṇāti tamāpadgrasate naram ||71||

72anasūyā'rjavaṃ śaucaṃ santoṣaḥ priyavāditā
|
damaḥ satyamanāyāso na bhavanti durātmanām ||72||

73ātmajñānamanāyāsastitikṣā dharmanityatā
|
vāk caiva guptā dānaṃ ca naitānyantyeṣu bhārata ||73||

74ākrośaparivādābhyāṃ vihiṃsantyabudhā budhān
|
vaktā pāpamupādatte kṣamamāṇo vimucyate ||74||

75hiṃsā balamasādhūnāṃ rājñāṃ daṇḍavidhirbalam
|
śuśrūṣā tu balaṃ strīṇāṃ kṣamā guṇavatāṃ balam ||75||

76vāksaṃyamo hi nṛpate suduṣkaratamo mataḥ
|
arthavacca vicitraṃ ca na śakyaṃ bahu bhāṣitum ||76||

77abhyāvahati kalyāṇaṃ vividhaṃ vāk subhāṣitā
|
saiva durbhāṣitā rājannanarthāyopapadyate ||77||

78rohate sāyakairviddhaṃ vanaṃ paraśunā hatam
|
vācā duruktaṃ bībhatsaṃ na saṃrohati vākkṣatam||78||

79karṇinālīkanārācānnirharanti śarīrataḥ
|
vākśalyastu na nirhartuṃ śakyo hṛdiśayo hi saḥ ||79||

80vāksāyakā vadanānniṣpatanti yairāhataḥ śocati rātryahāni
|
parasya nāmarmasu te patanti tān paṇḍito nāvasṛjet parebhyaḥ ||80||

81yasmai devāḥ prayacchanti puruṣāya parābhavam
|
buddhiṃ tasyāpakarṣanti so'vācīnāni paśyati||81||

82buddhau kaluṣabhūtāyāṃ vināśe pratyupasthite
|
anayo nayasaṅkāśo hṛdayānnāpasarpati ||82||

83seyaṃ buddhiḥ parītā te putrāṇāṃ bharatarṣabha
|
pāṇḍavānāṃ virodhena na cainānavabuddhyase ||83||

84rājā lakṣaṇasaṃpannastrailokyasyāpi yo bhavet
|
śiṣyaste śāsitā so'stu dhṛtarāṣṭra yudhiṣṭhiraḥ ||84||

85atīva sarvān putrāṃste bhāgadheyapuraskṛtaḥ
|
tejasā prajñayā caiva yukto dharmārthatattvavit ||85||

86anukrośādānṛśaṃsyādyo'sau dharmabhṛtāṃ varaḥ
|
gauravāttava rājendra bahūn kleśāṃstitikṣati ||86||
