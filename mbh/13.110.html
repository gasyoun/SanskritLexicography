Mahābhārata 13.110 (Anuśāsanaparvan)

1vaiśampāyana uvāca|
śaratalpagataṃ bhīṣmaṃ vṛddhaṃ kurupitāmaham
|
upagamya mahāprājñaḥ paryapṛcchadyudhiṣṭhiraḥ ||1||

2yudhiṣṭhira uvāca|
aṅgānāṃ rūpasaubhāgyaṃ priyaṃ caiva kathaṃ bhavet
|
dharmārthakāmasaṃyuktaḥ sukhabhāgī kathaṃ bhavet ||2||

3bhīṣma uvāca|
mārgaśīrṣasya māsasya candre mūlena saṃyute
|
pādau mūlena rājendra jaṃghāyāmatha rohiṇīm ||3||

4aśvinyāṃ sakthinī caiva ūrū cāṣāḍhayostathā
|
guhyaṃ tu phālgunī vidyātkṛttikā kaṭikāstathā ||4||

5nābhiṃ bhādrapade vidyādrevatyāmakṣimaṇḍalam
|
pṛṣṭhameva dhaniṣṭhāsu anurādhottarāstathā ||5||

6bāhubhyāṃ tu viśākhāsu hastau hastena nirdiśet
|
punarvasvaṅgulī rājannāśleṣāsu nakhāstathā ||6||

7grīvāṃ jyeṣṭhā ca rājendra śravaṇena tu karṇayoḥ
|
mukhaṃ puṣyeṇa dānena dantoṣṭhau svātirucyate ||7||

8hāsaṃ śatabhiṣāṃ caiva maghāṃ caivātha nāsikām
|
netre mṛgaśiro vidyāllalāṭe mitrameva tu ||8||

9bharaṇyāṃtu śiro vidyāt keśānārdrāṃ narādhipa
|
samāpte tu ghṛtaṃ dadyādbrāhmaṇe vedapārage ||9||

10subhago darśanīyaśca jñānabhāgyatha jāyate
|
jāyate paripūrṇāṅgaḥ paurṇamāsyeva candramāḥ ||10||
