Mahābhārata 3.7 (Āraṇyakaparvan)

1vaiśampāyana uvāca|
śrutvā ca viduraṃ prāptaṃ rājñā ca parisāntvitam
|
dhṛtarāṣṭrātmajo rājā paryatapyata durmatiḥ||1||

2sa saubaleyamānāyya karṇaduḥśāsanau tathā
|
abravīdvacanaṃ rājā praviśyābuddhijaṃ tamaḥ||2||

3eṣa pratyāgato mantrī dhṛtarāṣṭrasya dhīmataḥ
|
viduraḥ pāṇḍuputrāṇāṃ suhṛdvidvānhiterataḥ||3||

4yāvadasya punarbuddhiṃ viduro nāpakarṣati
|
pāṇḍavānayane tāvanmatrayadhvaṃ hitaṃ mama||4||

5atha paśyāmyahaṃ pārthānprāptāniha kathañcana
|
punaḥ śoṣaṃ gamiṣyāmi niramburniravagrahaḥ||5||

6viṣamudbandhanaṃ caiva śastramagnipraveśanam
|
kariṣye na hi tānṛddhānpunardraṣṭumihotsahe||6||

7śakuniruvāca|
kiṃ bāliśamatiṃ rājannāsthito'si viśāmpate
|
gatāste samayaṃ kṛtvā naitadevaṃ bhaviṣyati||7||

8satyavākye sthitāḥ sarve pāṇḍavā bharatarṣabha
|
pituste vacanaṃ tāta na grahīṣyanti karhicit||8||

9athavā te grahīṣyanti punareṣyanti vā puram
|
nirasya samayaṃ sarve paṇo'smākaṃ bhaviṣyati||9||

10sarve bhavāmo madhyasthā rājñaśchandānuvartinaḥ
|
chidraṃ bahu prapaśyantaḥ pāṇḍavānāṃ susaṃvṛtāḥ||10||

11duḥśāsana uvāca|
evametanmahāprājña yathā vadasi mātula
|
nityaṃ hi me kathayatastava buddhirvirocate||11||

12karṇa uvāca|
kāmamīkṣāmahe sarve duryodhana tavepsitam
|
aikamatyaṃ hi no rājansarveṣāmeva lakṣaye||12||

13nāgamiṣyanti te dhīrā akṛtvā kālasaṃvidam
|
āgamiṣyanti cenmohātpunardyūtena tāñjaya||13||

14vaiśampāyana uvāca|
evamuktastu karṇena rājā duryodhanastadā
|
nātihṛṣṭamanāḥ kṣipramabhavatsa parāṅmukhaḥ||14||

15upalabhya tataḥ karṇo vivṛtya nayane śubhe
|
roṣādduḥśāsanaṃ caiva saubalaṃ ca tameva ca||15||

16uvāca paramakruddha udyamyātmānamātmanā
|
atho mama mataṃ yattu tannibodhata bhūmipāḥ||16||

17priyaṃ sarve kariṣyāmo rājñaḥ kiṅkarapāṇayaḥ
|
na cāsya śaknumaḥ sthātuṃ priye sarve hyatandritāḥ||17||

18vayaṃ tu śastrāṇyādāya rathānāsthāya daṃśitāḥ
|
gacchāmaḥ sahitā hantuṃ pāṇḍavānvanagocarān||18||

19teṣu sarveṣu śānteṣu gateṣvaviditāṃ gatim
|
nirvivādā bhaviṣyanti dhārtarāṣṭrāstathā vayam||19||

20yāvadeva paridyūnā yāvacchokaparāyaṇāḥ
|
yāvanmitravihīnāśca tāvacchakyā mataṃ mama||20||

21tasya tadvacanaṃ śrutvā pūjayantaḥ punaḥ punaḥ
|
bāḍhamityeva te sarve pratyūcuḥ sūtajaṃ tadā||21||

22evamuktvā susaṃrabdhā rathaiḥ sarve pṛthakpṛthak
|
niryayuḥ pāṇḍavānhantuṃ sahitāḥ kṛtaniścayāḥ||22||

23tānprasthitānparijñāya kṛṣṇadvaipāyanaḥ prabhuḥ
|
ājagāma viśuddhātmā dṛṣṭvā divyena cakṣuṣā||23||

24pratiṣidhyātha tānsarvānbhagavā~llokapūjitaḥ
|
prajñācakṣuṣamāsīnamuvācābhyetya satvaram||24||
