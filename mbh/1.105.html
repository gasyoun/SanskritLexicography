Mahābhārata 1.105 (Ādiparvan)

1bhīṣma uvāca|
punarbharatavaṃśasya hetuṃ santānavṛddhaye
|
vakṣyāmi niyataṃ mātastanme nigadataḥ śṛṇu||1||

2brāhmaṇo guṇavānkaściddhanenopanimantryatām
|
vicitravīryakṣetreṣu yaḥ samutpādayetprajāḥ||2||

3vaiśaṃpāyana uvāca|
tataḥ satyavatī bhīṣmaṃ vācā saṃsajjamānayā
|
vihasantīva savrīḍamidaṃ vacanamabravīt||3||

4satyametanmahābāho yathā vadasi bhārata
|
viśvāsātte pravakṣyāmi santānāya kulasya naḥ||4||

5na te śakyamanākhyātumāpaddharmaṃ tathāvidham
|
tvameva naḥ kule dharmastvaṃ satyaṃ tvaṃ parā gatiḥ||5||

6tasmānniśamya satyaṃ me kuruṣva yadanantaram
|
dharmayuktasya dharmārthaṃ piturāsīttarī mama||6||

7sā kadācidahaṃ tatra gatā prathamayauvanam
|
atha dharmavidāṃ śreṣṭhaḥ paramarṣiḥ parāśaraḥ||7||

8ājagāma tarīṃ dhīmāṃstariṣyanyamunāṃ nadīm
|
sa tāryamāṇo yamunāṃ māmupetyābravīttadā||8||

9sāntvapūrvaṃ muniśreṣṭhaḥ kāmārto madhuraṃ vacaḥ
|
uktaṃ janmakulaṃ mahyamasmi dāśasutetyaham||9||

10tamahaṃ śāpabhītā ca piturbhītā ca bhārata
|
varairasulabhairuktā na pratyākhyātumutsahe||10||

11abhibhūya sa māṃ bālāṃ tejasā vaśamānayat
|
tamasā lokamāvṛtya naugatāmeva bhārata||11||

12matsyagandho mahānāsītpurā mama jugupsitaḥ
|
tamapāsya śubhaṃ gandhamimaṃ prādātsa me muniḥ||12||

13tato māmāha sa munirgarbhamutsṛjya māmakam
|
dvīpe'syā eva saritaḥ kanyaiva tvaṃ bhaviṣyasi||13||

14pārāśaryo mahāyogī sa babhūva mahānṛṣiḥ
|
kanyāputro mama purā dvaipāyana iti śrutaḥ||14||

15yo vyasya vedāṃścaturastapasā bhagavānṛṣiḥ
|
loke vyāsatvamāpede kārṣṇyātkṛṣṇatvameva ca||15||

16satyavādī śamaparastapasvī dagdhakilbiṣaḥ
|
samutpannaḥ sa tu mahānsaha pitrā tato gataḥ||16||

17sa niyukto mayā vyaktaṃ tvayā cāpratimadyutiḥ
|
bhrātuḥ kṣetreṣu kalyāṇamapatyaṃ janayiṣyati||17||

18sa hi māmuktavāṃstatra smareḥ kṛcchreṣu māmiti
|
taṃ smariṣye mahābāho yadi bhīṣma tvamicchasi||18||

19tava hyanumate bhīṣma niyataṃ sa mahātapāḥ
|
vicitravīryakṣetreṣu putrānutpādayiṣyati||19||

20vaiśaṃpāyana uvāca|
maharṣeḥ kīrtane tasya bhīṣmaḥ prāñjalirabravīt
|
dharmamarthaṃ ca kāmaṃ ca trīnetānyonupaśyati||20||

21arthamarthānubandhaṃ ca dharmaṃ dharmānubandhanam
|
kāmaṃ kāmānubandhaṃ ca viparītānpṛthakpṛthak||21||

22yo vicintya dhiyā dhīro vyavasyati sa buddhimān
|
tadidaṃ dharmayuktaṃ ca hitaṃ caiva kulasya naḥ||22||

23uktaṃ bhavatyā yacchreyastanmahyaṃ rocate bhṛśam
|
vaiśaṃpāyana uvāca|
tatastasminpratijñāte bhīṣmeṇa kurunandana||23||

24kṛṣṇadvaipāyanaṃ kālī cintayāmāsa vai munim
|
sa vedānvibruvandhīmānmāturvijñāya cintitam||24||

25prādurbabhūvāviditaḥ kṣaṇena kurunandana
|
tasmai pūjāṃ tataḥ kṛtvā sutāya vidhipūrvakam||25||

26pariṣvajya ca bāhubhyāṃ prasravairabhyaṣiñcata
|
mumoca bāṣpaṃ dāśeyī putraṃ dṛṣṭvā cirasya tu||26||

27tāmadbhiḥ pariṣicyārtāṃ maharṣirabhivādya ca
|
mātaraṃ pūrvajaḥ putro vyāso vacanamabravīt||27||

28bhavatyā yadabhipretaṃ tadahaṃ kartumāgataḥ
|
śādhi māṃ dharmatattvajñe karavāṇi priyaṃ tava||28||

29tasmai pūjāṃ tato'kārṣītpurodhāḥ paramarṣaye
|
sa ca tāṃ pratijagrāha vidhimanmantrapūrvakam||29||

30pūjito mantrapūrvaṃ tu vidhivatprītimāpa saḥ
|
tamāsanagataṃ mātā pṛṣṭvā kuśalamavyayam||30||

31satyavatyatha vīkṣyainamuvācedamanantaram
|
mātāpitroḥ prajāyante putrāḥ sādhāraṇāḥ kave||31||

32teṣāṃ pitā yathā svīmī tathā mātā na saṃśayaḥ
|
vidhānavihitaḥ sa tvaṃ yathā me prathamaḥ sutaḥ||32||

33vicitravīryo brahmarṣe tathā me'varajaḥ sutaḥ
|
yathaiva pitṛto bhīṣmastathā tvamapi mātṛtaḥ||33||

34bhrātā vicitravīryasya yathā vā putra manyase
|
ayaṃ śāntanavaḥ satyaṃ pālayansatyavikramaḥ||34||

35buddhiṃ na kurute'patye tathā rājyā'nuśāsane
|
sa tvaṃ vyapekṣayā bhrātuḥ santānāya kulasya ca||35||

36bhīṣmasya cāsya vacanānniyogācca mamānagha
|
anukrośācca bhūtānāṃ sarveṣāṃ rakṣaṇāya ca||36||

37ānṛśaṃsyācca yadbrūyāṃ tacchrutvā kartumarhasi
|
yavīyasastava bhrāturbhārye surasutopame||37||

38rūpayauvanasaṃpanne putrakāme ca dharmataḥ
|
tayorutpādayāpatyaṃ samartho hyasi putraka||38||

39anurūpaṃ kulasyāsya saṃtatyāḥ prasavasya ca
|
vyāsa uvāca|
vettha dharmaṃ satyavati paraṃ cāparameva ca||39||

40tathā tava mahāprājñe dharme praṇihitā matiḥ
|
tasmādahaṃ tvanniyogāddharmamuddiśya kāraṇam||40||

41īpsitaṃ te kariṣyāmi dṛṣṭaṃ hyetatsanātanam
|
bhrātuḥ putrānpradāsyāmi mitrāvaruṇayoḥ samān||41||

42vrataṃ caretāṃ te devyau nirdiṣṭamiha yanmayā
|
saṃvatsaraṃ yathānyāyaṃ tataḥ śuddhe bhaviṣyataḥ||42||

43nahi māmavratopetā upeyātkācidaṅganā
|
satyavatyuvāca
|
sadyo yathā prapadyete devyau garbhaṃ tathā kuru||43||

44arājakeṣu rāṣṭreṣu prajānāthā vinaśyati
|
naśyanti ca kriyāḥ sarvā nāsti vṛṣṭirna devatā||44||

45kathaṃ cārājakaṃ rāṣṭraṃ śakyaṃ dhārayituṃ prabho
|
tasmādgarbhaṃ samādhatsva bhīṣmaḥ saṃvardhayiṣyati||45||

46vyāsa uvāca|
yadi putraḥ pradātavyo mayā bhrāturakālikaḥ
|
virūpatāṃ me sahatāṃ tayoretatparaṃ vratam||46||

47yadi me sahate gandhaṃ rūpaṃ veṣaṃ tathā vapuḥ
|
adyaiva garbhaṃ kausalyā viśiṣṭaṃ pratipadyatām||47||

48vaiśaṃpāyana uvāca|
evamuktvā mahātejā vyāsaḥ satyavatīṃ tadā
|
śayane sā ca kausalyā śucivastrā hyalaṅkṛtā||48||

49samāgamanamākāṅkṣediti sā'ntarhito muniḥ
|
tato'bhigamya sā devī snuṣāṃ rahasi saṅgatām||49||

50dharmyamarthasamāyuktamuvāca vacanaṃ hitam
|
kausalye dharmatantraṃ tvāṃ yadbravīmi nibodha tat||50||

51bharatānāṃ samucchedo vyaktaṃ madbhāgyasaṃkṣayāt
|
vyathitāṃ māṃ ca saṃprekṣya pitṛvaṃśaṃ ca pīḍitam||51||

52bhīṣmo buddhimadānmahyaṃ kulasyāsya vivṛddhaye
|
sā ca buddhistvayyadhīnā putri prāpaya māṃ tathā||52||

53naṣṭaṃ ca bhārataṃ vaṃśaṃ punareva samuddhaṟa
|
putraṃ janaya suśroṇi devarājasamaprabham||53||

54sa hi rājyadhuraṃ gurvīmudvakṣyati kulasya naḥ
|
sā dharmato'nunīyaināṃ kathaṃciddharmacāriṇīm
|
bhojayāmāsa viprāṃśca devarṣīnatithīṃstathā||54||
