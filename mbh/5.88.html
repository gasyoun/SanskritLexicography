Mahābhārata 5.88 (Udyogaparvan)

1duryodhana uvāca|
yadāha viduraḥ kṛṣṇe sarvaṃ tat satyamacyute
|
anurakto hyasaṃhāryaḥ pārthān prati janārdanaḥ ||1||

2yattatsatkārasaṃyuktaṃ deyaṃ vasu janārdane
|
anekarūpaṃ rājendra na taddeyaṃ kadācana ||2||

3deśaḥ kālastathā'yukto na hi nārhati keśavaḥ
|
maṃsyatyadhokṣajo rājan bhayādarcati māmiti ||3||

4avamānaśca yatra syāt kṣatriyasya viśāmpate
|
na tat kuryādbudhaḥ kāryamiti me niścitā matiḥ ||4||

5sa hi pūjyatamo loke kṛṣṇaḥ pṛthulalocanaḥ
|
trayāṇāmapi lokānāṃ viditaṃ mama sarvathā ||5||

6na tu tasmai pradeyaṃ syāttathā kāryagatiḥ prabho
|
vigrahaḥ samupārabdho na hi śāmyatyavigrahāt ||6||

7vaiśampāyana uvāca|
tasya tadvacanaṃ śrutvā bhīṣmaḥ kurupitāmahaḥ
|
vaicitravīryaṃ rājānāmidaṃ vacanamabravīt ||7||

8satkṛto'satkṛto vā'pi na kruddhyeta janārdanaḥ
|
nālamenamavajñātuṃ nāvajñeyo hi keśavaḥ ||8||

9yattu kāryaṃ mahābāho manasā kāryatāṃ gatam
|
sarvopāyairna tacchakyaṃ kenacit kartumanyathā ||9||

10sa yadbrūyānmahābāhustatkāryamaviśaṅkayā
|
vāsudevena tīrthena kṣipraṃ saṃśāmya pāṇḍavaiḥ ||10||

11dharmyamarthyaṃ ca dharmātmā dhruvaṃ vaktā janārdanaḥ
|
tasmin vācyāḥ priyā vāco bhavatā bāndhavaiḥ saha ||11||

12duryodhana uvāca|
na paryāyo'sti yadrājan śriyaṃ niṣkevalāmaham
|
taiḥ sahemāmupāśnīyāṃ yāvajjīvaṃ pitāmaha ||12||

13idaṃ tu sumahat kāryaṃ śṛṇu me yat samarthitam
|
parāyaṇaṃ pāṇḍavānāṃ niyacchāmi janārdanam ||13||

14tasmin baddhe bhaviṣyanti vṛṣṇayaḥ pṛthivī tathā
|
pāṇḍavāśca vidheyā me sa ca prātarihaiṣyati ||14||

15atropāyān yathā samyaṅna buddhyeta janārdanaḥ
|
na cāpāyo bhavetkaścittadbhavān prabravītu me ||15||

16vaiśampāyana uvāca|
tasya tadvacanaṃ śrutvā ghoraṃ kṛṣṇābhisaṃhitam
|
dhṛtarāṣṭraḥ sahāmātyo vyathito vimanā'bhavat ||16||

17tato duryodhanamidaṃ dhṛtarāṣṭro'bravīdvacaḥ
|
maivaṃ vocaḥ prajāpāla naiṣa dharmaḥ sanātanaḥ ||17||

18dūtaśca hi hṛṣīkeśaḥ sambandhī ca priyaśca naḥ
|
apāpaḥ kauraveyeṣu sa kathaṃ bandhamarhati ||18||

19bhīṣma uvāca|
parītastava putro'yaṃ dhṛtarāṣṭra sumandadhīḥ
|
vṛṇotyanarthaṃ naivārthaṃ yācyamānaḥ suhṛjjanaiḥ ||19||

20imamutpathi vartantaṃ pāpaṃ pāpānubandhinam
|
vākyāni suhṛdāṃ hitvā tvamapyasyānuvartase ||20||

21kṛṣṇamakliṣṭakarmāṇamāsādyāyaṃ sudurmatiḥ
|
tava putraḥ sahāmātyaḥ kṣaṇena na bhaviṣyati ||21||

22pāpasyāsya nṛśaṃsasya tyaktadharmasya durmateḥ
|
notsahe'narthasaṃyuktāḥ śrotuṃ vācaḥ kathañcana ||22||

23ityuktvā bharataśreṣṭho vṛddhaḥ paramamanyumān
|
utthāya tasmāt prātiṣṭhadbhīṣmaḥ satyaparākramaḥ ||23||
