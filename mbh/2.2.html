Mahābhārata 2.2 (Sabhāparvan)

1vaiśampāyana uvāca|
uṣitvā khāṇḍavaprasthe sukhavāsaṃ janārdanaḥ
|
pārthaiḥ prītisamāyuktaiḥ pūjanārho'bhipūjitaḥ||1||

2gamanāya matiṃ cakre piturdarśanalālasaḥ
|
dharmarājamathāmantrya pṛthāṃ ca pṛthulocanaḥ||2||

3vavande caraṇau mūrdhnā jagadvandyaḥ pitṛṣvasuḥ
|
sa tayā mūrdhnyupāghrātaḥ pariṣvaktaśca keśavaḥ||3||

4dadarśānantaraṃ kṛṣṇo bhaginīṃ svāṃ mahāyaśāḥ
|
tāmupetya hṛṣīkeśaḥ prītyā bāṣpasamanvitaḥ||4||

5arthyaṃ tathyaṃ hitaṃ vākyaṃ laghuyuktamanuttaram
|
uvāca bhagavānbhadrāṃ subhadrāṃ bhadrabhāṣiṇīm||5||

6tayā svajanagāmīni śrāvito vacanāni saḥ
|
sampūjitaścāpyasakṛcchirasā cābhivāditaḥ||6||

7tāmanujñāya vārṣṇeyaḥ pratinandya ca bhāminīm
|
dadarśānantaraṃ kṛṣṇāṃ dhaumyaṃ cāpi janārdanaḥ||7||

8vavande ca yathānyāyaṃ dhaumyaṃ puruṣasattamaḥ
|
draupadīṃ sāntvayitvā ca āmantrya ca janārdanaḥ||8||

9bhrātṝnabhyagamadvidvānpārthena sahito balī
|
bhrātṛbhiḥ pañcabhiḥ kṛṣṇo vṛtaḥ śakra ivāmaraiḥ||9||

10yātrākālasya yogyāni karmāṇi garuḍadhvajaḥ
|
kartukāmaḥ śucirbhūtvā snātavānsamalaṅkṛtaḥ||10||

11arcayāmāsa devāṃśca dvijāṃśca yadupuṅgavaḥ
|
mālyajāpyanamaskārairgandhairuccāvacairapi||11||

12sa kṛtvā sarvakāryāṇi pratasthe tasthupāṃ varaḥ
|
upetya sa yaduśreṣṭho bāhyakakṣādvinirgataḥ||12||

13svastivācyārhato viprāndadhipātraphalākṣataiḥ
|
vasu pradāya ca tataḥ pradakṣiṇamathākarot||13||

14kāñcanaṃ rathamāsthāya tārkṣyaketanamāśugam
|
gadācakrāsiśārṅgādyairāyudhairāvṛtaṃ śubham||14||

15satithāvapyatha nakṣatre muhūrte ca guṇānvite
|
prayayau puṇḍarīkākṣaḥ śaibyasugrīvavāhanaḥ||15||

16anvāruroha cāpyenaṃ premṇā rājā yudhiṣṭhiraḥ
|
apāsya cāsya yantāraṃ dārukaṃ yantṛsattamam||16||

17abhīṣūnsamprajagrāha svayaṃ kurupatistadā
|
upāruhyārjunaścā'pi cāmaravyajanaṃ sitam||17||

18rukmadaṇḍaṃ bṛhadbāhurvidudhāva pradakṣiṇam
|
tathaiva bhīmaseno'pi yamābhyāṃ sahito balī||18||

19pṛṣṭhato'nuyayau kṛṣṇamṛtvikpaurajanaiḥ saha
|
sa tathā bhrātṛbhiḥ sarvaiḥ keśavaḥ paravīrahā||19||

20anvīyamānaḥ śuśubhe śiṣyairiva guruḥ priyaiḥ
|
pārthamāmantrya govindaḥ pariṣvajya supīḍitam ||20||

21yudhiṣṭharaṃ pūjayitvā bhīmasenaṃ yamau tathā
|
pariṣvakto bhṛśaṃ taistu yamābhyāmabhivāditaḥ||21||

22yojanārdhamatho gatvā kṛṣṇaḥ parapurañjayaḥ
|
yudhiṣṭhiraṃ samāmantrya nivartasveti bhārata||22||

23tato'bhivādya govindaḥ pādau jagrāha dharmavit
|
utthāpya dharmarājastu mūrdhnyupāghrāya keśavam||23||

24pāṇḍavo yādavaśreṣṭhaṃ kṛṣṇaṃ kamalalocanam
|
gamyatāmityanujñāpya dharmarājo yudhiṣṭhiraḥ||24||

25tatastaiḥ saṃvidaṃ kṛtvā yathāvanmadhusūdanaḥ
|
nivartya ca tathā kṛcchrātpāṇḍavānsapadānugān||25||

26svāṃ purīṃ prayayau hṛṣṭo yathā śakro'marāvatīm
|
locanairanujagmuste tamādṛṣṭipathāttadā||26||

27manobhiranujagmuste kṛṣṇaṃ prītisamanvayāt
|
atṛptamanasāmeva teṣāṃ keśavadarśane||27||

28kṣipramantardadhe śauriścakṣuṣāṃ priyadarśanaḥ
|
akāmā eva pārthāste govindagamānasāḥ||28||

29nivṛtyopayayustūrṇaṃ svaṃ puraṃ puruṣarṣabhāḥ
|
syandanenātha kṛṣṇo'pi tvaritaṃ dvārakāmagāt||29||

30sātvatena ca vīreṇa pṛṣṭhato yāyinā tadā
|
dārukeṇa ca sūtena sahito devakīsutaḥ
|
sa gato dvārakāṃ viṣṇurgarutmāniva vegavān||30||

31vaiśampāyana uvāca|
nivṛtya dharmarājastu saha bhrātṛbhiracyutaḥ
|
suhṛtparivṛto rājā praviveśa purottamam||31||

32visṛjya suhṛdaḥ sarvānbhrātṛnputrāṃśca dharmarāṭ
|
mumoda puruṣavyāghro draupadyā sahito nṛpa||32||

33keśavopi mudā yuktaḥ praviveśa purottamam
|
pūjyamāno yaduśreṣṭhairugrasenamukhaistathā||33||

34āhukaṃ pitaraṃ vṛddhaṃ mātaraṃ ca yaśasvinīm
|
abhivādya balaṃ caiva sthitaḥ kamalalocanaḥ||34||

35pradyumnasāmbaniśaṭhāṃścārudeṣṇaṃ gadaṃ tathā
|
aniruddhaṃ ca bhānuṃ ca pariṣvajya janārdanaḥ||35||

36sa vṛddhairabhyanujñāto rukmiṇyā bhavanaṃ yayau
|
mayo'pi sa mahābhāgaḥ sarvaratnavibhūṣitām
|
vidhivatkalpayāmāsa sabhāṃ dharmasutāya vai||36||
