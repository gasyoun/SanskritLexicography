Mahābhārata 13.92 (Anuśāsanaparvan)

1bhīṣma uvāca|
tathā nimau pravṛtte tu sarva eva maharṣayaḥ
|
pitṛyajñaṃ tu kurvanti vidhidṛṣṭena karmaṇā ||1||

2ṛṣayo dharmanityāstu kṛtvā nivapanānyuta
|
tarpaṇaṃ cāpyakurvanta tīrthāṃbhobhiryatavratāḥ ||2||

3nivāpairdīyamānaiśca cāturvarṇyena bhārata
|
tarpitāḥ pitaro devāstatrānnaṃ jarayanti vai ||3||

4ajīrṇaistvabhihanyante te devāḥ pitṛbhiḥ saha
|
somamevābhyapadyanta tadā hyannābhipīḍitāḥ ||4||

5te'bruvansomamāsādya pitaro'jīrṇapīḍitāḥ
|
nivāpānnena pīḍyāmaḥ śreyo no'tra vidhīyatām ||5||

6tānsomaḥ pratyuvācātha śreyaścedīpsitaṃ surāḥ
|
svayaṃbhūsadanaṃ yāta sa vaḥ śreyobhidhāsyati ||6||

7te somavacanāddevāḥ pitṛbhiḥ saha bhārata
|
meruśṛṃge samāsīnaṃ pitāmahamupāgaman ||7||

8pitara ūcuḥ |
nivāpānnena bhagavanbhṛśaṃ pīḍyāmahe vayam
|
prasādaṃ kuru no deva śreyo naḥ saṃvidhīyatām ||8||

9iti teṣāṃ vacaḥ śrutvā svayaṃbhūridamabravīt
|
eṣa me pārśvato vahniryuṣmacchreyobhidhāsyati ||9||

10agniruvāca|
sahitāstāta bhokṣyāmo nivāpe samupasthite
|
jarayiṣyatha cāpyannaṃ mayā sārdhaṃ na saṃśayaḥ ||10||

11etacchrutvā tu pitarastataste vijvarā bhavan
|
etasmātkāraṇāccāgneḥ prāktāvaddīyate nṛpa ||11||

12nivapte cāgnipūrvaṃ vai nivāpe puruṣarṣabha
|
na brahmarākṣasāstaṃ vai nivāpaṃ dharṣayantyuta ||12||

13rakṣāṃsi cāpavartante sthite deve hutāśane
|
pūrvaṃ piṇḍaṃ piturdadyāttato dadyātpitāmahe ||13||

14prapitāmahāya ca tata eṣa śrāddhavidhiḥ smṛtaḥ
|
būyāt śrāddhe ca sāvitrīṃ piṃḍe piṃḍe samāhitaḥ ||14||

15somāyeti ca vaktavyaṃ tathā pitṛmateti ca
|
rajasvalā ca yā nārī vyaṅgitā karṇayośca yā
|
nivāpe nopatiṣṭheta saṃgrāhyā nānyavaṃśajā ||15||

16jalaṃ prataramāṇaśca kīrtayeta pitāmahān
|
nadīmāsādya kurvīta pitṝṇāṃ piṇḍatarpaṇam ||16||

17pūrvaṃ svavaṃśajānāṃ tu kṛtvā'dbhistarpaṇaṃ punaḥ
|
suhṛtsambandhivargāṇāṃ tato da‌dyājjalāñjalim ||17||

18kalmāṣagoyugenātha yuktena tarato jalam
|
pitaro'bhilaṣante vai nāvaṃ cāpyadhirohitāḥ ||18||

19sadā nāvi jalaṃ tajjñāḥ prayacchanti samāhitāḥ
|
māsārdhe kṛṣṇapakṣasya kuryānnirvapaṇāni vai ||19||

20puṣṭirāyustathā vīryaṃ śrīścaiva pitṛbhaktitaḥ
|
pitāmahaḥ pulastyaśca vasiṣṭhaḥ pulahastathā||20||

21aṅgirāśca kratuścaiva kaśyapaśca mahānṛṣiḥ
|
ete kurukulaśreṣṭha mahāyogeśvarāḥ smṛtāḥ ||21||

22ete ca pitaro rājanneṣa śrāddhavidhiḥ paraḥ
|
pretāstu piṇḍasambandhānmucyante tena karmaṇā ||22||

23ityeṣā puruṣaśreṣṭha śrāddhotpattiryathāgamam
|
vyākhyātā pūrvanirdiṣṭā dānaṃ vakṣyāmyataḥ param ||23||
