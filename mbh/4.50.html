Mahābhārata 4.50 (Virāṭaparvan)

1aśvatthāmovāca
|
na ca tāvajjitā gāvo na ca sīmāntaraṃ gatāḥ
|
na hāstinapuraṃ prāptāstvaṃ ca karṇa vikatthase ||1||

2saṅgrāmāṃśca bahūñjitvā labdhvā ca vipulaṃ dhanam
|
vijitya ca parāṃ senāṃ nāhuḥ kiṃcana pauruṣam ||2||

3dahatyagniravākyastu tūṣṇīṃ bhāti divākaraḥ
|
tūṣṇīṃ dhārayate lokān vasudhā sacarācarān ||3||

4cāturvarṇyasya karmāṇi vihitāni svayaṃbhuvā
|
dhanaṃ yairadhigantavyaṃ yacca kurvanna duṣyati ||4||

5adhītya brāhmaṇo vedān yājayeta yajeta vā
|
kṣatriyo dhanurāśritya yajeccaiva na yājayet ||5||

6vaiśyo'dhigamya vittāni brahmakarmāṇi kārayet
|
śūdraḥ śuśrūṣaṇaṃ kuryāstriṣu varṇeṣu nityaśaḥ
|
vandanāyogavidhibhirvaitasīṃ vṛttimāsthitaḥ ||6||

7vartamānā yathāśāstraṃ prāpya cāpi mahīmimām
|
satkurvanti mahābhāgā gurūn suviguṇānapi ||7||

8prāpya dyūtena ko rājyaṃ kṣatriyastoṣṭumarhati
|
tathā nṛśaṃsarūpo'yaṃ dhārtarāṣṭraśca nirghṛṇaḥ ||8||

9tathā'dhigamya vittāni ko vikatthedvicakṣaṇaḥ
|
nikṛtyā vañcanāyogaiścaran vaitaṃsiko yathā ||9||

10katamaṃ dvairathaṃ yuddhaṃ yatrājaiṣīrdhanañjayam
|
nakulaṃ sahadevaṃ vā dhanaṃ yeṣāṃ tvayā hṛtam ||10||

11yudhiṣṭhiro jitaḥ kasmin bhīmaśca balināṃ varaḥ
|
indraprasthaṃ tvayā kasmin saṅgrāme nirjitaṃ purā ||11||

12tathaiva katamadyuddhaṃ yasmin kṛṣṇā jitā tvayā
|
ekavastrā sabhāṃ nītā duṣṭakarman rajasvalā ||12||

13mūlameṣāṃ mahatkṛttaṃ sārārthī candanaṃ yathā
|
karma kārayithāḥ sūta tatra kiṃ viduro'bravīt ||13||

14yathāśakti manuṣyāṇāṃ śamamālakṣayāmahe
|
anyeṣāmapi sattvānāmapi kīṭapipīlikaiḥ
|
draupadyāḥ saṃparikleśaṃ na kṣantuṃ pāṇḍavo'rhati ||14||

15kṣayāya dhārtarāṣṭrāṇāṃ prādurbhūto dhanañjayaḥ
|
tvaṃ punaḥ paṇḍito bhūtvā vācaṃ vaktumihecchasi ||15||

16vairāntakaraṇo jiṣṇurna naḥ śeṣaṃ kariṣyati ||16||

17naiṣa devān na gandharvān na surān na ca rākṣasān
|
bhayādiha na yudhyeta kuntīputro dhanañjayaḥ ||17||

18yaṃ yameṣo'tisaṃkruddhaḥ saṅgrāme nipatiṣyati
|
vṛkṣaṃ garutmān vegena vinihatya tameṣyati ||18||

19tvatto viśiṣṭaṃ vīryeṇa dhanuṣyamararāṭsamam
|
vāsudevasamaṃ yuddhe taṃ pārthaṃ ko na pūjayet ||19||

20devaṃ devena yudhyeta mānuṣeṇa ca mānuṣam
|
astraṃ hyastreṇa yo hanyāt ko'rjunena samaḥ pumān ||20||

21putrādanantaraṃ śiṣya iti dharmavido viduḥ
|
etenāpi nimittena priyo droṇasya pāṇḍavaḥ ||21||

22yathā tvamakarordyūtamindraprasthaṃ yathā'haraḥ
|
yathā''naiṣīḥ sabhāṃ kṛṣṇāṃ tathā yudhyasva pāṇḍavam ||22||

23ayaṃ te mātulaḥ prājñaḥ kṣatradharmasya kovidaḥ
|
durdyūtadevī gāndhāraḥ śakuniryudhyatāmiha ||23||

24nākṣān kṣipati gāṇḍīvaṃ na kṛtaṃ dvāparaṃ na ca
|
jvalato niśitānbāṇāṃstāṃstāṃnkṣipati gāṇḍivam ||24||

25na hi gāṇḍīvanirmuktā gārdhrapakṣāḥ sutejanāḥ
|
nāntareṣvavatiṣṭhante girīṇāmapi dāraṇāḥ ||25||

26antakaḥ pavano mṛtyustathā'gnirvaḍavāmukhaḥ
|
kuryurete kvaciccheṣaṃ na tu kruddho dhanañjayaḥ ||26||

27yathā sabhāyāṃ dyūtaṃ tvaṃ mātulena sahākaroḥ
|
tathā yudhyasva saṅgrāme saubalena surakṣitaḥ ||27||

28yudhyantāṃ kāmato yodhā nāhaṃ yotsye dhanañjayam
|
matsyo hyasmābhirāyodhyo yadyāgacchedgavāṃ padam ||28||
