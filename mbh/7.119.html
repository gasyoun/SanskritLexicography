Mahābhārata 7.119 (Droṇaparvan)

1sañjaya uvāca|
tataḥ sa sātyakirdhīmānmahātmā vṛṣṇipuṅgavaḥ
|
sudarśanaṃ nihatyājau yantāraṃ punarabravīt ||1||

2rathāśvanāgakalilaṃ śaraśaktyūrmimālinam
|
khaḍgamatsyaṃ gadāgrāhaṃ śūrāyudhamahāsvanam ||2||

3prāṇāpahāriṇaṃ raudraṃ vāditrotkruṣṭanāditam
|
yodhānāmasukhasparśaṃ durdharṣamajayaiṣiṇām ||3||

4tīrṇāḥ sma dustaraṃ tāta droṇānīkamahārṇavam
|
jalasandhabalenājau puruṣādairivāvṛtam ||4||

5ato'nyat pṛtanāśeṣaṃ manye kunadikāmiva
|
tartavyāmalpasalilāṃ codayāśvānasaṃbhramam||5||

6hastaprāptamahaṃ manye sāṃprataṃ savyasācinam
|
nirjitya durdharaṃ droṇaṃ sapadānugamāhave||6||

7hārdikyaṃ yodhavaryaṃ ca manye prāptaṃ dhanañjayam
|
na hi me jāyate trāso dṛṣṭvā sainyānyanekaśaḥ||7||

8vahneriva pradīptasya vane śuṣkatṛṇolape
|
paśya pāṇḍavamukhyena yātāṃ bhūmiṃ kirīṭinā||8||

9pattyaśvarathanāgaughaiḥ patitairviṣamīkṛtām
|
dravate tadyathā sainyaṃ tena bhagnaṃ mahātmanā ||9||

10rathairviparidhāvadbhirgajairaśvaiśca sārathe
|
kauśeyāruṇasaṅkāśametaduddhūyate rajaḥ||10||

11abhyāśasthamahaṃ manye śvetāśvaṃ kṛṣṇasārathim
|
sa eṣa śrūyate śabdo gāṇḍīvasyāmitaujasaḥ||11||

12yādṛśāni nimittāni mama prādurbhavanti vai
|
anastaṃgata āditye hantā saindhavamarjunaḥ||12||

13śanairviśrambhayannaśvān yāhi yatrārivāhinī
|
yatraite satalatrāṇāḥ suyodhanapurogamāḥ||13||

14daṃśitāḥ krūrakarmāṇaḥ kāmbojā yuddhadurmadāḥ
|
śarabāṇāsanadharā yavanāśca prahāriṇaḥ||14||

15śakāḥ kirātā daradā barbarāstāmraliptakāḥ
|
anye ca bahavo mlecchā vividhāyudhapāṇayaḥ||15||

16yatraite satalatrāṇāḥ suyodhanapurogamāḥ
|
māmevābhimukhāḥ sarve tiṣṭhanti samarārthinaḥ||16||

17etān sarathanāgāśvānnihatyājau sapattinaḥ
|
idaṃ durgaṃ mahāghoraṃ tīrṇamevopadhāraya||17||

18sūta uvāca|
na saṃbhramo me vārṣṇeya vidyate satyavikrama
|
yadyapi syāttava kruddho jāmadagnyo'grataḥ sthitaḥ||18||

19droṇo vā rathināṃ śreṣṭhaḥ kṛpo madreśvaro'pi vā
|
tathāpi saṃbhramo na syāttvāmāśritya mahābhuja||19||

20tvayā subahavo yuddhe nirjitāḥ śatrusūdana
|
daṃśitāḥ krūrakarmāṇaḥ kāmbojā yuddhadurmadāḥ||20||

21śarabāṇāsanadharā yavanāśca prahāriṇaḥ
|
śakāḥ kirātā daradā barbarāstāmraliptakāḥ||21||

22anye ca bahavo mlecchā vividhāyudhapāṇayaḥ
|
na ca me saṃbhramaḥ kaścidbhūtapūrvaḥ kathañcana||22||

23kimutaitat samāsādya dhīrasaṃyugagoṣpadam
|
āyuṣman katareṇa tvāṃ prāpayāmi dhanañjayam||23||

24keṣāṃ kruddho'si vārṣṇeya keṣāṃ mṛtyurupasthitaḥ
|
keṣāṃ saṃyamanīmadya gantumutsahate manaḥ||24||

25ke tvāṃ yudhi parākrāntaṃ kālāntakayamopamam
|
dṛṣṭvā vikramasaṃpannaṃ vidraviṣyanti saṃyuge||25||

26keṣāṃ vaivasvato rājā smarate'dya mahābhuja
|
sātyakiruvāca| muṇḍānetān haniṣyāmi dānavāniva vāsavaḥ||26||

27pratikṣāṃ pārayiṣyāmi kāmbojāneva māṃ vaha
|
adyaiṣāṃ kadanaṃ kṛtvā priyaṃ yāsyāmi pāṇḍavam||27||

28adya drakṣyanti me vīryaṃ kauravāḥ sasuyodhanāḥ
|
muṇḍānīke hate sūta sarvasainyeṣu cāsakṛt||28||

29adya kauravasainyasya dīryamāṇasya saṃyuge
|
śrutvā virāvaṃ bahudhā santapsyati suyodhanaḥ||29||

30adya pāṇḍavamukhyasya śvetāśvasya mahātmanaḥ
|
ācāryasya kṛtaṃ mārgaṃ darśayiṣyāmi saṃyuge ||30||

31adya madbāṇanihatān yodhamukhyān sahasraśaḥ
|
dṛṣṭvā duryodhano rājā paścāttāpaṃ gamiṣyati ||31||

32adya me kṣiprahastasya kṣipataḥ sāyakottamān
|
alātacakrapratimaṃ dhanurdrakṣyanti kauravāḥ||32||

33matsāyakacitāṅgānāṃ rudhiraṃ sravatāṃ muhuḥ
|
sainikānāṃ vadhaṃ dṛṣṭvā santapsyati suyodhanaḥ||33||

34adya meṃ kruddharūpasya nighnataśca varān varān
|
dvirarjunamimaṃ lokaṃ maṃsyate'dya suyodhanaḥ||34||

35adya rājasahasrāṇi nihatāni mayā raṇe
|
dṛṣṭvā duryodhano rājā santapsyati mahāmṛdhe||35||

36adya snehaṃ ca bhaktiṃ ca pāṇḍaveṣu mahātmasu
|
hatvā rājasahastrāṇi darśayiṣyāmi rājasu||36||

37balaṃ vīryaṃ kṛtajñatvaṃ mama jñāsyanti kauravāḥ
|
sañjaya uvāca|
evamuktastadā sūtaḥ śikṣitān sādhuvāhinaḥ||37||

38śaśāṅkasannikāśānvai vājino vyanudadbhṛśam
|
te pibanta ivākāśaṃ yuyudhānaṃ hayottamāḥ||38||

39prāpayan yavanāñśīghraṃ manaḥpavanaraṃhasaḥ
|
sātyakiṃ te samāsādya pṛtanāsvanivartinam||39||

40bahavo laghuhastāśca śaravarṣairavākiran
|
teṣāmiṣūnathāstrāṇi vegavānnataparvabhiḥ||40||

41acchinat sātyakī rājannainaṃ te prāpnuvañśarāḥ
|
rukmapuṅkhaiḥ suniśitairgārdhrapatrairajihmagaiḥ||41||

42uccakarta śirāṃsyugro yavanānāṃ bhujānapi
|
śaikyāyasāni varmāṇi kāṃsyāni ca samantataḥ||42||

43bhittvā dehāṃstathā teṣāṃ śarā jagmurmahītalam
|
te hanyamānā vīreṇa mlecchāḥ sātyakinā raṇe ||43||

44śataśo'bhyapataṃstatra vyasavo vasudhātale
|
supūrṇāyatamuktaistānavyavacchinnapiṇḍitaiḥ||44||

45pañca ṣaṭ sapta cāṣṭau ca bibheda yavanāñśaraiḥ
|
kāmbojānāṃ sahasraiśca śakānāṃ ca viśāṃpate||45||

46śabarāṇāṃ kirātānāṃ barbarāṇāṃ tathaiva ca
|
agamyarūpāṃ pṛthivīṃ māṃsaśoṇitakardamām||46||

47kṛtavāṃstatra śaineyaḥ kṣapayaṃstāvakaṃ balam
|
dasyūnāṃ saśirastrāṇaiḥ śirobhirlūnamūrdhajaiḥ||47||

48dīrghakūrcairmahī kīrṇā vibarhairaṇḍajairiva
|
rudhirokṣitasarvāṅgaistaistadāyodhanaṃ babhau ||48||

49kabandhaiḥ saṃvṛtaṃ sarvaṃ tāmrābhraiḥ khamivāvṛtam
|
vajrāśanisamasparśaiḥ suparvabhirajihmagaiḥ||49||

50te sātvatena nihatāḥ samāvavrurvasundharām
|
alpāvaśiṣṭāḥ saṃbhagnāḥ kṛcchramāṇā vicetasaḥ||50||

51jitāḥ saṃkhye mahārāja yuyudhānena daṃśitāḥ
|
pārṣṇibhiśca kaśābhiśca tāḍayantasturaṅgamān||51||

52javamuttamamāsthāya sarvataḥ prādravan bhayāt
|
kāmbojasainyaṃ vidrāvya durjayaṃ yudhi bhārata||52||

53yavanānāṃ ca tatsainyaṃ śakānāṃ ca mahadbalam
|
tataḥ sa puruṣavyāghraḥ sātyakiḥ satyavikramaḥ||53||

54praviṣṭastāvakāñjitvā sūtaṃ yāhītyacodayat
|
tattasya samare karma dṛṣṭvā'nyairakṛtaṃ purā||54||

55cāraṇāḥ sahagandharvāḥ pūjayāṃcakrire bhṛśam
|
taṃ yāntaṃ pṛṣṭhagoptāramarjunasya viśāṃpate
|
cāraṇāḥ prekṣya saṃhṛṣṭāstvadīyāścābhyapūjayan||55||
