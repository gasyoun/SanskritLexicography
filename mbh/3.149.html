Mahābhārata 3.149 (Āraṇyakaparvan)

1vaiśaṃpāyana uvāca|
evamukto mahāvāhurbhīmasenaḥ pratāpavān
|
praṇipatya tataḥ prītyā bhrātaraṃ hṛṣṭamānasaḥ||1||

2uvāca ślakṣṇayā vācā hanūmantaṃ kapīśvaram
|
mayā dhanyataro nāsti yadāryaṃ dṛṣṭavānaham||2||

3anugraho me sumahāṃstṛptiśca tava darśanāt
|
evaṃ tu kṛtamicchāmi tvayādya priyamātmanaḥ||3||

4yattvetadāsītplavataḥ sāgaraṃ makarālayam
|
rūpamapratimaṃ vīra tadicchāmi nirīkṣitum||4||

5evaṃ tuṣṭo bhaviṣyāmi śraddhāsyāmi ca te vacaḥ
|
evamuktaḥ sa tejasvī prahasya harirabravīt||5||

6na tacchakyaṃ tvayā draṣṭuṃ rūpaṃ nānyena kenacit
|
kālāvasthā tadā hyanyā na sā vartati sāṃpratam||6||

7anyaḥ kṛtayuge kālastretāyāṃ dvāpare paraḥ
|
ayaṃ pradhvaṃsanaḥ kālā nādya tadrūpamasti ma||7||

8bhūmirnadyo nagāḥ śailāḥ siddhā devā maharṣayaḥ
|
kālaṃ samanuvartante yathā bhāvā yuge yuge||8||

9balavarṣmaprabhāvā hi prahīyantyudbhavanti ca
|
tadalaṃ bata tadrūpaṃ draṣṭuṃ kurukulodvaha
|
yugaṃ samanuvartāmi kālo hi duratikramaḥ||9||

10bhīma uvāca|
yugasaṃkhyāṃ samācakṣva ācāraṃ ca yuge yuge
|
dharmakāmārthabhāvāṃśca karmavīrye bhavābhavau||10||

11hanūmānuvāca|
kṛtaṃ nāma yugaṃ tāta yatra dharmaḥ sanātanaḥ
|
kṛtameva na kartavyaṃ tasminkāle yugottame||11||

12na tatra dharmāḥ sīda‌nti kṣīyante na ca vai prajāḥ
|
tataḥ kṛtayugaṃ nāma kālena guṇatāṃ gatam||12||

13devadānavagandharvayakṣarākṣasapannagāḥ
|
nāsankṛtayuge tāta tadā na krayavikrayaḥ||13||

14na sāmaṛgyajurvarṇāḥ kriyā nāsīcca mānavī
|
abhidhyāya phalaṃ tatra dharmaḥ saṃnyāsa eva ca||14||

15na tasminyugasaṃsarge vyādhayo nendriyakṣayaḥ
|
nāsūyā nāpi ruditaṃ na darpo nāpi vaikṛtam||15||

16na vigrahaḥ kutastandrī na dveṣo na ca paiśunam
|
na bhayaṃ nāpi saṃtāpo na cerṣyā na ca matsaraḥ||16||

17tataḥ paramakaṃ brahma sā gatiryogināṃ parā
|
ātmāḥ ca sarvabhūtānāṃ śuklo nārāyaṇastadā||17||

18brāhmaṇāḥ kṣatriyā vaiśyāḥ śūdrāśca kṛtalakṣaṇāḥ
|
kṛte yuge samabhavansvakarmaniratāḥ prajāḥ||18||

19samāśrayaṃ samācāraṃ samajñānaṃ ca kevalam
|
tadā hi samakarmāṇo varṇā dharmānavāpnuvan||19||

20ekadevaladāyuktā ekamantravidhikriyāḥ
|
pṛthagdharmāstvekavedā dharmamekamanuvratāḥ||20||

21cāturāśramyayuktena karmaṇā kālayoginā
|
akāma phalasaṃyogātprāpnuvanti parāṃ gatim||21||

22ātmayogasamāyukto dharmo'yaṃ kṛtalakṣaṇaḥ
|
kṛte yuge catuṣpādaścāturvarṇyasya śāśvataḥ||22||

23etatkṛtayugaṃ nāma traiguṇyaparivarjitam
|
tretāmapi nibodha tvaṃ yasminsatraṃ pravartate||23||

24pādena hrasate dharmo raktatāṃ yāti cācyutaḥ
|
satyapravṛttāśca narāḥ kriyādharmaparāyaṇāḥ||24||

25tato yajñāḥ pravartante dharmāśca vividhāḥ kriyāḥ
|
tretāyāṃ bhāvasaṃkalpāḥ kriyādānaphalopagāḥ||25||

26pracalanti na vai dharmāttapodānaparāyaṇāḥ
|
svadharmasthāḥ kriyāvanto narāstretāyuge'bhavan||26||

27dvāpare ca yuge dharmo dvibhāgonaḥ pravartate
|
viṣṇurvai pītatāṃ yāti caturdhā veda eva ca||27||

28tato'nye ca caturvedāstrivedāśca tathāpare
|
dvivedāścaikavedāścāpyanṛcaśca tathāpare||28||

29evaṃ śāstreṣu bhinneṣu bahudhā nīyate kriyā
|
tapodānapravṛttā ca rājasī bhavati prajā||29||

30ekadevasya cājñānādvedāste bahavaḥ kṛtāḥ
|
sattvasya ceha vibhraṃśātsatye kaścidavasthitaḥ||30||

31satyātpracyavamānānāṃ vyādhayo bahavo'bhavan
|
kāmāścopadravāścaiva tadā vai daivakāritāḥ||31||

32yairardyamānāḥ subhṛśaṃ tapastapyanti mānavāḥ
|
kāmakāmāḥ svargakāmā yajñāṃstanvanti cāpare||32||

33evaṃ dvāparamāsādya prajāḥ kṣīyantyadharmataḥ
|
pādainaikena kaunteya dharmaḥ kaliyuge sthitaḥ||33||

34tāmasaṃ yugamāsādya kṛṣṇo bhavati keśavaḥ
|
vedācārāḥ praśāmyanti dharmayajñakriyāstathā||34||

35ītayo vyādhayastandrī doṣāḥ krodhādayastathā
|
upadravāḥ pravartante ādhayaḥ kṣudbhayaṃ tathā||35||

36yugeṣvāvartamāneṣu dharmo vyāvartate punaḥ
|
dharme vyāvartamāne tu loko vyāvartate punaḥ||36||

37loke kṣīṇe kṣayaṃ yānti bhāvā lokapravartakāḥ
|
yugakṣayakṛtā dharmāḥ prārthanāni vikurvate||37||

38etatkaliyugaṃ nāma acirādyatpravartate
|
yugānuvartanaṃ tvetatkurvanti cirajīvinaḥ||38||

39yacca te matparijñāne kautūhalamariṃdama
|
anarthakeṣu ko bhāvaḥ puruṣasya vijānataḥ||39||

40ettate sarvamākhyātaṃ yanmāṃ tvaṃ paripṛcchasi
|
yugasaṃkhyāṃ mahābāho svasti prāpnuhi gamyatām||40||
