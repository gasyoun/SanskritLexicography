Mahābhārata 8.57 (Karṇaparvan)

1sañjaya uvāca|
duryodhanastataḥ karṇamupetya bharatarṣabha
|
abravīnmadrarājaṃ ca tathaivānyāṃśca pārthivān ||1||

2yadṛcchayaitatsaṃprāptaṃ svargadvāramapāvṛtam
|
sukhinaḥ kṣatriyāḥ karṇa labhante yuddhamīdṛśam ||2||

3sadṛśaiḥ kṣatriyaiḥ śūraiḥ śūrāṇāṃ yuddhyatāṃ yudhi
|
iṣṭaṃ bhavati rādheya tadidaṃ samupasthitam ||3||

4hatvā ca pāṇḍavānyuddhesphītāmurvīmavāpsyatha
|
nihatā vā parairyuddhe vīralokamavāpsyatha ||4||

5duryodhanasya tacchrutvā vacanaṃ kṣatriyarṣabhāḥ
|
hṛṣṭā nādānudakrośanvāditrāṇi ca sarvaśaḥ ||5||

6tataḥ pramudite tasmin duryodhanabale tadā
|
harṣayaṃstāvakānyodhān drauṇirvacanamabravīt ||6||

7pratyakṣaṃ sarvasainyānāṃ bhavatāṃ cāpi paśyatām
|
nyastaśastro mama pitā dhṛṣṭadyumnena pātitaḥ ||7||

8sa tenāhamamarṣeṇa mitrārthe cāpi pārthivāḥ
|
satyaṃ vaḥ pratijānāmi tadvākyaṃ me nibodhata ||8||

9dhṛṣṭadyumnamahatvā'haṃ na vimokṣyāmi daṃśanam
|
anṛtāyāṃ pratijñāyāṃ nāhaṃ svargamavāpnuyām ||9||

10arjuno bhīmasenaśca yodho yo rakṣitā raṇe
|
dhṛṣṭadyumnasya taṃ saṃkhye nihaniṣyāmi sāyakaiḥ ||10||

11evamukte tataḥ sarvā sahitā bhāratī camūḥ
|
abhyadravata kaunteyāṃstathā te cāpi pāṇḍavāḥ ||11||

12sa saṃnipāto rathayūthapānāṃ babhūva rājannatibhīmarūpaḥ
|
janakṣayaḥ kālayugāntakalpaḥ prāvartatāgre kurusṛñjayānām ||12||

13tataḥ pravṛtte yudhi saṃprahāre bhūtāni sarvāṇi sadaivatāni
|
āsansametāni sahāpsarobhirdidṛkṣamāṇāni narapravīrān ||13||

14divyaiśca mālyairvividhaiśca gandhairdivyaiśca ratnairvividhairnarāgryān
|
raṇe svakarmodvahataḥ pravīrānavākirannapsarasaḥ prahṛṣṭāḥ ||14||

15samīraṇastāṃśca niṣevya gandhān siṣeva sarvānapi yodhamukhyān
|
niṣevyamāṇāstvanilena yodhāḥ parasparaghnā dharaṇīṃ nipetuḥ ||15||

16sā divyamālyairavakīryamāṇā suvarṇapuṃkhaiśca śarairvicitraiḥ
|
nakṣatrasaṅghairiva citritā dyauḥ kṣitirbabhau yodhavarairvicitrā ||16||

17tato'ntarikṣādapi sādhuvādairvāditraghoṣaiḥ samudīryamāṇaḥ
|
jyāghoṣanemisvananādacitraḥ samākulaḥ so'bhavatsaṃprahāraḥ ||17||
