Mahābhārata 13.131 (Anuśāsanaparvan)

1bhīṣma uvāca|
tataḥ sarve mahābhāgā devāśca pitaraśca ha
|
ṛṣayaśca mahābhāgāḥ pramathānvākyamabruvan ||1||

2bhavanto vai mahābhāgā aparokṣaniśācarāḥ
|
ucchiṣṭānaśucīnkṣudrānkathaṃ hiṃsatha mānavān ||2||

3ke ca smṛtāḥ pratīghātā yena martyānna hiṃsatha
|
rakṣoghnāni ca kāni syuryairgṛheṣu praṇaśyatha
|
śrotumicchāma yuṣmākaṃ sarvametanniśācarāḥ ||3||

4pramathā ūcuḥ |
maithunena sadocchiṣṭāḥ kṛte caivādharottare
|
mohānmāṃsāni khādeta vṛkṣamūle ca yaḥ svapet ||4||

5āmiṣaṃ śīrṣato yasya pādato yaśca saṃviśet
|
tata ucchiṣṭakāḥ sarve bahucchidrāśca mānavāḥ ||5||

6udake cāpyamedhyāni śleṣmāṇaṃ ca pramuñcati
|
ete bhakṣyāśca vadhyāśca mānuṣā nātra saṃśayaḥ ||6||

7evaṃ śīlasamācārāndharṣayāmo hi mānavān
|
śrūyatāṃ ca pratīghātānyairna śaknuma hiṃsitum ||7||

8gorocanāsamālambho vacāhastaśca yo bhavet
|
ghṛtāghṛtaṃ ca yo dadyānmastake tatparāyaṇaḥ ||8||

9ye ca māṃsaṃ na khādanti tānna śaknuma hiṃsitum
|
yasya cāgnigṛhe nityaṃ divārātrau ca dīpyate ||9||

10tarakṣoścarma daṃṣṭrāśca tathaiva girikacchapaḥ
|
ājyadhūmo biḍālaśca cchāgaḥ kṛṣṇo'tha piṅgalaḥ ||10||

11yeṣāmetāni tiṣṭhanti gṛheṣu gṛhamedhinām
|
tānyadhṛṣyāṇyagārāṇi piśitāśaiḥ sudāruṇaiḥ ||11||

12lokānasmadvidhā ye ca vicaranti yathāsukham
|
tasmādetāni geheṣu rakṣoghnāni viśāmpate
|
etadvaḥ kathitaṃ sarvaṃ yatra vaḥ saṃśayo mahān ||12||
