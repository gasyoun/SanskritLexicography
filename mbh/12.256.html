Mahābhārata 12.256 (Śāntiparvan)

1yudhiṣṭhira uvāca|
ya ime pṛthivīpālāḥ śerate pṛthivītale
|
pṛtanāmadhya ete hi gatasaṃjñā mahābalāḥ ||1||

2ekaikaśo bhīmabalā nāgāyutabalāstathā
|
ete hi nihatāḥ saṃkhye tulyatejobalairnaraiḥ ||2||

3naiṣāṃ paśyāmi hantāraṃ prāṇināṃ saṃyuge param
|
vikrameṇopasampannāstejobalasamanvitāḥ ||3||

4atha ceme mahāprājñāḥ śerate hi gatāsavaḥ
|
mṛtā iti ca śabdo'yaṃ vartatyeṣu gatāsuṣu ||4||

5ime mṛtā nṛpatayaḥ prāyaśo bhīmavikramāḥ
|
tatra me saṃśayo jātaḥ kutaḥ saṃjñā mṛtā iti ||5||

6kasya mṛtyuḥ kuto mṛtyuḥ kena mṛtyuriha prajāḥ
|
haratyamarasaṃkāśa tanme brūhi pitāmaha ||6||

7bhīṣma uvāca|
purā kṛtayuge tāta rājā hyāsīdakampanaḥ
|
sa śatruvaśamāpannaḥ saṃgrāme kṣīṇavāhanaḥ ||7||

8tasya putro harirnāma nārāyaṇasamo bale
|
sa śatrubhirhataḥ saṃkhye sabalaḥ sapadānugaḥ ||8||

9sa rājā śatruvaśagaḥ putraśokasamanvitaḥ
|
yadṛcchayā śāntiparo dadarśa bhuvi nāradam ||9||

10tasmai sa sarvamācaṣṭa yathāvṛttaṃ janeśvaraḥ
|
śatruābhirgrahaṇaṃ saṃkhye putrasya maraṇaṃ tathā ||10||

11tasya tadvacanaṃ śrutvā nārado'tha tapodhanaḥ
|
ākhyānamidamācaṣṭa putraśokāpahaṃ tadā ||11||

12nārada uvāca|
rājan śṛṇu samākhyānamadyedaṃ bahuvistaram
|
yathāvṛttaṃ śrutaṃ caiva mayedaṃ vasudhādhipa ||12||

13prajāḥ sṛṣṭāḥ mahātejāḥ prajāsarge pitāmahaḥ
|
atīva vṛddhā bahulā nāmṛṣyata punaḥ prajāḥ ||13||

14na hyantaramabhūtkiñcitkvacijjantubhiracyuta
|
nirucchvāsamivonnaddhaṃ trailokyamabhavannṛpa ||14||

15tasya cintā samutpannā saṃhāraṃ prati bhūpate
|
cintayannādhyagacchacca saṃhāre hetukāraṇam ||15||

16tasya roṣānmahārāja khebhyo'gnirudatiṣṭhata
|
tena sarvā diśo rājan dadāha sa pitāmahaḥ ||16||

17tato divaṃ bhuvaṃ khaṃ ca jagacca sacarācaram
|
dadāha pāvako rājan bhagavatkopasambhavaḥ ||17||

18tatrādahyanta bhūtāni jaṅgamāni dhruvāṇi ca
|
mahatā krodhavegena kupite prapitāmahe ||18||

19tato'dhvarajaṭaḥ sthāṇurvedādhvarapatiḥ śivaḥ
|
jagāma śaraṇaṃ devo brahmāṇaṃ paravīrahā ||19||

20tasminnabhigate sthāṇau prajānāṃ hitakāmyayā
|
abravītparamo devo jvalanniva tadā śivam ||20||

21karavāṇyadya kaṃ kāmaṃ varārho'si mato mama
|
kartā hyasmi priyaṃ śambho tava yaddhṛdi vartate ||21||
