Mahābhārata 7.75 (Droṇaparvan)

1sañjaya uvāca|
pratijñāte tu pārthena sindhurājavadhe tadā
|
vāsudevo mahābāhurdhanañjayamabhāṣata ||1||

2bhrātṝṇāṃ matamajñāya tvayā cācā pratiśrutam
|
saindhavaṃ cāsmi hanteti tatsāhasamidaṃ kṛtam ||2||

3asaṃmantrya mayā sārdhamatibhāro'yamudyataḥ
|
kathaṃ tu sarvalokasya nāvahāsyā bhavemahi ||3||

4dhārtarāṣṭrasya śibire mayā praṇihitāścarāḥ
|
ta ime śīghramāgamya pravṛttiṃ vedayanti naḥ ||4||

5tvayā vai saṃpratijñāte sindhurājavadhe prabhā
|
siṃhanādaḥ savāditraḥ sumahāniha taiḥ śrutaḥ ||5||

6tena śabdena vitrastā dhārtarāṣṭrāḥ sasaindhavāḥ
|
nākasmāt siṃha‌nādo'yamiti matvā vyavasthitāḥ ||6||

7sumahāñśabdasaṃpātaḥ kauravāṇāṃ mahābhuja
|
āsīnnāgāśvapattīnāṃ rathaghoṣaśca bhairavaḥ ||7||

8abhimanyorvadhaṃ śrutvā dhruvamārto dhanañjayaḥ
|
rārtrau niryāsyati kodhāditi matvā vyavasthitāḥ ||8||

9tairyatadbhiriyaṃ satyā śrutā satyavatastava
|
pratijñā sindhurājasya vadhe rājīvalocana ||9||

10tato vimanasaḥ sarve trastāḥ kṣudramṛgā iva
|
āsansuyodhanāmātyāḥ sa ca rājā jayadrathaḥ ||10||

11athotthāya sahāmātyairdīnaḥ śibiramātmanaḥ
|
āyātsauvīrasindhūnāmīśvaro bhṛśaduḥkhitaḥ ||11||

12sa mantrakāle saṃmantrya sarvāṃ naiḥśreyasīṃ kiyām
|
suyodhanamidaṃ vākyamabravīdrājasaṃsadi ||12||

13māmasau putra hanteti śvo'bhiyātā dhanañjayaḥ
|
pratijñāto hi senāyā madhye tena vadho mama ||13||

14tāṃ na devā na gandharvā nāsuroragarākṣasāḥ
|
utsahante'nyathā kartuṃ pratijñāṃ savyasācinaḥ ||14||

15te māṃ rakṣata saṃgrāme mā vo mūrdhni dhanañjayaḥ
|
padaṃ kṛtvā'pnuyāllakṣyaṃ tasmādatra vidhīyatām ||15||

16atha rakṣā na me saṃkhye kriyate kurunandana
|
anujānīhi māṃ rājan gamiṣyāmi gṛhān prati ||16||

17evamuktastvavākśīrṣo vimanāḥ sa suyodhanaḥ
|
śrutvā taṃ samayaṃ tasya dhyānamevānvapadyata ||17||

18tamārtamabhisaṃprekṣya rājā kila sa saindhavaḥ
|
mṛdu cātmahitaṃ caiva sāpekṣamidamuktavān ||18||

19neha paśyāmi bhavatāṃ tathāvīryaṃ dhanurdharam
|
yo'rjunasyāsramastreṇa pratihanyānmahāhave ||19||

20vāsudevasahāyasya gāṇḍīvaṃ dhunvato dhanuḥ
|
ko'rjunasyāgratastiṣṭhetsākṣādapi śatakratuḥ ||20||

21maheśvaro'pi pārthena śrūyate yodhitaḥ purā
|
padātinā mahāvīryo girau himavati prabhuḥ ||21||

22dānavānāṃ sahasrāṇi hiraṇyapuravāsinām
|
jaghānaikaratheneva devarājapracoditaḥ ||22||

23samāyukto hi kaunteyo vāsudevena dhīmatā
|
sāmarānapi lokāṃstrīn hanyāditi matirmama ||23||

24so'hamicchāmyanujñātuṃ rakṣituṃ vā mahātmanā
|
droṇena sahaputreṇa vīreṇa yadi manyase ||24||

25sa rājñā svayamācāryo bhṛśamatrārthito'rjuna
|
saṃvidhānaṃ ca vihitaṃ rathāśca kila sajjitāḥ ||25||

26karṇo bhūriśravā drauṇirvṛṣasenaśca durjayaḥ
|
kṛpaśca madrarājaśca ṣaḍete'sya purogamāḥ ||26||

27śakaṭaḥ padmakaścārdho vyūho droṇena nirmitaḥ
|
padmakarṇikamadhyasthaḥ sūcī pārśve jayadrathaḥ ||27||

28sthāsyate rakṣito vīraiḥ sindhurāṭ sa sudurmadaḥ
|
dhanuṣyastre ca vīrye ca prāṇe caiva tathaurase ||28||

29aviṣahyatamā hyete niścitāḥ pārtha ṣaḍrathāḥ
|
etānajitvā ṣaḍrathānnaiva prāpyo jayadrathaḥ||29||

30teṣāmekaikaśo vīryaṃ ṣaṇṇāṃ tvamanucintaya
|
sahitā hi naravyāghra na śakyā jetumañjasā ||30||

31bhūyastu mantrayiṣyāmi nītimātmahitāya vai
|
mantrajñaiḥ sacivaiḥ sārdhaṃ suhṛdbhiḥ kāryasiddhaye ||31||
