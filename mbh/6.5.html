Mahābhārata 6.5 (Bhīṣmaparvan)

1dhṛtarāṣṭra uvāca|
nadīnāṃ parvatānāṃ ca nāmadheyāni sañjaya
|
tathā janapadānāṃ ca ye cānye bhūmimāśritāḥ ||1||

2pramāṇaṃ ca pramāṇajña pṛthivyā mama sarvataḥ
|
nikhilena samācakṣva kānanāni ca sañjaya ||2||

3sañjaya uvāca|
pañcemāni mahārāja mahābhūtāni saṅgrahāt
|
jagatīsthāni sarvāṇi samānyāhurmanīṣiṇaḥ ||3||

4bhūmirāpastathā vāyuragnirākāśameva ca
|
guṇottarāṇi sarvāṇi teṣāṃ bhūmiḥ pradhānataḥ ||4||

5śabdaḥ sparśaśca rūpaṃ ca raso gandhaśca pañcamaḥ
|
bhūmerete guṇāḥ proktā ṛṣibhistattvavedibhiḥ ||5||

6catvāro'psu guṇā rājangandhastatra na vidyate
|
śabdaḥ sparśaśca rūpaṃ ca tejaso'tha guṇāstrayaḥ
|
śabdaḥ sparśaśca vāyostu akāśe śabda eva tu ||6||

7ete pañca guṇā rājanmahābhūteṣu pañcasu
|
vartante sarvalokeṣu yeṣu bhūtāḥ pratiṣṭhitāḥ ||7||

8anyonyaṃ nābhivartante sāmyaṃ bhavati vai yadā ||8||

9yadā tu viṣamībhāvamāviśanti parasparam
|
tadā dehairdehavanto vyatirohanti nānyathā ||9||

10ānupūrvyā vinaśyanti jāyante cānupūrvaśaḥ
|
sarvāṇyaparimeyāṇi tadeṣāṃ rūpamaiśvaram ||10||

11tatra tatra hi dṛśyante dhātavaḥ pāñcabhautikāḥ
|
teṣāṃ manuṣyāstarkeṇa pramāṇāni pracakṣate ||11||

12acintyāḥ khalu ye bhāvā na tāṃstarkeṇa sādhayet
|
prakṛtibhyaḥ paraṃ yattu tadacintyasya lakṣaṇam ||12||

13sudarśanaṃ pravakṣyāmi dvīpaṃ tu kurunandana
|
parimaṇḍalo mahārāja dvīpo'sau cakrasaṃsthitaḥ ||13||

14nadījālapraticchannaḥ parvataiścābhrasannibhaiḥ
|
puraiśca vividhākārai ramyairjanapadaistathā ||14||

15vṛkṣaiḥ puṣpaphalopetaiḥ sampannadhanadhānyavān
|
lavaṇena samudreṇa samantātparivāritaḥ ||15||

16yathā hi puruṣaḥ paśyedādarśe mukhamātmanaḥ
|
evaṃ sudarśanadvīpo dṛśyate candramaṇḍale ||16||

17dviraṃśe pippalastatatra dviraṃśe ca śaśo mahān
|
sarvauṣadhisamāvāyaḥ sarvataḥ parivāritaḥ ||17||

18āpastato'nyā vijñeyāḥ śeṣaḥ saṅkṣepa ucyate
|
tato'nya ucyate cāyamenaṃ saṅkṣepataḥ śrṛṇu ||18||
