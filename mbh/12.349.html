Mahābhārata 12.349 (Śāntiparvan)

1janamejaya uvāca|
sāṃkhyaṃ yogaḥ pāñcarātraṃ vedāraṇyakameva ca
|
jñānānyetāni brahmarṣe lokeṣu pracaranti ha||1||

2kimetānyekaniṣṭhāni pṛthaṅniṣṭhāni vā mune
|
prabrūhi vai mayā pṛṣṭaḥ pravṛttiṃ ca yathākramam||2||

3vaiśampāyana uvāca|
jajñe bahuśaṃ paramatyudāraṃ yaṃ dvīpamadhye sutamātmayogāt
|
parāśarātsatyavatī maharṣi tasmai namo'jñānatamonudāya||3||

4pitāmahādyaṃ pravadanti ṣaṣṭhaṃ maharṣimārṣeyavibhūtiyuktam
|
nārāyaṇasyāṃśajamekaputraṃ dvaipāyanaṃ veda mahānidhānam||4||

5tamādikāleṣu mahāvibhūtirnārāyaṇo brahmamahānidhānam
|
sasarja putrārthamudāratejā vyāsaṃ mahātmānamajaṃ purāṇam||5||

6janamejaya uvāca|
tvayaiva kathitaṃ pūrvaṃ saṃbhave dvijasattama
|
vasiṣṭhasya sutaḥ śaktiḥ śaktiputraḥ parāśaraḥ||6||

7parāśarasya dāyādaḥ kṛṣṇadvaipāyano muniḥ
|
bhūyo nārāyaṇasutaṃ tvamevainaṃ prabhāṣase||7||

8kimataḥ pūrvajaṃ janma vyāsasyāmitatejasaḥ
|
kathayasvottamamate janma nārāyaṇodbhavam||8||

9vaiśampāyana uvāca|
vedārthānvettukāmasya dharmiṣṭhasya taponidheḥ
|
gurorme jñānaniṣṭhasya himavatpāda āsataḥ||9||

10kṛtvā bhāratamākhyānaṃ tapa:śrāntasya dhīmataḥ
|
śuśrūṣāṃ tatparā rājan kṛtavanto vayaṃ tadā||10||

11sumanturjaiminiścaiva pailaśca sudṛḍhavrataḥ
|
ahaṃ caturthaḥ śiṣyo vai śukovyāsātmajastathā||11||

12ebhiḥ parivṛto vyāsa: śiṣyaiḥ pañcabhiruttamaiḥ
|
śuśubhe himavatpāde bhūtairbhūtapatiryathā||12||

13vedānāvartayansāṅgānbhāratārthāṃśca sarvaśaḥ
|
tamekamanasaṃ dāntaṃ yuktā vayamupāsmahe||13||

14kathāntare'tha kasmiṃścitpṛṣṭosmābhirdvijottamaḥ
|
vedārthānbhāratārthāṃśca janma nārāyaṇāttathā||14||

15sa pūrvamuktvā vedārthān bhāratārthāṃśca tattvavit
|
nārāyaṇādidaṃ janma vyāhartumupacakrame||15||

16śṛṇudhvamākhyānavaramidamārṣeyamuttamam
|
ādikālodbhavaṃ viprāstapasādhigataṃ mayā||16||

17prāpte prajāvisarge vai saptame padmasaṃbhave
|
nārāyaṇo mahāyogī śubhāśubhavivarjitaḥ||17||

18sasṛje nābhitaḥ pūrvaṃ brahmāṇamamitaprabhaḥ
|
tataḥ sa prādurabhavadathainaṃ vākyamabravīt||18||

19mama tvaṃ nābhito jātaḥ prajāsargakaraḥ prabhuḥ
|
sṛja prajāstvaṃ vividhā brahman sajaḍapaṇḍitāḥ||19||

20sa evamukto vimukhaścintāvyākulamānasaḥ
|
praṇamya varadaṃ devamuvāca harimīśvaram||20||

21kā śaktirmama deveśa prajāḥ sraṣṭuṃ namo'stu te
|
aprajñāvānahaṃ deva vidhatsva yadanantaram||21||

22sa evamukto bhagavānbhūtvā'thāntarhitastataḥ
|
cintayāmāsa deveśo buddhiṃ buddhimatāṃ varaḥ||22||

23svarūpiṇī tato buddhirupatasthe hariṃ prabhum
|
yogena caināṃ niryogaḥ svayaṃ niyuyuje tadā||23||

24sa tāmaiśvaryayogasthāṃ buddhiṃ gatimatīṃ satīm
|
uvāca vacanaṃ devo buddhiṃ vai prabhuravyayaḥ||24||

25brahmāṇaṃ praviśasveti lokasṛṣṭyarthasiddhaye
|
tatastamīśvarādiṣṭā buddhiḥ kṣipraṃ viveśa sā||25||

26athainaṃ buddhisaṃyuktaṃ punaḥ sa dadṛśe hariḥ
|
bhūyaścaiva vacaḥ prāha sṛjemā vividhāḥ prajāḥ||26||

27bāḍhamityeva kṛtvāsau yathā''jñāṃ śirasā hareḥ
|
evamuktvā sa bhagavāṃstatraivāntaradhīyata||27||

28prāpa cainaṃ muhūrtena saṃsthānaṃ devasaṃjñitam
|
tāṃ caiva prakṛtiṃ prāpya ekībhāvagato'bhavat||28||

29athāsya buddhirabhavatpunaranyā tadā kilā
|
sṛṣṭāḥ prajā imāḥ sarvā brahmaṇā parameṣṭhinā||29||

30daityadānavagandharvarakṣogaṇasamākulā
|
jātā hīyaṃ vasumatī bhārākrāntā tapasvinī||30||

31bahavo balinaḥ pṛthvyāṃ daityadānavarākṣasāḥ
|
bhaviṣyanti tapoyuktā varān prāpsyanti cottamān||31||

32avaśyameva taiḥ sarvairvaradānena darpitaiḥ
|
bādhitavyāḥ suragaṇā ṛṣayaśca tapodhanāḥ||32||

33tatra nyāyyamidaṃ kartuṃ bhārāvataraṇaṃ mayā
|
atha nānāsamudbhūtairvasudhāyāṃ yathākramam||33||

34nigraheṇa ca pāpānāṃ sādhūnāṃ pragraheṇa ca
|
iyaṃ tapasvinī satyā dhārayiṣyati medinī||34||

35mayā hyeṣā hi dhriyate pātālasthena bhoginā
|
mayā dhṛtā dhārayati jagadviśvaṃ carācaram||35||

36tasmātpṛthvyāḥ paritrāṇaṃ kariṣye saṃbhavaṃ gataḥ
|
evaṃ sa cintayitvā tu bhagavānmadhusūdanaḥ||36||

37rūpāṇyanekānyasṛjatprādurbhāve bhavāya saḥ
|
vārāhaṃ nārasiṃha ca vāmanaṃ mānuṣaṃ tathā||37||

38ebhirmayā nihantavyā durvinītāḥ surārayaḥ
|
atha bhūyo jagatsraṣṭā bhoḥśabdenānunādayan||38||

39sarasvatīmuccacāra tatra sārasvato'bhavat
|
apāntaratamā nāma suto vāksambhavaḥ prabhuḥ||39||

40bhūtabhavyabhaviṣyajñaḥ satyavādī dṛḍhavrataḥ
|
tamuvāca nataṃ mūrdhnā devānāmādiravyayaḥ||40||

41vedākhyāne śrutiḥ kāryā tvayā matimatāṃ vara
|
tasmātkuru yathājñaptaṃ mamaitadvacanaṃ mune||41||

42sena bhinnāstadā vedā manoḥ svāyaṃbhuve'ntare
|
tatastutoṣa bhagavānharistenāsya karmaṇā||42||

43tapasā ca sutaptena yamena niyamena ca
|
manvantareṣu putratvamevameva pravartakaḥ||43||

44bhaviṣyasyacalo brahmannapradhṛṣyaśca nityaśaḥ
|
punastiṣye ca saṃprāpte kuravo nāma bhāratāḥ||44||

45bhaviṣyanti mahātmāno rājānaḥ prathitā bhuvi
|
teṣāṃ tvattaḥ prasūtānāṃ kulabhedo bhaviṣyati||45||

46parasparavināśārthaṃ tvāmṛte dvijasattama
|
tatrāpyanekadhā vedānbhetsyase tapasā'nvitaḥ||46||

47kṛṣṇe yuge ca saṃprāpte kṛṣṇavarṇo bhaviṣyasi
|
dharmāṇāṃ vividhānāṃ ca kartā jñānakarastathā
|
bhaviṣyasi tapoyukto na ca rāgādvimokṣyase||47||

48vītarāgaśca putraste paramātmā bhaviṣyati
|
maheśvaraprasādena naitadvacanamanyathā||48||

49yaṃ mānasaṃ vai pravadanti viprāḥ pitāmahasyottamabuddhiyuktama
|
vasiṣṭhamagryaṃ ca taponidhānaṃ yasyātisūryaṃ vyatiricyate bhāḥ||49||

50tasyānvaye cāpi tato maharṣiḥ parāśaro nāma mahāprabhāvaḥ
|
pitā sa te vedanidhirvariṣṭho mahātapā vai tapaso nivāsaḥ||50||

51kānīnagarbhaḥ pitṛkanyakāyāṃ tasmādṛṣestvaṃ bhavitā ca putraḥ||51||

52bhūtabhavyabhaviṣyāṇāṃ chinnasarvārthasaṃśayaḥ
|
ye hyatikrāntakāḥ pūrvaṃ sahasrayugaparyayāḥ||52||

53tāṃśca sarvānmayoddiṣṭāndrakṣyase tapasā'nvitaḥ
|
punardrakṣyasi cānekasahasrayugaparyayān||53||

54anādinidhanaṃ loke cakrahastaṃ ca māṃ mune
|
anudhyānānmama mune naitadvacanamanyathā||54||

55bhaviṣyati mahāsattva khyātiścāpyatulā tava
|
śanaiścaraḥ sūryaputro bhaviṣyati manurmahān||55||

56tasminmanvantare caiva manvādigaṇapūrvakaḥ
|
tvameva bhavitā vatsa matprasādānna saṃśayaḥ||56||

57yatkiñcidvidyate loke sarvaṃ tanmadviceṣṭitam
|
anyo hyanyaṃ cintayati svacchandaṃ vidadhāmyaham||57||

58evaṃ sārasvatamṛṣimapāntaratamaṃ tathā
|
uktvā vacanamīśānaḥ sādhayasvetyathābravīt||58||

59so'haṃ tasya prasādena devasya harimedhasaḥ
|
apāntaratamā nāma tato jāto''jñayā hareḥ
|
punaśca jāto vikhyāto vasiṣṭhakulanandanaḥ||59||

60tadetatkathitaṃ janma mā pūrvakamātmanaḥ
|
nārāyaṇaprasādena tathā nārāyaṇāṃśajam||60||

61mayā hi sumahattaptaṃ tapaḥ paramadāruṇam
|
purā matimatāṃ śreṣṭhāḥ parameṇa samādhinā||61||

62etadvaḥ kathitaṃ sarvaṃ yanmāṃ pṛcchata putrakāḥ
|
pūrvajanma bhaviṣyaṃ ca bhaktānāṃ snehato mayā||62||

63vaiśampāyana uvāca|
eṣa te kathitaḥ pūrvaṃ sambhavo'smadgurornṛpa
|
vyāsasyākliṣṭamanaso yathā pṛṣṭaḥ punaḥ śṛṇu||63||

64sāṃkhyaṃ yogaḥ pāñcarātraṃ vedāḥ pāśupataṃ tathā
|
jñānānyetāni rājarṣe viddhi nāmāmatāni vai||64||

65sāṃkhyasya vaktā kapilaḥ paramarṣiḥ sa ucyate
|
hiraṇyagarbho yogasya vettā nānyaḥ purātanaḥ||65||

66apāntaratamāścaiva vedācāryaḥ sa ucyate
|
prācīnagarbhaṃ tamṛṣiṃ pravadantīha kecana||66||

67umāpatirbhūtapati: śrīkaṇṭho brahmaṇaḥ sutaḥ
|
uktavānidamavyagro jñānaṃ pāśupataṃ śivaḥ||67||

68pāñcarātrasya kṛtsnasya vettā tu bhagavānsvayam
|
sarveṣu ca nṛpaśreṣṭha jñāneṣveteṣu dṛśyate||68||

69yathāgamaṃ yathājñānaṃ niṣṭhā nārāyaṇaḥ prabhuḥ
|
na cainamevaṃ jānanti tamobhūtā viśāmpate||69||

70tameva śāstrakartāraḥ pravadanti manīṣiṇaḥ
|
niṣṭhāṃ nārāyaṇamṛrṣi nānyo'stīti vaco mama||70||

71ni:saṃśayeṣu sarveṣu nityaṃ vasati vai hariḥ
|
sasaṃśayānhetubalānnādhyāvasati mādhavaḥ||71||

72pāñcarātravido ye tu yathākramaparā nṛpa
|
ekāntabhāvopagatāste hariṃ praviśanti vai||72||

73sāṃkhyaṃ ca yogaṃ ca sanātane dve vedāśca sarve nikhilena rājan
|
sarvaiḥ samastairṛṣibhirnirukto nārāyaṇo viśvamidaṃ purāṇam||73||

74śubhāśubhaṃ karma samīritaṃ yatpravartate sarvalokeṣu kiñcit
|
tasmādṛṣestadbhavatīti vidyāddivyantarikṣe bhuvi cāpsu ceti||74||
