Mahābhārata 12.253 (Śāntiparvan)

1vyāsa uvāca |
śarīrādvipramuktaṃ hi sūkṣmabhūtaṃ śarīriṇam
|
karmabhiḥ paripaśyanti śāstroktaiḥ śāstravedinaḥ ||1||

2yathā marīcyaḥ sahitāścaranti sarvatra tiṣṭhanti ca dṛśyamānāḥ
|
dehairvimuktāni caranti lokāṃstathaiva sattvānyatimānuṣāṇi ||2||

3pratirūpaṃ yathaivāpsu tāpaḥ sūryasya lakṣyate
|
sattvavatsu tathā sattvaṃ pratirūpaṃ sa paśyati ||3||

4tāni sūkṣmāṇi sattvāni vimuktāni śarīrataḥ
|
svena sattvena sattvajñāḥ paśyanti niyatendriyaḥ ||4||

5svapatāṃ jāgratāṃ caiva sarveṣāmātmacintitam
|
pradhānādvaidhamuktānāṃ jahatāṃ karmajaṃ rajaḥ ||5||

6yathā'hani tathā rātrau yathā rātrau tathā'hani
|
vaśe tiṣṭhati sattvātmā satataṃ yogayoginām ||6||

7teṣāṃ nityaṃ sadā nityo bhūtātmā satataṃ guṇaiḥ
|
saptabhistvanvitaḥ sūkṣmaiścariṣṇurajarāmaraḥ ||7||

8manobuddhiparābhūtaḥ svadehaparadehavit
|
svapneṣvapi bhavatyeṣa vijñātā sukhaduḥkhayoḥ ||8||

9tatrāpi labhate duḥkhaṃ tatrāpi labhate sukham
|
krodhalobhau tu tatrāpi kṛtvā vyasanamṛrcchati ||9||

10prīṇitaścāpi bhavati mahato'rthānavāpya hi
|
karoti puṇyaṃ tatrāpi jīvanniva ca paśyati ||10||

11mahoṣmāntargataścāpi garbhatvaṃ samupeyivān
|
daśa māsānvasankukṣau naiṣonnamiva jīryate ||11||

12tametamatitejoṃ'śaṃ bhūtātmānaṃ hṛdi sthitam
|
tamorajobhyāmāviṣṭā nānupaśyanti mūrtiṣu ||12||

13yogaśāstraparā bhūtvā tamātmānaṃ parīpsavaḥ
|
anucchvāsānyamūrtāni yāni vajropamānyapi ||13||

14pṛthagbhūteṣu sṛṣṭeṣu caturthāśramakarmasu
|
samādhau yogamevaitacchāṇḍilyaḥ śamamabravīt ||14||

15viditvā sapta sūkṣmāṇi ṣaḍaṅgaṃ ca maheśvaram
|
pradhānaviniyogajñaḥ paraṃ brahmānupaśyati ||15||
