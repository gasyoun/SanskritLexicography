Mahābhārata 13.61 (Anuśāsanaparvan)

1yudhiṣṭhira uvāca|
dānaṃ yajñaḥ kriyā ceha kiṃsvitpretya mahāphalam
|
kasya jyāyaḥ phalaṃ proktaṃ kīdṛśebhyaḥ kathaṃ kadā ||1||

2etadicchāmi vijñātuṃ yāthātathyena bhārata
|
vidvan jijñāsamānāya dānadharmānpracakṣva me ||2||

3antarvedyāṃ ca yaddattaṃ śraddhayā cānṛśaṃsyataḥ
|
kiṃsvinnaiḥśreyasaṃ tāta tanme brūhi pitāmaha ||3||

4bhīṣma uvāca|
raudraṃ karma kṣatriyasya satataṃ tāta vartate
|
tasya vaitānikaṃ karma dānaṃ caiveha pāvanam ||4||

5na tu pāpakṛtāṃ rājñāṃ pratigṛhṇanti sādhavaḥ
|
etasmātkāraṇādyajñairyajedrājā''ptadakṣiṇaiḥ ||5||

6atha cetpratigṛhṇīyurdadyādaharaharnṛpaḥ
|
śraddhāmāsthāya paramāṃ pāvanaṃ hyetaduttamam ||6||

7brāhmaṇāṃstarpayandravyaistato yajñe yatavrataḥ
|
maitrān sādhūnvedavidaḥ śīlavṛttataporjitān‌ ||7||

8yatte tena kariṣyanti kṛtaṃ tena bhaviṣyati
|
yajñānsādhaya sādhubhyaḥ svādvannāndakṣiṇāvataḥ ||8||

9iṣṭaṃ dattaṃ ca manyethā ātmānaṃ dānakarmaṇā
|
pūjayethā yāyajūkāṃstavāpyaṃśo bhavedyathā ||9||

10prajāvato bharethāśca brāhmaṇān bahukāriṇaḥ
|
prajāvāṃstena bhavati yathā janayitā tathā ||10||

11yāvataḥ sādhudharmānvai santaḥ saṃvardhayantyuta
|
sarvasvaiścāpi bhartavyā narā ye bahukāriṇaḥ ||11||

12samṛddhaḥ saṃprayaccha tvaṃ brāhmaṇebhyo yudhiṣṭhira
|
dhenūranaḍuho'nnāni cchatraṃ vāsāṃsyupānahau ||12||

13ājyāni yajamānebhyastathā'nnāni ca bhārata
|
aśvavanti ca yānāni veśmāni śayanāni ca ||13||

14ete deyā vyuṣṭimanto laghupāyāśca bhārata
|
ajugupsāṃśca vijñāya brāhmaṇān vṛttikarśitān ||14||

15upacchannaṃ prakāśaṃ vā vṛttyā tānpratipālayet
|
rājasūyāśvamedhābhyāṃ śreyastatkṣatriyānprati ||15||

16evaṃ pāpairvinirmuktastvaṃ pūtaḥ svargamāpsyasi
|
sañcayitvā punaḥ kośaṃ yadrāṣṭraṃ pālayiṣyasi ||16||

17tena tvaṃ brahmabhūyatvamavāpsyasi dhanāni ca
|
ātmanaśca pareṣāṃ ca vṛttiṃ saṃrakṣa bhārata ||17||

18putravaccāpi bhṛtyānsvān prajāśca paripālaya
|
yogaḥ kṣemaśca te nityaṃ brāhmaṇeṣvastu bhārata ||18||

19tadarthaṃ jīvitaṃ te'stu mā tebhyo'pratipālanam
|
anartho brāhmaṇasyaiṣa yadvittanicayo mahān ||19||

20śriyā hyabhīkṣṇaṃ saṃvāso darpayetsaṃpramohayet
|
brāhmaṇeṣu pramūḍheṣu dharmo vipraṇaśeddhruvam
|
dharmapraṇāśe bhūtānāmabhāvaḥ syānna saṃśayaḥ ||20||

21yo rakṣibhyaḥ saṃpradāya rājā rāṣṭraṃ viluṃpati
|
yajñe rāṣṭrāddhanaṃ tasmādānayadhvamiti bruvan ||21||

22yaccādāya tadājñaptaṃ bhītaṃ dattaṃ sudāruṇam
|
yajedrājā na taṃ yajñaṃ praśaṃsantyasya sādhavaḥ ||22||

23apīḍitāḥ susaṃvṛddhā ye dadatyanukūlataḥ
|
tādṛśenāpyupāyena yaṣṭavyaṃ nodyamāhṛtaiḥ ||23||

24yadā pariniṣicyeta nihito vai yathāvidhi
|
tadā rājā mahāyajñairyajeta bahudakṣiṇaiḥ ||24||

25vṛddhabāladhanaṃ rakṣyamandhasya kṛpaṇasya ca
|
na khātapūrvaṃ kurvīta na rudantī dhanaṃ haret ||25||

26hataṃ kṛpaṇavittaṃ hi rāṣṭraṃ hanti nṛpaśriyam
|
dadyācca mahato bhogān kṣudbhayaṃ praṇudetsatām ||26||

27yeṣāṃ svādūni bhojyāni samavekṣyanti bālakāḥ
|
nāśnanti vidhivattāni kiṃ nu pāpataraṃ tataḥ ||27||

28yadi te tādṛśo rāṣṭre vidvānsīdetkṣudhā dvijaḥ
|
bhrūṇahatyāṃ ca gacchethāḥ kṛtvā pāpamivottamam ||28||

29dhiktasya jīvitaṃ rājño rāṣṭre yasyāvasīdati
|
dvijonyo vā manuṣyo'pi śibirāha vaco yathā ||||29||

30yasya sma viṣaye rājñaḥ snātakaḥ sīdati kṣudhā
|
avṛddhimeti tadrāṣṭraṃ vindate saharājakam ||30||

31krośantyo yasya vai rāṣṭrātdhriyaṃte tarasā striyaḥ
|
krośatāṃ patiputrāṇāṃ mṛto'sau na ca jīvati ||31||

32arakṣitāraṃ hartāraṃ viloptāramanāyakam
|
taṃ vai rājakaliṃ hanyuḥ prajāḥ sannahya nirghṛṇam ||32||

33ahaṃ vo rakṣitetyuktvā yo na rakṣati bhūmipaḥ
|
sa saṃhatya nihantavyaḥ śveva sonmāda āturaḥ ||33||

34pāpaṃ kurvanti yatkiñcitprajā rājñā hyarakṣitāḥ
|
caturthaṃ tasya pāpasya rājā vindati bhārata ||34||

35athāhuḥ sarvamevaiti bhūyordhamiti niścayaḥ
|
caturthaṃ matamasmākaṃ manoḥ śrutvā'nuśāsanam ||35||

36śubhaṃ vā yacca kurvanti prajā rājñā surakṣitāḥ
|
caturthaṃ tasya puṇyasya rājā cāpnoti bhārata ||36||

37jīvantaṃ tvā'nujīvantu prajāḥ sarvā yudhiṣṭhira
|
parjanyamiva bhūtāni mahādrumamivāṇḍajāḥ ||37||

38kuberamiva rakṣāṃsi śatakratumivāmarāḥ
|
jñātayastvā'nujīvantu suhṛdaśca parantapa ||38||
