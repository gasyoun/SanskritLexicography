Mahābhārata 6.93 (Bhīṣmaparvan)

1sañjaya uvāca|
vimukhīkṛtya sarvāṃstu tāvakān yudhi rākṣasaḥ
|
jighāṃsurbharataśreṣṭha duryodhanamupādravat ||1||

2tamāpatantaṃ saṃprekṣya rājānaṃ prati vegitam
|
abhyadhāvan jighāṃsantastāvakā yuddhadurmadāḥ ||2||

3tālamātrāṇi cāpāni vikarṣanto mahārathāḥ
|
tamekamabhyadhāvanta nadantaḥ siṃhasaṅghavat ||3||

4athainaṃ śaravarṣeṇa samantāt paryavākiran
|
parvataṃ vāridhārābhiḥ śaradīva balāhakāḥ ||4||

5sa gāḍhaviddho vyathitastotrārdita iva dvipaḥ
|
utpapāta tadā''kāśaṃ samantādvainateyavat ||5||

6vyanadat sumahānādaṃ jīmūta iva śāradaḥ
|
diśaḥ khaṃ vidiśaścaiva nādayan bhairavasvanaḥ ||6||

7rākṣasasya tu taṃ śabdaṃ śrutvā rājā yudhiṣṭhiraḥ
|
uvāca bharataśreṣṭha bhīmasenamarindamam ||7||

8yudhyate rākṣaso nūnaṃ dhārtarāṣṭrairmahārathaiḥ
|
yathā'sya śrūyate śabdo nadato bhairavaṃ svanam ||8||

9atibhāraṃ ca paśyāmi tasmin rākṣasapuṅgave
|
pitāmahaśca saṃkruddhaḥ pañcālān hantumudyataḥ ||9||

10teṣāṃ ca rakṣaṇārthāya yudhyate phālgunaḥ paraiḥ
|
etajjñātvā mahābāho kāryadvayamupasthitam ||10||

11gaccha rakṣasva haiḍimbaṃ saṃśayaṃ paramaṃ gatam
|
bhrāturvacanamājñāya tvaramāṇo vṛkodaraḥ ||11||

12prayayau siṃhanādena trāsayan sarvapārthivān
|
vegena mahatā rājan parvakāle yathodadhiḥ ||12||

13tamanvagāt satyadhṛtiḥ saucittiryuddhadurmadaḥ
|
śreṇimānvasudānaśca putraḥ kāśyasya cābhibhūḥ ||13||

14abhimanyumukhāścaiva draupadeyā mahārathāḥ
|
kṣatradevaśca vikrāntaḥ kṣatradharmā tathaiva ca ||14||

15anūpādhipatiścaiva nīlaḥ svabalamāsthitaḥ
|
mahatā rathavaṃśena haiḍimbaṃ paryavārayan ||15||

16kuñjaraiśca sadā mattaiḥ ṣaṭsahastraiḥ prahāribhiḥ
|
abhyarakṣanta sahitā rākṣasendraṃ ghaṭotkacam ||16||

17siṃhanādena mahatā nebhighoṣeṇa caiva ha
|
khuraśabda‌nipātaiśca kampayanto vasuṃdharām ||17||

18teṣāmāpatatāṃ śrutvā śabdaṃ taṃ tāvakaṃ balam
|
bhīmasenabhayodvignaṃ vivarṇavadanaṃ tathā ||18||

19parivṛttaṃ mahārāja parityajya ghaṭotkacam
|
tataḥ pravavṛte yuddhaṃ tatra teṣāṃ mahātmanām ||19||

20tāvakānāṃ pareṣāṃ ca saṃgrāmeṣvanivartinām
|
nānārūpāṇi śastrāṇi visṛjanto mahārathāḥ ||20||

21anyonyamabhidhāvantaḥ saṃprahāraṃ pracakrire
|
vyatiṣvaktaṃ mahāraudraṃ yuddhaṃ bhīrubhayāvaham ||21||

22hayā gajaiḥ samājagmuḥ pādātā rathibhiḥ saha
|
anyonyaṃ samare rājanprārthayānāḥ samabhyayuḥ ||22||

23sahasā cābhavattīvraṃ sannipātānmahadrajaḥ
|
gajāśvarathapattīnāṃ padanemisamuddhatam ||23||

24dhūmrāruṇaṃ rajastīvraṃ raṇabhūmiṃ samāvṛṇot
|
naiva sve na pare rājan samajānan parasparam ||24||

25pitā putraṃ na jānīte putro vā pitaraṃ tathā
|
nirmaryāde tathābhūte vaiśase lomaharṣaṇe ||25||

26śastrāṇāṃ bharataśreṭha manuṣyāṇāṃ ca garjatām
|
sumahānabhavacchabdaḥ pretānāmiva bhārata ||26||

27gajavājimanuṣyāṇāṃ śoṇitāntrataraṅgiṇī
|
prāvartata nadī tatra keśaśaivalaśādvalā ||27||

28narāṇāṃ caiva kāyebhyaḥ śirasāṃ patatāṃ raṇe
|
śuśruve sumahāñchabdaḥ patatāmaśmanāmiva ||28||

29viśiraskairmanuṣyaiśca cchinnagātraiśca vāraṇaiḥ
|
aśvaiḥ saṃbhinnadehaiśca saṃkīrṇā'bhūdvasuṃdharā ||29||

30nānāvidhāni śastrāṇi visṛjanto mahārathāḥ
|
anyonyamabhidhāvantaḥ saṃprahārārthamudyatāḥ ||30||

31hayā hayān samāsādya preṣitā hayasādibhiḥ
|
samāhatya raṇe'nyonyaṃ nipeturgatajīvitāḥ ||31||

32narā narān samāsādya krodharaktekṣaṇā bhṛśam
|
urāṃsyurobhiranyonyaṃ samāśliṣya nijaghnire ||32||

33preṣitāśca mahāmātrairvāraṇāḥ paravāraṇaiḥ
|
abhyaghnanta viṣāṇāgrairvāraṇāneva saṃyuge ||33||

34te jātarudhirotpīḍāḥ patākābhiralaṅkṛtāḥ
|
saṃsaktāḥ pratyadṛśyanta meghā iva savidyutaḥ ||34||

35kecidbhinnā viṣāṇāgnairbhinnakuṃbhāśca tomaraiḥ
|
vinadanto'bhyadhāvanta garjamānā ghanā iva ||35||

36keciddhastairdvidhā chinnaiśchinnagātrāstathā'pare
|
nipetustumule tasmiṃśchinnapakṣā ivādrayaḥ ||36||

37pārśvaistu dāritairanye vāraṇairvaravāraṇāḥ
|
mumucuḥ śoṇitaṃ bhūri dhātūniva mahīdharāḥ ||37||

38nārācanihatāstvanye tathā viddhāśca tomaraiḥ
|
vinadanto'bhyadhāvanta viśṛṅgā iva parvatāḥ ||38||

39kecit krodhasamāviṣṭā madāndhā niravagrahāḥ
|
rathān hayān padātīṃśca mamṛduḥ śataśo raṇe ||39||

40tathā hayā hayārohaistāḍitāḥ prāsatomaraiḥ
|
tena tenābhyavartanta kurvanto vyākulā diśaḥ ||40||

41rathino rathibhiḥ sārdhaṃ kulaputrāstanutyajaḥ
|
parāṃ śaktiṃ samāsthāya cakruḥ karmāṇyabhītavat ||41||

42svayaṃvara ivāmarde prajahruritaretaram
|
prārthayānā yaśo rājansvargaṃ vā yuddhaśālinaḥ ||42||

43tasmiṃstathā vartamāne saṃgrāme lomaharṣaṇe
|
dhārtarāṣṭraṃ mahat sainyaṃ prāyaśo vimukhī kṛtam ||43||
