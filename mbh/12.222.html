Mahābhārata 12.222 (Śāntiparvan)

1yudhiṣṭhira uvāca|
yadidaṃ karma loke'smin śubhaṃ vā yadi vā śubham
|
puruṣaṃ yojayatyeva phalayogena bhāratā ||1||

2kartā'sti tasya puruṣa utāho neti saṃśayaḥ
|
etadicchāmi tattvena tvattaḥ śrotuṃ pitāmaha ||2||

3bhīṣma uvāca|
atrāpyudāharantīmamitihāsaṃ purātanam
|
prahlādasya ca saṃvādamindrasya ca yudhiṣṭhira ||3||

4asaktaṃ dhūtapāpmānaṃ kule jātaṃ bahuśrutam
|
astabdhamanahaṅkāraṃ sattvasthaṃ samaye ratam ||4||

5tulyanindāstutiṃ dāntaṃ śūnyāgāranivāsinam
|
carācarāṇāṃ bhūtānāṃ viditaprabhavāpyayam ||5||

6akrudhyantamahaṣyantamapriyeṣu priyeṣu ca
|
kāñcane vā'tha loṣṭhe vā ubhayoḥ samadarśanam ||6||

7ātmani śreyasi jñāne dhīraṃ niścitaniścitam
|
parāvarajñaṃ bhūtānāṃ sarvajñaṃ samadarśanam ||7||

8śakraḥ prahlādamāsīnamekānte saṃyatendriyam
|
bubhutsamānastatprajñāmabhigamyedamabravīt ||8||

9yaiḥ kaścitsammato loke guṇaiḥ syātpuruṣo nṛṣu
|
bhavatyanapagān sarvāṃstānguṇān lakṣayāmahe ||9||

10atha te lakṣyate buddhiḥ samā bālajanairiha
|
ātmānaṃ manyamānaḥ san śreyeḥ kimiha manyase ||10||

11baddhaḥ pāśaiścyutaḥ sthānāddviṣatāṃ vaśamāgataḥ
|
śriyā vihīnaḥ prahlāda śocitavye na śocasi ||11||

12prajñālābhāttu daiteya utāho dhṛtimattayā
|
prahlāda sustharūpo'si paśyan vyasanamātmanaḥ ||12||

13iti saṃcoditastena dhīro niścitaniścayaḥ
|
uvāca ślakṣṇayā vācā svāṃ prajñāmanuvarṇayan ||13||

14prahlāda uvāca |
pravṛttiṃ ca nivṛttiṃ ca bhūtānāṃ yo na buddhyate
|
tasya stambho bhavedbālyānnāsti stambho'nupaśyataḥ ||14||

15svabhāvātsampravartante nivartante tathaiva ca
|
sarve bhāvāstathābhāvāḥ puruṣārtho na vidyate ||15||

16puruṣārthasya cābhāve nāsti kaścicca kārakaḥ
|
svayaṃ na kurvatastasya jātu māno bhavediha ||16||

17yastu kartāramātmānaṃ manyate sādhvasādhu vā
|
tasya doṣavatī prajñā atattvajñeti me matiḥ ||17||

18yadi syātpuruṣaḥ kartā śakrātmaśreyase dhruvam
|
ārambhāstasya siddhyeyurna tu jātu parābhavet ||18||

19aniṣṭasya hi nirvṛttiranivṛttiḥ priyasya ca
|
lakṣyate yatamānānāṃ puruṣārthastataḥ kutaḥ ||19||

20aniṣṭasyābhinirvṛttimiṣṭasaṃvṛttimeva ca
|
aprayatnena paśyāmaḥ keṣāñcittatsvabhāvataḥ ||20||

21pratirūpatarāḥ keciddṛśyante buddhimattarāḥ
|
virūpebhyo'lpabuddhibhyo lipsamānā dhanāgamam ||21||

22svabhāvapreritāḥ sarve niviśante guṇā yadā
|
śubhāśubhāstadā tatra kasya kiṃ mānakāraṇam ||22||

23svabhāvādeva tatsarvamiti me niścitā matiḥ
|
ātmapratiṣṭhā prajñā vā mama nāsti tato'nyathā ||23||

24karmajaṃ tviha manyante phalayogaṃ śubhāśubham
|
karmaṇāṃ viṣayaṃ kṛtsnamahaṃ vakṣyāmi tacchṛṇu ||24||

25yathā vedayate kaścidodanaṃ vāyaso hyadan
|
evaṃ sarvāṇi karmāṇi svabhāvasyaiva lakṣaṇam ||25||

26vikārāneva yo veda na veda prakṛtiṃ parām
|
tasya stambhau bhavedbālyānnāsti stambho'nupaśyataḥ ||26||

27svabhāvabhāvino bhāvān sarvāneveha niścayāt
|
buddhyamānasya darpo vā māno vā kiṃ kariṣyati ||27||

28veda dharmavidhiṃ kṛtsnaṃ bhūtānāṃ cāpyanityatām
|
tasmācchaka na śocāmi sarvaṃ hyevedamantavat ||28||

29nirmamo nirahaṃkāro nirāśīrmuktabandhanaḥ
|
svastho vyapetaḥ paśyāmi bhūtānāṃ prabhavāpyayau ||29||

30kṛtaprajñasya dāntasya vitṛṣṇasya nirāśiṣaḥ
|
nāyāso vidyate śakra paśyato lokamavyayam ||30||

31prakṛtau ca vikāre ca na me prītirna ca dviṣe
|
dveṣṭāraṃ ca na paśyāmi yo māmadya mamāyate ||31||

32nordhvaṃ nāvāṅna tiryakca na kvacicchakra kāmaye
|
na hi jñeye na vijñāne na jñāne karma vidyate ||32||

33śakra uvāca| yenaiṣā labhyate prajñā yenu śāntiravāpyate
|
prabrūhi tamupāyaṃ me samyak prahlāda pṛcchataḥ ||33||

34prahlāda uvāca |
ārjavenāpramādena prasādenātmavattayā
|
vṛddhaśuśraṣayā śakra puruṣo labhate mahat ||34||

35svabhāvāllabhate prajñāṃ śāntimeti svabhāvataḥ
|
svabhāvādeva tatsarvaṃ yatkiṃcidanupaśyasi ||35||

36ityukto daityapatinā śakro vismayamāgamat
|
prītimāṃśca tadā rājastadvākyaṃ pratyapūjayat ||36||

37sa tadā'bhyarcya daityendraṃ trailokyapatirīśvaraḥ
|
asurendramupāmantrya jagāma svaṃ niveśanam ||37||
