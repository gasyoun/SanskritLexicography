Mahābhārata 8.40 (Karṇaparvan)

1sañjaya uvāca|
adhikṣiptastu rādheyaḥ śalyenāmitatejasā
|
śalyamāha susaṃkruddho vākśalyamavadhārayan ||1||

2karṇa uvāca|
guṇān guṇavatāṃ śalya guṇavānvetti nāguṇaḥ
|
tvaṃ tu śalya guṇairhīnaḥ kiṃ jñāsyasi guṇāguṇam ||2||

3arjunasya mahāstrāṇi krodhaṃ vīryaṃ dhanuḥ śarān
|
ahaṃ śalyābhijānāmi vikramaṃ ca mahātmanaḥ ||3||

4tathā kṛṣṇasya māhātmyamṛṣabhasya mahīkṣitām
|
yathā'haṃ śalya jānāmi na tvaṃ jānāsi tattathā ||4||

5evamevātmano vīryamahaṃ vīryaṃ ca pāṇḍave
|
jānannevāhvaye yuddhe śalya gāṇḍīvadhāriṇam ||5||

6asti vā'yamiṣuḥ śalya supuṅkho raktabhojanaḥ
|
ekatūṇīśayaḥ patrī sudhautaḥ samalaṅkṛtaḥ ||6||

7śete candanacūrṇeṣu pūjito bahulāḥ samāḥ
|
āheyo viṣavānugro narāśvadvipasaṃghahā ||7||

8ghorarūpo mahāraudrastanutrāsthividāraṇaḥ
|
nirbhindyāṃ yena ruṣṭo'hamapi meruṃ mahāgirim ||8||

9tamahaṃ jātu nāsyeyamanyasmin phālgunādṛte
|
kṛṣṇādvā devakīputrātsatyaṃ cāpi śṛṇuṣva me ||9||

10tenāhamiṣuṇā śalya vāsudevadhanañjayau
|
yotsye paramasaṃkruddhastatkarma sadṛśaṃ mama ||10||

11sarveṣāṃ vṛṣṇivīrāṇāṃ kṛṣṇe lakṣmīḥ pratiṣṭhitā
|
sarveṣāṃ pāṇḍuputrāṇāṃ jayaḥ pārthe pratiṣṭhitaḥ ||11||

12ubhayaṃ tu samāsādya ko nivartitumarhati
|
tāvetau puruṣavyāghrau sametau syandane sthitau ||12||

13māmekamabhisaṃyātau sujātaṃ paśya śalya me
|
pitṛṣvasā mātulajau bhrātarāvaparājitau ||13||

14maṇī sūtra iva protau draṣṭāsi nihatau mayā
|
arjune gāṇḍivaṃ kṛṣṇe cakraṃ tārkṣyakapidhvajau ||14||

15bhīrūṇāṃ trāsajananaṃ śalya harṣakaraṃ mama
|
tvaṃ tu duṣprakṛtirmūḍho mahāyuddheṣvakovidaḥ ||15||

16bhayāvadīrṇaḥ saṃtrāsādabaddhaṃ bahu bhāṣase
|
saṃstauṣi tau tu kenāpi hetunā tvaṃ kudeśaja ||16||

17tau hatvā samare hantā tvāmadya sahabāndhavam
|
pāpadeśaja durbuddhe kṣudra kṣatriyapāṃsana ||17||

18suhṛdbhūtvā ripuḥ kiṃ māṃ kṛṣṇābhyāṃ bhīṣayiṣyasi
|
tau vā māmadya hantārau haniṣye vāpi tāvaham ||18||

19nāhaṃ bibhemi kṛṣṇābhyāṃ vijānannātmano balam
|
vāsudevasahasraṃ vā phālgunānāṃ śatāni vā ||19||

20ahameko haniṣyāmi joṣamāssva kudeśaja
|
striyo bālāśca vṛddhāśca prāyaḥ krīḍāgatā janāḥ ||20||

21yā gāthāḥ saṃpragāyanti kurvanto'dhyayanaṃ yathā
|
tā gāthāḥ śṛṇu me śalya madrakeṣu durātmasu ||21||

22brāhmaṇaiḥ kathitāḥ pūrvaṃ yathāvadrājasannidhau
|
śrutvā caikamanā mūḍha kṣama vā brūhi cottaram ||22||

23mitradhruṅmadrako nityaṃ yo no dveṣṭi sa madrakaḥ
|
madrake saṅgataṃ nāsti kṣudravākye narādhame ||23||

24durātmā madrako nityaṃ nityamānṛtiko'nṛjuḥ
|
yāvadantyaṃ hi daurātmyaṃ madrakeṣviti naḥ śrutam ||24||

25pitā putraśca mātā ca śvaśrūśvaśuramātulāḥ
|
jāmātā duhitā bhrātā naptā'nye te ca bāndhavāḥ ||25||

26vayasyābhyāgatāścānye dāsīdāsaṃ ca saṅgatam
|
puṃbhirvimiśrā nāryaśca jñātājñātāḥ svayecchayā ||26||

27yeṣāṃ gṛheṣvaśiṣṭānāṃ saktumatsyāśināṃ tathā
|
pītvā sīdhu sagomāṃsaṃ krandanti ca hasanti ca ||27||

28gāyanti cāpyabaddhāni pravartante ca kāmataḥ
|
kāmapralāpino'nyonyaṃ teṣu dharmaḥ kathaṃ bhavet ||28||

29madrakeṣvavalipteṣu prakhyātā śubhakarmasu
|
nāpi vairaṃ na sauhārdaṃ madrakeṇa samācaret ||29||

30madrake saṅgataṃ nāsti madrako hi sadā malaḥ
|
madrakeṣu ca saṃsṛṣṭaṃ śaucaṃ gāndhārakeṣu ca ||30||

31rājayājakayājye ca naṣṭaṃ dattaṃ havirbhavet
|
śūdrasaṃskārako vipro yathā yāti parābhavam ||31||

32yathā brahmadviṣo nityaṃ gacchantīha parābhavam
|
yathaiva saṅgataṃ kṛtvā naraḥ patati madrakaiḥ ||32||

33madrake saṅgataṃ nāsti hataṃ vṛścika te viṣam
|
ātharvaṇena mantreṇa yathā śāntiḥ kṛtā mayā ||33||

34iti vṛścikada‌ṣṭasya viṣavegahatasya ca
|
kurvanti bheṣajaṃ prājñāḥ satyaṃ taccāpi dṛśyate ||34||

35evaṃ vidvañjoṣamāssva śṛṇu cātrottaraṃ vacaḥ
|
vāsāṃsyutsṛjya nṛtyanti striyo yā madyamohitāḥ ||35||

36maithune'saṃyatāścāpi yathākāmavarāśca tāḥ
|
tāsāṃ putraḥ kathaṃ dharmaṃ madrako vaktumarhati ||36||

37yāstiṣṭhantyaḥ pramehanti yathaivoṣṭradaśerakāḥ
|
tāsāṃ vibhraṣṭadharmāṇāṃ nirlajjānāṃ tatastataḥ ||37||

38tvaṃ putrastādṛśīnāṃ hi dharmaṃ vaktumihecchasi
|
suvīrakaṃ yācyamānā madrikā karṣati sphicau ||38||

39adātukāmā vacanamidaṃ vadati dāruṇam
|
’mā māṃ suvīrakaṃ kaścidyācatāṃ dayitaṃ mama ||39||

40putraṃ dadyāṃ patiṃ dadyāṃ na tu dadyāṃ suvīrakam’
|
gauryo bṛhatyo nirhrīkā madrikāḥ kambalāvṛtāḥ ||40||

41ghasmarā naṣṭaśaucāśca prāya ityanuśuśruma
|
evamādi mayā'nyairvā śakyaṃ vaktuṃ bhavedbahu ||41||

42ākeśāgrānnakhāgrācca vaktavyeṣu kukarmasu
|
madrakāḥ sindhusauvīrāḥ dharmaṃ vidyuḥ kathaṃ tviha ||42||

43pāpadeśodbhavā mlecchā dharmāṇāmavicakṣaṇāḥ
|
eṣa mukhyatamo dharmaḥ kṣatriyasyeti naḥ śrutam ||43||

44yadājau nihataḥ śete sadbhiḥ samabhipūjitaḥ
|
āyudhānāṃ sāṃparāye yanmucyeyamahaṃ tataḥ ||44||

45mamaiṣa prathamaḥ kalpo nidhane svargamicchataḥ
|
soyaṃ priyaḥ sakhā cāsmi dhārtarāṣṭrasya dhīmataḥ ||45||

46tadarthe hi mama prāṇā yacca me vidyate vasu
|
vyaktaṃ tvamapyupahitaḥ pāṇḍavaiḥ pāpadeśaja ||46||

47yathā cāmitravatsarvaṃ tvamasmāsu pravartase
|
kāmaṃ na khalu śakyo'haṃ tvadvidhānāṃ śatairapi ||47||

48saṃgrāmādvimukhaḥ kartuṃ dharmajña iva nāstikaiḥ
|
sāraṅga iva gharmārtaḥ kāmaṃ vilapa śuṣya ca ||48||

49nāhaṃ bhīṣayituṃ śakyaḥ kṣatravṛtte vyavasthitaḥ
|
tanutyajāṃ nṛsiṃhānāmāhaveṣvanivartinām ||49||

50yā gatirguruṇā proktā purā rāmeṇa tāṃ smare
|
teṣāṃ trāṇārthamudyantaṃ vadhārthaṃ dviṣatāmapi ||50||

51viddhi māmāsthitaṃ vṛttaṃ paurūravasamuttamam
|
na tadbhūtaṃ prapaśyāmi triṣu lokeṣu madrapa ||51||

52yo māmasmāda‌bhiprāyādvārayediti me matiḥ
|
evaṃ vidvañjoṣamāssva trāsātkiṃ bahu bhāṣase ||52||

53na tvāṃ hatvā pradāsyāmi kravyādbhyo madrakādhama
|
mitrapratīkṣayā śalya dhṛtarāṣṭrasya cobhayoḥ ||53||

54apavādatitikṣābhistribhiretairhi jīvasi
|
punaścedīdṛśaṃ vākyaṃ madrarāja vadiṣyasi ||54||

55śiraste pātayiṣyāmi gadayā vajrakalpayā
|
śrotārastvidamadyeha draṣṭāro vā kudeśaja ||55||

56karṇaṃ vā jaghnatuḥ kṛṣṇau karṇo vā nijaghāna tau
|
evamuktvā tu rādheyaḥ punareva viśāṃpate
|
abravīnmadrarājānaṃ yāhi yāhītyasaṃbhramam ||56||
