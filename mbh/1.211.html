Mahābhārata 1.211 (Ādiparvan)

1nārada uvāca|
tato devarṣayaḥ sarve siddhāśca paramarṣayaḥ
|
jagmustadā paramārtiṃ dṛṣṭvā tatkadanaṃ mahat||1||

2tebhijagmurjitakrodhā jitātmāno jitendriyāḥ
|
pitāmahasya bhavanaṃ jagataḥ kṛpayā tadā||2||

3tato dadṛśurāsīnaṃ sahadevaiḥ pitāmaham
|
siddhairbrahmarṣibhiścaiva samantātparivāritam||3||

4tatra devo mahādevastatrāgnirvāyunā saha
|
candrādityau ca śakraśca pārameṣṭhyāstatharṣayaḥ||4||

5vaikhānasā vālakhilyā vānaprasthā marīcipāḥ
|
ajāścaivāvimūḍhāśca tejogarbhāstapasvinaḥ||5||

6ṛṣayaḥ sarva evaite pitāmahamupāgaman
|
tato'bhigamya te dīnāḥ sarva eva maharṣayaḥ||6||

7sundopasundayoḥ karma sarvameva śaśaṃsire
|
yathā hṛtaṃ yathā caiva kṛtaṃ yena krameṇa ca||7||

8nyavedayaṃstataḥ sarvamakhilena pitāmahe
|
tato devagaṇāḥ sarve te caiva paramarṣayaḥ||8||

9tamevārthaṃ puraskṛtya pitāmahamacodayan
|
tataḥ pitāmahaḥ śrutvā sarveṣāṃ tadvacastadā||9||

10muhūrtamiva saṃcintya kartavyasya ca niścayam
|
tayorvadhaṃ samuddiśya viśvakarmāṇamāhvayat||10||

11dṛṣṭvā ca viśvakarmāṇaṃ vyādideśa pitāmahaḥ
|
sṛjyatāṃ prārthanīyaikā pramadeti mahātapāḥ||11||

12pitāmahaṃ namaskṛtya tadvākyamabhinandya ca
|
nirmame yoṣitaṃ divyāṃ cintayitvā punaḥpunaḥ||12||

13triṣu lokeṣu yatkiṃcidbhūtaṃ sthāvarajaṅgamam
|
samānayaddarśanīyaṃ tattadatra sa viśvavit||13||

14koṭiśaścaiva ratnāni tasyā gātre nyaveśat
|
tāṃ ratnasaṅghātamayīmasṛjaddevarūpiṇīm||14||

15sā prayatnena mahatā nirmitā viśvakarmaṇā
|
triṣu lokeṣu nārīṇāṃ rūpeṇāpratimābhavat||15||

16na tasyāḥ sūkṣmamapyasti yadgātre rūpasaṃpadā
|
niyuktā yatra vā dṛṣṭirna sajjati nirīkṣatām||16||

17sā vigrahavatīva śrīḥ kāmarūpā vapuṣmatī
|
jahāra sarvabhūtānāṃ cakṣūṃṣi ca manāṃsi ca||17||

18tilaṃ tilaṃ samānīya ratnānāṃ yadvinirmitā
|
tilottameti tattasyā nāma cakre pitāmahaḥ||18||

19brahmāṇaṃ sā namaskṛtya prāñjalirvākyamabravīt
|
kiṃ kāryaṃ mayi bhūteśa yenāsmyadyeha nirmitā||19||

20pitāmaha uvāca|
gaccha sundopasundābhyāmasurābhyāṃ tilottame
|
prārthanīyena rūpeṇa kuru bhadre pralobhanam||20||

21tvatkṛte darśādeva rūpasaṃpatkṛtena vai
|
virodhaḥ syādyathā tābhyāmanyonyena tathā kuru||21||

22nārada uvāca|
sā tatheti pratijñāya namaskṛtya pitāmaham
|
cakāra maṇḍalaṃ tatra vibudhānāṃ pradakṣiṇam||22||

23prāṅmukho bhagavānāste dakṣiṇena maheśvaraḥ
|
devāścaivottareṇāsansarvatastvṛṣayo'bhavan||23||

24kurvantyā tu tadā tatra maṇḍalaṃ tatpradakṣiṇam
|
indraḥ sthāṇuśca bhagavāndhairyeṇa tu pratyavasthitau||24||

25draṣṭukāmasya cātyarthaṃ gatayā pārśvatastayā
|
anyadañcitapadmākṣaṃ dakṣiṇaṃ niḥsṛtaṃ mukham||25||

26pṛṣṭhataḥ parivartantyā paścimaṃ niḥsṛtaṃ mukham
|
gatayā cottaraṃ pārśvamuttaraṃ niḥsṛtaṃ mukham||26||

27mahendrasyāpi netrāṇāṃ pṛṣṭhataḥ pārśvatogrataḥ
|
raktāntānāṃ viśālānāṃ sahasraṃ sarvato'bhavat||27||

28evaṃ caturmukhaḥ sthāṇurmahādevo'bhavatpurā
|
tathā sahasranetraśca babhūva balasūdanaḥ||28||

29tathā devanikāyānāṃ maharṣīṇāṃ ca sarvaśaḥ
|
mukhāni cābhyavartanta yena yāti tilottamā||29||

30tasyā gātre nipatitā dṛṣṭisteṣāṃ mahātmanām
|
sarveṣāmeva bhūyiṣṭhamṛte devaṃ pitāmaham||30||

31gacchantyā tu tayā sarve devāśca paramarṣayaḥ
|
kṛtamityeva tatkāryaṃ menire rūpasaṃpadā||31||

32tilottamāyāṃ tasyāṃ tu gatāyāṃ lokabhāvanaḥ
|
sarvānvisarjayāmāsa devānṛṣigaṇāṃśca tān||32||
