Mahābhārata 1.101 (Ādiparvan)

1tato vivāhe nirvṛtte sa rājā śāntanurnṛpaḥ
|
tāṃ kanyāṃ rūpasaṃpannāṃ svagṛhe saṃnyaveśayat ||1||

2tataḥ śāntanavo dhīmānsatyavatyāmajāyata
|
vīraścitrāṅgado nāma vīryavānpuruṣeśvaraḥ ||2||

3athāparaṃ maheṣvāsaṃ satyavatyāṃ sutaṃ prabhuḥ
|
vicitravīryaṃ rājānaṃ janayāmāsa vīryavān ||3||

4aprāptavati tasmiṃstu yauvanaṃ puruṣarṣabhe
|
sa rājā śāntanurdhīmānkāladharmamupeyivān ||4||

5svargate śāntano bhīṣmaścitrāṅgadamariṃdamam
|
sthāpayāmāsa vai rājye satyavatyā mate sthitaḥ ||5||

6sa tu citrāṅgadaḥ śauryātsarvāṃścikṣepa pārthivān
|
manuṣyaṃ na hi mene saṃ kaṃcitsadṛśamātmanaḥ ||6||

7taṃ kṣipantaṃ surāṃścaiva manuṣyānasurāṃstathā
|
gandharvarājo balavāṃstulyanāmā'bhyayāttadā||7||

8tenāsya sumahadyuddhaṃ kurukṣetre babhūva ha
|
tayorbalavatostatra gandharvakurumukhyayoḥ
|
nadyāstīre sarasvatyāḥ samāstisrobhavadraṇaḥ ||8||

9tasminvimarde tumule śastravarṣasamākule
|
māyādhiko'vadhīdvīraṃ gandharvaḥ kurusattamam ||9||

10sa hatvā tu naraśreṣṭhaṃ citrāṅgadamariṃdamam
|
antāya kṛtvā gandharvo divamācakrame tataḥ || 10||

11tasminpuruṣaśārdūle nihate bhūritejasi
|
bhīṣmaḥ śāntanavo rājā pretakāryāṇyakārayat ||11||

12vicitravīryaṃ ca tadā bālamaprāptayauvanam
|
kururājye mahābāhurabhyaṣiñcadanantaram ||12||

13vicitravīryaḥ sa tadā bhīṣmasya vacane sthitaḥ
|
anvaśāsanmahārāja pitṛpaitāmahaṃ padam ||13||

14sa dharmaśāstrakuśalaṃ bhīṣmaṃ śāntanavaṃ nṛpaḥ
|
pūjayāmāsa dharmeṇa sa cainaṃ pratyapālayat || 14||
