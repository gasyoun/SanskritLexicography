Mahābhārata 12.126 (Śāntiparvan)

1bhīṣma uvāca|
praviśya sa mahāraṇyaṃ tāpasānāmathāśramam
|
āsasāda tato rājā śrāntacopāviśattadā ||1||

2taṃ kārmukadharaṃ dṛṣṭvā śramārtaṃ kṣudhitaṃ tadā
|
sametya ṛṣayastasminpūjāṃ cakruryathāvidhi ||2||

3sa pūjāmṛṣibhirdattāṃ sampragṛhya narādhipaḥ
|
apṛcchattāpasānsarvāṃstapaso vṛddhimuttamām ||3||

4te tasya rājño vacanaṃ sampragṛhya tapodhanāḥ
|
ṛṣayo rājaśārdūlaṃ tamapṛcchanprayojanam ||4||

5kena bhadra sukhārthena samprāpto'si tapovanam
|
padātirbaddhanistriṃśo dhanvī bāṇī nareśvara ||5||

6etadicchāmahe śrotuṃ kutaḥ prāpto'si mānada
|
kasminkule tu jātastvaṃ kiṃnāmā cāsi brūhi naḥ ||6||

7tataḥ sa rājā sarvebhyo dvijebhyaḥ puruṣarṣabha
|
ācacakṣe yathānyāyaṃ paricaryāṃ ca bhārata ||7||

8haihayānāṃ kule jātaḥ sumitro mitranandanaḥ
|
carāmi mṛgayūthāni nighnanbāṇaiḥ sahasraśaḥ ||8||

9balena mahatā guptaḥ sāmātyaḥ sāvarodhanaḥ
|
mṛgastu viddho bāṇena mayā sarati śalyavān ||9||

10taṃ dravantamanuprāpto vanametadyadṛcchayā
|
bhavatsakāśaṃ naṣṭaśrīrhatāśaḥ śramakarśitaḥ ||10||

11kiṃ nu duḥkhamato'nyadvai yadahaṃ śramakarśitaḥ
|
bhavatāmāśramaṃ prāpto hatāśo bhraṣṭalakṣaṇaḥ ||11||

12na rājalakṣaṇatyāgo na purasya tapodhanāḥ
|
duḥkhaṃ karoti tattīvraṃ yathā''śā vihatā mama ||12||

13himavānvā mahāśailaḥ samudro vā mahodadhiḥ
|
mahattvānnānvapadyetāṃ nabhaso vā'ntaraṃ tathā ||13||

14āśāyāstapasi śreṣṭhāstathā nāntamahaṃ gataḥ
|
bhavatāṃ viditaṃ sarvaṃ sarvajñā hi tapodhanāḥ ||14||

15bhavantaḥ sumahābhāgāstasmātpṛcchāmi saṃśayam
|
āśāvānpuruṣo yaḥ syādantarikṣamathāpi vā ||15||

16kiṃ nu jyāyastaraṃ loke mahattvātpratibhāti vaḥ
|
etadicchāmi tattvena śrotuṃ kimiha durlabham ||16||

17yadi guhyaṃ na vo nityaṃ tadā prabrūta māciram
|
na guhyaṃ śrotumicchāmi yuṣmadbhyo dvijasattamāḥ ||17||

18bhavattapovighāto vā yadi syādvirame tataḥ
|
yadi vā'sti kathāyogo yo'yaṃ praśno mayeritaḥ ||18||

19etatkāraṇasāmarthyaṃ śrotumicchāmi tattvataḥ
|
bhavanto'pi taponityā brūyuretatsamanvitāḥ ||19||
