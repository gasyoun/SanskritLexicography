Mahābhārata 12.16 (Śāntiparvan)

1vaiśampāyana uvāca|
arjunasya vacaḥ śrutvā bhīmaseno'tyamarṣaṇaḥ
|
dhairyamāsthāya tejasvī jyeṣṭhaṃ bhrātaramabravīt ||1||

2rājan viditadharmo'si na te'styaviditaṃ kvacit
|
upaśikṣāma te vṛttaṃ sadaiva na ca śaknumaḥ ||2||

3na vakṣyāmi na vakṣyāmītyevaṃ me manasi sthitam
|
atiduḥkhāttu vakṣyāmi tānnibodha janādhipa ||3||

4bhavataḥ sampramohena sarvaṃ saṃśayitaṃ kṛtam
|
viklavatvaṃ ca naḥ prāptamabalatvaṃ tathaiva ca ||4||

5kathaṃ hi rājā lokasya sarvaśāstraviśāradaḥ
|
mohamāpadyase dainyādyathā kāpuruṣastathā ||5||

6agatiśca gatiścaiva lokasya viditā tava
|
āyatyāṃ ca tadātve ca na te'styaviditaṃ prabho ||6||

7evaṃ gate mahārāja rājyaṃ prati janādhipa
|
hetumatra pravakṣyāmi tamihaikamanāḥ śṛṇu ||7||

8dvividho jāyate vyādhiḥ śārīro mānasastathā
|
parasparaṃ tayorjanma nirdvandvaṃ nopalabhyate ||8||

9śārīrājjāyate vyādhirmānaso nātra saṃśayaḥ
|
mānasājjāyate vāpi śārīra iti niścayaḥ ||9||

10śārīraṃ mānasaṃ duḥkhaṃ yo'tītamanuśocati
|
duḥkhena labhate duḥkhaṃ dvāvanarthau ca vindati ||10||

11śītoṣṇe caiva vāyuśca trayaḥ śārīrajā guṇāḥ
|
teṣāṃ guṇānāṃ sāmyaṃ yattadāhuḥ svasthalakṣaṇam ||11||

12teṣāmanyatamodreke vidhānamupadiśyate
|
uṣṇena bādhyate śītaṃ śītenoṣṇaṃ prabādhyate ||12||

13sattvaṃ rajastama iti mānasāḥ syustrayo guṇāḥ
|
teṣāṃ guṇānāṃ sāmyaṃ yattadāhuḥ svasthalakṣaṇam ||13||

14teṣāmanyatamotseke vidhānamupadiśyate
|
harṣeṇa bādhyate śoko harṣaḥ śokena bādhyate ||14||

15kaścitsukhe vartamāno duḥkhasya smartumicchati
|
kaścidduḥkhe vartamānaḥ sukhasya smartumicchati ||15||

16sa tvaṃ na duḥkhī duḥkhasya na sukhī ca sukhasya vā
|
na duḥkhī sukhajātasya na sukhī duḥkhajasya vā ||16||

17smartumicchasi kauravya diṣṭaṃ hi balavattaram
|
athavā te svabhāvo'yaṃ yena pārthiva kliśyase ||17||

18dṛṣṭvā sabhāgatāṃ kṛṣṇāmekavastrāṃ rajasvalām
|
miṣatāṃ pāṇḍuputrāṇāṃ na tasya smartumarhasi ||18||

19pravrājanaṃ ca nagarādajinaiśca vivāsanam
|
mahāraṇyanivāsaśca na tasya smartumarhasi ||19||

20jaṭāsurātparikleśaṃ citrasenena cāhavam
|
saindhavācca parikleśaṃ kathaṃ vismṛtavānasi ||20||

21punarajñātacaryāyāṃ kīcakena padā vadham
|
draupadyā rājaputryāśca kathaṃ vismṛtavānasi ||21||

22yacca te droṇabhīṣmābhyāṃ yuddhamāsīdarindama
|
manasaikena yoddhavyaṃ tatte yuddhamupasthitam ||22||

23yatra nāsti śaraiḥ kāryaṃ na mitrairne ca bandhubhiḥ
|
ātmanaikena yoddhavyaṃ tatte yuddhamupasthitam ||23||

24tasminnanirjite yuddhe prāṇānyadi vimokṣyase
|
anyaṃ dehaṃ samāsthāya tatastairapi yotsyase ||24||

25tasmādadyaiva gantavyaṃ yuddhyasva bharatarṣabha
|
paramavyaktarūpasya vyaktaṃ tyaktvā svakarmabhiḥ ||25||

26tasminnanirjite yuddhe kāmavasthāṃ gamiṣyasi
|
etajjitvā mahārāja kṛtakṛtyo bhaviṣyasi ||26||

27etāṃ buddhiṃ viniścitya bhūtānāmāgatiṃ gatim
|
pitṛpaitāmahe vṛtte śādhi rājyaṃ yathocitam ||27||

28diṣṭyā duryodhanaḥ pāpo nihataḥ sānuno yudhi
|
draupadyāḥ keśapāśasya diṣṭyā tvaṃ padavīṃ gataḥ ||28||

29yajasva vājimedhena vidhivaddakṣiṇāvatā
|
vayaṃ te kiṅkarāḥ pārtha vāsudevaśca vīryavān ||29||
