Mahābhārata 3.169 (Āraṇyakaparvan)

1arjuna uvāca|
tato'haṃ stūyamānastu tatra tatra maharṣibhiḥ
|
apaśyamudadhiṃ bhīmamapāṃ patimathāvyayam||1||

2phenavatyaḥ prakīrṇāśca saṃhatāśca samutthitāḥ
|
ūrmayaścātra dṛśyante valganta iva parvatāḥ||2||

3nāvaḥ sahasraśastatra ratnapūrṇāḥ samantataḥ
|
timiṅgilāḥ kacchapāśca tathā timitimiṅgilāḥ||3||

4makarāścātra dṛśyante jale magnā ivā'drayaḥ
|
śaṅkhānāṃ ca sahastrāṇi magnānyapsu samantataḥ||4||

5dṛśyante sma yathā rātrau tārāstanvabhrasaṃvṛtāḥ
|
tathā sahasraśastatra ratnasaṅghāḥ plavantyuta||5||

6vāyuśca ghūrṇate bhīmastada‌dbhutamivābhavat
|
tamudīkṣya mahāvegaṃ sarvāmbhonidhimuttamam||6||

7apaśyaṃ dānavākīrṇaṃ taddaityapuramantikāt
|
tatraiva mātalistūrṇaṃ nipatya pṛthivītale||7||

8rathaṃ taṃ tu samālakṣya prādravadrathayogavit
|
trāsayan rathaghoṣeṇa tat puraṃ samupādravat||8||

9rathaghoṣaṃ tu taṃ śrutvā stanayitnorivāmbare
|
manvānā devarājaṃ māmāvignā dānavā'bhavan||9||

10sarve saṃbhrāntamanasaḥ śaracāpadharāḥ sthitāḥ
|
tathā'si-śūla-paraśu-gadā-musala-pāṇayaḥ||10||

11tato dvārāṇi pidadhurdānavāstrastacetasaḥ
|
saṃvidhāya pure rakṣāṃ na sma kaścana dṛśyate||11||

12tataḥ śaṅkhamupādāya devadattaṃ mahāsvanam
|
paramāṃ sudamāśritya prādhamaṃ taṃ śanairaham||12||

13sa tu śabdo divaṃ stabdhvā pratiśabdamajījanat
|
vitresuśca nililyuśca bhūtāni sumahāntyapi||13||

14tato nivātakavacāḥ sarva eva khalaṃkṛtāḥ
|
daṃśitā vividhaistrāṇairvicitrāyudhapāṇayaḥ||14||

15āyaseśca mahāśūlairgadābhirmusalairapi
|
paṭṭiśaiḥ karavālaiśca rathacakraiśca bhārata||15||

16śataghnībhirbhuśuṇḍībhiḥ khaḍgaiścitraiḥ svalaṃkṛtaiḥ
|
pragṛhītairditeḥ putrāḥ prādurāsan sahastraśaḥ||16||

17tato vicārya bahuśo rathamārgeṣu tān hayān
|
prācodayat same deśe mātalirbharatarṣabha||17||

18tena teṣāṃ praṇunnānāmāśutvācchīghragāminām
|
nānvapaśyaṃ tadā kiṃcittanme'dbhutamivā'bhavat||18||

19tataste dānavāstatra vāditrāṇi sahasraśaḥ
|
vikṛtasvararūpāṇi bhṛśaṃ sarvāṇyanādayan||19||

20tena śabdena sahasā samudre parvatopamāḥ
|
āplavanta gataiḥ satyairmatsyāḥ śatasahasraśaḥ||20||

21tato vegena mahatā dānavā māmupādravan
|
vimuñcantaḥ śitān bāṇān śataśo'tha sahasraśaḥ||21||

22sa samprahārastumulasteṣāṃ ca mama bhārata
|
avartata mahāghoro nivātakavacāntakaḥ||22||

23tato devarṣayaścaiva dānavarṣigaṇāśca ye
|
brahmarṣayaśca siddhāśca samājagmurmahāmṛdhe||23||

24te vai māmanurūpābhirmadhurābhirjayaiṣiṇaḥ
|
astuvan munayo vāgbhiryathendraṃ tārakāmaye||24||
