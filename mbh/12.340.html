Mahābhārata 12.340 (Śāntiparvan)

1śaunaka uvāca|
kathaṃ sa bhagavāndevo yajñeṣvagraharaḥ prabhuḥ
|
yajñadhārī ca satataṃ vedavedāṅgavittathā||1||

2nivṛttaṃ cāsthito dharmaṃ kṣamī bhāgavataḥ prabhuḥ
|
nivṛttidharmānvidadhe sa eva bhagavānprabhuḥ||2||

3kathaṃ pravṛttidharmeṣu bhāgārhā devatāḥ kṛtāḥ
|
kathaṃ nivṛttidharmāśca kṛtā vyāvṛttabuddhayaḥ||3||

4etaṃ naḥ saṃśayaṃ saute chindhi guhyaṃ sanātanam
|
tvayā nārāyaṇakathāḥ śrutā vai dharmasaṃhitāḥ||4||

5sautiruvāca |
janamejayena yatpṛṣṭaḥ śiṣyo vyāsasya dhīmataḥ
|
tatte'haṃ kathayiṣyāmi paurāṇaṃ śaunakottama||5||

6śrutvā māhātmyametasya dehināṃ paramātmanaḥ
|
janamejayo mahāprājño vaiśampāyanamabravīt||6||

7janamejaya uvāca|
ime sabrahmakā lokāḥ sasurāsuramānavāḥ
|
kriyāsvabhyudayoktāsu saktā dṛśyanti sarvaśaḥ||7||

8mokṣaścoktastvayā brahmannirvāṇaṃ paramaṃ sukham
|
ye tu muktā bhavantīha puṇyapāpavivarjitāḥ||8||

9te sahasrārciṣaṃ devaṃ praviśantīha śuśruma
|
ayaṃ hi duranuṣṭheyo mokṣadharmaḥ sanātanaḥ||9||

10yaṃ hitvā devatāḥ sarvā havyakavyabhujo'bhavan
|
kiṃ ca brahmā ca rudraśca śakraśca balabhitprabhuḥ||10||

11sūryastārādhipo vāyuragnirvaruṇa eva ca
|
ākāśaṃ jagatī caiva ye ca śeṣā divaukasaḥ||11||

12pralayaṃ na vijānanti ātmanaḥ parinirmitam
|
tatastenāsthitā mārgaṃ dhruvamakṣaramavyayam||12||

13smṛtikālaparīmāṇaṃ pravṛttiṃ ye samāsthitāḥ
|
doṣaḥ kālaparīmāṇe mahāneṣa kriyāvatām||13||

14etanme saṃśayaṃ vipra hṛdi śalyamivārpitam
|
chindhītihāsakathanātparaṃ kautūhalaṃ hi me||14||

15kathaṃ bhāgaharāḥ proktā devatāḥ kratuṣu dvija
|
kimarthaṃ cādhvare brahmannijyante tridivaukasaḥ||15||

16ye ca bhāgaṃ pragṛhṇanti yajñeṣu dvijasattama
|
ye yajanto mahāyajñaiḥ kasya bhāgaṃ dadanti vai||16||

17vaiśampāyana uvāca|
aho gūḍhatamaḥ praśnastvayā pṛṣṭo janeśvara
|
nātaptatapasā hyeṣa nāvedaviduṣā tathā||17||

18nāpurāṇavidā caiva śakyo vyāhartumañjasā
|
hanta te kathayiṣyāmi yanme pṛṣṭaḥ purā guruḥ||18||

19kṛṣṇadvaipāyano vyāso vedavyāso mahānṛṣiḥ
|
sumanturjaiminiścaiva pailaśca sudṛḍhavrataḥ||19||

20ahaṃ caturthaḥ śiṣyo vai pañcamaśca śukaḥ smṛtaḥ
|
etānsamāgatānsarvānpañca śiṣyāndamānvitān||20||

21śaucācārasamāyuktāñjikrodhān jitendriyān
|
vedānadhyāpayāmāsa mahābhāratapañcamān||21||

22merau girivare ramye siddhacāraṇasevite
|
teṣāmabhyasyatāṃ vedān kadācitsaṃśayo'bhavat||22||

23eṣa vai yastvayā pṛṣṭastena teṣāṃ prakīrtitaḥ
|
tataḥ śruto mayā cāpi tavākhyeyo'dya bhārata||23||

24śiṣyāṇāṃ vacanaṃ śrutvā sarvājñānatamonudaḥ
|
parāśarasutaḥ śrīmānvyāso vākyamathābravīt||24||

25mayā hi sumahattaptaṃ tapaḥ paramadāruṇam
|
bhūtaṃ bhavyaṃ bhaviṣyaṃ ca jānīyāmiti sattamāḥ||25||

26tasya me taptatapaso nigṛhītendriyasya ca
|
nārāyaṇaprasādena kṣīrodasyānukūlataḥ||26||

27traikālikamidaṃ jñānaṃ prādurbhūtaṃ yathepsitam
|
tacchṛṇudhvaṃ yathānyāyaṃ vakṣye saṃśayamuttamam||27||

28yathā vṛttaṃ hi kalpādau dṛṣṭaṃ me jñānacakṣuṣā
|
paramātmeti yaṃ prāhuḥ sāṃkhyayogavido janāḥ||28||

29mahāpuruṣasaṃjñāṃ sa labhate svena karmaṇā
|
tasmātprasūtamavyaktaṃ pradhānaṃ taṃ vidurbudhāḥ||29||

30avyaktādvyaktamutpannaṃ lokasṛṣṭyarthamīśvarāt
|
aniruddho hi lokeṣu mahānātmeti kathyate||30||

31yo'sau vyaktatvamāpanno nirmame ca pitāmaham
|
so'haṃkāra iti proktaḥ sarvatejomayo hi saḥ||31||

32pṛthivī vāyurākāśamāpo jyotiśca pañcamam
|
ahaṃkāraprasūtāni mahābhūtāni pañcadhā||32||

33mahābhūtāni sṛṣṭvaiva tānguṇānnirmame punaḥ
|
bhūtebhyaścaiva niṣpannā mūrtimantaśca tān śṛṇu||33||

34marīciraṅgirāścātriḥ pulastyaḥ pulahaḥ kratuḥ
|
vasiṣṭhaśca mahātmā vai manuḥ svāyaṃbhuvastathā||34||

35jñeyāḥ prakṛtayo'ṣṭau tā yāsu lokāḥ pratiṣṭhitāḥ
|
vedavedāṅgasaṃyuktānyajñānyajñāṅgasaṃyutān||35||

36nirmame lokasiddhyarthaṃ brahmā lokapitāmahaḥ
|
aṣṭābhyaḥ prakṛtibhyaśca jātaṃ viśvamidaṃ jagat||36||

37rudro roṣātmako jāto daśānyānso'sṛjat svayam
|
ekādaśaite rudrāstu vikārapuruṣāḥ smṛtāḥ||37||

38te rudrāḥ prakṛtiścaiva sarve caiva surarṣayaḥ
|
utpannā lokasiddhyarthaṃ brahmāṇaṃ samupasthitāḥ||38||

39vayaṃ sṛṣṭā hi bhagavaṃstvayā ca prabhaviṣṇunā
|
yena yasminnadhīkāre vartitavyaṃ pitāmaha||39||

40yo'sau tvayābhinirdiṣṭo hyadhikāro'rthacintakaḥ
|
paripālyaḥ kathaṃ tena sāhaṃkāreṇa kartṛṇā||40||

41pradiśasva balaṃ tasya yo'dhikārārthacintakaḥ
|
evamukto mahādevo devāṃstānidamabravīt||41||

42brahmovāca|
sādhvahaṃ jñāpito devā yuṣmābhirbhadramastu vaḥ
|
mamāpyeṣā samutpannā cintā yā bhavatāṃ matā||42||

43lokatrayasya kṛtsnasya kathaṃ kāryaḥ parigrahaḥ
|
kathaṃ balakṣayo na syādyuṣmākaṃ hyātmanaśca me||43||

44itaḥ sarve'pi gacchāmaḥ śaraṇaṃ lokasākṣiṇam
|
mahāpuruṣamavyaktaṃ sa no vakṣyati yaddhitam||44||

45tataste brahmaṇā sārdhamṛṣayo vibudhāstathā
|
kṣīrodasyottaraṃ kūlaṃ jagmurlokahitārthinaḥ||45||

46te tapaḥ samupātiṣṭhanbrahmoktaṃ vedakalpitam
|
sa mahāniyamo nāma tapaścaryāsu dāruṇaḥ||46||

47ūrdhvā dṛṣṭirbāhavaśca ekāgraṃ ca mano'bhavat
|
ekapādāḥ sthitāḥ sarve kāṣṭhabhūtāḥ samāhitāḥ||47||

48divyaṃ varṣasahasraṃ te tapastaptvā sudāruṇam
|
śuśruvurmadhurāṃ vāṇīṃ vedavedāṅgabhūṣitām||48||

49śrībhagavānuvāca |
bho bhoḥ sabrahmakā devā ṛṣayaśca tapodhanāḥ
|
svāgatenārcya vaḥ sarvān śrāvaye vākyamuttamam||49||

50vijñātaṃ vo mayā kāryaṃ tacca lokahitaṃ mahat
|
pravṛttiyuktaṃ kartavyaṃ yuṣmatprāṇopabṛṃhaṇam||50||

51sutaptaṃ ca tapo devā mamārādhanakāmyayā
|
bhokṣyathāsya mahāsattvāstapasaḥ phalamuttamam||51||

52eṣa brahmā lokagururmahān lokapitāmahaḥ
|
yūyaṃ ca vibudhaśreṣṭhā māṃ yajadhvaṃ samāhitāḥ||52||

53sarve bhāgānkalpayadhvaṃ yajñeṣu mama nityaśaḥ
|
tathā śreyo'bhidhāsyāmi yathādhīkāramīśvarāḥ||53||

54vaiśampāyana uvāca|
śrutvaitaddevadevasya vākyaṃ hṛṣṭatanūruhāḥ
|
tataste vibudhāḥ sarve brahmā ca maharṣayaḥ||54||

55vedadṛṣṭena vidhinā vaiṣṇavaṃ kratumāharan
|
tasminsatre sadā brahmā svayaṃ bhāgamakalpayat||55||

56devā devarṣayaścaiva svaṃ svaṃ bhāgamakalpayan
|
te kārtayugadharmāṇo bhāgāḥ paramasatkṛtāḥ||56||

57prāhurādityavarṇaṃ taṃ puruṣaṃ tamasaḥ param
|
bṛhantaṃ sarvagaṃ devamīśānaṃ varadaṃ prabhum||57||

58tato'tha varado devastānsarvānamarānsthitān
|
aśarīro babhāṣedaṃ vākyaṃ khastho maheśvaraḥ||58||

59yena yaḥ kalpito bhāgaḥ sa tathā māmupāgataḥ
|
prīto'haṃ pradiśāmyadya phalamāvṛttilakṣaṇam||59||

60etadvo lakṣaṇaṃ devā matprasādasamudbhavam
|
svayaṃ yajñairyajamānāḥ samāptavaradakṣiṇaiḥ||60||

61yuge yuge bhaviṣyadhvaṃ pravṛttiphalabhāginaḥ
|
yajñairye cāpi yakṣyanti sarvalokeṣu vai surāḥ||61||

62kalpayiṣyanti vo bhāgāṃste narā vedakalpitān
|
yo me yathā kalpitavānbhāgamasminmahākratau||62||

63sa tathā yajñabhāgārho vedasūtre mayā kṛtaḥ
|
yūyaṃ lokānbhāvayadhvaṃ yajñabhāgaphalocitāḥ||63||

64sarvārthacintakā loke yathādhīkāranirmitāḥ
|
yāḥ kriyāḥ pracariṣyanti pravṛttiphalasatkṛtāḥ||64||

65ābhirāpyāyitabalā lokānvai dhārayiṣyatha
|
yūyaṃ hi bhāvitā yajñaiḥ sarvayajñeṣu mānavaiḥ||65||

66māṃ tato bhāvayiṣyadhvameṣā vo bhāvanā mama
|
ityarthaṃ nirmitā vedā yajñāścauṣadhibhiḥ saha||66||

67ebhiḥ samyakprayuktairhi prīyante devatāḥ kṣitau
|
nirmāṇametadyuṣmākaṃ pravṛttiguṇakalpitam||67||

68mayā kṛtaṃ suraśreṣṭhā yāvatkalpakṣayādiha
|
cintayadhvaṃ lokahitaṃ yathādhīkāramīśvarāḥ||68||

69marīciraṃgirāścātriḥ pulastyaḥ pulahaḥ kratuḥ
|
vasiṣṭha iti saptaite mānasā nirmitā hi te||69||

70ete vedavido mukhyā vedācāryāśca kalpitāḥ
|
pravṛttidharmiṇaścaiva prājāpatye ca kalpitāḥ||70||

71ayaṃ kriyāvatāṃ panthā vyaktībhūtaḥ sanātanaḥ
|
aniruddha iti prokto lokasargakaraḥ prabhuḥ||71||

72sanaḥ sanatsujātaśca sanakaḥ sasanandanaḥ
|
sanatkumāraḥ kapila: saptamaśca sanātanaḥ||72||

73saptaite mānasāḥ proktā ṛṣayo brahmaṇaḥ sutāḥ
|
svayamāgatavijñānā nivṛttiṃ dharmamāsthitāḥ||73||

74ete yogavido mukhyā: sāṃkhyajñānaviśāradāḥ
|
ācāryā dharmaśāstreṣu mokṣadharmapravartakāḥ||74||

75yato'haṃ prasṛtaḥ pūrvamavyaktāttriguṇo mahān
|
tasmātparataro yo'sau kṣetrajña iti kalpitaḥ||75||

76so'haṃ kriyāvatāṃ panthāḥ punarāvṛttidurlabhaḥ
|
yo yathā nirmito janturyasminyasmiṃśca karmaṇi||76||

77pravṛttau vā nivṛttau vā tatphalaṃ so'śnute mahat
|
eṣa lokagururbrahmā jagadādikaraḥ prabhuḥ||77||

78eṣa mātā pitā caiva yuṣmākaṃ ca pitāmahaḥ
|
mayā'nuśiṣṭo bhavitā sarvabhūtavarapradaḥ||78||

79asya caivātmajo rudro lalāṭādyaḥ samutthitaḥ
|
brahmānuśiṣṭo bhavitā sarvabhūtadharaḥ prabhuḥ||79||

80gacchadhvaṃ svānadhīkārāṃścintayadhvaṃ yathāvidhi
|
pravartantāṃ kriyāḥ sarvāḥ sarvalokeṣu mā ciram||80||

81pradiśyantāṃ ca karmāṇi prāṇināṃ gatayastathā
|
pariniṣṭhitakālāni āyūṃṣīha surottamāḥ||81||

82idaṃ kṛtayugaṃ nāma kālaḥ śreṣṭhaḥ pravartitaḥ
|
ahiṃsyā yajñapaśavo yuge'sminna tadanyathā||82||

83catuṣpātsakalo dharmo bhaviṣyatyatra vai surāḥ
|
tatastretāyugaṃ nāma trayī yatra bhaviṣyati||83||

84prokṣitā yatra paśavo vadhaṃ prāpsyanti vai makhe
|
yatra pādaścaturtho vai dharmasya na bhaviṣyati||84||

85tato vai dvāparaṃ nāma miśraḥ kālo bhaviṣyati
|
dvipādahīno dharmaśca yuge tasmin bhaviṣyati||85||

86tatastiṣye'tha saṃprāpte yuge kalipuraskṛte
|
ekapādasthito dharmo yatra tatra bhaviṣyati||86||

87devā devarṣayaścocustamevaṃvādinaṃ gurum
|
ekapādasthite dharme yatra kvacana gāmini||87||

88kathaṃ kartavyamasmābhirbhagavaṃstadvadasva naḥ
|
śrībhagavānuvāca |
yatra vedāśca yajñāśca tapaḥ satyaṃ damastathā||88||

89ahiṃsādharmasaṃyuktāḥ pracareyuḥ surottamāḥ
|
sa vo deśaḥ sevitavyo mā vo'dharmaḥ padā spṛśet||89||

90vyāsa uvāca |
te'nuśiṣṭā bhagavatā devāḥ sarṣigaṇāstathā
|
namaskṛtvā bhagavate jagmurdeśānyathepsitān||90||

91gateṣu tridivaukaḥsu brahmaikaḥ paryavasthitaḥ
|
didṛkṣurbhagavantaṃ tamaniruddhatanau sthitam||91||

92taṃ devo darśayāmāsa kṛtvā hayaśiro mahat
|
sāṅgānāvartayanvedānkamaṇḍalutridaṇḍadhṛk||92||

93tato'śvaśirasaṃ dṛṣṭvā taṃ devamamitaujasam
|
lokakartā prabhurbrahmā lokānāṃ hitakāmyayā||93||

94mūrdhnā praṇamya varadaṃ tasthau prāñjaliragrataḥ
|
sa pariṣvajya devena vacanaṃ śrāvitastadā||94||

95śrībhagavānuvāca |
lokakāryagatī: sarvāstvaṃ cintaya yathāvidhi
|
dhātā tvaṃ sarvabhūtānāṃ tvaṃ prabhurjagato guruḥ||95||

96tvayyāveśitabhāro'haṃ dhṛtiṃ prāpsyāmyathāñcasā
|
yadā ca surakāryaṃ te aviṣahyaṃ bhaviṣyati||96||

97prādurbhāvaṃ gamiṣyāmi tadā''tmajñānadaiśikaḥ
|
evamuktvā hayaśirāstatraivāntaradhīyata||97||

98tenānuśiṣṭo brahmāpi svalokamacirādgataḥ
|
evameṣa mahābhāgaḥ padmanābhaḥ sanātanaḥ||98||

99yajñeṣvagraharaḥ prokto yajñadhārī ca nityadā
|
nivṛttiṃ cāsthito dharmaṃ gatimakṣayadharmiṇām
|
pravṛttidharmānvidadhe kṛtvā lokasya citratām||99||

100yugādau prabuddho jagadvyutsasarja||100||

101tasmai namadhvaṃ devāya nirguṇāya mahātmane
|
ajāya viśvarūpāya dhāmne sarvadivaukasām||101||

102mahābhūtādhipataye rudrāṇāṃ pataye tathā
|
ādityapataye caiva vasūnāṃ pataye tathā||102||

103aśvibhyāṃ pataye caiva marutāṃ pataye tathā
|
vedayajñādhipataye vedāṅgapataye'pi ca||103||

104samudravāsine nityaṃ haraye muñjakeśine
|
śāntāya sarvabhūtānāṃ mokṣadharmānubhāṣiṇe||104||

105tapasāṃ tejasāṃ caiva pataye yaśasāmapi
|
vacasāṃ pataye nityaṃ saritāṃ pataye tathā||105||

106kapardine varāhāya ekaśṛṅgāya dhīmate
|
vivasvate'śvaśirase caturmūrtidhṛte sadā||106||

107guhyāya jñānadṛśyāya akṣarāya kṣarāya ca
|
eṣa devaḥ sañcarati sarvatragatiravyayaḥ||107||

108eṣa caitatparaṃ brahma jñeyo vijñānacakṣuṣā
|
evametatpurā dṛṣṭaṃ mayā vai jñānacakṣuṣā||108||

109kathitaṃ tacca vai sarvaṃ mayā pṛṣṭena tattvataḥ
|
kriyatāṃ madvacaḥ śiṣyāḥ sevyatāṃ harirīśvaraḥ
|
gīyatāṃ vedaśabdaiśca pūjyatāṃ ca yathāvidhi||109||

110vaiśampāyana uvāca|
ityuktāstu vayaṃ tena vedavyāsena dhīmatā
|
sarve śiṣyā: sutaścāsya śukaḥ paramadharmavit||110||

111sa cāsmākamupādhyāyaḥ sahāsmābhirviśāṃpate
|
caturvedodgatābhistamṛgbhiḥ samabhituṣṭuve||111||

112etatte sarvamākhyātaṃ yanmāṃ tvaṃ paripṛcchasi
|
evaṃ me'kathayadrājanpurā dvaipāyano guruḥ||112||

113yaścedaṃ śṛṇuyānnityaṃ yaścainaṃ parikīrtayet
|
namo bhagavate kṛtvā samāhitamatirnaraḥ||113||

114bhavatyarogo matimānbalarūpasamanvitaḥ
|
āturo mucyate rogādbaddho mucyeta bandhanāt||114||

115kāmānkāmī labhetkāmaṃ dīrghaṃ cāyuravāpnuyāt
|
brāhmaṇaḥ sarvavedī syātkṣatriyo vijayī bhavet||115||

116vaiśyo vipulalābhaḥ syācchūdraḥ sukhamavāpnuyāt
|
aputro labhate putraṃ kanyā caivepsitaṃ patim||116||

117lagnagarbhā vimucyeta garbhiṇī janayetsutam
|
vandhyā prasavamāpnoti putrapautrasamṛddhimat||117||

118kṣemeṇa gacchedadhvānamidaṃ yaḥ paṭhate pathi
|
yo'yaṃ kāmaṃ kāmayate sa tamāpnoti ca dhruvam||118||

119idaṃ maharṣervacanaṃ viniścitaṃ mahātmanaḥ puruṣavarasya kīrtitam
|
samāgamaṃ carṣidivaukasāmimaṃ niśamya bhaktāḥ susukhaṃ labhante||119||
