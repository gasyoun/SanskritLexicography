Mahābhārata 7.141 (Droṇaparvan)

1sañjaya uvāca|
tamudyataṃ mahābāhuṃ duḥśāsanarathaṃ prati
|
tvaritaṃ tvaraṇīyeṣu dhanañjayajayaiṣiṇam||1||

2trigartānāṃ maheṣvāsāḥ suvarṇavikṛtadhvajāḥ
|
senāsamudramāviṣṭamanantaṃ paryavārayan ||2||

3athainaṃ rathavaṃśena sarvataḥ sannivārya te
|
avākirañcharavrātaiḥ kruddhāḥ paramadhanvinaḥ||3||

4ajayadrājaputrāṃstān bhrājamānānmahāraṇe
|
ekaḥ pañcāśataṃ śatrūnsātyakiḥ satyavikramaḥ||4||

5saṃprāpya bhāratīmadhyaṃ talaghoṣasamākulam
|
asi-śakti-gadāpūrṇamaplavaṃ salilaṃ yathā ||5||

6tatrādbhutamapaśyāma śaineyacaritaṃ raṇe
|
pratīcyāṃ diśi taṃ dṛṣṭvā prācyāṃ paśyāmi lāghavāt||6||

7udīcīṃ dakṣiṇāṃ prācīṃ pratīcīṃ vidiśastathā
|
nṛtyannivācaracchūro yathā rathaśataṃ tathā||7||

8taddṛṣṭvā caritaṃ tasya siṃhavikrāntagāminaḥ
|
trigartāḥ saṃnyavartanta santaptāḥ svajanaṃ prati||8||

9tamanye śūrasenānāṃ śūrāḥ saṃkhye nyavārayan
|
niyacchantaḥ śaravrātairmattaṃ dvipamivāṅkuśaiḥ||9||

10tairvyavāharadāryātmā muhūrtādeva sātyakiḥ
|
tataḥ kaliṅgairyuyudhe so'cintyabalavikramaḥ||10||

11tāṃ ca senāmatikramya kaliṅgānāṃ duratyayām
|
atha pārthaṃ mahābāhurdhanañjayamupāsadat||11||

12taranniva jale śrānto yathā sthalamupeyivān
|
taṃ dṛṣṭvā puruṣavyāghraṃ yuyudhānaḥ samāśvasat||12||

13tamāyāntamabhiprekṣya keśavaḥ pārthamabravīt
|
asāvāyāti śaineyastava pārtha padānugaḥ||13||

14eṣa śiṣyaḥ sakhā caiva tava satyaparākramaḥ
|
sarvān yodhāṃstṛṇīkṛtya vijigye puruṣarṣabhaḥ||14||

15eṣa kauravayodhānāṃ kṛtvā ghoramupadravam
|
tava prāṇaiḥ priyatamaḥ kirīṭinneti sātyakiḥ||15||

16eṣa droṇaṃ tathā bhojaṃ kṛtavarmāṇameva ca
|
kadarthīkṛtya viśikhaiḥ phālgunābhyeti sātyakiḥ||16||

17dharmarājapriyānveṣī hatvā yodhān varān varān
|
śūraścaiva kṛtāstraśca phālgunābhyeti sātyakiḥ||17||

18kṛtvā suduṣkaraṃ karma sainyamadhye mahābalaḥ
|
tava darśanamanvicchan pāṇḍavābhyeti sātyakiḥ||18||

19bahūnekarathenājau yodhayitvā mahārathān
|
ācāryapramukhān pārtha prayātyeṣa sa sātyakiḥ||19||

20svabāhubalamāśritya vidārya ca varūthinīm
|
preṣito dharmarājena pārthaiṣo'bhyeti sātyakiḥ ||20||

21yasya nāsti samo yodhaḥ kauraveṣu kathañcana
|
so'yamāyāti kaunteya sātyakiryuddhadurmadaḥ||21||

22kurusainyādvimukto vai siṃho madhyādgavāmiva
|
nihatya bahulāḥsenāḥ pārthaiṣobhyeti sātyakiḥ||22||

23eṣa rājasahasrāṇāṃ vaktraiḥ paṅkajasannibhaiḥ
|
āstīrya vasudhāṃ pārtha kṣipramāyāti sātyakiḥ||23||

24eṣa duryodhanaṃ jitvā bhrātṛbhiḥ sahitaṃ raṇe
|
nihatya jalasandhaṃ ca kṣipramāyāti sātyakiḥ||24||

25rudhiraughavatīṃ kṛtvā nadīṃ śoṇitakardamām
|
tṛṇavadvyasya kauravyāneṣa hyāyāti sātyakiḥ||25||

26tataḥ prahṛṣṭaḥ kaunteyaḥ keśavaṃ vākyamabravīt
|
na me priyaṃ mahābāho yanmāmabhyeti sātyakiḥ||26||

27na hi jānāmi vṛttāntaṃ dharmarājasya keśava
|
sātvatena vihīnaḥ sa yadi jīvati vā na vā||27||

28etena hi mahābāho rakṣitavyaḥ sa pārthivaḥ
|
tameṣa kathamutsṛjya mama kṛṣṇa padānugaḥ||28||

29rājā droṇāya cotsṛṣṭaḥ saindhavaścānipātitaḥ
|
pratyudyāti ca śaineyameṣa bhūriśravā raṇe ||29||

30so'yaṃ gurutaro bhāraḥ saindhavārthe samāhitaḥ
|
jñātavyaśca hi me rājā rakṣitavyaśca sātyakiḥ||30||

31jayadrathaśca hantavyo lambate ca divākaraḥ
|
śrāntaścaiṣa mahābāhuralpaprāṇaśca sāṃpratam||31||

32pariśrāntā hayāścāsya hayayantā ca mādhava
|
na ca bhūriśravā śrāntaḥ sasahāyaśca keśava||32||

33apīdānīṃ bhavedasya kṣemamasmin samāgame
|
kaścinna sāgaraṃ tīrtvā sātyakiḥ satyavikramaḥ||33||

34goṣpadaṃ prāpya sīdeta mahaujāḥ śinipuṅgavaḥ
|
api kauravamukhyena kṛtāstreṇa mahātmanā||34||

35sametya bhūriśravasā svastimānsātyakirbhavet
|
vyatikramamimaṃ manye dharmarājasya keśava||35||

36ācāryādbhayamutsṛjya yaḥ praiṣayata sātyakim
|
grahaṇaṃ dharmarājasya khagaḥ śyena ivāmiṣam||36||

37nityamāśaṃsate droṇaḥ kaccit syātkuśalī nṛpaḥ||37||
