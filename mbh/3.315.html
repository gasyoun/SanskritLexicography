Mahābhārata 3.315 (Āraṇyakaparvan)

1vaiśampāyana uvāca|
dharmeṇa te'bhyanujñātāḥ pāṇḍavāḥ satyavikramāḥ
|
ajñātavāsaṃ vatsyantacchannā varṣaṃ trayodaśam||1||

2upopaviṣṭā vidvāṃsaḥ sahitāḥ saṃśitavratāḥ
|
ye tadbhaktā vasanti sma vanavāse tapasvinaḥ||2||

3tānabruvanmahātmānaḥ sthitāḥ prāñjalayastadā
|
abhyanujñāpayiṣyantastaṃ nivāsaṃ dhṛtavratāḥ||3||

4viditaṃ bhavatāṃ sarvaṃ dhārtarāṣṭairyathā vayam
|
chadmanā hṛtarājyāścānayāśca bahuśaḥ kṛtāḥ||4||

5uṣitāśca vane kṛcchre vayaṃ dvādaśavatsarān
|
ajñātavāsasamayaṃ śeṣaṃ varṣaṃ trayodaśam||5||

6tadvasāmo vayaṃ channāstadanujñātumarhatha
|
suyodhanaśca duṣṭātmā karṇaśca sahasaubalaḥ||6||

7jānato viṣamaṃ kuryurasmāsvatyantavairiṇaḥ
|
yuktacārāśca yuktāśca paurasya svajanasya ca||7||

8api nastadbhavedbhūyo yadvayaṃ brāhmaṇaiḥ saha
|
samastāḥ sveṣu rāṣṭreṣu svarājyasthā bhavemahi||8||

9vaiśampāyana uvāca|
ityuktvā duḥkhaśokārtī śucirdharmasutastadā
|
sammūrchito'bhavadrājā sāśrukaṇṭho yudhiṣṭhiraḥ||9||

10tamathāśvāsayan sarve brāhmaṇā bhrātṛbhiḥ saha
|
atha dhaumyo'bravīdvākyaṃ mahārthaṃ nṛpatiṃ tadā||10||

11rājan vidvān bhavān dāntaḥ satyasandho jitendriyaḥ
|
naivaṃvidhāḥ pramuhyante narāḥ kasyāñcidāpadi||11||

12devairapyāpadaḥ prāptācchannaiśca bahuśastathā
|
tatra tatra sapatnānāṃ nigrahārthaṃ mahātmabhiḥ||12||

13indreṇa niṣadhān prāpya giriprasthāśrame tadā
|
channenoṣya kṛtaṃ karma dviṣatāṃ ca vinigrahe||13||

14viṣṇunāśvaśiraḥ prāpya tathā'dityāṃ nivatsyatā
|
garbhe vadhārthaṃ daityānāmajñātenoṣitaṃ ciram||14||

15prāpya vāmanarupeṇa pracchannaṃ brahmarupiṇā
|
baleryathā hṛtaṃ rājyaṃ vikramaistacca te śrutam||15||

16hutāśanena yaccāpaḥ praviśya channamāsatā
|
vibudhānāṃ kṛtaṃ karma tacca sarvaṃ śrutaṃ tvayā||16||

17pracchannaṃ cāpi dharmajña hariṇā'rivinigrahe
|
vajraṃ praviśya śakrasya yat kṛtaṃ tacca te śrutam||17||

18aurveṇa vasatā channamūrau brahmarṣiṇā tadā
|
yat kṛtaṃ tāta deveṣu karma tatte'nagha śrutam||18||

19evaṃ vivasvatā tāta channenottamatejasā
|
nirdagdhāḥ śātravāḥ sarve vasatā bhuvi sarvaśaḥ||19||

20viṣṇunā vasatā cāpi gṛhe daśarathasya vai
|
daśagrīvo hatacchannaṃ saṃyuge bhīmakarmaṇā||20||

21evameva mahātmānaḥ pracchannāstatra tatra ha
|
ajayañchātravān yuddhe tathā tvamapi jeṣyasi||21||

22tathā dhaumyena dharmajño vākyaiḥ samparitoṣitaḥ
|
śāstrabuddhyā svabuddhyā ca na cacāla yudhiṣṭhiraḥ||22||

23athābravīt mahābāhurbhīmaseno mahābalaḥ
|
rājānaṃ balināṃ śreṣṭho girā sampariharṣayan||23||

24avekṣayā mahārāja tava gāṇḍīvadhanvanā
|
dharmānugatayā buddhyā na kiñcit sāhasaṃ kṛtam||24||

25sahadevo mayā nityaṃ nakulaśca nivāritau
|
śaktau vidhvaṃsane teṣāṃ śatrūṇāṃ bhīmavikramau||25||

26na vayaṃ tatprahāsyāmo yasmin mokṣyati no bhavān
|
bhavān vidhattāṃ tat sarvaṃ kṣipraṃ jeṣyāmahe ripūn||26||

27ityukte bhīmasenena brāhmaṇāḥ paramāśiṣā
|
uktvā cāpṛcchya bharatānyathāsvānsvānyayurgṛhān||27||

28sarve vedavido mukhyā yatayo munayastathā
|
āseduste yathānyāyaṃ punardarśanakāṅkṣayā||28||

29saha dhaumyena vidāṃsastathā pañca ca pāṇḍavāḥ
|
utthāya prayayurvīrāḥ kṛṣṇāmādāya dhanvinaḥ||29||

30krośamātramupāgamya tasmāddeśānnimittataḥ
|
śvobhūte manujavyāghrāśchannavāsārthamudyatāḥ||30||

31pṛthak śāstravidaḥ sarve sarve mantraviśāradāḥ
|
sandhivigrahakālajñā mantrāya samupāviśan||31||
