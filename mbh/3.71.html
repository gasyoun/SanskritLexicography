Mahābhārata 3.71 (Āraṇyakaparvan)

1bṛhadaśva uvāca| śrutvā vacaḥ sudevasya ṛtuparṇo narādhipaḥ
|
sāntvayañcchlakṣṇayā vācā bāhukaṃ pratyabhāṣata||1||

2vidarbhānyātumicchāmi damayantyāḥ svayaṃvaram
|
ekāhnā hayatattvajña manyase yadi bāhuka||2||

3evamuktasya kaunteya tena rājñā nalasya ha
|
vyadīryata mano duḥkhātpradadhyau ca mahāmanāḥ||3||

4damayantī vadedetatkuryādduḥkhena mohitā
|
asmadarthe bhavedvāyamupāyaścintito mahān||4||

5nṛśaṃsaṃ bata vaidarbhī bhartṛkāmā tapasvinī
|
mayā kṣudreṇa nikṛtā kṛpaṇā pāpabuddhinā||5||

6strīsvabhāvaścalo loke mama doṣaśca dāruṇaḥ
|
syādevamapi kuryātsā vivāsādgatasauhṛdā||6||

7mama śokena saṃvignā nairāśyāttanumadhyamā
|
naivaṃ sā karhicitkuryātsāpatyā ca viśeṣataḥ||7||

8yadatra satyaṃ vā'satyaṃ gatvā vetsyāmi niścayam
|
ṛtuparṇasya vai kāmamātmārthaṃ ca karomyaham||8||

9iti niścitya manasā bāhuko dīnamānasaḥ
|
kṛtāñjaliruvācedamṛtuparṇaṃ janādhipam||9||

10pratijānāmi te vākyaṃ gamiṣyāmi narādhipa
|
ekāhnā puruṣavyāghra vidarbhanagarīṃ nṛpa||10||

11tataḥ parīkṣāmaśvānāṃ cakre rājansa bāhukaḥ
|
aśvaśālāmupāgamya bhāṅgāsurinṛpājñayā||11||

12sa tvaryamāṇo bahuśa ṛtuparṇena bāhukaḥ
|
aśvāñjijñāsamāno vai vicārya ca punaḥpunaḥ
|
adhyagacchatkṛśānaśvānsamarthānadhvani kṣamān||12||

13tejobalasamāyuktānkulaśīlasamanvitān
|
varjitā~llakṣaṇairhīnaiḥ pṛthuprothānmahāhanūn||13||

14śuddhāndaśabhirāvartaiḥ sindhujānvātaraṃhasaḥ
|
dṛṣṭvā tānabravīdrājā kiñcitkopasamanvitaḥ||14||

15kimidaṃ prārthitaṃ kartuṃ pralabdhavyā na te vayam
|
kathamalpabalaprāṇā vakṣyantīme hayā mama
|
mahadadhvānamapi ca gantavyaṃ kathamīdṛśaiḥ||15||

16bāhuka uvāca|
eko lalāṭe dve mūrdhni dvau dvau pārśvopapārśvayoḥ
|
dvau dvau vakṣasi vijñeyau prayāṇe caika eva tu||16||

17ete hayā gamiṣyanti vidarbhānnātra saṃśayaḥ
|
yānanyānmanyase rājanbrūhi tānyojayāmi te||17||

18ṛtuparṇa uvāca| tvameva hayatattvajñaḥ kuśalo hyasi bāhuka
|
yānmanyase samarthāṃstvaṃ kṣipraṃ tāneva yojaya||18||

19tataḥ sadaśvāṃścaturaḥ kulaśīlasamanvitān
|
yojayāmāsa kuśalo javayuktānrathe nalaḥ||19||

20tato yuktaṃ rathaṃ rājā samārohattvarānvitaḥ
|
atha paryapatanbhūmau jānubhiste hayottamāḥ||20||

21tato naravaraḥ śrīmānnalo rājā viśāṃpate
|
sāntvayāmāsa tānaśvāṃstejobalasamanvitān||21||

22raśmibhiśca samudyamya nalo yātumiyeṣa saḥ
|
sūtamāropya vārṣṇeyaṃ javamāsthāya vai param||22||

23te codyamānā vidhivadbāhukena hayottamāḥ
|
samutpeturathākāśaṃ rathinaṃ mohayanniva||23||

24tathā tu dṛṣṭvā tānaśvānvahato vātaraṃhasaḥ
|
ayodhyādhipatiḥ śrīmānvismayaṃ paramaṃ yayau||24||

25rathaghoṣaṃ tu taṃ śrutvā hayasaṅgrahaṇaṃ ca tat
|
vārṣṇeyaścintayāmāsa bāhukasya hayajñatām||25||

26kiṃ nu syānmātalirayaṃ devarājasya sārathiḥ
|
tathā tallakṣaṇaṃ vīre bāhuke dṛśyate mahat||26||

27śālihotro'tha kiṃ nu syāddhayānāṃ kulatattvavit
|
mānuṣaṃ samanuprāpto vapuḥ paramaśobhanam||27||

28utāhosvidbhavedrājā nalaḥ parapurañjayaḥ
|
so'yaṃ nṛpatirāyāta ityevaṃ samacintayat||28||

29atha ceha nalo vidyāṃ vetti tāmeva bāhukaḥ
|
tulyaṃ hi lakṣaye jñānaṃ bāhukasya nalasya ca||29||

30api cedaṃ vayastulyaṃ bāhukasya nalasya ca
|
nāyaṃ nalo mahāvīryastadvidyaśca bhaviṣyati||30||

31pracchannā hi mahātmānaścaranti pṛthivīmimām
|
daivena vidhinā yuktāḥ śāstroktaiśca nirūpaṇaiḥ||31||

32bhavenna matibhedo me gātravairūpyatāṃ prati
|
pramāṇātparihīnastu bhavediti matirmama||32||

33vayaḥpramāṇaṃ tattulyaṃ rūpeṇa tu viparyayaḥ
|
nalaṃ sarvaguṇairyuktaṃ manye bāhukamantataḥ||33||

34evaṃ vicārya bahuśo vārṣṇoyaḥ paryacintayat
|
hṛdayena mahārāja puṇyaślokasya sārathiḥ||34||

35ṛtuparṇaśca rājendro bāhukasya hayajñatām
|
cintayanmumude rājā sahavārṣṇeyasārathiḥ||35||

36aikāgryaṃ ca tathotsāhaṃ hayasaṅgrahaṇaṃ ca tat
|
paraṃ yatnaṃ ca samprekṣya parāṃ mudamavāpa ha||36||
