Mahābhārata 4.29 (Virāṭaparvan)

1vaiśampāyana uvāca|tataḥ śāradvato vākyamityuvāca kṛpastadā
|
yuktaṃ prāptaṃ ca vṛddhena pāṇḍavān prati bhāṣitam ||1||

2dharmārthasahitaṃ ślakṣṇaṃ tattvataśca sahetukam
|
tatrānurūpaṃ bhīṣmeṇa mamāpyatra giraṃ śṛṇu ||2||

3teṣāṃ caiva gatistīrthairvāsaścaiṣāṃ pracintyatām
|
nītirvidhīyatāṃ cāpi sāmprataṃ yā hitā bhavet ||3||

4nāvajñeyo ripustāta prākṛto'pi bubhūṣatā
|
kiṃ punaḥ pāṇḍavāstāta sarvāstrakuśalā raṇe ||4||

5tasmāt satraṃ praviṣṭeṣu pāṇḍaveṣu mahātmasu
|
gūḍhabhāveṣu channeṣu kāle codayamāgate ||5||

6svarāṣṭre pararāṣṭre ca jñātavyaṃ balamātmanaḥ
|
udayaḥ pāṇḍavānāṃ ca prāpte kāle na saṃśayaḥ ||6||

7nivṛttasamayāḥ pārthā mahātmāno mahābalāḥ
|
mahotsāhā bhaviṣyanti pāṇḍavā hyamitaujasaḥ ||7||

8tasmādbalaṃ ca kośaśca nītiścāpi vidhīyatām
|
yathākālodaye prāpte samyaktaiḥ sandadhāmahe ||8||

9tāta buddhyā'pi tat sarvaṃ buddhyasva balamātmanaḥ
|
niyataṃ sarvamitreṣu balavatsvabaleṣu ca ||9||

10uccāvacaṃ balaṃ jñātvā madhyasthaṃ cāpi bhārata
|
prahṛṣṭamaprahṛṣṭaṃ ca sandadhāma tathā paraiḥ ||10||

11sāmnā dānena bhedena daṇḍena balikarmaṇā
|
nyāyenākramya ca parān balāccānamya durbalān ||11||

12sāntvayitvā tu mitrāṇi balaṃ cābhāṣyatāṃ sukham
|
sukośabalasaṃvṛddhaḥ samyak siddhimavāpsyasi ||12||

13yotsyase cāpi balibhiraribhiḥ pratyupasthitaiḥ
|
anyaistvaṃ pāṇḍavairvā'pi hīnaiḥ svabalavāhanaiḥ ||13||

14evaṃ sarvaṃ viniścitya vyavasāyaṃ svadharmataḥ
|
yathākālaṃ manuṣyendra ciraṃ sukhamavāpsyasi ||14||
