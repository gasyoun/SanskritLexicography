Mahābhārata 6.72 (Bhīṣmaparvan)

1sañjaya uvāca|
śikhaṇḍī saha matsyena virāṭena viśāṃpate
|
bhīṣmamāśu maheṣvāsamāsasāda sudurjayam ||1||

2droṇaṃ kṛpaṃ vikarṇaṃ ca maheṣvāsaṃ mahābalam
|
rājñaścānyān raṇe śūrān bahūnārcchaddhanañjayaḥ ||2||

3saindhavaṃ ca maheṣvāsaṃ sāmātyaṃ saha bandhubhiḥ
|
prācyāṃśca dākṣiṇātyāṃśca bhūmipān bhūmiparṣabha ||3||

4putraṃ ca te maheṣvāsaṃ duryodhanamamarṣaṇam
|
duḥsahaṃ caiva samare bhīmaseno'bhyavartata ||4||

5sahadevastu śakunimulūkaṃ ca mahāratham
|
pitāputrau maheṣvāsāvabhyavartata durjayau ||5||

6yudhiṣṭhiro mahārāja gajānīkaṃ mahārathaḥ
|
samavartata saṅgrāme putreṇa nikṛtastava ||6||

7mādrīputrastu nakulaḥ śūrasaṃkrandano yudhi
|
trigartānāṃ balaiḥ sārdhaṃ samasajjata pāṇḍavaḥ ||7||

8abhyavartanta saṃkruddhāḥ samare śālvakekayān
|
sātyakiścekitānaśca saubhadraśca mahārathaḥ ||8||

9dhṛṣṭaketuśca samare rākṣasaśca ghaṭotkacaḥ
|
putrāṇāṃ te rathānīkaṃ pratyudyātāḥ sudurjayāḥ ||9||

10senāpatirameyātmā dhṛṣṭadyumno mahābalaḥ
|
droṇena samare rājan samiyāyograkarmaṇā ||10||

11evamete maheṣvāsāstāvakāḥ pāṇḍavaiḥ saha
|
sametya samare śūrāḥ saṃprahāraṃ pracakrire ||11||

12madhyaṃdinagate sūrye nabhasyākulatāṃ gate
|
kuravaḥ pāṇḍaveyāśca nijaghnuritaretaram ||12||

13dhvajino hemacitrāṅgā vicaranto raṇājire
|
sapatākā rathā rejurvaiyāghraparivāraṇāḥ ||13||

14sametānāṃ ca samare jigīṣūṇāṃ parasparam
|
babhūva tumulaḥ śabdaḥ siṃhānāmiva nardatām ||14||

15tatrādbhutamapaśyāma saṃprahāraṃ sudāruṇam
|
yadakurvan raṇe śūrāḥ sṛñjayāḥ kurubhiḥ saha ||15||

16naiva khaṃ na diśo rājanna sūryaṃ śatrutāpana
|
vidiśo vā'pi paśyāmaḥ śarairmuktaiḥ samantataḥ ||16||

17śaktīnāṃ vimalāgrāṇāṃ tomarāṇāṃ tathā'syatām
|
nistriṃśānāṃ ca pītānāṃ nīlotpalanibhāḥ prabhāḥ ||17||

18kavacānāṃ vicitrāṇāṃ bhūṣaṇānāṃ prabhāstathā
|
khaṃ diśaḥ pradiśaścaiva bhāsayāmāsurojasā ||18||

19vapurbhiśca narendrāṇāṃ candrasūryasamaprabhaiḥ
|
virarāja tadā rājaṃstatra tatra raṇāṅgaṇam ||19||

20rathasaṅghā naravyāghrāḥ samāyāntaśca saṃyuge
|
virejuḥ samare rājan grahā iva nabhastale ||20||

21bhīṣmastu rathināṃ śreṣṭho bhīmasenaṃ mahābalam
|
avārayata saṃkruddhaḥ sarvasainyasya paśyataḥ ||21||

22tato bhīṣmavinirmuktā rukmapuṅkhāḥ śilāśitāḥ
|
abhyaghnan samare bhīmaṃ tailadhautāḥ sutejanāḥ ||22||

23tasya śaktiṃ mahāvegāṃ bhīmaseno mahābalaḥ
|
kruddhāśīviṣasaṅkāśāṃ preṣayāmāsa bhārata ||23||

24tāmāpatantīṃ sahasā rukmadaṇḍāṃ durāsadām
|
ciccheda samare bhīṣmaḥ śaraiḥ sannataparvabhiḥ ||24||

25tato'pareṇa bhallena pītena niśitena ca
|
kārmukaṃ bhīmasenasya dvidhā ciccheda bhārata ||25||

26sātyakistu tatastūrṇaṃ bhīṣmamāsādya saṃyuge
|
ākarṇaprahitaistīkṣṇairniśitaistigmatejanaiḥ ||26||

27śarairbahubhirānarchat pitaraṃ te janeśvara
|
tataḥ saṃghāya vai tīkṣṇaṃ śaraṃ paramadāruṇam ||27||

28vārṣṇeyasya rathādbhīṣmaḥ pātayāmāsa sārathim
|
tasyāśvāḥ pradrutā rājannihate rathasārathau ||28||

29tena tenaiva dhāvanti manomārutaraṃhasaḥ
|
tataḥ sarvasya sainyasya niḥsvanastumulo'bhavat ||29||

30hāhākāraśca saṃjajñe pāṇḍavānāṃ mahātmanām
|
abhyadravata gṛhṇīta hayānyacchata dhāvata ||30||

31ityāsīttumulaḥ śabdo yuyudhānarathaṃ prati
|
etasminneva kāle tu bhīṣmaḥ śāntanavastadā ||31||

32nyahanat pāṇḍavīṃ senāmāsurīmiva vṛtrahā
|
te vadhyamānā bhīṣmeṇa pañcālāḥ somakaiḥ saha ||32||

33sthirāṃ yuddhe matiṃ kṛtvā bhīṣmamevābhidudruvuḥ
|
dhṛṣṭadyumnamukhāścāpi pārthāḥ śāntanavaṃ raṇe ||33||

34abhyadhāvan jigīṣantastava putrasya vāhinīm
|
tathaiva kauravā rājan bhīṣmadroṇapurogamāḥ ||34||

35abhyadhāvanta vegena tato yuddhamavartata ||35||
