Mahābhārata 8.30 (Karṇaparvan)

1sañjaya uvāca|
tataḥ karṇaṃ puraskṛtya tvadīyā yuddhadurmadāḥ
|
punarāvṛtya saṃgrāmaṃ cakrurdevāsuropamam ||1||

2dvirada-nara-rathāśvaśaṅkhaśabdaiḥ parihṛṣitā vividhaiśca śastrapātaiḥ
|
dvirada-ratha-padātisādisaṅghāḥ parikupitābhimukhāḥ prajaghnire te ||2||

3śitaparaśvadhasāsipaṭṭiśairiṣubhiranekavidhaiśca sūditāḥ
|
dvirada-ratha-hayā mahāhave varapuruṣaiḥ puruṣāśca vāhanaiḥ ||3||

4kamaladinakarendusannibhaiḥ sitadaśanaiḥ sumukhākṣināsikaiḥ
|
ruciramukuṭakuṇḍalairmahī puruṣaśirobhirupastṛtā babhau ||4||

5parigha-musala-śakti-tomarai-rnakhara-bhuśuṇḍi-gadāśatairhatāḥ
|
dvirada-nara-hayāḥ sahasraśo rudhiranadīpravahāstadā'bhavan ||5||

6prahataratha-narāśva-kuñjaraṃ pratibhayadarśanamulbaṇavraṇam
|
tadahitahatamābabhau balaṃ pitṛpatirāṣṭramiva prajākṣaye ||6||

7atha tava naradeva sainikā-stava ca sutāḥ surasūnusannibhāḥ
|
amitabalapuraḥsarā raṇe kuruvṛṣabhāḥ śiniputramabhyayuḥ ||7||

8tadatirudhirabhīmamābabhau puruṣavarāśva-ratha-dvipākulam
|
lavaṇajalasamuddhatasvanaṃ balamasurāmarasainyasaprabham ||8||

9surapatisamavikramastatastridaśavarāvarajopamaṃ yudhi
|
dinakarakiraṇaprabhaiḥ pṛṣatkairavitanayo'bhyahanacchinipravīram ||9||

10tamapi sarathavājisārathiṃ śinivṛṣabho vividhaiḥ śaraistvaran
|
bhujagaviṣasamaprabhai raṇe puruṣavaraṃ samavāstṛṇottadā ||10||

11śinivṛṣabhaśarairnipīḍitaṃ tava suhṛdo vasuṣeṇamabhyayuḥ
|
tvaritamatirathā ratharṣabhaṃ dviradarathāśvapadātibhiḥ saha ||11||

12taduda‌dhinibhamādravadbalaṃ tvaritataraiḥ samabhidrutaṃ paraiḥ
|
drupadasutamukhaistadā'bhavat puruṣarathāśvagajakṣayo mahān ||12||

13atha puruṣavarau kṛtāhnikau bhavamabhipūjya yathāvidhi prabhum
|
arivadhakṛtaniścayau drutaṃ tava balamarjunakeśavau sṛtau ||13||

14jalada‌ninadaniḥsvanaṃ rathaṃ pavanavidhūtapatākaketanam
|
sitahayamupayāntamantikaṃ kṛtamanaso dadṛśustadā'rayaḥ ||14||

15atha visphārya gāṇḍīvaṃ rathe nṛtyannivārjunaḥ
|
śarasambādhamakarotkhaṃ diśaḥ pradiśastathā ||15||

16rathānvimānapratimānmajjayansāyudhadhvajān
|
sasārathīṃstadā bāṇairabhrāṇīvānilo'vadhīt ||16||

17gajān gajaprayantṝṃśca vaijayantyāyudhadhvajān
|
sādino'śvāṃśca pattīṃśca śarairninye yamakṣayam ||17||

18tamantakamiva kruddhamanivāryaṃ mahāratham
|
duryodhano'bhyayādeko nighnan bāṇairajihmagaiḥ ||18||

19tasyārjuno dhanuḥ sūtamaśvānketuṃ ca sāyakaiḥ
|
hatvā saptabhirekena chatraṃ ciccheda patriṇā ||19||

20navamaṃ ca samādhāya vyasṛjatprāṇaghātinam
|
duryodhanāyeṣuvaraṃ taṃ drauṇiḥ saptadhā'cchinat ||20||

21tato drauṇerdhanuśchittvā hatvā cāśvarathāñśaraiḥ
|
kṛpasyāpi tadatyugraṃ dhanuściccheda pāṇḍavaḥ ||21||

22hārdikyasya dhanuśchittvā dhvajaṃ cāśvāṃstadā'vadhīt
|
duḥśāsanasyeṣvasanaṃ chittvā rādheyamabhyayāt ||22||

23atha sātyakimutsṛjya tvarankarṇo'rjunaṃ tribhiḥ
|
viddhvā vivyādha viṃśatyā kṛṣṇaṃ pārthaṃ punaḥ punaḥ ||23||

24na glānirāsītkarṇasya kṣipataḥ sāyakān bahun
|
raṇe vinighnataḥ śatrūn kruddhasyeva śatakratoḥ ||24||

25atha sātyakirāgatya karṇaṃ viddhvā śitaiḥ śaraiḥ
|
navatyā navabhiścograiḥ śatena punarārpayat ||25||

26tataḥ pravīrāḥ pārthānāṃ sarve karṇamapīḍayan
|
yudhāmanyuḥ śikhaṇḍī ca draupadeyāḥ prabhadrakāḥ ||26||

27uttamaujā yuyutsuśca yamau pārṣata eva ca
|
cedikārūṣamatsyānāṃ kaikayānāṃ ca yadbalam ||27||

28cekitānaśca balavān dharmarājaśca suvrataḥ
|
ete rathāśvadviradaiḥ pattibhiścogravikramaiḥ ||28||

29parivārya raṇe karṇaṃ nānāśastrairavākiran
|
bhāṣanto vāgbhirugrābhiḥ sarve karṇavadhe dhṛtāḥ ||29||

30tāṃ śastravṛṣṭiṃ bahudhā karṇaśchittvā śitaiḥ śaraiḥ
|
apovāhāstravīryeṇa drumaṃ bhaṅktve mārutaḥ ||30||

31rathinaḥ samahāmātrān gajānaśvānsasādinaḥ
|
pattivrātāṃśca saṃkruddho nighnan karṇo vyadṛśyata ||31||

32tadvadhyamānaṃ pāṇḍūnāṃ balaṃ karṇāstratejasā
|
viśastrapatradehāsu prāya āsītparāṅmukham ||32||

33atha karṇāstramastreṇa pratihatyārjunaḥ smayan
|
diśaṃ khaṃ caiva bhūmiṃ ca prāvṛṇoccharavṛṣṭibhiḥ ||33||

34musalānīva saṃpetuḥ parighā iva ceṣavaḥ
|
śatadhnya iva cāpyanye vajrāṇyugrāṇi cāpare ||34||

35tairvadhyamānaṃ tat sainyaṃ sapattyaśva-ratha-dvipam
|
nimīlitākṣamatyarthaṃ babhrāma ca nanāda ca ||35||

36niṣkaivalyaṃ tadā yuddhaṃ prāpuraśva-nara-dvipāḥ
|
hanyamānāḥ śarairārtāstadā bhītāḥ pradudruvuḥ ||36||

37tvadīyānāṃ tadā yuddhe saṃsaktānāṃ jayaiṣiṇām
|
girimastaṃ samāsādya pratyapadyata bhānumān ||37||

38tamasā ca mahārāja rajasā ca viśeṣataḥ
|
na kiṃcitpratyapaśyāma śubhaṃ vā yadi vā'śubham ||38||

39te trasyanto maheṣvāsā rātriyuddhasya bhārata
|
apayānaṃ tataścakruḥ sahitāḥ sarvayodhibhiḥ ||39||

40kauraveṣvapayāteṣu tadā rājandinakṣaye
|
jayaṃ sumanasaḥ prāpya pārthāḥ svaśibiraṃ yayuḥ ||40||

41vāditraśabdairvividhaiḥ siṃhanādaiḥ sagarjitaiḥ
|
parānupahasantaśca stuvantaścācyutārjunau ||41||

42kṛte'vahāre tairvīraiḥ sainikāḥ sarva eva te
|
āśīrvācaḥ pāṇḍaveṣu prāyuñjanta nareśvarāḥ ||42||

43tataḥ kṛte'vahāre ca prahṛṣṭāstatra pāṇḍavāḥ
|
niśāyāṃ śibiraṃ gatvā nyavasanta nareśvarāḥ ||43||

44tato rakṣaḥ piśācāśca śvāpadāścaiva saṃghaśaḥ
|
jagmurāyodhanaṃ ghoraṃ rudrasyākrīḍasannibham ||44||
