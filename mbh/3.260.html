Mahābhārata 3.260 (Āraṇyakaparvan)

1yudhiṣṭhira uvāca|
vrīhidroṇaḥ parityaktaḥ kathaṃ tena mahātmanā
|
kasmai dattaśca bhagavan vidhinā kena cāttha me||1||

2pratyakṣadharmā bhagavān yasya tuṣṭo hi karmabhiḥ
|
saphalaṃ tasya janmāhaṃ manye saddharmacāriṇaḥ||2||

3vyāsa uvāca|
śiloñchavṛttirdharmātmā mudgalaḥ saṃyatendriyaḥ
|
āsīdrājan kurukṣetre satyavāganasūyakaḥ||3||

4atithivratī kriyāvāṃśca kāpotīṃ vṛttimāsthitaḥ
|
satramiṣṭīkṛtaṃ nāma samupāste mahātapāḥ||4||

5saputradāro hi muniḥ pakṣāhāro babhūva ha
|
kapotavṛttyā pakṣeṇa vrīhidroṇamupārjayat||5||

6darśaṃ ca paurṇamāsaṃ ca kurvan vigatamatsaraḥ
|
devatātithiśeṣeṇa kurute dehayāpanam||6||

7tasyendraḥ sahito devaiḥ sākṣāttribhuvaneśvaraḥ
|
pratyagṛhṇānmahārāja bhāgaṃ parvaṇi parvaṇi||7||

8sa parvakālaṃ kṛtvā tu munivṛttyā samanvitaḥ
|
atithibhyo dadāvannaṃ prahṛṣṭenāntarātmanā||8||

9vrīhidroṇasya taddhyasya dadato'nnaṃ mahātmanaḥ
|
śiṣṭaṃ mātsaryahīnasya vardhatyatithidarśanāt||9||

10tacchatānyapi bhuñjanti brāhmaṇānāṃ manīṣiṇām
|
munestyāgaviśuddhyā tu tadannaṃ vṛddhimarcchati||10||

11taṃ tu śuśrāva dharmiṣṭhaṃ mudgalaṃ saṃśitavratam
|
durvāsā nṛpa digvāsāstamathābhyājagāma ha||11||

12bibhraccāniyataṃ veṣamunmatta iva pāṇḍava
|
vikacaḥ paruṣā vāco vyāharan vividhā muniḥ||12||

13abhigamyātha taṃ vipramuvāca munisattamaḥ
|
annārthinamanuprāptaṃ viddhi māṃ dvijasattama||13||

14svāgataṃ te'stviti muniṃ mudgalaḥ pratyabhāṣata
|
pādyamācamanīyaṃ ca pratipādyārghyamuttamam||14||

15prādāt sa tāpasāyānnaṃ kṣudhitāyātithivratī
|
unmattāya parāṃ śraddhāmāsthāya sa dhṛtavrataḥ||15||

16tatastadannaṃ rasavat sa eva kṣudhayā'nvitaḥ
|
bubhuje kṛtsnamunmattaḥ prādāt tasmai ca mudgalaḥ||16||

17bhuktvā cānnaṃ tataḥ sarvamucchiṣṭenātmanastataḥ
|
athāṅgaṃ lilipe'nnena yathāgatamagācca saḥ||17||

18evaṃ dvitīye samprāpte yathākāle manīṣiṇaḥ
|
āgamya bubhuje sarvamannamuñchopajīvinaḥ||18||

19nirāhārastu sa muniruñchamārjayate punaḥ
|
na cainaṃ vikriyāṃ netumaśakanmudgalaṃ kṣudhā||19||

20na krodho na ca mātsaryaṃ nāvamāno na sambhramaḥ
|
saputradāramuñchantamāviveśa dvijottamam||20||

21tathā tamuñchadharmāṇaṃ durvāsā munisattamam
|
upatasthe yathākālaṃ ṣaṭkṛtvaḥ kṛtaniścayaḥ||21||

22na cāsya manasā kaścidvikāraṃ dadṛśe muniḥ
|
śuddhasattvasya śuddhaṃ sa dadṛśe nirmalaṃ manaḥ||22||

23tamuvāca tataḥ prītaḥ sa munirmudgalaṃ tataḥ
|
tvatsamo nāsti loke'smindātā mātsaryavarjitaḥ||23||

24kṣuddharmasaṃjñāṃ praṇudatyādatte dhairyameva ca
|
rasānusāriṇī jihvā karṣatyeva rasān prati||24||

25āhāraprabhavāḥ prāṇā mano durnigrahaṃ calam
|
manasaścendriyāṇāṃ cāpyaikāgryaṃ niścitaṃ tapaḥ||25||

26śrameṇopārjitaṃ tyaktuṃ duḥkhaṃ śuddhena cetasā
|
tat sarvaṃ bhavatā sādho yathāvadupapāditam||26||

27prītāḥ smo'nugṛhītāśca sametya bhavatā saha
|
indriyābhijayo dhairyaṃ saṃvibhāgo damaḥ śamaḥ||27||

28dayā satyaṃ ca dharmaśca tvayi sarvaṃ pratiṣṭhitam
|
jitāste karmabhirlokāḥ prāpto'si paramāṃ gatim||28||

29aho dānaṃ vighuṣṭaṃ te sumahat svargavāsibhiḥ
|
saśarīro bhavān gantā svargaṃ sucaritavrata||29||

30ityevaṃ vadatastasya tadā durvāsaso muneḥ
|
devadūto vimānena mudgalaṃ pratyupasthitaḥ||30||

31haṃsasārasayuktena kiṅkiṇījālamālinā
|
kāmagena vicitreṇa divyagandhavatā tathā||31||

32uvāca cainaṃ viprarṣiṃ vimānaṃ karmabhirjitam
|
samupāroha saṃsiddhiṃ prāpto'si paramāṃ mune||32||

33tamevaṃvādinamṛṣirdevadūtamuvāca ha
|
icchāmi bhavatā proktān guṇān svarganivāsinām||33||

34ke guṇāstatra vasatāṃ kiṃ tapaḥ kaśca niścayaḥ
|
svarge tatra sukhaṃ kiñca doṣo vā devadūtaka||34||

35satāṃ sāptapadaṃ mitramāhuḥ santaḥ kulocitāḥ
|
mitratāṃ ca puraskṛtya pṛcchāmi tvāmahaṃ vibho||35||

36yadatra tathyaṃ pathyaṃ ca tadbravīhyavicārayan
|
śrutvā tathā kariṣyāmi vyavasāyaṃ girā tava||36||
