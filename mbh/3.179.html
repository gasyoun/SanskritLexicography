Mahābhārata 3.179 (Āraṇyakaparvan)

1vaiśampāyana uvāca|
sa bhīmasenastejasvī tathā sarpavaśaṃ gataḥ
|
cintayāmāsa sarpasya vīryamatyadbhutaṃ mahat||1||

2uvāca ca mahāsarpaṃ kāmayā brūhi pannaga
|
kastvaṃ bho bhujagaśreṣṭha kiṃ mayā ca kariṣyati||2||

3pāṇḍavo bhīmaseno'haṃ dharmarājādanantaraḥ
|
nāgāyutasamaprāṇastvayā nītaḥ kathaṃ vaśam||3||

4siṃhāḥ kesariṇo vyāghrā mahiṣā vāraṇāstathā
|
samāgatāśca śataśo nihatāśca mayā yudhi||4||

5rākṣasāśca piśācāśca pannagāśca mahābalāḥ
|
bhujavegamaśaktā me soḍhuṃ pannagasattama||5||

6kiṃ nu vidyābalaṃ kiṃ nu varadānamatho tava
|
udyogamapi kurvāṇo vaśago'smi kṛtastvayā||6||

7asatyo vikramo nṝṇāmiti me dhīyate matiḥ
|
yathedaṃ me tvayā nāga balaṃ pratihataṃ mahat||7||

8vaiśampāyana uvāca|
ityevaṃvādinaṃ vīraṃ bhīmamakliṣṭakāriṇam
|
bhogena mahatā gṛhya samantāt paryaveṣṭayat||8||

9nigṛhyainaṃ mahābāhuṃ tataḥ sa bhujagastadā
|
vimucyāsya bhujau pīnāvidaṃ vacanamabravīt||9||

10viṣṭastvaṃ kṣudhitasyādya devairbhakṣo mahābhuja
|
diṣṭyā kālasya mahataḥ priyāḥ prāṇā hi dehinām||10||

11yathā tvidaṃ mayā prāptaṃ sarparūpamarindama
|
tathā'vaśyaṃ mayā khyāpyaṃ tavādya śṛṇu sattama||11||

12imāmavasthāṃ samprāpto hyahaṃ kopānmaharṣiṇām
|
śāpasyāntaṃ pariprepsuḥ sarvaṃ tat kathayāmi te||12||

13nahuṣo nāma rājarṣirvyaktaṃ te śrotramāgataḥ
|
tavaiva pūrvaḥ pūrveṣāmāyorvaṃśadharaḥ sutaḥ||13||

14so'haṃ śāpādagastyasya brāhmaṇānavamanya ca
|
imāmavasthāmāpannaḥ paśya daivamidaṃ mama||14||

15tvāṃ cedavadhyaṃ dāyādamatīva priyadarśanam
|
ahamadyopayokṣyāmi vidhānaṃ paśya yādṛśam||15||

16na hi me mucyate kaścit kathañcit pragrahaṃ gataḥ
|
gajo vā mahiṣo vā'pi ṣaṣṭhe kāle narottama||16||

17nāsi kevalasarpeṇa tiryagyoniṣu vartatā
|
gṛhītaḥ kauravaśreṣṭha varadānamidaṃ mama||17||

18patatā hi vimānāgrānmayā śakrāsanādrutam
|
kuru śāpāntamityukto bhagavān munisattamaḥ||18||

19sa māmuvāca tejasvī kṛpayā'bhipariplutaḥ
|
mokṣaste bhavitā rājan kasmāccit kālaparyayāt||19||

20tato'smi patito bhūmau na ca māmajahāt smṛtiḥ
|
smārtamasti purāṇaṃ me yathaivādhigataṃ tathā||20||

21yastu te vyāhṛtān praśnān pratibrūyādvibhāgavit
|
sa tvāṃ mokṣayitā śāpāditi māmabravīdṛṣiḥ||21||

22gṛhītasya tvayā rājan prāṇino'pi balīyasaḥ
|
satvabhraṃśo'dhikasyāpi sarvasyāśu bhaviṣyati||22||

23iti cāpyahamaśrauṣaṃ vacasteṣāṃ dayāvatām
|
mayi sañjātahārdānāmatha te'ntarhitā dvijāḥ||23||

24so'haṃ paramaduṣkarmā vasāmi niraye'śucau
|
sarpayonimimāṃ prāpya kālākāṅkṣī mahādyute||24||

25tamuvāca mahābāhurbhīmaseno bhujaṅgamam
|
na ca kupye mahāsarpa na cātmānaṃ vigarhaye||25||

26yasmādabhāvī bhāvī vā manuṣyaḥ sukhaduḥkhayoḥ
|
āgame yadi vā'pāye na tatra glapayenmanaḥ||26||

27daivaṃ puruṣakāreṇa ko vañcayitumarhati
|
daivameva paraṃ manye puruṣārtho nirarthakaḥ||27||

28paśya daivopaghātāddhi bhujavīryavyapāśrayam
|
imāmavasthāṃ samprāptamanimittamihādya mām||28||

29kiṃ tu nādyānuśocāmi tathātmānaṃ vināśitam
|
yathā tu vipine nyastān bhrātṝn rājyaparicyutān||29||

30himavāṃśca sudurgo'yaṃ yakṣarākṣasasaṅ‌kulaḥ
|
māṃ samudvīkṣamāṇāste prapatiṣyanti vihvalāḥ||30||

31vinaṣṭamatha māṃ śrutvā bhaviṣyanti nirudyamāḥ
|
dharmaśīlā mayā te hi bādhyante rājyagṛddhinā||31||

32athavā nārjuno dhīmān viṣādamupayāsyati
|
sarvāstravidanādhṛṣyo devagandharvarākṣasaiḥ||32||

33samarthaḥ sa mahābāhureko'pi sumahābalaḥ
|
devarājamapi sthānāt pracyāvayitumañjasā||33||

34kiṃ punardhṛtarāṣṭrasya putraṃ durdyūtadevinam
|
vidviṣṭaṃ sarvalokasya dambhamohaparāyaṇam||34||

35mātaraṃ caiva śocāmi kṛpaṇāṃ putragṛddhinīm
|
yā'smākaṃ nityamāśāste mahattvamadhikaṃ paraiḥ||35||

36tasyāḥ kathaṃ tvanāthāyā madvināśādbhujaṅgama
|
aphalāste bhaviṣyanti mayi sarve manorathāḥ||36||

37nakulaḥ sahadevaśca yamau ca guruvartinau
|
madbāhubalasaṅguptau nityaṃ puruṣamāninau||37||

38bhaviṣyato nirutsāhau bhraṣṭavīryaparākramau
|
madvināśāt paridyūnāviti me vartate matiḥ||38||

39evaṃvidhaṃ bahu tadā vilalāpa vṛkodraḥ
|
bhujaṅgabhogasaṃruddho nāśakacca viceṣṭitum||39||

40yudhiṣṭhirastu kaunteyo babhūvāsvasthacetanaḥ
|
aniṣṭadarśanān ghorānutpātān paricintayan||40||

41dāruṇaṃ hyaśivaṃ nādaṃ śivā dakṣiṇataḥ sthitā
|
dīptāyāṃ diśi vitrastā rauti tasyāśramasya ha||41||

42ekapakṣākṣicaraṇā vartikā ghoradarśanā
|
raktaṃ vamantī dadṛśe pratyādityamabhāsurā||42||

43pravavau cānilo rūkṣaścaṇḍaḥ śarkarakarṣaṇaḥ
|
apasavyāni sarvāṇi mṛgapakṣirutāni ca||43||

44pṛṣṭhato vāyasaḥ kṛṣṇo yāhi yāsīti śaṃsati
|
muhurmuhuḥ sphurati ca dakṣiṇo'sya bhujastathā||44||

45hṛdayaṃ caraṇaścāpi vāmo'sya paritapyati
|
savyasyākṣṇo vikāraścāpyaniṣṭaḥ samapadyata||45||

46dharmarājo'pi medhāvī manyamāno mahadbhayam
|
draupadīṃ paripapraccha kva bhīma iti bhārata||46||

47śaśaṃsa tasmai pāñcālī cirayātaṃ vṛkodaram
|
sa pratasthe mahābāhurdhaumyena sahito nṛpaḥ||47||

48draupadyā rakṣaṇaṃ kāryamityuvāca dhanañjayam
|
nakulaṃ sahadevaṃ ca vyādideśa dvijān prati||48||

49sa tasya padamunnīya tasmādevāśramāt prabhuḥ
|
mṛgayāmāsa kaunteyo bhīmasenaṃ mahāvane||49||

50sa prācīṃ diśamāsthāya mahato gajayūthapān
|
dadarśa pṛthivīṃ cihnairbhīmasya paricihnitām||50||

51tato mṛgasahasrāṇi mṛgendrāṇāṃ śatāni ca
|
patitāni vane dṛṣṭvā mārgaṃ tasyāviśannṛpaḥ||51||

52dhāvatastasya vīrasya mṛgārthaṃ vātaraṃhasaḥ
|
ūruvātavinirbhagnā drumā vyāvarjitāḥ pathi||52||

53sa gatvā taistadā cihnairdadarśa girigahvare
|
rūkṣamārutabhūyiṣṭhe niṣpatradrumasaṅkule||53||

54īriṇe nirjale deśe kaṇṭakidrumasaṃṅkule
|
aśmasthāṇukṣupākīrṇe sudurge viṣamotkaṭe
|
gṛhītaṃ bhujagendreṇa niśceṣṭamanujaṃ tadā||54||
