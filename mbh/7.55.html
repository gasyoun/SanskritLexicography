Mahābhārata 7.55 (Droṇaparvan)

1sañjaya uvāca|
śrutvā mṛtyusamutpattiṃ karmāṇyanupamāni ca
|
dharmarājaḥ punarvākyaṃ prasādyainamathābravīt ||1||

2yudhiṣṭhira uvāca| guravaḥ puṇyakarmāṇaḥ śakrapratimavikramāḥ
|
sthāne rājarṣayo brahmannanaghāḥ satyavādinaḥ ||2||

3bhūya eva tu māṃ tathyairvacobhirabhibṛṃhaya
|
rājarṣīṇāṃ purāṇānāṃ samāśvāsaya karmabhiḥ ||3||

4kiyantyo dakṣiṇā dattāḥ kaiśca dattā mahātmabhiḥ
|
rājarṣibhiḥ puṇyakṛdbhistadbhavān prabravītu me ||4||

5vyāsa uvāca| śaibyasya nṛpateḥ putraḥ sṛñjayo nāma nāmataḥ
|
sakhāyau tasya caivobhau ṛṣī parvatanāradau ||5||

6tau kadācid‌gṛhaṃ tasya praviṣṭau taddidṛkṣayā
|
vidhivaccārcitau tena prītau tatroṣatuḥ sukham ||6||

7taṃ kadācitsukhāsīnaṃ tābhyāṃ saha śucismitā
|
duhitābhyāgamat kanyā sṛñjayaṃ varavarṇinī ||7||

8tayā'bhivāditaḥ kanyāmabhyanandadyathāvidhi
|
tatsaliṅgābhirāśīrbhiriṣṭābhirabhitaḥ sthitām ||8||

9tāṃ nirīkṣyābravīdvākyaṃ parvataḥ prahasanniva
|
kasyeyaṃ cañcalāpāṅgī sarvalakṣaṇasaṃmatā ||9||

10utāho bhāḥ svidarkasya jvalanasya śikhā tviyam
|
śrīrhrīḥ kīrtirdhṛtiḥ puṣṭiḥ siddhiścandramasaḥ prabhā ||10||

11evaṃ bruvāṇaṃ devarṣiṃ nṛpatiḥ sṛñjayo'bravīt
|
mameyaṃ bhagavan kanyā matto varamabhīpsati ||11||

12nāradastvabravīdenaṃ dehi mahyamimāṃ nṛpa
|
bhāryārthaṃ sumahacchreyaḥ prāptuṃ cedicchase nṛpaḥ ||12||

13dadānītyeva saṃhṛṣṭaḥ sṛñjayaḥ prāha nāradam
|
parvatastu susaṅkruddho nāradaṃ vākyamabravīt ||13||

14hṛdayena mayā pūrvaṃ vṛtāṃ vai vṛtavānasi
|
yasmāddhṛtā tvayā vipra mā gāḥ svargaṃ yathepsayā ||14||

15evamukto nāradastaṃ pratyuvācottaraṃ vacaḥ
|
manovāgbuddhisambhāṣā dattā codakapūrvakam ||15||

16pāṇigrahaṇamantrāśca prathitaṃ varalakṣaṇam
|
na tveṣā niścitā niṣṭhā niṣṭhā saptapadī smṛtā ||16||

17anutpanne ca kāryārthe māṃ tvaṃ vyāhṛtavānasi
|
tasmāttvamapi na svargaṃ gamiṣyasi mayā vinā ||17||

18anyonyamevaṃ śaptvā vai tasthatustatra tau tadā
|
atha so'pi nṛpo viprān pānācchādanabhojanaiḥ ||18||

19putrakāmaḥ paraṃ śaktyā yatnāccopācaracchuciḥ
|
tasya prasannā viprendrāḥ kadācit putramīpsavaḥ ||19||

20tapaḥsvādhyāyaniratā vedavedāṅgapāragāḥ
|
sahitā nāradaṃ prāhurdehyasmai putramīpsitam ||20||

21tathetyuktvā dvijairuktaḥ sṛñjayaṃ nārado'bravīt
|
tubhyaṃ prasannā rājarṣe putramīpsanti brāhmaṇāḥ ||21||

22varaṃ vṛṇīṣva bhadraṃ te yādṛśaṃ putramīpsitam
|
tathoktaḥ prāñjalī rājā putraṃ vavre guṇānvitam ||22||

23yaśasvinaṃ kīrtimantaṃ tejasvinamarindamam
|
yasya mūtraṃ purīṣaṃ ca kledaḥ svedaśca kāñcanam ||23||

24suvarṇaṣṭhīvirityevaṃ tasya nāmābhavat kṛtam
|
tasmin varapradānena vardhayatyamitaṃ dhanam ||24||

25kārayāmāsa nṛpatiḥ sauvarṇaṃ sarvamīpsitam
|
gṛhaprākāradurgāṇi brāhmaṇāvasāthānyapi ||25||

26śayyāsanāni yānāni sthālī piṭharabhājanam
|
tasya rājño'pi yadveśma bāhyāścopaskarāśca ye ||26||

27sarvaṃ tat kāñcanamayaṃ kālena parivardhitam
|
atha dasyugaṇāḥ śrutvā dṛṣṭvā cainaṃ tathāvidham ||27||

28sambhūya tasya nṛpateḥ samārabdhāścikīrṣitum
|
kecittatrābruvan rājñaḥ putraṃ gṛhṇīma vai svayam ||28||

29so'syākaraḥ kāñcanasya tasya yatnaṃ carāmahe
|
tataste dasyavo lubdhāḥ praviśya nṛpaterguham ||29||

30rājaputraṃ tathā jahruḥ suvarṇaṣṭhīvinaṃ balāt
|
gṛhyainamanupāyajñā nītvā'raṇyamacetasaḥ ||30||

31hatvā viśasya cāpaśyan lubdhā vasu na kiñcana
|
tasya prāṇairvimuktasya naṣṭaṃ tadvaradaṃ vasu ||31||

32dasyavaśca tadā'nyonyaṃ jaghnurmūrkhā vicetasaḥ
|
hatvā parasparaṃ naṣṭāḥ kumāraṃ cādbhutaṃ bhuvi ||32||

33asambhāvyaṃ gatā ghoraṃ narakaṃ duṣṭakāriṇaḥ
|
taṃ dṛṣṭvā nihataṃ putraṃ varadattaṃ mahātapāḥ ||33||

34vilalāpa suduḥkhārto bahudhā karuṇaṃ nṛpaḥ
|
vilapantaṃ niśamyātha putraśokahataṃ nṛpam ||34||

35pratyadṛśyata devarṣirnāradastasya sannidhau
|
uvāca cainaṃ duḥkhārtaṃ vilapantamacetasam ||35||

36sṛñjayaṃ nārado'bhyetya tannibodha yudhiṣṭhira
|
kāmānāmavitṛptastvaṃ sṛñjayeha mariṣyasi ||36||

37yasya caite vayaṃ gehe uṣitā brahmavādinaḥ
|
āvikṣitaṃ maruttaṃ ca mṛtaṃ sṛñjaya śuśruma ||37||

38saṃvarto yājayāmāsa spardhayā vai bṛhaspateḥ
|
yasmai rājarṣaye prādāddhanaṃ sa bhagavān prabhuḥ ||38||

39haimaṃ himavataḥ pādaṃ yiyakṣorvividhaiḥ savaiḥ
|
yasya sendrā'maragaṇā bṛhaspatipurogamāḥ ||39||

40devā viśvasṛjaḥ sarve yajanānte samāsate
|
yajñavāṭasya sauvarṇāḥ sarve cāsan paricchadāḥ ||40||

41yasya sarvaṃ tadā hyannaṃ manobhiprāyagaṃ śuci
|
kāmato bubhujurviprāḥ sarve cānnārthino dvijāḥ ||41||

42payo dadhi ghṛtaṃ kṣaudraṃ bhakṣyaṃ bhojyaṃ ca śobhanam
|
yasya yajñeṣu sarveṣu vāsāṃsyābharaṇāni ca ||42||

43īpsitānyupatiṣṭhante prahṛṣṭān vedapāragān
|
marutaḥ pariveṣṭāro maruttasyābhavan gṛhe ||43||

44āvikṣitasya rājarṣerviśve devāḥ sabhāsadaḥ
|
yasya vīryavato rājñaḥ suvṛṣṭyā sasyasampadaḥ ||44||

45havirbhistarpitā yena samyak klṛptairdivaukasaḥ
|
ṛṣīṇāṃ ca pitṝṇāṃ ca devānāṃ sukhajīvinām ||45||

46brahmacaryaśrutimukheḥ sarvairdānaiśca sarvadā
|
śayanāsanapānāni svarṇarāśīśca dustyajāḥ ||46||

47tat sarvamamitaṃ vittaṃ dattaṃ viprebhya icchayā
|
so'nudhyātastu śakreṇa prajāḥkṛtvā nirāmayāḥ ||47||

48śraddadhāno jitān lokān gataḥ puṇyaduho'kṣayān
|
saprajaḥ sanṛpāmātyaḥ sadārāpatyabāndhavaḥ ||48||

49yauvanena sahastrābdaṃ marutto rājyamanvaśāt
|
sa cenmamāra sṛñjaya caturbhadratarastvayā ||49||

50putrāt puṇyatarastubhyaṃ mā putramanutapyathāḥ
|
ayajvānamadākṣiṇyamabhiśvai tyeti vyāharan ||50||
