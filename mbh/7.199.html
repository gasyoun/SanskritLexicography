Mahābhārata 7.199 (Droṇaparvan)

1sañjaya uvāca|
tataḥ sa kadanaṃ cakre ripūṇāṃ droṇanandanaḥ
|
yugānte sarvabhūtānāṃ kālasṛṣṭa ivāntakaḥ||1||

2dhvajadrumaṃ śastraśṛṅgaṃ hatanāgamahāśilam
|
aśvakiṃpuruṣākīrṇaṃ śarāsanalatāvṛtam||2||

3kravyādapakṣisaṃghuṣṭaṃ bhūtayakṣagaṇākulam
|
nihatya śātravān bhallaiḥ so'cinoddehaparvatam ||3||

4tato vegena mahatā vinadya sa nararṣabhaḥ
|
pratijñāṃ śrāvayāmāsa punareva tavātmajam||4||

5yasmādyudhyantamācāryaṃ dharmakañcukamāsthitaḥ
|
muñca śastramiti prāha kuntīputro yudhiṣṭhiraḥ||5||

6tasmātsaṃpaśyatastasya drāvayiṣyāmi vāhinīm
|
vidrāvya sarvān hantā'smi jālmaṃ pāñcālyameva tu||6||

7sarvānetān haniṣyāmi yadi yotsyanti māṃ raṇe
|
satyaṃ te pratijānāmi parivartaya vāhinīm||7||

8tacchrutvā tava putrastu vāhinīṃ paryavartayat
|
siṃhanādena mahatā vyapohya sumahadbhayam||8||

9tataḥ samāgamo rājankurupāṇḍavasenayoḥ
|
punarevābhavattīvraḥ pūrṇasāgarayoriva||9||

10saṃrabdhā hi sthirībhūtā droṇaputreṇa kauravāḥ
|
udagrā pāṇḍupañcālā droṇasya nidhanena ca||10||

11teṣāṃ paramahṛṣṭānāṃ jayamātmani paśyatām
|
saṃrabdhānāṃ mahāvegaḥ prādurāsīdviśāṃpate||11||

12yathā śiloccaye śailaḥ sāgaraiḥ sāgaro yathā
|
pratihanyeta rājendra tathā''sankurupāṇḍavāḥ||12||

13tataḥ śaṅkhasahasrāṇi bherīṇāmayutāni ca
|
avādayanta saṃhṛṣṭāḥ kurupāṇḍavasainikāḥ||13||

14yathā nirmathyamānasya sāgarasya tu niḥsvanaḥ
|
abhavattava sainyasya sumahānadbhutopamaḥ||14||

15prāduścakre tato drauṇirastraṃ nārāyaṇaṃ tadā
|
abhisandhāya pāṃḍūnāṃ pañcālānāṃ ca vāhinīm||15||

16prādurāsaṃstato bāṇā dīptāgrāḥ khe sahasraśaḥ
|
pāṇḍavānkṣapayiṣyanto dīptāsyāḥ pannagā iva||16||

17te diśaḥ khaṃ ca sainyaṃ ca samāvṛṇvanmahāhave
|
muhūrtādbhāskarasyeva loke rājan gabhastayaḥ||17||

18tathā'pare dyotamānā jyotīṃṣīvāmalāmbare
|
prādurāsanmahārāja kārṣṇāyasamayā guḍāḥ||18||

19catuścakrā dvicakrāśca śataghnyo bahulā gadāḥ
|
cakrāṇi ca kṣurāntāni maṃḍalānīva bhāsvataḥ||19||

20śastrākṛtibhirākīrṇamatīva puruṣarṣabha
|
dṛṣṭvā'ntarikṣamāvignāḥ pāṇḍupañcālasṛñjayāḥ||20||

21yathāyathā hyayudhyanta pāṇḍavānāṃ mahārathāḥ
|
tathātathā tadastraṃ vai vyavardhata janādhipa||21||

22vadhyamānāstadā'streṇa tena nārāyaṇena vai
|
dahyamānānaleneva sarvato'bhyarditā raṇe||22||

23yathā hi śiśirāpāye dahetkakṣaṃ hutāśanaḥ
|
tathā tadastraṃ pāṇḍūnāṃ dadāha dhvajinīṃ prabho||23||

24āpūryamāṇenāstreṇa sainye kṣīyati ca prabho
|
jagāma paramaṃ trāsaṃ dharmaputro yudhiṣṭhiraḥ||24||

25dravamāṇaṃ tu tat sainyaṃ dṛṣṭvā vigatacetanam
|
madhyasthatāṃ ca pārthasya dharmaputro'bravīdidam||25||

26dhṛṣṭadyumna palāyasva saha pāñcālasenayā
|
sātyake tvaṃ ca gacchasva vṛṣṇyandhakavṛto mahān||26||

27vāsudevo'pi dharmātmā kariṣyatyātmanaḥ kṣamam
|
śreyo hyupadiśatyeṣa lokasya kimutātmanaḥ||27||

28saṃgrāmastu na kartavyaḥ sarvasainyān bravīmi vaḥ
|
ahaṃ hi saha sodaryaiḥ pravekṣye havyavāhanam||28||

29bhīṣmadroṇārṇavaṃ tīrtvā saṃgrāme bhīrudustare
|
vimajjiṣyāmi salile sagaṇo drauṇigoṣpade||29||

30kāmaḥ saṃpadyatāmasya bībhatsorāśu māṃ prati
|
kalyāṇavṛttirācāryo mayā yudhi nipātitaḥ||30||

31yena bālaḥ sa saubhadro yuddhānāmaviśāradaḥ
|
samarthairbahubhiḥ krūrairghātito nābhipālitaḥ||31||

32yenāvibruvatā praśnaṃ tathā kṛṣṇā sabhāṃ gatā
|
upekṣitā saputreṇa dāsabhāvaṃ niyacchatī||32||

33jighāṃsurdhārtarāṣṭraśca śrānteṣvaśveṣu phālgunaḥ
|
kavacena tathā gupto rakṣārthaṃ saindhavasya ca||33||

34yena brahmāstraviduṣā pañcālāḥ satyajinmukhāḥ
|
kurvāṇā majjaye yatnaṃ samūlā vinipātitāḥ||34||

35yena pravrājyamānāśca rājyādvayamadharmataḥ
|
nivāryamāṇenāsmābhiranugantuṃ tadeṣitāḥ||35||

36yo'sāvatyantamasmāsu kurvāṇaḥ sauhṛdaṃ param
|
hatastadarthe maraṇaṃ gamiṣyāmi sabāndhavaḥ||36||

37evaṃ bruvati kaunteye dāśārhastvaritastataḥ
|
nivārya sainyaṃ bāhubhyāmidaṃ vacanamabravīt||37||

38śīghraṃ nyasyata śastrāṇi vāhebhyaścāvarohata
|
eṣa yogo'tra vihitaḥ pratiṣedhe mahātmanā||38||

39dvipāśvasyandanebhyaśca kṣitiṃ sarve'varohata
|
evametanna vo hanyādastraṃ bhūmau nirāyudhān||39||

40yathāyathā hi yudhyante yodhā hyastramidaṃ prati
|
tathātathā bhavantyete kauravā balavattarāḥ||40||

41nikṣepsyanti ca śastrāṇi vāhanebhyo'varuhya ye
|
tānnaitadastraṃ saṃgrāme nihaniṣyati mānavān||41||

42yattvetatpratiyotsyanti manasāpīha kecana
|
nihaniṣyati tānsarvān rasātalagatānapi||42||

43te vacastasya tacchrutvā vāsudevasya bhārata
|
īṣuḥ sarve samutsraṣṭuṃ manobhiḥ karaṇena ca||43||

44tata utsraṣṭukāmāṃstānastrāṇyālakṣya pāṃḍavaḥ
|
bhīmaseno'bravīdrājannidaṃ saṃharṣayanvacaḥ||44||

45na kathaṃcana śastrāṇi moktavyānīha kenacit
|
ahamāvārayiṣyāmi droṇaputrāstramāśugaiḥ||45||

46gadayā'pyanayā gurvyā hemavigrahayā raṇe
|
kālavatprahariṣyāmi drauṇerastraṃ viśātayan||46||

47na hi me vikrame tulyaḥ kaścidasti pumāniha
|
yathaiva savitustulyaṃ jyotiranyanna vidyate||47||

48paśyatemau hi me bāhū nāgarājakaropamau
|
samarthau parvatasyāpi śaiśirasya nipātane||48||

49nāgāyutasamaprāṇo hyahameko nareṣviha
|
śakro yathā'pratidvandvo divi deveṣu viśrutaḥ||49||

50adya paśyata me vīryaṃ bāhvoḥ pīnāṃsayoryudhi
|
jvalamānasya dīptasya drauṇerastrasya vāraṇe||50||

51yadi nārāyaṇāstrasya pratiyoddhā na vidyate
|
adyaitatpratiyotsyāmi paśyatsu kurupāṇḍuṣu||51||

52arjunārjuna bībhatso na nyasyaṃ gāṇḍivaṃ tvayā
|
śaśāṅkasyeva te paṅko nairmalyaṃ pātayiṣyati||52||

53arjuna uvāca|
bhīma nārāyaṇāstre me goṣu ca brāhmaṇeṣu ca
|
eteṣu gāṇḍivaṃ nyasyametaddhi vratamuttamam||53||

54evamuktastato bhīmo droṇaputramarindamam
|
abhyayānmeghaghoṣeṇa rathenādityavarcasā||54||

55sa enamiṣujālena laghutvācchīghravikramaḥ
|
nimeṣamātreṇāsādya kuntīputro'bhyavākirat||55||

56tato drauṇiḥ prahasyainaṃ dravantamabhibhāṣya ca
|
avākiratpradīptāgraiḥ śaraistairabhimantritaiḥ||56||

57pannagairiva dīptāsyairvamadbhirjvalanaṃ raṇe
|
avakīrṇo'bhavatpārthaḥ sphuliṅgairiva kāñcanaiḥ||57||

58tasya rūpamabhūdrājanbhīmasenasya saṃyuge
|
khadyotairāvṛtasyeva parvatasya dinakṣaye||58||

59tadastraṃ droṇaputrasya tasminpratisamasyati
|
avardhata mahārāja yathāgniraniloddhataḥ||59||

60vivardhamānamālakṣya tadastraṃ bhīmavikramam
|
pāṇḍusainyamṛte bhīmaṃ sumahadbhayamāviśat||60||

61tataḥ śastrāṇi te sarve samutsṛjya mahītale
|
avārohan rathebhyaśca hastyaśvebhyaśca sarvaśaḥ||61||

62teṣu nikṣiptaśastreṣu vāhanebhyaścyuteṣu ca
|
tadastravīryaṃ vipulaṃ bhīmamūrdhanyathāpatat||62||

63hāhākṛtāni bhūtāni pāṇḍavāśca viśeṣataḥ
|
bhīmasenamapaśyanta tejasā saṃvṛtaṃ tathā||63||
