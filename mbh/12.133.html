Mahābhārata 12.133 (Śāntiparvan)

1bhīṣma uvāca|
svarāṣṭrātpararāṣṭrācca kośaṃ saṃjanayennṛpaḥ
|
kośāddhi dharmaḥ kaunteya rājyamūlaṃ ca vardhate ||1||

2tasmātsaṃjanayetkośaṃ satkṛtya paripālayet
|
paripālyānutanuyādeṣa dharmaḥ sanātanaḥ ||2||

3na kośaḥ śuddhaśaucena na nṛśaṃsena jātucit
|
madhyamaṃ padamāsthāya kośasaṃgrahaṇaṃ caret ||3||

4abalasya kutaḥ kośo hyakośasya kuto balam
|
abalasya kuto rājyamarājñaḥ śrīrbhavetkutaḥ ||4||

5uccairvṛtteḥ śriyo hāniryathaiva maraṇaṃ tathā
|
tasmātkośaṃ balaṃ mitramatha rājā vivardhayet ||5||

6hīnakośaṃ hi rājānamavajānanti mānavāḥ
|
na cāsyālpena tuṣyanti kāryamapyutsahanti ca ||6||

7śriyo hi kāraṇādrājā sakriyāṃ labhate parām
|
sāsya gṛhati pāpāni vāso guhyamiva striyāḥ ||7||

8ṛddhimasyānu tapyante purā viprakṛtā narāḥ
|
śālāvṛkā ivājastraṃ jighāṃsumeva vindati ||8||

9īdṛśasya kuto rājñaḥ sukhaṃ bhavati bhārata
|
udyacchedeva na namedudyamo hyeva pauruṣam ||9||

10apyaparvaṇi bhajyeta na nameteha kasyacit
|
apyaraṇyaṃ samāśritya carenmṛgagaṇaiḥ saha ||10||

11na tvevojjhitamaryādairdasyubhiḥ sahitaścaret
|
dasyūnāṃ sulabhā senā raudrakarmasu bhārata ||11||

12ekāntato hyamaryādātsarvo'pyudvijate janaḥ
|
dasyavo'pyabhiśaṅkante niranukrośakāriṇaḥ ||12||

13sthāpayedeva maryādāṃ janacittaprasādinīm
|
alpe'pyarthe ca maryādā loke bhavati pūjitā ||13||

14nāyaṃ loko'sti na para iti vyavasito janaḥ
|
nālaṃ gantuṃ hi viśvāsaṃ nāstike bhayaśaṅkite ||14||

15yathā sadbhiḥ parādānamahiṃsā dasyubhiḥ kṛtā
|
anurajyanti bhūtāni samaryādeṣu dasyuṣu ||15||

16ayuddhyamānasya vadho dārāmarṣaḥ kṛtaghnatā
|
brahmavittasya cādānaṃ niḥśeṣakaraṇaṃ tathā ||16||

17striyā moṣaḥ patisthānaṃ dasyuṣvetadvigarhitam
|
saṃśleṣaṃ ca parastrībhirdasyuretāni varjayet ||17||

18abhisaṃdadhate ye ca viśvāsāyāsya mānavāḥ
|
aśeṣamevopalabhya kurvantīti viniścayaḥ ||18||

19tasmātsaśeṣaṃ kartavyaṃ svādhīnamapi dasyubhiḥ
|
na balastho'hamasmīti nṛśaṃsāni samācaret ||19||

20sa śeṣakāriṇastatra śeṣaṃ paśyanti sarvaśaḥ
|
niḥśeṣakāriṇo nityaṃ niḥśeṣakaraṇādbhayam ||20||
