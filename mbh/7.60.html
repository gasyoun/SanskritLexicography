Mahābhārata 7.60 (Droṇaparvan)

1nārada uvāca| bhagīrathaṃ ca rājānaṃ mṛtaṃ sṛñjaya śuśruma
|
yena bhāgīrathī gaṅgā cayanaiḥ kāñcanaiścitā ||1||

2yaḥ sahasraṃ sahasrāṇāṃ kanyā hemavibhūṣitāḥ
|
rājñaśca rājaputrāṃśca brāhmaṇebhyo hyamanyata ||2||

3sarvā rathagatāḥ kanyā rathāḥ sarve caturyujaḥ
|
rathe rathe śataṃ nāgāḥ sarve vai hemamālinaḥ ||3||

4sahasramaśvāścaikaikaṃ gajānāṃ pṛṣṭhato'nvayuḥ
|
aśve aśve śataṃ gāvo gavāṃ paścāda‌jāvikam ||4||

5tenākrāntā jalaughena dakṣiṇā bhūyasīrdadat
|
upahvare'tivyathitā tasyāṅke niṣasāda ha ||5||

6tathā bhāgīrathī gaṅgā urvaśī cābhavat purā
|
duhitṛtvaṃ gatā rājñaḥ putratvamagamat tadā ||6||

7tāṃ tu gāthāṃ jaguḥ prītā gandharvāḥ sūryavarcasaḥ
|
pitṛdevamanuṣyāṇāṃ śṛṇvatāṃ valguvādinaḥ ||7||

8bhagīrathaṃ yajamānamaikṣvākuṃ bhūridakṣiṇam
|
gaṅgā samudragā devī vavre pitaramīśvaram ||8||

9tasya sendraiḥ suragaṇairdevairyajñaḥ svalaṅkṛtaḥ
|
samyakparigṛhītaśca śāntavighno nirāmayaḥ ||9||

10yo ya iccheta vipro vai yatra yatrātmanaḥ priyam
|
bhagīrathastadā prītastatra tatrādadadvaśī ||10||

11nādeyaṃ brāhmaṇasyāsīdyasya yatsyāt priyaṃ dhanam
|
so'pi vipraprasādena brahmalokaṃ gato nṛpaḥ ||11||

12yena yātau makhamukhau diśāśāvihapādapāḥ
|
tenāvasthātumicchanti taṃ gatvā rājamīśvaram ||12||

13sa cenmamāra sṛñjaya caturbhadratarastvayā
|
putrāt puṇyatarastubhyaṃ mā putramanutapyathāḥ ||13||

14ayajvānamadākṣiṇyamabhiśvaityeti vyāharan ||14||
