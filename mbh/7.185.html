Mahābhārata 7.185 (Droṇaparvan)

1sañjaya uvāca|
tato duryodhano droṇamabhigamyābravīdidam
|
amarṣavaśamāpanno janayanharṣatejasī||1||

2duryodhana uvāca|
na marṣaṇīyāḥ saṃgrāme viśramantaḥ śramānvitāḥ
|
sapatnā glānamanaso labdhalakṣā viśeṣataḥ||2||

3yattu marṣitamasmābhirbhavataḥ priyakāmyayā
|
ta ete pariviśrāntāḥ pāṇḍavā balavattarāḥ||3||

4sarvathā parihīnāḥ sma tejasā ca balena ca
|
bhavatā pālyamānāste vivardhante punaḥ punaḥ||4||

5divyānyastrāṇi sarvāṇi brāhmādīni ca yāni ha
|
tāni sarvāṇi tiṣṭhanti bhavatyeva viśeṣataḥ||5||

6na pāṇḍaveyā na vayaṃ nānye loke dhanurdharāḥ
|
yudhyamānasya te tulyāḥ satyametadbravīmi te||6||

7sasurāsuragandharvānimā~llokān dvijottama
|
sarvāstravidbhavānhanyāddivyairastrairna saṃśayaḥ||7||

8sa bhavānmarṣayatyetāṃstvatto bhītānviśeṣataḥ
|
śiṣyatvaṃ vā puraskṛtya mama vā mandabhāgyatām||8||

9sañjaya uvāca|
evamuddharṣito droṇaḥ kopitaśca sutena te
|
samanyurabravīdrājanduryodhanamidaṃ vacaḥ||9||

10sthaviraḥ sanparaṃ śaktyā ghaṭe duryodhanāhave
|
ataḥ paraṃ mayā kāryaṃ kṣudraṃ vijayagṛddhinā||10||

11anastravidayaṃ sarvo hantavyo'stravidā janaḥ
|
yadbhavānmanyate cāpi śubhaṃ vā yadi vā'śubham||11||

12tadvai kartāsmi kauravya vacanāttava nānyathā
|
nihatya sarvapañcālān yuddhe kṛtvā parākramam||12||

13vimokṣye kavacaṃ rājansatyenāyudhamālabhe
|
manyase yacca kaunteyamarjunaṃ śāntamāhave||13||

14tasya vīryaṃ mahābāho śṛṇu satyena kaurava
|
taṃ na devā na gandharvā na yakṣā na ca rākṣasāḥ||14||

15utsahante raṇe jetuṃ kupitaṃ savyasācinam
|
khāṇḍaveyena bhagavānpratyudyātaḥ sureśvaraḥ||15||

16sāyakairvāritaścāpi varṣamāṇo mahātmanā
|
yakṣā nāgāstathā daityā ye cānye balagarvitāḥ||16||

17nihatāḥ puruṣendreṇa taccāpi viditaṃ tava
|
gandharvā ghoṣayātrāyāṃ citrasenādayo jitāḥ||17||

18yūyaṃ tairhrīyamāṇāśca mokṣitā dṛḍhadhanvanā
|
nivātakavacāścāpi devānāṃ śatravastathā||18||

19surairavadhyāḥ saṃgrāme tena vīreṇa nirjitāḥ
|
dānavānāṃ sahasrāṇi hiraṇyapuravāsinām||19||

20vijigye puruṣavyāghraḥ sa śakyo mānuṣaiḥ katham
|
pratyakṣaṃ caiva te sarvaṃ yathābalamidaṃ tava||20||

21kṣapitaṃ pāṇḍuputreṇa ceṣṭatāṃ no viśāṃpate
|
sañjaya uvāca|
taṃ tadā'bhipraśaṃsantamarjunaṃ kupitastadā||21||

22droṇaṃ tava suto rājanpunarevedamabravīt
|
ahaṃ duḥśāsanaḥ karṇaḥ śakunirmātulaśca me||22||

23haniṣyāmo'rjunaṃ saṃkhye dvidhā kṛtvā'dya bhāratīm
|
tasya tadvacanaṃ śrutvā bhāradvājo hasanniva||23||

24anvavartata rājānaṃ svasti te'sviti cābravīt
|
ko hi gāṃḍīvadhanvānaṃ jvalantamiva tejasā||24||

25akṣayaṃ kṣapayetkaściśitkṣatriyaḥ kṣatriyarṣabham
|
taṃ na vittapatirnendro na yamo na jaleśvaraḥ||25||

26nāsuroragarakṣāṃsi kṣapayeyuḥ sahāyudham
|
mūḍhāstvetāni bhāṣante yānīmānyāttha bhārata||26||

27yuddhe hyarjunamāsādya svastimānko vrajedgṛhān
|
tvaṃ tu sarvābhiśaṃkitvānniṣṭhuraḥ pāpaniścayaḥ||27||

28śreyasastvaddhite yuktāṃstattadvaktumihecchasi
|
gaccha tvamapi kaunteyamātmārthe jahi mā ciram||28||

29tvamapyāśaṃsaye yoddhuṃ kulajaḥ kṣatriyo hyasi
|
imānkiṃ kṣatriyānsarvānghātayiṣyasyanāgasaḥ||29||

30tvamasya mūlaṃ vairasya tasmādāsādayārjunam
|
eṣa te mātulaḥ prājñaḥ kṣatradharmamanuvrataḥ||30||

31durdyūtadevī gāndhāre prayātvarjunamāhave
|
eṣo'kṣakuśalo jihmo dyūtakṛtkitavaḥ śaṭhaḥ||31||

32devitā nikṛtiprajño yudhi jeṣyati pāṇḍavān
|
tvayā kathitamattyarthaṃ karṇena saha hṛṣṭavat||32||

33asakṛcchūnyavanmohāddhṛtarāṣṭrasya śṛṇvataḥ
|
ahaṃ ca tāta karṇaśca bhrātā duḥśāsanaśca me||33||

34pāṇḍuputrānhaniṣyāmaḥ sahitāḥ samare trayaḥ
|
iti te katthamānasya śrutaṃ saṃsadi saṃsadi||34||

35anutiṣṭha pratijñāṃ tāṃ satyavāk bhava taiḥ saha
|
eṣa te pāṇḍavaḥ śatruraviśaṅko'grataḥ sthitaḥ||35||

36kṣatradharmamavekṣasva ślāghyastava vadho jayāt
|
dattaṃ bhuktamadhītaṃ ca prāptamaiśvaryamīpsitam||36||

37kṛtakṛtyo'nṛṇaścāsi mā bhairyudhyasva pāṇḍavam
|
ityuktvā samare droṇo nyavartata yataḥ pare
|
dvaidhīkṛtya tataḥ senāṃ yuddhaṃ samabhavattadā||37||
