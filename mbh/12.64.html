Mahābhārata 12.64 (Śāntiparvan)

1bhīṣma uvāca|
cāturāśramyadharmāśca yatidharmāśca pāṇḍava
|
lokavedottarāścaiva kṣātradharme samāhitāḥ||1||

2sarvāṇyetāni karmāṇi kṣātre bharatasattama
|
nirāśiṣo jīvalokāḥ kṣatradharme vyavasthite||2||

3apratyakṣaṃ bahudvāraṃ dharmamāśramavāsinām
|
prakopayanti tadbhāvamāgamaireva śāśvatam||3||

4apare vacanaiḥ puṇyairvādino lokaniścayam
|
aniścayajñā dharmāṇāmadṛṣṭānte pare hatāḥ||4||

5pratyakṣaṃ sukhabhūyiṣṭhamātmasākṣikamacchalam
|
sarvalokahitaṃ dharmaṃ kṣatriyeṣu pratiṣṭhitam||5||

6dharmāśrame'dhyavasināṃ brāhmaṇānāṃ yudhiṣṭhira
|
yathā trayāṇāṃ varṇānāṃ saṃkhyātopaśrutiḥ purāḥ||6||

7rājadharmeṣvanumatā lokāḥ sucaritaiḥ saha
|
udāhṛtaṃ te rājendra yathā viṣṇuṃ mahaujasam||7||

8sarvabhūteśvaraṃ devaṃ prabhuṃ nārāyaṇaṃ purā
|
jagmuḥ subahuśaḥ śūrā rājāno daṇḍanītaye||8||

9ekaikamātmanaḥ karma tulayitvā''śramaṃ purā
|
rājānaḥ paryupāsanta dṛṣṭāntavacane sthitāḥ||9||

10sādhyā devā vasavaścāśvinau ca rudrāśca viśve marutāṃ gaṇāśca
|
sṛṣṭāḥ purā hyādidevena devāḥ kṣātre dharme vartayante ca siddhāḥ||10||

11atra te vartayiṣyāmi dharmamarthaviniścayam
|
nirmaryāde vartamāne dānavaikārṇave purā||11||

12babhūva rājā rājendra māndhātā nāma vīryavān
|
purā vasumatīpālo yajñaṃ cakre didṛkṣayā||12||

13anādimadhyanidhanaṃ devaṃ nārāyaṇaṃ prabhum
|
sa rājā rājaśārdūla māndhātā parameśvaram||13||

14jagāma śirasā pādau yajñe viṣṇormahātmanaḥ
|
darśayāmāsa taṃ viṣṇū rūpamāsthāya vāsavam||14||

15sa pārthivairvṛtaḥ sadbhirarcayāmāsa taṃ prabhum
|
tasya pārthivasiṃhasya tasya caiva mahātmanaḥ
|
saṃvādo'yaṃ mahānāsīdviṣṇuṃ prati mahādyutim||15||

16indra uvāca| kimiṣyate dharmabhṛtāṃ variṣṭha yaddraṣṭukāmo'si tamaprameyam
|
anantamāyāmitamantravīryaṃ nārāyaṇaṃ hyādidevaṃ purāṇam||16||

17nāsau devo viśvarūpo mayāpi śakyo draṣṭuṃ brahmaṇā vāpi sākṣāt
|
ye'nye kāmāstava rājan hṛdisthā dāsye caitāṃstvaṃ hi martyeṣu rājā||17||

18satye sthito dharmaparo jitendriyaḥ śūro dṛḍhaprītirataḥ surāṇām
|
buddhyā bhaktyā cottamaḥ śraddhayā ca tataste'haṃ dadhi varānyatheṣṭam||18||

19māndhātovāca
|
asaṃśayaṃ bhagavannādidevaṃ drakṣyāmi tvā'haṃ śirasā samprasādya
|
tyaktvā kāmāndharmakāmo hyaraṇyamicche gantuṃ satpathaṃ lokadṛṣṭam||19||

20kṣātrāddharmādvipulādaprameyāllokāḥ prāptāḥ sthāpitaṃ svaṃ yaśaśca
|
dharmo yo'sāvādidevātpravṛtto lokaśreṣṭhaṃ taṃ na jānāmi kartum||20||

21indra uvāca| asainikā dharmaparāśca dharme parāṃ gatiṃ na nayante hyayuktam
|
kṣātro dharmo hyādidevātpravṛttaḥ paścādanye śeṣabhūtāśca dharmāḥ||21||

22śeṣāḥ sṛṣṭā hyantavanto hyanantāḥ saprasthānāḥ kṣātradharmā viśiṣṭāḥ
|
asmindharme sarvadharmāḥ praviṣṭāstasmāddharmaṃ śreṣṭhamimaṃ vadanti||22||

23karmaṇā vai purā devā ṛṣayaścāmitaujasaḥ
|
trātāḥ sarve prasahyārīnkṣatradharmeṇa viṣṇunā||23||

24yadi hyasau bhagavānnāhaniṣyadripūn sarvānasurānaprameyaḥ
|
na brāhmaṇā na ca lokādikartā nāyaṃ dharmo nādidharmo'bhaviṣyat||24||

25imāmurvīṃ nājayadvikrameṇa devaśreṣṭhaḥ sāsurāmādidevaḥ
|
cāturvarṇyaṃ cāturāśramyadharmāḥ sarve na syurbrāhmaṇānāṃ vināśāt||25||

26naṣṭā dharmāḥ śatadhā śāśvatāste kṣātreṇa dharmeṇa punaḥ pravṛddhāḥ
|
yuge yuge hyādidharmāḥ pravṛttā lokajyeṣṭhaṃ kṣātradharmaṃ vadanti||26||

27ātmatyāgaḥ sarvabhūtānukampā lokajñānaṃ pālanaṃ mokṣaṇaṃ ca
|
viṣaṇṇānāṃ mokṣaṇaṃ pīḍitānāṃ kṣātre dharme vidyate pārthivānām||27||

28nirmaryādāḥ kāmamanyupravṛttā bhītā rājño nādhigacchanti pāpam
|
śiṣṭāścānye sarvadharmopapannāḥ sādhvācārāḥ sādhu dharmaṃ vadanti||28||

29putravatpālyamānāni rājadharmeṇa pārthivaiḥ
|
loke bhūtāni sarvāṇi carante nātra saṃśayaḥ||29||

30sarvadharmaparaṃ kṣātraṃ lokaśreṣṭhaṃ sanātanam
|
śaśvadakṣaraparyantamakṣaraṃ sarvatomukham||30||
