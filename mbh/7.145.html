Mahābhārata 7.145 (Droṇaparvan)

1dhṛtarāṣṭra uvāca|
tadavasthe hate tasmin bhūriśravasi kaurave
|
yathā bhūyo'bhavadyuddhaṃ tanmamācakṣva sañjaya||1||

2sañjaya uvāca|
bhūriśravasi saṃkrānte paralokāya bhārata
|
vāsudevaṃ mahābāhurarjunaḥ samacūcudat||2||

3codayāśvān bhṛśaṃ kṛṣṇa yato rājā jayadrathaḥ
|
śrūyate puṇḍarīkākṣa triṣu dharmeṣu vartate||3||

4pratijñāṃ saphalāṃ cāpi kartumarhasi me'nagha
|
astameti mahābāho tvaramāṇo divākaraḥ||4||

5etaddhi puruṣavyāghra mahadabhyudyataṃ mayā
|
kāryaṃ saṃrakṣyate caiṣa kurusenā mahārathaiḥ||5||

6yathā nābhyeti sūryo'staṃ yathā satyaṃ bhavedvacaḥ
|
codayāśvāṃstathā kṛṣṇa yathā hanyāṃ jayadratham||6||

7tataḥ kṛṣṇo mahābāhū rajatapratimān hayān
|
hayajñaścodayāmāsa jayadrathavadhaṃ prati||7||

8taṃ prayāntamamogheṣumutpatadbhirivāśugaiḥ
|
tvaramāṇā mahārāja senāmukhyāḥ samādravan||8||

9duryodhanaśca karṇaśca vṛṣaseno'tha madrarāṭ
|
aśvatthāmā kṛpaścaiva svayameva ca saindhavaḥ||9||

10samāsādya ca bībhatsuḥ saindhavaṃ samupasthitam
|
netrābhyāṃ krodhadīptābhyāṃ saṃprekṣannirdahanniva||10||

11tato duryodhano rājā rādheyaṃ tvarito'bravīt
|
arjunaṃ prekṣya saṃyātaṃ jayadrathavadhaṃ prati||11||

12ayaṃ sa vaikartana yuddhakālo vidarśayasvātmabalaṃ mahātman
|
yathā na vadhyeta raṇe'rjunena jayadrathaḥ karṇa tathā kuruṣva||12||

13alpāvaśeṣo divaso nṛvīra vighātayasvādya ripuṃ śaraughaiḥ
|
dinakṣayaṃ prāpya narapravīra dhruvo hi naḥ karṇajayo bhaviṣyati||13||

14saindhave rakṣyamāṇe tu sūryasyāstamanaṃ prati
|
mithyāpratijñaḥ kaunteyaḥ pravekṣyati hutāśanam||14||

15anarjunāyāṃ ca bhuvi muhūrtamapi mānada
|
jīvituṃ notsaheranvai bhrātaro'sya sahānugāḥ||15||

16vinaṣṭaiḥ pāṇḍaveyaiśca saśailavanakānanām
|
vasundharāmimāṃ karṇa bhokṣyāmo hatakaṇṭakām||16||

17daivenopahataḥ pārtho viparītaśca mānada
|
kāryākāryamajānānaḥ pratijñāṃ kṛtavān raṇe||17||

18nūnamātmavināśāya pāṇḍavena kirīṭinā
|
pratijñeyaṃ kṛtā karṇa jayadrathavadhaṃ prati||18||

19kathaṃ jīvati durdharṣe tvayi rādheya phālgunaḥ
|
anastaṃgata āditye hanyātsaindhavakaṃ nṛpam||19||

20rakṣitaṃ madrarājena kṛpeṇa ca mahātmanā
|
jayadrathaṃ raṇamukhe kathaṃ hanyāddhanañjayaḥ||20||

21drauṇinā rakṣyamāṇaṃ ca mayā duḥśāsanena ca
|
kathaṃ prāpsyati bībhatsuḥ saindhavaṃ kālacoditaḥ||21||

22yudhyante bahavaḥ śūrā lambate ca divākaraḥ
|
śaṅke jayadrathaṃ pārtho naiva prāpsyati mānada||22||

23sa tvaṃ karṇa mayā sārdhaṃ śūraiścānyairmahārathaiḥ
|
drauṇinā tvaṃ hi sahito madreśena kṛpeṇa ca||23||

24yudhyasva yatnamāsthāya paraṃ pārthena saṃyuge
|
evamuktastu rādheyastava putreṇa māriṣa||24||

25duryodhanamidaṃ vākyaṃ pratyuvāca kurūttamam
|
dṛḍhalakṣyeṇa vīreṇa bhīmasenena dhanvinā||25||

26bhṛśaṃ bhinnatanuḥ saṃkhye śarajālairanekaśaḥ
|
sthātavyamiti tiṣṭhāmi raṇe saṃprati mānada||26||

27nāṅgamiṃgati kiñcinme santaptasya maheṣubhiḥ
|
yotsyāmi tu yathāśaktyā tvadarthaṃ jīvitaṃ mama||27||

28yathā pāṇḍavamukhyo'sau na haniṣyati saindhavam
|
na hi me yudhyamānasya sāyakānasyataḥ śitān||28||

29saindhavaṃ prāpsyate vīraḥ savyasācī dhanañjayaḥ
|
yattu bhaktimatā kāryaṃ satataṃ hitakāṅkṣiṇā||29||

30tatkariṣyāmi kauravya jayo daive pratiṣṭhitaḥ
|
saindhavārthe paraṃ yatnaṃ kariṣyāmyadya saṃyuge||30||

31tvatpriyārthaṃ mahārāja jayo daive pratiṣṭhitaḥ
|
adya yotsye'rjunamahaṃ pauruṣaṃ svaṃ vyapāśritaḥ||31||

32tvadarthe puruṣavyāghra jayo daive pratiṣṭhitaḥ
|
adya yuddhaṃ kuruśreṣṭha mama pārthasya cobhayoḥ||32||

33paśyantu sarvasainyāni dāruṇaṃ lomaharṣaṇam
|
karṇakauravayorevaṃ raṇe saṃbhāṣamāṇayoḥ||33||

34arjuno niśitairbāṇairjaghāna tava vāhinīm
|
ciccheda niśitairbāṇaiḥ śūrāṇāmanivartinām||34||

35bhujānparighasaṃkāśānhastihastopamān raṇe
|
śirāṃsi ca mahābāhuściccheda niśitaiḥ śaraiḥ||35||

36hastihastānhayagrīvān rathākṣāṃśca samantataḥ
|
śoṇitāktānhayārohāngṛhītaprāsatomarān||36||

37kṣuraiściccheda bībhatsurdvidhaikaikaṃ tridhaiva ca
|
hayānvāraṇamukhyāṃśca prāpatanta samantataḥ||37||

38dhvajāśchatrāṇi cāpāni cāmarāṇi śirāṃsi ca
|
kakṣamagnirivodbhūtaḥ pradahaṃstava vāhinīm||38||

39acireṇa mahīṃ pārthaścakāra rudhirottarām
|
hatabhūyiṣṭhayodhaṃ tatkṛtvā tava balaṃ balī||39||

40āsasāda durādharṣaḥ saindhavaṃ satyavikramaḥ
|
bībhatsurbhīmasenena sātvatena ca rakṣitaḥ||40||

41prababhau bharataśreṣṭha jvalanniva hutāśanaḥ
|
taṃ tathā'vasthitaṃ dṛṣṭvā tvadīyā vīryasaṃpadā||41||

42nāmṛṣyanta maheṣvāsāḥ pāṇḍavaṃ puruṣarṣabhāḥ
|
duryodhanaśca karṇaśca vṛṣaseno'tha madrarāṭ||42||

43aśvatthāmā kṛpaścaiva svayameva ca saindhavaḥ
|
sannaddhāḥ saindhavasyārthe samāvṛṇvankirīṭinam||43||

44nṛtyantaṃ rathamārgeṣu dhanurjyātalaniḥsvanaiḥ
|
saṃgrāmakovidaṃ pārthaṃ sarve yuddhaviśāradāḥ||44||

45abhītāḥ paryavartanta vyāditāsyamivāntakam
|
saindhavaṃ pṛṣṭhataḥ kṛtvā jighāṃsanto'cyutārjunau||45||

46sūryāstamanamicchanto lohitāyati bhāskare
|
te bhujairbhogibhogābhairdhanūṃṣyānamya sāyakān||46||

47mumucuḥ sūryaraśmyābhāñchataśaḥ phālgunaṃ prati
|
tatastānasyamānāṃśca kirīṭī yuddhadurmadaḥ||47||

48dvidhā tridhā'ṣṭadhaikaikaṃ chittvā vivyādha tān rathān
|
siṃhalāṅgūlaketustu darśayanvīryamātmanaḥ||48||

49śāradvatīsuto rājannarjunaṃ pratyavārayat
|
sa viddhvā daśabhiḥ pārthaṃ vāsudevaṃ ca saptabhiḥ||49||

50atiṣṭhadrathamārgeṣu saindhavaṃ pratipālayan
|
athainaṃ kauravaśreṣṭhāḥ sarva eva mahārathāḥ||50||

51mahatā rathavaṃśena sarvataḥ pratyavārayan
|
visphārayaṃtaścāpāni visṛjantaśca sāyakān||51||

52saindhavaṃ paryarakṣanta śāsanāttanayasya te
|
tataḥ pārthasya śūrasya bāhvorbalamadṛśyata||52||

53iṣūṇāmakṣayatvaṃ ca dhanuṣo gāṇḍivasya ca
|
astrairastrāṇi saṃvārya drauṇeḥ śāradvatasya ca||53||

54ekaikaṃ daśabhirbāṇaiḥ sarvāneva samārpayat
|
taṃ drauṇiḥ pañcaviṃśatyā vṛṣasenaśca saptabhiḥ||54||

55duryodhanastu viṃśatyā karṇaśalyau tribhistribhiḥ
|
ta enamabhigarjanto vidhyantaśca punaḥ punaḥ||55||

56vidhunvataśca cāpāni sarvataḥ pratyavārayan
|
śliṣṭaṃ ca sarvataścakrū rathamaṇḍalamāśu te||56||

57sūryāstamanamicchantastvaramāṇā mahārathāḥ
|
ta enamabhinardanto vidhunvānā dhanūṃṣi ca||57||

58siṣicurmārgaṇaistīkṣṇairgiriṃ meghā ivāṃbubhiḥ
|
te mahāstrāṇi divyāni tatra rājanvyadarśayan||58||

59dhanañjayasya gātre tu śūrāḥ parighabāhavaḥ
|
hatabhūyiṣṭhayodhaṃ tatkṛtvā tava balaṃ balī||59||

60āsasāda durādharṣaḥ saindhavaṃ satyavikramaḥ
|
taṃ karṇaḥ saṃyuge rājanpratyavārayadāśugaiḥ||60||

61bhiṣato bhīmasenasya sātvatasya ca bhārata
|
taṃ pārtho daśabhirbāṇaiḥ pratyavidhyadraṇājire ||61||

62sūtaputraṃ mahābāhuḥ sarvasainyasya paśyataḥ
|
sātvataśca tribhirbāṇaiḥ karṇaṃ vivyādha māriṣa||62||

63bhīmasenastribhiścaiva punaḥ pārthaśca saptabhiḥ
|
tānkarṇaḥ prativivyādha paṣṭyā paṣṭyā mahārathaḥ||63||

64tadyuddhamabhavadrājankarṇasya bahubhiḥ saha
|
tatrādbhutamapaśyāma sūtaputrasya māriṣa||64||

65yadekaḥ samare kruddhastrīn rathānparyavārayat
|
phālgunastu mahābāhuḥ karṇaṃ vaikartanaṃ raṇe||65||

66sāyakānāṃ śatenaiva sarvamarmasvatāḍayat
|
rudhirokṣitasarvāṃgaḥ sūtaputraḥ pratāpavān||66||

67śaraiḥ pañcāśatā vīraḥ phālgunaṃ pratyavidhyata
|
tasya tallāghavaṃ dṛṣṭvā nāmṛṣyata raṇe'rjunaḥ||67||

68tataḥ pārtho dhanuśchittvā vivyādhainaṃ stanāṃtare
|
sāyakairnavabhirvīrastvaramāṇo dhanañjayaḥ||68||

69athānyaddhanurādāya sūtaputraḥ pratāpavān
|
sāyakairaṣṭasāhasraiśchādayāmāsa pāṇḍavam||69||

70tāṃ bāṇavṛṣṭimatulāṃ karṇacāpasamutthitām
|
vyadhamatsāyakaiḥ pārthaḥ śalabhāniva mārutaḥ||70||

71chādayāmāsa ca tadā sāyakairarjuno raṇe
|
paśyatāṃ sarvayodhānāṃ darśayan pāṇilāghavam||71||

72vadhārthaṃ cāsya samare sāyakaṃ sūryavarcasam
|
cikṣepa tvarayā yuktastvarākāle dhanañjayaḥ||72||

73tamāpatantaṃ vegena drauṇiściccheda sāyakam
|
ardhacandreṇa tīkṣṇena sa cchinnaḥ prāpatadbhuvi||73||

74karṇo'pi dviṣatāṃ hantā chādayāmāsa phālgunam
|
sāyakairbahusāhasraiḥ kṛtapratikṛtepsayā||74||

75tau vṛṣāviva nardantau narasiṃhau mahārathau
|
sāyakaistu praticchannaṃ cakratuḥ khamajihmagaiḥ||75||

76adṛśyau ca śaraughaistau nighnantāvitaretaram
|
karṇa pārtho'smi tiṣṭha tvaṃ karṇo'haṃ tiṣṭha phālguna||76||

77ityevaṃ tarjayantau tau vākśalyaistudatāṃ tadā
|
yudhyatāṃ samare vīrau citraṃ laghu ca suṣṭhu ca ||77||

78prekṣaṇīyau cābhavatāṃ sarvayodhasamāgame
|
praśasyamānau samare siddha-cāraṇa-pannagaiḥ||78||

79ayudhyetāṃ mahārāja parasparavadhaiṣiṇau
|
tato duryodhano rājaṃstāvakānabhyabhāṣata||79||

80yatnādrakṣata rādheyaṃ nāhatvā samare'rjunam
|
nivartiṣyati rādheya iti māmuktavān vṛṣaḥ||80||

81etasminnantare rājandṛṣṭvā karṇasya vikramam
|
ākarṇamuktairiṣubhiḥ karṇasya caturo hayān||81||

82anayatpretalokāya caturbhiḥ śvetavāhanaḥ
|
sārathiṃ cāsya bhallena rathanīḍādapātayat||82||

83chādayāmāsa sa śaraistava putrasya paśyataḥ
|
saṃchādyamānaḥ samare hatāśvo hatasārathiḥ||83||

84mohitaḥ śarajālena kartavyaṃ nābhyapadyata
|
taṃ tathā virathaṃ dṛṣṭvā rathamāropya taṃ tadā||84||

85aśvatthāmā mahārāja bhūyo'rjunamayodhayat
|
madrarājaśca kaunteyamavidhyattriṃśatā śaraiḥ||85||

86śāradvatastu viṃśatyā vāsudevaṃ samārpayat
|
dhanañjayaṃ dvādaśabhirājaghāna śilīmukhaiḥ||86||

87caturbhiḥ sindhurājaśca vṛṣasenaśca saptabhiḥ
|
pṛthak pṛthaṅmahārāja vivyadhuḥ kṛṣṇapāṇḍavau||87||

88tathaiva tānpratyavidhyatkuntīputro dhanañjayaḥ
|
droṇaputraṃ catuḥṣaṣṭyā madrarājaṃ śatena ca||88||

89saindhavaṃ daśabhirbāṇairvṛṣasenaṃ tribhiḥ śaraiḥ
|
śāradvataṃ ca viṃśatyā viddhvā pārtho nanāda ha||89||

90te pratijñāpratīghātamicchantaḥ savyasācinaḥ
|
sahitāstāvakāstūrṇamabhipeturdhanañjayam||90||

91athārjunaḥ sarvato vāruṇāstraṃ prāduścakre trāsayandhārtarāṣṭrān
|
taṃ pratyudīyuḥ kuravaḥ pāṇḍuputraṃ rathairmahārhaiḥ śaravarṣāṇyavarṣan||91||

92tatastu tasmiṃstumule samutthite sudāruṇe bhārata mohanīye
|
no'muhyata prāpya sa rājaputraḥ kirīṭamālī vyasṛjaccharaughān||92||

93rājyaprepsuḥ savyasācī kurūṇāṃ smarankleśān dvādaśavarṣavṛttān
|
gāṇḍīvamuktairiṣubhirmahātmā sarvā diśo vyāvṛṇodaprameyaḥ||93||

94pradīptolkamabhavaccāntarikṣaṃ mṛteṣu deheṣvapatanvayāṃsi
|
yatpiṅgalajyena kirīṭamālī kruddho ripūnājagavena hanti||94||

95tataḥ kirīṭī mahatā mahāyaśāḥ śarāsanenāsya śarānanīkajit
|
hayapravekottamanāgaghūrṇitān kurupravīrāniṣubhirvyapātayat||95||

96gadāśca gurvīḥ parighānayasmayānasīṃśca śaktīśca raṇe narādhipāḥ
|
mahānti śastrāṇi ca bhīmadarśanāḥ pragṛhya pārthaṃ sahasā'bhidudruvuḥ||96||

97tato yugāntābhrasamasvanaṃ mahanmahendracāpapratimaṃ ca gāṇḍivam
|
cakarṣa dorbhyāṃ vihasanbhṛśaṃ yayau dahaṃstvadīyān yamarāṣṭravardhanaḥ||97||

98sa tānudīrṇānsarathānsavāraṇānpadātisaṅghāṃśca mahādhanurdharaḥ
|
vipannasarvāyudhajīvitān raṇe cakāra vīro yamarāṣṭravardhanān||98||
