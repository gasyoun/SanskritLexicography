Mahābhārata 7.70 (Droṇaparvan)

1nārada uvāca|
rāmo mahātapāḥ śūro vīralokanamaskṛtaḥ
|
jāmagnyo'pyatiyaśā avitṛpto mariṣyati ||1||

2yasmādyamanuparyeti bhūmiṃ kurvannimāṃ sukhām
|
na cāsīdvikriyā yasya prāpya śriyamanuttamām ||2||

3yaḥ kṣatriyaiḥ parāmṛṣṭe vatse pitari cābruvan
|
tato'vadhīt kārtavīryamajitaṃ samare paraiḥ ||3||

4kṣatriyāṇāṃ catuḥṣaṣṭimayutāni sahasraśaḥ
|
tadā mṛtyoḥ sametāni ekena dhanuṣā'jayat ||4||

5brahmadviṣāṃ cātha tasmin sahasrāṇi caturdaśa
|
punaranyānnijagrāha dantakrūraṃ jaghāna ha ||5||

6sahasraṃ musalenāhan sahasramasinā'vadhīt
|
udbandhanāt sahasraṃ ca sahasramudake dhṛtam ||6||

7dantān bhaṃktvā sahasrasya karṇānnāsānyakṛtanta
|
tataḥ saptasahasrāṇāṃ kaṭudhūpamapāyayat ||7||

8śiṣṭān baddhvā ca hatvā vai teṣāṃ mūrdhni vibhidya ca
|
guṇāvatīmuttareṇa khāṇḍavāddakṣiṇena ca
|
giryante śatasāhasrā haihayāḥ samare hatāḥ ||8||

9sarathāśvagajā vīrā nihatāstatra śerate
|
piturvadhāmarṣitena jāmadagnyena dhīmatā ||9||

10nijaghne daśasāhasrān rāmaḥ paraśunā tadā
|
na hyamṛṣyata tā vāco yāstairbhṛśamudīritāḥ ||10||

11bhṛgau rāmābhidhāveti yadākrandan dvijottamāḥ
|
tataḥ kāśmīradaradān kuntikṣudrakamālavān ||11||

12aṅgavaṅgakaliṅgāṃśca videhāṃstāmraliptakān
|
rakṣovāhān vītihotrāṃstrigartān mārtikāvatān ||12||

13śibīnanyāṃśca rājanyān deśān deśān sahasraśaḥ
|
nijaghāna śitairbāṇairjāmadagnyaḥ pratāpavān ||13||

14koṭīśatasaha‌srāṇi kṣatriyāṇāṃ sahasraśaḥ
|
indragopakavarṇasya bandhujīvanibhasya ca ||14||

15rudhirasya parīvāhaiḥ pūrayitvā sarāṃsi ca
|
sarvānaṣṭādaśa dvīpān vaśamānīya bhārgavaḥ ||15||

16īje kratuśataiḥ puṇyaiḥ samāptavaradakṣiṇaiḥ
|
vedīmaṣṭanalotsedhāṃ sauvarṇāṃ vidhinirmitām ||16||

17sarvaratnaśataiḥ pūrṇāṃ patākāśatamālinīm
|
grāmyāraṇyaiḥ paśugaṇaiḥ sampūrṇāṃ ca mahīmimām ||17||

18rāmasya jāmadagnyasya pratijagrāha kaśyapaḥ
|
tataḥ śatasaha‌srāṇi dvipendrān hemabhūṣaṇān ||18||

19nirdasyuṃ pṛthivīṃ kṛtvā śiṣṭeṣṭajanasaṅkulām
|
kaśyapāya dadau rāmo hayamedhe mahāmakhe ||19||

20triḥsaptakṛtvaḥ pṛthivīṃ kṛtvā niḥkṣatriyāṃ prabhuḥ
|
iṣṭvā kratuśatairvīro brāhmaṇebhyo hyamanyata ||20||

21saptadvīpāṃ vasumatīṃ mārīco'gṛhṇata dvijaḥ
|
rāmaṃ prāvāca nirgaccha vasudhāto mamājñayā ||21||

22sa kaśyapasya vacanāt protsārya saritāṃ patim
|
iṣupāte yudhāṃ śreṣṭhaḥ kurvan brāhmaṇaśāsanam ||22||

23adhyāvasadgiriśreṣṭhaṃ mahendraṃ parvatottamam
|
evaṃ guṇaśatairyukto bhṛgūṇāṃ kīrtivardhanaḥ ||23||

24jāmadagnyo hyatiyaśā mariṣyati mahādyutiḥ
|
tvayā caturbhadrataraḥ putrāt puṇyatarastava ||24||

25ayajvānamadākṣiṇyaṃ mā putramanutapyathāḥ
|
ete caturbhadratarāstvayā bhadraśatādhikāḥ
|
mṛtā naravaraśreṣṭha mariṣyanti ca sṛñjaya ||25||
