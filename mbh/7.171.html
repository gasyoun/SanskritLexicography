Mahābhārata 7.171 (Droṇaparvan)

1sañjaya uvāca|
tataste prādravansarve tvaritā yuddhadurmadāḥ
|
amṛṣyamāṇāḥ saṃrabdhā yuyudhānarathaṃ prati||1||

2te rathaiḥ kalpitai rājanhemarūpyavibhūṣitaiḥ
|
sādibhiśca gajaiścaiva parivavruḥ samantataḥ||2||

3athainaṃ koṣṭhakīkṛtya sarvataste mahārathāḥ
|
siṃhanādāṃstataścakrustarjayanti sma sātyakim||3||

4te'bhyavarṣañcharaistīkṣṇaiḥ sātyakiṃ satyavikramam
|
tvaramāṇā mahāvīrā mādhavasya vadhaiṣiṇaḥ||4||

5tān dṛṣṭvā patatastūrṇaṃ śaineyaḥ paravīrahā
|
pratyagṛhṇānmahābāhuḥ pramuñcanviśikhānbahūn||5||

6tatra vīro maheṣvāsaḥ sātyakiryuddhadurmadaḥ
|
nicakarta śirāṃsyugraiḥ śaraiḥ sannataparvabhiḥ||6||

7hastihastānhayagrīvā bāhūnapi ca sāyudhān
|
kṣurapraiśchā‌dayāmāsa tāvakānāṃ sa mādhavaḥ||7||

8patitaiścāmaraiścaiva śvetacchatraiśca bhārata
|
babhūva dharaṇī pūrṇā nakṣatrairdyauriva prabho||8||

9eteṣāṃ yuyudhānena yudhyatāṃ yudhi bhārata
|
babhūva tumulaḥ śabdaḥ pretānāṃ krandatāmiva||9||

10tena śabdena mahatā pūritā'bhūdvasundharā
|
rātriḥ samabhavaccaiva tīvrarūpā bhayāvahā||10||

11dīryamāṇaṃ balaṃ dṛṣṭvā yuyudhānaśarāhatam
|
śrutvā ca vipulaṃ nādaṃ niśīthe lomaharṣaṇe||11||

12sutastavāvravīdrājan sārathiṃ rathināṃ varaḥ
|
yatraiṣa śabdastatrāśvāṃścodayeti punaḥ punaḥ||12||

13tena saṃcodyamānastu tatastāṃsturagottamān
|
sutaḥ saṃcodayāmāsa yuyudhānarathaṃ prati||13||

14tato duryodhanaḥ kruddho dṛḍhadhanvā jitaklamaḥ
|
śīghrahastaścitrayodhī yuyudhānamupādravat||14||

15tataḥ pūrṇāyatotsṛṣṭaiḥ śaraiḥ śoṇitabhojanaiḥ
|
duryodhanaṃ dvādaśabhirmādhavaḥ pratyavidhyata||15||

16duryodhanastena tathā pūrvamevārditaḥ śaraiḥ
|
śaineyaṃ daśabhirbāṇaiḥ pratyavidhyadamarṣitaḥ||16||

17tataḥ samabhavadyuddhaṃ tumulaṃ bharatarṣabha
|
pañcālānāṃ ca sarveṣāṃ bharatānāṃ ca dāruṇam||17||

18śaineyastu raṇe kruddhastava putraṃ mahāratham
|
sāyakānāmaśītyā tu vivyādhorasi bhārata||18||

19tato'sya vāhānsamare śarairninye yamakṣayam
|
sārathiṃ ca rathāttūrṇaṃ pātayāmāsa patriṇā||19||

20hatāśve tu rathe tiṣṭhanputrastava viśāṃpate
|
mumoca niśitānbāṇān śaineyasya rathaṃ prati||20||

21śarānpañcāśatastāṃstu śaineyaḥ kṛtahastavat
|
ciccheda samare rājanpreṣitāṃstanayena te||21||

22athāpareṇa bhallena muṣṭideśe mahaddhanuḥ
|
ciccheda tarasā yuddhe tava putrasya mādhavaḥ||22||

23viratho vidhanuṣkaśca sarvalokeśvaraḥ prabhuḥ
|
āruroha rathaṃ tūrṇaṃ bhāsvaraṃ kṛtavarmaṇaḥ||23||

24duryodhane parāvṛtte śaineyastava vāhinīm
|
drāvayāmāsa viśikhairniśāmadhye viśāṃpate||24||

25śakuniścārjunaṃ rājanparivārya samaṃtataḥ
|
rathairanekasāhastrairgajaiścāpi sahasraśaḥ||25||

26tathā hayasahastraiśca nānāśastrairavākirat
|
te mahāstrāṇi sarvāṇi vikiranto'rjunaṃ prati||26||

27arjunaṃ yodhayanti sma kṣatriyāḥ kālacoditāḥ
|
tānyarjunaḥ sahasrāṇi rathavāraṇavājinām||27||

28pratyavārayadāyastaḥ prakurvanvipulaṃ kṣayam
|
tatastu samare śūraḥ śakuniḥ saubalastadā||28||

29vivyādha niśitairbāṇairarjunaṃ prahasanniva
|
punaścaiva śatenāsya saṃrurodha mahāratham||29||

30tamarjunastu viṃśatyā vivyādha yudhi bhārata
|
athetarānmaheṣvāsāṃstribhistribhiravidhyata||30||

31nivārya tānbāṇagaṇairyudhi rājandhanañjayaḥ
|
jaghāna tāvakānyodhānvajrapāṇirivāsurān||31||

32bhujaiśchinnairmahīpāla hastihastopamairmṛdhe
|
samākīrṇā mahī bhāti pañcāsyairiva pannagaiḥ||32||

33śirobhiḥ sakirīṭaiśca sunasaiścārukuṇḍalaiḥ
|
saṃdaṣṭauṣṭhapuṭaiḥ kruddhaistathaivoddhṛtalocanaiḥ||33||

34niṣkacūḍāmaṇidharaiḥ kṣatriyāṇāṃ priyaṃvadaiḥ
|
paṅkajairiva vinyastaiḥ parvatairvibabhau mahī||34||

35kṛtvā tatkarma bībhatsurugramugraparākramaḥ
|
vivyādha śakuniṃ bhūyaḥ pañcabhirnataparvabhiḥ||35||

36atāḍayadulūkaṃ ca tribhireva tathā śaraiḥ
|
ulūkastu tathā viddho vāsudevamatāḍayat||36||

37nanāda ca mahānādaṃ pūrayanniva medinīm
|
arjunaḥ śakuneścāpaṃ sāyakairacchinadraṇe||37||

38ninye ca caturo vāhānyamasya sadanaṃ prati
|
tato rathādavaplutya saubalo bharatarṣabha||38||

39ulūkasya rathaṃ tūrṇamāruroha viśāṃpate
|
tāvekarathamārūḍhau pitāputrau mahārathau||39||

40pārthaṃ siṣicaturbāṇairgiriṃ meghāvivāmbubhiḥ
|
tau tu viddhvā mahārāja pāṇḍavo niśitaiḥ śaraiḥ||40||

41vidrāvayaṃstava camūṃ śataśo vyadhamaccharaiḥ
|
anilena yathā'bhrāṇi vicchinnāni samantataḥ||41||

42vicchinnāni tathā rājan balānyāsan viśāṃpate
|
tadbalaṃ bharataśreṣṭha vadhyamānaṃ tadā niśi||42||

43pradudrāva diśaḥ sarvā vīkṣyamāṇaṃ bhayārditam
|
utsṛjya vāhānsamare codayantastathā pare||43||

44saṃbhrāntāḥ paryadhāvanta tasmiṃstamasi dāruṇe
|
vijitya samare yodhāṃstāvakān bharatarṣabha||44||

45dadhmaturmuditau śaṃkhau vāsudevadhanañjayau
|
dhṛṣṭadyumno mahārāja droṇaṃ viddhvā tribhiḥ śaraiḥ||45||

46ciccheda dhanuṣastūrṇaṃ jyāṃ śareṇa śitena ha
|
tannidhāya dhanurbhūmau droṇaḥ kṣatriyamardanaḥ||46||

47ādade'nyaddhanuḥ śūro vegavatsāravattaram
|
dhṛṣṭadyumnaṃ tato droṇo viddhvā saptabhirāśugaiḥ||47||

48sārathiṃ pañcabhirbāṇai rājanvivyādha saṃyuge
|
taṃ nivārya śaraistūrṇaṃ dhṛṣṭadyumno mahārathaḥ||48||

49vyadhamatkauravīṃ senāmāsurīṃ maghavāniva
|
vadhyamāne bale tasmiṃstava putrasya māriṣa||49||

50prāvartata nadī ghorā śoṇitaughataraṅgiṇī
|
ubhayoḥ senayormadhye narāśvadvipavāhinī||50||

51yathā vaitaraṇī rājan yamarājapuraṃ prati
|
drāvayitvā tu tatsainyaṃ dhṛṣṭadyumnaḥ pratāpavān||51||

52abhyarājata tejasvī śakro devagaṇeṣviva
|
atha dadhmurmahāśaṃkhāndhṛṣṭadyumnaśikhaṇḍinau||52||

53yamau ca yuyudhānaśca pāṇḍavaśca vṛkodaraḥ
|
jitvā rathasahasrāṇi tāvakānāṃ mahārathāḥ||53||

54siṃhanādavāṃścakruḥ pāṇḍavā jitakāśinaḥ
|
paśyatastava putrasya karṇasya ca raṇotkaṭāḥ
|
tathā droṇasya śūrasya drauṇeścaiva viśāṃpate||54||
