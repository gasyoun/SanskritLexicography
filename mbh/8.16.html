Mahābhārata 8.16 (Karṇaparvan)

1dhṛtarāṣṭra uvāca|
yathā saṃśaptakaiḥ sārdhamarjunasyābhavadraṇaḥ
|
anyeṣāṃ ca mahīpānāṃ pāṇḍavaistadbravīhi me ||1||

2aśvatthāmnastu yadyuddhamarjunasya ca sañjaya
|
anyeṣāṃ ca mahīpānāṃ pāṇḍavaistadbravīhi me ||2||

3sañjaya uvāca|
śṛṇu rājanyathā vṛttaṃ saṃgrāmaṃ bruvato mama
|
vīrāṇāṃ śatrubhiḥ sārdhaṃ dehapāpmāsunāśanam ||3||

4pārthaḥ saṃśaptakabalaṃ praviśyārṇavasannibham
|
vyakṣobhayada‌mitraghno mahāvāta ivārṇavam ||4||

5śirāṃsyunmathya vīrāṇāṃ śitairbhallairdhanañjayaḥ
|
pūrṇacandrābhavaktrāṇi svakṣibhrūdaśanāni ca ||5||

6santastāra kṣitiṃ kṣipraṃ vinālairnalinairiva
|
suvṛttānāyatān puṣṭāṃścandanāgurubhūṣitān ||6||

7sāyudhānsatalatrāṃśca pañcāsyoragasannibhān
|
bāhūn kṣurairamitrāṇāṃ ciccheda samare'rjunaḥ ||7||

8dhuryān dhuryetarānsutāndhvajāṃścāpāni sāyakān
|
pāṇīn saratnānasakṛdbhallaiściccheda pāṇḍavaḥ ||8||

9rathān dvipān hayāṃścaiva sārohānarjuno yudhi
|
śarairanekasāhasrairninye rājan yamakṣayam ||9||

10taṃ pravīrāḥ susaṃrabdhā nardamānā ivarṣabhāḥ
|
vāsitārthamiva kruddhamabhidrutya madotkaṭāḥ ||10||

11nighnantamabhijaghnuste śaraiḥ śrṛṃgairivarṣabhāḥ
|
tasya teṣāṃ ca tadyuddhamabhavallomaharṣaṇam ||11||

12trailokyavijaye yadvaddaityānāṃ saha vajriṇā
|
astrairasrāṇi saṃvārya dviṣatāṃ sarvato'rjunaḥ ||12||

13iṣubhirbahubhistūrṇaṃ viddhvā prāṇāñjahāra saḥ
|
chinnatriveṇucakrākṣān hatayodhānsasārathīn ||13||

14viddhvastāyudhatūṇīrānsamunmathitaketanān
|
saṃchinnayoktraraśmīkān vivarūthānvikūbarān ||14||

15visrastabandhurayugān visrastākṣapramaṇḍalān
|
rathānviśakalīkurvan mahābhrāṇīva mārutaḥ ||15||

16vismāpayanprekṣaṇīyaṃ dviṣatāṃ bhayavardhanam
|
mahārathasahasrasya samaṃ karmākarojjayaḥ ||16||

17siddhadevarṣisaṃghāśca cāraṇāścāpi tuṣṭuvuḥ
|
devadundubhayo neduḥ puṣpavarṣāṇi cāpatan ||17||

18keśavārjunayormūrdhni prāha vākcāśarīriṇī
|
candrāgnyanilasūryāṇāṃ kāntidīptibaladyutīḥ ||18||

19yau sadā bibhraturvīrāvimau tau keśavārjunau
|
brahmeśānāvivājayyau vīrāvekarathe sthitau ||19||

20sarvabhūtavarau vīrau naranārāyaṇāvimau
|
ityetanmahadāścaryaṃ dṛṣṭvā śrutvā ca bhārata ||20||

21aśvatthāmā susaṃyattaḥ kṛṣṇāvabhyadravadraṇe
|
atha pāṇḍavamasyantamamitraghnakarāñcharān ||21||

22seṣuṇā pāṇinā''hūya prahasan drauṇirabravīt
|
yadi māṃ manyase vīra prāptamarhamihātithim ||22||

23tataḥ sarvātmanā tvadya yuddhātithyaṃ prayaccha me
|
evamācāryaputreṇa samāhūto yuyutsayā ||23||

24bahu mene'rjuno''tmānamiti cāha janārdanam
|
saṃśaptakāśca me vadhyā drauṇirāhvayate ca mām ||24||

25yadatrānantaraṃ prāptaṃ śaṃsa me taddhi mādhava
|
ātithyakarmābhyutthāya dīyatāṃ yadi manyase ||25||

26evamukto'vahatpārthaṃ kṛṣṇo droṇātmajāntike
|
jaitreṇa vidhinā''hūtaṃ vāyurindramivādhvare ||26||

27tamāmantryaikamanasaṃ keśavo drauṇimabravīt
|
aśvatthāman sthiro bhūtvā praharāśu sahasva ca ||27||

28nirveṣṭuṃ bhartṛpiṇḍaṃ hi kālo'yamupajīvinām
|
sūkṣmo vivādo viprāṇāṃ sthūlau kṣātrau jayājayau ||28||

29yāmabhyarthayase mohāddivyāṃ pārthasya satkriyām
|
tāmāptumicchan yudhyasva sthiro bhūtvā'dya pāṇḍavam ||29||

30ityukto vāsudevena tathetyuktvā dvijottamaḥ
|
vivyādha keśavaṃ ṣaṣṭyā nārācairarjunaṃ tribhiḥ ||30||

31tasyārjunaḥ susaṃkruddhastribhirbāṇaiḥ śarāsanam
|
ciccheda cānyadādatta drauṇirghorataraṃ dhanuḥ ||31||

32sajyaṃ kṛtvā nimeṣācca vivyādhārjunakeśavau
|
tribhiḥ śatairvāsudevaṃ sahasreṇa ca pāṇḍavam ||32||

33tataḥ śarasahasrāṇi prayutānyarbudāni ca
|
sasṛje drauṇirāyastaḥ saṃstabhya ca raṇe'rjunam ||33||

34iṣudherdhanuṣaścaiva jyāyāścaivātha māriṣa
|
bāhvoḥ karābhyāmuraso vadanaghrāṇanetrataḥ ||34||

35karṇābhyāṃ śirasoṅgebhyo lomavarmabhya eva ca
|
rathadhvajebhyaśca śarā niṣpeturbrahmavādinaḥ ||35||

36śarajālena mahatā viddhvā mādhavapāṇḍavau
|
nanāda mudito drauṇirmahāmeghaughaniḥsvanam ||36||

37tasya taṃ ninadaṃ śrutvā pāṇḍavo'cyutamabravīt
|
paśya mādhava daurātmyaṃ guruputrasya māṃ prati ||37||

38vadhaṃ prāptau manyate nau prāveśya śaraveśmani
|
eṣo'smi hanmi saṃkalpaṃ śikṣayā ca balena ca ||38||

39aśvatthāmnaḥ śarānastān chittvaikaikaṃ tridhā tridhā
|
vyadhamadbharataśreṣṭho nīhāramiva mārutaḥ ||39||

40tataḥ saṃśaptakān bhūyaḥ sāśvasūtarathadvipān
|
dhvajapattigaṇānugrairbāṇairvivyādha pāṇḍavaḥ ||40||

41ye ye dadṛśire tatra yadyadrūpāstadā janāḥ
|
te te tatra śarairvyāptaṃ menire''tmānamātmanā ||41||

42te gāṇḍīvapramuktāstu nānārūpāḥ patatriṇaḥ
|
krośe sāgre sthitān ghnanti dvipāṃśca puruṣānraṇe ||42||

43bhallaiśchinnāḥ karāḥ petuḥ kariṇāṃ madavarṣiṇām
|
yathā vane paraśubhirnikṛttāḥ sumahādrumāḥ ||43||

44paścāttu śailavatpetuste gajāḥ saha sādibhiḥ
|
vajrivajrapramathitā yathaivādricayāstathā ||44||

45gandharvanagarākārān rathāṃścaiva sukalpitān
|
vinītairjavanairyuktānāsthitān yuddhadurmadaiḥ ||45||

46śarairviśakalīkurvannamitrānabhyavīvṛṣat
|
svalaṃkṛtānaśvasādīn pattīṃścāhan dhanañjayaḥ ||46||

47dhanañjayayugāntārkaḥ saṃśaptakamahārṇavam
|
vyaśoṣayata duḥśoṣaṃ tīkṣṇaiḥ śaragabhastibhiḥ ||47||

48punardrauṇiṃ mahāśailaṃ nārācairvajrasannibhaiḥ
|
nirbibheda mahāvegaistvaranvajrīva parvatam ||48||

49tamācāryasutaḥ kruddhaḥ sāśvayantāramāśugaiḥ
|
yuyutsurāgamadyoddhuṃ pārthastānacchinaccharān ||49||

50tataḥ paramasaṃkruddhaḥ pāṇḍave'strāṇyavāsṛjat
|
aśvatthāmābhirūpāya gṛhānatithaye yathā ||50||

51atha saṃśaptakāṃstyaktvā pāṇḍavo drauṇimabhyayāt
|
apāṃkteyāniva tyaktvā dātā pāṃkteyamarthinam ||51||
