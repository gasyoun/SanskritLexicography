Mahābhārata 3.104 (Āraṇyakaparvan)

1yudhiṣṭhira uvāca|
kimarthaṃ sahasā vindhyaḥ pravṛddhaḥ krodhamūrcchitaḥ
|
etadicchāmyahaṃ śrotuṃ vistareṇa mahāmune||1||

2lomaśa uvāca|
adrirājaṃ mahāśailaṃ meruṃ kanakaparvatam
|
udayāstamane bhānuḥ pradakṣiṇamavartata||2||

3taṃ tu dṛṣṭvā tathā vindhyaḥ śailaḥ sūryamathābravīt
|
yathā hi merurbhavatā nityaśaḥ parigamyate||3||

4pradakṣiṇaśca kriyate māmevaṃ kuru bhāskara
|
evamuktastataḥ sūryaḥ śailendraṃ pratyabhāṣata||4||

5nāhamātmacchayā śailaṃ karomyenaṃ pradakṣiṇam
|
eṣa mārgaḥ pradiṣṭo me yairidaṃ nirmitaṃ jagat||5||

6evamuktastataḥ krodhātpravṛddhaḥ sahasācalaḥ
|
sūryācandramasormārgaṃ roddhumicchanparaṃtapa||6||

7tato devāḥ sahitāḥ sarva eva vindhyaṃ samāgamya mahādrirājam
|
nivārayāmāsurupāyatastaṃ na ca sma teṣāṃ vacanaṃ cakāra||7||

8athābhijagmurmunimāśramasthaṃ tapasvinaṃ dharmabhṛtāṃ variṣṭham
|
agastyamatyadbhutavīryavantaṃ taṃ cārthamūcuḥ sahitāḥ surāste||8||

9devā ūcuḥ| sūryācandramasormārgaṃ nakṣatrāṇāṃ gatiṃ tathā
|
śailarājo vṛṇotyeṣa vindhyaḥ krodhavaśānugaḥ||9||

10taṃ nivārayituṃ śakto nānyaḥ kaściddvijottama
|
ṛte tvāṃ hi mahābhāga tasmādenaṃ nivāraya||10||

11tacchrutvā vacanaṃ vipraḥ surāṇāṃ śailamabhyagāt
|
so'bhigamyābravīdvindhyaṃ sadāraḥ samupasthitam ||11||

12mārgamicchāmyahaṃ dattaṃ bhavatā parvatottama
|
dakṣiṇāmabhigantāsmi diśaṃ kāryeṇa kenacit ||12||

13yāvadāgamanaṃ mahyaṃ tāvattvaṃ pratipālaya
|
nivṛtte mayi śailendra tato vardhasva kāmataḥ||13||

14evaṃ sa samayaṃ kṛtvā vindhyenāmitrakarśana
|
adyāpi dakṣiṇāddeśādvāruṇirna nivartate||14||

15etatte sarvamākhyātaṃ yathā vindhyo na vardhate
|
agastyasya prabhāveṇa yanmāṃ tvaṃ paripṛcchasi||15||

16kāleyāstu yathā rājansuraiḥ sarvairniṣūditāḥ
|
agastyādvaramāsādya tanme nigadataḥ śṛṇu||16||

17tridaśānāṃ vacaḥ śrutvā maitrāvaruṇirabravīt
|
kimarthamabhiyātāḥ stha varaṃ mattaḥ kamicchatha
|
evamuktāstatastena devatā munimabruvan||17||

18evaṃ tvayecchāma kṛtaṃ hi kāryaṃ mahārṇavaṃ pīyamānaṃ mahātman
|
tato vadhiṣyāma sahānubandhān kāleyasaṃjñānsuravidviṣastān||18||

19tridaśānāṃ vacaḥ śrutvā tatheti munirabravīt
|
kariṣye bhavatāṃ kāmaṃ lokānāṃ ca mahatsukham||19||

20evamuktvā tato'gacchatsamudraṃ saritāṃ patim
|
ṛṣibhiśca tapaḥ siddhaiḥ sārdhaṃ devaiśca suvrata||20||

21manuṣyoragagandharvayakṣakiṃpuruṣāstathā
|
anujagmurmahātmānaṃ draṣṭukāmāstada‌dbhutam||21||

22tato'bhyagacchansahitāḥ samudraṃ bhīmaniḥsvanam
|
nṛtyantamiva cormībhirvalgantamiva vāyunā||22||

23hasantamiva phenaughaiḥ skhalantaṃ kandareṣu ca
|
nānāgrāhasamākīrṇaṃ nānādvijagaṇānvitam||23||

24agastyasahitā devāḥ sagandharvamahoragāḥ
|
ṛṣayaśca mahābhāgāḥ samāsedurmahodadhim||24||
