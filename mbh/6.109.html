Mahābhārata 6.109 (Bhīṣmaparvan)

1dhṛtarāṣṭra uvāca|
kathaṃ śikhaṇḍī gāṅgeyamabhyadhāvat pitāmaham
|
pāñcālyaḥ samare kruddho dharmātmānaṃ yatavratam ||1||

2ke'rakṣan pāṇḍavānīke śikhaṇḍinamudāyudhāḥ
|
tvaramāṇāstvarākāle jigīṣanto mahārathāḥ ||2||

3kathaṃ śāntanavo bhīṣmaḥ sa tasmin daśame'hani
|
ayudhyata mahāvīryaḥ pāṇḍavaiḥ saha sṛñjayaiḥ ||3||

4na mṛṣyāmi raṇe bhīṣmaṃ pratyudyātaṃ śikhaṇḍinam
|
kaccinna rathabhaṅgo'sya dhanurvā'śīryatāsyataḥ ||4||

5sañjaya uvāca|nāśīryata dhanuścāsya rathabhaṅgo na cāpyabhūt
|
yudhyamānasya saṅgrāme bhīṣmasya bharatarṣabha ||5||

6nighnataḥ samare śātrūn śaraiḥ sannataparvabhiḥ
|
anekaśatasāhasrāstāvakānāṃ mahārathāḥ ||6||

7tathā dantigaṇā rājan hayāścaiva susajjitāḥ
|
abhyavartanta yuddhāya puraskṛtya pitāmaham ||7||

8yathā tijñaṃ kauravye sa cāpi samitiñjayaḥ
|
pārthānāmakarodbhīṣmaḥ satataṃ samiti kṣayam ||8||

9yudhyamānaṃ maheṣvāsaṃ vinighnantaṃ parān śaraiḥ
|
pañcālāḥ pāṇḍavaiḥ sārdhaṃ sarve te nābhyavārayan ||9||

10daśame'hani samprāpte tatastāṃ ripuvāhinīm
|
kīryamāṇāṃ śitairbāṇaiḥ śataśo'tha sahasraśaḥ ||10||

11na hi bhīṣmaṃ maheṣvāsaṃ pāṇḍavāḥ pāṇḍupūrvaja
|
aśaknuvanraṇe jetuṃ pāśahastamivāntakam ||11||

12athopāyānmahārāja savyasācī dhanañjayaḥ
|
trāsayanrathinaḥ sarvān bībhatsuraparājitaḥ ||12||

13siṃhavadvinadannuccairdhanurjyāṃ vikṣipanmuhuḥ
|
śaraughān visṛjan pārtho vyacarat kālavadraṇe ||13||

14tasya śabdena vitrastāstāvakā bharatarṣabha
|
siṃhasyeva mṛgā rājan vyadravanta mahābhayāt ||14||

15jayantaṃ pāṇḍavaṃ dṛṣṭvā tvatsainyaṃ cābhipīḍitam
|
duryodhanastato bhīṣmamabravīdbhṛśapīḍitaḥ ||15||

16eṣa pāṇḍusutastāta śvetāśvaḥ kṛṣṇasārathiḥ
|
dahate māmakānsarvān kṛṣṇavartmeva kānanam ||16||

17paśya sainyāni gāṅgeya dravamāṇāni sarvaśaḥ
|
pāṇḍavena yudhāṃ śreṣṭha kālyamānāni saṃyuge ||17||

18yathā paśugaṇān pālaḥ saṅkālayati kānane
|
tathedaṃ māmakaṃ sainyaṃ kālyate śatrutāpana ||18||

19dhanañjayaśarairbhagnaṃ dravamāṇaṃ tatastataḥ
|
bhīmo'pyava durādharṣo vidrāvayati me balam ||19||

20sātyakiścekitānaśca mādrīputrau ca pāṇḍavau
|
abhimanyuḥ suvikrānto vāhinīṃ dravate mama ||20||

21dhṛṣṭadyumnastathā śūro rākṣasaśca ghaṭotkacaḥ
|
vyadrāvayetāṃ sahasā sainyaṃ mama mahāraṇe ||21||

22vadhyamānasya sainyasya sarvairetairmahārathaiḥ
|
nānyāṃ gatiṃ prapaśyāmi sthāne yuddhe ca bhārata ||22||

23ṛte tvāṃ puruṣavyāghra devatulyaparākrama
|
paryāptastu bhavān śīghraṃ pīḍitānāṃ gatirbhava ||23||

24sañjaya uvāca|evamukto mahārāja pitā devavratastava
|
cintayitvā muhūrtaṃ tu kṛtvā niścayamātmanaḥ ||24||

25tava saṃdhārayan putramabravīcchāntanoḥ sutaḥ
|
duryodhana vijānīhi sthiro bhūtvā viśāṃpate ||25||

26pūrvakālaṃ tava mayā pratijñātaṃ mahābala
|
hatvā daśasahasrāṇi kṣatriyāṇāṃ mahātmanām ||26||

27saṅgrāmādvyapayātavyametatkarma mamāhnikam
|
iti tatkṛtavāṃścāhaṃ yathoktaṃ bharatarṣabha ||27||

28adya cāpi mahat karma prakariṣye mahābala
|
ahaṃ vā'dya hataḥ śeṣye haniṣye vā'dya pāṇḍavān ||28||

29adya te puruṣavyāghra pratimokṣye ṛṇaṃ tava
|
bhartṛpiṇḍakṛtaṃ rājannihataḥ pṛtanāmukhe ||29||

30ityuktvā bharataśreṣṭha kṣatriyān pravapañcharaiḥ
|
āsasāda durādharṣaḥ pāṇḍavānāmanīkinīm ||30||

31anīkamadhye tiṣṭhantaṃ gāṅgeyaṃ bharatarṣabha
|
āśīviṣamiva kruddhaṃ pāṇḍavāḥ pratyavārayan||31||

32daśame'hani bhīṣmastu darśayan śaktimātmanaḥ
|
rājan śatasahasrāṇi so'vadhītkurunandana ||32||

33pañcālānāṃ ca ye śreṣṭhā rājaputrā mahārathāḥ
|
teṣāmādatta tejāṃsi jalaṃ sūrya ivāṃśubhiḥ ||33||

34hatvā daśa sahasrāṇi kuñjarāṇāṃ tarasvinām
|
sārohāṇāṃ mahārāja hayānāṃ cāyutaṃ tathā ||34||

35pūrṇe śatasahastre dve pādātānāṃ narottamaḥ
|
prajajvāla raṇe bhīṣmo vidhūma iva pāvakaḥ ||35||

36na cainaṃ pāṇḍaveyānāṃ kecicchekurnirīkṣitum
|
uttaraṃ mārgamāsthāya tapantamiva bhāskaram ||36||

37te pāṇḍaveyāḥ saṃrabdhā maheṣvāsena pīḍitāḥ
|
vadhāyābhyadravan bhīṣmaṃ sṛñjayāśca mahārathāḥ ||37||

38saṃyuddhyamāno bahubhirbhīṣmaḥ śāntanavastathā
|
avakīrṇo mahāmeruḥ śailo meghairivāvṛtaḥ ||38||

39putrāstu tava gāṅgeyaṃ samantāt paryavārayan
|
mahatyā senayā sārdhaṃ tato yuddhamavartata ||39||
