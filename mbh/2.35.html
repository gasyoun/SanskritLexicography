Mahābhārata 2.35 (Sabhāparvan)

1vaiśampāyana uvāca|
pitāmahaṃ guruṃ caiva pratyudgamya yudhiṣṭhiraḥ
|
abhivādya tato rājannidaṃ vacanamabravīt||1||

2bhīṣmaṃ droṇaṃ kṛpaṃ drauṇiṃ duryodhanaviviṃśatī
|
asminyajñe bhavanto māmanugṛhṇantu sarvaśaḥ||2||

3idaṃ vaḥ sumahaccaiva yadihāsti dhanaṃ mama
|
praṇayantu bhavanto māṃ yatheṣṭamabhimantritaḥ||3||

4evamuktvā sa tānsarvāndīkṣitaḥ pāṇḍavāgrajaḥ
|
yuyoja sa yathāyogamadhikāreṣvanantaram||4||

5bhakṣyabhojyādhikāreṣu duḥśāsanamayojayat
|
parigrahe brāhmaṇānāmaśvatthāmānamuktavān||5||

6rājñāṃ tu pratipūjārthaṃ sañjayaṃ nyayojayat
|
kṛtākṛtaparijñāne bhīṣṇadroṇau mahāmatī||6||

7hiraṇyasya suvarṇasya ratnānāṃ cānvavekṣaṇe
|
dakṣiṇānāṃ ca vai dāne kṛpaṃ rājā nyayojayat||7||

8tathā''nyānpuruṣavyāghrāṃstasmiṃstasminnyayojayat
|
bāhliko dhṛtarāṣṭraśca somadatto jayadrathaḥ
|
nakulena samānītāḥ svāmivattatra remire||8||

9kṣattā vyayakarastvāsīdviduraḥ sarvadharmavit
|
duryodhanastvarhaṇāni pratijagrāha sarvaśaḥ||9||

10caraṇakṣālane kṛṣṇo brāhmaṇānāṃ svayaṃ hyabhūt
|
sarvalokasamāvṛttaḥ piprīṣuḥ phalamuttamam||10||

11draṣṭukāmaḥ sabhāṃ caiva dharmarājaṃ yadhiṣṭhiram
|
na kaścidāharattatra sahasrāvaramarhaṇam||11||

12ratnaiśca bahubhistatra dharmarājamavardhayat
|
kathaṃ tu mama kauravyo ratnadānaiḥ samāpnuyāt||12||

13yajñamityeva rājānaḥ spardhamānā dadurdhanam
|
bhavanaiḥ savimānāgraiḥ sodarkairbalasaṃvṛtaiḥ||13||

14lokarājavimānaiśca brāhmaṇāvasathaiḥ saha
|
kṛtairāvasathairdivyairvimānapratimaistathā||14||

15vicitrai ratvavadbhiśca ṛddhyā paramayā yutaiḥ
|
rājabhiśca samāvṛttairatīva śrīsamṛddhibhiḥ
|
aśobhata sado rājankaunteyasya mahātmanaḥ||15||

16ṛddhyā tu varuṇaṃ devaṃ spardhamāno yudhiṣṭhiraḥ
|
ṣaḍagninātha yajñena so'yajaddakṣiṇāvatā||16||

17sarvāñjanānsarvakāmaiḥ samṛddhaiḥ samatarpayat
|
annavānbahubhakṣyaśca bhuktavajjanasaṃvṛtaḥ
|
ratnopahārasampanno babhūva sa samāgamaḥ||17||

18iḍājyahomāhutibhirmantraśikṣāviśāradaiḥ
|
tasminhi tatṛpurdevāstate yajñe maharṣibhiḥ||18||

19yathā devāstathā viprā dakṣiṇānnamahādhanaiḥ
|
tatṛpuḥ sarvavarṇāśca tasminyajñe mudā'nvitāḥ||19||
