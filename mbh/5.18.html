Mahābhārata 5.18 (Udyogaparvan)

1śalya uvāca|
tataḥ śakraḥ stūyamāno gandharvāpsarasāṃ gaṇaiḥ
|
airāvataṃ samāruhya dvipendraṃ lakṣaṇairyutam ||1||

2pāvakaḥ sumahātejā maharṣiśca bṛhaspatiḥ
|
yamaśca varuṇaścaiva kuberaśca dhaneśvaraḥ ||2||

3sarvairdevaiḥ parivṛtaḥ śakro vṛtraniṣūdanaḥ
|
gandharvairapsarobhiśca yātastribhuvanaṃ prabhuḥ ||3||

4sa sametya mahendrāṇyā devarājaḥ śatakratuḥ
|
mudā paramayā yuktaḥ pālayāmāsa devarāṭ ||4||

5tataḥ sa bhagavāṃstatra aṅgirāḥ samadṛśyata
|
atharvavedamantraiśca devendraṃ samapūjayat||5||

6tatastu bhagavānindraḥ saṃhṛṣṭaḥ samapadyata
|
varaṃ ca pradadau tasmai atharvāṅgirase tadā||6||

7atharvāṅgiraso nāma vede'smin vai bhaviṣyati
|
udāharaṇametaddhi yajñabhāgaṃ ca lapsyase||7||

8evaṃ saṃpūjya bhagavānatharvāṅgirasaṃ tadā
|
vyasarjayanmahārāja devarājaḥ śatakratuḥ ||8||

9saṃpūjya sarvāṃstridaśānṛṣīṃścāpi tapodhanān
|
indraḥ pramudito rājan dharmeṇāpālayat prajāḥ ||9||

10evaṃ duḥkhamanuprāptamindreṇa saha bhāryayā
|
ajñātavāsaśca kṛtaḥ śatrūṇāṃ vadhakāṅkṣayā ||10||

11nātra manyustvayā kāryo yatkliṣṭo'si mahāvane
|
draupadyā saha rājendra bhrātṛbhiśca mahātmabhiḥ ||11||

12evaṃ tvamapi rājendra rājyaṃ prāpsyasi bhārata
|
vṛtraṃ hatvā yathā prāptaḥ śakraḥ kauravanandana ||12||

13durācāraśca nahuṣo brahmadviṭ pāpacetanaḥ
|
agastyaśāpābhihato vinaṣṭaḥ śāśvatīḥ samāḥ||13||

14evaṃ tava durātmānaḥ śatravaḥ śatrusūdana
|
kṣipraṃ nāśaṃ gamiṣyanti karṇaduryodhanādayaḥ ||14||

15tataḥ sāgaraparyantāṃ bhokṣyase medinīmimām
|
bhrātṛbhiḥ sahito vīra draupadyā ca sahānayā ||15||

16upākhyānamidaṃ śakravijayaṃ vedasaṃmitam
|
rājñā vyūḍheṣvanīkeṣu śrotavyaṃ jayamicchatā||16||

17tasmāt saṃśrāvayāmi tvāṃ vijayaṃ jayatāṃ vara
|
saṃstūyamānā vardhante mahātmāno yudhiṣṭhira||17||

18kṣatriyāṇāmabhāvo'yaṃ yudhiṣṭhira mahātmanām
|
duryodhanāparādhena bhīmārjunabalena ca||18||

19ākhyānamindravijayaṃ ya idaṃ niyataḥ paṭhet
|
dhūtapāpmā jitasvargaḥ paratreha ca modate ||19||

20na cārijaṃ bhayaṃ tasya nāputro vā bhavennaraḥ
|
nāpadaṃ prāpnuyāt kiñciddīrghamāyuśca vindati
|
sarvatra dayamāpnoti na kadācit parājayam ||20||

21vaiśampāyana uvāca|
evamāśvāsito rājā śalyena bharatarṣabha
|
pūjayāmāsa vidhivacchalyaṃ dharmabhṛtāṃ varaḥ ||21||

22śrutvā tu śalyavacanaṃ kuntīputro yudhiṣṭhiraḥ
|
pratyuvāca mahābāhurmadrarājamidaṃ vacaḥ ||22||

23bhavān karṇasya sārathyaṃ kariṣyati na saṃśayaḥ
|
tatra tejovadhaḥ kāryaḥ karṇasyārjunasaṃstavaḥ ||23||

24śalya uvāca|
evametat kariṣyāmi yathā māṃ saṃprabhāṣase
|
yaccānyadapi śakṣyāmi tat kariṣyāmyahaṃ tava ||24||

25vaiśampāyana uvāca|
tatastvāmantrya kaunteyāñchalyo madrādhipastadā
|
jagāma sabalaḥ śrīmān duryodhanamarindama ||25||
