Mahābhārata 1.158 (Ādiparvan)

1brāhmaṇyuvāca|
na santāpastvayā kāryaḥ prākṛteneva karhicit
|
na hi santāpakālo'yaṃ vaidyasya tava vidyate||1||

2avaśyaṃ nidhanaṃ sarvairgantavyamiha mānavaiḥ
|
avaśyabhāvinyarthe vai saṃtāpo neha vidyate||2||

3bhāryā putro'tha duhitā sarvamātmārthamiṣyate
|
vyathāṃ jahi subuddhyā tvaṃ svayaṃ yāsyāmi tatra ca||3||

4etaddhi paramaṃ nāryāḥ kāryaṃ loke sanātanam
|
prāṇānapi parityajya yadbharturhitamācaret||4||

5tacca tatra kṛtaṃ karma tavāpīdaṃ sukhāvaham
|
bhavatyamutra cākṣayyaṃ loke'smiṃśca yaśaskaram||5||

6eṣa caiva gururdharmo yaṃ pravakṣyāmyahaṃ tava
|
arthaśca tava dharmaśca bhūyānatra pradṛśyate||6||

7yadarthamiṣyate bhāryā prāptaḥso'rthastvayā mayi
|
kanyā caikā kumāraśca kṛtāhamanṛṇā tvayā||7||

8samarthaḥ poṣaṇe cāsi sutayo rakṣaṇe tathā
|
na tvahaṃ sutayoḥ śaktā tathā rakṣaṇapoṣaṇe||8||

9mama hi tvadvihīnāyāḥ sarvaprāṇadhaneśvara
|
kathaṃ syātāṃ sutau bālau bhareyaṃ ca kathaṃ tvaham||9||

10kathaṃ hi vidhavā'nāthā bālaputrā vinā tvayā
|
mithunaṃ jīvayiṣyāmi sthitā sādhugate pathi||10||

11ahaṃ kṛtāvaliptaiśca prārthyamānāmimāṃ sutām
|
ayuktaistava saṃbandhe kathaṃ śakṣyāmi rakṣitum||11||

12utsṛṣṭamāmiṣaṃ bhūmau prārthayanti yathā khagāḥ
|
prārthayanti janāḥ sarve patihīnāṃ tathā striyam||12||

13sā'haṃ vicālyamānā vai prārthyamānā durātmabhiḥ
|
sthātuṃ pathi na śakṣyāmi sajjaneṣṭe dvijottama||13||

14kathaṃ tava kulasyaikamimaṃ bālamanāgasam
|
pitṛpaitāmahe mārge niyoktumahamutsahe||14||

15kathaṃ śakṣyāmi bāle'sminguṇānādhātumīpsitān
|
anāthe sarvato lupte yathā tvaṃ dharmadarśivān||15||

16imāmapi ca te bālāmanāthāṃ paribhūya mām
|
anarhāḥ prārthayiṣyanti śūdrā vedaśrutiṃ yathā||16||

17tāṃ cedahaṃ na ditseyaṃ tvadguṇairupabṛṃhitām
|
pramathyaināṃ hareyuste havirdhvāṅkṣā ivādhvarāt||17||

18saṃprekṣamāṇā putraṃ te nānurūpamivātmanaḥ
|
anarhavaśamāpannāmimāṃ cāpi sutāṃ tava||18||

19avajñātā ca lokeṣu tathānmānamajānatī
|
avaliptairairbrahmanmariṣyāmi na saṃśayaḥ||19||

20tau ca hīnau mayā bālau tvayā caiva tathātmajau
|
vinaśyetāṃ na sandeho matsyāviva jalakṣaye||20||

21tritayaṃ sarvathāpyevaṃ vinaśiṣyatyasaṃśayam
|
tvayā vihīnaṃ tasmāttvaṃ māṃ parityaktumarhasi||21||

22vyuṣṭireṣā parā strīṇāṃ pūrvaṃ bhartuḥ parāṃ gatim
|
gantuṃ brahmansaputrāṇāmiti dharmavido viduḥ||22||

23parityaktaḥ sutaścāyaṃ duhiteyaṃ tathā mayā
|
bāndhavāśca parityaktāstvadarthaṃ jīvitaṃ ca me||23||

24yajñaistapobhirniyamairdānaiśca vividhaistathā
|
viśiṣyate striyā bharturnityaṃ priyahite sthitiḥ||24||

25tadidaṃ yaccikīrṣāmi dharmaṃ paramasaṃmatam
|
iṣṭaṃ caiva hitaṃ caiva tava caiva kulasya ca||25||

26iṣṭāni cāpyapatyāni dravyāṇi suhṛdaḥ priyāḥ
|
āpaddharmapramokṣāya bhāryā cāpi satāṃ matam||26||

27āpadarthe dhanaṃ rakṣeddārānrakṣeddhanairapi
|
ātmānaṃ satataṃ rakṣeddārairapi dhanairapi||27||

28dṛṣṭādṛṣṭaphalārthaṃ hi bhāryā putro dhanaṃ gṛham
|
sarvametadvidhātavyaṃ budhānāmeṣa niścayaḥ||28||

29ekato vā kulaṃ kṛtsnamātmā vā kulavardhanaḥ
|
na samaṃ sarvameveti budhānāmeṣa niścayaḥ||29||

30sa kuruṣva mayā kāryaṃ tārayātmānamātmanā
|
anujānīhī māmārya sutau me paripālaya||30||

31avadhyāṃ striyamityāhurdharmajñā dharmaniścaye
|
dharmajñānrākṣasānāhurna hanyātsa ca māmapi||31||

32niḥsaṃśayaṃ vadhaḥ puṃsāṃ strīṇāṃ saṃśayito vadhaḥ
|
ato māmeva dharmajña prasthāpayitumarhasi||32||

33bhuktaṃ priyāṇyavāptāni dharmaśca carito mayā
|
tvatprasūtiḥ priyā prāptā na māṃ tapsyatyajīvitam||33||

34jātaputrā ca vṛddhā ca priyakāmā ca te sadā
|
samīkṣyaitadahaṃ sarvaṃ vyavasāyaṃ karomyataḥ||34||

35utsṛjyāpi hi māmārya prāpsyasyanyāmapi striyam
|
tataḥ pratiṣṭhito dharmo bhaviṣyati punastava||35||

36na cāpyadharmaḥ kalyāṇa bahupatnīkatāṃ nṛṇām
|
strīṇāmadharmaḥ sumahānbhartuḥ pūrvasya laṅghane||36||

37etatsarvaṃ samīkṣya tvamātmatyāgaṃ ca garhitam
|
ātmānaṃ tārayādyāśu kulaṃ cemau ca dārakau||37||

38vaiśaṃpāyana uvāca|
evamuktastayā bhartā tāṃ samāliṅgya bhārata
|
mumoca bāṣpaṃ śanakaiḥ sabhāryo bhṛśaduḥkhitaḥ||38||
