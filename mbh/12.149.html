Mahābhārata 12.149 (Śāntiparvan)

1bhīṣma uvāca|
vimānasthau tu tau rājan lubdhakaḥ khe dadarśa ha
|
dṛṣṭvā tau dampatī rājan vyacintayata tāṃ gatim ||1||

2īdṛśenaiva tapasā gaccheyaṃ paramāṃ gatim
|
iti buddhyā viniścitya gamanāyopacakrame ||2||

3mahāprasthānamāśritya lubdhakaḥ pakṣijīvakaḥ
|
niśceṣṭo marudāhāro nirmamaḥ svargakāṃkṣayā ||3||

4tato'paśyatsuvistīrṇaṃ hṛdyaṃ padmābhibhūṣitam
|
nānāpakṣigaṇākīrṇaṃ saraḥ śītajalaṃ śivam ||4||

5pipāsārto'pi taddṛṣṭvā tṛptaḥ syānnātra saṃśayaḥ
|
upavāsakṛśo'tyarthaṃ sa tu pārthivalubdhakaḥ ||5||

6anavekṣyaiva saṃhṛṣṭaḥ śvāpadādhyuṣitaṃ vanam
|
mahāntaṃ niścayaṃ kṛtvā lubdhakaḥ praviveśa ha ||6||

7praviśanneva sa vanaṃ nigṛhītaḥ sa kaṇṭakaiḥ
|
sa kaṇṭakairvibhinnāṅgo lohitārdrīkṛtacchaviḥ ||7||

8babhrāma tasminvijane nānāmṛgasamākule
|
tato drumāṇāṃ mahatā pavanena vane tadā ||8||

9udatiṣṭhata saṃgharṣātsumahān havyavāhanaḥ
|
tadvanaṃ vṛkṣasampūrṇaṃ latāviṭapasaṃkulam ||9||

10dadāha pāvakaḥ kruddho yugāntāgnisamaprabhaḥ
|
sa jvālaiḥ pavanodbhūtairvisphuliṅgaiḥ samantataḥ ||10||

11dadāha tadvanaṃ ghoraṃ mṛgapakṣisamākulam
|
tataḥ sa dehamokṣārthaṃ samprahṛṣṭena cetasā ||11||

12abhyadhāvata vardhantaṃ pāvakaṃ lubdhakastadā
|
tatastenāgninā dagdho lubdhako naṣṭakalmaṣaḥ
|
jagāma paramāṃ siddhiṃ tato bharatasattama ||12||

13tataḥ svargasthamātmānamapaśyadvigatajvaraḥ
|
yakṣagandharvasiddhānāṃ madhye bhrājantamindravat ||13||

14evaṃ khalu kapotaśca kapotī ca pativratā
|
lubdhakena saha svargaṃ gatāḥ puṇyena karmaṇā ||14||

15yāpi caivaṃvidhā nārī bhartāramanuvartate
|
virājate hi sā kṣipraṃ kapotīva divisthitā ||15||

16evametatpurāvṛttaṃ lubdhakasya mahātmanaḥ
|
kapotasya ca dharmiṣṭhā gatiḥ puṇyena karmaṇā ||16||

17yaścedaṃ śṛṇuyānnityaṃ yaścedaṃ parikīrtayet
|
nāśubhaṃ vidyate tasya manasāpi pramādataḥ ||17||

18yudhiṣṭhira mahāneṣa dharmo dharmabhṛtāṃ vara
|
goghreṣvapi bhavedasminiṣkṛtiḥ pāpakarmaṇaḥ ||18||

19na niṣkṛtirbhavet tasya yo hanyāccharaṇāgatam
|
itihāsamimaṃ śrutvā puṇyaṃ pāpapraṇāśanam
|
na durgatimavāpnoti svargalokaṃ ca gacchati ||19||
