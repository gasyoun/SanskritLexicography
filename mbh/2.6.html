Mahābhārata 2.6 (Sabhāparvan)

1vaiśamāpāyana uvāca|
sampūjyāthābhyanujñāto maharṣervacanātparam
|
pratyuvācānupūrvyeṇa dharmarājo yudhiṣṭhiraḥ||1||

2yudhiṣṭhira uvāca|
bhagavannyāyyamāhaitaṃ yathāvaddharmaniścayam
|
yathāśakti yathānyāyaṃ kriyate'yaṃ vidhirmayā||2||

3rājabhiryadyathā kāryaṃ purā vai tanna saṃśayaḥ
|
yathānyāyopanītārthaṃ kṛtaṃ hetumadarthavat||3||

4vayaṃ tu satpathaṃ teṣāṃ yātumicchāmahe prabho
|
na tu śakyaṃ tathā gantuṃ yathā tairniyatātmabhiḥ||4||

5vaiśampāyana uvāca|
ekamuktvā sa dharmātmā vākyaṃ tadabhipūjya ca
|
muhūrtātprāptakālaṃ ca dṛṣṭvā lokacaraṃ munim||5||

6nāradaṃ susthamāsīnamupāsīno yudhiṣṭhiraḥ
|
apṛcchatpāṇḍavastatra rājamadhye māhadyutiḥ||6||

7yudhiṣṭhira uvāca|
bhavānsañcarate lokānsadā nānāvidhānbahūn
|
brahmaṇā nirmitānpūrvaṃ prekṣamāṇo manojavaḥ||7||

8īdṛśī bhavitā kāciddṛṣṭapūrvā sabhā kvacit
|
ito vā śreyasī brahmaṃstanmamācakṣva pṛcchataḥ||8||

9vaiśampāyana uvāca|
tacchrutvā nāradastasya dharmarājasya bhāṣitam
|
pāṇḍavaṃ pratyuvācedaṃ smayanmadhurayā girā||9||

10nārada uvāca|
mānuṣeṣu na me tāta dṛṣṭapūrvā na ca śrutā
|
sabhā maṇimayī rājanyatheyaṃ tava bhārata||10||

11sabhāṃ tu pitṛrājasya varuṇasya ca dhīmataḥ
|
kathayiṣye tathendrasya kailāsanilayasya ca||11||

12brahmaṇaśca sabhāṃ divyāṃ kathayiṣye gataklamām
|
divyādivyairabhiprāyairupetāṃ viśvarūpiṇīm||12||

13devaiḥ pitṛgaṇaiḥ sādhyairyajvabhirniyatātmabhiḥ
|
juṣṭāṃ munigaṇaiḥ śāntairvedayajñaiḥ sadakṣiṇaiḥ
|
yadi te śravaṇe buddhirvartate bharatarṣabha||13||

14nāradenaivamuktastu dharmarājo yudhiṣṭhiraḥ
|
prāñjalirbhrātṛbhiḥ sārdhaṃ taiśca sarvairdvijottamaiḥ||14||

15nāradaṃ pratyuvācedaṃ dharmarājo mahāmanāḥ
|
sabhāḥ kathaya tāḥ sarvāḥ śrotumicchāmahe vayam||15||

16kiṃdravyāstāḥ sabhā brahmankiṃvistārāḥ kimāyatāḥ
|
pitāmahaṃ ca ke tasyāṃ sabhāyāṃ paryupāsate||16||

17vāsavaṃ devarājaṃ ca yamaṃ vaivasvataṃ ca ke
|
varuṇaṃ ca kuberaṃ ca sabhāyāṃ paryupāsate||17||

18etatsarvaṃ yathānyāyaṃ brahmarṣe vadatastava
|
śrotumicchāma sahitāḥ paraṃ kautūhalaṃ hi naḥ||18||

19evamuktaḥ pāṇḍavena nāradaḥ pratyabhāṣata
|
krameṇa rājandivyāstāḥ śrūyantāmiha naḥ sabhāḥ||19||
