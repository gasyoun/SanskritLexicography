Mahābhārata 6.45 (Bhīṣmaparvan)

1sañjaya uvāca|pūrvāhṇe tasya raudrasya yuddhamahno viśāmpate
|
prāvartata mahāghoraṃ rājñāṃ dehāvakartanam ||1||

2kurūṇāṃ sṛñjayānāṃ ca jigīṣūṇāṃ parasparam
|
siṃhānāmiva saṃhrādo divamurvīṃ ca nādayan ||2||

3āsītkilakilāśabdastalaśaṅkharavaiḥ saha
|
jajñire siṃhanādāśca śūrāṇāṃ pratigarjatām ||3||

4talatrābhihatāścaiva jyāśabdā bharatarṣabha
|
pattīnāṃ pādaśabdaśca vājināṃ ca mahāsvanaḥ ||4||

5totrāṃkuśanipātaśca āyudhānāṃ ca niḥsvanaḥ
|
ghaṇṭāśabdaśca nāgānāmanyonyamabhidhāvatām ||5||

6tasmin samudite śabde tumule lomaharṣaṇe
|
babhūva rathanirghoṣaḥ parjanyaninadopamaḥ ||6||

7te manaḥ kūramādhāya samabhityaktajīvitāḥ
|
pāṇḍavānabhyavartanta sarva evocchritadhvajāḥ ||7||

8atha śāntanavo rājannabhyadhāvaddhanañjayam
|
pragṛhya kārmukaṃ ghoraṃ kāladaṇḍopamaṃ raṇe ||8||

9arjuno'pi dhanurgṛhya gāṇḍīvaṃ lokaviśrutam
|
abhyadhāvata tejasvī gāṅgeyaṃ raṇamūrdhani ||9||

10tāvubhau kuruśārdūlau parasparavadhaiṣiṇau
|
gāṅgeyastu raṇe pārthaṃ viddhvā nākampayadbalī ||10||

11tathaiva pāṇḍavo rājan bhīṣmaṃ nākampayadyudhi
|
sātyakistu maheṣvāsaḥ kṛtavarmāṇamabhyayāt ||11||

12tayoḥ samabhavadyuddhaṃ tumulaṃ lomaharṣaṇam
|
sātyakiḥ kṛtavarmāṇaṃ kṛtavarmā ca sātyakim ||12||

13ānarcchatuḥ śarairghoraistakṣamāṇau parasparam
|
tau śarācitasarvāṅgau śuśubhāte mahābalau ||13||

14vasante puṣpaśabalau puṣpitāviva kiṃśukau
|
abhimanyurmaheṣvāsaṃ bṛhadbalamayodhayat ||14||

15tataḥ kosalarājā'sāvabhimanyorviśāmpate
|
dhvajaṃ ciccheda samare sārathiṃ ca nyapātayat ||15||

16saubhadrastu tataḥ kruddhaḥ pātite rathasārathau
|
bṛhadbalaṃ mahārāja vivyādha navabhiḥ śaraiḥ ||16||

17athāparābhyāṃ bhallābhyāṃ śitābhyāmarimardanaḥ
|
dhvajamekena ciccheda pārṣṇimekena sārathim ||17||

18anyonyaṃ ca śaraiḥ kruddhau tatakṣāte parasparam
|
māninaṃ samare dṛptaṃ kṛtavairaṃ mahāratham ||18||

19bhīmasenastava sutaṃ duryodhanamayodhayat
|
tāvubhau naraśārdūlau kurumukhyau mahābalau ||19||

20anyonyaṃ śaravarṣābhyāṃ vavṛṣāte raṇājire
|
tau vīkṣya tu mahātmānau kṛtinau citrayodhinau ||20||

21vismayaḥ sarvabhūtānāṃ samapadyata bhārata
|
duḥśāsanastu nakulaṃ pratyudyāya mahābalam ||21||

22avidhyanniśitairbāṇairbahubhirmarmabhedibhiḥ
|
tasya mādrīsutaḥ ketuṃ saśaraṃ ca śarāsanam ||22||

23ciccheda niśitairbāṇaiḥ prahasanniva bhārata
|
athainaṃ pañcaviṃśatyā kṣudrakāṇāṃ samārpayat ||23||

24putrastu tava durdharṣo nakulasya mahāhave
|
turaṅgāṃścicchide bāṇairdhvajaṃ caivābhyapātayat ||24||

25durmukhaḥ sahadevaṃ ca pratyudyāya mahābalam
|
vivyādha śaravarṣeṇa yatamānaṃ mahāhave ||25||

26sahadevastato vīro durmukhasya mahāraṇe
|
śareṇa bhṛśatīkṣṇena pātayāmāsa sārathim ||26||

27tāvanyonyaṃ samāsādya samare yuddhadurmadau
|
trāsayetāṃ śarairghoraiḥ kṛtapratikṛtaiṣiṇau ||27||

28yudhiṣṭhiraḥ svayaṃ rājā madrarājānamabhyayāt
|
tasya madrādhipaścāpaṃ dvidhā ciccheda māriṣa ||28||

29tadapāsya dhanuścchinnaṃ kuntīputro yudhiṣṭhiraḥ
|
anyatkārmukamādāya vegavadbalavattaram ||29||

30tato madreśvaraṃ rājā śaraiḥ sannataparvabhiḥ
|
chādayāmāsa saṃkruddhastiṣṭha tiṣṭheti cābravīt ||30||

31dhṛṣṭadyumnastato droṇamabhyadravata bhārata
|
tasya droṇaḥ susaṃkruddhaḥ parāsukaraṇaṃ dṛḍham ||31||

32tridhā ciccheda samare pāñcālyasya tu kārmukam
|
śaraṃ caiva mahāghoraṃ kāladaṇḍamivāparam ||32||

33preṣayāmāsa samare so'sya kāye nyamajjata
|
athānyaddhanurādāya sāyakāṃśca caturdaśa ||33||

34droṇaṃ drupadaputrastu prativivyādha saṃyuge
|
tāvanyonyaṃ susaṃkruddhau cakratuḥ subhṛśaṃ raṇam ||34||

35saumadattiṃ raṇe śaṅkho rabhasaṃ rabhaso yudhi
|
pratyudyayau mahārāja tiṣṭhatiṣṭheti cābravīt ||35||

36tasya vai dakṣiṇaṃ vīro nirbibheda raṇe bhujam
|
saumadattistathā śaṅkhaṃ jatrudeśe samāhanat ||36||

37tayostadabhavadyuddhaṃ ghorarūpaṃ viśāṃpate
|
dṛptayoḥ samare pūrvaṃ vṛtravāsavayoriva ||37||

38bāhlīkaṃ tu raṇe kruddhaṃ kruddharūpo viśāṃpate
|
abhyadravadameyātmā dhṛṣṭaketurmahārathaḥ ||38||

39bāhlīkastu raṇe rājan dhṛṣṭaketumamarṣaṇaḥ
|
śarairbahubhirānarcchat siṃhanādamathānadat ||39||

40cedirājastu saṃkruddho bāhlīkaṃ navabhiḥ śaraiḥ
|
vivyādha samare tūrṇaṃ matto mattamiva dvipam ||40||

41tau tatra samare kruddhau nardantau ca punaḥ punaḥ
|
samīyatuḥ susaṃkruddhāvaṅgārakabudhāviva ||41||

42rākṣasaṃ raudrakarmāṇaṃ krūrakarmā ghaṭotkacaḥ
|
alambuṣaṃ pratyudiyādbalaṃ śakra ivāhave ||42||

43ghaṭotkacastataḥ kruddho rākṣasaṃ taṃ mahābalam
|
navatyā sāyakaistīkṣṇairdārayāmāsa bhārata ||43||

44alambuṣastu samare bhaimaseniṃ mahābalam
|
bahudhā dārayāmāsa śaraiḥ sannataparvabhiḥ ||44||

45vyabhrājetāṃ tatastau tu saṃyuge śaravikṣatau
|
yathā devāsure yuddhe balaśakrau mahābalau ||45||

46śikhaṇḍī samare rājan drauṇimabhyudyayau balī
|
aśvatthāmā tataḥ kruddhaḥ śikhaṇḍinamupasthitam ||46||

47nārācena sutīkṣṇena bhṛśaṃ viddhvā hyakampayat
|
śikhaṇḍyapi tato rājan droṇaputramatāḍayat ||47||

48sāyakena supītena tīkṣṇena niśitena ca
|
tau jaghnutustadā'nyonyaṃ śarairbahurvidhairmṛdhe ||48||

49bhagadattaṃ raṇe śūraṃ virāṭo vāhinīpatiḥ
|
abhyayāttvarito rājaṃstato yuddhamavartata ||49||

50virāṭo bhagadattaṃ tu śaravarṣeṇa bhārata
|
abhyavarṣatsusaṃkruddho megho vṛṣṭyā ivācalam ||50||

51bhagadattastatastūrṇaṃ virāṭaṃ pṛthivīpatim
|
chādayāmāsa samare meghaḥ sūryamivoditam ||51||

52bṛhatkṣatraṃ tu kaikeyaṃ kṛpaḥ śāradvato yayau
|
taṃ kṛpaḥ śaravarṣeṇa cchādayāmāsa bhārata ||52||

53gautamaṃ kaikayaḥ kuddhaḥ śaravṛṣṭyā'bhyapūrayat
|
tāvanyonyaṃ hayān hatvā dhanuśchitvā ca bhārata ||53||

54virathāvasiyuddhāya samīyaturamarṣaṇau
|
tayostadabhavadyuddhaṃ ghorarūpaṃ sudāruṇam ||54||

55drupadastu tato rājan saindhavaṃ ve jayadratham
|
abhyudyayau hṛṣṭarūpo hṛṣṭarūpaṃ parantapaḥ ||55||

56tataḥ saindhavako rājā drupadaṃ viśikhaistribhiḥ
|
tāḍayāmāsa samare sa ca taṃ pratyavidhyata ||56||

57tayostadabhavadyuddhaṃ ghorarūpaṃ sudāruṇam
|
īkṣaṇaprītijananaṃ śukrāṅgārakayoriva ||57||

58vikarṇastu sutastubhyaṃ sutasomaṃ mahābalam
|
abhyayājjavanairaśvaistato yuddhamavartata ||58||

59vikarṇaḥ sutasomaṃ tu viddhvā nākampayaccharaiḥ
|
sutasomo vikarṇaṃ ca tada‌dbhutamivābhavat ||59||

60suśarmāṇaṃ naravyāghraścekitāno mahārathaḥ
|
abhyadravatsusaṃkuddhaḥ pāṇḍavārthe parākramī ||60||

61suśarmā tu mahārāja cekitānaṃ mahāratham
|
mahatā śaravarṣeṇa vārayāmāsa saṃyuge ||61||

62cekitāno'pi saṃrabdhaḥ suśarmāṇaṃ mahāhave
|
prācchādayattamiṣubhirmahāmegha ivācalam ||62||

63śakuniḥ prativindhyaṃ tu parākrāntaṃ parākramī
|
abhyadravata rājendra mattaḥ siṃha iva dvipam ||63||

64yaudhiṣṭhirastu saṃkruddhaḥ saubalaṃ niśitaiḥ śaraiḥ
|
vyadārayata saṅgrāme maghavāniva dānavam ||64||

65śakuniḥ prativindhyaṃ tu pratividhyantamāhave
|
vyadārayanmahāprājñaḥ śaraiḥ sannataparvabhiḥ ||65||

66sudakṣiṇaṃ tu rājendra kāmbojānāṃ mahāratham
|
śrutakarmā parākrāntamabhyadravata saṃyuge ||66||

67sudakṣiṇastu samare sāhadeviṃ mahāratham
|
viddhvā nākampayata vai mainākamiva parvatam ||67||

68śrutakarmā tataḥ kuddhaḥ kāmbojānāṃ mahāratham
|
śarairbahubhirānarcchaddārayanniva sarvaśaḥ ||68||

69irāvānatha saṃkruddhaḥ śrutāyuṣamarindamam
|
pratyudyayau raṇe yatto yattarūpaṃ parantapaḥ ||69||

70ārjunistasya samare hayān hatvā mahārathaḥ
|
nanāda balavannādaṃ tatsainyaṃ pratyapūrayat ||70||

71śrutāyustu tataḥ kruddhaḥ phālguneḥ samare hayān
|
nijaghāna gadāgreṇa tato yuddhamavartata ||71||

72vindānuvindāvāvantyau kuntibhojaṃ mahāratham
|
sasenaṃ sasutaṃ vīraṃ saṃsasajjaturāhave ||72||

73tatrādbhutamapaśyāma tayorghoraṃ parākramam
|
ayudhyetāṃ sthirau bhūtvā mahatyā senayā saha ||73||

74anuvindastu gadayā kuntibhojamatāḍayat
|
kuntibhojaśca taṃ tūrṇaṃ śaravrātairavākirat ||74||

75kuntibhojasutaścāpi vindaṃ vivyādha sākaiḥ
|
sa ca taṃ prativivyādha tada‌dbhutamivābhavat ||75||

76kekayā bhrātaraḥ pañca gāndhārānpañca māriṣa
|
sasainyāste sasainyāṃśca yodhayāmāsurāhave ||76||

77vīrabāhuśca te putro vairāṭiṃ rathasattamam
|
uttaraṃ yodhayāmāsa vivyādha niśitaiḥ śaraiḥ ||77||

78uttaraścāpi taṃ vīraṃ vivyādha niśitaiḥ śaraiḥ
|
cedirāṭ samare rājannulūkaṃ samabhidravat ||78||

79tathaiva śaravarṣeṇa ulūkaṃ samaviddhyata
|
ulūkaścāpi taṃ bāṇairniśitairlomavāhibhiḥ ||79||

80tayoryuddhaṃ samabhavaddhorarūpaṃ viśāṃpate
|
dārayetāṃ susaṃkruddhāvanyonyamaparājitau ||80||

81evaṃ dvandvasahasrāṇi rathavāraṇavājinām
|
padātīnāṃ ca samare tava teṣāṃ ca saṃkule ||81||

82muhūrtamiva tadyuddhamāsīnmadhuradarśanam
|
tata unmattavadrājanna prājñāyata kiñcana ||82||

83gajo gajena samare rathinaṃ ca rathī yayau
|
aśvo'śvaṃ samabhiprāyātpadātiśca padātinam ||83||

84tato yuddhaṃ sudurdharṣaṃ vyākulaṃ samapadyata
|
śūrāṇāṃ samare tatra samāsādyetaretaram ||84||

85tatra devarṣayaḥ siddhāścāraṇāśca samāgatāḥ
|
praikṣanta tadraṇaṃ ghoraṃ devāsurasamaṃ bhuvi ||85||

86tato dantisahasrāṇi rathānāṃ cāpi māriṣa
|
aśvaughāḥ puruṣaughāśca viparītaṃ samāyayuḥ ||86||

87tatra tatra pradṛśyante rathavāraṇapattayaḥ
|
sādinaśca nakhyāghra yudhyamānā muhurmuhuḥ ||87||
