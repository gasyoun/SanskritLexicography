Mahābhārata 1.94 (Ādiparvan)

1janamejaya uvāca|
bhagavañchrotumicchāmi purorvaṃśakarānnṛpān
|
yadvīryānyādṛśāṃścāpi yāvato yatparākramān||1||

2na hyasminśīlahīno vā nirvīryo vā narādhipaḥ
|
prajāvirahito vā'pi bhūtapūrvaḥ kathaṃcana||2||

3teṣāṃ prathitavṛttānāṃ rājñāṃ vijñānaśālinām
|
caritaṃ śrotumicchami vistareṇa tapodhana||3||

4vaiśaṃpāyana uvāca|
hanta te kathayiṣyāmi yanmāṃ tvaṃ paripṛcchasi
|
purorvaṃśadharānvīrāñcchakrapratimatejasaḥ
|
bhūridraviṇavikrāntānsarvalakṣaṇapūjitān||4||

5pravīreśvararaudrāśvāstrayaḥ putrā mahārathāḥ
|
pūroḥ pauṣṭyāmajāyanta pravīro vaṃśakṛttataḥ||5||

6manasyurabhavattasmācchūrasenīsutaḥ prabhuḥ
|
pṛthivyāścaturantāyā goptā rājīvalocanaḥ||6||

7śaktaḥ saṃhanano vāgmī sauvīrītanayāstrayaḥ
|
manasyorabhavanputrāḥ śūrāḥ sarve mahārathāḥ||7||

8anvagbhānuprabhṛtayo miśrakeśyāṃ manasvinaḥ
|
raudrāśvasya maheṣvāsā daśāpsarasi sūnavaḥ||8||

9yajvāno jajñire śūrāḥ prajāvanto bahuśrutāḥ
|
sarve sarvāstravidvāsaḥ sarve dharmaparāyaṇāḥ||9||

10ṛceyuratha kakṣeyuḥ kṛkaṇeyuśca vīryavān
|
sthaṇḍileyurvaneyuśca jaleyuśca mahāyaśāḥ||10||

11tejeyurbalāvāndhīmānsatyeyuścendravikramaḥ
|
dharmeyuḥ sannateyuśca daśamo devavikramaḥ||11||

12anādhṛṣṭirabhūtteṣāṃ vidvānbhuvi tathaikarāṭ
|
ṛceyuratha vikrānto devānāmiva vāsavaḥ||12||

13anādhṛṣṭisutastvāsīdrājasūyāśvamedhakṛt
|
matināra iti khyāto rājā paramadhārmikaḥ||13||

14matinārasutā rājaṃścatvāro'mitavikramāḥ
|
taṃsurmahānatiratho druhyuścāpratimadyutiḥ||14||

15teṣāṃ taṃsurmahāvīryaḥ pauravaṃ vaṃśamudvahan
|
ājahāra yaśo dīptaṃ jigāya ca vasuṃdharām||15||

16īlinaṃ tu sutaṃ taṃsurjanayāmāsa vīryavān
|
so'pi kṛtsnāmimāṃ bhūmiṃ vijigye jayatāṃ varaḥ||16||

17rathantaryāṃ sutānpañca pañcabhūtopamāṃstataḥ
|
īlino janayāmāsa duṣyantaprabhṛtīnnṛpān||17||

18duṣyantaṃ śūrabhīmau ca pravasuṃ vasumeva ca
|
teṣāṃ śreṣṭho'bhavadrājā duṣyanto janamejaya||18||

19duṣyantādbharato jajñe vidvānśākuntalo nṛpaḥ
|
tasmādbharatavaṃśasya vipratasthe mahadyaśaḥ||19||

20bharatastisṛṣu strīṣu nava putrānajījanat
|
nābhyanandata tānrājā nānurūpā mametyuta ||20||

21tatastānmātaraḥ kuddhāḥ putrānninyuryamakṣayam
|
tatastasya narendrasya vitathaṃ putrajanma tat ||21||

22tato mahadbhiḥ kratubhirījāno bharatastadā
|
lebhe putraṃ bharadvājādbhumanyuṃ nāma bhārata ||22||

23tataḥ putriṇamātmānaṃ jñātvā pauravanandanaḥ
|
bhumanyuṃ bharataśreṣṭha yauvarājye'bhyaṣecayat || 23||

24tato diviratho nāma bhumanyorabhavatsutaḥ
|
suhotraśca suhotā ca suhaviḥ suyajustathā ||24||

25puṣkariṇyāmṛcīkaśca bhumanyorabhayansutāḥ
|
teṣāṃ jyeṣṭhaḥ suhotrastu rājyamāpa mahīkṣitām||25||

26rājasūyāśvamedhādyaiḥ soyajadbahubhiḥ savaiḥ
|
sṛhotraḥ pṛthivīṃ kṛtsnāṃ bubhuje sāgarāmbarām|| 26||

27pūrṇāṃ hastigajāśvaiśca bahurakṣasamākulām
|
mamajeva mahī tasya bhūribhārāvapīḍitā ||27||

28hastyaśvarathasaṃpūrṇā manuṣyakalilā bhṛśam
|
suhotre rājani tadā dharmataḥ śāsati prajāḥ || 28 ||

29caityayūpāṅkitā cāsīdbhūmiḥ śatasahasraśaḥ
|
pravṛddhajanasasyā ca sarvadaiva vyarocata ||29||

30pekṣvākī janayāmāsa suhotrātpṛthivīpateḥ
|
ajamīḍhaṃ sumīḍhaṃ ca purumīḍhaṃ ca bhārata ||30||

31ajamīḍho varasteṣāṃ tasminvaṃśaḥ pratiṣṭhitaḥ
|
ṣaṭ‌putrānsopyajanayattisṛṣu strīṣu bhārata|| 31 ||

32ṛkṣaṃ dhūminyatho nīlī duṣyantaparameṣṭhinau
|
keśinyajanayajjahnuṃ sutau vrajanarūpiṇau||32||

33tatheme sarvapañcālā duṣyantaparameṣṭhinoḥ
|
anvayāḥ kuśikā rājañjahnoramitatejasaḥ||33||

34vrajanarūpiṇayorjyeṣṭhamṛkṣamāhurjanādhipam
|
ṛkṣātsaṃvaraṇo jajñe rājanvaṃśakaraḥ sutaḥ||34||

35ārkṣe saṃvaraṇe rājanpraśāsati vasuṃdharām
|
saṃkṣayaḥ sumahānāsītprajānāmiti naḥ śrutam ||35||

36vyaśīryata tato rāṣṭraṃ kṣayairnānāvidhaistadā
|
kṣunmṛtyubhyāmanāvṛṣṭyā vyādhibhiśca samāhatam ||36||

37abhyaghnan‌ bhāratāṃścaiva sapatnānāṃ balāni ca
|
cālayanvasudhāṃ cemāṃ balena caturaṅgiṇā ||37||

38abhyayāptaṃ ca pāñcālyo vijitya tarasā mahīm
|
akṣauhiṇībhirdaśabhiḥ sa enaṃ samare'jayat ||38||

39tataḥ sadāraḥ sāmātyaḥ saputraḥ sasuhṛjjanaḥ
|
rājā saṃvaraṇastasmātpalāyata mahābhayāt|| 39 ||

40sindhornadasya mahato nikuñje nyaghasattadā
|
nadīviṣayaparyante parvatasya samīpataḥ ||40||

41tatrāvasanbahūnkālānbhāratā durgamāśritāḥ
|
teṣāṃ nivasatāṃ tatra sahasraṃ parivatsarān|| 41 ||

42athābhyagacchadbharatānvasiṣṭho bhagavānṛṣiḥ
|
tamāgataṃ prayatnena pratyudgamyābhivādya ca ||42||

43arghyamanyāharaṃstasmai te sarve bhāratāstadā
|
nivedya sarvamṛṣaye satkāreṇa suvacase||43||

44tamāsane copaviṣṭaṃ rājā vavre svayaṃ tadā
|
purohito bhavānno'stu rājāya prayatemahi || 44 ||

45omityevaṃ vasiṣṭho'pi bhāratānpratyapadyata
|
athābhyaṣiñcatsāmrājye sarvakṣatrasya pauravam || 45 ||

46viṣāṇabhūtaṃ sarvasyāṃ pṛthivyāmiti naḥ śrutam
|
bharatādhyuṣitaṃ pūrvaṃ so'dhyatiṣṭhatpurottamam || 46 ||

47punarbalibhṛtaścaiva cakre sarvamahīkṣitaḥ
|
tataḥ sa pṛthivīṃ prāpya punarīje mahābalaḥ || 47||

48ājamīḍho mahāyajñairbahubhirbhūridakṣiṇaiḥ
|
tataḥ saṃvaraṇātsaurī tapatī suṣuve kurum || 48||

49rājatve taṃ prajāḥ sarvā dharmajña iti vavrire
|
tasya nāmnā'bhivikhyātaṃ pṛthivyāṃ kurujāṅgalam ||49||

50kurukṣetraṃ sa tapasā puṇyaṃ cakre mahātapāḥ
|
aśvavantamabhiṣyantaṃ tathā caitrarathaṃ munim ||50||

51janamejayaṃ ca vikhyātaṃ putrāṃścāsyānuśuśrumaḥ
|
pañcaitānvāhinīputrānvyajāyata manasvinī ||51||

52avikṣitaḥ parikṣittu śabalāśvastu vīryavān
|
ādirājo virājaśca śālmaliśca mahābalaḥ||52||

53uccaiḥśravā bhaṅgakāro jitāriścāṣṭamaḥ smṛtaḥ
|
eteṣāmanvavāye tu khyātāste karmajairguṇaiḥ
|
janamejayādayaḥ sapta tathaivānye mahārathāḥ ||53||

54parīkṣito'bhavanputrāḥ sarve dharmārthakovidāḥ
|
kakṣaseno'grasenau tu citrasenaśca vīryavān ||54||

55indrasenaḥ suṣeṇaśca bhīmasenaśca nāmataḥ
|
janamejayasya tanayā bhuvi khyātā mahābalāḥ||55||

56dhṛtarāṣṭraḥ prathamajaḥ pāṇḍurbāhlīka eva ca
|
niṣadhaśca mahātejāstathā jāmbūnado balī||56||

57kuṇḍodaraḥ padātiśca vasātiścāṣṭamaḥ smṛtaḥ
|
sarve dharmārthakuśalāḥ sarvabhūtahite ratāḥ ||57||

58dhṛtarāṣṭro'tha rājāsīttasya putro'tha kuṇḍikaḥ
|
hastī vitarkaḥ krāthaśca kuṇḍinaścāpiḥ pañcamaḥ||58||

59haviḥ śravāstathendrābho bhumanyuścāparājitaḥ
|
dhṛtarāṣṭrasutānāṃ tu trīnetānprathitānbhuvi || 59||

60pratīpaṃ dharmanetraṃ ca sunetraṃ cāpi bhārata
|
pratīpaḥ prathitasteṣāṃ babhūvāpratimo bhuvi||60||

61pratīpasya trayaḥ putrā jajñire bharatarṣabha
|
devāpiḥ śāntanuścaiva bāhlīkaśca mahārathaḥ || 61 ||

62devāpiśca pravavrāja teṣāṃ dharmahitepsayā
|
śāntanuśca mahīṃ lebhe bāhrīkañca mahārathaḥ||62||

63bharatasyānvaye jātāḥ satvavanto narādhipāḥ
|
devarṣikalpā nṛpate bahavo rājasattamāḥ ||63||

64evaṃvidhāścāpyapare devakalpā mahārathāḥ
|
jātā manoranvavāye ailavaṃśavivardhanāḥ ||64||
