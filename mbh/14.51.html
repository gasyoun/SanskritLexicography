Mahābhārata 14.51 (Āśvamedhikaparvan)

1brahmovāca|
bhūnānāmatha pañcānāṃ yathaiṣāmīśvaraṃ manaḥ
|
niyame ca visarge ca bhūtātmā mana eva ca||1||

2adhiṣṭhātā mano nityaṃ bhūtānāṃ mahatāṃ tathā
|
buddhiraiścaryamācaṣṭe kṣetrajñaśca sa ucyate||2||

3indriyāṇi mano yuṅkte sadaśvāniva sārathiḥ
|
indriyāṇi mano buddhiḥ kṣetrajñe yujyate sadā||3||

4mahadaśvasamāyuktaṃ buddhisaṃyamanaṃ ratham
|
samāruhya sa bhūtātmā samantāt paridhāvati||4||

5indriyagrāmasaṃyukto manaḥsārathireva ca
|
buddhisaṃyamano nityaṃ mahān brahmamayo sthaḥ||5||

6evaṃ yo vetti vidvānvai sadā brahmamayaṃ ratham
|
sa dhīraḥ sarvabhūteṣu na mohamadhigacchati||6||

7avyaktādi viśeṣāntaṃ saha sthāvarajaṅgamam
|
sūryacandraprabhālokaṃ grahanakṣatramaṇḍitam||7||

8nadīparvatajālaiśca sarvataḥ paribhūṣitam
|
vividhābhistathā cādbhiḥ satataṃ samalaṅkṛtam||8||

9ājīvaṃ sarvabhūtānāṃ sarvaprāṇabhṛtāṃ gatiḥ
|
etadbrahmavanaṃ nityaṃ tasmiṃścarati kṣetravit||9||

10loke'sminyāni sattvāni trasāni sthāvarāṇi ca
|
tānyevāgre pralīyante paścādbhūtakṛtā guṇāḥ
|
guṇebhyaḥ pañcabhūtāni eṣa bhūtasamucchraya:||10||

11devā manuṣyā gandharvāḥ piśācāsurarākṣasāḥ
|
sarve svabhāvataḥ sṛṣṭā na kriyābhyo na kāraṇāt||11||

12ete viśvasṛjo viprā jāyantīha punaḥ punaḥ
|
tebhyaḥ prasūtāsteṣveva mahābhūteṣu pañcasu
|
pralīyante yathākālamūrmayaḥ sāgare yathā||12||

13viśvasṛgbhyastu bhūtebhyo mahābhūtāstu sarvaśaḥ
|
bhūtebhyaścāpi pañcabhyo mukto gacchetparāṃ gatim||13||

14prajāpatiridaṃ sarvaṃ manasaivāsṛjatprabhuḥ
|
tathaiva devānṛṣayastapasā pratipedire||14||

15tapasaścānupūryeṇa phalamūlāśinastathā
|
trailokyaṃ tapasā siddhāḥ paśyantīha samāhitāḥ||15||

16auṣadhānyagadādīni nānāvidyāśca sarvaśaḥ
|
tapasaiva prasiddhyanti tapomūlaṃ hi sādhanam||16||

17yaddurāpaṃ durāmnāyaṃ durādharṣaṃ duranvayam
|
tatsarvaṃ tapasā sādhyaṃ tapo hi duratikramam||17||

18surāpo brahmahā steyī bhrūṇahā gurutalpagaḥ
|
tapasaiva sutaptena mucyate kilbiṣāttataḥ||18||

19manuṣyāḥ pitaro devāḥ paśavo mṛgapakṣiṇaḥ
|
yāni cānyāni bhūtāni trasāni sthāvarāṇi ca||19||

20tapaḥ parāyaṇā nityaṃ siddhyante tapasā sadā
|
tathaiva tapasā devā mahāmāyā divaṃ gatāḥ||20||

21āśīryuktāni karmāṇi kurvate ye tvatandritāḥ
|
ahaṅkārasamāyuktāste sakāśe prajāpateḥ||21||

22dhyānayogena śuddhena nirmamā nirahaṅkṛtāḥ
|
āpnuvanti mahātmāno mahāntaṃ lokamuttamam||22||

23dhyānayogamupāgamya prasannamatayaḥ sadā
|
sukhopacayamavyaktaṃ praviśantyātmavittamāḥ||23||

24dhyānayogādupāgamya nirmamā nirahaṅkṛtāḥ
|
avyaktaṃ praviśantīha mahatāṃ lokamuttamam||24||

25avyaktādeva sambhūtaḥ samasaṃjñāṃ gataḥ punaḥ
|
tamorajobhyāṃ nirmuktaḥ sattvamāsthāya kevalam||25||

26nirmuktaḥ sarvapāpebhyaḥ sarvaṃ sṛjati niṣkalam
|
kṣetrajña iti taṃ vidyādyastaṃ veda sa vedavit||26||

27cittaṃ cittādupāgamya munirāsīta saṃyataḥ
|
yaccittaṃ tanmayo vaśyaṃ guhyametatsanātanam||27||

28avyaktādiviśeṣāntamavidyālakṣaṇaṃ smṛtam
|
nibodhata tathā hīdaṃ guṇairlakṣaṇamityuta||28||

29dvyakṣarastu bhavenmṛtyustryakṣaraṃ brahma śāśvatam
|
mameti ca bhavenmṛtyurna mameti ca śāśvatam||29||

30karma kecitpraśaṃsanti mandabuddhiratā narāḥ
|
ye tu vṛddhā mahātmāno na praśaṃsanti karma te||30||

31karmaṇā jāyate janturmūrtimānṣoḍaśātmakaḥ
|
puruṣaṃ grasate'vidyā tadgrāhyamamṛtāśinām||31||

32tasmātkarmasu niḥsnehā ye kecitpāradarśinaḥ
|
vidyāmayo'yaṃ puruṣo na tu karmamayaḥ smṛtaḥ||32||

33ya evamamṛtaṃ nityamagrāhyaṃ śaśvadakṣaram
|
vaśyātmānamasaṃśliṣṭaṃ yo veda na mṛto bhavet||33||

34apūrvamakṛtaṃ nityaṃ ya enamavicāriṇam
|
ya evaṃ vindedātmānamagrāhyamamṛtāśanam
|
agrāhyo'mṛto bhavati sa ebhiḥ kāraṇairdhruvaḥ||34||

35āyojya sarvasaṃskārānsaṃyamyātmānamātmani
|
sa tadbrahma śubhaṃ vetti yasmādbhūyo na vidyate||35||

36prasāde caiva sattvasya prasādaṃ samavāpnuyāt
|
lakṣaṇaṃ hi prasādasya yathā syātsvapnadarśanam||36||

37gatireṣā tu muktānāṃ ye jñānapariniṣṭhitāḥ
|
pravṛttayaśca yāḥ sarvāḥ paśyanti pariṇāmajāḥ||37||

38eṣā gatirviraktānāmeṣa dharmaḥ sanātanaḥ
|
eṣā jñānavatāṃ prāptiretadvṛttamaninditam||38||

39samena sarvabhūteṣu niḥspṛheṇa nirāśiṣā
|
śakyā gatiriyaṃ gantuṃ sarvatra samadarśinā||39||

40etadvaḥ sarvamākhyātaṃ mayā viprarṣisattamāḥ
|
evamācarata kṣipraṃ tataḥ siddhimavāpsyatha||40||

41gururuvāca|
ityuktāste tu munayo guruṇā brahmaṇā tathā
|
kṛtavanto mahātmānastato lokamavāpnuvan||41||

42tvamapyetanmahābhāga mayoktaṃ brahmaṇo vacaḥ
|
samyagāācara śuddhātmaṃstataḥ siddhimavāpsyasi||42||

43vāsudeva uvāca|
ityuktaḥ sa tadā śiṣyo guruṇā dharmamuttamam
|
cakāra sarvaṃ kaunteya tato mokṣamavāptavān||43||

44kṛtakṛtyaśca sa tadā śiṣyaḥ kurukulodvaha
|
tatpadaṃ samanuprāpto yatra gatvā na śocati||44||

45arjuna uvāca|
ko nvasau brāhmaṇaḥ kṛṣṇa kaśca śiṣyo janārdana
|
śrotavyaṃ cenmayaitadvai tattvamācakṣva me vibho||45||

46vāsudeva uvāca|
ahaṃ gururmahābāho manaḥ śiṣyaṃ ca viddhi me
|
tvatprītyā guhyametacca kathitaṃ te dhanañjaya||46||

47mayi cedasti te prītirnityaṃ kurukulodvaha
|
adhyātmametacchrutvā tvaṃ samyagācara suvrata||47||

48tatastvaṃ samyagācīrṇaṃ dharme'sminnarikarṣaṇa
|
sarvapāpavinirmukto mokṣaṃ prāpsyasi kevalam||48||

49pūrvamapyetadevoktaṃ yuddhakāla upasthite
|
mayā tava mahābāho tasmādatra manaḥ kuru||49||

50mayā tu bharataśreṣṭha ciradṛṣṭaḥ pitā prabhuḥ
|
tamahaṃ draṣṭumicchāmi sammate tava phālguna||50||

51vaiśampāyana uvāca|
ityuktavacanaṃ kṛṣṇaṃ pratyuvāca dhanañjayaḥ
|
gacchāvo nagaraṃ kṛṣṇa gajasāhvayamadya vai||51||

52sametya tatra rājānaṃ dharmātmānaṃ yudhiṣṭhiram
|
samanujñāpya rājānaṃ svāṃ purīṃ yātumarhasi||52||
