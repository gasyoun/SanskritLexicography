Mahābhārata 7.81 (Droṇaparvan)

1sañjaya uvāca|
tataḥ pārthaḥ prasannātmā prāñjalirvṛṣabhadhvajam
|
dadarśotphullanayanaḥ samastaṃ tejasāṃ nidhim ||1||

2taṃ copahāraṃ sukṛtaṃ naiśaṃ naityakamātmanā
|
dadarśa tryaṃbakābhyāśe vāsudevaniveditam ||2||

3tato'bhipūjya manasā kṛṣṇaṃ śarvaṃ ca pāṇḍavaḥ
|
icchāmyahaṃ divyamastramityabhāṣata śaṃkaram ||3||

4tataḥ pārthasya vijñāya varārthe vacanaṃ tadā
|
vāsudevārjunau devaḥ smayamāno'bhyabhāṣata ||4||

5svāgataṃ vāṃ naraśreṣṭhau vijñātaṃ manasepsitam
|
yena kāmena saṃprāptau bhavadbhyāṃ taṃ dadāmyaham ||5||

6saro'mṛtamayaṃ divyamabhyāśe śatrusūdanau
|
tatra me taddhanurdivyaṃ śaraśca nihitaḥ purā ||6||

7yena devārayaḥ sarve mayā yudhi nipātitāḥ
|
tata ānīyatāṃ kṛṣṇau saśara dhanuruttamam ||7||

8tathetyuktvā tu tau vīrau sarvapāriṣadaiḥ saha
|
prasthitau tatsaro divyaṃ divyaiśvaryaśatairyutam ||8||

9nirdiṣṭaṃ yadvṛṣāṅkeṇa puṇyaṃ sarvārthasādhakam
|
tau jagmaturasaṃbhrāntau naranārāyaṇāvṛṣī ||9||

10tatastau tat saro gatvā sūryamaṇḍalasannibham
|
nāgamantarjale ghoraṃ dadṛśāte'rjunācyutau ||10||

11dvitīyaṃ cāparaṃ nāgaṃ sahasraśirasaṃ varam
|
vamantaṃ vipulā jvālā dadṛśāte'gnivarcasam ||11||

12tataḥ kṛṣṇaśca pārthaśca saṃspṛśyāṃbhaḥ kṛtāñjalī
|
tau nāgāvupatasthāte namasyantau vṛṣadhvajam ||12||

13gṛṇantau vedavidvāṃsau tadbrahma śatarudriyam
|
aprameyaṃ praṇamato gatvā sarvātmanā bhavam ||13||

14tatastau rudramāhātmyāddhitvā rūpaṃ mahoragau
|
dhanurbāṇaśca śatrughnaṃ taddvaṃdvaṃ samapadyata ||14||

15tau tajjagṛhatuḥ prītau dhanurbāṇaṃ ca suprabham
|
ājahraturmahātmānau dadatuśca mahātmane ||15||

16tataḥ pārśvādvṛṣāṅkasya brahmacārī nyavartata
|
piṃgākṣastapasaḥ kṣetraṃ balavānnīlalohitaḥ ||16||

17sa tadgṛhya dhanuḥśreṣṭhaṃ tasthau sthānaṃ samāhitaḥ
|
vicakarṣātha vidhivatsaśaraṃ dhanuruttamam ||17||

18tasya maurvīṃ ca muṣṭiṃ ca sthānaṃ cālakṣya pāṇḍavaḥ
|
śrutvā mantraṃ bhavaproktaṃ jagrāhācintyavikramaḥ ||18||

19sa sarasyeva taṃ bāṇaṃ mumocātibalaḥ prabhuḥ
|
cakāra ca punarvīrastasmin sarasi taddhatuḥ ||19||

20tataḥ prītaṃ bhavaṃ jñātvā smṛtimānarjunastadā
|
varamāraṇyake dattaṃ darśanaṃ śaṃkarasya ca ||20||

21manasā cintayāmāsa tanme saṃpadyatāmiti
|
tasya tanmatamājñāya prītaḥ prādādvaraṃ bhavaḥ ||21||

22tacca pāśupataṃ ghoraṃ pratijñāyāśca pāraṇam
|
tataḥ pāśupataṃ divyamavāpya punarīśvarāt ||22||

23saṃhṛṣṭaromā durdharṣaḥ kṛtaṃ kāryamamanyata
|
vavandatuśca saṃhṛṣṭau śirobhyāṃ taṃ maheśvaram ||23||

24anujñātau kṣaṇe tasmin bhavenārjunakeśavau
|
prāptau svaśibiraṃ vīrau mudā paramayā yutau ||24||

25tathā bhavenānumatau mahāsuranighātinā
|
indrāviṣṇū yathā prītau jaṃbhasya vadhakāṃkṣiṇau ||25||
