Mahābhārata 13.22 (Anuśāsanaparvan)

1yudhiṣṭhira uvāca|
kimāhurbharataśreṣṭha pātraṃ viprāḥ sanātanāḥ
|
brāhmaṇaṃ liṃginaṃ caiva brāhmaṇaṃ vā'pyaliṃginam ||1||

2bhīṣma uvāca|
svavṛttimabhipannāya liṃgine cetarāya ca
|
deyamāhurmahārāja ubhāvetau tapasvinau ||2||

3yudhiṣṭhira uvāca|
śraddhayā parayā pūto yaḥ prayaccheddvijātaye
|
havyaṃ kavyaṃ tathā dānaṃ ko doṣaḥ syātpitāmaha ||3||

4bhīṣma uvāca|
śraddhāpūto narastāta durdāto'pi na saṃśayaḥ
|
pūto bhavati sarvatra kimu tattvaṃ mahādyute ||4||

5yudhiṣṭhira uvāca|
na brāhmaṇaṃ parīkṣeta daiveṣu satataṃ naraḥ
|
kavyapradāne tu budhāḥ parīkṣyaṃ brāhmaṇaṃ viduḥ ||5||

6bhīṣma uvāca|
na brāhmaṇaḥ sādhayate havyaṃ daivātprasiddhyati
|
devaprasādādijyante yajamānairna saṃśayaḥ ||6||

7brāhmaṇān bharataśreṣṭha satataṃ brahmavādinaḥ
|
mārkaṇḍeyaḥ purā prāha iti lokeṣu buddhimān ||7||

8yudhiṣṭhira uvāca|
apūrvo'pyathavā vidvān saṃbaṃdhī vā yathā bhavet
|
tapasvī yajñaśīlo vā kathaṃ pātraṃ bhavettu saḥ ||8||

9bhīṣma uvāca|
|
kulīnaḥ karmakṛdvaidyastathaivāpyānṛśaṃsyavān
|
hrīmānṛjuḥ satyavādī pātraṃ pūrvaṃ ca ye trayaḥ ||9||

10tatremaṃ śṛṇu me pārtha caturṇāṃ tejasāṃ matam
|
pṛthivyāḥ kāśyapasyāgnermārkaṇḍeyasya caiva hi ||10||

11pṛthivyuvāca |
yathā mahārṇave kṣiptaḥ kṣipraṃ leṣṭurvinaśyati
|
tathā duścaritaṃ sarvaṃ trivṛtyāṃ ca nimajjati ||11||

12kāśyapa uvāca|
sarve ca vedāḥ saha ṣaḍbhiraṅgaiḥ sāṅkhyaṃ purāṇaṃ ca kule ca janma
|
naitāni sarvāṇi gatirbhavanti śīlavyapetasya nṛpa dvijasya ||12||

13agniruvāca|
adhīyānaḥ paṇḍitaṃ manyamāno yo vidyayā hanti yaśaḥ pareṣām
|
prabhraśyate'sau carate na satyaṃ lokāstasya hyantavanto bhavanti ||13||

14mārkaṇḍeya uvāca|
aśvamedhasahasraṃ ca satyaṃ ca tulayā dhṛtam
|
nābhijānāmi yadyasya satyasyārdhamavāpnuyāt ||14||

15bhīṣma uvāca|
ityuktvā te jagmurāśu catvāro'mitatejasaḥ
|
pṛthivī kāśyapo'gniśca prahṛṣṭāyuśca bhārgavaḥ ||15||

16yudhiṣṭhira uvāca|
yadi te brāhmaṇā loke yatino bhuñjate haviḥ
|
dattaṃ brāhmaṇakāmāya kathaṃ tatsukṛtaṃ bhavet ||16||

17bhīṣma uvāca|
ādiṛṣṭino ye rājendra brāhmaṇā vedapāragāḥ
|
bhuñjate brahmakāmāya vrataluptā bhavanti te ||17||

18yudhiṣṭhira uvāca|
|
anekāntaṃ bahudvāraṃ dharmamāhurmanīṣiṇaḥ
|
kiṃnimittaṃ bhavedatra tanme brūhi pitāmaha ||18||

19bhīṣma uvāca|
ahiṃsā satyamakrodha ānṛśaṃsyaṃ damastathā
|
ārjavaṃ caiva rājendra niścitaṃ dharmalakṣaṇam ||19||

20ye tu dharmaṃ praśaṃsantaścaranti pṛthivīmimām
|
anācarantastaddharmaṃ saṅkare'bhiratāḥ prabho ||20||

21tebhyo hiraṇyaṃ ratnaṃ vā gāmaśvaṃ vā dadāti yaḥ
|
daśa varṣāṇi viṣṭhāṃ sa bhuṅkte nirayamāsthitaḥ ||21||

22medānāṃ pulkasānāṃ ca tathaivāntevasāyinām
|
kṛtaṃ karmākṛtaṃ vāpi rāgamohena jalpatām ||22||

23vaiśvadevaṃ ca ye mūḍhā viprāya brahmacāriṇe
|
dadate neha rājendra te lokān bhuñjate'śubhān ||23||

24yudhiṣṭhira uvāca|
kiṃ paraṃ brahmacaryaṃ ca kiṃ paraṃ dharmalakṣaṇam
|
kiṃ ca śreṣṭhatamaṃ śaucaṃ tanme brūhi pitāmaha ||24||

25bhīṣma uvāca|
brahmacaryātparaṃ tāta madhumāṃsasya varjanam
|
maryādāyāṃ sthito dharmaḥ śamaścaivāsya lakṣaṇam ||25||

26yudhiṣṭhira uvāca|
kasminkāle careddharmaṃ kasminkāle'rthamācaret
|
kasminkāle sukhī ca syāttanme brūhi pitāmaha ||26||

27bhīṣma uvāca|
kalyamarthaṃ niṣeveta tato dharmamanantaram
|
paścātkāmaṃ niṣeveta na ca gacchetprasaṅgitām ||27||

28brāhmaṇāṃścaiva manyeta guruṃścāpyabhipūjayet
|
sarvabhūtānukalomaśca mṛduśīlaḥ priyaṃvadaḥ ||28||

29adhikāro yadanṛtaṃ yacca rājasu paiśunam
|
guroścālīkakaraṇaṃ tulyaṃ tadbrahmahatyayā ||29||

30praharenna narendreṣu na hanyādgāṃ tathaiva ca
|
bhrūṇahatyāsamaṃ caiva ubhayaṃ yo niṣevate ||30||

31nāgniṃ parityajejjātu na ca vedān parityajet
|
na ca brāhmaṇamākrośetsamaṃ tadbrahmahatyayā ||31||

32yudhiṣṭhira uvāca|
kīdṛśāḥ sādhavo viprāḥ kebhyo dattaṃ mahāphalam
|
kīdṛśānāṃ ca bhoktavyaṃ tanme brūhi pitāmaha ||32||

33bhīṣma uvāca|
akrodhanā dharmaparāḥ satyanityā dame ratāḥ
|
tādṛśāḥ sādhavo viprāstebhyo dattaṃ mahāphalam ||33||

34amāninaḥ sarvasahā dṛḍhārthā vijitendriyāḥ
|
sarvabhūtahitā maitrāstebhyo dattaṃ mahāphalam ||34||

35alubdhāḥ śucayo vaidyā hrīmantaḥ satyavādinaḥ
|
svakarmaniratā ye ca tebhyo dattaṃ mahāphalam ||35||

36sāṅgāṃśca caturo vedānadhīte yo dvijarṣabhaḥ
|
ṣaḍbhyaḥ pravṛttaḥ karmabhyastaṃ pātramṛṣayo viduḥ ||36||

37ye tvevaṃguṇajātīyāstebhyo dattaṃ mahāphalam
|
sahasraguṇamāpnoti guṇārhāya pradāyakaḥ ||37||

38prajñāśrutābhyāṃ vṛttena śīlena ca samanvitaḥ
|
tārayeta kulaṃ sarvameko'pīha dvijarṣabhaḥ ||38||

39gāmaśvaṃ vittamannaṃ vā tadvidhe pratipādayet
|
dravyāṇi cānyāni tathā pretyabhāve na śocati ||39||

40tārayeta kulaṃ sarvameko'pīha dvijottamaḥ
|
kimaṅga punarevaite tasmātpātraṃ samācaret ||40||

41niśamya ca guṇopetaṃ brāhmaṇaṃ sādhusammatam
|
dūrādānāyya satkṛtya saṃvataścāpi pūjayet ||41||
