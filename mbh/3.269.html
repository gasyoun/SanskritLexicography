Mahābhārata 3.269 (Āraṇyakaparvan)

1vaiśampāyana uvāca|
tato diśaḥ sampravihṛtya pārthā mṛgān varāhān mahiṣāṃśca hatvā
|
dhanurdharāḥ śreṣṭhatamāḥ pṛthivyāṃ pṛthakcarantaḥ sahitā babhūvuḥ||1||

2tato mṛgavyālagaṇānukīrṇaṃ mahāvanaṃ tadvihagopaghuṣṭam
|
bhrātṝṃśca tānabhyavadadyudhiṣṭhiraḥ śrutvā giro vyāharatāṃ mṛgāṇām||2||

3ādityadīptāṃ diśamabhyupetya mṛgā dvijāḥ krūramime vadanti
|
āyāsamagraṃ prativedayanto mahāvanaṃ śatrubhirbādhyamānam||3||

4kṣipraṃ nivartadhvamalaṃ mṛgairnau mano hi me dūyati dahyate ca
|
buddhiṃ samācchādya ca me samanyurudbhūyate prāṇapatiḥ śarīre||4||

5saraḥ suparṇena hṛtoragaṃ yathā rāṣṭraṃ yathā'rājakamāttalakṣmi
|
evaṃvidhaṃ me pratibhāti kāmyakaṃ śauṇḍairyathā pītarasaśca kumbhaḥ||5||

6te saindhavairatyanilogravegairmahājavairvājibhiruhyamānāḥ
|
yuktairbṛhadbhiḥ surathairnṛvīrāstadā''śramāyābhimukhā babhūvuḥ||6||

7teṣāṃ tu gomāyuranalpaghoṣo nivartatāṃ vāmamupetya pārśvam
|
pravyāharat tat pravimṛśya rājā provāca bhīmaṃ ca dhanañjayaṃ ca||7||

8yathā vadatyeṣa vihīnayoniḥ śālāvṛko vāmamupetya pārśvam
|
suvyaktamasmānavamanya pāpaiḥ kṛto'bhimardaḥ kurubhiḥ prasahya||8||

9ityeva te tadvanamāviśanto mahatyaraṇye mṛgayāṃ caritvā
|
bālāmapaśyanta tadā rudantīṃ dhātreyikāṃ preṣya vadhūṃ priyāyāḥ||9||

10tāmindrasenastvarito'bhisṛtya rathādavaplutya tato'bhyadhāvat
|
provāca caināṃ vacanaṃ narendra dhātreyikāmantitarastadānīm||10||

11kiṃ rodiṣi tvaṃ patitā dharaṇyāṃ kiṃ te mukhaṃ śuṣyati dīnavarṇam
|
kaccinna pāpaiḥ sunṛśaṃsakṛdbhiḥ pramāthitā draupadī rājaputrī||11||

12acintyarūpā suviśālanetrā śarīratulyā kurupuṅgavānām
|
yadyeva devī pṛthivīṃ praviṣṭā divaṃ prapannā'pyathavā samudram||12||

13tasyā gamiṣyanti pade hi pārthā yathā hi santapyati dharmaputraḥ
|
ko hīdṛśānāmarimardanānāṃ kleśakṣamānāmaparājitānām||13||

14prāṇaiḥ samāmiṣṭatamāṃ jihīrṣedanuttamaṃ ratnāmiva pramūḍhaḥ
|
na budhyate nāthavatīmihādya bahiśvaraṃ hṛdayaṃ pāṇḍavānām||14||

15kasyādya kāyaṃ pratibhidya ghorā mahīṃ pravekṣyanti śitāḥ śarāgryāḥ
|
mā tvaṃ śucastāṃ prati bhīru viddhi yathā'dya kṛṣṇā punareṣyatīti||15||

16nihatya sarvān dviṣataḥ samagrān pārthāḥ sameṣyantyatha yājñasenyā
|
athābravīccārumukhaṃ vimṛśya dhātreyikā sārathimindrasenam||16||

17jayadrathenāpahṛtā pramathya pañcandrakalpān paribhūya kṛṣṇā
|
tiṣṭhanti varmāni navānyamūni vṛkṣāśca na mlānti tathaiva bhagnāḥ||17||

18āvartayadhvaṃ hyanuyāta śītraṃ na dūrayātaiva hi rājaputrī
|
sannahyadhvaṃ sarva evendrakalpā mahānti cārūṇi ca daṃśanāni||18||

19gṛhṇīta cāpāni mahādhanāni śarāṃśca śīghraṃ padavīṃ caradhvam
|
purā hi nirbhartsanadaṇḍamohitā pramohacittā vadanena śuṣyatā||19||

20dadāti kasmaicidanarhate tanuṃ varājyapūrṇāmiva bhasmani srucam
|
purā tuṣāgnāviva hūyate haviḥ purā śmaśāne stragivāpavidhdyate||20||

21purā ca somo'dhvarago'valihyate śunā yathā viprajane pramohite
|
mahatyaraṇye mṛgayāṃ caritvā purā śṛgālo nalinīṃ vigāhate||21||

22mā vaḥ priyāyāḥ sunasaṃ sulocanaṃ candraprabhācchaṃ vadanaṃ prasannam
|
spṛśyācchubhaṃ kaścidakṛtyakārī śvā vai puroḍāśamivādhvarastham
|
etāni varmānyanuyāta śīghraṃ mā vaḥ kālaḥ kṣipramihātyagādvai||22||

23yudhiṣṭhira uvāca|
bhadre pratikrāma niyaccha vācaṃ mā'smatsakāśe paruṣāṇyavocaḥ
|
rājāno vā yadi vā rājaputrā balena mattā vañcanāṃ prāpnuvanti||23||

24vaiśampāyana uvāca|
etāvaduktvā prayayurhi śīghraṃ tānyeva vartmānyanuvartamānāḥ
|
muhurmuhurvyālavaducchvalanto jyāṃ vikṣipantaśca mahādhanurbhyaḥ||24||

25tato'paśyaṃstasya sainyasya reṇumudbhūtaṃ vai vājikhurapraṇunnam
|
padātīnāṃ madhyagataṃ ca dhaumyaṃ vikrośantaṃ bhīmamabhidraveti||25||

26te sāntvya dhaumyaṃ paridīnasattvāḥ sukhaṃ bhavānetviti rājaputrāḥ
|
śyenā yathaivāmiṣasamprayuktā javena tat sainyamathābhyadhāvan||26||

27teṣāṃ mahendropamavikramāṇāṃ saṃrabdhānāṃ gharṣaṇādyājñasenyāḥ
|
krodhaḥ prajajvāla jayadrathaṃ ca dṛṣṭvā priyāṃ tasya rathe sthitāṃ ca||27||

28pracukruśuścāpyatha sindhurājaṃ vṛkodaraścaiva dhanañjayaśca
|
yamau ca rājā ca mahādhanurdharāstasto diśaḥ saṃmumuhuḥ pareṣām||28||
