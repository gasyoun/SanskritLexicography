Mahābhārata 5.151 (Udyogaparvan)

1vaiśampāyana uvāca|
janārdanavacaḥ śrutvā dharmarājī yudhiṣṭhiraḥ
|
bhrātṝnuvāca dharmātmā samakṣaṃ keśavasya ha ||1||

2śrutaṃ bhavadbhiryadvṛttaṃ sabhāyāṃ kurusaṃsadi
|
keśavasyāpi yadvākyaṃ tat sarvamavadhāritam ||2||

3tasmāt senāvibhāgaṃ me kurudhvaṃ narasattamāḥ
|
akṣauhiṇyaśca saptaitāḥ sametā vijayāya vai ||3||

4tāsāṃ ye patayaḥ sapta vikhyātāstānnibodhata
|
drupadaśca virāṭaśca dhṛṣṭadyumnaśikhaṇḍino ||4||

5sātyakiścekitānaśca bhīmasenaśca vīryavān
|
ete senāpraṇetāro vīrāḥ sarve tanutyajaḥ ||5||

6sarve vedavidaḥ śūrāḥ sarve sucaritavratāḥ
|
hrīmanto nītimantaśca sarve yuddhaviśāradāḥ ||6||

7iṣvastrakuśalāḥ sarve tathā sarvāstrayodhinaḥ
|
saptānāmapi yo netā senānāṃ pravibhāgavit ||7||

8yaḥ saheta raṇe bhīṣmaṃ śarārciḥ pāvakopamam
|
taṃ tāvat sahadevātra prabrūhi kurunandana
|
svamataṃ puruṣavyāghra ko naḥ senāpatiḥ kṣamaḥ ||8||

9sahadeva uvāca|
saṃyukta ekaduḥkhaśca vīryavāṃśca mahīpatiḥ
|
yaṃ samāśritya dharmajñaṃ svamaṃśamanuyuñjmahe ||9||

10matsyo virāṭo balavān kṛtāstro yuddhadurmadaḥ
|
prasahiṣyati saṅgrāme bhīṣmaṃ tāṃśca mahārathān ||10||

11vaiśampāyana uvāca|
tathokte sahadevena vākye vākyaviśārada
|
nakulo'nantaraṃ tasmādidaṃ vacanamādade ||11||

12vayasā śāstrato dhairyāt kulenābhijanena ca
|
hrīmān balānvitaḥ śrīmān sarvaśāstraviśāradaḥ ||12||

13veda cāstraṃ bharadvājāddurdharṣaḥ satyasaṅgaraḥ
|
yo nityaṃ spardhate droṇaṃ bhīṣmaṃ caiva mahābalam ||13||

14ślāghyaḥ pārthivavaṃśasya pramukhe vāhinīpatiḥ
|
putrapautraiḥ parivṛtaḥ śataśākha iva drumaḥ ||14||

15yastatāpa tapo ghoraṃ sadāraḥ pṛthivīpatiḥ
|
roṣāddroṇavināśāya vīraḥ samiti śobhanaḥ ||15||

16pitevāsmān samādhatte yaḥ sadā pārthivarṣabhaḥ
|
śvaśuro drupado'smākaṃ senāgraṃ sa prakarṣatu ||16||

17sa droṇabhīṣmāvāyātau sahediti matirmama
|
sa hi divyāstravidrājā sakhā cāṅgiraso nṛpaḥ||17||

18mādrīsutābhyāmukte tu svamate kurunandanaḥ
|
vāsavirvāsavasamaḥ savyasācyabravīdvacaḥ ||18||

19yo'yaṃ tapaḥprabhāveṇa ṛṣisantoṣaṇena ca
|
divyaḥ puruṣa utpanno jvālāvarṇo mahābhujaḥ ||19||

20dhanuṣmān kavacī khaḍgī rathamāruhya daṃśitaḥ
|
divyairhayavarairyuktamagnikuṇḍāt samutthitaḥ ||20||

21garjanniva mahāmegho rathaghoṣeṇa vīryavān
|
siṃhasaṃhanano vīraḥ siṃhatulyaparākramaḥ ||21||

22siṃhoraskaḥ siṃhabhujaḥ siṃhavakṣā mahābalaḥ
|
siṃhapragarjano vīraḥ siṃhaskandho mahādyutiḥ ||22||

23subhrūḥ sudaṃṣṭraḥ suhanuḥ subāhuḥ sumukho'kṛśaḥ
|
sujatruḥ suviśālākṣaḥ supādaḥ supratiṣṭhitaḥ ||23||

24abhedyaḥ sarvaśastrāṇāṃ prabhinna iva vāraṇaḥ
|
jajñe droṇavināśāya satyavādī jitendriyaḥ ||24||

25dhṛṣṭadyumnamahaṃ manye sahedbhīṣmasya sāyakān
|
cajrāśanisamasparśān dīptāsyānuragāniva ||25||

26yamadūtasamān vege nipāte pāvakopamān
|
rāmeṇājau viṣahitān vajraniṣpeṣadāruṇān ||26||

27puruṣaṃ taṃ na paśyāmi yaḥ saheta mahāvratam
|
dhṛṣṭadyumnamṛte rājanniti me dhīyate matiḥ ||27||

28kṣiprahastaścitrayodhī mataḥ senāpatirmama
|
abhedyakavacaḥ śrīmān mātaṅga iva yūthapaḥ ||28||

29bhīmasena uvāca|
vadhārthaṃ yaḥ samutpannaḥ śikhaṇḍī drupadātmajaḥ
|
vadanti siddhā rājendra ṛṣayaśca samāgatāḥ ||29||

30yasya saṅgrāmamadhye tu divyamastraṃ prakurvataḥ
|
rūpaṃ drakṣyanti puruṣā rāmasyeva mahātmanaḥ ||30||

31na taṃ yuddhe prapaśyāmi yo bhindyāttu śikhaṇḍinam
|
śastreṇa samare rājan sannaddhaṃ syandane sthitam ||31||

32dvairathe samare nānyo bhīṣmaṃ hanyānmahāvratam
|
śikhaṇḍinamṛte vīraṃ sa me senāpatirmataḥ ||32||

33yudhiṣṭhira uvāca|
sarvasya jagatastāta sārāsāraṃ balābalam
|
sarvaṃ jānāti dharmātmā matameṣāṃ ca keśavaḥ ||33||

34yamāha kṛṣṇo dāśārhaḥ so'stu senāpatirmama
|
kṛtāstro'pyakṛtāstro vā vṛddho vā yadi vā yuvā ||34||

35eṣa no vijaye mūlameṣa tāta viparyaye
|
atra prāṇāśca rājyaṃ ca bhāvābhāvau sukhāsukhe ||35||

36eṣa dhātā vidhātā ca siddhiratra pratiṣṭhitā
|
yamāha kṛṣṇo dāśārhaḥ so'stu no vāhinīpatiḥ ||36||

37bravītu vadatāṃ śreṣṭho niśā samabhivartate
|
tataḥ senāpatiṃ kṛtvā kṛṣṇasya vaśavartinaḥ ||37||

38rātreḥ śeṣe vyatikrānte prayāsyāmo raṇājiram
|
adhivāsitaśastrāśca kṛtakautukamaṅgalāḥ ||38||

39vaiśampāyana uvāca|
tasya tadvacanaṃ śrutvā dharmarājasya dhīmataḥ
|
abravīt puṇḍarīkākṣo dhanañjayamavekṣya ha ||39||

40mamāpyete mahārāja bhavadbhirya udāhṛtāḥ
|
netārastava senāyā matā vikrāntayodhinaḥ ||40||

41sarva eva samarthā hi tava śatruṃ prabādhitum
|
indrasyāpi bhayaṃ hyete janayeyurmahāhave ||41||

42kiṃ punardhārtarāṣṭrāṇāṃ lubdhānāṃ pāpacetasām
|
mayā'pi hi mahābāho tvatpriyārthaṃ mahāhave ||42||

43kṛto yatno mahāṃstatra śamaḥ syāditi bhārata
|
dharmasya gatamānṛṇyaṃ na sma vācyā vivakṣatām ||43||

44kṛtāstraṃ manyate bāla ātmānamavicakṣaṇaḥ
|
dhārtarāṣṭro balasthaṃ ca paśyatyātmānamāturaḥ ||44||

45yujyatāṃ vāhinī sādhu vadhasādhyā hi me matāḥ
|
na dhārtarāṣṭrāḥ śakṣyanti sthātuṃ dṛṣṭvā dhanañjayam ||45||

46bhīmasenaṃ ca saṅkruddhaṃ yamau cāpi yamopamau
|
yuyudhānadvitīyaṃ ca dhṛṣṭadyumnamamarṣaṇam ||46||

47abhimanyuṃ draupadeyān virāṭadrupadāvapi
|
akṣauhiṇīpatīṃścānyānnarendrān bhīmavikramān ||47||

48sāravadbalamasmākaṃ duṣpradharṣaṃ durāsadam
|
dhārtarāṣṭrabalaṃ saṅkhye haniṣyati na saṃśayaḥ ||48||

49dhṛṣṭadyumnamahaṃ manye senāpatimarindama| vaiśampāyana uvāca|
evamukte tu kṛṣṇena saṃprāhṛṣyannarottamāḥ ||49||

50teṣāṃ prahṛṣṭamanasāṃ nādaḥ samabhavanmahān
|
yoga ityatha sainyānāṃ tvaratāṃ saṃpradhāvatām ||50||

51hayavāraṇaśabdāśca nemighoṣāśca sarvataḥ
|
śaṅkhadundubhighoṣāśca tumulāḥ sarvato'bhavan ||51||

52tadugraṃ sāgaranibhaṃ kṣubdhaṃ balasamāgamam
|
rathapattigajodagraṃ mahormibhirivākulam ||52||

53dhāvatāmāhvayānānāṃ tanutrāṇi ca badhnatām
|
prayāsyatāṃ pāṇḍavānāṃ sasainyānāṃ samantataḥ ||53||

54gaṅgeva pūrṇā durdharṣā samadṛśyata vāhinī
|
agrānīke bhīmaseno mādrīputrau ca daṃśitau ||54||

55saubhadro draupadeyāśca dhṛṣṭadyumnaśca pārṣataḥ
|
prabhadrakāśca pañcālā [prabhadrakāḥ sapañcālā] bhīmasenamukhā yayuḥ ||55||

56tataḥ śabdaḥ samabhavat samudrasyeva parvaṇi
|
hṛṣṭānāṃ saṃprayātānāṃ ghoṣo divamivāspṛśat ||56||

57prahṛṣṭā daṃśitā yodhāḥ parānīkavidāraṇāḥ
|
teṣāṃ madhye yayau rājā kuntīputro yudhiṣṭhiraḥ ||57||

58śakaṭāpaṇaveśāśca yānayugyaṃ ca sarvaśaḥ
|
kośaṃ yantrāyudhaṃ caiva ye ca vaidyāścikitsakāḥ ||58||

59phalgu yacca balaṃ kiñcidyaccāpi kṛśadurbalam
|
tatsaṅgṛhya yayau rājā ye cāpi paricārakāḥ ||59||

60upaplavye tu pāñcālī draupadī satyavādinī
|
saha strībhirnivavṛte dāsīdāsasamāvṛtā ||60||

61kṛtvā mūlapratīkāraṃ gulmaiḥ sthāvarajaṅgamaiḥ
|
skandhāvāreṇa mahatā prayayuḥ pāṇḍunandanāḥ ||61||

62dadato gāṃ hiraṇyaṃ ca brāhmaṇairabhisaṃvṛtāḥ
|
stūyamānā yayū rājan rathairmaṇivibhūṣitaiḥ ||62||

63kekayā dhṛṣṭaketuśca putraḥ kāśyasya cābhibhuḥ
|
śreṇimān vasudānaśca śikhaṇḍī cāparājitaḥ ||63||

64hṛṣṭāstuṣṭāḥ kavacinaḥ saśastrāḥ samalaṅkṛtāḥ
|
rājānamanvayuḥ sarve parivārya yudhiṣṭhiram ||64||

65jaghanārdhe virāṭaśca yājñaseniśca saumakiḥ
|
sudharmā kuntibhojaśca dhṛṣṭadyumnasya cātmajāḥ ||65||

66rathāyutāni catvāri hayāḥ pañcaguṇāstathā
|
pattisainyaṃ daśaguṇaṃ gajānāmayutāni ṣaṭ ||66||

67anādhṛṣṭiścekitāno dhṛṣṭaketuśca sātyakiḥ
|
parivārya yayuḥ sarve vāsudevadhanañjayau ||67||

68āsādya tu kurukṣetraṃ vyūḍhānīkāḥ prahāriṇaḥ
|
pāṇḍavāḥ samadṛśyanta nardanto vṛṣabhā iva ||68||

69te'vagāhya kurukṣetraṃ śaṅkhān dadhmurarindamāḥ
|
tathaiva dadhmatuḥ śaṅkhaṃ vāsudevadhanañjayau ||69||

70pāñcajanyasya nirghoṣaṃ visphūrjitamivāśaneḥ
|
niśamya sarvasainyāni samahṛṣyanta sarvaśaḥ ||70||

71śaṅkhadundubhisaṃhṛṣṭaḥ siṃhanādastarasvinām
|
pṛthivīṃ cāntarikṣaṃ ca sāgarāṃścānvanādayat ||71||
