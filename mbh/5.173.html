Mahābhārata 5.173 (Udyogaparvan)

1duryodhana uvāca|
kimarthaṃ bharataśreṣṭha naiva hanyāḥ śikhaṇḍinam
|
udyateṣumatho dṛṣṭvā samareṣvātatāyinam ||1||

2pūrvamuktvā mahābāho pañcālān saha somakaiḥ
|
haniṣyāmīti gāṅgeya tanme brūhi pitāmaha ||2||

3bhīṣma uvāca|
śṛṇu duryodhana kathāṃ sahaibhirvasudhādhipaiḥ
|
yadarthaṃ yudhi saṃprekṣya nāhaṃ hanyāṃ śikhaṇḍinam ||3||

4mahārājo mama pitā śāntanurlokaviśrutaḥ
|
diṣṭāntamāpa dharmātmā samaye bharatarṣabha ||4||

5tato'haṃ bharataśreṣṭha pratijñāṃ paripālayan
|
citrāṅgadaṃ bhrātaraṃ vai mahārājye'bhyaṣecayam ||5||

6tasmiṃśca nidhanaṃ prāpte satyavatyā mate sthitaḥ
|
vicitravīryaṃ rājānamabhyaṣiñcaṃ yathāvidhi ||6||

7mayā'bhiṣikto rājendra yavīyānapi dharmataḥ
|
vicitravīryo dharmātmā māmeva samudaikṣata ||7||

8tasya dārakriyāṃ tāta cikīrṣurahamapyuta
|
anurūpādiva kulādityeva ca mano dadhe ||8||

9tathā'śrauṣaṃ mahābāho tisraḥ kanyāḥ svayaṃvarāḥ
|
rūpeṇāpratimāḥ sarvāḥ kāśirājasutāstadā
|
ambāṃ caivāmbikāṃ caiva tathaivāmbālikāmapi ||9||

10rājānaśca samāhūtāḥ pṛthivyāṃ bharatarṣabha
|
ambā jyeṣṭhā'bhavattāsāmambikā tvatha madhyamā ||10||

11ambālikā ca rājendra rājakanyā yavīyasī
|
so'hamekarathenaiva gataḥ kāśipateḥ purīm ||11||

12apaśyaṃ tā mahābāho tisraḥ kanyāḥ svalaṃkṛtāḥ
|
rājñaścaiva samāhūtān pārthivān pṛthivīpate ||12||

13tato'haṃ tānnṛpān sarvānāhūya samare sthitān
|
rathamāropayāñcakre kanyāstā bharatarṣabha ||13||

14vīryaśulkāśca tā jñātvā samāropya rathaṃ tadā
|
avocaṃ pārthivān sarvānahaṃ tatra samāgatān
|
bhīṣmaḥ śāntanavaḥ kanyā haratīti punaḥ punaḥ ||14||

15te yatadhvaṃ paraṃ śaktyā sarve mokṣāya pārthivāḥ
|
prasahya hi harāmyeṣa miṣatāṃ vo nararṣabhāḥ ||15||

16tataste pṛthivīpālāḥ samutpeturudāyudhāḥ
|
yogo yoga iti kuddhāḥ sārathīnabhyacodayan ||16||

17te rathairgajasaṅkāśairgajaiśca gajayodhinaḥ
|
puṣṭaiścāśvairmahīpālāḥ samutpeturudāyudhāḥ ||17||

18tataste māṃ mahīpālāḥ sarva eva viśāṃpate
|
rathavrātena mahatā sarvataḥ paryavārayan ||18||

19tānahaṃ śaravarṣeṇa samaṃtāt paryavārayam
|
sarvānnṛpāṃścāpyajayaṃ devarāḍiva dānavān ||19||

20apātayaṃ śarairdīptaiḥ prahasan bharatarṣabha
|
teṣāmāpatatāṃ citrān dhvajān hemapariṣkṛtān ||20||

21ekaikena hi bāṇena bhūmau pātitavānaham
|
hayāṃsteṣāṃ gajāṃścaiva sārathīṃścāpyahaṃ raṇe ||21||

22te nivṛttāśca bhagnāśca dṛṣṭvā tallāghavaṃ mama
|
athāhaṃ hāstinapuramāyāṃ jitvā mahīkṣitaḥ ||22||

23tato'haṃ tāśca kanyā vai bhrāturarthāya bhārata
|
tacca karma mahābāho satyavatyai nyavedayam ||23||
