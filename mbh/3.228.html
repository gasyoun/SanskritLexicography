Mahābhārata 3.228 (Āraṇyakaparvan)

1mārkaṇḍeya uvāca|
skandapāriṣadān ghorān śṛṇuṣvādbhutadarśanān
|
vajraprahārāt skandasya jajñustatra kumārakāḥ||1||

2ye haranti śiśūn jātān garbhasthāṃścaiva dāruṇāḥ
|
vajraprahārāt kanyāśca jajñire'sya mahābalāḥ||2||

3kumārāste viśākhaṃ ca pitṛtve samakalpayan
|
sa bhūttvā bhagavān saṅkhye rakṣeśchāgamukhastadā||3||

4vṛtaḥ kanyāgaṇaiḥ sarvairātmīyaiḥ saha putrakaiḥ
|
mātṝṇāṃ prekṣatīnāṃ ca bhadraśākhaśca kausalaḥ||4||

5tataḥ kumārapitaraṃ skandamāhurjanā bhuvi
|
rudramagnimukhāṃ svāhāṃ pradeśeṣu mahābalam||5||

6yajanti putrakāmāśca putriṇaśca sadā janāḥ
|
yāstāstvajanayat kanyāstapo nāma hutāśanaḥ||6||

7kiṃ karomīti tāḥ skandaṃ samprāptāḥ samabhāṣayan
|
kumārya ūcuḥ
|
bhavema sarvalokasya mātaro vayamuttamāḥ||7||

8prasādāt tava pūjyāśca priyametat kuruṣva naḥ
|
so'bravīdbāḍhamityevaṃ bhaviṣyadhvaṃ pṛthagvidhāḥ||8||

9śivāścaivāśivāścaiva punaḥ punarudāradhīḥ
|
tataḥ saṅkalpya putratve skandaṃ mātṛgaṇo'gamat||9||

10kākī ca halimā caiva mālinī bṛṃhatā tathā
|
āryā palālā vaimitrā saptaitāḥ śiśumātaraḥ||10||

11etāsāṃ vīryasampannaḥ śiśurnāmātidāruṇaḥ
|
skandaprasādajaḥ putro lohitākṣo bhayaṅkaraḥ||11||

12eṣa vīrāṣṭakaḥ proktaḥ skandamātṛgaṇodbhavaḥ
|
chāgavaktreṇa sahito navakaḥ parikīrtyate||12||

13ṣaṣṭhaṃ chāgamayaṃ vaktraṃ skandasyaiveti viddhi tat
|
ṣaṭśirobhyantaraṃ rājannityaṃ mātṛgaṇārcitam||13||

14ṣaṇṇāṃ tu pravaraṃ tasya śīrṣāṇāmiha śabdyate
|
śaktiṃ yenāsṛjaddivyāṃ bhadraśākha iti sma ha||14||

15ityetaddvividhākāraṃ vṛttaṃ śuklasya pañcamīm
|
tatra yuddhaṃ mahāghoraṃ vṛttaṃ ṣaṣṭhyāṃ janādhipa||15||
