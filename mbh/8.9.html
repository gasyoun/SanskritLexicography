Mahābhārata 8.9 (Karṇaparvan)

1sañjaya uvāca|
śriyā kulena yaśasā tapasā ca śrutena ca
|
tvāmadya santo manyante yayātimiva nāhuṣam ||1||

2śrute maharṣipratimaḥ kṛtakṛtyo'si pārthiva
|
paryavasthāpayātmānaṃ mā viṣāde manaḥ kṛthāḥ ||2||

3dhṛtarāṣṭra uvāca|
daivameva paraṃ manye dhi‌kpauruṣamanarthakam
|
yatra śālapratīkāśaḥ karṇo'hanyata saṃyuge ||3||

4hatvā yudhiṣṭhirānīkaṃ pañcālānāṃ rathavrajān
|
pratāpya śaravarṣeṇa diśaḥ sarvā mahārathaḥ ||4||

5mohayitvā raṇe pārthān vajrahasta ivāsurān
|
sa kathaṃ nihataḥ śete vāyurugṇa iva drumaḥ ||5||

6śokasyāntaṃ na paśyāmi pāraṃ jalanidheriva
|
cintā me vardhate'tīva mumūrṣā cāpi jāyate ||6||

7karṇasya nidhanaṃ śrutvā vijayaṃ phālgunasya ca
|
aśraddheyamahaṃ manye vadhaṃ karṇasya sañjaya ||7||

8vajrasāramayaṃ nūnaṃ hṛdayaṃ durbhidaṃ mama
|
yacchrutvā puruṣavyāghraṃ hataṃ karṇe na dīryate ||8||

9āyurnūnaṃ sudīrghaṃ me vihitaṃ daivataiḥ purā
|
yatra karṇaṃ hataṃ śrutvā jīvāmīha suduḥkhitaḥ ||9||

10dhigjīvitamidaṃ caiva suhṛddhīnasya sañjaya
|
adya cāhaṃ daśāmetāṃ gataḥ sañjaya garhitām ||10||

11kṛpaṇaṃ vartayiṣyāmi śocyaḥ sarvasya mandadhīḥ
|
ahameva purā bhūtvā sarvalokasya satkṛtaḥ ||11||

12paribhūtaḥ kathaṃ sūta paraiḥ śakṣyāmi jīvitum
|
duḥkhātsuduḥkhavyasanaṃ prāptavānasmi sañjaya ||12||

13bhīṣmadroṇavadhenaiva karṇasya ca mahātmanaḥ
|
nāvaśeṣaṃ prapaśyāmi sūtaputre hate yudhi ||13||

14sa hi pārtha mahānāsītputrāṇāṃ mama sañjaya
|
yuddhe hi nihataḥ śūro visṛjansāyakān bahūn ||14||

15ko hi me jīvitenārthastamṛte puruṣarṣabham
|
rathādādhirathirnūnaṃ nyapatatsāyakārditaḥ ||15||

16parvatasyeva śikharaṃ vajrapātādvidāritam
|
sa śete pṛthivīṃ nūnaṃ śobhayan rudhirokṣitaḥ ||16||

17mātaṅga iva mattena dvipendreṇa nipātitaḥ
|
yo balaṃ dhārtarāṣṭrāṇāṃ pāṇḍavānāṃ yato bhayam ||17||

18so'rjunena hataḥ karṇaḥ pratimānaṃ dhanuṣmatām
|
sa hi vīro maheṣvāso mitrāṇāmabhayaṅkaraḥ ||18||

19śete vinihato vīro devendreṇa ivācalaḥ
|
paṅgorivādhvagamanaṃ daridrasyeva kāmitam ||19||

20duryodhanasya cākūtaṃ tṛṣitasyeva vipruṣaḥ
|
anyathā cintitaṃ kāryamanyathā tattu jāyate ||20||

21aho nu balavaddaivaṃ 'kālaśca duratikramaḥ'
|
palāyamānaḥ kṛpaṇo dīnātmā dīnapauruṣaḥ ||21||

22kaccidvinihataḥ sūta putro duḥśāsano mama
|
kaccinna dīnācaritaṃ kṛtavāṃstāta saṃyuge ||22||

23kaccinna nihataḥ śūro yathā'nye kṣatriyarṣabhāḥ
|
yudhiṣṭhirasya vacanaṃ mā yudhyasveti sarvadā ||23||

24duryodhano nābhyagṛhṇānmūḍhaḥ pathyamivauṣadham
|
śaratalpe śayānena bhīṣmeṇa sumahātmanā ||24||

25pānīyaṃ yācitaḥ pārthaḥ so'vidhyanmedinītalam
|
jalasya dhārāṃ janitāṃ dṛṣṭvā pāṇḍusutena ca ||25||

26abravītsa mahābāhustāta saṃśāmya pāṇḍavaiḥ
|
praśamāddhi bhavecchāntirmadantaṃ yuddhamastu vaḥ ||26||

27bhrātṛbhāvena pṛthivīṃ bhuṅkṣva pāṇḍusutaiḥ saha
|
akurvanvacanaṃ tasya nūnaṃ śocati putrakaḥ ||27||

28tadidaṃ samanuprāptaṃ vacanaṃ dīrghadarśinaḥ
|
ahaṃ tu nihatāmātyo hataputraśca sañjaya ||28||

29dyūtataḥ kṛcchramāpanno lūnapakṣa iva dvijaḥ
|
yathā hi śakuniṃ gṛhya chittvā pakṣau ca sañjaya ||29||

30visarjayanti saṃhṛṣṭāstāḍyamānāḥ kumārakāḥ
|
lūnapakṣatayā tasya gamanaṃ nopapadyate ||30||

31tathā'hamapi saṃprāpto lūnapakṣa iva dvijaḥ
|
kṣīṇaḥ sarvārthahīnaśca nirjñātirbandhuvarjitaḥ
|
kāṃ diśaṃ pratipatsyāmi dīnaḥ śatruvaśaṃ gataḥ ||31||

32vaiśampāyana uvāca|
ityevaṃ dhṛtarāṣṭro'tha vilapya bahu duḥkhitaḥ
|
provāca sañjayaṃ bhūyaḥ śokavyākulamānasaḥ ||32||

33dhṛtarāṣṭra uvāca|
yo'jayatsarvakāmbojānambaṣṭhānkekayaiḥ saha
|
gāndhārāṃśca videhāṃśca jitvā kāryārthamāhave ||33||

34duryodhanasya vṛddhyarthaṃ yo'jayatpṛthivīṃ prabhuḥ
|
sa jitaḥ pāṇḍavaiḥ śūraiḥ samare bāhuśālibhiḥ ||34||

35tasmin hate maheṣvāse karṇe yudhi kirīṭinā
|
ke vīrāḥ paryatiṣṭhanta tanmamācakṣva sañjaya ||35||

36kaccinnaikaḥ parityaktaḥ pāṇḍavairnihato raṇe
|
uktaṃ tvayā purā tāta yathā vīro nipātitaḥ ||36||

37bhīṣmamapratiyuddhyantaṃ śikhaṇḍī sāyakottamaiḥ
|
pātayāmāsa samare sarvaśastrabhṛtāṃ varam ||37||

38tathā draupadinā droṇo nyastasarvāyudho yudhi
|
yuktayogo maheṣvāsaḥ śarairbahubhirācitaḥ ||38||

39nihataḥ khaḍgamudyamya dhṛṣṭadyumnena sañjaya
|
antareṇa hatāvetau chalena ca viśeṣataḥ ||39||

40aśrauṣamahametadvai bhīṣmadroṇau nipātitau
|
bhīṣmadroṇau hi samare na hanyādvajrabhṛtsvayam ||40||

41nyāyena yudhyamānau hi tadvai satyaṃ bravīmi te
|
karṇaṃ tvasyantamastrāṇi divyāni ca bahūni ca ||41||

42kathamindropamaṃ vīraṃ mṛtyuryuddhe samaspṛśat
|
yasya vidyutprabhāṃ śaktiṃ divyāṃ kanakabhūṣaṇām ||42||

43prāyacchaddviṣatāṃ hantrīṃ kuṇḍalābhyāṃ purandaraḥ
|
yasya sarpamukho divyaḥ śaraḥ kāñcanabhūṣaṇaḥ ||43||

44aśeta nihataḥ patrī candaneṣvarisūdanaḥ
|
bhīṣmadroṇamukhānvīrānyo'vamanye mahārathān ||44||

45jāmadagnyānmahāghoraṃ brāhmamastramaśikṣata
|
yaśca droṇamukhān dṛṣṭvā vimukhānarditāñśaraiḥ ||45||

46saubhadrasya mahābāhurvyadhamatkārmukaṃ śitaiḥ
|
yaśca nāgāyutaprāṇaṃ vajraraṃhasamacyutam ||46||

47virathaṃ sahasā kṛtvā bhīmasenamathāhasat
|
sahadevaṃ ca nirjitya śaraiḥ sannataparvabhiḥ ||47||

48kṛpayā virathaṃ kṛtvā nāhanaddharmacintayā
|
yaśca māyāsahastrāṇi vikurvāṇaṃ jayaiṣiṇam ||48||

49ghaṭotkacaṃ rākṣasendraṃ śakraśaktyā nijaghnivān
|
etāṃśca divasānyasya yuddhe bhīto dhanañjayaḥ ||49||

50nāgamaddvairathaṃ bīraḥ sa kathaṃ nihato raṇe
|
saṃśaptakānāṃ yodhā ye āhvayanta sadā'nyataḥ ||50||

51etānhatvā haniṣyāmi paścādvaikartanaṃ raṇe
|
iti vyapadiśan pārtho varjayan sūtajaṃ raṇe ||51||

52sa kathaṃ nihato vīraḥ pārthena paravīrahā
|
rathabhaṅgo na cettasya dhanurvā na vyaśīryata ||52||

53na cedastrāṇi nirṇeśuḥ sa kathaṃ nihataḥ paraiḥ
|
ko hi śakto raṇe karṇaṃ vidhunvānaṃ mahaddhanuḥ ||53||

54vimuñcantaṃ śarān ghorān divyānyastrāṇi cāhave
|
jetuṃ puruṣaśārdūlaṃ śārdūlamiva veginam ||54||

55dhruvaṃ tasya dhanuśchinnaṃ ratho vā'pi mahīṅgataḥ
|
astrāṇi vā pranaṣṭāni yathā śaṃsasi me hatam ||55||

56na hyanyadapi paśyāmi kāraṇaṃ tasya nāśane
|
na hanmi phālgunaṃ yāvattāvatpādau na dhāvaye ||56||

57iti yasya mahāghoraṃ vratamāsīnmahātmanaḥ
|
yasya bhīto raṇe nidrāṃ dharmarājo yudhiṣṭhiraḥ ||57||

58trayodaśa samā nityaṃ nābhajatpuruṣarṣabhaḥ
|
yasya vīryavato vīryamupāśritya mahātmanaḥ ||58||

59mama putraḥ sabhāṃ bhāryāṃ pāṇḍūnāṃ nītavānbalāt
|
tatrāpi ca sabhāmadhye pāṇḍavānāṃ ca paśyatām ||59||

60dāsabhāryeti pāñcālīmabravītkurusannidhau
|
na santi patayaḥ kṛṣṇe sarve ṣaṇḍhatilaiḥ samāḥ ||60||

61upatiṣṭhasva bhartāramanyaṃ vā varavarṇini
|
ityevaṃ yaḥ purā vāco rūkṣāḥ saṃśrāvayan ruṣā ||61||

62sabhāyāṃ sūtajaḥ kṛṣṇāṃ sa kathaṃ nihataḥ paraiḥ
|
yadi bhīṣmo raṇaślāghī droṇo vā yudhi durmadaḥ ||62||

63na haniṣyati kaunteyānpakṣapātātsuyodhana
|
sarvāneva haniṣyāmi vyetu te mānaso jvaraḥ ||63||

64kiṃ kariṣyati gāṇḍīvamakṣayyau ca maheṣudhī
|
snigdhacandanadigdhasya maccharasyābhidhāvataḥ ||64||

65sa nūnamṛṣabhaskandho hyarjunena kathaṃ hataḥ
|
yaśca gāṇḍīvamuktānāṃ sparśasugramacintayan ||65||

66apatirhyasi kṛṣṇeti bruvanpārthānavaikṣata
|
yasya nāsīdbhayaṃ pārthaiḥ saputraiḥ sajanārdanaiḥ ||66||

67svavāhubalamāśritya muhūrtamapi sañjaya
|
tasya nāhaṃ vadhaṃ manye devairapi savāsavaiḥ ||67||

68pratīpamabhidhāvadbhiḥ kiṃpunastāta pāṇḍavaiḥ
|
na hi jyāṃ saṃspṛśānasya talatre vā'pi gṛhṇataḥ ||68||

69pumānādhiratheḥ sthātuṃ kaścitpramukhato'rhati
|
api syānmedinī hīnā somasūryaprabhāṃśubhiḥ ||69||

70na vadhaḥ puruṣendrasya saṃyugeṣvapalāyinaḥ
|
yena mandaḥ sahāyena bhrātrā duḥśāsanena ca ||70||

71vāsudevasya durbuddhiḥ pratyākhyānamarocata
|
sa nūnaṃ vṛṣabhaskandhaṃ karṇaṃ dṛṣṭvā nipātitam ||71||

72duḥśāsanaṃ ca nihataṃ manye śocati putrakaḥ
|
hataṃ vaikartanaṃ śrutvā dvairathe savyasācinā ||72||

73jayataḥ pāṇḍavān dṛṣṭvā kiṃsvidduryodhano'bravīt
|
durmarṣaṇaṃ hataṃ dṛṣṭvā vṛṣasenaṃ ca saṃyuge ||73||

74prabhagnaṃ ca valaṃ dṛṣṭvā vadhyamānaṃ mahārathaiḥ
|
parāṅmukhāṃśca rājñastu palāyanaparāyaṇān ||74||

75vidrutān rathino dṛṣṭvā manye śocati putrakaḥ
|
aneyaścābhimānī ca durbuddhirajitendriyaḥ ||75||

76hatotsāhaṃ balaṃ dṛṣṭvā kiṃsvidduryodhano'bravīt
|
svayaṃ vairaṃ mahatkṛtvā vāryamāṇaḥ suhṛdgaṇaiḥ ||76||

77pradhane hatabhūyiṣṭhaiḥ kiṃsvidduryodhano'bravīt
|
bhrātaraṃ nihataṃ dṛṣṭvā bhīmasenena saṃyuge ||77||

78rudhire pīyamāne ca kiṃdvidduryodhano'bravīt
|
saha gāndhārarājena sabhāyāṃ yadabhāṣata ||78||

79karṇo'rjunaṃ raṇe hantā hate tasmin kimabravīt
|
dyūtaṃ kṛtvā purā hṛṣṭo vañcayitvā ca pāṇḍavān ||79||

80śakuniḥ saubalastāta hate karṇe kimabravīt
|
kṛtavarmā maheṣvāsaḥ sātvatānāṃ mahārathaḥ ||80||

81hataṃ vaikartanaṃ dṛṣṭvā hārdikyaḥ kimabhāṣata
|
brāhmaṇāḥ kṣatriyā vaiśyā yasya śikṣāmupāsate ||81||

82dhanurvedaṃ cikīrṣanto droṇaputrasya dhīmataḥ
|
yuvā rūpeṇa saṃpanno darśanīyo mahāyaśāḥ ||82||

83aśvatthāmā hate karṇe kimabhāṣata sañjaya
|
ācāryo yo dhanurvede gautamo rathasattamaḥ ||83||

84kṛpaḥ śāradvatastāta hate karṇe kimabravīt
|
madrarājo maheṣvāsaḥ śalyaḥ samiti śobhanaḥ ||84||

85dṛṣṭvā vinihataṃ karṇaṃ sārathye rathināṃ varaḥ
|
kimabhāṣata sauvīro madrāṇāmadhipo balī ||85||

86dṛṣṭvā vinihataṃ sarve yodhā vāraṇadurjayāḥ
|
ye ca kecana rājānaḥ pṛthivyāṃ yoddhumāgatāḥ
|
vaikartanaṃ hataṃ dṛṣṭvā kānyabhāṣanta sañjaya ||86||

87droṇe tu nihate vīre rathavyāghre nararṣabhe
|
ke vā mukhamanīkānāmāsan sañjaya bhāgaśaḥ ||87||

88madrarājaḥ kathaṃ śalyo niyukto rathināṃ varaḥ
|
vaikartanasya sārathye tanmamācakṣva sañjaya ||88||

89ke'rakṣan dakṣiṇaṃ cakraṃ sūtaputrasya yudhyataḥ
|
vāmaṃ cakraṃ rarakṣurvā ke vā vīrasya pṛṣṭhataḥ ||89||

90ke karṇaṃ na jahuḥ śūrāḥ ke kṣudrāḥ prādravaṃstataḥ
|
kathaṃ ca vaḥ sametānāṃ hataḥ karṇo mahārathaḥ ||90||

91pāṇḍavāśca svayaṃ śūrāḥ pratyudīyurmahārathāḥ
|
sṛjantaḥ śaravarṣāṇi vāridhārā ivāmbudāḥ ||91||

92sa ca sarpamukho divyo maheṣupravarastadā
|
vyarthaḥ kathaṃ samabhavattanmamācakṣva sañjaya ||92||

93māmakasyāsya sainyasya hatotsedhasya sañjaya
|
avaśeṣaṃ na paśyāmi kakude mṛdite sati ||93||

94tau hi vīrau maheṣvāsau madarthe tyaktajīvitau
|
bhīṣmadroṇau hatau śrutvā ko nvartho jīvitena me ||94||

95punaḥ punarna mṛṣyāmi hataṃ karṇaṃ ca pāṇḍavaiḥ
|
yasya bāhvorbalaṃ tulyaṃ kuñjarāṇāṃ śataṃ śataiḥ ||95||

96droṇe hate ca yadvṛttaṃ kauravāṇāṃ paraiḥ saha
|
saṃgrāme naravīrāṇāṃ tanmamācakṣva sañjaya ||96||

97yathā karṇaśca kaunteyaiḥ saha yuddhamayojayat
|
tathā ca dviṣatāṃ hantā raṇe śāntastaducyatām ||97||
