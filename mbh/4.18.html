Mahābhārata 4.18 (Virāṭaparvan)

1draupadyuvāca
|
aśocyatvaṃ kutastasyā yasyā bhartā yudhiṣṭhiraḥ
|
jānan sarvāṇi duḥkhāni kiṃ māṃ tvaṃ paripṛcchasi ||1||

2yanmāṃ dāsīpravādena prātikāmī tadā'nayat
|
sabhāpariṣado madhye tanmāṃ dahati bhārata ||2||

3pārthivasya sutā nāma kā nu jīvati mādṛśī
|
anubhūyedṛśaṃ duḥkhamanyatra draupadīṃ prabho ||3||

4vanavāsagatāyāśca saindhavena durātmanā
|
parāmarśo dvirtāyo vai soḍhumutsahate tu kā ||4||

5matsyarājñaḥ samakṣaṃ tu tasya dhūrtasya paśyataḥ
|
kīcakena parāmṛṣṭā kā nu jīvati mādṛśī ||5||

6evaṃ bahuvidhaiḥ kleśaiḥ kliśyamānāṃ ca bhārata
|
na māṃ jānāsi kaunteya kiṃ phalaṃ jīvitena me ||6||

7yo'yaṃ rājño virāṭasya kīcako nāma bhārata
|
senānīḥ puruṣavyāghra śyālaḥ paramadurmatiḥ ||7||

8sa māṃ sairandhriveṣeṇa vasantīṃ rājaveśmani
|
nityamevāha duṣṭātmā bhāryā mama bhaveti vai ||8||

9tenopamantryamāṇāyā vadhārheṇa sapatnahan
|
kāleneva phalaṃ pakkaṃ hṛdayaṃ me vidīryate ||9||

10bhrātaraṃ ca vigarhasva jyeṣṭhaṃ durdyūtadevinam
|
yasyā'smi karmaṇā prāptā duḥkhametadanantakam ||10||

11ko hi rājyaṃ parityajya sarvasvaṃ cātmanā saha
|
pravrajyāyaiva dīvyeta vinā durdyūtadevinam ||11||

12yadi niṣkasahasreṇa yaccānyat sāravaddhanam
|
sāyaṃprātaradeviṣyadapi saṃvatsarān bahūn ||12||

13rukmaṃ hiraṇyaṃ vāsāṃsi yānaṃ yugyamajāvikam
|
aśvāśvatarasaṅghāṃśca na jātu kṣayamāvahet ||13||

14so'yaṃ dyūtapravādena śriyaḥ pratyavaropitaḥ
|
tūṣṇīmāste yathā mūḍhaḥ svāni karmāṇi cintayan ||14||

15daśa nāgasahastrāṇi hayānāṃ hemamālinām
|
yaṃ yāntamanuyāntīha so'yaṃ dyūtena jīvati ||15||

16rathāḥ śatasahasrāṇi nṛpāṇāmamitaujasām
|
upāsanta mahārājamindraprasthe yudhiṣṭhiram ||16||

17śataṃ dāsīsahasrāṇāṃ yasya nityaṃ mahānase
|
pātrīhastaṃ divārātramatithīn bhojayatyuta ||17||

18eṣa niṣkasahastrāṇi pradāya dadatāṃ varaḥ
|
dyūtajena hyanarthena mahatā samupāśritaḥ ||18||

19enaṃ hi svarasaṃpannā bahavaḥ sūtamāgadhāḥ
|
sāyaṃprātarupātiṣṭhansumṛṣṭamaṇikuṇḍalāḥ ||19||

20sahastramṛṣayo yasya nityamāsan sabhāsadaḥ
|
tapaḥśrutopasaṃpannāḥ sarvakāmairupasthitāḥ ||20||

21aṣṭāśītisahasrāṇi snātakā gṛhamedhinaḥ
|
triṃśaddāsīka ekaiko yān bibharti yudhiṣṭhiraḥ ||21||

22apratigrāhiṇāṃ caiva yatīnāmūrdharetasām
|
daśa cāpi sahasrāṇi so'yamāste nareśvaraḥ ||22||

23ānṛśaṃsyamanukrośaṃ saṃvibhāgastathaiva ca
|
yasminnetāni sarvāṇi so'yamāste nareśvaraḥ ||23||

24andhānvṛddhāṃstathā'nāthānbālān rāṣṭreṣu durgatān
|
bibharti vividhān rājā dhṛtimān satyavikramaḥ
|
saṃvibhāgamanā nityamānṛśaṃsyādyudhiṣṭhiraḥ ||||24||

25sa eṣa nirayaṃ prāpto matsyasya paricārakaḥ
|
sabhāyāṃ devitā rājñaḥ kaṅko būte yudhiṣṭhiraḥ ||25||

26indraprasthe nivasataḥ samaye yasya pārthivāḥ
|
āsan balibhṛtaḥ sarve so'dyānyairbhūtimicchati ||26||

27pārthivāḥ pṛthivīpālā yasyāsan vaśavartinaḥ
|
sa vaśe vivaśo rājā pareṣāmadya vartate ||27||

28pratāpya pṛthivīṃ sarvāṃ raśmimāniva tejasā
|
so'yaṃ rājño virāṭasya sabhāstāro yudhiṣṭhiraḥ ||28||

29yamupāsanta rājānaḥ sabhāyāmṛṣibhiḥ saha
|
tamupāsīnamadyānyaṃ paśya pāṇḍava pāṇḍavam ||29||

30sadasyaṃ samupāsīnaṃ parasya priyavādinam
|
dṛṣṭvā yudhiṣṭhiraṃ kopo vardhate māmasaṃśayam ||30||

31atadarhaṃ mahāprājñaṃ jīvitārthe'bhisaṃsthitam
|
dṛṣṭvā kasya na duḥkhaṃ syāddharmātmānaṃ yudhiṣṭhiram ||31||

32upāste sma sabhāyāṃ yaṃ kṛtsnā vīra vasundharā
|
tamupāsīnamapyanyaṃ paśya bhārata bhāratam ||32||

33evaṃ bahuvidhairduḥkhaiḥ pīḍyamānāmanāthavat
|
śokasāgaramadhyasthāṃ kiṃ māṃ bhīma na paśyasi ||33||
