Mahābhārata 13.166 (Anuśāsanaparvan)

1janamejaya uvāca|
śaratalpagate bhīṣme kauravāṇāṃ dhuraṃdhare
|
śayāne vīraśayane pāṇḍavaiḥ samupasthite ||1||

2yudhiṣṭhiro mahāprājño mama pūrvapitāmahaḥ
|
dharmāṇāmāgamaṃ śrutvā viditvā sarvasaṃśayān ||2||

3dānānāṃ ca vidhiṃ śrutvā cchinnadharmārthasaṃśayaḥ
|
yadanyadakarodvipra tanme śaṃsitumarhasi ||3||

4vaiśampāyana uvāca|
abhūnmuhūrtaṃ stimitaṃ sarvaṃ tadrājamaṇḍalam
|
tuṣṇīṃbhūte tatastasminpaṭe citramivārpitam ||4||

5muhūrtamiva ca dhyātvā vyāsaḥ satyavatīsutaḥ
|
nṛpaṃ śayānaṃ gāṅgeyamida‌māha vacastadā ||5||

6rājanprakṛtimāpannaḥ kururājo yudhiṣṭhiraḥ
|
sahito bhrātṛbhiḥ sarvaiḥ pārthivaiścānuyāyibhiḥ ||6||

7upāste tvāṃ naravyāghra saha kṛṣṇena dhīmatā
|
tamimaṃ purayānāya samanujñātumarhasi ||7||

8evamukto bhagavatā vyāsena pṛthivīpatiḥ
|
yudhiṣṭhiraṃ sahāmātyamanujajñe nadīsutaḥ ||8||

9uvāca cainaṃ madhuraṃ nṛpaṃ śāntanavo nṛpaḥ
|
praviśasva sva purīṃ rājanvyetu te mānaso jvaraḥ ||9||

10yajasva vividhairyajñerbahvannaiḥ svāptadakṣiṇaiḥ
|
yayātiriva rājendra śraddhādamapuraḥsaraḥ ||10||

11kṣatradharmarataḥ pārtha pitṝndevāṃśca tarpaya
|
śreyasā yokṣyase caiva vyetu te mānaso jvaraḥ ||11||

12rañjayasva prajāḥ sarvāḥ prakṛtīḥ parisāṃtvaya
|
suhṛdaḥ phalasatkārairarcayasva yathārhataḥ ||12||

13anu tvāṃ tāta jīvantu mitrāṇi suhṛdastathā
|
caityasthāne sthitaṃ vṛkṣaṃ phalavantamiva dvijāḥ ||13||

14āgantavyaṃ ca bhavatā samaye mama pārthiva
|
vinivṛtte dinakare pravṛtte cottarāyaṇe ||14||

15tathetyuktvā ca kaunteyaḥ sobhivādya pitāmaham
|
prayayau saparīvāro nagaraṃ nāgasāhvayam ||15||

16dhṛtarāṣṭraṃ puraskṛtya gāndhārīṃ ca pativratām
|
saha tairṛṣibhiḥ sarvairbhrātṛbhiḥ keśavena ca ||16||

17paurajānapadaiścaiva mantrivṛddhaiśca pārthiva
|
praviveśa kuruśreṣṭhaḥ puraṃ vāraṇasāhvayam ||17||
