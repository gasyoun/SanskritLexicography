Mahābhārata 7.139 (Droṇaparvan)

1sañjaya uvāca|
tataḥ karṇo mahārāja bhīmaṃ viddhā tribhiḥ śaraiḥ
|
mumoca śaravarṣāṇi vicitrāṇi bahūni ca ||1||

2vadhyamāno mahābāhuḥ sūtaputreṇa pāṇḍavaḥ
|
na vivyathe bhīmaseno bhidyamāna ivācalaḥ ||2||

3sa karṇaṃ karṇinā karṇe pītena niśitena ca
|
vivyādha subhṛśaṃ saṃkhye tailadhautena māriṣa ||3||

4sa kuṇḍalaṃ mahaccāru karṇasyāpātayadbhuvi
|
tapanīyaṃ mahārāja dīptaṃ jyotirivāmbarāt ||4||

5athāpareṇa bhallena sūtaputraṃ stanāntare
|
ājaghāna bhṛśaṃ kruddho hasanniva ghṛkodaraḥ ||5||

6punarasya tvaran bhīmo nārācān daśa bhārata
|
raṇe praiṣīnmahābāhurnirmuktāśīviṣopamān ||6||

7te lalāṭaṃ vinirmidya sūtaputrasya bhārata
|
viviśuścoditāstena valmīkamiva pannagāḥ ||7||

8lalāṭasthaistato bāṇaiḥ sūtaputro vyarocata
|
nīlotpalamayīṃ mālāṃ dhārayanvai yathā purā ||8||

9so'tividdho bhṛśaṃ karṇaḥ pāṇḍavena tarasvinā
|
rathakūbaramālaṃbya nyamīlayata locane ||9||

10sa muhūrtātpunaḥ saṃjñāṃ lebhe karṇaḥ paraṃtapaḥ
|
rudhirokṣitasaṃrvāgaḥ krodhamāhārayatparam ||10||

11tataḥ kruddho raṇe karṇaḥ pīḍito dṛḍhadhanvanā
|
vegaṃ cakre mahāvego bhīmasenarathaṃ prati ||11||

12tasmai karṇaḥ śataṃ rājanniṣūṇāṃ gārdhravāsasām
|
amarṣī balavān kruddhaḥ preṣayāmāsa bhārata ||12||

13tataḥ prāsṛjadugrāṇi śaravarṣāṇi pāṇḍavaḥ
|
samare tamanādṛtya tasya vīryamacintayan ||13||

14karṇastato mahārāja pāṇḍavaṃ navabhiḥ śaraiḥ
|
ājaghānorasi kruddhaḥ kruddharūpaṃ parantapa ||14||

15tāvubhau naraśārdūlau śārdūlāviva daṃṣṭriṇau
|
jīmūtāviva cānyonyaṃ pravavarṣaturāhave ||15||

16talaśabdaravaiścaiva trāsayetāṃ parasparam
|
śarajālaiśca vividhaistrāsayāmāsaturmṛdhe ||16||

17anyonyaṃ samare kruddhau kṛtapratikṛtaiṣiṇau
|
tato bhīmo mahābāhuḥ sūtaputrasya bhārata ||17||

18kṣurapreṇa dhanuśchittvā nanāda paravīrahā
|
tadapāsya dhanuśchinnaṃ sūtaputro mahārathaḥ ||18||

19anyatkārmukamādatta bhāraghnaṃ vegavattaram
|
tadapyatha nimeṣārdhāccicchedāsya vṛkodaraḥ ||19||

20tṛtīyaṃ ca caturthaṃ ca pañcamaṃ ṣaṣṭhameva hi
|
saptamaṃ cāṣṭamaṃ caiva navamaṃ daśamaṃ tathā ||20||

21ekādaśaṃ dvādaśaṃ ca trayodaśamathāpi ca
|
caturdaśaṃ pañcadaśaṃ ṣoḍaśaṃ ca vṛkodaraḥ ||21||

22tathā saptadaśaṃ vegādaṣṭādaśamathāpi vā
|
bahūni bhīmaściccheda karṇasyaivaṃ dhanūṃṣi hi ||22||

23nimeṣārdhāttataḥ karṇo dhanurhasto vyatiṣṭhata
|
dṛṣṭvā sa kuru-sauvīra-sindhuvīrabalakṣayam ||23||

24savarmadhvajaśastraiśca patitaiḥ saṃvṛtāṃ mahīm
|
hastyaśvarathadehāṃśca gatāsunprekṣya sarvaśaḥ ||24||

25sūtaputrasya saṃraṃbhāddīptaṃ vapurajāyata
|
sa visphārya mahaccāpaṃ kārtasvaravibhūṣitam ||25||

26bhīmaṃ praikṣata rādheyo ghoraṃ ghoreṇa cakṣuṣā
|
tataḥ kruddhaḥ śarānasyansūtaputro vyarocata ||26||

27madhyaṃdinagato'rciṣmāñśaradīva divākaraḥ
|
marīcivikacasyeva rājan bhānumato vapuḥ ||27||

28āsīdādhiratherghoraṃ vapuḥ śaraśatācitam
|
karābhyāmādadānasya saṃdadhānasya cāśugān ||28||

29karṣato muñcato bāṇānnāntaraṃ dadṛśe raṇe
|
agnicakropamaṃ ghoraṃ maṇḍalīkṛtamāyudham ||29||

30karṇasyāsīnmahīpāla savyadakṣiṇamasyataḥ
|
svarṇapuṃkhāḥ suniśitāḥ karṇacāpacyutāḥ śarāḥ ||30||

31prācchādayanmahārāja diśaḥ sūryasya ca prabhāḥ
|
tataḥ kanakapuṅkhānāṃ śarāṇāṃ nataparvaṇām ||31||

32dhanuścyutānāṃ viyati dadṛśe bahudhā vrajaḥ
|
bāṇāsanādādhiratheḥ prabhavanti sma sāyakāḥ ||32||

33śreṇīkṛtā vyarocanta rājan krauñcā ivāmbare
|
gārdhapatrāñśilādhautānkārtasvaravibhūṣitān ||33||

34mahāvegānpradīptāgrānmumocādhirathiḥ śarān
|
te tu cāpabaloddhūtāḥ śātakumbhavibhūṣitāḥ ||34||

35ajastramapatanbāṇā bhīmasenarathaṃ prati
|
te vyomni rukmavikṛtā vyakāśanta sahasraśaḥ ||35||

36śalabhānāmiva vrātāḥ śarāḥ karṇasamīritāḥ
|
cāpādādhiratherbāṇāḥ prapatantaścakāśire ||36||

37eko dīrgha ivātyarthamākāśe saṃsthitaḥ śaraḥ
|
parvataṃ vāridhārābhiśchādayanniva toyadaḥ ||37||

38karṇaḥ prācchādayatkruddho bhīmaṃ sāyakavṛṣṭibhiḥ
|
tatra bhārata bhīmasya balaṃ vīryaṃ parākramam
|
vyavasāyaṃ ca putrāste dadṛśuḥ sahasainikāḥ ||38||

39tāṃ samudramivodbhūtāṃ śaravṛṣṭiṃ samutthitām
|
acintayitvā bhīmastu kruddhaḥ karṇamupādravat ||39||

40rukmapṛṣṭhaṃ mahaccāpaṃ bhīmasyāsīdviśāṃpate
|
ākarṣānmaṇḍalībhūtaṃ śakracāpamivāparam ||40||

41tasmāccharāḥ prādurāsanpūrayanta ivāmbaram
|
suvarṇapuṅkhairbhīmena sāyakairnataparvabhiḥ ||41||

42gagane racitā mālā kāñcanīva vyarocata
|
||42||

43tato vyomni viṣaktāni śarajālāni bhāgaśaḥ
|
āhatāni vyaśīryanta bhīmasenasya patribhiḥ||43||

44karṇasya śarajālaughairbhīmasenasya cobhayoḥ
|
agnisphuliṅgasaṃsparśairañjogatibhirāhave ||44||

45taistaiḥ kanakapuṅkhānāṃ dyaurāsītsaṃvṛtā vrajaiḥ
|
na sma sūryastadā bhāti na sma vāti samīraṇaḥ ||45||

46śarajālāvṛte vyomni na prājñāyata kiñcana
|
sa bhīmaṃ chādayanbāṇaiḥ sūtaputraḥ pṛthagvidhaiḥ ||46||

47upārohadanādṛtya tasya vīryaṃ mahātmanaḥ
|
tayorvisṛjatostatra śarajālāni māriṣa ||47||

48vāyubhūtānyadṛśyanta saṃsaktānītaretaram
|
anyonyaśarasaṃsparśāttayormanujasiṃhayoḥ ||48||

49ākāśe bharataśreṣṭha pāvakaḥ samajāyata
|
tathā karṇaḥ śitān bāṇān karmāraparimārjitān ||49||

50suvarṇavikṛtān kruddhaḥ prāhiṇodvadhakāṃkṣayā
|
tānantarikṣe viśikhaistridhaikaikamaśātayat ||50||

51viśeṣayansūtaputraṃ bhīmastiṣṭheti cābravīt
|
punaścāsṛjadugrāṇi śaravarṣāṇi pāṇḍavaḥ ||51||

52amarṣī balavān kruddho didhakṣanniva pāvakaḥ
|
tataścaṭacaṭāśabdo godhāghātādabhūttayoḥ ||52||

53talaśabdaśca sumahān siṃhanādaśca bhairavaḥ
|
rathanemininādaśca jyāśabdaścaiva dāruṇaḥ ||53||

54yodhā vyupāramanyuddhāddidṛkṣantaḥ parākramam
|
karṇapāṇḍavayo rājan parasparavadhaiṣiṇoḥ ||54||

55devarṣisiddhagandharvāḥ sādhu sādhvityapūjayan
|
mumucuḥ puṣpavarṣaṃ ca vidyādharagaṇāstathā ||55||

56tato bhīmo mahābāhuḥ saṃrambhī dṛḍhavikramaḥ
|
astrairastrāṇi saṃvārya śarairvivyādha sūtajam ||56||

57karṇo'pi bhīmasenasya nivāryeṣūnmahābalaḥ
|
prāhiṇonnava nārācānāśīviṣasamān raṇe ||57||

58tāvadbhiratha tān bhīmo vyomni ciccheda patribhiḥ
|
nārācān sutaputrasya tiṣṭha tiṣṭheti cābravīt ||58||

59tato bhīmo mahābāhuḥ śaraṃ kruddhāntakopamam
|
mumocādhirathervīro yamadaṇḍamivāparam ||59||

60tamāpatantaṃ ciccheda rādheyaḥ prahasanniva
|
tribhiḥ śaraiḥ śaraṃ rājan pāṇḍavasya pratāpavān ||60||

61punaścāsṛjadugrāṇi śaravarṣāṇi pāṇḍavaḥ
|
tasya tānyādade karṇaḥ sarvāṇyastrāṇyabhītavat ||61||

62yudhyamānasya bhīmasya sūtaputro'stramāyayā
|
tasyeṣudhī dhanurjyāṃ ca bāṇaiḥ sannataparvabhiḥ ||62||

63raśmīnyokrāṇi cāśvānāṃ kruddhaḥ karṇo'cchinanmṛdhe
|
tasyāśvāṃśca punarhatvā sūtaṃ vivyādha pañcabhiḥ ||63||

64so'pasṛtya taṃ sūto yudhāmanyo rathaṃ yayau
|
vihasanniva bhīmasya kruddhaḥ kālānaladyutiḥ ||64||

65dhvajaṃ ciccheda rādheyaḥ patākāṃ ca vyapātayat
|
sa vidhanvā mahābāhuratha śaktiṃ parāmṛśat ||65||

66tāṃ vyavāsṛjadāvidhya kruddhaḥ karṇarathaṃ prati
|
tāmādhirathirāyastaḥ śaktiṃ kāñcanabhūṣaṇām ||66||

67āpatantīṃ maholkābhāṃ ciccheda daśabhiḥ śaraiḥ
|
sā'pataddaśadhā chinnā karṇasya niśitaiḥ śaraiḥ ||67||

68asyataḥ sūtaputrasya mitrārthe citrayodhinaḥ
|
sacarmādatta kaunteyo jātarūpapariṣkṛtam ||68||

69khaḍgaṃ cānyataraprepsurmṛtyoragre jayasya vā
|
tadasya tarasā kruddho vyadhamaccarma suprabham ||69||

70śarairbahubhiratyugraiḥ prahasanniva bhārata
|
sa vicarmā mahārāja virathaḥ krodhamūrcchitaḥ ||70||

71asiṃ prāsṛjadāvidhya tvaran karṇarathaṃ prati
|
sa dhanuḥ sūtaputrasya sajyaṃ chittvā mahānasiḥ ||71||

72papāta bhuvi rājendra kruddhaḥ sarpa ivāmbarāt
|
tataḥ prahasyādhirathiranyadādāya kārmukam ||72||

73śatrughnaṃ samare kruddho dṛḍhajyaṃ vegavattaram
|
vyāyacchatsa śarānkarṇaḥ kuntīputrajighāṃsayā ||73||

74sahasraśo mahārāja rukmapuṅkhānsutejanān
|
sa vadhyamāno balavān karṇacāpacyutaiḥ śaraiḥ ||74||

75vaihāyasaṃ prākramadvai karṇasya vyathayanmanaḥ
|
sa tasya caritaṃ dṛṣṭvā saṃgrāme vijayaiṣiṇaḥ ||75||

76layamāsthāya rādheyo bhīmasenamavañcayat
|
taṃ ca dṛṣṭvā rathopasthe nilīnaṃ vyathitendriyam ||76||

77dhvajamasya samāsādya tasthau bhīmo mahītale
|
tadasya kuravaḥ sarve cāraṇāścābhyapūjayan ||77||

78yadiyeṣa rathātkarṇaṃ hartuṃ tārkṣya ivoragama
|
sa cchinnadhanvā virathaḥ svadharmamanupālayan ||78||

79svarathaṃ pṛṣṭhataḥ kṛtvā yuddhāyaiva vyavasthitaḥ
|
tadvihatyāsya rādheyastata enaṃ samabhyayāt ||79||

80saṃrambhātpāṇḍavaṃ saṃkhye yuddhāya samupasthitam
|
tau sametau mahārāja spardhamānau mahābalau ||80||

81jīmūtāviva dharmānte garjamānau nararṣabhau
|
tayorāsītsaṃprahāraḥ kruddhayornarasiṃhayoḥ ||81||

82amṛṣyamāṇayoḥ saṃkhye devadānavayoriva
|
kṣīṇaśastrastu kaunteyaḥ karṇena samabhidrutaḥ ||82||

83dṛṣṭvā'rjunahatānnāgān patitān parvatopamān
|
rathamārgavighātārthaṃ vyāyudhaḥ praviveśa ha ||83||

84hastināṃ vrajamāsādya rathadurgaṃ praviśya ca
|
pāṇḍavo jīvitākāṃkṣī rādheyaṃ nābhyahārayat ||84||

85vyavasthānamathākāṅkṣandhanañjayaśarairhatam
|
udyamya kuñjaraṃ pārthastasthau parapurañjayaḥ ||85||

86mahauṣadhisamāyuktaṃ hanūmāniva parvatam
|
tamasya viśikhaiḥ karṇo vyadhamatkuñjaraṃ punaḥ ||86||

87hastyaṃgānyatha karṇāya prāhiṇotpāṇḍunandanaḥ
|
cakrāṇyaśvāṃstathā cānyadyadyatpaśyati bhūtale ||87||

88tattadādāya cikṣepa kruddhaḥ karṇāya pāṇḍavaḥ
|
tadasya sarvaṃ ciccheda kṣiptaṃ kṣiptaṃ śitaiḥ śaraiḥ ||88||

89bhīmo'pi muṣṭimudyamya vajragarbhāṃ sudāruṇām
|
hantumaicchatsūtaputraṃ saṃsmarannarjunaṃ kṣaṇāt ||89||

90śakto'pi nāvadhītkarṇaṃ samarthaḥ pāṇḍunandanaḥ
|
rakṣamāṇaḥ pratijñāṃ tāṃ yā kṛtā savyasācinā ||90||

91tamevaṃ vyākulaṃ bhīmaṃ bhūyo bhūyaḥ śitaiḥ śaraiḥ
|
mūrcchayā'bhiparītāṅgamakarotsūtanandanaḥ ||91||

92vyāyudhaṃ nāvadhīccainaṃ karṇaḥ kuntyā vacaḥ smaran
|
dhanuṣo'greṇa taṃ karṇaḥ so'bhidrutya parāmṛśat ||92||

93dhanuṣā spṛṣṭamātreṇa kruddhaḥ sarpa iva śvasan
|
ācchidya sa dhanustasya karṇaṃ mūrdhanyatāḍayat ||93||

94tāḍito bhīmasenena krodhādāraktalocanaḥ
|
vihasanniva rādheyo vākyametaduvāca ha ||94||

95punaḥ punastūbaraka mūḍha audariketi ca
|
akṛtāstraka mā yotsīrbāla saṃgrāmakātara ||95||

96yatra bhojyaṃ bahuvidhaṃ bhakṣyaṃ peyaṃ ca pāṇḍava
|
tatra tvaṃ durmate yogyo na yuddheṣu kadācana ||96||

97mūla-puṣpa-phalāhāro vrateṣu niyameṣu ca
|
ucitastvaṃ vane bhīma na tvaṃ yuddhaviśāradaḥ ||97||

98kva yuddhaṃ kva munitvaṃ ca vanaṃ gaccha vṛkodara
|
na tvaṃ yuddhocitastāta vanavāsaratirbhavān ||98||

99sūdān bhṛtyajanān dāsāṃstvaṃ gṛhe tvarayanbhṛśaṃ
|
yogyastāḍayituṃ krodhādbhojanārthaṃ vṛkodara ||99||

100munirbhūtvā'thavā bhīma phalānyādatsva durmate
|
vanāya vraja kaunteya na tvaṃ yuddhaviśāradaḥ ||100||

101phalamūlāśane śaktastvaṃ tathā'tithipūjane
|
na tvāṃ śastrasamudyoge yogyaṃ manye vṛkodara ||101||

102kaumāre yāni vṛttāni vipriyāṇi viśāṃpate
|
tāni sarvāṇi cāpyeva rūkṣāṇyaśrāvayadbhṛśam ||102||

103athainaṃ tatra saṃlīnamaspṛśaddhanuṣā punaḥ
|
prahasaṃśca punarvākyaṃ bhīmamāha vṛṣastadā ||103||

104yoddhavyaṃ māriṣānyatra na yoddhavyaṃ ca mādṛśaiḥ
|
mādṛśairyudhyamānānāmetaccānyacca vidyate ||104||

105gaccha vā yatra tau kṛṣṇau tau tvāṃ rakṣiṣyato raṇe
|
gṛhaṃ vā gaccha kauntaya kiṃ te yuddhena bālaka ||105||

106karṇasya vacanaṃ śrutvā bhīmaseno'tidāruṇaṃ
|
uvāca karṇaṃ prahasan sarveṣāṃ śṛṇvatāṃ vacaḥ ||106||

107jitastvamasakṛdduṣṭa katthase kiṃ vṛthā''tmanā
|
jayājayau mahendrasya loke dṛṣṭau purātanaiḥ ||107||

108mallayuddhaṃ mayā sārdhaṃ kuru duṣkulasaṃbhava
|
mahābalo mahābhogī kīcako nihato yathā ||108||

109tathā tvāṃ ghātayiṣyāmi paśyatsu sarvarājasu
|
bhīmasya matamājñāya karṇo buddhimatāṃ varaḥ ||109||

110virarāma raṇāttasmātpaśyatāṃ sarvadhanvinām
|
evaṃ taṃ virathaṃ kṛtvā karṇo rājan vyakatthayat ||110||

111pramukhe vṛṣṇisiṃhasya pārthasya ca mahātmanaḥ
|
tato rājañśilādhautāñśarān śākhāmṛgadhvajaḥ ||111||

112prāhiṇotsūtaputrāya keśavena pracoditaḥ
|
tataḥ pārthabhujotsṛṣṭāḥ śarāḥ kanakabhūṣaṇāḥ ||112||

113gāṃḍīvaprabhavāḥ karṇaṃ haṃsāḥ krauñcamivāviśan
|
sa bhujaṅgairivāviṣṭairgāṇḍīvapreṣitaiḥ śaraiḥ ||113||

114bhīmasenādapāsedhatsutaputraṃ dhanañjayaḥ
|
sacchinnadhanvā bhīmena dhanañjayaśarāhataḥ||114||

115karṇo bhīmādapāyāsīdrathena mahatā drutam
|
bhīmo'pi sātyakervāhaṃ samāruhya nararṣabhaḥ ||115||

116anvayādbhrātaraṃ saṃkhye pāṇḍavaṃ savyasācinam
|
tataḥ karṇaṃ samuddiśya tvaramāṇo dhanañjayaḥ ||116||

117nārācaṃ krodhatāmrākṣaḥ praiṣīnmṛtyumivāntakaḥ
|
sa garutmānivākāśe prārthayan bhujagottamam ||117||

118nārāco'bhyapatatkarṇaṃ tūrṇaṃ gāṇḍīvacoditaḥ
|
tamantarikṣe nārācaṃ drauṇiściccheda patriṇā ||118||

119dhanañjayabhayātkarṇamujjihīrṣanmahārathaḥ
|
tato drauṇiṃ catuḥṣaṣṭyā vivyādha kupitorjunaḥ ||119||

120śilīmukhairmahārāja mā gāstiṣṭheti cābravīt
|
sa tu mattagajākīrṇamanīkaṃ rathasaṅkulam ||120||

121tūrṇamabhyāviśaddauṇirdhanañjayaśarārditaḥ
|
tataḥ suvarṇapṛṣṭhānāṃ cāpānāṃ kūjatāṃ raṇe ||121||

122śabdaṃ gāṇḍīvaghoṣeṇa kaunteyo'bhyabhavadbalī
|
dhanañjayastathā yāntaṃ pṛṣṭhato drauṇimabhyagāt ||122||

123nātidīrghamivādhvānaṃ śaraiḥ saṃtrāsayanbalam
|
vidārya dehānnārācairnaravāraṇavājinām ||123||

124kaṅkabarhiṇavāsobhirbalaṃ vyadhamadarjunaḥ
|
