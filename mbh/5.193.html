Mahābhārata 5.193 (Udyogaparvan)

1sañjaya uvāca|
prabhātāyāṃ tu śarvaryāṃ punareva sutastava
|
madhye sarvasya sainyasya pitāmahamapṛcchata ||1||

2pāṇḍaveyasya gāṅgeya yadetatsainyamudyatam
|
prabhūtanaranāgāśvaṃ mahārathasamākulam ||2||

3bhīmārjunaprabhṛtibhirmaheṣvāsairmahābalaiḥ
|
lokapālasamairguptaṃ dhṛṣṭadyumnapurogamaiḥ ||3||

4apradhṛṣyamanāvāryamuddhūtamiva sāgaram
|
senāsāgaramakṣobhyamapi devairmahāhave ||4||

5kena kālena gāṅgeya kṣapayethā mahādyute
|
ācāryo vā maheṣvāsaḥ kṛpo vā sumahābalaḥ ||5||

6karṇo vā samaraślāghī drauṇirvā dvijasattamaḥ
|
divyāstraviduṣaḥ sarve bhavanto hi bale mama ||6||

7etadicchāmyahaṃ jñātuṃ paraṃ kautūhalaṃ hi me
|
hṛdi nityaṃ mahābāho vaktumarhasi tanmama ||7||

8bhīṣma uvāca|
anurūpaṃ kuruśreṣṭha tvayyetat pṛthivīpate
|
balābalamamitrāṇāṃ teṣāṃ yadiha pṛcchasi ||8||

9śṛṇu rājan mama raṇe yā śaktiḥ paramā bhavet
|
śastravīrye raṇe yacca bhujayośca mahābhuja ||9||

10ārjavenaiva yuddhena yoddhavya itaro janaḥ
|
māyāyuddhena māyāvī ityetaddharmaniścayaḥ ||10||

11hanyāmahaṃ mahābhāga pāṇḍavānāmanīkinīm
|
divase divase kṛtvā bhāgaṃ prāgāhnikaṃ mama ||11||

12yodhānāṃ daśasāhasraṃ kṛtvā bhāgaṃ mahādyute
|
sahasraṃ rathināmekameṣa bhāgo mato mama ||12||

13anenāhaṃ vidhānena sannaddhaḥ satatotthitaḥ
|
kṣapayeyaṃ mahat sainyaṃ kālenānena bhārata ||13||

14muñceyaṃ yadi vā'strāṇi mahānti samare sthitaḥ
|
śatasāhasraghātīni hanyāṃ māsena bhārata ||14||

15sañjaya uvāca|
śrutvā bhīṣmasya tadvākyaṃ rājā duryodhanastataḥ
|
paryapṛcchata rājendra droṇamaṅgirasāṃ varam ||15||

16ācārya kena kālena pāṇḍuputrasya sainikān
|
nihanyā iti taṃ droṇaḥ pratyuvāca hasanniva ||16||

17sthaviro'smi mahābāho mandaprāṇaviceṣṭitaḥ
|
śastrāgninā nirdaheyaṃ pāṇḍavānāmanīkinīm ||17||

18yathā bhīṣmaḥ śāntanavo māseneti matirmama
|
eṣā me paramā śaktiretanme paramaṃ balam ||18||

19dvābhyāmeva tu māsābhyāṃ kṛpaḥ śāradvato'bravīt
|
drauṇistu daśarātreṇa pratijajñe balakṣayam ||19||

20karṇastu pañcarātreṇa pratijajñe mahāstravit
|
tacchrutvā sūtaputrasya vākyaṃ sāgaragāsutaḥ ||20||

21jahāsa sasvanaṃ hāsaṃ vākyaṃ cedamuvāca ha
|
na hi yāvadraṇe pārthaṃ bāṇaśaṅkhadhanurdharam ||21||

22vāsudevasamāyuktaṃ rathenāyāntamāhave
|
samāgacchasi rādheya tenaivamabhimanyase
|
śakyamevaṃ ca bhūyaśca tvayā vaktuṃ yatheṣṭataḥ ||22||
