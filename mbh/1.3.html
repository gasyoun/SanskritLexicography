Mahābhārata 1.3 (Ādiparvan)

1sautiruvāca|
janamejayaḥ pārikṣitaḥ saha bhrātṛbhiḥ kurukṣetre dīrghasatramupāste
|
tasya bhrātarastrayaḥ śrutasena ugraseno bhīmasena iti
|
teṣu tatsatramupāsīneṣvāgacchatsārameyaḥ||1||

2janamejayasya bhrātṛbhirabhihato rorūyamāṇo mātuḥ samīpamupāgacchat ||2||

3taṃ mātā rorūyamāṇamuvāca
|
kiṃ rodiṣi kenāsyabhihata iti ||3||

4sa evamukto mātaraṃ pratyuvāca janamejayasya bhrātṛbhirabhihato'smīti ||4||

5taṃ mātā pratyuvāca vyaktaṃ tvayā tatrāparāddhaṃ yenātrābhihata iti ||5||

6sa tāṃ punaruvāca nāparādhyāmi kiṃcinnāvekṣe havīṃṣi nāvaliha iti ||6||

7tacchrutvā tasya mātā saramā putraduḥkhārtā tatsatramupāgacchadyatra sa janamejayaḥ saha bhrātṛbhirdīrghasatramupāste ||7||

8sa tayā kruddhayā tatrokto'yaṃ me putro na kiṃcidaparādhyati nāvekṣate havīṃṣi nāvaleḍhi kimarthamabhihata iti ||8||

9na kiṃciduktavantaste sā tānuvāca yasmādayamabhihato'napakārī tasmādadṛṣṭaṃ tvāṃ bhayamāgamiṣyatīti ||9||

10janamejaya evamukto devaśunyā saramayā bhṛśaṃ saṃbhrānto viṣaṇṇaścāsīt ||10||

11sa tasminsatre samāpte hāstinapuraṃ pratyetya purohitamanurūpamanviṣyamāṇaḥparaṃ yatnamakarodyo me pāpakṛtyāṃ śamayediti ||11||

12sa kadācinmṛgayāṃ gataḥ pārikṣito janamejayaḥ kasmiṃścitsvaviṣaya āśramamapaśyat ||12||

13tatra kaścidṛṣirāsāñcakre śrutaśravā nāma
|
tasya tapasyabhirataḥ putra āste somaśravā nāma ||13||

14tasya taṃ putramabhigamya janamejayaḥ pārikṣitaḥ paurohityāya vavre ||14||

15sa namaskṛtya tamṛṣimuvāca bhagavannayaṃ tava putro mama purohito'stviti ||15||

16sa evamuktaḥ pratyuvāca janamejayaṃ bho janamejaya putro'yaṃ mama sarpyāṃ jāto mahātapasvī svādhyāyasaṃpanno mattapovīryasaṃbhṛto macchukraṃ pītavatyāstasyāḥ kukṣau jātaḥ ||16||

17samartho'yaṃ bhavataḥ sarvāḥ pāpakṛtyāḥ śamayitumantareṇa mahādevakṛtyām ||17||

18asya tvekamupāṃśuvrataṃ yadenaṃ kaścidbrāhmaṇaḥ kaṃcidarthamabhiyācettaṃ tasmai dadyādayaṃ yadyetadutsahase tato nayasvainamiti ||18||

19tenaivamukto janamejayastaṃ pratyuvāca bhagavaṃstattathā bhaviṣyatīti ||19||

20sa taṃ purohitamupādāyopāvṛtto bhrātṝnuvāca mayā'yaṃ vṛta upādhyāyo yadayaṃ brūyāttatkāryamavicārayadbhirbhavadbhiriti
|
tenaivamuktā bhrātarastasya tathā cakruḥ sa tathā bhrātṝnsaṃdiśya takṣaśilāṃ pratyabhipratasthe taṃ ca deśaṃ vaśe sthāpayāmāsa ||20||

21etasminnantare kaścidṛṣirdhaumyo nāmāpodastasya śiṣyāstrayo babhūvuḥ ||21||

22upamanyurāruṇirvedaśceti sa ekaṃ śiṣyaṃmāruṇiṃ pāñcālyaṃ preṣayāmāsa gaccha kedārakhaṇḍaṃ badhāneti ||22||

23sa upādhyāyena saṃdiṣṭa āruṇiḥ pāñcālyastatra gatvā tatkedārakhaṇḍaṃ baddhuṃ nāśakat
|
sa kliśyamāno'paśyadupāyaṃ bhavatvevaṃ kariṣyāmīti ||23||

24sa tatra saṃviveśa kedārakhaṇḍe śayāne ca tathā tasmiṃstadudakaṃ tasthau ||24||

25tataḥ kadācidupādhyāya āpodo dhaumyaḥ śiṣyānapṛcchat kva āruṇiḥ pāñcālyo gata iti ||25||

26tau taṃ pratyūcurbhagavaṃstvayaiva preṣito gaccha kedārakhaṇḍaṃ badhāneti
|
sa evamuktastau śiṣyān pratyuvāca tasmāttatra sarve gacchāmo yatra sa gata iti ||26||

27sa tatra gatvā tasyāhvānāya śabdaṃ cakāraḥ
|
bho āruṇe pāñcālya kvāsi vatsaihīti ||27||

28sa tacchrutvā āruṇirupādhyāyavākyaṃ tasmātkedārakhaṇḍātsahasotthāya tamupādhyāyamupatasthe || 28||

29provāca cainamayamasmyatra kedārakhaṇḍe niḥsaramāṇamudakamavāraṇīyaṃ saṃroddhuṃ saṃviṣṭo bhagavacchabdaṃ śrutvaiva sahasā vidārya kedārakhaṇḍaṃ bhavantamupasthitaḥ ||29||

30tadabhivādaye bhagavantamājñāpayatu bhavānkamarthaṃ karavāṇīti || 30||

31sa evamukta upādhyāyaḥpratyuvāca yasmādbhavānkedārakhaṇḍaṃ vidāryotthitastasmāduddālaka eva nāmnā bhavānbhaviṣyatītyupādhyāyenānugṛhītaḥ ||31||

32yasmācca tvayā madvacanamanuṣṭhitaṃ tasmācchreyo'vāpsyasi
|
sarve ca te vedāḥ pratibhāsyanti sarvāṇi ca dharmaśāstrāṇīti ||32||

33sa evamukta upādhyāyeneṣṭaṃ deśaṃ jagāma
|
athāparaḥ śiṣyastasyaivāpodasya dhaumyasyopamanyurnāma||33||

34taṃ copādhyāyaḥ preṣayāmāsa vatsopamanyo gā rakṣasveti ||34||

35sa upādhyāyavacanādarakṣadgāḥ sa cāhani gā rakṣitvā divasakṣaye gurugṛhamāgamyopādhyāyasyāgrataḥ sthitvā namaścakre || 35||

36tamupādhyāyaḥpīvānamapaśyaduvāca cainaṃ vatsopamanyo kena vṛttiṃ kalpayasi pīvānasi dṛḍhamiti ||36||

37sa upādhyāyaṃ pratyuvāca bho bhaikṣyeṇa vṛttiṃ kalpayāmīti||37||

38tamupādhyāyaḥ pratyuvāca mayyanivedya bhaikṣyaṃ nopayoktavyamiti
|
sa tathetyukto bhaikṣyaṃ caritvopādhyāyāya nyavedayat ||38||

39sa tasmādupādhyāyaḥsarvameva bhaikṣyamagṛhṇāt
|
sa tathetyuktaḥ punararakṣadgā ahani rakṣitvā niśāmukhe gurukulamāgamya guroragrataḥ sthitvā namaścakre ||39||

40tamupādhyāyastathāpi pīvānameva dṛṣṭvovāca
|
vatsopamanyo sarvamaśeṣataste bhaikṣyaṃ gṛhṇāmi kenedānīṃ vṛttiṃ kalpayasīti ||40||

41sa evamukta upādhyāyaṃ pratyuvāca
|
bhagavate nivedya pūrvamaparaṃ carāmi tena vṛttiṃ kalpayāmīti||41||

42tamupādhyāyaḥ pratyuvāca naiṣā nyāyyā guruvṛttiranyeṣāmapi bhaikṣyopajīvināṃ vṛttyuparodhaṃ karoṣi ityevaṃ vartamāno lubdho'sīti ||42||

43sa tathetyuktvā gā arakṣat
|
rakṣitvā ca punarupādhyāyagṛhamāgamyopādhyāyasyāgrataḥ sthitvā namaścakre ||43||

44tamupādhyāyastathāpi pīvānameva dṛṣṭvā punaruvāca|
vatsopamanyo ahaṃ te sarvaṃ bhaikṣyaṃ gṛhṇāmi na cānyaccarasi pīvānasi bhṛśaṃ kena vṛttiṃ kalpayasīti||44||

45sa evamuktastamupādhyāyaṃ pratyuvāca
|
bho etāsāṃ gavāṃ payasā vṛttiṃ kalpayāmīti
|
tamuvācopādhyāyo naitannyāyyaṃ paya upayoktuṃ bhavato mayā nābhyanujñātamiti ||45||

46sa tatheti pratijñāya gā rakṣitvā punarupādhyāyagṛhametya guroragrataḥ sthitvā namaścakre ||46||

47tamupādhyāyaḥpīvānameva dṛṣṭvovāca
|
vatsopamanyo bhaikṣyaṃ nāśnāsi na cānyaccarasi payo na pibasi pīvānasi bhṛśaṃ kenedānīṃ vṛttiṃ kalpayasīti ||47||

48sa evamukta upādhyāyaṃ pratyuvāca
|
bhoḥ phenaṃ pibāpi yamime vatsā mātṝṇāṃ stanātpibanta udgiranti ||48||

49tamupādhyāyaḥ pratyuvāca
|
ete tvadanukampayā guṇavanto vatsāḥ prabhūtataraṃ phenamudgiranti
|
tadeṣāmapi vatsānāṃ vṛttyuparodhaṃ karoṣyevaṃ vartamānaḥ
|
phenamapi bhavānna pātumarhatīti
|
sa tatheti pratiśrutya punararakṣadgāḥ ||49||

50tathā pratiṣiddho bhaikṣyaṃ nāśnāti na cānyaccarati payo na pibati phenaṃ nopayuṅkte sa kadācidaraṇye kṣudhārto'rkapatrāṇyabhakṣayat ||50||

51sa tairarkapatrairbhakṣitaiḥ kṣāratiktakaṭurūkṣaistīkṣṇavipākaiścakṣuṣyupahato'ndho babhūva
|
tataḥ so'ndho'pi caṅkramyamāṇaḥ kūpe'patat ||51||

52atha tasminnanāgacchati sūrye cāstācalāvalambini upādhyāyaḥ śiṣyānavocat
|
nāyātyupamanyusta ūcurvanaṃ gato gā rakṣitumiti||52||

53tānāha upādhyāyaḥ mayopamanyuḥ sarvataḥ pratiṣiddhaḥ sa niyataṃ kupitastato nāgacchati ciraṃ tato'nveṣya
ityevamuktvā śiṣyaiḥ sārdhamaraṇyaṃ gatvā tasyāhvānāya śabdaṃ cakāra
|
bho upamanyo kvāsi vatsaihīti ||53||

54sa upādhyāyasya āhvānavacanaṃ śrutvā pratyuvācoccairayamasminkūpe patito'hamiti
|
tamupādhyāyaḥ pratyuvāca kathaṃ tvamasminkūpe patita iti ||54||

55sa upādhyāyaṃ pratyuvāca
|
arkapatrāṇi bhakṣayitvā'ndhībhūtosmyataḥ kūpe patita iti||55||

56tamupādhyāyaḥ pratyuvāca
|
aśvinau stuhi tau devabhiṣajau tvāṃ cakṣuṣmantaṃ kartārāviti
|
sa evamukta upādhyāyenopamanyuraśninau stotumupacakrame devāvaśvinau vāgbhirṛgbhiḥ ||56||

57prapūrvagau pūrvajau citrabhānū girā vā'śaṃsāmi tapasā hyanantau
|
divyau suparṇau virajau vimānāvadhikṣipantau bhuvanāni viśvā (1)||57||

58hiraṇmayau śakunī sāṃparāyau nāsatyadasrau sunasau vaijayantau
|
śuklaṃ vayantau tarasā suvemāvadhivyayantāvasitaṃ vivasvataḥ (2)||58||

59grastāṃ suparṇasya balena vartikāmamuñcatāmaśvinau saubhagāya
|
tāvatsuvṛttāvanamanta māyayā vasattamāgā aruṇā udāvahat (3)||59||

60ṣaṣṭiśca gāvastriśatāśca dhenava ekaṃ vatsaṃ suvate taṃ duhanti
|
nānā goṣṭhā vihitā ekadohanāstāvaśvinau duhato gharmamukthyam (4)||60||

61ekāṃ nābhiṃ saptaśatā arāḥ śritāḥ pradhiṣvanyā viṃśatirarpitā arāḥ
|
anemi cakraṃ parivartate'jaraṃ māyā'śvinau samanakti carṣaṇī (5)||61||

62ekaṃ cakraṃ vartate dvādaśāraṃ ṣaṇṇābhimekākṣamamṛtasya dhāraṇam
|
yasmindevā adhi viśve viṣaktāstāvaśvinau muñcataṃ mā viṣīdatam (6)||62||

63aśvināvindumamṛtaṃ vṛttabhūyau tirodhattāmaśvinau dāsapatnī
|
hitvā girimaśvinau gā mudā carantau tadvṛṣṭimahnā prasthitau balasya (7)||63||

64yuvāṃ diśo janayatho daśāgre samānaṃ mūrdhni rathayānaṃ viyanti
|
tāsāṃ yātamṛṣayo'nuprayānti devā manuṣyāḥ kṣitimācaranti (8)||64||

65yuvāṃ varṇānvikurutho viśvarūpāṃste'dhikṣiyante bhuvanāni viśvā
|
te bhānavo'pyanusṛtāścaranti devā manuṣyāḥ kṣitimācaranti (9)||65||

66tau nāsatyāvaśvinau vāṃ mahe'haṃ srajaṃ ca yāṃ vibhṛthaḥ puṣkarasya
|
tau nāsatvāvamṛtāvṛtāvṛdhāvṛte devāstatprapade na sūte (10)||66||

67mukhena garbhaṃ labhatāṃ yuvānau gatāsuretatprapadena sūte
|
sadyo jāto mātaramatti garbhastāvaśvinau muñcatho jīvase gām (11)||67||

68stotuṃ na śaknomi guṇairbhavantau cakṣurvihīnaḥ pathi saṃpramohaḥ
|
durge'hamasminpatito'smi kūpe yuvāṃ śaraṇyau śaraṇaṃ prapadye (12)||68||

69ityevaṃ tenābhiṣṭutāvaśvināvājagmaturāhatuścainaṃ prītau sva eṣa te'pūpo'śānainamiti ||69||

70sa evamuktaḥ pratyuvāca nānṛtamūcaturbhagavantau natvahametamapūpamupayoktumutsahe gurave'nivedyeti ||70||

71tatastamaśvināvūcatuḥ
|
āvābhyāṃ purastādbhavata upādhyāyenaivamevābhiṣṭutābhyāmapūpo datta upayuktaḥ sa tenānivedya gurave tvamapi tathaiva kuruṣva yathā kṛtamupādhyāyeneti ||71||

72sa evamuktaḥ pratyuvāca etatpratyanunaye bhavantāvaśvinau notsahe'hamanivedya gurave'pūpamupayoktumiti ||72||

73tamaśvināvāhatuḥ prītau svastavānayā gurubhaktyā upādhyāyasya te kārṣṇāyasā dantā bhavato'pi hiraṇmayā bhaviṣyanti cakṣuṣmāṃśca bhaviṣyasīti śreyaścāvāpsyasīti ||73||

74sa evamukto'śvibhyāṃ labdhacakṣurupādhyāyasakāśamāgamyābhyavādayat ||74||

75ācacakṣe ca sa cāsya prītimānbabhūva ||75||

76āha cainaṃ yathā'śvināvāhatustathā tvaṃ śreyo'vāpsyasīti ||76||

77sarve ca te vedāḥpratibhāsyanti sarvāṇi ca dharmaśāstrāṇīti
|
eṣā tasyāpi parīkṣopamanyoḥ|| 77 ||

78athāparaḥ śiṣyastasyaivāpodasya dhaumyasya vedo nāma tamupādhyāyaḥ samādideśa vatsa veda ihāsyatāṃ tāvanmama gṛhe kaṃcitkālaṃ śuśrūṣuṇā ca bhavitavyaṃ śreyaste bhaviṣyatīti ||78||

79sa tathetyuktvā gurukule dīrghakālaṃ guruśuśrūṣaṇaparo'vasat
|
gauriva nityaṃ guruṇā dhūrṣu niyojyamānaḥ śītoṣṇakṣuttṛṣṇāduḥkhasahaḥ sarvatrāpratikūlastasya mahatātkālena guruḥ paritoṣaṃ jagāma ||79||

80tatparitoṣācca śreyaḥ sarvajñatāṃ cāvāpa
|
eṣā tasyāpi parīkṣā vedasya ||80||

81sa upādhyāyenānujñātaḥ samāvṛttastasmādgurukulavāsādgṛhāśramaṃ pratyapadyata
|
tasyāpi svagṛhe vasatastrayaḥ śiṣyā babhūvuḥ sa śiṣyānna kiṃciduvāca karma vā kriyatāṃ guruśuśrūṣā veti
|
duḥkhābhijño hi gurukulavāsasya śiṣyānparikleśena yojayituṃ neyeṣa ||81||

82atha kasmiṃścitkāle vedaṃ brāhmaṇaṃ janamejayaḥ pauṣyaśca kṣatriyāvupetya varayitvopādhyāyaṃ cakratuḥ ||82||

83sa kadācidyājyakāryeṇābhiprasthita uttaṅkanāmānaṃ śiṣyaṃ niyojayāmāsa ||83||

84bho yatkiṃcidasmadgṛhe parihīyate tadicchāmyahamaparihīyamānaṃ bhavatā kriyamāṇamiti sa eva pratisaṃdiśyottaṅkaṃ vedaḥ pravāsaṃ jagāma ||84||

85athottaṅkaḥ śuśrūṣurguruniyogamanutiṣṭhamāno gurukule vasati sma
|
sa tatra vasamāna upādhyāyastrībhiḥ sahitābhirāhūyoktaḥ ||85||

86upādhyāyānī te ṛtumatī upādhyāyaśca proṣito'syā yathā'yamṛtarvandhyo na bhavati tathā kriyatāmeṣā viṣīdatīti ||86||

87evamuktastāḥ striyaḥ pratyuvāca
|
na mayā strīṇāṃ vacanādidamakāryaṃ karaṇīyam
|
na hyahamupādhyāyena saṃdiṣṭo'kāryamapi tvayā kāryamiti ||87||

88tasya punarupādhyāyaḥ kālāntareṇa gṛhamājagāma tasmātpravāsāt
|
sa tu tadvṛttaṃ tasyāśeṣamupalabhya prītimānabhūt ||88||

89uvāca cainaṃ vatsottaṅkaṃ kiṃ te priyaṃ karavāṇīti
|
dharmato hi śuśrūṣito'smi bhavatā tena prītiḥ paraspareṇa nau saṃvṛddhā tadanujāne bhavantaṃ sarvāneva kāmānavāpsyasi gamyatāmiti ||89||

90sa evamuktaḥ pratyuvāca kiṃ te priyaṃ karavāṇīti evamāhuḥ ||90||

91yaścādharmeṇa vai brūyādyaścādharmeṇa pṛcchati
|
tayoranyataraḥ praiti vidveṣaṃ cādhigacchati ||91||

92sohamanujñāto bhavatā icchāmīṣṭaṃ gurvarthamupahartumiti
|
tenaivamukta upādhyāyaḥ pratyuvāca vatsottaṅka uṣyatāṃ tāvaditi ||92||

93sa kadācittamupādhyāyamāhottaṅka ājñāpayatu bhavānkiṃ te priyamupāharāmi gurvarthamiti ||93||

94tamupādhyāyaḥ pratyuvāca vatsottaṅka bahuśo māṃ codayasi gurvarthamupāharāmīti tadgacchaināṃ praviśyopādhyāyānīṃ pṛccha kimupāharāmīti||94||

95eṣā yadbravīti tadupāharasveti
|
sa evamukta upādhyāyenopādhyāyinīmapṛcchadbhavatyupādhyāyenāsmyanujñāto gṛhaṃ gantumicchāmīṣṭaṃ te gurvarthamupadṛtyānṛṇo gantumiti||95||

96tadājñāpayatu bhavatī kimupāharāmi gurvarthamiti
|
saivamuktopādhyāyānī tamuttaṅkaṃ pratyuvāca
|
gaccha pauṣyaṃ prati rājānaṃ kuṇḍale bhikṣituṃ tasya kṣatriyayā pinaddhe ||96||

97ānayasva caturthe'hani puṇyakaṃ tābhyāmābaddhābhyāṃ śobhamānā brāhmaṇānpariveṣṭumicchāmi
|
tatsaṃpādayasva evaṃ hi kurvataḥ śreyo bhavitā'nyathā kutaḥ śreya iti ||97||

98sa evamuktastayā prātiṣṭhatottaṅkaḥ sa pathi gacchannapaśyadṛṣabhamatipramāṇaṃ tamadhirūḍhaṃ ca puruṣamatipramāṇameva sa puruṣa uttaṅkamabhyabhāṣata ||98||

99bho uttaṅkaitatpurīṣamasya ṛṣabhasya bhakṣayasveti sa evamukto naicchat ||99||

100tamāha puruṣo bhūyo bhakṣayasvottaṅka mā vicārayopādhyāyenāpi te bhakṣitaṃ pūrvamiti ||100||

101sa evamukto bāḍhamityuktvā tadā tadvṛṣabhasya mūtraṃ purīṣaṃ ca bhakṣayitvottaṅkaḥ saṃbhramādutthita evāpa upaspṛśya pratasthe ||101||

102yatra sa kṣatriyaḥ pauṣyastamupetyāsīnamapaśyaduttaṅkaḥ
|
sa uttaṅkastamupetyāśīrbhirabhinandyovāca ||102||

103arthī bhavantamupāgato'smīti sa enamabhivādyovāca
|
bhagavanpauṣyaḥ khalvahaṃ kiṃ karavāṇīti ||103||

104tamuvāca gurvarthaṃ kuṇḍalayorarthenābhyāgato'smīti
|
ye vai te kṣatriyā pinaddhe kuṇḍale te bhavāndātumarhatīti ||104||

105taṃ pratyuvāca pauṣyaḥ praviśyāntaḥpuraṃ kṣatriyā yācyatāmiti
|
sa tenaivamuktaḥ praviśyāntaḥpuraṃ kṣatriyāṃ nāpaśyat||105||

106sa pauṣyaṃ punaruvāca na yuktaṃ bhavatā'hamanṛtenopacarituṃ na hi te':ntaḥpure kṣatriyā sannihitā naināṃ paśyāmi ||106||

107sa evamuktaḥ pauṣyaḥ kṣaṇamātraṃ vimṛśyottaṅkaṃ pratyuvāca
|
niyataṃ bhavānucchiṣṭaḥ smara tāvanna hi sā kṣatriyā ucchiṣṭenāśucinā śakyā draṣṭuṃ pativratātvātsaiṣā nāśucerdarśanamupaitīti ||107||

108athaivamukta uttaṅkaḥ smṛtvovācāsti khalu mayitthitenopaspṛṣṭaṃ gacchatāṃ ceti
|
taṃ pauṣyaḥ pratyuvāca eṣa te vyatikramo notthitenopaspṛṣṭaṃ bhavati śīghraṃ gacchatā ceti||108||

109athottaṅkastaṃ tathetyuktvā prāṅmukha upaviśya suprakṣālitapāṇipādavadano niḥśabdābhiraphenābhiranuṣṇābhirhṛdgatābhiradbhistriḥ pītvā dviḥ parimṛjya khānyadbhirupaspṛśya cāntaḥpuraṃ praviveśa||109||

110tatastāṃ kṣatriyāmapaśyatsā ca dṛṣṭvaivottaṅkaṃ pratyutthāyābhivādyovāca svāgataṃ te bhagavannājñāpaya kiṃ karavāṇīti ||110||

111sa tāmuvācaite kuṇḍale gurvarthaṃ me bhikṣite dātumarhasīti
|
sā prītā tena tasya sadbhāvena pātramayamanatikramaṇīyaśceti matvā te kuṇḍale'vamucyāsmai prāyacchadāha takṣako nāgarājaḥ subhṛśaṃ prārthayatyapramatto netumarhasīti ||111||

112sa evamuktastāṃ kṣatriyāṃ pratyuvāca bhavatī sunirvṛtā bhava
|
na māṃ śaktastakṣako nāgarājo dharṣayitumiti||112||

113sa evamuktvā tāṃ kṣatriyāmāmantrya pauṣyasakāśamāgacchat
|
āha cainaṃ bhoḥ pauṣya prīto'smīti tamuttaṅkaṃ pauṣyaḥ pratyuvāca ||113||

114bhagavaṃścireṇa pātramāsādya te bhavāṃśca guṇavānatithistadicche śrāddhaṃ kartuṃ kriyatāṃ kṣaṇa iti||114||

115tamuttaṅkaḥ pratyuvāca kṛtakṣaṇa evāsmi śīghramicchāmi
|
yathopapannamannamupaskṛtaṃ bhavateti sa tathetyuktvā yathopapannenānnenainaṃ bhojayāmāsa ||115||

116athottaṅkaḥ sakeśaṃ śītamannaṃ dṛṣṭvā aśucyetaditi matvā taṃ pauṣyamuvāca
|
yasmānme aśucyannaṃ dadāsi tasmādandho bhaviṣyasīti||116||

117taṃ pauṣyaḥ pratyuvāca
|
yasmāttvamapyaduṣṭamannaṃ dūṣayasi tasmāttvamanapatyo bhaviṣyasīti tamuttaṅkaḥ pratyuvāca ||117||

118na yuktaṃ bhavatā'nnamaśuci dattvā pratiśāpaṃ dātuṃ tasmādannameva pratyakṣīkuru
|
tataḥ pauṣyastadannamaśuci dṛṣṭvā tasyāśucibhāvamaparokṣayāmāsa ||118||

119atha tadannaṃ muktakeśyā striyā yatkṛtamanuṣṇaṃ sakeśaṃ cāśucyetaditi matvā tamṛṣimuttaṅkaṃ prasādayāmāsa||119||

120bhagavannetadajñānādannaṃ sakeśamupāhṛtaṃ śītaṃ tatkṣāmaye bhavantaṃ na bhaveyamandha iti tamuttaṅkaḥ pratyuvāca||120||

121na mṛṣā bravīmi bhūtvā tvamandho nacirādanandho bhaviṣyasīti
|
mamāpi śāpo bhavatā datto na bhavediti||121||

122taṃ pauṣyaḥ pratyuvāca na cāhaṃ śaktaḥ śāpaṃ pratyādātuṃ na hi me manyuradyāpyupaśamaṃ gacchati kiṃ caitadbhavatā na jñāyate tathā||122||

123navanītaṃ hṛdayaṃ brāhmaṇasya vāci kṣuro nihitastīkṣṇadhāraḥ
|
tadubhayametadviparītaṃ kṣatriyasya vāṅnavanītaṃ hṛdayaṃ tīkṣṇadhāram ||123||

124tadevaṃ gate na śakto'haṃ tīkṣṇahṛdayatvāttaṃ śāpamanyathā kartuṃ gamyatāmiti
|
tamuttaṅkaḥ pratyuvāca
|
bhavatā'hamannasyāśucibhāvamālakṣya pratyanunītaḥ prāk ca te'bhihitam||124||

125yasmādaduṣṭamannaṃ dūṣayasi tasmādanapatyo bhaviṣyasīti
|
duṣṭe cānne naiṣa mama śāpo bhaviṣyatīti||125||

126sādhayāmastāvadityuktvā prātiṣṭhatottaṅkaste kuṇḍale gṛhītvā
|
so'paśyadatha pathi nagnaṃ kṣapaṇakamāgacchantaṃ muhurmuhurdṛśyamānaṃ ca ||126||

127athottaṅkaste kuṇḍale saṃnyasya bhūmāvudakārthaṃ pracakrame
|
etasminnantare sa kṣapaṇakastvaramāṇa upasṛtya te kuṇḍale gṛhītvā prādravat||127||

128tamuttaṅko'bhisṛtya kṛtodakakāryaḥ śuciḥ prayato namo devebhyo gurubhyaśca kṛtvā mahatā javena tamanvayāt||128||

129tasya takṣako dṛḍhamāsannaḥ sa taṃ jagrāha
|
gṛhītamātraḥ sa tadrūpaṃ vihāya takṣakasvarūpaṃ kṛtvā sahasā dharaṇyāṃ vivṛtaṃ mahābilaṃ praviveśa||129||

130praviśya ca nāgalokaṃ svabhavanamagacchat
|
athottaṅkastasyāḥ kṣatriyāyā vacaḥ smṛtvā taṃ takṣakamanvagacchat ||130||

131sa tadbilaṃ daṇḍakāṣṭhena cakhāna na cāśakat
|
taṃ kliśyamānamindro'paśyatsa vajraṃ preṣayāmāsa||131||

132gacchāsya brāhmaṇasya sāhāyyaṃ kuruṣveti
|
atha vajraṃ daṇḍakāṣṭhamanupraviśya tadbilamadārayat||132||

133tamuttaṅko'nuviveśa tenaiva bilena praviśya ca taṃ nāgalokamaparyantamanekavidhaprāsādaharmyavalabhīniryūhaśatasaṃkulamuccāvacakrīḍāścaryasthānāvakīrṇamapaśyat ||133||

134sa tatra nāgāṃstānastuvadebhiḥ ślokaiḥ - ya airāvatarājānaḥ sarpāḥ samitiśobhāḥ
|
kṣaranta iva jīmūtāḥ savidyutpavaneritāḥ||134||

135surūpā bahurūpāśca tathā kalmāṣakuṇḍalāḥ
|
ādityavannākapṛṣṭhe rejurairāvatodbhavāḥ||135||

136bahūni nāgaveśmāni gaṅgāyāstīra uttare
|
tatrasthānapi saṃstaumi mahataḥ pannagānaham||136||

137icchetko'rkāṃśusenāyāṃ cartumairāvataṃ vinā
|
śatānyaśītiraṣṭau ca sahasrāṇi ca viṃśatiḥ||137||

138sarpāṇāṃ pragrahā yānti dhṛtarāṣṭro yadaijati
|
ye cainamupasarpanti ye ca dūrapathaṃ gatāḥ ||138||

139ahamairāvatajyeṣṭhabhrātṛbhyo'karavaṃ namaḥ
|
yasya vāsaḥ kurukṣetre khāṇḍave cābhavatpurā ||139||

140taṃ nāgarājamastauṣaṃ kuṇḍalārthāya takṣakam
|
takṣakaścāśvasenaśca nityaṃ sahacarāvubhau ||140||

141kurukṣetraṃ ca vasatāṃ nadībhikṣumatīmanu
|
jaghanyajastakṣakaśca śrutaseneti yaḥ sutaḥ||141||

142avasadyo mahaddyumni prārthayannāgamukhyatām
|
karavāṇi sadācāhaṃ namastasmai mahātmane||142||

143evaṃ stutvā sa viprarṣiruttaṅko bhujagottamān
|
naiva te kuṇḍale lebhe tataścintāmupāgamat ||143||

144evaṃ stuvannapi nāgānyadā te kuṇḍale nālabhattadā'paśyatstriyau tantre adhiropya suveme paṭaṃ vayantyau
|
tasmiṃstantre kṛṣṇāḥ sitāśca tantavaścakraṃ cāpaśyat dvādaśāraṃ ṣaḍbhiḥ kumāraiḥ parivartyamānaṃ puruṣaṃ cāpaśyadaśvaṃ ca darśanīyam||144||

145sa tānsarvāṃstuṣṭāva ebhirmantravadeva ślokaiḥ||145||

146trīṇyarpitānyatra śatāni madhye ṣaṣṭiśca nityaṃ carati dhruve'smin
|
cakre caturviṃśatiparvayoge ṣaḍvai kumārāḥ parivartayanti (1)||146||

147tantraṃ cedaṃ viśvarūpe yuvatyau vayatastantūnsatataṃ vartayantyau
|
kṛṣṇānsitāṃścaiva vivartayantyau bhūtānyajasraṃ bhuvanāni caiva (2)||147||

148vajrasya bhartā bhuvanasya goptā vṛtrasya hantā namucernihantā
|
kṛṣṇe vasāno vasane mahātmā satyānṛte yo vivinakti loke (3)||148||

149yo vājinaṃ garbhamapāṃ purāṇaṃ vaiśvānaraṃ vāhanamabhyupaiti
|
namo'stu tasmai jagadīśvarāya lokatrayeśāya puraṃdarāya (4)||149||

150tataḥ sa enaṃ puruṣaḥ prāha prīto'smi te'hamanena stotreṇa kiṃ te priyaṃ karavāṇīti sa tamuvāca||150||

151nāgā me vaśamīyuriti sa cainaṃ puruṣaḥ punaruvāca etamaśvamapāne dhamasveti ||151||

152tato'śvasyāpānamadhamattato'śvāddhamyamānātsarvasrotobhyaḥ pāvakārciṣaḥ sadhūmā niṣpetuḥ||152||

153tābhirnāgaloka upadhūpite'tha saṃbhrāntastakṣako'gnestejobhayādviṣaṇṇaḥ kuṇḍale gṛhītvā sahasā bhavanānniṣkramyottaṅkamuvāca||153||

154ime kuṇḍale gṛhṇātu bhavāniti sa te pratijagrāhottaṅkaḥ pratigṛhya ca kuṇḍale'cintayat||154||

155adya tatpuṇyakamupādhyāyinyā dūraṃ cāhamabhyāgataḥ sa kathaṃ saṃbhāvayeyamiti tata enaṃ cintayānameva sa puruṣa uvāca||155||

156uttaṅka enamevāśvamadhiroha tvāṃ kṣaṇenaivopādhyāyakulaṃ prāpayiṣyatīti ||156||

157sa tathenyuktvā tamaśvamadhiruhya pratyājagāmopādhyāyakulamupādhyāyānī ca snātā keśānāvāpayantyupaviṣṭottaṅko nāgacchatīti śāpāyāsya mano dadhe||157||

158atha tasminnantare sa uttaṅkaḥ praviśya upādhyāyakulamupādhyāyānīmabhyavādayat te cāsyai kuṇḍale prāyacchatsā cainaṃ pratyuvāca||158||

159uttaṅka deśe kāle'bhyāgataḥ svāgataṃ te vatsa tvamanāgasi mayā śaptaḥ śreyastavopasthitaṃ siddhimāpnuhīti ||159||

160athottaṅka upādhyāyamabhyavādayat
|
tamupādhyāyaḥ pratyuvāca vatsottaṅka svāgataṃ te kiṃ ciraṃ kṛtamiti||160||

161tamuttaṅka upādhyāyaṃ pratyuvāca bhostakṣakeṇa me nāgarājena vighnaḥ kṛto'sminkarmaṇi tenā'smi nāgalokaṃ gataḥ||161||

162tatra ca mayā dṛṣṭe striyau tantre'dhiropya paṭaṃ vayantyau tasmiṃśca kṛṣṇāḥ sitāśca tantavaḥ kiṃ tat ||162||

163tatra ca mayā cakraṃ dṛṣṭaṃ dvādaśāraṃ ṣaṭcainaṃ kumārāḥ parivartayanti tadapi kim|| puruṣaścāpi mayā dṛṣṭaḥ sa cāpi kaḥ
|
aśvaścātipramāṇo dṛṣṭaḥ sa cā'pi kaḥ||163||

164pathi gacchatā ca mayā ṛṣabho dṛṣṭastaṃ ca puruṣo'dhirūḍhastenā'smi sopacāramukta uttaṅkāsya ṛṣabhasya purīṣaṃ bhakṣaya upādhyāyenāpi te bhakṣitamiti ||164||

165tatastasya vacanānmayā tadṛṣabhasya purīṣamupayuktaṃ sa cāpi kaḥ
|
tadetadbhavatopadiṣṭamiccheyaṃ śrotuṃ kiṃ taditi
|
sa tenaivamukta upādhyāyaḥ pratyuvāca ||165||

166ye te striyau dhātā vidhātā ca ye ca te kṛṣṇāḥ sitāstantavaste rātryahanī
|
yadapi taccakraṃ dvādaśāraṃ ṣaḍvai kumārāḥ parivartayanti te'pi ṣaḍṛtavaḥ saṃvatsaraścakram ||166||

167yaḥ puruṣaḥsa parjanyaḥ yo'śvaḥ so'gnirya ṛṣabhastvayā pathi gacchatā dṛṣṭaḥ sa airāvato nāgarāṭ ||167||

168yaścainamadhirūḍhaḥ puruṣaḥ sa cendraḥ yadapi te bhakṣitaṃ tasya ṛṣabhasya purīṣaṃ tadamṛtaṃ tena khalvasi tasminnāgabhavane na vyāpannastvam ||168||

169sa hi bhagavānindro mama sakhā tvadanukrośādimamanugrahaṃ kṛtavān
|
tasmātkuṇḍale gṛhītvā punarāgato'si ||169||

170tatsaumya gamyatāmanujāne bhavantaṃ śreyo'vāpsyasīti
|
sa upādhyāyenānujñāto bhagavānuttaṅkaḥ kruddhastakṣakaṃ praticikīrṣamāṇo hāstinapuraṃ pratasthe||170||

171sa hāstinapuraṃ prāpya na cirādviprasattamaḥ
|
samāgacchata rājānamuttaṅko janamejayam||171||

172purā takṣaśilāsaṃsthaṃ nivṛttamaparājitam
|
samyagvijayinaṃ dṛṣṭvā samantānmantribhirvṛtam||172||

173tasmai jayāśiṣaḥ pūrvaṃ yathānyāyaṃ prayujya saḥ
|
uvācainaṃ vacaḥ kāle śabdasaṃpannayā girā||173||

174uttaṅka uvāca|
anyasminkaraṇīye tu kārye pārthivasattama
|
bālyādivānyadevaṃ tvaṃ kuruṣe nṛpasattama||174||

175sautiruvāca|
evamuktastu vipreṇa sa rājā janamejayaḥ
|
arcayitvā yathānyāyaṃ pratyuvāca dvijottamam ||175||

176janamejaya uvāca|
āsāṃ prajānāṃ paripālanena svaṃ kṣatradharmaṃ paripālayāmi
|
prabrūhi me kiṃ karaṇīyamadya yenāsi kāryeṇa samāgatastvam||176||

177sautiruvāca|
sa evamuktastu nṛpottamena dvijottamaḥ puṇyakṛtāṃ variṣṭhaḥ
|
uvāca rājānamadīnasatvaṃ svameva kāryaṃ nṛpate kuruṣva ||177||

178uttaṅka uvāca|
takṣakeṇa mahīndrendra yena te hiṃsitaḥ pitā
|
tasmai pratikuruṣva tvaṃ pannagāya durātmane ||178||

179kāryakālaṃ hi manye'haṃ vidhidṛṣṭasya karmaṇaḥ
|
tadgacchāpacitiṃ rājanpitustasya mahātmanaḥ||179||

180tena hyanaparādhī sa daṣṭo duṣṭāntarātmanā
|
pañcatvamagamadrājā vajrāhata iva drumaḥ||180||

181baladarpasamutsiktastakṣakaḥ pannagādhamaḥ
|
akāryaṃ kṛtavānpāpo yo'daśatpitaraṃ tava ||181||

182rājarṣivaṃśagoptāramamarapratimaṃ nṛpam
|
yiyāsuṃ kāśyapaṃ caiva nyavartayata pāpakṛt||182||

183hotumarhasi taṃ pāpaṃ jvalite havyavāhane
|
sarpasatre mahārāja tvaritaṃ tadvidhīyatām ||183||

184evaṃ pituścāpacitiṃ kṛtavāṃstvaṃ bhaviṣyasi
|
mama priyaṃ ca sumahatkṛtaṃ rājan bhaviṣyati||184||

185karmaṇaḥ pṛthivīpāla mama yena durātmanā
|
vighnaḥ kṛto mahārāja gurvarthaṃ carato'nagha ||185||

186sautiruvāca|
etacchrutvā tu nṛpatistakṣakāya cukopa ha
|
uttaṅkavākyahaviṣā dīpto'gnirhaviṣā yathā ||186||

187apṛcchatsa tadā rājā mantriṇastānsuduḥkhitaḥ
|
uttaṅkasyaiva sānnidhye pituḥ svargagatiṃ prati ||187||

188tadaiva hi sa rājendro duḥkhaśokāpluto'bhavat
|
yadaiva vṛttaṃ pitaramuttaṅkādaśṛṇottadā ||188||
