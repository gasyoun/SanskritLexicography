Mahābhārata 7.57 (Droṇaparvan)

1nārada uvāca| rājānaṃ pauravaṃ vīraṃ mṛtaṃ sṛñjaya śuśruma
|
sahasraṃ yaḥ sahasrāṇāṃ śvetānaśvānavāsṛjat ||1||

2tasyāśvamedhe rājarṣerdeśāddeśāt samīyuṣām
|
śikṣākṣaravidhijñānāṃ nāsīt saṅkhyā vipaścitām ||2||

3vedavidyāvratasnātā vadānyāḥ priyadarśanāḥ
|
subhikṣācchādanagṛhāḥ suśayyāsanabhojanāḥ ||3||

4naṭa-nartaka-gandharvaiḥ pūrṇakairvardhamānakaiḥ
|
nityodyogaiśca krīḍadbhistatra sma pariharṣitāḥ ||4||

5yajñe yajñe yathākālaṃ dakṣiṇāḥ so'tyakālayat
|
dvipā daśasahasrākhyāḥ pramadāḥ kāñcanaprabhāḥ ||5||

6sadhvajāḥ sapatākāśca rathā hemamayāstathā
|
yaḥ sahasraṃ sahasrāṇi kanyā hemavibhūṣitāḥ ||6||

7dhūryujāśvagajārūḍhāḥ svagṛhakṣetragośatāḥ
|
śataṃ śatasahasrāṇi svarṇamālī mahātmanām ||7||

8nāvāṃ sahasrānucarān dakṣiṇāmatyakālayat
|
hemaśṛṅgyo raupyakhurāḥ savatsāḥ kāṃsyadohanāḥ ||8||

9dāsīdāsakharoṣṭrāśca prādādājāvikaṃ bahu
|
ratnānāṃ vividhānāṃ ca vividhāṃścānnaparvatān ||9||

10tasmin saṃvitate yajñe dakṣiṇāmatyakālayat
|
tatrāsya gāthā gāyanti ye purāṇavido janāḥ ||10||

11aṅgasya yajamānasya svadharmādhigatāḥ śubhāḥ
|
guṇottarāstu kratavastasyāsansārvakāmikāḥ||11||

12sa cenmamāra sṛñjaya caturbhadratarastvayā
|
putrāt puṇyatarastubhyaṃ mā putramanutapyathāḥ
|
ayajvānamadākṣiṇyamabhiśvaityeti vyāharan ||12||
