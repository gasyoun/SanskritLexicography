Mahābhārata 4.4 (Virāṭaparvan)

1yudhiṣṭhira uvāca|karmāṇyuktāni yuṣmābhiryāni yāni kariṣyatha
|
mama cāpi yathā buddhirucitā vidhiniścayāt ||1||

2purohito'yamasmākamagnihotrāṇi rakṣatu
|
sūdapaurogavaiḥ sārddhaṃ drupadasya niveśane ||2||

3indrasenamukhāśceme rathānādāya kevalān
|
yāntu dvāravatīṃ śīghramiti me vartate matiḥ ||3||

4imāśca nāryo draupadyāḥ sarvāśca paricārikāḥ
|
pāñcālāneva gacchantu sūdapaurogavaiḥ saha ||4||

5sarvairapi ca vaktavyaṃ na prājñāyanta pāṇḍavāḥ
|
gatā hyasmānapāhāya sarve dvaitavanāditi ||5||

6vaiśampāyana uvāca|evaṃ te'nyonyamāmantrya karmāṇyuktvā pṛthak pṛthak
|
dhaumyamāmantrayāmāsuḥ sa ca tān mantramabravīt||6||

7dhaumya uvāca|vihitaṃ pāṇḍavāḥ sarvaṃ brāhmaṇeṣu suhṛtsu ca
|
yāne praharaṇe caiva tathaivāgniṣu bhārata ||7||

8tvayā rakṣā vidhātavyā kṛṣṇāyāḥ phālgunena ca
|
viditaṃ vo yathā sarvaṃ lokavṛttamidaṃ tava ||8||

9vidite cāpi vaktavyaṃ suhṛdbhiranurāgataḥ
|
eṣa dharmaśca kāmaśca arthaścaiva sanātanaḥ ||9||

10ato'hamapi vakṣyāmi hetumatra nibodhata
|
hantemāṃ rājavasatiṃ rājaputrā bravīmyaham ||10||

11yathā rājakulaṃ prāpya sarvān doṣāṃstariṣyatha
|
durvasaṃ caiva kauravya jānatā rājaveśmani ||11||

12amānitermānitairvā ajñātaiḥ parivatsaram
|
tataścaturdaśe varṣe cariṣyatha yathāsukham ||12||

13dṛṣṭadvāro labheddraṣṭuṃ rājasveṣu na viśvaset
|
tadevāsanamanvicchedyatra nābhipatet paraḥ ||13||

14yo na yānaṃ na paryaṅkaṃ na pīṭhaṃ na gajaṃ ratham
|
ārohet sammato'smīti sa rājavasatiṃ vaset ||14||

15yatra yatrainamāsīnaṃ śaṅkeran duṣṭacāriṇaḥ
|
na tatropaviśedyo vai sa rājavasatiṃ vaset ||15||

16na cānuśiṣyādrājānamapṛcchantaṃ kadācana
|
tūṣṇīṃ tvenamupāsīta kāle samabhipūjayet ||16||

17asūyanti hi rājāno janānanṛtavādinaḥ
|
tathaiva cāvamanyante mantriṇaṃ vādinaṃ mṛṣā ||17||

18naiṣāṃ dāreṣu kurvīta maitrīṃ prājñaḥ kadācana
|
antaḥpuracarā ye ca dveṣṭi yānahitāśca ye ||18||

19vidite cāsya kurvīta kāryāṇi sulaghūnyapi
|
evaṃ vicarato rājñi na kṣatirjāyate kvacit ||19||

20gacchannapi parāṃ bhūmimapṛṣṭo hyaniyojitaḥ
|
jātyandha iva manyeta maryādāmanucintayan ||20||

21na hi putraṃ na naptāraṃ na bhrātaramarindamāḥ
|
samatikrāntamaryādaṃ pūjayanti narādhipāḥ ||21||

22yatnāccopacaredenamagnivaddevavattviha
|
anṛtenopacīrṇo hi hanyādeva na saṃśayaḥ ||22||

23yadyadbhartā'nuyuñjīta tat tadevānuvartayet
|
pramādamavalepaṃ ca kopaṃ ca parivarjayet ||23||

24samarthanāsu sarvāsu hitaṃ ca priyameva ca
|
saṃvarṇayet tadevāsya priyādapi hitaṃ bhavet ||24||

25anukūlo bhaveccāsya sarvārtheṣu kathāsu ca
|
apriyaṃ cāhitaṃ yat syāttadasmai nānuvarṇayet ||25||

26nāhamasya priyo'smīti matvā seveta paṇḍitaḥ
|
apramattaśca satataṃ hitaṃ kuryāt priyaṃ ca yat ||26||

27nāsyāniṣṭāni seveta nāhitaiḥ saha saṃvadet
|
svasthānānna vikampeta sa rājavasatiṃ vaset ||27||

28dakṣiṇaṃ vā'tha vāmaṃ vā pārśvamāsīta paṇḍitaḥ
|
rakṣiṇāṃ hyāttaśastrāṇāṃ sthānaṃ paścādvidhīyate ||28||

29nityaṃ hi pratiṣiddhaṃ tu purastādāsanaṃ mahat
|
na ca saṃdarśane kiñcit pravṛttamapi sañjayet ||29||

30api hyetaddaridrāṇāṃ vyalīkasthānamuttamam
|
na mṛṣā'bhihitaṃ rājñāṃ manuṣyeṣu prakāśayet ||30||

31asūyanti hi rājāno narānanṛtavādinaḥ
|
tathaiva cāvamanyante narān paṇḍitamāninaḥ ||31||

32śūro'smīti na dṛptaḥ syādbuddhimāniti vā punaḥ
|
priyamevācaran rājñaḥ priyo bhavati bhogavān ||32||

33aiśvaryaṃ prāpya duṣprāpaṃ priyaṃ prāpya ca rājataḥ
|
apramatto bhavedrājñaḥ priyeṣu ca hiteṣu ca ||33||

34yasya kopo mahābādhaḥ prasādaśca mahāphalaḥ
|
kastasya manasā'pīcchedanarthaṃ prājñasaṃmataḥ ||34||

35na coṣṭhau na bhujau jānū na ca vākyaṃ samākṣipet
|
sadā vātaṃ ca vācaṃ ca ṣṭhīvanaṃ cācarecchanaiḥ ||35||

36hāsyavastuṣu cānyasya vartamāneṣu keṣucit
|
nātigāḍhaṃ prahṛṣyeta na cāpyunmattavaddhaset ||66||

37na cātidhairyeṇa caredgurutāṃ hi vrajet tataḥ
|
smitaṃ tu mṛdupūrveṇa darśayeta prasādajam ||67||

38lābhe na harṣayedyastu na vyathedyo'vamānitaḥ
|
asaṃmūḍhaśca yo nityaṃ sa rājavasatiṃ vaset ||68||

39rājānaṃ rājaputraṃ vā saṃvarṇayati yaḥ sadā
|
amātyaḥ paṇḍito bhūtvā sa ciraṃ tiṣṭhate priyaḥ ||39||

40pragṛhītaśca yo'mātyo nigṛhītastvakāraṇaiḥ
|
na nirvadati rājānaṃ labhate saṃpadaṃ punaḥ ||40||

41pratyakṣaṃ ca parokṣaṃ ca guṇavādī vicakṣaṇaḥ
|
upajīvī bhavedrājño viṣaye yo'pi vā bhavet ||41||

42amātyo hi balādbhoktuṃ rājānaṃ prārthayeta yaḥ
|
na sa tiṣṭhecciraṃ sthānaṃ gacchecca prāṇasaṃśayam ||42||

43śreyaḥ sadā''tmano dṛṣṭvā paraṃ rājñā na saṃvadet
|
viśeṣayecca rājānaṃ yogyabhūmiṣu sarvadā ||43||

44amlāno balavān śūraśchāyevānugataḥ sadā
|
satyavādī mṛdurdāntaḥ sa rājavasatiṃ vaset ||44||

45anyasmin preṣyamāṇe tu purastādyaḥ samutpatet
|
ahaṃ kiṃ karavāṇīti sa rājavasatiṃ vaset ||45||

46āntare caiva bāhye ca rājñā yaścātha sarvadā
|
ādiṣṭo naiva kampeta sa rājavasatiṃ vaset ||46||

47yo vai gṛhebhyaḥ pravasan priyāṇāṃ nānusaṃsmaret
|
duḥkhena sukhamanvicchet sa rājavasatiṃ vaset ||47||

48samaveṣaṃ na kurvīta noccaiḥ sannihito vaset
|
na mantraṃ bahudhā kuryādevaṃ rājñaḥ priyo bhavet ||48||

49na karmaṇi niyuktaḥ san dhanaṃ kiñcidapi spṛśet
|
prāpnoti hi haran dravyaṃ bandhanaṃ yadi vā vadham ||49||

50yānaṃ vastramalaṅkāraṃ yaccānyat samprayacchati
|
tadeva dhārayennityamevaṃ priyataro bhavet ||50||

51evaṃ saṃyamya cittāni yatnataḥ pāṇḍunandanāḥ
|
saṃvatsaramimaṃ tāta tathāśīlā bubhūṣata
|
atha svaviṣayaṃ prāpya yathākāmaṃ kariṣyatha ||51||

52yudhiṣṭhira uvāca|anuśiṣṭāḥ sma bhadraṃ te naitadvaktā'sti kaścana
|
kuntīmṛte mātaraṃ no viduraṃ vā mahāmatim ||52||

53yadevānantaraṃ kāryaṃ tadbhavān kartumarhati
|
tāraṇāyāsya duḥkhasya prasthānāya jayāya ca ||53||

54vaiśampāyana uvāca|evamuktastato rājñā dhaumyo'tha dvijasattamaḥ
|
akarodvidhivat sarvaṃ prasthāne yadvidhīyate ||54||

55teṣāṃ samidhya tānagnīn mantravacca juhāva saḥ
|
samṛddhivṛddhilābhāya pṛthivīvijayāya ca ||55||

56agnīn pradakṣiṇīkṛtya brāhmaṇāṃśca tapodhanān
|
yājñasenīṃ puraskṛtya ṣaḍevātha pravavrajuḥ ||56||

57gateṣu teṣu vīreṣu dhaumyo'tha japatāṃ varaḥ
|
agnihotrāṇyupādāya pāñcālānabhyagacchata ||57||

58indrasenādayaścaiva yathoktāḥ prāpya yādavān
|
rathānaśvāṃśca rakṣantaḥ sukhamūṣuḥ susaṃvṛtāḥ ||58||
