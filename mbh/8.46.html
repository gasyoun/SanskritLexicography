Mahābhārata 8.46 (Karṇaparvan)

1sañjaya uvāca|
tataḥ parānīkasahaṃ vyūhamapratimaṃ kṛtam
|
samīkṣya karṇaḥ pārthānāṃ dhṛṣṭadyumnābhirakṣitam ||1||

2prayayau rathaghoṣeṇa siṃhanādaraveṇa ca
|
vāditrāṇāṃ ca ninadaiḥ kampayanniva medinīm ||2||

3vepamāna iva krodhādyuddhaśauṇḍaḥ parantapaḥ
|
prativyūhya mahātejā yathāvadbharatarṣabha ||3||

4vyadhamatpāṇḍavīṃ senāmāsurīṃ maghavāniva
|
yudhiṣṭhiraṃ cābhyahanadapasavyaṃ cakāra ha ||4||

5dhṛtarāṣṭra uvāca|
kathaṃ sañjaya rādheyaḥ pratyavyūhata pāṇḍavān
|
dhṛṣṭadyumnamukhānsarvān bhīmasenābhirakṣitān ||5||

6sarvāneva maheṣvāsānajayyānamarairapi
|
ke ca prapakṣau pakṣau vā mama sainyasya sañjaya ||6||

7'pravibhajya yathānyāyaṃ kathaṃ vā samavasthitāḥ
|
kathaṃ pāṇḍusutāścāpi pratyavyūhanta māmakān ||7||

8kathaṃ caiva mahadyuddhaṃ prāvartata sudāruṇam
|
kva ca bībhatsurabhavadyatkarṇo'yādyudhiṣṭhiram ||8||

9ko hyarjunasya sānnidhye śakto'bhyetuṃ yudhiṣṭhiram
|
sarvabhūtāni yo hyekaḥ khāṇḍave jitavānpurā
|
kastamanyastu rādheyātpratiyuddhyejjijīviṣuḥ ||9||

10sañjaya uvāca|
śṛṇu vyūhasya racanāmarjunaśca yathāgataḥ
|
parivārya nṛpaṃ svaṃ svaṃ saṅgrāmaścābhavadyathā ||10||

11kṛpaḥ śāradvato rājan māgadhāśca tarasvinaḥ
|
sātvataḥ kṛtavarmā ca dakṣiṇaṃ pakṣamāśritāḥ ||11||

12teṣāṃ prapakṣe śakunirulūkaśca mahārathaḥ
|
sādibhirvimalaprāsaistavānīkamarakṣatām ||12||

13gāndhāribhirasaṃbhrāntaiḥ pārvatīyaiśca durjayaiḥ
|
śalabhānāmiva vrātaiḥ piśācairiva durdṛśaiḥ ||13||

14caturviṃśatsahasrāṇi rathānāmanivartinām
|
saṃśaptakā yuddhaśauṇḍā vāmaṃ pārśvamapālayan ||14||

15samanvitāstava sutaiḥ kṛṣṇārjunajighāṃsavaḥ
|
teṣāṃ prapakṣāḥ kāmbojāḥ śakāśca yavanaiḥ saha ||15||

16nideśātsūtaputrasya sarathāḥ sāśvapattayaḥ
|
āhvayanto'rjunaṃ tasthuḥ keśavaṃ ca mahābalam ||16||

17madhye senāmukhe karṇo'pyavātiṣṭhata daṃśitaḥ
|
citravarmāṅgadaḥ sragvī pālayanvāhinīmukham ||17||

18rakṣamāṇaiḥ susaṃrabdhaiḥ putraiḥ śastrabhṛtāṃ varaḥ
|
vāhinīṃ pramukhe vīraḥ saṃprakarṣannaśobhata ||18||

19abhyavartanmahābāhuḥ sūryavaiśvānaraprabhaḥ
|
mahādvipaskandhagataḥ piṅgākṣaḥ priyadarśanaḥ ||19||

20duḥśāsano vṛtaḥ sainyaiḥ sthito vyūhasya pṛṣṭhataḥ
|
tamanvayānmahārāja svayaṃ duryodhano nṛpaḥ ||20||

21citrāstraiścitrasannāhaiḥ sodaryairabhirakṣitaḥ
|
rakṣyamāṇo mahāvīryaiḥ sahitairmadrakekayaiḥ ||21||

22aśobhata mahārāja devairiva śatakratuḥ
|
aśvatthāmā kurūṇāṃ ca ye pravīrā mahārathāḥ ||22||

23nityamattāśca mātaṅgāḥ śūrairmlecchaiḥ samanvitāḥ
|
anvayustadrathānīkaṃ kṣaranta iva toyadāḥ ||23||

24te dhvajairvaijayantībhirjvaladbhiḥ paramāyudhaiḥ
|
sādibhiścāsthitā rejurdrumavanta ivācalāḥ ||24||

25teṣāṃ padātināgānāṃ pādarakṣāḥ sahasraśaḥ
|
paṭṭiśāsidharāḥ śūrā babhūvuranivartinaḥ ||25||

26sādibhiḥ syandanairnāgairadhikaṃ samalaṅkṛtaiḥ
|
sa vyūharājo vibabhau devāsuracamūpamaḥ ||26||

27bārhaspatyaḥ suvihito nāyakena vipaścitā
|
nṛtyatīva mahāvyūhaḥ pareṣāṃ bhayamādadhat ||27||

28tasya pakṣaprapakṣebhyo niṣpatanti yuyutsavaḥ
|
hastyaśvarathamātaṅgāḥ prāvṛṣīva balāhakāḥ ||28||

29tataḥ senāmukhe karṇaṃ dṛṣṭvā rājā yudhiṣṭhiraḥ
|
dhanañjayamamitraghnamekavīramuvāca ha ||29||

30paśyārjuna mahāvyūhaṃ karṇena vihitaṃ raṇe
|
yuktaṃ pakṣaiḥ prapakṣaiśca parānīkaṃ prakāśate ||30||

31tadetadvai samālokya pratyamitraṃ mahadbalam
|
yathā nābhibhavatyasmāṃstathā nītirvidhīyatām ||31||

32evamukto'rjuno rājñā prāñjalirnṛpamabravīt
|
yathā bhavānāha tathā tatsarvaṃ na tadanyathā ||32||

33yastvasya vihito ghātastaṃ kariṣyāmi bhārata
|
pradhānavadha evāsya vināśastaṃ karomyaham ||33||

34yudhiṣṭhira uvāca|
tasmāttvameva rādheyaṃ bhīmasenaḥ suyodhanam
|
vṛṣasenaṃ ca nakulaḥ sahadevo'pi saubalam ||34||

35duḥśāsanaṃ śatānīko hārdikyaṃ śinipuṅgavaḥ
|
dhṛṣṭadyumno droṇasutaṃ svayaṃ yotsyāmyahaṃ kṛpam ||35||

36draupadeyā dhārtarāṣṭrān śiṣṭānsaha śikhaṇḍinā
|
te te ca tāṃstānahitānasmākaṃ ghnantu māmakāḥ ||36||

37sañjaya uvāca|
ityukto dharmarājena tathetyuktvā dhanañjayaḥ
|
vyādideśa svasainyāni svayaṃ cāgāccamūmukham ||37||

38agnirvaiśvānaraḥ pūrvo brahmenduḥ saptitāṃ gataḥ
|
tasmādyaḥ prathamaṃ jātastaṃ devā brāhmaṇaṃ viduḥ ||38||

39brahmeśānendravaruṇān kramaśo yo'vahatpurā
|
tamādyaṃ rathamāsthāya prayātau keśavārjunau ||39||

40atha taṃ rathamāyāntaṃ dṛṣṭvā'tyadbhutadarśanam
|
uvācādhirathiṃ śalyaḥ punastaṃ yuddhadurmadam ||40||

41ayaṃ saratha āyātaḥ śvetāśvaḥ kṛṣṇasārathiḥ
|
durvāraḥ sarvasainyānāṃ vipākaḥ karmaṇāmiva ||41||

42nighnannabhivān kaunteyo yaṃ karṇa paripṛcchasi
|
śrūyate tumulaḥ śabdo yathā meghasvano mahān ||42||

43dhruvametau mahātmānau vāsudevadhanañjayau
|
eṣa reṇuḥ samudbhūto divamāvṛtya tiṣṭhati ||43||

44cakranemipraṇunneva kampate karṇa medinī
|
pravātyeṣa mahāvāyurabhitastava vāhinīm ||44||

45kravyādā vyāharantyete mṛgāḥ krandanti bhairavam
|
paśya karṇa mahāghoraṃ bhayadaṃ lomaharṣaṇam ||45||

46kabandhaṃ meghasaṃkāśaṃ bhānumāvṛtya saṃsthitam
|
paśya yūthairbahuvidhairmṛgāṇāṃ sarvatodiśam ||46||

47balibhirdṛptaśārdūlairādityo'bhinirīkṣyate
|
paśya kaṅkāṃśca gṛdhrāṃśca samavetānsahasraśaḥ ||47||

48sthitānabhimukhān ghorānanyonyamabhibhāṣataḥ
|
rañjitāścāmarā yuktāstava karṇa mahārathe ||48||

49pravarāḥ prajvalantyete dhvajaścaiva prakampate
|
savepathūn hayānpaśya mahākāyānmahājavān ||49||

50plavamānāndarśanīyānākāśe garuḍāniva
|
dhruvameṣa nimitteṣu bhūmimāśritya prārthivāḥ ||50||

51svapsyanti nihatāḥ karṇa śataśo'tha sahasraśaḥ
|
śaṅkhānāṃ tumulaḥ śabdaḥ śrūyate lomaharṣaṇaḥ ||51||

52ānakānāṃ ca rādheya mṛdaṅgānāṃ ca sarvaśaḥ
|
bāṇaśabdānbahuvidhānnarāśvagajavājinām ||52||

53jyātalatreṣu śabdāṃśca śṛṇu karṇa mahātmanām
|
hemarūpyaprasṛṣṭānāṃ vāsasāṃ śilpinirmitāḥ ||53||

54nānāvarṇā rathe bhānti śvasanena prakampitāḥ
|
sahemacandratārārkāḥ patākāḥ kiṃkiṇīyutāḥ ||54||

55paśya karṇārjunasyaitāḥ saudāmanya ivāmbude
|
dhvajāḥ kaṇakaṇāyante vātenābhisamīritāḥ ||55||

56vibhrājanti rathe karṇa vimāne daivate yathā
|
sapatākā rathāścaite pañcālānāṃ mahātmanām ||56||

57paśya kuntīsutaṃ vīraṃ bībhatsumaparājitam
|
pradharṣayitumāyāntaṃ kapipravaraketanam ||57||

58eṣa dhvajāgre pārthasya prekṣaṇīyaḥ samantataḥ
|
dṛśyate vānaro bhīmo dviṣatāmaghavardhanaḥ ||58||

59etaccakraṃ gadā śārṅgaṃ śaṅkhaḥ kṛṣṇasya dhīmataḥ
|
atyarthaṃ bhrājate kṛṣṇe kaustubhastu maṇistataḥ ||59||

60eṣa śārṅgagadāpāṇirvāsudevo'tivīryavān
|
vāhayanneti turagānpāṇḍurānvātaraṃhasaḥ ||60||

61etatkūjati gāṇḍīvaṃ vikṛṣṭaṃ savyasācinā
|
ete hastavatā muktā ghnantyamitrāñśitāḥ śarāḥ ||61||

62viśālāyatatāmrākṣaiḥ pūrṇacandranibhānanaiḥ
|
eṣā bhūḥ kīryate rājñāṃ śirobhirapalāyinām ||62||

63ete suparighākārāḥ puṇyagandhānulepanāḥ
|
udyatāyudhaśauṇḍānāṃ pātyante sāyudhā bhujāḥ ||63||

64nirastanetrajihvāśca vājinaḥ saha sādibhiḥ
|
patitāḥ pātyamānāśca kṣitau kṣīṇāśca śerate ||64||

65ete parvataśṛṅgāṇāṃ tulyarūpā hatā dvipāḥ
|
saṃchinnabhinnāḥ pārthena pracarantyadrayo yathā ||65||

66gandharvanagarākārā rathā hatanareśvarāḥ
|
vimānānīva puṇyāni svargiṇāṃ nipatantyamī ||66||

67vyākulīkṛtamatyarthaṃ paśya sainyaṃ kirīṭinā
|
nānāmṛgasahasrāṇāṃ yūthaṃ kesariṇā yathā ||67||

68ghnantyete pārthivānvīrāḥ pāṇḍavāḥ samabhidrutāḥ
|
nāgāśvarathapattyoghāṃstāvakānsamabhighnataḥ ||68||

69eṣa sūrya ivāmbhodaiśchannaḥ pārtho na dṛśyate
|
dhvajāgraṃ dṛśyate tvasya jyāśabdaścāpi śrūyate ||69||

70adya drakṣyasi taṃ vīraṃ śvetāśvaṃ kṛṣṇasārathim
|
nighnantaṃ śātravānsaṃkhye yaṃ karṇa paripṛcchasi ||70||

71adya tau puruṣavyāghrau lohitākṣau parantapau
|
vāsudevārjunau karṇa draṣṭāsyekarathe sthitau ||71||

72sārathiryasya vārṣṇeyo gāṇḍīvaṃ yasya kārmukam
|
taṃ ceddhantāsi rādheya tvaṃ no rājā bhaviṣyasi ||72||

73eṣa saṃśaptakāhūtastānevābhimukho gataḥ
|
karoti kadanaṃ caiṣāṃ saṃgrāme dviṣatāṃ balī ||73||

74iti bruvāṇaṃ madreśaṃ karṇaḥ prāhātimanyunā
|
paśya saṃśaptakaiḥ kruddhaiḥ sarvataḥ samabhidrutaḥ ||74||

75eṣa sūrya ivāṃbhodaiśchannaḥ pārtho na dṛśyate
|
etadanto'rjunaḥ śalya nimagno yodhasāgare ||75||

76śalya uvāca|
varuṇaṃ ko'mbhasā hanyādindhanena ca pāvakam
|
ko vā'nilaṃ nigṛhṇīyātpibedvā ko mahārṇavam ||76||

77īdṛgrūpamahaṃ manye pārthasya yudhi vigraham
|
na hi śakyo'rjuno jetuṃ yudhi sendraiḥ surāsuraiḥ ||77||

78athavā paritoṣaste vācoktvā sumanā bhava
|
na sa śakyo yudhā jetumanyaṃ kuru manoratham ||78||

79bāhubhyāmuddharedbhūmiṃ dahetkuddha imāḥ prajāḥ
|
pātayettridivāddevānyo'rjunaṃ samare jayet ||79||

80paśya kuntīsutaṃ vīraṃ bhīmamakliṣṭakāriṇam
|
prabhāsantaṃ mahābāhuṃ sthitaṃ merumivāparam ||80||

81amarṣī nityasaṃrabdhaściraṃ vairamanusmaran
|
eṣa bhīmo jayaprepsuryudhi tiṣṭhati vīryavān ||81||

82eṣa dharmabhṛtāṃ śreṣṭho dharmarājo yudhiṣṭhiraḥ
|
tiṣṭhatyasukaraḥ saṃkhye paraiḥ parapurañjayaḥ ||82||

83etau ca puruṣavyāghrāvaśvināviva sodarau
|
nakulaḥ sahadevaśca tiṣṭhato yudhi durjayau ||83||

84amī sthitā draupadeyāḥ pañca pañcācalā iva
|
vyavasthitā yoddhukāmāḥ sarve'rjunasamā yudhi ||84||

85ete drupadaputrāśca dhṛṣṭadyumnapurogamāḥ
|
sphītāḥ satyajito vīrāstiṣṭhanti paramaujasaḥ ||85||

86asāvindra ivāsahyaḥ sātyakiḥ sātvatāṃ varaḥ
|
yuyutsurupayātyasmān kruddhāntakasamaḥ puraḥ ||86||

87iti saṃvada‌toreva tayoḥ puruṣasiṃhayoḥ
|
te sene samasajjetāṃ gaṅgāyamunavadbhṛśam ||87||
