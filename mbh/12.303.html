Mahābhārata 12.303 (Śāntiparvan)

1vasiṣṭha uvāca |
evamapratibuddhatvādavuddhamanuvartate
|
dehāddehasahasrāṇi tathā samabhipadyate||1||

2tiryagyonisahasreṣu kadāciddevatāsvapi
|
upapadyati saṃyogādguṇaiḥ saha guṇakṣayāt||2||

3mānuṣatvāddivaṃ yāti divo mānuṣyameva ca
|
mānuṣyānnirayasthānamānantyaṃ pratipadyate||3||

4kośakāro yathā''tmānaṃ kīṭa: samavarundhati
|
sūtratantuguṇairnityaṃ tathāyamaguṇo guṇaiḥ||4||

5dvandvameti ca nirdvandvastāsu tāsviha yoniṣu
|
śīrṣaroge'kṣiroge ca dantaśūle galagrahe||5||

6jalodare tṛṣāroge jvaragaṇḍe viṣūcake
|
śvitrakuṣṭhe'gnidagdhe ca sidhmāpasmārayorapi||6||

7yāni cānyāni dvandvāni prākṛtāni śarīriṣu
|
utpadyante vicitrāṇi tānyeṣo'pyabhimanyate||7||

8tiryagyonisahasreṣu kadāciddevatāsvapi
|
abhimanyatyabhīmānāttathaiva sukṛtānyapi||8||

9śuklavāsāśca durvāsāḥ śāyī nityamadhastathā
|
maṇḍūkaśāyī ca tathā vīrāsanagatastathā||9||

10cīradhāraṇamākāśe śayanaṃ sthānameva ca
|
iṣṭakāprastare caiva kaṇṭakaprastare tathā||10||

11bhasmaprastaraśāyī ca bhūmiśayyā taleṣu ca
|
vīrasthānāmbupaṅke ca śayanaṃ phalakeṣu ca||11||

12vividhāsu ca śayyāsu phalagṛdvyānvitastathā
|
muñjamekhalanagnatvaṃ kṣaumakṛṣṇājināni ca||12||

13śāṇīvālaparīdhāno vyāghracarmaparicchadaḥ
|
siṃhacarmaparīdhānaḥ paṭṭavāsāstathaiva ca||13||

14phalakaṃ paridhānaśca tathā kaṇṭakavastradhṛk
|
kīṭakāvasanaścaiva cīravāsāstathaiva ca||14||

15vastrāṇi cānyāni bahūnyabhimanyatyabuddhimān
|
bhojanāni vicitrāṇi ratnāni vividhāni ca||15||

16ekarātrāntarāśitvamekakālikabhojanam
|
caturthāṣṭamakālaśca ṣaṣṭhakālika eva ca||16||

17ṣaḍrātrabhojanaścaiva tathaivāṣṭāhabhojanaḥ
|
saptarātradaśāhāro dvādaśāhikabhojanaḥ||17||

18māsopavāsī mūlāśī phalāhārastathaiva ca
|
vāyubhakṣombupiṇyākadadhigomayabhojanaḥ||18||

19gomūtrabhojanaścaiva śākapuṣpāda eva ca
|
śaivālabhojanaścaiva tathā cā''mena vartayan||19||

20vartayan śīrṇaparṇaiśca prakīrṇaphalabhojanaḥ
|
vividhāni ca kṛcchrāṇi sevate siddhikāṃkṣayā||20||

21cāndrāyaṇāni vidhivalliṅgāni vividhāni ca
|
cāturāśramyapanthānamāśrayatyapathānapi||21||

22upāśramānapyaparānpāṣaṇḍānvividhānapi
|
viviktāśca śilācchāyāstathā prasravaṇāni ca||22||

23pulināni viviktāni viviktāni vanāni ca
|
devasthānāni puṇyāni viviktāni sarāṃsi ca||23||

24viviktāścāpi śailānāṃ guhā gṛhanibhopamāḥ
|
viviktāni ca japyāni vratāni vividhāni ca||24||

25niyamānvividhāṃścāpi vividhāni tapāṃsi ca
|
yajñāṃśca vividhākārānvidhīṃśca vividhāṃstathā||25||

26vaṇikpathaṃ dvijaṃ kṣatraṃ vaiśyaśūdrāṃstathaiva ca
|
dānaṃ ca vividhākāraṃ dīnāndhakṛpaṇādiṣu||26||

27abhimanyatyasambodhāttathaiva trividhān guṇān
|
sattvaṃ rajastamaścaiva dharmārthai kāma eva ca||27||

28prakṛtyātmānamevātmā evaṃ pravibhajatyuta
|
svadhākāravaṣaṭkārau svāhākāranamaskriyāḥ||28||

29yājanādhyāpanaṃ dānaṃ tathaivāhuḥ pratigraham
|
yajanādhyayane caiva yaccānyadapi kiñcana||29||

30janmamṛtyuvivāde ca tathā viśasane'pi ca
|
śubhāśubhamayaṃ sarvametadāhuḥ kriyāpatham||30||

31prakṛtiḥ kurute devī bhavaṃ pralayameva ca
|
divasānte guṇānetānabhyetyaiko'vatiṣṭhate||31||

32raśmijālamivādityastattatkāle niyacchati
|
evameṣo'sakṛtpūrvaṃ krīḍārthamabhimanyate||32||

33ātmarūpaguṇānetānvividhānhṛdayapriyān
|
evametāṃ vikurvāṇaḥ sargapralayadharmiṇīm||33||

34kriyāṃ kriyāpathe raktastriguṇāṃ triguṇādhipaḥ
|
kriyāṃ kriyāpathopetastathā taditi manyate||34||

35prakṛtyā sarvamevedaṃ jagadandhīkṛtaṃ vibho
|
rajasā tamasā caiva vyāptaṃ sarvamanekadhā||35||

36evaṃ dvandvānyathaitāni samāvartanti nityaśaḥ
|
mamaivaitāni jāyante dhāvante tāni māmiti||36||

37nistartavyānyathaitāni sarvāṇīti narādhipa
|
manyate'yaṃ hyabuddhatvāttathaiva sukṛtānyapi||37||

38bhoktavyāni mayaitāni devalokagatena vai
|
ihaiva cainaṃ bhokṣyāmi śubhāśubhaphalodayam||38||

39sukhameva tu kartavyaṃ sakṛtkṛtvā sukhaṃ mama
|
yāvadantaṃ ca me saukhyaṃ jātyāṃ jātyāṃ bhaviṣyati||39||

40bhaviṣyati ca me duḥkhaṃ kṛtenehāpyanantakam
|
mahadduḥkhaṃ hi mānuṣyaṃ niraye cāpi majjanam||40||

41nirayāccāpi mānuṣyaṃ kālenaiṣyāmyahaṃ punaḥ
|
manuṣyatvācca devatvaṃ devatvātpauruṣaṃ punaḥ||41||

42manuṣyatvācca nirayaṃ paryāyeṇopagacchati
|
ya evaṃ vetti nityaṃ vai nirātmātmaguṇairvṛtaḥ||42||

43tena devamanuṣyeṣu niraye copapadyate
|
mamatvenāvṛto nityaṃ tatraiva parivartate||43||

44sargakoṭisahasrāṇi maraṇāntāsu mūrtiṣu
|
ya evaṃ kurute karma śubhāśubhaphalātmakam||44||

45sa evaṃ phalamāpnoti triṣu lokeṣu mūrtimān
|
prakṛtiḥ kurute karma śubhāśubhaphalātmakam
|
prakṛtiśca tadaśnāti triṣu lokeṣu kāmagā||45||

46tiryagyonimanuṣyatvaṃ devaloke tathaiva ca
|
trīṇi sthānāni caitāni jānīyātprakṛtāni ha||46||

47aliṅgāṃ prakṛtiṃ tvāhurliṅgairanumimīmahe
|
tathaiva pauruṣaṃ liṅgamanumānāddhi manyate||47||

48sa liṅgāntaramāsādya prākṛtaṃ liṅgamavraṇam
|
vraṇadvārāṇyadhiṣṭhāya karmaṇā''tmani manyate||48||

49śrotrādīni tu sarvāṇi pañcakarmendriyāṇyatha
|
vāgādīni pravartante guṇeṣviha guṇaiḥ saha||49||

50ahametāni vai sarvaṃ mayyetānīndriyāṇi ha
|
nirindriyo hi manyeta vraṇavānasmi nirvaṇaḥ||50||

51aliṅgo liṅgamātmānamakālaḥ kālamātmanaḥ
|
asattvaṃ sattvamātmānamatattvaṃ tattvamātmanaḥ||51||

52amṛtyurmṛtyumātmānamacaraścaramātmanaḥ
|
akṣetraḥ kṣetramātmānamasargaḥ sargamātmanaḥ||52||

53atapāstapa ātmānamagatirgatimātmanaḥ
|
abhavo bhavamātmānamabhayo bhayamātmanaḥ||53||

54akṣaraḥ kṣaramātmānamabuddhistvabhimanyate||54||
