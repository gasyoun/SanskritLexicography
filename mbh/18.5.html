Mahābhārata 18.5 (Svargārohaṇaparvan)

1janamejaya uvāca|
bhīṣmadroṇau mahātmānau dhṛtarāṣṭraśca pārthivaḥ
|
virāṭadrupadau cobhau śaṅkhaścaivottarastathā ||1||

2dhṛṣṭakejurjayatseno rājā caiva sa satyajit
|
duryodhanasutāścaiva śakuniścaiva saubalaḥ ||2||

3karṇaputrāśca vikrāntā rājā caiva jayadrathaḥ
|
ghaṭotkavādayaścaiva ye cānye nānukīrtitāḥ ||3||

4ye cānye kīrtitā vīrā rājāno dīptamūrtayaḥ
|
svarge kālaṃ kiyantaṃ te tasthustadapi śaṃsa me ||4||

5āhosvicchāśvataṃ sthānaṃ teṣāṃ tatra dvijottama
|
ante vā karmaṇāṃ kāṃ te gatiṃ prāptā nararṣabhāḥ ||5||

6etadicchāmyahaṃ śrotuṃ procyamānaṃ dvijottama
|
tapasā hi pradīptena sarvaṃ tvamanupaśyasi||6||

7sautiruvāca |
ityuktaḥ sa tu viprarṣiranujñāto mahātmanā
|
vyāsena tasya nṛpaterākhyātumupacatrame ||7||

8vaiśampāyana uvāca|
na śakyaṃ karmaṇāmante sarveṣāṃ manujādhipa
|
prakètiṃ kiṃ nu samẏakte pṛcchaiṣā saṃprayojita ||8||

9śṛṇu guhyamidaṃ rājandevānāṃ bharatarṣabha
|
yaduvāca mahātejā divyacakṣuḥ pratāpavān ||9||

10muniḥ purāṇaḥ kauravya pārāśaryo mahāvrataḥ
|
agādhabuddhiḥ sarvajño gatijñaḥ sarvakarmaṇām ||10||

11tenoktaṃ karmaṇāmante praviśanti svikāṃ tanum
|
vasūneva mahātejā bhīṣmaḥ prāpa mahādyutiḥ ||11||

12aṣṭāveva hi dṛśyante vasavo bharatarṣabha
|
bṛhaspatiṃ viveśātha droṇo hyaṅgirasāṃ varam ||12||

13kṛtavarmā tu hārdikyaḥ praviveśa marudgaṇān
|
sanatkumāraṃ pradyumnaḥ praviveśa yathāgatam ||13||

14dhṛtarāṣṭro dhaneśasya lokānprāpa durāsadān
|
dhṛtarāṣṭro sahitā gandhārī ca yaśasvinī ||14||

15patnībhyāṃ sahitaḥ pāṇḍumahendrasadanaṃ yayau
|
virāṭadrupadau cobhau dhṛṣṭaketuśca pārthivaḥ ||15||

16niśaṭhākrūrasāmbāśca bhānuḥ kaṇvo vidūrathaḥ
|
bhūriśravāḥ śalaścaiva bhūriśca pṛthivīpatiḥ ||16||

17kaṃśaścaivograsenaśca vasudevastathaiva ca
|
uttaraśca saha bhrātrā śaṅkhena narapuṅgavaḥ ||17||

18viśveṣāṃ devatānāṃ te viviśurnarasattamāḥ
|
varcā nāma mahātejāḥ somaputraḥ pratāpavān ||18||

19sobhimanyurnṛsiṃhasya phalgunasya suto'bhavat
|
sa yuddhvā kṣatradharmeṇa yathā nānyaḥ pumānkvacit ||19||

20viveśa somaṃ dharmātmā karmaṇonte marārathaḥ
|
āviveśa raviṃ karṇo nihataḥ puruṣarṣabha ||20||

21dvāparaṃ śakuniḥ prāpa dhṛṣṭadyumnastu pāvakam
|
dhṛtarāṣṭrātmajāḥ sarve yātudhāna balotkaṭāḥ ||21||

22ṛdvimanto mahātmānaḥśakhapunā divaṃ gatāḥ
|
dharmamevāviśatkṣattā rājā caiva yudhiṣṭhiraḥ ||22||

23ananto bhagavāndevaḥ praviveśa rasātalam
|
pitāmahaniyogādvai yo yogādgāmadhārayat ||23||

24yaḥ sa nārāyaṇo nāma devadevaḥ sanātanaḥ
|
tasyāṃśo vāsudevastu karmaṇonte viveśa ha ||24||

25ṣoḍaśastrīsahasrāṇi vāsudevaparigrahāḥ
|
amajjaṃstāḥ sarasvatyāṃ kālena janamejaya ||25||

26tatra tyaktvā śarīrāṇi divamāruruhuḥ punaḥ
|
tāścaivāpsaraso bhūtvā vāsudevamupāviśan ||26||

27hatāstasminmahāyuddhe ye vīrāstu mahārathāḥ
|
ghaṭotkacādayaścaiva devānyakṣāṃśca bhejire ||27||

28duryodhanasahāyāśca rākṣasā ye'nukīrtitāḥ
|
prāptāste kramaśo rājansarvalokānanuttamān ||28||

29bhavanaṃ ca mahendrasya kuberasya ca dhīmataḥ
|
varuṇasya tathā lokānviviśuḥ puruṣarṣabhāḥ ||29||

30etatte sarvamākhyātaṃ vistareṇa mahādyute
|
kurūṇāṃ caritaṃ kṛtsnaṃ pāṇḍavānāṃ ca bhārata ||30||

31sautiruvāca
etacchrutvā dvijaśreṣṭhātsa rājā janamejayaḥ
|
vismito'bhavadatyarthaṃ yajñakarmāntareṣvatha ||31||

32tataḥ samāpayāmāsuḥ karma tattasya yājakāḥ
|
āstikaścābhavatprītaḥ parimokṣya bhujaṅgamān ||32||

33tato dvijātīnsarvāstāndakṣiṇābhiratoṣayat
|
pūjitāścāpi te rājñā tato jagmuryathāgatam ||33||

34visarjayitvā viprāṃstānrājā'pi janamejayaḥ
|
hṛṣṭastakṣaśilāyāḥ sa punarāyādgacāhvayam ||34||

35etatte sarvamākhyātaṃ vaiśampāyanakīrtitam
|
vyāsājñayā samājñātaṃ sarpasatre nṛpasya hi ||35||

36puṇyo'yamitihāsākhyaḥ pavitraṃ cedamuttamam
|
kṛṣṇena muninā vipra nirmitaṃ satyavādinā ||36||

37sarvajñena vidhijñena dharmajñānavatā satā
|
atīndriyeṇa śucinā tapasā bhāvitātmanā ||37||

38aiśvarye vartatā caiva sāṅkhyayogavatā tathā
|
naikatantravibuddhena dṛṣṭvā divyena cakṣuṣā ||38||

39kīrtiṃ prathayatā loke pāṇḍavānāṃ mahātmanām
|
anyeṣāṃ kṣatriyāṇāṃ ca bhūridraviṇatejasām ||39||

40' yaścedaṃ śrāvayedvidvānsadā parvaṇi parvaṇi
|
dhūtapāpmā jitasvargo brahmabhūyāya kalpate ||40||

41kārṣṇaṃ vedamimaṃ sarvaṃ śṛṇuyādyaḥ samāhitaḥ
|
brahmahatyādipāpānāṃ koṭistasya vinaśyati||41||

42yaścedaṃ śrāvayecchrāddhe brāhmaṇānpādamantataḥ
|
akṣayyamannapānaṃ vai pitṝṃstasyopatadiṣṭhate ||42||

43ahnā yadenaḥ kurute indriyairmanasā'pi vā
|
mahābhāratamākhyāya paścātsaṃdhyāṃ pramucyate ||43||

44yadrātrau kurute pāpaṃ brāhmaṇaḥ strīgaṇairvṛtaḥ
|
mahābhāratamākhyāya pūrvāṃ sandhyāṃ pramucyate ||44||

45mahattvādbhāravattvācca mahābhāratamucyate
|
niruktamasya yo veda sarvapāpaiḥ pramucyate ||45||

46aṣṭādaśapurāṇāni dharmaśāstrāṇi sarvaśaḥ
|
vedāḥ sāṅgāstathaikatra bhārataṃ caikataḥ sthitam ||46||

47śrūyatāṃ siṃhanādo'yamṛṣestasya mahātmanaḥ
|
aṣṭādaśapurāṇānāṃ karturvedamahodadheḥ ||47||

48tribhirvarṣairmahatpuṇyaṃ kṛṣṇadvaipāyanaḥ prabhuḥ
|
akhilaṃ bhārataṃ cedaṃ cakāra bhagavānmuniḥ ||48||

49ākarṇya bhaktyā satataṃ jayākhyaṃ bhārataṃ mahat
|
śrīśca kīrtistathā vidyā bhavanti sahitāḥ sadā||49||

50dharme cārthe ca kāme ca mokṣe ca bharatarṣabha
|
yadihāsti tadanyatra yannehāsti na kutricit ||50||

51jayo nāmetihāso'yaṃ śrotavyo mokṣamicchatā
|
brāhmaṇena ca rājñā garbhiṇyā caiva yoṣitā ||51||

52svargakāmo labhetsvargaṃ jayakāmo labhejjayam
|
garbhiṇī labhate putraṃ kanyāṃ vā bahubhāginīm ||52||

53anāgataśca mokṣaśca kṛṣṇadvaipāyanaḥ prabhuḥ
|
saṃdarbhaṃ bhāratasyāsya kṛtavāndharmakāmyayā ||53||

54ṣaṣṭiṃ śatasahasrāṇi cakārānyāṃ sa saṃhitām
|
triṃśacchatasahasrāṇi devaloke pratiṣṭhitam ||54||

55pitrye pañcadaśaṃ jñeyaṃ yakṣaloke caturdaśa
|
ekaṃ śatasahasraṃ tu mānuṣeṣu prabhāṣitam ||55||

56nārado'śrāvayaddevānasito devalaḥ pitṝn
|
rakṣoyakṣāñśuko martyānvaiśampāyana eva tu ||56||

57itihāsamimaṃ puṇyaṃ mahārthaṃ vedasaṃmitam
|
vyāsoktaṃ śrūyate yena kṛtvā brāhmṇamagrataḥ ||57||

58sa naraḥ sarvakāmāṃśca kīrtiṃ prāpyeha śaunaka
|
gacchetparamikāṃ siddhimatra me nāsti saṃśayaḥ ||58||

59bhāratādhyayanātpuṇyādapi pādamadhīyataḥ
|
śraddhayā parayā bhaktyā śrāvyate cāpi yena tu
|
ya imāṃ saṃhitāṃ puṇyāṃ putramadhyāpayacchukam ||59||

60mātāpitṛsahasrāṇi putradāraśatāni ca
|
saṃsāreṣvanubhūtāni yānti yāsyanti cāpare ||60||

61harṣasthānasahasrāṇi bhayasthānaśatāni ca
|
divasedivase mūḍhamāviśanti na paṇḍitam ||61||

62ūrdhvabāhurviraumyeṣa na ca kaścicchṛṇoti me
|
dharmādarthaśca kāmaśca sa kimarthaṃ na sevyate ||62||

63na jātu kāmānna bhayānna lobhā- ddharmaṃ tyajejjīvitasyāpi hetoḥ
|
nityo dharmaḥ sukhaduḥkhe tvanithye jīvo nityo heturasya tvanityaḥ ||63||

64imāṃ bhāratasāvitrīṃ prātarutthāyaḥ yaḥ paṭhet
|
sa bhārataphalaṃ prāpya paraṃ brahmādhigacchati ||64||

65yathā samudro bhagavānyathā hi himavāngiriḥ
|
khyātāvubhau ratnanidhī tathā bhāratamucyate ||65||

66kārṣṇaṃ devamimaṃ vidvāñśrāvayitvā'rthamaśnute
|
idaṃ bhāratamākhyānaṃ yaḥ paṭhetsusamāhitaḥ
|
sa gacchetparamāṃ siddhimiti me nāsti saṃśayaḥ ||66||

67dvaipāyanoṣṭhapuṭanissṛtamaprameyaṃ puṇyaṃ pavitramatha pāpaharaṃ śivaṃ ca
|
yo bhārataṃ samadhigacchati vācyamānaṃ kiṃ tasya puṣkarajalairabhiṣecanena ||67||

68yo gośataṃ kanakaśṛṅgamayaṃ dadāti viprāya vedaviduṣe subahuśrutāya
|
puṇyāṃ ca bhāratakathāṃ satataṃ śṛṇoti tulyaṃ phalaṃ bhavati tasya ca tasya caiva ||68||
