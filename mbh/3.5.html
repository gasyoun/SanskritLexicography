Mahābhārata 3.5 (Āraṇyakaparvan)

1vaiśampāyana uvāca|
pāṇḍavāstu vane vāsamuddiśya bharatarṣabhāḥ
|
prayayurjāhnavīkūlātkurukṣetraṃ sahānugāḥ||1||

2sarasvatīdṛṣadvatyau yamunāṃ ca niṣevya te
|
yayurvanenaiva vanaṃ satataṃ paścimāṃ diśam||2||

3tataḥ sarasvatīkūle sameṣu marudhanvasu
|
kāmyakaṃ nāma dadṛśurvanaṃ munijanapriyam||3||

4tatra te nyavasanvīrā vane bahumṛgadvije
|
anvāsyamānā munibhiḥ sāntvyamānāśca bhārata||4||

5vidurastvatha pāṇḍūnāṃ sadā darśanalālasaḥ
|
jagāmaikarathenaiva kāmyakaṃ vanamṛddhimat||5||

6tato gatvā viduraḥ kāmyakaṃ tacchīghrairaśvairvāhinā syandanena
|
dadarśāsīnaṃ dharmātmānaṃ vivikte sārdhaṃ draupadyā bhrātṛbhirbrāhmaṇaiśca||6||

7tato'paśyadviduraṃ tūrṇamārādabhyāyāntaṃ satyasandhaḥ sa rājā
|
athābravīdbhrātaraṃ bhīmasenaṃ kiṃ nu kṣattā vakṣyati naḥ sametya||7||

8kaccinnāyaṃ vacanātsaubalasya samāhvātā devanāyopayātaḥ
|
kaccitkṣudraḥ śakunirnāyudhāni jeṣyatyasmānpunarevākṣavatyām||8||

9samāhūtaḥ kenacidādraveti nāhaṃ śakto bhīmasenāpayātum
|
gāṇḍīve ca saṃśayite kathaṃ nu rājyaprāptiḥ saṃśayitā bhavennaḥ||9||

10vaiśampāyana uvāca|
tata utthāya viduraṃ pāṇḍaveyāḥ pratyagṛhṇannṛpate sarva eva
|
taiḥ satkṛtaḥ sa ca tānājamīḍho yathocitaṃ pāṇḍuputrānsameyāt||10||

11samāśvastaṃ viduraṃ te nararṣabhāstato'pṛcchannāgamanāya hetum
|
sa cāpi tebhyo vistarataḥ śaśaṃsa yathāvṛtto dhṛtarāṣṭro'mbikeyaḥ||11||

12vidura uvāca|
avocanmāṃ dhṛtarāṣṭro'nuguptamajātaśatro parigṛhyābhipūjya
|
evaṃ gate samatāmabhyupetya pathyaṃ teṣāṃ mama caiva bravīhi||12||

13mayāpyuktaṃ yatkṣamaṃ kauravāṇāṃ hitaṃ pathyaṃ dhṛtarāṣṭrasya caiva
|
tadvai tasmai na rucāmabhyupaiti tataścāhaṃ kṣamamanyanna manye||13||

14paraṃ śreyaḥ pāṇḍaveyā mayoktaṃ na me tacca śrutavānāmbikeyaḥ
|
yathāturasyeva hi pathyamannaṃ na rocatesmāsya taducyamānam||14||

15na śreyase nīyate'jātaśatro strīśrotriyasyeva gṛhe praduṣṭā
|
dhruvaṃ naro cedbharatarṣabhasya patiḥ kumāryā iva ṣaṣṭivarṣaḥ||15||

16dhruvaṃ vināśo nṛpa kauravāṇāṃ na vai śreyo dhṛtarāṣṭraḥ paraiti
|
yathā ca parṇe puṣkarasyāvasiktaṃ jalaṃ na tiṣṭhetpathyamuktaṃ tathāsmin||16||

17tataḥ kruddho dhṛtarāṣṭro'bravīnmāṃ yasminśraddhā bhārata tatra yāhi
|
nāhaṃ bhūyaḥ kāmaye tvāṃ sahāyaṃ mahīmimāṃ pālayituṃ puraṃ vā||17||

18so'haṃ tyakto dhṛtarāṣṭreṇa rājñā praśāsituṃ tvāmupayāto narendra
|
tadvai sarvaṃ yanmayoktaṃ sabhāyāṃ taddhāryatāṃ yatpravakṣyāmi bhūyaḥ||18||

19kleśaistīvrairyujyamānaḥ sapatnaiḥ kṣamāṃ kurvankālamupāsate yaḥ
|
saṃvardhayanstokamivāgnimātmavān sa vai bhuṅkte pṛthivīmeka eva||19||

20yasyāvibhaktaṃ vasu rājansahāyaistasya duḥkhe'pyaṃśabhājaḥ sahāyāḥ
|
sahāyānāmeṣa saṅgrahaṇe'bhyupāyaḥ sahāyāptau pṛthivīprāptimāhuḥ||20||

21satyaṃ śreṣṭhaṃ pāṇḍavavipralapaṃ tulyaṃ cānnaṃ saha bhojyaṃ sahāyaiḥ
|
ātmā caiṣāmagrato na sma pūjya evaṃvṛttirvardhate bhūmipālaḥ||21||

22yudhiṣṭhira uvāca|
evaṃ kariṣyāmi yathā bravīṣi parāṃ buddhimupagamyāpramattaḥ
|
yaccāpyanyaddeśakālopapannaṃ tadvai vācyaṃ tatkariṣyāmi kṛtsnam||22||
