Mahābhārata 5.90 (Udyogaparvan)

1vaiśampāyana uvāca|
athopagamya viduramaparāhṇe janārdanaḥ
|
pitṛṣvasāraṃ sa pṛthāmabhyagacchadarindamaḥ ||1||

2sā dṛṣṭvā kṛṣṇamāyāntaṃ prasannādityavarcasam
|
kaṇṭhe gṛhītvā prākrośat smarantī tanayān pṛthā ||2||

3teṣāṃ sattvavatāṃ madhye govindaṃ sahacāriṇam
|
cirasya dṛṣṭvā vārṣṇeyaṃ bāṣpamāhārayat pṛthā ||3||

4sā'bravīt kṛṣṇamāsīnaṃ kṛtātithyaṃ yudhāṃ patim
|
bāṣpagadgadapūrṇena mukhena pariśuṣyatā ||4||

5ye te bālyāt prabhṛtyeva guruśuśrūṣaṇe ratāḥ
|
parasparasya suhṛdaḥ sammatāḥ samacetasaḥ
|
nikṛtyā bhraṃśitā rājyājjanārhā nirjanaṃ gatāḥ ||5||

6vinītakrodhaharṣāśca brahmaṇyāḥ satyavādinaḥ
|
tyaktvā priyasukhe pārthā rudatīmapahāya mām ||6||

7ahārṣuśca vanaṃ yāntaḥ samūlaṃ hṛdayaṃ mama
|
atadarhā mahātmānaḥ kathaṃ keśava pāṇḍavāḥ ||7||

8ūṣurmahāvane tāta siṃha-vyāghra-gajākule
|
bālā vihīnāḥ pitrā te mayā satatalālitāḥ ||8||

9apaśyantaśca pitarau kathamūṣurmahāvane
|
śaṅkhadundubhinirghoṣairmṛdaṅgairveṇuniḥsvanaiḥ ||9||

10pāṇḍavāḥ samabodhyanta bālyātprabhṛti keśava
|
ye sma vāraṇaśabdena hayānāṃ heṣitena ca ||10||

11rathanemininādaiśca vyabodhyanta tadā gṛhe
|
śaṅkhabherīninādena veṇuvīṇānunādinā ||11||

12puṇyāhaghoṣamiśreṇa pūjyamānā dvijātibhiḥ
|
vastrai ratnairalaṅkāraiḥ pūjayanto dvijanmanaḥ ||12||

13gīrbhirmaṅgalayuktābhirbrāhmaṇānāṃ mahātmanām
|
arcitairarcanārhaiśca stuvadbhirabhinanditāḥ ||13||

14prāsādāgreṣvabodhyanta rāṅkavājinaśāyinaḥ
|
krūraṃ ca ninadaṃ śrutvā śvāpadānāṃ mahāvane ||14||

15na smopayānti nidrānte na tadarhā janārdana
|
bherī-mṛdaṅganinadaiḥ śaṅkha-vaiṇava-niḥsvanaiḥ ||15||

16strīṇāṃ gītaninādaiśca madhurairmadhusūdana
|
bandi-māgadha-sūtaiśca stuvadbhirbodhitāḥ katham ||16||

17mahāvaneṣvabodhyanta śvāpadānāṃ rutena ca
|
hrīmān satyadhṛtirdānto bhūtānāmanukampitā ||17||

18kāmadveṣau vaśe kṛtvā satāṃ vartmānuvartate
|
ambarīṣasya māndhāturyayāternahuṣasya ca ||18||

19bharatasya dilīpasya śiberauśīnarasya ca
|
rājarṣīṇāṃ purāṇānāṃ dhuraṃ dhatte durudvahām ||19||

20śīlavṛttopasampanno dharmajñaḥ satyasaṅgaraḥ
|
rājā sarvaguṇopetastrailokyasyāpi yo bhavet ||20||

21ajātaśatrurdharmātmā śuddhajāmbūnadaprabhaḥ
|
śreṣṭhaḥ kuruṣu sarveṣu dharmataḥ śrutavṛttataḥ
|
priyadarśo dīrghabhujaḥ kathaṃ kṛṣṇa yudhiṣṭhiraḥ ||21||

22yaḥ sa nāgāyutaprāṇo vātaraṃhā mahābalaḥ
|
sāmarṣaḥ pāṇḍavo nityaṃ priyo bhrātuḥ priyaṅkaraḥ ||22||

23kīcakasya tu sajñāteryo hantā madhusūdana
|
śūraḥ krodhavaśānāṃ ca hiḍimbasya bakasya ca ||23||

24parākrame śakrasamo mātariśvasamo bale
|
maheśvarasamaḥ krodhe bhīmaḥ praharatāṃ varaḥ ||24||

25krodhaṃ balamamarṣaṃ ca yo nidhāya paraṃtapaḥ
|
jitātmā pāṇḍavo'marṣī bhrātustiṣṭhati śāsane ||25||

26tejorāśiṃ mahātmānaṃ variṣṭhamamitaujasam
|
bhīmaṃ pradarśanenāpi bhīmasenaṃ janārdanam ||26||

27taṃ mamācakṣva vārṣṇeya kathamadya vṛkodaraḥ
|
āste parighabāhuḥ sa madhyamaḥ pāṇḍavo balī ||27||

28arjunenārjuno yaḥ sa kṛṣṇa bāhusahasriṇā
|
dvibāhuḥ spardhate nityamatītenāpi keśava ||28||

29kṣipatyekena vegena pañca bāṇaśatāni yaḥ
|
iṣvastre sadṛśo rājñaḥ kārtavīryasya pāṇḍavaḥ ||29||

30tejasā''dityasadṛśo maharṣisadṛśo dame
|
kṣamayā pṛthivītulyo mahendrasamavikramaḥ ||30||

31ādhirājyaṃ mahaddīptaṃ prathitaṃ madhusūdana
|
āhṛtaṃ yena vīryeṇa kurūṇāṃ sarvarājasu ||31||

32yasya bāhubalaṃ sarve pāṇḍavāḥ paryupāsate
|
sa sarvarathināṃ śreṣṭhaḥ pāṇḍavaḥ satyavikramaḥ ||32||

33yaṃ gatvā'bhimukhaḥ saṃkhye na jīvan kaścidāvrajet
|
yo jetā sarvabhūtānāmajeyo jiṣṇuracyuta ||33||

34yo'pāśrayaḥ pāṇḍavānāṃ devānāmiva vāsavaḥ
|
sa te bhrātā sakhā caiva kathamadya dhanañjayaḥ ||34||

35dayāvān sarvabhūteṣu hrīniṣevo mahāstravit
|
mṛduśca sukumāraśca dhārmikaśca priyaśca me ||35||

36sahadevo maheṣvāsaḥ śūraḥ samiti śobhanaḥ
|
bhrātṝṇāṃ kṛṣṇa śuśrūṣurdharmārthakuśalo yuvā ||36||

37sadaiva sahadevasya bhrātaro madhusūdana
|
vṛttaṃ kalyāṇavṛttasya pūjayanti mahātmanaḥ ||37||

38jyeṣṭhopacāyinaṃ vīraṃ sahadevaṃ yudhāṃ patim
|
śuśrūṣuṃ mama vārṣṇeya mādrīputraṃ pracakṣva me ||38||

39sukumāro yuvā śūro darśanīyaśca pāṇḍavaḥ
|
bhrātṝṇāṃ caiva sarveṣāṃ priyaḥ prāṇo bahiścaraḥ ||39||

40citrayodhī ca nakulo maheṣvāso mahābalaḥ
|
kaccitsa kuśalī kṛṣṇa vatso mama sukhaidhitaḥ ||40||

41sukhocitamaduḥkhārhaṃ sukumāraṃ mahāratham
|
api jātu mahābāho paśyeyaṃ nakulaṃ punaḥ ||41||

42pakṣmasampātaje kāle nakulena vinākṛtā
|
na labhāmi dhṛtiṃ vīra sā'dya jīvāmi paśya mām ||42||

43sarvaiḥ putraiḥ priyatarā draupadī me janārdana
|
kulīnā rūpasampannā sarvaiḥ samuditā guṇaiḥ ||43||

44putralokāt patilokaṃ vṛṇvānā satyavādinī
|
priyān putrān parityajya pāṇḍavānanurudhyate ||44||

45mahābhijanasampannā sarvakāmaiḥ supūjitā
|
īśvarī sarvakalyāṇī draupadī kathamacyuta ||45||

46yatibhiḥ pañcabhiḥ śūrairagnikalpaiḥ prahāribhiḥ
|
upapannā maheṣvāsairdraupadī duḥkhabhāginī ||46||

47caturdaśamidaṃ varṣaṃ yannāpaśyamarindama
|
putrādibhiḥ paridyūnāṃ draupadīṃ satyavādinīm ||47||

48na nūnaṃ karmabhiḥ puṇyairaśnute puruṣaḥ sukham
|
draupadī cettathāvṛttā nāśnute sukhamavyayam ||48||

49na priyo mama kṛṣṇāyā bībhatsurna yudhiṣṭhiraḥ
|
bhīmaseno yamau vā'pi yadapaśyaṃ sabhāgatām ||49||

50na me duḥkhataraṃ kiñcidbhūtapūrvaṃ tato'dhikam
|
strīdharmiṇīṃ draupadīṃ yacchvaśurāṇāṃ samīpagām ||50||

51ānāyitāmanāryeṇa krodhalobhānuvartinā
|
sarve prekṣanta kurava ekavastrāṃ sabhāgatām ||51||

52tatraiva dhṛtarāṣṭraśca mahārājaśca bāhlikaḥ
|
kṛpaśca somadattaśca nirviṇṇāḥ kuravastathā ||52||

53tasyāṃ saṃsadi sarveṣāṃ kṣattāraṃ pūjayāmyaham
|
vṛttena hi bhavatyāryo na dhanena na vidyayā ||53||

54tasya kṛṣṇa mahābuddhergaṃbhīrasya mahātmanaḥ
|
kṣattuḥ śīlamalaṅkāro lokānviṣṭabhya tiṣṭhati ||54||

55vaiśampāyana uvāca|
sā śokārtā ca hṛṣṭā ca dṛṣṭvā govindamāgatam
|
nānāvidhāni duḥkhāni sarvāṇyevānvakīrtayat ||55||

56pūrvairācaritaṃ yattatkurājabhirarindama
|
akṣadyūtaṃ mṛgavadhaḥ kaccideṣāṃ sukhāvaham ||56||

57tanmāṃ dahati yatkṛṣṇā sabhāyāṃ kurusannidhau
|
dhārtarāṣṭraiḥ parikliṣṭā yathā nakuśalaṃ tathā ||57||

58nirvāsanaṃ ca nagarāt pravrajyā ca paraṃtapa
|
nānāvidhānāṃ duḥkhānāmabhijñā'smi janārdana ||58||

59ajñātacaryā bālānāmavarodhaśca mādhava
|
na me kleśatamaṃ tatsyāt putraiḥ saha paraṃtapa ||59||

60duryodhanena nikṛtā varṣamadya caturdaśam
|
duḥkhādapi sukhaṃ naḥ syādyadi puṇyaphalakṣayaḥ ||60||

61na me viśeṣo jātvāsīt dhārtarāṣṭreṣu pāṇḍavaiḥ
|
tena satyena kṛṣṇa tvāṃ hatāmitraṃ śriyā vṛtam
|
asmādvimuktaṃ saṅgrāmāt paśyeyaṃ pāṇḍavaiḥ saha ||61||

62naiva śakyāḥ parājetuṃ sarvaṃ hyeṣāṃ tathāvidham
|
pitaraṃ tveva garheyaṃ nātmānaṃ na suyodhanam ||62||

63yenāhaṃ kuntibhojāya dhanaṃ vṛttairivārpitā
|
bālāṃ māmāryakastubhyaṃ krīḍantīṃ kanduhastikām ||63||

64adāttu kuntibhojāya sakhā sakhye mahātmane
|
sā'haṃ pitrā ca nikṛtā śvaśuraiśca paraṃtapa
|
atyantaduḥkhitā kṛṣṇā kiṃ jīvitaphalaṃ mama ||64||

65yanmāṃ vāgabravīnnaktaṃ sūtake savyasācinaḥ
|
putraste pṛthivīṃ jetā yaśaścāsya divaṃ spṛśet ||65||

66hatvā kurūn mahājanye rājyaṃ prāpya dhanañjayaḥ
|
bhrātṛbhiḥ saha kaunteyastrīn medhānāhariṣyati ||66||

67nāhaṃ tāmabhyasūyāmi namo dharmāya vedhase
|
kṛṣṇāya mahate nityaṃ dharmo dhārayati prajāḥ ||67||

68dharmaścedasti vārṣṇeya yathā vāgabhyabhāṣata
|
tvaṃ cāpi tattathā kṛṣṇa sarvaṃ saṃpādayiṣyasi ||68||

69na māṃ mādhava vaidhavyaṃ nārthanāśo na vairatā
|
tathā śokāya dahati yathā putrairvinābhavaḥ ||69||

70yā'haṃ gāṇḍīvadhanvānaṃ sarvaśastrabhṛtāṃ varam
|
dhanañjayaṃ na paśyāmi kā śāntirhṛdayasya me
|
itaścaturdaśaṃ varṣaṃ yannāpaśyaṃ yudhiṣṭhiram ||70||

71dhanañjayaṃ ca govinda yamau taṃ ca vṛkodaram
|
jīvanāśaṃ pranaṣṭānāṃ śrāddhaṃ kurvanti mānavāḥ ||71||

72arthataste mama mṛtāsteṣāṃ cāhaṃ janārdana
|
brūyā mādhava rājānaṃ dharmātmānaṃ yudhiṣṭhiram ||72||

73bhūyāṃste hīyate dharmo mā putraka vṛthā kṛthāḥ
|
parāśrayā vāsudeva yā jīvati dhigastu tām ||73||

74vṛtteḥ kārpaṇyalabdhāyā apratiṣṭhaiva jyāyasī
|
adho dhanañjayaṃ brūyā nityodyuktaṃ vṛkodaram ||74||

75yadarthaṃ kṣatriyā sūte tasya kālo'yamāgataḥ
|
asmiṃścedāgate kāle mithyā cātikramiṣyati ||75||

76lokasaṃbhāvitāḥ santaḥ sunṛśaṃsaṃ kariṣyatha
|
nṛśaṃsena ca vo yuktāṃstyajeyaṃ śāśvatīḥ samāḥ ||76||

77kāle hi samanuprāpte tyaktavyamapi jīvanam
|
mādrīputrau ca vaktavyau kṣatradharmaratau sadā ||77||

78vikrameṇārjitān bhogān vṛṇītaṃ jīvitādapi
|
vikramādhigatā hyarthāḥ kṣatradharmeṇa jīvataḥ ||78||

79mano manuṣyasya sadā prīṇanti puruṣottama
|
gatvā brūhi mahābāho sarvaśastrabhṛtāṃ varam ||79||

80arjunaṃ pāṇḍavaṃ vīraṃ draupadyāḥ padavīṃ cara
|
viditau hi tavātyantaṃ kruddhau tau tu yathāntakau ||80||

81bhīmārjunau nayetāṃ hi devānapi parāṃ gatim
|
tayoścaitadavajñānaṃ yatsā kṛṣṇā sabhāṃ gatā ||81||

82duḥśāsanaśca karṇaśca paruṣāṇyabhyabhāṣatām
|
duryodhano bhīmasenamabhyagacchanmanasvinam ||82||

83paśyatāṃ kuru‌mukhyānāṃ tasya drakṣyati yatphalam
|
na hi vairaṃ samāsādya praśāmyati vṛkodaraḥ ||83||

84sucirādapi bhīmasya na hi vairaṃ praśāmyati
|
yāvadantaṃ na nayati śātravāñchatrukarśanaḥ ||84||

85na duḥkhaṃ rājyaharaṇaṃ na ca dyūte parājayaḥ
|
pravrājanaṃ tu putrāṇāṃ na me tadduḥkhakāraṇam ||85||

86yattu sā bṛhatī śyāmā ekavastrā sabhāṃ gatā
|
aśṛṇot paruṣā vācaḥ kiṃ nu duḥkhataraṃ tataḥ ||86||

87strīdhirmiṇī varārohā kṣatradharmaratā sadā
|
nābhyagacchattadā nāthaṃ kṛṣṇā nāthavatī satī ||87||

88yasyā mama saputrāyāstvaṃ nātho madhusūdana
|
rāmaśca balināṃ śreṣṭhaḥ pradyumnaśca mahārathaḥ ||88||

89sā'hamevaṃvidhaṃ duḥkhaṃ saheyaṃ puruṣottama
|
bhīme jīvati durdharṣe vijaye cāpalāyini ||89||

90vaiśampāyana uvāca|
tata āśvāsayāmāsa putrādhibhirabhiplutām
|
pitṛṣvasāraṃ śocantīṃ śauriḥ pārthasakhaḥ pṛthām ||90||

91vāsudeva uvāca|
kā tu sīmantinī tvādṛk lokeṣvasti pitṛṣvasaḥ
|
śūrasya rājño duhitā ājamīḍhakulaṃ gatā ||91||

92mahākulīnā bhavatī hradāddhradamivāgatā
|
īśvarī sarvakalyāṇī bhartrā paramapūjitā ||92||

93vīrasūrvīrapatnī tvaṃ sarvaiḥ samuditā guṇaiḥ
|
sukhaduḥkhe mahāprājñe tvādṛśī soḍhumarhati ||93||

94nidrātandre krodhaharṣau śrutpipāse himātapau
|
etāni pārthā nirjitya nityaṃ vīrasukhe ratāḥ ||94||

95tyaktagrāmyasukhāḥ pārthā nityaṃ vīrasukhapriyāḥ
|
na tu svalpena tuṣyeyurmahotsāhā mahābalāḥ ||95||

96antaṃ dhīrā niṣevante madhyaṃ grāmyasukhapriyāḥ
|
uttamāṃśca parikleśān bhogāṃścātīva mānuṣān ||96||

97anteṣu remire dhīrā na te madhyeṣu remire
|
antaprāptiṃ sukhāmāhurduḥkhamantarametayoḥ ||97||

98abhivādayanti bhavatīṃ pāṇḍavāḥ saha kṛṣṇayā
|
ātmānaṃ ca kuśalinaṃ nivedyāhuranāmayam ||98||

99arogān sarvasiddhārthān kṣipraṃ drakṣyasi pāṇḍavān
|
īśvarān sarvalokasya hatāmitrān śriyāvṛtān ||99||

100evamāśvāsitā kuntī pratyuvāca janārdanam
|
putrādibhirabhidhvastā nigṛhyābuddhijaṃ tamaḥ ||100||

101kuntyuvāca
|
yadyatteṣāṃ mahābāho pathyaṃ syānmadhusūdana
|
yathā yathā tvaṃ manyethāḥ kuryāḥ kṛṣṇa tathā tathā ||101||

102avilopena dharmasya anikṛtyā paraṃtapa
|
prabhāvajñā'smi te kṛṣṇa satyasyābhijanasya ca ||102||

103vyavasthāyāṃ ca mitreṣu buddhivikramayostathā
|
tvameva naḥ kule dharmastvaṃ satyaṃ tvaṃ tapo mahat ||103||

104tvaṃ trātā tvaṃ mahadbrahma tvayi sarvaṃ pratiṣṭhitam
|
yathaivāttha tathaivaitattvayi satyaṃ bhaviṣyati ||104||

105vaiśampāyana uvāca|
tāmāmantrya ca govindaḥ kṛtvā cābhipradakṣiṇam
|
prātiṣṭhata mahābāhurduryodhanagṛhān prati ||105||
