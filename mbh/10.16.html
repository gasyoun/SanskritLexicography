Mahābhārata 10.16 (Sauptikaparvan)

1vaiśampāyana uvāca|
tadājñāya hṛṣīkeśo visṛṣṭaṃ pāpakarmaṇā
|
hṛṣyamāṇa idaṃ vākyaṃ drauṇiṃ pratyabravīttadā||1||

2virāṭasya sutāṃ pūrvaṃ snuṣāṃ gāṇḍīvadhanvanaḥ
|
upaplavyagatāṃ dṛṣṭvā vratavānbrāhmaṇo'bravīt||2||

3parikṣīṇeṣu kuruṣu putrastava bhaviṣyati
|
etadasya parikṣitvaṃ garbhasthasya bhaviṣyati||3||

4tasya tadvacanaṃ sādhoḥ satyametadbhaviṣyati
|
parikṣidbhavitā hyeṣāṃ punarvaṃśakaraḥ sutaḥ||4||

5evaṃ bruvāṇaṃ govindaṃ sātvatāṃ pravaraṃ tadā
|
drauṇiḥ paramasaṃrabdhaḥ pratyuvācedamuttaram||5||

6naitadevaṃ yathā''ttha tvaṃ pakṣapātena keśava
|
vacanaṃ puṇḍarīkākṣa na ca madvākyamanyathā||6||

7patiṣyati tadasraṃ hi garbhe tasyā mayodyatam
|
virāṭaduhituḥ kṛṣṇa yaṃ tvaṃ rakṣitumicchasi||7||

8śrībhagavānuvāca|
amoghaḥ paramāstrasya pātastasya bhaviṣyati
|
sa tu garbho mṛto jāto dīrghamāyuravāpsyati||8||

9tvāṃ tu kāpuruṣaṃ pāpaṃ viduḥ sarve manīṣiṇaḥ
|
asakṛtpāpakarmāṇaṃ bālajīvitaghātakam||9||

10tasmāttvamasya pāpasya karmaṇaḥ phalaprāpnuhi
|
trīṇi varṣasahasrāṇi cariṣyasi mahīmimām||10||

11aprāpnuvankvacitkāñcitsaṃvidaṃ jātu kenacit
|
nirjanānasahāyastvaṃ deśānpravicariṣyasi||11||

12bhavitrī na hi te kṣudrajanamadhyeṣu saṃsthitiḥ
|
pūyaśoṇitagandhī ca durgakāntārasaṃśrayaḥ||12||

13vicariṣyasi pāpātmā sarvavyādhisamanvitaḥ
|
vayaḥ prāpya parikṣittu vedavratamavāpya ca||13||

14kṛpācchāradvatācchūraḥ sarvāstrāṇyupapatsyate
|
viditvā paramāstrāṇi kṣatradharmavrate sthitaḥ||14||

15paṣṭiṃ varṣāṇi dharmātmā vasudhāṃ pālayiṣyati
|
itaścordhvaṃ mahābāhuḥ kururājo bhaviṣyati||15||

16parikṣinnāma nṛpatirbhiṣataste sudurmate
|
ahaṃ taṃ jīvayiṣyāmi dagdhaṃ śastrāgnitejasā
|
paśya me tapaso vīryaṃ satyasya ca narādhama||16||

17vyāsa uvāca|
yasmādanādṛtya kṛtaṃ tvayā'smānkarma dāruṇam
|
brāhmaṇasya sataścaiva yasmātte vṛttamīdṛśam||17||

18tasmādyaddevakīputra uktavānuttamaṃ vacaḥ
|
asaṃśayaṃ te tadbhāvi kṣatradharmastvayā''śritaḥ||18||

19aśvatthāmovāca|
sahaiva bhavatā brahmaṃsthāsyāmi puruṣeṣviha
|
satyavāgastu bhagavānayaṃ ca puruṣottamaḥ||19||

20vaiśampāyana uvāca|
pradāyātha maṇiṃ drauṇiḥ pāṇḍavānāṃ mahātmanām
|
jagāma vimanāsteṣāṃ sarveṣāṃ paśyatāṃ vanam||20||

21pāṇḍavaścāpi govindaṃ puraskṛtya hatadviṣaḥ
|
kṛṣṇadvaipāyanaṃ caiva nāradaṃ ca mahāmunim||21||

22droṇaputrasya sahajaṃ maṇimādāya satvarāḥ
|
draupadīmabhyadhāvanta prāyopetāṃ manasvinīm||22||

23vaiśampāyana uvāca|
tataste puruṣavyāghrāḥ sadaśvairanilopamaiḥ
|
abhyayuḥ sahadāśārhāḥ śibiraṃ punareva hi||23||

24avatīrya rathebhyastu tvaramāṇā mahārathāḥ
|
dadṛśurdraupadīṃ hṛṣṭāmārtāmārtatarāḥ svayam||24||

25tāmupetya nirānandāṃ duḥkhaśokasamanvitām
|
parivārya vyatiṣṭhanta pāṇḍavāḥ sahakeśavāḥ||25||

26tato rājñā'bhyanujñāto bhīmaseno mahābalaḥ
|
pradadau taṃ maṇiṃ divyaṃ vacanaṃ cedamabravīt||26||

27ayaṃ bhadre tava maṇiḥ putrahanturjitaḥ sa te
|
uttiṣṭha śokamutsṛjya kṣātradharmamanusmara||27||

28prayāṇe vāsudevasya śamārthamasitekṣaṇe
|
yānyuktāni tvayā bhīru vākyāni madhughātini||28||

29naiva me patayaḥ santi na putrā bhrātaro na ca
|
na vai tvamiti govinda śamamicchati rājani||29||

30uktavatyasi tīvrāṇi vākyāni puruṣottamam
|
kṣatradharmānurūpāṇi tāni saṃsmartumarhasi||30||

31hato duryodhanaḥ pāpo rājyasya paripanthikaḥ
|
duḥśāsanasya rudhiraṃ pītaṃ visphurato mayā||31||

32vairasya gatamānṛṇyaṃ na sma vācyā vivakṣatām
|
jitvā mukto droṇaputro brāhmaṇyādgauraveṇa ca||32||

33yaśo'sya patitaṃ devi śarīraṃ tvavaśeṣitam
|
viyojitaśca maṇinā bhraṃśitaścāyudhaṃ bhuvi||33||

34draupadyuvāca|
kevalānṛṇyamāptāsmi guruputro gururmama
|
śirasyetaṃ maṇiṃ rājā pratibadhnātu bhārata||34||

35taṃ gṛhītvā tato rājā śirasyevākarottadā
|
gurorucchiṣṭamityeva draupadyā vacanādapi||35||

36tato divyaṃ maṇivaraṃ śirasā dhārayanprabhuḥ
|
śuśubhe sa tadā rājā sacandra iva parvataḥ||36||

37uttasthau putraśokārtā tataḥ kṛṣṇā manasvinī
|
kṛṣṇaṃ cāpi mahābāhuḥ paripapraccha dharmarāṭ||37||
