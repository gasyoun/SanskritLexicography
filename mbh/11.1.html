Mahābhārata 11.1 (Strīparvan)

1janamejaya uvāca|hate duryodhane caiva hate sainye ca sarvaśaḥ
|
dhṛtarāṣṭro mahārāja śrutvā kimakaronmune||1||

2tathaiva kauravo rājā dharmaputro mahāmanāḥ
|
kṛpaprabhṛtayaścaiva kimakurvata te trayaḥ||2||

3aśvatthāmnaḥ śrutaṃ karma śāpaścānyonyakāritaḥ
|
vṛttānmutraṃ brūhi yadabhāṣata sañjayaḥ||3||

4vaiśampāyana uvāca|hate putraśate dīnaṃ chinnaśākhamiva drumam
|
putraśokābhisantaptaṃ dhṛtarāṣṭraṃ mahīpatim||4||

5dhyānamūkatvamāpannaṃ cintayā samabhiplutam
|
abhigamya mahārāja sañjayo vākyamabravīt||5||

6kiṃ śocasi mahārāja nāsti śoke sahāyatā
|
akṣauhiṇyo hatāścāṣṭau daśa caiva viśāmpate||6||

7nirjiteyaṃ vasumatī śūnyā samprati kevalā
|
nānādigbhyaḥ samāgamya nānādeśyā narādhipāḥ||7||

8sahaiva tava putreṇa sarve vai nidhanaṃ gatāḥ
|
pitṝṇāṃ putrapautrāṇāṃ jñātīnāṃ suhṛdāṃ tathā
|
gurūṇāṃ cānupūrvyeṇa ye cānye'nucarā hatāḥ||8||

9vaiśampāyana uvāca|tacchrutvā karuṇaṃ vākyaṃ putrapautravadhārditaḥ
|
papāta bhuvi durdharṣo vātāhata iva drumaḥ||9||

10dhṛtarāṣṭra uvāca|hataputro hatāmātyo hatasarvasuhṛjjanaḥ
|
duḥkhaṃ nūnaṃ gamiṣyāmi vicaranpṛthivīmimām||10||

11kinnu bandhuvihīnasya jīvitena mamādya vai
|
lūnapakṣasya iva me jarājīrṇasya pakṣiṇaḥ||11||

12hṛtarājyo hatabandhurhataputraśca vai tathā
|
na bhrājiṣye mahāprājña kṣīṇaraśmirivāṃśumān||12||

13na kṛtaṃ suhṛdāṃ vākyaṃ jāmadagnyasya jalpataḥ
|
nāradasya ca devarṣeḥ kṛṣṇadvaipāyanasya ca||13||

14sabhāmadhye tu kṛṣṇena yacchreyo'bhihitaṃ mama
|
alaṃ vaireṇa te rājanputraḥ saṅgṛdyatāmiti||14||

15tacca vākyamakṛtvā'haṃ bhṛśaṃ tapyāmi durmatiḥ
|
na hi śrotā'smi bhīṣmasya dharmayuktaṃ prabhāṣitam||15||

16duryodhanasya ca tathā vṛṣabhasyeva nardataḥ
|
duḥśāsanavadhaṃ śrutvā karṇasya ca viparyayam||16||

17droṇasūryoparāgaṃ ca hṛdayaṃ me vidīryate
|
na smarāmyātmanaḥ kiñcitpurā sañjaya duṣkṛtam||17||

18yasyedaṃ phalamadyeha mayā mūḍhena bhujyate
|
nūnaṃ vyapakṛtaṃ kiñcinmayā pūrveṣu janmasu||18||

19yena māṃ duḥkhabhāgeṣu dhātā karmasu yuktavān
|
pariṇāmaśca vayasaḥ sarvabandhukṣayaśca me||19||

20suhṛnmitravināśaśca daivayogādupāgataḥ
|
ko'nyo'sti duḥkhitataro matto'nyo hi pumānbhuvi||20||

21tanmamādyaiva paśyantu pāṇḍavāḥ saṃśitavratāḥ
|
vivṛtaṃ brahmalokasya dīrghamadhvānamāsthitam||21||

22vaiśampāyana uvāca|tasya lālapyamānasya bahuśokaṃ vitanvataḥ
|
śokāpahaṃ narendrasya sañjayo vākyamabravīt||22||

23śokaṃ rājanvyapanuda śrutāste vedaniścayāḥ
|
śāstrāgamāśca vividhā vṛddhebhyo nṛpasattama||23||

24sṛñjaye putraśokārte yadūcurmunayaḥ purā
|
yathā yauvanajaṃ darpamāsthite te sute nṛpa||24||

25na tvayā suhṛdāṃ vākyaṃ bruvatāmavadhāritam
|
svārthaśca na kṛtaḥ kaścillubdhena phalagṛddhinā||25||

26asinaivaikadhāreṇa svabuddhyā tu viceṣṭitam
|
prāyaśo'vṛttasampannāḥ satataṃ paryupāsitāḥ||26||

27yasya duḥśāsano mantrī rādheyaśca durātmavān
|
śakuniścaiva duṣṭātmā citrasenaśca durmatiḥ||27||

28śalyaśca yena vai sarvaṃ śalyabhūtaṃ kṛtaṃ jagat
|
kuruvṛddhasya bhīṣmasya gāndhāryā vidurasya ca||28||

29droṇasya ca mahārāja kṛpasya ca śaradvataḥ
|
kṛṣṇasya ca mahābāho nāradasya ca dhīmataḥ
|
ṛṣīṇāṃ ca tathā'nyeṣāṃ vyāsasyāmitatejasaḥ||29||

30na kṛtaṃ tena vacanaṃ tava putreṇa bhārata
|
na dharmaḥ satkṛtaḥ kaścinnityaṃ yuddhamabhīpsatā||30||

31alpabuddhirahaṅkārī nityaṃ yuddhamiti bruvan
|
krūro durmarṣaṇo nityamasantuṣṭaśca vīryavān||31||

32śrutavānasi medhāvī satyavāṃścaiva nityadā
|
na muhyantīdṛśāḥ santo buddhimanto bhavādṛśāḥ||32||

33na dharmaḥ satkṛtaḥ kaścittava putreṇa māriṣa
|
kṣapitāḥ kṣatriyāḥ sarve śatrūṇāṃ vardhitaṃ yaśaḥ||33||

34madhyastho hi tvamapyāsīrna kṣamaṃ kiñciduktavān
|
durdhareṇa tvayā bhārastulayā na samaṃ dhṛtaḥ||34||

35ādāveva manuṣyeṇa vartitavyaṃ yathākramam
|
yathā nātītamarthaṃ vai paścāttāpena yujyate||35||

36putragṛddhyā tvayā rājanpriyaṃ tasya cikīrṣitam
|
paścāttāpamimaṃ prāpto na tvaṃ śocitumarhasi||36||

37madhu yaḥ kevalaṃ dṛṣṭvā prapātaṃ nānupaśyati
|
sa bhraṣṭo madhulobhena śocatyeva yathā bhavān||37||

38arthānna śocanprāpnoti na śocanvindate phalam
|
na śocañśriyamāpnoti na śocanvindate param||38||

39svayamutpādayitvā'gniṃ vastreṇa pariveṣṭayan
|
dahyamāno manastāpaṃ bhajate na sa paṇḍitaḥ||39||

40tvayaiva sasutenāyaṃ vākyavāyusamīritaḥ
|
lobhājyena ca saṃsikto jvalitaḥ pārthapāvakaḥ||40||

41tasminsamiddhe patitāḥ śalabhā iva te sutāḥ
|
tānvai śarāgninirdagdhānna tvaṃ śocitumarhasi||41||

42yaccāśrupātakalilaṃ vadanaṃ vahase nṛpa
|
aśāstradṛṣṭametaddhi na praśaṃsanti paṇḍitāḥ||42||

43visphuliṅgā iva hyetāndahanti kila mānavān
|
jahīhi manyuṃ buddhyā vai dhārayātmānamātmanā||43||

44vaiśampāyana uvāca|evamāśvāsitastena sañjayena mahātmanā
|
viduro bhūya evāha buddhipūrvaṃ parantapa||44||
