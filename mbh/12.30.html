Mahābhārata 12.30 (Śāntiparvan)

1yudhiṣṭhira uvāca|
sa kathaṃ kāñcanaṣṭhīvī sañjayasya suto'bhavat
|
parvatena kimarthaṃ vā dattastena mamāra ca ||1||

2yadā varṣasahasrāyustadā bhavati mānavaḥ
|
kathamaprāptakaumāraḥ sañjayasya suto mṛtaḥ ||2||

3utāho nāmamātraṃ vai suvarṇaṣṭhīvino'bhavat
|
kathaṃ vā kāñcanaṣṭhīvītyetadicchāmi veditum ||3||

4śrīkṛṣṇa uvāca |
atra te varṇayiṣyāmi yathāvṛttaṃ janeśvara
|
nāradaḥ parvataścaiva dvāvṛṣī lokasattamau ||4||

5mātulo bhāgineyaśca devalokādihāgatau
|
vihartukāmau samprītyā mānuṣeṣu purā vibho ||5||

6haviḥ pavitrabhojyena devabhojyena caiva hi
|
nārado mātulaścaiva bhāgineyaśca parvataḥ ||6||

7tāvubhau tapasopetāvavanītalacāriṇau
|
bhuñjānau mānuṣānbhogān yathāvatparyadhāvatām ||7||

8prītimantau mudā yuktau samayaṃ caiva cakratuḥ
|
yo bhaveddhadi saṃkalpaḥ śubho vā yadi vā'śubhaḥ ||8||

9anyonyasya sa ākhyeyo mṛṣā śāpo'nyathā bhavet
|
tau tatheti pratijñāya maharṣī lokapūjitau ||9||

10sañjayaṃ śvaityamabhyetya rājānamidamūcatuḥ
|
āvāṃ bhavati vatsyāvaḥ kañcitkālaṃ hitāya te ||10||

11yathāvatpṛthivīpāla āvayoḥ praguṇī bhava
|
tatheti kṛtvā rājā tau satkṛtyopacacāra ha ||11||

12tataḥ kadācittau rājā mahātmānau tapodhanau
|
abravītparamaprītaḥ suteyaṃ varavarṇinī ||12||

13ekaiva mama kanyaiṣā yuvāṃ paricariṣyati
|
darśanīyā'navadyāṅgī śīlavṛttasamāhitā ||13||

14sukumārī kumārī ca padmakiñjalkasuprabhā
|
paramaṃ saumyamityuktaṃ tābhyāṃ rājā śaśāsa tām ||14||

15kanye viprāvupacara devavatpitṛvacca ha
|
sā tu kanyā tathetyuktvā pitaraṃ dharmacāriṇī ||15||

16yathānideśaṃ rājñastau satkṛtyopacacāra ha
|
tasyāstenopacāreṇa rūpeṇāpratimena ca ||16||

17nāradaṃ hṛcchayastūrṇaṃ sahasaivābhyapadyata
|
vavṛdhe hi tatastasya hṛdi kāmo mahātmanaḥ ||17||

18yathā śuklasya pakṣasya pravṛttau candramāḥ śanaiḥ
|
na ca taṃ bhāgineyāya parvatāya mahātmane ||18||

19śaśaṃsa hṛcchayaṃ tīvraṃ vrīḍamānaḥ sa dharmavit
|
tapasā ceṅgitaiścaiva parvato'tha bubodha tam ||19||

20kāmārtaṃ nāradaṃ kruddhaḥ śaśāpainaṃ tato bhṛśam
|
kṛtvā samayamavyagro bhavānvai sahito mayā ||20||

21yo bhaveddhṛdi saṃkalpaḥ śubho vā yadi vā'śubhaḥ
|
anyonyasya sa ākhyeya iti tadvai mṛṣā kṛtam ||21||

22bhavatā vacanaṃ brahmastasmādeṣa śapāmyaham
|
na hi kāmaṃ pravartantaṃ bhavānācaṣṭa me purā ||22||

23sukumāryāṃ kumāryāṃ te tasmādeṣa śapāmyaham
|
brahmacārī gururyasmāttapasvī brāhmaṇaśca san ||23||

24akārṣīḥ samayabhraṃśamāvābhyāṃ yaḥ kṛto mithaḥ
|
śapsye tasmātsusaṃkruddho bhavantaṃ taṃ nibodha me ||24||

25sukumārī ca te bhāryā bhaviṣyati na saṃśayaḥ
|
vānaraṃ caiva te rūpaṃ vivāhātprabhṛti prabho ||25||

26saṃdrakṣyanti narāścānye svarūpeṇa vināśanam
|
sa tadvākyaṃ tu vijñāya nāradaḥ parvataṃ tathā ||26||

27aśapattamapi krodhādbhāgineyaṃ sa mātulaḥ
|
tapasā brahmacaryeṇa satyena ca damena ca ||27||

28yukto'pi nityadharmaśca na vai svargamavāpsyasi
|
tau tu śaptvā bhṛśaṃ kruddhau parasparamamarṣaṇau ||28||

29pratijagmaturanyonyaṃ kruddhāviva gajottamau
|
parvataḥ pṛthivīṃ kṛtsnāṃ vicacāra mahāmatiḥ ||29||

30pūjyamāno yathānyāyaṃ tejasā svena bhārata
|
atha tāmalabhatkanyāṃ nāradaḥ sañjayātmajām ||30||

31dharmeṇa viprapravaraḥ sukumārīmaninditām
|
sā tu kanyā yathāśāpaṃ nāradaṃ taṃ dadarśa ha ||31||

32pāṇigrahaṇamantrāṇāṃ niyogādeva nāradam
|
sukumārī ca devarṣiṃ vānarapratimānanam ||32||

33naivāvamanyata tadā prītimatyeva cābhavat
|
upatasthe ca bhartāraṃ na cānyaṃ manasāpyagāt ||33||

34devaṃ muniṃ vā yakṣaṃ vā patitve pativatsalā
|
tataḥ kadācidbhagavānparvato'nucacāra ha ||34||

35vanaṃ virahitaṃ kiñcittatrāpaśyatsa nāradam
|
tato'bhivādya provāca nāradaṃ parvatastadā ||35||

36bhavānprasādaṃ kurutātsvargādeśāya me prabho
|
tamuvācā tato dṛṣṭvā parvataṃ nāradastathā ||36||

37kṛtāñjalimupāsīnaṃ dīnaṃ dīnataraḥ svayam
|
tvayā'haṃ prathamaṃ śapto vānarastvaṃ bhaviṣyasi ||37||

38ityuktena mayā paścācchaptastvamapi matsarāt
|
adyaprabhṛti vai vāsaṃ svarge nāvāpsyasīti ha ||38||

39tava naitaddhi sadṛśaṃ putrasthāne hi me bhavān
|
nivartayetāṃ tau śāpāvanyonyena tadā munī ||39||

40śrīsamṛddhaṃ tadā dṛṣṭvā nāradaṃ devarūpiṇam
|
sukumārī pradudrāva parapatyabhiśaṅkayā ||40||

41tāṃ parvatastato dṛṣṭvā pradravantīmaninditām
|
abravīttava bhartaiṣa nātra kāryā vicāraṇā ||41||

42ṛṣiḥ paramadharmātmā nārado bhagavānprabhuḥ
|
tavaivābhedyahṛdayo mā te'bhūdatra saṃśayaḥ ||42||

43sā'nunītā bahuvidhaṃ parvatena mahātmanā
|
śāpadoṣaṃ ca taṃ bhartuḥ śrutvā prakṛtimāgatā ||43||

44parvato'tha yayau svargaṃ nārado'bhyagamadgṛhān
|
vāsudeva uvāca |
pratyakṣakartā sarvasya nārado bhagavānṛṣiḥ
|
eṣa vakṣyati te pṛṣṭo yathāvṛttaṃ narottama ||44||
