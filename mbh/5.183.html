Mahābhārata 5.183 (Udyogaparvan)

1bhīṣma uvāca|
tato'haṃ niśi rājendra praṇamya śirasā tadā
|
brāhmaṇānāṃ pitṝṇāṃ ca devatānāṃ ca sarvaśaḥ ||1||

2naktaṃcarāṇāṃ bhūtānāṃ rājanyānāṃ viśāmpate
|
śayanaṃ prāpya rahite manasā samacintayam ||2||

3jāmadagnyena me yuddhamidaṃ paramadāruṇam
|
ahāni ca bahūnyadya vartate sumahātyayam ||3||

4na ca rāmaṃ mahāvīraryaṃ śaknomi raṇamūrdhani
|
vijetuṃ samare vipraṃ jāmadagnyaṃ mahābalam ||4||

5yadi śakyo mayā jetuṃ jāmadagnyaḥ pratāpavān
|
daivatāni prasannāni darśayantu niśāṃ mama ||5||

6tato niśi ca rājendra prasuptaḥ śaravikṣataḥ
|
dakṣiṇeneha pārśvena prabhātasamaye tadā ||6||

7tato'haṃ vipramukhyaistairyairasmi patito rathāt
|
utthāpito dhṛtaścaiva mā bhairiti ca sāntvitaḥ ||7||

8ta eva māṃ mahārāja svapnadarśanametya vai
|
parivāryābruvan vākyaṃ tannibodha kuruddhaha ||8||

9uttiṣṭha mā bhairgāṅgeya na bhayaṃ te'sti kiñcana
|
rakṣāmahe tvāṃ kauravya svaśarīraṃ hi no bhavān ||9||

10na tvāṃ rāmo raṇe jetā jāmadagnyaḥ kathañcana
|
tvameva samare rāmaṃ vijetā bharatarṣabha ||10||

11idamasraṃ sudayitaṃ pratyabhijñāsyate bhavān
|
viditaṃ hi tavāpyetat pūrvasmin dehadhāraṇe ||11||

12prājāpatyaṃ viśvakṛtaṃ prasvāpaṃ nāma bhārata
|
nahīdaṃ veda rāmo'pi pṛthivyāṃ vā pumān kvacit||12||

13tat smarasva mahābāho bhṛśaṃ saṃyojayasva ca
|
upasthāsyati rājendra svayameva tavānagha ||13||

14yena sarvān mahāvīryān praśāsiṣyasi kaurava
|
na ca rāmaḥ kṣayaṃ gantā tenāstreṇa narādhipa ||14||

15enasā na tu saṃyogaṃ prāpsyase jātu mānada
|
svapsyate jāmadagnyo'sau tvadbāṇabalapīḍitaḥ ||15||

16tato jitvā tvamevainaṃ punarutthāpayiṣyasi
|
astreṇa dayitenājau bhīṣma saṃbodhanena vai ||16||

17evaṃ kuruṣva kauravya prabhāte rathamāsthitaḥ
|
prasuptaṃ vā mṛtaṃ veti tulyaṃ manyāmahe vayam ||17||

18na ca rāmeṇa martavyaṃ kadācidapi pārthiva
|
tataḥ samutpannamidaṃ prasvāpaṃ yujyatāmiti ||18||

19ityuktvā'ntarhitā rājan sarva eva dvijottamāḥ
|
aṣṭau sadṛśarūpāste sarve bhāsuramūrtayaḥ ||19||
