Mahābhārata 14.77 (Āśvamedhikaparvan)

1vaiśampāyana uvāca|
saindhavairabhavadyuddhaṃ tatastasya kirīṭinaḥ
|
hataśeṣairmahārāja hatānāṃ ca sutairapi||1||

2te'vatīrṇamupaśrutya viṣayaṃ śvetavāhanam
|
pratyudyayuramṛṣyanto rājānaḥ pāṇḍavarṣabham||2||

3aśvaṃ ca taṃ parāmṛśya viṣayānte viṣopamāḥ
|
na bhayaṃ cakrire pārthādbhīmasenādanantarāt||3||

4te'vidūrāddhanuṣpāṇiṃ yajñiyasya hayasya ca
|
bībhatsuṃ pratyapadyanta padātinamavasthitam||4||

5tataste taṃ mahāvīryā rājānaḥ paryavārayan
|
jigīṣanto naravyāghraṃ pūrvaṃ vinikṛtā yudhi||5||

6te nāmānyapi gotrāṇi karmāṇi vividhāni ca
|
kīrtayantastadā pārthaṃ śaravarṣairavākiran||6||

7te kirantaḥ śaravrātānvāraṇaprativāraṇān
|
raṇe jayamabhīpsantaḥ kaunteyaṃ paryavārayan||7||

8te samīkṣya ca taṃ kṛṣṇamugrakarmāṇamāhave
|
sarve yuyudhire vīrā rathasthāstaṃ padātinam||8||

9te tamājaghnire vīraṃ nivātakavacāntakam
|
saṃśaptakanihantāraṃ hantāraṃ saindhavasya ca||9||

10tato rathasahasreṇa hayānāmayutena ca
|
koṣṭhakīkṛtya bībhatsuṃ prahṛṣṭamanaso'bhavan||10||

11taṃ smaranto vadhaṃ vīrāḥ sindhurājasya cāhave
|
jayadrathasya kauravya samare savyasācinā||11||

12tataḥ parjanyavatsarve śaravṛṣṭīravāsṛjan
|
taiḥ kīrṇaḥ śuśubhe pārtho ravirmeghāntare yathā||12||

13sa śaraiḥ samavacchannaścakāśe pāṇḍavarṣabhaḥ
|
pañjarāntarasañcārī śakunta iva bhārata||13||

14tato hāhākṛtaṃ sarvaṃ kaunteye śarapīḍite
|
trailokyamabhavadrājan ravirāsīcca niṣprabhaḥ||14||

15tato vavau mahārāja māruto lomaharṣaṇaḥ
|
rāhuragrasadādityaṃ yugapatsomameva ca||15||

16ulkāśca jaghnire sūryaṃ vikīryantyaḥ samantataḥ
|
vepathuścābhavadrājankailāsasya mahāgireḥ||16||

17mumucuḥ śvāsamatyuṣṇaṃ duḥkhaśokasamanvitāḥ
|
saptarṣayo jātabhayāstathā devarṣayo'pi ca||17||

18śaśaṃ cāśu vinirbhidya maṇḍalaṃ śaśino'patat
|
viparītā diśaścāpi sarvā dhūmākulāstathā||18||

19rāsabhāruṇasaṅkāśā dhanuṣmantaḥ savidyutaḥ
|
āvṛtya gaganaṃ meghā mumucurmāṃsaśoṇitam||19||

20evamāsīttadā vīre śaravarṣeṇa saṃvṛte
|
phālgune bharataśreṣṭha tadadbhutamivābhavat||20||

21tasya tenāvakīrṇasya śarajālena sarvataḥ
|
mohātpapāta gāṇḍīvamāvāpaśca karādapi||21||

22tasminmohamanuprāpte śarajālaṃ mahattadā
|
saindhavā mumucustūrṇaṃ gatasattve mahārathe||22||

23tato mohasamāpannaṃ jñātvā pārthaṃ divaukasaḥ
|
sarve vitrastamanasastasya śāntikṛto'bhavan||23||

24tato devarṣayaḥ sarve tathā saptarṣayo'pi ca
|
brahmarṣayaśca vijayaṃ jepuḥ pārthasya dhīmataḥ||24||

25tataḥ pradīpite devaiḥ pārthatejasi pārthiva
|
tasthāvacalavaddhīmān saṅgrāme paramāstravit||25||

26vicakarṣa dhanurdivyaṃ tataḥ kauravanandanaḥ
|
yantrasyeveha śabdo'bhūnmahāṃstasya punaḥ punaḥ||26||

27tataḥ sa śaravarṣāṇi pratyamitrānprati prabhuḥ
|
vavarṣa dhanuṣā pārtho varṣāṇīva purandaraḥ||27||

28tataste saindhavā yodhāḥ sarva eva sarājakāḥ
|
nādṛśyanta śaraiḥ kīrṇāḥ śalabhairiva pādapāḥ||28||

29tasya śabdena vitresurbhayārtāśca vidudruvuḥ
|
mumucuścāśru śokārtāḥ śuśucuścāpi saindhavāḥ||29||

30tāṃstu sarvānnaravyāghraḥ saindhavānvyacarat balī
|
alātacakravadrājaśarajālaiḥ samārpayat||30||

31tadindrajālapratimaṃ bāṇajālamamitrahā
|
visṛjya dikṣu sarvāsu mahendra iva vajrabhṛt||31||

32meghajālanibhaṃ sainyaṃ vidārya śaravṛṣṭibhiḥ
|
vibabhau kauravaśreṣṭhaḥ śaradīva divākaraḥ||32||
