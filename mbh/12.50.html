Mahābhārata 12.50 (Śāntiparvan)

1vaiśampāyana uvāca|
tato rāmasya tatkarma śrutvā rājā yudhiṣṭhiraḥ
|
vismayaṃ paramaṃ gatvā pratyuvāca janārdanam ||1||

2aho rāmasya vārṣṇeya śakrasyeva mahātmanaḥ
|
vikramo vasudhā yena krodhānniḥkṣatriyā kṛtā ||2||

3gobhiḥ samudreṇa tathā golāṃgūlarkṣavānaraiḥ
|
guptā rāmabhayodvignāḥ kṣatriyāṇāṃ kulodvahāḥ ||3||

4aho dhanyo nṛloko'yaṃ sabhāgyāśca narā bhuvi
|
yatra karmedṛśaṃ dharmyaṃ dvijena kṛtamityuta ||4||

5tathāvṛttau kathāṃ tāta tāvacyutayudhiṣṭhirau
|
jagmaturyatra gāṅgeyaḥ śaratalpagataḥ prabhuḥ ||5||

6tataste dadṛśurbhīṣmaṃ śaraprastaraśāyinam
|
svaraśmijālasaṃvītaṃ sāyaṃsūryasamaprabham ||6||

7upāsyamānaṃ munibhirdevairiva śatakratum
|
deśe paramadharmiṣṭhe nadīmoghavatīmanu ||7||

8dūrādeva tamālokya kṛṣṇo rājā ca dharmajaḥ
|
catvāraḥ pāṇḍavāścaiva te ca śāradvatādayaḥ ||8||

9avaskandyātha vāhebhyaḥ saṃyamya pracalaṃ manaḥ
|
ekīkṛtyendriyagrāmamupatasthurmahāmunīnan ||9||

10abhivādya tu govindaḥ sātyakiste ca pārthivāḥ
|
vyāsādīnṛṣimukhyāṃśca gāṅgeyamupatasthire ||10||

11tato vṛddhaṃ tathā dṛṣṭvā gāṅgeyaṃ yadukauravāḥ
|
parivārya tataḥ sarve niṣeduḥ puruṣarṣabhāḥ ||11||

12tato niśāmya gāṅgeyaṃ śāmyamānamivānalam
|
kiñciddīnamanā bhīṣmamiti hovāca keśavaḥ ||12||

13kaccijjñānāni sarvāṇi prasannāni yathā purā
|
kaccinna vyākulā caiva buddhiste vadatāṃ vara ||13||

14śarābhighātaduḥkhātte kaccidgātraṃ na dūyate
|
mānasādapi duḥkhāddhi śārīraṃ balavattaram ||14||

15varadānātpituḥ kāmaṃ chandamṛtyurasi prabho
|
śāntanordharmanityasya na tvetanmama kāraṇam ||15||

16susūkṣmo'pi tu dehe vai śalyo janayate rujam
|
kiṃ punaḥ śarasaṅghātaiścittasya tava pārthiva ||16||

17kāmaṃ naitattavākhyeyaṃ prāṇināṃ prabhavāpyayau
|
upadeṣṭuṃ bhavān śakto devānāmapi bhārata ||17||

18yacca bhūtaṃ bhaviṣyaṃ ca bhavacca puruṣarṣabha
|
sarvaṃ tajjñānavṛddhasya tava bhīṣma pratiṣṭhitam ||18||

19saṃhāraścaiva bhūtānāṃ dharmasya ca phalodayaḥ
|
viditaste mahāprājña tvaṃ hi dharmamayo nidhiḥ ||19||

20tvāṃ hi rājye sthitaṃ sphīte samagrāṅgamarogiṇam
|
strīsahasraiḥ parivṛtaṃ paśyāmīvordhvaretasam ||20||

21ṛte śāntanavādbhīṣmāttriṣu lokeṣu pārthiva
|
satyadharmānmahāvīryācchūrāddharmaikatatparāt ||21||

22mṛtyumāvārya tapasā śarasaṃstaraśāyinaḥ
|
nisargaprabhavaṃ kiñcinna ca tātānuśuśruma ||22||

23satye, tapasi, dāne ca, yajñādhikaraṇe tathā
|
dhanurvede ca vede ca nītyāṃ caivānurakṣaṇe ||23||

24anṛśaṃsaṃ śuciṃ dāntaṃ sarvabhūtahite ratam
|
mahārathaṃ tvatsadṛśaṃ na kañcidanuśuśruma ||24||

25tvaṃ hi devānsagandharvānasurān yakṣarākṣasān
|
śaktastvekarathenaiva vijetuṃ nātra saṃśayaḥ ||25||

26sa tvaṃ bhīṣma mahābāho vasūnāṃ vāsavopamaḥ
|
nityaṃ vipraiḥ samākhyāto navamo'navamo guṇaiḥ ||26||

27ahaṃ ca tvā'bhijānāmi yastvaṃ puruṣasattama
|
tridaśeṣvapi vikhyātastvaṃ śaktyā puruṣottamaḥ ||27||

28manuṣyeṣu manuṣyendra na dṛṣṭo na ca me śrutaḥ
|
bhavato vā guṇairyuktaḥ pṛthivyāṃ puruṣaḥ kvacit ||28||

29tvaṃ hi sarvaguṇai rājandevānapyatiricyase
|
tapasā hi bhavāñśaktaḥ sraṣṭuṃ lokāṃścarācarān ||29||

30kiṃ punaścātmano lokānuttamānuttamairguṇaiḥ
|
tadasya tapyamānasya jñātīnāṃ saṃkṣayena vai ||30||

31jyeṣṭhasya pāṇḍuputrasya śokaṃ bhīṣma vyapānuda
|
ye hi dharmāḥ samākhyātāścāturvarṇyasya bhārata ||31||

32cāturāśrabhyasaṃyuktāḥ sarve te viditāstava
|
cāturvidye ca ye proktāścāturhotre ca bhārata ||32||

33yoge sāṃkhye ca niyatā ye ca dharmāḥ sanātanāḥ
|
cāturvarṇyasya yaścokto dharmo na sma virudhyate ||33||

34sevyamānaḥ savaiyākhyo gāṅgeya viditastava
|
pratilomaprasūtānāṃ varṇānāṃ caiva yaḥ smṛtaḥ ||34||

35deśajātikulānāṃ na jānīṣe dharmalakṣaṇam
|
vedokto yaśca śiṣṭoktaḥ sadaiva viditastava ||35||

36itihāsapurāṇārthāḥ kārtsnyena viditāstava
|
dharmaśāstraṃ ca sakalaṃ nityaṃ manasi te sthitam ||36||

37ye ca kecana loke'sminnarthāḥ saṃśayakārakāḥ
|
teṣāṃ chettā nāsti loke tvadanyaḥ puruṣarṣabha ||37||

38sa pāṇḍaveyasya manaḥ samutthitaṃ narendra śokaṃ vyapakarṣa medhayā
|
bhavadvidhā hyuttamabuddhivistarā vimuhyamānasya narasya śāntaye ||38||
