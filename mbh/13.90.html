Mahābhārata 13.90 (Anuśāsanaparvan)

1yudhiṣṭhira uvāca|
kīdṛśebhyaḥ pradātavyaṃ bhavet śrāddhaṃ pitāmaha
|
dvijebhyaḥ kuruśārdūla tanme vyākhyātumarhasi ||1||

2bhīṣma uvāca|
brāhmaṇānna parīkṣeta kṣatriyo dānadharmavit
|
daive karmaṇi pitrye tu nyāyamāhuḥ parīkṣaṇam ||2||

3devatāḥ pūjayantīha daivenaiveha tejasā
|
upetya tasmāddevebhyaḥ sarvebhyo dāpayennaraḥ ||3||

4śrāddhe tvatha mahārāja parīkṣedbrāhmaṇānbudhaḥ
|
kulaśīlavayorūpairvidyayā'bhijanena ca ||4||

5teṣāmanye paṅktidūṣāstathā'nye paṅktipāvanāḥ
|
apāṅkteyāstu ye rājankīrtayiṣyāmi tān śṛṇu ||5||

6kitavo bhrūṇahā yakṣmī paśupālo nirākṛtiḥ
|
grāmapreṣyo vārdhuṣiko gāyanaḥ sarvavikrayī ||6||

7agāradāhī garadaḥ kuṇḍāśī somavikrayī
|
sāmudriko rājabhṛtyastailikaḥ kūṭakārakaḥ ||7||

8pitrā vivadamānaśca yasya copapatirgṛhe
|
abhiśastastathā stenaḥ śilpaṃ yaścopajīvati ||8||

9parvakāraśca sūcī ca mitradhruk pāradārikaḥ
|
avratānāmupādhyāyaḥ kāṇḍapṛṣṭhastathaiva ca ||9||

10śvabhiśca yaḥ parikrāmedyaḥ śunā daṣṭa eva ca
|
parivittiśca yaśca syādduścarmā gurutalpagaḥ ||10||

11kuśīlavo devalako nakṣatrairyaśca jīvati
|
īdṛśairbrāhmaṇairyuktamapāṅkteyairyudhiṣṭhira ||11||

12rakṣāṃsi gacchate havyamityāhurbrahmavādinaḥ
|
śrāddhaṃ bhuktvā tvadhīyīta vṛṣalītalpagaśca yaḥ ||12||

13purīṣe tasya te māsaṃ pitarastasya śerate
|
somavikrayiṇe viṣṭhā bhiṣaje pūyaśoṇitam ||13||

14naṣṭaṃ devalake dattamapratiṣṭhaṃ ca vārdhuṣe
|
yattu vāṇijake dattaṃ neha nāmutra tadbhavet ||14||

15bhasmanīva hutaṃ havyaṃ tathā paunarbhave dvije
|
ye tu dharmavyapeteṣu cāritrāpagateṣu ca
|
havyaṃ kavyaṃ prayacchanti teṣāṃ tatpretya naśyati ||15||

16jñānapūrvaṃ tu ye tebhyaḥ prayacchantyalpabuddhayaḥ
|
purīṣaṃ bhuñjate tasya pitaraḥ pretya niścayaḥ||16||

17etānimānvijānīyādapāṅkteyāndvijādhamān
|
śūdrāṇāmupadeśaṃ ca ye kurvantyalpacetasaḥ ||17||

18ṣaṣṭiṃ kāṇaḥ śataṃ ṣaṇḍhaḥ śvitrī yāvatprapaśyati
|
paṅktyā samupaviṣṭāyāṃ tāvaddūṣayate nṛpa ||18||

19yadveṣṭitaśirā bhuṅkte yadbhuṅkte dakṣiṇāmukhaḥ
|
sopānatkaśca yadbhuṅkte sarvaṃ vidyāttadāsuram ||19||

20asūyatā ca yaddattaṃ yacca śraddhāvivarjitam
|
sarvaṃ tadasurendrāya brahmā bhāgamakalpayat ||20||

21śvānaśca paṅktidūṣāśca nāvekṣerankathañcana
|
tasmātparisṛte dadyāttilāṃścānvavakīrayet||21||

22tilairvirahitaṃ śrāddhaṃ kṛtaṃ krodhavaśena ca
|
yātudhānāḥ piśācāśca vipraluṃpanti taddhaviḥ ||22||

23apāṅkto yāvataḥ pāṅktānbhuñjānānanupaśyati
|
tāvatphalādbhraṃśayati dātāraṃ tasya bāliśam ||23||

24ime tu bharataśreṣṭha vijñeyāḥ paṅktipāvanāḥ
|
ye tvatastānpravakṣyāmi parīkṣasveha tāndvijān ||24||

25vidyāvedavratastrātā brāhmaṇāḥ sarva eva hi
|
sadācāraparāścaiva vijñeyāḥ sarvapāvanāḥ ||25||

26pāṅkteyāṃstu pravakṣyāmi jñeyāste paṅktipāvanāḥ
|
triṇāciketaḥ pañcāgnistrisuparṇaḥ ṣaḍaṅgavit ||26||

27brahmadeyānusantānaśchaṃdogo jyeṣṭhasāmagaḥ
|
mātāpitroryaśca vaśyaḥ śrotriyo daśapūruṣaḥ ||27||

28ṛtukālābhigāmī ca dharmapatnīṣu yaḥ sadā
|
vedavidyāghratasnāto vipraḥ paṅktiṃ punātyuta ||28||

29atharvaśiraso'dhyetā brahmacārī yatavrataḥ
|
satyavādī dharmaśīlaḥ svakarmanirataśca saḥ ||29||

30ye ca puṇyeṣu tīrtheṣu abhiṣekakṛtaśramāḥ
|
makheṣu ca samantreṣu bhavantyavabhṛthaplutāḥ ||30||

31akrodhanā hyacapalāḥ kṣāntā dāntā jitendriyāḥ
|
sarvabhūtahitā ye ca śrāddheṣvetānnimantrayet ||31||

32eteṣu dattamakṣayyamete vai paṅktipāvanāḥ
|
ime pare mahābhāgā vijñeyāḥ paṅktipāvanāḥ ||32||

33yatayo mokṣadharmajñā yogāḥ sucaritavratāḥ
|
ye cetihāsaṃ prayatāḥ śrāvayanti dvijottamān ||33||

34ye ca bhāṣyavidaḥ kecidye ca vyākaraṇe ratāḥ
|
ardhāyate purāṇaṃ ye dharmaśāstrāṇyathāpi ca ||34||

35adhītya ca yathānyāyaṃ vidhivattasya kāriṇaḥ
|
upapanno gurukule satyavādī sahasraśaḥ ||35||

36agryāḥ sarveṣu vedeṣu sarvapravacaneṣu ca
|
yāvadete prapaśyanti paṅktyāstāvatpunantyuta ||36||

37tato hi pāvanātpaṅktyāḥ paṅktipāvana ucyate
|
krośādardhatṛtīyācca pāvayedeka eva hi ||37||

38brahmadeyānusantāna iti brahmavido viduḥ
|
anṛtviganupādhyāyaḥ sa cedagrāsanaṃ vrajet ||38||

39ṛtvigbhirabhyanujñātaḥ paṅktyā harati duṣkṛtaṃ
|
atha cedvedavitsarvaiḥ paṅktidoṣairvivarjitaḥ ||39||

40na ca syātpatito rājanpaṅktipāvana eva saḥ
|
tasmātsarvaprayatnena parīkṣyāmantrayeddvijān ||40||

41svakarmaniratānanyānkule jātānbahuśrutān
|
yasya mitrapradhānāni śrāddhāni ca havīṃṣi ca
|
na praṇinti pitṝndevānsvargaṃ ca na sa gacchati ||41||

42yaśca śrāddhe kurute saṅgatāni na devayānena pathā sa yāti
|
sa vai muktaḥ pippalaṃ bandhanādvā svargāllokāccyavate śrāddhamitraḥ ||42||

43tasmānmitraṃ śrāddhakṛnnādriyeta dadyānmitrebhyaḥ saṅgrahārthaṃ dhanāni
|
yanmanyate naiva śatruṃ na mitraṃ taṃ madhyasthaṃ bhojayeddhavyakavye ||43||

44yathoṣare bījamuptaṃ na rohenna cāvaptā prāpnuyādbījabhāgam
|
evaṃ śrāddhaṃ bhuktamanarhamāṇairna ceha nāmutra phalaṃ dadāti ||44||

45brāhmaṇo hyanadhīyānastṛṇāgniriva śāmyati
|
tasmai śrāddhaṃ na dātavyaṃ na hi bhasmani hūyate ||45||

46sambhojanī nāma piśācadakṣiṇā sā naiva devānna pitṝnupaiti
|
ihaiva lā bhrāmyati hanipuṇyā śālāntare gauriva naṣṭavatsā ||46||

47yathā'gnau śānte ghṛtamājuhoti tannaiva devānna pitṝnupaiti
|
tathā dattaṃ nartane gāyane ca yāṃ cānṛte dakṣiṇāmāvṛṇoti ||47||

48ubhau hinasti na bhunakti caiṣā yā cānṛte dakṣiṇā dīyate vai
|
āghātinī gārhitaiṣā patantī teṣāṃ pretānpātayeddevayānāt ||48||

49ṛrṣāṇāṃ samaye nityaṃ ye caranti yudhiṣṭhira
|
niścitāḥ sarvadharmajñāstāndevā brāhmaṇānviduḥ ||49||

50svādhyāyaniṣṭhā ṛṣayo jñānaniṣṭhāstathaiva ca
|
taponiṣṭhāśca boddhavyāḥ karmaniṣṭhāśca bhārata ||50||

51kavyāni jñānaniṣṭhebhyaḥ pratiṣṭhāpyāni bhārata
|
tatra ye brāhmaṇānkecinna nindanti hi te narāḥ ||51||

52ye tu nindanti jalpeṣu na tāñchrāddheṣu bhojayet
|
brāhmaṇā ninditā rājanhanyustraipuruṣaṃ kulam ||52||

53vaikhānasānāṃ vacanamṛṣīṇāṃ śrūyate nṛpa
|
dūrādeva parīkṣeta brāhmaṇānvedapāragān ||53||

54priyo vā yadi vā dveṣyasteṣāṃ tu śrāddhamāvapet
|
yaḥ sahasraṃ sahasrāṇāṃ bhojayedanṛtānnaraḥ
|
ekastānmantravitprītaḥ sarvānarhati bhārata ||54||
