Mahābhārata 6.107 (Bhīṣmaparvan)

1sañjaya uvāca|
yudhyatāmeva teṣāṃ tu bhāskare'stamupāgate
|
sandhyā samabhavaddhorā nāpaśyāma tato raṇam ||1||

2tato yudhiṣṭhiro rājā sandhyāṃ saṃdṛśya bhārata
|
vadhyamānaṃ ca bhīṣmeṇa tyaktāstraṃ bhayavihvalam ||2||

3svasainyaṃ ca parāvṛttaṃ palāyanaparāyaṇam
|
bhīṣmaṃ ca yudhi saṃrabdhaṃ pīḍayantaṃ mahāratham ||3||

4somakāṃśca jitān dṛṣṭvā nirutsāhān mahārathān
|
cintayitvā tato rājā avahāramarocayat ||4||

5tato'vahāraṃ sainyānāṃ cakre rājā yudhiṣṭhiraḥ
|
tathaiva tava sainyānāmavahāro hyabhūt tadā ||5||

6tato'vahāraṃ sainyānāṃ kṛtvā tatra mahārathāḥ
|
nyaviśanta kuruśreṣṭha saṅgrāme kṣatavikṣatāḥ ||6||

7bhīṣmasya samare karma cintayānāstu pāṇḍavāḥ
|
nālabhanta tadā śāntiṃ bhīṣmabāṇaprapīḍitāḥ ||7||

8bhīṣmo'pi samare jitvā pāṇḍavān sahṛsṛñjayān
|
pūjyamānastava sutaivendyamānaśca bhārata ||8||

9nyaviśat kurubhiḥ sārdhaṃ hṛṣṭarūpaiḥ samantataḥ
|
tato rātriḥ samabhavat sarvabhūtapramohinī ||9||

10tasmin rātrimukhe ghore pāṇḍavā vṛṣṇibhiḥ saha
|
sṛñjayāśca durādharṣā mantrāya samupāviśan ||10||

11ātmaniḥśreyasaṃ sarve prāptakālaṃ mahābalāḥ
|
mantrayāmāsuravyagrā mantraniścayakovidāḥ ||11||

12tato yudhiṣṭhiro rājā mantrayitvā ciraṃ nṛpa
|
vāsudevaṃ samudvīkṣya vacanaṃ cedamādade ||12||

13kṛṣṇa paśya mahātmānaṃ bhīṣmaṃ bhīmaparākramam
|
gajaṃ nalavanānīva vimṛdnantaṃ balaṃ mama ||13||

14na caivainaṃ mahātmānamutsahāmo nirīkṣitum
|
lelihyamānaṃ sainyeṣu pravṛddhamiva pāvakam ||14||

15yathā ghoro mahānāgastakṣako vai viṣolbaṇaḥ
|
tathā bhīṣmo raṇe kruddhastīkṣṇaśastraḥ pratāpavān ||15||

16gṛhītacāpaḥ samare pramuñcanniśitāñcharān
|
śakyo jetuṃ yamaḥ kruddho vajrapāṇiśca devarāṭ ||16||

17varuṇaḥ pāśabhṛccāpi saṅgado vā dhaneśvaraḥ
|
na tu bhīṣmaḥ susaṃkruddhaḥ śakyo jetuṃ mahāhave ||17||

18so'hamevaṃgate kṛṣṇa nimagnaḥ śokasāgare
|
ātmano buddhidaurbalyādbhīṣmamāsādya saṃyuge ||18||

19vanaṃ yāsyāmi durdharṣa śreyo vai tatra me gatam
|
na yuddhaṃ rocate kṛṣṇa hanti bhīṣmo hi naḥ sadā ||19||

20yathā prajvalitaṃ vahniṃ pataṅgaḥ samabhidravan
|
ekato mṛtyumabhyeti tathā'haṃ bhīṣmamīyivān ||20||

21kṣayaṃ nīto'smi vārṣṇeya rājyahetoḥ parākramī
|
bhrātaraścaiva me śūrāḥ sāyakairbhṛśapīḍitāḥ ||21||

22matkṛte bhrātṛsauhārdādrājyabhraṣṭā vanaṃ gatāḥ
|
parikliṣṭā tathā kṛṣṇā matkṛte madhusudana ||22||

23jīvitaṃ bahu manye'haṃ jīvitaṃ hyadya durlabham
|
jīvitasyādya śeṣeṇa cariṣye dharmamuttamam ||23||

24yadi te'hamanugrāhyo bhrātṛbhiḥ saha keśava
|
svadharmasyāvirodhena hitaṃ vyāhara keśava ||24||

25evaṃ śrutvā vacastasya kāruṇyādbahuvistaram
|
pratyuvāca tataḥ kṛṣṇaḥ sāntvayāno yudhiṣṭhiram ||25||

26dharmaputra viṣādaṃ tvaṃ mā kṛthāḥ satyasaṅgara
|
yasya te bhrātaraḥ śūrā durjayāḥ śatrusūdanāḥ ||26||

27arjuno bhīmasenaśca vāyvagnisamatejasau
|
mādrīputrau ca vikrāntau tridaśānāmiveśvarau ||27||

28māṃ vā niyuṃkṣva sauhārdādyotsye bhīṣmeṇa pāṇḍava
|
tvatprayukto mahārāja kiṃ na kuryāṃ mahāhave ||28||

29haniṣyāmi raṇe bhīṣmamāhūya puruṣarṣabham
|
paśyatāṃ dhārtarāṣṭrāṇāṃ yadi necchati phālgunaḥ ||29||

30yadi bhīṣme hate vīre jayaṃ paśyasi pāṇḍava
|
hantā'smyekarathenādya kuruvṛddhaṃ pitāmaham ||30||

31paśya me vikramaṃ rājan mahendrasyeva saṃyuge
|
vimuñcantaṃ mahāstrāṇi pātayiṣyāmi taṃ rathāt ||31||

32yaḥ śatruḥ pāṇḍuputrāṇāṃ macchatruḥ sa na saṃśayaḥ
|
madarthā bhavadīyā ye ye madīyāstavaiva te ||32||

33tava bhrātā mama sakhā sambandhī śiṣya eva ca
|
māṃsānyutkṛtya dāsyāmi phālgunārthe mahīpate ||33||

34eṣa cāpi naravyāghro matkṛte jīvitaṃ tyajet
|
eṣa naḥ samayastāta tārayema parasparam ||34||

35sa māṃ niyuṃkṣva rājendra yathā yoddhā bhavāmyahaṃ
|
pratijñātamupaplavye yattatpārthena pūrvataḥ ||35||

36ghātayiṣyāmi gāṅgeyamiti lokasya saṃnidhau
|
parirakṣyamidaṃ tāvadvacaḥ pārthasya dhīmataḥ ||36||

37anujñātaṃ tu pārthena mayā kāryaṃ na saṃśayaḥ
|
athavā phālgusyaiṣa bhāraḥ parimito raṇe ||37||

38sa haniṣyati saṅgrāme bhīṣmaṃ parapurañjayam
|
aśakyamapi kuryāddhi raṇe pārthaḥ samudyataḥ ||38||

39tridaśān vā samuyuktān sahitāndaityadānavaiḥ
|
nihanyādarjunaḥ saṃkhye kimu bhīṣmaṃ narādhipa ||39||

40viparīto mahāvīryo gatasattvo'lpajīvanaḥ
|
bhīṣmaḥ śāntanavo nūnaṃ kartavyaṃ nāvabudhyate ||40||

41yudhiṣṭhira uvāca|evametanmahābāho yathā vadasi mādhava
|
sarve hyete na paryāptāstava vegavidhāraṇe ||41||

42niyataṃ samavāpsyāmi sarvametadyathepsitam
|
yasya me puruṣavyāghra bhavān pakṣe vyavasthitaḥ ||42||

43sendrānapi raṇe devān jayeyaṃ jayatāṃ vara
|
tvayā nāthena govinda kimu bhīṣmaṃ mahāratham ||43||

44na tu tvāmanṛtaṃ kartumutsahe svātmagauravāt
|
ayudhyamānaḥ sāhāyyaṃ yathoktaṃ kuru mādhava ||44||

45samayastu kṛtaḥ kaścinmama bhīṣmeṇa saṃyuge
|
mantrayiṣye tavārthāya na tu yotsye kathañcana ||45||

46duryodhanārthaṃ yotsyāmi satyametaditi prabho
|
sa hi rājyasya me dātā mantrasyaiva ca mādhava ||46||

47tasmāddevavrataṃ bhūyo vadhopāyārthamātmanaḥ
|
bhavatā sahitāḥ sarve prayāma madhusūdana ||47||

48tadvayaṃ sahitā gatvā bhīṣmamāśu narottamam
|
na cirāt sarve vārṣṇeya mantraṃ pṛcchāma kauravam ||48||

49sa vakṣyati hitaṃ vākyaṃ satyamasmān janārdana
|
yathā ca vakṣyate kṛṣṇa tathā kartā'smi saṃyuge ||49||

50sa no jayasya dātā syānmantrasya ca dṛḍhavrataḥ
|
bālāḥ pitrā vihīnāśca tena saṃvardhitā vayam ||50||

51taṃ cetpitāmahaṃ vṛddhaṃ hantumicchāmi mādhava
|
pituḥ pitaramiṣṭaṃ ca dhigastu kṣatrajīvikām ||51||

52sañjaya uvāca|tato'bravīnmahārāja vārṣṇeyaḥ kurunandanam
|
rocate me mahāprājña rājendra tava bhāṣitam ||52||

53devavrataḥ kṛtī bhīṣmaḥ prekṣitenāpi nirdahet
|
gamyatāṃ sa vadhopāyaṃ praṣṭuṃ sāgaragāsutaḥ ||53||

54vaktumarhati satyaṃ sa tvayā pṛṣṭo viśaṣataḥ
|
te vayaṃ tatra gacchāmaḥ praṣṭuṃ kurupitāmaham ||54||

55gatvā śāntanavaṃ vṛddhaṃ mantraṃ pṛcchāma bhārata
|
sa vo dāsyati mantraṃ yaṃ tena yotsyāmahe parān ||55||

56evamāmantrya te vīrāḥ pāṇḍavāḥ pāṇḍupūrvajam
|
jagmuste sahitāḥ sarve vāsudevaśca vīryavān ||56||

57vimuktaśastrakavacā bhīṣmasya sadanaṃ prati
|
praviśya ca tadā bhīṣmaṃ śirobhiḥ praṇipedire||57||

58pūjayanto mahārāja pāṇḍavā bharatarṣabham
|
praṇamya śirasā cainaṃ bhīṣmaṃ śaraṇamabhyayuḥ ||58||

59tānuvāca mahābāhurbhīṣmaḥ kurupitāmahaḥ
|
svāgataṃ tava vārṣṇeya svāgataṃ te dhanañjaya ||59||

60svāgataṃ dharmaputrāya bhīmāya yamayostathā
|
kiṃ vā kāryaṃ karomyadya yuṣmākaṃ prītivardhanam ||60||

61sarvātmanā'pi kartā'smi yadapi syāt suduṣkaram
|
tathā bruvāṇaṃ gāṅgeyaṃ prītiyuktaṃ punaḥ punaḥ ||61||

62uvāca rājā dīnātmā prītiyuktamidaṃ vacaḥ
|
kathaṃ jayema sarvajña kathaṃ rājyaṃ labhemahi ||62||

63prajānāṃ saṃśayo na syāt kathaṃ tanme vada prabho
|
bhavān hi no vadhopāyaṃ bravītu svayamātmanaḥ ||63||

64bhavantaṃ samare vīra viṣahema kathaṃ vayam
|
na hi te sūkṣmamapyasti randhraṃ kurupitāmaha ||64||

65maṇḍalenaiva dhanuṣā dṛśyase saṃyuge sadā
|
ādadānaṃ sandadhānaṃ vikarṣantaṃ dhanurna ca ||65||

66paśyāmastvāṃ mahābāho rathe sūryamivāparam
|
rathāśvanaranāgānāṃ hantāraṃ paravīrahan ||66||

67ko'tha votsahate jetuṃ tvāṃ pumān bharatarṣabha
|
varṣatā śaravarṣāṇi saṃyuge vaiśasaṃ kṛtam ||67||

68kṣayaṃ nītā hi pṛtanā saṃyuge mahatī mama
|
yayā yudhi jayema tvāṃ yathā rājyaṃ bhṛśaṃ mama ||68||

69mama sainyasya ca kṣemaṃ tanme brūhi pitāmaha
|
tato'bravīcchāntanavaḥpāṇḍavān pāṇḍupūrvajaḥ ||39||

70na kathañcana kaunteya mayi jīvati saṃyuge
|
jayo bhavati sarvajña satyametadbravīmi te ||70||

71nirjite mayi yuddhena raṇe jeṣyatha pāṇḍavāḥ
|
kṣipraṃ mayi praharadhvaṃ yadīcchatha raṇe jayam ||71||

72anujānāmi vaḥ pārthāḥ praharadhvaṃ yathāsukham
|
evaṃ hi sukṛtaṃ manye bhavatāṃ vidito hyaham ||72||

73hate mayi hataṃ sarvaṃ tasmādeva vidhīyatām
|
yudhiṣṭhira uvāca|brūhi tasmādupāyaṃ no yathā yuddhe jayemahi ||73||

74bhavantaṃ samare kruddhaṃ daṇḍahastamivāntakam
|
śakyo vajradharī jatuṃ varuṇo'tha yamastathā ||74||

75na bhavān samare śakyaḥ sendrairapi surāsuraiḥ
|
bhīṣma uvāca|satyametanmahābāho yathā vadasi pāṇḍava ||75||

76nāhaṃ jetuṃ raṇe śakyaḥ sendrairapi surāsuraiḥ
|
āttaśastro raṇe yatto gṛhītavarakārmukaḥ ||76||

77tato māṃ nyastaśastraṃ tu ete hanyurmahārathāḥ
|
nikṣiptaśastre patite vimuktakavacadhvaje ||77||

78dravamāṇe ca bhīte ca tavāsmīti ca vādini
|
striyāṃ strīnāmadheye ca vikale caikaputriṇi ||78||

79apraśaste nare caiva na yuddhaṃ rocate mama
|
imaṃ me śṛṇu rājendra saṅkalpaṃ pūrvacintitam ||79||

80amaṅgalyadhvajaṃ dṛṣṭvā na yudhyeyaṃ kadācana
|
ya eṣa draupado rājaṃstava sainye mahārathaḥ ||80||

81śikhaṇḍī samarāmarṣī śūraśca samitiñjayaḥ
|
yathā'bhavacca strī pūrvaṃ paścātpuṃstvaṃ samāṃgataḥ ||81||

82jānanti ca bhavanto'pi sarvametadyathātatham
|
arjunaḥ samare śūraḥ puraskṛtya śikhaṇḍinam ||82||

83māmeva viśikhaistīkṣṇairabhidravatu daṃśitaḥ
|
amaṅgalyadhvaje tasmin strīpūrve ca viśeṣataḥ ||83||

84na prahartumabhīpsāmi gṛhīteṣuḥ kathañcana
|
tadantaraṃ samāsādya pāṇḍavo māṃ dhanañjayaḥ ||84||

85śarairghātayatu kṣipraṃ samantādbharatarṣabha
|
na taṃ paśyāmi lokeṣu māṃ hanyādyaḥ samudyatam ||85||

86ṛte kṛṣṇānmahābhāgātpāṇḍavādvā dhanañjayāt
|
eṣa tasmāt purodhāya kañcidanyaṃ mamāgrataḥ ||86||

87āttaśastro raṇe yatto gṛhītavarakārmukaḥ
|
māṃ pātayatu bībhatsurevaṃ tava jayo dhruvam ||87||

88etatkuruṣva kaunteya yathoktaṃ mama suvrata
|
saṅgrāme dhārtarāṣṭrāṃśca hanyāḥ sarvānsamāgatān ||88||

89sañjaya uvāca|te tu jñātvā tataḥ pārthā jagmuḥ svaśibiraṃ prati
|
abhivādya mahātmānaṃ bhīṣmaṃ kurupitāmaham ||89||

90tathoktavati gāṅgeye paralokāya dīkṣite
|
arjuno duḥkhasantaptaḥ savrīḍamidamabravīt ||90||

91guruṇā kuruvṛddhena kṛtaprajñenana dhīmatā
|
pitāmahena saṅgrāme kathaṃ yoddhā'smi mādhava ||91||

92krīḍatā hi mayā bālye vāsudeva mahāmanāḥ
|
pāṃsurūṣitagātreṇa mahātmā paruṣīkṛtaḥ ||92||

93yasyāhamadhiruhyāṅkaṃ bālaḥ kila gadāgraja
|
tātetyavocaṃ pitaraṃ pituḥ pāṇḍormahātmanaḥ ||93||

94nāhaṃ tātastava pitustāto'smi tava bhārata
|
iti māmabravīdbālye yaḥ sa vadhyaḥ kathaṃ mayā ||94||

95kāmaṃ vadhyatu sainyaṃ me nāhaṃ yotsye mahātmanā
|
jayo vā'stu vadho vā me kathaṃ vā kṛṣṇa manyase ||95||

96vāsudeva uvāca|pratijñāya vadhaṃ jiṣṇo purā bhīṣmasya saṃyuge
|
kṣatradharme sthitaḥ pārtha kathaṃ nainaṃ haniṣyasi ||96||

97pātayainaṃ rathāt pārtha kṣatriyaṃ yuddhadurmadam
|
nāhatvā yudhi gāṅgeyaṃ vijayaste bhaviṣyati ||97||

98dṛṣṭametat purā devairgamiṣyati yamakṣayam
|
yaddṛṣṭaṃ hi purā pārtha tattathā na tadanyathā ||98||

99na hi bhīṣmaṃ durādharṣaṃ vyāttānanamivāntakam
|
tvadanyaḥ śaknuyādyoddhumapi vajradharaḥ svayam ||99||

100jahi bhīṣmaṃ sthiro bhūtvā śṛṇu cedaṃ vaco mama
|
yathovāca purā śakraṃ mahābuddhirbṛhaspatiḥ ||100||

101jyāyāṃsamapi cedvṛddhaṃ guṇairapi samanvitam
|
ātatāyinamāyāntaṃ hanyādghātakamātmanaḥ ||101||

102śāśvato'yaṃ sthito dharmaḥ kṣatriyāṇāṃ dhanañjaya
|
yoddhavyaṃ rakṣitavyaṃ ca yaṣṭavyaṃ cānasūyubhiḥ ||102||

103arjuna uvāca|śikhaṇḍī nidhanaṃ kṛṣṇa bhīṣmasya bhavitā dhruvam
|
dṛṣṭvaiva hi sadā bhīṣmaḥ pāñcālyaṃ vinivartate ||103||

104te vayaṃ pramukhe tasya puraskṛtya śikhaṇḍinam
|
gāṅgeyaṃ pātayiṣyāma upāyeneti me matiḥ ||104||

105ahamanyān maheṣvāsān vārayiṣyāmi sāyakaiḥ
|
śikhaṇḍyapi yudhāṃ śreṣṭhaṃ bhīṣmamevābhiyodhayet ||105||

106śrutaṃ hi kurumukhyasya nāhaṃ hanyāṃ śikhaṇḍinam
|
kanyā hyeṣā purā bhūtvā puruṣaḥ samapadyata ||106||

107ityevaṃ niścayaṃ kṛtvā pāṇḍavāḥ sahamādhavāḥ
|
anumānya mahātmānaṃ prayayurhṛṣṭamānasāḥ
|
śayanāni yathāsvāni bhejire puruṣarṣabhāḥ ||107||
