Mahābhārata 3.289 (Āraṇyakaparvan)

1mārkaṇḍeya uvāca|
tāvubhau patitau dṛṣṭvā bhrātarau rāmalakṣmaṇau
|
babandha rāvaṇirbhūyaḥ śarairdattavaraistadā||1||

2tī vīrau śarabandhena baddhāvindrajitā raṇe
|
rejatuḥ puruṣavyāghrau śakuntāviva pañjare||2||

3tau dṛṣṭvā patitau bhūmau śataśaḥ sāyakaiścitau
|
sugrīvaḥ kapibhiḥ sārdhaṃ parivārya tataḥ sthitaḥ||3||

4suṣeṇamaindadvividaiḥ kumudenāṅgadena ca
|
hanumannīlatāraiśca nalena ca kapīśvaraḥ||4||

5tatastaṃ deśamāgamya kṛtakarmā vibhīṣaṇaḥ
|
bodhayāmāsa tau vīrau prajñāstreṇa prabodhitau||5||

6viśalyau cāpi sugrīvaḥ kṣaṇenaitau cakāra ha
|
viśalyayā mahauṣadhyā divyamantraprayuktayā||6||

7tau labdhasaṃjñau nṛvarau viśalyāvudatiṣṭatām
|
gatatandrīklamau cāpi kṣaṇenetau mahārathau||7||

8tato vibhīṣaṇaḥ pārtha rāmamikṣvākunandanam
|
uvāca vijvaraṃ dṛṣṭā kṛtāñjaliridaṃ vacaḥ||8||

9idamambho gṛhītvā tu rājarājasya śāsanāt
|
guhyako'bhyāgataḥ śvettāt tvatsakāśamarindama||9||

10idamambhaḥ kuberaste mahārāja prayacchati
|
antarhitānāṃ bhūtānāṃ darśanārthaṃ parantapaḥ||10||

11anena mṛṣṭanayano bhūtānyantarhitānyuta
|
bhavān drakṣyati yasmai ca pradāsyati naraḥ sa tu||11||

12tatheti rāmastadvāri pratigṛhyābhisaṃskṛtam
|
cakāra netrayoḥ śaucaṃ lakṣmaṇaśca mahāmanāḥ||12||

13sugrīvajāmbavantau ca hanumānaṅgadastathā
|
maindadvividanīlāśca prāyaḥ plavagasattamāḥ||13||

14tathā samabhavaccāpi yaduvāca vibhīṣaṇaḥ
|
kṣaṇenātīndriyāṇyeṣāṃ cakṣuṃṣyāsan yudhiṣṭhira||14||

15indrajit kṛtakarmā ca pitre karma tadā''tmanaḥ
|
nivedya punarāgacchat tvarayā''ji śiraḥprati||15||

16tamāpatantaṃ saṃkruddhaṃ punareva yuyutsayā
|
abhidudrāva saumitrirvibhīṣaṇamate sthitaḥ||16||

17akṛtāhnikamevainaṃ jighāṃsurjitakāśinam
|
śarairjaghāna saṅkruddhaḥ kṛtasaṃjño'tha lakṣmaṇaḥ||17||

18tayoḥ samabhavadyuddhaṃ tadā'nyonyaṃ jigīṣatoḥ
|
atīva citramāścaryaṃ śakrapralhādayoriva||18||

19avidhyādendrajittīkṣṇaiḥ saumitriṃ marmabhedibhiḥ
|
saumitriścānalasparśairavidhyadrāvaṇiṃ śaraiḥ||19||

20saumitriśarasaṃsparśādrāvāṇiḥ krodhamūrcchitaḥ
|
asṛjallakṣmaṇāyāṣṭau śarānāśīviṣopamān||20||

21tasyāsūn pāvakasparśaiḥ saumitriḥ pattribhistribhiḥ
|
yathā niraharadvīrastanme nigadataḥ śṛṇu||21||

22ekenāsya dhanuṣmantaṃ bāhuṃ dehādapātayat
|
dvitīyena sa nārācaṃ bhujaṃ bhūmau nyapātayat||22||

23tṛtīyena tu bāṇena pṛthudhāreṇa bhāsvatā
|
jahāra sunasaṃ cāpi śiro bhrājiṣṇukuṇḍalam||23||

24vinikṛttabhujaskandhaṃ kabandhaṃ bhīmadarśanam
|
taṃ hatvā sūtamapyastrairjaghāna balināṃ varaḥ||24||

25laṅkāṃ praveśayāmāsustaṃ rathaṃ vājinastadā
|
dadarśa rāvaṇastaṃ ca rathaṃ putravinākṛtam||25||

26sa putraṃ nihataṃ dṛṣṭvā trāsāt sambhrāntamānasaḥ
|
rāvaṇaḥ śokamohārto vaidehīṃ hantumudyataḥ||26||

27aśokavanikāsthāṃ tāṃ rāmadarśanalālasām
|
khaḍgamādāya duṣṭātmā javenābhipapāta ha||27||

28taṃ dṛṣṭrā tasya durbuddheravindhyaḥ pāpaniścayam
|
śamayāmāsa saṅkruddhaṃ śrūyatāṃ yena hetunā||28||

29mahārājye sthito dīpte na striyaṃ hantumarhasi
|
hataivaiṣā yadā strī ca bandhanasthā ca te vaśe||29||

30na caiṣā dehabhedena hatā syāditi me matiḥ
|
jahi bhartāramevāsyā hate tasmin hatā bhavet||30||

31na hi te vikrame tulyaḥ sākṣādapi śatakratuḥ
|
asakṛddhi tvayā sendrāstrāsitāstridaśā yudhi||31||

32evaṃ bahuvidhairvākyairavindhyo rāvaṇaṃ tadā
|
kruddhaṃ saṃśamayāmāsa jagṛhe ca sa tadvacaḥ||32||

33niryāṇe sa matiṃ kṛtvā nidhāyāsiṃ kṣapācaraḥ
|
ājñāpayāmāsa tadā ratho me kalpyatāmiti||33||
