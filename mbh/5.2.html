Mahābhārata 5.2 (Udyogaparvan)

1baladeva uvāca|
śrutaṃ bhavadbhirgadapūrvajasya vākyaṃ yathā dharmavadarthavacca
|
ajātaśatrośca hitaṃ hitaṃ ca duryodhanasyāpi tathaiva rājñaḥ ||1||

2ardhaṃ hi rājyasya visṛjya vīrāḥ kuntīsutāstasya kṛte yatante
|
pradāya cārdhaṃ dhṛtarāṣṭraputraḥ sukhī sahāsmābhiratīva modet ||2||

3labdhvā hi rājyaṃ pūruṣapravīrāḥ samyakpravṛtteṣu pareṣu caiva
|
dhruvaṃ praśāntāḥ sukhamāviśeyusteṣāṃ praśāntiśca hitaṃ prajānām ||3||

4duryodhanasyāpi mataṃ ca vettuṃ vaktuṃ ca vākyāni yudhiṣṭhirasya
|
priyaṃ ca me syādyadi tatra kaścidvrajecchamārthaṃ kurupāṇḍavānām ||4||

5sa bhīṣmamāmantrya kurupravīraṃ vaicitravīryaṃ ca mahānubhāvam
|
droṇaṃ saputraṃ viduraṃ kṛpaṃ ca gāndhārarājaṃ ca sasūtaputram ||5||

6sarve ca ye'nye dhṛtarāṣṭraputrā balapradhānā nigamapradhānāḥ
|
sthitāśca dharmeṣu tathā svakeṣu lokapravīrāḥ śrutakālavṛddhāḥ ||6||

7eteṣu sarveṣu samāgateṣu paureṣu vṛddheṣu ca saṃgateṣu
|
bravītu vākyaṃ praṇipātayuktaṃ kuntīsutasyārthakaraṃ yathā syāt ||7||

8sarvāsvavasthāsu ca te na kopyā grasto hi so'rtho balamāśritaistaiḥ
|
priyābhyupetasya yudhiṣṭhirasya dyūte prasaktasya hṛtaṃ ca rājyam ||8||

9nivāryamāṇaśca kurupravīraḥ sarvaiḥ suhṛdbhirhyayamapyatajjñaḥ
|
sa dīvyamānaḥ pratidīvya cainaṃ gāndhārarājasya sutaṃ matākṣam ||9||

10hitvā hi karṇaṃ ca suyodhanaṃ ca samāhvayaddevitumājamīḍhaḥ
|
durodarāstatra sahasraśo'nye yudhiṣṭhiro yānviṣaheta jetum ||10||

11utsṛjya tānsaubalameva cāyaṃ samāhvayattena jito'kṣavatyām
|
sa dīvyamānaḥ pratidevanena akṣeṣu nityaṃ svaparāṅmukheṣu ||11||

12saṃrambhamāṇo vijitaḥ prasahya tatrāparādhaḥ śakunerna kaścit
|
tasmātpraṇamyaiva vaco bravītu vaicitravīryaṃ bahusāmayuktam ||12||

13tathā hi śakyo dhṛtarāṣṭraputraḥ svārtheṃ niyoktuṃ puruṣeṇa tena
|
ayuddhamākāṅkṣata kauravāṇāṃ sāmnaiva duryodhanamāhvayadhvam||13||

14sāmnā jito'rtho'rthakaro bhaveta yuddhe'nayo bhavitā neha so'rthaḥ ||14||

15vaiśampāyana uvāca|
evaṃ bruvatyeva madhupravīre śinipravīraḥ sahasotpapāta
|
taccāpi vākyaṃ parinindya tasya samādade vākyamidaṃ samunyuḥ ||15||
