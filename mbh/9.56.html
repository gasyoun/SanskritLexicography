Mahābhārata 9.56 (Śalyaparvan)

1vaiśampāyana uvāca|
tato vāgyuddhamabhavattumulaṃ janamejaya
|
yatra duḥkhānvito rājā dhṛtarāṣṭro'bravīdidam||1||

2dhigastu khalu mānuṣyaṃ yasya niṣṭheyamīdṛśī
|
ekādaśa camūbhartā yajña putro mamānagha||2||

3ājñāpya sarvānnṛpatīn bhuktvā cemāṃ vasundharām
|
gadāmādāya vegena padātiḥ prasthito raṇe||3||

4bhūtvā hi jagato nātho hyanātha iva me sutaḥ
|
gadāmudyamya yo yāti kimanyadbhāgadheyataḥ||4||

5aho duḥkhaṃ mahatprāptaṃ putreṇa mama sañjaya
|
evamuktvā sa duḥkhārto virarāma janādhipaḥ||5||

6sañjaya uvāca|
sa meghaninado harṣānninadanniva govṛṣaḥ
|
ājuhāva tadā pārthaṃ yuddhāya yudhi vīryavān||6||

7bhīmamāhvāyamāne tu kururāje mahātmāni
|
prādurāsansughorāṇi rūpāṇi vividhānyuta||7||

8vavurvātāḥ sanirghātāḥ pāṃśuvarṣaṃ papāta ca
|
babhūvuśca diśaḥ sarvāstimireṇa samāvṛtāḥ||8||

9mahāsvanāḥ sunirvātāstumulā lomaharṣaṇā
|
petustatholkāḥ śataśaḥ sphoṭayantyo nabhastalāt||9||

10rāhuścāgrasadādityamaparvaṇi viśāmpate
|
cakampe ca mahākampaṃ pṛthivī savanadrumā||10||

11dīptāśca vātaḥ pravavurnīcaiḥ śarkarakarṣiṇaḥ
|
girīṇāṃ śikharāṇyeva nyapatanta mahītale||11||

12mṛgā bahuvidhākārāḥ sampatanti diśo daśa
|
dīptāḥ śivāścāpyanadan ghorarūpāḥ sudāruṇāḥ||12||

13nirghātāśca mahāghorā babhūvurlomaharṣaṇāḥ
|
dīptāyāṃ diśi rājendra mṛgāścāśubhavedinaḥ||13||

14udapānagatāścāpo vyavardhanta samantataḥ
|
aśarīrā mahānādāḥ śrūyante sma tadā nṛpa||14||

15evamādīni dṛṣṭvā''tha nimittāni vṛkodaraḥ
|
uvāca bhrātaraṃ jyeṣṭhaṃ dharmarājaṃ yudhiṣṭhiram||15||

16naiṣa śakto raṇe jetuṃ mandātmā māṃ suyodhanaḥ
|
adya krodhaṃ vimokṣyāmi vigūḍhaṃ hṛdaye ciram||16||

17suyodhane kauravendre khāṇḍave pāvako tathā
|
śalyamadyoddhariṣyāmi tava pāṇḍava hṛcchayam||17||

18nihatya gadayā pāpamimaṃ kurukulādhamam
|
adya kīrtimayīṃ mālāṃ pratimokṣyāmyahaṃ tvayi||18||

19hatvemaṃ pāpakarmāṇaṃ gadayā raṇamūrdhani
|
adyāsya śatadhā dehaṃ bhinadmi gadayā'nayā||19||

20nāyaṃ praveṣṭā nagaraṃ punarvāraṇasāhvayam
|
sarpotsargasya śayane viṣadānasya bhojane
|
||20||

21pramāṇakoṭyāṃ pātasya dāhasya jatuveśmani
|
sabhāyāmavahāsasya sarvasvaharaṇasya ca||21||

22varṣamajñātavāsasya vanavāsasya cānagha
|
adyāntameṣāṃ duḥkhānāṃ gantāhaṃ bharatarṣabha||22||

23ekāhrā vinihatyemaṃ bhaviṣyāmyātmano'nṛṇaḥ
|
adyāyurdhārtarāṣṭrasya durmaterakṛtātmanaḥ||23||

24samāptaṃ bharataśreṣṭha mātāpitrośca darśanam
|
adya saukhyaṃ tu rājendra kururājasya durmateḥ||24||

25samāptaṃ ca mahārāja nārīṇāṃ darśanaṃ punaḥ
|
adyāyaṃ kururājasya śāntanoḥ kulapāṃsanaḥ||25||

26prāṇān śriyaṃ ca rājyaṃ ca tyaktvā śeṣyati bhūtale
|
rājā ca dhṛtarāṣṭro'dya śrutvā putraṃ nipātitam||26||

27smariṣyatyaśubhaṃ karma yattacchakunibuddhijam
|
ityuktvā rājaśārdūla gadāmādāya vīryavān||27||

28abhyatiṣṭhata yuddhāya śakro vṛtramivāhvayan
|
tamudyatagadaṃ dṛṣṭvā kailāsamiva śṛṅgiṇam||28||

29bhīmasenaḥ punaḥ kruddho duryodhanamuvāca ha
|
rājñaśca dhṛtarāṣṭrasya tathā tvamapi cātmanaḥ||29||

30spara tadduṣkṛtaṃ karma yadvṛttaṃ vāraṇāvate
|
draupadī ca parikliṣṭā sabhāmadhye rajasvalā||30||

31dyūte na vañcito rājā yattvayā saubalena ca
|
vane duḥkhaṃ ca yatprāptamasmābhistvatkṛtaṃ mahat||31||

32virāṭanagare caiva yonyantaragatairiva
|
tatsarvaṃ pātayāmyadya diṣṭyā dṛṣṭo'si durmate||32||

33tvatkṛte'sau hataḥ śete śaratalpe pratāpavān
|
gāṅgeyo rathināṃ śreṣṭho nihato yājñaseninā||33||

34hato droṇaśca karṇaśca tathā śalyaḥ pratāpavān
|
vairāgnerādikartāsau śakuniḥ saubalo hataḥ||34||

35prātikāmī tathā pāpo draupadyāḥ kleśakṛddhataḥ
|
bhrātaraste hatāḥ sarve śūrā vikrāntayodhinaḥ||35||

36ete cānye ca bahavo nihatāstvatkṛte nṛpāḥ
|
tvāmadya nihaniṣyāmi gadayā nātra saṃśayaḥ||36||

37ityevamuccai rājendra bhāṣamāṇaṃ vṛkodaram
|
uvāca gatabhī rājan putraste satyavikramaḥ||37||

38kiṃ katthanena bahunā yudhyasva tvaṃ vṛkodara
|
adya te'haṃ vineṣyāmi yuddhaśraddhāṃ kulādhama||38||

39na hi duryodhanaḥ kṣudra kenacittvadvidhena vai
|
śakyastrāsayituṃ vācāṃ yathā'nyaḥ prākṛto naraḥ||39||

40cirakālepsitaṃ diṣṭyā hṛdayasthamidaṃ mama
|
tvayā saha gadāyuddhaṃ tridaśairupapāditam||40||

41kiṃ vācā bahunoktena katthitena ca durmate
|
vāṇī sampadyatāmeṣā karmaṇā mā ciraṃ kṛthāḥ||41||

42tasya tadvacanaṃ śrutvā sarva evābhyapūjayan
|
rājānaḥ somakāścaiva ye tatrāsan samāgatāḥ||42||

43tataḥ sampūjitaḥ sarvaiḥ samprahṛṣṭatanūruhaḥ
|
bhūyo dhīrāṃ matiṃ cakre yuddhāya kurunandanaḥ||43||

44unmattamiva mātaṅgaṃ talaśabdairnarādhipāḥ
|
bhūyaḥ saṃharṣayāñcakrurduryodhanamamarṣaṇam||44||

45taṃ mahātmā mahātmānaṃ gadāmudyamya pāṇḍavaḥ
|
abhidudrāva vegena dhārtarāṣṭraṃ vṛkodaraḥ||45||

46bṛṃhanti kuñjarāstatra hayā hreṣanti cāsakṛt
|
śastrāṇi cāpyadīpyanta pāṇḍavānāṃ jayaiṣiṇām||46||
