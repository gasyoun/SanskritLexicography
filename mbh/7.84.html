Mahābhārata 7.84 (Droṇaparvan)

1sañjaya uvāca|
tathā tu vadatāṃ teṣāṃ prādurāsīddhanañjayaḥ
|
didṛkṣurbharataśreṣṭhaṃ rājānaṃ sasuhṛdgaṇanam ||1||

2taṃ niviṣṭaṃ śubhāṃ kakṣyāmabhivandyāgrataḥ sthitam
|
tamutthāyārjunaṃ premṇā sasvaje pāṇḍavarṣabhaḥ ||2||

3mūrdhni cainamupāghrāya pariṣvajya ca bāhunā
|
āśiṣaḥ paramāḥ procya smayamāno'bhyabhāṣata ||3||

4vyaktamarjunasaṃgrāme dhruvaste vijayo mahān
|
yādṛgrūpā ca te cchāyā prasannaśca janārdanaḥ ||4||

5tamabravīt tato jiṣṇurmahadāścaryamuttamam
|
dṛṣṭavānasmi bhadraṃ te keśavasya prasādajam ||5||

6tatastatkathayāmāsa yathā dṛṣṭaṃ dhanañjayaḥ
|
āśvāsanārthaṃ suhṛdāṃ tryambakeṇa samāgamam ||6||

7tataḥ śirobhiravaniṃ spṛṣṭvā sarve ca vismitāḥ
|
namaskṛtya vṛṣāṅkāya sādhu sādhvityathābruvan‌ ||7||

8anujñātāstataḥ sarve suhṛdo dharmasūnunā
|
tvaramāṇāḥ susannaddhā hṛṣṭā yuddhāya niryayuḥ ||8||

9abhivādya tu rājānaṃ yuyudhānācyutārjunāḥ
|
hṛṣṭā viniryayuste vai yudhiṣṭhiraniveśanāt ||9||

10rathenaikena durdharṣo yuyudhānajanārdanau
|
jagmatuḥ sahitau vīrāvarjunasya niveśanam ||10||

11tatra gatvā hṛṣīkeśaḥ kalpayāmāsa sūtavat
|
rathaṃ rathavarasyājau vānararṣabhalakṣaṇam ||11||

12sa meghasamanirghoṣastaptakāñcanasaprabhaḥ
|
babhau rathavaraḥ klṛptaḥ śiśurdivasakṛdyathā ||12||

13tataḥ puruṣaśārdūlaḥ sajjaṃ sajjapuraḥsaraḥ
|
kṛtāhnikāya pārthāya nyavedayata taṃ ratham ||13||

14taṃ tu lokavaraḥ puṃsāṃ kirīṭī hemavarmabhṛt
|
cāpabāṇadharo vāhaṃ pradakṣiṇamavartata ||14||

15tapovidyāvayovṛddhaiḥ kriyāvadbhirjitendriyaiḥ
|
stūyamāno jayāśīrbhirāruroha mahāratham ||15||

16jaitraiḥ sāṃgrāmikairmantraiḥ pūrvameva rathottamam
|
abhimantritamarciṣmānudayaṃ bhāskaro yathā ||16||

17sa rathe rathināṃ śreṣṭhaḥ kāñcane kāñcanāvṛtaḥ
|
vibabhau vimalorciṣmān merāviva divākaraḥ ||17||

18anvāruruhatuḥ pārthaṃ yuyudhānajanārdanau
|
śaryāteryajñamāyāntaṃ yathendraṃ devamaśvinau ||18||

19atha jagrāha govindo raśmīnraśmividāṃ varaḥ
|
mātalirvāsavasyeva vṛtraṃ hantuṃ prayāsyataḥ ||19||

20sa tābhyāṃ sāhitaḥ pārtho rathapravaramāsthitaḥ
|
sahito budhaśukrābhyāṃ tamo nighnan yathā śaśī ||20||

21saindhavasya vadhaṃ prepsuḥ prayātaḥ śatrupūgahā
|
sahāṃbupatimitrābhyāṃ yathendrastārakāmaye ||21||

22tato vāditrānirghoṣairmāṅgalyaiśca stavaiḥ śubhaiḥ
|
prayāntamarjunaṃ vīraṃ māgadhāścaiva tuṣṭuvuḥ ||22||

23sajayāśīḥ sapuṇyāhaḥ sutamāga‌dhaniḥsvanaḥ
|
yukto vāditraghoṣeṇa teṣāṃ rātrikaro'bhavat ||23||

24tamanuprayato vāyuḥ puṇyagandhavahaḥ śubhaḥ
|
vavau saṃharṣayan pārthaṃ dviṣataścāpi śoṣayan
|
tatastasmin kṣaṇe rājan vividhāni śubhāni ca ||24||

25prādurāsannimittāni vijayāya bahūni ca
|
pāṇḍavānāṃ tvadīyānāṃ viparītāni māriṣa ||25||

26dṛṣṭvā'rjuno nimittāni vijayāya pradakṣiṇam
|
yuyudhānaṃ maheṣvāsamidaṃ vacanamabravīt ||26||

27yuyudhānādya yuddhe me dṛśyate vijayo dhruvaḥ
|
yathā hīmāni liṃgāni dṛśyante śinipuṅgava ||27||

28so'haṃ tatra gamiṣyāmi yatra saindhavako nṛpaḥ
|
yiyāsuryamalokāya mama vīryaṃ pratīkṣate ||28||

29yathā paramakaṃ kṛtyaṃ saindhavasya vadho mama
|
tathaiva sumahatkṛtyaṃ dharmarājasya rakṣaṇam ||29||

30sa tvamadya mahābāho rājānaṃ paripālaya
|
yathaiva hi mayā guptastvayā gupto bhavettathā ||30||

31na paśyāmi ca taṃ loke yastvāṃ yuddhe parājayet
|
vāsudevasamaṃ yuddhe svayamapyamareśvaraḥ ||31||

32tvayi cāhaṃ parāśvastaḥ pradyumne vā mahārathe
|
śaknuyāṃ saindhavaṃ hantumanapekṣo nararṣabha ||32||

33mayyapekṣā na kartavyā kathañcidapi sātvata
|
rājanyeva parā guptiḥ kāryā sarvātmanā tvayā ||33||

34na hi yatra mahābāhurvāsudevo vyavasthitaḥ
|
kiñcidvyāpadyate tatra yatrāhamapi ca dhruvam ||34||

35evamuktastu pārthena sātyakiḥ paravīrahā
|
tathetyuktvā'gamat tatra yatra rājā yudhiṣṭhiraḥ ||35||
