Mahābhārata 8.12 (Karṇaparvan)

1sañjaya uvāca|
te sene'nyonyamāsādya prahṛṣṭāśvanaradvipe
|
bṛhatyau saṃprajahrāte devāsurasamaprabhe ||1||

2tato nararathāśvebhaiḥ pattayaścogravikramāḥ
|
saṃprahārān bhṛśaṃ cakrurdehapāpmāsunāśanān ||2||

3pūrṇacandrārkapadmānāṃ kāntibhirgandhataḥ samaiḥ
|
uttamāṅgairnṛsiṃhānāṃ nṛsiṃhāstastarurmahīm ||3||

4ardhacandraistathā bhallaiḥ kṣuraprairasipaṭṭiśaiḥ
|
paraśvadhaiścāpyakṛntannuttamāṅgāni yudhyatām ||4||

5vyāyatāyatabāhūnāṃ vyāyatāyatabāhubhiḥ
|
bāhavaḥ pātitā rejurdharaṇyāṃ sāyudhāṅgadaiḥ||5||

6taiḥ sphuradbhirmahī bhāti raktāṅgulitalaistathā
|
garuḍaprahitairugraiḥ pañcāsyairuragairiva ||6||

7dviradasyandanāśvebhyaḥ peturvīrā dviṣaddhatāḥ
|
vimānebhyo yathā kṣīṇe puṇye svargasadastathā ||7||

8gadābhiranye gurvībhiḥ parighairmusalairapi
|
pothitāḥ śataśaḥ peturvīrā vīratarai raṇe ||8||

9rathā rathairvimathitā mattā mattairdvipā dvipaiḥ
|
sādinaḥ sādibhiścaiva tasminparamasaṃkule ||9||

10rathairnarā rathā nāgairaśvārohāśca pattibhiḥ
|
aśvārohaiḥ padātāśca nihatā yudhi śerate ||10||

11rathāśvapattayo nāgai rathāśvebhāśca pattibhiḥ
|
rathapattidvipāścāśvai rathaiścāpi naradvipāḥ ||11||

12rathāśvebhanarāṇāṃ tu narāśvebharathaiḥ kṛtam
|
pāṇipādaiśca śastraiśca rathaiśca kadanaṃ mahat ||12||

13tathā tasminbale śūrairvadhyamāne hate'pi ca
|
asmānabhyāyayuḥ pārthā vṛkodarapurogamāḥ ||13||

14dhṛṣṭadyumnaḥ śikhaṇḍī ca draupadeyāḥ prabhadrakāḥ
|
sātyakiścekitānaśca drāviḍaiḥ sainikaiḥ saha ||14||

15vṛtā vyūhena mahatā pāṇḍyāścolāḥ sakeralāḥ
|
vyūḍhoraskā dīrghabhujāḥ prāṃśavaḥ pṛthulocanāḥ ||15||

16āpīḍino raktadantā mattamātaṅgavikramāḥ
|
nānāvirāgavasanā gandhacūrṇāvacūrṇitāḥ ||16||

17baddhāsayaḥ pāśahastā vāraṇaprativāraṇāḥ
|
samānamṛtyavo rājannātyajanta parasparam ||17||

18kalāpinaścāpahastā dīrghakeśāḥ priyaṃvadāḥ
|
pattayaḥ sāddinaścānye ghorarūpaparākramāḥ ||18||

19athāpare punaḥ śūrāścedipañcālakekayāḥ
|
kārūṣāḥ kosalāḥ kāñcyā māgadhāścāpi dudruvuḥ ||19||

20teṣāṃ rathāśvanāgāśca pravarāścograpattayaḥ
|
nānāvādyadharairhṛṣṭā nṛtyanti ca hasanti ca ||20||

21tasya sainyasya mahato mahāmātravarairvṛtaḥ
|
madhye vṛkodaro'bhyāyāttvadīyānnāgadhūrgataḥ ||21||

22sa nāgapravaro'tyugro vidhivatkalpito babhau
|
u‌dyāgrādribhavanaṃ yathā'bhyuditabhāskaram ||22||

23tasyāyasaṃ varma varaṃ vararatnavibhūṣitam
|
tārāvyāptasya nabhasaḥ śāradasya samatviṣam ||23||

24sa tomaravyagrakaraścārumauliḥ svalaṃkṛtaḥ
|
śaranmadhyaṃdinārkābhastejasā vyadahadripūn ||24||

25taṃ dṛṣṭvā dviradaṃ dūrātkṣemadhūrtirdvipasthitaḥ
|
āhvayannabhidudrāva pramanāḥ pramanastaram ||25||

26tayoḥ samabhavadyuddhaṃ dvipayorugrarūpayoḥ
|
yadṛcchayā drumavatormahāparvatayoriva ||26||

27saṃsaktanāgau tau vīrau tomarairitaretaram
|
balavatsūryaraśmyābhairbhittvā'nyonyaṃ vinedatuḥ ||27||

28vyapasṛtya tu nāgābhyāṃ maṇḍalāni viceratuḥ
|
pragṛhya cobhau dhanuṣī jaghnaturvai parasparam ||28||

29kṣveḍitāsphoṭitaravairbāṇaśabdaistu sarvataḥ
|
tau janaṃ harṣayantau ca siṃhanādaṃ pracakratuḥ ||29||

30samudyatakarābhyāṃ tau dvipābhyāṃ kṛtināvubhau
|
vātoddhūtapatākābhyāṃ yuyudhāte mahābalau ||30||

31tāvanyonyasya dhanuṣī chittvā'nyonyaṃ vinedatuḥ
|
śaktitomaravarṣeṇa prāvṛṇmeghāvivāmbubhiḥ ||31||

32kṣemadhūrtistadā bhīmaṃ tomareṇa stanāntare
|
nirbibhedātivegena ṣaḍbhiścāpyaparairnadan ||32||

33sa bhīmasenaḥ śuśubhe tomarai raṅgamāśritaiḥ
|
krodhadīptavapurmeghaiḥ saptasaptirivāṃśumān ||33||

34tato bhāskaravarṇābhamañjogatimayasmayam
|
sasarja tomaraṃ bhīmaḥ pratyamitrāya yatnavān ||34||

35tataḥ kulūtādhipatiścāpamānamya sāyakaiḥ
|
daśabhistomaraṃ bhittvā ṣaṣṭyā vivyādha pāṇḍavam ||35||

36atha kārmukamādāya bhīmo jaladaniḥsvanam
|
riporabhyardayannāgamunnadanpāṇḍavaḥ śaraiḥ ||36||

37sa śaraughārdito nāgo bhīmasenena saṃyuge
|
gṛhyamāṇo'pi nātiṣṭhadvātoddhūta ivāmbudaḥ ||37||

38tamabhyadhāvaddviradaṃ bhīmo bhīmasya nāgarāṭ
|
mahāvāteritaṃ meghaṃ vātoddhūta ivāṃbudaḥ ||38||

39sannivāryātmano nāgaṃ kṣemadhūrtiḥ pratāpavān
|
vivyādhābhidrutaṃ bāṇairbhīmasenasya kuñjaram ||39||

40tataḥ sādhuvisṛṣṭena kṣureṇānataparvaṇā
|
chittvā śarāsanaṃ śatrornāgamāmitramārdayat ||40||

41tataḥ kruddho raṇe bhīmaṃ kṣemadhūrtiḥ parābhinat
|
jaghāna cāsya dviradaṃ nārācaiḥ sarvamarmasu ||41||

42sa papāta mahānāgo bhīmasenasya bhārata
|
purā nāgasya patanādavaplutya sthito mahīm ||42||

43tasya bhīmo'pi dviradaṃ gadayā samapothayat
|
tasmātpramathitānnāgāt kṣemadhūrtimavaplutam ||43||

44udyatāyudhamāyāntaṃ gada‌yā'han vṛkodaraḥ
|
sa papāta hataḥ sāsirvyasustamabhito dvipam ||44||

45vajraprabhagnamacalaṃ siṃho vajrahato yathā
|
taṃ hataṃ nṛpatiṃ dṛṣṭvā kulūtānāṃ yaśaskaram
|
prādravadvyathitā senā tvadīyā bharatarṣabha ||45||
