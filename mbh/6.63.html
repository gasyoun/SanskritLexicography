Mahābhārata 6.63 (Bhīṣmaparvan)

1sañjaya uvāca|
hate tasmingajānīke putro duryodhanastava
|
bhīmasenaṃ ghnatetyevaṃ sarvasainyānyacodayat ||1||

2tataḥ sarvāṇyanīkāni tava putrasya śāsanāt
|
abhyadravan bhīmasenaṃ nadantaṃ bhairavān ravān ||2||

3taṃ balaughamaparyantaṃ devairapi suduḥsaham
|
āpatantaṃ suduṣpāraṃ samudramiva parvaṇi ||3||

4rathanāgāśvakalilaṃ śaṅkhadundubhināditam
|
anantarathapādātaṃ narendrastimitahradam ||4||

5taṃ bhīmasenaḥ samare mahoda‌dhimivāparam
|
senāsāgaramakṣobhyaṃ veleva samavārayat ||5||

6tadāścaryamapaśyāma pāṇḍavasya mahātmanaḥ
|
bhīmasenasya samare rājan karmātimānuṣam ||6||

7udīrṇānpārthivānsarvānsāśvānsarathakuñjarān
|
asaṃbhramaṃ bhīmaseno gadayā samavārayat ||7||

8sa saṃvārya balaughāṃstān gadayā rathināṃ varaḥ
|
atiṣṭhattumule bhīmo girirmerurivācalaḥ ||8||

9tasminsutumule ghore kāle paramadāruṇe
|
bhrātaraścaiva putrāśca dhṛṣṭadyumnaśca pārṣataḥ ||9||

10draupadeyā'bhimanyuśca śikhaṇḍī cāparājitaḥ
|
na prājahan bhīmasenaṃ bhaye jāte mahābalam ||10||

11tataḥ śaikyāyasīṃ gurvīṃ pragṛhya mahatīṃ gadām
|
adhāvattāvakān yodhān daṇḍapāṇirivāntakaḥ||11||

12pothayan rathavṛndāni vājivṛndāni cābhibhūḥ
|
karṣayan rathavṛndāni bāhuvegena pāṇḍavaḥ ||12||

13vinighnan vyacaratsaṃkhye yugānte kālavadvibhuḥ
|
ūruvegena saṃkarṣan rathajālāni pāṇḍavaḥ ||13||

14balāni saṃmamardāśu naḍvalānīva kuñjaraḥ
|
mṛdnanrathebhyo rathino gajebhyo gajayodhinaḥ ||14||

15sādinaścāśvapṛṣṭhebhyo bhūmau cāpi padātinaḥ
|
gadayā vyadhamatsarvānvāto vṛkṣānivaujasā ||15||

16bhīmaseno mahābāhustava putrasya vai bale
|
sā'pi majjāvasāmāṃsaiḥ pradigdhā rudhireṇa ca ||16||

17adṛśyata mahāraudro gadā nāgāśvapātanī
|
tatra tatra hataiścāpi manuṣya-gaja-vājibhiḥ ||17||

18raṇāṅgaṇaṃ samabhavanmṛtyorāvāsasannibham
|
pinākamiva rudrasya kuddhasyābhighnataḥ paśūn ||18||

19yamadaṇḍopamāmugrāmindrāśanisamasvanām
|
dadṛśurbhīmasenasya raudrīṃ viśasanīṃ gadām ||19||

20āviddhyato gadāṃ tasya kaunteyasya mahātmanaḥ
|
babhau rūpaṃ mahāghoraṃ kālasyeva yugakṣaye ||20||

21taṃ tathā mahatīṃ senāṃ drāvayantaṃ punaḥ punaḥ
|
dṛṣṭvā mṛtyumivāyāntaṃ sarve vimanaso'bhavan ||21||

22yato yataḥ prekṣate sma gadāmudyamya pāṇḍavaḥ
|
tena tena sma dīryante sarvasainyāni bhārata ||22||

23pradārayantaṃ sainyāni balenāmitavikramam
|
grasamānamanīkāni vyāditāsyamivāntakam ||23||

24taṃ tathā bhīmakarmāṇaṃ pragṛhītamahāgadam
|
dṛṣṭvā vṛkodaraṃ bhīṣmaḥ sahasaiva samabhyayāt ||24||

25mahatā rathaghoṣeṇa rathenādityavarcasā
|
chādayan śaravarṣeṇa parjanya iva vṛṣṭimān ||25||

26tamāyāntaṃ tathā dṛṣṭvā vyāttānanamivāntakam
|
bhīṣmaṃ bhīmo mahābāhuḥ pratyudīyādamarṣitaḥ ||26||

27tasminkṣaṇe sātyakiḥ satyasandhaḥ śinipravīro'bhyapatatpitāmaham
|
nighnannamitrān dhanuṣā dṛḍhena saṃkampayaṃstava putrasya sainyam ||27||

28taṃ yāntamaśvai rajataprakāśaiḥ śarān vapantaṃ niśitān supuṅkhān
|
nāśaknuvan dhārayituṃ tadānīṃ sarve gaṇā bhārata ye tvadīyāḥ ||28||

29avidhyadenaṃ daśabhiḥ pṛṣatkairalambuṣo rākṣaso'sau tadānīm
|
śaraiścaturbhiḥ pratividdhya taṃ ca naptā śinerabhyapatadrathena ||29||

30anvāgataṃ vṛṣṇivaraṃ niśamya taṃ śatrumadhye parivartamānam
|
pradrāvayantaṃ kurupuṅgavāṃśca punaḥ punaśca praṇadantamājau ||30||

31yodhāstvadīyāḥ śaravarṣairavarṣan meghā yathā bhūdharamambuvegaiḥ
|
tathā'pi taṃ dhārayituṃ na śekurmadhyandine sūryamivātapantam ||31||

32na tatra kaścinnaviṣaṇṇa āsīdṛte rājansomadattasya putrāt
|
sa vai samādāya dhanurmahātmā bhūriśravā bhārata saumadattiḥ ||32||

33dṛṣṭvā rathān svānvyapanīyamānān
|
pratyudyayau sātyakiṃ yoddhumicchan ||33||
