Mahābhārata 7.63 (Droṇaparvan)

1nārada uvāca| yayātiṃ nāhuṣaṃ caiva mṛtaṃ sṛñjaya śuśruma
|
rājasūyaśatairiṣṭvā so'śvamedhaśatena ca ||1||

2puṇḍarīkasahasreṇa vājapeyaśataistathā
|
atirātrasahasreṇa cāturmāsyaiśca kāmataḥ
|
agniṣṭomaiśca vividhaiḥ satraiśca prājyada‌kṣiṇaiḥ ||2||

3abrāhmaṇānāṃ yadvittaṃ pṛthivyāmasti kiñcana
|
tat sarvaṃ parisaṅkhyāya tato brāhmaṇasātkarot ||3||

4sarasvatī puṇyatamā nadīnāṃ tathā samudrāḥ saritaḥ sādrayaśca
|
ījānāya puṇyatamāya rājñe ghṛtaṃ payo duduhurnāhuṣāya ||4||

5vyūḍhe devāsure yuddhe kṛtvā devasahāyatām
|
caturdhā vyabhajat sarvāṃ caturbhyaḥ pṛthivīmimām ||5||

6yajñairnānāvidhairiṣṭvā prajāmutpādya cottamām
|
devayānyāṃ cauśanasyāṃ śarmiṣṭhāyāṃ ca dharmataḥ ||6||

7devāraṇyeṣu sarveṣu vijahārāmaropamaḥ
|
ātmanaḥ kāmacāreṇa dvitīya iva vāsavaḥ ||7||

8yadā nābhyagamacchāntiṃ kāmānāṃ sarvaveda‌vit
|
tato gāthāmimāṃ gītvā sadāraḥ prāviśadvanam ||8||

9yatpṛthivyāṃ vrīhiyavaṃ hiraṇyaṃ paśavaḥ striyaḥ
|
nālamekasya tatsarvamiti matvā śamaṃ vrajet ||9||

10evaṃ kāmān parityajya yayātirdhṛtimetya ca
|
pūruṃ rājye pratiṣṭhāpya prayāto vanamīśvaraḥ ||10||

11sa cenmamāra sṛñjaya caturbhadratarastvayā
|
putrāt puṇyatarastubhyaṃ mā putramanutapyathāḥ
|
ayajvānamadākṣiṇyamabhiśvaityeti vyāharan ||11||
