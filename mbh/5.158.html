Mahābhārata 5.158 (Udyogaparvan)

1vaiśampāyana uvāca|
etasminneva kāle tu bhīṣmakasya mahātmanaḥ
|
hiraṇyaromṇo nṛpateḥ sākṣādindrasakhasya vai ||1||

2ākūtīnāmadhipatirbhojasyātiyaśasvinaḥ
|
dākṣiṇātyapateḥ putro dikṣu rukmīti viśrutaḥ ||2||

3yaḥ kimpuruṣasiṃhasya gandhamādanavāsinaḥ
|
kṛtsnaṃ śiṣyo dhanurvedaṃ catuṣpāda‌mavāptavān ||3||

4yo māhendraṃ dhanurlebhe tulyaṃ gāṇḍīvatejasā
|
śārṅgeṇa ca mahābāhuḥ sammitaṃ divyalakṣaṇam ||4||

5trīṇyevaitāni divyāni dhanūṃṣi divi cāriṇām
|
vāruṇaṃ gāṇḍivaṃ tatra māhendraṃ vijayaṃ dhanuḥ
|
śārṅgaṃ tu vaiṣṇavaṃ prāhurdivyaṃ tejomayaṃ dhanuḥ ||5||

6dhārayāmāsa tatkṛṣṇaḥ parasenābhayāvaham
|
gāṇḍīvaṃ pāvakāllebhe khāṇḍave pākaśāsaniḥ ||6||

7drumādrukmī mahātejā vijayaṃ pratyapadyata
|
saṃchidya mauravān pāśānnihatya murumojasā ||7||

8nirjitya narakaṃ bhaumamāhṛtya maṇikuṇḍale
|
ṣoḍaśa strīsahasrāṇi ratnāni vividhāni ca ||8||

9pratipede hṛṣīkeśaḥ śārṅgaṃ ca dhanuruttamam
|
rukmī tu vijayaṃ labdhvā dhanurmeghanibhasvanam ||9||

10vibhīṣayanniva jagat pāṇḍavānabhyavartata
|
nāmṛṣyata purā yo'sau svabāhubalagarvitaḥ ||10||

11rukmiṇyā haraṇaṃ vīro vāsudevena dhīmatā
|
kṛtvā pratijñāṃ nāhatvā nivartiṣye janārdanam ||11||

12tato'nvadhāvadvārṣṇeyaṃ sarvaśastrabhṛtāṃ varaḥ
|
senayā caturaṅgiṇyā mahatyā dūrapātayā ||12||

13vicitrāyudhavarmiṇyā gaṅgayeva pravṛddhayā
|
sa samāsādya vārṣṇeyaṃ yogānāmīśvaraṃ prabhum ||13||

14vyaṃsito vrīḍito rājannājagāma sa kuṇḍinam
|
yatraiva kṛṣṇena raṇe nirjitaḥ paravīrahā ||14||

15tatra bhojakaṭaṃ nāma kṛtaṃ nagaramuttamam
|
sainyena mahatā tena prabhūtagajavājinā ||15||

16puraṃ tadbhuvi vikhyātaṃ nāmnā bhojakaṭaṃ nṛpa
|
sa bhojarājaḥ sainyena mahatā parivāritaḥ ||16||

17akṣauhiṇyā mahāvīryaḥ pāṇḍavān kṣipramāgamat
|
tataḥ sa kavacī dhanvī talī khaḍgī śarāsanī ||17||

18dhvajenādityavarṇena praviveśa mahācamūm
|
viditaḥ pāṇḍaveyānāṃ vāsudevapriyepsayā ||18||

19yudhiṣṭhirastu taṃ rājā pratyudgamyābhyapūjayat
|
sa pūjitaḥ pāṇḍuputrairyathānyāyaṃ susaṃstutaḥ ||19||

20pratigṛhya tu tān sarvān viśrāntaḥ sahasainikaḥ
|
uvāca madhye vīrāṇāṃ kuntīputraṃ dhanañjayam ||20||

21sahāyo'smi sthito yuddhe yadi bhīto'si pāṇḍava
|
kariṣyāmi raṇe sāhyamasahyaṃ tava śatrubhiḥ ||21||

22na hi me vikrame tulyaḥ pumānastīha kaścana
|
haniṣyāmi raṇe bhāgaṃ yanme dāsyasi pāṇḍava ||22||

23api droṇakṛpau vīrau bhīṣmakarṇāvatho punaḥ
|
athavā sarvaṃ evaite tiṣṭhantu vasudhādhipāḥ ||23||

24nihatya samare śatrūṃstava dāsyāmi medinīm
|
ityukto dharmarājasya keśavasya ca sannidhau ||24||

25śṛṇvatāṃ pārthivendrāṇāmanyeṣāṃ caiva sarvaśaḥ
|
vāsudevamabhiprekṣya dharmarājaṃ ca pāṇḍavam ||25||

26uvāca dhīmān kaunteyaḥ prahasya sakhipūrvakam
|
kauravāṇāṃ kule jātaḥ pāṇḍoḥ putro viśeṣataḥ ||26||

27droṇaṃ vyapadiśan śiṣyo vāsudevasahāyavān
|
bhīto'smīti kathaṃ brūyāṃ dadhāno gāṇḍivaṃ dhanuḥ ||27||

28yudhyamānasya me vīra gandharvaiḥ sumahābalaiḥ
|
sahāyo ghoṣayātrāyāṃ kastadāsīt sakhā mama ||28||

29tathā pratibhaye tasmin devadānavasaṅkule
|
khāṇḍave yuddhyamānasya kaḥ sahāyastadā'bhavat ||29||

30nivātakavacairyuddhe kālakeyaiśca dānavaiḥ
|
tatra me yuddhyamānasya kaḥ sahāyastadā'bhavat ||30||

31tathā virāṭanagare kurubhiḥ saha saṅgare
|
yudhyato bahubhistatra kaḥ sahāyo'bhavanmama ||31||

32upajīvya raṇe rudraṃ śakraṃ vaiśravaṇaṃ yamam
|
varuṇaṃ pāvakaṃ caiva kṛpaṃ droṇaṃ ca mādhavam ||32||

33dhārayan gāṇḍivaṃ divyaṃ dhanustejomayaṃ dṛḍham
|
akṣayyaśarasaṃyukto divyāstraparibṛṃhitaḥ ||33||

34kathamasmadvidho brūyādbhīto'smīti yaśoharam
|
vacanaṃ naraśārdūla vajrāyudhamapi svayam ||34||

35nāsmi bhīto mahābāho sahāyārthaśca nāsti me
|
yathākāmaṃ yathāyogaṃ gaccha vā'nyatra tiṣṭha vā ||35||

36vinivartya tato rukmī senāṃ sāgarasaṃnibhām
|
duryodhanamupāgacchattathaivaṃ bharatarṣabha ||36||

37tathaiva cābhigamyainamuvāca vasudhādhipaḥ
|
pratyākhyātaśca tenāpi sa tadā śūramāninā ||37||

38dvāveva tu mahārāja tasmādyuddhādapeyatuḥ
|
rauhiṇeyaśca vārṣṇeyo rukmī ca vasudhādhipa ||38||

39gate rāme tīrthayātrāṃ bhīṣmakasya sute tathā
|
upāviśan pāṇḍaveyā mantrāya punareva ca ||39||

40samitirdharmarājasya sā pārthivasamākulā
|
śuśubhe tārakaiścitrā dyauścandreṇeva bhārata ||40||
