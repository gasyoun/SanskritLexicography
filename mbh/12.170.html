Mahābhārata 12.170 (Śāntiparvan)

1bhīṣma uvāca|
giraṃ tāṃ madhurāṃ śrutvā gautamo vismitastadā
|
kautūhalānvito rājan rājadharmāṇamaikṣata ||1||

2rājadharmovāca |
bhoḥ kaśyapasya putro'haṃ mātā dākṣāyaṇī ca me
|
atithistvaṃ guṇopetaḥ svāgataṃ te dvijottama ||2||

3bhīṣma uvāca|
tasmai dattvā sa satkāraṃ vidhidṛṣṭena karmaṇā
|
śālapuṣpamayīṃ divyāṃ bṛsīṃ vai samakalpayat ||3||

4bhagīratharathākrāntadeśān gaṅgāniṣevitān
|
ye caranti mahāmīnāstāṃśca tasyānvakalpayat ||4||

5vahniṃ cāpi susaṃdīptaṃ mīnāṃścāpi supīvarān
|
sa gautamāyātithaye nyavedayata kāśyapiḥ ||5||

6bhuktavantaṃ ca taṃ vipraṃ prītātmānaṃ mahātapāḥ
|
klamāpanayanārthaṃ sa pakṣābhyāmabhyavījayat ||6||

7tato viśrāntamāsīnaṃ gotrapraśnamapṛcchata
|
so'bravīdgautamo'smīti brahma nānyadudāharat ||7||

8tasmai parṇamayaṃ divyaṃ divyapuṣpādhivāsitam
|
gandhāḍhyaṃ śayanaṃ prādātsa śiśye tatra vai sukham ||8||

9athopaviṣṭaṃ śayane gautamaṃ dharmarāṭ tadā
|
papraccha kāśyapo vāgmī kimāgamanakāraṇam ||9||

10tato'bravīdgautamastaṃ daridro'haṃ mahāmate
|
samudragamanākāṅkṣī dravyārthamiti bhārata ||10||

11taṃ kāśyapo'bavītprīto notkaṇṭhāṃ kartumarhasi
|
kṛtakāryo dvijaśreṣṭha sadravyo yāsyase gṛhān ||11||

12caturvidhā hyarthasiddhirbṛhaspatimataṃ yathā
|
pāramparyaṃ tathā daivaṃ kāmyaṃ maitramiti prabho ||12||

13prādurbhūto'smi te mitraṃ suhṛttvaṃ ca mama tvayi
|
so'haṃ tathā yatiṣyāmi bhaviṣyasi yathārthavān ||13||

14tataḥ prabhātasamaye sukhaṃ dṛṣṭvā'bravīdidam
|
gaccha saumya pathā'nena kṛtakṛtyo bhaviṣyasi ||14||

15itastriyojanaṃ gatvā rākṣasādhipatirmahān
|
virūpākṣa iti khyātaḥ sakhā mama mahābalaḥ ||15||

16taṃ gaccha dvijamukhya tvaṃ sa madvākyapracoditaḥ
|
kāmānabhīpsitāṃstubhyaṃ dātā nāstyatra saṃśayaḥ ||16||

17ityuktaḥ prayayau rājan gautamo vigataklamaḥ
|
phalānyamṛtakalpāni bhakṣayan sa yatheṣṭataḥ ||17||

18candanāgurumukhyāni tvakpatrāṇāṃ vanāni ca
|
tasmin pathi mahārāja sevamāno drutaṃ yayau ||18||

19tato meruvrajaṃ nāma nagaraṃ śailatoraṇam
|
śailaprākāravapraṃ ca śailayantrākulaṃ tathā ||19||

20viditaścābhavattasya rākṣasendrasya dhīmataḥ
|
prahitaḥ suhṛdā rājan prīyamāṇaḥ priyātithiḥ ||20||

21tataḥ sa rākṣasendraḥ svān preṣyānāha yudhiṣṭhira
|
gautamo nagaradvārācchīghramānīyatāmiti ||21||

22tataḥ puravarāttasmātpuruṣāḥ śyenaceṣṭanāḥ
|
gautametyabhibhāṣantaḥ puradvāramupāgaman ||22||

23te tamūcurmahārāja rājapreṣyāstadā dvijam
|
tvarasva tūrṇamāgaccha rājā tvāṃ draṣṭumicchati ||23||

24rākṣasādhipatirvīro virūpākṣa iti śrutaḥ
|
sa tvāṃ tvarati vai draṣṭuṃ tatkṣipraṃ saṃvidhīyatām ||24||

25tataḥ sa prādravadvipro vismayādvigalaklamaḥ
|
gautamaḥ paramardhiṃ tāṃ paśyan paramavismitaḥ ||25||

26taireva sahito rājño veśma tūrṇamupādravat
|
darśanaṃ rākṣasendrasya kāṅkṣamāṇo dvijastadā ||26||
