Mahābhārata 1.229 (Ādiparvan)

1janamejaya uvāca|
kimarthaṃ śārṅgakānagnirna dadāha tathā gate
|
tasminvane dahyamāne brahmannetatpracakṣva me||1||

2adāhe hyaśvasenasya dānavasya mayasya ca
|
kāraṇaṃ kīrtitaṃ brahmañcchārṅgakāṇāṃ na kīrtitam||2||

3tadetadadbhutaṃ brahmañcchārṅgakāṇāmanāmayam
|
kīrtayasvāgnisaṃmarde kathaṃ te na vināśitāḥ||3||

4vaiśaṃpāyana uvāca|
yadarthaṃ śārṅgakānagnirna dadāha tathāgate
|
tatte sarvaṃ pravakṣyāmi yathābhūtamarindama||4||

5dharmajñānāṃ mukhyatamastapasvī saṃśitavrataḥ
|
āsīnmaharṣiḥ śrutavānmandapāla iti śrutaḥ||5||

6sa mārgamāśrito rājannṛṣīṇāmūrdhvaretasām
|
svādhyāyavāndharmaratastapasvī vijitendriyaḥ||6||

7sa gatvā tapasaḥ pāraṃ dehamutsṛjya bhārata
|
jagāma pitṛlokāya na lebhe tatra tatphalam||7||

8sa lokānaphalāndṛṣṭvā tapasā nirjitānapi
|
papraccha dharmarājasya samīpasthāndivaukasaḥ||8||

9mandapāla uvāca|
kimarthamāvṛtā lokā mamaite tapasā'rjitāḥ
|
kiṃ mayā na kṛtaṃ tatra yasyaitatkarmaṇaḥ phalam||9||

10tatrāhaṃ tatkariṣyāmi yadarthamidamāvṛtam
|
phalametasya tapasaḥ kathayadhvaṃ divaukasaḥ||10||

11devā ūcuḥ|
ṛṇino mānavā brahmañjāyante yena tacchṛṇu
|
kriyābhirbrahmacaryeṇa prajayā ca na saṃśayaḥ||11||

12tadapākriyate sarvaṃ yajñena tapasā śrutaiḥ
|
tapasvī yajñakṛccāsi na ca te vidyate prajā||12||

13ta ime prasavasyārthe tava lokāḥ samāvṛtāḥ
|
prajāyasva tato lokānupabhokṣyasi puṣkalān||13||

14punnāmno narakātputrastrāyate pitaraṃ śrutiḥ
|
tasmādapatyasantāne yatasva brahmasattama||14||

15vaiśaṃpāyana uvāca|
tacchrutvā mandapālastu vacasteṣāṃ divaukasām
|
kva nu śīghramapatyaṃ syādbahulaṃ cetyacintayat||15||

16sa cintayannabhyagacchatsubahuprasavānkhagān
|
śārṅgikāṃ śārṅgako bhūtvā jaritāṃ samupeyivān||16||

17tasyāṃ putrānajanayaccaturo brahmavādinaḥ
|
tānapāsya sa tatraiva jagāma lapitāṃ prati||17||

18bālānsa tānaṇḍagatānsaha mātrā munirvane
|
tasmingate mahābhāge lapitāṃ prati bhārata||18||

19apatyasnehasaṃyuktā jaritā bahvacintayat
|
tena tyaktā na saṃtyājyānṛṣīnaṇḍagatānvane||19||

20na jahau putraśokārtā jaritā khāṇḍave sutān
|
babhāra caitānsaṃjātānsvavṛttyā snehaviplavā||20||

21tato'gniṃ khāṇḍavaṃ dagdhumāyāntaṃ dṛṣṭavānṛṣiḥ
|
mandapālaścaraṃstasminvane lapitayā saha||21||

22taṃ saṃkalpaṃ viditvāgnerjñātvā putrāṃśca bālakān
|
so'bhituṣṭāva viprarṣibrārhmaṇo jātavedasam||22||

23putrānprativadanbhīto lokapālaṃ mahaujasam
|
mandapāla uvāca|
tvamagne sarvalokānāṃ mukhaṃ tvamasi havyavāṭ||23||

24tvamantaḥ sarvabhūtānāṃ gūḍhaścarasi pāvaka
|
tvāmekamāhuḥ kavayastvāmāhustrividhaṃ punaḥ||24||

25tvāmaṣṭadhā kalpayitvā yajñavāhamakalpayan
|
tvayā viśvamidaṃ sṛṣṭaṃ vadanti paramarṣayaḥ||25||

26tvadṛte hi jagatkṛtsnaṃ sadyo naśyeddhutāśana
|
tubhyaṃ kṛtvā namo viprāḥ svakarmavijitāṃ gatim||26||

27gacchanti saha patnībhiḥ sutairapi ca śāśvatīm
|
tvāmagne jaladānāhuḥ khe viṣaktānsavidyutaḥ||27||

28dahanti sarvabhūtāni tvatto niṣkramya hetayaḥ
|
jātavedastvayaivedaṃ viśvaṃ sṛṣṭaṃ mahādyute||28||

29tavaiva karmavihitaṃ bhūtaṃ sarvaṃ carācaram
|
tvayāpo vihitāḥ pūrvaṃ tvayi sarvamidaṃ jagat||29||

30tvayi havyaṃ ca kavyaṃ ca yathāvatsaṃpratiṣṭhitam
|
tvameva dahano deva tvaṃ dhātā tvaṃ bṛhaspatiḥ||30||

31tvamaśvinau yamau mitraḥ somastvamasi cānilaḥ
|
vaiśaṃpāyana uvāca|
evaṃ stutastadā tena mandapālena pāvakaḥ||31||

32tutoṣa tasya nṛpate muneramitatejasaḥ
|
uvāca cainaṃ prītātmā kimiṣṭaṃ karavāṇi te||32||

33tamabravīnmandapālaḥ prāñjalirhavyavāhanam
|
pradahankhāṇḍavaṃ dāvaṃ mama putrānvisarjaya||33||

34tatheti tatpratiśrutya bhagavānhavyavāhanaḥ
|
khāṇḍave tena kāle na prajajvāla didakṣayā||34||
