Mahābhārata 6.110 (Bhīṣmaparvan)

1sañjaya uvāca|arjunastu raṇe rājan dṛṣṭvā bhīṣmasya vikramam
|
śikhaṇḍinamathovāca samabhyehi pitāmaham ||1||

2na cāpi bhīstvayā kāryā bhīṣmādadya kathañcana
|
ahamenaṃ śaraistīkṣṇaiḥ pātayiṣye rathottamāt ||2||

3evamuktastu pārthena śikhaṇḍī bharatarṣabha
|
abhyadravata gāṅgeyaṃ śrutvā pārthasya bhāṣitam ||3||

4dhṛṣṭadyumnastathā rājan saubhadraśca mahārathaḥ
|
hṛṣṭāvādravatāṃ bhīṣmaṃ śrutvā pārthasya bhāṣitam ||4||

5virāṭadrupadau vṛddhau kuntibhojaśca daṃśitaḥ
|
abhyadravata gāṅgeyaṃ putrasya tava paśyataḥ ||5||

6nakulaḥ sahadevaśca dharmarājaśca vīryavān
|
tathetarāṇi sainyāni sarvāṇyeva viśāṃpate ||6||

7samādravanta gāṅgeyaṃ śrutvā pārthasya bhāṣitam
|
pratyudya-stāvakāśca sametāṃstānmahārathān ||7||

8yathāśakti yathotsāhaṃ tanme nigadataḥ śṛṇu
|
citraseno mahārāja cekitānaṃ samabhyayāt ||8||

9bhīṣmaprepsuṃ raṇe yāntaṃ vṛṣaṃ vyāghraśiśuryathā
|
dhṛṣṭadyumnaṃ mahārāja bhīṣmāntikasupāgatam ||9||

10tvaramāṇaṃ raṇe yattaṃ kṛtavarmā nyavārayat
|
bhīmasenaṃ susaṃkruddhaṃ gāṅgeyasya vadhaiṣiṇam ||10||

11tvaramāṇo mahārāja saumadattirnyavārayat
|
tathaiva nakulaṃ śūraṃ kirantaṃ sāyakān bahūn ||11||

12vikarṇo vārayāmāsa icchan bhīṣmasya jīvitam
|
sahadevaṃ tathā rājan yāntaṃ bhīṣmarathaṃ prati ||12||

13vārayāmāsa saṃkruddhaḥ kṛpaḥ śāradvato yudhi
|
rākṣasaṃ krūrakarmāṇaṃ bhaimaseniṃ mahābalam ||13||

14bhīṣmasya nidhanaṃ prepsuṃ durmukho'bhyadravadbalī
|
sātyakiṃ samare yāntaṃ tava putro nyavārayat ||14||

15abhimanyuṃ mahārāja yāntaṃ bhīṣmarathaṃ prati
|
sudakṣiṇo mahārāja kāmbojaḥ pratyavārayat ||15||

16virāṭadrupadau vṛddhau sametāvarimardanau
|
aśvatthāmā tataḥ kruddho vārayāmāsa bhārata ||16||

17tathā pāṇḍusutaṃ jyeṣṭhaṃ bhīṣmasya vadhakāṅkṣiṇam
|
bhāradvājo raṇe yatto dharmaputramavārayat ||17||

18arjunaṃ rabhasaṃ yuddhe puraskṛtya śikhaṇḍinam
|
bhīṣmaprepsuṃ mahārāja bhāsayantaṃ diśo daśa ||18||

19duḥśāsano maheṣvāso vārayāmāsa saṃyuge
|
anye ca tāvakā yodhāḥ pāṇḍavānāṃ mahārathān ||19||

20bhīṣmasyābhimukhānyātānvārayāmāsurāhave
|
dhṛṣṭadyumnastu sainyāni prākrośaṃstu punaḥ punaḥ ||20||

21abhyadravata saṃrabdho bhīṣmamekaṃ mahārathaḥ
|
eṣo'rjuno raṇe bhīṣmaṃ prayāti kurunandana ||21||

22abhyadravata mābhaiṣṭa bhīṣmo hi prāpsyate na vaḥ
|
arjunaṃ samare yoddhuṃ notsahetāpi vāsavaḥ ||22||

23kimu bhīṣmo raṇe vīrā gatasattvo'lpajīvitaḥ
|
iti senāpateḥ śrutvā pāṇḍavānāṃ mahārathāḥ ||23||

24abhyadravanta saṃhṛṣṭā gāṅgeyasya rathaṃ prati
|
āgacchamānān samare vāryoghān pralayāniva ||24||

25avārayanta saṃhṛṣṭāstāvakāḥ puruṣarṣabhāḥ
|
duḥśāsano mahārāja bhayaṃ tyaktvā mahārathaḥ ||25||

26bhīṣmasya jīvitākāṅkṣī dhanañjayamupādravat
|
tathaiva pāṇḍavāḥ śūrā gāṅgeyasya rathaṃ prati ||26||

27abhyadravanta saṅgrāme tava putrān mahārathāḥ
|
tatrādbhutamapaśyāma citrarūpaṃ viśāṃpate ||27||

28duḥśāsanarathaṃ prāpya yat pārtho nātyavartata
|
yathā vārayate velā kṣubdhatoyaṃ mahārṇavam ||28||

29tathaiva pāṇḍavaṃ kruddhaṃ tava putro nyavārayat
|
ubhau tau rathināṃ śreṣṭhāvubhau bhārata durjayau ||29||

30ubhau candrārkasadṛśau kāntyā dīptyā ca bhārata
|
tathā tau jātasarambhāvanyonyavadhakāṅkṣiṇau ||30||

31samīyaturmahāsaṃkhye mayaśakrau yathā purā
|
duḥśāsano mahārāja pāṇḍavaṃ viśikhaistribhiḥ ||31||

32vāsudevaṃ ca viṃśatyā tāḍayāmāsa saṃyuge
|
tato'rjuno jātamanyurvārṣṇeyaṃ vīkṣya pīḍitam ||32||

33duḥśāsanaṃ śatenājau nārācānāṃ samārpayat
|
te tasya kavacaṃ bhittvā papuḥ śoṇitamāhave ||33||

34duḥśāsanastribhiḥ kruddhaḥ pārthaṃ vivyādha patribhiḥ
|
lalāṭe bharataśreṣṭha śaraiḥ sannataparvabhiḥ ||34||

35lalāṭasthaistu tairbāṇaiḥ śuśubhe pāṇḍavo raṇe
|
yathā merurmahārāja śṛṅgairatyarthamucchritaiḥ ||35||

36so'tividdho maheṣvāsaḥ putreṇa tava dhanvinā
|
vyarājata raṇe pārthaḥ kiṃśukaḥ puṣpavāniva ||36||

37duḥśāsanaṃ tataḥ kuddhaḥ pīḍayāmāsa pāṇḍavaḥ
|
parvaṇīva susaṃkruddho rāhuḥ pūrṇaṃ niśākaram ||37||

38pīḍyamāno balavatā purastava viśāṃpate
|
vivyādha samare pārthaṃ kaṅkapatraiḥ śilāśitaiḥ ||38||

39tasya pārtho dhanuśchittvā rathaṃ cāsya tribhiḥśaraiḥ
|
ājaghāna tataḥ paścāt putraṃ te niśitaiḥ śaraiḥ ||39||

40so'nyat kārmukamādāya bhīṣmasya pramukhe sthitaḥ
|
arjunaṃ pañcaviṃśatyā bāhvorurasi cārpayat ||40||

41tasya kuddho mahārāja pāṇḍavaḥ śatrutāpanaḥ
|
apraiṣīdviśikhānghorānyamadaṇḍopamānbahūn ||41||

42aprāptāneva tānbāṇāṃściccheda tanayastava
|
yatamānasya pārthasya tadadbhutamivābhavat ||42||

43pārthaṃ ca niśitairbāṇairavidhyat tanayastava
|
tataḥ kruddho raṇe pārthaḥ śarān saṃdhāya kārmuke ||43||

44preṣayāmāsa samare svarṇapuṅkhāñchilāśitān
|
nyamajjaṃste mahārāja tasya kāye mahātmanaḥ ||44||

45yathā haṃsā mahārāja taḍāgaṃ prāpya bhārata
|
pīḍitaścaiva putraste pāṇḍavena mahātmanā ||45||

46hitvā pārthaṃ raṇe tūrṇaṃ bhīṣmasya rathamāvrajat
|
agādhe majjatastasya dvīpo bhīṣmo'bhavattadā ||46||

47pratilabhya tataḥ saṃjñāṃ putrastava viśāṃpate
|
avārayattataḥ śūro bhūya eva parākramī ||47||

48śaraiḥ suniśitaiḥ pārthaṃ yathā vṛtraṃ purandaraḥ
|
nirbibheda mahākāyo vivyathe naiva cārjunaḥ ||48||
