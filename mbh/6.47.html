Mahābhārata 6.47 (Bhīṣmaparvan)

1sañjaya uvāca|gatapūrvāhṇabhūyiṣṭhe tasminnahani dāruṇe
|
vartamāne tathā raudre mahāvīravarakṣaye ||1||

2durmukhaḥ kṛtavarmā ca kṛpaḥ śalyo viviṃśatiḥ
|
bhīṣmaṃ jugupurāsādya tava putreṇa coditāḥ ||2||

3etairatirathairguptaḥ pañcabhirbharatarṣabhaḥ
|
pāṇḍavānāmanīkāni vijagāhe mahārathaḥ ||3||

4cedikāśikarūṣeṣu pañcāleṣu ca bhārata
|
bhīṣmasya bahudhā tālaścalatketuradṛśyata ||4||

5sa śirāṃsi raṇe'rīṇāṃ rathāṃśca sayugadhvajān
|
nicakarta mahāvegairbhallaiḥ sannataparvabhiḥ ||5||

6nṛtyato rathamārgeṣu bhīṣmasya bharatarṣabha
|
bhṛśamārtasvaraṃ cakrurnāgā marmaṇi tāḍitāḥ ||6||

7abhimanyuḥ susaṃkuddhaḥ piśaṅgaisturagottamaiḥ
|
saṃyuktaṃ rathamāsthāya prāyādbhīṣmarathaṃ prati ||7||

8jāmbūnadavicitreṇa karṇikāreṇa ketunā
|
abhyavartata bhīṣmaṃ ca tāṃścaiva rathasattamān ||8||

9sa tālaketostīkṣṇena ketumāhatya patriṇā
|
bhīṣmeṇa yuyudhe vīrastasya cānurathaiḥ saha ||9||

10kṛtavarmāṇamekena śalyaṃ pañcabhirāśugaiḥ
|
viddhvā navabhirānarcchacchitāgraiḥ prapitāmaham ||10||

11pūrṇāyatavisṛṣṭena samyak praṇihitena ca
|
dhvajamekena vivyādha jāmbūnadapariṣkṛtam ||11||

12durmukhasya tu bhallena sarvāvaraṇabhedinā
|
jahāra sāratheḥ kāyācchiraḥ sannataparvaṇā ||12||

13dhanuściccheda bhallena kārtasvaravibhūṣitam
|
kṛpasya niśitāgreṇa tāṃśca tīkṣṇamukhaiḥ śaraiḥ ||13||

14jaghāna paramakruddho nṛtyanniva mahārathaḥ
|
tasya lāghavamudvīkṣya tutuṣurdevatā api ||14||

15labdhalakṣatayā kārṣṇeḥ sarve bhīṣmamukhā rathāḥ
|
sattvavantamamanyanta sākṣādiva dhanañjayam ||15||

16tasya lāghavamārgasthamalātasadṛśaprabham
|
diśaḥ paryapataccāpaṃ gāṇḍīvamiva ghoṣavat ||16||

17tamāsādya mahāvegairbhīṣmo navabhirāśugaiḥ
|
vivyādha samare tūrṇamārjuniṃ paravīrahā ||17||

18dhvajaṃ cāsya tribhirbhalaiściccheda paramaujasaḥ
|
sārathiṃ ca tribhirbāṇairājaghāna yatavrataḥ ||18||

19tathaiva kṛtavarmā ca kṛpaḥ śalyaśca māriṣaḥ
|
viddhvā nākampayatkārṣṇiṃ mainākamiva parvatam ||19||

20sa taiḥ parivṛtaḥ śūro dhārtarāṣṭrairmahārathaiḥ
|
vavarṣa śaravarṣāṇi kārṣṇiḥ pañcarathān prati ||20||

21tatasteṣāṃ sahasrāṇi saṃvārya śaravṛṣṭibhiḥ
|
nanāda balavān kārṣṇirbhīṣmāya visṛjan śarān ||21||

22tatrāsya sumahadrājan bāhvorbalamadṛśyata
|
yatamānasya samare bhīṣmamardayataḥ śaraiḥ ||22||

23parākrāntasya tasyaiva bhīṣmo'pi prāhiṇoccharān
|
sa tāṃściccheda samare bhīṣmacāpacyutān śarān ||23||

24tato dhvajamamogheṣurbhīṣmasya navabhiḥ śaraiḥ
|
ciccheda samare vīrastata uccukruśurjanāḥ ||24||

25sa rājato mahāskandhastālo hemavibhūṣitaḥ
|
saubhadraviśikhaiśchinnaḥ papāta bhuvi bhārata ||25||

26taṃ tu saubhadraviśikhaiḥ pātitaṃ bharatarṣabha
|
dṛṣṭvā bhīmo nanādoccaiḥ saubhadramabhiharṣayan ||26||

27atha bhīṣmo mahāstrāṇi divyāni subahūni ca
|
prāduścakre mahāraudre raṇe tasmin mahābalaḥ ||27||

28tataḥ śarasahasreṇa saubhadraṃ prapitāmahaḥ
|
avākiradameyātmā tada‌dbhutamivābhavat ||28||

29tato daśa maheṣvāsāḥ pāṇḍavānāṃ mahārathāḥ
|
rakṣārthamabhyadhāvanta saubhadraṃ tvaritā rathaiḥ ||29||

30virāṭaḥ saha putreṇa dhṛṣṭadyumnaśca pārṣataḥ
|
bhīmaśca kekayāścaiva sātyakiśca viśāṃpate ||30||

31teṣāṃ javenāpatatāṃ bhīṣmaḥ śāntanavo raṇe
|
pāñcālyaṃ tribhirānarcchatsātyakiṃ navabhiḥ śaraiḥ ||31||

32pūrṇāyatavisṛṣṭena kṣureṇa niśitena ca
|
dhvajamekena ciccheda bhīmasenasya patriṇā ||32||

33jāmbūnadamayaḥ śrīmān kesarī sa narottama
|
papāta bhīmasenasya bhīṣmeṇa mathito rathāt ||33||

34tato bhīmastribhirviddhvā bhīṣmaṃ śāntanavaṃ raṇe
|
kṛpamekena vivyādha kṛtavarmāṇamaṣṭabhiḥ ||34||

35pragṛhītāgrahastena vairāṭirapi dantinā
|
abhyadravata rājānaṃ madrādhipatimuttaraḥ ||35||

36tasya vāraṇarājasya javenāpatato rathe
|
śalyo nivārayāmāsa vegamapratimaṃ śaraiḥ ||36||

37tasya kruddhaḥ sa nāgendro bṛhataḥ sādhuvāhinaḥ
|
padā yugamadhiṣṭhāya jaghāna caturo hayān ||37||

38sa hatāśve rathe tiṣṭhan madrādhipatirāyasīm
|
uttarāntakarīṃ śaktiṃ cikṣepa bhujagopamām ||38||

39tayā bhinnatanutrāṇaḥ praviśya vipulaṃ tamaḥ
|
sa papāta gajaskandhātpramuktāṅkuśatomaraḥ ||39||

40asimādāya śalyo'pi avaplutya rathottamāt
|
tasya vāraṇarājasya cicchedātha mahākaram ||40||

41bhinnamarmā śaraśataiśchinnahastaḥ sa vāraṇaḥ
|
bhīmamārtasvaraṃ kṛtvā papāta ca mamāra ca ||41||

42etadīdṛśakaṃ kṛtvā madrarājo narādhipa
|
āruroha rathaṃ tūrṇaṃ bhāsvaraṃ kṛtavarmaṇaḥ ||42||

43uttaraṃ vai hataṃ dṛṣṭvā vairāṭirbhrātaraṃ tadā
|
kṛtavarmaṇā ca sahitaṃ dṛṣṭvā śalyamavasthitam ||43||

44śvetaḥ krodhāt prajajvāla haviṣā havyavāḍiva
|
sa visphārya mahaccāpaṃ śakracāpopamaṃ balī ||44||

45abhyadhāvajjighāṃsan vai śalyaṃ madrādhipaṃ balī
|
mahatā rathavaṃśena samantāt parivāritaḥ ||45||

46muñcan bāṇamayaṃ varṣaṃ prāyācchalyarathaṃ prati
|
tamāpatantaṃ saṃprekṣya mattavāraṇavikramam ||46||

47tāvakānāṃ rathāḥ sapta samantāt paryavārayan
|
madrarājamabhīpsanto mṛtyordaṃṣṭrāntaraṃ gatam ||47||

48bṛhadbalaśca kausalyo jayatsenaśca māgadhaḥ
|
tathā rukmaratho rājan śalyaputraḥ pratāpavān ||48||

49vindānuvindāvāvantyau kāṃbojaśca sudakṣiṇaḥ
|
bṛhatkṣatrasyaḥ dāyādaḥ saindhavaśca jayadrathaḥ ||49||

50nānāvarṇavicitrāṇi dhanūṃṣi ca mahātmanām
|
visphāritāni dṛśyante toyadeṣviva vidyutaḥ ||50||

51te tu bāṇamayaṃ varṣaṃ śvetamūrdhanyapātayan
|
nidāghānte'niloddhūtā meghā iva nage jalam ||51||

52tataḥ kruddho maheṣvāsaḥ saptabhallaiḥ sutejanaiḥ
|
dhanūṃṣi teṣāmācchidya mamarda pṛtanāpatiḥ ||52||

53nikṛtānyeva tāni sma samadṛśyanta bhārata
|
tataste tu nimeṣārdhāt pratyapadyan dhanūṃṣi ca ||53||

54sapta caiva pṛṣatkāṃśca śvetasyoparyapātayan
|
tataḥ punarameyātmā bhallaiḥ saptabhirāśugaiḥ
|
nicakarta mahābāhusteṣāṃ cāpāni dhanvinām ||54||

55te nikṛttamahācāpāstvaramāṇā mahārathāḥ
|
rathaśaktīḥ parāmṛśya vinedurbhairavān ravān ||55||

56anvayurbharataśreṣṭha sapta śvetarathaṃ prati
|
tatastā jvalitāḥ sapta mahendrāśaniniḥsvanāḥ ||56||

57aprāptāḥ satabhirbhalaiściccheda paramāstravit
|
tataḥ samādāya śaraṃ sarvakāyavidāraṇam ||57||

58prāhiṇodbharataśreṣṭha śveto rukmarathaṃ prati
|
tasya dehe nipatito bāṇo vajrātigo mahān ||58||

59tato rukmaratho rājan sāyakena dṛḍhāhataḥ
|
niṣasāda rathopasthe kaśmalaṃ cāviśanmahat ||59||

60taṃ visaṃjñaṃ vimanasaṃ tvaramāṇastu sārathiḥ
|
apovāha na saṃbhrāntaḥ sarvalokasya paśyataḥ||60||

61tato'nyān ṣaṭ samādāya śveto hemavibhūṣitān
|
teṣāṃ ṣaṇṇāṃ mahābāhurdhvajaśīrṣāṇyapātayat ||61||

62hayāṃśca teṣāṃ nirbhidya sārathīṃśca parantapa
|
śaraiścaitān samākīrya prāyācchalyarathaṃ prati ||62||

63tato halahalāśabdastava sainyeṣu bhārata
|
dṛṣṭvā senāpatiṃ tūrṇaṃ yāntaṃ śalyarathaṃ prati ||63||

64tato bhīṣmaṃ puraskṛtya tatra putro mahābalaḥ
|
vṛtastu sarvasainyena prāyācchvetarathaṃ prati ||64||

65mṛtyorāsyamanuprāptaṃ madrarājamamocayat
|
tato yuddhaṃ samabhavattumulaṃ lomaharṣaṇam ||65||

66tāvakānāṃ pareṣāṃ ca vyatiṣaktarathadvipam
|
saubhadre bhīmasene ca sātyakau ca mahārathe ||66||

67kaikeye ca virāṭe ca dhṛṣṭadyumne ca pārṣate
|
eteṣu narasiṃheṣu cedimatsyeṣu caiva ha
|
vavarṣa śaravarṣāṇi kuruvṛddhaḥ pitāmahaḥ ||67||
