Mahābhārata 12.240 (Śāntiparvan)

1vyāsa uvāca |
pṛcchatastava satputra yathāvadiha sattvataḥ
|
sāṃkhyajñānena saṃyuktaṃ yadetatkīrtitaṃ mayā ||1||

2yogakṛtyaṃ tu te kṛtsnaṃ vartayiṣyāmi tacchṛṇu
|
ekatvaṃ buddhimanasorindriyāṇāṃ ca sarvaśaḥ ||2||

3ātmano vyāpinastāta jñānametadanuttamam
|
tadetadupaśāntena dāntenādhyātmaśīlinā ||3||

4ātmārāmeṇa buddhena boddhavyaṃ śucikarmaṇā
|
yogadoṣānsamucchidya pañca yānkavayo viduḥ ||4||

5kāmaṃ krodhaṃ ca lobhaṃ ca bhayaṃ svapnaṃ ca pañcamam
|
krodhaṃ śamena jayati kāmaṃ saṃkalpavarjanāt ||5||

6sattvasaṃsevanāddhīro nidrāmucchettumarhati
|
dhṛtyā śiśnodaraṃ rakṣetpāṇipādaṃ ca cakṣuṣā ||6||

7cakṣuḥśrotre ca manasā manovācaṃ ca karmaṇā
|
apramādādbhayaṃ jahyāddambhaṃ prājñopasevanāt ||7||

8evametān yogadoṣān jayennityamatandritaḥ
|
agnīṃśca brāhmaṇāṃścārceddevatāḥ praṇameta ca ||8||

9varjayeduśatīṃ vācaṃ hiṃsāyuktāṃ manonudām
|
brahma tejomayaṃ śukraṃ yasya sarvamidaṃ rasaḥ ||9||

10etasya bhūtaṃ bhavyasya dṛṣṭaṃ sthāvarajaṅgamam
|
dhyānamadhyayanaṃ dānaṃ satyaṃ hrīrārjavaṃ kṣamā ||10||

11śaucamācārasaṃśuddhirindriyāṇāṃ ca nigrahaḥ
|
etairvivardhate tejaḥ pāpmānaṃ cāpakarṣati ||11||

12siddhyanti cāsya sarvārthā vijñānaṃ ca pravartate
|
samaḥ sarveṣu bhūteṣu labdhālabdhena vartayan ||12||

13dhūtapāpmā tu tejasvī laghvāhāro jitendriyaḥ
|
kāmakrodhau vaśe kṛtvā ninīṣedbrahmaṇaḥ padam ||13||

14manasaścendriyāṇāṃ ca kṛtvaikāmyaṃ samāhitaḥ
|
pūrvarātrāparārdhaṃ ca dhārayenmana ātmani ||14||

15jantoḥ pañcendriyasyāsya yadekaṃ chidramindriyam
|
tato'sya sravate prajñā dṛteḥ pādādivodakam ||15||

16manastu pūrvamādadyāt kumīnamiva matsyahā
|
tataḥ śrotraṃ tataścakṣurjihvāṃ ghrāṇaṃ ca yogavit ||16||

17tata etāni saṃyamya manasi sthāpayedyatiḥ
|
tathaivāpohya saṃkalpānmano hyātmani dhārayet ||17||

18pañcendriyāṇi sandhāya manasi sthāpayedyatiḥ
|
yadaitānyavatiṣṭhanti manaḥṣaṣṭhānyathātmani ||18||

19prasīdanti ca saṃsthāya tadā brahma prakāśate
|
vidhūma iva dīptārcirāditya iva dīptimān ||19||

20vaidyuto'gnirivākāśe dṛśyate''tmā tathā''tmani
|
sarvastatra sa sarvatra vyāpakatvācca dṛśyate ||20||

21taṃ paśyanti mahātmāno brāhmaṇā ye manīṣiṇaḥ
|
dhṛtimanto mahāprājñāḥ sarvabhūtahite ratāḥ ||21||

22evaṃ parimitaṃ kālamācaransaṃśitavrataḥ
|
āsīno hi rahasyeko gacchedakṣarasātmatām ||22||

23pramoho bhrama āvarto prāṇaṃ śravaṇadarśane
|
adbhutāni rasasparśe śītoṣṇe mārutākṛtiḥ ||23||

24pratibhāmupasargāṃścāpyupasaṃgṛhya yogataḥ
|
tāṃstattvavidanādṛtya ātmanyeva nivartayet ||24||

25kuryātparicayaṃ yoge traikālye niyato muniḥ
|
giriśṛṅge tathā caitye vṛkṣāgreṣu ca yojayet ||25||

26saṃniyamyendriyagrāmaṃ koṣṭhe bhāṇḍamanā iva
|
ekāgraṃ cintayennityaṃ yogānnodvejayenmanaḥ ||26||

27yenopāyena śakyeta saṃniyantuṃ calaṃ manaḥ
|
taṃ ca yukto niṣeveta na caiva vicalettataḥ ||27||

28śūnyā giriguhāścaiva devatāyatanāni ca
|
śūnyāgārāṇi caikāgro nivāsārthamupakramet ||28||

29nābhiṣvajetparaṃ vācā karmaṇā manasā'pi vā
|
upekṣako yatāhāro labdhālabdhe samo bhavet ||29||

30yaścainamabhinandeta yaścainamapavādayet
|
samastayoścāpyubhayornābhidhyāyecchubhāśubham ||30||

31na prahṛṣyeta lābheṣu nālābheṣu ca cintayet
|
samaḥ sarveṣu bhūteṣu sadharmā mātariśvanaḥ ||31||

32evaṃ svasthātmanaḥ sādhoḥ sarvatra samadarśinaḥ
|
ṣaṇmāsānnityayuktasya śabdabrahmātivartate ||32||

33vedanārtāḥ prajñā dṛṣṭvā samaloṣṭhāśmakāñcanaḥ
|
etasminvirato mārge viramenna ca mohitaḥ ||33||

34api varṇāvakṛṣṭastu nārī vā dharmakāṅkṣiṇī
|
tāvapyetena mārgeṇa gacchetāṃ paramāṃ gatim ||34||

35ajaṃ purāṇamajaraṃ sanātanaṃ yadindriyairupalabheta niścalaiḥ
|
aṇoraṇīyo mahato mahattaraṃ tadā''tmanā paśyati muktamātmavān ||35||

36idaṃ maharṣervacanaṃ mahātmano yathāvaduktaṃ manasā'nudṛśya ca
|
avekṣya cemāṃ parameṣṭhisāmyatāṃ prayānti cābhūtagatiṃ manīṣiṇaḥ ||36||
