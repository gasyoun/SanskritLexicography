Mahābhārata 7.167 (Droṇaparvan)

1sañjaya uvāca|
sahadevamathāyāntaṃ droṇaprepsuṃ viśāṃpate
|
karṇo vaikartano yuddhe vārayāmāsa bhārata||1||

2sahadevastu rādheyaṃ viddhvā navabhirāśugaiḥ
|
punarvivyādha daśabhirviśikhairnataparvabhiḥ||2||

3taṃ karṇaḥ prativivyādha śatena nataparvaṇām
|
sajyaṃ cāsya dhanuḥ śīghraṃ ciccheda laghuhastavat||3||

4tato'nyaddhanurādāya mādrīputraḥ pratāpavān
|
karṇaṃ vivyādha viṃśatyā tada‌dbhutamivābhavat||4||

5tasya karṇo hayānhatvā śaraiḥ sannataparvabhiḥ
|
sārathiṃ cāsya bhallena drutaṃ ninye yamakṣayam||5||

6virathaḥ sahadevastu khaḍgaṃ carma samādade
|
tadapyasya śaraiḥ karṇo vyadhamatprahasanniva||6||

7atha gurvīṃ mahādhorāṃ hemacitrāṃ mahāgadām
|
preṣayāmāsa saṃkruddho vaikartanarathaṃ prati||7||

8tāmāpatantīṃ sahasā sahadevapracoditām
|
vyaṣṭaṃbhayaccharaiḥ karṇo bhūmau caināmapātayat||8||

9gadāṃ vinihatāṃ dṛṣṭvā sahadevastvarānvitaḥ
|
śaktiṃ cikṣepa karṇāya tāmapyasyācchinaccharaiḥ||9||

10sasaṃbhramaṃ tatastūrṇamavaplutya rathottamāt
|
sahadevo mahārāja dṛṣṭvā karṇaṃ vyavasthitam||10||

11rathacakraṃ pragṛhyājau mumocādhirathaṃ prati
|
tadā'patadvai sahasā kālacakramivodyatam||11||

12śarairanekasāhastrairācchinatsūtanandanaḥ
|
tasmiṃstu nihate cakre sūtajena mahātmanā||12||

13īṣādaṇḍakayokrāṃśca yugāni vividhāni ca
|
hastyaṃgāni tathāśvāṃśca mṛtāṃśca puruṣānbahūn||13||

14cikṣepa karṇamuddiśya karṇastānvyadhamaccharaiḥ
|
sa nirāyudhamātmānaṃ jñātvā mādravatīsutaḥ||14||

15vāryamāṇastu viśikhaiḥ sahadevo raṇaṃ jahau
|
tamabhidrutya rādheyo muhūrtādbharatarṣabha||15||

16abravītprahasanvākyaṃ sahadevaṃ viśāṃpate
|
mā yudhyasva raṇe'dhīra viśiṣṭai rathibhiḥ saha||16||

17sadṛśairyudhya mādreya vaco me mā viśaṃkithāḥ
|
athainaṃ dhanuṣo'greṇa tudanbhūyo'bravīdvacaḥ||17||

18eṣo'rjuno raṇe tūrṇaṃ yudhyate kurubhiḥ saha
|
tatra gacchasva mādreya gṛhaṃ vā yadi manyase||18||

19evamuktvā tu taṃ karṇo rathena rathināṃ varaḥ
|
prāyātpāñcālapāṇḍūnāṃ sainyāni pradahanniva||19||

20vadhaṃ prāptaṃ tu mādreyaṃ nāvadhītsamare'rihā
|
kuntyāḥ smṛtvā vaco rājan satyasandho mahāyaśāḥ||20||

21sahadevastato rājanvimanāḥ śarapīḍitaḥ
|
karṇavākśarataptaśca jīvitānniravidyata||21||

22āruroha rathaṃ cāpi pāñcālyasya mahātmanaḥ
|
janamejayasya samare tvarāyukto mahārathaḥ||22||

23virāṭaṃ sahasenaṃ tu droṇaṃ vai drutamāgatam
|
madrarājaḥ śaraugheṇa cchādayāmāsa dhanvinam||23||

24tayoḥ samabhavadyuddhaṃ samare dṛḍhadhanvinoḥ
|
yādṛśaṃ hyabhavadrājan jaṃbhavāsavayoḥ purā||24||

25madrarājo mahārāja virāṭaṃ vāhinīpatim
|
ājaghne tvaritastūrṇaṃ śatena nataparvaṇām||25||

26prativivyādha taṃ rājannavabhirniśitaiḥ śaraiḥ
|
punaścainaṃ trisaptatyā bhūyaścaiva śatena tu||26||

27tasya madrādhipo hatvā caturo rathavājinaḥ
|
sutaṃ dhvajaṃ ca samare śarābhyāṃ saṃnyapātayat||27||

28hatāśvāttu rathāttūrṇamavaplutya mahārathaḥ
|
tasthau visphārayaṃścāpaṃ vimuñcaṃśca śitāñcharān||28||

29śatānīkastato dṛṣṭvā bhrātaraṃ hatavāhanam
|
rathenābhyapatattūrṇaṃ sarvalokasya paśyataḥ||29||

30śatānīkamathāyāntaṃ madrarājo mahāmṛdhe
|
viśikhairbahubhirviddhvā tato ninye yamakṣayam||30||

31tasmiṃstu nihate vīre virāṭo rathasattamaḥ
|
āruroha rathaṃ tūrṇaṃ tameva dhvajamālinam||31||

32tato visphārya nayane krodhāddviguṇavikramaḥ
|
madrarājarathaṃ tūrṇaṃ chā‌yāmāsa patribhiḥ||32||

33tato madrādhipaḥ kruddhaḥ śareṇānataparvaṇā
|
ājaghānorasi dṛḍhaṃ virāṭaṃ vāhinīpatim||33||

34so'tividdho mahārāja rathopastha upāviśat
|
kaśmalaṃ cāviśattīvraṃ virāṭo bharatarṣabha||34||

35sārathistamapovāha samare śaravikṣatam
|
tataḥ sā mahatī senā prādravanniśi bhārata||35||

36vadhyamānā śaraśataiḥ śalyenāhavaśobhinā
|
tāṃ dṛṣṭvā vidrutāṃ senāṃ vāsudevadhanañjayau||36||

37prayātau tatra rājendra yatra śalyo vyavasthitaḥ
|
tau tu pratyudyayau rājan rākṣasendro hyalaṃbuṣaḥ||37||

38aṣṭacakrasamāyuktamāsthāya pravaraṃ ratham
|
turaṅgamamukhairyuktaṃ piśācairghoradarśanaiḥ||38||

39lohitārdrapatākaṃ taṃ raktamālyavibhūṣitam
|
kārṣṇāyasamayaṃ ghoramṛkṣacarmasamāvṛtam||39||

40raudreṇa citrapakṣeṇa vivṛtākṣeṇa kūjatā
|
dhvajenocchritadaṇḍena gṛdhrarājena rājatā||40||

41sa babhau rākṣaso rājanbhinnāñjanacayopamaḥ
|
rurodhārjunamāyāntaṃ prabhañjanamivādrirāṭra||41||

42kiranbāṇagaṇān rājañśataśo'rjunamūrdhani
|
atitīvraṃ mahadyuddhaṃ nararākṣasayostadā||42||

43draṣṭṝṇāṃ prītijananaṃ sarveṣāṃ tatra bhārata
|
gṛdhra-kāka-balolūka-kaṅka-gomāyuharṣaṇam||43||

44tamarjunaḥ śatanava patriṇāṃ samatāḍayat
|
navabhiśca śitairbāṇairdhvajaṃ ciccheda bhārata||44||

45sārathiṃ ca tribhirbāṇaistribhireva triveṇukam
|
dhanurekena ciccheda caturbhiścaturo hayān||45||

46punaḥ sajyaṃ kṛtaṃ cāpaṃ tadapyasya dvidhā'cchinat
|
virathasyodyataṃ khaḍgaṃ śareṇāsya dvidhā'karot||46||

47athainaṃ niśitairbāṇaiścaturbhirbharatarṣabha
|
pārtho'vidhyadrākṣasendraṃ sa viddhaḥ prādravadbhayāt||47||

48taṃ vijityārjunastūrṇaṃ droṇāntikamupāyayau
|
kirañśaragaṇān rājannaravāraṇavājiṣu||48||

49vadhyamānā mahārāja pāṇḍavena yaśasvinā
|
sainikā nyapatannurvyāṃ vātanunnā iva dumāḥ||49||

50teṣu tūtsādyamāneṣu phālgunana mahātmanā
|
saṃprādravadbalaṃ sarvaṃ putrāṇāṃ te viśāṃpate||50||
