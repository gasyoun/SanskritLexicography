Mahābhārata 12.220 (Śāntiparvan)

1yudhiṣṭhira uvāca|
kiṃ kurvansukhamāpnoti kiṃ kurvanduḥkhamāpnuyāt
|
kiṃ kurvannirbhayo loke siddhaścarati bhārata ||1||

2bhīṣma uvāca|
damameva praśaṃsanti vṛddhāḥ śrutisamādhayaḥ
|
sarveṣāmeva varṇānāṃ brāhmaṇasya viśeṣataḥ ||2||

3nādāntasya kriyāsiddhiryathāvadupapadyate
|
kriyā tapaśca satyaṃ ca dame sarvaṃ pratiṣṭhitam ||3||

4damastejo vardhayati pavitraṃ dama ucyate
|
vipāpmā nirbhayo dāntaḥ puruṣo vindate mahat ||4||

5sukhaṃ dāntaḥ prasvapiti sukhaṃ ca pratibuddhyate
|
sukhaṃ loke viparyeti manaścāsya prasīdati ||5||

6tejo damena dhriyate tanna tīkṣṇo'dhigacchati
|
amitrāṃśca bahūnnityaṃ pṛthagātmani paśyati ||6||

7kravyādbhya iva bhūtānamadāntebhyaḥ sadā bhayam
|
teṣāṃ vipratiṣedhārthaṃ rājā sṛṣṭaḥ svayambhuvā ||7||

8āśrameṣu ca sarveṣu dama eva viśiṣyate
|
yacca teṣu phalaṃ dharme bhūyo dānte taducyate ||8||

9teṣāṃ liṅgāni vakṣyāmi yeṣāṃ samudayo damaḥ
|
akārpaṇyamasaṃrambhaḥ saṃtoṣaḥ śraddadhānatā ||9||

10akrodha ārjavaṃ nityaṃ nātivādo'bhimānitā
|
gurupūjā'nasūyā ca dayā bhūteṣvapaiśunam ||10||

11janavādamṛṣāvādastutinindāvivarjanam
|
sādhukāmaśca spṛhayennāyatiṃ pratyayeṣu ca ||11||

12avairakṛtsūpacāraḥ samo nindāpraśaṃsayoḥ
|
suvṛttaḥ śīlasampannaḥ prasannātmātmavānprabhuḥ ||12||

13prāpya loke ca satkāraṃ svargaṃ vai pretya gacchati
|
durgamaṃ sarvabhūtānāṃ prāpayanmodate sukhī ||13||

14sarvabhūtahite yukto na sma yo dviṣate janam
|
mahāhrada ivākṣobhyaḥ prajñātṛptaḥ prasīdati ||14||

15abhayaṃ yasya bhūtebhyaḥ sarveṣāmabhayaṃ yataḥ
|
namasyaḥ sarvabhūtānāṃ dānto bhavati buddhimān ||15||

16na hṛṣyati mahatyarthe vyasane ca na śocati
|
sa vai parimitaprajñaḥ sa dānto dvija ucyate ||16||

17karmabhiḥ śrutisampannaḥ sadbhirācaritaḥ śuciḥ
|
sadaiva damasaṃyuktastasya bhuṅkte mahāphalam ||17||

18anasūyā kṣamā śāntiḥ saṃtoṣaḥ priyavāditā
|
satyaṃ dānamanāyāso naiṣa mārgo durātmanām ||18||

19kāmakrodhau ca lobhaśca parasyerṣyāvikatthanā
|
kāmakrodhau vaśe kṛtvā brahmacārī jitendriyaḥ ||19||

20vikramya ghore tapasi brāhmaṇaḥ saṃśitavrataḥ
|
kālākāṅkṣī carellokānnirapāya ivātmavān ||20||
