Mahābhārata 6.97 (Bhīṣmaparvan)

1sañjaya uvāca|
tato duryodhano rājā śakuniścāpi saubalaḥ
|
duḥśāsanaśca putraste sūtaputraśca durjayaḥ ||1||

2samāgamya mahārāja mantraṃ cakrurvivakṣitam
|
kathaṃ pāṇḍusutāḥ saṃkhye jetavyāḥ sagaṇā iti ||2||

3tato duryodhano rājā sarvāṃstānāha mantriṇaḥ
|
sūtaputraṃ samābhāṣya saubalaṃ ca mahābalam ||3||

4droṇo bhīṣmaḥ kṛpaḥ śalyaḥ saumadattiśca saṃyuge
|
na pārthān pratibādhante na jāne tacca kāraṇam ||4||

5avadhyamānāste cāpi kṣapayanti balaṃ mama
|
so'smi kṣīṇabalaḥ karṇa kṣīṇaśastraśca saṃyuge ||5||

6nikṛtaḥ pāṇḍavaiḥ śūrairavadhyairdaivatairapi
|
so'haṃ saṃśayamāpannaḥ prahariṣye kathaṃ raṇe ||6||

7tamabravīnmahārājaṃ sūtaputro narādhipam
|
karṇa uvāca|mā śoca bharataśreṣṭha kariṣye'haṃ priyaṃ tava ||7||

8bhīṣmaḥ śāntanavastūrṇamapayātu mahāraṇāt
|
nivṛtte yudhi gāṅgeye nyastaśastre ca bhārata ||8||

9ahaṃ pārthān haniṣyāmi sahitān sarvasomakaiḥ
|
paśyato yudhi bhīṣmasya śape satyena te nṛpa ||9||

10pāṇḍaveṣu dayāṃ nityaṃ sa hi bhīṣmaḥ karoti va
|
aśaktaśca raṇe bhīṣmo jetumetānmahārathān ||10||

11abhimānī raṇe bhīṣmo nityaṃ cāpi raṇapriyaḥ
|
sa kathaṃ pāṇḍavānyuddhe jeṣyate tāta saṃgatān ||11||

12satvaṃ śīghramito gatvā bhīṣmasya śibiraṃ prati
|
anumānya guruṃ vṛddhaṃ śastraṃ nyāsaya bhārata ||12||

13nyastaśastre tato bhīṣme nihatānpaśya pāṃḍavān
|
mayaikena raṇe rājan sasuhṛdgaṇabāndhavān ||13||

14evamuktastu karṇena putro duryodhanastava
|
abravīdbhrātaraṃ tatra duḥśāsanamidaṃ vacaḥ ||14||

15anuyātraṃ yathā sarvaṃ sajjībhavati sarvaśaḥ
|
duḥśāsana tathā kṣipraṃ sarvamevopapādaya ||15||

16evamuktvā tato rājan karṇamāha janeśvaraḥ
|
anumānya raṇe bhīṣmameṣo'haṃ dvipadāṃ varam ||16||

17āgamiṣye tataḥ kṣipraṃ tvatsakāśamarindama
|
apakrānte tato bhīṣme prahariṣyasi saṃyuge ||17||

18niṣpapāta tatastūrṇaṃ putrastava viśāṃpate
|
sahito bhrātṛbhistaistu devairiva śatakratuḥ ||18||

19tatastaṃ nṛpaśārdūlaṃ śārdūlasamavikramam
|
ārohayaddhayaṃ tūrṇaṃ bhrātā duḥśāsanastadā ||19||

20aṅgadī baddhamukuṭo hastābharaṇavānnṛpa
|
dhārtarāṣṭro mahārāja vibabhau sa pathi vrajan ||20||

21bhaṇḍīpuṣpanikāśena tapanīyanibhena ca
|
anuliptaḥ parārddhyena candanena sugandhinā ||21||

22arajombarasaṃvītaḥ siṃhakhelagatirnṛpa
|
śuśubhe vimalārciṣmānnabhasīva divākaraḥ ||22||

23taṃ prayāntaṃ naravyāghraṃ bhīṣmasya śibiraṃ prati
|
anujagmurmaheṣvāsāḥ sarvalokasya dhanvinaḥ ||23||

24bhrātaraśca maheṣvāsāstridaśā iva vāsavam
|
hayānanye samāruhya gajānanye ca bhārata ||24||

25rathānanye naraśreṣṭhaṃ parivavruḥ samantataḥ
|
āttaśastrāśca suhṛdo rakṣaṇārthaṃ mahīpateḥ ||25||

26prādurbabhūvuḥ sahitāḥ śakrasyevāmarā divi
|
sa pūjyamānaḥ kurubhiḥ kauravāṇāṃ mahābalaḥ ||26||

27prayayau sadanaṃ rājā gāṅgeyasya yaśasvinaḥ
|
anvīyamānaḥ satataṃ sodaraiḥ parivāritaḥ ||27||

28dakṣiṇaṃ dakṣiṇaḥ kāle saṃbhṛtya svabhujaṃ tadā
|
hastihastopamaṃ śaikṣaṃ sarvaśatrunibarhaṇam ||28||

29pragṛhṇannañjalīnnṝṇāmudyatān sarvato diśaḥ
|
śuśrāva madhurā vāco nānādeśanivāsinām ||29||

30saṃstūyamānaḥ sūtaiśca māgadhaiśca mahāyaśāḥ
|
pūjayānaśca tān sarvān sarvalokeśvareśvaraḥ ||30||

31pradīpaiḥ kāñcanaistatra gandhatailāvasecitaiḥ
|
parivavrurmahārājaṃ prajvaladbhiḥ samantataḥ ||31||

32sa taiḥ parivṛto rājā pradīpaiḥ kāñcanairjvalan
|
śuśubhe candramā yukto dīptairiva mahāgrahaiḥ ||32||

33kāñcanoṣṇīṣiṇastatra vetrajharjharapāṇayaḥ
|
protsāhayantaḥ śanakaistaṃ janaṃ sarvato diśam ||33||

34saṃprāpya tu tato rājā bhīṣmasya sadanaṃ śubham
|
avatīrya hayāccāpi bhīṣmaṃ prāpya janeśvaraḥ ||34||

35abhivādya tato bhīṣmaṃ niṣaṇṇaḥ paramāsane
|
kāñcane sarvatobhadre sparddhyāstaraṇasaṃvṛte ||35||

36uvāca prāñjalirbhīṣmaṃ bāṣpakaṇṭho'śrulocana
|
tvāṃ vayaṃ hi samāśritya saṃyuge śatrusūdana ||36||

37utsahema raṇe jetuṃ sendrānapi surāsurān
|
kimu pāṇḍusutān vīrān sasuhṛndgāṇabāṃdhava ||37||

38tasmādarhasi gāṅgeya kṛpāṃ kartuṃ mayi prabho
|
jahi pāṇḍusutānvīrānmahendra iva dānavān ||38||

39ahaṃ sarvānmahārāja nihaniṣyāmi somakān
|
pañcālān kekayaiḥ sārdhaṃ karūṣāṃśceti bhārata ||39||

40tvadvacaḥ satyamevāstu jahi pārthān samāgatān
|
somakāṃśca maheṣvāsān satyavāgbhava bhārata ||40||

41dayayā yadi vā rājan dveṣyabhāvānmama prabho
|
mandabhāgyatayā vāpi mama rakṣasi pāṇḍavān ||41||

42anujānīhi samare karṇamāhavaśobhinam
|
sa jeṣyati raṇe pārthān sasuhṛdgaṇabāṃdhavān ||42||

43sa evamuktvā nṛpatiḥ putro duryodhanastava
|
novāca vacanaṃ kiñcidbhīṣmaṃ satyaparākramam ||43||
