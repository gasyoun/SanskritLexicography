Mahābhārata 5.168 (Udyogaparvan)

1bhīṣma uvāca|
acalo vṛṣakaścaiva sahitau bhrātarāvubhau
|
rathau tava durādharṣau śatrūn vidhvaṃsayiṣyataḥ ||1||

2balavantau naravyāghrau dṛḍhakrodhau prahāriṇau
|
gāndhāramukhyau taruṇau darśanīyau mahābalau ||2||

3sakhā te dayito nityaṃ ya eṣa raṇakarkaśaḥ
|
utsāhyati rājaṃstvāṃ vigrahe pāṇḍavaiḥ saha ||3||

4paruṣaḥ katthano nīcaḥ karṇo vaikartanastava
|
mantrī netā ca bandhuśca mānī cātyantamucchritaḥ ||4||

5eṣa naiva rathaḥ karṇo na cāpyatiratho raṇe
|
viyuktaḥ kavacenaiṣa sahajena vicetanaḥ ||5||

6kuṇḍalābhyāṃ ca divyābhyāṃ viyuktaḥ satataṃ ghṛṇī
|
abhiśāpācca rāmasya brāhmaṇasya ca bhāṣaṇāt ||6||

7karaṇānāṃ viyogācca tena me'rdharatho mataḥ
|
naiṣa phālgunamāsādya punarjīvan vimokṣyase ||7||

8tato'bravīt punardroṇaḥ sarvaśastrabhṛtāṃ varaḥ
|
evametadyathā''ttha tvaṃ na mithyā'sti kadācana ||8||

9raṇe raṇe'bhimānī ca vimukhaścāpi dṛśyate
|
ghṛṇī karṇaḥ pramādī ca tena me'rdharatho mataḥ ||9||

10etacchrutvā tu rādheyaḥ krodhādutphālya locane
|
uvāca bhīṣmaṃ rādheyastudan vāgbhiḥ pratodavat ||10||

11pitāmaha yatheṣṭaṃ māṃ vākśarairupakṛntasi
|
anāgasaṃ sadā dveṣādevameva pade pade ||11||

12marṣayāmi ca tat sarvaṃ duryodhanakṛtena vai
|
tvaṃ tu māṃ manyase mandaṃ yathā kāpuruṣaṃ tathā ||12||

13bhavānardharatho mahyaṃ mato vai nātra saṃśayaḥ
|
sarvasya jagataścaiva gāṅgeyo na mṛṣā vadet ||13||

14kurūṇāmahito nityaṃ na ca rājā'vabudhyate
|
ko hi nāma samāneṣu rājasudārakarmasu ||14||

15tejovadhamimaṃ kuryādvibhedayiṣurāhave
|
yathā tvaṃ guṇavidveṣādaparāgaṃ cikīrṣasi ||15||

16na hāyanerna palitairna vittairna ca bandhubhiḥ
|
mahārathatvaṃ saṃkhyātuṃ śakyaṃ kṣatrasya kaurava ||16||

17balajyeṣṭhaṃ smṛtaṃ kṣetraṃ mantrajyeṣṭhā dvijātayaḥ
|
dhanajyeṣṭhāḥ smṛtā vaiśyāḥ śūdrāstu vayasādhikāḥ ||17||

18yathecchakaṃ svayaṃ brūyā rathānatirathāṃstathā
|
kāmadveṣasamāyukto mohāt prakurute bhavān ||18||

19duryodhana mahābāho sādhu samyagavekṣyatām
|
tyajyatāṃ duṣṭabhāvo'yaṃ bhīṣmaḥ kilviṣakṛttava ||19||

20bhinnā hi senā nṛpate duḥsandheyā bhavatyuta
|
maulā hi puruṣavyāghra kimu nānāsamutthitāḥ ||20||

21eṣāṃ dvaidhaṃ samutpannaṃ yodhānāṃ yudhi bhārata
|
tejovadho naḥ kriyate pratyakṣeṇa viśeṣataḥ ||21||

22rathānāṃ kva ca vijñānaṃ kva ca bhīṣmo'lpacetanaḥ
|
ahamāvārayiṣyāmi pāṇḍavānāmanīkinīm ||22||

23āsādya māmamogheṣu gamiṣyanti diśo daśa
|
pāṇḍavāḥ sahapañcālāḥ śārdūlaṃ vṛṣabhā iva ||23||

24kva ca yuddhaṃ vimardo vā mantre suvyāhṛtāni ca
|
kva ca bhīṣmo gatavayā mandātmā kālacoditaḥ ||24||

25ekākī spardhate nityaṃ sarveṇa jagatā saha
|
na cānyaṃ puruṣaṃ kañcinmanyate moghadarśanaḥ ||25||

26śrotavyaṃ khalu vṛddhānāmiti śāstranidarśanam
|
na tveva hyativṛddhānāṃ punarbālā hi te matāḥ ||26||

27ahameko haniṣyāmi pāṇḍavānāmanīkinīm
|
suyuddhe rājaśārdūla yaśo bhīṣmaṃ gamiṣyati ||27||

28kṛtaḥ senāpatistveṣa tvayā bhīṣmo narādhipa
|
senāpatau yaśo gantā na tu yodhān kathañcana ||28||

29nāhaṃ jīvati gāṅgeye yotsye rājan kathañcana
|
hate bhīṣme tu yoddhā'smi sarvaireva mahārathaiḥ ||29||

30bhīṣma uvāca|
samudyato'yaṃ bhāro me sumahān sāgaropamaḥ
|
dhārtarāṣṭrasya saṅgrāme varṣapūgābhicintitaḥ ||30||

31tasminnabhyāgate kāle pratapte lomaharṣaṇe
|
mitho bhedo na me kāryastena jīvasi sūtaja ||31||

32na hyahaṃ tvadya vikramya sthaviro'pi śiśostava
|
yuddhaśraddhāmahaṃ chindyāṃ jīvitasya ca sūtaja ||32||

33jāmadagnyena rāmeṇa mahāstrāṇi vimuñcatā
|
na me vyathā kṛtā kācittvaṃ tu me kiṃ kariṣyati ||33||

34kāmaṃ naitat praśaṃsanti santaḥ svabalasaṃstavam
|
vakṣyāmi tu tvāṃ saṃtapto nihīnakulapāṃsana ||34||

35sametaṃ pārthivaṃ kṣatraṃ kāśirājasvayaṃvare
|
nirjityaikarathenaiva yāḥ kanyāstarasā hatāḥ ||35||

36īdṛśānāṃ sahasrāṇi viśiṣṭānāmatho punaḥ
|
mayaikena nirastāni sasainyāni raṇājire ||36||

37tvāṃ prāpya vairapuruṣaṃ kurūṇāmanayo mahān
|
upasthito vināśāya yatasva puruṣo bhava ||37||

38yuddhyasva samare pārthaṃ yena vispardhase saha
|
drakṣyāmi tvāṃ vinirmuktamasmādyuddhāt sudurmate ||38||

39tamuvāca tato rājā dhārtarāṣṭraḥ pratāpavān
|
māṃ samīkṣasva gāṅgeya kāryaṃ hi mahadudyatam ||39||

40cintyatāmidamekāgraṃ mama niḥśreyasaṃ param
|
ubhāvapi bhavantau me mahat karma kariṣyataḥ ||40||

41bhūyaśca śrotumicchāmi pareṣāṃ rathasattamān
|
ye caivātirathāstatra ye caiva rathayūthapāḥ ||41||

42balābalamamitrāṇāṃ śrotumicchāmi kaurava
|
prabhātāyāṃ rajanyāṃ vai idaṃ yuddhaṃ bhaviṣyati ||42||
