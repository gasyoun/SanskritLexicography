Mahābhārata 4.22 (Virāṭaparvan)

1bhīmasena uvāca|tathā bhadre kariṣyāmi yathā tvaṃ bhīru bhāṣase
|
adya taṃ sūdayiṣyāmi kīcakaṃ sahabāndhavam ||1||

2asyāḥ pradoṣe śarvaryāḥ kuruṣvānena saṅgatam
|
duḥkhaṃ śokaṃ ca nirdhūya yājñaseni śucismite ||2||

3yaiṣā nartanaśāleha matsyarājena kāritā
|
divā'tra kanyā nṛtyanti rātrau yānti yathāgṛham ||3||

4tatrāsti śayanaṃ divyaṃ dṛḍhāṅgaṃ supratiṣṭhitam
|
tatrāsya darśayiṣyāmi pūrvapretān pitāmahān ||4||

5yathā ca tvāṃ na paśyeyuḥ kurvāṇāṃ tena saṃvidam
|
kuryāstathā tvaṃ kalyāṇi yathā sannihito bhavet ||5||

6vaiśampāyana uvāca|tathā tau kathayitvā tu bāṣpamutsṛjya duḥkhitau
|
rātriśeṣaṃ tamatyugraṃ dhārayāmāsaturhṛdi ||6||

7tasyāṃ rātryāṃ vyatītāyāṃ prātarutthāya kīcakaḥ
|
gatvā rājakulāyaiva draupadīmidamabravīt ||7||

8sabhāyāṃ paśyato rājñaḥ pātayitvā padā'hanam
|
na caivālabhase trāṇamabhipannā balīyasā ||8||

9pravādeneha matsyānāṃ rājā nāmnā'yamucyate
|
ahameva hi matsyānāṃ rājā vai vāhinīpatiḥ ||9||

10māṃ sukhaṃ pratipadyasva dāso bhīru bhavāmi te
|
ahnāya tava suśroṇi śataṃ niṣkān dadāmyaham||10||

11dāsīśataṃ ca te dadyāṃ dāsānāmapi cāparam
|
rathaṃ cāśvatarīyuktamastu nau bhīru saṅgamaḥ ||11||

12draupadyuvāca
|
evaṃ me samayaṃ tvadya pratipadyasva kīcaka
|
na tvāṃ sakhā vā bhrātā vā jānīyāt saṅgataṃ mayā ||12||

13anupravādādbhītā'smi gandharvāṇāṃ yaśasvinām
|
evaṃ me pratijānīhi tato'haṃ vaśagā tava ||13||

14kīcaka uvāca|evametat kariṣyāmi yathā suśroṇi bhāṣase
|
eko bhadre gamiṣyāmi śūnyamāvasathaṃ tava ||14||

15samāgamārthaṃ rambhoru tvayā madanamohitaḥ
|
yathā tvāṃ naiva paśyeyurgandharvāḥ sūryavarcasaḥ ||15||

16draupadyuvāca
|
yadetannartanāgāraṃ matsyarājena kāritam
|
divā'tra kanyā nṛtyanti rātrau yānti yathāgṛham ||16||

17tamisre tatra gacchethā gandharvāstanna jānate
|
tatra doṣaḥ parihṛto bhaviṣyati na saṃśayaḥ ||17||

18vaiśampāyana uvāca|tamarthamapi jalpantyāḥ kṛṣṇāyāḥ kīcakena ha
|
divasārdhaṃ samabhavanmāsenaiva samaṃ nṛpa ||18||

19kīcako'tha gṛhaṃ gatvā bhṛśaṃ harṣapariplutaḥ
|
sairandhrīrūpiṇaṃ mūḍho mṛtyuṃ taṃ nāvabuddhavān ||19||

20gandhābharaṇamālyeṣu vyāsaktaḥ saviśeṣataḥ
|
alañcake tadā''tmānaṃ satvaraḥ kāmamohitaḥ ||20||

21tasya tatkurvataḥ karma kālo dīrgha ivābhavat
|
anucintayataścāpi tāmevāyatalocanām ||21||

22āsīdabhyadhikā cāpi śrīḥ śriyaṃ pramumukṣataḥ
|
nirvāṇakāle dīpasya vartīmiva didhakṣataḥ ||22||

23kṛtasaṃpratyayastasyāḥ kīcakaḥ kāmamohitaḥ
|
nājānāddivasaṃ yāntaṃ cintayānaḥ samāgamam ||23||

24tatastu draupadī gatvā tadā bhīmaṃ mahānase
|
upātiṣṭhata kalyāṇī kauravyaṃ patimantikam ||24||

25tamuvāca sukeśāntā kīcakasya mayā kṛtaḥ
|
saṅgamo nartanāgāre yathā'vocaḥ parantapa ||25||

26śūnyaṃ sa nartanāgāramāgamiṣyati kīcakaḥ
|
eko niśi mahābāho kīcakaṃ taṃ niṣūdaya ||26||

27taṃ sūtaputraṃ kaunteya kīcakaṃ madadarpitam
|
gatvā tvaṃ nartanāgāraṃ nirjīvaṃ kuru pāṇḍava ||27||

28darpācca sūtaputro'sau gandharvānavamanyate
|
taṃ tvaṃ praharatāṃ śreṣṭha hradānnāgamivoddhara ||28||

29aśru duḥkhābhibhūtāyā mama mārjasva bhārata
|
ātmanaścaiva bhadraṃ te kuru mānaṃ kulasya ca ||29||

30bhīmasena uvāca|svāgataṃ te varārohe yanmāṃ vedayase priyam
|
na hyanyaṃ kañcidicchāmi sahāyaṃ varavarṇini ||30||

31yā me prītistvayā''khyātā kīcakasya samāgame
|
hatvā hiḍimbaṃ sā prītirmamāsīdvaravarṇini ||31||

32satyaṃ bhrātṝṃśca dharmaṃ ca puraskṛtya bravīmi te
|
kīcakaṃ nihaniṣyāmi vṛtraṃ devapatiryathā ||32||

33taṃ gahvare prakāśe vā pothayiṣyāmi kīcakam
|
atha cedapi yotsyanti hiṃse matsyānapi dhruvam ||33||

34tato duryodhanaṃ hatvā pratipatsye vasundharām
|
kāmaṃ matsyamupāstāṃ hi kuntīputro yudhiṣṭhiraḥ ||34||

35draupadyuvāca
|
yathā na santyajethāstvaṃ satyaṃ vai matkṛte vibho
|
nigūḍhastvaṃ tathā pārtha kīcakaṃ taṃ niṣūdaya ||35||

36bhīmasena uvāca|evametat kariṣyāmi yathā tvaṃ bhīru bhāṣase
|
adya taṃ sūdayiṣyāmi kīcakaṃ saha bāndhavaiḥ ||36||

37adṛśyamānastasyātha tamasvinyāmanindite
|
nāgo bilvamivākramya pothayiṣyāmyahaṃ śiraḥ
|
alabhyāmicchatastasya kīcakasya durātmanaḥ ||37||

38vaiśampāyana uvāca|bhīmo'tha prathamaṃ gatvā rātrau channa upāviśat
|
mṛgaṃ haririvādṛśyaḥ pratyākāṅkṣata kīcakam ||38||

39kīcakaścāpyalaṅkṛtya yathākāmamupāgamat
|
tāṃ velāṃ nartanāgāraṃ pāñcālīsaṅgamāśayā ||39||

40manyamānaḥ sa saṅketamāgāraṃ prāviśacca tat
|
praviśya ca sa tadveśma tamasā saṃvṛtaṃ mahat ||40||

41pūrvāgataṃ tatastatra bhīmamapratimaujasam
|
ekāntāvasthitaṃ canamāsasāda sa durmatiḥ ||41||

42śayānaṃ śayane tatra sūtaputraḥ parāmṛśat
|
jājvalyamānaṃ kopena kṛṣṇādharṣaṇajena ha ||42||

43upasaṅgamya caivainaṃ kīcakaḥ kāmamohitaḥ
|
harṣonmathitacittātmā smayamāno'bhyabhāṣata ||43||

44prāpitaṃ te mayā vittaṃ bahurūpamanantakam
|
yatkṛtaṃ dhanaratnāḍhyaṃ dāsīśataparicchadam ||44||

45rūpalāvaṇyayuktābhiryuvatībhiralaṅkṛtam
|
gṛhaṃ cāntaḥpuraṃ subhru krīḍārativirājitam
|
tatsarvaṃ tvāṃ samuddiśya sahasā'hamupāgataḥ ||45||

46akasmānmāṃ praśaṃsanti sadā gṛhagatāḥ striyaḥ
|
suvāsā darśanīyaśca nānyo'sti tvādṛśaḥ pumān ||46||

47bhīmasena uvāca|diṣṭyā tvaṃ darśanīyo'tha diṣṭyā''tmānaṃ praśaṃsasi
|
īdṛśastu tvayā sparśaḥ spṛṣṭapūrvo na karhicit ||47||

48sparśaṃ vetsi vidagdhastvaṃ kāmadharmavicakṣaṇaḥ
|
strīṇāṃ prītikaro nānyastvatsamaḥ puruṣastviha ||48||

49vaiśampāyana uvāca|ityuktvā taṃ mahābāhurbhīmo bhīmaparākramaḥ
|
sahasotpatya kaunteyaḥ prahasyeda‌muvāca ha ||49||

50adya tvāṃ bhaginī pāpaṃ kṛṣyamāṇaṃ mayā bhuvi
|
drakṣyate'dripratīkāśaṃ siṃheneva mahāgajam ||50||

51nirābādhā tvayi hate sairandhrī vicariṣyati
|
sukhameva cariṣyanti sairandhryāḥ patayaḥ sadā ||51||

52tato jagrāha keśeṣu mālyavatsu mahābalaḥ
|
sa keśeṣu parāmṛṣṭo balena balināṃ varaḥ ||52||

53ākṣipya keśān vegena bāhvorjagrāha pāṇḍavam
|
bāhuyuddhaṃ tayorāsīt kruddhayornarasiṃhayoḥ ||53||

54vasante vāsitāhetorbalavadgajayoriva
|
kīcakānāṃ tu mukhyasya narāṇāmuttamasya ca ||54||

55vālisugrīvayorbhrātroḥ pureva kapisiṃhayoḥ
|
anyonyamapi saṃrabdhau parasparajayaiṣiṇau ||55||

56tataḥ samudyamya bhujau pañcaśīrṣāvivoragau
|
nakhadaṃṣṭrābhiranyonyaṃ ghnataḥ krodhaviṣoddhatau ||56||

57vegenābhihato bhīmaḥ kīcakena balīyasā
|
sthirapratijñaḥ sa raṇe padānna calitaḥ padam ||57||

58tāvanyonyaṃ samāśliṣya prakarṣantau parasparam
|
ubhāvapi prakāśete pravṛddhau vṛṣabhāviva ||58||

59tayorhyāsīt sutumulaḥ saṃprahāraḥ sudāruṇaḥ
|
nakhadantāyudhavatorvyāghrayoriva dṛptayoḥ ||59||

60abhipatyātha bāhubhyāṃ pratyagṛhṇādamarṣitaḥ
|
mātaṅga iva mātaṅgaṃ prabhinnakaraṭāmukham ||60||

61sa cāpyenaṃ tadā bhīmaḥ pratijagrāha vīryavān
|
tamākṣipat kīcako'tha balena balināṃ varaḥ||61||

62tayorbhujaviniṣpeṣādubhayorbalinostadā
|
śabdaḥ samabhavaddhoro veṇusphoṭasamo yudhi ||62||

63athainamākṣipya balādgṛhamadhye vṛkodaraḥ
|
dhūnayāmāsa vegena vāyuścaṇḍa iva drumam ||63||

64bhīmena ca parāmṛṣṭo durbalo balinā raṇe
|
prāspandata yathāprāṇaṃ vicakarṣa ca pāṇḍavam ||64||

65īṣadākalitaṃ cāpi krodhāddrutapadaṃ sthitam
|
kīcako balavān bhīmaṃ jānubhyāmākṣipadbhuvi ||65||

66pātito bhuvi bhīmastu kīcakena balīyasā
|
utpapātātha vegena daṇḍapāṇirivāntakaḥ ||66||

67spardhayā ca balonmattau tāvubhau sūtapāṇḍavau
|
niśīye paryakarṣetāṃ balinau nirjane sthale ||67||

68tatastadbhavanaṃ śreṣṭhaṃ prākampata muhurmuhuḥ
|
balavaccāpi saṃkruddhāvanyonyaṃ prati garjataḥ ||68||

69talābhyāṃ sa tu bhīmena vakṣasyabhihato balī
|
kīcako roṣasaṃtaptaḥ padānna calitaḥ padam ||69||

70muhūrtaṃ tu sa taṃ vegaṃ sahitvā bhuvi duḥsaham
|
balādahīyata tadā sūto bhīmabalārditaḥ ||70||

71taṃ hīyamānaṃ vijñāya bhīmaseno mahābalaḥ
|
vakṣasyānīya vegena mamardainaṃ vicetasam ||71||

72krodhāviṣṭo viniḥśvasya punaścainaṃ vṛkodaraḥ
|
jagrāha jayatāṃ śreṣṭhaḥ keśeṣveva tadā bhṛśam ||72||

73gṛhītvā kīcakaṃ bhīmo virarāja mahābalaḥ
|
śārdūlaḥ piśitākāṅkṣī gṛhīttveva mahāmṛgam ||73||

74tata enaṃ pariśrāntamupalabhya vṛkodaraḥ
|
yoktrayāmāsa bāhubhyāṃ paśuṃ raśanayā yathā ||74||

75nadantaṃ ca mahānādaṃ bhinnabherīsamasvanam
|
bhrāmayāmāsa suciraṃ visphurantamacetasam ||75||

76pragṛhya tarasā dorbhyāṃ kaṇṭhaṃ tasya vṛkodaraḥ
|
apīḍayata kṛṣṇāyāstadā kopopaśāntaye ||76||

77atha taṃ bhagnasarvāṅgaṃ vyāviddhanayanāmbaram
|
ākramya ca kaṭīdeśe jānunā kīcakādhamam
|
apīḍayata bāhubhyāṃ paśumāramamārayat ||77||

78taṃ viṣīdantamājñāya kīcakaṃ pāṇḍunandanaḥ
|
bhūtale bhrāmayāmāsa vākyaṃ cedamuvāca ha ||78||

79adyāhamanṛṇo bhūtvā bhrāturbhāryāpahāriṇam
|
śāntiṃ labdhā'smi paramāṃ hatvā sairandhrikaṇṭakam ||79||

80ityevamuktvā puruṣapravīrastaṃ kīcakaṃ krodhasarāganetraḥ
|
āsrastavastrābharaṇaṃ sphurantamudbhrāntanetraṃ vyasumutsasarja ||80||

81niṣpiṣya pāṇinā pāṇiṃ saṃda‌ṣṭauṣṭhapuṭaṃ balī
|
samākramya ca saṃkruddho balena balināṃ varaḥ ||81||

82tasya pādau ca pāṇī ca śiro grīvāṃ ca sarvaśaḥ
|
kāye praveśayāmāsa paśoriva pinākadhṛk ||82||

83taṃ saṃmathitasarvāṅgaṃ māṃsapiṇḍopamaṃ kṛtam
|
kṛṣṇāyā darśayāmāsa bhīmaseno mahābalaḥ ||83||

84uvāca ca mahātejā draupadīṃ yoṣitāṃ varām
|
paśyainamehi pāñcāli kāmuko'yaṃ yathā kṛtaḥ ||84||

85evamuktvā mahārāja bhīmo bhīmaparākramaḥ
|
pādena pīḍayāmāsa tasya kāyaṃ durātmanaḥ ||85||

86tato'gniṃ tatra prajvālya darśayitvā tu kīcakam
|
pāñcālīṃ sa tadā vīra idaṃ vacanamabravīt ||86||

87prārthayanti sukeśānte ye tvāṃ śīlaguṇānvitām
|
evaṃ te bhīru vadhyante kīcakaḥ śobhate yathā ||87||

88tat kṛtvā duṣkaraṃ karma kṛṣṇāyāḥ priyamuttamam
|
tathā sa kīcakaṃ hatvā gatvā roṣasya vai śamam‌ ||88||

89āmantrya draupadīṃ kṛṣṇāṃ kṣipramāyānmahānasam
|
kīcakaṃ ghātayitvā tu draupadī yoṣitāṃ varā
|
prahṛṣṭā gatasaṃtāpā sabhāpālānuvāca ha ||89||

90kīcako'yaṃ hataḥ śete gandharvaiḥ patibhirmama
|
parastrīkāmasaṃmattastatrāgacchata paśyata ||90||

91tacchrutvā bhāṣitaṃ tasyā nartanāgārarakṣiṇaḥ
|
sahasaiva samājagmurādāyolkāḥ sahasraśaḥ ||91||

92tato gatvā'tha tadveśma kīcakaṃ vinipātitam
|
gatāsuṃ dadṛśurbhūmau rudhireṇa samukṣitam ||92||

93pāṇipādavihīnaṃ tu dṛṣṭvā ca vyathitā'bhavan
|
nirīkṣanti tataḥ sarve paraṃ vismayamāgatāḥ ||93||

94amānuṣaṃ kṛtaṃ karma taṃ dṛṣṭvā vinipātitam
|
kvāsya grīvā kva caraṇau kva pāṇī kva śirastathā
|
iti sma taṃ parīkṣante gandharveṇa hataṃ tadā ||94||
