Mahābhārata 5.144 (Udyogaparvan)

1vaiśampāyana uvāca|
asiddhānunaye kṛṣṇe kurubhyaḥ pāṇḍavān gate
|
abhigamya pṛthāṃ kṣattā śanaiḥ śocannivābravīt ||1||

2jānāsi me jīvaputri bhāvaṃ nityamavigrahe
|
krośato na ca gṛhṇīte vacanaṃ me suyodhanaḥ ||2||

3upapanno hyasau rājā cedipāñcālakekayaiḥ
|
bhīmārjunābhyāṃ kṛṣṇena yuyudhānayamairapi ||3||

4upaplavye niviṣṭo'pi dharmameva yudhiṣṭhiraḥ
|
kāṅkṣate jñātisauhārdādbalavān durbalo yathā ||4||

5rājā tu dhṛtarāṣṭro'yaṃ vayovṛddho na śāmyati
|
mattaḥ putramadenaiva vidharme pathi vartate ||5||

6jayadrathasya karṇasya tathā duḥśāsanasya ca
|
saubalasya ca durbuddhyā mitho bhedaḥ prapatsyate ||6||

7adharmeṇa hi dharmiṣṭhaṃ kṛtaṃ vaikāryamīdṛśam
|
yeṣāṃ teṣāmayaṃ dharmaḥ sānubandho bhaviṣyati ||7||

8kriyamāṇe balāddharme kurubhiḥ ko na sañjvaret
|
asāmnā keśave yāte samudyokṣyanti pāṇḍavāḥ ||8||

9tataḥ kurūṇāmanayo bhavitā vīranāśanaḥ
|
cintayanna labhe nidrāmahaḥsu ca niśāsu ca ||9||

10śrutvā tu kuntī tadvākyamarthakāmena bhāṣitam
|
sā niḥśvasantī duḥkhārtā manasā vimamarśa ha ||10||

11dhigastvarthaṃ yatkṛte'yaṃ mahān jñātivadhaḥ kṛtaḥ
|
vartsyate suhṛdāṃ caiva yuddhe'smin vai parābhavaḥ ||11||

12pāṇḍavāścedipañcālā yādavāśca samāgatāḥ
|
bhārataiḥ saha yotsyanti kiṃ nu duḥkhamataḥ param ||12||

13paśye doṣaṃ dhruvaṃ yuddhe tathā'yuddhe parābhavam
|
adhanasya mṛtaṃ śreyo na hi jñātikṣayo jayaḥ ||13||

14iti me cintayantyā vai hṛdi duḥkhaṃ pravartate
|
pitāmahaḥ śāntanava ācāryaśca yudhāṃ patiḥ ||14||

15karṇaśca dhārtarāṣṭrārthaṃ vardhayanti bhayaṃ mama
|
nācāryaḥ kāmavān śiṣyairdroṇo yuddhyeta jātucit ||15||

16pāṇḍaveṣu kathaṃ hārdaṃ kuryānna ca pitāmahaḥ
|
ayaṃ tveko vṛthādṛṣṭirdhārtarāṣṭrasya durmateḥ ||16||

17mohānuvartī satataṃ pāpo dveṣṭi ca pāṇḍavān
|
mahatyanarthe nirbandhī balavāṃśca viśeṣataḥ ||17||

18karṇaḥ sadā pāṇḍavānāṃ tanme dahati samprati
|
āśaṃse tvadya karṇasya mano'haṃ pāṇḍavān prati ||18||

19prasāda‌yitumāsādya darśayantī yathātatham
|
toṣito bhagavān yatra durvāsā me varaṃ dadau ||19||

20āhvānaṃ mantrasaṃyuktaṃ vasantyāḥ pitṛveśmani
|
sā'hamantaḥpure rājñaḥ kuntibhojapuraskṛtā ||20||

21cintayantī bahuvidhaṃ hṛdayena vidūyatā
|
balābalaṃ ca mantrāṇāṃ brāhmaṇasya ca vāgbalam ||21||

22strībhāvādbalabhāvācca cintayantī punaḥ punaḥ
|
dhātryā visrabdhayā guptā sakhījanavṛtā tadā ||22||

23doṣaṃ pariharantī ca pituścāritryarakṣiṇī
|
kathaṃ na sukṛtaṃ me syānnāparādhavatī katham ||23||

24bhaveyamiti sañcintya brāhmaṇaṃ taṃ namasya ca
|
kautūhalāttu taṃ labdhvā bāliśyādācaraṃ tadā
|
kanyā satī devamarkamāsādayamahaṃ tataḥ ||24||

25yo'sau kānīnagarbho me putravat parirakṣitaḥ
|
kasmānna kuryādvacanaṃ pathyaṃ bhrātṛhitaṃ tathā ||25||

26iti kuntī viniścitya kāryaniścayamuttamam
|
kāryārthamabhiniścitya yayau bhāgīrathīṃ prati ||26||

27ātmajasya tatastasya ghṛṇinaḥ satyasaṅginaḥ
|
gaṅgātīre pṛthā'śrauṣīdvedādhyayananiḥsvanam ||27||

28prāṅmukhasyordhvabāhoḥ sā paryatiṣṭhata pṛṣṭhataḥ
|
japyāvasānaṃ kāryārthaṃ pratīkṣantī tapasvinī ||28||

29atiṣṭhat sūryatāpārtā karṇasyottaravāsasi
|
kauravyapatnī vārṣṇeyī padmamāleva śuṣyatī ||29||

30āpṛṣṭhatāpājjaptvā sa parivṛtya yatavrataḥ
|
dṛṣṭvā kuntīmupātiṣṭha‌dabhivādya kṛtāñjaliḥ ||30||

31yathānyāyaṃ mahātejā mānī dharmabhṛtāṃ varaḥ
|
utsmayan praṇataḥ prāha kuntīṃ vaikartano vṛṣaḥ ||31||
