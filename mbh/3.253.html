Mahābhārata 3.253 (Āraṇyakaparvan)

1janamejaya uvāca|
vasamāneṣu pārtheṣu vane tasmin mahātmasu
|
dhārtarāṣṭrā maheṣvāsāḥ kimakurvata sattamāḥ||1||

2karṇo vaikartanaścaiva śakuniśca mahābalaḥ
|
bhīṣmadroṇakṛpāścaiva tanme śaṃsitumarhasi||2||

3vaiśampāyana uvāca|
evaṃ gateṣu pārtheṣu visṛṣṭe ca suyodhane
|
āgate hāstinapuraṃ mokṣite pāṇḍunandanaiḥ||3||

4bhīṣmo'bravīt mahārāja dhārtarāṣṭramidaṃ vacaḥ
|
uktaṃ tāta yathā pūrvaṃ gacchataste tapovanam||4||

5gamanaṃ me na rucitaṃ tava tatra kṛtaṃ ca te
|
tataḥ prāptaṃ tvayā vīra grahaṇaṃ śatrubhirbalāt||5||

6mokṣitaścāsi dharmajñaiḥ pāṇḍavairna ca lajjase
|
pratyakṣaṃ tava gāndhāre sasainyasya viśāṃpate||6||

7sūtaputro'payādbhīto gandharvāṇāṃ tadā raṇāt
|
krośatastava rājendra sasainyasya nṛpātmaja||7||

8dṛṣṭaste vikramaścaiva pāṇḍavānāṃ mahātmanām
|
karṇasya ca mahābāho sūtaputrasya durmateḥ||8||

9na cāpi pādabhākkarṇaḥ pāṇḍavānāṃ nṛpottama
|
dhanurvede ca śaurye ca dharme vā dharmavatsala||9||

10tasmādahaṃ kṣamaṃ manye pāṇḍavaistairmahātmabhiḥ
|
sandhiṃ sandhividāṃ śreṣṭha kulasyāsya vivṛddhaye||10||

11evamuktaśca bhīṣmeṇa dhārtarāṣṭro janeśvaraḥ
|
prahasya sahasā rājan vipratasthe sasaubalaḥ||11||

12taṃ tu prasthitamājñāya karṇaduḥśāsanādayaḥ
|
anujagmurmaheṣvāsā dhārtarāṣṭra mahābalam||12||

13tāṃstu samprasthitān dṛṣṭvā bhīṣmaḥ kurupitāmahaḥ
|
lajjayā brīḍito rājan jagāma svaṃ niveśanam||13||

14gate bhīṣme mahārāja dhārtarāṣṭro janeśvaraḥ
|
punarāgamya taṃ deśamamantrayata mantribhiḥ||14||

15kimasmākaṃ bhavecchreyaḥ kiṃ kāryamavaśiṣyate
|
kathaṃ ca sukṛtaṃ tat syānmantrayāmo'dya yaddhitam||15||

16karṇa uvāca|
duryodhana nibodhedaṃ yat tvāṃ vakṣyāmi kaurava
|
bhīṣmo'smānnindati sadā pāṇḍavāṃśca praśaṃsati||16||

17tvaddveṣācca mahābāho mamāpi dveṣṭumarhati
|
vigarhate ca māṃ nittyaṃ tvatsamīpe nareśvara||17||

18sā'haṃ bhīṣmavacastadvai na mṛṣyāmīha bhārata
|
tvatsamakṣaṃ yaduktaṃ ca bhīṣmeṇāmitrakarṣaṇa||18||

19pāṇḍavānāṃ yaśo rājaṃstava nindāṃ ca bhārata
|
anujānīhi māṃ rājan sabhṛtyabalavāhanam||19||

20jeṣyāmi pṛthivīṃ rājan saśailavanakānanām
|
jitā ca pāṇḍavairbhūmiścaturbhirbalaśālibhiḥ||20||

21tāmahaṃ te vijeṣyāmi eka eva na saṃśayaḥ
|
sampaśyatu sudurbuddhirbhīṣmaḥ kurukulādhamaḥ||21||

22anindyaṃ nindate yo hi apraśaṃsyaṃ praśaṃsati
|
sa paśyatu balaṃ me'dya ātmānaṃ tu vigarhatu||22||

23anujānīhi māṃ rājan dhruvo hi vijayastava
|
pratijānāmi te satyaṃ rājannāyudhamālabhe||23||

24tacchrutvā tu vaco rājan karṇasya bharatarṣabha
|
prītyā paramayā yuktaḥ karṇamāha narādhipaḥ||24||

25dhanyo'smyanugṛhīto'smi yasya me tvaṃ mahābalaḥ
|
hiteṣu vartase nityaṃ saphalaṃ janma cādya me||25||

26yadā ca manyase vīra sarvaśatrunibarhaṇam
|
tadā nirgaccha bhadraṃ te hyanuśādhi ca māmiti||26||

27evamuktastadā karṇo dhārtarāṣṭreṇa dhīmatā
|
sarvamājñāpayāmāsa prāyātrikamarindama||27||

28prayayau ca maheṣvāso nakṣatre śubhadaivate
|
śubhe tithau muhūrte ca pūjyamāno dvijātibhiḥ||28||

29maṅgalaiśca śubhaiḥ snāto vāgbhiścāpi prapūjitaḥ
|
nādayan rathaghoṣeṇa trailokyaṃ sacarācaram||29||
