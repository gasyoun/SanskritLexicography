Mahābhārata 1.103 (Ādiparvan)

1vaiśaṃpāyana uvāca|
tataḥ satyavatī dīnā kṛpaṇā putragṛddhinī
|
putrasya kṛtvā kāryāṇi snuṣābhyāṃ saha bhārata||1||

2samāśvāsya snuṣe te ca bhīṣmaṃ śastrabhṛtāṃ varam
|
dharmaṃ ca pitṛvaṃśaṃ ca mātṛvaṃśaṃ ca bhāvinī
|
prasamīkṣya mahābhāgā gāṅgeyaṃ vākyamabravīt||2||

3śantanordharmanityasya kauravyasya yaśasvinaḥ
|
tvayi piṇḍaśca kīrtiśca saṃtānaṃ ca pratiṣṭhitam||3||

4yathākarma śubhaṃ kṛtvā svargopagamanaṃ dhruvam
|
yathā cāyurdhruvaṃ satye tvayi dharmastathā dhruvaḥ||4||

5vettha dharmāṃśca dharmajña samāsenetareṇa ca
|
vividhāstvaṃ śrutīrvettha vedāṅgāni ca sarvaśaḥ||5||

6vyavasthānaṃ ca te dharme kulācāraṃ ca lakṣaye
|
pratipattiṃ ca kṛcchreṣu śukrāṅgirasayoriva||6||

7tasmātsubhṛśamāśvasya tvayi dharmabhṛtāṃ vara
|
kārye tvāṃ viniyokṣyāmi tacchrutvā kartumarhasi||7||

8mama putrastava bhrātā vīryavānsupriyaśca te
|
bāla eva gataḥ svargamaputraḥ puruṣarṣabha||8||

9ime mahiṣyau bhrātuste kāśirājasute śubhe
|
rūpayauvanasaṃpanne putrakāme ca bhārata||9||

10tayorutpādayāpatyaṃ santānāya kulasya naḥ
|
manniyogānmahābāho dharmaṃ kartumihārhasi||10||

11rājye caivābhiṣicyasva bhāratānanuśādhi ca
|
dārāṃśca kuru dharmeṇa mā nimajjīḥ pitāmahān||11||

12vaiśaṃpāyana uvāca|
tathocyamāno mātrā sa suhṛdbhiśca parantapaḥ
|
ityuvācātha dharmātmā dharmyamevottaraṃ vacaḥ||12||

13asaṃśayaṃ paro dharmastvayā mātarudāhṛtaḥ
|
tvamapatyaṃ prati ca me pratijñāṃ vettha vai parām||13||

14jānāsi ca yathāvṛttaṃ śulkahetostvadantare
|
sa satyavati satyaṃ te pratijānāmyahaṃ punaḥ||14||

15parityajeyaṃ trailokyaṃ rājyaṃ deveṣu vā punaḥ
|
yadvā'pyadhikametābhyāṃ na tu satyaṃ kathañcana||15||

16tyajecca pṛthivī gandhamāpaśca rasamātmanaḥ
|
jyotistathā tyajedrūpaṃ vāyuḥ sparśaguṇaṃ tyajet||16||

17prabhāṃ samutsṛjedarko dhūmaketustathoṣmatām
|
tyajecchabdaṃ tathā''kāśaṃ somaḥ śītāṃśutāṃ tyajet||17||

18vikramaṃ vṛtrahā jahyāddharmaṃ jahyācca dharmarāṭ
|
na tvahaṃ satyamutsraṣṭuṃ vyavaseyaṃ kathaṃcana||18||

19evamuktā tu putreṇa bhūridraviṇatejasā
|
mātā satyavatī bhīṣmamuvāca tadanantaram||19||

20jānāmi te sthitiṃ satye parāṃ satyaparākrama
|
icchansṛjethāstrīlloঁkānanyāṃstvaṃ svena tejasā||20||

21jānāmi caivaṃ satyaṃ tanmadarthe yacca bhāṣitam
|
āpaddharmaṃ tvamāvekṣya vaha paitāmahīṃ dhuram||21||

22yathā te kulatantuśca dharmaśca na parābhavet
|
suhṛdaśca prahṛṣyeraṃstathā kuru parantapa||22||

23lālapyamānāṃ tāmevaṃ kṛpaṇāṃ putragṛddhinīm
|
dharmādapetaṃ bruvatīṃ bhīṣmo bhūyo'bravīdidam||23||

24rājñi dharmānavekṣasva mā naḥ sarvānvyanīnaśaḥ
|
satyāccyutiḥ kṣatriyasya na dharmeṣu praśasyate||24||

25śāntanorapi santānaṃ yathā syādakṣayaṃ bhuvi
|
tatte dharmaṃ pravakṣyāmi kṣātraṃ rājñi sanātanam||25||

26śrutvā taṃ pratipadyasva prājñaiḥ saha purohitaiḥ
|
āpaddharmārthakuśalairlokatantramavekṣya ca||26||
