Mahābhārata 5.118 (Udyogaparvan)

1nārada uvāca|
tathaiva tāṃ śriyaṃ tyaktvā kanyā bhūtvā yaśasvinī
|
mādhavī gālavaṃ vipramabhyayāt satyasaṅgarā ||1||

2gālavo vimṛśanneva svakāryagatamānasaḥ
|
jagāma bhojanagaraṃ draṣṭumauśīnaraṃ nṛpam ||2||

3tamuvācātha gatvā sa nṛpatiṃ satyavikramam
|
iyaṃ kanyā sutau dvau te janayiṣyati pārthivau ||3||

4asyāṃ bhavānavāptārtho bhavitā pretya ceha ca
|
somārkapratisaṅkāśau janayitvā sutau nṛpa ||4||

5śulkaṃ tu sarvadharmajña hayānāṃ candravarcasām
|
ekataḥ śyāmakarṇānāṃ deyaṃ mahyaṃ catuḥśatam ||5||

6gurvartho'yaṃ samārambho na hayaiḥ kṛtyamasti me
|
yadi śakyaṃ mahārāja kriyatāmavicāritam ||6||

7anapatyo'si rājarṣe putrau janaya pārthiva
|
pitṝn putraplavena tvamātmānaṃ caiva tāraya ||7||

8na putraphalabhoktā hi rājarṣe pātyate divaḥ
|
na yāti narakaṃ ghoraṃ yathā gacchantyanātmajāḥ ||8||

9etaccānyacca vividhaṃ śrutvā gālavabhāṣitam
|
uśīnaraḥ prativaco dadau tasya narādhipaḥ ||9||

10śrutavānasmi te vākyaṃ yathā vadasi gālava
|
vidhistu balavān brahman pravaṇaṃ hi mano mama ||10||

11śate dve tu mamāśvānāmīdṛśānāṃ dvijottama
|
itareṣāṃ sahasrāṇi subahūni caranti me ||11||

12ahamapyekamevāsyāṃ janayiṣyāmi gālava
|
putraṃ dvija gataṃ mārgaṃ gamiṣyāmi parairaham ||12||

13mūlyenāpi samaṃ kuryāṃ tavāhaṃ dvijasattama
|
paurajānapadārthaṃ tu mamārtho nātmabhogataḥ ||13||

14kāmato hi dhanaṃ rājā pārakyaṃ yaḥ prayacchati
|
na sa dharmeṇa dharmātmanyujyate yaśasā na ca ||14||

15so'haṃ pratigrahīṣyāmi dadātvetāṃ bhavān mama
|
kumārīṃ devagarbhābhāmekaputrabhavāya me ||15||

16tathā tu bahudhā kanyāmuktavantaṃ narādhipam
|
uśīnaraṃ dvijaśreṣṭho gālavaḥ pratyapūjayat ||16||

17uśīnaraṃ pratigrāhya gālavaḥ prayayau vanam
|
reme sa tāṃ samāsādya kṛtapuṇya iva śriyam ||17||

18kandareṣu ca śailānāṃ nadīnāṃ nirjhareṣu ca
|
udyāneṣu vicitreṣu vaneṣūpavaneṣu ca ||18||

19harmyeṣu ramaṇīyeṣu prāsādaśikhareṣu ca
|
vātāyanavimāneṣu tathā garbhagṛheṣu ca ||19||

20tato'sya samaye jajñe putro bālaraviprabhaḥ
|
śibirnāmnā'bhivikhyāto yaḥ sa pārthivasattamaḥ ||20||

21upasthāya sa taṃ vipro gālavaḥ pratigṛhya ca
|
kanyāṃ prayātaṃstāṃ rājan dṛṣṭavān vinatātmajam ||21||
