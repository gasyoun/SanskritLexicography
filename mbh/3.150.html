Mahābhārata 3.150 (Āraṇyakaparvan)

1bhīmasena uvāca|
pūrvarūpa‌madṛṣṭvā te na yāsyāmi kathaṃcana
|
yadi te'hamanugrāhyo darśayātmānamātmanā||1||

2vaiśaṃpāyana uvāca|
evamuktastu bhīmena smitaṃ kṛtvā plavaṃgamaḥ
|
tadrūpaṃ darśayāmāsa yadvai sāgaralaṅghane||2||

3bhrātuḥ priyamabhīpsanvai cakāra sumahadvapuḥ
|
dehastasya tato'tīva vardhatyāyāmavistaraiḥ||3||

4sadrumaṃ kadalīṣaṇḍaṃ chādayannamitadyutiḥ
|
giriścocchrayamākramya tasthau tatra ca vānaraḥ||4||

5samucchritamahākāyo dvitīya iva parvataḥ
|
tāmrekṣaṇastīkṣṇadaṃṣṭro bhṛkuṭīkuṭilānanaḥ||5||

6dīrghalāṅgūlamāvidhya diśo vyāpya sthitaḥ kapiḥ
|
tadrūpaṃ mahadālakṣya bhrātuḥ kauravanandanaḥ||6||

7visiṣmiye tadā bhīmo jahṛṣe ca punaḥ punaḥ
|
tamarkamiva tejobhiḥ sauvarṇamiva parvatam||7||

8pradīptamiva cākāśaṃ dṛṣṭvā bhīmo nyamīlayat
|
ābabhāṣe ca hanumānbhīmasenaṃ smayanniva||8||

9etāvadiha śaktastvaṃ draṣṭuṃ rūpaṃ mamānagha
|
vardhe'haṃ cāpyato bhūyo yāvanme manasi sthitam
|
bhīmaśatruṣu cātyarthaṃ vardhate mūrtirojasā||9||

10vaiśaṃpāyana uvāca|
tada‌dbhutaṃ mahāraudraṃ vindhyaparvatasaṃnibham
|
dṛṣṭvā hanūmato varṣma saṃmrāntaḥ pavanātmajaḥ||10||

11pratyuvāca tato bhīmaḥ saṃprahṛṣṭatanūruhaḥ
|
kṛtāñjaliradīnātmā hanūmantamavasthitam||11||

12dṛṣṭaṃ pramāṇaṃ vipulaṃ śarīrasyāsya te vibho
|
saṃharasva mahāvīrya svayamātmānamātmanā||12||

13na hi śaknomi tvāṃ draṣṭuṃ divākaramivoditam
|
aprameyamanādhṛṣyaṃ mainākamiva parvatam||13||

14vismayaścaiva me vīra sumahānmanaso'dya vai
|
yadrāmastvayi pārśvasthe svayaṃ rāvaṇamabhyagāt||14||

15tvameva śaktastāṃ laṅkāṃ suyodhāṃ sahavāhanām
|
svabāhubalamāśritya vināśayitumañjasā||15||

16na hi te kiṃcidaprāpyaṃ mārutātmaja vidyate
|
tava naikasya paryāpto rāvaṇaḥ sagaṇo yudhi||16||

17vaiśaṃpāyana uvāca|
evamuktastu bhīmena hanūmānplavagottamaḥ
|
pratyuvāca tato vākyaṃ snigdhagambhīrayā girā||17||

18hanūmānuvāca|
evametanmahābāho yathā vadasi bhārata
|
bhīmasena na paryāpto mamāsau rākṣasādhamaḥ||18||

19mayā tu nihate tasminrāvaṇe lokakaṇṭake
|
kīrtirnaśyedrāghavasya tata etadupekṣitam||19||

20tena vīreṇa taṃ hatvā sagaṇaṃ rākṣasādhamam
|
ānītā svapuraṃ sītā kīrtiścākhyāpitā nṛṣu||20||

21tadgaccha vipulaprajña bhrātuḥ priyahite rataḥ
|
ariṣṭaṃ kṣemamadhvānaṃ vāyunā parirakṣitaḥ||21||

22eṣa panthāḥ kuruśreṣṭha saugandhikavanāya te
|
drakṣyase dhanadodyānaṃ rakṣitaṃ yakṣarākṣasaiḥ||22||

23na ca te tarasā kāryaḥ kusumāvacayaḥ svayam
|
daivatāni hi mānyāni puruṣeṇa viśeṣataḥ||23||

24balihomanamaskārairmantraiśca bharatarṣabha
|
daivatāni prasādaṃ hi bhaktyā kurvanti bhārata||24||

25mā tāta sāhasaṃ kārṣīḥ svadharmaṃ paripālaya
|
svadharmasthaḥ paraṃ dharmaṃ budhyasva gamayasva ca||25||

26na hi dharmamavijñāya vṛddhānanupasevya ca
|
dharmārthai vedituṃ śakyau vṛhaspatisamairapi||26||

27adharmo yatra dharmākhyo dharmaścādharmasaṃjñitaḥ
|
sa vijñeyo vibhāgena yatra muhyantyabuddhayaḥ||27||

28ācārasaṃbhavo dharmo dharme vedāḥ pratiṣṭhitāḥ
|
vedairyajñāḥ samutpannā yajñairdevāḥ pratiṣṭhitāḥ||28||

29vedācāravidhānoktairyaśairdhāryanti devatāḥ
|
bṛhaspatyuśanaḥproktairnayairdhāyanti mānavāḥ||29||

30paṇyākaravaṇijyābhiḥ kṛṣyāgojāvipoṣaṇaiḥ
|
vārtayā dhāryate sarvaṃ dharmairetairdvijātibhiḥ||30||

31trayī vārtā daṇḍanītististro vidyā vijānatām
|
tābhiḥ samyakprayuktābhirlokayātrā vidhīyate||31||

32sā ceddharmakṛtā na syāttrayīdharmamṛte bhuvi
|
daṇḍanītimṛte cāpi nirmaryādamidaṃ bhavet||32||

33vārtādharme hyavartinyo vinaśyeyurimāḥ prajāḥ
|
supravṛttaistribhirhyetairdharmaṃ sūyanti vai prajāḥ||33||

34dvijātīnāmṛtaṃ dharmo hyekaścaivaikavarṇikaḥ
|
yajñādhyayanadānāni trayaḥ sādhāraṇāḥ smṛtāḥ||34||

35yājanādhyāpanaṃ vipre dharmaścaiva pratigrahaḥ
|
pālanaṃ kṣatriyāṇāṃ vai vaiśyadharmaśca poṣaṇam||35||

36śuśrūṣā ca dvijātīnāṃ śūdrāṇāṃ dharma ucyate
|
bhaikṣyahomavratairhīnāstathaiva guruvāsitāḥ||36||

37kṣatradhamarmo'tra kaunteya tava dharmo'tra rakṣaṇam
|
svadharmaṃ pratipadyasva vinīto niyatendriyaḥ||37||

38vṛddhaiḥ saṃmantrya sadbhiśca buddhimadbhiḥ śrutānvitaiḥ
|
āsthitaḥ śāsti daṇḍena vyasanī paribhūyate||38||

39nigrahānugrahaiḥ samyagyadā rājā pravartate
|
tadā bhavanti lokasya maryādāḥ suvyavasthitāḥ||39||

40tasmāddeśe ca durge ca śatrumitrabaleṣu ca
|
nityaṃ cāreṇa boddhavyaṃ sthānaṃ vṛddhiḥ kṣayastathā||40||

41rājñāmupāyaścāraśca buddhimantraparākramāḥ
|
nigrahapragrahau caiva dākṣyaṃ vai kāryasādhakam||41||

42sāmnā dānena bhedena daṇḍenopekṣaṇena ca
|
sādhanīyāni karmāṇi samāsavyāsayogataḥ||42||

43mantramūlā nayāḥ sarve cārāśca bharatarṣabha
|
sumantritena yā siddhistāṃ dvijaiḥ saha mantrayet||43||

44striyā mūḍhena bālena lubdhena laghunāpi vā
|
na mantrayīta guhyāni yeṣu conmādalakṣaṇam||44||

45mantrayetsaha vidvadbhiḥ śaktaiḥ karmāṇi kārayet
|
snigdhaiśca nītivinyāsānmūrkhānsarvatra varjayet||45||

46dhārmikāndharmakāryeṣu arthakāryeṣu paṇḍitān
|
strīṣu klībānniyuñjīta krūrānkrūreṣu karmasu||46||

47svebhyaścaiva parebhyaśca kāryākāryasamudbhavā
|
buddhiḥ karmasu vijñeyā ripūṇāṃ ca balābalam||47||

48buddhyā svapratipanneṣu kuryātsādhuṣvanugraham
|
nigrahaṃ cāpyaśiṣṭeṣu nirmaryādeṣu kārayet||48||

49nigrahe pragrahe samyagyadā rājā pravartate
|
tadā bhavati lokasya maryādā suvyavasthitā||49||

50eṣa te'bhihitaḥ pārtha ghoro dharmo duranvayaḥ
|
taṃ svadharmavibhāgena vinayastho'nupālaya||50||

51tapodharmadamejyābhirviprā yānti yathā divam
|
dānātithyakriyādharmairyānti vaiśyāśca sadgatim||51||

52kṣatraṃ yāti tathā svargaṃ bhuvi nigrahapālanaiḥ
|
samyakpraṇītadaṇḍā hi kāmadveṣavivarjitāḥ
|
alubdhā vigatakrodhāḥ satāṃ yānti salokatām||52||
