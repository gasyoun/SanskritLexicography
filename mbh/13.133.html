Mahābhārata 13.133 (Anuśāsanaparvan)

1maheśvara uvāca|
sāramuddhṛtya yuṣmābhiḥ sādhudharma udāhṛtaḥ
|
dharmaguhyamidaṃ mattaḥ śṛṇudhvaṃ sarva eva ha ||1||

2yeṣāṃ dharmāśritā buddhiḥ śraddadhānāśca ye narāḥ
|
teṣāṃ syādupadeṣṭavyaḥ sarahasyo mahāphalaḥ ||2||

3nirudvignastu yo dadyānmāsamekaṃ gavāhnikam
|
ekabhaktaṃ tathā'śnīyācchrūyatāṃ tasya yatphalam ||3||

4imā gāvo mahābhāgāḥ pavitraṃ paramaṃ smṛtāḥ
|
trīnlokāndhārayanti sma sadevāsuramānuṣān ||4||

5tāsu caiva mahāpuṇyaṃ śuśrūṣā ca mahāphalam
|
ahanyahani dharmeṇa yujyate vai gavāhnikaḥ ||5||

6mayā hyetā hyanujñātāḥ pūrvamāsankṛte yuge
|
tato'hamanunīto vai brahmaṇā padmayoninā ||6||

7tasmādvrajasthānagatastiṣṭhatyupari me vṛṣaḥ
|
rame'haṃ saha gobhiśca tasmātpūjyāḥ sadaiva tāḥ ||7||

8mahāprabhāvā varadā varaṃ dadyurupāsitāḥ
|
tā gāvo'syānumanyante sarvakarmasu yatphalam ||8||

9tasya tatra caturbhāgo yo dadāti gavāhnikam ||9||
