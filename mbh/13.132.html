Mahābhārata 13.132 (Anuśāsanaparvan)

1bhīṣma uvāca|
tataḥ padmapratīkāśaḥ padmodbhūtaḥ pitāmahaḥ
|
uvāca vacanaṃ devānvāsavaṃ ca śacīpatim ||1||

2ayaṃ mahābalo nāgo rasātalacaro balī
|
tejasvī reṇuko nāma mahāsattvaparākramaḥ ||2||

3atitejasvinaḥ sarve mahāvīryā mahāgajāḥ
|
dhārayanti mahīṃ kṛtsnāṃ saśailavanakānanām ||3||

4bhavadbhiḥ samanujñāto reṇukastānmahāgajān
|
dharmaguhyāni sarvāṇi gatvā pṛcchatu tatra vai ||4||

5pitāmahavacaḥ śrutvā te devā reṇukaṃ tadā
|
preṣayāmāsuravyagrā yatra te dharaṇīdharāḥ ||5||

6reṇuka uvāca|
anujñāto'smi devaiśca pitṛbhiśca mahāvalāḥ
|
dharmaguhyāni yuṣmākaṃ śrotumicchāmi tattvataḥ
|
kathayadhvaṃ mahābhāgā yadvastattvaṃ manīṣitam||6||

7diggajā ūcuḥ |
kārtike māsi cāśleṣā bahulasyāṣṭamī śivā
|
tena nakṣatrayogena yo dadāti guḍaudanam ||7||

8imaṃ mantraṃ japañchrāddhe yatāhāro hyakopanaḥ
|
baladevaprabhṛtayo ye nāgā balavattarāḥ ||8||

9anantā hyakṣayā nityaṃ bhoginaḥ sumahābalāḥ
|
teṣāṃ kulodbhavā ye ca mahābhūtā bhujaṅgamāḥ ||9||

10te me baliṃ prayacchantu calatejobhivṛddhaye
|
yadā nārāyaṇaḥ śrīmānujjahāra vasuṃdharām ||10||

11tadbalaṃ tasya devasya dharāmuddharatastathā
|
evamuktvā baliṃ tatra valmīke tu nivedayet ||11||

12gajendrakusumākīrṇaṃ nīlavastrānulepanam
|
nirvapettaṃ tu valmīke astaṃ yāte divākare ||12||

13evaṃ tuṣṭāstataḥ sarve adhastādbhārapīḍitāḥ
|
śramaṃ taṃ nāvabudhyāmo dhārayanto vasuṃdharām ||13||

14evaṃ manyāmahe sarve bhārārtā nirapekṣiṇaḥ
|
brāhmaṇaḥ kṣatriyo vaiśyaḥ śūdro vā yadyupoṣitaḥ ||14||

15evaṃ saṃvatsaraṃ kṛtvā dānaṃ bahuphalaṃ labhet
|
valmīke balimādāya tanno bahuphalaṃ matam ||15||

16ye ca nāgā mahāvīryāstriṣu lokeṣu kṛtsnaśaḥ
|
kṛtātithyā bhaveyuste śataṃ varṣāṇi tattvataḥ ||16||

17diggajānāṃ ca tacchrutvā devatāḥ pitarastathā
|
ṛṣayaśca mahābhāgāḥ pūjayanti sma reṇukam ||17||
