Mahābhārata 13.55 (Anuśāsanaparvan)

1cyavana uvāca|
varaśca gṛhyatāṃ matto yaśca te saṃśayo hṛdi
|
taṃ prabūhi naraśreṣṭha sarvaṃ sampādayāmi te ||1||

2kuśika uvāca|
yadi prīto'si bhagavaṃstato me vada bhārgava
|
kāraṇaṃ śrotumicchāmi madgṛhe vāsakāritam ||2||

3śayanaṃ caikapārśvena divasānekaviṃśatim
|
akiñciduktvā gamanaṃ bahiśca munipuṅgava ||3||

4antardhānamakasmācca punareva ca darśanam
|
punaśca śayanaṃ vipra divasānekaviṃśatim ||4||

5tailābhyaktasya gamanaṃ bhojanaṃ ca gṛhe mama
|
samupānīya vividhaṃ yaddagdhaṃ jātavedasā ||5||

6niryāṇaṃ ca rathenāśu sahasā yatkṛtaṃ tvayā
|
dhanānāṃ ca visargasya vanasyāpi ca darśanam ||6||

7prāsādānāṃ bahūnāṃ ca kāñcanānāṃ mahāmune
|
maṇividrumapādānāṃ paryaṅkāṇāṃ ca darśanam ||7||

8punaścādarśanaṃ tasya śrotumicchāmi kāraṇam
|
atīva hyatra muhyāmi cintayāno bhṛgūdvaha ||8||

9na caivātrādhigacchāmi sarvasyāsya viniścayam
|
etadicchāmi kārtsnyena satyaṃ śrotuṃ tapodhana ||9||

10cyavana uvāca|
śṛṇu sarvamaśeṣeṇa yadidaṃ yena hetunā
|
na hi śakyamanākhyātumevaṃ pṛṣṭena pārthiva ||10||

11pitāmahasya vadataḥ purā devasamāgame
|
śrutavānasmi yadrājaṃstanme nigadataḥ śṛṇu ||11||

12brahmakṣatravirodhena bhavitā kulasaṅkaraḥ
|
pautraste bhavitā rājaṃstejovīryasamanvitaḥ ||12||

13tataste kulanāśārthamahaṃ tvāṃ samupāgataḥ
|
cikīrṣankuśikocchedaṃ sandidhakṣuḥ kulaṃ tava ||13||

14tato'hamāgamya pure tvāmavocaṃ mahīpate
|
niyamaṃ kañcidārapsye śuśrūṣā kriyatāmiti ||14||

15na ca te duṣkṛtaṃ kiñcidahamāsādayaṃ gṛhe
|
tena jīvasi rājarṣe na bhavethāstvamanyathā ||15||

16evaṃ buddhiṃ samāsthāya divasānekaviṃśatim
|
supto'smi yadi māṃ kaścidbodhayoditi pārthiva ||16||

17yadā tvayā sabhāryeṇa saṃsupto na prabodhitaḥ
|
ahaṃ tadaiva te prīto manasā rājasattama ||17||

18utthāya cāsmi niṣkrāṃto yadi māṃ tvaṃ mahīpate
|
pṛccheḥ kva yāsyasītyevaṃ śapeyaṃ tvāmiti prabho ||18||

19antarhitaḥ punaścāsmi punareva ca te gṛhe
|
yogamāsthāya saṃsupto divasānekaviṃśatim ||19||

20kṣudhitau māmasūyethāṃ śramādveti narādhipa
|
evaṃ buddhiṃ samāsthāya karśitau vāṃ kṣudhā mayā ||20||

21na ca te'bhūtsusūkṣmo'pi manyurmanasi pārthiva
|
sabhāryasya naraśreṣṭha tena te prītimānaham ||21||

22bhojanaṃ ca samānāyya yattadā dīpitaṃ mayā
|
kruddhyethā yadi mātsaryāditi tanmarṣitaṃ ca me ||22||

23tato'haṃ rathamāruhya tvāmavocaṃ narādhipa
|
sabhāryo māṃ vahasveti tacca tvaṃ kṛtavāṃstathā ||23||

24aviśaṅko narapate prīto'haṃ cāpi tena ha
|
dhanotsarge'pi ca kṛte na tvāṃ krodhaḥ pradharṣayat ||24||

25tataḥ prītena te rājan punaretatkṛtaṃ tava
|
sabhāryasya vanaṃ bhūyastadviddhi manujādhipa ||25||

26prītyarthaṃ tava caitanme svargasandarśanaṃ kṛtam
|
yatte vane'sminnṛpate dṛṣṭaṃ divyaṃ nidarśanam ||26||

27svargoddeśastvayā rājan saśarīreṇa pārthiva
|
muhūrtamanubhūto'sau sabhāryeṇa nṛpottama ||27||

28nidarśanārthaṃ tapaso dharmasya ca narādhipa
|
tatra yā''sītspṛhā rājaṃstaccāpi viditaṃ mayā ||28||

29brāhmaṇyaṃ kāṅkṣase hi tvaṃ tapaśca pṛthivīpate
|
avamanya narendratvaṃ devendratvaṃ ca pārthiva ||29||

30evametadyathā''ttha tvaṃ brāhmaṇyaṃ tāta durlabham
|
brāhmaṇe sati carṣitvamṛṣitve ca tapasvitā ||30||

31bhaviṣyatyeṣa te kāmaḥkuśikātkauśiko dvijaḥ
|
tṛtīyaṃ puruṣaṃ tubhyaṃ brāhmaṇatvaṃ gamiṣyati ||31||

32vaṃśaste pārthivaśreṣṭha bhṛgūṇāmeva tejasā
|
pautraste bhavitā vipra tapasvī pāvakadyutiḥ ||32||

33yaḥ sa devamanuṣyāṇāṃ bhayamutpādayiṣyati
|
trayāṇāmeva lokānāṃ satyametadbravīmi te ||33||

34varaṃ gṛhāṇa rājarṣe yatte manasi vartate
|
tīrthayātrāṃ gamiṣyāmi purā kālo'bhivartate ||34||

35kuśika uvāca|
eṣa eva varo me'dya yastvaṃ prīto mahāmune
|
bhavatvetadyathā''ttha tvaṃ bhavetpautro mamānagha ||35||

36brāhmaṇyaṃ me kulasyāstu bhagavanneṣa me varaḥ
|
punaścākhyātumicchāmi bhagavanvistareṇa vai ||36||

37kathameṣyati vipratvaṃ kulaṃ me bhṛgunandana
|
kaścāsau bhavitā bandhurmama kaścāpi saṃmataḥ ||37||
