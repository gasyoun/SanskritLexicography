Mahābhārata 3.105 (Āraṇyakaparvan)

1lomaśa uvāca|
samudraṃ sa samāsādya vāruṇirbhagavānṛṣiḥ
|
uvāca sahitāndevānṛṣīṃścaiva samāgatān||1||

2ahaṃ lokahitārthaṃ vai pibāmi varuṇālayam
|
bhavadbhiryada‌nuṣṭheyaṃ tacchīghraṃ saṃvidhīyatām||2||

3etāvaduktvā vacanaṃ maitrāvaruṇiracyutaḥ
|
samudramapibatkruddhaḥ sarvalokasya paśyataḥ||3||

4pīyamānaṃ samudraṃ taṃ dṛṣṭvā sendrāstadāmarāḥ
|
vismayaṃ paramaṃ jagmuḥ stutibhiścāpyapūjayan||4||

5tvaṃ nastrātā vidhātā ca lokānāṃ lokabhāvana
|
tvatprasādātsamucchedaṃ na gacchetsāmaraṃ jagat||5||

6sa pūjyamānastrida‌śairmahātmā gandharvatūryeṣu nadatsu sarvaśaḥ
|
divyaiśca puṣpairavakīryamāṇo mahārṇavaṃ niḥsalilaṃ cakāra||6||

7dṛṣṭvā kṛtaṃ niḥsalilaṃ mahārṇavaṃ surāḥ samastāḥ paramaprahṛṣṭāḥ
|
pragṛhya divyāni varāyudhāni tāndānavāñjaghnuradīnasattvāḥ||7||

8te vadhyamānāstridaśairmahātmābhirmahābalairvegibhirunnadadbhiḥ
|
na sehire vegavatāṃ mahātmanāṃ vegaṃ tadā dhārayituṃ divaukasām||8||

9te vadhyamānāstridaśairdānavā bhīmaniḥsvanāḥ
|
cakruḥ sutumulaṃ yuddhaṃ muhūrtamiva bhārata||9||

10te pūrvaṃ tapasā dagdhā munibhirbhāvitātmabhiḥ
|
yatamānāḥ paraṃ śaktyā tridaśairviniṣūditāḥ||10||

11te hemaniṣkābharaṇāḥ kuṇḍalāṅgadadhāriṇaḥ
|
nihatā bahvaśobhanta puṣpitā iva kiṃśukāḥ||11||

12hataśeṣāstataḥ kecitkāleyā manujottama
|
vidārya vasudhāṃ devīṃ pātālatalamāsthitāḥ||12||

13nihatāndānavāndṛṣṭvā tridaśā munipuṅgavam
|
tuṣṭuvurvividhairvākyairidaṃ vacanamabruvan||13||

14tvatprasādānmahābāho lokaiḥ prāptaṃ mahatsukham
|
tvattejasā ca nihatāḥ kāleyāḥ krūravikramāḥ||14||

15pūrayasva mahābāho samudraṃ lokabhāvana
|
yattvayā salilaṃ pītaṃ tadasminpunarutsṛja||15||

16evamuktaḥ pratyuvāca bhagavānmunipuṅgavaḥ
|
jīrṇaṃ taddhi mayā toyamupāyo'nyaḥ pracintyatām||16||

17pūraṇārthaṃ samudrasya bhavadbhiryatnamāsthitaiḥ
|
etacchrutvā tu vacanaṃ maharṣerbhāvitātmanaḥ||17||

18vismitāśca viṣaṇṇāca babhūvuḥ sahitāḥ surāḥ
|
parasparamanujñāpya praṇamya munipuṅgavam||18||

19prajāḥ sarvā mahārāja viprajagmuryathāgatam
|
tridaśā viṣṇunā sārthamupajagmuḥ pitāmaham||19||

20pūraṇārthaṃ samudrasya mantrayitvā punaḥ punaḥ
|
ūcuḥ prāñjalayaḥ sarve sāgarasyābhipūraṇam||20||
