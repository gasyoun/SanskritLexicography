Mahābhārata 8.36 (Karṇaparvan)

1duryodhana uvāca|
ayaṃ te karṇa sārathyaṃ madrarājaḥ kariṣyati
|
kṛṣṇādabhyadhiko yantā deveśasyeva mātaliḥ ||1||

2yathā harihayairyuktaṃ saṃgṛhṇāti sa mātaliḥ
|
śalyastathā tavādyāyaṃ saṃyantā rathavājinām ||2||

3yodhe tvayi rathasthe ca madrarāje ca sārathau
|
rathaśreṣṭho dhruvaṃ saṃkhye pārthānabhibhaviṣyati ||3||

4sañjaya uvāca|
tato duryodhano bhūyo madrarājaṃ tarasvinam
|
uvāca rājansaṃgrāme'dhyuṣite paryupasthite ||4||

5karṇasya yaccha saṃgrāme madrarāja hayottamān
|
tvayā'bhigupto rādheyo vijeṣyati dhanañjayam ||5||

6ityukto rathamāsthāya tatheti prāha bhārata
|
śalye'bhyupagate karṇaḥ sārathiṃ sumanā'bravīt ||6||

7tvaṃ sūta syandanaṃ mahyaṃ kalpayetyasakṛttvaran
|
tato jaitraṃ rathavaraṃ gandharvanagaropamam ||7||

8vidhivatkalpitaṃ bhadraṃ jayetyuktvā nyavedayat
|
taṃ rathaṃ rathināṃ śreṣṭhaḥ karṇo'bhyarcya yathāvidhi ||8||

9saṃpāditaṃ brahmavidā pūrvameva purodhasā
|
kṛtvā pradakṣiṇaṃ yatnādupasthāya ca bhāskaram ||9||

10samīpasthaṃ madrarājamāroha tvamathābravīt
|
tataḥ karṇasya durdharṣaṃ syandanapravaraṃ mahat ||10||

11āruroha mahātejāḥ śalyaḥ siṃha ivācalam
|
tataḥ śalyāśritaṃ dṛṣṭvā karṇaḥ svaṃ rathamuttamam ||11||

12adhyatiṣṭhadyathā'mbhodaṃ vidyutvantaṃ divākaraḥ
|
tāvekarathamārūḍhāvādityāgnisamatviṣau ||12||

13abhrājetāṃ yathā meghaṃ sūryāgnī sahitau divi
|
saṃstūyamānau tau vīrau tadāstāṃ dyutimattamau ||13||

14ṛtviksadasyairindrāgnī stūyamānāvivādhvare
|
sa śalyasaṃgṛhītāśve rathe karṇaḥ sthito babhau ||14||

15dhanurvisphārayan ghoraṃ pariveṣīva bhāskaraḥ
|
āsthitaḥ sa rathaśreṣṭhaṃ karṇaḥ śaragabhastimān ||15||

16prababhau puruṣavyāghro mandrastha ivāṃśumān
|
taṃ rathasthaṃ mahābāhuṃ yuddhāyāmitatejasam ||16||

17duryodhanastu rādheyamidaṃ vacanamabravīt
|
akṛtaṃ droṇabhīṣmābhyāṃ duṣkaraṃ karma saṃyuge ||17||

18kuruṣvādhirathe vīra miṣatāṃ sarvadhanvinām
|
manogataṃ mama hyāsīdbhīṣmadroṇau mahārathau ||18||

19arjunaṃ bhīmasenaṃ ca nihantārāviti dhruvam
|
tābhyāṃ yadakṛtaṃ vīra vīrakarma mahāmṛdhe ||19||

20tat karma kuru rādheya vajrapāṇirivāparaḥ
|
gṛhāṇa dharmarājaṃ vā jahi vā tvaṃ dhanañjayam ||20||

21bhīmasenaṃ ca rādheya mādrīputrau yamāvapi
|
jayaśca te'stu bhadraṃ te prayāhi puruṣarṣabha ||21||

22pāṇḍuputrasya sainyāni kuru sarvāṇi bhasmasāt
|
tatastūryasahasrāṇi bherīṇāmayutāni ca ||22||

23vādyamānānyarājanta meghaśabdo yathā divi
|
pratigṛhya tu tadvākyaṃ rathastho rathasattamaḥ ||23||

24abhyabhāṣata rādheyaḥ śalyaṃ yuddhaviśāradam
|
codayāśvānmahābāho yāvaddhanmi dhanañjayam ||24||

25bhīmasenaṃ yamau cobhau rājānaṃ ca yudhiṣṭhiram
|
adya paśyatu me śalya bāhuvīryaṃ dhanañjayaḥ ||25||

26asyataḥ kaṅkapatrāṇāṃ sahasrāṇi śatāni ca
|
adya kṣepsyāmyahaṃ śalya śarānparamatejanān ||26||

27pāṇḍavānāṃ vināśāya duryodhanajayāya ca
|
śalya uvāca|
sūtaputra kathaṃ nu tvaṃ pāṇḍavānavamanyase ||27||

28sarvāstrajñānmaheṣvāsān sarvāneva mahābalān
|
anivartino mahābhāgānajayyānsatyavikramān ||28||

29api santanayeyurye bhayaṃ sākṣācchatakratoḥ
|
yadā śroṣyasi nirghoṣaṃ visphūrjitamivāśaneḥ ||29||

30rādheya gāṇḍivasyājau tadā naivaṃ vadiṣyasi
|
yadā drakṣyasi bhīmena kuñjarānīkamāhave ||30||

31viśīrṇadantaṃ nihataṃ tadā naivaṃ vadiṣyasi
|
yadā drakṣyasi saṃgrāme dharmaputraṃ yamau tathā ||31||

32śitaiḥ pṛṣatkaiḥ kurvāṇānabhracchāyāmivāmbare
|
asyataḥ kṣiṇvataścārīn laghuhastāndurāsadān
|
pārthivānapi cānyāṃstvaṃ tadā naivaṃ vadiṣyasi ||32||

33sañjaya uvāca|
anādṛtya tu tadvākyaṃ madrarājena bhāṣitam
|
yāhītyevābravītkarṇo madrarājaṃ tarasvinam ||33||
