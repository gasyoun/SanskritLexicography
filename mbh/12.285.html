Mahābhārata 12.285 (Śāntiparvan)

1yudhiṣṭhira uvāca|
adhyātmaṃ nāma yadidaṃ puruṣasyeha vidyate
|
yadadhyātmaṃ yataścaiva tanme brūhi pitāmaha ||1||

2bhīṣma uvāca|
sarvajñānaṃ paraṃ buddhyā yanmāṃ tvamanupṛcchasi
|
tadvyākhyāsyāmi te tāta tasya vyākhyāmimāṃ śṛṇu ||2||

3pṛthivī vāyurākāśamāpo jyotiśca pañcamam
|
mahābhūtāni bhūtānāṃ sarveṣāṃ prabhavāpyayau ||3||

4sa teṣāṃ guṇasaṃghātaḥ śarīraṃ bharatarṣabha
|
satataṃ hi pralīyante guṇāste prabhavanti ca ||4||

5tataḥ sṛṣṭāni bhūtāni tāni yānti punaḥ punaḥ
|
mahābhūtāni bhūtebhya ūrmayaḥ sāgare yathā ||5||

6prasārayitvehāṅgāni kūrmaḥ saṃharate yathā
|
tadvadbhūtāni bhūtānāmalpīyāṃsi sthavīyasām ||6||

7ākāśātkhalu yo ghoṣaḥ saṃghātastu mahīguṇaḥ
|
vāyoḥ prāṇo rasastvadbhyo rūpaṃ tejasa ucyate ||7||

8ityetanmayamevaitatsarvaṃ sthāvarajaṅgamam
|
pralaye ca tamabhyeti tasmāduddiśyate punaḥ ||8||

9mahābhūtāni pañcaiva sarvabhūteṣu bhūtakṛt
|
viṣayānkalpayāmāsa yasminyadanupaśyati ||9||

10śabdaśrotre tathā khāni trayamākāśayonijam
|
rasaḥ snehaśca jihvā ca apāmete guṇāḥ smṛtāḥ ||10||

11rūpaṃ cakṣurvipākaśca trividhaṃ jyotirucyate
|
ghreyaṃ ghrāṇaṃ śarīraṃ ca ete bhūmiguṇāḥ smṛtāḥ ||11||

12prāṇaḥ sparśaśca ceṣṭā ca vāyorete guṇāḥ smṛtāḥ
|
iti sarvaguṇā rājan vyākhyātāḥ pāñcabhautikāḥ ||12||

13sattvaṃ rajastamaḥ kālaḥ karma buddhiśca bhārata
|
manaḥṣaṣṭhāni caiteṣu īśvaraḥ samakalpayat ||13||

14yadūrdhvaṃ pādatalayoravāṅmūrdhnaśca paśyasi
|
etasminneva kṛtsneyaṃ vartate buddhirantare ||14||

15indriyāṇi nare pañca ṣaṣṭhaṃ tu mana ucyate
|
saptamīṃ buddhimevāhuḥ kṣetrajñaḥ punaraṣṭamaḥ ||15||

16indriyāṇi ca kartā ca vicetavyāni bhāgaśaḥ
|
tamaḥ sattvaṃ rajaścaiva te'pi bhāvāstadāśrayāḥ ||16||

17cakṣurālocanāyaiva saṃśayaṃ kurute manaḥ
|
buddhiradhyavasānāya sākṣī kṣetrajña ucyate ||17||

18tamaḥ sattvaṃ rajati kālaḥ karma ca bhārata
|
guṇairnenīyate buddhirbuddhirevendriyāṇi ca
|
manaḥṣaṣṭhāni sarvāṇi buddhyabhāve kuto gaṇāḥ ||18||

19yena paśyati taccakṣuḥ śṛṇvatī śrotramucyate
|
jighratī bhavati ghrāṇaṃ rasatī rasanā rasān ||19||

20sparśanaṃ sparśatī sparśānbuddhirvikriyate'sakṛt
|
yadā prārthayate kiṃcittadā bhavati sā manaḥ ||20||

21adhiṣṭhānāni buddhyā hi pṛthagetāni pañcadhā
|
indriyāṇīti tānyāhusteṣu duṣṭeṣu duṣyati ||21||

22puruṣe tiṣṭhatī buddhistriṣu bhāveṣu vartate
|
kadācillabhate prītiṃ kadācidapi śocati ||22||

23na sukhena na duḥkhena kadācidapi vartate
|
seyaṃ bhāvātmikā bhāvāṃstrīnetānparivartate ||23||

24saritāṃ sāgaro bhartā yathā velāmivormivān
|
iti bhāvagatā buddhirbhāve manasi vartate ||24||

25pravartamānaṃ tu rajastadbhāvenānuvartate
|
praharṣaḥ prītirānandaḥ sukhaṃ saṃśāntacittatā ||25||

26kathañcidupapadyante puruṣe sāttvikā guṇāḥ
|
paridāhastathā śokaḥ santāpo'pūrtirakṣamā ||26||

27liṅgāni rajasastāni dṛśyante hetvahetubhiḥ
|
avidyā rāgamohau ca pramādaḥ stabdhatā bhayam ||27||

28asamṛddhistathā dainyaṃ pramohaḥ svapnatandritā
|
kathañcidupavartante vividhāstāmasā guṇāḥ ||28||

29tatra yatprītisaṃyuktaṃ kāye manasi vā bhavet
|
vartate sāttviko bhāva ityupekṣeta tattathā ||29||

30atha yadduḥkhasaṃyuktamaprītikaramātmanaḥ
|
pravṛttaṃ raja ityeva tadasaṃrabhya cintayet ||30||

31atha yanmohasaṃyuktaṃ kāye manasi vā bhavet
|
apratarkyamavijñeyaṃ tamastadupadhārayet ||31||

32iti buddhigatīḥ sarvā vyākhyātā yāvatīriha
|
etadbuddhvā bhavedbuddhaḥ kimanyadbuddhalakṣaṇam ||32||

33sattvakṣetrajñayoretadantaraṃ viddhi sūkṣmayoḥ
|
sṛjate'tra guṇāneka eko na sṛjate guṇān ||33||

34pṛthagbhūtau prakṛtyā tu samprayuktau ca sarvadā
|
yathā matsyo'dbhiranyaḥ syātsamprayukto bhavettathā ||34||

35na guṇā vidurātmānaṃ sa guṇānveda sarvataḥ
|
paridraṣṭā guṇanāṃ tu saṃsraṣṭā manyate yathā||35||

36āśrayo nāsti sattvasya guṇasargeṇa cetanā
|
sattvamasya sṛjantyanye guṇānveda kadācana ||36||

37sṛjate hi guṇānsattvaṃ kṣetrajñaḥ paripaśyati
|
samprayogastayoreṣa sattvakṣetrajñayordhruvaḥ ||37||

38indriyaistu pradīpārthaṃ kriyate buddhirantarā
|
niścakṣurbhirajānādbhirindriyāṇi pradīpavat ||38||

39evaṃsvabhāvamevaitattadbuddhvā viharannaraḥ
|
aśocannaprahaṣyaṃśca sa vai vigatamatsaraḥ ||39||

40svabhāvasiddhamevaitadyadimānsṛjate guṇān
|
urṇanābhiryathā sūtraṃ vijñeyāstantuvadguṇāḥ ||40||

41pradhvastā na nivartante pravṛttirnopalabhyate
|
evameke vyavasyanti nivṛttiriti cāpare ||41||

42itīdaṃ hṛdayagranthiṃ buddhicintāmayaṃ dṛḍham
|
vimucya sukhamāsīta viśokaśchinnasaṃśayaḥ ||42||

43tāmyeyuḥ pracyutāḥ pṛthvīṃ mohapūrṇāṃ nadīṃ narāḥ
|
yathā gādhamavidvāṃso buddhiyogamayaṃ tathā ||43||

44naiva tāmyanti vidvāṃsaḥ plavantaḥ pāramambhasaḥ
|
adhyātmaviduṣo dhīrā jñānaṃ tu paramaṃ plavaḥ ||44||

45na bhavati viduṣāṃ mahadbhayaṃ yadaviduṣāṃ sumahadbhayaṃ bhavet
|
na hi gatiradhikā'sti kasyacitsakṛdupadarśayatīha tulyatām ||45||

46yatkaroti bahudoṣamekatastacca dūṣayati yatpurā kṛtam
|
nāpriyaṃ tadubhayaṃ karotyasau yacca dūṣayati yatkaroti ca ||46||
