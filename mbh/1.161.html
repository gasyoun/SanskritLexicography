Mahābhārata 1.161 (Ādiparvan)

1kuntyuvāca|
na viṣādastvayā kāryo bhayādasmātkathaṃcana
|
upāyaḥ paridṛṣṭo'tra tasmānmokṣāya rakṣasaḥ||1||

2ekastavasuto bālaḥ kanyā caikā tapasvinī
|
na caitayostathā patnyā gamanaṃ tava rocaye||2||

3mama pañca sutā brahmaṃsteṣāmeko gamiṣyati
|
tvadarthaṃ balimādāya tasya pāpasya rakṣasaḥ||3||

4brāhmaṇa uvāca|
nāhametatkariṣyāmi jīvitārthī kathaṃcana
|
brāhmaṇasyātitheścaiva svārthe prāṇānviyojayan||4||

5na tvetadakulīnāsu nādharmiṣṭhāsu vidyate
|
yadbrāhmamārthaṃ visṛjedātmānamapi cātmajam||5||

6ātmanastu mayā śreyo boddhavyamiti rocate
|
brahmavadhyātmavadhyā vā śreyānātmavadho mama||6||

7brahmavadhyā paraṃ pāpaṃ niṣkṛtirnātra vidyate
|
abuddhipūrvaṃ kṛtvāpi varamātmavadho mama||7||

8na tvahaṃ vadhamākāṅkṣe svayamevātmanaḥ śubhe
|
paraiḥ kṛte vadhe pāpaṃ na kiṃcinmayi vidyate||8||

9abhisandhikṛte tasminbrāhmaṇasya vadhe mayā
|
niṣkṛtiṃ na prapaśyāmi nṛśaṃsaṃ kṣudrameva ca||9||

10āgatasya gṛhaṃ tyāgastathaiva śaraṇārthinaḥ
|
yācamānasya ca vadho nṛśaṃso garhito budhaiḥ||10||

11kuryānna ninditaṃ karma na nṛśaṃsaṃ kathaṃcana
|
iti pūrve mahātmāna āpaddharmavido viduḥ||11||

12śreyāṃstu sahadārasya vināśo'dya mama svayam
|
brāhmaṇasya vadhaṃ nāhamanumaṃsye kadācana||12||

13kuntyuvāca|
mamāpyeṣā matirbrahmanviprā rakṣyā iti sthirā
|
na cāpyaniṣṭaḥ putro me yadi putraśataṃ bhavet||13||

14na cāsau rākṣasaḥ śakto mama putravināśane
|
vīryamanmantrasiddhaśca tejasvī ca suto mama||14||

15rākṣasāya ca tatsarvaṃ prāpayiṣyati bhojanam
|
mokṣayiṣyati cātmānamiti me niścitā matiḥ||15||

16samāgatāśca vīreṇa dṛṣṭapūrvāśca rākṣasāḥ
|
balavanto mahākāyā nihatāścāpyanekaśaḥ||16||

17na tvidaṃ keṣucidbrahmānvyāhartavyaṃ kathaṃcana
|
vidyārthino hi me putrānviprakuryuḥ kutūhalāt||17||

18guruṇā cānanujñāto grāhayedyatsuto mama
|
na sa kuryāttathā kāryaṃ vidyayeti satāṃ matam||18||

19evamuktastu pṛthayā sa vipro bhāryayā saha
|
hṛṣṭaḥ saṃpūjayāmāsa tadvākyamamṛtopamam||19||

20tataḥ kuntī ca vipraśca sahitāvanilātmajam
|
tamabrūtāṃ kuruṣveti sa tathetyabravīcca tau||20||
