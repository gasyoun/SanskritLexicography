Mahābhārata 13.165 (Anuśāsanaparvan)

1vaiśampāyana uvāca|
śaratalpagataṃ bhīṣmaṃ pāṇḍavo'tha kurūdvahaḥ
|
yudhiṣṭhiro hitaṃ prepsurapṛcchatkalmaṣāpaham ||1||

2yudhiṣṭhira uvāca|
kiṃ śreyaḥ puruṣasyeha kiṃ kurvansukhamedhate
|
vipāpmā sa bhavetkena kiṃ vā kalmaṣanāśanam ||2||

3vaiśampāyana uvāca|
tasmai śuśrūṣamāṇāya bhūyaḥ śāntanavastadā
|
daivaṃ vaṃśaṃ yathānyāyamācaṣṭa puruṣarṣabha ||3||

4bhīṣma uvāca|
ayaṃ daivatavaṃśo vai ṛṣivaṃśasamanvitaḥ
|
trisandhyaṃ paṭhitaḥ putra kalmaṣāpaharaḥ paraḥ ||4||

5yadahnā kurute pāpamindriyaiḥ puruṣaścaran
|
buddhipūrvamabuddhirvā rātrau yaccāpi sandhyayoḥ ||5||

6mucyate sarvapāpebhyaḥ kīrtayanvai śuciḥ sadā
|
nāndho na badhiraḥ kāle kurute svastimānsadā ||6||

7tiryagyoniṃ na gacchecca narakaṃ saṅkarāṇi ca
|
na ca duḥkhabhayaṃ tasya maraṇe sa na muhyati ||7||

8devāsuragururdevaḥ sarvabhūtanamaskṛtaḥ
|
acintyo'thāpyanirdeśyaḥ sarvaprāṇo hyayonijaḥ ||8||

9pitāmaho jagannāthaḥ sāvitrī brahmaṇaḥ satī
|
vedabhūratha kartā ca viṣṇurnārāyaṇaḥ prabhuḥ ||9||

10umāpatirvirūpākṣaḥ skandaḥ senāpatistathā
|
viśākho hutabhugvāyuścandrasūryau prabhā karau ||10||

11śakraḥ śacīpatirdevo yamo dhūmorṇayā saha
|
varuṇaḥ saha gauryā ca saha ṛddhyā dhaneśvaraḥ ||11||

12saumyā gauḥ surabhirdevī viśravāśca mahānṛṣiḥ
|
saṅkalpaḥ sāgaro gaṅgā sravantyo'tha marudgaṇaḥ ||12||

13vālakhilyāstapaḥsiddhāḥ kṛṣṇadvaipāyanastathā
|
nāradaḥ parvataścaiva viśvāvasurhahāhuhūḥ ||13||

14tumburuścitrasenaśca devadūtaśca viśrutaḥ
|
devakanyā mahābhāgā divyāścāpsarasāṃ gaṇāḥ ||14||

15urvaśī menakā raṃbhā miśrakeśī hyalaṃbuṣā
|
viśvācī ca ghṛtācī ca pañcacūḍā tilottamā ||15||

16ādityā vasavo rudrāḥ sāśvinaḥ pitaropi ca
|
dharmaḥ śrutaṃ tapo dīkṣā vyavasāyaḥ pitāmahaḥ ||16||

17śarvaryo divasāścaiva mārīcaḥ kaśyapastathā
|
śukro bṛhaspatirbhomo budho rāhuḥ śanaiśvaraḥ ||17||

18nakṣatrāṇyūtavaścaiva māsāḥ pakṣāḥ savatsarāḥ
|
vainateyāḥ samudrāśca kadrujāḥ pannagāstathā ||18||

19śatadruśca vipāśā ca candrabhāgā sarasvatī
|
sindhuśca devikā caiva prabhāsaṃ puṣkarāṇi ca ||19||

20gaṅgā mahānadī veṇā kāverī narmadā tathā
|
kulaṃpunā viśalyā ca karatoyāṃbuvāhinī ||20||

21sarayūrgaṇḍakī caiva lohitaśca mahānadaḥ
|
tāmrā'ruṇā vetravatī parṇāśā gautamī tathā ||21||

22godāvarī ca veṇyā ca kṛṣṇaveṇā tathā'drijā
|
dṛṣadvatī ca kāverī cakṣurmandākinī tathā ||22||

23prayāgaṃ ca prabhāsaṃ ca puṇyaṃ naimiṣameva ca
|
tacca viśveśvarasthānaṃ yatra tadvimalaṃ saraḥ ||23||

24puṇyatīrthaṃ susalilaṃ kurukṣetraṃ prakīrtitam
|
sindhūttamaṃ tapodānaṃ jaṃbūmārgamathāpi ca ||24||

25hiraṇvatī vitastā ca tathā plakṣavatī nadī
|
vedasmṛtirvedavatī mālavāthāśvavatyapi ||25||

26bhūmibhāgāstathā puṇyā gaṅgādvāramathāpi ca
|
ṛṣikulyāstathā medhyā nadyaḥ siṃdhuvahāstathā ||26||

27carmaṇvatī nadī puṇyā kauśikī yamunā tathā
|
nadī bhīmarathī caiva bāhudā ca mahānadī ||27||

28māhendravāṇī tridivā nīlikā ca sarasvatī
|
nandā cāparanandā ca tathā tīrthamahāhṛdaḥ ||28||

29gayā'tha phalgutīrthaṃ ca dharmāraṇyaṃ surairvṛtam
|
tathā devanadī puṇyā saraśca brahmanirmitam ||29||

30puṇyaṃ trilokavikhyātaṃ sarvapāpaharaṃ śivam
|
himavānparvataścaiva divyauṣadhisamanvitaḥ ||30||

31vindhyo dhātuvicitrāṅgastīrthavānauṣadhānvitaḥ
|
merurmahendro malayaḥ śvetaśca rajatāvṛtaḥ ||31||

32śṛṅgavānmando nīlo niṣadho dardurastathā
|
citrakūṭo'janābhaśca parvato gandhamādanaḥ ||32||

33puṇyaḥ somagiriścaiva tathaivānye mahīdharāḥ
|
diśaśca vidiśaścaiva kṣitiḥ sarve mahīruhāḥ ||33||

34viśvedevā nabhaścaiva nakṣatrāṇi grahāstathā
|
pāntu naḥ satataṃ devāḥ kīrtitā'kīrtitā mayā ||34||

35kīrtayāno naro hotānmucyate sarvakilviṣaiḥ
|
stuvaṃśca pratinandaṃśca mucyate sarvato bhayāt ||35||

36sarvasaṅkarapāpebhyo devatāstavanaṃdakaḥ
|
devatānantaraṃ viprāṃstapaḥsiddhāṃstapodhikān ||36||

37kīrtitāmkīrtayiṣyāmi sarvapāpapramocanān
|
yavakrīto'tha raibhyaśca kakṣīvānauśijastathā ||37||

38bhṛgvaṅgirāstathā kaṇvo medhātithiratha prabhuḥ
|
barhī ca guṇasampannaḥ prācīṃ diśasupāśritāḥ ||38||

39bhadrāṃ diśaṃ mahābhāgā ulmucuḥ pramucustathā
|
mumucuśca mahābhāgaḥ svastyātreyaśca vīryavān ||39||

40mitrāvaruṇayoḥ putrastathā'gastyaḥ pratāpavān
|
dṛḍhāyuścordhvabāhuśca viśrutāvṛṣisattamau ||40||

41paścimāṃ diśamāśritya ya edhante nibodha tān
|
uṣaṃguḥ saha sodaryaiḥ parivyādhaśca vīryavān ||41||

42ṛṣirdīrghatamāścaiva gautamaḥ kāśyapastathā
|
ekataśca dvitaścaiva tritaścaiva mahānṛṣiḥ ||42||

43atreḥ putraśca dharmātmā tathā sārasvataḥ prabhuḥ
|
uttarāṃ diśamāśrityaya edhante nibodha tān ||43||

44atrirvasiṣṭhaḥ śaktiśca pārāśaryaśca vīryavān
|
viśvāmitro bharadvājo jamadagnistathaiva ca ||44||

45ṛcīkaputro rāmaśca ṛṣirauddālakistathā
|
śvetaketuḥ kohalaśca vipulo devalastathā ||45||

46devaśarmā ca dhaumyaśca hastikāśyapa eva ca
|
lomaśo nāciketaśca lomaharṣaṇa eva ca ||46||

47ṛṣirugraśravāścaiva bhārgavaścyavanastathā
|
eṣa vai samavāyaśca ṛṣidevasamanvitaḥ ||47||

48ādyaḥ prakīrtito rājansarvapāpapramocanaḥ
|
nṛgo yayātirnahuṣo yaduḥ pūruśca vīryavān ||48||

49dhuṃdhumāro dilīpaśca sagaraśca pratāpavān
|
kṛśāśvo yauvanāśvaśca citrāśvaḥ satyavāṃstathā ||49||

50duṣyanto bharataścaiva cakravartī mahāyaśāḥ
|
pavano janakaścaiva tathā dṛṣṭaratho nṛpaḥ ||50||

51raghurnaravaraścaiva tathā daśaratho nṛpaḥ
|
rāmo rākṣasahā vīraḥ śaśabindurbhagīrathaḥ ||51||

52hariścandro maruttaśca tathā dṛḍharatho nṛpaḥ
|
mahodaryo hyalarkaśca ailaścaiva narādhipaḥ ||52||

53karaṃdhamo naraśreṣṭhaḥ kadhmoraśca narādhipaḥ
|
dakṣombarīṣaḥ kukuro raivataśca mahāyaśāḥ ||53||

54kuruḥ saṃvaraṇaścaiva māṃdhātā satyavikramaḥ
|
mucukundaśca rājarṣirjahnurjāhnavisevitaḥ ||54||

55ādirājaḥ pṛthurvainyo mitrabhānuḥ priyaṅkaraḥ
|
trasaddasyustathā rājā śveto rājarṣisattamaḥ ||55||

56mahābhiṣaśca vikhyāto nimirājā tathā'ṣṭakaḥ
|
āyuḥ kṣupaśca rājarṣiḥ kakṣeyuśca narādhipaḥ ||56||

57pratardano divodāsaḥ sudāsaḥ kosaleśvaraḥ
|
ailo nalaśca rājarṣirmanuścaiva prajāpatiḥ ||57||

58havidhraśca pṛṣadhraśca pratīpaḥ śāṃtanustathā
|
ajaḥ prācīnabarhiśca tathekṣvākurmahāyaśāḥ ||58||

59anaraṇyo narapatirjānujaṃghastathaiva ca
|
kakṣasenaśca rājarṣirye cānye cānukīrtitāḥ ||59||

60kalyamutthāya yo nityaṃ saṃdhye dve'stamayodaye
|
paṭhecchuciranāvṛttaḥ sa dharmaphalabhāgbhavet ||60||

61devā devarṣayaścaiva stutā rājarṣayastathā
|
puṣṭimāyuryaśaḥ svargaṃ vidhāsyanti mameśvarāḥ ||61||

62mā vighnaṃ mā ca me pāpaṃ mā ca me paripaṃthinaḥ
|
dhruvo jayo me nityaḥ syātparatra ca śumā gatiḥ ||62||
