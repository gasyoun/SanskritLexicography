Mahābhārata 7.175 (Droṇaparvan)

1dhṛtarāṣṭra uvāca|
yattadvaikartanaḥ karṇo rākṣasaśca ghaṭotkacaḥ
|
niśīthe samasajjetāṃ tadyuddhamabhavatkatham||1||

2kīdṛśaṃ cābhavadrūpaṃ tasya ghorasya rakṣasaḥ
|
rathaśca kīdṛśastasya hayāḥ sarvāyudhāni ca||2||

3kiṃpramāṇā hayāstasya rathaketurdhanustathā
|
kīdṛśaṃ varma caivāsya śirastrāṇaṃ ca kīdṛśam||3||

4pṛṣṭastvametadācakṣva kuśalo hyasi sañjaya
|
sañjaya uvāca|
lohitākṣo mahākāyastāmrāsyo nimnitodaraḥ||4||

5ūrdhvaromā hariśmaśruḥ śaṃkukarṇo mahāhanuḥ
|
ākarṇadāritāsyaśca tīkṣṇadaṃṣṭraḥ karālavān||5||

6sudīrghatāmrajihvoṣṭho lambabhrūḥ sthūlanāsikaḥ
|
nīlāṅgo lohitagrīvo girivarṣmā bhayaṃkaraḥ||6||

7mahākāyo yo mahābāhurmahāśīrṣo mahābalaḥ
|
vikṛtaḥ paruṣasparśo vikacodvṛddhapiṇḍakaḥ||7||

8sthūlasphiggūḍhanābhiśca śithilopacayo mahān
|
tathaiva hastābharaṇī mahāmāyo'ṅgadī tathā||8||

9urasā dhārayanniṣkamagnimālāṃ yathā'calaḥ
|
tasya hemamayaṃ citraṃ bahurūpāṅgaśobhitam||9||

10toraṇapratimaṃ śubhraṃ kirīṭaṃ mūrdhnyaśobhata
|
kuṇḍale bālasūryābhe mālāṃ hemamayīṃ śubhām||10||

11dhārayanvipulaṃ kāṃsyaṃ kavacaṃ ca mahāprabham
|
kiṃkiṇīśatanirghoṣaṃ raktadhvajapatākinam||11||

12ṛkṣacarmāvanaddhāṅgaṃ nalvamātraṃ mahāratham
|
sarvāyudhavaropetamāsthitaṃ dhvajamālinam||12||

13aṣṭacakrasamāyuktaṃ meghagaṃbhīraniḥsvanam
|
mattamātaṅgasaṃkāśā lohitākṣā vibhīṣaṇāḥ||13||

14kāmavarṇajavā yuktā balavantaḥ śataṃ hayāḥ
|
vahanto rākṣasaṃ ghoraṃ vālavanto jitaśramāḥ||14||

15vipulābhiḥ saṭābhiste hreṣamāṇā muhurmuhuḥ
|
rākṣaso'sya virūpākṣaḥ sūto dīptāsyakuṃḍalaḥ||15||

16raśmibhiḥ sūryaraśmyābhaiḥ sañjagrāha hayān raṇe
|
sa tena sahitastasthāvaruṇena yathā raviḥ||16||

17saṃsakta iva cābhreṇa yathā'drirmahatā mahān
|
divaḥspṛk sumahānketuḥ syandane'sya samucchritaḥ||17||

18raktottamāṅgaḥ kravyādo gṛdhraḥ paramabhabhīṣaṇaḥ
|
vāsavāśaninirghoṣaṃ dṛḍhajyamativikṣipan||18||

19vyaktaṃ kiṣkuparīṇāhaṃ dvādaśāratnikārmukam
|
rathākṣamātrairiṣubhiḥ sarvāḥ pracchādayandiśaḥ||19||

20tasyāṃ vīrāpahāriṇyāṃ niśāyāṃ karṇamabhyayāt
|
tasya vikṣipataścāpaṃ rathe viṣṭabhya tiṣṭhataḥ||20||

21aśrūyata dhanurghoṣo visphūrjitamivāśaneḥ
|
tena vitrāsyamānāni tava sainyāni bhārata||21||

22samakampanta sarvāṇi sindhoriva mahormayaḥ
|
tamāpatantaṃ saṃprekṣya virūpākṣaṃ vibhīṣaṇam||22||

23utsmayanniva rādheyastvaramāṇo'bhyavārayat
|
tataḥ karṇo'bhyayādenamasyannasyantamantikāt||23||

24mātaṅga iva mātaṅgaṃ yūtharṣabhamivarṣabhaḥ
|
sa sannipātastumulastayorāsīdviśāṃpate||24||

25karṇarākṣasayo rājannindraśambarayoriva
|
tau pragṛhya mahāvege dhanuṣī bhīmaniḥsvane||25||

26prācchādayetāmanyonyaṃ takṣamāṇau maheṣubhiḥ
|
tataḥ pūrṇāyatotsṛṣṭairiṣubhirnataparvabhiḥ||26||

27nyavārayetāmanyonyaṃ kāṃsye nirbhidya varmaṇī
|
tau nakhairiva śārdūlau dantairiva mahādvipau||27||

28rathaśaktibhiranyonyaṃ viśikhaiśca tatakṣatuḥ
|
sañchindantau ca gātrāṇi sandadhānau ca sāyakān||28||

29dahantau ca śarolkābhirduṣprekṣyau ca babhūvatuḥ
|
tau tu vikṣatasarvāṅgau rudhiraughapariplutau||29||

30vibhrājetāṃ yathā vāri sravantau gairikācalau
|
tau śarāgravinunnāṅgau nirbhindantau parasparam||30||

31nākampayetāmanyonyaṃ yatamānau mahādyutī
|
tatpravṛttaṃ niśāyuddhaṃ ciraṃ samamivābhavat||31||

32prāṇayordīvyato rājankarṇarākṣasayormṛdhe
|
tasya sandadhatastīkṣṇāñcharāṃścāsaktamasyataḥ||32||

33dhanurghoṣeṇa vitrastāḥ sve pare ca tadā'bhavan
|
ghaṭotkacaṃ yadā karṇo viśeṣayati no nṛpa||33||

34tataḥ prāduṣkaroddivyamastramastravidāṃ varaḥ
|
karṇena saṃdhitaṃ dṛṣṭvā divyamastraṃ ghaṭotkacaḥ||34||

35prāduścakre mahāmāyāṃ rākṣasīṃ pāṇḍunandanaḥ
|
śūlamudgaradhāriṇyā śailapādapahastayā||35||

36rakṣasāṃ ghorarūpāṇāṃ mahatyā senayā vṛtaḥ
|
tamudyatamahācāpaṃ dṛṣṭvā te vyathitā nṛpāḥ||36||

37bhūtāntakamivāyāntaṃ kāladaṇḍogradhāriṇam
|
ghaṭotkacaprayuktena siṃhanādena bhīṣitāḥ||37||

38prasusruvurgajā mūtraṃ vivyadhuśca narā bhṛśam
|
tato'śmavṛṣṭiratyugrā mahatyāsītsamantataḥ||38||

39ardharātre'dhikabalairvimuktā rakṣasāṃ balaiḥ
|
āyasāni ca cakrāṇi bhuśuṇḍyaḥ śaktitomarāḥ||39||

40patantyaviralāḥśūlāḥ śataghnyaḥ paṭṭiśāstathā
|
tadugramatiraudraṃ ca dṛṣṭvā yuddhaṃ narādhipa||40||

41putrāśca tava yodhāśca vyathitā vipradudruvuḥ
|
tatraiko'strabalaślāghī karṇo mānī na vivyathe||41||

42vyadhamacca śarairmāyāṃ tāṃ ghaṭotkacanirmitām
|
māyāyāṃ tu prahīṇāyāmamarṣācca ghaṭotkacaḥ||42||

43visasarja śarān ghorānsūtaputraṃ ta āviśan
|
tataste rudhirābhyaktā bhittvā karṇaṃ mahāhave||43||

44viviśurdharaṇīṃ bāṇāḥ saṃkruddhā iva pannagāḥ
|
sūtaputrastu saṃkruddho laghuhastaḥ pratāpavān||44||

45ghaṭotkacamatikramya bibheda daśabhiḥ śaraiḥ
|
ghaṭotkaco vinirbhinnaḥ sūtaputreṇa marmasu||45||

46cakraṃ divyaṃ sahastrāramagṛhṇādvyathito bhṛśam
|
kṣurāntaṃ bālasūryābhaṃ maṇiratnavibhūṣitam||46||

47cikṣepādhiratheḥ kruddho bhaimasenirjighāṃsayā
|
praviddhamativegena vikṣiptaṃ karṇasāyakaiḥ||47||

48abhāgyasyeva saṃkalpastanmoghamapatadbhuvi
|
ghaṭotkacastu saṃkruddho dṛṣṭvā cakraṃ nipātitam||48||

49karṇaṃ prācchādayadbāṇaiḥ svarbhānuriva bhāskaram
|
sūtaputrastvasaṃbhrānto rudropendrendravikramaḥ||49||

50ghaṭotkacarathaṃ tūrṇaṃ chādayāmāsa patribhiḥ
|
ghaṭotkacena kruddhena gadā hemāṅgadā tadā||50||

51kṣiptā bhrāmya śaraiḥ sā'pi karṇenābhyāhatā'patat
|
tato'ntarikṣamutpatya kālamegha ivonnadan||51||

52pravavarṣa mahākāyo drumavarṣaṃ nabhastalāt
|
tato māyāvinaṃ karṇo bhīmasenasutaṃ divi||52||

53mārgaṇairabhivivyādha ghanaṃ sūrya ivāṃśubhiḥ
|
tasya sarvānhayān hatvā saṃchidya śatadhā ratham||53||

54abhyavarṣaccharaiḥ karṇaḥ parjanya iva vṛṣṭimān
|
na cāsyāsīdanirbhinnaṃ gātre dvyaṅgulamantaram||54||

55so'dṛśyata muhūrtena śvāvicchalalito yathā
|
na hayānna rathaṃ tasya na dhvajaṃ na ghaṭotkacam||55||

56dṛṣṭavantaḥ sma samare śaraughairabhisaṃvṛtam
|
sa tu karṇasya taddivyamastramastreṇa śātayan||56||

57māyāyuddhena māyāvī sūtaputramayodhayat
|
so'yodhayattadā karṇaṃ māyayā lāghavena ca||57||

58alakṣyamāṇāni divi śarajālāni cāpatan
|
bhaimasenirmahāmāyo māyayā kurusattama||58||

59vicacāra mahākāyo mohayanniva bhārata
|
sa tu kṛtvā virūpāṇi vadanānyaśubhāni ca||59||

60agrasatsūtaputrasya divyānyastrāṇi māyayā
|
punaścāpi mahākāyaḥ saṃchinnaḥ śatadhā raṇe||60||

61gatasattvo nirutsāhaḥ patitaḥ khāddhyadṛśyata
|
taṃ hataṃ manyamānāḥ sma prāṇadankurupuṅgavāḥ||61||

62atha dehairnavairanyairdikṣu sarvāsvadṛśyata
|
punaścāpi mahākāyaḥ śataśīrṣaḥ śatodara||62||

63vyadṛśyata mahābāhurmaināka iva parvataḥ
|
aṅguṣṭhamātro bhūtvā ca punareva sa rākṣasaḥ||63||

64sāgarormirivoddhūtastiryagūrdhvamavartata
|
vasudhāṃ dārayitvā ca punarapsu nyamajjata||64||

65adṛśyata tadā tatra punarunmajjito'nyataḥ
|
so'vatīrya punastasthau rathe hemapariṣkṛte||65||

66kṣitiṃ khaṃ ca diśaścaiva māyayā'bhyetya daṃśitaḥ
|
gatvā karṇarathābhyāśaṃ vyacaratkuṇḍalānanaḥ||66||

67prāha vākyamasaṃbhrāntaḥ sūtaputraṃ viśāṃpate
|
tiṣṭhedānīṃ kva me jīvansūtaputra gamiṣyasi||67||

68yuddhaśraddhāmahaṃ te'dya vineṣyāmi raṇājire
|
ityuktvā roṣatāmrākṣaṃ rakṣaḥ krūraparākramam||68||

69utpapātāntarikṣaṃ ca jahāsa ca suvistaram
|
karṇamabhyahanaccaiva gajendramiva kesarī||69||

70rathākṣamātrairiṣubhirabhyavarṣaddhaṭotkacaḥ
|
rathināmṛṣabhaṃ karṇaṃ dhārābhiriva toyadaḥ||70||

71śaravṛṣṭiṃ ca tāṃ karṇo dūrātprāptāmaśātayat
|
dṛṣṭvā ca vihatāṃ māyāṃ karṇena bharatarṣabha||71||

72ghaṭotkacastato māyāṃ sasarjāntarhitaḥ punaḥ
|
so'bhavadgiriratyuccaḥ śikharaistarusaṃkaṭaiḥ||72||

73śūlaprāsāsimusalajalaprasravaṇo mahān
|
tamañjanacayaprakhyaṃ karṇo dṛṣṭvā mahīdharam||73||

74prapātairāyudhānyugrāṇyudvahantaṃ na cukṣubhe
|
smayanniva tataḥ karṇo divyamastramudairayat||74||

75tataḥ so'streṇa śailendro vikṣipto vai vyanaśyata
|
tataḥ sa toyado bhūtvā nīlaḥ sendrāyudho divi||75||

76aśmavṛṣṭibhiratyugraḥ sūtaputramavākirat
|
atha sandhāya vāyavyamastramastravidāṃ varaḥ||76||

77vyadhamatkālameghaṃ taṃ karṇo vaikartano vṛṣaḥ
|
sa mārgaṇagaṇaiḥ karṇo diśaḥ pracchādya sarvaśaḥ||77||

78jaghānāstraṃ mahārāja ghaṭotkacasamīritam
|
tataḥ prahasya samare bhaimasenirmahābalaḥ||78||

79prāduścakre mahāmāyāṃ karṇaṃ prati mahāratham
|
sa dṛṣṭvā punarāyāntaṃ rathena rathināṃ varam||79||

80ghaṭotkacamasaṃbhrāntaṃ rākṣasairbahubhirvṛtam
|
siṃhaśārdūlasadṛśairmattamātaṅgavikramaiḥ||80||

81gajasthaiśca rathasthaiśca vājipṛṣṭhagataistathā
|
nānāśastradharairghorairnānākavacabhūṣaṇaiḥ||81||

82vṛtaṃ ghaṭotkacaṃ krūrairmarudbhiriva vāsavam
|
dṛṣṭvā karṇo maheṣvāso yodhayāmāsa rākṣasam||82||

83ghaṭotkacastataḥ karṇaṃ viddhvā pañcabhirāśugaiḥ
|
nanāda bhairavaṃ nādaṃ bhīṣayansarvapārthivān||83||

84bhūyaścāñjalikenātha sa mārgaṇagaṇaṃ mahat
|
karṇahastasthitaṃ cāpaṃ cicchedāśu ghaṭotkacaḥ||84||

85athānyaddhanurādāya dṛḍhaṃ bhārasahaṃ mahat
|
vicakarṣa balātkarṇa indrāyudhamivocchritam||85||

86tataḥ karṇo mahārāja preṣayāmāsa sāyakān
|
suvarṇapuṅkhāñchatrughnān khecarān rākṣasānprati||86||

87tadbāṇairarditaṃ yūthaṃ rakṣasāṃ pīnavakṣasām
|
siṃhenevārditaṃ vanyaṃ gajānāmākulaṃ kulam||87||

88vidhamya rākṣasānbāṇaiḥ sāśvasūtagajānvibhuḥ
|
dadāha bhagavānvahnirbhūtānīva yugakṣaye||88||

89sa hatvā rākṣasīṃ senāṃ śuśubhe sūtanandanaḥ
|
pureva tripuraṃ dagdhvā divi devo maheśvaraḥ||89||

90teṣu rājasahastreṣu pāṇḍaveyeṣu māriṣa
|
nainaṃ nirīkṣitumapi kaścicchaknoti pārthivaḥ||90||

91ṛte ghaṭotkacādrājan rākṣasendrānmahābalāt
|
bhīmavīryabalopetāt kruddhādvaivasvatādiva||91||

92tasya kruddhasya netrābhyāṃ pāvakaḥ samajāyata
|
maholkābhyāṃ yathā rājan sārciṣaḥ snehabindavaḥ||92||

93talaṃ talena saṃhatya sandaśya daśanacchadam
|
rathamāsthāya ca punarmāyayā nirmitaṃ tadā||93||

94yuktaṃ gajanibhairvāhaiḥ piśācavadanaiḥ kharaiḥ
|
sa sūtamabravītkruddhaḥ sūtaputrāya māṃ vaha||94||

95sa yayau ghorarūpeṇa rathena rathināṃ varaḥ
|
dvairathaṃ sūtaputreṇa punareva viśāṃpate||95||

96sa cikṣepa punaḥ kruddhaḥ sūtaputrāya rākṣasaḥ
|
aṣṭacakrāṃ mahāghorāmaśaniṃ rudranirmitām||96||

97dviyojanasamutsedhāṃ yojanāyāmavistarām
|
āyasīṃ nicitāṃ śūlaiḥ kadambamiva kesaraiḥ||97||

98tāmavaplutya jagrāha karṇo nyasya mahaddhanuḥ
|
cikṣepa caināṃ tasyaiva syandanātso'vapupluve||98||

99sāśvasūtadhvajaṃ yānaṃ bhasma kṛtvā mahāprabhā
|
viveśa vasudhāṃ bhittvā surāstatra visismiyuḥ||99||

100karṇaṃ tu sarvabhūtāni pūjayāmāsurañjasā
|
yadavaplutya jagrāha devasṛṣṭāṃ mahāśanim||100||

101evaṃ kṛtvā raṇe karṇa āruroha rathaṃ punaḥ
|
tato mumoca nārācānsūtaputraḥ parantapa||101||

102aśakyaṃ kartumanyena sarvabhūteṣu mānada
|
yadakārṣīttadā karṇaḥ saṃgrāme bhīmadarśane||102||

103sa hanyamāno nārācairdhārābhiriva parvataḥ
|
gandharvanagarākāraḥ punarantaradhīyata||103||

104evaṃ sa vai mahākāyo māyayā lāghavena ca
|
astrāṇi tāni divyāni jaghāna ripusūdanaḥ||104||

105nihanyamāneṣvastreṣu māyayā tena rakṣasā
|
asaṃbhrāntastadā karṇastadrakṣaḥ pratyayudhyata||105||

106tataḥ kruddho mahārāja bhaimasenirmahābalaḥ
|
cakāra bahudhā''tmānaṃ bhīṣayāṇo mahārathān||106||

107tato digbhyaḥ samāpetuḥ siṃhavyāghratarakṣavaḥ
|
agnijihvāśca bhujagā vihagāścāpyayomukhāḥ||107||

108sa kīryamāṇo viśikhaiḥ karṇacāpacyutaiḥ śaraiḥ
|
nāgarāḍiva duṣprekṣyastatraivāntaradhīyata||108||

109rākṣasāśca piśācāśca yātudhānāstathaiva ca
|
śālāvṛkāśca bahavo vṛkāśca vikṛtānanāḥ||109||

110te karṇaṃ kṣapayiṣyantaḥ sarvataḥ samupādravan
|
athainaṃ vāgbhirugrābhistrāsayāṃcakrire tadā||110||

111udyatairbahubhirghorairāyudhaiḥ śoṇitokṣitaiḥ
|
teṣāmanekairekaikaṃ karṇo vivyādha sāyakaiḥ||111||

112pratihatya tu tāṃ māyāṃ divyenāstreṇa rākṣasīm
|
ājaghāna hayānasya śaraiḥ sannataparvabhiḥ||112||

113te bhagnā vikṣatāṅgāśca bhinnapṛṣṭhāśca sāyakaiḥ
|
vasudhāmanvapadyanta paśyatastasya rakṣasaḥ||113||

114sa bhagnamāyo haiḍimbiḥ karṇaṃ vaikartanaṃ tadā
|
eṣa te vidadhe mṛtyumityuktvā'ntaradhīyata||114||
