Mahābhārata 7.3 (Droṇaparvan)

1sañjaya uvāca|
śaratalpe mahātmānaṃ śayānamamitaujasam
|
mahāvātasamūhena samudramiva śoṣitam||1||

2dṛṣṭvā pitāmahaṃ bhīṣmaṃ sarvakṣatrāntakaṃ gurum
|
divyairastrairmaheṣvāsaṃ pātitaṃ savyasācinā||2||

3jayāśā tava putrāṇāṃ saṃbhagnā śarma varma ca
|
apārāṇāmiva dvīpamagādhe gādhamicchatām||3||

4srotasā yāmuneneva śaraugheṇa pariplutam
|
mahendreṇeva mainākamasahyaṃ bhuvi pātitam||4||

5nabhaścyutamivādityaṃ patitaṃ dharaṇītale
|
śatakratumivācintyaṃ purā vṛtreṇa nirjitam||5||

6mohanaṃ sarvasainyasya yudhi bhīṣmasya pātanam
|
kakudaṃ sarvasainyānāṃ lakṣma sarvadhanuṣmatām||6||

7dhanañjayaśarairvyāptaṃ pitaraṃ te mahāvratam
|
taṃ vīraśayane vīraṃ śayānaṃ puruṣarṣabham||7||

8bhīṣmamādhirathirdṛṣṭvā bharatānāṃ mahādyutiḥ
|
avatīrya rathādārto bāṣpavyākulitākṣaram||8||

9abhivādyāñjaliṃ baddhvā vandamāno'bhyabhāṣata
|
karṇo'hamasmi bhadraṃ te vada māmabhibhārata||9||

10puṇyayā kṣemyayā vācā cakṣuṣā cāvalokaya
|
na nūnaṃ sukṛtasyeha phalaṃ kaścit samaśnute||10||

11yatra dharmaparo vṛddhaḥ śete bhuvi bhavāniha
|
kośasaṃcayane mantre vyūhe praharaṇeṣu ca||11||

12nāhamanyaṃ prapaśyāmi kurūṇāṃ kurupuṅgava
|
buddhyā viśuddhayā yukto yaḥ kurūṃstārayedbhayāt||12||

13yodhāṃstu bahudhā hatvā pitṛlokaṃ gamiṣyati
|
adyaprabhṛti saṃkruddhā vyāghrā iva mṛgakṣayam||13||

14pāṇḍavā bharataśreṣṭha kariṣyanti kurukṣayam
|
adya gāṇḍīvaghoṣasya vīryajñāḥ savyasācinaḥ||14||

15kuravaḥ santrasiṣyanti vajrapāṇerivāsurāḥ
|
adya gāṇḍīvamuktānāmaśanīnāmiva svanaḥ||15||

16trāsayiṣyati bāṇānāṃ kurūnanyāṃśca pārthivān
|
samiddho'gniryathā vīra mahājvālo drumān dahet||16||

17dhārtarāṣṭrānpradhakṣyanti tathā bāṇāḥ kirīṭinaḥ
|
yena yena prasarato vāyvagnī sahitau vane||17||

18tena tena pradahato bhūrigulmatṛṇadrumān
|
yādṛśo'gniḥ samudbhūtastādṛk pārtho na saṃśayaḥ||18||

19yathā vāyurnaravyāghra tathā kṛṣṇo na saśayaḥ
|
nadataḥ pāñcajanyasya rasato gāṇḍivasya ca||19||

20śrutvā sarvāṇi sainyāni trāsaṃ yāsyaṃti bhārata
|
kapidhvajasyotpatato rathasyāmitrakarṣiṇaḥ||20||

21śabdaṃ soḍhuṃ na śakṣyanti tvāmṛte vīra pārthivāḥ
|
ko hyarjunaṃ yodhayituṃ tvadanyaḥ pārthivo'rhati||21||

22yasya divyāni karmāṇi pravadanti manīṣiṇaḥ
|
amānuṣaiśca saṃgrāmastryambakeṇa mahātmanā||22||

23tasmāccaiva varaṃ prāpto duṣprāpamakṛtātmabhiḥ
|
ko'nyaḥśakto raṇe jetuṃ pūrvaṃ yo na jitastvayā||23||

24jito yena raṇe rāmo bhavatā vīryaśālinā
|
kṣatriyāntakaro ghoro devadānavadarpahā||24||

25tamadyāhaṃ pāṇḍavaṃ yuddhaśauṇḍamamṛṣyamāṇo bhavatā cānuśiṣṭaḥ
|
āśīviṣaṃ dṛṣṭiharaṃ sughoraṃ śūraṃ śakṣyāmyastrabalānnihantum||25||
