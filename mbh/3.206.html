Mahābhārata 3.206 (Āraṇyakaparvan)

1mārkaṇḍeya uvāca|
kaściddvijātipravaro vedādhyāyī tapodhanaḥ
|
tapasvī dharmaśīlaśca kauśiko nāma bhārata||1||

2sāṅgopaniṣado vedānadhīte dvijasattamaḥ
|
sa vṛkṣamūle kasmiṃścidvedānuccārayan sthitaḥ||2||

3upariṣṭhācca vṛkṣasya balākā sannyalīyata
|
tayā purīṣamutsṛṣṭaṃ brāhmaṇasya tadopari||3||

4tāmavekṣya tataḥ kruddhaḥ samapadhyāyata dvijaḥ
|
bhṛśaṃ krodhābhibhūtena balākā sā nirīkṣitā||4||

5apadhyātā ca vipreṇa nyapataddharaṇītale
|
balākāṃ patitāṃ dṛṣṭvā gatasattvāmacetanām||5||

6kāruṇyādabhisantaptaḥ paryaśocata tāṃ dvijaḥ
|
akāryaṃ kṛtavānasmi roṣarāgabalātkṛtaḥ||6||

7mārkaṇḍeya uvāca|
ityuktvā bahuśo vidvān grāmaṃ bhaikṣyāya saṃśritaḥ
|
grāme śucīni pracaran kulāni bharatarṣabha||7||

8praviṣṭastat kulaṃ yatra pūrvaṃ caritavāṃstu saḥ
|
dehīti yācamāno'sau tiṣṭhetyuktaḥ striyā tataḥ||8||

9śaucaṃ tu yāvat kurute bhājanasya kuṭumbinī
|
etasminnantare rājan kṣudhāsampīḍito bhṛśam||9||

10bhartā praviṣṭaḥ sahasā tasyā bharatasattama
|
sā tu dṛṣṭvā patiṃ sādhvī brāhmaṇaṃ vyavahāya tam||10||

11pādyamācamanīyaṃ vai dadau bhartustathā''sanam
|
prahvā paryacaraccāpi bhartāramasitekṣaṇā||11||

12āhāreṇātha bhakṣyaiśca bhojyaiḥ sumadhuraistathā
|
ucchiṣṭaṃ bhāvitā bharturbhuṅkte nityaṃ yudhiṣṭhira||12||

13daivataṃ ca patiṃ mene bhartuścittānusāriṇī
|
karmaṇā manasā vācā nānyacittā'bhyagāt patim||13||

14taṃ sarvabhāvopagatā patiśuśrūṣaṇe ratā
|
sādhvācārā śucirdakṣā kuṭumbasya hitaiṣiṇī||14||

15bhartuścāpi hitaṃ yat tat satataṃ sā'nuvartate
|
devatātithibhṛtyānāṃ śvaśrūśvaśurayostathā||15||

16śuśrūṣaṇaparā nityaṃ satataṃ saṃyatendriyā
|
sā brāhmaṇaṃ tadā dṛṣṭvā saṃsthitaṃ bhaikṣyakāṅkṣiṇam||16||

17kurvatī patiśuśrūṣāṃ sasmārātha śubhekṣaṇā
|
vrīḍitā sā'bhavat sādhvī tadā bharatasattama
|
bhikṣāmādāya viprāya nirjagāma yaśasvinī||17||

18brāhmaṇa uvāca|
kimidaṃ bhavati tvaṃ māṃ tiṣṭhetyuktvā varāṅgane
|
uparodhaṃ kṛtavatī na visarjitavatyasi||18||

19mārkaṇḍeya uvāca|
brāhmaṇaṃ krodhasantaptaṃ jvalantamiva tejasā
|
dṛṣṭvā sādhvī manuṣyendra sāntvapūrvaṃ vaco'bravīt||19||

20stryuvāca| kṣantumarhasi me vidvan bhartā me devataṃ mahat
|
sa cāpi kṣudhitaḥ śrāntaḥ prāptaḥ śuśrūṣito mayā||20||

21brāhmaṇa uvāca|
brāhmaṇā na garīyāṃso garīyāṃste patiḥ kṛtaḥ
|
gṛhasthadharme vartantī brāhmaṇānavamanyase||21||

22indro'pyeṣāṃ praṇamate kiṃ punarmānavo bhuvi
|
avaliptena jānīṣe vṛddhānāṃ na śrutaṃ tvayā||22||

23brāhmaṇā hyagnisadṛśā daheyuḥ pṛthivīmapi
|
stryuvāca|
nāhaṃ balākā viprarṣe tyaja krodhaṃ tapodhana||23||

24anayā kruddhayā dṛṣṭyā kuddhaḥ kiṃ māṃ kariṣyasi
|
nāvajānāmyahaṃ viprān devaistulyān manasvinaḥ||24||

25aparādhamimaṃ vipra kṣantumarhasi me'nagha
|
jānāmi tejo viprāṇāṃ mahābhāgyaṃ ca dhīmatām||25||

26apeyaḥ sāgaraḥ krodhāt kṛto hi lavaṇodakaḥ
|
tathaiva dīptatapasāṃ munīnāṃ bhāvitātmanām||26||

27yeṣāṃ krodhāgniradyāpi daṇḍakenopaśāmyati
|
brāhmaṇānāṃ paribhavādvātāpiḥ sudurātmavān||27||

28agastyamṛṣimāsādya jīrṇaḥ krūro mahāsuraḥ
|
bahuprabhāvāḥ śrūyante brāhmaṇānāṃ mahātmanām||28||

29krodhaḥ suvipalo brahman prasādaśca mahātmanām
|
asmiṃstvatikrame brahman kṣantumarhasi me'nagha||29||

30patiśuśrūṣayā dharmo yaḥ sa me rocate dvija
|
daivateṣvapi sarveṣu bhartā me daivataṃ param||30||

31aviśeṣeṇa tasyāhaṃ kuryāṃ dharmaṃ dvijottama
|
śuśrūṣāyāḥ phalaṃ paśya patyurbrāhmaṇa yādṛśam||31||

32balākā hi tvayā dagdhā roṣāt tadviditaṃ mayā
|
krodhaḥ śatruḥ śarīrastho manuṣyāṇāṃ dvijottama||32||

33yaḥ krodhamohau tyajati taṃ devā brāhmaṇaṃ viduḥ
|
yo vadediha satyāni guruṃ santoṣayeta ca||33||

34hiṃsitaśca na hiṃseta taṃ devā brāhmaṇaṃ viduḥ
|
jitendriyo dharmaparaḥ svādhyāyanirataḥ śuciḥ||34||

35kāmakrodhau vaśau yasya taṃ devā brāhmaṇaṃ viduḥ
|
yasya cātmasamo loko dharmajñasya manasvinaḥ||35||

36sarvadharmeṣu ca ratastaṃ devā brāhmaṇaṃ viduḥ
|
yo'dhyāpayedadhīyīta yajedvā yājayīta vā||36||

37dadyādvāpi yathāśakti taṃ devā brāhmaṇaṃ viduḥ
|
brahmacārī vadānyo yo'pyadhīyāddvijapuṅgavaḥ||37||

38svādhyāyavānamatto vai taṃ devā brāhmaṇaṃ viduḥ
|
yadbrāhmaṇānāṃ kuśalaṃ tadeṣāṃ parikīrtayet||38||

39satyaṃ tathā vyāharatāṃ nānṛte ramate manaḥ
|
dharmaṃ tu brāhmaṇasyāhuḥ svādhyāyaṃ damamārjavam||39||

40indriyāṇāṃ nigrahaṃ ca śāśvataṃ dvijasattama
|
satyārjave dharmamāhuḥ paraṃ dharmavido janāḥ||40||

41durjñeyaḥ śāśvato dharmaḥ sa ca satye pratiṣṭhitaḥ
|
śrutipramāṇo dharmaḥ syāditi vṛddhānuśāsanam||41||

42bahudhā dṛśyate dharmaḥ sūkṣma eva dvijottama
|
bhagavānapi dharmajñaḥ svādhyāyanirataḥ śuciḥ||42||

43na tu tattvena bhagavan dharmaṃ vetsīti me matiḥ
|
yadi vipra na jānīṣe dharmaṃ paramakaṃ dvija||43||

44dharmavyādhaṃ tataḥ pṛccha gatvā tu mithilāṃ purīm
|
mātāpitṛbhyāṃ śuśrūṣuḥ satyavādī jitendriyaḥ||44||

45mithilāyāṃ vasedvyādhaḥ sa te dharmān pravakṣyati
|
tatra gacchasva bhadraṃ te yathākāmaṃ dvijottama||45||

46atyuktamapi me sarvaṃ kṣantumarhasyanindita
|
striyo hyavadhyāḥ sarveṣāṃ ye dharmamabhivindate||46||

47brāhmaṇa uvāca|
prīto'smi tava bhadraṃ te gataḥ krodhaśca śobhane
|
upālambhastvayā'tyukto mama niḥśreyasaṃ param
|
svasti te'stu gamiṣyāmi sādhayiṣyāmi śobhane||47||

48mārkaṇḍeya uvāca|
tathā visṛṣṭo nirgamya svameva bhavanaṃ yayau
|
vinindan sa svamātmānaṃ kauśiko dvijasattamaḥ||48||
