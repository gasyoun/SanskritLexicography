Mahābhārata 8.96 (Karṇaparvan)

1sañjaya uvāca|
tathā nipatite karṇe parasainye ca vidrute
|
āśliṣya pārthaṃ dāśārho harṣādvacanamabravīt ||1||

2hato vajrabhṛtā vṛtrastvayā karṇo dhanañjaya
|
vṛtrakarṇavadhaṃ ghoraṃ kathayiṣyanti mānavāḥ ||2||

3vajreṇa nihato vṛtraḥ saṃyuge bhūritejasā
|
tvayā tu nihataḥ karṇo dhanuṣā niśitaiḥ śaraiḥ ||3||

4tamimaṃ vikramaṃ loke prathitaṃ te yaśaskaram
|
nivedayāvaḥ kaunteya kururājasya dhīmataḥ ||4||

5vadhaṃ karṇasya saṃgrāme dīrghakālacikīrṣitam
|
nivedya dharmarājāya tvamānṛṇyaṃ gamiṣyasi ||5||

6vartamāne mahāyuddhe tava karṇasya cobhayoḥ
|
draṣṭumāyodhanaṃ pūrvamāgato dharmanandanaḥ ||6||

7bhṛśaṃ tu gāḍhaviddhatvānnāśakat sthātumāhave
|
tataḥ sa śibiraṃ gatvā sthitavānpuruṣarṣabhaḥ ||7||

8tathetyuktaḥ keśavastu pārthena yadupuṅgavaḥ
|
paryāvartayadavyagro rathaṃ rathavarasya tam ||8||

9evamuktvā'rjunaṃ kṛṣṇaḥ sainikānidamabravīt
|
parānabhimukhā yattāstiṣṭhadhvaṃ bhadramastu vaḥ ||9||

10dhṛṣṭadyumnaṃ yudhāmanyuṃ mādrīputrau vṛkodaram
|
yuyudhānaṃ ca govinda idaṃ vacanamabravīt ||10||

11yāvadāvedyate rājñe hataḥ karṇo'rjunena vai
|
tāvadbhavadbhiryattaistu bhavitavyaṃ narādhipaiḥ ||11||

12sa taiḥ śūrairanujñāto yayau rājaniveśanam
|
pārthamādāya govindo dadarśa ca yudhiṣṭhiram ||12||

13śayānaṃ rājaśārdūlaṃ kāñcane śayanottame
|
agṛhṇītāṃ ca muditau caraṇau pārthivasya tau ||13||

14tayoḥ praharṣamālakṣya harṣādaśrūṇyavartayat
|
rādheyaṃ nihataṃ mattvā samuttasthau yudhiṣṭhiraḥ ||14||

15uvāca ca mahābāhuḥ punaḥ punararindamaḥ
|
vāsudevārjunau premṇā tāvubhau pariṣasvaje ||15||

16tattasmai tadyathāvṛttaṃ vāsudevaḥ sahārjunaḥ
|
kathayāmāsa karṇasya nidhanaṃ yadupuṅgavaḥ ||16||

17īṣadutsmayamānastu kṛṣṇo rājānamabravīt
|
yudhiṣṭhiraṃ hatāmitraṃ kṛtāñjalirathācyutaḥ ||17||

18diṣṭyā gāṇḍīvadhanvā ca pāṇḍavaśca vṛkodaraḥ
|
tvaṃ cāpi kuśalī rājan mādrīputrau ca pāṇḍavau ||18||

19muktā vīrakṣayādasmātsaṃgrāmāllomaharṣaṇāt
|
kṣipramuttarakālāni kuru kāryāṇi pāṇḍava ||19||

20hato vaikartano rājansūtaputro mahārathaḥ
|
diṣṭyā jayasi rājendra diṣṭyā vardhasi bhārata ||20||

21yastu dyūtajitāṃ kṛṣṇāṃ prāhasatpuruṣādhamaḥ
|
tasyādya sūtaputrasya bhūmiḥ pibati śoṇitam ||21||

22śete'sau śarapūrṇāṅgaḥ śatruste kurupuṅgava
|
taṃ paśya puruṣavyāghra vibhinnaṃ bahubhiḥ śaraiḥ ||22||

23hatāmitrāmimāmurvīmanuśādhi mahābhuja
|
yatto bhūtvā sahāsmābhirbhuṅkṣva bhogāṃśca bhogāṃśca puṣkalān ||23||

24sañjaya uvāca|
iti śrutvā vacastasya keśavasya mahātmanaḥ
|
dharmaputraḥ prahṛṣṭātmā dāśārhaṃ vākyamabravīt ||24||

25diṣṭyā diṣṭyeti rājendra vākyaṃ cedamuvāca ha
|
naitaccitraṃ mahābāho tvayi devakinandana ||25||

26tvayā sārathinā pārtho yatnavānahanacca tam
|
na taccitraṃ mahābāho yuṣmadbuddhiprasādajam ||26||

27pragṛhya ca kuruśreṣṭha sāṅgadaṃ dakṣiṇaṃ bhujam
|
uvāca dharmabhṛtpārtha ubhau tau keśavārjunau ||27||

28naranārāyaṇau devau kathitau nāradena me
|
dharmātmānau mahātmānau purāṇāvṛṣisattamau ||28||

29asakṛccāpi medhāvī kṛṣṇadvaipāyano mama
|
kathāmetāṃ mahābhāga kathayāmāsa tattvavit ||29||

30tava kṛṣṇa prasādena pāṇḍavo'yaṃ dhanañjayaḥ
|
jigāyābhimukhaḥ śatrūnna cāsīdvimukhaḥ kvacit ||30||

31jayaścaiva dhruvo'smākaṃ na tvasmākaṃ parājayaḥ
|
yadā tvaṃ yudhi pārthasya sārathyamupajagmivān ||31||

32bhīṣmo droṇaśca karṇaśca mahātmā gautamaḥ kṛpaḥ
|
anye ca bahavaḥ śūrā ye ca teṣāṃ padānugāḥ ||32||

33tvadbuddhyā nihate karṇe hatā govinda sarvathā
|
ityuktvā dharmarājastu rathaṃ hemavibhūṣitam ||33||

34śvetavarṇairhayairyuktaṃ kālavālairmanojavaiḥ
|
āsthāya puruṣavyāghraḥ svabalenābhisaṃvṛtaḥ ||34||

35prayayau sa mahābāhurdraṣṭumāyodhanaṃ tadā
|
kṛṣṇārjunābhyāṃ vīrābhyāmanumantrya tataḥ priyam ||35||

36ābhāṣamāṇastau vīrāvubhau mādhavaphālgunau
|
sa dadarśa raṇe karṇaṃ śayānaṃ puruṣarṣabham ||36||

37yathā kadambakusumaṃ kesaraiḥ sarvatovṛtam
|
citaṃ śaraśataiḥ karṇaṃ dharmarājo dadarśa saḥ ||37||

38gandhatailāvasiktābhiḥ kāñcanībhiḥ sahasraśaḥ
|
dīpikābhiḥ kṛtodyotaṃ paśyante vai vṛtaṃ tadā ||38||

39saṃchinnabhinnakavacaṃ bāṇaiśca vida‌līkṛtam
|
saputraṃ nihataṃ dṛṣṭvā karṇaṃ rājā yudhiṣṭhiraḥ ||39||

40siṃjātapratyayo'tīva vīkṣya caivaṃ punaḥ punaḥ
|
praśaśaṃsa naravyāghrāvubhau mādhavapāṇḍavau ||40||

41adya rājāsmi govinda pṛthivyāṃ bhrātṛbhiḥ saha
|
tvayā nāthena vīreṇa viduṣā paripālitaḥ ||41||

42hataṃ śrutvā naravyāghraṃ rādheyamatimāninam
|
nirāśo'dya durātmā'sau dhārtarāṣṭro bhaviṣyati ||42||

43jīvite caiva rājye ca hate rādhātmaje raṇe
|
tvatprasādādvayaṃ caiva kṛtārthāḥ puruṣarṣabha ||43||

44diṣṭyā jayasi govinda diṣṭyā śatrurnipātitaḥ
|
diṣṭyā gāṇḍīvadhanvā ca vijayī pāṇḍunandanaḥ ||44||

45trayodaśa samāstīrṇā jāgareṇa suduḥkhitāḥ
|
svapsyāmo'dya sukhaṃ rātrau tvatprasādānmahābhuja ||45||

46evaṃ sa bahuśo rājā praśaśaṃsa janārdanam
|
arjunaṃ ca kuruśreṣṭhaṃ dharmarājo yudhiṣṭhiraḥ ||46||

47sañjaya uvāca|
dṛṣṭvā ca nihataṃ karṇaṃ saputraṃ pārthasāyakaiḥ
|
punarjātamivātmānaṃ mene ca sa mahīpatiḥ ||47||

48sametya ca mahārāja kuntīputraṃ yudhiṣṭhiram
|
harṣayanti sma rājānaṃ harṣayuktā mahārathāḥ ||48||

49nakulaḥ sahadevaśca pāṇḍavaśca vṛkodaraḥ
|
sātyakiśca mahārāja vṛṣṇīnāṃ pravaro rathaḥ ||49||

50dhṛṣṭadyumnaḥ śikhaṇḍī ca pāṇḍupañcālasṛñjayāḥ
|
pūjayanti sma kaunteyaṃ nihate sūtanandane ||50||

51te vardhayittvā nṛpatiṃ dharmātmānaṃ yudhiṣṭhiram
|
jitakāśino labdhalakṣyā yuddhaśauṇḍāḥ prahāriṇaḥ ||51||

52stuvantaḥ stavayuktābhirvāgbhiḥ kṛṣṇau parantapau
|
jagmuḥ svaśibirāyaiva mudā yuktā mahārathāḥ||52||

53evameṣa kṣayo vṛttaḥ sumahāঁllomaharṣaṇaḥ
|
tava durmantrite rājan kimarthamanuśocasi ||53||

54vaiśampāyana uvāca|
śrutvaitadapriyaṃ rājā dhṛtarāṣṭro'mbikāsutaḥ
|
papāta bhūmau niśceṣṭaśchinnamūla iva drumaḥ ||54||

55tathā sā patitā devī gāndhārī dīrghadarśinī
|
śuśoca bahulālāpaiḥ karṇasya nidhanaṃ yudhi ||55||

56tāṃ paryagṛhṇādviduro nṛpatiṃ sañjayastathā
|
paryāśvāsayatāṃ caiva tāvubhāveva bhūmipam ||56||

57tathaivotthāpayāmāsurgāndhārīṃ kuruyoṣitaḥ
|
sa daivaṃ paramaṃ matvā bhavitavyaṃ ca pārthivaḥ ||57||

58parāṃ pīḍāṃ samāśritya naṣṭacitto mahātapāḥ
|
cintāśokaparītātmā na jajñe mohapīḍitaḥ
|
sa samāśvāsito rājā tūṣṇīmāsīdvicetanaḥ ||58||

59imaṃ mahāyuddhamakhaṃ mahātmanordhanañjayasyādhiratheśca yaḥ paṭhet
|
sa samyagiṣṭasya makhasya yatphalaṃ tadāpnuyātsaṃśravaṇācca bhārata ||59||

60makho hi viṣṇurbhagavānsanātano vada‌nti taccāgnyanilendumānavaḥ
|
ato'nasūyuḥ śṛṇuyāt paṭhecca yaḥ sa sarvalokānucaraḥ sukhī bhavet ||60||

61tāṃ sarvadā bhaktimupāgatā narāḥ paṭhanti puṇyāṃ varasaṃhitāmimām
|
dhanena dhānyena yaśasā ca mānuṣā nandanti te nātra vicāraṇā'sti ||61||

62ato'nasūyuḥ śṛṇuyātsadā tu vai naraḥ sa sarvāṇi sukhāni cāpnuyāt
|
viṣṇuḥ svayaṃbhūrbhagavān bhavaśca tuṣyanti te tasya narottamasya ||62||

63vedāvāptirbāhmaṇasyeha dṛṣṭā raṇe balaṃ kṣatriyāṇāṃ jayo yudhi
|
dhanajyeṣṭhāścāpi bhavanti vaiśyāḥ śūdrā''rogyaṃ prāpnuvantīha sarve ||63||

64tathaiva viṣṇurbhagavānsanātanaḥ sa cātra devaḥ parikīrtyate yataḥ
|
tataḥ sa kāmān labhate sukhī naro mahāmunestasya vaco'rcitaṃ yathā ||64||

65kapilānāṃ savatsānāṃ varṣamekaṃ nirantaram
|
yo dadyātsukṛtaṃ taddhi śravaṇātkarṇaparvaṇaḥ ||65||
