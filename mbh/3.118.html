Mahābhārata 3.118 (Āraṇyakaparvan)

1vaiśaṃpāyana uvāca|
gacchansa tīrthāni mahānubhāvaḥ puṇyāni ramyāṇi dadarśa rājā
|
sarvāṇi viprairupaśobhitāni kvacitkvacidbhārata sāgarasya||1||

2sa vṛttavāṃsteṣu kṛtābhiṣekaḥ sahānujaḥ pārthivaputrapautraḥ
|
samudragāṃ puṇyatamāṃ praśastāṃ jagāma pārikṣita pāṇḍuputraḥ||2||

3tatrāpi cāplutya mahānubhāvaḥ saṃtarpayāmāsa pitṝnsurāṃśca
|
dvijātimukhyeṣu dhanaṃ visṛjya godāvarīṃ sāgaragāmagacchat||3||

4tato vipāpmā draviḍeṣu rājansamudramāsādya ca lokapuṇyam
|
agastyatīrthaṃ ca mahāpavitraṃ nārītīrthānyatha vīro dadarśa||4||

5tatrārjunasyāgryadhanurdharasya niśamya tatkarma narairaśakyam
|
saṃpūjyamānaḥ paramarṣisaṅghaiḥ parāṃ mudaṃ pāṇḍusutaḥ sa lebhe||5||

6sa teṣu tīrtheṣvabhiṣiktagātraḥ kṛṣṇāsahāyaḥ sahito'nujaiśca
|
saṃpūjayanvikramamarjunasya reme mahīpāla patiḥ pṛthivyāḥ||6||

7tataḥ sahastrāṇi gavāṃ pradāya tīrtheṣu teṣvambudharottamasya
|
hṛṣṭaḥ saha bhrātṛbhirarjunasya saṃkīrtayāmāsa gavāṃ pradānam||7||

8sa tāni tīrthāni ca sāgarasya puṇyāni cānyāni bahūni rājan
|
krameṇa gacchanparipūrṇakāmaḥ śūrpārakaṃ puṇyatamaṃ dadarśa||8||

9tatrodadheḥ kaṃcidatītya deśaṃ khyātaṃ pṛthivyāṃ vanamāsasāda
|
taptaṃ surairatra tapaḥ purastādiṣṭaṃ tathā puṇyaparairnarendraiḥ||9||

10sa tatra tāmagryadhanurdharasya vedīṃ dadarśāyatapīnabāhuḥ
|
ṛcīkaputrasya tapasvisaṅghaiḥ samāvṛtāṃ puṇyakṛdarcanīyām||10||

11tato vasūnāṃ vasudhādhipaḥ sa marudgaṇānāṃ ca tathāśvinośca
|
vaivasvatādityadhaneśvarāṇāmindrasya viṣṇoḥ saviturvibhośca||11||

12bhavasya candrasya divākarasya paterapāṃ sādhyagaṇasya caiva
|
dhātuḥ pitṝṇāṃ ca tathā mahātmā rudrasya rājansagaṇasya caiva||12||

13sarasvatyāḥ siddhagaṇasya caiva puṇyāśca ye cāpyamarāstathānye
|
puṇyāni cāpyāyatanāni teṣāṃ dadarśa rājā sumanoharāṇi||13||

14teṣūpavāsānvibudhānupoṣya dattvā ca ratnāni mahānti rājā
|
tīrtheṣu sarveṣu pariplutāṅgaḥ punaḥ sa śūrpārakamājagāma||14||

15sa tena tīrthena tu sāgarasya punaḥ prayātaḥ saha sodarīyaiḥ
|
dvijaiḥ pṛthivyāṃ prathitaṃ mahādbhistīrthaṃ prabhāsaṃ samupājagāma||15||

16tatrābhiṣiktaḥ pṛthulohitākṣaḥ sahānujairdevagaṇānpitṝṃśca
|
saṃtarpayāmāsa tathaiva kṛṣṇā te cāpi viprāḥ saha lomaśena||16||

17sa dvādaśāhaṃ jalavāyubhakṣaḥ kurvankṣapāhaḥsu tadābhiṣekam
|
samantato'gnīnupadīpayitvā tepe tapo dharmabhṛtāṃ variṣṭhaḥ||17||

18tamugramāsthāya tapaścarantaṃ śuśrāva rāmaśca janārdanaśca
|
tau sarvavṛṣṇipravarau sasainyau yudhiṣṭhiraṃ jagmaturājamīḍham||18||

19te vṛṣṇayaḥ pāṇḍusutānsamīkṣya bhūmau śayānānmaladigdhagātrān
|
anarhatīṃ draupadīṃ cāpi dṛṣṭvā suduḥkhitāścukruśurārtanādam||19||

20tataḥ sa rāmaṃ ca janārdanaṃ ca kārṣṇiṃ ca sāmbaṃ ca śineśca pautram
|
anyāṃśca vṛṣṇīnupagamya pūjāṃ cakre yathādharmamahīnasattvaḥ||20||

21te cāpi sarvānpratipūjya pārthāṃstaiḥ satkṛtāḥ pāṇḍusutaistathaiva
|
yudhiṣṭhiraṃ saṃparivārya rājannupāviśandevagaṇā yathendram||21||

22teṣāṃ sa sarvaṃ caritaṃ pareṣāṃ vane ca vāsaṃ paramapratītaḥ
|
astrārthamindrasya gataṃ ca pārthaṃ niveśanaṃ hṛṣṭamanāḥ śaśaṃsa||22||

23śrutvā tu te tasya vacaḥ pratītāstāṃścāpi dṛṣṭvā sukṛśānatīva
|
netrodbhavaṃ saṃmumucurmahārhā duḥkhārtijaṃ vāri mahānubhāvāḥ||23||
