Mahābhārata 12.335 (Śāntiparvan)

1bhīṣma uvāca|
sa evamukto dvipadāṃ variṣṭho nārāyaṇenottamapūruṣeṇa
|
jagāda vākyaṃ dvipadāṃ variṣṭhaṃ nārāyaṇaṃ lokahitādhivāsam||1||

2nārada uvāca|
yadarthamātraprabhaveṇa janma kṛtaṃ tvayā dharmagṛhe caturdhā
|
tatsādhyatāṃ lokahitārthamadya gacchāmi draṣṭuṃ prakṛtiṃ tavādyām||2||

3pūjāṃ gurūṇāṃ satataṃ karomi parasya guhyaṃ na tu bhinnapūrvam
|
vedāḥ svadhītā mama lokanātha taptaṃ tapo nānṛtamuktapūrvam||3||

4guptāni catvāri yathāgamaṃ me śatrau ca mitre ca samo'smi nityam
|
taṃ cādidevaṃ satataṃ prapanna ekāntabhāvena vṛṇomyajasram||4||

5ebhirviśeṣaiḥ pariśuddhasattva: kasmānna paśyeyamanantamīśam
|
tatpārameṣṭhyasya vaco niśamya nārāyaṇaḥ śāśvatadharmagoptā||5||

6gaccheti taṃ nāradamuktavānsa saṃpūjayitvā''tmavidhikriyābhiḥ
|
tato visṛṣṭaḥ parameṣṭhiputraḥ so'bhyarcayitvā tamṛṣiṃ purāṇam||6||

7khamutpapātottamayogayuktastato'dhimerau sahasā nililye
|
tatrāvatasthe ca munirmuhūrtamekāntamāsādya gireḥ sa śṛṅge||7||

8ālokayannuttarapaścimena dadarśa cāpyadbhutamuktarūpam
|
kṣīrodadheryottarato hi dvīpaḥ śvetaḥ sa nāmnā prathito viśālaḥ||8||

9meroḥ sahasraiḥ sa hi yojanānāṃ dvātriṃśatordhvaṃ kavibhirniruktaḥ
|
anindriyāścānaśanāśca tatra niṣpandahīnāḥ susugandhinaste||9||

10śvetāḥ pumāṃso gatasarvapāpāścakṣurmuṣaḥ pāpakṛtāṃ narāṇām
|
vajrāsthikāyāḥ samamānonmānā divyāvayavarūpāḥ śubhasāropetāḥ||10||

11chatrākṛtiśīrṣā meghaughaninādāḥ samamuṣkacatuṣkā rājīvacchatapādāḥ
|
ṣaṣṭyā dantairyuktāḥ śuklairaṣṭābhirdaṃṣṭrābhirye jihvābhirye viśvavaktraṃ lelihyante sūryaprakhyam||11||

12devaṃ bhaktyā viśvotpanna yasmātsarve lokāḥ saṃprasūtāḥ
|
vedā dharmā munayaḥ śāntā devāḥ sarve tasya nisargaḥ||12||

13yudhiṣṭhira uvāca|
anindriyā nirāhārā aniṣpandāḥ sugandhinaḥ
|
kathaṃ te puruṣā jātāḥ kā teṣāṃ gatiruttamā||13||

14ye ca muktā bhavantīha narā bharatasattama
|
teṣāṃ lakṣaṇametaddhi tacchvetadvīpavāsinām||14||

15tasmānme saṃśayaṃ chindhi paraṃ kautūhalaṃ hi me
|
tvaṃ hi sarvakathārāmastvāṃ caivopāśritā vayam||15||

16bhīṣma uvāca|
vistīrṇaiṣā kathā rājan śrutā me pitṛsannidhau
|
yaiṣā tava hi vaktavyā kathāsāro hi sā matā||16||

17rājoparicaro nāma babhūvādhipatirbhuvaḥ
|
ākhaṇḍalasakhaḥ khyāto bhakto nārāyaṇaṃ harim||17||

18dhārmiko nityabhaktaśca piturnityamatandritaḥ
|
sāmrājyaṃ tena samprāptaṃ nārāyaṇavarātpurā||18||

19sātvataṃ vidhimāsthāya prāksūryamukhaniḥsṛtam
|
pūjayāmāsa deveśaṃ taccheṣeṇa pitāmahān||19||

20pitṛśeṣeṇa viprāṃśca saṃvibhajyāśritāṃśca saḥ
|
śeṣānnabhuk satyaparaḥ sarvabhūteṣvahiṃsakaḥ||20||

21sarvabhāvena bhaktaḥ sa devadevaṃ janārdanam
|
anādimadhyanidhanaṃ lokakartāramavyayam||21||

22tasya nārāyaṇe bhaktiṃ vahato'mitrakarṣiṇaḥ
|
ekaśayyāsanaṃ devo dattavāndevarāṭ svayam||22||

23ātmarājyaṃ dhanaṃ caiva kalatraṃ vāhanaṃ tathā
|
yattadbhāgavataṃ sarvamiti tatprokṣitaṃ sadā||23||

24kāmyanaimittikā rājan yajñiyāḥ paramakriyāḥ
|
sarvāḥ sātvatamāsthāya vidhiṃ cakre samāhitaḥ||24||

25pāñcarātravido mukhyāstasya gehe mahātmanaḥ
|
prāyaṇaṃ bhagavatproktaṃ bhuñjate vā'grabhojanam||25||

26tasya praśāsato rājyaṃ dharmeṇāmitraghātinaḥ
|
nānṛtā vāk samabhavanmano duṣṭaṃ na cābhavat||26||

27na ca kāyena kṛtavānsa pāpaṃ paramaṇvapi
|
ye hi te ṛṣayaḥ khyātāḥ sapta citraśikhaṇḍinaḥ||27||

28tairekamatibhirbhūtvā yatproktaṃ śāstramuttamam
|
vedaiścaturbhiḥ samitaṃ kṛtaṃ merau mahāgirau||28||

29āsyaiḥ saptabhirudgīrṇaṃ lokadharmamanuttamam
|
marīciratryaṅgirasau pulastyaḥ pulahaḥ kratuḥ
|
vasiṣṭhaśca mahātejāste hi citraśikhaṇḍinaḥ||29||

30sapta prakṛtayo hyetāstathā svāyambhuvo'ṣṭamaḥ
|
etābhirdhāryate lokastābhyaḥ śāstraṃ viniḥsṛtam||30||

31ekāgramanaso dāntā munayaḥ saṃyame ratāḥ
|
bhūtabhavyabhaviṣyajñāḥ satyadharmaparāyaṇāḥ||31||

32idaṃ śreya idaṃ brahma idaṃ hitamanuttamam
|
lokānsañcintya manasā tataḥ śāstraṃ pracakrire||32||

33tatra dharmārthakāmā hi mokṣaḥ paścācca kīrtitaḥ
|
maryādā vividhāścaiva divi bhūmau ca saṃsthitāḥ||33||

34ārādhya tapasā devaṃ hariṃ nārāyaṇaṃ prabhum
|
divyaṃ varṣasahasraṃ vai sarve te ṛṣibhiḥ saha||34||

35nārāyaṇānuśāstā hi tadā devī sarasvatī
|
viveśa tānṛṣīnsarvān lokānāṃ hitakāmyayā||35||

36tataḥ pravartitā samyaktapovidbhirdvijātibhiḥ
|
śabde cārthe ca hetau ca eṣā prathamasargajā||36||

37ādādeva hi tacchāstramoṃkārasvarapūjitam
|
ṛṣibhiḥ śrāvitaṃ yatra tatra kāruṇiko hyasau||37||

38tataḥ prasanno bhagavānanirdiṣṭaśarīragaḥ
|
ṛṣīnuvāca tānsarvānadṛśyaḥ puruṣottamaḥ||38||

39kṛtaṃ śatasahasraṃ hi ślokānāmidamuttamam
|
lokatantrasya kṛtsnasya yasmāddharmaḥ pravartate||39||

40pravṛttau ca nivṛttau ca yasmādetadabhaviṣyati
|
yajurṛksāmabhirjuṣṭamatharvāṃgirasaistathā||40||

41yathā pramāṇaṃ hi mayā kṛto brahmā prasādataḥ
|
rudraśca krodhajo viprā yūyaṃ prakṛtayastathā||41||

42sūryācandramasau vāyurbhūmirāpo'gnireva ca
|
sarve ca nakṣatragaṇā yacca bhūtābhiśabditam||42||

43adhikāreṣu vartante yathāsvaṃ brahmavādinaḥ
|
sarve pramāṇaṃ hi yathā tathā tacchāstramuttamam||43||

44bhaviṣyati pramāṇaṃ vai etanmadanuśāsanam
|
tasmātpravakṣyate dharmānmanuḥ svāyambhuvaḥ svayam||44||

45uśanā bṛhaspatiścaiva yadotpannau bhaviṣyataḥ
|
tadā pravakṣyataḥ śāstraṃ yuṣmanmatibhiruddhṛtam||45||

46svāyaṃbhuveṣu dharmeṣu śāstre cauśanase kṛte
|
bṛhaspatimate caiva lokeṣu praticārite||46||

47yuṣmatkṛtamidaṃ śāstraṃ prajāpālo vasustataḥ
|
bṛhaspatisakāśādvai prāpsyate dvijasattamāḥ||47||

48sa hi sadbhāvito rājā madbhaktaśca bhaviṣyati
|
tena śāstreṇa lokeṣu kriyāḥ sarvāḥ kariṣyati||48||

49etaddhi yuṣmacchāstrāṇāṃ śāstramuttamasaṃjñitam
|
etadarthyaṃ ca dharmyaṃ ca rahasyaṃ caitaduttamam||49||

50asya pravartanāccaiva prajāvanto bhaviṣyatha
|
sa ca rājaśriyā yukto bhaviṣyati mahānvasuḥ||50||

51saṃsthite tu nṛpe tasmin śāstrametatsanātanam
|
antardhāsyati tatsarvametadvaḥ kathitaṃ mayā||51||

52etāvaduktvā vacanamadṛśyaḥ puruṣottamaḥ
|
visṛjya tānṛṣīnsarvānkāmapi prasṛto diśam||52||

53tataste lokapitaraḥ sarvalokārthacintakāḥ
|
prāvartayanta tacchāstraṃ dharmayoniṃ sanātanam||53||

54utpanne'ṅgirase caiva yuge prathamakalpite
|
sāṅgopaniṣadaṃ śāstraṃ sthāpayitvā bṛhaspatau||54||

55jagmuryathepsitaṃ deśaṃ tapase kṛtaniścayāḥ
|
dhāraṇāḥ sarvalokānāṃ sarvadharmapravartakāḥ||55||
