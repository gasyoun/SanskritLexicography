Mahābhārata 1.217 (Ādiparvan)

1vargovāca|
tato vayaṃ pravyathitāḥ sarvā bhāratasattama
|
ayāma śaraṇaṃ vipraṃ taṃ tapodhanamacyutam||1||

2rūpeṇa vayasā caiva kandarpeṇa ca darpitāḥ
|
ayuktaṃ kṛtavatyaḥ sma kṣantumarhasi no dvija||2||

3eṣa eva vadho'smākaṃ suparyāptastapodhana
|
yadvayaṃ saṃśitātmānaṃ pralobdhuṃ tvāmihāgatāḥ||3||

4avadhyāstu striyaḥ sṛṣṭā manyante dharmacāriṇaḥ
|
tasmāddharmeṇa vardha tvaṃ nāsmanhiṃsitumarhasi||4||

5sarvabhūteṣu dharmajña maitro brāhmaṇa ucyate
|
satyo bhavatu kalyāṇa eṣa vādo manīṣiṇām||5||

6śaraṇaṃ ca prapannānāṃ śiṣṭāḥ kurvanti pālanam
|
śaraṇaṃ tvāṃ prapannāḥ smastasmāttvaṃ kṣantumarhasi||6||

7vaiśaṃpāyana uvāca|
evamuktaḥ sa dharmātmā brāhmaṇaḥ śubhakarmakṛt
|
prasādaṃ kṛtavānvīra ravisomasamaprabhaḥ||7||

8brāhmaṇa uvāca|
śataṃ śatasahasraṃ tu sarvamakṣayyavācakam
|
parimāṇaṃ śataṃ tvetannedamakṣayyavācakam||8||

9yadā ca vo grāhabhūtā gṛhṇantīḥ puruṣāñjale
|
utkarṣati jalāttasmātsthalaṃ puruṣasattamaḥ||9||

10tadā yūyaṃ punaḥ sarvāḥ svaṃ rūpaṃ pratipatsyatha
|
anṛtaṃ noktapūrvaṃ me hasatāpi kadācana||10||

11tāni sarvāṇi tīrthāni tataḥ prabhṛti caiva ha
|
nārītīrthāni nāmneha khyātiṃ yāsyanti sarvaśaḥ
|
puṇyāni ca bhaviṣyanti pāvanāni manīṣiṇām||11||

12vargovāca
|
tato'bhivādya taṃ vipraṃ kṛtvā cāpi pradakṣiṇam
|
acintayāmo'pasṛtya tasmāddeśātsuduḥkhitāḥ||12||

13kva nu nāma vayaṃ sarvāḥ kālenālpena taṃ naram
|
samāgacchema yo nastadrūpamāpādayetpunaḥ||13||

14tā vayaṃ cintayitvaiva muhūrtādiva bhārata
|
dṛṣṭavatyo mahābhāgaṃ devarṣimuta nāradam||14||

15saṃprahṛṣṭāḥ sma taṃ dṛṣṭvā devarṣimamitadyutim
|
abhivādya ca taṃ pārtha sthitāḥ sma vrīḍitānanāḥ||15||

16sa nopṛcchadduḥkhamūlamuktavatyo vayaṃ ca tam
|
śrutvā tatra yathāvṛttamidaṃ vacanamabravīt||16||

17dakṣiṇe sāgarānūpe pañca tīrthāni santi vai
|
puṇyāni ramaṇīyāni tāni gacchata mā ciram||17||

18tatrāśu puruṣavyāghraḥ pāṇḍaveyo dhanañjayaḥ
|
mokṣayiṣyati śuddhātmā duḥkhādasmānna saṃśayaḥ||18||

19tasya sarvā vayaṃ vīra śrutvā vākyamito gatāḥ
|
tadidaṃ satyamevādya mokṣitāhaṃ tvayā'nagha||19||

20etāstu mama tāḥ sakhyaścatasro'nyā jale śritāḥ
|
kuru karma śubhaṃ vīra etāḥ sarvā vimokṣaya||20||

21vaiśaṃpāyana uvāca|
tatastāḥ pāṇḍavaśreṣṭhaḥ sarvā eva viśāṃpate
|
tasmācchāpāsasīnātmā mokṣayāmāsa vīryavān||21||

22utthāya ca jalāttasmātpratilabhya vapuḥ svakam
|
tāstadā'psaraso rājannadṛśyanta yathā purā||22||

23tīrthāni śodhayitvā tu tathānujñāya tāḥ prabhuḥ
|
citrāṅgadāṃ punardraṣṭuṃ maṇalūraṃ punaryayau||23||

24tasyāmajanayatputraṃ rājānaṃ babhruvāhanam
|
taṃ dṛṣṭvā pāṇḍavo rājaṃścitravāhanamabravīt||24||

25citrāṅgadāyāḥ śulkaṃ tvaṃ gṛhāṇa babhruvāhanam
|
anena ca bhaviṣyāmi ṛṇānmukto narādhipa||25||

26citrāṅgadāṃ punarvākyamabravītpāṇḍunandanaḥ
|
iha vai bhava bhadraṃ te vardhethā babhruvāhanam||26||

27indrapasthanivāsaṃ me tvaṃ tatrāgatya raṃsyasi
|
kuntīṃ yudhiṣṭhiraṃ bhīmaṃ bhrātarau me kanīyasau||27||

28āgatya tatra paśyethā anyānapi ca bāndhavān
|
bāndhavaiḥ sahitāḥ sarvairnandase tvamanindite||28||

29dharme sthitaḥ satyadhṛtiḥ kaunteyo'tha yudhiṣṭhiraḥ
|
jitvā tu pṛthivīṃ sarvāṃ rājasūyaṃ kariṣyati||29||

30tatrāgacchanti rājānaḥ pṛthivyāṃ nṛpasaṃjñitāḥ
|
bahūni ratnānyādāya āgamiṣyati te pitā||30||

31ekasārthaṃ prayātāsi citravāhanasevayā
|
drakṣyāmi rājasūye tvāṃ putraṃ pālaya mā śucaḥ||31||

32babhruvāhananāmnā tu mama prāṇo mahīcaraḥ
|
tasmādbharasva putraṃ vai puruṣaṃ vaṃśavardhanam||32||

33citrāvāhanadāyādaṃ dharmātpauravanandanam
|
pāṇḍavānāṃ priyaṃ putraṃ tasmātpālaya sarvadā||33||

34viprayogeṇa saṃtāpaṃ mā kṛthāstvamanindite
|
citrāṅgadāmevamuktvā gokarṇamabhitogatam||34||

35ādyaṃ paśupateḥ sthānaṃ darśanādeva muktidam
|
yatra pāpo'pi manujaḥ prāpnotyabhayadaṃ padam||35||
