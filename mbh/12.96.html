Mahābhārata 12.96 (Śāntiparvan)

1bhīṣma uvāca|
nādharmeṇa mahīṃ jetuṃ lipsata jagatīpatiḥ
|
adharmavijayaṃ labdhvā ko nu manyeta bhūmipaḥ||1||

2adharmayukto vijayo hyadhruvo'svargya eva ca
|
sādayatyeṣa rājānaṃ mahīṃ ca bharatarṣabha||2||

3viśīrṇakavacaṃ caiva tavāsmīti ca vādinam
|
kṛtāñjaliṃ nyastaśastraṃ gṛhītvā na hi hiṃsayet||3||

4balena vijito yaśca na taṃ yudhyeta bhūmipaḥ
|
saṃvatsaraṃ vipraṇayettasmājjātaḥ punarbhavet||4||

5nārvāksaṃvatsarātkanyā praṣṭavyā vikramāhṛtā
|
evameva dhanaṃ sarvaṃ yaccānyatsahasā''hṛtam||5||

6na tu vadhyadhanaṃ tiṣṭhetpibeyurbrāhmaṇāḥ payaḥ
|
yuñjīrannapyanaḍuhaḥ kṣantavyaṃ vā tadā bhavet||6||

7rājñā rājaiva yoddhavyastathā dharmo vidhīyate
|
nānyo rājānamabhyasyedarājanyaḥ kathañcana||7||

8anīkayoḥ saṃhatayoryadīyādbrāhmaṇo'ntarā
|
śāntimicchannubhayato na yoddhavyaṃ tadā bhavet||8||

9maryādāṃ śāśvatīṃ bhindyādbrāhmaṇaṃ yo'bhilaṅghayet
|
atha cellaṅghayedeva maryādāṃ kṣatriyabruvaḥ||9||

10asaṃkhyeyastadūrdhvaṃ syādanādeyaśca saṃsadi
|
yastu dharmavilopena maryādābhedanena ca||10||

11tāṃ vṛttiṃ nānuvarteta vijīgīṣurmahīpatiḥ
|
dharmalabdhāddhi vijayāllābhaḥ ko'bhyadhiko bhavet||11||

12sahasānāryabhūtāni kṣiprameva prasādayet
|
sāntvena bhogadānena sa rājñāṃ paramo nayaḥ||12||

13bhujyamānā hyayogena svarāṣṭrādabhitāpitāḥ
|
amitrāstamupāsīranvyasanaughapratīkṣiṇaḥ||13||

14amitropagrahaṃ cāsya te kuryaḥ kṣipramāpadi
|
saṃtuṣṭāḥ sarvato rājan rājavyasanakāṃkṣiṇaḥ||14||

15nāmitro vinikartavyo nāticchedyaḥ kathañcana
|
jīvitaṃ hyapyaticchinnaḥ saṃtyajecca kadācana||15||

16alpenāpi ca saṃyuktastuṣyatyeva narādhipaḥ
|
śuddhaṃ jīvitamevāpi tādṛśo bahu manyate||16||

17yasya sphīto janapadaḥ sampannaḥ priyarājakaḥ
|
santuṣṭabhṛtyasacivo dṛḍhamūlaḥ sa pārthivaḥ||17||

18ṛtvikpurohitācāryā ye cānye śrutasattamāḥ
|
pūjārhāḥ pūjitā yasya sa vai lokaviducyate||18||

19etenaiva ca vṛttena mahīṃ prāpa surottamaḥ
|
anena cendraviṣayaṃ vijigīṣanti pārthivāḥ||19||

20bhūmivarjaṃ dhanaṃ rājā jitvā rājanmahāhave
|
api cānnauṣadhīḥ śaśvadājahāra pratardanaḥ||20||

21agnihotrāgniśeṣaṃ ca havirbhojanameva ca
|
ājahāra divodāsastato viprakṛto'bhavat||21||

22sarājakāni rāṣṭrāṇi nābhāgo dakṣiṇāṃ dadau
|
anyatra śrotriyasvācca tāpasārthācca bhārata||22||

23uccāvacāni vittāni dharmajñānāṃ yudhiṣṭhira
|
āsan rājñāṃ purāṇānāṃ sarvaṃ tanmama rocate||23||

24sarvavidyātirekeṇa jayamicchenmahīpatiḥ
|
na māyayā na dambhena ya icchedbhūtimātmanaḥ||24||
