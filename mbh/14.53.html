Mahābhārata 14.53 (Āśvamedhikaparvan)

1vaiśampāyana uvāca|
tathā prayāntaṃ vārṣṇeyaṃ dvārakāṃ bharatarṣabhāḥ
|
pariṣvajya nyavartanta sānuyātrā parantapāḥ||1||

2punaḥ punaśca vārṣṇeyaṃ paryaṣvajata phālgunaḥ
|
ā cakṣurviṣayāccainaṃ sa dadarśa punaḥ punaḥ||2||

3kṛcchreṇaiva tu tāṃ pārtho govinde viniveśitām
|
sañjahāra tato dṛṣṭiṃ kṛṣṇaścāpyaparājitaḥ||3||

4tasya prayāṇe yānyāsannimittāni mahātmanaḥ
|
bahūnyadbhutarūpāṇi tāni me gadataḥ śṛṇu||4||

5vāyurvegena mahatā rathasya purato vavau
|
kurvanniḥśarkaraṃ mārgaṃ virajaskamakaṇṭakam||5||

6vavarṣa vāsavaścaiva toyaṃ śuci sugandhi ca
|
divyāni caiva puṣpāṇi purataḥ śārṅgadhanvanaḥ||6||

7sa prayāto mahābāhuḥ sameṣu marudhanvasu
|
dadarśātha muniśreṣṭhamuttaṅkamamitaujasam||7||

8sa taṃ sampūjya tejasvī muniṃ pṛthulalocanaḥ
|
pūjitastena ca tadā paryapṛcchadanāmaya||8||

9sa pṛṣṭaḥ kuśalaṃ tena sampūjya madhusūdanam
|
uttaṅko brāhmaṇaśreṣṭhastataḥ papraccha mādhavam||9||

10kaccicchaure tvayā gatvā kurupāṇḍavasadma tat
|
kṛtaṃ saubhrātramacalaṃ tanme vyākhyātumarhasi||10||

11api sandhāya tānvīrānupāvṛtto'si keśava
|
sambandhinaḥ svadayitānsatataṃ vṛṣṇipuṅgava||11||

12kaccitpāṇḍusutāḥ pañca dhṛtarāṣṭrasya cātmajāḥ
|
lokeṣu vihariṣyanti tvayā saha parantapa||12||

13svarāṣṭre te ca rājānaḥ kaccitprāpsyanti vai sukham
|
kauraveṣu praśānteṣu tvayā nāthena keśava||13||

14yā me sambhāvanā tāta tvayi nityamavartata
|
api sā saphalā tāta kṛtā te bharatān prati||14||

15śrībhagavānuvāca|
kṛto yatno mayā pūrvaṃ sauśāmye kauravānprati
|
nāśakyanta yadā sāmye te sthāpayitumañjasā||15||

16tataste nidhanaṃ prāptāḥ sarve sasutabāndhavāḥ
|
na diṣṭamapyatikrāntuṃ śakyaṃ buddhyā balena vā||16||

17maharṣe viditaṃ bhūyaḥ sarvametattavānagha
|
te'tyakrāmanmatiṃ mahyaṃ bhīṣmasya vidurasya ca||17||

18tato yamakṣayaṃ jagmuḥ samāsādyetaretaram
|
pañcaiva pāṇḍavāḥ śiṣṭā hatāmitrā hatātmajāḥ
|
dhārtarāṣṭrāśca nihatāḥ sarve sasutabāndhavāḥ||18||

19ityuktavacane kṛṣṇe bhṛśaṃ krodhasamanvitaḥ
|
uttaṅka ityuvācainaṃ roṣādutphullalocanaḥ||19||

20uttaṅka uvāca|
yasmācchaktena te kṛṣṇa na trātāḥ kurupuṅgavāḥ
|
sambandhinaḥ priyāstasmācchapsye'haṃ tvāmasaṃśayam||20||

21na ca te prasabhaṃ yasmātte nigṛhya nivāritāḥ
|
tasmānmanyuparītastvāṃ śapsyāmi madhusūdana||21||

22tvayā śaktena hi satā mithyācāreṇa mādhava
|
te parītāḥ kuruśreṣṭhā naśyantaḥ sma hyapekṣitāḥ||22||

23vāsudeva uvāca|
śṛṇu me vistareṇedaṃ yadvakṣye bhṛgunandana
|
gṛhāṇānunayaṃ cāpi tapasvī hyasi bhārgava||23||

24śrutvā ca me tadadhyātma muñcethāḥ śāpamadya vai
|
na ca māṃ tapasā'lpena śakto'bhibhavituṃ pumān||24||

25na ca te tapaso nāśamicchāmi tapatāṃ vara
|
tapaste sumahaddīptaṃ guravaścāpi toṣitāḥ||25||

26kaumāraṃ brahmacaryaṃ te jānāmi dvijasattama
|
duḥkhārjitasya tapasastasmānnecchāmi te vyayam||26||
