Mahābhārata 1.133 (Ādiparvan)

1vaiśaṃpāyana uvāca|
tato dhanañjayaṃ droṇaḥ smayamāno'bhyabhāṣata
|
tvayedānīṃ prahartavyametallakṣyaṃ vilokyatām||1||

2madvākyasamakālaṃ te moktavyo'tra bhaveccharaḥ
|
vitatya kārmukaṃ putra tiṣṭha tāvanmuhūrtakam||2||

3evamuktaḥ savyasācī maṇḍalīkṛtakārmukaḥ
|
tasthau bhāsaṃ samuddiśya guruvākyapracoditaḥ||3||

4muhūrtādiva taṃ droṇastathaiva samabhāṣata
|
paśyasyenaṃ sthitaṃ bhāsaṃ drumaṃ māmapi cārjuna||4||

5paśyāmyekaṃ bhāsamiti droṇaṃ pārtho'bhyabhāṣata
|
na tu vṛkṣaṃ bhavantaṃ vā paśyāmīti ca bhārata||5||

6tataḥ prītamanā droṇo muhūrtādiva taṃ punaḥ
|
pratyabhāṣata durdharṣaḥ pāṇḍavānāṃ mahāratham||6||

7bhāsaṃ paśyasi yadyenaṃ tathā brūhi punarvacaḥ
|
śiraḥ paśyāmi bhāsasya na gātramiti so'bravīt||7||

8arjunenaivamuktastu droṇo hṛṣṭatanūruhaḥ
|
muñcasvetyabravītpārthaṃ sa mumocāvicārayan||8||

9tatastasya nagasthasya kṣureṇa niśitena ca
|
śira utkṛtya tarasā pātayāmāsa pāṇḍavaḥ||9||

10tasminkarmaṇi saṃsiddhe paryaṣvajata pāṇḍavam
|
mene ca drupadaṃ saṅkhye sānubandhaṃ parājitam||10||

11kasyacittvatha kālasya saśiṣyo'ṅgirasāṃ varaḥ
|
jagāma gaṅgāmabhito majjituṃ bharatarṣabha||11||

12avagāḍhamatho droṇaṃ salile salilecaraḥ
|
grāho jagrāha balavāñjaṅghānte kālacoditaḥ||12||

13sa samartho'pi mokṣāya śiṣyānsarvānacodayat
|
grāhaṃ hatvā tu mokṣyadhvaṃ māmiti tvarayanniva||13||

14tadvākyasamakālaṃ tu bībhatsurniśitaiḥ śaraiḥ
|
avāryaiḥ pañcabhirgrāhaṃ magnamambhasyatāḍayat||14||

15itare tvatha saṃmūḍhāstatrapatra prapedire
|
taṃ tu dṛṣṭvā kriyopetaṃ droṇo'manyata pāṇḍavam||15||

16viśiṣṭaṃ sarvaśiṣyebhyaḥ prītimāṃścābhavattadā
|
sa pārthabāṇairbahudhā khaṇḍaśaḥ parikalpitaḥ||16||

17grāhaḥ pañcatvamāpede jaṅghāṃ tyaktvā mahātmanaḥ
|
athābravīnmahātmānaṃ bhāradvājo mahāratham||17||

18gṛhāṇedaṃ mahābāho viśiṣṭamatidurdharam
|
astraṃ brahmaśiro nāma saprayoganivartanam||18||

19na ca te mānuṣeṣvetatprayoktavyaṃ kathaṃcana
|
jagadvinirdahedetadalpatejasi pātitam||19||

20asāmānyamidaṃ tāta lokeṣvastraṃ nigadyate
|
taddhārayethāḥ prayataḥ śṛṇu cedaṃ vaco mama||20||

21bādhetāmānuṣaḥ śatruryadi tvāṃ vīra kaścana
|
tadvadhāya prayuñjīthāstadastramidamāhave||21||

22tatheti saṃpratiśrutya bībhatsuḥ sa kṛtāñjaliḥ
|
jagrāha paramāstraṃ tadāha cainaṃ punarguruḥ
|
bhavitā tvatsamo nānyaḥ pumā~lloke dhanurdharaḥ||22||
