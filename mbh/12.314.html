Mahābhārata 12.314 (Śāntiparvan)

1yājñavalkya uvāca |
ete pradhānasya guṇāstrayaḥ puruṣasattama
|
kṛtsnasya caiva jagatastiṣṭhantyanapagāḥ sadā||1||

2avyaktarūpo bhagavān śatadhā ca sahasradhā
|
śatadhā sahasradhā caiva tathā śatasahasradhā||2||

3koṭiśaśca karotyeṣa pratyagātmānamātmanā
|
sāttvikasyottamaṃ sthānaṃ rājasasyeha madhyamam||3||

4tāmasasyādhamaṃ sthānaṃ prāhuradhyātmacintakāḥ
|
kevaleneha puṇyena gatimūrdhvāmavāpnuyāt||4||

5puṇyapāpena mānuṣyamadharmeṇāpyadhogatim
|
dvandvameṣāṃ trayāṇāṃ tu sannipātaṃ ca tadvataḥ||5||

6sattvasya rajasaścaiva tamasaśca śṛṇuṣva me
|
sattvasya tu rajo dṛṣṭaṃ rajasaśca tamastathā||6||

7tamasaśca tathā sattvaṃ sattvasyāvyaktameva ca
|
avyaktaḥ sattvasaṃyukto devalokamavāpnuyāt||7||

8rajaḥsattvasamāyukto mānuṣeṣu prapadyate
|
rajastamobhyāṃ saṃyuktastiryagyoniṣu jāyate||8||

9rājasaistāmasaiḥ sattvairyukto mānuṣamāpnuyāt
|
puṇyapāpaniyuktānāṃ sthānamāhurmahātmanām||9||

10śāśvataṃ cāvyayaṃ caivamakṣayaṃ cāmṛtaṃ ca tat
|
jñānināṃ saṃbhavaṃ śreṣṭhaṃ sthānamavraṇamacyutam
|
atīndriyamabījaṃ ca janmamṛtyutamonudam||10||

11avyaktasthaṃ paraṃ yattatpṛṣṭaste'haṃ narādhipa
|
sa eṣa prakṛtistho hi tatstha ityabhidhīyate||11||

12acetanā caiva matā prakṛtiścāpi pārthiva
|
etenādhiṣṭhitā caiva sṛjate saṃharatyapi||12||

13janaka uvāca |
anādinidhanāvetāvubhāveva mahāmate
|
amūrtimantāvacalāvaprakaṃpyaguṇāguṇau||13||

14agrāhyāvṛṣiśārdūla kathameko hyacetanaḥ
|
cetanāvāṃstathā caikaḥ kṣetrajña iti bhāṣitaḥ||14||

15tvaṃ hi viprendra kārtsnyena mokṣadharmamupāsase
|
sākalyaṃ mokṣadharmasya śrotumicchāmi tattvataḥ||15||

16astitvaṃ kevalatvaṃ ca vinābhāvaṃ tathaiva ca
|
daivatāni ca me brūhi dehaṃ yānyāśritāni vai||16||

17tathaivotkrāmiṇaḥ sthānaṃ dehino vai vipadyataḥ
|
kālena yaddhi prāpnoti sthānaṃ tatprabravīhi me||17||

18sāṃkhyajñānaṃ ca tattvena pṛthagyogaṃ tathaiva ca
|
ariṣṭāni ca tattvāni vaktumarhasi sattama
|
viditaṃ sarvametatte pāṇāvāmalakaṃ yathā||18||
