Mahābhārata 12.48 (Śāntiparvan)

1vaiśampāyana uvāca|
tataḥ sa ca hṛṣīkeśaḥ sa ca rājā yudhiṣṭhiraḥ
|
kṛpādayaśca te sarve catvāraḥ pāṇḍavāśca te ||1||

2rathaistairnagaraprakhyaiḥ patākā-dhvajaśobhitaiḥ
|
yayurāśu kurukṣetraṃ vājibhiḥ śīghragāmibhiḥ ||2||

3te'vatīrya kurukṣetraṃ keśamajjāsthisaṅkulam
|
dehanyāsaḥ kṛto yatra kṣatriyaistairmahātmabhiḥ ||3||

4gajāśvadehāsthicayaiḥ parvatairiva sañcitam
|
naraśīrṣakapālaiśca śaṅkhairiva ca sarvaśaḥ ||4||

5citāsahasrapracitaṃ varmaśastrasamākulam
|
āpānabhūmiṃ kālasya tathā bhuktojjhitāmiva ||5||

6bhūtasaṅghānucaritaṃ rakṣogaṇaniṣevitam
|
paśyantaste kurukṣetraṃ yayurāśu mahārathāḥ ||6||

7gacchanneva mahābāhuḥ sa vai yādavanandanaḥ
|
yudhiṣṭhirāya provāca jāmadagnyasya vikramam ||7||

8amī rāmahṛdāḥ pañca dṛśyante pārtha dūrataḥ
|
teṣu santarpayāmāsa pitṝn kṣatriyaśoṇitaiḥ ||8||

9triḥsaptakṛtvo vasudhāṃ kṛtvā niḥkṣatriyāṃ prabhuḥ
|
ihedānīṃ tato rāmaḥ karmaṇo virarāma ha ||9||

10yudhiṣṭhira uvāca|
tri:saptakṛtvaḥ pṛthivī kṛtā niḥkṣatriyā purā
|
rāmeṇeti tathā''ttha tvamatra me saṃśayo mahān ||10||

11kṣatrabījaṃ yathā dagdhaṃ rāmeṇa yadupuṅgava
|
kathaṃ bhūyaḥ samutpattiḥ kṣatrasyāmitavikrama ||11||

12mahātmanā bhagavatā rāmeṇa yadupuṅgava
|
kathamutsāditaṃ kṣatraṃ kathaṃ vṛddhimupāgatam ||12||

13mahatā rathayuddhena koṭiśaḥ kṣatriyā hatāḥ
|
tathā'bhūcca mahī kīrṇā kṣatriyairvadatāṃ vara ||13||

14kimarthaṃ bhārgaveṇedaṃ kṣatramutsāditaṃ purā
|
rāmeṇa yaduśārdūla kurukṣetre mahātmanā ||14||

15etanme chindhi vārṣṇeya saṃśayaṃ tārkṣyaketana
|
āgamo hi paraḥ kṛṣṇa tvatto no vāsavānuja ||15||

16vaiśampāyana uvāca|
tato yathāvatsa gadāgrajaḥ prabhuḥ śaśaṃsa tasmai nikhilena tattvataḥ
|
yudhiṣṭhirāyāpratimaujase tadā yathā'bhavat kṣatriyasaṅkulā mahī ||16||
