Mahābhārata 13.130 (Anuśāsanaparvan)

1bhīṣma uvāca|
tatastvṛṣigaṇāḥ sarve pitaraśca sadevatāḥ
|
arundhatīṃ tapovṛddhāmapṛcchanta samāhitāḥ ||1||

2samānaśīlāṃ vīryeṇa vasiṣṭhasya mahātmanaḥ
|
tvatto dharmarahasyāni śrotumicchāmahe vayam
|
yatte guhyatamaṃ bhadre tatprabhāṣitumarhasi ||2||

3arundhatyuvāca |
tapovṛddhirmayā prāptā bhavatāṃ smaraṇena vai
|
bhavatāṃ ca prasādena dharmānvakṣyāmi śāśvatān||3||

4saguhyānsarahasyāṃśca tān śṛṇudhvamaśeṣataḥ
|
śraddadhāne prayoktavyā yasya śuddhaṃ tathā manaḥ ||4||

5aśraddadhāno mānī ca brahmahā gurutalpagaḥ
|
asaṃbhāṣyā hi catvāro naiṣāṃ dharmaṃ prakāśayet||5||

6ahanyahani yo dadyātkapilāṃ dvādaśīḥ samāḥ
|
māsi māsi ca satreṇa yo yajeta sadā naraḥ||6||

7gavāṃ śatasahasraṃ ca yo dadyājjyeṣṭhapuṣkare
|
na taddharmaphalaṃ tulyamatithiryasya tuṣyati ||7||

8śrūyatāṃ cāparo dharmo manuṣyāṇāṃ sukhāvahaḥ
|
śraddadhānena kartavyaḥ saharasyo mahāphalaḥ ||8||

9kalyamutthāya gomadhye gṛhya darbhānsahodakān
|
niṣiñceta gavāṃ śṛṅge mastakena ca tajjalam||9||

10pratīccheta nirāhārastasya dharmaphalaṃ śṛṇu
|
śrūyante yāni tīrthāni triṣu lokeṣu kānicit||10||

11siddhacāraṇajuṣṭāni sevitāni maharṣibhiḥ
|
abhiṣekaḥ samasteṣāṃ gavāṃ śṛṅgodakasya ca ||11||

12sādhu sādhviti coddiṣṭaṃ daivataiḥ pitṛbhistathā
|
bhūtaiścaiva susaṃhṛṣṭaiḥ pūjitā sā'pyarundhatī ||12||

13pitāmaha uvāca|
aho dharmo mahābhāge sarahasya udāhṛtaḥ
|
varaṃ dadāmi te dhanye tapaste vardhatāṃ sadā ||13||

14yama uvāca|
ramaṇīyā kathā divyā yuṣmatto yā mayā śrutā
|
śrūyatāṃ citraguptasya bhāṣitaṃ mama ca priyam ||14||

15rahasyaṃ dharmasaṃyuktaṃ śakyaṃ śrotuṃ maharṣibhiḥ
|
śraddadhānena martyena ātmano hitamicchatā ||15||

16na hi puṇyaṃ tathā pāpaṃ kṛtaṃ kiñcidvinaśyati
|
parvakāle ca yatkiñcidādityaṃ cādhitiṣṭhati ||16||

17pretalokaṃ gate martye tattatsarvaṃ vibhāvasuḥ
|
pratijānāti puṇyātmā tacca tatropayujyate ||17||

18kiñciddharmaṃ pravakṣyāmi citraguptamataṃ śubham
|
pānīyaṃ caiva dīpaṃ ca dātavyaṃ satataṃ tathā ||18||

19upānahau ca cchatraṃ ca kapilā ca yathātatham
|
puṣkare kapilā deyā brāhmaṇe vedapārage||19||

20agnihotraṃ ca yatnena sarvaśaḥ pratipālayet
|
ayaṃ caivāparo dharmaścitraguptena bhāṣitaḥ ||20||

21phalamasya pṛthaktvena śrotumarhanti sattamāḥ
|
pralayaṃ sarvabhūtaistu gantavyaṃ kālaparyayāt ||21||

22tatra durgamanuprāptāḥ śruttṛṣṇāparipīḍitāḥ
|
dahyamānā vipacyante na tatrāsti palāyanam ||22||

23andhakāraṃ tamo ghoraṃ praviśantyalpabuddhayaḥ
|
tatra dharmaṃ pravakṣyāmi yena durgāṇi santaret||23||

24alpavyayaṃ mahārthaṃ ca pretya caiva sukhodayam
|
pānīyasya guṇā divyāḥ pretaloke viśeṣataḥ ||24||

25tatra puṇyodakā nāma nadī teṣāṃ vidhīyate
|
akṣayaṃ salilaṃ tatra śītalaṃ hyamṛtopamam ||25||

26sa tatra toyaṃ pibati pānīyaṃ yaḥ prayacchati
|
pradīpasya pradānena śrūyatāṃ guṇavistaraḥ ||26||

27tamondhakāraṃ niyataṃ dīpado na prapaśyati
|
prabhāṃ cāsya prayacchanti somabhāskarapāvakāḥ||27||

28devatāścānumanyante vimalāḥ sarvato diśaḥ
|
dyotate ca yathā''dityaḥ pretalokagato naraḥ ||28||

29tasmāddīpaḥ pradātavyaḥ pānīyaṃ ca viśeṣataḥ
|
kapilāṃ ye prayacchanti brāhmaṇe vedapārage||29||

30puṣkare ca viśeṣeṇa śrūyatāṃ tasya yatphalam
|
gośataṃ savṛṣaṃ tena dattaṃ bhavati śāśvatam ||30||

31pāpaṃ karma ca yatkiñcidbrahmahatyāsamaṃ bhavet
|
śodhayetkapilā hyekā pradattaṃ gośataṃ yathā ||31||

32tasmāttu kapilā deyā kaumudyāṃ jyeṣṭhapuṣkare
|
na teṣāṃ viṣamaṃ kiñcinna duḥkhaṃ na ca kaṇṭakāḥ||32||

33upānahau ca yo dadyātpātrabhūte dvijottame
|
chatradāne sukhāṃ chāyāṃ labhate paralokagaḥ ||33||

34na hi dattasya dānasya nāśo'stīha kadācana
|
citraguptamataṃ śrutvā hṛṣṭaromā vibhāvasuḥ ||34||

35uvāca devatāḥ sarvāḥ pitṝṃścaiva mahādyutiḥ
|
śrutaṃ hi citraguptasya dharmaguhyaṃ mahātmanaḥ ||35||

36śraddadhānāśca ye martyā brāhmaṇeṣu mahātmasu
|
dānametatprayacchanti na teṣāṃ vidyate bhayam ||36||

37dharmadoṣāstvime pañca yeṣāṃ nāstīha niṣkṛtiḥ
|
asaṃbhāṣyā anācārā varjanīyā narādhamāḥ ||37||

38brahmahā caiva goghnaśca paradārarataśca yaḥ
|
aśraddadhānaśca naraḥ striyaṃ yaścopajīvati ||38||

39pretalokagatā hyete narake pāpakarmiṇaḥ
|
pacyante vai yathā mīnāḥ pūyaśoṇitabhojanāḥ||39||

40asaṃbhāṣyāḥ pitṝṇāṃ ca devānāṃ caiva pañca te
|
snātakānāṃ ca viprāṇāṃ ye cānye ca tapodhanāḥ||40||
