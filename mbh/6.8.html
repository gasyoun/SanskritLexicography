Mahābhārata 6.8 (Bhīṣmaparvan)

1dhṛtarāṣṭra uvāca|
varṣāṇāṃ caiva nāmāni parvatānāṃ ca sañjaya
|
ācakṣva me yathātattvaṃ ye ca parvatavāsinaḥ ||1||

2sañjaya uvāca|
dakṣiṇena tu śvetasya nīlasyaivottareṇa tu
|
varṣaṃ ramaṇakaṃ nāma jāyante tatra mānavāḥ ||2||

3śuklābhijanasampannāḥ sarve supriyadarśanāḥ
|
niḥsapatnāśca te sarve jāyante tatra mānavāḥ ||3||

4daśa varṣasahasrāṇi śatāni daśa pañca ca
|
jīvanti te mahārāja nityaṃ muditamānasāḥ ||4||

5dakṣiṇena tu nīlasya niṣadhasyottareṇa tu
|
varṣaṃ hiraṇmayaṃ nāma yatra hairaṇvatī nadī ||5||

6yatra cāyaṃ mahārāja pakṣirāṭ patagottamaḥ
|
yakṣānugā mahārāja dhaninaḥ priyadarśanāḥ ||6||

7mahābalāstatra janā rājanmuditamānasāḥ
|
ekādaśasahasrāṇi varṣāṇāṃ te janādhipa ||7||

8āyuḥpramāṇaṃ jīvanti śatāni daśa pañca ca
|
śrṛṅgāṇi ca vicitrāṇi trīṇyeva manujādhipa ||8||

9ekaṃ maṇimayaṃ tatra tathaikaṃ raukmamadbhutam
|
sarvaratnamayaṃ caikaṃ bhavanairupaśobhitam ||9||

10tatra svayaṃprabhā devī nityaṃ vasati śāṇḍilī
|
uttareṇa tu śrṛṅgasya samudrānte janādhipa ||10||

11varṣamairāvataṃ nāma tasmācchṛṅgamataḥ param
|
na tatra sūryastapati na jīryante ca mānavāḥ ||11||

12candramāśca sanakṣatro jyotirbhūta ivāvṛtaḥ
|
padmaprabhāḥ padmavarṇāḥ padmapatranibhekṣaṇāḥ ||12||

13padmapatrasugandhāśca jāyante tatra mānavāḥ
|
aniṣpandā iṣṭagandhā nirāhārā jitendriyāḥ ||13||

14devalokacyutāḥ sarve tathā virajaso nṛpa
|
trayodaśasahasrāṇi varṣāṇāṃ te janādhipa ||14||

15āyuḥpramāṇaṃ jīvanti narā bharatasattama
|
kṣīrodasya samudrasya tathaivottarataḥ prabhuḥ
|
harirvasati vaikuṇṭhaḥ śakaṭe kanakāmaye ||15||

16aṣṭacakraṃ hi tadyānaṃ bhūtayuktaṃ manojavam
|
agnivarṇaṃ mahātejo jāmbūnadavibhūṣitam ||16||

17sa prabhuḥ sarvabhūtānāṃ vibhuśca bharatarṣabha
|
saṅkṣepo vistaraścaiva kartā kārayitā tathā ||17||

18pṛthivyāpastathā''kāśaṃ vāyustejaśca pārthiva
|
sa yajñaḥ sarvabhūtanāmāsyaṃ tasya hutāśanaḥ ||18||

19vaiśampāyana uvāca|
evamuktaḥ sañjayena dhṛtarāṣṭro mahāmanāḥ
|
dhyānamanvagamadrājan putrānprati janādhipa ||19||

20sa vicintya mahātejāḥ punarevābravīdvacaḥ
|
asaṃśayaṃ sūtaputra kālaḥ saṅkṣipate jagat ||20||

21sṛjate ca punaḥ sarvaṃ neha vidyati śāśvatam
|
naro nārāyaṇaścaiva sarvajñaḥ sarvabhūtahṛt ||21||

22devā vaikuṇṭhamityāhurnarā viṣṇumiti prabhum ||22||
