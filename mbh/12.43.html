Mahābhārata 12.43 (Śāntiparvan)

1vaiśampāyana uvāca|
abhiṣikto mahāprājño rājyaṃ prāpta yudhiṣṭhiraḥ
|
dāśārhaṃ puṇḍarīkākṣamuvāca prāñjaliḥ śuciḥ ||1||

2tava kṛṣṇa prasādena nayena ca balena ca
|
buddhyā ca yaduśārdūla tathā vikramaṇena ca ||2||

3punaḥ prāptamidaṃ rājyaṃ pitṛpaitāmahaṃ mayā
|
namaste puṇḍarīkākṣa punaḥ punararindama ||3||

4tvāmekamāhuḥ puruṣaṃ tvāmāhuḥ sātvatāṃ patim
|
nāmabhistvāṃ bahuvidhaiḥ stuvanti prayatā dvijāḥ ||4||

5viśvakarmannamaste'stu viśvātmanviśvasambhava
|
viṣṇo jiṣṇo hare kṛṣṇa vaikuṇṭha puruṣottama ||5||

6adityāḥ saptadhā tvaṃ tu purāṇo garbhatāṃ gataḥ
|
pṛśnigarbhastvamevaikastriyugaṃ tvāṃ vadantyapi ||6||

7śuciśravā hṛṣīkeśo ghṛtārcirhaṃsa ucyate
|
tricakṣuḥ śambhurekastvaṃ vibhurdāmodaro'pi ca ||7||

8varāho'gnirbṛhadbhānurvṛṣabhastārkṣyalakṣaṇaḥ
|
anīkasāhaḥ puruṣaḥ śipiviṣṭa urukramaḥ ||8||

9variṣṭha ugrasenānīḥ satyo vājasanirguhaḥ
|
acyutaścyāvano'rīṇāṃ saṃskṛto vikṛtirvṛṣaḥ ||9||

10kṛṣṇadharmastvamevādivṛṣadarbho vṛṣākapiḥ
|
sindhurvidharmastrikakup tridhāmā tridivācyutaḥ ||10||

11samrāḍ virāṭ svarāṭ caiva surarājo bhavodbhavaḥ
|
vibhurbhūratibhūḥ kṛṣṇaḥ kṛṣṇavartmā tvameva ca ||11||

12sviṣṭakṛdbhiṣajāvartaḥ kapilastvaṃ ca vāmanaḥ
|
yajño dhruvaḥ pataṅgaśca yajñasenastvamucyase ||12||

13śikhaṇḍī nahuṣo babhrurdivaspṛk tvaṃ punarvasuḥ
|
subabhrū rukmayajñaśca suṣeṇo dundubhistathā ||13||

14gabhastinemiḥ śrīpadmaḥ puṣkaraḥ puṣpadhāraṇaḥ
|
ṛbhurvibhuḥ sarvasūkṣmaścāritraṃ caiva paṭhyase ||14||

15ambhonidhistvaṃ brahmā tvaṃ pavitraṃ dhāma dhāmavit
|
hiraṇyagarbhaṃ tvāmāhuḥ svadhā svāhā ca keśava ||15||

16yonistvamasya pralayaśca kṛṣṇa tvamevedaṃ sṛjasi viśvamagre
|
viśvaṃ cedaṃ tvadvaśe viśvayone namo'stu te śārṅgacakrāsipāṇe ||16||

17evaṃ stuto dharmarājena kṛṣṇaḥ sabhāmadhye prītimānpuṣkarākṣaḥ
|
tamabhyanandadbhārataṃ puṣkalābhirvāgbhirjyeṣṭhaṃ pāṇḍavaṃ yādavāgryaḥ ||17||
