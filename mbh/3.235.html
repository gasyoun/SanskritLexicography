Mahābhārata 3.235 (Āraṇyakaparvan)

1vaiśampāyana uvāca|
mārkaṇḍeyādibhirvipraiḥ pāṇḍavaiśca mahātmabhiḥ
|
kathābhiranukūlābhiḥ saha sthitvā janārdanaḥ||1||

2tatastaiḥ saṃvidaṃ kṛtvā yathāvanmadhusūdanaḥ
|
ārurukṣū rathaṃ satyāmāhvayāmāsa keśavaḥ||2||

3satyabhāmā tatastatra svajitvā drupadātmajām
|
uvāca vacanaṃ hṛdyaṃ yathābhāvaṃ samāhitam||3||

4kṛṣṇe mā bhūt tavotkaṇṭhā mā vyathā mā prajāgaraḥ
|
bhartṛbhirdevasaṅkāśairjitāṃ prāpsyasi medinīm||4||

5na hyevaṃ śīlasampannā naivaṃ pūjitalakṣaṇāḥ
|
prāpnuvanti ciraṃ kleśaṃ yathā tvamasitekṣaṇe||5||

6avaśyaṃ ca tvayā bhūmiriyaṃ nihatakaṇṭakā
|
bhartṛbhiḥ saha bhoktavyā nirdvandveti śrutaṃ mayā||6||

7dhārtarāṣṭravadhaṃ kṛtvā vairāṇi pratiyātya ca
|
yudhiṣṭhirasthāṃ pṛthivīṃ drakṣyase drupadātmaje||7||

8yāstāḥ pravrājamānāṃ tvāṃ prāhasan darpamohitāḥ
|
tāḥ kṣipraṃ hatasaṅkalpā drakṣyasi tvaṃ kurustriyaḥ||8||

9tava duḥkhopapannāyā yairācaritamapriyam
|
viddhi samprasthitān sarvāṃstān kṛṣṇe yamasādanam||9||

10putraste prativindhyaśca sutasomastathāvidhaḥ
|
śrutakarmā'rjuniścaiva śatānīkaśca nākuliḥ||10||

11sahadevācca yo jātaḥ śrutasenastavātmajaḥ
|
sarve kuśalino vīrāḥ kṛtāstrāśca sutāstava||11||

12abhimanyuriva prītā dvāravatyāṃ ratā bhṛśam
|
tvamiveṣāṃ subhadrā ca prītyā sarvātmanā sthitā ||12||

13prīyate tava nirdvandvā tebhyaśca vigatajvarā
|
duḥkhitā tena duḥkhena sukhena sukhitā tathā||13||

14bhajet sarvātmanā caiva pradyumnajananī tathā
|
bhānuprabhṛtibhiścainān viśinaṣṭi ca keśavaḥ||14||

15bhojanācchādane caiṣāṃ nityaṃ me śvaśuraḥ sthitaḥ
|
rāmaprabhṛtayaḥ sarve bhajantyandhakavṛṣṇayaḥ||15||

16tulyo hi praṇayasteṣāṃ pradyumnasya ca bhāvini
|
evamādi priyaṃ satyaṃ hṛdyamuktvā manonugam||16||

17gamanāya manaścakre vāsudevarathaṃ prati
|
tāṃ kṛṣṇāṃ kṛṣṇamahiṣī cakārābhipradakṣiṇam||17||

18āruroha rathaṃ śaureḥ satyabhāmā'tha bhāvinī
|
smayitvā tu yaduśreṣṭho draupadīṃ parisāntvya ca
|
upāvartya tataḥ śīghrairhayaiḥ prāyāt puraṃ svakam||18||
