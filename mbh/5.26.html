Mahābhārata 5.26 (Udyogaparvan)

1yudhiṣṭhira uvāca|
kāṃ nu vācaṃ sañjaya me śṛṇoṣi yuddhaiṣiṇīṃ yena yuddhādbibheṣi
|
ayuddhaṃ vai tāta yuddhādgarīyaḥ kastallabdhvā jātu yuddhyeta sūta ||1||

2akurvataścet puruṣasya sañjaya siddhyet saṅkalpo manasā yaṃ yamicchet
|
na karma kuryādviditaṃ mamaitadanyatra yuddhādbahu yallaghīyaḥ ||2||

3kuto yuddhaṃ jātu naro'vagacchet ko devaśapto hi vṛṇīta yuddham
|
sukhaiṣiṇaḥ karma kurvanti pārthā dharmādahīnaṃ yacca lokasya pathyam ||3||

4dharmodayaṃ sukhamāśaṃsamānāḥ kṛcchropāyaṃ tattvataḥ karma duḥkham
|
sukhaṃ prepsurvijighāṃsuśca duḥkhaṃ ya indriyāṇāṃ prītirasānugāmī||4||

5karmābhidhyā svaśarīraṃ dunoti yayā pramukto na karoti duḥkham
|
yathedhyamānasya samiddhatejaso bhūyo balaṃ vardhate pāvakasya||5||

6kāmārthalābhena tathaiva bhūyo na tṛpyate sarpiṣevāgniriddhaḥ
|
saṃpaśyemaṃ bhogacayaṃ mahāntaṃ sahāsmābhirdhṛtarāṣṭrasya rājñaḥ||6||

7nāśreyānīśvaro vigrahāṇāṃ nāśreyānvai gītaśabdaṃ śṛṇoti
|
nāśreyānvai sevate mālyagandhānna cāpyaśreyānanulepanāni||7||

8nāśreyānvai prāvārān saṃvivaste kathaṃ tvasmān saṃpraṇudet kurubhyaḥ
|
atraiva syādabudhasyaiva kāmaḥ prāyaḥ śarīre hṛdayaṃ dunoti ||8||

9svayaṃ rājā viṣamasthaḥ pareṣu sāmarthyamanvicchati tanna sādhu
|
yathātmanaḥ paśyati vṛttameva tathā pareṣāmapi so'bhyupaiti ||9||

10āsannamagniṃ tu nidāghakāle gambhīrakakṣe gahane visṛjya
|
yathā vivṛddhaṃ vāyuvaśena śocet kṣemaṃ mumukṣuḥ śiśiravyapāye ||10||

11prāptaiśvaryo dhṛtarāṣṭro'dya rājā lālapyate sañjaya kasya hetoḥ
|
pragṛhya durbuddhimanārjave rataṃ putraṃ mandaṃ mūḍhamamantriṇaṃ tu ||11||

12anāptavaccāptatamasya vācaḥ suyodhano vidurasyāvamatya
|
sutasya rājā dhṛtarāṣṭraḥ priyaiṣī saṃbudhyamāno viśate'dharmameva ||12||

13medhāvinaṃ hyarthakāmaṃ kurūṇāṃ bahuśrutaṃ vāgminaṃ śīlavantam
|
sa taṃ rājā dhṛtarāṣṭraḥ kurubhyo na sasmāra viduraṃ putrakāmyāt ||13||

14mānaghnasyāsau mānakāmasya cerṣoḥ saṃrambhiṇaścārthadharmātigasya
|
durbhāṣiṇo manyuvaśānugasya kāmātmano daurhṛdairbhāvitasya ||14||

15aneyasyāśreyaso dīrghamanyormitradruhaḥ sañjaya pāpabuddheḥ
|
sutasya rājā dhṛtarāṣṭraḥ priyaiṣī prapaśyamānaḥ prājahāddharmakāmau ||15||

16tadaiva me sañjaya dīvyato'bhūnmatiḥ kurūṇāmāgataḥ syādabhāvaḥ
|
kāvyāṃ vācaṃ viduro bhāṣamāṇo na vindate yaddhārtarāṣṭrāt praśaṃsām ||16||

17kṣatturyadā nānvavartanta buddhiṃ kṛcchraṃ kurūn sūta tadā'bhyājagāma
|
yāvat prajñāmanvavartanta tasya tāvatteṣāṃ rāṣṭravṛddhirbabhūva ||17||

18tadarthalubdhasya nibodha me'dya ye mantriṇo dhārtarāṣṭrasya sūta
|
duḥśāsanaḥ śakuniḥ sūtaputro gāvalgaṇe paśya saṃmohamasya ||18||

19so'haṃ na paśyāmi parīkṣamāṇaḥ kathaṃ svasti syāt kurusṛñjayānām
|
āttaiśvaryo dhṛtarāṣṭraḥ parebhyaḥ pravrājite vidure dīrghadṛṣṭau ||19||

20āśaṃsate vai dhṛtarāṣṭraḥ saputro mahārājyamasapatnaṃ pṛthivyām
|
tasmin śamaḥ kevalaṃ nopalabhyaḥ sarvaṃ svakaṃ madgate manyate'rtham ||20||

21yattat karṇo manyate pāraṇīyaṃ yuddhe gṛhītāyudhamarjunaṃ vai
|
āsaṃśca yuddhāni purā mahānti kathaṃ karṇo nābhavaddvīpa eṣām ||21||

22karṇaśca jānāti suyodhanaśca droṇaśca jānāti pitāmahaśca
|
anye ca ye kuravastatra santi yathārjunānnāstyaparo dhanurdharaḥ ||22||

23jānantyetat kuravaḥ sarva eva ye cāpyanye bhūmipālāḥ sametāḥ
|
duryodhane rājyamihābhavadyathā arindame phālgune'vidyamāne ||23||

24tenānubandhaṃ manyate dhārtarāṣṭraḥ śakyaṃ hartuṃ pāṇḍavānāṃ mamatvam
|
kirīṭinā tālamātrāyudhena tadvedinā saṃyugaṃ tatra gatvā ||24||

25gāṇḍīvavisphāritaśabdamājāvaśṛṇvānā dhārtarāṣṭrā dhriyante
|
kruddhaṃ na cedīkṣate bhīmasenaṃ suyodhano manyate siddhamartham ||25||

26indro'pyetannotsahettāta hartumaiśvaryaṃ no jīvati bhīmasene
|
dhanañjaye nakule caiva sūta tathā vīre sahadeve sahiṣṇau ||26||

27sa cedetāṃ pratipadyeta buddhiṃ vṛddho rājā saha putreṇa sūta
|
evaṃ raṇe pāṇḍavakopadagdhā na naśyeyuḥ sañjaya dhārtarāṣṭrāḥ ||27||

28jānāmi tvaṃ kleśamasmāsu vṛttaṃ tvāṃ pūjayan sañjayāhaṃ kṣameyam
|
yaccāsmākaṃ kauravairbhṛtapūrvaṃ yā no vṛttirdhārtarāṣṭre tadā''sīt ||28||

29adyāpi tattatra tathaiva vartatāṃ śāntiṃ gamiṣyāmi yathā tvamāttha
|
indreprasthe bhavatu mamaiva rājyaṃ suyodhano yacchatu bhāratāgryaḥ ||29||
