Mahābhārata 12.2 (Śāntiparvan)

1vaiśampāyana uvāca|
sa evamuktastu munirnarado vadatāṃ varaḥ
|
kathayāmāsa tatsarvaṃ yathā śaptaḥ sa sūtajaḥ||1||

2evametanmahābāho yathā vadasi bhārata
|
na karṇārjunayoḥ kiṃcida‌viṣahyaṃ bhavedraṇe ||2||

3guhyametattu devānāṃ kathayiṣyāmi te'nagha
|
tannibodha mahābāho yathā vṛttamidaṃ purā ||3||

4kṣetraṃ svargaṃ kathaṃ gacchecchastrapūtamiti prabho
|
saṃgharṣajananastasmātkanyāgarbho vinirmitaḥ ||4||

5sa bālastejasā yuktaḥ sūtaputratvamāgataḥ
|
cakārāṅgirasāṃ śreṣṭhāddhanurvedaṃ gurostadā ||5||

6sa balaṃ bhīmasenasya phālgunasya ca lāghavam
|
buddhiṃ ca tava rājendra yamayorvinayaṃ tadā ||6||

7sakhyaṃ ca vāsudevena bālye gāṇḍīvadhanvanaḥ
|
prajānāmanurāgaṃ ca cintayāno vyadahyata ||7||

8sa sakhyamakarodbālye rājñā duryodhanena ca
|
yuṣmābhirnityasaṃdviṣṭo daivāccāpi khabhāvataḥ ||8||

9vīryādhikamathālakṣya dhanurvede dhanañjayam
|
droṇaṃ rahasyupāgamya karṇo vacanamabravīt ||9||

10brahmāstraṃ vettumicchāmi sarahasyanivartanam
|
arjunena samaṃ cāhaṃ yudhyeyamiti me matiḥ ||10||

11samaḥ śiṣyeṣu vaḥ snehaḥ putre caiva tathā dhruvam
|
tvatprasādānna māṃ brūyurakṛtāstraṃ vicakṣaṇāḥ||11||

12droṇastathoktaḥ karṇena sāpekṣaḥ phālgunaṃ prati
|
daurātmyaṃ caiva karṇasya viditvā tamuvāca ha ||12||

13brahmāstraṃ brāhmaṇo vidyādyathāvaccaritavrataḥ
|
kṣatriyo vā tapasvī yo nānyo vidyātkathañcana ||13||

14ityukto'ṅgirasāṃ śreṣṭhamāmantrya pratipūjya ca
|
jagāma sahasā rāmaṃ mahendraṃ parvataṃ prati ||14||

15sa tu rāmamupāgamya śirasā'bhipraṇamya ca
|
brāhmaṇo bhārgavo'smīti gauraveṇābhyagacchata ||15||

16rāmastaṃ pratijagrāha pṛṣṭvā gotrādi sarvaśaḥ
|
uṣyatāṃ svāgataṃ ceti prītimāṃścābhavadbhṛśam ||16||

17tatra karṇasya vasato mahendre svargasannibhe
|
gandharvai rākṣasairyakṣairdevaiścāsītsamāgamaḥ ||17||

18sa tatreṣvastramakarodbhṛguśreṣṭhādyathāvidhi
|
priyaścābhavadatyarthaṃ deva-dānava-rakṣasām ||18||

19sa kadācitsasudrānte vicarannāśramāntike
|
ekaḥ khaḍgadhanuṣpāṇiḥ paricakrāma sūryajaḥ ||19||

20so'gnihotraprasaktasya kasyacidbrahmavādinaḥ
|
jaghānājñānataḥ pārtha homadhenuṃ yadṛcchayā ||20||

21tadajñānakṛtaṃ matvā brāhmaṇāya nyavedayat
|
karṇaḥ prasādayaṃścainamidamityabravīdvacaḥ ||21||

22abuddhipūrvaṃ bhagavan dhenureṣā hatā tava
|
mayā tatra prasādaṃ ca kuruṣveti punaḥ punaḥ ||22||

23taṃ sa vipro'bravītkruddho vācā nirbhartsayanniva
|
durācāravadhārhastvaṃ phalaṃ prāpnuhi durmate ||23||

24yena vispardhase nityaṃ yadarthaṃ ghaṭase'niśam
|
yudhyatastena te pāpa bhūmiścakraṃ grasiṣyati ||24||

25tataścakre mahīgraste mūrdhānaṃ te vicetasaḥ
|
pātayiṣyati vikramyaḥ śatrurgaccha narādhama ||25||

26yatheyaṃ gaurhatā mūḍha pramattena tvayā mama
|
pramattasya tathā'rātiḥ śiraste pātayiṣyati ||26||

27śaptaḥ prasāda‌yāmāsa karṇastaṃ dvijasattamam
|
gobhirdhanaiśca ratnaiśca sa cainaṃ punarabravīt ||27||

28na hi me'vyāhṛtaṃ kuryātsarvaloko'pi kevalam
|
gaccha vā tiṣṭha vā yadvā kāryaṃ te tatsamācara ||28||

29ityukto brāhmaṇenātha karṇo dainyāda‌dhomukhaḥ
|
rāmamabhyagamadbhītastadeva manasā smaran ||29||
