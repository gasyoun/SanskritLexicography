Mahābhārata 1.68 (Ādiparvan)

1janamejaya uvāca|
tvattaḥ śrutamidaṃ brahmandevadānavarakṣasām
|
aṃśāvataraṇaṃ samyaggandharvāpsaraso tathā ||1||

2imaṃ tu bhūya icchāmi kurūṇāṃ vaṃśamāditaḥ
|
kathyamānaṃ tvayā vipra viprarṣigaṇasaṃnidhau ||2||

3vaiśaṃpāyana uvāca|
yauravāṇāṃ vaṃśakaro duṣyanto nāma vīryavān
|
pṛthivyāścaturantāyā goptā bharatasattama ||3||

4caturbhāgaṃ bhuvaḥ kṛtsnaṃ yo bhuṅkte manujeśvaraḥ
|
samudrāvaraṇāṃścāpi deśānsa samitiṃjayaḥ ||4||

5āmlecchāvadhikānsarvānsa bhuṅkte ripumardanaḥ
|
ratnākarasamudrāṃ tāṃścāturvarṇyajanāvṛtān ||5||

6na varṇasaṃkarakaro na kṛṣyākarakṛjjanaḥ
|
na pāpakṛtkaścidāsīttasminrājani śāsati ||6||

7dharme ratiṃ sevamānā dharmārthāvamipedire
|
tadā narā naravyāghra tasmiñjanapadeśvare ||7||

8nāsaccaurabhayaṃ tāta na kṣudhāmayamaṇvapi
|
nāsīdvyādhibhayaṃ cāpi tasmiñjanapadeśvare||8||

9svadharme remire varṇā daive karmaṇi niḥspṛhāḥ
|
tamāśritya mahīpālamāsaṃścaivākutobhayāḥ ||9||

10kālavarṣī ca parjanyaḥ sasyāni rasavanti ca
|
sarvaratnasamṛddhā ca mahī paśumatī tathā ||10||

11svakarmaniratā viprā nānṛtaṃ teṣu vidyate
|
sa cādbhutamahārvāryoṃ vajrasaṃhanano yuvā||11||

12udyamya mandaraṃ dorbhyāṃ vahetsavanakānanam
|
catuṣpathagadayuddhe sarvapraharaṇeṣu ca||12||

13nāgapṛṣṭhe'śvapṛṣṭhe ca babhūva pariniṣṭhitaḥ
|
bale viṣṇusamaścāsīttejasā bhāskaropamaḥ||13||

14akṣobhyatve'rṇavasamaḥ sahiṣṇutve dharāsamaḥ
|
saṃmataḥ sa mahīpālaḥ prasannapurarāṣṭravān ||14||

15bhūyo dharmaparairbhāvairmuditaṃ janamādiśat ||15||
