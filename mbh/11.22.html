Mahābhārata 11.22 (Strīparvan)

1gāndhāryuvāca|āvantyaṃ bhīmasenena bhakṣayanti nipātitam
|
gṛdhragomāyavaḥ śūraṃ dūrabandhumabandhuvat||1||

2taṃ paśya kadanaṃ kṛtvā śūrāṇāṃ madhusūdana
|
śayānaṃ vīraśayane rudhireṇa samukṣitam||2||

3taṃ sṛgālāśca kaṅkāśca kravyādāśca pṛthagvidhāḥ
|
tena tena vikarṣanti paśya kālasya paryayam||3||

4śayānaṃ vīraśayane śūramākrandakāriṇam
|
āvantyamabhito nāryo rudatyaḥ paryupāsate||4||

5prātipeyaṃ maheṣvāsaṃ hataṃ bhallena bāhlikam
|
prasuptamiva śārdulaṃ paśya kṛṣṇa manasvinam||5||

6atīva mukhavarṇo'sya nihatasyāpi śobhate
|
somasyavābhipūrṇasya paurṇamāsyāṃ samudyataḥ||6||

7putraśokābhitaptena pratijñāṃ cābhirakṣatā
|
pākaśāsaninā saṅkhye vārdhakṣatrirnipātitaḥ||7||

8ekādaśa camūrbhittvā rakṣyamāṇaṃ mahātmanā
|
satyaṃ cikīrṣatā paśya hatamenaṃ jayadratham||8||

9sindhusauvīrabhartāraṃ darpapūrṇaṃ manasvinam
|
bhakṣayanti śivā gṛdhrā janārdana jayadratham||9||

10saṃrakṣyamāṇaṃ bhāryābhiranuraktābhiracyuta
|
bhīṣayantyo vikarṣanti gahanaṃ nimnamantikāt||10||

11tametāḥ paryupāsante rakṣyamāṇaṃ mahābhujam
|
sindhusauvīrabhartāraṃ kāmbhojayavanastriyaḥ||11||

12yadā kṛṣṇāmupādāya prādravatkekayaiḥ saha
|
tadaiva vadhyaḥ pāṇḍūnāṃ janārdana jayadrathaḥ||12||

13duḥśalāṃ mānayadbhistu tadā mukto jayadrathaḥ
|
kathamadya na tāṃ kṛṣṇa mānayanti sma te punaḥ||13||

14saiṣā mama sutā bālā vilapantī ca duḥkhitā
|
ātmanā hanti cātmānamākrośantī ca pāṇḍavān||14||

15kinnu duḥkhataraṃ kṛṣṇa paraṃ mama bhaviṣyati
|
yatsutā vidhavā bālā snuṣāśca nihateśvarāḥ||15||

16hā hā dhigdhuḥśalāṃ paśya vītaśokabhayāmiva
|
śiro bharturanāsādya dhāvamānāmitastataḥ||16||

17vārayāmāsa yaḥ sarvānpāṇḍavānputragṛddhinaḥ
|
sa hatvā vipulāḥ senāḥ svayaṃ mṛtyuvaśaṃ gataḥ||17||

18taṃ mattamiva mātaṅgaṃ vīraṃ paramadurjayam
|
parivārya rudantyetāḥ striyaścandropamānanāḥ||18||
