Mahābhārata 6.101 (Bhīṣmaparvan)

1dhṛtarāṣṭra uvāca|
ārjuniṃ samare śūraṃ vinighnantaṃ mahārathān
|
alambuṣaḥ kathaṃ yuddhe pratyayudhyata sañjaya ||1||

2ārṣyaśṛṅgiṃ kathaṃ caiva saubhadraḥ paravīrahā
|
tanmamācakṣva tattvena yathāvṛttaṃ sma saṃyuge ||2||

3dhanañjayaśca kiṃ cakre mama sainyeṣu saṃyuge
|
bhīmo vā rathināṃ śreṣṭho rākṣaso vā ghaṭotkacaḥ ||3||

4nakulaḥ sahadevo vā sātyakirvā mahārathaḥ
|
etadācakṣva me satyaṃ kuśalo hyasi sañjaya ||4||

5sañjaya uvāca|hanta te'haṃ pravakṣyāmi saṅgrāmaṃ lomaharṣaṇam
|
yathā'bhūdrākṣasendrasya saubhadrasya ca māriṣa ||5||

6arjunaśca yathā saṃkhye bhīmasenaśca pāṇḍavaḥ
|
nakulaḥ sahadevaśca raṇe cakruḥ parākramam ||6||

7tathaiva tāvakāḥ sarve bhīṣmadroṇapuraḥsarāḥ
|
adbhutāni vicitrāṇi cakruḥ karmāṇyabhītavat ||7||

8alambuṣastu samare abhimanyuṃ mahāratham
|
vinadya sumahānādaṃ tarjayitvā muhurmuhuḥ ||8||

9abhidudrāva vegena tiṣṭha tiṣṭheti cābravīt
|
abhimanyuśca vegena siṃhavadvinadanmuhuḥ ||9||

10ārṣyaśṛṅgiṃ maheṣvāsaṃ pituratyantavairiṇam
|
tataḥ samīyatuḥ saṃkhye tvaritau nararākṣasau ||10||

11rathābhyāṃ rathinau śreṣṭhau yathā vai devadānavau
|
māyāvī rākṣasaśreṣṭho divyāstraścaiva phālguniḥ ||11||

12tataḥ kārṣṇirmahārāja niśitaiḥ sāyakaistribhiḥ
|
ārṣyaśṛṅgiṃ raṇe vidhvā punarvivyādha pañcabhiḥ ||12||

13alambuṣo'pi saṃkruddhaḥ kārṣṇiṃ navabhirāśugaiḥ
|
hṛdi vivyādha vegena totrairiva mahādvipam ||13||

14tataḥ śarasahastreṇa kṣiprakārī niśācaraḥ
|
arjunasya sutaṃ saṃkhye pīḍayāmāsa bhārata ||14||

15abhimanyustataḥ kruddho navabhirnataparvabhiḥ
|
bibheda niśitaibāṇai rākṣasendraṃ mahorasi ||15||

16te tasya viviśustūrṇaṃ kāyaṃ nirbhidya marmasu
|
sa tairvibhinnasarvāṅgaḥ śuśubhe rākṣasottamaḥ ||16||

17puṣpitaiḥ kiṃśukai rājan saṃstīrṇa iva parvataḥ
|
sandhārayāṇaśca śarān hemapuṅkhān mahābalaḥ ||17||

18vibabhau rākṣasaśreṣṭhaḥ sajvāla iva parvataḥ
|
tataḥ kruddho mahārāja ārṣyaśṛṅgiramarṣaṇaḥ ||18||

19mahendrapratimaṃ kārṣṇiṃ chādayāmāsa patribhiḥ
|
tena te viśikhā muktā yamadaṇḍopamāḥ śitāḥ ||19||

20abhimanyuṃ vinirbhidya prāviśanta dharātalam
|
tathaivārjuninā muktāḥ śarāḥ kanakabhūṣaṇāḥ ||20||

21alambuṣaṃ vinirbhidya prāviśanta dharātalam
|
saubhadrastu raṇe rakṣaḥ śaraiḥ sannataparvabhiḥ ||21||

22cakre vimukhamāsādya mayaṃ śakra ivāhave
|
vimukhaṃ ca raṇe rakṣo vadhyamānaṃ raṇe'riṇā ||22||

23prāduścakre mahāmāyāṃ tāmasīṃ paratāpanām
|
tataste tamasā sarve vṛtāścāsan mahīpate ||23||

24nābhimanyumapaśyanta naiva svānna parān raṇe
|
abhimanyuśca tadṛṣṭvā ghorarūpaṃ mahattamaḥ ||24||

25prāduścakre'stramatyugraṃ bhāskaraṃ kurunandanaḥ
|
tataḥ prakāśamabhavajjagat sarvaṃ mahīpate ||25||

26tāṃ cābhijaghnivānmāyāṃ rākṣasasya durātmanaḥ
|
saṅkṛddhaśca mahāvīryo rākṣasendraṃ narottamaḥ ||26||

27chāda‌yāmāsa samare śaraiḥ sannataparvabhiḥ
|
bahvīstathānyā māyāśca prayuktāstena rakṣasā ||27||

28sarvāstravida‌meyātmā vārayāmāsa phālguniḥ
|
hatamāyaṃ tato rakṣo vadhyamānaṃ ca sāyakaiḥ ||28||

29rathaṃ tatraiva santyajya prādravan mahato bhayāt
|
tasmin vinirjite tūrṇaṃ kūṭayodhini rākṣase ||29||

30ārjuniḥ samare sainyaṃ tāvakaṃ saṃmamarda ha
|
madāndho gandhanāgendraḥ sapadmāṃ padminīmiva ||30||

31tataḥ śāntanavo bhīṣmaḥ sainyaṃ dṛṣṭvā'bhividrutam
|
mahatā śaravarṣeṇa saubhadraṃ paryavārayat ||31||

32koṣṭhīkṛtya ca taṃ vīraṃ dhārtarāṣṭrā mahārathāḥ
|
ekaṃ subahavo yuddhe tatakṣuḥ sāyakairdṛḍham ||32||

33sa teṣāṃ rathināṃ vīraḥ pitustulyaparākramaḥ
|
sadṛśo vāsudevasya vikrameṇa balena ca ||33||

34ubhayoḥ sadṛśaṃ karma sa piturmātulasya ca
|
raṇe bahuvidhaṃ cakre sarvaśastrabhṛtāṃ varaḥ ||34||

35tato dhanañjayo vīro vinighnaṃstava sainikān
|
āsasāda raṇe bhīṣmaṃ putraprepsuramarṣaṇaḥ ||35||

36tathaiva samare rājanpitā devavratastava
|
āsasāda raṇe pārthaṃ svarbhānuriva bhāskaram ||36||

37tataḥ sarathanāgāśvāḥ putrāstava janeśvara
|
parivavrū raṇe bhīṣmaṃ jugupuśca samantataḥ ||37||

38tathaiva pāṇḍavā rājan parivārya dhanañjayam
|
raṇāya mahate yuktā daṃśitā bharatarṣabha ||38||

39śāradvatastato rājan bhīṣmasya pramukhe sthitam
|
arjunaṃ pañcaviṃśatyā sāyakānāṃ samācinot ||39||

40pratyudgamyātha vivyādha sātyakistaṃ śitaiḥ śaraiḥ
|
pāṇḍavapriyakāmārthaṃ śārdūla iva kuñjaram ||40||

41gautamo'pi tvarāyukto mādhavaṃ navabhiḥ śaraiḥ
|
hṛdi vivyādha saṅkruddhaḥ kaṅkapatraparicchadaiḥ ||41||

42śaineyo'pi tataḥ kuddhaścāpamānamya vegavān
|
gautamāntakaraṃ tūrṇaṃ samādhatta śilīmukham ||42||

43tamāpatantaṃ vegena śakāśanisamadyutim
|
dvidhā ciccheda saṃkruddho drauṇiḥ paramakopanaḥ ||43||

44samutsṛjyātha śaineyo gautamaṃ rathināṃ varaḥ
|
abhyadravadraṇe drauṇiṃ rāhuḥ khe śaśinaṃ yathā ||44||

45tasya droṇasutaścāpaṃ dvidhā ciccheda bhārata
|
athainaṃ chinnadhanvānaṃ tāḍayāmāsa sāyakaiḥ ||45||

46so'nyat kārmukamādāya śatrughnaṃ bhārasādhanam
|
drauṇiṃ ṣaṣṭyā mahārāja bāhvorurasi cārpayat ||46||

47sa viddho vyathitaścaiva muhūrtaṃ kaśmalāyutaḥ
|
niṣasāda rathopasthe dhvajayaṣṭiṃ samāśritaḥ ||47||

48pratilabhya tataḥ saṃjñāṃ droṇaputraḥ pratāpavān
|
vārṣṇeyaṃ samare kruddho nārācena samārpayat ||48||

49śaineyaṃ sa tu nirbhidya prāviśaddharaṇītalam
|
vasantakāle balavānbilaṃ sarpaśiśuryathā ||49||

50athāpareṇa bhallena mādhavasya dhvajottamam
|
ciccheda samare drauṇiḥ siṃhanādaṃ mumoca ha ||50||

51punaścainaṃ śarairghoraiśchādayāmāsa bhārata
|
nidāghānte mahārāja yathā megho divākaram ||51||

52sātyako'pi mahārāja śarajālaṃ nihatya tat
|
drauṇimabhyakirattūrṇaṃ śarajālairanekadhā ||52||

53tāpayāmāsa ca drauṇiṃ śaineyaḥ paravīrahā
|
vimukto meghajālena yathaiva tapanastathā ||53||

54śarāṇāṃ ca sahasreṇa punareva samudyataḥ
|
sātyakiśchādayāmāsa nanāda ca mahābalaḥ ||54||

55dṛṣṭvā putraṃ ca taṃ grastaṃ rāhuṇeva niśākaram
|
abhyadravata śaineyaṃ bhāradvājaḥ pratāpavān ||55||

56vivyādha ca sutīkṣṇena pṛṣatkena mahāmṛdhe
|
parīpsansvasutaṃ rājan vārṣṇeyenābhipīḍitam ||56||

57sātyakistu raṇe hitvā guruputraṃ mahāratham
|
droṇaṃ vivyādha viṃśatyā sarvapāraśavaiḥ śaraiḥ ||57||

58tadantaramameyātmā kaunteyaḥ śatrutāpanaḥ
|
abhyadravadraṇe kruddho droṇaṃ prati mahārathaḥ ||58||

59tato droṇaśca pārthaśca sameyetāṃ mahāmṛdhe
|
yathā budhaśca śukraśca mahārāja nabhastale ||59||
