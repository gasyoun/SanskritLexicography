Mahābhārata 7.190 (Droṇaparvan)

1sañjaya uvāca|
pañcālānāṃ tato droṇo'pyakarotkadanaṃ mahat
|
yathā kruddho raṇe śakro dānavānāṃ kṣayaṃ purā||1||

2droṇāstreṇa mahārāja vadhyamānāḥ pare yudhi
|
nātrasanta raṇe droṇātsattvavanto mahārathāḥ||2||

3yudhyamānā mahārāja pañcālāḥ sṛñjayāstathā
|
droṇamevābhyayuryuddhe yodhayanto mahārathāḥ||3||

4teṣāṃ tu cchādyamānānāṃ pañcālānāṃ samantataḥ
|
abhavadbhairavo nādo vadhyatāṃ śaravṛṣṭibhiḥ||4||

5vadhyamāneṣu saṃgrāme pañcāleṣu mahātmanā
|
udīryamāṇe droṇāstre pāṇḍavānbhayamāviśat||5||

6dṛṣṭvā'śvana rayodhānāṃ vipulaṃ ca kṣayaṃ yudhi
|
pāṇḍaveyā mahārāja nāśaśaṃsurjayaṃ tadā||6||

7kacciddroṇo na naḥ sarvān kṣapayetparamāstravit
|
samiddhaḥ śiśirāpāye dahankakṣamivānalaḥ||7||

8na cainaṃ saṃyuge kaścitsamarthaḥ prativīkṣitum
|
na cainamarjuno jātu pratiyudhyeta dharmavit||8||

9trastānkuntīsutāndṛṣṭvā droṇasāyakapīḍitān
|
matimāñśreyase yuktaḥ keśavo'rjunamabravīt||9||

10naiṣa yuddhe na saṃgrāme jetuṃ śakyaḥ kathañcana
|
sadhanurdhanvināṃ śreṣṭho devairapi savāsavaiḥ||10||

11nyastaśastrastu saṃgrāme śakyo hantuṃ bhavennṛbhiḥ
|
āsthīyatāṃ jaye yogo dharmamutsṛjya pāṇḍavāḥ||11||

12yathā na saṃyuge sarvānna hanyādrukmavāhanaḥ
|
aśvatthāmni hate naiṣa yudhyediti matirmama||12||

13taṃ hataṃ saṃyuge kaścidasmai śaṃsatu mānavaḥ
|
etannārocayadrājan kuntīputro dhanañjayaḥ||13||

14anye tvarocayansarve kṛcchreṇa tu yudhiṣṭhiraḥ
|
tato bhīmo mahābāhuranīke sve mahāgajam||14||

15jaghāna gadayā rājannaśvatthāmānamityuta
|
parapramathanaṃ ghoraṃ mālavasyendravarmaṇaḥ||15||

16bhīmasenastu savrīḍamupetya droṇamāhave
|
aśvatthāmā hata iti śabdamuccaiścakāra ha||16||

17aśvatthāmeti hi gajaḥ khyāto nāmnā hato'bhavat
|
kṛtvā manasi taṃ bhīmo mithyā vyāhṛtavāṃstadā||17||

18bhīmasenavacaḥ śrutvā droṇastatparamāpriyam
|
manasā sannagātro'bhūdyathā saikatamambhasi||18||

19śaṅkamānaḥ sa tanmithyā vīryajñaḥ svasutasya vai
|
hataḥ sa iti ca śrutvā naiva dhairyādakampata||19||

20sa labdhvā cetanāṃ droṇaḥ kṣaṇenaiva samāśvasat
|
anucintyātmanaḥ putramaviṣahyamarātibhiḥ||20||

21sa pārṣatamabhidrutya jighāṃsurmṛtyumātmanaḥ
|
avākiratsahasreṇa tīkṣṇānāṃ kaṅkapatriṇām||21||

22taṃ viṃśatisahasrāṇi pañcālānāṃ nararṣabhāḥ
|
tathā carantaṃ saṃgrāme sarvato'vākirañcharaiḥ||22||

23śaraistairācitaṃ droṇaṃ nāpaśyāma mahāratham
|
bhāskaraṃ jaladairuddhaṃ varṣāsviva viśāṃpate||23||

24vidhūya tānbāṇagaṇānpañcālānāṃ mahārathaḥ
|
prāduścake tato droṇo brāhmamastraṃ paraṃtapaḥ||24||

25vadhāya teṣāṃ śūrāṇāṃ pañcālānāmamarṣitaḥ
|
tato vyarocata droṇo vinighnansarvasainikān||25||

26śirāṃsyapātayaccāpi pañcālānāṃ mahāmṛdhe
|
tathaiva parighākārānbāhūnkanakabhūṣaṇān||26||

27te vadhyamānāḥ samare bhāradvājena pārthivāḥ
|
medinyāmanvakīryanta vātanunnā iva drumāḥ||27||

28kuñjarāṇāṃ ca patatāṃ hayaughānāṃ ca bhārata
|
agamyarūpā pṛthivī māṃsaśoṇitakardamā||28||

29hatvā viṃśatisāhasrānpañcālānāṃ rathavrajān
|
atiṣṭhadāhave droṇo vidhūmo'gniriva jvalan||29||

30tathaiva ca punaḥ kruddho bhāradvājaḥ pratāpavān
|
vasudānasya bhallena śiraḥ kāyādapāharat||30||

31punaḥ pañcaśatānmatsyān ṣaṭsahastrāṃśca sṛñjayān
|
hastināmayutaṃ hatvā jaghānāśvāyutaṃ punaḥ||31||

32kṣatriyāṇāmabhāvāya dṛṣṭvā droṇamavasthitam
|
ṛṣayo'bhyāgatāstūrṇaṃ havyavāhapurogamāḥ||32||

33viśvāmitro jamada‌gnirbharadvājo'tha gautamaḥ
|
vasiṣṭhaḥ kaśyapo'triśca brahmalokaṃ ninīṣavaḥ||33||

34sikatāḥ pṛśnayo gargā vālakhilyā marīcipāḥ
|
bhṛgavo'ṅgirasaścaiva sūkṣmāścānye maharṣayaḥ||34||

35ta enamabruvansarve droṇamāhavaśobhinam
|
adharmataḥ kṛtaṃ yuddhaṃ samayo nidhanasya te||35||

36nyasyāyudhaṃ raṇe droṇa samīkṣāsmānavasthitān
|
nātaḥ krūrataraṃ karma punaḥ kartumihārhasi||36||

37vedavedāṅgaviduṣaḥ satyadharmaratasya te
|
brāhmaṇasya viśeṣeṇa tavaitannopapadyate||37||

38tyajāyudhamamogheṣo tiṣṭha vartmani śāśvate
|
paripūrṇaśca kālaste vastuṃ loke'dya mānuṣe||38||

39brahmāstreṇa tvayā dagdhā anastrajñā narā bhuvi
|
yadetadīdṛśaṃ vipra kṛtaṃ karma na sādhu tat||39||

40nyasyāyudhaṃ raṇe vipra droṇa mā tvaṃ ciraṃ kṛthāḥ
|
mā pāpiṣṭhataraṃ karma kariṣyasi punardvija||40||

41iti teṣāṃ vacaḥ śrutvā bhīmasenavacaśca tat
|
dhṛṣṭadyumnaṃ ca saṃprekṣya raṇe sa vimanā'bhavat||41||

42saṃdihyamāno vyathitaḥ kuntīputraṃ yudhiṣṭhiram
|
ahataṃ vā hataṃ veti papraccha sutamātmanaḥ||42||

43sthirā buddhirhi droṇasya na pārtho vakṣyate'nṛtam
|
trayāṇāmapi lokānāmaiśvaryārthe kathañcana||43||

44tasmāttaṃ paripapraccha nānyaṃ kañciddvijarṣabhaḥ
|
tasmiṃstasya hi satyāśā bālyātprabhṛti pāṇḍave||44||

45tato niṣpāṇḍavāmurvīṃ kariṣyantaṃ yudhāṃ patim
|
droṇaṃ jñātvā dharmarājaṃ govindo vyathito'bravīt||45||

46yadyardhadivasaṃ droṇo yudhyate manyumāsthitaḥ
|
satyaṃ bravīmi te senā vināśaṃ samupaiṣyati||46||

47sa bhavāṃstrātu no droṇātsatyājjyāyo'nṛtaṃ vacaḥ
|
anṛtaṃ jīvitasyārthe vadanna spṛśyate'nṛtaiḥ||47||

48tayoḥ saṃvadatorevaṃ bhīmaseno'bravīdidam
|
śrutvaivaṃ tu mahārāja vadhopāyaṃ mahātmanaḥ||48||

49gāhamānasya te senāṃ mālavasyendravarmaṇaḥ||49||

50aśvatthāmeti vikhyāto gajaḥ śakragajopamaḥ
|
nihato yudhi vikramya tato'haṃ droṇamabruvam||50||

51aśvatthāmā hato brahmannivartasvāhavāditi
|
nūnaṃ nāśraddadhadvākyameṣa me puruṣarṣabhaḥ||51||

52sa tvaṃ goviṃdavākyāni mānayasva jayaiṣiṇaḥ
|
droṇāya nihataṃ śaṃsa rājañśāradvatīsutam||52||

53tvayokto naiva yudhyeta jātu rājandvijarṣabhaḥ
|
satyavān hi triloke'smin bhavān khyāto janādhipa||53||

54tasya tadvacanaṃ śrutvā kṛṣṇavākyapracoditaḥ
|
bhāvitvācca mahārāja vaktuṃ samupacakrame||54||

55tamatathyabhaye magno jaye sakto yudhiṣṭhiraḥ
|
avyaktamabravīdrājanhataḥ kuñjara ityuta||55||

56tasya pūrvaṃ rathaḥ pṛthvyāścaturaṅgulamucchritaḥ
|
babhūvaivaṃ ca tenokte tasya vāhāḥ spṛśanmahīm||56||

57yudhiṣṭhirāttu tadvākyaṃ śrutvā droṇo mahārathaḥ
|
putravyasanasantapto nirāśo jīvite'bhavat||57||

58āgaskṛtamivātmānaṃ pāṇḍavānāṃ mahātmanām
|
ṛṣivākyena manvānaḥ śrutvā ca nihataṃ sutam||58||

59vicetāḥ paramodvigno dhṛṣṭadyumnamavekṣya ca
|
yoddhuṃ nāśaknuvadrājanyathāpūrvamarindamaḥ||59||
