Mahābhārata 7.118 (Droṇaparvan)

1sañjaya uvāca|
droṇaṃ sa jitvā puruṣapravīrastathaiva hārdikyamukhāṃstvadīyān
|
prahasya sūtaṃ vacanaṃ babhāṣe śinipravīraḥ kurupuṅgavāgrya ||1||

2nimittamātraṃ vayamadya sūta dagdhārayaḥ keśavaphālgunābhyām
|
hatānnihanmeha nararṣabheṇa vayaṃ sureśātmasamudbhavena ||2||

3tamevamuktyā śinipuṅgavastadā mahāmṛdhe so'gryadhanurdharo'rihā
|
kiran samantāt sahasā śarān balī samāpatacchyena ivāmiṣaṃ yathā ||3||

4taṃ yāntamaśvaiḥ śaśiśaṃkhavarṇairvigāhya sainyaṃ puruṣapravīram
|
nāśaknuvan vārayituṃ samantādādityaraśmipratimaṃ rathāgryam ||4||

5asahyavikrāntamadīnasattvaṃ sarve gaṇā bhārata ye tvadīyāḥ
|
sahasranetrapratimaprabhāvaṃ divīva sūryaṃ jaladavyapāya ||5||

6amarṣapūrṇastvaticitrayodhī śarāsanī kāñcanavarmadhārī
|
sudarśanaḥ sātyakimāpatantaṃ nyavārayadrājavaraḥ prasahya ||6||

7tayorabhūdbhārata saṃprahāraḥ sudāruṇastaṃ samatipraśaṃsan
|
yodhāstvadīyāśca hi somakāśca vṛtrendrayoryuddhamivāmaraughāḥ ||7||

8śaraiḥ sutīkṣṇaiḥ śataśo'bhyavidhyat sudarśanaḥ sātvatamukhyamājau
|
anāgatāneva tu tān pṛṣatkāṃściccheda rājañśinipuṅgavo'pi ||8||

9tathaiva śakrapratimo'pi sātyakiḥ sudarśane yān kṣipati sma sāyakān
|
dvidhā tridhā tānakarot sudarśanaḥ śarottamaiḥ syandanavaryamāsthitaḥ ||9||

10tān vīkṣya bāṇānnihatāṃstadānīṃ sudarśanaḥ sātyakibāṇavegaiḥ
|
krodhāddidhakṣanniva tigmatejāḥ śarānamuñcattapanīyacitrān ||10||

11punaḥ sa bāṇaistribhiragnikalpairākarṇapūrṇaniśitaiḥ supuṅkhaiḥ
|
vivyādha dehāvaraṇaṃ vibhidya te sātyakerāviviśuḥ śarīram ||11||

12tathaiva tasyāvanipālaputraḥ sandhāya bāṇairaparairdhvaladbhiḥ
|
ājaghnivāṃstān rajataprakāśāṃścaturbhiraśvāṃścaturaḥ prasahya ||12||

13tathā tu tenābhihatastarasvī naptā śinerindrasamānavīryaḥ
|
sudarśanasyeṣugaṇaiḥ sutīkṣṇairhayānihatyāśu nanāda nādam ||13||

14athāsya sūtasya śiro nikṛtya bhallena śakrāśanisannibhena
|
sudarśanasyāpi śinipravīraḥ kṣureṇa kālānalasannibhena ||14||

15sakuṇḍalaṃ pūrṇaśaśiprakāśaṃ bhrājiṣṇu vaktraṃ vicakarta dehāt
|
yathā purā vajradharaḥ prasahya balasya saṃkhye'tibalasya rājan ||15||

16nihatya taṃ pārthivaputrapautraṃ raṇe yadūnāmṛṣabhastarasvī
|
mudā sametaḥ parayā mahātmā rarāja rājan surarājakalpaḥ ||16||

17tato yayāvarjuna eva yena nivārya sainyaṃ tava mārgaṇaughaiḥ
|
sadaśvayuktena rathena rāja~llokaṃ visismāpayiṣurnṛvīraḥ ||17||

18tattasya vismāpayanīyamagryamapūjayan yodhavarāḥ sametāḥ
|
pravartamānāniṣugocare'rīn dadāha bāṇairhutabhugyathaiva ||18||
