Mahābhārata 5.194 (Udyogaparvan)

1vaiśampāyana uvāca|
etacchrutvā tu kaunteyaḥ sarvān bhrātṝnupahvare
|
āhūya bharataśreṣṭha idaṃ vacanamabravīt ||1||

2yudhiṣṭhira uvāca|
dhārtarāṣṭrasya sainyeṣu ye cārapuruṣā mama
|
te pravṛttiṃ prayacchanti mamemāṃ vyuṣitāṃ niśām ||2||

3duryodhanaḥ kilāpṛcchadāpageyaṃ mahāvratam
|
kena kālena pāṇḍūnāṃ hanyāḥ sainyamiti prabho ||3||

4māseneti ca tenokto dhārtarāṣṭraḥ sudurmatiḥ
|
tāvatā cāpi kālena droṇo'pi pratijajñivān ||4||

5gautamo dviguṇaṃ kālamuktavāniti naḥ śrutam
|
drauṇistu daśarātreṇa pratijajñe mahāstravit ||5||

6tathā divyāstravit karṇaḥ saṃpṛṣṭaḥ kurusaṃsadi
|
pañcabhirdivasairhantuṃ sasainyaṃ pratijajñivān ||6||

7tasmādahamapīcchāmi śrotumarjuna te vacaḥ
|
kālena kiyatā śatrūn kṣapayeriti phālguna ||7||

8evamukto guḍākeśaḥ pārthivena dhanañjayaḥ
|
vāsudevaṃ samīkṣyedaṃ vacanaṃ pratyabhāṣata ||8||

9sarva ete mahātmānaḥ kṛtāstrāścitrayodhinaḥ
|
asaṃśayaṃ mahārāja hanyureva na saṃśayaḥ ||9||

10apaitu te manastāpo yathā satyaṃ bravīmyaham
|
hanyāmekarathenaiva vāsudevasahāyavān ||10||

11sāmarānapi lokāṃstrīn sarvān sthāvarajaṅgamān
|
bhūtaṃ bhavyaṃ bhaviṣyaṃ ca nimeṣāditi me matiḥ ||11||

12yattaddhoraṃ paśupatiḥ prādādastraṃ mahanmama
|
kairāte dvandvayuddhe tu tadidaṃ mayi vartate ||12||

13yadyugānte paśupatiḥ sarvabhūtāni saṃharan
|
prayukte puruṣavyāghra tadidaṃ mayi vartate ||13||

14tanna jānāti gāṅgeyo na droṇo na ca gautamaḥ
|
na ca droṇasuto rājan kuta eva tu sūtajaḥ ||14||

15na tu yuktaṃ raṇe hantuṃ divyairastraiḥ pṛthagjanam
|
ārjavenaiva yuddhena vijeṣyāmo vayaṃ parān ||15||

16tatheme puruṣavyāghrāḥ sahāyāstatra pārthiva
|
sarve divyāstravidvāṃsaḥ sarve yuddhābhikāṅkṣiṇaḥ ||16||

17vedāntāvabhṛthasnātāḥ sarva ete'parājitāḥ
|
nihanyuḥ samare senāṃ devānāmapi pāṇḍava ||17||

18śikhaṇḍī yuyudhānaśca dhṛṣṭadyumnaśca pārṣataḥ
|
bhīmaseno yamau cobhau yudhāmanyūttamaujasau ||18||

19virāṭadrupadau cobhau bhīṣmadroṇasamau yudhi
|
śaṅkhaścaiva mahābāhurhaiḍimbaśca mahābalaḥ ||19||

20putro'syāñjanaparvā tu mahābalaparākramaḥ
|
śaineyaśca mahāvāhuḥ sahāyo raṇakovidaḥ ||20||

21abhimanyuśca balavān draupadyāḥ pañca cātmajāḥ
|
svayaṃ cāpi samartho'si trailokyotsādane'pi ca ||21||

22krodhādyaṃ puruṣaṃ paśyestathā śakrasamadyute
|
sa kṣipraṃ na bhavedvyaktamiti tvāṃ vedmi kaurava ||22||
