Mahābhārata 9.22 (Śalyaparvan)

1sañjaya uvāca|
putrastu te mahārāja rathastho rathināṃ varaḥ
|
durutsaho babhau yuddhe yathā rudraḥ pratāpavān||1||

2tasya bāṇasahasraistu pracchannā hyabhavanmahī
|
parāṃśca siṣice bāṇairdhārābhiriva parvatān||2||

3na ca so'sti pumānkaścitpāṇḍavānāṃ balārṇave
|
hayo gajo ratho vāpi yaḥ syādbāṇairavikṣataḥ||3||

4yaṃ yaṃ hi samare yodhaṃ prapaśyāmi viśāmpate
|
sa sa bāṇaiścito'bhūdvai putreṇa tava bhārata||4||

5yathā sainyena rajasā samudbhūtena vāhinī
|
pratyadṛśyata sañchannā tathā bāṇairmahātmanaḥ||5||

6bāṇabhūtāmapaśyāma pṛthivīṃ pṛthivīpate
|
duryodhanena prakṛtāṃ kṣiprahastena dhanvinā||6||

7teṣu yodhasahasreṣu tāvakeṣu pareṣu ca
|
eko duryodhano hyāsītpumāniti matirmama||7||

8tatrādbhutamapaśyāma tava putrasya vikramam
|
yadekaṃ sahitāḥ pārthā nābhyavartanta bhārata||8||

9yudhiṣṭhiraṃ śatenājau vivyādha bharatarṣabha
|
bhīmasenaṃ ca saptatyā sahadevaṃ ca pañcabhiḥ||9||

10nakulaṃ ta catuḥṣaṣṭyā dhṛṣṭadyumnaṃ ca pañcabhiḥ
|
saptabhirdraupadeyāṃśca tribhirvivyādha sātyakim||10||

11dhanuściccheda bhallena sahadevasya māriṣa
|
tadapāsya dhanuśchinnaṃ mādrīputraḥ pratāpavān||11||

12abhyadravata rājānaṃ pragṛhyānyanmahaddhanuḥ
|
tato duryodhanaṃ saṅkhye vivyādha daśabhiḥ śaraiḥ||12||

13nakulastu tato vīro rājānaṃ navabhiḥ śaraiḥ
|
ghorarūpairmaheṣvāso vivyādha ca nanāda ca||13||

14sātyakiścaiva rājānaṃ śareṇānataparvaṇā
|
draupadeyāstrisaptatyā dharmarājaśca pañcabhiḥ||14||

15aśītyā bhīmasenaśca śarai rājānamārpayan
|
samantātkīryamāṇastu bāṇasaṅdhairmahātmabhiḥ||15||

16na cacāla mahārāja sarvasainyasya paśyataḥ
|
lāghavaṃ sauṣṭhavaṃ cāpi vīryaṃ cāpi mahātmanaḥ||16||

17atisarvāṇi bhūtāni dadṛśuḥ sarvamānavāḥ
|
dhārtarāṣṭrā hi rājendra yodhāstu svalpamantaram||17||

18apaśyamānā rājānaṃ paryavartanta daṃśitāḥ
|
teṣāmāpatatāṃ ghorastumulaḥ samapadyata||18||

19kṣubdhasya hi samudrasya prāvṛṭkāle yathā svanaḥ
|
samāsādya raṇe te tu rājānamaparājitam||19||

20pratyudyayurmaheṣvāsāḥ pāṇḍavānātatāyinaḥ
|
bhīmasenaṃ raṇe kruddho droṇaputro nyavārayat||20||

21nānābāṇairmahārāja pramuktaiḥ sarvatodiśam
|
nājñāyanta raṇe vīrā na diśaḥ pradiśaḥ kutaḥ||21||

22tāvubhau krūrakarmāṇāvubhau bhārata duḥsahau
|
ghorarūpamayudhyetāṃ kṛtapratikṛtaiṣiṇau||22||

23trāsayantau diśaḥ sarvā jyākṣepakaṭhinatvacau
|
śakunistu raṇe vīro yudhiṣṭhiramapīḍayat||23||

24tasyāśvāṃścaturo hatvā subalasya suto vibho
|
nādaṃ cakāra balavatsarvasainyāni kopayan||24||

25etasminnantare vīraṃ rājānamaparājitam
|
apovāha rathenājau sahadevaḥ pratāpavān||25||

26athānyaṃ rathamāsthāya dharmaputro yudhiṣṭhiraḥ
|
śakuniṃ narabhirviddhvā punarvivyādha pañcabhiḥ||26||

27nanāda ca mahānādaṃ pravaraḥ sarvadhanvinām
|
tadyuddhamabhavaccitraṃ ghorarūpaṃ ca māriṣa||27||

28prekṣatāṃ prītijananaṃ siddhacāraṇasevitam
|
ulūkastu maheṣvāsaṃ nakulaṃ yuddhadurmadam||28||

29abhyadravadameyātmā śaravarṣaiḥ samantataḥ
|
tathaiva nakulaḥ śūraḥ saubalasya sutaṃ raṇe||29||

30śaravarṣeṇa mahatā samantātparyavārayat
|
tau tatra samare vīro kulaputrau mahārathau||30||

31yodhayantāvapaśyetāṃ kṛtapratikṛtaiṣiṇau
|
tathaiva kṛtavarmāṇaṃ śaineyaḥ śatrutāpanaḥ||31||

32yodhayañśuśubhe rājanbaliṃ śakra ivāhave
|
duryodhano dhanuśchittvā dhṛṣṭadyumnasya saṃyuge||32||

33athainaṃ chinnadhanvānaṃ vivyādha niśitaiḥ śaraiḥ
|
dhṛṣṭadyumno'pi samare pragṛhya paramāyudham||33||

34rājānaṃ yodhayāmāsa paśyatāṃ sarvadhanvinām
|
tayoryuddhaṃ mahaccāsītsaṅgrāme bharatarṣabha||34||

35prabhinnayoryathāsaktaṃ mattayorvarahastinoḥ
|
gautamastu raṇe kruddho draupadeyānmahābalān||35||

36vivyādha bahubhiḥ śūraḥ śaraiḥ sannataparvabhiḥ
|
tasya tairabhavadyuddhamindriyairiva dehinaḥ||36||

37ghorarūpamasaṃvāryaṃ nirmaryādamavartata
|
te ca sampīḍayāmāsurindriyāṇīva bāliśam||37||

38sa ca tānprati saṃrabdhaḥ pratyayodhayadāhave
|
evaṃ citramabhūdyuddhaṃ tasya taiḥ saha bhārata||38||

39utthāyotthāya hi yathā dehināmindrayairvibho
|
narāścaiva naraiḥ sārdhaṃ dantino dantibhistathā||39||

40hayā hayaiḥ samāsaktā rathino rāthabhiḥ saha
|
saṅkulaṃ cābhavadbhūyo ghorarūpaṃ viśāmpate||40||

41idaṃ citramidaṃ ghoramidaṃ raudramiti prabho
|
yuddhānyāsanmahārāja ghorāṇi ca bahūni ca||41||

42te samāsādya samare parasparamarindamāḥ
|
vyanadaṃścaiva jaghnuśca samāsādya mahāhave||42||

43teṣāṃ patrasamudbhūtaṃ rajastīvramadṛśyata
|
vātena coddhataṃ rājandhāvadbhiścāśvasādibhiḥ||43||

44rathanemisamudbhūtaṃ niḥśvāsaiścāpi dantinām
|
rajaḥsandhyābhrakalilaṃ divākarapathaṃ yayau||44||

45rajasā tena sampṛkto bhāskaro niṣprabhaḥ kṛtaḥ
|
sañchāditā'bhavadbhūmiste ca śūrā mahārathāḥ||45||

46muhūrtādiva saṃvṛttaṃ nīrajaskaṃ samantataḥ
|
vīraśoṇitasiktāyāṃ bhūmau bharatasattama||46||

47upāśāmyattatastīvraṃ tadrajo ghoradarśanam
|
tato'paśyamahaṃ bhūyo dvandvayuddhāni bhārata||47||

48yathāprāṇaṃ yathāśreṣṭhaṃ madhyāhne vai sudāruṇe
|
varmaṇāṃ tatra rājendra vyadṛśyantojjvalā prabhāḥ||48||

49śabdaśca tumulaḥ saṅkhye śarāṇāṃ patatāmabhūt
|
mahāveṇuvanasyeva dahyamānasya parvate||49||
