Mahābhārata 5.43 (Udyogaparvan)

1dhṛtarāṣṭra uvāca|
kasyaiṣa maunaḥ katarannu maunaṃ prabrūhi vidvanniha maunabhāvam
|
maunena vidvānuta yāti maunaṃ kathaṃ mune maunamihācaranti ||1||

2sanatsujāta uvāca|
yato na vedā manasā sahainamanupraviśanti tato'tha maunam
|
yatrotthito vedaśabdastathā'yaṃ sa tanmayatvena vibhāti rājan ||2||

3dhṛtarāṣṭra uvāca|
ṛco yajūṃṣi yo veda sāmavedaṃ ca veda yaḥ
|
pāpāni kurvan pāpena lipyate kiṃ na lipyate ||3||

4sanatsujāta uvāca|
nainaṃ sāmānyṛco vā'pi na yajūṃṣyavicakṣaṇam
|
trāyante karmaṇaḥ pāpānna te mithyā bravīmyaham ||4||

5na cchandāṃsi vṛjināttārayanti māyāvinaṃ māyayā vartamānam
|
nīḍaṃ śakuntā iva jātapakṣāśchandāṃsyenaṃ prajahatyantakāle ||5||

6dhṛtarāṣṭra uvāca|
na cedvedā vinā dharmaṃ trātuṃ śaktā vicakṣaṇa
|
atha kasmāt pralāpo'yaṃ brāhmaṇānāṃ sanātanaḥ ||6||

7sanatsujāta uvāca|
tasyaiva nāmādiviśeṣarūpairidaṃ jagadbhāti mahānubhāva
|
nirdiśya samyakpravadanti vedāstadviśvavairūpyamudāharanti ||7||

8tadarthamuktaṃ tapa etadijyā tābhyāmasau puṇyamupaiti vidvān
|
puṇyena pāpaṃ vinihatya paścāt sañjāyate jñānavidīpitātmā ||8||

9jñānena cātmānamupaiti vidvānathānyathā vargaphalānukāṅkṣī
|
asmin kṛtaṃ tatparigṛhya sarvamamutra bhuṅkte punareti mārgam ||9||

10asmi~lloke tapastaptaṃ phalamanyatra bhujyate
|
brāhmaṇānāmime lokā dhātve tapasi tiṣṭhatām||10||

11dhṛtarāṣṭra uvāca|
kathaṃ samṛddhamasamṛddhaṃ tapo bhavati kevalam
|
sanatsujāta tadbrūhi yathā vidyāma tadvayam ||11||

12sanatsujāta uvāca|
niṣkalmaṣaṃ tapastvetat kevalaṃ paricakṣate
|
etat samṛddhamapyṛddhaṃ tapo bhavati kevalam ||12||

13tapomūlamidaṃ sarvaṃ yanmāṃ pṛcchasi kṣatriya
|
tapasā vedavidvāṃsaḥ paraṃ tvamṛtamāpnuyuḥ ||13||

14dhṛtarāṣṭra uvāca|
kalmaṣaṃ tapaso brūhi śrutaṃ niṣkalmaṣaṃ tapaḥ
|
sanatsujāta yenedaṃ vidyāṃ guhyaṃ sanātanam ||14||

15sanatsujāta uvāca|
krodhādayo dvādaśa yasya doṣāstathā nṛśaṃsāni daśatri rājan
|
dharmādayo dvādaśaite pitṝṇāṃ śāstre guṇā ye viditā dvijānām ||15||

16krodhaḥ kāmo lobhamohau vidhitsā kṛpāsūye mānaśokau spṛhā ca
|
īrṣyā jugupsā ca manuṣyadoṣā varjyāḥ sadā dvādaśaite narāṇām ||16||

17ekaikaḥ paryupāste ha manuṣyān manujarṣabha
|
lipsamāno'ntaraṃ teṣāṃ mṛgāṇāmiva lubdhakaḥ ||17||

18vikatthanaḥ spṛhayālurmanasvī bibhratkoṣaṃ capalo'rakṣaṇaśca
|
etān pāpāḥ ṣaṇnarāḥ pāpadharmān prakurvate notrasantaḥ sudurge ||18||

19saṃbhogasaṃvidviṣamo'timānī dattānutāpī kṛpaṇo balīyān
|
vargapraśaṃsī vanitāsu dveṣṭā ete pare sapta nṛśaṃsavargāḥ ||19||

20dharmaśca satyaṃ ca damastapaśca āmātsaryaṃ hrīstitikṣā'nasūyā
|
yajñaśca dānaṃ ca dhṛtiḥ śrutaṃ ca vratāni vai dvādaśa brāhmaṇasya ||20||

21yastvetebhyaḥ prabhaveddvādaśabhyaḥ sarvāmapīmāṃ pṛthivīṃ sa śiṣyāt
|
tribhirdvābhyāmekato vā'rthito yastasya svamastīti sa veditavyaḥ ||21||

22damastyāgo'pramādaśca eteṣvamṛtamāhitam
|
tāni satyamukhānyāhurbrāhmaṇā ye manīṣiṇaḥ ||22||

23damo hyaṣṭādaśaguṇaḥ pratikūlaṃ kṛtākṛte
|
anṛtaṃ cābhyasūyā ca kāmārthau ca tathā spṛhā||23||

24krodhaḥ śokastathā tṛṣṇā lobhaḥ paiśūnyameva ca
|
matsaraśca vihiṃsā ca paritāpastathā'ratiḥ||24||

25apasmāraścātivādastathā saṃbhāvanā'tmani
|
etairvimukto doṣairyaḥ sa dāntaḥ sadbhirucyate ||25||

26mado'ṣṭādaśadoṣaḥ syāttyāgo bhavati ṣaḍvidhaḥ
|
viparyayāḥ smṛtā ete madadoṣā udāhṛtāḥ ||26||

27śreyāṃstu ṣaḍvidhastyāgastṛtīyo duṣkaro bhavet
|
tena duḥkhaṃ taratyeva bhinnaṃ tasmin jitaṃ kṛte ||27||

28śreyāṃstu ṣaḍvidhastyāgaḥ śriyaṃ prāpya na hṛṣyati
|
iṣṭāpūrte dvitīyaṃ syānnityavairāgyayogataḥ ||28||

29kāmatyāgaśca rājendra sa tṛtīya iti smṛtaḥ
|
apyavācyaṃ vadantyetaṃ sa tṛtīyo guṇaḥ smṛtaḥ||29||

30tyaktairdravyairyadbhavati nopayuktaiśca kāmataḥ
|
na ca dravyaistadbhavati nopayuktaiśca kāmataḥ ||30||

31na ca karmasvasiddheṣu duḥkhaṃ tena ca na glapet
|
sarvaireva guṇairyukto dravyavānapi yo bhavet ||31||

32apriye ca samutpanne vyathāṃ jātu na gacchati
|
iṣṭān putrāṃśca dārāṃśca na yāceta kadācana ||32||

33arhate yācamānāya pradeyaṃ tacchubhaṃ bhavet
|
apramādī bhavedetaiḥ sa cāpyaṣṭaguṇo bhavet ||33||

34satyaṃ dhyānaṃ samādhānaṃ codyaṃ vairāgyameva ca
|
asteyaṃ brahmacaryaṃ ca tathā'saṃgrahameva ca||34||

35evaṃ doṣā madasyoktāstān doṣān parivarjayet
|
tathā tyāgo'pramādaśca sa cāpyaṣṭaguṇo mataḥ||35||

36aṣṭau doṣāḥ pramādasya tān doṣān parivarjayet
|
indriyebhyaśca pañcabhyo manasaścaiva bhārata
|
atītānāgatebhyaśca muktyupetaḥ sukhī bhavet ||36||

37satyātmā bhava rājendra satye lokāḥ pratiṣṭhitāḥ
|
tāṃstu satyamukhānāhuḥ satye hyamṛtamāhitam ||37||

38nivṛttenaiva doṣeṇa tapovratamihācaret
|
etaddhātṛkṛtaṃ vṛttaṃ satyameva satāṃ vratam ||38||

39doṣairetairviyuktastu guṇairetaiḥ samanvitaḥ
|
etatsamṛddhamatyarthaṃ tapo bhavati kevalam ||39||

40yanmāṃ pṛcchasi rājendra saṃkṣepāt prabravīmi te
|
etat pāpaharaṃ puṇyaṃ janmamṛtyujarāpaham ||40||

41dhṛtarāṣṭra uvāca|
ākhyānapañcamairvedairbhūyiṣṭhaṃ kathyate janaḥ
|
tathā cānye caturvedāstrivedāśca tathā pare ||41||

42dvivedāścaikavedāścāpyanṛcaśca tathā pare
|
teṣāṃ tu kataraḥ sa syādyamahaṃ veda vai dvijam ||42||

43sanatsujāta uvāca|
ekasya vedasyājñānādvedāste bahavaḥ kṛtāḥ
|
satyasyaikasya rājendra satye kaścidavasthitaḥ||43||

44evaṃ vedamavijñāya prājño'hamiti manyate
|
dānamadhyayanaṃ yajño lobhādetat pravartate||44||

45satyāt pracyavamānānāṃ saṅkalpaśca tathā bhavet
|
tato yajñaḥ pratāyeta satyasyaivāvadhāraṇāt ||45||

46manasā'nyasya bhavati vācānyasyātha karmaṇā
|
saṅkalpasiddhaḥ puruṣaḥ saṅkalpānadhitiṣṭhati ||46||

47anaibhṛtyena caitasya dīkṣitavratamācaret
|
nāmaitaddhātunirvṛttaṃ satyameva satāṃ param||47||

48jñānaṃ vai nāma pratyakṣaṃ parokṣaṃ jāyate tapaḥ
|
vidyādbahu paṭhantaṃ tu dvijaṃ vai bahupāṭhinam ||48||

49tasmāt kṣatriya māmaṃsthā jalpitenaiva vai dvijam
|
ya eva satyānnāpaiti sa jñeyo brāhmaṇastvayā||49||

50chandāṃsi nāma kṣatriya tānyatharvā purā jagau maharṣisaṅgha eṣaḥ
|
chandovidaste ya uta nādhītavedā na vedavedyasya vidurhi tattvam||50||

51chandāṃsi nāma dvipadāṃ variṣṭha svacchandayogena bhavanti tatra
|
chandovidastena ca tānadhītya gatā na vedasya na vedyamāryāḥ||51||

52na vedānāṃ veditā kaścidasti kaścittvetān budhyate vā'pi rājan
|
yo veda vedānna sa veda vedyaṃ satye sthito yastu sa veda vedyam ||52||

53na vedānāṃ veditā kaścidasti vedyena vedaṃ na vidurna vedyam
|
yo veda vedaṃ sa ca veda vedyaṃ yo veda vedyaṃ na sa veda satyam||53||

54yo veda vedān sa ca veda vedyaṃ na taṃ vidurvedavido na vedāḥ
|
tathā'pi vede na vidanti vedaṃ ye brāhmaṇā vedavido bhavanti||54||

55dhāmāṃśabhāgasya tathā hi vedā yathā ca śākhā hi mahīruhasya
|
saṃvedane caiva yathā''mananti tasmin hi satye paramātmano'rthe||55||

56abhijānāmi brāhmaṇaṃ vyākhyātāraṃ vicakṣaṇam
|
yaśchinnavicikitsaḥ sa vyācaṣṭe sarvasaṃśayān||56||

57nāsya paryeṣaṇaṃ gacchet prācīnaṃ nota dakṣiṇam
|
nārvācīnaṃ kutastiryaṅnādiśaṃ tu kathañcana ||57||

58tasya paryeṣaṇaṃ gacchet pratyarthiṣu kathañcana
|
avicinvannimaṃ vede tapaḥ paśyati taṃ prabhum||58||

59tūṣṇīṃbhūta upāsīta na ceṣṭenmanasā'pi ca
|
upāvartasva tadbrahma antarātmani viśrutam||59||

60maunānna sa munirbhavati nāraṇyavasanānmuniḥ
|
svalakṣaṇaṃ tu yo veda sa muniḥ śreṣṭha ucyate||60||

61sarvārthānāṃ vyākaraṇādvaiyākaraṇa ucyate
|
tanmūlato vyākaraṇaṃ vyākarotīti tattathā||61||

62pratyakṣadarśī lokānāṃ sarvadarśī bhavennaraḥ
|
satye vai brāhmaṇastiṣṭhaṃstadvidvān sarvavidbhavet ||62||

63dharmādiṣu sthito'pyevaṃ kṣatriya brahma paśyati
|
vedānāṃ cānupūrvyeṇa etadbuddhyā bravīmi te ||63||
