Mahābhārata 5.149 (Udyogaparvan)

1vāsudeva uvāca|
evamukte tu gāndhāryā dhṛtarāṣṭro janeśvaraḥ
|
duryodhanamuvācedaṃ rājamadhye janādhipa ||1||

2duryodhana nibodhedaṃ yattvāṃ vakṣyāmi putraka
|
tathā tat kuru bhadraṃ te yadyasti pitṛ‌gauravam ||2||

3somaḥ prajāpatiḥ pūrvaṃ kurūṇāṃ vaṃśavardhanaḥ
|
somādbabhūva ṣaṣṭho'yaṃ yayātirnahuṣātmajaḥ ||3||

4tasya putrā babhūvurhi pañca rājarṣisattamāḥ
|
teṣāṃ yadurmahātejā jyeṣṭhaḥ samabhavat prabhuḥ ||4||

5pūruryavīyāṃśca tato yo'smākaṃ vaṃśavardhanaḥ
|
śarmiṣṭhayā samprasūto duhitrā vṛṣaparvaṇaḥ ||5||

6yaduśca bharataśreṣṭha devayānyāḥ suto'bhavat
|
dauhitrastāta śukrasya kāvyasyāmitatejasaḥ ||6||

7yādavānāṃ kulakaro balavān vīryasammataḥ
|
avamene sa tu kṣatraṃ darpapūrṇaḥ sumandadhīḥ ||7||

8na cātiṣṭhat pituḥ śāstre baladarpavimohitaḥ
|
avamene ca pitaraṃ bhrātṝṃścāpyaparājitaḥ ||8||

9pṛthivyāṃ caturantāyāṃ yadurevābhavadbalī
|
vaśe kṛtvā sa nṛpatīnnyavasannāgasāhvaye ||9||

10taṃ pitā paramakruddho yayātirnahuṣātmajaḥ
|
śaśāpa putraṃ gāndhāre rājyāccāpi vyaropayat ||10||

11ye cainamanvavartanta bhrātaro baladarpitāḥ
|
śaśāpa tānabhikruddho yayātistanayānatha ||11||

12yavīyāṃsaṃ tataḥ pūruṃ putraṃ svavaśavartinam
|
rājye niveśayāmāsa vidheyaṃ nṛpasattamaḥ ||12||

13evaṃ jyeṣṭho'pyathotsikto na rājyamabhijāyate
|
yavīyāṃso'pi jāyante rājyaṃ vṛddhopasevayā ||13||

14tathaiva sarvadharmajñaḥ piturmama pitāmahaḥ
|
pratīpaḥ pṛthivīpālastriṣu lokeṣu viśrutaḥ ||14||

15tasya pārthivasiṃhasya rājyaṃ dharmeṇa śāsataḥ
|
trayaḥ prajajñire putrā devakalpā yaśasvinaḥ ||15||

16devāpirabhavacchreṣṭho bāhlīkastadanantaram
|
tṛtīyaḥ śāntanustāta dhṛtimān me pitāmahaḥ ||16||

17devāpistu mahātejāstvagdoṣī rājasattamaḥ
|
dhārmikaḥ satyavādī ca pituḥ śuśrūṣaṇe rataḥ ||17||

18paurajānapadānāṃ ca sammataḥ sādhusatkṛtaḥ
|
sarveṣāṃ bālavṛddhānāṃ devāpirhṛdayaṅgamaḥ ||18||

19vadānyaḥ satyasandhaśca sarvabhūtahite rataḥ
|
vartamānaḥ pituḥ śāstre brāhmaṇānāṃ tathaiva ca ||19||

20bāhlīkasya priyo bhrātā śāntanośca mahātmanaḥ
|
saubhrātraṃ ca paraṃ teṣāṃ sahitānāṃ mahātmanām ||20||

21atha kālasya paryāye vṛddho nṛpatisattamaḥ
|
sambhārānabhiṣekārthaṃ kārayāmāsa śāstrataḥ ||21||

22kārayāmāsa sarvāṇi maṅgalārthāni vai vibhuḥ
|
taṃ brāhmaṇāśca vṛddhāśca paurajānapadaiḥ saha ||22||

23sarve nivārayāmāsurdevāperabhiṣecanam
|
sa tacchrutvā tu nṛpatirabhiṣekanivāraṇam
|
aśrukaṇṭho'bhavadrājā paryaśocata cātmajam ||23||

24evaṃ vadānyo dharmajñaḥ satyasandhaśca so'bhavat
|
priyaḥ prajānāmapi saṃstvagdoṣeṇa pradūṣitaḥ ||24||

25hīnāṅgaṃ pṛthivīpālaṃ nābhinandanti devatāḥ
|
iti kṛtvā nṛpaśreṣṭhaṃ pratyaṣedhan dvijarṣabhāḥ ||25||

26tataḥ pravyathitāṅgo'sau putraśokasamanvitaḥ
|
nivāritaṃ nṛpaṃ dṛṣṭvā devāpiḥ saṃśrito vanam ||26||

27bāhlīko mātulakulaṃ tyaktvā rājyaṃ samāśritaḥ
|
pitṛbhrātṝn parityajya prāptavān paramarddhimat ||27||

28bāhlīkena tvanujñātaḥ śāntanurlokaviśrutaḥ
|
pitaryuparate rājan rājā rājyamakārayat ||28||

29tathaivāhaṃ matimatā paricintyeha pāṇḍunā
|
jyeṣṭhaḥ prabhraṃśito rājyāddhīnāṅga iti bhārata ||29||

30pāṇḍustu rājyaṃ samprāptaḥ kanīyānapi sannṛpaḥ
|
vināśe tasya putrāṇāmidaṃ rājyamarindama ||30||

31mayyabhāgini rājyāya kathaṃ tvaṃ rājyamicchasi
|
arājaputro hyasvāmī parasvaṃ hartumicchasi ||31||

32yudhiṣṭhiro rājaputro mahātmā nyāyāgataṃ rājyamidaṃ ca tasya
|
sa kauravasyāsya kulasya bhartā praśāsitā caiva mahānubhāvaḥ ||32||

33sa satyasandhaḥ sa tathā'pramattaḥ śāstre sthito bandhujanasya sādhuḥ
|
priyaḥ prajānāṃ suhṛdānukampī jitendriyaḥ sādhujanasya bhartā ||33||

34kṣamā titikṣā dama ārjavaṃ ca satyavratatvaṃ śrutamapramādaḥ
|
bhūtānukampā hyanuśāsanaṃ ca yudhiṣṭhire rājaguṇāḥ samastāḥ ||34||

35arājaputrastvamanāryavṛtto lubdhaḥ sadā bandhuṣu pāpabuddhiḥ
|
kramāgataṃ rājyamidaṃ pareṣāṃ hartuṃ kathaṃ śakṣyasi durvinīta ||35||

36prayaccha rājyārdhamapetamohaḥ savāhanaṃ tvaṃ saparicchadaṃ ca
|
tato'vaśeṣaṃ tava jīvitasya sahānujasyaiva bhavennarendra ||36||
