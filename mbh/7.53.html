Mahābhārata 7.53 (Droṇaparvan)

1sthāṇuruvāca| prajāsarganimittaṃ hi kṛto yatnastvayā vibho
|
tvayā sṛṣṭāśca vṛddhāśca bhūtagrāmāḥ pṛthagvidhāḥ ||1||

2tāstaveha punaḥ krodhāt prajā dahyanti sarvaśaḥ
|
tā dṛṣṭvā mama kāruṇyaṃ prasīda bhagavan prabho ||2||

3brahmovāca
|
saṃhartuṃ na ca me kāma etadevaṃ bhavediti
|
pṛthivyā hitakāmaṃ tu tato māṃ manyurāviśat ||3||

4iyaṃ hi māṃ sahādevī bhārārtā samacudat
|
saṃhārārthaṃ mahādeva bhāreṇābhihatā satī ||4||

5tato'haṃ nādhigacchāmi tathā bahuvidhaṃ tadā
|
saṃhāramaprameyasya tato māṃ manyurāviśat ||5||

6rudra uvāca| saṃhārārthaṃ prasīdasva mā ruṣo vasudhādhipa
|
mā prajāḥ sthāvarāścaiva jaṅgamāśca vyanīnaśaḥ ||6||

7tava prasādādbhagavannidaṃ vartettridhā jagat
|
anāgatamatītaṃ ca yacca samprati vartate ||7||

8bhagavan krodhasandīptaḥ krodhādagnimavāsṛjat
|
sa dahatyaśmakūṭāni drumāṃśca saritastathā ||8||

9palvalāni ca sarvāṇi sarve caiva tṛṇolapāḥ
|
sthāvaraṃ jaṅgamaṃ caiva niḥśeṣaṃ kurute jagat ||9||

10tadetadbhasmasādbhūtaṃ jagat sthāvarajaṅgamam
|
prasīda bhagavan sa tvaṃ roṣo na syādvaro mama ||10||

11sarve hi sṛṣṭā naśyanti tava deva kathañcana
|
tasmānnivartatāṃ tejastvayyevedaṃ pralīyatām ||11||

12tat paśya deva subhṛśaṃ prajānāṃ hitakāmyayā
|
yatheme prāṇinaḥ sarve nivarteraṃstathā kuru ||12||

13abhāvaṃ neha gaccheyurutsannajananāḥ prajāḥ
|
ādideva niyukto'smi tvayā lokeṣu lokakṛt ||13||

14mā vinaśyejjagannātha jagat sthāvarajaṅgamam
|
prasādābhimukhaṃ devaṃ tasmādevaṃ bravīmyaham ||14||

15nārada uvāca| śrutvā hi vacanaṃ devaḥ prajānāṃ hitakāraṇe
|
tejaḥ sandhārayāmāsa punarevāntarātmani ||15||

16tato'gnimupasaṃhṛtya bhagavā~llokasatkṛtaḥ
|
pravṛttaṃ ca nivṛttaṃ ca kathayāmāsa vai prabhuḥ ||16||

17upasaṃharatastasya tamagniṃ roṣajaṃ tathā
|
prādurbabhūva viśvebhyo gobhyo nārī mahātmanaḥ ||17||

18kṛṣṇaraktā tathā piṅgaraktajihvāsyalocanā
|
kuṇḍalābhyāṃ ca rājendra taptābhyāṃ taptabhūṣaṇā ||18||

19sā niḥsṛtya tathā khebhyo dakṣiṇāṃ diśamāśritā
|
smayamānā ca sā'vekṣya devau viśveśvarāvubhau ||19||

20tāmāhūya tadā devo lokādinidhaneśvaraḥ
|
mṛtyo iti mahīpāla jahi cemāḥ prajā iti ||20||

21tvaṃ hi saṃhārabuddhyātha prādurbhūtā ruṣo mama
|
tasmāt saṃhara sarvāstvaṃ prajāḥ sajaḍapaṇḍitāḥ ||21||

22mama tvaṃ hi niyogena tataḥ śreyo hyavāpsyasi
|
evamuktā tu sā tena mṛtyuḥ kamalalocanā ||22||

23dadhyau cātyarthamabalā praruroda ca susvaram
|
pāṇibhyāṃ pratijagrāha tānyaṇi pitāmahaḥ
|
sarvabhūtahitārthāya tāṃ cāpyanunayat tadā ||23||
