Mahābhārata 7.104 (Droṇaparvan)

1sañjaya uvāca|
tāvakā hi samīkṣyaivaṃ vṛṣyandhakakurūttamau
|
prāgatvaran jighāṃsantastathaiva vijayaḥ parān ||1||

2suvarṇacitrairvaiyāghraiḥ svanavadbhirmahārathaiḥ
|
dīpayanto diśaḥ sarvā jvaladbhiriva pāvakaiḥ ||2||

3rukmapuṃkhaiśca duṣprekṣyaiḥ kārmukaiḥ pṛthivīpate
|
kūjadbhiratulānnādān kopitaisturagairiva ||3||

4bhūriśravāḥ śalaḥ karṇo vṛṣaseno jayadrathaḥ
|
kṛpaśca madrarājaśca drauṇiśca rathināṃ varaḥ ||4||

5te pibanta ivākāśamaśvairaṣṭau mahārathāḥ
|
vyarājayan daśa diśo vaiyāghrairhemacandrakaiḥ ||5||

6te daṃśitāḥ susaṃrabdhā rathairmeghaughaniḥsvanaiḥ
|
samāvṛṇvandaśa diśaḥ pārthasya niśitaiḥ śaraiḥ ||6||

7kaulūtakā hayāścitrā vahantastānmahārathān
|
vyaśobhanta tadā śīghrā dīpayanto diśo daśa ||7||

8ājāneyairmahāvegairnānādeśasamutthitaiḥ
|
pārvatīyairnadījaiśca saindhavaiśca hayottamaiḥ ||8||

9kuruyodhavarā rājaṃstava putraṃ parīpsavaḥ
|
dhanañjayarathaṃ śīghraṃ sarvataḥ samupādravan ||9||

10te pragṛhya mahāśaṃkhān dadhmuḥ puruṣasattamāḥ
|
pūrayanto divaṃ rājan pṛthivīṃ ca sasāgarām ||10||

11tathaiva dadhmatuḥ śaṅkhau vāsudevadhanañjayau
|
pravarau sarvadevānāṃ sarvaśaṅkhavarau bhuvi ||11||

12devadattaṃ ca kaunteyaḥ pāñcajanyaṃ ca keśavaḥ
|
śabdastu devadattasya dhanañjayasamīritaḥ ||12||

13pṛthivīṃ cāntarikṣaṃ ca diśaścaiva samāvṛṇot
|
tathaiva pāñcajanyo'pi vāsudevasamīritaḥ ||13||

14sarvaśabdānatikramya pūrayāmāsa rodasī
|
tasmiṃstathā vartamāne dāruṇe nādasaṃkule ||14||

15bhīrūṇāṃ trāsajanane śūrāṇāṃ harṣavardhane
|
pravāditāsu bherīṣu jharjhareṣvānakeṣu ca ||15||

16mṛdaṃgeṣvapi rājendra vādyamāneṣvanekaśaḥ
|
mahārathāḥ samāhūtā duryodhanahitaiṣiṇaḥ ||16||

17amṛṣyamāṇāstaṃ śabdaṃ kruddhāḥ paramadhanvinaḥ
|
nānādeśyā mahīpālāḥ svasainyaparirakṣiṇaḥ ||17||

18amarṣitā mahāśaṃkhān dadhmurvīrā mahārathāḥ
|
kṛte pratikariṣyantaḥ keśavasyārjunasya ca ||18||

19babhūva tava tatsainyaṃ śaṃkhaśabdasamīritam
|
udvignarathanāgāśvamasvasthamiva vā vibho ||19||

20tat praviddhamivākāśaṃ śūraiḥ śaṃkhavināditam
|
babhūva bhṛśamudvignaṃ nirghātairiva nāditam ||20||

21sa śabdaḥ sumahān rājan diśaḥ sarvā vyanādayat
|
trāsayāmāsa tat sainyaṃ yugānta iva saṃbhṛtaḥ ||21||

22tato duryodhano'ṣṭau ca rājānaste mahārathāḥ
|
jayadrathasya rakṣārthaṃ pāṇḍavaṃ paryavārayan ||22||

23tato drauṇistrisaptatyā vāsudevamatāḍayat
|
arjunaṃ ca tribhirbhalairdhvajamaśvāṃśca pañcabhiḥ ||23||

24tamarjunaḥ pṛṣatkānāṃ śataiḥ ṣaḍbhiratāḍayat
|
atyarthamiva saṃkruddhaḥ pratividdhe janārdane ||24||

25karṇaṃ ca daśabhirviddhvā vṛṣasenaṃ tribhistathā
|
śalyasya saśaraṃ cāpaṃ muṣṭau ciccheda vīryavān ||25||

26gṛhītvā dhanuranyattu śalyo vivyādha pāṃḍavam
|
bhūriśravāstribhirbāṇairhemapuṃkhaiḥ śilāśitaiḥ ||26||

27karṇo dvātriṃśatā caiva vṛṣasenaśca saptabhiḥ
|
jayadrathastrisaptatyā kṛpaśca daśabhiḥ śaraiḥ ||27||

28madrarājaśca daśabhirvivyadhuḥ phālgunaṃ raṇe
|
tataḥ śarāṇāṃ ṣaṣṭyā tu drauṇiḥ pārthamavākirat ||28||

29vāsudevaṃ ca viṃśatyā punaḥ pārthe ca pañcabhiḥ
|
prahasaṃstu naravyāghraḥ śvetāśvaḥ kṛṣṇasārathiḥ ||29||

30pratyavidhyatsa tānsarvāndarśayanpāṇilāghavam
|
karṇaṃ dvādaśabhirviddhvā vṛṣasenaṃ tribhiḥ śaraiḥ ||30||

31śalyasya saśaraṃ cāpaṃ muṣṭideśe vyakṛntata
|
saumadattiṃ tribhirviddhvā śalyaṃ ca daśabhiḥ śaraiḥ ||31||

32śitairagniśikhākārairdrauṇiṃ vivyādha cāṣṭabhiḥ
|
gautamaṃ pañcaviṃśatyā saindhavaṃ ca śatena ha ||32||

33punardrauṇiṃ ca saptatyā śarāṇāṃ so'bhyatāḍayat
|
bhūriśravāstu saṃkruddhaḥ pratodaṃ cicchide hareḥ ||33||

34arjunaṃ ca trisaptatyā bāṇānāmājaghāna ha ||34||

35tataḥ śaraśataistīkṣṇaistānarīn śvetavāhanaḥ
|
pratyaṣedhaddrutaṃ kruddho mahāvāto ghanāniva ||35||
