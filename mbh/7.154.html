Mahābhārata 7.154 (Droṇaparvan)

1dhṛtarāṣṭra uvāca|
yattadā prāviśatpāṇḍūnācāryaḥ kupito balī
|
uktvā duryodhanaṃ mandaṃ mama śāstrātigaṃ sutam||1||

2praviśya vicarantaṃ ca rathe śūramavasthitam
|
kathaṃ droṇaṃ maheṣvāsaṃ pāṇḍavāḥ paryavārayan||2||

3ke'rakṣandakṣiṇaṃ cakramācāryasya mahāhave
|
ke cottaramarakṣanta nighnataḥ śātravānbahūn||3||

4ke cāsya pṛṣṭhato'nvāsan vīrā vīrasya yodhinaḥ
|
ke purastādavartanta rathinastasya śatravaḥ||4||

5manye tānaspṛśacchītamativelamanārtavam
|
manye te samavepanta gāvo vai śiśire yathā||5||

6yatprāviśanmaheṣvāsaḥ pañcālānaparājitaḥ
|
nṛtyan sa rathamārgeṣu sarvaśastrabhṛtāṃ varaḥ||6||

7nirdahan sarvasainyāni pañcālānāṃ ratharṣabhaḥ
|
dhūmaketuriva kruddhaḥ kathaṃ mṛtyumupeyivān||7||

8sañjaya uvāca|
sāyānhe saindhavaṃ hatvā rājñā pārthaḥ sametya ca
|
sātyakiśca maheṣvāso droṇamevābhyadhāvatām||8||

9tathā yudhiṣṭhirastūrṇaṃ bhīmasenaśca pāṇḍavaḥ
|
pṛthakcamūbhyāṃ saṃyattau droṇamevābhyadhāvatām||9||

10tathaiva nakulo dhīmānsahadevaśca durjayaḥ
|
dhṛṣṭadyumnaḥ sahānīko virāṭaśca sakekayaḥ||10||

11matsyāḥ śālvāḥ sasenāśca droṇameva yayuryudhi
|
drupadaśca tathā rājā pañcālairabhirakṣitaḥ||11||

12dhṛṣṭadyumnapitā rājandroṇamevābhyavartata
|
draupadeyā maheṣvāsā rākṣasaśca ghaṭotkacaḥ||12||

13sasainyāste nyavartanta droṇameva mahādyutim
|
prabhadrakāśca pañcālāḥ ṣaṭsahasrāḥ prahāriṇaḥ||13||

14droṇamevābhyavartanta puraskṛtya śikhaṇḍinam
|
tathetare naravyāghrāḥ pāṇḍavānāṃ mahārathāḥ||14||

15sahitāḥ saṃnyavartanta droṇameva dvijarṣabham
|
teṣu śūreṣu yuddhāya gateṣu bharatarṣabha||15||

16babhūva rajanī ghorā bhīrūṇāṃ bhayavardhinī
|
yodhānāmaśivā raudrā rājannantakagāminī||16||

17kuñjarāśvamanuṣyāṇāṃ prāṇāntakaraṇī tadā
|
tasyāṃ rajanyāṃ ghorāyāṃ nadantyaḥ sarvataḥ śivāḥ||17||

18nyavedayanbhayaṃ ghoraṃ sajvālakavalairmukhaiḥ
|
ulūkāścāpyadṛśyanta śaṃsanto vipulaṃ bhayam||18||

19viśeṣataḥ kauravāṇāṃ dhvajinyāmatidāruṇāḥ
|
tataḥ sainyeṣu rājendra śabdaḥ samabhavanmahān||19||

20bherīśabdena mahatā mṛdaṅgānāṃ svanena ca
|
gajānāṃ bṛṃhitaiścāpi turaṅgāṇāṃ ca heṣitaiḥ||20||

21khuraśabda‌nnipātaiśca tumulaḥ sarvato'bhavat
|
tataḥ samabhavadyuddhaṃ sandhyāyāmatidāruṇam||21||

22droṇasya ca mahārāja sṛñjayānāṃ ca sarvaśaḥ
|
tamasā cāvṛte loke na prājñāyata kiñcana||22||

23sainyena rajasā caiva samantādutthitena ha
|
narasyāśvasya nāgasya samasajjata śoṇitam||23||

24nāpaśyāma rajo bhaumaṃ kaśmalenābhisaṃvṛtāḥ
|
rātrau vaṃśavanasyeva dahyamānasya parvate||24||

25ghoraścaṭacaṭāśabdaḥ śastrāṇāṃ patatāmabhūt
|
mṛdaṅgānakanirhrādairjharjharaiḥ paṭahaistathā||25||

26phetkārairhreṣitaiḥ śabdaiḥ sarvamevākulaṃ babhau
|
naiva sve na pare rājanprājñāyanta tamovṛte||26||

27unmattamiva tatsarvaṃ babhūva rajanīmukhe
|
bhaumaṃ rajo'tha rājendra śoṇitena praṇāśitam||27||

28śātakaumbhaiśca kavacairbhūṣaṇaiśca tamo'bhyagāt
|
tataḥ sā bhāratī senā maṇihemavibhūṣitā||28||

29dyaurivāsītsanakṣatrā rajanyāṃ bharatarṣabha
|
gomāyubalasaṃghuṣṭā śaktidhvajasamākulā||29||

30vāraṇābhirutā ghorā kṣveḍitotkruṣṭanāditā
|
tatrābhavanmahāśabdastumulo lomaharṣaṇaḥ||30||

31samāvṛṇvandiśaḥ sarvā mahendrāśaniniḥsvanaḥ
|
sā niśīthe mahārāja senā'dṛśyata bhāratī||31||

32aṅgadaiḥ kuṇḍalairniṣkaiḥ śastraiścaivāvabhāsitā
|
tatra nāgā rathāścaiva jāṃbūnadavibhūṣitāḥ||32||

33niśāyāṃ pratyadṛśyanta meghā iva savidyutaḥ
|
ṛṣṭi-śakti-gadā-bāṇa-musala-prāsa-paṭṭiśāḥ||33||

34saṃpatanto vyadṛśyanta bhrājamānā ivāgnayaḥ
|
duryodhanapurovātāṃ rathanāgabalāhakām||34||

35vāditraghoṣastanitāṃ cāpavidyuddhvajairvṛtām
|
droṇapāṇḍavaparjanyāṃ khaḍgaśaktigadāśanim||35||

36śaradhārāstrapavanāṃ bhṛśaṃ śītoṣṇasaṃkulām
|
ghorāṃ vismāpanīmugrāṃ jīvitacchidamaplavām||36||

37tāṃ prāviśannatibhayāṃ senāṃ yuddhacikīrṣavaḥ
|
tasmin rātrimukhe ghore mahāśabdaninādite||37||

38bhīrūṇāṃ trāsajanane śūrāṇāṃ harṣavardhane
|
rātriyuddhe mahāghore vartamāne sudāruṇe||38||

39droṇamabhyadravan kruddhāḥ sahitāḥ pāṇḍusṛñjayāḥ
|
ye ye pramukhato rājannāvartanta mahārathāḥ||39||

40tānsarvānvimukhāṃścakre kāṃścinninye yamakṣayam
|
tāni nāgasahastrāṇi rathānāmayutāni ca||40||

41padātihayasaṅghānāṃ prayutānyarbudāni ca
|
droṇenaikena nārācairnirbhinnāni niśāmukhe||41||
