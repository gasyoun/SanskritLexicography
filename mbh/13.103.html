Mahābhārata 13.103 (Anuśāsanaparvan)

1yudhiṣṭhira uvāca|
dānaṃ bahuvidhākāraṃ śāntiḥ satyamahiṃsitam
|
svadāratuṣṭiścoktā te phalaṃ dānasya caiva yat ||1||

2pitāmahasya viditaṃ kimanyattapaso balāt
|
tapaso yatparaṃ te'dya tanno vyākhyātumarhasi ||2||

3bhīṣma uvāca|
tapaḥ pracakṣate yāvattāvalloko yudhiṣṭhira
|
mataṃ mamātra kaunteya tapo nānaśanātparam ||3||

4atrāpyudāharantīmamitihāsaṃ purātanam
|
bhagīrathasya saṃvādaṃ brahmaṇaśca mahātmanaḥ ||4||

5atītya suralokaṃ ca gavāṃ lokaṃ ca bhārata
|
ṛṣilokaṃ ca so'gacchat bhagīratha iti śrutam ||5||

6taṃ tu dṛṣṭvā vacaḥ prāha brahmā rājanbhagīrathan
|
kathaṃ bhagīrathāgāstvamimaṃ lokaṃ durāsadam ||6||

7na hi devā na gandharvā na manuṣyā bhagīratha
|
āyāntyataptatapasaḥ kathaṃ vai tvamihāgataḥ ||7||

8bhagīratha uvāca|
niṣkāṇāṃ vai hyadadaṃ brāhmaṇebhyaḥ śataṃ sahastrāṇi sadaiva dānam
|
brāhmaṃ vrataṃ nityamāsthāya vidvanna tvevāhaṃ tasya phalādihāgām ||8||

9daśaikarātrāndaśapañcarātrānekādaśaikādaśakān kratūṃśca
|
jyotiṣṭomānāṃ ca śataṃ yadiṣṭaṃ phalena tenāpi ca nāgato'ham ||9||

10yaccāvasaṃ jāhnavītīranityaḥ śataṃ samāstapyamānastapo'ham
|
adāṃ ca tatrāśvatarīsahasraṃ nārīpuraṃ na ca tenāhamāgām ||10||

11daśāyutāni cāśvānāṃ goyutāni ca viṃśatim
|
puṣkareṣu dvijātibhyaḥ prādāṃ śatasahasraśaḥ ||11||

12suvarṇacandrottamadhāriṇīnāṃ kanyottamānāmadadaṃ sahasram
|
ṣaṣṭiṃ sahasrāṇi vibhūṣitānāṃ jāmbūnadairābharaṇairna tena||12||

13daśārbudānyadadaṃ gosavejyāsvekaikaśo daśa gāṃ lokanātha
|
samānavatsāḥ payasā samanvitāḥ suvarṇakāṃsyopaduhā na tena ||13||

14āptoryāmeṣu niyatamekaikasmindaśādadam
|
gṛṣṭīnāṃ kṣīradātrīṇāṃ rohiṇīnāṃ śatāni ca ||14||

15dogdhrīṇāṃ vai gavāṃ cāpi prayutāni daśaiva ha
|
prādāṃ daśaguṇaṃ brahmanna tenāhamihāgataḥ ||15||

16vājināṃ bāhlijātānāmayutānyadadaṃ daśa
|
karkāṇāṃ hemamālānāṃ na ca tenāhamāgataḥ ||16||

17koṭīśca kāñcanasyāṣṭau prādāṃ brahmandaśānvaham
|
ekaikasmin kratau tena phalenāhaṃ na cāgataḥ ||17||

18vājināṃ śyāmakarṇānāṃ haritānāṃ pitāmaha
|
prādāṃ hemasrajāṃ brahmankoṭīrdaśa ca sapta ca ||18||

19īṣādantānmahākāyānkāñcanasragvibhūṣitān
|
padmino vai sahasrāṇi prādāṃ daśa ca sapta ca ||19||

20alaṅkṛtānāṃ deveśa divyaiḥ kanakabhūṣaṇaiḥ
|
rathānāṃ kāñcanāṅgānāṃ sahastrāṇyadadaṃ daśa ||20||

21sapta cānyāni yuktāni vājibhiḥ samalaṅkṛtaiḥ
|
dakṣiṇāvayavāḥ kecidvedairye saṃprakīrtitāḥ ||21||

22vājapeyeṣu daśasu prādāṃ teṣvapi cāpyaham
|
śakratulyaprabhāvāṇāmijyayā vikrameṇa ha ||22||

23sahasraṃ niṣkakaṇṭhānāmadadaṃ dakṣiṇāmaham
|
vijitya bhūpatīnsarvānarthairiṣṭvā pitāmaha ||23||

24aṣṭabhyo rājasūyebhyo na ca tenāhamāgataḥ
|
srotaśca yāvadgaṅgāyāśchannamāsījjagatpate ||24||

25dakṣiṇābhiḥ pravṛttābhirmama nāgāṃ ca tatkṛte
|
vājināṃ ca sahastre dve suvarṇaśatabhūṣite ||25||

26varaṃ grāmaśataṃ cāhamekaikasya tridhā'dadam
|
tapasvī niyatāhāraḥ śamamāsthāya vāgyataḥ ||26||

27dīrghakālaṃ himavati gaṅgāyāśca durutsahām
|
mūrdhnā dhārāṃ mahādevaḥ śirasā yāmadhārayat
|
na tenāpyahamāgacchaṃ phaleneha pitāmaha ||27||

28śamyākṣepairayajaṃ yacca devānsādyaskrānāmayutaiścāpi yattat
|
trayodaśadvādaśāhaiśca deva sapauṇḍarīkānna ca teṣāṃ phalena ||28||

29aṣṭau sahasrāṇi kakudmināmahaṃ śuklarṣabhāṇāmadadaṃ dvijebhyaḥ
|
ekaikaṃ vai kāñcanaṃ śrṛṅgamebhyaḥ patnīścaiṣāmadadaṃ niṣkakaṇṭhīḥ ||29||

30hiraṇyaratnanicayānadadaṃ ratnaparvatān
|
dhanadhānyasamṛddhāśca grāmāścānye sahasraśaḥ ||30||

31śataṃ śatānāṃ gṛṣṭīnāmadadaṃ cāpyatandritaḥ
|
iṣṭvā'nekairmahāyajñairbrāhmaṇebhyo na tena ca ||31||

32ekādaśāhairayajaṃ sadakṣiṇairdvirdvādaśāhairaśvamedhaiśca deva
|
ārkāyaṇaiḥ ṣoḍaśabhiśca brahmasteṣāṃ phaleneha na cāgato'smi ||32||

33niṣkaikakaṇṭhamadadaṃ yojanāyataṃ tadvistīrṇaṃ kāñcanapādapānām
|
vanaṃ vṛtānāṃ ratnavibhūṣitānāṃ na caiva teṣāmāgato'haṃ phalena ||33||

34turāyaṇaṃ hi vratamapyadhṛṣyamakrodhano'karavaṃ triṃśato'bdān
|
śataṃ gavāmaṣṭaśatāni caiva dine dine hyadadaṃ brāhmaṇebhyaḥ ||34||

35payasvinīnāmatha rohiṇīnāṃ tathaivānyānanaḍuho lokanātha
|
prādāṃ nityaṃ brāhmaṇebhyaḥ sureśa nehāgatastena phalena cāham ||35||

36triṃśadagnīnahaṃ brahmannayajaṃ yacca nityadā
|
aṣṭābhiḥ sarvamedhaiśca naramedhaiśca saptabhiḥ ||36||

37daśabhirviśvajidbhiśca śatairaṣṭāda‌śottaraiḥ
|
na caiva teṣāṃ deveśa phalenāhamihāgamam ||37||

38sarayvāṃ bāhudāyāṃ ca gaṅgāyāmatha naimiṣe
|
gavāṃ śatānāmayutamadadaṃ na ca tena vai ||38||

39indreṇa guhyaṃ nihitaṃ vai guhāyāṃ yadbhārgavastapasehābhyavindat
|
jājvalyamānamuśanastejaseha tatsādhayāmāsamahaṃ vareṇya ||39||

40tato me brāhmaṇāstuṣṭāstasminkarmaṇi sādhite
|
sahasramṛṣayaścāsanye vai tatra samāgatāḥ ||40||

41uktastairasmi gaccha tvaṃ brahmalokamiti prabho
|
prītenoktasahasreṇa brāhmaṇānāmahaṃ prabho
|
imaṃ lokamanuprāpto mābhūtte'tra vicāraṇā ||41||

42kāmaṃ yathāvadvihitaṃ vidhātrā pṛṣṭena vācyaṃ tu mayā yathāvat
|
tapo hi nānyaccānaśanānmataṃ me namo'stu te devavara prasīda ||42||

43bhīṣma uvāca|
ityuktavantaṃ brahmā tu rājānaṃ sa bhagīratham
|
pūjayāmāsa pūjārhaṃ vidhidṛṣṭena karmaṇā ||43||

44tasmādanaśanairyukto viprānpūjaya nityadā
|
viprāṇāṃ vacanātsarvaṃ paratreha ca sidhyati ||44||

45vāsobhirannairgobhiśca śumairnaiveśikairapi
|
śubhaiḥ suragaṇaiścāpi stoṣyā eva dvijāstathā
|
etadeva paraṃ guhyamalobhena samācara||45||
