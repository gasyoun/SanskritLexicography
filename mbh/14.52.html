Mahābhārata 14.52 (Āśvamedhikaparvan)

1vaiśampāyana uvāca|
tato'bhyanodayatkṛṣṇo yujyatāmiti dārukam
|
muhūrtādiva cācaṣṭa yuktamityeva dārukaḥ||1||

2tathaiva cānuyātrādi codayāmāsa pāṇḍavaḥ
|
sajjayadhvaṃ prayāsyāmo nagaraṃ gajasāhvayam||2||

3ityuktāḥ sainikāste tu sajjībhūtā viśāmpate
|
ācakhyuḥ sajjamityevaṃ pārthāyāmitatejase||3||

4tatastau rathamāsthāya prayātau kṛṣṇapāṇḍavau
|
vikurvāṇau kathāścitrāḥ prīyamāṇau viśāmpate||4||

5rathasthaṃ tu mahātejā vāsudevaṃ dhanañjayaḥ
|
punarevābravīdvākyamidaṃ bharatasattama||5||

6tvatprasādājjayaḥ prāpto rājñā vṛṣṇikulodvaha
|
niyatāḥ śatravaścāpi prāptaṃ rājyamakaṇṭakam||6||

7nāthavantaśca bhavatā pāṇḍavā madhusūdana
|
bhavantaṃ plamāsādya tīrṇāḥ sma kurusāgaram||7||

8viśvakarmannamaste'stu viśvātmanviśvasattama
|
tatha tvāmabhijānāmi yathā cāhaṃ bhavanmataḥ||8||

9tvattejaḥ sambhavo nityaṃ bhūtātmā madhusūdana
|
ratiḥ krīḍāmayī tubhyaṃ māyā te rodasī vibho||9||

10tvayi sarvamidaṃ viśvaṃ yadidaṃ sthāṇu jaṅgamam
|
tvaṃ hi sarvaṃ vikuruṣe bhūtagrāmaṃ caturvidham||10||

11pṛthivīṃ cāntarikṣaṃ ca dyāṃ caiva madhusūdana
|
hasitaṃ te'malā jyotsnā ṛtavaścendriyāṇi te||11||

12prāṇo vāyuḥ satatagaḥ krodho mṛtyuḥ sanātanaḥ
|
prasāde cāpi padmā śrīrnityaṃ tvayi mahāmate||12||

13ratistuṣṭirdhṛtiḥ kṣāntirmatiḥ kāntiścarācaram
|
tvameveha yugānteṣu nidhanaṃ procyase'nadha||13||

14sudīrgheṇāpi kālena na te śakyā guṇā mayā
|
ātmā ca paramātmā ca namaste nalinekṣaṇa||14||

15vidito me sudurdharṣa nāradāddevalāttathā
|
kṛṣṇadvaipāyanāccaiva tathā kurupitāmahāt||15||

16tvayi sarvaṃ samāsaktaṃ tvamevaiko janeśvaraḥ
|
yaccānugrahasaṃyuktametaduktaṃ tvayānadha||16||

17etatsarvamahaṃ samyagācariṣye janārdana
|
idaṃ cādbhutamatyantaṃ kṛtamasmatpriyepsayā||17||

18yatpāpo nihataḥ saṅkhye kauravyo dhṛtarāṣṭrajaḥ
|
tvayā dagdhaṃ hi tatsainyaṃ mayā vijitamāhave||18||

19bhavatā tatkṛtaṃ karma yenāvāpto jayo mayā
|
duryodhanasya saṅgrāme tava buddhiparākramaiḥ||19||

20karṇasya ca vadhopāyo yathāvatsampradarśitaḥ
|
saindhavasya ca pāpasya bhūriśravasa eva ca||20||

21ahaṃ ca prīyamāṇena tvayā devakinandana
|
yaduktastatkariṣyāmi na hi me'tra vicāraṇā||21||

22rājānaṃ ca samāsādya dharmātmānaṃ yudhiṣṭhiram
|
codayiṣyāmi dharmajña gamanārthaṃ tavānagha||22||

23rucitaṃ hi mamaitatte dvārakāgamanaṃ prabho
|
acirādeva draṣṭā tvaṃ mātulaṃ me janārdana||23||

24baladevaṃ ca durdharṣaṃ tathānyānvṛṣṇipuṅgavān
|
evaṃ sambhāṣamāṇau tau prāptau vāraṇasāhvayam||24||

25tathā viviśatuścobhau samprahṛṣṭanarākulam
|
tau gatvā dhṛtarāṣṭrasya gṛhaṃ śakragṛhopamam||25||

26dadṛśāte mahārāja dhṛtarāṣṭraṃ janeśvaram
|
viduraṃ ca mahābuddhiṃ rājānaṃ ca yudhiṣṭhiram||26||

27bhīmasenaṃ ca durdharṣaṃ mādrīputrau ca pāṇḍavau
|
dhṛtarāṣṭramupāsīnaṃ yuyutsuṃ cāparājitam||27||

28gāndhārī ca mahāprajñāṃ pṛthāṃ kṛṣṇāṃ ca bhāminīm
|
subhadrādyāśca tāḥ sarvā bharatānāṃ striyastathā||28||

29dadṛśāte striyaḥ sarvā gāndhārīparicārikāḥ
|
tataḥ sametya rājānaṃ dhṛtarāṣṭramarindamau||29||

30nivedya nāmadheye sve tasya pādāvagṛhṇatām
|
gāndhāryāśca pṛthāyāśca dharmarājasya caiva hi||30||

31bhīmasya ca mahātmānau tathā pādāvagṛhṇatām
|
kṣattāraṃ cāpi saṅgṛhya pṛṣṭvā kuśalamavyayam||31||

32taiḥ sārdhaṃ nṛpatiṃ vṛddhaṃ tatastau paryupāsatām
|
tato niśi mahārājo dhṛtarāṣṭra: kurūdvahān||32||

33janārdanaṃ ca medhāvī vyasarjayata vai gṛhān
|
te'nujñātā nṛpatinā yayuḥ svaṃ svaṃ niveśanam||33||

34dhanañjayagṛhāneva yayau kṛṣṇastu vīryavān
|
tatrārcito yathānyāyaṃ sarvakāmarūpasthitaḥ||34||

35kṛṣṇaḥ suṣvāpa medhāvī dhanañjayasahāyavān
|
prabhātāyāṃ tu śarvaryāṃ kṛtvā paurvāhnikī kriyām||35||

36dharmarājasya bhavanaṃ jagmatuḥ paramārcitau
|
yatrāste sa sahāmātyo dharmarājo mahābalaḥ||36||

37tau praviśya mahātmānau tadgṛhaṃ paramārcitam
|
dharmarājaṃ dadṛśaturdevarājamivāśvinau||37||

38samāsādya tu rājānaṃ vārṣṇeyakurupuṅgavau
|
niṣīdaturanujñātau prīyamāṇena tena tau||38||

39tataḥ sa rājā medhāvī vivakṣū prekṣya tāvubhau
|
provāca vadatāṃ śreṣṭho vacanaṃ rājasattamaḥ||39||

40yudhiṣṭhira uvāca|
vivakṣū hi yuvāṃ manye vīrau yadukurudvahau
|
būtaṃ kartāsmi sarve vāṃ nacirānmā vicāryatām||40||

41ityuktaḥ phālgunastatra dharmarājānamabravīt
|
vinītavadupāgamya vākyaṃ vākyaviśāradaḥ||41||

42ayaṃ ciroṣito rājan vāsudevaḥ pratāpavān
|
bhavantaṃ samanujñāpya pitaraṃ draṣṭumicchati||42||

43sa gacchedabhyanujñāto bhavatā yadi manyase
|
ānartanagarīṃ vīrastadanujñātumarhasi||43||

44yudhiṣṭhira uvāca|
puṇḍarīkākṣa bhadraṃ te gaccha tvaṃ madhusūdana
|
purīṃ dvāravatīmadya draṣṭuṃ śūrasutaṃ prabho||44||

45rocate me mahābāho gamanaṃ tava keśava
|
mātulaściradṛṣṭo me tvayā devī ca devakī||45||

46sametya mātulaṃ gatvā baladevaṃ ca mānada
|
pūjayethā mahāprājña madvākyena yathārhataḥ||46||

47smarethāścāpi māṃ nityaṃ bhīmaṃ ca balinā varam
|
phālgunaṃ sahadevaṃ ca nakulaṃ caiva mānada||47||

48ānartānavalokya tvaṃ pitaraṃ ca mahābhuja
|
vṛṣṇīśca punarāgaccherhayamedhe mamānagha||48||

49sa gaccha ratnānyādāya vividhāni vasūni ca
|
yaccāpyanyanmanojñaṃ te tadapyādatsva sātvata||49||

50iyaṃ ca vasudhā kṛtsnā prasādāttava keśava
|
asmānupagatā vīra nihatāścāpi śatravaḥ||50||

51evaṃ bruvati kauravye dharmarāje yudhiṣṭhire
|
vāsudevo varaḥ puṃsāmidaṃ vacanamabravīt||51||

52tavaiva ratnāni dhanaṃ ca kevalaṃ dharā tu kṛtsnā tu mahābhujādya vai
|
yadasti cānyaddraviṇaṃ gṛhe mama tvameva tasyeśvara nityamīśvaraḥ||52||

53tathetyathoktaḥ pratipūjitastadā gadāgrajo dharmasutena vīryavān
|
pitṛṣvasāraṃ tvavadadyathāvidhi sampūjitaścāpyagamatpradakṣiṇam||53||

54tayā sa samyakpratinanditastata stathaiva sarvairvidurādibhistathā
|
viniryayau nāgapurādgadāgrajo rathena divyena caturbhujaḥ svayam||54||

55rathe subhadrāmadhiropya bhāvinīṃ yudhiṣṭhirasyānumate janārdanaḥ
|
pitṛṣvasuścāpi tathā mahābhujo viniryayau paurajanābhisaṃvṛtaḥ||55||

56tamanvayādvānaravaryaketanaḥ sa sātyakirmādravatīsutāvapi
|
agādhabuddhirviduraśca mādhavaṃ svayaṃ ca bhīmo gajarājavikramaḥ||56||

57nivartayitvā kururāṣṭravardhanāṃstataḥ sa sarvānviduraṃ ca vīryavān
|
janārdano dārukamāha satvaraḥ pracodayāśvāniti sātyakiṃ tathā||57||

58tato yayau śatrugaṇapramardanaḥ śinipravīrānugato janārdanaḥ
|
yathā nihatyārigaṇaṃ śatakraturdivaṃ tathā''nartapurīṃ pratāpavān||58||
