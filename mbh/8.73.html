Mahābhārata 8.73 (Karṇaparvan)

1sañjaya uvāca|
tataḥ punarameyātmā keśavo'rjunamabravīt
|
kṛtasaṃkalpamāyāntaṃ vadhe karṇasya bhārata ||1||

2adya saptadaśāhāni vartamānasya bhārata
|
vināśasyātighorasya naravāraṇavājinām ||2||

3bhūtvā hi vipulā senā tāvakānāṃ paraiḥ saha
|
anyonyaṃ samaraṃ prāpya kiñciccheṣā viśāṃpate ||3||

4bhūtvā vai kauravāḥ pārtha prabhūtagajavājinaḥ
|
tvāṃ vai śatruṃ samāsādya vinaṣṭā raṇamūrdhani ||4||

5ete te pṛthivīpālāḥ sṛñjayāśca samāgatāḥ
|
tvāṃ samāsādya durdharṣaṃ pāṇḍavāśca vyavasthitāḥ ||5||

6pañcālaiḥ pāṇḍavairmatsyaiḥ kārūṣaiścedibhiḥ saha
|
tvayā guptairamitraghnaiḥ kṛtaḥ śatrugaṇakṣayaḥ ||6||

7ko hi śakto raṇe jetuṃ kauravāṃstāta saṃyuge
|
anyatra pāṇḍavānyuddhe tvayā guptānmahārathān ||7||

8śaktastvaṃ hi raṇe jetuṃ sasurāsuramānuṣān
|
trīn lokānsamare yuktānkiṃ punaḥ kauravaṃ balam ||8||

9bhagadattaṃ ca rājānaṃ ko'nyaḥ śaktastvayā vinā
|
jetuṃ puruṣaśārdūla yo'pi syādvāsavopamaḥ ||9||

10tathemāṃ vipulāṃ senāṃ guptāṃ pārtha tvayā'nagha
|
na śekuḥ pārthivāḥ sarve cakṣurbhirapi vīkṣitum ||10||

11tathaiva satataṃ pārtha rakṣitābhyāṃ tvayā raṇe
|
dhṛṣṭadyumnaśikhaṇḍibhyāṃ bhīṣmadroṇau nipātitau ||11||

12ko hi śakto raṇe pārtha bhāratānāṃ mahārathau
|
bhīṣmadroṇau yudhā jetuṃ śakratulyaparākramau ||12||

13ko hi śāntanavaṃ bhīṣmaṃ droṇaṃ vaikartanaṃ kṛpam
|
drauṇiṃ ca saumadattiṃ ca kṛtavarmāṇameva ca ||13||

14saindhavaṃ madrarājānaṃ rājānaṃ ca suyodhanam
|
vīrān kṛtāstrān samare sarvānevānivartinaḥ ||14||

15akṣauhiṇīpatīnugrānsaṃhatān yuddhadurmadān
|
tvāmṛte puruṣavyāghra jetuṃ śaktaḥ pumāniha ||15||

16śreṇyaśca bahulāḥ kṣīṇāḥ pradīrṇāśvarathadvipāḥ
|
nānājanapadāścogrāḥ kṣatriyāṇāmamarṣiṇām ||16||

17govāsadāsamīyānāṃ vasātīnāṃ ca bhārata
|
prācyānāṃ vāṭadhānānāṃ bhojānāṃ cābhimāninām ||17||

18udīrṇāśvagajā senā sarvakṣatrasya bhārata
|
tvāṃ samāsādya nidhanaṃ gatā bhīmaṃ ca bhārata ||18||

19ugrāśca bhīmakarmāṇastuṣārā yavanāḥ khaśāḥ
|
dārvābhisārā daradāḥ śakā māṭharataṅgaṇāḥ ||19||

20āndhrakāśca puliṃdāśca kirātāścogravikramāḥ
|
mlecchāśca parvatīyāśca sāgarānūpavāsinaḥ ||20||

21saṃraṃbhiṇo yuddhaśauṇḍā balino daṇḍapāṇayaḥ
|
ete suyodhanasyārthe saṃrabdhāḥ kurubhiḥ saha ||21||

22na śakyā yudhi nirjetuṃ tvadanyena parantapa
|
dhārtarāṣṭramudagraṃ hi vyūḍhaṃ dṛṣṭvā mahadbalam ||22||

23yadi tvaṃ na bhavestrātā pratīyātko nu mānavaḥ
|
tatsāgaramivoddhūtaṃ rajasā saṃvṛtaṃ balam ||23||

24vidārya pāṇḍavaiḥ kruddhaistvayā guptairhataṃ vibho
|
māgadhānāmadhipatirjayatseno mahābalaḥ ||24||

25adya saptaiva cāhāni hataḥ saṃkhye'bhimanyunā
|
tato daśasahasrāṇi gajānāṃ bhīmakarmaṇām ||25||

26jaghāna gadayā bhīmastasya rājñaḥ paricchadam
|
tato'nye'bhihatā nāgā rathāśca śataśo balāt ||26||

27tadevaṃ samare pārtha vartamāne mahābhaye
|
bhīmasenaṃ samāsādya tvāṃ ca pāṇḍava kauravāḥ ||27||

28savājirathamātaṅgā mṛtyulokamito gatāḥ
|
tathā senāmukhe tatra nihate pārtha pāṇḍavaiḥ ||28||

29bhīṣmaḥ prāsṛjadugrāṇi śarajālāni māriṣa
|
sa cedikāśipañcālān karūṣān matsyakekayān ||29||

30śaraiḥ pracchādya nidhanamanayatparamāstravit
|
tasya cāpacyutairbāṇaiḥ paradehavidāraṇaiḥ ||30||

31pūrṇamākāśamabhavadrukmapuṃkhairajihmagaiḥ
|
hanyādrathasahastrāṇi ekaikenaiva muṣṭinā ||31||

32lakṣaṃ naradvipānhatvā sametānsamahābalān
|
gatyā daśamyā te gatvā jaghnurvājirathadvipān ||32||

33hitvā navagatīrduṣṭāḥ sa bāṇānāhave'tyajat
|
dināni daśa bhīṣmeṇa nighnatā tāvakaṃ balam ||33||

34śūnyāḥ kṛtā rathopasthā hatāśca gajavājinaḥ
|
darśayittvā''tmano rūpaṃ rudropendrasamaṃ yudhi ||34||

35pāṇḍavānāmanīkāni pragṛhyāsau vyaśātayat
|
vinighnanpṛthivīpālāṃścedipañcālakekayān ||35||

36adahatpāṇḍavīṃ senāṃ rathāśvagajasaṃkulām
|
majjantamaplave mandamujjihīrṣuḥ suyodhanam ||36||

37tathā carantaṃ samare tapantamiva bhāskaram
|
padātikoṭisāhastrāḥ pravarāyudhapāṇayaḥ ||37||

38na śekuḥ sṛñjayā draṣṭuṃ tathaivānye mahīkṣitaḥ
|
vicarantaṃ tathā taṃ tu saṃgrāme jitakāśinam ||38||

39sarvodyamena mahatā pāṇḍavānsamabhidravat
|
sa tu vidrāvya samare pāṇḍavānsṛñjayānapi ||39||

40eka eva raṇe bhīṣma ekavīratvamāgataḥ
|
taṃ śikhaṇḍī samāsādya tvayā gupto mahāvratam ||40||

41jaghāna puruṣavyāghraṃ śaraiḥ sannataparvabhiḥ
|
sa eṣa patitaḥ śete śaratalpe pitāmahaḥ ||41||

42tvāṃ prāpya puruṣavyāghraṃ vṛtraḥ prāpyeva vāsavam
|
droṇaḥ pañcadinānyugro vidhamya ripuvāhinīm ||42||

43kṛtvā vyūhamabhedyaṃ ca pātayitvā mahārathān
|
jayadrathasya samare kṛtvā rakṣāṃ mahārathaḥ ||43||

44antakapratimaścogro rātriyuddhe'dahatprajāḥ
|
dagdhvā yodhāñcharairvīro bhāradvājaḥ pratāpavān ||44||

45dhṛṣṭadyumnaṃ samāsādya sa gataḥ paramāṃ gatim
|
yadi vā'dya bhavānyuddhe sūtaputramukhānrathān ||45||

46nāvārayiṣyaḥ saṃgrāme na sma droṇo vyanaṅkṣyata
|
bhavatā tu balaṃ sarvaṃ dhārtarāṣṭrasya vāritam ||46||

47tato droṇo hato yuddhe pārṣatena dhanañjayaḥ
|
evaṃ vā ko raṇe kuryāttvadanyaḥ kṣatriyo yudhi ||47||

48yādṛśaṃ te kṛtaṃ pārtha jayadrathavadhaṃ prati
|
nivārya senāṃ mahatīṃ hatvā śūrāṃśca pārthivān ||48||

49nihataḥ saindhavo rājā tvayā'strabalatejasā
|
āścaryaṃ sindhurājasya vadhaṃ jānanti pārthivāḥ ||49||

50anāścaryaṃ hi tattvattastvaṃ hi pārtha mahārathaḥ
|
tvāṃ hi prāpya raṇe kṣatramekāhāditi bhārata ||50||

51naśyamānamahaṃ yuktaṃ manyeyamiti me matiḥ
|
seyaṃ pārthacamūrghorā dhārtarāṣṭrasya saṃyuge ||51||

52hatasarvasvavīrā hi bhīṣmadroṇau yadā hatau
|
śīrṇapravarayodhā'dya hatavājirathadvipā ||52||

53hīnā sūryendunakṣatrairdyorivābhāti bhāratī
|
vidhvastā hi raṇe pārtha seneyaṃ bhīmavikrama ||53||

54āsurīva purā senā śakrasyeva parākramaiḥ
|
teṣāṃ hatāvaśiṣṭāstu santi pañca mahārathāḥ ||54||

55aśvatthāmā kṛtavarmā karṇo madrādhipaḥ kṛpaḥ
|
tāṃstvamadya naravyāghra hatvā pañca mahārathān ||55||

56hatāmitraḥ prayacchorvī rājñe sadvīpapattanām
|
sākāśajalapātālāṃ saparvatamahāvanām ||56||

57prāpnotvamitavīryaśrīradya pārtho vasuṃdharām
|
etāṃ purā viṣṇuriva hatvā daiteyadānavān ||57||

58prayaccha medinīṃ rājñe śakrāyaiva hariryathā
|
adya modantu pañcālā nihateṣvariṣu tvayā
|
viṣṇunā nihateṣveva dānaveyeṣu devatāḥ ||58||

59yadi vā dvipadāṃ śreṣṭhaṃ droṇaṃ mānayato gurum
|
aśvatthāmni kṛpā te'sti kṛpe vācāryagauravāt ||59||

60atyantāpacitānbandhūnmānayanmātṛbāndhavān
|
kṛtavarmāṇamāsādya na neṣyasi yamakṣayam ||60||

61bhrātaraṃ māturāsādya śalyaṃ madrajanādhipam
|
yadi tvamaravindākṣa dayāvānna jighāṃsasi ||61||

62imaṃ pāpamatiṃ kṣudramatyantaṃ pāṇḍavānprati
|
karṇamadya naraśreṣṭha jahyāḥ suniśitaiḥ śaraiḥ ||62||

63etatte sukṛtaṃ karma nātra kiṃcana yujyate
|
vayamapyanujānīmo nātra doṣo'sti kaścana ||63||

64dahane yatsaputrāyā niśi mātustavānagha
|
dyūtārthe yacca yuṣmāsu prāvartata suyodhanaḥ ||64||

65tasya sarvasya duṣṭātmā karṇo vai mūlamityuta
|
karṇāddhi manyate trāṇaṃ nityameva suyodhanaḥ ||65||

66tato māmapi saṃrabdho nigrahītuṃ pracakrame
|
sthirā buddhirnarendrasya dhārtarāṣṭrasya mānada ||66||

67karṇaḥ pārthān raṇe sarvānvijeṣyati na saṃśayaḥ
|
karṇamāśritya kaunteya dhārtarāṣṭreṇa vigrahaḥ ||67||

68rocito bhavatā sārdhaṃ jānatā'pi balaṃ tava
|
karṇo hi bhāṣate nityamahaṃ pārthānsamāgatān ||68||

69vāsudevaṃ ca dāśārhaṃ vijeṣyāmi mahāratham
|
protsāhayandurātmānaṃ dhārtarāṣṭraṃ sudurmatim ||69||

70samitau garjate karṇastamadya jahi bhārata
|
yacca yuṣmāsu pāpaṃ vai dhārtarāṣṭraḥ prayuktavān ||70||

71tatra sarvatra duṣṭātmā karṇaḥ pāpamatirmukham
|
yacca taddhārtarāṣṭrasya krūraiḥ ṣaḍbhirmahārathaiḥ ||71||

72apaśyaṃ nihataṃ vīraṃ saubhadramṛṣabhekṣaṇam
|
droṇadrauṇikṛpānvīrānkarṣayantaṃ nararṣabhān ||72||

73nirmanuṣyāṃśca mātaṃgānvirathāṃśca mahārathān
|
vyaśvārohāṃśca turagānpattīnvyāyudhajīvinaḥ ||73||

74kurvantamṛṣabhaskaṃdhaṃ kuruvṛṣṇiyaśaskaram
|
vidhamantamanīkāni vyathayantaṃ mahārathān ||74||

75manuṣyavājimātaṅgānprāhiṇvantaṃ yamakṣayam
|
śaraiḥ saubhadramāyāntaṃ dahantamiva vāhinīm ||75||

76tanme dahati gātrāṇi sakhe satyena te śape
|
yattatrāpi ca duṣṭātmā karṇo'bhyadruhyata prabho ||76||

77aśaknuvaṃścābhimanyoḥ karṇaḥ sthātuṃ raṇe'grataḥ
|
saubhadraśaranirbhinno visaṃjñaḥ śoṇitokṣitaḥ ||77||

78niḥśvasan krodhasaṃdīpto vimukhaḥ sāyakārditaḥ
|
apayānakṛtotsāho nirāśaścāpi jīvite ||78||

79tasthau suvihvalaḥ saṃkhye prahārajanitaśramaḥ
|
atha droṇasya samare tatkālasadṛśaṃ tadā ||79||

80śrutvā karṇo vacaḥ krūraṃ tataściccheda kārmukam
|
tataśchinnāyudhaṃ tena raṇe pañca mahārathāḥ ||80||

81taṃ caiva nikṛtiprajñāḥ prāharañcharavṛṣṭibhiḥ
|
tasminvinihate vīre sarveṣāṃ duḥkhamāviśat ||81||

82prāhasatsa tu duṣṭātmā karṇaḥ sa ca suyodhanaḥ
|
yacca karṇo'bravītkṛṣṇāṃ sabhāyāṃ paruṣaṃ vacaḥ ||82||

83pramukhe pāṇḍaveyānāṃ kurūṇāṃ ca nṛśaṃsavat
|
vinaṣṭāḥ pāṇḍavāḥ kṛṣṇe śāśvataṃ narakaṃ gatāḥ ||83||

84patimanyaṃ pṛthuśroṇi vṛṇīṣva mṛdubhāṣiṇi
|
eṣā tvaṃ dhṛtarāṣṭrasya dāsībhūtā niveśanam ||84||

85praviśārālapadmākṣi na santi patayastava
|
na pāṇḍavāḥ prabhavanti tava kṛṣṇe kathañcana ||85||

86dāsabhāryā ca pāñcāli svayaṃ dāsī ca śobhane
|
adya duryodhano hyekaḥ pṛthivyāṃ nṛpatiḥ smṛtaḥ ||86||

87sarve cāsya mahīpālā yogakṣemamupāsate
|
paśyedānīṃ yathā bhadre vinaṣṭāḥ pāṇḍavāḥ samam ||87||

88anyonyaṃ samudīkṣante dhārtarāṣṭrasya tejasā
|
vyaktaṃ ṣaṇḍhatilā hyete na pureva nimajjitāḥ ||88||

89preṣyavaccāpi rājānamupasthāsyanti kauravam
|
ityuktavānadharmajñastadā paramadurmatiḥ ||89||

90pāpaḥ pāpavacaḥ karṇaḥ śṛṇvatastava bhārata
|
adya pāpasya tadvākyaṃ suvarṇavikṛtāḥ śarāḥ ||90||

91śamayantu śilādhautāstvayāstā jīvitacchidaḥ
|
yāni cānyāni duṣṭātmā pāpāni kṛtavāṃstvayi ||91||

92tānyadya jīvitaṃ cāsya śamayantu śarāstava
|
gāṇḍīvaprahitānghorānadya gātraiḥspṛśañcharān ||92||

93karṇaḥ smaratu duṣṭātmā vacanaṃ droṇabhīṣmayoḥ
|
suvarṇapuṅkhā nārācāḥ śatrughnā vaidyutaprabhāḥ ||93||

94tvayā'stāstasya varmāṇi bhittvā pāsyanti śoṇitam
|
ugrāstvadbhujanirmuktā marma bhittvā mahāśarāḥ ||94||

95adya karṇaṃ mahāvegāḥ preṣayantu yamakṣayam
|
adya hāhākṛtā dīnā viṣaṇṇāstvaccharārditāḥ ||95||

96prapatantaṃ rathātkarṇaṃ paśyantu vasudhādhipāḥ
|
adya śoṇitasaṃmagnaṃ śayānaṃ patitaṃ bhuvi
|
apaviddhāyudhaṃ karṇaṃ dīnāḥ paśyantu bāndhavāḥ ||96||

97hastikakṣo mahānasya bhallenonmathitastvayā
|
prakampamānaḥ patatu bhūmāvapi rathadhvajaḥ ||97||

98tvayā śaraśataiśchinnaṃ rathaṃ hemavibhūṣitam
|
hatayodhāśvamutsṛjya bhītaḥ śalyaḥ palāyatām ||98||

99tvaṃ cetkarṇasutaṃ pārtha sūtaputrasya paśyataḥ
|
pratijñāvāraṇārthāya nihaniṣyasi sāyakaiḥ ||99||

100hataṃ karṇastu taṃ dṛṣṭvā priyaṃ putraṃ durātmavān
|
smaratāṃ droṇabhīṣmābhyāṃ vacaḥ kṣattuśca mānada ||100||

101tataḥ suyodhano dṛṣṭvā hatamādhirathiṃ tvayā
|
nirāśo jīvite tvadya rājye caiva bhavatvariḥ ||101||

102ete dravanti pañcālā vadhyamānāḥ śitaiḥ śaraiḥ
|
karṇena bharataśreṣṭha pāṇḍavānujjihīrṣavaḥ ||102||

103pañcālān draupadeyāṃśca dhṛṣṭadyumnaśikhaṇḍinau
|
dhṛṣṭadyumnatanūjāṃśca śatānīkaṃ ca nākulim ||103||

104nakulaṃ sahadevaṃ ca durmukhaṃ janamejayam
|
sudharmāṇaṃ sātyakiṃ ca viddhi karṇavaśaṃ gatān ||104||

105abhyāhatānāṃ karṇena pañcālānāmasau raṇe
|
śrūyate ninado ghorastvadbandhūnāṃ parantapa ||105||

106na tveva bhītāḥ pañcālāḥ kathaṃcitsyuḥ parāṅmukhāḥ
|
na hi mṛtyuṃ maheṣvāsā gaṇayanti mahāraṇe ||106||

107ya ekaḥ pāṇḍavīṃ senāṃ śaraughaiḥ samaveṣṭayat
|
taṃ samāsādya pañcālā bhīṣmānnāsanparāṅgukhāḥ ||107||

108te kathaṃ karṇamāsādya vidraveyurmahārathāḥ
|
yastvekaḥ sarvapañcālānahanyahani nāśayan ||108||

109kālavaccarate vīraḥ pañcālānāṃ rathavraje
|
tamapyāsādya samare mitrārthe mitravatsala ||109||

110tathā jvalantamastrāgniṃ guruṃ sarvadhanuṣmatām
|
nirdahantaṃ ca samare durdharṣaṃ droṇamojasā ||110||

111te nityamuditā jetuṃ mṛdhe śatrūnariṃdama
|
na jātvādhiratherbhītāḥ pañcālāḥ syuḥ parāṅmukhāḥ ||111||

112teṣāmāpatatāṃ śūraḥ pañcālānāṃ tarasvinām
|
ādattāsūñśaraiḥ karṇaḥ pataṅgānāmivānalaḥ ||112||

113ete dravanti pañcālā drāvyante yodhibhirdhruvam
|
karṇena bharataśreṣṭha paśya paśya tathākṛtān ||113||

114tāṃstathā'bhimukhānvīrānmitrārthe tyaktajīvitān
|
kṣayaṃ nayati rādheyaḥ pañcālāñchataśo raṇe ||114||

115tadbhārata maheṣvāsānagādhe majjato'plave
|
karṇārṇave plavo bhūtvā pañcālāṃstrātumarhasi ||115||

116astraṃ hi rāmātkarṇena bhārgavādṛṣisattamāt
|
yadupāttaṃ mahāghoraṃ tasya rūpamudīryate ||116||

117tāpanaṃ sarvasainyānāṃ ghorarūpaṃ sudāruṇam
|
samāvṛtya mahāsenāṃ jvalantaṃ svena tejasā ||117||

118ete caranti saṃgrāme karṇacāpacyutāḥ śarāḥ
|
bhramarāṇāmiva vrātāstāpayanti sma tāvakān ||118||

119ete dravanti pañcālā dikṣu sarvāsu bhārata
|
karṇāstraṃ samare prāpya durnivāryamanātmabhiḥ ||119||

120eṣa bhīmo dṛḍhakrodho vṛtaḥ pārtha samantataḥ
|
sṛñjayairyodhayankarṇaṃ pīḍyate niśitaiḥ śaraiḥ ||120||

121pāṇḍavānsṛñjayāṃścaiva pañcālāṃścaiva bhārata
|
hanyādupekṣitaḥ karṇo rogo dehamivāgataḥ ||121||

122nānyaṃ tvatto hi paśyāmi yodhaṃ yaudhiṣṭhire bale
|
yaḥ samāsādya rādheyaṃ svastimānāvrajedgṛham ||122||

123tamadya niśitairbāṇairvinihatya nararṣabha
|
yathāpratijñaṃ pārtha tvaṃ kṛtvā kīrtimavāpnuhi ||123||
