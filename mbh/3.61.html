Mahābhārata 3.61 (Āraṇyakaparvan)

1bṛhadaśva uvāca| tatastu yāte vārṣṇeye puṇyaślokasya dīvyataḥ
|
puṣkareṇa hṛtaṃ rājyaṃ yaccānyadvastu kiṃcana||1||

2hṛtarājyaṃ nalaṃ rājanprahasanpuṣkaro'bravīt
|
dyūtaṃ pravartatāṃ bhūyaḥ pratipāṇo'sti kastava||2||

3śiṣṭā te damayantyekā sarvamanyajjitaṃ mayā
|
damayantyāḥ paṇaḥ sādhu vartatāṃ yadi manyase||3||

4puṣkareṇaivamuktasya puṇyaślokasya manyunā
|
vyadīryateva hṛdayaṃ na cainaṃ kiṃcidabravīt||4||

5tataḥ puṣkaramālokya nalaḥ paramamanyumān
|
utsṛjya sarvagātrebhyo bhūṣaṇāni mahāyaśāḥ||5||

6ekavāsā hyasaṃvītaḥ suhṛcchokavivardhanaḥ
|
niścakrāma tato rājā tyaktvā suvipulāṃ śriyam||6||

7damayantyekavastrātha gacchantaṃ pṛṣṭhatonvagāt
|
sa tayā bāhyataḥ sārdhaṃ trirātraṃ naiṣadhovasat||7||

8puṣkarastu mahārāja ghoṣayāmāsa vai pure
|
nale yaḥ samyagātiṣṭhetsa gacchedvadhyatāṃ mama||8||

9puṣkarasya tu vākyena tasya vidveṣaṇena ca
|
paurā na tasya satkāraṃ kṛtavanto yudhiṣṭhira||9||

10sa tathā nagarābhyāse satkārārho na satkṛtaḥ
|
triratramuṣito rājā jalamātreṇa vartayan||10||

11pīḍyamānaḥ kṣudhā tatra phalamūlāni karṣayan
|
prātiṣṭhata tato rājā damayantī tamanvagāt||11||

12kṣudhayā pīḍyamānastu nalo bahutithehani
|
apaśyacchakunānkāṃściddhiraṇyasadṛśacchadān||12||

13sa cintayāmāsa tadā niṣadhādhipatirbalī
|
asti bhakṣyo mamādyāyaṃ vasu cedaṃ bhaviṣyati||13||

14tatastānparidhānena vāsasā sa samāvṛṇot
|
tasya tadvastramādāya sarve jagmurvihāyasā||14||

15utpatantaḥ khagā vākyametadāhustato nalam
|
dṛṣṭvā digvāsasaṃ bhūmau sthitaṃ dīnamamadhomukham||15||

16vayamakṣāḥ sudurbuddhe tava vāso jihīrṣavaḥ
|
āgatā na hi naḥ prītiḥ savāsasi gate tvayi||16||

17tānsamīpagatānakṣānātmānaṃ ca vivāsasam
|
puṇyaślokastadā rājā damayantīmathābravīt||17||

18yeṣāṃ prakopādaiśvaryātpracyutohamanindite
|
prāṇayātrāṃ na vindeyaṃ duḥkhitaḥ kṣudhayānvitaḥ||18||

19yeṣāṃ kṛte na satkāramakurvanmayi naiṣadhāḥ
|
ime te śakunā bhūtvā vāso bhīru haranti me||19||

20vaiṣamyaṃ paramaṃ prāpto duḥkhito gatacetanaḥ
|
bhartā te'haṃ nibodhedaṃ vacanaṃ hitamātmanaḥ||20||

21ete gacchanti bahavaḥ panthāno dakṣiṇāpatham
|
avantīmṛkṣavantaṃ ca samatikramya parvatam||21||

22eṣa vindhyo mahāśailaḥ payoṣṇī ca samudragā
|
āśramāśca maharṣīṇāṃ bahumūlaphalānvitāḥ||22||

23eṣa panthā vidarbhāṇāmasau gacchati kosalān
|
ataḥparaṃ ca deśo'yaṃ dakṣiṇe dakṣiṇāpathaḥ||23||

24etadvākyaṃ nalo rājā damayantīṃ samāhitaḥ
|
uvācāsakṛdārto hi bhaimīmuddiśya bhārata||24||

25tataḥ sā vāṣpakalayā vācā duḥkhena karṣitā
|
uvāca damayantī taṃ naiṣadhaṃ karuṇaṃ vacaḥ||25||

26udvejate me hṛdayaṃ sīdantyaṅgāni sarvaśaḥ
|
tava pārthiva saṃkalpaṃ cintayantyāḥ punaḥ punaḥ||26||

27hṛtarājyaṃ hṛtadravyaṃ vivastraṃ kṣucchramānvitam
|
kathamutsṛjya gaccheyamahaṃ tvāṃ nirjane vane||27||

28śrāntasya te kṣudhārtasya cintayānasya tatsukham
|
vane ghore mahārāja nāśayiṣyāmyahaṃ klamam||28||

29na ca bhāryāsamaṃ kiṃcidvidyate bhiṣajāṃ matam
|
auṣadhaṃ sarvaduḥkheṣu satyametadbravīmi te||29||

30nala uvāca| evametadyathāttha tvaṃ damayanti sumadhyame
|
nāsti bhāryāsamaṃ mitraṃ narasyārtasya bheṣajam||30||

31na cāhaṃ tyaktukāmastvāṃ kimalaṃ bhīru śaṅkase
|
tyajeyamahamātmānaṃ na caiva tvāmanindite||31||

32damayantyuvāca
|
yadi māṃ tvaṃ mahārāja na vihātumihecchasi
|
tatkimarthaṃ vidarbhāṇāṃ panthāḥ samupadiśyate||32||

33avaimi cāhaṃ nṛpate na tu māṃ tyaktumarhasi
|
cetasā tvapakṛṣṭena māṃ tyajethā mahīpate||33||

34panthānaṃ hi mamābhīkṣṇamākhyāsi ca narottama
|
ato nimittaṃ śokaṃ me vardhayasyamaropama||34||

35yadi cāyamabhiprāyastava jñātīnvrajediti
|
sahitāveva gacchāvo vidarbhānyadi manyase||35||

36vidarbharājastatra tvāṃ pūjayiṣyati mānada
|
tena tvaṃ pūjito rājansukhaṃ vatsyasi no gṛhe||36||
