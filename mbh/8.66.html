Mahābhārata 8.66 (Karṇaparvan)

1yudhiṣṭhira uvāca|
svāgataṃ devakīmātaḥ svāgataṃ te dhanañjaya
|
priyaṃ me darśanaṃ gāḍhaṃ yuvayoracyutārjunau ||1||

2akṣatābhyāmariṣṭābhyāṃ hataḥ karṇo mahārathaḥ
|
āśīviṣasamaṃ yuddhe sarvaśastraviśāradam ||2||

3agragaṃ dhārtarāṣṭrāṇāṃ sarveṣāṃ śarma varma ca
|
rakṣitaṃ vṛṣasenena suṣeṇena ca dhanvinā ||3||

4anujñātaṃ mahāvīryaṃ rāmeṇāstra sudurjayam
|
agryaṃ sarvasya lokasya rathinaṃ lokaviśrutam ||4||

5trātāraṃ dhārtarāṣṭrāṇāṃ gantāraṃ vāhinīmukhe
|
hantāraṃ parasainyānāmamitragaṇamardanam ||5||

6duryodhanahite yuktamasmadduḥkhāya codyatam
|
apradhṛṣyaṃ mahāyuddhe devairapi savāsavaiḥ ||6||

7analānilayostulyaṃ tejasā ca balena ca
|
pātālamiva gaṃbhīraṃ suhṛdāṃ nandivardhanam ||7||

8antakaṃ mama mitrāṇāṃ hatvā karṇaṃ mahāmṛdhe
|
diṣṭyā yuvāmanuprāptau jittvā'suramivāmarau ||8||

9ghoraṃ yuddhamadīnena mayā hyadyācyutārjunau
|
kṛtaṃ tenāntakeneva prajāḥ sarvā jighāṃsatā ||9||

10tena ketuśca me cchinno hatau ca pārṣṇisārathī
|
hatavāhastataścāsmi yuyudhānasya paśyataḥ ||10||

11dhṛṣṭadyumnasya yamayorvīrasya ca śikhaṇḍinaḥ
|
paśyatāṃ draupadeyānāṃ pañcālānāṃ ca sarvaśaḥ ||11||

12etāñjitvā mahāvīryaḥ karṇaḥ śatrugaṇān bahun
|
jitavānmāṃ mahābāho yatamāno mahāraṇe ||12||

13abhisṛtya ca māṃ yuddhe paruṣāṇyuktavānbahu
|
tatra tatra yudhāṃ śreṣṭha paribhūya na saṃśayaḥ ||13||

14bhīmasenaprabhāvāttu yajjīvāmi dhanañjaya
|
bahunā'tra kimuktena nāhaṃ tat soḍhumutsahe ||14||

15trayodaśāhaṃ varṣāṇi yasmādbhīto dhanañjaya
|
na sma nidrāṃ labhe rātrau na cāhani sukhaṃ kvacit ||15||

16tasya dveṣeṇa saṃyuktaḥ paridahye dhanañjaya
|
ātmano maraṇe yāto vādhrīṇasa iva dvipaḥ ||16||

17tasyāyamagamatkālaścintayānasya me ciram
|
kathaṃ karṇo mayā śakyo yuddhe kṣapayituṃ bhavet ||17||

18jāgratsvapaṃśca kaunteya karṇameva sadā hyaham
|
paśyāmi tatra tatraiva karṇabhūtamidaṃ jagat ||18||

19yatra yatra hi gacchāmi karṇādbhīto dhanañjaya
|
tatra tatra hi paśyāmi karṇamevāgrataḥ sthitam ||19||

20so'haṃ tenaiva vīreṇa samareṣvapalāyinā
|
sahayaḥ sarathaḥ pārtha jittvā jīvan visarjitaḥ ||20||

21ko nu me jīvitenārtho rājyenārtho bhavetpunaḥ
|
mamaivaṃ vikṣatasyādya karṇenāhavaśobhinā ||21||

22na prāptapūrvaṃ yadbhīṣmātkṛpāddroṇācca saṃyuge
|
tat prāptamadya me yuddhe sūtaputrānmahārathāt ||22||

23sa tvāṃ pṛcchāmi kaunteya yathā'dya kuśalaṃ tathā
|
tanmamācakṣva kārtsyena yathā karṇo hatastvayā ||23||

24śakratulyabalo yuddhe yamatulyaḥ parākrame
|
rāmatulyastathā'streṇa sa kathaṃ vai niṣūditaḥ ||24||

25mahārathaḥ samākhyātaḥ sarvayuddhaviśāradaḥ
|
dhanurdharāṇāṃ pravaraḥ sarveṣāmekapūruṣaḥ ||25||

26pūjito dhṛtarāṣṭreṇa saputreṇa viśāṃpate
|
tvadarthameva rādheyaḥ sa kathaṃ nihatastvayā ||26||

27dhārtarāṣṭro hi yodheṣu sarveṣveva sadā'rjuna
|
tava mṛtyuṃ raṇe karṇaṃ manyate puruṣarṣabha ||27||

28sa tvayā puruṣavyāghra kathaṃ yuddhe niṣūditaḥ
|
tanmamācakṣva kaunteya yathā karṇo hatastvayā ||28||

29yudhyamānasya ca śiraḥ paśyatāṃ suhṛdāṃ hṛtam
|
tvayā puruṣaśārdūla siṃheneva yathā ruroḥ ||29||

30yaḥ paryupāsīt pradiśo diśaśca tvāṃ sūtaputraḥ samare parīpsan
|
ditsuḥ karṇaḥ samare hastiṣaṅgavaṃ sa hīdānīṃ kaṅkapatraiḥ sutīkṣṇaiḥ ||30||

31tvayā raṇe nihataḥ sūtaputraḥ kaccicchete bhūmitale durātmā
|
priyaśca me paramo vai kṛto'yaṃ tvayā raṇe sūtaputraṃ nihatya ||31||

32yaḥ sarvataḥ paryapatattvadarthe sadārcito garvitaḥ sūtaputraḥ
|
sa śūramānī samare sametya kaccittvayā nihataḥ saṃyuge'sau ||32||

33raukmaṃ varaṃ hastigajāśvayuktaṃ rathaṃ praditsuryaḥ parebhyastvadarthe
|
sadā raṇe spardhate yaḥ sa pāpaḥ kaccittvayā nihatastāta yuddhe ||33||

34yo'sau sadā śūramadena matto vikatthate saṃsadi kauravāṇām
|
priyo'tyarthaṃ tasya suyodhanasya kaccitsa pāpo nihatastvayā'dya ||34||

35kaccitsamāgamya dhanuḥprayuktaistvatpreṣitairlohitāṅgairvihaṅgaiḥ
|
śete sa pāpaḥ suvibhinnagātraḥ kaccidbhagnau dhārtarāṣṭrasya bāhū ||35||

36yo'sau sadā ślāghate rājamadhye duryodhanaṃ harṣayandarpapūrṇaḥ
|
ahaṃ hantā phālgunasyeti mohātkaccidvacastasya na vai tathā tat ||36||

37nāhaṃ pādau dhāvayiṣye kadācidyāvatsthitaḥ pārtha ityalpabuddheḥ
|
vrataṃ tasyaitat sarvadā śakrasūno kaccittvayā nihataḥ so'dya karṇaḥ ||37||

38yo'sau kṛṣṇāmabravīdduṣṭabuddhiḥ karṇaḥ sabhāyāṃ kuruvīramadhye
|
kiṃ pāṇḍavāṃstvaṃ na jahāsi kṛṣṇe sudurbalānpatitānhīnasattvān ||38||

39yo'sau karṇaḥ pratyajānāttvadarthe nāhaṃ hatvā saha kṛṣṇena pārtham
|
ihopayāteti sa pāpabuddhiḥ kaccicchete śarasaṃbhinnagātraḥ ||39||

40kaccitsaṃgrāmo vidito vai tavāyaṃ samāgame sṛñjayakauravāṇām
|
yatrāvasthāmīdṛśīṃ prāpito'haṃ kaccittvayā so'dya hato durātmā ||40||

41kaccittvayā tasya sumandabuddhergāṇḍīvamuktairviśikhairjvaladbhiḥ
|
sakuṇḍalaṃ bhānumaduttamāṅgaṃ kāyātprakṛttaṃ yudhi savyasācin ||41||

42yattanmayā bāṇasamarpitena dhyāto'si karṇasya vadhāya vīra
|
tanme tvayā kaccidamoghamadya dhyānaṃ kṛtaṃ karṇanipātanena ||42||

43yaddarpapūrṇaḥ sa suyodhano'smānudīkṣate karṇasamāśrayeṇa
|
kaccittvayā so'dya samāśrayo'sya bhagnaḥ parākramya suyodhanasya ||43||

44yo naḥ purā ṣaṇḍhatilānavocatsabhāmadhye kauravāṇāṃ samakṣam
|
sa durmatiḥ kaccidupetya saṃkhye tvayā hataḥ sūtaputro hyamarṣī ||44||

45yaḥ sūtaputraḥ prahasan durātmā purā'bravīnnirjitāṃ saubalena
|
svayaṃ prasahyānaya yājñasenīmapīha kaccitsa hatastvayā'dya ||45||

46yaḥ śastrabhṛcchreṣṭhatamaḥ pṛthivyāṃ pitāmahaṃ vyākṣipadalpacetāḥ
|
saṃkhyāyamāno'rdharathaḥ sa kaccittvayā hato'dyādhirathirmahātman ||46||

47amarṣajaṃ nikṛtisamīraṇeritaṃ hṛdi sthitaṃ jvalanamimaṃ sadā mama
|
hato mayā so'dya sametya karṇa iti bruvan praśamayase'dya phālguna ||47||

48bravīhi me durlabhametadadya kathaṃ tvayā nihataḥ sūtaputraḥ
|
anudhyāye tvāṃ satataṃ pravīra vṛtre hate'sau bhagavānivendraḥ ||48||
