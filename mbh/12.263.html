Mahābhārata 12.263 (Śāntiparvan)

1jājaliruvāca |
ayaṃ pravartito dharmastulāṃ dhārayatā tvayā
|
svargadvāraṃ ca vṛttiṃ ca bhūtānāmavarotsyate ||1||

2kṛṣyā hyannaṃ prabhavati tatastvamapi jīvasi
|
paśubhiścauṣadhībhiśca mā jīvanti vāṇija ||2||

3tato yajñaḥ prabhavati nāstikyamapi jalpasi
|
na hi vartedayaṃ loko vārtāmutsṛjya kevalām ||3||

4tulādhāra uvāca |
vakṣyāmi jājale vṛttiṃ nāsmi brāhmaṇa nāstikaḥ
|
na yajñaṃ ca vinindāmi yajñavittu sudurlabhaḥ ||4||

5namo brāhmaṇayajñāya ye ca yajñavido janāḥ
|
svayajñaṃ brāhmaṇā hitvā kṣatrayajñamihāsthitāḥ ||5||

6lubdhairvittaparairbrahmannāstikaiḥ sampravartitam
|
vedavādānavijñāya satyābhāsamivānṛtam ||6||

7idaṃ deyamidaṃ deyamiti cāyaṃ praśasyate
|
ataḥ stainyaṃ prabhavati vikarmāṇi ca jājale ||7||

8yadeva sukṛtaṃ havyaṃ tena tuṣyanti devatāḥ
|
namaskāreṇa haviṣā svādhyāyairauṣadhaistathā ||8||

9pūjā syāddevatānāṃ hi yathā śāstranidarśanam
|
iṣṭāpūrtādasādhūnāṃ viguṇā jāyate prajā ||9||

10lubdhebhyo jāyate lubdhaḥ samebhyo jāyate samaḥ
|
yajamānā yathā''tmānamṛtvijaśca tathā prajāḥ
|
yajñātprajā prabhavati nabhasoṃ'mbha ivāmalam ||10||

11agnau prāstāhutirbrahmannādityamupagacchati
|
ādityājjāyate vṛṣṭirvṛṣṭerannaṃ tataḥ prajāḥ ||11||

12tasmātsuniṣṭhitāḥ pūrve sarvānkāmāṃśca lebhire
|
akṛṣṭapacyā pṛthivī āśīrbhirvīrudho'bhavan ||12||

13na te yajñeṣvātmasu vā phalaṃ paśyanti kiṃcana
|
śaṅkamānāḥ phalaṃ yajñe ye yajerankathaṃcana ||13||

14jāyante sādhavo dhūrtā lubdhā vittaprayojanāḥ
|
sa sma pāpakṛtāṃ lokāngacchedaśubhakarmaṇā ||14||

15pramāṇamapramāṇena yaḥ kuryādaśubhaṃ naraḥ
|
pāpātmā so'kṛtaprajñaḥ sadaiveha dvijottama ||15||

16kartavyamiti kartavyaṃ vetti vai brāhmaṇo bhayam
|
brahmaiva vartate loke naiva kartavyatāṃ punaḥ ||16||

17viguṇaṃ ca punaḥ karma jyāya ityanuśuśruma
|
sarvabhūtopaghātaśca phalabhāve ca saṃyamaḥ ||17||

18satyayajñā damayajñā arthalubdhārthatṛptayaḥ
|
utpannatyāginaḥ sarve janā āsannamatsarāḥ ||18||

19kṣetrakṣetrajñatattvajñāḥ svayajñapariniṣṭhitāḥ
|
brāhmaṃ vedamadhīyantastoṣayantyaparānapi ||19||

20akhilaṃ daivataṃ sarvaṃ brahma brahmaṇi saṃśritam
|
tuṣyanti tṛpyato devāstṛptātṛptasya jājale ||20||

21yathā sarvarasaistṛpto nābhinandati kiṃcana
|
tathā prajñānatṛptasya nityatṛptiḥ sukhodayā ||21||

22dharmādhārā dharmasukhāḥ kṛtsnavyavasitāstathā
|
āsti nastattvato bhūya iti prājñastvavekṣate ||22||

23jñānavijñāninaḥ kecitparaṃ pāraṃ titīrṣavaḥ
|
atīva puṇyadaṃ puṇyaṃ puṇyābhijanasaṃhitam ||23||

24yatra gatvā na śocanti na ca cyavanti vyathanti ca
|
te tu tadbrahmaṇaḥ sthānaṃ prāpnuvantīha sāttvikāḥ ||24||

25naiva te svargamicchanti na yajanti yaśodhanaiḥ
|
satāṃ vartmānuvartante yajante cāvihiṃsayā ||25||

26vanaspatīnoṣadhīyaśca phalaṃ mūlaṃ ca te viduḥ
|
na caitānṛtvijo lubdhā yājayanti phalārthinaḥ ||26||

27svameva cārthaṃ kurvāṇā yajñaṃ cakruḥ punardvijāḥ
|
pariniṣṭhatakarmāṇaḥ prajānugrahakāmyayā ||27||

28tasmāttānṛtvijo lubdhā yājayantyaśubhānnarān
|
prāpayeyuḥ prajāḥ svarge svadharmācaraṇena vai
|
iti me vartate buddhiḥ samā sarvatra jājale ||28||

29yāni yajñeṣvihejyanti sadā prājñā dvijarṣabhāḥ
|
tena te devayānena pathā yānti mahāmune ||29||

30āvṛttistasya caikasya nāstyāvṛttirmanīṣiṇaḥ
|
ubhau tau devayānena gacchato jājale yathā ||30||

31savyaṃ caiṣāmanaḍuho yujyanti ca vahanti ca
|
svayamusrāśca duhyante manaḥsaṃkalpasiddhibhiḥ ||31||

32svayaṃ yūpānupādāya yajante svāptadakṣiṇaiḥ
|
yastathā bhāvitātmā syātsa gāmālabdhumarhati ||32||

33oṣadhībhistathā brahmanyajeraṃstena tādṛśāḥ
|
iti tyāgaṃ puraskṛtya tādṛśaṃ prabravīmi te ||33||

34nirāśiṣamanārambhaṃ nirnamaskāramastutim
|
akṣīṇaṃ kṣīṇakarmāṇaṃ taṃ devā brāhmaṇaṃ viduḥ ||34||

35na śrāvayanna ca yajanna dadadbrāhmaṇeṣu ca
|
kāmyāṃ vṛttiṃ lipsamānaḥ kāṃ gatiṃ yāti jājale
|
idaṃ tu daivataṃ kṛtvā yathā yajñamavāpnuyāt ||35||

36jājaliruvāca |
na vai munīnāṃ śṛṇumaḥ sma tattvaṃ pṛcchāmi te vāṇija kaṣṭametat
|
pūrve pūrve cāsya nāvekṣamāṇā nātaḥ paraṃ tamṛṣayaḥ sthāpayanti ||36||

37yasminnevātmatīrthena paśavaḥ prāpnuyurmakham
|
atha sma karmaṇā kena vāṇija prāpnuyātsukham
|
śaṃsa te tanmahāprājña bhṛśaṃ vai śraddadhāmi te ||37||

38tulādhāra uvāca |
uta yajñā utāyajñā makhaṃ nārhanti te kvacit
|
ājyena payasā dadhnā pūrṇāhutyā viśeṣataḥ
|
bālaiḥ śṛṅgeṇa pādena sambharatyeva gaurmakham ||38||

39patnī cānena vidhinā prakaroti niyojayan
|
iṣṭaṃ tu daivataṃ kṛtvā yathā yajñamavāpnuyāt ||39||

40puroḍāśo hi sarveṣāṃ paśūnāṃ madhya ucyate
|
sarvā nadyaḥ sarasvatyaḥ sarve puṇyāḥ śiloccayāḥ ||40||

41jājale tīrthamātmaiva mā sma deśātithirbhava
|
etānīdṛśakāndharmānācaranniha jājale
|
kāraṇairdharmamanvicchansa lokānāpnute śubhān ||41||

42bhīṣma uvāca|
etānīdṛśakān dharmāṃstulādhāraḥ praśaṃsati
|
upapattyābhisampannānnityaṃ sadbhirniṣevitān ||42||
