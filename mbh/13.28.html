Mahābhārata 13.28 (Anuśāsanaparvan)

1bhīṣma uvāca|
evamukto mataṅgastu saṃśitātmā yatavrataḥ
|
atiṣṭhadekapādena varṣāṇāṃ śatamacyutaḥ ||1||

2tamuvāca tataḥ śakraḥ punareva mahāyaśāḥ
|
brāhmaṇyaṃ durlabhaṃ tāta prārthayāno na lapsyase ||2||

3mataṅga paramaṃ sthānaṃ prārthayanvinaśiṣyasi
|
mā kṛthāḥ sāhasaṃ putra naiṣa dharmapathastava ||3||

4na hi śakyaṃ tvayā prāptuṃ brāhmaṇyamiha durmate
|
aprāpyaṃ prārthayāno hi na cirādvinaśiṣyasi ||4||

5mataṅga paramaṃ sthānaṃ vāryamāṇo'sakṛnmayā
|
cikīrṣasyeva tapasā sarvathā na bhaviṣyasi ||5||

6tiryagyonigataḥ sarvo mānuṣyaṃ yadi gacchati
|
sa jāyate pulkaso vā caṇḍālo vā'pyasaṃśayaḥ ||6||

7pulkasaḥ pāpayonirvā yaḥ kaścidiha lakṣyate
|
sa tasyāmeva suciraṃ mataṅga parivartate ||7||

8tato daśaśate kāle labhate śūdratāmapi
|
śūdrayonāvapi tato bahuśaḥ parivartate ||8||

9tatastriṃśadguṇe kāle labhate vaiśyatāmapi
|
vaiśyatāyāṃ ciraṃ kālaṃ tatraiva parivartate ||9||

10tataḥ ṣaṣṭiguṇe kāle rājanyo nāma jāyate
|
tataḥ ṣaṣṭiguṇe kāle labhate brahmabandhutām ||10||

11brahmabandhuściraṃ kālaṃ tatastu parivartate
|
tatastu dviśate kāle labhate kāṇḍapṛṣṭhatām ||11||

12kāṇḍapṛṣṭhiściraṃ kālaṃ tatraiva parivartate
|
tatastu triśate kāle labhate japatāmapi ||12||

13taṃ ca prāpya ciraṃ kālaṃ tatraiva parivartate
|
tataścatuḥśate kāle śrotriyo nāma jāyate
|
śrotriyatve ciraṃ kālaṃ tatraiva parivartate ||13||

14tadevaṃ śokaharṣau tu kāmadveṣau ca putraka
|
atimānātivādau ca praviśete dvijādhamam ||14||

15tāṃścejjayati śatrūnsa tadā prāpnoti sadgatim
|
atha te vai jayantyenaṃ tālāgrādiva pātyate ||15||

16mataṅga saṃpradhāryaivaṃ yadahaṃ tvāmacūcudam
|
vṛṇīṣva kāmamanyaṃ tvaṃ brāhmaṇyaṃ hi sudurlabham ||16||
