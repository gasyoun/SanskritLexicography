Mahābhārata 6.96 (Bhīṣmaparvan)

1sañjaya uvāca|
putraṃ vinihataṃ śrutvā irāvantaṃ dhanañjayaḥ
|
duḥkhena mahatā''viṣṭo niḥśvasanpannago yathā ||1||

2abravītsamare rājanvāsudevamidaṃ vacaḥ
|
idaṃ nūnaṃ mahāprājño viduro dṛṣṭavān purā ||2||

3kurūṇāṃ pāṇḍavānāṃ ca kṣayaṃ ghoraṃ mahāmatiḥ
|
sa tato nivāritavāndhṛtarāṣṭraṃ janeśvaram ||3||

4anye ca bahavo vīrāḥ saṃgrāme madhusūdana
|
nihatāḥ kauravaiḥ saṃkhye tathā'smābhiśca kauravāḥ ||4||

5arthahetornaraśreṣṭho kriyate karma kutsitam
|
dhigarthānyatkṛte hyevaṃ kriyate jñātisaṅkṣayaḥ ||5||

6adhanasya mṛtaṃ śreyo na ca jñātivadhāddhanam
|
kiṃ nu prāpsyāmahe kṛṣṇa hatvā jñātīn samāgatān ||6||

7duryodhanāparādhena śakuneḥ saubalasya ca
|
kṣatriyā nidhanaṃ yānti karṇadurmantritena ca ||7||

8idānīṃ ca vijānāmi sukṛtaṃ madhusūdana
|
kṛtaṃ rājñā mahābāho yācatā ca suyodhanam ||8||

9rājyārdhaṃ pañca vā grāmān nākārṣīt sa ca durmatiḥ
|
dṛṣṭvā hi kṣatriyān śūrān śayānān dharaṇītale ||9||

10nindāmi bhṛśamātmānaṃ dhigastu kṣatrajīvikām
|
aśaktamiti māmete jñāsyante kṣatriyā raṇe ||10||

11yuddhaṃ tu me na rucitaṃ jñātibhirmadhusūdana
|
sañcodaya hayān śīghraṃ dhārtarāṣṭracamūṃ prati ||11||

12pratariṣye mahāpāraṃ bhujābhyāṃ samarodadhim
|
nāyaṃ yāpayituṃ kālo vidyate mādhava kvacit ||12||

13evamuktastu pārthena keśavaḥ paravīrahā
|
codayāmāsa tānaśvānpāṇḍurān vātaraṃhasaḥ ||13||

14atha śabdo mahānāsīttava sainyasya bhārata
|
mārutoddhūtavegasya sāgarasyeva parvaṇi ||14||

15aparāhṇe mahārāja saṃgrāmaḥ samapadyata
|
parjanyasamanirghoṣo bhīṣmasya saha pāṇḍavaiḥ ||15||

16tato rājaṃstava sutā bhīmasenamupādravan
|
parivārya raṇe droṇaṃ vasavo vāsavaṃ yathā ||16||

17tataḥ śāntanavo bhīṣmaḥ kṛpaśca rathināṃ varaḥ
|
bhagadattaḥ suśarmā ca dhanañjayamupādravan ||17||

18hārdikyo bāhlikaścaiva sātyakiṃ samabhidrutau
|
ambaṣṭhakastu nṛpatirabhimanyumavasthitaḥ ||18||

19śeṣāstvanye mahārāja śeṣāneva mahārathān
|
tataḥ pravavṛte yuddhaṃ ghorarūpaṃ bhayāvaham ||19||

20bhīmasenastu saṃprekṣya putrāṃstava janeśvara
|
prajajvāla raṇe kruddho haviṣā havyavāḍiva ||20||

21putrāstu tava kaunteyaṃ chādayāñcakrire śaraiḥ
|
prāvṛṣīva mahārāja jaladā iva parvatam ||21||

22sa cchādyamāno bahudhā putraistava viśāṃpate
|
sṛkkiṇī saṃlihanvīraḥ śārdūla iva darpitaḥ ||22||

23vyūḍhoraskaṃ tato bhīmaḥ pātayāmāsa bhārata
|
kṣurapreṇa sutīkṣṇena so'bhavadgatajīvitaḥ ||23||

24apareṇa tu bhallena pītena niśitena tu
|
apātayatkuṇḍalinaṃ siṃhaḥ kṣudramṛgaṃ yathā ||24||

25tataḥ suniśitānpītānsamādatta śilīmukhān
|
sasarja tvarayā yuktaḥ putrāṃste prāpya māriṣa ||25||

26preṣitā bhīmasenena śarāste dṛḍhadhanvanā
|
apātayanta putrāṃste rathebhyaḥ sumahārathān ||26||

27anādhṛṣṭiṃ kuṇḍabhediṃ vairāṭaṃ dīrghalocanam
|
dīrghabāhuṃ subāhuṃ ca tathaiva kanakadhvajam ||27||

28prapatanta sma vīrāste virejurbharatarṣabha
|
vasante puṣpaśabalāścūtāḥ prapatitā iva ||28||

29tataḥ pradudruvuḥ śeṣāstava putrā mahāhave
|
taṃ kālamiva manyanto bhīmasenaṃ mahābalam ||29||

30droṇastu samare vīraṃ nirdahantaṃ sutāṃstava
|
yathādriṃ vāridhārābhiḥ samaṃtādvyakiraccharaiḥ ||30||

31tatrādbhutamapaśyāma kuntīputrasya pauruṣam
|
droṇena vāryamāṇo'pi nijaghne yatsutāṃstava ||31||

32yathā govṛṣabho varṣaṃ sandhārayati svāt patat
|
bhīmastathā droṇamuktaṃ śaravarṣamadīdharat ||32||

33adbhutaṃ ca mahārāja tatra cakre vṛkodaraḥ
|
yatputrāṃste'vadhītsaṃkhye droṇaṃ caiva nyavārayat ||33||

34putreṣu tava vīreṣu cikrīḍārjunapūrvajaḥ
|
mṛgeṣviva mahārāja caran vyāghro mahābalaḥ ||34||

35yathā hi paśumadhyastho dārayeta paśūn vṛkaḥ
|
vṛkodarastava sutāṃstathā vyadrāvayadraṇe ||35||

36gāṅgeyo bhagadattaśca gautamaśca mahārathāḥ
|
pāṇḍavaṃ rabhasaṃ yuddhe vārayāmāsurarjunaram ||36||

37astrairastrāṇi saṃvārya teṣāṃ so'tiratho raṇe
|
pravīrāṃstava sainyeṣu preṣayāmāsa mṛtyave ||37||

38abhimanyustu rājānamambaṣṭhaṃ lokaviśrutam
|
virathaṃ rathināṃ śreṣṭhaṃ vārayāmāsa sāyakaiḥ ||38||

39viratho vadhyamānastu saubhadreṇa yaśasvinā
|
avaplutya rathāttūrṇamambaṣṭho vasudhādhipaḥ ||39||

40asiṃ cikṣepa samare saubhadrasya mahātmanaḥ
|
āruroha rathaṃ caiva hārdikyasya mahābalaḥ ||40||

41āpatantaṃ tu nistriṃśaṃ yuddhamārgaviśāradaḥ
|
lāghavādvyaṃsayāmāsa saubhadraḥ paravīrahā ||41||

42vyaṃsitaṃ vīkṣya nistriṃśaṃ saubhadreṇa raṇe tadā
|
sādhu sādhviti sainyānāṃ praṇādo'bhūdviśāṃpate ||42||

43dhṛṣṭadyumnamukhāstvanye tava sainyamayodhayan
|
tathaiva tāvakāḥ sarve pāṇḍusainyamayodhayan ||43||

44tatrākrando mahānāsīttava teṣāṃ ca bhārata
|
nighnatāṃ dṛḍhamanyonyaṃ kurvatāṃ karma duṣkaram ||44||

45anyonyaṃ hi raṇe śūrāḥ keśeṣvākṣipya māninaḥ
|
nakhadantairayudhyanta muṣṭibhirjānubhistathā ||45||

46talaiścaivātha nistriṃśairbāhubhiśca susaṃsthitaiḥ
|
vivaraṃ prāpya cānyonyamanayanyamasādanam ||46||

47nyahanacca pitā putraṃ putraśca pitaraṃ tathā
|
vyākulīkṛtasarvāṃgā yuyudhustatra mānavāḥ ||47||

48raṇe cārūṇi cāpāni hemapṛṣṭhāni māriṣa
|
hatānāmapaviddhāni kalāpāśca mahādhanāḥ ||48||

49jātarūpamayaiḥ puṅkhaiḥ rājatairniśitāḥ śarāḥ
|
tailadhautā vyarājanta nirmuktabhujagopamāḥ ||49||

50hastidantatsarūn khaḍgān jātarūpapariṣkṛtān
|
carmāṇi cāpaviddhāni rukmacitrāṇi dhanvinām ||50||

51suvarṇavikṛtaprāsān paṭṭiśānhemabhūṣitān
|
jātarūpamayāścarṣṭīḥ śaktīśca kanakojjvalāḥ ||51||

52susannāhāśca patitā musalāni gurūṇi ca
|
parighānpaṭṭiśāṃścaiva bhindipālāṃśca māriṣa ||52||

53patitānvividhāṃścāpāṃścitrānhemapariṣkṛtān
|
kuthā bahuvidhākārāścāmarān vyajanāni ca ||53||

54nānāvidhāni śastrāṇi pragṛhya patitā narāḥ
|
jīvanta iva dṛśyante gatasattvā mahārathāḥ ||54||

55gadāvimathitairgātraipursalairbhinnamastakāḥ
|
gajavājirathakṣuṇṇāḥ śerate sma narāḥ kṣitau ||55||

56tathaivāśvanṛnāgānāṃ śarīrairvibabhau tadā
|
saṃchannā vasudhā rājan parvatairiva sarvaśaḥ ||56||

57samare patitaiścaiva śaktyṛṣṭiśaratomaraiḥ
|
nistriṃśaiḥ paṭṭiśaiḥ prāsairayaskuntaiḥ paraśvadhaiḥ ||57||

58parighairbhindipālaiśca śataghnībhiśca māriṣa
|
śarīraiḥ śastranirbhinnaiḥ samāstīryata modanī ||58||

59viśabdairalpaśabdaiśca śoṇitaughapariplutaiḥ
|
gatāsubhiramitraghna vibabhau nicitā mahī ||59||

60satalatraiḥ sakeyūrairbāhubhiścanda‌nokṣitaiḥ
|
hastihastopamaiśchinnairūrubhiśca tarasvinām ||60||

61baddhacūḍāmaṇivaraiḥ śirobhiśca sakuṇḍalaiḥ
|
pātitairṛṣabhākṣāṇāṃ babhau bhārata medinī ||61||

62kavacaiḥ śoṇitādigdhairviprakīrṇaiśca kāñcanaiḥ
|
rarāja subhṛśaṃ bhūmiḥ śāntārcibhirivānalaiḥ||62||

63vipraviddhaiḥ kalāpaiśca patitaiśca śarāsanaiḥ
|
viprakīrṇaiḥ śaraiścaiva rukmapuṅkhaiḥ samantataḥ ||63||

64rathaiśca sarvato bhagnaiḥ kiṅkiṇījālabhūṣitaiḥ
|
vājibhiśca hatairbāṇaiḥ srastajihvaiḥ saśoṇitaiḥ ||64||

65anukarṣaiḥ patākābhirupāsaṅgairdhvajairapi
|
pravīrāṇāṃ mahāśaṅkhairviprakīrṇaiśca pāṇḍuraiḥ||65||

66srastahastaiśca mātaṅgaiḥ śayānairvibabhau mahī
|
nānārūpairalaṅkāraiḥ pramadevābhyalaṅkṛtā ||66||

67dantibhiścāparaistatra saprāsairgāḍhavedanaiḥ
|
karaiḥ śabdaṃ vimuñcadbhiḥ śīkaraṃ ca muhurmuhuḥ ||67||

68vibabhau tadraṇasthānaṃ syandamānairivācalaiḥ
|
nānārāgaiḥ kambalaiśca paristomaiśca dantinām ||68||

69vaidūryamaṇidaṇḍaiśca patitairaṅkuśaiḥ śubhaiḥ
|
ghaṇṭābhiśca gajendrāṇāṃ patitābhiḥ samantataḥ ||69||

70vipāṭitavicitrābhiḥ kuthābhiraṅkuśaistathā
|
graiveyaiścitrarūpaiśca rukmakakṣyābhireva ca ||70||

71yantraiśca bahudhācchinnaistomaraiścāpi kāñcanaiḥ
|
aśvānāṃ reṇukapilai rukmacchannairuraśchadaiḥ ||71||

72sādināṃ bhujagaiśchinaiḥ patitaiḥ sāṅgadaistathā
|
prāsaiśca vimalaistīkṣṇaurvimalābhistatharṣṭibhiḥ||72||

73uṣṇīṣaiśca tathā citrairvipraviddhaistatastataḥ
|
vicitrairbāṇavarṣaiśca jātarūpapariṣkṛtaiḥ ||73||

74aśvāstaraparistomai rāṅkavairmṛditaistathā
|
narendracūḍāmaṇibhirvicitraiśca mahādhanaiḥ ||74||

75chatraistathā'paviddhaiśca cāmarairvyajainarapi
|
padmendu tibhiścaiva vadanaiścārukuṇḍalaiḥ ||75||

76klṛptaśmaśrubhiratyarthaṃ vīrāṇāṃ samalaṅkṛtaiḥ
|
apaviddhairmahārāja suvarṇojjvalakuṇḍalaiḥ ||76||

77grahanakṣatraśabalā dyaurivārsādvasundharā
|
evamete mahāsene mṛdite tatra bhārata ||77||

78parasparaṃ samāsādya tava teṣāṃ ca saṃyuge
|
teṣu śrānteṣu bhagneṣu mṛditeṣu ca bhārata ||78||

79rātriḥ samabhavattatra nāpaśyāma tato'nugān
|
tato'vahāraṃ sainyānāṃ pracakruḥ kurupāṇḍavāḥ ||79||

80rajanīmukhe suraudre tu vartamāne mahābhaye
|
avahāraṃ tataḥ kṛtvā sahitāḥ kurupāṇḍavāḥ
|
nyaviśanta yathākālaṃ gatvā svaśibiraṃ tadā ||80||
