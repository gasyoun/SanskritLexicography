Mahābhārata 14.65 (Āśvamedhikaparvan)

1brāhmaṇā ūcuḥ|
kriyatāmupahāro'dya tryambakasya mahātmanaḥ
|
dattvāpahāraṃ nṛpate tataḥ svārthaṃ yatāmahe||1||

2śrutvā tu vacanaṃ teṣāṃ brāhmaṇānāṃ yudhiṣṭhiraḥ
|
girīśasya yathānyāyamupahāra mupāharat||2||

3ājyena tarpayitvāgniṃ vidhivatsaṃskṛtena ca
|
mantrasiddhaṃ caruṃ kṛtvā purodhāḥ sa yayau tadā||3||

4sa gṛhītvā sumanaso mantrapūtā janādhipa
|
modakaiḥ pāyasenātha māṃsaiścopāharadbalim||4||

5sumanobhiśca citrābhirlājairuccāvacairapi
|
sarvaṃ sviṣṭatamaṃ kṛtvā vidhivadvedapāragaḥ||5||

6kiṅkarāṇāṃ tataḥ paścāccakāra balimuttamam
|
yakṣendrāya kuberāya maṇibhadrāya caiva ha||6||

7tathānyeṣāṃ ca yakṣāṇāṃ bhūtānāṃ patayaśca ye
|
kṛsareṇa ca māṃsena nivāpaistilasaṃyutaiḥ||7||

8odanaṃ kumbhaśaḥ kṛtvā purodhāḥ samupāharat
|
brāhmaṇebhyaḥ sahasrāṇi gavāṃ dattvā tu bhūmipaḥ||8||

9naktañcarāṇāṃ bhūtānāṃ vyādideśa baliṃ tadā
|
dhūpagandhaniruddhaṃ tatsumanobhiśca saṃvṛtam||9||

10śuśubhe sthānamatyartha devadevasya pārthiva
|
kṛtvā pūjāṃ tu rudrasya gaṇānāṃ caiva sarvaśaḥ||10||

11yayau vyāsaṃ puraskṛtya nṛpo ratnanidhiṃ prati
|
pūjayitvā dhanādhyakṣaṃ praṇipatyābhivādya ca||11||

12sumanobhirvicitrābhirapūpaiḥ kṛsareṇa ca
|
śaṅkhādīṃśca nidhīnsarvānnidhipālāṃśca sarvaśaḥ||12||

13arcayitvā dvijāgryānsa svasti vācya ca vīryavān
|
teṣāṃ puṇyāhaghoṣeṇa tejasā samavasthitaḥ||13||

14prītimānsa kuruśreṣṭhaḥ khānayāmāsa taddhanam
|
tataḥ pātrīḥ sakarakā bahurūpā manoramāḥ||14||

15bhṛṅgarāṇi kaṭāhāni kalaśānvardhamānakān
|
bahūni ca vicitrāṇi bhājanāni sahasraśaḥ||15||

16uddhārayāmāsa tadā dharmarājo yudhiṣṭhiraḥ
|
teṣāṃ rakṣaṇamapyāsīnmahānkarapuṭastathā||16||

17naddhaṃ ca bhājanaṃ rājaṃstulādharmabhavannṛpa
|
vāhanaṃ pāṇḍuputrasya tatrāsīttu viśāmpate||17||

18ṣaṣṭiruṣṭrasahasrāṇi śatāni dviguṇā hayāḥ
|
vāraṇāśca mahārāja sahasraśatasammitāḥ||18||

19śakaṭāni rathāścaiva tāvadeva kareṇavaḥ
|
kharāṇāṃ puruṣāṇāṃ ca parisaṅkhyā na vidyate||19||

20etadvittaṃ tadabhavadyaduddaghe yudhiṣṭhiraḥ
|
ṣoḍaśāṣṭau caturviṃśatsahasraṃ bhāralakṣaṇam||20||

21eteṣvādāya taddravyaṃ punarabhyarcya pāṇḍavaḥ
|
mahādevaṃ prati yayau puraṃ nāgāhvayaṃ prati||21||

22dvaipāyanābhyanujñātaḥ puraskṛtya purohitam
|
goyute goyute caiva nyavasatpuruṣarṣabhaḥ||22||

23sā purābhimukhā rājannuvāha mahatī camūḥ
|
kṛcchrāddraviṇabhārārtā harṣayantī kurūdvahān||23||
