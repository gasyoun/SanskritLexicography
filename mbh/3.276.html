Mahābhārata 3.276 (Āraṇyakaparvan)

1mārkaṇḍeya uvāca|
tato brahmarṣayaḥ sarve siddhā devarṣayastathā
|
havyavāhaṃ puraskṛtya brahmāṇaṃ śaraṇaṃ gatāḥ||1||

2agniruvāca|
yo'sau viśravasaḥ putro daśagrīvo mahābalaḥ
|
avadhyo varadānena kṛto bhagavatā purā||2||

3sa bādhate prajāḥ sarvā viprakārairmahābalaḥ
|
tato nastrātu bhagavānnānyastrātā hi vidyate||3||

4brahmovāca|
na sa devāsuraiḥ śakyo yuddhe jetuṃ vibhāvaso
|
vihitaṃ tatra yat kāryamabhitastasya nigrahaḥ||4||

5tadarthamavatīrṇo'sau manniyogāccaturbhujaḥ
|
viṣṇuḥ praharatāṃ śreṣṭhaḥ sa tat karma kariṣyati||5||

6mārkaṇḍeya uvāca|
pitāmahastatasteṣāṃ sannidhau śakramabravīt
|
sarvairdevagaṇaiḥ sārdhaṃ sambhava tvaṃ mahītale||6||

7viṣṇoḥ sahāyānṛkṣīṣu vānarīṣu ca sarvaśaḥ
|
janayadhvaṃ sutān vīrān kāmarūbalānvitān||7||

8tato bhāgānubhāgena devagandharvadānavāḥ
|
avatartuṃ mahīṃ sarve mantrayāmāsurañjasā||8||

9teṣāṃ samakṣaṃ gandharvīṃ dundubhīṃ nāma nāmataḥ
|
śaśāsa varado devo gaccha kāryārthasiddhaye||9||

10pitāmahavacaḥ śrutvā gandharvī dundubhī tataḥ
|
mantharā mānuṣe loke kubjā samabhavat tadā||10||

11śakraprabhṛtayaścaiva sarve te surasattamāḥ
|
vānararkṣavarastrīṣu janayāmāsurātmajān||11||

12te'nvavartan paztṝn sarve yaśasā ca balena ca
|
bhettāro giriśṛṅgāṇāṃ sālatālaśilāyudhāḥ||12||

13vajrasaṃhananāḥ sarve sarve caughabalāstathā
|
kāmavīryabalāścaiva sarve yuddhaviśāradāḥ||13||

14nāgāyutasamaprāṇā vāyuvegasamā jave
|
yatrecchakanivāsāśca kecidatra vanaukasaḥ||14||

15evaṃ vidhāya tat sarvaṃ bhagavā~llokabhāvanaḥ
|
mantharāṃ bodhayāmāsa yadyat kāryaṃ yathā yathā||15||

16sā tadvacaḥ samājñāya tathā cakre manojavā
|
itaścetaśca gacchantī vairasandhukṣaṇe ratā||16||
