Mahābhārata 8.18 (Karṇaparvan)

1sañjaya uvāca|
athottareṇa pāṇḍūnāṃ senāyāṃ dhvanirutthitaḥ
|
rathanāgāśvapattīnāṃ daṇḍadhāreṇa vadhyatām ||1||

2nivartayitvā tu rathaṃ keśavo'rjunamabravīt
|
vāhayanneva turagān garuḍānilaraṃhasaḥ ||2||

3māgadho'pyativikrānto dviradena pramāthinā
|
bhagada‌ttādanavaraḥ śikṣayā ca balena ca ||3||

4enaṃ hattvā nihantāsi punaḥ saṃśaptakāniti
|
vākyānte prāpayat pārthaṃ daṇḍadhārāntikaṃ prati ||4||

5sa māgadhānāṃ pravaro'ṅkuśagrahe grahe'prasahyo vikaco yathā grahaḥ
|
sapatnasenāṃ pramamātha dāruṇo mahīṃ samagrāṃ vikaco yathā grahaḥ ||5||

6sukalpitaṃ dānavanāgasannibhaṃ mahābhranirhrādamamitramardanam
|
rathāśvamātaṅgagagaṇānsahasraśaḥ samāsthito hanti śarairnarānapi ||6||

7rathānadhiṣṭhāya savājisārīnnarāṃśca pādairdvirado vyapothayat
|
dvipāṃśca padbhyāṃ mamṛde kareṇa dvipottamo hanti ca kālacakravat ||7||

8narāṃstu kārṣṇāyasavarmabhūṣaṇānnipātya sāśvānapi pattibhiḥ saha
|
vyapothayaddantivareṇa śuṣmiṇā sa śabdavatsthūlanalaṃ yathā tathā ||8||

9athārjuno jyātalaneminiḥsvane mṛdaṅgabherībahuśaṃkhanādite
|
rathāśvamātaṅgasahasrasaṃkule rathottamenābhyapataddvipottamam ||9||

10tato'rjunaṃ dvādaśabhiḥ śarottamairjanārdanaṃ ṣoḍaśabhiḥ samārpayat
|
sa daṇḍadhārasturagāṃstribhistribhistato nanāda prajahāsa cāsakṛt ||10||

11tato'sya pārthaḥ saguṇeṣukārmukaṃ cakarta bhallairdhvajamapyalaṃkṛtam
|
punarniyantṝnsaha pādagoptṝṃstataḥ sa cukrodha girivrajeśvaraḥ ||11||

12tato'rjunaṃ bhinnakaṭena dantinā ghanāghanenānilatulyavarcasā
|
atīva cukṣobhayiṣurjanārdanaṃ dhanañjayaṃ cābhijaghāna tomaraiḥ ||12||

13athāsya bāhū dvipahastasannibhau śiraśca pūrṇendunibhānanaṃ tribhiḥ
|
kṣuraiḥ praciccheda sahaiva pāṇḍavastato dvipaṃ bāṇaśataiḥ samārpayat ||13||

14sa pārthabāṇaistapanīyabhūṣaṇaiḥ samācitaḥ kāñcanavarmabhṛddvipaḥ
|
tathā cakāśe niśi parvato yathā dāvāgninā prajvalitauṣadhidrumaḥ ||14||

15sa vedanārto'mbuda‌nisvano nadaṃścaran bhraman praskhalitāntaro'dravata
|
papāta rugṇaḥ saniyantṛkastathā yathā girirvajravidāritastathā ||15||

16himāvadātena suvarṇamālinā himādrikūṭapratimena dantinā
|
hate raṇe bhrātari daṇḍa āvrajajjighāṃsurindrāvarajaṃ dhanañjayam ||16||

17satomarairarkakaraprabhaistribhirjanārdanaṃ pañcabhirarjunaṃ śitaiḥ
|
samarpayitvā vinanāda nardayaṃstato'sya bāhū nicakarta pāṇḍavaḥ ||17||

18kṣuraprakṛttau subhṛśaṃ satomarau śubhāṅgadau candanarūṣitau bhujau
|
gajātpatantau yugapadvirejaturyathā'driśṛṅgādrucirau mahoragau ||18||

19tathā'rdhacandreṇa hataṃ kirīṭinā papāta daṇḍasya śiraḥ kṣitiṃ dvipāt
|
sa śoṇitārdro nipatanvireje divākaro'stādiva paścimāṃ diśam ||19||

20atha dvipaṃ śvetavarābhrasannibhaṃ divākarāṃśupratimaiḥ śarottamaiḥ
|
bibheda pārthaḥ sa papāta nādayan himādrikūṭaṃ kuliśāhataṃ yathā ||20||

21tato'pare tatpratimā gajottamā jigīṣavaḥ saṃyati savyasācinā
|
tathā kṛtāste ca yathaiva tau dvipau tataḥ prabhagnnaṃ sumahadriporbalam ||21||

22gajā rathāśvāḥ puruṣāśca saṃghaśaḥ parasparaghnāḥ paripeturāhave
|
parasparaṃ praskhalitāḥ samāhitā bhṛśaṃ nipeturbahubhāṣiṇo hatāḥ ||22||

23athārjunaṃ sve parivārya sainikāḥ puraṃdaraṃ devagaṇā ivābruvan
|
abhaiṣma yasmānmaraṇādiva prajāḥ sa vīra diṣṭyā nihatastvayā ripuḥ ||23||

24na cedarakṣiṣya imaṃ janaṃ bhayāddviṣadbhirevaṃ balibhiḥ prapīḍitam
|
tathā bhaviṣyaddviṣatāṃ pramodanaṃ yathā hateṣveṣviha no'risūdana ||24||

25itīva bhūyaśca suhṛdbhirīḍitā niśamya vācaḥ sumanāstato'rjunaḥ
|
yathānurūpaṃ pratipūjya taṃ janaṃ jagāma saṃśaptakasaṃghahā punaḥ ||25||
