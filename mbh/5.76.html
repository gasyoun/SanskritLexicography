Mahābhārata 5.76 (Udyogaparvan)

1vaiśampāyana uvāca|
tathokto vāsudevena nityamanyuramarṣaṇaḥ
|
sadaśvavatsamādhāvadbabhāṣe tadanantaram ||1||

2bhīmasena uvāca|
anyathā māṃ cikīrṣantamanyathā manyase'cyuta
|
praṇītabhāvamatyarthaṃ yudhi satyaparākramam ||2||

3vetsi dāśārha satyaṃ me dīrghakālaṃ sahoṣitaḥ
|
uta vā māṃ na jānāsi plavan hrada ivāplave ||3||

4tasmādanabhirūpābhirvāgbhirmāṃ tvaṃ samarchasi
|
kathaṃ hi bhīmasenaṃ māṃ jānan kaścana mādhava ||4||

5brūyādapratirūpāṇi yathā māṃ vaktumarhasi
|
tasmādidaṃ pravakṣyāmi vacanaṃ vṛṣṇinandana ||5||

6ātmanaḥ pauruṣaṃ caiva balaṃ ca na samaṃ paraiḥ
|
sarvathā nāryakarmetat praśaṃsā svayamātmanaḥ ||6||

7ativādāpaviddhastu vakṣyāmi balamātmanaḥ
|
paśyeme rodasī kṛṣṇa yayorāsannimāḥ prajāḥ ||7||

8acale cāpratiṣṭhe cāpyanante sarvamātarau
|
yadime sahasā kruddhe sameyātāṃ śile iva ||8||

9ahamete nigṛhṇīyāṃ bāhubhyāṃ sacarācare
|
paśyaitadantaraṃ bāhvormahāparighayoriva ||9||

10ya etatprāpya mucyeta na taṃ paśyāmi pūruṣam
|
himavāṃśca samudraśca vajrī vā balamit svayam ||10||

11mayā'bhipannaṃ trāyeran balamāsthāya na trayaḥ
|
yuddhārhān kṣatriyān sarvān pāṇḍaveṣvātatāyinaḥ ||11||

12adhaḥ pādatalenaitānadhiṣṭhāsyāmi bhūtale
|
na hi tvaṃ nābhijānāsi mama vikramamacyuta ||12||

13yathā mayā vinirjitya rājāno vaśagāḥ kṛtāḥ
|
atha cenmāṃ na jānāsi sūryasyevodyataḥ prabhām ||13||

14vigāḍhe yudhi sambādhe vetsyase māṃ janārdana
|
paruṣairākṣipasi kiṃ vraṇaṃ pūtimivonnayan ||14||

15yathāmati bravīmyetadviddhi māmadhikaṃ tataḥ
|
draṣṭā'si yudhi sambādhe pravṛtte vaiśase'hani ||15||

16mayā praṇunnānmātaṅgānrathinaḥ sādinastathā
|
tathā narānabhikruddhaṃ nighnantaṃ kṣatriyarṣabhān ||16||

17draṣṭā māṃ tvaṃ ca lokaśca vikarṣantaṃ varān varān
|
na me sīdanti majjāno na mamodvepate manaḥ ||17||

18sarvalokādabhikruddhānna bhayaṃ vidyate mama
|
kiṃ tu sauhṛdamevaitat kṛpayā madhusūdana
|
sarvāṃstitikṣe saṃkleśān mā sma no bharatā naśan ||18||
