Mahābhārata 7.148 (Droṇaparvan)

1dhṛtarāṣṭra uvāca|
tathā gateṣu śūreṣu teṣāṃ mama ca sañjaya
|
kiṃ vai bhīmastadā'kārṣīttanmamācakṣva sañjaya||1||

2sañjaya uvāca|
viratho bhīmaseno vai karṇavākśalyapīḍitaḥ
|
amarṣavaśamāpannaḥ phālgunaṃ vākyamabravīt||2||

3punaḥ punastūbaraka mūḍha audariketi ca
|
akṛtāstraka mā yotsīrbāla saṃgrāmakātara||3||

4iti māmabravītkarṇaḥ paśyataste dhanañjaya
|
evaṃ vaktā ca me vadhyastena cokto'smi bhārata||4||

5etadvrataṃ mahābāho tvayā saha kṛtaṃ mayā
|
tathaitanmama kaunteya yathā tava na saṃśayaḥ||5||

6tadvadhāya naraśreṣṭha smaraitadvacanaṃ mama
|
yathā bhavati tatsatyaṃ tathā kuru dhanañjaya||6||

7tacchrutvā vacanaṃ tasya bhīmasyāmitavikramaḥ
|
tato'rjuno'bravītkarṇaṃ kiṃcidabhyetya saṃyuge||7||

8karṇa karṇa vṛthādṛṣṭe sūtaputrātmasaṃstuta
|
adharmabuddhe śṛṇu me yattvāṃ vakṣyāmi sāṃpratam||8||

9dvividhaṃ karma śūrāṇāṃ yuddhe jayaparājayau
|
tau cāpyanityau rādheya vāsavasyāpi yudhyataḥ||9||

10mumūrṣuryuyudhānena viratho vikalendriyaḥ
|
madvadhyastvamiti jñātvā jitvā jīvanvisarjitaḥ||10||

11yadṛcchayā raṇe bhīmaṃ yudhyamānaṃ mahābalam
|
kathañcidvirathaṃ kṛtvā yattvaṃ rūkṣamabhāṣathāḥ||11||

12adharmastveṣa sumahānanāryacaritaṃ ca tat
|
nāriṃ jitvā'tikatthante na ca jalpanti durvacaḥ||12||

13na ca kañcana nindanti santaḥ śūrā nararṣabhāḥ
|
tvaṃ tu prākṛtavijñānasvattadvadasi sūtaja||13||

14bahvabaddhamakarṇyaṃ ca cāpalādaparīkṣitam
|
yudhyamānaṃ parākrāntaṃ śūramāryavrate ratam||14||

15yadavoco'priyaṃ bhīmaṃ naitatsatyaṃ vacastava
|
paśyatāṃ sarvasainyānāṃ keśavasya mamaiva ca||15||

16viratho bhīmasenena kṛto'si bahuśo raṇe
|
na ca tvāṃ paruṣaṃ kiñciduktavānpāṇḍunandanaḥ||16||

17yasmāttu bahu rūkṣaṃ ca śrāvitaste vṛkodaraḥ
|
parokṣaṃ yacca saubhadro yuṣmābhirnihato mama||17||

18tasmādasyāvalepasya sadyaḥ phalamavāpnuhi
|
tvayā tasya dhanuśchinnamātmanāśāya durmate||18||

19tasmādvadhyo'si me mūḍha sabhṛtya-suta-bāndhavaḥ
|
kuru tvaṃ sarvakṛtyāni mahat te bhayamāgatam||19||

20hantāsmi vṛṣasenaṃ te prekṣamāṇasya saṃyuge
|
ye cānyepyupayāsyanti buddhimohena māṃ nṛpāḥ||20||

21tāṃśca sarvānhaniṣyāmi satyenāyudhamālabhe
|
tvāṃ ca mūḍhā kṛtaprajñamatimāninamāhave||21||

22dṛṣṭvā duryodhano mando bhṛśaṃ tapsyati pātitam
|
arjunena pratijñāte vadhe karṇasutasya tu||22||

23mahānsutumulaḥ śabdo babhūva rathināṃ tadā
|
tasminnākulasaṃgrāme vartamāne mahābhaye||23||

24mandaraśmiḥ sahasrāṃśurastaṃ girimupādravat
|
tato rājanhṛṣīkeśaḥ saṃgrāmaśirasi sthitam||24||

25tīrṇapratijñaṃ bībhatsuṃ pariṣvajyainamabravīt
|
diṣṭyā saṃpāditā jiṣṇo pratijñā mahatī tvayā||25||

26diṣṭyā vinihataḥ pāpo vṛddhakṣatraḥ sahātmajaḥ
|
dhārtarāṣṭrabalaṃ prāpya devasenāpi bhārata||26||

27sīdeta samare jiṣṇo nātra kāryā vicāraṇā
|
na taṃ paśyāmi lokeṣu cintayanpuruṣaṃ kvacit||27||

28tvadṛte puruṣavyāghra ya etadyodhayedbalam
|
mahāprabhāvā bahavastvayā tulyādhikā'pi vā||28||

29sametāḥ pṛthivīpālā dhārtarāṣṭrasya kāraṇāt
|
te tvāṃ prāpya raṇe kruddhā nābhyavartanta daṃśitāḥ||29||

30tava vīryaṃ balaṃ caiva rudraśakāntakopamam
|
nedṛśaṃ śaknuyātkaścidraṇe kartuṃ parākramam||30||

31yādṛśaṃ kṛtavānadya tvamekaḥ śatrutāpanaḥ
|
evameva hate karṇe sānubandhe durātmani||31||

32vardhayiṣyāmi bhūyastvāṃ vijitāriṃ hatadviṣam
|
tamarjunaḥ pratyuvāca prasādāttava mādhava||32||

33pratijñeyaṃ mayā tīrṇā vibudhairapi dustarā
|
anāścaryo jayasteṣāṃ yeṣāṃ nātho'si keśava||33||

34tvatprasādānmahīṃ kṛtsnāṃ saṃprāpsyati yudhiṣṭhiraḥ
|
tava prabhāvo vārṣṇeya tavaiva vijayaḥ prabho||34||

35vardhanīyāstava vayaṃ sadaiva madhusūdana
|
evamuktastataḥ kṛṣṇaḥ śanakairvāhayanhayān||35||

36darśayāmāsa pārthāya krūramāyodhanaṃ mahat
|
śrīkṛṣṇa uvāca|
prārthayanto jayaṃ yuddhe prathitaṃ ca mahadyaśaḥ||36||

37pṛthivyāṃ śerate śūrāḥ pārthivāstvaccharairhatāḥ
|
vikīrṇaśastrābharaṇā vipannāśva-ratha-dvipāḥ
|
saṃchinnabhinnamarmāṇo vaiklavyaṃ paramaṃ gatāḥ||37||

38sasattvā gatasattvāśca prabhayā parayā yutāḥ
|
sajīvā iva lakṣyante gatasattvā narādhipāḥ||38||

39teṣāṃ śaraiḥ svarṇapuṃkhaiḥ śastraiśca vividhaiḥ śitaiḥ
|
vāhanairāyudhaiścaiva saṃpūrṇāṃ paśya medinīm||39||

40varmabhiścarmabhirhāraiḥ śirobhiśca sakuṇḍalaiḥ
|
uṣṇīṣairmukuṭaiḥ sragbhiścūḍāmaṇibhirambaraiḥ||40||

41kaṇṭhasūtrairaṅgadaiśca niṣkairapi ca saprabhaiḥ
|
anyaiścābharaṇaiścitrairbhāti bhārata medinī||41||

42anukarṣairupāsaṅgaiḥ patākābhirdhvajaistathā
|
upaskarairadhiṣṭhānairīṣādaṇḍakabandhuraiḥ||42||

43cakraiḥ pramathitaścitrairakṣaiśca bahudhā raṇe
|
yugairyoktaiḥ kalāpaiśca dhanurbhiḥ sāyakaistathā||43||

44paristomaiḥ kuthābhiśca parighairaṅkuśaistathā
|
śaktibhirbhindipālaiśca tūrṇaiḥ śūlaiḥ paraśvadhaiḥ||44||

45prāsaiśca tomaraiścaiva kuntairyaṣṭibhireva ca
|
śataghnībhirbhuśuṇḍībhiḥ khaḍgaiḥ paraśubhistathā||45||

46musalairmudgaraiścaiva gadābhiḥ kuṇapaistathā
|
suvarṇavikṛtābhiśca kaśābhirbharatarṣabha||46||

47ghaṇṭābhiśca gajendrāṇāṃ bhāṇḍaiśca vividhairapi
|
sragbhiśca nānābharaṇairvastraiścaiva mahādhanaiḥ||47||

48apaviddhairbabhau bhūmirgṛhairdyauriva śāradī
|
pṛthivyāṃ pṛthivīhetoḥ pṛthivīpatayo hatāḥ||48||

49pṛthivīmupaguhyāṅgaiḥ suptāḥ kāntāmiva priyām
|
imāṃśca girikūṭābhānnāgānairāvatopamān||49||

50kṣarataḥ śoṇitaṃ bhūri śastracchedadarīmukhaiḥ
|
darīmukhairiva girīn gairikāṃbuparisravān||50||

51tāṃśca bāṇahatānvīra paśya niṣṭanataḥ kṣitau
|
hayāṃśca patitānpaśya svarṇabhāṇḍavibhūṣitān||51||

52gandharvanagarākārān rathāṃśca nihateśvarān
|
chinnadhvajapatākākṣānvicakrānhatasārathīn||52||

53nikṛttakūbarayugānbhagneṣānbandhurānprabho
|
paśya pārtha hayānbhūmau vimānopamadarśanān||53||

54patīṃśca nihatānvīra śataśo'tha sahasraśaḥ
|
dhanurbhṛtaścarmabhṛtaḥ śayānān rudhirokṣitān||54||

55mahīmāliṃgya sarvāṅgaiḥ pāṃsudhvastaśiroruhān
|
paśya yodhānmahābāho tvaccharairbhinnavigrahān||55||

56nipātitadvipa-ratha-vājisaṃkula-masṛgvasā-piśitasamṛddhakardamam
|
niśācara-śva-vṛka-piśācamodanaṃ mahītalaṃ naravara paśya durddaśam||56||

57idaṃ mahattvayyupapadyate prabho raṇājire karma yaśobhivardhanam
|
śatakratau cāpi ca devasattame mahāhave jaghnuṣi daityadānavān||57||

58sañjaya uvāca|
evaṃ sandarśayan kṛṣṇo raṇabhūmiṃ kirīṭine
|
svaiḥ sametaḥ samuditaiḥ pāñcajanyaṃ vyanādayat||58||

59sa darśayanneva kirīṭine'rihā janārdanastāmaribhūmimañjasā
|
ajātaśatruṃ samupetya pāṇḍavaṃ nivedayāmāsa hataṃ jayadratham||59||
