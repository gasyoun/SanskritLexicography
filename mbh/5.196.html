Mahābhārata 5.196 (Udyogaparvan)

1vaiśampāyana uvāca|
tathaiva rājā kaunteyo dharmaputro yudhiṣṭhiraḥ
|
dhṛṣṭadyumnamukhān vīrāṃścodayāmāsa bhārata ||1||

2cedikāśikarūṣāṇāṃ netāraṃ dṛḍhavikramam
|
senāpatimamitraghnaṃ dhṛṣṭaketumathādiśat ||2||

3virāṭaṃ drupadaṃ caiva yuyudhānaṃ śikhaṇḍinam
|
pāñcālyau ca maheṣvāsau yudhāmanyūttamaujasau||3||

4te śūrāścitravarmāṇastaptakuṇḍaladhāriṇaḥ
|
ājyāvasiktā jvalitā dhiṣṇyeṣviva hutāśanāḥ ||4||

5aśobhanta maheṣvāsā grahāḥ prajvalitā iva
|
atha sainyaṃ yathāyogaṃ pūjayitvā nararṣabhaḥ ||5||

6dideśa tānyanīkāni prayāṇāya mahīpatiḥ
|
teṣāṃ yudhiṣṭhiro rājā sasainyānāṃ mahātmanām ||6||

7vyādideśa sabāhyānāṃ bhakṣyabhojyamanuttamam
|
sagajāśvamanuṣyāṇāṃ ye ca śilpopajīvinaḥ ||7||

8abhimanyuṃ bṛhantaṃ ca draupadeyāṃśca sarvaśaḥ
|
ghṛṣṭadyumnamukhānetān prāhiṇot pāṇḍunandanaḥ ||8||

9bhīmaṃ ca yuyudhānaṃ ca pāṇḍavaṃ ca dhanañjayam
|
dvitīyaṃ preṣayāmāsa balaskandhaṃ yudhiṣṭhiraḥ ||9||

10bhāṇḍaṃ samāropayatāṃ caratāṃ saṃpradhāvatām
|
hṛṣṭānāṃ tatra yodhānāṃ śabdo divamivāspṛśat ||10||

11svayameva tataḥ paścādvirāṭadrupadānvitaḥ
|
athāparairmahīpālaiḥ saha prāyānmahīpatiḥ ||11||

12bhīmadhanvāyanī senā dhṛṣṭadyumnena pālitā
|
gaṅgeva pūrṇā stimitā syandamānā vyadṛśyata ||12||

13tataḥ punaranīkāni nyayojayata buddhimān
|
mohayan dhṛtarāṣṭrasya putrāṇāṃ buddhiniścayam ||13||

14draupadeyānmaheṣvāsānabhimanyuṃ ca pāṇḍavaḥ
|
nakulaṃ sahadevaṃ ca sarvāṃścaiva prabhadrakān ||14||

15daśa cāśvasahasrāṇi dvisahastrāṇi dantinām
|
ayutaṃ ca padātīnāṃ rathāḥ pañcaśataṃ tathā ||15||

16bhīmasenasya durdharṣaṃ prathamaṃ prādiśadbalam
|
madhyame ca virāṭaṃ ca jayatsenaṃ ca pāṇḍavaḥ ||16||

17mahārathau ca pāñcālyau yudhāmanyūttamaujasau
|
vīryavantau mahātmānau gadākārmukadhāriṇau ||17||

18anvayātāṃ tadā madhye vāsudevadhanañjayau
|
babhūvuratisaṃrabdhāḥ kṛtapraharaṇā narāḥ ||18||

19teṣāṃ viṃśatisāhasrā hayāḥ śūrairadhiṣṭhitāḥ
|
pañca nāgasahastrāṇi rathavaṃśāśca sarvaśaḥ ||19||

20padāyataśca ye śūrāḥ kārmukāsigadādharāḥ
|
sahasraśo'nvayuḥ paścādagrataśca sahasraśaḥ ||20||

21yudhiṣṭhiro yatra sainye svayameva balārṇave
|
tatra te pṛthivīpālā bhūyiṣṭhaṃ paryavasthitāḥ ||21||

22tatra nāgasahasrāṇi hayānāmayutāni ca
|
tathā rathasahastrāṇi padātīnāṃ ca bhārata ||22||

23cekitānaḥ svasainyena mahatā pārthivarṣabha
|
dhṛṣṭaketuśca cedīnāṃ praṇetā pārthivo yayau ||23||

24sātyakiśca maheṣvāso vṛṣṇīnāṃ pravaro rathaḥ
|
vṛtaḥ śatasahastreṇa rathānāṃ praṇudan balī ||24||

25kṣatradevabrahmadevau rathasthau puruṣarṣabhau
|
jaghanaṃ pālayantau ca pṛṣṭhato'nuprajagmatuḥ ||25||

26śakaṭāpaṇaveśāśca yānaṃ yugyaṃ ca sarvaśaḥ
|
tatra nāgasahasrāṇi hayānāmayutāni ca
|
phalgu sarvaṃ kalatraṃ ca yatkiñcit kṛśadurbalam ||26||

27kośasañcayavāhāṃśca koṣṭhāgāraṃ tathaiva ca
|
gajānīkena saṃgṛhya śanaiḥ prāyādyudhiṣṭhiraḥ ||27||

28tamanvayāt satyadhṛtiḥ saucittiryuddhadurmadaḥ
|
śreṇimān vasudānaśca putraḥ kāśyasya vā vibhuḥ||28||

29rathā viṃśatisāhasrā ye teṣāmanuyāyinaḥ
|
hayānāṃ daśa koṭyaśca mahatāṃ kiṃkiṇīkinām ||29||

30gajā viṃśatisāhasrā īṣādantāḥ prahāriṇaḥ
|
kulīnā bhinnakaraṭā meghā iva visarpiṇaḥ ||30||

31ṣaṣṭhirnāgasahastrāṇi daśānyāni ca bhārata
|
yudhiṣṭhirasya yānyāsan yudhi senā mahātmanaḥ ||31||

32kṣaranta iva jīmūtāḥ prabhinnakaraṭāmukhāḥ
|
rājānamanvayuḥ paścāccalanta iva parvatāḥ ||32||

33evaṃ tasya balaṃ bhīmaṃ kuntīputrasya dhīmataḥ
|
yadāśrityātha yuyudhe dhārtarāṣṭraṃ suyodhanam ||33||

34tato'nye śataśaḥ paścāt sahastrāyutaśo narāḥ
|
nardantaḥ prayayusteṣāmanīkāni sahasraśaḥ ||34||

35tatra bherīsahasrāṇi śaṅkhānāmayutāni ca
|
nyavādayanta saṃhṛṣṭāḥ sahastrāyutaśo narāḥ ||35||
