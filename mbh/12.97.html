Mahābhārata 12.97 (Śāntiparvan)

1yudhiṣṭhira uvāca|
kṣatradharmāddhi pāpīyānna dharmo'sti narādhipa
|
apayānena yuddhena rājā hanti mahājanam||1||

2atha sma karmaṇā kena lokān jayati pārthivaḥ
|
vidvan jijñāsamānāya prabūhi bharatarṣabha||2||

3bhīṣma uvāca|
nigraheṇa ca pāpānāṃ sādhūnāṃ saṃgraheṇa ca
|
yajñairdānaiśca rājāno bhavanti śucayo'malāḥ||3||

4uparundhanti rājāno bhūtāni vijayārthinaḥ
|
ta eva vijayaṃ prāpya vardhayanti punaḥ prajāḥ||4||

5apavidhyanti pāpāni dāna-yajña-tapobalaiḥ
|
anugrahāya bhūtānāṃ puṇyameṣāṃ vivardhate||5||

6yathaiva kṣetraniryātā niryātaṃ kṣetrameva ca
|
hinasti dhānyaṃ kakṣaṃ ca na ca dhānyaṃ vinaśyati||6||

7evaṃ śastrāṇi muñcanto ghnanti vadhyānanekadhā
|
tasyaiṣā niṣkṛtiḥ kṛtsnā bhūtānāṃ bhāvanaṃ punaḥ||7||

8yo bhūtāni dhanākrāntyā vadhātkleśācca rakṣati
|
dasyubhyaḥ prāṇadānātsa dhanadaḥ sukhado virāṭ||8||

9sa sarvayajñairījāno rājā'thābhayadakṣiṇaiḥ
|
anubhūyeha bhadrāṇi prāpnotīndrasalokatām||9||

10brāhmaṇārthe samutpanne yo'ribhiḥ sṛtya yudhyati
|
ātmānaṃ yūpamutsṛjya sa yajño'nantadakṣiṇaḥ||10||

11abhīto vikiran śatrūn pratigṛhya śarāṃstathā
|
na tasmāttridaśāḥ śreyo bhuvi paśyanti kiñcana||11||

12tasya śastrāṇi yāvanti tvacaṃ bhindanti saṃyuge
|
tāvataḥ so'śnute lokān sarvakāmaduho'kṣayān||12||

13yadasya rudhiraṃ gātrādāhaye sampravartate
|
saha tenaiva raktena sarvapāpaiḥ pramucyate||13||

14yāni duḥkhāni sahate kṣatriyo yudhi tāpitaḥ
|
tena tena tapo bhūya iti dharmavido viduḥ||14||

15pṛṣṭhato bhīravaḥ saṃkhye vartante dharmapūruṣāḥ
|
śūrāccharaṇamicchantaḥ parjanyādiva jīvanam||15||

16yadi śūrastathā kṣemaṃ pratirakṣedyathā'bhaye
|
pratirūpaṃ janaṃ kuryānna cettadvartate tathā||16||

17yadi te kṛtamājñāya namaskuryuḥ sadaivatam
|
yuktaṃ nyāyyaṃ ca kuryuste na ca tadvartate tathā||17||

18puruṣāṇāṃ samānānāṃ dṛśyate mahadantaram
|
saṃgrāme'nīkavelāyāmutkruṣṭe'bhipatantyuta||18||

19patatyabhimukhaḥ śūraḥ parānbhīruḥ palāyate
|
āsthāya svargyamadhvānaṃ sahāyānviṣame tyajet||19||

20mā sma tāṃstādṛśāṃstāta janiṣṭhāḥ puruṣādhamān
|
ye sahāyān raṇe hitvā svastimanto gṛhānyayuḥ||20||

21asvasti tebhyaḥ kurvanti devā indrapurogamāḥ
|
tyāgena yaḥ sahāyānāṃ svānprāṇāṃstrātumicchati||21||

22taṃ hanyuḥ kāṣṭhaloṣṭhairvā daheyurvā kaṭāgninā
|
paśuvanmārayeyurvā kṣatriyā ye syurīdṛśāḥ||22||

23adharmaḥ kṣatriyasyaiṣa yacchayyāmaraṇaṃ bhavet
|
visṛjan śleṣmamūtrāṇi kṛpaṇaṃ paridevayan||23||

24avikṣatena dehena pralayaṃ yo'dhigacchati
|
kṣatriyo nāsya tatkarma praśaṃsanti purāvidaḥ||24||

25na gṛhe maraṇaṃ tāta kṣatriyāṇāṃ praśasyate
|
śauṭīrāṇāmaśauṭīryamadharmaṃ kṛpaṇaṃ ca tat||25||

26idaṃ duḥkhaṃ mahatkaṣṭaṃ pāpīya iti naṣṭanan
|
pratidhvastamukhaḥ pūtiramātyānanuśocayan||26||

27arogāṇāṃ spṛhayate muhurmṛtyumapīcchati
|
vīro dṛpto'bhimānī ca nedṛśaṃ mṛtyumarhati||27||

28raṇeṣu kadanaṃ kṛtvā jñātibhiḥ parivāritaḥ
|
tīkṣṇaiḥ śastrairabhikliṣṭaḥ kṣatriyo mṛtyumarhati||28||

29śūro hi kāmamanyubhyāmāviṣṭo yuddhyate bhṛśam
|
hanyamānāni gātrāṇi parairnaivāvabudhyate||29||

30sa saṃkhye nidhanaṃ prāpya praśastaṃ lokapūjitam
|
svadharmaṃ vipulaṃ prāpya śakrasyaiti salokatām||30||

31sarvopāyai raṇamukhamātiṣṭhaṃstyaktajīvitaḥ
|
prāpnotīndrasya sālokyaṃ śūraḥ pṛṣṭhamadarśayan||31||

32yatra yatra hataḥ śūraḥ śatrubhiḥ parivāritaḥ
|
akṣayā~llabhate lokānyadi dainyaṃ na sevate||32||
