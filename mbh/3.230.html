Mahābhārata 3.230 (Āraṇyakaparvan)

1mārkaṇḍeya uvāca|
śriyā juṣṭaṃ mahāsenaṃ devasenāpatiṃ kṛtam
|
saptarṣipatnyaḥ ṣaḍ devyastatsakāśamathāgaman||1||

2ṛṣibhiḥ samparityaktā dharmayuktā mahāvratāḥ
|
drutamāgamya cocustā devasenāpatiṃ prabhum||2||

3vayaṃ putraparityaktā bhartṛbhirdevasammitaiḥ
|
akāraṇādruṣā taistu puṇyasthānāt paricyutāḥ||3||

4asmābhiḥ kila jātastvamiti kenāpyudāhṛtam
|
tatsatyametat saṃśrutya tasmānnastrātumarhasi||4||

5akṣayaśca bhavet svargastvatprasādāddhi naḥ prabho
|
tvāṃ putraṃ cāpyabhīpsāmaḥ kṛtvaitadanṛṇo bhava||5||

6skanda uvāca|
mātaro hi bhavantyo me suto vo'hamaninditāḥ
|
yadvāpīcchata tat sarvaṃ sambhaviṣyati vastathā||6||

7mārkaṇḍeya uvāca|
vivakṣantaṃ tataḥ śakraṃ kiṃ kāryamiti so'bravīt
|
uktaḥ skandena brūhīti so'bravīdvāsavastataḥ||7||

8abhijit spardhamānā tu rohiṇyā kanyasī svasā
|
icchantī jyeṣṭhatāṃ devī tapastaptuṃ vanaṃ gatā||8||

9tatra mūḍho'smi bhadraṃ te nakṣatraṃ gaganāccyutam
|
kālaṃ tvimaṃ paraṃ skanda brahmaṇā saha cintaye||9||

10dhaniṣṭhādistadā kālo brahmaṇā parikalpitaḥ
|
rohiṇī hyabhavat pūrvamevaṃ saṅkhyā samā'bhavat||10||

11evamukte tu śakreṇa tridivaṃ kṛttikā gatāḥ
|
nakṣatraṃ saptaśīrṣābhaṃ bhāti tadvahnidaivatam||11||

12vinatā cābravīt skandaṃ mama tvaṃ piṇḍadaḥ sutaḥ
|
icchāmi nityamevāhaṃ tvayā putra sahāsitum||12||

13skandra uvāca|
evamastu namaste'stu putrasnehāt praśādhi mām
|
snuṣayā pūjyamānā vai devi vatsyasi nityadā||13||

14mārkaṇḍeya uvāca|
atha mātṛgaṇaḥ sarvaḥ skandaṃ vacanamabravīt
|
vayaṃ sarvasya lokasya mātaraḥ kavibhiḥ stutāḥ
|
icchāmo mātarastubhyaṃ bhavituṃ pūjayasva naḥ||14||

15skanda uvāca|
mātaro hi bhavatyo me bhavatīnāmahaṃ sutaḥ
|
ucyatāṃ yanmayā kāryaṃ bhavatīnāmathepsitam||15||

16mātara ūcuḥ| yāstu tā mātaraḥ pūrvaṃ lokasyāsya prakalpitāḥ
|
asmākaṃ tu bhavet sthānaṃ tāsāṃ caiva na tadbhavet||16||

17bhavema pūjyā lokasya na tāḥ pūjyāḥ surarṣabha
|
prajā'smākaṃ hṛtāstābhistvatkṛte tāḥ prayaccha naḥ||17||

18skanda uvāca|
vṛttāḥ prajā na tāḥ śakyā bhavatībhirniṣevitum
|
anyāṃ vaḥ kāṃ prayacchāmi prajāṃ yāṃ manasecchatha||18||

19mātara ūcuḥ
icchāma tāsāṃ mātṝṇāṃ prajā bhoktuṃ prayaccha naḥ
|
tvayā saha pṛthagbhūtā ye ca tāsāmatheśvarāḥ||19||

20skanda uvāca|
prajā vo dadmi kaṣṭaṃ tu bhavatībhirudāhṛtam
|
parirakṣata bhadraṃ vaḥ prajāḥ sādhu namaskṛtāḥ||20||

21mātara ūcuḥ| parirakṣāma bhadraṃ te prajāḥ skanda yathecchasi
|
tvayā no rocate skanda sahavāsāṃściraṃ prabho||21||

22skanda uvāca|
yāvat ṣoḍaśa varṣāṇi bhavanti taruṇāḥ prajāḥ
|
prabādhata manuṣyāṇāṃ tāvadrūpaiḥ pṛthagvidhaiḥ||22||

23ahaṃ ca vaḥ pradāsyāmi raudramātmānamavyayam
|
paramaṃ tena sahitā sukhaṃ vatsyatha pūjitāḥ||23||

24mārkaṇḍeya uvāca|
tataḥ śarīrāt skandasya puruṣaḥ pāvakaprabhaḥ
|
bhoktuṃ prajāḥ sa martyānāṃ niṣpapāta mahāprabhaḥ||24||

25apatat sahasā bhūmau visaṃjño'tha kṣudhārditaḥ
|
skandena so'bhyanujñāto raudrarūpo'bhavadgrahaḥ||25||

26skandāpasmāramityāhurgrahaṃ taṃ dvijasattamāḥ
|
vinatā tu mahāraudrā kathyate śakunigrahaḥ||26||

27pūtanāṃ rākṣasīṃ prāhustaṃ vidyāt pūtanāgraham
|
kaṣṭā dāruṇarūpeṇa ghorarūpā niśācarī||27||

28piśācī dāruṇākārā kathyate śītapūtanā
|
garbhān sā mānuṣīṇāṃ tu harate ghoradarśanā||28||

29aditiṃ revatīṃ prāhurgrahastasyāstu raivataḥ
|
so'pi bālān mahāghoro bādhate vai mahāgrahaḥ||29||

30daityānāṃ yā ditirmātā tāmāhurmukhamaṇḍikām
|
atyarthaṃ śiśumāṃsena samprahṛṣṭā durāsadā||30||

31kumārāśca kumāryaśca ye proktāḥ skandasambhavāḥ
|
te'pi garbhabhujaḥ sarve kauravya sumahāgrahāḥ||31||

32tāsāmeva tu patnīnāṃ patayaste prakīrtitāḥ
|
ājāyamānān gṛhṇanti bālakān raudrakarmiṇaḥ||32||

33gavāṃ mātā tu yā prājñaiḥ kathyate surabhirnupa
|
śakunistāmathāruhya saha bhuṅkte śiśūn bhuvi||33||

34saramā nāma yā mātā śunāṃ devī janādhipa
|
sā'pi garbhān samādatte mānuṣīṇāṃ sadaiva hi||34||

35pādapānāṃ ca yā mātā karañjanilayā hi sā
|
varadā sā hi saumyā ca nityaṃ bhūtānukampinī||35||

36karañje tāṃ namasyanti tasmāt putrārthino narāḥ
|
ime tvaṣṭādaśānye vai grahā māṃsamadhupriyāḥ||36||

37dvipañcarātraṃ tiṣṭhanti satataṃ sūtikāgṛhe
|
kadrūḥ sūkṣmavapurbhūtvā garbhiṇīṃ praviśatyatha||37||

38bhuṅkte sā tatra taṃ garbhaṃ sā tu nāgaṃ prasūyate
|
gandharvāṇāṃ tu yā mātā sā garbhaṃ gṛhya gacchati||38||

39tato vilīnagarbhā sā mānuṣī bhuvi dṛśyate
|
yā janitrī tvapsarasāṃ garbhamāste pragṛhya sā||39||

40upanaṣṭaṃ tato garbhaṃ kathayanti manīṣiṇaḥ
|
lohitasyodadheḥ kanyā dhātrī skandasya sā smṛtā||40||

41lohitāyanirityevaṃ kadambe sā hi pūjyate
|
puruṣeṣu yathā rudrastathā''ryāṃ pramadāsvapi||41||

42āryā mātā kumārasya pṛthakkāmārthamijyate
|
evamete kumārāṇāṃ mayā proktā mahāgrahāḥ||42||

43yāvat ṣoḍaśa varṣāṇi śiśūnāṃ hyaśivāstataḥ
|
ye ca mātṛgaṇāḥ proktāḥ puruṣāścaiva ye grahāḥ||43||

44sarve skandagrahā nāma jñeyā nityaṃ śarīribhiḥ
|
teṣāṃ praśamanaṃ kāryaṃ snānaṃ dhūpamathāñjanam
|
balikarmopahārāśca skandasyejyāviśeṣataḥ||44||

45evamabhyarcitāḥ sarve prayacchanti śubhaṃ nṛṇām
|
āyurvīryaṃ ca rājendra samyakpūjānamaskṛtāḥ||45||

46ūrdhvaṃ tu ṣoḍaśādvarṣādye bhavanti grahā nṛṇām
|
tānahaṃ sampravakṣyāmi namaskṛtya maheśvaram||46||

47yaḥ pakṣyati naro devān jāgradvā śayito'pi vā
|
unmādyati sa tu kṣipraṃ taṃ tu devagrahaṃ viduḥ||47||

48āsīnaśca śayānaśca yaḥ paśyati naraḥ pitṝn
|
unmādyati sa tu kṣipraṃ sa jñeyastu pitṛgrahaḥ||48||

49avamanyati yaḥ siddhān kruddhāścāpi śapanti yam
|
unmādyati sa tu kṣipraṃ jñeyaḥ siddhagrahastu saḥ||49||

50upāghrāti ca yo gandhān rasāṃścāpi pṛthagvidhān
|
unmādyati sa tu kṣipraṃ sa jñeyo rākṣaso grahaḥ||50||

51gandharvāścāpi yaṃ divyāḥ saṃviśanti naraṃ bhuvi
|
unmādyati sa tu kṣipraṃ graho gāndharva eva saḥ||51||

52adhirohanti yaṃ nityaṃ piśācāḥ puruṣaṃ prati
|
unmādyati sa tu kṣipraṃ grahaḥ paiśāca eva saḥ||52||

53āviśanti ca yaṃ yakṣāḥ puruṣaṃ kālaparyaye
|
unmādyati sa tu kṣipraṃ jñeyo yakṣagrahastu saḥ||53||

54yasya doṣaiḥ prakupitaṃ cittaṃ muhyati dehinaḥ
|
unmādyati sa tu kṣipraṃ sādhanaṃ tasya śāstrataḥ||54||

55vaiklavyācca bhayāccaiva ghorāṇāṃ cāpi darśanāt
|
unmādyati sa tu kṣipraṃ sāntvaṃ tasya tu sādhanam||55||

56kaścit krīḍitukāmo vai bhoktukāmastathā'paraḥ
|
abhikāmastathaivānya ityeṣa trividho grahaḥ||56||

57yāvat saptativarṣāṇi bhavantyete grahā nṛṇām
|
ataḥ paraṃ dehināṃ tu grahatulyo bhavejjvaraḥ||57||

58aprakīrṇendriyaṃ dāntaṃ śuciṃ nityamatandritam
|
āstikaṃ śraddadhānaṃ ca varjayanti sadā grahāḥ||58||

59ityeṣa te grahoddeśo mānuṣāṇāṃ prakīrtitaḥ
|
na spṛśanti grahā bhaktānnarān devaṃ maheśvaram||59||
