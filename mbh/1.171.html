Mahābhārata 1.171 (Ādiparvan)

1arjuna uvāca|
tāpatya iti yadvākyamuktavānasi māmiha
|
tadahaṃ jñātumicchāmi tāpatyārthaṃ viniścitam||1||

2tapatī nāma kā caiṣā tāpatyā yatkṛte vayam
|
kaunteyā hi vayaṃ sādho tattvamicchāmi veditum||2||

3vaiśaṃpāyana uvāca|
evamuktaḥ sa gandharvaḥ kuntīputraṃ dhanañjayam
|
viśrutaṃ triṣu lokeṣu śrāvayāmāsa vai kathām||3||

4gandharva uvāca|
hanta te kathayiṣyāmi kathāmetāṃ manoramām
|
yathāvadakhilāṃ pārtha sarvabuddhimatāṃ vara||4||

5uktavānasmi yena tvāṃ tāpatya iti yadvacaḥ
|
tatte'haṃ kathayiṣyāmi śṛṇuṣvaikamanā bhava||5||

6ya eṣa divi dhiṣṇyena nākaṃ vyāpnoti tejasā
|
etasya tapatī nāma babhūva sadṛśī sutā||6||

7vivasvato vai devasya sāvitryavarajā vibho
|
viśrutā triṣu lokeṣu tapatī tapasā yutā||7||

8na devī nāsurī caiva na yakṣī na ca rākṣasī
|
nāpsarā na ca gandharvī tathā rūpeṇa kācana||8||

9suvibhaktā'navadyāṅgī svasitāyatalocanā
|
svācārā caiva sādhvī ca suveṣā caiva bhāminī||9||

10ta tasyāḥ sadṛśaṃ kaṃcittriṣu lokeṣu bhārata
|
bhartāraṃ savitā mene rūpaśīlaguṇaśrutaiḥ||10||

11saṃprāptayauvanāṃ paśyandeyāṃ duhitaraṃ tu tām
|
nopalebhe tataḥ śāntiṃ saṃpradānaṃ vicintayan||11||

12atharkṣaputraḥ krānteya kurūṇāmṛṣabho balī
|
sūryamārādhayāmāsa nṛpaḥ saṃvaraṇastadā||12||

13ardhyamālyopahārādyairgandhaiśca niyataḥ śuciḥ
|
niyamairupavāsaiśca tapobhirvividhairapi||13||

14suśrūṣuranahaṃvādī śuciḥ pauravanandana
|
aṃśumantaṃ samudyantaṃ pūjayāmāsa bhaktimān||14||

15tataḥ kṛtajñaṃ dharmajñaṃ rūpeṇāsadṛśaṃ bhuvi
|
tapatyāḥ sadṛśaṃ mene sūryaḥ saṃvaraṇaṃ patim||15||

16dātumaicchattataḥ kanyāṃ tasmai saṃvaraṇāya tām
|
nṛpottamāya kauravya viśrutābhijanāya ca||16||

17yathā hi divi dīptāṃśuḥ prabhāsayati tejasā
|
tathā bhuvi mahipālo dīptyā saṃvaraṇo'bhavat||17||

18yathā'rcayanti cādityamudyantaṃ brahmavādinaḥ
|
tathā saṃvaraṇaṃ pārtha brāhmaṇāvarajāḥ prajāḥ||18||

19sa somamatikāntatvādādityamati tejasā
|
babhūva nṛpratiḥ śrīmānsuhṛdāṃ durhṛdāmapi||19||

20evaṃguṇasya nṛpatestathāvṛttasya kaurava
|
tasmai dātuṃ manaścakre tapatīṃ tapanaḥ svayam||20||

21sa kadācidatho rājā śrīmānamitavikramaḥ
|
cacāra mṛgayāṃ pārtha parvatopavane kila||21||

22carato mṛgayāṃ tasya kṣutpipāsāsamanvitaḥ
|
mamāra rājñaḥ kaunteya girāvapratimo hayaḥ||22||

23sa mṛtāśvaścaranpārtha padbhyāmeva girau nṛpaḥ
|
dadarśāsadṛśīṃ loke kanyāmāyatalocanām||23||

24sa eka ekāmāsādya kanyāṃ parabalārdanaḥ
|
tasthau nṛpatiśārdūlaḥ paśyannavicalekṣaṇaḥ||24||

25sa hi tāṃ tarkayāmāsa rūpato nṛpatiḥ śriyam
|
punaḥ saṃtarkayāmāsa raverbhraṣṭāmiva prabhām||25||

26vapuṣā varcasā caiva śikhāmiva vibhāvasoḥ
|
prasannatvena kāntyā ca candrarekhāmivāmalām||26||

27giripṛṣṭhe tu sā yasminsthitā svasitalocanā
|
vibhrājamānā śuśubhe pratimeva hiraṇmayī||27||

28tasyā rūpeṇa sa girirveṣeṇa ca viśeṣataḥ
|
sa savṛkṣakṣupalato hiraṇmaya ivābhavat||28||

29avamene ca tāṃ dṛṣṭvā sarvalokeṣu yoṣitaḥ
|
avāptaṃ cātmano mene sa rājā cakṣuṣaḥ phalam||29||

30janmaprabhṛti yatkiciṃddṛṣṭavānsa mahīpatiḥ
|
rūpaṃ na sadṛśaṃ tasyāstarkayāmāsa kiṃcana||30||

31tayā baddhamanaścakṣuḥ pāśairguṇamayaistadā
|
na cacāla tato deśādbubudhe na ca kiṃcana||31||

32asyā nūnaṃ viśālākṣyāḥ sadevāsuramānuṣam
|
lokaṃ nirmathya dhātredaṃ rūpamāviṣkṛtaṃ kṛtam||32||

33evaṃ saṃtarkayāmāsa rūpadraviṇasaṃpadā
|
kanyāmasadṛśīṃ loke nṛpaḥ saṃvaraṇastadā||33||

34tāṃ ca dṛṣṭvaiva kalyāṇīṃ kalyāṇābhijano nṛpaḥ
|
jagāma manasā cintāṃ kāmabāṇena pīḍitaḥ||34||

35dahyamānaḥ sa tīvreṇa nṛpatirmanmathāgninā
|
apragalbhāṃ pragalbhastāṃ tadovāca manoharām||35||

36kā'si kasyāsi rambhoru kimarthaṃ ceha tiṣṭhasi
|
kathaṃ ca nirjane'raṇye carasyekā śucismite||36||

37tvaṃ hi sarvānavadyāṅgī sarvābharaṇabhūṣitā
|
vibhūṣaṇamivaiteṣāṃ bhūṣaṇānāmabhīpsitam||37||

38na devīṃ nāsurīṃ caiva na yakṣīṃ na ca rākṣasīm
|
na ca bhogavatīṃ manye na gandhavīṃ na mānuṣīm||38||

39yā hi dṛṣṭvā mayā kāścicchrutā vā'pi varāṅganāḥ
|
na tāsāṃ sadṛśīṃ manye tvāmahaṃ mattakāśini||39||

40dṛṣṭvaiva cāruvadane candrātkāntataraṃ tava
|
vadanaṃ padmapatrākṣaṃ māṃ mathnātīva manmathaḥ||40||

41evaṃ tāṃ sa mahīpālo babhāṣe na tu sā tadā
|
kāmārtaṃ nirjane'raṇye pratyabāṣatha kiṃcana||41||

42tato lālapyamānasya pārthivasyāyatekṣaṇā
|
saudāminīva cābhreṣu tatraivāntaradhīyata||42||

43tāmanveṣṭuṃ sa nṛpatiḥ paricakrāma sarvataḥ
|
vanaṃ vanajapatrākṣīṃ bhramannunmattavattadā||43||

44apaśyamānaḥ sa tu tāṃ bahu tatra vilapya ca
|
niśceṣṭaḥ pārthivaśreṣṭho muhūrtaṃ sa vyatiṣṭhata||44||
