Mahābhārata 12.284 (Śāntiparvan)

1janamejaya uvāca|
prācetasasya dakṣasya kathaṃ vaivasvate'ntare
|
vināśamagamadbrahmanhayamedhaḥ prajāpateḥ ||1||

2devyā manyukṛtaṃ matvā kruddhaḥ sarvātmakaḥ prabhuḥ
|
prasādāttasya dakṣeṇa sa yajñaḥ sandhitaḥ katham
|
etadveditumiccheyaṃ tanme brūhi yathātatham ||2||

3vaiśampāyana uvāca|
purā himavataḥ pṛṣṭhe dakṣo vai yajñamāharat
|
gaṅgādvāre śubhe deśe ṛṣisiddhaniṣevite ||3||

4gandharvāpsarasākīrṇe nānādrumalatāvṛte
|
ṛṣisaṅghaiḥ parivṛtaṃ dakṣaṃ dharmabhṛtāṃ varam ||4||

5pṛthivyāmantarikṣe ca ye ca svarlokavāsinaḥ
|
sarve prāñjalayo bhūtvā upatasthuḥ prajāpatim ||5||

6devadānavagandharvāḥ piśācoragarākṣasāḥ
|
hāhāhūhūśca gandharvau tumbururnāradastathā ||6||

7viśvāvasurviśvaseno gandharvāpsarasastathā
|
ādityā vasavo rudrāḥ sādhyāḥ saha marudgaṇaiḥ ||7||

8indreṇa sahitāḥ sarve āgatā yajñabhāginaḥ
|
uṣmapāḥ somapāścaiva dhūmapā ājyapāstathā ||8||

9ṛṣayaḥ pitaraścaiva āgatā brahmaṇā saha
|
ete cānye ca bahavo bhūtagrāmāścaturvidhāḥ ||9||

10jarāyujāṇḍajāścaiva sahasā svedajodbhijaiḥ
|
āhūtā mantritāḥ sarve devāśca saha patnibhiḥ ||10||

11virājante vimānasthā dīpyamānā ivāgnayaḥ
|
tāndṛṣṭvā manyunā''viṣṭo dadhīcirvākyamabravīt ||11||

12nāyaṃ yajño na vā dharmo yatra rudro na ijyate
|
vadhabandhaṃ prapannā vai kinnu kālasya paryayaḥ ||12||

13kinnu mohānna paśyanti vināśaṃ paryupasthitam
|
upasthitaṃ mahāghoraṃ na budhyanti mahādhvare ||13||

14ityuktvā sa mahāyogī paśyati dhyānacakṣuṣā
|
sa paśyati mahādevaṃ devīṃ ca varadāṃ śubhām ||14||

15nāradaṃ ca mahātmānaṃ tasyā devyāḥ samīpataḥ
|
saṃtoṣaṃ paramaṃ lebhe iti niścitya yogavit ||15||

16ekamantrāstu te sarve yeneśo na nimantritaḥ
|
tasmāddeśādapakramya dadhīcirvākyamabravīt ||16||

17apūjyapūjanāccaiva pūjyānāṃ cāpyapūjanāt
|
nṛghātakasamaṃ pāpaṃ śaśvatprāpnoti mānavaḥ ||17||

18anṛtaṃ noktapūrvaṃ me na ca vakṣye kadācana
|
devatānāmṛṣīṇāṃ ca madhye satyaṃ bravīmyaham ||18||

19āgataṃ paśubhartāraṃ sraṣṭāraṃ jagataḥ patim
|
adhvare hyagrabhoktāraṃ sarveṣāṃ paśyata prabhum ||19||

20dakṣa uvāca |
santi no bahavo rudrāḥ śūlahastāḥ kapardinaḥ
|
ekādaśasthānagatā nāhaṃ vedmi maheśvaram ||20||

21dadhīciruvāca |
sarveṣāmeva mantro'yaṃ yenāsau na nimantritaḥ
|
yathā'haṃ śaṃkarādūrdhvaṃ nānyaṃ paśyāmi daivatam
|
tathā dakṣasya vipulo yajño'yaṃ na bhaviṣyati ||21||

22dakṣa uvāca |
etanmakheśāya suvarṇapātre haviḥ samastaṃ vidhimantrapūtam
|
viṣṇornayāmyapratimasya bhāgaṃ prabhurvibhuścāhavanīya eṣaḥ ||22||

23devyuvāca |
kiṃ nāma dānaṃ niyamaṃ tapo vā kuryāmahaṃ yena patirmamādya
|
labheta bhāgaṃ bhagavānacintyo hyardhaṃ tathā bhāgamatho tṛtīyam ||23||

24evaṃ bruvāṇāṃ bhagavānsa patnīṃ prahṛṣṭarūpaḥ kṣubhitāmuvāca
|
na vetsi māṃ devi kṛśodarāṅgi kiṃ nāma yuktaṃ vacanaṃ makheśe ||24||

25ahaṃ vijānāmi viśālanetre dhyānena hīnā na vidantyasantaḥ
|
tavādya mohena ca sendradevā lokāstrayaḥ sarvata eva mūḍhāḥ ||25||

26māmadhvare śaṃsitāraḥ stuvanti rathantaraṃ sāmagāścopagānti
|
māṃ brāhmaṇā brahmavido yajante mamādhvaryavaḥ kalpayante ca bhāgam ||26||

27devyuvāca |
suprākṛto'pi puruṣaḥ sarvastrījanasaṃsadi
|
stauti garvāyate cāpi svamātmānaṃ na saṃśayaḥ ||27||

28bhagavānuvāca |
nātmānaṃ staumi deveśi paśya me tanumadhyame
|
yaṃ vakṣyāmi varārohe yāgārthe varavarṇini ||28||

29ityuktvā bhagavānpatnīmumāṃ prāṇairapi priyām
|
so'sṛjadbhagavānvaktrādbhūtaṃ ghoraṃ praharṣaṇam ||29||

30tamuvācākṣipa makhaṃ dakṣasyeti maheśvaraḥ
|
tato vaktrādvimuktena siṃhenaikena līlayā ||30||

31devyā manyuvyapohārthaṃ hato dakṣasya vai kratuḥ
|
manyunā ca mahābhīmā mahākālī maheśvarī ||31||

32ātmanaḥ karmasākṣitve tena sārdhaṃ sahānugā
|
devasyānumataṃ matvā praṇamya śirasā tataḥ ||32||

33ātmanaḥ sadṛśaḥ śauryādbalarūpasamanvitaḥ
|
sa eva bhagavān krodhaḥ pratirūpasamanvitaḥ ||33||

34anantabalavīryaśca anantabalapauruṣaḥ
|
vīrabhadra iti khyāto devyā manyupramārjakaḥ ||34||

35so'sṛjadromakūpebhyo raumyānnāma gaṇeśvarān
|
rudratulyā gaṇā raudrā rudravīryaparākramāḥ ||35||

36te nipetustatastūrṇaṃ dakṣayajñavihiṃsayā
|
bhīmarūpā mahākāyāḥ śataśo'tha sahasraśaḥ ||36||

37tataḥ kalakilāśabdairākāśaṃ pūrayanniva
|
tena śabdena mahatā trastāstatra divaukasaḥ ||37||

38parvatāśca vyaśīryanta cakampe ca vasundharā
|
mārutāścaiva ghūrṇante cukṣubhe varuṇālayaḥ ||38||

39agnayo naiva dīpyante naiva dīpyati bhāskaraḥ
|
grahā naiva prakāśante nakṣatrāṇi na candramāḥ ||39||

40ṛṣayo na prakāśante na devā na ca mānuṣāḥ
|
evaṃ tu timirībhūtaṃ nirdahantyapamānitāḥ ||40||

41praharantyapare ghorā yūpānutpāṭayanti ca
|
pramardanti tathā cānye vimardanti tathā pare ||41||

42ādhāvanti pradhāvanti vāyuvegā manojavāḥ
|
cūrṇyante yajñapātrāṇi divyānyābharaṇāni ca ||42||

43viśīryamāṇā dṛśyante tārā iva nabhastale
|
divyānnapānabhakṣyāṇāṃ rāśayaḥ parvatopamāḥ ||43||

44kṣīranadyo'tha dṛśyante ghṛtapāyasakardamāḥ
|
dadhimaṇḍodakā divyāḥ khaṇḍaśarkaravālukāḥ ||44||

45ṣaḍrasā nivahantyetā guḍakulyā manoramāḥ
|
uccāvacāni māṃsāni bhakṣyāṇi vividhāni ca ||45||

46pānakāni ca divyāni lehyacoṣyāṇi yāni ca
|
bhuñjate vividhairvaktrairvilumpantyākṣipanti ca ||46||

47rudrakopānmahākāyāḥ kālāgnisadṛśopamāḥ
|
kṣobhayansurasainyāni bhīṣayantaḥ samantataḥ ||47||

48krīḍanti vividhākārāścikṣipuḥ surayoṣitaḥ
|
rudrakodhātprayatnena sarvadevaiḥ surakṣitam ||48||

49taṃ yajñamadahacchīghraṃ rudrakarmā sabhantataḥ
|
cakāra bhairavaṃ nādaṃ sarvabhūtabhavaṃkaram ||49||

50chittvā śiro vai yajñasya nanāda ca mumoda ca
|
tato brahmādayo devā dakṣaścaiva prajāpatiḥ ||50||

51ūcuḥ prājjalayaḥ sarve kathyatāṃ ko bhavāniti
|
vīrabhadra uvāca |
nāhaṃ rudro na vā devī naiva bhoktumihāgataḥ ||51||

52devyā manyukṛtaṃ matvā kruddhaḥ sarvātmakaḥ prabhuḥ
|
draṣṭuṃ vā naiva viprendrānnaiva kautūhalena vā ||52||

53tava yajñavighātārthaṃ samprāptaṃ viddhi māmiha
|
vīrabhadra iti khyāto rudrakopādviniḥsṛtaḥ ||53||

54bhadrakālīti vikhyātā devyāḥ kopādviniḥsṛtā
|
preṣitau devadevena yajñāntikamihāgatau ||54||

55śaraṇaṃ gaccha viprendra devadevamumāpatim
|
varaṃ krodho'pi devasya varadānaṃ na cānyataḥ ||55||

56vīrabhadravacaḥ śrutvā dakṣo dharmabhṛtāṃ varaḥ
|
toṣayāmāsa stotreṇa praṇipatya maheśvaram ||56||

57prapadye devamīśānaṃ śāśvataṃ dhruvamavyayam
|
mahādevaṃ mahātmānaṃ viśvasya jagataḥ patim ||57||

58dakṣaprajāpateryajñe dravyaistaiḥ susamāhitaiḥ
|
āhūtā devatāḥ sarvā ṛṣayaśca tapodhanāḥ ||58||

59devo nāhūyate tatra viśvakarmā maheśvaraḥ
|
tatra kruddhā mahādevī gaṇāṃstatra vyasarjayat ||59||

60pradīptayajñavāṭe tu vidruteṣu dvijātiṣu
|
tārāgaṇamanuprāpte raudre dīpte mahātmani ||60||

61śūlanirbhinnahṛdayaiḥ kūjadbhiḥ paricārakaiḥ
|
nikhātotpāṭitairyūpairapaviddhairitastataḥ ||61||

62utpatadbhiḥ patadbhiśca gṛdhrairāmiṣagṛddhibhiḥ
|
pakṣapātavinirdhūtaiḥ śivāśatanināditaiḥ ||62||

63yakṣagandharvasaṃghaiśca piśācoragarākṣasaiḥ
|
prāṇāpānau saṃnirudhya vaktrasthānena yatnataḥ ||63||

64vicārya sarvato dṛṣṭiṃ bahudṛṣṭiramitrajit
|
sahasā devadeveśo hyagnikuṇḍātsamutthitaḥ ||64||

65bibhratsūryasahasrasya tejaḥ saṃvartakopamaḥ
|
smitaṃ kṛtvābravīdvākyaṃ brūhi kiṃ karavāṇi te ||65||

66śrāvite ca makhādhyāye devānāṃ guruṇā tataḥ
|
tumavācāñjaliṃ kṛtvā dakṣo devaṃ prajāpatiḥ ||66||

67bhītaśaṅkitavitrastaḥ sabāṣpavadanekṣaṇaḥ
|
yadi prasanno bhagavānyadi cāhaṃ bhavatpriyaḥ ||67||

68yadi vā'hamanugrāhyo yadi vā varado mama
|
yaddagdhaṃ bhakṣitaṃ pītaśitaṃ yacca nāśitam ||68||

69cūrṇīkṛtāpaviddhaṃ ca yajñasambhāramīdṛśam
|
dīrghakālena mahatā prayatnena susaṃcitam
|
tanna mithyābhavenmahyaṃ varametamahaṃ vṛṇe ||69||

70tathā'stvityāha bhagavānbhaganetraharo haraḥ
|
dharmādhyakṣo virūpākṣastryakṣo devaḥ prajāpatiḥ ||70||

71jānubhyāmavanīṃ gatvā dakṣo labdhvā bhavādvaram
|
nāmnāmaṣṭasahasreṇa stutavānvṛṣabhadhvajam ||71||

72yudhiṣṭhira uvāca|
yairnāmadheyaiḥ stutavāndakṣo devaṃ prajāpatiḥ
|
vaktumarhasi me tāta śrotuṃ śraddhā mamānagha ||72||

73bhīṣma uvāca|
śrūyatāṃ devadevasya nāmānyadbhutakarmaṇaḥ
|
gūḍhavratasya guhyāni prakāśāni ca bhārata ||73||

74namaste devadeveśa devāribalasūdana
|
devendrabalaviṣṭambha devadānavapūjita ||74||

75sahasrākṣa virūpākṣa tryakṣa yakṣādhipapriya
|
sarvataḥpāṇipādānta sarvato'kṣiśiromukha ||75||

76sarvataḥśrutima~lloke sarvamāvṛtya tiṣṭhasi
|
śaṅkhakarṇa mahākarṇa kumbhakarṇārṇavālaya ||76||

77gajendrakarṇa gokarṇa pāṇikarṇa namo'stu te
|
śatodara śatāvarta śatajihva namo'stu te ||77||

78gāyanti tvā gāyatriṇo'rcantyarkamarkiṇaḥ
|
brahmāṇaṃ tvāṃ śatakratumūrdhvaṃ khamiva menire ||78||

79mūrtau hi te mahāmūrte samudrāmbarasannibha
|
sarvā vai devatā hyasmingāvo goṣṭha ivāsate ||79||

80bhavaccharīre paśyāmi somamagniṃ jaleśvaram
|
ādityamatha vai viṣṇuṃ brahmāṇaṃ ca bṛhaspatim ||80||

81bhagavānkāraṇaṃ kārya kriyā karaṇameva ca
|
asataśca sataścaiva tathaiva prabhavāpyayau ||81||

82namo bhavāya śarvāya rudrāya varadāya ca
|
paśūnāṃ pataye nityaṃ namo'stvandhakaghātine ||82||

83trijaṭāya triśīrṣāya triśūlavarapāṇine
|
tryambakāya trinetrāya tripuraghnāya vai namaḥ ||83||

84namaścaṇḍāya kuṇḍāya aṇḍāyāṇḍadharāya ca
|
daṇḍine samakarṇāya daṇḍimuṇḍāya vai namaḥ ||84||

85namordhvadaṃṣṭrakeśāya śuklāyāvatatāya ca
|
vilohitāya dhūmrāya nīlagrīvāya vai namaḥ ||85||

86namo'stvapratirūpāya virūpāya śivāya ca
|
sūryāya sūryamālāya sūryadhvajapatākine ||86||

87namaḥ pramathanāthāya vṛṣaskandhāya dhanvine
|
śatruṃ damāya daṇḍāya parṇacīrapaṭāya ca ||87||

88namo hiraṇyagarbhāya hiraṇyakavacāya ca
|
hiraṇyakṛtacūḍāya hiraṇyapataye namaḥ ||88||

89namaḥ stutāya stutyāya stūyamānāya vai namaḥ
|
sarvāya sarvabhakṣāya sarvabhūtāntarātmane ||89||

90namo hotre'tha mantrāya śukladhvajapatākine
|
namo nābhāya nābhyāya namaḥ kaṭakaṭāya ca ||90||

91namo'stu kṛśanāsāya kṛśāṅgāya kṛśāya ca
|
saṃhṛṣṭāya vihṛṣṭāya namaḥ kilakilāya ca ||91||

92namo'stu śayamānāya śayitāyotthitāya ca
|
sthitāya dhāvamānāya muṇḍāya jaṭilāya ca ||92||

93namo nartanaśīlāya mukhavāditravādine
|
nādyopahāralubdhāya gītavāditraśāline ||93||

94namo jyeṣṭhāya śreṣṭhāya balapramathanāya ca
|
kālanāthāya kalyāya kṣayāyopakṣayāya ca ||94||

95bhīmadundubhihāsāya bhīmavratadharāya ca
|
ugrāya ca namo nityaṃ namo'stu daśabāhave ||95||

96namaḥ kapālahastāya citibhasmapriyāya ca
|
vibhīṣaṇāya bhīṣmāya bhīmavratadharāya ca ||96||

97namo vikṛtavaktrāya khaḍgajihvāya daṃṣṭriṇe
|
pakvāmamāṃsalubdhāya tumbīvīṇāpriyāya ca ||97||

98namo vṛṣāya vṛṣyāya govṛṣāya vṛṣāya ca
|
kaṭaṃkaṭāya daṇḍāya namaḥ pacapacāya ca ||98||

99namaḥ sarvavariṣṭhāya varāya varadāya ca
|
varamālyagandhavastrāya varātivarade namaḥ ||99||

100namo raktaviraktāya bhāvanāyākṣamāline
|
sambhinnāya vibhinnāya chāyāyātapanāya ca ||100||

101aghoraghorarūpāya ghoraghoratarāya ca
|
namaḥ śivāya śāntāya namaḥ śāntatamāya ca ||101||

102ekapādbahunetrāya ekaśīrṣṇe namo'stu te
|
rudrāya kṣudralubdhāya saṃvibhāgapriyāya ca ||102||

103pañcālāya sitāṅgāya namaḥ śamaśamāya ca
|
namaścaṇḍikaghaṇṭāya ghaṇṭāyāghaṇṭaghaṇṭine ||103||

104sahasrādhmātaghaṇṭāya ghaṇṭāmālāpriyāya ca
|
prāṇaghaṇṭāya gandhāya namaḥ kalakalāya ca ||104||

105hūṃhūṃhūṃkārapārāya hūṃhūṃkārapriyāya ca
|
namaḥ śamaśame nityaṃ girivṛkṣālayāya ca ||105||

106garbhamāṃsasṛgālāya tārakāya tarāya ca
|
namo yajñāya yajine hutāya prahutāya ca ||106||

107yajñavāhāya dāntāya tapyāyātapanāya ca
|
namastaṭāya taṭyāya taṭānāṃ pataye namaḥ ||107||

108annadāyānnapataye namastvannabhuje tathā
|
namaḥ sahasraśīrṣāya sahasracaraṇāya ca ||108||

109sahasrodyataśūlāya sahasranayanāya ca
|
namo bālārkavarṇāya bālarūpadharāya ca ||109||

110bālānucaragoptāya bālakrīḍanakāya ca
|
namo vṛddhāya lubdhāya kṣubdhāya kṣobhaṇāya ca ||110||

111taraṅgāṅkitakeśāya muñjakeśāya vai namaḥ
|
namaḥ ṣaṭkarmatuṣṭāya trikarmaniratāya ca ||111||

112varṇāśramāṇāṃ vidhivatpṛthakkarmanivartine
|
namo ghuṣyāya ghoṣāya namaḥ kalakalāya ca ||112||

113śvetapiṅgalanetrāya kṛṣṇaraktekṣaṇāya ca
|
prāṇabhagnāya daṇḍāya sphoṭanāya kṛśāya ca ||113||

114dharmakāmārthamokṣāṇāṃ kathanīyakathāya ca
|
sāṃkhyāya sāṃkhyamukhyāya sāṃkhyayogapravartine ||114||

115namo rathyavirathyāya catuṣpatharathāya ca
|
kṛṣṇājinottarīyāya vyālayajñopavītine ||115||

116īśāna vajrasaṃghāta harikeśa namo'stu te
|
tryambakāmbikanāthāya vyaktāvyakta namo'stu te ||116||

117kāma kāmada kāmaghna tṛptātṛptavicāriṇe
|
sarva sarvada sarvaghna sandhyārāga namo'stu te ||117||

118mahāmeghacayaprakhya mahākāla namo'stu te
|
sthūlajīrṇāṅgajaṭile valkalājinadhāriṇe ||118||

119dīptasūryāgnijaṭile valkalājinavāsase
|
sahasrasūryapratima taponitya namo'stu te ||119||

120unmādanaśatāvarta gaṅgātoyārdramūrdhaja
|
candrāvarta yugāvarta meghāvarta namo'stu te ||120||

121tvamannamattā bhoktā ca annado'nnabhugeva ca
|
annasraṣṭā ca paktā ca pakvabhukpavano'nalaḥ ||121||

122jarāyujāṇḍajāścaiva svedajāśca tathodbhijāḥ
|
tvameva devadeveśa bhūtagrāmaścaturvidhaḥ ||122||

123carācarasya sraṣṭā tvaṃ pratihartā tathaiva ca
|
tvāmāhurbrahmaviduṣo brahma brahmavidāṃ vara ||123||

124manasaḥ paramā yoniḥ khaṃ vāyuryotiṣāṃ nidhiḥ
|
ṛksāmāni tathoṅkāramāhustvāṃ brahmavādinaḥ ||124||

125hāyihāyihuvāhāyihāvuhāyi tathā'sakṛt
|
gāyanti tvāṃ suraśreṣṭha sāmagā brahmavādinaḥ ||125||

126yajurmayo ṛṅmayaśca tvamāhutimayastathā
|
paṭhyase stutibhiścaiva vedopaniṣadāṃ gaṇaiḥ ||126||

127brāhmaṇāḥ kṣatriyā vaiśyāḥ śūdrā varṇāvarāśca ye
|
tvameva meghasaṃghāśca vidyutstanitagarjitaḥ ||127||

128saṃvatsarastvamṛtavo māso māsārdhameva ca
|
yugaṃ nimeṣāḥ kāṣṭhāstvaṃ nakṣatrāṇi grahāḥ kalāḥ ||128||

129vṛkṣāṇāṃ kakudo'si tvaṃ girīṇāṃ śikharāṇi ca
|
vyāghro mṛgāṇāṃ patatāṃ tārkṣyo'nantaśca bhoginām ||129||

130kṣīrodo hṛdadhīnāṃ ca yantrāṇāṃ dhanureva ca
|
vajraḥ praharaṇānāṃ ca vratānāṃ satyameva ca ||130||

131tvameva dveṣa icchā ca rāgo mohaḥ kṣamākṣame
|
vyavasāyo dhṛtirlobhaḥ kāmakrodhau jayājayau ||131||

132tvaṃ gadī tvaṃ śarī cāpī khaṭvāṅgī jharjharī tathā
|
chettā bhettā prahartā tvaṃ netā mantā pitā mataḥ ||132||

133daśalakṣaṇasaṃyukto dharmo'rthaḥ kāma eva ca
|
gaṅgā samudrāḥ saritaḥ palvalāni sarāṃsi ca ||133||

134latā valyastṛṇauṣadhyaḥ paśavo mṛgapakṣiṇaḥ
|
dravyakarmasamārambhaḥ kālaḥ puṣpaphalapradaḥ ||134||

135ādiścāntaśca devānāṃ gāyatryoṅkāra eva ca
|
harito rohito nīlaḥ kṛṣṇo raktastathāruṇaḥ
|
kadruśca kapilaścaiva kapoto mecakastathā ||135||

136avarṇaśca suvarṇaśca varṇakāro ghanopamaḥ
|
suvarṇanāmā ca tathā suvarṇapriya eva ca ||136||

137tvamindraśca yamaścaiva varuṇo dhanado'nalaḥ
|
upaplavaścitrabhānuḥ svarbhānurbhānureva ca ||137||

138hotraṃ hotā ca homyaṃ ca hutaṃ caiva tathā prabhuḥ
|
trisauparṇaṃ tathā brahma yajuṣāṃ śatarudriyam ||138||

139pavitraṃ ca pavitrāṇāṃ maṅgalānāṃ ca maṅgalam
|
giriko hiṃḍuko vṛkṣo jīvaḥ pudgala eva ca ||139||

140prāṇaḥ sattvaṃ rajaścaiva tamaścāpramadastathā
|
prāṇo'pānaḥ samānaśca udāno vyāna eva ca ||140||

141unmeṣaśca nimeṣaśca kṣutaṃ jṛmbhitameva ca
|
lohitāntargatā dṛṣṭirmahāvaktro mahodaraḥ ||141||

142sūcīromā hariśmaśrurdhvakeśaścalācalaḥ
|
gītavāditratattvajño gītavādanakapriyaḥ ||142||

143matsyo jalacaro jālyo'kalaḥ kelikalaḥ kaliḥ
|
akālaścātikālaśca duṣkālaḥ kāla eva ca ||143||

144mṛtyuḥ kṣuraśca kṛtyaśca pakṣo pakṣakṣayaṃkaraḥ
|
meghakālo mahādaṃṣṭraḥ saṃvartakabalāhakaḥ ||144||

145ghaṇṭo'ghaṇṭo ghaṭī ghaṇṭī carucelī milīmilī
|
brahmakāyikamagnīnāṃ daṇḍī muṇḍastridaṇḍadhṛk ||145||

146caturyugaścaturvedaścāturhotrapravartakaḥ
|
cāturāśramyanetā ca cāturvarṇyakaraśca yaḥ ||146||

147sadā cākṣapriyo dhūrto gaṇādhyakṣo gaṇādhipaḥ
|
raktamālyāmbaradharo giriśo girikapriyaḥ ||147||

148śilpikaḥ śilpināṃ śreṣṭhaḥ sarvaśilpapravartakaḥ
|
bhaganetrāṅkuśaścaṇḍaḥ pūṣṇo dantavināśanaḥ ||148||

149svāhā svadhā vaṣaṭkāro namaskāro namo namaḥ
|
gūḍhavato guhyatapāstārakastārakāmayaḥ ||149||

150dhātā vidhātā saṃdhātā vidhātā dhāraṇo'dharaḥ
|
brahmā tapaśca satyaṃ ca brahmacaryamathārjavam ||150||

151bhūtātmā bhūtakṛdbhūto bhūtabhavyabhavodbhavaḥ
|
bhūrbhuvaḥ svaritaścaiva dhruvo dānto maheśvaraḥ ||151||

152dīkṣito'dīkṣitaḥ kṣānto durdānto'dāntanāśanaḥ
|
candrāvartayugāvartaḥ saṃvartaḥ sampravartakaḥ ||152||

153kāmo binduraṇuḥ sthūlaḥ karṇikārasrajapriyaḥ
|
nandīmukho bhīmamukhaḥ sumukho durmukho'mukhaḥ ||153||

154caturmukho bahumukho raṇeṣvagnimukhastathā
|
hiraṇyagarbhaḥ śakunirmahoragapatirvirāṭ ||154||

155adharmahā mahāpārśvaścaṇḍadhāro gaṇādhipaḥ
|
gonardo gopratāraśca govṛṣeśvaravāhanaḥ ||155||

156trailokyagoptā govindo gomārgo'mārga eva ca
|
śreṣṭhaḥ sthiraśca sthāṇuśca niṣkampaḥ kampa eva ca ||156||

157durvāraṇo durviṣaho duḥsaho duratikramaḥ
|
durdharṣo duṣprakampaśca durviṣo durjayo jayaḥ ||157||

158śaśaḥ śaśāṅkaḥ śamanaḥ śītoṣṇakṣujjarādhikṛt
|
ādhayo vyādhayaścaiva vyādhahā vyādhireva ca ||158||

159mama yajñamṛgavyādho vyādhīnāmāgamo gamaḥ
|
śikhaṇḍī puṇḍarīkākṣaḥ puṇḍarīkavanālayaḥ ||159||

160daṇḍadhārastryambakaśca ugradaṇḍo'ṇḍanāśanaḥ
|
viṣāgnipāḥ suraśreṣṭhaḥ somapāstvaṃ marutpatiḥ ||160||

161amṛtapāstvaṃ jagannātha devadeva gaṇeśvaraḥ
|
viṣāgnipā mṛtyupāśca kṣīrapāḥ somapāstathā
|
madhuścyutānāmagrapāstvaṃ tvameva tuṣitādyapāḥ ||161||

162hiraṇyaretāḥ puruṣastvameva tvaṃ strī pumāṃstvaṃ ca napuṃsakaṃ ca
|
bālo yuvā sthaviro jīrṇadaṃṣṭrastvaṃ nāgendra śakrastvaṃ viśvakṛdviśvakartā ||162||

163viśvakṛdviśvakṛtāṃ vareṇyastvaṃ viśvabāho viśvarūpastejasvī viśvatomukhaḥ
|
candrādityau cakṣuṣī te hṛdayaṃ ca pitāmahaḥ ||163||

164mahodadhiḥ sarasvatī vāgbalamanalo'nilaḥ ahorātraṃ nimeṣonmeṣakarma ||164||

165na brahmā na ca govindaḥ paurāṇā ṛṣayo na te
|
māhātmyaṃ vedituṃ śaktā yāthātathyena te śiva ||165||

166yā mūrtayaḥ susūkṣmāste na mahyaṃ yānti darśanam
|
trāhi māṃ satataṃ rakṣa pitā putramivaurasam ||166||

167rakṣa māṃ rakṣaṇīyo'haṃ tavānagha namo'stu te
|
bhaktānukampī bhagavānbhaktaścāhaṃ sadā tvayi ||167||

168yaḥ sahasrāṇyanekāni puṃsāmāvṛtya durdṛśaḥ
|
tiṣṭhatyekaḥ samudrānte sa me goptāstu nityaśaḥ ||168||

169yaṃ vinidrā jitaśvāsāḥ sattvasthāḥ saṃyatendriyāḥ
|
jyotiḥ paśyanti yuñjānāstasmai yogātmane namaḥ ||169||

170jaṭile daṇḍine nityaṃ lambodaraśarīriṇe
|
kamaṇḍaluniṣaṅgāya tasmai brahmātmane namaḥ ||170||

171yasya keśeṣu jīmūtā nadyaḥ sarvāṅgasandhiṣu
|
kukṣau samudrāścatvārastasmai toyātmane namaḥ ||171||

172sambhakṣya sarvabhūtāni yugānte paryupasthite
|
yaḥ śete jalamadhyasthastaṃ prapadye'mbuśāyinam ||172||

173praviśya vadanaṃ rāhoryaḥ somaṃ pibate niśi
|
grasatyarkaṃ ca svarbhānurbhūtvā māṃ so'bhirakṣatu ||173||

174ye cānupatitā garbhā yathā bhāgānupāsate
|
namastebhyaḥ svadhā svāhā prāpnuvantu mudantu te ||174||

175ye'ṅguṣṭhamātrāḥ puruṣā dehasthāḥ sarvadehinām
|
rakṣantu te hi māṃ nityaṃ nityaṃ cāpyāyayantu mām ||175||

176yena rodanti dehasthā dehino rodayanti ca
|
harṣayanti na hṛṣyanti namastebhyo'stu nityaśaḥ ||176||

177ye nadīṣu samudreṣu parvateṣu guhāsu ca
|
vṛkṣāleṣu goṣṭheṣu kāntāre gahaneṣu ca ||177||

178catuṣpatheṣu rathyāsu catvareṣu taṭeṣu ca
|
hastyaśvarathaśālāsu jīrṇodyānālayeṣu ca ||178||

179yeṣu pañcasu bhūteṣu diśāsu vidiśāsu ca
|
candrārkayormadhyagatā ye ca candrārkaraśmiṣu ||179||

180rasātalagatā ye ca ye ca tasmai paraṃ gatāḥ
|
namastebhyo namastebhyo namastebhyo'stu nityaśaḥ ||180||

181yeṣāṃ na vidyate saṃkhyā pramāṇaṃ rūpameva ca
|
asaṃkhyeyaguṇā rudrā namastebhyo'stu nityaśaḥ ||181||

182sarvabhūtakaro yasmātsarvabhūtapatirharaḥ
|
sarvabhūtāntarātmā ca tena tvaṃ na nimantritaḥ ||182||

183tvameva hījyase yasmādyajñairvividhadakṣiṇaiḥ
|
tvameva kartā sarvasya tena tvaṃ na nimantritaḥ ||183||

184athavā māyayā deva sūkṣmayā tava mohitaḥ
|
etasmātkāraṇādvāpi tena tvaṃ na nimintritaḥ ||184||

185prasīda mama bhadraṃ te bhava bhāvagatasya me
|
tvayi me hṛdayaṃ deva tvayi buddhirmanastvayi ||185||

186stutvaivaṃ sa mahādevaṃ virarāma prajāpatiḥ
|
bhagavānapi suprītaḥ punardakṣamabhāṣata ||186||

187parituṣṭo'smi te dakṣa stavenānena suvrata
|
bahunātra kimuktena matsamīpe bhaviṣyasi ||187||

188aśvamedhasahasrasya vājapeyaśatasya ca
|
prajāpate matprasādātphalabhāgī bhaviṣyasi ||188||

189athainamabravīdvākyaṃ lokasyādhipatirbhavaḥ
|
āśvāsanakaraṃ vākyaṃ vākyavidvākyasammatam ||189||

190dakṣa dakṣa na kartavyo manyurvighnamimaṃ prati
|
ahaṃ yajñaharastubhyaṃ dṛṣṭametatpurātanam ||190||

191bhūyaśca te varaṃ dadmi taṃ tvaṃ gṛhṇīṣva suvrata
|
prasannavadano bhūtvā tadihaikamanāḥ śṛṇu ||191||

192vedātṣaḍaṅgāduddhṛtya sāṃkhyayogācca yuktitaḥ
|
tapaḥ sutaptaṃ vipulaṃ duścaraṃ devadānavaiḥ ||192||

193apūrvaṃ sarvatobhadraṃ sarvatomukhamavyayam
|
abdairdaśāhasaṃyuktaṃ gūḍhamaprājñaninditam ||193||

194varṇāśramakṛtairdharmairviparītaṃ kvacitsamam
|
gatāntaradhyavasitamatyāśramamidaṃ vratam ||194||

195mayā pāśupataṃ dakṣa śubhamutpāditaṃ purā
|
tasya cīrṇasya tatsamyakphalaṃ bhavati puṣkalam
|
taccāstu te mahābhāga tyajyatāṃ mānaso jvaraḥ ||195||

196evamuktvā mahādevaḥ sapatnīkaḥ sahānugaḥ
|
adarśanamanuprāpto dakṣasyāmitavikramaḥ ||196||

197dakṣaproktaṃ stavamimaṃ kīrtayedyaḥ śṛṇoti vā
|
nāśubhaṃ prāpnuyātkiṃciddīrghamāyuravāpnuyāt ||197||

198yathā sarveṣu deveṣu variṣṭhe bhagavāñchivaḥ
|
tathā stavo variṣṭho'yaṃ stavānāṃ brahmasammitaḥ ||198||

199yaśorājyasukhaiśvaryakāmārthadhanakāṃkṣibhiḥ
|
śrotavyo bhaktimāsthāya vidyākāmaiśca yatnataḥ ||199||

200vyādhito duḥkhito dīnaścoragrasto bhayārditaḥ
|
rājakāryābhiyukto vā mucyate mahato bhayāt ||200||

201anenaiva tu dehena gaṇānāṃ samatāṃ vrajet
|
tejasā yaśasā caiva yukto bhavati nirmalaḥ ||201||

202na rākṣasāḥ piśācā vā na bhūtā na vināyakāḥ
|
vighnaṃ kuryurguhe tasya yatrāyaṃ paṭhyate stavaḥ ||202||

203śṛṇuyāccaiva yā nārī tadbhaktā brahmacāriṇī
|
pitṛpakṣe mārtṛpakṣe pūjyā bhavati devavat ||203||

204śṛṇuyādyaḥ stavaṃ kṛtsnaṃ kīrtayedvā samāhitaḥ
|
tasya sarvāṇi karmāṇi siddhiṃ gacchantyabhīkṣṇaśaḥ ||204||

205manasā cintitaṃ yacca yacca vācā'nukīrtitam
|
sarvaṃ sampadyate tasya stavasyāsyānukīrtanāt ||205||

206devasya ca guhasyāpi devyā nandīśvarasya ca
|
baliṃ suvihitaṃ kṛtvā damena niyamena ca ||206||

207tatastu yukto gṛhṇayānnāmānyāśu yathākramam
|
īpsitān labhate so'rthān bhogānkāmāṃśca mānavaḥ ||207||

208mṛtaśca svargamāpnoti tiryakṣu ca na jāyate
|
ityāha bhagavānvyāsaḥ parāśarasutaḥ prabhuḥ ||208||
