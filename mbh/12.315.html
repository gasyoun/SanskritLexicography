Mahābhārata 12.315 (Śāntiparvan)

1yājñavalkya uvāca |
na śakyo nirguṇastāta guṇīkartuṃ viśāmpate
|
guṇavāṃścāpyaguṇavānyathātattvaṃ nibodha me||1||

2guṇairhi guṇavānena nirguṇaścāguṇastathā
|
prāhurevaṃ mahātmāno munayastattvadarśinaḥ||2||

3guṇasvabhāvastvavyakto guṇānnaivātivartate
|
upayuṅkte ca tāneva sa caivājñaḥ svabhāvataḥ||3||

4avyaktastu na jānīte puruṣo'jñaḥ svabhāvataḥ
|
na mattaḥ paramo'stīti nityamevābhimanyate||4||

5anena kāraṇenaitadavyaktaṃ syādacetanam
|
nityatvāccākṣaratvācca kṣaratvānna tadanyathā||5||

6yadā'jñānena kurvīta guṇasargaṃ punaḥ punaḥ
|
yadātmānaṃ na jānīte tadātmāpi na mucyate||6||

7kartṛtvāccāpi sargāṇāṃ sargadharmā tathocyate
|
kartṛtvāccāpi yogānāṃ yogadharmā tathocyate||7||

8kartṛtvātprakṛtīnāṃ ca tathā prakṛtidharmitā||8||

9kartṛtvāccāpi bījānāṃ bījadharmā tathocyate
|
guṇānāṃ prasavatvācca pralayatvāttathaiva ca||9||

10upekṣatvādananyatvādabhimānācca kevalam
|
manyante yatayaḥ siddhā adhyātmajñā gatajvarāḥ
|
anityaṃ nityamavyaktaṃ vyaktametaddhi śuśruma||10||

11avyaktaikatvamityāhurnānātvaṃ puruṣāstathā
|
sarvabhūtadayāvantaḥ kevalaṃ jñānamāsthitāḥ||11||

12anyaḥ sa puruṣo'vyaktastvadhruvo dhruvasaṃjñakaḥ
|
yathā muñja iṣīkāṇāṃ tathaivaitaddhi jāyate||12||

13anyacca maśakaṃ vidyādanyaccodumbaraṃ tathā
|
na codumbarasaṃyogairmaśakastatra lipyate||13||

14anya eva tathā matsyastadanyadudakaṃ smṛtam
|
na codakasya sparśena matsyo lipyati sarvaśaḥ||14||

15anyo hyagnirukhāpyanyā nityamevamavehi bhoḥ
|
na copalipyate so'gnirukhāsaṃsparśanena vai||15||

16puṣkaraṃ tvanyadevātra tathā'nyadudakaṃ smṛtam
|
na codakasya sparśena lipyate tatra puṣkaram||16||

17eteṣāṃ sahavāsaṃ ca nivāsaṃ caiva nityaśaḥ
|
yāthātathyena paśyanti na nityaṃ prākṛtā janāḥ||17||

18ye tvanyathaiva paśyanti na samyakteṣu darśanam
|
te vyaktaṃ nirayaṃ ghoraṃ praviśanti punaḥ punaḥ||18||

19sāṃkhyadarśanametatte parisaṃkhyānamuttamam
|
evaṃ hi parisaṃkhyāya sāṃkhyāḥ kevalatāṃ gatāḥ||19||

20ye tvanye tattvakuśalāsteṣāmetannidarśanam
|
ataḥ paraṃ pravakṣyāmi yogānāmanudarśanam||20||
