Mahābhārata 13.27 (Anuśāsanaparvan)

1yudhiṣṭhira uvāca|
prajñāśrutābhyāṃ vṛttena śīlena ca yathā bhavān
|
guṇaiśca vividhaiḥ sarvairvayasā ca samanvitaḥ ||1||

2bhavān viśiṣṭo buddhyā ca prajñayā tapasā tathā
|
tasmādbhavantaṃ pṛcchāmi dharmaṃ dharmabhṛtāṃ vara ||2||

3nānyastvadanyo lokeṣu praṣṭavyo'sti narādhipa
|
kṣatriyo yadi vā vaiśyaḥ śūdro vā rājasattama ||3||

4brāhmaṇyaṃ prāpnuyādyena tanme vyākhyātumarhasi
|
tapasā vā sumahatā karmaṇā vā śrutena vā
|
brāhmaṇyamatha cedicchettanme brūhi pitāmaha ||4||

5bhīṣma uvāca|
brāhmaṇyaṃ tāta duṣprāpyaṃ varṇaiḥ kṣatrādibhistribhiḥ
|
paraṃ hi sarvabhūtānāṃ sthānametadyudhiṣṭhira ||5||

6bahvīstu saṃsaran yonīrjāyamānaḥ punaḥ punaḥ
|
paryāye tāta kasmiṃścidbrāhmaṇo nāma jāyate ||6||

7atrāpyudāharantīmamitihāsaṃ purātanam
|
mataṅgasya ca saṃvādaṃ gardabhyāśca yudhiṣṭhira ||7||

8dvijāteḥ kasyacittāta tulyavarṇaḥ sutastvabhūt
|
mataṅgo nāma nāmnā vai sarvaiḥ samudito guṇaiḥ ||8||

9sa yajñakāraḥ kaunteya pitrotsṛṣṭaḥ parantapa
|
prāyādgardabhayuktena rathenāpyāśugāminā ||9||

10sa bālaṃ gardabhaṃ rājan vahantaṃ māturantike
|
niravidhyatpratodena nāsikāyāṃ punaḥ punaḥ ||10||

11tatra tīvraṃ vraṇaṃ dṛṣṭvā gardabhī putragṛddhinī
|
uvāca mā śucaḥ putra cāṇḍālastvadhitiṣṭhati ||11||

12brāhmaṇe dāruṇaṃ nāsti maitro brāhmaṇa ucyate
|
ācāryaḥ sarvabhūtānāṃ śāstā kiṃ prahariṣyati ||12||

13ayaṃ tu pāpaprakṛtirbāle na kurute dayām
|
svayoniṃ mānayatyeṣa bhāvo bhāvaṃ niyacchati ||13||

14etacchrutvā mataṅgastu dāruṇaṃ rāsabhīvacaḥ
|
avatīrya rathāttūrṇaṃ rāsabhīṃ pratyabhāṣata ||14||

15brūhi rāsabhi kalyāṇi mātā me yena dūṣitā
|
kathaṃ māṃ vetsi caṇḍālaṃ kṣipraṃ rāsabhi śaṃsa me ||15||

16kathaṃ māṃ vetsi caṇḍālaṃ brāhmaṇyaṃ yena naśyate
|
tattvenaitanmahāprājñe brūhi sarvamaśeṣataḥ ||16||

17gardabhyuvāca
|
brāhmaṇyāṃ vṛṣalena tvaṃ mattāyāṃ nāpitena ha
|
|
jātastvamasi cāṇḍālo brāhmaṇyaṃ tena te'naśat ||17||

18evamukto mataṅgastu pratiprāyādgṛhaṃ prati
|
tamāgatamabhiprekṣya pitā vākyamathābravīt ||18||

19mayā tvaṃ yajñasaṃsiddhau niyukto gurukarmaṇi
|
kasmātpratinivṛtto'si kaccinna kuśalaṃ tava ||19||

20mataṅga uvāca|
antyayonirayonirvā kathaṃ sa kuśalī bhavet
|
kuśalaṃ tu kutastasya yasyeyaṃ jananī pitaḥ ||20||

21brāhmaṇyāṃ vṛṣalājjātaṃ pitarvediyatīva mām
|
amānuṣī gardabhīyaṃ tasmāttapsye tapo mahat ||21||

22evamuktvā sa pitaraṃ pratasthe kṛtaniścayaḥ
|
tato gatvā mahāraṇyamatapatsumahattapaḥ ||22||

23tataḥ sa tāpayāmāsa vibudhāṃstapasānvitaḥ
|
mataṅgaḥ sukhasaṃprepsuḥ sthānaṃ sucaritādapi ||23||

24taṃ tathā tapasā yuktamuvāca harivāhanaḥ
|
mataṅga tapsyase kiṃ tvaṃ bhogānutsṛjya mānuṣān ||24||

25varaṃ dadāmi te haṃta vṛṇīṣva tvaṃ yadicchasi
|
yaccāpyavāpyaṃ hṛdi te sarvaṃ tadbrūhi māciram ||25||

26mataṅga uvāca|
brāhmaṇyaṃ kāmayāno'hamidamārabdhavāṃstapaḥ
|
gaccheyaṃ tadavāpyeha vara eṣa vṛto mayā ||26||

27bhīṣma uvāca|
etacchrutvā tu vacanaṃ tamuvāca purandaraḥ
|
mataṅga durlabhamidaṃ vipratvaṃ prārthyate tvayā ||27||

28brāhmaṇyaṃ prārthayānastvamaprāpyamakṛtātmabhiḥ
|
vinaśiṣyasi durbuddhe tadupārama māciram ||28||

29śreṣṭhatāṃ sarvabhūteṣu tapo'rthaṃ nātivartate
|
tadagryaṃ prārthayānastvamacirādvinaśiṣyasi ||29||

30devatāsuramartyeṣu yatpavitraṃ paraṃ smṛtam
|
caṇḍālayonau jātena na tatprāpyaṃ kathañcana ||30||
