Mahābhārata 12.217 (Śāntiparvan)

1bhīṣma uvāca|
na sa veda paraṃ brahma yo na veda catuṣṭayam
|
vyaktāvyaktaṃ ca yattattvaṃ samproktaṃ paramarṣiṇā ||1||

2vyaktaṃ mṛtyumukhaṃ vidyādavyaktamṛtaṃ padam
|
pravṛttilakṣaṇaṃ dharmamṛṣirnārāyaṇo'bravīt ||2||

3tatraivāvasthitaṃ sarvaṃ trailokyāṃ sacarācaram
|
nivṛttilakṣaṇaṃ dharmamavyaktaṃ brahma śāśvatam ||3||

4pravṛttilakṣaṇaṃ dharmaṃ prajāpatirathābravīt
|
pravṛttiḥ punarāvṛttirnivṛttiḥ paramā gatiḥ ||4||

5tāṃ gatiṃ paramāmeti nivṛttiparamo muniḥ
|
jñānatattvaparo nityaṃ śubhāśubhanidarśakaḥ ||5||

6tadevametau vijñeyāvavyaktapuruṣāvubhau
|
avyaktapuruṣābhyāṃ tu yatsyādanyanmahattaram ||6||

7taṃ viśeṣamavekṣeta viśeṣeṇa vicakṣaṇaḥ
|
anādyantāvubhāvetāvaliṅgau cāpyubhāvapi ||7||

8ubhau nityāvavicalau mahadbhyaśca mahattarau
|
sāmānyametadubhayorevaṃ hyanyadviśeṣaṇam ||8||

9prakṛtyā sargadharmiṇyā tathā triguṇadharmayā
|
viparītamato vidyāt kṣetrajñasya svalakṣaṇam ||9||

10prakṛteśca vikārāṇāṃ draṣṭāramaguṇānvitam
|
agrāhyau puruṣāvetāvaliṅgatvādasaṃhatau ||10||

11saṃyogalakṣaṇotpattiḥ karmaṇā gṛhyate yathā
|
karaṇaiḥ karmanivṛttiḥ kartā yadyadviceṣṭate
|
kīrtyate śabdasaṃjñābhiḥ ko'hameṣo'pyasāviti ||11||

12uṣṇīṣavānyathā vastraistribhirbhavati saṃvṛtaḥ
|
saṃvṛto'yaṃ tathā dehī sattva-rājasa-tāmasaiḥ ||12||

13tasmāccatuṣṭayaṃ vedyametairhetubhirāvṛtam
|
yathāsaṃjño hyayaṃ samyagantakāle na muhyati ||13||

14śriyaṃ divyāmabhiprepsurvarṣmavānmanasā śuciḥ
|
śārīrairniyamairugraiścarenniṣkalmaṣaṃ tapaḥ ||14||

15trailokyaṃ tapasā vyāptamantarbhūtena bhāsvatā
|
sūryaśca candramāścaiva bhāsatastapasā divi ||15||

16prakāśastapaso jñānaṃ loke saṃśabditaṃ tapaḥ
|
rajastamoghnaṃ yatkarma tapasastatsvalakṣaṇam ||16||

17brahmacaryamahiṃsā ca śārīraṃ tapa ucyate
|
vāṅmanoniyamaḥ samyaṅmānasaṃ tapa ucyate ||17||

18vidhijñebhyo dvijātibhyo grāhyamannaṃ viśiṣyate
|
āhāraniyamenāsya pāpmā śāmyati rājasaḥ ||18||

19vaimanasyaṃ ca viṣaye yāntyasya karaṇāni ca
|
tasmāttanmātramādadyādyāvadatra prayojanam ||19||

20antakāle balotkarṣācchanaiḥ kuryādanāturaḥ
|
evaṃ yuktena manasā jñānaṃ yadupapadyate ||20||

21rajovarjyo'pyayaṃ dehī dehavāñchabdavaccaret
|
kāryairavyāhatamatirvairāgyātprakṛtau sthitaḥ ||21||

22ā dehādapramādācca dehāntādvipramucyate
|
hetuyuktaḥ sadā sargo bhūtānāṃ pralayastathā ||22||

23parapratyayasarge tu niyatirnānuvartate
|
bhāvāntaprabhavaprajñā āsate ye viparyayam ||23||

24dhṛtyā dehāndhārayanto buddhisaṃkṣiptacetasaḥ
|
sthānebhyo dhvaṃsamānāśca sūkṣmatvāttadupāsate ||24||

25yathāgamaṃ ca gatvā vai buddhyā tatraiva buddhyate
|
dehāntaṃ kaścidanvāste bhāvitātmā nirāśrayam ||25||

26yuktaṃ dhāraṇayā samyak sataḥ kecidupāsate
|
abhyasyanti paraṃ devaṃ vidyutsaṃśabditākṣaram ||26||

27antakāle hyupāsante tapasā dagdhakilbiṣāḥ
|
sarva ete mahātmāno gacchanti paramāṃ gatim ||27||

28sūkṣmaṃ viśeṣaṇaṃ teṣāmavekṣecchāstracakṣuṣā
|
dehāntaṃ paramaṃ vidyādvimuktamaparigraham ||28||

29antarikṣādanyataraṃ dhāraṇāsaktamānasam
|
martyalokādvimucyante vidyāsaṃsaktacetasaḥ||29||

30brahmabhūtā virajasastato yānti parāṃ gatim
|
evamekāyanaṃ dharmamāhurvedavido janāḥ ||30||

31yathājñānamupāsantaḥ sarve yānti parāṃ gatim
|
kaṣāyavarjitaṃ jñānaṃ yeṣāmutpadyate'calam
|
yānti te'pi parā~llokān vimucyante yathābalam ||31||

32bhagavantamajaṃ divyaṃ viṣṇumavyaktasaṃjñitam
|
bhāvena yānti śuddhā ye jñānatṛptā nirāśiṣaḥ ||32||

33jñātvā''tmasthaṃ hariṃ caiva na nivartanti te'vyayāḥ
|
prāpya tatparamaṃ sthānaṃ modante'kṣaramavyayam ||33||

34etāvadetadvijñānametadasti ca nāsti ca
|
tṛṣṇābaddhaṃ jagatsarvaṃ cakravatparivartate ||34||

35bisatanturyathaivāyamantaḥsthaḥ sarvato bise
|
tṛṣṇātanturanādyantastathā dehagataḥ sadā ||35||

36sūcyā sūtraṃ yathā vastre saṃsārayati vāyakaḥ
|
tadvatsaṃsārasūtraṃ hi tṛṣṇāsūcyā nibaddhyate ||36||

37vikāraṃ prakṛtiṃ caiva puruṣaṃ ca sanātanam
|
yo yathāvadvijānāti sa vitṛṣṇo vimucyate ||37||

38prakāśaṃ bhagavānetadṛṣirnārāyaṇo'mṛtam
|
bhūtānāmanukampārthaṃ jagāda jagato gatiḥ ||38||
