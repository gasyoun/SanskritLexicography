Mahābhārata 4.53 (Virāṭaparvan)

1vaiśampāyana uvāca|tathā vyūḍheṣvanīkeṣu kauraveyeṣu bhārata
|
upāyāda‌rjunastūrṇaṃ rathaghoṣaṇa nādayan ||1||

2dadṛśuste dhvajāgraṃ vai śuśruvuśca mahāsvanam
|
dodhūyamānasya bhṛśaṃ gāṇḍīvasya ca niḥsvanam ||2||

3tatastu sarvamālokya droṇo vacanamabravīt
|
mahārathamanuprāptaṃ dṛṣṭvā gāṇḍīvadhanvinam ||3||

4droṇa uvāca|etat dhvajāgraṃ pārthasya dūrataḥ saṃprakāśate
|
eṣa ghoṣaḥ sa rathajo roravīti ca vānaraḥ ||4||

5eṣa tiṣṭhan rathaśreṣṭhe rathe ca rathināṃ varaḥ
|
utkarṣati dhanuḥ śreṣṭhaṃ gāṇḍīvamaśanisvanam ||5||

6imau ca bāṇau sahitau pāda‌yorme vyavasthitau
|
aparau cāpyatikrāntau kaṇā saṃspṛśya me śarau ||6||

7niruṣya hi vane vāsaṃ kṛtvā karmātimānuṣam
|
abhivādayate pārthaḥ śrotre ca paripṛcchati ||7||

8ciradṛṣṭo'yamasmābhiḥ prajñāvān bāndhavapriyaḥ
|
atīva jvalito lakṣmyā pāṇḍuputro dhanaṃjayaḥ ||8||

9rathī śarī cārutalī niṣaṅgī śaṅkhī patākī kavacī kirīṭī
|
khaḍgī ca dhanvī ca vibhāti pārthaḥ śikhī vṛtaḥ srugbhirivājyasiktaḥ ||9||

10arjuna uvāca|iṣupāte ca senāyā hayān saṃyaccha sārathe
|
yāvat samīkṣe sainye'smin kvāsau kurukulādhamaḥ ||10||

11sarvānetānanādṛtya dṛṣṭvā tamatimāninam
|
tasya mūrdhni patiṣyāmi tata ete parājitāḥ ||11||

12eṣa vyavasthito droṇo drauṇiśca tadanantaram
|
bhīṣmaḥ kṛpaśca karṇaśca maheṣvāsāḥ samāgatāḥ ||12||

13rājānaṃ nātra paśyāmi gāḥ samādāya gacchati
|
dakṣiṇaṃ mārgamāsthāya śaṅke jīvaparāyaṇaḥ ||13||

14utsṛjaitadrathānīkaṃ gaccha yatra suyodhanaḥ
|
tatraiva yotsye vairāṭe nāsti yuddhaṃ nirāmiṣam
|
taṃ jitvā vinivartiṣye gāḥ samādāya vai punaḥ ||14||

15vaiśampāyana uvāca|evamuktaḥ sa vairāṭirhayān saṃyamya yatnataḥ
|
niyamya ca tato raśmīn yatra te kurupuṅgavāḥ
|
acodayat tato vāhān yatra duryodhano gataḥ ||15||

16utsṛjya rathavaṃśaṃ tu prayāte śvetavāhane
|
abhiprāyaṃ viditvā ca kṛpo vacanamabravīt ||16||

17naiṣo'ntareṇa rājānaṃ bībhatsuḥ sthātumicchati
|
tasya pārṣṇiṃ grahīṣyāmo javenābhiprayāsyataḥ ||17||

18na hyenamatisaṃkruddhameko yudhyeta saṃyuge
|
anyo devāt sahasrākṣāt kṛṣṇādvā devakī sutāt
|
ācāryācca saputrādvā bhāradvājānmahārathāt ||18||

19kiṃ no gāvaḥ kariṣyanti dhanaṃ vā vipulaṃ tathā
|
duryodhanaḥ pārthajale purā nauriva majjati ||19||

20tathaiva gatvā bībhatsurnāma viśrāvya cātmanaḥ
|
śalabhairiva tāṃ senāṃ śaraiḥ śīghramavākirat ||20||

21kīryamāṇāḥ śaraughaistu yodhāste pārthacoditaiḥ
|
nāpaśyannāvṛtāṃ bhūmiṃ nāntarikṣaṃ ca patribhiḥ ||21||

22teṣāmāpatatāṃ yuddhe nāpayāne'bhavanmatiḥ
|
śīghratvameva pārthasya pūjayanti sma cetasā ||22||

23tataḥ śaṅkhaṃ pradadhmau sa dviṣatāṃ lomaharṣaṇam
|
visphārya ca dhanuḥśreṣṭhaṃ dhvaje bhūtānyacodayat ||23||

24tasya śaṅkhasya śabdena rathanemisvanena ca
|
gāṇḍīvasya ca ghoṣeṇa pṛthivī samakampata ||24||

25amānuṣāṇāṃ bhūtānāṃ teṣāṃ ca dhvajavāsinām
|
ūrdhvaṃ pucchān vidhunvānā rebhamāṇāḥ samantataḥ
|
gāvaḥ pratinyavartanta diśamāsthāya dakṣiṇām ||25||
