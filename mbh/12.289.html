Mahābhārata 12.289 (Śāntiparvan)

1yudhiṣṭhira uvāca|
tiṣṭhate me sadā tāta kautūhalamidaṃ hṛdi
|
tadahaṃ śrotumicchāmi tvattaḥ kurupitāmaha ||1||

2kathaṃ devarṣiruśanāḥ sadā kāvyo mahāmatiḥ
|
asurāṇāṃ priyakaraḥ surāṇāmapriye rataḥ ||2||

3vardhayāmāsa tejaśca kimarthamamitaujasām
|
nityaṃ vairanibaddhāśca dānavāḥ surasattamaiḥ ||3||

4kathaṃ cāpyuśanā prāpa śukratvamamaradyutiḥ
|
ṛddhiṃ ca sa kathaṃ prāptaḥ sarvametadbravīhi me ||4||

5na yāti ca sa tejasvī madhyena nabhasaḥ katham
|
etadicchāmi vijñātuṃ nikhilena pitāmaha ||5||

6bhīṣma uvāca|
śṛṇu rājannavahitaḥ sarvametadyathātatham
|
yathāmati yathā caitacchrutapūrvaṃ mayā'nagha ||6||

7eṣa bhārgavadāyādo munirmānyo dṛḍhavrataḥ
|
surāṇāṃ vipriyakaro nimitte kāraṇātmake ||7||

8indro'tha dhanado rājā yakṣarakṣo'dhiyaḥ sadā
|
prabhaviṣṇuśca kośasya jagataśca tathā prabhuḥ ||8||

9tasyātmānamathāviśya yogasiddho mahāmuniḥ
|
ruddhvā dhanapatiṃ devaṃ yogena hṛtavānvasu ||9||

10hṛte dhane tataḥ śarma na lebhe dhanadastathā
|
āpannamanyuḥ saṃvignaḥ so'bhyagātsurasattamam ||10||

11nivedayāmāsa tadā śivāyāmitatejase
|
devaśreṣṭhāya rudrāya saumyāya bahurūpiṇe ||11||

12yogātmakenośanasā ruddhvā mama hṛtaṃ vasu
|
yogenātmagataṃ kṛtvā niḥsṛtaśca mahātapāḥ ||12||

13etacchrutvā tataḥ kruddho mahāyogī maheśvaraḥ
|
saṃraktanayano rājanśūlamādāya tasthivān ||13||

14kvāsau kvāsāviti prāha gṛhītvā paramāyudham
|
uśanā dūratastasya babhau jñātvā cikīrṣitam ||14||

15sa mahāyogino buddhvā taṃ roṣaṃ vai mahātmanaḥ
|
gatimāgamanaṃ vetti sthānaṃ caiva tataḥ prabhuḥ ||15||

16saṃcintyogreṇa tapasā mahātmānaṃ maheśvaram
|
uśanā yogasiddhātmā śūlāgre pratyadṛśyat ||16||

17vijñātarūpaḥ sa tadā tapaḥsiddho'tha dhanvinā
|
jñātvā śūlaṃ ca deveśaḥ pāṇinā samanāmayat ||17||

18ānatenātha śūlena pāṇinā'mitatejasā
|
pinākamiti covāca śūlamugrāyudhaḥ prabhuḥ ||18||

19pāṇimadhyagataṃ dṛṣṭvā bhārgavaṃ tamumāpatiḥ
|
āsyaṃ vivṛtya kakudī pāṇinā prākṣipacchanaiḥ ||19||

20sa tu praviṣṭa uśanā koṣṭhaṃ māheśvaraṃ prabhuḥ
|
vyacaraccāpi tatrāsau mahātmā bhṛgunandanaḥ ||20||

21yudhiṣṭhira uvāca|
kimarthaṃ vyacaradrājanuśanā tasya dhīmataḥ
|
jaṭhare devadevasya kiṃ cākārṣīnmāhadyutiḥ ||21||

22bhīṣma uvāca|
purā so'ntarjalagatāḥ sthāṇubhūto mahāvrataḥ
|
varṣāṇāmabhavadrājanprayutānyarbudāni ca ||22||

23udatiṣṭhattapastaptvā duścaraṃ ca mahāhradāt
|
tato devātidevastaṃ brahmā vai samasarpata ||23||

24tapovṛddhimapṛcchacca kuśalaṃ caivamavyayaḥ
|
tapaḥ sucīrṇamiti ca provāca vṛṣabhadhvajaḥ ||24||

25tatsaṃyogena vṛddhiṃ cāpyapaśyatsa tu śaṃkaraḥ
|
mahāmatiracintyātmā satyadharmarataḥ sadā ||25||

26sa tenāḍhyo mahāyogī tapasā ca dhanena ca
|
vyarājata mahārāja triṣu lokeṣu vīryavān ||26||

27tataḥ pinākī yogātmā dhyānayogaṃ samāviśat
|
uśanā tu samadvigno nililye jaṭhare tataḥ ||27||

28tuṣṭāva ca mahāyogī devaṃ tatrastha eva ca
|
niḥsāraṃ kāṅkṣamāṇaḥ sa tena sma pratihanyate ||28||

29uśanā tu tathovāca jaṭharastho mahāmuniḥ
|
prasādaṃ me kuruṣveti punaḥ punararindama ||29||

30tamuvāca mahādevo gaccha śiśnena mokṣaṇam
|
iti sarvāṇi srotāṃsi ruddhvā tridaśapuṅgavaḥ ||30||

31apaśyamānastaddvāraṃ sarvataḥ pihito muniḥ
|
paryakrāmaddahyamāna itaścetaśca tejasā ||31||

32sa vai niṣkramya śiśnena śukratvamabhipedivān
|
kāryeṇa tena nabhaso nādhyagacchata madhyataḥ ||32||

33viniṣkrāntaṃ tu taṃ dṛṣṭvā jvalantamiva tejasā
|
bhavo roṣasamāviṣṭaḥ śūlodyatakaraḥ sthitaḥ ||33||

34avārayata taṃ devī kruddhaṃ paśupatiṃ patim
|
putratvamagamaddevyā vārite śaṃkare ca saḥ ||34||

35devyuvāca
|
hiṃsanīyastvayā naiva mama putratvamāgataḥ
|
na hi devodarātkaścinniḥsṛto nāśamṛcchati ||35||

36tataḥ prīto bhavo devyāḥ prahasaṃścedamabravīt
|
gacchatveṣa yathākāmamiti rājanpunaḥ punaḥ ||36||

37tataḥ praṇamya varadaṃ devaṃ devīmumāṃ tathā
|
uśanā prāpa taddhīmāngatimiṣṭāṃ mahāmuniḥ ||37||

38etatte kathitaṃ tāta bhārgavasya mahātmanaḥ
|
caritaṃ bharataśreṣṭha yanmāṃ tvaṃ paripṛcchasi ||38||
