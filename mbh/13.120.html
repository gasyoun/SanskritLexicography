Mahābhārata 13.120 (Anuśāsanaparvan)

1yudhiṣṭhira uvāca|
vidyātapobhyāṃ dānācca kimeteṣāṃ viśiṣyate
|
pṛcchāmi tvāṃ satāṃ śreṣṭha tanme brūhi pitāmaha ||1||

2bhīṣma uvāca|
atrāpyudāharantīmamitihāsaṃ purātanam
|
maitreyasya ca saṃvādaṃ kṛṣṇadvaipāyanasya ca ||2||

3kṛṣṇadvaipāyano rājannajñātacaritaṃ caran
|
vārāṇasyāmupātiṣṭhanmaitreyaṃ svairiṇīkule ||3||

4tamupasthitamāsīnaṃ jñātvā sa munisattamam
|
arcitvā bhojayāmāsa maitreyo'śanamuttamam ||4||

5tadannamuttamaṃ bhuktvā guṇavatsārvakāmikam
|
pratiṣṭhamāno'smayata prītaḥ kṛṣṇo mahāmanāḥ ||5||

6tamutsmayantaṃ saṃprekṣya maitreyaḥ kṛṣṇamabravīt
|
kāraṇaṃ brūhi dharmātmanvyasmayiṣṭhāḥ kutaśca te ||6||

7tapasvino dhṛtimataḥ pramodaḥ samupāgataḥ
|
etatpṛcchāmi te vidvannabhivādya praṇamya ca
|
ātmanaśca tapobhāgyaṃ mahābhāgyaṃ taveha ca ||7||

8pṛthagācaratastāta pṛthagātmasukhātmanoḥ
|
alpāntaramahaṃ manye viśiṣṭamapi cānvayāt ||8||

9vyāsa uvāca|
aticchedātivādābhyāṃ smayo'yaṃ samupāgataḥ
|
asatyaṃ vedavacanaṃ kasmādvedo'nṛtaṃ vadet ||9||

10trīṇyeva tu padānyāhuḥ puruṣasyottamaṃ vratam
|
na druhyeccaiva dadyācca satyaṃ caiva paraṃ vadet ||10||

11iti vedoktamṛṣibhiḥ purastātparikalpitam
|
idānīṃ caiva naḥ kṛtyaṃ purastācca pariśrutam ||11||

12alpo'pi tādṛśo dāyo bhavatyuta mahāphalaḥ
|
tṛṣitāya ca te dattaṃ hṛda‌yenānasūyatā ||12||

13tṛṣitastṛṣitāya tvaṃ datvaitaddarśanaṃ mama
|
ajaiṣīrmahato lokānmahāyajñairiva prabho ||13||

14tato dānapavitreṇa prīto'smi tapasaiva ca
|
puṇyasyaiva hi te sattvaṃ puṇyasyaiva ca darśanam ||14||

15puṇyasyaivābhigandhaste manye karmavidhānajam
|
adhikaṃ mārjanāttāta tathā caivānulepanāt ||15||

16śubhaṃ sarvapavitrebhyo dānameva paraṃ dvija
|
no cetsarvapavitrebhyo dānameva paraṃ bhavet ||16||

17yānīmānyuttamānīha vedoktāni praśaṃsasi
|
teṣāṃ śreṣṭhataraṃ dānamiti me nātra saṃśayaḥ ||17||

18dānakṛdbhiḥ kṛtaḥ panthā yena yānti manīṣiṇaḥ
|
te hi prāṇasya dātārasteṣu dharmaḥ pratiṣṭhitaḥ ||18||

19yathā vedāḥ svadhītāśca yathā cendriyasaṃyamaḥ
|
sarvatyāgo yathā ceha tathā dānamanuttamam ||19||

20tvaṃ hi tāta mahābuddhe sukhameṣyasi śobhanam
|
sukhātsukhataraprāptimāpnute matimānnaraḥ ||20||

21tannaḥ pratyakṣameveda‌mupalabhyamasaṃśayam
|
śrīmantaḥ prāpnuvantyarthāndānaṃ yajñaṃ tathā sukham ||21||

22sukhādeva paraṃ duḥkhaṃ duḥkhādapyaparaṃ sukham
|
dṛśyate hi mahāprājña niyataṃ vai svabhāvataḥ ||22||

23trividhānīha vṛttāni narasyāhurmanīṣiṇaḥ
|
puṇyamanyatpāpamayanna puṇyaṃ na ca pāpakam ||23||

24na vṛttaṃ manyate tasya manyate na ca pātakam
|
tathā svakarmanirvṛttaṃ na puṇyaṃ na ca pāpakam ||24||

25yajñadānatapaḥśīlā narā ve puṇyakarmiṇaḥ
|
ye'bhidruhyanti bhūtāni te vai pāpakṛto janāḥ ||25||

26dravyāṇyādadate caiva duḥkhaṃ yānti patanti ca
|
tato'nyatkarma yatkiñcinna puṇyaṃ na ca pātakam ||26||

27ramasvaidhasva modasva dehi caiva yajasva ca
|
na tvāmabhibhaviṣyanti vaidyā na ca tapasvinaḥ ||27||
