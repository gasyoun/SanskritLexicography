Mahābhārata 3.286 (Āraṇyakaparvan)

1mārkaṇḍeya uvāca|
tataḥ prahastaḥ sahasā samabhyetya vibhīṣaṇam
|
gadayā tāḍayāmāsa vinadya raṇakarkaśaḥ||1||

2sa tayā'bhihato dhīmān gadayā bhīmavegayā
|
nākampata mahābāhurhimavāniva susthiraḥ||2||

3tataḥ pragṛhya vipulāṃ śataghaṇṭāṃ vibhīṣaṇaḥ
|
anumantrya mahāśaktiṃ cikṣepāsya śiraḥ prati||3||

4patantyā sa tayā vegādrākṣaso'śanivegayā
|
hṛtottamāṅgo dadṛśe vātarugṇa iva drumaḥ||4||

5ttaṃ dṛṣṭvā nihataṃ saṅkhye prahastaṃ kṣaṇadācaram
|
abhidudrāva dhūmrākṣo vegena mahatā kapīn||5||

6tasya meghopamaṃ sainyamāpatadbhīmadarśanam
|
dṛṣṭvaiva sahasā dīrṇā raṇe vānarapuṅgavāḥ||6||

7tatastān sahasā dīrṇān dṛṣṭvā vānarapuṅgavān
|
niryayau kapiśārdūlo hanūmān mārutātmajaḥ||7||

8taṃ dṛṣṭvā'vasthitaṃ saṅkhye harayaḥ pavanātmajam
|
mahatyā tvarayā rājan sannyavartanta sarvaśaḥ||8||

9tataḥ śabdo mahānāsīt tumulo lomaharṣaṇaḥ
|
rāmarāvaṇasainyānāmanyonyamabhidhāvatām||9||

10tasmin pravṛtte saṅgrāme ghore rudhirakardame
|
dhūmrākṣaḥ kapisainyaṃ taddrāvayāmāsa pattribhiḥ||10||

11taṃ sa rakṣomahāmātramāpatantaṃ sapatnajit
|
pratijagrāha hanumāṃstarasā pavanātmajaḥ||11||

12tayoryuddhamabhūdghoraṃ harirākṣasavīrayoḥ
|
jigīṣatoryudhā'nyonyamindrapralhādayoriva||12||

13gadābhiḥ parighaiścaiva rākṣaso jaghnivān kapim
|
kapiśca jaghnivān rakṣaḥ saskandhaviṭapairdrumaiḥ||13||

14tatastamatikopena sāśvaṃ sarathasārathim
|
dhūmrākṣamavadhīt kruddho hanumān mārutātmajaḥ||14||

15tatastaṃ nihataṃ dṛṣṭvā dhūmrākṣaṃ rākṣasottamam
|
harayo jātavisrambhā jaghnuranye ca sainikān||15||

16te vadhyamānā haribhirbalibhirjitakāśibhiḥ
|
rākṣasā bhagnasaṅkalpā laṅkāmabhyapatanbhayāt||16||

17te'bhipattya puraṃ bhagnā hataśeṣā niśācarāḥ
|
sarvaṃ rājñe yathāvṛttaṃ rāvaṇāya nyavedayan||17||

18śrutvā tu rāvaṇastebhyaḥ prahastaṃ nihataṃ yudhi
|
dhūmrākṣaṃ ca maheṣvāsaṃ sasainyaṃ vānararṣabhaiḥ||18||

19sudīrghamiva niḥśvasya samutpatya varāsanāt
|
uvāca kumbhakarṇasya karmakālo'yamāgataḥ||19||

20ityevamuktvā vividhairvāditraiḥ sumahāsvanaiḥ
|
śayānamatinidrāluṃ kumbhakarṇamabodhayat||20||

21prabodhya mahatā cainaṃ yatnenāgatasādhvasaḥ
|
svasthamāsīnamavyagraṃ vinidraṃ rākṣasādhipaḥ||21||

22tato'bravīddaśagrīvaḥ kumbhakarṇaṃ mahābalam
|
dhanyo'si yasya te nidrā kumbhakarṇeyamīdṛśī||22||

23ya idaṃ dāruṇākāraṃ na jānīṣe mahābhayam
|
eṣa tīrtvā'rṇavaṃ rāmaḥ setunā haribhiḥ saha||23||

24avamanyeha naḥ sarvān karoti kadanaṃ mahat
|
mayā tvapahṛtā bhāryā sītā nāmāsya jānakī||24||

25tāṃ netuṃ sa ihāyāto badhvā setuṃ mahārṇave
|
tena caiva prahastādirmahān naḥ svajano hataḥ||25||

26tasya nānyo nihantā'sti tvāmṛte śatrukarśana
|
sa daṃśito'bhiniryāya tvamadya balināṃ vara||26||

27rāmādīn samare sarvān jahi śatrūnarindama
|
dūṣaṇāvarajau caiva vajravegapramāthinau||27||

28tau tvāṃ balena mahatā sahitāvanuyāsyataḥ
|
ityuktvā rākṣasapatiḥ kumbhakarṇaṃ tarasvinam
|
saṃdideśetikartavye vajravegapramāthinau||28||

29tathetyuktvā tu tau vīrau rāvaṇaṃ dūṣaṇānujau
|
kumbhakarṇaṃ puraskṛtya tūrṇaṃ niryayatuḥ purāt||29||
