Mahābhārata 7.80 (Droṇaparvan)

1sañjaya uvāca|
kuntīputrastu taṃ mantraṃ smaranneva dhanañjayaḥ
|
pratijñāmātmano rakṣanmumohācintyavikramaḥ ||1||

2taṃ tu śokena saṃtaptaṃ svapne kapivaradhvajam
|
āsasāda mahātejā dhyāyantaṃ garuḍadhvajaḥ ||2||

3pratyutthānaṃ ca kṛṣṇasya sarvāvastho dhanañjayaḥ
|
na lopayati dharmātmā bhaktyā premṇā ca sarvadā ||3||

4pratyutthāya ca govindaṃ sa tasmā āsanaṃ dadau
|
na cāsane svayaṃ buddhiṃ bībhatsurvyada‌dhāt tadā ||4||

5tataḥ kṛṣṇo mahātejā jānan pārthasya niścayam
|
kuntīputramidaṃ vākyamāsīnaḥ sthitamabravīt ||5||

6mā viṣāde manaḥ pārtha kṛthāḥ kālo hi durjayaḥ
|
kālaḥ sarvāṇi bhūtāni niyacchati pare vidhau ||6||

7kimarthaṃ ca viṣādaste tadbrūhi dvipadāṃ vara
|
na śocyaṃ viduṣāṃ śreṣṭha śokaḥ kāryavināśanaḥ ||7||

8yattu kāryaṃ bhavet kāryaṃ karmaṇā tat samācara
|
hīnaceṣṭasya yaḥ śokaḥ sa hi śatrurdhanañjaya ||8||

9śocannandayate śatrūn karśayatyapi bāndhavān
|
kṣīyate ca narastasmānna tvaṃ śocitumarhasi ||9||

10ityukto vāsudevena bībhatsuraparājitaḥ
|
ābabhāṣe tadā vidvānidaṃ vacanamarthavat ||10||

11mayā pratijñā mahatī jayadrathavadhe kṛtā
|
śvo'smi hantā durātmānaṃ putraghnamiti keśava ||11||

12matpratijñāvighātārthaṃ dhārtarāṣṭraiḥ kilācyuta
|
pṛṣṭhataḥ saindhavaḥ kāryaḥ sarvairgupto mahārathaiḥ ||12||

13daśa caikā ca tāḥ kṛṣṇa akṣauhiṇyaḥ sudurjayāḥ
|
hatāvaśeṣāstatremā hanta mādhava saṃkhyayā ||13||

14tābhiḥ parivṛtaḥ saṃkhye sarvaiścaiva mahārathaiḥ
|
kathaṃ śakyeta saṃdraṣṭuṃ durātmā kṛṣṇa saindhavaḥ ||14||

15pratijñāpāraṇaṃ cāpi na bhaviṣyati keśava
|
pratijñāyāṃ ca hīnāyāṃ kathaṃ jīvati madvidhaḥ ||15||

16duḥkhopāyasya me vīra vikāṃkṣā parivartate
|
drutaṃ ca yāti savitā tata etadbravīmyaham ||16||

17śokasthānaṃ tu tacchrutvā pārthasya dvijaketanaḥ
|
saṃspṛśyāṃbhastataḥ kṛṣṇaḥ prāṅmukhaḥ samavasthitaḥ ||17||

18idaṃ vākyaṃ mahātejā babhāṣe puṣkarekṣaṇaḥ
|
hitārthaṃ pāṇḍuputrasya saindhavasya vadhe kṛtī ||18||

19pārtha pāśupataṃ nāma paramāstraṃ sanātanam
|
yena sarvān mṛdhe daityān jaghne devo maheśvaraḥ ||19||

20yadi tadviditaṃ te'dya śvo hantā'si jayadratha
|
athājñātaṃ prapadyasva manasā vṛṣabhadhvajam ||20||

21taṃ devaṃ manasā dhyātvā joṣamāsva dhanañjaya
|
tatastasya prasādāttvaṃ bhaktaḥ prāpsyasi tanmahat ||21||

22tataḥ kṛṣṇavacaḥ śrutvā saṃspṛśyāṃbho dhanañjayaḥ
|
bhūmāvāsīna ekāgro jagāma manasā bhavam ||22||

23tataḥ praṇihito brāhme muhūrte śubhalakṣaṇe
|
ātmānamarjuno'paśyadgagane saha‌keśavam ||23||

24puṇyaṃ himavataḥ pādaṃ maṇimantaṃ ca parvatam
|
jyotirbhiśca samākīrṇaṃ siddhacāraṇasevitam ||24||

25vāyuvegagatiḥ pārthaḥ svaṃ bheje sahakeśavaḥ
|
keśavena gṛhītaḥ sa dakṣiṇe vibhunā bhuje ||25||

26prekṣamāṇo bahūn bhāvān jagāmādbhutadarśanān
|
udīcyāṃ diśi dharmātmā so'paśyacchvetaparvatam ||26||

27kuberasya vihāre ca nalinīṃ padmabhūṣitām
|
saricchreṣṭhāṃ ca tāṃ gaṅgāṃ vīkṣamāṇo bahūdakām ||27||

28sadā puṣpaphalairvṛkṣairupetāṃ sphaṭikopalām
|
siṃhavyāghrasamākīrṇāṃ nānāmṛgasamākulām ||28||

29puṇyāśramavatīṃ ramyāṃ manojñāṃḍajasevitām
|
mandarasya pradeśāṃśca kinnarodgītanāditān ||29||

30hemarūpyamayaiḥ śṛṅgernānauṣadhividīpitān
|
tathā mandāravṛkṣaiśca puṣpitairupaśobhitān ||30||

31snigdhāṃjanacayākāraṃ saṃprāptaḥ kālaparvatam
|
brahmatuṅganadīścānyāstathā janapadānapi ||31||

32sa tuṅgaṃ śataśṛṅgaṃ ca śaryātivanameva ca
|
puṇyamaśvaśiraḥsthānaṃ sthānamātharvaṇasya ca ||32||

33vṛṣadaṃśaṃ ca śailendraṃ mahāmandarameva ca
|
apsarobhiḥ samākīrṇaṃ kinnaraiścopaśobhitam ||33||

34tasmin śaile vrajan pārthaḥ sakṛṣṇaḥ samavaikṣata
|
śubhaiḥ prastravaṇairjuṣṭāṃ hemadhātuvibhūṣitām ||34||

35candraraśmiprakāśāṅgīṃ pṛthivīṃ puramālinīm
|
samudrāṃścādbhutākārānapaśyadbahulākarān ||35||

36viyaddyāṃ pṛthivīṃ caiva tathā viṣṇupadaṃ vrajan
|
vismitaḥ saha kṛṣṇena kṣipto bāṇa ivābhyagāt ||36||

37grahanakṣatrasomānāṃ sūryāgnyośca samatviṣam
|
apaśyata tadā pārtho jvalantamiva parvatam ||37||

38samāsādya tu taṃ śailaṃ śailāgre samavasthitam
|
taponityaṃ mahātmānamapaśyadvṛṣabhadhvajam ||38||

39sahasramiva sūryāṇāṃ dīpyamānaṃ svatejasā
|
śūlinaṃ jaṭilaṃ gauraṃ valkalājinavāsasam ||39||

40nayanānāṃ sahasraiśca vicitrāṅgaṃ mahaujasam
|
pārvatyā sahitaṃ devaṃ bhūtasaṅghaiśca bhāsvaraiḥ ||40||

41gītavāditrasannādairhāsyalāsyasamanvitam
|
valgitāsphoṭitotkṛṣṭaiḥ puṇyairgandhaiśca sevitam ||41||

42stūyamānaṃ stavairdivyairṛṣibhirbrahmavādibhiḥ
|
goptāraṃ sarvabhūtānāmiṣvāsadharamacyutam ||42||

43vāsudevastu taṃ dṛṣṭvā jagāma śirasā kṣitim
|
pārthena saha dharmātmā gṛṇan brahma sanātanam ||43||

44lokadiṃ viśvakarmāṇamajamīśānamavyayam
|
manasaḥ paramaṃ yoniṃ svaṃ vāyuṃ jyotiṣāṃ nidhim ||44||

45sraṣṭāraṃ vāridhārāṇāṃ bhuvaśca prakṛtiṃ parām
|
devadānavayakṣāṇāṃ mānavānāṃ ca sādhanam ||45||

46yogānāṃ ca paraṃ dhāma dṛṣṭaṃ brahmavidāṃ nidhim
|
carācarasya sraṣṭāraṃ pratihartārameva ca ||46||

47kālakopaṃ mahātmānaṃ śakrasūryaguṇodayam
|
vavande taṃ tadā kṛṣṇo vāṅmanobuddhikarmabhiḥ ||47||

48yaṃ prapadyanti vidvāṃsaḥ sūkṣmādhyātmapadaiṣiṇaḥ
|
tamajaṃ kāraṇātmānaṃ jagmatuḥ śaraṇaṃ bhavam ||48||

49arjunaścāpi taṃ devaṃ bhūyo bhūyo'pyavandata
|
jñātvā taṃ sarvabhūtādiṃ bhūtabhavyabhavodbhavam ||49||

50tatastāvāgatau dṛṣṭvā naranārāyaṇāvubhau
|
suprasannamanāḥ śarvaḥ provāca prahasanniva ||50||

51svāgataṃ vo naraśreṣṭhāvuttiṣṭhetāṃ gataklamau
|
kiṃ ca vāmīpsitaṃ vīrau manasaḥ kṣipramucyatām ||51||

52yena kāryeṇa saṃprāptau yuvāṃ tatsādhayāmi kim
|
vriyatāmātmanaḥ śreyastat sarvaṃ pradadāni vām ||52||

53tatastadvacanaṃ śrutvā pratyutthāya kṛtāñjalī
|
vāsudevārjunau sarvaṃ tuṣṭuvāte mahāmatī ||53||

54bhaktyā stavena divyena mahātmānāvaninditau
|
kṛṣṇārjunāvūcatuḥ
|
||54||

55namo bhavāya śarvāya rudrāya varadāya ca
|
paśūnāṃ pataye nityamugrāya ca kapardine ||55||

56mahādevāya bhīmāya tryaṃbakāya ca śāntaye
|
īśānāya makhaghnāya namo'stvandhakaghātine ||56||

57kumāragurave tubhyaṃ nīlagrīvāya vedhase
|
pinākine haviṣyāya satyāya vibhave sadā ||57||

58vilohitāya dhūmrāya vyādhāyānaparājite
|
nityanīlaśikhaṇḍāya śūline divyacakṣuṣe ||58||

59hotre potre trinetrāya vyādhāya vasuretase
|
acintyāyāmbikābhartre sarvadevastutāya ca ||59||

60vṛṣadhvajāya muṇḍāya jaṭine brahmacāriṇe
|
tapyamānāya salile brahmaṇyāyājitāya ca ||60||

61viśvātmane viśvasṛje viśvamāvṛtya tiṣṭhate
|
namo namaste sevyāya bhūtānāṃ prabhave sadā ||61||

62brahmavaktrāya sarvāya śaṃkarāya śivāya ca
|
namo'stu vācaspataye prajānāṃ pataye namaḥ ||62||

63namo viśvasya pataye mahatāṃ pataye namaḥ
|
namaḥ sahasraśirase sahasrabhujamṛtyave ||63||

64sahasranetrapādāya namo'saṃkhyeyakarmaṇe
|
namo hiraṇyavarṇāya hiraṇyakavacāya ca
|
bhaktānukampine nityaṃ sidhyatāṃ no varaḥ prabho ||64||

65sañjaya uvāca|
evaṃ stutvā mahādevaṃ vāsudevaḥ sahārjunaḥ
|
prasādayāmāsa bhavaṃ tadā hyastropalabdhaye ||65||
