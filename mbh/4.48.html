Mahābhārata 4.48 (Virāṭaparvan)

1karṇa uvāca|sarvānāyuṣmato bhītān saṃtrastāniva lakṣaye
|
ayuddhamanasaścaiva sarvāṃścaivānavasthitān ||1||

2yadyeṣa rājā matsyānāṃ yadi bībhatsurāgataḥ
|
ahamāvārayiṣyāmi veleva makarālayam ||2||

3mama cāpaprayuktānāṃ śarāṇāṃ nataparvaṇām
|
nāvṛttirgacchatāṃ teṣāṃ sarpāṇāmiva sarpatām ||3||

4rukmapuṅkhāḥ sutīkṣṇāgrā muktā hastavatā mayā
|
chādayantu śarāḥ pārthaṃ śalabhā iva pādapam ||4||

5śarāṇāṃ puṅkhasaktānāṃ maurvyābhihatayā dṛḍham
|
śrūyatāṃ talayoḥ śabdo bheryorāhatayoriva ||5||

6samāhito hi bībhatsurvarṣāṇyaṣṭau ca pañca ca
|
jātasnehaśca yuddhe'smin mayi saṃprahariṣyati ||6||

7pātrībhūtaśca kaunteyo brāhmaṇo guṇavāniva
|
śaraughān pratigṛhṇātu mayā muktān sahasraśaḥ ||7||

8eṣa caiva maheṣvāsastriṣu lokeṣu viśrutaḥ
|
ahaṃ cāpi naraśreṣṭhāda‌rjunānnāvaraḥ kvacit ||8||

9itaścetaśca nirmuktaiḥ kāñcanairgārdhravājitaiḥ
|
dṛśyatāmadya vai vyoma khadyotairiva saṃvṛtam ||9||

10adyāhamṛṇamakṣayyaṃ purā vācā prātaśrutam
|
dhārtarāṣṭrāya dāsyāmi nihatya samare'rjunam ||10||

11antarācchidyamānānāṃ puṅkhānāṃ vyatiśīryatām
|
śalabhānāmivākāśe pracāraḥ saṃpradṛśyatām ||11||

12indrāśanisamasparśaṃ mahendrasamatejasam
|
ardayiṣyāmyahaṃ pārthamulkābhiriva kuñjaram ||12||

13rathādatirathaṃ śūraṃ sarvaśastrabhṛtāṃ varam
|
vivaśaṃ pārthamādāsye garutmāniva pannagam ||13||

14tamagnimiva durdharṣamasiśaktiśarendhanam
|
pāṇḍavāgnimahaṃ dīptaṃ pradahantamivāhitam ||14||

15aśvavegapurovāto rathaughastanayitnumān
|
śaradhāro mahāmeghaḥ śamayiṣyāmi pāṇḍavam ||15||

16matkārmukavinirmuktāḥ pārthamāśīviṣopamāḥ
|
śarāḥ samabhisarpantu valmīkamiva pannagāḥ ||16||

17sutejanai rukmapuṅkhaiḥ sudhautairnataparvabhiḥ
|
ācitaṃ paśya kaunteyaṃ karṇikārairivācalam ||17||

18jāmadagnyānmayā hyastraṃ yatprāptamṛṣisattamāt
|
tadupāśritya vīryaṃ ca yudhyeyamapi vāsavam ||18||

19dhvajāgre vānarastiṣṭhan bhallena nihato mayā
|
adyaiva patatāṃ bhūmau vinadan bhairavān ravān ||19||

20śatrormayā vipannānāṃ bhūtānāṃ dhvajavāsinām
|
diśaḥ pratiṣṭhamānānāmastu śabdo divaṃgamaḥ ||20||

21adya duryodhanasyāhaṃ śalyaṃ hṛdi cirasthitam
|
samūlamuddhariṣyāmi bībhatsuṃ pātayan rathāt ||21||

22hatāśvaṃ virathaṃ pārthaṃ pauruṣe paryavasthitam
|
niḥśvasantaṃ yathā nāgamadya paśyantu kauravāḥ ||22||

23kāmaṃ gacchantu kuravo dhanamādāya kevalam
|
ratheṣu vā'pi tiṣṭhanto yuddhaṃ paśyantu māmakam ||23||
