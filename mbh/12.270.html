Mahābhārata 12.270 (Śāntiparvan)

1kapila uvāca |
vedāḥ pramāṇaṃ lokānāṃ na vedāḥ pṛṣṭhataḥ kṛtāḥ
|
dve brahmaṇī veditavye śabdabrahma paraṃ ca yat ||1||

2śabdabrahmaṇi niṣṇātaḥ paraṃ brahmādhigacchati
|
śarīrametatkurute yadvede kurute tanum ||2||

3kṛtaśuddhaśarīro hi pātraṃ bhavati brāhmaṇaḥ
|
ānantyamatra buddhyedaṃ karmaṇāṃ tadbravīmi te ||3||

4anāgamamanaitihyaṃ pratyakṣaṃ lokasākṣikam
|
dharma ityeva ye yajñānvitanvanti nirāśiṣaḥ ||4||

5utpannatyāgino lubdhāḥ kṛpāsūyāvivarjitāḥ
|
dhanānāmeṣa vai panthāstīrtheṣu pratipādanam ||5||

6anāśritāḥ pāpakarma kadācitkarmayoginaḥ
|
manaḥsaṃkalpasaṃsiddhā viśuddhajñānaniścayāḥ ||6||

7akrudhyanto'nasūyanto nirahaṅkāramatsarāḥ
|
jñānaniṣṭhāstriśuklāśca sarvabhūtahite ratāḥ ||7||

8āsan gṛhasthā bhūyiṣṭhā avyutkrāntāḥ svakarmasu
|
rājānaśca tathā yuktā brāhmaṇāśca yathāvidhi ||8||

9samā hyārjavasampannāḥ saṃtuṣṭā jñānaniścayāḥ
|
pratyakṣadharmāḥ śucayaḥ śraddadhānāḥ parāvare ||9||

10purastādbhāvitātmāno yathāvaccaritavratāḥ
|
caranti dharmaṃ kṛcche'pi durge caivāpi saṃhatāḥ ||10||

11saṃhatya dharmaṃ caratāṃ purā''sītsukhameva tat
|
teṣāṃ nāsīdvidhātavyaṃ prāyaścittaṃ kathaṃcana ||11||

12satyaṃ hi dharmamāsthāya durādharṣatamā matāḥ
|
na mātrāmanurudhyante na dharmacchalamantataḥ ||12||

13ya eva prathamaḥ kalpastamevābhyācaransaha
|
teṣāṃ nāsīdvidhātavyaṃ prāyaścittaṃ kadācana ||13||

14tasminvidhau sthitānāṃ hi prāyaścittaṃ na vidyate
|
durbalātmana utpannaṃ prāyaścittamiti śrutiḥ ||14||

15evaṃ bahuvidhā viprāḥ purāṇā yajñavāhanāḥ
|
traividyavṛddhāḥ śucayo vṛttavanto yaśasvinaḥ ||15||

16yajanto'haraharyajñairnirāśīrbandhanā budhāḥ
|
teṣāṃ yajñāśca vedāśca karmāṇi ca yathāgamam ||16||

17āgamāśca yathākāle saṃkalpāśca yathākramam
|
apetakāmakrodhānāṃ duścarācārakarmaṇām ||17||

18svakarmabhiḥ saṃśitānāṃ prakṛtyā śaṃsitātmanām
|
ṛjūnāṃ śamanityānāṃ sveṣu karmasu vartatām ||18||

19sarvamānantyamevāsīditi naḥ śāśvatī śrutiḥ
|
teṣāmadīnasattvānāṃ duścarācārakarmaṇām ||19||

20svakarmabhiḥ sambhṛtānāṃ tapo ghoratvamāgatam
|
taṃ sadācāramāścaryaṃ purāṇaṃ śāśvataṃ dhruvam ||20||

21aśaknuvadbhiścarituṃ kiṃciddharmeṣu sūkṣmatām
|
nirāpaddharma ācāro hyapramādo'parābhavaḥ ||21||

22sarvavarṇeṣu jāteṣu nāsītkaścidvyatikramaḥ
|
vyastamekaṃ caturdhā hi brāhmaṇā āśramaṃ viduḥ ||22||

23taṃ santo vidhivatprāpya gacchanti paramāṃ gatim
|
gṛhebhya eva niṣkramya vanamanye samāśritāḥ ||23||

24gṛhamevābhisaṃśritya tato'nye brahmacāriṇaḥ
|
ta ete divi dṛśyante jyotirbhūtā dvijātayaḥ ||24||

25nakṣatrāṇīva dhiṣṇyeṣu bahavastārakāgaṇāḥ
|
ānantyamupasamprāptāḥ santoṣāditi vaidikam ||25||

26yadyāgacchanti saṃsāraṃ punaryoniṣu tādṛśāḥ
|
na lipyante pāpakṛtyaiḥ kadācitkarmayonitaḥ ||26||

27evameva brahmacārī śuśrūṣurghoraniścayaḥ
|
evaṃ yukto brāhmaṇaḥ syādanyo brāhmaṇako bhavet ||27||

28karmaivaṃ puruṣasyāha śubhaṃ vā yadi vā'śubham
|
evaṃ pakvakaṣāyāṇāmānantyena śrutena ca ||28||

29sarvamānantyamāsīdvai evaṃ naḥ śāśvatī śrutiḥ
|
teṣāmapetatṛṣṇānāṃ nirṇiktānāṃ śubhātmanām ||29||

30caturthopaniṣaddharmaḥ sādhāraṇa iti smṛtiḥ
|
saṃsiddhaiḥ sādhyate nityaṃ brāhmaṇairniyatātmabhiḥ ||30||

31saṃtoṣamūlastyāgātmā jñānādhiṣṭhānamucyate
|
apavargamatirnityo yatidharmaḥ sanātanaḥ ||31||

32sādhāraṇaḥ kevalo vā yathābalamupāsate
|
gacchatāṃ gacchatāṃ kṣemaṃ durbalo'trāvasīdati
|
brahmaṇaḥ padamanvicchansaṃsārānmucyate śuciḥ ||32||

33syūmaraśmiruvāca |
ye bhuñjate ye dadate yajante'dhīyate ca ye
|
mātrābhirupalabdhābhirye vā tyāgaṃ samāśritāḥ ||33||

34eteṣāṃ pretyabhāve tu katamaḥ svargajittamaḥ
|
etadācakṣva me brahman yathātattvena pṛcchataḥ ||34||

35kapila uvāca |
parigrahāḥ śubhāḥ sarve guṇatāmabhyupāgatāḥ
|
na tu tyāgasukhaṃ prāptā etattvamapi paśyasi ||35||

36syūmaraśmiruvāca |
bhavanto jñānaniṣṭhā vai gṛhasthāḥ karmaniścayāḥ
|
āśramāṇāṃ ca sarveṣāṃ niṣṭhāyāmaikyamucyate ||36||

37ekatvena pṛthaktvena viśeṣo nātra dṛśyate
|
tadyathāvadyathānyāyaṃ bhagavānprabravītu me ||37||

38kapila uvāca |
śarīrapaktiḥ karmāṇi jñānaṃ tu paramā gatiḥ
|
kaṣāye karmabhiḥ pakve rasajñāne ca tiṣṭhati ||38||

39ānṛśaṃsyaṃ kṣamā śāntirahiṃsā satyamārjavam
|
adroho'nabhimānaśca hrīstitikṣā śamastathā ||39||

40panthāno brahmaṇastvete etaiḥ prāpnoti yatparam
|
tadvidvānanubuddhyeta manasā karmaniścayam ||40||

41yāṃ viprāḥ sarvataḥ śāntā viśuddhā jñānaniścayāḥ
|
gatiṃ gacchanti saṃtuṣṭāstāmāhuḥ paramāṃ gatim ||41||

42vedāṃśca veditavyaṃ ca viditvā ca yathāsthitim
|
evaṃ vedavidityāhurato'nyo vātarecakaḥ ||42||

43sarvaṃ vidurvedavido vede sarvaṃ pratiṣṭhitam
|
vede hi niṣṭhā sarvasya yadyadasti ca nāsti ca ||43||

44eṣaiva niṣṭhā sarvatra yattadasti ca nāsti ca
|
etadantaṃ ca madhyaṃ ca saccā'sacca vijānataḥ ||44||

45samāptaṃ tyāga ityeva sarvavedeṣu niṣṭhitam
|
saṃtoṣa ityanugatamapavarge pratiṣṭhitam ||45||

46ṛtaṃ satyaṃ viditaṃ veditavyaṃ sarvasyāmā sthāvaraṃ jaṅgamaṃ ca
|
sarvaṃ sukhaṃ yacchivamuttaraṃ ca brahmāvyaktaṃ prabhavaścāvyayaṃ ca ||46||

47tejaḥ kṣamā śāntiranāmayaṃ śubhaṃ tathāvidhaṃ vyoma sanātanaṃ dhruvam
|
etaiḥ sarvairgamyate buddhinetraistasmai namo brahmaṇe brāhmaṇāya ||47||
