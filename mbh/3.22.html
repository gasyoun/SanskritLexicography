Mahābhārata 3.22 (Āraṇyakaparvan)

1vāsudeva uvāca|
tato'haṃ bharataśreṣṭha pragṛhya ruciraṃ dhanuḥ
|
śarairapātayaṃ saubhācchirāṃsi vibudhadviṣām||1||

2śarāṃścāśīviṣākārānūrdhvagāṃstigmatejasaḥ
|
preṣayaṃ śālvarājāya śārṅgamuktānsuvāsasaḥ||2||

3tato nādṛśyata tadā saubhaṃ kurukulodvaha
|
antarhitaṃ māyayābhūttato'haṃ vismito'bhavam||3||

4atha dānavasaṅghāste vikṛtānanamūrdhajāḥ
|
udakrośanmahārāja dhiṣṭhite mayi bhārata||4||

5tato'straṃ śabdasāhaṃ vai tvaramāṇo mahāraṇe
|
ayojayaṃ tadvadhāya tataḥ śabda upāramat||5||

6hatāste dānavāḥ sarve yaiḥ sa śabda udīritaḥ
|
śarairādityasaṅkāśairjvalitaiḥ śabdasādhanaiḥ||6||

7tasminnuparate śabde punarevānyato'bhavat
|
śabdo'paro mahārāja tatrāpi prāharaṃ śaraiḥ||7||

8evaṃ daśa diśaḥ sarvāstiryagūrdhvaṃ ca bhārata
|
nādayāmāsurasurāste cāpi nihatā mayā||8||

9tataḥ prāgjyotiṣaṃ gatvā punareva vyadṛśyata
|
saubhaṃ kāmagamaṃ vīra mohayanmama cakṣuṣī||9||

10tato lokāntakaraṇo dānavo dāruṇākṛtiḥ
|
śilāvarṣeṇa mahatā sahasā māṃ samāvṛṇot||10||

11so'haṃ parvatavarṣeṇa vadhyamānaḥ punaḥ punaḥ
|
valmīka iva rājendra parvatopacito'bhavam||11||

12tato'haṃ parvatacitaḥ sahayaḥ sahasārathiḥ
|
aprakhyātimiyāṃ rājansarvataḥ parvataiścitaḥ||12||

13tato vṛṣṇipravīrā ye mamāsansainikāstadā
|
te bhayārtā diśaḥ sarve sahasā vipradudruvuḥ||13||

14tato hāhākṛtamabhūtsarvaṃ kila viśāṃpate
|
dyauśca bhūmiśca khaṃ caivādṛśyamāne tathā mayi||14||

15tato viṣaṇṇamanaso mama rājansuhṛjjanāḥ
|
ruruduścukruśuścaiva duḥkhaśokasamanvitāḥ||15||

16dviṣatāṃ ca praharṣo'bhūdārtiścādviṣatāmapi
|
evaṃ vijitavānvīra paścādaśrauṣamacyuta||16||

17tato'hamindradayitaṃ sarvapāṣāṇabhedanam
|
vajramudyamya tānsarvānparvatānsamaśātayam||17||

18tataḥ parvatabhārārtā mandaprāṇaviceṣṭitāḥ
|
hayā mama mahārāja vepamānā ivābhavan||18||

19meghajāla ivākāśe vidāryābhyuditaṃ ravim
|
dṛṣṭvā māṃ bāndhavāḥ sarve harṣamāhārayanpunaḥ||19||

20tataḥ parvatabhārārtānmandaprāṇaviceṣṭitān
|
hayānsaṃdṛśya māṃ sūtaḥ prāha tātkālikaṃ vacaḥ||20||

21sādhu saṃpaśya vārṣṇeya śālvaṃ saubhapatiṃ sthitam
|
alaṃ kṛṣṇāvamanyainaṃ sādhu yatnaṃ samācara||21||

22mārdavaṃ sakhitāṃ caiva śālvādadya vyapāhara
|
jahi śālvaṃ mahābāho mainaṃ jīvaya keśava||22||

23sarvaiḥ parākramairvīra vadhyaḥ śatruramitrahan
|
na śatruravamantavyo durbalo'pi balīyasā||23||

24yo'pi syātpīṭhagaḥ kaścitkiṃ punaḥ samare sthitaḥ
|
sa tvaṃ puruṣaśārdūla sarvayatnairimaṃ prabho||24||

25jahi vṛṣṇikulaśreṣṭha mā tvāṃ kālotyagātpunaḥ
|
naiṣa mārdavasādhyo vai mato nāpi sakhā tava||25||

26yena tvaṃ yodhito vīra dvārakā cāvamarditā
|
evamādi tu kaunteya śrutvāhaṃ sārathervacaḥ||26||

27tattvametaditi jñātvā yuddhe matimadhārayam
|
vadhāya śālvarājasya saubhasya ca nipātane||27||

28dārukaṃ cābruvaṃ vīra muhūrtaṃ sthīyatāmiti
|
tato pratihataṃ divyamabhedyamativīryavān||18||

29āgneyamastraṃ dayitaṃ sarvasāhaṃ mahāprabham
|
yojayaṃ tatra dhanuṣā dānavāntakaraṃ raṇe||19||

30yakṣāṇāṃ rākṣasānāṃ ca dānavānāṃ ca saṃyuge
|
rājñāṃ ca pratilomānāṃ bhasmāntakaraṇaṃ mahat||30||

31kṣurāntamamalaṃ cakraṃ kālāntakayamopamam
|
anumantryāhamatulaṃ dviṣatāṃ vinibarhaṇam||31||

32jahi saubhaṃ svavīryeṇa ye cātra ripavo mama
|
ityuktvā bhujavīryeṇa tasmai prāhiṇavaṃ ruṣā||32||

33rūpaṃ sudarśanasyāsīdākāśe patatastadā
|
dvitīyasyeva sūryasya yugānte prapatiṣyataḥ||33||

34tatsamāsādya nagaraṃ saubhaṃ vyapagatatviṣam
|
madhyena pāṭayāmāsa krakaco dārvivocchritam||34||

35dvidhā kṛtaṃ tataḥ saubhaṃ sudarśanabalāddhatam
|
maheśvaraśaroddhūtaṃ papāta tripuraṃ yathā||35||

36tasminnipatite saubhe cakramāgātkaraṃ mama
|
punaścādāya vegena śālvāyetyahamabruvam||36||

37tataḥ śālvaṃ gadāṃ gurvīmāvidhyantaṃ mahāhave
|
dvidhā cakāra sahasā prajajvāla ca tejasā||37||

38tasminvinihite vīre dānavāstrastacetasaḥ
|
hāhābhūtā diśo jagmurarditā mama sāyakaiḥ||38||

39tato'haṃ samavasthāpya rathaṃ saubhasamīpataḥ
|
śaṅkhaṃ pradhmāpya harṣeṇa suhṛdaḥ paryaharṣayam||39||

40tanmeruśikharākāraṃ vidhvastāṭṭālagopuram
|
dahyamānamabhiprekṣya striyastāḥ saṃpradudruvuḥ||40||

41evaṃ nihatya samare saubhaṃ śālvaṃ nipātya ca
|
ānartātpunarāgamya suhṛdāṃ prītimāvaham||41||

42tadetatkāraṇaṃ rājanyadahaṃ nāgasāhvayam
|
nāgamaṃ paravīraghna na hi jīvetsuyodhanaḥ||42||

43mayyāgatethavā vīrā dyūtaṃ na bhavitā tathā
|
adyāhaṃ kiṃ kariṣyāmi bhinnaseturivodakam||43||

44vaiśampāyana uvāca|
evamuktvā mahābāhuḥ kauravaṃ puruṣottamaḥ
|
āmantrya prayayau śrīmānpāṇḍavānmadhusūdanaḥ||44||

45abhivādya mahābāhurdharmarājaṃ yudhiṣṭhiram
|
rājñā mūrdhanyupāghrāto bhīmena ca mahābhujaḥ||45||

46pariṣvaktaścārjunena yamābhyāṃ cābhivāditaḥ
|
saṃmānitaśca dhaumyena draupadyā cārcitośrubhiḥ||46||

47subhadrāmabhimanyuṃ ca rathamāropya kāccanam
|
āruroha rathaṃ kṛṣṇaḥ pāṇḍavairabhipūjitaḥ||47||

48śaibyasugrīvayuktena rathenādityavarcasā
|
dvārakāṃ prayayau kṛṣṇaḥ samāśvāsya yudhiṣṭhiram||48||

49tataḥ prayāte dāśārhe dhṛṣṭadyumno'pi pārṣataḥ
|
draupadeyānupādāya prayayau svapuraṃ tadā||49||

50dhṛṣṭaketuḥ svasāraṃ ca samādāyātha cedirāṭ
|
jagāma pāṇḍavāndṛṣṭvā ramyāṃ śuktimatīṃ purīm||50||

51kekayāścāpyanujñātāḥ kaunteyenāmitaujasā
|
āmantrya pāṇḍavānsarvānprayayuste'pi bhārata||51||

52brāhmaṇāśca viśaścaiva tathā viṣayavāsinaḥ
|
visṛjyamānāḥ subhṛśaṃ na tyajanti sma pāṇḍavān||52||

53samavāyaḥ sa rājendra sumahādbhutadarśanaḥ
|
āsīnmahātmanāṃ teṣāṃ kāmyake bharatarṣabha||53||

54yudhiṣṭhirastu viprāṃstānanumānya mahāmanāḥ
|
śaśāsa puruṣānkāle rathānyojayateti vai||54||
