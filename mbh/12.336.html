Mahābhārata 12.336 (Śāntiparvan)

1bhīṣma uvāca|
tato'tīte mahākalpe utpanne'ṅgirasaḥ sute
|
babhūvurnirvṛtā devā jāte devapurohite||1||

2bṛhadbrahma mahacceti śabdāḥ paryāyavācakāḥ
|
ebhiḥ samanvito rājanguṇairvidvān bṛhaspatiḥ||2||

3tasya śiṣyo babhūvāgryo rājoparicaro vasuḥ
|
adhītavāṃstadā śāstraṃ samyak citraśikhaṇḍijam||3||

4sa rājā bhāvitaḥ pūrvaṃ daivena vidhinā vasuḥ
|
pālayāmāsa pṛthivīṃ divamākhaṇḍalo yathā||4||

5tasya yajño mahānāsīdaśvamedho mahātmanaḥ
|
bṛhaspatirupādhyāyastatra hotā babhūva ha||5||

6prajāpatisutāścātra sadasyāścābhavaṃstrayaḥ
|
ekataśca dvitaścaiva tritaścaiva maharṣayaḥ||6||

7dhanuṣākhyo'tha raibhyaśca arvāvasuparāvasū
|
ṛṣirmedhātithiścaiva tāṇḍyaścaiva mahānṛṣiḥ||7||

8ṛṣiḥ śāntirmahābhāgastathā vedaśirāśca yaḥ
|
ṛṣiśreṣṭhaśca kapila: śālihotrapitā smṛtaḥ||8||

9ādyaḥ kaṭhastaittiriśca vaiṣampāyanapūrvajaḥ
|
kaṇvo'tha devahotraśca ete ṣoḍaśa kīrtitāḥ||9||

10saṃbhūtāḥ sarvasaṃbhārāstasminrājanmahākratau
|
na tatra paśughāto'bhūtsarājaivaṃ sthito'bhavat||10||

11ahiṃsraḥ śucirakṣudro nirāśīḥ karmasaṃstutaḥ
|
āraṇyakapadodbhūtā bhāgāstatropakalpitāḥ||11||

12prītastato'sya bhagavāndevadevaḥ purātanaḥ
|
sākṣāttaṃ darśayāmāsa so'dṛśyo'nyena kenacit||12||

13svayaṃ bhāgamupāghrāya puroḍāśaṃ gṛhītavān
|
adṛśyena hṛto bhāgo devena harimedhasā||13||

14bṛhaspatistataḥ kruddhaḥ srucamudyamya vegitaḥ
|
ākāśaṃ ghnansrucaḥ pātai roṣādaśrūṇyavartayat||14||

15uvāca coparicaraṃ mayā bhāgo'yamudyataḥ
|
grāhyaḥ svayaṃ hi devena matpratyakṣaṃ na saṃśayaḥ||15||

16yudhiṣṭhira uvāca|
udyatā yajñabhāgā hi sākṣātprāptāḥ surairiha
|
kimarthamiha na prāpto darśanaṃ sa harirvibhuḥ||16||

17bhīṣma uvāca|
tataḥ sa taṃ samaddhūtaṃ bhūmipālo mahānvasuḥ
|
prasādayāmāsa muniṃ sadasyāste ca sarvaśaḥ||17||

18ūcuścainamasaṃbhrāntā na roṣaṃ kartumarhasi
|
naiṣa dharmaḥ kṛtayuge yastvaṃ roṣamacīkṛthāḥ||18||

19aroṣaṇo hyasau devo yasya bhāgo'yamudyataḥ
|
na śakyaḥ sa tvayā draṣṭumasmābhirvā bṛhaspate||19||

20yasya prasādaṃ kurute sa vaitaṃ draṣṭumarhati
|
ekatadvitatritāścocustataścitraśikhaṇḍinaḥ||20||

21vayaṃ hi brahmaṇaḥ putrā mānasāḥ parikīrtitāḥ
|
gatāḥ niḥśreyasārthaṃ hi kadāciddiśamuttarām||21||

22taptvā varṣasahasrāṇi caritvā tapa uttamam
|
ekapādāḥ sthitāḥ samyakkāṣṭhabhūtāḥ samāhitāḥ||22||

23meroruttarabhāge tu kṣīrodasyānukūlataḥ
|
sa deśo yatra nastaptaṃ tapaḥ paramadāruṇam||23||

24kathaṃ paśyema hi vayaṃ devaṃ nārāyaṇātmakam
|
vareṇyaṃ varadaṃ taṃ vai devadevaṃ sanātanam||24||

25kathaṃ paśyema hi vayaṃ devaṃ nārāyaṇaṃ tviti
|
atha vratasyāvabhṛthe vāguvācāśarīriṇī||25||

26snigdhagaṃbhīrayā vācā praharṣaṇakarī vibho
|
sutaptaṃ vastapo viprāḥ prasannenāntarātmanā||26||

27yūyaṃ jijñāsavo bhaktāḥ kathaṃ drakṣyatha taṃ vibhum
|
kṣīrodadheruttarataḥ śvetadvīpo mahāprabhaḥ||27||

28tatra nārāyaṇaparā mānavāścandravarcasaḥ
|
ekāntabhāvopagatāste bhaktāḥ puruṣottamam||28||

29te sahasrārciṣaṃ devaṃ praviśanti sanātanam
|
anindriyā nirāhārā aniṣpandāḥ sugandhinaḥ||29||

30ekāntinaste puruṣāḥ śvetadvīpanivāsinaḥ
|
gacchadhvaṃ tatra munayastatrātmā me prakāśitaḥ||30||

31atha śrutvā vayaṃ sarve vācaṃ tāmaśarīriṇīm
|
yathākhyātena mārgeṇa taṃ deśaṃ pratipedire||31||

32prāpya śvetaṃ mahādvīpaṃ taccittāstaddidṛkṣavaḥ
|
tato'smadṛṣṭiviṣayastadā pratihato'bhavat||32||

33na ca paśyāma puruṣaṃ tattejohṛtadarśanāḥ
|
tato naḥ prādurabhavadvijñānaṃ devayogajam||33||

34na kilātaptatapasā śakyate draṣṭumañjasā
|
tataḥ punarvarṣaśataṃ taptvā tātkālikaṃ mahat||34||

35vratāvasāne ca śubhānnarāndadṛśire vayam
|
śvetāṃścandrapratīkāśānsarvalakṣaṇalakṣitān||35||

36nityāñjalikṛtānbrahma japataḥ prāgudaṅmukhān
|
mānaso nāma sa japo japyate tairmahātmabhiḥ||36||

37tenaikāgramanastvena prīto bhavati vai hariḥ
|
yā'bhavanmuniśārdūla bhāḥ sūryasya yugakṣaye||37||

38ekaikasya prabhā tādṛksā'bhavanmānavasya ha
|
tejonivāsaḥ sa dvīpa iti vai menire vayam||38||

39na tatrābhyadhikaḥ kaścitsarve te samatejasaḥ
|
atha sūryasahasrasya prabhāṃ yugapadutthitām||39||

40sahasā dṛṣṭavantaḥ sma punareva bṛhaspate
|
sahitāścābhyadhāvanta tataste mānavā drutam||40||

41kṛtāñjalipuṭā hṛṣṭā nama ityeva vādinaḥ
|
tato hi vadatāṃ teṣāmaśrauṣma vipulaṃ dhvanim||41||

42baliḥ kilopahriyate tasya devasya tairnaraiḥ
|
vayaṃ tu tejasā tasya sahasā hṛtacetasaḥ||42||

43na kiṃcidapi paśyāmo hatacakṣurbalendriyāḥ
|
ekastu śabdo vitataḥ śruto'samābhirudīritaḥ||43||

44jitaṃ te puṇḍarīkākṣa namaste viśvabhāvana
|
namaste'stu hṛṣīkeśa mahāpuruṣapūrvaja||44||

45iti śabdaḥ śruto'smābhiḥ śikṣākṣarasamanvitaḥ
|
etasminnantare vāyuḥ sarvagandhavahaḥ śuciḥ||45||

46divyānyuvāha puṣpāṇi karmaṇyāścauṣadhīstathā
|
tairiṣṭaḥ pañcakālajñairharirekāntibhirnarai||46||

47bhaktyā paramayā yuktairmanovākkarmabhistadā
|
nūnaṃ tatrāgato devo yathā tairvāgudīritā||47||

48vayaṃ tvenaṃ na paśyāmo mohitāstasya māyayā
|
mārute sannivṛtte ca balau ca pratipādite||48||

49cintāvyākulitātmāno jātā: smo'ṅgirasāṃ vara
|
mānavānāṃ sahasreṣu teṣu vai śuddhayoniṣu||49||

50asmānna kaścinmanasā cakṣuṣā vā'pyapūjayat
|
te'pi svasthā munigaṇā ekabhāvamanuvratāḥ||50||

51nāsmāsu dadhire bhāvaṃ brahmabhāvamanuṣṭhitāḥ
|
tato'smānsupariśrāntāṃstapasā cātikarśitān||51||

52uvāca svasthaṃ kimapi bhūtaṃ tatrāśarīrakam
|
deva uvāca |
dṛṣṭā vaḥ puruṣāḥ śvetāḥ sarvendriyavivarjitāḥ||52||

53dṛṣṭo bhavati deveśa ebhirdṛṣṭairdvijottamaiḥ
|
gacchadhvaṃ munayaḥ sarve yathāgatamito'cirāt||53||

54na sa śakyastvabhaktena draṣṭuṃ devaḥ kathañcana
|
kāmaṃ kālena mahatā ekāntitvamupāgataiḥ||54||

55śakyo draṣṭuṃ sa bhagavānprabhāmaṇḍaladurdṛśaḥ
|
mahatkāryaṃ ca kartavyaṃ yuṣmābhirdvijasattamāḥ||55||

56itaḥ kṛtayuge'tīte viparyāsaṃ gate'pi ca
|
vaivasvate'ntare viprāḥ prāpte tretāyuge punaḥ||56||

57surāṇāṃ kāryasiddhyarthaṃ sahāyā vai bhaviṣyathā
|
tatastadadbhutaṃ vākyaṃ niśamyaivāmṛtopamam||57||

58tasya prasādātprāptāḥ smo deśamīpsitamañjasā
|
evaṃ sutapasā caiva havyakavyaistathaiva ca||58||

59devo'smābhirna dṛṣṭaḥ sa kathaṃ tvaṃ draṣṭumarhasi
|
nārāyaṇo mahadbhūtaṃ viśvasṛgdhavyakavyabhuk||59||

60anādinidhano'vyakto devadānavapūjitaḥ
|
evamekatavākyena dvitatritamatena ca||60||

61anunītaḥ sadasyaiśca bṛhaspatirudāradhīḥ
|
samāpayattato yajñaṃ daivataṃ samapūjayat||61||

62samāptayajño rājā'pi prajāṃ pālitavānvasuḥ
|
brahmaśāpāddivo bhraṣṭaḥ praviveśa mahīṃ tataḥ||62||

63sa rājā rājaśārdūla satyadharmaparāyaṇaḥ
|
antarbhūmigataścaiva satataṃ dharmavatsalaḥ||63||

64nārāyaṇaparo bhūtvā nārāyaṇajapaṃ japan
|
tasyaiva ca prasādena punarevotthitastu saḥ||64||

65mahītalādgataḥ sthānaṃ brahmaṇaḥ samanantaram
|
parāṃ gatimanuprāpta iti naiṣṭhikamañjasā||65||
