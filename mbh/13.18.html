Mahābhārata 13.18 (Anuśāsanaparvan)

1vaiśampāyana uvāca|
mahāyogī tataḥ prāha kṛṣṇadvaipāyano muniḥ
|
paṭhasva putra bhadraṃ te prīyatāṃ te maheśvaraḥ ||1||

2purā putra mayā merau tapyatā paramaṃ tapaḥ
|
putrahetormahārāja stava eṣo'nukīrtitaḥ ||2||

3labdhavānīpsitānkāmānahaṃ vai pāṇḍunandana
|
tathā tvamapi śarvāddhi sarvānkāmānavāpsyasi ||3||

4kapilaśca tataḥ prāha sāṃkhyarṣirdevasaṃmataḥ
|
mayā janmānyanekāni bhaktyā cārādhito bhavaḥ ||4||

5prītaśca bhagavān jñānaṃ dadau mama bhavāntakam
|
cāruśīrṣastataḥ prāha śakrasya dayitaḥ sakhā
|
ālamvāyana ityevaṃ viśrutaḥ karuṇātmakaḥ ||5||

6mayā gokarṇamāsādya tapastaptvā śataṃ samāḥ
|
ayonijānāṃ dāntānāṃ dharmajñānāṃ suvarcasām ||6||

7ajarāṇāmaduḥkhānāṃ śatavarṣasahasriṇām
|
labdhaṃ putraśataṃ śarvātpurā pāṇḍunṛpātmaja ||7||

8vālmīkiścāha bhagavānyudhiṣṭhiramidaṃ vacaḥ
|
vivāde sāgnimunibhirbrahmaghno vai bhavāniti ||8||

9uktaḥ kṣaṇena cāviṣṭastenādharmeṇa bhārata
|
so'hamīśānamanaghamamoghaṃ śaraṇaṃ gataḥ ||9||

10muktaścāsmi tataḥ pāpaistato duḥkhavināśanaḥ
|
āha māṃ tripuraghno vai yaśaste'gryaṃ bhaviṣyati ||10||

11jāmadagnyaśca kaunteyamidaṃ dharmabhṛtāṃ varaḥ
|
ṛṣimadhye sthitaḥ prāha jvalanniva divākaraḥ ||11||

12pitṛvipravadhenāhamārto vai pāṇḍavāgraja
|
śucirbhūtvā mahādevaṃ gato'smi śaraṇaṃ nṛpa ||12||

13nāmabhiścāstuvaṃ devaṃ tatastuṣṭo'bhavadbhavaḥ
|
paraśuṃ ca tato devo divyānyastrāṇi caiva me ||13||

14pāpaṃ ca te na bhavitā ajeyaśca bhaviṣyasi
|
na te prabhavitā mṛtyurajaraśca bhaviṣyasi ||14||

15āha māṃ bhagavānevaṃ śikhaṇḍī śivavigrahaḥ
|
tadavāptaṃ ca me sarvaṃ prasādāttasya dhīmataḥ ||15||

16viśvāmitrastadovāca kṣatriyo'haṃ tadā'bhavam
|
brāhmaṇo'haṃ bhavānīti mayā cārādhito bhavaḥ ||16||

17tatprasādānmayā prāptaṃ brāhmaṇyaṃ durlabhaṃ mahat
|
asito devalaścaiva prāha pāṇḍusutaṃ nṛpam ||17||

18śāpācchakrasya kaunteya vibho dharmo'naśattadā
|
tanme dharmaṃ yaśaścāgryamāyuścaivādadatprabhuḥ ||18||

19ṛṣirgṛtsamado nāma śakrasya dayitaḥ sakhā
|
prāhājamīḍhaṃ bhagavān bṛhaspatisamadyutiḥ ||19||

20variṣṭho nāma bhagavāṃścākṣuṣasya manoḥ sutaḥ
|
śatakratoracintyasya satre varṣasahastrike ||20||

21vartamāne'bravīdvākyaṃ sāmni hyuccārite mayā
|
rathantare dvijaśreṣṭha na samyagiti vartate ||21||

22samīkṣasva punarbuddhyā pāpaṃ tyaktvā dvijottama
|
ayajñavāhinaṃ pāpamakārṣīstvaṃ sudurmate ||22||

23evamuktvā mahākrodhaḥ prāha śaṃbhuṃ punarvacaḥ
|
prajñayā rahito duḥkhī nityabhīto vanecaraḥ ||23||

24daśavarṣasahasrāṇi daśāṣṭau ca śatāni ca
|
naṣṭapānīya pavane mṛgairanyaiśca varjite ||24||

25ayajñīyadrame deśe rurusiṃhaniṣevite
|
bhavitā tvaṃ mṛgaḥ krūro mahāduḥkhasamanvitaḥ ||25||

26tasya vākyasya nirdhane pārtha jāto hyahaṃ mṛgaḥ
|
tato māṃ śaraṇaṃ prāptaṃ prāha yogī maheśvaraḥ ||26||

27ajaraścāmaraścaiva bhavitā duḥkhavarjitaḥ
|
sāmyaṃ mamāstu te saukhyaṃ yuvayorvardhatāṃ kratuḥ ||27||

28anugrahānevameṣa karoti bhagavān vibhuḥ
|
paraṃ dhātā vidhātā ca sukhaduḥkhe ca sarvadā ||28||

29acintya eṣa bhagavānkarmaṇā manasā girā
|
na me tāta yudhiśreṣṭha vidyayā paṇḍitaḥ samaḥ ||29||

30vāsudevastadovāca punarmatimatāṃ varaḥ
|
suvarṇākṣo mahādevastapasā toṣito mayā ||30||

31tato'tha bhagavānāha prīto māṃ vai yudhiṣṭhira
|
arthātpriyataraḥ kṛṣṇa matprasādādbhaviṣyasi ||31||

32aparājitaśca yuddheṣu tejaścaivānalopamam
|
evaṃ sahasraśaścānyānmahādevo varaṃ dadau ||32||

33maṇimanthe'tha śaile vai purā saṃpūjito mayā
|
varṣāyutasahasrāṇāṃ sahasraṃ śatameva ca ||33||

34tato māṃ bhagavānprīta idaṃ vacanamabravīt
|
varaṃ vṛṣṇīṣva bhadraṃ te yaste manasi vartate ||34||

35tataḥ praṇamya śirasā idaṃ vacanamabruvam
|
yadi prīto mahādevo bhaktyā paramayā prabhuḥ ||35||

36nityakālaṃ taveśāna bhaktirbhavatu me sthirā
|
evamastviti bhagavānstatroktvā'ntaradhīyata ||36||

37jaigīṣavya uvāca|
mamāṣṭaguṇamaiśvaryaṃ dattaṃ bhagavatā purā
|
yatnenānyena balinā vārāṇasyāṃ yudhiṣṭhira ||37||

38garga uvāca|
catuḥṣaṣṭyaṅgamadadatkalājñānaṃ mamādbhutam
|
sarasvatyāstaṭe tuṣṭo manoyajñena pāṇḍava ||38||

39mama sahasraṃ tu sutānāṃ brahmavādinām
|
āyuścaiva saputrasya saṃvatsaraśatāyutam ||39||

40parāśara uvāca|
prasādyeha purā śarvaṃ manasā'cintayaṃ nṛpa
|
mahātapā mahātejā mahāyogī mahāyaśāḥ ||40||

41vedavyāsaḥ śriyāvāso brāhmaṇaḥ karuṇānvitaḥ
|
apyasāvīpsitaḥ putro mama syādvai maheśvarāt ||41||

42iti matvā hṛdi mataṃ prāha māṃ surasattamaḥ
|
mayi saṃbhāvanā yāsyāḥ phalātkṛṣṇo bhaviṣyati ||42||

43sāvarṇasya manoḥ sarge saptarṣiśca bhaviṣyati
|
vedānāṃ ca sa vai vaktā kuruvaṃśakarastathā ||43||

44itihāsasya kartā ca putraste jagato hitaḥ
|
bhaviṣyati mahendrasya dayitaḥ sa mahāmuniḥ ||44||

45ajaraścāmaraścaiva parāśarasutastava
|
evamuktvā sa bhagavāṃstatraivāntaradhīyata ||45||

46yudhiṣṭhira mahāyogī vīryavānakṣayo'vyayaḥ
|
māṇḍavya uvāca|
acauraścauraśaṅkāyāṃ śūle bhinno hyahaṃ tadā ||46||

47tatrasthena stuto devaḥ prāha māṃ vai nareśvara
|
mokṣaṃ prāpsyasi śūlācca jīviṣyasi samārbudam ||47||

48rujā śūlakṛtā caiva na te vipra bhaviṣyati
|
ādhibhirvyādhibhiścaiva varjitastvaṃ bhaviṣyasi ||48||

49pādāccaturthātsaṃbhūta ātmā yasmānmune tava
|
tvaṃ bhaviṣyasyanupamo janma vai saphalaṃ kuru ||49||

50tīrthābhiṣekaṃ sakalaṃ tvamavighnena cāpsyasi
|
svargaṃ caivākṣayaṃ vipra viddhāmi tavorjitam ||50||

51evamuktvā tu bhagavān vareṇyo vṛṣavāhanaḥ
|
maheśvaro mahārāja kṛttivāsā mahādyutiḥ ||51||

52sagaṇo daivataśreṣṭhastatraivāntaradhīyata
|
gālava uvāca|
viśvāmitrābhyanujñāto hyahaṃ pitaramāgataḥ ||52||

53abravīnmāṃ tato mātā duḥkhitā rudatī bhṛśam
|
kauśikenābhyanujñātaṃ putraṃ veda‌vibhūṣitam ||53||

54na tāta taruṇaṃ dāntaṃ pitā tvāṃ paśyate'nagha
|
śrutvā jananyā vacanaṃ nirāśo gurudarśane ||54||

55niyatātmā mahādevamapaśyaṃ so'bravīcca mām
|
pitā mātā ca te tvaṃ ca putra mṛtyuvivarjitāḥ ||55||

56bhaviṣyatha viśa kṣipraṃ draṣṭāsi pitaraṃ kṣaye
|
anujñāto bhagavatā gṛhaṃ gatvā yudhiṣṭhira ||56||

57apaśyaṃ pitaraṃ tāta iṣṭiṃ kṛtvā viniḥsṛtam
|
upaspṛśya gṛhītvedhmaṃ kuśāṃśca śaraṇākurūn ||57||

58tānvisṛjya ca māṃ prāha pitā sāsrāvilekṣaṇaḥ
|
praṇamantaṃ pariṣvajya mūrdhnyupāghrāya pāṇḍava ||58||

59diṣṭyā dṛṣṭo'si me putra kṛtavidya ihāgataḥ
|
vaiśampāyana uvāca|
etānyatyadbhutānyeva karmāṇyatha mahātmanaḥ||59||

60proktāni sunibhiḥ śrutvā vismayāmāsa pāṇḍavaḥ
|
tataḥ kṛṣṇo'bravīdvākyaṃ punarmatimatāṃ varaḥ ||60||

61yudhiṣṭhiraṃ dharmanidhiṃ puruhūtamiveśvaraḥ
|
vāsudeva uvāca|
upamanyurmayi prāha tapanniva divākaraḥ ||61||

62aśubhaiḥ pāpakarmāṇo ye narāḥ kaluṣīkṛtāḥ
|
īśānaṃ na prapadyante tamorājasavṛttayaḥ ||62||

63īśvaraṃ saṃprapadyante dvijā bhāvitabhāvanāḥ
|
sarvathā vartamāno'pi yo bhaktaḥ parameśvare ||63||

64sadṛśo'raṇyavāsīnāṃ munīnāṃ bhāvitātmanām
|
brahmatvaṃ keśavatvaṃ vā śakratvaṃ vā suraiḥ saha ||64||

65trailokyasyādhipatyaṃ vā tuṣṭo rudraḥ prayacchati
|
manasā'pi śivaṃ tāta ye prapadyanti mānavāḥ ||65||

66vidhūya sarvapāpāni devaiḥ saha vasanti te
|
bhittvā bhittvā ca kūlāni hutvā sarvamidaṃ jagat ||66||

67yajeddevaṃ virūpākṣaṃ na sa pāpena lipyate
|
sarvalakṣaṇahīno'pi yukto vā sarva pātakaiḥ ||67||

68sarvaṃ tudati tatpāpaṃ bhāvayañchivamātmanā
|
kīṭapakṣipataṅgānāṃ tiraścāmapi keśava ||68||

69mahādevaprapannānāṃ na bhayaṃ vidyate kvacit
|
evameva mahādevaṃ bhaktā ye mānavā bhuvi ||69||

70na te saṃsāravaśagā iti me niścitā matiḥ
|
tataḥ kṛṣṇo'bravīdvākyaṃ dharmaputraṃ yudhiṣṭhiram ||70||

71viṣṇuruvāca|
ādityacandrāvanilānalau ca dyaurbhūmirāpo vasavo'tha viśve
|
dhātāryamā śukrabṛhaspatī ca rudrāḥ sasādhyā varuṇo'tha gopaḥ ||71||

72brahmā śakro māruto brahma satyaṃ vedā yajñā dakṣiṇā vedavāhāḥ
|
somo yaṣṭā yacca havyaṃ haviśca rakṣā dīkṣā saṃyamā ye ca kecit ||72||

73svāhā vauṣaṭ brāhmaṇāḥ saurabheyī dharmaṃ cāgryaṃ kālacakraṃ balaṃ ca
|
yaśo damo buddhimatāṃ sthitiśca śubhāśubhaṃ ye munayaśca sapta ||73||

74agryā buddhirmanasā darśane ca sparśaścāgryaḥ karmaṇāṃ yā ca siddhiḥ
|
gaṇā devānāmūṣmapāḥ somapāśca lekhāḥ suyāmāstuṣitā brahmakāyāḥ ||74||

75ābhāsurā gandhapā dhūmapāśca vācā viruddhāśca manoviruddhāḥ
|
śuddhāśca nirmāṇaratāśca devāḥ sparśāśanā darśapā ājyapāśca ||75||

76cintyadyotā ye ca deveṣu mukhyā ye cāpyanye devatāścājamīḍha
|
suparṇa-gandharva-piśāca-dānavā yakṣāstathā cāraṇa-pannagāśca ||76||

77sthūlaṃ sūkṣmaṃ mṛdu cāpyasūkṣmaṃ duḥkhaṃ sukhaṃ duḥkhamanantaraṃ ca
|
sāṃkhyaṃ yogaṃ tatparāṇāṃ paraṃ ca śarvājjātaṃ viddhi yatkīrtitaṃ me ||77||

78tatsaṃbhūtā bhūtakṛto vareṇyāḥ sarve devā bhuvanasyāsya gopāḥ
|
āviśyemāṃ dharaṇīṃ ye'bhyarakṣan purātanīṃ tasya devasya sṛṣṭim ||78||

79vicinvantastapasā tatsthavīyaḥ kiñcittattvaṃ prāṇahetornato'smi
|
dadātu devaḥ sa varāniheṣṭānabhiṣṭuto naḥ prabhuravyayaḥ sadā ||79||

80imaṃ stavaṃ sanniyatendriyaśca bhūtvā śuciryaḥ puruṣaḥ paṭheta
|
abhagnayogo niyato māsamekaṃ saṃprāpnuyādaśvamedhe phalaṃ yat ||80||

81vedān kṛtsnān brāhmaṇaḥ prāpnuyāttu jayennṛpaḥ pārtha mahīṃ ca kṛtsnām
|
vaiśyo lābhaṃ prāpnuyānnaipuṇaṃ ca śūdro gatiṃ pretya tathā sukhaṃ ca ||81||

82stavarājamimaṃ kṛttvā rudrāya dadhire manaḥ
|
sarvadoṣāpahaṃ puṇyaṃ pavitraṃ ca yaśasvinaḥ ||82||

83yāvantyasya śarīreṣu romakūpāṇi bhārata
|
tāvantyabdasahasrāṇi svarge vasati mānavaḥ ||83||
