Mahābhārata 12.60 (Śāntiparvan)

1vaiśampāyana uvāca|
tataḥ punaḥ sa gāṅgeyamabhivādya pitāmaham
|
prāñjalirniyato bhūtvā paryapṛcchadyudhiṣṭhiraḥ||1||

2ke dharmāḥ sarvavarṇānāṃ cāturvarṇyasya ke pṛthak
|
cāturvarṇyāśramāṇāṃ ca rājadharmāśca ke matāḥ||2||

3kena vai vardhate rāṣṭraṃ rājā kena vivardhate
|
kena paurāśca bhṛtyāśca vardhante bharatarṣabha||3||

4kośaṃ daṇḍaṃ ca durgaṃ ca sahāyānmantriṇastathā
|
ṛtvikpurohitācāryānkīdṛśānvarjayennṛpaḥ||4||

5keṣu viśvasitavyaṃ syāt rājñā kasyāñcidāpadi
|
kuto vā''tmā dṛḍhaṃ rakṣyastanme brūhi pitāmaha||5||

6bhīṣma uvāca|
namo dharmāya mahate namaḥ kṛṣṇāya vedhase
|
brāhmaṇebhyo namaskṛtya dharmānvakṣyāmi śāśvatān||6||

7akrodhaḥ satyavacanaṃ saṃvibhāgaḥ kṣamā tathā
|
prajanaḥ sveṣu dāreṣu śaucamadroha eva ca||7||

8ārjavaṃ bhṛtyabharaṇaṃ navaite sārvavarṇikāḥ
|
brāhmaṇasya tu yo dharmastaṃ te vakṣyāmi kevalam||8||

9damameva mahārāja dharmabāhuḥ purātanam
|
svādhyāyābhyasanaṃ caiva tatra karma samāpyate||9||

10taṃ ceddvijamupāgacchedvartamānaṃ svakarmaṇi
|
akurvāṇaṃ vikarmāṇi śāntaṃ prajñānatarpitam||10||

11kurvītāpatyasantānamatho dadyādyajeta ca
|
saṃvibhajya ca bhoktavyaṃ dhanaṃ sadbhiritīryate||11||

12pariniṣṭhitakāryastu svādhyāyenaiva brāhmaṇaḥ
|
kuryādanyatra vā kuryānmaitro brāhmaṇa ucyate||12||

13kṣatriyasyāpi yo dharmastaṃ te vakṣyāmi bhārata
|
dadyādrājanna yāceta yajeta na ca yājayet||13||

14nādhyāpayedadhīyīta prajāśca paripālayet
|
nityodyukto dasyuvadhe raṇe kuryātparākramam||14||

15ye tu kratubhirījānāḥ śrutavantaśca bhūmipāḥ
|
ya evāhavajetārasta eṣāṃ lokajittamāḥ||15||

16avikṣatena dehena saparādyo nivartate
|
kṣatriyo nāsya tatkarma praśaṃsanti purāvidaḥ||16||

17evaṃ hi kṣatrabandhūnāṃ mārgamāhuḥ pradhānataḥ
|
nāsya kṛtyatamaṃ kiṃcidanyaddasyunibarhaṇāt||17||

18evaṃ hi kṣatrabandhūnāṃ mārgamāhuḥ pradhānataḥ
|
tasmādrājñā viśeṣeṇa yoddhavyaṃ dharmamīpsatā||18||

19sveṣu dharmeṣvavasthāpya prajāḥ sarvā mahīpatiḥ
|
dharmeṇa sarvakṛtyāni śamaniṣṭhāni kārayet||19||

20pariniṣṭhitakāryastu nṛpatiḥ paripālanāt
|
kuryādanyanna vā kuryādaindro rājanya ucyate||20||

21vaiśyasyāpi hi yo dharmastaṃ te vakṣyāmi śāśvatam
|
dānamadhyayanaṃ yajñaḥ śaucena dhanasaṃcayaḥ||21||

22pitṛvatpālayedvaiśyo yuktaḥ sarvānpaśūniha
|
vikarma tadbhavedanyatkarma yatsa samācaret||22||

23rakṣayā sa hi teṣāṃ vai mahatsukhamavāpnuyāt
|
prajāpatirhi vaiśyāya sṛṣṭvā paridadau paśūn||23||

24brāhmaṇāya ca rājñe ca sarvāḥ paridade prajāḥ
|
tasya vṛttiṃ pravakṣyāmi yacca tasyopajīvanam||24||

25ṣaṇṇāmekāṃ pibeddhenuṃ śatācca mithunaṃ haret
|
labdhācca saptamaṃ bhāgaṃ tathā śṛṅge kalā khure||25||

26sasyānāṃ sarvabījānāmeṣā sāṃvatsarī bhṛtiḥ
|
na ca vaiśyasya kāmaḥ syānna rakṣeyaṃ paśūniti||26||

27vaiśye cecchati nānyena rakṣitavyāḥ kathaṃcana
|
śūdrasyāpi hi yo dharmastaṃ te vakṣyāmi bhārata||27||

28prajāpatirhi varṇānāṃ dāsaṃ śūdramakalpayat
|
tasmācchūdrasya varṇānāṃ paricaryā vidhīyate||28||

29teṣāṃ śuśrūṣaṇāccaiva mahatsukhamavāpnuyāt
|
śūdra etānparicarettrīnvarṇānanupūrvaśaḥ||29||

30saṃcayāṃśca na kurvīta jātu śūdraḥ kathaṃcana
|
pāpīyānhi dhanaṃ labdhvā vaśe kuryādgarīyasaḥ||30||

31rājñā vā samanujñātaḥ kāmaṃ kurvīta dhārmikaḥ
|
tasya vṛttiṃ pravakṣyāmi yacca tasyopajīvanam||31||

32avaśyaṃ bharaṇīyo hi varṇānāṃ śūdra ucyate
|
chatraṃ veṣṭanamauśīramupānadvyajanāni ca||32||

33yātayāmāni deyāni śūdrāya paricāriṇe
|
adhāryāṇi viśīrṇāni vasanāni dvijātibhiḥ||33||

34śūdrāyaiva pradeyāni tasya dharmadhanaṃ hi tat
|
yaṃ ca kañciddvijātīnāṃ śūdraḥ śuśrūṣurāvrajet||34||

35kalpyāṃ tena tu te prāhurvṛttiṃ dharmavido janāḥ
|
deyaḥ piṇḍo'napatyāya bhartavyau vṛddhadurbalau||35||

36śūdreṇa tu na hātavyo bhartā kasyāñcidāpadi
|
atirekeṇa bhartavyo bhartā dravyaparikṣaye||36||

37na hi svamasti śūdrasya bhartṛhāryedhano hi saḥ
|
uktastrayāṇāṃ varṇānāṃ yajñastasya ca bhārata
|
svāhākāravaṣaṭkārau mantraḥ śūdre na vidyate||37||

38tasmācchūdraḥ pākayajñairyajetāvratavānsvayam
|
pūrṇapātramayīmāhuḥ pākayajñasya dakṣiṇām||38||

39śūdraḥ paijavano nāma sahasrāṇāṃ śataṃ dadau
|
aindrāgnena vidhānena dakṣiṇāmitiḥ naḥ śrutam||39||

40yato hi sarvavarṇānāṃ yajñastasyaiva bhārata
|
agre sarveṣu yajñeṣu śraddhāyajño vidhīyate||40||

41daivataṃ hi mahacchraddhā pavitraṃ yajatāṃ ca yat
|
daivataṃ hi paraṃ viprāḥ svena svena parasparam||41||

42ayajanniha satraiste taistaiḥ kāmaiḥ samāhitāḥ
|
saṃsṛṣṭā brāhmaṇaireva triṣu varṇeṣu sṛṣṭayaḥ||42||

43devānāmapi ye devā yadbrūyuste paraṃ hitam
|
tasmādvarṇaiḥ sarvayajñāḥ saṃsṛjyante na kāmyayā||43||

44ṛgjayuḥsāmavitpūjyo nityaṃ syāddevavaddvijaḥ
|
anṛgyajurasāmā ca prājāpatya upadravaḥ
|
yajño manīṣayā tāta sarvavarṇeṣu bhārata||44||

45nāsya yajñakṛto devā īhante netare janāḥ
|
tataḥ sarveṣu varṇeṣu śraddhāyajño vidhīyate||45||

46svaṃ daivataṃ brāhmaṇaḥ svena nityaṃ parānvarṇānayajannaivamāsīt
|
adharo vitānaḥ saṃsṛṣṭo vaiśyo brāhmaṇastriṣu varṇeṣu yajñasṛṣṭaḥ||46||

47tasmādvarṇā ṛjavo jñātivarṇāḥ saṃsṛjyante tasya vikāra eva
|
ekaṃ sāma yajurekamṛgekā vipraścaiko niścaye teṣu sṛṣṭaḥ||47||

48atra gāthā yajñagītāḥ kīrtayanti purāvidaḥ
|
vaikhānasānāṃ rājendra munīnāṃ yaṣṭumicchatām||48||

49udite'nudite vāpi śraddadhāno jitendriyaḥ
|
vahniṃ juhoti dharmeṇa śraddhā vai kāraṇaṃ mahat||49||

50yatskannamasya tatpūrvaṃ yadaskannaṃ taduttaram
|
bahūni yajñarūpāṇi nānākarmaphalāni ca||50||

51tāni yaḥ samprajānāti jñānaniścayaniścitaḥ
|
dvijātiḥ śraddhayopetaḥ sa yaṣṭuṃ puruṣo'rhati||51||

52steno vā yadi vā pāpo yadi vā pāpakṛttamaḥ
|
yaṣṭumicchati yajñaṃ yaḥ sādhumeva vadanti tam||52||

53ṛṣayastaṃ praśaṃsanti sādhu caitadasaṃśayam
|
sarvathā sarvadā varṇairyaṣṭavyamiti nirṇayaḥ||53||

54na hi yajñasamaṃ kiñcittriṣu lokeṣu vidyate
|
tasmād yaṣṭavyamityāhuḥ puruṣeṇānasūyatā
|
śraddhā pavitramāśritya yathāśakti yathecchayā||54||
