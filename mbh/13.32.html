Mahābhārata 13.32 (Anuśāsanaparvan)

1yudhiṣṭhira uvāca|
pitāmaha mahāprājña sarvaśāstraviśārada
|
tvatto'haṃ śrotumicchāmi dharme bharatasattama ||1||

2śaraṇāgataṃ ye rakṣanti bhūtagrāmaṃ caturvidham
|
kiṃ tasya bharataśreṣṭha phalaṃ bhavati tattvataḥ ||2||

3bhīṣma uvāca|
idaṃ śṛṇu mahāprājña dharmaputra mahāyaśaḥ
|
itihāsaṃ purāvṛttaṃ śaraṇārthaṃ mahāphalam ||3||

4prapātyamānaḥ śyenena kapotaḥ priyadarśanaḥ
|
vṛṣadarbhaṃ mahābhāgaṃ narendraṃ śaraṇaṃ gataḥ ||4||

5sa taṃ dṛṣṭvā viśuddhātmā trāsādaṅkamupāgatam
|
āśvāsyāśvasihītyāha na te'sti bhayamaṃḍaja ||5||

6bhayaṃ te sumahatkasmātkutra kiṃ vā kṛtaṃ tvayā
|
yena tvamiha saṃprāpto visaṃjño bhrāntacetanaḥ ||6||

7navanīlotpalāpīḍa cāruvarṇa sudarśana
|
dāḍimāśokapuṣpākṣa mā trasasvābhayaṃ tava ||7||

8matsakāśamanuprāptaṃ na tvāṃ kaścitsamutsahet
|
manasā grahaṇaṃ kartuṃ rakṣādhyakṣapuraskṛtam ||8||

9kāśirājyaṃ tadadyaiva tvadarthaṃ jīvitaṃ tathā
|
tyajeyaṃ bhava visrabdhaḥ kapota na bhayaṃ tava ||9||

10śyena uvāca|
mamaitadvihitaṃ bhakṣyaṃ na rājaṃstrātumarhasi
|
atikrāṃtaṃ ca prāptaṃ ca prayatnāccopapāditam ||10||

11māṃsaṃ ca rudhiraṃ cāsya majjā medaśca me hitam
|
paritoṣakaro hyeṣa mama mā'syāgrato bhava ||11||

12tṛṣṇā me bādhate'tyugrā kṣudhā nirdahatīva mām
|
muñcainaṃ na hi śakṣyāmi rājanmandayituṃ kṣudhām ||12||

13mayā hyānusṛto hyeṣa matpakṣanakhavikṣataḥ
|
kiciducchvāsaniḥśvāsaṃ na rājan goptumarhasi ||13||

14yadi svaviṣaye rājan prabhustvaṃ rakṣaṇe nṛṇām
|
khecarasya tṛṣārtasya na tvaṃ prabhurathottama ||14||

15yadi vairiṣu bhṛtyeṣu svajanavyavahārayoḥ
|
viṣayeṣvindriyāṇāṃ ca ākāśe mā parākrama ||15||

16prabhutvaṃ hi parākramya samyak pakṣahareṣu te
|
yadi tvamiha dharmārthī māmapi draṣṭumarhasi ||16||

17bhīṣma uvāca|
śrutvā śyenasya tadvākyaṃ rājarṣirvismayaṃ gataḥ
|
saṃbhāvya cainaṃ tadvākyaṃ tadarthī pratyabhāṣata ||17||

18rājovāca
|
govṛṣī vā varāho vā mṛgo vā māhiṣo'pi vā
|
tvadarthamadya kriyatāṃ kṣudhāpraśamanāya te ||18||

19śaraṇāgataṃ na tyajeyamiti me vratamāhitam
|
na muñcati mamāṃgāni dvijo'yaṃ paśya vai dvija ||19||

20śyena uvāca|
na varāhaṃ na cokṣāṇaṃ na cānyānvividhāndvijān
|
bhakṣyāmi mahārāja kimannādyena tena me ||20||

21yastu me vihito bhakṣyaḥ svayaṃ devaiḥ sanātanaḥ
|
śyenāḥ kapotān khādanti sthitireṣā sanātanī ||21||

22uśīnara kapote tu yadi snehastavānagha
|
tatastvaṃ me prayacchādya svamāṃsaṃ tulayā dhṛtam ||22||

23rājovāca
|
mahānanugraho me'dya yastvamevamihāttha mām
|
bāḍhameva kariṣyāmītyuktvā'sau rājasattamaḥ ||23||

24utkṛtyotkṛtya māṃsāni tulayā samatolayan
|
antaḥpure tatastasya striyo ratnavibhūṣitāḥ ||24||

25hāhābhūtā viniṣkrāntāḥ śrutvā paramaduḥkhitāḥ
|
tāsāṃ ruditaśabdena mantribhṛtyajanasya ca ||25||

26babhūva sumahānnādo meghagambhīraniḥsvanaḥ
|
niruddhaṃ gaganaṃ sarvaṃ śubhraṃ meveḥ samantataḥ ||26||

27mahī pracalitā cāsīttasya satyena karmaṇā
|
sa rājā pārśvataścaiva bāhubhyāmūrutaśca yat ||27||

28tāni māṃsāni saṃcchidya tulāṃ pūrayate'śanaiḥ
|
tathāpi na samastena kapotena babhūva ha ||28||

29asthibhūto yadā rājā nirmāṃso rudhirasravaḥ
|
tulāṃ tataḥ samārūḍhaḥ svaṃ māṃsakṣayamutsṛjan ||29||

30tataḥ sendrāstrayo lokāstaṃ narendramupasthitāḥ
|
bheryaścākāśagaistatra vāditā devadundubhiḥ ||30||

31amṛtenāvasiktaśca vṛṣadarbho nareśvaraḥ
|
divyaiśca susukhairmālyairabhivṛṣṭaḥ punaḥ panaḥ ||31||

32devagandharvasaṃghātairapsarobhiśca sarvataḥ
|
nṛttaścaivopagītaśca pitāmaha iva prabhuḥ ||32||

33hemaprasādasambādhaṃ maṇikāñcanatoraṇam
|
sa vaidūryamaṇistambhaṃ vimānaṃ samadhiṣṭhitaḥ ||33||

34sa rājarṣirgataḥ svargaṃ karmaṇā tena śāśvatam
|
śaraṇāgateṣu caivaṃ tvaṃ kuru sarvaṃ yudhiṣṭhira ||34||

35bhaktānāmanuraktānāmāśritānāṃ ca rakṣitā
|
dayāvānsarvabhūteṣu paratra sukhamedhate ||35||

36sādhuvṛtto hi yo rājā sadvṛttamanutiṣṭhati
|
kiṃ na prāptaṃ bhavettena savyājeneha karmaṇā ||36||

37sa rājarṣerviśuddhātmā dhīraḥ satyaparākramaḥ
|
kāśīnāmīśvaraḥ khyātastriṣu lokeṣu karmaṇā ||37||

38yo'pyanyaḥ kārayedevaṃ śaraṇāgatarakṣaṇam
|
so'pi gaccheta tāmeva gatiṃ bharatasattama ||38||

39idaṃ vṛttaṃ hi rājarṣe vṛṣadarbhasya kīrtayan
|
pūtātmā vai bhavelloke śruṇuyādyaśca nityaśaḥ ||39||
