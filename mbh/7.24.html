Mahābhārata 7.24 (Droṇaparvan)

1dhṛtarāṣṭra uvāca| vyathayeyurime senāṃ devānāmapi sañjaya
|
āhave ye nyavartanta vṛkodaramukhā nṛpāḥ||1||

2saṃprayuktaḥ kilaivāyaṃ diṣṭairbhavati pūruṣaḥ
|
tasminneva ca sarvārthāḥ pradṛśyante pṛthagvidhāḥ||2||

3dīrghaṃ viproṣitaḥ kālamaraṇye jaṭilo'jinī
|
ajñātaścaiva lokasya vijahāra yudhiṣṭhiraḥ||3||

4sa eva mahatīṃ senāṃ samāvartayadāhave
|
kimanyaddaivasaṃyogānmama putrasya cābhavat||4||

5yukta eva hi bhāgyena dhruvamutpadyate naraḥ
|
sa tathā kṛṣyate tena na yathā svayamicchati||5||

6dyūtavyasanamāsādya kleśito hi yudhiṣṭhiraḥ
|
sa punarbhāgadheyena sahāyānupalabdhavān||6||

7adya me kekayā labdhāḥ kāśikāḥ kosalāśca ye
|
cedayaścāpare vaṅgā māmeva samupāśritāḥ||7||

8pṛthivī bhūyasī tāta mama pārthasya no tathā
|
iti māmabravīt sūta mando duryodhanaḥ purā||8||

9tasya senāsamūhasya madhye droṇaḥ surakṣitaḥ
|
nihataḥ pārṣatenājau kimanyadbhāgadheyataḥ||9||

10madhye rājñāṃ mahābāhuṃ sadā yuddhābhinandinam
|
sarvāstrapāragaṃ droṇaṃ kathaṃ mṛtyurupeyivān ||10||

11samanuprāptakṛcchro'haṃ mohaṃ paramamāgataḥ
|
bhīṣmadroṇau hatau śrutvā nāhaṃ jīvitumutsahe||11||

12yanmāṃ kṣattā'bravīttāta prapaśyan putragṛddhinam
|
duryodhanena tat sarvaṃ prāptaṃ sūta mayā saha||12||

13nṛśaṃsaṃ tu paraṃ tu syāttyaktvā duryodhanaṃ yadi
|
putraśeṣaṃ cikīrṣeyaṃ kṛtsnaṃ na maraṇaṃ vrajet||13||

14yo hi dharmaṃ parityajya bhavatyarthaparo naraḥ
|
so'smācca hīyate lokāt kṣudrabhāvaṃ ca gacchati||14||

15adya cāpyasya rāṣṭrasya hatotsāhasya sañjaya
|
avaśeṣaṃ na paśyāmi kakude mṛdite sati||15||

16kathaṃ syādavaśeṣo hi dhuryayorabhyatītayoḥ
|
yau nityamupajīvāmaḥ kṣamiṇau puruṣarṣabhau||16||

17vyaktameva ca me śaṃsa yathā yuddhamavartata
|
ke'yudhyan ke vyapākurvan ke kṣudrāḥ prādravan bhayāt||17||

18dhanañjayaṃ ca me śaṃsa yadyaccakre ratharṣabhaḥ
|
tasmādbhayaṃ no bhūyiṣṭhaṃ bhrātṛvyācca vṛkodarāt||18||

19yathā''sīcca nivṛtteṣu pāṇḍaveyeṣu sañjaya
|
mama sainyāvaśeṣasya sannipātaḥ sudāruṇaḥ||19||

20kathaṃ ca vo manastāta nivṛtteṣvabhavat tadā
|
māmakānāṃ ca ye śūrāḥ ke kāṃstatra nyavārayan||20||
