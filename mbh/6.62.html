Mahābhārata 6.62 (Bhīṣmaparvan)

1dhṛtarāṣṭra uvāca|
daivameva paraṃ manye pauruṣādapi sañjaya
|
yat sainyaṃ mama putrasya pāṇḍusainyena bādhyate ||1||

2nityaṃ hi māmakāṃstāta hatāneva hi śaṃsasi
|
avyagrāṃśca prahṛṣṭāṃśca nityaṃ śaṃsasi pāṇḍavān ||2||

3hīnān puruṣakāreṇa māmakānadya sañjaya
|
pātitān pātyamānāṃśca hatāneva ca śaṃsasi ||3||

4yudhyamānān yathāśakti ghaṭamānānān jayaṃ prati
|
pāṇḍavā hi jayantyeva jīyante caiva māmakāḥ ||4||

5so'haṃ tīvrāṇi duḥkhāni duryodhanakṛtāni ca
|
śroṣyāmi satataṃ tāta duḥsahāni bahūni ca ||5||

6tamupāyaṃ na paśyāmi jīyeran yena pāṇḍavān
|
māmakā vijayaṃ yuddhe prāpnuyuryena sañjaya ||6||

7sañjaya uvāca|kṣayaṃ manuṣyadehānāṃ gajavājirathakṣayam
|
śṛṇu rājan sthiro bhūtvā tavaivāpanayo mahān ||7||

8dhṛṣṭadyumnastu śalyena pīḍito navabhiḥ śaraiḥ
|
pīḍayāmāsa saṃkruddho madrādhipatimāyasaiḥ ||8||

9tatrādbhutamapaśyāma pārṣatasya parākramam
|
nyavārayata yastūrṇaṃ śalyaṃ samitiśobhanam ||9||

10nāntaraṃ dṛśyate tatra tayośca rathinostadā
|
muhūrtamiva tadyuddhaṃ tayoḥ samamivābhavat ||10||

11tataḥ śalyo mahārāja dhṛṣṭadyumnasya saṃyuge
|
dhanuściccheda bhallena pītena niśitena ca ||11||

12athainaṃ śaravarṣeṇa cchādayāmāsa saṃyuge
|
giriṃ jalāgame yadvajjaladā jalavṛṣṭibhiḥ ||12||

13abhimanyustataḥ kruddho dhṛṣṭadyumne ca pīḍite
|
abhidudrāva vegena madrarājarathaṃ prati ||13||

14tato madrādhiparathaṃ kārṣṇiḥ prāpyātikopanaḥ
|
ārtāyanimameyātmā vivyādha niśitaiḥ śaraiḥ ||14||

15tatastu tāvakā rājan parīpsanto'rjuniṃ raṇe
|
madrarājarathaṃ tūrṇaṃ parivāryāvatasthire ||15||

16duryodhano vikarṇaśca duḥśāsanaviviṃśatī
|
durmarṣaṇo duḥsahaśca citraseno'tha durmukhaḥ ||16||

17satyavrataśca bhadraṃ te purumitraśca bhārata
|
ete madrādhiparathaṃ pālayantaḥ sthitā raṇe ||17||

18tān bhīmasenaḥ saṃkruddho dhṛṣṭadyumnaśca pārṣataḥ
|
draupadeyā'bhimanyuśca mādrīputrau ca pāṇḍavau ||18||

19dhārtarāṣṭrān daśarathān daśaiva pratyavārayan
|
nānārūpāṇi śastrāṇi visṛjanto viśāṃpate ||19||

20abhyavartanta saṃhṛṣṭāḥ parasparavadhaiṣiṇaḥ
|
te vai sameyuḥ saṅgrāme rājan durmantrite tava ||20||

21tasmin daśarathe kuddhe vartamāne mahābhaye
|
tāvakānāṃ pareṣāṃ vā prekṣakā rathino'bhavan ||21||

22śastrāṇyanekarūpāṇi visṛjanto mahārathāḥ
|
anyonyamabhinardantaḥ saṃprahāraṃ pracakrire ||22||

23te tadā jātasaṃrambhāḥ sarve'nyonyaṃ jighāṃsavaḥ
|
anyonyamabhimardantaḥ spardhamānāḥ parasparam ||23||

24anyonyaspardhayā rājan jñātayaḥ saṅgatā mithaḥ
|
mahāstrāṇi vimuñcantaḥ samāpeturamarṣiṇaḥ ||24||

25duryodhanastu saṅkruddho dhṛṣṭadyumnaṃ mahāraṇe
|
vivyādha niśitairbāṇaiścaturbhiḥ samare drutam ||25||

26durmarṣaṇaśca viṃśatyā citrasenaśca pañcabhiḥ
|
durmukho navabhirbāṇairduḥsahaścāpi saptabhiḥ ||26||

27viviṃśatiḥ pañcabhiśca tribhirduḥśāsanastathā
|
tān pratyavidhyadrājendra pārṣataḥ śatrutāpanaḥ ||27||

28ekaikaṃ pañcaviṃśatyā darśayan pāṇilāghavam
|
satyavrataṃ ca samare purumitraṃ ca bhārata ||28||

29abhimanyuravidhyat tu daśabhirdaśabhiḥ śaraiḥ
|
mādrīputrau tu samare mātulaṃ mātṛnanda‌nau ||29||

30avidhyetāṃ śaraistīkṣṇaistada‌dbhutamivābhavat
|
tataḥ śalyo mahārāja svasrīyau rathināṃ varau ||30||

31śarairbahubhirānarchat kṛtapratikṛtaiṣiṇau
|
chādyamānā tatastau tu mādrīputrau na celatuḥ ||31||

32atha duryodhanaṃ dṛṣṭvā bhīmaseno mahābalaḥ
|
vidhitsuḥ kalahasyāntaṃ gadāṃ jagrāha pāṇḍavaḥ ||32||

33tamudyatagadaṃ dṛṣṭvā kailāsamiva śṛṅgiṇam
|
bhīmasenaṃ mahābāhuṃ putrāste prādravan bhayāt ||33||

34duryodhanastu saṅkruddho māgadhaṃ samacodayat
|
anīkaṃ daśasāhasraṃ kuñjarāṇāṃ tarasvinām ||34||

35gajānīkena sahitastena rājā suyodhanaḥ
|
māgadhaṃ purataḥ kṛtvā bhīmasenaṃ samabhyayāt ||35||

36āpatantaṃ ca taṃ dṛṣṭvā gajānīkaṃ vṛkodaraḥ
|
gadāpāṇiravārohadrathāt siṃha ivonnadan ||36||

37adrisāramayīṃ gurvīṃ pragṛhya mahatīṃ gadām
|
abhyadhāvadgajānīkaṃ vyāditāsya ivāntakaḥ ||37||

38sa gajān gadayā nighnan vyacarat samare balī
|
bhīmaseno mahābāhuḥ savajra iva vāsavaḥ ||38||

39tasya nādena mahatā manohṛdayakampinā
|
vyatyaceṣṭanta saṃhatya gajā bhīmasya garjataḥ ||39||

40tatastu draupadīputrāḥ saubhadraśca mahārathaḥ
|
nakulaḥ sahadevaśca dhṛṣṭadyumnaśca pārṣataḥ ||40||

41pṛṣṭhaṃ bhīmasya rakṣantaḥ śaravarṣeṇa vāraṇān
|
abhyavarṣanta dhāvanto meghā iva girīn yathā ||41||

42kṣuraiḥ kṣuraprairbhallaiśca pītaiścāñjalikaiḥ śitaiḥ
|
vyaharannuttamāṅgāni pāṇḍavā gajayodhinām ||42||

43śirobhiḥ prapatadbhiśca bāhubhiśca vibhūṣitaiḥ
|
aśmavṛṣṭirivābhāti pāṇibhiśca sahāṃkuśaiḥ ||43||

44hatottamāṅgāḥ skandheṣu gajānāṃ gajayodhinaḥ
|
adṛśyantācalāgreṣu drumā bhagnaśikhā iva ||44||

45dhṛṣṭadyumnahatānanyānapaśyāma mahāgajān
|
patataḥ pātyamānāṃśca pārṣatena mahātmanā ||45||

46māgadho'tha mahīpālo gajamairāvaṇopamam
|
preṣayāmāsa samare saubhadrasya rathaṃ prati ||46||

47tamāpatantaṃ saṃprekṣya māgadhasya mahāgajam
|
jaghānaikeṣuṇā vīraḥ saubhadraḥ paravīrahā ||47||

48tasyāvarjitanāgasya kārṣṇiḥ parapurañjayaḥ
|
rājño rajatapuṅkhena bhallenāpāharacchiraḥ ||48||

49vigāhya tadgajānīkaṃ bhīmaseno'pi pāṇḍavaḥ
|
vyacaratsamare mṛdnan gajānindro girīniva ||49||

50ekaprahāranihatān bhīmasenena dantinaḥ
|
apaśyāma raṇe tasmin girīnvajrahatāniva ||50||

51bhagnadantān bhagnakaṭān bhagnasakthāṃśca vāraṇān
|
bhagnapṛṣṭhatrikānanyānnihatān parvatopamān ||51||

52nadataḥ sīdataścānyān vimukhān samare gatān
|
vidrutān bhayasaṃvignāṃstathā viśakṛto'parān ||52||

53bhīmasenasya mārgeṣu patitān parvatopamān
|
apaśyaṃ nihatānnāgān rājanniṣṭhīvato'parān ||53||

54vamanto rudhiraṃ cānye bhinnakumbhā mahāgajāḥ
|
vihvalanto gatā bhūmiṃ śailā iva dharātale ||54||

55medorudhiradigdhāṅgo vasāmajjāsamukṣitaḥ
|
vyacarat samare bhīmo daṇḍapāṇirivāntakaḥ ||55||

56gajānāṃ rudhiraklinnāṃ gadāṃ bibhradvṛkodaraḥ
|
ghoraḥ pratibhayaścāsītpinākīva pinākadhṛk ||56||

57saṃmathyamānāḥ kruddhena bhīmasenena dantinaḥ
|
sahasā prādravan kliṣṭā mṛdnantastava vāhinīm ||57||

58taṃ hi vīraṃ maheṣvāsaṃ saubhadrapramukhā rathāḥ
|
paryarakṣanta yudhyantaṃ vajrāyudhamivāmarāḥ ||58||

59śoṇitāktāṃ gadāṃ bibhradukṣitāṃ gajaśoṇitaiḥ
|
kṛtānta iva raudrātmā bhīmaseno vyadṛśyata ||59||

60vyāyacchamānaṃ gadayā dikṣu sarvāsu bhārata
|
apaśyāma raṇe bhīmaṃ nṛtyantamiva śaṃkaram ||60||

61yamadaṇḍopamāṃ gurvīmindrāśanisamasvanām
|
apaśyāma mahārāja raudrāṃ viśasanīṃ gadām ||61||

62vimiśrāṃ keśamajjābhiḥ pradigdhāṃ rudhireṇa ca
|
pinākamiva rudrasya kruddhasyābhighnataḥ paśūn ||62||

63yathā paśūnāṃ saṃghātaṃ yaṣṭyā pālaḥ prakālayet
|
tathā bhīmo gajānīkaṃ ga‌dayā samakālayat ||63||

64gadayā vadhyamānāste mārgaṇaiśca samantataḥ
|
svānyanīkāni mṛdnantaḥ prādravan kuñjarāstava ||64||

65mahāvāta ivābhrāṇi vidhamitvā sa vāraṇān
|
atiṣṭhattumule bhīmaḥ śmaśāna iva śūlabhṛt ||65||
