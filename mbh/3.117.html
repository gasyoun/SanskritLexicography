Mahābhārata 3.117 (Āraṇyakaparvan)

1rāma uvāca|
mamāparādhāttaiḥ kṣudrairhatastvaṃ tāta bāliśaiḥ
|
kārtavīryasya dāyādairvane mṛga iveṣubhiḥ||1||

2dharmajñasya kathaṃ tāta vartamānasya satpathe
|
mṛtyurevaṃvidho yuktaḥ sarvabhūteṣvanāgasaḥ||2||

3kiṃ nu tairna kṛtaṃ pāpaṃ yairbhavāṃstapasi sthitaḥ
|
ayudhyamāno vṛddhaḥ sanhataḥ śaraśataiḥ śitaiḥ||3||

4kiṃ nu te tatra vakṣyanti saciveṣu suhatsu ca
|
ayudhyamānaṃ dharmajñamekaṃ hattvā'napatrapāḥ||4||

5lālapyaivaṃ sakaruṇaṃ bahu nānāvidhaṃ nṛpa
|
pretakāryāṇi sarvāṇi pituścakre mahātapāḥ||5||

6dadāha pitaraṃ cāgnau rāmaḥ parapuraṃjayaḥ
|
pratijajñe vadhaṃ cāpi sarvakṣatrasya bhārata||6||

7saṃkruddho'tibalaḥ saṃkhye śastramādāya vīryavān
|
jaghnivānkārtavīryasya sutāneko'ntakopamaḥ||7||

8teṣāṃ cānugatā ye ca kṣatriyāḥ kṣatriyarṣabha
|
tāṃśca sarvānavāmṛdgādrāmaḥ praharatāṃ varaḥ||8||

9triḥsaptakṛtvaḥ pṛthivīṃ kṛtvā niḥkṣatriyāṃ prabhuḥ
|
samantapañcake pañca cakāra rudhirahradān||9||

10sa teṣu tarpayāmāsa bhṛgūnbhṛgukulodvahaḥ
|
sākṣāddadarśa carcīkaṃ sa ca rāmaṃ nyavārayat||10||

11tato yajñena mahatā jāmadagnyaḥ pratāpavān
|
tarpayāmāsa devendramṛtvigbhyaḥ pradadau mahīm||11||

12vedīṃ cāpyadadaddhaimīṃ kaśyapāya mahātmane
|
daśavyāmāyatāṃ kṛtvā navotsedhāṃ viśāṃpate||12||

13tāṃ kaśyapasyānumate brāhmaṇāḥ khāṇḍaśastadā
|
vyabhajaṃste tadā rājanprakhyātāḥ khāṇḍavāyanāḥ||13||

14sa pradāya mahīṃ tasmai kaśyapāya mahātmane
|
asminmahendre śailendre vasatyamitavikramaḥ||14||

15evaṃ vairamabhūttasya kṣatriyairlokavāsibhiḥ
|
pṛthivī cāpi vijitā rāmeṇāmitatejasā||15||

16vaiśaṃpāyana uvāca|
tataścaturdaśīṃ rāmaḥ samayena mahāmanāḥ
|
darśayāmāsa tānviprāndharmarājaṃ ca sānujam||16||

17sa tamānarca rājendra bhrātṛbhiḥ sahitaḥ prabhuḥ
|
dvijānāṃ ca parāṃ pūjāṃ cakre nṛpatisattamaḥ||17||

18arcitvā jāmadagnyaṃ sa pūjitastena coditaḥ
|
mahendra uṣya tāṃ rātriṃ prayayau dakṣiṇāmukhaḥ||18||
