Mahābhārata 2.47 (Sabhāparvan)

1vaiśampāyana uvāca|
vasanduryodhanastasyāṃ sabhāyāṃ puruṣarṣabha
|
śanairdadarśa tāṃ sarvāṃ sabhāṃ śakuninā saha||1||

2tasyāṃ divyānabhiprāyāndadarśa kurunandanaḥ
|
na dṛṣṭapūrvā ye tena nagare nāgasāhvaye||2||

3sa kadācitsabhāmadhye dhārtarāṣṭro mahīpatiḥ
|
sphāṭikaṃ sthalamāsādya jalamityabhiśaṅkayā||3||

4svavastrotkarṣaṇaṃ rājā kṛtavānbuddhimohitaḥ
|
durmanā vimukhaścaiva paricakrāma tāṃ sabhām||4||

5tataḥ sthale nipatito durmanā vrīḍito nṛpaḥ
|
niḥśvasanvimukhaścāpi paricakrāma tāṃ sabhām
|
||5||

6tataḥ sphāṭikatoyāṃ vai sphāṭikāmbujaśobhitām
|
vāpīṃ matvā sthalamiva savāsāḥ prāpatajjale||6||

7jale nipatitaṃ dṛṣṭvā bhīmaseno mahābalaḥ
|
jahāsa jahasuścaiva kiṅkarāśca suyodhanam||7||

8vāsāṃsi ca śubhānyasmai pradadū rājaśāsanāt
|
tathāgataṃ tu taṃ dṛṣṭvā bhīmaseno mahābalaḥ||8||

9arjunaśca yamau cobhau sarve te prāhasaṃstadā
|
nāmarṣayattatasteṣāmavahāsamamarṣaṇaḥ||9||

10ākāraṃ rakṣamāṇastu na sa tānsamudaikṣata
|
punarvasanamutkṣipya pratiriṣyanniva sthalam||10||

11āruroha tataḥ sarve jahasuśca punarjanāḥ
|
dvāraṃ tu pihitākāraṃ sphāṭikaṃ prekṣya bhūmipaḥ
|
praviśannāhato mūrdhni vyāghūrṇita iva sthitaḥ||11||

12tādṛśaṃ ca paraṃ dvāraṃ sphāṭikorukavāṭakam
|
vighaṭṭayankarābhyāṃ tu niṣkramyāgre papāta ha ||12||

13dvāraṃ tu vitatākāraṃ samāpede punaśca saḥ
|
tadvṛttaṃ ceti manvāno dvārasthānādupāramat||13||

14evaṃ pralambhānvividhānprāpya tatra viśāmpate
|
pāṇḍaveyābhyanujñātastato duryodhano nṛpaḥ||14||

15apahṛṣṭena manasā rājasūye mahākratau
|
prekṣya tāmadbhutāmṛddhiṃ jagāma gajasāhvayam||15||

16pāṇḍavaśrīprataptasya dhyāyamānasya gacchataḥ
|
duryodhanasya nṛpateḥ pāpā matirajāyata||16||

17pārthānsumanaso dṛṣṭvā pārthivāṃśca vaśānugān
|
kṛtsnaṃ cāpi hitaṃ lokamākumāraṃ kurūdvaha||17||

18mahimānaṃ paraṃ cāpi pāṇḍavānāṃ mahātmanām
|
dūryodhano dhārtarāṣṭro vivarṇaḥ samapadyata||18||

19sa tu gacchannanekāgnaḥ sabhāmeko'nvacintayat
|
śriyaṃ ca tāmanupamāṃ dharmarājasya dhīmataḥ||19||

20pramatto dhṛtarāṣṭrasya putro duryodhanastadā
|
nābhyabhāṣatsubalajaṃ bhāṣamāṇaṃ punaḥ punaḥ||20||

21anekāgraṃ tu taṃ dṛṣṭvā śakuniḥ pratyabhāṣata
|
duryodhana kutomūlaṃ niḥśvasanniva gacchasi||21||

22duryodhana uvāca|
dṛṣṭvemāṃ pṛthivīṃ kṛtsnāṃ yudhiṣṭhiravaśānugām
|
jitāmastrapratāpena śvetāśvasya mahātmanaḥ||22||

23taṃ ca yajñaṃ tathābhūtaṃ dṛṣṭvā pārthasya mātulaḥ
|
yathā śakrasya deveṣu tathābhūtaṃ mahādyuteḥ||23||

24amarṣeṇa tu sampūrṇo dahyamāno divāniśam
|
śuciśukrāgame kāle śuṣyettoyamivālpakam||24||

25paśya sātvatamukhyena śiśupālo nipātitaḥ
|
na ca tatra pumānāsītkaścittasya padānugaḥ||25||

26dahyamānā hi rājānaḥ pāṇḍavotthena vahninā
|
kṣāntavanto'parādhaṃ te ko hi tatkṣantumarhati||26||

27vāsudevena tatkarma yathā'yuktaṃ mahatkṛtam
|
siddhaṃ ca pāṇḍuputrāṇāṃ pratāpena mahātmanām||27||

28tathā hi ratnānyādāya vividhāni nṛpā nṛpam
|
upātiṣṭhanta kaunyeyaṃ vaiśyā iva karapradāḥ||28||

29śriyaṃ tathāgataṃ dṛṣṭvā jvalantīmiva pāṇḍave
|
amarṣavaśamāpanno dahyāmi na tathocitaḥ||29||

30evaṃ sa niścayaṃ kṛtvā tato vacanamabravīt
|
punargāndhāranṛpatiṃ dahyamāna ivāgninā||30||

31vahnimeva pravekṣyāmi bhakṣayiṣyāmi vā viṣam
|
apo vāpi pravekṣyāmi na hi śakṣyāmi jīvitum||31||

32ko hi nāma pumāṃlloke marṣayiṣyati satvavān
|
sapatnānṛddhyato dṛṣṭvā hīnamātmānameva ca||32||

33so'haṃ na strī na cāpyastrī na pumānnāpumānapi
|
yo'haṃ tāṃ marṣayāmyadya tādṛśīṃ śriyamāgatām||33||

34īśvaratvaṃ pṛthivyāśca vasumattāṃ ca tādṛśīm
|
yajñaṃ ca tādṛśaṃ dṛṣṭvā mādṛśaḥ ko na sañjvaret||34||

35aśaktaścaika evāhaṃ tāmāhartuṃ nṛpaśriyam
|
sahāyāṃśca na paśyāmi tena mṛtyuṃ vicintaye||35||

36daivameva paraṃ manye pauruṣaṃ ca nirarthakam
|
dṛṣṭvā kuntīsute śuddhāṃ śriyaṃ tāmahatāṃ tathā||36||

37kṛto yatno mayā pūrvaṃ vināśe tasya saubala
|
tacca sarvamatikramya saṃvṛddho'psviva paṅkajam||37||

38na daivaṃ paraṃ manye pauruṣaṃ ca nirarthakam
|
dhārtarāṣṭrāśca hīyante pārthā vardhanti nityaśaḥ||38||

39so'haṃ śriyaṃ ca tāṃ dṛṣṭvā sabhāṃ tāṃ ca tathāvidhām
|
rakṣibhiścāvahāsaṃ taṃ paritapye yathā'gninā||39||

40samāmabhyanujānīhi mātulādya suduḥkhitam
|
amarṣaṃ ca samāviṣṭaṃ dhṛtarāṣṭre nivedaya||40||
