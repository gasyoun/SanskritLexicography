Mahābhārata 8.82 (Karṇaparvan)

1sañjaya uvāca|
tataḥ karṇaḥ kuruṣu pradruteṣu varūthinā śvetahayena rājan
|
pāñcālaputrān vyadhamatsūtaputro maheṣubhirvāta ivābhrasaṅghān ||1||

2sūtaṃ rathādañjalikairnipātya jaghāna cāśvāñjanamejayasya
|
śatānīkaṃ sutasomaṃ ca bhallairavākiraddhanuṣī cāpyakṛntat ||2||

3dhṛṣṭadyumnaṃ nirbibhedātha ṣaḍbhirjaghānāśvāṃstarasā tasya saṃkhye
|
hatvā cāśvān sātyakeḥ sūtaputraḥ kaikeyaputraṃ nyavadhīdviśokam ||3||

4tamabhyadhāvannihate kumāre kaikeyasenāpatirugrakarmā
|
śarairvidhunvan bhṛśamugravegaiḥ karṇātmajaṃ cāpyahanatprasenam ||4||

5tasyārdhacandraistribhiruccakarta prahasya bāhū ca śiraśca karṇaḥ
|
sa syandanādgāmagamadgatāsuḥ paraśvadhaiḥ śāla ivāvarugṇaḥ ||5||

6hatāśvamañjogatibhiḥ prasenaḥ śinipravīraṃ niśitaiḥ pṛṣatkaiḥ
|
pracchādya nṛtyanniva karṇaputraḥ śaineyabāṇābhihataḥ papāta ||6||

7putre hate krodhaparītacetāḥ karṇaḥ śinīnāmṛṣabhaṃ jighāṃsuḥ
|
hato'si śaineya iti bruvansa vyavāsṛjadbāṇamamitrasāham ||7||

8tamasya ciccheda śaraṃ śikhaṇḍī tribhistribhiśca pratutoda karṇam
|
śikhaṇḍinaḥ kārmukaṃ ca dhvajaṃ ca chittvā kṣurābhyāṃ nyapatatsujātaḥ ||8||

9śikhaṇḍinaṃ ṣaḍbhiravidhyadugro dhārṣṭradyumneḥ sa śiraścoccakarta
|
tathā'bhinatsutasomaṃ śareṇa susaṃśitenādhirathirmahātmā ||9||

10athākrande tumule vartamāne dhārṣṭradyumne nihate tatra kṛṣṇaḥ
|
apāñcālyaṃ kriyate yāhi pārtha karṇaṃ jahītyabravīdrājasiṃha ||10||

11tataḥ prahasyāśu narapravīro rathaṃ rathenādhiratherjagāma
|
bhaye teṣāṃ trāṇamicchansubāhurabhyāhatānāṃ rathayūthapena ||11||

12visphārya gāṇḍīvamathograghoṣaṃ jyayā samāhatya tale bhṛśaṃ ca
|
bāṇāndhakāraṃ sahasaiva kṛtvā jaghāna nāgāśvarathadhvajāṃśca ||12||

13pratiśrutaḥ prāharadantarikṣe guhā girīṇāmapatanvayāṃsi
|
yanmaṇḍalajyena vijṛṃbhamāṇo raudre muhūrte'bhyapatatkirīṭī ||13||

14taṃ bhīmaseno'nuyayau rathena pṛṣṭhe rakṣanpāṇḍavamekavīraḥ
|
tau rājaputrau tvaritau rathābhyāṃ karṇāya yātāvaribhirviṣaktau ||14||

15tatrāntare sumahānsūtaputraścakre yuddhaṃ somakānsaṃpragṛhya
|
rathāśvamātaṅgagaṇāñjaghāna pracchādayāmāsa śarairdiśaśca ||15||

16tamuttamaujā janamejayaśca kruddhau yudhāmanyuśikhaṇḍinau ca
|
karṇaṃ bibheduḥ sahitāḥ pṛṣatkaiḥ sannardamānāḥ saha pārṣatena ||16||

17te pañca pañcālarathapravīrā vaikartanaṃ karṇamabhidravantaḥ
|
tasmādrathāccyāvayituṃ na śekurdhairyātkṛtātmānamivendriyārthāḥ ||17||

18teṣāṃ dhanūṃṣi dhvajavājisūtāṃstūrṇaṃ patākāśca nikṛtya bāṇaiḥ
|
tānpañcabhistvabhyahanatpṛṣatkaiḥ karṇastataḥ siṃha ivonnanāda ||18||

19tasyāsyatastānabhinighnataśca jyābāṇahastasya dhanuḥsvanena
|
sādridrumā syātpṛthivī viśīrṇetyatīva matvā janatā vyaṣīdat ||19||

20sa śakracāpapratimena dhanvanā bhṛśāyatenādhirathiḥ śarānsṛjan
|
babhau raṇe dīptamarīcimaṇḍalo yathāṃśumālī pariveṣavāṃstathā ||20||

21śikhaṇḍinaṃ dvādaśabhiḥ parābhinacchitaiḥ śaraiḥ ṣa‌ḍbhirathottamaujasam
|
tribhiryudhāmanyumavidhyadāśugaistribhistribhiḥ somakapārṣatātmajau ||21||

22parājitāḥ pañca mahārathāstu te mahāhave sūtasutena māriṣa
|
nirudyamāstasthuramitranandanā yathendriyārthā''tmavatā parājitāḥ ||22||

23nimajjatastānatha karṇasāgare vipannanāvo vaṇijo yathā'rṇave
|
uddadhrire naubhirivārṇavādrathaiḥ sukalpitairdraupadijāḥ svamātulān ||23||

24tataḥ śinīnāmṛṣabhaḥ śitaiḥ śarairnikṛtya karṇaprahitāniṣūnbahūn
|
vidārya karṇaṃ niśitairayasmayaistavātmajaṃ jyeṣṭhamavidhyaṣṭabhiḥ ||24||

25kṛpo'tha bhojaśca tavātmajastathā svayaṃ ca karṇo niśitairatāḍayat
|
sa taiścaturbhiryuyudhe yadūttamo digīśvarairdaityapatiryathā tathā ||25||

26samātateneṣvasanena kūjatā bhṛśāyatenāmitabāṇavarṣiṇā
|
babhūva durdharṣataraḥ sa sātyakiḥ śarannabhomadhyagato yathā raviḥ ||26||

27punaḥ samāsthāya rathān sudaṃśitāḥ śinipravīraṃ jugupuḥ parantapāḥ
|
sametya pañcālamahārathā raṇe marudgaṇāḥ śakramivārinigrahe ||27||

28tato'bhavadyuddhamatīva dāruṇaṃ tavāhitānāṃ tava sainikaiḥ saha
|
rathāśvamātaṅgavināśanaṃ tathā yathā surāṇāmasuraiḥ purā'bhavat ||28||

29rathā dvipā vājipadātayastathā bhavanti nānāvidhaśastraveṣṭitāḥ
|
paraspareṇābhihatāśca caskhalurvinedurārtā vyasavo'pataṃstathā ||29||

30tathāgataṃ bhīmamabhīstavātmajaḥ sasāra rājāvarajaḥ kirañśaraiḥ
|
tamabhyadhāvattvarito vṛkodaro mahāruruṃ siṃha ivābhipedivān ||30||

31tatastayoryuddhamatīva dāruṇaṃ pradīvyatoḥ prāṇadurodaraṃ dvayoḥ
|
paraspareṇābhiniviṣṭaroṣayorudagrayoḥ śambaraśakrayoryathā ||31||

32śaraiḥ śarīrārtikaraiḥ sutejanairnijaghnatustāvitaretaraṃ bhṛśam
|
sakṛtprabhinnāviva vāsitāntare mahāgajau manmathasaktacetasau ||32||

33tavātmajasyātha vṛkodarastvaran dhanuḥkṣurābhyāṃ dhvajameva cācchinat
|
lalāṭamapyasya bibheda patriṇā śiraśca kāyātprajahāra sāratheḥ ||33||

34sa rājaputro'nyadavāpya kārmukaṃ vṛkodaraṃ dvādaśabhiḥ parābhinat
|
svayaṃ niyacchaṃsturagānajihmagaiḥ śaraiśca bhīmaṃ punarapyavīvṛṣat ||34||

35tataḥ śaraṃ sūryamarīcisaprabhaṃ suvarṇavajrottamaratnabhūṣitam
|
mahendravajrāśanipātaduḥsahaṃ mumoca bhīmāṅgavidāraṇakṣamam ||35||

36sa tena nirviddhatanurvṛkodaro nipātitaḥ srastatanurgatāsuvat
|
prasārya bāhū rathavaryamāśritaḥ punaḥ sa saṃjñāmupalabhya cānadat ||36||
