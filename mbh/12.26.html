Mahābhārata 12.26 (Śāntiparvan)

1vaiśampāyana uvāca|
asminneva prakaraṇe dhanañjayamudāradhīḥ
|
abhinītataraṃ vākyamityuvāca yudhiṣṭhiraḥ||1||

2yadetanmanyase pārtha na jyāyo'sti dhanāditi
|
na svargo na sukhaṃ nārtho nirdhanasyeti tanmṛṣā ||2||

3svādhyāyayajñasaṃsiddhā dṛśyante bahavo janāḥ
|
taporatāśca munayo yeṣāṃ lokāḥ sanātanāḥ||3||

4ṛṣīṇāṃ samayaṃ śaśvadye rakṣanti dhanañjaya
|
āśritāḥ sarvadharmajñā devāstānbrāhmaṇānviduḥ||4||

5svādhyāyaniṣṭhān hi ṛṣīn jñānaniṣṭhāṃstathāparān
|
buddhyethāḥ santataṃ cāpi dharmaniṣṭhān dhanañjaya ||5||

6jñānaniṣṭheṣu kāryāṇi pratiṣṭhāpyāni pāṇḍava
|
vaikhānasānāṃ vacanaṃ yathā no viditaṃ prabho ||6||

7ajāśca pṛśnayaścaiva sikatāścaiva bhārata
|
aruṇāḥ ketavaścaiva svādhyāyena divaṃ gatāḥ ||7||

8avāpyaitāni karmāṇi vedoktāni dhanañjaya
|
dānamadhyayanaṃ yajño nigrahaścaiva durgrahaḥ ||8||

9dakṣiṇena ca panthānamaryamṇo ye divaṃ gatāḥ
|
etān kriyāvatāṃ lokānuktavānpūrvamapyaham ||9||

10uttareṇa tu panthānaṃ niyamādyaṃ prapaśyasi
|
ete yāgavatāṃ lokā bhānti pārtha sanātanāḥ ||10||

11tatrottarāṃ gatiṃ pārtha praśaṃsanti purāvidaḥ
|
santoṣo vai svargatamaḥ santoṣaḥ paramaṃ sukham ||11||

12tuṣṭerna kiñcitparamaṃ sā samyak pratitiṣṭhati
|
vinītakrodhaharṣasya satataṃ siddhiruttamā ||12||

13atrāpyudāharantīmāṃ gāthāṃ gītāṃ yayātinā
|
yo'bhipretyāharetkāmānkūrmo'ṅgānīva sarvaśaḥ ||13||

14yadā cāyaṃ na bibheti yadā cāsmānna bibhyati
|
yadā necchati na dveṣṭi brahma sampadyate tadā ||14||

15yadā na bhāvaṃ kurute sarvabhūteṣu pāpakam
|
karmaṇā manasā vācā brahma sampadyate tadā ||15||

16vinītamānamohaśca bahusaṅgavivarjitaḥ
|
tadā''tmajyotiṣaḥ sādhornirvāṇamupapadyate ||16||

17idaṃ tu śṛṇu me pārtha bruvataḥ saṃyatendriyaḥ
|
dharmamanye vṛttamanye dhanamīhanti cāpare ||17||

18dhanahetorya īheta tasyānīhā garīyasī
|
bhūyāndoṣo hi vittasya yaśca dharmastadāśrayaḥ ||18||

19pratyakṣamanupaśyāmi tvamapi draṣṭumarhasi
|
varjanaṃ varjanīyānāmīhamānena duṣkaram ||19||

20ye vittamabhipadyante samyaktvaṃ teṣu durlabham
|
druhyataḥ praiti tatprāhuḥ pratikūlaṃ yathātatham ||20||

21yastu sambhinnavṛttaḥ syādvītaśokabhayo naraḥ
|
alpena tṛṣito druhyan bhrūṇahatyāṃ na budhyate ||21||

22duṣyantyādadato bhṛtyā nityaṃ dasyubhayādiva
|
durlabhaṃ ca dhanaṃ prāpya bhṛśaṃ dattvā'nutapyate ||22||

23adhanaḥ kasya kiṃ vācyo vimuktaḥ sarvaśaḥ sukhī
|
devasvamupagṛhyaiva dhanena na sukhī bhavet ||23||

24tatra gāthāṃ yajñagītāṃ kīrtayanti purāvidaḥ
|
trayīmupāśritāṃ loke yajñasaṃstarakārikām ||24||

25yajñāya sṛṣṭāni dhanāni dhātrā yajñāya sṛṣṭaḥ puruṣo rakṣitā ca
|
tasmātsarvaṃ yajña evopayojyaṃ dhanaṃ na kāmāya hitaṃ praśastam ||25||

26etatsvārthe ca kaunteya dhanaṃ dhanavatāṃ vara
|
dhātā dadāti martyebhyo yajñārthamiti viddhi tat ||26||

27tasmādbuddhyanti puruṣā na hi tatkasyaciddhruvam
|
śraddhādhānastato loko dadyāccaiva yajeta ca ||27||

28labdhasya tyāgamityāhurna bhogaṃ na ca saṃkṣayam
|
tasya kiṃ sañcayenārthaḥ kārye jyāyasi tiṣṭhati ||28||

29ye svadharmādapetebhyaḥ prayacchantyalpabuddhayaḥ
|
śataṃ varṣāṇi te pretya purīṣaṃ bhuñjate janāḥ ||29||

30anarhate yaddadāti na dadāti yadarhate
|
arhānarhāparijñānāddānadharmo'pi duṣkaraḥ ||30||

31labdhānāmapi vittānāṃ boddhavyau dvāvatikramau
|
apātre pratipattiśca pātre cāpratipādanam ||31||
