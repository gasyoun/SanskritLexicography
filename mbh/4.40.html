Mahābhārata 4.40 (Virāṭaparvan)

1vaiśampāyana uvāca| tāṃ śamīmupasaṃgamya pārtho vairāṭimabravīt
|
sukumāraṃ samājñāya saṅgrāme nātikovidam ||1||

2samādiṣṭo mayā kṣipraṃ dhanūṃṣyavaharottara
|
nemāni hi tvadīyāni soḍhuṃ śakṣyanti me balam
|
bhāraṃ cāpi guruṃ voḍhuṃ kuñjaraṃ vā pramarditum ||2||

3mama vā bāhuvikṣepaṃ śatrūniha vijeṣyataḥ
|
tasmādbhūmiṃjayāroha śamīmetāṃ palāśirnām ||3||

4asyāṃ hi pāṇḍuputrāṇāṃ dhanūṃṣi nihitānyuta
|
yudhiṣṭhirasya bhīmasya bībhatsoryamayostathā ||4||

5dhvajāḥ śarāśca śūrāṇāṃ divyāni kavacāni ca
|
atra caitanmahāvīryaṃ dhanuḥ pārthasya gāṇḍivam ||5||

6ekaṃ śatasahasreṇa saṃmitaṃ rāṣṭravardhanam
|
vyāyāmasahamatyarthaṃ tṛṇarājasamaṃ mahat ||6||

7sarvāyudhamahāmātraṃ śatrusaṃbādhakārakam
|
suvarṇavikṛtaṃ divyaṃ ślakṣṇamāyatamavraṇam ||7||

8alaṃ bhāraṃ guruṃ voḍhuṃ dāruṇaṃ cārudarśanam
|
tādṛśānyeva sarvāṇi balavanti dṛḍhāni ca
|
yudhiṣṭhirasya bhīmasya bībhatsoryamayostathā ||8||
