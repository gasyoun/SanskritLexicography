Mahābhārata 4.64 (Virāṭaparvan)

1vaiśampāyana uvāca|tataḥ śāntanavo bhīṣmo bharatānāṃ pitāmahaḥ
|
vadhyamāneṣu yodheṣu dhanañjayamupādravat ||1||

2pragṛhya kārmukaśreṣṭhaṃ jātarūpapariṣkṛtam
|
śarānādāya tīkṣṇāgrān marmabhedān pramāthinaḥ ||2||

3pāṇḍureṇātapatreṇa dhriyamāṇena mūrdhani
|
śuśubhe sa naravyāghro giriḥ sūryodaye yathā ||3||

4pradhmāya śaṅkhaṃ gāṅgeyo dhārtarāṣṭrānpraharṣayan
|
pradakṣiṇamupāvṛtya bībhatsuṃ samavārayat ||4||

5tamudīkṣya samāyāntaṃ kaunteyaḥ paravīrahā
|
pratyagṛhṇātprahṛṣṭātmā dhārādharamivācalaḥ ||5||

6tato bhīṣmaḥ śarānaṣṭau dhvaje pārthasya vīryavān
|
samārpayanmahāvegān śvasamānānivoragān ||6||

7te dhvajaṃ pāṇḍuputrasya samāsādya patatriṇaḥ
|
jvalantaṃ kapimājaghnurdhvajāgranilayāṃśca tān ||7||

8tato bhallena mahatā pṛthudhāreṇa pāṇḍavāḥ
|
chatraṃ ciccheda bhīṣmasya tū tadapatadbhuvi ||8||

9dhvajaṃ caivāsya kaunteyaḥ śarairabhyahanadbhṛśam
|
śīghrakṛdrathavāhāṃśca tathobhau pārṣṇisārathī ||9||

10amṛṣyamāṇastadbhīṣmo jānannapi sa pāṇḍavam
|
divyenāstreṇa mahatā dhanañjayamavākirat ||10||

11tathaiva pāṇḍavo bhīṣme divyamastramudīrayan
|
pratyagṛhṇādameyātmā mahāmeghamivācalaḥ ||11||

12tayostadbhavadyuddhaṃ tumulaṃ lomaharṣaṇam
|
bhīṣmeṇa saha pārthena balivāsavayoriva ||12||

13praikṣanta kuravaḥ sarve yodhāśca sahasainikāḥ
|
bhallairbhallāḥ samāgamya bhīṣmapāṇḍavayoryudhi
|
antarikṣe vyarājanta khadyotāḥ prāvṛṣīva hi ||13||

14agnicakramivāviddhaṃ savyadakṣiṇamasyataḥ
|
gāṇḍīvamabhavadrājan pārthasya sṛjataḥ śarān ||14||

15tataḥ saṃchādayāmāsa bhīṣmaṃ śaraśataiḥ śitaiḥ
|
parvataṃ vāridhārābhiśchādayanniva toyadaḥ ||15||

16tāṃ sa velāmivodbhūtāṃ śaravṛṣṭiṃ samutthitām
|
vyadhamatsāyakairbhīṣmaḥ pāṇḍavaṃ samavārayat ||16||

17tatastāni nikṛttāni śarajālāni bhāgaśaḥ
|
samare ca vyaśīryanta phālgunasya rathaṃ prati ||17||

18tataḥ kanakapuṅkhānāṃ śaravṛṣṭiṃ samutthitām
|
pāṇḍavasya rathāttūrṇaṃ śalabhānāmivāyatim
|
vyadhamattāṃ punastasya bhīṣmaḥ śaraśataiḥ śitaiḥ ||18||

19tataste kuravaḥ sarve sādhu sādhviti cābruvan
|
duṣkaraṃ kṛtavān bhīṣmo yadarjunamayodhayat ||19||

20balavāṃstaruṇo dakṣaḥ kṣiprakārī dhanañjayaḥ
|
ko'nyaḥ samarthaḥ pārthasya vegaṃ dhārayituṃ raṇe ||20||

21ṛte śāntanavādbhīṣmātkṛṣṇādvā devakīsutāt
|
ācāryapravarādvā'pi bhāradvājānmahābalāt ||21||

22astrairasrāṇi saṃvārya krīḍantau bharatarṣabhau
|
cakṣūṃṣi sarvabhūtānāṃ mohayantau mahābalau ||22||

23prājāpatyaṃ tathaivaindramāgneyaṃ raudradāruṇam
|
kauberaṃ vāruṇaṃ caiva yāmyaṃ vāyavyameva ca
|
prayuñjānau mahātmānau samare tau viceratuḥ ||23||

24vismitānyatha bhūtāni tau dṛṣṭvā saṃyuge tadā
|
sādhu pārtha mahābāho sādhu bhīṣmeti cābruvan ||24||

25nāyaṃ yukto manuṣyeṣu yo'yaṃ saṃdṛśyate mahān
|
mahāstrāṇāṃ saṃprayogaḥ samare bhīṣmapārthayoḥ ||25||

26vaiśampāyana uvāca|evaṃ sarvāstraviduṣorastrayuddhamavartata
|
astrayuddhe tu nirvṛtte śarayuddhamavartata ||26||

27atha jiṣṇurupāvṛtya kṣuradhāreṇa kārmukam
|
cakarta bhīṣmasya tadā jātarūpapariṣkṛtam ||27||

28nimeṣāntaramātreṇa bhīṣmo'nyatkārmukaṃ raṇe
|
samādāya mahābāhuḥ sajyaṃ cakre mahārathaḥ
|
śarāṃśca subahūn kruddho mumocāśu dhanañjaye ||28||

29arjuno'pi śarāṃstīkṣṇānbhīṣmāya niśitānbahūn
|
cikṣepa sumahātejāstathā bhīṣmaśca pāṇḍave ||29||

30tayordivyāstraviduṣorasyatorniśitān śarān
|
na viśeṣastadā rājan lakṣyate sma mahātmanoḥ ||30||

31athāvṛṇoddaśa diśaḥ śarairatirathastadā
|
kirīṭamālī kaunteyaḥ śūraḥ śāntanavastathā ||31||

32atīvaṃ pāṇḍavo bhīṣmaṃ bhīṣmaścāvi pāṇḍavam
|
babhūva tasminsaṅgrāme rājaṃlloke tada‌dbhutam ||32||

33pāṇḍavena hatāḥ śūrā bhīṣmasya ratharakṣiṇaḥ
|
śerate sma tadā rājan kaunteyasyābhito ratham ||33||

34tato gāṇḍīvanirmuktā niramitraṃ cikīrṣavaḥ
|
āgacchanpuṅkhasaṃśliṣṭāḥ śvetavāhanapatriṇaḥ ||34||

35niṣpatanto rathāttasya dhautā hairaṇyavāsasaḥ
|
ākāśe samadṛśyanta haṃsānāmiva paṅktayaḥ ||35||

36tasya taddivyamastraṃ hi vigāḍhaṃ citramasyataḥ
|
prekṣante smāntarikṣasthāḥ sarve devāḥ savāsavāḥ||36||

37taṃ dṛṣṭvā paramaprīto gandharvaścitramadbhutam
|
śaśaṃsa devarājāya citrasenaḥ pratāpavān ||37||

38paśyemānpārthanirmuktānsaṃsaktāniva gacchataḥ
|
citrarūpamidaṃ jiṣṇordivyamastramudīryataḥ ||38||

39nedaṃ manuṣyāḥ saṃdadhyurnahīdaṃ teṣu vidyate
|
paurāṇānāṃ mahāstrāṇāṃ vicitro'yaṃ samāgamaḥ ||39||

40ādadānasya hi śarān saṃghāya ca vimuñcataḥ
|
vikarṣataśca gāṇḍīvaṃ nāntaraṃ samadṛśyata ||40||

41madhyandinagataṃ sūryaṃ pratapantamivāmbare
|
nāśaknuvanta sainyāni pāṇḍavaṃ prati vīkṣitum ||41||

42tathaiva bhīṣmaṃ gāṅgeyaṃ draṣṭuṃ notsahate janaḥ ||42||

43ubhau viśrutakarmāṇāvubhau tīvraparākramau
|
ubhau sadṛśakarmāṇāvubhau yudhi sudurjayau ||43||

44ityukto devarājastu pārthabhīṣmasamāgamam
|
pūjayāmāsa divyena puṣpavarṣeṇa bhārata ||44||

45tataḥ śāntanavo bhīṣmo vāmaṃ pārśvamatāḍayat
|
paśyataḥ pratisaṃdhāya vidhyataḥ savyasācinaḥ ||45||

46tataḥ prahasya bībhatsuḥ pṛthudhāreṇa kārmukam
|
ciccheda gārdhrapatreṇa bhīṣmasyādityatejasaḥ ||46||

47athainaṃ daśabhirbāṇaiḥ pratyavidhyat stanāntare
|
yatamānaṃ parākrāntaṃ kuntīputro dhanañjayaḥ ||47||

48sa pīḍito mahābāhurgṛhītvā rathakūbaram
|
gāṅgeyo yuddhadurdharṣastasthau dīrghamivāntaram ||48||

49taṃ visaṃjñamapovāha saṃyantā rathavājinām
|
upadeśamanusmṛtya rakṣamāṇo mahāratham ||49||
