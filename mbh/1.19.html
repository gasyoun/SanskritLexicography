Mahābhārata 1.19 (Ādiparvan)

1sautiruvāca|
athāvaraṇamukhyāni nānāpraharaṇāni ca
|
pragṛhyābhyadravandevānsahitā daityadānavāḥ||1||

2tatastadamṛtaṃ devo viṣṇurādāya vīryavān
|
jahāra dānavendrebhyo nareṇa sahitaḥ prabhuḥ||2||

3tato devagaṇāḥ sarve papustadamṛtaṃ tadā
|
viṣṇoḥ sakāśātsaṃprāpya saṃbhrame tumule sati||3||

4tataḥ pibatsu tatkālaṃ deveṣvamṛtamīpsitam
|
rāhurvibudharūpeṇa dānavaḥ prāpibattadā||4||

5tasya kaṇṭhamanuprāpte dānavasyāmṛte tadā
|
ākhyātaṃ candrasūryābhyāṃ surāṇāṃ hitakāmyayā||5||

6tato bhagavatā tasya śiraśchinnamalaṃkṛtam
|
cakrāyudhena cakreṇa pibato'mṛtamojasā||6||

7tacchailaśṛṅgapratimaṃ dānavasya śiro mahat
|
cakracchinnaṃ khamutpatya nanādātibhayaṃkaram||7||

8tatkabandhaṃ papātāsya visphuraddharaṇītale
|
saparvatavanadvīpāṃ daityasyākampayanmahīm||8||

9tato vairavinirbandhaḥ kṛto rāhumukhena vai
|
śāśvataścandrasūryābhyāṃ grasatyadyāpi caiva tau||9||

10vihāya bhagavāṃścāpi strīrūpamatulaṃ hariḥ
|
nānāpraharaṇairbhīmairdānavānsamakampayat||10||

11tataḥ pravṛttaḥ saṃgrāmaḥ samīpe lavaṇāmbhasaḥ
|
surāṇāmasurāṇāṃ ca sarvaghorataro mahān||11||

12prāsāśca vipulāstīkṣṇā nyapatanta sahasraśaḥ
|
tomarāśca sutīkṣṇāgrāḥ śastrāṇi vividhāni ca||12||

13tato'surāścakrabhinnā vamanto rudhiraṃ bahu
|
asiśaktigadārugṇā nipeturdharaṇītale||13||

14chinnāni paṭṭiśaiścaiva śirāṃsi yudhi dāruṇaiḥ
|
taptakāñcanamālīni nipeturaniśaṃ tadā||14||

15rudhireṇānuliptāṅgā nihatāśca mahāsurāḥ
|
adrīṇāmiva kūṭāni dhāturaktāni śerate||15||

16hāhākāraḥ samabhavattatra tatra sahasraśaḥ
|
anyo'nyaṃ chindatāṃ śastrairāditye lohitāyati||16||

17parighairāyasaistīkṣṇaiḥ sannikarṣe ca muṣṭibhiḥ
|
nighnatāṃ samare'nyo'nyaṃ śabdo divamivāspṛśat||17||

18chinddhi bhinddhi pradhāva tvaṃ pātayābhisareti ca
|
vyaśrūyanta mahāghorāḥ śabdāstatra samantataḥ||18||

19evaṃ sutumule yuddhe vartamāne mahābhaye
|
naranārāyaṇau devau samājagmaturāhavam||19||

20tatra divyaṃ dhanurdṛṣṭvā narasya bhagavānapi
|
cintayāmāsa taccakraṃ viṣṇurdānavasūdanam||20||

21tato'mbarāccintitamātramāgataṃ mahāprabhaṃ cakramamitratāpanam
|
vibhāvasostulyamakuṇṭhamaṇḍalaṃ sudarśanaṃ saṃyati bhīmadarśanam||21||

22tadāgataṃ jvalitahutāśanaprabhaṃ
bhayaṃkaraṃ karikarabāhuracyutaḥ
|
mumoca vai prabalavadugravegavān
mahāprabhaṃ paranagarāvadāraṇam||22||

23tadantakajvalanasamānavarcasaṃ
punaḥpunarnyapatata vegavattadā
|
vidārayadditidanujānsahasraśaḥ
kareritaṃ puruṣavareṇa saṃyuge||23||

24dahatkvacijjvalana ivāvalelihat
prasahya tānasuragaṇānnyakṛntata
|
praveritaṃ viyati muhuḥ kṣitau tathā
papau raṇe rudhiramatho piśācavat||24||

25tathā'surā giribhiradīnacetaso
muhurmuhuḥ suragaṇamārdayaṃstadā
|
mahābalā vigalitameghavarcasaḥ
sahasraśo gaganamabhiprapadya ha||25||

26athāmbarādbhayajananāḥ prapedire
sapādapā bahuvidhamegharūpiṇaḥ
|
mahādrayaḥ parigalitāgrasānavaḥ
parasparaṃ drutamabhihatya sasvanāḥ||26||

27tato mahī pravicalitā sakānanā
mahādripātābhihatā samantataḥ
|
parasparaṃ bhṛśamabhigarjatāṃ muhū
raṇājire bhṛśamabhisaṃpravartite||27||

28narastato varakanakāgrabhūṣaṇaiḥ
maheṣubhirgaganapathaṃ samāvṛṇot
|
vidārayangiriśikharāṇi patribhiḥ
mahābhaye'suragaṇavigrahe tadā||28||

29tato mahīṃ lavaṇajalaṃ ca sāgaraṃ
mahāsurāḥ praviviśurarditāḥ suraiḥ
|
viyadgataṃ jvalitahutāśanaprabhaṃ
sudarśanaṃ parikupitaṃ niśāmya te||29||

30tataḥ surairvijayamavāpya mandaraḥ
svameva deśaṃ gamitaḥ supūjitaḥ
|
vinādya khaṃ divamapi caiva sarvaśaḥ
tato gatāḥ saliladharā yathāgatam||30||

31tato'mṛtaṃ sunihitameva cakrire
surāḥ purāṃ mudamabhigamya puṣkalām
|
dado ca taṃ nidhimamṛtasya rakṣituṃ
kirīṭine balabhidathāmaraiḥ saha||31||
