Mahābhārata 12.165 (Śāntiparvan)

1bhīṣma uvāca|
hṛtārtho yakṣyamāṇaśva sarvavedāntagaśca yaḥ
|
ācāryapitṛkāryārthaṃ svādhyāyārthamathāpi ca ||1||

2ete vai sādhavo dṛṣṭā brāhmaṇā dharmabhikṣavaḥ
|
niḥsvebhyo deyametebhyo dānaṃ vidyā ca bhārata ||2||

3anyatra dakṣiṇādānaṃ deyaṃ bharatasattama
|
anyebhyo'pi bahirvedi cākṛtānnaṃ vidhīyate ||3||

4sarvaratnāni rājā hi yathārhaṃ pratipādayet
|
brāhmaṇā eva vedāśca yajñāśca bahudakṣiṇāḥ ||4||

5anyonyavibhavācārā yajante guṇataḥ sadā
|
yasya traivārṣikaṃ bhaktaṃ paryāptaṃ bhṛtyavṛttaye
|
adhikaṃ cāpi vidyeta sa somaṃ pātumarhati ||5||

6yajñaścetpratiruddhaḥ syādaṃśenaikena yajvanaḥ
|
brāhmaṇasya viśeṣeṇa dhārmike sati rājani ||6||

7yo vaiśyaḥ syādbahupaśurhīnakraturasomapaḥ
|
kuṭumbāttasya tadvittaṃ yajñārthaṃ pārthivo haret ||7||

8āharedatha no kiñcatkāmaṃ śūdrasya veśmanaḥ
|
na hi yajñeṣu śūdrasya kiñcidasti parigrahaḥ ||8||

9yo'nāhitāgniḥ śatagurayajvā ca sahasraguḥ
|
tayorapi kuṭumbābhyāmāharedavicārayan ||9||

10adātṛbhyo haredvittaṃ vikhyāpya nṛpatiḥ sadā
|
tathaivācarato dharmo nṛpateḥ syādathākhilaḥ ||10||

11tathaiva śṛṇu me bhaktaṃ bhaktāni ṣaḍanaśnataḥ
|
aśvastanavidhānena hartavyaṃ hīnakarmaṇaḥ ||11||

12khalātkṣetrāttathārāmādyato vā'pyupapadyate
|
ākhyātavyaṃ nṛpasyaitatpṛcchate'pṛcchate'pi vā ||12||

13na tasmai dhārayeddaṇḍaṃ rājā dharmeṇa dharmavit
|
kṣatriyasya tu bāliśyādbrāhmaṇaḥ kliśyate kṣudhā ||13||

14śrutaśīle samājñāya vṛttimasya prakalpayet
|
athainaṃ parirakṣeta pitā putramivaurasam ||14||

15iṣṭiṃ vaiśvānarīṃ nityaṃ nirvayedabdaparyaye
|
anukalpaḥ paro dharmo dharmavādaistu kevalam ||15||

16viśvairdevaiśca sādhyaiśca brāhmaṇaiśca maharṣibhiḥ
|
āpatsu maraṇādbhītairvidhiḥ pratinidhīkṛtaḥ ||16||

17prabhuḥ prathamakalpasya yo'nukalpe na vartate
|
na sāmparāyikaṃ tasya durmatervidyate phalam ||17||

18na brāhmaṇo nivedeta kiṃcidrājani vedavit
|
svavīryādrājavīryācca svavīryaṃ balavattaram ||18||

19tasmādrājñaḥ sadā tejo duḥsahaṃ brahmavādinām
|
kartā śāstā vidhātā ca brāhmaṇo deva ucyate ||19||

20tasminnākuśalaṃ brūyānna śuṣkāmīrayedgiram
|
kṣatriyo bāhuvīryeṇa taredāpadamātmanaḥ ||20||

21dhanairvaiśyaśca śūdraśca, mantrairhomaiśca vai dvijaḥ
|
naiva kanyā na yuvatirnāmantrajño na bāliśaḥ ||21||

22pariveṣṭāgnihotrasya bhavennāsaṃskṛtastathā
|
narakaṃ nipatantyete juhvānāḥ sa ca yasya tat
|
tasmādvaitānakuśalo hotā syādvedapāragaḥ ||22||

23prājāpatyamadattvāśvamagnyādheyasya dakṣiṇām
|
anāhitāgniriti sa procyate dharmadarśibhiḥ ||23||

24puṇyāni yāni kurvīta śraddadhāno jitendriyaḥ
|
anāptadakṣiṇairyajñairna yajeta kathañcana ||24||

25prajāḥ paśūṃśca svargaṃ ca hanti yajño hyadakṣiṇaḥ
|
indriyāṇi yaśaḥ kīrtimāyuścāpyavakṛntati ||25||

26udakyāmāsate ye ca dvijāḥ kecidanagnayaḥ
|
homaṃ cāśrotriyaṃ yeṣāṃ te sarve pāpakarmiṇaḥ ||26||

27udapānodake grāme brāhmaṇo vṛṣalīpatiḥ
|
uṣitvā dvādaśa samāḥ śūdrakarmaiva gacchati ||27||

28abhāryāṃ śayane bibhracchūdraṃ vṛddhaṃ ca vai dvijaḥ
|
abrāhmaṇaṃ manyamānastṛṇeṣvāsīta pṛṣṭhataḥ
|
tathā saṃśudhyate rājan śṛṇu cātra vaco mama ||28||

29yadekarātreṇa karoti pāpaṃ nikṛṣṭavarṇaṃ brāhmaṇaḥ sevamānaḥ
|
sthānāsanābhyāṃ viharan vratī sa tribhirvarṣaiḥ śamayedātmapāpam ||29||

30na narmayuktamanṛtaṃ hinasti na strīṣu rājanna vivāhakāle
|
na gurvarthaṃ nātmano jīvitārthe pañcānṛtānyāhurapātakāni ||30||

31śraddadhānaḥ śubhāṃ vidyāṃ hīnādapi samāpnuyāt
|
suvarṇamapi cāmedhyādādadītāvicārayan ||31||

32strīratnaṃ duṣkulāccāpi viṣādapyamṛtaṃ pibet
|
adūṣyā hi striyo ratnamāpa ityeva dharmataḥ ||32||

33gobrāhmaṇahitārthaṃ ca varṇānāṃ saṃkareṣu ca
|
vaiśyo gṛhṇīta śastrāṇi paritrāṇārthamātmanaḥ ||33||

34surāpānaṃ brahmahatyā gurutalpamathāpi vā
|
anirdeśyāni manyante prāṇāntamiti dhāraṇā ||34||

35suvarṇaharaṇaṃ stainyaṃ viprasvaṃ ceti pātakam
|
viharanmadyapānācca agamyāgamanādapi ||35||

36patitaiḥ samprayogācca brāhmaṇīyonitastathā
|
acireṇa mahārāja patito vai bhavatyuta ||36||

37saṃvatsareṇa patati patitena sahācaran
|
yājanādhyāpanādyaunānna tu yānāsanāśanāt ||37||

38etāni hitvā'to'nyāni nirdeśyānīti bhārata
|
nirdeśyānena vidhinā kālenāvyasanī bhavet ||38||

39annaṃ vīryaṃ grahītavyaṃ pretakarmaṇyapātite
|
triṣu tveteṣu pūrveṣu na kurvīta vicāraṇām ||39||

40amātyānvā gurūnvāpi jahyāddharmeṇa dhārmikaḥ
|
prāyaścittāni kuryācca na tairarhanti saṃvidam ||40||

41adharmakārī dharmeṇa tapasā hanti kilviṣam
|
bruvaṃstena iti stenaṃ tāvatprāpnoti kilbiṣam ||41||

42astenaṃ stena ityuktvā dviguṇaṃ pāpamāpnuyāt
|
tribhāgaṃ brahmahatyāyāḥ kanyā prāpnoti duṣyatī ||42||

43yastu dūṣayitā tasyāḥ śeṣaṃ prāpnoti pāpmanaḥ
|
brāhmaṇānavagarhyeha spṛṣṭvā gurutaraṃ bhavet ||43||

44varṣāṇāṃ hi śataṃ tāvatpratiṣṭhāṃ nādhigacchati
|
sahasraṃ caiva varṣāṇāṃ nipatya narakaṃ vaset ||44||

45tasmānnaivāvagarhyeta naiva jātu nipātayet
|
śoṇitaṃ yāvataḥ pāṃsūnsaṃgṛhṇīyāddvijakṣatāt ||45||

46tāvatīḥ sa samā rājan narake pratipadyate
|
bhrūṇahā''havamadhye tu śuddhyate śastrapātataḥ ||46||

47ātmānaṃ juhuyādagnau samiddhe tena śuddhyate
|
surāpo vāruṇīmuṣṇāṃ pītvā pāpādvimucyate ||47||

48tayā sa kāye nirdagdhe mṛtyuṃ vā prāpya śuddhyati
|
lokāṃśca labhate vipro nānyathā labhate hi saḥ ||48||

49gurutalpamadhiṣṭhāya durātmā pāpacetanaḥ
|
stryākārāṃ pratimāṃ liṃgya mṛtyunā so'bhiśuddhyati ||49||

50athavā śiśnavṛṣaṇāvādāyāñjalinā svayam ||50||

51nairṛtīṃ diśamāsthāya nipatetsa tvajihmagaḥ
|
brāhmaṇārthe'pi vā prāṇān saṃtyajettena śuddhyati ||51||

52aśvamedhena vā'pīṣṭvā athavā gosavena vā
|
agniṣṭomena vā samyagiha pretya ca pūjyate ||52||

53tathaiva dvādaśasamāḥ kapālī brahmahā bhavet
|
brahmacārī bhavennityaṃ svakarma khyāpayanmuniḥ ||53||

54evaṃ vā tapasā yukto brahmahā savanī bhavet
|
evaṃ tu samabhijñātāmātreyīṃ vā nipātayet ||54||

55dviguṇā brahmahatyā vai ātreyīnidhane bhavet
|
surāpo niyatāhāro brahmacārī kṣitīśayaḥ ||55||

56ūrdhvaṃ tribhyo'pi varṣebhyo yajetāgniṣṭutā param
|
ṛṣabhaikasahasraṃ vā gā dattvā śaucamāpnuyāt ||56||

57vaiśyaṃ hatvā tu varṣe dve ṛṣabhaikaśataṃ ca gāḥ
|
śūdraṃ hatvābdamevaikamṛṣabhaṃ ca śataṃ ca gāḥ ||57||

58śvavarāhasvarān hatvā śaudrameva vrataṃ caret
|
mārjāracāṣamaṇḍūkān kākaṃ vyālaṃ ca mūṣikam ||58||

59uktaḥ paśusamo doṣo rājanprāṇinipātanāt
|
prāyaścittānyathānyāni pravakṣyāmyanupūrvaśaḥ ||59||

60alpe vā'pyatha śoceta pṛthak saṃvatsaraṃ caret
|
trīṇi śrotriyabhāryāyāṃ paradāre ca dve smṛte ||60||

61kāle caturthe bhuñjāno brahmacārī vratī bhavet
|
sthānāsanābhyāṃ viharettrirahnābhyupayannapaḥ
|
evameva nirākartā yaścāgnīnapavidhyati ||61||

62tyajatyakāraṇe yaśca pitaraṃ mātaraṃ gurum
|
patitaḥ syātsa kauravya yathā dharmeṣu niścayaḥ ||62||

63grāsācchādanamātraṃ tu dadyāditi nidarśanam
|
bhāryāyāṃ vyabhicāriṇyāṃ niruddhāyāṃ viśeṣataḥ
|
yatpuṃsaḥ paradāreṣu tadenāṃ cārayedvratam ||63||

64śreyāṃsaṃ śayanaṃ hitvā yā'nyaṃ pāpaṃ nigacchati
|
śvabhistāmardayedrājā saṃsthāne bahuvistare ||64||

65pumāṃsamunnayetprājñaḥ śayane tapta āyase
|
apyādadhīta dārūṇi tatra dahyeta pāpakṛt ||65||

66eṣa daṇḍo mahārāja strīṇāṃ bhartṛṣvatikramāt
|
saṃvatsaro'bhiśastasya duṣṭasya dviguṇo bhavet ||66||

67dve tasya trīṇi varṣāṇi catvāri sahasevini
|
kucaraḥ pañcavarṣāṇi caredbhaikṣyaṃ munivrataḥ ||67||

68parivittiḥ parivettā yā caiva parividyate
|
pāṇigrahāstvadharmeṇa sarve te patitāḥ smṛtāḥ ||68||

69careyuḥ sarva evaite vīrahā yadvrataṃ caret
|
cāndrāyaṇaṃ carenmāsaṃ kṛcchraṃ vā pāpaśuddhaye ||69||

70parivettā prayaccheta tāṃ snuṣāṃ parivittaye
|
jyeṣṭhena tvabhyanujñāto yavīyānapyanantaram
|
evaṃ ca mokṣamāpnoti tau ca sā caiva dharmataḥ ||70||

71amānuṣīṣu govarjyamanāvṛṣṭirna duṣyati
|
adhiṣṭhātravamantāraṃ paśūnāṃ puruṣaṃ viduḥ ||71||

72paridhāyordhvavālaṃ tu pātramādāya mṛnmayam
|
caretsaptagṛhānnityaṃ svakarma parikīrtayan ||72||

73tatraiva labdhabhojī syāddvādaśāhātsa śuddhyati
|
caretsaṃvatsaraṃ cāpi tadvrataṃ yena kṛntati ||73||

74bhavettu mānuṣeṣvevaṃ prāyaścittamanuttamam
|
dānaṃ vā dānaśakteṣu sarvametatprakalpayet ||74||

75anāstikeṣu gomātraṃ dānamekaṃ pracakṣate
|
śvavarāhamanuṣyāṇāṃ kukkuṭasya kharasya ca ||75||

76māṃsaṃ mūtraṃ purīṣaṃ ca prāśya saṃskāramarhati
|
brāhmaṇāstu surāpasya gandhamādāya somapaḥ ||76||

77apastryahaṃ pibeduṣṇaṃ tryahamuṣṇaṃ payaḥ pibet
|
tryahamuṣṇaṃ payaḥ pītvā vāyubhakṣo bhavettryaham ||77||

78evametatsamuddiṣṭaṃ prāyaścittaṃ sanātanam
|
brāhmaṇasya viśeṣeṇa yadajñānena sambhavet ||78||
