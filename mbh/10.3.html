Mahābhārata 10.3 (Sauptikaparvan)

1sañjaya uvāca|
kṛpasya vacanaṃ śrutvā dharmārthasahitaṃ śubham
|
aśvatthāmā mahārāja duḥkhaśokasamanvitaḥ||1||

2dahyamānastu śokena pradīptenāgninā yathā
|
krūraṃ manastataḥ kṛtvā tāvubhau pratyabhāṣata||2||

3puruṣe puruṣe buddhiryā yā bhavati śobhanā
|
tuṣyanti ca pṛthak sarve prajñayā te svayā svayā||3||

4sarvo hi manyate loka ātmānaṃ buddhimattaram
|
sarvasyātmā bahumataḥ sarvātmānaṃ praśaṃsati||4||

5sarvasya hi svakā prajñā sādhuvāde pratiṣṭhitā
|
parabuddhiṃ ca nindanti svāṃ praśaṃsanti cāsakṛt||5||

6kāraṇāntarayogena yoge yeṣāṃ samāgatiḥ
|
anyonyena ca tuṣyanti bahu manyanti cāsakṛt||6||

7tasyaiva tu manuṣyasya sā sā buddhistadā tadā
|
kālayoge viparyāsaṃ prāpyānyonyaṃ vipadyate||7||

8vicitratvāttu cittānāṃ manuṣyāṇāṃ viśeṣataḥ
|
cittavaiklavyamāsādya sā sā buddhiḥ prajāyate||8||

9yathā hi vaidyaḥ kuśalo jñātvā vyādhiṃ yathāvidhi
|
bhaiṣajyaṃ kurute yogātpraśamārthamiti prabho||9||

10evaṃ kāryasya yogārthaṃ buddhiṃ kurvanti mānavāḥ
|
prajñayā hi svayā yuktāstāṃ ca nidanti mānavāḥ||10||

11anyayā yauvane martyo buddhyā bhavati mohitaḥ
|
madhye'nyayā jarāyāṃ tu so'nyāṃ rocayate matim||11||

12vyasanaṃ vā mahāghoraṃ samṛddhiṃ cāpi tādṛśīm
|
avāpya puruṣo bhoja kurute buddhivaikṛtam||12||

13ekasminneva puruṣe sā sā buddhistadā tadā
|
bhavatyakṛtadharmatvātsā tasyaiva na rocate||13||

14niścitya tu yathāprajñaṃ yāṃ matiṃ sādhu paśyati
|
tayā prakurute bhāvaṃ sā tasyodyogakārikā||14||

15sarvo hi puruṣo bhoja sādhvetādati niścitaḥ
|
kartumārabhate prīto maraṇādiṣu karmasu||15||

16sarve hi buddhimājñāya prajñāṃ vāpi svakāṃ narāḥ
|
ceṣṭante vividhāṃ ceṣṭāṃ hitamityeva jānate||16||

17upajātā vyasanajā yeyamadya matirmama
|
yuvayostāṃ pravakṣyāmi mama śokavināśinīm||17||

18prajāpatiḥ prajāḥ sṛṣṭvā karmatāsu vidhāya ca
|
varṇe varṇe samādhatte hyekaikaṃ guṇabhāgguṇam||18||

19brāhmaṇe vedamagryaṃ tu kṣatriye teja uttamam
|
dākṣyaṃ vaiśye ca śūdre ca sarvavarṇānukūlatām||19||

20adānto brāhmaṇo'sādhurnistejāḥ kṣatriyo'dhamaḥ
|
adakṣo nindyate vaiśyaḥ śūdraśca pratikūlavān||20||

21so'smi jātaḥ kule śreṣṭhe brāhmaṇānāṃ supūjite
|
mandabhāgyatayā'smyetaṃ kṣatradharmamanuṣṭhitaḥ||21||

22kṣatradharmaṃ viditvāhaṃ yadi brāhmaṇyamāśritaḥ
|
prakuryāṃ sumahatkarma na me tatsādhusammatam||22||

23dhārayaṃśca dhanurdivyaṃ divyānyastrāṇi cāhave
|
pitaraṃ nihataṃ dṛṣṭvā kiṃ nu vakṣyāmi saṃsadi||23||

24so'hamadya yathākāmaṃ kṣatradharmamupāsyatam
|
gantā'smi padavīṃ rājñaḥ pituścāpi mahātmanaḥ||24||

25adya svapsyanti pāñcālā viśvastā jitakāśinaḥ
|
vimuktayugyakavacā harṣeṇa ca samanvitāḥ||25||

26jayaṃ matvā''tmanaścaiva śrāntā vyāyāmakarṣitāḥ
|
teṣāṃ niśi prasuptānāṃ susthānāṃ śibire svake||26||

27avaskandaṃ kariṣyāmi śibirasyādya duṣkaram
|
tānavaskandya śibire pretabhūtavicetasaḥ||27||

28sūdayiṣyāmi vikramya maghavāniva dānavān
|
adya tānsahitānsarvān dhṛṣṭadyumnapurogamān||28||

29sūdayiṣyāmi vikramya kakṣaṃ dīpta ivānalaḥ
|
nihatya caiva pāñcālān śāntiṃ labdhā'smi sattama||29||

30pāñcāleṣu bhaviṣyāmi sūdayannadya saṃyuge
|
pinākapāṇiḥ saṅkruddhaḥ svayaṃ rudraḥ paśuṣviva||30||

31adyāhaṃ sarvapāñcālānnihatya ca nikṛṣya ca
|
ardayiṣyāmi saṃhṛṣṭo raṇe pāṇḍusutāṃstathā||31||

32adyāhaṃ sarvapañcālaiḥ kṛtvā bhūmiṃ śarīriṇīm
|
prahṛtyaikaikaśasteṣu bhaviṣyāmyanṛṇaḥ pituḥ||32||

33duryodhanasya karṇasya bhīṣmasaindhavayorapi
|
gamayiṣyāmi pāñcālānpadavīmadya durgamām||33||

34adya pāñcālarājasya dhṛṣṭadyumnasya vai niśi
|
na cirātpramathiṣyāmi paśoriva śirobalāt||34||

35adya pāñcālapāṇḍūnāṃ śayitānātmajānniśi
|
khaḍgena niśitenājau pramathiṣyāmi gautama||35||

36adya pāñcālasenāṃ tāṃ nihatya niśi sauptike
|
kṛtakṛtyaḥ sukhī caiva bhaviṣyāmi mahāmate||36||
