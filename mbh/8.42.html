Mahābhārata 8.42 (Karṇaparvan)

1sañjaya uvāca|
madrādhipasyādhiradhirmahātmā vaco niśamyāpriyamapratītaḥ
|
uvāca śalyaṃ viditaṃ mamaitadyathāvidhāvarjunavāsudevau ||1||

2śaure rathaṃ vāhayato'rjunasya balaṃ mahāstrāṇi ca pāṇḍavasya
|
ahaṃ vijānāmi yathāvadadya parokṣabhūtaṃ tava tattu śalya ||2||

3tau cāpyahaṃ śastrabhṛtāṃ variṣṭhau vyapetabhīryodhayiṣyāmi kṛṣṇau
|
saṃtāpayatyabhyadhikaṃ nu rāmācchāpo'dya māṃ brāhmaṇasattamācca ||3||

4avasaṃ vai brāhmaṇacchadmanā'haṃ rāme purā divyamastraṃ cikīrṣuḥ
|
tatrāpi me devarājena vighno hitārthinā phālyunasyaiva śalya ||4||

5kṛto bibhedena mamorumetya praviśya kīṭasya tanuṃ virūpām
|
mamorumetya prabibheda kīṭaḥ supte gurau tatra śiro nidhāya ||5||

6ūrūprabhedācca mahānbabhūva śarīrato me ghanaśoṇitaughaḥ
|
gurorbhayāccāpi na colavānahaṃ tato vibuddho dadṛśe sa vipraḥ ||6||

7sa dhairyayuktaṃ prasamīkṣya māṃ vai na tvaṃ vipraḥ ko'si satyaṃ vadeti
|
tasmai tadā''tmānamahaṃ yathāvadākhyātavānsūta ityeva śalya ||7||

8sa māṃ niśamyātha mahātapasvī saṃśaptavān roṣaparītacetāḥ
|
sūtopadhāvāptamidaṃ tavāstraṃ na karmakāle pratibhāsyati tvām ||8||

9anyatra tasmāttava mṛtyukālādabrāhmaṇe brahma na hi dhruvaṃ syāt
|
tadadya paryāptamatīva cāstramasminsaṃgrāme tumule'tīva bhīme ||9||

10yo'yaṃ śalya bharateṣūpapannaḥ prakarṣaṇaḥ sarvaharo'tibhīmaḥ
|
so'bhimanye kṣatriyāṇāṃ pravīrān pratāpitā balavānvai vimardaḥ ||10||

11śalyogradhanvānamahaṃ variṣṭhaṃ tarasvinaṃ bhīmamasahyavīryam
|
satyapratijñaṃ yudhi pāṇḍaveyaṃ dhanañjayaṃ mṛtyumukhaṃ nayiṣye ||11||

12astraṃ tato'nyatpratipannamadya yena kṣepsye samare śatrupūgān
|
pratāpinaṃ balavantaṃ kṛtāstraṃ tamugradhanvānamamitaujasaṃ ca ||12||

13krūraṃ śūraṃ raudramamitrasāhaṃ dhanañjayaṃ saṃyuge'haṃ haniṣye
|
apāṃ patirvegavānaprameyo nimajjayiṣyanbahulāḥ prajāśca ||13||

14mahāvegaṃ saṃkurute samudro velā cainaṃ dhārayatyaprameyam
|
pramuñcantaṃ bāṇasaṅghānameyānmarmacchido vīrahaṇaḥ suputrān ||14||

15kuntīputraṃ yatra yotsyāmi yuddhe jyāṃ karṣatāmuttamamadya loke
|
evaṃ balenātibalaṃ mahāstraṃ samudrakalpaṃ sudurāpamugram ||15||

16śaraughiṇaṃ pārthivānmajjayantaṃ veleva pārthamiṣubhiḥ saṃsahiṣye
|
adyāhave yasya na tulyamanyaṃ manye manuṣyaṃ dhanurādadānam ||16||

17surāsurān yudhi vai yo jayeta tenādya me paśya yuddhaṃ sughoram
|
atīva mānī pāṇḍavo yuddhakāmo hyamānuṣaireṣyati me mahāstraiḥ ||17||

18tasyāstramastraiḥ pratihatya saṃkhye bāṇottamaiḥ pātayiṣyāmi pārtham
|
sahastraraśmipratimaṃ jvalantaṃ diśaśca sarvāḥ pratapantamugram ||18||

19tamonudaṃ megha ivātimātraṃ dhanañjayaṃ chādayiṣyāmi bāṇaiḥ
|
vaiśvānaraṃ dhūmaśikhaṃ jvalantaṃ tejasvinaṃ lokamidaṃ dahantam ||19||

20parjanyabhūtaḥ śaravarṣairyathāgniṃ tathā pārthaṃ śamayiṣyāmi yuddhe
|
āśīviṣaṃ durdharamaprameyaṃ sutīkṣṇadaṃṣṭraṃ jvalanaprabhāvam ||20||

21krodhapradīptaṃ tvahitaṃ mahāntaṃ kuntīputraṃ śamayiṣyāmi bhallaiḥ
|
pramāthinaṃ balavantaṃ prahāriṇaṃ prabhañjanaṃ mātariśvānamugram ||21||

22yuddhe sahiṣye himavānivācalo dhanañjayaṃ kruddhamamṛṣyamāṇam
|
viśāradaṃ rathamārgeṣu śaktaṃ dhuryaṃ nityaṃ samareṣu pravīram ||22||

23loke varaṃ sarvadhanurdharāṇāṃ dhanañjayaṃ saṃyuge saṃsahiṣye
|
adyāve yasya na tulyamanyaṃ manye manuṣyaṃ dhanurādadānam ||23||

24sarvāmimāṃ yaḥ pṛthivīṃ vijigye tena prayoddhā'smi sametya saṃkhye
|
yaḥ sarvabhūtāni sadaivatāni prasthe'jayatkhāṇḍave savyasācī ||24||

25ko jīvitaṃ rakṣyamāṇo hi tena yuyutsedvai mānuṣo māmṛte'nyaḥ
|
mānī kṛtāstraḥ kṛtahastayogo divyāstravicchvetahayaḥ pramāthī ||25||

26tasyāhamadyātirathasya kāyācchiro hariṣyāmi śitaiḥ pṛṣatkaiḥ
|
yotsyāmyenaṃ śalya dhanañjayaṃ vai mṛtyuṃ puraskṛtya raṇe jayaṃ vā ||26||

27anyo hi na hyekarathena martyo yudhyeta yaḥ pāṇḍavamindra kalpam
|
tasyāhave pauruṣaṃ pāṇḍavasya brūyāṃ hṛṣṭaḥ samitau kṣatriyāṇām ||27||

28kiṃ tvaṃ mūrkhaḥ prasabhaṃ mūḍhacetā mamāvocaḥ pauruṣaṃ phālgunasya
|
apriyo yaḥ puruṣo niṣṭhuro hi kṣudraḥ kṣeptā kṣamiṇaścākṣamāvān ||28||

29hanyāmahaṃ tvādṛśānāṃ śatāni kṣamāmyahaṃ kṣamayā kālayogāt
|
avocastvaṃ pāṇḍavārthe'priyāṇi pradharṣayanmāṃ mūḍhavatpāpakarman ||29||

30mayā''rjave jihmamatirhatastvaṃ mitradrohī sāptapadaṃ hi maitram
|
kālastvayaṃ pratyupayāti dāruṇo duryodhano yuddhamupāgamadyat ||30||

31asyārthasiddhiṃ tvabhikāṃkṣamāṇastanmanyase yatra naikāntyamasti
|
mitraṃ mindernandateḥ prīyatervā saṃtrāyaterminutermodatervā ||31||

32bravīmi te sarvamidaṃ mamāsti taccāpi sarvaṃ mama vetti rājā
|
śatruḥ śadeḥ śāsatervā śyatervā ṛṇātervā śvasateḥ sīdatervā ||32||

33upasargādbahudhā sūdateśca prāyeṇa sarvaṃ tvayi tacca mahyam
|
duryodhanārthaṃ tava ca priyārthaṃ yaśo'rthamātmārthamapīśvarārtham ||33||

34tasmādahaṃ pāṇḍavavāsudevau yotsye yatnātkarma tatpaśya me'dya
|
astrāṇi paśyādya mamottamāni brāhmāṇi divyānyatha mānuṣāṇi ||34||

35āsādayiṣyāmyahamugravīryaṃ dvipo dvipaṃ mattamivātimattaḥ
|
astraṃ brāhmaṃ manasā yudhyajeyaṃ kṣepsye pārthāyāprameyaṃ jayāya
|
tenāpi me naiva mucyeta yuddhe na cetpatedviṣame me'dya cakram ||35||

36vaivasvatāddaṇḍahastādvaruṇādvāpi pāśinaḥ
|
sagadādvā dhanapateḥ savajrādvāpi vāsavāt ||36||

37anyasmādapi kasmāccidamitrādātatāyinaḥ
|
iti śalya vijānīhi yathā nāhaṃ bibhemyataḥ
|
tasmānna me bhayaṃ pārthānnāpi caiva janārdanāt ||37||

38saha yuddhaṃ hi me tābhyāṃ sāṃparāye bhaviṣyati
|
kadācidvijayasyāhamastrahetoraṭannṛpa ||38||

39ajñānāddhi kṣipanbāṇān ghorarūpān bhayānakān
|
homadhenvā vatsamasya pramatta iṣuṇā'hanam ||39||

40carantaṃ vijane śalya tato'nuvyājahāra mām
|
yasmāttvatyā pramattena homadhenvā hataḥ sutaḥ ||40||

41śvabhre te patatāṃ cakramiti māṃ brāhmaṇo'bravīt
|
yudhyamānasya saṃgrāme prāptasyaikāyanaṃ bhayam ||41||

42tasmādbibhemi balavadbrāhmaṇavyāhṛtādaham
|
ete hi somarājāna īśvarāḥ sukhaduḥkhayoḥ ||42||

43adāṃ tasmai gosahasraṃ balīvardāṃśca ṣaṭśatān
|
prasādaṃ na labhe śalya brāhmaṇānmadrakeśvara ||43||

44īṣādantān saptaśatān dāsīdāsaśatāni ca
|
dadato dvijamukhyo me prasādaṃ na cakāra saḥ ||44||

45kṛṣṇānāṃ śvetavatsānāṃ sahasrāṇi caturdaśa
|
āharaṃ na labhe tasmātprasādaṃ dvijasattamāt ||45||

46ṛddhaṃ gṛhaṃ sarvakāmairyacca me vasu kiñcana
|
tatsarvamasmai satkṛtya prayacchāmi na cecchati ||46||

47tato'bravīnmāṃ yācantamaparādhaṃ prayatnataḥ
|
vyāhṛtaṃ yanmayā sūta tattathā na tadanyathā ||47||

48anṛtoktaṃ prajāṃ hanyāttataḥ pāpamavāpnuyām
|
tasmāddharmābhirakṣārthaṃ nānṛtaṃ vaktumutsahe ||48||

49mā tvaṃ brahma gatiṃ hiṃsyāḥ prāyaścittaṃ kṛtaṃ tvayā
|
madvākyaṃ nānṛtaṃ loke kaścitkuryātsamāpnuhi ||49||

50ityetatte mayā proktaṃ kṣiptenāpi suhṛttayā
|
jānāmi tvāṃ vikṣipantaṃ joṣamāsvottaraṃ śṛṇu ||50||
