Mahābhārata 1.115 (Ādiparvan)

1vaiśampāyana uvāca|
tataḥ putraśataṃ jajñe gāndhāryāṃ janamejaya
|
dhṛtarāṣṭrasya vaiśyāyāmekaścāpi śatātparaḥ ||1||

2pāṇḍoḥ kuntyāṃ ca mādryāṃ ca putrāḥ pañca mahārathāḥ
|
devebhyaḥ samapadyanta santānāya kulasya vai ||2||

3janamejaya uvāca| kathaṃ putraśataṃ jajñe gāndhāryāṃ dvijasattama
|
kiyatā caiva kālena teṣāmāyuśca kiṃ param ||3||

4kathaṃ caikaḥ sa vaiśyāyāṃ dhṛtarāṣṭrasuto'bhavat
|
kathaṃ ca sadṛśīṃ bhāryāṃ gāndhārīṃ dharmacāriṇīm||4||

5ānukūlye vartamānāṃ dhṛtarāṣṭro'bhyavartata
|
kathaṃ ca śaptasya sataḥ pāṇḍostena mahātmanā ||5||

6samutpannā daivatebhyaḥ putrāḥ pañca mahārathāḥ
|
etadvidvanyathānyāyaṃ vistareṇa tapodhana ||6||

7kathayasva na me tṛptiḥ kathyamāneṣu bandhuṣu
|
vaiśaṃpāyana uvāca| kṣucchramābhipariglānaṃ dvaipāyanamupasthitam ||7||

8toṣayāmāsa gāndhārī vyāsastasyai varaṃ dadau
|
sā vavre sadṛśaṃ bhartuḥ putrāṇāṃ śatamātmanaḥ ||8||

9tataḥ kālena sā garbhaṃ dhṛtarāṣṭrādathāgrahīt
|
saṃvatsaradvayaṃ taṃ tu gāndhārī garbhamāhitam ||9||

10aprajā dhārayāmāsa tatastāṃ duḥkhamāviśat
|
śrutvā kuntīsutaṃ jātaṃ bālārkasamatejasam || 10||

11udarasyātmanaḥ sthairyamupalabhyānvacintayat
|
ajñātaṃ dhṛtarāṣṭrasya yatnena mahatā tataḥ ||11||

12sodaraṃ ghātayāmāsa gāndhārī duḥkhamūrcchitā
|
tato jajñe māṃsapeśī lohāṣṭhīleva saṃhatā || 12||

13dvivarṣasaṃbhṛtā kukṣau tāmutsraṣṭuṃ pracakrame
|
atha dvaipāyano jñātvā tvaritaḥ samupāgamat ||13||

14tāṃ sa māṃsamayīṃ peśīṃ dadarśa japatāṃ varaḥ
|
tato'bravītsaubaleyīṃ kimidaṃ te cikīrṣitam ||14||

15sā cātmano mataṃ satyaṃ śaśaṃsa paramarṣaye
|
gāndhāryuvāca
|
jyeṣṭhaṃ kuntīsutaṃ jātaṃ śrutvā ravisamaprabham||15||

16duḥkhena parameṇedamudaraṃ ghātitaṃ mayā
|
śataṃ ca kila putrāṇāṃ vistīrṇaṃ me tvayā purā ||16||

17iyaṃ ca me māṃsapeśī jātā putraśatāya vai
|
vyāsa uvāca|
evametatsaubaleyi naitajjātvanyathā bhavet || 17||

18vitathaṃ noktapūrvaṃ me svaireṣvapi kuto'nyathā
|
ghṛtapūrṇaṃ kuṇḍaśataṃ kṣiprameva vidhīyatām || 18||

19sugupteṣu ca deśeṣu rakṣā caiva vidhīyatām
|
śītābhiradbhiraṣṭhīlāmimāṃ ca pariṣiñcaya || 19 ||

20vaiśaṃpāyana uvāca|
sā sicyamānā tvaṣṭhīlā babhūva śatadhā tadā
|
aṅguṣṭhaparvamātrāṇāṃ garbhāṇāṃ pṛthageva tu ||20||

21ekādhikaśataṃ pūrṇaṃ yathāyogaṃ viśāṃpate
|
māṃsapeśyāstadā rājan kramaśaḥ kālaparyayāt || 21||

22tatastāṃsteṣu kuṇḍeṣu garbhānavadadhe tadā
|
svanugupteṣu deśeṣu rakṣāṃ vai vyadadhāttataḥ ||22||

23śaśaṃsa caiva bhagavānkālenaitāvatā punaḥ
|
udghāṭanīyānyetāni kuṇḍānīti ca saubalīm|| 23 ||

24ityuktvā bhagavānvyāsastathā pratinidhāya ca
|
jagāma tapase dhīmānhimavantaṃ śiloccayam || 24||

25jajñe krameṇa caitena teṣāṃ duryodhano nṛpaḥ
|
janmatastu pramāṇena jyeṣṭho rājā yudhiṣṭhiraḥ || 25 ||

26tadākhyātaṃ tu bhīṣmāya vidurāya ca dhīmate
|
yasminnahani durdharṣo jajñe duryodhanastadā ||26||

27tasminneva mahābāhurjajñe bhīmo'pi vīryavān
|
sa jātamātra evātha dhṛtarāṣṭrasuto nṛpa ||27||

28rāsabhārāvasadṛśaṃ rurāva ca nanāda ca
|
taṃ kharāḥ pratyabhāṣanta gṛdhragomāyuvāyasāḥ || 28 ||

29vātāśca pravavuścāpi digdāhaścābhavattadā
|
tatastu bhītavadrājā dhṛtarāṣṭro'bravīdidam || 29||

30samānīya bahūnviprānbhīṣmaṃ vidurameva ca
|
anyāṃśca suhṛdo rājankurūnsarvāṃstathaiva ca || 30||

31yudhiṣṭhiro rājaputro jyeṣṭho naḥ kulavardhanaḥ
|
prāptaḥ svaguṇato rājyaṃ na tasminvācyamasti naḥ ||31||

32ayaṃ tvanantarastasmādapi rājā bhaviṣyati
|
etadvibrūta me tathyaṃ yadatra bhavitā dhruvam ||32||

33vākyasyaitasya nidhane dikṣu sarvāsu bhārata
|
kravyādāḥ prāṇadandhorāḥ śivāścāśivaśaṃsinaḥ ||33||

34lakṣayitvā nimittāni tāni ghorāṇi sarvaśaḥ
|
te'bruvanbrāhmaṇā rājanviduraśca mahāmatiḥ || 34||

35yathemāni nimittāni ghorāṇi manujādhipa
|
utthitāni sute jāte jyeṣṭhe te puruṣarṣabha ||35||

36vyaktaṃ kulāntakaraṇo bhavitaiṣa sutastava
|
tasya śāntiḥ parityāge guptāvapanayo mahān|| 36||

37śatamekonamapyastu putrāṇāṃ te mahīpate
|
tyajainamekaṃ śāntiṃ cetkulasyecchasi bhārata||37||

38ekena kuru vai kṣemaṃ kulasya jagatastathā
|
tyajedekaṃ kulasyārthe grāmasyārthe kulaṃ tyajet ||38||

39grāmaṃ janapadasyārthe ātmārthe pṛthivīṃ tyajet
|
sa tathā vidureṇoktastaiśca sarvairdvijottamaiḥ || 39||

40na cakāra tathā rājā putrasnehasamanvitaḥ
|
tataḥ putraśataṃ pūrṇaṃ dhṛtarāṣṭrasya pārthiva ||40||

41māsamātreṇa saṃjajñe kanyācaikā śatādhikā
|
gāndhāryāṃ kliśyamānāyāmudareṇa vivardhatā || 41 ||

42dhṛtarāṣṭraṃ mahārājaṃ vaiśyā paryacaratkila
|
tasminsaṃvatsare rājandhṛtarāṣṭrānmahāyaśāḥ || 42 ||

43jajñe dhīmāṃstatastasyāṃ yuyutsuḥ karaṇo nṛpa
|
evaṃ putraśataṃ jajñe dhṛtarāṣṭrasya dhīmataḥ || 43||

44mahārathānāṃ vīrāṇāṃ kanyā caikā śatādhikā
|
yuyutsuśca mahātejā vaiśyāputraḥ pratāpavān ||44||
