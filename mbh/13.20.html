Mahābhārata 13.20 (Anuśāsanaparvan)

1bhīṣma uvāca|
atha sā strī tamuvāca bāḍhamevaṃ bhavatviti
|
tailaṃ divyamupādāya snānaśāṭīmupānayat ||1||

2anujñātā ca mininā sā strī tena mahātmanā
|
athāsya tailenāṅgāni sarvāṇyevābhyamṛkṣata ||2||

3śanaiścotsāditastatra snānaśālāmupāgamat
|
bhadrāsanaṃ tataścitraṃ ṛṣiranvagamannavam ||3||

4athopaviṣṭaśca yadā tasminbhadrāsane tadā
|
snāpayāmāsa śanakaistamṛṣiṃ sukhahastavat ||4||

5divyaṃ ca vidhivaccakre sopacāraṃ munestadā
|
sa tena susukhoṣṇena tasyā hastasukhena ca ||5||

6vyatītāṃ rajanīṃ kṛtsnāṃ nājānātsa mahāvrataḥ
|
tata utthāya sa munistadā paramavismitaḥ ||6||

7pūrvasyāṃ diśi sūryaṃ ca so'paśyaduditaṃ divi
|
tasya buddhiriyaṃ kintu mohastattvamidaṃ bhavet ||7||

8athopāsya sahastrāṃśuṃ kiṃ karomītyuvāca tām
|
sā cāmṛtarasaprakhyaṃ ṛṣerannamupāharat ||8||

9tasya svādutayā'nnasya na prabhūtaṃ cakāra saḥ
|
vyagamaccāpyahaḥśeṣaṃ tataḥ sandhyāgamatpunaḥ ||9||

10atha sā strī bhagavantaṃ supyatāmityacodayat
|
tatra vai śayane divye tasya tasyāśca kalpite ||10||

11pṛthakcaiva tathā suptau sā strī sa ca munistadā
|
tathārdharātre sā strī tu śayanaṃ tadupāgamat ||11||

12aṣṭāvakra uvāca|
na bhadre paradāreṣu mano me saṃprasajjati
|
uttiṣṭha bhadre bhadraṃ te svayaṃ vai viramasva ca ||12||

13bhīṣma uvāca|
sā tadā tena vipreṇa tathā tena nivartitā
|
svatantrā'smītyuvācarṣiṃ na dharmacchalamasti te ||13||

14aṣṭāvakra uvāca|
nāsti svatantratā strīṇāmasvatantrā hi yoṣitaḥ
|
prajāpatimataṃ hyetanna strī svātantryamarhati ||14||

15stryuvāca
|
bādhate maithunaṃ vipra mama bhaktiṃ ca paśya vai
|
adharmaṃ prāpsyase vipra yanmāṃ tvaṃ nābhinandasi ||15||

16aṣṭāvakra uvāca|
haranti doṣajātāni naraṃ jātaṃ yathecchakam
|
prabhavāmi sadā dhṛtyā bhadre svaśayanaṃ vraja ||16||

17stryuvāca
|
śirasā praṇame vipra prasādaṃ kartumarhasi
|
bhūmau nipatamānāyāḥ śaraṇaṃ bhava me'nagha ||17||

18yadi vā doṣajātaṃ tvaṃ paradāreṣu paśyasi
|
ātmānaṃ sparśayāmyadya pāṇiṃ gṛhṇīṣva me dvija ||18||

19na doṣo bhavitā caiva satyenaitadbravīmyaham
|
svatantrāṃ māṃ vijānīhi yo'dharmaḥ so'stu vai mayi
|
tvayyāveśitacittā ca svatantrā'smi bhajasva mām ||19||

20aṣṭāvakra uvāca|
svatantrā tvaṃ kathaṃ bhadre brūhi kāraṇamatra vai
|
nāsti triloke strī kācidyā vai svātantryamarhati ||20||

21pitā rakṣati kaumāre bhartā rakṣati yauvane
|
putrāśca sthāvire kāle nāsti strīṇāṃ svatantratā ||21||

22stryuvāca
|
kaumāraṃ brahmacaryaṃ me kanyaivāsmi na saṃśayaḥ
|
patnīṃ kuruṣva māṃ vipra śraddhāṃ vijahi mā mama ||22||

23aṣṭāvakra uvāca|
yathā mama tathā tubhyaṃ yathā tubhyaṃ tathā mama
|
jijñāseyamṛṣestasya vighnaḥ satyaṃ na kiṃ bhavet ||23||

24āścaryaṃ paramaṃ hīdaṃ kiṃtu śreyo hi me bhavet
|
divyābharaṇavastrā hi kanyeyaṃ māmupasthitā ||24||

25kiṃ tvasyāḥ paramaṃ rūpaṃ jīrṇamāsītkathaṃ punaḥ
|
kanyārūpamihādyaiyaṃ kimivātrottaraṃ bhavet ||25||

26yathā paraṃ śaktidhṛterne vyutthāsye kathañcana
|
na rocate hi vyutthānaṃ satyenāsādayāmyaham ||26||
