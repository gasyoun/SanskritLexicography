Mahābhārata 12.7 (Śāntiparvan)

1vaiśampāyana uvāca|
yudhiṣṭhirastu dharmātmā śokavyākulacetanaḥ
|
śuśoca duḥkhasantaptaḥ smṛtvā karṇaṃ mahārathanam ||1||

2āviṣṭo duḥkhaśokābhyāṃ niḥśvasaṃśca punaḥ punaḥ
|
dṛṣṭvā'rjunamuvācedaṃ vacanaṃ śokakarśitaḥ ||2||

3yudhiṣṭhira uvāca|
yadbhaikṣyamācariṣyāma vṛṣṇyandhakapure vayam
|
jñātīnniṣpuruṣānkṛtvā nemāṃ prāpsyāma durgatim ||3||

4amitrā naḥ samuddhārthā vṛttārthāḥ kuravaḥ kila
|
ātmānamātmanā hatvā kiṃ dharmaphalamāpnumaḥ ||4||

5dhigastu kṣātramācāraṃ dhigastu balapauruṣam
|
dhigastvamarṣaṃ yanemāmāpadaṃ gamitā vayam ||5||

6sādhu kṣamā damaḥ śaucaṃ vairāgyaṃ cāpyamatsaraḥ
|
ahiṃsā satyavacanaṃ nityāni vanacāriṇām ||6||

7vayaṃ tu lobhānmohācca daṃbhaṃ mānaṃ ca saṃśritāḥ
|
imāmavasthāṃ saṃprāptā rājyalābhabubhutsayā ||7||

8trailokyasyāpi rājyena nāsmānkaścitpraharṣayet
|
bāndhavānnihatāndṛṣṭvā pṛthivyāṃ vijayaiṣiṇaḥ ||8||

9te vayaṃ pṛthivīhetoravadhyān pṛthivīśvarān
|
saṃparityajya jīvāmo hīnārthā hatabāndhavāḥ ||9||

10āmiṣe gṛdhyamānānāmaśubhaṃ vai śunāmiva
|
āmiṣaṃ caiva no hīṣṭamāmiṣasya vivarjanam ||10||

11na pṛthivyā sakalayā na suvarṇasya rāśibhiḥ
|
na gavāśvena sarveṇa te tyājyā ya ime hatāḥ ||11||

12kāmamanyuparītāste krodhaharṣasamanvitāḥ
|
mṛtyuyānaṃ samāruhya gatā vaivasvatakṣayam ||12||

13bahukalyāṇasaṃyuktānicchanti pitaraḥ sutān
|
tapasā brahmacaryeṇa satyena ca titikṣayā ||13||

14upavāsaistathejyābhirvratakautukamaṅgalaiḥ
|
labhante mātaro garbhān māsāndaśa ca bibhrati ||14||

15yadi svasti prajāyante jātā jīvanti vā yadi
|
saṃbhāvitā jātabalāste dadyuryadi naḥ sukham ||15||

16iha cāmutra caiveti kṛpaṇāḥ phalahetavaḥ
|
tāsāmayaṃ samudyogo nirvṛttaḥ kevalo'phalaḥ ||16||

17yadāsāṃ nihatā putrā yuvāno mṛṣṭakuṇḍalāḥ||17||

18abhuktvā pārthivān bhogānṛṇānyanapahāya ca
|
pitṛbhyo devatābhyaśca gatā vaivasvatakṣayam||18||

19yadaiṣāmamba pitarau jātakāmāvubhāvapi
|
sañjātadhanaratneṣu tadaiva nihatā nṛpāḥ ||19||

20saṃyuktāḥ kāmamanyubhyāṃ krodhaharṣāsamañjasāḥ
|
na te jayaphalaṃ kiñcidbhoktāro jātu karhicit ||20||

21pañcālānāṃ kurūṇāṃ ca hatā eva hi ye hatāḥ
|
na cetsarvānayaṃ lokaḥ paśyet svenaiva karmaṇā ||21||

22vayamevāsya lokasya vināśe kāraṇaṃ smṛtāḥ
|
dhṛtarāṣṭrasya putreṣu tatsarvaṃ pratipatsyati ||22||

23sadaiva nikṛtiprajño dveṣṭā māyopajīvanaḥ
|
mithyāvinītaḥ satatamasmāsvanapakāriṣu ||23||

24na sakāmā vayaṃ te ca na cāsmābhirna tairjitam
|
na tairmukteyamavanirna nāryo gītavāditam ||24||

25nāmātyasuhṛdāṃ vākyaṃ na ca śrutavatāṃ śrutam
|
na ratnāni parārdhyāni na bhūrna draviṇāgamaḥ ||25||

26asmaddveṣeṇa santaptaḥ sukhaṃ na smeha vindati
|
ṛddhimasmāsu tāṃ dṛṣṭvā vivarṇo hariṇaḥ kṛśaḥ ||26||

27dhṛtarāṣṭraśca nṛpatiḥ saubalena niveditaḥ
|
taṃ pitā putragṛddhitvādanumene naye sthitaḥ ||27||

28anapekṣyaiva pitaraṃ gāṅgeyaṃ viduraṃ tathā
|
asaṃśayaṃ kṣayaṃ rājā yathaivāhaṃ tathā gataḥ ||28||

29aniyamyāśuciṃ lubdhaṃ putraṃ kāmavaśānugam
|
yaśasaḥ patito dīptādghātayitvā sahodarān ||29||

30imau hi vṛddhau śokāgnau prakṣipya sa suyodhanaḥ
|
asmatpradveṣasaṃguktaḥ pāpabuddhiḥ sadaiva ha ||30||

31ko hi bandhuḥ kulīnaḥ saṃstathā brūyātsuhṛjjane
|
yathā'sāvavadadvākyaṃ yuyutsuḥ kṛṣṇasannidhau ||31||

32ātmano hi vayaṃ doṣādvinaṣṭāḥ śāśvatīḥ samāḥ
|
pradahanto diśaḥ sarvā bhāsvarā iva tejasā ||32||

33so'smākaṃ vairapuruṣo durmatiḥ pragrahaṃ gataḥ
|
duryodhanakṛte hyetatkulaṃ no vinipātitam ||33||

34avadhyānāṃ vadhaṃ kṛtvā loke prāptāḥ sma vācyatām
|
kulasyāsyāntakaraṇaṃ durmatiṃ pāpapūruṣam ||34||

35rājā rāṣṭreśvaraṃ kṛtvā dhṛtarāṣṭro'dya śocati
|
hatāḥ śūrāḥ kṛtaṃ pāpaṃ viṣayaḥ svo vināśitaḥ ||35||

36hatvā no vigato manyuḥ śoko māṃ rundhayatyayam
|
dhanañjayakṛtaṃ pāpaṃ kalyāṇenopahanyate ||36||

37khyāpanenānutāpena dānena tapasā'pi vā
|
nivṛttyā tīrthagamanācchruti-smṛti-japena vā ||37||

38tyāgavāṃśca punaḥ pāpaṃ nālaṃkartumiti śrutiḥ
|
tyāgavāñjanmamaraṇe nāpnotīti śrutiryadā ||38||

39prāptavartmā kṛtamatirbrahma saṃpadyate tadā
|
sa dhanañjaya nirdvandvo munirjñānasamanvitaḥ ||39||

40vanamāmantrya vaḥ sarvān gamiṣyāmi parantapa
|
na hi kṛtsnatamo dharmaḥ śakyaḥ prāpnumiti śrutiḥ ||40||

41parigrahavatā tanme pratyakṣamarisūdana
|
mayā nisṛṣṭaṃ pāpaṃ hi parigrahamabhīpsatā ||41||

42janmakṣayanimittaṃ ca prāptuṃ śakyamiti śrutiḥ
|
saparigrahamutsṛjya kṛtsraṃ rājyaṃ sukhāni ca ||42||

43gamiṣyāmi vinirmukto viśoko nirmamaḥ kvacit
|
praśādhi tvamimāmurvīṃ kṣemāṃ nihatakaṇṭakām ||43||

44na mamārtho'sti rājyena bhogairvā kurunandana
|
etāvaduktvā vacanaṃ kururājo yudhiṣṭhiraḥ
|
upāramattataḥ pārthaḥ kanīyānabhyabhāṣata ||44||
