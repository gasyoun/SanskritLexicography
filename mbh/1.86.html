Mahābhārata 1.86 (Ādiparvan)

1vaiśaṃpāyana uvāca|
evaṃ sa nāhuṣo rājā yayātiḥ putramīpsitam
|
rājye'bhiṣicya mudito vānaprastho'bhavanmuniḥ||1||

2uṣitvā ca vane vāsaṃ brāhmaṇaiḥ saṃśitavrataḥ
|
phalamūlāśano dāntastataḥ svargamito gataḥ||2||

3sa gataḥ svarnivāsaṃ taṃ nivasanmuditaḥ sukhī
|
kālena nātimahatā punaḥ śakreṇa pātitaḥ||3||

4nipatanpracyutaḥ svargādaprāpto medinītalam
|
sthita āsīdantarikṣe sa tadeti śrutaṃ mayā||4||

5tata eva punaścāpi gataḥ svargamiti śrutam
|
rājñā vasumatā sārdhamaṣṭakena ca vīryavān||5||

6pratardanena śivinā sametya kila saṃsadi
|
janamejaya uvāca|
karmaṇā kena sa divaṃ punaḥ prāpto mahīpatiḥ||6||

7sarvametadaśeṣeṇa śrotumicchāmi tattvataḥ
|
kathyamānaṃ tvayā vipra viprarṣigaṇasaṃnidhau||7||

8devarājasamo hyāsīdyayātiḥ pṛthivīpatiḥ
|
vardhanaḥ kuruvaṃśasya vibhāvasusamadyutiḥ||8||

9tasya vistīrṇayaśasaḥ satyakīrtermahātmanaḥ
|
caritaṃ śrotumicchāmi divi ceha ca sarvaśaḥ||9||

10vaiśaṃpāyana uvāca|
hanta te kathayiṣyāmi yayāteruttarāṃ kathām
|
divi ceha ca puṇyārthāṃ sarvapāpapraṇāśinīm||10||

11yayātirnāhuṣo rājā pūruṃ putraṃ kanīyasam
|
rājye'bhiṣicya muditaḥ prāvavrāja vanaṃ tadā||11||

12antyuṣe sa vinikṣipya putrānyadupurogamān
|
phalamūlāśano rājā vane saṃnyavasacciram||12||

13śaṃsitātmā jitakrodhastarpayanpitṛdevatāḥ
|
agnīṃśca vidhivajjuhvanvānaprasthavidhānataḥ||13||

14athitīnpūjayāmāsa vanyena haviṣā vibhuḥ
|
śiloñchavṛttimāsthāya śeṣānnakṛtabhojanaḥ||14||

15pūrṇaṃ varṣasahasraṃ ca evaṃ vṛttirabhūnnṛpaḥ
|
abbhakṣaḥ śaradastriṃśadāsīnniyatavāṅmanāḥ||15||

16tataśca vāyubhakṣo'bhūtsaṃvatsaramatandritaḥ
|
tathā pañcāgnimadhye ca tapastepe sa vatsaram||16||

17ekapādaḥ stitaścāsītṣaṇmāsānanilāśanaḥ
|
puṇyakīrtistataḥ svarge jagāmāvṛtya rodasī||17||
