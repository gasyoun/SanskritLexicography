Mahābhārata 7.113 (Droṇaparvan)

1sañjaya uvāca|
prayāte tava sainyaṃ tu yuyudhāne yuyutsayā
|
dharmarājo mahārāja svenānīkena saṃvṛtaḥ||1||

2prāyāddroṇarathaṃ prepsuryuyudhānasya pṛṣṭhataḥ
|
tataḥ pāñcālarājasya putraḥ samaradurmadaḥ||2||

3prākrośatpāṇḍavānīke vasudānaśca pārthivaḥ
|
āgacchata praharata drutaṃ viparidhāvata ||3||

4yathā sukhena gaccheta sātyakiryuddhadurmadaḥ
|
mahārathā hi bahavo yatiṣyantyasya nirjaye ||4||

5iti bruvanto vegena nipetuste mahārathāḥ
|
vayaṃ pratijigīṣantastatra tān samabhidrutāḥ ||5||

6tataḥ śabdo mahānāsīdyuyudhānarathaṃ prati
|
ākīryamāṇā dhāvantī tava putrasya vāhinī||6||

7sātvatena mahārāja śatadhā'bhivyaśīryata
|
tasyāṃ vidīryamāṇāyāṃ śineḥ putro mahārathaḥ||7||

8saptavīrān maheṣvāsānagrānīkeṣvapothayat
|
athānyānapi rājendra nānājanapadeśvarān ||8||

9śarairanalasaṃkāśairninye vīrānyamakṣayam
|
śatamekena vivyādha śatenaikaṃ ca patriṇām ||9||

10dvipārohān ‌dvipāṃścaiva hayārohān hayāṃstathā
|
rathinaḥ sāśvasutāṃca jaghāneśaḥ paśūniva||10||

11taṃ tathā'dbhutakarmāṇaṃ śarasaṃpātavarṣiṇam
|
na kecanābhyadhāvan vai sātyakiṃ tava sainikāḥ||11||

12te bhītā mṛdyamānāśca prasṛṣṭā dīrghabāhunā
|
āyodhanaṃ jahurvīrā dṛṣṭvā tamatimāninam||12||

13tamekaṃ bahudhā'paśyan mohitāstasya tejasā
|
rathairvimathitaiścaiva bhagnanīḍaiśca māriṣa ||13||

14cakrairvimathitaiśchatrairdhvajaiśca vinipātitaiḥ
|
anukarṣaiḥ patākābhiḥ śirastrāṇaiḥ sakāñcanaiḥ||14||

15bāhubhiścandanādigdhaiḥ sāṅgadaiśca viśāṃpate
|
hastihastopamaiścāpi bhujaṅgābhogasannibhaiḥ ||15||

16ūrubhiḥ pṛthivī cchannā manujānāṃ narādhipa
|
śaśāṅkasannibhaiścaiva vadanaiścārukuṇḍalaiḥ ||16||

17patitairṛṣabhākṣāṇāṃ sā babhāvati medinī
|
gajaiśca bahudhā chinnaiḥ śayānaiḥ parvatopamaiḥ ||17||

18rarājātibhṛśaṃ bhūmirvikīrṇairiva parvataiḥ
|
tapanīyamayairyoktrairmuktājālavibhūṣitaiḥ ||18||

19uraśchadairvicitraiśca vyaśobhanta turaṃgamāḥ
|
gatasattvā mahīṃ prāpya pramṛṣṭā dīrghabāhunā||19||

20nānāvidhāni sainyāni tava hatvā tu sātvataḥ
|
praviṣṭastāvakaṃ sainyaṃ drāvayitvā camūṃ bhṛśam||20||

21tatastenaiva mārgeṇa yena yāto dhanañjayaḥ
|
iyeṣa sātyakirgantuṃ tato droṇena vāritaḥ||21||

22bhāradvājaṃ samāsādya yuyudhānaśca sātyakiḥ
|
na nyavartata saṃkruddho velāmiva jalāśayaḥ||22||

23nivārya tu raṇe droṇo yuyudhānaṃ mahāratham
|
vivyādha niśitairbāṇaiḥ pañcabhirmarmabhedibhiḥ||23||

24sātyakistu raṇe droṇaṃ rājan vivyādha saptabhiḥ
|
hemapuṃkhaiḥ śilādhautaiḥ kaṅkabarhiṇavājitaiḥ||24||

25taṃ ṣaḍbhiḥ sāyakairdroṇaḥ sāśvayantāramārdayat
|
sa taṃ na mamṛṣe droṇaṃ yuyudhāno mahārathaḥ ||25||

26siṃhanādaṃ tataḥ kṛtvā droṇaṃ vivyādha sātyakiḥ
|
daśabhiḥ sāyakaiścānyaiḥ ṣaḍbhiraṣṭābhireva ca ||26||

27yuyudhānaḥ punardroṇaṃ vivyādha daśabhiḥ śaraiḥ
|
ekena sārathiṃ cāsya caturbhiścaturo hayān||27||

28dhvajamekena bāṇena vivyādha yudhi māriṣa
|
taṃ droṇaḥ sāśvayantāraṃ sarathadhvajamāśugaiḥ||28||

29tvaran prācchādayadbāṇaiḥ śalabhānāmiva vrajaiḥ
|
tathaiva yuyudhāno'pi droṇaṃ bahubhirāśugaiḥ ||29||

30ācchādayadasaṃbhrāntastato droṇa uvāca ha
|
tavācāryo raṇaṃ hitvā gataḥ kāpuruṣo yathā||30||

31yudhyamānaṃ ca māṃ hitvā pradakṣiṇamavartata
|
tvaṃ hi me yudhyato nādya jīvan yāsyasi mādhava||31||

32yadi māṃ tvaṃ raṇe hitvā na yāsyācāryavaddrutam
|
sātyakiruvāca| dhanañjayasya padavīṃ dharmarājasya śāsanāt||32||

33gacchāmi svasti te brahmanna me kālātyayo bhavet
|
ācāryānugato mārgaḥ śiṣyairanvāsyate sadā||33||

34tasmādeva vrajāmyāśu yathā me sa gururgataḥ
|
sañjaya uvāca|
etāvaduktvā śaineya ācāryaṃ parivarjayan ||34||

35prayātaḥ sahasā rājan sārathiṃ cedamabravīt
|
droṇaḥ kariṣyate yatnaṃ sarvathā mama vāraṇe ||35||

36yatto yāhi raṇe sūta śṛṇu cedaṃ vacaḥ param
|
etadālokyate sainyamāvantyānāṃ mahāprabham||36||

37asyānantaratastvetaddākṣiṇātyaṃ mahadbalam
|
tadanantarametacca bālhikānāṃ mahadbalam ||37||

38bālhikābhyāśato yuktaṃ karṇasya ca mahadbalam
|
anyonyena hi sainyāni bhinnānyetāni sārathe||38||

39anyonyaṃ samupāśritya na tyakṣyanti raṇājiram
|
etadantaramāsādya codayāśvān prahṛṣṭavat ||39||

40madhyamaṃ jayamāsthāya vaha māmatra sārathe
|
bālhikā yatra dṛśyante nānāpraharaṇodyatāḥ||40||

41dākṣiṇātyāśca bahavaḥ sūtaputrapurogamāḥ
|
hastyaśvarathasaṃbādhaṃ yaccānīkaṃ vilokyate||41||

42nānādeśasamutthaiśca padātibhiradhiṣṭhitam
|
etāvaduktvā yantāraṃ brāhmaṇaṃ parivarjayan||42||

43madhyato yāhi yatrograṃ karṇasya ca mahadbalam
|
taṃ droṇo'nuyayau kruddho vikiran viśikhān bahūn ||43||

44yuyudhānaṃ mahābhāgaṃ gacchantamanivartinam
|
karṇasya sainyaṃ sumahada‌bhihatya śitaiḥ śaraiḥ||44||

45prāviśadbhāratīṃ senāmaparyantāṃ ca sātyakiḥ
|
praviṣṭe yuyudhāne tu sainikeṣu druteṣu ca ||45||

46amarṣī kṛtavarmā tu sātyakiṃ paryavārayat
|
tamāpatantaṃ viśikhaiḥ paḍbhirāhatya sātyakiḥ||46||

47caturbhiścaturo'syāśvānājaghānāśu vīryavān
|
tataḥ punaḥ ṣoḍaśabhirnataparvabhirāśugaiḥ ||47||

48sātyakiḥ kṛtavarmāṇaṃ pratyavidhyat stanāntare
|
sa tāḍyamāno viśikhairbahubhistigmatejanaiḥ ||48||

49sātvatena mahārāja kṛtavarmā na cakṣame
|
sa vatsadantaṃ sandhāya jihmagānilasannibham||49||

50ākṛṣya rājannākarṇādvivyādhorasi sātyakim
|
sa tasya dehāvaraṇaṃ bhitvā dehaṃ ca sāyakaḥ ||50||

51sa puṃkhapatraḥ pṛthivīṃ viveśa rudhirokṣitaḥ
|
athāsya bahubhirbāṇairacchinat paramāstravit ||51||

52sa mārgaṇagaṇaṃ rājan kṛtavarmā śarāsanam
|
vivyādha ca raṇe rājan sātyakiṃ satyavikramam||52||

53daśabhirviśikhaistīkṣṇairabhikruddhaḥ stanāntare
|
tataḥ praśīrṇe dhanuṣi śaktyā śaktimatāṃ varaḥ||53||

54jaghāna dakṣiṇaṃ bāhuṃ sātyakiḥ kṛtavarmaṇaḥ
|
tato'nyat sudṛḍhaṃ cāpaṃ pūrṇamāyamya sātyakiḥ||54||

55vyasṛjadviśikhāṃstūrṇaṃ śataśo'tha sahasraśaḥ
|
sarathaṃ kṛtavarmāṇaṃ samantāt paryavārayat ||55||

56chādayitvā raṇe rājan hārdikyaṃ sa tu sātyakiḥ
|
athāsya bhallena śiraḥ sāratheḥ samakṛntata ||56||

57sa papāta hataḥ sūto hārdikyasya mahārathāt
|
tataste yantṛrahitāḥ prādravaṃsturagā bhṛśam ||57||

58atha bhojastu saṃbhrānto nigṛhya turagān svayam
|
tasthau vīro dhanuṣpāṇistat sainyānyabhyapūjayan ||58||

59sa muhūrtamivāśvasya sadaśvān samanodayat
|
vyapetabhīramitrāṇāmāvahat sumahadbhayam ||59||

60sātyakiścābhyagāttasmātsa tu bhīmamupādravat
|
yuyudhāno'pi rājendra bhojānīkādviniḥsṛtaḥ||60||

61prayayau tvaritastūrṇaṃ kāmbojānāṃ mahācamūm
|
sa tatra bahubhiḥ śūraiḥ sanniruddho mahārathaiḥ ||61||

62na cacāla tadā rājansātyakiḥ satyavikramaḥ
|
saṃdhāya ca camūṃ droṇo bhāje bhāraṃ nivaśya ca||62||

63abhyadhāva‌draṇe yatto yuyudhānaṃ yuyutsayā
|
tathā tamanudhāvanta yuyudhānasya pṛṣṭhataḥ ||63||

64nyavārayanta saṃhṛṣṭāḥ pāṇḍusainye bṛhattamāḥ
|
samāsādya tu hārdikyaṃ rathānāṃ pravaraṃ ratham ||64||

65pañcālā vigatotsāhā bhīmasenapurogamāḥ
|
vikramya vāritā rājan vīreṇa kṛtavarmaṇā ||65||

66yatamānāṃśca tān sarvānīṣadvigatacetasaḥ
|
abhitastān śaraugheṇa klāntavāhānakārayat||66||

67nigṛhītāstu bhojena bhojānīkepsavo raṇe
|
atiṣṭhannāryavadvīrāḥ prārthayanto mahadyaśaḥ ||67||
