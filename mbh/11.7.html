Mahābhārata 11.7 (Strīparvan)

1dhṛtarāṣṭra uvāca|aho'bhihitamākhyāna bhavatā tattvadarśinā
|
bhūya eva tu me harṣaḥ śrutvā vāgamṛtaṃ tava||1||

2vidura uvāca|śṛṇu bhūyaḥ pravakṣyāmi mārgasyaitasya vistaram
|
yacchrutvā vipramucyante saṃsārebhyo vicakṣaṇāḥ||2||

3yathā tu puruṣo rājandīrghamadhvānamāsthitaḥ
|
kvacitkvacicchramācchrāntaḥ kurute vāsameva vā||3||

4evaṃ saṃsāraparyāye garbhavāseṣu bhārata
|
kurvanti durbudhā vāsaṃ mucyante tatra paṇḍitāḥ||4||

5tasmādadhvānamevaitamāhuḥ śāstravido janāḥ
|
yattu saṃsāragahanaṃ vanamāhurmanīṣiṇaḥ||5||

6soyaṃ lokasamāvarto martyānāṃ bharatarṣabha
|
carāṇāṃ sthāvarāṇāṃ ca na gṛdhyettatra pāṇḍitaḥ||6||

7śārīrā mānasāścaiva martyānāṃ ye tu vyādhayaḥ
|
pratyakṣāśca parokṣāśca te vyālāḥ kathitā budhaiḥ||7||

8kliśyamānāśca tairnityaṃ vāryamāṇāśca bhārata
|
svakarmabhirmahāvyālairnodvijantyalpabuddhayaḥ||8||

9athāpi tairvimucyeta vyādhibhiḥ puruṣo nṛpa
|
āvṛṇotyeva taṃ paścājjarā rūpavināśinī||9||

10śabdarūparasasparśagandhaiśca vividhairapi
|
majjamāṃsamahāpaṅke nirālambe samantataḥ||10||

11saṃvatsarartavo māsāśca pakṣāhorātrasandhayaḥ
|
krameṇāsyopayuñjanti rūpamāyustathaiva ca||11||

12ete kālasya nidhayo naitāñjānanti durbudhāḥ
|
dhātrā'bhilikhitānyāhuḥ sarvabhūtāni karmaṇā||12||

13rathaḥ śarīraṃ bhūtānāṃ satvamāhustu sārathim
|
indriyāṇi hayānāhuḥ karmabuddhistu raśmayaḥ||13||

14teṣāṃ hayānāṃ yo vegaṃ dhāvatāmanudhāvati
|
sa tu saṃsāracakre'smiṃścakravatparivartate||14||

15yastānsaṃyamate buddhyā saṃyato na nivartate
|
yastu saṃsāracakre'smiṃścakravatparivartate||15||

16bhramamāṇā na muhyanti saṃsāre na bhramanti te
|
saṃsāre bhramatāṃ rājanduḥkhametaddhi jāyate||16||

17taspādasya nivṛttyarthaṃ yatnamevācaredbudhaḥ
|
upekṣā nātra kartavyā śataśākhaḥ pravardhate||17||

18yatendriyo naro rājankrodhalobhanirākṛtaḥ
|
santuṣṭaḥ satyavādī yaḥ sa śāntimadhigacchati||18||

19yāmyamāhū rathaṃ hyenaṃ muhyante yena durbudhāḥ
|
sa caitatprāpnuyādrājanyattvaṃ prāpto narādhipa||19||

20anutarṣulamevaitadduḥkhaṃ bhavati māriṣa
|
rājyanāśaṃ suhṛnnāśaṃ sutanāśaṃ ca bhārata
|
sādhuḥ paramaduḥkhānāṃ duḥkhabhaiṣajyamācaret||20||

21jñānauṣadhamavāpyeha dūrapāraṃ mahauṣadham
|
chindyādduḥkhamahāvyādhiṃ naraḥ saṃyatamānasaḥ||21||

22na vikramo na cāpyartho na mitraṃ na suhṛjjanaḥ
|
tathonmocayate duḥkhādyathātmā sthiraniścayaḥ||22||

23tasmānmaitraṃ samāsthāya śīlamāpadya bhārata
|
damastyāgo'pramādaśca te trayo brahmaṇo hayāḥ||23||

24śīlaraśmisamāyuktaḥ sthito yo mānase rathe
|
tyaktvā mṛtyubhayaṃ rājanbrahmalokaṃ sa gacchati||24||

25abhayaṃ sarvabhūtebhyo yo dadāti mahīpate
|
sa gacchati paraṃ sthānaṃ viṣṇoḥ padamanāmayam||25||

26na tatkratusahasreṇa nopavāsaiśca nityaśaḥ
|
abhayasya ca dānena yatphalaṃ prāpnuyānnaraḥ||26||

27na hyātmanaḥ priyataraṃ kiñcidbhūteṣu niścitam
|
aniṣṭaṃ sarvabhūtānāṃ maraṇaṃ nāma bhārata||27||

28tasmātsarveṣu bhūteṣu dayā kāryā vipaścitā
|
nānāmohasamāyuktā buddhijālena saṃvṛtāḥ||28||

29asūkṣmadṛṣṭayo mandā bhrāmyante tatra tatra ha
|
susūkṣmadṛṣṭayo rājanvrajanti brahma śāśvatam||29||
