Mahābhārata 4.10 (Virāṭaparvan)

1vaiśampāyana uvāca|sahadevo'pi gopānāṃ kṛtvā veṣamanuttamam
|
bhāṣāṃ caiṣāṃ samāsthāya virāṭamupayādatha ||1||

2goṣṭhamāsādya tiṣṭhantaṃ bhavanasya samīpataḥ
|
rājā'tha dṛṣṭvā puruṣān prāhiṇojjātavismayaḥ ||2||

3tamāyāntamabhiprekṣya bhrājamānaṃ nararṣabham
|
samupasthāya vai rājā papraccha kurunandanam ||3||

4kasya vā tvaṃ kuto vā tvaṃ kiṃ vā tvaṃ tu cikīrṣasi
|
na hi me dṛṣṭapūrvastvaṃ tattvaṃ brūhi nararṣabha ||4||

5saṃprāpya rājānamamitratāpanaṃ tato'bravīnmeghamahaughaniḥsvanaḥ
|
vaiśyo'smi nāmnā'hamariṣṭanemirgosaṅkhya āsaṃ kurupuṅgavānām ||5||

6vastuṃ tvayīcchāmi viśāṃ variṣṭha tān rājasiṃhān na hi vedmi pārthān
|
na śakyate jīvitumapyakarmaṇā na ca tvadanyo mama rocate nṛpaḥ ||6||

7virāṭa uvāca|tvaṃ brāhmaṇo yadi vā kṣatriyo'si samudranemīśvararūpavānasi
|
ācakṣva me tattvamamitrakarśana na vaiśyakarma tvayi vidyate kṣamam ||7||

8kasyāsi rājño viṣayādihāgataḥ kiṃ vā'pi śilpaṃ tava vidyate kṛtam
|
kathaṃ tvamasmāsu nivatsyase sadā vadasva kiṃ cāpi taveha vetanam ||8||

9sahadeva uvāca|pañcānāṃ pāṇḍuputrāṇāṃ jyeṣṭho bhrātā yudhiṣṭhiraḥ
|
tasyāṣṭaśatasāhasrā gavāṃ vargāḥ śataṃ śatam ||9||

10apare śatasāhastrā dvistāvantastathā pare
|
teṣāṃ gosaṅkhya āsaṃ vai tantipāleti māṃ viduḥ||10||

11bhūtaṃ bhavyaṃ bhaviṣyaṃ ca yacca saṅkhyāgataṃ gavām
|
na me'styaviditaṃ kiñcit samantāddaśayojanam ||11||

12guṇāḥ suviditā hyāsan mama tasya mahātmanaḥ
|
āsīcca sa mayā dṛṣṭaḥ kururājo yudhiṣṭhiraḥ ||12||

13kṣipraṃ ca gāvo bahulā bhavanti na tāsu rogo bhavatīha kaścana
|
taistairupāyairviditaṃ mamaitadetāni śilpāni mayi sthitāni ||13||

14ṛṣabhāṃścāpi jānāmi rājan pūjitalakṣaṇān
|
yeṣāṃ mūtramupāghrāya api vandhyā prasūyate ||14||

15virāṭa uvāca|śataṃ sahasrāṇi samāhitāni savarṇavarṇasya vimiśritān guṇaiḥ
|
paśūn sapālān bhavate dadāmyaham tvadāśrayā me paśavo bhavantviha ||15||

16vaiśampāyana uvāca|tathā sa rājño vidito viśāmpateruvāsa tatraiva sukhaṃ narottamaḥ
|
na cainamanye'pi viduḥ kathañcana prādācca tasmai bharaṇaṃ yathepsitam ||16||
