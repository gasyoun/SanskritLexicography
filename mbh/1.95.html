Mahābhārata 1.95 (Ādiparvan)

1janamejaya uvāca||
śrutastvatto mayā brahmanpūrveṣāṃ sambhavo mahān
|
udārāścāpi vaṃśe'sminrājāno me pariśrutāḥ ||1||

2kiṃ tu laghvarthasaṃyuktaṃ priyākhyānaṃ na māmati
|
prīṇātyato bhavānbhūyo vistareṇa bravītu me ||2||

3etāmeva kathāṃ divyāmā prajāpatito manoḥ
|
teṣāmājananaṃ puṇyaṃ kasya na prītimāvahet ||3||

4saddharmaguṇamāhātmyairabhivardhitamuttamam
|
viṣṭabhya lokāṃstrīneṣāṃ yaśaḥ sphītamavasthitam ||4||

5guṇaprabhāvavīryaujaḥsattvotsāhavatāmaham
|
na tṛpyāmi kathāṃ śṛṇvannamṛtāsvādasaṃmitām ||5||

6vaiśampāyana uvāca|
śṛṇu rājanpurā samyaṅmayā dvaipāyanācchrutam
|
procyamānamidaṃ kṛtsnaṃ svavaṃśajananaṃ śubham ||6||

7dakṣādaditiraditervivasvānvivasvato manurmanorilā ilāyāḥ purūravāḥ
|
purūravasa āyurāyuṣo
nahuṣo nahuṣādyayātiryayāterdve bhārye babhūvatuḥ ||7||

8uśanaso duhitā devayānī vṛṣaparvaṇaśca duhitā śarmiṣṭhā nāma ||8||

9atrānuvaṃśo bhavati
|
yaduṃ ca turvasuṃ caiva devayānī vyajāyata
|
druhyuṃ cānuṃ ca pūruṃ ca śarmiṣṭhā vārṣaparvaṇī ||9||

10tatra yadoryādavāḥ pūroḥ pauravāḥ ||10||

11pūrostu bhāryā kausalyā nāma tasyāmasya jajñe
janamejayo nāma yastrīnaśvamedhānājahāra viśvajitā ceṣṭvā vanaṃ praviveśa ||11||

12janamejayaḥ khalvanantāṃ nāmopayeme mādhavīṃ
tasyāmasya jajñe prācinvān yaḥ prācīṃ diśaṃ
jigāya yāvatsūryodayāttatastasya prācinvatvam||12||

13prācinvānkhalvaśmakīmupayeme yādavīṃ tasyāmasya jajñe saṃyātiḥ ||13||

14saṃyātiḥ khalu dṛṣadvato duhitaraṃ varāṅgīṃ nāmopayeme tasyāmasya jajñe ahaṃyātiḥ||14||

15ahaṃyātiḥ khalu kṛtavīryaduhitaramupayeme bhānu-
matīṃ nāma tasyāmasya jajñe sārvabhaumaḥ ||15||

16sārvabhaumaḥ khalu jitvā jahāra kaikeyīṃ sunandāṃ
nāma tāmupayeme tasyāmasya jajñe jayatseno nāma||16||

17jayatsenaḥ khalu vaidarbhīmupayeme suśravāṃ nāma
tasyāmasya jajñe avācīnaḥ||17||

18avācīno'pi vaidarbhīmaparāmevopayeme maryādāṃ
nāma tasyāmasya jajñe arihaḥ||18||

19arihaḥ khalvāṅgīmupayeme tasyāmasya jajñe mahābhaumaḥ||19||

20mahābhaumaḥ khalu prāsenajitīmupayeme suyajñāṃ nāma
tasyāmasya jajñe ayutanāyī yaḥ puruṣamedhā-
nāmayutamānayattenāsyāyutanāyitvam||20||

21ayutanāyī khalu pṛthuśravaso duhitaramupayeme kāmāṃ nāma
|
tasyāmasya jajñe akrodhanaḥ ||21||

22sa khalu kāliṅgīṃ karambhāṃ nāmopayeme
|
tasyāmasya jajñe devātithiḥ||22||

23devātithiḥ khalu vaidehīmupayeme maryādāṃ nāma
|
tasyāmasya jajñe'riho nāma||23||

24arihaḥ khalvāṅgeyīmupayeme sudevāṃ nāma
|
tasyāṃ putramajanayadṛkṣam ||24||

25ṛkṣaḥ khalu takṣakaduhitaramupayeme jvālāṃ nāma
|
tasyāṃ putraṃ matināraṃ nāmotpādayāmāsa ||25||

26matināraḥ khalu sarasvatyāṃ guṇasamanvitaṃ dvādaśavārṣikaṃ
satramājahāra samāpte ca satre sarasvatyabhigamya taṃ bhartāraṃ varayāmāsa
|
tasyāṃ putramajanayattaṃsuṃ nāma ||26||

27atrānuvaṃśo bhavati
|
taṃsuṃ sarasvatī putraṃ matinārādajījanat
|
īlinaṃ janayāmāsa kāliṅgyāṃ taṃ surātmajam ||27||

28īlinastu rathantaryāṃ duṣyantādyānpañca putrānajījanat||28||

29duḥṣantaḥ khalu viśvāmitraduhitaraṃ śakuntalāṃ nāmopayeme
|
tasyāmasya jajñe bharataḥ ||29||

30atrānuvaṃśaślokau bhavataḥ
|
bhastrā mātā pituḥ putro yena jātaḥ sa eva saḥ
|
bharasva putraṃ duṣyanta māvamaṃsthāḥ śakuntalām ||30||

31retodhāḥ putra unnayati naradeva yamakṣayāt
|
tvaṃ cāsya dhātā garbhasya satyamāha śakuntalā ||31||

32tato'sya bharatatvaṃ bharataḥ khalu kāśeyīmupayeme
sārvasenīṃ sunandāṃ nāma tasyāmasya jajñe bhumanyuḥ ||32||

33bhumanyuḥ khalu dāśārhīmupayeme vijayāṃ nāma tasyāmasya jajñe suhotraḥ||33||

34suhotraḥ khalvikṣvākukanyāmupayeme suvarṇāṃ nāma
tasyāmasya jajñe hastī
|
ya idaṃ hāstinapuraṃ sthāpayāmāsa
|
etadasya hāstinapuratvam ||34||

35hastī khalu traigartīmupayeme yaśodharāṃ nāma
|
tasyāmasya jajñe vikuṇṭhano nāma||35||

36vikuṇṭhanaḥ khalu dāśārhīmupayeme sudevāṃ nāma
|
tasyāmasya ajamīḍho nāma||36||

37ajamīḍhasya caturviṃśaṃ putraśataṃ babhūva kaikeyyāṃ gāndhāryāṃ
viśālāyāmṛkṣāyāṃ ceti pṛthakpṛthagvaṃśadharā nṛpatayaḥ
|
tatra vaṃśakaraḥ saṃvaraṇaḥ ||37||

38saṃvaraṇaḥ khalu vaivasvatīṃ tapatīṃ nāmopayeme
|
tasyāmasya jajñe kuruḥ ||38||

39kuruḥ khalu dāśārhīmupayeme śubhāṅgīṃ nāma
|
tasyāmasya jajñe vidūra:||39||

40vidūrastu māgadhīmupayeme sampriyāṃ nāma
|
tasyāmasya jajñe anaśvā nāma||40||

41anaśvā khalu māgadhīmupayeme amṛtāṃ nāma
|
tasyāmasya jajñe parīkṣit ||41||

42parīkṣitkhalu bāhudāmupayeme suyaśāṃ nāma
|
tasyāmasya jajñe bhīmasenaḥ||42||

43bhīmasenaḥ khalu kaikeyīmupayeme sukumārīṃ nāma
|
tasyāmasya jajñe patiśravā nāma||43||

44pratiśravasaḥ pratīpaḥ khalu śaibyāmupayeme sunandāṃ nāma
|
tasyāṃ putrānutpādayāmāsa devāpiṃ śāntanuṃ bāhlīkaṃ ceti ||44||

45devāpiḥ khalu bāla evāraṇyaṃ viveśa
|
śāntanustu mahīpālo babhūva||45||

46atrānuvaṃśaśloko bhavati
|
yaṃ yaṃ karābhyāṃ spṛśati jīrṇaṃ sa sukhamaśnute
|
punaryuvā ca bhavati tasmāttaṃ śāntanuṃ viduḥ ||
iti tadasya śāntanutvam ||46||

47śāntanuḥ khalu gaṅgāṃ bhāgīrathīmupayeme
|
tasyāmasya jajñe devavrato yamāhurbhīṣmamiti ||47||

48bhīṣmaḥ khalu pituḥ priyacikīrṣayā satyavatīṃ
mātaramudavāhayat yāmāhurgandhakālīmiti||48||

49tasyāṃ pūrvaṃ kānīno garbhaḥ parāśarāddvaipāyano'bhavat
|
tasyāmeva śantanoranyau dvau putrau babhūvatuḥ ||49||

50vicitravīryaścitrāṅgadaśca tayoraprāptayauvana eva
citrāṅgado gandharveṇa hataḥ vicitravīryastu rājāsīt||50||

51vicitravīryaḥ khalu kausalyātmaje'mbikā
bālike kāśirājaduhitarāvupayeme||51||

52vicitravīryastvanapatya eva videhatvaṃ prāptastataḥ
satyavatyacintayanmā dauṣyanto vaṃśa ucchedaṃ vrajediti||52||

53sā dvaipāyanamṛṣiṃ manasā cintayāmāsa
|
sa tasyāḥ purataḥ sthitaḥ kiṃ karavāṇīti ||53||

54sā tamuvāca bhrātā tavānapatya eva svaryāto vicitravīryaḥ
|
sādhvapatyaṃ tasyotpādayeti ||54||

55sa tathetyuktvā trīnputrānutpādayāmāsa
dhṛtarāṣṭraṃ pāṇḍuṃ viduraṃ ceti||55||

56tatra dhṛtarāṣṭrasya rājñaḥ putraśataṃ babhūva gāndhāryāṃ varadānāddvaipāyanasya||56||

57teṣāṃ dhṛtarāṣṭrasya putrāṇāṃ catvāraḥ pradhānā babhūvurduryodhano duḥśāsano vikarṇaścitrasena iti ||57||

58pāṇḍostu dve bhārye babhūvatuḥ kuntī pṛthā nāma mādrī cetyubhe strīratne||58||

59atha pāṇḍurmṛgayāṃ caranmaithunagatamṛṣimapaśyanmṛgyāṃ vartamānam
|
tathaivādbhutamanāsāditakāmarasamatṛptaṃ ca bāṇenājaghāna||59||

60sa bāṇaviddha uvāca pāṇḍum caratā dharmamimaṃ yena tvayābhijñena kāmarasasyāhamanavāptakāmaraso nihatastasmāttvamapyetāmavasthāmāsādyānavāptakāmarasaḥ pañcatvamāpsyasi kṣiprameveti sa vivarṇarūpastathā pāṇḍuḥ śāpaṃ pariharamāṇo nopāsarpata bhārye vākyaṃ covāca||60||

61svacāpalyādidaṃ prāptavānaham śṛṇomi ca nānapatyasya lokā santīti
|
sā tvaṃ madarthe putrānutpādayeti kuntīmuvāca sā tathoktā putrānutpādayāmāsa
|
dharmādyudhiṣṭhiraṃ mārutādbhīmasenaṃ śakrādarjunamiti ||61||

62tāṃ saṃhṛṣṭarūpaḥ pāṇḍuruvāca |
iyaṃ te sapatnyanapatyā sādhvasyā apatyamutpādyatāmiti
|
evamastviti kuntī tāṃ vidyāṃ mādryāḥ prāyacchat||62||

63mādryāmaśvibhyāṃ nakulasahadevāvutpāditau ||63||

64mādrīṃ khalvalaṅkṛtāṃ dṛṣṭvā pāṇḍurbhāvaṃ cakre sa tāṃ spṛṣṭvaiva videhatvaṃ prāptaḥ ||64||

65tatrainaṃ citāsthaṃ mādrī samanvāruroha
|
uvāca kuntīm yamayorapramattayā tvayā bhavitavyamiti ||65||

66tataste pāṇḍavāḥ kuntyā sahitā hāstinapuramānīya
tāpasairbhīṣmasya ca vidurasya ca niveditāḥ
|
sarvavarṇānāṃ ca nivedyāṃ tarhitāstāpasā babhūvuḥ prekṣyamāṇānāṃ teṣām||66||

67tacca vākyamupaśrutya bhagavatāmantarikṣātpuṣpavṛṣṭiḥ papāta
devadundubhayaśca praṇeduḥ||67||

68pratigṛhītāśca pāṇḍavāḥ piturnidhanamāvedayantastasyaurdhvadehikaṃ
nyāyataśca kṛtavantastāṃstatra nivasataḥ pāṇḍavānbālyātprabhṛti duryodhano nāmarṣayat||68||

69pāpācāro rākṣasīṃ buddhimāśrito'nekairupāyairuddhartuṃ ca
vyavasito bhāvitvāccārthasya na śakitāste samuddhartum||69||

70tataśca dhṛtarāṣṭreṇa vyājena vāraṇāvatamanupreṣitā gamanamarocayan||70||

71tatrāpi jatugṛhe dagdhuṃ samārabdhā na śakitā viduramantritena ||71||

72tasmācca hiḍimbamantarā hatvā ekacakrāṃ gatāḥ ||72||

73tasyāmapyekacakrāyāṃ bakaṃ nāma rākṣasaṃ hatvā pāñcālanagaramabhigatāḥ ||73||

74tatra draupadīṃ bhāryāmavindan svaviṣayaṃ cābhijagmuḥ||74||

75kuśalinaḥ putrāṃścotpādayāmāsuḥ prativindhyaṃ yudhiṣṭhiraḥ
|
sutasomaṃ vṛkodaraḥ śrutakīrtimarjunaḥ śatānīkaṃ nakulaḥ
śrutakarmāṇaṃ sahadeva iti ||75||

76yudhiṣṭhirastu govāsanasya śaibyasya devikāṃ nāma kanyāṃ svayaṃvarāllebhe
|
tasyāṃ putraṃ janayāmāsa yaudheyaṃ nāma ||76||

77bhīmaseno'pi kāśyāṃ baladharāṃ nāmopayeme vīryaśulkām
|
tasyāṃ putraṃ sarvagaṃ nāmotpādayāmāsa||77||

78arjunaḥ khalu dvāravatīṃ gatvā bhaginīṃ vāsudevasya subhadrāṃ bhadrabhāṣiṇīṃ bhāryāmudāvahat
|
svaviṣayaṃ cābhyajagāma kuśalī tasyāṃ putramabhimanyumatīva guṇasampannaṃ dayitaṃ vāsudevasyā'janayat||78||

79nakulastu caidyāṃ kareṇuvatīṃ nāma bhāryāmudavahat
|
tasyāṃ putraṃ niramitraṃ nāmājanayat ||79||

80sahadevo'pi mādrīmeva svayaṃvare vijayāṃ nāmopayeme
|
bhadrarājasya dyutimato duhitaraṃ tasyāṃ putramajanayatsuhotraṃ nāma ||80||

81bhīmasenastu pūrvameva hiḍimbāyāṃ rākṣasyāṃ ghaṭotkacaṃ nāma putramutpādayāmāsa||81||

82ityete ekādaśa pāṇḍavānāṃ putrāsteṣāṃ vaṃśakaro'bhimanyuḥ||82||

83sa virāṭasya duhitaramupayeme uttarāṃ nāma
|
tasyāmasya parāsurgarbho'bhavattamutsaṅgena
pratijagrāha pṛthā niyogātpuruṣottamasya vāsudevasya ṣāṇmāsikaṃ garbhamahamenaṃ jīvayiṣyāmīti ||83||

84sa bhagavatā vāsudevenāsaṃjātabalavīryaparākramo kālajāto'strāgninā dagdhastejasā
svena saṃjīvitaḥ jīvayitvā cainamuvāca parikṣīṇe kule jāto bhavatvayaṃ parikṣinnāmeti||84||

85parikṣitkhalu mādravatīṃ nāmopayeme tvanmātaraṃ tasyāṃ bhāvan janamejayaḥ||85||

86bhavato puṣṭamāyāṃ dvau putrau jajñāte
|
śatānīkaḥ śaṅkukarṇaśca
śatānīkasya vaidehyāṃ putra utpannoścamedhadatta iti||86||

87eṣa pūrorvaṃśaḥ pāṇḍavānāṃ ca kīrtito dhanyaḥ puṇyaḥ paramapavitraḥ satataṃ śrotayvo
brāhmaṇairniyamavadbhiranantaraṃ kṣatriyaiḥ svadharmanirataiḥ tathā śūdrairapi trivarṇaśuśrūṣubhiḥ śraddaddhānairiti||87||

88itihāsamimaṃ puṇyamaśeṣataḥ śrāvayiṣyanti ye narāḥ śroṣyanti vā niyatātmano vimatsarā
maitrā vedaparāstepi svargajitaḥ puṇyalokā bhavānti satataṃ devabrāhmaṇamanuṣyāṇāṃ mānyāḥ saṃpūjyāśca||88||

89paraṃ hīdaṃ bhārataṃ bhagavatā vyāsena proktaṃ pāvanaṃ ye brāhmaṇādayo varṇāḥ śraddadhānā amatsarā
maitrā vedasaṃpannāḥ śroṣyanti te'pi svargajitaḥ sukṛtino'śocyāḥ kṛtākṛte bhavanti||89||

90bhavati cātra ślokaḥ
|
idaṃ hi vedaiḥ samitaṃ pavitramapi cottamam
|
dhanyaṃ yaśasyamāyuṣyaṃ śrotavyaṃ niyatātmabhiḥ||90||
