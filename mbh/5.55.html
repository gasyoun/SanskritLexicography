Mahābhārata 5.55 (Udyogaparvan)

1duryodhana uvāca|
na bhetavyaṃ mahārāja na śocyā bhavatā vayam
|
samarthāḥ sma parān jetuṃ balinaḥ samare vibho ||1||

2vane pravrājitān pārthān yadā''yānmadhusūdanaḥ
|
mahatā balacakreṇa pararāṣṭrāvamardinā ||2||

3kekayā dhṛṣṭaketuśca dhṛṣṭadyumnaśva pārṣataḥ
|
rājānaścānvayuḥ pārthān bahavo'nye'nuyāyinaḥ ||3||

4indraprasthasya cādūrāt samājagmurmahārathāḥ
|
vyagarhayaṃśca saṅgamya bhavantaṃ kurubhiḥ saha ||4||

5te yudhiṣṭhiramāsīnamajinaiḥ prativāsitam
|
kṛṣṇapradhānāḥ saṃhatya paryupāsanta bhārata ||5||

6pratyādānaṃ ca rājyasya kāryamūcurnarādhipāḥ
|
bhavataḥ sānubandhasya samucchedaṃ cikīrṣavaḥ ||6||

7śrutvā caivaṃ mayoktāstu bhīṣmadroṇakṛpāstadā
|
jñātikṣayabhayādrājan bhītena bharatarṣabha ||7||

8tataḥ sthāsyanti samaye pāṇḍavā iti me matiḥ
|
samucchedaṃ hi naḥ kṛtsnaṃ vāsudevaścikīrṣati ||8||

9ṛte ca vidurāt sarve yūyaṃ vadhyā matā mama
|
dhṛtarāṣṭrastu dharmajño na vadhyaḥ kurusattamaḥ ||9||

10samucchedaṃ ca kṛtsnaṃ naḥ kṛtvā tāta janārdanaḥ
|
ekarājyaṃ kurūṇāṃ sma cikīrṣati yudhiṣṭhire ||10||

11tatra kiṃ prāptakālaṃ naḥ praṇipātaḥ palāyanam
|
prāṇān vā saṃparityajya pratiyudhyāmahe parān ||11||

12pratiyuddhe tu niyataḥ syādasmākaṃ parājayaḥ
|
yudhiṣṭhirasya sarve hi pārthivā vaśavartinaḥ ||12||

13viraktarāṣṭrāśca vayaṃ mitrāṇi kupitāni naḥ
|
dhikkṛtāḥ pārthivaiḥ sarvaiḥ svajanena ca sarvaśaḥ ||13||

14praṇipāte na doṣo'sti sandhirnaḥ śāśvatīḥ samāḥ
|
pitaraṃ tveva śocāmi prajñānetraṃ janādhipam ||14||

15matkṛte duḥkhamāpannaṃ kleśaṃ prāptamanantakam
|
kṛtaṃ hi tava putraiśca pareṣāmavarodhanam
|
matpriyārthaṃ puraivaitadviditaṃ te narottama ||15||

16te rājño dhṛtarāṣṭrasya sāmātyasya mahārathāḥ
|
vairaṃ pratikariṣyanti kulocchedena pāṇḍavāḥ ||16||

17tato droṇo'bravīdbhīṣmaḥ kṛpo drauṇiśca bhārata
|
matvā māṃ mahatīṃ cintāmāsthitaṃ vyathitendriyam ||17||

18abhidrugdhāḥ pare cenno na bhetavyaṃ parantapa
|
asamarthāḥ pare jetumasmān yudhi samāsthitān ||18||

19ekaikaśaḥ samarthāḥ smo vijetuṃ sarvapārthivān
|
āgacchantu vineṣyāmo darpameṣāṃ śitaiḥ śaraiḥ ||19||

20puraikena hi bhīṣmeṇa vijitāḥ sarvapārthivāḥ
|
mṛte pitaryatikruddho rathainaikena bhārata ||20||

21jaghāna subahūṃsteṣāṃ saṃrabdhaḥ kurusattamaḥ
|
tataste śaraṇaṃ jagmurdevavratamimaṃ bhayāt ||21||

22sa bhīṣmaḥ susamartho'yamasmābhiḥ sahito raṇe
|
parān vijetuṃ tasmātte vyetu bhīrbharatarṣabha ||22||

23ityeṣāṃ niścayo hyāsīttatkāle'mitatejasām
|
purā pareṣāṃ pṛthivī kṛtsnā''sīdvaśavartinī ||23||

24asmān punaramī nādya samarthā jetumāhave
|
chinnapakṣāḥ pare hyadya vīryahīnāśca pāṇḍavāḥ ||24||

25asmatsaṃsthā ca pṛthivī vartate bharatarṣabha
|
ekārthāḥ sukhaduḥkheṣu samānītāśca pārthivāḥ ||25||

26apyagniṃ praviśeyuste samudraṃ vā parantapa
|
madarthaṃ pārthivāḥ sarve tadviddhi kurusattama ||26||

27unmattamiva cāpi tvāṃ prahasantīha duḥkhitam
|
vilapantaṃ bahuvidhaṃ bhītaṃ paravikatthane ||27||

28eṣāṃ hyekaikaśo rājñāṃ samarthaḥ pāṇḍavān prati
|
ātmānaṃ manyate sarvo vyetu te bhayamāgatam ||28||

29jetuṃ samagrāṃ senāṃ me vāsavo'pi na śaknuyāt
|
hantumakṣayyarūpeyaṃ brahmaṇo'pi svayaṃbhuvaḥ ||29||

30yudhiṣṭhiraḥ puraṃ hitvā pañca grāmān sa yācati
|
bhīto hi māmakāt sainyāt prabhāvāccaiva me vibho ||30||

31samarthaṃ manyase yacca kuntīputraṃ vṛkodaram
|
tanmithyā na hi me kṛtsnaṃ prabhāvaṃ vetsi bhārata ||31||

32matsamo hi gadāyuddhe pṛthivyāṃ nāsti kaścana
|
nāsīt kaścidatikrānto bhavitā na ca kaścana ||32||

33yukto duḥkhoṣitaścāhaṃ vidyāpāragatastathā
|
tasmānna bhīmānnānyebhyo bhayaṃ me vidyate kvacit ||33||

34duryodhanasamo nāsti gadāyāmiti niścayaḥ
|
saṅkarṣaṇasya bhadraṃ te yattadainamupāvasam ||34||

35yuddhe saṅkarṣaṇasamo balenābhyadhiko bhuvi
|
gadāprahāraṃ bhīmo me na jātu viṣahedyudhi ||35||

36ekaṃ prahāraṃ yaṃ dadyāṃ bhīmāya ruṣito nṛpa
|
sa evainaṃ nayeddhoraḥ kṣipraṃ vaivasvatakṣayam ||36||

37iccheyaṃ ca gadāhastaṃ rājan draṣṭuṃ vṛkodaram
|
suciraṃ prārthito hyeṣa mama nityaṃ manorathaḥ ||37||

38gadayā nihato hyājau mayā pārtho vṛkodaraḥ
|
viśīrṇagātraḥ pṛthivīṃ parāsuḥ prapatiṣyati ||38||

39gadāprahārābhihato himavānapi parvataḥ
|
sakṛnmayā vidīryeta giriḥ śatasahasradhā ||39||

40sa cāpyetadvijānāti vāsudevārjunau tathā
|
duryodhanasamo nāsti gadāyāmiti niścayaḥ ||40||

41tatte vṛkodaramayaṃ bhayaṃ vyetu mahāhave
|
vyapaneṣyāmyahaṃ hyenaṃ mā rājan vimanā bhava ||41||

42tasmin mayā hate kṣipramarjunaṃ bahavo rathāḥ
|
tulyarūpā viśiṣṭāśca kṣepsyanti bharatarṣabha ||42||

43bhīṣmo droṇaḥ kṛpo drauṇiḥ karṇo bhūriśravāstathā
|
prāgjyotiṣādhipaḥ śalyaḥ sindhurājo jayadrathaḥ ||43||

44ekaika eṣāṃ śaktastu hantuṃ bhārata pāṇḍavān
|
sametāstu kṣaṇenaitānneṣyanti yamasādanam
|
samagrā pārthivī senā pārthamekaṃ dhanañjayam ||44||

45kasmādaśaktā nirjetumiti heturna vidyate
|
śaravrātaistu bhīṣmeṇa śataśo nicito'vaśaḥ ||45||

46droṇadrauṇikṛpaiścaiva gantā pārtho yamakṣayam
|
pitāmaho'pi gāṅgeyaḥ śāntanoradhi bhārata ||46||

47brahmarṣisadṛśo jajñe devairapi suduḥsahaḥ
|
na hantā vidyate cāpi rājan bhīṣmasya kaścana ||47||

48pitrā hyuktaḥ prasannena nākāmastvaṃ mariṣyasi
|
brahmarṣeśca bharadvājāddroṇo droṇyāmajāyata ||48||

49droṇājjajñe mahārāja drauṇiśca paramāstravit
|
kṛpaścācāryamukhyo'yaṃ maharṣergautamādapi ||49||

50śarastaṃbodbhavaḥ śrīmānavadhya iti me matiḥ
|
ayonijāstrayo hyete pitā mātā ca mātulaḥ ||50||

51aśvatthāmno mahārāja sa ca śūraḥ sthito mama
|
sarva ete mahārāja devakalpā mahārathāḥ ||51||

52śakrasyāpi vyathāṃ kuryuḥ saṃyuge bharatarṣabha
|
naiteṣāmarjunaḥ śakta ekaikaṃ prati vīkṣitum ||52||

53sahitāstu naravyāghrā haniṣyanti dhanañjayam
|
bhīṣmadroṇakṛpāṇāṃ ca tulyaḥ karṇo mato mama ||53||

54anujñātaśca rāmeṇa matsamo'sīti bhārata
|
kuṇḍale rucire cāstāṃ karṇasya sahaje śubhe ||54||

55te śacyarthaṃ mahendreṇa yācitaḥ sa paraṃtapaḥ
|
amoghayā mahārāja śaktyā paramabhīmayā ||55||

56tasya śaktyopagūḍhasya kasmājjīveddhanañjayaḥ
|
vijayo me dhruvaṃ rājan phalaṃ pāṇāvivāhitam ||56||

57abhivyaktaḥ pareṣāṃ ca kṛtsno bhuvi parājayaḥ
|
ahnā hokena bhīṣmo'yaṃ prayutaṃ hanti bhārata ||57||

58tatsamāśca maheṣvāsā droṇa-drauṇi-kṛpā api
|
saṃśaptakānāṃ vṛndāni kṣatriyāṇāṃ parantapa ||58||

59arjunaṃ vayamasmān vā nihanyāt kapiketanaḥ
|
taṃ cālamiti manyante savyasācivadhe dhṛtāḥ ||59||

60pārthivāḥ sa bhavāṃstebhyo hyakasmādvyathate katham
|
bhīmasene ca nihate ko'nyo yudhyeta bhārata ||60||

61pareṣāṃ tanmamācakṣva yadi vetthaṃ parantapa
|
pañca te bhrātaraḥ sarve dhṛṣṭadyumno'tha sātyakiḥ ||61||

62pareṣāṃ sapta ye rājan yodhāḥ sāraṃ balaṃ matam
|
asmākaṃ tu viśiṣṭā ye bhīṣmadroṇakṛpādayaḥ ||62||

63drauṇirvikartanaḥ karṇaḥ somadatto'tha vālhikaḥ
|
prāgjyotiṣādhipaḥ śalya āvantyau ca jayadrathaḥ ||63||

64duḥśāsano durmukhaśca duḥsahaśca viśāmpate
|
śrutāyuścitrasenaśca purumitro viviṃśatiḥ ||64||

65śalo bhūriśravāścaiva vikarṇaśca tavātmajaḥ
|
akṣauhiṇyo hi me rājan daśaikā ca samāhṛtāḥ ||65||

66nyūnāḥ pareṣāṃ saptaiva kasmānme syāt parājayaḥ
|
balaṃ triguṇato hīnaṃ yodhyaṃ prāha bṛhaspatiḥ
|
parebhyastriguṇā ceyaṃ mama rājannanīkinī ||66||

67guṇahīnaṃ pareṣāṃ ca bahu paśyāmi bhārata
|
guṇodayaṃ bahuguṇamātmanaśca viśāmpate ||67||

68etat sarvaṃ samājñāya balāgryaṃ mama bhārata
|
nyūnatāṃ pāṇḍavānāṃ ca na mohaṃ gantumarhasi ||68||

69ityuktvā sañjayaṃ bhūyaḥ paryapṛcchata bhārata
|
vivitsuḥ prāptakālāni jñātvā parapurañjayaḥ ||69||
