Mahābhārata 14.22 (Āśvamedhikaparvan)

1brāhmaṇa uvāca|
atrāpyudāharantīmamitihāsaṃ purātanam
|
subhage saptahotṝṇāṃ vidhānamiha yādṛśam||1||

2ghrāṇaścakṣuśca jihvā ca tvak śrotraṃ caiva pañcamam
|
mano buddhiśca saptaite hotāraḥ pṛthagāśritāḥ||2||

3sūkṣme'vakāśe tiṣṭhanto na paśyantītaretaram
|
etānvai saptahotṝṃstvaṃ svabhāvādviddhi śobhane||3||

4brāhmaṇyuvāca|
sūkṣme'vakāśe santaste kathaṃ nānyonyadarśinaḥ
|
kathaṃsvabhāvā bhagavannetadācakṣva me prabho||4||

5brāhmaṇa uvāca|
guṇājñānamavijñānaṃ guṇajñānamabhijñatā
|
parasparaṃ guṇānete nābhijānanti karhicit||5||

6jihvā cakṣustathā śrotraṃ vāṅmano buddhireva ca
|
na gandhānadhigacchanti ghrāṇastānadhigacchati||6||

7dhrāṇaṃ cakṣustathā śrotraṃ vāṅmano buddhireva ca
|
na rasānadhigacchanti jihvā tānadhigacchati||7||

8ghrāṇaṃ jihvā tathā śrotraṃ vāṅmano buddhireva ca
|
na rūpāṇyadhigacchanti cakṣustānyadhigacchati||8||

9ghrāṇaṃ jihvā tataścakṣuḥ śrotraṃ buddhirmanastathā
|
na sparśānadhigacchanti tvak ca tānadhigacchati||9||

10ghrāṇaṃ jihvā ca cakṣuśca vāṅmano buddhireva ca
|
na śabdānadhigacchanti śrotraṃ tānadhigacchati||10||

11ghrāṇaṃ jihvā ca cakṣuśca tvak śrotraṃ buddhireva ca
|
saṃśayaṃ nādhigacchanti manastamadhigacchati||11||

12ghrāṇaṃ jihvā ca cakṣuśca tvak śrotraṃ mana eva ca
|
na niṣṭhāmadhigacchanti buddhistāmadhigacchati||12||

13atrāpyudāharantīmamitihāsaṃ purātanam
|
indriyāṇāṃ ca saṃvādaṃ manasaścaiva bhāmini||13||

14mana uvāca|
nāghrāti māmṛte ghrāṇaṃ rasaṃ jihvā na vetti ca
|
rūpaṃ cakṣurna gṛhṇāti tvak sparśaṃ nāvabudhyate||14||

15na śrotraṃ budhyate śabdaṃ mayā hīnaṃ kathañcana
|
pravaraṃ sarvabhūtānāmahamasmi sanātanam||15||

16agārāṇīva śūnyāni śāntārciṣa ivāgnayaḥ
|
indriyāṇi na bhāsante mayā hīnāni nityaśaḥ||16||

17kāṣṭhānīvārdraśuṣkāṇi yatamānairapīndriyaiḥ
|
guṇārthānnādhigacchanti māmṛte sarvajantavaḥ||17||

18evametadbhavetsatyaṃ yathaitanmanyate bhavān
|
ṛte'smānasmādarthoṃstvaṃ bhogān bhuṅkte bhavān yadi||18||

19yadyasmāsu pralīneṣu tarpaṇaṃ prāṇadhāraṇam
|
bhogān bhuṅkte bhavān satyaṃ yathaitanmanyate tathā||19||

20athavāsmāsu līneṣu tiṣṭhatsu viṣayeṣu ca
|
yadi saṅkalpamātreṇa bhuṅkte bhogān yathārthavat||20||

21atha cenmanyase siddhimasmadartheṣu nityadā
|
ghrāṇena rūpamādatsva rasamādatsva cakṣuṣā||21||

22śrotreṇa gandhānādatsva sparśānādatsva jihvayā
|
tvacā ca śabdamādatsva buddhyā sparśamathāpi ca||22||

23balavanto hyaniyamā niyamā durbalīyasām
|
bhogānapūrvānādatsva nocchiṣṭaṃ bhoktumarhati||23||

24yathā hi śiṣyaḥ śāstāraṃ śrutyarthamabhidhāvati
|
tataḥ śrutamupādāya śrutyarthamupatiṣṭhati||24||

25viṣayānevamasmābhirdarśitānabhimanyase
|
anāganānatītāṃśca svapne jāgaraṇe tathā||25||

26vaimanasyaṃ gatānāṃ ca jantūnāmalpacetasām
|
asmadarthe kṛte kārye dṛśyate prāṇadhāraṇam||26||

27bahūnapi hi saṅkalpān matvā svapnānupāsya ca
|
bubhukṣayā pīḍyamāno viṣayāneva dhāvati||27||

28agāramadvāramiva praviśya saṅkalpabhogān viṣaye nibaddhān
|
prāṇakṣaye śāntimupaiti nityaṃ dārukṣaye'gnirjvalito yathaiva||28||

29kāmaṃ tu naḥ sveṣu guṇeṣu saṅgaḥ kāmaṃ ca nānyonyaguṇopalabdhiḥ
|
asmānvinā nāsti tavopalabdhi stāvadṛte tvāṃ na bhajet praharṣaḥ||29||
