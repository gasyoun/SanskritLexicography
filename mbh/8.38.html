Mahābhārata 8.38 (Karṇaparvan)

1sañjaya uvāca|
prayāṇe ca tataḥ karṇo harṣayanvāhinīṃ tava
|
ekaikaṃ samare dṛṣṭvā pāṇḍavānparyapṛcchata ||1||

2yo māmadya mahātmānaṃ darśayecchvetavāhanam
|
tasmai dadyāmabhipretaṃ dhanaṃ yanmanasecchati ||2||

3na cettada‌bhimanyeta tasmai dadyāmahaṃ punaḥ
|
śakaṭaṃ ratnasaṃpūrṇaṃ yo me brūyāddhanañjayam ||3||

4na cettada‌bhimanyeta puruṣo'rjunadarśivān
|
śataṃ dadyāṃ gavāṃ tasmai naityakaṃ kāṃsyadohanam ||4||

5śataṃ grāmavarāṃścaiva dadyāmarjunadarśine
|
tathā tasmai punardadyāṃ śvetamaśvatarīratham ||5||

6yuktamañjanakeśībhiryo me brūyāddhanañjayam
|
na cettada‌bhimanyeta puruṣo'rjunada‌rśivān ||6||

7anyaṃ vā'smai punardadyāṃ sauvarṇaṃ hastiṣaḍgavam
|
tathāpyasmai punardadyāṃ strīṇāṃ śatamalaṅkṛtam ||7||

8śyāmānāṃ niṣkakaṇṭhīnāṃ gītavādyavipaścitām
|
na cettadabhimanyeta puruṣo'rjunadarśivān ||8||

9tasmai dadyāñśataṃ nāgān śataṃ grāmāñśataṃ rathān
|
suvarṇasya ca mukhyasya hayāgryāṇāṃ śataṃ śatān ||9||

10ṛddhyā guṇaiḥ sudāntāṃśca dhuryavāhānsuśikṣitān
|
tathā suvarṇaśṛṅgīṇāṃ godhenūnāṃ catuḥśatam ||10||

11dadyāṃ tasmai savatsānāṃ yo me brūyāddhanañjayam
|
na cetta‌dabhimanyeta puruṣo'rjunadarśivān ||11||

12anyadasmai varaṃ dadyāṃ śvetān pañcaśatān hayān
|
hemabhāṇḍaparicchannānsumṛṣṭamaṇibhūṣaṇān ||12||

13sudāntānapi caivāhaṃ dadyāmaṣṭāda‌śāparān
|
rathaṃ ca śubhraṃ sauvarṇaṃ dadyāṃ tasmai salaṅkṛtam ||13||

14yuktaṃ paramakāmbojairyo me brūyāddhanañjayam
|
na cetta‌dabhimanyeta puruṣo'rjunadarśivān ||14||

15anyadasmai varaṃ dadyāṃ kuñjarāṇāṃ śatāni ṣaṭ
|
kāñcanairvividhairbhāṇḍairācchannān hemamālinaḥ ||15||

16utpannānaparānteṣu vinītān hastiśikṣakaiḥ
|
na cettada‌bhimanyeta puruṣo'rjunadarśivān ||16||

17anyadasmai varaṃ dadyāṃ vaiśyagrāmāṃścaturdaśa
|
susphītān dhanasaṃyuktān pratyāsannavanodakān ||17||

18akutobhayān susaṃpannān rājabhojyāṃścaturdaśa
|
dāsīnāṃ niṣkakaṇṭhīnāṃ māgadhīnāṃ śataṃ tathā
|
pratyagravayasāṃ dadyāṃ yo me būyāddhanañjayam ||18||

19na cettadabhimanyeta puruṣo'rjunadarśivān
|
anyaṃ tasmai varaṃ dadyāṃ yamasau kāmayetsvayam ||19||

20putra-dārānvihārāṃśca yadanyadvittamasti me
|
tacca tasmai punardadyāṃ yadyacca manasecchati ||20||

21hatvā ca sahitau kṛṣṇau tayorvittāni sarvaśaḥ
|
tasmai dadyāmahaṃ yo me prabrūyātkeśavārjunau ||21||

22etā vācaḥ subahuśaḥ karṇa uccārayan yudhi
|
dadhmau sāgarasaṃbhūtaṃ susvaraṃ śaṅkhamuttamam ||22||

23tā vācaḥ sūtaputrasya tathā yuktā niśamya tu
|
duryodhano mahārāja saṃhṛṣṭaḥ sānugo'bhavat ||23||

24tato duṃdubhinirghoṣo mṛdaṅgānāṃ ca sarvaśaḥ
|
siṃhanādaḥ savāditraḥ kuñjarāṇāṃ ca niḥsvanaḥ ||24||

25prādurāsīttadā rājansainyeṣu puruṣarṣabha
|
yodhānāṃ saṃprahṛṣṭānāṃ tathā samabhavatsvanaḥ ||25||

26tathā prahṛṣṭe sainye tu plavamānaṃ mahāratham
|
vikatthamānaṃ ca tadā rādheyamarikarṣaṇam
|
madrarājaḥ prahasyedaṃ vacanaṃ pratyabhāṣata ||26||
