Mahābhārata 3.114 (Āraṇyakaparvan)

1vaiśaṃpāyana uvāca|
tataḥ prayātaḥ kauśikyāḥ pāṇḍavo janamejaya
|
ānupūrvyeṇa sarvāṇi jagāmāyatanānyatha||1||

2sa sāgaraṃ samāsādya gaṅgāyāḥ saṅgame nṛpa
|
nadīśatānāṃ pañcānāṃ madhye cakre samāplavam||2||

3tataḥ samudratīreṇa jagāma vasudhādhipaḥ
|
bhrātṛbhiḥ sahito vīraḥ kaliṅgānprati bhārata||3||

4lomaśa uvāca|
ete kaliṅgāḥ kaunteya tatra vaitaraṇī nadī
|
yatrāyajata dharmo'pi devāñcharaṇametya vai||4||

5ṛṣibhiḥ samupāyuktaṃ yajñiyaṃ giriśobhitam
|
uttaraṃ tīrametaddhi satataṃ dvijasevitam||5||

6samānaṃ devayānena pathā svargamupeyuṣaḥ
|
atra vai ṛṣayo'nye'pi purā ṛtubhirījire||6||

7atraiva rudro rājendra paśumādattavānmakhe
|
paśumādāya rājendra bhāgo'yamiti cābravīt||7||

8hṛte paśau tadā devāstamūcurbharatarṣabha
|
mā parasvamabhidrogdhā mā dharmānsakalānvaśīḥ||8||

9tataḥ kalyāṇarūpābhirvāgbhiste rudramastuvan
|
iṣṭyā cainaṃ tarpayitvā mānayāṃcakrire tadā||9||

10tataḥ sa paśumutsṛjya devayānena jagmivān
|
tatrānuvaṃśo rudrasya taṃ nibodha yudhiṣṭhira||10||

11ayātayāmaṃ sarvebhyo bhāgebhyo bhāgamuttamam
|
devāḥ saṃkalpayāmāsurbhayādrudrasya śāśvatam||11||

12imāṃ gāthāmatra gāyannapaḥ spṛśati yo naraḥ
|
devayāno'sya panthāśca cakṣuṣābhiprakāśate||12||

13vaiśaṃpāyana uvāca|
tato vaitaraṇīṃ sarve pāṇḍavā draupadī tathā
|
avatīrya mahābhāgāstarpayāṃcakrire pitṝn||13||

14yudhiṣṭhira uvāca|
upaspṛśyeha vidhivadasyāṃ nadyāṃ tapobalāt
|
mānuṣā‌dasmi viṣayādapetaḥ paśya lomaśa||14||

15sarvālloṃkānprapaśyāmi prasādāttava suvrata
|
vaikhānasānāṃ japatāmeṣa śabdo mahātmanām||15||

16lomaśa uvāca|
triśataṃ vai sahasrāṇi yojanānāṃ yudhiṣṭhira
|
yatra dhvaniṃ śṛṇoṣyenaṃ tūṣṇīmāsva viśāṃpate||16||

17etatsvayaṃbhuvo rājanvanaṃ divyaṃ prakāśate
|
yatrāyajata rājendra viśvakarmā pratāpavān||17||

18yasminyajñe hi bhūrdattā kaśyapāya mahātmane
|
saparvatavanoddeśā dakṣiṇārthe svayaṃbhuvā||18||

19avāsīdacca kaunteya dattamātrā mahī tadā
|
uvāca cāpi kupitā lokeśvaramidaṃ prabhum||19||

20na māṃ martyāya bhagavankasmaiciddātumarhasi
|
pradānaṃ moghametatte yāsyāmyeṣā rasātalam||20||

21viṣīdantīṃ tu tāṃ dṛṣṭvā kaśyapo bhagavānṛṣiḥ
|
prasādayāmbabhūvātha tato bhūmiṃ viśāṃpate||21||

22tataḥ prasannā pṛthivī tapasā tasya pāṇḍava
|
punarunnahya salilādvedīrūpā sthitā babhau||22||

23saiṣā prakāśate rājanvedīsaṃsthānalakṣaṇā
|
āruhyātra mahārāja vīryavānvai bhaviṣyasi||23||

24saiṣā sāgaramāsādya rājanvedī samāśritā
|
etāmāruhya bhadraṃ te tvamekastara sāgaram||24||

25ahaṃ ca te svastyayanaṃ prayokṣye yathā tvamenāmadhirohase'dya
|
spṛṣṭā hi martyena tataḥ samudrameṣā vedī praviśatyājamīḍha||25||

26oṃ namo viśvaguptāya namo viśvaparāya te
|
sāṃnidhyaṃ kuru deveśa sāgare lavaṇāmbhasi||26||

27agnirmitro yonirāpo'tha devyo viṣṇoretastvamamṛtasya nābhiḥ
|
evaṃ bruvanpāṇḍava satyavākyaṃ vedīmimāṃ tvaṃ tarasādhiroha||27||

28agniśca te yoniriḍā ca deho retodhā viṣṇoramṛtasya nābhiḥ
|
evaṃ japanpāṇḍava satyavākyaṃ tato'vagāheta patiṃ nadīnām||28||

29anyathā hi kuruśreṣṭha devayonirapāṃ patiḥ
|
kuśāgreṇāpi kaunteya na spraṣṭavyo mahodadhiḥ||29||

30vaiśaṃpāyana uvāca|
tataḥ kṛtasvastyayano mahātmā yudhiṣṭhiraḥ sāgaramabhyagacchat
|
kṛtvā ca tacchāsanamasya sarvaṃ mahendramāsādya niśāmuvāsa||30||
