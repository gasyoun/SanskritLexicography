Mahābhārata 5.178 (Udyogaparvan)

1bhīṣma uvāca|
evamuktastadā rāmo jahi bhīṣmamiti prabho
|
uvāca rudatīṃ kanyāṃ codayantīṃ punaḥ punaḥ ||1||

2kāśye na kāmaṃ gṛhṇāmi śastraṃ vai varavarṇini
|
ṛte brahmavidāṃ hetoḥ kimanyat karavāṇi te ||2||

3vācā bhīṣmaśca śālvaśca mama rājñi vaśānugau
|
bhaviṣyato'navadyāṅgi tat kariṣyāmi mā śucaḥ ||3||

4na tu śastraṃ grahīṣyāmi kathañcidapi bhāvini
|
ṛte niyogādviprāṇāmeṣa me samayaḥ kṛtaḥ ||4||

5ambovāca
|
mama duḥkhaṃ bhagavatā vyapaneyaṃ yatastataḥ
|
tacca bhīṣmaprasūtaṃ me taṃ jahīśvara mā ciram ||5||

6rāma uvāca|
kāśikanye punarbrūhi bhīṣmaste caraṇāvubhau
|
śirasā vandanārho'pi grahīṣyati girā mama ||6||

7ambovāca
|
jahi bhīṣmaṃ raṇe rāma garjantamasuraṃ yathā
|
samāhūto raṇe rāma mama cedicchasi priyam
|
pratiśrutaṃ ca yadapi tat satyaṃ kartumarhasi ||7||

8bhīṣma uvāca|
tayoḥ saṃvadatorevaṃ rājan rāmāmbayostadā
|
ṛṣiḥ paramadharmātmā idaṃ vacanamabravīt ||8||

9śaraṇāgatāṃ mahābāho kanyāṃ na tyaktumarhasi
|
yadi bhīṣmo raṇe rāma samāhūtastvayā mṛdhe ||9||

10nirjito'smīti vā brūyāt kuryādvā vacanaṃ tava
|
kṛtamasyā bhavet kāryaṃ kanyāyā bhṛgunandana ||10||

11vākyaṃ satyaṃ ca te vīra bhaviṣyati kṛtaṃ vibho
|
iyaṃ cāpi pratijñā te tadā rāma mahāmune ||11||

12jitvā vai kṣatriyān sarvān brāhmaṇeṣu pratiśrutā
|
brāhmaṇaḥ kṣatriyo vaiśyaḥ śūdraścaiva raṇe yadi ||12||

13brahmadviḍbhavitā taṃ vai haniṣyāmīti bhārgava
|
śaraṇārthe prapannānāṃ bhītānāṃ śaraṇārthinām ||13||

14na śakṣyāmi parityāgaṃ kartuṃ jīvan kathañcana
|
yaśca kṛtsnaṃ raṇe kṣatraṃ vijeṣyati samāgatam ||14||

15dīptātmānamahaṃ taṃ ca haniṣyāmīti bhārgava
|
sa evaṃ vijayī rāma bhīṣmaḥ kurukulodvahaḥ || tena yudhyasva saṅgrāme sametya bhṛgunandana ||15||

16rāma uvāca|
smarāmyahaṃ pūrvakṛtāṃ pratijñāmṛṣisattama
|
tathaiva ca cariṣyāmi yathā sāmnaiva lapsyate ||16||

17kāryametanmahadbrahman kāśikanyāmanogatam
|
gamiṣyāmi svayaṃ tatra kanyāmādāya yatra saḥ ||17||

18yadi bhīṣmo raṇaślāghī na kariṣyati me vacaḥ
|
haniṣyāmyenamudriktamiti me niścitā matiḥ ||18||

19na hi bāṇā mayotsṛṣṭāḥ sajjantīha śarīriṇām
|
kāyeṣu viditaṃ tubhyaṃ purā kṣatriyasaṅgare ||19||

20evamuktvā tato rāmaḥ saha tairbrahmavādibhiḥ
|
prayāṇāya matiṃ kṛtvā samuttasthau mahātapāḥ ||20||

21tataste tāmuṣitvā tu rajanīṃ tatra tāpasāḥ
|
hutāgnayo japtajapyāḥ pratasthurmajjighāṃsayā ||21||

22abhyagacchattato rāmaḥ saha tairbrahmavādibhiḥ
|
kurukṣetraṃ mahārāja kanyayā saha bhārata ||22||

23nyaviśanta tataḥ sarve parigṛhya sarasvatīm
|
tāpasāste mahātmāno bhṛguśreṣṭhapuraskṛtāḥ ||23||

24bhīṣma uvāca|
tatastṛtīye divase saṃdideśa vyavasthitaḥ
|
kuru priyaṃ sa me rājan prāpto'smīti mahāvrataḥ ||24||

25tamāgatamahaṃ śrutvā viṣayāntaṃ mahābalam
|
abhyagacchaṃ javenāśu prītyā tejonidhiṃ prabhum ||25||

26gāṃ puraskṛtya rājendra brāhmaṇaiḥ parivāritaḥ
|
ṛtvigbhirdevakalpaiśca tathaiva ca purohitaiḥ ||26||

27sa māmabhigataṃ dṛṣṭvā jāmadagnyaḥ pratāpavān
|
pratijagrāha tāṃ pūjāṃ vacanaṃ cedamabravīt ||27||

28rāma uvāca|
bhīṣma kāṃ buddhimāsthāya kāśirājasutā tadā
|
akāmena tvayā''nītā punaścaiva visarjitā ||28||

29vibhraṃśitā tvayā hīyaṃ dharmādāste yaśasvinī
|
parāsṛṣṭāṃ tvayā hīmāṃ ko hi gantumihārhati ||29||

30pratyākhyātā hi śālvena tvayā''nīteti bhārata
|
tasmādimāṃ manniyogāt pratigṛhṇīṣva bhārata ||30||

31svadharmaṃ puruṣavyāghra rājaputrī labhatviyam
|
na yuktastvavamāno'yaṃ rājñāṃ kartuṃ tvayā'nagha ||31||

32tatastaṃ vai vimanasamudīkṣyāhamathābruvam
|
nāhamenāṃ punardadyāṃ brahman bhrātre kathañcana ||32||

33śālvasyāhamiti prāha purā māmeva bhārgava
|
mayā caivābhyanujñātā gateyaṃ nagaraṃ prati ||33||

34na bhayānnāpyanukrośānnārthalobhānna kāmyayā
|
kṣātraṃ dharmamahaṃ jahyāmiti me vratamāhitam ||34||

35atha māmabravīdrāmaḥ krodhaparyākulekṣaṇaḥ
|
na kariṣyasi cedetadvākyaṃ me narapuṅgava ||35||

36haniṣyāmi sahāmātyaṃ tvāmadyeti punaḥ punaḥ
|
saṃraṃbhādabravīdrāmaḥ krodhaparyākulekṣaṇaḥ ||36||

37tamahaṃ gīrbhiriṣṭābhiḥ punaḥ punararindama
|
ayācaṃ bhṛguśārdūlaṃ na caiva praśaśāma saḥ ||37||

38praṇamya tamahaṃ mūrdhnā bhūyo brāhmaṇasattamam
|
abruvaṃ kāraṇaṃ kiṃ tadyattvaṃ yuddhaṃ mayecchasi ||38||

39iṣvastraṃ mama bālasya bhavataiva caturvidham
|
upadiṣṭaṃ mahābāho śiṣyo'smi tava bhārgava ||39||

40tato māmabravīdrāmaḥ krodhasaṃraktalocanaḥ
|
jānīṣe māṃ guruṃ bhīṣma gṛhṇāsīmāṃ na caiva ha ||40||

41sutāṃ kāśyasya kauravya matpriyārthaṃ mahāmate
|
na hi te vidyate śāntiranyathā kurunandana ||41||

42gṛhāṇemāṃ mahābāho rakṣasva kulamātmanaḥ
|
tvayā vibhraṃśitā hīyaṃ bhartāraṃ nādhigacchati ||42||

43tathā bravantaṃ tamahaṃ rāmaṃ parapurañjayam
|
naitadevaṃ punarbhāvi brahmarṣe kiṃ śrameṇa te ||43||

44gurutvaṃ tvayi saṃprekṣya jāmadagnya purātanam
|
prasādaye tvāṃ bhagavaṃstyaktaiṣā tu purā mayā ||44||

45ko jātu parabhāvāṃ hi nārīṃ vyālīmavasthitām
|
vāsayeta gṛhe jānan strīṇāṃ doṣo mahātyayaḥ ||45||

46na bhayādvāsavasyāpi dharmaṃ jahyāṃ mahāvrata
|
prasīda mā vā yadvā te kāryaṃ tatkuru mā ciram ||46||

47ayaṃ cāpi viśuddhātman purāṇe śrūyate vibho
|
maruttena mahābuddhe gītaḥ śloko mahātmanā ||47||

48"gurorapyavaliptasya kāryākāryamajānataḥ
|
utpathapratipannasya parityāgo vidhīyate " ||48||

49sa tvaṃ gururiti premṇā mayā saṃmānito bhṛśam
|
guruvṛttiṃ na jānīṣe tasmādyotsyāmi vai tvayā ||49||

50guruṃ na hanyāṃ samare brāhmaṇaṃ ca viśeṣataḥ
|
viśeṣatastapovṛddhamevaṃ kṣāntaṃ mayā tava ||50||

51udyateṣumatho dṛṣṭvā brāhmaṇaṃ kṣatrabandhuvat
|
yo hanyāt samare kuddhaṃ yudhyantamapalāyinam ||51||

52brahmahatyā na tasya syāditi dharmeṣu niścayaḥ
|
kṣatriyāṇāṃ sthito dharme kṣatriyo'smi tapodhana ||52||

53yo yathā vartate yasmiṃstasminneva pravartayan
|
nādharmaṃ samavāpnoti na cāśreyaśca vindati ||53||

54arthe vā yadi vā dharme samartho deśakālavit
|
arthasaṃśayamāpannaḥ śreyānniḥsaṃśayo naraḥ ||54||

55yasmāt saṃśayite'pyarthe'yathānyāyaṃ pravartase
|
tasmādyotsyāmi sahitastvayā rāma mahāhave ||55||

56paśya me bāhuvīryaṃ ca vikramaṃ cātimānuṣam
|
evaṃ gate'pi tu mayā yacchakyaṃ bhṛgunandana ||56||

57tatkariṣye kurukṣetre yotsye vipra tvayā saha
|
dvandve rāma yatheṣṭaṃ me sajjībhava mahādyute ||57||

58tatra tvaṃ nihato rāma mayā śaraśatārditaḥ
|
prāpsyase nirjitā~llokān śastrapūto mahāraṇe ||58||

59sa gaccha vinivartasva kurukṣetraṃ raṇapriya
|
tatraiṣyāmi mahābāho yuddhāya tvāṃ tapodhana ||59||

60api yatra tvayā rāma kṛtaṃ śaucaṃ purā pituḥ
|
tatrāhamapi hatvā tvāṃ śaucaṃ kartā'smi bhārgava ||60||

61tatra rāma samāgaccha tvaritaṃ yuddhadurmada
|
vyapaneṣyāmi te darpaṃ paurāṇaṃ brāhmaṇadhruvaḥ ||61||

62yaccāpi katthase rāma bahuśaḥ parivatsare
|
nirjitāḥ kṣatriyā loke mayaikeneti tacchṛṇu ||62||

63na tadā jātavān bhīṣmaḥ kṣatriyo vā'pi madvidhaḥ
|
paścājjātāni tejāṃsi tṛṇeṣu jvalitaṃ tvayā ||63||

64yaste yuddhamayaṃ darpaṃ kāmaṃ ca vyapanāśayet
|
so'haṃ jāto mahābāho bhīṣmaḥ parapurañjayaḥ
|
vyapaneṣyāmi te darpaṃ yuddhe rāma na saṃśayaḥ ||64||

65bhīṣma uvāca|
tato māmabravīdrāmaḥ prahasanniva bhārata
|
diṣṭyā bhīṣma mayā sārdhaṃ yoddhamicchasi saṅgare||65||

66ayaṃ gacchāmi kauravya kurukṣetraṃ tvayā saha
|
bhāṣitaṃ te kariṣyāmi tatrāgaccha paraṃtapa ||66||

67tatra tvāṃ nihataṃ mātā mayā śaraśatācitam
|
jāhnavī paśyatāṃ bhīṣma gṛdhrakaṅkabalāśanam ||67||

68kṛpaṇaṃ tvāmabhiprekṣya siddhacāraṇasevitā
|
mayā vinihataṃ devī rodatāmadya pārthiva ||68||

69atadarhā mahābhāgā bhagīrathasutā'naghā
|
yā tvāmajījananmandaṃ yuddhakāmukamāturam ||69||

70ehi gaccha mayā bhīṣma yuddhakāmuka durmada
|
gṛhāṇa sarvaṃ kauravya rathādi bharatarṣabha ||70||

71iti bruvāṇaṃ tamahaṃ rāmaṃ parapurañjayam
|
praṇamya śirasā rāmamevamastvityathābruvam ||71||

72evamuktvā yayau rāmaḥ kurukṣetraṃ yuyutsayā
|
praviśya nagaraṃ cāhaṃ satyavatyai nyavedayam ||72||

73tataḥ kṛtasvastyayano mātrā ca pratinanditaḥ
|
dvijātīn vācya puṇyāhaṃ svasti caiva mahādyūte ||73||

74rathamāsthāya ruciraṃ rājataṃ pāṇḍurairhayaiḥ
|
sūpaskaraṃ svadhiṣṭhānaṃ vaiyāghraparivāraṇam ||74||

75upapannaṃ mahāśastraiḥ sarvopakaraṇānvitam
|
tatkulīnena vīreṇa hayaśāstravidāraṇe ||75||

76yattaṃ sūtena śiṣṭena bahuśo dṛṣṭakarmaṇā
|
daṃśitaḥ pāṇḍureṇāhaṃ kavacena vapuṣmatā ||76||

77pāṇḍuraṃ kārmukaṃ gṛhya prāyāṃ bharatasattama
|
pāṇḍureṇātapatreṇa dhriyamāṇena mūrdhani ||77||

78pāṇḍuraiścāpi vyajanairvījyamāno narādhipa
|
śuklavāsāḥ sitoṣṇīṣaḥ sarvaśuklavibhūṣaṇaḥ||78||

79stūyamāno jayāśībhirniṣkramya gajasāhvayāt
|
kurukṣetraṃ raṇakṣetramupāyāṃ bharatarṣabha ||79||

80te hayāścoditāstena sūtena paramāhave
|
avahanmāṃ bhṛśaṃ rājan manomārutaraṃhasaḥ ||80||

81gatvā'haṃ tat kurukṣetraṃ sa ca rāmaḥ pratāpavān
|
yuddhāya sahasā rājan parākrāntau parasparam ||81||

82tataḥ saṃdarśane'tiṣṭhaṃ rāmasyātitapasvinaḥ
|
pragṛhya śaṅkhapravaraṃ tataḥ prādhamamuttamam ||82||

83tatastatra dvijā rājaṃstāpasāśca vanaukasaḥ
|
apaśyanta raṇaṃ divyaṃ devāḥ sendragaṇāstadā ||83||

84tato divyāni mālyāni prādurāsaṃstatastataḥ
|
vāditrāṇi ca divyāni meghavṛndāni caiva ha ||84||

85tataste tāpasāḥ sarve bhārgavasyānuyāyinaḥ
|
prekṣakāḥ samapadyanta parivārya raṇājiram ||85||

86tato māmabravīddevī sarvabhūtahitaiṣiṇī
|
mātā svarūpiṇī rājan kimidaṃ te cikīrṣitam ||86||

87gatvā'haṃ jāmadagnyaṃ tu prayāciṣye kurūdvaha
|
bhīṣmeṇa saha mā yotsīḥ śiṣyeṇeti punaḥ punaḥ ||87||

88mā maivaṃ putra nirbandhaṃ kuru vipreṇa pārthiva
|
jāmadagnyena samare yoddhamityeva bhartsayat ||88||

89kinna vai kṣatriyahaṇo haratulyaparākramaḥ
|
viditaḥ putra rāmaste yatastaṃ yoddhumicchasi ||89||

90tato'hamabruvaṃ devīmabhivādya kṛtāñjaliḥ
|
sarvaṃ tadbharataśreṣṭha yathāvṛttaṃ svayaṃvare ||90||

91yathā ca rāmo rājendra mayā pūrvaṃ pracoditaḥ
|
kāśirājasutāyāśca yathā karma purātanam ||91||

92tataḥ sā rāmamabhyetya jananī me mahānadī
|
madarthaṃ tamṛṣiṃ vīkṣya kṣamayāmāsa bhārgavam ||92||

93bhīṣmeṇa saha mā yotsīḥ śiṣyeṇeti vaco'bravīt
|
sa ca tāmāha yācantīṃ bhīṣmameva nivartaya
|
na ca me kurute kāmamityahaṃ tamupāgamam ||93||

94vaiśampāyana uvāca|
tato gaṅgā sutasnehādbhīṣmaṃ punarupāgamat
|
na cāsyāścākarodvākyaṃ krodhaparyākulekṣaṇaḥ ||94||

95athādṛśyata dharmātmā bhṛguśreṣṭho mahātapāḥ
|
āhvayāmāsa ca tadā yuddhāya dvijasattamaḥ ||95||
