Mahābhārata 13.4 (Anuśāsanaparvan)

1bhīṣma uvāca|
śrūyatāṃ pārtha tattvena viśvāmitro yathā purā
|
brāhmaṇatvaṃ gatastāta brahmarṣitvaṃ tathaiva ca ||1||

2bharatasyānvaye caivājamīḍho nāma pārthivaḥ
|
babhūva bharataśreṣṭha yajvā dharmabhṛtāṃ varaḥ ||2||

3tasya putro mahānāsījjanhurnāma nareśvaraḥ
|
duhitṛtvamanuprāptā gaṅgā yasya mahātmanaḥ ||3||

4tasyātmajastulyaguṇaḥ sindhudvīpo mahāyaśāḥ
|
sindhudvīpācca rājarṣirbalākāśvo mahābalaḥ ||4||

5vallabhastasya tanayaḥ sākṣāddharma ivāparaḥ
|
kuśikastasya tanayaḥ sahasrākṣasamadyutiḥ ||5||

6kuśikasyātmajaḥ śrīmān gādhirnāma janeśvaraḥ
|
aputraḥ prasavenārthī vanavāsasupāvasat ||6||

7kanyā jajñe sutāttasya vane nivasataḥ sataḥ
|
nāmnā satyavatī nāma rūpeṇāpratimā bhuvi ||7||

8tāṃ vavre bhārgavaḥ śrīmāṃścyavanasyātmasambhavaḥ
|
ṛcīka iti vikhyāto vipule tapasi sthitaḥ ||8||

9satāṃ na pradadau tasmai ṛcīkāya mahātmane
|
daridra iti matvā vai gādhiḥ śatrunibarhaṇaḥ ||9||

10pratyākhyāya punaryātamabravīdrājasattamaḥ
|
śulkaṃ pradīyatāṃ mahyaṃ tato vatsyasi me sutām ||10||

11ṛcīka uvāca|
kiṃ prayacchāmi rājendra tubhyaṃ śulkamahaṃ nṛpa
|
duhiturbrūhyasaṃsakto mā'bhūttatra vicāraṇā ||11||

12gādhiruvāca|
candraraśmiprakāśānāṃ hayānāṃ vātaraṃhasām
|
ekataḥ śyāmakarṇānāṃ sahasraṃ dehi bhārgava ||12||

13bhīṣma uvāca|
tataḥ sa bhṛguśārdūlaścyavanasyātmajaḥ prabhuḥ
|
abravīdvaruṇaṃ devamādityaṃ patimambhasām ||13||

14ekataḥ śyāmakarṇānāṃ hayānāṃ candravarcasām
|
sahasraṃ vātavegānāṃ bhikṣe tvāṃ devasattama ||14||

15tatheti varuṇo deva ādityo bhṛgusattamam
|
uvāca yatra te cchandastatrotthāsyanti vājinaḥ ||15||

16dhyātamātraṃ ṛcīkena hayānāṃ candravarcasām
|
gaṅgājalātsamuttasthau sahasraṃ vipulaujasām ||16||

17adūre kānyakubjasya gaṅgāyāstīramuttamam
|
aśvatīrthaṃ tadadyāpi mānavaiḥ paricakṣyate ||17||

18tato vai gādhaye tāta sahasraṃ vājināṃ śubham
|
ṛcīkaḥ pradadau prītaḥ śulkārthaṃ tapatāṃ varaḥ ||18||

19tataḥ sa vismito rājā gādhiḥśāpabhayena ca
|
dadau tāṃ samalaṅkṛtya kanyāṃ bhṛgusutāya vai ||19||

20jagrāha vidhivatpāṇiṃ tasyā brahmarṣisattamaḥ
|
sā ca taṃ patimāsādya paraṃ harṣamavāpa ha ||20||

21sa tutoṣa ca brahmarṣistasyā vṛttena bhārata
|
chandayāmāsa caivaināṃ vareṇa varavarṇinīm ||21||

22mātre tatsarvamācakhyau sā kanyā rājasattama
|
atha tāmabravīnmātā sutāṃ kiñcida‌vāṅmukhīṃ ||22||

23mamāpi putri bhartā te prasādaṃ kartumarhati
|
apatyasya pradānena samarthaśca mahātapāḥ ||23||

24tataḥ sā tvaritaṃ gatvā tatsarvaṃ pratyavedayat
|
mātuścikīrṣitaṃ rājan ṛcīkastāmathābravīt ||24||

25guṇavantamapatyaṃ sā acirājjanayiṣyati
|
mama prasādātkalyāṇi mā'bhūtte praṇayo'nyathā ||25||

26tava caiva guṇaślādhī putra utpatsyate mahān
|
asmadvaṃśakaraḥ śrīmānsatyametadbravīmi te ||26||

27ṛtusnātā ca sā'śvatthaṃ tvaṃ ca vṛkṣamudumbaram
|
pariṣvajethāḥ kalyāṇi tata evamavāpsyathaḥ ||27||

28carudvayāmidaṃ caiva mantrapūtaṃ śucismite
|
tvaṃ ca sā copabhuñjītaṃ tataḥ putrāvavāpsyathaḥ ||28||

29tataḥ satyavatī hṛṣṭā mātaraṃ pratyabhāṣata
|
yadṛcīkena kathitaṃ taccācakhyau carudvayam ||29||

30tāmuvāca tato mātā sutāṃ satyavatīṃ tadā
|
putri pūrvopapannāyāḥ kuruṣva vacanaṃ mama ||30||

31bhartrā ya eṣa dattaste carurmantrapuraskṛtaḥ
|
enaṃ prayaccha mahyaṃ tvaṃ madīyaṃ tvaṃ gṛhāṇa ca ||31||

32vyatyāsaṃ vṛkṣayoścāpi karavāva śucismite
|
yadi pramāṇaṃ vacanaṃ mama māturanindite ||32||

33svamapatyaṃ viśiṣṭaṃ hi sarva icchatyanāvilam
|
vyaktaṃ bhagavatā cātra kṛtamevaṃ bhaviṣyati ||33||

34tato me tvaccarau bhāvaḥ pādape ca sumadhyame
|
kathaṃ viśiṣṭo bhrātā me bhavedityeva cintaya ||34||

35tathā ca kṛtavatyau te mātā satyavatī ca sā
|
atha garbhāvanuprāpte ubhe te vai yudhiṣṭhira ||35||

36dṛṣṭvā garbhamanuprāptāṃ bhāryāṃ sa ca mahānṛṣiḥ
|
uvāca tāṃ satyavatīṃ durmanā bhṛgusattamaḥ ||36||

37vyatyāsenopayuktaste carurvyaktaṃ bhaviṣyati
|
vyatyāsaḥ pādape cāpi suvyaktaṃ te kṛtaḥ śubhe ||37||

38mayā hi viśvaṃ yadbrahma tvaccarau sanniveśitam
|
kṣatravīryaṃ ca sakalaṃ carau tasyā niveśinam ||38||

39trailokyavikhyātaguṇaṃ tvaṃ vipraṃ janayiṣyasi
|
sā ca kṣatraṃ viśiṣṭaṃ vai tata etatkṛtaṃ mayā ||39||

40vyatyāsastu kṛto yasmāttvayā mātrā ca te śubhe
|
tāsmātsā brāhmaṇaṃ śreṣṭhaṃ mātā te janayiṣyati ||40||

41kṣatriyaṃ tūgrakarmāṇaṃ tvaṃ bhadre janayiṣyasi
|
na hi te tatkṛtaṃ sādhu mātṛsnehena bhāvini ||41||

42sā śrutvā śokasantaptā papāna varavarṇinī
|
bhūmau satyavatī rājañcchinneva rucirā latā ||42||

43pratilabhya ca sā saṃjñā śirasā praṇipatya ca
|
uvāca bhāryā bhartāraṃ gādheyī bhārgavarṣabham ||43||

44prasādayantyāṃ bhāryāyāṃ mayi brahmavidāṃvara
|
prasādaṃ kuru viprarṣe na mesyātkṣatriyaḥ sutaḥ ||44||

45kāmaṃ mamograkarmā vai pātro bhavitumarhati
|
na tu me syātsuto brahmanneva me dīyatāṃ varaḥ ||45||

46evamastviti hovāca svāṃ bhāryāṃ sumahātapāḥ
|
tataḥ sā janayāmāsa jamadagniṃ sutaṃ śubham ||46||

47viśvāmitraṃ cājanayadgādhibhāryā yaśasvinī
|
ṛṣeḥ prasādādrājendra brahmarṣerbrahmavādinam ||47||

48tato brāhmaṇatāṃ yāto viśvāmitro mahātapāḥ
|
kṣatriyaḥ so'nyatha tathā brahmavaṃśasya kārakaḥ ||48||

49tasya putrā mahātmāno brahmavaṃśavivardhanāḥ
|
tapasvino brahmavido gotrakartāra eva ca ||49||

50madhucchandaśca bhagavān devarātaśca vīryavān
|
akṣīṇaśca śakuntaśca babhruḥ kālapathattathā ||50||

51yājñavalkyaśca vikhyātastathā sthūṇo mahāvrataḥ
|
ulūko yamadūtaśca tatharṣiḥ saindhavāyanaḥ ||51||

52valgujaṅghaśca bhagavān gālavaśca mahānṛṣiḥ
|
ṛṣirvajrastathā khyātaḥ sālaṅkāyana eva ca ||52||

53līlāḍhyo nārada‌ścaiva tayā kūrvāmukhaḥ smṛtaḥ
|
vādularmusalaśceva vakṣogrīvastathaiva ca ||53||

54āṅghriko naikadṛkcaiva śilāyūpaḥ śitaḥ śuciḥ
|
cakrako mārutantavyo vātaghno'thāśvalāyanaḥ ||54||

55śyāmāyano'tha gārgyaśca jābāliḥ suśrutastathā
|
kārīṣiratha saśrutyaḥ paraporavatantavaḥ ||55||

56mahānṛṣiśca kapilastatharṣistāḍakāyanaḥ
|
tathaiva copagahanasta varṣiścāsurāyaṇaḥ ||56||

57mārdamarṣirhiraṇyākṣo jaṅgārirbābhravāyaṇiḥ
|
bhūtirvibhūtiḥ sūtaśca surakṛtta tathaiva ca ||57||

58arālirnāvikaścaiva cāmpeyojjayanau tathā
|
navatanturbakanakhaḥ seyano yātireva ca ||58||

59ambhoruhaścārumatsyaḥ śirīṣī cātha gārdabhiḥ
|
ūrjayonirudāpekṣī nāradī ca mahānṛṣiḥ ||59||

60viśvāmitrātmajāḥ seva munayo brahmavādinaḥ
|
tathaiva kṣatriyo rājan viśvāmitro mahātapāḥ ||60||

61ṛcīkenāhitaṃ brahma parametadyudhiṣṭhira
|
etatte sarvamākhyātaṃ tattvena bharatarṣabha ||61||

62viśvāmitrasya vai janma somasūryāgnitejasaḥ
|
yatra yatra ca sandeho bhūyaste rājasattama
|
tatra tatra ca māṃ brūhi cchettā'smi tava saṃśayam ||62||
