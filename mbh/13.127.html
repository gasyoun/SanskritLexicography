Mahābhārata 13.127 (Anuśāsanaparvan)

1vibhāvasuruvāca|
salilasyāñjaliṃ pūrṇamakṣatāśca ghṛtottarāḥ
|
somasyottiṣṭhamānasya tajjalaṃ cākṣatāṃśca tān||1||

2sthito hyabhimukho martyaḥ paurṇamāsyāṃ baliṃ haret
|
agnikāryaṃ kṛtaṃ tena hutāścāsyāgnayastrayaḥ||2||

3vanaspatiṃ ca yo hanyādamāvāsyāmabuddhimān
|
api hyekena patreṇa lipyate brahmahatyayā ||3||

4dantakāṣṭhaṃ tu yaḥ khādedamāvāsyāmabuddhimān
|
hiṃsitaścandramāstena pitaraścodvijanti ca ||4||

5havyaṃ na tasya devāśca pratigṛhṇanti parvasu
|
kupyante pitaraścāsya kule vaṃśo'sya hīyate||5||

6śrīruvāca|
prakīrṇaṃ bhājanaṃ yatra bhinnabhāṇḍamathāsanam
|
yoṣitaścaiva hanyante kaśmalopahate gṛhe ||6||

7devatāḥ pitaraścaiva utsave parvaṇīṣu vā
|
nirāśāḥ pratigacchanti kaśmalopahatādgṛhāt ||7||

8aṅgirā uvāca|
yastu saṃvatsaraṃ pūrṇaṃ dadyāddīpaṃ karañjake
|
suvarcalāmūlahastaḥ prajā tasya vivardhate ||8||

9gārgya uvāca|
ātithyaṃ satataṃ kuryāddīpaṃ dadyātpratiśraye
|
varjayāno divā svāpaṃ na ca māṃsāni bhakṣayet||9||

10gobrāhmaṇaṃ na hiṃsyācca puṣkarāṇi ca kīrtayet
|
eṣa śreṣṭhatamo dharmaḥ sarahasyo mahāphalaḥ ||10||

11api kratuśatairiṣṭvā kṣayaṃ gacchati taddhaviḥ
|
na tu kṣīyanti te dharmāḥ śraddadhānaiḥ prayojitāḥ
|
11||

12idaṃ ca paramaṃ guhyaṃ sarahasyaṃ nibodhata
|
śrāddhakalpe ca daive ca tairthike parvaṇīṣu ca ||12||

13rajasvalā ca yā nārī śvitrikā'putrikā ca yā
|
etābhiścakṣuṣā dṛṣṭaṃ havirnāśnanti devatāḥ ||13||

14pitaraśca na tuṣyanti varṣāṇyapi trayodaśa
|
śuklavāsāḥ śucirbhūtvā brāhmaṇān svasti vācayet
|
kīrtayedbhārataṃ caiva tathā syādakṣayaṃ haviḥ ||14||

15dhaumya uvāca|
bhinnabhāṇḍaṃ ca khaṭvāṃ ca kukuṭaṃ śunakaṃ tathā
|
apraśastāni sarvāṇi yaśca vṛkṣo gṛheruhaḥ ||15||

16bhinnabhāṇḍe kaliṃ prāhuḥ khaṭvāyāṃ tu dhanakṣayaḥ
|
kukkuṭe śunake caiva havirnāśnanti devatāḥ
|
vṛkṣamūle dhruvaṃ sattvaṃ tasmādvṛkṣaṃ na ropayet ||16||

17jamadagniruvāca|
yo yajedaśvamedhena vājapeyaśatena ha
|
avākśirā vā lambeta satraṃ vā sphītamāharet||17||

18na yasya hṛdayaṃ śuddhaṃ narakaṃ sa dhruvaṃ vrajet
|
tulyaṃ yajñaśca satyaṃ ca hṛdayasya ca śuddhatā ||18||

19śuddhena manasā dattvā saktuprasthaṃ dvijātaye
|
brahmalokamanuprāptaḥ paryāptaṃ tannidarśanam ||19||
