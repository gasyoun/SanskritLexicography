Mahābhārata 12.200 (Śāntiparvan)

1yudhiṣṭhira uvāca|
kimuttaraṃ tadā tau sma cakratustasya bhāṣite
|
brāhmaṇo vā'thavā rājā tanme brūhi pitāmaha ||1||

2athavā tau gatau tatra yadetatkīrtitaṃ tvayā
|
saṃvādo vā tayoḥ ko'bhūtkiṃ vā tau tatra cakratuḥ ||2||

3bhīṣma uvāca|
tathetyevaṃ pratiśrutya dharmaṃ sampūjya ca prabho
|
yamaṃ kālaṃ ca mṛtyuṃ ca svargaṃ sampūjya cārhataḥ ||3||

4pūrvaṃ ye cāpare tatra sametā brāhmaṇarṣabhāḥ
|
sarvānsampūjya śirasā rājānaṃ so'bravīddvijaḥ ||4||

5phalenānena saṃyukto rājarṣe gaccha mukhyatām
|
bhavatā cābhyanujñāto japeyaṃ bhūya eva ha ||5||

6varaśca mama pūrvaṃ hi datto devyā mahābala
|
śraddhā te japato nityaṃ bhavatviti viśāmpate ||6||

7rājovāca |
yadyevamaphalā siddhiḥ śraddhā ca japituṃ tava
|
gaccha vipra mayā sārdhaṃ jāpakaṃ phalamāpnuhi ||7||

8brāhmaṇa uvāca|
kṛtaḥ prayatnaḥ sumahānsarveṣāṃ sannidhāviha
|
saha tulyaphalāvāvāṃ gacchāvo yatra nau gatiḥ ||8||

9vyavasāyaṃ tayostatra viditvā tridaśeśvaraḥ
|
saha devairupayayau lokapālaistathaiva ca ||9||

10sādhyāśca viśve maruto vādyāni sumahānti ca
|
nadyaḥ śailāḥ samudrāśca tīrthāni vividhāni ca ||10||

11tapāṃsi saṃyogavidhirvedāḥ stobhāḥ sarasvatī
|
nāradaḥ parvataścaiva viśvāvasurhahāhuhūḥ ||11||

12gandharvaścitrasenaśca parivāragaṇairyutaḥ
|
nāgāḥ siddhāśca munayo devadevaḥ prajāpatiḥ ||12||

13viṣṇuḥ sahasraśīrṣaśca devo'cintyaḥ samāgamat
|
avādyantāntarikṣe ca bheryastūryāṇi vā vibho ||13||

14puṣpavarṣāṇi divyāni tatra teṣāṃ mahātmanām
|
nanṛtuścāpsaraḥsaṃghāstatra tatra samantataḥ ||14||

15atha svargastathā rūpī brāhmaṇaṃ vākyamabravīt
|
saṃsiddhastvaṃ mahābhāgastvaṃ ca siddhastathā nṛpa ||15||

16atha tau sahitau rājannanyonyavidhinā tataḥ
|
viṣayapratisaṃhāramubhāveva pracakratuḥ ||16||

17prāṇāpānau tathodānaṃ samānaṃ vyānameva ca
|
evaṃ tau manasi sthāpya dadhatuḥ prāṇayormanaḥ ||17||

18upasthitakṛtau tau ca nāsikāgramadho bhruvoḥ
|
bhṛkuṭyā caiva manasā śanairdhārayatastadā ||18||

19niśceṣṭābhyāṃ śarīrābhyāṃ sthiradṛṣṭī samāhitau
|
jitātmānau tathā''dhāya mūrdhanyātmānameva ca ||19||

20tāludeśamathoddālya brāhmaṇasya mahātmanaḥ
|
jyotirjvālā sumahatī jagāma tridivaṃ tadā ||20||

21hāhākārastathā dikṣu sarveṣāṃ sumahānabhūt
|
tajjyotiḥ stūyamānaṃ sma brāhmaṇaṃ prāviśattadā ||21||

22tataḥ svāgatamityāha tattejaḥ prapitāmahaḥ
|
prādeśamātraṃ puruṣaṃ pratyudgamya viśāmpate ||22||

23bhūyaścaivāparaṃ prāha vacanaṃ madhura tadā
|
jāpakaistulyaphalatā yogānāṃ nātra saṃśayaḥ ||23||

24yogasya tāvadetebhyaḥ pratyakṣaṃ phaladarśanam
|
jāpakānāṃ viśiṣṭaṃ tu pratyutthānaṃ samāhitam ||24||

25uṣyatāṃ mayi cetyuktvā cetayatsatataṃ punaḥ
|
athāsyaṃ praviveśāsya brāhmaṇo vigatajvaraḥ ||25||

26rājāpyetena vidhinā bhagavantaṃ pitāmaham
|
yathaiva dvijaśārdūlastathaiva prāviśattadā ||26||

27svayambhuvamatho devā abhivādya tato'bruvan
|
jāpakānāṃ viśiṣṭaṃ tu pratyutthānaṃ samāhitam ||27||

28jāpakārthamayaṃ yatno yadarthaṃ vayamāgatāḥ
|
kṛtapūjāvimau tulyau tvayā tulyaphalāvimau ||28||

29yogajāpakayodṛṣṭaṃ phalaṃ sumahadadya vai
|
sarvā~llokānatikramya gacchetāṃ yatra vāñchitam ||29||

30brahmovāca|
mahāsmṛtiṃ paṭhedyastu tathaivānusmṛtiṃ śubhām
|
tāvapyetena vidhinā gacchetāṃ matsalokatām ||30||

31yaśca yoge bhavedbhaktaḥ so'pi nāstyatra saṃśayaḥ
|
vidhinānena dehānte mama lokānavāpnuyāt
|
sādhaye gamyatāṃ caiva yathāsthānāni siddhaye ||31||

32bhīṣma uvāca|
ityuktvā sa tadā devastatraivāntaradhīyata
|
āmantrya ca tato devā yayuḥ svaṃ svaṃ niveśanam ||32||

33te ca sarve mahātmāno dharmaṃ satkṛtya tatra vai
|
pṛṣṭhato'nuyayū rājansarve suprītacetasaḥ ||33||

34etatphalaṃ jāpakānāṃ gatiścaiṣā prakīrtitā
|
yathāśrutaṃ mahārāja kiṃ bhūyaḥ śrotumicchasi ||34||
