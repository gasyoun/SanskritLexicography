Mahābhārata 12.287 (Śāntiparvan)

1yudhiṣṭhira uvāca|
atattvajñasya śāstrāṇāṃ satataṃ saṃśayātmanaḥ
|
akṛtavyavasāyasya śreyo brūhi pitāmaha ||1||

2bhīṣma uvāca|
gurupūjā ca satataṃ vṛddhānāṃ paryupāsanam
|
śravaṇaṃ caiva śāstrāṇāṃ kūṭasthaṃ śreya ucyate ||2||

3atrāpyudāharantīmamitihāsaṃ purātanam
|
gālavasya ca saṃvādaṃ devarṣernāradasya ca ||3||

4vītamohaklamaṃ vipraṃ jñānatṛptaṃ jitendriyaḥ
|
śreyaskāmo yatātmānaṃ nāradaṃ gālavo'bravīt ||4||

5yaiḥ kaścitsammato loke guṇaiśca puruṣo mune
|
bhavatyanapagānsarvāṃstānguṇān lakṣayāmahe ||5||

6bhavānevaṃvidho'smākaṃ saṃśayaṃ chettumarhati
|
amūḍhaściramūḍhānāṃ lokatattvamajānatām ||6||

7jñāne hyevaṃ pravṛttiḥ syātkāryāṇāmaviśeṣataḥ
|
yatkāryaṃ na vyavasyāmastadbhavānvaktumarhati ||7||

8bhagavannāśramāḥ sarve pṛthagācāradarśinaḥ
|
idaṃ śreya idaṃ śreya iti sarve prabodhitāḥ ||8||

9tāṃstu viprasthitān dṛṣṭvā śāstraiḥ śāstrābhinandinaḥ
|
svaśāstraiḥ parituṣṭāśca śreyo nopalabhāmahe ||9||

10śāstraṃ yadi bhavedekaṃ śreyo vyaktaṃ bhavettadā
|
śāstraiśca bahubhirbhūyaḥ śreyo guhyaṃ praveśitam ||10||

11etasmātkāraṇācchreyaḥ kalilaṃ pratibhāti me
|
bravītu bhagavāṃstanme upasanno'smyadhīhi bhoḥ ||11||

12nārada uvāca|
āśramāstāta catvāro yathāsaṃkalpitāḥ pṛthak
|
tānsarvānanupaśya tvaṃ samāśrityeti gālava ||12||

13teṣāṃ teṣāṃ tathā hi tvamāśramāṇāṃ tatastataḥ
|
nānārūpaguṇoddeśaṃ paśya viprasthitaṃ pṛthak ||13||

14na yānti caiva te samyagabhipretamasaṃśayam
|
anye paśyaṃstathā samyagāśramāṇāṃ parāṃ gatim ||14||

15yattu niḥśreyasaṃ samyak taccaivāsaṃśayātmakam
|
anugrahaṃ ca mitrāṇāmamitrāṇāṃ ca nigraham ||15||

16saṃgrahaṃ ca trivargasya śreya āhurmanīṣiṇaḥ
|
nivṛttiḥ karmaṇaḥ pāpātsatataṃ puṇyaśīlatā ||16||

17sadbhiśca samudācāraḥ śreya etadasaṃśayam
|
mārdavaṃ sarvabhūteṣu vyavahāreṣu cārjavam ||17||

18vāk caiva madhurā proktā śreya etadasaṃśayam
|
daivatebhyaḥ pitṛbhyaśca saṃvibhāgo'tithiṣvapi ||18||

19asaṃtyāgaśca bhṛtyānāṃ śreya etadasaṃśayam
|
satyasya vacanaṃ śreyaḥ satyajñānaṃ tu duṣkaram ||19||

20yadbhūtahitamatyantametatsatyaṃ bravīmyaham
|
ahaṃkārasya ca tyāgaḥ pramādasya ca nigrahaḥ ||20||

21saṃtoṣaścaikacaryā ca kūṭasthaṃ śreya ucyate
|
dharmeṇa vedādhyayanaṃ vedāntānāṃ tathaiva ca ||21||

22jñānārthānāṃ ca jijñāsā śreya etadasaṃśayam
|
śabda-rūpa-rasa-sparśānsaha gandhena kevalān ||22||

23nātyarthamupaseveta śreyaso'rthī kathañcana ||23||

24naktaṃcaryāṃ divāsvapnamālasyaṃ paiśunaṃ madam
|
atiyogamayogaṃ ca śreyaso'rthī parityajet ||24||

25ātmotkarṣaṃ na mārgeta pareṣāṃ parinindayā
|
svaguṇaireva mārgeta viprakarṣaṃ pṛthagjanāt ||25||

26nirguṇāstveva bhūyiṣṭhamātmasambhāvitā narāḥ
|
doṣairanyānguṇavataḥ kṣipantyātmaguṇakṣayāt ||26||

27anūcyamānāstu punaste manyantu mahājanāt
|
guṇavattaramātmānaṃ svena mānena darpitāḥ ||27||

28abruvankasyacinnindāmātmapūjāmavarṇayan
|
vipaścidguṇasampannaḥ prāpnotyeva mahadyaśaḥ ||28||

29abruvanvā'tisurabhirgandhaḥ sumanasāṃ śuciḥ
|
tathaivāvyāharanbhāti vimalo bhānurambare ||29||

30evamādīni cānyāni parityaktāni medhayā
|
jvalanti yaśasā loke yāni na vyāharanti ca ||30||

31na loke dīpyate mūrkhaḥ kevalātmapraśaṃsayā
|
api cāpihitaḥ śvabhre kṛtavidyaḥ prakāśate ||31||

32asaduccairapi proktaḥ śabdaḥ samupaśāmyati
|
dīpyate tveva lokeṣu śanairapi subhāṣitam ||32||

33mūḍhānāmavaliptānāmasāraṃ bhāṣitaṃ bahu
|
darśayatyantarātmānamagnirūpamivāṃśumān ||33||

34etasmātkāraṇātprajñāṃ mṛgayante pṛthagvidhām
|
prajñālābho hi bhūtānāmuttamaḥ pratibhāti me ||34||

35nāpṛṣṭaḥ kasyacidbrūyānnāpyanyāyena pṛcchataḥ
|
jñānavānapi medhāvī jaḍavatsamupāviśet ||35||

36tato vā saṃparīkṣeta dharmanityeṣu sādhuṣu
|
manuṣyeṣu vadānyeṣu svadharmanirateṣu ca ||36||

37caturṇāṃ yatra varṇānāṃ dharmavyatikaro bhavet
|
na tatra vāsaṃ kurvīta śreyorthī vai kathaṃcana ||37||

38nirārambho'pyayamiha yathālabdhopajīvanaḥ
|
puṇyaṃ puṇyeṣu vimalaṃ pāpaṃ pāpeṣu cāpnuyāt ||38||

39apāmagnestathendośca sparśaṃ vedayate yathā
|
tathā paśyāmahe sparśamubhayoḥ puṇyapāpayoḥ ||39||

40apaśyanto'nuviṣayaṃ bhuñjate vighasāśinaḥ
|
bhujānāścātmaviṣayānviṣayānviddhi karmaṇām ||40||

41yatrāgamayamānānāmasatkāreṇa pṛcchatām
|
prabrūyādbrahmaṇo dharmaṃ tyajettaṃ deśamātmavān ||41||

42śiṣyopādhyāyikāvṛttiryatra syātsusamāhitā
|
yathāvacchāstrasampannā kastaṃ deśaṃ parityajet ||42||

43ākāśasthā dhruvaṃ yatra doṣaṃ brūyurvipaścitām
|
ātmapūjābhikāmo vai ko vasettatra paṇḍitaḥ ||43||

44yatra saṃloḍitā lubdhaiḥ prāyaśo dharmasetavaḥ
|
pradīptamiva cailāntaṃ kastaṃ deśaṃ na santyajet ||44||

45yatra dharmamanāśaṅkāścareyurvītamatsarāḥ
|
bhavettatra vaseccaiva puṇyaśīleṣu sādhuṣu ||45||

46dharmamarthanimittaṃ ca careyuryatra mānavāḥ
|
na tānanuvasejjātu te hi pāpakṛto janāḥ ||46||

47karmaṇā yatra pāpena vartante jīvitepsavaḥ
|
vyavadhāvettatastūrṇaṃ sasarpāccharaṇādiva ||47||

48yena khaṭvāṃ samārūḍhaḥ karmaṇā'nuśayī bhavet
|
āditastanna kartavyamicchatā bhavamātmanaḥ ||48||

49yatra rājā ca rājñaśca puruṣāḥ pratyanantarāḥ
|
kuṭumbināmagrabhujastyajettadrāṣṭramātmavān ||49||

50śrotriyāstvagrabhoktāro dharmanityāḥ sanātanāḥ
|
yājanādhyāpane yuktā yatra tadrāṣṭramāvaset ||50||

51svāhā svadhā vaṣaṭkārā yatra samyaganuṣṭhitāḥ
|
ajasraṃ caiva vartante vasettatrāvicārayan ||51||

52aśucīnyatra paśyeta brāhmaṇānvṛttikarśitān
|
tyajettadrāṣṭramāsannamupasṛṣṭamivāmiṣam ||52||

53prīyamāṇā narā yatra prayaccheyurayācitāḥ
|
svasthacitto vasettatra kṛtakṛtya ivātmavān ||53||

54daṇḍo yatrāvinīteṣu satkāraśca kṛtātmasu
|
carettatra vasecceva puṇyaśīleṣu sādhuṣu ||54||

55upasṛṣṭeṣu dānteṣu durācāreṣu sādhuṣu
|
avinīteṣu lubdheṣu sumahaddaṇḍadhāraṇam ||55||

56yatra rājā dharmanityo rājyaṃ dharmeṇa pālayet
|
apāsya kāmānkāmeśo vasettatrāvicārayan ||56||

57yathā śīlā hi rājānaḥ sarvānviṣayavāsinaḥ
|
śreyasā yojayatyāśu śreyasi pratyupasthite ||57||

58pṛcchataste mayā tāta śreya etadudāhṛtam
|
na hi śakyaṃ pradhānena śreyaḥ saṃkhyātumātmanaḥ ||58||

59evaṃ pravartamānasya vṛttiṃ prāṇihitātmanaḥ
|
tapasaiveha bahulaṃ śreyo vyaktaṃ bhaviṣyati ||59||
