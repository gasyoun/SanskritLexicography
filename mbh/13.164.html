Mahābhārata 13.164 (Anuśāsanaparvan)

1bhīṣma uvāca|
kāryate yacca kriyate saccāsacca kṛtākṛtam
|
tatrāśvasīta satkṛtvā asatkṛtvā na viśvaset ||1||

2kāla eva sarvakāle nigrahānugrahau dadat
|
buddhimāviśya bhūtānāṃ dharmādharmau pravartate ||2||

3yadā tvasya bhavedbuddhirdharmārthasya pradarśanāt
|
tadāśvasīta dharmātmā dṛḍhabuddhirna viśvaset ||3||

4etāvanmātrametaddhi bhūtānāṃ prājñalakṣaṇam
|
kālayuktopyubhayaviccheṣaṃ yuktaṃ samācaret ||4||

5yathā hyupasthitaiśvaryāḥ prajāyante na rājasāḥ
|
evamevātmanātmānaṃ pūjayantīha dhārmikāḥ ||5||

6na hyadharmatayā'dharmaṃ dadyātkālaḥ kathañcana
|
tasmādviśuddhamātmānaṃ jānīyāddharmacāriṇam ||6||

7spraṣṭumapyasamartho hi jvalantamiva pāvakam
|
adharmaḥ saṃtato dharmaṃ kālena parirakṣitam ||7||

8kāryāvetau hi dharmeṇa dharmo hi vijayāvahaḥ
|
trayāṇāmapi lokānāmālokaḥ kāraṇaṃ bhavet ||8||

9na tu kaścinnayetprājño gṛhītvaiva kare naram
|
ucyamānastu dharmeṇa dharmalokabhayacchale ||9||

10śūdro'haṃ nādhikāro me cāturāśramyasevane
|
iti vijñānamapare nātmanyupadadhatyuta ||10||

11viśeṣeṇa ca vakṣyāmi cāturvarṇyasya liṅgataḥ
|
pañcabhūtaśarīrāṇāṃ sarveṣāṃ sadṛśātmanām ||11||

12lokadharme ca dharme ca viśeṣakaraṇaṃ kṛtam
|
yathaikatvaṃ punaryānti prāṇinastatra vistaraḥ ||12||

13adhruvo hi kathaṃ lokaḥ smṛto dharmaḥ kathaṃ dhruvaḥ
|
yatra kālo dhruvastāta tatra dharmaḥ sanātanaḥ ||13||

14sarveṣāṃ tulyadehānāṃ sarveṣāṃ sadṛśātmanām
|
kālo dharmeṇa saṃyuktaḥ śeṣa eva svayaṃ guruḥ ||14||

15evaṃ sati na doṣo'sti bhūtānāṃ dharmasevane
|
tiryagyonāvapi satāṃ loka eva mato guruḥ ||15||
