Mahābhārata 1.71 (Ādiparvan)

1vaiśaṃpāyana uvāca|
tato gacchanmahābāhureko'mātyānvisṛjya tān
|
nāpaśyaccāśrame tasmiṃstamṛṣiṃ saṃśitavratam||1||

2so'paśyamānastamṛṣiṃ śūnyaṃ dṛṣṭvā tathā''śramam
|
uvāca ka ihetyuccairvanaṃ sannādayanniva||2||

3śrutvā'tha tasya taṃ śabdaṃ kanyā śrīriva rūpiṇī
|
niścakrāmāśramāttasmāttāpasīveṣadhāriṇī||3||

4sā taṃ dṛṣṭvaiva rājānaṃ duṣyantamasitekṣaṇā
|
svāgataṃ ta iti kṣipramuvāca pratipūjya ca||4||

5āsanenārcayitvā ca pādyenārghyeṇa caiva hi
|
papracchānāmayaṃ rājankuśalaṃ ca narādhipam||5||

6yathāvadarcayitvā'tha pṛṣṭvā cānāmayaṃ tadā
|
uvāca smayamāneva kiṃ kāryaṃ kriyatāmiti||6||

7tāmabravīttato rājā kanyāṃ madhurabhāṣiṇīm
|
dṛṣṭvā sarvānavadyāṅgīṃ yathāvatpratipūjitaḥ||7||

8āgato'haṃ mahābhāgamṛṣiṃ kaṇvamupāsitum
|
kva gato bhagavānbhadre ganmamācakṣva śobhane||8||

9śakuntalovāca
|
gataḥ pitā me bhagavānphalānyāhartumāśramāt
|
muhūrtaṃ saṃpratīkṣasva draṣṭāsyenamupāgatam||9||

10vaiśaṃpāyana uvāca|
apaśyamānastamṛṣiṃ tathā coktastayā ca saḥ
|
tāṃ dṛṣṭvā ca varārohāṃ śrīmatīṃ cāruhāsinīm||10||

11vibhrājamānāṃ vapuṣā tapasā ca damena ca
|
rūpayauvanasaṃpannāmityuvāca mahīpatiḥ||11||

12kā tvaṃ kasyāsi suśroṇi kimarthaṃ cāgatā vanam
|
evaṃrūpaguṇopetā kutastvamasi śobhane||12||

13darśanādeva hi śubhe tvayā me'pahṛtaṃ manaḥ
|
icchāmi tvāmahaṃ jñātuṃ tanmamācakṣva śobhane||13||

14evamuktā tu sā kanyā tena rājñā tamāśrame
|
uvāca hasatī vākyamidaṃ sumadhurākṣaram||14||

15kaṇvasyāhaṃ bhagavato duṣyanta duhitā matā
|
tapasvino dhṛtimato dharmajñasya mahātmanaḥ||15||

16duṣyanta uvāca|
ūrdhvaretā mahābhāge bhagavā~llokapūjitaḥ
|
caleddhi vṛttāddharmopi na caletsaṃśitavrataḥ||16||

17kathaṃ tvaṃ tasya duhitā saṃbhūtā varavarṇinī
|
saṃśayo me mahānatra tanme chettumihārhasi||17||

18śakuntalovāca
|
yathā'yamāgamo mahyaṃ yathā cedamabhūtpurā
|
śṛṇu rājanyathātattvaṃ yathā'smi duhitā muneḥ||18||

19ṛṣiḥ kaścidihāgamya mama janmābhyacodayat
|
tasmai provāca bhagavānyathā tacchṛṇu pārthivā||19||

20kaṇva uvāca|
tapyamānaḥ kila purā viśvāmitro mahattapaḥ
|
subhṛśaṃ tāpayāmāsa śakraṃ suragaṇeśvaram||20||

21tapasā dīptavīryo'yaṃ sthānānmāṃ cyāvayediti
|
bhītaḥ purandarastasmānmenakāmidamabravīt||21||

22guṇairapsarasāṃ divyairmenake tvaṃ viśiṣyase
|
śreyo me kuru kalyāṇi yattvāṃ vakṣyāmi tacchṛṇu||22||

23asāvādityaśaṅkāśo viśvāmitro mahātapāḥ
|
tapyamānastapo ghoraṃ mama kampayate manaḥ||23||

24menake tava bhāro'yaṃ viśvāmitraḥ sumadhyame
|
śaṃsitātmā sudurdharṣa ugre tapasi vartate||24||

25sa māṃ na cyāvayetsthānāttaṃ vai gatvā pralobhaya
|
cara tasya tapovighnaṃ kuru me'vighnamuttamam||25||

26rūpayauvanamādhuryaceṣṭitasmitabhāṣaṇaiḥ
|
lobhayitvā varārohe tapasastaṃ nivartaya||26||

27menakovāca
|
mahātejāḥ sa bhagavāṃstathaiva ca mahātapāḥ
|
kopanaśca tathā hyenaṃ jānāti bhagavānapi||27||

28tejastapasaścaiva kopasya ca mahātmanaḥ
|
tvamapyudvijase yasya nodvijeyamahaṃ katham||28||

29mahābhāgaṃ vasiṣṭhaṃ yaḥ putrairiṣṭairvyayojayat
|
kṣatrajātaśca yaḥ pūrvamabhavadbrāhmaṇo balāt||29||

30śaucārthaṃ yo nadīṃ cakre durgamāṃ bahubhirjalaiḥ
|
yāṃ tāṃ puṇyatamāṃ loke kauśikīti vidurjanāḥ||30||

31babhāra yatrāsya purā kāle durge mahātmanaḥ
|
dārānmataṅgo dharmātmā rājarṣirvyādhatāṃ gataḥ||31||

32atītakāle durbhikṣe abhyetya punarākṣamam
|
muniḥ pāreti nadyā vai nāma cakre tadā prabhuḥ||32||

33mataṅgaṃ yājayāñcakre yatra prītamanāḥ svayam
|
tvaṃ ca somaṃ bhayādyasya gataḥ pātuṃ sureśvara||33||

34cakārānyaṃ ca lokaṃ vai kruddho nakṣatrasaṃpadā
|
pratiśravaṇapūrvāṇi nakṣatrāṇi cakāra yaḥ
|
guruśāpahatasyāpi triśaṅkoḥ śaraṇaṃ dadau||34||

35etāni yasya karmāṇi tasyāhaṃ bhṛśamudvije
|
yathā'sau na dahetkruddhastathā''jñāpaya māṃ vibho||35||

36tejasā nirdahellokānkampayeddharaṇīṃ padā
|
saṃkṣipecca mahāmeruṃ tūrṇamāvartayeddiśaḥ||36||

37tādṛśaṃ tapasā yuktaṃ pradīptamiva pāvakam
|
kathamasmadvidhā nārī jitendriyamabhispṛśet||37||

38hutāśanamukhaṃ dīptaṃ sūryacandrākṣitārakam
|
kālajihvaṃ suraśreṣṭha kathamasmadvidhā spṛśet||38||

39yamaśca somaśca maharṣayaśca
sādhyā viśve vālasvilyāśca sarve
|
ete'pi yasyodvijante prabhāvā-
ttasmātkasmānmādṛśī nodvijeta||39||

40tvayaivamuktā ca kathaṃ samīpa-
mṛṣerna gaccheyamahaṃ surendra
|
rakṣāṃ tu me cintaya devarāja
yathā tvadarthaṃ rakṣitā'haṃ careyam||40||

41kāmaṃ tu me mārutastatra vāsaḥ
prakrīḍitāyā vivṛṇotu deva
|
bhavecca me mathastatra kārye
sahāyabhūtastu tava prasādāt||41||

42vanācca vāyuḥ surabhiḥ pravāyāttasminkāle tamṛṣiṃ lobhayantyāḥ
|
tathetyuktvā vihite caiva tasmiṃstato yayau sāśramaṃ kauśikasya||42||
