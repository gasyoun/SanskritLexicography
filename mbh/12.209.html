Mahābhārata 12.209 (Śāntiparvan)

1yudhiṣṭhira uvāca|
pitāmaha mahāprājña yudhi satyaparākrama
|
śrotumicchāmi kārtsnyena kṛṣṇamavyayamīśvaram ||1||

2yaccāsya tejaḥ sumahadyacca karma purā kṛtam
|
tanme sarvaṃ yathātattvaṃ brūhi tvaṃ puruṣarṣabha ||2||

3tiryagyonigato rūpaṃ kathaṃ dhāritavānprabhuḥ
|
kena kāryanisargeṇa tamākhyāhi mahābala ||3||

4bhīṣma uvāca|
purāhaṃ mṛgayāṃ yāto mārkaṇḍeyāśrame sthitaḥ
|
tatrāpaśyaṃ munigaṇān samāsīnānsahasraśaḥ ||4||

5tataste madhuparkeṇa pūjāṃ cakruratho mayi
|
pratigṛhya ca tāṃ pūjāṃ pratyanandamṛṣīnaham ||5||

6kathaiṣā kathitā tatra kaśyapena maharṣiṇā
|
manaḥprahlādinīṃ divyāṃ tāmihaikamanāḥ śṛṇu ||6||

7purā dānavamukhyā hi krodhalobhasamanvitāḥ
|
balena mattā śataśo narakādyā mahāsurāḥ ||7||

8tathaiva cānye bahavo dānavā yuddhadurmadāḥ
|
na sahante sma devānāṃ samṛddhiṃ tāmanuttamām ||8||

9dānavairardyamānāstu devā devarṣayastathā
|
na śarma lebhire rājan viśamānāstatastataḥ ||9||

10pṛthivīmārtarūpāṃ te samapaśyan divaukasaḥ
|
dānavairabhisaṃstīrṇāṃ ghorarūpairmahābalaiḥ ||10||

11bhārārtāmaprahṛṣṭāṃ ca duḥkhitāṃ sannimajjatīm
|
athāditeyāḥ saṃtrastā brahmāṇamidamabruvan ||11||

12kathaṃ śakṣyāmahe brahmandānavairabhimardanam
|
svayaṃbhūstānuvācedaṃ nisṛṣṭo'tra vidhirmayā ||12||

13te vareṇābhisampannā balena ca madena ca
|
nāvabudhyanti sammūḍhā viṣṇumavyaktadarśanam ||13||

14varāharūpiṇaṃ devamadhṛṣyamamarairapi
|
eṣa vegena gatvā hi yatra te dānavādhamāḥ ||14||

15antarbhūmigatā ghorā nivasanti sahasraśaḥ
|
śamayiṣyati tacchrutvā jahṛṣuḥ surasattamāḥ ||15||

16tato viṣṇurmahātejā vārāhaṃ rūpamāsthitaḥ
|
antarbhūmiṃ sampraviśya jagāma ditijānprati ||16||

17dṛṣṭvā ca sahitāḥ sarve daityāḥ sattvamamānuṣam
|
prasahya tarasā sarve saṃtasthuḥ kālamohitāḥ ||17||

18tataste samabhidrutya varāhaṃ jagṛhuḥ samam
|
saṃkruddhāśca varāhaṃ taṃ vyakarṣanta samantataḥ ||18||

19dānavendrā mahākāyā mahāvīryabalocchritāḥ
|
nāśaknuvaṃśca kiṃcitte tasya kartuṃ tadā vibho ||19||

20tato'gacchanvismayaṃ te dānavendrā bhayaṃ tathā
|
saṃśayaṃ gatamātmānaṃ menire ca sahasraśaḥ ||20||

21tato devādhidevaḥ sa yogātmā yogasārithaḥ
|
yogamāsthāya bhagavāṃstadā bharatasattama ||21||

22vinanāda mahānādaṃ kṣobhayan daityadānavān
|
sannāditā yena lokāḥ sarvāścaiva diśo daśa ||22||

23tena sannādaśabdena lokānāṃ kṣobha āgamat
|
saṃtrastāśca bhṛśaṃ loke devāḥ śakrapurogamāḥ ||23||

24nirviceṣṭaṃ jagaccāpi babhūvātibhṛśaṃ tadā
|
sthāvaraṃ jaṅgamaṃ caiva tena nādena mohitam ||24||

25tataste dānavāḥ sarve tena nādena bhīṣitāḥ
|
peturgatāsavaścaiva viṣṇutejaḥpramohitāḥ ||25||

26rasātalagataścāpi varāhastridaśadviṣām
|
khurairvidārayāmāsa māṃsamedo'sthisaṃcayān ||26||

27nādena tena mahatā sanātana iti smṛtaḥ
|
padmanābho mahāyogī bhūtācāryaḥ sa bhūtarāṭ ||27||

28tato devagaṇāḥ sarve pitāmahamupādravan
|
tatra gatvā mahātmānamūcuścaiva jagatpatim ||28||

29nādo'yaṃ kīdṛśo deva naitaṃ vidma vayaṃ prabho
|
ko'sau hi kasya vā nādo yena vihvalitaṃ jagat ||29||

30devāśca dānavāścaiva mohitāstasya tejasā
|
etasminnantare viṣṇurvārāhaṃ rūpamāsthitaḥ
|
udatiṣṭhanmahābāho stūyamāno maharṣibhiḥ ||30||

31pitāmaha uvāca|
nihatya dānavapatīn mahāvarṣmā mahābalaḥ
|
eṣa devo mahāyogī bhūtātmā bhūtabhāvanaḥ ||31||

32sarvabhūteśvaro yogī munirātmā tathā''tmanaḥ
|
sthirībhavata kṛṣṇo'yaṃ sarvavighnavināśanaḥ ||32||

33kṛtvā karmātisādhvetadaśakyamamitaprabhaḥ
|
samāyātaḥ svamātmānaṃ mahābhāgo mahādyutiḥ ||33||

34padmanābho mahāyogī mahātmā bhūtabhāvanaḥ
|
na santāpo na bhīḥ kāryā śoko vā surasattamāḥ ||34||

35vidhireṣa prabhāvaśca kālaḥ saṃkṣayakārakaḥ
|
lokāndhārayatā tena nādo mukto mahātmanā ||35||

36sa eṣa hi mahābāhuḥ sarvalokanamaskṛtaḥ
|
acyutaḥ puṇḍarīkākṣaḥ sarvabhūtādirīśvaraḥ ||36||
