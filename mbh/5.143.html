Mahābhārata 5.143 (Udyogaparvan)

1sañjaya uvāca|
keśavasya tu tadvākyaṃ karṇaḥ śrutvā''hitaḥ śubham
|
abravīdabhisaṃpūjya kṛṣṇaṃ taṃ madhusūdanam ||1||

2jānan māṃ kiṃ mahābāho saṃmohayitu‌micchasi
|
yo'yaṃ pṛthivyāḥ kārtsnyena vināśaḥ samupasthitaḥ ||2||

3nimittaṃ tatra śakunirahaṃ duḥśāsanastathā
|
duryodhanaśca nṛpatirdhṛtarāṣṭrasuto'bhavat ||3||

4asaṃśayamidaṃ kṛṣṇa mahadyuddhamupasthitam
|
pāṇḍavānāṃ kurūṇāṃ ca ghoraṃ rudhirakardamam ||4||

5rājāno rājaputrāśca duryodhanavaśānugāḥ
|
raṇe śastrāgninā dagdhāḥ prāpsyanti yamasādanam ||5||

6svapnā hi bahavo ghorā dṛśyante madhusūdana
|
nimittāni ca ghorāṇi tathotpātāḥ sudāruṇāḥ ||6||

7parājayaṃ dhārtarāṣṭre vijayaṃ ca yudhiṣṭhire
|
śaṃsanta iva vārṣṇeya vividhā romaharṣaṇāḥ ||7||

8prājāpatyaṃ hi nakṣatraṃ grahastīkṣṇo mahādyutiḥ
|
śanaiścaraḥ pīḍayati pīḍayan prāṇino'dhikam ||8||

9kṛtvā cāṅgārako vakraṃ jyeṣṭhāyāṃ madhusūdana
|
anurādhāṃ prārthayate maitraṃ saṅgamayanniva ||9||

10nūnaṃ mahadbhayaṃ kṛṣṇa kurūṇāṃ samupasthitam
|
viśeṣeṇa hi vārṣṇeya citrāṃ pīḍayate grahaḥ ||10||

11somasya lakṣma vyāvṛttaṃ rāhurarkamupaiti ca
|
divaścolkāḥ patantyetāḥ sanirghātāḥ sakampanāḥ ||11||

12niṣṭananti ca mātaṅgā muñcantyaśrūṇi vājinaḥ
|
pānīyaṃ yavasaṃ cāpi nābhinandanti mādhava ||12||

13prādurbhūteṣu caiteṣu bhayamāhurupasthitam
|
nimitteṣu mahābāho dāruṇaṃ prāṇināśanam ||13||

14alpe bhukte purīṣaṃ ca prabhūtamiha dṛśyate
|
vājināṃ vāraṇānāṃ ca manuṣyāṇāṃ ca keśava ||14||

15dhārtarāṣṭrasya sainyeṣu sarveṣu madhusūdana
|
parābhavasya talliṅgamiti prāhurmanīṣiṇaḥ ||15||

16prahṛṣṭaṃ vāhanaṃ kṛṣṇa pāṇḍavānāṃ pracakṣate
|
pradakṣiṇā mṛgāścaiva tatteṣāṃ jayalakṣaṇam ||16||

17apasavyā mṛgāḥ sarve dhārtarāṣṭrasya keśava
|
vācaścāpyaśarīriṇyastat parābhavalakṣaṇam ||17||

18mayūrāḥ puṇyaśakunā haṃsa-sārasa-cātakāḥ
|
jīvañjīvakasaṅghāścāpyanugacchanti pāṇḍavān ||18||

19gṛdhrāḥ kaṅkā bakāḥ śyenā yātudhānāstathā vṛkāḥ
|
makṣikāṇāṃ ca saṅghātā anudhāvanti kauravān ||19||

20dhārtarāṣṭrasya sainyeṣu bherīṇāṃ nāsti niḥsvanaḥ
|
anāhatāḥ pāṇḍavānāṃ nadanti paṭahāḥ kila ||20||

21udapānāśca nardanti yathā govṛṣabhāstathā
|
dhārtarāṣṭrasya sainyeṣu tat parābhavalakṣaṇam ||21||

22māṃsaśoṇitavarṣaṃ ca vṛṣṭaṃ devena mādhava
|
tathā gandharvanagaraṃ bhānumatsamupasthitam ||22||

23saprākāraṃ saparikhaṃ savapraṃ cārutoraṇam
|
kṛṣṇaśca parighastatra bhānumāvṛtya tiṣṭhati ||23||

24udayāstamane sandhye vedayantī mahadbhayam
|
śivā ca vāśate ghoraṃ tat parābhavalakṣaṇam ||24||

25ekapakṣākṣicaraṇāḥ pakṣiṇo madhusūdana
|
utsṛjanti mahadghoraṃ tat parābhavalakṣaṇam ||25||

26kṛṣṇagrīvāśca śakunā raktapādā bhayānakāḥ
|
sandhyāmabhimukhā yānti tat parābhavalakṣaṇam ||26||

27brāhmaṇān prathamaṃ dveṣṭi gurūṃśca madhusūdana
|
bhṛtyān bhaktimataścāpi tat parābhavalakṣaṇam ||27||

28pūrvā diglohitākārā śastravarṇā ca dakṣiṇā
|
āmapātrapratīkāśā paścimā madhusūdana
|
uttarā śaṅkhavarṇābhā diśāṃ varṇā udāhṛtāḥ ||28||

29pradīptāśca diśaḥ sarvā dhārtarāṣṭrasya mādhava
|
mahadbhayaṃ vedayanti tasminnutpātadarśane ||29||

30sahasrapādaṃ prāsādaṃ svapnānte sma yudhiṣṭhiraḥ
|
adhirohanmayā dṛṣṭaḥ saha bhrātṛbhiracyuta ||30||

31śvetoṣṇīṣāśca dṛśyante sarve vai śuklavāsasaḥ
|
āsanāni ca śubhrāṇi sarveṣāmupalakṣaye ||31||

32tava cāpi mayā kṛṣṇa svapnānte rudhirāvilā
|
antreṇa pṛthivī dṛṣṭā parikṣiptā janārdana ||32||

33asthisañcayamārūḍhaścāmitaujā yudhiṣṭhiraḥ
|
suvarṇapātryāṃ saṃhṛṣṭo bhuktavān ghṛtapāyasam ||33||

34yudhiṣṭhiro mayā dṛṣṭo grasamāno vasundharām
|
tvayā dattāmimāṃ vyaktaṃ bhokṣyate sa vasundharām ||34||

35uccaṃ parvatamārūḍho bhīmakarmā vṛkodaraḥ
|
gadāpāṇirnaravyāghro grasanniva mahīmimām ||35||

36kṣapayiṣyati naḥ sarvān sa suvyaktaṃ mahāraṇe
|
viditaṃ me hṛṣīkeśa yato dharmastato jayaḥ ||36||

37pāṇḍuraṃ gajamārūḍho gāṇḍīvī sa dhanañjayaḥ
|
tvayā sārdhaṃ hṛṣīkeśa śriyā paramayā jvalan ||37||

38yūyaṃ sarve vadhiṣyadhvaṃ tatra me nāsti saṃśayaḥ
|
pārthivān samare kṛṣṇa duryodhanapurogamān ||38||

39nakulaḥ sahadevaśca sātyakiśca mahārathaḥ
|
śuklakeyūrakaṇṭhatrāḥ śuklamālyāṃbarāvṛtāḥ ||39||

40adhirūḍhā naravyāghrā naravāhanamuttamam
|
traya ete mayā dṛṣṭāḥ pāṇḍuracchatravāsasaḥ ||40||

41śvetoṣṇīṣāśca dṛśyante traya ete janārdana
|
dhārtarāṣṭreṣu sainyeṣu tān vijānīhi keśava ||41||

42aśvatthāmā kṛpaścaiva kṛtavarmā ca sātvataḥ
|
raktoṣṇīṣāśca dṛśyante sarve mādhava pārthivāḥ||42||

43uṣṭraprayuktamārūḍhau bhīṣmadroṇau mahārathau
|
mayā sārdhaṃ mahābāho dhārtarāṣṭreṇa vā vibho||43||

44agastyaśāstāṃ ca diśaṃ prayātāḥ sma janārdana
|
acireṇaiva kālena prāpsyāmo yamasādanam ||44||

45ahaṃ cānye ca rājāno yacca tat kṣatramaṇḍalam
|
gāṇḍīvāgniṃ pravekṣyāma iti me nāsti saṃśayaḥ ||45||

46kṛṣṇa uvāca|
upasthitavināśeyaṃ nūnamadya vasundharā
|
yathā hi me vacaḥ karṇa nopaiti hṛdayaṃ tava ||46||

47sarveṣāṃ tāta bhūtānāṃ vināśe pratyupasthite
|
anayo nayasaṅkāśo hṛdayānnāpasarpati ||47||

48karṇa uvāca|
api tvāṃ kṛṣṇa paśyāma jīvanto'smānmahāraṇāt
|
samuttīrṇā mahābāho vīrakṣatravināśanāt ||48||

49athavā saṅgamaḥ kṛṣṇa svarge no bhavitā dhruvam
|
tatredānīṃ sameṣyāmaḥ punaḥ sārdhaṃ tvayā'nagha ||49||

50sañjaya uvāca|
ityuktvā mādhavaṃ karṇaḥ pariṣvajya ca pīḍitam
|
visarjitaḥ keśavena rathopasthādavātarat ||50||

51tataḥ svarathamāsthāya jāmbūnadavibhūṣitam
|
sahāsmābhirnivavṛte rādheyo dīnamānasaḥ ||51||

52tataḥ śīghrataraṃ prāyāt keśavaḥ sahasātyakiḥ
|
punaruccārayan vāṇīṃ yāhi yāhīti sārathim ||52||
