Mahābhārata 6.79 (Bhīṣmaparvan)

1sañjaya uvāca|
tato duryodhano rājā lohitāyati bhāskare
|
saṅgrāmarabhaso bhīmaṃ hantukāmo'bhyadhāvata ||1||

2tamāyāntamabhiprekṣya nṛvīraṃ dṛḍhavairiṇam
|
bhīmasenaḥ susaṅkruddha idaṃ vacanamabravīt ||2||

3ayaṃ sa kālaḥ saṃprāpto varṣapūgābhivāñchitaḥ
|
adya tvāṃ nihaniṣyāmi yadi notsṛjase raṇam ||3||

4adya kuntyāḥ parikleśaṃ vanavāsaṃ ca kṛtsnaśaḥ
|
draupadyāśca parikleśaṃ praṇeṣyāmi hate tvayi ||4||

5yatpurā matsarī bhūtvā pāṇḍavānavamanyase
|
tasya pāpasya gāndhāre pakṣya vyasanamāgatam ||5||

6karṇasya matamāsthāya saubalasya ca yatpurā
|
acintya pāṇḍavān kāmādyatheṣṭaṃ kṛtavānasi ||6||

7yācamānaṃ ca yanmohāddāśārhamavamanyase
|
ulūkasya samādeśaṃ yaddadāsi ca hṛṣṭavat ||7||

8tena tvāṃ nihaniṣyāmi sānubandhaṃ sabāndhavam
|
samīkariṣye tatpāpaṃ yatpurā kṛtavānasi ||8||

9evamuktvā dhanurghoraṃ vikṛṣyodbhrāmya cāsakṛt
|
samādhatta śarān ghorānmahāśanisamaprabhān ||9||

10ṣaḍviṃśatimasaṃkruddho mumocāśu suyodhane
|
jvalitāgniśikhākārān vajrakalpānajihmagān ||10||

11tato'sya kārmukaṃ dvābhyāṃ sūtaṃ dvābhyāṃ ca vivyadhe
|
caturbhiraśvān javanānanayadyamasādanam ||11||

12dvābhyāṃ ca suvikṛṣṭābhyāṃ śarābhyāmarimardanaḥ
|
chatraṃ ciccheda samare rājñastasya narottama ||12||

13ṣaḍbhiśca tasya ciccheda jvalantaṃ dhvajamuttamam
|
chitvā taṃ ca nanādoccaistava putrasya paśyataḥ ||13||

14rathācca sa dhvajaḥ śrīmānnānāratnavibhūṣitāt
|
papāta sahasā bhūmau vidyujjaladharādiva ||14||

15jvalantaṃ sūryasaṅkāśaṃ nāgaṃ maṇimayaṃ śubham
|
dhvajaṃ kurupateśchinnaṃ dadṛśuḥ sarvapārthivāḥ ||15||

16athenaṃ daśabhirbāṇaistotrairiva mahādvipam
|
ājaghāna raṇe vīraṃ smayanniva mahārathaḥ ||16||

17tataḥ sa rājā sindhūnāṃ rathaśreṣṭho mahārathaḥ
|
duryodhanasya jagrāha pārṣṇiṃ satpuruṣairvṛtaḥ ||17||

18kṛpaśca rathināṃ śreṣṭhaḥ kauravyamamitaujasam
|
āropayadrathaṃ rājan duryodhanamamarṣaṇam ||18||

19sa gāḍhaviddho vyathito bhīmasenena saṃyuge
|
niṣasāda rathopasthe rājan duryodhanastadā ||19||

20parivārya tato bhīmaṃ jetukāmo jayadrathaḥ
|
rathairanekasāhastrairbhīmasyāvārayaddiśaḥ ||20||

21dhṛṣṭaketustato rājannabhimanyuśca vīryavān
|
kekayā draupadeyāśca tava putrānayodhayan ||21||

22citrasenaḥ sucitraśca citrāṅgaścitradarśanaḥ
|
cārucitraḥ sucāruśca tathā nandopanandakau ||22||

23aṣṭāvete maheṣvāsāḥ sukumārā yaśasvinaḥ
|
abhimanyurathaṃ rājan samantāt paryavārayan ||23||

24ājaghāna tatastūrṇamabhimanyurmahāmanāḥ
|
ekaikaṃ pañcabhirbāṇaiḥ śitaiḥ sannataparvabhiḥ ||24||

25vajramṛtyupratīkāśairvicitrāyudhaniḥsṛtaiḥ
|
amṛṣyamāṇāste sarve saubhadraṃ rathasattamam ||25||

26vavṛṣurmārgaṇaistīkṣṇairgiriṃ merumivāmbudāḥ
|
sa pīḍyamānaḥ samare kṛtāstro yuddhadurmadaḥ ||26||

27abhimanyurmahārāja tāvakān samakampayat
|
yathā devāsure yuddhe vajrapāṇirmahāsurān ||27||

28vikarṇasya tato bhallān preṣayāmāsa bhārata
|
caturdaśa rathaśreṣṭho ghorānāśīviṣopamān ||28||

29sa tairvikarṇasya rathāt pātayāmāsa vīryavān
|
dhvajaṃ sūtaṃ hayāṃścaiva nṛtyamāna ivāhave ||29||

30punaścānyānśarānpītānakuṇṭhāgrān śilāśitān
|
preṣayāmāsa saṃkruddho vikarṇāya mahābalaḥ ||30||

31te vikarṇaṃ samāsādya kaṅkabarhiṇavāsasaḥ
|
bhittvā dehaṃ gatā bhūmiṃ jvalanta iva pannagāḥ ||31||

32te śarā hemapuṅkhāgrā vyadṛśyanta mahītale
|
vikarṇarudhiraklinnā vamanta iva śoṇitam ||32||

33vikarṇaṃ vīkṣya nirbhinnaṃ tasyaivānye sahodarāḥ
|
abhyadravanta samare saubhadrapramukhān rathān ||33||

34abhiyātvā tathaivānyān rathāṃstān sūryavarcasaḥ
|
avidhyan samare'nyonyaṃ saṃraṃbhādyuddhadurmadāḥ ||34||

35durmukhaḥ śrutakarmāṇaṃ vidhvā saptabhirāśugaiḥ
|
dhvajamekena ciccheda sārathiṃ cāsya saptabhiḥ ||35||

36aśvān jāṃbūnadairjālaiḥ pracchannān vātaraṃhasaḥ
|
jaghāna ṣaḍbhirāsādya sārathiṃ cābhyapātayat ||36||

37sa hatāśve rathe tiṣṭhan śrutakarmā mahārathaḥ
|
śaktiṃ cikṣepa saṃkruddho maholkāṃ jvalitāmiva ||37||

38sā durmukhasya vimalaṃ varma bhittvā yaśasvinaḥ
|
vidārya prāviśadbhūmiṃ dīpyamānā svatejasā ||38||

39taṃ dṛṣṭvā virathaṃ tatra sutasomo mahārathaḥ
|
paśyatāṃ sarvasainyānāṃ rathamāropayatsvakam ||39||

40śrutakīrtistathā vīro jayatsenaṃ sutaṃ tava
|
abhyayāt samare rājan hantukāmo yaśasvinam ||40||

41tasya vikṣipataścāpaṃ śrutakīrtermahāsvanam
|
ciccheda samare tūrṇaṃ jayatsenaḥ sutastava ||41||

42kṣurapreṇa sutīkṣṇena prahasanniva bhārata
|
taṃ dṛṣṭvā chinnadhanvānaṃ śatānīkaḥ sahodaram ||42||

43abhyapadyata tejasvī siṃhavanninadanmuhuḥ
|
śatānīkastu samare dṛḍhaṃ visphārya kārmukam ||43||

44vivyādha daśabhistūrṇaṃ jayatsenaṃ śilīmukhaiḥ
|
nanāda sumahānādaṃ prabhinna iva vāraṇaḥ ||44||

45athānyena sutīkṣṇena sarvāvaraṇabhedinā
|
śatānīko jayatsenaṃ vivyādha hṛdaye bhṛśam ||45||

46tathā tasmin vartamāne duṣkarṇo bhrāturantike
|
ciccheda samare cāpaṃ nākuleḥ krodhamūrcchitaḥ ||46||

47athānyaddhanurādāya bhārasāhamanuttamam
|
samādatta śarān ghorān śatānīko mahābalaḥ ||47||

48tiṣṭha tiṣṭheti cāmantrya duṣkarṇaṃ bhrāturagrataḥ
|
mumocāsmai śitānbāṇān jvalitānpannagāniva ||48||

49tato'sya dhanurekena dvābhyāṃ sūtaṃ ca māriṣa
|
ciccheda samare tūrṇaṃ taṃ ca vivyādha saptabhiḥ ||49||

50aśvān manojavāṃstasya karburān vātaraṃhasaḥ
|
jaghāna niśitaistūrṇaṃ sarvān dvāda‌śabhiḥ śaraiḥ ||50||

51athāpareṇa bhallena suyuktenāśupātinā
|
duṣkarṇaṃ sudṛḍhaṃ kruddho vivyādha hṛdaye bhṛśam ||51||

52sa papāta tato bhūmau vajrāhata iva drumaḥ
|
duṣkarṇaṃ vyathitaṃ dṛṣṭvā pañca rājanmahārathāḥ ||52||

53jighāṃsantaḥ śatānīkaṃ sarvataḥ paryavārayan
|
chādyamānaṃ śaravrātaiḥ śatānīkaṃ yaśasvinam ||53||

54abhyadhāvanta saṃkruddhāḥ kekayāḥ pañca sodarāḥ
|
tānabhyāpatataḥ prekṣya tava putrā mahārathāḥ ||54||

55pratyudyayurmahārāja gajāniva mahāgajāḥ
|
durmukho durjayaścaiva tathā durmarṣaṇo yuvā ||55||

56śatruñjayaḥ śatrusahaḥ sarve kruddhā yaśasvinaḥ
|
pratyudyātā mahārāja kekayān bhrātaraḥ samam ||56||

57rathairnagarasaṃkāśairhayairyuktairmanojavaiḥ
|
nānāvarṇavicitrābhiḥ patākābhiralaṃkṛtaiḥ ||57||

58varacāpadharā vīrā vicitrakavacadhvajāḥ
|
viviśuste paraṃ sainyaṃ siṃhā iva vanādvanam ||58||

59teṣāṃ sutumulaṃ yuddhaṃ vyatiṣaktarathādvipam
|
avartata mahāraudraṃ nighnatāmitaretaram ||59||

60anyonyāgaskṛtāṃ rājan dharmarāṣṭravivardhanam
|
muhūrtāstamite sūrye cakruryuddhaṃ sudāruṇam ||60||

61rathinaḥ sādinaścātha vyakīryanta sahasraśaḥ
|
tataḥ śāntanavaḥ kuddhaḥ śaraiḥ sannataparvabhiḥ||61||

62nāśayāmāsa senāṃ tāṃ bhīṣmasteṣāṃ mahātmanām
|
pañcālānāṃ ca sainyāni śarairninye yamakṣayam ||62||

63evaṃ bhittvā maheṣvāsaḥ pāṇḍavānāmanīkinīm
|
kṛtvā'vahāraṃ sainyānāṃ yayau svaśibiraṃ nṛpa ||63||

64dharmarājo'pi saṃprekṣya dhṛṣṭadyumnavṛkoda‌rau
|
mūrdhni caitāvupāghrāya prahṛṣṭaḥ śibiraṃ yayau ||64||
