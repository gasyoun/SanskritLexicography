Mahābhārata 14.1 (Āśvamedhikaparvan)

2vaiśampāyana uvāca|
kṛtodakaṃ tu rājānaṃ dhṛtarāṣṭra yudhiṣṭhiraḥ
|
puraskṛtya mahābāhuruttatārākulendriyaḥ||2||

3uttīrya tu mahābāhurbāṣpavyākulalocanaḥ
|
papāta tīre gaṅgāyā vyādhaviddha iva dvipaḥ||3||

4taṃ sīdamānaṃ jagrāha bhīmaḥ kṛṣṇena coditaḥ
|
maivamityabravīccainaṃ kṛṣṇaḥ parabalārdanaḥ||4||

5tamārtaṃ patitaṃ bhūmau śvasantaṃ ca punaḥ punaḥ
|
dadṛśuḥ pārthivā rājan dharmaputraṃ yudhiṣṭhiram||5||

6taṃ dṛṣṭvā dīnamanasaṃ gatasattvaṃ nareśvaram
|
bhūyaḥ śokasamāviṣṭāḥ pāṇḍavāḥ samupāviśan||6||

7rājā tu dhṛtarāṣṭraśca putraśokābhipīḍitaḥ
|
vākyamāha mahābuddhiḥ prajñācakṣurnareśvaram||7||

8uttiṣṭha kuruśārdūla kuru kāryamanantaram
|
kṣatradharmeṇa kaunteya jiteyamavanī tvayā||8||

9bhuṃkṣva bhogān bhrātṛbhiśca suhṛddhiśca mano'nugān
|
śocitavyaṃ na paśyāmi tvayā dharmabhṛtāṃ vara||9||

10śocitavyaṃ mayā caiva gāndhāryā ca mahīpate
|
yayoḥ putraśataṃ naṣṭaṃ svapnalabdhaṃ yathā dhanam||10||

11aśrutvā hitakāmasya vidurasya mahātmanaḥ
|
vākyāni sumahārthāni paritapyāmi durmatiḥ||11||

12uktavān viduro yanmāṃ dharmātmā divyadarśanaḥ
|
duryodhanāparādhena kulaṃ te vinaśiṣyati||12||

13svasti cedicchase rājan kulasya kuru me vacaḥ
|
vadhyatāmeṣa duṣṭātmā mando rājā suyodhanaḥ||13||

14karṇaśca śakuniścaiva nainaṃ paśyatu karhicit
|
dyūtasaṅghātamapyeṣāmapramādena vāraya||14||

15abhiṣecaya rājānaṃ dharmātmānaṃ yudhiṣṭhiram
|
sa pālayiṣyati vaśī dharmeṇa pṛthivīmimām||15||

16atha necchasi rājānaṃ kuntīputraṃ yudhiṣṭhiram
|
meḍhībhūtaḥ svayaṃ rājyaṃ pratigṛhṇīṣva pārthiva||16||

17samaṃ sarveṣu bhūteṣu vartamānaṃ narādhipa
|
anujīvantu sarve tvāṃ jñātayo bhrātṛbhiḥ saha||17||

18evaṃ bruvati kaunteya vidure dīrghadarśini
|
duryodhanamahaṃ pāpamanvavarta vṛthāmatiḥ||18||

19aśrutvā tasya dhīrasya vākyāni madhurāṇyaham
|
phalaṃ prāpya mahadduḥkhaṃ nimagnaḥ śokasāgare||19||

20vṛddhau hi te'dya pitarau paśya nau duḥkhitau nṛpa
|
na śocitavyaṃ bhavatā paśyāmīha janādhipa||20||
