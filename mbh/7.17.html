Mahābhārata 7.17 (Droṇaparvan)

1te sene śibiraṃ gatvā nyaviśetāṃ viśāṃpate
|
yathābhāgaṃ yathānyāyaṃ yathāgulma ca sarvaśaḥ||1||

2kṛtvā'vahāraṃ sainyānāṃ droṇaḥ paramadurmanāḥ
|
duryodhanamabhiprekṣya savrīḍamidamabravīt||2||

3uktametanmayā pūrvaṃ na tiṣṭhati dhanañjaye
|
śakyo grahītuṃ saṃgrāme devairapi yudhiṣṭhiraḥ||3||

4iti tadvaḥ prayatatāṃ kṛtaṃ pārthena saṃyuge
|
mā viśaṅkīrvaco mahyamajeyau kṛṣṇapāṇḍavau||4||

5apanīte tu yogena kenacicchvetavāhane
|
tata eṣyati te rājan vaśameṣa yudhiṣṭhiraḥ||5||

6kaścidāhūya taṃ saṃṅkhye deśamanyaṃ prakarṣatu
|
tamajitvā na kaunteyo nivarteta kathaṃcana||6||

7etasminnantare śūnye dharmarājamahaṃ nṛpa
|
grahīṣyāmi camūṃ bhittvā dhṛṣṭadyumnasya paśyataḥ||7||

8arjunena vihīnastu yadi notsṛjate raṇam
|
māmupāyāntamālokya gṛhītaṃ viddhi pāṇḍavam||8||

9evaṃ te'haṃ mahārāja dharmaputraṃ yudhiṣṭhiram
|
samāneṣyāmi sagaṇaṃ vaśamadya na saṃśayaḥ||9||

10yadi tiṣṭhati saṃgrāme muhūrtamapi pāṇḍavaḥ
|
athāpayāti saṃgrāmādvijayāttadviśiṣyate||10||

11sañjaya uvāca|
droṇasya tadvacaḥ śrutvā trigartādhipatistadā
|
bhrātṛbhiḥ sahito rājannidaṃ vacanamabravīt||11||

12vayaṃ vinikutā rājan sadā gāṇḍīvadhanvanā
|
anāgaḥsvapi cāgastatkṛtamasmāsu tena vai||12||

13te vayaṃ smaramāṇāstān vinikārān pṛthagvidhān
|
kodhāgninā dahyamānā naśemahi sadā niśi||13||

14sa no diṣṭyāstrasampannaścakṣurviṣayamāgataḥ
|
kartāraḥ sma vayaṃ karma yaccikīrṣāma hṛdgatam||14||

15bhavataśca priyaṃ yatsyādasmākaṃ ca yaśaskaram
|
vayamenaṃ haniṣyāmo nikṛṣyāyodhanādbahiḥ||15||

16adyāstvanarjunā bhūmiratrigartā'tha vā punaḥ
|
satyaṃ te pratijānīmo naitanmithyā bhaviṣyati||16||

17evaṃ satyarathaścoktvā satyavarmā ca bhārata
|
satyavrataśca satyeṣuḥ satyakarmā tathaiva ca||17||

18sahitā bhrātaraḥ pañca rathānāmayutena ca
|
nyavartanta mahārāja kṛtvā śapathamāhave||18||

19mālavāstuṇḍikerāśca rathānāmayutaistribhiḥ
|
suśarmā ca naravyāghrastrigartaḥ prasthalādhipaḥ||19||

20māvellakairlalitthaiśca sahito madrakairapi
|
rathānāmayutenaiva so'gamadbhātṛbhiḥ saha||20||

21nānājanapadebhyaśca rathānāmayutaṃ punaḥ
|
samutthitaṃ viśiṣṭānāṃ śapathārthamupāgamat||21||

22tato jvalanamānarcya hutvā sarve pṛthak pṛthak
|
jagṛhuḥ kuśacīrāṇi citrāṇi kavacāni ca||22||

23te ca baddhatanutrāṇā ghṛtāktāḥ kuśacīriṇaḥ
|
maurvīmekhalino vīrāḥ sahasraśatadakṣiṇāḥ||23||

24yajvānaḥ putriṇo lokyāḥ kṛtakṛtyāstanutyajaḥ
|
yokṣyamāṇāstadā''tmānaṃ yaśasā vijayena ca||24||

25brahmacaryaśrutimukhaiḥ kratubhiścāptada‌kṣiṇaiḥ
|
prāpyān lokān suyuddhena kṣiprameva yiyāsavaḥ||25||

26brāhmaṇāṃstarpayitvā ca niṣkān dattvā pṛthak pṛthak
|
gāśca vāsāṃsi ca punaḥ samābhāṣya parasparam||26||

27prajvālya kṛṣṇavartmānamupāgamya raṇavratam
|
tasminnagnau tadā cakruḥ pratijñāṃ dṛḍhaniścayāḥ||27||

28śṛṇvatāṃ sarvabhūtānāmuccairvāco babhāṣire
|
sarve dhanañjayavadhe pratijñāṃ cāpi cakrire||28||

29ye vai lokāścāvratināṃ ye caiva brahmaghātinām
|
madyapasya ca ye lokā gurudāraratasya ca||29||

30brahmasvahāriṇaścaiva rājapiṇḍāpahāriṇaḥ
|
śaraṇāgataṃ ca tyajato yācamānaṃ tathā ghnataḥ||30||

31agāradāhināṃ caiva ye ca gāṃ nighnatāmapi
|
apakāriṇāṃ ca ye lokā ye ca brahmadviṣāmapi||31||

32svabhāryāmṛtukāleṣu mohādvai nābhigacchatām
|
śrāddhamaithunikānāṃ ca ye cāpyātmāpahāriṇām||32||

33nyāsāpahāriṇāṃ ye ca śrutaṃ nāśayatāṃ ca ye
|
klībena yudhyamānānāṃ ye ca nīcānusāriṇām||33||

34nāstikānāṃ ca ye lokā ye'gnimātṛpitṛtyajām
|
tānāpnuyāmahe lokān ye ca pāpakṛtāmapi||34||

35yadyahatvā vayaṃ yuddhe nivartema dhanañjayam
|
tena cābhyarditāstrāsādbhavema hi parāṅmukhāḥ||35||

36yadi tvasukaraṃ loke karma kuryāma saṃyuge
|
iṣā~llokān prāpnuyāmo vayamadya na saṃśayaḥ||36||

37evamuktvā tadā rājaṃste'bhyavartanta saṃyuge
|
āhvayanto'rjunaṃ vīrāḥ pitṛjuṣṭāṃ diśaṃ prati||37||

38āhūtastairnaravyāghraiḥ pārthaḥ parapurañjayaḥ
|
dharmarājamidaṃ vākyamapadāntaramabravīt||38||

39āhūto na nivarteyamiti me vratamāhitam
|
saṃśaptakāśca māṃ rājannāhvayanti mahāmṛdhe||39||

40eṣa ca bhrātṛbhiḥ sārdhaṃ suśarmā'hvayate raṇe
|
vadhāya sagaṇasyāsya māmanujñātumarhasi||40||

41naitacchaknomi saṃsoḍhumāhvānaṃ puruṣarṣabha
|
satyaṃ te pratijānāmi hatān viddhi parān yudhi||41||

42yudhiṣṭhira uvāca|
śrutaṃ te tattvatastāta yaddroṇasya cikīrṣitam
|
yathā tadanṛtaṃ tasya bhavet tattvaṃ samācara||42||

43droṇo hi balavān śūraḥ kṛtāstraśca jitaśramaḥ
|
pratijñātaṃ ca tenaitaṅgrahaṇaṃ me mahāratha||43||

44arjuna uvāca|
ayaṃ vai satyajidrājannadya tvāṃ rakṣitā yudhi
|
dhriyamāṇe ca pāñcālye nācāryaḥ kāmamāpsyati||44||

45hate tu puruṣavyāghre raṇe satyajiti prabho
|
sarvairapi sametairvā na sthātavyaṃ kathañcana||45||

46sañjaya uvāca|
anujñātastato rājñā pariṣvaktaśca phālgunaḥ
|
premṇā dṛṣṭaśca bahudhā hyāśiṣaścāsya yojitāḥ||46||

47vihāyainaṃ tataḥ pārthastrigartān pratyayādbalī
|
kṣudhitaḥ kṣudvighātārthaṃ siṃho mṛgagaṇāniva||47||

48tato dauryodhanaṃ sainyaṃ mudā paramayā yutam
|
ṛte'rjunaṃ bhṛśaṃ kruddhaṃ dharmarājasya nigrahe||48||

49tato'nyonyena te sainye samājagmaturojasā
|
gaṅgāsarayvau vegena prāvṛṣīvolbaṇodake||49||
