Mahābhārata 5.11 (Udyogaparvan)

1śalya uvāca|
ṛṣayo'thābruvan sarve devāśca tridiveśvarāḥ
|
ayaṃ vai nahuṣaḥ śrīmān devarājye'bhiṣicyatām ||1||

2tejasvī ca yaśasvī ca dhārmikaścaiva nityadā
|
te gatvā tvabruvan sarve rājā no bhava pārthiva ||2||

3sa tānuvāca nahuṣo devānṛṣigaṇāṃstathā
|
pitṛbhiḥ sahitān rājan parīpsan hitamātmanaḥ ||3||

4durbalo'haṃ na me śaktirbhavatāṃ paripālane
|
balavān jñāyate rājā balaṃ śakre hi nityadā ||4||

5tamabruvan punaḥ sarve devā ṛṣipurogamāḥ
|
asmākaṃ tapasā yuktaḥ pāhi rājyaṃ triviṣṭape ||5||

6parasparabhayaṃ ghoramasmākaṃ hi na saṃśayaḥ
|
abhiṣicyasva rājendra bhava rājā triviṣṭape ||6||

7devadānavayakṣāṇāmṛṣīṇāṃ rakṣasāṃ tathā
|
pitṛgandharvabhūtānāṃ cakṣurviṣayavartinām||7||

8teja ādāsyase paśyan balavāṃśca bhaviṣyasi
|
dharmaṃ puraskṛtya sadā sarvalokādhipo bhava ||8||

9brahmarṣīścāpi devāṃśca gopāyasva triviṣṭape
|
abhiṣiktaḥ sa rājendra tato rājā triviṣṭape ||9||

10dharmaṃ puraskṛtya tadā sarvalokādhipo'bhavat
|
sudurlabhaṃ varaṃ labdhvā prāpya rājyaṃ triviṣṭape||10||

11dharmātmā satataṃ bhūtvā kāmātmā samapadyata
|
devodyāneṣu sarveṣu nandanopavaneṣu ca||11||

12kailāse himavatpṛṣṭhe mandare śvetaparvate
|
sahye mahendre malaye samudreṣu saritsu ca ||12||

13apsarobhiḥ parivṛto devakanyāsamāvṛtaḥ
|
nahuṣo devarājo'tha krīḍan bahuvidhaṃ tadā ||13||

14śrṛṇvan divyā bahuvidhāḥ kathāḥ śrutimanoharāḥ
|
vāditrāṇi ca sarvāṇi gītaṃ ca madhurasvanam ||14||

15viśvāvasurnāradaśca gandharvāpsarasāṃ gaṇāḥ
|
ṛtavaḥ ṣaṭ ca devendraṃ mūrtimanta upasthitāḥ ||15||

16mārutaḥ surabhirvāti manojñaḥ sukhaśītalaḥ
|
evaṃ ca krīḍatastasya nahuṣasya durātmanaḥ ||16||

17saṃprāptā darśanaṃ devī śakrasya mahiṣī priyā
|
sa tāṃ saṃdṛśya duṣṭātmā prāha sarvān sabhāsadaḥ ||17||

18indrasya mahiṣī devī kasmānmāṃ nopatiṣṭhati
|
ahamindro'smi devānāṃ lokānāṃ ca tatheśvaraḥ ||18||

19āgacchatu śacī mahyaṃ kṣipramadya niveśanam
|
tacchrutvā durmanā devī bṛhaspatimuvāca ha ||19||

20rakṣa māṃ nahuṣādbrahmaṃstvāmasmi śaraṇaṃ gatā
|
sarvalakṣaṇasaṃpannāṃ brahmaṃstvaṃ māṃ prabhāṣase||20||

21devarājasya dayitāmatyantaṃ sukhabhāginīm
|
avaidhavyena yuktāṃ cāpyekapatnīṃ pativradām ||21||

22uktavānasi māṃ pūrvamṛtāṃ tāṃ kuru vai giram
|
noktapūrvaṃ ca bhagavan vṛthā te kiñcidīśvara ||22||

23tasmādetadbhavet satyaṃ tvayoktaṃ dvijasattama
|
bṛhaspatirathovāca śakrāṇīṃ bhayamohitām ||23||

24yaduktā'si mayā devi satyaṃ tadbhavitā dhruvam
|
drakṣyase devarājānamindraṃ śīghramihāgatam ||24||

25na bhettavyaṃ ca nahuṣāt satyametadbravīmi te
|
samānayiṣye śakreṇa na cirādbhavatīmaham||25||

26atha śuśrāva nahuṣa śakrāṇīṃ śaraṇaṃ gatām
|
bṛhaspateraṅgirasaścukrodha sa nṛpastadā ||26||
