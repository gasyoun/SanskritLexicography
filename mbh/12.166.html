Mahābhārata 12.166 (Śāntiparvan)

1vaiśampāyana uvāca|
kathāntaramathāsādya khaḍgayuddhaviśāradaḥ
|
nakulaḥ śaratalpasthamidamāha pitāmaham ||1||

2nakula uvāca| dhanuḥ praharaṇaṃ śreṣṭhamatīvātra pitāmaha
|
matastu mama dharmajña khaḍga eva susaṃśitaḥ ||2||

3viśīrṇe kārmuke rājan prakṣīṇeṣu ca vājiṣu
|
khaḍgena śakyate yuddhe sādhvātmā parirakṣitum ||3||

4śarāsanadharāṃścaiva gadāśaktidharāṃstathā
|
ekaḥ khaḍgadharo vīraḥ samarthaḥ pratibādhitum ||4||

5atra me saṃśayaścaiva kautūhalamatīva ca
|
kiṃ svitpraharaṇaṃ śreṣṭhaṃ sarvayuddhaṣu pārthiva ||5||

6kathaṃ cotpāditaḥ khaḍgaḥ kasmai cārthāya kena ca
|
pūrvācāryaṃ ca khaḍgasya prabrūhi prapitāmaha ||6||

7vaiśampāyana uvāca|
tasya tadvacanaṃ śrutvā mādrīputrasya dhīmataḥ
|
sa tu kauśala saṃyuktaṃ sūkṣmacitrārthasammatam ||7||

8tatastasyottaraṃ vākyaṃ svaravarṇopapāditam
|
śikṣayā copapannāya droṇaśiṣyāya bhārata ||8||

9uvāca sa tu dharmajño dhanurvedasya pāragaḥ
|
śaratalpagato bhīṣmo nakulāya mahātmane ||9||

10bhīṣma uvāca|
tattvaṃ śṛṇuṣva mādreya yadetatparipṛcchasi
|
prabodhito'smi bhavatā dhātumāniva parvataḥ ||10||

11salilaikārṇavaṃ tāta purā sarvamabhūdidam
|
niṣprakampamanākāśamanirdeśyamahītalam ||11||

12tamasā''vṛtamasparśamatigambhīradarśanam
|
niḥśabdaṃ cāprameyaṃ ca tatra jajñe pitāmahaḥ ||12||

13so'sṛjadvātamagniṃ ca bhāskaraṃ cāpi vīryavān
|
ākāśamasṛjaccordhvamadho bhūmiṃ ca nairṛtīm ||13||

14nabhaḥ sacandratāraṃ ca nakṣatrāṇi grahāṃstathā
|
saṃvatsarānṛtūnmāsānpakṣānatha lavān kṣaṇān ||14||

15tataḥ śarīraṃ lokasthaṃ sthāpayitvā pitāmahaḥ
|
janayāmāsa bhagavān putrānuttamatejasaḥ ||15||

16marīcimṛṣimatriṃ ca pulastyaṃ pulahaṃ ṛtum
|
vasiṣṭhāṅgirasau cobhau rudraṃ ca prabhumīśvaram ||16||

17prācetasastathā dakṣaḥ kanyāṣaṣṭimajījanat
|
tā vai brahmarṣayaḥ sarvāḥ prajārthaṃ pratipedire ||17||

18tābhyo viśvāni bhūtāni devāḥ pitṛgaṇāstathā
|
gandharvāpsarasaścaiva rakṣāṃsi vividhāni ca ||18||

19patatrimṛgamīnāśca plavaṅgāśca mahoragāḥ
|
tathā pakṣigaṇāḥ sarve jalasthalavicāriṇaḥ ||19||

20udbhidaḥ svedajāścaiva sāṇḍajāśca jarāyujāḥ
|
jajñe tāta jagatsarvaṃ tathā sthāvarajaṅgamam ||20||

21bhūtasargamimaṃ kṛtvā sarvalokapitāmahaḥ
|
śāśvataṃ vedapaṭhitaṃ dharmaṃ prayuyuje tataḥ ||21||

22tasmin dharme sthitā devāḥ sahācāryapurohitāḥ
|
ādityā vasavo rudrāḥ sasādhyā marudataśvinaḥ ||22||

23bhṛgvatryaṅgirasaḥ siddhāḥ kāśyapāśca tapodhanāḥ
|
vasiṣṭhagautamāgastyāstathā nāradaparvatau ||23||

24ṛṣayo vālakhilyāśca prabhāsāḥ sikatāstathā
|
ghṛtapāḥ somavāyavyā vaiśvānaramarīcipāḥ ||24||

25akṛṣṭāścaiva haṃsāśca ṛṣayo vā'gniyonayaḥ
|
vānaprasthāḥ pṛśnayaśca sthitā brahmānuśāsane ||25||

26dānavendrāstvatikramya tatpitāmahaśāsanam
|
dharmasyāpacayaṃ cakruḥ krodhalobhasamanvitāḥ ||26||

27hiraṇyakaśipuścaiva hiraṇyākṣo virocanaḥ
|
śambaro vipracittiśca virādho namucirbaliḥ ||27||

28ete cānye ca bahavaḥ sagaṇā daityadānavāḥ
|
dharmasetumatikramya remire dharmaniścayāḥ ||28||

29sarve tulyābhijātīyā yathā devāstathā vayam
|
ityevaṃ dharmamāsthāya spardhamānāḥ surarṣibhiḥ ||29||

30na priyaṃ nāpyanukrośaṃ cakrurbhūteṣu bhārata
|
trīnupāyānupākramya daṇḍena rurudhuḥ prajāḥ ||30||

31na jagmuḥ saṃvidaṃ taiśca darpādasurasattamāḥ
|
atha vai bhagavānbrahmā brahmarṣibhirupasthitaḥ ||31||

32tadā himavataḥ śṛṅge suramye padmatārake
|
śatayojanavistāre maṇiratnacayācite ||32||

33tasmin girivare putra puṣpitadrumakānane
|
tasthau sa vibudhaśreṣṭho brahmā lokārthasiddhaye ||33||

34tato varṣasahasrānte vitānamakarotprabhuḥ
|
vidhinā kalpadṛṣṭena yathāvaccopapāditam ||34||

35ṛṣibhiryajñapaṭubhiryathāvatkarmakartṛbhiḥ
|
samidbhiḥ parisaṃkīrṇaṃ dīpyamānaiśca pāvakaiḥ ||35||

36kāñcanairyajñabhāṇḍaiśca bhrājiṣṇubhiralaṃkṛtam
|
vṛtaṃ devagaṇaiścaiva pravarairyajñamaṇḍalam ||36||

37tathā brahmarṣibhiścaiva sadasyairupaśobhitam
|
tatra ghoratamaṃ vṛttamṛṣīṇāṃ me pariśrutam ||37||

38candramā vimalaṃ vyoma yathābhyuditatārakam
|
vikīryāgniṃ tathā bhūtamutthitaṃ śrūyate tadā ||38||

39nīlotpalasavarṇābhaṃ tīkṣṇadaṃṣṭra kṛśodaram
|
prāṃśuṃ sudurdharṣataraṃ tathaiva hyamitaujasam ||39||

40tasminnutapatamāne ca pracacāla vasundharā
|
mahormikalitāvartaścukṣubhe sa mahodadhiḥ ||40||

41peturulkā mahotpātāḥ śākhāśca mumucurdrumāḥ
|
apraśāntā diśaḥ sarvāḥ pavanaścāśivo vavau ||41||

42muhurmuhuśca bhūtāni prāvyathanta bhayāttathā
|
tataḥ sa tumulaṃ dṛṣṭvā taṃ ca bhūtamupasthitam ||42||

43maharṣisuragandharvānuvācedaṃ pitāmahaḥ
|
mayaivaṃ cintitaṃ bhūtamasirnāmaiṣa vīryavān ||43||

44rakṣaṇārthāya lokasya vadhāya ca suradviṣām
|
tatastadrūpamutsṛjya babhau nistriṃśa eva saḥ ||44||

45vimalastīkṣṇadhāraśca kālāntaka ivodyataḥ
|
tataḥ sa śitikaṇṭhāya rudrāyārṣabhaketave ||45||

46brahmā dadāvasiṃ tīkṣṇamadharmaprativāraṇam
|
tataḥ sa bhagavān rudro maharṣijanasaṃstutaḥ ||46||

47pragṛhyāsimameyātmā rūpamanyaccakāra ha
|
caturbāhuḥ spṛśanmūrdhnā bhūsthito'pi divākaram ||47||

48urdhvadṛṣṭirmahāliṅgo mukhājjvālāḥ samutsṛjan
|
vikurvan bahudhā varṇānnīlapāṇḍuralohitān ||48||

49bibhratkṛṣṇājinaṃ vāso hema pravaratārakam
|
netraṃ caikaṃ lalāṭena bhāskarapratimaṃ vahan ||49||

50śuśubhāte'tivimale dve netre kṛṣṇapiṅgale
|
tato devo mahādevaḥ śūlapāṇirbhagākṣihā ||50||

51sampragṛhya tu nistriṃśaṃ kālāgnisamavarcasam
|
trikūṭaṃ carma codyamya savidyutamivāmbudam
|
cacāra vividhānmārgānmahābalaparākramaḥ ||51||

52vidhunvannasimākāśe tathā yuddhacikīrṣayā
|
tasya nādaṃ vinadato mahāhāsaṃ ca muñcataḥ ||52||

53babhau pratibhayaṃ rūpaṃ tadā rudrasya bhārata
|
tadrūpadhāriṇaṃ rudraṃ raudrakarmacikīrṣayā ||53||

54niśamya dānavāḥ sarve hṛṣṭāḥ samabhidudruvuḥ
|
aśmabhiścābhyavarṣanta pradīptaiśca tatholmukaiḥ ||54||

55ghoraiḥ praharaṇaiścānyaiḥ kṣuradhārairayomayaiḥ
|
tatastu dānavānīkaṃ sampraṇetāramucyatam ||55||

56rudraṃ dṛṣṭvā balodbhūtaṃ pramumoha cacāla ca
|
citraṃ śīghrapadatvācca carantamasipāṇinam ||56||

57tamekamasurāḥ sarve sahasramiti menire
|
chindan bhindan rujan kṛntan dārayan pothayannapi ||57||

58acaradvairisaṃgheṣu dāvāgniriva kakṣagaḥ
|
asivegaprabhagnāste chinnabāhūruvakṣasaḥ ||58||

59samprakīrṇāṃtragātrāśca petururvyāṃ mahābalāḥ
|
apare dānavā bhagnāḥ khaḍgapātāvapīḍitāḥ ||59||

60anyonyamabhinardanto diśaḥ sampratipedire
|
bhūmiṃ kecitpraviviśuḥ parvatānapare tathā ||60||

61apare jagmurākāśamapare'mbhaḥ samāviśan
|
tasminmahati saṃvṛtte samare bhṛśadāruṇe ||61||

62babhūva bhūḥ pratibhayā māṃsaśoṇitakardamā
|
dānavānāṃ śarīraiśca patitaiḥ śoṇitokṣitaiḥ ||62||

63samākīrṇā mahābāho śailairiva sakiṃśukaiḥ
|
sa rudro dānavān hatvā kṛtvā dharmottaraṃ jagat ||63||

64raudraṃ rūpamathotkṣipya cakre rūpaṃ śivaṃ śivaḥ
|
tato maharṣayaḥ sarve sarve devagaṇāstathā ||64||

65jayenādbhutakalpena devadevaṃ tathā'rcayan
|
tataḥ sa bhagavān rudro dānavakṣatajokṣitam ||65||

66asiṃ dharmasya goptāraṃ dadau satkṛtya viṣṇave
|
viṣṇurmarīcaye prādānmarīcirbhagavānapi ||66||

67maharṣibhyo dadau khaḍgamṛṣayo vāsavāya ca
|
mahendro lokapālebhyo lokapālāstu putrakā ||67||

68manave sūryaputrāya daduḥ khaḍgaṃ suvistaram
|
ucuścainaṃ tathā vākyaṃ mānuṣāṇāṃ tvamīśvaraḥ ||68||

69asinā dharmagarbheṇa pālayasva prajā iti
|
dharmasetumatikrāntāḥ sthūlasūkṣmātmakāraṇāt ||69||

70vibhajya daṇḍaṃ rakṣyāstu dharmato na yadṛcchayā
|
durvācā nigraho daṇḍo hiraṇyabahulastathā ||70||

71vyaṅgatā ca śarīrasya vadho vā'nalpakāraṇāt
|
aseretāni rūpāṇi durvārādīni nirdiśet ||71||

72aserevaṃ pramāṇāni paripālya vyatikramāt
|
sa visṛjyātha putraṃ svaṃ prajānāmadhipaṃ tataḥ ||72||

73manuḥ prajānāṃ rakṣārthaṃ kṣupāya pradadāvasim
|
kṣupājjagrāha cekṣvākurikṣvākośca purūravāḥ ||73||

74āyuśca tasmāllebhe taṃ nahuṣaśca tato bhuvi
|
yayātirnahuṣāccāpi pūrustasmācca labdhavān ||74||

75amūrtarayasastasmāttato bhūmiśayo nṛpaḥ
|
bharataścāpi dauṣyantirlebhe bhūmiśayādasim ||75||

76tasmāllebhe ca dharmajño rājannailavilastathā
|
tatastvailavilāllebhe dhundhumāro nareśvaraḥ ||76||

77dhundhumārācca kāmbojo mucukundastato'labhat
|
mucukundānmaruttaśca maruttādapi raivataḥ ||77||

78raivatādyuvanāśvaśca yuvanāśvāttato raghuḥ
|
ikṣvākuvaṃśajastasmāddhariṇāśvaḥ pratāpavān ||78||

79hariṇāśvādasiṃ lebhe śunakaḥ śunakādapi
|
uśīnaro vai dharmātmā tasmādbhojaḥ sa yādavaḥ ||79||

80yadubhyaśca śibirlebhe śibeścāpi pratardanaḥ
|
pratardanādaṣṭakaśca pṛṣadaśvo'ṣṭakādapi ||80||

81pṛṣadaśvādbharadvājo droṇastasmātkṛpastataḥ
|
tatastvaṃ bhrātṛbhiḥ sārdhaṃ paramāsimavāptavān ||81||

82kṛttikāstasya nakṣatramaseragniśca daivatam
|
rohiṇīṃ gotramasyātha rudraśca gururuttamaḥ ||82||

83aseraṣṭau hi nāmāni rahasyāni nibodha me
|
pāṇḍaveya sadā yāni kīrtayan labhate jayam ||83||

84asirviśasanaḥ khaḍgastīkṣṇadhāro durāsadaḥ
|
śrīgarbho vijayaścaiva dharmapālastathaiva ca ||84||

85agnyaḥ praharaṇānāṃ ca khaḍgo mādravatīsuta
|
maheśvarapraṇītaśca purāṇe niścayaṃ gataḥ ||85||

86pṛthustūtpādayāmāsa dhanurādyamarindamaḥ
|
teneyaṃ pṛthivī dugdhā sasyāni subahūnyapi
|
dharmeṇa ca yathāpūrvaṃ vainyena parirakṣitā ||86||

87tadetadārṣaṃ mādreya pramāṇaṃ kartumarhasi
|
aseśca pūjā kartavyā sadā yuddhaviśāradaiḥ ||87||

88ityeṣa prathamaḥ kalpo vyākhyātaste suvistarāt
|
aserutpattisaṃsargo yathāvadbharatarṣabha ||88||

89sarvathaitadidaṃ śrutvā khaḍgasādhanamuttamam
|
labhate puruṣaḥ kīrtiṃ pretya cānantyamaśnute ||89||
