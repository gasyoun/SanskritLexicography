Mahābhārata 12.250 (Śāntiparvan)

1śuka uvāca |
yasmāddharmātparo dharmo vidyate neha kaścana
|
yo viśiṣṭaśca dharmebhyastaṃ bhavān prabravītu me ||1||

2vyāsa uvāca |
dharmaṃ te sampravakṣyāmi purāṇamṛṣibhiḥ kṛtam
|
viśiṣṭaṃ sarvadharmabhyastamihaikamanāḥ śṛṇu ||2||

3indriyāṇi pramāthīni buddhyā saṃyamya yatnataḥ
|
sarvato niṣpatiṣṇūni pitā bālānivātmajān ||3||

4manasaścendriyāṇāṃ cāpyaikāgryaṃ paramaṃ tapaḥ
|
tajjyāyaḥ sarvadharmebhyaḥ sa dharmaḥ para ucyate ||4||

5tāni sarvāṇi saṃdhāya manaḥṣaṣṭhāni medhayā
|
ātmatṛpta ivāsīta bahucintyamacintayan ||5||

6gocarebhyo nivṛttāni yadā sthāsyanti veśmani
|
tadā tvamātmanā''tmānaṃ paraṃ drakṣyasi śāśvatam ||6||

7sarvātmānaṃ mahātmānaṃ vidhūmamiva pāvakam
|
taṃ paśyanti mahātmāno brāhmaṇā ye manīṣiṇaḥ ||7||

8yathā puṣpaphalopeto bahuśākho mahādrumaḥ
|
ātmano nābhijānīte kva me puṣpaṃ kva me phalam ||8||

9evamātmā na jānīte kva gamiṣye kutastvaham
|
anyo hyatrāntarātmā'sti yaḥ sarvamanupaśyati ||9||

10jñānadīpena dīptena paśyatyātmānamātmani
|
dṛṣṭvā tvamātmanātmānaṃ nirātmā bhava sarvavit ||10||

11vimuktaḥ sarvapāpebhyo muktatvaca ivoragaḥ
|
parāṃ buddhimavāpyeha vipāpmā vigatajvaraḥ ||11||

12sarvataḥsrotasaṃ ghorāṃ nadīṃ lokapravāhinīm
|
pañcendriyagrāhavatīṃ manaḥsaṃkalparodhasam ||12||

13lobhamohatṛṇacchannāṃ kāmakrodhasarīsṛpām
|
satyatīrthānṛtakṣobhāṃ krodhapaṅkāṃ saridvarām ||13||

14avyaktaprabhavāṃ śīghrāṃ dustarāmakṛtātmabhiḥ
|
pratarasva nadīṃ buddhyā kāmagrāhasamākulām ||14||

15saṃsārasāgaragamāṃ yonipātāladustarām
|
ātmakarmodbhavāṃ tāta jihvāvartāṃ durāsadām ||15||

16yāṃ taranti kṛtaprajñā dhṛtimanto manīṣiṇaḥ
|
tāṃ tīrṇaḥ sarvato mukto vidhṛtātmā''tmavicchuciḥ ||16||

17uttamāṃ buddhimāsthāya brahmabhūyānbhaviṣyasi
|
saṃtīrṇaḥ sarvasaṃsārātprasannātmā vikalmaṣaḥ ||17||

18bhūmiṣṭhānīva bhūtāni parvatastho niśāmaya
|
akrudhyannapradṛṣyaṃśca na nṛśaṃsamatistathā ||18||

19tato drakṣyasi sarveṣāṃ bhūtānāṃ prabhavāpyayau
|
enaṃ vai sarvabhūtebhyo viśiṣṭaṃ menire budhāḥ
|
dharmaṃ dharmabhṛtāṃ śreṣṭhā munayastattvadarśinaḥ ||19||

20ātmano vyāpino jñānamidaṃ putrānuśāsanam
|
prayatāya pravaktavyaṃ hitāyānugatāya ca ||20||

21ātmajñānamidaṃ guhyaṃ sarvaguhyatamaṃ mahat
|
abruvaṃ yadahaṃ tāta ātmasākṣikamañjasā ||21||

22naiva strī na pumānetannaiva cedaṃ napuṃsakam
|
aduḥkhamasukhaṃ brahma bhūtabhavyabhavātmakam ||22||

23naitajjñātvā pumānstrī vā punarbhavamavāpnute
|
abhavapratipattyarthametaddharmaṃ vidhīyate ||23||

24yathā matāni sarvāṇi tathaitāni yathā tathā
|
kathitāni mayā putra bhavanti na bhavanti ca ||24||

25tatprītiyuktena guṇānvitena putreṇa satputra damānvitena
|
pṛṣṭo hi samprītamanā yathārthaṃ brūyātsutasyeha yaduktametat ||25||
