Mahābhārata 8.58 (Karṇaparvan)

1sañjaya uvāca|
evameṣa mahānāsītsaṃgrāmaḥ pṛthivīkṣitām
|
kruddhe'rjune tathā karṇe bhīmasene ca pāṇḍave ||1||

2droṇaputraṃ parājitya jitvā cānyānmahārathān
|
abravīdarjuno rājanvāsudevamidaṃ vacaḥ ||2||

3paśya kṛṣṇa mahābāho dravantīṃ pāṇḍavīṃ camūm
|
karṇaṃ paśya ca saṃgrāme kālayantaṃ mahārathān ||3||

4na ca paśyāmi dāśārha dharmarājaṃ yudhiṣṭhiram
|
nāpi keturyudhāṃ śreṣṭha dharmarājasya dṛśyate ||4||

5tribhāgaścāvaśiṣṭo'yaṃ divasasya janārdana
|
na ca māṃ dhārtarāṣṭreṣu kaccidyudhyati saṃyuge ||5||

6tasmāttvaṃ matpriyaṃ kurvanyāhi yatra yudhiṣṭhiraḥ
|
dṛṣṭvā kuśalinaṃ yuddhe dharmaputraṃ sahānujam ||6||

7punaryoddhā'smi vārṣṇeya śatrubhiḥ saha saṃyuge
|
tataḥ prāyādrathenānuśu bībhatsorvacanāddhariḥ ||7||

8yato yudhiṣṭhiro rājā sṛñjayāśca mahārathāḥ
|
ayudhyaṃstāvakaiḥ sārdhaṃ mṛtyuṃ kṛtvā nivartanam ||8||

9tataḥ saṃgrāmabhūmiṃ tāṃ vartamāne janakṣaya
|
avekṣamāṇo govindaḥ savyasācinamabravīt ||9||

10paśya pārtha mahāraudro vartate bharatakṣayaḥ
|
pṛthivyāṃ kṣatriyāṇāṃ vai duryodhanakṛte mahān ||10||

11paśya bhārata cāpāni rukmapṛṣṭhāni dhanvinām
|
mṛtānāmapaviddhāni kalāpāṃśca mahādhanān ||11||

12jātarūpamayaiḥ puṅkhaiḥ śarāṃścānataparvaṇaḥ
|
tailadhautāṃśca nārācān nirmuktānpannagāniva ||12||

13hastidantatsarūn khaḍgān jātarūpapariṣkṛtān
|
varmāṇi cāpaviddhāni rukmagarbhāṇi bhārata ||13||

14suvarṇavikṛtān prāsān śaktīḥ kanakabhūṣaṇāḥ
|
jāmbūnadamayaiḥ paṭṭairbaddhāśca vipulā gadāḥ ||14||

15jātarūpamayīścarṣṭīḥ paṭṭiśān hemabhūṣaṇān
|
daṇḍaiḥ kanakacitraiśca vipraviddhānparaśvadhān ||15||

16ayaḥkuntāṃśca patitān musalāni gurūṇi ca
|
śataghnīḥ paśya citrāśca vipulānparighāṃstathā ||16||

17cakrāṇi cāpaviddhāni tomarāṃśca mahāraṇe
|
nānāvidhāni śastrāṇi pragṛhya jayagṛddhinaḥ ||17||

18jīvanta iva dṛśyante gatasattvāstarasvinaḥ
|
gadāvimathitairgātrairmusalairbhinnamastakān ||18||

19gaja-vāji-rathakṣuṇṇānpaśya yodhānsahasraśaḥ
|
manuṣya-haya-nāgānāṃ śaraśaktyṛṣṭipaṭṭiśaiḥ ||19||

20parighairāyasairghorairayaskuntaiḥ paraśvadhaiḥ
|
śarīrairbahubhiśchinnaiḥ śoṇitaughapariplutaiḥ ||20||

21gatāsubhiramitraghna saṃvṛtā raṇabhūmayaḥ
|
bāhubhiścandanādigdhaiḥ sāṅgadairhemabhūṣitaiḥ ||21||

22satalatraiḥ sakeyūrairbhāti bhārata medinī
|
sāṅgulitrairbhujāgraiśca vipraviddhairalaṅkṛtaiḥ ||22||

23hastihastopamaiśchinnairūrubhiśca tarasvinām
|
baddhacūḍāmaṇivaraiḥ śirobhiśca sakuṇḍalaiḥ ||23||

24patitairṛṣabhākṣāṇāṃ virājati vasundharā
|
kabandhaiḥ śoṇitādigdhaiśchinnagātraśirodharaiḥ ||24||

25bhūrbhāti bharataśreṣṭha śāntārcibhirivāgnibhiḥ
|
rathāṃśca bahudhā bhagnān hemakiṅkiṇinaḥ śubhān ||25||

26vājinaśca hatānpaśya niṣkīrṇāntrān śarāhatān
|
anukarṣānupāsaṅgān patākā vividhadhvajān ||26||

27rathināṃ ca mahāśaṃkhān pāṇḍurāṃśca prakīrṇakān
|
nirastajihvān mātaṅgān śayānān parvatopamān ||27||

28vaijayantīrvicitrāśca hatāṃśca gajavājinaḥ
|
vāraṇānāṃ paristomāṃstathaivājinakambalān ||28||

29vipāṭitavicitrāṃśca rūpyacitrān kuthāṅkuśān
|
bhinnāśca bahudhā ghaṇṭā mahadbhiḥ patitairgajaiḥ ||29||

30vaidūryadaṇḍāṃśca śubhān patitānaṅkuśānbhuvi
|
baddhāḥ sādibhujāgreṣu suvarṇavikṛtāḥ kaśāḥ ||30||

31vicitramaṇicitrāṃśca jātarūpapariṣkṛtān
|
aśvāstaraparistomānrāṅkavānpatitān bhuvi ||31||

32cūḍāmaṇīnnarendrāṇāṃ vicitrāḥ kāñcanasrajaḥ
|
chatrāṇi cāpaviddhāni cāmaravyajanāni ca ||32||

33candra-nakṣatrabhāsaiśca vadanaiścārukuṇḍalaiḥ
|
kluptaśmaśrubhiratyarthaṃ vīrāṇāṃ samalaṅkṛtaiḥ ||33||

34vadanaiḥ paśya saṃchannāṃ mahīṃ śoṇitakardamām
|
sajīvāṃścāparānpaśya kūjamānānsamaṃtataḥ ||34||

35upāsyamānān bahuśo nyastaśastrairviśāṃpate
|
jñātibhiḥ sahitāṃstatra rodamānairmuhurmuhuḥ ||35||

36vyutkrāntānaparānyodhāṃśchādayitvā tarasvinaḥ
|
punaryuddhāya gacchanti jayagṛddhāḥ pramanyavaḥ ||36||

37apare tatra tatraiva paridhāvanti mānavāḥ
|
jñātibhiḥ patitaiḥ śūrairyācyamānāstathoda‌kum ||37||

38jalārthaṃ ca gatāḥ kecinniṣprāṇā bahavo'rjuna
|
sannivṛttāśca te śūrāstānvai dṛṣṭvā vicetasaḥ ||38||

39jalaṃ tyaktvā pradhāvanti krośamānāḥ parasparam
|
jalaṃ pītvā mṛtānpaśya pibato'nyāṃśca māriṣa ||39||

40parityajya priyānanye bāndhavānbāndhavapriyāḥ
|
vyutkrāntāḥ samadṛśyanta tatra tatra mahāraṇe ||40||

41tathā parānnaraśreṣṭha sandaṣṭauṣṭhapuṭānpunaḥ
|
bhrukuṭīkuṭilairvaktraiḥ prekṣamāṇānsamantataḥ ||41||

42evaṃ bruvaṃstadā kṛṣṇo yayau yatra yudhiṣṭhiraḥ
|
arjunaścāpi nṛpaterdarśanārthaṃ mahāraṇe ||42||

43yāhi yāhīti govindaṃ muhurmuhuracodayat
|
tāṃ yuddhabhūmiṃ pārthasya darśayitvā ca mādhavaḥ ||43||

44tvaramāṇastataḥ kṛṣṇaḥ pārthamāha śanairidam
|
paśya pāṇḍava rājānamupayātāṃśca pārthivān ||44||

45karṇaṃ paśya mahāraṅge jvalantamiva pāvakam
|
asau bhīmo maheṣvāsaḥ sannivṛtto raṇaṃ prati ||45||

46tamete vinivartante dhṛṣṭadyumnapurogamāḥ
|
pañcālasṛñjayānāṃ ca pāṇḍavānāṃ ca ye mukham ||46||

47nivṛttaiśca punaḥ pārthairbhagnaṃ śatrubalaṃ mahat
|
kauravāndravato hyeṣa karṇo rodhaya te'rjuna ||47||

48antakapratimo vege śakratulyaparākramaḥ
|
asau gacchati kauravya drauṇiḥ śastrabhṛtāṃ varaḥ ||48||

49tameva pradrutaṃ saṃkhye dhṛṣṭadyumno mahārathaḥ
|
anuprayāti saṃgrāme hatānpaśya ca sṛñjayān ||49||

50sarvamāha sudurdharṣo vāsudevaḥ kirīṭine
|
tato rājanmahāghoraḥ prādurāsīnmahāraṇaḥ ||50||

51siṃhanādaravāścaiva prādurāsansamāgame
|
ubhayoḥ senayo rājan mṛtyuṃ kṛtvā nivartanam ||51||

52evameṣa kṣayo vṛttaḥ pṛthivyāṃ pṛthivīpate
|
tāvakānāṃ pareṣāṃ ca rājandurmantrite tava ||52||
