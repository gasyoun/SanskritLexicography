Mahābhārata 8.72 (Karṇaparvan)

1sañjaya uvāca|
prasādya dharmarājānaṃ prahṛṣṭenāntarātmanā
|
pārthaḥ provāca govindaṃ sūtaputravadhodyataḥ ||1||

2kalpatāṃ me ratho bhūyo yujyantāṃ ca hayottamāḥ
|
āyudhāni ca sarvāṇi sajjantāṃ me mahārathe ||2||

3upāvṛttāśca turagāḥ śikṣitāścāśvasādibhiḥ
|
rathopakaraṇaiḥ sajjā upāyāntuṃ tvarānvitāḥ ||3||

4prayāhi śīghraṃ govinda sūtaputrajighāṃsayā
|
evamukto mahārāja phālgunena mahātmanā ||4||

5uvāca dārukaṃ kṛṣṇaḥ kuru sarvaṃ yathā'bravīt
|
arjuno bharataśreṣṭha śreṣṭhaḥ sarvadhanuṣmatām ||5||

6ājñaptastvatha kṛṣṇena dāruko rājasattama
|
yojayāmāsa sa rathaṃ vaiyāghraṃ śatrutāpanam ||6||

7sajjaṃ nivedayāmāsa pāṇḍavasya mahātmanaḥ
|
yuktaṃ tu taṃ rathaṃ dṛṣṭvā dārukeṇa mahātmanā ||7||

8āpṛcchya dharmarājānaṃ brāhmaṇānsvasti vācya ca
|
sumaṅgalasvastyayanamāruroha rathottamam ||8||

9tasya rājā mahāprājño dharmarājo yudhiṣṭhiraḥ
|
āśiṣo'yuṅkta sa tataḥ prāyātkarṇarathaṃ prati ||9||

10tamāyāntaṃ maheṣvāsaṃ dṛṣṭvā bhūtāni bhārata
|
nihataṃ menire karṇaṃ pāṇḍavena mahātmanā ||10||

11babhūvurvimalāḥ sarvā diśo rājansamantataḥ
|
cāṣāśca śatapatrāśca krauñcāścaiva janeśvara ||11||

12pradakṣiṇamakurvanta tadā vai pāṇḍunandanam
|
bahavaḥ pakṣiṇo rājanpunnāmānaḥ śubhāḥ śivāḥ ||12||

13tvarayanto'rjunaṃ yuddhe hṛṣṭarūpā vavāśire
|
kaṅkā gṛdhrā bakāḥ śyenā vāyasāśca viśāṃpate ||13||

14agratastasya gacchanti māṃsahetorbhayānakāḥ
|
nimittāni ca dhanyāni pāṇḍavasya śaśaṃsire ||14||

15vināśamarisainyānāṃ karṇasya ca vadhaṃ prati
|
prayātasyātha pārthasya mahān svedo vyajāyata ||15||

16cintā ca vipulā jajñe kathaṃ cedaṃ bhaviṣyati
|
tato gāṇḍīvadhanvānamabravīnmadhusūdanaḥ ||16||

17dṛṣṭvā pārthaṃ tathā''yāntaṃ cintāparigataṃ tadā
|
vāsudeva uvāca|
gāṇḍīvadhanvansaṃgrāme ye tvayā dhanuṣā jitāḥ
|
na teṣāṃ mānuṣo jetā tvadanya iha vidyate ||17||

18dṛṣṭā hi bahavaḥ śūrāḥ śakratulyaparākramāḥ
|
tvāṃ prāpya samare śūraṃ te gatāḥ paramāṃ gatim ||18||

19ko hi droṇaṃ ca bhīṣmaṃ ca bhagadattaṃ ca māriṣa
|
vindānuvindāvāvantyau kāmbojaṃ ca sudakṣiṇam ||19||

20śrutāyuṣaṃ mahāvīryamacyutāyuṣameva ca
|
pratyudgamya bhavetkṣemī yo na syāttvamiva prabho ||20||

21tava hyastrāṇi divyāni lāghavaṃ balameva ca
|
asaṃmohaśca yuddheṣu vijñānasya ca saṃnatiḥ ||21||

22vedhaḥ pātaśca lakṣeṣu yogaścaiva tathā'rjuna
|
bhavāndevānsagandharvān hanyātsaha carācarān ||22||

23pṛthivyāṃ tu raṇe pārtha na yoddhā tvatsamaḥ pumān
|
dhanurgrāhā hi ye kecit kṣatriyā yuddhadurmadāḥ ||23||

24ādevāttvatsamaṃ teṣāṃ na paśyāmi śṛṇomi ca
|
brahmaṇā ca prajāḥ sṛṣṭā gāṇḍīvaṃ ca mahaddhanuḥ ||24||

25yena tvaṃ yudhyase pārtha tasmānnāsti tvayā samaḥ
|
avaśyaṃ tu mayā vācyaṃ yatpathyaṃ tava pāṇḍava ||25||

26māvamaṃsthā mahābāho karṇamāhavaśobhinam
|
karṇo hi balavāndṛptaḥ kṛtāstraśca mahārathaḥ ||26||

27kṛtī ca citrayodhī ca deśakālasya kovidaḥ
|
bahunā'tra kimuktena saṃkṣepācchṛṇu pāṇḍava ||27||

28tvatsamaṃ tvadviśiṣṭaṃ vā karṇaṃ manye mahāratham
|
paramaṃ yatnamāsthāya tvayā vadhyo mahāhave ||28||

29tejasā vahnisadṛśo vāyuvegasamo jave
|
antakapratimaḥ krodhe siṃhasaṃhanano balī ||29||

30aṣṭaratnirmahābāhurvyūḍhoraskaḥ sudurjayaḥ
|
abhimānī ca śūraśca pravīraḥ priyadarśanaḥ ||30||

31sarvayodhaguṇairyukto mitrāṇāmabhayaṃkaraḥ
|
satataṃ pāṇḍavadveṣī dhārtarāṣṭrahite rataḥ ||31||

32sarvairavadhyo rādheyo devairapi savāsavaiḥ
|
ṛte tvāmiti me buddhistadadya jahi sūtajam ||32||

33devairapi hi saṃyattairbibhradbhirmāṃsaśoṇitam
|
aśakyaḥ sa ratho jetuṃ sarvairapi yuyutsubhiḥ ||33||

34durātmānaṃ pāpavṛttaṃ nṛśaṃsaṃ duṣṭaprajñaṃ pāṇḍaveyeṣu nityam
|
hīnasvārthaṃ pāṇḍaveyairvirodhe hatvā karṇaṃ niścitārtho bhavādya ||34||

35taṃ sūtaputraṃ rathināṃ variṣṭhaṃ niṣkālikaṃ kālavaśaṃ nayādya
|
taṃ sūtaputraṃ rathināṃ variṣṭhaṃ hatvā prītiṃ dharmarāje kuruṣva ||35||

36jānāmi te pārtha vīryaṃ yathāvaddurvāraṇīyaṃ ca surāsuraiśca
|
sadā'vajānāti hi pāṇḍuputrānasau darpātsūtaputro durātmā ||36||

37ātmānaṃ manyate vīraṃ yena pāpaḥ suyodhanaḥ
|
tamadya mūlaṃ pāpānāṃ jahi sautiṃ dhanañjaya ||37||

38khaḍgajihvaṃ dhanurāsyaṃ śaradaṃṣṭraṃ tarasvinam
|
dṛptaṃ puruṣaśārdūlaṃ jahi karṇaṃ dhanañjaya ||38||

39ahaṃ tvāmanujānāmi vīryeṇa ca balena ca
|
jahi karṇaṃ raṇe śūraṃ mātaṅgamiva kesarī ||39||

40yasya vīryeṇa vīryaṃ te dhārtarāṣṭro'vamanyate
|
tamadya pārtha saṃgrāme karṇaṃ vaikartanaṃ jahi ||40||
