Mahābhārata 1.153 (Ādiparvan)

1vaiśaṃpāyana uvāca|
tāṃ viditvā ciragatāṃ hiḍimbo rākṣaseśvaraḥ
|
avatīrya drumāttasmādājagāmāśu pāṇḍavān||1||

2lohitākṣo mahābāhurūrdhvakeśo mahānanaḥ
|
meghasaṅghātavarṣmā ca tīkṣṇadaṃṣṭro bhayānakaḥ||2||

3tamāpatantaṃ dṛṣṭvaiva tathā vikṛtadarśanam
|
hiḍimbovāca vitrastā bhīmasenamidaṃ vacaḥ||3||

4āpatatyeṣa duṣṭātmā saṃkruddhaḥ puruṣādakaḥ
|
sā'haṃ tvāṃ bhrātṛbhiḥ sārdhaṃ yadbravīmi tathā kuru||4||

5ahaṃ kāmagamā vīra rakṣobalasamanvitā
|
āruhe māṃ mama śroṇiṃ neṣyāmi tvāṃ vihāyasā||5||

6prabodhayaitānsaṃsuptānmātaraṃ ca parantapa
|
sarvāneva gamiṣyābhi gṛhītvā vo vihāyasā||6||

7bhīma uvāca|
mābhaistvaṃ pṛthusuśroṇi naiṣa kaścinmayi sthite
|
ahamenaṃ haniṣyāmi paśyantyāste sumadhyame||7||

8nāyaṃ pratibalo bhīru rākṣasāpasado mama
|
soḍhuṃ yudhi parispandamathavā sarvarākṣasāḥ||8||

9paśya bāhū suvṛttau me hastihastanibhāvimau
|
ūrū parighasaṅkāśau saṃhataṃ cāpyuro mahat||9||

10vikramaṃ me yathendrasya sā'dya drakṣyasi śobhane
|
māvamaṃsthāḥ pṛthuśroṇi matvā māmiha mānuṣam||10||

11hiḍimbovāca
|
nāvamanye naravyāghra tvāmahaṃ devarūpiṇam
|
dṛṣṭaprabhāvastu mayā mānuṣeṣveva rākṣasaḥ||11||

12vaiśaṃpāyana uvāca|
tathā saṃjalpatastasya bhīmasenasya bhārata
|
vācaḥ śuśrāva tāḥ kruddho rākṣasaḥ puruṣādakaḥ||12||

13avekṣyamāṇastasyāśca hiḍimbo mānuṣaṃ vapuḥ
|
sragdāmapūritaśikhaṃ samagrendunibhānanām||13||

14subhrūnāsākṣikeśāntāṃ sukumāranakhatvacam
|
sarvābharaṇasaṃyuktāṃ susūkṣmāmbaravāsasam||14||

15tāṃ tathā mānuṣaṃ rūpaṃ bibhratīṃ sumanoharam
|
puṃskāmāṃ śaṅkamānaśca cukrodha puruṣādakaḥ||15||

16saṃkruddho rākṣasastasyā bhaginyāḥ kurusattama
|
utphālya vipule netre tatastāmidamabravīt||16||

17ko hi me bhoktukāmasya vighnaṃ carati durmatiḥ
|
na bibheṣi hiḍimbe kiṃ matkopādvipramohitā||17||

18dhiktvāmasati puṃskāme mama vipriyakāriṇi
|
pūrveṣāṃ rākṣasendrāṇāṃ sarveṣāmayaśaskari||18||

19yānimānāśritā'kārṣīrvipriyaṃ samuhanmama
|
eṣa tānadya vai sarvānhaniṣyāmi tvayā saha||19||

20evamuktvā hiḍimbāṃ sa hiḍimbo lohitekṣaṇaḥ
|
vadhāyābhipapātaināndantairdantānupaspṛśan||20||

21tamāpatantaṃ saṃprekṣya bhīmaḥ praharatāṃ varaḥ
|
bhartsayāmāsa tejasvī tiṣṭha tiṣṭheti cābravīt||21||

22vaiśaṃpāyana uvāca|
bhīmasenastu taṃ dṛṣṭvā rākṣasaṃ prahasanniva
|
bhaginīṃ pratisaṅkruddhamidaṃ vacanamabravīt||22||

23kiṃ te hiḍimba etairvā sukhasuptaiḥ prabodhitaiḥ
|
māmāsādaya durbuddhe tarasā tvaṃ narāśana||23||

24mayyeva praharaihi tvaṃ na striyaṃ hantumarhasi
|
viśeṣato'napakṛte pareṇāpakṛte sati||24||

25na hīyaṃ svavaśābālā kāmayatyadya māmiha
|
coditaiṣā hyanaṅgena śarīrāntaracāriṇā||25||

26bhaginī tava durvṛtta rakṣasāṃ vai yaśohara
|
tvanniyogena caiveyaṃ rūpaṃ mama samīkṣya ca||26||

27kāmayatyadya māṃ bhīrustava naiṣāparādhyati
|
anaṅgena kṛte doṣe nemāṃ garhitumarhasi||27||

28mayi tiṣṭhati duṣṭātmanna striyaṃ hantumarhasi
|
saṃgacchasva mayā sārdhamekenaiko narāśana||28||

29ahameko gamiṣyāmi tvāmadya yamasādanam
|
adya madbalaniṣpiṣṭaṃ śiro rākṣasa dīryatām
|
kuñjarasyeva pādena viniṣpiṣṭaṃ balīyasaḥ||29||

30adya gātrāṇi te kaṅkāḥ śyenā gomāyavastathā
|
karṣantu bhuvi saṃhṛṣṭā nihatasya mayā mṛdhe||30||

31kṣaṇenādya kariṣye'hamidaṃ vanamarākṣasam
|
purā yaddūṣitaṃ nityaṃ tvayā bhakṣayatā narān||31||

32adya tvāṃ bhaginī rakṣaḥ kṛṣyamāṇaṃ mayā'sakṛt
|
drakṣyatyadripratīkāśaṃ siṃheneva mahādvipam||32||

33nirābādhāstvayi hate mayā rākṣasapāṃsana
|
vanametaccariṣyanti puruṣā vanacāriṇaḥ||33||

34hiḍimba uvāca|
garjitena vṛthā kiṃ te katthitena ca mānuṣa
|
kṛtvaitatkarmaṇā sarvaṃ katthethā mā ciraṃ kṛthāḥ||34||

35balinaṃ manyase yaccāpyātmānaṃ saparākramam
|
jñāsyasyadya samāgamya mayātmānaṃ balādhikam||35||

36na tāvadetānhiṃsiṣye svapantvete yathāsukham
|
eṣa tvāmeva durbuddhe nihanmyadyāpriyaṃvadam||36||

37pītvā tavāsṛggātrebhyastataḥ paścādimānapi
|
haniṣyāmi tataḥ paścādimāṃ vipriyakāriṇīm||37||

38vaiśaṃpāyana uvāca|
evamuktvā tato bāhuṃ pragṛhya puruṣādakaḥ
|
abhyadravata saṃkruddho bhīmasenamarindama||38||

39tasyābhidravatastūrṇaṃ bhīmo bhīmaparākramaḥ
|
vegena prahitaṃ bāhuṃ nijagrāha hasanniva||39||

40nigṛhya taṃ balādbhīmo visphurantaṃ cakarṣa ha
|
tasmāddeśāddhanūṃṣyaṣṭau siṃhaḥ kṣudramṛgaṃ yathā||40||

41tataḥ sa rākṣasaḥ kruddhaḥ pāṇḍavena balārditaḥ
|
bhīmasenaṃ samāliṅgya vyanadadbhairavaṃ ravam||41||

42punarbhīmo balādenaṃ vicakarṣa mahābalaḥ
|
mā śabdaḥ sukhasuptānāṃ bhrātṝṇāṃ me bhavediti||42||

43anyonyaṃ tau samāsādya vicakarṣaturojasā
|
hiḍimbo bhīmasenaśca vikramaṃ cakratuḥ param||43||

44babhañjatustadā vṛkṣā~llatāścākarṣatustadā
|
mattāviva caṃ saṃrabdhau vāraṇau ṣaṣṭihāyanau||44||

45tayoḥ śabdena mahatā vibuddhāste nararṣabhāḥ
|
saha mātrā ca dadṛśurhiḍimbāmagrataḥ sthitām||45||
