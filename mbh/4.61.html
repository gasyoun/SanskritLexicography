Mahābhārata 4.61 (Virāṭaparvan)

1vaiśampāyana uvāca|tato vaikartanaṃ jitvā pārtho vairāṭimabravīt
|
etanmāṃ prāpayānīkaṃ yatra tālo hiraṇmayaḥ ||1||

2atra śāntanavo bhīṣmo rathe'smākaṃ pitāmahaḥ
|
kvāṅkṣamāṇo mayā yuddhaṃ tiṣṭhatyamaradarśanaḥ ||2||

3atha sainyaṃ mahaddṛṣṭvā rathanāgahayākulam
|
abravīduttaraḥ pārthamapaviddhaḥ śarairbhṛśam ||3||

4nāhaṃ śakṣyāmi vīreha niyantuṃ te hayottamān
|
viṣīdanti mama prāṇā mano vihvalatīva me ||4||

5astrāṇāmiva divyānāṃ prabhāvaḥ saṃprayujyatām
|
tvayā ca kurubhiścaiva dravantīva diśo daśa ||5||

6gandhena mūrchitaścāhaṃ vasārudhiramedasām
|
dvaidhībhūtaṃ mano me'dya tava caiva prapaśyataḥ ||6||

7adṛṣṭapūrvaḥ śūrāṇāṃ mayā saṃkhye samāgamaḥ
|
gadāpātena mahatā śaṅkhānāṃ niḥsvanena ca ||7||

8siṃhanādaiśca śūrāṇāṃ gajānāṃ bṛṃhitaistathā
|
gāṇḍīvaśabdena bhṛśamaśanipratimena ca
|
śrutiḥ smṛtiśca me vīra pranaṣṭā mūḍhacetasaḥ ||8||

9alātacakrapratimaṃ maṇḍalaṃ satataṃ tvayā
|
vyākṣipyamāṇaṃ samare gāṇḍīvaṃ ca prakarṣatā
|
dṛṣṭiḥ pracalitā vīra hṛdayaṃ dīryatīva me ||9||

10vapuścograṃ tava raṇe kruddhasyeva pinākinaḥ
|
vyāyacchatastava bhujaṃ dṛṣṭvā bhīrme bhavatyapi ||10||

11nādadānaṃ na saṃdhānaṃ na muñcantaṃ śarottamān
|
tvāmahaṃ saṃprapaśyāmi paśyannapi na cetanaḥ ||11||

12avasīdanti me prāṇā bhūriyaṃ calatīva ca
|
na ca pratodaṃ raśmīṃśca saṃyantuṃ śaktirasti me ||12||

13arjuna uvāca|mā bhaiṣīḥ stambhayātmānaṃ tvayā'pi narapuṅgava
|
atyadbhutāni karmāṇi kṛtāni raṇamūrdhani ||13||

14rājaputro'si bhadraṃ te kule matsyasya viśrute
|
jātastvaṃ śatrudamane nāvasīditumarhasi ||14||

15dhṛtiṃ kṛtvā suvipulāṃ rājaputra rathe mama
|
yudhyamānasya samare hayān saṃyaccha śatruhan ||15||

16vaiśampāyana uvāca|evamuktvā mahābāhurvairāṭiṃ narasattamaḥ
|
arjuno rathināṃ śreṣṭha uttaraṃ vākyamabravīt ||16||

17senāgramāśu bhīṣmasya prāpayasvaitadeva mām
|
ācchetsyāmyahametasya dhanurjyāmapi cāhave ||17||

18asyantaṃ divyamastraṃ māṃ citramadya niśāmaya
|
śatahradāmivāyāntīṃ stanayitnorivāmbare ||18||

19suvarṇapṛṣṭhaṃ gāṇḍīvaṃ drakṣyanti kuravo mama
|
dakṣiṇenātha vāmena katareṇa svidasyati ||19||

20iti māṃ saṅgatāḥ sarve tarkayiṣyanti śatravaḥ
|
śoṇitodāṃ rathāvartāṃ nāganakrāṃ duratyayām
|
nadīṃ praskandayiṣyāmi paralokapravāhinīm ||20||

21pāṇipādaśiraḥpṛṣṭhabāhuśākhānirantaram
|
vanaṃ kurūṇāṃ chetsyāmi śaraiḥ sannataparvabhiḥ ||21||

22jayataḥ kauravīṃ senāmekasya mama dhanvinaḥ
|
śataṃ mārgā bhaviṣyanti pāvakasyeva kānane ||22||

23mayā cakramivāviddhaṃ sainyaṃ drakṣyasi kevalam
|
iṣvastre śikṣitaṃ citramahaṃ darśayitā'smi te ||23||

24asaṃbhrānto rathe tiṣṭha sameṣu viṣameṣu ca
|
divamāvṛtya tiṣṭhantaṃ giriṃ bhindyāṃ sma patribhiḥ ||24||

25ahamindrasya vacanāt saṅgrāme'bhyahanaṃ purā
|
paulomān kālakhañjāṃśca sahasrāṇi śatāni ca ||25||

26ahamindrāddṛḍhāṃ muṣṭiṃ brahmaṇaḥ kṛtahastatām
|
pragāḍhe tumulaṃ citramiti viddhi prajāpateḥ ||26||

27ahaṃ pāre samudrasya hiraṇyapuravāsinām
|
jitvā ṣaṣṭiṃ sahastrāṇi rathināmugradhanvinām ||27||

28śīryamāṇāni kūlāni pravṛddheneva vāriṇā
|
mayā kurūṇāṃ vṛndāni pātyamānāni paśya vai ||28||

29dhvajavṛkṣaṃ pattitṛṇaṃ rathasiṃhagaṇāyutam
|
vanamādīpayiṣyāmi kurūṇāmastratejasā ||29||

30tānahaṃ rathanīḍebhyaḥ śaraiḥ sannataparvabhiḥ
|
yattān sarvānatibalān yotsyamānānavasthitān
|
ekaḥ saṃkālayiṣyāmi vajrapāṇirivāsurān ||30||

31raudraṃ rudrādahaṃ hyastraṃ vāruṇaṃ varuṇādapi
|
astramāgneyamagneśca vāyavyaṃ mātariśvanaḥ
|
vajrādīni tathā'strāṇi śakrādahamavāptavān ||31||

32dhārtarāṣṭravanaṃ ghoraṃ narasiṃhābhirakṣitam
|
ahamutpāṭayiṣyāmi vairāṭe vyetu te bhayam ||32||

33vaiśampāyana uvāca|evamāśvāsitastena vairāṭiḥ savyasācinā
|
vyavāgāhadrathānīkaṃ bhīmaṃ bhīṣmābhirakṣitam ||33||

34tamāyāntaṃ mahābāhuṃ jigīṣantaṃ raṇe kurūn
|
abhyavārayadavyagraḥ krūrakarmā''pagāsutaḥ ||34||

35tasya jiṣṇurupāvṛtya dhvajaṃ mūlādapātayat
|
vikṛṣya kaladhautāgraiḥ sa viddhaḥ prāpatadbhuvi ||35||

36taṃ citramālyābharaṇāḥ kṛtavidyā manasvinaḥ
|
āgacchan bhīmadhanvānaṃ catvāraśca mahābalāḥ ||36||

37duḥśāsano vikarṇaśca duḥsaho'tha viviṃśatiḥ
|
āgatya bhīmadhanvānaṃ bībhatsuṃ paryavārayan ||37||

38duḥśāsanastu bhallena viddhvā vairāṭamuttaram
|
dvitīyenārjunaṃ vīraḥ pratyavidhyat stanāntare ||38||

39tasya jiṣṇurupāvṛtya pṛthudhāreṇa kārmukam
|
cakarta gārdhrapatreṇa jātarūpapariṣkṛtam ||39||

40athainaṃ pañcabhiḥ paścāt pratyavidhyat stanāntare
|
so'payāto raṇaṃ hitvā pārthabāṇaprapīḍitaḥ ||40||

41taṃ vikarṇaḥ śaraistīkṣṇairgṛdhrapatrairajihmagaiḥ
|
vivyādha paravīraghnamarjunaṃ dhṛtarāṣṭrajaḥ ||41||

42tatastamapi kaunteyaḥ śareṇānataparvaṇā
|
lalāṭe'bhyahanat tūrṇaṃ sa viddhaḥ prāpatadrathāt ||42||

43tataḥ pārthamabhidrutya duḥsahaḥ sa viviṃśatiḥ
|
avākiraccharaistīkṣṇaiḥ parīpsurbhrātaraṃ raṇe ||43||

44tāvubhau gārdhrapatrābhyāṃ niśitābhyāṃ dhanañjayaḥ
|
viddhvā yugapadavyagrastayorvāhānasūdayat ||44||

45tau hatāśvau vibhinnāṅgau dhṛtarāṣṭrātmajāvubhau
|
abhipatya rathairanyairapanītau padānugaiḥ ||45||

46sarvā diśaścābhyapatadbibhatsuraparājitaḥ
|
kirīṭamālī kaunteyo labdhalakṣo mahābalaḥ ||46||
