Mahābhārata 5.19 (Udyogaparvan)

1vaiśampāyana uvāca|
yuyudhānastato vīraḥ sātvatānāṃ mahārathaḥ
|
mahatā caturaṅgeṇa balenāgādyudhiṣṭhiram ||1||

2tasya yodhā mahāvīryā nānādeśasamāgatāḥ
|
nānāpraharaṇā vīrāḥ śobhayāñcakrire balam ||2||

3paraśvadhairbhindipālaiḥ śūlatomaramudgaraiḥ
|
parighairyaṣṭibhiḥ pāśaiḥ karavālaiśca nirmalaiḥ ||3||

4khaḍgakārmukanirvyūhaiḥ śaraiśca vividhairapi
|
tailadhautaiḥ prakāśadbhiḥ sadā'śobhataṃ vai balam ||4||

5tasya meghaprakāśasya sauvarṇaiḥ śobhitasya ca
|
babhūva rūpaṃ sainyasya meghasyeva savidyutaḥ ||5||

6akṣauhiṇī tu sā senā tadā yaudhiṣṭhiraṃ balam
|
praviśyāntardadhe rājan sāgaraṃ kunadī yathā ||6||

7tathaivākṣauhiṇīṃ gṛhya cedīnāmṛṣabho balī
|
dhṛṣṭaketurupāgacchat pāṇḍavānamitaujasaḥ ||7||

8māgadhaśca jayatseno jārāsandhirmahābalaḥ
|
akṣauhiṇyaiva sainyasya dharmarājamupāgamat ||8||

9tathaiva pāṇḍyo rājendra sāgarānūpavāsibhiḥ
|
vṛto bahuvidhairyodhairyudhiṣṭhiramupāgamat||9||

10tasya sainyamatīvāsīt tasmin balasamāgame
|
prekṣaṇīyataraṃ rājan suveṣaṃ balavat tadā ||10||

11drupadasyāpyabhūtsenā nānādeśasamāgataiḥ
|
śobhitā puruṣaiḥ śūraiḥ putraiścāsya mahārathaiḥ ||11||

12tathaiva rājā matsyānāṃ virāṭo vāhinīpatiḥ
|
pārvatīyairmahīpālaiḥ sahitaḥ pāṇḍavāniyāt ||12||

13itaścetaśca pāṇḍūnāṃ samājagmurmahātmanām
|
akṣauhiṇyastu saptaiva vividhadhvajasaṅkulāḥ ||13||

14yuyutsamānāḥ kurubhiḥ pāṇḍavān samaharṣayan
|
tathaiva dhārtarāṣṭrasya harṣaṃ samabhivardhayan ||14||

15bhagadatto mahīpālaḥ senāmakṣauhiṇīṃ dadau
|
tasya cīnaiḥ kirātaiśca kāñcanairiva saṃvṛtam ||15||

16babhau balamanādhṛṣyaṃ karṇikāravanaṃ yathā
|
tathā bhūriśravāḥ śūraḥ śalyaśca kurunandana ||16||

17duryodhanamupāyātāvakṣauhiṇyā pṛthak pṛthak
|
kṛtavarmā ca hārdikyo bhojāndhakukuraiḥ saha ||17||

18akṣauhiṇyaiva senāyā duryodhanamupāgamat
|
tasya taiḥ puruṣavyāghrairvanamālādharairbalam ||18||

19aśobhata yathā mattairvanaṃ prakrīḍitairgajaiḥ
|
jayadrathamukhāścānye sindhusauvīravāsinaḥ ||19||

20ājagmuḥ pṛthivīpālāḥ kampayanta ivācalān
|
teṣāmakṣauhiṇī senā bahulā vibabhau tadā||20||

21vidhūyamāno vātena bahurūpa ivāmbudaḥ
|
sudakṣiṇaśca kāmbojo yavanaiśca śakaistathā ||21||

22upājagāma kauravyamakṣauhiṇyā viśāmpate
|
tasya senāsamāvāyaḥ śalabhānāmivābabhau ||22||

23sa ca saṃprāpya kauravyaṃ tatraivāntardadhe tadā
|
tathā mahiṣmatīvāsī nīlo nīlāyudhaiḥ saha ||23||

24mahīpālo mahāvīryairdakṣiṇāpathavāsibhiḥ
|
āvantyau ca mahīpālau mahābalasusaṃvṛtau ||24||

25pṛthagakṣauhiṇībhyāṃ tāvabhiyātau suyodhanam
|
kekayāśca naravyāghrāḥ sodaryāḥ pañca pārthivāḥ||25||

26saṃharṣayantaḥ kauravyamakṣauhiṇyā samādravan
|
tatastatastu sarveṣāṃ bhūmipānāṃ mahātmanām ||26||

27tisro'nyāḥ samavartanta vāhinyo bharatarṣabha
|
evamekādaśāvṛttāḥ senā duryodhanasya tāḥ ||27||

28yuyutsamānāḥ kaunteyānnānādhvajasamākulāḥ
|
na hāstinapure rājannavakāśo'bhavat tadā ||28||

29rājñāṃ svabalamukhyānāṃ prādhānyenāpi bhārata
|
tataḥ pañcanadaṃ caiva kṛtsnaṃ ca kurujāṅgalam ||29||

30tathā rohitakāraṇyaṃ marubhūmiśca kevalā
|
ahicchatraṃ kālakūṭaṃ gaṅgākūlaṃ ca bhārata ||30||

31vāraṇaṃ vāṭadhānaṃ ca yāmunaścaiva parvataḥ
|
eṣa deśaḥ suvistīrṇaḥ prabhūtadhanadhānyavān ||31||

32babhūva kauraveyāṇāṃ balenātīva saṃvṛtaḥ
|
tatra sainyaṃ tathā yuktaṃ dadarśa sa purohitaḥ ||32||

33yaḥ sa pāñcālarājena preṣitaḥ kauravān prati ||33||
