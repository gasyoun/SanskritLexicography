Mahābhārata 1.69 (Ādiparvan)

1janamejaya uvāca|
saṃbhavaṃ bharatasyāhaṃ caritaṃ ca mahāmateḥ
|
śakuntalāyāścotpattiṃ śrotumicchāmi tattvataḥ||1||

2duṣyantena ca vīreṇa yathā prāptā śakuntalā
|
taṃ vai puruṣasiṃhasya bhagavanvistaraṃ tvaham||2||

3śrotumicchāmi tattvajña sarvaṃ matimatāṃ vara
|
vaiśaṃpāyana uvāca|
sa kadācinmahābāhuḥ prabhūtabalavāhanaḥ||3||

4vanaṃ jagāma gahanaṃ hayanāgaśatairvṛtaḥ
|
balena caturaṅgeṇa vṛtaḥ paramavalgunā||4||

5khaḍgaśaktidharairvīrairgadāmusalapāṇibhiḥ
|
prāsatomarahastaiśca yayau yodhaśatairvṛtaḥ||5||

6siṃhanādaiśca yodhānāṃ śaṅkhadundubhiniḥsvanaiḥ
|
rathanemisvanaiścaiva sanāgavarabṛṃhitaiḥ||6||

7nānāyudhadharaiścāpi nānāveṣadharaistathā
|
hreṣitasvanamiśraiśca kṣveḍitāsphoṭitasvanaiḥ||7||

8āsītkilakilāśabdastasmingacchati pārthive
|
prāsādavaraśṛṅgasthāḥ parayā nṛpaśobhayā||8||

9dadṛśustaṃ striyastatra śūramātmayaśaskaram
|
śakropamamamitraghnaṃ paravāraṇavāraṇam||9||

10paśyantaḥ strīgaṇāstatra vajrapāṇiṃ sma menire
|
ayaṃ sa puruṣavyāghro raṇe vasuparākramaḥ||10||

11yasya bāhubalaṃ prāpya na bhavantyasuhṛdgaṇāḥ
|
iti vāco bruvantyastāḥ striyaḥ premṇā narādhipam||11||

12tuṣṭuvuḥ puṣpavṛṣṭīśca sasṛjustasya mūrdhani
|
tatratatra ca viprendraiḥ stūyamānaḥ samantataḥ||12||

13niryayau paramaprītyā vanaṃ mṛgajighāṃsayā
|
taṃ devarājapratimaṃ mattavāraṇadhūrgatam||13||

14dvijakṣatriyaviṭśūdrā niryāntamanujagmire
|
dadṛśurvardhamānāste āśīrbhiśca jayena ca||14||

15sudūramanujagmustaṃ paurajānapadāstathā
|
nyavartanta tataḥ paścādanujñātā nṛpeṇa ha||15||

16suparṇapratimenātha rathena vasudhādhipaḥ
|
mahīmāpūrayāmāsa ghoṣeṇa tridivaṃ tathā||16||

17sa gacchandadṛśe dhīmānnandanapratimaṃ vanam
|
bilvārkakhadirākīrṇaṃ kapitthadhavasaṃkulam||17||

18viṣamaṃ parvatasrastai raśmabhiśca samāvṛtam
|
nirjalaṃ nirmanuṣyaṃ ca bahuyojanamāyatam||18||

19mṛgasiṃhairvṛtaṃ ghorairanyaiścāpi vanecaraiḥ
|
tadvanaṃ manujavyāghraḥ sabhṛtyabalavāhanaḥ||19||

20loḍayāmāsa duṣyantaḥ sūdayanvividhānmṛgān
|
bāṇagocarasaṃprāptāṃstatra vyāghragaṇānbahūn||20||

21pātayāmāsa duṣyanto nirbibheda ca sāyakaiḥ
|
dūrasthānsāyakaiḥ kāṃścidabhinatsa narādhipaḥ||21||

22abhyāśamāgatāṃścānyānkhaḍgena nirakṛntata
|
kāṃścideṇānsamājaghne śaktyā śaktimatāṃ varaḥ||22||

23gadāmaṇḍalatattvajñaścacārāmitavikramaḥ
|
tomarairasibhiścāpi gadāmusalakampanaiḥ||23||

24cacāra sa vinighnanvai vanyāṃstatra mṛgadvijān
|
rājñā cādbhutavīryeṇa yodhaiśca samarapriyaiḥ||24||

25loḍyamānaṃ mahāraṇyaṃ tatyajuḥ sma mṛgādhipāḥ
|
tatra vidrutayūthāni hatayūthapatīni ca||25||

26mṛgayūthānyathautsukyācchabdaṃ cakrustatastataḥ
|
śuṣkāścāpi nadīrgatvā jalanairāśyakarśitāḥ||26||

27vyāyāmaklāntahṛdayāḥ patanti sma vicetasaḥ
|
kṣutpipāsāparītāśca śrāntāśca patitā bhuvi||27||

28kecittatra naravyāghrairabhakṣyanta bubhukṣitaiḥ
|
kecidagnimathotpādya saṃsādhya ca vanecarāḥ||28||

29bhakṣayanti sma māṃsāni prakuṭya vidhivattadā
|
tatra kecidgajā mattā balinaḥ śastravikṣatāḥ||29||

30saṃkocyāgrakarānbhītāḥ prādravanti sma vegitāḥ
|
śakṛnmūtraṃ sṛjantaśca kṣarantaḥ śoṇitaṃ bahu||30||

31vanyā gajavarāstatra mamṛdurmanujānbahūn
|
tadvanaṃ balameghena śaradhāreṇa saṃvṛtam
|
vyarocata mṛgākīrṇaṃ rājñā hatamṛgādhipam||31||
