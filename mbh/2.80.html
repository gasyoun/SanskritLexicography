Mahābhārata 2.80 (Sabhāparvan)

1vaiśampāyana uvāca|
tamāgatamatho rājā viduraṃ dīrghadarśinam
|
sāśaṅka iva prapaccha dhṛtarāṣṭo'mbikāsutaḥ||1||

2dhṛtarāṣṭra uvāca|
kathaṃ gacchati kaunteyo dharmaputro yudhiṣṭhiraḥ
|
bhīmasenaḥ savyasācī mādrīputrau ca pāṇḍavau||2||

3dhaumyaścaiva kathaṃ kṣattardraupadī ca yaśasvinī
|
śrotumicchāmyahaṃ sarvaṃ teṣāṃ śaṃsa viceṣṭitam||3||

4vidura uvāca|
vastreṇa saṃvṛtya mukhaṃ kuntīputro yudhiṣṭhiraḥ
|
bāhū viśālau sampaśyanbhīmo gacchati pāṇḍavaḥ||4||

5sikatā vapansavyasācī rājānamanugacchati
|
mādrīputraḥ sahadevo mukhamālipya gacchati||5||

6pāṃsūpaliptasarvāṅgo nakulaścittavihvalaḥ
|
darśanīyatamo loke rājānamanugacchati||6||

7kṛṣṇā tu keśaiḥ pracchādya mukhamāyatalocanā
|
darśanīyā prarudatī rājānamanugacchati||7||

8dhaumyo raudrāṇi sāmāni yāmyāni ca viśāmpate
|
gāyangacchati mārgeṣu kuśānādāya pāṇinā||8||

9dhṛtarāṣṭra uvāca|
vividhānīha rūpāṇi kṛtvā gacchanti pāṇḍavāḥ
|
tanmamācakṣva vidura kasmādevaṃ vrajanti te||9||

10vidura uvāca|
nikṛtasyāpi te putrairhṛte rājye dhaneṣu ca
|
na dharmāccalate buddhirdharmarājasya dhīmataḥ||10||

11yo'sau rājā ghṛṇī nityaṃ dhārtarāṣṭreṣu bhārata
|
nikṛtyā bhraṃśitaḥ krodhānnonmīlayati locane||11||

12nāhaṃ janaṃ nirdaheyaṃ dṛṣṭvā ghoreṇa cakṣuṣā
|
sa pidhāya mukhaṃ rājā tasmādgacchati pāṇḍavaḥ||12||

13yathā ca bhīmo vrajati nigadataḥ śṛṇu
|
bāhvorbalena nāsti samo mameti bharatarṣabha||13||

14bāhū viśālau kṛtvā'sau tena bhīmopi gacchati
|
bāhū vidarśayanrājanbāhudraviṇadarpitaḥ||14||

15cikīrṣankarma śatrubhyo bāhudravyānurūpataḥ
|
pradiśañśarasampātānkuntīputro'rjunastadā||15||

16sikatā vapansavyasācī rājānamanugacchati
|
asaktāḥ sikatāstasya yathā samprati bhārata
|
asaktaṃ śaravarṣāṇi tathā mokṣyati śatruṣu||16||

17na me kaścidvijānīyānmukhamadyeti bhārata
|
mukhamālipya tenāsau sahadevo'pi gacchati||17||

18nāhaṃ manāṃsyādadeyaṃ mārge strīṇāmiti prabho
|
pāṃsūpaliptasarvāṅgo nakulastena gacchati||18||

19ekavastrā prarudatī muktakeśī rajasvalā
|
śoṇitenāktavasanā draupadī vākyamabravīt||19||

20yatkṛte'hamidaṃ prāptā teṣāṃ varṣe caturdaśe
|
hatapatyo hatasutā hatabandhujanapriyāḥ||20||

21bandhuśoṇitadigdhāṅgyo muktakeśyo rajasvalāḥ
|
evaṃ kṛtodakā bhāryāḥ pravekṣyanti gajāhvayam||21||

22kṛtvā tu nairṛtāndarbhāndhīro dhaumyaḥ purohitaḥ
|
sāmāni gāyanyāmyāni purato yāti bhārata||22||

23hateṣu bharateṣvājau kurūṇāṃ guravastadā
|
evaṃ sāmāni gāsyantītyuktvā dhaumyopi gacchati||23||

24hāhā gacchanti no nāthāḥ samavekṣadhvamīdṛśam
|
aho dhikkuruvṛddhānāṃ bālānāmiva ceṣṭitam||24||

25rāṣṭrebhyaḥ pāṇḍudāyādā~llobhānnirvāsayanti ye
|
anāthāḥ sma vayaṃ sarve viyuktāḥ pāṇḍunandanaiḥ||25||

26durvinīteṣu lubdheṣu kā prītiḥ kauraveṣu naḥ
|
iti paurāḥ suduḥ khārtāḥ krośanti sma punaḥ punaḥ||26||

27evamākāraliṅgaiste vyavasāyaṃ manogatam
|
kathayantaśca kaunteyā vanaṃ jagmurmanasvinaḥ||27||

28evaṃ teṣu narāgryeṣu niryatsu gajasāhvayāt
|
anabhre vidyutaścāsanbhūmiśca samakampata||28||

29rāhuragrasadādityamaparvaṇi viśāmpate
|
ulkā cāpyapasavyena puraṃ kṛtvā vyaśīryata||29||

30pratyāharanti kravyādā gṛdhragomāyuvāyasāḥ
|
devāyatanacaityeṣu prākārāṭṭālakeṣu ca||30||

31evamete mahotpātāḥ prādurāsandurāsadāḥ
|
bharatānāmabhāvāya rājandurmantrite tava||31||

32vaiśampāyana uvāca|
evaṃ pravadatoreva tayostatra viśāmpate
|
dhṛtarāṣṭrasya rājñaśca vidurasya ca dhīmataḥ||32||

33nāradaśca sabhāmadhye kurūṇāmagrataḥ sthitaḥ
|
maharṣibhiḥ parivṛto raudraṃ vākyamuvāca ha||33||

34itaścaturdaśe varṣe vinaṅkṣyantīha kauravāḥ
|
duryodhanāparādhena bhīmārjunabalena ca||34||

35ityuktvā divamākramya kṣipramantaradhīyata
|
brāhmīṃ śriyaṃ suvipulāṃ bibhraddevarṣisattamaḥ||35||

36tato duryodhanaḥ karṇaḥ śakuniścāpi saubalaḥ
|
droṇaṃ dvīpamamanyanta rājyaṃ cāsmai nyavedayan||36||

37athābravīttato droṇo duryodhanamamarṣaṇam
|
duḥśāsanaṃ ca karṇaṃ ca sarvāneva ca bhāratān||37||

38avadhyānpāṇḍavānprāhurdevaputrāndvijātayaḥ
|
ahaṃ vai śaraṇaṃ prāptānvartamāno yathābalam||38||

39gantā sarvātmanā bhaktyā dhārtarāṣṭrānsarājakān
|
notsaheyaṃ parityaktuṃ daivaṃ hi balavattaram||39||

40dharmataḥ pāṇḍuputrā vai vanaṃ gacchanti nirjitāḥ
|
te ca dvādaśa varṣāṇi vane vatsyanti pāṇḍavāḥ||40||

41caritabrahmacaryāśca krodhāmarṣavaśānugāḥ
|
vairaṃ niryātayiṣyanti mahadduḥkhāya pāṇḍavāḥ||41||

42mayā ca bhraṃśito rājandrupadaḥ sakhivigrahe
|
putrārthamayajadrājā vadhāya mama bhārata||42||

43yājopayājatapasā putraṃ lebhe sa pāvakāt
|
dhṛṣṭadyumnaṃ draupadīṃ ca vedīmadhyātsumadhyamām||43||

44dhṛṣṭadyumnastu pārthānāṃ syālaḥ sambandhato mataḥ
|
pāṇḍavānāṃ priyaratastasmānmāṃ bhayamāviśat||44||

45jvālāvarṇo devadatto dhanuṣmānkavacī śarī
|
martyadharmatayā tasmādadya me sādhvaso mahān||45||

46gato hi pakṣatāṃ teṣāṃ pārṣataḥ paravīrahā
|
rathātirathasaṅkhyāyāṃ yo'graṇīrarjuno yuvā||46||

47sṛṣṭaprāṇo bhṛśataraṃ tena cetsaṅgamo mama
|
kimanyadduḥkhamadhikaṃ paramaṃ bhuvi kauravāḥ||47||

48dhṛṣṭadyumno droṇamṛtyuriti viprathitaṃ vacaḥ
|
madvadhāya śruto'pyeṣa loke cāpyativiśrutaḥ||48||

49so'yaṃ nūnamanuprāptastvatkṛte kāla uttamaḥ
|
tvaritaṃ kuruta śreyo naitadetāvatā kṛtam||49||

50muhūrtaṃ sukhamevaitattālacchāyeva haimanī
|
yajadhvaṃ ca mahāyajñairbhogānaśnīta datta ca||50||

51itaścaturdaśe varṣe mahatprāpsyatha vaiśasam
|
droṇasya vacanaṃ śrutvā dhṛtarāṣṭro'bravīdidam||51||

52samyagāha guruḥ kṣattarupāvartaya pāṇḍavān
|
yadi te na nivartante satkṛtā yāntu pāṇḍavāḥ||52||

53saśastrarathapādātā bhogavantaśca putrakāḥ||53||
