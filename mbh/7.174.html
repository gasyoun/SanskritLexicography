Mahābhārata 7.174 (Droṇaparvan)

1sañjaya uvāca|
dṛṣṭvā ghaṭotkacaṃ rājansūtaputrarathaṃ prati
|
āyāntaṃ tu tathāyuktaṃ jighāṃsuṃ karṇamāhave||1||

2abravīttatra putraste duḥśāsanamidaṃ vacaḥ
|
etadrakṣo raṇe tūrṇaṃ dṛṣṭvā karṇasya vikramam||2||

3abhiyāti drutaṃ karṇaṃ tadvāraya mahāratham
|
vṛtaḥ sainyena mahatā yāhi yatra mahābalaḥ||3||

4karṇo vaikartano yuddhe rākṣasena yuyutsati
|
rakṣa karṇaṃ raṇe yatto vṛtaḥ sainyena mānada||4||

5mā karṇaṃ rākṣaso ghoraḥ pramādānnāśayiṣyati
|
etasminnantare rājañjaṭāsurasuto balī||5||

6duryodhanamupāgamya prāha praharatāṃ varaḥ
|
duryodhana tavāmitrānprakhyātān yuddhadurmadān||6||

7pāṇḍavān hantumicchāmi tvayā''jñaptaḥ sahānugān
|
jaṭāsuro mama pitā rakṣasāṃ grāmaṇīḥ purā||7||

8ayujya karma rakṣoghnaṃ kṣudraiḥ pārthairnipātitaḥ
|
tasyāpacitimicchāmi śatruśoṇitapūjayā|| śatrumāṃsaiśca rājendra māmanujñātumarhasi||8||

9tamabravīttato rājā prīyamāṇaḥ punaḥ punaḥ
|
droṇakarṇādibhiḥ sārdhaṃ paryāpto'haṃ dviṣadvadhe||9||

10tvaṃ tu gaccha mayā''jñapto jahi yuddhe ghaṭotkacam
|
rākṣasaṃ krūrakarmāṇaṃ rakṣomānuṣasaṃbhavam||10||

11pāṇḍavānāṃ hitaṃ nityaṃ hastyaśvarathaghātinam
|
vaihāyasagataṃ yuddhe preṣayeryamasādanam||11||

12tathetyuktvā mahākāyaḥ samāhūya ghaṭotkacam
|
jāṭāsurirbhaimaseniṃ nānāśastrairavākirat||12||

13alaṃbuṣaṃ ca karṇaṃ ca kurusainyaṃ ca dustaram
|
haiḍimbiḥ pramamāthaiko mahāvāto'mbudāniva||13||

14tato māyābalaṃ dṛṣṭvā rakṣastūrṇamalambuṣaḥ
|
ghaṭotkacaṃ śaravrātairnānāliṅgaiḥ samārpayat||14||

15viddhvā ca bahubhirbāṇairbhaimaseniṃ mahābalaḥ
|
vyadrāvayaccharavrātaiḥ pāṇḍavānāmanīkinīm||15||

16tena vidrāvyamāṇāni pāṇḍusainyāni bhārata
|
niśīthe viprakīryante vātanunnā ghanā iva||16||

17ghaṭotkacaśarairnunnā tathaiva tava vāhinī
|
niśīthe prādravadrājannutsṛjyolkāḥ sahasraśaḥ||17||

18alaṃbuṣastataḥ kruddho bhaimaseniṃ mahāmṛdhe
|
ājaghne daśabhirbāṇaistotrairiva mahādvipam||18||

19tilaśastasya saṃvāhaṃ sutaṃ sarvāyudhāni ca
|
ghaṭotkacaḥ praciccheda praṇadaṃścātidāruṇam||19||

20tataḥ karṇaṃ śaravrātaiḥ kurūnanyānsahasraśaḥ
|
alaṃbuṣaṃ cābhyavarṣanmegho merumivācalam||20||

21tataḥ saṃcukṣubhe sainyaṃ kurūṇāṃ rākṣasārditam
|
uparyupari cānyonyaṃ caturaṅgaṃ mamarda ha||21||

22jāṭāsurirmahārāja viratho hatasārathiḥ
|
ghaṭotkacaṃ raṇe kruddho muṣṭinā'bhyahanaddṛḍham||22||

23muṣṭinā'bhyāhatastena pracacāla ghaṭotkacaḥ
|
kṣitikampe yathā śailaḥ savṛkṣastṛṇagulmavān||23||

24tataḥ sa parighābhena dviṭsaṅghaghnena bāhunā
|
jāṭāsuriṃ bhaimaseniravadhīnmuṣṭinā bhṛśam||24||

25taṃ pramathya tataḥ kruddhastūrṇaṃ haiḍimbirākṣipat
|
dorbhyāmindradhvajābhābhyāṃ niṣpipeṣa ca bhūtale||25||

26jāṭāsurirmokṣayitvā ātmānaṃ ca ghaṭotkacāt
|
punarutthāya vegena ghaṭotkacamupādravat||26||

27alaṃbuṣo'pi vikṣipya samutkṣipya ca rākṣasam
|
ghaṭotkacaṃ raṇe roṣānniṣpipeṣa ca bhūtale||27||

28tayoḥ samabhavadyuddhaṃ garjatoratikāyayoḥ
|
ghaṭotkacālambuṣayostumulaṃ lomaharṣaṇam||28||

29viśeṣayantāvanyonyaṃ māyābhiratimāyinau
|
yuyudhāte mahāvīryāvindravairocanāviva||29||

30pāvakāmbunidhī bhūtvā punargaruḍatakṣakau
|
punarmeghamahāvātau punarvajramahācalau||30||

31punaḥ kuñjaraśārdūlau punaḥ svarbhānubhāskarau
|
evaṃ māyāśatasṛjāvanyonyavadhakāṃkṣiṇau||31||

32bhṛśaṃ citramayudhyetāmalaṃbuṣaghaṭotkacau
|
paridhaiśca gadābhiśca prāsa-mudgara-paṭṭiśaiḥ||32||

33musalaiḥ parvatāgraiśca tāvanyonyaṃ vijaghnatuḥ
|
hayābhyāṃ ca gajābhyāṃ ca rathābhyāṃ ca padātibhiḥ||33||

34yuyudhāte mahāmāyau rākṣasapravarau yudhi
|
tato ghaṭotkaco rājannalambuṣavadhepsayā||34||

35utpapāta bhṛśaṃ kruddhaḥ śyenavannipapāta ca
|
gṛhītvā ca mahākāyaṃ rākṣasendramalambuṣam||35||

36udyamya nyavadhīdbhūmau mayaṃ viṣṇurivāhave
|
tato ghaṭotkacaḥ khaḍgamuddhṛtyādbhutadarśanam||36||

37raudrasya kāyāddhi śiro bhīmaṃ vikṛtadarśanam
|
sphuratastasya samare nadataścātibhairavam||37||

38nicakarta mahārāja śatroramitavikramaḥ
|
śirastaccāpi saṃgṛhya keśeṣu rudhirokṣitam||38||

39yayau ghaṭotkacastūrṇaṃ duryodhanarathaṃ prati
|
abhyetya ca mahābāhuḥ smayamānaḥ sa rākṣasaḥ||39||

40śiro rathe'sya nikṣipya vikṛtānanamūrdhajam
|
prāṇadadbhairavaṃ nādaṃ prāvṛṣīva balāhakaḥ||40||

41abravīcca tato rājanduryodhanamidaṃ vacaḥ
|
eṣa te nihato bandhustvayā dṛṣṭo'sya vikramaḥ||41||

42punardraṣṭāsi karṇasya niṣṭhāmetāṃ tathā''tmanaḥ
|
‘svadharmamarthaṃ kāmaṃ ca tritayaṃ yo'bhivāñchati||42||

43riktapāṇirna paśyeta rājānaṃ brāhmaṇaṃ striyam'
|
tiṣṭhasva tāvatsuprīto yāvatkarṇaṃ vadhāmyaham||43||

44evamuktvā tataḥ prāyātkarṇaṃ prati nareśvara
|
kirañcharagaṇāṃstīkṣṇān ruṣito raṇamūrdhani||44||

45tataḥ samabhavadyuddhaṃ ghorarūpaṃ bhayānakam
|
vismāpanaṃ mahārāja nararākṣasayormṛdhe||45||
