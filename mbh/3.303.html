Mahābhārata 3.303 (Āraṇyakaparvan)

1janamejaya uvāca|
ki tadguhyaṃ na cākhyātaṃ karṇāyehoṣṇaraśminā
|
kīdṛśe kuṇḍale te ca kavacaṃ caiva kīdṛśam||1||

2kutaśca kavacaṃ tasya kuṇḍale caiva sattama
|
etadicchāmyahaṃ śrotuṃ tanme brūhi tapodhana||2||

3vaiśampāyana uvāca|
ayaṃ rājan bravīmyetat tasya guhāṃ vibhāvasoḥ
|
yādṛśe kuṇḍale te ca kavacaṃ caiva yādṛśam||3||

4kuntibhojaṃ purā rājan brāhmaṇaḥ paryupasthitaḥ
|
tigmatejā mahāprāṃśuḥ śmaśrudaṇḍajaṭādharaḥ||4||

5darśanīyo'navadyāṅgastejasā prajvalanniva
|
madhupiṅgo madhuravāk tapaḥsvādhyāyabhūṣaṇaḥ||5||

6sa rājānaṃ kuntibhojamabravīt sumahātapāḥ
|
bhikṣāmicchāmi vai bhoktuṃ tava gehe vimatsara||6||

7na me vyalīkaṃ kartavyaṃ tvayā vā tava cānugaiḥ
|
evaṃ vatsyāmi te gehe yadi te rocate'nagha||7||

8yathākāmaṃ ca gaccheyamāgaccheyaṃ tathaiva ca
|
śayyāsane ca me rājannāparādhyeta kaścana||8||

9tamabravīt kuntibhojaḥ prītiyuktamidaṃ vacaḥ
|
evamastu paraṃ ceti punaścainamathābravīt||9||

10mama kanyā mahāprājña pṛthā nāma yaśasvinī
|
śīlavṛttānvitā sādhvī niyatā caiva bhācinī||10||

11upasthāsyati sā tvāṃ vai pūjayā'navamanya ca
|
tasyāśca śīlavṛttena tuṣṭiṃ samupayāsyasi||11||

12evamuktvā tu taṃ vipramabhipūjya yathāvidhi
|
uvāca kanyāmabhyetya pṛthāṃ pṛthulalocanām||12||

13ayaṃ vatse mahābhāgo brāhmaṇo vastumicchati
|
mama gehe mayā cāsya tathetyevaṃ pratiśrutam||13||

14tvayi vatse parāśvasya brāhmaṇasyābhirādhanam
|
tanme vākyamamithyā tvaṃ kartumarhasi karhicit||14||

15ayaṃ tapasvī bhagavān svādhyāyaniyato dvijaḥ
|
yadyadbrūyānmahātejāstattaddeyamamatsarāt||15||

16brāhmaṇo hi paraṃ tejo brāhmaṇo hi paraṃ tapaḥ
|
brāhmaṇānāṃ namaskāraiḥ sūryo divi virājate||16||

17amānayan hi mānārhān vātāpiśca mahāsuraḥ
|
nihato brahmadaṇḍena tālajaṅghastathaiva ca||17||

18so'yaṃ vatse mahābhāra āhitastvayi sāmpratam
|
tvaṃ sadā niyatā kuryā brāhmaṇasyābhirādhanam||18||

19jānāmi praṇidhānaṃ te bālyātprabhṛti nandini
|
brāhmaṇeṣviha sarveṣu gurubandhuṣu caiva ha||19||

20tathā preṣyeṣu sarveṣu mitrasambandhimātṛṣu
|
mayi caiva yathāvat tvaṃ sarvamāvṛtya vartase||20||

21na hyatuṣṭo jano'stīha pure cāntaḥpure ca te
|
samyagvṛttyā'navadyāṅgi tava bhṛtyajaneṣvapi||21||

22sandeṣṭatyāṃ tu manye tvāṃ dvijātiṃ kopanaṃ prati
|
pṛthe bāleti kṛtvā vai sutā cāsi mameti ca||22||

23vṛṣṇīnāṃ ca kule jātā śūrasya dayitā sutā
|
dṛtā prītimatā mahyaṃ pitrā bālā purā svayam||23||

24vasudevasya bhaginī sutānāṃ pravarā mama
|
agryamagre pratijñāya tenāsi duhitā mama||24||

25tādṛśe hi kule jātā kule caiva vivardhitā
|
sukhāt sukhamanuprāptā hradāddhrādāmivāgatā||25||

26dauṣkuleyā viśeṣeṇa kathañcit pragrahaṃ gatāḥ
|
bālabhāvādvikurvanti prāyaśaḥ pramadāḥ śubhe||26||

27pṛthe rājakule janma rūpaṃ cāpi tavādbhutam
|
tena tenāsi sampannā samupetā ca bhāvinī||27||

28sā tvaṃ darpaṃ parityajya dambhaṃ mānaṃ ca bhāvini
|
ārādhya varadaṃ vipraṃ śreyasā yokṣyase pṛthe||28||

29evaṃ prāpsyasi kalyāṇi kalyāṇamanaghe dhruvam
|
kopite ca dvijaśreṣṭhe kṛtsnaṃ dahyeta me kulam||29||
