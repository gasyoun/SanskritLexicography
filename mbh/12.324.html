Mahābhārata 12.324 (Śāntiparvan)

1bhīṣma uvāca|
sa labdhvā paramaṃ devādvaraṃ satyavatīsutaḥ
|
araṇī sahite gṛhya mamanthāgnicikīrṣayā||1||

2atha rūpaṃ paraṃ rājanbibhratīṃ svena tejasā
|
ghṛtācīṃ nāmāpsarasamapaśyadbhagavānṛṣiḥ||2||

3ṛṣirapsarasaṃ dṛṣṭvā sahasā kāmamohitaḥ
|
abhavadbhagavānvyāso vane tasminyudhiṣṭhira||3||

4sā ca dṛṣṭvā tadā vyāsaṃ kāmasaṃvignamānasam
|
śukī bhūtvā mahārāja ghṛtācī samupāgamat||4||

5sa tāmapsarasaṃ dṛṣṭvā rūpeṇānyena saṃvṛtām
|
śarīrajenānugadaḥ sarvagātrātigena ha||5||

6sa tu dhairyeṇa mahatā nigṛhṇanhṛcchayaṃ muniḥ
|
na śaśāka niyantuṃ tadvyāsa: pravisṛtaṃ manaḥ||6||

7bhāvitvāccaiva bhāvasya ghṛtācyā vapuṣā hṛtaḥ
|
yatnānniyacchatastasya muneragnicikīrṣayā||7||

8araṇyāmeva sahasā tasya śukramavāpatat
|
so'viśaṃkena manasā tathaiva dvijasattamaḥ||8||

9araṇī mamantha brahmarṣistasyāṃ jajñe śuko nṛpa
|
śukre nirmathyamāne sa śuko jajñe mahātapāḥ||9||

10paramarṣirmahāyogī araṇīgarbhasaṃbhavaḥ
|
yathā'dhvare samiddho'gnirbhāti havyamudāvahan||10||

11tathārūpa: śuko jajñe prajvalanniva tejasā
|
bibhratpituśca kauravya rūpavarṇamanuttamam||11||

12babhau tadā bhāvitātmā vidhūma iva pāvakaḥ
|
taṃ gaṅgā saritāṃ śreṣṭhā merupṛṣṭhe janeśvara||12||

13svarūpiṇī tadābhyetya tarpayāmāsa vāriṇā
|
antarikṣācca kauravya daṇḍaḥ kṛṣṇājinaṃ ca ha||13||

14papāta bhūmiṃ rājendra śukasyārthe mahātmanaḥ
|
jegīyante sma gandharvā nanṛtuścāpsarogaṇāḥ||14||

15devadundubhayaścaiva prāvādyanta mahāsvanāḥ
|
viśvāvasuśca gandharvastathā tumburunāradau||15||

16hāhāhūhūśca gandharvau tuṣṭuvuḥ śukasaṃbhavam
|
tatra śakrapurogāśca lokapālāḥ samāgatāḥ||16||

17devā devarṣayaścaiva tathā brahmarṣayo'pi ca
|
divyāni sarvapuṣpāṇi pravavarṣa ca mārutaḥ||17||

18jaṅgamājaṅgamaṃ caiva prahṛṣṭamabhavajjagat
|
taṃ mahātmā svayaṃ prītyā devyā saha mahādyutiḥ||18||

19jātamātraṃ muneḥ putraṃ vidhinopānayattadā
|
tasya deveśvaraḥ śakro divyamadbhutadarśanam||19||

20dadau kamaṇḍaluṃ prītyā devavāsāṃsi vā vibho
|
haṃsāśca śatapatrāśca sārasāśca sahasraśaḥ||20||

21pradakṣiṇamavartanta śukāścāṣāśca bhārata
|
āraṇeyastato divyaṃ prāpya janma mahādyutiḥ||21||

22tatraivovāsa medhāvī vratacārī samāhitaḥ
|
utpannamātraṃ taṃ vedāḥ sarahasyāḥ sasaṃgrahāḥ||22||

23upatasthurmahārāja yathā'sya pitaraṃ tathā
|
bṛhaspatiṃ ca vavre sa vedavedāṅgabhāṣyavit||23||

24upādhyāyaṃ mahārāja dharmamevānucintayan
|
so'dhītya nikhilānvedānsarahasyānsasaṃgrahān||24||

25itihāsaṃ ca kārtsnyena rājaśāstrāṇi vā vibho
|
gurave dakṣiṇāṃ dattvā samāvṛtto mahāmuniḥ||25||

26ugraṃ tapaḥ samārebhe brahmacārī samāhitaḥ
|
devatānāmṛṣīṇāṃ ca bālye'pi sa mahātapāḥ
|
saṃmantraṇīyo mānyaśca jñānena tapasā tathā||26||

27na tvasya ramate buddhirāśrameṣu narādhipa
|
triṣu gārhasthyamūleṣu mokṣadharmānudarśinaḥ||27||
