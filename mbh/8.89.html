Mahābhārata 8.89 (Karṇaparvan)

1sañjaya uvāca|
tau śaṅkhabherīninade samṛddhe samīyatuḥ śvetahayau narāgryau
|
vaikartanaḥ sūtaputro'rjunaśca durmantrite tava putrasya rājan ||1||

2yathā gajau haimavatau prabhinnau pravṛddhadantāviva vāsitārthe
|
tathā samājagmaturugravīryau dhanañjayaścādhirathiśca vīrau ||2||

3balāhakeneva mahābalāhako yadṛcchayā vā giriṇā yathā giriḥ
|
tathā dhanurjyātalaneminiḥsvanaiḥ samīyatustāviṣuvarṣavarṣiṇau ||3||

4pravṛddhaśṛṅgadrumavīrudoṣadhī pravṛddhanānāvidhānirjharaukasau
|
yathā'calau vā calitau mahābalau tathā mahāstrairitaretaraṃ hataḥ ||4||

5sa sannipātastu tayormahānabhūtsureśavairocanayoryathā purā
|
śarairvinunnāṅganiyantṛvāhayoḥ suduḥsaho'nyaiḥ kaṭuśoṇitodakaḥ ||5||

6prabhūtapadmotpalamatsyakacchapau mahāhradau pakṣigaṇairivāvṛtau
|
susannikṛṣṭāvaniloddhatau yathā tathā rathau tau dhvajinau samīyatuḥ ||6||

7ubhau mahendrasya samānavikramāvubhau mahendrapratimau mahārathau
|
mahendravajrapratimaiśca sāyakairmahendravṛtrāviva saṃprajaghnutuḥ ||7||

8sanāgapattyaśvarathe śubhe bale vicitravarmābharaṇāmbarāyudhe
|
cakampaturvismayanīyarūpe viyadgatāścārjunakarṇasaṃyuge ||8||

9bhujāḥ savastrāṅgulayaḥ samucchritāḥ sasiṃhanādairhṛṣitairdidṛkṣubhiḥ
|
yadajuno matta iva dvipo dvipaṃ samabhyayādādhirathiṃ jighāṃsayā ||9||

10udakrośansomakāstatra pārthaṃ puraḥsarāścārjuna bhindhi karṇam
|
chindhyasya mūrdhānamalaṃ cireṇa śraddhāṃ ca rājyāddhṛtarāṣṭrasūnoḥ ||10||

11tathā'smākaṃ bahavastatra yodhāḥ karṇaṃ tathā yāhi yāhītyavocan
|
jahyarjunaṃ karṇa śaraiḥ sutīkṣṇaiḥ punarvanaṃ yāntu cirāya pārthāḥ ||11||

12tathā karṇaḥ prathamaṃ tatra pārthaṃ maheṣubhirdaśabhiḥ pratyavidhyat
|
taṃ cārjunaḥ pratyaviddhyacchitāgraiḥ kakṣāntare daśabhiḥ saṃprahasya ||12||

13parasparaṃ tau viśikhaiḥ supuṃkhaistatakṣatuḥ sūtaputro'rjunaśca
|
parasparaṃ tau bibhidurvimarde subhīmamabhyāpatatuśca hṛṣṭau ||13||

14tato'rjunaḥ prāsṛjadugradhanvā bhujāvubhau gāṇḍivaṃ cānumṛjya
|
nārācanālīkavarāhakarṇān kṣurāṃstathā sāñjalikārdhacandrān ||14||

15te sarvataḥ samakīryanta rājan pārtheṣavaḥ karṇarathaṃ viśantaḥ
|
avāṅmukhāḥ pakṣigaṇā dinānte viśanti ketārthamivāśu vṛkṣam ||15||

16yānarjunaḥ sabhrukuṭīkaṭākṣaṃ karṇāya rājannasṛjajjitāriḥ
|
tānsāyakairgrasate sūtaputraḥ kṣiptān kṣiptān‌ pāṇḍavasyāśu saṃghān ||16||

17tato'stramāgneyamamitrasādhanaṃ mumoca karṇāya mahendrasunuḥ
|
bhūmyantarikṣe ca diśo'rkamārgaṃ prāvṛttya deho'sya babhūva dīptaḥ ||17||

18yodhāśca sarve jvalitāmbarā bhṛśaṃ pradudruvustatra vidagdhavasrāḥ
|
śabdaśca ghoro'tibabhūva tatra yathā vane veṇuvanasya dahyataḥ ||18||

19tadvīkṣya karṇo jvalanāstramudyataṃ sa vāruṇaṃ tatpraśamārthamāhave
|
samutsṛjan sūtasutaḥ pratāpavān sa tena vahniṃ śamayāṃbabhūva ||19||

20balāhakaughaśca diśastarasvī cakāra sarvāstimireṇa saṃvṛtāḥ
|
tato dharitrīdharatulyarodhasaḥ samantato vai parivārya vāriṇā ||20||

21taiścātivegātsa tathāvidho'pi nītaḥ śamaṃ vahniratipracaṇḍaḥ
|
balāhakaireva digantarāṇi vyāptāni sarvāṇi yathā nabhaśca ||21||

22tathā ca sarvāstimireṇa vai diśo meghairvṛtā na pradṛśyeta kiṃcit
|
athāpovāhyābhrasaṃghānsamastān vāyavyāstreṇāpatataḥ sa karṇāt ||22||

23tato'pyastraṃ dayitaṃ devarājñaḥ prāduścakre vajramatiprabhāvam
|
gāṇḍīvaṃ jyāṃ viśikhāṃścānumaṃtrya dhanañjayaḥ śatrubhirapradhṛṣyaḥ ||23||

24tataḥ kṣuraprāñjalikārdhacandrā nālīkanārācavarāhakarṇāḥ
|
gāṇḍīvataḥ prādurāsansutīkṣṇāḥ sahasraśo vajrasamānavegāḥ ||24||

25te karṇamāsādya mahāprabhāvāḥ sutejanā gārdhrapatrāḥ suvegāḥ
|
gātreṣu sarveṣu hayeṣu cāpi śarāsane yugacakre dhvaje ca ||25||

26nirbhidya tūrṇaṃ viviśuḥ sutīkṣṇāstārkṣyatrastā bhūmimivoragāste
|
śarācitāṅgo rudhirārdragātraḥ karṇastadā roṣavivṛttanetraḥ ||26||

27dṛḍhajyamānāmya samudraghoṣaṃ prāduścakre bhārgavāstraṃ mahātmā
|
mahendraśastrābhimukhānvimuktāṃśchittvā karṇaḥ pāṇḍavasyeṣusaṃghān ||27||

28tasyāstramastreṇa nihatya so'tha jaghāna saṃkhye rathanāgapattīn
|
amṛṣyamāṇaśca mahendrakarmā mahāraṇe bhārgavāstrapratāpāt ||28||

29pañcālānāṃ pravarāṃścāpi yodhān krodhāviṣṭaḥ sūtaputrastarasvī
|
bāṇairvivyādhāhave supramuktaiḥ śilāśitairukmapuṃkhaiḥ prasahya ||29||

30tatpañcālāḥ somakāścāpi rājan karṇenājau pīḍyamānāḥ śaraughaiḥ
|
krodhāviṣṭā vivyadhustaṃ samantāttīkṣṇairbāṇaiḥ sūtaputraṃ sametāḥ ||30||

31tān sūtaputro nijaghāna bāṇaiḥ pañcālānāṃ rathanāgāśvasaṃghān
|
abhyardayadbāṇagaṇaiḥ prasahya viddhvā harṣātsaṃgare sūtaputraḥ ||31||

32te bhinnadehā vyasavo nipetuḥ karṇeṣubhirbhūmitale svanantaḥ
|
kruddhena siṃhena yathebhayūthā mahāvane bhīmabalena tadvat ||32||

33pañcālānāṃ pravarānsaṃnihatya prasahya yodhānakhilānadīnaḥ
|
tataḥ sa rājanvirarāja karṇo yathāmbare bhāskara ugraraśmiḥ ||33||

34karṇasya matvā tu jayaṃ tvadīyāḥ parāṃ mudaṃ siṃhanādāṃśca cakruḥ
|
sarve hyamanyanta bhṛśāhatau ca karṇena kṛṣṇāviti kauravendra ||34||

35tattādṛśaṃ prekṣya mahārathasya karṇasya vīryaṃ ca parairasahyam
|
dṛṣṭvā ca karṇena dhanañjayasya tathā''jimadhye nihataṃ tadastram ||35||

36tatastvamarṣī krodhasandīptanetro vātātmajaḥ pāṇinā pāṇimārcchat
|
bhīmo'bravīdarjunaṃ satyasandhamamarṣito niḥśvasañjātamanyuḥ ||36||

37kathaṃ nu pāpo'yamapetadharmaḥ sūtātmajaḥ samare'dya prasahya
|
pañcālānāṃ yodhamukhyānanekānnijaghnivāṃstava jiṣṇo samakṣam ||37||

38pūrvaṃ devairajitaṃ kālakeyaiḥ sākṣātsthāṇorbāhusaṃsparśametya
|
kathaṃ nu tvāṃ sūtaputraḥ kirīṭinnatheṣubhirdaśabhiḥ prāgaviddhyat ||38||

39tvayā kṣiptāṃścāgrasadbāṇasaṃghānāścaryametatpratibhāti me'dya
|
kṛṣṇāparikleśamanusmara tvaṃ yathābravītṣaṇḍhatilān sma vācaḥ ||39||

40rūkṣāḥ sutīkṣṇāśca hi pāpabuddhiḥ sūtātmajo'yaṃ gatabhīrdurātmā
|
saṃsmṛtya sarvaṃ tadihādya pāpaṃ jahyāśu karṇaṃ yudhi savyasācin ||40||

41kasmādupekṣāṃ kuruṣe kirīṭinnupekṣituṃ nāyamihādya kālaḥ
|
yayā dhṛtyā sarvabhūtānyajaiṣīrgrāsaṃ dadatkhāṇḍave pāvakāya ||41||

42tayā dhṛtyā sūtaputraṃ jahi tvamahaṃ cainaṃ gadayā pothayiṣye
|
athābravīdvāsudevo'pi pārthaṃ dṛṣṭvā ratheṣūnpratihanyamānān ||42||

43amīmṛdatsarvapāte'dya karṇo hyastrairastraṃ kimidaṃ bho kirīṭin
|
sa vīra ki muhyasi nāvadhatse nadantyete kuravaḥ saṃprahṛṣṭāḥ ||43||

44karṇaṃ puraskṛtya vidurhi sarve tavāstramastrairvinipātyamānam
|
yayā dhṛtyā nihataṃ tāmasāstraṃ yuge yuge rākṣasāścāpi ghorāḥ ||44||

45daṃbhodbhavāścāsurāścāhaveṣu tayā dhṛtyā jahi karṇaṃ tvamadya
|
anena cāsya kṣuraneminādya saṃchindhi mūrdhānamareḥ prasahya ||45||

46mayā visṛṣṭena sudarśanena vajreṇa śakro namucerivāreḥ
|
kirātarūpī bhagavānsudhṛtyā tvayā mahātmā paritoṣito'bhūt ||46||

47tāṃ tvaṃ punarvīra dhṛtiṃ gṛhītvā sahānubandhaṃ jahi sūtaputram
|
tato mahīṃ sāgaramekhalāṃ tvaṃ sapattanāṃ grāmavatīṃ samṛddhām ||47||

48prayaccha rājñe nihatārisaṅghāṃ yaśaśca pārthātulamāpnuhi tvam
|
sa evamukto'tibalo mahātmā cakāra buddhiṃ hi vadhāya sautaḥ ||48||

49sa codito bhīmajanārdanābhyāṃ smṛtvā tathā''tmānamavekṣya sarvam
|
ihātmanaścāgamane viditvā prayojanaṃ keśavamityuvāca ||49||

50prāduṣkaromyeṣa mahāstramugraṃ śivāya lokasya vadhāya sauteḥ
|
tanme'nujānātu bhavānsurāśca brahmā bhavo vedavidaśca sarve ||50||

51ityucya devaṃ sa tu savyasācī namaskṛtvā brahmaṇe so'mitātmā
|
taduttamaṃ brāhmamasahyamastraṃ prāduścakre manasā yadvidheyam ||51||

52tadasya hatvā virarāja karṇo muktvā śarānmegha ivāmbudhārāḥ
|
samīkṣya karṇena kirīṭinastu tathā''jimadhye nihataṃ tadastram ||52||

53tato'marṣī balavān krodhadīpto bhīmo'bravīda‌rjunaṃ satyasandham
|
nanu tvāhurveditāraṃ mahāstraṃ brāhmaṃ vidheyaṃ paramaṃ janāstat ||53||

54tasmādanyadyojaya savyasācinniti smokto yo'jayatsavyasācī
|
tato diśaḥ pradiśaścāpi sarvāḥ samāvṛṇotsāyakairbhūritejāḥ ||54||

55gāṇḍīvamuktairbhujagairivograirdivākarāṃśupratimairjvaladbhiḥ
|
sṛṣṭāstu bāṇā bharatarṣabheṇa śataṃ śatānīva suvarṇapuṃkhāḥ ||55||

56prācchādayankarṇarathaṃ kṣaṇena yugāntavahnyarkakaraprakāśāḥ
|
tataśca śūlāni paraśvadhāni cakrāṇi nārācaśatāni caiva ||56||

57niścakramurghoratarāṇi yodhāstato hyahanyanta samantato'pi
|
chinnaṃ śiraḥ kasyacidājimadhye papāta yodhasya parasya kāyāt ||57||

58bhayena so'pyāśu papāta bhūmāvanyaḥ pranaṣṭaḥ patitaṃ vilokya
|
anyasya sāsirnipapāta kṛtto yodhasya bāhuḥ karihastatulyaḥ ||58||

59anyasya savyaḥ saha varmaṇā ca kṣuraprakṛttaḥ patito dharaṇyām
|
evaṃ samastānapi yodhamukhyānvidhvaṃsayāmāsa kirīṭamālī ||59||

60śaraiḥ śarīrāntakaraiḥ sughorairdauryodhanaṃ sainyamaśeṣameva
|
vaikartanenāpi tathā''jimadhye sahasraśo bāṇagaṇā visṛṣṭāḥ ||60||

61te ghoṣiṇaḥ pāṇḍavamabhyupeyuḥ parjanyamuktā iva vāridhārāḥ
|
tataḥ sa kṛṣṇaṃ ca kirīṭinaṃ ca vṛkodaraṃ cāpratimaprabhāvaḥ ||61||

62tribhistribhirbhīmabalo nihatya nanāda ghoraṃ mahatā svareṇa
|
sa karṇabāṇābhihataḥ kirīṭī bhīmaṃ tathā prekṣya janārdanaṃ ca ||62||

63amṛṣyamāṇaḥ punareva pārthaḥ śarāndaśāṣṭau ca samudbavarha
|
sa ketumekena śareṇa viddhvā śalyaṃ caturbhistribhireva karṇam ||63||

64tataḥ sa muktairdaśabhirjaghāna sabhāpatiṃ kāñcanavarmanaddham
|
sa rājaputro viśirā vibāhurvivājisūto vidhanurviketuḥ ||64||

65hato rathāgrādapatatsa rugṇaḥ paraśvadhaiḥ śāla ivāvakṛttaḥ
|
punaśca karṇaṃ tribhiraṣṭabhiśca dvābhyāṃ caturbhirdaśabhiśca viddhvā ||65||

66catuḥśatān dviradānsāyudhānvai hatvā rathānaṣṭaśatāñjaghāna
|
sahasraśo'śvāṃśca punaḥ sa sādīnaṣṭau sahasrāṇi ca pattivīrān ||66||

67karṇaṃ sasūtaṃ sarathaṃ saketumadṛśyamañjogatibhiḥ pracakre
|
athākrośan kuravo vadhyamānā dhanañjayenādhirathiṃ samantāt ||67||

68muñcābhividhyārjunamāśu karṇa bāṇaiḥ purā hanti kurūnsamagrān
|
sa coditaḥ sarvayatnena karṇo mumoca bāṇānsubahūnabhīkṣṇam ||68||

69te pāṇḍupañcālagaṇānnijaghnurmarmacchidaḥ śoṇitapāṃsudigdhāḥ
|
tāvuttamau sarvadhanurdharāṇāṃ mahābalau sarvasapatnasāhau ||69||

70nijaghnatuścāhitasainyamugramanyonyamapyastravidau mahāstraiḥ
|
athopayātastvarito didṛkṣurmantrauṣadhībhirnirujo viśalyaḥ ||70||

71kṛttaḥ suhṛdbhirbhiṣajāṃ variṣṭhairyudhiṣṭhirastatra suvarṇavarmā
|
tathopayātaṃ yudhi dharmarājaṃ dṛṣṭvā mudā sarvabhūtānyanandan ||71||

72rāhorvimuktaṃ vimalaṃ samagraṃ candraṃ yathaivābhyuditaṃ tathaiva
|
dṛṣṭvā tu mukhyāvatha yudhyamānau didṛkṣavaḥ śūravarāvarighnau ||72||

73karṇaṃ ca pārthaṃ ca vilokayantaḥ khasthā mahīsthāśca janāvatasthuḥ
|
sa kārmukajyātalasannipātaḥ sumuktabāṇastumulo babhūva ||73||

74ghnatostathā'nyonyamiṣupravekairdhanañjayasyādhiratheśca tatra
|
tato dhanurjyā sahasā'tikṛṣṭā sughoṣamacchidyata pāṇḍavasya ||74||

75tasmin kṣaṇe pāṇḍavaṃ sūtaputraḥ samācinot kṣudrakāṇāṃ śatena
|
nirmuktasarpapratimairabhīkṣṇaṃ tailapradhautaiḥ khagapatravājaiḥ ||75||

76ṣaṣṭyā bibhedāśu ca vāsudevamanantaraṃ phālgunamaṣṭabhiśca
|
pūṣātmajo marmasu nirbibheda marutsutaṃ cāyutaśaḥ śarāgryaiḥ ||76||

77kṛṣṇaṃ ca pārthaṃ ca tathā dhvajaṃ ca pārthānujānsomakānpātayaṃśca
|
prācchādayaṃste viśikhaiḥ pṛṣatkairjīmūtasaṃghā nabhasīva sūryam ||77||

78āgacchatastānviśikhairanekairvyaṣṭaṃbhayatsūtaputraḥ kṛtāsraḥ
|
tairastamastraṃ vinihatya sarvaṃ jaghāna teṣāṃ rathavājināgān ||78||

79tathā tu sainyapravarāṃśca rājannabhyardayanmārgaṇaiḥ sūtaputraḥ
|
te bhinnadehā vyasavo nipetuḥ karṇeṣubhirbhūmitale svanantaḥ ||79||

80siṃhena kruddhena yathāśvayūthyā mahābalā bhīmabalena tadvat
|
punaśca pāñcālavarāstathā'nye tadantare karṇadhanañjayābhyām ||80||

81praskandanto balinā sādhumuktaiḥ karṇena bāṇairnihatāḥ prasahya
|
jayaṃ matvā vipulaṃ vai tvadīyāstalānnijaghnuḥ siṃhanādāṃśca neduḥ ||81||

82sarve hyamanyanta vaśe kṛtau tau karṇena kṛṣṇāviti te vimarde
|
tato dhanurjyāmavanāmya śīghraṃ śarānastānādhirathervidhamya ||82||

83susaṃkhdhaḥ karṇaśarakṣatāṅgo raṇe pārthaḥ kauravān pratyagṛhṇāt
|
jyāṃ cānumṛjyābhyahanattalatre bāṇāndhakāraṃ sahasā ca cakre ||83||

84karṇaṃ ca śalyaṃ ca kurūṃśca sarvān bāṇairavidhyatprasabhaṃ kirīṭī
|
na pakṣiṇo babhramurantarikṣe tadā mahāstreṇa kṛte'ndhakāre ||84||

85vāyurviyatsthairīrito bhūtasaṅghairuvāha divyaḥ surabhistadānīm
|
śalyaṃ ca pārtho daśabhiḥ pṛṣatkairbhṛśaṃ tanutre prahasannavidhyat ||85||

86tataḥ karṇaṃ dvādaśabhiḥ sumuktairviddhvā punaḥ saptabhirabhyaviddhyat
|
sa pārthabāṇāsanavegamuktairdṛḍhāhataḥ patribhirugravegaiḥ ||86||

87vibhinnagātraḥ kṣatajokṣitāṅgaḥ karṇo babhau rudra ivātateṣuḥ
|
prakrīḍamāno'tha śmaśānamadhye raudre muhūrte rudhirārdragātraḥ ||87||

88tatastribhistaṃ tridaśādhipopamaṃ śarairbibhedādhirathirdhanañjayam
|
śarāṃśca pañca jvalitānivoragān praveśayāmāsa jighāṃsayā'cyutam ||88||

89te varma bhittvā puruṣottamasya suvarṇacitrā nyapatansumuktāḥ
|
vegena gāmāviviśuḥ suvegāḥ snātvā ca karṇādvimukhāḥ pratīyuḥ ||89||

90tān pañca bhallairdaśabhiḥ sumuktaistridhā tridhaikaikamathoccakarta
|
dhanañjayāstrairnyapatan pṛthivyāṃ mahāhayastakṣakaputrapakṣāḥ ||90||

91tataḥ prajajvāla kirīṭamālī krodhena kakṣaṃ pradahannivāgniḥ
|
tathā vinunnāṅgamavekṣya kṛṣṇaṃ sarveṣubhiḥ karṇabhujaprasṛṣṭaiḥ ||91||

92sa karṇamākarṇavikṛṣṭasṛṣṭaiḥ śaraiḥ śarīrāntakarairjvaladbhiḥ
|
marmasvavidhyatsa cacāla duḥkhāddaivā‌davātiṣṭhata dhairyabuddhiḥ ||92||

93tataḥ śaraughaiḥ pradiśo diśaśca raveḥ prabhā karṇarathaśca rājan
|
adṛśyamāsītkupite dhanañjaye tuṣāranīhāravṛtaṃ yathā nabhaḥ ||93||

94sa cakrarakṣānatha pādakṣān puraḥsarān pṛṣṭhagopāṃśca sarvān
|
duryodhanenānumatānarighnaḥ samudyatānsarathānsārabhūtān ||94||

95dvisāhasrānsamare savyasācī kurupravīrānṛṣabhaḥ kurūṇām
|
kṣaṇena sarvānsarathāśvasūtānnināya rājan kṣayamekavīraḥ ||95||

96tato'palāyanta vihāya karṇaṃ tavātmajāḥ kuravo ye'vaśiṣṭāḥ
|
hatānapākīrya śarakṣatāṃśca lālapyamānāṃstanayān pitṝṃśca ||96||
