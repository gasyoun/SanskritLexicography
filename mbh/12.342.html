Mahābhārata 12.342 (Śāntiparvan)

1arjuna uvāca |
agnīṣomau kathaṃ pūrvamekayonī pravartitau
|
eṣa me saṃśayo jātastaṃ chindhi madhusūdana||1||

2śrībhagavānuvāca |
hanta te vartayiṣyāmi purāṇaṃ pāṇḍunandana
|
ātmatejodbhavaṃ pārtha śṛṇuṣvaikamanā mama||2||

3saṃprakṣālanakāle'tikrānte caturyugasahasrānte avyakte sarvabhūte pralaye sarvabhūtasthā varajaṅgame
|
jyotirdharaṇivāyurahite andhe tamasi jalaikārṇave loke||3||

4āpa ityevaṃ brahmabhūtasaṃjñake'dvitīye pratiṣṭhite||4||

5na vai rātryāṃ na divase na sati nāsati na vyakte na cāpyavyakte vyavasthite||5||

6evamasyāṃ vyavasthāyāṃ nārāyaṇaguṇāśrayādajarāmarādanindriyādagrāhyādasambhavātsatyādahiṃsrāllalāmādvividhapravṛttiviśeṣāda- vairādakṣayādamarādajarādamūrtitaḥ sarvavyāpinaḥ sarvakartuḥ śāśvatastamasaḥ puruṣaḥ prādurbhūto hariravyayaḥ||6||

7nidarśanamapi hyatra bhavati||7||

8nāsīdaho na rātrirāsīnna sadāsīnnāsadāsīttama eva purastādabhavadviśvarūpam
|
sā viśvarūpasya rajanī hi evamasyārtho'nubhāṣyaḥ||8||

9yo brāhmaṇamukhe juhotīti kṛtvā bravīmi bhūtasargaḥ kṛto brahmaṇā bhūtāni ca pratiṣṭhāpya trailokyaṃ dhāryata iti mantravādo'pi hi bhavati||9||

10tvamagne yajñānāṃ hotā viśveṣāṃ hito devānāṃ mānuṣāṇāṃ ca jagata iti||10||

11nidarśanaṃ ecātra bhavati viśveṣāmagne yajñānāṃ tvaṃ hoteti
|
tvaṃ hito devairmanuṣyairjagata iti||11||

12agnirhi yajñānāṃ hotā kartā sa cāgnirbrahma||12||

13na hyṛte mantrāṇāṃ havanamasti na vinā puruṣaṃ tapaḥ sambhavati|| havirmantrāṇāṃ saṃpūjā vidyate devamānuṣaṛṣīṇāmanena tvaṃ hoteti niyuktaḥ|| ye ca mānuṣahotrādhikārāste ca brāhmaṇasya hi yājanaṃ vidhīyate na kṣatravaiśyayordvijātyostasmādbrāhmaṇā hyagnibhūtā yajñānudvahanti
|
yajñāste devāṃstarpayanti pṛthivīṃ bhāvayanti śatapathe'pi hi brāhmaṇamukhe bhavati||13||

14agnau samiddhe sa juhoti yo vidvān brāhmaṇamukhenāhutiṃ juhoti||14||

15evamapyagnibhūtā brāhmaṇā vidvāṃso'gniṃ bhāvayanti
|
agnirviṣṇuḥ sarvabhūtānyanupraviśya prāṇāndhārayati||15||

16api cātra sanatkumāragītāḥ ślokā bhavanti brahmā viśvaṃ sṛjatpūrvaṃ sarvādirniravaskṛtam
|
brahmaghoṣairdivaṃ gacchantyamarā brahmayonayaḥ||16||

17brāhmaṇānāṃ matirvākyaṃ karma śraddhāṃ tapāṃsi ca
|
dhārayanti mahīṃ dyāṃ ca śaikyo vāgamṛtaṃ tathā||17||

18nāsti satyātparo dharmo nāsti mātṛsamo guruḥ
|
brāhmaṇebhyaḥ paraṃ nāsti pretya ceha ca bhūtaye||18||

19naiṣāmukṣā vahati nota vāhā na gargaro mathnati sampradāne
|
apadhvastā dasyubhūtā bhavanti yeṣāṃ rāṣṭre brāhmaṇā vṛttihīnāḥ||19||

20vedapurāṇetihāsaprāmāṇyānnārāyaṇamukhodgatāḥ sarvātmānaḥ sarvakartāraḥ sarvabhāvāśca brāhmaṇāśca||20||

21vāksaṃyamakāle hitasya varapradasya devadevasya brāhmaṇāḥ prathamaṃ prādurbhūtā brāhmaṇebhyaśca śeṣā varṇāḥ prādurbhūtāḥ||21||

22itthaṃ ca surāsuraviśiṣṭā brāhmaṇā ya eva mayā brahmabhūtena purā svayamevotpāditāḥ surāsuramaharṣayo bhūtaviśeṣāḥ sthāpitā nigṛhītāśca||22||

23ahalyādharṣaṇanimittaṃ hi gautamāddhariśmaśrutāmindraḥ prāptaḥ kauśikanimittaṃ cendro muṣkaviyogaṃ meṣavṛṣaṇatvaṃ cāvāpa||23||

24aśvinorgrahapratiṣedhodyatavajrasya purandarasya cyavanena stambhitau bāhū||24||

25kratuvadhaprāptamanyunā ca dakṣeṇa bhūyastapasā cātmānaṃ sayojya netrākṛtiranyā lalāṭe rudrasyotpāditā||25||

26tripuravadhārthaṃ dīkṣāmupagatasya rudrasya uśanasā jaṭā: śirasa utkṛtya prayuktāstataḥ prādurbhūtā bhujagāstairasya bhujagaiḥ pīḍyamānaḥ kaṇṭho nīlatāmupagataḥ, pūrve ca manvantare svāyambhuve nārāyaṇahastagrahaṇānnīlakaṇṭhatvameva ca||26||

27amṛtotpādanapuraścaraṇatāmupagatasyāṅgiraso bṛhaspaterupaspṛśato na prasādaṃ gatavatyaḥ kilāpaḥ, atha bṛhaspatirapāṃ cukrodha yasmānmamopaspṛśataḥ kaluṣībhūtā na ca prasādamupagatāstasmādadyaprabhṛti jhaṣamakarakacchapajantubhiḥ kaluṣī bhavateti, tadā prabhṛtyāpo yādobhiḥ saṃkīrṇāḥ sampravṛttāḥ||27||

28viśvarūpo hi vai tvāṣṭraḥ purohito devānāmāsīt, svasrīyo'surāṇāṃ sa pratyakṣaṃ devebhyo bhāgamadātparokṣamasurebhyaḥ||28||

29atha hiraṇyakaśipuṃ puraskṛtya viśvarūpamātaraṃ svasāramasurā varamayācanta he svasarayante putrastvāṣṭro viśvarūpastriśirā devānāṃ purohitaḥ pratyakṣaṃ devebhyo bhāgamadāt parokṣamasmākaṃ tato devā vardhante vayaṃ kṣīyāmastadenaṃ tvaṃ vārayitumarhasi tathā yathāsmān bhajediti||29||

30atha viśvarūpaṃ nandanavanamupagataṃ mātovāca putra kiṃ parapakṣavardhanastvaṃ mātulapakṣaṃ nāśayasi, nārhasyevaṃ kartumiti sa viśvarūpo māturvākyamanatikramaṇīyamiti matvā saṃpūjya hiraṇyakaśipumagāt||30||

31hairaṇyagarbhācca vasiṣṭhāddhiraṇyakaśipuḥ śāpaṃ prāptavānyasmāttvayānyo vṛto hotā tasmādasamāptayajñastvamapūrvātsattvajātādvadhaṃ prāpsyasīti tacchāpadānāddhiraṇyakaśipuḥ prāptavānvadham||31||

32atha viśvarūpo mātṛpakṣavardhano'tyarthaṃ tapasyabhavattasya vratabhaṅgārthamindro bahvīḥśrīmatyopsaraso niyuyoja tāśca dṛṣṭvā manaḥ kṣubhitaṃ tasyābhavattāsu cāpsaraḥsu nacirādeva sakto'bhavatsaktaṃ cainaṃ jñātvā apsarasa ūcurgacchāmahe vayaṃ yathāgatamiti||32||

33tāstvāṣṭra uvāca kva gamiṣyathāsyatāṃ tāvanmayā saha śreyo bhaviṣyantīti tāstamabruvanvayaṃ devastriyo'psarasa indraṃ devaṃ varadaṃ purā prabhaviṣṇuṃ vṛṇīmaha iti||33||

34atha tā viśvarūpo'bravīdadyaiva sendrā devā na bhaviṣyantīti tato mantrān jajāpa tairmantrairavardhata triśirā ekenāsyena sarvalokeṣu yathāvaddvijaiḥ kriyāvadbhiryajñeṣu suhutaṃ somaṃ papāvekenānnamekena sendrāndevānathendrastaṃ vivardhamānaṃ somapānāpyāyitasarvagātraṃ dṛṣṭvā cintāmāpede devaiḥ||34||

35te devāḥ sendrā brahmāṇamabhijagmusta ūcurviśvarūpeṇa sarvayajñeṣu suhutaḥ somaḥ pīyate vayamabhāgāḥ saṃvṛttā asurapakṣo vardhate vayaṃ kṣīyāmastadarhasi no vidhātuṃ śreyo'nantaramiti||35||

36tānbrahmovāca ṛṣirbhārgavastapastapyate dadhīcaḥ sa yācyatāṃ varaṃ sa yathā kalevaraṃ jahyāttathā vidhīyatāṃ tasyāsthibhirvajraṃ kriyatāmiti||36||

37tato devāstatrāgacchanyatra dadhīco bhagavānṛṣistapastepe sendrā devāstaṃ tathābhigamyocurbhagavaṃstapaḥ sukuśalamabhinnaṃ ceti||37||

38tāndadhīca uvāca svāgataṃ bhavaddhya ucyatāṃ kiṃ kriyatāmiti yadvakṣyatha tatkariṣyāmi||38||

39te tamabruvan śarīraparityāgaṃ lokahitārthaṃ bhagavānkartumarhatīti||39||

40atha dadhīcastathaivāvimanāḥ sukhaduḥkhasamo mahāyogī ātmānaṃ samādhāya śarīraparityāgaṃ cakāra||40||

41tasya paramātmanyapasṛte tānyasthīni dhātā saṃgṛhya vajramakarottena vajreṇābhedyenāmapradhṛṣyeṇa brahmāsthisambhūtena viṣṇupraviṣṭenendro viśvarūpaṃ jaghāna śirasāṃ cāsya cchedanamakarottasmādanantaraṃ viśvarūpagātramathanasaṃbhavaṃ tvaṣṭrotpāditamevāriṃ vṛtramindro jaghāna||41||

42tasyāṃ dvaidhībhūtāyāṃ brahmavadhyāyāṃ bhayādindro devarājyaṃ paryatyajadapsu saṃbhavāṃ ca śītalāṃ mānasasarogatāṃ nalinīṃ pratipede tatra caiśvaryayogādaṇumātro bhūtvā bisagranthiṃ praviveśa||42||

43atha brahmavadhyābhayapranaṣṭe trailokyanāthe śacīpatau jagadanīśvaraṃ babhūva devān rajastamaścāviveśa mantrā na prāvartanta maharṣīṇāṃ rakṣāṃsi prādurabhavan brahma cotsādanaṃ jagāmānindrāścābalā lokāḥ supradhṛṣyā babhūvuḥ||43||

44atha devā ṛṣayaścāyuṣaḥ putraṃ nahuṣaṃ devarājye'bhiṣiṣicurnahuṣaḥ pañcabhiḥ śatairjyotiṣāṃ lalāṭe jvaladbhiḥ sarvatejoharaistriviṣṭapaṃ pālayāṃbabhūva||44||

45atha lokāḥ prakṛtimāpedire svasthāśca hṛṣṭāśca babhūvuḥ||45||

46athovāca nahuṣaḥ sarvaṃ māṃ śakropabhuktamupasthitamṛte śacīmiti sa evamuktvā śacīsamīpamagamaduvācaināṃ subhage'hamindro devānāṃ bhajasva māmiti taṃ śacī pratyuvāca prakṛtyā tvaṃ dharmavatsalaḥ somavaṃśodbhavaśca nārhasi parapatnīdharṣaṇaṃ kartumiti||46||

47tāmathovāca nahuṣa aindraṃ padamadhyāste mayā'hamindrasya rājyaratnaharo nātrādharmaḥ kaścittvamindropabhukteti sā tamuvācāsti mama kiñcidvratamaparyavasitaṃ tasyāvabhṛthe tvāmupagamiṣyāmi kaiścidevāhobhiriti sa śacyevamabhihito jagāma ||47||

48atha śacī duḥkhaśokārtā bhartṛdarśanalālasā nahuṣabhayagṛhītā bṛhaspatimupāgacchatsa ca tāmatyudvignāṃ dṛṣṭvaiva dhyānaṃ praviśya bhartṛkāryatatparāṃ jñātvā bṛhaspatiruvācānenaiva vratena tapasā cānvitā devīṃ varadāmupaśrutimāhvaya tadā sā te indraṃ darśayiṣyatīti sā'tha mahāniyamasthitā devīṃ varadāmupaśrutiṃ mantrairāhvayati sopaśrutiḥ śacīsamīpamagāduvāca caināmiyamasmīti tvayā''hūtopasthitā kiṃ te priyaṃ karavāṇīti tāṃ mūrdhnā praṇamyovāca śacī bhagavatyarhasi me bhartāraṃ darśayituṃ tvaṃ satyā ṛtā ceti saināṃ mānasaṃ saro'nayattatrendraṃ bisagranthigatamadarśayat||48||

49tāmatha patnīṃ kṛśāṃ glānāṃ cendro dṛṣṭvā cintayāmbabhūva aho mama duḥkhamidamupagataṃ naṣṭaṃ hi māmiyamanviṣya yatpatnyabhyagamadduḥkhārteti tāmindra uvāca kathaṃ vartayasīti sā tamuvāca nahuṣo māmāhvayati patnīṃ kartuṃ kālaścāsya mā kṛta iti tāmindra uvāca gaccha nahuṣastvayā vācyo'pūrveṇa māmṛṣiyuktena yānena tvamadhirūḍha udvahasveti indrasya mahānti vāhanāni santi mana:priyāṇyadhirūḍhāni mayātvamanyenopayātumarhasīti saivamuktā hṛṣṭā jagāmendro'pi bisagranthimevāviveśa bhūyaḥ||49||

50athendrāṇīmabhyāgatāṃ dṛṣṭvā tāmuvāca nahuṣaḥ pūrṇaḥ sa kāla iti taṃ śacyabravīcchakreṇa yathoktaṃ sa maharṣiyuktaṃ vāhanamadhirūḍhaḥ śacīsamīpamupāgacchat||50||

51atha maitrāvaruṇiḥ kuṃbhayoniragastyaṛṣivaro maharṣīn dhikkriyamāṇāṃstānnahuṣeṇāpaśyat padbhyāṃ ca tamaspṛśat tata: sa nahuṣamabravīdakāryapravṛtta pāpa patasva mahīṃ sarpo bhava yāvadbhūmirgirayaśca tiṣṭheyustāvaditi sa maharṣivākyasamakālameva tasmādyānādavāpatat||51||

52athānindraṃ punastrailokyamabhavat tato devā ṛṣayaśca bhagavantaṃ viṣṇuṃ śaraṇamindrārthe'bhijagmurūcuścainaṃ bhagavannindraṃ brahmahatyābhibhūtaṃ trātumarhasīti tataḥ sa varadastānabravīdaśvamedhaṃ yajñaṃ vaiṣṇavaṃ śakro'bhiyajatāṃ tataḥ svasthānaṃ prāpsyatīti tato devā ṛṣayaścendraṃ nāpaśyanyadā tadā śacīmūcurgaccha subhage indramānayasveti sā punastatsaraḥ samabhyagacchadindraśca tasmātsarasaḥ pratyutthāya bṛhaspatimabhijagāma bṛhaspatiścāśvamedhaṃ mahākratuṃ śakrāyāharat tatra kṛṣṇasāraṅgaṃ medhyamaśvamutsṛjya vāhanaṃ tameva kṛtvā indra marutpatiṃ bṛhaspati: svaṃ sthānaṃ prāpayāmāsa||52||

53tataḥ sa devarāṭ devairṛṣibhiḥ stūyamānastriviṣṭapastho niṣkalmaṣo babhūva ha brahmavadhyāṃ caturṣu sthāneṣu vanitāgnivanaspatigoṣu vyabhajadevamindro brahmatejaḥprabhāvopabṛṃhitaḥ śatruvadhaṃ kṛtvā svaṃ sthānaṃ prāpitaḥ||53||

54ākāśagaṅgāgataśca purā bharadvājo maharṣirupāspṛśattrīn kramān kramatā viṣṇunā'bhyāsādita: sa bharadvājena sasalilena pāṇinorasi tāḍitaḥ salakṣaṇoraskaḥ saṃvṛttaḥ||54||

55bhṛguṇā maharṣiṇā śapto'gniḥ sarvabhakṣatvamupānītaḥ||55||

56aditirvaidevānāmannamapacadetadbhuktvā'surānhaniṣyantīti tatra budho vratacaryāsamāptāvāgacchadaditiṃ cāvocadbhikṣāṃ dehīti tatra devaiḥ pūrvametatprāśyaṃ nānyenetyaditirbhikṣāṃ nādādatha bhikṣāpratyākhyānaruṣitena budhena brahmabhūtenāditiḥ śaptā aditerudare bhaviṣyati vyathā vivasvato dvitīyajanmanyaṇḍasaṃjñitasya aṇḍaṃ māturadityā māritaṃ sa mārtaṇḍo vivasvānabhavacchrāddhadevaḥ||56||

57dakṣasya yā vai duhitaraḥ ṣaṣṭirāsaṃstābhyaḥ kaśyapāya trayodaśaprādāddaśa dharmāya daśa manave saptaviṃśatimindave tāsu tulyāsu nakṣatrākhyāṃ gatāsu somo rohiṇyāmabhyadhikaṃ prītimānabhūttatastāḥ śiṣṭāḥ palya īrṣyāvatyaḥ pituḥ samīpaṃ gatvemamarthaṃ śaśaṃsurbhagavannasmāsu tulyaprabhāvāsu somo rohiṇīṃ pratyadhikaṃ bhajatīti so'bravīdyakṣmainamāviśyateti dakṣaśāpātsomaṃ rājānaṃ yakṣmā viveśa sa yakṣmaṇā''viṣṭo dakṣamagāddakṣaścainamabravīnna samaṃ vartayasīti tatrarṣayaḥ somamabruvankṣīyase yakṣmaṇā paścimāyāṃ diśi samudre hiraṇyasarastīrthaṃ tatra gatvā ātmānamabhiṣecayasvetyathāgacchatsomastatra hiraṇyasarastīrthaṃ gatvā cātmanaḥ secanamakarot snātvā cātmānaṃ pāpmano mokṣayāmāsa tatra cāvabhāsitastīrthe yadā somastadā prabhṛti ca tīrthaṃ tatprabhāsamiti nāmnā khyātaṃ babhūva||57||

58tacchāpādadyāpi kṣīyate somo'māvāsyāntarasthaḥ paurṇamāsīmātre'dhiṣṭhito meghalekhāpraticchannaṃ vapudarśayati meghasadṛśaṃ varṇamagamattadasya śaśa lakṣma vimalamabhavat||58||

59sthūlaśirā maharṣirmeroḥ prāguttare digvibhāge tapastepe tatastasya tapastapyamānasya sarvagandhavahaḥ śucirvāyurvāyamānaḥ śarīramaspṛśatsa tapasā tāpitaśarīraḥ kṛśo vāyunopavījyamāno hṛdaye paritoṣamagamattatra kila tasyānilavyajanakṛtaparitoṣasya sadyo vanaspatayaḥ puṣpaśobhāṃ nidarśitavanta iti sa etān śaśāpa na sarvakālaṃ puṣpavanto bhaviṣyatheti||59||

60nārāyaṇo lokahitārthaṃ vaḍavāmukho nāma purā maharṣirbabhūva merau tapastapyataḥ samudra āhūto nāgatastenāmarṣitenātmagātroṣmaṇā samudraḥ stimitajalaḥ kṛtaḥ svedaprasyandanasadṛśaścāpya lavaṇabhāvo janitaḥ||60||

61uktaścāpyapeyo bhaviṣyasyetacca te toyaṃ vaḍavāmukhasaṃjñitena pepīyamānaṃ madhuraṃ bhaviṣyati tadetadadyāpi vaḍavāmukhasaṃjñitenānuvartinā toyaṃ samudrātpīyate||61||

62himavato girerduhitaramumāṃ kanyāṃ rudraścakame bhṛgurapi ca maharṣirhimavantamāgatyābravīt kanyāmimāṃ me dehīti tamabravīddhimavānabhilakṣito varo rudra iti tamabravīdbhṛguryasmāttvayā'haṃ kanyāvaraṇakṛtabhāvaḥ pratyākhyātastasmānna ratnānāṃ bhavānbhājanaṃ bhaviṣyatīti||62||

63adyaprabhṛtyetadavasthitamṛṣivacanaṃ tadevaṃvidhaṃ māhātmyaṃ brāhmaṇānām||63||

64kṣatramapi ca brāhmaṇaprasādādeva śāśvatīmavyayāṃ ca pṛthivīṃ patnīmabhigamya bubhuje||64||

65yadetadbrahmāgnīṣomīyaṃ tena jagaddhāryate||65||

66sūryācandramasau cakṣuḥ keśāścaivāṃśavaḥ smṛtāḥ
|
bodhayaṃstāpayaṃścaiva jagaduttiṣṭhate pṛthak||66||

67bodhanāttāpanāccaiva jagato harṣaṇaṃ bhavet
|
agnīṣomakṛtairebhiḥ karmabhiḥ pāṇḍunandana
|
hṛṣīkeśo'hamīśāno varado lokabhāvanaḥ||67||

68iḻopahūtayogena hare bhāgaṃ kratuṣvaham
|
varṇaśca me hariḥ śreṣṭhastasmāddharirahaṃ smṛtaḥ||68||

69dhāma sāro hi bhūtānāmṛtaṃ caiva vicāritam
|
ṛtadhāmā tato vipraiḥ sadyaścāhaṃ prakīrtitaḥ||69||

70naṣṭāṃ ca dharaṇīṃ pūrvamavindaṃ vai guhāgatām
|
govinda iti tenāhaṃ devairvāgbhirabhiṣṭutaḥ||70||

71śipiviṣṭeti cākhyāyāṃ hīnaromā ca yo bhavet
|
tenāviṣṭaṃ tu yatkiñcicchipiviṣṭeti ca smṛtaḥ||71||

72yāsko māmṛṣiravyagro naikayajñeṣu gītavān
|
śipiviṣṭa iti hyasmādguhyanāmadharo hyaham||72||

73stutvā māṃ śipiviṣṭeti yāska ṛṣirudāradhīḥ
|
matprasādādadho naṣṭaṃ niruktamabhijagmivān||73||

74na hi jāto na jāyeyaṃ na janiṣye kadācana
|
kṣetrajñaḥ sarvabhūtānāṃ tasmādahamajaḥ smṛtaḥ||74||

75noktapūrvaṃ mayā kṣudramaślīlaṃ vā kadācana
|
ṛtā brahmasutā sā me satyā devī sarasvatī||75||

76saccāsaccaiva kaunteya mayāveśitamātmani
|
pauṣkare brahmasadane satyaṃ māmṛṣayo viduḥ||76||

77sattvānna cyutapūrvo'haṃ sattvaṃ vai viddhi matkṛtam
|
janmanīhā bhavetsattvaṃ paurvikaṃ me dhanaṃjaya||77||

78nirāśī:karmasaṃyuktaḥ sattvataścāpyakalmaṣaḥ
|
sātvatajñānadṛṣṭo'haṃ sattvatāmiti sāttvataḥ||78||

79kṛṣāmi medinīṃ pārtha bhūtvā kārṣṇāyaso mahān
|
kṛṣṇo varṇaśca me yasmāttasmātkṛṣṇo'hamarjuna||79||

80mayā saṃśleṣitā bhūmiradbhirvyoma ca vāyunā
|
vāyuśca tejasā sārdhaṃ vaikuṇṭhatvaṃ tato mama||80||

81nirvāṇaṃ paramaṃ brahma dharmo'sau para ucyate
|
tasmānna cyutapūrvo'hamacyutastena karmaṇā||81||

82pṛthivīnabhasī cobhe viśrute viśvatomukhe
|
tayoḥ saṃdhāraṇārthaṃ hi māmadhokṣajamañjasā||82||

83niruktaṃ vedaviduṣo vedaśabdārthacintakāḥ
|
te māṃ gāyanti prāgvaṃśe adhokṣaja iti sthitiḥ||83||

84śabda ekapadaireṣa vyāhṛtaḥ paramarṣibhiḥ
|
nānyo hyadhokṣajo loke ṛte nārāyaṇaṃ prabhum||84||

85ghṛtaṃ mamārciṣo loke jantūnāṃ prāṇadhāraṇam
|
ghṛtārcirahamavyagrairvedajñaiḥ parikīrtitaḥ||85||

86trayo hi dhātavaḥ khyātāḥ karmajā iti me smṛtāḥ
|
pittaṃ śleṣmā ca vāyuśca eṣa saṃghāta ucyate||86||

87etaiśca dhāryate janturetaiḥ kṣīṇaiśca kṣīyate
|
āyurvedavidastasmāttridhātuṃ māṃ pracakṣate||87||

88vṛṣo hi bhagavāndharmaḥ khyāto lokeṣu bhārata
|
naighaṇṭukapadākhyāne viddhi māṃ vṛṣamuttamam||88||

89kapirvarāhaḥ śreṣṭhaśca dharmaśca vṛṣa ucyate
|
tasmādvṛṣākapiṃ prāha kaśyapo māṃ prajāpatiḥ||89||

90na cādiṃ na madhyaṃ tathā caiva nāntaṃ kadācidvidante surāścāsurāśca
|
anādyo hyamadhyastathā cāpyanantaḥ pragīto'hamīśo vibhurlokasākṣī||90||

91śucīni śravaṇīyāni śṛṇomīha dhanañjaya
|
na ca pāpāni gṛhṇāmi tato'haṃ vai śuciśravāḥ||91||

92ekaśṛṅgaḥ purā bhūtvā varāho nandivardhanaḥ
|
imāṃ coddhṛtavānbhūmimekaśṛṅgastato hyayam||92||

93tathaivāsaṃ trikakudo vārāhaṃ rūpamāsthitaḥ
|
trikakuttena vikhyātaḥ śarīrasya tu māpanāt||93||

94viriñca iti yatproktaṃ kāpilaṃ jñānacintakaiḥ
|
sa prajāpatirevāhaṃ cetanātsarvalokakṛt||94||

95vidyāsahāyavantaṃ māmādityasthaṃ sanātanam
|
kapilaṃ prāhurācāryāḥ sāṃkhyā niścitaniścayāḥ||95||

96hiraṇyagarbho dyutimānya eṣa cchandasi stutaḥ
|
yogaiḥ saṃpūjyate nityaṃ sa evāhaṃ bhuvi smṛtaḥ||96||

97ekaviṃśatisāhasraṃ ṛgvedaṃ māṃ pracakṣate
|
sahasraśākhaṃ yatsāma ye vai vedavido janāḥ||97||

98gāyantyāraṇyake viprā madbhaktāste hi durlabhāḥ
|
ṣaṭpañcāśatamaṣṭau ca saptatriṃśatamityuta||98||

99yasmiñśākhā yajurvede so'hamādhvaryave smṛtaḥ
|
pañcakalpamatharvāṇaṃ kṛtyābhiḥ paribṛṃhitam||99||

100kalpayanti hi māṃ viprā atharvāṇavidastathā
|
śākhābhedāśca ye kecidyāśca śākhāsu gītayaḥ||100||

101svaravarṇasamuccārāḥ sarvāṃstānviddhi matkṛtān
|
yattaddhayaśiraḥ pārtha samudeti varapradam||101||

102so'hamevottare bhāge kramākṣaravibhāgavit
|
vāmādeśitamārgeṇa matprasādānmahātmanā||102||

103pāñcālena kramaḥ prāptastasmādbhūtātsanātanāt
|
bābhravyagotra: sa babhau prathama kramapāragaḥ||103||

104nārāyaṇādvaraṃ labdhvā prāpya yogamanuttamam
|
kramaṃ praṇīya śikṣāṃ ca praṇayitvā sa gālavaḥ||104||

105kaṇḍarīko'tha rājā ca brahmadattaḥ pratāpavān
|
jātīmaraṇajaṃ duḥkhaṃ smṛtvā smṛtvā punaḥ punaḥ||105||

106saptajātiṣu mukhyatvādyogānāṃ saṃpadaṃ gataḥ
|
purā'hamātmajaḥ pārtha prathitaḥ kāraṇāntare||106||

107dharmasya kuruśārdūla tato'haṃ dharmajaḥ smṛtaḥ
|
naranārāyaṇau pūrvaṃ tapastepaturavyayam||107||

108dharmayānaṃ samārūḍhau parvate gandhamādane
|
tatkālasamaye caiva dakṣayajño babhūva ha||108||

109na caivākalpayadbhāgaṃ dakṣo rudrasya bhārata
|
tato dadhīcivacanāddakṣayajñamapāharat||109||

110sasarja śūlaṃ kopena prajvalantaṃ muhurmuhuḥ
|
tacchūlaṃ bhasmasātkṛtvā dakṣayajñaṃ savistaram||110||

111āvayoḥ sahasāgacchadbadaryāśramamantikāt
|
vegena mahatā pārtha patannārāyaṇorasi||111||

112tatastattejasā''viṣṭāḥ keśā nārāyaṇasya ha
|
babhūvurmuñjavarṇāstu tato'haṃ muñjakeśavān||112||

113tacca śūlaṃ vinirdhūtaṃ huṃkāreṇa mahātmanā
|
jagāma śaṃkarakaraṃ nārāyaṇasamāhatam||113||

114atha rudra upādhāvattāvṛṣī tapasānvitau
|
tata enaṃ samudbhūtaṃ kaṇṭhe jagrāha pāṇinā||114||

115nārāyaṇaḥ sa viśvātmā tenāsya śitikaṇṭhatā
|
atha rudravighātārthamiṣīkāṃ nara uddharan||115||

116mantraiśca saṃyuyojāśu so'bhavatparaśurmahān
|
kṣiptaśca sahasā tena khaṇḍanaṃ prāptavāstadā||116||

117tato'haṃ khaṇḍaparaśuḥ smṛtaḥ paraśukhaṇḍanāt
|
arjuna uvāca |
asminyuddhe tu vārṣṇeya trailokyaśamane tadā||117||

118ko jayaṃ prāptavāṃstatra śaṃsaitanme janārdana
|
śrībhagavānuvāca |
tayoḥ saṃlagnayoryuddhe rudranārāyaṇātmanoḥ||118||

119udvignāḥ sahasā kṛtsnāḥ sarve lokāstadābhavan
|
nāgṛhṇātpāvakaḥ śubhaṃ makheṣu suhutaṃ haviḥ||119||

120vedā na pratibhānti sma ṛṣīṇāṃ bhāvitātmanām
|
devān rajastamaścaiva samāviviśatustadā||120||

121vasudhā saṃcakampe ca nabhaśca vipaphāla ha
|
niṣprabhāṇi ca tejāṃsi brahmā caivāsanacyutaḥ||121||

122agācchoṣaṃ samudraśca himavāṃśca vyaśīryata
|
tasminnevaṃ samutpanne nimitte pāṇḍunandana||122||

123brahmā vṛto devagaṇairṛṣibhiśca mahātmabhiḥ
|
ājagāmāśu taṃ deśaṃ yatra yuddhamavartata||123||

124so'ñjalipragraho bhūtvā caturvaktro niruktagaḥ
|
uvāca vacanaṃ rudraṃ lokānāmastu vai śivam ||124||

125nyasyāyudhāni viśveśa jagato hitakāmyayā
|
yadakṣaramathāvyaktamīśaṃ lokasya bhāvanam||125||

126kūṭasthaṃ kartṛ nirdvandvamakarteti ca yaṃ viduḥ
|
vyaktibhāvagatasyāsya ekā mūrtiriyaṃ śubhā||126||

127naro nārāyaṇaścaiva jātau dharmakulodvahau
|
tapasā mahatā yuktau devaśreṣṭhau mahāvratau||127||

128ahaṃ prasādajastasya kutaścitkāraṇāntare
|
tvaṃ caiva krodhajastāta pūrvasarge sanātanaḥ||128||

129mayā ca sārddhaṃ varada vibudhaiśca maharṣibhiḥ
|
prasādayāśu lokānāṃ śāntirbhavatu mā ciram||129||

130brahmaṇā tvevamuktastu rudraḥ krodhāgnimutsṛjan
|
prasādayāmāsa tato devaṃ nārāyaṇaṃ prabhum
|
śaraṇaṃ ca jagāmādyaṃ vareṇyaṃ varadaṃ prabhum||130||

131tato'tha varado devo jitakrodho jitendriyaḥ
|
prītimānabhavattatra rudreṇa saha saṃgataḥ||131||

132ṛṣibhirbrahmaṇā caiva vibudhaiśca supūjitaḥ
|
uvāca devamīśānamīśaḥ sa jagato hariḥ||132||

133nāvayorantaraṃ kiñcinmā te'bhūdbuddhiranyathā
|
adyaprabhṛti śrīvatsaḥ śūlāṃko me bhavatvayam
|
mama pāṇyaṅkitaścāpi śrīkaṇṭhastvaṃ bhaviṣyasi||133||

134śrībhagavānuvāca |
evaṃ lakṣaṇamutpādya parasparakṛtaṃ tadā
|
sakhyaṃ caivātulaṃ kṛtvā rudreṇa sahitāvṛṣī||134||

135tapastepaturavyagrau visṛjya tridivaukasaḥ
|
eṣa te kathitaḥ pārtha nārāyaṇajayo mṛdhe||135||

136nāmāni caiva guhyāni niruktāni ca bhārata
|
ṛṣibhiḥ kathitānīha yāni saṃkīrtitāni te||136||

137evaṃ bahuvidhai rūpaiścarāmīha vasundharām
|
brahmalokaṃ ca kaunteya golokaṃ ca sanātanam||137||

138mayā tvaṃ rakṣito yuddhe mahāntaṃ prāptavāñjayam
|
yastu te so'grato yāti yuddhe saṃpratyupasthite||138||

139taṃ viddhi rudra kaunteya devadevaṃ kapardinam
|
kālaḥ sa eva kathitaḥ krodhajeti mayā tava||139||

140nihatāstena vai pūrvaṃ hatavānasi yān ripūn
|
aprameyaprabhāvaṃ taṃ devadevamumāpatim
|
namasva devaṃ prayato viśveśaṃ haramakṣayam||140||

141yaśca te kathitaḥ pūrvaṃ krodhajeti punaḥ punaḥ
|
tasya prabhāva evāgre yacchrutaṃ te dhanañcaya||141||
