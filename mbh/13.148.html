Mahābhārata 13.148 (Anuśāsanaparvan)

1nārada uvāca|
atha vyomni mahāñchabdaḥ savidyutstanayitnumān
|
medhaiśca gaganaṃ nīlaṃ saṃruddhamabhavaddhanaiḥ ||1||

2prāvṛṣīva ca parjanyo vavṛṣe nirmalaṃ payaḥ
|
tamaścaivābhavaddhoraṃ diśaśca na cakāśire ||2||

3tato devagirau tasmin ramye puṇye sanātane
|
na śarvaṃ bhūtasaṃghaṃ vā dadṛśurmunayastadā ||3||

4vyabhraṃ ca gaganaṃ sadyaḥ kṣaṇena samapadyata
|
tīrthayātrāṃ tato viprā jagmuścānye yathāgatam ||4||

5tada‌dbhutamacintyaṃ ca dṛṣṭvā te vismitā'bhavan
|
śaṅkarasyomayā sārdhaṃ saṃvādaṃ tvatkathāśrayam ||5||

6sa bhavān puruṣavyāghra brahmabhūtaḥ sanātanaḥ
|
yadarthamanuśiṣṭāḥ smo giripṛṣṭhe mahātmanā ||6||

7dvitīyaṃ tvadbhutamidaṃ tvattejaḥ kṛtamadya vai
|
dṛṣṭvā ca vismitāḥ kṛṣṇa sā ca naḥ smṛtirāgatā ||7||

8etatte devadevasya māhātmyaṃ kathitaṃ prabho
|
kapardino girīśasya mahābāho janārdana ||8||

9ityuktaḥ sa tadā kṛṣṇastapovananivāsibhiḥ
|
mānayāmāsa tānsarvānṛṣīn devakinandanaḥ ||9||

10atharṣayaḥ saṃprahṛṣṭāḥ punaste kṛṣṇamabruvan
|
punaḥ punardarśayāsmān sadaiva madhusūdana ||10||

11na hi naḥsā ratiḥ svarge yā ca tvaddarśane vibho
|
tadṛtaṃ ca mahābāho yadāha bhagavānbhavaḥ ||11||

12etatte sarvamākhyātaṃ rahasyamarikarśana
|
tvameva hyarthatattvajñaḥ pṛṣṭo'smān pṛcchase yadā ||12||

13tadasmābhiridaṃ guhyaṃ tvatpriyārthamudāhṛtam
|
na ca te'viditaṃ kiñcittriṣu lokeṣu vidyate ||13||

14janma caiva prasūtiśca yaccānyatkāraṇaṃ vibho
|
vayaṃ tu bahucāpalyādaśaktā guhyadhāraṇe ||14||

15tataḥ sthite tvayi vibho laghutvātpralapāmahe
|
na hi kiñcittadāśvaryaṃ yanna vetti bhavāniha ||15||

16divi vā bhuvi vā deva sarvaṃ hi viditaṃ tava
|
sādhayāma vayaṃ kṛṣṇa buddhiṃ puṣṭimavāpnuhi ||16||

17putraste sadṛśastāta viśiṣṭo vā bhaviṣyati
|
mahāprabhāvasaṃyukto dīptikīrtikaraḥ prabhuḥ ||17||

18bhīṣma uvāca|
tataḥ praṇamya deveśaṃ yādavaṃ puruṣottamam
|
pradakṣiṇamupāvṛtya prajagmuste maharṣayaḥ ||18||

19so'yaṃ nārāyaṇaḥ śrīmān dīptyā paramayā yutaḥ
|
vrataṃ yathāvattaccīrvā dvārakāṃ punarāgamat ||19||

20pūrṇe ca daśame māsi putro'sya paramādbhutaḥ
|
rukmiṇyāṃ saṃmato jajñe śūro vaṃśadharaḥ prabho ||20||

21sa kāmaḥ sarvabhūtānāṃ sarvabhāgavato nṛpa
|
asurāṇāṃ surāṇāṃ ca caratyantargataḥ sadā ||21||

22so'yaṃ puruṣaśārdūlo meghavarṇaścaturbhujaḥ
|
saṃśritaḥ pāṇḍavān premṇā bhavantaścainamāśritāḥ ||22||

23kīrtirlakṣmīrdhṛtiścaiva svargamārgastathaiva ca
|
yatraiṣa saṃsthitastatra devo viṣṇustrivikramaḥ ||23||

24sendrā devāstrayastriṃśadeṣa nātra vicāraṇā
|
ādidevo mahādevaḥ sarvabhūtapratiśrayaḥ ||24||

25anādinidhano'vyakto mahātmā madhusūdanaḥ
|
ayaṃ jāto mahātejāḥ surāṇāmarthasiddhaye ||25||

26sudustarārthatattvasya vaktā kartā ca mādhavaḥ
|
tava pārthaṃ jayaḥ kṛtsnastava kīrtistathā'tulā ||26||

27taveyaṃ pṛthivī devī kṛtsnā nārāyaṇā''śrayāt
|
ayaṃ nāthastavācintyo yasya nārāyaṇo gatiḥ ||27||

28sa bhavāṃstvamupādhvaryū raṇāgnau hutavānnṛpān
|
kṛṣṇasruveṇa mahatā yugāntāgnisamena vai ||28||

29duryodhanaśca śocyo'sau saputrabhrātṛbāndhavaḥ
|
kṛtavānyo'budhaḥ krodhāddharigāṇḍīvivigraham ||29||

30daiteyā dānavendrāśca mahākāyā mahābalāḥ
|
cakrāgnau kṣayamāpannā dāvāgnau śalabhā iva ||30||

31pratiyoddhuṃ na śakyo hi mānuṣaireṣa saṃyuge
|
vihīnaiḥ puruṣavyāghra sattvaśaktibalādibhiḥ ||31||

32jayo yogī yugāntābhaḥ savyasācī raṇāgragaḥ
|
tejasā hatavān sarvaṃ suyodhanabalaṃ nṛpa ||32||

33yattu govṛṣabhāṃkena munibhyaḥ samudāhṛtam
|
purāṇaṃ himavatpṛṣṭhe tanme nigadataḥ śṛṇu ||33||

34yāvattasya bhavetpuṣṭistejo dīptiḥ parākramaḥ
|
prabhāvaḥ sannatirjanma kṛṣṇe tattriguṇaṃ vibho ||34||

35kaḥ śaknotyanyathākartuṃ tadyadi syāttathā śṛṇu
|
yatra kṛṣṇo hi bhagavāṃstatra puṣṭiranuttamā ||35||

36vayaṃ tvihālpamatayaḥ paratantrāḥ suviklavāḥ
|
jñānapūrvaṃ prapannāḥ smo mṛtyoḥ panthānamavyayam ||36||

37bhavāṃścāpyārjavaparaḥ pūrvaṃ kṛtvā pratiśrayam
|
rājavṛttaṃ na labhate pratijñāpālane rataḥ ||37||

38apyaivātmavadhaṃ loke rājaṃstvaṃ bahu manyase
|
na hi pratijñā yā dattā tāṃ prahātumarindama ||38||

39kālenāyaṃ janaḥ sarvo nihato raṇamūrdhani
|
vayaṃ ca kālena hatāḥ kālo hi parameśvaraḥ ||39||

40na hi kālena kālajñaḥ spṛṣṭaḥ śocitumarhasi
|
kālo lohitaraktākṣaḥ kṛṣṇo daṇḍī sanātanaḥ ||40||

41tasmāt kuntīsuta jñātīnneha śocitumarhasi
|
vyapetamanyurnityaṃ tvaṃ bhava kauravanandana ||41||

42mādhavasyāsya māhātmyaṃ śrutaṃ yatkathitaṃ mayā
|
tadeva tāvatparyāptaṃ sajjanasya nidarśanam ||42||

43vyāsasya vacanaṃ śrutvā nāradasya ca dhīmataḥ
|
svayaṃ caiva mahārāja kṛṣṇasyārhatamasya vai ||43||

44prabhāvaścārṣipūgasya kathitaḥ sumahān mayā
|
maheśvarasya saṃvādaṃ śailapuḍyāśca bhārata ||44||

45dhārayiṣyati yaścainaṃ mahāpuruṣasaṃbhavam
|
śṛṇuyātkathayedvā yaḥ sa śreyo labhate param ||45||

46bhavitāraśca tasyātha sarve kāmā yathepsitāḥ
|
pretya svargaṃ ca labhate naro nāstyatra saṃśayaḥ ||46||

47nyāyyaṃ śreyo'bhikāmena pratipattuṃ janārdanaḥ
|
eṣa evākṣayo vipraiḥ stuto rājan janārdanaḥ ||47||

48maheśvaramukhotsṛṣṭā ye ca dharmaguṇāḥ smṛtāḥ
|
te tvayā manasā dhāryāḥ kururāja divāniśam ||48||

49evaṃ te vartamānasya samyagdaṇḍadharasya ca
|
prajāpālanadakṣasya svargaloko bhaviṣyati ||49||

50dharmeṇāpi sadā rājan prajā rakṣitumarhasi
|
yastasya vipulo daṇḍaḥ samyagdharmaḥ sa kīrtyate ||50||

51ya eṣa kathito rājan mayā sajjanasānnidhau
|
śaṅkarasyomayā sārdhaṃ saṃvādo dharmasaṃhitaḥ ||51||

52śrutvā vā śrotukāmo vāpyarcayedvṛṣabhadhvajam
|
viśuddheneha bhāvena ya icchedbhūtimātmanaḥ ||52||

53eṣa tasyānavadyasya nāradasya mahātmanaḥ
|
saṃdeśo devapūjārthaṃ taṃ tathā kuru pāṇḍava ||53||

54etadatyadbhutaṃ vṛttaṃ puṇye hi bhavati prabho
|
vāsudevasya kaunteya sthāṇoścaiva svabhāvajam ||54||

55daśavarṣasahasrāṇi badaryāmeṣa śāśvataḥ
|
tapaścacāra vipulaṃ saha gāṇḍīvadhanvanā ||55||

56triyugau puṇḍarīkākṣau vāsudevadhanañjayau
|
viditau nāradādetau mama vyāsācca pārthiva ||56||

57bāla eva mahābāhuścakāra kadanaṃ mahat
|
kaṃsasya puṇḍarīkākṣo jñātitrāṇārthakāraṇāt ||57||

58karmaṇāmasya kaunteya nāntaṃ saṃkhyātumutsahe
|
śāśvatasya purāṇasya puruṣasya yudhiṣṭhira ||58||

59dhruvaṃ śreyaḥ paraṃ tāta bhaviṣyati tavottamam
|
yasya te puruṣavyāghraḥ sakhā cāyaṃ janārdanaḥ ||59||

60duryodhanaṃ tu śocāmi pretya loke'pi durmatim
|
yatkṛte pṛthivī sarvā vinaṣṭā sahayadvipā ||60||

61duryodhanāparādhena karṇasya śakunestathā
|
duḥśāsanacaturthānāṃ kuravo nidhanaṃ gatāḥ ||61||

62vaiśampāyana uvāca|
evaṃ saṃbhāṣamāṇe tu gāṅgeye puruṣarṣabhe
|
tūṣṇīṃ babhūva kauravyo madhye teṣāṃ mahātmanām ||62||

63tacchrutvā vismayaṃ jagmurdhṛtarāṣṭrādayo nṛpāḥ
|
saṃpūjya manasā kṛṣṇaṃ sarve prāñjalayo'bhavan ||63||

64ṛṣayaścāpi te sarve nāradapramukhāstadā
|
pratigṛhyābhyanandanta tadvākyaṃ pratipūjya ca ||64||

65ityetada‌khilaṃ sarvaiḥ pāṇḍavo bhrātṛbhiḥ saha
|
śrutavānsumahāścaryaṃ puṇyaṃ bhīṣmānuśāsanam ||65||

66yudhiṣṭhirastu gāṅgeyaṃ viśrāntaṃ bhūridākṣaṇam
|
punareva mahābuddhiḥ paryapṛcchanmahīpatiḥ ||66||
