Mahābhārata 3.160 (Āraṇyakaparvan)

1janamejaya uvāca|
ārṣṭiṣeṇāśrame tasmin mama pūrvapitāmahāḥ
|
pāṇḍoḥ putrā mahātmānaḥ sarve divyaparākramāḥ||1||

2kiyantaṃ kālamavasan parvate gandhamādane
|
kiṃ ca cakrurmahāvīryāḥ sarve'tibalapauruṣāḥ||2||

3kāni cābhyavahāryāṇi tatra teṣāṃ mahātmanām
|
vasatāṃ lokavīrāṇāmāsaṃstadbūhi sattama||3||

4vistareṇa ca me śaṃsa bhīmasenaparākramam
|
yadyaccake mahābāhustasmin haimavate girau||4||

5na khalvāsīt punaryuddhaṃ tasya yakṣairdvijottama
|
kaccit samāgamasteṣāmāsīdvaiśravaṇasya ca||5||

6tatra hyāyāti dhanada ārṣṭiṣeṇo yathābravīt
|
etadicchāmyahaṃ śrotuṃ vistareṇa tapodhana
|
na hi me śṛṇvatastṛptirasti teṣāṃ viceṣṭitam||6||

7vaiśampāyana uvāca|
etadātmahitaṃ śrutvā tasyāpratimatejasaḥ
|
śāsanaṃ satataṃ cakrustathaiva bharatarṣabhāḥ||7||

8bhuñjānā munibhojyāni rasavanti phalāni ca
|
śuddhabāṇahatānāṃ ca mṛgāṇāṃ piśitānyapi||8||

9medhyāni himavatpṛṣṭhe madhūni vividhāni ca
|
evaṃ te nyavasaṃstatra pāṇḍavā bharatarṣabhāḥ||9||

10tathā nivasatāṃ teṣāṃ pañcamaṃ varṣamabhyagāt
|
śṛṇvatāṃ lomaśoktāni vākyāni vividhānyuta||10||

11kṛtyakāla upasthāsya iti coktvā ghaṭotkacaḥ
|
rākṣasaiḥ saha sarvaiśca pūrvameva gataḥ prabho||11||

12ārṣṭiṣeṇāśrame teṣāṃ vasatāṃ vai mahātmanām
|
agacchan bahavo māsāḥ paśyatāṃ mahadadbhutam||12||

13taistatra viharadbhiśca ramamāṇaiśca pāṇḍavaiḥ
|
prītimanto mahābhāgā munayaścāraṇāstathā||13||

14ājagmuḥ pāṇḍavān draṣṭuṃ śuddhātmāno yatavratāḥ
|
te taiḥ saha kathāṃ cakrurdivyāṃ bharatasattamāḥ||14||

15tataḥ katipayāhasya mahāhradanivāsinam
|
ṛddhimantaṃ mahānāgaṃ suparṇaḥ sahasā''harat||15||

16prākampata mahāśailaḥ prāmṛdyanta mahādrumāḥ
|
dadṛśuḥ sarvabhūtāni pāṇḍavāśca tadadbhutam||16||

17tataḥ śailottamasyāgrāt pāṇḍavān prati mārutaḥ
|
avahat sarvamālyāni gandhavanti śubhāni ca||17||

18tatra puṣpāṇi divyāni suhṛdbhiḥ saha pāṇḍavāḥ
|
dadṛśuḥ pañcavarṇāni draupadī ca yaśasvinī||18||

19bhīmasenaṃ tataḥ kṛṣṇā kāle vacanamabravīt
|
vivikte parvatoddeśe sukhāsīnaṃ mahābhujam||19||

20suparṇānilavegena śvasanena mahābalāt
|
pañcavarṇā nipātyante puṣpāṇi bharatarṣabha||20||

21pratyakṣaṃ sarvabhūtānāṃ nadīmaśvarathāṃ prati
|
khāṇḍave satyasaṃdhena bhrātrā tava mahātmanā||21||

22gandharvoragarakṣāṃsi vāsavaśca nivāritaḥ
|
hatā māyāvinaścogrā dhanuḥ prāptaṃ ca gāṇḍivam||22||

23tavāpi sumahattejo mahadbāhubalaṃ ca te
|
aviṣahyamanādhṛṣyaṃ śakratulyaparākrama||23||

24tvadbāhubalavegena trāsitāḥ sarvarākṣasāḥ
|
hitvā śailaṃ prapadyantāṃ bhīmasena diśo daśa||24||

25tataḥ śailottamasyāgraṃ citramālyadharaṃ śivam
|
vyapetabhayasaṃmohāḥ paśyantu suhṛdastava||25||

26evaṃ praṇihitaṃ bhīma cirātprabhṛti me manaḥ
|
draṣṭumicchāmi śailāgraṃ tvadbāhubalapālitā||26||

27tataḥ kṣiptamivātmānaṃ draupadyā sa paraṃtapaḥ
|
nāmṛṣyata mahābāhuḥ prahāramiva sadgavaḥ||27||

28siṃharṣabhagatiḥ śrīmānudāraḥ kanakaprabhaḥ
|
manasvī balavān dṛpto mānī śuraśca pāṇḍavaḥ||28||

29lohitākṣaḥ pṛthuvyaṃso mattavāraṇavikramaḥ
|
siṃhadaṃṣṭro bṛhatskandhaḥ śālapota ivodgataḥ||29||

30mahātmā cārusarvāṅgaḥ kambugrīvo mahābhujaḥ
|
rukmapṛṣṭhaṃ dhanuḥ khaḍgaṃ tūṇāṃścāpi parāmṛśat||30||

31sa kesarīva cotsiktaḥ prabhinna iva vāraṇaḥ
|
vyapetabhayasaṃmohaḥ śailamabhyapatadbalī||31||

32taṃ mṛgendramivāyāntaṃ prabhinnamiva vāraṇam
|
dadṛśuḥ sarvabhūtāni bāṇakārmukadhāriṇam||32||

33draupadyā vardhayan harṣaṃ gadāmādāya pāṇḍavaḥ
|
vyapetabhayasaṃmohaḥ śailarājaṃ samāśritaḥ||33||

34na glānirna ca kātaryaṃ na vaiklavyaṃ na matsaraḥ
|
kadācijjuṣate pārthamātmajaṃ mātariśvanaḥ||34||

35tadekāyanamāsādya viṣamaṃ bhīmadarśanam
|
bahutālocchrayaṃ śṛṅgamāruroha mahābalaḥ||35||

36sakinnaramahānāgamunigandharvarākṣasān
|
harṣayan parvatasyāgramāruhya sa mahābalaḥ||36||

37tato vaiśravaṇāvāsaṃ dadarśa bharatarṣabhaḥ
|
kāñcanaiḥ sphāṭikaiścaiva veśmabhiḥ samalaṃkṛtam||37||

38prākāreṇa parikṣiptaṃ sauvarṇena samantataḥ
|
sarvaratnadhutimatā sarvodyānavatā tathā||38||

39śailādabhyucchrayavatā cayāṭṭālakaśobhinā
|
dvāratoraṇanirvyūhadhvajasaṃvāhaśobhinā||39||

40vilāsinībhiratyarthaṃ nṛtyantībhiḥ samantataḥ
|
vāyunā dhūyamānābhiḥ patākābhiralaṃkṛtam||40||

41dhanuṣkoṭimavaṣṭabhya vakrabhāvena bāhunā
|
paśyamānaḥ sa khedena draviṇādhipateḥ puram||41||

42modayan sarvabhūtāni gandhamādanasaṃbhavaḥ
|
sarvagandhavahastatra mārutaḥ susukho vavau||42||

43citrā vividhavarṇābhāścitramañjaridhāriṇaḥ
|
acintyā vividhāstatra drumāḥ paramaśobhinaḥ||43||

44ratnajālaparikṣiptaṃ citramālyavibhūṣitam
|
rākṣasādhipateḥ sthānaṃ dadṛśe bharatarṣabhaḥ||44||

45gadākhaḍgadhanuṣpāṇiḥ samabhityaktajīvitaḥ
|
bhīmaseno mahābāhustasthau giririvācalaḥ||45||

46tataḥ śaṅkhamupādhmāsīddviṣatāṃ lomaharṣaṇam
|
jyāghoṣatalaśabdaṃ ca kṛtvā bhūtānyamohayat||46||

47tataḥ prahṛṣṭaromāṇastaṃ śabdamabhidudruvuḥ
|
yakṣarākṣasagandharvāḥ pāṇḍavasya samīpataḥ||47||

48gadāparighanistriṃśaśūlaśaktiparaśvadhāḥ
|
pragṛhītā vyarocanta yakṣarākṣasabāhubhiḥ||48||

49tataḥ pravavṛte yuddhaṃ teṣāṃ tasya ca bhārata
|
taiḥ prayuktān mahāmāyaiḥ śūlaśaktiparaśvadhān||49||

50bhallairbhīmaḥ praciccheda bhīmavegataraistataḥ
|
antarikṣagatānāṃ ca bhūmiṣṭhānāṃ ca garjatām||50||

51śarairvivyādha gātrāṇi rākṣasānāṃ mahābalaḥ
|
sā lohitamahāvṛṣṭirabhyavarṣanmahābalam||51||

52gadāparighapāṇīnāṃ rakṣasāṃ kāyasaṃbhavāḥ
|
kāyebhyaḥ pracyutā dhārā rākṣasānāṃ samantataḥ||52||

53bhīmabāhubalotsṛṣṭairāyudhairyakṣarakṣasām
|
vinikṛttāni dṛśyante śarīrāṇi śirāṃsi ca||53||

54pracchādyamānaṃ rakṣobhiḥ pāṇḍavaṃ priyadarśanam
|
dadṛśuḥ sarvabhūtāni sūryamabhragaṇairiva||54||

55sa raśmibhirivādityaḥ śarairarinighātibhiḥ
|
sarvānārchanmahābāhurbalavān satyavikramaḥ||55||

56abhitarjayamānāśca ruvantaśca mahāravān
|
na mohaṃ bhīmasenasya dadṛśuḥ sarvarākṣasāḥ||56||

57yakṣā vikṛtasarvāṅgā bhīmasenabhayārditāḥ
|
bhīmamārtasvaraṃ cakrurviprakīrṇamahāyudhāḥ||57||

58utsṛjya te gadāśūlānasiśaktiparaśvadhān
|
dakṣiṇāṃ diśamājagmustrāsitā dṛḍhadhanvanā||58||

59tatra śūlagadāpāṇirvyūḍhorasko mahābhujaḥ
|
sakhā vaiśravaṇasyāsīnmaṇimānnāma rākṣasaḥ||59||

60adarśayadadhīkāraṃ pauruṣaṃ ca mahābalaḥ
|
sa tān dṛṣṭvā parāvṛttānsmayamāna ivābravīt||60||

61ekena bahavaḥ saṅkhye mānuṣeṇa parājitāḥ
|
prāpya vaiśravaṇāvāsaṃ kiṃ vakṣyatha dhaneśvaram||61||

62evamābhāṣya tān sarvānabhyavartata rākṣasaḥ
|
śaktiśūlagadāpāṇirabhyadhāvatsa pāṇḍavam||62||

63tamāpatantaṃ vegena prabhinnamiva vāraṇam
|
vatsadantaistribhiḥ pārśve bhīmasenaḥ samārdayat||63||

64maṇimānapi saṃkruddhaḥ pragṛhya mahatīṃ gadām
|
prāhiṇodbhīmasenāya parigṛhya mahābalaḥ||64||

65vidyudrūpāṃ mahāghorāmākāśe mahatīṃ gadām
|
śarairbahubhirānarcchadbhīmasenaḥ śilāśitaiḥ||65||

66pratyahanyanta te sarve gadāmāsādya sāyakāḥ
|
na vegaṃ dhārayāmāsurgadāvegasya vegitāḥ||66||

67gadāyuddhasamācāraṃ buddhyamānaḥ sa vīryavān
|
vyaṃsayāmāsa taṃ tasya prahāraṃ bhīmavikramaḥ||67||

68tataḥ śaktiṃ mahāghorāṃ rukmadaṇḍāmayasmayīm
|
tasminnevāntare dhīmān prajahārātha rākṣasaḥ||68||

69sā bhujaṃ bhīmanirhrādā bhittvā bhīmasya dākṣaṇam
|
sāgnijvālā mahāraudrā papāta sahasā bhuvi||69||

70so'tividdho maheṣvāsaḥ śaktyāmitaparākramaḥ
|
gadāṃ jagrāha kaunteyaḥ krodhaparyākulekṣaṇaḥ||70||

71rukmapaṭṭapinaddhāṃ tāṃ śatrūṇāṃ bhayavardhinīm
|
pragṛhyātha nadan bhīmaḥ śaikyāṃ sarvāyasīṃ gadām||71||

72tarasā cābhidudrāva maṇimantaṃ mahābalam
|
dīpyamānaṃ mahāśūlaṃ pragṛhya maṇimānapi||72||

73prāhiṇodbhīmasenāya vegena mahatā nadan
|
bhaṅktvā śūlaṃ gadāgreṇa gadāyuddhaviśāradaḥ||73||

74abhidudrāva taṃ hantuṃ garutmāniva pannagam
|
so'ntarikṣamavaplutya vidhūya sahasā gadām||74||

75pracikṣepa mahābāhurvinadya raṇamūrdhani
|
sendrāśanirivendreṇa visṛṣṭā vātaraṃhasā||75||

76hatvā rakṣaḥ kṣitiṃ prāpya kṛtyeva nipapāta ha
|
taṃ rākṣasaṃ bhīmabalaṃ bhīmasenena pātitam||76||

77dadṛśuḥ sarvabhūtāni siṃheneva gavāṃ patim
|
taṃ prekṣya nihataṃ bhūmau hataśeṣā niśācarāḥ
|
bhīmamārtasvaraṃ kṛtvā jagmuḥ prācīṃ diśaṃ prati||77||
