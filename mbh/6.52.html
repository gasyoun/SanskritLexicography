Mahābhārata 6.52 (Bhīṣmaparvan)

1dhṛtarāṣṭra uvāca|evaṃ vyūḍheṣvanīkeṣu māmakeṣvitareṣu ca
|
kathaṃ praharatāṃ śreṣṭhāḥ saṃprahāra pracakrire ||1||

2sañjaya uvāca|samaṃ vyūḍheṣvanīkeṣu sannaddharuciradhvajam
|
apāramiva saṃdṛśya sāgarapratimaṃ balam ||2||

3teṣāṃ madhye sthito rājan putro duryodhanastava
|
abravīttāvakān sarvān yuddhyadhvamiti daṃśitāḥ ||3||

4te manaḥ krūramādhāya samabhityaktajīvitāḥ
|
pāṇḍavānabhyavartanta sarva evocchritadhvajāḥ ||4||

5tato yuddhaṃ samabhavattumulaṃ lomaharṣaṇam
|
tāvakānāṃ pareṣāṃ ca vyatiṣaktarathadvipam ||5||

6muktāstu rathibhirbāṇā rukmapuṅkhāḥ sutejasaḥ
|
sannipeturakuṇṭhāgrā nāgeṣu ca hayeṣu ca ||6||

7tathā pravṛtte saṃgrāme dhanurudyamya daṃśitaḥ
|
abhipatya mahābāhurbhīṣmo bhīmaparākramaḥ ||7||

8sobhadre bhīmasene ca sātyakau ca mahārathe
|
kaikeye ca virāṭe ca dhṛṣṭadyumne ca pārṣate ||8||

9eteṣu naravīreṣu cedimatsyeṣu cābhibhūḥ
|
vavarṣa śaravarṣāṇi vṛddhaḥ kurupitāmahaḥ ||9||

10abhidyata tato vyūhastasmin vīrasamāgame
|
sarveṣāmeva sainyānāmāsīdvyatikaro mahān ||10||

11sādino dhvajinaścaiva hatapravaravājinaḥ
|
vipradrutarathānīkāḥ samapadyanta pāṇḍavāḥ ||11||

12arjunastu naravyāghro dṛṣṭvā bhīṣmaṃ mahāratham
|
vārṣṇeyamabravīt kruddho yāhi yatra pitāmahaḥ ||12||

13eṣa bhīṣmaḥ susaṃkruddho vārṣṇeya mama vāhinīm
|
nāśayiṣyati suvyaktaṃ duryodhanahite rataḥ ||13||

14eṣa droṇaḥ kṛpaḥ śalyo vikarṇaśca janārdana
|
dhārtarāṣṭrāśca sahitā duryodhanapurogamāḥ ||14||

15pañcālānnihaniṣyanti rakṣitā dṛḍhadhanvanā
|
so'haṃ bhīṣmaṃ vadhiṣyāmi sainyahetorjanārdana ||15||

16tamabravīdvāsudevo yatto bhava dhanañjaya
|
eṣa tvāṃ prāpayiṣyāmi pitāmaharathaṃ prati ||16||

17evamuktvā tataḥ śaurī rathaṃ taṃ lokaviśrutam
|
prāpayāmāsa bhīṣmasya rathaṃ prati janeśvara ||17||

18caladbahupatākena balākāvarṇavājinā
|
samucchritamahābhīmanadadvānaraketunā ||18||

19mahatā meghanādena rathenāmitatejasā
|
vinighnan kauravānīkaṃ śūrasenāṃśca pāṇḍavaḥ ||19||

20prāyāccharaṇadaḥ śīghraṃ suhṛdāṃ harṣavardhanaḥ
|
tamāpatantaṃ vegena prabhinnamiva vāraṇam ||20||

21trāsayantaṃ raṇe śūrān mardayantaṃ ca sāyakaiḥ
|
saindhavapramukhairguptaḥ prācyasauvīrakekayaiḥ ||21||

22sahasā pratyudīyāya bhīṣmaḥ śāntanavo'rjunam
|
ko hi gāṇḍīvadhanvānamanyaḥ kurupitāmahāt ||22||

23droṇavaikartanābhyāṃ vā rathī saṃyātumarhati
|
tato bhīṣmo mahārāja sarvalokamahārathaḥ ||23||

24arjunaṃ saptasaptatyā nārācānāṃ samācinot
|
droṇaśca pañcaviṃśatyā kṛpaḥ pañcāśatā śaraiḥ ||24||

25duryodhanaścatuḥṣaṣṭyā śalyaśca navabhiḥ śaraiḥ
|
saindhavo navabhiścaiva śakuniścāpi pañcabhiḥ ||25||

26vikarṇo daśabhirbhallai rājan vivyādha pāṇḍavam
|
sa tairviddho maheṣvāsaḥ samantānniśitaiḥ śaraiḥ ||26||

27na vivyathe mahābāhurbhidyamāna ivācalaḥ
|
sa bhīṣmaṃ pañcaviṃśatyā kṛpaṃ ca navabhiḥ śaraiḥ ||27||

28droṇaṃ ṣaṣṭyā naravyāghro vikarṇaṃ ca tribhiḥ śaraiḥ
|
śalyaṃ caiva tribhirbāṇai rājānaṃ caiva pañcabhiḥ ||28||

29pratyavidhyadameyātmā kirīṭī bharatarṣabha
|
taṃ sātyakirvirāṭaśca dhṛṣṭadyumnaśca pārṣataḥ ||29||

30draupadeyā'bhimanyuśca parivavrurdhanañjayam
|
tato droṇaṃ maheṣvāsaṃ gāṅgeyasya priye ratam ||30||

31abhyavartata pāñcālyaḥ saṃyuktaḥ saha somakaiḥ
|
bhīṣmastu rathināṃ śreṣṭho rājan vivyādha pāṇḍavam ||31||

32aśītyā niśitairbāṇaistato'krośanta tāvakāḥ
|
teṣāṃ tu ninadaṃ śrutvā sahitānāṃ prahṛṣṭavat ||32||

33praviveśa tato madhyaṃ narasiṃhaḥ pratāpavān
|
teṣāṃ mahārathānāṃ sa madhyaṃ prāpya dhanañjayaḥ ||33||

34cikrīḍa dhanuṣā rāja~llakṣaṃ kṛtvā mahārathān
|
tato duryodhano rājā bhīṣmamāha janeśvaraḥ ||34||

35pīḍyamānaṃ svakaṃ sainyaṃ dṛṣṭvā pārthena saṃyuge
|
eṣa pāṇḍusutastāta kṛṣṇena sahito balī ||35||

36yatatāṃ sarvasainyānāṃ mūlaṃ naḥ parikṛntati
|
tvayi jīvati gāṅgeya droṇe ca rathināṃ vare ||36||

37tvatkṛte caiva karṇo'pi nyastaśastro viśāṃpate
|
na yudhyati raṇe pārthaṃ hitakāmaḥ sadā mama ||37||

38sa tathā kuru gāṅgeya yathā hanyeta phālgunaḥ
|
evamuktastato rājan pitā devavratastava ||38||

39dhik kṣātraṃ dharmamityuktvā prāyāt pārtharathaṃ prati
|
ubhau śvetahayau rājan saṃsaktau prekṣya pārthivāḥ ||39||

40siṃhanādānbhṛśaṃ cakruḥ śaṅkhān dadhmuśca māriṣa
|
drauṇirduryodhanaścaiva vikarṇaśca tavātmajaḥ ||40||

41parivārya raṇe bhīṣmaṃ sthitā yuddhāya māriṣa
|
tathaiva pāṇḍavāḥ sarve parivārya dhanañjayam ||41||

42sthitā yuddhāya mahate tato yuddhamavartata
|
gāṅgeyastu raṇe pārthamānarcchannavabhiḥ śaraiḥ ||42||

43tamarjunaḥ pratyavidhyaddaśabhirmarmabhedibhiḥ
|
tataḥ śarasahasreṇa suprayuktena pāṇḍavaḥ ||43||

44arjunaḥ samaraślāghī bhīṣmasyāvārayaddiśaḥ
|
śarajālaṃ tatastattu śarajālena māriṣa ||44||

45vārayāmāsa pārthasya bhīṣmaḥ śāntanavastadā
|
ubhau paramasaṃhṛṣṭāvubhau yuddhābhinandinau ||45||

46nirviśeṣamayudhyetāṃ kṛtapratikṛtaiṣiṇau
|
bhīṣmacāpavimuktāni śarajālāni saṅghaśaḥ ||46||

47śīryamāṇānyadṛśyanta bhinnānyarjunasāyakaiḥ
|
tathaivārjunamuktāni śarajālāni sarvaśaḥ ||47||

48gāṅgeyaśaranunnāni prāpatanta mahītale
|
arjunaḥ pañcaviṃśatyā bhīṣmamārcchacchitaiḥ śaraiḥ ||48||

49bhīṣmo'pi samare pārthaṃ vivyādha niśitaiḥ śaraiḥ
|
anyonyasya hayān viddhvā dhvajau ca sumahābalau ||49||

50ratheṣāṃ rathacakre ca cikrīḍaturarindamau
|
tataḥ kruddho mahārāja bhīṣmaḥ praharatāṃ varaḥ ||50||

51vāsudevaṃ tribhirbāṇairājaghāna stanāntare
|
bhīṣmacāpacyutaistaistu nirviddho madhusūdanaḥ ||51||

52virarāja raṇe rājan sapuṣpa iva kiṃśukaḥ
|
tato'rjuno bhṛśaṃ kruddho nirviddhaṃ prekṣya mādhavam ||52||

53sārathiṃ kuruvṛddhasya nirbibheda śitaiḥ śaraiḥ
|
yatamānau tu tau vīrāvanyonyasya vadhaṃ prati ||53||

54na śaknutāṃ tadā'nyonyamabhisandhātumāhave
|
tau maṇḍalāni citrāṇi gatapratyāgatāni ca ||54||

55adarśayetāṃ bahudhā sūtasāmarthyalāghavāt
|
antaraṃ ca prahāreṣu tarkayantau parasparam ||55||

56rājannantaramārgasthau sthitāvāstāṃ muhurmuhuḥ
|
ubhau siṃharavonmiśraṃ śaṅkhaṃśabdaṃ ca cakratuḥ ||56||

57tathaiva cāpanirghoṣaṃ cakratustau mahārathau
|
tayoḥ śaṅkhaninādena rathanemisvanena ca ||57||

58dāritā sahasā bhūmiścakampe ca nanāda ca
|
nobhayorantaraṃ kaściddadṛśe bharatarṣabha ||58||

59balinau yuddhadurdharṣāvanyonyasadṛśāvubhau
|
cihnamātreṇa bhīṣmaṃ tu prajajñustatra kauravāḥ ||59||

60tathā pāṇḍusutāḥ pārthaṃ cihnamātreṇa jajñire
|
tayornuvarayordṛṣṭvā tādṛśaṃ taṃ parākramam ||60||

61vismayaṃ sarvabhūtāni jagmurbhārata saṃyuge
|
na tayorvivaraṃ kaścidraṇe paśyati bhārata ||61||

62dharme sthitasya hi yathā na kaścidvṛjinaṃ kvacit
|
ubhau ca śarajālena tāvadṛśyau babhūvatuḥ ||62||

63prakāśau ca punastūrṇaṃ babhūvaturubhau raṇe
|
tatra devāḥ sagandharvāścāraṇāścarṣibhiḥ saha ||63||

64anyonyaṃ pratyabhāṣanta tayordṛṣṭvā parākramam
|
na śakyau yudhi saṃrabdhau jetumetau kathañcana ||64||

65sadevāsuragandharvairlokairapi mahārathau
|
āścaryabhūtaṃ lokeṣu yuddhametanmahādbhutam ||65||

66naitādṛśāni yuddhāni bhaviṣyanti kathañcana
|
na hi śakyo raṇe jetuṃ bhīṣmaḥ pārthena dhīmatā ||66||

67sadhanuḥ sarathaḥ sāśvaḥ pravapan sāyakān raṇe
|
tathaiva pāṇḍavaṃ yuddhe devairapi durāsadam ||67||

68na vijetuṃ raṇe bhīṣma utsaheta dhanurdharam
|
ālokādapi yuddhaṃ hi samametadbhaviṣyati ||68||

69iti sma vāco'śrūyanta proccarantyastatastataḥ
|
gāṅgeyārjunayoḥ saṃkhye stavayuktā viśāṃpate ||69||

70tvadīyāstu tadā yodhāḥ pāṇḍaveyāśca bhārata
|
anyonyaṃ samare jaghnustayostatra parākrame ||70||

71śitadhāraistathā khaḍgairvimalaiśca paraśvadhaiḥ
|
śarairanyaiśca bahubhiḥ śastrairnānāvidhairapi ||71||

72ubhayoḥ senayoḥ śūrā nyakṛntanta parasparam
|
vartamāne tathā ghore tasmin yuddhe sudāruṇe
|
droṇapāñcālyayo rājan mahānāsītsamāgamaḥ ||72||
