Mahābhārata 4.51 (Virāṭaparvan)

1bhīṣma uvāca|sādhu paśyati vai drauṇiḥ kṛpaḥ sādhvanupaśyati
|
karṇastu kṣatradharmeṇa kevalaṃ yoddhamicchati ||1||

2ācāryo nābhivaktavyaḥ puruṣeṇa vijānatā
|
deśakālau tu saṃprekṣya yoddhavyamiti me matiḥ ||2||

3yasya sūryasamāḥ pañca sapatnāḥ syuḥ prahāriṇaḥ
|
kathamabhyudaye teṣāṃ na pramuhyeta paṇḍitaḥ ||3||

4svārthe sarve vimuhyanti ye'pi dharmavido janāḥ
|
tasmādrājan bravīmyeṣa vākyaṃ te yadi rocate ||4||

5karṇo hi yadavocat tvāṃ tejaḥ saṃjananāya tat
|
ācāryaputraḥ kṣamatāṃ mahat kāryamupasthitam ||5||

6nāyaṃ kālo virodhasya kaunteye samupasthite
|
kṣantavyaṃ bhavatā sarvamācāryeṇa kṛpeṇa ca ||6||

7bhavatāṃ hi kṛtāstratvaṃ yathā''ditye prabhā tathā
|
yathā candramaso lakṣmīḥ sarvathā nāpakṛṣyate ||7||

8evaṃ bhavatsu brāhmaṇyaṃ brahmāstraṃ ca pratiṣṭhitam
|
catvāra ekato vedāḥ kṣātramekatra dṛśyate ||8||

9naitat samastamubhayaṃ kasmiṃścidanuśuśruma
|
anyatra bhāratācāryāt saputrāditi me matiḥ ||9||

10vedāntāśca purāṇāni itihāsaṃ purātanam
|
jāmadagnyamṛte rājan ko droṇādadhiko bhavet ||10||

11brahmāstraṃ caiva vedāśca naitadanyatra dṛśyate
|
ācāryaputraḥ kṣamatāṃ nāyaṃ kālo vibhedane ||11||

12sarve saṃhatya yudhyāmaḥ pākaśāsanimāgatam ||12||

13balasya vyasanānīha yānyuktāni manīṣibhiḥ
|
mukhyo bhedo hi teṣāṃ tu pāpiṣṭho viduṣāṃ mataḥ ||13||

14aśvatthāmovāca | naiva nyāyyamidaṃ vācyamasmākaṃ puruṣarṣabha
|
kiṃ tu roṣaparītena guruṇā bhāṣitā guṇāḥ ||14||

15śatrorapi guṇā grāhyā doṣā vācyā gurorapi
|
sarvathā sarvayatnena putre śiṣye hitaṃ vadet ||15||

16duryodhana uvāca|ācārya eṣa kṣamatāṃ śāntiratra vidhīyatām
|
abhidyamāne tu gurau tadvṛttaṃ roṣakāritam ||16||

17vaiśampāyana uvāca|tato duryodhano droṇaṃ kṣamayāmāsa bhārata
|
saha karṇena bhīṣmeṇa kṛpeṇa ca mahātmanā ||17||

18droṇa uvāca|yadetat prathamaṃ vākyaṃ bhīṣmaḥ śāntanavo'bravīt
|
tenaivāhaṃ prasanno vai nītiratra vidhīyatām ||18||

19yathā duryodhanaṃ pārtho nopasarpati saṅgare
|
sāhasādyadi vā mohāt tathā nītirvidhīyatām ||19||

20vanavāse hyanirvṛtte darśayenna dhanañjayaḥ
|
dhanaṃ cālabhamāno'tra nādya tat kṣantumarhati ||20||

21yathā nāyaṃ samāyuñjyāddhārtarāṣṭrān kathañcana
|
na ca senā parājayyāt tathā nītirvidhīyatām||21||

22uktaṃ duryodhanenāpi purastādvākyamīdṛśam
|
tadanusmṛtya gāṅgeya yathāvadvaktumarhasi ||22||
