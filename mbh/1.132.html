Mahābhārata 1.132 (Ādiparvan)

1vaiśaṃpāyana uvāca|
tataḥ saṃpūjito droṇo bhīṣmeṇa dvipadāṃ varaḥ
|
viśaśrāma mahātejāḥ pūjitaḥ kuruveśmani||1||

2viśrānte'tha gurau tasminpautrānādāya kauravān
|
śiṣyatvena dadau bhīṣmo vasūni vividhāni ca||2||

3gṛhaṃ ca suparicchannaṃ dhanadhānyasamākulam
|
bhāradvājāya suprītaḥ pratyapādayata prabhuḥ||3||

4sa tāñśiṣyānmaheṣvāsaḥ pratijagrāha kauravān
|
pāṇḍavāndhārtarāṣṭrāṃśca droṇo muditamānasaḥ||4||

5pratigṛhya ca tānsarvāndroṇo vacanamabravīt
|
rahasyekaḥ pratītātmā kṛtopasadanāṃstathā||5||

6droṇa uvāca|
kāryaṃ me kāṅkṣitaṃ kiṃciddhṛdi saṃparivartate
|
kṛtāstraistatpradeyaṃ me tadetadvadatānaghāḥ||6||

7vaiśaṃpāyana uvāca|
tacchrutvā kauraveyāste tūṣṇīmāsanviśāṃpate
|
arjunastu tataḥ sarvaṃ pratijajñe parantapa||7||

8tato'rjunaṃ tadā mūrdhni samāghrāya punaḥ punaḥ
|
prītipūrvaṃ pariṣvajya praruroda mudā tadā||8||

9tato droṇaḥ pāṇḍuputrānastrāṇi vividhāni ca
|
grāhayāmāsa divyāni mānuṣāṇi ca vīryavān||9||

10rājaputrāstathā cānye sametya bharatarṣabha
|
abhijagmustato droṇamastrārthe dvijasattamam||10||

11vṛṣṇayaścāndhakāścaiva nānādeśyāśca pārthivāḥ
|
sūtaputraśca rādheyo guruṃ droṇamiyāttadā||11||

12spardhamānastu pārthena sūtaputro'tyamarṣaṇaḥ
|
duryodhanaṃ samāśritya so'vamanyata pāṇḍavān||12||

13abhyayātsa tato droṇaṃ dhanurvedacikīrṣayā
|
śikṣābhujavalodyogaisteṣu sarveṣu pāṇḍavaḥ||13||

14astravidyānurāgācca viśiṣṭo'bhavadarjunaḥ
|
tulyeṣvastraprayogeṣu lāghave sauṣṭaveṣu ca||14||

15sarveṣāmeva śiṣyāṇāṃ babhūvābhyadhiko'rjunaḥ
|
aindrimapratimaṃ droṇa upadeśeṣvamanyata||15||

16evaṃ sarvakumārāṇāmiṣvastraṃ pratyapādayat
|
kamaṇḍaluṃ ca sarveṣāṃ prāyacchaccirakāraṇāt||16||

17putrāya ca dadau kumbhamavilambanakāraṇāt
|
yāvatte nopagacchanti tāvadasmai parāṃ kriyām||17||

18droṇa ācaṣṭa putrāya tatkarma jiṣṇurauhata
|
tataḥ sa vāruṇāstreṇa pūrayitvā kamaṇḍalum||18||

19samamācāryaputreṇa gurumabhyeti phālgunaḥ
|
ācāryaputrāttasmāttu viśeṣopacaye'pṛthak||19||

20na vyahīyata medhāvī pārtho'pyastravidāṃ varaḥ
|
arjunaḥ paramaṃ yatnamātiṣṭhadgurupūjane||20||

21astre ca paramaṃ yogaṃ priyo droṇasya cābhavat
|
taṃ dṛṣṭvā nityamudyuktamiṣvastraṃ prati phālgunam||21||

22āhūya vacanaṃ droṇo rahaḥ sūdamabhāṣata
|
andhakāre'rjunāyānnaṃ na deyaṃ te kadācana
|
na cākhyeyamidaṃ cāpi madvākyaṃ vijaye tvayā||22||

23tataḥ kadācidbhuñjāne pravavau vāyurarjune
|
tena tatra pradīpaḥ sa dīpyamāno vilopitaḥ||23||

24bhukta eva tu kaunteyo nāsyādanyatra vartate
|
hastastejasvinastasya anugrahaṇakāraṇāt||24||

25tadabhyāsakṛtaṃ matvā rātrāvapi sa pāṇḍavaḥ
|
yogyāṃ cakre mahābāhurdhanuṣā pāṇḍunandanaḥ||25||

26tasya jyātalanirghoṣaṃ droṇaḥ śuśrāva bhārata
|
upetya cainamutthāya pariṣvajyedamabravīt||26||

27droṇa uvāca|
prayatiṣye tathā kartuṃ yathā nānyo dhanurdharaḥ
|
tvatsamo bhavitā loke satyametadbravīmi te||27||

28vaiśaṃpāyana uvāca|
tato droṇo'rjunaṃ bhūyo hayeṣu ca gajeṣu ca
|
ratheṣu bhūmāvapi ca raṇaśikṣāmaśikṣayat||28||

29gadāyuddhe'si caryāyāṃ tomaraprāsaśaktiṣu
|
droṇaḥ saṃkīrṇayuddhe ca śikṣayāmāsa kauravān||29||

30tasya tatkauśalaṃ śrutvā dhanurvedajighṛkṣavaḥ
|
rājāno rājaputrāśca samājagmuḥ sahasraśaḥ||30||

31tato niṣādarājasya hiraṇyadhanuṣaḥ sutaḥ
|
ekalavyo mahārāja droṇamabhyājagāma ha||31||

32na sa taṃ pratijagrāha naiṣādiriti cintayan
|
śiṣyaṃ dhanuṣi dharmajñasteṣāmevānvavekṣayā||32||

33sa tu droṇasya śirasā pādau gṛhya parantapaḥ
|
araṇyamanusaṃprāpya kṛtvā droṇaṃ mahīmayam||33||

34tasminnācāryavṛttiṃ ca paramāmāsthitastadā
|
iṣvastre yogamātasthe paraṃ niyamamāsthitaḥ||34||

35parayā śraddhayopeto yogena parameṇa ca
|
vimokṣādānasandhāne laghutvaṃ paramāpa saḥ|| 35||

36atha droṇābhyanujñātāḥ kadācitkurupāṇḍavāḥ
|
rathairviniryayuḥ sarve mṛgayāmarimardanāḥ||36||

37tatropakaraṇaṃ gṛhya naraḥ kaścidyadṛcchayā
|
rājannanujagāmaikaḥ śvānamādāya pāṇḍavān||37||

38teṣāṃ vicaratāṃ tatra tattatkarmacikīrṣayā
|
śvā caransa vane mūḍho naiṣādiṃ prati jagmivān||38||

39sa kṛṣṇamaladigdhāṅgaṃ kṛṣṇājinajaṭāgharam
|
naiṣādiṃ śvā samālakṣya bhaṣaṃstasthau tadantike||39||

40tadā tasyātha bhaṣataḥ śunaḥ sapta śarānmukhe
|
lāghavaṃ darśayannastre mumoca yugapadyathā||40||

41sa tu śvā śarapūrṇāsyaḥ pāṇḍavānājagāma ha
|
taṃ dṛṣṭvā pāṇḍavā vīrāḥ paraṃ vismayamāgatāḥ||41||

42lāghavaṃ śabdhavedhitvaṃ dṛṣṭvā tatparamaṃ tadā
|
prekṣya taṃ vrīḍitāścāsanpraśaśaṃsuśca sarvaśaḥ||42||

43taṃ tato'nveṣamāṇāste vane vananivāsinam
|
dadṛśuḥ pāṇḍavā rājannasyantamaniśaṃ śarān||43||

44na cainamabhyajānaṃste tadāvikṛtadarśanam
|
tathainaṃ paripapracchuḥ ko bhavānkasya vetyuta||44||

45ekalavya uvāca|
niṣādādhipatervīrā hiraṇyadhanuṣaḥ sutam
|
droṇaśiṣyaṃ ca māṃ vitta dhurvedakṛtaśramam||45||

46vaiśaṃpāyana uvāca|
te tamājñāya tattvena punarāgamya pāṇḍavāḥ
|
yathāvṛttaṃ vane sarvaṃ droṇāyācakhyuradbhutam||46||

47kaunteyastvarjuno rājannekalavyamanusmaran
|
raho droṇaṃ samāsādya praṇayādidamabravīt||47||

48arjuna uvāca|
nanvahaṃ parirabhyaikaḥ prītipūrvamidaṃ vacaḥ
|
bhavatokto na me śiṣyastvadviśiṣṭo bhaviṣyati||48||

49atha kasmānmadviśiṣṭo lokādapi ca vīryavān
|
anyo'sti bhavataḥ śiṣyo niṣādādhipateḥ sutaḥ||49||

50vaiśaṃpāyana uvāca|
muhūrtamiva taṃ droṇaścintayitvā viniścayam
|
savyasācinamādāya naiṣādiṃ prati jagmivān||50||

51dadarśa maladigdhāṅgaṃ jaṭilaṃ cīravāsasam
|
ekalavyaṃ dhanuṣpāṇimasyantamaniśaṃ śarān||51||

52ekalavyastu taṃ dṛṣṭvā droṇamāyāntamantikāt
|
abhigamyopasaṃgṛhya jagāma śirasā mahīm||52||

53pūjayitvā tato droṇaṃ vidhivatsa niṣādajaḥ
|
nivedya śiṣyamātmānaṃ tasthau prāñjaliragrataḥ||53||

54tato droṇo'bravīdrājannekalavyamidaṃ vacaḥ
|
yadi śiṣyo'si me vīra vetanaṃ dīyatāṃ mama||54||

55ekalavyastu tacchrutvā prīyamāṇo'bravīdidam
|
ekalavya uvāca|
kiṃ prayacchāmi bhagavannājñāpayatu māṃ guruḥ||55||

56na hi kiṃcidadeyaṃ me gurave brahmavittama
|
vaiśaṃpāyana uvāca|
tamabravīttvayāṅguṣṭho dakṣiṇo dīyatāmiti||56||

57ekalavyastu tacchrutvā vaco droṇasya dāruṇam
|
pratijñāmātmano rakṣansatye ca sa niyataḥ sadā||57||

58tathaiva hṛṣṭavadanastathaivādīnamānasaḥ
|
chittvāvicārya taṃ prādāddroṇāyāṅguṣṭhamātmanaḥ||58||

59tataḥ śaraṃ tu naiṣādīraṅgulībhirvyakarṣata
|
na tathā ca sa śīghro'bhūdyathā pūrvaṃ narādhipa||59||

60tato'rjunaḥ prītamanā babhūva vigatajvaraḥ
|
droṇaśca satyavāgāsīnnānyo'bhibhavitā'rjunaṃ||60||

61droṇasya tu tadā śiṣyau gadāyogyau babhūvatuḥ
|
duryodhanaśca bhīmaśca sadā saṃrabdhamānasau||61||

62aśvatthāmā rahasyeṣu sarveṣvabhyadhiko'bhavat
|
tathātipuruṣānanyāntsārukau yamajāvubhau||62||

63yudhiṣṭhiro rathaśreṣṭhaḥ sarvatra tu dhanañjayaḥ
|
prathitaḥ sāgarāntāyāṃ rathayūthapayūthapaḥ||63||

64buddhiyogabalotsāhaḥ sarvāstreṣu ca niṣṭhitaḥ
|
astre gurvanurāge ca viśiṣṭo'bhavadarjunaḥ||64||

65tulyeṣvastropadeśeṣu sauṣṭhavena ca vīryavān
|
ekaḥ sarvakumārāṇāṃ babhūvātiratho'rjunaḥ||65||

66prāṇādhikaṃ bhīmasenaṃ kṛtavidyaṃ dhanañjayam
|
dhārtarāṣṭrā durātmāno nāmṛṣyanta parasparam||66||

67tāṃstu sarvānsamānīya sarvavidyāstraśikṣitān
|
droṇaḥ praharaṇajñāne jijñāsuḥ puruṣarṣabhaḥ||67||

68kṛtrimaṃ bhāsamāropya vṛkṣāgre śilpibhiḥ kṛtam
|
avijñātaṃ kumārāṇāṃ lakṣyabhūtamupādiśat||68||

69droṇa uvāca|
śīghraṃ bhavantaḥ sarve'pi dhanūṃṣyādāya sarvaśaḥ
|
bhāsametaṃ samuddiśya tiṣṭhadhvaṃ sandhiteṣataḥ||69||

70madvākyasamakālaṃ tu śiro'sya vinipātyatām
|
ekaikaśo niyokṣyāmi tathā kuruta putrakāḥ||70||

71vaiśaṃpāyana uvāca|
tato yudhiṣṭhiraṃ pūrvamuvācāṅgirasāṃ varaḥ
|
saṃdhatsva bāmaṃ durdharṣa madvākyānte vimuñca tam||71||

72tato yudhiṣṭhiraḥ pūrvaṃ dhanurgṛhya parantapaḥ
|
tasthau bhāsaṃ samuddiśya guruvākyapracoditaḥ||72||

73tato vitatadhanvānaṃ droṇastaṃ kurunandanam
|
sa muhūrtāduvācedaṃ vacanaṃ bharatarṣabha||73||

74paśyasi tvaṃ drumāgrasthaṃ bhāsaṃ naravarātmaja
|
paśyāmītyevamācāryaṃ pratyuvāca yudhiṣṭhiraḥ||74||

75sa muhūrtādiva punardroṇastaṃ pratyabhāṣata
|
droṇa uvāca|
atha vṛkṣamimaṃ māṃ vā bhrātṝnvā'pi prapaśyasi||75||

76tamuvāca sa kaunteyaḥ paśyāmyenaṃ navaspatim
|
bhavantaṃ ca tathā bhrātṝnbhāsaṃ ceti punaḥpunaḥ||76||

77tamuvācāpasarpeti droṇo'prītamanā iva
|
naitacchakyaṃ tvayā veddhuṃ lakṣyamityeva kutsayan||77||

78tato duryodhanādīṃstāndhārtarāṣṭrānmahāyaśāḥ
|
tenaiva kramayogena jijñāsuḥ paryapṛcchata||78||

79anyāṃśca śiṣyānbhīmādīnrājñaścaivānyadeśajān
|
tathā ca sarve tatsarvaṃ paśyāma iti kutsitāḥ||79||
