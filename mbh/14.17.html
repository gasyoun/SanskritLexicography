Mahābhārata 14.17 (Āśvamedhikaparvan)

1vāsudeva uvāca|
tatastasyopasaṅgṛhya pādau praśnān sudurvacān
|
papraccha tāṃśca dharmānsa prāha dharmabhṛtāṃ varaḥ||1||

2kaśyapa uvāca|
kathaṃ śarīraṃ cyavate kathaṃ caivopapadyate
|
kathaṃ kaṣṭācca saṃsārāt saṃsaranparimucyate||2||

3ātmā ca prakṛtiṃ muktvā taccharīraṃ vimuñcati
|
śarīrataśca nirmuktaḥ kathamanyat prapadyate||3||

4kathaṃ śubhāśubhe cāyaṃ karmaṇī svakṛte naraḥ
|
upabhuṅkte kva vā karma videhasyāvatiṣṭhate||4||

5brāhmaṇa uvāca|
evaṃ sañcoditaḥ siddhaḥ praśnāṃstān pratyabhāṣata
|
ānupūryeṇa vārṣṇeya tanme nigadataḥ śṛṇu||5||

6siddha uvāca|
āyuḥ kīrtikarāṇīha yāni kṛtyāni sevate
|
śarīragrahaṇe yasmiṃsteṣu kṣīṇeṣu sarvaśaḥ||6||

7āyuḥkṣayaparītātmā viparītāni sevate
|
buddhirvyāvartate cāsya vināśe pratyupasthite||7||

8sattvaṃ balaṃ ca kālaṃ ca viditvā cātmanastathā
|
ativelamupāśnāti svaviruddhānyanātmavān||8||

9yadāyamatikaṣṭāni sarvāṇyupaniṣevate
|
atyarthamapi vā bhuṅkte na vā bhukto kadācana||9||

10duṣṭānnāmiṣapānaṃ ca yadanyonyavirodhi ca
|
guru cāpyamitaṃ bhuṅktaṃ nātijīrṇe'pi vā punaḥ||10||

11vyāyāmamatimātraṃ ca vyavāyaṃ copasevate
|
satataṃ karmalābhādvā prāptaṃ vegaṃ vidhārayet||11||

12rasābhiyuktamannaṃ vā divā svapnaṃ ca sevate
|
apakvānāgate kāle svayaṃ doṣān prakopayet||12||

13svadoṣakopanādrogaṃ labhate maraṇāntikam
|
api vodvandhanādīni parītāni vyavasthati||13||

14tasya taiḥ kāraṇairjantoḥ śarīraṃ cyavate tadā
|
jīvitaṃ procyamānaṃ tadyathāvadupadhāraya||14||

15ūṣmā prakupitaḥ kāye tīvravāyusamīritaḥ
|
śarīramanuparyetya sarvānprāṇān ruṇaddhi vai||15||

16atyarthaṃ balavānūṣmā śarīre parikopitaḥ
|
bhinatti jīvasthānāni marmāṇi viddhi tattvataḥ||16||

17tataḥ savedanaḥ sadyo jīvaḥ pracyavate kṣarāt
|
śarīraṃ tyajate jantuśchidyamāneṣu marmasu||17||

18vedanābhiḥ parītātmā tadviddhi dvijasattama
|
jātīmaraṇasaṃvignāḥ satataṃ sarvajantavaḥ||18||

19dṛśyante santyajantaśca śarīrāṇi dvijarṣabha
|
garbhasaṅkramaṇe cāpi marmaṇāmatisarpaṇe||19||

20tādṛśīmeva labhate vedanāṃ mānavaḥ punaḥ
|
bhinnasandhiratha kledamadbhiḥ sa labhate naraḥ||20||

21yathā pañcasu bhūteṣu sambhūtatvaṃ niyacchati
|
śaityātprakupita: kāye tīvravāyusamīritaḥ||21||

22yaḥ sa pañcasu bhūteṣu prāṇāpāne vyavasthitaḥ
|
sa gacchatyūrdhvago vāyuḥ kṛcchrānmuktvā śarīriṇaḥ||22||

23śarīraṃ ca jahātyevaṃ nirucchvāsaśca dṛśyate
|
sa niruṣmā nirucchvāso ni:śrīko hatacetanaḥ||23||

24brahmaṇā samparityakto mṛta ityucyate naraiḥ
|
srotobhiryairvijānāti indriyārthāśarīrabhṛt||24||

25taireva na vijānāti prāṇānāhārasambhavān
|
tatraiva kurute kāye ya: sa jīvaḥ sanātanaḥ||25||

26tathā yadyadbhavedyuktaṃ sannipāte kvacit kvacit
|
tattanmarma vijānīhi śāstradṛṣṭaṃ hi tattathā||26||

27teṣu marmasu bhinneṣu tataḥ sa samudīrayan
|
āviśya hṛdayaṃ jantoḥ sattvaṃ cāśu ruṇaddhi vai||27||

28tataḥ sacetano janturnābhijānāti kiñcana
|
tamasā saṃvṛtajñānaḥ saṃvṛteṣveva marmasu
|
sa jīvo niradhiṣṭhānaścālyate mātariśvanā||28||

29tataḥ sa taṃ mahocchvāsaṃ bhṛśamucchvasya dāruṇam
|
niṣkrāmankampayatyāśu taccharīramacetanam||29||

30sa jīvaḥ pracyutaḥ kāyāt karmabhiḥ svaiḥ samāvṛtaḥ
|
abhitaḥ svaiḥ śubhaiḥ puṇyaiḥ pāpairvāpyupapadyate||30||

31brāhmaṇā jñānasampannā yathāvacchrutaniścayāḥ
|
itaraṃ kṛtapuṇyaṃ vā taṃ vijānanti lakṣaṇaiḥ||31||

32yathāndhakāre khadyotaṃ līyamānaṃ tatastataḥ
|
cakṣuṣmantaḥ prapaśyanti tathā ca jñānacakṣuṣaḥ||32||

33paśyantyevaṃvidhaṃ siddhā jīvaṃ divyena cakṣuṣā
|
cyavantaṃ jāyamānaṃ ca yoniṃ cānupraveśitam||33||

34tasya sthānāni dṛṣṭāni trividhānīha śāstrataḥ
|
karmabhūmiriyaṃ bhūmiryatra tiṣṭhanti jantavaḥ||34||

35tataḥ śubhāśubhaṃ kṛtvā labhante sarvadehinaḥ
|
ihaivoccāvacān bhogān prāpnuvanti svakarmabhiḥ||35||

36ihaivāśubhakarmāṇaḥ karmabhirnirayaṃ gatāḥ
|
avāggatiriyaṃ kaṣṭā yatra pacyanti mānavāḥ
|
tasmātsudurlabho mokṣo rakṣyaścātmā tato bhṛśam||36||

37ūrdhvaṃ tu jantavo gatvā yeṣu sthāneṣvavasthitāḥ
|
kīrtyamānāni tānīha tattvataḥ sannibodha me||37||

38tacchrutvā naiṣṭhikī buddhiṃ buddhyethāḥ karmaniścayam
|
tārārūpāṇi sarvāṇi yatraitaccandramaṇḍalam||38||

39yatra vibhrājate loke svabhāsā sūryamaṇḍalam
|
sthānānyetāni jānīhi janānāṃ puṇyakarmaṇām||39||

40karmakṣayācca te sarve cyavante vai punaḥ punaḥ
|
tatrāpi ca viśeṣo'sti divi nīcoccamadhyamaḥ||40||

41na ca tatrāpi santoṣo dṛṣṭrā dīptatarāṃ śriyam
|
ityetā gatayaḥ sarvāḥ pṛthakte samudīritāḥ||41||

42upapattiṃ tu vakṣyāmi garbhasyāhamataḥ param
|
tathā tanme nigadataḥ śṛṇuṣvāvahito dvija||42||
