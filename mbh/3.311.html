Mahābhārata 3.311 (Āraṇyakaparvan)

1janamejaya uvāca|
evaṃ hṛtāyāṃ bhāryāyāṃ prāpya kleśamanuttamam
|
pratipadya tataḥ kṛṣṇāṃ kimakurvata pāṇḍavāḥ||1||

2vaiśampāyana uvāca|
evaṃ hṛtāyāṃ kṛṣṇāyāṃ prāpya kleśamanuttamam
|
vihāya kāmyakaṃ rājā saha bhrātṛbhiracyutaḥ||2||

3punardvaitavanaṃ ramyamājagāma yudhiṣṭhiraḥ
|
svādumūlaphalaṃ ramyaṃ vicitrabahupādapam||3||

4anubhuktaphalāhārāḥ sarva eva mitāśanāḥ
|
nyavasan pāṇḍavāstatra kṛṣṇayā saha bhāryayā||4||

5vasan dvaitavane rājā kuntīputro yudhiṣṭhiraḥ
|
bhīmaseno'rjunaścaiva mādrīputrau ca pāṇḍavau||5||

6brāhmaṇārthe parākrāntā dharmātmāno yatavratāḥ
|
kleśamārcchanta vipulaṃ sukhodarkaṃ parantapāḥ||6||

7tasmin prativasantaste yatprāpuḥ kurusattamāḥ
|
vana kleśaṃ sukhodarkaṃ tatpravakṣyāmi te śṛṇu||7||

8araṇīsahitaṃ manthaṃ brāhmaṇasya tapasvinaḥ
|
mṛgasya gharṣamāṇasya viṣāṇe samasajjata||8||

9tadādāya gato rājaṃstvaramāṇo mahāmṛgaḥ
|
āśramāntaritaḥ śīghraṃ plavamāno mahājavaḥ||9||

10hriyamāṇaṃ tu taṃ dṛṣṭvā sa vipraḥ kurusattama
|
tvarito'bhyāgamat tatra agnihotraparīpsayā||10||

11ajātaśatrumāsīnaṃ bhrātṛbhiḥ sahitaṃ vane
|
āgamya brāhmaṇastūrṇaṃ santaptaścedamabravīt||11||

12araṇīsahitaṃ manthaṃ samāsaktaṃ vanaspatau
|
mṛgasya gharṣamāṇasya viṣāṇe samasajjata||12||

13tamādāya gato rājaṃratvaramāṇo mahāmṛgaḥ
|
āśramāt tvaritaḥ śīghraṃ plavamāno mahājavaḥ||13||

14tasya gatvā padaṃ rājannāsādya ca mahāmṛgam
|
agnihotraṃ na lupyeta tadānayata pāṇḍavāḥ||14||

15brāhmaṇasya vacaḥ śrutvā santapto'tha yudhiṣṭhiraḥ
|
dhanurādāya kaunteyaḥ prādravadbhrātṛbhiḥ saha||15||

16sannaddhā dhanvinaḥ sarve prādravannarapuṅgavāḥ
|
brāhmaṇārthe yatantaste śīghramanvagamanmṛgam||16||

17karṇinālīkanārācānutsṛjanto mahārathāḥ
|
nāvidhyan pāṇḍavāstatra paśyanto mṛgamantikāt||17||

18teṣāṃ prayatamānānāṃ nādṛśyata mahāmṛgaḥ
|
apaśyanto mṛgaṃ śāntā duḥkhaṃ prāptā manasvinaḥ||18||

19śītalacchāyamāgamya nyagrodhaṃ gahane vane
|
kṣutpipāsāparītāṅgāḥ pāṇḍavāḥ samupāviśan||19||

20teṣāṃ samupaviṣṭānāṃ nakulo duḥkhitastadā
|
abravīdbhrātaraṃ śreṣṭhamamarṣāt kurunandanam||20||

21nāsmin kule jātu mamajja dharmo na cālasyādarthalopo babhūva ha
|
anuttarāḥ sarvabhūteṣu bhūyaḥ samprāptāḥ smaḥ saṃśayaṃ kinnu rājan||21||
