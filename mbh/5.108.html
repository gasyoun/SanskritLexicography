Mahābhārata 5.108 (Udyogaparvan)

1suparṇa uvāca|
anuśiṣṭo'smi devena gālava jñānayoginā
|
brūhi kāmaṃ tu kāṃ yāmi draṣṭuṃ prathamato diśam ||1||

2pūrvāṃ vā dakṣiṇāṃ vā'hamathavā paścimāṃ diśam
|
uttarāṃ vā dvijaśreṣṭha kuto gacchāmi gālava ||2||

3yasyāmudayate pūrvaṃ sarvalokaprabhāvanaḥ
|
savitā yatra saṃdhyāyāṃ sādhyānāṃ vartate tapaḥ ||3||

4yasyāṃ pūrvaṃ matiryātā yayā vyāptamidaṃ jagat
|
cakṣuṣī yatra dharmasya yantravai supratiṣṭhite ||4||

5kṛtaṃ yato hutaṃ havyaṃ sarpate sarvatodiśam
|
etaddvāraṃ dvijaśreṣṭha divasasya tathā'dhvanaḥ ||5||

6atra pūrvaṃ prasūtā vai dākṣāyaṇyaḥ prajāḥ striyaḥ
|
yasyāṃ diśi pravṛddhāśca kaśyapasyātmasaṃbhavāḥ ||6||

7ato mūlaṃ surāṇāṃ śrīryatra śakro'bhyaṣicyata
|
surarājyena viprarṣe devaiścātra tapaścitam ||7||

8etasmāt kāraṇādbrahman pūrvetyeṣā digucyate
|
yasmāt pūrvatare kāle pūrvamevāvṛtā suraiḥ ||8||

9ata eva ca sarveṣāṃ purvāmāśāṃ pracakṣate
|
pūrvaṃ sarvāṇi kāryāṇi daivāni sukhamīpsatā ||9||

10atra vedān jagau pūrvaṃ bhagavān lokabhāvanaḥ
|
atraivoktā savitrā''sīt sāvitrī brahmavādiṣu ||10||

11atra dattāni sūryeṇa yajūṃṣi dvijasattama
|
atra labdhavaraḥ somaḥ suraiḥ kratuṣu pīyate ||11||

12atra tṛptā hutavahāḥ svāṃ yonimupabhuñjate
|
atra pātālamāśritya varuṇaḥ śriyamāpa ca ||12||

13atra pūrvaṃ vasiṣṭhasya paurāṇasya dvijarṣabha
|
sūtiścaiva pratiṣṭhā ca nidhanaṃ ca prakāśate ||13||

14oṅkārasyātra jāyante sṛtayo daśatīrdaśa
|
pibanti munayo yatra havirdhūmaṃ sma dhūmapāḥ ||14||

15prokṣitā yatra bahavo varāhādyā mṛgā vane
|
śakreṇa yajñabhāgārthe daivateṣu prakalpitāḥ ||15||

16atrāhitāḥ kṛtaghnāśca mānuṣāścāsurāśca ye
|
udayaṃstān hi sarvān vai krodhāddhanti vibhāvasuḥ ||16||

17etaddvāraṃ trilokasya svargasya ca sukhasya ca
|
eṣa pūrvo diśāṃ bhāgo viśāvo'tra yadīcchasi ||17||

18priyaṃ kāryaṃ hi me tasya yasyāsmi vacane sthitaḥ
|
brūhi gālava yāsyāmi śṛṇu cāpyaparāṃ diśam ||18||
