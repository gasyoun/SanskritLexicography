Mahābhārata 18.7 (Svargārohaṇaparvan)

1imāṃ bhāratasāvitrīṃ prātarutthāya yaḥ paṭhet
|
saptajanma kṛtaiḥ pāpaiḥ sa muktaḥ sukham edhate||91||

2divā vā yadi vā rātrau vaneṣu viṣayeṣu ca
|
na bhayaṃ vidyate kiṃcit kāryasiddhiḥ bhaviṣyati||92||

3yat phalaṃ go sahasrasya svarṇenālaṅkṛtasya ca
|
dattasya vidhinā pātre tat phalam labhate naraḥ||93||

4ahorātra kṛtaṃ pāpaṃ śravaṇāt eva naśyati
|
saṃvatsarakṛtaṃ pāpaṃ paṭhanāt eva naśyati||94||

5paṭhatāṃ śṛṇvatāṃ caiva viṣṇoḥ mahātmyamuttamam
|
duḥsvapnanāśanaṃ caiva susvapnam ca bhaviṣyati||95||

6bhārataṃ pañcamaṃ vedaḥ yaḥ paṭhet śṛṇuyādapi
|
sa mukta sarvapāpebhyo viṣṇusāyujyamāpnuyāt||96||

7bhārataṃ pādamātreṇa śṛṇvan pāpaiḥ pramucyate
|
śṛṇu rājan yathā vṛttaṃ tathā vakṣyāmi te kathām||97||

8sā kathā bhāratī puṇyā draupadī sā pativratā
|
pāṇḍavānāṃ snuṣā dhanyā prasīda puruṣottama||98||

9gavāṃ śataṃ kanakaśṛṅgamayaṃ dadāti viprāya vedaviduṣe ca bahuśrutāya
|
puṇyāṃ ca bhāratakathāṃ paṭhati śṛṇoti tulyaṃ phalaṃ bhavati tasya ca tasya caiva||99||

10ādau pāṇḍavadhārtarāṣṭrajananaṃ lākṣāgṛhe dāhanam
dyūtaṃ śrīharaṇaṃ vane viharaṇaṃ matsyālaye vartanam
|
līlāgograhaṇaṃ raṇe viharaṇaṃ saṃdhikriyājṛṃbhaṇam
paścādbhīṣmasuyodhanādinidhanaṃ hyetanmahābhāratam||100||

11dharmo vivarthati yudhiṣṭhirakīrtanena pāpaṃ praṇaśyati vṛkodarakīrtanena
|
śatrurvinaśyati dhanañjayakīrtanena mādrīsutau kathayatāṃ na bhavanti rogāḥ||
