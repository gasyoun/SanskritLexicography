Mahābhārata 12.79 (Śāntiparvan)

1yudhiṣṭhira uvāca|
kva samutthāḥ kathaṃśīlā ṛtvijaḥ syuḥ pitāmaha
|
kathaṃvidhāśca rājendra tadbrūhi vadatāṃ vara||1||

2bhīṣma uvāca|
pratikarma parācāra ṛtvijāṃ sma vidhīyate
|
chandaḥ sāmādi vijñāya dvijānāṃ śrutameva ca||2||

3ye tvekamatayo nityaṃ vīrāṇāṃ priyavādinaḥ
|
parasparasya suhṛdaḥ samantātsamadarśinaḥ||3||

4anṛśaṃsāḥ satyavākyā akusīdā atharjavaḥ
|
adroho'nabhimānaśca hrīstitikṣā damaḥ śamaḥ||4||

5dhīmānsatyadhṛtirdānto bhūtānāmavihiṃsakaḥ
|
akāmadveṣasaṃyuktastribhiḥ śuklaiḥ samanvitaḥ||5||

6ahiṃsako jñānatṛptaḥ sa brahmāsanamarhati
|
ete mahartvijastāta sarve mānyā yathārhataḥ||6||

7yudhiṣṭhira uvāca|
yadidaṃ vedavacanaṃ dakṣiṇāsu vidhīyate
|
idaṃ deyamidaṃ deyaṃ na kvacidvyavatiṣṭhate||7||

8nedaṃ pratidhanaṃ śāstramāpaddharmānuśāstrataḥ
|
ājñā śāstrasya ghoreyaṃ na śaktiṃ samavekṣate||8||

9śraddhāvatā ca yaṣṭavyamityeṣā vaidikī śrutiḥ
|
mithyopetasya yajñasya kimu śraddhā kariṣyati||9||

10bhīṣma uvāca|
na vedānāṃ paribhavānna śāṭhyena na māyayā
|
kaścinmahadavāpnoti mā te'bhūdbuddhirīdṛśī||10||

11yajñāṅgaṃ dakṣiṇā tāta vedānāṃ paribṛṃhaṇam
|
na yajñā dakṣiṇāhīnāstārayanti kathaṃcana||11||

12śaktistu pūrṇapātreṇa sammitā na samā'bhavat
|
avaśyaṃ tāta yaṣṭavyaṃ tribhirvarṇairyathāvidhi||12||

13somo rājā brāhmaṇānāmityeṣā vaidikī sthitiḥ
|
taṃ ca vikretumicchanti na vṛthā vṛttiriṣyate||13||

14tena krītena yajñena tato yajñaḥ pratāyate
|
ityevaṃ dharmato dhyātamṛṣibhirdharmacāribhiḥ||14||

15pumānyajñaśca somaśca nyāyavṛtto yadā bhavet
|
anyāyavṛttaḥ puruṣo na parasya na cātmanaḥ||15||

16śarīravṛttamāsthāya ityeṣā śrūyate śrutiḥ
|
nātisamyak praṇītāni brāhmaṇānāṃ mahātmanām||16||

17tapo yajñādapi śreṣṭhamityeṣā paramā śrutiḥ
|
tatte tapaḥ pravakṣyāmi vidvaṃstadapi me śṛṇu||17||

18ahiṃsā satyavacanamānṛśaṃsyaṃ damo ghṛṇā
|
etattapo vidurdhīrā na śarīrasya śoṣaṇam||18||

19aprāmāṇyaṃ ca vedānāṃ śāstrāṇāṃ cābhilaṅghanam
|
avyavasthā ca sarvatra tadvai nāśanamātmanaḥ||19||

20nibodha devahotṝṇāṃ vidhānaṃ pārtha yādṛśam
|
cittiḥ sruk
cittiḥ sruk | cittamājyam | vāgvediḥ | ādhītaṃ barhiḥ | keto agniḥ | vijñātamagniḥ | vākpatirhotā | mana upavaktā | prāṇo haviḥ | sāmādhvaryuḥ, iti daśa hotāraḥ(taittirīyāraṇyakam 3.1) |
cittamājyaṃ ca pavitraṃ jñānamuttamam||20||

21sarvaṃ jihmaṃ mṛtyupadamārjavaṃ brahmaṇaḥ padam
|
etāvān jñānaviṣayaḥ kiṃ pralāpaḥ kariṣyati||21||
