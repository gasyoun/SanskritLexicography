Mahābhārata 3.38 (Āraṇyakaparvan)

1janamejaya uvāca|
bhagavañcchrotumicchāmi pārthasyākliṣṭakarmaṇaḥ
|
vistareṇa kathāmetāṃ yathāstrāṇyupalabdhavān||1||

2yathā ca puruṣavyāghro dīrghabāhurdhanañjayaḥ
|
vanaṃ praviṣṭastejasvī nirmanuṣyamabhītavat||2||

3kiṃ ca tena kṛtaṃ tatra vasatā brahmavittama
|
kathaṃ ca bhagavānsthāṇurdevarājaśca toṣitaḥ||3||

4etadicchāmyahaṃ śrotuṃ tvatprasādādvijottama
|
tvaṃ hi sarvajña divyaṃ ca mānuṣaṃ caiva vetthaha||4||

5atyadbhutatamaṃ brahmanromaharṣaṇamarjunaḥ
|
bhavena saha saṅgrāmaṃ cakārāpratimaṃ kila||5||

6purā praharatāṃ śreṣṭhaḥ saṅgrāmeṣvaparājitaḥ
|
yacchrutvā narasiṃhānāṃ dainyaharṣātivismayāt||6||

7śūrāṇāmapi pārthānāṃ hṛdayāni cakampire
|
yadyacca kṛtavānanyatpārthastadakhilaṃ vada||7||

8na hyasya ninditaṃ jiṣṇoḥ susūkṣmamapi lakṣaye
|
caritaṃ tasya śūrasya tanme sarvaṃ prakīrtaya||8||

9vaiśaṃpāyana uvāca|
kathayiṣyāmi te tāta kathāmetāṃ mahātmanaḥ
|
divyāṃ pauravaśārdūla mahatīmadbhuto'pamām||9||

10gātrasaṃsparśasambaddhāṃ tryambakeṇa sahānagha
|
pārthasya devadevena śṛṇu samyaksamāgamam||10||

11yudhiṣṭhiraniyogātsa jagāmāmitavikramaḥ
|
śakraṃ sureśvaraṃ draṣṭuṃ devadevaṃ ca śaṅkaram||11||

12divyaṃ taddhanurādāya khaḍgaṃ ca kanakatsarum
|
mahābalo mahābāhurarjunaḥ kāryasiddhaye||12||

13diśaṃ hyudīcīṃ kauravyo himavacchikharaṃ prati
|
aindriḥ sthiramanā rājansarvalokamahārathaḥ||13||

14tvarayā parayā yuktastapase dhṛtaniścayaḥ
|
vanaṃ kaṇṭakitaṃ ghorameka evānvapadyata||14||

15nānāpuṣpaphalopetaṃ nānāpakṣiniṣevitam
|
nānāmṛgagaṇākīrṇaṃ siddhacāraṇasevitam||15||

16tataḥ prayāte kaunteye vanaṃ mānuṣavarjitam
|
śaṅkhānāṃ paṭahānāṃ ca śabdaḥ samabhavaddivi||16||

17puṣpavarṣaṃ cānumahannipapāta mahītale
|
meghajālaṃ ca vitataṃ chādayāmāsa sarvataḥ||17||

18so'tītya vanadurgāṇi sannikarṣe mahāgireḥ
|
śuśubhe himavatpṛṣṭhe vasamāno'rjunastadā||18||

19tatrāpaśyaddrumānphullānvihagairvalgunāditān
|
nadīśca vipulāvartā vaidūryavimalaprabhāḥ||19||

20haṃsakāraṇḍavodgītāḥ sārasābhirutāstathā
|
puṃskokilarutāścaiva krauñcabarhiṇanāditāḥ||20||

21manoharavanopetāstasminnatiratho'rjunaḥ
|
puṇyaśītāmalajalāḥ paśyanprītamanābhavat||21||

22ramaṇīye vanoddeśe ramamāṇo'rjunastadā
|
tapasyugre vartamāna ugratejā mahāmanāḥ||22||

23darbhacīraṃ nivasyātha daṇḍājinavibhūṣitaḥ
|
śīrṇaṃ ca patitaṃ bhūmau parṇaṃ samupayuktavān||23||

24pūrṇe pūrṇe trirātre tu māsamekaṃ phalāśanaḥ
|
dviguṇena hi kālena dvitīyaṃ māsamatyayāt||24||

25tṛtīyamapi māsaṃ sa pakṣeṇāhāramācaran
|
caturthe tvatha samprāpte māse bharatasattamaḥ||25||

26vāyubhakṣo mahābāhurabhavatpāṇḍunandanaḥ
|
ūrdhvabāhurnirālambaḥ padāṅguṣṭhāgradhiṣṭhitaḥ||26||

27sadopasparśanāccāsya babhūvuramitaujasaḥ
|
vidyudambhoruhanibhā jaṭāstasya mahātmanaḥ||27||

28tato maharṣayaḥ sarve jagmurdevaṃ pinākinam
|
niveda‌yiṣavaḥ pārthaṃ tapasyugre samāsthitam||28||

29taṃ praṇamya mahādevaṃ śaśaṃsuḥ pārthakarma tat
|
eṣa pārthoṃ mahātejā himavatpṛṣṭhamāsthitaḥ||29||

30ugre tapasi duṣpāre sthito dhūmāyayandiśaḥ
|
tasya deveśa na vayaṃ vidmaḥ sarve cikīrṣitam||30||

31santāpayati naḥ sarvānasau sādhu nivāryatām
|
teṣāṃ tadvacanaṃ śrutvā munīnāṃ bhāvitātmanām||31||

32umāpatirbhūtapatirvākyametaduvāca ha
|
mahādeva uvāca|
na vo viṣādaḥ kartavyaḥ phālgunaṃ prati sarvaśaḥ||32||

33śīghraṃ gacchata saṃhṛṣṭā yathāgatamatandritāḥ
|
ahamasya vijānāmi saṅkalpaṃ manāsa sthitam||33||

34nāsya svargaspṛhā kācinnaiśvaryasya tathāyuṣaḥ
|
yattasya kāṅkṣitaṃ sarvaṃ tatkariṣye'hamadya vai||34||

35vaiśaṃpāyana uvāca|
tacchrutvā śarvavacanamṛṣayaḥ satyavādinaḥ
|
prahṛṣṭamanaso jagmuryathā svānpunarālayān||35||
