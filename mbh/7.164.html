Mahābhārata 7.164 (Droṇaparvan)

1sañjaya uvāca|
prakāśite tadā loke rajasā tamasāvṛte
|
samājagmuratho vīrāḥ parasparavadhaiṣiṇaḥ||1||

2te sametya raṇe rājan śastraprāsāsidhāriṇaḥ
|
parasparamudaikṣanta parasparakṛtāgasaḥ||2||

3pradīpānāṃ sahasraiśca dīpyamānaiḥ samaṃtataḥ
|
ratnācitaiḥ svarṇadaṇḍairgandhatailāvasiñcitaiḥ||3||

4devagandharvadīpādyaiḥ prabhābhiradhikojjvalaiḥ
|
virarāja tadā bhūmirgrahairdyauriva bhārata||4||

5ulkāśataiḥ prajvalitai raṇabhūmirvyarājata
|
dahyamāneva lokānāmabhāve ca vasundharā||5||

6vyadīpyanta diśaḥ sarvāḥ pradīpaistaiḥ samaṃtataḥ
|
varṣāpradoṣe khadyotairvṛtā vṛkṣā ivābabhuḥ||6||

7asajjanta tato vīrā vīreṣveva pṛthak pṛthak
|
nāgā nāgaiḥ samājagmusturagā hayasādibhiḥ||7||

8rathā rathavaraireva samājagmurmudā yutāḥ
|
tasmin rātrimukhe ghore tava putrasya śāsanāt ||8||

9caturaṅgasya sainyasya saṃpātaśca mahānabhūt
|
tato'rjuno mahārāja kauravāṇāmanīkinīm||9||

10vyadhamattvarayā yuktaḥ kṣapayansarvapārthivān
|
dhṛtarāṣṭra uvāca|
tasminpraviṣṭe saṃrabdhe mama putrasya vāhinīm||10||

11amṛṣyamāṇe durdharṣe kathamāsīnmano hi vaḥ
|
kimakurvata sainyāni praviṣṭe parapīḍane||11||

12duryodhanaśca kiṃ kṛtyaṃ prāptakālamamanyata
|
ke cainaṃ samare vīraṃ pratyudyayurarindamāḥ||12||

13droṇaṃ ca ke vyarakṣanta praviṣṭe śvetavāhane
|
ke'rakṣandakṣiṇaṃ cakraṃ ke ca droṇasya savyataḥ||13||

14ke pṛṣṭhataścāpyabhavanvīrā vīrānvinighnataḥ
|
ke purastādaṅgacchanta nighnantaḥ śātravān raṇe||14||

15yatprāviśanmaheṣvāsaḥ pañcālānaparājitaḥ
|
nṛtyanniva naravyāghro rathamārgeṣu vīryavān||15||

16yo dadāha śarairdroṇaḥ pañcālānāṃ rathavrajān
|
dhūmaketuriva kruddhaḥ kathaṃ mṛtyumupeyivān||16||

17avyagrāneva hi parānkathayasyaparājitān
|
hṛṣṭānudīrṇānsaṃgrāme na tathā sūta māmakān||17||

18hatāṃścaiva vidīrṇāṃśca viprakīrṇāṃśca śaṃsasi
|
rathino virathāṃścaiva kṛtān yuddheṣu māmakān||18||

19sañjaya uvāca|
droṇasya matamājñāya yoddhakāmasya tāṃ niśāṃm
|
duryodhano mahārāja vaśyān bhrātṝnuvāca ha||19||

20karṇaṃ ca vṛṣasenaṃ ca madrarājaṃ ca kaurava
|
durdharṣaṃ dīrghabāhuṃ ca ye ca teṣāṃ padānugāḥ||20||

21droṇaṃ yattāḥ parākrāntāḥ sarve rakṣantu pṛṣṭhataḥ
|
hārdikyo dakṣiṇaṃ cakraṃ śalyaścaivottaraṃ tathā||21||

22trigartānāṃ ca ye śūrā hataśiṣṭā mahārathāḥ
|
tāṃścaiva purataḥ sarvānputraste samacodayat||22||

23ācāryo hi susaṃyatto bhṛśaṃ yattāśca pāṃḍavāḥ
|
taṃ rakṣata susaṃyattā nighnantaṃ śātravān raṇe||23||

24droṇo hi balavān yuddhe kṣiprahastaḥ pratāpavān
|
nirjayettridaśān yuddhe kimu pārthānsasomakān||24||

25te yūyaṃ sahitāḥ sarve bhṛśaṃ yattā mahārathāḥ
|
droṇaṃ rakṣata pāñcālāddhṛṣṭadyumnānmahārathāt||25||

26pāṇḍavīyeṣu sainyeṣu na taṃ paśyāma kañcana
|
yo yodhayedraṇe droṇaṃ dhṛṣṭadyumnādṛte nṛpaḥ||26||

27tasmātsarvātmanā manye bhāradvājasya rakṣaṇam
|
suguptaḥ pāṇḍavān hanyāt sṛñjayāṃśca sasomakān||27||

28sṛñjayeṣvatha sarveṣu nihateṣu camūmukhe
|
dhṛṣṭadyumnaṃ raṇe drauṇirhaniṣyati na saṃśayaḥ||28||

29tathā'rjunaṃ ca rādheyo haniṣyati mahārathaḥ
|
bhīmasenamahaṃ cāpi yuddhe jeṣyābhi dīkṣitaḥ||29||

30śeṣāṃśca pāṇḍavān yodhāḥ prasabhaṃ hīnatejasaḥ
|
so'yaṃ mama jayo vyakto dīrghakālaṃ bhaviṣyati||30||

31tasmādrakṣata saṃgrāme droṇameva mahāratham
|
ityuktvā bharataśreṣṭha putro duryodhanastava||31||

32vyādideśa tathā sainyaṃ tasmiṃstamasi dāruṇe
|
tataḥ pravavṛte yuddhaṃ rātrau bharatasattama||32||

33ubhayoḥ senayorghoraṃ parasparajigīṣayā
|
arjunaḥ kauravaṃ sainyamarjunaṃ cāpi kauravāḥ||33||

34nānāśastrasamāvāyairanyonyaṃ samapīḍayan
|
drauṇiḥ pāñcālarājaṃ ca bhāradvājaśca sṛñjayān||34||

35chādayāṃcakrire saṃkhye śaraiḥ sannataparvabhiḥ
|
pāṇḍupāñcālasainyānāṃ kauravāṇāṃ ca bhārata||35||

36āsīnniṣṭānako ghoro nighnatāmitaretaram
|
naivāsmābhistathā pūrvairdṛṣṭapūrvaṃ tathāvidham||36||

37śrutaṃ vā yādṛśaṃ yuddhamāsīdraudraṃ bhayānakam||37||
