Mahābhārata 5.21 (Udyogaparvan)

1vaiśampāyana uvāca|
tasya tadvacanaṃ śrutvā prajñāvṛddho mahādyutiḥ
|
saṃpūjyainaṃ yathākālaṃ bhīṣmo vacanamabravīt ||1||

2diṣṭyā kuśalinaḥ sarve saha dāmodareṇa te
|
diṣṭyā sahāyavantaśca diṣṭyā dharme ca te ratāḥ ||2||

3diṣṭyā ca sandhikāmāste bhrātaraḥ kurunandanāḥ
|
diṣṭyā na yuddhamanasaḥ pāṇḍavāḥ saha bāndhavaiḥ ||3||

4bhavatā satyamuktaṃ tu sarvametanna saṃśayaḥ
|
atitīkṣṇaṃ tu te vākyaṃ brāhmaṇyāditi me matiḥ ||4||

5asaṃśayaṃ kleśitāste vane ceha ca pāṇḍavāḥ
|
prāptāśca dharmataḥ sarvaṃ piturdhanamasaṃśayam ||5||

6kirīṭi balavān pārthaḥ kṛtāstraśca mahārathaḥ
|
ko hi pāṇḍusutaṃ yuddhe viṣaheta dhanañjayam ||6||

7api vajradharaḥ sākṣāt kimutānye dhanurbhṛtaḥ
|
trayāṇāmapi lokānāṃ samartha iti me matiḥ ||7||

8bhīṣme bruvati tadvākyaṃ dhṛṣṭamākṣipya manyunā
|
duryodhanaṃ samālokya karṇo vacanamabravīt ||8||

9na tatrāviditaṃ brahman loke bhūtena kenacit
|
punaruktena kiṃ tena bhāṣitena punaḥ punaḥ ||9||

10duryodhanārthe śakunirdyūte nirjitavān purā
|
samayena gato'raṇyaṃ pāṇḍuputro yudhiṣṭhiraḥ ||10||

11sa taṃ samayamāśritya rājyaṃ necchati paitṛkam
|
balamāśritya matsyānāṃ pāñcālānāṃ ca mūrkhavat ||11||

12duryodhano bhayādvidvanna dadyāt pādamantataḥ
|
dharmatastu mahīṃ kṛtsnāṃ pradadyācchatrave'pi ca ||12||

13yadi kāṅkṣanti te rājyaṃ pitṛpaitāmahaṃ punaḥ
|
yathāpratijñaṃ kālaṃ taṃ carantu vanamāśritāḥ ||13||

14tato duryodhanasyāṅke vartantāmakutobhayāḥ
|
adhārmikīṃ tu mā buddhiṃ maurkhyātkurvantu kevalāt ||14||

15atha te dharmamutsṛjya yuddhamicchanti pāṇḍavāḥ
|
āsādyemān kuruśreṣṭhān smariṣyanti vaco mama ||15||

16bhīṣma uvāca|
kiṃ nu rādheya vācā te karma tat smartumarhasi
|
eka eva yadā pārthaḥ ṣaḍrathāñjitavān yudhi ||16||

17bahuśo jīyamānasya karma dṛṣṭaṃ tadaiva te
|
na cedevaṃ kariṣyāmo yadayaṃ brāhmaṇo'bravīt
|
dhruvaṃ yudhi hatāstena bhakṣayiṣyāma pāṃsukān||17||

18vaiśampāyana uvāca|
dhṛtarāṣṭrastato bhīṣmamanumānya prasādya ca
|
avabhartsya ca rādheyamidaṃ vacanamabravīt ||18||

19asmaddhitaṃ vākyamidaṃ bhīṣmaḥ śāntanavo'bravīt
|
pāṇḍavānāṃ hitaṃ caiva sarvasya jagatastathā ||19||

20cintayitvā tu pārthebhyaḥ preṣayiṣyāmi sañjayam
|
sa bhavān prati yātvadya pāṇḍavāneva mā ciram ||20||

21sa taṃ satkṛtya kauravyaḥ preṣayāmāsa pāṇḍavān
|
sabhāmadhye samāhūya sañjayaṃ vākyamabravīt ||21||
