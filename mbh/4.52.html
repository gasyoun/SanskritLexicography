Mahābhārata 4.52 (Virāṭaparvan)

1bhīṣma uvāca|kalāḥ kāṣṭhāśca yujyante muhūrtāśca dināni ca
|
ardhamāsāśca māsāśca nakṣatrāṇi grahāstathā ||1||

2ṛtavaścāpi yujyante tathā saṃvatsarā api
|
evaṃ kālavibhāgena kālacakraṃ pravartate ||2||

3teṣāṃ kālātirekeṇa jyotiṣāṃ ca vyatikramāt
|
pañcame pañcame varṣe dvau māsāvupajāyataḥ ||3||

4eṣāmabhyadhikā māsāḥ pañca ca dvādaśa kṣapāḥ
|
trayodaśānāṃ varṣāṇāmiti me vartate matiḥ ||4||

5sarvaṃ yathāvaccaritaṃ yadyadebhiḥ pratiśrutam
|
evametad‌dhruvaṃ jñātvā tato bībhatsurāgataḥ ||5||

6sarve caiva mahātmānaḥ sarve dharmārthakovidāḥ
|
yeṣāṃ yudhiṣṭhiro rājā kasmāddharme'parādhnuyuḥ ||6||

7alubdhāścaiva kaunteyāḥ kṛtavantaśca duṣkaram
|
na cāpi kevalaṃ rājyamiccheyuste'nupāyataḥ ||7||

8tadaiva te hi vikrāntumīṣuḥ kauravanandanāḥ
|
dharmapāśanibaddhāstu na celuḥ kṣatriyavratāt ||8||

9yaccānṛta iti khyāyādyaḥ sa gacchet parābhavam
|
vṛṇuyurmaraṇaṃ pārthā nānṛtatvaṃ kathaṃcana ||9||

10prāptakāle tu prāptavyaṃ notsṛjeyurnararṣabhāḥ
|
api vajrabhṛtā guptaṃ tathāvīryā hi pāṇḍavāḥ ||10||

11pratiyudhyema samare sarvaśastrabhṛtāṃ varam
|
tasmādyadatra kalyāṇaṃ loke sadbhiranuṣṭhitam || tat saṃvidhīyatāṃ śīghraṃ mā vo hyartho'bhyagāt param ||11||

12na hi paśyāmi saṅgrāme kadācidapi kaurava
|
ekāntasiddhiṃ rājendra saṃprāptaśca dhanaṃjayaḥ ||12||

13saṃpravṛtte tu saṅgrāme bhāvābhāvau jayājayau
|
avaśyamekaṃ spṛśato dṛṣṭametadasaṃśayam ||13||

14tasmādyuddhocitaṃ karma karma vā dharmasaṃhitam
|
kriyatāmāśu rājendra saṃprāptaśca dhanañjayaḥ ||14||

15duryodhana uvāca|nāhaṃ rājyaṃ pradāsyāmi pāṇḍavānāṃ pitāmaha
|
yuddhopacārikaṃ yattu tacchīghraṃ pravidhīyatām ||15||

16bhīṣma uvāca|atra yā māmikā buddhiḥ śrūyatāṃ yadi rocate
|
sarvathā hi mayā śreyo vaktavyaṃ kurunandana ||16||

17kṣipraṃ balacaturbhāgaṃ gṛhya gaccha puraṃ prati
|
tato'paraścaturbhāgo gāḥ samādāya gacchatu ||17||

18vayaṃ cārdhena sainyasya pratiyotsyāma pāṇḍavam
|
ahaṃ droṇaśca karṇaśca aśvatthāmā kṛpastathā
|
pratiyotsyāma bībhatsumāgataṃ kṛtaniścayam ||18||

19matsyaṃ vā punarāyātamāgataṃ vā śatakratum
|
ahamāvārayiṣyāmi veleva makarālayam ||19||

20vaiśampāyana uvāca|tadvākyaṃ ruruce teṣāṃ bhīṣmaṇoktaṃ mahātmanā
|
tathā hi kṛtavān rājā kauravāṇāmanantaram ||20||

21bhīṣmaḥ prasthāpya rājānaṃ godhanaṃ tadanantaram
|
senāmukhyān vyavasthāpya vyūhituṃ saṃpracakrame ||21||

22bhīṣma uvāca|ācārya madhye tiṣṭha tvamaśvatthāmā tu savyataḥ
|
kṛpaḥ śāradvato dhīmān pārśvaṃ rakṣatu dakṣiṇam ||22||

23agrataḥ sūtaputrastu karṇastiṣṭhatu daṃśitaḥ
|
ahaṃ sarvasya sainyasya paścāt sthāsyāmi pālayan ||23||
