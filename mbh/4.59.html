Mahābhārata 4.59 (Virāṭaparvan)

1vaiśampāyana uvāca|tato drauṇirmahārāja prayayāvarjunaṃ raṇe
|
taṃ pārthaḥ pratijagrāha vāyuvegamivoddhatam
|
śarajālena mahatā varṣamāṇamivāmbudam ||1||

2tayordevāsurasamaḥ saṃnipāto mahānabhūt
|
kiratoḥ śarajālāni vṛtravāsavayoriva ||2||

3na sma sūryastadā bhāti na ca vāti samīraṇaḥ
|
śarajālāvṛte vyomni cchāyābhūte samantataḥ ||3||

4mahāṃścaṭacaṭāśabdo yodhayorhanyamānayoḥ
|
dahyatāmiva veṇūnāmāsīt parapurañjaya ||4||

5hayānasyārjunaḥ sarvān kṛtavānalpajīvitān
|
te rājanna prajānanta diśaṃ kāñcana mohitāḥ ||5||

6tato drauṇirmahārvāryaḥ pārthasya vicariṣyataḥ
|
vivaraṃ sūkṣmamālokya jyāṃ ciccheda kṣureṇa ha
|
tadasyāpūjayan devāḥ karma dṛṣṭvā'timānuṣam ||6||

7droṇo bhīṣmaśca karṇaśca kṛpaścaiva mahārathāḥ
|
sādhu sādhviti bhāṣanto'pūjayan karma tasya tat ||7||

8tato drauṇirdhanuḥ śreṣṭhamapakṛṣya ratharṣabham
|
punarevāhanat pārthaṃ hṛdaye kaṅkapatribhiḥ ||8||

9tataḥ pārtho mahābāhuḥ prahasya svanavat tadā
|
yojayāmāsa navayā maurvyā gāṇḍīvamojasā ||9||

10tato'rdhacandramāvṛtya tena pārthaḥ samāgamat
|
vāraṇeneva mattena matto vāraṇayūthapaḥ ||10||

11tataḥ pravavṛte yuddhaṃ pṛthivyāmekavīrayoḥ
|
raṇamadhye dvayorevaṃ sumahallomaharṣaṇam ||11||

12tau vīrau dadṛśuḥ sarve kuravo vismayānvitāḥ
|
yudhyamānau mahāvīryau yūthapāviva saṃgatau ||12||

13tau samājaghnaturvīrāvanyonyaṃ puruṣarṣabhau
|
śarairāśīviṣākārairjvaladbhiriva pannagaiḥ ||13||

14akṣayyāviṣudhī divyau pāṇḍavasya mahātmanaḥ
|
tena pārtho raṇe śūrastasthau giririvācalaḥ ||14||

15aśvatthāmnaḥ punarbāṇāḥ kṣipramabhyasyato raṇe
|
jagmuḥ parikṣayaṃ tūrṇamabhūt tenādhiko'rjunaḥ ||15||

16tataḥ karṇo mahācāpaṃ vikṛṣyābhyadhikaṃ tathā
|
avākṣipat tataḥ śabdo hāhākāro mahānabhūt ||16||

17tataścakṣurdadhe pārtho yatra visphāryate dhanuḥ
|
dadarśa tatra rādheyaṃ tasya kopo vyavardhata ||17||

18sa roṣavaśamāpannaḥ karṇameva jighāṃsayā
|
tamaikṣata vivṛttābhyāṃ netrābhyāṃ kurupuṅgavaḥ ||18||

19tathā tu vimukhe pārthe droṇaputrasya sāyakāt
|
tvaritāḥ puruṣā rājannupājahruḥ sahasraśaḥ ||19||

20utsṛjya ca mahāvāhurdroṇaputraṃ dhanañjayaḥ
|
abhidudrāva sahasā karṇameva sapatnajit ||20||

21tamabhidrutya kaunteyaḥ krodhasaṃraktalocanaḥ
|
kāmayan dvairathaṃ tena yuddhaṃ vacanamabravīt ||21||
