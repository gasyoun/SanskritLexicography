Mahābhārata 13.150 (Anuśāsanaparvan)

1yudhiṣṭhira uvāca|
pitāmaha mahāprājña sarvaśāstraviśārada
|
kiṃ japyaṃ japato nityaṃ bhaveddharmaphalaṃ mahat||1||

2prasthāne vā praveśe vā pravṛtte vāpi karmaṇi
|
daive vā śrāddhakāle vā kiṃ japyaṃ karmasādhanam ||2||

3śāntikaṃ pauṣṭikaṃ rakṣā śatrughnaṃ bhayanāśanam
|
japyaṃ yadbrahma samitaṃ tadbhavān vaktumarhati ||3||

4bhīṣma uvāca|
vyāsaproktamimaṃ mantraṃ śṛṇuṣvaikamanā nṛpa
|
sāvitryā vihitaṃ divyaṃ sadyaḥ pāpavimocanam ||4||

5śṛṇu mantravidhiṃ kṛtsnaṃ procyamānaṃ mayā'nagha
|
yaṃ śrutvā pāṇḍavaśreṣṭha sarvapāpaiḥ pramucyate ||5||

6rātrāvahani dharmajña japan pāpairna lipyate
|
tatte'haṃ saṃpravakṣyāmi śṛṇuṣvaikamanā nṛpa ||6||

7āyuṣmān bhavate caiva yaṃ śrutvā pārthivātmaja
|
puruṣastu susiddhārthaḥ pretya ceha ca modate ||7||

8sevitaṃ satataṃ rājan purā rājarṣisattamaiḥ
|
kṣatradharmaparairnityaṃ satyavrataparāyaṇaiḥ ||8||

9ida‌māhnikamavyagryaṃ kurvadbhirniyataiḥ sadā
|
nṛpairbharataśārdūla prāpyate śrīranuttamā ||9||

10namo vasiṣṭhāya mahāvratāya parāśaraṃ vedanidhiṃ namasye
|
namo'stvanantāya mahoragāya namo'stu siddhebhya ihākṣayebhyaḥ ||10||

11namostvṛṣibhyaḥ paramaṃ pareṣāṃ deveṣu devaṃ varadaṃ varāṇām
|
sahasraśīrṣāya namaḥ śivāya sahastranāmāya janārdanāya ||11||

12ajaikapāda‌hirbudhnyaḥ pinākī cāparājitaḥ
|
ṛtaśca pitṛrūpaśca tryambakaśca maheśvaraḥ ||12||

13vṛṣākapiśca śambhuśca havano'theśvarastathā
|
ekādaśaite prathitā rudrāstribhuvaneśvarāḥ ||13||

14śatametatsamāmnātaṃ śatarudre mahātmanām
|
aṃśo bhagaśca mitraśca varuṇaśca jaleśvaraḥ ||14||

15tathā dhātāryamā caiva jayanto bhāskarastathā
|
tvaṣṭā pūṣā tathaivendro dvādaśo viṣṇurucyate ||15||

16ityete dvādaśādityāḥ kāśyapeyā iti śrutiḥ
|
dharo dhruvaśca somaśca sāvitrothānilo'nalaḥ ||16||

17pratyūṣaśca prabhāsaśca vasavo'ṣṭau prakīrtitāḥ
|
nāsatyaścāpi dattaśca smṛtau dvāvaśvināvapi ||17||

18mārtaṇḍasyātmajāvetau saṃjñānāsāvinirgatau
|
ataḥ paraṃ pravakṣyāmi lokānāṃ karmasākṣiṇaḥ ||18||

19api yajñasya vettāro dattasya sukṛtasya ca
|
adṛśyāḥ sarvabhūteṣu paśyanti tridaśeśvarāḥ ||19||

20śubhāśubhāni karmāṇi mṛtyuḥ kālaśca sarvaśaḥ
|
viśvedevāḥ pitṛgaṇā mūrtimantastapodhanāḥ ||20||

21munayaścaiva siddhāśca tapomokṣaparāyaṇāḥ
|
śucismitāḥ kīrtayatāṃ prayacchanti śubhaṃ nṛṇām ||21||

22prajāpatikṛtānetān lokān divyena tejasā
|
vasanti sarvalokeṣu prayatāḥ sarvakarmasu ||22||

23prāṇānāmīśvarānetān kīrtayanprayato naraḥ
|
dharmārthakāmairvipulairyujyate saha nityaśaḥ ||23||

24lokāṃśca labhate puṇyānviśveśvarakkṛtāñchubhān
|
ete devāstrayastriṃśatsarvabhūtagaṇeśvarāḥ ||24||

25nandīśvaro mahākāyo grāmaṇīrvṛṣabhadhvajaḥ
|
īśvarāḥ sarvalokānāṃ gaṇeśvaravināyakāḥ ||25||

26saumyā raudrā gaṇāścaiva yogabhūtagaṇāstathā
|
jyotīṃṣi sarito vyoma suparṇaḥ patageśvaraḥ ||26||

27pṛthivyāṃ tapasā siddhāḥ sthāvarāśca carāśca ha
|
himavān girayaḥ sarve catvāraśca mahārṇavāḥ ||27||

28bhavasyānucarāścaiva haratulyaparākramāḥ
|
viṣṇurdevotha jiṣṇuśca skandaścāmbikayā saha ||28||

29kīrtayanprayataḥ sarvānsarvapāpaiḥ pramucyate
|
ata ūrdhvaṃ pravakṣyāmi mānavānṛṣisattamān ||29||

30yavakrītaśca raibhyaśca arvāvasuparāvasū
|
auśijaścaiva kakṣīvān balaścāṅgirasaḥ sutaḥ ||30||

31ṛṣirmedhātitheḥ putraḥ kaṇvo barhiṣadastathā
|
brahmatejomayāḥ sarve kīrtitā lokabhāvanāḥ ||31||

32labhante hi śubhaṃ sarve rudrānalavasuprabhāḥ
|
bhuvi kṛtvā śubhaṃ karma modante divi daivataiḥ ||32||

33mahendraguravaḥ sapta prācīṃ vai diśamāśritāḥ
|
prayataḥ kīrtayedetān śakraloke mahīyate ||33||

34unmucuḥ pramucuścaiva svastyātreyaśca vīryavān
|
dṛḍhavyaścordhvabāhuśca tṛṇasomāṅgirāstathā ||34||

35mitrāvaruṇayoḥ putrastathāgastyaḥ pratāpavān
|
dharmarājartvijaḥ sapta dakṣiṇāṃ diśamāśritāḥ ||35||

36dṛḍheyuśca ṛteyuśca parivyādhaśca kīrtimān
|
ekataśca dvitaścaiva tritaścādityasannibhāḥ ||36||

37atreḥ putraśca dharmātmā ṛṣiḥ sārasvatastathā
|
varuṇasyartvijaḥ sapta paścimāṃ diśamāśritāḥ ||37||

38atrirvasiṣṭho bhagavān kaśyapaśca mahānṛṣiḥ
|
gautamaśca bharadvājo viśvāmitro'tha kauśikaḥ ||38||

39ṛcīkatanayaścogro jamadagniḥ pratāpavān
|
dhaneśvarasya guravaḥ saptaite uttarāśritāḥ ||39||

40apare munayaḥ sapta dikṣu sarvāsvadhiṣṭhitāḥ
|
kīrtisvastikarā nṝṇāṃ kīrtitā lokabhāvanāḥ ||40||

41dharmaḥ kāmaśca kālaśca vasurvāsukireva ca
|
anantaḥ kapilaścaiva saptaite dharaṇīdharāḥ ||41||

42rāmovyāsastathā drauṇiraśvatthāmā ca lomaśaḥ
|
ityete munayo divyā ekaikaḥ sapta saptadhā ||42||

43śāntisvastikarā loke diśāṃpālāḥ prakīrtitāḥ
|
yasyāṃ yasyāṃ diśi hyete tanmukhaḥ śaraṇaṃ vrajet ||43||

44sraṣṭāraḥ sarvabhūtānāṃ kīrtitā lokapāvanāḥ
|
saṃvarto merusāvarṇo mārkaṇḍeyaśca dhārmikaḥ ||44||

45sāṃkhyayogau nāradaśca durvāsāśca mahānṛṣiḥ
|
atyantatapaso dāntāstriṣu lokeṣu viśrutāḥ ||45||

46apare rudrasaṅkāśāḥ kīrtitā brahmalaukikāḥ
|
aputro labhate putraṃ daridro labhate dhanam ||46||

47tathā dharmārthakāmeṣu siddhiṃ ca labhate naraḥ
|
pṛthaṃ vainyaṃ nṛpavaraṃ pṛthvī yasyābhavatsutā ||47||

48prajāpatiṃ sārvabhaumaṃ kīrtayedvasudhādhipam
|
ādityavaṃśaprabhavaṃ mahendrasamavikramam ||48||

49purūravasamailaṃ ca triṣu lokeṣu viśrutam
|
budhasya dayitaṃ putraṃ kīrtayedvasudhādhipam ||49||

50trilokaviśrutaṃ vīraṃ bharataṃ ca prakīrtayet
|
gavāmayena yajñena yeneṣṭaṃ vai kṛte yuge ||50||

51rantidevaṃ mahādevaṃ kīrtayetparamadyutim
|
viśvajittapasopetaṃ lakṣaṇyaṃ lokapūjitam ||51||

52tathā śvetaṃ ca rājarṣiṃ kīrtayetparamadyutim
|
sagarasyātmajā yena plāvitāstāritāstathā ||52||

53hutāśanasamānetān mahārūpān mahaujasaḥ
|
ugrakāyānmahāsattvānkīrtayetkīrtivardhanān ||53||

54devānṛṣigaṇāṃścaiva nṛpāṃśca jagatīśvarān
|
sāṃkhyaṃ yogaṃ ca paramaṃ havyaṃ kavyaṃ tathaiva ca ||54||

55kīrtitaṃ paramaṃ brahma sarvaśrutiparāyaṇam
|
maṅgalyaṃ sarvabhūtānāṃ pavitraṃ bahukīrtitam ||55||

56vyādhipraśamanaṃ śreṣṭhaṃ pauṣṭikaṃ sarvakarmaṇām
|
prayataḥ kīrtayeccaitān kalyaṃ sāyaṃ ca bhārata ||56||

57ete vai yānti varṣanti bhānti vānti sṛjanti ca
|
ete vināyakāḥ śreṣṭhā dakṣāḥ kṣāntā jitendriyāḥ ||57||

58narāṇāmaśubhaṃ sarve vyapohanti prakīrtitāḥ
|
sākṣibhūtā mahātmānaḥ pāpasya sukṛtasya ca ||58||

59etānvai kalyamutthāya kīrtayan śubhamaśnute
|
nāgnicaurabhayaṃ tasya na mārgapratirodhanam ||59||

60etān kīrtayatāṃ nityaṃ duḥsvapno naśyate nṛṇām
|
mucyate sarvapāpebhyaḥ svastimāṃśca gṛhān ‌vrajet ||60||

61dīkṣākāleṣu sarveṣu yaḥ paṭhenniyato dvijaḥ
|
nyāyavānātmanirataḥ kṣāṃto dāṃto'nasūyakaḥ ||61||

62rogārto vyādhiyukto vā paṭhan pāpātpramucyate
|
vāstumadhye tu paṭhataḥ kule svastyayanaṃ bhavet ||62||

63kṣetramadhye tu paṭhataḥ sarvaṃ sasyaṃ prarohati
|
gacchataḥ kṣemamadhvānaṃ grāmāntaragataḥ paṭhan ||63||

64ātmanaśca sutānāṃ ca dārāṇāṃ ca dhanasya ca
|
bījānāmoṣadhīnāṃ ca rakṣāmetāṃ prayojayet ||64||

65etān saṃgrāmakāle tu paṭhataḥ kṣatriyasya tu
|
vrajanti ripavo nāśaṃ kṣemaṃ ca parivartate ||65||

66etāndaive ca pitrye ca paṭhataḥ puruṣasya hi
|
bhuñjate pitaraḥ kavyaṃ havyaṃ ca tridivaukasaḥ ||66||

67na vyādhiśvāpadabhayaṃ na dvipānna hi taskarāt
|
kaśmalaṃ laghutāṃ yāti pāpmanā ca pramucyate ||67||

68yānapātre ca yāne ca pravāse rājaveśmani
|
parāṃ siddhimavāpnoti sāvitrīṃ hyuttamāṃ paṭhan ||68||

69na ca rājabhayaṃ teṣāṃ na piśācānna rākṣasāt
|
nāgnyambupavanavyālādbhayaṃ tasyopajāyate ||69||

70caturṇāmapi varṇānāmāśramasya viśeṣataḥ
|
karoti satataṃ śāntiṃ sāvitrīmuttamāṃ paṭhan ||70||

71nāgnirdahati kāṣṭhāni sāvitrī yatra paṭhyate
|
na tatra bālo mriyate na ca tiṣṭhanti pannagāḥ ||71||

72na teṣāṃ vidyate duḥkhaṃ gacchanti paramāṃ gatim
|
ye śṛṇvanti mahadbrahma sāvitrīguṇakīrtanam ||72||

73gavāṃ madhye tu paṭhato gāvo'sya bahuvatsalāḥ
|
prasthāne vā pravāse vā sarvāvasthāṃ gataḥ paṭhet ||73||

74japatāṃ juhvatāṃ caiva nityaṃ ca prayatātmanām
|
ṛṣīṇāṃ paramaṃ japyaṃ guhyametannarādhipa ||74||

75yāthātathyena siddhasya itihāsaṃ purātanam
|
parāśaramataṃ divyaṃ śakrāya kathitaṃ purā ||75||

76tadetatte samākhyātaṃ tathyaṃ brahma sanātanam
|
hṛdayaṃ sarvabhūtānāṃ śrutireṣā sanātanī ||76||

77somādityānvayāḥ sarve rāghavāḥ kuravastathā
|
paṭhanti śucayo nityaṃ sāvitrīṃ prāṇināṃ gatim ||77||

78abhyāse nityaṃ devānāṃ saptarṣīṇāṃ dhruvasya ca
|
mokṣaṇaṃ sarvakṛcchrāṇāṃ mocayatyaśubhātsadā ||78||

79vṛddhaiḥ kāśyapagautamaprabhṛtibhibhṛgvaṃnirotryādibhiḥ
|
śukrāgastyabṛhaspatiprabhṛtibhirbrahmarṣibhiḥ sevitam|| bhāradvājamataṃ ṛcīkatanayaiḥ prāptaṃ vasiṣṭhāt punaḥ
|
sāvitrīmadhigamya śakravasubhiḥ kṛtsnā jitā dānavāḥ||79||

80yo gośataṃ kanakaśṭaṅgamayaṃ dadāti viprāya veda‌viduṣe ca bahuśrutāya
|
divyāṃ ca bhāratakathāṃ kathayecca nityaṃ tulyaṃ phalaṃ bhavati tasya ca tasya caiva ||80||

81dharmo vivardhati bhṛgoḥ parikīrtanena vīryaṃ vivardhati vasiṣṭhanamonatena
|
saṃgrāmajidbhavati caiva raghuṃ namasyan syāda‌śvinau ca parikīrtayato na rogaḥ ||81||

82eṣā te kathitā rājan sāvitrī brahma śāśvatī
|
vivakṣurasi yaccānyattatte vakṣyāmi bhārata ||82||
