Mahābhārata 12.264 (Śāntiparvan)

1tulādhāra uvāca |
sadbhirvā yadi vā'sadbhiḥ panthānamimatāsthitam
|
pratyakṣaṃ kriyatāṃ sādhu tato jñāsyasi tadyathā ||1||

2ete śakuntā bahavaḥ samantādvicaranti ha
|
tavottamāṅge sambhūtāḥ śyenāścānyāśca jāyataḥ ||2||

3āhūyainānmahābrahmanviśamānāṃstatastataḥ
|
paśyemānhastapādaiśca śliṣṭāndeheṣu sarvaśaḥ ||3||

4sambhāvayanti pitaraṃ tvayā sambhāvitāḥ khagāḥ
|
asaṃśayaṃ pitā vai tvaṃ putrānāhūya jājale ||4||

5bhīṣma uvāca|
tato jājalinā tena samāhūtāḥ patatriṇaḥ
|
vācamuccārayanti sma dharmasya vacanātkila ||5||

6ahiṃsādikṛtaṃ karma iha caiva paratra ca
|
śraddhāṃ nihanti vai brahmansā hatā hanti taṃ naram ||6||

7samānāṃ śraddadhānānāṃ saṃyatānāṃ sucetasām
|
kurvatāṃ yajña ityeva na yajño jātu neṣyate ||7||

8śraddhā vaivasvatī seyaṃ sūryasya duhitā dvija
|
sāvitrī prasavitrī ca bahirvāṅmanasī tataḥ ||8||

9vāgvṛddhaṃ trāyate śraddhā manovṛddhaṃ ca bhārata
|
śraddhāvṛddhaṃ vāṅmanasī na karma trātumarhati ||9||

10atra gāthā brahmagītāḥ kīrtayanti purāvidaḥ
|
śuceraśraddadhānasya śraddadhānasya cāśuceḥ ||10||

11devā vittamamanyanta sadṛśaṃ yajñakarmaṇi
|
śrotriyasya kadaryasya vadānyasya ca vādhuṣeḥ ||11||

12mīmāṃsitvobhayaṃ devāḥ samamannamakalpayan
|
prajāpatistānuvāca viṣamaṃ kṛtamityuta ||12||

13śraddhāpūtaṃ vadānyasya hatamaśraddhayetarat
|
bhojyamannaṃ vadānyasya kadaryasya na vādhuṣeḥ ||13||

14aśraddadhāna evaiko devānāṃ nārhate haviḥ
|
tasyaivānnaṃ na bhoktavyamiti dharmavido viduḥ ||14||

15aśraddhā paramaṃ pāpaṃ śraddhā pāpapramocinī
|
jahāti pāpaṃ śraddhāvān sarpo jīrṇāmiva tvacam ||15||

16jyāyasī yā pavitrāṇāṃ nivṛttiḥ śraddhayā saha
|
nivṛttaśīladoṣo yaḥ śraddhāvānpūta eva saḥ ||16||

17kiṃ tasya tapasā kāryaṃ kiṃ vṛttena kimātmanā
|
śraddhāmayo'yaṃ puruṣo yo yacchraddhaḥ sa eva saḥ ||17||

18iti dharmaḥ samākhyātaḥ sadbhirdharmārthadarśibhiḥ
|
vayaṃ jijñāsamānāstu samprāptā dharmadarśanāt ||18||

19śraddhāṃ kuru mahāprājña tataḥ prāpsyasi yatparam
|
śraddhāvān śraddadhānaśca dharmaścaiva hi jājale
|
svavartmani sthitaścaiva garīyāneva jājale ||19||

20bhīṣma uvāca|
tato'cireṇa kālena tulādhāraḥ sa eva ca
|
divaṃ gatvā mahāprājñau viharetāṃ yathāsukham ||20||

21svaṃ svaṃ sthānamupāgamya svakarmaphalanirjitam
|
evaṃ bahuvidhārthaṃ ca tulādhāreṇa bhāṣitam ||21||

22samyakcedamupālabdho dharmaścoktaḥ sanātanaḥ
|
tasya vikhyātavīryasya śrutvā vākyāni sa dvijaḥ ||22||

23tulādhārasya kaunteya śāntimevānvapadyata
|
evaṃ bahumatārthaṃ ca tulādhāreṇa bhāṣitam
|
yathaupamyopadeśena kiṃ bhūyaḥ śrotumicchasi ||23||
