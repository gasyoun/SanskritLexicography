Mahābhārata 14.80 (Āśvamedhikaparvan)

1vaiśampāna uvāca|
tato bahutaraṃ bhīrurvilapya kamalekṣaṇā
|
mumoha duḥkhasantaptā papāta ca mahītale||1||

2pratilabhya ca sā saṃjñāṃ devī divyavapurdharā
|
ulūpīṃ pannagasutāṃ dṛṣṭvedaṃ vākyamabravīt||2||

3ulūpi paśya bhartāraṃ śayānaṃ nihataṃ raṇe
|
tvatkṛte mama putreṇa bāṇena samitiñjayam||3||

4nanu tvamāryadharmajñā nanu cāsi pativratā
|
yattvatkṛte'yaṃ patitaḥ patiste nihato raṇe||4||

5kintu sarvāparādho'yaṃ yadi te'dya dhanañjayaḥ
|
kṣamasva yācyamānā vai jīvayasva dhanañjayam||5||

6nanu tvamārye dharmajñā trailokyaviditā śubhe
|
yadghātayitvā putreṇa bhartāraṃ nānuśocasi||6||

7nāhaṃ śocāmi tanayaṃ hataṃ pannaganandini
|
patimeva tu śocāmi yasyānidhyamidaṃ kṛtam||7||

8ityuktvā sā tadā devīmulūpī pannagātmajām
|
bhartāramabhigamyedamityuvāca yaśasvinī||8||

9uttiṣṭha kurumukhyasya priyamukhya mama priya
|
ayamaśvo mahābāho mayā te parimokṣitaḥ||9||

10nanu tvayā nāma vibho dharmarājasya yajñiyaḥ
|
ayamaśvo'nusartavyaḥ sa śeṣa kiṃ mahītale||10||

11tvayi prāṇā mamāyattā: kurūṇāṃ kurunandana
|
sa kasmātprāṇado'nyeṣāṃ prāṇānsantyaktavānasi||11||

12ulūpi sādhu paśyemaṃ patiṃ nipatitaṃ bhuvi
|
putraṃ cemaṃ samutpādya ghātayitvā na śocasi||12||

13kāmaṃ svapitu bālo'yaṃ bhūmau mṛtyuvaśaṃ gataḥ
|
lohitākṣo guḍākeśo vijayaḥ sādhu jīvatu||13||

14nāparādho'sti subhage narāṇāṃ bahubhāryatā
|
pramadānāṃ bhavatyeṣa mā te'bhūdbuddhirīdṛśī||14||

15sakhyaṃ caitatkṛtaṃ dhātrā śaśvadavyayameva tu
|
sakhyaṃ samabhijānīhi satyaṃ saṅgatamastu te||15||

16putreṇa ghātayitvainaṃ patiṃ yadi na me'dya vai
|
jīvantaṃ darśayasyadya parityakṣyāmi jīvitam||16||

17sāhaṃ duḥkhānvitā devi patiputravinākṛtā
|
ihaiva prāyamāśiṣye prekṣantyāste na saṃśayaḥ||17||

18ityuktvā pannagasutāṃ sapatnī caitravāhanī
|
tataḥ prāyamupāsīnā tūṣṇīmāsījjanādhipa||18||

19vaiśampāyana uvāca|
tato vilapya viratā bhartuḥ pādau pragṛhya sā
|
upaviṣṭābhavaddīnā socchvāsaṃ putramīkṣatī||19||

20tataḥ saṃjñāṃ punarladhvā sa rājā babhruvāhanaḥ
|
mātaraṃ tāmathālokya raṇabhūmāvathābravīt||20||

21ito duḥkhataraṃ kiṃ nu yanme mātā sukhaidhitā
|
bhūmau nipatitaṃ vīramanuśete mṛtaṃ patim||21||

22nihantāraṃ raṇe'rīṇāṃ sarvaśastrabhṛtāṃ varam
|
mayā vinihataṃ saṅkhye prekṣate durmaraṃ bata||22||

23aho'syā hṛdayaṃ devyā dṛḍhaṃ yanna vidīryate
|
vyūḍhoraskaṃ mahāvāhuṃ prekṣantyā nihataṃ patim||23||

24durmaraṃ puruṣeṇeha manye hyadhvanyanāgate
|
yatra nāhaṃ na me mātā viprayujyeta jīvitāt||24||

25hā hā dhikkuruvīrasya sannāhaṃ kāñcanaṃ bhuvi
|
apaviddhaṃ hatasyeha mayā putreṇa paśyata||25||

26bho bho paśyata me vīraṃ pitaraṃ brāhmaṇā bhuvi
|
śayānaṃ vīraśayane mayā putreṇa pātitam||26||

27brāhmaṇāḥ kurumukhyasya ye muktā hayasāriṇaḥ
|
kurvanti śāntiṃ kāmasya raṇe yo'yaṃ mayā hataḥ||27||

28vyādiśantu ca kiṃ viprāḥ prāyaścittamihādya me
|
anṛśaṃsasya pāpasya pitṛhantū raṇājire||28||

29duścarā dvādaśasamā hatvā pitaramadya vai
|
mameha sunṛśaṃsasya saṃvītasyāsya carmaṇā||29||

30śiraḥkapāle cāsyaiva yuñjataḥ pituradya me
|
prāyaścitaṃ hi nāstyanyaddhatvā'dya pitaraṃ mama||30||

31paśya nāgottamasute bhartāraṃ nihataṃ mayā
|
kṛtaṃ priyaṃ mayā ne'dya nihatya samare'rjunam||31||

32so'hamadya gamiṣyāmi gatiṃ pitṛniṣevitām
|
na śaknopyātmanā''tmānamahaṃ dhārayituṃ śubhe||32||

33sā tvaṃ mayi mṛte mātastathā gāṇḍīvadhanvani
|
bhava prītimatī devi satyenātmānamālabhe||33||

34ityuktvā sa tato rājā duḥkhaśokasamāhataḥ
|
upaspṛśya mahārāja duḥkhādvacanamabravīt||34||

35śṛṇvantu sarvabhūtāni sthāvarāṇi carāṇi ca
|
tvaṃ ca mātaryatha satyaṃ bravīmi bhujagottame||35||

36yadi nottiṣṭhati jayaḥ pitā me narasattamaḥ
|
asminneva raṇoddeśe śoṣayiṣye kalevaram||36||

37na hi me pitaraṃ hatvā niṣkṛtirvidyate kvacit
|
narakaṃ pratipatsyāmi dhruvaṃ guruvadhārditaḥ||37||

38vīraṃ hi kṣatriyaṃ hatvā gośatena pramucyate
|
pitaraṃ tu nihatyaivaṃ durlabhā niṣkṛtirmama||38||

39eṣa eko mahātejāḥ pāṇḍuputro dhanañjayaḥ
|
pitā ca mama dharmātmā tasya me niṣkṛtiḥ kutaḥ||39||

40ityevamuktvā nṛpate dhanañjayasuto nṛpaḥ
|
upaspṛśyābhavattūṣṇīṃ prāyopeto mahāmatiḥ||40||

41vaiśampāyana uvāca|
prāyopaviṣṭe nṛpatau maṇipūreśvare tadā
|
pitṛśokasamāviṣṭe saha mātrā parantapa||41||

42ulūpī cintayāmāsa tadā sañjīvanaṃ maṇim
|
sa copātiṣṭhata tadā pannagānāṃ parāyaṇam||42||

43taṃ gṛhītvā tu kauravya nāgarājapateḥ sutā
|
manaḥprahlādanīṃ vācaṃ sainikānāmathābravīt||43||

44uttiṣṭha mā śucaḥ putra naiva jiṣṇustvayā jitaḥ
|
ajeyaḥ puruṣaireṣa tathā devaiḥ savāsavaiḥ||44||

45mayā tu mohanī nāma māyaiṣā sampradarśitā
|
priyārthaṃ puruṣendrasya pituste'dya yaśasvinaḥ||45||

46jijñāsuhyeṣa putrasya balasya tava kauravaḥ
|
saṅgrāme yuddhyato rājannāgataḥ paravīrahā||46||

47tasmādasi mayā putra yuddhāya paricoditaḥ
|
mā pāpamātmanaḥ putra śaṅkethā hyaṇvapi prabho||47||

48ṛṣireṣa mahānātmā purāṇaḥ śāśvato'kṣaraḥ
|
nainaṃ śakto hi saṅgrāme jetuṃ śakto'pi putraka||48||

49ayaṃ tu me maṇirdivyaḥ samānīto viśāmpate
|
matānmṛtānpannagendrānyo jīvayati nityadā||49||

50enamasyorasi tvaṃ ca sthāpayasva pituḥ prabho
|
sañjīvitaṃ tadā pārthaṃ satvaṃ draṣṭāsi pāṇḍavam||50||

51ityuktaḥ sthāpayāmāsa tasyorasi maṇiṃ tadā
|
pārthasyāmitatejāḥ sa pituḥ snehādapāpakṛt||51||

52tasminnyaste maṇau vīro jiṣṇurujjīvitaḥ prabhuḥ
|
cirasupta ivottasthau mṛṣṭalohitalocanaḥ||52||

53tamutthitaṃ mahātmānaṃ labdhasaṃjñaṃ manasvinam
|
samīkṣya pitaraṃ svasthaṃ vavande babhruvāhanaḥ||53||

54usthite puruṣavyāghra punarlakṣmīvati prabho
|
divyāḥ sumanasaḥ puṇyā vavṛṣe pākaśāsanaḥ||54||

55anāhatā dundubhayo vinedurmeghaniḥsvanāḥ
|
sādhu sādhviti cākāśe babhūva sumahānsvanaḥ||55||

56utthāya ca mahābāhuḥ paryāśvasto dhanañjayaḥ
|
babhruvāhanamāliṅgya samājighrata mūrdhani||56||

57dadarśa cāpi dūre'sya mātaraṃ śokakarśitām
|
ulūpyā saha tiṣṭhantīṃ tato'pṛcchaddhanañjayaḥ||57||

58kimidaṃ lakṣyate sarve śokavismayaharṣavat
|
raṇājiramamitraghna yadi jānāsi śaṃsa me||58||

59jananī ca kimarthaṃ te raṇabhūmimupāgatā
|
nāgendraduhitā ceyamulūpī kimihāgatā||59||

60jānāmyahamidaṃ yuddhaṃ tvayā madvacanātkṛtam
|
strīṇāmāgamane hetumahamicchāmi veditum||60||

61tamuvāca tathā pṛṣṭo maṇipūrapatistadā
|
prasādya śirasā vidvānulūpī pṛcchyatāmiyam||61||
