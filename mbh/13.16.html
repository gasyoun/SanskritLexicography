Mahābhārata 13.16 (Anuśāsanaparvan)

1upamanyuruvāca|
ṛṣirāsītkṛte tāta taṇḍirityeva viśrutaḥ
|
daśavarṣasahasrāṇi tena devaḥ samādhinā ||1||

2ārādhito'bhūdbhaktena tasyodarkaṃ niśāmaya
|
sa dṛṣṭavānmahādevamastauṣīcca stavairvibhum ||2||

3iti taṇḍistapoyogātparamātmānamavyayam
|
cintayitvā mahātmānamidamāha suvismitaḥ ||3||

4yaṃ paṭhanti sadā sāṃkhyāścintayanti ca yoginaḥ
|
paraṃ pradhānaṃ puruṣamadhiṣṭhātāramīśvaram ||4||

5utpattau ca vināśe ca kāraṇaṃ yaṃ vidurbudhāḥ
|
devāsuramunīnāṃ ca paraṃ yasmānna vidyate ||5||

6ajaṃ tamahamīśānamanādinidhanaṃ prabhum
|
atyantasukhinaṃ devamanaghaṃ śaraṇaṃ vraje ||6||

7evaṃ bruvanneva tadā dadarśa tapasāṃ nidhim
|
tamavyayamanaupamyamacintyaṃ śāśvataṃ dhruvam ||7||

8niṣkalaṃ sakalaṃ brahma nirguṇaṃ guṇagocaram
|
yogināṃ paramānandamakṣaraṃ mokṣasaṃjñitam ||8||

9manorindrāgnimarutāṃ viśvasya brahmaṇo gatim
|
agrāhyamacalaṃ śuddhaṃ buddhigrāhyaṃ manomayam ||9||

10durvijñeyamasaṃkhyeyaṃ duṣprāpamakṛtātmabhiḥ
|
yoniṃ viśvasya jagatastamasaḥ parataḥ param ||10||

11yaḥ prāṇavantamātmānaṃ jyotirjīvasthitaṃ manaḥ
|
taṃ devaṃ darśanākāṃkṣī bahūnvarṣagaṇānṛṣiḥ ||11||

12tapasyugre sthito bhūtvā dṛṣṭvā tuṣṭāva ceśvaram
|
taṇḍiruvāca|
pavitrāṇāṃ pavitrastvaṃ gatirgatimatāṃ vara ||12||

13atyugraṃ tejasāṃ tejastapasāṃ paramaṃ tapaḥ
|
viśvāvasuhiraṇyākṣapuruhūtanamaskṛta ||13||

14bhūrikalyāṇada vibho paraṃ satyaṃ namo'stu te
|
jātīmaraṇabhīrūṇāṃ yatīnāṃ yatatāṃ vibho ||14||

15nirvāṇada sahasrāṃśo namaste'stu sukhāśraya
|
brahmā śatakraturviṣṇurviśvedevā maharṣayaḥ ||15||

16na vidustvāṃ tu tattvena kuto vetsyāmahe vayam
|
tvattaḥ pravartate sarvaṃ tvayi sarvaṃ pratiṣṭhitam ||16||

17kālākhyaḥ puruṣākhyaśca brahmākhyaśca tvameva hi
|
tanavaste smṛtāstisraḥ purāṇajñaiḥ surarṣibhiḥ ||17||

18adhipauruṣamadhyātmamadhibhūtādhidaivatam
|
adhilokādhivijñānamadhiyajñastvameva hi ||18||

19tvāṃ viditvātmadehasthaṃ durvidaṃ daivatairapi
|
vidvāṃso yānti nirmuktāḥ paraṃ bhāvamanāmayam ||19||

20anicchatastava vibho janmamṛtyuranekataḥ
|
dvāraṃ tu svargamokṣāṇāmākṣeptā tvaṃ dadāsi ca ||20||

21tvaṃ vai svargaśca mokṣaśca kāmaḥ krodhastvameva ca
|
sattvaṃ rajastamaścaiva adhaścordhvaṃ tvameva hi ||21||

22brahmā bhavaśca viṣṇuśca skandendrau savitā yamaḥ
|
varuṇendū manurdhātā vidhātā tvaṃ dhaneśvaraḥ ||22||

23bhūrvāyuḥ salilāgniśca khaṃ vāgbuddhiḥ sthitirmatiḥ
|
karma satyānṛte cobhe tvamevāsti ca nāsti ca ||23||

24indriyāṇīndriyārthāśca prakṛtibhyaḥ paraṃ dhruvam
|
viśvāviśvaparo bhāvaścintyācintyastvameva hi ||24||

25yaccaitatparamaṃ brahma yacca tatparamaṃ padam
|
yā gatiḥ sāṃkhyayogānāṃ sa bhavānnātra saṃśayaḥ ||25||

26nūnamadya kṛtārthāḥ sma nūnaṃ prāptāḥ satāṃ gatim
|
yāṃ gatiṃ prārthayantīha jñānanirmalabuddhayaḥ ||26||

27aho mūḍhāḥ sma suciramimaṃ kālamacetasā
|
yanna vidmaḥ paraṃ devaṃ śāśvataṃ yaṃ vidurbudhāḥ ||27||

28seyamāsāditā sākṣāttvadbhaktirjanmabhirmayā
|
bhaktānugrahakṛddevo yaṃ jñātvā'mṛtamaśrute ||28||

29devāsuramunīnāṃ tu yacca guhyaṃ sanātanam
|
guhāyāṃ nihitaṃ brahma durvijñeya munerapi ||29||

30sa eṣa bhagavān devaḥ sarvakṛtsarvatomukhaḥ
|
sarvātmā sarvadarśī ca sarvagaḥ sarvaveditā ||30||

31dehakuddehabhṛddehī dehabhugdehināṃ gatiḥ
|
prāṇakṛtprāṇabhṛtprāṇī prāṇadaḥ prāṇināṃ gatiḥ ||31||

32adhyātmagatiriṣṭānāṃ dhyāyināmātmavedinām
|
apunarbhavakāmānāṃ yā gatiḥ so'yamīśvaraḥ ||32||

33ayaṃ ca sarvabhūtānāṃ śubhāśubhagatipradaḥ
|
ayaṃ ca janmamaraṇe vidadhyātsarvajantuṣu
|
ayaṃ saṃsiddhikāmānāṃ yā gatiḥ so'yamīśvaraḥ ||33||

34bhūrādyānsarvabhuvanānutpādya sadivaukasaḥ
|
dadhāti devastanubhiraṣṭābhiryo bibharti ca ||34||

35ataḥ pravartate sarvamasminsarvaṃ pratiṣṭhitam
|
asmiṃśca pralayaṃ yāti ayamekaḥ sanātanaḥ ||35||

36ayaṃ sa satyakāmānāṃ satyalokaḥ paraṃ satām
|
apavargaśca muktānāṃ kaivalyaṃ cātmavedinām ||36||

37ayaṃ brahmādibhiḥ siddhairguhāyāṃ gopitaḥ prabhuḥ
|
devāsuramanuṣyāṇāmaprakāśo bhavediti ||37||

38taṃ tvāṃ devāsuranarāstattvena na vidurbhavam
|
mohitāḥ khalvanenaiva hṛdisthenāprakāśinā ||38||

39ye cainaṃ pratipadyante bhaktiyogena bhāvitāḥ
|
teṣāmevātmanā''tmānaṃ darśayatyeṣa hṛcchayaḥ ||39||

40yaṃ jñātvā na punarjanma maraṇaṃ cāpi vidyate
|
yaṃ viditvā paraṃ vedyaṃ veditavyaṃ na vidyate ||40||

41yaṃ labdhvā paramaṃ lābhaṃ nādhikaṃ manyate budhaḥ
|
yāṃ sūkṣmāṃ paramāṃ prāptiṃ gacchannavyayamakṣayam ||41||

42yaṃ sāṃkhyā guṇatattvajñāḥ sāṃkhyaśāstraviśāradāḥ
|
sūkṣmajñānatarāḥ sūkṣmaṃ jñātvā mucyanti bandhanaiḥ ||42||

43yaṃ ca vedavido vedyaṃ vedānte ca pratiṣṭhitam
|
prāṇāyāmaparā nityaṃ yaṃ viśanti japanti ca ||43||

44oṃkārarathamāruhya te viśanti maheśvaram
|
ayaṃ sa devayānānāmādityo dvāramucyate ||44||

45ayaṃ ca pitṛyānānāṃ candramā dvāramucyate
|
eṣa kāṣṭhā diśaścaiva saṃvatsarayugādi ca ||45||

46divyādivyaḥ paro lābha ayane dakṣiṇottare
|
enaṃ prajāpatiḥ pūrvamārādhya bahubhiḥ stavaiḥ ||46||

47prajārthaṃ varayāmāsa nīlalohitasaṃjñitam
|
ṛgbhiryamanuśāsanti tattve karmaṇi bahvṛcāḥ ||47||

48yajurbhiryattridhā vedyaṃ juhvatyadhvaryavo'dhvare
|
sāmabhiryaṃ ca gāyanti sāmagāḥ śuddhabuddhayaḥ ||48||

49ṛtaṃ satyaṃ paraṃ brahma stuvantyātharvaṇā dvijāḥ
|
yajñasya paramā yoniḥ patiścāyaṃ paraḥ smṛtaḥ ||49||

50rātryahaḥśrotranayanaḥ pakṣamāsaśirobhujaḥ
|
ṛtuvīryastapodhairyo hyabdaguhyorupāda‌vān ||50||

51mṛtyuryamo hutāśaśca kālaḥ saṃhāravegavān
|
kālasya paramā yoniḥ kālaścāyaṃ sanātanaḥ ||51||

52candrādityau sanakṣatrau grahāśca saha vāyunā
|
dhruvaḥ saptarṣayaścaiva bhuvanāḥ sapta eva ca ||52||

53pradhānaṃ mahadadvyaktaṃ viśeṣāntaṃ savaikṛtam
|
brahmādistambaparyantaṃ bhūtādi sadasacca yat ||53||

54aṣṭau prakṛtayaścaiva prakṛtibhyaśca yaḥ paraḥ
|
asya devasya yadbhāgaṃ kṛtsnaṃ saṃparivartate ||54||

55etatparamamānandaṃ yattacchāśvatameva ca
|
eṣā gatirviraktānāmeṣa bhāvaḥ paraḥ satām ||55||

56etatpadamanudvignametadbrahma sanātanam
|
śāstravedāṅgaviduṣāmetaddhyānaṃ paraṃ padam ||56||

57iyaṃ sā paramā kāṣṭhā iyaṃ sā paramā kalā
|
iyaṃ sā paramā siddhiriyaṃ sā paramā gatiḥ ||57||

58iyaṃ sā paramā śāntiriyaṃ sā nirvṛtiḥ parā
|
yaṃ prāpya kṛtakṛtyāḥ sma ityamanyanta yoginaḥ ||58||

59iyaṃ tuṣṭiriyaṃ siddhiriyaṃ śrutiriyaṃ smṛtiḥ
|
adhyātmagatiriṣṭānāṃ viduṣāṃ prāptiravyayā ||59||

60yajatāṃ kāmayānānāṃ makhairvipuladakṣiṇaiḥ
|
yā gatiryajñaśīlānāṃ sā gatistvaṃ na saṃśayaḥ ||60||

61samyak yogajapaiḥ śāntirniyamairdehatāpanaiḥ
|
tapyatāṃ yā gatirdeva paramā sā gatirbhavān ||61||

62karmanyāsakṛtānāṃ ca viraktānāṃ tatastataḥ
|
yā gatirbrahmasadane sā gatistvaṃ sanātana ||62||

63apunarbhavakāmānāṃ vairāgye vartatāṃ ca yā
|
prakṛtīnāṃ layānāṃ ca sā gatistvaṃ sanātana ||63||

64jñānavijñānayuktānāṃ nirupākhyā nirañjanā
|
kaivalyā yā gatirdeva paramā sā gatirbhavān ||64||

65vedaśāstrapurāṇoktāḥ pañcaitā gatayaḥ smṛtāḥ
|
tvatprasādāddhi labhyante na labhyante'nyathā vibho ||65||

66iti taṇḍistaporāśistuṣṭāveśānamātmanā
|
jagau ca paramaṃ brahma yatpurā lokakṛjjagau ||66||

67upamanyuruvāca|
evaṃ stuto mahādevastaṇḍinā brahmavādinā
|
uvāca bhagavān deva umayā sahitaḥ prabhuḥ ||67||

68brahmā śatakraturviṣṇurviśvedevā maharṣayaḥ
|
na vidustvāmiti tatastuṣṭaḥ provāca taṃ śivaḥ ||68||

69śrībhagavānuvāca
|
akṣayaścāvyayaścaiva bhavitā duḥkhavarjitaḥ
|
yaśasvī tejasā yukto divyajñānasamanvitaḥ ||69||

70ṛṣīṇāmabhigamyaśca sūtrakartā sutastava
|
matprasādāddvijaśreṣṭha bhaviṣyati na saṃśayaḥ ||70||

71kaṃ vā kāmaṃ dadāmyadya brūhi yadvatsa kāṃkṣase
|
prāñjaliḥ sa uvācedaṃ tvayi bhaktirdṛḍhā'stu me ||71||

72upamanyuruvāca|
etān dattvā varāndevo vandyamānaḥ surarṣibhiḥ
|
stūyamānaśca vibudhaistatraivāntaradhīyata ||72||

73antarhite bhagavati sānuge yādaveśvara
|
ṛṣirāśramamāgamya mamaitatproktavāniva||73||

74yāni ca prathitānyādau taṇḍirākhyātavānmama
|
nāmāni mānavaśreṣṭha tāni tvaṃ śruṇu siddhaye||74||

75daśanāmasahasrāṇi deveṣvāha pitāmahaḥ
|
sarvasya śāstreṣu tathā daśanāmaśatāni ca||75||

76guhyānīmāni nāmāni taṇḍirbhagavato'cyuta
|
devaprasādāddeveśaḥ purā prāha mahātmane||76||
