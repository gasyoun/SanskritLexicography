Mahābhārata 6.94 (Bhīṣmaparvan)

1sañjaya uvāca|
svasainyaṃ nihataṃ dṛṣṭvā rājā duryodhanaḥ svayam
|
abhyadhāvata saṃkruddho bhīmasenamarindamam ||1||

2pragṛhya sumahaccāpamindrāśanisamasvanam
|
mahatā śaravarṣeṇa pāṇḍavaṃ samavākirat ||2||

3ardhacandraṃ ca sandhāya sutīkṣṇaṃ lomavāhinam
|
bhīmasenasya ciccheda cāpaṃ krodhasamanvitaḥ ||3||

4tadantaraṃ ca saṃprekṣya tvaramāṇo mahārathaḥ
|
prasaṃdadhe śitaṃ bāṇaṃ girīṇāmapi dāraṇam ||4||

5tenorasi mahārāja bhīmasenamatāḍayat
|
sa gāḍhaviddho vyathitaḥ sṛkkiṇī parisaṃlihan ||5||

6samālalambe tejasvī dhvajaṃ hemapariṣkṛtam
|
tathā vimanasaṃ dṛṣṭvā bhīmasenaṃ ghaṭotkacaḥ ||6||

7krodhenābhiprajajvāla didhakṣanniva pāvakaḥ
|
abhimanyumukhāścāpi pāṇḍavānāṃ mahārathāḥ ||7||

8samabhyadhāvan krośanto rājānaṃ jātasaṃbhramāḥ
|
saṃprekṣyaitānsaṃpatataḥ saṃkuddhānjātasaṃbhramān ||8||

9bhāradvājo'bravīdvākyaṃ tāvakānāṃ mahārathān
|
kṣipraṃ gacchata bhadraṃ vo rājānaṃ parirakṣata ||9||

10saṃśayaṃ paramaṃ prāptaṃ majjantaṃ vyasanārṇave
|
ete kruddhā maheṣvāsāḥ pāṇḍavānāṃ mahārathāḥ ||10||

11bhīmasenaṃ puraskṛtya duryodhanamupādravan
|
nānāvidhāni śastrāṇi visṛjanto jaye dhṛtāḥ ||11||

12nadanto bhairavānnādāṃstrāsayantaśca bhūmipān
|
tadācāryavacaḥ śrutvā saumada‌ttipurogamāḥ ||12||

13tāvakāḥ samavartanta pāṇḍavānāmanīkinīm
|
kṛpo bhūriśravāḥ śalyo droṇaputro viviṃśatiḥ ||13||

14citraseno vikarṇaśca saindhavo'tha bṛhadbalaḥ
|
āvantyau ca maheṣvāsau kauravaṃ paryavārayan ||14||

15te viṃśatipadaṃ gatvā saṃprahāraṃ pracakrire
|
pāṇḍavā dhārtarāṣṭrāśca parasparajighāṃsavaḥ ||15||

16evamuktvā mahābāhurmahadvisphārya kārmukam
|
bhāradvājastato bhīmaṃ ṣaḍviṃśatyā samārpayat ||16||

17bhūyaścainaṃ mahābāhuḥ śaraiḥ śīghramavākirat
|
parvataṃ vāridhārābhiḥ prāvṛṣīva balāhakaḥ ||17||

18taṃ pratyavidhyaddaśabhirbhīmasenaḥ śilīmukhaiḥ
|
tvaramāṇo maheṣvāsaḥ savye pārśve mahābalaḥ ||18||

19sa gāḍhaviddho vyathito vayovṛddhaśca bhārata
|
pranaṣṭasaṃjñaḥ sahasā rathopastha upāviśat ||19||

20guruṃ pravyathitaṃ dṛṣṭvā rājā duryodhanaḥ svayam
|
drauṇāyaniśca saṃkruddhau bhīmasenamabhidrutau ||20||

21tāvāpatantau saṃprekṣya kālāntakayamopamau
|
bhīmaseno mahābāhurgadāmādāya satvaram ||21||

22avaplutya rathāt tūrṇaṃ tasthau giririvācalaḥ
|
samudyamya gadāṃ gurvī yamadaṇḍopamāṃ raṇe ||22||

23tamudyatagadaṃ dṛṣṭvā kailāsamiva śṛṅgiṇam
|
kauravo droṇaputraśca sahitāvabhyadhāvatām ||23||

24tāvāpatantau sahitau tvaritau balināṃ varau
|
abhyadhāvata vegena tvaramāṇo vṛkodaraḥ ||24||

25tamāpatantaṃ saṃprekṣya saṃkruddhaṃ bhīmadarśanam
|
samabhyadhāvaṃstvaritāḥ kauravāṇāṃ mahārathāḥ ||25||

26bhāradvājamukhāḥ sarve bhīmasenajighāṃsayā
|
nānāvidhāni śastrāṇi bhīmasyorasyapātayan ||26||

27sahitāḥ pāṇḍavaṃ sarve pīḍayantaḥ samantataḥ
|
taṃ dṛṣṭvā saṃśayaṃ prāptaṃ pīḍyamānaṃ mahāratham ||27||

28abhimanyuprabhṛtayaḥ pāṇḍavānāṃ mahārathāḥ
|
abhyadhāvan parīpsantaḥ prāṇāṃstyaktvā sudustyajān ||28||

29anūpādhipatiḥ śūro bhīmasya dayitaḥ sakhā
|
nīlo nīlāṃbudaprakhyaḥ saṃkruddho drauṇimabhyayāt ||29||

30spardhate hi maheṣvāso nityaṃ droṇasutena saḥ
|
sa visphārya mahaccāpaṃ drauṇiṃ vivyādha patriṇā ||30||

31yathā śakro mahārāja purā vivyādha dānavam
|
vipracittiṃ durādharṣaṃ devatānāṃ bhayaṅkaram ||31||

32yena lokatrayaṃ krodhāt trāsitaṃ svena tejasā
|
tathā nīlena nirbhinnaḥ sumuktena patatriṇā ||32||

33saṃjātarudhirotpīḍo drauṇiḥ krodhasamanvitaḥ
|
sa visphārya dhanuścitramindrāśanisamasvanam ||33||

34dadhne nīlavināśāya matiṃ matimatāṃ varaḥ
|
tataḥ sandhāya vimalān bhallān karmāramārjitān ||34||

35jaghāna caturo vāhān pātayāmāsa ca dhvajam
|
saptamena ca bhallena nīlaṃ vivyādha vakṣasi ||35||

36sa gāḍhaviddho vyathito rathopastha upāviśat
|
mohitaṃ vīkṣya rājānaṃ nīlamabhracayopamam ||36||

37ghaṭotkaco'bhisaṃkruddho jñātibhiḥ parivāritaḥ
|
abhidudrāva vegena drauṇimāhavaśobhinam ||37||

38tathetare cābhyadhāvan rākṣasā yuddhadurmadāḥ
|
tamāpatantaṃ saṃprekṣya rākṣasaṃ ghoradarśanam ||38||

39abhyadhāvata tejasvī bhāradvājātmajastvaran
|
nijaghāna ca saṃkruddho rākṣasān bhīmadarśanān ||39||

40ye'bhavannagrataḥ kruddhā rākṣasasya puraḥsarāḥ
|
vimukhāṃścaiva tān dṛṣṭvā drauṇicāpacyutaiḥ śaraiḥ ||40||

41akruddhyata mahākāyo bhaimasenirghaṭotkacaḥ
|
prāduścakre tato māyāṃ ghorarūpāṃ sudāruṇām ||41||

42mohayansamare drauṇiṃ māyāvī rākṣasādhipaḥ
|
tataste tāvakāḥ sarve māyayā vimukhīkṛtāḥ ||42||

43anyonyaṃ samapaśyanta nikṛttā medinītale
|
viceṣṭamānāḥ kṛpaṇāḥ śoṇitena pariplutāḥ ||43||

44droṇaṃ duryodhanaṃ śalyamaśvatthāmānameva ca
|
prāyaśaśca maheṣvāsā ye pradhānāḥ sma kauravāḥ ||44||

45vidhvastā rathinaḥ sarve rājānaśca nipātitāḥ
|
hayāścaiva hayārohāḥ sannikṛttāḥ sahasraśaḥ ||45||

46taddṛṣṭvā tāvakaṃ sainyaṃ vidrutaṃ śibiraṃ prati
|
mama prākrośato rājaṃstathā devavratasya ca ||46||

47yudhyadhvaṃ mā palāyadhvaṃ māyaiṣā rākṣasī raṇe
|
ghaṭotkacapramukteti nātiṣṭhanta vimohitāḥ ||47||

48naiva te śraddadhurbhītā vadatorāvayorvacaḥ
|
tāṃśca pradravato dṛṣṭvā jayaṃ prāptāśca pāṇḍavāḥ ||48||

49ghaṭotkacena sahitāḥ siṃhanādān pracakrire
|
śaṅkhadundubhinirghoṣaiḥ samantānnedire bhṛśam ||49||

50evaṃ tava balaṃ sarvaṃ haiḍimbena durātmanā
|
sūryāstamanavelāyāṃ prabhagnaṃ vidrutaṃ diśaḥ ||50||
