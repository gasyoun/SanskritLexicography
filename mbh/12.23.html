Mahābhārata 12.23 (Śāntiparvan)

1vaiśampāyana uvāca|
evamuktastu kaunteyo guḍākeśena pāṇḍavaḥ
|
novāca kiñcitkauravyastato dvaipāyano'bravīt ||1||

2vyāsa uvāca |
bībhatsorvacanaṃ saumya satyametadyudhiṣṭhira
|
śāstradṛṣṭaḥ paro dharmaḥ sthito gārhasthyamāśritaḥ ||2||

3svadharmaṃ cara dharmajña yathāśāstraṃ yathāvidhi
|
na hi gārhasthyamutsṛjya tavāraṇyaṃ vidhīyate ||3||

4gṛhasthaṃ hi sadā devāḥ pitaro'tithayastathā
|
bhṛtyāścaivopajīvanti tān bharasva mahīpate ||4||

5vayāṃsi paśavaścaiva bhūtāni ca janādhipa
|
gṛhasthaireva dhāryante tasmācchreṣṭho gṛhāśramī ||5||

6so'yaṃ caturṇāmeteṣāmāśramāṇāṃ durācaraḥ
|
taṃ carādya vidhiṃ pārtha duścaraṃ durbalendriyaiḥ ||6||

7vedajñānaṃ ca te kṛtsnaṃ tapaścācaritaṃ mahat
|
pitṛpaitāmahaṃ rājyaṃ dhuryavadvoḍhumarhasi ||7||

8tapo yajñastathā vidyā bhaikṣyamindriyasaṃyamaḥ
|
dhyānamekāntaśīlatvaṃ tuṣṭirjñānaṃ ca śaktitaḥ||8||

9brāhmaṇānāṃ mahārāja ceṣṭā saṃsiddhikārikā
|
kṣatriyāṇāṃ tu vakṣyāmi tavāpi viditaṃ punaḥ ||9||

10yajño vidyā samutthānamasantoṣaḥ śriyaṃ prati
|
daṇḍadhāraṇamugratvaṃ prajānāṃ paripālanam ||10||

11vedajñānaṃ tathā kṛtsnaṃ tapaḥ sucaritaṃ tathā
|
draviṇopārjanaṃ bhūri pātre ca pratipādanam ||11||

12etāni rājñāṃ karmāṇi sukṛtāni viśāṃpate
|
imaṃ lokamamuṃ caiva sādhayantīti naḥ śrutam ||12||

13eṣāṃ jyāyastu kaunteya daṇḍadhāraṇamucyate
|
balaṃ hi kṣatriye nityaṃ bale daṇḍaḥ samāhitaḥ ||13||

14etā vidyāḥ kṣatriyāṇāṃ rājan saṃsiddhikārikāḥ
|
api gāthāmimāṃ cāpi bṛhaspatiragāyata ||14||

15bhūmiretau nigirati sarpo bilaśayāniva
|
rājānaṃ cāviroddhāraṃ brāhmaṇaṃ cāpravāsinam ||15||

16sudyumnaścāpi rājarṣiḥ śrūyate daṇḍadhāraṇāt
|
prāptavānparamāṃ siddhiṃ dakṣaḥ prācetaso yathā ||16||

17yudhiṣṭhira uvāca|
bhagavankarmaṇā kena sudyumno vasudhādhipaḥ
|
saṃsiddhiṃ paramāṃ prāptaḥ śrotumicchāmi taṃ nṛpam ||17||

18vyāsa uvāca |
atrāpyudāharantīmamitihāsaṃ purātanam
|
śaṅkhaśca likhitaścāstāṃ bhrātarau saṃśitavratau ||18||

19tayorāvasathāvāstāṃ ramaṇīyau pṛthaka pṛthak
|
nityapuṣpaphalairvṛkṣairupetau bāhudāmanu ||19||

20tataḥ kadācillikhitaḥ śaṅkhasyāśramamāgataḥ
|
yadṛcchayā'tha śaṅkho'pi niṣkrānto'bhavadāśramāt ||20||

21so'bhigamyāśramaṃ bhrātuḥ śaṅkhasya likhitastadā
|
phalāni pātayāmāsa samyakpariṇatānyuta ||21||

22tānyupādāya visrabdho bhakṣayāmāsa sa dvijaḥ
|
tasmiṃśca bhakṣayatyeva śaṅkho'pyāśramamāgataḥ ||22||

23bhakṣayantaṃ tu taṃ dṛṣṭvā śaṅkho bhrātaramabravīt
|
kutaḥ phalānyavāptāni hetunā kena khādasi ||23||

24so'bravīdbhrātaraṃ jyeṣṭhamupasṛtyābhivādya ca
|
ita eva gṛhītāni mayeti prahasanniva ||24||

25tamabravīttathā śaṅkhastīvraroṣasamanvitaḥ
|
steyaṃ tvayā kṛtamidaṃ phalānyādadatā svayam ||25||

26gaccha rājānamāsādya svakarma kathayasva vai
|
adattādānamevaṃ hi kṛtaṃ pārthivasattama ||26||

27stenaṃ māṃ tvaṃ viditvā ca svadharmamanupālaya
|
śīghraṃ dhāraya caurasya mama daṇḍaṃ narādhipa ||27||

28ityuktastasya vacanātsudyumnaṃ sa narādhipam
|
abhyagacchanmahābāho likhitaḥ saṃśitavrataḥ ||28||

29sudyumnastvantapālebhyaḥ śrutvā likhitamāgatam
|
abhyagacchatsahāmātyaḥ padbhyāmeva janeśvaraḥ ||29||

30tamabravītsamāgamya sa rājā dharmavittamam
|
kimāgamanamācakṣva bhagavankṛtameva tat ||30||

31evamuktaḥ sa viprarṣiḥ sudyumnamidamabravīt
|
pratiśrutya kariṣyeti śrutvā tatkartumarhasi ||31||

32anisṛṣṭāni guruṇā phalāni manujarṣabha
|
bhakṣitāni mahārāja tatra māṃ śādhi mā ciram ||32||

33sudyumna uvāca |
pramāṇaṃ cenmato rājā bhavato daṇḍadhāraṇe
|
anujñāyāmapi tathā hetuḥ syādbrāhmaṇarṣabha ||33||

34sa bhavānabhyanujñātaḥ śucikarmā mahāvrataḥ
|
brūhi kāmānato'nyāṃstvaṃ kariṣyāmi hi te vacaḥ ||34||

35vyāsa uvāca |
saṃcchādyamāno brahmarṣi pārthivena mahātmanā
|
nānyaṃ sa varayāmāsa tasmāddaṇḍādṛte varam ||35||

36tataḥ sa pṛthivīpālo likhitasya mahātmanaḥ
|
karau pracchedayāprāsa dhṛtadaṇḍo jagāma saḥ ||36||

37sa gatvā bhrātaraṃ śaṅkhamārtarūpo'bravīdidam
|
dhṛtadaṇḍasya durbuddherbhavāṃtat kṣantumarhati ||37||

38śaṅkha uvāca |
na kupye tava dharmajña na tvaṃ dūṣayase mama
|
dharmastu te vyatikrāntastataste niṣkṛtiḥ kṛtā ||38||

39tvaṃ gatvā bāhudāṃ śīghraṃ tarpayasva yathāvidhi
|
devānnṛṣīnpitṝṃścaivaṃ mā cādharme manaḥ kṛthāḥ ||39||

40tasya tadvacanaṃ śrutvā śaṅkhasya likhitastadā
|
avagāhyāpagāṃ puṇyāmudakārthaṃ pracakrame ||40||

41prādurāstāṃ tatastasya karau jalajasannibhau
|
tataḥ sa vismito bhrāturdarśayāmāsa tau karau ||41||

42tatastamabravīcchaṅkhastapasedaṃ kṛtaṃ mayā
|
mā ca te'tra viśaṅkā'bhūddaivamatra vidhīyate ||42||

43likhita uvāca |
kiṃ tu nāhaṃ tvayā pūtaḥ pūrvameva mahādyute
|
yasya te tapaso vīryamīdṛśaṃ dvijasattama ||43||

44śaṅkha uvāca |
evametanmayā kāryaṃ nāhaṃ daṇḍadharastava
|
sa ca pūto narapatistvaṃ cāpi pitṛbhiḥ saha ||44||

45vyāsa uvāca |
sa rājā pāṇḍavaśreṣṭha śreyānvai tena karmaṇā
|
prāptavānparamāṃ siddhiṃ dakṣaḥ prācetaso yathā ||45||

46eṣa dharmaḥ kṣatriyāṇāṃ prajānāṃ paripālanam
|
utpatho'nyo mahārāja mā sma śoke manaḥ kṛthāḥ ||46||

47bhrāturasya hitaṃ vākyaṃ śruṇu dharmajña sattama
|
daṇḍa eva hi rājendra kṣatradharmo na muṇḍanam||47||
