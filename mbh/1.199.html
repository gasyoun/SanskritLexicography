Mahābhārata 1.199 (Ādiparvan)

1vaiśaṃpāyana uvāca|
pāṇḍavaiḥ saha saṃyogaṃ gatasya drupadasya ha
|
na babhūva bhayaṃ kiṃciddevebhyo'pi kathaṃcana||1||

2kuntīmāsādya tā nāryo drupadasya mahātmanaḥ
|
nāma saṃkīrtayantyo'syā jagmuḥ pādau svamūrdhabhiḥ||2||

3kṛṣṇā ca kṣaumasaṃvītā kṛtakautukamaṅgalā
|
kṛtābhivādanā śvaśrvā tasthau prahvā kṛtāñjaliḥ||3||

4rūpalakṣaṇasaṃpannāṃ śīlācārasamanvitām
|
draupadīmavadatpremṇā pṛthāśīrvacanaṃ snuṣām||4||

5yathendrāṇī harihaye svāhā caiva vibhāvasau
|
rohiṇī ca yathā some damayantī yathā nale||5||

6yathā vaiśravaṇe bhadrā vasiṣṭhe cāpyarundhatī
|
yathā nārāyaṇe lakṣmīstathā tvaṃ bhava bhartṛṣu||6||

7jīvasūrvīrasūrbhadre bahusaukhyasamanvitā
|
subhagā bhogasaṃpannā yajñapatnī pativratā||7||

8atithīnāgatānsādhūnvṛddānbālāṃstathā gurūn
|
pūjayantyā yathānyāyaṃ śaśvadgacchantu te samāḥ||8||

9kurujāṅgalamukhyeṣu rāṣṭreṣu nagareṣu ca
|
anu tvamabhiṣicyasva nṛpatiṃ dharmavatsalā||9||

10patibhirnirjitāmurvīṃ vikrameṇa mahābalaiḥ
|
kuru brāhmaṇasātsarvāmaśvamedhe mahākratau||10||

11pṛthivyāṃ yāni ratnāni guṇavanti guṇānvite
|
tānyāpnuhi tvaṃ kalyāṇi sukhinī śaradāṃ śatam||11||

12yathā ca tvābhinandāmi vadhvadya kṣaumasaṃvṛtām
|
tathā bhūyo'bhinandiṣye jātaputrāṃ guṇānvitām||12||

13vaiśaṃpāyana uvāca|
tatastu kṛtadārebhyaḥ pāṇḍubhyaḥ prāhiṇoddhariḥ
|
vaidūryamaṇicitrāṇi haimānyābharaṇāni ca||13||

14vāsāṃsi ca mahārhāṇi nānādeśyāni mādhavaḥ
|
kambalājinaratnāni sparśavanti śubhāni ca||14||

15śayanāsanayānāni vividhāni mahānti ca
|
vaidūryavajracitrāṇi śataśo bhājanāni ca||15||

16rūpayauvanadākṣiṇyairupetāśca svalaṅkṛtāḥ
|
preṣyāḥ saṃpradadau kṛṣṇo nānādeśyāḥ svalaṅkṛtāḥ||16||

17gajānvinītānbhadrāṃśca sadaśvāṃśca svalaṅkṛtān
|
rathāṃśca dāntānsauvarṇaiḥ śubhraiḥ paṭṭairalaṅkṛtān||17||

18koṭiśaśca suvarṇaṃ ca teṣāmakṛtakaṃ tathā
|
vīthīkṛtamameyātmā prāhiṇonmadhusūdanaḥ||18||

19tatsarvaṃ pratijagrāha dharmarājo yudhiṣṭhiraḥ
|
mudā paramayā yukto govindapriyakāmyayā||19||
