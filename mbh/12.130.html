Mahābhārata 12.130 (Śāntiparvan)

1yudhiṣṭhira uvāca|
mitraiḥ prahīyamāṇasya bahvamitrasya kā gatiḥ
|
rājñaḥ saṃkṣīṇakośasya balahīnasya bhārata ||1||

2duṣṭāmātyasahāyasya cyutamantrasya sarvataḥ
|
rājyātpracyavamānasya gatimagryamapaśyataḥ ||2||

3paracakrābhiyātasya pararāṣṭrāṇi mṛdgataḥ
|
vigrahe vartamānasya durbalasya valīyasā ||3||

4asaṃvihitarāṣṭrasya deśakālāvajānataḥ
|
aprāpyaṃ ca bhavetsāntvaṃ bhedo vā'pyatipīḍanāt
|
jīvitaṃ tvarthaheturvā tatra kiṃ sukṛtaṃ bhavet ||4||

5bhīṣma uvāca|
guhyaṃ dharmaja mā prākṣīratīva bharatarṣabha
|
apṛṣṭho notsahe vaktuṃ dharmametaṃ yudhiṣṭhira ||5||

6dharmo hyaṇīyānvacanādbuddhiśca bharatarṣabha
|
śrutvopāsya sadācāraiḥ sādhurbhavati sa kvacit ||6||

7karmaṇā buddhipūrveṇa bhavatyāḍhyo na vā punaḥ
|
tādṛśo'yamanupraśnaḥ saṃvyavasyaḥ svayā dhiyā ||7||

8upāyaṃ dharmabahulaṃ yātrārthaṃ śṛṇu bhārata
|
nāhametādṛśaṃ dharmaṃ bubhūṣe dharmakāraṇāt ||8||

9duḥkhādāna iha hyeṣa syāttu paścātkṣayopamaḥ
|
abhigamyamatīnāṃ hi sarvāsāmeva niścayaḥ ||9||

10yathā yathā hi puruṣo nityaṃ śāstramavekṣate
|
tathā tathā vijānāti vijñānamatha rocate ||10||

11avijñānādayogo hi puruṣasyopajāyate
|
vijñānādapi yogaśca yogo bhūtikaraḥ paraḥ ||11||

12aśaṅkamāno vacanamanasūyuridaṃ śṛṇu
|
rājñaḥ kośakṣayādeva jāyate balasaṃkṣayaḥ ||12||

13kośaṃ ca janayedrājā nirjalebhyo yathā jalam
|
kālaṃ prāpyānugṛhṇīyādeṣa dharmaḥ sanātanaḥ
|
upāyadharmaṃ prāpyemaṃ pūrvairācaritaṃ janaiḥ ||13||

14anyo dharmaḥ samarthānāmāpatsvanyaśca bhārata
|
prākkośātprāpyate dharmo vṛttirdharmādgarīyasī ||14||

15dharmaṃ prāpya nyāyavṛttiṃ na balīyānna vindati
|
yasmādbalasyopapattirekāntena na vidyate ||15||

16tasmādāpatsvadharmo'pi śrūyate dharmalakṣaṇaḥ
|
adharmo jāyate tasminniti vai kavayo viduḥ ||16||

17anantaraṃ kṣatriyasya tatra kiṃ vicikitsyate
|
yathāsya dharmo na glāyenneyācchatruvaśaṃ yathā
|
tat kartavyamihetyāhurnātmānamavasādayet ||17||

18sarvātmanaiva dharmasya na parasya na cātmanaḥ
|
sarvopāyairujjihīrṣedātmānamiti niścayaḥ ||18||

19tatra dharmavidāṃ tāta niścayo dharmanaipuṇam
|
udyamo naipuṇaṃ kṣātre bāhuvīryāditi śrutiḥ ||19||

20kṣatriyo vṛttisaṃrodhe kasya nādātumarhati
|
anyatra tāpasasvācca brāhmaṇasvācca bhārata ||20||

21yathā vai brāhmaṇaḥ sīdannayājyamapi yājayet
|
abhojyānāni cāśnīyāttathedaṃ nātra saṃśayaḥ ||21||

22pīḍitasya kimu dvāramutpatho vidhṛtasya ca
|
advārataḥ pradravati yathā bhavati pīḍitaḥ ||22||

23yasya kośabalaglānyā sarvalokaparābhavaḥ
|
bhaikṣyacaryā na vihitā na ca viṭśūdrajīvikā ||23||

24svadharmānantarā vṛttirjātyānanupajīvataḥ
|
jahataḥ prathamaṃ kalpamanukalpena jīvanam ||24||

25āpadgatena dharmāṇāmanyāyenopajīvanam
|
api hyetadbrāhmaṇeṣu dṛṣṭaṃ vṛttiparikṣaye ||25||

26kṣatriye saṃśayaḥ kasmādityevaṃ niścitaṃ sadā
|
ādadīta viśiṣṭebhyo nāvasīdetkathaṃcana ||26||

27hantāraṃ rakṣitāraṃ ca prajānāṃ kṣatriyaṃ viduḥ
|
tasmātsaṃrakṣatā kāryamādānaṃ kṣatrabandhunā ||27||

28anyatra rājanhiṃsāyā vṛttirnehāsti kasyacit
|
apyaraṇyasamutthasya ekasya carato muneḥ ||28||

29na śaṅkhalikhitāṃ vṛttiṃ śakyamāsthāya jīvitum
|
viśeṣataḥ kuruśreṣṭha prajāpālanamīpsayā ||29||

30parasparaṃ hi saṃrakṣā rājñā rāṣṭreṇa cāpadi
|
nityameva hi kartavyā eṣa dharmaḥ sanātanaḥ ||30||

31rājā rāṣṭraṃ yathā''patsu dravyaughairapi rakṣati
|
rāṣṭreṇa rājā vyasane rakṣitavyastathā bhavet ||31||

32kośaṃ daṇḍaṃ balaṃ mitraṃ yadanyadapi saṃcitam
|
na kurvītāntaraṃ rāṣṭre rājā parigataḥ kṣudhā ||32||

33bījaṃ bhaktena sampādyamiti dharmavido viduḥ
|
atraitacchambarasyāhumahāmāyasya darśanam ||33||

34dhiktasya jīvitaṃ rājño rāṣṭraṃ yasyāvasīdati
|
avṛttyānyamanuṣyo'pi yo vaideśika ityapi ||34||

35rājñaḥ kośabalaṃ mūlaṃ kośamūlaṃ punarbalam
|
tanmūlaṃ sarvadharmāṇāṃ dharmamūlāḥ punaḥ prajāḥ ||35||

36nānyānapīḍayitveha kośaḥ śakyaḥ kuto balam
|
tadarthaṃ pīḍayitvā ca doṣaṃ prāptuṃ na so'rhati ||36||

37akāryamapi yajñārthaṃ kriyate yajñakarmasu
|
etasmātkāraṇādrājā na doṣaṃ prāptumarhati ||37||

38arthārthamanyadbhavati viparītamathāparam
|
anarthārthamathāpyanyattatsarvaṃ hyarthakāraṇam
|
evaṃ buddhyā samprapaśyenmedhāvī kāryaniścayam ||38||

39yajñārthamanyadbhavati yajño'nyārthastathā paraḥ
|
yajñasyārthārthamevānyattatsarvaṃ yajñasādhanam ||39||

40upamāmatra vakṣyāmi dharmatattvaprakāśinīm
|
yūpaṃ chindanti yajñārthaṃ tatra ye paripanthinaḥ ||40||

41drumāḥ kecana sāmantā dhruvaṃ chindanti tānapi
|
te cāpi nipatanto'nyānnighnantyeva vanaspatīn ||41||

42evaṃ kośasya mahato ye narāḥ paripanthinaḥ
|
tānahatvā na paśyāmi siddhimatra paraṃtapa ||42||

43dhanena jayate lokāvubhau paramimaṃ tathā
|
satyaṃ ca dharmavacanaṃ yathā nāstyadhanastathā ||43||

44sarvopāyairādadīta dhanaṃ yajñaprayojanam
|
na tulyadoṣaḥ syādevaṃ kāryākāryeṣu bhārata ||44||

45naitau sambhavato rājankathaṃcidapi pārthiva
|
na haraṇyeṣu paśyāmi dhanavṛddhānahaṃ kvacit ||45||

46yadidaṃ dṛśyate vittaṃ pṛthivyāmiha kiṃcana
|
mamedaṃ syānmamedaṃ syādityevaṃ kākṣate janaḥ ||46||

47na ca rājyasamo dharmaḥ kaścidasti parantapa
|
dharmaḥ saṃśabdito rājñāmāpadarthamato'nyathā ||47||

48dānena karmaṇā cānye tapasā'nye tapasvinaḥ
|
buddhyā dākṣyeṇa caivānye vindanti dhanasaṃcayān ||48||

49adhanaṃ durbalaṃ prāhurdhanena balavānbhavet
|
sarvaṃ dhanavatā prāpyaṃ sarvaṃ tarati kośavān ||49||

50kośena dharmaḥ kāmaśca paralokastathā hyayam
|
taṃ ca dharmeṇa lipseta nādharmeṇa kadācana ||50||
