Mahābhārata 12.345 (Śāntiparvan)

1vaiśampāyana uvāca|
kasyacittvatha kālasya nāradaḥ parameṣṭhijaḥ
|
daivaṃ kṛtvā yathānyāyaṃ pitryaṃ cakre tataḥ param||1||

2tatastaṃ vacanaṃ prāha jyeṣṭho dharmātmajaḥ prabhuḥ
|
ka ijyate dvijaśreṣṭha daive pitrye ca kalpite||2||

3tvayā matimatāṃ śreṣṭha tanme śaṃsa yathāgamam
|
kimetatkriyate karma phalaṃ vāsya kimiṣyate||3||

4nārada uvāca|
tvayaitatkathitaṃ pūrvaṃ daivaṃ kartavyamityapi
|
daivataṃ ca paro yajñaḥ paramātmā sanātanaḥ||4||

5tatastadbhāvito nityaṃ yaje vaikuṇṭhamavyayam
|
tasmācca prasṛtaḥ pūrvaṃ brahmā lokapitāmahaḥ||5||

6mama vai pitaraṃ prītaḥ parameṣṭhyapyajījanat
|
ahaṃ saṃkalpajastasya putraḥ prathamakalpitaḥ||6||

7yajāmi vai pitṝnsādho nārāyaṇavidhau kṛte
|
evaṃ sa eva bhagavānpitā mātā pitāmahaḥ||7||

8ijyate pitṛyajñeṣu tathā nityaṃ jagatpatiḥ
|
śrutiścāpyaparā devī putrānhi pitaro'yajan||8||

9vedaśrutiḥ praṇaṣṭā ca punaradhyāpitā sutaiḥ
|
tataste mantradā putrāḥ pitṛtvamupapedire||9||

10nūnaṃ suraistadviditaṃ yuvayorbhāvitātmanoḥ
|
putrāśca pitaraścaiva parasparamapūjayan||10||

11trīnpiṇḍānnyasya vai pṛthvyāṃ pūrvaṃ dattvā kuśāniti
|
kathaṃ tu piṇḍasaṃjñāṃ te pitaro lebhire purā||11||

12naranārāyaṇāvūcatuḥ
|
imāṃ hi dharaṇīṃ pūrvaṃ naṣṭāṃ sāgaramekhalām
|
govinda ujjahārāśu vārāṃ rūpamāsthitaḥ||12||

13sthāpayitvā tu dharaṇīṃ sve sthāne puruṣottamaḥ
|
jalakardamaliptāṅgo lokakāryārthamudyataḥ||13||

14prāpte cāhnikakāle tu madhyadeśagate ravau
|
daṃṣṭrāvilagnāṃstrīnpiṇḍānvidhūya sahasā prabhuḥ||14||

15sthāpayāmāsa vai pṛthvyāṃ kuśānāstīrya nārada
|
sa teṣvātmānamuddiśya pitryaṃ cakre yathāvidhi||15||

16saṃkalpayitvā trīnpiṇḍānsvenaiva vidhinā prabhuḥ
|
ātmagātroṣmasaṃbhūtaiḥ snehagarbhaistilairapi||16||

17prokṣyāpavargaṃ deveśaḥ prāṅmukhaḥ kṛtavānsvayam
|
maryādāsthāpanārthaṃ ca tato vacanamuktavān||17||

18vṛṣākapiruvāca |
ahaṃ hi pitaraḥ sraṣṭumudyato lokakṛtsvayam
|
yasya cintayataḥ sadyaḥ pitṛkāryavidhīnparān||18||

19daṃṣṭrābhyāṃ pravinirdhūtā mamaite dakṣiṇāṃ diśam
|
āśritā dharaṇīṃ piṇḍāstasmātpitara eva te||19||

20trayo mūrtivihīnā vai piṇḍamūrtidharāsvime
|
bhavantu pitaro loke mayā sṛṣṭāḥ sanātanāḥ||20||

21pitā pitāmahaścaiva tathaiva prapitāmahaḥ
|
ahamevātra vijñeyastriṣu piṇḍeṣu saṃsthitaḥ||21||

22nāsti matto'dhikaḥ kaścitko vānyo'rcyo mayā svayam
|
ko vā mama pitā loke ahameva pitāmahaḥ||22||

23pitāmahapitā caiva ahamevātra kāraṇam
|
ityetaduktvā vacanaṃ devadevo vṛṣākapiḥ||23||

24varāhaparvate vipra datvā piṇḍānsavistarān
|
ātmānaṃ pūjayitvaiva tatraivādarśanaṃ gataḥ||24||

25eṣā tasya sthitirvipra pitaraḥ piṇḍasaṃjñitāḥ
|
labhante satataṃ pūjāṃ vṛṣākapivaco yathā||25||

26ye yajanti pitṝndevāngurūṃścaivātithīṃstathā
|
gāścaiva dvijamukhyāṃśca pṛthivīṃ mātaraṃ yathā||26||

27karmaṇā manasā vācā viṣṇumeva yajanti te
|
antargataḥ sa bhagavānsarvasattvaśarīragaḥ||27||

28samaḥ sarveṣu bhūteṣu īśvaraḥ sukhaduḥkhayoḥ
|
mahānmahātmā sarvātmā nārāyaṇa iti śrutiḥ||28||
