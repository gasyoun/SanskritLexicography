Mahābhārata 7.140 (Droṇaparvan)

1dhṛtarāṣṭra uvāca|
ahanyahani me dīptaṃ yaśaḥ patati sañjaya
|
hatā me bahavo yodhā manye kālasya paryayam ||1||

2dhanañjayaḥ susaṃkruddhaḥ praviṣṭo māmakaṃ balam
|
rakṣitaṃ drauṇikarṇābhyāmapraveśyaṃ surairapi ||2||

3tābhyāmūrjitavīryābhyāmāpyāyitaparākramaḥ
|
sahitaḥ kṛṣṇabhīmābhyāṃ śinīnāmṛṣabheṇa ca ||3||

4tadāprabhṛti māṃ śoko dahatyagnirivāśayam
|
grastāniva prapaśyāmi bhūmipālān sasaindhavān ||4||

5apriyaṃ sumahatkṛtvā sindhurājaḥ kirīṭinaḥ
|
cakṣurviṣayamāpannaḥ kathaṃ jīvitamāpnuyāt ||5||

6anumānācca paśyāmi nāsti sañjaya saindhavaḥ
|
yuddhaṃ tu tadyathāvṛttaṃ tanmamācakṣva tattvataḥ ||6||

7yacca vikṣobhya mahatīṃ senāmāloḍya cāsakṛt
|
ekaḥ praviṣṭaḥ saṃkruddho nalinīmiva kuñjaraḥ ||7||

8tasya me vṛṣṇivīrasya brūhi yuddhaṃ yathātatham
|
dhanañjayārthe yattasya kuśalo hāsi sañjaya ||8||

9sañjaya uvāca|
tathā tu vaikartanapīḍitaṃ taṃ bhīmaṃ prayāntaṃ puruṣapravīram
|
samīkṣya rājannaravīramadhye śinipravīro'nuyayau rathena ||9||

10nadanyathā vajradharastapānte jvalanyathā jaladānte ca sūryaḥ
|
nighnannamitrān dhanuṣā dṛḍhena sa kampayaṃstava putrasya senām ||10||

11taṃ yāntamaśvai rajataprakāśairāyodhane vīrataraṃ nadantam
|
nāśaknuvanvārayituṃ tvadīyāḥ sarve rathā bhārata mādhavāgryam ||11||

12amarṣapūrṇastvanivṛttayodhī śarāsanī kāñcanavarmadhārī
|
alambuṣaḥ sātyakiṃ mādhavāgryamavārayadrājavaro'bhipatya ||12||

13tayorabhūdbhārata saṃprahāro yathāvidho naiva babhūva kaścit
|
prekṣanta evāhavaśobhinau tau yodhātvadīyāśca pare ca sarve ||13||

14āvidhyadenaṃ daśabhiḥ pṛṣatkairalambuṣo rājavaraḥ prasahya
|
anāgatāneva tu tān pṛṣatkāṃściccheda bāṇaiḥ śinipuṅgavo'pi ||14||

15punaḥ sa bāṇaistribhiragnikalpairākarṇapūrṇairniśitaiḥ sapuṅkhaiḥ
|
vivyādha dehāvaraṇaṃ vidārya te sātyakerāviviśuḥ śarīram ||15||

16taiḥ kāyamasyāsyagnyanilaprabhāvairvidārya bāṇairniśitairjvaladbhiḥ
|
ājaghnivāṃstān rajataprakāśānaśvāṃścaturbhiścaturaḥ prasahya ||16||

17tathā tu tenābhihatastarasvī naptā śineścakradharaprabhāvaḥ
|
alambuṣasyottamavegavadbhiraśvāṃścaturbhirnijaghāna bāṇaiḥ ||17||

18athāsya sūtasya śiro nikṛtya bhallena kālānalasannibhena
|
sakuṇḍalaṃ pūrṇaśaśiprakāśaṃ bhrājiṣṇu vaktraṃ nicakarta dehāt ||18||

19nihatya taṃ pārthivaputrapautraṃ saṃkhye yadūnāmṛṣabhaḥ pramāthī
|
tato'nvayādarjunameva vīraḥ sainyāni rājaṃstava sannivārya ||19||

20anvāgataṃ vṛṣṇivīraṃ samīkṣya tathā'rimadhye parivartamānam
|
ghnantaṃ kurūṇāmiṣubhirbalāni punaḥ punarvāyumivābhrapūgān ||20||

21tato'vahansaindhavāḥ sādhu dāntā go-kṣīra-kundendu-himaprakāśāḥ
|
suvarṇajālāvatatāḥ sadaśvā yato yataḥ kāmayate nṛsiṃhaḥ ||21||

22athātmajāste sahitābhipeturanye ca yodhāstvaritāstvadīyāḥ
|
kṛtvā mukhaṃ bhārata yodhamukhyaṃ duḥśāsanaṃ tvatsutamājamīḍha ||22||

23te sarvataḥ saṃparivārya saṃkhye śaineyamājaghnuranīkasāhāḥ
|
sa cāpi tānpravaraḥ sātvatānāṃ nyavārayadbāṇajālena vīraḥ ||23||

24nivārya tāṃstūrṇamamitraghātī naptā śineḥ patribhiragnikalpaiḥ
|
duḥśāsanasyābhijaghāna vāhānudyamya bāṇāsanamājamīḍha ||24||

25tato'rjuno harṣamavāpa saṃkhye kṛṣṇaśca dṛṣṭvā puruṣapravīram ||25||
