Mahābhārata 5.71 (Udyogaparvan)

1dhṛtarāṣṭra uvāca|
cakṣuṣmatāṃ vai spṛhayāmi sañjaya drakṣyanti ye vāsudevaṃ samīpe
|
bibhrājamānaṃ vapuṣā pareṇa prakāśayantaṃ pradiśo diśaśca ||1||

2īrayantaṃ bhāratīṃ bhāratānāmabhyarcanīyāṃ śaṅkarīṃ sṛñjayānām
|
bubhūṣadbhirgrahaṇīyāmanindyāṃ parāsūnāmagrahaṇīyarūpām ||2||

3samudyantaṃ sātvatamekavīraṃ praṇetāramṛṣabhaṃ yādavānām
|
nihantāraṃ kṣobhaṇaṃ śātravāṇāṃ muñcantaṃ ca dviṣatāṃ vai yaśāṃsi ||3||

4draṣṭāro hi kuravastaṃ sametā mahātmānaṃ śatruhaṇaṃ vareṇyam
|
bruvantaṃ vācamanṛśaṃsarūpāṃ vṛṣṇiśreṣṭhaṃ mohayantaṃ madīyān ||4||

5ṛṣiṃ sanātanatamaṃ vipaścitaṃ vācaḥ samudraṃ kalaśaṃ yatīnām
|
ariṣṭanemiṃ garuḍaṃ suparṇaṃ hariṃ prajānāṃ bhuvanasya dhāma ||5||

6sahasraśīrṣaṃ puruṣaṃ purāṇamanādimadhyāntamanantakīrtim
|
śukrasya dhātāramajaṃ ca nityaṃ paraṃ pareṣāṃ śaraṇaṃ prapadye ||6||

7trailokyanirmāṇakaraṃ janitraṃ devāsurāṇāmatha nāgarakṣasām
|
narādhipānāṃ viduṣāṃ pradhānamindrānujaṃ taṃ śaraṇaṃ prapadye ||7||
