Mahābhārata 5.51 (Udyogaparvan)

1dhṛtarāṣṭra uvāca|
sarva ete mahotsāhā ye tvayā parikīrtitāḥ
|
ekatastveva te sarve sametā bhīma ekataḥ ||1||

2bhīmasenāddhi me bhūyo bhayaṃ sañjāyate mahat
|
kruddhādamarṣaṇāttāta vyāghrādiva mahāruroḥ ||2||

3jāgarmi rātrayaḥ sarvā dīrghamuṣṇaṃ ca niḥśvasan
|
bhīto vṛkodarāttāta siṃhāt paśurivāparaḥ ||3||

4na hi tasya mahābāhoḥ śakrapratimatejasaḥ
|
sainye'smin pratipaśyāmi ya enaṃ viṣahedyudhi ||4||

5amarṣaṇaśca kaunteyo dṛḍhavairaśca pāṇḍavaḥ
|
anarmahāsī sonmādastiryakprekṣī mahāsvanaḥ ||5||

6mahāvego mahotsāho mahābāhurmahābalaḥ
|
mandānāṃ mama putrāṇāṃ yuddhenāntaṃ kariṣyati ||6||

7urugrāhagṛhītānāṃ gadāṃ bibhradvṛkodaraḥ
|
kurūṇāmṛṣabho yuddhe daṇḍapāṇirivāntakaḥ ||7||

8aṣṭāsrimāyasīṃ ghorāṃ gadāṃ kāñcanabhūṣaṇām
|
manasā'haṃ prapaśyāmi brahmadaṇḍamivodyatam ||8||

9yathā mṛgāṇāṃ yūtheṣu siṃho jātabalaścaret
|
māmakeṣu tathā bhīmo baleṣu vicariṣyati ||9||

10sarveṣāṃ mama putrāṇāṃ sa ekaḥ krūravikramaḥ
|
bahvāśī vipratīpaśca bālye'pi rabhasaḥ sadā ||10||

11udvepate me hṛdayaṃ ye me duryodhanādayaḥ
|
bālye'pi tena yudhyanto vāraṇeneva marditāḥ ||11||

12tasya vīryeṇa saṅkliṣṭā nityameva sutā mama
|
sa eva heturbhedasya bhīmo bhīmaparākramaḥ ||12||

13grasamānamanīkāniṃ naravāraṇavājinām
|
paśyāmīvāgrato bhīmaṃ krodhamūrchitamāhave ||13||

14astre droṇārjunasamaṃ vāyuvegasamaṃ jave
|
maheśvarasamaṃ krodhe ko hanyādbhīmamāhave ||14||

15sañjayācakṣva me śūraṃ bhīmasenamamarṣaṇam
|
atilābhaṃ tu manye'haṃ yattena ripughātinā ||15||

16tadaiva na hatāḥ sarve putrā mama manasvinā
|
yena bhīmabalā yakṣā rākṣasāśca purā hatāḥ ||16||

17kathaṃ tasya raṇe vegaṃ mānuṣaḥ prasahiṣyati
|
na sa jātu vaśe tasthau mama bālye'pi sañjaya ||17||

18kiṃ punarmama duṣputraiḥ kliṣṭaḥ samprati pāṇḍavaḥ
|
niṣṭhuro roṣaṇo'tyarthaṃ bhajyetāpi na saṃnamet
|
tiryakprekṣī saṃhatabhrūḥ kathaṃ śāmyedvṛkodaraḥ ||18||

19śūrastathā'pratibalo gaurastāla ivonnataḥ
|
pramāṇato bhīmasenaḥ prādeśenādhiko'rjunāt ||19||

20javena vājino'tyeti balenātyeti kuñjarān
|
avyaktajalpī madhvakṣo madhyamaḥ pāṇḍavo balī ||20||

21iti bālye śrutaḥ pūrvaṃ mayā vyāsamukhāt purā
|
rūpato rvāryataścaiva yāthātathyena pāṇḍavaḥ ||21||

22āyasena sa daṇḍena rathānnāgānnarān hayān
|
haniṣyati raṇe kruddho raudraḥ krūraparākramaḥ ||22||

23amarṣī nityasaṃrabdho bhīmaḥ praharatāṃ varaḥ
|
mayā tāta pratīpāni kurvan pūrvaṃ vimānitaḥ ||23||

24niṣkarṇāmāyasīṃ sthūlāṃ supārśvāṃ kāñcanīṃ gadām
|
śataghnīṃ śatanirhrādāṃ kathaṃ śakṣyanti me sutāḥ ||24||

25apāramaplavāgādhaṃ samudraṃ śaravedhanam
|
bhīmasenamayaṃ durgaṃ tāta mandāstitīrṣavaḥ ||25||

26krośato me na śṛṇvanti bālāḥ paṇḍitamāninaḥ
|
viṣamaṃ na hi manyante prapātaṃ madhudarśinaḥ ||26||

27saṃyugaṃ ye gamiṣyanti nararūpeṇa mṛtyunā
|
niyataṃ coditā dhātrā siṃheneva mahāmṛgāḥ ||27||

28śaikyāṃ tāta catuṣkiṣkuṃ ṣaḍasrimamitaujasam
|
prahitāṃ duḥkhasaṃsparśāṃ kathaṃ śakṣyanti me sutāḥ ||28||

29gadāṃ bhrāmayatastasya bhindato hastimastakān
|
sṛkkiṇī lelihānasya bāṣpamutsṛjato muhuḥ ||29||

30uddiśya nāgān patataḥ kurvato bhairavānravān
|
pratīpaṃ patato mattān kuñjarān pratigarjataḥ ||30||

31vigāhya rathamārgeṣu varānuddiśya nighnataḥ
|
agneḥ prajvalitasyeva api mucyeta me prajā ||31||

32vīthīṃ kurvanmahābāhurdrāvayanmama vāhinīm
|
nṛtyanniva gadāpāṇiryugāntaṃ darśayiṣyati ||32||

33prabhinna iva mātaṅgaḥ prabhañjan puṣpitān drumān
|
pravekṣyati raṇe senāṃ putrāṇāṃ me vṛkodaraḥ ||33||

34kurvan rathān vipuruṣān visārathihayadhvajān
|
ārujan puruṣavyāghro rathinaḥ sādinastathā ||34||

35gaṅgāvega ivānūpāṃstīrajān vividhān drumān
|
prabhaṅkṣyati raṇe senāṃ putrāṇāṃ mama sañjaya ||35||

36diśo nūnaṃ gamiṣyanti bhīmasenabhayārditāḥ
|
mama putrāśca bhṛtyāśca rājānaścaiva sañjaya ||36||

37yena rājā mahāvīryaḥ praviśyāntaḥpuraṃ purā
|
vāsudevasahāyena jarāsandho nipātitaḥ ||37||

38kṛtsneyaṃ pṛthivī devī jarāsandhena dhīmatā
|
māgadhendreṇa balinā vaśe kṛtvā pratāpitā ||38||

39bhīṣmapratāpāt kuravo nayenāndhakavṛṣṇayaḥ
|
yanna tasya vaśe jagmuḥ kevalaṃ daivameva tat ||39||

40sa gatvā pāṇḍuputreṇa tarasā bāhuśālinā
|
anāyudhena vīreṇa nihataḥ kiṃ tato'dhikam ||40||

41dīrghakālasamāsaktaṃ viṣamāśīviṣo yathā
|
sa mokṣyati raṇe tejaḥ putreṣu mama sañjaya ||41||

42mahendra iva vajreṇa dānavān devasattamaḥ
|
bhīmaseno gadāpāṇiḥ sūdayiṣyati me sutān ||42||

43aviṣahyamanāvāryaṃ tīvravegaparākramam
|
paśyāmīvātitāmrākṣamāpatantaṃ vṛkodaram ||43||

44agadasyāpyadhanuṣo virathasya vivarmaṇaḥ
|
bāhubhyāṃ yuddhyamānasya kastiṣṭhedagrataḥ pumān ||44||

45bhīṣmo droṇaśca vipro'yaṃ kṛpaḥ śāradvatastathā
|
jānantyete yathaivāhaṃ vīryajñastasya dhīmataḥ ||45||

46āryavrataṃ tu jānantaḥ saṅgarāntaṃ vidhitsavaḥ
|
senāmukheṣu sthāsyanti māmakānāṃ nararṣabhāḥ ||46||

47balīyaḥ sarvato diṣṭaṃ puruṣasya viśeṣataḥ
|
paśyannapi jayaṃ teṣāṃ na niyacchāmi yatsutān ||47||

48te purāṇaṃ maheṣvāsā mārgamaindraṃ samāsthitāḥ
|
tyakṣyanti tumule prāṇān rakṣantaḥ pārthivaṃ yaśaḥ ||48||

49yathaiṣāṃ māmakāstāta tathaiṣāṃ pāṇḍavā api
|
pautrā bhīṣmasya śiṣyāśca droṇasya ca kṛpasya ca ||49||

50ye tvasmadāśrayaṃ kiñciddattamiṣṭaṃ ca sañjaya
|
tasyāpacitimāryatvāt kartāraḥ sthavirāstrayaḥ ||50||

51ādadānasya śastraṃ hi kṣatradharmaṃ parīpsataḥ
|
nidhanaṃ kṣatriyasyājau varamevāhuruttamam ||51||

52sa vai śocāmi sarvān vai ye yuyutsanti pāṇḍavaiḥ
|
vikruṣṭaṃ vidureṇādau tadetadbhayamāgatam ||52||

53na tu manye vighātāya jñānaṃ duḥkhasya sañjaya
|
bhavatyatibalaṃ hyetajjñānasyāpyupaghātakam ||53||

54ṛṣayo hyapi nirmuktāḥ paśyanto lokasaṅgrahān
|
sukhairbhavanti sukhinastathā duḥkhena duḥkhitāḥ ||54||

55kiṃ punarmohamāsaktastatra tatra sahasradhā
|
putreṣu rājyadāreṣu pautreṣvapi ca bandhuṣu ||55||

56saṃśaye tu mahatyasmin kiṃ nu me kṣamamuttaram
|
vināśaṃ hyeva paśyāmi kurūṇāmanucintayan ||56||

57dyūtapramukhamābhāti kurūṇāṃ vyasanaṃ mahat
|
mandenaiśvaryakāmena lobhāt pāpamidaṃ kṛtam ||57||

58manye paryāyadharmo'yaṃ kālasyātyantagāminaḥ
|
cakre pradhirivāsakto nāsya śakyaṃ palāyitum ||58||

59kinnu kuryāṃ kathaṃ kuryāṃ kva nu gacchāmi sañjaya
|
ete naśyanti kuravo mandāḥ kālavaśaṃ gatāḥ ||59||

60avaśo'haṃ tadā tāta putrāṇāṃ nihate śate
|
śroṣyāmi ninadaṃ strīṇāṃ kathaṃ māṃ maraṇaṃ spṛśet ||60||

61yathā nidāghe jvalanaḥ samiddho dahet kakṣaṃ vāyunā codyamānaḥ
|
gadāhastaḥ pāṇḍavo vai tathaiva hantā madīyān sahito'rjunena ||61||
