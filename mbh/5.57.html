Mahābhārata 5.57 (Udyogaparvan)

1dhṛtarāṣṭra uvāca|
kāṃstatra sañjayāpaśyaḥ prītyarthena samāgatān
|
ye yotsyante pāṇḍavārthe putrasya mama vāhinīm ||1||

2sañjaya uvāca|
mukhyamandhakavṛṣṇīnāmapaśyaṃ kṛṣṇamāgatam
|
cekitānaṃ ca tatraiva yuyudhānaṃ ca sātyakim ||2||

3pṛthagakṣauhiṇībhyāṃ tu pāṇḍavānabhisaṃśritau
|
mahārathau samākhyātāvubhau puruṣamāninau ||3||

4akṣauhiṇyā'tha pāñcālyo daśabhistanayairvṛtaḥ
|
satyajitpramukhairvīrairdhṛṣṭadyumnapurogamaiḥ ||4||

5drupado vardhayan mānaṃ śikhaṇḍiparipālitaḥ
|
upāyātsarvasainyānāṃ praticchādya tadā vapuḥ ||5||

6virāṭaḥ saha putrābhyāṃ śaṅkhenaivottareṇa ca
|
sūryadattādibhirvīrairmadirākṣapurogamaiḥ ||6||

7sahitaḥ pṛthivīpālo bhrātṛbhistanayaistathā
|
akṣauhiṇyaiva sainyānāṃ vṛtaḥ pārthaṃ samāśritaḥ ||7||

8jārāsandhirmāgadhaśca dhṛṣṭaketuśca cedirāṭ
|
pṛthak pṛthaganuprāptau pṛthagakṣauhiṇīvṛttau ||8||

9kekayā bhrātaraḥ pañca sarve lohitakadhvajāḥ
|
akṣauhiṇīparivṛtāḥ pāṇḍavānabhisaṃśritāḥ ||9||

10etānetāvatastatra tānapaśyaṃ samāgatān
|
ye pāṇḍavārthe yotsyanti dhārtarāṣṭrasya vāhinīm ||10||

11yo veda mānuṣaṃ vyūhaṃ daivaṃ gāndharvamāsuram
|
sa tatra senāpramukhe dhṛṣṭadyunno mahārathaḥ ||11||

12bhīṣmaḥ śāṃtanavo rājan‌ bhāgaḥ kluptaḥ śikhaṇḍinaḥ
|
taṃ virāṭo'nusaṃyātā sārdhaṃ matsyaiḥ prahāribhiḥ ||12||

13jyeṣṭhasya pāṇḍuputrasya bhāgo madrādhipo balī
|
tau tu tatrābruvan kecidviṣamau no matāviti ||13||

14duryodhanaḥ sahasutaḥ sārdhaṃ bhrātṛśatena ca
|
prācyāśca dākṣiṇātyāśca bhīmasenasya bhāgataḥ ||14||

15arjunasya tu bhāgena karṇo vaikartano mataḥ
|
aśvatthāmā vikarṇaśca saindhavaśca jayadrathaḥ ||15||

16aśakyāścaiva ye kecit pṛthivyāṃ śūramāninaḥ
|
sarvāṃstānarjunaḥ pārthaḥ kalpayāmāsa bhāgataḥ ||16||

17maheṣvāsā rājaputrā bhrātaraḥ pañca kekayāḥ
|
kekayāneva bhāgena kṛtvā yotsyanti saṃyuge ||17||

18teṣāmeva kṛto bhāgo mālavāḥ śālvakāstathā
|
trigartānāṃ caiva mukhyau yau tau saṃśaptakāviti ||18||

19duryodhanasutāḥ sarve tathā duḥśāsanasya ca
|
saubhadreṇa kṛto bhāgo rājā caiva bṛhadvalaḥ ||19||

20draupadeyā maheṣvāsāḥ suvarṇavikṛtadhvajāḥ
|
dhṛṣṭadyumnamukhā droṇamabhiyāsyanti bhārata ||20||

21cekitānaḥ somadattaṃ dvairathe yoddhumicchati
|
bhojaṃ tu kṛtavarmāṇaṃ yuyudhāno yuyutsati ||21||

22sahadevastu mādreyaḥ śūraḥ saṅkranda‌no yudhi
|
svamaṃśaṃ kalpayāmāsa śyālaṃ te subalātmajam ||22||

23ulūkaṃ caiva kaitavyaṃ ye ca sārasvatā gaṇāḥ
|
nakulaḥ kalpayāmāsa bhāgaṃ mādravatīsutaḥ ||23||

24ye cānye pārthivā rājan pratyudyāsyanti saṅgare
|
samāhvānena tāṃścāpi pāṇḍuputrā akalpayan ||24||

25evameṣāmanīkāni pravibhaktāni bhāgaśaḥ
|
yatte kāryaṃ saputrasya kriyatāṃ tadakālikam ||25||

26dhṛtarāṣṭra uvāca|
na santi sarve putrā me mūḍhā durdyūtadevinaḥ
|
yeṣāṃ yuddhaṃ balavatā bhamina raṇamūrdhani ||26||

27rājānaḥ pārthivāḥ sarve prokṣitāḥ kāladharmaṇā
|
gāṇḍīvāgniṃ pravekṣyanti pataṅgā iva pāvakam ||27||

28vidrutāṃ vāhinīṃ manye kṛtavairairmahātmabhiḥ
|
tāṃ raṇe ke'nuyāsyanti prabhagnāṃ pāṇḍavairyudhi ||28||

29sarve hyatirathāḥ śūrāḥ kīrtimantaḥ pratāpinaḥ
|
sūryapāvakayostulyāstejasā samitiñjayāḥ ||29||

30yeṣāṃ yudhiṣṭhiro netā goptā ca madhusūdanaḥ
|
yodhau ca pāṇḍavau vīrau savyasācivṛkodarau ||30||

31nakulaḥ sahadevaśca dhṛṣṭadyumnaśca pārṣataḥ
|
sātyakirdrupada‌ścaiva dhṛṣṭaketuśca sānujaḥ ||31||

32uttamaujāśca pāñcālyo yudhāmanyuśca durjayaḥ
|
śikhaṇḍī kṣatradevaśca tathā vairāṭiruttaraḥ ||32||

33kāśayaścedayaścaiva matsyāḥ sarve ca sṛñjayāḥ
|
virāṭaputro babhruśca pāñcālāśca prabhadrakāḥ ||33||

34yeṣāmindro'pyakāmānāṃ na haret pṛthivīmimām
|
vīrāṇāṃ raṇadhīrāṇāṃ ye bhindyuḥ parvatānapi ||34||

35tān sarvaguṇasampannānamanuṣyapratāpinaḥ
|
krośato mama duṣputro yoddhumicchati sañjaya ||35||

36duryodhana uvāca|
ubhau sva ekajātīyau tathobhau bhūmigocarau
|
atha kasmāt pāṇḍavānāmekato manyase jayam ||36||

37pitāmahaṃ ca droṇaṃ ca kṛpaṃ karṇaṃ ca durjayam
|
jayadrathaṃ somadattamaśvatthāmānameva ca ||37||

38sutejaso maheṣvāsānindro'pi sahito'maraiḥ
|
aśaktaḥ samare jetuṃ kiṃ punastāta pāṇḍavāḥ ||38||

39sarve ca pṛthivīpālā madarthe tāta pāṇḍavān
|
āryāḥ śastrabhṛtaḥ śūrāḥ samarthāḥ pratibādhitum ||39||

40na māmakān pāṇḍavāste samarthāḥ prativīkṣitum
|
parākrānto hyahaṃ pāṇḍūn saputrān yoddhumohave ||40||

41matpriyaṃ pārthivāḥ sarve ye cikīrṣanti bhārata
|
te tānāvārayiṣyanti aiṇeyāniva tantunā ||41||

42mahatā rathavaṃśena śarajālaiśca māmakaiḥ
|
abhidrutā bhaviṣyanti pāñcālāḥ pāṇḍavaiḥ saha ||42||

43dhṛtarāṣṭra uvāca|
unmatta iva me putro vilapatyeṣa sañjaya
|
na hi śakto raṇe jetuṃ dharmarājaṃ yudhiṣṭhiram ||43||

44jānāti hi yathā bhīṣmaḥ pāṇḍavānāṃ yaśasvinām
|
balavattāṃ saputrāṇāṃ dharmajñānāṃ mahātmanām ||44||

45yato nārocayadayaṃ vigrahaṃ tairmahātmabhiḥ
|
kiṃ tu sañjaya me brūhi punasteṣāṃ viceṣṭitam ||45||

46kastāṃstarasvino bhūyaḥ sandīpayati pāṇḍavān
|
arciṣmato maheṣvāsān haviṣā pāvakāniva ||46||

47sañjaya uvāca|
dhṛṣṭadyumnaḥ sadaivaitān sandīpayati bhārata
|
yuddhyadhvamiti mā bhaiṣṭa yuddhādbharatasattamāḥ ||47||

48ye kecitpārthivāstatra dhārtarāṣṭreṇa saṃvṛtāḥ
|
yuddhe samāgamiṣyanti tumale śastrasaṅkule ||48||

49tān sarvānāhave kruddhān sānubandhān samāgatān
|
ahamekaḥ samādāsye timirmatsyānivodakāt ||49||

50bhīṣmaṃ droṇaṃ kṛpaṃ karṇaṃ drauṇiṃ śalyaṃ suyodhanam
|
etāṃścāpi nirotsyāmi veleva makarālayam ||50||

51tathā bruvantaṃ dharmātmā prāha rājā yudhiṣṭhiraḥ
|
tava dhairyaṃ ca vīryaṃ ca pāñcālāḥ pāṇḍavaiḥ saha ||51||

52sarve samadhirūḍhāḥ sma sāṅgrāmānnaḥ samuddhara
|
jānāmi tvāṃ mahābāho kṣetradharme vyavasthitam ||52||

53samarthamekaṃ paryāptaṃ kauravāṇāṃ vinigrahe
|
purastādupayātānāṃ kauravāṇāṃ yuyutsatām ||53||

54bhavatā yadvidhātavyaṃ tannaḥ śreyaḥ parantapa
|
saṅgrāmādapayātānāṃ bhagnānāṃ śaraṇaiṣiṇām ||54||

55pauruṣaṃ darśayan śūro yastiṣṭhedagrataḥ pumān
|
krīṇīyāttaṃ sahastreṇa iti nītimatāṃ matam ||55||

56sa tvaṃ śūraśca vīraśca vikrāntaśca nararṣabha
|
bhayārtānāṃ paritrātā saṃyugeṣu na saṃśayaḥ ||56||

57evaṃ bruvati kaunteye dharmātmani yudhiṣṭhire
|
dhṛṣṭadyumna uvācedaṃ māṃ vaco gatasādhvasam
|
sarvān janapadān sūta yodhā duryodhanasya ye ||57||

58sabāhlikān kurūn brūyāḥ prātipeyān śaradvataḥ
|
sūtaputraṃ tathā droṇaṃ sahaputraṃ jayadratham ||58||

59duḥśāsanaṃ vikarṇaṃ ca tathā duryodhanaṃ nṛpam
|
bhīṣmaṃ ca brūhi gatvā tvamāśu gaccha ca mā ciram ||59||

60yudhiṣṭhiraḥ sādhu naivābhyupeyo mā vo'vadhīdarjuno devaguptaḥ
|
rājyaṃ daddhvaṃ dharmarājasya tūrṇaṃ yācadhvaṃ vai pāṇḍavaṃ lokavīram ||60||

61naitādṛśo hi yodho'sti pṛthivyāmiha kaścana
|
yathāvidhaḥ savyasācī pāṇḍavaḥ satyavikramaḥ ||61||

62devairhi saṃbhṛto divyo ratho gāṇḍīvadhanvanaḥ
|
na sa jeyo manuṣyeṇa mā sma kṛddhvaṃ mano yudhi ||62||
