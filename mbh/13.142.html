Mahābhārata 13.142 (Anuśāsanaparvan)

1umovāca
|
deśeṣu ramaṇīyeṣu nadīnāṃ nirjhareṣu ca
|
sravantīnāṃ nikuñjeṣu parvateṣu vaneṣu ca ||1||

2deśeṣu ca pavitreṣu phalavatsu samāhitāḥ
|
mūlavatsu ca medhyeṣu vasanti niyatavratāḥ ||2||

3teṣāmapi vidhiṃ puṇyaṃ śrotumicchāmi śaṅkara
|
vānaprastheṣu deveśa svaśarīropajīviṣu ||3||

4maheśvara uvāca|
vānaprastheṣu yo dharmastaṃ me śṛṇu samāhitā
|
śrutvā caikamanā devi dharmabuddhiparā bhava ||4||

5saṃsiddhairniyamaiḥ sadbhirvanavāsamupāgataiḥ
|
vānaprasthairidaṃ karma kartavyaṃ śṛṇu yādṛśam ||5||

6trikālamabhiṣekaṃ ca pitṛdevārcanaṃ tathā
|
agnihotraparisyanda iṣṭihomavidhistathā ||6||

7nīvāragrahaṇaṃ caiva phalamūlaniṣevaṇam
|
iṅgudairaṇḍatailānāṃ snehārthe ca niṣevaṇam ||7||

8yogacaryākṛtaiḥ siddhaiḥ kāmakrodhavivarjitaiḥ
|
vīraśayyāmupāsadbhirvīrasthānopasevibhiḥ ||8||

9yuktairyogavahaiḥ sadbhirgrīṣme pañcatapaistathā
|
maṇḍūkayoganiyatairyathānyāyaṃ niṣevibhiḥ ||9||

10vīrāsanaratairnityaṃ sthaṇḍile śayanaṃ tathā
|
śītatoyāgniyogaśca cartavyo dharmabuddhibhiḥ ||10||

11abbhakṣairvāyubhakṣaiśca śaivalottarabhojanaiḥ
|
aśmakuṭṭaistathā dāṃtaiḥ saṃprakṣālaistathāparaiḥ ||11||

12cīravalkalasaṃvītairmṛgacarmānivāsibhiḥ
|
kāryā yātrā yathākālaṃ yathādharmaṃ yathāvidhi ||12||

13vananityairvanacarairvanasthairvanagocaraiḥ
|
vanaṃ gurumivāsādya vastavyaṃ vanajīvibhiḥ ||13||

14teṣāṃ homakriyā dharmaḥ pañcayajñaniṣevaṇam
|
bhāgaṃ ca pañcayajñasya vedoktasyānupālanam ||14||

15aṣṭamī yajñaparatā cāturmāsyaniṣevaṇam
|
paurṇamāsā‌dayo yajñā nityayajñastathaiva ca ||15||

16vimuktā dārasaṃyogairvimuktāḥ sarvasaṅkaraiḥ
|
vimuktāḥ sarvapāpaiśca caranti munayo vane ||16||

17srugbhāṇḍaparamā nityaṃ tretāgniśaraṇāḥ sadā
|
santaḥ satpathanityā ye te yānti paramāṃ gatim ||17||

18brahmalokaṃ mahāpuṇyaṃ somalokaṃ ca śāśvatam
|
gacchanti munayaḥ siddhāḥ satyadharmavyapāśrayāḥ ||18||

19eṣa dharmo mayā devi vānaprasthāśritaḥ śubhaḥ
|
vistareṇātha saṃpanno yathāsthūlamudāhṛtaḥ ||19||

20umovāca
|
bhagavansarvabhūteśa sarvabhūtanamaskṛta
|
yo dharmo munisaṅgasya siddhivādeṣu taṃ vada ||20||

21siddhivādeṣu saṃsiddhāstathā vananivāsinaḥ
|
svairiṇo dārasaṃyuktāsteṣāṃ dharmaḥ kathaṃ smṛtaḥ ||21||

22maheśvara uvāca|
svairiṇastapasā devi sarve dāravihāriṇaḥ
|
teṣāṃ mauṇḍyaṃ kaṣāyaśca vāse rātriśca kāraṇam ||22||

23trikālamabhiṣekaśca hotraṃ tvṛṣikṛtaṃ mahat
|
samādhisatpathasthānaṃ yathoddiṣṭaniṣevaṇam ||23||

24ye ca te pūrvakathitā dharmāste vanavāsinām
|
yadi sevanti dharmāṃstānāpnuvanti tapaḥphalam ||24||

25ye ca dampatidharmāṇaḥ svadāraniyatendriyāḥ
|
caranti vidhidṛṣṭaṃ tada‌nukālābhigāminaḥ ||25||

26teṣāmṛṣikṛto dharmo dharmiṇāmupapadyate
|
na kāmakārātkāmonyaḥ saṃsevyo dharmadarśibhiḥ ||26||

27sarvabhūteṣu yaḥ samyagdadātyabhayadakṣiṇām
|
hiṃsādoṣavimuktā''tmā sa vai dharmeṇa yujyate ||27||

28sarvabhūtānukampī yaḥ sarvabhūtārjavavrataḥ
|
sarvabhūtātmabhūtaśca sa vai dharmeṇa yujyate ||28||

29sarvavedeṣu vā snānaṃ sarvabhūteṣu cārjavam
|
ubhe ete same syātāmārjavaṃ vā viśiṣyate ||29||

30ārjavaṃ dharmamityāhuradharmo jihma ucyate
|
ārjaveneha saṃyukto naro dharmeṇa yujyate ||30||

31ārjave tu rato nityaṃ vasatyamarasannidhau
|
tasmādārjavayuktaḥ syādya iccheddharmamātmanaḥ ||31||

32kṣānto dānto jitakrodho dharmabhūto vihiṃsakaḥ
|
dharme ratamanā nityaṃ naro dharmeṇa yujyate ||32||

33vyapetatandrirdharmātmā śaktyā satpathamāśritaḥ
|
cāritraparamo buddho brahmabhūyāya kalpate ||33||

34umovāca
|
āśramābhiratā deva tāpasā ye tapodhanāḥ
|
dīptimantaḥ kayā caiva caryayā'tha bhavanti te ||34||

35rājāno rājaputrāśca nirdhanā ye mahādhanāḥ
|
karmaṇā kena bhagavanprāpnuvanti mahāphalam ||35||

36nityaṃ sthānamupāgamya divyacandanabhūṣitāḥ
|
kena vā karmaṇā deva bhavanti vanagocarāḥ ||36||

37etanme saṃśayaṃ deva tapaścaryā''śritaṃ śubham
|
śaṃsa sarvamaśeṣeṇa tryakṣa tripuranāśana ||37||

38maheśvara uvāca|
upavāsavratairdāntā hyahiṃsrāḥ satyavādinaḥ
|
saṃsiddhāḥ pretya gandharvaiḥ saha modantyanāmayāḥ ||38||

39maṇḍūkayogaśayano yathānyāyaṃ yathāvidhi
|
dīkṣāṃ carati dharmātmā sa nāgaiḥ saha modate ||39||

40śaṣpaṃ mṛgamukhocchiṣṭaṃ yo mṛgaiḥ saha bhakṣati
|
dīkṣito vai mudā yuktaḥ sa gacchatyamarāvatīm ||40||

41śaivālaṃ śīrṇaparṇaṃ vā tadvratī yo niṣevate
|
śītayogavaho nityaṃ sa gacchetparamāṃ gatim ||41||

42vāyubhakṣo'mbubhakṣo vā phalamūlāśanopi vā
|
yakṣeṣvaiśvaryamādhāya modate'psarasāṃ gaṇaiḥ ||42||

43agniyogavaho grīṣme vidhidṛṣṭena karmaṇā
|
cīrtvā dvādaśavarṣāṇi rājā bhavati pārthivaḥ ||43||

44āhāraniyamaṃ kṛtvā munirdvādaśavārṣikam
|
maruṃ saṃsādhya yatnena rājā bhavati pārthivaḥ ||44||

45sthaṇḍile śuddhamākāśaṃ parigṛhya samantataḥ
|
praviśya ca mudā yukto dīkṣāṃ dvādaśavārṣikīm ||45||

46dehaṃ cānaśane tyaktvā sa svarge sukhamedhate
|
sthaṇḍilasya phalānyāhuryānāni śayanāni ca ||46||

47gṛhāṇi ca mahārhāṇi candraśubhrāṇi bhāmini
|
ātmānamupajīvanyo niyato niyatāśanaḥ ||47||

48dehaṃ vā'naśane tyaktyā sa svargaṃ samupāśnute
|
ātmānamupajīvanyo dīkṣāṃ dvādaśavārṣikīm ||48||

49tyaktvā mahārṇave dehaṃ vāruṇaṃ lokamaśnute
|
ātmānamupajīvanyo dīkṣāṃ dvādaśavārṣikīm ||49||

50aśmanā caraṇau bhittvā guhyakeṣu sa modate
|
sādhayitvā''tmanā''tmānaṃ nirdvandvo niṣparigrahaḥ ||50||

51cīrtvā dvādaśavarṣāṇi dīkṣāmetāṃ manogatām
|
svargalokamavāpnoti devaiśca saha modate ||51||

52ātmānamupajīvanyo dīkṣāṃdvādaśavārṣikīm
|
hutvā'gnau dehamutsṛjya vahniloke mahīyate ||52||

53yastu devi yathānyāyaṃ dīkṣito niyato dvijaḥ
|
ātmanyātmānamādhāya nirmamo dharmalālasaḥ ||53||

54cīrtvā dvādaśavarṣāṇi dīkṣāmetāṃ manogatām
|
araṇīsahitaṃ skandhe vadhvā gacchatyanāvṛtaḥ ||54||

55vīrādhyānagato nityaṃ vīrāsanaratastathā
|
vīrasthāyī ca satataṃ sa vīragatimāpnuyāt ||55||

56sa śakralokago nityaṃ sarvakāmapuraskṛtaḥ
|
divyapuṣpasamākīrṇo divyacandanabhūṣitaḥ ||56||

57sukhaṃ vasati dharmātmā divi devagaṇaiḥ saha
|
vīralokagato nityaṃ vīrayogasahaḥ sadā ||57||

58sattvasthaḥ sarvamutsṛjya dīkṣito niyataḥ śuciḥ
|
vīrādhvānaṃ prapadyedyastasya lokāḥ sanātanāḥ ||58||

59kāmagena vimānena sa vai carati cchandataḥ
|
śakralokagataḥ śrīmānmodate ca nirāmayaḥ ||59||
