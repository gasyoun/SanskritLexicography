Mahābhārata 13.96 (Anuśāsanaparvan)

1yudhiṣṭhira uvāca|
evaṃ prayācati tadā bhāskare munisattamaḥ
|
jamadagnirmahātejāḥ kiṃ kāryaṃ pratyapadyata ||1||

2bhīṣma uvāca|
sa tathā yācamānasya muniragnisamaprabhaḥ
|
jamadagniḥ śamaṃ naiva jagāma kurunandana ||2||

3tataḥ sūryo madhurayā vācā tamidamabravīt
|
kṛtāñjalirviprarūpī praṇamyainaṃ viśāmpate ||3||

4calaṃ nimittaṃ viprarṣe sadā sūryasya gacchataḥ
|
kathaṃ calaṃ bhetsyasi tvaṃ sadā yāntaṃ divākaram ||4||

5jamada‌gniruvāca|
sthiraṃ cāpi calaṃ cāpi jāne tvāṃ jñānacakṣuṣā
|
avaśyaṃ vinayādhānaṃ kāryamadya mayā tava ||5||

6madhyāhne vai nimeṣārdhaṃ tiṣṭhasi tvaṃ divākara
|
tatra bhetsyāmi sūrya tvāṃ na me'trāsti vicāraṇā ||6||

7sūrya uvāca|
asaṃśayaṃ māṃ viprarṣe bhetsyase dhanvināṃ vara
|
apakāriṇaṃ māṃ viddhi bhagavañcharaṇāgatam ||7||

8bhīṣma uvāca|
tataḥ prahasya bhagavān jamada‌gniruvāca tam
|
na bhīḥ sūrya tvayā kāryā prāṇapātagato hyasi ||8||

9brāhmaṇeṣvārjavaṃ yaśca sthairyaṃ ca dharaṇītale
|
saumyatāṃ caiva somasya gāṃbhīryaṃ varuṇasya ca ||9||

10dīptimagneḥ prabhāṃ meroḥ pratāpaṃ tapanasya ca
|
etānyatikramedyo vai sa hanyāccharaṇāgatam ||10||

11bhavetsa gurutalpī ca brahmahā ca sa vai bhavet
|
surāpānaṃ sa kuryācca yo hanyāccharaṇāgatam ||11||

12etasya tvapanītasya samādhiṃ tāta cintaya
|
yathā sukhagamaḥ panthā bhavettvadraśmibhāvitaḥ ||12||

13bhīṣma uvāca|
etāvaduktvā sa tadā tūṣṇīmāsīdbhṛgūttamaḥ
|
atha sūryo'dadattasmai chatropānahamāśu vai ||13||

14sūrya uvāca|
maharṣe śirasastrāṇaṃ chatraṃ madraśmivāraṇam
|
pratigṛhṇīṣva padbhyāṃ ca trāṇārthaṃ carmapāduke ||14||

15adyaprabhṛti caiveha loke saṃpracariṣyati
|
puṇyakeṣu ca sarveṣu paramakṣayyameva ca ||15||

16bhīṣma uvāca|
chatropānahametattu sūryeṇaitatpravartitam
|
puṇyametada‌bhikhyātaṃ triṣu lokeṣu bhārata ||16||

17tasmātprayaccha vipreṣu chatropānahasuttamam
|
dharmasteṣu mahānbhāvī na me'trāsti vicāraṇā ||17||

18chatraṃ hi bharataśreṣṭha yaḥ pradadyāddvijātaye
|
śubhraṃ śataśalākaṃ vai sa pretya sukhamedhate ||18||

19sa śakraloke vasati pūjyamāno dvijātibhiḥ
|
apsarobhiśca satataṃ devaiśca bharatarṣabha ||19||

20dahyamānāya viprāya yaḥ prayacchatyupānahau
|
snātakāya mahābāho saṃśitāya dvijātaye ||20||

21so'pi lokānavāpnoti daivatairabhipūjitān
|
goloke sa mudā yukto vasati pretya bhārata ||21||

22etatte bharataśreṣṭha mayā kārtsnyena kīrtitam
|
chatropānahadānasya phalaṃ bharatasattama ||22||
