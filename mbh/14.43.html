Mahābhārata 14.43 (Āśvamedhikaparvan)

1brahmovāca|
manuṣyāṇāṃ tu rājanyaḥ kṣatriyo madhyamo guṇaḥ
|
kuñjaro vāhanānāṃ ca siṃhaścāraṇyavāsinām||1||

2aviḥ paśūnāṃ sarveṣāmahistu bilavāsinām
|
gavāṃ govṛṣabhaścaiva strīṇāṃ puruṣa eva ca||2||

3nyagrodhe jambuvṛkṣaśca pippalaḥ śālmalistathā
|
śiṃśapā meṣaśṛṅgaśca tathā kīcakaveṇavaḥ||3||

4ete drumāṇāṃ rājāno loke'smin nātra saṃśayaḥ
|
himavānpāriyātraśca sahyo vindhyastrikūṭavān||4||

5śveto nīlaśca bhāsaśca koṣṭhavāṃścaiva parvataḥ
|
guruskandho mahendraśca mālyavānparvatastathā||5||

6ete parvatarājāno gaṇānāṃ marutastathā
|
sūryo grahāṇāmadhipo nakṣatrāṇāṃ ca candramāḥ||6||

7yamaḥ pitṝṇāmadhipaḥ saritāmatha sāgaraḥ
|
ambhasāṃ varuṇo rājā marutāmindra ucyate||7||

8arko'dhipatiruṣṇānāṃ jyotiṣāmindurucyate
|
agnirbhūtapatirnityaṃ brāhmaṇānāṃ bṛhaspatiḥ||8||

9oṣadhīnāṃ patiḥ somo viṣṇurbalavatāṃ varaḥ
|
tvaṣṭādhirājo rūpāṇāṃ paśūnāmīśvaraḥ śivaḥ||9||

10dīkṣitānāṃ tathā yajño devānāṃ maghavā tathā
|
diśāmudīcī viprāṇāṃ somo rājā pratāpavān||10||

11kuberaḥ sarvaratnānāṃ devatānāṃ purandaraḥ
|
eṣa bhūtādhipaḥ sargaḥ prajānāṃ ca prajāpatiḥ||11||

12sarveṣāmeva bhūtānāmahaṃ brahmamayo mahān
|
bhūtaṃ parataraṃ matto viṣṇorvā'pi na vidyate||12||

13rājādhirājaḥ sarveṣāṃ viṣṇurbrahmamayo mahān
|
īśvaratvaṃ vijānīdhvaṃ kartāramakṛtaṃ harim||13||

14narakinnarayakṣāṇāṃ gandharvoragarakṣasām
|
devadānavanāgānāṃ sarveṣāmīśvaro hi saḥ||14||

15bhagadevānuyātānāṃ sarvāsāṃ vāmalocanā
|
māheśvarī mahādevī procyate pārvatī hi sā||15||

16umāṃ devīṃ vijānīdhvaṃ nārīṇāmuttamāṃ śubhām
|
ratīnāṃ vasumatyastu strīṇāmapsarasastathā||16||

17dharmakāmāśca rājāno brāhmaṇā dharmasetavaḥ
|
tasmādrājā dvijātīnāṃ prayateta sma rakṣaṇe||17||

18rājñāṃ hi viṣaye yeṣāmavasīdanti sādhavaḥ
|
hīnāste svaguṇaiḥ sarvaiḥ pretya conmārgagāminaḥ||18||

19rājñāṃ hi viṣaye yeṣāṃ sādhavaḥ parirakṣitāḥ
|
te'smi~lloke pramodante sukhaṃ pretya ca bhuñjate||19||

20prāpnuvanti mahātmāna iti vitta dvijarṣabhāḥ
|
ata ūrdhvaṃ pravakṣyāmi niyataṃ dharmalakṣaṇam||20||

21ahiṃsā paramo dharmo hiṃsā cādharmalakṣaṇā
|
prakāśalakṣaṇā devā manuṣyāḥ karmalakṣaṇāḥ||21||

22śabdalakṣaṇamākāśaṃ vāyustu sparśalakṣaṇaḥ
|
jyotiṣāṃ lakṣaṇaṃ rūpamāpaśca rasalakṣaṇāḥ||22||

23dhāriṇī sarvabhūtānāṃ pṛthivī gandhalakṣaṇā
|
svaravyañjanasaṃskārā bhāratī śabdalakṣaṇā||23||

24manaso lakṣaṇaṃ cintā cintoktā buddhilakṣaṇā
|
manasā cintitānarthān buddhyā ceha vyavasyati||24||

25buddhirhi vyavasāyena lakṣyate nātra saṃśayaḥ
|
lakṣaṇaṃ manaso dhyānamavyaktaṃ sādhulakṣaṇam||25||

26pravṛttilakṣaṇo yogo jñānaṃ saṃnyāsalakṣaṇam
|
tasmājjñānaṃ puraskṛtya saṃnyasediha buddhimān||26||

27saṃnyāsī jñānasaṃyuktaḥ prāpnoti paramāṃ gatim
|
atīto dvandvamabhyeti tamomṛtyujarātigaḥ||27||

28dharmalakṣaṇasaṃyuktamuktaṃ vo vidhivanmayā
|
guṇānāṃ grahaṇaṃ samyagvakṣyāmyahamataḥ param||28||

29pārthivo yastu gandho vai ghrāṇena hi sa gṛhyate
|
ghrāṇasthaśca tathā vāyurgandhajñāne vidhīyate||29||

30apāṃ dhātū raso nityaṃ jihvayā sa tu gṛhyate
|
jihvāsthaśca tathā somo rasajñāne vidhīyate||30||

31jyotiṣaśca guṇo rūpaṃ cakṣuṣā tacca gṛhyate
|
cakṣuḥsthaśca sadā''dityo rūpajñāne vidhīyate||31||

32vāyavyastu sadā sparśastvacā prajñāyate ca saḥ
|
tvaksthaścaiva sadā vāyuḥ sparśane sa vidhīyate||32||

33ākāśasya guṇo hyeṣa śrotreṇa ca sa gṛhyate
|
śrotrasthāśca diśaḥ sarvāḥ śabdajñāne prakīrtitāḥ||33||

34manasaśca guṇaścintā prajñayā sa tu gṛhyate
|
hṛdisthaścetano dhāturmanojñāne vidhīyate||34||

35buddhiradhyavasāyena jñānena ca mahāṃstathā
|
niścitya grahaṇādvyaktamavyaktaṃ nātra saṃśayaḥ||35||

36aliṅgagrahaṇo nityaḥ kṣetrajño nirguṇātmakaḥ
|
tasmādaliṅgaḥ kṣetrajñaḥ kevalaṃ jñānalakṣaṇaḥ||36||

37avyaktaṃ kṣetramuddiṣṭaṃ guṇānāṃ prabhavāpyayam
|
sadā paśyāmyahaṃ līno vijānāmi śṛṇomi ca||37||

38puruṣastadvijānīte tasmātkṣetrajña ucyate
|
guṇavṛttaṃ tathā vṛttaṃ kṣetrajñaḥ paripaśyati||38||

39ādimadhyāvasānāntaṃ sṛjyamānamacetanam
|
na guṇā vidurātmānaṃ sṛjyamānāḥ punaḥ punaḥ||39||

40na satyaṃ vindate kaścitkṣetrajñastveva vindati
|
guṇānāṃ guṇabhūtānāṃ yat paraṃ paramaṃ mahat||40||

41tasmādguṇāṃśca sattvaṃ ca parityajyeha dharmavit
|
kṣīṇadoṣo guṇātītaḥ kṣetramaṃ praviśatyatha||41||

42nirdvandvo nimaskāro niḥsvāhākāra eva ca
|
acalaścāniketaśca kṣetrajñaḥ sa paro vibhuḥ||42||
