Mahābhārata 5.94 (Udyogaparvan)

1vaiśampāyana uvāca|
tathā kathayatoreva tayorbuddhimatostadā
|
śivā nakṣatrasampannā sā vyatīyāya śarvarī ||1||

2dharmārthakāmayuktāśca vicitrārthapadākṣarāḥ
|
śṛṇvato vividhā vāco vidurasya mahātmanaḥ ||2||

3kathābhiranurūpābhiḥ kṛṣṇasyāmitatejasaḥ
|
akāmasyeva kṛṣṇasya sā vyatīyāya śarvarī ||3||

4tatastu svarasampannā bahavaḥ sūtamāgadhāḥ
|
śaṅkhadundubhinirghoṣaiḥ keśavaṃ pratyabodhayan ||4||

5tata utthāya dāśārha ṛṣabhaḥ sarvasātvatām
|
sarvamāvaśyakaṃ cakre prātaḥkāryaṃ janārdanaḥ ||5||

6kṛtodakānujapyaḥ sa hutāgniḥ samalaṅkṛtaḥ
|
tataścādityamudyantamupātiṣṭhata mādhavaḥ ||6||

7atha duryodhanaḥ kṛṣṇaṃ śakuniścapi saubalaḥ
|
sandhyāṃ tiṣṭhantamabhyetya dāśārhamaparājitam ||7||

8ācakṣetāṃ tu kṛṣṇasya dhṛtarāṣṭraṃ sabhāgatam
|
kurūṃśca bhīṣmapramukhān rājñaḥ sarvāṃśca pārthivān ||8||

9tvāmarthayante govinda divi śakramivāmarāḥ
|
tāvabhyanandadgovindaḥ sāmnā paramavalgunā ||9||

10tato vimala āditye brāhmaṇebhyo janārdanaḥ
|
dadau hiraṇyaṃ vāsāṃsi gāścāśvāṃśca paraṃtapaḥ ||10||

11visṛjya bahuratnāni dāśārhamaparājitam
|
tiṣṭhantamupasaṅgamya vavande sārathistadā ||11||

12tato rathena śubhreṇa mahatā kiṅkiṇīkinā
|
hayottamayujā śīghramupātiṣṭhata dārukaḥ ||12||

13tamupasthitamājñāya rathaṃ divyaṃ mahāmanāḥ
|
mahābhraghananirghoṣaṃ sarvaratnavibhūṣitam ||13||

14agniṃ pradakṣiṇaṃ kṛtvā brāhmaṇāṃśca janārdanaḥ
|
kaustubhaṃ maṇimāmucya śriyā paramayā jvalan ||14||

15kurubhiḥ saṃvṛtaḥ kṛṣṇo vṛṣṇibhiścābhirakṣitaḥ
|
ātiṣṭhata rathaṃ śauriḥ sarvayādavanandanaḥ ||15||

16anvāruroha dāśārhaṃ viduraḥ sarvadharmavit
|
sarvaprāṇabhṛtāṃ śreṣṭhaṃ sarvabuddhimatāṃ varam ||16||

17tato duryodhanaḥ kṛṣṇaṃ śakuniścāpi saubalaḥ
|
dvitīyena rathenainamanvayātāṃ paraṃtapam ||17||

18sātyakiḥ kṛtavarmā ca vṛṣṇīnāṃ cāpare rathāḥ
|
pṛṣṭhato'nuyayuḥ kṛṣṇaṃ gajaraiśvai rathairapi ||18||

19teṣāṃ hemapariṣkārairyuktāḥ paramavājibhiḥ
|
gacchatāṃ ghoṣiṇaścitrarathā rājan virejire ||19||

20saṃmṛṣṭasaṃsiktarajaḥ pratipede mahāpatham
|
rājarṣicaritaṃ kāle kṛṣṇo dhīmān śriyā jvalan ||20||

21tataḥ prayāte dāśārhe prāvādyantaikapuṣkarāḥ
|
śaṃkhāśca dadhmire tatra vādyānyanyāni yāni ca ||21||

22pravīrāḥ sarvalokasya yuvānaḥ siṃhavikramāḥ
|
parivārya rathaṃ śaureragacchanta paraṃtapāḥ ||22||

23tato'nye bahusāhasrā vicitrādbhutavāsasaḥ
|
asiprāsāyudhadharāḥ kṛṣṇasyāsan puraḥsarāḥ ||23||

24gajāḥ pañcaśatāstatra rathāścāsan sahasraśaḥ
|
prayāntamanvayurvīraṃ dāśārhamaparājitam ||24||

25puraṃ kurūṇāṃ saṃvṛttaṃ draṣṭukāmaṃ janārdanam
|
sabālavṛddhaṃ sastrīkaṃ rathyāgatamarindama ||25||

26vedikāmāśritābhiśca samākrāntānyanekaśaḥ
|
pracalantīva bhāreṇa yoṣidbhirbhavanānyuta ||26||

27sa pūjyamānaḥ kurubhiḥ saṃśṛṇvan madhurāḥ kathāḥ
|
yathārhaṃ pratisatkurvan prekṣamāṇaḥ śanairyayau ||27||

28tataḥ sabhāṃ samāsādya keśavasyānuyāyinaḥ
|
sa śaṃkhairveṇunirghoṣairdiśaḥ sarvā vyanādayan ||28||

29tataḥ sā samitiḥ sarvā rājñāmamitatejasām
|
saṃprākampata harṣeṇa kṛṣṇāgamanakāṅkṣayā ||29||

30tato'bhyāśagate kṛṣṇe samahṛṣyannarādhipāḥ
|
śrutvā taṃ rathanirghoṣaṃ parjanyaninadopamam ||30||

31āsādya tu sabhādvāramṛṣabhaḥ sarvasātvatām
|
avatīrya rathācchauriḥ kailāsaśikharopamāt ||31||

32navameghaprartākāśāṃ jvalantīmiva tejasā
|
mahendrasadanaprakhyāṃ praviveśa sabhāṃ tataḥ ||32||

33pāṇau gṛhītvā viduraṃ sātyakiṃ ca mahāyaśāḥ
|
jyotīṃṣyādityavadrājankurūn prācchādayan śriyā ||33||

34agrato vāsudevasya karṇaduryodhanāvubhau
|
vṛṣṇayaḥ kṛtavarmā cāpyāsan kṛṣṇasya pṛṣṭhataḥ ||34||

35dhṛtarāṣṭraṃ puraskṛtya bhīṣmadroṇādayastataḥ
|
āsanebhyo'calan sarve pūjayanto janārdanam ||35||

36abhyāgacchati dāśārhe prajñācakṣurnareśvaraḥ
|
sahaiva droṇabhīṣmābhyāmudatiṣṭhan mahāyaśāḥ ||36||

37uttiṣṭhati mahārāje dhṛtarāṣṭre janeśvare
|
tāni rājasahasrāṇi samuttasthuḥ samantataḥ ||37||

38āsanaṃ sarvatobhadraṃ jāmbūnadapariṣkṛtam
|
kṛṣṇārthe kalpitaṃ tatra dhṛtarāṣṭrasya śāsanāt ||38||

39smayamānastu rājānaṃ bhīṣmadroṇau ca mādhavaḥ
|
abhyabhāṣata dharmātmā rājñaścānyānyathāvayaḥ ||39||

40tatra keśavamānarcuḥ samyagabhyāgataṃ sabhām
|
rājānaḥ pārthivāḥ sarve kuravaśca janārdanam ||40||

41tatra tiṣṭhan sa dāśārho rājamadhye paraṃtapaḥ
|
apaśyadantarikṣasthānṛṣīn parapurañjayaḥ
|
tatastānabhisaṃprekṣya nāradapramukhānṛṣīn ||41||

42abhyabhāṣata dāśārho bhīṣmaṃ śāntanavaṃ śanaiḥ
|
pārthivīṃ samitiṃ draṣṭumṛṣayo'bhyāgatā nṛpa ||42||

43nimantryantāmāsanaiśca satkāreṇa ca bhūyasā
|
naiteṣvanupaviṣṭeṣu śakyaṃ kenacidāsitum ||43||

44pūjā prayujyatāmāśu munīnāṃ bhāvitātmanām
|
ṛṣīn śāntanavo dṛṣṭvā sabhādvāramupasthitān ||44||

45tvaramāṇastato bhṛtyānāsanānītyacodayat
|
āsanānyatha mṛṣṭāni mahānti vipulāni ca ||45||

46maṇikāñcanacitrāṇi samājahrustatastataḥ
|
teṣu tatropaviṣṭeṣu gṛhītārgheṣu bhārata ||46||

47niṣasādāsane kṛṣṇo rājānaśca yathāsanam
|
duḥśāsanaḥ sātyakaye dadāvāsanamuttamam ||47||

48viviṃśatirdadau pīṭhaṃ kāñcanaṃ kṛtavarmaṇe
|
avidūre tu kṛṣṇasya karṇaduryodhanāvubhau ||48||

49ekāsane mahātmānau niṣīdaturamarṣaṇau
|
gāndhārarājaḥ śakunirgāndhārairabhirakṣitaḥ ||49||

50niṣasādāsane rājā sahaputro viśāmpate
|
viduro maṇipīṭhe tu śuklaspardhyājinottare ||50||

51saṃspṛśannāsanaṃ śaurermahāmatirupāviśat
|
cirasya dṛṣṭvā dāśārhaṃ rājānaḥ sarva eva te ||51||

52amṛtasyeva nātṛpyan prekṣamāṇā janārdanam
|
atasīpuṣpasaṅkāśaḥ pītavāsā janārdanaḥ ||52||

53vyabhrājata sabhāmadhye hemnīvopahito maṇiḥ
|
tatastūṣṇīṃ sarvamāsīdgovindagatamānasam
|
na tatra kaścit kiñcidvā vyājahāra pumān kvacit ||53||
