Mahābhārata 4.66 (Virāṭaparvan)

1vaiśampāyana uvāca|āhūyamānaśca sa tena saṃkhye mahātmanā vai dhṛtarāṣṭraputraḥ
|
nivartitastasya girāṅ‌kuśena mahāgajo matta ivāṅkuśena ||1||

2so'mṛṣyamāṇo vacasā'bhimṛṣṭo mahārathenātirathastarasvī
|
paryāvavartātha rathena vīro bhogī yathā pādatalābhimṛṣṭaḥ ||2||

3taṃ prekṣya karṇaḥ parivartamānaṃ nivartya saṃstabhya ca viddhagātram
|
duryodhanasyottarato'bhyagacchat pārthaṃ nṛvīro yudhi hemamālī ||3||

4bhīṣmastataḥ śāntanavo vivṛtya hiraṇyakakṣastvarayā'bhiṣaṅgī
|
duryodhanaṃ paścimato'bhyarakṣat pārthānmahābāhuradhijyadhanvā ||4||

5droṇaḥ kṛpaścaiva viviṃśatiśca duḥśāsanaścaiva vivṛtya śīghram
|
sarve purastādvitatorucāpā duryodhanārthaṃ tvaritā'bhyupeyuḥ ||5||

6sa tānyanīkāni nivartamānānyālokya pūrṇaughanibhāni pārthaḥ
|
haṃso yathā meghamivāpatantaṃ dhanañjayaḥ pratyatapattarasvī ||6||

7te sarvataḥ saṃparivārya pārthamastrāṇi divyāni samādadānāḥ
|
vavarṣurabhyetya śaraiḥ samantānmeghā yathā bhūdharamambuvargaiḥ ||7||

8tato'stramastreṇa nivārya teṣāṃ gāṇḍīvadhanvā kurupuṅgavānām
|
saṃmohane śatrusaho'nyadastraṃ prāduścakāraindrirapāraṇīyam ||8||

9tato diśaścānudiśo vivṛtya śaraiḥ sudhārairniśitaiḥ supatraiḥ
|
gāṇḍīvaghoṣeṇa manāṃsi teṣāṃ mahābalaḥ pravyathayāñcakāra ||9||

10tataḥ punarbhīmaravaṃ pragṛhya dorbhyāṃ mahāśaṅkhamudāraghoṣam
|
vyanādayatsa pradiśo diśaḥ khaṃ bhuvaṃ ca pārtho dviṣatāṃ nihantā ||10||

11te śaṅkhanādena kurupravīrāḥ saṃmohitāḥ pārthasamīritena
|
utsṛjya cāpāni durāsadāni sarve tadā śāntiparā babhuvuḥ ||11||

12tathā visaṃjñeṣu ca teṣu pārthaḥ smṛtvā ca vākyāni tathottarāyāḥ
|
niryāhi madhyāditi matsyaputramuvāca yāvatkuravo visaṃjñāḥ ||12||

13ācāryaśāradvatayoḥ suśukle karṇasya pītaṃ ruciraṃ ca vastram
|
drauṇeśca rājñaśca tathaiva nīle vastre samādatsva narapravīra ||13||

14bhīṣmasya saṃjñā tu tathaiva manye jānāti so'strapratighātameṣaḥ
|
etasya vāhān kuru saṃyatastvamevaṃ hi yātavyamamūḍhasaṃjñaiḥ ||14||

15raśmīn samutsṛjya tato mahātmā rathādavaplutya virāṭaputraḥ
|
vastrāṇyupādāya mahārathānāṃ tūrṇaṃ punaḥ svaṃ rathamāruroha ||15||

16tato'nvaśāsaccaturaḥ sadaśvān putro virāṭasya hiraṇyakakṣān
|
te tadvyatīyurdhvajināmanīkaṃ śvetā vahanto'rjunamājimadhyāt ||16||

17tathā'nuyāntaṃ puruṣapravīraṃ bhīṣmaḥ śarairabhyahanattarasvī
|
sa cāpi bhīṣmasya hayānnihatya vivyādha pārtho daśabhiḥ pṛṣatkaiḥ ||17||

18tato'rjuno bhīṣmamapāsya yuddhe viddhvā'sya yantāramariṣṭhadhanvā
|
tasthau vimukto rathavṛndamadhyānmeghaṃ vidāryeva sahastraraśmiḥ ||18||

19labdhvā hi saṃjñāṃ tu kurupravīrāḥ pārthaṃ nirīkṣyātha surendrakalpam
|
raṇe vimuktaṃ sthitamekamājau sa dhārtarāṣṭrastvaritaṃ babhāṣe ||19||

20ayaṃ kathaṃ vai bhavato vimuktastathā pramathnīta yathā na mucyet
|
tamabravīcchāntanavaḥ prahasya kva te gatā buddhirabhūt kva vīryam ||20||

21śāntiṃ parāṃ prāpya yadā sthito'bhūrutsṛjya bāṇāṃśca dhanurvicitram
|
na tveṣa bībhatsuralaṃ nṛśaṃsaṃ kartuṃ na pāpe'sya mano viśiṣṭam ||21||

22trailokyahetorna jahetsvadharmaṃ sarve na tasmānnihatā raṇe'smin
|
kṣipraṃ kurūn yāhi kurupravīra vijitya gāśca pratiyātu pārthaḥ
|
mā te svako'rtho nipateta mohāttatsaṃvidhātavyamariṣṭabandham ||22||

23vaiśampāyana uvāca|duryodhanastasya tu tanniśamya pitāmahasyātmahitaṃ vaco'tha
|
atītakāmo yudhi so'tyamarṣī rājā viniḥśvasya babhūva tūṣṇīm ||23||

24tadbhīṣmavākyaṃ hitamīkṣya sarve dhanañjayāgniṃ ca vivardhamānam
|
nivartanāyaiva mano nidadhyurduryodhanaṃ te parirakṣamāṇāḥ ||24||

25tānprasthitānpratimanāḥ sa pārtho dhanañjayaḥ prekṣya kurupravīrān
|
abhāṣamāṇo'nunayaṃ muhūrtaṃ vaco'bravīt samparihṛtya bhūyaḥ ||25||

26pitāmahaṃ śāntanavaṃ ca vṛddhaṃ droṇaṃ guruṃ ca praṇipatya mūrdhnā
|
drauṇiṃ kṛpaṃ caiva kurūṃśca mānyāñcharairvicitrairabhivādya caiva ||26||

27duryodhanasyottamaratnacitraṃ ciccheda pārtho mukuṭaṃ śareṇa
|
āmantrya vīrāṃśca tathaiva mānyān gāṇḍīvaghoṣeṇa vinādya lokān ||27||

28sa devadattaṃ sahasā vinādya vidārya vīro dviṣatāṃ manāṃsi
|
dhvajena sarvānabhibhūya śatrūn sahemamālena virājamānaḥ ||28||

29dṛṣṭvā prayātāṃstu kurūn kirīṭī hṛṣṭo'bravīttatra sa matsyaputram
|
āvartayāśvān paśavo jitāste yātāḥ pare yāhi puraṃ prahṛṣṭaḥ ||29||

30devāstu dṛṣṭvā mahadadbhutaṃ tadyuddhaṃ kurūṇāṃ saha phālgunena
|
jagmuryathāsvaṃ bhavanaṃ pratītāḥ pārthasya karmāṇi vicintayantaḥ ||30||
