Mahābhārata 3.159 (Āraṇyakaparvan)

1vaiśampāyana uvāca|
yudhiṣṭhirastamāsādya tapasā dagdhakilbiṣam
|
abhyavādayata prītaḥ śirasā nāma kīrtayan||1||

2tataḥ kṛṣṇā ca bhīmaśca yamau ca sutapasvinau
|
śirobhiḥ prāpya rājarṣiṃ parivāryopatasthire||2||

3tathaiva dhaumyo dharmajñaḥ pāṇḍavānāṃ purohitaḥ
|
yathānyāyamupākrāntastamṛṣiṃ saṃśitavratam||3||

4anvajānāt sa dharmajño munirdivyena cakṣuṣā
|
pāṇḍoḥ putrān kuruśreṣṭhānāsyatāmiti cābravīt||4||

5kurūṇāmṛṣabhaṃ pārthaṃ pūjayitvā mahātapāḥ
|
saha bhrātṛbhirāsīnaṃ paryapṛcchadanāmayam||5||

6nānṛte kuruṣe bhāvaṃ kacciddharme pravartase
|
mātā pitrośca te vṛttiḥ kaccitpārtha na sīdati||6||

7kaccitte guravaḥ sarve vṛddhā vaidyāśca pūjitāḥ
|
kaccinna kuruṣe bhāvaṃ pārtha pāpeṣu karmasu||7||

8sukṛtaṃ pratikartuṃ ca kacciddhātuṃ ca duṣkṛtam
|
yathānyāyaṃ kuruśreṣṭha jānāsi na vikatthase||8||

9yathārhaṃ mānitāḥ kaccittvayā nandanti sādhavaḥ
|
vaneṣvapi vasankacciddharmamevānuvatase||9||

10kacciddhaumyastvadācārairna pārtha paritapyate
|
dānadharmatapaḥśaucairārjavena titikṣayā||10||

11pitṛpaitāmahaṃ vṛttaṃ kaccitpārthānuvartase
|
kaccidrājarṣiyātena pathā gacchasi pāṇḍava||11||

12sve sve kila kule jāte putre naptari vā punaḥ
|
pitaraḥ pitṛlokasthāḥ śocanti ca hasanti ca||12||

13kiṃ tasya duṣkṛte'smābhiḥ saṃprāptavyaṃ bhaviṣyati
|
kiṃ cāsya sukṛte'smābhiḥ prāptavyamiti śobhanam||13||

14pitā mātā tathaivāgnirgururātmā ca pañcamaḥ
|
yasyaite pūjitāḥ pārtha tasya lokāvubhau jitau||14||

15yudhiṣṭhira uvāca|
bhagavānārya māhaitadyathāvaddharmaniścayam
|
yathāśakti yathānyāyaṃ kriyate vidhivanmayā||15||

16ārṣṭiṣeṇa uvāca|
abbhakṣā vāyubhakṣāśca plavamānā vihāyasā
|
juṣante parvataśreṣṭhamṛṣayaḥ parvasaṃdhiṣu||16||

17kāminaḥ saha kāntābhiḥ parasparamanuvratāḥ
|
dṛśyante śailaśṛṅgasthā yathā kiṃpuruṣā nṛpa||17||

18arajāṃsi ca vāsāṃsi vasānāḥ kauśikāni ca
|
dṛśyante bahavaḥ pārtha gandharvāpsarasāṃ gaṇāḥ||18||

19vidyādharagaṇāścaiva sragviṇaḥ priyadarśanāḥ
|
mahoragagaṇāścaiva suparṇāścoragādayaḥ||19||

20asya copari śailasya śrūyate parvasaṃdhiṣu
|
bherī paṇavaśaṅkhānāṃ mṛdaṅgānāṃ ca niḥsvanaḥ||20||

21ihasthaireva tatsarvaṃ śrotavyaṃ bharatarṣabhāḥ
|
na kāryā vaḥ kathaṃcitsyāttatrābhigamane matiḥ||21||

22na cāpyataḥ paraṃ śakyaṃ gantuṃ bharatasattamāḥ
|
vihāro hyatra devānāmamānuṣagatistu sā||22||

23īṣaccapalakarmāṇāṃ manuṣyamiha bhārata
|
dviṣanti sarvabhūtāni tāḍayanti ca rākṣasāḥ||23||

24asyātikramya śikharaṃ kailāsasya yudhiṣṭhira
|
gatiḥ paramasiddhānāṃ devarṣīṇāṃ prakāśate||24||

25cāpalādiha gacchantaṃ pārtha yānamitaḥ param
|
ayaḥśūlādibhirghnanti rākṣasāḥ śatrusūdana||25||

26apsarobhiḥ parivṛtaḥ samṛddhyā naravāhanaḥ
|
iha vaiśravaṇastāta parvasaṃdhiṣu dṛśyate||26||

27śikharasthaṃ samāsīnamadhipaṃ yakṣarakṣasām
|
prekṣante sarvabhūtāni bhānumantamivoditam||27||

28devadānavasiddhānāṃ tathā vaiśravaṇasya ca
|
gireḥ śikharamudyānamidaṃ bharatasattama||28||

29upāsīnasya dhanadaṃ tumburoḥ parvasaṃdhiṣu
|
gītasāmasvanastāta śrūyate gandhamādane||29||

30etadevaṃvidhaṃ citramiha tāta yudhiṣṭhira
|
prekṣante sarvabhūtāni bahuśaḥ parvasaṃdhiṣu||30||

31bhuñjānā munibhojyāni rasavanti phalāni ca
|
vasadhvaṃ pāṇḍavaśreṣṭhā yāvadarjunadarśanāt||31||

32na tāta capalairbhāvyamiha prāptaiḥ kathaṃcana
|
uṣitveha yathākāmaṃ yathāśraddhaṃ vihṛtya ca
|
tataḥ śastrajitāṃ tāta pṛthivīṃ pālayiṣyasi||32||
