Mahābhārata 6.66 (Bhīṣmaparvan)

1bhīṣma uvāca|
tataḥ sa bhagavān devo lokānāmīśvareśvaraḥ
|
brahmāṇaṃ pratyuvācedaṃ snigdhagaṃbhīrayā girā ||1||

2viditaṃ tāta yogānme sarvametattavepsitam
|
tathā tadbhavitetyuktvā tatraivāntaradhīyata ||2||

3tato devarṣigandharvā vismayaṃ paramaṃ gatāḥ
|
kautūhalaparāḥ sarve pitāmahamathābruvan ||3||

4ko nvayaṃ yo bhagavatā praṇamya vinayādvibho
|
vāgbhiḥ stuto variṣṭhābhiḥ śrotumicchāma taṃ vayam ||4||

5evamuktastu bhagavān pratyuvāca pitāmahaḥ
|
devabrahmarṣigandharvānsarvān madhurayā girā ||5||

6yattatparaṃ bhaviṣyaṃ ca bhavitavyaṃ ca yatparam
|
bhūtātmā ca prabhuścaiva brahma yacca paraṃ padam ||6||

7tenāsmi kṛtasaṃvādaḥ prasannenaṃ surarṣabhāḥ
|
jagato'nugrahārthāya yācito me jagatpatiḥ ||7||

8mānuṣaṃ lokamātiṣṭha vāsudeva iti śrutaḥ
|
asurāṇāṃ vadhārthāya saṃbhavasva mahītale ||8||

9saṃgrāme nihatā ye te daitya-dānava-rākṣasāḥ
|
ta ime nṛṣu saṃbhūtā ghorarūpā mahābalāḥ ||9||

10teṣāṃ vadhārthaṃ bhagavānnareṇa sahito vaśī
|
mānuṣīṃ yonimāsthāya cariṣyati mahatile ||10||

11naranārāyaṇau yau tau purāṇāvṛṣisattamau
|
sahitau mānuṣe loke saṃbhūtāvamitadyutī ||11||

12ajeyau samare yattau sahitairamarairapi
|
mūḍhāstvetau na jānanti naranārāyaṇāvṛṣī ||12||

13tasyāhamagrajaḥ putraḥ sarvasya jagataḥ prabhuḥ
|
vāsudevo'rcanīyo vaḥ sarvalokamaheśvaraḥ ||13||

14tathā manuṣyo'yamiti kadācitsurasattamāḥ
|
nāvajñeyo mahāvīryaḥ śaṃkha-cakra-gadādharaḥ ||14||

15etat paramakaṃ guhyametat paramakaṃ padam
|
etat paramakaṃ brahma etat paramakaṃ yaśaḥ ||15||

16etadakṣaramavyaktametadvai śāśvataṃ mahaḥ
|
yattatpuruṣasaṃjñaṃ vai gīyate jñāyate na ca ||16||

17etatparamakaṃ teja etatparamakaṃ sukham
|
etatparamakaṃ satyaṃ kīrtitaṃ viśvakarmaṇā ||17||

18tasmātsendraiḥ suraiḥ sarvairlokaiścāmitavikramaḥ
|
nāvajñeyo vāsudevo mānuṣo'yamiti prabhuḥ ||18||

19yaśca mānuṣamātro'yamiti brūyātsa mandadhīḥ
|
hṛṣīkeśamavajñānāttamāhuḥ puruṣādhamam ||19||

20yoginaṃ taṃ mahātmānaṃ praviṣṭaṃ mānuṣīṃ tanum
|
avamanyedvāsudevaṃ tamāhustāmasaṃ janāḥ ||20||

21devaṃ carācarātmānaṃ śrīvatsāṅkaṃ suvarcasam
|
padmanābhaṃ na jānāti tamāhustāmasaṃ budhāḥ ||21||

22kirīṭakaustubhadharaṃ mitrāṇāmabhayaṃkaram
|
avajānanmahātmānaṃ ghore tamasi majjati ||22||

23evaṃ viditvā tattvārthaṃ lokānāmaśvireśvaraḥ
|
vāsudevo namaskāryaḥ sarvalokaiḥ surottamāḥ ||23||

24bhīṣma uvāca|evamuktvā sa bhagavāndevānsarṣigaṇān purā
|
visṛjya sarvabhūtātmā jagāma bhavanaṃ svakam ||24||

25tato devāḥ sagandharvā munayo'psaraso'pi ca
|
kathāṃ tāṃ brahmaṇā gītāṃ śrutvā prītā divaṃ yayuḥ ||25||

26etacchrutaṃ mayā tāta ṛṣīṇāṃ bhāvitātmanām
|
vāsudevaṃ kathayatāṃ samavāye purātanam ||26||

27rāmasya jāmadagnyasya mārkaṇḍeyasya dhīmataḥ
|
vyāsa-nārada‌dyoścāpi sakāśādbharatarṣabha ||27||

28etamarthaṃ ca vijñāya śrutvā ca prabhumavyayam
|
vāsudevaṃ mahātmānaṃ lokānāmīśvareśvaram ||28||

29yasya caivātmajo brahmā sarvasya jagataḥ pitā
|
kathaṃ na vāsudevo'yamarcyaścejyaśca mānavaiḥ ||29||

30vārito'si mayā tāta munibhirvedapāragaiḥ
|
mā gaccha saṃyugaṃ tena vāsudevena dhanvinā ||30||

31māṃ pāṇḍavaiḥ sārdhamiti tattvaṃ mohānna budhyase
|
manye tvāṃ rākṣasaṃ krūraṃ tathā cāsi tamovṛtaḥ ||31||

32yasmāddviṣasi govindaṃ pāṇḍavaṃ taṃ dhanañjayam
|
naranārāyaṇau devau ko'nyo dviṣyāddhi mānavaḥ ||32||

33tasmādbravīmi te rājanneṣa vai śāśvato'vyayaḥ
|
sarvalokamayo nityaḥ śāstā dhātrīdharo dhruvaḥ ||33||

34yo dhārayati lokāṃstrīṃścarācaraguruḥ prabhuḥ
|
yoddhā jayaśca jetā ca sarvaprakṛtirīśvaraḥ ||34||

35rājan sarvamayo hyeṣa tamorāgavivarjitaḥ
|
yataḥ kṛṣṇastato dharmo yato dharmastato jayaḥ ||35||

36tasya māhātmyayogena yogenātmamayena ca
|
dhṛtāḥ pāṇḍusutā rājan jayaścaiṣāṃ bhaviṣyati ||36||

37śreyoyuktāṃ sadā buddhiṃ pāṇḍavānāṃ dadhāti yaḥ
|
balaṃ caiva raṇe nityaṃ bhayebhyaścaiva rakṣati ||37||

38sa eṣa śāśvato devaḥ sarvaguhyamayaḥ śivaḥ
|
vāsudeva iti jñeyo yanmāṃ pṛcchasi bhārata ||38||

39brāhmaṇaiḥ kṣatriyairvaiśyaiḥ śūdraiśca kṛtalakṣaṇaiḥ
|
sevyate'bhyarcyate caiva nityayuktaiḥ svakarmabhiḥ ||39||

40dvāparasya yugasyānte ādau kaliyugasya ca
|
sātvataṃ vidhimāsthāya gītaḥ saṅkarṣaṇena vai ||40||

41sa eṣa sarvaṃ suramartyalokaṃ samudrakakṣyāntaritāṃ purīṃ ca
|
yuge yuge mānuṣaṃ caiva vāsaṃ punaḥ punaḥ sṛjate vāsudevaḥ ||41||
