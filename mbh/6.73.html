Mahābhārata 6.73 (Bhīṣmaparvan)

1sañjaya uvāca|
virāṭo'tha tribhirbāṇairbhīṣmamārcchanmahāratham
|
vivyādha turagāṃścāsya tribhirbāṇairmahārathaḥ ||1||

2taṃ pratyavidhyaddaśabhirbhīṣmaḥ śāntanavaḥ śaraiḥ
|
rukmapuṅkhairmaheṣvāsaḥ kṛtahasto mahābalaḥ ||2||

3drauṇirgāṇḍīvadhanvānaṃ bhīmadhanvā mahārathaḥ
|
avidhyadiṣubhiḥ ṣa‌ḍbhirdṛḍhahastaḥ stanāntare ||3||

4kārmukaṃ tasya ciccheda phālgunaḥ paravīrahā
|
avidhyacca bhṛśaṃ tīkṣṇaiḥ patribhiḥ śatrukarśanaḥ ||4||

5so'nyat kārmukamādāya vegavān krodhamūrcchitaḥ
|
amṛṣyamāṇaḥ pārthena kārmukacchedamāhave ||5||

6avidhyatphālgunaṃ rājannavatyā niśitaiḥ śaraiḥ
|
vāsudevaṃ ca saptatyā vivyādha parameṣubhiḥ ||6||

7tataḥ krodhābhitāmrākṣaḥ kṛṣṇena saha phālgunaḥ
|
dīrghamuṣṇaṃ ca niḥśvasya cintayitvā punaḥpunaḥ ||7||

8dhanuḥ prapīḍya vāmena kareṇāmitrakarśanaḥ
|
gāṇḍīvadhanvā saṃkruddhaḥ śitān sannataparvaṇaḥ ||8||

9jīvitāntakarān ghorān samādatta śilīmukhān
|
taistūrṇaṃ samare'vidhyaddrauṇiṃ balavatāṃ varaḥ ||9||

10tasya te kavacaṃ bhittvā papuḥ śoṇitamāhave
|
na vivyathe ca nirbhinno drauṇirgāṇḍīvadhanvanā ||10||

11tathaiva ca śarān drauṇiḥ pravimuñcannavihvalaḥ
|
tasthau sa samare rājaṃstrātumicchan mahāvratam ||11||

12tasya tat sumahat karma śaśaṃsuḥ kurusattamāḥ
|
yat kṛṣṇābhyāṃ sametābhyāmabhyāpatata saṃyuge ||12||

13sa hi nityamanīkeṣu yudhyate'bhayamāsthitaḥ
|
astragrāmaṃ sasaṃhāraṃ droṇāt prāpya sudurlabham ||13||

14mamaiṣa ācāryasuto droṇasyāpi priyaḥ sutaḥ
|
brāhmaṇaśca viśeṣeṇa mānanīyo mameti ca ||14||

15samāsthāya matiṃ vīro bībhatsuḥ śatrutāpanaḥ
|
kṛpāṃ cakre rathaśreṣṭho bhāradvājasutaṃ prati ||15||

16drauṇiṃ tyaktvā tato yuddhe kaunteyaḥ śvetavāhanaḥ
|
yuyudhe tāvakānnighnaṃstvaramāṇaḥ parākramī ||16||

17duryodhanastu daśabhirgārdhrapatraiḥ śilāśitaiḥ
|
bhīmasenaṃ maheṣvāsaṃ rukmapuṅkhaiḥ samārpayat ||17||

18bhīmasenaḥ susaṃkruddhaḥ parāsukaraṇaṃ dṛḍham
|
citraṃ kārmukamādatta śarāṃśca niśitān daśa ||18||

19ākarṇaprahitaistīkṣṇairvegavadbhirajihmagaiḥ
|
avidhyat tūrṇamavyagraḥ kururājaṃ mahorasi ||19||

20tasya kāñcanasūtrasthaḥ śaraiḥ saṃchādito maṇiḥ
|
rarājorasi khe sūryo grahairiva samāvṛtaḥ ||20||

21putrastu tava tejasvī bhīmasenena tāḍitaḥ
|
nāmṛṣyata yathā nāgastalaśabdaṃ madotkaṭaḥ ||21||

22tataḥ śarairmahārāja rukmapuṅkhaiḥ śilāśitaiḥ
|
bhīmaṃ vivyādha saṃkruddhastrāsayāno varūthinīm ||22||

23tau yudhyamānau samare bhṛśamanyonyavikṣatau
|
putrau te devasaṅkāśau vyarocetāṃ mahābalau ||23||

24citrasenaṃ naravyāghraṃ saubhadraḥ paravīrahā
|
avidhyaddaśabhirbāṇaiḥ purumitraṃ ca saptabhiḥ ||24||

25satyavrataṃ ca saptatyā viddhvā śakrasamo yudhi
|
nṛtyanniva raṇe vīra ārtiṃ naḥ samajījanat ||25||

26taṃ pratyavidhyaddaśabhiścitrasenaḥ śilīmukhaiḥ
|
satyavrataśca navabhiḥ purumitraśca saptabhiḥ ||26||

27sa viddho vikṣaran raktaṃ śatrusaṃvāraṇaṃ mahat
|
ciccheda citrasenasya citraṃ kārmukamārjuniḥ ||27||

28bhittvā cāsya tanutrāṇaṃ śareṇorasyatāḍayat
|
tataste tāvakā vīrā rājaputrā mahārathāḥ ||28||

29sametya yudhi saṃrabdhā vivyadhurniśitaiḥ śaraiḥ
|
tāṃśca sarvān śaraistīkṣṇairjaghāna paramāstravit||29||

30tasya dṛṣṭvā tu tat karma parivavruḥ sutāstava
|
dahantaṃ samare sainyaṃ vane kakṣaṃ yatholbaṇam ||30||

31apetaśiśire kāle samiddhamiva pāvakam
|
atyarocata saubhadrastava sainyāni nāśayan ||31||

32tattasya caritaṃ dṛṣṭvā pautrastava viśāṃpate
|
lakṣmaṇo'bhyapatattūrṇaṃ sātvatīputramāhave ||32||

33abhimanyustu saṃkruddho lakṣmaṇaṃ śubhalakṣaṇam
|
vivyādha niśitaiḥ ṣaḍbhiḥ sārathiṃ ca tribhiḥ śaraiḥ ||33||

34tathaiva lakṣmaṇo rājan saubhadraṃ niśitaiḥ śaraiḥ
|
avidhyata mahārāja tada‌dbhutamivābhavat ||34||

35tasyāśvāṃścaturo hatvā sārathiṃ ca mahābalaḥ
|
abhyadravata saubhadro lakṣmaṇaṃ niśitaiḥ śaraiḥ ||35||

36hatāśve tu rathe tiṣṭhaঁllakṣmaṇaḥ paravīrahā
|
śaktiṃ cikṣepa saṃkruddhaḥ saubhadrasya rathaṃ prati ||36||

37tāmāpatantīṃ sahasā ghorarūpāṃ durāsadām
|
abhimanyuḥ śaraistīkṣṇaiściccheda bhujagopamām ||37||

38tataḥ svarathamāropya lakṣmaṇaṃ gautamastadā
|
apovāha rathenājau sarvasainyasya paśyataḥ ||38||

39tataḥ samākule tasmin vartamāne mahābhaye
|
abhyadravan jighāṃsantaḥ parasparavadhaiṣiṇaḥ ||39||

40tāvakāśca maheṣvāsāḥ pāṇḍavāśca mahārathāḥ
|
juhvantaḥ samare prāṇānnijaghnuritaretaram ||40||

41muktakeśā vikavacā virathāśchinnakārmukāḥ
|
bāhubhiḥ samayudhyanta sṛñjayāḥ kurubhiḥ saha ||41||

42tato bhīṣmo mahābāhuḥ pāṇḍavānāṃ mahātmanām
|
senāṃ jaghāna saṃkruddho divyairastrairmahābalaḥ ||42||

43hatairaśvairgajaistatra narairaśvaiśca pātitaiḥ
|
rathibhiḥ sādibhiścaiva samāstīryata medinī ||43||
