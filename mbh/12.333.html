Mahābhārata 12.333 (Śāntiparvan)

1bhīṣma uvāca|
ityevamuktvā vacanaṃ brahmarṣiḥ sumahātapāḥ
|
prātiṣṭhata śukaḥ siddhiṃ hitvā doṣāṃścaturvidhān||1||

2tamo hṛṣṭavidhaṃ hitvā jahau pañcavidhaṃ rajaḥ
|
tataḥ sattvaṃ jahau dhīmāṃstadadbhutamivābhavat||2||

3tatastasminpade nitye nirguṇe liṅgavarjite
|
brahmaṇi pratyatiṣṭhatsa vidhūmo'gniriva jvalan||3||

4ulkāpātā diśāṃ dāho bhūmikampastathaiva ca
|
prādurbhūtaḥ kṣaṇe tasmiṃstadadbhutamivābhavat||4||

5drumāḥ śākhāśca mumucuḥ śikharāṇi ca parvatāḥ
|
nirghātaśabdaiśca girirhimavāndīryatīva ha||5||

6na babhāse sahasrāṃśurna jajvāla ca pāvakaḥ
|
hradāśca saritaścaiva cukṣubhuḥ sāgarāstathā||6||

7vavarṣa vāsavastoyaṃ rasavacca sugandhi ca
|
vavau samīraṇaścāpi divyagandhavahaḥ śuciḥ||7||

8sa śṛṅge prathame divye himavanmerusaṃbhave
|
saṃśliṣṭe śvetapīte dve rukmarūpyamaye śubhe||8||

9śatayojanavistāre tiryagūrdhvaṃ ca bhārata
|
udīcīṃ diśamāsthāya rucire saṃdadarśa ha||9||

10so'viśaṅkena manasā tathaivābhyapatacchukaḥ
|
tataḥ parvataśṛṅge dve sahasaiva dvidhākṛte||10||

11adṛśyetāṃ mahārāja tadadbhutamivābhavat
|
tataḥ parvataśṛṅgābhyāṃ sahasaiva viniḥsṛtaḥ||11||

12na ca pratijaghānāsya sa gatiṃ parvatottamaḥ
|
tato mahānabhūcchabdo divi sarvadivaukasām||12||

13gandharvāṇāmṛṣīṇāṃ ca ye ca śailanivāsinaḥ
|
dṛṣṭvā śukamatikrāntaṃ parvataṃ ca dvidhākṛtam||13||

14sādhu sādhviti tatrāsīnnādaḥ sarvatra bhārata
|
sa pūjyamāno devaiśca gandharvairṛṣibhistathā||14||

15yakṣarākṣasasaṃghaiśca vidyādharagaṇaistathā
|
divyaiḥ puṣpaiḥ samākīrṇamantarikṣaṃ samantataḥ||15||

16āsītkila mahārāja śukābhitapane tadā
|
tato mandākinīṃ ramyāmupariṣṭādabhivrajan||16||

17śuko dadarśa dharmātmā puṣpitadrumakānanām
|
tasyāṃ krīḍantyabhiratāste caivāpsarasāṃ gaṇāḥ||17||

18śūnyākāraṃ nirākārāḥ śukaṃ dṛṣṭvā vivāsasaḥ
|
taṃ prakrāmantamājñāya pitā snehasamanvitaḥ||18||

19uttamāṃ gatimāsthāya pṛṣṭhato'nusasāra ha
|
śukastu mārutādūrdhvaṃ gatiṃ kṛtvāntarikṣagām||19||

20darśayitvā prabhāvaṃ svaṃ brahmabhūto'bhavattadā
|
mahāyogagatiṃ tvanyāṃ vyāsotthāya mahātapāḥ||20||

21nimeṣāntaramātreṇa śukābhipatanaṃ yayau
|
sa dadarśa dvidhā kṛtvā parvatāgraṃ śukaṃ gatam||21||

22śaśaṃsurṛṣayastatra karma putrasya tattadā
|
tataḥ śuketi dīrgheṇa śabdenākranditastadā||22||

23svayaṃ pitrā svareṇoccaistrīlloঁkānanunādya vai
|
śukaḥ sarvagato bhūtvā sarvātmā sarvatomukhaḥ||23||

24pratyabhāṣata dharmātmā bho śabdenānunādayan
|
tata ekākṣaraṃ nādaṃ bhorityeva samīrayan||24||

25pratyāharajjagatsarvamuccaiḥ sthāvarajaṅgamam
|
tataḥ prabhṛti cādyāpi śabdānuccāritānpṛthak||25||

26girigahvarapṛṣṭheṣu vyāharanti śukaṃ prati
|
antarhitaḥ prabhāvaṃ tu darśayitvā śukastadā||26||

27guṇānsantyajya śabdādīnpadamabhyagamatparam
|
mahimānaṃ tu taṃ dṛṣṭvā putrasyāmitatejasaḥ||27||

28niṣasāda giriprasthe putramevānucintayan
|
tato mandākinītīre krīḍanto'psarasāṃ gaṇāḥ||28||

29āsādya tamṛṣiṃ sarvāḥ saṃbhrāntā gatacetasaḥ
|
jale nililyire kāścitkāścidgulmān prapedire||29||

30vasanānyādaduḥ kāścittaṃ dṛṣṭvā munisattamam
|
tāṃ muktatāṃ tu vijñāya muniḥ putrasya vai tadā||30||

31saktatāmātmanaścaiva prīto'bhūdvrīḍitaśca ha||31||

32taṃ devagandharvavṛto maharṣigaṇapūjitaḥ
|
pinākahasto bhagavānabhyāgacchata śaṃkaraḥ||32||

33tamuvāca mahādevaḥ sāntvapūrvamidaṃ vacaḥ
|
putraśokābhisaṃtaptaṃ kṛṣṇadvaipāyanaṃ tadā||33||

34agnerbhūmerapāṃ vāyorantarikṣasya caiva ha
|
vīryeṇa sadṛśaḥ putraḥ purā mattastvayā vṛtaḥ||34||

35sa tathālakṣaṇo jātastapasā tava saṃbhṛtaḥ
|
mama caiva prasādena brahmatejomayaḥ śuciḥ||35||

36sa gatiṃ paramāṃ prāpto duṣprāpāmajitendriyaiḥ
|
daivatairapi viprarṣe taṃ tvaṃ kimanuśocasi||36||

37yāvatsthāsyanti girayo yāvatsthāsyanti sāgarāḥ
|
tāvattavākṣayā kīrtiḥ saputrasya bhaviṣyati||37||

38chāyāṃ svaputrasadṛśīṃ sarvato'napagāṃ sadā
|
drakṣyase tvaṃ ca loke'sminmatprasādānmahāmune||38||

39so'nunīto bhagavatā svayaṃ rudreṇa bhārata
|
chāyāṃ paśyansamāvṛttaḥ sa muniḥ parayā mudā||39||

40iti janma gatiścaiva śukasya bharatarṣabha
|
vistareṇa samākhyātā yanmāṃ tvaṃ paripṛcchasi||40||

41etadācaṣṭa me rājan devarṣirnāradaḥ purā
|
vyāsaścaiva mahāyogī saṃjalpeṣu pade pade||41||

42itihāsamimaṃ puṇyaṃ mokṣadharmopasaṃhitam
|
dhārayedyaḥ śamaparaḥ sa gacchetparamāṃ gatim||42||
