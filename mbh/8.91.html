Mahābhārata 8.91 (Karṇaparvan)

1sañjaya uvāca|
tamabravīdvāsudevo rathastho rādheya diṣṭyā smarasīha dharmam
|
prāyeṇa nīcā vyasaneṣu magnā nindanti daivaṃ kukṛtaṃ na tu svam ||1||

2yaddraupadīmekavastrāṃ sabhāyāmānāyayestvaṃ ca suyodhanaśca
|
duḥśāsanaḥ śakuniḥ saubalaśca na te karṇa pratyabhāttatra dharmaḥ ||2||

3yadā sabhāyāṃ rājānamanakṣajñaṃ yudhiṣṭhiram
|
ajaiṣīcchakunirjñānāt kva te dharmastadā gataḥ ||3||

4vanavāse vyatīte ca karṇa varṣe trayodaśe
|
na prayacchasi yadrājyaṃ kva te dharmastadā gataḥ ||4||

5yadbhīmasenaṃ sarpaiśca viṣayuktaiśca bhojanaiḥ
|
ācarattvanmate rājā kva te dharmastadā gataḥ ||5||

6yadvāraṇāvate pārthānsuptāñjatugṛhe tadā
|
ādīpayastvaṃ rādheya kva te dharmastadā gataḥ ||6||

7yadā rajasvalāṃ kṛṣṇāṃ duḥśāsanavaśe sthitām
|
sabhāyāṃ prāhasaḥ karṇa kva te dharmastadā gataḥ ||7||

8yadanāryaiḥ purā kṛṣṇāṃ kliśyamānāmanāgasam
|
upaprekṣasi rādheya kva te dharmastadā gataḥ ||8||

9vinaṣṭāḥ pāṇḍavāḥ kṛṣṇe śāśvataṃ narakaṃ gatāḥ
|
patimanyaṃ vṛṇīṣveti vadaṃstvaṃ gajagāminīm ||9||

10upaprekṣasi rādheya kva te dharmastadā gataḥ
|
rājyalubdhaḥ punaḥ karṇa samāhvayasi pāṇḍavān ||10||

11yadā śakunimāśritya kva te dharmastadā gataḥ
|
yadā'bhimanyuṃ bahavo yuddhe jaghnurmahārathāḥ
|
parivārya raṇe bālaṃ kva te dharmastadā gataḥ ||11||

12yadyeṣa dharmastatra na vidyate hi kiṃ sarvathā tāluviśoṣaṇena ||12||

13adyeha dharmyāṇi vidhatsva sūta tathāpi jīvanna vimokṣyase hi
|
nalo hyakṣairnirjitaḥ puṣkareṇa punaryaśo rājyamavāpa vīryāt ||13||

14prāptāstathā pāṇḍavā bāhuvīryātsarvaiḥ sametāḥ parivṛttalobhāḥ
|
nihatya śatrūnsamare pravṛddhān sasomakā rājyamavāpnuyuste ||14||

15tathā gatā dhārtarāṣṭrā vināśaṃ dharmābhiguptaiḥ satataṃ nṛsiṃhaiḥ
|
sañjaya uvāca|
evamuktastadā karṇo vāsudevena bhārata ||15||

16lajjayāvanato bhūtvā nottaraṃ kiñciduktavān
|
krodhātprasphuramāṇauṣṭho dhanurudyamya bhārata ||16||

17yodhayāmāsa vai pārthaṃ mahāvegaparākramaḥ
|
tato'bravīdvāsudevaḥ phālgunaṃ puruṣarṣabham ||17||

18divyāstreṇaiva nirbhidya pātayasva mahābala
|
evamuktastu devena krodhamāgāttadārjunaḥ ||18||

19manyumabhyāviśaddhoraṃ smṛtvā tattu dhanañjayaḥ
|
tasya kruddhasya sarvebhyaḥ srotobhyastejasorciṣaḥ ||19||

20prādurāsaṃstadā rājaṃstada‌dbhutamivābhavat
|
tatsamīkṣya tataḥ karṇo brahmāstreṇa dhanañjayam ||20||

21abhyavarṣatpunaryatnamakarodrathasarjane
|
brahmāstreṇaiva taṃ pārtho vavarṣa śaravṛṣṭibhiḥ ||21||

22tadastramastreṇāvārya prajahāra ca pāṇḍavaḥ
|
tato'nyadastraṃ kaunteyo dayitaṃ jātavedasaḥ ||22||

23mumoca karṇamuddiśya tatprajajvāla tejasā
|
vāruṇena tataḥ karṇaḥ śamayāmāsa pāvakam ||23||

24jīmūtaiśca diśaḥ sarvāścakre timiradurdināḥ
|
pāṇḍaveyastvasaṃbhrānto vāyavyāstreṇa vīryavān ||24||

25apovāha tadā'bhrāṇi rādheyasya prapaśyataḥ
|
tataḥ śaraṃ mahāghoraṃ jvalantamiva pāvakam ||25||

26ādade pāṇḍuputrasya sūtaputro jighāṃsayā
|
yojyamāne tatastasmin bāṇe dhanuṣi pūjite ||26||

27cacāla pṛthivī rājan saśailavanakānanā
|
vavau saśarkaro vāyurdiśaśca rajasā vṛtāḥ ||27||

28hāhākāraśca sañjajñe surāṇāṃ divi bhārata
|
tamiṣuṃ sandhitaṃ dṛṣṭvā sūtaputreṇa māriṣa ||28||

29viṣādaṃ paramaṃ jagmuḥ pāṇḍavā dīnacetasaḥ
|
sa sāyakaḥ karṇabhujapramuktaḥ śakrāśaniprakhyaruciḥ śitāgraḥ ||29||

30bhujāntaraṃ prāpya dhanañjayasya viveśa valmīkamivoragottamaḥ
|
sa gāḍhaviddhaḥ samare mahātmā vighūrṇamānaḥ ślathahastagāṇḍivaḥ ||30||

31cacāla bībhatsuramitramardanaḥ kṣiteḥ prakampe ca yathā'calottamaḥ
|
tadantaraṃ prāpya vṛṣo mahāratho rathāṅgamurvīgatamujjihīrṣuḥ ||31||

32rathādavaplutya nigṛhya dorbhyāṃ śaśāka devānna mahābalo'pi
|
tataḥ kirīṭī pratilabhya saṃjñāṃ jagrāha bāṇaṃ yamadaṇḍakalpam ||32||

33tato'rjunaḥ prāñjalikaṃ mahātmā tato'bravīdvāsudevo'pi pārtham
|
chindhyasya mūrdhānamareḥ śareṇa na yāvadārohati vai rathaṃ vṛṣaḥ ||33||

34tathaiva sampūjya sa tadvacaḥ prabhostataḥ śaraṃ prajvalitaṃ pragṛhya
|
jaghāna kakṣāmamalārkavarṇāṃ mahārathe rathacakre vimagne ||34||

35taṃ hastikakṣāpravaraṃ ca ketuṃ suvarṇamuktāmaṇivajrapṛṣṭham
|
jñānaprakarṣottamaśilpiyuktaiḥ kṛtaṃ surūpaṃ tapanīyacitram ||35||

36jayāspadaṃ tava sainyasya nityamamitravitrāsanīḍyarūpam
|
vikhyātamādityasamaṃ sma loke tviṣā samaṃ pāvaka-bhānu-candraiḥ ||36||

37tataḥ kṣurapreṇa susaṃśitena suvarṇapuṅkhena hutāgnivarcasā
|
śriyā jvalantaṃ dhvajamunmamātha mahārathasyādhiratheḥ kirīṭī ||37||

38yaśaśca darpaśca tathā priyāṇi sarvāṇi kāryāṇi ca tena ketunā
|
sākaṃ kurūṇāṃ hṛ‌dayāni cāpatan babhūva hāheti ca niḥsvano mahān ||38||

39dṛṣṭvā dhvajaṃ pātitamāśukāriṇā kurupravīreṇa nikṛttamāhave
|
nāśaṃsire sūtaputrasya sarve jayaṃ tadā bhārata ye tvadīyāḥ ||39||

40atha tvaran karṇavadhāya pārtho mahendravajrānaladaṇḍasannibham
|
ādatta cāthāñjalikaṃ niṣaṅgātsahasraraśmeriva raśmimuttamam ||40||

41marmacchidaṃ śoṇitamāṃsadigdhaṃ vaiśvānarārkapratimaṃ mahārham
|
narāśvanāgāsuharaṃ tryaratniṃ ṣaḍvājamañjogatimugravegam ||41||

42sahasranetrāśanitulyavīryaṃ kālānalaṃ vyāttamivātighoram
|
pinākanārāyaṇacakrasannibhaṃ bhayaṅkaraṃ prāṇabhṛtāṃ vināśanam ||42||

43jagrāha pārthaḥ sa śaraṃ prahṛṣṭo yo devasaṅghairapi durnivāryaḥ
|
saṃpūjito yaḥ satataṃ mahātmā devāsurānyo vijayenmaheṣuḥ ||43||

44taṃ vai pramṛṣṭaṃ prasamīkṣya yuddhe cacāla sarvaṃ sacarācaraṃ jagat
|
svasti jagatsyādṛṣayaḥ pracukruśustamudyataṃ prekṣya mahāhaveṣum ||44||

45tatastu taṃ vai śaramaprameyaṃ gāṇḍīvadhanvā dhanuṣi vyayojayat
|
yuktvā mahāstreṇa pareṇa cāpaṃ vikṛṣya gāṇḍīvamuvāca satvaram ||45||

46ayaṃ mahāstraprahito mahāśaraḥ śarīrahṛccāsuharaśca durhṛdaḥ
|
tapo'sti taptaṃ guravaśca toṣitā mayā yadīṣṭaṃ suhṛdāṃ śrutaṃ tathā ||46||

47anena satyena nihantvayaṃ śaraḥ susaṃśritaḥ karṇamariṃ mamorjitam
|
ityūcivāṃstaṃ pramumoca bāṇaṃ dhanañjayaḥ karṇavadhāya ghoram ||47||

48kṛtyāmatharvāṅgirasīmivogrāṃ dīptāmasahyāṃ yudhi mṛtyunāpi
|
bruvan kirīṭī tamatiprahṛṣṭo hyayaṃ śaro me vijayāvaho'stu ||48||

49jighāṃsurarkendusamaprabhāvaḥ karṇaṃ mayāsto nayatāṃ yamāya
|
teneṣuvaryeṇa kirīṭamālī prahṛṣṭarūpo vijayāvahena ||49||

50jighāṃsurarkendusamaprabheṇa cakre viṣaktaṃ ripumātatāyī
|
tathā vimukto balinā'rkatejāḥ prajvālayāmāsa diśo nabhaśca
|
tato'rjunastasya śiro jahāra vṛtrasya vajreṇa yathā mahendraḥ ||50||

51śarottamenāñjalikena rājaṃstadā mahāstrapratimantritena
|
pārtho'parāhṇe śira uccakarta vaikartanasyātha mahendrasūnuḥ ||51||

52tatprāpataccāñjalikena cchinnamapāsya kāyo nipapāta paścāt
|
tadudyatādityasamānatejasaṃ śarannabhomadhyagabhāskaropamam ||52||

53varāṅgamurvyāmapataccamūmukhe divākaro'stādiva raktamaṇḍalaḥ
|
tato'sya dehaṃ satataṃ sukhocitaṃ surūpamatyarthamudārakarmaṇaḥ ||53||

54pareṇa kṛcchreṇa śiraḥ samatyajadgṛhaṃ mahardhīva susaṅgamīśvaraḥ
|
śarairvibhinnaṃ vyasu tatsuvarcasaḥ papāta karṇasya śarīramucchritam ||54||

55sravadvraṇaṃ gairikatoyavisravaṃ gireryathā vajrahataṃ mahāśiraḥ
|
dehācca karṇasya nipātitasya tejaḥ sūryaṃ khaṃ vitatyāviveśa ||55||

56tada‌dbhutaṃ sarvamanuṣyayodhāḥ saṃdṛṣṭavanto nihate sma karṇe
|
tataḥ śaṅkhān pāṇḍavā dadhmuruccairdṛṣṭvā karṇaṃ pātitaṃ phālgunena ||56||

57tathaiva kṛṣṇaśca dhanañjayaśca hṛṣṭau yamau dadhmaturvārijātau
|
taṃ somakāḥ prekṣya hataṃ śayānaṃ sainyaiḥ sārdhaṃ siṃhanādānpracakuḥ ||57||

58tūryāṇi sañjaghnuratīva hṛṣṭā vāsāṃsi caivādudhuvurbhujāṃśca
|
saṃvardhayantaśca narendrayodhāḥ pārthaṃ samājagmuratīva hṛṣṭāḥ ||58||

59balānvitāścāpare hyapyanṛtyannanyonyamāśliṣya nadanta ūcuḥ
|
dṛṣṭvā tu karṇaṃ bhuvi vā vipannaṃ kṛttaṃ rathātsāyakairarjunasya ||59||

60mahānilenādrimivāpaviddhaṃ yajñāvasāne'gnimiva praśāntam
|
rarāja karṇasya śiro nikṛttamastaṃ gataṃ bhāskarasyeva bimbam ||60||

61śarairācitasarvāṅgaḥ śoṇitaughapariplutaḥ
|
vibhāti dehaḥ karṇasya svaraśmibhirivāṃśumān ||61||

62pratāpya senāmāmitrīṃ dīptaiḥ śaragabhastibhiḥ
|
balinā'rjunakālena nīto'staṃ karṇabhāskaraḥ ||62||

63astaṃ gacchanyathādityaḥ prabhāmādāya gacchati
|
tathā jīvitamādāya karṇasyeṣurjagāma saḥ
|
aparāhṇe'parāhṇo'sya sūtaputrasya māriṣa ||63||

64chinnamañjalikenājau sotsedhamapatacchiraḥ
|
uparyupari sainyānāmasya śatrostadañjasā
|
śiraḥ karṇasya sotsedhamiṣuḥ so'pyaharaddrutam ||64||

65karṇaṃ tu śūraṃ patitaṃ pṛthivyāṃ śarācitaṃ śoṇitadigdhagātram
|
dṛṣṭvā śayānaṃ bhuvi madrarājaśchinnadhvajenātha yayau rathena ||65||

66hate karṇe kuravaḥ prādravanta bhayārditā gāḍhaviddhāśca saṃkhye
|
avekṣamāṇā muhurarjunasya dhvajaṃ mahāntaṃ vapuṣā jvalantam ||66||

67sahasranetrapratimānakarmaṇaḥ sahasrapatrapratimānanaṃ śubham
|
sahasraraśmirdinasaṃkṣaye yathā tathā'patatkarṇaśiro vasundharām ||67||
