Mahābhārata 13.147 (Anuśāsanaparvan)

1ṛṣaya ūcuḥ |
pinākin bhaganetraghna sarvalokanamaskṛta
|
māhātmyaṃ vāsudevasya śrotumicchāmi śaṅkara ||1||

2īśvara uvāca|
pitāmahādapi varaḥ śāśvataḥ puruṣo hariḥ
|
kṛṣṇo jāmbūnadābhāso vyabhre sūrya ivoditaḥ ||2||

3daśabāhurmahātejā devatāriniṣūdanaḥ
|
śrīvatsāṅko hṛṣīkeśaḥ sarvadaivatapūjitaḥ ||3||

4brahmā tasyodarabhavastasyāhaṃ ca śirobhavaḥ
|
śiroruhebhyo jyotīṃṣi romabhyaśca surāsurāḥ ||4||

5ṛṣayo dehasambhūtāstasya lokāśca śāśvatāḥ
|
pitāmahagṛhaṃ sākṣātsarvadevagṛhaṃ ca saḥ ||5||

6so'syāḥ pṛthivyāḥ kṛtsnāyāḥ sraṣṭā tribhuvaneśvaraḥ
|
saṃhartā caiva bhūtānāṃ sthāvarasya carasya ca ||6||

7sa hi devavaraḥ sākṣāddevanāthaḥ paraṃtapaḥ
|
sarvajñaḥ sarvasaṃśliṣṭaḥ sarvagaḥ sarvatomukhaḥ ||7||

8paramātmā hṛṣīkeśaḥ sarvavyāpī maheśvaraḥ
|
na tasmātparamaṃ bhūtaṃ triṣu lokeṣu kiñcana ||8||

9sanātano vai madhuhā govinda iti viśrutaḥ
|
sa sarvānpārthivānsaṃkhye ghātayiṣyati mānadaḥ ||9||

10surakāryārthamutpanno mānuṣaṃ vapurāsthitaḥ
|
na hi devagaṇāḥ śaktāstrivikramavinākṛtāḥ ||10||

11bhuvane devakāryāṇi kartuṃ nāyakavarjitāḥ
|
nāyakaḥ sarvabhūtānāṃ sarvadevanamaskṛtaḥ ||11||

12etasya devanāthasya devakāryaparasya ca
|
brahmabhūtasya satataṃ brahmarṣiśaraṇasya ca ||12||

13brahmā vasati garbhasthaḥ śarīre sukhasaṃsthitaḥ
|
śarvaḥ sukhaṃ saṃśritaśca śarīre sukhasaṃsthitaḥ ||13||

14sarvāḥ sukhaṃ saṃśritāśca śarīre tasya devatāḥ
|
sa devaḥ puṇḍarīkākṣaḥ śrīgarbhaḥ śrīsahoṣitaḥ ||14||

15śārṅgacakrāyudhaḥ khaḍgī sarvanāgaripudhvajaḥ
|
uttamena sa śīlena damena ca śamena ca ||15||

16parākrameṇa vīryeṇa vapuṣā darśanena ca
|
āroheṇa pramāṇena dhairyeṇārjavasaṃpadā ||16||

17ānṛśaṃsyena rūpeṇa balena ca samanvitaḥ
|
astraiḥ samuditaḥ sarvairdivyairadbhutadarśanaiḥ ||17||

18yogamāyaḥ sahasrākṣo nirapāyo mahāmanāḥ
|
vīro mitrajanaślāghī jñātibandhujanapriyaḥ ||18||

19kṣamāvāṃścānahaṃvādī brahmaṇyo brahmanāyakaḥ
|
bhayahartā bhayārtānāṃ mitrāṇāṃ nandivardhanaḥ ||19||

20śaraṇyaḥ sarvabhūtānāṃ dīnānāṃ pālane rataḥ
|
śrutavānarthasaṃpannaḥ sarvabhūtanamaskṛtaḥ ||20||

21samāśritānāṃ varadaḥ śatrūṇāmapi dharmavit
|
nītijño nītisaṃpanno brahmavādī jitendriyaḥ ||21||

22bhavārthamiha devānāṃ buddhyā paramayā yutaḥ
|
prājāpatye śubhe mārge mānave dharmasaṃskṛte ||22||

23samutpatsyati govindo manorvaṃśe mahātmanaḥ
|
aṅgo nāma manoḥ putro antardhāmā tataḥ paraḥ ||23||

24antardhāmno havirdhāmā prajāpatiraninditaḥ
|
prācīnabarhirbhavitā havirdhāmnaḥ suto mahān ||24||

25tasya pracetaḥpramukhā bhaviṣyanti daśātmajāḥ
|
prācetasastathā dakṣo bhaviteha prajāpatiḥ ||25||

26dākṣāyaṇyāstathā''dityo manurādityatastathā
|
manośca vaṃśaja ilā sudyumnaśca bhaviṣyati ||26||

27budhātpurūravāścāpi tasmādāyurbhaviṣyati
|
nahuṣo bhavitā tasmādyayātistasya cātmajaḥ ||27||

28yadustasmānmahāsattvaḥ kroṣṭā tasmādbhaviṣyati
|
kroṣṭuścaiva mahānputro vṛjinīvānbhaviṣyati ||28||

29vṛjinīvataśca bhavitā uṣa‌ṅguraparājitaḥ
|
upa‌ṅgorbhavitā putraḥ śūraścitrarathastathā ||29||

30tasya tvavarajaḥ putraḥ śūro nāma bhaviṣyati
|
teṣāṃ vikhyātavīryāṇāṃ caritraguṇaśālinām ||30||

31yajvanāṃ suviśuddhānāṃ vaṃśe brāhmaṇasaṃmate
|
sa śūraḥ kṣatriyaśreṣṭho mahāvīryo mahāyaśāḥ
|
svavaṃśavistarakaraṃ janayiṣyati mānadaḥ ||31||

32vasudeva iti khyātaṃ putramānakadundubhim
|
tasya putraścaturbāhurvāsudevo bhaviṣyati ||32||

33dātā brāhmaṇasatkartā brahmabhūto dvijapriyaḥ
|
rājño māgadhasaṃruddhān mokṣayiṣyati yādavaḥ ||33||

34jarāsandhaṃ tu rājānaṃ nirjitya girigahvare
|
sarvapārthivaratnāḍhyo bhaviṣyati sa vīryavān ||34||

35pṛthivyāmapratihato vīryeṇa ca bhaviṣyati
|
vikrameṇa ca saṃpannaḥ sarvapārthivapārthivaḥ ||35||

36śūraseneṣu bhūtvā sa dvārakāyāṃ vasanprabhuḥ
|
pālayiṣyati gāṃ devīṃ vijitya nayavitsadā ||36||

37taṃ bhavantaḥ samāsādya vāṅmālyairarhaṇairvaraiḥ
|
arcayantu yathānyāyaṃ brahmāṇamiva śāśvatam ||37||

38yo hi māṃ draṣṭumiccheta brahmāṇaṃ ca pitāmaham
|
draṣṭavyastena bhagavānvāsudevaḥ pratāpavān ||38||

39dṛṣṭe tasminnahaṃ dṛṣṭo na me'trāsti vicāraṇā
|
pitāmaho vā deveśa iti vitta tapodhanāḥ ||39||

40sa yasya puṇḍarīkākṣaḥ prītiyukto bhaviṣyati
|
tasya devagaṇaḥ prīto brahmapūrvo bhaviṣyati ||40||

41yaśca taṃ mānave loke saṃśrayiṣyati keśavam
|
tasya kīrtirjayaścaiva svargaścaiva bhaviṣyati ||41||

42dharmāṇāṃ deśikaḥ sākṣāt sa bhaviṣyati dharmabhāk
|
dharmavadbhiḥ sa deveśo namaskāryaḥ sadodyataiḥ ||42||

43dharma eva paro hi syāttasminnabhyarcite vibhau
|
sa hi devo mahātejāḥ prajāhitacikīrṣayā ||43||

44dharmārthaṃ puruṣavyāghra ṛṣikoṭīḥ sasarja ha
|
tāḥ sṛṣṭāstena vibhunā parvate gandhamādane ||44||

45sanatkumārapramukhāstiṣṭhanti tapasānvitāḥ
|
tasmātsa vāgmī dharmajño namasyo dvijapuṅgavāḥ ||45||

46divi śreṣṭho hi bhagavānharirnārāyaṇaḥ prabhuḥ
|
vandito hi sa vandeta mānito mānayīta ca
|
arhitaścārhayennityaṃ pūjitaḥ pratipūjayet ||46||

47dṛṣṭaḥ paśyedaharahaḥ saṃśritaḥ pratisaṃśrayet
|
arcitaścārcayennityaṃ sa devo dvijasattamāḥ ||47||

48etattasyānavadyasya viṣṇorvai paramaṃ vratam
|
ādidevasya mahataḥ sajjanācaritaṃ sadā ||48||

49bhuvane'bhyarcito nityaṃ devairapi sanātanaḥ
|
abhayenānurūpeṇa yujyante tamanuvratāḥ ||49||

50karmaṇā manasā vācā sa namasyo dvijaiḥ sadā
|
yatnavadbhirupasthāya draṣṭavyo devakīsutaḥ ||50||

51eṣa vo'bhihito mārgo mayā vai munisattamāḥ
|
taṃ dṛṣṭvā sarvaśo devaṃ dṛṣṭāḥ syuḥ surasattamāḥ ||51||

52mahāvarāhaṃ taṃ devaṃ sarvalokapitāmaham
|
ahaṃ caiva namasyāmi nityameva jagatpatim ||52||

53tatra ca tritayaṃ dṛṣṭaṃ bhaviṣyati na saṃśayaḥ
|
samastā hi vayaṃ devāstasya dehe vasāmahe ||53||

54tasya caivāgrajo bhrātā sitādrinicayaprabhaḥ
|
halī bala iti khyāto bhaviṣyati dharādharaḥ ||54||

55triśirāstasya divyaśca śātakuṃbhamayo drumaḥ
|
dhvajastṛṇendro devasya bhaviṣyati rathāśritaḥ ||55||

56śironāgairmahābhogaiḥ parikīrṇaṃ mahātmabhiḥ
|
bhaviṣyati mahābāhoḥ sarvalokeśvarasya ca ||56||

57cintitāni sameṣyanti śastrāṇyastrāṇi caiva ha
|
anantaśca sa evokto bhagavān hariravyayaḥ ||57||

58samādiṣṭaśca vibudhairdarśaya tvāmiti prabho
|
suparṇo yasya vīryeṇa kaśyapasyātmajo balī
|
antaṃ naivāśakaddraṣṭuṃ devasya paramātmanaḥ ||58||

59sa ca śeṣo vicarate parayā vai mudā yutaḥ
|
antarvasati bhogena parirabhya vasundharām ||59||

60ya eva viṣṇuḥ so'nanto bhagavān vasudhādharaḥ
|
yo rāmaḥ sa hṛṣīkeśo yo'cyutaḥ sa dharādharaḥ ||60||

61tāvubhau puruṣavyāghrau divyau divyaparākramau
|
draṣṭavyau mānanīyau ca cakralāṅgaladhāriṇau ||61||

62eṣa vo'nugrahaḥ prokto mayā puṇyastapodhanāḥ
|
yadbhavanto yaduśreṣṭhaṃ pūjayeyuḥ prayatnataḥ ||62||
