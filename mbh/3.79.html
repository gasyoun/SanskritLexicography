Mahābhārata 3.79 (Āraṇyakaparvan)

1bṛhadaśva uvāca| praśānte tu pure hṛṣṭe saṃpravṛtte mahotsave
|
mahatyā senayā rājā damayantīmupānayat||1||

2damayantīmapi pitā satkṛtya paravīrahā
|
prāsthāpayadameyātmā bhīmo bhīmaparākramaḥ||2||

3āgatāyāṃ tu vaidarbhyāṃ saputrāyāṃ nalo nṛpaḥ
|
vartayāmāsa mudito devarāḍiva nandane||3||

4tathā prakāśatāṃ yāto jambūdvīpe sa rājasu
|
punaḥ śaśāsā tadrājyaṃ pratyāhṛtya mahāyaśāḥ||4||

5īje ca vividhairyajñairvidhivaccāptadakṣiṇaiḥ
|
tathā tvamapi rājendra sasuhṛdvakṣyase'cirāt||5||

6duḥkhametādṛśaṃ prāpto nalaḥ parapurañjayaḥ
|
devanena naraśreṣṭha sabhāryo bharatarṣabha||6||

7ekākinaiva sumahannalena pṛthivīpate
|
duḥkhamāsāditaṃ ghoraṃ prāptaścābhyudayaḥ punaḥ||7||

8tvaṃ punarbhrātṛsahitaḥ kṛṣṇayā caiva pāṇḍava
|
ramase'sminmahāraṇye dharmamevānucintayan||8||

9brāhmaṇaiśca mahābhāgairvedavedāṅgapāragaiḥ
|
nityamanvāsyase rājaṃstatra kā paridevanā||9||

10karkoṭakasya nāgasya damayantyā nalasya ca
|
ṛtuparṇasya rājarṣeḥ kīrtanaṃ kalināśanam||10||

11itihāsamimaṃ cāpi kalināśanamacyuta
|
śakyamāśvasituṃ śrutvā tvadvidhena viśāṃpate||11||

12asthiratvaṃ ca sañcintya puruṣārthasya nityadā
|
tasyodaye vyaye cāpi na cintayitumarhasi||12||

13śrutvetihāsaṃ nṛpate samāśvasihi mā śucaḥ
|
vyasane tvaṃ mahārāja na viṣīditumarhasi||13||

14viṣamāvasthite daive pauruṣe'phalatāṃ gate
|
viṣādayanti nātmānaṃ sattvopāśrayiṇo narāḥ||14||

15ye cedaṃ kathayiṣyanti nalasya caritaṃ mahat
|
śroṣyanti cāpyabhīkṣṇaṃ vai nālakṣmīstānbhajiṣyati||15||

16arthāstasyopapatsyante dhanyatāṃ ca gamiṣyati
|
itihāsamimaṃ śrutvā purāṇaṃ śaśvaduttamam||16||

17putrānpautrānpaśūṃścāpi labhate nṛṣu cāgryatām
|
ārogyaprītimāṃścaiva bhaviṣyati na saṃśayaḥ||17||

18bhayāttrāsyasi yacca tvamāhvayiṣyati māṃ punaḥ
|
akṣajña iti tatte'haṃ nāśayiṣyāmi pārthiva||18||

19vedākṣahṛdayaṃ kṛtsnamahaṃ satyaparākrama
|
upapadyasva kaunteya prasanno'haṃ bravīmi te||19||

20vaiśampāyana uvāca|
tato hṛṣṭamanā rājā bṛhadaśvamuvāca ha
|
bhagavannakṣahṛdayaṃ jñātumicchāmi tattvataḥ||20||

21tato'kṣahṛdayaṃ prādātpāṇḍavāya mahātmane
|
dattvā cāśvaśiro'gacchadupaspraṣṭuṃ mahātapāḥ||21||

22bṛhadaśve gate pārthamaśrauṣītsavyasācinam
|
vartamanaṃ tapasyugre vāyubhakṣaṃ manīṣiṇam||22||

23brāhmaṇebhyastapasvibhyaḥ sampatadbhyastatastataḥ
|
tīrthaśailavanebhyaśca sametebhyo dṛḍhavrataḥ||23||

24iti pārtho mahābāhurdurāpaṃ tapa āsthitaḥ
|
na tathā dṛṣṭapūrvo'nyaḥ kaścidugratapā iti||24||

25yathā dhanañjayaḥ pārthastapasvī niyatavrataḥ
|
munirekacaraḥ śrīmāndharmo vigrahavāniva||25||

26taṃ śrutvā pāṇḍavo rājaṃstapyamānaṃ mahāvane
|
anvaśocata kaunteyaḥ priyaṃ vai bhrātaraṃ jayam||26||

27dahyamānena tu hṛdā śaraṇārthī mahāvane
|
brāhmaṇānvividhajñānānparyapṛcchadyudhiṣṭhiraḥ||27||
