Mahābhārata 5.191 (Udyogaparvan)

1bhīṣma uvāca|
tataḥ śikhaṇḍino mātā yathātattvaṃ narādhipa
|
ācacakṣe mahābāho bhartre kanyāṃ śikhaṇḍinīm ||1||

2aputrayā mayā rājan sapatnīnāṃ bhayādidam
|
kanyā śikhaṇḍinī jātā puruṣo vai niveditā ||2||

3tvayā caiva naraśreṣṭha tanme prītyā'numoditam
|
putrakarma kṛtaṃ caiva kanyāyāḥ pārthivarṣabha ||3||

4bhāryā coḍhā tvayā rājan daśārṇādhipateḥ sutā
|
mayā ca pratyabhihitaṃ devavākyārthadarśanāt
|
kanyā bhūtvā pumān bhāvītyevaṃ caitadupekṣitam ||4||

5etacchrutvā drupado yajñasenaḥ sarvaṃ tattvaṃ mantravidbhyo nivedya
|
mantraṃ rājā mantrayāmāsa rājan yathāyuktaṃ rakṣaṇe vai prajānām ||5||

6sambandhakaṃ caiva samarthya tasmin dāśārṇake vai nṛpatau narendra
|
svayaṃ kṛtvā vipralambhaṃ yathāvanmantraikāgro niścayaṃ vai jagāma ||6||

7svabhāvaguptaṃ nagaramāpatkāle tu bhārata
|
gopayāmāsa rājendra sarvataḥ samalaṅkṛtam ||7||

8ārtiṃ ca paramāṃ rājā jagāma saha bhāryayā
|
daśārṇapatinā sārdhaṃ virodhe bharatarṣabha ||8||

9kathaṃ sambandhinā sārdhaṃ na me syādvigraho mahān
|
iti sañcintya manasā devatāmarcayattadā ||9||

10taṃ tu dṛṣṭvā tadā rājan devī devaparaṃ tadā
|
arcāṃ prayuñjānamatho bhāryā vacanamabravīt ||10||

11devānāṃ pratipattiśca satyaṃ sādhumatā satām
|
kimu duḥkhārṇavaṃ prāpya tasmādarcayatāṃ gurūn ||11||

12daivatāni ca sarvāṇi pūjyantāṃ bhūridakṣiṇam
|
agnayaścāpi hūyantāṃ dāśārṇapratiṣedhane ||12||

13ayuddhena nivṛttiṃ ca manasā cintaya prabho
|
devatānāṃ prasādena sarvametadbhaviṣyati ||13||

14mantribhirmantritaṃ sārdhaṃ tvayā pṛthulalocana
|
purasyāsyāvināśāya yacca rājaṃstathā kuru ||14||

15daivaṃ hi mānuṣopetaṃ bhṛśaṃ siddhyati pārthiva
|
parasparavirodhāddhi siddhirasti na caitayoḥ ||15||

16tasmādvidhāya nagare vidhānaṃ sacivaiḥ saha
|
arcayasva yathākāmaṃ daivatāni viśāṃpate ||16||

17evaṃ saṃbhāṣamāṇau tu dṛṣṭvā śokaparāyaṇau
|
śikhaṇḍinī tadā kanyā vrīḍiteva tapasvinī ||17||

18tataḥ sā cintayāmāsa matkṛte duḥkhitāvubhau
|
imāviti tataścakre matiṃ prāṇavināśane ||18||

19evaṃ sā niścayaṃ kṛtvā bhṛśaṃ śokaparāyaṇā
|
nirjagāma gṛhaṃ tyaktvā gahanaṃ nirjanaṃ vanam ||19||

20yakṣeṇarddhimatā rājan sthūṇākarṇena pālitam
|
tadbhayādeva ca jano visarjayati tadvanam ||20||

21tatra ca sthūṇabhavanaṃ sudhāṃmṛttikalepanam
|
lājollāpikadhūmāḍhyamuccaprākāratoraṇam ||21||

22tat praviśya śikhaṇḍī sā drupadasyātmajā nṛpa
|
anaśnānā bahutithaṃ śarīramudaśoṣayat ||22||

23darśayāmāsa tāṃ yakṣaḥ sthūṇo mārdavasaṃyutaḥ
|
kimartho'yaṃ tavāraṃbhaḥ kariṣye brūhi mā ciram ||23||

24aśakyamiti sā yakṣaṃ punaḥ punaruvāca ha
|
kariṣyāmīti vai kṣipraṃ pratyuvācātha guhyakaḥ ||24||

25dhaneśvarasyānucaro varado'smi nṛpātmaje
|
adeyamapi dāsyāmi brūhi yatte vivakṣitam ||25||

26tataḥ śikhaṇḍī tat sarvamakhilena nyavedayat
|
tasmai yakṣapradhānāya sthūṇākarṇāya bhārata ||26||

27śikhaṇḍyuvāca
|
aputro me pitā yakṣa na cirānnāśameṣyati
|
abhiyāsyati sakrodho daśārṇādhipatirhi tam ||27||

28mahābalo mahotsāhaḥ sahemakavaco nṛpaḥ
|
tasmādrakṣasva māṃ yakṣa mātaraṃ pitaraṃ ca me ||28||

29pratijñāto hi bhavatā duḥkhapratiśamo mama
|
bhaveyaṃ puruṣo yakṣa tvatprasādādaninditaḥ ||29||

30yāvadeva sa rājā vai nopayāti puraṃ mama
|
tāvadeva mahāyakṣa prasādaṃ kuru guhyaka ||30||
