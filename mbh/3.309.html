Mahābhārata 3.309 (Āraṇyakaparvan)

1vaiśampāyana uvāca|
etasminneva kāle tu dhṛtarāṣṭrasya vai sakhā
|
suto'dhiratha ityeva sadāro jāhnavīṃ yayau||1||

2tasya bhāryā'bhavadrājan rūpeṇāsadṛśī bhuvi
|
rādhā nāma mahābhāgā na sā putramavindata||2||

3apatyārthe paraṃ yatnamakarocca viśeṣataḥ
|
sā dadarśātha mañjūṣāguhyamānāṃ yadṛcchayā||3||

4dattarakṣāpratisarāmanvālambhanaśobhanām
|
ūrmītaraṅgairjāhnavyāḥ samānītāmupahvaram||4||

5sā tu kautūhalāt prāptāṃ grāhayāmāsa bhāvinī
|
tato nivedayāmāsa sūtasyādhirathasya vai||5||

6saṃ tāmuddhṛtya mañjūṣāmutsārya jalamantikāt
|
yantrairudghāṭayāmāsa so'paśyat tatra bālakam||6||

7taruṇādityasaṅkāśaṃ hemavarmadharaṃ tathā
|
mṛṣṭakuṇḍalayuktena vadanena virājatā||7||

8sa sūto bhāryayā sārdhaṃ vismayotphullalocanaḥ
|
aṅkamāropya taṃ bālaṃ bhāryāṃ vacanamabravīt||8||

9idamatyadbhutaṃ bhīru yatto jāto'smi bhāvinī
|
dṛṣṭavān devagarbho'yaṃ manye'smākamupāgataḥ||9||

10anapatyasya putro'yaṃ devairdatto dhruvaṃ mama
|
ityuktvā taṃ dadau putraṃ rādhāyai sa mahīpate||10||

11pratijagrāha taṃ rādhā vidhivaddivyarūpiṇam
|
putraṃ kamalagarbhābhaṃ devagarbhaṃ śriyā vṛtam||11||

12pupoṣa cainaṃ vidhivadvavṛdhe sa ca vīryavān
|
tataḥprabhṛti cāpyanye prābhavannaurasāḥ sutāḥ||12||

13vasuvarmadharaṃ dṛṣṭvā taṃ bālaṃ hemakuṇḍalam
|
nāmāsya vasuṣeṇeti tataścakrurdvijātayaḥ||13||

14evaṃ sa sūtaputratvaṃ jagāmāmitavikramaḥ
|
vasuṣeṇa iti khyāto vṛṣa ityeva ca prabhuḥ||14||

15sūtasya vavṛdhe'ṅgeṣu śreṣṭhaḥ putraḥ sa vīryavān
|
cāreṇa viditaścāsīt pṛthayā divyavarmabhṛt||15||

16sūtastvadhirathaḥ putraṃ vivṛddhaṃ samayena tam
|
dṛṣṭvā prasthāpayāmāsa puraṃ vāraṇasāhvayam||16||

17tatropasadanaṃ cakre droṇasyeṣvastrakarmaṇi
|
sakhyaṃ duryodhanenaivamagamat sa ca vīryavān||17||

18droṇāt kṛpācca rāmācca so'stragrāmaṃ caturvidham
|
labdhvā loke'bhavat khyātaḥ parameṣvāsatāṃ gataḥ||18||

19sandhāya dhārtarāṣṭreṇa pārthānāṃ vipriye rataḥ
|
yoddhumāśaṃsate nityaṃ phālgunena mahātmanā||19||

20sadā hi tasya spardhā''sīdarjunena viśāmpate
|
arjunasya ca karṇena yato dṛṣṭo babhūva saḥ||20||

21etadguhyaṃ mahārāja sūryasyāsīnna saṃśayaḥ
|
yaḥ sūryasambhavaḥ karṇaḥ kuntyāṃ sūtakule tathā||21||

22taṃ tu kuṇḍalinaṃ dṛṣṭvā varmaṇā ca samanvitam
|
avadhyaṃ samare matvā paryatapyadyudhiṣṭhiraḥ||22||

23yadā ca karṇo rājendra bhānumantaṃ divākaram
|
stauti madhyandine prāpte prāñjaliḥ salilotthitaḥ||23||

24tatrainamupatiṣṭhanti brāhmaṇā dhanahetunā
|
nādeyaṃ tasya tatkāle kiñcidasti dvijātiṣu||24||

25tamindro brāhmaṇo bhūtvā bhikṣāṃ dehītyupasthitaḥ
|
svāgataṃ ceti rādheyastamatha pratyabhāṣata||25||
