Mahābhārata 5.145 (Udyogaparvan)

1karṇa uvāca|
rādheyo'hamādhirathiḥ karṇastvāmabhivādaye
|
prāptā kimarthaṃ bhavatī brūhi kiṃ karavāṇi te ||1||

2kuntyuvāca
|
kaunteyastvaṃ na rādheyo na tavādhirathaḥ pitā
|
nāsi sūtakule jātaḥ karṇa tadviddhi me vacaḥ||2||

3kānīnastvaṃ mayā jātaḥ pūrvajaḥ kukṣiṇā dhṛtaḥ
|
kuntirājasya bhavane pārthastvamasi putraka ||3||

4prakāśakarmā tapano yo'yaṃ devo virocanaḥ
|
ajījanattvāṃ mayyeṣa karṇa śastrabhṛtāṃ varam ||4||

5kuṇḍalī baddhakavaco devagarbhaḥ śriyā vṛtaḥ
|
jātastvamasi durdharṣa mayā putra piturgṛhe ||5||

6sa tvaṃ bhrātṝnasambuddhya mohādyadupasevase
|
dhārtarāṣṭrānna tadyuktaṃ tvayi putra viśeṣataḥ ||6||

7etaddharmaphalaṃ putra narāṇāṃ dharmaniścaye
|
yattuṣyantyasya pitaro mātā cāpyekada‌rśinī ||7||

8arjunenārjitāṃ pūrvaṃ hṛtāṃ lobhāda‌sādhubhiḥ
|
ācchidya dhārtarāṣṭrebhyo bhuṅkṣva yaudhiṣṭhirīṃ śriyam ||8||

9adya paśyanti kuravaḥ karṇārjunasamāgamam
|
saubhrātreṇa samālakṣya saṃnamantāmasādhavaḥ ||9||

10karṇārjunau vai bhavetāṃ yathā rāmajanārdanau
|
asādhyaṃ kiṃ nu loke syādyuvayoḥ saṃhitātmanoḥ ||10||

11karṇa śobhiṣyase nūnaṃ pañcabhirbhrātṛ‌bhirvṛtaḥ
|
devaiḥ parivṛto brahmā vedyāmiva mahādhvare ||11||

12upapanno guṇaiḥ sarvairjyeṣṭhaḥ śreṣṭheṣu bandhuṣu
|
sūtaputreti mā śabdaḥ pārthastvamasi vīryavān ||12||
