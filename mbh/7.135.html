Mahābhārata 7.135 (Droṇaparvan)

1dhṛtarāṣṭra uvāca|
daivameva paraṃ manye dhikpauruṣamanarthakam
|
yatrādhirathirāyatto nātaratpāṇḍavaṃ raṇe ||1||

2karṇaḥ pārthānsagoviṃdān jetumutsahate raṇe
|
na ca karṇasamaṃ yodhaṃ loke paśyāmi kañcana||2||

3iti duryodhanasyāhamaśrauṣaṃ jalpato muhuḥ
|
karṇo hi balavāñchūro dṛḍhadhanvā jitaklamaḥ||3||

4iti māmabravītsūta mando duryodhanaḥ purā
|
vasuṣeṇasahāyaṃ māṃ nālaṃ devāpi saṃyuge ||4||

5kiṃ nu pāṃḍusutā rājan gatasattvā vicetasaḥ
|
tatra taṃ nirjitaṃ dṛṣṭvā bhujaṅgamiva nirviṣam ||5||

6yuddhātkarṇamapakrāntaṃ kiṃsvidduryodhano'bravīt
|
aho durmukhamevaikaṃ yuddhānāmaviśāradam||6||

7prāveśayaddhutavahaṃ pataṅgamiva mohitaḥ
|
aśvatthāmā madrarājaḥ kṛpaḥ karṇaśca saṃgatāḥ||7||

8na śaktāḥ pramukhe sthātuṃ nūnaṃ bhīmasya sañjaya
|
te'pi cāsya mahāghoraṃ balaṃ nāgāyutopamam||8||

9jānanto vyavasāyaṃ ca krūraṃ mārutatejasaḥ
|
kimarthaṃ krūrakarmāṇaṃ yamakālāntakopamam||9||

10balasaṃraṃbhavīryajñāḥ kopayiṣyanti saṃyuge
|
karṇastveko mahābāhuḥ svabāhubaladarpitam ||10||

11bhīmasenamanādṛtya raṇe'yudhyata sūtajaḥ
|
yo'jayatsamare karṇaṃ puraṃdara ivāsuram ||11||

12na sa pāṇḍusuto jetuṃ śakyaḥ kenacidāhave
|
droṇaṃ yaḥ saṃpramathyaikaḥ praviṣṭo mama vāhinīm||12||

13bhīmo dhanañjayānveṣī kastamārcchejjijīviṣuḥ
|
ko hi saṃjaya bhīmasya sthātumutsahate'grataḥ||13||

14udyatāśanihastasya mahendrasyeva dānavaḥ
|
pretarājapuraṃ prāpya nivartetāpi mānavaḥ||14||

15na bhīmasenaṃ saṃprāpya nivarteta kadācana
|
pataṅgā iva vahniṃ te prāviśannalpacetasaḥ ||15||

16ye bhīmasenaṃ saṃkruddhamanvadhāvanvimohitāḥ
|
yattatsabhāyāṃ bhīmena mama putravadhāśrayam||16||

17uktaṃ saṃraṃbhiṇo'greṇa kurūṇāṃ śṛṇvatāṃ tadā
|
tannūnamabhisañcintya dṛṣṭvā karṇaṃ ca nirjitam||17||

18duḥśāsanaḥ saha bhrātrā bhayādbhīmādupāramat
|
yaśca saṃjaya durbuddhirabravītsamitau muhuḥ ||18||

19karṇo duḥśāsano'haṃ ca jeṣyāmo yudhi pāṇḍavān
|
sa nūnaṃ virathaṃ dṛṣṭvā karṇaṃ bhīmena nirjitam||19||

20pratyākhyānācca kṛṣṇasya bhṛśaṃ tapyati putrakaḥ
|
dṛṣṭvā bhrātṝnhatānsaṃkhye bhīmasenena daṃśitān ||20||

21ātmāparādhe sumahannūnaṃ tapyati putrakaḥ
|
ko hi jīvitamanvicchanpratīpaṃ pāṃḍavaṃ vrajet ||21||

22bhīmaṃ bhīmāyudhaṃ kruddhaṃ sākṣātkālamiva sthitam
|
vaḍavāmukhamadhyastho mucyetāpi hi mānavaḥ||22||

23na bhīmamukhasaṃprāpto mucyediti matirmama
|
na pārthā na ca pañcālā na ca keśavasātyakī||23||

24jānate yudhi saṃrabdhā jīvitaṃ parirakṣitum
|
aho mama sutānāṃ hi vipannaṃ sūta jīvitam||24||

25sañjaya uvāca|
yastvaṃ śocasi kauravya vartamāne mahābhaye
|
tvamasya jagato mūlaṃ vināśasya na saṃśayaḥ||25||

26svayaṃ vairaṃ mahat kṛtvā putrāṇāṃ vacane sthitaḥ
|
ucyamāno na gṛhṇīṣe martyaḥ pathyamivauṣadham||26||

27svayaṃ pītvā mahārāja kālakūṭaṃ sudurjaram
|
tasyedānīṃ phalaṃ kṛtsnamavāpnuhi narottama||27||

28yattu kutsayase yodhānyudhyamānānmahābalān
|
tatra te vartayiṣyāmi yathā yuddhamavartata||28||

29dṛṣṭvā karṇaṃ tu putrāste bhīmasenaparājitam
|
nāmṛṣyanta maheṣvāsāḥ sodaryāḥ paṃca bhārata||29||

30durmarṣaṇo duḥsahaśca durmado durdharo jayaḥ
|
pāṇḍavaṃ citrasannāhāstaṃ pratīpamupādravan||30||

31te samantānmahābāhuṃ parivārya vṛkodaram
|
diśaḥ śaraiḥ samāvṛṇvañśalabhānāmiva vrajaiḥ||31||

32āgacchatastānsahasā kumārāndevarūpiṇaḥ
|
pratijagrāha samare bhīmaseno hasanniva||32||

33tava dṛṣṭvā tu tanayān bhīmasenapurogamān
|
abhyavartata rādheyo bhīmasenaṃ mahābalam ||33||

34visṛjanviśikhāṃstīkṣṇān svarṇapuṅkhāñchilāśitān
|
taṃ tu bhīmo'bhyayāttūrṇaṃ vāryamāṇaḥ sutaistava ||34||

35kuravastu tataḥ karṇaṃ parivārya samantataḥ
|
avākiran bhīmasenaṃ śaraiḥ sannataparvabhiḥ||35||

36tānbāṇaiḥ pañcaviṃśatyā sāśvān rājannararṣabhān
|
sasūtān bhīmadhanuṣo bhīmo ninye yamakṣayam ||36||

37prāpatansyandanebhyaste sārdhaṃ sutairgatāsavaḥ
|
citrapuṣpadharā bhagnā vāteneva mahādrumāḥ||37||

38tatrādbhutamapaśyāma bhīmasenasya vikramam
|
saṃvāryādhirathiṃ bāṇairyajjaghāna tavātmajān ||38||

39sa vāryamāṇo bhīmena śitairbāṇaiḥ samantataḥ
|
sūtaputro mahārāja bhīmasenamavaikṣata ||39||

40taṃ bhīmasenaḥ saṃraṃbhātkrodhasaṃraktalocanaḥ
|
visphārya sumahaccāpaṃ muhuḥ karṇamavaikṣata ||40||
