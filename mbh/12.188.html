Mahābhārata 12.188 (Śāntiparvan)

1bhṛguruvāca |
asṛjadbrāhmaṇāneva pūrvaṃ brahmā prajāpatīn
|
ātmatejobhinirvṛttān bhāskarāgnisamaprabhān ||1||

2tataḥ satyaṃ ca dharmaṃ ca tapo brahma ca śāśvatam
|
ācāraṃ caiva śaucaṃ ca svargāya vidadhe prabhuḥ ||2||

3deva-dānava-gandharvā daityāsura-mahoragāḥ
|
yakṣa-rākṣasa-nāgāśca piśācā manujāstathā ||3||

4brāhmaṇāḥ kṣatriyā vaiṣyāḥ śūdrāśca dvijasattama
|
ye cānye bhūtasaṅghānāṃ varṇāstāṃścāpi nirmame ||4||

5brāhmaṇānāṃ sito varṇaḥ kṣatriyāṇāṃ tu lohitaḥ
|
vaiśyānāṃ pītako varṇaḥ śūdrāṇāmasitastathā ||5||

6bharadvāja uvāca |
cāturvarṇyasya varṇena yadi varṇo vibhidyate
|
sarveṣāṃ khalu varṇānāṃ dṛśyate varṇasaṃkaraḥ ||6||

7kāmaḥ krodho bhayaṃ lobhaḥ śokaścintā kṣudhā śramaḥ
|
sarveṣāṃ naḥ prabhavati kasmādvarṇo vibhidyate ||7||

8svedamūtrapurīṣāṇi śleṣmā pittaṃ saśoṇitam
|
tanuḥ kṣarati sarveṣāṃ kasmādvarṇo vibhajyate ||8||

9jaṅgamānāmasaṃkhyeyāḥ sthāvarāṇāṃ ca jātayaḥ
|
teṣāṃ vividhavarṇānāṃ kuto varṇaviniścayaḥ ||9||

10bhṛguruvāca |
na viśeṣo'sti varṇānāṃ sarvaṃ brāhmamidaṃ jagat
|
brahmaṇā pūrvasṛṣṭaṃ hi karmabhirvarṇatāṃ gatam ||10||

11kāmabhogapriyāstīkṣṇāḥ krodhanāḥ priyasāhasāḥ
|
tyaktasvadharmā raktāṅgāste dvijāḥ kṣatratāṃ gatāḥ ||11||

12gobhyo vṛttiṃ samāsthāya pītāḥ kṛṣyupajīvinaḥ
|
svadharmānnānutiṣṭhanti te dvijā vaiśyatāṃ gatāḥ ||12||

13hiṃsānṛtapriyā lubdhāḥ sarvakarmopajīvinaḥ
|
kṛṣṇāḥ śaucaparibhraṣṭāste dvijāḥ śūdratāṃ gatāḥ ||13||

14ityetaiḥ karmabhirvyastā dvijā varṇāntaraṃ gatāḥ
|
dharmo yajñakriyā teṣāṃ nityaṃ na pratiṣidhyate ||14||

15ityete caturo varṇā yeṣāṃ brāhmī sarasvatī
|
vihitā brahmaṇā pūrvaṃ lobhāttvajñānatāṃ gatāḥ ||15||

16brāhmaṇā brahmatantrasthāstapasteṣāṃ na naśyati
|
brahma dhārayatāṃ nityaṃ vratāni niyamāṃstathā ||16||

17brahma caiva paraṃ sṛṣṭaṃ ye na jānanti te'dvijāḥ
|
teṣāṃ bahuvidhāstvanyāstatra tatra hi jātayaḥ ||17||

18piśācā rākṣasāḥ pretā vividhā mlecchajātayaḥ
|
praṇaṣṭajñānavijñānāḥ svacchandācāraceṣṭitāḥ ||18||

19prajā brāhmaṇapasaṃskārāḥ svakarmakṛtaniścayāḥ
|
ṛṣibhiḥ svena tapasā sṛjyante cāpare'paraiḥ ||19||

20ādidevasamudbhūtā brahmamūlākṣayā'vyayā
|
sā sṛṣṭirmānasī nāma dharmatantraparāyaṇā ||20||
