Mahābhārata 6.65 (Bhīṣmaparvan)

1dhṛtarāṣṭra uvāca|
bhayaṃ me sumahajjātaṃ vismayaścaiva sañjaya
|
śrutvā pāṇḍukumārāṇāṃ karma devaiḥ suduṣkaram ||1||

2putrāṇāṃ ca parābhāvaṃ śrutvā sañjaya sarvaśaḥ
|
cintā me mahatī sūta bhaviṣyati kathaṃ tviti ||2||

3dhruvaṃ viduravākyāni dhakṣyanti hṛdayaṃ mama
|
yathā hi dṛśyate sarvaṃ daivayogena sañjaya ||3||

4yatra bhīṣmamukhān sarvān śastrajñān yodhasattamān
|
pāṇḍavānāmanīkeṣu yodhayanti prahāriṇaḥ ||4||

5kenāvadhyā mahātmānaḥ pāṇḍuputrā mahābalāḥ
|
kena dattavarāstāta kiṃ vā jñānaṃ vidanti te ||5||

6yena kṣayaṃ na gacchanti divi tārāgaṇā iva
|
punaḥ punarna mṛṣyāmi hataṃ sainyaṃ tu pāṇḍavaiḥ ||6||

7mayyeva daṇḍaḥ patati daivātparamadāruṇaḥ
|
yathā'vadhyāḥ pāṇḍusutā yathā vadhyāśca me sutāḥ ||7||

8etanme sarvamācakṣva yāthātathyena sañjaya
|
na hi pāraṃ prapaśyāmi duḥkhasyāsya kathañcana ||8||

9samudrasyeva mahato bhujābhyāṃ pratarannaraḥ
|
putrāṇāṃ vyasanaṃ manye dhruvaṃ prāptaṃ sudāruṇam ||9||

10ghātayiṣyati me sarvān putrān bhīmo na saṃśayaḥ
|
na hi paśyāmi taṃ vīraṃ yo me rakṣetsutān raṇe ||10||

11dhruvaṃ vināśaḥ saṃprāptaḥ putrāṇāṃ mama sañjaya
|
tasmānme kāraṇaṃ sūta śaktiṃ caiva viśeṣataḥ ||11||

12pṛcchato vai yathātattvaṃ sarvamākhyātumarhasi
|
duryodhanaśca yaccakre dṛṣṭvā svān vimukhān raṇe ||12||

13bhīṣmadroṇau kṛpaścaiva saubalaśca jayadrathaḥ
|
drauṇirvā'pi maheṣvāso vikarṇo vā mahābalaḥ ||13||

14niścayo vā'pi kasteṣāṃ tadā hyāsīnmahātmanām
|
vimukheṣu mahāprājña mama putreṣu sañjaya ||14||

15sañjaya uvāca|śṛṇu rājannavahitaḥ śrutvā caivāvadhāraya
|
naiva mantrakṛtaṃ kiñcinnaiva māyāṃ tathāvidhām ||15||

16na vai vibhīṣikāṃ kāñcidrājan kurvanti pāṇḍavāḥ
|
yudhyanti te yathānyāyaṃ śaktimantaśca saṃyuge ||16||

17dharmeṇa sarvakāryāṇi jīvitādīni bhārata
|
ārabhante sadā pārthāḥ prārthayānā mahadyaśaḥ ||17||

18na te yuddhānnivartante dharmopetā mahābalāḥ
|
śriyā paramayā yuktā yato dharmastato jayaḥ ||18||

19tenāvadhyā raṇe pārthā jayayuktāśca pārthiva
|
tava putrā durātmānaḥ pāpeṣvabhiratāḥ sadā ||19||

20niṣṭhurā hīnakarmāṇastena hīyanti saṃyuge
|
subahūni nṛśaṃsāni putraistava janeśvara ||20||

21nikṛtānīha pāṇḍūnāṃ nīcairiva yathā naraiḥ
|
sarvaṃ ca tadanādṛtya putrāṇāṃ tava kilbiṣam ||21||

22sāpahnavāḥ sadaivāsan pāṇḍavāḥ pāṇḍupūrvaja
|
na caitān bahu manyante putrāstava viśāṃpate ||22||

23tasya pāpasya satataṃ kriyamāṇasya karmaṇaḥ
|
sāṃprataṃ sumahaddhoraṃ phalaṃ prāptaṃ janeśvara ||23||

24sa tvaṃ bhuṅkṣva mahārāja saputraḥ sasuhṛjjanaḥ
|
nāvabudhyasi yadrājan vāryamāṇaḥ suhṛjjanaiḥ ||24||

25vidureṇātha bhīṣmeṇa droṇena ca mahātmanā
|
tathā mayā cāpyasakṛddhāryamāṇo na budhyase ||25||

26vākyaṃ hitaṃ ca pathyaṃ ca martyāḥ pathyamivauṣadham
|
putrāṇāṃ matamājñāya jitān‌ manyasi pāṇḍavān ||26||

27śṛṇu bhūyo yathātattvaṃ yanmāṃ tvaṃ paripṛcchasi
|
kāraṇaṃ bharataśreṣṭha pāṇḍavānāṃ jayaṃ prati ||27||

28tatte'haṃ kathayiṣyāmi yathāśrutamariṃdama
|
duryodhanena saṃpṛṣṭa etamarthaṃ pitāmahaḥ ||28||

29dṛṣṭvā bhrātṝn raṇe sarvānnirjitāṃstu mahārathān
|
śokasaṃmūḍhahṛdayo niśākāle sma kauravaḥ ||29||

30pitāmahaṃ mahāprājñaṃ vinayenopagamya ha
|
yadabravīt sutaste'sau tanme śṛṇu janeśvara ||30||

31duryodhana uvāca|
droṇaśca tvaṃ ca śalyaśca kṛpo drauṇistathaiva ca
|
kṛtavarmā ca hārdikyaḥ kāṃbojaśca sudakṣiṇaḥ ||31||

32bhūriśravā vikarṇaśca bhagadattaśca vīryavān
|
mahārathāḥ samākhyātāḥ kulaputrāstanutyajaḥ ||32||

33trayāṇāmapi lokānāṃ paryāptā iti me matiḥ
|
pāṇḍavānāṃ samastāśca nātiṣṭhanta parākrame ||33||

34tatra me saṃśayo jātastanmamācakṣva pṛcchataḥ
|
yaṃ samāśritya kaunteyā jayantyasmān kṣaṇe kṣaṇe||34||

35bhīṣma uvāca|śṛṇu rājan vaco mahyaṃ yathā vakṣyāmi kaurava
|
bahuśaśca mayokto'si na ca me tattvayā kṛtam ||35||

36kriyatāṃ pāṇḍavaiḥ sārdhaṃ śamo bharatasattama
|
etat kṣemamahaṃ manye pṛthivyāstava vā vibho ||36||

37bhuṅkṣvemāṃ pṛthivīṃ rājan bhrātṛbhiḥ sahitaḥ sukhī
|
darhṛdastāpayan sarvānnandayaṃścāpi bāndhavān ||37||

38na ca me krośatastāta śrutavānasi vai purā
|
tadidaṃ samanuprāptaṃ yat pāṇḍūnavamanyase ||38||

39yaśca heturavadhyatve teṣāmakliṣṭakarmaṇām
|
taṃ śṛṇuṣva mahābāho mama kīrtayataḥ prabho ||39||

40nāsti lokeṣu tadbhūtaṃ bhavitā no bhaviṣyati
|
yo jayet pāṇḍavān sarvānpālitāñchārṅgadhanvanā ||40||

41yattu me kathitaṃ tāta munibhirbhāvitātmabhiḥ
|
purāṇagītaṃ dharmajña tacchṛṇuṣva yathātatham ||41||

42purā kila surāḥ sarve ṛṣayaśca samāgatāḥ
|
pitāmahamupāseduḥ parvate gandhamādane ||42||

43teṣāṃ madhye samāsīnaḥ prajāpatirapaśyata
|
vimānaṃ prajvaladbhāsā sthitaṃ pravaramambare ||43||

44dhyānenāvedya tadbrahmā kṛtvā ca niyato'ñjalim
|
namaścakāra hṛṣṭātmā puruṣaṃ parameśvaram ||44||

45ṛṣayastvatha devāśca dṛṣṭvā brahmāṇamutthitam
|
sthitāḥ prāñjalayaḥ sarve paśyanto mahada‌dbhutam ||45||

46yathāvacca tamabhyarcya brahmā brahmavidāṃ varaḥ
|
jagāda jagataḥ sraṣṭā paraṃ paramadharmavit ||46||

47viśvāvasurviśvamūrtirviśveśo viṣvakseno viśvakarmā vaśī ca
|
viśveśvaro vāsudevo'si tasmādyogātmānaṃ daivataṃ tvāmupaimi ||47||

48jaya viśva mahādeva jaya lokahite rata
|
jaya yogīśvara vibho jaya yogaparāvara ||48||

49padmagarbha viśālākṣa jaya lokeśvareśvara
|
bhūtabhavyabhavannātha jaya saumyātmajātmaja ||49||

50asaṅkhyeyaguṇādhāra jaya sarvaparāyaṇa
|
nārāyaṇa suduṣpāra jaya śārṅgadhanurdhara ||50||

51jaya sarvaguṇopeta viśvamūrte nirāmaya
|
viśveśvaramahābāho jaya lokārthatatpara ||51||

52mahoragavarāhādya harikeśa vibho jaya
|
harivāsa diśāmīśa viśvavāsāmitāvyaya ||52||

53vyaktāvyaktāmitasthāna niyatendriya satkriya
|
asaṅkhyeyātmabhāvajña jaya gambhīrakāmada ||53||

54ananta viditabrahman nityabhūtavibhāvana
|
kṛtakārya kṛtaprajña dharmajña vijayāvaha ||54||

55guhyātmansarvayogātman sphuṭasaṃbhūtasaṃbhava
|
bhūtādya lokatattveśa jaya bhūtavibhāvana ||55||

56ātmayone mahābhāga kalpasaṃkṣepatatpara
|
udbhāvana manobhāva jaya brahmajayapriya ||56||

57nisargasarganirata kāmeśa parameśvara
|
amṛtodbhava sadbhāva muktātman vijayaprada ||57||

58prajāpatipate deva padmanābha mahābala
|
ātmabhūta mahābhūta sattvātman jaya sarvadā ||58||

59pādau tava dharā devī diśo bāhū divaṃ śiraḥ
|
mūrtiste'haṃ surāḥ kāyaścandrādityau ca cakṣuṣī||59||

60balaṃ tapaśca satyaṃ ca karma dharmātmakaṃ tava
|
tejo'gniḥ pavanaḥ śvāsa āpaste svedasaṃbhavāḥ ||60||

61aśvinau śravaṇau nityaṃ devī jihvā sarasvatī
|
vedāḥ saṃskāraniṣṭhā hi tvayīdaṃ jagadāśritam ||61||

62na saṃkhyānaṃ parīmāṇaṃ na tejo na parākramam
|
na balaṃ yogayogīśa jānīmaste na saṃbhavam ||62||

63tvadbhaktiniratā deva niyamaistvāṃ samāśritāḥ
|
arcayāmaḥ sadā viṣṇo parameśaṃ maheśvaram ||63||

64ṛṣayo devagandharvā yakṣarākṣasapannagāḥ
|
piśācā mānuṣāścaiva mṛgapakṣisarīsṛpāḥ ||64||

65evamādi mayā sṛṣṭaṃ pṛthivyāṃ tvatprasādajam
|
padmanābha viśālākṣa kṛṣṇa duḥkhapraṇāśana ||65||

66tvaṃ gatiḥ sarvabhūtānāṃ tvaṃ natā tvaṃ jagadguruḥ
|
tvatprasādena deveśa sukhino vibudhāḥ sadā ||66||

67pṛthivī nirbhayā deva tvatprasādātsadā'bhavat
|
tasmādbhava viśālākṣa yaduvaṃśavivardhanaḥ ||67||

68dharmasaṃsthāpanārthāya daityānāṃ ca vadhāya ca
|
jagato dhāraṇārthāya vijñāpyaṃ kuru me vibho ||68||

69yattatparamaka guhyaṃ tvatprasādādidaṃ vibho
|
vāsudeva tadetatte mayodgītaṃ yathātatham ||69||

70sṛṣṭvā saṃkarṣaṇaṃ devaṃ svayamātmānamātmanā
|
kṛṣṇa tvamātmanāstrākṣīḥ pradyumnaṃ cātmasaṃbhavam ||70||

71pradyumnāda‌niruddhaṃ tvaṃ yaṃ vidurviṣṇumavyayam
|
aniruddho'sṛjanmāṃ vai brahmāṇaṃ lokadhāriṇam ||71||

72vāsudevamayaḥ so'haṃ tvayaivāsmi vinirmitaḥ
|
vibhajya bhāgaśo''tmānaṃ vraja mānuṣatāṃ vibho ||72||

73tatrāsuravadhaṃ kṛtvā sarvalokasukhāya vai
|
dharmaṃ prāpya yaśaḥ prāpya yogaṃ prāpsyasi tattvataḥ ||73||

74tvāṃ hi brahmarṣayo loke devāścāmitavikrama
|
taistairhi nāmabhiryuktā gāyanti paramātmakam ||74||

75sthitāśca sarve tvayi bhūtasaṃghāḥ kṛtvā''śrayaṃ tvāṃ varadaṃ subāho
|
anādimadhyāntamapārayogaṃ lokasya setuṃ pravada‌nti viprāḥ ||75||
