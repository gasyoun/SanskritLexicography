Mahābhārata 13.144 (Anuśāsanaparvan)

1umovāca
|
bhagavan sarvabhūteśa devāsuranamaskṛta
|
dharmādharmai nṛṇāṃ deva brūhi me'saṃśayaṃ vibho ||1||

2karmaṇā manasā vācā trividhaṃ hi naraḥ sadā
|
badhyate bandhanaiḥ pāśairmucyate'pyathavā punaḥ ||2||

3kena śīlena vṛttena karmaṇā kīdṛśena vā
|
samācārairguṇaiḥ kairvā svargaṃ yāntīha mānavāḥ ||3||

4maheśvara uvāca|
devi dharmārthatattvajñe dharmanitye dame rate
|
sarvaprāṇihitaḥ praśnaḥ śrūyatāṃ buddhivardhanaḥ ||4||

5satyadharmaratāḥ santaḥ sarvaliṅgavivarjitāḥ
|
dharmalabdhārthabhoktāraste narāḥ svargagāminaḥ ||5||

6nādharmeṇa na dharmeṇa badhyante chinnasaṃśayāḥ
|
pralayotpattitattvajñāḥ sarvajñāḥ sarvadarśinaḥ ||6||

7vītarāgā vimucyante puruṣāḥ karmabandhanaiḥ
|
karmaṇā manasā vācā ye na hiṃsanti kiñcana ||7||

8ye na sajjanti kasmiṃścitte na badhyanti karmabhiḥ
|
prāṇātipātādviratāḥ śīlavanto dayānvitāḥ ||8||

9tulyadveṣyapriyā dāntā sucyante karmabandhanaiḥ
|
sarvabhūtadayāvanto viśvāsyāḥ sarvajantuṣu ||9||

10tyaktahiṃsāsamācārāste narāḥ svargagāminaḥ
|
parasve nirmamā nityaṃ paradāravivarjakāḥ ||10||

11dharmalabdhānnabhoktāraste narāḥ svargagāminaḥ
|
mātṛvatsvasṛvaccaiva nityaṃ duhitṛvacca ye ||11||

12paradāreṣu vartante te narāḥ svargagāminaḥ
|
stainyānnivṛttāḥ satataṃ santuṣṭāḥ svadhanena ca ||12||

13svabhāgyānyupajīvanti te narāḥ svargagāminaḥ
|
svadāraniratā ye ca ṛtukālābhigāminaḥ ||13||

14agrāmyasukhabhogāśca te narāḥ svargagāminaḥ
|
paradāreṣu ye nityaṃ caritrāvṛtalocanāḥ ||14||

15yatendriyāḥ śīlaparāste narāḥ svargagāminaḥ
|
eṣa devakṛto mārgaḥ sevitavyaḥ sadā naraiḥ ||15||

16akaṣāyakṛtaścaiva mārgaḥ sevyaḥ sadā budhaiḥ
|
dānadharmatapoyuktaḥ śīlaśaucadayātmakaḥ ||16||

17vṛttyarthaṃ dharmahetorvā sevitavyaḥ sadā naraiḥ
|
svargavāsamabhīpsadbhirna sevyastvata uttaraḥ ||17||

18umovāca
|
vācā tu badhyate yena mucyate'pyathavā punaḥ
|
tāni karmāṇi me deva vada bhūtapate'nagha ||18||

19maheśvara uvāca|
ātmahetoḥ parārthe vā narmahāsyāśrayāttathā
|
ye mṛṣā na vadantīha te narāḥ svargagāminaḥ ||19||

20vṛttyarthaṃ dharmahetorvā kāmakārāttathaiva ca
|
anṛtaṃ ye na bhāṣante te narāḥ svargagāminaḥ ||20||

21ślakṣṇāṃ vāṇīṃ nirābādhāṃ madhurāṃ pāpavarjitām
|
svāgatenābhibhāṣante te narāḥ svargagāminaḥ ||21||

22paruṣaṃ ye na bhāṣante kaṭukaṃ niṣṭhuraṃ tathā
|
apaiśunyaratāḥ santaste narāḥ svargagāminaḥ ||22||

23piśunāṃ na prabhāṣante mitrabheda‌karīṃ giram
|
ṛtaṃ maitraṃ tu bhāṣante te narāḥ svargagāminaḥ ||23||

24ye varjayanti paruṣaṃ paradrohaṃ ca mānavāḥ
|
sarvabhūtasamā dāntāste narāḥ svargagāminaḥ ||24||

25śaṭhapralāpādviratā viruddhaparivarjakāḥ
|
saumyapralāpino nityaṃ te narāḥ svargagāminaḥ ||25||

26na kopādvyāharante ye vācaṃ hṛdayadāraṇīm
|
sāntvaṃ vadanti kruddhā'pi te narāḥ svargagāminaḥ ||26||

27eṣa vāṇīkṛto devi dharmaḥ sevyaḥ sadā naraiḥ
|
śubhaḥ satyaguṇo nityaṃ varjanīyo mṛṣā budhaiḥ ||27||

28umovāca
|
manasā badhyate yena karmaṇā puruṣaḥ sadā
|
tanme brūhi mahābhāga devadeva pinākadhṛt ||28||

29maheśvara uvāca|
mānaseneha dharmeṇa saṃyuktāḥ puruṣāḥ sadā
|
svargaṃ gacchanti kalyāṇi tanme kīrtayataḥ śṛṇu ||29||

30duṣpraṇītena manasā duṣpraṇītatarākṛtiḥ
|
mano badhyati yeneha śṛṇu vākyaṃ śubhānane ||30||

31araṇye vijane nyastaṃ parasvaṃ dṛśyate yadā
|
manasā'pi na hiṃsanti te narāḥ svargagāminaḥ ||31||

32grāme gṛhe vā ye dravyaṃ pārakyaṃ vijane sthitam
|
nābhinandanti vai nityaṃ te narāḥ svargagāminaḥ ||32||

33tathaiva paradārān ye kāmavṛttān rahogatān
|
manasā'pi na hiṃsanti te narāḥ svargagāminaḥ ||33||

34śatruṃ mitraṃ ca ye nityaṃ tulyena manasā narāḥ
|
bhajanti maitrāḥ saṅgamya te narāḥ svargagāminaḥ ||34||

35śrutavanto dayāvantaḥ śucayaḥ satyasaṅgarāḥ
|
svairarthaiḥ parisantuṣṭāste narāḥ svargagāminaḥ ||35||

36avairā ye tvanāyāsā maitrīcittaratāḥ sadā
|
sarvabhūtadayāvantaste narāḥ svargagāminaḥ ||36||

37śraddhāvanto dayāvantaścokṣāścokṣajanapriyāḥ
|
dharmādharmavido nityaṃ te narāḥ svargagāminaḥ ||37||

38śubhānāmaśubhānāṃ ca karmaṇāṃ phalasañcaye
|
vipākajñāśca ye devi te narāḥ svargagāminaḥ ||38||

39nyāyopetā guṇopetā devadvijaparāḥ sadā
|
samutthānamanuprāptāste narāḥ svargagāminaḥ ||39||

40śubhaiḥ karmaphalairdevi mayaite parikīrtitāḥ
|
svargamārgaparā bhūyaḥ kiṃ tvaṃ śrotumihecchasi ||40||

41umovāca
|
mahānme saṃśayaḥ kaścinmartyānprati maheśvara
|
tasmāttvaṃ naipuṇenādya mama vyākhyātumarhasi ||41||

42kenāyurlabhate dīrghaṃ karmaṇā puruṣaḥ prabho
|
tapasā vāpi deveśa kenāyurlabhate mahat ||42||

43kṣīṇāyuḥ kena bhavati karmaṇā bhuvi mānavaḥ
|
vipākaṃ karmaṇāṃ deva vaktumarhasyanindita ||43||

44apare ca mahābhāgyā mandabhāgyāstathā'pare
|
akulīnāstathā cānye kulīnāśca tathā'pare ||44||

45durdarśāḥ kecidābhānti narāḥ kāṣṭhamayā iva
|
priyadarśāstathā cānye darśanādeva mānavāḥ ||45||

46duṣprajñāḥ kecidābhānti kecidābhānti paṇḍitāḥ
|
mahāprājñāstathaivānye jñānavijñānabhāvinaḥ ||46||

47alpābādhāstathā kecinmahābādhāstathā'pare
|
dṛśyante puruṣā deva tanme vyākhyātumarhasi ||47||

48maheśvara uvāca|
hanta te'haṃ pravakṣyāmi devi karmaphalodayam
|
martyaloke naraḥ sarvo yena svaphalamaśnute ||48||

49prāṇātipāte yo raudro daṇḍahastodyataḥ sadā
|
nityamudyataśastraśca hanti bhūtagaṇānnaraḥ ||49||

50nirdayaḥ sarvabhūtānāṃ nityamudvegakārakaḥ
|
api kīṭapipīlānāmaśaraṇyaḥ sunirghṛṇaḥ ||50||

51evaṃbhūto naro devi nirayaṃ pratipadyate
|
viparītastu dharmātmā rūpavānabhijāyate ||51||

52pāpena karmaṇā devi vadhyo hiṃsāratirnaraḥ
|
apriyaḥ sarvabhūtānāṃ hīnāyurupajāyate ||52||

53nirayaṃ yāti hiṃsātmā yāti svargamahiṃsakaḥ
|
yātanāṃ niraye raudrāṃ sa kṛcchrāṃ labhate naraḥ ||53||

54yaḥ kaścinnirayāttasmātsamuttarati karhicit
|
mānuṣyaṃ labhate cāpi hīnāyustatra jāyate ||54||

55pāpena karmaṇā devi baddho hiṃsāratirnaraḥ
|
apriyaḥ sarvabhūtānāṃ hīnāyurupajāyate ||55||

56yastu śuklābhijātīyaḥ prāṇighātavivarjakaḥ
|
nikṣiptaśastro nirdaṇḍo na hiṃsati kadācana ||56||

57na ghātayati no hanti ghnantaṃ naivānumodate
|
sarvabhūteṣu sasneho yathā''tmani tathā'pare ||57||

58īdṛśaḥ puruṣotkarṣo devi devatvamaśnute
|
upapannānsukhānbhogānupāśnāti mudā yutaḥ ||58||

59atha cenmānuṣe loke kadācidupapadyate
|
tatra dīrghāyurutpannaḥ sa naraḥ sukhamedhate ||59||

60eṣa dīrghāyuṣāṃ mārgaḥ suvṛttānāṃ sukarmiṇām
|
prāṇihiṃsāvimokṣeṇa brahmaṇā samudīritaḥ ||60||
