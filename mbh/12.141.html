Mahābhārata 12.141 (Śāntiparvan)

1yudhiṣṭhira uvāca|
hīne paramake dharme sarvalokābhilaṃghite
|
adharme dharmatāṃ nīte dharme cādharmatāṃ gate ||1||

2maryādāsu vinaṣṭāsu kṣubhite dharmaniścaye
|
rājabhiḥ pīḍite loke parairvā'pi viśāmpate ||2||

3sarvāśrameṣu mūḍheṣu karmasūpahateṣu ca
|
kāmāllobhācca mohācca bhayaṃ paśyatsu bhārata ||3||

4aviśvasteṣu sarveṣu nityaṃ bhīteṣu pārthiva
|
nikṛtyā hanyamāneṣu vañcayatsu parasparam ||4||

5sampradīpteṣu deśeṣu brāhmaṇe cātipīḍite
|
avarṣati ca parjanye mitho bhede samutthite ||5||

6sarvasmindasyusādbhūte pṛthivyāmupajīvane
|
kenasvidbrāhmaṇo jīvejjaghanye kāla āgate ||6||

7atitikṣuḥ putrapautrānanukrośānnarādhipa
|
kathamāpatsu varteta tanme brūhi pitāmaha ||7||

8kathaṃ ca rājā varteta loke kaluṣatāṃ gate
|
kathamarthācca dharmācca na hīyeta parantapa ||8||

9bhīṣma uvāca|
rājamūlā mahābāho yogakṣemasuvṛṣṭayaḥ
|
prajāsu vyādhayaścaiva maraṇaṃ ca bhayāni ca ||9||

10kṛtaṃ tretāṃ dvāparaṃ ca kaliśca bharatarṣabha
|
rājamūlā iti matirmama nāstyatra saṃśayaḥ ||10||

11tasmiṃstvabhyāgate kāle prajānāṃ doṣakārake
|
vijñānabalamāsthāya jīvitavyaṃ bhavettadā ||11||

12atrāpyudāharantīmamitihāsaṃ purātanam
|
viśvāmitrasya saṃvādaṃ cāṇḍālasya ca pakkaṇe ||12||

13tretādvāparayoḥ saṃdhau tadā daivavidhikramāt
|
anāvṛṣṭirabhūdghorā loke dvādaśavārṣikī ||13||

14prajānāmativṛddhānāṃ yugānte samupasthite
|
tretāvimokṣasamaye dvāparapratipādane ||14||

15navavarṣasahastrākṣaḥ pratilomo'bhavadguruḥ
|
jagāma dakṣiṇaṃ mārgaṃ somo vyāvṛttalakṣaṇaḥ ||15||

16nāvaśyāyo'pi tatrābhūtkuta evābhrajātayaḥ
|
nadyaḥ saṃkṣiptatoyaughāḥ kiṃcidantargatāstataḥ ||16||

17sarāṃsi saritaścaiva kūpāḥ prasravaṇāni ca
|
hatatviṣo na lakṣyante nisargāddaivakāritāt ||17||

18upaśuṣkajalasthāyā vinivṛttasabhāprapā
|
nivṛttayajñasvādhyāyā nirvaṣaṭkāramaṅgalā ||18||

19ucchinnakṛṣigorakṣā nivṛttavipaṇāpaṇā
|
nivṛttayūpasambhārā vipranaṣṭamahotsavā ||19||

20asthisaṃcayasaṃkīrṇā mahābhūtaravākulā
|
śūnyabhūyiṣṭhanagarā dagdhagrāmaniveśanā ||20||

21kvaciddhoraiḥ kvacicchastraiḥ kvacidrājabhirāturaiḥ
|
parasparabhayāccaiva śūnyabhūyiṣṭhanirjanā ||21||

22gatadaivatasaṃsthānā vṛddhalokanirākṛtā
|
gojāvimahiṣīhīnā parasparaparāhatā ||22||

23hataviprā hatārakṣā praṇaṣṭauṣadhisaṃcayā
|
sarvabhūtarutaprāyā babhūva vasudhā tadā ||23||

24tasminpratibhaye kāle kṣate dharme yudhiṣṭhira
|
babhūvuḥ kṣudhitā mārtyāḥ khādamānāḥ parasparam ||24||

25ṛṣayo niyamāṃstyaktvā parityajyāgnidevatāḥ
|
āśramānsamparityajya paryadhāvannitastataḥ ||25||

26viśvāmitro'tha bhagavānmaharṣiraniketanaḥ
|
kṣudhāparigato dhīmānsamantātparyadhāvata ||26||

27tyaktvā dārāṃśca putrāṃśca kasmiṃśca janasaṃsadi
|
bhakṣyābhakṣyasamo bhūtvā niragniraniketanaḥ ||27||

28sa kadācitparipatan śvapacānāṃ niveśanam
|
hiṃstrāṇāṃ prāṇighātānāmāsasāda vane kvacit ||28||

29vibhinnakalaśākīrṇaṃ śvacarmacchedanāyutam
|
varāhakharabhagnāsthikapālaghaṭasaṃkulam ||29||

30mṛtacailaparistīrṇaṃ nirmālyakṛtabhūṣaṇam
|
sarpanirmokamālābhiḥ kṛtacihnakuṭīmaṭham ||30||

31kukkuṭārāvabahulaṃ gardabhadhvanināditam
|
udghoṣadbhiḥ kharairvākyaiḥ kalahadbhiḥ parasparam ||31||

32ulūkapakṣidhvanibhirdevatāyatanairvṛtam
|
lohaghaṇṭāpariṣkāraṃ śvayūthaparivāritam ||32||

33tatpraviśya kṣudhāviṣṭo viśvāmitro mahānṛṣiḥ
|
āhārānveṣaṇe yuktaḥ paraṃ yatnaṃ samāsthitaḥ ||33||

34na ca kvacidavindatsa bhikṣamāṇo'pi kauśikaḥ
|
māṃsamannaṃ phalaṃ mūlamanyadvā tatra kiñcana ||34||

35aho kṛcchraṃ mayā prāptamiti niścitya kauśikaḥ
|
papāta bhūmau daurbalyāttasmiṃścāṇḍālapakkaṇe ||35||

36sa cintayāmāsa muniḥ kiṃ nu me sukṛtaṃ bhavet
|
kathaṃ vṛthā na mṛtyuḥ syāditi pārthivasattama ||36||

37sa dadarśa śvamāṃsasya kutantrī vivatāṃ muniḥ
|
cāṇḍālasya gṛhe rājan sadyaḥ śastrahatasya vai ||37||

38sa cintayāmāsa tadā stainyaṃ kāryamito mayā
|
na hīdānīmupāyo me vidyate prāṇadhāraṇe ||38||

39āpatsu vihitaṃ stainyaṃ viśiṣṭaṃ ca mahīyasaḥ
|
vipreṇa prāṇarakṣārthaṃ kartavyamiti niścayaḥ ||39||

40hīnādādeyamādau syātsamānāttadanantaram
|
asambhave vā''dīta viśiṣṭādapi dhārmikāt ||40||

41so'hamantyāvasāyānāṃ harāmyenāṃ pratigrahāt
|
na stainyadoṣaṃ paśyāmi hariṣyāmi śvajāghanīm ||41||

42etāṃ buddhiṃ samāsthāya viśvāmitro mahāmuniḥ
|
tasmindeśe sa suṣvāpa śvapaco yatra bhārata ||42||

43sa vigāḍhāṃ niśāṃ dṛṣṭvā supte cāṇḍālapakkaṇe
|
śanairutthāya bhagavānpraviveśa kuṭīmataḥ ||43||

44sa supta iva cāṇḍālaḥ śleṣmāpihitalocanaḥ
|
paribhinnasvaro rūkṣaḥ provācāpriyadarśanaḥ ||44||

45śvapaca uvāca |
kaḥ kutantrī ghaṭṭayati supte cāṇḍālapakkaṇe
|
jāgarmi nātra supto'smi hato'sīti ca dāruṇaḥ ||45||

46viśvāmitrastato bhītaḥ sahasā tamuvāca ha
|
tatra vrīḍākulamukhaḥ sodvegastena karmaṇā ||46||

47viśvāmitro'hamāyuṣmannāgato'haṃ bubhukṣitaḥ
|
mā vadhīrmama sadbuddhe yadi samyak prapaśyasi ||47||

48cāṇḍālastadvacaḥ śrutvā maharṣerbhāvitātmanaḥ
|
śayanādupasambhrānta udyayau prati taṃ tataḥ ||48||

49sa visṛjyāśru netrābhyāṃ bahumānātkṛtāñjaliḥ
|
uvāca kauśikaṃ rātrau brahmankiṃ te cikīrṣitam ||49||

50viśvāmitrastu mātaṅgamuvāca parisāntvayan
|
kṣudhito'haṃ gataprāṇo hariṣyāmi śvajāghanīm ||50||

51kṣudhitaḥ kaluṣaṃ yāto nāsti hrīraśanārthinaḥ
|
kṣucca māṃ dūṣayatyatra hariṣyāmi śvajāghanīm ||51||

52avasīdanti me prāṇāḥ śrutirme naśyati kṣudhā
|
durbalo naṣṭasaṃjñaśca bhakṣyābhakṣyavivarjitaḥ ||52||

53so'dharmaṃ buddhyamāno'pi hariṣyāmi śvajāghanīm
|
aṭan bhaikṣyaṃ na vindāmi yadā yuṣmākamālaye ||53||

54tadā buddhiḥ kṛtā pāpe hariṣyāmi śvajāghanīm
|
agnirmukhaṃ purodhāśca devānāṃ śuciṣāḍvibhuḥ ||54||

55yathāvatsarvabhugbrahmā tathā māṃ viddhi dharmataḥ
|
tamuvāca sa cāṇḍālo maharṣe śṛṇu me vacaḥ ||55||

56śrutvā tattvaṃ tathā''tiṣṭha yathā dharmo na hīyate
|
dharmaṃ vāpi viprarṣe śṛṇu yatte bravīmyaham ||56||

57śṛgālādadhamaṃ śvānaṃ pravadanti manīṣiṇaḥ
|
tasyāpyadhama uddeśaḥ śarīrasya śvajāghanī ||57||

58nedaṃ samyagvyavasitaṃ maharṣe dharmagarhitam
|
cāṇḍālasvasya haraṇamabhakṣyasya viśeṣataḥ ||58||

59sādhvanyamanupaśya tvamupāyaṃ prāṇadhāraṇe
|
na māṃsalobhāttapaso nāśaste syānmahāmune ||59||

60jānatā vihitaṃ dharmaṃ na kāryo dharmasaṃkaraḥ
|
mā sma dharmaṃ parityākṣīstvaṃ hi dharmabhṛtāṃ varaḥ ||60||

61viśvāmitrastato rājannityukto bharatarṣabha
|
kṣudhārtaḥ pratyuvācedaṃ punareva mahāmuniḥ ||61||

62nirāhārasya sumahānmama kālo'bhidhāvataḥ
|
na vidyate'pyupāyaśca kaścinme prāṇadhāraṇe ||62||

63yena yena viśeṣeṇa karmaṇā yena kenacit
|
abhyujjīvetsādyamānaḥ samartho dharmamācaret ||63||

64aindro dharmaḥ kṣatriyāṇāṃ brāhmaṇānāmathāgnikaḥ
|
brahmavahnirmama balaṃ bhakṣyāmi śamayan kṣudhām ||64||

65yathā yathaiva jīveddhi tatkartavyamahelayā
|
jīvitaṃ maraṇācchreyo jīvan dharmamavāpnuyāt ||65||

66so'haṃ jīvitamākāṅkṣannabhakṣyasyāpi bhakṣaṇam
|
vyavasye buddhipūrvaṃ vai tadbhavānanumanyatām ||66||

67balavantaṃ kariṣyāmi praṇotsyāmyaśubhāni tu
|
tapobhirvidyayā caiva jyotīṃṣīva mahattamaḥ ||67||

68śvapaca uvāca |
naitatkhādanprāpnute dīrghamāyurnaiva prāṇānnāmṛtasyeva tṛptiḥ
|
bhikṣāmanyāṃ bhikṣa mā te mano'stu śvabhakṣaṇe śvā hyabhakṣyo dvijānām ||68||

69viśvāmitra uvāca |
na durbhikṣe sulabhaṃ māṃsamanyacchvapāka manye na ca me'sti vittam
|
kṣudhārtaścāhamagatirnirāśaḥ śvamāṃse cāsmin ṣaḍrasān sādhu manye ||69||

70śvapaca uvāca|
pañca pañcanakhā bhakṣyā brahmakṣatrasya vai viśaḥ
|
yathā śāstraṃ pramāṇaṃ te mā'bhakṣye mānasaṃ kṛthāḥ ||70||

71viśvāmitra uvāca |
agastyenāsuro jagdho vātāpiḥ kṣudhitena vai
|
ahamāpadgataḥ kṣutto bhakṣayiṣye śvajāghanīm ||71||

72śvapaca uvāca |
bhikṣāmanyāmāhareti na ca kartumihārhasi
|
na nūnaṃ kāryametadvai hara kāmaṃ śvajāghanīm ||72||

73viśvāmitra uvāca |
śiṣṭā vai kāraṇaṃ dharme tadvṛttamanuvartaye
|
parāṃ medhyāśanādenāṃ bhakṣyāṃ manye śvajāghanīm ||73||

74śvapaca uvāca |
asatā yatsamācīrṇaṃ na ca dharmaḥ sanātanaḥ
|
nākāryamiha kāryaṃ vai mā chalenāśubhaṃ kṛthāḥ ||74||

75viśvāmitra uvāca |
na pātakaṃ nāvamatamṛṣiḥ sankartumarhati
|
samau ca śvamṛgau manye tasmādbhokṣye śvajāghanīm ||75||

76śvapaca uvāca |
yadbrāhmaṇārthe kṛtamarthitena tenarṣiṇā tadavasthādhikāre
|
sa vai dharmo yatra na pāpamasti sarvairupāyairguravo hi rakṣyāḥ ||76||

77viśvāmitra uvāca |
mitraṃ ca me brāhmaṇasyāyamātmā priyaśca me pūjyatamaśca loke
|
taṃ dhartukāmo'hamimāṃ jihīrṣe nṛśaṃsānāmīdṛśānāṃ na bibhye ||77||

78śvapaca uvāca |
kāmaṃ narā jīvitaṃ santyajanti na cābhakṣye kvacitkurvanti buddhim
|
sarvānkāmānprāpnuvantīha vidvan priyasva kāmaṃ sahitaḥ kṣudhaiva ||78||

79viśvāmitra uvāca |
sthāne bhavetsaṃśayaḥ pretyabhāve niḥsaṃśayaḥ karmaṇāṃ vai vināśaḥ
|
ahaṃ punarvratanityaḥ śamātmā mūlaṃ rakṣyaṃ bhakṣayiṣyāmyabhakṣyam ||79||

80buddhyātmake vyaktamastīti puṇyaṃ mohātmake yatra yathā śvabhakṣye
|
yadyapyetatsaṃśayātmā carāmi nāhaṃ bhaviṣyāmi yathā tvameva ||80||

81śvapaca uvāca| gopanīyamidaṃ duḥkhamiti me niścitā matiḥ
|
duṣkṛto brāhmaṇaḥ satraṃ yastvāmahamupālabhe ||81||

82viśvāmitra uvāca |
pibantyevodakaṃ gāvo maṇḍūkeṣu ruvatsvapi
|
na te'dhikāro dharme'sti mā bhūrātmapraśaṃsakaḥ ||82||

83śvapaca uvāca |
suhṛdbhūtvā'nuśāse tvāṃkṛpā hi tvayi me dvija
|
yadidaṃ śreya ādhatsva mā lobhātpātakaṃ kṛthāḥ ||83||

84viśvāmitra uvāca |
suhṛnme tvaṃ sukhepsuścedāpado māṃ samuddhara
|
jāne'haṃ dharmato''tmānaṃ śaunīmutsṛja jāghanīm ||84||

85śvapaca uvāca |
naivātsahe bhavato dātumetāṃ nopekṣituṃ hriyamāṇaṃ svamannam
|
ubhau syāvaḥ pāpalokāvaliptau dātā cāhaṃ brāhmaṇastvaṃ pratīcchan ||85||

86viśvāmitra uvāca |
adyāhametadvṛjinaṃ karma kṛtvā jīvaṃścariṣyāmi mahāpavitram
|
sa pūtātmā dharmamevābhipatsye yadetayorguru tadvai bravīhi ||86||

87śvapaca uvāca |
ātmaiva sākṣī kuladharmakṛtye tvameva jānāsi yadatra duṣkṛtam
|
yo hyādriyādbhakṣyamiti śvamāṃsaṃ manye na tasyāsti vivarjanīyam ||87||

88viśvāmitra uvāca |
upādāne khādane cāsti doṣaḥ kāryātyaye nityamatrāpavādaḥ
|
yasmin hiṃsā nānṛtaṃ vācyaleśo bhakṣyakriyā yatra na tadgarīyaḥ ||88||

89śvapaca uvāca |
yadyeṣa hetustava khādane syānna te vedaḥ kāraṇaṃ nāryadharmaḥ
|
tasmādbhakṣye bhakṣaṇe vā dvijendra doṣaṃ na paśyāmi yathedamatra ||89||

90viśvāmitra uvāca |
naivātipāpaṃ bhakṣamāṇasya dṛṣṭaṃ surāṃ tu pītvā patatīti śabdaḥ
|
anyonyakāryāṇi yathā tathaiva na pāpamātreṇa kṛtaṃ hinasti ||90||

91śvapaca uvāca |
asthānato hīnataḥ kutsitādvā tadvidvāṃsaṃ bādhate sādhuvṛttam
|
śvānaṃ punaryo labhate'bhiṣaṅgāttenāpi daṇḍaḥ sahitavya eva ||91||

92bhīṣma uvāca|
evamuktvā nivavṛtte mātaṅgaḥ kauśikaṃ tadā
|
viśvāmitro jahāraiva kṛtabuddhiḥ śvajāghanīm ||92||

93tato jagrāha sa śvāṅgaṃ jīvitārthī mahāmuniḥ
|
sadārastāmupāhṛtya vane bhoktumiyeṣa saḥ ||93||

94athāsya buddhirabhavadvidhināhaṃ śvajāghanīm
|
bhakṣayāmi yathākāmaṃ pūrvaṃ santarpya devatāḥ ||94||

95tato'gnimupasaṃhṛtya brāhmeṇa vidhinā muniḥ
|
aindrāgneyena vidhinā caḻaṃ śrapayata svayam ||95||

96tataḥ samārabhatkarma daivaṃ pitryaṃ ca bhārata
|
āhūya devānindrādīn bhāgaṃ bhāgaṃ vidhikramāt ||96||

97etasminneva kāle tu pravavarṣa sa vāsavaḥ
|
saṃjīvayan prajāḥ sarvā janayāmāsa cauṣadhīḥ ||97||

98viśvāmitro'pi bhagavāṃstapasā dagdhakilbiṣaḥ
|
kālena mahatā siddhimavāpa paramādbhutām ||98||

99sa saṃhṛtya ca tatkarma anāsvādya ca taddhaviḥ
|
toṣayāmāsa devāṃśca pitṝṃśca dvijasattamaḥ ||99||

100evaṃ vidvānadīnātmā vyasanastho jijīviṣuḥ
|
sarvopāyairupāyajño dīnamātmānamuddharet ||100||

101etāṃ buddhiṃ samāsthāya jīvitavyaṃ sadā bhavet
|
jīvanpuṇyamavāpnoti puruṣo bhadramaśnute ||101||

102tasmātkaunteya viduṣā dharmādharmaviniścaye
|
buddhimāsthāya loke'smin vartitavyaṃ kṛtātmanā ||102||
