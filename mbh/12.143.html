Mahābhārata 12.143 (Śāntiparvan)

1yudhiṣṭhira uvāca|
pitāmaha mahāprājña sarvaśāstraviśārada
|
śaraṇaṃ pālayānasya yo dharmastaṃ bravīhi me ||1||

2bhīṣma uvāca|
mahāndharmo mahārāja śaraṇāgatapālane
|
arhaḥ praṣṭuṃ bhavāṃścaiva praśnaṃ bharatasattama ||2||

3śibiprabhṛtayo rājan rājānaḥ śaraṇāgatān
|
paripālya mahātmānaḥ saṃsiddhiṃ paramāṃ gatāḥ ||3||

4śrūyate ca kapotena śatruḥ śaraṇamāgataḥ
|
pūjitaśca yathānyāyaṃ svaiśca māṃsairnimantritaḥ ||4||

5yudhiṣṭhira uvāca|
kathaṃ kapotena purā śatruḥ śaraṇamāgataḥ
|
svamāṃsaṃ bhojitaḥ kāṃ ca gatiṃ lebhe sa bhārata ||5||

6bhīṣma uvāca|
śṛṇu rājankathāṃ divyāṃ sarvapāpapraṇāśinīm
|
nṛpatermucukundasya kathitāṃ bhārgaveṇa vai ||6||

7imarthaṃ purā śatruḥ pārtha mucukundo narādhipaḥ
|
bhārgavaṃ paripapraccha praṇataḥ puruṣarṣabha ||7||

8tasmai śuśrūṣamāṇāya bhārgavo'kathayatkathām
|
imāṃ yathā kapotena siddhiḥ prāptā narādhipa ||8||

9muniruvāca |
dharmaniścayasaṃyuktāṃ kāmārthasahitāṃ kathām
|
śṛṇuṣvāvahito rājan gadato me mahābhuja ||9||

10kaścitkṣudrasamācāraḥ pṛthivyāṃ kālasammitaḥ
|
vicacāra mahāraṇye ghoraḥ śakunilubdhakaḥ ||10||

11kākola iva kṛṣṇāṅgo raktākṣaḥ kālasammitaḥ
|
dīrghajaṃgho hrasvapādo mahāvaktro mahāhanuḥ ||11||

12naiva tasya suhṛtkaścinna sambandhī na bāndhavāḥ
|
sa hi taiḥ samparityaktastena raudreṇa karmaṇā ||12||

13naraḥ pāpasamācārastyaktavyo dūrato budhaiḥ
|
ātmānaṃ yo'bhisaṃdhatte so'nyasya syātkathaṃ hitaḥ ||13||

14ye nṛśaṃsā durātmānaḥ prāṇiprāṇaharā narāḥ
|
udvejanīyā bhūtānāṃ vyālā iva bhavanti te ||14||

15sa vai kṣārakamādāya dvijān hatvā vane sadā
|
cakāra vikrayaṃ teṣāṃ pataṅgānāṃ janādhipa ||15||

16evaṃ tu vartamānasya tasya vṛttiṃ durātmanaḥ
|
agamatsumahānkālo na cādharmamabudhyata ||16||

17tasya bhāryāsahāyasya ramamāṇasya śāśvatam
|
daivayogavimūḍhasya nānyā vṛttirarocata ||17||

18tataḥ kadācittasthāya vanasthasya samantataḥ
|
pātayanniva vṛkṣāṃstānsumahānvātasambhramaḥ ||18||

19meghasaṃkulamākāśaṃ vidyunmaṇḍalamaṇḍitam
|
saṃchannastu muhūrtena nausāthairiva sāgaraḥ ||19||

20vāridhārāsamūhena sampraviṣṭaḥ śatakratuḥ
|
kṣaṇena pūrayāmāsa salilena vasundharām ||20||

21tato dhārākule kāle sambhramannaṣṭacetanaḥ
|
śītārtastadvanaṃ sarvamākulenāntarātmanā ||21||

22naiva nimna sthalaṃ vāpi so'vindata vihaṅgahā
|
pūrito hi jalaughena tasya mārgo vanasya ca ||22||

23pakṣiṇo varṣavegena hatā līnāstadābhavan
|
mṛga-siṃha-varāhāśca sthalamāśritya śerate ||23||

24mahatā vātavarṣeṇa trāsitāste vanaukasaḥ
|
bhayārtāśca kṣudhārtāśca babhramuḥ sahitā vane ||24||

25sa tu śītahatairgātrairna jagāma na tasthivān
|
dadarśa patitāṃ bhūmau kapotīṃ śītavihvalām ||25||

26dṛṣṭvā''rto'pi hi pāpātmā sa tāṃ pañjarake'kṣipat
|
svayaṃ duḥkhābhibhūto'pi duḥkhamevākarotpare ||26||

27pāpātmā pāpakāritvātpāpameva cakāra saḥ
|
so'paśyattarukhaṇḍeṣu meghanīlavanaspatim ||27||

28sevyamānaṃ vihaṅgaughaiśchāyāvāsaphalārthibhiḥ
|
dhātrā paropakārāya sa sādhuriva nirmitaḥ ||28||

29athābhavatkṣaṇenaiva viyadvimalatārakam
|
mahatsara ivotphullaṃ kumudaccharitodakam ||29||

30tārāḍhyaṃ kumudākāramākāśaṃ nirmalaṃ bahu
|
ghanairmuktaṃ nabho dṛṣṭvā lubdhakaḥ śītavihvalaḥ ||30||

31diśo vilokayāmāsa vigāḍhāṃ prekṣya śarvarīm
|
dūrato me niveśaśca asmāddeśāditi prabho ||31||

32kṛtabuddhirdrume tasminvastuṃ tāṃ rajanīṃ tataḥ
|
sāñjaliḥ praṇatiṃ kṛtvā vākyamāha vanaspatim ||32||

33śaraṇaṃ yāmi yānyasmin daivatāni vanaspatau
|
sa śilāyāṃ śiraḥ kṛtvā parṇānyāstīrya bhūtale
|
duḥkhena mahatā''viṣṭastataḥ suṣvāpa pakṣihā ||33||
