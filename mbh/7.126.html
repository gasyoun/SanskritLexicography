Mahābhārata 7.126 (Droṇaparvan)

1sañjaya uvāca|
vyūheṣvāloḍyamāneṣu pāṇḍavānāṃ tatastataḥ
|
sudūramanvayuḥ pārthāḥ pañcālāḥ saha somakaiḥ ||1||

2vartamāne tathā raudre saṃgrāme lomaharṣaṇe
|
saṃkṣaye jagatastīvre yugānta iva bhārata ||2||

3droṇe yudhi parākrānte nardamāne muhurmuhuḥ
|
pañcāleṣu ca kṣīṇeṣu vadhyamāneṣu pāṇḍuṣu ||3||

4nāpaśyaccharaṇaṃ kiñciddharmarājo yudhiṣṭhiraḥ
|
cintayāmāsa rājendra kathametadbhaviṣyati ||4||

5tato vīkṣya diśaḥsarvāḥ savyasācididṛkṣayā
|
yudhiṣṭhiro dadarśātha naiva pārthaṃ na mādhavam ||5||

6so'paśyannaraśārdūlaṃ vānararṣabhalakṣaṇam
|
gāṃḍīvasya ca nirghoṣamaśṛṇvan vyathitendriyaḥ ||6||

7apaśyat sātyakiṃ cāpi vṛṣṇīnāṃ pravaraṃ ratham
|
cintayā'bhiparītāṃgo dharmarājo yudhiṣṭhiraḥ ||7||

8nādhyagacchattadā śāntiṃ tāvapaśyanarottamau
|
lokopakrośabhīrutvāddharmarājo mahāmanāḥ ||8||

9acintayanmahābāhuḥ śaineyasya rathaṃ prati
|
padavīṃ preṣitaścaiva phālgunasya mayā raṇe ||9||

10śaineyaḥ sātyakiḥ satyo mitrāṇāmabhayaṃkaraḥ
|
tadidaṃ hyekamevāsīddvidhā jātaṃ mamādya vai ||10||

11sātyakiśca hi vijñeyaḥ pāṇḍavaśca dhanañjayaḥ
|
sātyakiṃ preṣayitvā tu pāṇḍavasya padānugam ||11||

12sātvatasyāpi kaṃ yuddhe preṣayiṣye padānugam
|
kariṣyāmi prayatnena bhrāturanveṣaṇaṃ yadi ||12||

13yuyudhānamananviṣya loko māṃ garhayiṣyati
|
cāturanveṣaṇaṃ kṛtvā dharmaputro yudhiṣṭhiraḥ ||13||

14parityajati vārṣṇeyaṃ sātyakiṃ satyavikramam
|
lokāpavādabhīrutvātso'haṃ pārthaṃ vṛkodaram ||14||

15padavīṃ preṣāyeṣyāmi mādhavasya mahātmanaḥ
|
yathaiva ca mama prītirarjune śatrusūdane ||15||

16tathaiva vṛṣṇivīre'pi sātvate yuddhadurmade
|
atibhāre niyuktaśca mayā śaineyanandanaḥ ||16||

17sa tu mitroparodhena gauravātsu mahābalaḥ
|
praviṣṭo bhāratīṃ senāṃ makaraḥ sāgaraṃ yathā ||17||

18asau hi śrūyate śabdaḥ śūrāṇāmanivartinām
|
mithaḥ saṃyudhyamānānāṃ vṛṣṇivīreṇa dhīmatā ||18||

19prāptakālaṃ subalavanniścitaṃ bahudhā hi me
|
tatraiva pāṇḍaveyasya bhīmasenasya dhanvinaḥ ||19||

20gamanaṃ rocate mahyaṃ yatra yātau mahārathau
|
na cāpyasahyaṃ bhīmasya vidyate bhuvi kiṃcana ||20||

21śakto hyeṣa raṇe yattaḥ pṛthivyāṃ sarvadhanvinām
|
svabāhubalamāsthāya prativyūhitumañjasā ||21||

22yasya bāhuvalaṃ sarve samāśritya mahātmanaḥ
|
vanavāsānnivṛttāḥ sma na ca yuddheṣu nirjitāḥ ||22||

23ito gate bhīmasene sātvataṃ prati pāṇḍave
|
sanāthau bhavitārau hi yudhi sātvataphālgunau ||23||

24kāmaṃ tvaśocanīyau tau raṇe sātvataphālgunau
|
rakṣitau vāsudevena svayaṃ śastraviśāradau ||24||

25avaśyaṃ tu mayā kāryamātmanaḥ śokanāśanam
|
tasmādbhīmaṃ niyokṣyāmi sātvatasya padānugam ||25||

26tataḥ pratikṛtaṃ manye vidhānaṃ sātyakiṃ prati
|
evaṃ niścitya manasā dharmaputro yudhiṣṭhiraḥ ||26||

27yantāramabravīdrājā bhīmaṃ prati nayasva mām
|
dharmarājavacaḥ śrutvā sārathirhayakovidaḥ ||27||

28rathaṃ hemapariṣkāraṃ bhīmāntikamupānayat
|
bhīmasenamanujñāpya prāptakālamacintayat ||28||

29kaśmalaṃ prāviśadrājā bahu tatra samādiśat
|
sa kaśmalasamāviṣṭo bhīmamāhūya pārthivaḥ ||29||

30abravīdvacanaṃ rājan kuntīputro yudhiṣṭhiraḥ
|
yaḥ sadevānsagandharvān daityāṃścaikaratho'jayat ||30||

31tasya lakṣma na paśyāmi bhīmasenānujasya te
|
tato'bravīddharmarājaṃ bhīmasenastathāgatam ||31||

32naivādrākṣaṃ na cāśrauṣaṃ tava kaśmalamīdṛśam
|
purā'tiduḥkhadīrṇānāṃ bhavān‌ gatirabhūddhi naḥ ||32||

33uttiṣṭhottiṣṭha rājendra śādhi kiṃ karavāṇi te
|
na hyasādhyamakāryaṃ vā vidyate mama mānada ||33||

34ājñāpaya kuruśreṣṭha mā ca śoke manaḥ kṛthāḥ
|
tamabravīdaśrupūrṇaḥ kṛṣṇasarpa iva śvasan ||34||

35bhīmasenamidaṃ vākyaṃ pramlānavadano nṛpaḥ
|
yathā śaṃkhasya nirghoṣaḥ pāñcajanyasya śrūyate ||35||

36pūrito vāsudevena saṃrabdhena yaśasvinā
|
nūnamadya hataḥ śete tavā bhrātā dhanañjayaḥ ||36||

37tasmin vinihate nūnaṃ yudhyate'sau janārdanaḥ
|
yasya sattvavato vīryaṃ hyupajīvanti pāṇḍavāḥ ||37||

38yaṃ bhayeṣvabhigacchanti sahasrākṣamivāmarāḥ
|
sa śūraḥ saindhavaprepsuranvayādbhāratīṃ camūm ||38||

39tasya vai gamanaṃ vidmo bhīma nāvartanaṃ punaḥ
|
śyāmo yuvā guḍākeśo darśanīyo mahārathaḥ ||39||

40vyūḍhorasko mahābāhurmattadviradavikramaḥ
|
cakoranetrastāmrāsyo dviṣatāṃ bhayavardhanaḥ ||40||

41tadidaṃ mama bhadraṃ te śokasthānamarindamam
|
arjunārthe mahābāho sātvatasya ca kāraṇāt ||41||

42vardhate haviṣevāgniridhyamānaḥ punaḥ punaḥ
|
tasya lakṣma na paśyāmi tena vindāmi kaśmalam ||42||

43taṃ viddhi puruṣavyāghraṃ sātvataṃ ca mahāratham
|
sa taṃ mahārathaṃ paścāda‌nuyātastathānujam ||43||

44tamapaśyanmahābāhumahaṃ vindāmi kaśmalam
|
pārthe tasminhate caiva yudhyate nūnamagraṇīḥ ||44||

45sahāyo nāsya vai kaścittena vindāmi kaśmalam
|
tasminkṛṣṇo hate nūnaṃ yudhyate yuddhakovidaḥ ||45||

46na hi me śudhyate bhāvastayoreva parantapa
|
sa tatra gaccha kaunteya yatra yāto dhanañjayaḥ ||46||

47sātyakiśca mahāvīryaḥ kartavyaṃ yadi manyase
|
vacanaṃ mama dharmajña bhrātā jyeṣṭho bhavāmi te ||47||

48na te'rjunastathā jñeyo jñātavyaḥ sātyakiryathā
|
cikīṣurmatpriyaṃ pārtha sa yātaḥ savyasācinaḥ
|
padavīṃ durgamāṃ ghorāmagamyāmakṛtātmabhiḥ ||48||

49dṛṣṭvā kuśalinau kṛṣṇau sātvataṃ caiva sātyakim
|
saṃvidaṃ caiva kuryāstvaṃ siṃhanādena pāṇḍava ||49||
