Mahābhārata 12.167 (Śāntiparvan)

1vaiśampāyana uvāca|
ityuktavati bhīṣme tu tūṣṇīṃbhūte yudhiṣṭhiraḥ
|
papracchāvasathaṃ gatvā bhrātṝn vidurapañcamān ||1||

2dharme cārthe ca kāme ca lokavṛttiḥ samāhitā
|
teṣāṃ garīyān katamo madhyamaḥ ko laghuśca kaḥ ||2||

3kasmiṃścātmā nidhātavyastrivargavijayāya vai
|
saṃhṛṣṭā naiṣṭhikaṃ vākyaṃ yathāvadvaktumarhatha ||3||

4tato'rthagatitattvajñaḥ prathamaṃ pratibhānavān
|
jagāda viduro vākyaṃ dharmaśāstramanusmaran ||4||

5vidura uvāca |
bāhuśrutyaṃ tapastyāgaḥ śraddhā yajñakriyā kṣamā
|
bhāvaśuddhirdayā satyaṃ saṃyamaścātmasampadaḥ ||5||

6etadevābhipadyasva mā te'bhūccalitaṃ manaḥ
|
etanmūlau hi dharmārthāvetadekapadaṃ hi me ||6||

7dharmeṇaivarṣayastīrṇā dharme lokāḥ pratiṣṭhitāḥ
|
dharmeṇa devā vavṛdhurdharme cārthaḥ samāhitaḥ ||7||

8dharmo rājan guṇaḥ śreṣṭho madhyamo hyartha ucyate
|
kāmo yavīyāniti ca pravadanti manīṣiṇaḥ ||8||

9tasmāddharmapradhānena bhavitavyaṃ yatātmanā
|
tathā ca sarvabhūteṣu vartitavyaṃ yathātmani ||9||

10vaiśampāyana uvāca|
samāptavacane tasminnarthaśāstraviśāradaḥ
|
pārtho dharmārthatattvajño jagau vākyaṃ pracoditaḥ ||10||

11arjuna uvāca |
karmabhūmiriyaṃ rājanniha vārtā praśasyate
|
kṛṣirvāṇijyagorakṣaṃ śilpāni vividhāni ca ||11||

12artha ityeva sarveṣāṃ karmaṇāmavyatikramaḥ
|
na hyṛte'rthena varteta dharmakāmāviti śrutiḥ ||12||

13viṣayairarthavān dharmamārādhayitumuttamam
|
kāmaṃ ca carituṃ śakto duṣprāpamakṛtātmabhiḥ ||13||

14arthasyāvayavāvetau dharmakāmāviti śrutiḥ
|
arthasiddhyā vinirvṛttāvubhāvetau bhaviṣyataḥ ||14||

15tadgatārthaṃ hi puruṣaṃ viśiṣṭatarayonayaḥ
|
brahmāṇamiva bhūtāni satataṃ paryupāsate ||15||

16jaṭājinadharā dāntāḥ paṅkadigdhā jitendriyāḥ
|
muṇḍā nistantavaścāpi vasantyārthārthinaḥ pṛthak ||16||

17kāṣāyavasanāścānye śmaśrulā hrīniṣeviṇaḥ
|
vidvāṃsaścaiva śāntāśca muktāḥ sarvaparigrahaiḥ ||17||

18arthārthinaḥ santi kecidapare svargakāṅkṣiṇaḥ
|
kulapratyāgamāścaike svaṃ svaṃ dharmamanuṣṭhitāḥ ||18||

19āstikā nāstikāścaiva niyatāḥ saṃyame pare
|
aprajñānaṃ tamobhūtaṃ prajñānaṃ tu prakāśitā ||19||

20bhṛtyān bhogairdviṣo daṇḍairyo yojayati so'rthavān
|
etanmatimatāṃ śreṣṭha mataṃ mama yathātatham
|
anayostu nibodha tvaṃ vacanaṃ vākyakaṇṭhayoḥ ||20||

21vaiśampāyana uvāca|
tato dharmārthakuśalau mādrīputrāvanantaram
|
nakulaḥ sahadevaśca vākyaṃ jagadatuḥ param ||21||

22nakulasahadevāvūcatuḥ
|
āsīnaśca śayānaśca vicarannapi vā sthitaḥ
|
arthayogaṃ dṛḍhaṃ kuryādyogairuccāvacairapi ||22||

23asmiṃstu vai vinirvṛtte durlabhe paramapriye
|
iha kāmānavāpnoti pratyakṣaṃ nātra saṃśayaḥ ||23||

24yo'rtho dharmeṇa saṃyukto dharmo yaścārthasaṃyutaḥ
|
taddhitvā'mṛtasaṃvādaṃ tasmādetau matāviha ||24||

25anarthasya na kāmo'sti tathā'rtho'dharmiṇaḥ kutaḥ
|
tasmādudvijate loko dharmārthādyo bahiṣkṛtaḥ ||25||

26tasmāddharmapradānena sādhyo'rthaḥ saṃyatātmanā
|
viśvasteṣu hi bhūteṣu kalpate sarvameva hi ||26||

27dharmaṃ samācaretpūrvaṃ tato'rthaṃ dharmasaṃyutam
|
tataḥ kāmaṃ caretpaścātsiddhārthaḥ sa hi tatparam ||27||

28vaiśampāyana uvāca|
virematustu tadvākyamuktvā tāvaśvinoḥ sutau
|
bhīmasenastadā vākyamidaṃ vaktuṃ pracakrame ||28||

29bhīmasena uvāca |
nākāmaḥ kāmayatyarthaṃ nākāmo dharmamicchati
|
kāmaḥ kāmayāno'sti tasmātkāmo viśiṣyate ||29||

30kāmena yuktā ṛṣayastapasyeva samāhitāḥ
|
palāśaphalamūlādā vāyubhakṣāḥ susaṃyatāḥ ||30||

31vedopavedeṣvapare yuktāḥ svādhyāyapāragāḥ
|
śrāddhayajñakriyāyāṃ ca tathā dānapratigrahe ||31||

32vaṇijaḥ karṣakā gopāḥ kāravaḥ śilpinastathā
|
devakarmakṛtaścaiva yuktāḥ kāmena karmasu ||32||

33samudraṃ vā viśantyanye narāḥ kāmena saṃyutāḥ
|
kāmo hi vividhākāraḥ sarvaṃ kāmena santatam ||33||

34nāsti nāsīnnābhaviṣyadbhūtaṃ kāmātmakātparam
|
etatsāraṃ mahārāja dharmārthāvatra saṃsthitau ||34||

35navanītaṃ yathā dadhnastathā kāmo'rthadharmataḥ
|
śreyastailaṃ hi piṇyākādghṛtaṃ śreya udaśvitaḥ
|
śreyaḥ puṣpaphalaṃ kāṣṭhāt kāmo dharmārthayorvaraḥ ||35||

36puṣpato madhviva rasaḥ kāma ābhyāṃ tathā smṛtaḥ
|
kāmo dharmārthayoryoniḥ kāmaścātha tadātmakaḥ ||36||

37nākāmato brāhmaṇāḥ svannamarthānnākamato dadati brāhmaṇebhyaḥ
|
nākāmato vividhā lokaceṣṭā tasmātkāmaḥ prāk trivargasya dṛṣṭaḥ ||37||

38sucāruveṣābhiralaṃkṛtābhirmadotkaṭābhiḥ priyadarśanābhiḥ
|
ramasva yoṣābhirupetya kāmaṃ kāmo hi rājanparamo bhavennaḥ ||38||

39buddhirmamaiṣā parikhāsthitasya mābhūdvicārastava dharmaputra
|
syātsaṃhitaṃ sadbhiraphalgusāraṃ mameti vākyaṃ paramānṛśaṃsam ||39||

40dharmārthakāmāḥ samameva sevyā yo hyekabhaktaḥ sa naro jaghanyaḥ
|
tayostu dākṣyaṃ pravadanti madhyaṃ sa uttamo yo'bhiratastrivarge ||40||

41prājñaḥ suhṛccandanasāralipto vicitramālyābharaṇairupetaḥ
|
tato vacaḥ saṃgrahavistareṇa proktvātha vīrān virarāma bhīmaḥ ||41||

42tato muhūrtādatha dharmarājo vākyāni teṣāmanucintya samyak
|
uvāca vācā vitathaṃ smayanvai labdhaśrutāṃ dharmabhṛtāṃ variṣṭhaḥ ||42||

43yudhiṣṭhira uvāca|
niḥsaṃśayaṃ niścitadharmaśāstrāḥ sarve bhavanto viditapramāṇāḥ
|
vijñātukāmasya mameha vākyamuktaṃ yadvai naiṣṭhikaṃ tacchrutaṃ me
|
idaṃ tvavaśyaṃ gadato mamāpi vākyaṃ nibodhadhvamananyabhāvāḥ ||43||

44yo vai na pāpe nirato na puṇye nārthe na dharme manujo na kāme
|
vimuktadoṣaḥ samaloṣṭakāñcano vimucyate duḥkhasukhārthasiddheḥ ||44||

45bhūtāni jātismaraṇātmakāni jarāvikāraiśca samanvitāni
|
bhūyaśca taistaiḥ pratibodhitāni mokṣaṃ praśaṃsanti na taṃ ca vidmaḥ ||45||

46snehena yuktasya na cāsti muktiriti svayambhūrbhagavānuvāca
|
budhāśca nirvāṇaparā bhavanti tasmānna kuryātpriyamapriyaṃ ca ||46||

47etatpradhānaṃ ca na kāmakāro yathā niyukto'smi tathā karomi
|
bhūtāni sarvāṇi vidhirniyuṅkte vidhirbalīyāniti vitta sarve ||47||

48na karmaṇā''pnotyanavāpyamarthe yadbhāvi tadvai bhavatīti vitta
|
trivargahīno'pi hi vindate'rthaṃ tasmādaho lokahitāya guhyam ||48||

49vaiśampāyana uvāca|
tatastadagryaṃ vacanaṃ manonugaṃ samastamājñāya tato hi hetumat
|
tadā praṇeduśca jaharṣire ca te kurupravīrāya ca cakrire'ñjalim ||49||

50sucāruvarṇākṣaracārubhūṣitāṃ manonugāṃ nirdhutavākyakaṇṭakām
|
niśamya tāṃ pārthiva pārthabhāṣitāṃ giraṃ narendrāḥ praśaśaṃsureva te ||50||

51sa cāpi tān dharmasuto mahāmanāstadā pratītānpraśaśaṃsa vīryavān
|
punaśca papraccha saridvarāsutaṃ tataḥ paraṃ dharmamahīnacetasam ||51||
