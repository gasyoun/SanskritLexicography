Mahābhārata 13.134 (Anuśāsanaparvan)

1skanda uvāca|
mamāpyanumato dharmastaṃ śṛṇudhvaṃ samāhitāḥ
|
nīlaṣaṇḍasya śṛṃgābhyāṃ gṛhītvā mṛttikāṃ tu yaḥ ||1||

2abhiṣekaṃ tryahaṃ kuryāttasya dharmaṃ nibodhata
|
śodhayedaśubhaṃ sarvamādhipatyaṃ paratra ca ||2||

3yāvacca jāyate martyastāvacchūro bhaviṣyati
|
idaṃ cāpyaparaṃ guhyaṃ sarahasyaṃ nibodhata ||3||

4pragṛhyaudumbaraṃ pātraṃ pakvānnaṃ madhunā saha
|
somasyottiṣṭhamānasya paurṇamāsyāṃ bali haret ||4||

5tasya dharmaphalaṃ nityaṃ śraddadhānā nibodhata
|
sādhyā rudrāstathā''dityā viśvedevāstathāśvinau ||5||

6maruto vasavaścaiva pratigṛhṇanti taṃ balim
|
somaśca vardhate tena samudraśca mahodadhiḥ ||6||

7eṣa dharmo mayoddiṣṭaḥ sarahasyaḥ sukhāvahaḥ ||7||

8viṣṇuruvāca|
dharmaguhyāni sarvāṇi devatānāṃ mahātmanām
|
ṛṣīṇāṃ caiva guhyāni yaḥ paṭhedāhnikaṃ sadā ||8||

9śṛṇuyādvā'nasūyuryaḥ śraddadhānaḥ samāhitaḥ
|
nāsya vighnaḥ prabhavati bhayaṃ cāsya na vidyate ||9||

10ye ca dharmāḥ śubhāḥ puṇyāḥ sarahasyā udāhṛtāḥ
|
teṣāṃ dharmaphalaṃ tasya yaḥ paṭheta jitendriyaḥ ||10||

11nāsya pāpaṃ prabhavati na ca pāpena lipyate
|
paṭhedvā śrāvayedvāpi śrutvā vā labhate phalam ||11||

12bhuñjate pitaro devā havyaṃ kavyamathākṣayam
|
śrāvayaṃścāpi viprendrān parvasu prayato naraḥ ||12||

13ṛṣīṇāṃ devatānāṃ ca pitṝṇāṃ caiva nityadā
|
bhavatyabhimataḥ śrīmān dharmeṣu prayataḥ sadā ||13||

14kṛtvāpi pāpakaṃ karma mahāpātakavarjitam
|
rahasyadharmaṃ śrutvemaṃ sarvapāpaiḥ pramucyate ||14||

15bhīṣma uvāca|
etaddharmarahasyaṃ vai devatānāṃ narādhipa
|
vyāsoddiṣṭaṃ mayā proktaṃ sarvadevanamaskṛtam ||15||

16pṛthivī ratnasaṃpūrṇā jñānaṃ cedamanuttamam
|
idameva tataḥ śrāvyamiti manyeta dharmavit ||16||

17nāśraddadhānāya na nāstikāya na naṣṭadharmāya na nirghṛṇāya
|
na hetuduṣṭāya gurudviṣe vā nānātmabhūtāya nivedyametat ||17||
