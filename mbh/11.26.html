Mahābhārata 11.26 (Strīparvan)

1śrībhagavānuvācauttiṣṭhottiṣṭha gāndhāri mā ca śoke manaḥ kṛthāḥ
|
tavaiva hyaparādhena kuravo nidhanaṃ gatāḥ||1||

2yattvaṃ putraṃ durātmānamīrṣumatyantamāninam
|
duryodhanaṃ puraskṛtya duṣkṛtaṃ sādhu manyase||2||

3niṣṭhuraṃ vairapuruṣaṃ vṛddhānāṃ śāsanātigam
|
kathamātmakṛtaṃ doṣaṃ mayyādhātumihecchasi||3||

4bhṛtaṃ vā yadi vā naṣṭaṃ yo'tītamanuśocasi
|
duḥkhena labhate duḥkhaṃ dvāvanarthau prapadyate||4||

5taporthīyaṃ brāhmaṇī dhatta garbhaṃ gaurvoḍhāraṃ dhāvitāraṃ turaṅgī
|
śūdrā dāsaṃ paśupālaṃ ca vaiśyā vadhārthīyaṃ kṣatriyā rājaputrī||5||

6vaiśampāyana uvāca|tacchṛtvā vāsudevasya punariktaṃ vaci'priyam
|
tūṣṇīṃ babhūva gāndhārī śokavyākulalocanā||6||

7dhṛtarāṣṭrastu rājarṣirnigṛhyāvuddhijaṃ tamaḥ
|
paryapṛcchata dharmajño dharmarājaṃ yudhiṣṭhiram||7||

8jīvatāṃ parimāṇajñaḥ sainyānāmasi pāṇḍava
|
hatānāṃ yadi jānīṣe parimāṇaṃ vadasva me||8||

9yudhiṣṭhira uvāca|daśāyutānāmayutaṃ sahasrāṇi ca viṃśatiḥ
|
koṭyaḥ ṣaṣṭiśca ṣaṭ caiva hyasmin rājan mṛdhe hatāḥ||9||

10alakṣitānāṃ vīrāṇāṃ sahasrāṇi caturdaśa
|
daśa cānyāni rājendra śataṃ ṣaṣṭiśca pañca ca||10||

11dhṛtarāṣṭra uvāca|yudhiṣṭhira gatiṃ kāṃ te gatāḥ puruṣasattama
|
ācakṣva me mahābāho sarvajño hyasi me mataḥ||11||

12yudhiṣṭhira uvāca|yairhutāni śarīrāṇi hṛṣṭaiḥ paramasaṃyuge
|
devarājasamā~llokāngatāste satyavikramāḥ||12||

13ye tvahṛṣṭena manasā martavyamiti bhārata
|
yudhyamānā hatāḥ saṅkhye gandharvaiḥ saha saṅgatāḥ||13||

14ye ca saṅgrāmabhūmiṣṭhā yācamānāḥ parāṅmukhāḥ
|
śastreṇa nidhanaṃ prāptā gatāste guhyakānprati||14||

15pātyamānāḥ parairye tu hīyamānā nirāyudhāḥ
|
hlīniṣevā mahātmānaḥ parānabhimukhā raṇe||15||

16chidyamānāḥ śitaiḥ śastraiḥ kṣatradharmaparāyaṇāḥ
|
gatāste brahmasadanaṃ na me'trāsti vicāraṇā||16||

17ye tvatra nihatā rājannantarāyodhanaṃ prati
|
yathākathañcitpuruṣāste gatāstūttarān kurūn||17||

18dhṛtarāṣṭra uvāca|kena jñānabalenaivaṃ putra paśyasi siddhavat
|
tanme vada mahābāho śrotavyaṃ yadi vai mayā||18||

19yudhiṣṭhira uvāca|nideśādbhavataḥ pūrvaṃ vane vicaratā mayā
|
tīrthayātrāprasaṅgena samprāpto'yamanugrahaḥ||19||

20devarṣirlomaśo dṛṣṭastataḥ prāpto'smyanusmṛtim
|
divyaṃ cakṣurapi prāptaṃ jñānayogena vai purā||20||

21dhṛtarāṣṭra uvāca|anāthānāṃ janānāṃ ca sanāthānāṃ ca bhārata
|
kaccitteṣāṃ śarīrāṇi dhakṣyanti vidhipūrvakam||21||

22na yeṣāmasti saṃskartā na ca ye'trāhitāgnayaḥ
|
vayaṃ ca teṣāṃ kuryāmo bahutvāttāta karmaṇām||22||

23yānsuparṇāśca gṛdhrāśca vikarṣanti yatastataḥ
|
teṣāṃ tu karmaṇā lokā bhaviṣyanti yudhiṣṭhira||23||

24vaiśampāyana uvāca|evamukto mahārāja kuntīputro yudhiṣṭhiraḥ
|
ādideśa sudharmāṇaṃ dhaumyaṃ sūtaṃ ca sañjayam||24||

25viduraṃ ca mahābuddhiṃ yuyutsuṃ caiva kauravam
|
indrasenamukhāṃścaiva bhṛtyānsūtāṃśca sarvaśaḥ||25||

26bhavantaḥ kārayantveṣāṃ pretakāryāṇyaśeṣataḥ
|
yathā cānāthavatkiñciccharīraṃ na vinaśyati||26||

27śāsanāddharmarājasya kṣattā sūtaśca sañjayaḥ
|
sudharmā dhaumyasahita indrasenādayastathā||27||

28candanāgurukāṣṭhāni tathā kālīyakānyuta
|
ghṛtaṃ tailaṃ ca gandhāṃśca kṣaumāṇi vasanāni ca||28||

29samāhṛtya mahārhāṇi dārūṇāṃ caiva sañcayān
|
rathāṃśca mṛditāstatra nānāpraharaṇāni ca||29||

30citāḥ kṛtvā prayatnena yathāmukhyānnarādhipān
|
dāhayāmāsuravyāgrāḥ śāstradṛṣṭena karmaṇā||30||

31duryodhanaṃ ca rājānaṃ bhrātṝṃścāsya śatādhikān
|
śalyaṃ śalaṃ ca rājānaṃ bhūriśravasameva ca||31||

32jayadrathaṃ ca rājānamabhimanyuṃ ca bhārata
|
dauḥśāsaniṃ lakṣmaṇaṃ ca dhṛṣṭaketuṃ ca pārthivam||32||

33bṛhantaṃ somadattaṃ sṛñjayāṃśca śatādhikān
|
rājānaṃ kṣemadhanvānaṃ virāṭadrupadau tathā||33||

34śikhaṇḍinaṃ ca pāñcālyaṃ dhṛṣṭadyumnaṃ ca pārṣatam
|
yudhāmanyuṃ ca vikrāntamuttamaujasameva ca||34||

35kausalyaṃ draupadeyāṃśca śakuniṃ cāpi saubalam
|
acalaṃ vṛṣakaṃ caiva bhagadattaṃ ca pārthivam||35||

36karṇaṃ vaikartanaṃ caiva sahaputramamarṣaṇam
|
kekayāṃśca maheṣvāsāṃstrigartāṃśca mahārathān||36||

37ghaṭotkacaṃ rākṣasendraṃ bakabhrātarameva ca
|
alambuṣaṃ rākṣasendraṃ jalasandhaṃ ca pārthivam||37||

38etāṃścānyāṃśca subahūnpārthivāṃśca sahasraśaḥ
|
dhṛtadhārāhutairdīptaiḥ pāvakaiḥ samadāhayan||38||

39pitṛmedhāśca keṣāñcitprāvartanta mahātmanām
|
sāmabhiścāpyagāyanta te'nvaśocanta cāparaiḥ||39||

40sāmnāmṛcāṃ ca nādena strīṇāṃ ca ruditasvanaiḥ
|
kaśmalaṃ sarvabhūtānāṃ niśāyāṃ samapadyata||40||

41te vidhūmāḥ pradīptāśca dīpyamānāśca pāvakāḥ
|
nabhasīvānvadṛśyanta grahāstanvabhrasaṃvṛtāḥ||41||

42ye cāpyanāthāstatrāsannānādeśasamāgatāḥ
|
tāṃśca sarvānsamānāyya rāśīnkṛtvā sahasraśaḥ||42||

43citvā dārubhiravyagraiḥ prabhūtaiḥ snehapācitaiḥ
|
dāhayāmāsa tānsarvānviduro rājaśāsanāt||43||

44kārayitvā kriyāsteṣāṃ kururājo yudhiṣṭhiraḥ
|
dhṛtarāṣṭraṃ puraskṛtya gaṅgāmabhimukho'gamat||44||
