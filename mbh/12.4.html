Mahābhārata 12.4 (Śāntiparvan)

1nārada uvāca|
karṇastu samavāpyaivamastraṃ bhārgavanandanāt
|
duryodhanena sahito mumude bharatarṣabha ||1||

2tataḥ kadācidrājānaḥ samājagmuḥ svayaṃvare
|
kaliṅgaviṣaye rājan rājñaścitrāṅgadasya ca ||2||

3śrīmadrājapuraṃ nāma nagaraṃ tatra bhārata
|
rājānaḥ śataśastatra kanyārthe samupāgaman ||3||

4śrutvā duryodhanastatra sametān sarvapārthivān
|
rathena kāñcanāṅgena karṇena sahito yayau ||4||

5tataḥ svayaṃvare tasminsaṃpravṛtte mahotsava
|
samājagmurnṛpatayaḥ kanyātha nṛpasattama ||5||

6śiśupālo jarāsandho bhīṣmako vakra eva ca
|
kapotaromā nīlaśca rukmī ca dṛḍhavikramaḥ ||6||

7sṛgālaśca mahārājaḥ strīrājyādhipatiśca yaḥ
|
aśokaḥ śatadhanvā ca bhojo vīraśca nāmataḥ ||7||

8ete cānye ca bahavo dakṣiṇāṃ diśamāśritāḥ
|
mlecchācāryāśca rājānaḥ prācyodīcyāstathaiva ca ||8||

9kāñcanāṅgadinaḥ sarve śuddhajāmbūnadaprabhāḥ
|
sarve bhāsvaradehāśca vyāghrā iva balotkaṭāḥ ||9||

10tataḥ samupaviṣṭeṣu teṣu rājasu bhārata
|
viveśa raṃgaṃ sā kanyā dhātrī varṣavarānvitā ||10||

11tataḥ saṃśrāvyamāṇeṣu rājñāṃ nāmasu bhārata
|
atyakrāmaddhārtarāṣṭraṃ sā kanyā varavarṇiī ||11||

12duryodhanastu kauravyo nāmarṣayata laṅghanam
|
pratyaṣedhacca tāṃ kanyāmasatkṛtya narādhipān ||12||

13sa vīryamadamattatvādbhīṣmadroṇāvupāśritaḥ
|
rathamāropya tāṃ kanyāmājahāra narādhipaḥ ||13||

14tamanvagādrathī khaḍgī baddhagodhāṅgulitravān
|
karṇaḥ śastrabhṛtāṃ śreṣṭhaḥ pṛṣṭhataḥ puruṣarṣabha ||14||

15tato vimardaḥ sumahān rājñāmāsīdyuyutsatām
|
sannahyatāṃ tanutrāṇi rathānyojayatāmapi ||15||

16te'bhyadhāvanta saṃkruddhāḥ karṇaduryodhanāvubhau
|
śaravarṣāṇi muñcanto meghāḥ parvatayoriva ||16||

17karṇasteṣāmāpatatāmekaikena śareṇa ha
|
dhanūṃṣi ca śaravrātānpātayāmāsa bhūtale ||17||

18tatā vidhanuṣaḥ kāṃścit kāṃścidudyatakārmukān
|
kāṃściccodvahato bāṇān ratha-śakti-gadāstathā ||18||

19lāghavādvyākulīkṛtya karṇaḥ praharatāṃ varaḥ
|
hatasūtāṃśca bhūyiṣṭhānavajigye narādhipān ||19||

20ta svayaṃ vāhayanto'śvān pāhi pāhīti vādinaḥ
|
vyapeyuste raṇaṃ hitvā rājāno bhagnamānasāḥ ||20||

21duryodhanastu karṇena pālyamāno'bhyayāttadā
|
hṛṣṭaḥ kanyāmupādāya nagaraṃ nāgasāhvayam ||21||
