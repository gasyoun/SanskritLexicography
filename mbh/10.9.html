Mahābhārata 10.9 (Sauptikaparvan)

1sañjaya uvāca|
te hatvā sarvapāñcālāndraupadeyāṃśca sarvaśaḥ
|
āgacchansahitāstatra yatra duryodhano hataḥ||1||

2gatvā cainamapaśyanta kiścitprāṇaṃ janādhipam
|
tato rathebhyaḥ praskandya parivavrustavātmajam||2||

3taṃ bhagnasakthaṃ rājendra kṛcchraprāṇamacetasam
|
vamantaṃ rudhiraṃ vaktrādapaśyanvasudhātale||3||

4vṛtaṃ samantādbahubhiḥ śvāpadairghauradarśanaiḥ
|
śālāvṛkagaṇaiścaiva bhakṣayiṣyadbhirantikāt||4||

5nivārayantaṃ kṛcchrāttān śvāpadāṃśca cisvādiṣūn
|
viceṣṭamānaṃ mahyāṃ ca subhṛśaṃ gāḍhavedanam||5||

6taṃ śayānaṃ tathā dṛṣṭvā bhūmau surudhirokṣitam
|
hataśiṣṭāstrayo vīrāḥ śokārtāḥ paryavārayan||6||

7aśvatthāmā kṛpaścaiva kṛtavarmā ca sātvataḥ
|
taistribhiḥ śoṇitādigdhairniḥśvasadbhirmahārathaiḥ||7||

8śuśubhe sa vṛto rājā vedī tribhirivāgnibhiḥ
|
te taṃ śayānaṃ samprekṣya rājānamatathocitam||8||

9aviṣahyena duḥkhena tataste rurudusrayaḥ
|
tatastu rudhiraṃ hastairmukhānnirmṛjya tasya hi
|
raṇe rājñaḥ śayānasya kṛpaṇaṃ paryadevayan||9||

10kṛpa uvāca|
na daivasyātibhāro'sti yadayaṃ rudhirokṣitaḥ
|
ekādaśacamūbharttā śete duryodhano hataḥ||10||

11paśya cāmīkarābhasya cāmīkaravibhūṣitām
|
gadāṃ gadāpriyasyemāṃ samīpe patitāṃ bhuvi||11||

12iyamenaṃ gadā śūraṃ na jahāti raṇe raṇe
|
svargāyāpi vrajantaṃ hi na jahāti yaśasvinam||12||

13paśyemāṃ saha vīreṇa jāmbūnadavibhūṣitām
|
śayānāṃ śayane harmye bhāryāṃ prītimatīmiva||13||

14yo'yaṃ mūrdhābhiṣiktānāmagre jātaḥ parantapaḥ
|
sa hato grasate pāṃsūnpaśya kālasya paryayam||14||

15yenājau nihatā bhūmāvaśerata hatadviṣaḥ
|
sa bhūmau nihataḥ śete kururājaḥ parairayam||15||

16bhayānnamanti rājāno yasya sma śatasaṅghaśaḥ
|
sa vīraśayane śete kravyādbhiḥ parivāritaḥ||16||

17upāsata dvijāḥ pūrvamartha hetoryamīśvaram
|
upāsate ca taṃ hyadya kravyādā māṃsahetavaḥ||17||

18sañjaya uvāca|
taṃ śayānaṃ kuruśreṣṭhaṃ tato bharatasattama
|
aśvatthāmā samālokya karuṇaṃ paryadevayat||18||

19āhustvāṃ rājaśārdūla mukhyaṃ sarvadhanuṣmatām
|
dhanādhyakṣopamaṃ yuddhe śiṣyaṃ saṅkarṣaṇasya ca||19||

20kathaṃ vivaramadrākṣīdbhīmasenastavānagha
|
balinaṃ kṛtinaṃ nityaṃ sa ca pāpātmavānnṛpa||20||

21kālo nūnaṃ mahārāja loke'sminbalavattaraḥ
|
paśyāmo nihataṃ tvāṃ ca bhīmasenena saṃyuge||21||

22kathaṃ tvāṃ sarvadharmajñaṃ kṣudraḥ pāpo vṛkodaraḥ
|
nikṛtyā hatavānmando nūnaṃ kālo duratyayaḥ||22||

23dharmayuddhe hyadharmeṇa samāhūyaujasā mṛdhe
|
gadayā bhīmasenena nirbhagne sakthinī tava||23||

24adharmeṇa hatasyājau mṛdyamānaṃ padā śiraḥ
|
ya upekṣitavān kṣudraṃ dhik kṛṣṇaṃ dhigyudhiṣṭhiram||24||

25yuddheṣvapavadiṣyanti yodhā nūnaṃ vṛkodaram
|
yāvatsthāsyanti bhūtāni nikṛtyā hyasi pātitaḥ||25||

26nanu rāmo'bravīdrājaṃstvāṃ sadā yadunandanaḥ
|
duryodhanasamo nāsti gadayā iti vīryavān||26||

27ślāghate tvāṃ hi vārṣṇeyo rājasaṃsatsu bhārata
|
sa śiṣyo mama kauravyo gadāyuddha iti prabho||27||

28yāṃ gatiṃ kṣatriyasyāhuḥ praśastāṃ paramarṣayaḥ
|
hatasyābhimukhasyājau prāptastvamasi tāṃ gatim||28||

29duryodhana na śocāmi tvāmahaṃ puruṣarṣabha
|
hataputrau tu śocāmi gāndhārīṃ pitaraṃ ca te||29||

30bhikṣukau vicariṣyete śocantau pṛthivīmimām
|
dhigastu kṛṣṇaṃ vārṣṇeyamarjunaṃ cāpi durmatim||30||

31dharmajñamāninau yau tvāṃ vadhyamānamupekṣatām
|
pāṇḍavāścāpi te sarve kiṃ vakṣyanti narādhipa||31||

32kathaṃ duryodhano'smābhirhata ityanapatrapāḥ
|
dhanyastvamasi gāndhāre yastvamāyodhane hṛtaḥ||32||

33prāyaśo'bhimukhaḥ śatrūndharmeṇa puruṣarṣabha
|
hataputrā hi gāndhārī nihatajñātibāndhavā||33||

34prajñācakṣuśca durdharṣaḥ kāṃ gatiṃ pratipatsyate
|
dhigastu kṛtavarmāṇaṃ māṃ kṛpaṃ ca mahāratham||34||

35ye vayaṃ na gatāḥ svargaṃ tvāṃ puraskṛtya pārthivam
|
dātāraṃ sarvakāmānāṃ rakṣitāraṃ prajāhitam||35||

36yadvayaṃ nānugacchāma tvāṃ dhigasmānnarādhamān
|
kṛpasya tava vīryeṇa mama caiva pituśca me||36||

37sabhṛtyānāṃ naravyāghra ratnavanti gṛhāṇi ca
|
tava prasādādasmābhiḥ samitraiḥ saha bāndhavaiḥ||37||

38avāptāḥ kratavo mukhyā bahavo bhūridakṣiṇāḥ
|
kutaścāpīdṛśaṃ pāpāḥ pravartiṣyāmahe vayam||38||

39yādṛśena puraskṛtya tvaṃ gataḥ sarvapārthivān
|
vayameva trayo rājan gacchantaṃ paramāṃ gatim||39||

40yadvai tvāṃ nānugacchāmastena dhakṣyāmahe vayam
|
tatsvargahīnā hīnārthāḥ smarantaḥ sukṛtasya te||40||

41kiṃ nāma tadbhavetkarma yena tvāṃ na vrajāma vai
|
duḥkhaṃ nūnaṃ kuruśreṣṭha cariṣyāma mahīmimām||41||

42hīnānāṃ nastvayā rājankutaḥ śāntiḥ kutaḥ sukham
|
gatvaiva tu mahārāja sametya ca mahārathān||42||

43yathājyeṣṭhaṃ yathāśreṣṭhaṃ pūjayervacanānmama
|
ācāryaṃ pūjayitvā ca ketuṃ sarvadhanuṣmatām||43||

44hataṃ mayādya śaṃsethā dhṛṣṭadyumnaṃ narādhipa
|
pariṣvajethā rājānaṃ bālhikaṃ sumahāratham||44||

45saindhavaṃ somadattaṃ ca bhūriśravasameva ca
|
tathā pūrvagatānanyān svarge pārthivasattamān||45||

46asmadvākyātpariṣvajya saṃpṛcchetvamanāmayam||46||

47sañjaya uvāca|
ityevamuktvā rājānaṃ bhagnasakthamacetanam
|
aśvatthāmā samudvīkṣya punarvacanamabravīt||47||

48duryodhana jīvasi tvaṃ vākyaṃ śrotrasukhaṃ śṛṇu
|
sapta pāṇḍavataḥ śeṣā dhārtarāṣṭrāstrayo vayam||48||

49te caiva bhrātaraḥ pañca vāsudevo'tha sātyakiḥ
|
ahaṃ ca kṛtavarmā ca kṛpaḥ śāradvatastathā||49||

50draupadeyā hatāḥ sarve dhṛṣṭadyumnasya cātmajāḥ
|
pāñcālā nihatāḥ sarve matsyaśeṣaṃ ca bhārata||50||

51kṛte pratikṛtaṃ paśya hataputrā hi pāṇḍavāḥ
|
sauptike śibiraṃ teṣāṃ hataṃ sanaravāhanam||51||

52mayā ca pāpakarmā'sau dhṛṣṭadyumno mahīpate
|
praviśya śibiraṃ rātrau paśumāreṇa māritaḥ||52||

53duryodhanastu tāṃ vācaṃ niśamya manasaḥ priyām
|
pratilabhya punaśceta idaṃ vacanamabravīt||53||

54na me'karottadgāṅgeyo na karṇo na ca te pitā
|
yattvayā kṛpabhojābhyāṃ sahitenādya me kṛtam||54||

55sa ca senāpatiḥ kṣudro hataḥ sārdhaṃ śikhaṇḍinā
|
tena manye maghavatā samamātmānamadya vai||55||

56svasti prāpnuta bhadraṃ vaḥ svarge naḥ saṅgamaḥ punaḥ
|
ityevamuktvā tūṣṇīṃ sa kururājo mahāmanāḥ||56||

57prāṇānupāsṛjadvīraḥ suhṛdāṃ duḥkhamutsṛjan
|
apākrāmaddivaṃ puṇyāṃ śarīraṃ kṣitimāviśat||57||

58evaṃ te nidhanaṃ yātaḥ putro duryodhano nṛpa
|
agre yātvā raṇe śūraḥ paścādvinihataḥ paraiḥ||58||

59tathaiva te pariṣvaktāḥ pariṣvajya ca te nṛpam
|
punaḥ punaḥ prekṣamāṇāḥ svakānāruruhū rathān||59||

60ityevaṃ droṇaputrasya niśamya karuṇāṃ giram
|
pratyūṣakāle śokārttāḥ prādravannagaraṃ prati||60||

61evameṣa kṣayo vṛttaḥ kurupāṇḍavasenayoḥ
|
ghoro viśasano raudro rājan durmantrite tava||61||

62tava putre gate svargaṃ śokārtasya mamānagha
|
ṛṣidattaṃ pranaṣṭaṃ taddivyadarśitvamadya vai||62||

63vaiśampāyana uvāca|
iti śrutvā sa nṛpatiḥ putrasya nidhanaṃ tadā
|
niḥśvasya dīrghamuṣṇaṃ ca tataścintāparo'bhavat||63||
