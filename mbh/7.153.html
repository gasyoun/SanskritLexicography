Mahābhārata 7.153 (Droṇaparvan)

1sañjaya uvāca|
tadudīrṇaṃ gajānīkaṃ balaṃ tava janādhipa
|
pāṇḍusenāmatikramya yodhayāmāsa sarvataḥ||1||

2pañcālāḥ kuravaścaiva yodhayantaḥ parasparam
|
yamarāṣṭrāya mahate paralokāya dīkṣitāḥ||2||

3śūrāḥ śūraiḥ samāgamya śara-tomara-śaktibhiḥ
|
vivyadhuḥ samare'nyonyaṃ ninyuścaiva yamakṣayam||3||

4rathināṃ rathibhiḥ sārdhaṃ rudhirasrāvadāruṇam
|
prāvartata mahadyuddhaṃ nighnatāmitaretaram||4||

5vāraṇāśca mahārāja samāsādya parasparam
|
viṣāṇairdārayāmāsuḥ susaṃkruddhā madotkaṭāḥ||5||

6hayārohānhayārohāḥ prāsa-śakti-paraśvadhaiḥ
|
bibhidustumule yuddhe prārthayanto mahadyaśaḥ||6||

7pattayaśca mahābāho śataśaḥ śastrapāṇayaḥ
|
anyonyamārdayan rājannityaṃ yattāḥ parākrame||7||

8gotrāṇāṃ nāmadheyānāṃ kulānāṃ caiva māriṣa
|
śravaṇāddhi vijānīmaḥ pañcālānkurubhiḥ saha||8||

9te'nyonyaṃ samare yodhāḥ śara-śakti-paraśvadhaiḥ
|
praiṣayanparalokāya vicaranto hyabhītavat||9||

10śarā daśa diśo rājaṃsteṣāṃ muktāḥ sahasraśaḥ
|
na bhrājante yathātattvaṃ bhāskare'staṃgate'pi ca||10||

11tathā prayudhyamāneṣu pāṇḍaveyeṣu bhārata
|
duryodhano mahārāja vyavāgāhata tadbalam||11||

12saindhavasya vadhenaiva bhṛśaṃ duḥkhasamanvitaḥ
|
martavyamiti sañcintya prāviśacca dviṣadbalam||12||

13nādayan rathaghoṣeṇa kampayanniva medinīm
|
abhyavartata putraste pāṇḍavānāmanīkinīm||13||

14sa sannipātastumulastasya teṣāṃ ca bhārata
|
abhavatsarvasainyānāmabhāvakaraṇo mahān||14||

15yathā madhyandine sūryaṃ pratapantaṃ gabhastibhiḥ
|
tathā tava sutaṃ madhye pratapantaṃ śarārcibhiḥ||15||

16na śekurbhrātaraṃ yuddhe pāṇḍavāḥ samudīkṣitum
|
palāyanakṛtotsāhā nirutsāhā dviṣajjaye||16||

17paryadhāvanta pañcālā vadhyamānā mahātmanā
|
rukmapuṅkhaiḥ prasannāgraistava putreṇa dhanvinā||17||

18adyamānāḥ śaraistūrṇaṃ nyapatanpāṇḍusainikāḥ
|
na tādṛśaṃ raṇe karma kṛtavantastu tāvakāḥ||18||

19yādṛśaṃ kṛtavān rājā putrastava viśāṃpate
|
putreṇa tava sā senā pāṇḍavī mathitā raṇe||19||

20nalinī dviradeneva samantātphullapaṅkajā
|
śrīṇatoyānilārkābhyāṃ hatatviḍiva padminī||20||

21babhūva pāṇḍavī senā tava putrasya tejasā
|
pāṇḍusenāṃ hatāṃ dṛṣṭvā tava putreṇa bhārata||21||

22bhīmasenapurogāstu pañcālāḥ samupādravan
|
sa bhīmasenaṃ daśabhirmādrīputrau tribhistribhiḥ||22||

23virāṭadrupadau ṣaḍbhiḥ śatena ca śikhaṇḍinam
|
dhṛṣṭadyumnaṃ ca saptatyā dharmaputraṃ ca saptabhiḥ||23||

24kekayāṃścaiva cedīṃśca bahubhirniśitaiḥ śaraiḥ
|
sātvataṃ pañcabhirviddhvā draupadeyāṃstribhistribhiḥ||24||

25ghaṭotkacaṃ ca samare viddhvā siṃha ivānadat
|
śataśaścāparānyodhānsadvipāṃśca mahāraṇe||25||

26śarairavacakartograiḥ kruddho'ntaka iva prajāḥ
|
sā tena pāṇḍavī senā vadhyamānā śilīmukhaiḥ||26||

27tava putreṇa saṃgrāme vidudrāva narādhipa
|
taṃ tapantamivādityaṃ kururājaṃ mahāhave||27||

28nāśakanvīkṣituṃ rājanpāṇḍuputrasya sainikāḥ
|
tato yudhiṣṭhiro rājā kupito rājasattama||28||

29abhyadhāvatkurupāta tava putraṃ jighāṃsayā
|
tāvubhau yudhi kauravyau samīyaturarindamā||29||

30svārthahetoḥ parākrāntau duryodhanayudhiṣṭhirau
|
tato duryodhanaḥ kruddhaḥ śaraiḥ sannataparvabhiḥ||30||

31vivyādha daśabhistūrṇaṃ dhvajaṃ ciccheda ceṣuṇā
|
indrasenaṃ tribhiścaiva lalāṭe jaghnivānnṛpa||31||

32sārathiṃ dayitaṃ rājñaḥ pāṇḍavasya mahātmanaḥ
|
dhanuśca punaranyena cakartāsya mahārathaḥ||32||

33caturbhiścaturaścaiva bāṇairvivyādha vājinaḥ
|
tato yudhiṣṭhiraḥ kruddho nimeṣādiva kārmukam||33||

34anyadādāya vegena kauravaṃ pratyavārayat
|
tasya tānnighnataḥ śatrūn rukmapṛṣṭhaṃ mahaddhanuḥ||34||

35bhallābhyāṃ pāṇḍavo jyeṣṭhastridhā ciccheda māriṣa
|
vivyādha cainaṃ daśabhiḥ samyagastaiḥ śitaiḥ śaraiḥ||35||

36marma bhittvā tu te sarve saṃlagnāḥ kṣitimāviśan
|
tataḥ parivṛtā yodhāḥ parivavruryudhiṣṭhiram||36||

37vṛtrahatyai yathā devāḥ parivavruḥ purandaram
|
tato yudhiṣṭhiro rājā tava putrasya māriṣa
|
śaraṃ ca sūryaraśmyābhamatyugramanivāraṇam||37||

38hā hato'sīti rājānamuktvā'muñcadyudhiṣṭhiraḥ
|
sa tenākarṇamuktena viddho bāṇena kauravaḥ||38||

39niṣasāda rathopasthe bhṛśaṃ saṃmūḍhacetanaḥ
|
tataḥ pāñcālyasenānāṃ bhṛśamāsīdravo mahān||39||

40hato rājeti rājendra muditānāṃ samantataḥ
|
bāṇaśabdaravaścograḥ śuśruve tatra māriṣa||40||

41atha droṇo drutaṃ tatra pratyadṛśyata saṃyuge
|
hṛṣṭo duryodhanaścāpi dṛḍhamādāya kārmukam||41||

42tiṣṭha tiṣṭheti rājānaṃ bruvanpāṇḍavamabhyayāt
|
pratyudyayustaṃ tvaritāḥ pañcālā jayagṛddhinaḥ||42||

43tāndroṇaḥ pratijagrāha parīpsankurusattamam
|
caṇḍavātoddhutānmeghānnighnan raśmimuco yathā||43||

44tato rājanmahānāsītsaṃgrāmo bhūrivardhanaḥ
|
tāvakānāṃ pareṣāṃ ca sametānāṃ yuyutsayā||44||
