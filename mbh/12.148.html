Mahābhārata 12.148 (Śāntiparvan)

1bhīṣma uvāca|
tato gate śākunike kapotī prāha duḥkhitā
|
saṃsmṛtya sā ca bhartāraṃ rudato śokakarśitā ||1||

2nāhaṃ te vipriyaṃ kānta kadācidapi saṃsmare
|
sarvāpi vidhavā nārī bahuputrāpi śocate ||2||

3śocyā bhavati bandhūnāṃ patihīnā tapasvinī
|
lālitāhaṃ tvayā nityaṃ bahumānācca pūjitā ||3||

4vacanairmadhuraiḥ snigdhairasaṃkliṣṭamanoharaiḥ
|
kandareṣu ca śailānāṃ nadīnāṃ nirjhareṣu ca ||4||

5dumāgeṣu ca ramyeṣu ramitāhaṃ tvayā saha
|
ākāśagamane caiva vihṛtā'haṃ tvayā sukham ||5||

6ramāmi sma purā kānta tanme nāstyadya kiñcana
|
mitaṃ dadāti hi pitā mitaṃ bhrātā mitaṃ sutaḥ ||6||

7amitasya hi dātāraṃ bhartāraṃ kā na pūjayet
|
nāsti bhartṛsamo nātho nāsti bhartṛsamaṃ sukham ||7||

8visṛjya dhanasarvasvaṃ bhartā vai śaraṇaṃ striyāḥ
|
na kāryamiha me nātha jīvitena tvayā vinā ||8||

9patihīnā tu kā nārī satī jīvitumutsahet
|
evaṃ vilapya bahudhā karuṇaṃ sā suduḥkhitā ||9||

10pativratā sampradīptaṃ praviveśa hutāśanam
|
tataścitrāṅgadadharaṃ bhartāraṃ sā'nvapaśyata ||10||

11vimānasthaṃ sukṛtibhiḥ pūjyamānaṃ mahātmabhiḥ
|
citramālyāmbaradharaṃ sarvābharaṇabhūṣitam ||11||

12vimānaśatakoṭībhirāvṛtaṃ puṇyakarmabhiḥ
|
tataḥ svargaṃ gataḥ pakṣī vimānavaramāsthitaḥ
|
karmaṇā pūjitastatra reme sa saha bhāryayā ||12||
