Mahābhārata 12.121 (Śāntiparvan)

1yudhiṣṭhira uvāca|
ayaṃ pitāmahenokto rājadharmaḥ sanātanaḥ
|
īśvaraśca mahādaṇḍo daṇḍe sarvaṃ pratiṣṭhitam||1||

2devatānāmṛṣīṇāṃ ca pitṝṇāṃ ca mahātmanām
|
yakṣarakṣaḥpiśācānāṃ sādhyānāṃ ca viśeṣataḥ||2||

3sarveṣāṃ prāṇināṃ loke tiryagyoninivāsinām
|
sarvavyāpī mahātejā daṇḍaḥ śreyāniti prabho||3||

4ityevamuktaṃ bhavatā daṇḍe vai sacarācaram
|
paśyatā lokamāsaktaṃ sasurāsuramānuṣam||4||

5etadicchāmyahaṃ jñātuṃ tattvena bharatarṣabha
|
ko daṇḍaḥ kīdṛśo da ḍaḥ kiṃrūpaḥ kiṃparāyaṇaḥ
|
kimātmakaḥ kathaṃbhūtaḥ kathaṃmūrtiḥ kathaṃ prabho||5||

6jāgarti ca kathaṃ daṇḍaḥ prajāsvavahitātmakaḥ
|
kaśca pūrvāparamidaṃ jāgarti pratipālayan||6||

7kaśca vijñāyate pūrvaṃ ko varo daṇḍasaṃjñitaḥ
|
kiṃsaṃsthaśca bhaveddaṇḍaḥ kā vā'sya gatirucyate||7||

8bhīṣma uvāca|
śṛṇu kauravya yo daṇḍo vyavahāro yathā ca saḥ
|
yasmin hi sarvamāyattaṃ sa daṇḍa iha kevalaḥ||8||

9dharmasaṃkhyā mahārāja vyavahāra itīṣyate
|
tasya lopaḥ kathaṃ na syāllokeṣvavahitātmanaḥ||9||

10ityevaṃ vyavahārasya vyavahāratvamiṣyate
|
api caitatpurā rājanmanunā proktamāditaḥ||10||

11supraṇītena daṇḍena priyāpriyasamātmanā
|
prajā rakṣati yaḥ samyagdharma eva sa kevalaḥ||11||

12yathoktametadvacanaṃ prāgeva manunā purā
|
yanmayoktaṃ manuṣyendra brahmaṇo vacanaṃ mahat||12||

13prāgidaṃ vacanaṃ proktamataḥ prāgvacanaṃ viduḥ
|
vyavahārasya cākhyānadvyavahāra ihocyate||13||

14daṇḍe trivargaḥ satataṃ supraṇīte pravartate
|
daivaṃ hi paramo daṇḍo rūpato'gnirivotthitaḥ||14||

15nīlotpaladalaśyāmaścaturdaṣṭraścaturbhujaḥ
|
aṣṭapānnaikanayanaḥ śaṃkukarṇordhvaromavān||15||

16jaṭī dvijihvastāmrāsyo mṛgarājatanucchadaḥ
|
etadrūpaṃ bibharyugraṃ daṇḍo nityaṃ durādharaḥ||16||

17asirdhanurgadā śaktistriśūlaṃ mudgaraḥ śaraḥ
|
musalaṃ paraśuścakraṃ pāśo daṇḍarṣṭitomarāḥ||17||

18sarvapraharaṇīyāni santi yānīha kānicit
|
daṇḍa eva sa sarvātmā loke carati mūrtimān||18||

19bhindaṃśchindan rujankṛntandārayanpāṭayaṃstathā
|
ghātayannabhidhāvaṃśca daṇḍa eva caratyuta||19||

20asirviśasano dharmastīkṣṇavarmā durādharaḥ
|
śrīgarbho vijayaḥ śāstā vyavahāraḥ sanātanaḥ||20||

21śāstraṃ brāhmaṇamantrāśca śāstā prāgvadatāṃ varaḥ
|
dharmapālo'kṣaro devaḥ satyago nityago'grajaḥ||21||

22asaṃgo rudratanayo manurjyeṣṭhaḥ śivaṃkaraḥ
|
nāmānyetāni daṇḍasya kīrtitāni yudhiṣṭhira||22||

23daṇḍo hi bhagavānviṣṇurdaṇḍo nārāyaṇaḥ prabhuḥ
|
śaśvadrūpaṃ mahadbibhranmahānpuruṣa ucyate||23||

24tathoktā brahmakanyeti lakṣmīrvṛttiḥ sarasvatī
|
daṇḍanītirjagaddhātrī daṇḍo hi bahuvigrahaḥ||24||

25arthānarthau sukhaṃ duḥkhaṃ dharmādharmo balābale
|
daurbhāgyaṃ bhāgadheyaṃ ca puṇyāpuṇye guṇāguṇau||25||

26kāmākāmāvṛturmāsaḥ śarvarī divasaḥ kṣaṇaḥ
|
apramādaḥ pramādaśca harṣakrodhau śamo damaḥ||26||

27daivaṃ puruṣakāraśca mokṣāmokṣau bhayābhaye
|
hiṃsāhise tapo yajñaḥ saṃyamo'tha viṣāviṣam||27||

28antaścādiśca madhyaṃ ca kṛtyānāṃ ca prapañcanam
|
madaḥ pramādo darpaśca dambho dhairyaṃ nayānayau||28||

29aśaktiḥ śaktirityevaṃ mānastambhau vyayāvyayau
|
vinayaśca visargaśca kālākālau ca bhārata||29||

30anṛtaṃ jñānitā satyaṃ śraddhāśraddhe tathaiva ca
|
klībatā vyavasāyaśca lābhālābhau jayājayau||30||

31tīkṣṇatā mṛdutā mṛtyurāgamānāgamau tathā
|
virodhaścāvirodhaśca kāryākārye balābale||31||

32asūyā cānasūyā ca dharmādharmau tathaiva ca
|
apatrapānapatrape hrīśca sampadvipatpadam||32||

33tejaḥ karmāṇi pāṇḍityaṃ vākśaktistattvabuddhitā
|
evaṃ daṇḍasya kauravya loke'smin bahurūpatā||33||

34na syādyadīha daṇḍo vai pramatheyuḥ parasparam
|
bhayāddaṇḍasya nānyo'nyaṃ ghnanti caiva yudhiṣṭhira||34||

35daṇḍena rakṣyamāṇā hi rājannaharahaḥ prajāḥ
|
rājānaṃ vardhayantīha tasmāddaṇḍaḥ parāyaṇam||35||

36vyavasthāpayati kṣipramimaṃ lokaṃ nareśvara
|
satye vyavasthito dharmo brāhmaṇeṣvavatiṣṭhate||36||

37dharmayuktā dvijaśreṣṭhā devayuktā bhavanti ca
|
babhūva yajño vedebhyo yajñaḥ prīṇāti devatāḥ||37||

38prītāśca devatā nityamindre parivadantyapi
|
annaṃ dadāti śakraścāpyanugṛhṇannimāḥ prajāḥ||38||

39prāṇāśca sarvabhūtānāṃ nityamanne pratiṣṭhitāḥ
|
tasmātprajāḥ pratiṣṭhante daṇḍo jāgarti tāsu ca||39||

40evaṃprayojanaścaiva daṇḍaḥ kṣatriyatāṃ gataḥ
|
rakṣanprajāḥ sa jāgarti nityaṃ svavahito'kṣaraḥ||40||

41īśvaraḥ puruṣaḥ prāṇaḥ sattvaṃ cittaṃ prajāpatiḥ
|
bhūtātmā jīva ityevaṃ nāmabhiḥ procyate'ṣṭabhiḥ||41||

42adadaddaṇḍamevāsmai dhṛtamaiśvaryameva ca
|
balena yaśca saṃyuktaḥ sadā pañcavidhātmakaḥ||42||

43kulaṃ bahudhanāmātyāḥ prajñā proktā balāni tu
|
āhāryamaṣṭakairdravyairbalamanyadyudhiṣṭhira||43||

44hastino'śvā rathāḥ pattirnāvo viṣṭistathaiva ca
|
daiśikāścāvikāścaiva tadaṣṭāṅgaṃ balaṃ smṛtam||44||

45athavāṅgasya yuktasya rathino hastiyāyinaḥ
|
aśvārohāḥ padātāśca mantriṇo rasadāśca ye||45||

46bhikṣukāḥ prāḍvivākāśca mauhūrtā daivacintakāḥ
|
kośo mitrāṇi dhānyaṃ ca sarvopakaraṇāni ca||46||

47saptaprakṛti cāṣṭāṅgaṃ śarīramiha yadviduḥ
|
rājyasya daṇḍamevāṅgaṃ daṇḍaḥ prabhava eva ca||47||

48īśvareṇa prayatnena kāraṇātkṣatriyasya ca
|
daṇḍo dattaḥ samānātmā daṇḍo hīdaṃ sanātanam||48||

49rājñāṃ pūjyatamo nānyo yathā dharmaḥ pradarśitaḥ
|
brahmaṇā lokarakṣārthaṃ svadharmasthāpanāya ca||49||

50bhartṛpratyaya utpanno vyavahārastathā'paraḥ
|
tasmādyaḥ sahito dṛṣṭo bhartupratyayalakṣaṇaḥ||50||

51vyavahārastu vedātmā vedapratyaya ucyate
|
maulaśca naraśārdūla śāstroktaśca tathā paraḥ||51||

52ukto yaścāpi daṇḍo'sau bhartṛpratyayalakṣaṇaḥ
|
jñeyo naḥ sa narendrasthau daṇḍaḥ pratyaya eva ca||52||

53daṇḍapratyayadṛṣṭo'pi vyavahārātmakaḥ smṛtaḥ
|
vyavahāraḥ smṛto yaśca sa vedaviṣayātmakaḥ||53||

54yaśca vedaprasūtātmā sa dharmo guṇadarśanaḥ
|
dharmapratyaya uddiṣṭo yathādharmaṃ kṛtātmabhiḥ||54||

55vyavahāraḥ prajāgoptā brahmadiṣṭo yudhiṣṭhira
|
trīndhārayati lokānvai satyātmā bhūtivardhanaḥ||55||

56yaśca daṇḍaḥ sa dṛṣṭo no vyavahāraḥ sanātanaḥ
|
vyavahāraśca dṛṣṭo yaḥ sa veda iti niścitam||56||

57yaśca vedaḥ sa vai dharmo yaśca dharmaḥ sa satpathaḥ
|
brahmā pitāmahaḥ pūrvaṃ babhūvātha prajāpatiḥ||57||

58lokānāṃ sa hi sarveṣāṃ sasurāsurarakṣasām
|
samanuṣyoragavatāṃ kartā caiva sa bhūtakṛt||58||

59tato'nyo vyavahāro'yaṃ bhartṛpratyayalakṣaṇaḥ
|
tasmādidamathovāca vyavahāranidarśanam||59||

60mātā pitā ca bhrātā ca bhāryā caiva purohitāḥ
|
nādaṇḍyo vidyate rājño yaḥ svadharme na tiṣṭhati||60||
