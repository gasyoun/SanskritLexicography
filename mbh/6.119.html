Mahābhārata 6.119 (Bhīṣmaparvan)

1sañjaya uvāca|
evaṃ te pāṇḍavāḥ sarve puraskṛtya śikhaṇḍinam
|
vivyadhuḥ samare bhīṣmaṃ parivārya samantataḥ ||1||

2śataghnībhiḥ sughorābhiḥ parighaiśca paraśvadhaiḥ
|
mudgarairmusalaiḥ prāsaiḥ kṣepaṇīyaiśca sarvaśaḥ ||2||

3śaraiḥ kanakapuṅkhaiśca śaktitomarakampanaiḥ
|
nārācairvatsadantaiśca bhuśuṇḍībhiśca sarvaśaḥ ||3||

4atāḍayan raṇe bhīṣmaṃ sahitāḥ sarvasṛñjayāḥ
|
sa viśīrṇatanutrāṇaḥ pīḍito bahubhistadā ||4||

5na vivyathe tadā bhīṣmo bhidyamāneṣu marmasu
|
sandīptaśaracāpāgnirastraprasṛtamārutaḥ ||5||

6neminirhrādasantāpo mahāstrodayapāvakaḥ
|
citracāpamahājvālo vīrakṣayamahendhanaḥ ||6||

7yugāntāgnisamaprakhyaḥ pareṣāṃ samapadyata
|
vivṛttya rathasaṅghānāmantareṇa viniḥsṛtaḥ ||7||

8dṛśyate sma narendrāṇāṃ punarmadhyagataścaran
|
tataḥ pañcālarājaṃ ca dhṛṣṭaketumacintya ca ||8||

9pāṇḍavānīkinīmadhyamāsasāda viśāṃpate
|
tataḥ sātyakibhīmau ca pāṇḍavaṃ ca dhanañjayam ||9||

10drupadaṃ ca virāṭaṃ ca dhṛṣṭadyumnaṃ ca pārṣatam
|
bhīmaghoṣairmahāvegairmarmāvaraṇabhedibhiḥ ||10||

11ṣaḍetānniśitairbhīṣmaḥ pravivyādhottamaiḥ śaraiḥ
|
tasya te niśitānbāṇānsannivārya mahārathāḥ ||11||

12daśabhirdaśabhirbhīṣmamardayāmāsurojasā
|
śikhaṇḍī tu mahābāṇān‌ yānmumoca mahārathaḥ ||12||

13na cakruste rujaṃ tasya svarṇapuṅkhāḥ śilāśitāḥ
|
tataḥ kirīṭī saṃrabdho bhīṣmamevābhyadhāvata ||13||

14śikhaṇḍinaṃ puraskṛtya dhanuścāsya samācchinat
|
bhīṣmasya dhanuṣaścheda nāmṛṣyanta mahārathāḥ ||14||

15droṇaśca kṛtavarmā ca saindhavaśca jayadrathaḥ
|
bhūriśravāḥ śalaḥ śalyo bhagadattastathaiva ca ||15||

16saptaite paramakruddhāḥ kirīṭinamabhidrutāḥ
|
tatra śastrāṇi divyāni darśayanto mahārathāḥ ||16||

17abhipeturbhṛśaṃ kruddhāśchādayantaśca pāṇḍavam
|
teṣāmāpatatāṃ śabdaḥ śuśruve phālgunaṃ prati ||17||

18uddhṛtānāṃ yathā śabdaḥ samudrāṇāṃ yugakṣaye
|
ghnatānayata gṛhṇīta viddhyadhvamavakartata ||18||

19ityāsīttumulaḥ śabdaḥ phālgunasya rathaṃ prati
|
taṃ śabdaṃ tumulaṃ śrutvā pāṇḍavānāṃ mahārathāḥ ||19||

20abhyadhāvan parīpsantaḥ phālgunaṃ bharatarṣabha
|
sātyakirbhīmasenaśca dhṛṣṭadyumnaśca pārṣataḥ ||20||

21virāṭadrupadau cobhau rākṣasaśca ghaṭotkacaḥ
|
abhimanyuśca saṃkruddhaḥ saptaite krodhamūrchitāḥ ||21||

22samabhyadhāvaṃstvaritāścitrakārmukadhāriṇaḥ
|
teṣāṃ samabhavadyuddhaṃ tumulaṃ lomaharṣaṇam ||22||

23saṃgrāme bharataśreṣṭha devānāṃ dānavairiva
|
śikhaṇḍī tu raṇe śreṣṭho rakṣyamāṇaḥ kirīṭinā ||23||

24avidhyaddaśabhirbhīṣmaṃ chinnadhanvānamāhave
|
sārathiṃ daśabhiścāsya dhvajaṃ caikena cicchide ||24||

25so'nyatkārmukamādāya gāṅgeyo vegavattaram
|
tadapyasya śitairbāṇaistribhiściccheda phālgunaḥ ||25||

26evaṃ sa pāṇḍavaḥ kruddha āttamāttaṃ punaḥpunaḥ
|
dhanuściccheda bhīṣmasya savyasācī parantapaḥ ||26||

27sa chinnadhanvā saṃkruddhaḥ sṛkkiṇī parisaṃlihan
|
śaktiṃ jagrāha tarasā girīṇāmapi dāraṇīm ||27||

28tāṃ ca cikṣepa saṃkuddhaḥ phālgunasya rathaṃ prati
|
tāmāpatantīṃ saṃprekṣya jvalantīmaśanīmiva ||28||

29samādatta śitān bhallān pañca pāṇḍavanandanaḥ
|
tasya ciccheda tāṃ śaktiṃ pañcadhā pañcabhiḥ śaraiḥ ||29||

30saṃkruddho bharataśreṣṭha bhīṣmabāhupraveritām
|
sā papāta tathā cchinnā saṃkruddhena kirīṭinā ||30||

31meghavṛndaparibhraṣṭā vicchinneva śatahradā
|
chinnāṃ tāṃ śaktimālokya bhīṣmaḥ krodhasamanvitaḥ ||31||

32acintayadraṇe vīro buddhyā parapurañjayaḥ
|
śakto'haṃ dhanuṣaikena nihantuṃ sarvapāṇḍavān ||32||

33yadyeṣāṃ na bhavedgoptā viṣvakseno mahābalaḥ
|
kāraṇadvayamāsthāya nāhaṃ yotsyāmi pāṇḍavān ||33||

34avadhyatvācca pāṇḍūnāṃ strībhāvācca śikhaṇḍinaḥ
|
pitrā tuṣṭena me pūrvaṃ yadā kālīmudāvaham ||34||

35svacchandamaraṇaṃ dattamavadhyatvaṃ raṇe tathā
|
tasmānmṛtyumahaṃ manye prāptakālamivātmanaḥ ||35||

36evaṃ jñātvā vyavasitaṃ bhīṣmasyāmitatejasaḥ
|
ṛṣayo vasavaścaiva viyatsthā bhīṣmamabruvan ||36||

37yatte vyavasitaṃ tāta tadasmākamapi priyam
|
tatkuruṣva mahārāja yuddhe buddhiṃ nivartaya ||37||

38asya vākyasya nidhane prādurāsīcchivo'nilaḥ
|
anulomaḥ sugandhī ca pṛṣataiśca samanvitaḥ ||38||

39devadundubhayaścaiva saṃpraṇedurmahāsvanāḥ
|
papāta puṣpavṛṣṭiśca bhīṣmasyopari māriṣa ||39||

40na ca tacchuśruve kaścitteṣāṃ saṃvadatāṃ nṛpa
|
ṛte bhīṣmaṃ mahābāhuṃ māṃ cāpi munitejasā ||40||

41saṃbhramaśca mahānāsīt tridaśānāṃ viśāṃpate
|
patiṣyati rathādbhīṣme sarvalokapriye tadā ||41||

42iti devagaṇānāṃ ca vākyaṃ śrutvā mahātapāḥ
|
tataḥ śāntanavo bhīṣmo bībhatsuṃ nātyavartata ||42||

43bhidyamānaḥ śitairbāṇaiḥ sarvāvaraṇabhedibhiḥ
|
śikhaṇḍī tu mahārāja bharatānāṃ pitāmaham ||43||

44ājaghānorasi kruddho navabhirniśitaiḥ śaraiḥ
|
sa tenābhihataḥ saṃkhye bhīṣmaḥ kurupitāmahaḥ ||44||

45nākampata mahārāja kṣitikampe yathācalaḥ
|
tataḥ prahasya bībhatsurvyākṣipan gāṇḍivaṃ dhanuḥ ||45||

46gāṅgeyaṃ pañcaviṃśatyā kṣudrakāṇāṃ samarpayat
|
punaḥ punaḥ śatairenaṃ tvaramāṇo dhanañjayaḥ ||46||

47sarvagātreṣu saṃkruddhaḥ sarvamarmasvatāḍayat
|
evamanyairapi bhṛśaṃ viddhyamānaḥ sahasraśaḥ ||47||

48tānapyāśu śarairbhīṣmaḥ pravivyādha mahārathaḥ
|
taiśca muktāñcharān bhīṣmo yudhi satyaparākramaḥ ||48||

49nivārayāmāsa śaraiḥ samaṃ sannataparvabhiḥ
|
śikhaṇḍī tu raṇe bāṇān yānmumoca mahārathaḥ ||49||

50na cakruste rujaṃ tasya rukmapuṅkhāḥ śilāśitāḥ
|
tataḥ kirīṭī saṃkruddho bhīṣmamevābhyavartata ||50||

51śikhaṇḍinaṃ puraskṛtya dhanuścāsya samācchinat
|
athainaṃ navabhirviddhvā dhvajamekena cicchide ||51||

52sārathiṃ viśikhaiścāsya daśabhiḥ samakampayat
|
so'nyat kārmukamādāya gāṅgeyo balavattaram ||52||

53tadapyasya śitairbhallaistridhā tribhiraghātayat
|
nimeṣārdhena kaunteya āttamāttaṃ mahāraṇe ||53||

54evamasya dhanūṃṣyājau ciccheda subahūnyathā
|
tataḥ śāntanavo bhīṣmo bībhatsuṃ nātyavartata ||54||

55athainaṃ pañcaviṃśatyā kṣudrakāṇāṃ samārpayat
|
so'tividdho maheṣvāso duḥśāsanamabhāṣata ||55||

56eṣa pārtho raṇe kuddhaḥ pāṇḍavānāṃ mahārathaḥ
|
śarairanekasāhastrairmāmevābhyahanadraṇe ||56||

57na caiṣa samare śakyo jetuṃ vajrabhṛtā api
|
na cāpi sahitā vīrā devadānavarākṣasāḥ ||57||

58māṃ cāpi śaktāṃ nirjetuṃ kimu martyā mahārathāḥ
|
evaṃ tayoḥ saṃvadatoḥ phālguno niśitaiḥ śaraiḥ ||58||

59śikhaṇḍinaṃ puraskṛtya bhīṣmaṃ vivyādha saṃyuge
|
tato duḥśāsanaṃ bhūyaḥ smayamāna ivābravīt ||59||

60atividdhaḥ śitairbāṇairbhṛśaṃ gāṇḍīvadhanvanā
|
vajrāśanisamasparśā arjunena śarā yudhi ||60||

61muktāḥ sarve'vyavacchinnā neme bāṇāḥ śikhaṇḍinaḥ
|
nikṛntamānā marmāṇi dṛḍhāvaraṇabhedinaḥ ||61||

62musalā iva me ghnanti neme bāṇāḥ śikhaṇḍinaḥ
|
vajradaṇḍasamasparśā vajravegadurāsadāḥ ||62||

63mama prāṇānārujanti neme bāṇāḥ śikhaṇḍinaḥ
|
nāśayantīva me prāṇān yamadūtā ivāhitāḥ ||63||

64gadāparighasaṃsparśā neme bāṇāḥ śikhaṇḍinaḥ
|
bhujagā iva saṃkruddhā lelihānā viṣolbaṇāḥ ||64||

65samāviśaṃti marmāṇi neme bāṇāḥ śikhaṇḍinaḥ
|
arjunasya ime bāṇā neme bāṇāḥ śikhaṇḍinaḥ ||65||

66kṛntanti mama gātrāṇi māghamāṃ segavā iva
|
sarve hyapi na me duḥkhaṃ kuryuranye narādhipāḥ ||66||

67vīraṃ gāṇḍīvadhanvānamṛte jiṣṇuṃ kapidhvajam
|
iti buvañchāntanavau didhakṣuriva pāṇḍavān ||67||

68śaktiṃ bhīṣmaḥ sa pārthāya tataścikṣepa bhārata
|
tāmasya viśikhaiśchittvā tridhā tribhirapātayat ||68||

69paśyatāṃ kuruvīrāṇāṃ sarveṣāṃ tava bhārata
|
carmāthādatta gāṅgeyo jātarūpapariṣkṛtam ||69||

70khaḍgaṃ cānyataraprepsurmṛtyoragre jayāya vā
|
tasya tacchatadhā carma vyadhamatsāyakaistathā ||70||

71rathādanavarūḍhasya tada‌dbhutamivābhavat
|
tato yudhiṣṭhiro rājā svānyanīkānyacodayat ||71||

72abhidravata gāṅgeyaṃ mā vo'stu bhayamaṇvapi
|
atha te tomaraiḥ prāsairbāṇaughaiśca samantataḥ ||72||

73paṭṭiśaiśca sunistriṃśairnārācaiśca tathā śitaiḥ
|
vatsadantaiśca bhallaiśca tamekamabhidudruvuḥ ||73||

74siṃhanādastato ghoraḥ pāṇḍavānāmabhūttadā
|
tathaiva tava putrāśca tedurbhīṣmajayaiṣiṇaḥ ||74||

75tamekamabhyarakṣanta siṃhanādāṃśca cakrire
|
tatrāsīt tumulaṃ yuddhaṃ tāvakānāṃ paraiḥ saha ||75||

76daśame'haniṃ rājendra bhīṣmārjunasamāgame
|
āsīdgāṅga ivāvarto muhūrtamudadheriva ||76||

77sainyānāṃ yudhyamānānāṃ nighnatāmitaretaram
|
asaumyarūpā pṛthivī śoṇitāktā'bhavattadā ||77||

78samaṃ ca viṣamaṃ caiva na prājñāyata kiñcana
|
yodhānāmayutaṃ hatvā tasmin sa daśame'hani ||78||

79atiṣṭhadāhave bhīṣmo bhidyamāneṣu marmasu
|
tataḥ senāmukhe tasmin sthitaḥ pārtho dhanurdharaḥ ||79||

80madhyena kurusainyānāṃ drāvayāmāsa vāhinīm
|
vayaṃ śvetahayādbhītāḥ kuntīputrāddhanañjayāt ||80||

81pīḍyamānāḥ śitaiḥ śastraiḥ prādravāma raṇe tadā
|
sauvīrāḥ kitavāḥ prācyāḥ pratīcyodīcyamālavāḥ ||81||

82abhīṣāhāḥ śūrasenāḥ śivayo'tha vasātayaḥ
|
śālvāśrayāstrigartāśca ambaṣṭhāḥ kekayaiḥ saha ||82||

83sarva ete mahātmānaḥ śarārtā vraṇapīḍitāḥ
|
saṃgrāme na jahurbhīṣmaṃ yudhyamānaṃ kirīṭinā ||83||

84tatastamekaṃ bahavaḥ parivārya samantataḥ
|
parikālya kurūn sarvān śaravarṣairavākiran ||84||

85nipātayata gṛhṇīta yudhyadhvamavakṛntata
|
ityāsīt tumulaḥ śabdo rājan bhīṣmarathaṃ prati ||85||

86nihatya samare rājan śata‌śo'tha sahasraśaḥ
|
na tasyāsīdanirbhinnaṃ gātre dvyaṅgulamantaram ||86||

87evaṃbhūtastava pitā śarairviśakalīkṛtaḥ
|
śitāgraiḥ phālgunenājau prākśirāḥ prāpatadrathāt ||87||

88kiñciccheṣe dinakare putrāṇāṃ tava paśyatām
|
hāheti divi devānāṃ pārthivānāṃ ca bhārata ||88||

89patamāne rathādbhīṣme babhūva sumahāsvanaḥ
|
saṃpatantamabhiprekṣya mahātmānaṃ pitāmaham ||89||

90saha bhīṣmeṇa sarveṣāṃ prāpatan hṛda‌yāni naḥ
|
sa papāta mahābāhurvasudhāmanunādayan ||90||

91indradhvaja ivotsṛṣṭaḥ ketuḥ sarvadhanuṣmatām
|
dharaṇīṃ na sa pasparśa śarasaṅghaiḥ samāvṛtaḥ ||91||

92śaratalpe maheṣvāsaṃ śayānaṃ puruṣarṣabham
|
rathātprapatitaṃ cainaṃ divyo bhāvaḥ samāviśat ||92||

93abhyavarṣacca parjanyaḥ prākampata ca medinī
|
patan sa dadṛśe cāpi dakṣiṇena divākaram ||93||

94saṃjñaḥ copālabhadvīraḥ kālaṃ sañcintya bhārata
|
antarikṣe ca śuśrāva divyā vācaḥ samantataḥ ||94||

95kathaṃ mahātmā gāṅgeyaḥ sarvaśastrabhṛtāṃ varaḥ
|
kālakartā naravyāghraḥ saṃprāpte dakṣiṇāyane ||95||

96sthito'smīti ca gāṅgeyastacchrutvā vākyamabravīt
|
dhārayāmāsa ca prāṇān patito'pi mahītale ||96||

97uttarāyaṇamanvicchan bhīṣmaḥ kurupitāmahaḥ
|
tasya tanmatamājñāya gaṅgā himavataḥ sutā ||97||

98maharṣīn haṃsarūpeṇa preṣayāmāsa tatra vai
|
tataḥ saṃpātino haṃsāstvaritā mānasaukasaḥ ||98||

99ājagmuḥ sahitā draṣṭuṃ bhīṣmaṃ kurupitāmaham
|
yatra śete naraśreṣṭhaḥ śaratalpe pitāmahaḥ ||99||

100te tu bhīṣmaṃ samāsādya ṛṣayo haṃsarūpiṇaḥ
|
apaśyañcharatalpasthaṃ bhīṣmaṃ kurukulodvaham ||100||

101te taṃ dṛṣṭvā mahātmānaṃ kṛtvā cāpi pradakṣiṇam
|
gāṅgeyaṃ bharataśreṣṭhaṃ dakṣiṇena ca bhāskaram ||101||

102itaretaramāmantrya prāhustatra manīṣiṇaḥ
|
bhīṣmaḥ kathaṃ mahātmā sansaṃsthātā dakṣiṇāyane ||102||

103ityuktvā prasthitā haṃsā dakṣiṇāmabhito diśam
|
saṃprekṣya vai mahābuddhiścintayitvā ca bhārata ||103||

104tānabravīcchāntanavo nāhaṃ gantā kathañcana
|
dakṣiṇāvarta āditye etanme manasi sthitam ||104||

105gamiṣyāmi svakaṃ sthānamāsīdyanme purātanam
|
udagāyana āditye haṃsāḥ satyaṃ bravīmi vaḥ ||105||

106dhārayiṣyāmyahaṃ prāṇānuttarāyaṇakāṅkṣayā
|
aiśvaryabhūtaḥ prāṇānāmutsargo hi yato mama ||106||

107tasmātprāṇān dhārayiṣye mumūrṣurudagāyane
|
yaśca datto varo mahyaṃ pitrā tena mahātmanā ||107||

108chandato mṛtyurityevaṃ tasya cāstu varastathā
|
dhārayiṣye tataḥ prāṇānutsarge niyate sati ||108||

109ityuktvā tāṃstadā haṃsānsa śete śaratalpagaḥ
|
evaṃ kurūṇāṃ patite śṛṅge bhīṣme mahaujasi ||109||

110pāṇḍavāḥ sṛñjayāścaiva siṃhanādaṃ pracakrire
|
tasminhate mahāsattve bharatānī pitāmahe ||110||

111na kiñcitpratyapadyanta putrāste bharatarṣabha
|
saṃmohaścaiva tumulaḥ kurūṇāmabhavat tadā ||111||

112kṛpaduryodhanamukhā niḥśvasya rurudustataḥ
|
viṣādācca ciraṃ kālamatiṣṭhanvigatendriyāḥ ||112||

113dadhyuścaiva mahārāja na yuddhe dadhire manaḥ
|
ūrugrāhagṛhītāśca nābhyadhāvanta pāṇḍavān ||113||

114avadhye śantanoḥ putre hete bhīṣme mahaujasi
|
abhāvaḥ sahasā rājan kururājasya tarkitaḥ ||114||

115hatapravīrāstu vayaṃ nikṛttāśca śitaiḥ śaraiḥ
|
kartavyaṃ nābhijānīmo nirjitāḥ savyasācinā ||115||

116pāṇḍavāśca jayaṃ labdhvā paratra ca parāṃ gatim
|
sarve dadhmurmahāśa‌ṅkhān śūrāḥ parighabāhavaḥ ||116||

117somakāśca sapañcālāḥ prāhṛṣyanta janeśvara
|
tatastūryasahasreṣu nadatsu sa mahābalaḥ ||117||

118āsphoṭayāmāsa bhṛśaṃ bhīmarono nanāda ca
|
senayorubhayoścāpi gāṅgeye nihate vibhau ||118||

119saṃnyasya vīrāḥ śastrāṇi prādhyāyanta samaṃtataḥ
|
prākrośanprādravaṃścānye jagmurmāhaṃ tathāpare ||119||

120kṣatraṃ cānye'bhyanindanta bhīṣmaṃ cānye'bhyapūjayan
|
ṛṣayaḥ pitaraścaiva praśaśaṃsurmahāvratam ||120||

121bharatānāṃ ca ye pūrṣe te cainaṃ praśaśaṃsire
|
mahopaniṣadaṃ caiva yogamāsthāya vīryavān ||121||

122japan‌ śāntanavo dhīmān kālākāṅkṣī sthito'bhavat ||122||
