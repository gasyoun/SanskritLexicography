Mahābhārata 7.170 (Droṇaparvan)

1sañjaya uvāca|
tasminsutumule yuddhe vartamāne bhayāvahe
|
dhṛṣṭadyumno mahārāja droṇamevābhyavartata||1||

2saṃdadhāno dhanuḥśreṣṭhaṃ jyāṃ vikarṣanpunaḥ punaḥ
|
abhyadravata droṇasya rathaṃ rukmavibhūṣitam||2||

3dhṛṣṭadyumnamathāyāntaṃ droṇasyāntacikīrṣayā
|
parivavrurmahārāja pañcālāḥ pāṇḍavaiḥ saha||3||

4tathā parivṛtaṃ dṛṣṭvā droṇamācāryasattamam
|
putrāste sarvato yattā rarakṣudrauṇamāhave||4||

5balārṇavau tatastau tu sameyātāṃ niśāmukhe
|
vātoddhūtau kṣubdhasattvau bhairavau sāgarāviva||5||

6tato droṇaṃ mahārāja pāñcālyaḥ pañcabhiḥ śaraiḥ
|
vivyādha hṛdaye tūrṇaṃ siṃhanādaṃ nanāda ca||6||

7taṃ droṇaḥ pañcaviṃśatyā viddhvā bhārata saṃyuge
|
cicchedānyena bhallena dhanurasya mahāsvanam||7||

8dhṛṣṭadyumnastu nirviddho droṇena bharatarṣabha
|
utsasarja dhanustūrṇaṃ sandaśya daśanacchadam||8||

9tataḥ kruddho mahārāja dhṛṣṭadyumnaḥ pratāpavān
|
ādade'nyaddhanuḥ śreṣṭhaṃ droṇasyāntācakarṣiyā||9||

10vikṛṣya ca dhanuścitramākarṇātparavīrahā
|
droṇasyāntakaraṃ ghoraṃ vyasṛjatsāyakaṃ tataḥ||10||

11sa visṛṣṭo balavatā śaro ghoro mahāmṛdhe
|
bhāsayāmāsa tatsainyaṃ divākara ivoditaḥ||11||

12taṃ tu dṛṣṭvā śaraṃ ghoraṃ deva-gandharva-mānavāḥ
|
svastyastu samare rājan droṇāyetyabruvanvacaḥ||12||

13taṃ tu sāyakamāyāntamācāryasya rathaṃ prati
|
karṇo dvādaśadhā rājaṃściccheda kṛtahastavat||13||

14sa cchinno bahudhā rājan sutaputreṇa dhanvinā
|
nipapāta śarastūrṇaṃ nirviṣo bhujago yathā||14||

15dhṛṣṭadyumnaṃ tataḥ karṇo vivyādha daśabhiḥ śaraiḥ
|
pañcabhirdoṇaputrastu svayaṃ droṇastu saptabhiḥ||15||

16śalyaśca daśabhirbāṇaistribhirduḥśāsanastathā
|
duryodhanastu viṃśatyā śakuniścāpi pañcabhiḥ||16||

17pāñcālyaṃ tvarayā'vidhyansarva eva mahārathāḥ
|
sa viddhaḥ saptabhirvīrairdroṇasyārthe mahāhave||17||

18sarvānasaṃbhramādrājan pratyaviddhyantribhistribhiḥ
|
droṇaṃ drauṇiṃ ca karṇaṃ ca vivyādha ca tavātmajam||18||

19te bhinnā dhanvinā tena dhṛṣṭadyumnaṃ punarmṛdhe
|
vivyadhuḥ pañcabhistūrṇamekaiko rathināṃ varaḥ||19||

20drumasenastu saṃkruddho rājanvivyādha patriṇā
|
tribhiścānyaiḥ śaraistūrṇaṃ tiṣṭha tiṣṭheti cābravīt||20||

21sa tu taṃ prativivyādha tribhistīkṣṇairajihmagaiḥ
|
svarṇapuṅkhaiḥ śilādhautaiḥ prāṇāntakaraṇairyudhi||21||

22bhallenānyena tu punaḥ suvarṇojvalakuṇḍalam
|
nicakarta śiraḥ kāyāddrumasenasya vīryavān||22||

23tacchiro nyapatadbhūmau sandaṣṭauṣṭhapuṭaṃ raṇe
|
mahāvātasamudbhūtaṃ pakvaṃ tālaphalaṃ yathā||23||

24tānsa viddhvā punaryodhān vīraḥ suniśitaiḥ śaraiḥ
|
rādheyasyācchinadbhallaiḥ kārmukaṃ citrayodhinaḥ||24||

25na tu tanmamṛṣe karṇo dhanuṣaśchedanaṃ tathā
|
nikartanamivātyugraṃ lāṃgūlasya mahāhariḥ||25||

26so'nyaddhanuḥ samādāya krodharaktekṣaṇaḥ śvasan
|
abhyadravaccharaughaistaṃ dhṛṣṭadyumnaṃ mahābalam||26||

27dṛṣṭvā karṇaṃ tu saṃrabdhaṃ te vīrāḥ ṣaḍratharṣabhāḥ
|
pāñcālyaputraṃ tvaritāḥ parivavrurjighāṃsayā||27||

28ṣaṇṇāṃ yodhapravīrāṇāṃ tāvakānāṃ puraskṛtam
|
mṛtyorāsyamanuprāptaṃ dhṛṣṭadyumnamamaṃsmahi||28||

29etasminneva kāle tu dāśārho vikirañcharān
|
dhṛṣṭadyumnaṃ parākrāntaṃ sātyakiḥ pratyapadyata||29||

30tamāyāntaṃ maheṣvāsaṃ sātyakiṃ yuddhadurmadam
|
rādheyo daśabhirbāṇaiḥ pratyavidhyadajihmagaiḥ||30||

31taṃ sātyakirmahārāja vivyādha daśabhiḥ śaraiḥ
|
paśyatāṃ sarvavīrāṇāṃ māṃ gāstiṣṭheti cābravīt||31||

32sa sātyakestu balinaḥ karṇasya ca mahātmanaḥ
|
āsītsamāgamo rājan balivāsavayoriva||32||

33trāsayan rathaghoṣeṇa kṣatriyān kṣatriyarṣabhaḥ
|
rājīvalocanaṃ karṇaṃ sātyakiḥ pratyavidhyata||33||

34kampayanniva ghoṣeṇa dhanuṣo vasudhāṃ balī
|
sūtaputro mahārāja sātyakiṃ pratyayodhayat||34||

35vipāṭhakarṇinārācairvatsadantaiḥ kṣurairapi
|
karṇaḥ śaraśataiścāpi śaineyaṃ pratyaviddhyata||35||

36tathaiva yuddhyamāno'pi vṛṣṇīnāṃ pravaro yudhi
|
abhyavarṣaccharaiḥ karṇaṃ tadyuddhamabhavatsamam||36||

37tāvakāśca mahārāja karṇaputraśca daṃśitaḥ
|
sātyakiṃ vivyadhustūrṇaṃ samaṃtānniśitaiḥ śaraiḥ||37||

38astrairastrāṇi saṃvārya teṣāṃ karṇasya vā vibho
|
aviddhyatsātyakiḥ kruddho vṛṣasenaṃ stanāntare||38||

39tena bāṇena nirviddho vṛṣaseno viśāṃpate
|
nyapatatsa rathe mūḍho dhanurutsṛjya vīryavān||39||

40tataḥ karṇo hataṃ matvā vṛṣasenaṃ mahāratham
|
putraśokābhisantaptaḥ sātyakiṃ pratyapīḍayat||40||

41pīḍyamānastu karṇena yuyudhāno mahārathaḥ
|
vivyādha bahubhiḥ karṇaṃ tvaramāṇaḥ punaḥ punaḥ||41||

42sa karṇaṃ daśabhirviddhvā vṛṣasenaṃ ca saptabhiḥ
|
sa hastāvāyadhanuṣī tayościccheda sātvataḥ||42||

43tāvanye dhanuṣī sajye kṛtvā śatrubhayaṃkare
|
yuyudhānamavidhyetāṃ samaṃtānniśitaiḥ śaraiḥ||43||

44vartamāne tu saṃgrāme tasminvīravarakṣaye
|
atīva śuśruve rājan gāṇḍīvasya mahāsvanaḥ||44||

45śrutvā tu rathanirghoṣaṃ gāṇḍīvasya ca niḥsvanam
|
sūtaputro'bravīdrājan duryodhanamidaṃ vacaḥ||45||

46eṣa sarvāṃ camūṃ hatvā mukhyāṃścaiva nararṣabhān
|
pauravāṃśca maheṣvāso vikṣipannuttamaṃ dhanuḥ||46||

47pārtho vijayate tatra gāṇḍīvaninado mahān
|
śrūyate rathaghoṣaśca vāsavasyeva nardataḥ||47||

48karoti pāṇḍavo vyaktaṃ karmaupayikamātmanaḥ
|
eṣā vidāryate rājanbahudhā bhāratī camūḥ||48||

49viprakīrṇānyanekāni na hi tiṣṭhanti karhicit
|
vāteneva samuddhūtamabhrajālaṃ vidīryate||49||

50savyasācinamāsādya bhinnā nauriva sāgare
|
dravatāṃ yodhamukhyānāṃ gāṇḍīvapreṣitaiḥ śaraiḥ||50||

51viddhānāṃ śataśo rājan śrūyate niḥsvano mahān
|
śṛṇu dundubhinirghoṣamarjunasya rathaṃ prati||51||

52niśīthe rājaśārdūla stanayitnorivāmbare
|
hāhākāraravāṃścaiva siṃhanādāṃśca puṣkalān||52||

53śṛṇu śabdānbahuvidhānarjunasya rathaṃ prati
|
ayaṃ madhye sthito'smākaṃ sātyakiḥ sātvatāṃ varaḥ||53||

54iha cellabhyate lakṣyaṃ kṛtsnāñjeṣyāmahe parān
|
eṣa pāñcālarājasya putro droṇena saṃgataḥ||54||

55sarvataḥ saṃvṛto yodhaiḥ śūraiśca rathasattamaiḥ
|
sātyakiṃ yadi hanyāma dhṛṣṭadyumnaṃ ca pārṣatam||55||

56asaṃśayaṃ mahārāja dhruvo no vijayo bhavet
|
saubhadravadimau vīrau parivārya mahārathau||56||

57prayatāmo mahārāja nihantuṃ vṛṣṇipārṣatau
|
savyasācī purobhyeti droṇānīkāya bhārata||57||

58saṃsaktaṃ sātyakiṃ jñātvā bahubhiḥ kurupuṅgavaiḥ
|
tatra gacchantu bahavaḥ pravarā rathasattamāḥ||58||

59yāvatpārtho na jānāti sātyakiṃ bahubhirvṛtam
|
te tvaradhvaṃ tathā śūrāḥ śarāṇāṃ mokṣaṇe bhṛśam||59||

60yathā tviha vrajatyeṣa paralokāya mādhavaḥ
|
tathā kuru mahārāja sunītyā suprayuktayā||60||

61karṇasya matamāsthāya putraste prāha saubalam
|
yathendraḥ samare rājanprāha viṣṇuṃ yaśasvinam||61||

62vṛtaḥ sahastrairdaśabhirgajānāmanivartinām
|
rathaiśca daśasāhastraistūrṇaṃ yāhi dhanañjayam||62||

63duḥśāsano durviṣahaḥ subāhurduḥpradharṣaṇaḥ
|
ete tvāmanuyāsyanti pattibhirbahubhirvṛtāḥ||63||

64jahi kṛṣṇau mahābāho dharmarājaṃ ca mātula
|
nakulaṃ sahadevaṃ ca bhīmasenaṃ tathaiva ca||64||

65devānāmiva devendra jayāśā tvayi me sthitā
|
jahi mātula kaunteyānasurāniva pāvakiḥ||65||

66evamukto yayau pārthānputreṇa tava saubalaḥ
|
mahatyā senayā sārdhaṃ saha putraiśca te vibho||66||

67priyārthaṃ tava putrāṇāṃ didhakṣuḥ pāṇḍunandanān
|
tataḥ pravavṛte yuddhaṃ tāvakānāṃ paraiḥ saha||67||

68prayāte saubale rājanpāṇḍavānāmanīkinīm
|
balena mahatā yuktaḥ sūtaputrastu sātvatam||68||

69abhyayāttvarito yuddhe kirañśaraśatānbahūn
|
tathaiva pārthivāḥ sarve sātyakiṃ paryavārayan||69||

70bhāradvājastato gatvā dhṛṣṭadyumnarathaṃ prati
|
mahadyuddhaṃ tadāsīttu droṇasya niśi bhārata
|
dhṛṣṭadyumnena vīreṇa pañcālaiśca sahādbhutam||70||
