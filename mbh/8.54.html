Mahābhārata 8.54 (Karṇaparvan)

1sañjaya uvāca|
kṛtavarmā kṛpo drauṇiḥ sūtaputraśca māriṣa
|
ulūkaḥ saubalaścaiva rājā ca saha sodaraiḥ ||1||

2sīdamānāṃ camūṃ dṛṣṭvā pāṇḍuputrabhayārditām
|
samujjahruḥ sma vegena bhinnāṃ nāvamivārṇave ||2||

3tato yuddhamatīvāsīnmuhūrtamiva bhārata
|
bhīrūṇāṃ trāsajananaṃ śūrāṇāṃ harṣavardhanam ||3||

4kṛpeṇa śaravarṣāṇi pratimuktāni saṃyuge
|
sṛñjayāṃśchādayāmāsuḥ śalabhānāṃ vrajā iva ||4||

5śikhaṇḍī ca tataḥ kruddho gautamaṃ tvarito yayau
|
vavarṣa śaravarṣāṇi samantāddvijapuṅgavam ||5||

6kṛpastu śaravarṣaṃ tadvinihatya mahāstravit
|
śikhaṇḍinaṃ raṇe kruddho vivyādha daśabhiḥ śaraiḥ ||6||

7tataḥ śikhaṇḍī kupitaḥ śaraiḥ saptabhirāhave
|
kṛpaṃ vivyādha kupitaṃ kaṅkapatrairajihmagaiḥ ||7||

8tataḥ kṛpaḥ śaraistīkṣṇaiḥ so'tividdho mahārathaḥ
|
vyaśvasūtarathaṃ cakre śikhaṇḍinamatho dvijaḥ ||8||

9hatāśvāttu tato yānādavaplutya mahārathaḥ
|
khaḍgaṃ carma tathā gṛhya satvaraṃ brāhmaṇaṃ yayau ||9||

10tamāpatantaṃ sahasā śaraiḥ sannataparvabhiḥ
|
chādayāmāsa samare tada‌dbhutamivābhavat ||10||

11tatrādbhutamapaśyāma śilānāṃ plavanaṃ yathā
|
niśceṣṭastadraṇe rājañchikhaṇḍī samatiṣṭhata ||11||

12kṛpeṇa cchāditaṃ dṛṣṭvā nṛpottamaśikhaṇḍinam
|
pratyudyayau kṛpaṃ tūrṇaṃ dhṛṣṭadyumno mahārathaḥ ||12||

13dhṛṣṭadyumnaṃ tato yāntaṃ śāradvatarathaṃ prati
|
pratijagrāha vegena kṛtavarmā mahārathaḥ ||13||

14yudhiṣṭhiramathāyāntaṃ śāradvatarathaṃ prati
|
saputraṃ sahasainyaṃ ca droṇaputro nyavārayat ||14||

15nakulaṃ sahadevaṃ ca tvaramāṇau mahārathau
|
pratijagrāha te putraḥ śaravarṣeṇa vārayan ||15||

16bhīmasenaṃ karūṣāṃśca kekayānsaha sṛñjayaiḥ
|
karṇo vaikartano yuddhe vārayāmāsa bhārata ||16||

17śikhaṇḍinastato bāṇānkṛpaḥ śāradvato yudhi
|
prāhiṇottvarayā yukto didhakṣuriva māriṣa ||17||

18tāñcharānpreṣitāṃstena samantātsvarṇabhūṣitān
|
ciccheda khaḍgamāvidhya bhrāmayaṃśca punaḥ punaḥ ||18||

19śatacandraṃ ca taccarma gautamastasya bhārata
|
vyadhamatsāyakaistūrṇaṃ tata uccukruśurjanāḥ ||19||

20sa vicarmā mahārāja khaḍgapāṇirupādravat
|
kṛpasya vaśamāpanno mṛtyorāsyamivāturaḥ ||20||

21śāradvataśarairgrastaṃ kliśyamānaṃ mahābalaḥ
|
citraketusuto rājansuketustvarito yayau ||21||

22vikiranbrāhmaṇaṃ yuddhe bahubhirniśitaiḥ śaraiḥ
|
abhyāpatada‌meyātmā gautamasya rathaṃ prati ||22||

23dṛṣṭvā ca yuktaṃ taṃ yuddhe brāhmaṇaṃ caritavratam
|
apayātastatastūrṇaṃ śikhaṇḍī rājasattama ||23||

24suketustu tato rājan gautamaṃ navabhiḥ śaraiḥ
|
viddhvā vivyādha saptatyā punaścainaṃ tribhiḥ śaraiḥ ||24||

25athāsya saśaraṃ cāpaṃ punaściccheda māriṣa
|
sārathiṃ ca śareṇāsya bhṛśaṃ marmasvatāḍayat ||25||

26gautamastu tataḥ kruddho dhanurguhya navaṃ dṛḍham
|
suketuṃ triṃśatā bāṇaiḥ sarvamarmasvatāḍayat ||26||

27sa vihvalitasarvāṅgaḥ pracacāla rathottame
|
bhūmikaṃpe yathā vṛkṣaścacāla kampito bhṛśam ||27||

28calatastasya kāyāttu śiro jvalitakuṇḍalam
|
soṣṇīṣaṃ saśirastrāṇaṃ kṣurapreṇa tvapātayat ||28||

29tacchiraḥ prāpatadbhūmau śyenāhṛtamivāmiṣam
|
tato'sya kāyo vasudhāṃ paścātprāpatadacyuta ||29||

30tasmin hate mahārāja trastāstasya purogamāḥ
|
gautamaṃ samare tyaktvā dudruvuste diśo daśa ||30||

31dhṛṣṭadyumnaṃ tu samare sannivārya mahārathaḥ
|
kṛtavarmā'bravīddhṛṣṭastiṣṭha tiṣṭheti bhārata ||31||

32tadabhūttumulaṃ yuddhaṃ vṛṣṇipārṣatayo raṇe
|
āmiṣārthe yathā yuddhaṃ śyenayoḥ kruddhayornṛpa ||32||

33dhṛṣṭadyumnastu samare hārdikyaṃ navabhiḥ śaraiḥ
|
ājaghānorasi kruddhaḥ pīḍayan hṛdikātmajam ||33||

34kṛtavarmā tu samare pārṣatena dṛḍhāhataḥ
|
pārṣataṃ sarathaṃ sāśvaṃ chāda‌yāmāsa sāyakaiḥ ||34||

35sarathaśchādito rājan dhṛṣṭadyumno na dṛśyate
|
meghairiva paricchanno bhāskaro jaladhāribhiḥ ||35||

36vidhūya taṃ bāṇagaṇaṃ śaraiḥ kanakabhūṣaṇaiḥ
|
vyarocata raṇe rājan dhṛṣṭadyumnaḥ kṛtavraṇaḥ ||36||

37tatastu pārṣataḥ kruddhaḥ śastravṛṣṭiṃ sudāruṇām
|
kṛtavarmāṇamāsādya vyasṛjatpṛtanāpatiḥ ||37||

38tāmāpatantīṃ sahasā śastravṛṣṭiṃ sudāruṇām
|
śarairanekasāhasrairhārdikyo'vārayadyudhi ||38||

39dṛṣṭvā tu vāritāṃ yuddhe śastravṛṣṭiṃ durāsadām
|
kṛtavarmāṇamāsādya vārayāmāsa pārṣataḥ ||39||

40sārathiṃ cāsya tarasā prāhiṇodyamasādanam
|
bhallena śitadhāreṇa sa hataḥ prāpatadrathāt ||40||

41dhṛṣṭadyumnastu balavāñjitvā śatruṃ mahābalam
|
kauravānsamare tūrṇaṃ vārayāmāsa sāyakaiḥ ||41||

42tataste tāvakā yodhā dhṛṣṭadyumnamupādravan
|
siṃhanādaravaṃ kṛtvā tato yuddhamavartata ||42||
