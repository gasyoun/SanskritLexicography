Mahābhārata 10.18 (Sauptikaparvan)

1śrībhagavānuvāca|
tato devayuge'tīte devā vai samakalpayan
|
yajñaṃ vedapramāṇena vidhivadyaṣṭumīpsavaḥ||1||

2kalpayāmāsuratha te sādhanāni havīṃṣi ca
|
bhāgārhā devatāścaiva yajñiyaṃ dravyameva ca||2||

3tā vai rudramajānantyo yāthātathyena devatāḥ
|
nākalpayanta devasya sthāṇorbhāgaṃ narādhipa||3||

4so'kalpyamāne bhāge tu kṛttivāsā makhe'maraiḥ
|
tataḥ sādhanamanvicchandhanurādau sasarja ha||4||

5lokayajñaḥ kriyāyajño gṛhayajñaḥ sanātanaḥ
|
pañcabhūtanṛyajñaśca jajñe sarvamidaṃ jagat||5||

6lokayajñairnṛyajñaiśca kapardī vidadhe dhanuḥ
|
dhanuḥ sṛṣṭamabhūttasya pañcakiṣkupramāṇataḥ||6||

7vaṣaṭkāro'bhavajjyā tu dhanuṣastasya bhārata
|
yajñāṅgāni ca catvāri tasya saṃnahane'bhavan||7||

8tataḥ kruddho mahādevastadupāpādāya kārmukam
|
ājagāmātha tatraiva yatra devāḥ samījire||8||

9tamāttakārmukaṃ dṛṣṭvā brahmacāriṇamavyayam
|
vivyathe pṛthivī devī parvatāśca cakampire||9||

10na vavau pavanaścaiva nāgnirjajvāla vaidhitaḥ
|
vyabhramaccāpi saṃvignaṃ divi nakṣatramaṇḍalam||10||

11na babhau bhāskaraścāpi somaḥ śrīmuktamaṇḍalaḥ
|
timireṇākulaṃ sarvamākāśaṃ cābhavadvṛtam||11||

12abhibhūtāstato devā viṣayānna prajajñire
|
na pratyabhācca yajñaḥ sa devatāstresire tathā||12||

13tataḥ sa yajñaṃ vivyādha raudreṇa hṛdi patriṇā
|
apakrāntastato yajño mṛgo bhūtvā sa pāvakaḥ||13||

14sa tu tenaiva rūpeṇa divyaṃ prāpya vyarājata
|
anvīyamāno rudreṇa yudhiṣṭhira nabhastale||14||

15apakrānte tato yajñe saṃjñā na pratyabhātsurān
|
naṣṭasaṃjñeṣu deveṣu na prājñāyata kiñcana||15||

16tryambakaḥ saviturbāhū bhagasya nayane tathā
|
pūṣṇaśca dṛśanān kruddho dhanuṣkoṭyā vyaśātayat||16||

17prādravanta tato devā yajñāṅgāni ca sarvaśaḥ
|
kecittatraiva ghūrṇanto gatāsava ivābhavan||17||

18sa tu vidrāvya tatsarvaṃ śitikaṇṭho'vahasya ca
|
avaṣṭabhya dhanuṣkoṭiṃ rurodha vibudhāṃstataḥ||18||

19tato vāgamarairuktā jyāṃ tasya dhanuṣo'cchinat
|
atha tatsahasā rājaṃśchinnajyaṃ visphuraddhanuḥ||19||

20tato vidhanuṣaṃ devā devaśreṣṭhamupāgaman
|
śaraṇaṃ saha yajñena prasādaṃ cākarotprabhuḥ||20||

21tataḥ prasanno bhagavān sthāpya kopaṃ jalāśaye
|
sa jalaṃ pāvako bhūtvā śoṣayatyaniśaṃ prabho||21||

22bhagasya nayane caiva bāhū ca savitustathā
|
prādātpūṣṇaśca daśanānpunaryajñāṃśca pāṇḍava||22||

23tataḥ susthamidaṃ sarvaṃ babhūva punareva hi
|
sarvāṇi ca havīṃṣyasya devā bhāgamakalpayan||23||

24tasmin kruddhe'bhavatsarvamasusthaṃ bhuvanaṃ prabho
|
prasanne ca punaḥ susthaṃ prasanno'sya ca vīryavān||24||

25tataste nihatāḥ sarve tava putrā mahārathāḥ
|
anye ca bahavaḥ śūrāḥ pāñcālasya padānugāḥ||25||

26na tanmanasi kartavyaṃ na ca taddrauṇinā kṛtam
|
mahādevaprasādena kuru kāryamanantaram||26||
