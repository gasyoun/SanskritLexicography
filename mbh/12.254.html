Mahābhārata 12.254 (Śāntiparvan)

1vyāsa uvāca |
hṛdi kāmadrumaścitro mohasaṃcayasambhavaḥ
|
krodhamānamahāskandho vidhitsāpariṣecanaḥ ||1||

2tasya cājñānamādhāraḥ pramādaḥ pariṣecanam
|
so'bhyasūyāpalāśo hi purā duṣkṛtasāravān ||2||

3sammohacintāviṭapaḥ śokaśākho bhayāṅkuraḥ
|
mohanībhiḥ pipāsābhirlatābhiranuveṣṭitaḥ ||3||

4upāsate mahāvṛkṣaṃ sulubdhāstatphalepsavaḥ
|
āyasaiḥ saṃyutāḥ pāśaiḥ phaladaṃ pariveṣṭya tam ||4||

5yastānpāśānvaśe kṛtvā taṃ vṛkṣamapakarṣati
|
gataḥ sa duḥkhayorantaṃ tyajamānastayordvayoḥ ||5||

6saṃrohatyakṛtaprajñaḥ sadā yena hi pādapam
|
sa tameva tato hanti viṣagranthirivāturam ||6||

7tasyānugatamūlasya mūlamūddhriyate balāt
|
yogaprasādātkṛtinā sāmyena paramāsinā ||7||

8evaṃ yo veda kāmasya kevalasya nivartanam
|
bandhaṃ vai kāmaśāstrasya sa duḥkhānyativartate ||8||

9śarīraṃ puramityāhuḥ svāminī buddhiriṣyate
|
tattvabuddheḥ śarīrasthaṃ mano nāmārthacintakam ||9||

10indriyāṇi manaḥpaurāstadarthaṃ tu parākṛtiḥ
|
tatra dvau dāruṇau doṣau tamo nāma rajastathā
|
tadarthamupajīvanti paurāḥ saha pureśvaraiḥ ||10||

11advāreṇa tamevārthaṃ dvau doṣāvupajīvataḥ
|
tatra buddhirhi durdharṣā manaḥ sāmānyamaśnute ||11||

12paurāścāpi manastrastāsteṣāmapi calā sthitiḥ
|
tadarthaṃ buddhiradhyāste so'narthaḥ pariṣīdati ||12||

13yadarthaṃ pṛthagadhyāste manastatpariṣīdati
|
pṛthagbhūtaṃ mano buddhyā mano bhavati kevalam ||13||

14tatrainaṃ vidhṛtaṃ śūnyaṃ rajaḥ paryavatiṣṭhate
|
tanmanaḥ kurute sakhyaṃ rajasā saha saṅgatam
|
taṃ cādāya janaṃ pauraṃ rajase samprayacchati ||14||
