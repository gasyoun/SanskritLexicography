Mahābhārata 1.140 (Ādiparvan)

1vaiśaṃpāyana uvāca|
śrutvā pāṇḍusutānvīrānbalodriktānmahaujasaḥ
|
dhṛtarāṣṭro mahīpālaścintāmagamadāturaḥ||1||

2tata āhūya mantrajñaṃ rājaśāstrārthavittamam
|
kaṇikaṃ mantriṇāṃ śreṣṭhaṃ dhṛtarāṣṭra'bravīdvacaḥ||2||

3dhṛtarāṣṭra uvāca|
utsaktāḥ pāṇḍavā nityaṃ tebhyo'sūye dvijottama
|
tatra me niścitatamaṃ sandhivigrahakāraṇam
|
kaṇika tvaṃ mamācakṣva kariṣye vacanaṃ tava||3||

4vaiśaṃpāyana uvāca|
sa prasannamanāstena paripṛṣṭo dvijottamaḥ
|
uvāca vacanaṃ tīkṣṇaṃ rājaśāstrārthadarśanam||4||

5śṛṇu rājannidaṃ tatra procyamānaṃ mayānagha
|
na me'bhyasūyā kartavyā śrutvaitatkurusattama||5||

6nityamudyatadaṇḍaḥ syānnityaṃ vivṛtapauruṣaḥ
|
acchidraśchidradarśī syātpareṣāṃ vivarānugaḥ||6||

7nityamudyatadaṇḍāddhi bhṛśamudvijate janaḥ
|
tasmātsarvāṇi kāryāṇi daṇḍenaiva vidhārayet||7||

8nāsya cchidraṃ paraḥ paśyecchidreṇa paramanviyāt
|
gūhetkūrma ivāṅgāni rakṣedvivaramātmanaḥ||8||

9nāsamyakkṛtyakārī syādupakramya kadācana
|
kaṇṭako hyapi duśchinna āsrāvaṃ janayecciram||9||

10vadhameva praśaṃsanti śatrūṇāmapakāriṇām
|
suvidīrṇaṃ suvikrāntaṃ suyuddhaṃ supalāyitam||10||

11āpadyāpadi kāle kurvīta na vicārayet
|
nāvajñeyo ripustāta durbalo'pi kathaṃcana||11||

12alpo'pyagnirvanaṃ kṛtsnaṃ dahatyāśrayasaṃśrayāt
|
andhaḥ syādandhavelāyāṃ bādhiryamapi cāśrayet||12||

13kuryāttṛṇamayaṃ cāpaṃ śayīta mṛgaśāyikām
|
sāntvādibhirupāyaistu hanyācchatruṃ vaśe sthitam||13||

14dayā na tasminkartavyā śaraṇāgata ityuta
|
nirudvigno hi bhavati na hatājjāyate bhayam||14||

15hanyādamitraṃ dānena tathā pūrvāpakāriṇam
|
hanyāttrīnpañca sapteti parapakṣasya sarvaśaḥ||15||

16mūlamevāditaśchindyātparapakṣasya nityaśaḥ
|
tataḥ sahāyāṃstatpakṣānsarvāṃśca tadanantaram||16||

17chinnamūle hyadhiṣṭhāne sarve tajjīvino hatāḥ
|
kathaṃ nu śākhāstiṣṭheraṃśchinnamūle vanaspatau||17||

18ekāgraḥ syādavivṛto nityaṃ vivaradarśakaḥ
|
rājannityaṃ sapatneṣu nityodvignaḥ samācaret||18||

19agnyādhānena yajñena kāṣāyeṇa jaṭājinaiḥ
|
lokānviśvāsayitvaiva tato lumpedyathā vṛkaḥ||19||

20aṅkuśaṃ śocamityāhurarthānāmupadhāraṇe
|
ānāmya phalitāṃ śākhāṃ pakvaṃ pakvaṃ praśātayet||20||

21phalārtho'yaṃ samārambho loke puṃsāṃ vipaścitām
|
vahedamitraṃ skandhena yāvatkālasya paryayaḥ||21||

22tataḥ pratyāgate kāle bhindyādghaṭamivāśmani
|
amitro na vimoktavyaḥ kṛpaṇaṃ bahvapi bruvan||22||

23kṛpā na tasminkartavyā hanyādevāpakāriṇam
|
hanyādamitraṃ sāntvena tathā dānena vā punaḥ||23||

24tathaiva bhedadaṇḍābhyāṃ sarvopāyaiḥ praśātayet
|
dhṛtarāṣṭra uvāca|
kathaṃ sāntvena dānena bhedairdaṇḍena vā punaḥ||24||

25amitraḥ śakyate hantuṃ tanme brūhi yathātatham
|
kaṇika uvāca|
śṛṇu rājanyathā vṛttaṃ vane nivasataḥ purā||25||

26jambukasya mahārāja nītiśāstrārthadarśinaḥ
|
atha kaścitkṛtaprajñaḥ śṛgālaḥ svārthapaṇḍitaḥ||26||

27sakhibhirnyavasatsārdhaṃ vyāghrākhuvṛkababhrubhiḥ
|
te'paśyanvipine tasminbalinaṃ mṛgayūthapam||27||

28aśaktā grahaṇe tasya tato mantramamantrayan
|
jambuka uvāca|
asakṛdyatito hyeṣa hantuṃ vyāghravane tvayā||28||

29yuvā vai javasaṃpanno buddhiśālī na śakyate
|
mūṣiko'sya śayānasya caraṇau bhakṣayatvayam||29||

30athainaṃ bhakṣitaiḥ pādairvyāghro gṛhṇātu vai tataḥ
|
tato vai bhakṣayiṣyāmaḥ sarve muditamānasāḥ||30||

31jambukasya tu tadvākyaṃ tathā cakraḥ samāhitāḥ
|
mūṣikā bhakṣitaiḥ pādairmṛgaṃ vyāghro'vadhīttadā||31||

32dṛṣṭvaivāceṣṭamānaṃ tu bhūmau mṛgakalevaram
|
snātvā''gacchata bhadraṃ vo rakṣāmītyāha jambukaḥ||32||

33śṛgālavacanātte'pi gatāḥ sarve nadīṃ tataḥ
|
sa cintāparamo bhūtvā tasthau tatraiva jambukaḥ||33||

34athājagāma pūrvaṃ tu jñātvā vyāghro mahābalaḥ
|
dadarśa jambukaṃ caiva cintākulitamānasam||34||

35vyāghra uvāca|
kiṃ śocasi mahāprājña tvaṃ no buddhimatāṃ varaḥ
|
aśitvā piśitānyadya vihariṣyāmahe vayam||35||

36jambuka uvāca|
śṛṇu me tvaṃ mahābāho yadvākyaṃ mūṣiko'bravīt
|
dhigbalaṃ mṛgarājasya mayādyāyaṃ mṛgo hataḥ||36||

37madbāhubalamāśritya tṛptimadya gamiṣyati
|
garjamānasya tasyaivamato bhakṣyaṃ na rocaye||37||

38vyāghra uvāca|
bravīti yadi sa hyevaṃ kāle hyasminprabodhitaḥ
|
svabāhubalamāśritya haniṣye'haṃ vanecarān||38||

39khādiṣye tatra māṃsāni ityuktvā prasthito vanam
|
etasminneva kāle tu mūṣiko'pyājagāma ha||39||

40tamāgatamabhiprekṣya śṛgālo'pyabravīdvacaḥ
|
jambuka uvāca|
śṛṇu mūṣika bhadraṃ te nakulo yadihābravīt||40||

41mṛgamāṃsaṃ na khādeyaṃ garametanna rocate
|
mūṣikaṃ bhakṣayiṣyāmi tadbhavānanumanyatām||41||

42tacchrutvā mūṣiko vākyaṃ saṃtrastaḥ pragato bilam
|
tataḥ snātvā sa vai tatra ājagāma vṛko napṛ||42||

43tamāgatamidaṃ vākyamabravījjambukastadā
|
mṛgarājo hi saṃkruddho na te sādhu bhaviṣyati||43||

44sakalatrastvihāyāti kuruṣva yadanantaram
|
evaṃ saṃcoditastena jambukena tadā vṛkaḥ||44||

45tato'valumpanaṃ kṛtvā prayātaḥ piśitāśanaḥ
|
etasminneva kāle tu nakulo'pyājagāma ha||45||

46tamuvāca mahārāja nakulaṃ jambuko vane
|
svabāhubalamāśritya nirjitāste'nyato gatāḥ||46||

47mama datvā niyuddhaṃ tvaṃ bhuṅkṣva māṃsaṃ yathepsitam
|
nakula uvāca|
mṛgarājo vṛkaścaiva buddhimānapi mūṣikaḥ||47||

48nirjitā yattvayā vīrāstasmādvīrataro bhavān
|
na tvayāpyutsahe yoddhumityuktvā so'pyupāgamat||48||

49kaṇika uvāca|
evaṃ teṣu prayāteṣu jambuko hṛṣṭamānasaḥ
|
khādati sma tadā māṃsamekasminmantraniścayāt||49||

50evaṃ samācarannityaṃ sukhamedheta bhūpatiḥ
|
bhayena bhedayedbhīruṃ śūramañjalikarmaṇā||50||

51lubdhamarthapradānena samaṃ nyūnaṃ tathaujasā
|
evaṃ te kathitaṃ rājan śṛṇu cāpyaparaṃ tathā||51||

52putraḥ sakhā vā bhrātā vā pitā vā yadi vā guruḥ
|
ripusthāneṣu vartanto hantavyā bhūtimicchatā||52||

53śapathenāpyariṃ hanyādarthadānena vā punaḥ
|
viṣeṇa māyayā vāpi nopekṣete kathaṃcana
|
ubhau cetsaṃśayopetau śraddhavāṃstatra vardhate||53||

54gurorapyavaliptasya kāryākāryamajānataḥ
|
utpathapratipannasya nyāyyaṃ bhavati śāsanam||54||

55kruddho'pyakruddharūpaḥ syātsmitapūrvābhibhāṣitā
|
na cāpyanyamapadhvaṃsetkadācitkopasaṃyutaḥ||55||

56prahariṣyanpriyaṃ brūyātpraharannapi bhārata
|
prahṛtya ca kṛpāyīta śoceta ca rudeta ca||56||

57āśvāsayeccāpi paraṃ sāntvadharmārthavṛttibhiḥ
|
athāsya praharetkāle tathā vicalite pathi||57||

58api ghorāparādhasya dharmamāśritya tiṣṭhataḥ
|
sa hi pracchādyate doṣaḥ śailo meghairivāsitaiḥ||58||

59yaḥ syādanuprāptavadhastasyāgāraṃ pradīpayet
|
adhanānnāsti kāṃścaurānviṣaye sve na vāsayet||59||

60pratyutthānāsanādyena saṃpradānena kenacit
|
ativiśrabdhaghātīsyāttīkṣṇaṃdaṣṭro nimagnakaḥ||60||

61aśaṅkitebhyaḥ śaṅketa śaṅkitebhyaśca sarvaśaḥ
|
aśaṅkyādbhayamutpannamapi mūlaṃ nikṛntati||61||

62na viśvasedaviśvaste viśvaste nātiviśvaset
|
viśvāsādbhayamutpannaṃ mūlānyapi nikṛntati||62||

63cāraḥ suvihitaḥ kārya ātmanaśca parasya vā
|
pāṣaṇḍāṃstāpasādīṃśca pararāṣṭreṣu yojayet||63||

64udyāneṣu vihāreṣu devatāyataneṣu ca
|
pānāgāreṣu rathyāsu sarvatīrtheṣu cāpyatha||64||

65catvareṣu ca kūpeṣu parvateṣu vaneṣu ca
|
samavāyeṣu sarveṣu saritsu ca vicārayet||65||

66vācā bhṛśaṃ vinītaḥ syāddhṛdayena tathā kṣuraḥ
|
smitapūrvābhibhāṣī syātsṛṣṭo raudrasya karmaṇaḥ||66||

67añjaliḥ śapathaḥ sāntvaṃ śirasā pādavandanam
|
āśākaraṇamityevaṃ kartavyaṃ bhūtimicchatā||67||

68supuṣpitaḥ syādaphalaḥ phalavānsyāddurāruhaḥ
|
āmaḥ syātpakvasaṅkāśo na ca jīryeta karhicit||68||

69trivarge trividhā pīḍā hyanubandhāstathaiva ca
|
anubandhāḥ śubhā jñeyāḥ pīḍāstu parivarjayet||69||

70dharmaṃ vicarataḥ pīḍā sāpi dvābhyāṃ niyacchati
|
arthaṃ cāpyarthalubdhasya kāmaṃ cātipravartinaḥ||70||

71agarvitātmā yuktaśca sāntvayukto'nasūyitā
|
avekṣitārthaḥ śuddhātmā mantrayīta dvijaiḥ sahā||71||

72karmaṇā yena kenaiva mṛdunā dāruṇena ca
|
uddhareddīnamātmānaṃ samartho dharmamācaret||72||

73na saṃśayamanāruhya naro bhadrāṇi paśyati
|
saṃśayaṃ punarāruhya yadi jīvati paśyati||73||

74yasya buddhiḥ paribhavettamatītena sāntvayet
|
anāgatena durbuddhiṃ pratyutpannena paṇḍitam||74||

75yo'riṇā saha sandhāya śayīta kṛtakṛtyavat
|
sa vṛkṣāgre yathāsuptaḥ patitaḥ pratibudhyate||75||

76mantrasaṃvaraṇe yatnaḥ sadā kāryo'nasūyatā
|
ākāramabhirakṣeta cāreṇāpyanupālitaḥ||76||

77nācchitvā paramarmāṇi nākṛtvā karma dāruṇam
|
nāhatvā matsyaghātīva prāpnoti mahatīṃ śriyam||77||

78karśitaṃ vyādhitaṃ klinnamapānīyamaghāsakam
|
pariviśvastamandaṃ ca prahartavyamarerbalam||78||

79nārthiko'rthinamabhyeti kṛtārthe nāsti saṅgatam
|
tasmātsarvāṇi sādhyāni sāvaśeṣāṇi kārayet||79||

80saṃgrahe vigrahe caiva yatnaḥ kāryo'nasūyatā
|
utsāhaścāpi yatnena kartavyo bhūtimicchatā||80||

81nāsya kṛtyāni budhyeranmitrāṇi ripavastathā
|
ārabdhānyeva paśyeransuparyavasitānyapi||81||

82bhītavatsaṃvidhātavyaṃ yāvadbhayamanāgatam
|
āgataṃ tu bhayaṃ dṛṣṭvā prahartavyamabhītavat||82||

83daivenopahataṃ śatrumanugṛhṇāti yo naraḥ
|
sa mṛtyumupagṛhṇīyādgarbhamaśvatarī yathā||83||

84anāgataṃ hi budhyeta yacca kāryaṃ puraḥ sthitam
|
na tu buddhikṣayātkiṃcidatikrāmetprayojanam||84||

85utsāhaścāpi yatnena kartavyo bhūtimicchatā
|
vibhajya deśakālau ca daivaṃ dharmādayastrayaḥ
|
naiḥśreyasau tu tau jñeyau deśakālāviti sthitiḥ||85||

86tālavatkurute mūlaṃ bālaḥ śatrurupekṣitaḥ
|
gahane'gnirivotsṛṣṭaḥ kṣipraṃ saṃjāyate mahān||86||

87agniṃ stokamivātmānaṃ sandhukṣayati yo naraḥ
|
sa vardhamāno grasate mahāntamapi saṃcayam||87||

88āśāṃ kālavatīṃ kuryātkālaṃ vighnena yojayet
|
vighnaṃ nimittato brūyānnimittaṃ vā'pi hetutaḥ||88||

89kṣuro bhūtvā haretprāṇānniśitaḥ kālasādhanaḥ
|
praticchanno lomahārī dviṣatāṃ parikartanaḥ||89||

90pāṇḍaveṣu yathānyāyamanyeṣu ca kurūdvaha
|
vartamāno na majjestvaṃ tathā kṛtyaṃ samācara||90||

91sarvakalyāṇasaṃpanno viśiṣṭa iti niścayaḥ
|
tasmāttvaṃ pāṇḍuputrebhyo rakṣātmānaṃ narādhipa||91||

92bhrātṛvyā balino yasmātpāṇḍuputrā narādhipa
|
paścāttāpo yathā na syāttathā nītirvidhīyatām||92||

93vaiśaṃpāyana uvāca|
evamuktvā saṃpratasthe kaṇikaḥ svagṛhaṃ tataḥ
|
dhṛtarāṣṭro'pi kauravyaḥ śokārtaḥ samapadyata||93||
