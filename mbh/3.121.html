Mahābhārata 3.121 (Āraṇyakaparvan)

1lomaśa uvāca|
nṛgeṇa yajamānena someneha purandaraḥ
|
tarpitaḥ śrūyate rājansa tṛpto mudamabhyagāt||1||

2iha devaiḥ sahendraiśca prajāpatibhireva ca
|
iṣṭaṃ bahuvidhairyajñairmahadbhirbhūridakṣiṇaiḥ||2||

3āmūrtarayasaśceha rājā vajradharaṃ prabhuḥ
|
tarpayāmāsa somena hayamedheṣu saptasu||3||

4tasya saptasu yajñeṣu sarvamāsīddhiraṇmayam
|
vānaspatyaṃ ca bhaumaṃ ca yaddravyaṃ niyataṃ makhe||4||

5caṣālayūpacamasāḥ sthālyaḥ pātryaḥ srucaḥ sruvāḥ
|
teṣveva cāsya yajñeṣu prayogāḥ sapta viśrutāḥ||5||

6saptaikaikasya yūpasya caṣālāścopari sthitāḥ
|
tasya sma yūpānyajñeṣu bhrājamānānhiraṇmayān||6||

7svayamutthāpayāmāsurdevāḥ sendrā yudhiṣṭhira
|
teṣu tasya makhāgryeṣu gayasya pṛthivīpateḥ||7||

8amādyadindraḥ somena dakṣiṇābhirdvijātayaḥ
|
prasaṃkhyānānasaṃkhyeyānpratyagṛhṇandvijātayaḥ||8||

9sikatā vā yathā loke yathā vā divi tārakāḥ
|
yathā vā varṣato dhārā asaṃkhyeyāḥ sma kenacit||9||

10tathaiva tadasaṃkhyeyaṃ dhanaṃ yatpradadau gayaḥ
|
sadasyebhyo mahārāja teṣu yajñeṣu saptasu||10||

11bhavetsaṃkhyeyametaddhi yadetatparikīrtitam
|
na tasya śakyāḥ saṃkhyātuṃ dakṣiṇā dakṣiṇāvataḥ||11||

12hiraṇmayībhirgobhiśca kṛtābhirviśvakarmaṇā
|
brāhmaṇāṃstarpayāmāsa nānādigbhyaḥ samāgatān||12||

13alpāvaśeṣā pṛthivī caityairāsīnmahātmanaḥ
|
gayasya yajamānasya tatra tatra viśāṃpate||13||

14sa lokānprāptavānaindrānkarmaṇā tena bhārata
|
salokatāṃ tasya gacchetpayoṣṇyāṃ ya upaspṛśet||14||

15tasmāttvamatra rājendra bhrātṛbhiḥ sahito'cyuta
|
upaspṛśya mahīpāla dhūtapāpmā bhaviṣyasi||15||

16vaiśaṃpāyana uvāca|
sa payoṣṇyāṃ naraśreṣṭhaḥ snātvā vai bhrātṛbhiḥ saha
|
vaidūryaparvataṃ caiva narmadāṃ ca mahānadīm||16||

17samāgamata tejasvī bhrātṛbhiḥ sahito'nagha
|
tatrāsya sarvāṇyācakhyau lomaśo bhagavānṛṣiḥ||17||

18tīrthāni ramaṇīyāni puṇyānyāyatanāni ca
|
yathāyogaṃ yathāprīti prayayau bhrātṛbhiḥ saha
|
tatra tatrā‌dadadvittaṃ brāhmaṇebhyaḥ sahastraśaḥ||18||

19lomaśa uvāca|
devānāmeti kaunteya tathā rājñāṃ salokatām
|
vaidūryaparvataṃ dṛṣṭvā narmadāmavatīrya ca||19||

20saṃdhireṣa naraśreṣṭha tretāyā dvāparasya ca
|
enamāsādya kaunteya sarvapāpaiḥ pramucyate||20||

21eṣa śaryātiyajñasya deśastāta prakāśate
|
sākṣādyatrāpibatsomamaśvibhyāṃ saha kauśikaḥ||21||

22cukopa bhārgavaścāpi mahendrasya mahātapāḥ
|
saṃstambhayāmāsa ca taṃ vāsavaṃ cyavanaḥ prabhu
|
sukanyāṃ cāpi bhāryāṃ sa rājaputrīmavāptavān||22||

23yudhiṣṭhira uvāca|
kathaṃ viṣṭambhitastena bhagavānpākaśāsanaḥ
|
kimarthaṃ bhārgavaścāpi kopaṃ cakre mahātapāḥ||23||

24nāsatyau ca kathaṃ brahmankṛtavānsomapīthinau
|
etatsarvaṃ yathāvṛttamākhyātu bhagavānmama||24||
