Mahābhārata 12.210 (Śāntiparvan)

1yudhiṣṭhira uvāca|
yogaṃ me paramaṃ tāta mokṣasya vada bhārata
|
tamahaṃ tattvato jñātumicchāmi vadatāṃ vara ||1||

2bhīṣma uvāca|
atrāpyudāharantīmamitihāsaṃ purātanam
|
saṃvādaṃ mokṣasaṃyuktaṃ śiṣyasya guruṇā saha ||2||

3kaścidbrāhmaṇamāsīnamācāryamṛṣisattamam
|
tejorāśiṃ mahātmānaṃ satyasandhaṃ jitendriyam ||3||

4śiṣyaḥ paramamedhāvī śreyo'rthī susamāhitaḥ
|
caraṇāvupasaṃgṛhya sthitaḥ prāñjalirabravīt ||4||

5upāsanātprasanno'si yadi vai bhagavanmama
|
saṃśayo me mahānkaścit tanme vyākhyātumarhasi
|
kutaścāhaṃ kutaśca tvaṃ tatsamyagbrūhi yatparam ||5||

6kathaṃ ca sarvabhūteṣu sameṣu dvijasattama
|
samyagvṛttāni vartante viparātāḥ kṣayodayāḥ ||6||

7vedeṣu cāpi yadvākyaṃ laukikaṃ vyāpakaṃ ca yat
|
etadvidvan yathātattvaṃ sarvaṃ vyākhyātumarhasi ||7||

8gururuvāca |
śṛṇu śiṣya mahāprājña brahma guhyamidaṃ param
|
adhyātmaṃ sarvavidyānāmāgamānāṃ ca yadvasu ||8||

9vāsudevaḥ paramidaṃ viśvasya brahmaṇo mukham
|
satyaṃ jñānamatho yajñastitikṣā dama ārjavam ||9||

10puruṣaṃ sanātanaṃ viṣṇuṃ yaṃ taṃ vedavido viduḥ
|
svargapralayakartāramavyaktaṃ brahma śāśvatam ||10||

11tadidaṃ brahma vārṣṇeyamitihāsaṃ śṛṇuṣva me
|
brāhmaṇo brāhmaṇaiḥ śrāvyo rājanyaḥ kṣatriyaistathā ||11||

12vaiśyo vaiśyaistathā śrāvyaḥ śūdraḥ śūdrairmahāmanāḥ
|
māhātmyaṃ devadevasya viṣṇoramitatejasaḥ ||12||

13arhastvamasi kalyāṇaṃ vārṣṇeyaṃ śṛṇu yatparam
|
kālacakramanādyantaṃ bhāvābhāvasvalakṣaṇam ||13||

14trailokyaṃ sarvabhūteśe cakravatparivartate
|
yattadakṣaramavyaktamamṛtaṃ brahma śāśvatam
|
vadanti puruṣavyāghra keśavaṃ puruṣarṣabham ||14||

15pitṝndevānṛṣīṃścaiva tathā vai yakṣarākṣasān
|
nāgāsuramanuṣyāṃśca sṛjate paramo'vyayaḥ ||15||

16tathaiva vedaśāstrāṇi lokadharmāṃśca śāśvatān
|
pralayaṃ prakṛtiṃ prāpya yugādau sṛjate punaḥ ||16||

17yathārtāvṛtuliṅgāni nānārūpāṇi paryaye
|
dṛśyante tāni tānyeva tathā bhāvā yugādiṣu ||17||

18atha yadyadyadā bhāti kālayogādyugādiṣu
|
tattadutpadyate jñānaṃ lokayātrāvidhānajam ||18||

19yugānte'ntarhitānvedānsetihāsān maharṣayaḥ
|
lebhire tapasā pūrvamanujñātāḥ svayambhuvā ||19||

20vedavidveda bhagavān vedāṅgāni bṛhaspatiḥ
|
bhārgavo nītiśāstraṃ tu jagāda jagato hitam ||20||

21gāndharvaṃ nārado veda bharadvājo dhanurgraham
|
devarṣicaritaṃ gārgyaḥ kṛṣṇātreyaścikitsitam ||21||

22nyāyatantrāṇyanekāni taistairuktāni vādibhiḥ
|
hetvāgamasadācārairyaduktaṃ tadupāsyatām ||22||

23anādyaṃ tatparaṃ brahma na devā narṣayo viduḥ
|
ekastadveda bhagavān dhātā nārāyaṇaḥ prabhuḥ ||23||

24nārāyaṇādṛṣigaṇāstathā mukhyāḥ surāsurāḥ
|
rājarṣayaḥ purāṇāśca paramaṃ duḥkhabheṣajam ||24||

25puruṣādhiṣṭhitān bhāvān prakṛtiḥ sūyate yadā
|
hetuyuktamataḥ pūrvaṃ jagatsamparivartate ||25||

26dīpādanye yathā dīpāḥ pravartante sahasraśaḥ
|
prakṛtiḥ sūyate tadvadānantyānnāpacīyate ||26||

27avyaktātkarmajā buddhirahaṃkāraṃ prasūyate
|
ākāśaṃ cāpyahaṃkārādvāyurākāśasambhavaḥ ||27||

28vāyostejastataścāpa adbhyo'tha vasudhodgatā
|
mūlaprakṛtayo hyaṣṭau jagadetāsvavasthitam ||28||

29jñānendriyāṇyataḥ pañca pañca karmendriyāṇyapi
|
viṣayāḥ pañca caikaṃ ca vikāre ṣoḍaśaṃ manaḥ ||29||

30śrotraṃ tvakcakṣuṣī jihvā ghrāṇaṃ jñānendriyāṇyatha
|
pādau pāyurūpasthaśca hastau vākkarmaṇī api ||30||

31śabdaḥ sparśaśca rūpaṃ ca raso gandhastathaiva ca
|
vijñeyaṃ vyāpakaṃ cittaṃ teṣu sarvagataṃ manaḥ ||31||

32rasajñāne tu jihveyaṃ vyāhṛte vāk tathocyate
|
indriyairvividhairyuktaṃ sarvaṃ vyaktaṃ manastathā ||32||

33vidyāttu ṣoḍaśaitāni daivatāni vibhāgaśaḥ
|
deheṣu jñānakartāramupāsīnamupāsate ||33||

34tadvatsomaguṇā jihvā gandhastu pṛthivīguṇaḥ
|
śrotaṃ nabhoguṇaṃ caiva cakṣuragnerguṇastathā
|
sparśaṃ vāyuguṇaṃ vidyātsarvabhūteṣu sarvadā ||34||

35manaḥ sattvaguṇaṃ prāhuḥ sattvamavyaktajaṃ tathā
|
sarvabhūtātmabhūtasthaṃ tasmādbuddhyeta buddhimān ||35||

36ete bhāvā jagatsarvaṃ vahanti sacarācaram
|
śritā virajasaṃ devaṃ yamāhuḥ prakṛteḥ param ||36||

37navadvāraṃ puraṃ puṇyametairbhāvaiḥ samanvitam
|
vyāpya śete mahānātmā tasmātpuruṣa ucyate ||37||

38ajaraḥ so'maraścaiva vyaktāvyaktopadeśavān
|
vyāpakaḥ saguṇaḥ sūkṣmaḥ sarvabhūtaguṇāśrayaḥ ||38||

39yathā dīpaḥ prakāśātmā hrasvo vā yadi vā mahān
|
jñānātmānaṃ tathā vidyāt puruṣaṃ sarvajantuṣu ||39||

40śrotraṃ vedayate vedyaṃ sa śṛṇoti sa paśyati
|
kāraṇaṃ tasya deho'yaṃ sa kartā sarvakarmaṇām ||40||

41agnirdārugato yadvadbhinne dārau na dṛśyate
|
tathaivātmā śarīrastho yogenaivātra dṛśyate ||41||

42agniryathā hyupāyena mathitvā dāru dṛśyate
|
tathaivātmā śarīrastho yogenaivātra dṛśyate ||42||

43nadīṣvāpo yathā yuktā yathā sūrye marīcayaḥ
|
saṃtatatvād yathā yānti tathā dehāḥ śarīriṇām ||43||

44svapnayoge yathaivātmā pañcendriyasamāyutaḥ
|
dehamutsṛjya vai yāti tathaivātmopalabhyate ||44||

45karmaṇā bādhyate rūpaṃ karmaṇā copalabhyate
|
karmaṇā nīyate'nyatra svakṛtena balīyasā ||45||

46sa tu dehādyathā dehaṃ tyaktvā'nyaṃ pratipadyate
|
tathā'nyaṃ sampravakṣyāmi bhūtagrāmaṃ svakarmajam ||46||
