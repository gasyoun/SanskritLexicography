Mahābhārata 8.83 (Karṇaparvan)

1sañjaya uvāca|
tatrākarodduṣkaraṃ rājaputro duḥśāsanastumulaṃ yuddhyamānaḥ
|
ciccheda bhīmasya dhanuḥ śareṇa ṣaṣṭyā śaraiḥ sārathimapyavidhyat ||1||

2sa tatkṛtvā rājaputrastarasvī vivyādha bhīmaṃ navabhiḥ pṛṣatkaiḥ
|
tato'bhinadbahubhiḥ kṣiprameva vareṣubhirbhīmasenaṃ mahātmā ||2||

3tataḥ kruddho bhīmasenastarasvī śaktiṃ cogrāṃ prāhiṇotte sutāya
|
tāmāpatantīṃ sahasā'tighorāṃ dṛṣṭvā sutaste jvalitāmivolkām ||3||

4ākarṇapūrṇairiṣubhirmahātmā ciccheda putro daśabhiḥ pṛṣatkaiḥ
|
dṛṣṭvā tu tat karma kṛtaṃ suduṣkaraṃ prāpūjayansarvayodhāḥ prahṛṣṭāḥ ||4||

5athāśu bhīmaṃ ca śareṇa bhūyo gāḍhaṃ sa vivyādha sutastvadīyaḥ
|
cukrodha bhīmaḥ punarāśu tasmai bhṛśaṃ prajajvāla ruṣā'bhivīkṣya ||5||

6viddho'smi vīrāśu bhṛśaṃ tvayādya sahasva bhūyo'pi gadāprahāram
|
uktvaivamuccaiḥ kupito'tha bhīmo jagrāha tāṃ bhīmagadāṃ vadhāya ||6||

7uvāca cādyāhamahaṃ durātman pāsyāmi te śoṇitamājimadhye
|
athaivamuktastanayastavogrāṃ śaktiṃ vegātprāhiṇonmṛtyurūpām ||7||

8āvidhya bhīmo'pi gadāṃ sughorāṃ vicikṣipe roṣaparītamūrtiḥ
|
sā tasya śaktiṃ sahasā virujya putraṃ tavājau tāḍayāmāsa mūrdhni ||8||

9sa vikṣarannāga iva prabhinno gadāmasmai tumule prāhiṇodvai
|
tayā'haraddaśa dhanvantarāṇi duḥśāsanaṃ bhīmasenaḥ prasahya ||9||

10tayā hataḥ patito vepamāno duḥśāsano gadayā vegavatyā
|
vidhvastavarmābharaṇāmbarasrak viceṣṭamāno bhṛśavedanāturaḥ ||10||

11hayāḥ sasūtā nihatā narendra cūrṇīkṛtaścāsya rathaḥ patantyā
|
duḥśāsanaṃ pāṇḍavāḥ prekṣya sarve hṛṣṭāḥ pañcālāḥ siṃhanādānamuñcan ||11||

12taṃ pātayitvā'tha vṛkodaro'tha jagarja harṣeṇa vinādayan diśaḥ
|
nādena tenākhilapārśvavartino mūrchākulāḥ patitāstvājamīḍha ||12||

13bhīmo'pi vegādavatīrya yānādduḥśāsanaṃ vegavānabhyadhāvat
|
tataḥ smṛtvā bhīmasenastarasvī sāpatnakaṃ yat prayuktaṃ sutaiste ||13||

14tasminsughore tumule vartamāne pradhānabhūyiṣṭhataraiḥ samantāt
|
duḥśāsanaṃ tatra samīkṣya rājan bhīmo mahābāhuracintyakarmā ||14||

15smṛtvā'tha keśagrahaṇaṃ ca devyā vastrāpahāraṃ ca rajasvalāyāḥ
|
anāgaso bhartṛparāṅmukhāyā duḥkhāni dattānyapi vipracintya ||15||

16jajvāla krodhādatha bhīmasena ājyaprasikto hi yathā hutāśaḥ
|
tatrāha karṇaṃ ca suyodhanaṃ ca kṛpaṃ drauṇiṃ kṛtavarmāṇameva ||16||

17nihanmi duḥśāsanamadya pāpaṃ saṃrakṣyatāmadya samastayodhāḥ
|
ityevamuktvā sahasā'bhyadhāvannihantukāmo'tibalastarasvī ||17||

18tathā tu vikramya raṇe vṛkodaro mahāgajaṃ kesariko yathaiva
|
nigṛhya duḥśāsanamekavīraḥ suyodhanasyādhiratheḥ samakṣam ||18||

19rathādavaplutya gataḥ sa bhūmau yatnena tasminpraṇidhāya cakṣuḥ
|
asiṃ samudyamya sitaṃ sudhāraṃ kaṇṭhe padā''kramya ca vepamānam ||19||

20uvāca tadgauriti yadbruvāṇo dṛṣṭo vadeḥ karṇasuyodhanābhyām
|
ye rājasūyāvabhṛthe pavitrā jātāḥ kacā yājñasenyā durātman ||20||

21te pāṇinā katareṇāvakṛṣṭāstadbrūhi tvāṃ pṛcchati bhīmasenaḥ
|
śrutvā tu tadbhīmavacaḥ sughoraṃ duḥśāsano bhīmasenaṃ nirīkṣya ||21||

22jajvāla bhīmaṃ sa tadā smayena saṃśṛṇvatāṃ kauravasomakānām
|
uktastadā''jau sa tathā saroṣaṃ jagāda bhīmaṃ parivartanetraḥ ||22||

23'ayaṃ karikarākāraḥ pīnastanavimardanaḥ
|
gosahasrapradātā ca kṣatriyāntakaraḥ karaḥ ||23||

24anena yājñasenyā me bhīma keśā vikarṣitāḥ
|
paśyatāṃ kurumukhyānāṃ yuṣmākaṃ ca sabhāsadām' ||24||

25evaṃ tvasau rājasutaṃ niśamya bruvantamājau vinipīḍya vakṣaḥ
|
bhīmo balāttaṃ pratigṛhya dorbhyāmuccairnanādātha samastayodhān ||25||

26uvāca yasyāsti balaṃ sa rakṣatvasau bhavedadya nirastabāhuḥ
|
duḥśāsanaṃ jīvitaṃ protsṛjantamākṣipya yodhāṃstarasā mahābalaḥ ||26||

27evaṃ kruddho bhīmasenaḥ kareṇa utpāṭayāmāsa bhujaṃ mahātmā
|
duḥśāsanaṃ tena sa vīramadhye jaghāna vajrāśanisannibhena ||27||

28utkṛtya vakṣaḥ patitasya bhūmāvathāpibacchoṇitamasya koṣṇam
|
tato nipātyāsya śiropakṛtya tenāsinā tava putrasya rājan ||28||

29satyāṃ cikīrṣurmatimān pratijñāṃ bhīmo'pibacchoṇitamasya koṣṇam
|
āsvādya cāsvādya ca vīkṣamāṇaḥ kruddho hi cainaṃ nijagāda vākyam ||29||

30'stanyasya māturmadhusarpiṣorvā mādhvīkapānasya ca satkṛtasya
|
divyasya vā toyarasasya pānātpayodadhibhyāṃ mathitācca mukhyāt ||30||

31anyāni pānāni ca yāni loke sudhāmṛtasvādurasāni tebhyaḥ
|
sarvebhya evābhyadhiko raso'yaṃ mamādya cāsyāhitalohitasya’ ||31||

32athāha bhīmaḥ punarugrakarmā duḥśāsanaṃ krodhaparītacetāḥ
|
gatāsumālokya vihasya susvaraṃ 'kiṃ vā kuryāṃ mṛtyunā rakṣito'si' ||32||

33evaṃ bruvāṇaṃ punarādravantamāsvādyamānaṃ tamatiprahṛṣṭam
|
ye bhīmasenaṃ dadṛśustadānīṃ bhayena te'pi vyathitā nipetuḥ ||33||

34ye cāpi nāsan vyathitā manuṣyāsteṣāṃ karebhyaḥ patitaṃ hi śastram
|
bhayācca saṃcukruśurasvaraiste nimīlitākṣā dadṛśuḥ samantataḥ ||34||

35taṃ tatra bhīmaṃ dadṛśuḥ samantāddauḥśāsanaṃ tadrudhiraṃ pibantam
|
sarve'palāyanta bhayābhipannā na vai manuṣyo'yamiti bruvāṇāḥ ||35||

36tasmin kṛte bhīmasenena rūpe dṛṣṭvā janāḥ śoṇitaṃ pīyamānam
|
saṃprādravaṃścitrasenena sārdhaṃ bhīmaṃ rakṣo bhāṣamāṇā bhayārtāḥ ||36||

37yudhāmanyuṃ pradrutaṃ citrasenaṃ sahānīkastvabhyayādrājaputraḥ
|
vivyādha cainaṃ niśitaiḥ pṛṣatkairvyapetabhīḥ saptabhirāśumuktaiḥ ||37||

38saṃkrāntabhoga iva lelihāno mahoragaḥ krodhaviṣaṃ sisṛkṣuḥ
|
nivṛtya pāñcālajamabhyavidhyattribhiḥ śaraiḥ sārathimasya ṣaḍbhiḥ ||38||

39tataḥ supuṅkhena suyantritena susaṃśitāgreṇa śareṇa śūraḥ
|
ākarṇamuktena samāhitena yudhāmanyustasya śiro jahāra ||39||

40tasmin hate bhrātari citrasene kruddhaḥ karṇaḥ pauruṣaṃ darśayānaḥ
|
vyadrāvayatpāṇḍavānāmanīkaṃ pratyudyāto nakulenāmitaujāḥ ||40||

41bhīmo'pi hatvā tatraiva duḥśāsanamamarṣaṇam
|
pūrayittvā'ñjaliṃ bhūyo rudhirasyograniḥsvanaḥ ||41||

42śṛṇvatāṃ lokavīrāṇāmidaṃ vacanamabravīt
|
eṣa te rudhiraṃ kaṇṭhātpibāmi puruṣādhama ||42||

43brūhīdānīṃ tu saṃhṛṣṭaḥ punargauriti gauriti
|
ye tadā'smānpranṛtyanti punargauriti gauriti ||43||

44tānvayaṃ pratinṛtyāmaḥ punargauriti gauriti
|
pramāṇakoṭyāṃ śayanaṃ kālakūṭasya bhojanam ||44||

45daṃśanaṃ cāhibhiḥ kṛṣṇairdāhaṃ ca jatuveśmani
|
dyūtena rājyaharaṇamaraṇye vasatiśca yā ||45||

46draupadyāḥ keśapakṣasya grahaṇaṃ ca sudāruṇam
|
iṣvastrāṇi ca saṃgrāmeṣvasukhāni ca veśmani ||46||

47virāṭabhavane yaśca kleśo'smākaṃ pṛthagvidhaḥ
|
śakunerdhārtarāṣṭrasya rādheyasya ca mantrite ||47||

48anubhūtāni duḥkhāni teṣāṃ hetustvameva hi
|
duḥkhānyetāni jānīmo na sukhāni kadācana ||48||

49dhṛtarāṣṭrasya daurātmyātsaputrasya sadā vayam
|
ityuktvā vacanaṃ rājañjayaṃ prāpya vṛkodaraḥ
|
punarāha mahārāja smayaṃstau keśavārjunau ||49||

50asṛgdigdho vistravallohitāsyaḥ kruddho'tyarthaṃ bhīmasenastarasvī
|
duḥśāsane yadraṇe saṃśrutaṃ me tadvai satyaṃ kṛtamadyeha vīrau ||50||

51atraiva dāsyāmyaparaṃ dvitīyaṃ duryodhanaṃ yajñapaśuṃ viśasya
|
śiro mṛditvā ca padā durātmanaḥ śāntiṃ lapsye kauravāṇāṃ samakṣam ||51||

52etāvaduktvā vacanaṃ prahṛṣṭo nanāda coccai rudhirārdragātraḥ
|
nanarda caivātibalo mahātmā vṛtraṃ nihatyeva sahasranetraḥ ||52||
