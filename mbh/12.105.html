Mahābhārata 12.105 (Śāntiparvan)

1muniruvāca| atha cetpauruṣaṃ kiṃcitkṣatriyātmani paśyasi
|
bravīmi tāṃ tu te nītiṃ rājyasya pratipattaye||1||

2tāṃ cecchaknoṣi nirmātuṃ karma caiva kariṣyasi
|
śṛṇu sarvamaśeṣeṇa yattvāṃ vakṣyāmi tattvataḥ||2||

3ācariṣyasi cetkarma mahato'rthānavāpsyasi
|
rājyaṃ rājyasya mantraṃ vā mahatīṃ vā punaḥ śriyam||3||

4athaitadrocate rājanpunarbrūhi bravīmi te
|
rājovāca
|
bravītu bhagavānnītimupapanno'smyahaṃ prabho||4||

5amogho'yaṃ bhavatvadya tvayā saha samāgamaḥ
|
muniruvāca| hitvā dambhaṃ ca kāmaṃ ca krodhaṃ harṣaṃ bhayaṃ tathā||5||

6apyamitrāṇi sevasva praṇipatya kṛtāñjaliḥ
|
tamuttamena śaucena karmaṇā cābhidhāraya||6||

7dātumarhati te vittaṃ vaidehaḥ satyasaṃgaraḥ
|
pramāṇaṃ sarvabhūteṣu pragrahaṃ ca bhaviṣyasi||7||

8tataḥ sahāyānsotsāhā~llapsyase'vyasanān śucīn
|
vartamānaḥ svaśāstreṇa saṃyatātmā jitendriyaḥ||8||

9abhyuddharati cātmānaṃ prasādayati ca prajāḥ
|
tenaiva tvaṃ dhṛtimatā śrīmatā cābhisatkṛtaḥ||9||

10pramāṇaṃ sarvabhūteṣu gatvā ca grahaṇaṃ mahat
|
tataḥ suhṛdbalaṃ labdhvā mantrayitvā sumantribhiḥ||10||

11āntarairbhedayitvā'rīnbilvaṃ bilvena bhedaya
|
parairvā saṃvidaṃ kṛtvā balamapyasya ghātaya||11||

12alabhyā ye śubhā bhāvāḥ striyaścācchādanāni ca
|
śayyāsanāni yānāni mahārhāṇi gṛhāṇi ca||12||

13pakṣiṇo mṛgajātāni rasagandhaḥ phalāni ca
|
teṣveva sajjayethāstvaṃ yathā naśyatvayaṃ paraḥ||13||

14yadyevaṃ pratiṣeddhavyo yadyupekṣaṇamarhati
|
na jātu vivṛtaḥ kāryaḥ śatruḥ sunayamicchatā||14||

15ramasva paramāmitre viṣaye prājñasammataḥ
|
bhajasva śvetakākīyairmitradharmamanarthakaiḥ||15||

16ārambhāṃścāsya mahato duścarāṃśca prayojaya
|
nadīvacca virodhāṃśca balavadbhirvirudhyatām||16||

17udyānāni mahārhāṇi śayanānyāsanāni ca
|
pratibhogasukhenaiva kośamasya virecaya||17||

18yajñadāne praśādhyasmai brāhmaṇānanuvarṇya tān
|
te tvāṃ pratikariṣyanti taṃ bhokṣyanti vṛkā iva||18||

19asaṃśayaṃ puṇyaśīlaḥ prāpnoti paramāṃ gatim
|
triviṣṭape puṇyatamaṃ sthānaṃ prāpnoti mānavaḥ||19||

20kośakṣaye tvamitrāṇāṃ vaśaṃ kausalya gacchati
|
ubhayatra prayuktasya dharmeṇādharma eva ca||20||

21phalārthamūlaṃ vyucchidyettena nandanti śatravaḥ
|
na cāsmai mānuṣaṃ karma daivamasyopavarṇaya||21||

22asaṃśayaṃ daivaparaḥ kṣiprameva vinaśyati
|
yājayainaṃ viśvajitā sarvasvena viyujya tam||22||

23tato gacchasi siddhārthaḥ pīḍyamānaṃ mahājanam
|
yogadharmamidaṃ puṇyaṃ kaṃcidasyopavarṇayet||23||

24api tyāgaṃ bubhūṣeta kaccidgacchedanāmayam
|
siddhenauṣadhiyogena sarvaśatruvināśinā
|
nāgānaśvānmanuṣyāṃśca kṛtakairupaghātayet||24||

25ete cānye ca bahavo dambhayogāḥ sucintitāḥ
|
śakyā viṣahatā kartuṃ puruṣeṇa kṛtātmanā||25||
