Mahābhārata 6.26 (Bhīṣmaparvan)

1sañjaya uvāca —
taṃ tathā kṛpayāviṣṭamaśrupūrṇākulekṣaṇam
|
viṣīdantamidaṃ vākyamuvāca madhusūdanaḥ ||1||

2śrībhagavānuvāca —
kutastvā kaśmalamidaṃ viṣame samupasthitam
|
anāryajuṣṭamasvargyamakīrtikaramarjuna ||2||

3klaibyaṃ mā sma gamaḥ pārtha naitattvayyupapadyate
|
kṣudraṃ hṛdayadaurbalyaṃ tyaktvottiṣṭha parantapa ||3||

4arjuna uvāca —
kathaṃ bhīṣmamahaṃ saṃ‍khye droṇaṃ ca madhusūdana
|
iṣubhiḥ pratiyotsyāmi pūjārhāvarisūdana ||4||

5gurūnahatvā hi mahānubhāvān śreyo bhoktuṃ bhaikṣamapīha loke
|
hatvārthakāmāṃstu gurūnihaiva bhuñjīya bhogānrudhirapradigdhān ||5||

6na caitadvidmaḥ kataranno garīyo yadvā jayema yadi vā no jayeyuḥ
|
yāneva hatvā na jijīviṣāmaste'vasthitāḥ pramukhe dhārtarāṣṭrāḥ ||6||

7kārpaṇyadoṣopahatasvabhāvaḥ pṛcchāmi tvāṃ dharmasaṃmūḍhacetāḥ
|
yacchreyaḥ syānniścitaṃ brūhi tanme śiṣyaste'haṃ śādhi māṃ tvāṃ prapannam ||7||

8na hi prapaśyāmi mamāpanudyādyacchokamucchoṣaṇamindriyāṇām
|
avāpya bhūmāvasapatnamṛddhaṃ rājyaṃ surāṇāmapi cādhipatyam ||8||

9sañjaya uvāca —
evamuktvā hṛṣīkeśaṃ guḍākeśaḥ parantapaḥ
|
na yotsya iti govindamuktvā tūṣṇīṃ babhūva ha ||9||

10tamuvāca hṛṣīkeśaḥ prahasanniva bhārata
|
senayorubhayormadhye viṣīdantamidaṃ vacaḥ ||10||

11aśocyānanvaśocastvaṃ prajñāvādāṃśca bhāṣase
|
gatāsūnagatāsūṃśca nānuśocanti paṇḍitāḥ ||11||

12na tvevāhaṃ jātu nāsaṃ na tvaṃ neme janādhipāḥ
|
na caiva na bhaviṣyāmaḥ sarve vayamataḥ param ||12||

13dehino'sminyathā dehe kaumāraṃ yauvanaṃ jarā
|
tathā dehāntaraprāptirdhīrastatra na muhyati ||13||

14mātrāsparśāstu kaunteya śītoṣṇasukhaduḥkhadāḥ
|
āgamāpāyino'nityāstāṃstitikṣasva bhārata ||14||

15yaṃ hi na vyathayantyete puruṣaṃ puruṣarṣabha
|
samaduḥkhasukhaṃ dhīraṃ so'mṛtatvāya kalpate ||15||

16nāsato vidyate bhāvo nābhāvo vidyate sataḥ
|
ubhayorapi dṛṣṭo'ntastvanayostattvadarśibhiḥ ||16||

17avināśi tu tadviddhi yena sarvamidaṃ tatam
|
vināśamavyayasyāsya na kaścitkartumarhati ||17||

18antavanta ime dehā nityasyoktāḥ śarīriṇaḥ
|
anāśino'prameyasya tasmādyudhyasva bhārata ||18||

19ya enaṃ vetti hantāraṃ yaścainaṃ manyate hatam
|
ubhau tau na vijānīto nāyaṃ hanti na hanyate ||19||

20na jāyate mriyate vā kadācinnāyaṃ bhūtvābhavitā vā na bhūyaḥ
|
ajo nityaḥ śāśvato'yaṃ purāṇo na hanyate hanyamāne śarīre ||20||

21vedāvināśinaṃ nityaṃ ya enamajamavyayam
|
kathaṃ sa puruṣaḥ pārtha kaṃ ghātayati hanti kam ||21||

22vāsāṃsi jīrṇāni yathā vihāya navāni gṛhṇāti naro'parāṇi
|
tathā śarīrāṇi vihāya jīrṇānyanyāni saṃyāti navāni dehī ||22||

23nainaṃ chindanti śastrāṇi nainaṃ dahati pāvakaḥ
|
na cainaṃ kledayantyāpo na śoṣayati mārutaḥ ||23||

24acchedyo'yamadāhyo'yamakledyo'śoṣya eva ca
|
nityaḥ sarvagataḥ sthāṇuracalo'yaṃ sanātanaḥ ||24||

25avyakto'yamacintyo'yamavikāryo'yamucyate
|
tasmādevaṃ viditvainaṃ nānuśocitumarhasi ||25||

26atha cainaṃ nityajātaṃ nityaṃ vā manyase mṛtam
|
tathāpi tvaṃ mahābāho naivaṃ śocitumarhasi ||26||

27jātasya hi dhruvo mṛtyurdhruvaṃ janma mṛtasya ca
|
tasmādaparihārye'rthe na tvaṃ śocitumarhasi ||27||

28avyaktādīni bhūtāni vyaktamadhyāni bhārata
|
avyaktanidhanānyeva tatra kā paridevanā ||28||

29āścaryavatpaśyati kaścidenamāścaryavadvadati tathaiva cānyaḥ
|
āścaryavaccainamanyaḥ śṛṇoti śrutvāpyenaṃ veda na caiva kaścit ||29||

30dehī nityamavadhyo'yaṃ dehe sarvasya bhārata
|
tasmātsarvāṇi bhūtāni na tvaṃ śocitumarhasi ||30||

31svadharmamapi cāvekṣya na vikampitumarhasi
|
dharmyāddhi yuddhācchreyo'nyatkṣattriyasya na vidyate ||31||

32yadṛcchayā copapannaṃ svargadvāramapāvṛtam
|
sukhinaḥ kṣatriyāḥ pārtha labhante yuddhamīdṛśam ||32||

33atha cettvamimaṃ dharmyaṃ saṅgrāmaṃ na kariṣyasi
|
tataḥ svadharmaṃ kīrtiṃ ca hitvā pāpamavāpsyasi ||33||

34akīrtiṃ cāpi bhūtāni kathayiṣyanti te'vyayām
|
sambhāvitasya cākīrtirmaraṇādatiricyate ||34||

35bhayādraṇāduparataṃ maṃsyante tvāṃ mahārathāḥ
|
yeṣāṃ ca tvaṃ bahumato bhūtvā yāsyasi lāghavam ||35||

36avācyavādāṃśca bahūnvadiṣyanti tavāhitāḥ
|
nindantastava sāmarthyaṃ tato duḥkhataraṃ nu kim ||36||

37hato vā prāpsyasi svargaṃ jitvā vā bhokṣyase mahīm
|
tasmāduttiṣṭha kaunteya yuddhāya kṛtaniścayaḥ ||37||

38sukhaduḥkhe same kṛtvā lābhālābhau jayājayau
|
tato yuddhāya yujyasva naivaṃ pāpamavāpsyasi ||38||

39eṣā te'bhihitā sāṅ‍khye buddhiryoge tvimāṃ śṛṇu
|
buddhyā yukto yayā pārtha karmabandhaṃ prahāsyasi ||39||

40nehābhikramanāśo'sti pratyavāyo na vidyate
|
svalpamapyasya dharmasya trāyate mahato bhayāt ||40||

41vyavasāyātmikā buddhirekeha kurunandana
|
bahuśākhā hyanantāśca buddhayo'vyavasāyinām ||41||

42yāmimāṃ puṣpitāṃ vācaṃ pravadantyavipaścitaḥ
|
vedavādaratāḥ pārtha nānyadastīti vādinaḥ ||42||

43kāmātmānaḥ svargaparā janmakarmaphalapradām
|
kriyāviśeṣabahulāṃ bhogaiśvaryagatiṃ prati ||43||

44bhogaiśvaryaprasaktānāṃ tayāpahṛtacetasām
|
vyavasāyātmikā buddhiḥ samādhau na vidhīyate ||44||

45traiguṇyaviṣayā vedā nistraiguṇyo bhavārjuna
|
nirdvandvo nityasattvastho niryogakṣema ātmavān ||45||

46yāvānartha udapāne sarvataḥsamplutodake
|
tāvān sarveṣu vedeṣu brāhmaṇasya vijānataḥ ||46||

47karmaṇyevādhikāraste mā phaleṣu kadācana
|
mā karmaphalaheturbhūrmā te saṅgo'stvakarmaṇi ||47||

48yogasthaḥ kuru karmāṇi saṅgaṃ tyaktvā dhanañjaya
|
siddhyasiddhyoḥ samo bhūtvā samatvaṃ yoga ucyate ||48||

49dūreṇa hyavaraṃ karma buddhiyogāddhanañjaya
|
buddhau śaraṇamanviccha kṛpaṇāḥ phalahetavaḥ ||49||

50buddhiyukto jahātīha ubhe sukṛtaduṣkṛte
|
tasmādyogāya yujyasva yogaḥ karmasu kauśalam ||50||

51karmajaṃ buddhiyuktā hi phalaṃ tyaktvā manīṣiṇaḥ
|
janmabandhavinirmuktāḥ padaṃ gacchantyanāmayam ||51||

52yadā te mohakalilaṃ buddhirvyatitariṣyati
|
tadā gantāsi nirvedaṃ śrotavyasya śrutasya ca ||52||

53śrutivipratipannā te yadā sthāsyati niścalā
|
samādhāvacalā buddhistadā yogamavāpsyasi ||53||

54arjuna uvāca —
sthitaprajñasya kā bhāṣā samādhisthasya keśava
|
sthitadhīḥ kiṃ pṛbhāṣeta kimāsīta vrajeta kim ||54||

55śrībhagavānuvāca —
prajahāti yadā kāmānsarvānpārtha manogatān
|
ātmanyevātmanā tuṣṭaḥ sthitaprajñastadocyate ||55||

56duḥkheṣvanudvignamanāḥ sukheṣu vigataspṛhaḥ
|
vītarāgabhayakrodhaḥ sthitadhīrmunirucyate ||56||

57yaḥ sarvatrānabhisnehastattatprāpya śubhāśubham
|
nābhinandati na dveṣṭi tasya prajñā pratiṣṭhitā ||57||

58yadā saṃharate cāyaṃ kūrmo'ṅgānīva sarvaśaḥ
|
indriyāṇīndriyārthebhyastasya prajñā pratiṣṭhitā ||58||

59viṣayā vinivartante nirāhārasya dehinaḥ
|
rasavarjaṃ raso'pyasya paraṃ dṛṣṭvā nivartate ||59||

60yatato hyapi kaunteya puruṣasya vipaścitaḥ
|
indriyāṇi pramāthīni haranti prasabhaṃ manaḥ ||60||

61tāni sarvāṇi saṃyamya yukta āsīta matparaḥ
|
vaśe hi yasyendriyāṇi tasya prajñā pratiṣṭhitā ||61||

62dhyāyato viṣayānpuṃsaḥ saṅgasteṣūpajāyate
|
saṅgātsañjāyate kāmaḥ kāmātkrodho'bhijāyate ||62||

63krodhādbhavati saṃmohaḥ saṃmohātsmṛtivibhramaḥ
|
smṛtibhraṃśādbuddhināśo buddhināśātpraṇaśyati ||63||

64rāgadveṣaviyuktaistu viṣayānindriyaiścaran
|
ātmavaśyairvidheyātmā prasādamadhigacchati ||64||

65prasāde sarvaduḥkhānāṃ hānirasyopajāyate
|
prasannacetaso hyāśu buddhiḥ paryavatiṣṭhate ||65||

66nāsti buddhirayuktasya na cāyuktasya bhāvanā
|
na cābhāvayataḥ śāntiraśāntasya kutaḥ sukham ||66||

67indriyāṇāṃ hi caratāṃ yanmano'nuvidhīyate
|
tadasya harati prajñāṃ vāyurnāvamivāmbhasi ||67||

68tasmādyasya mahābāho nigṛhītāni sarvaśaḥ
|
indriyāṇīndriyārthebhyastasya prajñā pratiṣṭhitā ||68||

69yā niśā sarvabhūtānāṃ tasyāṃ jāgarti saṃyamī
|
yasyāṃ jāgrati bhūtāni sā niśā paśyato muneḥ ||69||

70āpūryamāṇamacalapratiṣṭhaṃ samudramāpaḥ praviśanti yadvat
|
tadvatkāmā yaṃ praviśanti sarve sa śāntimāpnoti na kāmakāmī ||70||

71vihāya kāmānyaḥ sarvānpumāṃścarati niḥspṛhaḥ
|
nirmamo nirahaṅkāraḥ sa śāntimadhigacchati ||71||

72eṣā brāhmī sthitiḥ pārtha naināṃ prāpya vimuhyati
|
sthitvāsyāmantakāle'pi brahmanirvāṇamṛcchati ||72||
