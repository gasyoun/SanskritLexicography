Mahābhārata 2.14 (Sabhāparvan)

1sarvairguṇairmahārāja rājasūyaṃ tvamarhasi
|
jānatastveva te sarvaṃ kiñcidvakṣyāmi bhārata||1||

2jāmadagnyena rāmeṇa kṣatraṃ yadavaśeṣitam
|
tasmādavarajaṃ loke yadidaṃ kṣatrasaṃjñitam||2||

3kṛto'yaṃ kalusaṅkalpaḥ kṣatriyairvasudhādhipa
|
nideśavāgbhistatteha viditaṃ bharatarṣabha||3||

4ailasyekṣvākuvaṃśasya prakṛtiṃ paricakṣate
|
rājānaḥ śreṇibaddhāśca tathā'nye kṣatriyā bhuvi||4||

5ailavaṃśyāśca ye rājaṃstathaivekṣvākavo nṛpāḥ
|
tāni caikaśataṃ viddhi kulāni bharatarṣabha||5||

6yayātestveva bhojānāṃ vistaro guṇato mahān
|
bhajate'dya mahārāja vistaraṃ sacaturdiśam||6||

7teṣāṃ tathaiva tāṃ lakṣmīṃ sarvakṣatramupāsate
|
idānīmeva vai rājañjarāsandho mahīpatiḥ||7||

8abhibhūya śriyaṃ teṣāṃ kulānāmabhiṣecitaḥ
|
sthito mūrdhni narendrāṇāmojasākramya sarvaśaḥ||8||

9sovanīṃ madhyamāṃ bhuktvā mitho bhedamamanyata
|
prabhuryastu paro rājā yasminnekavaśe jagat||9||

10sa sāmrājyaṃ mahārāja prāpto bhavati yogataḥ
|
taṃ sa rājā jarāsandhaṃ saṃśritya kila sarvaśaḥ||10||

11rājansenāpatirjātaḥ śiśupālaḥ pratāpavān
|
tameva ca mahārāja śiṣyavatsamupasthitaḥ||11||

12vakraḥ karūṣādhipatirmāyāyodhī mahābalaḥ
|
aparau ca mahāvīryau mahātmānau samāśritau||12||

13jarāsandhaṃ mahāvīryaṃ tau haṃsaḍimbhakāvubhau
|
vakradantaḥ karūṣasya karabho meghavāhanaḥ
|
mūrdhnā divyamaṇiṃ bibhradyamadbhutamaṇiṃ viduḥ||13||

14maruṃ ca narakaṃ caiva śāsti yo yavanādhipaḥ
|
aparyantabalo rājā pratīcyāṃ varuṇo yathā||14||

15bhagadatto mahārāja vṛddhastava pituḥ sakhā
|
sa vācā praṇatastasya karmaṇā ca viśeṣataḥ||15||

16snehabaddhaśca manasā pitṛvadbhaktimāṃstvayi
|
pratīcyāṃ dakṣiṇaṃ cāntaṃ pṛthivyāḥ prati yo nṛpaḥ||16||

17mātulo bhavataḥ śūraḥ purujitkuntivardhanaḥ
|
sa te sannitimānekaḥ snehataḥ śatrusūdanaḥ||17||

18jarāsandhaṃ gatastveva purā yo na mayā hataḥ
|
puruṣottamavijñāto yosau cediṣu durmatiḥ||18||

19ātmānaṃ pratijānāti loke'sminpuruṣottamam
|
ādatte satataṃ mohādyaḥ sa cihnaṃ ca māmakam||19||

20vaṅgapuṇḍrakirāteṣu rājā balasamanvitaḥ
|
pauṇḍrako vāsudeveti yo'sau loke'bhiviśrutaḥ||20||

21caturthabhāṅmahārāja bhojam indrasakho balī
|
vidyābalādyo vyajayatsa pāṇḍyakrathakaiśikān||21||

22bhrātā yasyākṛtiḥ śūro jāmadagnyasamo'bhavat
|
sa bhakto māgadhaṃ rājā bhīṣmakaḥ paravīrahā||22||

23priyāṇyācarataḥ prahvānsadā sambandhinastataḥ
|
bhajato na bhajatyasmānapriyeṣu vyavasthitaḥ||23||

24na kulaṃ sabalaṃ rājannabhyajānāttathātmanaḥ
|
paśyamāno yaśodīptaṃ jarāsandhamupasthitaḥ||24||

25udīcyāśca tathā bhojāḥ kulānyaṣṭādaśa prabho
|
jarāsandhabhayādeva pratīcīṃ diśamāsthitāḥ||25||

26śūrasenā bhadrakārā bodhāḥ śālvāḥ paṭaccarāḥ
|
susthalāśca sukuṭṭāśca kulindāḥ kuntibhiḥ saha||26||

27śālvāyanāśca rājānaḥ sodaryānucaraiḥ saha
|
dakṣiṇā ye ca pañcālāḥ pūrvāḥ kuntiṣu kośalāḥ||27||

28tathottarāṃ diśaṃ cāpi parityajya bhayārditāḥ
|
matsyāḥ sannyastapādāśca dakṣiṇāṃ diśamāśritāḥ||28||

29tathaiva sarvapañcālā jarāsandhabhāyarditāḥ
|
svarājyaṃ samparityajya vidrutāḥ sarvatodiśam||29||

30kasyacittvatha kālasya kaṃso nirmathya yādavān
|
bārhadrathasute devyāvupāgacchadvṛthāmatiḥ||30||

31astiḥ prāstiśca nāmnā te sahadevānuje'bale
|
balena tena svajñātīnabhibhūya vṛthāmatiḥ||31||

32śraiṣṭhyaṃ prāptaḥ sa tasyāsīdatīvāpanayo mahān
|
bhojarājanyavṛddhaiśca pīḍyamānairdurātmanā||32||

33jñātitrāṇamabhīpsadbhirasmatsambhāvanā kṛtā
|
datvā''krūrāya sutanuṃ tāmāhukasutāṃ tadā||33||

34saṅkarṣaṇadvitīyena jñātikāryaṃ mayā kṛtam
|
hatau kaṃsasunāmānau mayā rāmeṇa cāpyuta||34||

35bhaye tu samatikrānte jarāsandhe samudyate
|
mantro'yaṃ mantrito rājankulairaṣṭādaśāvaraiḥ||35||

36anāramanto nighnanto mahāstraiḥ śatrughātibhiḥ
|
na hanyāmo vayaṃ tasya tribhirvarṣaśatairbalam||36||

37tasya hyamarasaṅkāśau balena balināṃ varau
|
nāmabhyāṃ haṃsaḍimbhakāvaśastranidhanāvubhau||37||

38tāvubhau sahitau vīrau jarāsandhaśca vīryavān
|
trayastrayāṇāṃ lokānāṃ paryāptā iti me matiḥ||38||

39na hi kevalamasmākaṃ yāvanto'nye ca pārthivāḥ
|
tathaiva teṣāmāsīcca buddhirbuddhimatāṃ vara||39||

40atha haṃsa iti khyātaḥ kaścidāsīnmahānnṛpaḥ
|
rāmeṇa sa hatastatra saṅgrāme'ṣṭādaśāvare||40||

41hato haṃsa iti proktasya kenāpi bhārata
|
tacchrutvā ḍibiko rājanyamunāmbhasyamajjata ||41||

42vinā hasena loke'sminnāhaṃ jīvitumutsahe
|
ityetāṃ matimāsthāya ḍimbhako nidhanaṃ gataḥ||42||

43tathā tu ḍibikaṃ śrutvā haṃsaḥ parupurañjayaḥ
|
prapede yamunāmeva sopi tasyāṃ nyamajjata||43||

44tau sa rājā jarājandhaḥ śrutvā ca nidhanaṃ gatau
|
puraṃ śūnyena manasā prayayau bharatarṣabha||44||

45tato vayamitraghna tasminpratigate nṛpe
|
punarāndinaḥ sarve madhurāyāṃ vasāmahe||45||

46yadā tvabhyetya pitaraṃ sā vai rājīvalocanā
|
kaṃsabhāryā jarāsandhaṃ duhitā māgadhaṃ nṛpam
|
codayatyeva rājendra pativyasanaduḥkhitā||46||

47patighnaṃ me jahītyevaṃ punaḥ punararindama
|
tato vayaṃ mahārāja taṃ mantraṃ pūrvamantritam||47||

48saṃsmaranto vimanaso vyapayātā narādhipa
|
pṛthaktvena mahārāja saṅkṣipya mahatīṃ śriyam||48||

49palāyāmo bhayāttasya sasutajñātibāndhavāḥ
|
iti sañcintya sarve sma pratīcīṃ diśamāśritāḥ||49||

50kuśasthalīṃ purīṃ ramyāṃ raivatenopaśobhitām
|
tato niveśaṃ tasyāṃ ca kṛtavanto vayaṃ nṛpa||50||

51tathaiva durgasaṃskāraṃ devairapi durāsadam
|
striyo'pi yasyāṃ yudhyeyuḥ kimu vṛṣṇimahārathāḥ||51||

52tasyāṃ vayamamitraghna nivasāmo'kutobhayāḥ
|
ālocya girimukhyaṃ taṃ māgadhaṃ tīrṇameva ca||52||

53mādhavāḥ kuruśārdūla parāṃ mudamavāpnuvan
|
evaṃ vayaṃ jarāsandhādabhitaḥ kṛtakilbiṣāḥ||53||

54sāmarthyavantaḥ sambandhādgomantaṃ samupāśritāḥ
|
triyojanāyataṃ sadma triskandhaṃ yojanāvadhi||54||

55yojanānte śatadvāraṃ vīravikramatoraṇam
|
aṣṭādaśāvarairnaddhaṃ kṣatriyairyuddhadurmadaiḥ||55||

56aṣṭādaśa sahasrāṇi bhrātṝṇāṃ santi naḥ kale
|
āhukasya śataṃ putrā ekaikastridaśāvaraḥ||56||

57cārudeṣṇaḥ saha bhrātrā cakradevo'tha sātyakiḥ
|
ahaṃ ca rohiṇeyaśca sāmbaḥ pradyumna eva ca||57||

58evamete rathāḥ sapta rājannanyānnibodha me
|
kṛtavarmā hyanādhṛṣṭiḥ samīkaḥ samirtijayaḥ||58||

59kaṅkaḥ śaṅkuśca kuntiśca saptaite vai mahārathāḥ
|
putrau cāndhakabhojasya vṛddho rājā ca te daśa||59||

60vajrasaṃhananā vīrī vīryavanto mahārathāḥ
|
smaranto madhyamaṃ deśaṃ vṛṣṇimadhye vyavasthitāḥ ||60||

61sa tvaṃ samrāḍguṇairyuktaḥ sadā bharatasattama
|
kṣetre samrājamātmānaṃ kartumarhasi bharata||61||

62na tu śakyaṃ jarāsandhe jīvamāne mahābale
|
rājasūyastvayā'vāptumeṣā rājanmatirmama||62||

63tena ruddhā hi rājānaḥ sarve jitvā girivraje
|
kandare parvatendrasya siṃheneva mahādvipāḥ||63||

64sa hi rājā jarāsandho yiyakṣurvasudhādhipaiḥ
|
mahādevaṃ mahātmānamumāpatimarindama||64||

65ārādhya tapasogreṇa nirjitāstena pārthivāḥ
|
pratijñāyāśca pāraṃ sa gataḥ pārthivasattama||65||

66sa hi nirjitya nirjitya pārthivānpṛtanāgatān
|
puramānīya baddhvā ca cakāra puruṣavrajam||66||

67vayaṃ caiva mahārāja jarāsandhabhayāttadā
|
madhurāṃ samparityajya gatā dvāravatīṃ purīm||67||

68yadi tvenaṃ mahārāja yajñaṃ prāptumabhīpsasi
|
yatasva teṣāṃ mokṣāya jarāsandhavadhāya ca||68||

69samārambho na śakyo'yamanyathā kurunandana
|
rājasūyasya kārtsnyena kartuṃ matimatāṃ vara||69||

70ityeṣā me matī rājanyathā vā manyase'nagha
|
evaṃgate mamācakṣva svayaṃ niścitya hetubhiḥ||70||
