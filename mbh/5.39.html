Mahābhārata 5.39 (Udyogaparvan)

1dhṛtarāṣṭra uvāca|
anīśvaro'yaṃ puruṣo bhavābhave sūtraprotā dārumayīva yoṣā
|
dhātrā tu diṣṭasya vaśe kṛto'yaṃ tasmādvada tvaṃ śravaṇe dhṛto'ham ||1||

2vidura uvāca|
aprāptakālaṃ vacanaṃ bṛhaspatirapi bruvan
|
labhate buddhyavajñānamavamānaṃ ca bhārata||2||

3priyo bhavati dānena priyavādena cāparaḥ
|
mantramūlabalenānyo yaḥ priyaḥ priya eva saḥ||3||

4dveṣyo na sādhurbhavati na medhāvī na paṇḍitaḥ
|
priye śubhāni kāryāṇi dveṣye pāpāni caiva ha||4||

5uktaṃ mayā jātamātre'pi rājan duryodhanaṃ tyaja putraṃ tvamekam
|
tasya tyāgāt putraśatasya vṛddhirasyātyāgāt putraśatasya nāśaḥ||5||

6na vṛddhirbahu mantavyā yā vṛddhiḥ kṣayamāvahet
|
kṣayo'pi bahu mantavyo yaḥ kṣayo vṛddhimāvahet ||6||

7na sa kṣayo mahārāja yaḥ kṣayo vṛddhimāvahet
|
kṣayaḥ sa tviha mantavyo yaṃ labdhvā bahu nāśayet||7||

8samṛddhā guṇataḥ kecidbhavanti dhanato'pare
|
dhanavṛddhān guṇairhīnān dhṛtarāṣṭra vivarjaya ||8||

9dhṛtarāṣṭra uvāca|
sarvaṃ tvamāyatīyuktaṃ bhāṣase prājñasaṃmatam
|
na cotsahe sutaṃ tyaktuṃ yato dharmastato jayaḥ||9||

10vidura uvāca|
atīva guṇasampanno na jātuḥ vinayānvitaḥ
|
susūkṣmamapi bhūtānāmupamardamupekṣate ||10||

11parāpavādaniratāḥ paraduḥkhodayeṣu ca
|
parasparavirodhe ca yatante satatotthitāḥ||11||

12sadoṣaṃ darśanaṃ yeṣāṃ saṃvāse sumahadbhayam
|
arthādāne mahān doṣaḥ pradāne ca mahadbhayam ||12||

13ye vai bhedanaśīlāstu sakāmā nistrapāḥ śaṭhāḥ
|
ye pāpā iti vikhyātāḥ saṃvāse parigarhitāḥ||13||

14yuktāścānyairmahādoṣairye narāstān vivarjayet
|
nivartamāne sauhārde prītirnīce praṇaśyati||14||

15yā caiva phalanirvṛtiḥ sauhṛde caiva yatsukham
|
yatate cāpavādāya yatnamārabhate kṣaye||15||

16alpe'pyapakṛte mohānna śāntimadhigacchati
|
tādṛśaiḥ saṅgataṃ nīcairnṛśaṃsairakṛtātmabhiḥ||16||

17niśāmya nipuṇaṃ buddhyā vidvān dūrādvivarjayet
|
yo jñātimanugṛhṇāti daridraṃ dīnamāturam||17||

18sa putrapaśubhirvṛddhiṃ śreyaścānantyamaśnute
|
jñātayo vardhanīyāstairya icchantyātmanaḥ śubham ||18||

19kulavṛddhiṃ ca rājendra tasmāt sādhu samācara
|
śreyasā yokṣyate rājan kurvāṇo jñātisatkriyām ||19||

20viguṇā hyapi saṃrakṣyā jñātayo bharatarṣabha
|
kiṃ punarguṇavantaste tvatprasādābhikāṅkṣiṇaḥ||20||

21prasādaṃ kuru vīrāṇāṃ pāṇḍavānāṃ viśāmpate
|
dīyatāṃ grāmakāḥ kecitteṣāṃ vṛttyarthamīśvara ||21||

22evaṃ loke yaśaḥ prāptaṃ bhaviṣyati narādhipa
|
vṛddhena hi tvayā kāryaṃ putrāṇāṃ tāta śāsanam||22||

23mayā cāpi hitaṃ vācyaṃ viddhi māṃ tvaddhitaiṣiṇam
|
jñātibhirvigrahastāta na kartavyaḥ śubhārthinā
|
sukhāni saha bhojyāni jñātibhirbharatarṣabha||23||

24saṃbhojanaṃ saṃkathanaṃ saṃprītiśca parasparam
|
jñātibhiḥ saha kāryāṇi na virodhaḥ kadācana||24||

25jñātayastārayantīha jñātayo majjayanti ca
|
suvṛttāstārayantīha durvṛttā majjayanti ca||25||

26suvṛtto bhava rājendra pāṇḍavān prati mānada
|
adharṣaṇīyaḥ śatrūṇāṃ tairvṛtastvaṃ bhaviṣyasi||26||

27śrīmantaṃ jñātimāsādya yo jñātiravasīdati
|
digdhahastaṃ mṛga iva sa enastasya vindati ||27||

28paścādapi naraśreṣṭha tava tāpo bhaviṣyati
|
tān vā hatān sutān vā'pi śrutvā tadanucintaya ||28||

29yena khaṭvāṃ samārūḍhaḥ paritapyeta karmaṇā
|
ādāveva na tat kuryādadhruve jīvite sati ||29||

30na kaścinnāpanayate pumānanyatra bhārgavāt
|
śeṣasaṃpratipattistu buddhimatsveva tiṣṭhati||30||

31duryodhanena yadyetat pāpaṃ teṣu purā kṛtam
|
tvayā tatkulavṛddhena pratyāneyaṃ nareśvara ||31||

32tāṃstvaṃ pade pratiṣṭhāpya loke vigatakalmaṣaḥ
|
bhaviṣyasi naraśreṣṭha pūjanīyo manīṣiṇām ||32||

33suvyāhṛtāni dhīrāṇāṃ phalataḥ paricintya yaḥ
|
adhyavasyati kāryeṣu ciraṃ yaśasi tiṣṭhati ||33||

34asamyagupayuktaṃ hi jñānaṃ sukuśalairapi
|
upalabhyaṃ cāviditaṃ viditaṃ cānanuṣṭhitam||34||

35pāpodayaphalaṃ vidvān yo nārabhati vardhate ||35||

36yastu pūrvakṛtaṃ pāpamavimṛśyānuvartate||36||

37agādhapaṅke durmedhā viṣame vinipātyate
|
mantrabhedasya ṣaṭ prājño dvārāṇīmāni lakṣayet||37||

38arthasantatikāmaśca rakṣedetāni nityaśaḥ
|
madaṃ svapnamavijñānamākāraṃ cātmasaṃbhavam||38||

39duṣṭāmātyeṣu viśraṃbhaṃ dūtāccākuśalādapi
|
dvārāṇyetāni yo jñātvā saṃvṛṇoti sadā nṛpa||39||

40trivargācaraṇe yuktaḥ sa śatrūnadhitiṣṭhati
|
na vai śrutamavijñāya vṛddhānanupasevya vā||40||

41dharmārthau vedituṃ śakyau bṛhaspatisamairapi
|
naṣṭaṃ samudre patitaṃ naṣṭaṃ vākyamaśṛṇvati||41||

42anātmani śrutaṃ naṣṭaṃ naṣṭaṃ hutamanagnikam
|
matyā parīkṣya medhāvī buddhyā sampādya cāsakṛt||42||

43śrutvā dṛṣṭvā'tha vijñāya prājñairmaitrīṃ samācaret
|
akīrtiṃ vinayo hanti hantyanarthaṃ parākramaḥ||43||

44hanti nityaṃ kṣamā krodhamācāro hantyalakṣaṇam
|
paricchadena kṣetreṇa veśmanā paricaryayā||44||

45parīkṣeta kulaṃ rājan bhojanācchādanena ca
|
upasthitasya kāmasya prativādo na vidyate||45||

46api nirmuktadehasya kāmaraktasya kiṃ punaḥ
|
prājñopasevinaṃ vaidyaṃ dhārmikaṃ priyadarśanam ||46||

47mitravantaṃ suvākyaṃ ca suhṛdaṃ paripālayet
|
duṣkulīnaḥ kulīno vā maryādāṃ yo na laṅghayet||47||

48dharmāpekṣī mṛdurhrīmān sa kulīnaśatādvaraḥ
|
yayościttena vā cittaṃ nibhṛtaṃ nibhṛtena vā||48||

49sameti prajñayā prajñā tayormaitrī na jīryati
|
durbuddhimatkṛtaprajñaṃ channaṃ kūpaṃ tṛṇairiva||49||

50vivarjayīta medhāvī tasmin mautrī praṇaśyati
|
avalipteṣu mūrkheṣu raudrasāhasikeṣu ca||50||

51tathaivāpetadharmeṣu na maitrīmācaredbudhaḥ
|
kṛtajñaṃ dhārmikaṃ satyamakṣudraṃ dṛḍhabhaktikam ||51||

52jitendriyaṃ sthitaṃ sthityāṃ mitramatyāgi ceṣyate
|
indriyāṇāmanutsargo mṛtyunā'pi viśiṣyate||52||

53atyarthaṃ punarutsargaḥ sādayeddaivatānapi
|
mārdavaṃ sarvabhūtānāmanasūyā kṣamā dhṛtiḥ||53||

54āyuṣyāṇi budhāḥ prāhurmitrāṇāṃ cāvimānanā
|
apanītaṃ sunītena yo'rthaṃ pratyāninīṣate ||54||

55matimāsthāya sudṛḍhāṃ tadakāpuruṣavratam
|
āyatyāṃ pratikārajñastadātve dṛḍhaniścayaḥ ||55||

56atīte kāryaśeṣajño naro'rthairna prahīyate
|
karmaṇā manasā vācā yadabhīkṣṇaṃ niṣevate ||56||

57tadevāpaharatyenaṃ tasmā kalyāṇamācaret
|
maṅgalālambhanaṃ yogaḥ śrutamutthānamārjavam||57||

58bhūtimetāni kurvanti satāṃ cābhīkṣṇadarśanam
|
anirvedaḥ śriyo mūlaṃ lābhasya ca śubhasya ca||58||

59mahān bhavatyanirviṇṇaḥ sukhaṃ cānantyamaśnute
|
nātaḥ śrīmattaraṃ kiṃcidanyat pathyatamaṃ matam||59||

60prabhaviṣṇoryathā tāta kṣamā sarvatra sarvadā
|
kṣamedaśaktaḥ sarvasya śaktimān dharmakāraṇāt
|
arthānarthau samau yasya tasya nityaṃ kṣamā hitā||60||

61yat sukhaṃ sevamāno'pi dharmārthābhyāṃ na hīyate
|
kāmaṃ tadupaseveta na mūḍhavratamācaret ||61||

62duḥkhārteṣu pramatteṣu nāstikeṣvalaseṣu ca
|
na śrīrvasatyadānteṣu ye cotsāhavivarjitāḥ||62||

63ārjavena naraṃ yuktamārjavāt savyapatrapam
|
aśaktaṃ manyamānāstu dharṣayanti kubuddhayaḥ ||63||

64atyāryamatidātāramatiśūramativratam
|
prajñābhimāninaṃ caiva śrīrbhayānnopasarpati ||64||

65na cātiguṇavatsveṣā nātyantaṃ nirguṇeṣu ca
|
naiṣā guṇān kāmayate nairguṇyānnānurajyate||65||

66unmattā gaurivāndhā śrīḥ kvacidevāvatiṣṭhate
|
agnihotraphalā vedāḥ śīlavṛttaphalaṃ śrutam||66||

67ratiputraphalā nārī dattabhuktaphalaṃ dhanam
|
adharmopārjitairarthairyaḥ karotyaurdhvadehikam||67||

68na sa tasya phalaṃ pretya bhuṅkte'rthasya durāgamāt
|
kāntāre vanadurgeṣu kṛcchrāsvāpatsu saṃbhrame||68||

69udyateṣu ca śastreṣu nāsti sattvavatāṃ bhayam
|
utthānaṃ saṃyamo dākṣyamapramādo dhṛtiḥ smṛtiḥ||69||

70samīkṣya ca samārambho viddhi mūlaṃ bhavasya tu
|
tapobalaṃ tāpasānāṃ brahma brahmavidāṃ balam ||70||

71hiṃsā balamasādhūnāṃ kṣamā guṇavatāṃ balam
|
aṣṭau tānyavrataghnāni āpo mūlaṃ phalaṃ payaḥ||71||

72havirbrāhmaṇakāmyā ca gurorvacanamauṣadham
|
na tatparasya sandadhyāt pratikūlaṃ yadātmanaḥ||72||

73saṅgraheṇaiva dharmaḥ syāt kāmādanyaḥ pravartate
|
akrodhena jayetkrodhamasādhuṃ sādhunā jayet||73||

74jayet kadaryaṃ dānena jayet satyena cānṛtam
|
strīdhūrtake'lase bhīrau caṇḍe puruṣamānini||74||

75caure kṛtaghne viśvāso na kāryo na ca nāstike
|
abhivādanaśīlasya nityaṃ vṛddhopasevinaḥ ||75||

76catvāri saṃpravardhante kīrtirāpuryaśo balam
|
atikleśena ye'rthāḥ syurdharmasyātikrameṇa vā||76||

77arervā praṇipātena mā sma teṣu manaḥ kṛthāḥ
|
avidyaḥ puruṣaḥ śocyaḥ śocyaṃ maithunamaprajam||77||

78nirāhārāḥ prajāḥ śocyāḥ śocyaṃ rāṣṭramarājakam
|
adhvā jarā dehavatāṃ parvatānāṃ jalaṃ jarā||78||

79asaṃbhogo jarā strīṇāṃ vākśalyaṃ manaso jarā
|
anāmnāyamalā vedā brāhmaṇasyāvrataṃ malam||79||

80malaṃ pṛthivyā bāhlīkāḥ puruṣasyānṛtaṃ malam
|
kautūhalamalā sādhvī vipravāsamalāḥ striyaḥ||80||

81suvarṇasya malaṃ rūpyaṃ rūpyasyāpi malaṃ trapu
|
jñeyaṃ trapumalaṃ sīmaṃ sīsasyāpi malaṃ malam ||81||

82na svapnena jayennidrāṃ na kāmena jayetstriyaḥ
|
nendhanena jayedagniṃ na pānena surāṃ jayet ||82||

83yasya dānajitaṃ mitraṃ śatravo yudhi nirjitāḥ
|
annapānajitā dārāḥ saphalaṃ tasya jīvitam ||83||

84sahasriṇo'pi jīvanti jīvanti śatinastathā
|
dhṛtarāṣṭra vimuñcecchāṃ na kathañcinna jīvyate ||84||

85yatpṛthivyāṃ vrīhiyavaṃ hiraṇyaṃ paśavaḥ striyaḥ
|
nālamekasya tatsarvamiti paśyanna muhyati||85||

86rājan bhūyo bravīmi tvāṃ putreṣu samamācara
|
samatā yadi te rājan sveṣu pāṇḍusuteṣu vā||86||
