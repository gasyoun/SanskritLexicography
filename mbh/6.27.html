Mahābhārata 6.27 (Bhīṣmaparvan)

1arjuna uvāca —
jyāyasī cetkarmaṇaste matā buddhirjanārdana
|
tatkiṃ karmaṇi ghore māṃ niyojayasi keśava ||1||

2vyāmiśreṇeva vākyena buddhiṃ mohayasīva me
|
tadekaṃ vada niścitya yena śreyo'hamāpnuyām ||2||

3śrībhagavānuvāca —
loke'smindvividhā niṣṭhā purā proktā mayānagha
|
jñānayogena sāṅ‍khyānāṃ karmayogena yoginām ||3||

4na karmaṇāmanārambhānnaiṣkarmyaṃ puruṣo'śnute
|
na ca saṃnyasanādeva siddhiṃ samadhigacchati ||4||

5na hi kaścitkṣaṇamapi jātu tiṣṭhatyakarmakṛt
|
kāryate hyavaśaḥ karma sarvaḥ prakṛtijairguṇaiḥ ||5||

6karmendriyāṇi saṃyamya ya āste manasā smaran
|
indriyārthānvimūḍhātmā mithyācāraḥ sa ucyate ||6||

7yastvindriyāṇi manasā niyamyārabhate'rjuna
|
karmendriyaiḥ karmayogamasaktaḥ sa viśiṣyate ||7||

8niyataṃ kuru karma tvaṃ karma jyāyo hyakarmaṇaḥ
|
śarīrayātrāpi ca te na prasidhyedakarmaṇaḥ ||8||

9yajñārthātkarmaṇo'nyatra loko'yaṃ karmabandhanaḥ
|
tadarthaṃ karma kaunteya muktasaṅgaḥ samācara ||9||

10sahayajñāḥ prajāḥ sṛṣṭvā purovāca prajāpatiḥ
|
anena prasaviṣyadhvameṣa vo'stviṣṭakāmadhuk ||10||

11devānbhāvayatānena te devā bhāvayantu vaḥ
|
parasparaṃ bhāvayantaḥ śreyaḥ paramavāpsyatha ||11||

12iṣṭānbhogānhi vo devā dāsyante yajñabhāvitāḥ
|
tairdattānapradāyaibhyo yo bhuṅkte stena eva saḥ ||12||

13yajñaśiṣṭāśinaḥ santo mucyante sarvakilbiṣaiḥ
|
bhuñjate te tvaghaṃ pāpā ye pacantyātmakāraṇāt ||13||

14annādbhavanti bhūtāni parjanyādannasambhavaḥ
|
yajñādbhavati parjanyo yajñaḥ karmasamudbhavaḥ ||14||

15karma brahmodbhavaṃ viddhi brahmākṣarasamudbhavam
|
tasmātsarvagataṃ brahma nityaṃ yajñe pratiṣṭhitam ||15||

16evaṃ pravartitaṃ cakraṃ nānuvartayatīha yaḥ
|
aghāyurindriyārāmo moghaṃ pārtha sa jīvati ||16||

17yastvātmaratireva syādātmatṛptaśca mānavaḥ
|
ātmanyeva ca santuṣṭastasya kāryaṃ na vidyate ||17||

18naiva tasya kṛtenārtho nākṛteneha kaścana
|
na cāsya sarvabhūteṣu kaścidarthavyapāśrayaḥ ||18||

19tasmādasaktaḥ satataṃ kāryaṃ karma samācara
|
asakto hyācarankarma paramāpnoti pūruṣaḥ ||19||

20karmaṇaiva hi saṃsiddhimāsthitā janakādayaḥ
|
lokasaṅgrahamevāpi sampaśyankartumarhasi ||20||

21yadyadācarati śreṣṭhastattadevetaro janaḥ
|
sa yatpramāṇaṃ kurute lokastadanuvartate ||21||

22na me pārthāsti kartavyaṃ triṣu lokeṣu kiñcana
|
nānavāptamavāptavyaṃ varta eva ca karmaṇi ||22||

23yadi hyahaṃ na varteya jātu karmaṇyatandritaḥ
|
mama vartmānuvartante manuṣyāḥ pārtha sarvaśaḥ ||23||

24utsīdeyurime lokā na kuryāṃ karma cedaham
|
saṅkarasya ca kartā syāmupahanyāmimāḥ prajāḥ ||24||

25saktāḥ karmaṇyavidvāṃso yathā kurvanti bhārata
|
kuryādvidvāṃstathāsaktaścikīrṣurlokasaṅgraham ||25||

26na buddhibhedaṃ janayedajñānāṃ karmasaṅginām
|
joṣayetsarvakarmāṇi vidvānyuktaḥ samācaran ||26||

27prakṛteḥ kriyamāṇāni guṇaiḥ karmāṇi sarvaśaḥ
|
ahaṅkāravimūḍhātmā kartāhamiti manyate ||27||

28tattvavittu mahābāho guṇakarmavibhāgayoḥ
|
guṇā guṇeṣu vartanta iti matvā na sajjate ||28||

29prakṛterguṇasaṃmūḍhāḥ sajjante guṇakarmasu
|
tānakṛtsnavido mandānkṛtsnavinna vicālayet ||29||

30mayi sarvāṇi karmāṇi saṃnyasyādhyātmacetasā
|
nirāśīrnirmamo bhūtvā yudhyasva vigatajvaraḥ ||30||

31ye me matamidaṃ nityamanutiṣṭhanti mānavāḥ
|
śraddhāvanto'nasūyanto mucyante te'pi karmabhiḥ ||31||

32ye tvetadabhyasūyanto nānutiṣṭhanti me matam
|
sarvajñānavimūḍhāṃstānviddhi naṣṭānacetasaḥ ||32||

33sadṛśaṃ ceṣṭate svasyāḥ prakṛterjñānavānapi
|
prakṛtiṃ yānti bhūtāni nigrahaḥ kiṃ kariṣyati ||33||

34indriyasyendriyasyārthe rāgadveṣau vyavasthitau
|
tayorna vaśamāgacchettau hyasya paripanthinau ||34||

35śreyānsvadharmo viguṇaḥ paradharmātsvanuṣṭhitāt
|
svadharme nidhanaṃ śreyaḥ paradharmo bhayāvahaḥ ||35||

36arjuna uvāca —
atha kena prayukto'yaṃ pāpaṃ carati pūruṣaḥ
|
anicchannapi vārṣṇeya balādiva niyojitaḥ ||36||

37śrībhagavānuvāca —
kāma eṣa krodha eṣa rajoguṇasamudbhavaḥ
|
mahāśano mahāpāpmā viddhyenamiha vairiṇam ||37||

38dhūmenāvriyate vahniryathādarśo malena ca
|
yatholbenāvṛto garbhastathā tenedamāvṛtam ||38||

39āvṛtaṃ jñānametena jñānino nityavairiṇā
|
kāmarūpeṇa kaunteya duṣpūreṇānalena ca ||39||

40indriyāṇi mano buddhirasyādhiṣṭhānamucyate
|
etairvimohayatyeṣa jñānamāvṛtya dehinam ||40||

41tasmāttvamindriyāṇyādau niyamya bharatarṣabha
|
pāpmānaṃ prajahihyenaṃ jñānavijñānanāśanam ||41||

42indriyāṇi parāṇyāhurindriyebhyaḥ paraṃ manaḥ
|
manasastu parā buddhiryo buddheḥ paratastu saḥ ||42||

43evaṃ buddheḥ paraṃ buddhvā saṃstabhyātmānamātmanā
|
jahi śatruṃ mahābāho kāmarūpaṃ durāsadam ||43||
