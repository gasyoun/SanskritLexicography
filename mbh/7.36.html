Mahābhārata 7.36 (Droṇaparvan)

1sañjaya uvāca|
saubhadrastadvacaḥ śrutvā dharmarājasya dhīmataḥ
|
acodayata yantāraṃ droṇānīkāya bhārata||1||

2tena sañcodyamānastu yāhi yāhīti sārathiḥ
|
pratyuvāca tato rājannabhimanyumidaṃ vacaḥ||2||

3atibhāro'yamāyuṣmannāhitastvayi pāṇḍavaiḥ
|
saṃpradhārtha kṣaṇaṃ buddhyā tatastvaṃ yoddhumarhasi||3||

4ācāryo hi kṛtī droṇaḥ paramāstra kṛtaśramaḥ
|
atyantasukhasaṃvṛddhastvaṃ cāyuddhaviśāradaḥ||4||

5tato'bhimanyuḥ prahasan sārathiṃ vākyamabravīt
|
sārathe ko nvayaṃ droṇaḥ samagraṃ kṣatrameva vā||5||

6airāvatagataṃ śakraṃ sahāmaragaṇairaham
|
athavā rudramīśānaṃ sarvabhūtagaṇārcitam
|
yodhayeyaṃ raṇamukhe na me kṣatre'dya vismayaḥ||6||

7na mamaitaddviṣatsainyaṃ kalāmarhati ṣoḍaśīm
|
api viśvajitaṃ viṣṇuṃ mātulaṃ prāpya sūtaja||7||

8pitaraṃ cārjunaṃ yuddhe na bhīrmāmupayāsyati
|
abhimanyuśca tāṃ vācaṃ kadarthīkṛtya sāratheḥ||8||

9yāhītyevābravīdenaṃ droṇānīkāya mā ciram
|
tataḥ sannodayāmāsa hayānāśu trihāyanān||9||

10nātihṛṣṭamanāḥ sūto hemabhāṇḍaparicchadān
|
te preṣitāḥ sumitreṇa droṇānīkāya vājinaḥ||10||

11droṇamabhyadravan rājan mahāvegaparākramam
|
tamudīkṣya tathā yāntaṃ sarve droṇapurogamāḥ
|
abhyavartanta kauravyāḥ pāṇḍavāśca tamanvayuḥ||11||

12sa karṇikārapravarocchritadhvajaḥ suvarṇavarmārjunirarjunādvaraḥ
|
yuyutsayā droṇamukhān mahārathān samāsadatsiṃhaśiśuryathā dvipān||12||

13te viṃśatipade yattāḥ saṃprahāraṃ pracakrire
|
āsīdgāṅga ivāvarto muhūrtamudadhāviva||13||

14śūrāṇāṃ yudhyamānānāṃ nighnatāmitaretaram
|
saṃgrāmastumulo rājan prāvartata sudāruṇaḥ||14||

15pravartamāne saṃgrāme tasminnatibhayaṃkare
|
droṇasya miṣato vyūhaṃ bhittvā prāviśadārjuniḥ||15||

16taṃ praviṣṭaṃ vinighnantaṃ śatrusaṃghānmahābalam
|
hastyaśvarathapattyaughāḥ parivavrurudāyudhāḥ||16||

17nānāvāditraninadaiḥ kṣveḍitotkṛṣṭagarjitaiḥ
|
huṃkāraiḥ siṃhanādaiśca tiṣṭha tiṣṭheti niḥsvanaiḥ||17||

18ghorairhalahalāśabdairmā gāstiṣṭhaihi māmiti
|
asāvahamamitreti pravadanto muhurmuhuḥ||18||

19bṛṃhitaiḥ siñjitaihāsaiḥ karanemisvanairapi
|
sannādayanto vasudhāmabhidudruvurārjunim||19||

20teṣāmāpatatāṃ vīraḥ śīghrayodhī mahābalaḥ
|
kṣiprāstro nyavadhīdrājan marmajño marmabhedibhiḥ||20||

21te hanyamānā vivaśā nānāliṅgaiḥ śitaiḥśaraiḥ
|
abhipetuḥ subahuśaḥ śalabhā iva pāvakam||21||

22tatasteṣāṃ śarīraiśca śarīrāvayavaiśca saḥ
|
santastāra kṣitiṃ kṣipraṃ kuśairvedimivādhvare||22||

23baddhagodhāṅgulitrāṇānsaśarāsanasāyakān
|
sāsicarmāṃkuśābhīṣūn satomaraparaśvadhān||23||

24sagadāyoguḍaprāsān sarṣṭitomarapaṭṭiśān
|
samiṃdipālaparighān saśaktivarakampanān||24||

25sapratodamahāśaṃkhān sakuntān sakacagrahān
|
samudgarakṣepaṇīyān sapāśaparighopalān||25||

26sakeyūrāṅgadān bāhūn hṛdyagandhānulepanāt
|
sañcicchedārjunistūrṇaṃ tvadīyānāṃ sahasraśaḥ||26||

27taiḥ sphuradbhirmahārāja śuśubhe bhūḥ sulohitaiḥ
|
pañcāsyaiḥ pannagaiśchinnairgaruḍeneva māriṣa||27||

28sunāsānanakeśāntairavraṇaiścārukuṇḍalaiḥ
|
saṃda‌ṣṭauṣṭhapuṭaiḥ krodhāt kṣaradbhiḥ śoṇitaṃ bahu||28||

29sa cārumukuṭoṣṇīṣairmaṇiratnavibhūṣitaiḥ
|
vinālanalinākārairdivākaraśaśiprabhaḥ||29||

30hitapriyaṃvadaiḥ kāle bahubhiḥ puṇyagandhibhiḥ
|
dviṣacchirobhiḥ pṛthivīṃ sa vai tastāra phālguniḥ||30||

31gandharvanagarākārān vidhivat kalpitān rathān
|
vīṣāmukhān dvitriveṇūn nyastadaṇḍakabandhurān||31||

32vijaṃghākūbarāṃstatra vinemidaśanānapi
|
vicakropaskaropasthān bhagnopakaraṇānapi||32||

33prapātitopastaraṇān hatayodhān sahasraśaḥ
|
śarairviśakalīkurvan dikṣu sarvāsvadṛśyata||33||

34punardvipān dvipārohānvaijayantyaṃkuśadhvajān
|
tūṇān varmāṇyatho kakṣyā graiveyāṃśca sakaṃbalān||34||

35ghaṇṭāḥ śuṇḍāviṣāṇāgrān chatramālāḥ padānugān
|
śarairniśitadhārāgraiḥ śātravāṇāmaśātayat||35||

36vanāyujān pārvatīyān kāmbojānatha bālhikān
|
sthiravāladhikarṇākṣān javanān sādhuvāhinaḥ||36||

37ārūḍhān śikṣitairyodhaiḥ śaktyṛṣṭiprāsayodhibhiḥ
|
vidhvastacāmaramukhān vipraviddhaprakīrṇakān||37||

38nirastajihvānayanānniṣkīrṇāntrayakṛddhanān
|
hatārohāṃcchinnaghaṇṭān kravyādagaṇamodakān||38||

39nikṛttacarmakavacān śakṛnmūtrāsṛgāplutān
|
nipātayannaśvavarāṃstāvakān sa vyarocata||39||

40eko viṣṇurivācintyaṃ kṛtvā karma suduṣkaram
|
tathā nirmathitaṃ tena tryaṅgaṃ tava balaṃ mahat||40||

41yathā'surabalaṃ ghoraṃ tryambakena mahaujasā
|
kṛtvā karma raṇe'sahyaṃ parairārjunirāhave||41||

42abhinacca padātyoghāṃstvadīyāneva sarvaśaḥ
|
evamekena tāṃ senāṃ saubhadreṇa śitaiḥ śaraiḥ||42||

43bhṛśaṃ viprahatāṃ dṛṣṭvā skandenevāsurīṃ camūm
|
tvadīyāstava putrāśca vīkṣamāṇā diśo daśa||43||

44saṃśuṣkāsyāścalannetrāḥ prasvinnā romaharṣiṇaḥ
|
palāyanakṛtotsāhā nirutsāhā dviṣajjaye||44||

45gotranāmabhiranyonyaṃ krandanto jīvitaiṣiṇaḥ
|
hatān putrān pitṝn bhrātṝn baṃdhūn saṃbandhinastathā||45||

46prātiṣṭhanta samutsṛsya tvarayanto hayadvipān||46||
