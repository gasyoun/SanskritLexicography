Mahābhārata 12.302 (Śāntiparvan)

1yudhiṣṭhira uvāca|
kiṃ tadakṣaramityuktaṃ yasmānnāvartate punaḥ
|
kiṃ ca tatkṣaramityuktaṃ yasmādāvartate punaḥ||1||

2akṣarakṣarayorvyaktiṃ pṛcchāmyariniṣūdan
|
upalabdhaṃ mahābāho tattvena kurunandana||2||

3tvaṃ hi jñānānidhiviprairucyase vedapāragaiḥ
|
ṛṣibhiśca mahābhāgairyatibhiśca mahātmabhiḥ||3||

4śeṣamalpaṃ dinānāṃ te dakṣiṇāyanabhāskare
|
āvṛte bhagavatyarke gantāsi paramāṃ gatim||4||

5tvayi pratigate śreyaḥ kutaḥ śroṣyāmahe vayam
|
kuruvaṃśapradīpastvaṃ jñānadīpena dīpyase||5||

6tadetacchrotumicchāmi tvattaḥ kurukulodvaha
|
na tṛpyāmīha rājendra śṛṇvannamṛtamīdṛśam||6||

7bhīṣma uvāca|
atra te vartayiṣyāmi itihāsaṃ purātanam
|
vasiṣṭhasya ca saṃvādaṃ karālajanakasya ca||7||

8vasiṣṭhaṃ śreṣṭhamāsīnamṛṣīṇāṃ bhāskaradyutim
|
papraccha janako rājā jñānaṃ naiḥśreyasaṃ param||8||

9paramadhyātmakuśalamadhyātmagatiniścayam
|
maitrāvaruṇimāsīnamabhivādya kṛtāñjaliḥ||9||

10svakṣaraṃ praśritaṃ vākyaṃ madhuraṃ cāpyanulbaṇam
|
papraccharṣivaraṃ rājā karālajanakaḥ purā||10||

11bhagan śrotumicchāmi paraṃ brahma sanātanam
|
yasmānna punarāvṛttimāpnuvanti manīṣiṇaḥ||11||

12yacca tatkṣaramityuktaṃ yatredaṃ kṣarate jagat
|
yaccākṣaramiti proktaṃ śivaṃ kṣemyamanāmayam||12||

13vasiṣṭha uvāca |
śrūyatāṃ pṛthivīpāla kṣaratīdaṃ yathā jagat
|
yanna kṣarati pūrveṇa yāvatkālena vā'pyatha||13||

14yugaṃ dvādaśasāhasraṃ kalpaṃ viddhi caturyugam
|
daśakalpaśatāvṛttamahastadbrāhmamucyate||14||

15rātriścaitāvatī rājan yasyānte pratibuddhyate
|
sṛjatyanantakarmāṇaṃ mahāntaṃ bhūtamagrajam||15||

16mūrtimantamamūrtātmā viśvaṃ śambhuḥ svayambhuvaḥ
|
aṇimā laghimā prāptirīśānaṃ jyotiravyayam||16||

17sarvataḥ pāṇipādaṃ tatsarvatokṣiśiromukham
|
sarvataḥśrutimalloke sarvamāvṛtya tiṣṭhati||17||

18hiraṇyagarbho bhagavāneṣa buddhiriti smṛtaḥ
|
mahāniti ca yogeṣu viriñciriti cāpyajaḥ||18||

19sāṃkhye ca paṭhyate śāstre nāmabhirbahudhātmakaḥ
|
vicitrarūpo viśvātmā ekākṣara iti smṛtaḥ||19||

20vṛtaṃ naikātmakaṃ yena kṛtaṃ trailokyamātmanā
|
tathaiva bahurūpatvādviśvarūpa iti smṛtaḥ||20||

21eṣa vai vikriyāpannaḥ sṛjatyātmānamātmanā
|
ahaṅkāraṃ mahātejāḥ prajāpatimahaṃkṛtam||21||

22avyaktādvyaktamāpannaṃ vidyāsargaṃ vadanti tam
|
mahāntaṃ cāpyahaṅkāramavidyāsargameva ca||22||

23avidhiśca vidhiścaiva samutpannau tathaikataḥ
|
vidyāvidyeti vikhyāte śrutiśāstrārthacintakaiḥ||23||

24bhūtasargamahaṅkārāttṛtīyaṃ viddhi pārthiva
|
ahaṅkāreṣu sarveṣu caturthaṃ viddhi vaikṛtam||24||

25vāyurjyotirathākāśamāpo'tha pṛthivī tathā
|
śabdaḥ sparśaśca rūpaṃ ca raso gandhastathaiva ca||25||

26evaṃ yugapadutpannaṃ daśavargamasaṃśayam
|
pañcamaṃ viddhi rājendra bhautikaṃ sargamarthavat||26||

27śrotraṃ tvakcakṣuṣī jihvā ghrāṇameva ca pañcamam
|
vākca hastau ca pādau ca pāyurmeḍhraṃ tathaiva ca||27||

28buddhīndriyāṇi caitāni tathā karmendriyāṇi ca
|
sambhūtānīha yugapanmanasā saha pārthivam||28||

29eṣā tattvacaturviṃśā sarvākṛtiṣu vartate
|
yāṃ jñātvā nābhiśocanti brāhmaṇāstattvadarśinaḥ||29||

30etaddehaṃ samākhyānaṃ trailokye sarvadehiṣu
|
veditavyaṃ naraśreṣṭha sadevanaradānave||30||

31sayakṣabhūtagandharve sakinnaramahorage
|
sacāraṇapiśāce vai sadevarṣiniśācare||31||

32sadaṃśakīṭamaśake sapūtikṛmimūṣike
|
śuni śvapāke caiṇeye sacāṇḍāle sapulkase||32||

33hastyaśvakharaśārdūle savṛkṣe gavi caiva ha
|
yacca mūrtimayaṃ kiñcitsarvatraitannidarśanam||33||

34jale bhuvi tathā''kāśe nānyatreti viniścayaḥ
|
sthānaṃ dehavatāmāsīdityevamanuśuśruma||34||

35kṛtsnametāvatastāta kṣarate vyaktasaṃjñitam
|
ahanyahani bhūtātmā tataḥ kṣara iti smṛtaḥ||35||

36etadakṣaramityuktaṃ kṣaratīdaṃ yathā jagat
|
jaganmohātmakaṃ prāhuravyaktādvyaktasaṃjñakam||36||

37mahāṃścaivāgrajo nityametatkṣaranidarśanam
|
kathitaṃ te mahārāja yanmāṃ tvaṃ paripṛcchasi||37||

38pañcaviṃśatimo viṣṇurnistattvastattvasaṃjñitaḥ
|
tattvasaṃśrayaṇādetattattvamāhurmanīṣiṇaḥ||38||

39yanmartyamasṛjadvyaktaṃ tattanmūrtyadhitiṣṭhati
|
caturviṃśatimo vyakto hyamūrtaḥ pañcaviṃśakaḥ||39||

40sa eva hṛdi sarvāsu mūrtiṣvātiṣṭhate''tmavān
|
kevalaścetano nityaḥ sarvamūrtiramūrtimān||40||

41sargapralayadharmiṇyā sa sargapralayātmakaḥ
|
gocare vartate nityaṃ nirguṇaṃ guṇasaṃjñitam||41||

42evameṣa mahānātmā sargapralayakovidaḥ
|
vikurvāṇaḥ prakṛtimānabhimanyatyabuddhimān||42||

43tamaḥsattvarajoyuktastāsu tāsviha yoniṣu
|
līyate pratibuddhatvādabuddhajanasevanāt||43||

44sahavāsavināśitvānnānyo'hamiti manyate
|
yo'haṃ so'hamiti hyuktvā guṇānevānuvartate||44||

45tamasā tāmasānbhāvānvividhānpratipadyate
|
rajasā rājasāṃścaiva sāttvikānsattvasaṃśrayāt||45||

46śuklalohitakṛṣṇāni rūpāṇyetāni trīṇi tu
|
sarvāṇyetāni rūpāṇi yānīha prākṛtāni vai||46||

47tāmasā nirayaṃ yānti rājasā mānuṣānatha
|
sāttvikā devalokāya gacchanti sukhabhāginaḥ||47||

48niṣkaivalyena pāpena tiryagyonimavāpnuyāt
|
puṇyapāpena mānuṣyaṃ puṇyenaikena devatāḥ||48||

49evamavyaktaviṣayaṃ kṣaramāhurmanīṣiṇaḥ
|
pañcaviṃśatimo yo'yaṃ jñānādeva pravartate||49||
