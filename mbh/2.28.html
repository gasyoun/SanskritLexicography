Mahābhārata 2.28 (Sabhāparvan)

1vaiśampāyana uvāca|
sa śvetaparvataṃ vīraḥ samatikramya vīryavān
|
deśaṃ kiṃpuruṣāvāsaṃ drumaputreṇa rakṣitam||1||

2mahatā sannipātena kṣatriyāntakareṇa ha
|
ajayatpāṇḍavaśreṣṭhaḥ kare cainaṃ nyaveśayat||2||

3taṃ jitvā hāṭakaṃ nāma deśaṃ guhyakarakṣitam
|
pākaśāsaniravyagraḥ saha sainyaḥ samāsadat||3||

4tāṃstu sāntvena nirjitya mānasaṃ sara uttamam
|
ṛṣikulyāstathā sarvā dadarśa kurunandanaḥ||4||

5saro mānasamāsādya hāṭakānabhitaḥ prabhuḥ
|
gandharvarakṣitaṃ deśamajayatpāṇḍavastataḥ||5||

6tatra tittirikalmāṣānmaṇḍūkākhyānhayottamān
|
lebhe sa karamatyantaṃ gandharvanagarāttadā||6||

7uttaraṃ harivarṣaṃ tu sa samāsādya pāṇḍavaḥ
|
iyeṣa jetuṃ taṃ deśaṃ pākaśāsanandanaḥ||7||

8tata enaṃ mahāvīryaṃ mahākāyā mahābalāḥ
|
dvārapālāḥ samāsādya hṛṣṭā vacanamabruvan||8||

9pārtha nedaṃ tvayā śakyaṃ puraṃ jetuṃ kathañcana
|
upāvartasva kalyāṇa paryāptamidamacyuta||9||

10idaṃ puraṃ yaḥ praviśedghruvaṃ na sa bhavennaraḥ
|
prīyāmahe tvayā vīra paryāpto vijayastava||10||

11na cātra kiñcijjetavyamarjunātra pradṛśyate
|
uttarāḥ kuruvo hyete nātra yuddhaṃ pravartate||11||

12praviṣṭo'pi hi kaunteya neha drakṣyasi kiñcana
|
na hi mānuṣadehena śakyamatrābhivīkṣitum||12||

13atheha puruṣavyāghra kiñcidanyaccikīrṣasi
|
tatprabrūhi kariṣyāmo vacanāttava bhārata||13||

14tatastānabravīdrājannarjunaḥ prahasanniva
|
pārthivatvaṃ cikīrṣāmi dharmarājaśca dhīmataḥ||14||

15na pravekṣyāmi vo deśaṃ viruddhaṃ yadi mānuṣaiḥ
|
yudhiṣṭhirāya yatkiñcitkarapaṇyaṃ pradīyatām||15||

16tato divyāni vastrāṇi divyānyābharaṇāni ca
|
kṣaumājināni divyāni tasya te pradaduḥ karam||16||

17evaṃ sa puruṣavyāghro vijitya diśamuttarām
|
saṅgrāmānsubahūnkṛtvā kṣatriyairdasyubhistathā||17||

18sa vinirjitya rājñastānkare ca viniveśya tu
|
dhanānyādāya sarvebhyo ratnāni vividhāni ca||18||

19hayāṃstittirikalmāṣāñcchukapatranibhānapi
|
mayūrasadṛśānyānsarvānanilaraṃhasaḥ||19||

20vṛtaḥ sumahatā rājanbalena caturaṅgiṇā
|
ājagāma punarvīraḥ śakraprasthaṃ purottamam||20||

21dharmarājāya tatpārtho dhanaṃ sarvaṃ savāhanam
|
nyavedayadanujñātastena rājñā gṛhānyayau||21||
