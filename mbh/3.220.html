Mahābhārata 3.220 (Āraṇyakaparvan)

1mārkaṇḍeya uvāca|
kāśyapo hyatha vāsiṣṭhaḥ prāṇaśca prāṇaputrakaḥ
|
agnirāṅgirasaścaiva cyavanastriṣu varcakaḥ||1||

2acarat sa-tapastīvraṃ putrārthe bahuvārṣikam
|
putraṃ labheyaṃ dharmiṣṭhaṃ yaśasā brahmaṇā samam||2||

3mahāvyāhṛtibhirdhyātaḥ pañcabhistaistadā tvatha
|
jajñe tejo mahārciṣmān pañcavarṇaḥ prabhāvanaḥ||3||

4samiddho'gniḥ śirastasya bāhū sūryanibhau tathā
|
tvaṅnetre ca suvarṇābhe kṛṣṇe jaṅghe ca bhārata||4||

5pañcavarṇaḥ sa tapasā kṛtastaiḥ pañcabhirjanaiḥ
|
pāñcajanyaḥ śruto devaḥ pañcavaṃśakarastu saḥ||5||

6daśavarṣasahasrāṇi tapastaptvā mahātapāḥ
|
janayat pāvakaṃ ghoraṃ pitṝṇāṃ sa prajāḥ sṛjan||6||

7bṛhadrathantaraṃ mūrdhno vaktrādvā tarasāharau
|
śivaṃ nābhyāṃ balādindraṃ vāyvagnī prāṇato'sṛjat||7||

8bāhubhyāmanudāttau ca viśve bhūtāni caiva ha
|
etān sṛṣṭvā tataḥ pañca pitṝṇāmasṛjat sutān||8||

9bṛhadrathasya praṇidhiḥ kāśyapasya mahattaraḥ
|
bhānuraṅgiraso dhīraḥ putro varcasya saubharaḥ||9||

10prāṇasya cānudāttastu vyākhyātāḥ pañcaviṃśatiḥ
|
devān yajñamuṣaścānyān sṛjan pañcadaśottarān||10||

11subhīmamatibhīmaṃ ca bhīmaṃ bhīmabalābalam
|
etān yajñamuṣaḥ pañca devānāṃ hyasṛjat tapaḥ||11||

12sumitraṃ mitravantaṃ ca mitrajñaṃ mitravardhanam
|
mitradharmāṇamityetān devānabhyasṛjat tapaḥ||12||

13surapravīraṃ vīraṃ ca surejaśaṃ ca suvarcasam
|
surāṇāmapi hantāraṃ pañcaitānasṛjat tapaḥ||13||

14trividhaṃ saṃsthitā hyete pañca pañca pṛthak pṛthak
|
muṣṇantyatra sthitā hyete svargato yajñayājinaḥ||14||

15teṣāmiṣṭaṃ harantyete nighnanti ca mahaddhaviḥ
|
spardhayā havyavāhānāṃ nighnantyete haranti ca||15||

16bahirvedyāṃ tadādānaṃ kuśalaiḥ sampravartitam
|
tadetenopasarpanti yatra cāgniḥ sthito bhavet||16||

17citāgnerudvahannājyaṃ pakṣābhyāṃ tat pravartite
|
mantraiḥ praśamitā hyete neṣṭaṃ muṣṇanti yajñiyam||17||

18bṛhadukthatapasyaiva putrau bhūmimupāśritaḥ
|
agnihotre hūyamāne pṛthivyāṃ sadbhirijyate||18||

19rathantaraśca tapasaḥ putro'gniḥ paripaṭhyate
|
mitravindāya vai tasmai haviradhvaryavo viduḥ||19||

20mumude paramaprītaḥ saha putrairmahāyaśāḥ||20||
