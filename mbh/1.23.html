Mahābhārata 1.23 (Ādiparvan)

1sautiruvāca|
taṃ samudramatikramya kadrūrvinatayā saha
|
nyapatatturagābhyāśe na cirādiva śīghragā||1||

2tataste taṃ hayaśreṣṭhaṃ dadṛśāte mahājavam
|
śaśāṅkakiraṇaprakhyaṃ kālavālamubhe tadā||2||

3niśāmya ca bahūnvālānkṛṣṇānpucchasamāśritān
|
viṣaṇṇarūpāṃ vinatāṃ kadrūrdāsye nyayojayat||3||

4tataḥ sā vinatā tasminpaṇitena parājitā
|
abhavadduḥkhasaṃtaptā dāsībhāvaṃ samāsthitā||4||

5etasminnantare cāpi garuḍaḥ kāla āgate
|
vinā mātrā mahātejā vidāryāṇḍamajāyata||5||

6mahāsattvabalopetaḥ sarvā vidyotayandiśaḥ
|
kāmarūpaḥ kāmagamaḥ kāmavīryo vihaṃgamaḥ||6||

7agnirāśirivodbhāsansamiddho'tibhayaṃkaraḥ
|
vidyudvispaṣṭapiṅgākṣo yugāntāgnisamaprabhaḥ||7||

8pravṛddhaḥ sahasā pakṣī mahākāyo nabhogataḥ
|
ghoro ghorasvano raudro vahniraurva ivāparaḥ||8||

9taṃ dṛṣṭvā śaraṇaṃ jagmurdevāḥ sarve vibhāvasum
|
praṇipatyābruvaṃścainamāsīnaṃ viśvarūpiṇam||9||

10agne mā tvaṃ pravardhiṣṭhāḥ kaccinno na didhakṣasi
|
asau hi rāśiḥ sumahānsamiddhastava sarpati||10||

11agniruvāca|
naitadevaṃ yathā yūyaṃ manyadhvamasurārdanāḥ
|
garuḍo balavāneṣa mama tulyaśca tejasā||11||

12jātaḥ paramatejasvī vinatānandavardhanaḥ
|
tejorāśimimaṃ dṛṣṭvā yuṣmānmohaḥ samāviśat||12||

13nāgakṣayakaraścaiva kāśyapeyo mahābalaḥ
|
devānāṃ ca hite yuktastvahito daityarakṣasām||13||

14na bhīḥ kāryā kathaṃ cātra paśyadhvaṃ sahitā mayā
|
evamuktāstadā gatvā garuḍaṃ vāgbhirastuvan||14||

15te dūrādabhyupetyainaṃ devāḥ sarṣigaṇāstadā
|
devā ūcuḥ|
tvamṛṣistvaṃ mahābhāgastvaṃ devaḥ patageśvaraḥ||15||

16tvaṃ prabhustapanaḥ sūryaḥ parameṣṭhī prajāpatiḥ
|
tvamindrastvaṃ hayamukhastvaṃ śarvastvaṃ jagatpatiḥ||16||

17tvaṃ mukhaṃ padmajo viprastvamagniḥ pavanastathā
|
tvaṃ hi dhātā vidhātā ca tvaṃ viṣṇuḥ surasattamaḥ||17||

18tvaṃ mahānabhibhūḥ śaśvadamṛtaṃ tvaṃ mahadyaśaḥ
|
tvaṃ prabhāstvamabhipretaṃ tvaṃ nastrāṇamanuttamam||18||

19balormimānsādhuradīnasattvaḥ
samṛddhimāndurviṣahastvameva
|
tvattaḥ sṛtaṃ sarvamahīnakīrte
hyanāgataṃ copagataṃ ca sarvam||19||

20tvamuttamaḥ sarvamidaṃ carācaraṃ
gabhastibhirbhānurivāvabhāsase
|
samākṣipanbhānumataḥ prabhāṃ muhuḥ
tvamantakaḥ sarvamidaṃ dhruvādhruvam||20||

21divākaraḥ parikupito yathā dahet
prajāstathā dahasi hutāśanaprabhaḥ
|
bhayaṃkaraḥ pralaya ivāgnirutthitaḥ
vināśayanyugaparivartanāntakṛt||21||

22khageśvaraṃ śaraṇamupāgatā vayaṃ
mahaujasaṃ jvalanasamānavarcasam
|
taḍitprabhaṃ vitimiramabhragocaraṃ
mahābalaṃ garuḍamupetya khecaram||22||

23parāvaraṃ varadamajayyavikramaṃ
tavaujasa sarvamidaṃ pratāpitam
|
jagatprabho taptasuvarṇavarcasā
tvaṃ pāhi sarvāṃśca surānmahātmanaḥ||23||

24bhayānvitā nabhasi vimānagāmino
vimānitā vipathagatiṃ prayānti te
|
ṛṣeḥ sutastvamasi dayāvataḥ prabho
mahātmanaḥ khagavara kaśyapasya ha||24||

25sa mā krudhaḥ kuru jagato dayāṃ parāṃ
tvamīśvaraḥ praśamamupaihi pāhi naḥ
|
mahāśanisphuritasamasvanena te
diśo'mbaraṃ tridivamiyaṃ ca medinī||25||

26calanti naḥ svagahṛdayāni cāniśaṃ
nigṛhyatāṃ vapuridamagnisaṃnibham
|
tava dyutiṃ kupitakṛtāntasaṃnibhāṃ
niśamya naścalati mano'vyavasthitam
|
pasīda naḥ patagapate prayācatāṃ
śivaśca no bhava bhagavansukhāvahaḥ||26||

27evaṃ stutaḥ suparṇastu devaiḥ sarṣigaṇaistadā
|
tejasaḥ pratisaṃhāramātmanaḥ sa cakāra ha||27||
