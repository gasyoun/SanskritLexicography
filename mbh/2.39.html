Mahābhārata 2.39 (Sabhāparvan)

1vaiśampāyana uvāca|
evamuktvā tato bhīṣṇo virarāma mahābalaḥ
|
vyājahārottaraṃ tatra sahadevo'rthavadvacaḥ||1||

2keśavaṃ keśihantāramaprameyaparākramam
|
pūjyamānaṃ mayā yo vaḥ kṛṣṇaṃ na sahate nṛpāḥ||2||

3sarveṣāṃ balināṃ mūrdhni mayedaṃ nihitaṃ padam
|
evamukte mayā samyaguttaraṃ prabravītu saḥ||3||

4sa eva hi mayā vadhyo bhaviṣyati na saṃśayaḥ
|
matimantaśca ye kecidācāryaṃ pitaraṃ gurum||4||

5arcyamarcitamarghyārhamanujānantu te nṛpāḥ
|
tato na vyājahāraiṣāṃ kaścidbuddhimatāṃ satām||5||

6mānināṃ balināṃ rājñāṃ madhye vai darśite pade
|
tato'patatpuṣpavṛṣṭiḥ sahadevasya mūrdhani||6||

7adṛśyarūpā vācaścāpyabruvansādhusādhviti
|
avidhyadajinaṃ kṛṣṇaṃ bhaviṣyadbhūtajalpakaḥ||7||

8sarvasaṃśayanirmoktā nāradaḥ sarvalokavit
|
uvācākhilabhūtānāṃ madhye spaṣṭataraṃ vacaḥ||8||

9kṛṣṇaṃ kamalapatrākṣaṃ nārcayiṣyanti ye narāḥ
|
jīvanmṛtāstu te jñeyā na saṃbhāṣyāḥ kadācanā||9||

10vaiśampāyana uvāca|
pūjayitvā ca pūjārhānbrahmakṣatraviśeṣavit
|
sahadevo nṛṇāṃ devaḥ samāpayata karma tat||10||

11tasminnabhyarcite kṛṣṇe sunīthaḥ śatrukarṣaṇaḥ
|
atitāmrekṣaṇaḥ kopāduvāca manujādhipān||11||

12sthitaḥ senāpatiryo'haṃ manvadhvaṃ kiṃ tu sāmpratam
|
yudhi tiṣṭhāma sannahya sametānvṛṣṇipāṇḍavān||12||

13iti sarvānsamutsādya rājñastāṃścedipuṅgavaḥ
|
yajñopaghātāya tataḥ sāmantrayata rājabhiḥ||13||

14tatrāhūtā gatāḥ sarve sunīthapramukhā gaṇāḥ
|
samadṛśyanta saṅkruddhā vivarṇavadanāstathā||14||

15yudhiṣṭhirābhiṣekaṃ ca vāsudevasya cārhaṇam
|
na syādyathā tathā kāryamevaṃ sarve tadā'bruvan||15||

16niṣkarṣānniścayātsarve rājānaḥ krodhamūrchitāḥ
|
abruvaṃstatra rājāno nirvedādātmaniścayāt||16||

17suhṛdbhirvāryamāṇānāṃ teṣāṃ hi vapurābabhau
|
āmiṣādapakṛṣṭānāṃ siṃhānāmiva garjatām||17||

18taṃ balaughamaparyantaṃ rājasāgaramakṣayam
|
kurvāṇaṃ samayaṃ kṛṣṇo yuddhāya bubudhe tadā||18||
