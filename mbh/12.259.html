Mahābhārata 12.259 (Śāntiparvan)

1yudhiṣṭhira uvāca|
ime vai mānavāḥ sarve dharmaṃ prati viśaṅkitāḥ
|
ko'yaṃ dharmaḥ kuto dharmastanme brūhi pitāmaha ||1||

2dharmastvayamihārthaḥ kimutrārtho'pi vā bhavet
|
ubhayārtho hi vā dharmastanme brūhi pitāmaha ||2||

3bhīṣma uvāca|
sadācāraḥ smṛtirvedāstrividhaṃ dharmalakṣaṇam
|
caturthamarthamityāhuḥ kavayo dharmalakṣaṇam ||3||

4api hyuktāni dharmyāṇi vyavasyantyuttarāvare
|
lokayātrārthameveha dharmasya niyamaḥ kṛtaḥ ||4||

5ubhayatra sukhodarka iha caiva paratra ca
|
alabdhvā nipuṇaṃ dharmaṃ pāpaḥ pāpena yujyate ||5||

6na ca pāpakṛtaḥ pāpānmucyante kecidāpadi
|
apāpavādī bhavati yathā bhavati dharmakṛt
|
dharmasya niṣṭhā tvācārastamevāśritya bhotsyase ||6||

7yathā dharmasamāviṣṭo dhanaṃ gṛhṇāti taskaraḥ
|
ramate nirharaṃstenaḥ paravittamarājake ||7||

8yadā'sya taddharantyanye tadā rājānamicchati
|
tadā teṣāṃ spṛhayate ye vai tuṣṭāḥ svakairdhanaiḥ ||8||

9abhītaḥ śucirabhyeti rājadvāramaśaṅkitaḥ
|
na hi duścaritaṃ kiṃcidantarātmani paśyati ||9||

10satyasya vacanaṃ sādhu na satyādvidyate param
|
satyena vidhṛtaṃ sarvaṃ sarvaṃ satye pratiṣṭhitam ||10||

11api pāpakṛto raudrāḥ kṛtvā pṛthak pṛthak
|
adrohamavisaṃvādaṃ pravartante tadāśrayāḥ ||11||

12te cenmitho dhṛtiṃ kuryurvinaśyeyurasaṃśayam
|
na hartavyaṃ paradhanamiti dharmaḥ sanātanaḥ ||12||

13manyante balavantastaṃ durbalaiḥ sampravartitam
|
yadā niyatidaurbalyamathaiṣāmeva rocate ||13||

14na hyatyantaṃ balavanto bhavanti sukhino'pi vā
|
tasmādanārjave buddhirna kāryā te kadācana ||14||

15asādhubhyo'sya na bhayaṃ na caurebhyo na rājataḥ
|
akiṃcitkasyacitkurvan nirbhayaḥ śucirāvaset ||15||

16sarvataḥ śaṅkate steno mṛgo grāmamiveyivān
|
bahudhā''caritaṃ pāpamanyatraivānupaśyati ||16||

17muditaḥ śucirabhyeti sarvato nirbhayaḥ sadā
|
na hi duścaritaṃ kiṃcidātmano'nyeṣu paśyati ||17||

18dātavyamityayaṃ dharma ukto bhūtahite rataiḥ
|
taṃ manyante dhanayutāḥ kṛpaṇaiḥ sampravartitam ||18||

19yadā niyatikārpaṇyamathaiṣāmeva rocate
|
na hatyantaṃ dhanvanto bhavanti sukhino'pi vā ||19||

20yadanyairvihitaṃ necchedātmanaḥ karma pūruṣaḥ
|
na tatpareṣu kurvīta jānannapriyayātmanaḥ ||20||

21yo'nyasya syādupapatiḥ sa kaṃ kiṃ vaktumarhati
|
yadanyasya tataḥ kuryānna mṛṣyediti me matiḥ ||21||

22jīvituṃ yaḥ svayaṃ cecchetkathaṃ so'nyaṃ praghātayet
|
yadyadātmani ceccheta tatparasyāpi cintayet ||22||

23atiriktaiḥ saṃvibhajedbhogairanyānakiṃcanān
|
etasmātkāraṇāddhātrā kusīdaṃ sampravartitam ||23||

24yasmiṃstu devāḥ samaye santiṣṭheraṃstathā bhavet
|
athavā lābhasamaye sthitidharme'pi śobhanā ||24||

25sarvaṃ priyābhyupagataṃ dharmamāhurmanīṣiṇaḥ
|
paśyaitaṃ lakṣaṇoddeśaṃ dharmādharme yudhiṣṭhira ||25||

26lokasaṃgrahasaṃyuktaṃ vidhātrā vihitaṃ purā
|
sūkṣmadharmārthaniyataṃ satāṃ caritamuttamam ||26||

27dharmalakṣaṇamākhyātametatte kurusattama
|
tasmādanārjave buddhirna te kāryā kathaṃcana ||27||
