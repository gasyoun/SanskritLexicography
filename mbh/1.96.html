Mahābhārata 1.96 (Ādiparvan)

1vaiśaṃpāyana uvāca|
ikṣvākuvaṃśaprabho rājāsītpṛthivīpatiḥ
|
mahābhiṣagiti khyātaḥ satyavāksatyavikramaḥ||1||

2so'śvamedhasahasreṇa rājasūyaśatena ca
|
toṣayāmāsa deveśaṃ svargaṃ lebhe tataḥ prabhuḥ||2||

3tataḥ kadācidbrahmāṇamupāsāṃcakrire surāḥ
|
tatra rājarṣayo hyāsansa ca rājā mahābhiṣak||3||

4atha gaṅgā saricchreṣṭhā samupāyātpitāmaham
|
tasyā vāsaḥ samuddhūtaṃ mārutena śaśiprabham||4||

5tato bhavansuragaṇāḥ sahasā vāṅmukhāstadā
|
mahābhiṣaktu rājarṣiraśaṅko dṛṣṭavānnadīm||5||

6so'padhyāto bhagavatā brahmaṇā tu mahābhiṣa:
|
uktaśca jāto martyeṣu punarlokānavāpsyasi||6||

7yayāhṛtamanāścāsi gaṅgayā tvaṃ hi durmate
|
sā te vai mānuṣe loke vipriyāṇyā cariṣyati||7||

8yadā te bhavitā manyustadā śāpādvimokṣyate
|
vaiśaṃpāyana uvāca|
sa cintayitvā nṛpatirnṛpānanyāṃstapodhanān||8||

9pratīpaṃ rocayāmāsa pitaraṃ bhūritejasam
|
mahābhiṣaṃ tu taṃ dṛṣṭvā nadī dhairyāccyutaṃ nṛpam||9||

10tameva manasā dhyāyantyupāvartatsaridvarā
|
sā tu vidhvastavapuṣaḥ kaśmalābhihatānnṛpa||10||

11dadarśa pathi gacchantī vasūndevāndivaukasaḥ
|
tathārūpāṃśca tāndṛṣṭvā prapaccha saritāṃ varā||11||

12kimidaṃ naṣṭarūpāḥ stha kaccitkṣemaṃ divaukasām
|
tāmūcurvasavo devāḥ śaptāḥ smo vai mahānadi||12||

13alpe'parādhe saṃrambhādvasiṣṭhena mahātmanā
|
vimūḍhā hi vayaṃ sarve pracchannamṛṣisattamam||13||

14sandhyāṃ vasiṣṭhamāsīnaṃ tamatyabhisṛtāḥ purā
|
tena kopādvayaṃ śaptā yonau saṃbhavateti ha||14||

15na tacchakyaṃ nivartayituṃ yaduktaṃ brahmavādinā
|
tvamasmānmānuṣī bhūtvā sṛja putrānvasūnbhuvi||15||

16na mānuṣīṇāṃ jaṭharaṃ praviśema vayaṃ śubhe
|
ityuktā taiśca vasubhistathetyuktvā'bravīdidam||16||

17gaṅgovāca
|
martyeṣu puruṣaśreṣṭhaḥ ko vaḥ kartā bhaviṣyati
|
vasava ūcuḥ|
pratīpasya suto rājā śāntanurlokaviśrutaḥ
|
bhavitā mānuṣeloke sa naḥ kartā bhaviṣyati||17||

18gaṅgovāca
|
mamāpyevaṃ mataṃ devā yathā māṃ vadathānaghāḥ
|
priyaṃ tasya kariṣyāmi yuṣmākaṃ caitadīpsitam||18||

19vasava ūcuḥ|
jātānkumārānsvānapsu prakṣeptuṃ vai tvamarhasi
|
yathā na cirakālaṃ no niṣkṛtiḥ syāttrilokage||19||

20gaṅgovāca
|
evametatkariṣyāmi putrastasya vidhīyatām
|
nāsya moghaḥ saṅgamaḥ syātputrahetormayā saha||20||

21vasava ūcuḥ|
turīyārdhaṃ pradāsyāmo vīryasyaikaikaśo vayam
|
tena vīryeṇa putraste bhavitā tasya cepsitaḥ||21||

22na saṃpatsyati martyeṣu punastasya tu santatiḥ
|
tasmādaputraḥ putraste bhaviṣyati sa vīryavān||22||

23evaṃ te samayaṃ kṛtvā gaṅgayā vasavaḥ saha
|
jagmuḥ saṃhṛṣṭamanaso yathā saṃkalpamañjasā||23||
