Mahābhārata 14.33 (Āśvamedhikaparvan)

1brāhmaṇa uvāca|
nāhaṃ tathā bhīru carāmi loke yathā tvaṃ māṃ tarjayase svabuddhyā
|
vipro'smi mukto'smi vanecaro'smi gṛhasthadharmā vratavāṃstathāsmi||1||

2nāhamasmi yathā māṃ tvaṃ paśyase ca śubhāśubhe
|
mayā vyāptamidaṃ sarvaṃ yatkiñcijjagatīgatam||2||

3ye kecijjantavo loke jaṅgamāḥ sthāvarāśca ha
|
teṣāṃ māmantakaṃ viddhi dārūṇāmiva pāvakam||3||

4rājyaṃ pṛthivyāṃ sarvasyāmathavāpi triviṣṭape
|
tathā buddhiriyaṃ vetti buddhireva dhanaṃ mama||4||

5ekaḥ panthā brāhmaṇānāṃ yena gacchanti tadvidaḥ
|
gṛheṣu vanavāseṣu guruvāseṣu bhikṣuṣu||5||

6liṅgairbahubhiragrairekā buddhirupāsyate
|
nānāliṅgāśramasthānāṃ yeṣāṃ buddhiḥ śamātmikā||6||

7te bhāvamekamāyānti saritaḥ sāgaraṃ yathā
|
buddhyāyaṃ gamyate mārgaḥ śarīreṇa na gamyate
|
ādyantavanti karmāṇi śarīraṃ karmabandhanam||7||

8tasmātte subhage nāsti paralokakṛtaṃ bhayam
|
tadbhāvabhāvaniratā mamaivātmānameṣyasi||8||
