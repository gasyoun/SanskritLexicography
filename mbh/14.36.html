Mahābhārata 14.36 (Āśvamedhikaparvan)

1brāhmaṇa uvāca|
tadavyaktamanudriktaṃ sarvavyāpi dhruvaṃ sthiram
|
navadvāraṃ puraṃ vidyāttriguṇaṃ pañcadhātukam||1||

2ekādaśaparikṣepaṃ manovyākaraṇātmakam
|
buddhisvāmikamityetatparamekādaśaṃ bhavet||2||

3trīṇi srotāṃsi yānyasminnāpyāyante punaḥ punaḥ
|
pranāḍyastisra evaitāḥ pravartante guṇātmikā:||3||

4tamo rajastathā sattvaṃ guṇānetān pracakṣate
|
anyonyamithunāḥ sarve tathānyonyānujīvinaḥ||4||

5anyonyāpāścayāścāpi tathānyonyānuvartinaḥ
|
anyonyavyatiṣaktāśca triguṇāḥ pañcadhātavaḥ||5||

6tamaso mithunaṃ sattvaṃ sattvasya mithunaṃ rajaḥ
|
rajasaścāpi sattvaṃ syātsattvasya mithunaṃ tamaḥ||6||

7niyamyate tamo yatra rajastatra pravartate
|
niyamyate rajo yatra sattvaṃ tatra pravartate||7||

8naiśātmaka tamo vidyāttriguṇaṃ moḍasaṃjñitam
|
adharmalakṣaṇaṃ caiva niyataṃ pāpakarmasu
|
tāmasaṃ rūpametattu dṛśyate cāpi saṅgattam||8||

9prakṛtyātmakamevāhū rajaḥ paryāyakārakam
|
pravṛttaṃ sarvabhūteṣu dṛśyamutpattilakṣaṇam||9||

10prakāśaṃ sarvabhūteṣu lāghavaṃ śraddadhānatā
|
sāttvikaṃ rūpamevaṃ tu lāghavaṃ sādhusammitam||10||

11eteṣāṃ guṇatattvāni vakṣyante tattvahetubhiḥ
|
samāsavyāsayuktāni tattvatastāni bodhata||11||

12sammoho'jñānamatyāgaḥ karmaṇāmavinirṇayaḥ
|
svapnaḥ stambho bhayaṃ lobhaḥ svataḥ sukṛtadūṣaṇam||12||

13asmṛtiścāvipākaśca nāstikyaṃ bhinnavṛttitā
|
nirviśeṣatvamandhatvaṃ jaghanyaguṇavṛttitā||13||

14akṛte kṛtamānitvamajñāne jñānamānitā
|
amaitrī vikṛtābhāvo hyaśraddhā mūḍhabhāvanā||14||

15anārjavamasaṃjñatvaṃ karma pāpacetanā
|
gurutvaṃ sannabhāvatvamavaśitvamavāggatiḥ||15||

16sarva ete guṇā vṛttāstāmasāḥ samprakīrtitāḥ
|
ye cānye vihitā bhāvā loke'smin bhāvasaṃjñitāḥ||16||

17tatra tatra niyamyante sarve te tāmasā guṇāḥ
|
parivādakathā nityaṃ devabrāhmaṇavaidikī||17||

18atyāgaścābhimānaśca moho manyustathākṣamā
|
matsaraścaiva bhūteṣu tāmasaṃ vṛttamipyate||18||

19vṛthārambhā hi ye kecidvṛthā dānāni yāni ca
|
vṛthā bhakṣaṇamityetattāmasaṃ vṛttamiṣyate||19||

20ativādo'titikṣā ca mātsaryamabhimānitā
|
aśraddadhānatā caiva tāmasaṃ vṛttamiṣyate||20||

21evaṃvidhāśca ye kecilloke'smin pāpakarmiṇaḥ
|
manuṣyā bhinnamaryādāste sarve tāmasāḥ smṛtāḥ||21||

22teṣāṃ yonīḥ pravakṣyāmi niyatāḥ pāpakarmiṇām
|
avāṅnirayabhāvā ye tiryanirayagāminaḥ||22||

23sthāvarāṇi ca bhūtāni paśavo vāhanāni ca
|
kravyādā dandaśūkāśca kṛmikīṭavihaṅgamāḥ||23||

24aṇḍajā jantavaścaiva sarve cāpi catuṣpadāḥ
|
unmattā badhirā mūkā ye cānye pāparogiṇaḥ||24||

25magnāstamasi durvṛttāḥ svakarma kṛtalakṣaṇāḥ
|
avāksrotasa ityete magnāstamasi tāmasāḥ||25||

26teṣāmutkarṣamudrekaṃ vakṣyāmyahamataḥ param
|
yathā te sukṛtā~llokā~llabhante puṇyakarmiṇaḥ||26||

27anyathā pratipannāstu vivṛddhā ye ca karmaṇaḥ
|
svakarmaniratānāṃ ca brāhmaṇānāṃ śubhaiṣiṇām||27||

28saṃskāreṇordhvamāyānti yatamānāḥ salokatām
|
svarge gacchanti devānāmityeṣā vaidikī śrutiḥ||28||

29anyathā pratipannāste vibuddhāḥ sveṣa karmasu
|
punarāvṛttidharmāṇaste bhavantīha mānuṣā:||29||

30pāpayoniṃ samāpannāścaṇḍālā mūkacūcukāḥ
|
varṇānparyāyaśaścāpi prāpnuvantyuttarottaram||30||

31śūdrayonimatikramya ye cānye tāmasā guṇāḥ
|
srotomadhye samāgamya vartante tāmase guṇe||31||

32abhiṣvaṅgastu kāmeṣu mahāmoha iti smṛtaḥ
|
ṛṣayo munayo devā muhyantyatra sukhepsavaḥ||32||

33tamo moho mahāmohastāmisraḥ krodhasaṃjñitaḥ
|
maraṇaṃ tvandhatāmisrastāmisraḥ krodha ucyate||33||

34varṇato guṇataścaiva yonitaścaiva tattvataḥ
|
sarvametattamo viprāḥ kīrtitaṃ vo yathāvidhi||34||

35konvetadbudhyate sādhu konvetatsādhu paśyati
|
atattve tattvadarśī yastamasastattvalakṣaṇam||35||

36tamoguṇā bahuvidhāḥ prakīrtitā yathāvaduktaṃ ca tamaḥ parāvaram
|
naro hi yo veda guṇānimānsadā sa tāmasaiḥ sarvaguṇaiḥ pramucyate||36||
