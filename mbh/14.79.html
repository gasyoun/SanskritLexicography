Mahābhārata 14.79 (Āśvamedhikaparvan)

1vaiśampāyana uvāca|
śrutvā tu nṛpatiḥ prāptaṃ pitaraṃ babhruvāhanaḥ
|
niryayau vinayenātha brāhmaṇārthapuraḥsaraḥ||1||

2maṇipūreśvaraṃ tvevamupayātaṃ dhanañjayaḥ
|
nābhyanandatsa medhāvī kṣatradharmamanusmaran||2||

3uvāca ca sa dharmātmā samanyuḥ phālgunastadā
|
prakriyeyaṃ na te yuktā bahistvaṃ kṣatradharmataḥ||3||

4saṃrakṣyamāṇaṃ turagaṃ yaudhiṣṭhiramupāgatam
|
yajñiyaṃ viṣayānte māṃ nāyautsīḥ kiṃ nu putraka||4||

5dhik tvāmastu sudurbuddhiṃ kṣatradharmabahiṣkṛtam
|
yo mā yuddhāya samprāptaṃ sāmnaiva pratyagṛhṇathāḥ||5||

6na tvayā puruṣārtho hi kaścidastīha jīvatā
|
yastvaṃ strīvadyathāprāptaṃ māṃ sāmnā pratyagṛhṇathāḥ||6||

7yadyahaṃ nyastaśastrastvāmāgaccheyaṃ sudurmate
|
prakriyeyaṃ bhavedyuktā tāvattava narādhama||7||

8tamevamuktaṃ bharvā tu viditvā pannagātmajā
|
amṛṣyamāṇā bhittvorvīmulūpī samupāgamat||8||

9sā dadarśa tataḥ putraṃ vimṛśantamadhomukham
|
santarjyamānamasakṛtpitrā yuddhārthinā prabho||9||

10tataḥ sā cārusarvāṅgī samupetyoragātmajā
|
ulūpī prāha vacanaṃ dharmyaṃ dharmaviśāradam||10||

11ulūpīṃ māṃ nibodha tvaṃ mātaraṃ pannagātmajām
|
kuruṣva vacanaṃ putra dharmaste bhavitā paraḥ||11||

12yudhvasvainaṃ kuruśreṣṭhaṃ pitaraṃ yuddhadurmadam
|
evameṣa hi te prīto bhaviṣyati na saṃśayaḥ||12||

13evaṃ durmarṣito rājā sa mātrā babhruvāhanaḥ
|
manaścakre mahātejā yuddhāya bharatarṣabha||13||

14sannahya kāñcanaṃ varma śirastrāṇaṃ ca bhānumat
|
tūṇīraśatasambādhamāruroha rathottamam||14||

15sarvopakaraṇopetaṃ yuktamaśvairmanojavaiḥ
|
sa cakropaskaraṃ śrīmānhemabhāṇḍapariṣkṛtam||15||

16paramārcitamucchritya dhvajaṃ siṃhaṃ hiraṇmayam
|
prayayau pārthamuddiśya sa rājā babhruvāhanaḥ||16||

17tato'bhyetya hayaṃ vīro yajñiyaṃ pārtharakṣitam
|
grāhayāmāsa puruṣairhayaśikṣāviśāradaiḥ||17||

18gṛhītaṃ vājinaṃ dṛṣṭvā prītātmā sa dhanañjayaḥ
|
putraṃ rathasthaṃ bhūmiṣṭhaḥ saṃnyavārayadāhave||18||

19sa tatra rājā taṃ vīraṃ śarasandhairanekaśaḥ
|
ardayāmāsa niśitairāśīviṣaviṣopamaiḥ||19||

20tayoḥ samabhavadyuddhaṃ pituḥ putrasya cātulam
|
devāsuraraṇaprakhyamubhayoḥ prīyamāṇayoḥ||20||

21kirīṭinaṃ pravivyādha śareṇānataparvaṇā
|
jatrudeśe naravyāghraṃ prahasanbabhruvāhanaḥ||21||

22so'bhyagātsaha puṅkhena valmīkamiva pannagaḥ
|
vinirbhidya ca kaunteyaṃ praviveśa mahītalam||22||

23sa gāḍhavedano dhīmānālambya dhanuruttamam
|
divyaṃ tejaḥ samāviśya pramīta iva sā'bhavat||23||

24sa saṃjñāmupalabhyātha praśasya puruṣarṣabhaḥ
|
putraṃ śakrātmajo vākyamidamāha mahādyutiḥ||24||

25sādhu sādhu mahābāho vatsa citrāṅgadātmaja
|
sadṛśaṃ karma te dṛṣṭvā prītimānasmi putraka||25||

26vimuñcāmyeṣa te bāṇānputra yuddhe sthiro bhava
|
ityevamuktvā nārācairabhyavarṣadamitrahā||26||

27tānsa gāṇḍīvanirmuktānvajrāśanisamaprabhān
|
nārācānacchinadrājā bhallaiḥ sarvāṃstridhā dvidhā||27||

28tasya pārthaḥ śarairdivyairdhvajaṃ hemapariṣkṛtam
|
suvarṇatālapratimaṃ kṣureṇāpāharadrathāt||28||

29hayāṃścāsya mahākāyānmahāvegānarindama
|
cakāra rājannirjīvānprahasanniva pāṇḍavaḥ||29||

30sa rathādavatīryātha rājā paramakopanaḥ
|
padātiḥ pitaraṃ kruddho yodhayāmāsa pāṇḍavam||30||

31samprīyamāṇaḥ pārthānāmṛṣabhaḥ putravikramāt
|
nātyarthaṃ pīḍayāmāsa putraṃ vajradharātmajaḥ||31||

32sa manyamāno vimukhaṃ pitaraṃ babhruvāhanaḥ
|
śarairāśīviṣākāraiḥ punarevārdayadbalī||32||

33tataḥ sa bālyātpitaraṃ vivyādha hṛdi patriṇā
|
niśitena supuṅkhena balavadbabhruvāhanaḥ||33||

34viveśa pāṇḍavaṃ rājanmarma bhittvātiduḥkhakṛt
|
sa tenātibhṛśaṃ viddhaḥ putreṇa kurunandanaḥ||34||

35mahīṃ jagāma mohārtastato rājandhanañjayaḥ
|
tasminnipatite vīre kauravāṇāṃ dhurandhare||35||

36so'pi mohaṃ jagāmātha tataścitrāṅgadāsutaḥ
|
vyāyamya saṃyuge rājā dṛṣṭvā ca pitaraṃ hatam||36||

37pūrvameva sa bāṇaughairgāḍhaviddho'rjunena ha
|
papāta so'pi dharaṇīmāliṅgya raṇamūrdhani||37||

38bhartāraṃ nihataṃ dṛṣṭvā putraṃ ca patitaṃ bhuvi
|
citrāḍgadā paritrastā praviveśa raṇājire||38||

39śokasantaptahṛdayā rudatī vepatī bhṛśam
|
maṇipūrapatermātā dadarśa nihataṃ patim||39||
