Mahābhārata 12.24 (Śāntiparvan)

1vaiśampāyana uvāca|
punareva maharṣistaṃ kṛṣṇadvaipāyano muniḥ
|
ajātaśatruṃ kaunteyamidaṃ vacanamabravīt ||1||

2araṇye vasatāṃ tāta bhrātṝṇāṃ te manasvinām
|
manorathā mahārāja ye tatrāsan yudhiṣṭhira ||2||

3tāni me bharataśreṣṭha prāpnuvantu mahārathāḥ
|
praśādhi pṛthivīṃ pārtha yayātiriva nāhuṣaḥ ||3||

4araṇye duḥkhavasatiranubhūtā tapasvibhiḥ
|
duḥkhasyānte naravyāghra sukhānyanubhavantu vai ||4||

5dharmamarthaṃ ca kāmaṃ ca bhrātṛbhiḥ saha bhārata
|
anubhūya tataḥ paścātprasthātā'si viśāṃpate ||5||

6arthināṃ ca pitṝṇāṃ ca devatānāṃ ca bhārata
|
ānṛṇyaṃ gaccha kaunteya tatsarvaṃ ca kariṣyati ||6||

7sarvamedhāśvamedhābhyāṃ yajasva kurunandana
|
tataḥ paścānmahārāja gamiṣyasi parāṃ gatim ||7||

8bhrātṝṃśca sarvān kratubhiḥ saṃyojya bahudakṣiṇaiḥ
|
samprāptaḥ kīrtimatulāṃ pāṇḍaveya bhaviṣyasi ||8||

9vidmaste puruṣavyāghra vacanaṃ kurusattama
|
śṛṇuṣvaivaṃ yathā kurvanna dharmāccyavase nṛpa ||9||

10ādadānasya vijayaṃ vigrahaṃ ca yudhiṣṭhira
|
samānadharmakuśalāḥ sthāpayanti nareśvara ||10||

11deśakālapratīkṣī yo dasyūnmarṣayate nṛpaḥ
|
śāstrajāṃ buddhimāsthāya yujyate nainasā hi saḥ ||11||

12ādāya baliṣaḍbhāgaṃ yo rāṣṭraṃ nābhirakṣati
|
pratigṛhṇāti tat pāpaṃ caturthāṃśena bhūmipaḥ ||12||

13nibodha ca yathā tiṣṭhan dharmānna cyavate nṛpaḥ
|
nigrahāddharmaśāstrāṇāmanuruddhyannapetabhīḥ ||13||

14kāma-krodhāvanādṛtya piteva samadarśanaḥ
|
śāstrajāṃ buddhimāsthāya yujyate nainasā hi saḥ ||14||

15daivenābhyāhato rājā karmakāle mahādyute
|
na sādhayati yatkarma na tatrāturatikramam ||15||

16tarasā buddhipūrvaṃ vā nigrāhyā eva śatravaḥ
|
pāpaiḥ saha na sandadhyādrājyaṃ puṇyaṃ ca kārayet ||16||

17śūrāścāryāśca satkāryā vidvāṃsaśca yudhiṣṭhira
|
gomino dhaninaścaiva paripālyā viśeṣataḥ ||17||

18vyavahāreṣu dharmeṣu yoktavyāśca bahuśrutāḥ
|
guṇayukte'pi naikasmin viśvaseta vicakṣaṇaḥ ||18||

19arakṣitā durvinīto mānī stabdho'bhyasūyakaḥ
|
enasā yujyate rājā durdānta iti cocyate ||19||

20ye rakṣyamāṇā hīyante daivenābhyāhatā nṛpa
|
taskaraiścāpi hīyante sarvaṃ tadrājakilbiṣam ||20||

21sumantrite sunīte ca sarvataścopapādite
|
pauruṣe karmaṇi kṛte nāstyadharmo yudhiṣṭhira ||21||

22vicchidyante samārabdhāḥ siddhyante cāpi daivataḥ
|
kṛte puruṣākāraṃ tu nainaḥ spṛśati pārthivam ||22||

23atra te rājaśārdūla vartayiṣye kathāmimām
|
yadvṛttaṃ pūrvarājarṣerhayagrīvasya pāṇḍava ||23||

24śatrūnhatvā hatasyājau śurasyākliṣṭakarmaṇaḥ
|
asahāyasya saṃgrāme nirjitasya yudhiṣṭhira||24||

25yatkarma vai nigrahe śātravāṇāṃ yogaścāgryaḥ pālane mānavānām
|
kṛtvā karma prāpya kīrtiṃ sa yuddhādvājigrīvo modate svargaloke ||25||

26saṃyuktātmāṃ samareṣvātatāyī śastraiśchinno dasyubhirvadhyamānaḥ
|
aśvagrīvaḥ karmaśīlo mahātmā saṃsiddhārtho modate svargaloke ||26||

27dhanuryūpo raśanā jyā śaraḥ sruk sruvaḥ khaḍgo rudhiraṃ yatra cājyajam
|
ratho vedī kāmajo yuddhamagniścāturhotraṃ caturo vājimukhyāḥ ||27||

28hutvā tasminyajñavahnāvathārīn pāpānmukto rājasiṃhastarasvī
|
prāṇān hutvā cāvabhṛthe raṇe sa vājigrīvo modate devaloke ||28||

29rāṣṭraṃ rakṣanbuddhipūrvaṃ nayena saṃtyaktātmā yajñaśīlo mahātmā
|
sarvāllokān vyāpya kīrtyā manasvī vājigrīvo modate devaloke ||29||

30daivīṃ siddhiṃ mānuṣīṃ daṇḍanītiṃ yoganyāsaiḥ pālayitvā mahīṃ ca
|
tasmādrājā dharmaśīlo mahātmā vājigrīvo modate devaloke ||30||

31vidvāṃstyāgī śraddadhānaḥ kṛtajñastyaktvā lokaṃ mānuṣaṃ karma kṛtvā
|
medhāvināṃ viduṣāṃ saṃmatānāṃ tanutyajāṃ lokamākramya rājā ||31||

32samyagvedān prāpya śāstrāṇyadhītya samyagrājyaṃ pālayitvā mahātmā
|
cāturvarṇyaṃ sthāpayitvā svadharme vājigrīvo modate devaloke ||32||

33jitvā saṃgrāmānpālayitvā prajāśca somaṃ pītvā tarpayitvā dvijāgryān
|
yuktyā daṇḍaṃ dhārayitvā prajānāṃ yuddhe kṣīṇe modate devaloke ||33||

34vṛttaṃ yasya ślāghanīyaṃ manuṣyāḥ santo vidvāṃso'rhayantyarhaṇīyam
|
svargaṃ jitvā vīralokānavāpya siddhiṃ prāptaḥ puṇyakīrtirmahātmā ||34||
