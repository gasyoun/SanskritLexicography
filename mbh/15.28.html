Mahābhārata 15.28 (Āśramavāsikaparvan)

1vaiśampāyana uvāca|
tataḥ samupaviṣṭeṣu pāṇḍaveṣu mahātmasu
|
vyāsaḥ satyavatīputra idaṃ vacanamabravīt||1||

2dhṛtarāṣṭra mahābāho kaccitte vartate tapaḥ
|
kaccinmanaste prīṇāti vanavāse narādhipa||2||

3kacciddhṛdi na te śoko rājanputravināśajaḥ
|
kaccijjñānāni sarvāṇi suprasannāni te'nagha||3||

4kaccidbuddhiṃ dṛḍhāṃ kṛtvā carasyāraṇyakaṃ vidhim
|
kaccidvadhūśca gāndhārī na śokenābhibhūyate||4||

5mahāprajñā buddhimatī devī dharmārthadarśinī
|
āgamāpāyatattvajñā kaccideṣā na śocati||5||

6kaccitkuntī ca rājaṃstvāṃ śuśrūṣatyanahaṅkṛtā
|
yā parityajya svaṃ putraṃ guruśuśrūṣaṇe ratā||6||

7kacciddharmasuto rājā tvayā pratyabhinanditaḥ
|
bhīmārjunayamāścaiva kaccidete'pi sāntvitāḥ||7||

8kaccinnandasi dṛṣṭvaitān kaccitte nirmalaṃ manaḥ
|
kaccicca śuddhabhāvo'si jātajñāno narādhipa||8||

9etaddhi tritayaṃ śreṣṭhaṃ sarvabhūteṣu bhārata
|
nirvairatā mahārāja satyamakrodha eva ca||9||

10kaccitte na ca moho'sti vanavāsena bhārata
|
svavaśe vanyamannaṃ vā upavāso'pi vā bhavet||10||

11viditaṃ cāpi rājendra vidurasya mahātmanaḥ
|
gamanaṃ vidhinā'nena dharmasya sumahātmanaḥ||11||

12māṇḍavyaśāpāddhi sa vai dharmo viduratāṃ gataḥ
|
mahābuddhirmahāyogī mahātmā sumahāmanāḥ||12||

13bṛhaspatirvā deveṣu śukro vāpyasureṣu ca
|
na tathā buddhisampanno yathā sa puruṣarṣabhaḥ||13||

14tapobalavyayaṃ kṛtvā sucirātsambhṛtaṃ tadā
|
māṇḍavyenarṣiṇā dharmo hyabhibhūtaḥ sanātanaḥ||14||

15niyogādbrahmaṇaḥ pūrve mayā svena balena ca
|
vaicitravīryake kṣetre jātaḥ sa sumahāmatiḥ||15||

16bhrātā tava mahārāja devadevaḥ sanātanaḥ
|
dhāraṇānmanasā dhyānādyaṃ dharmaṃ kavayo viduḥ||16||

17satyena saṃvardhayati yo damena śamena ca
|
ahiṃsayā ca dānena tapyamānaḥ sanātanaḥ||17||

18yena yogabalājjātaḥ kururājo yudhiṣṭhiraḥ
|
dharma ityeṣa nṛpate prājñenāmitabuddhinā||18||

19yathā vahniryathā vāyuryathā''paḥ pṛthivī yathā
|
yathā''kāśaṃ tathā dharma iha cāmutra ca sthitaḥ||19||

20sarvagaścaiva rājendra sarvaṃ vyāpya carācaram
|
dṛśyate devadevaiḥ sa siddhairnirmuktakalmaṣaiḥ||20||

21yo hi dharmaḥ sa viduro viduro yaḥ sa pāṇḍavaḥ
|
sa eṣa rājan dṛśyaste pāṇḍavaḥ preṣyavatsthitaḥ||21||

22praviṣṭaḥ sa mahātmānaṃ bhrātā te buddhisattamaḥ
|
dṛṣṭvā mahātmā kaunteyaṃ mahāyogabalānvitaḥ||22||

23tvāṃ cāpi śreyasā yokṣye na cirādbharatarṣabha
|
saṃśayacchedanārthāya prāptaṃ māṃ viddhi putrakā||23||

24na kṛtaṃ yaiḥ purā kaiścitkarma loke maharṣibhiḥ
|
āścaryabhūtaṃ tapasaḥ phalaṃ taddarśayāmi vaḥ||24||

25kimicchasi mahīpāla mattaḥ prāptumabhīpsitam
|
draṣṭuṃ spraṣṭumatha śrotuṃ tatkartā'smi tavānagha||25||
