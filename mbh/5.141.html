Mahābhārata 5.141 (Udyogaparvan)

1karṇa uvāca|
asaṃśayaṃ sauhṛdānme praṇayāccāttha keśava
|
sakhyena caiva vārṣṇeya śreyaskāmatayaiva ca ||1||

2sarvaṃ caivābhijānāmi pāṇḍoḥ putro'smi dharmataḥ
|
nigrahāddharmaśāstrāṇāṃ yathā tvaṃ kṛṣṇa manyase ||2||

3kanyā garbhaṃ samādhatta bhāskarānmāṃ janārdana
|
ādityavacanāccaiva jātaṃ māṃ sā vyasarjayat ||3||

4so'smi kṛṣṇa tathā jātaḥ pāṇḍoḥ putro'smi dharmataḥ
|
kuntyā tvahamapākīrṇo yathā na kuśalaṃ tathā ||4||

5sūto hi māmadhiratho dṛṣṭvaivābhyānayadgṛhān
|
rādhāyāścaiva māṃ prādāt sauhārdānmadhusūdana ||5||

6matsnehāccaiva rādhāyāṃ sadyaḥ kṣīramavātarat
|
sā me mūtraṃ purīṣaṃ ca pratijagrāha mādhava ||6||

7tasyāḥ piṇḍavyapanayaṃ kuryādasmadvidhaḥ katham
|
dharmaviddharmaśāstrāṇāṃ śravaṇe satataṃ rataḥ ||7||

8tathā māmabhijānāti sūtaścādhirathaḥ sutam
|
pitaraṃ cābhijānāmi tamahaṃ sauhṛdāt sadā ||8||

9sa hi me jātakarmādi kārayāmāsa mādhava
|
śāstradṛṣṭena vidhinā putraprītyā janārdana ||9||

10nāma vai vasuṣeṇeti kārayāmāsa vai dvijaiḥ
|
bhāryāścoḍhā mama prāpte yauvane tatparigrahāt ||10||

11tāsu putrāśca pautrāśca mama jātā janārdana
|
tāsu me hṛdayaṃ kṛṣṇa sañjātaṃ kāmabandhanam ||11||

12na pṛthivyā sakalayā na suvarṇasya rāśibhiḥ
|
harṣādbhayādvā govinda mithyā kartuṃ tadutsahe ||12||

13dhṛtarāṣṭrakule kṛṣṇa duryodhanasamāśrayāt
|
mayā trayodaśa samā bhuktaṃ rājyamakaṇṭakam ||13||

14iṣṭaṃ ca bahubhiryajñaiḥ saha sūtairmayā'sakṛt
|
āvāhāśca vivāhāśca saha sūtairmayā kṛtāḥ ||14||

15māṃ ca kṛṣṇa samāsādya kṛtaḥ śastrasamudyamaḥ
|
duryodhanena vārṣṇeya vigrahaścāpi pāṇḍavaiḥ ||15||

16tasmādraṇe dvairathe māṃ pratyudyātāramacyuta
|
vṛtavān paramaṃ kṛṣṇa pratīpaṃ savyasācinaḥ ||16||

17vadhādbandhādbhayādvā'pi lobhādvā'pi janārdana
|
anṛtaṃ notsahe kartuṃ dhārtarāṣṭrasya dhīmataḥ ||17||

18yadi hyadya na gaccheyaṃ dvairathaṃ savyasācinā
|
akīrtiḥ syāddhṛṣīkeśa mama pārthasya cobhayoḥ ||18||

19asaṃśayaṃ hitārthāya brūyāstvaṃ madhusūdana
|
sarvaṃ ca pāṇḍavāḥ kuryustvadvaśitvānna saṃśayaḥ ||19||

20mantrasya niyamaṃ kuryāstvamatra madhusūdana
|
etadatra hitaṃ manye sarvaṃ yādavanandana ||20||

21yadi jānāti māṃ rājā dharmātmā saṃyatendriyaḥ
|
kuntyāḥ prathamajaṃ putraṃ na sa rājyaṃ grahīṣyati ||21||

22prāpya cāpi mahadrājyaṃ tadahaṃ madhusūdana
|
sphītaṃ duryodhanāyaiva saṃpradadyāmarindama ||22||

23sa eva rājā dharmātmā śāśvato'stu yudhiṣṭhiraḥ
|
netā yasya hṛṣīkeśo yoddhā yasya dhanañjayaḥ ||23||

24pṛthivī tasya rāṣṭraṃ ca yasya bhīmo mahārathaḥ
|
nakulaḥ sahadevaśca draupadeyāśca mādhava ||24||

25dhṛṣṭadyumnaśca pāñcālyaḥ sātyakiśca mahārathaḥ
|
uttamaujā yudhāmanyuḥ satyadharmā ca saumakiḥ ||25||

26caidyaśca cekitānaśca śikhaṇḍī cāparājitaḥ
|
indragopakavarṇāśca kekayā bhrātarastathā
|
indrāyudhasavarṇaśca kuntibhojo mahāmanāḥ ||26||

27mātulo bhīmasenasya śyenajicca mahārathaḥ
|
śaṅkhaḥ putro virāṭasya nidhistvaṃ ca janārdana ||27||

28mahānayaṃ kṛṣṇa kṛtaḥ kṣatrasya samudānayaḥ
|
rājyaṃ prāptamidaṃ dīptaṃ prathitaṃ sarvarājasu ||28||

29dhārtarāṣṭrasya vārṣṇeya śastrayajño bhaviṣyati
|
asya yajñasya vettā tvaṃ bhaviṣyasi janārdana ||29||

30ādhvaryavaṃ ca te kṛṣṇa kratāvasmin bhaviṣyati
|
hotā caivātra bībhatsuḥ sannaddhaḥ sa kapidhvajaḥ ||30||

31gāṇḍīvaṃ sruk tathā cājyaṃ vīryaṃ puṃsāṃ bhaviṣyati
|
aindraṃ pāśupataṃ brāhmaṃ sthūṇākarṇaṃ ca mādhava
|
mantrāstatra bhaviṣyanti prayuktāḥ savyasācinā ||31||

32anuyātaśca pitaramadhiko vā parākrame
|
gītaṃ stotraṃ sa saubhadraḥ samyak tatra bhaviṣyati||32||

33udgātā'tra punarbhīmaḥ prastotā sumahābalaḥ
|
vinadan sa naravyāghro nāgānīkāntakṛdraṇe ||33||

34sa caiva tatra dharmātmā śaśvadrājā yudhiṣṭhiraḥ
|
japairhomaiśca saṃyukto brahmatvaṃ kārayiṣyati ||34||

35śaṅkhaśabdāḥ samurajā bheryaśca madhusūdana
|
utkṛṣṭasiṃhanādaśca subrahmaṇyo bhaviṣyati ||35||

36nakulaḥ sahadevaśca mādrīputrau yaśasvinau
|
śāmitraṃ tau mahāvīryau samyak tatra bhaviṣyataḥ ||36||

37kalmāṣadaṇḍā govinda vimalā rathapaṅktayaḥ
|
yūpāḥ samupakalpantāmasmin yajñe janārdana ||37||

38karṇinālīkanārācā vatsadantopabṛṃhaṇāḥ
|
tomarāḥ somakalaśāḥ pavitrāṇi dhanūṃṣi ca ||38||

39asayo'tra kapālāni puroḍāśāḥ śirāṃsi ca
|
havistu rudhiraṃ kṛṣṇa tasmin yajñe bhaviṣyati ||39||

40idhmāḥ paridhayaścaiva śaktayo vimalā gadāḥ
|
sadasyā droṇaśiṣyāśca kṛpasya ca śaradvataḥ ||40||

41iṣavo'tra paristomā muktā gāṇḍīvadhanvanā
|
mahārathaprayuktāśca droṇadrauṇipracoditāḥ ||41||

42pratiprāsthānikaṃ karma sātyakistu kariṣyati
|
dīkṣito dhārtarāṣṭro'tra patnī cāsya mahācamūḥ ||42||

43ghaṭotkaco'tra śāmitraṃ kariṣyati mahābalaḥ
|
atirātre mahābāho vitate yajñakarmaṇi ||43||

44dakṣiṇā tvasya yajñasya dhṛṣṭadyumnaḥ pratāpavān
|
vaitānike karmamukhe jāto yatkṛṣṇa pāvakāt ||44||

45yadabruvamahaṃ kṛṣṇa kaṭukāni sma pāṇḍavān
|
priyārthaṃ dhārtarāṣṭrasya tena tapye hyakarmaṇā ||45||

46yadā drakṣyasi māṃ kṛṣṇa nihataṃ savyasācinā
|
punaścittistadā cāsya yajñasyātha bhaviṣyati ||46||

47duḥśāsanasya rudhiraṃ yadā pāsyati pāṇḍavaḥ
|
ānardaṃ nardataḥ samyak tadā sūyaṃ bhaviṣyati ||47||

48yadā droṇaṃ ca bhīṣmaṃ ca pāñcālyau pātayiṣyataḥ
|
tadā yajñāvasānaṃ tadbhaviṣyati janārdana ||48||

49duryodhanaṃ yadā hantā bhīmaseno mahābalaḥ
|
tadā samāpsyate yajño dhārtarāṣṭrasya mādhava ||49||

50snuṣāśca prasnuṣāścaiva dhṛtarāṣṭrasya saṅgatāḥ
|
hateśvarā naṣṭaputrā hatanāthāśca keśava ||50||

51rudantyaḥ saha gāndhāryā śva-gṛdhra-kurarākule
|
sa yajñe'sminnavabhṛtho bhaviṣyati janārdana ||51||

52vidyāvṛddhā vayovṛddhāḥ kṣatriyāḥ kṣatriyarṣabha
|
vṛthā mṛtyuṃ na kurvīraṃstvatkṛte madhusūdana ||52||

53śastreṇa nidhanaṃ gacchet samṛddhaṃ kṣatramaṇḍalam
|
kurukṣetre puṇyatame trailokyasyāpi keśava ||53||

54tadatra puṇḍarīkākṣa vidhatsva yadabhīpsitam
|
yathā kārtsnyena vārṣṇeya kṣatraṃ svargamavāpnuyāt ||54||

55yāvat sthāsyanti girayaḥ saritaśca janārdana
|
tāvat kīrtibhavaḥ śabdaḥ śāśvato'yaṃ bhaviṣyati ||55||

56brāhmaṇāḥ kathayiṣyanti mahābhāratamāhavam
|
samāgameṣu vārṣṇeya kṣatriyāṇāṃ yaśodhanam ||56||

57samupānaya kaunteyaṃ yuddhāya mama keśava
|
mantrasaṃvaraṇaṃ kurvannityameva parantapa ||57||
