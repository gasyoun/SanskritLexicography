Mahābhārata 13.95 (Anuśāsanaparvan)

1yudhiṣṭhira uvāca|
yadidaṃ śrāddhakṛtyeṣu dīyate bharatarṣabha
|
chatraṃ copānahau caiva kenaitatsaṃpravartitam ||1||

2kathaṃ caitatsamutpannaṃ kimarthaṃ caiva dīyate
|
na kevalaṃ śrāddhakṛtye puṇyakeṣvapi dīyate ||2||

3bahuṣvapi nimitteṣu puṇyamāśritya dīyate
|
etadvistarato brahman śrotumicchāmi tattvataḥ ||3||

4bhīṣma uvāca|
śṛṇu rājannavahitaśchatropānahavistaram
|
yathaitatprathitaṃ loke yathā caitatpravartitam ||4||

5yathā cākṣayyatāṃ prāptaṃ puṇyatāṃ ca yathā gatam
|
sarvametadaśeṣeṇa pravakṣyāmi narādhipa ||5||

6jamadagneśca saṃvādaṃ sūryasya ca mahātmanaḥ
|
purā sa bhagavān sākṣāddha‌nuṣā'krīḍayatprabho ||6||

7saṃdhāya saṃdhāya śarāṃścikṣepa kila bhārgavaḥ
|
tān kṣiptān reṇukā sarvāṃstasyeṣūndīptatejasaḥ ||7||

8ānīya sā tadā tasmai prādādasakṛdacyuta
|
atha tena sa śabdena jyāyāścaiva śarasya ca ||8||

9prahṛṣṭaḥ saṃpracikṣepa sā ca pratyājahāra tān
|
tato madhyāhnamārūḍhe jyeṣṭhāmūle divākare ||9||

10sa sāyakāndvijo muktvā reṇukāmidamabravīt
|
gacchānaya viśālākṣi śarānetāndhanuścyutān ||10||

11yāvadetānpunaḥ subhru kṣipāmīti janādhipa
|
sā gacchantyantarā chāyāṃ vṛkṣamāśritya bhāminī ||11||

12tasthau tasyā hi santaptaṃ śiraḥ pādau tathaiva ca
|
sthitā sā tu muhūrtaṃ vai bhartuḥ śāpabhayācchubhā ||12||

13yayāvānayituṃ bhūyaḥ sāyakānasitekṣaṇā
|
pratyājagāma ca śarāṃstānādāya yaśasvinī ||13||

14sā vai khinnā sucārvaṅgī padbhyāṃ duḥkhaṃ niyacchatī
|
upā''jagāma bhartāraṃ bhayādbhartuḥ pravepatī ||14||

15sa tāmṛṣistadā kruddho vākyamāha śubhānanām
|
reṇuke kiṃ cireṇa tvamāgateti punaḥ punaḥ ||15||

16reṇukovāca
|
śirastāvatpradīptaṃ me pādau caiva tapodhana
|
suryatejoniruddhā'haṃ vṛkṣacchāyāṃ samāśritā ||16||

17etasmātkāraṇādbrahmaścirāyaitatkṛtaṃ mayā
|
etacchrutvā mama vibho mā krudhastvaṃ tapodhana ||17||

18jamada‌gniruvāca|
adyainaṃ dīptakiraṇaṃ reṇuke tava duḥkhadam
|
śarairnipātayiṣyāmi suryamastrāgnitejasā ||18||

19bhīṣma uvāca|
sa visphārya dhanurdivyaṃ gṛhītvā ca śarānbahūn
|
atiṣṭhatsūryamabhito yato yāti tatomukhaḥ ||19||

20atha taṃ prekṣya sannaddhaṃ sūryo'bhyetya tathā'bravīt
|
dvijarūpeṇa kaunteya kiṃ te sūryoparādhyate ||20||

21āda‌tte raśmibhiḥ sūryo divi tiṣṭhaṃstatastataḥ
|
rasaṃ hṛtaṃ vai varṣāsu pravarṣati divākaraḥ ||21||

22tato'nnaṃ jāyate vipra manuṣyāṇāṃ sukhāvaham
|
annaṃ prāṇā iti yathā vedeṣu paripaṭhyate ||22||

23athābhreṣu nigūḍhaśca raśmibhiḥ parivāritaḥ
|
saptadvīpānimānbrahmanvarṣeṇābhipravarṣati ||23||

24tatastadauṣadhīnāṃ ca vīrudhāṃ puṣpapatrajam
|
sarvaṃ varṣābhinirvṛttamannaṃ saṃbhavati prabho ||24||

25jātakarmāṇi sarvāṇi vratopanayanāni ca
|
godānāni vivāhāśca tathā yajñasamṛddhayaḥ ||25||

26śāstrāṇi dānāni tathā saṃyogā vittasaṃcayāḥ
|
annataḥ saṃpravartante yathā tvaṃ vettha bhārgava ||26||

27ramaṇīyāni yāvanti yāvadāraṃbhikāṇi ca
|
sarvamannātprabhavati viditaṃ kīrtayāmi te ||27||

28sarvaṃ hi vettha vipra tvaṃ yadetatkīrtitaṃ mayā
|
prasādaye tvāṃ viprarṣe kiṃ te sūryaṃ nipātya vai ||28||
