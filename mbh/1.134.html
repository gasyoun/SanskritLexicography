Mahābhārata 1.134 (Ādiparvan)

1vaiśaṃpāyana uvāca|
kṛtāstrāndhārtarāṣṭrāṃśca pāṇḍuputrāṃśca bhārata
|
dṛṣṭvā droṇo'bravīdrājandhṛtarāṣṭraṃ janeśvaram||1||

2kṛpasya somadattasya bāhlīkasya ca dhīmataḥ
|
gāṅgeyasya ca sānnidhye vyāsasya vidurasya ca||2||

3rājansaṃprāptavidyāste kumārāḥ kurusattama
|
te darśayeyuḥ svāṃ śikṣāṃ rājannanumate tava|| 3||

4tato'bravīnmahārājaḥ prahṛṣṭenāntarātmanā
|
dhṛtarāṣṭra uvāca|
bhāradvāja mahatkarma kṛtaṃ te dvijasattama||4||

5yadānumanyase kālaṃ yasmindeśe yathāyathā
|
tathā tathā vidhānāya svayamājñāpayasva mām||5||

6spṛhayāmyadya nirvedānpuruṣāṇāṃ sacakṣuṣām
|
astrahetoḥ parākrāntānye me drakṣyanti putrakān||6||

7kṣattaryadgururācāryo bravīti kuru tattathā
|
na hīdṛśaṃ priyaṃ manye bhavitā dharmavatsala||7||

8tato rājānamāmantrya nirgato viduro bahiḥ
|
bhāradvājo mahāprājño māpayāmāsa medinīm||8||

9samāmavṛkṣāṃ nirgulmāmudakprasravaṇānvitām
|
tasyāṃ bhūmau baliṃ cakre tithau nakṣatrapūjite||9||

10avaghuṣṭe samāje ca tadarthaṃ vadatāṃ varaḥ
|
raṅgabhūmau suvipulaṃ śāstradṛṣṭaṃ yathāvidhi||10||

11prekṣāgāraṃ suvihitaṃ cakraste tasya śilpinaḥ
|
rakṣāṃ sarvāyudhopetāṃ strīṇāṃ caiva nararṣabha||11||

12mañcāṃśca kārayāmāsuryatra jānapadā janāḥ
|
vipulānucchrayopetāśin bikāśca mahādhanāḥ||12||

13tasmiṃstato'hani prāpte rājā sasacivastadā
|
bhīṣmaṃ pramukhataḥ kṛtvā kṛpaṃ cācāryasattamam||13||

14muktājālaparikṣiptaṃ vaidūryamamiśobhitam
|
śātakumbhamayaṃ divyaṃ prekṣāgāramupāgamat||14||

15gāndhārī ca mahābhāgā kuntī ca jayatāṃ vara
|
striyaśca rājñaḥ sarvāstāḥ sapreṣyāḥ saparicchadāḥ||15||

16harṣādāruruhurmañcānmeruṃ devastriyo yathā
|
brāhmaṇakṣatriyādyaṃ ca cāturvarṇyaṃ purāddrutam||16||

17darśanepsuḥ samabhyāgātkumārāṇāṃ kṛtāstratām
|
kṣaṇenaikasthatāṃ tatra darśanepsurjagāma ha||17||

18pravāditaiśca vāditrairjanakautūhalena ca
|
mahārṇava iva kṣubdhaḥ samājaḥ so'bhavattadā||18||

19tataḥ śuklāmbaradharaḥ śuklayajñopavītavān
|
śuklakeśaḥ sitaśmaśruḥ śuklālyānulepanaḥ||19||

20raṅgamadhyaṃ tadācāryaḥ saputraḥ praviveśa ha
|
nabho jaladharairhīnaṃ sāṅgāraka ivāṃśumān||20||

21sa yathā samayaṃ cakre baliṃ balavatāṃ varaḥ
|
brāhmaṇāṃstu sumantrajñānkārayāmāsa maṅgalam||21||

22sukhapuṇyāhaghoṣasya puṇyasya samanantaram
|
viviśurvividhaṃ gṛhya śastropakaraṇaṃ narāḥ||22||

23tato baddhāṅgulitrāṇā baddhakakṣyā mahārathāḥ
|
baddhatūṇāḥ sadhanuṣo viviśurbharatarṣabhāḥ||23||

24anujyeṣṭhaṃ ca te tatra yudhiṣṭhirapunarogamāḥ
|
cakrurastraṃ mahāvīryāḥ kumārāḥ paramādbhutam||24||

25keciccharākṣepabhayācchirāṃsyavananāmire
|
manujā dhṛṣṭamapare vīkṣāñcakruḥ suvismitāḥ||25||

26te sma lakṣyāṇi bibhidurbāṇairnāmāṅkaśobhitaiḥ
|
vividhairlāghavotsṛṣṭairuhyanto vājibhirdrutam||26||

27tatkumārabalaṃ tatra gṛhītaśarakārmukam
|
gandharvanagarākāraṃ prekṣya te vismitā'bhavan||27||

28sahasā cukruśuścānye narāḥ śatasahasraśaḥ
|
vismayotphullanayanāḥ sādhu sādhviti bhārata||28||

29kṛtvā dhanuṣi te mārgānrathacaryāsu cāsakṛt
|
gajapṛṣṭhe'śvapṛṣṭhe ca niyuddhe ca mahābalāḥ||29||

30gṛhītakhaḍgacarmāṇastato bhūyaḥ prahāriṇaḥ
|
tsarumārgānyathoddiṣṭāṃśceruḥ sarvāsu bhūmiṣu||30||

31lāghavaṃ sauṣṭhavaṃ śobhāṃ sthiratvaṃ dṛḍhamuṣṭitām
|
dadṛśustatra sarveṣāṃ prayogaṃ khaḍgacarmaṇoḥ||31||

32atha tau nityasaṃhṛṣṭau suyodhanavṛkodarau
|
avatīrṇau gadāhastāvekaśṛṅgāvivācalau||32||

33baddhakakṣyau mahābāhū pauruṣe paryavasthitau
|
bṛṃhantau vāsitāhetoḥ samadāviva kuñjarau||33||

34tau pradakṣiṇasavyāni maṇḍalāni mahābalau
|
ceraturmaṇḍalagatau samadāviva kuñjarau||34||

35viduro dhṛtarāṣṭrāya gāndhāryāḥ pāṇḍavāraṇiḥ
|
nyavedayetāṃ tatsarvaṃ kumārāṇāṃ viceṣṭitam||35||
