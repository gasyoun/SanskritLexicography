Mahābhārata 5.161 (Udyogaparvan)

1sañjaya uvāca|
senāniveśaṃ saṃprāptaḥ kaitavyaḥ pāṇḍavasya ha
|
samāgataḥ pāṇḍaveyairyudhiṣṭhiramabhāṣata ||1||

2abhijño dūtavākyānāṃ yathoktaṃ dhruvato mama
|
duryodhanasamādeśaṃ śrutvā na kroddhumarhasi ||2||

3yudhiṣṭhira uvāca|
ulūka na bhayaṃ te'sti brūhi tvaṃ vigatajvaraḥ
|
yanmataṃ dhārtarāṣṭrasya lubdhasyādīrghadarśinaḥ ||3||

4tato dyutimatāṃ madhye pāṇḍavānāṃ mahātmanām
|
sṛñjayānāṃ ca matsyānāṃ kṛṣṇasya ca yaśasvinaḥ ||4||

5drupadasya saputrasya virāṭasya ca sannidhau
|
bhūmipānāṃ ca sarveṣāṃ madhye vākyaṃ jagāda ha ||5||

6ulūka uvāca|
idaṃ tvāmabravīdrājā dhārtarāṣṭro mahāmanāḥ
|
śṛṇvatāṃ kuruvīrāṇāṃ tannibodha yudhiṣṭhira ||6||

7parājito'si dyūtena kṛṣṇā cānāyitā sabhām
|
śakyo'marṣo manuṣyeṇa kartuṃ puruṣamāninā ||7||

8dvādaśaiva tu varṣāṇi vane dhiṣṇyādvivāsitaḥ
|
saṃvatsaraṃ virāṭasya dāsyamāsthāya coṣitaḥ ||8||

9amarṣaṃ rājyaharaṇaṃ vanavāsaṃ ca pāṇḍava
|
draupadyāśca parikleśaṃ saṃsmaran puruṣo bhava ||9||

10aśaktena ca yacchaptaṃ bhīmasenena pāṇḍava
|
duḥśāsanasya rudhiraṃ pīyatāṃ yadi śakyate ||10||

11lohābhisāro nirvṛttaḥ kurukṣetramakardamam
|
samaḥ panthā bhṛtāste'śvāḥ śvo yudhyasva sakeśavaḥ ||11||

12asamāgamya bhīṣmeṇa saṃyuge kiṃ vikatthase
|
ārurukṣuryathā mandaḥ parvataṃ gandhamādanam ||12||

13evaṃ katthasi kaunteya akatthan puruṣo bhava
|
sūtaputraṃ sudurdharṣaṃ śalyaṃ ca balināṃ varam ||13||

14droṇaṃ ca balināṃ śreṣṭhaṃ śacīpatisamaṃ yudhi
|
ajitvā saṃyuge pārtha rājyaṃ kathamihecchasi ||14||

15brāhme dhanuṣi cācāryaṃ veda‌yorantagaṃ dvayoḥ
|
yudhi dhuryamavikṣobhyamanīkacaramacyutam ||15||

16droṇaṃ mahādyutiṃ pārtha jetumicchasi tanmṛṣā
|
na hi śuśruma vātena merumunmathitaṃ girim ||16||

17anilo vā vahenmeruṃ dyaurvā'pi nipatenmahīm
|
yugaṃ vā parivarteta yadyevaṃ syādyathā''ttha mām ||17||

18ko hyasti jīvitākāṅkṣī prāpyemamarimardanam
|
gajo vājī ratho vā'pi punaḥ svasti gṛhānvrajet ||18||

19kathamābhyāmabhidhyātaḥ saṃspṛṣṭo dāruṇena vā
|
raṇe jīvan vimucyeta padā bhūmimupaspṛśan ||19||

20kiṃ darduraḥ kūpaśayo yathemāṃ na budhyase rājacamūṃ sametām
|
durādharṣāṃ devacamūprakāśāṃ guptāṃ narendraistridaśairiva dyām ||20||

21prācyaiḥ pratīcyairatha dākṣiṇātyairudīcyakāmbojaśakaiḥ khaśaiśca
|
śālvaiḥ samatsyaiḥ kurumukhyadeśyairmlecchaiḥ pulindairdraviḍāndhrakāñcyaiḥ ||21||

22nānājanaughaṃ yudhi saṃpravṛddhaṃ gāṅgaṃ yathā vegamapāraṇīyam
|
māṃ ca sthitaṃ nāgabalasya madhye yuyutsase manda kimalpabuddhe ||22||

23ityevamuktvā rājānaṃ dharmaputraṃ yudhiṣṭhiram
|
abhyāvṛtya punarjipṇumulūkaḥ pratyabhāṣata ||23||

24akatthamāno yudhyasva katthase'rjuna kiṃ bahu
|
paryāyātsiddhiretasya naitatsidhyati katthanāt ||24||

25yadīdaṃ katthanālloke sidhyet karma dhanañjaya
|
sarve bhaveyuḥ siddhārthāḥ katthane ko hi durgataḥ ||25||

26jānāmi te vāsudevaṃ sahāyaṃ jānāmi te gāṇḍivaṃ tālamātram
|
jānāmyetat tvādṛśo nāsti yoddhā jānānaste rājyametaddharāmi ||26||

27na tu paryāyadharmeṇa rājyaṃ prāpnoti mānuṣaḥ
|
manasaivānukūlāni vidhātā kurute vaśe ||27||

28trayodaśa samā bhuktaṃ rājyaṃ vilapatastava
|
bhūyaścaiva praśāsiṣye nihatya tvāṃ sabāndhavam ||28||

29kva tadā gāṇḍivaṃ te'bhūdyattvaṃ dāsa paṇairjitaḥ
|
kva tadā bhīmasenasya balamāsīcca phālguna ||29||

30sagadādbhīmasenādvā pārthādvā'pi sagāṇḍivāt
|
na vai mokṣastadā vo'bhūdvinā kṛṣṇāmaninditām ||30||

31sā vo dāsye samāpannān mokṣayāmāsa pārṣatī
|
amānuṣyaṃ samāpannān dāsakarmaṇyavasthitān ||31||

32avocaṃ yat ṣaṇḍhatilānahaṃ vastathyameva tat
|
dhṛtā hi veṇī pārthena virāṭanagare tadā ||32||

33sūdakarmaṇi ca śrāntaṃ virāṭasya mahānase
|
bhīmasenena kaunteya yacca tanmama pauruṣam ||33||

34evametat sadā daṇḍaṃ kṣatriyāḥ kṣatriye dadhuḥ
|
veṇīṃ kṛtvā ṣaṇḍhaveṣaḥ kanyāṃ nartitavānasi ||34||

35na bhayādvāsudevasya na cāpi tava phālguna
|
rājyaṃ prati pradāsyāmi yuddhyasva sahakeśavaḥ ||35||

36na māyā hīndrajālaṃ vā kuhakā vā'pi bhīṣaṇā
|
āttaśastrasya me yuddhe vahanti pratigarjanāḥ ||36||

37vāsudevasahasraṃ vā phālgunānāṃ śatāni vā
|
āsādya māmamogheṣuṃ draviṣyanti diśo daśa ||37||

38saṃyugaṃ gaccha bhīṣmeṇa bhindhi vā śirasā girim
|
taremaṃ vā mahāgādhaṃ bāhubhyāṃ puruṣodadhim ||38||

39śāradvatamahāmīnaṃ viviṃśatimahoragam
|
bṛhadbalamahodvelaṃ saumadattitimiṅgilam ||39||

40bhīṣmavegamaparyantaṃ droṇagrāhadurāsadam
|
karṇaśalyajhaṣāvartaṃ kāmbojavaḍavāmukham ||40||

41duḥśāsanaughaṃ śala-śalyamatsyaṃ suṣeṇa-citrāyudhanāga-nakram
|
jayadrathādriṃ purumitragādhaṃ durmarṣaṇodaṃ śakuniprapātam ||41||

42śastraughamakṣayyamatipravṛddhaṃ yadā'vagāhya śramanaṣṭacetāḥ
|
bhaviṣyasi tvaṃ hatasarvabāndhavastadā manaste paritāpameṣyati ||42||

43tadā manaste tridivādivāśucernivartitā pārtha mahīpraśāsanāt
|
praśāmya rājyaṃ hi sudurlabhaṃ tvayā bubhūṣitaḥ svarga ivātapasvinā ||43||
