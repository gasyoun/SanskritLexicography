Mahābhārata 12.228 (Śāntiparvan)

1yudhiṣṭhira uvāca|
pūrvarūpāṇi me rājan puruṣasya bhaviṣyataḥ
|
parābhaviṣyataścaiva tanme brūhi pitāmaha ||1||

2bhīṣma uvāca|
mana eva manuṣyasya pūrvarūpāṇi śaṃsati
|
bhaviṣyataśca bhadraṃ te tathaiva na bhaviṣyataḥ ||2||

3atrāpyudāharantīmamitihāsaṃ purātanam
|
śriyā śakrasya saṃvādaṃ taṃ nibodha yudhiṣṭhira ||3||

4mahatastapaso vyuṣṭyā paśya~llokau parāvarau
|
sāmānyamṛṣibhirgatvā brahmalokanivāsibhiḥ ||4||

5brahmevāmitadīptaujāḥ śāntapāpmā mahātapāḥ
|
vicacāra yathākāmaṃ triṣu lokeṣu nāradaḥ ||5||

6kadācitprātarutthāya pispṛkṣuḥ salilaṃ śuci
|
dhruvadvārabhavāṃ gaṅgāṃ jagāmāvatatāra ca ||6||

7sahasranayanaścāpi vajrī śambarapākahā
|
tasyā devarṣijuṣṭāyāstīramabhyājagāma ha ||7||

8tāvāplutya yatātmānau kṛtajapyau samāsataḥ
|
nadyāḥ pulinamāsādya sūkṣmakāñcanavālukam ||8||

9puṇyakarmabhirākhyātā devarṣikathitāḥ kathāḥ
|
cakratustau tathā''sīnau maharṣikathitāstathā ||9||

10pūrvavṛttavyapetāni kathayantau samāhitau
|
atha bhāskaramudyantaṃ raśmijālapuraskṛtam ||10||

11pūrṇamaṇḍalamālokya tāvutthāyopatasthatuḥ
|
abhitastūdayantaṃ tamarkamarkamivāparam ||11||

12ākāśe dadṛśe jyotirudyatārciḥ samaprabham
|
tayoḥ samīyaṃ taṃ prāptaṃ pratyadṛśyata bhārata ||12||

13tatsuparṇārkacaritamāsthitaṃ vaiṣṇavaṃ padam
|
bhābhirapratimaṃ bhāti trailokyamavabhāsayat ||13||

14tatrābhirūpaśobhābhirapsarobhiḥ puraskṛtām
|
bṛhatīmaṃśumatprakhyāṃbṛhadbhānorivārciṣam ||14||

15nakṣatrakalpābharaṇāṃ tāṃ mauktikasamasrajam
|
śriyaṃ dadṛśatuḥ padmāṃ sākṣātpadmadalasthitām ||15||

16sā'varuhya vimānāgrādaṅganānāmanuttamā
|
abhyāgacchattrilokeśaṃ devarṣiṃ cāpi nāradam ||16||

17nāradānugataḥ sākṣānmaghavāṃstāmupāgamat
|
kṛtāñjalipuṭo devīṃ nivedyātmānamātmanā ||17||

18cakre cānupamāṃ pūjāṃ tasyāścāpi sa sarvavit
|
devarājaḥ śriyaṃ rājan vākyaṃ cedamuvāca ha ||18||

19śakra uvāca |
kā tvaṃ kena ca kāryeṇa samprāptā cāruhāsini
|
kutaścāgamyate subhru gantavyaṃ kva ca te śubhe ||19||

20śrīruvāca |
puṇyeṣu triṣu lokeṣu sarve sthāvarajaṅgamāḥ
|
mamātmabhāvamicchanto yatante paramātmanā ||20||

21sā'haṃ vai paṅkaje jātā sūryaraśmivibodhite
|
bhūtyarthaṃ sarvabhūtānāṃ padmā śrīḥ padmamālinī ||21||

22ahaṃ lakṣmīrahaṃ bhūtiḥ śrīścāhaṃ balasūdana
|
ahaṃ śraddhā ca medhā ca sannatirvijitiḥ sthitiḥ ||22||

23ahaṃ dhṛtirahaṃ siddhirahaṃ tvidbhūtireva ca
|
ahaṃ svāhā svadhā caiva sannatirniyatiḥ smṛtiḥ ||23||

24rājñāṃ vijayamānānāṃ senāgreṣu dhvajeṣu ca
|
nivāse dharmaśīlānāṃ viṣayeṣu pureṣu ca ||24||

25jitakāśini śūre ca saṃgrāmeṣvanivartini
|
nivasāmi manuṣyendre sadaiva balasūdana ||25||

26dharmanitye mahābuddhau brahmaṇye satyavādini
|
praśrite dānaśīle ca sadaiva nivasāmyaham ||26||

27asureṣvavasaṃ pūrvaṃ satyadharmanibandhanā
|
viparītāṃstu tān buddhvā tvayi vāsamarocayam ||27||

28śakra uvāca |
kathaṃ vṛtteṣu daityeṣu tvamavātsīrvarānane
|
dṛṣṭvā ca kimihāgāstvaṃ hitvā daiteyadānavān ||28||

29śrīruvāca| svadharmamanutiṣṭhatsu dhairyādacaliteṣu ca
|
svargamārgābhirāmeṣu sattveṣu niratā hyaham ||29||

30dānādhyayanayajñejyāpitṛdaivatapūjanam
|
gurūṇāmatithīnāṃ ca teṣāṃ satyamavartata ||30||

31susammṛṣṭaguhāścāsan jitastrīkā hutāgnayaḥ
|
guruśuśrūṣakā dāntā brahmaṇyāḥ satyavādinaḥ ||31||

32śraddadhānā jitakrodhā dānaśīlā'nasūyavaḥ
|
bhṛtaputrā bhṛtāmātyā bhṛtadārā hyanīrṣavaḥ ||32||

33amarṣeṇa na cānyonyaṃ spṛhayante kadācana
|
na ca jātūpatapyanti dhīrāḥ parasamṛddhibhiḥ ||33||

34dātāraḥ saṃgṛhītāra āryāḥ karuṇavedinaḥ
|
mahāprasādā ṛjavo dṛḍhabhaktā jitendriyāḥ||34||

35saṃtuṣṭabhṛtyasacivāḥ kṛtajñāḥ priyavādinaḥ
|
yathārhamānārthakarā hrīniṣevā yatavratāḥ ||35||

36nityaṃ parvasu susnātāḥ svanuliptāḥ svalaṃkṛtāḥ
|
upavāsatapaḥśīlāḥ pratītā brahmavādinaḥ ||36||

37nainānabhyudiyātsūryo na cāpyāsan prageśayāḥ
|
rātrau dadhi ca saktūṃśca nityameva vyavarjayan ||37||

38kalyaṃ ghṛtaṃ cānvavekṣanprayatā brahmavādinaḥ
|
maṅgalyānyapi cāpaśyan brāhmaṇāṃścāpyapūjayan ||38||

39sadā hi vadatāṃ dharmaṃ sadā cāpratigṛhṇatām
|
ardhaṃ ca rātryāḥ svapatāṃ divā cāsvapatāṃ tathā ||39||

40kṛpaṇānāthavṛddhānāṃ durbalāturayoṣitām
|
dayāṃ ca saṃvibhāgaṃ ca nityamevānvamodatām ||40||

41trastaṃ viṣaṇṇamudvignaṃ bhayārtaṃ vyādhitaṃ kṛśam
|
hatasvaṃ vyasanārtaṃ ca nityamāśvāsayanti te ||41||

42dharmamevānvavartanta na hiṃsanti parasparam
|
anukūlāśca kāryeṣu guruvṛddhopasevinaḥ ||42||

43pitṝndevātithīṃścaiva yathāvatte'bhyapūjayan
|
avaśeṣāṇi cāśnanti nityaṃ satyatapodhṛtāḥ ||43||

44naike'śnanti susampannaṃ na gacchanti parastriyam
|
sarvabhūteṣvavartanta yathā''tmani dayāṃ prati ||44||

45naivākāśe na paśuṣu viyonau ca na parvasu
|
indriyasya visargaṃ te rocayanti kadācana ||45||

46nityaṃ dānaṃ tathā dākṣyamārjavaṃ caiva nityadā
|
utsāho'thānahaṃkāraḥ paramaṃ sauhṛdaṃ kṣamā ||46||

47satyaṃ dānaṃ tapaḥ śaucaṃ kāruṇyaṃ vāganiṣṭhurā
|
mitreṣu cānabhidrohaḥ sarvaṃ teṣvabhavatprabho ||47||

48nidrā tandrīrasamprītirasūyāthānavekṣitā
|
aratiśca viṣādaśca spṛhā cāpyaviśanna tān ||48||

49sā'hamevaṃguṇeṣveva dānaveṣvavasaṃ purā
|
prajāsargamupādāya naikaṃ yugaviparyayam ||49||

50tataḥ kālaviparyāse teṣāṃ guṇaviparyayāt
|
apaśyaṃ nirgataṃ dharmaṃ kāmakrodhavaśātmanām ||50||

51sabhāsadāṃ ca vṛddhānāṃ satāṃ kathayatāṃ kathāḥ
|
prāhasannabhyasūyaṃśca sarvavṛddhānguṇāvarāḥ ||51||

52yuvānaśca samāsīnā vṛddhānapi gatānsataḥ
|
nābhyutthānābhivādābhyāṃ yathāpūrvamapūjayan ||52||

53vartayatyeva pitari putraḥ prabhavate tathā
|
abhṛtya bhṛtyatāṃ prāpya khyāpayantyanapatrapāḥ ||53||

54tathā dharmādapetena karmaṇā garhitena ye
|
mahataḥ prāpnuvantyarthāṃsteṣāṃ tatrābhavatspṛhā ||54||

55uccaiścābhyavadan rātrau nīcaistatrāgnirajvalat
|
putrāḥ pitṝnatyacarannāryaścātyacaran patīn ||55||

56mātaraṃ pitaraṃ vṛddhamācāryamatithiṃ gurum
|
gurutvānnābhyanandanta kumārānnānvapālayan ||56||

57bhikṣāṃ balimadattvā ca svayamannāni bhuñjate
|
aniṣṭvā'saṃvibhajyātha pitṛdevātithīn gurūn ||57||

58na śaucamanuruddhyanta teṣāṃ sūdajanāstathā
|
manasā karmaṇā vācā bhakṣyamāsīdanāvṛtam ||58||

59viprakīrṇāni dhānyāni kākamūṣikabhojanam
|
apāvṛtaṃ payo'tiṣṭhaducchiṣṭāścāspṛśan ghṛtam ||59||

60kuddālaṃ dātrapiṭakaṃ prakīrṇaṃ kāṃsyabhājanam
|
dravyopakaraṇaṃ sarvaṃ nānvavaikṣatkuṭumbinī ||60||

61prākārāgāravidhvaṃsānna sma te pratikurvate
|
nādriyante paśūnbaddhvā yavasenodakena ca ||61||

62bālānāṃ prekṣamāṇānāṃ svayaṃ bhakṣyamabhakṣayan
|
tathā bhṛtyajanaṃ sarvamasaṃtarpya ca dānavāḥ ||62||

63pāyasaṃ kṛsaraṃ māṃsamapūpānatha śaṣkulīḥ
|
apācayannātmano'rthe vṛthāmāṃsānyabhakṣayan ||63||

64utsūryaśāyinaścāsan sarve cāsan prageniśāḥ
|
avartankalahāścātra divārātraṃ gṛhe gṛhe ||64||

65anāryāścāryamāsīnaṃ paryupāsanna tatra ha
|
āśramasthān vidharmasthāḥ prādviṣanta parasparam ||65||

66saṃkarāścābhyavartanta na ca śaucamavartata
|
ye ca vedavido viprā vispaṣṭamanṛcaśca ye ||66||

67nirantaraviśeṣāste bahumānāvamānayoḥ
|
hāramābharaṇaṃ veṣaṃ gataṃ sthitamavekṣitam ||67||

68asevanta bhujiṣyā vai durjanācaritaṃ vidhim
|
striyaḥ puruṣaveṣeṇa puṃsaḥ strīveṣadhāriṇaḥ ||68||

69krīḍārativihāreṣu parāṃ mudamavāpnuvan
|
prabhavadbhiḥ purā dāyānahebhyaḥ pratiyāditān ||69||

70nābhyavartanta nāstikyādvartantaḥ sambhaveṣvapi
|
mitreṇābhyarthitaṃ mitramarthasaṃśayite kvacit ||70||

71bālakoṭyagramātreṇa svārthenāghnata tadvasu
|
parasvādānarucayo vipaṇavyavahāriṇaḥ ||71||

72adṛśyantāryavarṇeṣu śūdrāścāpi tapodhanāḥ
|
adhīyante'vratāḥ kecidvṛthā vratamathāpare ||72||

73aśuśrūṣurguroḥ śiṣyaḥ kaścicchiṣyasakho guruḥ
|
pitā caiva janitrī ca śrāntau vṛttasotsavāviva ||73||

74aprabhutve sthitau vṛddhāvannaṃ prārthayataḥ sutān
|
tatra vedavidaḥ prājñā gāmbhīrye sāgaropamāḥ ||74||

75kṛpyādiṣvabhavansaktā mūrkhāḥ śrāddhānyabhuñjata
|
prāḥ prātaśca supraśnaṃ kalpanaṃ preṣaṇakriyāḥ ||75||

76śiṣyānaprahitāsteṣāmakurvan guravaḥ svayam
|
śvaśrūśvaśurayoragre vadhūḥ preṣyānaśāsata ||76||

77anvaśāsacca bhartāraṃ samāhvāyābhijalpati
|
prayatnenāpi cārakṣaccitaṃ putrasya vai pitā ||77||

78vyabhajaccāpi saṃrambhādduḥkhavāsaṃ tathā'vasat
|
agnidāhena corairvā rājabhirvā hṛtaṃ dhanam ||78||

79dṛṣṭvā dveṣātprāhasanta suhṛtsambhāvitā hyapi
|
kṛtaghnā nāstikāḥ pāpā gurudārābhimarśinaḥ ||79||

80abhakṣyabhakṣaṇaratā nirmaryādā hatatviṣaḥ
|
teṣvevamādīnācārānācaratsu viparyaye ||80||

81nāhaṃ devendra vatsyāmi dānaveṣviti me matiḥ
|
tanmāṃ svayamanuprāptāmabhinanda śacīpate ||81||

82tvayā'rcitāṃ māṃ deveśa puro dhāsyanti devatāḥ
|
yatrāhaṃ tatra matkāntā madviśiṣṭā madarpaṇāḥ ||82||

83sapta devyo jayāṣṭamyo vāsameṣyanti te'ṣṭadhā
|
āśā śraddhā dhṛtiḥ kṣāntirvijitiḥ sannatiḥ kṣamā ||83||

84aṣṭamī vṛttiretāsāṃ purogā pākaśāsana
|
tāścāhaṃ cāsurāṃstyaktvā yuṣmadviṣayamāgatāḥ ||84||

85tridaśeṣu nivatsyāmo dharmaniṣṭhāntarātmasu
|
ityuktavacanāṃ devīṃ prītyarthaṃ ca nanandatuḥ ||85||

86nāradaścātra devarṣirvṛtrahantā ca vāsavaḥ
|
tato'nalasakho vāyuḥ pravavau devavartmasu ||86||

87iṣṭagandhaḥ sukhasparśaḥ sarvendriyasukhāvahaḥ
|
śucau vā'bhyarthite deśe tridaśāḥ prāyaśaḥ sthitāḥ ||87||

88lakṣmīsahitamāsīnaṃ maghavantaṃ didṛkṣavaḥ ||88||

89tato divaṃ prāpya sahasralocanaḥ striyopapannaḥ suhṛdā maharṣiṇā
|
rathena haryaśvayujā surarṣabhaḥ sadaḥ surāṇāmabhisatkṛto yayau ||89||

90atheṅgitaṃ vajradharasya nāradaḥ śriyaśca devyā manasā vicārayan
|
śriyai śaśaṃsāmaradṛṣṭapauruṣaḥ śivena tatrāgamanaṃ maharṣibhiḥ ||90||

91tato'mṛtaṃ dyauḥ pravavarṣa bhāsvatī pitāmahasyāyatane svayambhuvaḥ
|
anāhatā dundubhayo'tha nedire tathā prasannāva diśaścakāśire ||91||

92yathartu sasyeṣu vavarṣa vāsavo na dharmamārgādvicacāla kaścana
|
anekaratnākarabhūṣaṇā ca bhūḥ sughoṣaghoṣā bhuvanaukasāṃ jaye ||92||

93kriyābhirāmā manujā manasvino babhuḥ śubhe puṇyakṛtāṃ pathi sthitāḥ
|
narāmarāḥ kinnarayakṣarākṣasāḥ samṛddhimantaḥ sumanasvino'bhavan ||93||

94na jātvakāle kusumaṃ kutaḥ phalaṃ papāta vṛkṣātpavaneritādapi
|
rasapradāḥ kāmadughāśca dhevano na dāruṇā vāgvicacāra kasyacit ||94||

95imāṃ saparyāṃ saha sarvakāmadaiḥ śriyaśca śakrapramukhaiśca daivataiḥ
|
paṭhanti ye viprasadaḥsamāgatāḥ samṛddhakāmāḥ śriyamāpnuvanti te ||95||

96tvayā kurūṇāṃ vara yatpracoditaṃ bhavābhavasyeha paraṃ nidarśanam
|
tadadya sarvaṃ parikīrtitaṃ mayā parīkṣya tattvaṃ parigantumarhasi ||96||
