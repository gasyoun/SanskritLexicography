Mahābhārata 7.32 (Droṇaparvan)

1sañjaya uvāca|
pratighātaṃ tu sainyasya nāmṛṣyata vṛkodaraḥ
|
so'bhyāhanadguruṃ ṣaṣṭyā karṇaṃ ca daśabhiḥ śaraiḥ||1||

2tasya droṇaḥ śitairbāṇaistīkṣṇadhārairajihmagaiḥ
|
jīvitāntamabhiprepsurmarmāṇyāśu jaghāna ha||2||

3ānantaryamabhiprepsuḥ ṣaḍviṃśatyā samārpayat
|
karṇo dvādaśabhirbāṇairaśvatthāmā ca saptabhiḥ||3||

4ṣaḍbhirduryodhano rājā tata enamathākirat
|
bhīmaseno'pi tān sarvān pratyavidhyanmahābalaḥ||4||

5droṇaṃ pañcāśateṣūṇāṃ karṇaṃ ca daśabhiḥ śaraiḥ
|
duryodhanaṃ dvādaśabhirdrauṇimaṣṭābhirāśugaiḥ||5||

6ārāvaṃ tumulaṃ kurvannabhyavartata tān raṇe
|
tasmin saṃtyajati prāṇān mṛtyusādhāraṇīkṛte||6||

7ajātaśatrustān yodhān bhīmaṃ trātetyacodayat
|
te yayurbhīmasenasya samīpamamitaujasaḥ||7||

8yuyudhānaprabhṛtayo mādrīputrau ca pāṇḍavau
|
te sametya susaṃrabdhāḥ sahitāḥ puruṣarṣabhāḥ||8||

9maheṣvāsavarairguptā droṇānīkaṃ bibhitsavaḥ
|
samāpeturmahāvīryā bhīmaprabhṛtayo rathāḥ||9||

10tān pratyagṛhṇādavyagro droṇo'pi rathināṃ varaḥ
|
mahārathānatibalān vīrān samarayodhinaḥ||10||

11bāhyaṃ mṛtyubhayaṃ kṛtvā tāvakān pāṇḍavā yayuḥ
|
sādinaḥ sādino'bhyaghnaṃstathaiva rathino rathān||11||

12āsīcchaktyasisaṃpāto yuddhamāsītparaśvadhaiḥ
|
prakṛṣṭamasiyuddhaṃ ca babhūva kaṭukodayam||12||

13kuñjarāṇāṃ ca saṃpāte yuddhamāsīt sudāruṇam
|
apatatkuñjarādanyo hayādanyastvavākśirāḥ||13||

14naro bāṇavinirbhinno rathādanyaśca māriṣa
|
tatrānyasya ca saṃmarde patitasya vivarmaṇaḥ||14||

15śiraḥ pradhvaṃsayāmāsa vakṣasyākramya kuñjaraḥ
|
aparāṃścāpare'mṛdnan vāraṇāḥ patitānnarān||15||

16viṣāṇaiścāvaniṃ gatvā vyabhindan rathino bahūn
|
narāntraiḥ kecidapare viṣāṇālagnasaṃśrayaiḥ||16||

17babhramuḥ samare nāgā mṛdnantaḥ śataśo narān
|
kārṣṇāyasatanutrāṇānnarāśvarathakuñjarān||17||

18patitān pothayāṃcakrurdvipāḥ sthūlanalāniva
|
gṛdhrapatrādhivāsāṃsi śayanāni narādhipāḥ||18||

19hrīmantaḥ kālasaṃparkāt suduḥkhānyanuśerate
|
hanti smātra pitā putraṃ rathenābhyetya saṃyuge||19||

20putraśca pitaraṃ mohānnirmaryāda‌mavartata
|
ratho bhagno dhvajaśchinnaśchatramurvyāṃ nipātitam||20||

21yugārdhaṃ cchinnamādāya pradudrāva tathā hayaḥ
|
sāsirbāhurnipatitaḥ śiraśchinnaṃ sakuṇḍalam||21||

22gajenākṣipya balinā rathaḥ saṃcūrṇitaḥ kṣitau
|
rathinā tāḍito nāgo nārācenāpatat akṣatā||22||

23sārohaścāpatadvājī gajenābhyahitā bhṛśam
|
nirmaryādaṃ mahadyuddhamavartata sudāruṇam||23||

24hā tāta hā putra sakhe kvāsi tiṣṭha kva dhāvasi
|
praharāhara jahyenaṃ smitakṣveḍitagarjitaiḥ||24||

25ityevamuccaranti sma śrūyante vividhā giraḥ
|
narasyāśvasya nāgasya samasajjata śoṇitam||25||

26upāśāmyadrajo bhaumaṃ bhīrūn kaśmalamāviśat
|
cakreṇa cakramāsādya vīro vīrasya saṃyuge||26||

27atīteṣu pathe kāle jahāra gadayā śiraḥ
|
āsīt keśaparāmarśo muṣṭiyuddhaṃ ca dāruṇam||27||

28nakhairdantaiśca śūrāṇāmadvīpe dvīpamicchatām
|
tatrācchidyata śūrasya sakhaṅgo bāhurudyataḥ||28||

29sadhanuścāparasyāpi saśaraḥ sāṃkuśastathā
|
ākrośadanyamanyo'tra tathānyo vimukho'dravat||29||

30anyaḥ prāptasya cānyasya śiraḥ kāyāda‌pāharat
|
saśabdamadravaccāyaḥ śabdādanyo'trasadbhṛśam||30||

31svānanyo'tha parānanyo jaghāna niśitaiḥ śaraiḥ
|
giriśṛṃgopamaścātra nārācena nipātitaḥ||31||

32mātaṅgo nyapatadbhūmau nadīrodha ivoṣṇave
|
tathaiva rathinaṃ nāgaḥ kṣaran giririvārujan||32||

33abhyatiṣṭhat padā bhūmau sahāśvaṃ sahasārathim
|
śūrān praharato dṛṣṭvā kutāstrān rudhirokṣitān||33||

34bahūnapyāviśanmoho bhīrūn hṛdayadurbalān
|
sarvamāvignamabhavanna prājñāyata kiṃcana||34||

35sainyena rajasā dhvastaṃ nirmaryādamavartata
|
tataḥ senāpatiḥ śīghramayaṃ kāla iti bruvan||35||

36nityābhitvaritāneva tvarayāmāsa pāṇḍavān
|
kurvantaḥ śāsanaṃ tasya pāṇḍavā bāhuśālinaḥ||36||

37saro haṃsā ivāpeturghnanto droṇarathaṃ prati
|
gṛhṇītādravatānyonyaṃ vibhītā vinikṛntata||37||

38ityāsīttumulaḥ śabdo durdharṣasya rathaṃ prati
|
tato droṇaḥ kṛpaḥ karṇo drauṇī rājā jayadrathaḥ||38||

39vindānuvindāvāvantyau śalyaścaitānnyavārayan
|
te tvāryadharmasaṃrabdhā durnivārā durāsadāḥ||39||

40śarārtā na jahurdoṇaṃ pañcālāḥ pāṇḍavaiḥ saha
|
tato droṇotisaṃkruddho visṛjañśataśaḥ śarān||40||

41cedipañcālapāṇḍūnāmakarot kadanaṃ mahat
|
tasya jyātalanirghoṣaḥ śuśruve dikṣu māriṣa||41||

42vajrasaṃhrādasaṃkāśastrāsayan mānavān bahūn
|
etasminnantare jiṣṇurjitvā saṃśaptakānbahūn||42||

43abhyayāttatra yatrāsau droṇaḥ pāṇḍūn pramardati
|
tāñcharaughānmahāvartān śoṇitodān mahāhradān||43||

44tīrṇaḥ saṃśaptakān hatvā pratyadṛśyata phālgunaḥ
|
tasya kīrtimato lakṣma sūryapratimatejasaḥ||44||

45dīpyamānamapaśyāma tejasā vānaradhvajam
|
saṃśaptakasamudraṃ tamucchoṣyāstragabhastibhiḥ||45||

46sapāṇḍavayugāntārkaḥ kurūnapyabhyatītapat
|
pradadāha kurūn sarvānarjunaḥ śastratejasā||46||

47yugānte sarvabhūtāni dhūmaketurivotthitaḥ
|
tena bāṇasahasraughairgajāśvarathayodhinaḥ||47||

48tāḍyamānāḥ kṣitiṃ jagmurmuktakeśāḥ śarārditāḥ
|
kecidārtasvanaṃ cakrurvineśurapare punaḥ||48||

49pārthabāṇahatāḥ kecinnipeturvigatāsavaḥ
|
teṣāmutpatitān kāṃścit patitāṃśca parāṅmukhān||49||

50na jaghānārjuno yodhān yodhavratamanusmaran
|
te vikīrṇarathāścitrāḥ prāyaśaśca parāṅmukhāḥ||50||

51kuravaḥ karṇa karṇeti hāheti ca vicukruśuḥ
|
tamādhirathirākrandaṃ vijñāya śaraṇaiṣiṇām||51||

52māmaiṣṭeti pratiśrutya yayāvabhimukho'rjunam
|
sa bhāratarathaśreṣṭhaḥ sarvabhārataharṣaṇaḥ||52||

53prāduścakre tadāgneyamastramastravidāṃ varaḥ
|
tasya dīptaśaraughasya dīptacāpadharasya ca||53||

54śaraughāñcharajālena vidudhāva dhanañjayaḥ
|
tathaivādhirathistasya bāṇāñjvalitatejasaḥ||54||

55astramastreṇa saṃvārya prāṇadadvisṛjan śarān
|
dhṛṣṭadyumnaśca bhīmaśca sātyakiśca mahārathaḥ||55||

56vivyadhuḥ karṇamāsādya tribhistribhirajihmagaiḥ
|
arjunāstraṃ tu rādheyaḥ saṃvārya śaravṛṣṭibhiḥ||56||

57teṣāṃ trayāṇāṃ cāpāni ciccheda viśikhaistribhiḥ
|
te nikṛttāyudhāḥ śūrā nirviṣā bhujagā iva||57||

58rathaśaktīḥ samutkṣipya bhṛśaṃ siṃhā ivānadan
|
tā bhujāgrairmahāvegā nisṛṣṭā bhujagoṣamāḥ||58||

59dīpyamānā mahāśaktyo jagmurādhirathiṃ prati
|
tā nikṛtya śaravrātaistribhistribhirajihmagaiḥ||59||

60nanāda balavān karṇaḥ pārthāya visṛjañcharān
|
arjunaścāpi rādheyaṃ viddhvā saptabhirāśugaiḥ||60||

61karṇādavarajaṃ bāṇairjaghāna niśitaiḥ śaraiḥ
|
tataḥ śatruñjayaṃ hatvā pārthaḥ ṣadbhirajihmagaiḥ||61||

62jahāra sadyo bhallena vipāṭasya śiro rathāt
|
paśyatāṃ dhārtarāṣṭrāṇāmekenaiva kirīṭinā||62||

63pramukhe sūtaputrasya sodaryā nihatāstrayaḥ
|
tato bhīmaḥ samutpatya svarathādvainateyavat||63||

64varāsinā karṇapakṣān jaghāna daśa pañca ca
|
punastu rathamāsthāya dhanurādāya cāparam||64||

65vivyādha daśabhiḥ karṇaṃ sūtamaśvāṃśca pañcabhiḥ
|
dhṛṣṭadyumno'pyasivaraṃ carmacādāya bhāsvaram||65||

66jaghāna candravarmāṇaṃ bṛhatkṣatraṃ ca naiṣadham
|
tataḥ svarathamāsthāya pāñcālyo'nyacca kārmukam||66||

67ādāya karṇaṃ vivyādha trisaptatyā nadan raṇe
|
śaineyo'pyanyadādāya dhanurindusamadyutiḥ||67||

68sūtaputraṃ catuḥṣaṣṭyā viddhvā siṃha ivānadan
|
bhallābhyāṃ sādhumuktābhyāṃ chittvā karṇasya kārmukam||68||

69punaḥ karṇaṃ tribhirbāṇairbāhvorurasi cārpayat
|
tato duryodhano droṇo rājā caiva jayadrathaḥ||69||

70nimajjamānaṃ rādheyamujjahruḥ sātyakārṇavāt
|
pattyaśvarathamātaṅgāstvadīyāḥ śataśo'pare||70||

71karṇamevābhyadhāvanta trāsyamānāḥ prahāriṇaḥ
|
dhṛṣṭadyumnaśca bhīmaśca saubhadro'rjuna eva ca||71||

72nakulaḥ sahadevaśca sātyakiṃ jugupū raṇe
|
evameṣa mahāraudraḥ kṣayārthaṃ sarvadhanvinām||72||

73tāvakānāṃ pareṣāṃ ca tyaktvā prāṇānabhūdraṇaḥ
|
padātirathanāgāśvāḥ gajāśvarathapattibhiḥ||73||

74rathino nāgapattyaśvai rathapattī rathādvipaiḥ
|
aśvairaśvā gajairnāgā rathino rathibhiḥ saha||74||

75saṃyuktāḥ samadṛśyanta pattayaścāpi pattibhiḥ
|
evaṃ sukalilaṃ yuddhamāsītkravyādaharṣaṇam
|
mahadbhistairabhītānāṃ yamarāṣṭravivardhanam||75||

76tato hatā nararathavājikuñjarairanekaśo dviparathapattivājinaḥ
|
gajairgajā rathibhirudāyudhā rathā hayairhayāḥ pattigaṇaiśca pattayaḥ||76||

77rathairddhipā dviradavarairmahāhayā hayairnarā vararathibhiśca vājinaḥ
|
nirastajihvādaśanekṣaṇāḥ kṣitau kṣayaṃ gatāḥ pramathitavarmabhūṣaṇāḥ||77||

78tathā parairbahukaraṇairvarāyudhairhatā gatāḥ pratibhayadarśanāḥ kṣitim
|
vipothitā hayagajapādatāḍitā bhṛśākulā rathamukhanemibhiḥ kṣatāḥ||78||

79pramodane śvāpadapakṣirakṣasāṃ janakṣaye vartati tatra dāruṇe
|
mahābalāste kupitāḥ parasparaṃ niṣūdayantaḥ pravicerurojasā||79||

80tato bale bhṛśalulite parasparaṃ nirīkṣamāṇe rudhiraughasaṃplute
|
divākare'staṃ girimāsthite śanairubhe prayāte śibirāya bhārata||80||
