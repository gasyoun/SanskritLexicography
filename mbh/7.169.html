Mahābhārata 7.169 (Droṇaparvan)

1sañjaya uvāca|
nakulaṃ rabhasaṃ yuddhe nighnantaṃ vāhinīṃ tava
|
abhyayātsaubalaḥkruddhastiṣṭha tiṣṭheti cābravīt||1||

2kṛtavairau tu tau vīrāvanyonyavadhakāṃkṣiṇau
|
śaraiḥ pūrṇāyatotsṛṣṭairanyonyamabhijaghnatuḥ||2||

3yathaiva nakulo rājan śaravarṣāṇyamuñcata
|
tathaiva saubalaścāpi śikṣāṃ sandarśayanyudhi||3||

4tāvubhau samare śūrau śarakaṇṭakinau tadā
|
vyarājetāṃ mahārāja śvāvidhau śalalairiva||4||

5rukmapuṃkhairajihmāgraiḥ śaraiśchinnatanucchadau
|
rudhiraughapariklinnau vyabhrājetāṃ mahāmṛdhe||5||

6tapanīyanibhau citrau kalpavṛkṣāviva drumau
|
kiṃśukāviva cotphullau prakāśete raṇājire||6||

7tāvubhau samare śūrau śarakaṇṭakinau tadā
|
vyarājetāṃ mahārāja kaṇṭakairiva śālmalī||7||

8sujihyaṃ prekṣamāṇau ca rājanvivṛtalocanau
|
krodhasaṃraktanayanau nirdahantau parasparam||8||

9syālastu tava saṃkruddho mādrīputraṃ hasanniva
|
karṇinaikena vivyādha hṛdaye niśitena ha||9||

10nakulastu bhṛśaṃ viddhaḥ syālena tava dhanvinā
|
niṣasāda rathopasthe kaśmalaṃ cāviśanmahat||10||

11atyantavairiṇaṃ dṛptaṃ dṛṣṭvā śatruṃ tathāgatam
|
nanāda śakunī rājaṃstapānte jalado yathā||11||

12pratilabhya tataḥ saṃjñāṃ nakulaḥ pāṇḍunandanaḥ
|
abhyayātsaubalaṃ bhūyo vyāttānana ivāntakaḥ||12||

13saṃkruddhaḥ śakuniṃ ṣaṣṭhyā vivyādha bharatarṣabha
|
punaścainaṃ śatenaiva nārācānāṃ stanāntare||13||

14athāsya saśaraṃ cāpaṃ muṣṭideśe'cchinattadā
|
dhvajaṃ ca tvaritaṃ chittvā rathādbhūmāvapātayat||14||

15viśikhena ca tīkṣṇena pītena niśitena ca
|
ūrū nirbhidya caikena nakulaḥ pāṇḍunandanaḥ||15||

16śyenaṃ sapakṣaṃ vyādhena pātayāmāsa taṃ tadā
|
so'tividdho mahārāja rathopastha upāviśat||16||

17dhvajayaṣṭiṃ parikliśya kāmukaḥ kāminīṃ yathā
|
taṃ visaṃjñaṃ nipatitaṃ dṛṣṭvā syālaṃ tavānagha||17||

18apovāha rathenāśu sārathirdhvajinīmukhāt
|
tataḥ saṃcukruśuḥ pārthā ye ca teṣāṃ padānugāḥ||18||

19nirjitya ca raṇe śatruṃ nakulaḥ śatrutāpanaḥ
|
abravītsārathiṃ kruddho droṇānīkāya māṃ vaha||19||

20tasya tadvacanaṃ śrutvā mādrīputrasya sārathiḥ
|
prāyāttena tadā rājan yatra droṇo vyavasthitaḥ||20||

21śikhaṇḍinaṃ tu samare droṇaprepsuṃ viśāṃpate
|
kṛpaḥ śāradvato yattaḥ pratyagacchatsavegitaḥ||21||

22gautamaṃ drutamāyāntaṃ droṇānakimarindamam
|
vivyādha navabhirbhallaiḥ śikhaṇḍī prahasanniva||22||

23tamācāryo mahārāja viddhvā pañcabhirāśugaiḥ
|
punarvivyādha viṃśatyā putrāṇāṃ priyakṛttava||23||

24mahadyuddhaṃ tayorāsīdghorarūpaṃ bhayānakam
|
yathā devāsure yuddhe śaṃbarāmararājayoḥ||24||

25śarajālāvṛtaṃ vyoma cakratustau mahārathau
|
meghāviva tapāpāye vīrau samaradurmadau||25||

26prakṛtyā ghorarūpaṃ tadāsīddhorataraṃ punaḥ
|
rātriśca bharataśreṣṭha yodhānāṃ yuddhaśālinām||26||

27kālarātrinibhā hyāsīdghorarūpā bhayānakā
|
śikhaṇḍī tu mahārāja gautamasya mahaddhanuḥ||27||

28ardhacandreṇa ciccheda sajyaṃ saviśikhaṃ tadā
|
tasya kruddhaḥ kṛpo rājañśaktiṃ cikṣepa dāruṇām||28||

29svarṇadaṇḍāmakuṇṭhāgrāṃ karmāraparimārjitām
|
tāmāpatantīṃ ciccheda śikhaṇḍī bahubhiḥ śaraiḥ||29||

30sā'patanmedinīṃ dīptā bhāsayantī mahāprabhā
|
athānyaddhanurādāya gautamo rathināṃ varaḥ||30||

31prācchādayacchitairbāṇairmahārāja śikhaṇḍinam
|
sa cchādyamānaḥ samare gautamena yaśasvinā||31||

32nyaṣīdata rathopasthe śikhaṇḍī rathināṃ varaḥ
|
sīdantaṃ cainamālokya kṛpaḥ śāradvato yudhi||32||

33ājaghne bahubhirbāṇairjighāṃsanniva bhārata
|
vimukhaṃ tu raṇe dṛṣṭvā yājñaseniṃ mahāratham||33||

34pañcālāḥ somakāścaiva parivavruḥ samantataḥ
|
tathaiva tava putrāśca parivavrurdvijottamam||34||

35mahatyā senayā sārdhaṃ tato yuddhamavartata
|
rathānāṃ ca raṇe rājannanyonyamabhidhāvatām||35||

36babhūva tumulaḥ śabdo meghānāṃ garjatāmiva
|
dravatāṃ sādināṃ caiva gajānāṃ ca viśāṃpate||36||

37anyonyamabhito rājan krūramāyodhanaṃ babhau
|
pattīnāṃ dravatāṃ caiva pādaśabdena medinī||37||

38akampata mahārāja bhayatrasteva cāṅganā
|
rathino rathamāruhya pradrutā vegavattaram||38||

39agṛhṇanbahavo rājañśalabhānvāyasā iva
|
tathā gajānprabhinnāṃśca saṃprabhinnā mahāgajāḥ||39||

40tasminneva pade yattā nigṛhṇanti sma bhārata
|
sādī sādinamāsādya pattayaśca padātinam||40||

41samāsādya raṇe'nyonyaṃ saṃrabdhā nāticakramuḥ
|
dhāvatāṃ dravatāṃ caiva punarāvartatāmapi||41||

42babhūva tatra sainyānāṃ śabdaḥ suvipulo niśi
|
dīpyamānāḥ pradīpāśca rathavāraṇavājiṣu||42||

43adṛśyanta mahārāja maholkā iva khāccyyutāḥ
|
sā niśā bharataśreṣṭha pradīpairavabhāsitā||43||

44divasapratimā rājanbabhūva raṇamūrdhani
|
ādityena yathā vyāptaṃ tamo loke praṇaśyati||44||

45tathā naṣṭaṃ tamo ghoraṃ dīpairdīptairitastataḥ
|
divaṃ ca pṛthivīṃ caiva diśaśca pradiśastathā||45||

46rajasā tamasā vyāptā dyotitāḥ prabhayā punaḥ
|
astrāṇāṃ kavacānāṃ ca maṇīnāṃ ca mahātmanām||46||

47antardadhuḥ prabhāḥ sarvā dīpaistairavabhāsitāḥ
|
tasminkolāhale yuddhe vartamāne niśāmukhe||47||

48na kiṃcidvidurātmānamayamasmīti bhārata
|
avadhītsamare putraṃ pitā bharatasattama||48||

49putraśca pitaraṃ mohātsakhāyaṃ ca sakhā tathā
|
svīstrīyaṃ pātulaścāpi svastrīyaścāpi mātulam||49||

50sve svānpare parāṃścāpi nijaghnuritaretaram
|
nirmaryādamabhūdyuddhaṃ rātrau bhīrubhayānakam||50||
