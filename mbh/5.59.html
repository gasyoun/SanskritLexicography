Mahābhārata 5.59 (Udyogaparvan)

1dhṛtarāṣṭra uvāca|
yadabrūtāṃ mahātmānau vāsudevadhanañjayau
|
tanme brūhi mahāprājña śuśrūṣe vacanaṃ tava ||1||

2sañjaya uvāca|
śṛṇu rājan yathā dṛṣṭau yathā kṛṣṇadhanañjayau
|
ūcatuścāpi yadvīrau tatte vakṣyāmi bhārata ||2||

3pādāṅgulīrabhiprekṣan prayato'haṃ kṛtāñjaliḥ
|
śuddhāntaṃ prāviśaṃ rājannākhyātuṃ naradevayoḥ ||3||

4naivābhimanyurna yamau taṃ deśamabhiyānti vai
|
yatra kṛṣṇau ca kṛṣṇā ca satyabhāmā ca bhāminī ||4||

5ubhau madhvāsavakṣībāvubhau candanarūṣitau
|
sragviṇau varavastrau tau divyābharaṇabhūṣitau ||5||

6naikaratnavicitraṃ tu kāñcanaṃ mahadāsanam
|
vividhāstaraṇākīrṇaṃ yatrāsātāmarinda‌mau ||6||

7arjunotsaṅgagau pādau keśavasyopalakṣaye
|
arjunasya ca kṛṣṇāyāṃ satyāyāṃ ca mahātmanaḥ ||7||

8kāñcanaṃ pādapīṭhaṃ tu pārtho me prādiśattadā
|
tadahaṃ pāṇinā spṛṣṭvā tato bhūmāvupāviśam ||8||

9ūrdhvarekhātalau pādau pārthasya śubhalakṣaṇau
|
pāda‌pīṭhādapahṛtau tatrāpaśyamahaṃ śubhau ||9||

10śyāmau bṛhantau taruṇau śālaskandhāvivodgatau
|
ekāsanagatau dṛṣṭvā bhayaṃ māṃ mahadāviśat ||10||

11indraviṣṇusamāvetau mandātmā nāvabuddhyate
|
saṃśrayāddroṇabhīṣmābhyāṃ karṇasya ca vikatthanāt ||11||

12nideśasthāvimau yasya mānasastasya setsyate
|
saṅkalpo dharmarājasya niścayo me tadā'bhavat ||12||

13satkṛtaścānnapānābhyāmāsīno labdhasatkriyaḥ
|
añjaliṃ mūrdhni sandhāya tau sandeśamacodayam||13||

14dhanurguṇakiṇāṅkena pāṇinā śubhalakṣaṇam
|
pādamānamayan pārthaḥ keśavaṃ samacodayat ||14||

15indraketurivotthāya sarvābharaṇabhūṣitaḥ
|
indravīryopamaḥ kṛṣṇaḥ saṃviṣṭo mābhyabhāṣata ||15||

16vācaṃ sa vadatāṃ śreṣṭho hlādinīṃ vacanakṣamām
|
trāsinīṃ dhārtarāṣṭrāṇāṃ mṛdupūrvāṃ sudāruṇām ||16||

17vācaṃ tāṃ vacanārhasya śikṣākṣarasamanvitām
|
aśrauṣamahamiṣṭārthāṃ paścāddhṛdayahāriṇīm ||17||

18vāsudeva uvāca|
sañjayedaṃ vaco brūyā dhṛtarāṣṭraṃ manīṣiṇam
|
kurumukhyasya bhīṣmasya droṇasyāpi ca śṛṇvataḥ ||18||

19āvayorvacanāt sūta jyeṣṭhānapyamivādayan
|
yavīyasaśca kuśalaṃ paścāt pṛṣṭvaivamuttaram ||19||

20yajadhvaṃ vividhairyajñairviprebhyo dattadakṣiṇāḥ
|
putrairdāraiśca modadhvaṃ mahadvo bhayamāgatam ||20||

21arthāṃstyajata pātrebhyaḥ sutān prāpnuta kāmajān
|
priyaṃ priyebhyaścarata rājā hi tvarate jaye ||21||

22ṛṇametatpravṛddhaṃ me hṛdayānnāpasarpati
|
yadgovindeti cukrośa kṛṣṇā māṃ dūravāsinam ||22||

23tejomayaṃ durādharṣaṃ gāṇḍīvaṃ yasya kārmukam
|
maddvitīyena teneha vairaṃ vaḥ savyasācinā ||23||

24maddvitīyaṃ punaḥ pārthaṃ kaḥ prārthayitumicchati
|
yo na kālaparīto vā'pyapi sākṣāt purandaraḥ ||24||

25bāhubhyāsudvahedbhūmiṃ dahet kruddha imāḥ prajāḥ
|
pātayettridivāddevān yo'rjunaṃ samare jayet ||25||

26devāsuramanuṣyeṣu yakṣa-gandharva-bhogiṣu
|
na taṃ paśyāmyahaṃ yuddhe pāṇḍavaṃ yo'bhyayādraṇe ||26||

27yattadvirāṭanagare śrūyate mahadadbhutam
|
ekasya ca bahūnāṃ ca paryāptaṃ tannidarśanam ||27||

28ekena pāṇḍuputreṇa virāṭanagare yadā
|
bhagnāḥ palāyata diśaḥ paryāptaṃ tannidarśanam ||28||

29balaṃ vīryaṃ ca tejaśca śīghratā laghuhastatā
|
aviṣādaśca dhairyaṃ ca pārthānnānyatra vidyate ||29||

30ityabravīddhṛṣīkeśaḥ pārthamuddharṣayan girā
|
garjan samayavarṣīva gagane pākaśāsanaḥ ||30||

31keśavasya vacaḥ śrutvā kirīṭī śvetavāhanaḥ
|
arjunastanmahadvākyamabravīdromaharṣaṇam ||31||
