Mahābhārata 13.30 (Anuśāsanaparvan)

1yudhiṣṭhira uvāca|
śrutaṃ me mahadākhyānametatkurukulodvaha
|
suduṣprāpaṃ yadbravīṣi brāhmaṇyaṃ vadatāṃ vara ||1||

2viśvāmitreṇa ca purā brāhmaṇyaṃ prāptamityuta
|
śrūyate vadase tacca duṣprāpamiti sattama ||2||

3vītahavyaśca nṛpatiḥ śruto me vipratāṃ gataḥ
|
tadeva tāvadgāṅgeya śrotumicchāmyahaṃ vibho ||3||

4sa kena karmaṇā prāpto brāhmaṇyaṃ rājasattamaḥ
|
vareṇa tapasā cāpi tanme vyākhyātumarhasi ||4||

5bhīṣma uvāca|
śrṛṇu rājan yathā rājā vītahavyo mahāyaśāḥ
|
rājarṣirdurlabhaṃ prāpto brāhmaṇyaṃ lokasatkṛtam ||5||

6manormahātmanastāta prajā dharmeṇa śāsataḥ
|
babhūva putro dharmātmā śaryātiriti viśrutaḥ ||6||

7tasyānvavāye dvau rājan rājānau sambabhūvatuḥ
|
haihayastalajaṃghaśca vatsasya jayatāṃ vara ||7||

8haihayasya tu rājendra daśasu strīṣu bhārata
|
śataṃ babhūva putrāṇāṃ śūrāṇāmanivartinām ||8||

9tulyarūpaprabhāvānāṃ balināṃ yuddhaśālinām
|
dhanurvede ca vede ca sarvatraiva kṛtaśramāḥ ||9||

10kāśiṣvapi nṛpo rājan divodāsapitāmahaḥ
|
haryaśva iti vikhyāto babhūva jayatāṃ varaḥ ||10||

11sa vītahavyadāyādairāgatya puruṣarṣabha
|
gaṅgāyamunayormadhye saṃgrāme vinipātitaḥ ||11||

12taṃ tu hatvā narapatiṃ haihayāste mahārathāḥ
|
pratijagmuḥ purīṃ ramyāṃ vatsānāmakutobhayāḥ ||12||

13haryaśvasya ca dāyādaḥ kāśirājo'bhyaṣicyata
|
sudevo devasaṅkāśaḥ sākṣāddharma ivāparaḥ ||13||

14sa pālayāmāsa mahīṃ dharmātmā kāśinandanaḥ
|
tairvītahavyairāgatya yudhi sarvairvinirjitaḥ ||14||

15tamathājau vinirjitya pratijagmuryathāgatam
|
saudevastvatha kāśiśo divodāso'bhyaṣicyata ||15||

16divodāsastu vijñāya vīryaṃ teṣāṃ yatātmanām
|
vārāṇasīṃ mahātejā nirmame śakraśāsanāt ||16||

17viprakṣatriyasambādhāṃ vaiśyaśūdrasamākulām
|
naikadravyoccayavatīṃ samṛddhavipaṇāpaṇām ||17||

18gaṅgāyā uttare kūle vaprānte rājasattama
|
gomatyā dakṣiṇe kūle śakrasyevāmarāvatīm ||18||

19tatra taṃ rājaśārdūlaṃ nivasantaṃ mahīpatim
|
āgatya haihayā bhūyaḥ paryadhāvanta bhārata ||19||

20sa niṣkramya dadau yuddhaṃ tebhyo rājā mahābalaḥ
|
devāsurasamaṃ ghoraṃ divodāso mahādyutiḥ ||20||

21sa tu yuddhaṃ mahārāja dinānāṃ daśatīrdaśa
|
hatavāhanabhūyiṣṭhastato dainyamupāgamat ||21||

22hayayodhastato rājan kṣīṇakośaśca bhūmipaḥ
|
divodāsaḥ purīṃ tyaktvā palāyanaparo'bhavat ||22||

23gatvā''śramapadaṃ ramyaṃ bharadvājasya dhīmataḥ
|
jagāma śaraṇaṃ rājā kṛtāñjalirarindama ||23||

24tamuvāca bharadvājo jyeṣṭhaḥ putro bṛhaspateḥ
|
purodhāḥ śīlasampanno divodāsaṃ mahīpatim ||24||

25kimāgamanakṛtyaṃ te sarvaṃ prabrūhi me nṛpa
|
yatte priyaṃ tatkariṣye na me'trāsti vicāraṇā ||25||

26rājovāca
|
bhagavanvaitahavyairme yuddhe vaṃśaḥ praṇāśitaḥ
|
ahamekaḥ paridyūno bhavantaṃ śaraṇaṃ gataḥ ||26||

27śiṣyasnehena bhagavaṃstvaṃ māṃ rakṣitumarhasi
|
ekaśeṣaḥ kṛto vaṃśo mama taiḥ pāpakarmabhiḥ ||27||

28tamuvāca mahābhāgo bharadvājaḥ pratāpavān
|
na bhetavyaṃ na bhetavyaṃ saudeva vyetu te bhayam ||28||

29ahamiṣṭiṃ kariṣyāmi putrārthaṃ te viśāmpate
|
vītahavyasahasrāṇi yena tvaṃ prahariṣyasi ||29||

30tata iṣṭiṃ cakārarṣistasya vai putrakāmikīm
|
athāsya tanayo jajñe pratardana iti śrutaḥ ||30||

31sa jātamātro vavṛdhe samāḥ saptatrayodaśa
|
vadaṃ cāpi jagau kṛtsnaṃ dhanurvedaṃ ca bhārata ||31||

32yogena ca samāviṣṭo bharadvājena dhīmatā
|
tejolokyaṃ sa saṃgṛhya tasmindeśe samāviśat ||32||

33tataḥ sa kavacī dhanvī stūyamānaḥ surarṣibhiḥ
|
bandibhirvandyamānaśca babhau sūrya ivoditaḥ ||33||

34sa rathī baddhanistriṃśo babhau dīpta ivānalaḥ
|
prayayau sa dhanurdhunvan khaḍgī carmī śarāsanī ||34||

35taṃ dṛṣṭvā paramaṃ harṣaṃ sudevatanayo yayau
|
mene ca manasā dagdhān vaitahavyānsa pārthivaḥ ||35||

36tato'sau yauvarājye ca sthāpayitvā pratardanam
|
kṛtakṛtyaṃ tadā''tmānaṃ sa rājā abhyanandata ||36||

37tatastu vaitahavyānāṃ vadhāya sa mahīpateḥ
|
putraṃ prasthāpayāmāsa pratardanamarindamam ||37||

38sarathaḥ sa tu santīrya gaṃgāmāśu parākramī
|
prayayau vītahavyānāṃ purīṃ parapuraṃjayaḥ ||38||

39vaitahavyāstu saṃśrutya rathaghoṣaṃ samuddhatam
|
niryayurnagarākārai rathaiḥ pararathārujaiḥ ||39||

40niṣkramya te naravyāghrā daṃśitāścitrayodhinaḥ
|
pratardanaṃ samājagmuḥ śaravarṣairudāyudhāḥ ||40||

41śastraiśca vividhākārai rathaughaiśca yudhiṣṭhira
|
abhyavarṣanta rājānaṃ himavantamivambudāḥ ||41||

42astrairastrāṇi saṃvārya teṣāṃ rājā pratardanaḥ
|
jaghāna tānmahātejā vajrānalasamaiḥ śaraiḥ ||42||

43kṛttottamāṃgāste rājan bhallaiḥ śatasahasraśaḥ
|
apatan rudhirārdrāṃgā nikṛttā iva kiṃśukāḥ ||43||

44hateṣu teṣu sarveṣu vītahavyaḥ suteṣvatha
|
prādravannagaraṃ hitvā bhṛgorāśramamapyuta ||44||

45yayau bhṛguṃ ca śaraṇaṃ vītahavyo narādhipaḥ
|
abhayaṃ ca dadau tasmai rājñe rājan bhṛgustadā ||45||

46athānupadamevāśu tatrāgacchatpratardanaḥ
|
sa prāpya cāśramapadaṃ divodāsātmajo'bravīt ||46||

47bho bho ke'trāśrame santi bhṛgoḥ śiṣyā mahātmanaḥ
|
draṣṭumicche munimahaṃ tasyācakṣata māmiti ||47||

48sa taṃ viditvā tu bhṛgurniścakrāmāśramāttadā
|
pūjayāmāsa ca tato vidhinā nṛpasattamam ||48||

49uvāca cainaṃ rājendra kiṃ kāryaṃ brūhi pārthiva
|
sa covāca nṛpastasmai yadāgamanakāraṇam ||49||

50rājovāca
|
ayaṃ brahmannito rājā vītahavyo visṛjyatām
|
tasya putrairhi me kṛtsno brahmanvaṃśaḥ praṇāśitaḥ ||50||

51utsāditaśca viṣayaḥ kāśīnāṃ ratnasañcayaḥ
|
etasya vīryadṛptasya hataṃ putraśataṃ mayā ||51||

52asyedānīṃ vadhādadya bhaviṣyāmyanṛṇaḥ pituḥ
|
tamuvāca kṛpāviṣṭo bhṛgurdharmabhṛtāṃ varaḥ ||52||

53nehāsti kṣatriyaḥ kaścitsarve hīme dvijātayaḥ
|
etattu vacanaṃ śrutvā bhṛgostathyaṃ pratardanaḥ ||53||

54pādāvupaspṛśya śanaiḥ prahṛṣṭo vākyamabravīt
|
evamapyasmi bhagavan kṛtakṛtyo na saṃśayaḥ ||54||

55ya eṣa rājā vīryeṇa svajātiṃ tyājito mayā
|
anujānīhi māṃ brahman dhyāyasva ca śivena mām ||55||

56tyājito hi mayā jātimeva rājā bhṛgūdvaha
|
tatastenābhyanujñāto yayau rājā pratardanaḥ ||56||

57yathāgataṃ mahārāja muktvā viṣāmivoragaḥ
|
bhṛgorvacanamātreṇa sa ca brahmarṣitāṃ gataḥ ||57||

58vītahavyo mahārāja brahmavāditvameva ca
|
tasya gṛtsamadaḥ putro rūpeṇendra ivāparaḥ ||58||

59śakrastvamiti yo daityairnigṛhītaḥ kilābhavat
|
ṛgvede vartate cāgryā śrutiryasya mahātmanaḥ ||59||

60yatra gṛtsamado rājan brāhmaṇaiḥ sa mahīyate
|
sa brahmacārī viprarṣiḥ śrīmān gṛtsamado'bhavat ||60||

61putro gṛtsamadasyāpi sucetā abhavaddvijaḥ
|
varcāḥ sutejasaḥ putro vihavyastasya cātmajaḥ ||61||

62vihavyasya tu putrastu vitatyastasya cātmajaḥ
|
vitatyasya sutaḥ satyaḥ santaḥ satyasya cātmajaḥ ||62||

63śravāstasya sutaścārṣiḥ śravasaścābhavattamaḥ
|
tamasaśca prakāśo'bhūttanayo dvijasattamaḥ
|
prakāśasya ca vāgīndro babhūva jayatāṃ varaḥ ||63||

64tasyātmajaśca pramitirvedavedāṅgapāragaḥ
|
ghṛtācyāṃ tasya putrastu rururnāmodapadyata ||64||

65pramadvarāyāṃ tu ruroḥ putraḥ samudapadyata
|
śunako nāma viprarṣiryasya putro'tha śaunakaḥ ||65||

66evaṃ vipratvamagamadvītahavyo narādhipaḥ
|
bhṛgoḥ prasādādrājendra kṣatriyaḥ kṣatriyarṣabha ||66||

67tathaiva kathito vaṃśo mayā gārtsamadastava
|
vistareṇa mahārāja kimanyadanupṛcchasi ||67||
