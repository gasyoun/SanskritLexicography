Mahābhārata 2.16 (Sabhāparvan)

1samrāḍguṇamabhīpsanvai yuṣmānsvārthaparāyaṇaḥ
|
kathaṃ prahiṇuyāṃ kṛṣṇa so'haṃ kevalasāhasāt||1||

2bhīmārjunāvubhau netre mano manye janārdanam
|
manaścakṣurvihīnasya kīdṛśaṃ jīvitaṃ bhavet||2||

3jarāsandhabalaṃ prāpya duṣpāraṃ bhīmavikramam
|
yamo'pi na vijetājau tatra vaḥ kiṃ viceṣṭitam||3||

4asmiṃstvarthāntare yuktamanarthaḥ pratipadyate
|
tasmānna pratipattistu kāryā yuktā matā mama||4||

5yathāhaṃ vimṛśāmyekastattāvacchrūyatāṃ mama
|
saṃnyāsaṃ rocaye sādhu kāryasyāsya janārdana
|
pratihanti mano me'dya rājasūyo dūrāharaḥ||5||

6pārthaḥ prāpya dhanuśreṣṭhamakṣayyau ca maheṣudhī
|
rathaṃ dhvajaṃ sabhāṃ caiva yudhiṣṭhiramabhāṣata||6||

7arjuna uvāca|
dhanuḥ śastraṃ śarā vīryaṃ pakṣo bhūmiryaśo balam
|
prāptametanmaya rājanduṣprāpaṃ yadabhīpsitam||7||

8kule janma praśaṃsanti vaidyāḥ sādhu suniṣṭhitāḥ
|
balena sadṛśaṃ nāsti vīryaṃ tu mama rocate||8||

9kṛtavīryakule jāto nirvīryaḥ kiṃ kariṣyati
|
nirvīrye tu kule jāto vīryavāṃstu viśiṣyate||9||

10kṣatriyaḥ sarvaśo rājanyasya vṛttirdviṣajjaye
|
sarvaiguṇairvihīno'pi vīryavānhi tarendripūn||10||

11sarvairapi guṇairyukto nirvīryaḥ kiṃ kariṣyati
|
guṇībhūtā guṇāḥ sarve tiṣṭhanti hi parākrame||11||

12jayasya hetuḥ siddhirhi karma daivaṃ ca saṃśritam
|
saṃyukto hi balaiḥ kaścitpramādānnopayujyate||12||

13tena dvāreṇa śatrubhyaḥ kṣīyate sabalo ripuḥ||13||

14dainyaṃ yathā balavati tathā moho balānvite
|
tāvubhau nāśakau hetū rājñā tyājyau jayārthinā||14||

15jarāsandhivināśaṃ ca rājñāṃ ca parirakṣaṇam
|
yadi kuryām yajñārthaṃ kiṃ tataḥ paramaṃ bhavet||15||

16anārambhe hi niyato bhavedaguṇaniścayaḥ
|
guṇānniḥsaṃśayādrājannairguṇyaṃ manyase katham||16||

17kāṣāyaṃ sulabhaṃ paścānmunīnāṃ śamamicchatām
|
sāmrājyaṃ tu bhavecchakyaṃ vayaṃ yotsyāmahe parān||17||
