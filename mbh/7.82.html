Mahābhārata 7.82 (Droṇaparvan)

1sañjaya uvāca|
tayoḥ saṃvadatorevaṃ kṛṣṇadārukayostathā
|
sā'tyagādrajanī rājannatha rājā'nvabudhyata ||1||

2paṭhanti pāṇisvanikā māgadhā madhuparkikāḥ
|
vaitālikāśca sūtāśca tuṣṭuvuḥ puruṣarṣabham ||2||

3nartakāścāpyanṛtyanta jagugātāni gāyakāḥ
|
kuruvaṃśastavārthāni madhuraṃ raktakaṇṭhinaḥ ||3||

4mṛdaṃgā jharjharā bheryaḥ paṇavānakagomukhāḥ
|
āḍambarāśca śaṃkhāśca dundubhyaśca mahāsvanāḥ ||4||

5evametāni sarvāṇi tathānyānyapi bhārata
|
vādayanti susaṃhṛṣṭāḥ kuśalāḥ sādhuśikṣitāḥ ||5||

6sa meghasamanirghoṣo mahān śabdo'spṛśāddivam
|
pārthivapravaraṃ suptaṃ yudhiṣṭhiramabodhayat ||6||

7pratibuddhaḥ sukhaṃ supto mahārhe śayanottame
|
utthāyāvaśyakāryārthaṃ yayau snānagṛhaṃ nṛpaḥ ||7||

8tataḥ śuklāmbarāḥ snātāstaruṇāḥ śatamaṣṭa ca
|
snāpakāḥ kāñcanaiḥ kumbhaiḥ pūrṇaiḥ samupatasthire ||8||

9bhadrāsane sūpaviṣṭaḥ paridhāyāmbaraṃ laghu
|
sasnau candanasaṃyuktaiḥ pānīyairabhimantritaiḥ ||9||

10utsāditaḥ kaṣāyeṇa balavadbhiḥ suśikṣitaiḥ
|
āplutaḥ sādhivāsena jalena sa sugandhinā ||10||

11rājahaṃsanibhaṃ prāpya uṣṇīṣaṃ śithilārpitam
|
jalakṣayanimittaṃ vai veṣṭayāmāsa mūrdhani ||11||

12hariṇā candanenāṅgamupalipya mahābhujaḥ
|
sragvī cākliṣṭavasanaḥ prāṅmukhaḥ prāñjaliḥ sthitaḥ ||12||

13jajāpa japyaṃ kaunteyaḥ satāṃ mārgamanuṣṭhitaḥ
|
tatrāgniśaraṇaṃ daptiṃ praviveśa vinītavat ||13||

14samidbhiḥ sapavitrābhiragnimāhutibhistathā
|
mantrapūtābhirarcitvā niścakrāma gṛhāt tataḥ ||14||

15dvitīyāṃ puruṣavyāghraḥ kakṣyāṃ nirgamya pārthivaḥ
|
tato vedavido vṛddhānapaśyadbrāhmaṇarṣabhān ||15||

16dāntān vedavratasnātān snātānavabhṛtheṣu ca
|
sahasrānucarān saurān sahasraṃ cāṣṭa cāparān ||16||

17akṣataiḥ sumanobhiśca vācayitvā mahābhujaḥ
|
tān ‌dvijān madhusarpibhyāṃ phalaiḥ śreṣṭhaiḥ sumaṃgalaiḥ ||17||

18prādāt kāñcanamekaikaṃ niṣkaṃ viprāya pāṇḍavaḥ
|
alaṃkṛtaṃ cāśvaśataṃ vāsāṃsīṣṭāśca dakṣiṇāḥ ||18||

19tathā gāḥ kapilā dogdhrīḥ savatsāḥ pāṇḍunandanaḥ
|
hemaśṛṅgā raupyakhurā datvā tebhyaḥ prada‌kṣiṇam ||19||

20svastikān vardhamānāṃśca nandyāvartāṃśca kāñcanām
|
mālyaṃ ca jalakuṃbhāṃśca jvalitaṃ ca hutāśanam ||20||

21pūrṇānyakṣatapātrāṇi rucakaṃ rocanāstathā
|
svalaṃkṛtāḥ śubhāḥ kanyā dadhisarpirmadhūdakam ||21||

22maṅgalyān pakṣiṇaścaiva yaccānyadapi pūjitam
|
dṛṣṭvā spṛṣṭvā ca kaunteyo bāhyāṃ kakṣyāṃ tato'gamat ||22||

23tatastasyāṃ mahābāhostiṣṭhataḥ paricārakāḥ
|
sauvarṇaṃ sarvatobhadraṃ muktāvaidūryamaṇḍitam ||23||

24parārdhyāstaraṇāstīrṇaṃ sottaracchadamṛddhimat
|
viśvakarmakṛtaṃ divyamupajahrurvarāsanam ||24||

25tatra tasyopaviṣṭasya bhūṣaṇāni mahātmanaḥ
|
upājahrurmahārhāṇi preṣyāḥ śubhrāṇi sarvaśaḥ ||25||

26muktābharaṇaveṣasya kaunteyasya mahātmanaḥ
|
rūpamāsīnmahārāja dviṣatāṃ śokavardhanam ||26||

27cāmaraiścandraraśmyābhairhemadaṇḍaiḥ suśobhanaiḥ
|
dodhyamānaiḥ śuśubhe vidyudbhiriva toyadaḥ||27||

28saṃstūyamānaḥ sūtaiśca vandyamānaśca bandi‌bhiḥ
|
upagīyamāno gandharvairāste sma kurunandanaḥ ||28||

29tato muhūrtādāsīttu syandanānāṃ svano mahān
|
nemighoṣaśca rathināṃ khuraghoṣaśca vājinām ||29||

30hrādena gajaghaṇṭānāṃ śaṃkhānāṃ ninadena ca
|
narāṇāṃ padaśabdaiśca kampatīva sma medinī ||30||

31tataḥ śuddhāntamāsādya jānubhyāṃ bhūtale sthitaḥ
|
śirasā vandanīyaṃ tamabhivādya janeśvaram ||31||

32kuṇḍalībaddhanistriṃśaḥ sannaddhakavaco yuvā
|
abhipraṇamya śirasā dvāḥstho dharmātmajāya vai ||32||

33nyavedayaddhṛṣīkeśamupayāntaṃ mahātmane
|
so'bravīt‌ puruṣavyāghraḥ svāgatenaiva mādhavam ||33||

34arghyaṃ caivāsanaṃ cāsmai dīyatāṃ paramārcitam
|
tataḥ praveśya vārṣṇeyamupaveśya varāsane
|
pūjayāmāsa vidhivaddharmarājo yudhiṣṭhiraḥ ||34||
