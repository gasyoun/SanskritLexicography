Mahābhārata 5.72 (Udyogaparvan)

1vaiśampāyana uvāca|
sañjaye pratiyāte tu dharmarājo yudhiṣṭhiraḥ
|
abhyabhāṣata dāśārhamṛṣabhaṃ sarvasātvatām ||1||

2ayaṃ sa kālaḥ saṃprāpto mitrāṇāṃ mitravatsala
|
na ca tvadanyaṃ paśyāmi yo na āpatsu tārayet ||2||

3tvāṃ hi mādhavamāśritya nirbhayā moghadarpitam
|
dhārtarāṣṭraṃ sahāmātyaṃ svayaṃ samanuyuṅkṣmahe ||3||

4yathā hi sarvāsvāpatsu pāsi vṛṣṇīnarindama
|
tathā te pāṇḍavā rakṣyāḥ pāhyasmān mahato bhayāt ||4||

5śrībhagavānuvāca
|
ayamasmi mahābāho brūhi yatte vivakṣitam
|
kariṣyāmi hi tatsarvaṃ yattvaṃ vakṣyasi bhārata ||5||

6yudhiṣṭhira uvāca|
śrutaṃ te dhārtarāṣṭrasya saputrasya cikīrṣitam
|
etaddhi sakalaṃ kṛṣṇa sañjayo māṃ yadabravīt ||6||

7tanmataṃ dhṛtarāṣṭrasya so'syātmā vivṛtāntaraḥ
|
yathoktaṃ dūta ācaṣṭe vadhyaḥ syādanyathā bruvan ||7||

8apradānena rājyasya śāntimasmāsu mārgati
|
lubdhaḥ pāpena manasā carannasamamātmanaḥ ||8||

9yattaddvādaśa varṣāṇi vaneṣu hyuṣitā vayam
|
chadmanā śaradaṃ caikāṃ dhṛtarāṣṭrasya śāsanāt ||9||

10sthātā naḥ samaye tasmin dhṛtarāṣṭra iti prabho
|
nāhāsma samayaṃ kṛṣṇa taddhi no brāhmaṇā viduḥ ||10||

11gṛddho rājā dhṛtarāṣṭraḥ svadharmaṃ nānupaśyati
|
vaśyatvāt putragṛddhitvānmandasyānveti śāsanam ||11||

12suyodhanamate tiṣṭhan rājā'smāsu janārdana
|
mithyā carati lubdhaḥ san caran hi priyamātmanaḥ||12||

13ito duḥkhataraṃ kiṃ nu yadahaṃ mātaraṃ tataḥ
|
saṃvidhātuṃ na śaknomi mitrāṇāṃ vā janārdana ||13||

14kāśibhiścedipāñcālairmatsyaiśca madhusūdana
|
bhavatā caiva nāthena pañca grāmā vṛtā mayā ||14||

15avisthalaṃ vṛkasthalaṃ mākandī vāraṇāvatam
|
avasānaṃ ca govinda kañcidevātra pañcamam ||15||

16pañca nastāta dīyantāṃ grāmā vā nagarāṇi vā
|
vasema sahitā yeṣu mā ca no bharatā naśan ||16||

17na ca tānapi duṣṭātmā dhārtarāṣṭro'numanyate
|
svāmyamātmani matvā'sāvato duḥkhataraṃ nu kim ||17||

18kule jātasya vṛddhasya paravitteṣu gṛddhyataḥ
|
lobhaḥ prajñānamāhanti prajñā hanti hatā hriyam ||18||

19hrīrhatā bādhate dharmaṃ dharmo hanti hataḥ śriyam
|
śrīrhatā puruṣaṃ hanti puruṣasyādhanaṃ vadhaḥ ||19||

20adhanāddhi nivartante jñātayaḥ suhṛdo dvijāḥ
|
apuṣpāda‌phalādvṛkṣādyathā kṛṣṇa patatriṇaḥ ||20||

21etacca maraṇaṃ tāta yanmattaḥ patitādiva
|
jñātayo vinivartante pretasattvādivāsavaḥ ||21||

22nātaḥ pāpīyasīṃ kāñcidavasthāṃ śambaro'bravīt
|
yatra naivādya na prātarbhojanaṃ pratidṛśyate ||22||

23dhanamāhuḥ paraṃ dharmaṃ dhane sarvaṃ pratiṣṭhitam
|
jīvanti dhanino loke mṛtā ye tvadhanā narāḥ ||23||

24ye dhanādapakarṣanti naraṃ svabalamāsthitāḥ
|
te dharmamarthaṃ kāmaṃ ca pramathnanti naraṃ ca tam ||24||

25etāmavasthāṃ prāpyaike maraṇaṃ vavrire janāḥ
|
grāmāyaike vanāyaike nāśāyaike pravavrajuḥ ||25||

26unmādameke puṣyanti yāntyanye dviṣatāṃ vaśam
|
dāsyameke ca gacchanti pareṣāmarthahetunā ||26||

27āpadevāsya maraṇāt puruṣasya garīyasī
|
śriyo vināśastaddhyasya nimittaṃ dharmakāmayoḥ ||27||

28yadasya dharmyaṃ maraṇaṃ śāśvataṃ lokavartma tat
|
samantāt sarvabhūtānāṃ na tadatyeti kaścana ||28||

29na tathā bādhyate kṛṣṇa prakṛtyā nirdhano janaḥ
|
yathā bhadrāṃ śriyaṃ prāpya tayā hīnaḥ sukhaidhitaḥ ||29||

30sa tadā''tmāparādhena saṃprāpto vyasanaṃ mahat
|
sendrān garhayate devānnātmānaṃ ca kathañcana ||30||

31na cāsya sarvaśāstrāṇi prabhavanti nibarhaṇe
|
so'bhikrudhyati bhṛtyānāṃ suhṛdaścābhyasūyati ||31||

32tattadā manyurevaiti sa bhūyaḥ saṃpramuhyati
|
sa mohavaśamāpannaḥ krūraṃ karma niṣevate ||32||

33pāpakarmatayā caiva saṅkaraṃ tena puṣyati
|
saṅkaro narakāyaiva sā kāṣṭhā pāpakarmaṇām ||33||

34nacetprabudhyate kṛṣṇa narakāyaiva gacchati
|
tasya prabodhaḥ prajñaiva prajñācakṣustariṣyati ||34||

35prajñālābhe hi puruṣaḥ śāstrāṇyevānvavekṣate
|
śāstraniṣṭhaḥ punardharmaṃ tasya hrīraṅgamuttamam ||35||

36hrīmān hi pāpaṃ pradveṣṭi tasya śrīrabhivardhate
|
śrīmān sa yāvadbhavati tāvadbhavati pūruṣaḥ ||36||

37dharmanityaḥ praśāntātmā kāryayogavahaḥ sadā
|
nādharme kurute buddhiṃ na ca pāpe pravartate ||37||

38ahrīko vā vimūḍho vā naiva strī na punaḥ pumān
|
nāsyādhikāro dharme'sti yathā śūdrastathaiva saḥ ||38||

39hrīmānavati devāṃśca pitṝnātmānameva ca
|
tenāmṛtatvaṃ vrajati sā kāṣṭhā puṇyakarmaṇām ||39||

40tadidaṃ mayi te dṛṣṭaṃ pratyakṣaṃ madhusūdana
|
yathā rājyāt paribhraṣṭo vasāmi vasatīrimāḥ ||40||

41te vayaṃ na śriyaṃ hātumalaṃ nyāyena kenacit
|
atra no yatamānānāṃ vadhaścedapi sādhu tat ||41||

42tatra naḥ prathamaḥ kalpo yadvayaṃ te ca mādhava
|
praśāntāḥ śamabhūtāśca śriyaṃ tāmaśnuvīmahi ||42||

43tatraiṣā paramā kāṣṭhā raudrakarmakṣayodayā
|
yadvayaṃ kauravān hatvā tāni rāṣṭrāṇyavāpnumaḥ ||43||

44ye punaḥ syurasambaddhā anāryāḥ kṛṣṇa śatravaḥ
|
teṣāmapyavadhaḥ kāryaḥ kiṃ punarye syurīdṛśāḥ ||44||

45jñātayaścaiva bhūyiṣṭhāḥ sahāyā guravaśca naḥ
|
teṣāṃ vadho'tipāpīyān kiṃ nu yuddhe'sti śobhanam ||45||

46pāpaḥ kṣatriyadharmo'yaṃ vayaṃ ca kṣatrabandhavaḥ
|
sa naḥ svadharmo dharmo vā vṛttiranyā vigarhitā ||46||

47śūdraḥ karoti śuśrūṣāṃ vaiśyā vai paṇyajīvikāḥ
|
vayaṃ vadhena jīvāmaḥ kapālaṃ brāhmaṇairvṛtam ||47||

48kṣatriyaḥ kṣatriyaṃ hanti matsyo matsyena jīvati
|
śvā śvānaṃ hanti dāśārha paśya dharmo yathāgataḥ ||48||

49yuddhe kṛṣṇa kalirnityaṃ prāṇāḥ sīdanti saṃyuge
|
balaṃ tu nītimādhāya yudhye jayaparājayau ||49||

50nātmacchandena bhūtānāṃ jīvitaṃ maraṇaṃ tathā
|
nāpyakāle sukhaṃ prāpyaṃ duḥkhaṃ vā'pi yadūttama ||50||

51eko hyapi bahun hanti ghnantyekaṃ bahavo'pyuta
|
śūraṃ kāpuruṣo hanti ayaśasvī yaśasvinam ||51||

52jayo naivobhayordṛṣṭo nobhayośca parājayaḥ
|
tathaivāpacayo dṛṣṭo vyapayāne kṣayavyayau ||52||

53sarvathā vṛjinaṃ yuddhaṃ ko ghnanna pratihanyate
|
hatasya ca hṛṣīkeśa samau jayaparājayau ||53||

54parājayaśca maraṇānmanye naiva viśiṣyate
|
yasya syādvijayaḥ kṛṣṇa tasyāpyapacayo dhruvam ||54||

55antato dayitaṃ ghnanti kecidapyapare janāḥ
|
tasyāṅga balahīnasya putrān bhrātṝnapaśyataḥ ||55||

56nirvedo jīvite kṛṣṇa sarvataścopajāyate
|
ye hyeva dhīrā hrīmanta āryāḥ karuṇavedinaḥ ||56||

57ta eva yuddhe hanyante yavīyān mucyate janaḥ
|
hatvā'pyanuśayo nityaṃ parānapi janārdana ||57||

58anubandhaśca pāpo'tra śeṣaścāpyavaśiṣyate
|
śeṣo hi balamāsādya na śeṣamanuśeṣayet ||58||

59sarvocchede ca yatate vairasyāntavidhitsayā
|
jayo vairaṃ prasṛjati duḥkhamāste parājitaḥ ||59||

60sukhaṃ praśāntaḥ svapiti hitvā jayaparājayau
|
jātavairaśca puruṣo duḥkhaṃ svapiti nityadā ||60||

61anirvṛttena manasā sasarpa iva veśmani
|
utsādayati yaḥ sarvaṃ yaśasā sa vimucyate ||61||

62akīrtiṃ sarvabhūteṣu śāśvatīṃ sa niyacchati
|
na hi vairāṇi śāmyanti dīrghakāladhṛtānyapi ||62||

63ākhyātāraśca vidyante pumāṃścedvidyate kule
|
na cāpi vairaṃ vaireṇa keśava vyupaśāmyati ||63||

64haviṣā'gniryathā kṛṣṇa bhūya evābhivardhate
|
ato'nyathā nāsti śāntirnityamantaramantataḥ ||64||

65antaraṃ lipsamānānāmayaṃ doṣo nirantaraḥ
|
pauruṣe yo hi balavānādhirhṛdayabādhanaḥ
|
tasya tyāgena vā śāntirmaraṇenāpi vā bhavet ||65||

66athavā mūlaghātena dviṣatāṃ madhusūdana
|
phalanirvṛttiriddhā syānna nṛśaṃsataraṃ bhavet ||66||

67yā tu tyāgena śāntiḥ syāttadṛte vadha eva saḥ
|
saṃśayācca samucchedāddviṣatāmātmanastathā ||67||

68na ca tyaktuṃ tadicchāmo na cecchāmaḥ kulakṣayam
|
atra yā praṇipātena śāntiḥ saiva garīyasī ||68||

69sarvathā yatamānānāmayuddhamabhikāṅkṣatām
|
sāntve pratihate yuddhaṃ prasiddhaṃ nāparākramaḥ ||69||

70pratighātena sāntvasya dāruṇaṃ saṃpravartate
|
tacchunāmiva sampāte paṇḍitairupalakṣitam ||70||

71lāṅgūlacālanaṃ kṣveḍā prativāco vivartanam
|
dantadarśanamārāvastato yuddhaṃ pravartate ||71||

72tatra yo balavān kṛṣṇa jitvā so'tti tadāmiṣam
|
evameva manuṣyeṣu viśeṣo nāsti kaścana ||72||

73sarvathā tvetaducitaṃ durbaleṣu balīyasām
|
anādaro virodhaśca praṇipātī hi durbalaḥ ||73||

74pitā rājā ca vṛddhaśca sarvathā mānamarhati
|
tasmānmānyaśca pūjyaśca dhṛtarāṣṭro janārdana ||74||

75putrasnehaśca balavān dhṛtarāṣṭrasya mādhava
|
sa putravaśamāpannaḥ praṇipātaṃ prahāsyati ||75||

76tatra kiṃ manyase kṛṣṇa prāptakālamanantaram
|
kathamarthācca dharmācca na hīyema hi mādhava ||76||

77īdṛśe'tyarthakṛcchre'smin kamanyaṃ madhusūdana
|
upasaṃpraṣṭumarhāmi tvāmṛte puruṣottama ||77||

78priyaśca priyakāmaśca gatijñaḥ sarvakarmaṇām
|
ko hi kṛṣṇāsti nastvādṛk sarvaniścayavitsuhṛt ||78||

79vaiśampāyana uvāca|
evamuktaḥ pratyuvāca dharmarājaṃ janārdanaḥ
|
ubhayoreva vāmarthe yāsyāmi kurusaṃsadam ||79||

80śamaṃ tatra labheyaṃ cedyuṣmadarthamahāpayan
|
puṇyaṃ me sumahadrājaṃścaritaṃ syānmahāphalam ||80||

81mocayeyaṃ mṛtyupāśāt saṃrabdhān kurusṛñjayān
|
pāṇḍavān dhārtarāṣṭrāṃśca sarvāṃ ca pṛthivīmimām ||81||

82yudhiṣṭhira uvāca|
na mamaitanmataṃ kṛṣṇa yattvaṃ yāyāḥ kurūn prati
|
suyodhanaḥ sūktamapi na kariṣyati te vacaḥ ||82||

83sametaṃ pārthivaṃ kṣatraṃ duryodhanavaśānugam
|
teṣāṃ madhyāvataraṇaṃ tava kṛṣṇa na rocaye ||83||

84na hi naḥ prīṇayeddravyaṃ na devattvaṃ kutaḥ sukham
|
na ca sarvāmaraiśvaryaṃ tava droheṇa mādhava ||84||

85śrībhagavānuvāca
|
jānāmyetāṃ mahārāja dhārtarāṣṭrasya pāpatām
|
avācyāstu bhaviṣyāmaḥ sarvaloke mahīkṣitām ||85||

86na cāpi mama paryāptāḥ sahitāḥ sarvapārthivāḥ
|
kruddhasya saṃyuge sthātuṃ siṃhasyevetare mṛgāḥ ||86||

87atha cette pravarteta mayi kiñcidasāmpratam
|
nirdaheyaṃ kurūn sarvāniti me dhīyate matiḥ ||87||

88na jātu gamanaṃ pārtha bhavettatra nirarthakam
|
arthaprāptiḥ kadācit syādantato vā'pyavācyatā ||88||

89yudhiṣṭhira uvāca|
yattubhyaṃ rocate kṛṣṇa svasti prāpnuhi kauravān
|
kṛtārthaṃ svastimantaṃ tvāṃ drakṣyāmi punarāgatam ||89||

90viṣvaksena kurūn gatvā bharatān śamayan prabho
|
yathā sarve sumanasaḥ saha syāma sucetasaḥ ||90||

91bhrātā cāsi sakhā cāsi bībhatsormama ca priyaḥ
|
sauhṛdenāviśaṅkyo'si svasti prāpnuhi bhūtaye ||91||

92asmān vettha parān vettha vetthārthānvettha bhāṣitum
|
yadyadasmaddhitaṃ kṛṣṇa tattadvācyaḥ suyodhanaḥ ||92||

93yadyadharmeṇa saṃyuktamupapadyeddhitaṃ vacaḥ
|
tattatkeśava bhāṣethāḥ sāntvaṃ vā yadi vetarat||93||
