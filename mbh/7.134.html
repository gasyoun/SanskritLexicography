Mahābhārata 7.134 (Droṇaparvan)

1sañjaya uvāca|
sarvathā virathaḥ karṇaḥ punarbhīmena nirjitaḥ
|
rathamanyaṃ samāsthāya punarvivyādha pāṇḍavam||1||

2mahāgajāvivāsādya viṣāṇāgraiḥ parasparam
|
śaraiḥ pūrṇāyatotsṛṣṭairanyonyamabhijaghnatuḥ||2||

3atha karṇaḥ śaravrātairbhīmasenaṃ samārpayat
|
nanāda ca mahānādaṃ punarvivyādha corasi ||3||

4taṃ bhīmo daśabhirbāṇaiḥ pratyavidhyada‌jihmagaiḥ
|
punarvivyādha saptatyā śarāṇāṃ nataparvaṇām||4||

5karṇaṃ tu navabhirbhīmo bhittvā rājaṃstanāntare
|
dhvajamekena vivyādha sāyakena śitena ha ||5||

6sāyakānāṃ tataḥ pārthastriṣaṣṭyā pratyavidhyata
|
totrairiva mahānāgaṃ kaśābhiriva vājinam||6||

7so'tividdho mahārāja pāṇḍavena yaśasvinā
|
sṛkkiṇī lelihanvīraḥ krodharaktāntalocanaḥ||7||

8tataḥ śaraṃ mahārāja sarvakāyāvadāraṇam
|
prāhiṇodbhīmasenāya balāyendra ivāśanim||8||

9sa nirbhidya raṇe pārthaṃ sūtaputradhanuścyutaḥ
|
agacchaddārayan bhūmiṃ citrapuṅkhaḥ śilīmukhaḥ||9||

10tato bhīmo mahābāhuḥ krodhasaṃraktalocanaḥ
|
vajrakalpāṃ catuṣkiṣkuṃ gurvīṃ rukmāṅgadāṃ gadām||10||

11prāhiṇotsūtaputrāya ṣaḍasrāmavicārayan
|
tayā jaghānādhiratheḥ sadaśvānsādhuvāhinaḥ||11||

12gadayā bhārataḥ kruddho vajreṇendra ivāsurān
|
tato bhīmo mahābāhuḥ kṣurābhyāṃ bharatarṣabha||12||

13dhvajamādhiratheśchittvā sūtamabhyahanaccharaiḥ
|
hatāśvasūtamutsṛjya sarathaṃ patitadhvajam ||13||

14visphārayandhanuḥ karṇastasthau bhārata durmanāḥ
|
tatrādbhutamapaśyāma rādheyasya parākramam ||14||

15viratho rathināṃ śreṣṭho vārayāmāsa yadripum
|
virathaṃ taṃ naraśreṣṭhaṃ dṛṣṭvā''dhirathimāhave ||15||

16duryodhanastato rājannabhyabhāṣata durmukham
|
eṣa durmukha rādheyo bhīmena virathīkṛtaḥ ||16||

17taṃ rathena naraśreṣṭhaṃ saṃpādaya mahāratham
|
tato duryodhanavacaḥ śrutvā bhārata durmukhaḥ||17||

18tvaramāṇo'bhyayātkarṇaṃ bhīmaṃ cāvārayaccharaiḥ
|
durmukhaṃ prekṣya saṃgrāme sūtaputrapadānugam||18||

19vāyuputraḥ prahṛṣṭo'bhūtsṛkkiṇī parisaṃlihan
|
tataḥ karṇaṃ mahārāja vārayitvā śilīmukhaiḥ||19||

20durmukhāya rathaṃ tūrṇaṃ preṣayāmāsa pāṇḍavaḥ
|
tasminkṣaṇe mahārāja navabhirnataparvabhiḥ||20||

21sumukhairdurmukhaṃ bhīmaḥ śarairninye yamakṣayam
|
tatastamevādhirathiḥ syandanaṃ durmukhe hate||21||

22āsthitaḥ prababhau rājan dīpyamāna ivāṃśumān
|
śayānaṃ bhinnamarmāṇaṃ durmukhaṃ śoṇitokṣitam||22||

23dṛṣṭvā karṇo'śrupūrṇākṣo muhūrtaṃ nābhyavartata
|
taṃ gatāsumatikramya kṛtvā karṇaḥ pradakṣiṇam||23||

24dīrghamuṣṇaṃ śvasanvīro na kiñcitpratyapadyata
|
tasmiṃstu vivare rājan nārācāngārdhravāsasaḥ||24||

25prāhiṇotsūtaputrāya bhīmasenaścaturdaśa
|
te tasya kavacaṃ bhittvā svarṇacitraṃ mahaujasaḥ||25||

26hemapuṅkhā mahārāja vyaśobhanta diśo daśa
|
apibansūtaputrasya śoṇitaṃ raktabhojanāḥ||26||

27kruddhā iva manuṣyendra bhujaṅgāḥ kālacoditāḥ
|
prasarpamāṇā medinyāṃ te vyarocanta mārgaṇāḥ||27||

28ardhapraviṣṭāḥ saṃrabdhā bilānīva mahoragāḥ
|
taṃ pratyavidhyadrādheyo jāṃbūnada‌vibhūṣitaiḥ||28||

29caturdaśabhiratyugrairnārācairavicārayan
|
te bhīmasenasya bhujaṃ savyaṃ nirbhidya patriṇaḥ||29||

30prāviśanmedinīṃ bhīmāḥ krauñcaṃ patrarathā iva
|
te vyarocanta nārācāḥ praviśanto vasuṃdharām||30||

31gacchatyastaṃ dinakare dīpyamānā ivāṃśavaḥ
|
sa nirbhinno raṇe bhīmo nārācairmarmabhedibhiḥ ||31||

32susrāva rudhiraṃ bhūri parvataḥ salilaṃ yathā
|
sa bhīmastribhirāyattaḥ sūtaputraṃ patatribhiḥ||32||

33suparṇavegairvivyādha sārathiṃ cāsya saptabhiḥ
|
sa vihvalo mahārāja karṇo bhīmaśarāhataḥ||33||

34prādravajjavanairaśvai raṇaṃ hitvā mahābhayāt
|
bhīmasenastu visphārya cāpaṃ hemapariṣkṛtam||34||

35āhave'tiratho'tiṣṭhajjvalanniva hutāśanaḥ||35||
