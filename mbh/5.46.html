Mahābhārata 5.46 (Udyogaparvan)

1sanatsujāta uvāca|
yattacchukraṃ mahajjyotirdīpyamānaṃ mahadyaśaḥ
|
tadvai devā upāsate tasmāt sūryo virājate
|
yoginastaṃ prapaśyanti bhagavantaṃ sanātanam ||1||

2śukrādbrahma prabhavati brahma śukreṇa vardhate
|
tacchukraṃ jyotiṣāṃ madhye'taptaṃ tapati tāpanam
|
yoginastaṃ prapaśyanti bhagavantaṃ sanātanam ||2||

3āpo'tha adbhyaḥ salilasya madhye ubhau devau śiśriyāte'ntarikṣe
|
atandritaḥ saviturvivasvānubhau bibharti pṛthivīṃ divaṃ ca
|
yoginastaṃ prapaśyanti bhagavantaṃ sanātanam ||3||

4ubhau ca devau pṛthivīṃ divaṃ ca diśaśca śukraṃ bhuvanaṃ bibharti
|
tasmāddiśaḥ saritaśca sravanti tasmāt samudrā vihitā mahāntaḥ
|
yoginastaṃ prapaśyanti bhagavantaṃ sanātanam ||4||

5cakre rathasya tiṣṭhanto'dhruvasyāvyayakarmaṇaḥ
|
ketumantaṃ vahantyaśvāstaṃ divyamajaraṃ divi
|
yoginastaṃ prapaśyanti bhagavantaṃ sanātanam ||5||

6na sādṛśye tiṣṭhati rūpamasya na cakṣuṣā paśyati kaścidenam | manīṣayā'tho manasā hṛdā ca ya enaṃ viduramṛtāste bhavanti
|
yoginastaṃ prapaśyanti bhagavantaṃ sanātanam ||6||

7dvādaśapūgāṃ saritaṃ pibanto devarakṣitām
|
madhvīkṣantaśca te tasyāḥ sañcaranti ghoram
|
yoginastaṃ prapaśyanti bhagavantaṃ sanātanam ||7||

8tadardhamāsaṃ pibati sañcitya bhramaro madhu
|
īśānaḥ sarvabhūteṣu havirbhūtamakalpayat
|
yoginastaṃ prapaśyanti bhagavantaṃ sanātanam ||8||

9hiraṇyaparṇamaśvatthamabhipadya hyapakṣakāḥ
|
te tatra pakṣiṇo bhūtvā prapatanti yathā diśam
|
yoginastaṃ prapaśyanti bhagavantaṃ sanātanam ||9||

10pūrṇāt pūrṇānyuddharanti pūrṇāt pūrṇāni cakrire
|
haranti pūrṇāt pūrṇāni pūrṇamevāvaśiṣyate|| yoginastaṃ prapaśyanti bhagavantaṃ sanātanam ||10||

11tasmādvai vāyurāyātastasmiṃśca prayataḥ sadā
|
tasmādagniśca somaśca tasmiṃśca prāṇa ātataḥ ||11||

12sarvameva tato vidyāttattadvaktuṃ na śaknumaḥ
|
yoginastaṃ prapaśyanti bhagavantaṃ sanātanam ||12||

13apānaṃ girati prāṇaḥ prāṇaṃ girati candramāḥ
|
ādityo girate candramādityaṃ girate paraḥ
|
yoginastaṃ prapaśyanti bhagavantaṃ sanātanam ||13||

14ekaṃ pādaṃ notkṣipati salilāddhaṃsa uccaran
|
taṃ cetsatatamūrdhvāya na mṛtyurnāmṛtaṃ bhavet
|
yoginastaṃ prapaśyanti bhagavantaṃ sanātanam ||14||

15aṅguṣṭhamātraḥ puruṣo'ntarātmā liṅgasya yogena sa yāti nityam
|
tamīśamīḍyamanukalpamādyaṃ paśyanti mūḍhā na virājamānam
|
yoginastaṃ prapaśyanti bhagavantaṃ sanātanam ||15||

16asādhanā vā'pi sasādhanā vā samānametaddṛśyate mānuṣeṣu
|
samānametadamṛtasyetarasya muktāstatra madhva utsaṃ samāpuḥ
|
yoginastaṃ prapaśyanti bhagavantaṃ sanātanam ||16||

17ubhau lokau vidyayā vyāpya yāti tadā hutaṃ cāhutamagnihotram
|
mā te brāhmī laghutāmādadhīta prajñānaṃ syānnāma dhīrā labhante
|
yoginastaṃ prapaśyanti bhagavantaṃ sanātanam ||17||

18evaṃrūpo mahātmā sa pāvakaṃ puruṣo giran
|
yo vai taṃ puruṣaṃ veda tasyehārtho na riṣyate
|
yoginastaṃ prapaśyanti bhagavantaṃ sanātanam ||18||

19yaḥ sahasraṃ sahasrāṇāṃ pakṣān santatya sampatet
|
madhyame madhya āgacchedapi cetsyānmanojavaḥ
|
yoginastaṃ prapaśyanti bhagavantaṃ sanātanam ||19||

20na darśane tiṣṭhati rūpamasya paśyanti cainaṃ suviśuddhasattvāḥ
|
hito manīṣī manasā na tapyate ye pravrajeyuramṛtāste bhavanti
|
yoginastaṃ prapaśyanti bhagavantaṃ sanātanam ||20||

21gūhanti sarpā iva gahvarāṇi svaśikṣayā svena vṛttena martyāḥ
|
teṣu pramuhyanti janā vimūḍhā yathā'dhvānaṃ mohayante bhayāya
|
yoginastaṃ prapaśyanti bhagavantaṃ sanātanam ||21||

22nāhaṃ sadā'satkṛtaḥ syāṃ na mṛtyurnacāmṛtyuramṛtaṃ me kutaḥ syāt
|
satyānṛte satyasamānabandhane sataśca yonirasataścaika eva
|
yoginastaṃ prapaśyanti bhagavantaṃ sanātanam ||22||

23na sādhunā nota asādhunā vā'samānametaddṛśyate mānuṣeṣu
|
samānametadamṛtasya vidyādevaṃ yukto madhu tadvai parīpset
|
yoginastaṃ prapaśyanti bhagavantaṃ sanātanam ||23||

24nāsyātivādā hṛdayaṃ tāpayanti nānadhītaṃ nāhutamagnihotram
|
mano brāhmī laghutāmādadhīta prajñāṃ cāsmai nāma dhīrā labhante
|
yoginastaṃ prapaśyanti bhagavantaṃ sanātanam ||24||

25evaṃ yaḥ sarvabhūteṣu ātmānamanupaśyati
|
anyatrānyatra yukteṣu kiṃ sa śocettataḥ param ||25||

26yathodapāne mahati sarvataḥ samplutodake
|
evaṃ sarveṣu vedeṣu brāhmaṇasya vijānataḥ ||26||

27aṅguṣṭhamātraḥ puruṣo mahātmā na dṛśyate sauhṛdi saṃniviṣṭaḥ
|
ajaścaro divārātramatandritaśca sa taṃ matvā kavirāste prasannaḥ ||27||

28ahameva smṛto mātā pitā putro'smyahaṃ punaḥ
|
ātmā'hamapi sarvasya yacca nāsti yadasti ca ||28||

29pitāmaho'smi sthaviraḥ pitā putraśca bhārata
|
mamaiva yūyamātmasthā na me yūyaṃ na vo vayam ||29||

30ātmaiva sthānaṃ mama janma cātmā otaproto'hamajarapratiṣṭhaḥ
|
ajaścaro divārātramatandrito'haṃ māṃ vijñāya kavirāste prasannaḥ||30||

31aṇoraṇīyān sumanāḥ sarvabhūteṣu jāgrati
|
pitaraṃ sarvabhūteṣu puṣkare nihitaṃ viduḥ ||31||
