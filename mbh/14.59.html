Mahābhārata 14.59 (Āśvamedhikaparvan)

1janamejaya uvāca|
uttaṅkasya varaṃ dattvā govindo dvijasattama
|
ata ūrdhvaṃ mahābāhuḥ kiṃ cakāra mahāyaśāḥ||1||

2vaiśampāyana uvāca|
uttaṅkasya varaṃ dattvā prāyātsātyakinā saha
|
dvārakāmeva govindaḥ śīghravegairmahāhayaiḥ||2||

3sarāṃsi saritaścaiva vanāni ca girīṃstathā
|
atikramyāsasādātha ramyāṃ dvāravatīṃ purīm||3||

4vartamāne mahārāja mahe raivatakasya ca
|
upāyātpuṇḍarīkākṣo yuyudhānānupastadā||4||

5alaṅkṛtastu sa girirnārūpairvicitritaiḥ
|
babhau ratnamayaiḥ kośaiḥ saṃvṛtaḥ puruṣarṣabha||5||

6kāñcanasragbhiragryābhiḥ sumanobhistathaiva ca
|
vāsobhiśca mahāśailaḥ kalpavṛkṣaistathaiva ca||6||

7dīpavṛkṣaiśca sauvarṇairabhīkṣṇamupaśobhitaḥ
|
guhānirjharadeśeṣu divābhūto babhūva ha||7||

8patākābhirvicitrābhiḥ saghaṇṭābhiḥ samantataḥ
|
pumbhiḥ strībhiśca saṅghuṣṭaḥ pragīta iva cābhavat||8||

9atīva prekṣaṇīyo'bhūnmerurmunigaṇairiva
|
mattānāṃ hṛṣṭarūpāṇāṃ strīṇāṃ puṃsāṃ ca bhārata||9||

10gāyatāṃ parvatendrasya divaspṛgiva niḥsvanaḥ
|
pramattamattasammattakṣveḍitotkruṣṭasaṅkulaḥ||10||

11tathā kilakilāśabdairbhūdharo'bhūnmanoharaḥ
|
vipaṇāpaṇavānramyo bhakṣyabhojyavihāravān||11||

12vastramālyotkarayuto vīṇāveṇumṛdaṅgavān
|
surāmairaiyamiśreṇa bhakṣyabhojyena caiva ha||12||

13dīnāndhakṛpaṇādibhyo dīyamānena cāniśam
|
babhau paramakalyāṇo mahastasya mahāgireḥ||13||

14puṇyāvasathavānvīra puṇyakṛdbhirniṣevitaḥ
|
vihāro vṛṣṇivīrāṇāṃ mahe raivatakasya ha||14||

15sa nago veśmasaṅkīrṇo devaloka ivābabhau
|
tadā ca kṛṣṇa sānnidhyamāsādya bharatarṣabha||15||

16śakrasadmapratīkāśo babhūva sa hi śailarāṭ
|
tataḥ sampūjyamānaḥ sa viveśa bhavanaṃ śubham||16||

17govindaḥ sātyakiścaiva jagāma bhavanaṃ svakam
|
viveśa ca prahṛṣṭātmā cirakālapravāsataḥ||17||

18kṛtvā nasukaraṃ karma dānaveṣviva vāsavaḥ
|
upāyāntaṃ tu vārṣṇeyaṃ bhojavṛṣṇyandhakāstathā||18||

19abhyagacchanmahātmānaṃ devā iva śatakratum
|
sa tānabhyarcya medhāvī pṛṣṭvā ca kuśalaṃ tadā
|
abhyavādayata prītaḥ pitaraṃ mātaraṃ tadā||19||

20tābhyāṃ sa sampariṣvaktaḥ sāntvitaśca mahābhujaḥ
|
upopaviṣṭaiḥ sarvaistairvṛṣṇibhiḥ parivāritaḥ||20||

21sa viśrānto mahātejāḥ kṛtapādāvanejanaḥ
|
kathayāmāsa tatsarvaṃ pṛṣṭaḥ pitrā mahāhavam||21||
