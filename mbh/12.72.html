Mahābhārata 12.72 (Śāntiparvan)

1bhīṣma uvāca|
ya eva tu sato rakṣedasataśca nivartayet
|
sa eva rājñaḥ kartavyo rājan rājapurohitaḥ||1||

2atrāpyudāharantīmamitihāsaṃ purātanam
|
purūravasa ailasya saṃvādaṃ mātariśvanaḥ||2||

3purūravā uvāca| kutaḥsvidbrāhmaṇo jāto varṇāścāpi kutastrayaḥ
|
kasmācca bhavati śreṣṭhastanme vyākhyātumarhasi||3||

4mātariśvovāca
|
brāhmaṇo mukhataḥ sṛṣṭo brahmaṇo rājasattama
|
bāhubhyāṃ kṣatriyaḥ sṛṣṭa ūrubhyāṃ vaiśya eva ca||4||

5varṇānāṃ paricaryārthaṃ trayāṇāṃ bharatarṣabha
|
varṇaścaturthaḥ paścāt tu padbhyāṃ śūdro vinirmitaḥ||5||

6brāhmaṇo jāyamāno hi pṛthivyāmanujāyate
|
īśvaraḥ sarvabhūtānāṃ dharmakośasya guptaye||6||

7ataḥ pṛthivyā yantāraṃ kṣatriyaṃ daṇḍadhāriṇam
|
dvitīyaṃ daṇḍamakarotprajānāmanuguptaye||7||

8vaiśyastu dhanadhānyena trīnvarṇānvidhṛyādimān
|
śūdro hyetānparicarediti brahmānuśāsanam||8||

9aila uvāca| dvijasya kṣatrabandhorvā kasyeyaṃ pṛthivī bhavet
|
dharmataḥ saha vittena samyagvāyo pracakṣva me||9||

10vāyuruvāca| viprasya sarvamevaitadyatkiñcijjagatīgatam
|
jyeṣṭhenābhijaneneha taddharmakuśalā viduḥ||10||

11svameva brāhmaṇo bhuṃkte svaṃ vaste svaṃ dadāti ca
|
gururhi sarvavarṇānāṃ jyeṣṭhaḥ śreṣṭhaśca vai dvijaḥ||11||

12patyabhāve yathaiva strī devaraṃ kurute patim
|
eṣa te prathamaḥ kalpa āpadyanyo bhavedataḥ||12||

13yadi svarga paraṃ sthānaṃ svadharmaṃ parimārgasi
|
yat kiñcijjayase bhūmiṃ brāhmaṇāya nivedaya||13||

14śrutavṛttopapannāya dharmajñāya tapasvine
|
svadharmaparitṛptāya yo na vittaparo bhavet||14||

15yo rājānaṃ nayedbuddhyā sarvataḥ paripūrṇayā
|
brāhmaṇo hi kule jātaḥ kṛtaprajño vinītavān||15||

16śreyo nayati rājānaṃ bruvaṃścitrāṃ sarasvatīm
|
rājā carati yaṃ dharmaṃ brāhmaṇena nidarśitam||16||

17śuśrūṣuranahaṃvādī kṣatradharmavrate sthitaḥ
|
tāvatā satkṛtaḥ prājñaściraṃ yaśasi tiṣṭhati||17||

18tasya dharmasya sarvasya bhāgī rājapurohitaḥ
|
evameva prajāḥ sarvā rājānamabhisaṃśritāḥ||18||

19samyagvṛttāḥ svadharmasthā na kutaścidbhayānvitāḥ
|
rāṣṭre caranti yaṃ dharmaṃ rājñā sādhvabhirakṣitāḥ||19||

20caturthaṃ tasya dharmasya rājā bhāgaṃ tu vindati
|
devā manuṣyāḥ pitaro gandharvoragarākṣasāḥ||20||

21yajñamevopajīvanti nāsti ceṣṭamarājake
|
tato dattena jīvanti devatāḥ pitarastathā||21||

22rājanyevāsya dharmasya yogakṣemaḥ pratiṣṭhitaḥ
|
chāyāyāmapsu vāyau ca sukhamuṣṇe'dhigacchati||22||

23agnau vāsasi sūrye ca sukhaṃ śīte'dhigacchati
|
śabde, sparśe, rase, rūpe, gandhe ca ramate manaḥ||23||

24teṣu bhogeṣu sarveṣu na bhīto labhate sukham
|
abhayasya hi yo dātā tasyaiva sumahatphalam
|
na hi prāṇasamaṃ dānaṃ triṣu lokeṣu vidyate||24||

25indro rājā yamo rājā dharmo rājā tathaiva ca
|
rājā bibharti rūpāṇi rājñā sarvamidaṃ dhṛtam||25||
