Mahābhārata 7.98 (Droṇaparvan)

1dhṛtarāṣṭra uvāca|
bāṇe tasminnikṛtte tu dhṛṣṭadyumne ca mokṣite
|
tena vṛṣṇipravīreṇa yuyudhānena sañjaya ||1||

2amarṣito maheṣvāsaḥ sarvaśastrabhṛtāṃ varaḥ
|
naravyāghraḥ śineḥ pautre droṇaḥ kimakarodyudhi ||2||

3sañjaya uvāca|
sampradrutaḥ krodhaviṣo vyāditāsyaśarāsanaḥ
|
tīkṣṇadhāreṣudaśanaḥ sitanārācadaṃṣṭravān ||3||

4saṃrambhāmarṣatāmrākṣo mahoraga iva śvasan
|
naravīraḥ prabhuditaḥ śoṇairaśvairmahājavaiḥ ||4||

5utpatadbhirivākāśe krāmadbhiriva parvatam
|
rukmapuṅkhāñcharānasyanyuyudhānamupādravat ||5||

6śarapātamahāvarṣaṃ rathaghoṣabalāhakam
|
kārmukākarṣavikṣepaṃ nārācabahuvidyutam ||6||

7śaktikhaḍgāśanidharaṃ krodhavegasamutthitam
|
droṇameghamanāvāryaṃ hayamārutacoditam ||7||

8dṛṣṭvaivābhipatantaṃ taṃ śūraḥ parapurañjayaḥ
|
uvāca sūtaṃ śaineyaḥ prahasanyuddhadurmadaḥ ||8||

9enaṃ vai brāhmaṇaṃ śūra svakarmaṇyanavasthitam
|
āśrayaṃ dhārtarāṣṭrasya rājño duḥkhabhayāpaham ||9||

10śīghraṃ prajavitairaśvaiḥ pratyudyāhi prahṛṣṭavat
|
ācāryaṃ rājaputrāṇāṃ satataṃ śūramāninam ||10||

11tato rajatasaṅkāśā mādhavasya hayottamāḥ
|
droṇasyābhimukhāḥ śīghramagacchan vātaraṃhasaḥ ||11||

12tatastau droṇaśainayau yuyudhāte parantapau
|
śarairanekasāhasraistāḍayantau parasparam ||12||

13iṣujālāvṛtaṃ vyoma cakratuḥ puruṣarṣabhau
|
pūrayāmāsaturvīrāvubhau daśa diśaḥ śaraiḥ ||13||

14meghāvivātapāpāye dhārābhiritaretaram
|
na sma sūryastadā bhāti na vavau ca samīraṇaḥ ||14||

15iṣujālāvṛtaṃ ghoramandhakāraṃ samantataḥ
|
anādhṛṣyamivānyeṣāṃ śūrāṇāmabhavattadā ||15||

16andhakārīkṛte loke droṇaśanayayoḥ śaraiḥ
|
tayoḥ śīghrāstraviduṣordroṇasātvatayostadā ||16||

17nāntaraṃ śaravṛṣṭīnāṃ dadṛśe narasiṃhayoḥ
|
iṣūṇāṃ sannipātena śabdo dhārābhighātajaḥ ||17||

18śuśruve śakramuktānāmaśanīnāmiva svanaḥ
|
nārācairvyatividdhānāṃ śarāṇāṃ rūpamābabhau ||18||

19āśīviṣavidaṣṭānāṃ sarpāṇāmiva bhārata
|
tayorjyātalanirghoṣaḥ śuśruve yuddhaśauṇḍayoḥ ||19||

20ajasraṃ śailaśṛṅgāṇāṃ vajreṇāhanyatāmiva
|
ubhayostau rathau rājaṃste cāśvastau ca sārathī ||20||

21rukmapuṅkhaiḥ śaraiśchinnāścitrarūpā babhustadā
|
nirmalānāmajihmānāṃ nārācānāṃ viśāmpate ||21||

22nirmuktāśīviṣābhānāṃ sampāto'bhūtsudāruṇaḥ
|
ubhayoḥ patite chatre tathaiva patitau dhvajau ||22||

23ubhau rudhirasiktāṅgāvubhau ca vijayaiṣiṇau
|
sravadbhiḥ śoṇitaṃ gātraiḥ prasrutāviva vāraṇau ||23||

24anyonyamabhyavidhyetāṃ jīvitāntakaraiḥ śaraiḥ
|
garjitotkruṣṭasannādāḥ śaṅkhadundubhiniḥsvanāḥ ||24||

25upāramanmahārāja vyājahāra na kaścana
|
tūṣṇīmbhūtānyanīkāni yodhā yuddhādupāraman ||25||

26dadarśa dvairathaṃ tābhyāṃ jātakautūhalo janaḥ
|
rathino hastiyantārā hayārohāḥ padātayaḥ ||26||

27avaikṣantācalairnetraiḥ parivārya nararṣabhau
|
hastyanīkānyatiṣṭhanta tathānīkāni vājinām ||27||

28tathaiva rathavāhinyaḥ prativyūhya vyavasthitāḥ
|
muktāvidrumacitraiśca maṇikāñcanabhūṣitaiḥ ||28||

29dhvajairābharaṇaiścitraiḥ kavacaiśca hiraṇmayaiḥ
|
vaijayantīpatākābhiḥ paristomāṅgakambalaiḥ ||29||

30vimalairniśitaiḥ śastrairhayānāṃ ca prakīrṇakaiḥ
|
jātarūpamayībhiśca rājatībhiśca mūrdhasu ||30||

31gajānāṃ kumbhamālābhirdantaveṣṭaiśca bhārata
|
sabalākāḥ sakhadyotāḥ sairāvataśatahradāḥ ||31||

32adṛśyantoṣṇaparyāye meghānāmiva vāgurāḥ
|
apaśyannasmadīyāśca te ca yaudhiṣṭhirāḥ sthitāḥ ||32||

33tadyuddhaṃ yuyudhānasya droṇasya ca mahātmanaḥ
|
vimānāgragatā devā brahmasomapurogamāḥ ||33||

34siddhacāraṇasaṅghāśca vidyādharamahoragāḥ
|
gatapratyāgatākṣepaiścitrairastravighātibhiḥ ||34||

35vividhairvismayaṃ jagmustayoḥ puruṣasiṃhayoḥ
|
hastalāghavamastreṣu darśayantau mahābalau ||35||

36anyonyamabhividhyetāṃ śaraistau droṇasātyakī
|
tato droṇasya dāśārhaḥ śarāṃściccheda saṃyuge ||36||

37patribhiḥ sudṛḍhairāśu dhanuścaiva mahādyuteḥ
|
nimeṣāntaramātreṇa bhāradvājo'paraṃ dhanuḥ ||37||

38sajyaṃ cakāra tadapi cicchedāsya ca sātyakiḥ
|
tatastvaran punardroṇo dhanurhasto vyatiṣṭhata ||38||

39sajyaṃ sajyaṃ dhanuścāsya ciccheda niśitaiḥ śaraiḥ
|
evamekaśataṃ chinnaṃ dhanuṣāṃ dṛḍhadhanvinā ||39||

40na cāntaraṃ tayordṛṣṭaṃ sandhāne cchedane'pi ca
|
tato'sya saṃyuge droṇo dṛṣṭvā karmātimānuṣam ||40||

41yuyudhānasya rājendra manasaitadacintayat
|
etadastrabalaṃ rāme kārtavīrye dhanañjaye ||41||

42bhīṣme ca puruṣavyāghre yadidaṃ sātvatāṃ vare
|
taṃ cāsya manasā droṇaḥ pūjayāmāsa vikramam ||42||

43lāghavaṃ vāsavasyeva samprekṣya dvijasattamaḥ
|
tutoṣāstravidāṃ śreṣṭhastathā devāḥ savāsavāḥ ||43||

44na tāmālakṣayāmāsurlaghutāṃ śīghracāriṇaḥ
|
devāśca yuyudhānasya gandharvāśca viśāmpate ||44||

45siddhacāraṇasaṅghāśca vidurdroṇasya karma tat
|
tato'nyaddhanurādāya droṇaḥ kṣatriyamardanaḥ ||45||

46astrairastravidāṃ śreṣṭho yodhayāmāsa bhārata
|
tasyāstrāṇyastramāyābhiḥ pratihatya sa sātyakiḥ ||46||

47jaghāna niśitairbāṇaistadadbhutamivābhavat
|
tasyātimānuṣaṃ karma dṛṣṭvā'nyairasamaṃ raṇe ||47||

48yuktaṃ yogena yogajñāstāvakāḥ samapūjayan
|
yadastramasyati droṇastadevāsyati sātyakiḥ ||48||

49tamācāryo'tha sambhrānto'yodhayacchatrutāpanaḥ
|
tataḥ kruddho mahārāja dhanurvedasya pāragaḥ ||49||

50vadhāya yuyudhānasya divyamastramudairayat
|
tadāgneyaṃ mahāghoraṃ ripughnamupalakṣya saḥ ||50||

51divyamastraṃ maheṣvāso vāruṇaṃ samudairayat
|
hāhākāro mahānāsīddṛṣṭvā divyāstradhāriṇau ||51||

52na vicerustadākāśe bhūtānyākāśagānyapi
|
astre te vāruṇāgneye tābhyāṃ bāṇasamāhite ||52||

53na yāvadabhyapadyetāṃ vyāvartadatha bhāskaraḥ
|
tato yudhiṣṭhiro rājā bhīmasenaśca pāṇḍavaḥ ||53||

54nakulaḥ sahadevaśca paryarakṣanta sātyakim
|
dhṛṣṭadyumnamukhaiḥ sārdhaṃ virāṭaśca sakekayaḥ ||54||

55matsyāḥ śālveyasenāśca droṇamājagmurañjasā
|
duḥśāsanaṃ puraskṛtya rājaputrāḥ sahasraśaḥ ||55||

56droṇamabhyupapadyanta sapatnaiḥ parivāritam
|
tato yuddhamabhūdrājaṃsteṣāṃ tava ca dhanvinām ||56||

57rajasā saṃvṛte loke śarajālasamāvṛte
|
sarvamāvignamabhavanna prājñāyata kiñcana
|
sainyena rajasā dhvaste nirmaryādamavartata ||57||
