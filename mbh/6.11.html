Mahābhārata 6.11 (Bhīṣmaparvan)

1dhṛtarāṣṭra uvāca|
jambūkhaṇḍastvayā prokto yathāvadiha sañjaya
|
viṣkambhamasya prabrūhi parimāṇaṃ tu tattvataḥ ||1||

2samudrasya pramāṇaṃ ca samyagacchidradarśanam
|
śākadvīpaṃ ca me brūhi kuśadvīpaṃ ca sañjaya ||2||

3śālmaliṃ caiva tattvena krauñcadvīpaṃ tathaiva ca
|
brūhi gāvalgaṇe sarvaṃ rāhoḥ somārkayostathā ||3||

4sañjaya uvāca|
rājansubahavo dvīpā yairidaṃ santataṃ jagat
|
sapta dvīpānpravakṣyāmi candrādityau grahaṃ tathā ||4||

5aṣṭādaśa sahasrāṇi yojanāni viśāmpate
|
ṣaṭ śatāni ca pūrṇāni viṣkambho jambuparvataḥ ||5||

6lāvaṇasya samudrasya viṣkambho dviguṇaḥ smṛtaḥ
|
nānājanapadākīrṇo maṇividrumacitritaḥ ||6||

7naikadhātuvicitraiśca parvatairupaśobhitaḥ
|
siddhacāraṇasaṅkīrṇaḥ sāgaraḥ parimaṇḍalaḥ ||7||

8śākadvīpaṃ ca vakṣyāmi yathāvadiha pārthiva
|
śṛṇu me tvaṃ yathānyāyaṃ bruvataḥ kurunandana ||8||

9jambūdvīpapramāṇena dviguṇaḥ sa narādhipa
|
viṣkambheṇa mahārāja sāgaro'pi vibhāgaśaḥ ||9||

10kṣīrodo bharataśreṣṭha yena samparivāritaḥ
|
tatra puṇyā janapadāstatra na mriyate janaḥ ||10||

11kuta eva hi durbhikṣaṃ kṣamātejoyutā hi te
|
śākadvīpasya saṅkṣepo yathāvadbharatarṣabha ||11||

12ukta eṣa mahārāja kimanyatkathayāmi te
|
dhṛtarāṣṭra uvāca|
śākadvīpasya saṅkṣepo yathāvadiha sañjaya ||12||

13uktastvayā mahāprājña vistaraṃ brūhi tattvataḥ
|
sañjaya uvāca|
tathaiva parvatā rājansaptātra maṇibhūṣitāḥ ||13||

14ratnākarāstathā nadyasteṣāṃ nāmāni me śṛṇu
|
atīva guṇavatsarvaṃ tatra puṇyaṃ janādhipa ||14||

15devarṣigandharvayutaḥ prathamo merurucyate
|
prāgāyato mahārāja malayo nāma parvataḥ ||15||

16tato meghāḥ pravartante prabhavanti ca sarvaśaḥ
|
tataḥ pareṇa kauravya jaladhāro mahāgiriḥ ||16||

17tato nityamupādatte vāsavaḥ paramaṃ jalam
|
tato varṣaṃ prabhavati varṣakāle janeśvara ||17||

18uccairgirī raivatako yatra nityaṃ pratiṣṭhitā
|
revatī divi nakṣatraṃ pitāmahakṛto vidhiḥ ||18||

19uttareṇa tu rājendra śyāmo nāma mahāgiriḥ
|
navameghaprabhaḥ prāṃśuḥ śrīmānujjvalavigrahaḥ ||19||

20yataḥ śyāmatvamāpannāḥ prajā janapadeśvara
|
dhṛtarāṣṭra uvāca|
sumahānsaṃśayo me'dya prokto'yaṃ sañjaya tvayā ||20||

21prajāḥ kathaṃ sūtaputra samprāptāḥ śyāmatāmiha
|
sañjaya uvāca|
sarveṣveva mahārāja dvīpeṣu kurunandana ||21||

22gauraḥ kṛṣṇaśca patagastayorvarṇāntare nṛpa
|
śyāmo yasmātpravṛtto vai tasmācchyāmo giriḥ smṛtaḥ ||22||

23tataḥ paraṃ kauravendra durgaśailo mahodayaḥ
|
kesaraḥ kesarayuto yato vātaḥ pravartate ||23||

24teṣāṃ yojanaviṣkambho dviguṇaḥ pravibhāgaśaḥ
|
varṣāṇi teṣu kauravya saptoktāni manīṣibhiḥ ||24||

25mahāmerurmahākāśo jaladaḥ kumudottaraḥ
|
jaladhāro mahārāja sukumāra iti smṛtaḥ ||25||

26revatasya tu kaumāraḥ śyāmasya maṇikāñcanaḥ
|
kesarasyātha maudākī pareṇa tu mahāpumān ||26||

27parivārya tu kauravya dairghyaṃ hrasvatvameva ca
|
jambūdvīpena saṅkyātastasya madhye mahādrumaḥ ||27||

28śāko nāma mahārāja prajā tasya sadānugā
|
tatra puṇyā janapadāḥ pūjyate tatra śaṅkaraḥ ||28||

29tatra gacchanti siddhāśca cāraṇā daivatāni ca
|
dhārmikāśca prajā rājaṃścatvāro'tīva bhārata ||29||

30varṇāḥ svakarmaniratā na ca steno'tra dṛśyate
|
dīrghāyuṣo mahārāja jarāmṛtyuvivarjitāḥ ||30||

31prajāstatra vivardhante varṣāsviva samudragāḥ
|
nadyaḥ puṇyajalāstatra gaṅgā ca bahudhā gatā ||31||

32sukumārī kumārī ca śītāśī veṇikā tathā
|
mahānadī ca kauravya tathā maṇijalā nadī ||32||

33cakṣurvardhanikā caiva nadī bharatasattama
|
tatra pravṛttāḥ puṇyodā nadyaḥ kurukulodvaha ||33||

34sahasrāṇāṃ śatānyeva yato varṣati vāsavaḥ
|
na tāsāṃ nāmadheyāni parimāṇaṃ tathaiva ca ||34||

35śakyante parisaṅkhyātuṃ puṇyāstā hi saridvarāḥ
|
tatra puṇyā janapadāścatvāro lokasammatāḥ ||35||

36maṅgāśca maśakāścaiva mānasā mandagāstathā
|
maṅgā brāhmaṇabhūyiṣṭhāḥ svakarmaniratā nṛpa ||36||

37maśakeṣu ca rājanyā dhārmikāḥ sarvakāmadāḥ
|
mānasāśca mahārāja vaiśyadharmopajīvinaḥ ||37||

38sarvakāmasamāyuktāḥ śūrā dharmārthaniścitāḥ
|
śūdrāstu mandagā nityaṃ puruṣā dharmaśīlinaḥ ||38||

39na tatra rājā rājendra na daṇḍo na ca daṇḍikāḥ
|
svadharmeṇaiva dharmajñāste rakṣanti parasparam ||39||

40etāvadeva śakyaṃ tu tatra dvīpe prabhāṣitum
|
etadeva ca śrotavyaṃ śākadvīpe mahaujasi ||40||
