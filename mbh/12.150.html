Mahābhārata 12.150 (Śāntiparvan)

1yudhiṣṭhira uvāca|
abuddhipūrvaṃ yatpāpaṃ kuryādbharatasattama
|
mucyate sa kathaṃ tasmādetatsarvaṃ bravīhi me ||1||

2bhīṣma uvāca|
atra te vartayiṣyāmi purāṇamṛṣisaṃstutam
|
indrotaḥ śaunako vipro yadāha janamejayam ||2||

3āsīdrājā mahāvīryaḥ parikṣijjanamejayaḥ
|
abuddhipūrvāmāgacchadbrahmahatyāṃ mahīpatiḥ ||3||

4brāhmaṇāḥ sarva evaite tatyajuḥ sapurohitāḥ
|
sa jagāma vanaṃ rājā dahyamāno divāniśam ||4||

5prajābhiḥ sa parityaktaścakāra kuśalaṃ mahat
|
ativelaṃ tapastepe dahyamānaḥ sa manyunā ||5||

6brahmahatyāpanodārthamapṛcchadbrāhmaṇān bahūn
|
paryaṭan pṛthivīṃ kṛtsnāṃ deśe deśe narādhipaḥ ||6||

7tatretihāsaṃ vakṣyāmi dharmasyāsyopabṛṃhaṇam
|
dahyamānaḥ pāpakṛtyā jagāma janamejayaḥ ||7||

8cariṣyamāṇa indrotaṃ śaunakaṃ saṃśitavratam
|
samāsādyopajagrāha pādayoḥ paripīḍayan ||8||

9ṛṣirdṛṣṭvā nṛpaṃ tatra jagarhe subhṛśaṃ tadā
|
kartā pāpasya mahato bhrūṇahā kimihāgataḥ ||9||

10kiṃ tvayā'smāsu kartāvyaṃ mā māṃ sprākṣīḥ kathaṃcana
|
gaccha gaccha na te sthānaṃ prīṇātyasmāniti bruvan ||10||

11rudhirasyeva te gandhaḥ śavasyeva ca darśanam
|
aśivaḥ śivasaṃkāśo mṛto jīvannivāṭasi ||11||

12brahmamṛtyuraśuddhātmā pāpamevānucintayan
|
prabuddhyase prasvapiṣi vartase parame sukhe ||12||

13moghaṃ te jīvitaṃ rājan parikliṣṭaṃ ca jīvasi
|
pāpāyaiva hi sṛṣṭo'si karmaṇe hi yavīyase ||13||

14bahukalyāṇamicchanti īhante pitaraḥ sutān
|
tapasā daivatejyābhirvandanena titikṣayā ||14||

15pitṛvaṃśamimaṃ paśya tvatkṛte narakaṃ gatam
|
nirarthāḥ sarva evaiṣāmāśābandhāstvadāśrayāḥ ||15||

16yānpūjayanto vindanti svargamāyuryaśaḥ prajāḥ
|
teṣu tvaṃ satataṃ dveṣṭā brāhmaṇeṣu nirarthakaḥ ||16||

17imaṃ lokaṃ vimucya tvamavāṅmūrddhā patiṣyasi
|
aśāśvatīḥ śāśvatīśca samāḥ pāpena karmaṇā ||17||

18ardhamāno yatra gṛdhraiḥ śitikaṇṭhairayomukhaiḥ
|
tataśca punarāvṛttaḥ pāpayoniṃ gamiṣyasi ||18||

19yadidaṃ manyase rājan nāyamasti kutaḥ paraḥ
|
prati smārayitārastvāṃ yamadūtā yamakṣaye ||19||
