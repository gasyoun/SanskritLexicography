Mahābhārata 18.4 (Svargārohaṇaparvan)

1vaiśampāyana uvāca
tato yudhiṣṭhiro rājā devaiḥ sarṣimarudgaṇaiḥ
|
stūyamāno yayau tatra yatra te kurupuṅgavāḥ ||1||

2dadarśa tatra govindaṃ brāhmeṇa vapuṣā'nvitam
|
tenaiva dṛṣṭapūrveṇa sādṛśyenaiva sūcitam ||2||

3dīpyamānaṃ svavapuṣā divyairastrairupasthitam
|
cakraprabhṛtibhirghorairdivyaiḥ puruṣavigrahaiḥ ||3||

4upāsyamānaṃ vīreṇa phalgunena suvarcasā
|
tathāsvarūpaṃ kaunteyo dadarśa madhusūdanam ||4||

5tāvubhau puruṣavyāghrau samudvīkṣya yudhiṣṭhiram
|
yathāvatpratipedāte pūjayā devapūjitau ||5||

6aparasminnathoddeśe karṇaṃ śastrabhṛtāṃ varam
|
dvādaśādityasahitaṃ dadarśa kurunandanaḥ ||6||

7athāparasminnuddeśe marudgaṇavṛtaṃ vibhum
|
bhīmasenamathāpaśyattenaiva vapuṣā'nvitam ||7||

8vāyormūrtimataḥ pārśve divyamūrtisamanvitam
|
śriyā paramayā yuktaṃ siddhiṃ paramikāṃ gatam ||8||

9aśvinostu tathā sthāne dīpyamānau svatejasā
|
nakulaṃ sahadevaṃ ca dadarśa kurunandanaḥ ||9||

10tathā dadarśa pāñcālīṃ kamalotpalamālinīm
|
vapuṣā svargamākramya tiṣṭhantīmarkavarcasam ||10||

11tatraināṃ sahasā rājā spraṣṭumaicchadyudhiṣṭhiraḥ
|
tato'sya bhagavānindraḥ kathayāmāsa devarāṭ ||11||

12śrīreṣā draupadīrūpā tvadarthe mānuṣaṃ gatā
|
ayonijā lokakāntā puṇyagandhā yudhiṣṭhira ||12||

13ratyarthaṃ bhavatāṃ hyeṣā nirmitā śūlapāṇinā
|
drupadasya kule jātā bhavadbhiścopajīvitā ||13||

14ete pañca mahābhāgā gandharvāḥ pāvakaprabhāḥ
|
draupadyāstanayā rājanyuṣmākamamitaujasaḥ ||14||

15paśya gandharvarājānaṃ dhṛtarāṣṭraṃ manīṣiṇam
|
enaṃ ca tvaṃ vijānīhi bhrātaraṃ pūrvajaṃ pituḥ ||15||

16ayaṃ te pūrvajo bhrātā kaunteyaḥ pāvakadyutiḥ
|
sūryaputro rathiśreṣṭho rādheya iti viśrutaḥ ||16||

17ādityasahito bhāti paśyainaṃ puruṣarṣabham
|
sādhyānāmatha devānāṃ viśveṣāṃ marutāmapi
|
||17||

18gaṇeṣu paśya rājendra vṛṣṇandhakamahārathān
|
sātyakipramukhānvīrānbhojāṃścaiva mahābalān ||18||

19somena sahitaṃ paśya saubhadramaparājitam
|
abhimanyuṃ maheṣvāsaṃ niśākarasamadyutim ||19||

20eṣa pāṇḍurmaheṣvāsaḥ kuntyā mādryā ca saṅgataḥ
|
vimānena sadā'bhyeti pitā tava mamāntikam ||20||

21vasubhiḥ sahitaṃ paśya bhīṣmaṃ śāntanavaṃ nṛpam
|
droṇaṃ bṛhaspateḥ pārśve gurumenaṃ niśāmaya ||21||

22ete cānye mahīpālā yodhāstava ca pāṇḍava
|
gandharvasahitā yānti yakṣapuṇyajanaistathā ||22||

23guhyakānāṃ gatiṃ cāpi kecitprāptā narādhipāḥ
|
tyaktvā dehaṃ jitaḥ svargaḥ puṇyavāgbuddhikarmabhiḥ ||23||
