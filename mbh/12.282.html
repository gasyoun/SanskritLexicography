Mahābhārata 12.282 (Śāntiparvan)

1vṛtrasya tu mahārāja jvarāviṣṭasya sarvaśaḥ
|
abhavanyāni liṅgāni śarīre tāni me śruṇu ||1||

2jvalitāsyo'bhavadghoro vaivarṇyaṃ cāgamatparam
|
gātrakampaśca sumahān śvāsaścāpyabhavanmahān ||2||

3romaharṣaśca tīvro'bhūnniḥśvāsaśca mahānnṛpa
|
śivā cāśivasaṃkāśā tasya vaktrātsudāruṇā ||3||

4niṣpapāta mahāghorā smṛtiḥ sā tasya bhārata
|
ulkāśca jvalitāstasya dīptāḥ pārśve prapedire ||4||

5gṛdhrāḥ kaṅkā balākāśca vāco'muñcansudāruṇāḥ
|
vṛtrasyopari saṃsṛṣṭāścakravatparibabhramuḥ ||5||

6tatastaṃ rathamāsthāya devā'pyāyita āhave
|
vajrodyatakaraḥ śakrastaṃ daityaṃ samavaikṣata ||6||

7āmanuṣamatho nādaṃ sa mumoca mahāsuraḥ
|
vyajṛmbhaccaiva rājendra tīvrajvarasamanvitaḥ ||7||

8athāsya jṛmbhataḥ śakrastato vajramavāsṛjat
|
sa vajraḥ sumahātejāḥ kālāgnisadṛśopamaḥ ||8||

9kṣiprameva mahākāyaṃ vṛtraṃ daityamapātayat
|
tato nādaḥ samabhavatpunareva samantataḥ ||9||

10vṛtraṃ vinihataṃ dṛṣṭvā devānāṃ bharatarṣabhaḥ
|
vṛtraṃ tu hatvā maghavā dānavārirmahāyaśāḥ ||10||

11vajreṇa viṣṇuyuktena divameva samāviśat
|
atha vṛtrasya kauravya śarīrādabhiniḥsṛtā ||11||

12brahmavadhyā mahāghorā raudrā lokabhayāvahā
|
karāladaśanā bhīmā vikṛtā kṛṣṇapiṅgalā ||12||

13prakīrṇamūrdhajā caiva ghoranetrā ca bhārata
|
kapālamālinī caiva kṛtyeva bharatarṣabha ||13||

14rudhirārdrā ca dharmajña cīravalkalavāsinī
|
sā'bhiniṣkramya rājendra tādṛgrūpā bhayāvahā ||14||

15vajriṇaṃ mṛgamāyāsa tadā bharatasattama
|
kasyacittvatha kālasya vṛtrahā kurunandana ||15||

16svargāyābhimukhaḥ prāyāllokānāṃ hitakāmyayā
|
sā viniḥsaramāṇaṃ tu dṛṣṭvā śakraṃ mahaujasam ||16||

17jagrāha vadhyā devendraṃ sulagnā cābhavattadā
|
sa hi tasminsamutpanne brahmavadhyākṛte bhaya ||17||

18nalinyā bisamadhyastha uvāsābdagaṇānbahūn
|
anusṛtya tu yatnātsa tayā vai brahmahatyayā ||18||

19tadā gṛhītaḥ kauravya nistejāḥ samapadyata
|
tasyā vyapohane śakraḥ paraṃ yatnaṃ cakāra ha ||19||

20na cāśakattāṃ devendro brahmavadhyāṃ vyapohitum
|
gṛhīta eva tu tayā devendro bharatarṣabha ||20||

21pitāmahamupāgamya śirasā pratyapūjayat
|
jñātvā gṛhītaṃ śakraṃ sa dvijapravaravadhyayā ||21||

22brahmā sa cintayāmāsa tadā bharatasattama
|
tāmuvāca mahābāho brahmavadhyāṃ pitāmahaḥ ||22||

23svareṇa madhureṇātha sāntvayanniva bhārata
|
mucyatāṃ tridaśendro'yaṃ matpriyaṃ kuru bhāvini
|
brūhi kiṃ te karomyadya kāmaṃ kiṃ tvamihecchasi ||23||

24brūhi kiṃ te karomyadya kāmaṃ kiṃ tvamihecchasi ||24||

25brahmavadhyovāca |
trilokapūjite deve prīte trailokyakartari
|
kṛtameva hi manyāmi nivāsaṃ tu vidhatsva me ||25||

26tvayā kṛteyaṃ maryādā lokasaṃrakṣaṇārthinā
|
sthāpanā vai sumahatī tvayā deva pravartitā ||26||

27prīte tu tvayi dharmajña sarvalekeśvara prabho
|
śakrādapagamiṣyāmi nivāsaṃ saṃvidhatsva me ||27||

28bhīṣma uvāca|
tatheti tāṃ prāha tadā brahmavadhyāṃ pitāmahaḥ
|
upāyataḥ sa śakrasya brahmavadhyāṃ vyapohata ||28||

29tataḥ svayambhuvā dhyātastatra vahnirmahātmanā
|
brahmāṇamupasaṃgamya tato vacanamabravīt ||29||

30prāpto'smi bhagavandeva tvatsakāśamanindita
|
yatkartavyaṃ mayā deva tadbhavānvaktumarhasi ||30||

31brahmovāca|
bahudhā vibhajiṣyāmi brahmavadhyāmimāmaham
|
śakrasyāghavimokṣārthaṃ caturbhāgaṃ pratīccha vai ||31||

32agniruvāca |
mama mokṣasya ko'nto vai brahmandhyāyasva vai prabho
|
etadicchāmi vijñātuṃ tattvato lokapūjita ||32||

33brahmovāca|
yastvāṃ jvalantamāsādya svayaṃ vai mānavaḥ kvacit
|
bījauṣadhirasairvanhe na yakṣyati tamovṛtaḥ ||33||

34tameṣā yāsyati kṣipraṃ tatraiva ca nivatsyati
|
brahmavadhyā havyavāha vyetu te mānaso jvaraḥ ||34||

35ityuktaḥ pratijagrāha tadvaco havyakavyabhuk
|
pitāmahasya bhagavāṃstathā ca tadabhūtprabho ||35||

36tato vṛkṣauṣadhitṛṇaṃ samāhūya pitāmahaḥ
|
imamarthaṃ mahārāja vaktuṃ samupacakrame ||36||

37tato vṛkṣauṣadhitṛṇaṃ tathaivoktaṃ yathātatham
|
vyathitaṃ vahnivadrājanbrahmāṇamidamabravīt ||37||

38asmākaṃ brahmavadhyāyāḥ ko'nto lokapitāmaha
|
daivenābhihatānasmānna punarhantumarhasi ||38||

39vayamagniṃ tathā śītaṃ varṣaṃ ca pavaneritam
|
sahāmaḥ satataṃ deva tathā cchedanabhedane ||39||

40brahmavadhyāmimāmadya bhavataḥ śāsanādvayam
|
grahīṣyāmastrilokeśa mokṣaṃ cintayatāṃ bhavān ||40||

41brahmovāca|
parvakāle tu samprāpte yo vai cchedanabhedanam
|
kariṣyati naro mohāttameṣā'nugamiṣyati ||41||

42bhīṣma uvāca|
tato vṛkṣauṣadhitṛṇamevamuktaṃ mahātmanā
|
brahmāṇamabhisampūjya jagāmāśu yathāgatam ||42||

43āhūyāpsaraso devastato lokapitāmahaḥ
|
vācā madhurayā prāha sāntvayanniva bhārata ||43||

44iyamindrādanuprāptā brahmavadhyā varāṅganāḥ
|
caturthamasyā bhāgāṃśaṃ mayoktāḥ sampratīcchata ||44||

45apsarasa ūcuḥ| grahaṇe kṛtabuddhīnāṃ deveśa tava śāsanāt
|
mokṣaṃ samayato'smākaṃ cintayasva pitāmaha ||45||

46brahmovāca|
rajasvalāsu nārīṣu yo vai maithunamācaret
|
tameṣā yāsyati kṣipraṃ vyetu vo mānaso jvaraḥ ||46||

47bhīṣma uvāca|
tatheti hṛṣṭamanasa ityuktvā'psarasāṃ gaṇāḥ
|
svāni sthānāni samprāpya remire bharatarṣabha ||47||

48tatastrilokakṛddevaḥ punareva mahātapāḥ
|
apaḥsaṃcintayāmāsa dhyātāstāścāpyathāgaman ||48||

49tāstu sarvāḥ samāgamya brahmāṇamamitaujasam
|
idamūcurvaco rājanpraṇipatya pitāmaham ||49||

50imāḥ sma deva samprāptāstvatsakāśamarindama
|
śāsanāttava lokeśa samājñāpaya naḥ prabho ||50||

51brahmovāca|
iyaṃ vṛtrādanuprāptā puruhūtaṃ mahābhayā
|
brahmavadhyā caturthāṃśamasyā yūyaṃ pratīcchata ||51||

52āpa ūcuḥ| evaṃ bhavatu lokeśa yathā vadasi naḥ prabho
|
mokṣaṃ samayato'smākaṃ sañcintayitumarhasi ||52||

53tvaṃ hi deveśa sarvasya jagataḥ paramā gatiḥ
|
ko'nyaḥ prasādo hi bhavedyannaḥ kṛcchrātsamuddharet ||53||

54brahmovāca|
alpā iti matiṃ kṛtvā yo naro buddhimohitaḥ
|
śleṣmamūtrapurīṣāṇi yuṣmāsu pratimokṣyati ||54||

55tamiyaṃ yāsyati kṣipraṃ tatraiva ca nivatsyati
|
tathā vo bhavitā mokṣa iti satyaṃ bravīmi vaḥ ||55||

56tato vimucya devendraṃ brahmavadhyā yudhiṣṭhira
|
yathā visṛṣṭaṃ taṃ vāsamagamaddevaśāsanāt ||56||

57evaṃ śakreṇa saṃprāptā brahmavadhyā janādhipa
|
pitāmahamanujñāpya so'śvamedhamakalpayat ||57||

58śrūyate ca mahārāja saṃprāptā vāsavena vai
|
brahmavadhyā tataḥ śuddhiṃ hayamedhena labdhavān ||58||

59samavāpya śriyaṃ devo hatvārīṃśca sahasraśaḥ
|
praharṣamatulaṃ lebhe vāsavaḥ pṛthivīpate ||59||

60vṛtrasya rudhirāccaiva śikhaṇḍāḥ pārtha jajñire
|
dvijātibhirabhakṣyāste dīkṣitaśca tapodhanaiḥ ||60||

61sarvāvasthaṃ tvamapyeṣāṃ dvijātīnāṃ priyaṃ kuru
|
ime hi bhūtale devāḥ prathitāḥ kurunandana ||61||

62evaṃ śakreṇa kauravya buddhisaukṣmyānmahāsuraḥ
|
upāyapūrvaṃ nihato vṛtro hyamitatejasā ||62||

63evaṃ tvamapi kaunteya pṛthivyāmaparājitaḥ
|
bhaviṣyasi yathā devaḥ śatakraturamitrahā ||63||

64ye tu śakrakathāṃ divyāmimāṃ parvasu parvasu
|
vipramadhye vadiṣyanti na te prāpsyanti kilbiṣam ||64||

65ityetadvṛtramāśritya śakrasyātyadbhutaṃ mahat
|
kathitaṃ karma te tāta kiṃ bhūyaḥ śrotumicchasi ||65||
