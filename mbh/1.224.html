Mahābhārata 1.224 (Ādiparvan)

1vaiśaṃpāyana uvāca|
sa tu nairāśyamāpannaḥ sadā glānisamanvitaḥ
|
pitāmahamupāgacchatsaṃkruddho havyavāhanaḥ||1||

2tacca sarvaṃ yathānyāyaṃ brahmaṇe saṃnyavedayat
|
uvāca caivanaṃ bhagavānmuhūrtaṃ sa vicintya tu||2||

3upāyaḥ paridṛṣṭo me yathā tvaṃ dhakṣyase'nagha
|
kālaṃ ca kaṃcitkṣamatāṃ tatastāṃ vakṣyate'nala||3||

4bhaviṣyataḥ sahāyau te naranārāyaṇau tadā
|
tābhyāṃ tvaṃ sahito dāvaṃ dhakṣyase havyavāhana||4||

5evamastviti taṃ vahnirbrahmāṇaṃ pratyabhāṣata
|
saṃbhūtau tau viditvā tu naranārāyaṇāvṛṣī||5||

6kālasya mahato rājaṃstasya vākyaṃ svayaṃbhuvaḥ
|
anusmṛtya jagāmātha punareva pitāmaham||6||

7abravīcca tadā brahmā yathā tvaṃ dhakṣyase'nala
|
khāṇḍavaṃ dāvamadyaiva miṣato'sya śacīpateḥ||7||

8naranārāyaṇau yau tau pūrvadevau vibhāvaso
|
saṃprāptau mānuṣe loke kāryārthaṃ hi divaukasām||8||

9arjunaṃ vāsudevaṃ ca yau tau loko'bhimanyate
|
tāvetau sahitāvehi khāṇḍavasya samīpataḥ||9||

10tau tvaṃ yācasva sāhāyye dāhārthaṃ khāṇḍavasya ca
|
tato dhakṣyasi taṃ dāvaṃ rakṣitaṃ tridaśairapi||10||

11tau tu satvāni sarvāṇi yatnato vārayiṣyataḥ
|
devarājaṃ ca sahitau tatra me nāsti saṃśayaḥ||11||

12etacchrutvā tu vacanaṃ tvarito havyavāhanaḥ
|
kṛṣṇapārthāvupāgamya yamarthaṃ tvabhyabhāṣata||12||

13taṃ te kathitavānasmi pūrvameva nṛpottama
|
tacchrutvā vacanaṃ tvagnerbībhatsurjātavedasam||13||

14abravīnnṛpaśārdūla tatkālasadṛśaṃ vacaḥ
|
didhakṣuṃ khāṇḍavaṃ dāvamakāmasya śatakratoḥ||14||

15arjuna uvāca|
uttamāstrāṇi me santi divyāni ca bahūni ca
|
yairahaṃ śaknuyāṃ yoddhumapi vajradharānbahūn||15||

16dhanurme nāsti bhagavanbāhuvīryeṇa saṃmitam
|
kurvataḥ samare yatnaṃ vegaṃ yadviṣahenmama||16||

17śaraiśca mertho bahubhirakṣayaiḥ kṣipramasyataḥ
|
na hi voḍhuṃ rathaḥ śaktaḥ śarānmama yathepsitān||17||

18aśvāṃśca divyāniccheyaṃ pāṇḍurānvātaraṃhasaḥ
|
rathaṃ ca meghanirghoṣaṃ sūryapratimatejasam||18||

19tathā kṛṣṇasya vīryeṇa nāyudhaṃ vidyate samam
|
yena nāgānpiśācāṃśca nihanyānmādhavo raṇe||19||

20upāyaṃ karmasiddhau ca bhaghavanvaktumarhasi
|
nivārayeyaṃ yenendraṃ varṣamāṇaṃ mahāvane||20||

21pauruṣeṇa tu yatkāryaṃ tatkartārau sva pāvaka
|
karaṇāni samarthāni bhagavandātumarhasi||21||
