Mahābhārata 7.27 (Droṇaparvan)

1sañjaya uvāca|
yanmāṃ pārthasya sa ṅgrāme karmāṇi paripṛcchasi
|
tacchruṇuṣva mahābāho pārtho yadakarodraṇe||1||

2rajo dṛṣṭvā samudbhūtaṃ śrutvā ca gajaniḥsvanam
|
bhagadatte vikurvāṇe kaunteyaḥ kṛṣṇamabravīt||2||

3yathā prāgjyotiṣo rājā gajena madhusūdana
|
tvaramāṇo viniṣkrānto dhruvaṃ tasyaiṣa niḥsvanaḥ||3||

4indrādanavaraḥ saṃkhye gajayānaviśāradaḥ
|
prathamo gajayodhānāṃ pṛthivyāmiti me matiḥ||4||

5sa cāpi dviradaśreṣṭhaḥ sadā'pratigajo yudhi
|
sarvaśastrātigaḥ saṃkhye kṛtakarmā jitaklamaḥ||5||

6sahaḥ śastranipātānāmagnisparśasya cānagha
|
sa pāṇḍavabalaṃ sarvamadyaiko nāśayiṣyati||6||

7na cāvābhyāmṛte'nyo'sti śaktastaṃ pratibādhitum
|
tvaramāṇastato yāhi yataḥ prāgjyotiṣādhipaḥ||7||

8dṛptaṃ saṃkhye dvipabalādvayasā cāpi vismitam
|
athainaṃ preṣayiṣyāmi balahantuḥ priyātithim||8||

9vacanādatha kṛṣṇastu prayayau savyasācinaḥ
|
dīryate bhagadattena yatra pāṇḍavavāhinī||9||

10taṃ prayāntaṃ tataḥ paścādāhvayanto mahārathāḥ
|
saṃśaptakāḥ samārohan saha‌srāṇi caturdaśa||10||

11daśaiva tu sahasrāṇi trigartānāṃ mahārathāḥ
|
catvāri ca sahastrāṇi vāsudevasya cānugāḥ||11||

12dīryamāṇāṃ camūṃ dṛṣṭvā bhagadattena māriṣa
|
āhūyamānasya ca tairabhavaddṛdayaṃ dvidhā||12||

13kiṃ tu śreyaskaraṃ karma bhavedadyeti cintayan
|
iha vā vinivarteyaṃ gaccheyaṃ vā yudhiṣṭhiram||13||

14tasya buddhyā vicāryaivamarjunasya kurūdvaha
|
abhavadbhūyasī buddhiḥ saṃśaptakavadhe sthirā||14||

15sa sannivṛttaḥ sahasā kapipravaraketanaḥ
|
eko rathasahasrāṇi nihantuṃ vāsavī raṇe||15||

16sāhi duryodhanasyāsīnmatiḥ karṇasya cobhayoḥ
|
arjunasya vadhopāye tena dvaidhamakalpayat||16||

17sa tu dolāyamāno'bhūddvaidhībhāvena pāṇḍavaḥ
|
vadhena tu narāgryāṇāmakarot tāṃ mṛṣā tadā||17||

18tataḥ śatasahasrāṇi śarāṇāṃ nataparvaṇām
|
asṛjannarjune rājan saṃśaptakamahārathāḥ||18||

19naiva kuntīsutaḥ pārtho naiva kṛṣṇo janārdanaḥ
|
na hayā na rathā rājan dṛśyante sma śaraiścitāḥ||19||

20tadā mohamanuprāptaḥ siṣvide hi janārdanaḥ
|
tatastān prāyaśaḥ pārtho brahmāstreṇa nijaghnivān||20||

21śataśaḥ pāṇayaśchinnāḥ seṣujyātalakārmukāḥ
|
ketavo vājinaḥ sūtā rathinaścāpatan kṣitau||21||

22drumācalāgrāmbudharaiḥ samakāyāḥ sukalpitāḥ
|
hatārohāḥ kṣitau peturdvipāḥ pārthaśarāhatāḥ||22||

23vi viddhakuthā nāgāśchinnabhāṇḍāḥ parāsavaḥ
|
sārohāstu raṇe peturmathitā mārgaṇairbhṛśam||23||

24sarṣṭiprāsāsinakharāḥ samudgaraparaśvadhāḥ
|
vicchinnā bāhavaḥ peturnṛṇāṃ bhallaiḥ kirīṭinā||24||

25bālādityāgbujendūnāṃ tulyarūpāṇi māriṣa
|
saṃcchinnānyarjunaśaraiḥ śirāṃsturvyāṃ prapedire||25||

26jajvālālaṅkatā senā patribhiḥ prāṇibhojanaiḥ
|
nānārūpaistadā'mitrān kruddhe nighnati phālgune||26||

27kṣobhayantaṃ tadā senāṃ dviradaṃ nalinīmiva
|
dhanañjayaṃ bhūtagaṇāḥ sādhu sādhvityapūjayan||27||

28dṛṣṭvā tat karma pārthasya vāsavasyeva mādhavaḥ
|
vismayaṃ paramaṃ gatvā prāñjalistamuvāca ha||28||

29karmaitat pārtha śakreṇa yamena dhanadena ca
|
duṣkaraṃ samare yat te kṛtamadyeti me matiḥ||39||

30yugapaccaiva saṃgrāme śataśo'tha sahasraśaḥ
|
patitā eva me dṛṣṭāḥ saṃśaptakamahārathāḥ||40||

31saṃśaptakāṃstato hatvā bhūyiṣṭhā ye vyavasthitāḥ
|
bhagada‌ttāya yāhīti kṛṣṇaṃ pārtho'bhyanodayat||42||
