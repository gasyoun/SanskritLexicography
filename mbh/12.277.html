Mahābhārata 12.277 (Śāntiparvan)

1yudhiṣṭhira uvāca|
atikrāmati kāle'smin sarvabhūtabhayāvahe
|
kiṃ śreyaḥ pratipadyeta tanme brūhi pitāmaha ||1||

2bhīṣma uvāca|
atrāpyudāharantīmamitihāsaṃ purātanam
|
pituḥ putreṇa saṃvādaṃ taṃ nibodha yudhiṣṭhira ||2||

3dvijāteḥ kasyacitpārtha svādhyāyaniratasya vai
|
putro babhūva medhāvī medhāvī nāma nāmataḥ ||3||

4so'bravītpitaraṃ putraḥ svādhyāyakaraṇe ratam
|
mokṣadharmeṣvakuśalaṃ mokṣadharmavicakṣaṇaḥ ||4||

5putra uvāca |
dhīraḥ kiṃsvittāta kuryātprajānan kṣipraṃ hyāyurbhraśyate mānavānām
|
pitastathā''khyāhi yathārthayogaṃ mamānupūrvyā yena dharmaṃ careyam ||5||

6pitovāca |
adhītya vedānbrahmacaryeṣu putra putrānicchetpāvanāya pitṝṇām
|
agnīnādhāya vidhivacceṣṭayajño vanaṃ praviśyātha munirbubhūṣet ||6||

7putra uvāca|
evamabhyāhate loke sarvataḥ parivārite
|
amoghāsu patantīṣu kiṃ dhīra iva bhāṣase ||7||

8pitovāca |
kathamabhyāhato lokaḥ kena vā parivāritaḥ
|
amoghāḥ kāḥ patantīha kiṃ nu bhīṣayasīva mām ||8||

9putra uvāca |
mṛtyunā'bhyāhato loko jarayā parivāritaḥ
|
ahorātrāḥ patantīme tacca kasmānna buddhyase ||9||

10yadāhameva jānāmi na mṛtyustiṣṭhatīti ha
|
so'haṃ kathaṃ pratīkṣiṣye jñānenāpihitaścaran ||10||

11rātryāṃ rātryāṃ vyatītāyāmāyuralpataraṃ yadā
|
gādhodake matsya iva sukhaṃ vindeta kastadā ||11||

12puṣpāṇīva vicinvantamanyatra gatamānasam
|
anavāpteṣu kāmeṣu mṛtyurabhyeti mānavam ||12||

13śvaḥ kāryamadya kurvīta pūrvāhne cāparāhnikam
|
na hi pratīkṣate mṛtyuḥ kṛtaṃ vā'sya na vā kṛtam ||13||

14adyaiva kuru yacchreyo mā tvāṃ kālo'tyagānmahān
|
ko hi jānāti kasyādya mṛtyukālo bhaviṣyati ||14||

15akṛteṣveva kāryeṣu mṛtyurvai samprakarṣati
|
yuvaiva dharmaśīlaḥ syādanimittaṃ hi jīvitam ||15||

16kṛte dharme bhavetprītiriha pretya ca śāśvatī
|
mohena hi samāviṣṭaḥ putradārārthamudyataḥ ||16||

17kṛtvā kāryamakāryaṃ vā tuṣṭimeṣāṃ prayacchati
|
taṃ putrapaśusampannaṃ vyāsaktamanasaṃ naram ||17||

18suptaṃ vyāghraṃ mahaugho vā mṛtyurādāya gacchati
|
sañcinvānakamevainaṃ kāmānāmavitṛptakam ||18||

19vṛkīvoraṇamāsādya mṛtyurādāya gacchati
|
idaṃ kṛtamidaṃ kāryamidamanyatkṛtākṛtam ||19||

20evamīhāsamāyuktaṃ mṛtyurādāya gacchati
|
kṛtānāṃ phalamaprāptaṃ kāryāṇāṃ karmasaṅginām ||20||

21kṣetrāpaṇagṛhāsaktaṃ mṛtyurādāya gacchati
|
durbalaṃ balavantaṃ ca prājñaṃ śūraṃ jaḍaṃ kavim ||21||

22aprāptasarvakāmārthaṃ mṛtyudārāya gacchati
|
mṛtyurjarā ca vyādhiśca duḥkhaṃ cānekakāraṇam ||22||

23asaṃtyājyaṃ yadā martyaiḥ kiṃ svastha iva tiṣṭhasi
|
jātamevāntako'ntāya jarā cābhyeti dehinam ||23||

24anuṣattu dvayenaite bhāvāḥ sthāvarajaṅgamāḥ
|
na mṛtyusenāmāyāntīṃ jātu kaścitprabādhate ||24||

25balātsatyamṛte tvekaṃ satye hyamṛtamāśritam
|
mṛtyorvā gṛhametadvai yā grāme vasato ratiḥ ||25||

26devānāmeṣa vai goṣṭho yadaraṇyamiti śrutiḥ
|
nibandhanī rajjureṣā yā grāme vasato ratiḥ ||26||

27chittvaināṃ sukṛto yānti naināṃ chindanti duṣkṛtaḥ
|
yo na hiṃsati sattvāni manovākkarmahetubhiḥ ||27||

28jīvitārthāpanayanaiḥ prāṇibhirna sa baddhyate
|
tasmātsatyavratācāraḥ satyavrataparāyaṇaḥ ||28||

29satyakāmaḥ samo dāntaḥ satyenaivāntakaṃ jayet
|
amṛtaṃ caiva mṛtyuśca dvayaṃ dehe pratiṣṭhitam ||29||

30mṛtyurāpadyate mohātsatyenāpadyate'mṛtam
|
so'haṃ satyamahiṃsārthī kāmakrodhabahiṣkṛtaḥ ||30||

31samāśritya sukhaṃ kṣemī mṛtyuṃ hāsyāmyamṛtyuvat
|
śāntiyajñarato dānto brahmayajñe sthito muniḥ ||31||

32vāṅmanaḥkarmayajñaśca bhaviṣyāmyudagāyane
|
paśuyajñaiḥ kathaṃ hiṃsrairmādṛśo yaṣṭumarhati ||32||

33antavadbhiruta prājñaḥ kṣatrayajñaiḥ piśācavat
|
ātmanyevātmanā jāta ātmaniṣṭho'prajaḥ pitaḥ ||33||

34ātmayajño bhaviṣyāmi na māṃ tārayati prajā
|
yasya vāṅmanasī syātāṃ samyak praṇihite sadā ||34||

35tapastyāgaśca yogaśca sa taiḥ sarvamavāpnuyāt
|
nāsti vidyāsamaṃ cakṣurnāsti vidyāsamaṃ phalam ||35||

36nāsti rāgasamaṃ duḥkhaṃ nāsti tyāgasamaṃ sukham ||36||

37naitādṛśaṃ brāhmaṇasyāsti vittaṃ yathaikatā samatā satyatā ca
|
śīle sthitirdaṇḍavidhānamārjavaṃ tatastataścoparamaḥ kriyābhyaḥ ||37||

38kiṃ te dhanairbāndhavairvāpi kiṃ te kiṃ te dārairbrāhmaṇa yo mariṣyasi
|
ātmānamanviccha guhāṃ praviṣṭaṃ pitāmahāste kva gatāḥ pitā ca ||38||

39bhīṣma uvāca|
putrasyaitadvacaḥ śrutvā tathā'kārṣītpitā nṛpa
|
tathā tvamapi vartasva satyadharmaparāyaṇaḥ ||39||
