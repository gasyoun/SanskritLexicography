Mahābhārata 14.83 (Āśvamedhikaparvan)

1vaiśampāyana uvāca|
māgadhenārcito rājanpāṇḍavaḥ śvetavāhanaḥ
|
dakṣiṇāṃ diśamāsthāya cārayāmāsa taṃ hayam||1||

2tataḥ sa punarāvartya hayaḥ kāmacaro balī
|
āsasāda purīṃ ramyāṃ cedīnāṃ śuktisāhvayām||2||

3śarabheṇārcitastatra śiśupālasutena saḥ
|
yuddhapūrvaṃ tadā tena pūjayā ca mahābalaḥ||3||

4tato'rcito yayau rājaṃstadā sa turagottamaḥ
|
kāśīnagānkosalāṃśca kirātānatha tegaṇān||4||

5pūjāṃ tatra yathānyāyaṃ pratigṛhya dhanañjayaḥ
|
punarāvṛtya kaunteyo daśārṇānagamattadā||5||

6tatra citrāṅgado nāma balavānarimardanaḥ
|
tena yuddhamabhūttasya vijayasyātibhairavam||6||

7taṃ cāpi vaśamānīya kirīṭī puruṣarṣabhaḥ
|
niṣādarājño viṣayamekalavyasya jagmivān||7||

8ekalavyasutaścainaṃ yuddhena jagṛhe tadā
|
tatra cakre niṣādaiḥ sa saṅgrāmaṃ lomaharṣaṇam||8||

9tatastamapi kaunteyaḥ samareṣvaparājitaḥ
|
jigāya yudhi durdharṣo yajñavighnārthamāgatam||9||

10sa taṃ jitvā mahārāja naiṣādiṃ pākaśāsaniḥ
|
arcitaḥ prayayau bhūyo dakṣiṇaṃ salilārṇavam||10||

11tatrāpi draviḍairāndhrai raudrairmāhiṣakairapi
|
tathā kollagireyaiśca yuddhamāsītkirīṭinaḥ||11||

12tāṃścāpi vijayo jitvā nātitīvreṇa karmaṇā
|
turaṅgamavaśenātha surāṣṭrānabhito yayau||12||

13gokarṇamatha cāsādya prabhāsamapi jagmivān
|
tato dvāravatīṃ ramyāṃ vṛṣṇivīrābhimālitām||13||

14āsasāda hayaḥ śrīmānkururājasya yajñiyaḥ
|
tamunmathya hayaśreṣṭhaṃ yādavānāṃ kumārakāḥ||14||

15prayayustāṃstadā rājannugraseno nyavārayat
|
tataḥ purādviniṣkramya vṛṣṇyandhakapatistadā||15||

16sahito vasudevena mātulena kirīṭinaḥ
|
tau sametya kuruśreṣṭhaṃ vidhivatprītipūrvakam||16||

17parayā bhārataśreṣṭhaṃ pūjayā samavasthitau
|
tatastābhyāmanujñāto yayau yena hayo gataḥ||17||

18tataḥ sa paścimaṃ deśaṃ samudrasya tadā hayaḥ
|
krameṇa vyacaratsphītaṃ tataḥ pañcanadaṃ yayau||18||

19tasmādapi sa kauravya gandhāraviṣayaṃ hayaḥ
|
vicacāra yathākāmaṃ kaunteyānugatastadā||19||

20tato gāndhārarājena yuddhamāsītkirīṭinaḥ
|
ghoraṃ śakuniputreṇa pūrvavairānusāriṇā||20||
