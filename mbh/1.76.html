Mahābhārata 1.76 (Ādiparvan)

1janamejaya uvāca|
yayātiḥ pūrvajo'smākaṃ daśamo yaḥ prajāpateḥ
|
kathaṃ sa śukratanayāṃ lebhe paramadurlabhām||1||

2etadicchāmyahaṃ śrotuṃ vistareṇa tapodhana
|
ānupūrvyā ca me śaṃsa rājño vaṃśakarānpṛthak||2||

3vaiśaṃpāyana uvāca|
yayātirāsīnnṛpatirdevarājasamadyutiḥ
|
taṃ śukravṛṣaparvāṇau vavrāte vai yathā purā||3||

4tatte'haṃ saṃpravakṣyāmi pṛcchate janamejaya
|
devayānyāśca saṃyogaṃ yayāternāhuṣasya ca||4||

5surāṇāmasurāṇāṃ ca samajāyata vai mithaḥ
|
aiśvaryaṃ pratisaṃgharṣastrailokye sacarācare||5||

6jigīṣayā tato devā vavrire'ṅgirasaṃ munim
|
paurohityena yājyārthe kāvyaṃ tūśanasaṃ pare||6||

7brāhmaṇau tāvubhau nityamanyonyaspardhinau bhṛśam
|
tatra devā nijaghnuryāndānavānyudhi saṃgatān||7||

8tānpunarjīvayāmāsa kāvyo vidyābalāśrayāt
|
tataste punarutthāya yodhayāṃcakrire surān||8||

9asurāstu nijaghnuryānsurānsamaramūrdhani
|
na tānsañjīvayāmāsa bṛhaspatirudāradhīḥ||9||

10na hi veda sa tāṃ vidyāṃ yāṃ kāvyo vetti vīryavān
|
sañjīvinīṃ tato devā viṣādamagamanparam||10||

11te tu devā bhayodvignāḥ kāvyāduśanasastadā
|
ūcuḥ kacamupāgamya jyeṣṭhaṃ putraṃ bṛhaspateḥ||11||

12bhajamānānbhajasvāsmānkuru naḥ sāhyamuttamam
|
yā sā vidyā nivasati brāhmaṇe'mitatejasi||12||

13śukre tāmāhara kṣipraṃ bhāgabhāṅgo bhaviṣyasi
|
vṛṣaparvasamīpe hi śakyo draṣṭuṃ tvayā dvijaḥ||13||

14rakṣate dānavāṃstatra na sa rakṣatyadānavān
|
tamārādhayituṃ śakto bhavānpūrvavayāḥ kavim||14||

15devayānīṃ ca dayitāṃ sutāṃ tasya mahātmanaḥ
|
tvamārādhayituṃ śakto nānyaḥ kaścana vidyate||15||

16śīladākṣiṇyamādhuryairācāreṇa damena ca
|
devayānyāṃ hi tuṣṭāyāṃ vidyāṃ tāṃ prāpsyasi dhruvam||16||

17tathetyuktvā tataḥ prāyādbṛhaspatisutaḥ kacaḥ
|
tadā'bhipūjito devaiḥ samīpe vṛṣaparvaṇaḥ||17||

18sa gatvā tvarito rājandevaiḥ saṃpreṣitaḥ kacaḥ
|
asurendrapure śukraṃ dṛṣṭvā vākyamuvāca ha||18||

19ṛṣeraṅgirasaḥ pautraṃ putraṃ sākṣādbṛhaspateḥ
|
nāmnā kacamitikhyātaṃ śiṣyaṃ gṛhṇāt māṃ bhavān||19||

20brahmacaryaṃ cariṣyāmi tvayyahaṃ paramaṃ gurau
|
anumanyasva māṃ brahmansahasraṃ parivatsarān||20||

21śukra uvāca|
kaca susvāgataṃ te'stu pratigṛhṇāmi te vacaḥ
|
arcayiṣye'hamarcyaṃ tvāmarcito'stu bṛhaspatiḥ||21||

22vaiśaṃpāyana uvāca|
kacastu taṃ tathetyuktvā pratijagrāha tadvratam
|
ādiṣṭaṃ kaviputreṇa śukreṇośanasā svayam||22||

23vratasya prāptakālaṃ sa yathoktaṃ pratyagṛhṇata
|
ārādhayannupādhyāyaṃ devayānīṃ ca bhārata||23||

24nityamārādhayiṣyaṃstāṃ yuvā yauvanagocare
|
gāyannṛtyanvādayaṃśca devayānīmatoṣayat||24||

25sa śīlayandevayānīṃ kanyāṃ saṃprāptayauvanām
|
puṣpaiḥ phalaiḥ preṣaṇaiśca toṣayāmāsa bhārata||25||

26devayānyapi taṃ vipraṃ niyamavratadhāriṇam
|
gāyantī ca lalantī ca rahaḥ paryacarattathā||26||

27pañcavarṣaśatānyevaṃ kacasya carato vratam
|
tatrātīyurathobuddhvā dānavāstaṃ tataḥ kacam||27||

28gā rakṣantaṃ vane dṛṣṭvā rahasyekamamarṣitāḥ
|
jaghnurbṛhaspaterdveṣādvidyārakṣārthameva ca||28||

29hatvā śālāvṛkebhyaśca prāyaccha~llavaśaḥ kṛtam
|
tato gāvo nivṛttāstā agopāḥ svaṃ niveśanam||29||

30sā dṛṣṭvā rahitā gāśca kacenābhyāgatā vanāt
|
uvāca vacanaṃ kāle devayānyatha bhārata||30||

31devayānyuvāca|
āhutaṃ cāgnihotraṃ te sūryaścāstaṃ gataḥ prabho
|
agopāścāgatā gāvaḥ kacastāta na dṛśyate||31||

32vyaktaṃ hato mṛto vāpi kacastāta bhaviṣyati
|
taṃ vinā na ca jīveyamiti satyaṃ bravīmi te||32||

33śukra uvāca|
ayamehīti saṃśabdya mṛtaṃ saṃjīvayāmyaham
|
tataḥ saṃjīvinīṃ vidyāṃ prayujya kacamāhvayat||33||

34bhittvā bhittvā śarīrāṇi vṛkāṇāṃ sa vinirgataḥ
|
āhūtaḥ prādurabhavatkaco hṛṣṭo'tha vidyayā||34||

35kasmāccirāyito'sīti pṛṣṭastāmāha bhārgavīm
|
samidhaśca kuśādīni kāṣṭhabhāraṃ ca bhāmini||35||

36gṛhītvāśramabhārāttaṃ vaṭavṛkṣaṃ samāśritaḥ
|
gāvaśca sahitāḥ sarvā vṛkṣacchāyāmupāśritāḥ||36||

37asurāstatra māṃ dṛṣṭvā kastvamityabhyacodayan
|
bṛhaspatisutaścāhaṃ kaca ityabhiviśrutaḥ||37||

38ityuktamātre māṃ hatvā peṣīkṛtvā tu dānavāḥ
|
dattvā śālāvṛkebhyastu sukhaṃ jagmuḥ svamālayam||38||

39āhūto vidyayā bhadre bhārgaveṇa mahātmanā
|
tvatsamīpamihāyātaḥ kathaṃcitprāptajīvitaḥ||39||

40hato'hamiti cācakhyau pṛṣṭo brāhmaṇakanyayā
|
sa punardevayānyoktaḥ puṣpāhāro yadṛcchayā||40||

41vanaṃ yayau kaco vipro dadṛśurdānavāśca tam
|
punastaṃ peṣayitvā tu samudrāmbhasyamiśrayan||41||

42ciraṃ gataṃ punaḥ kanyā pitre taṃ saṃnyavedayat
|
vipreṇa punarāhūto vidyayā gurudehajaḥ
|
punarāvṛtya tadvṛttaṃ nyavedayata tadyathā||42||

43tatastṛtīyaṃ hatvā taṃ dagdhvā kṛtvā ca cūrṇaśaḥ
|
prāyacchanbrāhmaṇāyaiva surāyāmasurāstathā||43||

44devayānyatha bhūyo'pi pitaraṃ vākyamabravīt
|
puṣpāhāraḥ preṣaṇakṛtkacastāta na dṛśyate||44||

45vyaktaṃ hato mṛto vāpi kacastāta bhaviṣyati
|
taṃ vinā na ca jīveyaṃ kacaṃ satyaṃ bravīmi te||45||

46śukra uvāca|
bṛhaspateḥ sutaḥ putri kacaḥ pretagatiṃ gataḥ
|
vidyayā jīvito'pyevaṃ hanyate karavāma kim||46||

47maivaṃ śuco mā ruda devayāni na tvādṛśī martyamanupraśocate
|
yasyāstava brahma ca brāhmaṇāśca sendrā devā vasavo'thāśvinau ca||47||

48suradviṣaścaiva jagacca sarvamupasthāne sannamanti prabhāvāt
|
aśakyo'sau jīvayituṃ dvijātiḥ saṃjīvito badhyate caiva bhūyaḥ||48||

49devayānyuvāca|
yasyāṅgirā vṛddhatamaḥ pitāmaho bṛhaspatiścāpi pitā taponidhiḥ
|
ṛṣeḥ putraṃ tamatho vāpi pautraṃ kathaṃ na śoceyamahaṃ na rudyām||49||

50sa brahmacārī ca tapodhanaśca sadotthitaḥ karmasu caiva dakṣaḥ
|
kacasya mārgaṃ pratipatsye na bhokṣye priyo hi me tāta kaco'bhirūpaḥ||50||

51vaiśaṃpāyana uvāca|
sa pīḍito devayānyā maharṣiḥ samāhvayatsaṃrabhacceva kāvyaḥ
|
asaṃśayaṃ māmasurā dviṣanti ye me śiṣyānāgatānsūdayanti||51||

52abrāhmaṇaṃ kartumicchanti raudrāste māṃ yathā vyabhicaranti nityam
|
apyasya pāpasya bhavedihāntaḥ kaṃ brahmahatyā na dahedapīndram||52||

53gurorhi bhīto vidyayā copahūtaḥ śanairvakyaṃ jaṭhare vyājahāra
|
vaiśaṃpāyana uvāca|
tamabravītkena pathopanītastvaṃ codare tiṣṭhasi brūhi vipra||53||

54kaca uvāca|
tava prasādānna jahāti māṃ smṛtiḥ smarāmi sarvaṃ yacca yathā ca vṛttam
|
na tvevaṃ syāttapasaḥ saṃkṣayo me tataḥ kleśaṃ ghoramimaṃ sahāmi||54||

55asuraiḥ surāyāṃ bhavato'smi datto hatvā dagdhvā cūrṇayitvā ca kāvya
|
brāhmīṃ māyāṃ cāsurīṃ vipra māyāṃ tvayi sthite kathamevātivartet||55||

56śukra uvāca|
kiṃ te priyaṃ karavāṇyadya vatse vadhena me jīvitaṃ syātkacasya
|
nānyatra kukṣermama bhedanena dṛśyetkaco madgato devayāni||56||

57devayānyuvāca|
dvau māṃ śokāvagnikalpau dahetāṃ kacasya nāśastava caivopaghātaḥ
|
kacasya nāśe mama śarma nāsti tavopaghāte jīvituṃ nāsmi śaktā||57||

58śukra uvāca|
saṃsiddharūpo'si bṛhaspateḥ suta yattvāṃ bhaktaṃ bhajate devayānī
|
vidyāmimāṃ prāpnuhi jīvanīṃ tvaṃ na cedindraḥ kacarūpī tvamadya||58||

59na nivartetpunarjīvankaścidanyo mamodarāt
|
brāhmaṇaṃ varjayitvaikaṃ tasmādvidyāmavāpnuhi|| 59||

60putro bhūtvā bhāvaya bhāvito māmasmaddehādupaniṣkramya tāta
|
samīkṣethā dharmavatīmavekṣāṃ guroḥ sakāśātprāpya vidyāṃ savidyaḥ||60||

61vaiśaṃpāyana uvāca|
guroḥ sakāśātsamavāpya vidyāṃ bhittvā kukṣiṃ nirvicakrāma vipraḥ
|
kaco'bhirūpastatkṣaṇādbrāhmaṇasya śuklātyaye paurṇamāsyāmivenduḥ||61||

62dṛṣṭvā ca taṃ patitaṃ brahmarāśimutthāpayāmāsa mṛtaṃ kaco'pi
|
vidyāṃ siddhāṃ tāmavāpyābhivādya tataḥ kacastaṃ gurumityuvāca||62||

63yaḥ śrotrayoramṛtaṃ sanniṣiñcedyo me na vidyasya yathā mamāyam
|
taṃ manye'haṃ pitaraṃ mātaraṃ ca tasmai na druhyetkṛtamasya jānan||63||

64ṛtasya dātāramanuttamasya nidhiṃ nidhīnāmapi labdhavidyāḥ
|
yenādriyante gurumarcanīyaṃ pāpā~llokāṃste vrajantyapratiṣṭhāḥ||64||

65vaiśaṃpāyana uvāca|
surāpānādvacanāṃ prāpya vidvānsaṃjñānāśaṃ caiva mahātighoram
|
dṛṣṭvā kacaṃ cāpi tathābhirūpaṃ pītaṃ tadā surayā mohitena||65||

66samanyurutthāya mahānubhāvastadośanā viprahitaṃ cikīrṣuḥ
|
surāpānaṃ pratisaṃjātamanyuḥ kāvyaḥ svayaṃ vākyamidaṃ jagāda||66||

67yo brāhmaṇo'dyaprabhṛtīha kaścinmohātsurāṃ pāsyati mandabuddhiḥ
|
apetadharmā brahmahā caiva sa syādasmiṃlloke garhitaḥ syātpare ca||67||

68mayā caitāṃ vipradharmoktisīmāṃ maryādāṃ vai sthāpitāṃ sarvaloke
|
santo viprāḥ śuśruvāṃso gurūṇāṃdevā lokāścopaśṛṇvantu sarve||68||

69itīdamuktvā sa mahānubhāvastaponidhīnāṃ nidhiraprameyaḥ
|
tāndānavāndaivavimūḍhabuddhīnidaṃ samāhūya vaco'bhyuvāca||69||

70ācakṣe vo dānavā bāliśāḥstha siddhaḥ kaco vatsyati matsakāśe
|
sañjīvinīṃ prāpya vidyāṃ mahātmā tulyaprabhāvo brāhmaṇo brahmabhūtaḥ||70||

71etāvaduktvā vacanaṃ virarāma sa bhārgavaḥ
|
dānavā vismayāviṣṭāḥ prayayuḥ svaṃ niveśanam||71||

72guroruṣya sakāśe tu daśavarṣaśatāni saḥ
|
anujñātaḥ kaco gantumiyeṣa tridaśālayam||72||
