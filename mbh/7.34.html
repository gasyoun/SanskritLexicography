Mahābhārata 7.34 (Droṇaparvan)

1sañjaya uvāca|
samare'tyugrakarmāṇaḥ karmabhirvyañjitaśramāḥ
|
sakṛṣṇāḥ pāṇḍavāḥ pañca devairapi durāsadāḥ||1||

2sattvakarmānvayairbuddhyā kīrtyā ca yaśasā śriyā
|
naiva bhūto na bhavitā naiva tulyaguṇaḥ pumān||2||

3satyadharmarato dānto viprapūjādibhirguṇaiḥ
|
sadaiva tridivaṃ prāpto rājā kila yudhiṣṭhiraḥ||3||

4yugānte cāntako rājan jāmadagnyaśca vīryavān
|
rathastho bhīmasenaśca kathyante sadṛśāstrayaḥ||4||

5pratijñākarmadakṣasya raṇe gāṇḍīvadhanvanaḥ
|
upamāṃ nādhigacchāmi pārthasya sadṛśīṃ kṣitau||5||

6guruvātsalyamatyantaṃ naibhṛtyaṃ vinayo damaḥ
|
nakule'prātirūpyaṃ ca śauryaṃ ca niyatāni ṣaṭ||6||

7śruta-gāmbhīrya-mādhurya-satya-rūpa-parākramaiḥ
|
sadṛśo devayorvīraḥ sahadevaḥ kilāśvinoḥ||7||

8ye ca kṛṣṇe guṇāḥ sphītāḥ pāṇḍaveṣu ca ye guṇāḥ
|
abhimanyau kilaikasthā dṛśyante guṇasañcayāḥ||8||

9yudhiṣṭhirasya vīryeṇa kṛṣṇasya caritena ca
|
karmabhirbhīmasenasya sadṛśo bhīmakarmaṇaḥ||9||

10dhanañjayasya rūpeṇa vikrameṇa śrutena ca
|
vinayāt sahadevasya sadṛśo nakulasya ca||10||

11dhṛtarāṣṭra uvāca| abhimanyumahaṃ sūta saubhadramaparājitam
|
śrotumicchāmi kārtsnyena kathamāyodhane hataḥ||11||

12sañjaya uvāca|
sthiro bhava mahārāja śokaṃ dhāraya durdharam
|
mahāntaṃ bandhunāśaṃ te kathayiṣyāmi tacchṛṇu||12||

13cakravyūho mahārāja ācāryeṇābhikalpitaḥ
|
tatra śakropamāḥ sarve rājāno viniveśitāḥ||13||

14ārāsthāneṣu vinyastāḥ kumārāḥ sūryavarcasaḥ
|
saṃghāto rājaputrāṇāṃ sarveṣāmabhavat tadā||14||

15kṛtābhisamayāḥ sarve suvarṇavikṛtadhvajāḥ
|
raktāmbaradharāḥ sarve sarve raktavibhūṣaṇāḥ||15||

16sarve raktapatākāśca sarve vai hemamālinaḥ
|
candanāgurudigdhāṅgāḥ sragviṇaḥ sūkṣmavāsasaḥ||16||

17sahitāḥ paryadhāvanta kārṣṇiṃ prati yuyutsavaḥ
|
teṣāṃ daśasahastrāṇi babhūvurdṛḍhadhanvinām||17||

18pautraṃ tava puraskṛtya lakṣmaṇaṃ priyadarśanam
|
anyonyasamaduḥkhāste anyonyasamasāhasāḥ||18||

19anyonyaṃ spardhamānāśca anyonyasya hite ratāḥ
|
duryodhanastu rājendra sainyamadhye vyavasthitaḥ||19||

20karṇa-duḥśāsana-kṛpairvṛto rājā mahārathaiḥ
|
devarājopamaḥ śrīmāñchvetacchatrābhisaṃvṛtaḥ||20||

21cāmaravyajanākṣepairudayanniva bhāskaraḥ
|
pramukhe tasya sainyasya droṇo'vasthitanāyakaḥ||21||

22sindhurājastathā'tiṣṭhacchrīmānmerurivācalaḥ
|
sindhurājasya pārśvasthā aśvatthāmapurogamāḥ||22||

23sutāstava mahārāja triṃśattridaśasannibhāḥ
|
gāndhārarājaḥ kitavaḥ śalyo bhūriśravāstathā||23||

24pārśvataḥ sindhurājasya vyarājanta mahārathāḥ
|
tataḥ pravavṛte yuddhaṃ tumulaṃ lomaharṣaṇam||24||

25tāvakānāṃ pareṣāṃ ca mṛtyuṃ kṛtvā nivartanam||25||
