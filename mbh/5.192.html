Mahābhārata 5.192 (Udyogaparvan)

1bhīṣma uvāca|
śikhaṇḍivākyaṃ śrutvā'tha sa yakṣo bharatarṣabha
|
provāca manasā cintya daivenopanipīḍitaḥ ||1||

2bhavitavyaṃ tathā taddhi mama duḥkhāya kaurava
|
bhadre kāmaṃ kariṣyāmi samayaṃ tu nibodha me ||2||

3kiñcit kālāntare dāsye pulliṅgaṃ svamidaṃ tava
|
āgantavyaṃ tvayā kāle satyaṃ caiva vadasva me ||3||

4prabhuḥ saṃkalpasiddho'smi kāmacārī vihaṅgamaḥ
|
matprasādāt puraṃ caiva trāhi bandhūṃśca kevalam ||4||

5strīliṅgaṃ dhārayiṣyāmi tadevaṃ pārthivātmaje
|
satyaṃ me pratijānīhi kariṣyāmi priyaṃ tava ||5||

6śikhaṇḍyuvāca
|
pratidāsyāmi bhagavan pulliṅgaṃ tava suvrata
|
kiñcitkālāntaraṃ strītvaṃ dhārayasva niśācara ||6||

7pratiyāte daśārṇe tu pārthive hemavarmaṇi
|
kanyaiva hi bhaviṣyāmi puruṣastvaṃ bhaviṣyasi ||7||

8bhīṣma uvāca|
ityuktvā samayaṃ tatra cakrāte tāvubhau nṛpa
|
anyo'nyasyābhisandehe to saṃkrāmayatāṃ tataḥ ||8||

9strīliṅgaṃ dhārayāmāsa sthūṇāyakṣo'tha bhārata
|
yakṣarūpaṃ ca taddīptaṃ śikhaṇḍī pratyapadyata ||9||

10tataḥ śikhaṇḍī pāñcālyaḥ puṃstvamāsādya pārthiva
|
viveśa nagaraṃ hṛṣṭaḥ pitaraṃ ca samāsadat ||10||

11yathāvṛttaṃ tu tat sarvamācakhyau drupadasya tat
|
drupadastasya tacchrutvā harṣamāhārayat param ||11||

12sabhāryastacca sasmāra maheśvaravacastadā
|
tataḥ saṃpreṣayāmāsa daśārṇādhipaternṛpaḥ ||12||

13puruṣo'yaṃ mama sutaḥ śraddhattāṃ me bhavāniti
|
atha dāśārṇako rājā sahasā'bhyāgamattadā ||13||

14pañcālarājaṃ drupadaṃ duḥkhaśokasamanvitaḥ
|
tataḥ kāmpilyamāsādya daśārṇādhipatistataḥ ||14||

15preṣayāmāsa satkṛtya dūtaṃ brahmavidāṃ varam
|
brūhi madvacanāddūta pāñcālyaṃ taṃ nṛpādhamam ||15||

16yanme kanyāṃ svakanyārthe vṛtavānāsa durmate
|
phalaṃ tasyāvalepasya drakṣyasyadya na saṃśayaḥ ||16||

17evamuktaśca tenāsau brāhmaṇo rājasattama
|
dūttaḥ prayāto nagaraṃ dāśārṇanṛpacoditaḥ ||17||

18tata āsādayāmāsa purodhā drupadaṃ pure
|
tasmai pāñcālako rājā gāmarghyaṃ ca susatkṛtam ||18||

19prāpayāmāsa rājendra saha tena śikhaṇḍinā
|
tāṃ pūjāṃ nābhyanandat sa vākyaṃ cedamuvāca ha ||19||

20yaduktaṃ tena vīreṇa rājñā kāñcanavarmaṇā
|
yatte'hamadhamācāra duhitrā'smyabhivañcitaḥ ||20||

21tasya pāpasya karaṇāt phalaṃ prāpnuhi durmate
|
dehi yuddhaṃ narapate mamādya raṇamūrdhani ||21||

22uddhariṣyāmi te sadyaḥ sāmātyasutabāndhavam
|
tadupālambhasaṃyuktaṃ śrāvitaḥ kila pārthivaḥ ||22||

23daśārṇapatinā cokto mantrimadhye purodhasā
|
abhavadbharataśreṣṭha drupadaḥ praṇayānataḥ ||23||

24yadāha māṃ bhavān brahman sambandhivacanādvacaḥ
|
asyottaraṃ prativaco dūto rājñe vadiṣyati ||24||

25tataḥ saṃpreṣayāmāsa drupado'pi mahātmane
|
hiraṇyavarmaṇe dūtaṃ brāhmaṇaṃ vedapāragam ||25||

26tamāgamya tu rājānaṃ daśārṇādhipatiṃ tadā
|
tadvākyamādade rājan yaduktaṃ drupadena ha ||26||

27āgamaḥ kriyatāṃ vyaktaḥ kumāro'yaṃ suto mama
|
mithyaitaduktaṃ kenāpi tadaśraddheyamityuta ||27||

28tataḥ sa rājā drupadasya śrutvā vimarṣayukto yuvatīrvariṣṭhāḥ
|
saṃpreṣayāmāsa sucārurūpāḥ śikhaṇḍinaṃ strī pumānveti vettum ||28||

29tāḥ preṣitāstattvabhāvaṃ viditvā prītyā rājñe tacchaśaṃsurhi sarvam
|
śikhaṇḍinaṃ puruṣaṃ kauravendra dāśārṇarājāya mahānubhāvam ||29||

30tataḥ kṛtvā tu rājā sa āgamaṃ prītimānatha
|
sambandhinā samāgamya hṛṣṭo vāsamuvāsa ha ||30||

31śikhaṇḍine ca muditaḥ prādādvittaṃ janeśvaraḥ
|
hastino'śvāṃśca gāścaiva dāsyo'tha bahulāstathā ||31||

32pūjitaśca pratiyayau nirbhartsya tanayāṃ kila
|
vinītakilbiṣe prīte hemavarmaṇi pārthive ||32||

33pratiyāte daśārṇe tu hṛṣṭarūpā śikhaṇḍinī
|
kasyacittvatha kālasya kubero naravāhanaḥ
|
lokayātrāṃ prakurvāṇaḥ sthūṇasyāgānniveśanam ||33||

34sa tadgṛhasyopari vartamāna ālokayāmāsa dhanādhigoptā
|
sthūṇasya yakṣasya viveśa veśma svalaṅkṛtaṃ mālyaguṇairvicitraiḥ ||34||

35lājyaiśca gandhaiśca tathā vitānairabhyarcitaṃ dhūpanadhūpitaṃ ca
|
dhvajaiḥ patākābhiralaṅkṛtaṃ ca bhakṣyānnapeyāmiṣadantahomam ||35||

36tat sthānaṃ tasya dṛṣṭvā tu sarvataḥ samalaṅkṛtam
|
maṇiratnasuvarṇānāṃ mālābhiḥ paripūritam ||36||

37nānākusumagandhāḍhyaṃ siktasaṃmṛṣṭaśobhitam
|
athābravīdyakṣapatistānyakṣānanugāṃstadā ||37||

38svalaṅkṛtamidaṃ veśma sthūṇasyāmitavikramāḥ
|
nopasarpati māṃ caiva kasmādadya sa mandadhīḥ ||38||

39yasmājjānan sa mandātmā māmasau nopasarpati
|
tasmāttasmai mahādaṇḍo dhāryaḥ syāditi me matiḥ ||39||

40yakṣā ūcuḥ|‌> drupadasya sutā rājan rājño jātā śikhaṇḍinī
|
tasyā nimitte kasmiṃścit prādāt puruṣalakṣaṇam ||40||

41agrahīllakṣaṇaṃ strīṇāṃ strībhūto tiṣṭhate gṛhe
|
nopasarpati tenāsau savrīḍaḥ strīsarūpavān ||41||

42etasmāt kāraṇādrājan sthūṇo na tvādya sarpati
|
śrutvā kuru yathānyāyaṃ vimānamiha tiṣṭhatām ||42||

43ānīyatāṃ sthūṇa iti tato yakṣādhipo'bravīt
|
kartā'smi nigrahaṃ tasya pratyuvāca punaḥ punaḥ ||43||

44so'bhyagacchata yakṣendramāhūtaḥ pṛthivīpate
|
strīsarūpo mahārāja tasthau vrīḍāsamanvitaḥ ||44||

45taṃ śaśāpātha saṃkuddho dhanadaḥ kurunandana
|
evameva bhavatvadya strītvaṃ pāpasya guhyakāḥ ||45||

46tato'bravīdyakṣapatirmahātmā yasmādadāstvavamanyeha yakṣān
|
śikhaṇḍino lakṣaṇaṃ pāpabuddheḥ strīlakṣaṇaṃ cāgrahīḥ pāpakarman ||46||

47apravṛttaṃ sudurbuddhe yasmādetattvayā kṛtam
|
tasmādadya prabhṛtyeva strī tvaṃ sā puruṣastathā ||47||

48tataḥ prasādayāmāsuryakṣā vaiśravaṇaṃ kila
|
sthūṇasyārthe kuruṣvāntaṃ śāpasyeti punaḥ punaḥ||48||

49tato mahātmā yakṣendraḥ pratyuvācānugāminaḥ
|
sarvān yakṣagaṇāṃstāta śāpasyāntacikīrṣayā ||49||

50śikhaṇḍini hate yakṣāḥ svaṃ rūpaṃ pratipatsyate
|
sthūṇo yakṣo nirudvego bhavatviti mahāmanāḥ||50||

51ityuktvā bhagavān devo yakṣarājaḥ supūjitaḥ
|
prayayau sahitaḥ sarvairnimeṣāntaracāribhiḥ ||51||

52sthūṇastu śāpaṃ saṃprāpya tatraiva nyavasattadā
|
samaye cāgamattūrṇaṃ śikhaṇḍī taṃ kṣapācaram ||52||

53so'bhigamyābravīdvākyaṃ prāpto'smi bhagavanniti
|
tamabravīttataḥ sthūṇaḥ prīto'smīti punaḥ punaḥ ||53||

54ārjavenāgataṃ dṛṣṭvā rājaputraṃ śikhaṇḍinam
|
sarvameva yathāvṛttamācacakṣe śikhaṇḍine ||54||

55yakṣa uvāca|
śapto vaiśravaṇenāhaṃ tvatkṛte pārthivātmaja
|
gacchedānīṃ yathākāmaṃ cara lokān yathāsukham||55||

56diṣṭametat purā manye na śakyamativartitum
|
gamanaṃ tava ceto hi paulastyasya ca darśanam||56||

57bhīṣma uvāca|
evamuktaḥ śikhaṇḍī tu sthūṇayakṣeṇa bhārata
|
pratyājagāma nagaraṃ harṣeṇa mahatā vṛtaḥ ||57||

58pūjayāmāsa vividhairgandhamālyairmahādhanaiḥ
|
dvijātīn devatāścaiva caityānatha catuṣpathān ||58||

59drupadaḥ saha putreṇa siddhārthena śikhaṇḍinā
|
mudaṃ ca paramāṃ lebhe pāñcālyaḥ saha bāndhavaiḥ ||59||

60śiṣyārthaṃ pradadau cātha droṇāya kurupuṅgava
|
śikhaṇḍinaṃ mahārāja putraṃ strīpūrviṇaṃ tathā ||60||

61pratipede catuṣpādaṃ dhanurvedaṃ nṛpātmajaḥ
|
śikhaṇḍī saha yuṣmābhirdhṛṣṭadyumnaśca pārṣataḥ ||61||

62mama tvetaccarāstāta yathāvat pratyavedayan
|
jaḍāndhabadhirākārā ye muktā drupade mayā ||62||

63evameṣa mahārāja strīpumān drupadātmajaḥ
|
sa saṃbhūtaḥ kuruśreṣṭha śikhaṇḍī rathasattamaḥ ||63||

64jyeṣṭhā kāśipateḥ kanyā ambānāmeti viśrutā
|
drupadasya kule jātā śikhaṇḍī bharatarṣama ||64||

65nāhamenaṃ dhanuṣpāṇiṃ yuyutsuṃ samupasthitam
|
muhūrtamapi paśyeyaṃ prahareyaṃ na cāpyuta ||65||

66vratametanmama sadā pṛthivyāmapi viśrutam
|
striyāṃ strīpūrvake caiva strīnāmni strīsarūpiṇi ||66||

67na muñceyamahaṃ bāṇamiti kauravanandana
|
na hanyāmahametena kāraṇena śikhaṇḍinam ||67||

68etattattvamahaṃ veda janma tāta śikhaṇḍinaḥ
|
tato nainaṃ haniṣyāmi samareṣvātatāyinam ||68||

69yadi bhīṣmaḥ striyaṃ hanyāt santaḥ kuryurvigarhaṇam
|
nainaṃ tasmāddhaniṣyāmi dṛṣṭvā'pi samare sthitam ||69||

70vaiśampāyana uvāca|
etacchrutvā tu kauravyo rājā duryodhanastadā
|
muhūrtamiva sa dhyātvā bhīṣme yuktamamanyata ||70||
