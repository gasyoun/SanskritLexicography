Mahābhārata 3.55 (Āraṇyakaparvan)

1bṛhadaśva uvāca| tebhyaḥ pratijñāya nalaḥ kariṣya iti bhārata
|
athaitānparipapraccha kṛtāñjalirupasthitaḥ||1||

2ke vai bhavantaḥ kaścāsau yasyāhaṃ dūta īpsitaḥ
|
kiṃ ca tadvo mayā kāryaṃ kathayadhvaṃ yathātatham||2||

3evamukte naiṣadhena maghavānabhyabhāṣata
|
amarānvai nibodhāsmāndamayantyarthamāgatān||3||

4ahamindro'yamagniśca tathaivāyamapāṃ patiḥ
|
śarīrāntakaro nṝṇāṃ yamo'yamapi pārthiva||4||

5tvaṃ vai samāgatānasmāndamayantyai nivedaya
|
lokapālā mahendrādyāḥ samāyānti didṛkṣavaḥ||5||

6prāptumicchanti devāstvāṃ śakrognirvaruṇo yamaḥ
|
teṣāmanyatamaṃ devaṃ patitve varayasva ha||6||

7evamuktaḥ sa śakreṇa nalaḥ prāñjalirabravīt
|
ekārthaṃ samupetaṃ māṃ na preṣayitumarhatha||7||

8katha hi jātasaṃkalpaḥ striyamutsṛjate pumān
|
parārthamīdṛśaṃ vaktaṃ tatkṣamantu mareśvarāḥ||8||

9devā ūcuḥ|kariṣya iti saṃśrutya pūrvamasmāsu naiṣadha
|
na kariṣyasi kasmāttvaṃ vraja naiṣadha mā ciram||9||

10bṛhadaśva uvāca|evamuktaḥ sa devaistairnaiṣadhaḥ punarabravīt
|
surakṣitāni veśmāni praveṣṭuṃ kathamutsahe||10||

11pravekṣyasīti taṃ śakraḥ punarevābhyabhāṣata
|
jagāma sa tathetyuktvā damayantyā niveśanam||11||

12dadarśa tatra vaidarbhīṃ sakhīgaṇasamāvṛtām
|
dedīpyamānāṃ vapuṣā śriyā ca varavarṇinīm||12||

13atīvasukumārāṅgīṃ tanumadhyāṃ sulocanām
|
ākṣipantīmiva prabhāṃ śaśinaḥ svena tejasā||13||

14tasya dṛṣṭvaiva vavṛdhe kāmastāṃ cāruhāsinīm
|
satyaṃ cikīrṣamāṇastu dhārayāmāsa hṛcchayam||14||

15tatastā naiṣadhaṃ dṛṣṭvā saṃbhrāntāḥ paramāṅganāḥ
|
āsanebhyaḥ samutpetustejasā tasya dharṣitāḥ||15||

16praśaśaṃsuśca suprītā nalaṃ tā vismayānvitāḥ
|
na cainamabhyabhāṣanta manobhistvabhyapūjayan||16||

17ahorūpamahokāntiraho dhairyaṃ mahātmanaḥ
|
ko'yaṃ devo'thavā yakṣo gandharvo vā bhaviṣyati||17||

18na tāstaṃ śaknuvanti sma vyāhartumapi kiṃcana
|
tejasā dharṣitāstasya lajjāvatyo varāṅganāḥ||18||

19athainaṃ smayamānaṃ tu smitapūrvābhibhāṣiṇī
|
damayantī nalaṃ vīramabhyabhāṣata vismitā||19||

20kastvaṃ sarvānavadyāṅga mama hṛcchayavardhana
|
prāptosyamaravadvīra jñātumicchāmi te'nagha||20||

21kathamāgamanaṃ ceha kathaṃ cāsi na lakṣitaḥ
|
surakṣitaṃ hi me veśma rājā caivograśāsanaḥ||21||

22evamuktastu vaidarbhyā nalastāṃ pratyuvāca ha
|
nala uvāca|
nalaṃ māṃ viddhi kalyāṇi devadūtamihāgatam||22||

23devāstvāṃ prāptumicchanti śakro'gnirvaruṇo yamaḥ
|
teṣāmanyatamaṃ devaṃ patiṃ varaya śobhane||23||

24teṣāmeva prabhāveṇa praviṣṭo'hamalakṣitaḥ
|
praviśantaṃ na māṃ kaścidapaśyannāpyavārayat||24||

25etadarthamahaṃ bhadre preṣitaḥ surasattamaiḥ
|
etacchrutvā śubhe buddhiṃ prakuruṣva yathecchasi||25||
