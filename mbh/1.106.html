Mahābhārata 1.106 (Ādiparvan)

1vaiśaṃpāyana uvāca|
tataḥ satyavatī kāle vadhūṃ snātāmṛtau tadā
|
saṃveśayantī śayane śanairvacanamabravīt||1||

2kausalye devaraste'sti so'dya tvā'nupravekṣyati
|
apramattā pratīkṣainaṃ niśīthe hyāgamiṣyati||2||

3śvaśrvāstadvacanaṃ śrutvā śayānā śayane śubhe
|
sācintayattadā bhīṣmamanyāṃśca kurupuṅgavān||3||

4tato'mbikāyāṃ prathamaṃ niyuktaḥ satyavāgṛṣiḥ
|
dīpyamāneṣu dīpeṣu śaraṇaṃ praviveśa ha||4||

5tasya kṛṣṇasya kapilāṃ jaṭāṃ dīpte ca locane
|
babhrūṇi caiva śmaśrūṇi dṛṣṭvā devī nyamīlayat||5||

6saṃbabhūva tayā sārdhaṃ mātuḥ priyacikīrṣayā
|
bhayātkāśisutā taṃ tu nāśaknodabhivīkṣitum||6||

7tato niṣkrāntamāgamya mātā putramuvāca ha
|
apyasyā guṇavānputra rājaputro bhaviṣyati||7||

8niśamya tadvaco māturvyāsaḥ satyavatīsutaḥ
|
nāgāyutasamaprāṇo vidvānrājarṣisattamaḥ||8||

9mahābhāgo mahāvīryo mahābuddhirbhaviṣyati
|
tasya cāpi śataṃ putrā bhaviṣyanti mahātmanaḥ||9||

10kiṃ tu mātuḥ sa vaiguṇyādandha eva bhaviṣyati
|
tasya tadvacanaṃ śrutvā mātā putramathābravīt||10||

11nāndhaḥ kurūṇāṃ nṛpatiranurūpastapodhana
|
jñātivaṃśasya goptāraṃ pitṝṇāṃ vaṃśavardhanam||11||

12dvitīyaṃ kuruvaṃśasya rājānaṃ dātumarhasi
|
sa tatheti pratijñāya niścakrāma mahāyaśāḥ||12||

13sā'pi kālena kausalyā suṣuvendhaṃ tamātmajam
|
punareva tu sā devī paribhāṣya snuṣāṃ tataḥ||13||

14ṛṣimāvāhayatsatyā yathā pūrvamarindama
|
tatastenaiva vidhinā maharṣistāmapadyata||14||

15ambālikāmathā'bhyāgādṛṣiṃ dṛṣṭvā ca sā'pi tam
|
vivarṇā pāṇḍusaṃkāśā samapadyata bhārata||15||

16tāṃ bhītāṃ pāṇḍusaṃkāśāṃ viṣaṇṇāṃ prekṣya bhārata
|
vyāsaḥ satyavatīputra idaṃ vacanamabravīt||16||

17yasmātpāṇḍutvamāpannā virūpaṃ prekṣya māmiha
|
tasmādeṣa sutaste vai pāṇḍureva bhaviṣyati||17||

18nāma cāsyaitadeveha bhaviṣyati śubhānane
|
ityuktvā sa nirākrāmadbhagavānṛṣisattamaḥ||18||

19tato niṣkrāntamālokya satyā putramathābravīt
|
śaśaṃsa sa punarmātre tasya bālasya pāṇḍutām||19||

20taṃ mātā punarevānyamekaṃ putramayācata
|
tatheti ca maharṣistāṃ mātaraṃ pratyabhāṣata||20||

21tataḥ kumāraṃ sā devī prāptakālamajījanat
|
pāṇḍuṃ lakṣaṇasaṃpannaṃ dīpyamānamiva śriyā||21||

22yasya putrāmaheṣvāsā jajñire pañca pāṇḍavāḥ
|
ṛtukāle tato jyeṣṭhāṃ vadhūṃ tasmai nyayojayat||22||

23sā tu rūpaṃ ca gandhaṃ ca maharṣeḥ pravicintya tam
|
nākarodvacanaṃ devyā bhayātsurasutopamā||23||

24tataḥ svairbhūṣaṇairdāsīṃ bhūṣayitvā'psaropamām
|
preṣayāmāsa kṛṣṇāya tataḥ kāśipateḥ sutā||24||

25sā tamṛṣimanuprāptaṃ pratyudgamyābhivādya ca
|
saṃviveśābhyanujñātā satkṛtyopacacāra ha||25||

26kāmopabhogena rahastasyāṃ tuṣṭimagādṛṣiḥ
|
tayā sahoṣito rājanmaharṣiḥ saṃśitavrataḥ||26||

27uttiṣṭhannabravīdenāmabhujiṣyā bhaviṣyasi
|
ayaṃ ca te śubhe garbhaḥ śreyānudaramāgataḥ
|
dharmātmā bhavitā loke sarvabuddhimatāṃ varaḥ||27||

28sa jajñe viduro nāma kṛṣṇadvaipāyanātmajaḥ
|
dhṛtarāṣṭrasya vai bhrātā pāṇḍoścaiva mahātmanaḥ||28||

29dharmo vidurarūpeṇa śāpāttasya mahātmanaḥ
|
māṇḍavyasyārthatattvajñaḥ kāmakrodhavivarjitaḥ||29||

30kṛṣṇadvaipāyano'pyetatsatyavatyai nyavedayat
|
pralambhamātmanaścaiva śūdrāyāḥ putrajanma ca||30||

31sa dharmasyānṛṇo bhūtvā punarmātrā sametya ca
|
tasyai garbhaṃ samāvedya tatraivāntaradhīyata||31||

32ete vicitravīryasya kṣetre dvaipāyanādapi
|
jajñire devagarbhābhāḥ kuruvaṃśavivardhanāḥ||32||
