Mahābhārata 7.5 (Droṇaparvan)

1sañjaya uvāca|
rathasthaṃ puruṣavyāghraṃ dṛṣṭvā karṇamavasthitam
|
dṛṣṭo duryodhano rājannidaṃ vacanamabravīt||1||

2sanāthamiva manye'haṃ bhavatā pālitaṃ balam
|
atra kiṃ nu samarthaṃ yaddhitaṃ tat saṃpradhāryatām||2||

3karṇa uvāca|
brūhi naḥ puruṣavyāghra tvaṃ hi prājñatamo nṛpa
|
yathā cārthapatiḥ kṛtyaṃ paśyate na tathetaraḥ||3||

4te sma sarve tava vacaḥ śrotukāmā nareśvara
|
nānyāyyaṃ hi bhavān vākyaṃ būyāditi matirmama||4||

5duryodhana uvāca|
bhīṣmaḥ senāpraṇetāsīdvayasā vikrameṇa ca
|
śrutena copasaṃpannaḥ sarvairyodhagaṇaistathā||5||

6tenātiyaśasā karṇa ghnatā śatrugaṇānmama
|
suyuddhena daśāhāni pālitāḥ smo mahātmanā||6||

7tasminnasukaraṃ karma kṛtavatyāsthite divam
|
kaṃ nu senāpraṇetāraṃ manyase tadanantaram||7||

8na vinā nāyakaṃ senā muhūrtamapi tiṣṭhati
|
āhaveṣvāhavaśreṣṭha netṛhīneva naurjale||8||

9yathā hyakarṇadhārā nau rathaścāsārathiryathā
|
dravedyatheṣṭaṃ tadvatsyādṛte senāpatiṃ balam||9||

10adeśiko yathā sārthaḥ sarvaḥ kṛcchraṃ samṛcchati
|
anāyakā tathā senā sarvān doṣānsamarchati||10||

11sa bhavān vīkṣya sarveṣu māmakeṣu mahātmasu
|
paśya senāpatiṃ yuktamanu śāntanavādiha||11||

12yaṃ hi senāpraṇetāraṃ bhavān vakṣyati saṃyuge
|
taṃ vayaṃ sahitāḥ sarve kariṣyāmo na saṃśayaḥ||12||

13karṇa uvāca|
sarva eva mahātmāna ime puruṣasattamāḥ
|
senāpatitvamarhanti nātra kāryā vicāraṇā||13||

14kulasaṃhananajñānairbalavikramabuddhibhiḥ
|
yuktāḥ śrutajñā dhīmanta āhaveṣvanivartinaḥ||14||

15yugapanna tu te śakyāḥ kartuṃ sarve puraḥsarāḥ
|
eka eva tu kartavyo yasmin vaiśeṣikā guṇāḥ||15||

16anyonyaspardhināṃ hyeṣāṃ yadyekaṃ yaṃ kariṣyasi
|
śeṣā vimanaso vyaktaṃ na yotsyanti hitāstava||16||

17ayaṃ ca sarvayodhānāmācāryaḥ sthaviro guruḥ
|
yuktaḥ senāpatiḥ kartuṃ droṇaḥ śastrabhṛtāṃ varaḥ||17||

18ko hi tiṣṭhati durdharṣe droṇe śastrabhṛtāṃ vare
|
senāpatiḥsyādanyosmācchukrāṃgirasadarśanāt||18||

19na ca so'pyasti te yodhaḥ sarvarājasu bhārata
|
droṇaṃ yaḥ samare yāntamanuyāsyati saṃyuge||19||

20eṣa senāpraṇetṝṇāmeṣa śastrabhṛtāmapi
|
eṣa buddhimatāṃ caiva śreṣṭho rājan gurustava||20||

21evaṃ duryodhanācāryamāśu senāpatiṃ kuru
|
jigīṣanto surān saṃkhye kārtikemivāmarāḥ||21||
