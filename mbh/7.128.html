Mahābhārata 7.128 (Droṇaparvan)

1sañjaya uvāca|
samuttīrṇaṃ rathānīkaṃ pāṇḍavaṃ vihasan raṇe
|
vivārayiṣurācāryaḥ śaravarṣairavākirat ||1||

2pibanniva śaraughāṃstān droṇacāpaparicyutān
|
so'bhyadravata sodaryānmohayan balamāyayā ||2||

3taṃ mṛdhe vegamāsthāya nṛpāḥ paramadhanvinaḥ
|
coditāstava putraiśca sarvataḥ paryavārayan ||3||

4sa taistu saṃvṛto bhīmaḥ prahasanniva bhārata
|
udyacchan sa gadāṃ tebhyaḥ sughorāṃ siṃhavannadan ||4||

5avāsṛjacca vegena śatrupakṣavināśinīm
|
indrāśanirivendreṇa praviddhā saṃhatātmanā
|
prāmathnātsā mahārāja sainikāṃstava saṃyuge ||5||

6ghoṣeṇa mahatā rājan pūrayantīva medinīm
|
jvalaṃtī tejasā bhīmā trāsayāmāsa te sutān ||6||

7tāṃ patantīṃ mahāvegāṃ dṛṣṭvā tejo'bhisaṃvṛtām
|
prādravaṃstāvakāḥ sarve nadanto bhairavān ravān ||7||

8taṃ ca śabdamasahyaṃ ve tasyāḥ saṃlakṣya māriṣa
|
prāpatanmanujāstatra rathebhyo rathinastadā ||8||

9te hanyamānā bhīmena gadāhastena tāvakāḥ
|
prādravanta raṇe bhītā vyāghraghrātā mṛgā iva ||9||

10sa tān vidrāvya kaunteyaḥ saṃkhye'mitrān durāsadān
|
suparṇa iva vegena pakṣirāḍatyagāccamūm ||10||

11tathā tu viprakurvāṇaṃ rathayūthapayūthapam
|
bhāradvājo mahārāja bhīmasenaṃ samabhyayāt ||11||

12bhīmaṃ tu samare droṇo vārayitvā śarormibhiḥ
|
akarotsahasā nādaṃ pāṇḍūnāṃ bhayamādadhat ||12||

13tadyuddhamāsīt sumahaddhoraṃ devāsuropamam
|
droṇasya ca mahārāja bhīmasya ca mahātmanaḥ ||13||

14yadā tu viśikhaistīkṣṇairdroṇacāpaviniḥsṛtaiḥ
|
vadhyante samare vīrāḥ śataśo'tha sahasraśaḥ ||14||

15tato rathādavaplutya vegamāsthāya pāṇḍavaḥ
|
nimīlya nayane rājan padātirdroṇamabhyayāt ||15||

16aṃse śiro bhīmasenaḥ karau kṛtvorasi sthirau
|
vegamāsthāya balavān manonilagarutmatām ||16||

17yathā hi govṛṣo varṣaṃ pratigṛhṇāti līlayā
|
tathā bhīmo naravyāghraḥ śaravarṣaṃ samagrahīt ||17||

18sa vadhyamānaḥ samare rathaṃ droṇasya māriṣa
|
īṣāyāṃ pāṇinā gṛhya pracikṣepa mahābalaḥ ||18||

19droṇastu sattvaro rājan kṣipto bhīmena saṃyuge
|
rathamanyaṃ samāruhya vyūhadvāraṃ yayau punaḥ ||19||

20tamāyāntaṃ tathā dṛṣṭvā bhagnotsāhaṃ guruṃ tadā
|
gatvā vegāt punarbhīmo dhuraṃ gṛhya rathasya tu ||20||

21tamapyatirathaṃ bhīmaścikṣepa bhṛśaroṣitaḥ
|
evamaṣṭau rathāḥ kṣiptā bhīmasenena līlayā ||21||

22vyadṛśyata nimeṣeṇa punaḥ svarathamāsthitaḥ
|
dṛśyate tāvakairyodhairvismayotphullalocanaiḥ ||22||

23tasmin kṣaṇe tasya yantā tūrṇamaśvānacodayat
|
bhīmasenasya kauravyastada‌dbhutamivābhavat ||23||

24tataḥ svarathamāsthāya bhīmaseno mahābalaḥ
|
abhyadravata vegena tava putrasya vāhinīm ||24||

25sa mṛdūnankṣatriyānājau vāto vṛkṣānivoddhataḥ
|
āgacchaddārayan senāṃ sindhuvego nagāniva ||25||

26bhojānīkaṃ samāsādya hārdikyenābhirakṣitam
|
pramathya tarasā vīrastadapyatibalo'bhyayāt ||26||

27saṃtrāsayannanīkāni talaśabdena pāṇḍavaḥ
|
ajayat sarvasainyāni śārdūla iva govṛṣān ||27||

28bhojānīkamatikramya daradānāṃ ca vāhinīm
|
tathā mlecchagaṇānanyānbahūnyuddhaviśāradān ||28||

29sātyakiṃ caiva saṃprekṣya yudhyamānaṃ mahāratham
|
rathena yattaḥ kaunteyo vegena prayayau tadā ||29||

30bhīmaseno mahārāja draṣṭukāmo dhanañjayam
|
atītya samare yodhāṃstāvakān pāṇḍunandanaḥ ||30||

31so'paśyadarjunaṃ tatra yudhyamānaṃ mahāratham
|
saindhavasya vadhārthaṃ hi parākrāntaṃ parākramī ||31||

32taṃ dṛṣṭvā puruṣavyāghraścukrośa mahato ravān
|
prāvṛ‌ṭkāle mahārāja nardanniva balāhakaḥ ||32||

33taṃ tasya ninadaṃ ghoraṃ pārthaḥ śuśrāva nardataḥ
|
vāsudevaśca kauravya bhīmasenasya saṃyuge ||33||

34tau śrutvā yugapadvīrau ninadaṃ tasya śuṣmiṇaḥ
|
punaḥ punaḥ prāṇadatāṃ didṛkṣantau vṛkodaram ||34||

35tataḥ pārtho mahānādaṃ muñcanvai mādhavaśca ha
|
abhyayātāṃ mahārāja nardantau govṛṣāviva ||35||

36bhīmasenaravaṃ śrutvā phālgunasya ca dhanvinaḥ
|
aprīyata mahārāja dharmaputro yudhiṣṭhiraḥ ||36||

37viśokaścābhavadrājā śrutvā taṃ ninadaṃ tayoḥ
|
dhanañjayasya samare jayamāśāstavānvibhuḥ ||37||

38tathā tu nardamāne vai bhīmasene madotkaṭe
|
smitaṃ kṛtvā mahābāhurdharmaputro yudhiṣṭhiraḥ ||38||

39hṛdgataṃ manasā prāha dhyātvā dharmabhṛtāṃ varaḥ
|
dattā bhīma tvayā saṃvitkṛtaṃ guruvacastathā ||39||

40na hi teṣāṃ jayo yuddhe yeṣāṃ dveṣṭāsi pāṇḍava
|
diṣṭyā jīvati saṃgrāme savyasācī dhanañjayaḥ ||40||

41diṣṭyā ca kuśalī vīraḥ sātyakiḥ satyavikramaḥ
|
diṣṭyā śṛṇomi garjantau vāsudevadhanañjayau ||41||

42yena śakraṃ raṇe jitvā tarpito havyavāhanaḥ
|
sa hantā dviṣatāṃ saṃkhye diṣṭya jīvati phālgunaḥ ||42||

43yasya bāhubalaṃ sarve vayamāśritya jīvitāḥ
|
sa hantā ripusainyānāṃ diṣṭyā jīvati phālgunaḥ ||43||

44nivātakavacā yena devairapi sudurjayāḥ
|
nirjitā dhanuṣaikena diṣṭyā pārthaḥ sa jīvati ||44||

45kauravān sahitān sarvān gograhārthe samāgatān
|
yo'jayanmatsyanagare diṣṭyā pārthaḥ sa jīvati ||45||

46kālakeyasahasrāṇi caturdaśa mahāraṇe
|
yo'vadhīdbhujavīryeṇa diṣṭyā pārthaḥ sa jīvati ||46||

47gandharvarājaṃ balinaṃ duryodhanakṛte ca vai
|
jitavān yo'stravīryeṇa diṣṭyā pārthaḥ sa jīvati ||47||

48kirīṭamālī balavāñchvetāśvaḥ kṛṣṇasārathiḥ
|
mama priyaśca satataṃ diṣṭyā pārthaḥ sa jīvati ||48||

49putraśokābhisantaptaścikīrṣan karma duṣkaram
|
jayadrathavadhānveṣī pratijñāṃ kṛtavān hi yaḥ ||49||

50kaccitsa saindhavaṃ saṃkhye haniṣyati dhanañjayaḥ
|
kaccittīrṇapratijñaṃ hi vāsudevena rakṣitam ||50||

51anastamita āditye sameṣyāmyahamarjunam
|
kaccitsaindhavako rājā duryodhanahite rataḥ ||51||

52nandayiṣyatyamitrānhi phālgunena nipātitaḥ
|
kaccidduryodhano rājā phālgunena nipātitam ||52||

53dṛṣṭvā saindhavakaṃ saṃkhye śamamasmāsu dhāsyati
|
dṛṣṭvā vinihatān bhrātṝn bhīmasenena saṃyuge
|
kaccidduryodhano mandaḥ śamamasmāsu dhāsyati ||53||

54dṛṣṭvā cānyānmahāyodhān pātitān dharaṇītale
|
kaccidduryodhano mandaḥ paścāttāpaṃ gamiṣyati ||54||

55kaccidbhīṣmeṇa no vairaṃ śamamekena yāsyati
|
śeṣasya rakṣaṇārthaṃ ca sandhāsyati suyodhanaḥ ||55||

56evaṃ bahuvidhaṃ tasya rājñaścintayatastadā
|
kṛpayā'bhiparītasya ghoraṃ yuddhamavartata ||56||
