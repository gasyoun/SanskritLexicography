Mahābhārata 8.14 (Karṇaparvan)

1sañjaya uvāca|
śrutakarmā tato rājaṃścitrasenaṃ mahīpatim
|
ājaghne samare kruddhaḥ pañcāśadbhiḥ śilīmukhaiḥ ||1||

2abhisārastu taṃ rājannavabhirnataparvabhiḥ
|
śrutakarmāṇamāhatya sūtaṃ vivyādha pañcabhiḥ ||2||

3śrutakarmā tataḥ kruddhaścitrasenaṃ camūmukhe
|
nārācena sutīkṣṇena marmadeśe samārpayat ||3||

4so'tividdho mahārāja nārācena mahātmanā
|
mūrchāmabhiyayau vīraḥ kaśmalaṃ cāviveśa ha ||4||

5etasminnantare cainaṃ śrutakīrtirmahāyaśāḥ
|
navatyā jagatīpālaṃ chādayāmāsa patribhiḥ ||5||

6pratilabhya tataḥ saṃjñāṃ citraseno mahārathaḥ
|
dhanuściccheda bhallena taṃ ca vivyādha saptabhiḥ ||6||

7so'nyatkārmukamādāya vegaghnaṃ rukmabhūṣitam
|
citrarūpadharaṃ cakre citrasenaṃ śarormibhiḥ ||7||

8sa śaraiścitrito rājā citramālyadharo yuvā
|
yuveva samare'śobhadgoṣṭhīmadhye svalaṃkṛtaḥ ||8||

9śrutakarmāṇamatha vai nārācena stanāntare
|
bibheda tarasā śūrastiṣṭha tiṣṭheti cābravīt ||9||

10śrutakarmā'pi samare nārācena samarpitaḥ
|
sustrāva rudhiraṃ tatra gairikārdra ivācalaḥ ||10||

11tataḥ sa rudhirāktāṅgo rudhireṇa kṛtacchaviḥ
|
rarājaḥ samare vīraḥ sapuṣpa iva kiṃśukaḥ ||11||

12śrutakarmā tato rājañśatruṇā samabhidrutaḥ
|
śatrusaṃvāraṇaṃ kruddho dvidhā ciccheda kārmukam ||12||

13athainaṃ chinnadhanvānaṃ nārācānāṃ śataistribhiḥ
|
chādayansamare rājan vivyādha ca supatribhiḥ ||13||

14tato'pareṇa bhallena tīkṣṇena niśitena ca
|
jahāra saśirastrāṇaṃ śirastasya mahātmanaḥ ||14||

15tacchiro nyapatadbhūmau citrasenasya dīptimat
|
yadṛcchayā yathā candraścyutaḥ svargānmahītalam ||15||

16rājānaṃ nihataṃ dṛṣṭvā te'bhisāraṃ tu māriṣa
|
abhyadravanta vegena citrasenasya sainikāḥ ||16||

17tataḥ kruddho maheṣvāsastatsainyaṃ prādravaccharaiḥ
|
antakāle yathā kruddhaḥ sarvabhūtāni pretarāṭ ||17||

18te vadhyamānāḥ samare tava pautreṇa dhanvinā
|
vyadravanta diśastūrṇaṃ dāvadagdhā iva dvipāḥ ||18||

19tāṃstu vidravato dṛṣṭvā nirutsāhān dviṣajjaye
|
drāvayanniṣubhistīkṣṇaiḥ śrutakarmā vyarocata ||19||

20prativindhyastataścitraṃ bhittvā pañcabhirāśugaiḥ
|
sārathiṃ ca tribhirviddhvā dhvajamekeṣuṇā'pi ca ||20||

21taṃ citro navabhirbhallairbāhvorurasi cārpayat
|
svarṇapuṃkhaiḥ prasannāgnaiḥ kaṅkabarhiṇavājitaiḥ ||21||

22prativindhyo dhanuśchittvā tasya bhārata sāyakaiḥ
|
pañcabhirniśitairbāṇairathainaṃ saḥ hi jaghnivān ||22||

23tataḥ śaktiṃ mahārāja svarṇaghaṇṭāṃ durāsadām
|
prāhiṇottava pautrāya ghorāmagniśikhāmiva ||23||

24tāmāpantatīṃ sahasā maholkāpratimāṃ tadā
|
dvidhā ciccheda samare prativindhyo hasanniva ||24||

25sā papāta dvidhā chinnā prativindhyaśaraiḥ śitaiḥ
|
yugānte sarvabhūtāni trāsayantī yathā'śaniḥ ||25||

26śaktiṃ tāṃ prahatāṃ dṛṣṭvā citro gṛhya mahāgadām
|
prativindhyāya cikṣepa rukmajālavibhūṣitām ||26||

27sā jaghāna hayāṃstasya sārathiṃ ca mahāraṇe
|
rathaṃ pramṛdya vegena dharaṇīmanvapadyata ||27||

28etasminneva kāle tu rathādāplutya bhārata
|
śaktiṃ cikṣepa citrāya svarṇadaṇḍāmalaṃkṛtām ||28||

29tāmāpatantīṃ jagrāha citro rājanmahāmanāḥ
|
tatastāmeva cikṣepa prativindhyāya pārthivaḥ ||29||

30samāsādya raṇe śūraṃ prativindhyaṃ mahāprabhā
|
nirbhidya dakṣiṇaṃ bāhuṃ nipapāta mahītale
|
patitā bhāsayaccaiva taṃ deśamaśaniryathā ||30||

31prativindhyastato rājaṃstomaraṃ hemabhūṣitam
|
preṣayāmāsa saṃkruddhaścitrasya vadhakāṃkṣayā ||31||

32sa tasya gātrāvaraṇaṃ bhittvā hṛdayameva ca
|
jagāma dharaṇīṃ tūrṇaṃ mahoraga ivāśayam ||32||

33sa papāta tadā rājā tomareṇa samāhataḥ
|
prasārya vipulau bāhū pīnau parighasannibhau ||33||

34citraṃ saṃprekṣya nihataṃ tāvakā raṇaśobhinaḥ
|
abhyadravanta vegena prativindhyaṃ samaṃtataḥ ||34||

35sṛjanto vividhānbāṇān śataghnīśca sakiṃkiṇīḥ
|
tamavacchādayāmāsuḥ sūryamabhragaṇā iva ||35||

36tānvidhamya mahābāhuḥ śarajālena saṃyuge
|
vyadrāvayattava camūṃ vajrahasta ivāsurīm ||36||

37te vadhyamānāḥ samare tāvakāḥ pāṇḍavairnṛpa
|
viprakīryanta sahasā vātanunnā ghanā iva ||37||

38vipradrute bale tasminvadhyamāne samaṃtataḥ
|
drauṇireko'bhyayāttūrṇaṃ bhīmasenaṃ mahābalam ||38||

39tataḥ samāgamo ghoro babhūva sahasā tayoḥ
|
yathā devāsure yuddhe vṛtravāsavayoriva ||39||
