Mahābhārata 12.95 (Śāntiparvan)

1yudhiṣṭhira uvāca|
atha yo vijigīṣeta kṣatriyaḥ kṣatriyaṃ yudhi
|
kastasya vijaye dharmo hyetaṃ pṛṣṭo bravīhi me||1||

2bhīṣma uvāca|
sasahāyo'sahāyo vā rāṣṭramāgamya bhūmipaḥ
|
brūyādahaṃ vo rājeti rakṣiṣyāmi ca vaḥ sadā||2||

3mama dharmabaliṃ datta kiṃ vā māṃ pratipatsyatha
|
te cettamāgataṃ tatra vṛṇuyuḥ kuśalaṃ bhavet||3||

4te cedakṣatriyāḥ santo virudhyerankathaṃcana
|
sarvopāyairniyantavyā vikarmasthā narādhipa||4||

5aśastraṃ kṣatriyaṃ matvā śastraṃ gṛhṇātyathāparaḥ
|
trāṇāyāpyasamarthaṃ taṃ manyamānamatīva ca||5||

6yudhiṣṭhira uvāca|
atha yaḥ kṣatriyo rājā kṣatriyaṃ pratyupāvrajet
|
kathaṃ samprati yoddhavyastanme brūhi pitāmaha||6||

7bhīṣma uvāca|
naivāsannaddhakavaco yoddhavyaḥ kṣatriyo raṇe
|
eka ekena vācyaśca visṛjeti kṣipāmi ca||7||

8sa cetsannaddha āgacchetsannaddhavyaṃ tato bhavet
|
sa cetsasainya āgacchetsasainyastamathāhvayet||8||

9sa cennikṛtyā yuddhyeta nikṛtyā pratiyodhayet
|
atha ceddharmato yuddhyeddharmeṇaiva nivārayet||9||

10nāśvena rathinaṃ yāyādudiyādrathinaṃ rathī
|
vyasane na prahartavyaṃ na bhītāya jitāya ca||10||

11iṣurlipto na karṇī syādasatāmetadāyudham
|
yathārthameva yoddhavyaṃ na kruddhyeta jighāṃsataḥ||11||

12sādhūnāṃ tu yadā bhedātsādhuścedvyasanī bhavet
|
niṣprāṇo nābhihantavyo nānapatyaḥ kathaṃcana||12||

13bhagnaśastro vipannaśca kṛttajyo hatavāhanaḥ
|
cikitsyaḥ syātsvaviṣaye prāpyo vā svagṛhe bhavet||13||

14nirvraṇaśca sa moktavya eṣa dharmaḥ sanātanaḥ
|
tasmāddharmeṇa yoddhavyamiti svāyambhuvo'bravīt||14||

15satsu nityaḥ satāṃ dharmastamāsthāya na nāśayet
|
yo vai jayatyadharmeṇa kṣatriyo dharmasaṃgaraḥ||15||

16ātmānamātmanā hanti pāpo nikṛtijīvanaḥ
|
karma caitadasādhūnāmasādhūn sādhunā jayet||16||

17dharmeṇa nidhanaṃ śreyo na jayaḥ pāpakarmaṇā
|
nādharmaścarito rājansadyaḥ phalati gauriva||17||

18mūlāni ca praśākhāśca dahansamadhigacchati
|
pāpena karmaṇā vittaṃ labdhvā pāpaḥ prahṛṣyati||18||

19sa vardhamānaḥ steyena pāpaḥ pāpe prasajjati
|
na dharmo'stīti manvānaḥ śucīnavahasanniva||19||

20aśraddadhānaśca bhavedvināśamupagacchati
|
sambaddho vāruṇaiḥ pāśairamartya iva manyate||20||

21mahādṛtirivādhmātaḥ sukṛte naiva vartate
|
tataḥ samūlo hriyate nadīkūlādiva drumaḥ||21||

22athainamabhinindanti bhinnaṃ kumbhamivāśmani
|
tasmāddharmeṇa vijayaṃ kośaṃ lipseta bhūmipaḥ||22||
