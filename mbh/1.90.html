Mahābhārata 1.90 (Ādiparvan)

1aṣṭaka uvāca|
yadā'vaso nandane kāmarūpī saṃvatsarāṇāmayutaṃ śatānām
|
kiṃ kāraṇaṃ kārtayugapradhāna hitvā ca tvaṃ vasudhāmanvapadyaḥ||1||

2yayātiruvāca|
jñātiḥ suhṛtsvajano vā yatheha kṣīṇe vitte tyajyate mānavairhi
|
tathā tatra kṣīṇapuṇyaṃ manuṣyaṃ tyajanti sadyaḥ seśvarā devasaṅghāḥ||2||

3aṣṭaka uvāca|
tasminkathaṃ kṣīṇapuṇyā bhavanti saṃmuhyate me'tra mano'timātram
|
kiṃ vā viśiṣṭāḥ kasya dhāmopayānti tadvai brūhi kṣetravittvaṃ mato me||3||

4yayātiruvāca|
imaṃ bhaumaṃ narakaṃ te patanti lalāpyamānā naradeva sarve
|
te kaṅkagomāyubalāśanārthe kṣīṇe puṇye bahudhā pravrajanti||4||

5tasmādetadvarjanīyaṃ narendra duṣṭaṃ loke garhaṇīyaṃ ca karma
|
ākhyātaṃ te pārthiva sarvameva bhūyaścedānīṃ vada kiṃ te vadāmi||5||

6aṣṭaka uvāca|
yadā tu tānvitudante vayāṃsi tathā gṛdhrāḥ śitikaṇṭhāḥ pataṅgāḥ
|
kathaṃ bhavanti kathamābhavanti na bhaumamanyaṃ narakaṃ śṛṇomi||6||

7yayātiruvāca|
ūrdhvaṃ dehātkarmaṇo jṛmbhamāṇādvyaktaṃ pṛthivyāmanusaṃcaranti
|
imaṃ bhaumaṃ narakaṃ te patanti nāvekṣante varṣapūgānanekān||7||

8ṣaṣṭiṃ sahasrāṇi patanti vyomni tathā aśītiṃ parivatsarāṇi
|
tānvai tudanti patataḥ prapātaṃ bhīmā bhaumā rākṣasāstīkṣṇadaṃṣṭrāḥ||8||

9aṣṭaka uvāca|
yadenasaste patatastudanti bhīmā bhaumā rākṣasāstīkṣṇadaṃṣṭrāḥ
|
kathaṃ bhavanti kathamābhavanti kathaṃbhūtā garbhabhūtā bhavanti||9||

10yayātiruvāca|
asraṃ retaḥ puṣpaphalānupṛktamanveti tadvai puruṣeṇa sṛṣṭam
|
sa vai tasyā raja āpadyate vai sa garbhabhūtaḥ samupaiti tatra||10||

11vanaspatīnoṣadhīścāviśanti āpo vāyuṃ pṛthivīṃ cāntarikṣam
|
catuṣpadaṃ dvipadaṃ cātisarvamevaṃbhūtā garbhabhūtā bhavanti||11||

12aṣṭaka uvāca|
anyadvapurvidadhātīha garbhamutāhosvitsvena kāyena yāti
|
āpadyamāno narayonimetāmācakṣva me saṃśayātprabravīmi||12||

13śarīradehātisamucchrayaṃ ca cakṣuḥśrotre labhate kena saṃjñām
|
etattattvaṃ sarvamācakṣva pṛṣṭaḥ kṣetrajñaṃ tvāṃ tāta manyāma sarve||13||

14yayātiruvāca|
vāyuḥ samutkarṣati garbhayonimṛtau retaḥ puṣpaphalānupṛktam
|
sa tatra tanmātrakṛtādhikāraḥ krameṇa saṃvardhayatīha garbham||14||

15sa jāyamāno vigṛhītamātraḥ saṃjñāmadhiṣṭhāya tato manuṣyaḥ
|
sa śrotrābhyāṃ vedayatīha śabdaṃ sa vai rūpaṃ paśyati cakṣuṣā ca||15||

16ghrāṇena gandhaṃ jihvayā'tho rasaṃ ca tvacā sparśaṃ manasā vedabhāvam
|
ityaṣṭakehopahitaṃ hi viddhi mahātmanaḥ prāṇabhṛtaḥ śarīre||16||

17aṣṭaka uvāca|
yaḥ saṃsthitaḥ puruṣo dahyate vā nikhanyate vāpi nikṛṣyate vā
|
abhāvabhūtaḥ sa vināśametya kenātmānaṃ cetayate parastāt||17

18yayātiruvāca|
hitvā so'sūnsuptavanniṣṭanitvā purodhāya sukṛtaṃ duṣkṛtaṃ vā
|
anyāṃ yoniṃ pavanāgrānusārī hitvā dehaṃ bhajate rājasiṃha||18||

19puṇyāṃ yoniṃ puṇyakṛto vrajanti pāpāṃ yoniṃ pāpakṛto vrajanti
|
kīṭāḥ pataṅgāśca bhavanti pāpā na me vivakṣā'sti mahānubhāva||19||

20catuṣpadā dvipadāḥ ṣaṭpadāśca tathābhūtā garbhabhūtā bhavanti
|
ākhyātametannikhilena sarvaṃ bhūyastu kiṃ pṛcchasi rājasiṃha||20||

21aṣṭaka uvāca|
kiṃsvitkṛtvā labhate tāta lokānmartyaḥ śreṣṭhāṃstapasā vidyayā ca
|
tanme pṛṣṭaḥ śaṃsa sarvaṃ yathāvacchubhā~llokānyena gacchetkrameṇa||21||

22yayātiruvāca|
tapaśca dānaṃ ca śamo damaśca hrīrārjavaṃ sarvabhūtānukampā
|
svargasya lokasya vadanti santo dvārāṇi saptaiva mahānti puṃsām
|
naśyanti mānena tamo'bhibhūtāḥ puṃsaḥ sadaiveti vadanti santaḥ||22||

23adhīyānaḥ paṇḍitaṃmanyamāno yo vidyayā hanti yaśaḥ pareṣām
|
tasyāntavantaśca bhavanti lokā na cāsya tadbrahma phalaṃ dadāti||23||

24catvāri karmāṇyabhayaṅkarāṇi bhayaṃ prayacchantyayathākṛtāni
|
mānāgnihotramuta mānamaunaṃ mānenādhītamuta mānayajñaḥ||24||

25na mānamānyo mudamādadīta na santāpaṃ prāpnuyāccāvamānāt
|
santaḥ sataḥ pūjayantīha loke nāsādhavaḥ sādhubuddhiṃ labhante||25||

26iti dadyāmiti yaja ityadīya iti vratam
|
ityetāni bhayānyāhustāni varjyāni sarvaśaḥ||26||

27ye cāśrayaṃ vedayante purāṇaṃ manīṣiṇo mānasamārgaruddham
|
tadvaḥ śreyastena saṃyogametya parāṃ śāntiṃ prāpnuyuḥ pretya ceha||27||
