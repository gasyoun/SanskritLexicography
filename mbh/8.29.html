Mahābhārata 8.29 (Karṇaparvan)

1dhṛtarāṣṭra uvāca|
atitīvrāṇi duḥkhāni duḥsahāni bahūni ca
|
tvatto'haṃ sañjayāśrauṣaṃ putrāṇāṃ caiva saṃkṣayam ||1||

2yathā tvaṃ me kathayase yathā yuddhamavartata
|
na santi sūta kauravyā iti me niścitā matiḥ ||2||

3duryodhanaśca virathaḥ kṛtastatra mahārathaḥ
|
dharmaputraḥ kathaṃ cakre tasya vā nṛpatiḥ katham ||3||

4aparāhṇe kathaṃ yuddhamabhavallomaharṣaṇam
|
tanmamācakṣva tattvena kuśalo hyasi sañjaya ||4||

5sañjaya uvāca|
saṃsakteṣu tu sainyeṣu vadhyamāneṣu bhāgaśaḥ
|
rathamanyaṃ samāsthāya putrastava viśāṃpate ||5||

6krodhena mahatā yuktaḥ saviṣo bhujago yathā
|
duryodhanaḥ samālakṣya dharmarājaṃ yudhiṣṭhiram ||6||

7provāca sūtaṃ tvarito yāhi yāhīti bhārata
|
tatra māṃ prāpaya kṣipraṃ sārathe yatra pāṇḍavaḥ ||7||

8dhriyamāṇātapatreṇa rājā rājati daṃśitaḥ
|
sa sūtaścodito rājñā rājñaḥ syandanamuttamam ||8||

9yudhiṣṭhirasyābhimukhaṃ preṣayāmāsa saṃyuge
|
tato yudhiṣṭhiraḥ kruddhaḥ prabhinna iva kuñjaraḥ ||9||

10sārathiṃ codayāmāsa yāhi yatra suyodhanaḥ
|
tau samājagmaturvīrau bhrātarau rathasattamau ||10||

11sametya ca mahāvīrau saṃrabdhau yuddhadurmadau
|
vavarṣaturmaheṣvāsau śarairanyonyamāhave ||11||

12tato duryodhano rājā dharmaśīlasya māriṣa
|
śilāśitena bhallena dhanuściccheda saṃyuge ||12||

13taṃ nāmṛṣyata saṃkruddho hyavamānaṃ yudhiṣṭhiraḥ
|
apavidhya dhanuśchinnaṃ krodhasaṃraktalocanaḥ ||13||

14anyatkārmukamādāya dharmaputraścamūmukhe
|
duryodhanasya ciccheda dhvajaṃ kārmukameva ca ||14||

15athānyaddhanurādāya prāvidhyata yudhiṣṭhiram
|
tāvanyonyaṃ susaṃkruddhau śastravarṣāṇyamuñcatām ||15||

16siṃhāviva susaṃrabdhau parasparajigīṣayā
|
jaghnatustau raṇe'nyonyaṃ nardamānau vṛṣāviva ||16||

17antaraṃ mārgamāṇau ca ceratustau mahārathau
|
tataḥ pūrṇāyatotsṛṣṭaiḥ śaraistau tu kṛtavraṇau ||17||

18virejaturmahārāja kiṃśukāviva puṣpitau
|
tato rājanvimuñcantau siṃhanādānmuhurmuhuḥ ||18||

19talayośca tathā śabdān dhanuṣaśca mahāhave
|
śaṅkhaśabdavarāṃścaiva cakratustau nareśvarau ||19||

20anyonyaṃ tau mahārāja pīḍayāñcakraturbhṛśam
|
tato yudhiṣṭhiro rājā putraṃ tava śaraistribhiḥ ||20||

21ājaghānorasi kruddho vajravegairdurāsadaiḥ
|
prativivyādha taṃ tūrṇaṃ tava putro mahīpatiḥ ||21||

22pañcabhirniśitairbāṇaiḥ svarṇapuṅkhaiḥ śilāśitaiḥ
|
tato duryodhano rājā śaktiṃ cikṣepa bhārata ||22||

23sarvapārasavīṃ tīkṣṇāṃ maholkāpratimāṃ tadā
|
tāmāpatantīṃ sahasā dharmarājaḥ śitaiḥ śaraiḥ ||23||

24tribhiściccheda sahasā taṃ ca vivyādha pañcabhiḥ
|
nipapāta tataḥ sā'tha svarṇadaṇḍā mahāsvanā ||24||

25nipatantī maholkeva vyarājacchikhisannibhā
|
śaktiṃ vinihatāṃ dṛṣṭvā putrastava viśāṃpate ||25||

26navabhirniśitairbhallairnijaghāna yudhiṣṭhiram
|
so'tividdho balavatā śatruṇā śatrutāpanaḥ ||26||

27duryodhanaṃ samuddiśya bāṇaṃ jagrāha satvaraḥ
|
samādhatta ca taṃ bāṇaṃ dhanurmadhye mahābalaḥ ||27||

28cikṣepa ca mahārāja tataḥ kruddhaḥ parākramī
|
sa tu bāṇaḥ samāsādya tava putraṃ mahāratham ||28||

29vyāmohayata rājānaṃ dharaṇīṃ ca dadāra ha
|
tato duryodhanaḥ kruddho gadāmudyamya vegitaḥ ||29||

30vidhitsuḥ kalahasyāntaṃ dharmarājamupādravat
|
tamudyatagadaṃ dṛṣṭvā daṇḍahastamivāntakam ||30||

31dharmarājo mahāśaktiṃ prāhiṇottava sūnave
|
dīpyamānāṃ mahāvegāṃ maholkāṃ jvalitāmiva ||31||

32rathasthaḥ sa tayā viddho varma bhittvā stanāntare
|
bhṛśaṃ saṃvignahṛdayaḥ papāta ca mumoha ca ||32||

33bhīmastamāha ca tataḥ pratijñāmanucintayan
|
nāyaṃ vadhyastava nṛpa ityuktaḥ sa nyavartata ||33||

34tatastvaritamāgamya kṛtavarmā tavātmajam
|
pratyapadyata rājānaṃ nimagnaṃ vyasanārṇave ||34||

35gadāmādāya bhīmo'pi hemapaṭṭapariṣkṛtām
|
abhidudrāva vegena kṛtavarmāṇamāhave ||35||

36evaṃ tadadbhavadyuddhaṃ tvadīyānāṃ paraiḥ saha
|
aparāhṇe mahārāja kāṅkṣatāṃ vijayaṃ yudhi ||36||
