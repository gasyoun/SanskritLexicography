Mahābhārata 2.10 (Sabhāparvan)

1nārada uvāca|
sabhā vaiśravaṇī rājañcchatayojanamāyatā
|
vistīrṇā saptatiścaiva yojanāni sitaprabhā||1||

2tapasā nirjitā rājansvayaṃ vaiśravaṇena sā
|
śaśiprabhā prāvaraṇā kailāsaśikharopamā||2||

3guhyakairuhyamānā sā khe viṣakteva śobhate
|
divyā hemamayairuccaiḥ prāsādairupaśobhitā||3||

4mahāratnavatī citrā divyagandhā manoramā
|
sitābhraśikharākārā plavamāneva dṛśyate||4||

5divyā hemamayairaṅgairvidyudbhiriva citritā
|
tasyāṃ vaiśravaṇo rājā vicitrābharaṇāmbaraḥ||5||

6strīsahasrairvṛtaḥ śrīmānāste jvalitakuṇḍalaḥ
|
divākaranibhe puṇye divyāstaraṇasaṃvṛte
|
divyapādopadhāne ca niṣaṇṇaḥ paramāsane||6||

7mandārāṇāmudārāṇāṃ vanāni pariloḍayan
|
saugandhikavanānāṃ ca gandhaṃ gandhavaho vahan||7||

8nalinyāścālakākhyāyā nandanasya vanasya ca
|
śīto hṛdayasaṃhlādī vāyustamupasevate||8||

9tatra devāḥ sagandharvā gaṇairapsarasāṃ vṛtāḥ
|
divyatānairmahārāja gāyanti sma sabhāgatāḥ||9||

10miśrakeśī ca rambhā ca citrasenā śucismitā
|
cārunetrā ghṛtācī ca menakā puñjikasthalā||10||

11viśvācī sahajanyā ca pramlocā urvaśī irā
|
vargā ca saurabheyī ca samīcī budbudā latā||11||

12etāḥ sahasraśaścānyā nṛtyagītaviśāradāḥ||12||

13upatiṣṭhanti dhanadaṃ gandharvāpsarasāṃ gaṇāḥ
|
aniśaṃ divyavāditrairnṛtyagītaiśca sa sabhā||13||

14aśūnyā rucirā bhāti gandharvāpsarasāṃ gaṇaiḥ
|
kinnarā nāma gandharvā narā nāma tathā'pare||14||

15māṇibhadro'tha dhanadaḥ śvetabhadraśca guhyakaḥ
|
kaśerako gaṇḍakaṇḍūḥ pradyotaśca mahābalaḥ||15||

16kustambaruḥ piśācaśca gajakarṇo viśālakaḥ
|
varāhakarṇastāmrauṣṭhaḥ phalakakṣaḥ phalodakaḥ||16||

17haṃsacūḍaḥ śaikhāvarto hemanetro vibhīṣaṇaḥ
|
puṣpānanaḥ piṅgalakaḥ śoṇitodaḥ pravālakaḥ||17||

18vṛkṣavāsyaniketaśca cīravāsāśca bhārata
|
ete cānye ca bahavo yakṣāḥ śatasahasraśaḥ||18||

19sadā bhagavatī lakṣmīstatraiva nalakūbaraḥ
|
ahaṃ ca bahuśastasyāṃ bhavantyanye ca madvidhāḥ||19||

20brahmarṣayo bhavantyatra tathā devarṣayo'pare
|
kravyādāśca tathaivānye gandharvāśca mahābalāḥ||20||

21upāsate mahātmānaṃ tasyāṃ dhanadamīśvaram
|
bhagavānbhūtasaṅghaiśca vṛtaḥ śatasahasraśaḥ||21||

22umāpatiḥ paśupatiḥ śūlabhṛdbhaganetrahā
|
tryambako rājaśārdūla devī ca vigataklamā||22||

23vāmanairvikaṭaiḥ kubjaiḥ kṣatajākṣairmahāravaiḥ
|
medomāṃsāśanairugrairugradhanvā mahābalaḥ||23||

24nānāpraharaṇairugrairvātairiva mahājavaiḥ
|
vṛtaḥ sakhāyamanvāste sadaiva dhanadaṃ nṛpa||24||

25prahṛṣṭāḥ śataśaścānye bahuśaḥ saparicchadāḥ
|
gandharvāṇāṃ ca patayo viśvāvasurhahāhuhūḥ||25||

26tumburuḥ prarvataścaiva śailūṣaśca tathā'paraḥ
|
citrasenaśca gītajñastathā citrarathopi ca||26||

27ete cānye ca gandharvā dhaneśvaramupāsate
|
vidyādharādhipaścaiva cakradharmā sahānujaiḥ||27||

28upācarati tatra sma dhanānāmīśvaraṃ prabhum
|
kinnarāḥ śataśastatra dhanānāmīśvaraṃ prabhum||28||

29āsate cāpi rājāno bhagadattapurogamāḥ
|
drumaḥ kiṃpuruṣeśaśca upāste dhanadeśvaram||29||

30rākṣasādhipatiścaiva mahendro gandhamādanaḥ
|
saha yakṣaiḥ sagandharvaiḥ saha sarvairniśācaraiḥ||30||

31vibhīṣaṇaśca dharmiṣṭha upāste bhrātaraṃ prabhum
|
himavānpāriyātraśca vindhyakailāsamandarāḥ||31||

32malayo darduraścaiva mahendro gandhamādanaḥ
|
indrakīlaḥ sunābhaśca tathā divyau ca parvatau||32||

33ete cānye ca bahavaḥ sarve merupurogamāḥ
|
upāsate mahātmānaṃ dhanānāmīśvaraṃ prabhum||33||

34nandīśvaraśca bhagavānmahākālastathaiva ca
|
śaṅkukarṇamukhāḥ sarve divyāḥ pāriṣadāstathā||34||

35kāṣṭhaḥ kuṭī mukho dantī vijayaśca tapodhikaḥ
|
śvetaśca vṛṣabhastatra nardannāste mahābalaḥ||35||

36dhanadaṃ rākṣasāścānye piśācāśca upāsate
|
pāriṣadaiḥ parivṛtamupāyāntaṃ maheśvaram||36||

37sadā hi devadeveśaṃ śivaṃ trailokyabhāvanam
|
praṇamya mūrdhnā paulastyo bahurūpamumāpatim||37||

38tato'bhyanujñāṃ samprāpya mahādevāddhaneśvaraḥ
|
āste kadācidbhagavānbhavo dhanapateḥ sakhā||38||

39nidhipravaramukhyau ca śaṅkhapadmau dhaneśvarau
|
sarvānnidhīnpragṛhyātha upāstāṃ vai dhaneśvaram||39||

40sā sabhā tādṛśī ramyā mayā dṛṣṭāntarikṣagā
|
pitāmahasabhāṃ rājankīrtayiṣye nibodha tām||40||
