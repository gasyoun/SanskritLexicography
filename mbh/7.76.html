Mahābhārata 7.76 (Droṇaparvan)

1arjuna uvāca| ṣaḍrathān dhārtarāṣṭrasya manyase yān balādhikān
|
teṣāṃ vāya mamārdhena na tulyamiti me matiḥ ||1||

2astramastreṇa sarveṣāmeteṣāṃ madhusūdana
|
mayā drakṣyasi nirbhinnaṃ jayadrathabadhaiṣiṇā ||2||

3droṇasya miṣataścāhaṃ sagaṇasya vilapyataḥ
|
mūrdhānaṃ sindhurājasya pātayiṣyāmi bhūtale ||3||

4yadi sādhyāśca rudrāśca vasavaśca sahāśvinaḥ
|
marutaśca sahendreṇa viśvedevāḥ saheśvarāḥ ||4||

5pitaraḥ sahagandharvāḥ suparṇāḥ sāgarādayaḥ
|
dyaurviyat pṛthivī ceyaṃ diśaśca sadigīśvarāḥ ||5||

6grāmāraṇyāni bhūtāni sthāvarāṇi carāṇi ca
|
trātāraḥ sindhurājasya bhavanti madhusūdana ||6||

7tathāpi bāṇairnihataṃ śvo draṣṭā'si raṇe mayā
|
satyena ca śape kṛṣṇa tathaivāyudhamālabhe ||7||

8yasya goptā maheṣvāsastasya pāpasya durmateḥ
|
tameva prathamaṃ droṇamabhiyāsyāmi keśava ||8||

9tasmin dyūtamidaṃ baddhaṃ manyate sa suyodhanaḥ
|
tasmāttasyaiva senāgraṃ bhitvā yāsyāmi saindhavam ||9||

10draṣṭā'si śvo maheṣvāsānnārācaistigmatejitaiḥ
|
śṛṅgāṇīva girervajrairdāryamāṇānmayā yudhi ||10||

11naranāgāśvadehebhyo visraviṣyati śoṇitam
|
patadbhyaḥ patitebhyaśca vibhinnebhyaḥ śitaiḥ śaraiḥ ||11||

12gāṇḍīvapreṣitā bāṇā mano'nilasamā jave
|
nṛnāgāśvān videhāsūna kartāraśca sahasraśaḥ ||12||

13yamāt kuberādvaruṇādindrādrudrācca yanmayā
|
upāttamastraṃ ghoraṃ taddraṣṭāro'tra narā yudhi ||13||

14brāhmaṇāstreṇa cāstrāṇi hanyamānāni saṃyuge
|
mayā draṣṭā'si sarveṣāṃ saindhavasyābhirakṣiṇām ||14||

15śaravegasamutkṛttai rājñāṃ keśava mūrdhabhiḥ
|
āstīryamāṇāṃ pṛthivīṃ draṣṭā'si śvo mayā yudhi ||15||

16kravyādāṃstarpayiṣyāmi drāvayiṣyāmi śātravān
|
suhṛdo nandayiṣyāmi pramathiṣyāmi saindhavam ||16||

17bahvāgaskṛt kusambandhī pāpadeśasamudbhavaḥ
|
mayā saindhavako rājā hataḥ svān śocayiṣyati ||17||

18sarvakṣīrānnabhoktāraṃ pāpācāraṃ raṇājire
|
mayā sarājakaṃ bāṇairbhinnaṃ drakṣyasi saindhavam ||18||

19tathā prabhāte kartā'smi yathā kṛṣṇa suyodhanaḥ
|
nānyaṃ dhanurdharaṃ loke maṃsyate matsamaṃ yudhi ||19||

20gāṇḍīvaṃ ca dhanurdivyaṃ yoddhā cāhaṃ nararṣabha
|
tvaṃ ca yantā hṛṣīkeśa kiṃ nu syādajitaṃ mayā ||20||

21tava prasādādbhagavan kiṃ nāvāptaṃ raṇe mama
|
aviṣahyaṃ hṛṣīkeśa kiṃ jānanmāṃ vigarhase ||21||

22yathā lakṣma sthiraṃ candre samudre ca yathā jalam
|
evametāṃ pratijñāṃ me satyāṃ viddhi janārdana ||22||

23māvamaṃsthā mamāstrāṇi māvamaṃsthā dhanurdṛḍham
|
māvamaṃsthā balaṃ bāhvormāvamaṃsthā dhanañjayam ||23||

24tathābhiyāmi saṃgrāmaṃ na jīyeyaṃ jayāmi ca
|
tena satyena saṃgrāme hataṃ viddhi jayadratham ||24||

25dhruvaṃ vai brāhmaṇe satyaṃ dhruvā sādhuṣu sannatiḥ
|
śrīrdhruvā'pi ca yajñeṣu dhruvo nārāyaṇe jayaḥ ||25||

26sañjaya uvāca|
evamuktvā hṛṣīkeśaṃ svayamātmānamātmanā
|
sandideśārjuno nardan vāsaviḥ keśavaṃ prabhum ||26||

27yathā prabhātāṃ rajanīṃ kalpitaḥ syādratho mama
|
tathā kāryaṃ tvayā kṛṣṇa kāryaṃ hi mahadudyatam ||27||
