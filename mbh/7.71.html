Mahābhārata 7.71 (Droṇaparvan)

1vyāsa uvāca|
puṇyamākhyānamāyuṣyaṃ śrutvā ṣoḍaśarājakaram
|
avyāharannarapatistūṣṇīmāsīt sa sṛñjayaḥ || 1 ||

2tamabravīt tathā'sīnaṃ nārado bhagavānṛṣiḥ
|
śrutaṃ kīrtayato mahyaṃ gṛhītaṃ te mahādyute ||2||

3āhosvidantato naṣṭaṃ śraddhāṃ śūdrīpatāviva
|
sa evamuktaḥ pratyāha prāñjaliḥ sṛñjayastadā ||3||

4etacchrutvā mahābāho dhanyamākhyānamuttamam
|
rājarṣīṇāṃ purāṇānāṃ yajvanāṃ dakṣiṇāvatām ||4||

5vismayena hate śoke tamasīvārkatejasā
|
vipāpmāsyavyathopeto brūhi kiṃ karavāṇyaham ||5||

6nārada uvāca| diṣṭyā'pahṛtaśokastvaṃ vṛṇīṣveha yadicchasi
|
tat tat prapatsyase sarvaṃ na mṛṣāvādino vayam ||6||

7sṛñjaya uvāca| etenaiva pratīto'haṃ prasanno yadbhavān mama
|
prasanno yasya bhagavānna tasyāstīha durlabham ||7||

8nārada uvāca| mṛtaṃ dadāni te putraṃ dasyubhirnihataṃ vṛthā
|
uddhṛtya narakāt kaṣṭāt paśuvatprokṣitaṃ yathā ||8||

9vyāsa uvāca| prādurāsīt tataḥ putraḥ sṛñjayasyādbhutaprabhaḥ
|
prasannenarṣiṇā dattaḥ kuberatanayopamaḥ ||9||

10tataḥ saṃgamya putreṇa prītimānabhavannṛpaḥ
|
īje ca kratubhiḥ puṇyaiḥ samāptavaradakṣiṇaiḥ ||10||

11akṛtārthaśca bhītaśca na ca sānnāhiko hataḥ
|
ayajvā tvanapatyaśca tato'sau jīvitaḥ punaḥ ||11||

12śūro vīraḥ kṛtārthaśca pratāpyārīnsahasraśaḥ
|
abhimanyurgato vīraḥ pṛtanābhimukho hataḥ ||12||

13brahmacaryeṇa yān kāṃścit prajñayā ca śrutena ca
|
iṣṭaiśca kratubhiryānti tāṃste putro'kṣayān gataḥ ||13||

14vidvāṃsaḥ karmabhiḥ puṇyaiḥ svargamīhanti nityaśaḥ
|
na tu svargādayaṃ lokaḥ kāmyate svargavāsibhiḥ ||14||

15tasmāt svargagataṃ putramarjunasya hataṃ raṇe
|
na cehānayituṃ śakyaṃ kiñcidaprāpyamīhitam ||15||

16yāṃ yogino dhyānaviviktadarśanāḥ prayānti yāṃ cottamayajvino janāḥ
|
tapobhiriddhairanuyānti yāṃ tathā tāmakṣayāṃ te tanayo gato gatim ||16||

17antāt punarbhāgavato virājate rājeva vīro hyamṛtātmaraśmibhiḥ
|
tāmaindavīmātmatanuṃ dvijocitāṃ gato'bhimanyurna sa śokamarhati ||17||

18evaṃ jñātvā sthiro bhūtvā jahyarīn dhairyamāpnuhi
|
jīvanta eva naḥ śocyā na tu svargagato'nagha ||18||

19śocato hi mahārāja aghamevābhivardhate
|
tasmācchokaṃ parityajya śreyase prayatedbudhaḥ ||19||

20praharṣamabhimānaṃ ca sukhaprāptiṃ ca cintayan
|
etadbuddhvā budhāḥ śokaṃ na śokaḥ śoka ucyate ||20||

21evaṃ vidvan samuttiṣṭha prayato bhava mā śucaḥ
|
śrutaste saṃbhavo mṛtyostapāṃsyanupamāni ca ||21||

22sarvabhūtasamatvaṃ ca cañcalāśca vibhūtayaḥ
|
sṛñjayasya tu taṃ putraṃ mṛtaṃ saṃjīvitaṃ punaḥ ||22||

23evaṃ vidvanmahārāja mā śucaḥ sādhayāmyaham
|
etāvaduktvā bhagavāṃstatraivāntaradhīyata ||23||

24vāgīśāne bhagavati vyāse vyabhranabhaḥprabhe
|
gate matimatāṃ śreṣṭhe samāśvāsya yudhiṣṭhiram ||24||

25pūrveṣāṃ pārthivendrāṇāṃ mahendrapratimaujasām
|
nyāyādhigatavittānāṃ tāṃ śrutvā yajñasaṃpadam ||25||

26saṃpūjya manasā vidvānviśoko'bhūdyudhiṣṭhiraḥ
|
punaścācintayaddīnaḥ kiṃsvidvakṣye dhanañjayam ||26||
