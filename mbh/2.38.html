Mahābhārata 2.38 (Sabhāparvan)

1vaiśampāyana uvāca|
tato yudhiṣṭhiro rājā śiśupālamupādravat
|
uvāca cainaṃ madhuraṃ sāntvapūrvamidaṃ vacaḥ||1||

2nedaṃ yuktaṃ mahīpāla yādṛśaṃ vai tvamuktavān
|
adharmaśca paro rājanpāruṣyaṃ ca nirarthakam||2||

3na hi dharmaṃ paraṃ jātu nāvabudhyeta pārthivaḥ
|
bhīṣmaḥ śāntanavastvenaṃ māvamaṃsthāstvamanyathā||3||

4paśya caitānmahīpālāṃstvatto vṛddhatarānbahūn
|
mṛṣyante cārhaṇāṃ kṛṣṇe tadvatvaṃ kṣantumarhasi||4||

5veda tattvena kṛṣṇaṃ hi bhīṣṇaścedipate bhṛśam
|
na hyenaṃ tvaṃ tathā vettha yathainaṃ veda kauravaḥ||5||

6bhīṣma uvāca|
nāsmai deyo hyanunayo nāyamarhati sāntvanam
|
lokavṛddhatame kṛṣṇe yo'rhaṇāṃ nābhimanyate||6||

7kṣatriyaḥ kṣatriyaṃ jitvā raṇe raṇakṛtāṃ varaḥ
|
yo muñcati vaśe kṛtvā gururbhavati tasya saḥ||7||

8asyāṃ hi samitau rājñāmekamapyajitaṃ yudhi
|
na paśyāmi mahīpālaṃ sātvatīputratejasā||8||

9na hi kevalamasmākamayamarcyatamo'cyutaḥ
|
trayāṇāmapi lokānāmarcanīyo mahābhujaḥ||9||

10kṛṣṇena hi jitā yuddhe bahavaḥ kṣatriyarṣabhāḥ
|
jagatsarvaṃ ca vārṣṇeye nikhilena pratiṣṭhitam||10||

11tasmātsatsvapi vṛddheṣu kṛṣṇamarcāma netarān
|
evaṃ vaktuṃ na cārhastvaṃ mā te'bhūdbuddhirīdṛśī||11||

12jñānavṛddhā mayā rājanbahavaḥ paryupāsitāḥ
|
teṣāṃ kathayatāṃ śaurerahaṃ guṇavato guṇān||12||

13samāgatānāmaśrauṣaṃ bahūnbahumatānsatām
|
karmāṇyapi ca yānyasya janmaprabhṛti dhīmataḥ||13||

14bahuśaḥ kathyamānāni narairbhūyaḥ śrutāni me
|
na kevalaṃ vayaṃ kāmāccedirāja janārdanam||14||

15na sambandhaṃ puraskṛtya kṛtārthaṃ vā kathañcana
|
arcāmahe'rcitaṃ sadbhirbhuvi bhūtasukhāvaham||15||

16yaśaḥ śauryaṃ jayaṃ cāsya vijñāyārcāṃ prayuñjmahe
|
na ca kaścidihāsmābhiḥ suvālopyaparīkṣitaḥ||16||

17guṇairvṛddhānatikramya harirarcyatamo mataḥ
|
jñānavṛddho dvijātīnāṃ kṣatriyāṇāṃ balādhikaḥ||17||

18vaiśyānāṃ dhānyadhanavānśūdrāṇāmeva janmataḥ
|
pūjyatāyāṃ ca govinde hetū dvāvapi saṃsthitau||18||

19vedavedāṅgavijñānaṃ balaṃ cābhyadhikaṃ tathā
|
nṛṇāṃ loke hi ko'nyosti viśiṣṭaḥ keśavādṛte||19||

20dānaṃ dākṣyaṃ śrutaṃ śauryaṃ hrīḥ kīrtirbuddhiruttamā
|
saṃtatiḥ śrīrdhṛtistuṣṭiḥ puṣṭiśca niyatā'cyute||20||

21tamimaṃ lokasampannamācāryaṃ pitaraṃ gurum
|
arghyamarcitamarcārhaṃ sarve saṅkṣantumarhatha||21||

22ṛtviggururvivāhyaśca snātako nṛpatiḥ priyaḥ
|
sarvametaddhṛṣīkeśastasmādabhyarcito'cyutaḥ||22||

23kṛṣṇa eva hi lokānāmutpattirapi cāvyayaḥ
|
kṛṣṇasya hi kṛte viśvamidaṃ bhūtaṃ carācaram||23||

24eṣa prakṛtiravyaktā kartā caiva sanātanaḥ
|
paraśca sarvabhūtebhyastasmātpūjyatamo'cyutaḥ||24||

25buddhirmano mahadvāyustejombhaḥ khaṃ mahī ca yā
|
caturvidhaṃ ca yadbhūtaṃ sarvaṃ kṛṣṇe pratiṣṭhitam||25||

26ādityaścandramāścaiva nakṣatrāṇi grahāśca ye
|
diśaśca vidiśaścaiva sarvaṃ kṛṣṇe pratiṣṭhitam||26||

27agnihotramukhā vedā gāyatrī cchandasāṃ mukham
|
rājā mukhaṃ manuṣyāṇāṃ nadīnāṃ sāgaro mukham||27||

28nakṣatrāṇāṃ mukhaṃ candra ādityastejasāṃ mukham
|
parvatānāṃ mukhaṃ merurgaruḍaḥ patatāṃ mukham||28||

29ūrdhvaṃ tiryagadhaścaiva yāvatī jagato gatiḥ
|
sadevakeṣu lokeṣu bhagavānkeśavo mukham||29||

30ayaṃ tu puruṣo bālaḥ śiśupālo na budhyate
|
sarvatra sarvadā kṛṣṇaṃ tasmādevaṃ prabhāṣate||30||

31yo hi dharmaṃ vicinuyādutkṛṣṭaṃ matimānnaraḥ
|
sa vai paśyedyathā dharmaṃ na tathā cedirāḍayam||31||

32savṛddhabāleṣvathavā pārthiveṣu mahātmasu
|
ko nārhaṃ manyate kṛṣṇaṃ ko vāpyenaṃ na pūjayet||32||

33athaināṃ duṣkṛtāṃ pūjāṃ śiśupālo vyavasyati
|
duṣkṛtāyāṃ yathānyāyaṃ tathā'yaṃ kartumarhati||33||
