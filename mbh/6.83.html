Mahābhārata 6.83 (Bhīṣmaparvan)

1dhṛtarāṣṭra uvāca|
bahūni hi vicitrāṇi dvairathāni sma sañjaya
|
pāṇḍūnāṃ māmakaiḥ sārdhamaśrauṣaṃ tava jalpataḥ ||1||

2na caiva māmakaṃ kiñcit hṛṣṭaṃ śaṃsasi sañjaya
|
nityaṃ pāṇḍusutān hṛṣṭānabhagnān saṃpraśaṃsasi ||2||

3jīyamānān vimanaso māmakān vigataujasaḥ
|
vadase saṃyuge sūta diṣṭametanna saṃśayaḥ ||3||

4sañjaya uvāca|yathāśakti yathotsāhaṃ yuddhe ceṣṭanti tāvakāḥ
|
darśayānāḥ paraṃ śaktyā pauruṣaṃ puruṣarṣabha ||4||

5gaṅgāyāḥ suranadyā vai svādu bhūtvā yathodakam
|
mahodadherguṇābhyāsāllavaṇatvaṃ nigacchati ||5||

6tathā tat pauruṣaṃ rājaṃstāvakānāṃ parantapa
|
prāpya pāṇḍusutān vīrān vyarthaṃ bhavati saṃyuge ||6||

7ghaṭamānān yathāśakti kurvāṇān karma duṣkaram
|
na doṣeṇa kuruśreṣṭha kauravān gantumarhasi ||7||

8tavāparādhāt sumahān saputrasya viśāṃpate
|
pṛthivyāḥ prakṣayo ghoro yamarāṣṭravivardhanaḥ ||8||

9ātmadoṣāt samutpannaṃ śocituṃ nārhase nṛpa
|
na hi rakṣanti rājānaḥ sarvathā'trāpi jīvitam ||9||

10yuddhe sukṛtināṃ lokānicchanto vasudhādhipāḥ
|
camūṃ vigāhya yudhyante nityaṃ svargaparāyaṇāḥ ||10||

11pūrvāhṇe tu mahārāja prāvartata janakṣayaḥ
|
taṃ tvamekamanā bhūtvā śṛṇu devāsuropamam ||11||

12āvantyau tu maheṣvāsau mahāsenau mahābalau
|
irāvantamabhiprekṣya sameyātāṃ raṇotkaṭau ||12||

13teṣāṃ pravavṛte yuddhaṃ sumahallomaharṣaṇam
|
irāvāṃstu susaṅkruddho bhrātarau devarūpiṇau ||13||

14vivyādha niśitaistūrṇaṃ śaraiḥ sannataparvabhiḥ
|
tāvenaṃ pratyavidhyetāṃ samare citrayodhinau ||14||

15yudhyatāṃ hi tathā rājan viśeṣo na vyadṛśyata
|
yatatāṃ śatrunāśāya kṛtapratikṛtaiṣiṇām ||15||

16irāvāṃstu tato rājannanuvindasya sāyakaiḥ
|
caturbhiścaturo vāhānanayadyamasādanam ||16||

17bhallābhyāṃ ca sutīkṣṇābhyāṃ dhanuḥ ketuṃ ca māriṣa
|
ciccheda samare rājaṃstad‌dbhutamivābhavat ||17||

18tyaktvā'nuvindo'tha rathaṃ vindasya rathamāsthitaḥ
|
dhanurgṛhītvā paramaṃ bhārasādhanamuttamam ||18||

19tāvekasthau raṇe vīgavāvantyau rathināṃ varau
|
śarān mumucatustūrṇamirāvati mahātmani ||19||

20tābhyāṃ muktā mahāvegāḥ śarāḥ kāñcanabhūṣaṇāḥ
|
divākarapathaṃ prāpya cchā‌dayāmāsurambaram ||20||

21irāvāṃstu raṇe kuddho bhrātarau tau mahārathau
|
vavarṣa śaravarṣeṇa sārathiṃ cāpyapātayat ||21||

22tasmiṃstu patite bhūmau gatasattve tu sārathau
|
rathaḥ pradudrāva diśaḥ samudbhrāntahayastataḥ ||22||

23tau sa jitvā mahārāja nāgarājasutāsutaḥ
|
pauruṣaṃ khyāpayaṃstūrṇaṃ vyadhamattava vāhinīm ||23||

24sā vadhyamānā samare dhārtarāṣṭrī mahācamūḥ
|
vegān bahuvidhāṃścakre viṣaṃ pītveva mānavaḥ ||24||

25haiḍimbo rākṣasendrastu bhagadattaṃ samādravat
|
rathenādityavarṇena sadhvajena mahābalaḥ ||25||

26tataḥ prāgjyotiṣo rājā nāgarājaṃ samāsthitaḥ
|
yathā vajradharaḥ pūrvaṃ saṅgrāme tārakāmaye ||26||

27tatra devāḥ sagandharvā ṛṣayaśca samāgatāḥ
|
viśeṣaṃ na sma vividurhaiḍimbabhagadattayoḥ ||27||

28yathā surapatiḥ śakrastrāsayāmāsa dānavān
|
tathaiva samare rājā drāvayāmāsa pāṇḍavān ||28||

29tena vidrāvyamāṇāste pāṇḍavāḥ sarvatodiśam
|
trātāraṃ nābhyagacchantaḥ sveṣvanīkeṣu bhārata ||29||

30bhaimaseniṃ rathasthaṃ tu tatrāpaśyāma bhārata
|
śeṣā vimanaso bhūtvā prādravanta mahārathāḥ ||30||

31nivṛtteṣu tu pāṇḍūnāṃ punaḥ sainyeṣu bhārata
|
āsīnniṣṭhānakā ghorastava sainyasya saṃyuge ||31||

32ghaṭotkacastato rājan bhagadattaṃ mahāraṇe
|
śaraiḥ pracchā‌dayāmāsa meruṃ girimivāmbudaḥ ||32||

33nihatya tān śarān rājā rākṣasasya dhanuścyutān
|
bhaimaseniṃ raṇe tūrṇaṃ sarvamarmasvatāḍayat ||33||

34sa tāḍyamāno bahubhiḥ śaraiḥ sannataparvabhiḥ
|
na vivyathe rākṣasendro bhidyamāna ivācalaḥ ||34||

35tasya prāgjyotiṣaḥ kruddhastomarāṃśca caturdaśa
|
preṣayāmāsa samare tāṃściccheda sa rākṣasaḥ ||35||

36sa tāṃśchitvā mahābāhustomarānniśitaiḥ śaraiḥ
|
bhagadattaṃ ca vivyādha saptatyā kaṅkapatribhiḥ ||36||

37tataḥ prāgjyotiṣo rājā prahasanniva bhārata
|
tasyāśvāṃścaturaḥ saṅkhye pātayāmāsa sāyakaiḥ ||37||

38sa hatāśve rathe tiṣṭhan rākṣasendraḥ pratāpavān
|
śaktiṃ cikṣepa vegena prāgjyotiṣagajaṃ prati ||38||

39tāmāpatantīṃ sahasā hemadaṇḍāṃ suveginīm
|
tridhā ciccheda nṛpatiḥ sā vyakīryata medinīm ||39||

40śaktiṃ vinihatāṃ dṛṣṭvā haiḍimbaḥ prādravadbhayāt
|
yathendrasya raṇātpūrvaṃ namucirdaityasattamaḥ ||40||

41taṃ vijitya raṇe śūraṃ vikrāntaṃ khyātapauruṣam
|
ajeyaṃ samare vīraṃ yamena varuṇena ca ||41||

42pāṇḍavīṃ samare senāṃ saṃmamarda sa kuñjaraḥ
|
yathā vanagajo rājan mṛdnaṃścarati padminīm ||42||

43madreśvarastu samare yamābhyāṃ samasajjata
|
svasrīyau chādayāñcakre śaraughaiḥ pāṇḍunandanau ||43||

44sahadevastu samare mātulaṃ dṛśya saṃgatam
|
avārayaccharaugheṇa megho yadvaddivākaram ||44||

45chādyamānaḥ śaraugheṇa hṛṣṭarūpataro'bhavat
|
tayoścāpyabhavatprītiratulā mātṛkāraṇāt ||45||

46tataḥ prahasya samare nakulasya mahārathaḥ
|
aśvāṃśca caturo rājaṃścaturbhiḥ sāyakottamaiḥ ||46||

47preṣayāmāsa samare yamasya sadanaṃ prati
|
hatāśvāttu rathāttūrṇamavaplutya mahārathaḥ ||47||

48āruroha tato yānaṃ bhrātureva yaśasvinaḥ
|
ekasthau tu raṇe śūrau dṛḍhe vikṣipya kārmu ||48||

49madrarājarathaṃ tūrṇaṃ chādayāmāsatuḥ kṣaṇāt
|
sa cchādyamāno bahubhiḥ śaraiḥ sannataparvabhiḥ ||49||

50svastrīyābhyāṃ naravyāghro nākampata yathā'calaḥ
|
prahasanniva tāṃ cāpi śastravṛṣṭiṃ jaghāna ha ||50||

51sahadevastataḥ kruddhaḥ śaramudgṛhya vīryavān
|
madrarājamabhiprekṣva preṣayāmāsa bhārata ||51||

52sa śaraḥ preṣitastena garuḍānilavegavān
|
madrarājaṃ vinirbhidya nipapāta mahītale ||52||

53sa gāḍhaviddho vyathito rathopasthe mahārathaḥ
|
niṣasāda mahārāja kaśmalaṃ ca jagāma ha ||53||

54taṃ visaṃjñaṃ nipatitaṃ sūtaḥ saṃprekṣya saṃyuge
|
apovāha rathenājau yamābhyāmabhipīḍitam ||54||

55dṛṣṭvā madreśvararathaṃ dhārtarāṣṭrāḥ parāṅmukham
|
sarve vimanaso bhūtvā nedamastītyacintayan ||55||

56nirjitya mātulaṃ saṃkhye mādrīputrau mahārathau
|
dadhmaturmuditau śaṃkhau siṃhanādaṃ ca nedatuḥ ||56||

57abhidudruvaturhṛṣṭau tava sainyaṃ viśāṃpate
|
yathā daityacamūṃ rājannindropendrāvivāmarau ||57||
