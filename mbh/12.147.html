Mahābhārata 12.147 (Śāntiparvan)

1bhīṣma uvāca|
tataḥ sa lubdhakaḥ paśyan kṣudhayāpi pariplutaḥ
|
kapotamagnipatitaṃ vākyaṃ punaruvāca ha ||1||

2kimīdṛśaṃ nṛśaṃsena mayā kṛtamabuddhinā
|
bhaviṣyati hi me nityaṃ pātakaṃ kṛtajīvinaḥ
|
sa vinindaṃstathā''tmānaṃ punaḥ punaruvāca ha ||2||

3aviśvāsyaḥ sudurbuddhiḥ sadā nikṛtiniścayaḥ
|
śubhaṃ karma parityajya so'haṃ śakunilubdhakaḥ ||3||

4nṛśaṃsasya mamādyāyaṃ pratyādeśo na saṃśayaḥ
|
dattaḥ svamāṃsaṃ dahatā kapotena mahātmanā ||4||

5so'haṃ tyakṣye priyānprāṇānputrāndārāṃstathaiva ca
|
upadiṣṭe hi me dharmaḥ kapotena mahātmanā ||5||

6adyaprabhṛti dehaṃ svaṃ sarvabhogairvivarjitam
|
yathā svalpaṃ saro grīṣme śoṣayiṣyāmyahaṃ tathā ||6||

7kṣutpipāsātapasahaḥ kṛśo dhamanisantataḥ
|
upavāsairbahuvidhaiścariṣye pāralaukikam ||7||

8aho dehapradānena darśitātithipūjanā
|
tasmāddharmaṃ cariṣyāmi dharmo hi paramā gatiḥ||8||

9dṛṣṭo dharmo hi dharmiṣṭhe yādṛśo vihagottame
|
evamuktvā viniścitya raudrakarmā sa lubdhakaḥ||10||

10mahāprasthānamāśritya prayayau saṃśitavrataḥ ||10||

11tato yaṣṭiṃ śalākāṃ ca kṣārakaṃ pañjaraṃ tathā
|
tāṃ ca baddhāṃ kapotīṃ sa pramucya visasarja ha ||11||
