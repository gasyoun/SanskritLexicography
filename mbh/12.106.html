Mahābhārata 12.106 (Śāntiparvan)

1rājovāca
|
na nikṛtyā na dambhena brahmannicchāmi jīvitum
|
nādharmayuktāniccheyamarthānsumahato'pyaham||1||

2purastādeva bhagavanmayaitadapavarjitam
|
yena māṃ nābhiśaṅketa yena kṛtsnaṃ hitaṃ bhavet||2||

3ānṛśaṃsyena dharmeṇa loke hyasmin jijīviṣuḥ
|
nāhametadalaṃ kartuṃ naitattvayyupapadyate||3||

4muniruvāca| upapannastvametena yathā kṣatriya bhāṣase
|
prakṛtyā hyupapanno'si buddhyā vā bahudarśanaḥ||4||

5ubhayoreva vāmarthe yatiṣye tava tasya ca
|
saṃśleṣaṃ vā kariṣyāmi śāśvataṃ hyanapāyinam||5||

6tvādṛśaṃ hi kule jātamanṛśaṃsaṃ bahuśrutam
|
amātyaṃ ko na kurvīta rājyapraṇayakovidam||6||

7yastvaṃ pracyāvito rājyādvyasanaṃ cottamaṃ gataḥ
|
ānṛśaṃsyena vṛttena kṣatriyecchasi jīvitum||7||

8āgantā madgṛhaṃ tāta vaidehaḥ satyasaṃgaraḥ
|
athāhaṃ taṃ niyokṣyāmi tatkariṣyatyasaṃśayam||8||

9tata āhūya vaidehaṃ munirvacanamabravīt
|
ayaṃ rājakule jāto viditābhyantaro mama||9||

10ādarśa iva śuddhātmā śāradaścandramā yathā
|
nāsminpaśyāmi vṛjinaṃ sarvato me parīkṣitaḥ||10||

11tena te saṃdhirevāstu viśvasāsminyathā mayi
|
na rājyamanamātyena śakyaṃ śāstumapi tryaham||11||

12amātyaḥ śūra eva syādbuddhisampanna eva vā
|
tābhyāṃ caivobhayaṃ rājanpaśya rājyaprayojanam||12||

13dharmātmanāṃ kvacilloke nānyāsti gatirīdṛśī
|
mahātmā rājaputro'yaṃ satāṃ mārgamanuṣṭhitaḥ||13||

14susaṃgṛhītastvevaiṣa tvayā dharmapurogamaḥ
|
saṃsevyamānaḥ śatrūṃste gṛhṇīyānmahato gaṇān||14||

15yadyayaṃ pratiyuddhyettvāṃ svakarma kṣatriyasya tat
|
jigīṣamāṇastvāṃ yuddhe pitṛpaitāmahe pade||15||

16tvaṃ cāpi pratiyuddhyethā vijigīṣuvrate sthitaḥ
|
ayudhvaiva niyogānme vaśe kuruhite sthitaḥ||16||

17sa tvaṃ dharmamavekṣasva hitvā lobhamasāmpratam
|
na ca kāmānna ca drohātsvadharmaṃ hātumarhasi||17||

18naiva nityaṃ jayastāta naiva nitvaṃ parājayaḥ
|
tasmādbhojayativyaśca bhoktavyaśca paro janaḥ||18||

19ātmanyapi ca saṃdṛśyāvubhau jayaparājayau
|
niḥśeṣakāriṇāṃ tāta niḥśeṣakaraṇādbhayam||19||

20ityuktaḥ pratyuvācedaṃ vacanaṃ brāhmaṇarṣabham
|
pratipūjyābhisatkṛtya pūjārhamanumānya ca||20||

21yathā brūyānmahāprājño yathā brūyānmahāśrutaḥ
|
śreyaskāmo yathā brūyādubhayoreva tatkṣamam||21||

22yadyadvacanamukto'smi kariṣyāmi ca tattathā
|
etaddhi paramaṃ śreyo na me'trāsti vicāraṇā||22||

23tataḥ kausalyamāhūya maithilo vākyamabravīt
|
dharmato nītitaścaiva lokaśca vijito mayā||23||

24ahaṃ tvayā cātmaguṇairjitaḥ pārthivasattama
|
ātmānamanavajñāya jitavadvartatāṃ bhavān||24||

25nāvamanyāmi te buddhiṃ nāvamanye ca pauruṣam
|
nāvamanye jayāmiti jitavadvartatāṃ bhavān||25||

26yathāvatpūjito rājangṛhaṃ gantāsi me bhṛśam
|
tataḥ sampūjya tau vipraṃ viśvastau jagmaturgṛhān||26||

27vaidehastvatha kausalyaṃ praveśya gṛhamañjasā
|
pādyārghyamadhuparkaistaṃ pūjārhaṃ pratyapūjayat||27||

28dadau duhitaraṃ cāsmai ratnāni vividhāni ca
|
eṣa rājñāṃ paro dharmo nityau jayaparājayau||28||
