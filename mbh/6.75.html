Mahābhārata 6.75 (Bhīṣmaparvan)

1sañjaya uvāca|
te viśramya tato rājan sahitāḥ kurupāṇḍavāḥ
|
vyatītāyāṃ tu śarvaryāṃ punaryuddhāya niryayuḥ ||1||

2tatra śabdo mahānāsīttava teṣāṃ ca bhārata
|
yujyatāṃ rathamukhyānāṃ kalpyatāṃ caiva dantinām ||2||

3saṃnahyatāṃ padātīnāṃ hayānāṃ caiva bhārata
|
śaṅkhadundubhinādaśca tumulaḥ sarvato'bhavat ||3||

4tato yudhiṣṭhiro rājā dhṛṣṭadyumnamabhāṣata
|
vyūhaṃ vyūhya mahābāho makaraṃ śatrunāśanam ||4||

5evamuktastu pārthena dhṛṣṭadyumno mahārathaḥ
|
vyādideśa mahārāja rathino rathināṃ varaḥ ||5||

6śiro'bhūddrupadastasya pāṇḍavaśca dhanañjayaḥ
|
cakṣuṣī sahadevaśca nakulaśca mahārathaḥ ||6||

7tuṇḍamāsīnmahārāja bhīmaseno mahābalaḥ
|
saubhadro draupadeyāśca rākṣasaśca ghaṭotkacaḥ ||7||

8sātyakirdharmarājaśca vyūhagrīvāṃ samāsthitāḥ
|
pṛṣṭhamāsīnmahārāja virāṭo vāhinīpatiḥ ||8||

9dhṛṣṭadyumnena sahito mahatyā senayā vṛtaḥ
|
kekayā bhrātaraḥ pañca vāmapārarśvaṃ samāśritāḥ ||9||

10dhṛṣṭaketurnaravyāghraścekitānaśca vīryavān
|
dakṣiṇaṃ pakṣamāśritya sthitau vyūhasya rakṣaṇe ||10||

11pā‌dayostu mahārāja sthitaḥ śrīmān mahārathaḥ
|
kuntibhojaḥ śatānīko mahatyā senayā vṛtaḥ ||11||

12śikhaṇḍī tu maheṣvāsaḥ somakaiḥ saṃvṛto balī
|
irāvāṃśca tataḥ pucche makarasya vyavasthitau ||12||

13evametaṃ mahāvyūhaṃ vyūhya bhārata pāṇḍavāḥ
|
sūryodaye mahārāja punaryuddhāya daṃśitāḥ ||13||

14kauravānabhyayustūrṇaṃ hastyaśvarathapattibhiḥ
|
samucchritairdhvajaiśchatraiḥ śastraiśca vimalaiḥ śitaiḥ ||14||

15vyūḍhaṃ dṛṣṭvā tu tat sainyaṃ pitā devavratastava
|
krauñcena mahatā rājan pratyavyūhata vāhinīm ||15||

16tasya tuṇḍe maheṣvāso bhāradvājo vyarocata
|
aśvatthāmā kṛpaścaiva cakṣurāsīnnareśvaraḥ ||16||

17kṛtavarmā tu sahitaḥ kāmbojavarabāhlikaiḥ
|
śirasyāsīnnaraśreṣṭhaḥ śreṣṭhaḥ sarvadhanuṣmatām ||17||

18grīvāyāṃ śūrasenaśca tava putraśca māriṣa
|
duryodhano mahārāja rājabhirbahubhirvṛtaḥ ||18||

19prāgjyotiṣastu sahito madrasauvīrakekayaiḥ
|
urasyabhūnnaraśreṣṭha mahatyā senayā vṛtaḥ ||19||

20svasenayā ca sahitaḥ suśarmā prasthalādhipaḥ
|
vāmapakṣaṃ samāśritya daṃśitaḥ samavasthitaḥ ||20||

21tuṣārā yavanāścaiva śakāśca saha cūcupaiḥ
|
dakṣiṇaṃ pakṣamāśritya sthitā vyūhasya bhārata ||21||

22śrutāyuśca śatāyuśca saumada‌ttiśca māriṣa
|
vyūhasya jaghane tasthū rakṣamāṇāḥ parasparam ||22||

23tato yuddhāya sañjagmuḥ pāṇḍavāḥ kauravaiḥ saha
|
sūryodaye mahārāja tato yuddhamabhūnmahat ||23||

24pratīyū rathino nāgā nāgāṃśca rathino yayuḥ
|
hayārohān rathārohā rathinaścāpi sādinaḥ ||24||

25sādinaśca hayān rājan rathinaśca mahāraṇe
|
hastyārohān hayārohā rathinaḥ sādinastathā ||25||

26rathinaḥ pattibhiḥ sārdhaṃ sādinaścāpi pattibhiḥ
|
anyonyaṃ samare rājan pratyadhāvannamarṣitāḥ ||26||

27bhīmasenārjunayamairguptā cānyairmahārathaiḥ
|
śuśubhe pāṇḍavī senā nakṣatrairiva śarvarī ||27||

28tathā bhīṣma-kṛpa-droṇa-śalya-duryodhanādibhiḥ
|
tavāpi ca babhau senā grahairdyauriva saṃvṛtā ||28||

29bhīmasenastu kaunteyo droṇaṃ dṛṣṭvā parākramī
|
abhyayājjavanairaśvairbhāradvājasya vāhinīm ||29||

30droṇastu samare kruddho bhīmaṃ navabhirāyasaiḥ
|
vivyādha samaraślādhī marmāṇyuddiśya vīryavān ||30||

31dṛḍhāhatastato bhīmo bhāradvājasya saṃyuge
|
sārathiṃ preṣayāmāsa yamasya sadanaṃ prati ||31||

32sa saṅgṛhya svayaṃ vāhān bhāradvājaḥ pratāpavān
|
vyadhamat pāṇḍavīṃ senāṃ tūlarāśimivānalaḥ ||32||

33te vadhyamānā droṇena bhīṣmeṇa ca narottamāḥ
|
sṛñjayāḥ kekayaiḥ sārdhaṃ palāyanaparā'bhavan ||33||

34tathaiva tāvakaṃ sainyaṃ bhīmārjunaparikṣatam
|
muhyate tatra tatraiva samadeva varāṅganā ||34||

35abhidyetāṃ tato vyūhau tasmin vīravarakṣaye
|
āsīdvyatikaro ghorastava teṣāṃ ca bhārata ||35||

36tadadbhutamapaśyāma tāvakānāṃ paraiḥ saha
|
ekāyanagatāḥ sarve yadayudhyanta bhārata ||36||

37pratisaṃvārya cāstrāṇi te'nyonyasya viśāṃpate
|
yuyudhuḥ pāṇḍavāścaiva kauravāśca mahābalāḥ ||37||
