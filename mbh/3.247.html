Mahābhārata 3.247 (Āraṇyakaparvan)

1janamejaya uvāca|
śatrubhirjitabaddhasya pāṇḍavaiśca mahātmabhiḥ
|
mokṣitasya yudhā paścānmāninaḥ sudurātmanaḥ||1||

2katthanasyāvaliptasya garvitasya ca nityaśaḥ
|
sadā ca pauruṣaudāryaiḥ pāṇḍavānavamanyataḥ||2||

3duryodhanasya pāpasya nityāhaṅkāravādinaḥ
|
praveśo hāstinapure duṣkaraḥ pratibhāti me||3||

4tasya lajjānvitasyaiva śokavyākulacetasaḥ
|
praveśaṃ vistareṇa tvaṃ vaiśampāyana kīrtaya||4||

5vaiśampāyana uvāca|
dharmarājanisṛṣṭastu dhārtarāṣṭraḥ suyodhanaḥ
|
lajjayā'dhomukhaḥ sīdannupāsarpat suduḥkhitaḥ||5||

6svapuraṃ prayayau rājā caturaṅgabalānugaḥ
|
śokopahatayā buddhyā cintayānaḥ parābhavam||6||

7vimucya pathi yānāni deśe suyavasodake
|
sanniviṣṭaḥ śubhe ramye bhūmibhāge yathepsitam||7||

8hastyaśvarathapādātaṃ yathāsthānaṃ nyaveśayat
|
athopaviṣṭaṃ rājānaṃ paryaṅke jvalanaprabhe||8||

9upaplutaṃ yathā somaṃ rāhuṇā rātrisaṅkṣaye
|
upāgamyābravīt karṇo duryodhanamidaṃ tadā||9||

10diṣṭyā jīvasi gāndhāre diṣṭyā naḥ saṅgamaḥ punaḥ
|
diṣṭyā tvayā jitāścaiva gandharvāḥ kāmarūpiṇaḥ||10||

11diṣṭyā samagrān paśyāmi bhrātṝṃste kurunandana
|
vijigīṣūn raṇe yuktān nirjitārīn mahārathān||11||

12ahaṃ tvabhidrutaḥ sarvairgandharvaiḥ paśyatastava
|
nāśaknuvaṃ sthāpayituṃ dīryamāṇāṃ ca vāhinīm||12||

13śarakṣatāṅgaśca bhṛśaṃ vyapayāto'bhipīḍitaḥ
|
idaṃ tvatyadbhutaṃ manye yadyuṣmāniha bhārata||13||

14ariṣṭānakṣatāṃścāpi sadārabalavāhanān
|
vimuktān samprapaśyāmi yuddhāt tasmādamānuṣāt||14||

15naitasya kartā loke'smin pumān vidyati bhārata
|
yat kṛtaṃ te mahārāja saha bhrātṛbhirāhave||15||

16vaiśampāyana uvāca|
evamuktastu karṇena rājā duryodhanastadā
|
uvāca cāṅgarājānaṃ bāṣpagadgadayā girā||16||
