Mahābhārata 9.23 (Śalyaparvan)

1sañjaya uvāca|
vartamāne tadā yuddhe ghorarūpe bhayānake
|
abhajyata balaṃ tatra tava putrasya pāṇḍavaiḥ||1||

2tāṃstu yatnena mahatā sannivārya mahārathān
|
putraste yodhayāmāsa pāṇḍavānāmanīkinīm||2||

3nivṛttāḥ sahasā yodhāstava putrajayaiṣiṇaḥ
|
sannivṛtteṣu teṣvevaṃ yuddhamāsītsudāruṇam||3||

4tāvakānāṃ pareṣāṃ ca devāsuraraṇopamam
|
pareṣāṃ tava sainye vā nāsītkaścitparāṅmukhaḥ||4||

5anumānena yudhyante saṃjñābhiśca parasparam
|
teṣāṃ kṣayo mahānāsīdyudhyatāmitaretaram||5||

6tato yudhiṣṭhiro rājā krodhena mahatā yutaḥ
|
jigīṣamāṇaḥ saṅgrāme dhārtarāṣṭrānsarājakān||6||

7tribhiḥ śāradvataṃ viddhvā rukmapuṅkhaḥ śilāśitaiḥ
|
caturbhirnijaghānāśvānnārācaiḥ kṛtavarmaṇaḥ||7||

8aśvatthāmā tu hārdikyamapovāha yaśasvinam
|
atha śāradvato'ṣṭābhiḥ pratyaviddhyadyudhiṣṭhiram||8||

9tato duryodhano rājā rathānsaptaśatān raṇe
|
praiṣayadyatra rājāsau dharmaputro yudhiṣṭhiraḥ||9||

10te rathā rathibhiryuktā manomārutaraṃhasaḥ
|
abhyadravanta saṅgrāme kaunteyasya rathaṃ prati||10||

11te samantānmahārāja parivārya yudhiṣṭhiram
|
adṛśyaṃ sāyakaiścakrurmeghā iva divākaram||11||

12te dṛṣṭvā dharmarājānaṃ kauraveyaistathā kṛtam
|
nāmṛṣyanta susaṃrabdhāḥ śikhaṇḍipramukhā rathāḥ||12||

13rathairaśvavarairyuktaiḥ kiṅkiṇījālasaṃvṛtaiḥ
|
ājagmuratha rakṣantaḥ kuntīputraṃ yudhiṣṭhiram||13||

14tataḥ pravavṛte raudraḥ saṅgrāmaḥ śoṇitodakaḥ
|
pāṇḍavānāṃ kurūṇāṃ ca yamarāṣṭravivardhanaḥ||14||

15rathānsaptaśatānhatvā kurūṇāmātatāyinām
|
pāṇḍavāḥ saha pañcālaiḥ punarevābhyavārayan||15||

16tatra yuddhaṃ mahaccāsīttava putrasya pāṇḍavaiḥ
|
na ca tattādṛśaṃ dṛṣṭaṃ naiva cāpi pariśrutam||16||

17vartamāne tadā yuddhe nirmaryāde samantataḥ
|
vadhyamāneṣu yodheṣu tāvakeṣvitareṣu ca||17||

18vinadatsu ca yodheṣu śaṅkhavaryaiśca pūritaiḥ
|
utkruṣṭaiḥ siṃhanādaiśca garjitaiścaiva dhanvinām||18||

19atipravṛtte yuddhe ca chidyamāneṣu marmasu
|
dhāvamāneṣu yodheṣu jayagṛddhiṣu māriṣa||19||

20saṃhāre sarvato jāte pṛthivyāṃ śokasambhave
|
bahvīnāmuttamastrīṇāṃ sīmantoddharaṇe tathā||20||

21nirmaryāde mahāyuddhe vartamāne sudāruṇe
|
prādurāsanvināśāya tadotpātāḥ sudāruṇāḥ||21||

22cacāla śabdaṃ kurvāṇā saparvatavanā mahī
|
sadaṇḍāḥ solmukā rājan kīryamāṇāḥ samantataḥ||22||

23ulkāḥ peturdivo bhūmāvāhatya ravimaṇḍalam
|
viṣvagvātāḥ prādurāsannīcaiḥ śarkaravarṣiṇaḥ||23||

24aśrūṇi mumucurnāgā vepathuṃ cāspṛśanbhṛśam
|
etān ghorānanādṛtya samutpātānsudāruṇān||24||

25punaryuddhāya saṃyattāḥ kṣatriyāstasthuravyathāḥ
|
ramaṇīye kurukṣetre puṇye svargaṃ yiyāsavaḥ||25||

26tato gāndhārarājasya putraḥ śakunirabravīt
|
yuddhyadhvamagrato yāvatpṛṣṭhato hanmi pāṇḍavān||26||

27tato naḥ samprayātānāṃ madrayodhāstarasvinaḥ
|
hṛṣṭāḥ kilakilāśabdamakurvanta pare tathā||27||

28asmāṃstu punarāsādya labdhalakṣā durāsadāḥ
|
śarāsanāni dhunvantaḥ śaravarṣairavākiran||28||

29tato hataṃ paraistatra madrarājabalaṃ tadā
|
duryodhanabalaṃ dṛṣṭvā punarāsīt parāṅmukham||29||

30gāndhārarājastu punarvākyamāha tato balī
|
nivartadhvamadharmajñā yudhyadhvaṃ kiṃ sṛtena vaḥ||30||

31anīkaṃ daśasāhasramaśvānāṃ bharatarṣabha
|
āsīdgāndhārarājasya viśālaprāsayodhinām||31||

32balena tena vikramya vartamāne janakṣaye
|
pṛṣṭhataḥ pāṇḍavānīkamabhyaghnanniśitaiḥ śaraiḥ||32||

33tadabhramiva vātena kṣipyamāṇaṃ samantataḥ
|
abhajyata mahārāja pāṇḍūnāṃ sumahadbalam||33||

34tato yudhiṣṭhiraḥ prekṣya bhagnaṃ svabalamantikāt
|
abhyanādayadavyagraḥ sahadevaṃ mahābalam||34||

35asau subalaputro no jaghanaṃ pīḍya daṃśitaḥ
|
sainyāni sūdayatyeṣa paśya pāṇḍava durmatim||35||

36gaccha tvaṃ draupadeyaiśca śakuniṃ saubalaṃ jahi
|
rathanīkamahaṃ dhakṣye pañcālasahito'nagha||36||

37gacchantu kuñjarāḥ sarve vājinaśca saha tvayā
|
pādātāśca trisāhasrāḥ śakuniṃ tairvṛto jahi||37||

38tato gajāḥ saptaśatāścāpapāṇibhirāsthitāḥ
|
pañca cāśvasahasrāṇi sahadevaśca vīryavān||38||

39pādātāśca trisāhasrā draupadeyāśca sarvaśaḥ
|
raṇe hyabhyedravaṃste tu śakuniṃ yuddhadurmadam||39||

40tatastu saubalo rājannabhyatikramya pāṇḍavān
|
jaghāna pṛṣṭhataḥ senāṃ jayagṛddhaḥ pratāpavān||40||

41aśvarohāstu saṃrabdhāḥ pāṇḍavānāṃ tarasvinām
|
prāviśansaubalānīkamabhyatikramya tān rathān||41||

42te tatra sādināḥ śūrāḥ saubalasya mahadbalam
|
raṇamadhye vyatiṣṭhanta śaravarṣairavākiran||42||

43tadudyatagadāprāsamākapuruṣasevitam
|
prāvartata mahadyuddhaṃ rājandurmantrite tava||43||

44upāramanta jyāśabdāḥ prekṣakā rathino'bhavan
|
na hi sveṣāṃ pareṣāṃ vā viśeṣaḥ pratyadṛśyata||44||

45śūrabāhuvisṛṣṭānāṃ śaktīnāṃ bharatarṣabha
|
jyotiṣāmiva sampātamapaśyankurupāṇḍavāḥ||45||

46ṛṣṭibhirvimalābhiśca tatra tatra viśāmpate
|
sampatantībhirākāśamāvṛtaṃ bahvaśobhata||46||

47prāsānāṃ patatāṃ rājan rūpamāsītsamantataḥ
|
śalabhānāmivākāśe tadā bharatasattama||47||

48rudhirokṣitasarvāṅgā vipraviddhairniyantṛbhiḥ
|
hayāḥ paripatanti sma śataśo'tha sahasraśaḥ||48||

49anyoyaṃ paripiṣṭāśca samāsādya parasparam
|
āvikṣatāḥ sma dṛśyante vamanto rudhiraṃ mukhaiḥ||49||

50tato'bhavattamo ghoraṃ sainyena rajasā vṛte
|
tānapākramato'drākṣaṃ tasmāddeśādarindama||50||

51aśvān rājanmanuṣyāṃśca rajasā saṃvṛte sati
|
bhūmau nipatitāścānye vamanto rudhiraṃ bahu||51||

52keśākeśi samālagnā na śekuśceṣṭituṃ narāḥ
|
anyonyamaśvapṛṣṭhebhyo vikarṣanto mahābalāḥ||52||

53mallā iva samāsādya nijaghnuritaretaram
|
aśvaiśca vyapakṛṣyanta bahavo'tra gatāsavaḥ||53||

54bhūmau nipatitāścānye bahavo vijayaiṣiṇaḥ
|
tatra tatra vyadṛśyanta puruṣāḥ śūramāninaḥ||54||

55raktokṣitaiśchinnabhujairavakṛṣṭaśiroruhaiḥ
|
vyadṛśyata mahī kīrṇā śataśo'tha sahasraśaḥ||55||

56dūraṃ na śakyaṃ tatrāsīdgantumaśvena kenacit
|
sāśvārohairhatairaśvairāvṛte vasudhātale||56||

57rudhirokṣitasannāhairāttaśastrairudāyudhaiḥ
|
nānāpraharaṇairghoraiḥ parasparavadhaiṣibhiḥ||57||

58susannikṛṣṭaiḥ saṅgrāme hatabhūyiṣṭhasainikaiḥ
|
sa muhūrtaṃ tato yuddhvā saubalo'tha viśāmpate||58||

59ṣaṭsāhasrairhayaiḥ śiṣṭairapāyācchakunistataḥ
|
tathaiva pāṇḍavānīkaṃ rudhireṇa samukṣitam||59||

60ṣaṭsāhasrairhayaiḥ śiṣṭairapāyācchāntavāhanam
|
aśvārohāśca pāṇḍūnāmabruvan rudhirokṣitāḥ||60||

61susannikṛṣṭe saṅgrāme bhūyiṣṭhe tyaktajīvitāḥ
|
na hi śakyaṃ sthairyoddhuṃ kuta eva mahāgajaiḥ||61||

62rathāneva rathā yāntu kuñjarāḥ kuñjarānapi
|
pratiyāto hi śakuniḥ svamanīkamavasthitaḥ||62||

63na punaḥ saubalo rājā yuddhamabhyāgamiṣyati
|
tatastu draupadeyāśca te ca mattā mahādvipāḥ||63||

64prayayuryatra pāñcālyo dhṛṣṭadyumno mahārathaḥ
|
sahadevo'pi kauravya rajomeghe samutthite||64||

65ekākī prayayau tatra yatra rājā yudhiṣṭhiraḥ
|
tatasteṣu prayāteṣu śakuniḥ saubalaḥ punaḥ||65||

66pārśvato'bhyahanatkruddho dhṛṣṭadyumnasya vāhinīm
|
tatpunastumulaṃ yuddhaṃ prāṇāṃstyaktvābhyavartata||66||

67tāvakānāṃ pareṣāṃ ca parasparavadhaiṣiṇām
|
te cānyonyamavaikṣanta tasmin vīrasamāgame||67||

68yodhāḥ paryapatan rājan śataśo'tha sahasraśaḥ
|
asibhiśchidyamānānāṃ śirasāṃ lokasaṃkṣaye||68||

69prādurāsīnmahāśabdastālānāṃ patatāmiva
|
vimuktānāṃ śarīrāṇāṃ chinnānāṃ patatāṃ bhuvi||69||

70sāyudhānāṃ ca bāhūnāmurūṇāṃ ca viśāmpate
|
āsītkaṭakaṭāśabdaḥ sumahā~llomaharṣaṇaḥ||70||

71nighnanto niśitaiḥ śastrairbhātṝnputrānsakhīnapi
|
yodhāḥ paripatanti sma yathāmiṣakṛte khagāḥ||71||

72anyonyaṃ pratisaṃrabdhāḥ samāsādya parasparam
|
ahaṃ pūrvamahaṃ pūrvamiti nighnansahasraśaḥ||72||

73saṅghātenāsanabhraṣṭairaśvārohairgatāsubhiḥ
|
hayāḥ paripatanti sma śataśo'tha sahasraśaḥ||73||

74sphuratāṃ prativiṣṭānāmaśvānāṃ śīghragāminām
|
stanatāṃ ca manuṣyāṇāṃ sannaddhānāṃ viśāmpate||74||

75śaktyṛṣṭiprāsaśabdaśca tumulaḥ samapadyata
|
bhindatāṃ paramarmāṇi rājan durmantrite tava||75||

76śramābhibhūtāḥ saraṃbdhāḥ śrāntavāhāḥ pipāsavaḥ
|
vikṣatāśca śitaiḥ śastrairabhyavartanta tāvakāḥ||76||

77mattā rudhiragandhena bahavo'tra vicetasaḥ
|
jaghnuḥ parānsvakāṃścaiva prāptānprāptānanantarān||77||

78bahavaśca gataprāṇāḥ kṣatriyā jayagṛddhinaḥ
|
bhūmāvabhyapatan rājan śaravṛṣṭibhirāvṛtāḥ||78||

79vṛkagṛdhraśṛgālānāṃ tumule modane'hani
|
āsīdbalakṣayo ghorastava putrasya paśyataḥ||79||

80narāśvakāyaiḥ sañchannā bhūmirāsīdviśāmpate
|
rudhirodakacitrā ca bhīrūṇāṃ bhayavardhinī||80||

81asibhiḥ paṭṭiśaiḥ śūlaistakṣamāṇāḥ punaḥ punaḥ
|
tāvakāḥ pāṇḍaveyāśca na nyavartanta bhārata||81||

82praharanto yathāśakti yāvatprāṇasya dhāraṇam
|
yodhāḥ paripatanti sma vamanto rudhiraṃ vraṇaiḥ||82||

83śiro gṛhītvā keśeṣu kabandhaḥ sma pradṛśyate
|
udyamya ca śitaṃ khaḍgaṃ rudhireṇa pariplutam||83||

84tathotthiteṣu bahuṣu kabadheṣu narādhipa
|
tathā rudhiragandhena yodhāḥ kaśmalamāviśan||84||

85mandībhūte tataḥ śabde pāṇḍavānāṃ mahadbalam
|
alpāvaśiṣṭaisturagairabhyavartata saubalaḥ||85||

86tato'bhyadhāvaṃstvaritāḥ pāṇḍavā jayagṛddhinaḥ
|
padātayaśca nāgāśca sādinaścodyatāyudhāḥ||86||

87koṣṭhakīkṛtya cāpyenaṃ parikṣipya ca sarvaśaḥ
|
śastrairnānāvidhairjaghnuryuddhapāraṃ titīrṣavaḥ||87||

88tvadīyāstāṃstu samprekṣya sarvataḥ samabhidrutān
|
rathāśvapattidviradāḥ pāṇḍavanabhidudruvuḥ||88||

89kecitpadātayaḥ padbhirmuṣṭibhiśca parasparam
|
nijaghnuḥ samare śūrāḥ kṣīṇaśastrāstato'patan||89||

90rathebhyo rathinaḥ peturdvipebhyo hastisādinaḥ
|
vimānebhyo divo bhraṣṭāḥ siddhāḥ puṇyakṣayādiva||90||

91evamanyonyamāyattā yodhā jaghnurmahāhave
|
pitṝn bhrātṝnvayasyāṃśca putrānāpi tathā pare||91||

92evamāsīdamaryādaṃ yuddhaṃ bharatasattama
|
prāsāsibāṇakalilaṃ vartamāne sudāruṇe||92||
