Mahābhārata 1.15 (Ādiparvan)

1sautiruvāca|
mātrā hi bhujagāḥ śaptāḥ pūrvaṃ brahmavidāṃ vara
|
janamejayasya vo yajñe dhakṣyatyanilasārathiḥ||1||

2tasya śāpasya śāntyarthaṃ pradadau pannagottamaḥ
|
svasāramṛṣaye tasmai suvratāya mahātmane||2||

3sa ca tāṃ pratijagrāha vidhidṛṣṭena karmaṇā
|
āstīko nāmaputraśca tasyāṃ jajñe mahāmanāḥ||3||

4tapasvī ca mahātmā ca vedavedāṅgapāragaḥ
|
samaḥ sarvasya lokasya pitṛmātṛbhayāpahaḥ||4||

5atha dīrghasya kālasya pāṇḍaveyo narādhipaḥ
|
ājahāra mahāyajñaṃ sarpasatramiti śrutiḥ||5||

6tasminpravṛtte satre tu sarpāṇāmantakāya vai
|
mocayāmāsa tānnāgānāstīkaḥ sumahātapāḥ||6||

7bhrātṝṃśca mātulāṃścaiva tathaivānyānsa pannagān
|
pitṝṃśca tārayāmāsa saṃtatyā tapasā tathā||7||

8vrataiśca vividhairbrahmansvādhyāyaiścānṛṇo'bhavat
|
devāṃśca tarpayāmāsa yajñairvividhadakṣiṇaiḥ||8||

9ṛṣīṃśca brahmacaryeṇa santatyā ca pitāmahān
|
apahṛtya guruṃ bhāraṃ pitṝṇāṃ saṃśitavrataḥ||9||

10jaratkārurgataḥ svargaṃ sahitaḥ svaiḥ pitāmahaiḥ
|
āstīkaṃ ca sutaṃ prāpya dharmaṃ cānuttamaṃ muniḥ||10||

11jaratkāruḥ sumahatā kālena svargameyivān
|
etadākhyānamāstīkaṃ yathāvatkathitaṃ mayā
|
prabrūhi bhṛguśārdūla kimanyatkathayāmi te||11||
