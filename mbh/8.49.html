Mahābhārata 8.49 (Karṇaparvan)

1sañjaya uvāca|
vidārya karṇastāṃ senāṃ yudhiṣṭhiramathādravat
|
rathahastyaśvapattīnāṃ sahastraiḥ parivāritaḥ ||1||

2nānāyudhasahasrāṇi preritānyaribhirvṛṣaḥ
|
chittvā bāṇaśatairugraistānavidhyadasaṃbhramāt ||2||

3nicakarta śirāṃsyeṣāṃ bāhūnūrūṃśca sūtajaḥ
|
te hatā vasudhāṃ peturbhagnāścānye vidudruvuḥ ||3||

4drāviḍāstu niṣādāstu punaḥ sātyakicoditāḥ
|
abhyadravajjighāṃsantaḥ pattayaḥ karṇamāhave ||4||

5te vibāhuśirastrāṇāḥ prahatāḥ karṇasāyakaiḥ
|
petuḥ pṛthivyāṃ yugapacchinnaṃ śālavanaṃ yathā ||5||

6evaṃ yodhaśatānyājau sahasrāṇyayutāni ca
|
hatānīyurmahīṃ dehairyaśasā pūrayandiśaḥ ||6||

7atha vaikartanaṃ karṇaṃ raṇe kruddhamivāntakam
|
rurudhuḥ pāṇḍupañcālā vyādhiṃ mantrauṣadhairiva ||7||

8sa tānpramṛdyābhyapatatpunareva yudhiṣṭhiram
|
mantrauṣadhikriyātīto vyādhiratyulvaṇo yathā ||8||

9sa rājagṛddhibhī ruddhaḥ pāṇḍupañcālakekayaiḥ
|
nāśakattānatikrāntuṃ mṛtyurbrahmavido yathā ||9||

10tato yudhiṣṭhiraḥ karṇamadūrasthaṃ nivāritam
|
abravītparavīraghnaṃ krodhasaṃraktalocanaḥ ||10||

11karṇa karṇa vṛthādṛṣṭe sūtaputra vacaḥ śṛṇu
|
sadā spardhasi saṃgrāme phālgunena tarasvinā ||11||

12tathāsmānbādhase nityaṃ dhārtarāṣṭramate sthitaḥ
|
yadbalaṃ yacca te vīryaṃ pradveṣo yastu pāṇḍuṣu ||12||

13tatsarvaṃ darśayasvādya paurupaṃ mahadāsthitaḥ
|
yuddhaśraddhāṃ ca te'dyāhaṃ vineṣyāmi mahāhave ||13||

14evamuktvā mahārāja karṇaṃ pāṇḍusutastadā
|
suvarṇapuṅkhairdaśabhirvivyādhāyasmayaiḥ śaraiḥ ||14||

15taṃ sūtaputro daśabhiḥ pratyaviddhyada‌rindamaḥ
|
vatsadantairmaheṣvāsaḥ prahasanniva bhārata ||15||

16so'vajñāya tu nirviddhaḥ sūtaputreṇa māriṣa
|
prajajvāla tataḥ krodhāddhaviṣeva hutāśanaḥ ||16||

17jvālāmālāparikṣipto rājño deho vyadṛśyata
|
yugānte dagdhukāmasya saṃvartāgnerivāparaḥ ||17||

18tato visphārya sumahaccāpaṃ hemapariṣkṛtam
|
samādhatta śitaṃ bāṇaṃ girīṇāmapi dāraṇam ||18||

19tataḥ pūrṇāyatotkṛṣṭaṃ yamadaṇḍanibhaṃ śaram
|
mumoca tvarito rājā sūtaputrajighāṃsayā ||19||

20sa tu vegavatā mukto bāṇo vajrāśanisvanaḥ
|
viveśa sahasā karṇaṃ savye pārśve mahāratham ||20||

21sa tu tena prahāreṇa pīḍitaḥ pramumoha vai
|
srastagātro mahābāhurdhanurutsṛjya syandane ||21||

22gatāsuriva niścetāḥ śalyasyābhimukho'patat
|
rājāpi bhūyo nājaghne karṇaṃ pārthahitepsayā ||22||

23tato hāhākṛtaṃ sarvaṃ dhārtarāṣṭrabalaṃ mahat
|
vivarṇamukhabhūyiṣṭhaṃ karṇaṃ dṛṣṭvā tathāgatam ||23||

24siṃhanādaśca saṃjajñe kṣvelāḥ kilakilāstathā
|
pāṇḍavānāṃ mahārāja dṛṣṭvā rājñaḥ parākramam ||24||

25pratilabhya tu rādheyaḥ saṃjñāṃ nāticirādiva
|
dadhre rājavināśāya manaḥ krūraparākramaḥ ||25||

26sa hemavikṛtaṃ cāpaṃ visphārya vijayaṃ mahat
|
avākiradameyātmā pāṇḍavaṃ niśitaiḥ śaraiḥ ||26||

27tataḥ kṣurābhyāṃ pāñcālyau cakrarakṣau mahātmanaḥ
|
jaghāna candradevaṃ ca daṇḍadhāraṃ ca saṃyuge ||27||

28tāvubhau dharmarājasya pravīrau paripārśvataḥ
|
rathābhyāśe cakāśete candrasyeva punarvasū ||28||

29yudhiṣṭhiraḥ punaḥ karṇamaviddhyastriṃśatā śaraiḥ
|
suṣeṇaṃ satyasenaṃ ca tribhistribhiratāḍayat ||29||

30śalyaṃ navatyā vivyādha trisaptatyā ca sūtajam
|
tāṃstasya goptṝnvivyādha tribhistribhirajihmagaiḥ ||30||

31tataḥ prahasyādhirathirvidhunvānaḥ sa kārmukam
|
bhittvā bhallena rājānaṃ viddhvā ṣaṣṭyā'nada‌ttadā ||31||

32tataḥ pravīrāḥ pāṇḍūnāmabhyadhāvannamarṣitāḥ
|
yudhiṣṭhiraṃ parīpsantaḥ karṇamabhyardayañcharaiḥ ||32||

33sātyakiścekitānaśca yuyutsuḥ pāṇḍya eva ca
|
dhṛṣṭadyumnaḥ śikhaṇḍī ca draupadeyāḥ prabhadrakāḥ ||33||

34yamau ca bhīmasenaśca śiśupālasya cātmajaḥ
|
kārūṣā matsyaśeṣāśca kekayāḥ kāśikosalāḥ ||34||

35ete ca tvaritā vīrā vasuṣeṇamatāḍayan
|
janamejayaśca pāñcālyaḥ karṇaṃ vivyādha sāyakaiḥ ||35||

36vārāhakarṇanārācairnālīkairniśitaiḥ śaraiḥ
|
vatsadantairvipāṭhaiśca kṣurapraiścaṭakāmukhaiḥ ||36||

37nānāpraharaṇaiścograi rathahastyaśvasādibhiḥ
|
sarvato'bhyadravatkarṇaṃ parivārya jighāṃsayā ||37||

38sa pāṇḍavānāṃ pravaraiḥ sarvataḥ samabhidrutaḥ
|
udīrayan brāhmamastraṃ śarairāpūrayandiśaḥ ||38||

39tataḥ śaramahājvālo vīryoṣmā karṇapāvakaḥ
|
nirdahanpāṇḍavavanaṃ vīraḥ paryacaradraṇe ||39||

40sa sandhāya mahāstrāṇi maheṣvāsā mahāmanāḥ
|
prahasya puruṣendrasya śaraiściccheda kārmukam ||40||

41tataḥ sandhāya navatiṃ nimeṣānnataparvaṇām
|
bibheda kavacaṃ rājño raṇe karṇaḥ śitaiḥ śaraiḥ ||41||

42tadvarma hemavikṛtaṃ ratnacitraṃ babhau patat
|
savidyudabhraṃ savituḥ śliṣṭaṃ vātahataṃ yathā ||42||

43tadaṅgātpuruṣendrasya bhraṣṭaṃ varma vyarocata
|
ratnairalaṅkṛtaṃ citrairvyabhraṃ niśi yathā nabhaḥ ||43||

44chinnavarmā śaraiḥ pārtho rudhireṇa samukṣitaḥ
|
tataḥ sarvāyasīṃ śaktiṃ cikṣepādhirathiṃ prati ||44||

45tāṃ jvalantīmivākāśe śaraiściccheda saptabhiḥ
|
sā chinnā bhūmimagamanmaheṣvāsasya sāyakaiḥ ||45||

46tato bāhvorlarlāṭe ca hṛdi caiva yudhiṣṭhiraḥ
|
caturbhistomaraiḥ karṇaṃ tāḍayitvā nadanmudā ||46||

47udbhinnarudhiraḥ karṇaḥ kruddhaḥ sarpa iva śvasan
|
dhvajaṃ ciccheda bhallena tribhirvivyādha pāṇḍavam ||47||

48iṣudhī cāsya ciccheda rathaṃ ca tilaśo'cchinat
|
kālavālāstu ye pārthaṃ dantavarṇā'vahanhayāḥ ||48||

49tairyuktaṃ rathamāsthāya prāyādrājā parāṅmukhaḥ
|
evaṃ pārthobhyupāyātsa nihataḥ pārṣṇisārathiḥ ||49||

50aśaknuvanpramukhataḥ sthātuṃ karṇasya durmanāḥ
|
abhidrutya tu rādheyaḥ pāṇḍuputraṃ yudhiṣṭhiram ||50||

51vajracchatrāṃkuśairmatsyairdhvajakūrmāmbujādibhiḥ
|
lakṣaṇairupapannena pāṇḍunā pāṇḍunandanam ||51||

52pavitrīkartumātmānaṃ skandhe saṃspṛśya pāṇinā
|
grahītumicchansa balāt kuntīvākyaṃ ca so'smarat ||52||

53taṃ śalyaḥ prāha mā karṇa gṛhīthāḥ pārthivottamam
|
gṛhītamātro hatvā tvāṃ mā kariṣyati bhasmasāt ||53||

54abravītprahasanrājan kutsayanniva pāṇḍavam
|
kathaṃ nāma kule jātaḥ kṣatradharme vyavasthitaḥ ||54||

55prajahyātsamaraṃ bhītaḥ prāṇānrakṣanmahāhave
|
na bhavān kṣatradharmeṣu kuśalo hīti me matiḥ ||55||

56brāhme bale bhavān yuktaḥ svādhyāye yajñakarmaṇi
|
mā sma yuddhyasva kaunteya mā sma vīrānsamāsadaḥ ||56||

57mā caitānapriyaṃ brūhi mā vai vraja mahāraṇam ||57||

58vaktavyā māriṣānye tu na vaktavyāstu mādṛśāḥ
|
mādṛśānvibruvanyuddhe etadanyacca lapsyase
|
svagṛhaṃ gaccha kaunteya yatra tau keśavārjunau ||58||

59na hi tvāṃ samare rājan hanyātkarṇaḥ kathañcana
|
evamuktvā tataḥ pārthaṃ visṛjya ca mahābalaḥ ||59||

60nyahanatpāṇḍavīṃ senāṃ vajrahasta ivāsurīm
|
tatopāyāddrutaṃ rājanvrīḍanniva nareśvaraḥ ||60||

61athāpayati rājānaṃ matvānvīyustamacyutam
|
cedipāṇḍavapañcālāḥ sātyakiśca mahārathaḥ ||61||

62draupadeyāstathā śūrā mādrīputrau ca pāṇḍavau
|
tato yudhiṣṭhirānīkaṃ dṛṣṭvā karṇaḥ parāṅmukham ||62||

63kurubhiḥ sahito vīraḥ prahṛṣṭaḥ pṛṣṭhato'nvagāt
|
bherīśaṅkhamṛdaṃgānāṃ kārmukāṇāṃ ca niḥsvanaḥ ||63||

64babhūva dhārtarāṣṭrāṇāṃ siṃhanādaravastathā
|
yudhiṣṭhirastu kauravya rathamāruhya satvaram ||64||

65śrutakīrtermahārāja dṛṣṭavānkarṇavikramam
|
kālyamānaṃ balaṃ dṛṣṭvā dharmarājo yudhiṣṭhiraḥ ||65||

66svān yodhānabravītkuddho nighnataitān kimāsataḥ
|
tato rājñā'bhyanujñātāḥ pāṇḍavānāṃ mahārathāḥ ||66||

67bhīmasenamukhāḥ sarve putrāṃste pratyupādravan
|
abhavattumulaḥ śabdo yodhānāṃ tatra bhārata ||67||

68rathahastyaśvapattīnāṃ śastrāṇāṃ ca tatastataḥ
|
uttiṣṭhata praharata praitābhipatateti ca ||68||

69iti bruvāṇā hyanyonyaṃ jaghnuryodhā mahāraṇe
|
abhracchāyeva tatrāsīccharavṛṣṭibhirambare ||69||

70samāvṛtairnaravarairnighnadbhiritaretaram
|
vipatākadhvajacchatrā vyaśvasūtāyudhā raṇe ||70||

71vyaṅgāṅgāvayavāḥ petuḥ kṣitau kṣīṇāḥ kṣitīśvarāḥ
|
pravaṇādiva śailānāṃ śikharāṇi dvipottamāḥ ||71||

72sārohā nihatāḥ peturvajrabhinnā ivādrayaḥ
|
chinnabhinnaviparyastairvarmālaṅkārabhūṣaṇaiḥ ||72||

73sārohāsturagāḥ peturhatavīrāḥ sahasraśaḥ
|
vipraviddhāyudhāścaiva virathāśca rathairhatāḥ ||73||

74prativīraiśca saṃmarde pattisaṃghāḥ sahasraśaḥ
|
viśālāyatatāmrākṣaiḥ padmendusadṛśānanaiḥ ||74||

75śirobhiryuddhaśauṇḍānāṃ sarvataḥ saṃvṛtā mahī
|
yathā bhuvi tathā vyomni niḥsvanaṃ śuśruvurjanāḥ ||75||

76vimānairapsaraḥ saṅghairgītavāditraniḥsvanaiḥ
|
hatānabhimukhānvīrān vīraiḥ śatasahasraśaḥ ||76||

77āropyāropya gacchanti vimāneṣvapsarogaṇāḥ
|
taddṛṣṭvā mahadāścaryaṃ pratyakṣaṃ svargalipsayā ||77||

78prahṛṣṭamanasaḥ śūrāḥ kṣipraṃ jaghnuḥ parasparam
|
rathino rathibhiḥ sārdhaṃ citraṃ yuyudhurāhave ||78||

79pattayaḥ pattibhirnāgāḥ saha nāgairhayairhayāḥ
|
evaṃ pravṛtte saṃgrāme gaja-vāji-narakṣaye ||79||

80sainyena rajasā vṛtte sve svāñjaghnuḥ pare parān
|
kacākaci yuddhamāsīddantādanti nakhānakhi ||80||

81muṣṭiyuddhaṃ niyuddhaṃ ca dehapāpmāsunāśanam
|
tathā vartati saṃgrāme gaja-vāji-narakṣaye ||81||

82narāśvanāgadehebhyaḥ prasṛtā lohitāpagā
|
gajāśvanaradehānsā vyuvāha patitānbahūn ||82||

83narāśva-gajasambādhe narāśva-gaja-sādinām
|
lohitodā mahāghorā māṃsaśoṇitakardamā ||83||

84narāśvagajadehānāṃ vahantī bhīrubhīṣaṇā
|
tasyāḥ pāramapāraṃ ca vrajanti vijayaiṣiṇaḥ ||84||

85gādhena cotplavantaśca nimajjyonmajya cāpare
|
te tu lohitadigdhāṅgā raktavarmāyudhāmbarāḥ ||85||

86sasrustasyāṃ papuścāsyāṃ mamluśca bharatarṣabha
|
rathānaśvānnarānnāgānāyudhābharaṇāni ca ||86||

87vasanānyatha varmāṇi vadhyamānān hatānapi
|
bhūmiṃ khaṃ dyāṃ diśaścaiva prāyaḥ paśyāma lohitam ||87||

88lohitasya tu gandhena sparśena ca rasena ca
|
rūpeṇa cātiraktena śabdena ca visarpatā ||88||

89viṣādaḥ sumahānāsītprāyaḥ sainyasya bhārata
|
tattu viprahataṃ sainyaṃ bhīmasenamukhāstadā ||89||

90bhūyaḥ samādravanvīrāḥ sātyakipramukhāstadā
|
teṣāmāpatatāṃ vegamaviṣahyaṃ nirīkṣya ca ||90||

91putrāṇāṃ te mahāsainyamāsīdrājan parāṅmukham
|
tatprakīrṇarathāśvebhaṃ naravājisamākulam ||91||

92vidhvastavarmakavacaṃ praviddhāyudhakārmukam
|
vyadravattāvakaṃ sainyaṃ loḍyamānaṃ samaṃtataḥ
|
siṃhārditamivāraṇye yathā gajakulaṃ tathā ||92||
