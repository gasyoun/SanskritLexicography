Mahābhārata 8.63 (Karṇaparvan)

1sañjaya uvāca|
karṇo'pi śarajālena kekayānāṃ mahārathān
|
vyadhamatparameṣvāsānagrataḥ paryavasthitān ||1||

2teṣāṃ prayatamānānāṃ rādheyasya nivāraṇe
|
rathān pañcaśatānkarṇaḥ prāhiṇodyamasādanam ||2||

3aviṣahyaṃ tato dṛṣṭvā rādheyaṃ yudhi yodhinaḥ
|
bhīmasenamupāgacchankarṇabāṇaprapīḍitāḥ ||3||

4rathānīkaṃ vidāryaiva śarajālairanekadhā
|
karṇa ekarathenaiva yudhiṣṭhiramupādravat ||4||

5senāniveśamārcchantaṃ mārgaṇaiḥ kṣatavikṣatam
|
yamayormadhyagaṃ vīraṃ śanairyāntaṃ vicetasam ||5||

6samāsādya tu rājānaṃ duryodhanahitepsayā
|
sūtaputrastribhistīkṣṇairvivyādha parameṣubhiḥ ||6||

7tathaiva rājā rādheyaṃ pratyavidhyatstanāntare
|
śaraistribhiśca yantāraṃ caturbhiścaturo hayān ||7||

8cakrarakṣau tu pārthasya mādrīputrau parantapau
|
tāvapyadhāvatāṃ karṇaṃ rājānaṃ mā vadhīriti ||8||

9tau pṛthak śaravarṣābhyāṃ rādheyamabhyavarṣatām
|
nakulaḥ sahadevaśca paramaṃ yatnamāsthitau ||9||

10tathaiva tau pratyavidhyatsūtaputraḥ pratāpavān
|
bhallābhyāṃ śitadhārābhyāṃ mahātmānāvarinda‌mau ||10||

11dantavarṇāṃstu rādheyo nijaghāna manojavān
|
yudhiṣṭhirasya saṃgrāme kālavālānhayottamān ||11||

12tato'pareṇa bhallena śirastrāṇamapātayat
|
kaunteyasya maheṣvāsaḥ prahasanniva sūtajaḥ ||12||

13tathaiva nakulasyāpi hayānhatvā pratāpavān
|
īṣāṃ dhanuśca ciccheda mādrīputrasya dhīmataḥ ||13||

14tau hatāśvau hatarathau pāṇḍavau bhṛśavikṣatau
|
bhrātarāvāruruhatuḥ sahadevarathaṃ tadā ||14||

15tau dṛṣṭvā mātulastatra virathau paravīrahā
|
abhyabhāṣata rādheyaṃ madrarājo'nukaṃpayā ||15||

16yoddhavyamadya pārthena phālgunena tvayā saha
|
kimarthaṃ dharmarājena yudhyase bhṛśaroṣitaḥ ||16||

17kṣīṇaśastrāstrakavacaḥ kṣīṇabāṇo vibāṇadhiḥ
|
śrāntasārathivāhaśca cchanno'strairaribhistathā ||17||

18pārthamāsādya rādheya upahāsyo bhaviṣyasi
|
evamukto'pi karṇastu madrarājena saṃyuge ||18||

19tathaiva karṇaḥ saṃrabdho yudhiṣṭhiramatāḍayat
|
śaraistīkṣṇaiḥ parāvidhya mādrīputrau ca pāṇḍavau ||19||

20prahasya samare karṇaścakāra vimukhaṃ śaraiḥ
|
tataḥ śalyaḥ prahasyedaṃ karṇaṃ punaruvāca ha ||20||

21rathasthamatisaṃrabdhaṃ yudhiṣṭhiravadhe dhṛtam
|
yadarthaṃ dhārtarāṣṭreṇa satataṃ mānito bhavān ||21||

22taṃ pārthaṃ jahi rādheya kiṃ te hatvā yudhiṣṭhiram
|
śaṃkhayordhmāyatoḥ śabdaḥ sumahāneṣa kṛṣṇayoḥ ||22||

23śrūyate cāpaghoṣo'yaṃ prāvṛṣīvāmbudasya ha
|
asau nighnanrathodārānarjunaḥ śaravṛṣṭibhiḥ ||23||

24sarvāṃ grasati naḥ senāṃ karṇa paśyainamāhave
|
pṛṣṭharakṣau ca śūrasya yudhāmanyūttamaujasau ||24||

25uttaraṃ cāsya vai śūraścakraṃ rakṣati sātyakiḥ
|
dhṛṣṭadyumnastathā cāsya cakraṃ rakṣati dakṣiṇam ||25||

26bhīmasenaśca vai rājñā dhārtarāṣṭreṇa yudhyate
|
yathā na hanyāttaṃ bhīmaḥ sarveṣāṃ no'dya paśyatām ||26||

27tathā rādheya kriyatāṃ rājā mucyeta no yathā
|
paśyainaṃ bhīmasenena grastamāhavaśobhinam ||27||

28yadi tvāsādya mucyeta vismayaḥ sumahān‌ bhavet
|
paritrāhyenamabhyetya saṃśayaṃ paramaṃ gatam ||28||

29kiṃ nu mādrīsutau hatvā rājānaṃ ca yudhiṣṭhiram
|
iti śalyavacaḥ śrutvā rādheyaḥ pṛthivīpate
|
dṛṣṭvā duryodhanaṃ caiva bhīmagrastaṃ mahāhave ||29||

30rājagṛddhī bhṛśaṃ caiva śalyavākyapracoditaḥ
|
ajātaśatrumutsṛjya mādrīputrau ca pāṇḍavau ||30||

31tava putraṃ paritrātumabhyadhāvata vīryavān
|
madrarājapraṇudi‌tairaśvairākāśagairiva ||31||

32gate karṇe tu kaunteyaḥ pāṇḍuputro yudhiṣṭhiraḥ
|
apāyājjavanairaśvaiḥ sahadevaśca māriṣa ||32||

33tābhyāṃ sa sahitastūrṇaṃ vrīḍanniva nareśvaraḥ
|
prāpya senāniveśaṃ ca mārgaṇaiḥ kṣatavikṣataḥ ||33||

34avatīrṇo rathāttūrṇamāviśacchayanaṃ śubham
|
apanītaśalyaḥ subhṛśaṃ hṛcchalyābhinipīḍitaḥ ||34||

35so'bravīdbhrātarau rājā mādrīputrau mahārathau
|
anīkaṃ bhīmasenasya pāṇḍavāvāśu gacchatām ||35||

36jīmūta iva nardaṃstu yudhyate sa vṛkodaraḥ
|
tato'nyaṃ rathamāsthāya nakulo rathapuṅgavaḥ ||36||

37sahadevaśca tejasvī bhrātarau śatrukarṣaṇau
|
turagairagryaraṃhobhiryātvā bhīmasya śuṣmiṇau
|
anīkaiḥ sahitau tatra bhrātarau samavasthitau ||37||
