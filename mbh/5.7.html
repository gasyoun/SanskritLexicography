Mahābhārata 5.7 (Udyogaparvan)

1vaiśampāyana uvāca|
purohitaṃ te prasthāpya nagaraṃ nāgasāhvayam
|
dūtān prasthāpayāmāsuḥ pārthivebhyastatastataḥ ||1||

2prasthāpya dūtānanyatra dvārakāṃ puruṣarṣabhaḥ
|
svayaṃ jagāma kauravyaḥ kuntīputro dhanañjayaḥ ||2||

3gate dvāravatīṃ kṛṣṇe baladeve ca mādhave
|
saha vṛṣṇyandhakaiḥ sarvairbhojaiśca śataśastadā ||3||

4sarvamāgamayāmāsa pāṇḍavānāṃ viceṣṭitam
|
dhṛtarāṣṭrātmajo rājā gūḍhaiḥ praṇihitaiścaraiḥ ||4||

5sa śrutvā mādhavaṃ yāntaṃ sadaśvairanilopamaiḥ
|
balena nātimahatā dvārakāmabhyayāt purīm ||5||

6tameva divasaṃ cāpi kaunteyaḥ pāṇḍunandanaḥ
|
ānartanagarīṃ ramyāṃ jagāmāśu dhanañjayaḥ ||6||

7tau yātvā puruṣavyāghrau dvārakāṃ kurunandanau
|
suptaṃ dadṛśatuḥ kṛṣṇaṃ śayānaṃ cābhijagmatuḥ ||7||

8tataḥ śayāne govinde praviveśa suyodhanaḥ
|
ucchīrṣataśca kṛṣṇasya niṣasāda varāsane||8||

9tataḥ kirīṭī tasyānupraviveśa mahāmanāḥ
|
paścāccaiva tu sa kṛṣṇasya prahvo'tiṣṭhar kṛtāñjaliḥ ||9||

10pratibuddhaḥ sa vārṣṇeyo dadarśāgre kirīṭinam
|
sa tayoḥ svāgataṃ kṛtvā yathāvatpratipūjya tau ||10||

11tadāgamanajaṃ hetuṃ papraccha madhusūdanaḥ
|
tato duryodhanaḥ kṛṣṇāmuvāca prahasanniva||11||

12vigrahe'smin bhavān sāhyaṃ mama dātumihārhati
|
samaṃ hi bhavataḥ sakhyaṃ mama caivārjune'pi ca||12||

13tathā saṃbandhakaṃ tulyamasmākaṃ tvayi mādhava
|
ahaṃ cābhigataḥ pūrvaṃ tvāmadya madhusūdana||13||

14pūrvaṃ cābhigataṃ santo bhajante pūrvasāriṇaḥ
|
tvaṃ ca śreṣṭhatamo loke satāmadya janārdana ||14||

15satataṃ saṃmataścaiva sadvṛttamanupālaya| kṛṣṇa uvāca|
bhavānabhigataḥ pūrvamatra me nāsti saṃśayaḥ
|
dṛṣṭastu prathamaṃ rājan mayā pārtho dhanañjayaḥ ||15||

16tava pūrvābhigamanāt pūrvaṃ cāpyasya darśanāt
|
sāhāyyamubhayoreva kariṣyāmi suyodhana ||16||

17pravāraṇaṃ tu bālānāṃ pūrvaṃ kāryamiti śrutiḥ
|
tasmāt pravāraṇaṃ pūrvamarhaḥ pārtho dhanañjayaḥ ||17||

18matsaṃhananatulyānāṃ gopānāmarbudaṃ mahat
|
nārāyaṇā iti khyātāḥ sarve saṅgrāmayodhinaḥ ||18||

19te vā yudhi durādharṣā bhavantvekasya sainikāḥ
|
ayudhyamānaḥ saṃgrāme nyastaśastro'hamekataḥ ||19||

20ābhyāmanyataraṃ pārtha yatte hṛdyataraṃ matam
|
tadvṛṇītāṃ bhavānagre pravāryastvaṃ hi dharmataḥ ||20||

21vaiśampāyana uvāca|
evamuktastu kṛṣṇena kuntīputro dhanañjayaḥ
|
ayudhyamānaṃ saṅgrāme varayāmāsa keśavam||21||

22nārāyaṇamamitraghnaṃ kāmājjātamajaṃ nṛṣu
|
sarvakṣatrasya purato devadānavayorapi ||22||

23duryodhanastu tat sainyaṃ sarvamāvarayattadā
|
sahasrāṇāṃ sahasraṃ tu yodhānāṃ prāpya bhārata ||23||

24kṛṣṇaṃ cāpahṛtaṃ jñātvā saṃprāpa paramāṃ mudam
|
duryodhanastu tatsainyaṃ sarvamādāya pārthivaḥ ||24||

25tato'bhyayādbhīmabalo rauhiṇeyaṃ mahābalam
|
sarvaṃ cāgamane hetuṃ sa tasmai saṃnyavedayat
|
pratyuvāca tataḥ śaurirddhārtarāṣṭramidaṃ vacaḥ ||25||

26baladeva uvāca|
viditaṃ te naravyāghra sarvaṃ bhavitumarhati
|
yanmayoktaṃ virāṭasya purā vaivāhike tadā ||26||

27nigṛhyokto hṛṣīkeśastvadarthaṃ kurunandana
|
mayā saṃbandhakaṃ tulyamiti rājan punaḥ punaḥ ||27||

28na ca tadvākyamuktaṃ vai keśavaṃ pratyapadyata
|
na cāhamutsahe kṛṣṇaṃ vinā sthātumapi kṣaṇam||28||

29nāhaṃ sahāyaḥ pārthasya nāpi duryodhanasya vai
|
iti me niścitā buddhirvāsudevamavekṣya ha ||29||

30jāto'si bhārate vaṃśe sarvapārthivapūjite
|
gaccha yudhyasva dharmeṇa kṣātreṇa puruṣarṣabha ||30||

31vaiśampāyana uvāca|
ityevamuktastu tadā pariṣvajya halāyudham
|
kṛṣṇaṃ cāpahṛtaṃ jñātvā yuddhānmene jitaṃ jayam||31||

32so'bhyayāt kṛtavarmāṇaṃ dhṛtarāṣṭrasuto nṛpaḥ
|
kṛtavarmā dadau tasya senāmakṣauhiṇīṃ tadā ||32||

33sa tena sarvasainyena bhīmena kurunandanaḥ
|
vṛtaḥ pariyayau hṛṣṭaḥ suhṛdaḥ saṃpraharṣayan ||33||

34tataḥ pītāmbaradharo jagatsraṣṭā janārdanaḥ
|
gate duryodhane kṛṣṇaḥ kirīṭinamathābravīt ||34||

35ayudhyamānaḥ kāṃ bṛddhimāsthāyāhaṃ vṛtastvayā| arjuna uvāca|
bhavān samarthastān sarvānnihantuṃ nātra saṃśayaḥ
|
nihantumahamapyekaḥ samarthaḥ puruṣarṣabha ||35||

36bhavāṃstu kīrtimā~lloke tadyaśastvāṃ gamiṣyati
|
yaśasāṃ cāhamapyartho tasmādasi mayā vṛtaḥ ||36||

37sārathyaṃ tu tvayā kāryamiti me mānasaṃ sadā
|
cirarātrepsitaṃ kāmaṃ tadbhavān kartumarhati ||37||

38vāsudeva uvāca|
upapannamidaṃ pārtha yatspardhasi mayā saha
|
sārathyaṃ te kariṣyāmi kāmaḥ sampadyatāṃ tava ||38||

39vaiśampāyana uvāca|
evaṃ pramuditaḥ pārthaḥ kuṣṇena sahitastadā
|
vṛto dāśārhapravaraiḥ punarāyādyudhiṣṭhiram ||39||
