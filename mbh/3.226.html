Mahābhārata 3.226 (Āraṇyakaparvan)

1mārkaṇḍeya uvāca|
tasmin jāte mahāsattve mahāsene mahābale
|
samuttasthurmahotpātā ghorarūpāḥ pṛthagvidhāḥ||1||

2strīpuṃsorviparatiṃ ca tathā dvandvāni yāni ca
|
grahā dīptā diśaḥ khaṃ ca rarāsa ca mahī bhṛśam||2||

3ṛṣayaśca mahāghorān dṛṣṭvotpātān samantataḥ
|
akurvan śāntimudvignā lokānāṃ lokabhāvanāḥ||3||

4nivasanti vane ye tu tasmiṃścaitrarathe janāḥ
|
te'bruvanneṣa no'narthaḥ pāvakenāhito mahān||4||

5saṅgamya ṣaḍbhiḥ patnībhiḥ saptarṣīṇāmiti sma ha
|
apare garuḍīmāhustvayā'nartho'yamāhṛtaḥ||5||

6yairdṛṣṭā sā tadā devī tasyā rūpeṇa gacchatī
|
na tu tat svāhayā karma kṛtaṃ jānāti vai janaḥ||6||

7suparṇī tu vacaḥ śrutvā mamāyaṃ tanayastviti
|
upagamya śanaiḥ skandamāhāhaṃ jananī tava||7||

8atha saptarṣayaḥ śrutvā jātaṃ putraṃ mahaujasam
|
tattyajuḥ ṣaṭ tadā patnīrvinā devīmarundhatīm||8||

9ṣaḍbhireva tadā jātamāhustadvanavāsinaḥ
|
saptarṣīnāha ca svāhā mama putro'yamityuta||9||

10ahaṃ jāne naitadevamiti rājan punaḥ punaḥ
|
viśvāmitrastu kṛtvoṣṭiṃ saptarṣīṇāṃ mahāmuniḥ||10||

11pāvakaṃ kāmasantaptamadṛṣṭaḥ pṛṣṭhato'nvagāt
|
tat tena nikhilaṃ sarvamavabuddhaṃ yathātatham||11||

12viśvāmitrastu prathamaṃ kumāraṃ śaraṇaṃ gataḥ
|
stavaṃ divyaṃ sampracakraṃ mahāsenasya cāpi saḥ||12||

13maṅgalāni ca sarvāṇi kaumārāṇi trayodaśa
|
jātakarmādikāstasya kriyāścakre mahāmuniḥ||13||

14ṣaḍvakrasya tu māhātmyaṃ kukkuṭasya tu sādhanam
|
śaktyā devyāḥ sādhanaṃ ca tathā pāriṣadāmapi||14||

15viśvāmitraścakāraitat karma lokahitāya vai
|
tasmādṛṣiḥ kumārasya viśvāmitro'bhavat priyaḥ||15||

16anvajānācca svāhāyā rūpānyatvaṃ mahāmuniḥ
|
abravīcca munīn sarvānnāparādhyanti vai striyaḥ||16||

17śrutvā tu tattvatastasmāt te patnīḥ sarvato'tyajan
|
mārkaṇḍeya uvāca|
skandaṃ śrutvā tadā devā vāsavaṃ sahitā'bruvan||17||

18aviṣahya balaṃ skandaṃ jahi śakrāśu māciram
|
yadi vā na nihaṃsyenaṃ devendro'yaṃ bhaviṣyati||18||

19trailokyaṃ sannigṛhyāsmāṃstvāṃ ca śakra mahābala
|
sa tānuvāca vyathito bālo'yaṃ sumahābalaḥ||19||

20sraṣṭāramapi lokānāṃ yudhi vikramya nāśayet
|
na bālamutsahe hantumiti śakraḥ prabhāṣate||20||

21te'bruvan nāsti te vīryaṃ yata evaṃ prabhāṣase
|
sarvāstvadyābhigacchantu skandaṃ lokasya mātaraḥ||21||

22kāmavīryā ghnantu vainaṃ tathetyuktvā ca tā yayuḥ
|
tamapratibalaṃ dṛṣṭvā viṣaṇṇavadanāstu tāḥ||22||

23aśakyo'yaṃ vicintyaivaṃ tameva śaraṇaṃ yayuḥ
|
ūcuścainaṃ tvamasmākaṃ putro bhava mahābala||23||

24abhinandasva naḥ sarvāḥ prastutāḥ snehaviklavāḥ
|
tāsāṃ tadvacanaṃ śrutvā pātukāmaḥ stanān prabhuḥ||24||

25tāḥ sampūjya mahāsenaḥ kāmāṃścāsāṃ pradāya saḥ
|
apaśyadagnimāyāntaṃ pitaraṃ balināṃ balī||25||

26sa tu sampūjitastena saha mātṛgaṇena ha
|
parivārya mahāsenaṃ rakṣamāṇaḥ sthitaḥ śivaḥ||26||

27sarvāsāṃ yā tu mātṝṇāṃ nārī krodhasamudbhavā
|
dhātrī svaputravat skandaṃ śūlahastā'bhyarakṣata||27||

28lohitasyodadheḥ kanyā krūrā lohitabhojanā
|
pariṣvajya mahāsenaṃ putravat paryarakṣata||28||

29agnirbhūtvā naigameyaśchāgavakro bahuprajaḥ
|
ramayāmāsa śailasthaṃ bālaṃ krīḍanakairiva||29||
