Mahābhārata 6.111 (Bhīṣmaparvan)

1sañjaya uvāca|
sātyakiṃ daṃśitaṃ yuddhe bhīṣmāyābhyudyataṃ raṇe
|
ārṣyaśṛṅgirmaheṣvāso vārayāmāsa saṃyuge ||1||

2mādhavastu susaṃkruddho rākṣasaṃ navabhiḥ śaraiḥ
|
ājaghāna raṇe rājan prahasanniva bhārata ||2||

3tathaiva rākṣaso rājan mādhavaṃ navabhiḥ śaraiḥ
|
ardayāmāsa rājendra saṃkruddhaḥ śinipuṅgavam ||3||

4śaineyaḥ śarasaṅghaṃ tu preṣayāmāsa saṃyuge
|
rākṣasāya susaṃkruddho mādhavaḥ paravīrahā ||4||

5tato rakṣo mahābāhuṃ sātyakiṃ satyavikramam
|
vivyādha viśikhaistīkṣṇaiḥ siṃhanādaṃ nanāda ca ||5||

6mādhavastu bhṛśaṃ viddho rākṣasena raṇe tadā
|
vāryamāṇaśca tejasvī jahāsa ca nanāda ca ||6||

7bhagadattastataḥ kuddho mādhavaṃ niśitaiḥ śaraiḥ
|
tāḍayāmāsa samare totrairiva mahāgajam ||7||

8vihāya rākṣasaṃ yuddhe śaineyo rathināṃ varaḥ
|
prāgjyotiṣāya cikṣepa śarān saṃnataparvaṇaḥ ||8||

9tasya prāgjyotiṣo rājā mādhavasya mahaddhanuḥ
|
ciccheda śatadhāreṇa bhallena kṛtahastavat ||9||

10athānyaddhanurādāya vegavat paravīrahā
|
bhagadattaṃ raṇe kruddhaṃ vivyādha niśitaiḥ śaraiḥ ||10||

11so'tividdho maheṣvāsaḥ sṛkkiṇī parisaṃlihan
|
śaktiṃ kanakavaidūryabhūṣitāmāyasīṃ dṛḍhām ||11||

12yamadaṇḍopamāṃ ghorāṃ cikṣepa paramāhave
|
tāmāpatantīṃ sahasā tasya bāhubaleritām ||12||

13sātyakiḥ samare rājandvidhā ciccheda sāyakaiḥ
|
tataḥ papāta sahasā maholkeva hataprabhā ||13||

14śaktiṃ vinihatāṃ dṛṣṭvā putrastava viśāṃpate
|
mahatā rathavaṃśena vārayāmāsa mādhavam ||14||

15tathā parivṛtaṃ dṛṣṭvā vārṣṇeyānāṃ mahāratham
|
duryodhano bhṛśaṃ kruddho bhrātṝn sarvānuvāca ha ||15||

16tathā kuruta kauravyā yathā vaḥ sātyako yudhi
|
na jīvan pratiniryāti mahato'smādrathavrajāt ||16||

17tasminhate hataṃ manye pāṇḍavānāṃ mahadbalam
|
tatheti ca vacastasya parigṛhya mahārathāḥ ||17||

18śaineyaṃ yodhayāmāsurbhīṣmāyābhyudyataṃ raṇe
|
kāmbojarājo balavān vārayāmāsa saṃyuge ||18||

19ārjuniṃ nṛpatirviddhvā śaraiḥ sannataparvabhiḥ
|
punareva catuḥṣaṣṭyā rājanvivyādha taṃ nṛpa ||19||

20sudakṣiṇastu samare punarvivyādha pañcabhiḥ
|
sārathiṃ cāsya navabhiricchanbhīṣmasyajīvitam ||20||

21tadyuddhamāsīt sumahattayostatra samāgame
|
yadā'bhyadhāvadgāṅgeyaṃ śikhaṇḍī śatrukarśanaḥ ||21||

22virāṭadrupadau vṛddhau vārayantau mahācamūm
|
bhīṣmaṃ ca yudhi saṃrabdhāvādravantau mahārathau ||22||

23aśvatthāmā raṇe kruddhaḥ sabhiyādrathasattamaḥ
|
tataḥ pravavṛte yuddhaṃ tayostasya ca bhārata ||23||

24virāṭo daśabhirmallairājaghāna parantapa
|
yatamānaṃ maheṣvāsaṃ drauṇimāhavaśobhinam ||24||

25drupadaśca tribhirbāṇairvivyādha niśitaistadā
|
guruputraṃ samāsādya praharantau mahābalau ||25||

26aśvatthāmā tatastau tu vivyādha bahubhiḥ śaraiḥ
|
virāṭadrupadau vīrau bhīṣmaṃ prati samudyatau ||26||

27tatrādbhutamapaśyāma vṛddhayoścaritaṃ mahat
|
yaddrauṇisāyakān ghorān pratyavārayatāṃ yudhi ||27||

28sahadevaṃ tathā yāntaṃ kṛpaḥ śāradvato'bhyayāt
|
yathā nāgo vane nāgaṃ matto mattamupādravat ||28||

29kṛpaśca samare śūro mādrīputraṃ mahāratham
|
ājaghāna śaraistūrṇaṃ saptatyā rukmabhūṣaṇaiḥ ||29||

30tasya mādrīsutaścāpaṃ dvidhā ciccheda sāyakaiḥ
|
athainaṃ chinnadhanvānaṃ vivyādha navabhiḥ śaraiḥ ||30||

31so'nyat kārmukamādāya samare bhārasādhanam
|
mādrīputraṃ susaṃhṛṣṭo daśabhirniśitaiḥ śaraiḥ ||31||

32ājaghānorasi kuddha icchan bhīṣmasya jīvitam
|
tathaiva pāṇḍavo rājañchāradvatamamarṣaṇam ||32||

33ājaghānorasi kuddho bhīṣmasya vadhakāṅkṣayā
|
tayoryuddhaṃ samabhavadghorarūpaṃ bhayāvaham ||33||

34nakulaṃ tu raṇe kruddho vikarṇaḥ śatrutāpanaḥ
|
vivyādha sāyakaiḥ ṣaṣṭyā rakṣan bhīṣmaṃ mahābalam ||34||

35nakulo'pi bhṛśaṃ viddhastava putreṇa dhīmatā
|
vikarṇaṃ saptasaptatyā nirbibheda śilīmukhaiḥ ||35||

36tatra tau naraśārdūlau bhīṣmahetoḥ paraṃtapau
|
anyonyaṃ jaghnaturvīrau goṣṭhe govṛṣabhāviva ||36||

37ghaṭotkacaṃ raṇe yāntaṃ nighnantaṃ tava vāhinīm
|
durmukhaḥ samare prāyādbhīṣmahetoḥ parākramī ||37||

38haiḍimbastu raṇe rājan durmukhaṃ śatrutāpanam
|
ājaghānorasi kruddhaḥ śareṇānataparvaṇā ||38||

39bhīmasenasutaṃ cāpi durmukhaḥ sumukhaiḥ śaraiḥ
|
ṣaṣṭyā vīro nadan hṛṣṭo vivyādha raṇamūrdhani ||39||

40dhṛṣṭadyumnaṃ tathā yāntaṃ bhīṣmasya vadhakāṃkṣiṇam
|
hārdikyo vārayāmāsa rathaśreṣṭhaṃ mahārathaḥ ||40||

41hārdikyaḥ pārṣataṃ cāpi viddhvā pañcabhirāyasaiḥ
|
punaḥ pañcāśatā tūrṇaṃ tiṣṭha tiṣṭheti cābravīt||41||

42ājaghāna mahābāhuḥ pārṣataṃ taṃ mahāratham
|
taṃ caiva pārṣato rājan hārdikyaṃ navabhiḥ śaraiḥ ||42||

43vivyādha niśitaistīkṣṇaiḥ kaṅkapatrairajihmagaiḥ
|
tayoḥ samabhavadyuddhaṃ bhīṣmahetormahāhave ||43||

44anyonyātiśaye yuktaṃ yathā vṛtramahendrayoḥ
|
bhīmasenaṃ tathā yāntaṃ bhīṣmaṃ prati mahāratham ||44||

45bhūriśravābhyayāttūrṇaṃ tiṣṭha tiṣṭheti cābravīt
|
saumadattiratho bhīmamājaghāna stanāntare ||45||

46nārācena sutīkṣṇena rukmapuṅkhena saṃyuge
|
uraḥsthena babhau tena bhīmasenaḥ pratāpavān ||46||

47skandaśaktyā yathā krauṃcaḥ purā nṛpatisattama
|
tau śarān sūryasaṅkāśān karmāraparimārjitān||47||

48anyonyasya raṇe kruddhau cikṣipāte nararṣabhau
|
bhīmo bhīṣmavadhākāṅkṣī saumadattiṃ mahāratham ||48||

49tathā bhīṣmajaye gṛdhruḥ saumadattistu pāṇḍavam
|
kṛtapratikṛte yattau yodhayāmāsatū raṇe ||49||

50yudhiṣṭhiraṃ tu kaunteyaṃ mahatyā senayā vṛtam
|
bhīṣmābhimukhamāyāntaṃ bhāradvājo nyavārayat ||50||

51droṇasya rathanirghoṣaṃ parjanyaninadopamam
|
śrutvā prabhadrakā rājan samakampanta māriṣa ||51||

52sā senā mahatī rājan pāṇḍuputrasya saṃyuge
|
droṇena vāritā yattā na cacāla padātpadam ||52||

53cekitānaṃ raṇe yattaṃ bhīṣmaṃ prati janeśvara
|
citrasenastava sutaḥ kruddharūpamavārayat ||53||

54bhīṣmahetoḥ parākrāntaścitrasenaḥ parākramī
|
cekitānaṃ paraṃ śaktyā yodhayāmāsa bhārata ||54||

55tathaiva cekitāno'pi citrasenamavārayat
|
tadyuddhamāsīt sumahattayostatra samāgame ||55||

56arjuno vāryamāṇastu bahuśastatra bhārata
|
vimukhīkṛtya putraṃ te senāṃ tava mamarda ha ||56||

57duḥśāsano'pi parayā śaktyā pārthamavārayat
|
kathaṃ bhīṣmaṃ na no hanyāditi niścitya bhārata ||57||

58sā vadhyamānā samare putrasya tava vāhinī
|
loḍyate rathibhiḥ śreṣṭhaistatra tatraiva bhārata ||58||
