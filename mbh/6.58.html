Mahābhārata 6.58 (Bhīṣmaparvan)

1sañjaya uvāca|
tataste pārthivāḥ kruddhāḥ phālgunaṃ vīkṣya saṃyuge
|
rathairanekasāhasraiḥ samantāt paryavārayan ||1||

2athainaṃ rathavṛndena koṣṭhakīkṛtya bhārata
|
śaraiḥ subahusāhastraiḥ samantādabhyavārayan ||2||

3śaktīśca vimalāstīkṣṇā gadāśca parighaiḥ saha
|
prāsān paraśvadhāṃścaiva mudgarān musalānapi ||3||

4cikṣipuḥ samare kruddhāḥ phālgunasya rathaṃ prati
|
śastrāṇāmatha tāṃ vṛṣṭiṃ śalabhānāmivāyatim ||4||

5rurodha sarvataḥ pārthaḥ śaraiḥ kanakabhūṣaṇaiḥ
|
tatra tallāghavaṃ dṛṣṭā bībhatsoratimānuṣam ||5||

6deva-dānava-gandharvāḥ piśācoragarākṣasāḥ
|
sādhu sādhviti rājendra phālgunaṃ pratyapūjayan ||6||

7sātyakiścābhimanyuśca mahatyā senayā vṛttau
|
gāndhārān samare śūrān jagmatuḥ sahasaubalān ||7||

8tatra saubalakāḥ kruddhā vārṣṇeyasya rathottamam
|
tilaśaścicchiduḥ krodhācchastrairnānāvidhairyudhi ||8||

9sātyakistu rathaṃ tyaktvā vartamāne bhayāvahe
|
abhimanyo rathaṃ tūrṇamāruroha paraṃtapaḥ ||9||

10tāvekarathasaṃyuktau sābaleyasya vāhinīm
|
vyadhametāṃ śitastūrṇaṃ śaraiḥ sannataparvabhiḥ ||10||

11droṇabhīṣmau raṇe yattau dharmarājasya vāhinīm
|
nāśayetāṃ śaraistīkṣṇaiḥ kaṅkapatraparicchadaiḥ ||11||

12tato dharmasuto rājā mādrīputrau ca pāṇḍavau
|
miṣatāṃ sarvasainyānāṃ droṇānīkamupādravan ||12||

13tatrāsīt sumahadyuddhaṃ tumulaṃ lomaharṣaṇam
|
yathā devāsuraṃ yuddhaṃ pūrvamāsīt sudāruṇam ||13||

14kurvāṇau sumahat karma bhīmasenaghaṭotkacau
|
duryodhanastato'bhyetya tāvubhāvapyavārayat ||14||

15tatrādbhutamapaśyāma haiḍimbasya parākramam
|
atītya pitaraṃ yuddhe yadayudhyata bhārata ||15||

16bhīmasenastu saṃkruddho duryodhanamamarṣaṇam
|
hṛdyavidhyatpṛṣatkena prahasanniva pāṇḍavaḥ ||16||

17tato duryodhano rājā prahāravarapīḍitaḥ
|
niṣasāda rathopasthe kaśmalaṃ ca jagāma ha ||17||

18taṃ visaṃjñaṃ viditvā tu tvaramāṇo'sya sārathiḥ
|
apovāha raṇādrājaṃstataḥ sainyamabhajyata ||18||

19tatastāṃ kauravīṃ senāṃ dravamāṇāṃ samantataḥ
|
nighnan bhīmaḥ śaraistīkṣṇairanuvavrāja pṛṣṭhataḥ ||19||

20pārṣataśca rathaśreṣṭho dharmaputraśca pāṇḍavaḥ
|
droṇasya paśyataḥ sanyaṃ gāṅgeyasya ca paśyataḥ ||20||

21jaghnaturviśikhaistīkṣṇaiḥ parānīkavināśanaiḥ
|
dravamāṇaṃ tu tat sainyaṃ tava putrasya saṃyuge ||21||

22nāśaknutāṃ vārayituṃ bhīṣmadroṇau mahārathau
|
vāryamāṇaṃ ca bhīṣmeṇa droṇena ca mahātmanā ||22||

23vidravatyeva tat sainyaṃ paśyatordroṇabhīṣmayoḥ
|
tato rathasahastreṣu vidravatsu tatastataḥ ||23||

24tāvāsthitāvekarathaṃ saubhadraśinipuṅgavau
|
saubalīṃ samare senāṃ śātayetāṃ samantataḥ ||24||

25śuśubhāte tadā tau tu śaineyakurupuṅgavau
|
amāvāsyāṃ gatau yadvatsomasūryau nabhastale ||25||

26arjunastu tataḥ kruddhastava sainyaṃ viśāṃpate
|
vavarṣa śaravarṣeṇa dhārābhiriva toyadaḥ ||26||

27vadhyamānaṃ tatastatra śaraiḥ pārthasya saṃyuge
|
dudrāva kauravaṃ sainyaṃ viṣādabhayakaṃpitam ||27||

28dravatastān samālakṣya bhīṣmadroṇau mahārathau
|
nyavārayetāṃ saṃrabdhau duryodhanahitaiṣiṇau ||28||

29tato duryodhano rājā samāśvasya viśāṃpate
|
nyavartayata tat sainyaṃ dravamāṇaṃ samaṃtataḥ ||29||

30yatra yatra sutastubhyaṃ yaṃ yaṃ paśyati bhārata
|
tatra tatra nyavartanta kṣatrīyāṇāṃ mahārathāḥ ||30||

31tānnivṛttān samīkṣyaiva tato'nye'pītare janāḥ
|
anyonyaspardhayā rājan lajjayā cāvatasthire ||31||

32punarāvartatāṃ teṣāṃ vega āsīdviśāṃpate
|
pūryataḥ sāgarasyeva candrasyodayanaṃ prati ||32||

33sannivṛttāṃstatastāṃstu dṛṣṭvā rājā suyodhanaḥ
|
abravīt tvarito gatvā bhīṣmaṃ śāntanavaṃ vacaḥ ||33||

34pitāmaha nibodhedaṃ yat tvāṃ vakṣyāmi bhārata
|
nānurūpamahaṃ manye tvayi jīvati kaurava ||34||

35droṇe cāstravidāṃ śreṣṭhe saputre sasuhṛjjane
|
kṛpe caiva maheṣvāse dravate yadvarūthinī ||35||

36na pāṇḍavān pratibalāṃstava manye kathañcana
|
tathā droṇasya saṃgrāme drauṇeścaiva kṛpasya ca ||36||

37anugrāhyāḥ pāṇḍusutāstava nūnaṃ pitāmaha
|
yathemāṃ kṣamase vīra vadhyamānā varūthinīm ||37||

38so'smi vācyastvayā rājanpūrvameva samāgame
|
na yotsye pāṇḍavānsaṃkhye nāpi pārṣatasātyakī ||38||

39śrutvā tu vacanaṃ tubhyamācāryasya kṛpasya ca
|
karṇena sahitaḥ kṛtyaṃ cintayānastadaiva hi ||39||

40yadi nāhaṃ parityājyo yuvābhyāmiha saṃyuge
|
vikrameṇānurūpeṇa yudhyetāṃ puruṣarṣabhau ||40||

41etacchrutvā vaco bhīṣmaḥ prahasan vai muhurmuhuḥ
|
abravīttanayaṃ tubhyaṃ krodhādudvṛtya cakṣuṣī ||41||

42bahuśo'si mayā rājaṃstathyamukto hitaṃ vacaḥ
|
ajeyāḥ pāṇḍavā yuddhe devairapi savāsavaiḥ ||42||

43yattu śakyaṃ mayā kartuṃ vṛddhenādya nṛpottama
|
kariṣyāmi yathāśakti prekṣedānīṃ sabāndhavaḥ ||43||

44adya pāṇḍusutānekaḥ sasainyān saha bandhubhiḥ
|
so'haṃ nivārayiṣyāmi sarvalokasya paśyataḥ ||44||

45evamukte tu bhīṣmeṇa putrāstava janeśvara
|
dadhmuḥ śaṅkhān mudā yuktā bherīḥ sañjaghnire bhṛśam ||45||

46pāṇḍavā hi tato rājan śrutvā taṃ ninadaṃ mahat
|
dadhmuḥ śaṅkhāṃśca bherīśca murajāṃścāpyanādayan ||46||
