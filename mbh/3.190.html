Mahābhārata 3.190 (Āraṇyakaparvan)

1vaiśampāyana uvāca|
yudhiṣṭhirastu kaunteyo mārkaṇḍeyaṃ mahāmunim
|
punaḥ papraccha sāmrājye bhaviṣyāṃ jagato gatim||1||

2yudhiṣṭhira uvāca|
āścaryabhūtaṃ bhavataḥ śrutaṃ no vadatāṃ vara
|
mune bhārgava yadvṛttaṃ yugādau prabhavātyayam||2||

3asmin kaliyuge tvasti punaḥ kautūhalaṃ mama
|
samākuleṣu dharmeṣu kiṃ tu śeṣaṃ bhaviṣyati||3||

4kiṃvīryā mānavāstatra kimāhāravihāriṇaḥ
|
kimāyuṣaḥ kiṃvasanā bhaviṣyanti yugakṣaye||4||

5kāñca kāṣṭhāṃ samāsādya punaḥ sampatsyate kṛtam
|
vistareṇa mune brūhi vicitrāṇīha bhāṣase||5||

6ityuktaḥ sa muniśreṣṭhaḥ punarevābhyabhāṣata
|
ramayan vṛṣṇiśārdūlaṃ pāṇḍavāṃśca mahānṛṣiḥ||6||

7mārkaṇḍeya uvāca|
śṛṇu rājan mayā dṛṣṭaṃ yat purā śrutameva ca
|
anubhūtaṃ ca rājendra devadevaprasādajam||7||

8bhaviṣyaṃ sarvalokasya vṛttāntaṃ bharatarṣabha
|
kaluṣaṃ kālamāsādya kathyamānaṃ nibodha me||8||

9kṛte catuṣpāt sakalo nirvyājopādhivarjitaḥ
|
vṛṣaḥ pratiṣṭhito dharmo manuṣye bharatarṣabha||9||

10adharmapādaviddhastu tribhiraṃśaiḥ pratiṣṭhitaḥ
|
tretāyāṃ dvāpare'rdhena vyāmiśro dharma ucyate||10||

11tribhiraṃśairadharmastu lokānākramya tiṣṭhati
|
tāmasaṃ yugamāsādya tadā bharatasattama||11||

12caturthāṃśena dharmastu manuṣyānupatiṣṭhati
|
āyurvīryamatho buddhirbalaṃ tejaśca pāṇḍava||12||

13manuṣyāṇāmanuyugaṃ hrasatīti nibodha me
|
rājāno brāhmaṇā vaiśyāḥ śūdrāścaiva yudhiṣṭhira||13||

14vyājairdharmaṃ cariṣyanti dharmavaitaṃsikā narāḥ
|
satyaṃ saṅkṣepsyate loke naraiḥ paṇḍitamānibhiḥ||14||

15satyahānyā tatasteṣāmāyuralpaṃ bhaviṣyati
|
āyuṣaḥ prakṣayādvidyāṃ na śakṣyantyupajīvitum||15||

16vidyāhīnānavijñānāllobho'pyabhibhaviṣyati
|
lobhakrodhaparā mūḍhāḥ kāmāsaktāśca mānavāḥ||16||

17vairabaddhā bhaviṣyanti parasparavadhaiṣiṇaḥ
|
brāhmaṇāḥ kṣatriyā vaiśyāḥ saṅkīryantaḥ parasparam||17||

18śūdratulyā bhaviṣyanti tapaḥsatyavivārjatāḥ
|
antyā madhyā bhaviṣyanti madhyāścāntyā na saṃśayaḥ||18||

19īdṛśo bhavitā loko yugānte paryupasthite
|
vastrāṇāṃ pravarā śāṇī dhānyānāṃ koradūṣakāḥ||19||

20bhāryāmitrāśca puruṣā bhaviṣyanti yugakṣaye
|
matsyāmiṣeṇa jīvanto duhantaścāpyajaiḍakam||20||

21goṣu naṣṭāsu puruṣā ye'pi nityaṃ dhṛtavratāḥ
|
te'pi lobhasamāyuktā bhaviṣyanti yugakṣaye||21||

22anyonyaṃ parimuṣṇanto hiṃsayantaśca mānavāḥ
|
ajapā nāstikāḥ stenā bhaviṣyanti yugakṣaye||22||

23sarittīreṣu kuddālairvāpayiṣyanti cauṣadhīḥ
|
tāścāpyalpaphalāsteṣāṃ bhaviṣyanti yugakṣaye||23||

24śrāddhe daive ca puruṣā ye'pi nityaṃ dhṛtavratāḥ
|
te'pi lobhasamāyuktā bhokṣyantīha parasparam||24||

25pitā putrasya bhoktā ca pituḥ putrastathaiva ca
|
atikrāntāni bhojyāni bhaviṣyanti yugakṣaye||25||

26na vratāni cariṣyanti brāhmaṇā vedanindakāḥ
|
na yakṣyanti na hoṣyanti hetuvādavimohitāḥ
|
nimneṣvīhāṃ kariṣyanti hetuvādavimohitāḥ||26||

27nimne kṛṣiṃ kariṣyanti yokṣyanti dhuri dhenukāḥ
|
ekahāyanavatsāṃśca yojayiṣyanti mānavāḥ||27||

28putraḥ pitṛvadhaṃ kṛtvā pitā putravadhaṃ tathā
|
nirudvego bṛhadvādī na nindāmupalapsyate||28||

29mlecchabhūtaṃ jagat sarvaṃ niṣkriyaṃ yajñavarjitam
|
bhaviṣyati nirānandamanutsavamatho tathā||29||

30prāyaśaḥ kṛpaṇānāṃ hi tathā bandhumatāmapi
|
vidhavānāṃ ca vittāni hariṣyantīha mānavāḥ||30||

31svalpavīryabalāḥ stabdhā lobhamohaparāyaṇāḥ
|
tatkathādānasantuṣṭā duṣṭānāmapi mānavāḥ||31||

32parigrahaṃ kariṣyanti māyācāraparigrahāḥ
|
samāhvayantaḥ kaunteya rājānaḥ pāpabuddhayaḥ||32||

33parasparavadhodyuktā mūrkhāḥ paṇḍitamāninaḥ
|
bhaviṣyanti yugasyānte kṣatriyā lokakaṇṭakāḥ||33||

34arakṣitāro lubdhāśca mānāhaṅkāradarpitāḥ
|
kevalaṃ daṇḍarucayo bhaviṣyanti yugakṣaye||34||

35ākramyākramya sādhūnāṃ dārāṃścāpi dhanāni ca
|
bhokṣyante niranukrośā rudatāmapi bhārata||35||

36na kanyāṃ yācate kaścinnāpi kanyā pradīyate
|
svayaṃgrāhā bhaviṣyanti yugānte samupasthite||36||

37rājānaścāpyasantuṣṭāḥ parārthān mūḍhacetasaḥ
|
sarvopāyairhariṣyanti yugānte paryupasthite||37||

38mlecchībhūtaṃ jagat sarvaṃ bhaviṣyati na saṃśayaḥ
|
hasto hastaṃ parimuṣedyugānte samupasthite||38||

39satyaṃ saṅkṣipyate loke naraiḥ paṇtitamānibhiḥ
|
sthavirā bālamatayo bālāḥ sthavirabuddhayaḥ||39||

40bhīrustathā śūramānī śūrā bhīruviṣādinaḥ
|
na viśvasanti cānyonyaṃ yugānte paryupasthite||40||

41ekāhāryaṃ yugaṃ sarvaṃ lobhamohavyavasthitam
|
adharmo varddhate tatra na tu dharmaḥ pravartate||41||

42brāhmaṇāḥ kṣatriyā vaiśyā na śipyanti janādhipa
|
ekavarṇastadā loko bhaviṣyati yugakṣaye||42||

43na kṣaṃsyati pitā putraṃ putraśca pitaraṃ tathā
|
bhāryāśca patiśuśruṣāṃ na kariṣyanti saṅkṣaye||43||

44ye yavānnā janapadā godhūmānnāstathaiva ca
|
tān deśān saṃśrayiṣyanti yugānte paryupasthite||44||

45svairācārāśca puruṣā yoṣitaśca viśāmpate
|
anyonyaṃ na sahiṣyanti yugānte paryupasthite||45||

46mlecchabhūtaṃ jagat sarvaṃ bhaviṣyati yudhiṣṭhira
|
na śrāddhaistarpayiṣyanti daivatānīha mānavāḥ||46||

47na kaścit kasyacicchrotā na kaścit kasyacidguruḥ
|
tamograstastadā loko bhaviṣyati janādhipa||47||

48paramāyuśca bhavitā tadā varṣāṇi ṣoḍaśa
|
tataḥ prāṇān vimokṣyanti yugānte samupasthite||48||

49pañcame vā'tha ṣaṣṭhe vā varṣe kanyā prasūyate
|
saptavarṣāṣṭavarṣāśca prajāsyanti narāstadā||49||

50patyau strī tu tadā rājan puruṣo vā striyaṃ prati
|
yugānte rājaśārdula na toṣamupayāsyati||50||

51alpadravyā vṛthāliṅgā hiṃsā ca prabhaviṣyati
|
na kaścit kasyaciddātā bhaviṣyati yugakṣaye||51||

52aṭṭaśūlā janapadāḥ śivaśūlāścatuṣpathāḥ
|
keśaśūlāḥ striyaścāpi bhaviṣyanti yugakṣaye||52||

53mlecchācārāḥ sarvabhakṣā dāruṇāḥ sarvakarmasu
|
bhāvinaḥ paścime kāle manuṣyā nātra saṃśayaḥ||53||

54krayavikrayakāle ca sarvaḥ sarvasya vañcanam
|
yugānte bharataśreṣṭha vittalobhāt kariṣyati||54||

55jñānāni cāpyavijñāya kariṣyanti kriyāstathā
|
ātmacchandena vartante yugānte samupasthite||55||

56svabhāvāt krūrakarmāṇaścānyonyamabhiśaṃsinaḥ
|
bhavitāro janāḥ sarve samprāpte tu yugakṣaye||56||

57ārāmāṃścaiva vṛkṣāṃśca nāśayiṣyanti nirvyathāḥ
|
bhavitā saṃśayo loke jīvitasya hi dehinām||57||

58tathā lobhābhibhūtāśca bhaviṣyanti narā nṛpa
|
brāhmaṇāṃśca haniṣyanti brāhmaṇasvopabhoginaḥ||58||

59hāhākṛtā dvijāścaiva bhayārtā vṛṣalārditāḥ
|
trātāramalabhanto vai bhramiṣyanti mahīmimām||59||

60jīvitāntakarāḥ krūrā raudrāḥ prāṇivihiṃsikāḥ
|
yadā bhaviṣyanti narāstadā saṅkṣepsyate yugam||60||

61āśrayiṣyanti ca nadīḥ parvatān viṣamāṇi ca
|
pradhāvamānā vitrastā dvijāḥ kurukulodvaha||61||

62dasyubhiḥ pīḍitā rājan kākā iva dvijottamāḥ
|
kurājabhiśca satataṃ karabhāraprapīḍitāḥ||62||

63dhairyaṃ tyaktvā mahīpāla dāruṇe yugasaṅkṣaye
|
vikarmāṇi kariṣyanti śūdrāṇāṃ paricārakāḥ||63||

64śūdrā dharmaṃ pravakṣyanti brāhmaṇāḥ paryupāsakāḥ
|
śrotāraśca bhaviṣyanti prāmāṇyena vyavasthitāḥ||64||

65viparītaśca loko'yaṃ bhaviṣyatyadharottaraḥ
|
eḍūkān pūjayiṣyanti varjayiṣyanti devatāḥ||65||

66śūdrāḥ paricariṣyanti na dvijān yugasaṅkṣaye
|
āśrameṣu maharṣīṇāṃ brāhmaṇāvasatheṣu ca||66||

67devasthāneṣu caityeṣu nāgānāmālayeṣu ca
|
eḍūkacihnā pṛthivī na devagṛhabhūṣitā||67||

68bhaviṣyati yuge kṣīṇe tadyugāntasya lakṣaṇam
|
yadā raudrā dharmahīnā māṃsādāḥ pānapāstathā||68||

69bhaviṣyanti narā nityaṃ tadā saṅkṣepsyate yugam
|
puṣpaṃ puṣpe yadā rājan phale vā phalamāśritam||69||

70prajāsyati mahārāja tadā saṅkṣepsyate yugam
|
akālavarṣī parjanyo bhaviṣyati gate yuge||70||

71akrameṇa manuṣyāṇāṃ bhaviṣyanti tadā kriyāḥ
|
virodhamatha yāsyanti vṛṣalā brāhmaṇaiḥ saha||71||

72mahī mlecchajanākīrṇā bhaviṣyati tato'cirāt
|
karabhārabhayādviprā bhajiṣyanti diśo daśa||72||

73nirviśeṣā janapadāstathā viṣṭikarārditāḥ
|
āśramānupalapsyanti phalamūlopajīvinaḥ||73||

74evaṃ paryākule loke maryādā na bhaviṣyati
|
na sthāsyantyupadeśe ca śiṣyā vipriyakāriṇaḥ||74||

75ācāryopanidhiścaiva bhartsyate tadanantaram
|
arthayuktyā pravatsyanti mitrasambandhibāndhavāḥ||75||

76abhāvaḥ sarvabhūtānāṃ yugānte sambhaviṣyati
|
diśaḥ prajvalitāḥ sarvā nakṣatrāṇyaprabhāṇi ca||76||

77jyotīṃṣi pratikūlāni vātāḥ paryākulāstathā
|
ulkāpātāśca bahavo mahābhayanidarśakāḥ||77||

78ṣaḍbhiranyaiśca sahito bhāskaraḥ pratapiṣyati
|
tumulāścāpi nirhrādā digdāhāścāpi sarvaśaḥ||78||

79kabandhāntarhito bhānurudayāstamane tadā
|
akālavarṣī bhagavān bhaviṣyati sahasradṛk||79||

80sasyāni ca na rokṣyanti yugānte paryupasthite
|
abhīkṣṇaṃ krūravādinyaḥ paruṣā ruditapriyāḥ||80||

81bhartṝṇāṃ vacane caiva na sthāsyanti tataḥ striyaḥ
|
putrāśca mātāpitarau haniṣyanti yugakṣaye||81||

82sūdayiṣyanti ca patīn striyaḥ putrānapāśritāḥ
|
aparvaṇi mahārāja sūryaṃ rāhurupaiṣyati||82||

83yugānte hutabhukcāpi sarvataḥ prajvaliṣyati
|
pānīyaṃ bhojanaṃ cāpi yācamānāstadā'dhvagāḥ||83||

84na lapsyante nivāsaṃ ca nirastāḥ pathi śerate
|
nirghātavāyasā nāgāḥ śakunāḥ samṛgadvijāḥ||84||

85rūkṣā vāco vimokṣyanti yugānte paryupasthite
|
mitrasambandhinaścāpi santyakṣyanti narāstadā||85||

86janaṃ parijanaṃ cāpi yugānte paryupasthite
|
atha deśān diśaścāpi pattanāni purāṇi ca||86||

87kramaśaḥ saṃśrayiṣyanti yugānte paryupasthite
|
hā tāta hā sutetyevaṃ tadā vācaḥ sudāruṇāḥ||87||

88vikrośamānaśvānyonyaṃ jano gāṃ paryaṭiṣyati
|
tatastumulasaṅghāte vartamāne yugakṣaye||88||

89dvijātipūrvako lokaḥ krameṇa prabhaviṣyati
|
tataḥ kālāntare'nyasmin punarlokavivṛddhaye||89||

90bhaviṣyati punardaivamanukūlaṃ yadṛcchayā
|
yadā sūryaśca candraśca tathā tiṣyabṛhaspatī||90||

91ekarāśau sameṣyanti prapatsyati tadā kṛtam
|
kālavarṣī ca parjanyo nakṣatrāṇi śubhāni ca||91||

92pradakṣiṇā grahāścāpi bhaviṣyantyanulomagāḥ
|
kṣemaṃ subhikṣamārogyaṃ bhaviṣyati nirāmayam||92||

93kalkī viṣṇuyaśā nāma dvijaḥ kālapracoditaḥ
|
utpatsyate mahāvīryo mahābuddhiparākramaḥ||93||

94sambhūtaḥ sambhalagrāme brāhmaṇāvasathe śubhe
|
manasā tasya sarvāṇi vāhanānyāyudhāni ca||94||

95upasthāsyanti yodhāśca śastrāṇi kavacāni ca
|
sa dharmavijayī rājā cakravartī bhaviṣyati||95||

96sa cemaṃ saṅkulaṃ lokaṃ prasāda‌mupaneṣyati
|
utthito brāhmaṇo dīptaḥ kṣayāntakṛdudāradhīḥ||96||

97saṅkṣepako hi sarvasya yugasya parivartakaḥ
|
sa sarvatra gatān kṣudrān brāhmaṇaiḥ parivāritaḥ
|
utsādayiṣyati tadā sarvamlecchagaṇān dvijaḥ||97||
