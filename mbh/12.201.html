Mahābhārata 12.201 (Śāntiparvan)

1yudhiṣṭhira uvāca|
kiṃ phalaṃ jñānayogasya vedānāṃ niyamasya ca
|
bhūtātmā ca kathaṃ jñeyastanme brūhi pitāmaha ||1||

2bhīṣma uvāca|
atrāpyudāharantīmamitihāsaṃ purātanam
|
manoḥ pajāpatervādaṃ maharṣeśca bṛhaspateḥ ||2||

3prajāpatiṃ śreṣṭhatamaṃ prajānāṃ devarṣisaṃghapravaro maharṣiḥ
|
bṛhaspatiḥ praśnamimaṃ purāṇaṃ papraccha śiṣyo'tha guruṃ praṇamya ||3||

4yatkāraṇaṃ yatra vidhiḥ pravṛtto jñāne phala yatpravadanti viprāḥ
|
yanmantraśabdairakṛtaprakāśaṃ taducyatāṃ me bhagavanyathāvat ||4||

5yaccārthaśāstrāgamamantravidbhiryajñairanekairatha gopradānaiḥ
|
phalaṃ mahadbhiryadupāsyate ca kiṃ tatkathaṃ vā bhavitā kva vā tat ||5||

6mahī mahījāḥ pavano'ntarikṣaṃ jalaukasaścaiva jalaṃ divaṃ ca
|
divaukasaścāpi yataḥ prasūtāstaducyatāṃ me bhagavan purāṇam ||6||

7jñānaṃ yataḥ prārthayate naro vai tatastadarthā bhavati pravṛttiḥ
|
na cāpyahaṃ veda paraṃ purāṇaṃ mithyāpravṛttiṃ ca kathaṃ nu kuryām ||7||

8ṛksāmasaṃghāṃśca yajūṃṣi cāpi cchandāṃsi nakṣatragatiṃ niruktam
|
adhītya ca vyākaraṇaṃ sakalpaṃ śikṣāṃ ca bhūtaprakṛtiṃ na vedmi ||8||

9sa me bhavān śaṃsatu sarvametatsāmānyaśabdaiśca viśeṣaṇaiśca
|
sa me bhavān śaṃsatu tāvadetajjñāne phalaṃ karmaṇi vā yadasti ||9||

10yathā ca dehāccyavate śarīrī punaḥ śarīraṃ ca yathābhyupaiti
|
manuruvāca |
yadyatpriyaṃ yasya sukhaṃ tadāhustadeva duḥkhaṃ pravadantyaniṣṭam ||10||

11iṣṭaṃ ca me syāditaracca na syādetatkṛte karmavidhiḥ pravṛttaḥ
|
iṣṭaṃ tvaniṣṭaṃ ca na māṃ bhajetetyetatkṛte jñānavidhiḥ pravṛttaḥ ||11||

12kāmātmakāśchandasi karmayogā ebhirvimuktaḥ paramaśnuvīta
|
nānāvidhe karmapathe sukhārthī naraḥ pravṛtto na paraṃ prayāti
|
bṛhaspatiruvāca |
iṣṭaṃ tvaniṣṭaṃ ca sukhāsukhe ca sāśīstvabacchandati karmabhiśca ||12||

13manuruvāca|
ebhirvimuktaḥ paramāviveśa etatkṛte karmavidhiḥ pravṛttaḥ
|
kāmātmakāṃśchandati karmayoga ebhirvimuktaḥ paramādadīta ||13||

14ātmādibhiḥ karmabhirindhyamāno dharme pravṛtto dyutimānsukhārthī
|
paraṃ hi tatkarmapathādapetaṃ nirāśiṣaṃ brahmaparaṃ hyavaiti ||14||

15prajāḥ sṛṣṭā manasā karmaṇā ca dvāvevaitau satpathau lokajuṣṭau
|
dṛṣṭaṃ karma śāśvataṃ cāntavacca manastyāgaḥ kāraṇaṃ nānyadasti ||15||

16svenātmanā cakṣuriva praṇetā niśātyaye tamasā saṃvṛtātmā
|
jñānaṃ tu vijñānaguṇena yuktaṃ karmāśubhaṃ paśyati varjanīyam ||16||

17sarpānkuśāgrāṇi tathodapānaṃ jñātvā manuṣyāḥ parivarjayanti
|
ajñānatastatra patanti kecijjñāne phalaṃ paśya yathā viśiṣṭam ||17||

18kṛtsnastu mantro vidhivatprayukto yathā yathoktāstviha dakṣiṇāśca
|
annapradānaṃ manasaḥ samādhiḥ pañcātmakaṃ karmaphalaṃ vadanti ||18||

19guṇātmakaṃ karma vadanti vedāstasmānmantro mantrapūrvaṃ hi karma
|
vidhirvidheyaṃ manasopapattiḥ phalasya bhoktā tu tathā śarīrī ||19||

20śabdāśca rūpāṇi rasāśca puṇyāḥ sparśāśca gandhāśca śubhāstathaiva
|
naro nasaṃsthānagataḥ prabhuḥ syādetatphalaṃ siddhyati karmaloke ||20||

21yadyaccharīreṇa karoti karma śarīrayuktaḥ samupāśnute tat
|
śarīramevāyatanaṃ sukhasya duḥkhasya cāpyāyatanaṃ śarīram ||21||

22vācā tu yatkarma karoti kiṃcidvācaiva sarvaṃ samupāśnute tat
|
manastu yatkarma karoti kiñcinmanaḥstha evāyamupāśnute tat ||22||

23yathā yathā karmaguṇaṃ phalārthī karotyayaṃ karmaphale niviṣṭaḥ
|
tathā tathā'yaṃ guṇasamprayuktaḥ śubhāśubhaṃ karmaphalaṃ bhunakti ||23||

24matsyo yathā srota ivābhipātī tathā kṛtaṃ pūrvamupaiti karma
|
śubhe tvasau tuṣyati duṣkṛte tu na tuṣyate vai paramaḥ śarīrī ||24||

25yato jagatsarvamidaṃ prasūtaṃ jñātvā''tmavanto vyatiyānti yattat
|
yanmantraśabdairakṛtaprakāśaṃ taducyamānaṃ śṛṇu me paraṃ yat ||25||

26rasairvimuktaṃ vividhaiśca gandhairaśabdamasparśamarūpavacca
|
agrāhyamavyaktamavarṇamekaṃ pañcaprakārānsasṛje prajānām ||26||

27na strī pumānnāpi napuṃsakaṃ ca na sanna cāsatsadasacca tanna
|
paśyanti yadbrahmavido manuṣyāstadakṣaraṃ na kṣaratīti viddhi ||27||
