Mahābhārata 7.150 (Droṇaparvan)

1sañjaya uvāca|
saindhave nihate rājan putrastava suyodhanaḥ
|
aśrupūrṇamukho dīno nirutsāho dviṣajjaye||1||

2durmanā niḥśvasan duṣṭo bhagnadaṃṣṭra ivoragaḥ
|
āgaskṛtsarvalokasya putraste'rtiṃ parāmagāt||2||

3dṛṣṭvā tatkadanaṃ ghoraṃ svabalasya kṛtaṃ mahat
|
jiṣṇunā bhīmasenena sātvatena ca saṃyuge||3||

4sa vivarṇaḥ kṛśo dīno bāṣpaviplutalocanaḥ
|
amanyatārjunasamo na yoddhā bhuvi vidyate||4||

5na droṇo na ca rādheyo nāśvatthāmā kṛpo na ca
|
kruddhasya samare sthātuṃ paryāptā iti māriṣa||5||

6nirjitya hi raṇe pārthaḥ sarvānmama mahārathān
|
avadhītsaindhavaṃ saṃkhye na ca kaścidavārayat||6||

7sarvathā hatamevedaṃ kauravāṇāṃ mahadbalam
|
na hyasya vidyate trātā sākṣādapi purandaraḥ||7||

8yamupāśritya saṃgrāme kṛtaḥ śastrasamudyamaḥ
|
sa karṇo nirjitaḥ saṃkhye hataścaiva jayadrathaḥ||8||

9yasya vīryaṃ samāśritya śamaṃ yācantamacyutam
|
tṛṇavattamahaṃ manye sa karṇo nirjito yudhi||9||

10evaṃ klāntamanā rājannupāyāddroṇamīkṣitum
|
āgaskṛtsarvalokasya putraste bharatarṣabha||10||

11tatastatsarvamācakhyau kurūṇāṃ vaiśasaṃ mahat
|
parānvijayataścāpi dhārtarāṣṭrānnimajjataḥ||11||

12duryodhana uvāca|
paśya mūrdhābhiṣiktānāmācārya kadanaṃ mahat
|
kṛtvā pramukhataḥ śūraṃ bhīṣmaṃ mama pitāmaham||12||

13taṃ nihatya pralubdho'yaṃ śikhaṇḍī pūrṇamānasaḥ
|
pāñcālyaiḥ sahitaḥ sarvaiḥ senāgramabhivartate||13||

14aparaścāpi durdharṣaḥ śiṣyaste savyasācinā
|
akṣauhiṇīḥ sapta hatvā hato rājā jayadrathaḥ||14||

15asmadvijayakāmānāṃ suhṛdāmupakāriṇām
|
gantāsmi kathamānṛṇyaṃ gatānāṃ yamasādanam||15||

16ye madarthaṃ parīpsante vasudhāṃ vasudhādhipāḥ
|
te hitvā vasudhaiśvaryaṃ vasudhāmadhiśerate||16||

17so'haṃ kāpuruṣaḥ kṛtvā mitrāṇāṃ kṣayamīdṛśam
|
aśvamedhasahasreṇa pāvituṃ na samutsahe||17||

18mama lubdhasya pāpasya tathā dharmāpacāyinaḥ
|
vyāyāmena jigīṣantaḥ prāptā vaivasvatakṣayam||18||

19kathaṃ patitavṛttasya pṛthivī suhṛdāṃ druhaḥ
|
vivaraṃ nāśakaddvātuṃ mama pārthivasaṃsadi||19||

20yo'haṃ rudhirasiktāṅgaṃ rājñāṃ madhye pitāmaham
|
śayānaṃ nāśakaṃ trātuṃ bhīṣmamāyodhane hatam||20||

21taṃ māmanāryapuruṣaṃ mitradruhamadhārmikam
|
kiṃ vakṣyati hi durdharṣaḥ sametya paralokajit||21||

22jalasandhaṃ maheṣvāsaṃ paśya sātyakinā hatam
|
madarthamudyataṃ śūraṃ prāṇāṃstyaktvā mahāratham||22||

23kāmbojaṃ nihataṃ dṛṣṭvā tathā'lambuṣameva ca
|
anyānbahūṃśca suhṛdo jīvitārtho'dya ko mama||23||

24vyāyacchanto hatāḥ śūrā madarthe ye'parāṅmukhāḥ
|
yatamānāḥ paraṃ śaktyā vijetumahitānmama||24||

25teṣāṃ gatvāhamānṛṇyamadya śaktyā parantapa
|
tarpayiṣyāmi tāneva jalena yamunāmanu||25||

26satyaṃ te pratijānāmi sarvaśastrabhṛtāṃ vara
|
iṣṭāpūrtena ca śape vīryeṇa ca sutairapi||26||

27nihatya tān raṇe sarvān paścālān pāṇḍavaiḥ saha
|
śāntiṃ labdhā'smi teṣāṃ vā raṇe gantā salokatām||27||

28so'haṃ tatra gamiṣyāmi yatra te puruṣarṣabhāḥ
|
hatā madarthe saṃgrāme yuddhyamānāḥ kirīṭinā||28||

29na hīdānīṃ sahāyā me parīpsantyanupaskṛtāḥ
|
śreyo hi pāṇḍūnmanyante na tathā'smānmahābhuja||29||

30svayaṃ hi mṛtyurvihitaḥ satyasandhena saṃyuge
|
bhavānupekṣāṃ kurute śiṣyatvādarjunasya hi||30||

31ato vinihatāḥ sarve ye'smajjayacikīrṣavaḥ
|
karṇameva tu paśyāmi saṃpratyasmajjayaiṣiṇam||31||

32yo hi mitramavijñāya yāthātathyena mandadhīḥ
|
mitrārthe yojayatyenaṃ tasya so'rtho'vasīdati||32||

33tādṛgrūpaṃ kṛtamidaṃ mama kāryaṃ suhṛttamaiḥ
|
mohāllabdhasya pāpasya jihmasya dhanamīhataḥ||33||

34hato jayadrathaścaiva saumadattiśca vīryavān
|
abhīṣāhāḥ śūrasenāḥ śibayo'tha vasātayaḥ||34||

35so'hamadya gamiṣyāmi yatra te puruṣarṣabhāḥ
|
hatā madarthe saṃgrāme yuddhyamānāḥ kirīṭinā||35||

36na hi me jīvitenārthastānṛte puruṣarṣabhān
|
ācāryaḥ pāṇḍuputrāṇāmanujānātu no bhavān||36||
