Mahābhārata 1.2 (Ādiparvan)

1ṛṣaya ūcuḥ|
samantapañcakamiti yaduktaṃ sūtanandana
|
etatsarvaṃ yathātattvaṃ śrotumicchāmahe vayam||1||

2sautiruvāca|
śṛṇudhvaṃ mama bho viprā bruvataśca kathāḥ śubhāḥ
|
samantapañcakākhyaṃ ca śrotumarhatha sattamāḥ||2||

3tretādvāparayoḥ sandhau rāmaḥ śastrabhṛtāṃ varaḥ
|
asakṛtpārthivaṃ kṣatraṃ jaghānāmarṣacoditaḥ||3||

4sa sarvaṃ kṣatramutsādya svavīryeṇānaladyutiḥ
|
samantapañcake pañca cakāra raudhiranhvadān||4||

5sa teṣu rudhirāmbhaḥsu hradeṣu krodhamūrcchitaḥ
|
pitṝnsaṃtarpayāmāsa rudhireṇeti naḥ śrutam||5||

6atharcīkādayo'bhyetya pitaro rāmamabruvan
|
rāma rāma mahābhāga prītāḥ sma tava bhārgava||6||

7anayā pitṛbhaktyā ca vikrameṇa tava prabho
|
varaṃ vṛṇīṣva bhadraṃ te yamicchasi mahādyute||7||

8rāma uvāca|
yadi me pitaraḥ prītā yadyanugrāhyatāmayi
|
yacca roṣābhibhūtena kṣatramutsāditaṃ mayā||8||

9ataśca pāpānmucye'hameṣa me prārthito varaḥ
|
hradāśca tīrthabhūtā me bhaveyurbhuvi viśrutāḥ||9||

10evaṃ bhaviṣyatītyevaṃ pitarastamathābruvan
|
taṃ kṣamasveti niṣiṣidhustataḥ sa virarāma ha||10||

11teṣāṃ samīpe yo deśo hradānāṃ rudhirāmbhasām
|
samantapañcakamiti puṇyaṃ tatparikīrtitam||11||

12yena liṅgena yo deśo yuktaḥ samupalakṣyate
|
tenaiva nāmnā taṃ deśaṃ vācyamāhurmanīṣiṇaḥ||12||

13antare caiva saṃprāpte kalidvāparayorabhūt
|
samantapañcake yuddhaṃ kurupāṇḍavasenayoḥ||13||

14tasminparamadharmiṣṭhe deśe bhūdoṣavarjite
|
aṣṭādaśa samājagmurakṣauhiṇyo yuyutsayā||14||

15sametya taṃ dvijāstāśca tatraiva nidhaṃ gatāḥ
|
etannāmābhinirvṛttaṃ tasya deśasya vai dvijāḥ||15||

16puṇyaśca ramaṇīyaśca sa deśo vaḥ prakīrtitaḥ
|
tadetatkathitaṃ sarvaṃ mayā brāhmaṇasattamāḥ|| yathā deśaḥ sa vikhyātastriṣu lokeṣu suvratāḥ||16||

17ṛṣaya ūcuḥ|
akṣauhiṇya iti proktaṃ yattvayā sūtanandana
|
etadicchāmahe śrotuṃ sarvameva yathātatham||17||

18akṣauhiṇyāḥ parīmāṇaṃ narāśvarathadantinām
|
yathāvaccaiva no brūhi sarvaṃ hi viditaṃ tava||18||

19sautiruvāca|
eko ratho gajaścaiko narāḥ pañca padātayaḥ
|
trayaśca turagāstajjñaiḥ pattirityabhidhīyate||19||

20pattiṃ tu triguṇāmetāmāhuḥ senāmukhaṃ budhāḥ
|
trīṇi senāmukhānyeko gulma ityabhidhīyate||20||

21trayo gulmā gaṇo nāma vāhinī tu gaṇāstrayaḥ
|
smṛtāstisrastu vāhinyaḥ pṛtaneti vicakṣaṇaiḥ||21||

22camūstu pṛtanāstisrastisraścamvastvanīkinī
|
anīkinīṃ daśaguṇāṃ prāhurakṣauhiṇīṃ budhāḥ||22||

23akṣauhiṇyāḥ prasaṃkhyātā rathānāṃ dvijasattamāḥ
|
saṃkhyāgaṇitatattvajñaiḥ sahasrāṇyekaviṃśatiḥ||23||

24śatānyuparicaivāṣṭau tathā bhūyaśca saptatiḥ
|
gajānāṃ ca parīmāṇametadeva vinirdiśet||24||

25jñeyaṃ śatasahasraṃ tu sahasrāṇi navaiva tu
|
narāṇāmapi pañcāśacchatāni trīṇi cānaghāḥ||25||

26pañca ṣaṣṭisahasrāṇi tathāśvānāṃ śatāni ca
|
daśottarāṇi ṣaṭ prāhuryathāvadiha saṃkhyayā||26||

27etāmakṣauhiṇīṃ prāhuḥ saṃkhyātattvadito janāḥ
|
yāṃ vaḥ kathitavānasmi vistareṇa tapodhanāḥ||27||

28etayā saṃkhyayā hyāsankurupāṇḍavasenayoḥ
|
akṣauhiṇyo dvijaśreṣṭhāḥ piṇḍitāṣṭādaśaiva tu||28||

29sametāstatra vai deśe tatraiva nidhaṃ gatāḥ
|
kauravānkāraṇaṃ kṛtvā kālenādbhutakarmaṇā||29||

30ahāni yuyudhe bhīṣmo daśaiva paramāstravit
|
ahāni pañca droṇastu rarakṣa kuruvāhinīm||30||

31ahanī yuyudhe dve tu karṇaḥ parabalārdanaḥ
|
śalyo'rdhadivasaṃ caiva gadāyuddhamataḥ param||31||

32tasyaiva divasasyāntaṃ drauṇihārdikyagautamāḥ
|
prasuptaṃ niśi viśvastaṃ jaghnuryaudhiṣṭhiraṃ balam||32||

33yattu śaunaka satre te bhāratākhyānamuttamam
|
janamejayasya tatsatre vyāsaśiṣyeṇa dhīmatā||33||

34kathitaṃ vistarārthaṃ ca yaśo vīryaṃ mahīkṣitām
|
pauṣyaṃ tatra ca paulomamāstīkaṃ cāditaḥ smṛtam||34||

35vicitrārthapadākhyānamanekasamayānvitam
|
pratipannaṃ naraiḥ prājñairvairāgyamiva mokṣibhiḥ||35||

36ātmeva veditavyeṣu priyeṣviva hi jīvitam
|
itihāsaḥ pradhānārthaḥ śreṣṭhaḥ sarvāgameṣvayam||36||

37anāśrityedamākhyānaṃ kathā bhuvi na vidyate
|
āhāramanapāśritya śarīrasyeva dhāraṇam||37||

38tadetadbhārataṃ nāma kavibhistūpajīvyate
|
udayatepsubhirbhṛtyairabhijāta iveśvaraḥ||38||

39itihāsottame yasminnarpitā buddhiruttamā
|
svaravyañjanayoḥ kṛtsnā lokavedāśrayeva vāk||39||

40tasya prajñābhipannasya vicitrapadaparvaṇaḥ
|
sūkṣmārthanyāyayuktasya vedārthairbhūṣitasya ca||40||

41bhāratasyetihāsasya śrūyatāṃ parvasaṃgrahaḥ
|
parvānukramaṇī pūrvaṃ dvitīyaḥ parvasaṃgrahaḥ||41||

42pauṣyaṃ paulomamāstīkamādiraṃśāvatāraṇam
|
tataḥ saṃbhavaparvoktamadbhutaṃ romaharṣaṇam||42||

43dāho jatugṛhasyātra haiḍimbaṃ parva cocyate
|
tato bakavadhaḥ parva parva caitrarathaṃ tataḥ||43||

44tataḥ svayaṃvaro devyāḥ pāñcālyāḥ parva cocyate
|
kṣātradharmeṇa nirjitya tato vaivāhikaṃ smṛtam||44||

45vidurāgamanaṃ parva rājyalābhastathaiva ca
|
arjunasya vane vāsaḥ subhadrāharaṇaṃ tataḥ||45||

46subhadrāharaṇādūrdhvaṃ jñeyā haraṇahārikā
|
tataḥ khāṇḍavadāhākhyaṃ tatraiva mayadarśanam||46||

47sabhāparva tataḥ proktaṃ mantraparva tataḥ param
|
jarāsandhavadhaḥ parva parva digvijayaṃ tathā||47||

48parva digvijayādūrdhvaṃ rājasūyikamucyate
|
tataścārghābhiharaṇaṃ śiśupālavadhastataḥ||48||

49dyūtaparva tataḥ proktamanudyūtamaḥ param
|
tata āraṇyakaṃ parva kirmīravadha ucyate||49||

50arjunasyābhigamanaṃ parva jñeyamataḥ param
|
īśvarārjunayoryuddhaṃ parva kairātasaṃjñitam||50||

51indralokābhigamanaṃ parva jñeyamataḥ param
|
nalopākhyānamapi ca dhārmikaṃ karuṇodayam||51||

52tīrthayātrā tataḥ parva kururājasya dhīmataḥ
|
jaṭāsuravadhaḥ parva yakṣayuddhamataḥ param||52||

53nivātakavacairyuddhaṃ parva cājagaraṃ tataḥ
|
mārkaṇḍeyasamāsyā ca parvānantaramucyate||53||

54saṃvādaśca tataḥ parva draupadīsatyabhāmayoḥ
|
ghoṣayātrā tataḥ parva mṛgasvapnodbhavaṃ tataḥ||54||

55vrīhidrauṇikamākhyānamaindradyumnaṃ tathaiva ca
|
draupadīharaṇaṃ parva jayadrathavimokṣaṇam||55||

56pativratāyā māhātmyaṃ sāvitryāścaivamadbhutam
|
rāmopākhyānamatraiva parva jñeyamataḥ param||56||

57kuṇḍalāharaṇaṃ parva tataḥ paramihocyate
|
āraṇeyaṃ tataḥ parva vairāṭaṃ tadanantaram
|
pāṇḍavānāṃ praveśaśca samayasya ca pālanam||57||

58kīcakānāṃ vadhaḥ parva parva grograhaṇaṃ tataḥ
|
abhimanyośca vairāṭyāḥ parva vaivāhikaṃ smṛtam||58||

59udyogaparva vijñeyamata ūrdhvaṃ mahādbhutam
|
tataḥ saṃjayayānākhyaṃ parva jñeyamataḥ param||59||

60prajāgaraṃ tathā parva dhṛtarāṣṭrasya cintayā
|
parva sānatsujātaṃ vai guhyamadhyātmadarśanam||60||

61yānasandhistataḥ parva bhagavadyānameva ca
|
mātalīyamupākhyānaṃ caritaṃ gālavasya ca||61||

62sāvitraṃ vāmadevyaṃ ca vainyopākhyānameva ca
|
jāmadagnyamupākhyānaṃ parva ṣoḍaśarājakam||62||

63sabhāpraveśaḥ kṛṣṇasya vidulāputraśāsanam
|
udyogaḥ sainyaniryāṇaṃ viśvopākhyānameva ca||63||

64jñeyaṃ vivādaparvātra karṇasyāpi mahātmanaḥ
|
niryāṇaṃ ca tataḥ parva kurupāṇḍavasenayoḥ||64||

65rathātirathasaṃkhyā ca parvoktaṃ tadanantaram
|
ulūkadūtāgamanaṃ parvāmarṣavivardhanam||65||

66ambopākhyānamatraiva parva jñeyamataḥ param
|
bhīṣmābhiṣecanaṃ parva tataścādbhutamucyate||66||

67jambūkhaṇḍavinirmāṇaṃ parvoktaṃ tadanantaram
|
bhūmiparva tataḥ proktaṃ dvīpavistārakīrtanam||67||

68parvoktaṃ bhagavadgītā parva bhīṣmavadhastataḥ
|
droṇābhiṣecanaṃ parva saṃśaptakavadhastataḥ||68||

69abhimanyuvadhaḥ parva pratijñāparva cocyate
|
jayadrathavadhaḥ parva ghaṭotkacavadhastataḥ||69||

70tato droṇavadhaḥ parva vijñeyaṃ lomaharṣaṇam
|
mokṣo nārāyaṇāstrasya parvānantaramucyate||70||

71karṇaparva tato jñeyaṃ śalyaparva tataḥ param
|
hradapraveśanaṃ parva gadāyuddhamataḥ param||71||

72sārasvataṃ tataḥ parva tīrthavaṃśānukīrtanam
|
ata ūrdhvaṃ subībhatsaṃ parva sauptikamucyate||72||

73aiṣīkaṃ parva coddiṣṭamata ūrdhvaṃ sudāruṇam
|
jalapradānikaṃ parva strīvilāpastataḥ param||73||

74śrāddhaparva tato jñeyaṃ kurūṇāmaurdhvadehikam
|
cārvākanigrahaḥ parva rakṣaso brahmarūpiṇaḥ||74||

75ābhiṣecanikaṃ parva dharmarājasarya dhīmataḥ
|
pravibhāgo gṛhāṇāṃ ca parvoktaṃ tadanantaram||75||

76śāntiparva tato yatra rājadharmānuśāsanam
|
āpaddharmaśca parvoktaṃ mokṣadharmastataḥ param||76||

77śukapraśnābhigamanaṃ brahmapraśnānuśāsanam
|
prādurbhāvaśca durvāsaḥsaṃvādaścaiva māyayā||77||

78tataḥ parva parijñeyamānuśāsanikaṃ param
|
svargārohaṇikaṃ caiva tato bhīṣmasya dhīmataḥ||78||

79tato'śvamedhikaṃ parva sarvapāpapraṇāśanam
|
anugītā tataḥ parva jñeyamadhyātmavācakam||79||

80parva cāśramavāsākhyaṃ putradarśanameva ca
|
nāradāgamanaṃ parva tataḥ paramihocyate||80||

81mausalaṃ parva coddiṣṭaṃ tato ghoraṃ sudāruṇam
|
mahāprasthānikaṃ parva svargārohaṇikaṃ tataḥ||81||

82harivaṃśastataḥ parva purāṇaṃ khilasaṃjñitam
|
viṣṇuparva śiśoścaryā viṣṇoḥ kaṃsavadhastathā||82||

83bhaviṣyaṃ parva cāpyuktaṃ khileṣvevādbhutaṃ mahat
|
etatparvaśataṃ pūrṇaṃ vyāsenoktaṃ mahātmanā||83||

84yathāvatsūtaputreṇa laumaharṣaṇinā tataḥ
|
uktāni naimiśāraṇye parvāṇyaṣṭādaśaiva tu||84||

85samāso bhāratasyāyamatroktaḥ parvasaṃgrahaḥ
|
pauṣyaṃ paulomamāstīkamādiraṃśāvatāraṇam||85||

86saṃbhavo jatuveśmākhyaṃ hiḍimbabakayorvadhaḥ
|
tathā caitrarathaṃ devyāḥ pāñcālyāśca svayaṃvaraḥ||86||

87kṣātradharmeṇa nirjitya tato vaivāhikaṃ smṛtam
|
vidurāgamanaṃ caiva rājyalābhastathaiva ca||87||

88vanavāso'rjunasyāpi subhadrāharaṇaṃ tataḥ
|
haraṇāharaṇaṃ caiva dahanaṃ khāṇḍavasya ca||88||

89mayasya darśanaṃ caiva ādiparvaṇi kathyate
|
pauṣye parvaṇi māhātmyamuttaṅkasyopavarṇitam||89||

90paulome bhṛguvaṃśasya vistāraḥ parikīrtitaḥ
|
āstīke sarvanāgānāṃ garuḍasya ca saṃbhavaḥ||90||

91kṣīrodamathaṃ caiva janmoccaiḥśravasastathā
|
yajataḥ sarpasatreṇa rājñaḥ pārikṣitasya ca||91||

92katheyamabhinirvṛttā bhāratānāṃ mahātmanām
|
vividhāḥ saṃbhavā rājñāmuktāḥ saṃbhavaparvaṇi||92||

93anyeṣāṃ caiva śūrāṇāmṛṣerdvaipāyanasya ca
|
aṃśāvataraṇaṃ cātra devānāṃ parikīrtitam||93||

94daityānāṃ dānavānāṃ ca yakṣāṇāṃ ca mahaujasām
|
nāgānāmatha sarpāṇāṃ gandharvāṇāṃ patattriṇām||94||

95anyeṣāṃ caiva bhūtānāṃ vividhānāṃ samudbhavaḥ
|
maharṣerāśramapade kaṇvasya ca tapasvinaḥ||95||

96śakuntalāyāṃ duṣyantādbharataścāpi jajñivān
|
yasya lokeṣu nāmnedaṃ prathitaṃ bhārataṃ kulam||96||

97vasūnāṃ punarutpattirbhāgīrathyāṃ mahātmanām
|
śantanorveśmani punasteṣāṃ cārohaṇaṃ divi||97||

98tejoṃ'śānāṃ ca saṃpāto bhīṣmasyāpyatra saṃbhavaḥ
|
rājyānnivartanaṃ tasya brahmacaryavrate sthitiḥ||98||

99pratijñāpālanaṃ caiva rakṣā citrāṅgadasya ca
|
hate citrāṅgade caiva rakṣā bhrāturyavīyasaḥ||99||

100vicitravīryasya tathā rājye saṃpratipādanam
|
dharmasya nṛṣu saṃbhūtiraṇīmāṇḍavyaśāpajā||100||

101kṛṣṇadvaipāyanāccaiva prasūtirvaradānajā
|
dhṛtarāṣṭrasya pāṇḍośca pāṇḍavānāṃ ca saṃbhavaḥ||101||

102vāraṇāvatayātrāyāṃ mantro duryodhanasya ca
|
kūṭasya dhārtarāṣṭreṇa preṣaṇaṃ pāṇḍavānprati||102||

103hitopadeśaśca pathi dharmarājasya dhīmataḥ
|
vidureṇa kṛto yatra hitārthaṃ mlecchabhāṣayā||103||

104vidurasya ca vākyena suruṅgopakramakriyā
|
niṣādyāḥ pañcaputrāyāḥ suptāyā jatu veśmani||104||

105purocanasya cātraiva dahanaṃ saṃprakīrtitam
|
pāṇḍavānāṃ vane ghore hiḍimbāyāśca darśanam||105||

106tatraiva ca hiḍimbasya vadho bhīmānmahābalāt
|
ghaṭotkacasya cotpattiṃratraiva parikīrtitā||106||

107maharṣerdarśanaṃ caiva vyāsasyāmitatejasaḥ
|
tadājñayaikacakrāyāṃ brāhmaṇasya niveśane||107||

108ajñātacaryayā vāso yatra teṣāṃ prakīrtitaḥ
|
bakasya nidhanaṃ caiva nāgarāṇāṃ ca vismayaḥ||108||

109saṃbhavaścaiva kṛṣṇāyā dhṛṣṭadyumnasya caiva ha
|
brāhmaṇātsamupaśrutya vyāsavākyapracoditāḥ||109||

110draupadīṃ prārthayantaste svayaṃvaradidṛkṣayā
|
pañcālānabhito jagmuryatra kautūhalānvitāḥ||110||

111aṅgāraparṇaṃ nirjitya gaṅgākūle'rjunastadā
|
sakhyaṃ kṛtvā tatastena tasmādeva ca śuśruve||111||

112tāpatyamatha vāsiṣṭhamaurvaṃ cākhyānamuttamam
|
bhrātṛbhiḥ sahitaḥ sarvaiḥ pañcālānabhito yayau||112||

113pāñcālanagare cāpi lakṣyaṃ bhittvā dhanañjayaḥ
|
draupadīṃ labdhavānatra madhye sarvamahīkṣitām||113||

114bhīmasenārjunau yatra saṃrabdhānpṛthivīpatīn
|
śalyakarṇau ca tarasā jitavantau mahāmṛdhe||114||

115dṛṣṭvā tayośca tadvīryamaprameyamamānuṣam
|
śaṅkamānau pāṇḍavāṃstān rāmakṛṣṇau mahāmatī||115||

116jagmatustaiḥ samāgantuṃ śālāṃ bhārgavaveśmani
|
pañcānāmekapatnītve vimarśo drupadasya ca||116||

117pañcendrāṇāmupākhyānamatraivādbhutamucyate
|
draupadyā devavihīto vivāhaścāpyamānuṣaḥ||117||

118kṣattuśca dhārtarāṣṭreṇa preṣaṇaṃ pāṇḍavānprati
|
vidurasya ca saṃprāptirdarśanaṃ keśavasya ca||118||

119khāṇḍavaprasthavāsaśca tathā rājyārdhasarjanam
|
nāradasyājñayā caiva draupadyāḥ samayakriyā||119||

120sundopasundayostadvadākhyānaṃ parikīrtitam
|
anantaraṃ ca draupadyā sahāsīnaṃ yudhiṣṭhiram||120||

121anu praviśya viprārthe phālguno gṛhya cāyudham
|
mokṣayitvā gṛhaṃ gatvā viprārthaṃ kṛtaniścayaḥ||121||

122samayaṃ pālayanvīro vanaṃ yatra jagāma ha
|
pārthasya vanavāse ca ulūpyā pathi saṅgamaḥ||122||

123puṇyatīrthānusaṃyānaṃ babhruvāhanajanma ca
|
tatraiva mokṣayāmāsa pañca so'psarasaḥ śubhāḥ||123||

124śāpādgrāhatvamāpannā brāhmaṇasya tapasvinaḥ
|
prabhāsatīrthe pārthena kṛṣṇasya ca samāgamaḥ||124||

125dvārakāyāṃ subhadrā ca kāmayānena bhāminī
|
vāsudevasyānumate prāptā caiva kirīṭinā||125||

126gṛhītvā haraṇaṃ prāpte kṛṣṇe devakinandane
|
abhimanyoḥ subhadrāyāṃ janma cottamatejasaḥ||126||

127draupadyāstanayānāṃ ca saṃbhavo'nuprakīrtitaḥ
|
vihārārthaṃ ca gatayoḥ kṛṣṇayoryamunāmanu||127||

128saṃprāptiścakradhanuṣoḥ khāṇḍavasya ca dāhanam
|
mayasya mokṣo jvalanādbhujaṅgasya ca mokṣaṇam||128||
