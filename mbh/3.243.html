Mahābhārata 3.243 (Āraṇyakaparvan)

1yudhiṣṭhira uvāca|
asmānabhigatāṃstāta bhayārtāñcharaṇaiṣiṇaḥ
|
kauravān viṣamaprāptān kathaṃ brūyāstvamīdṛśam||1||

2bhavanti bhedā jñātīnāṃ kalahāśca vṛkodara
|
prasaktāni ca vairāṇi kuladharmo na naśyati||2||

3yadā tu kaścijjñātīnāṃ bāhyaḥ prārthayate kulam
|
na marṣayanti tat santo bāhyenābhipradharṣaṇam||3||

4jānātyeṣa hi durbuddhirasmāniha ciroṣitān
|
sa evaṃ paribhūyāsmānakārṣīdidamapriyam||4||

5duryodhanasya grahaṇādgandharveṇa balāt prabho
|
strīṇāṃ vāhyābhimarśācca hataṃ bhavati naḥ kulam||5||

6śaraṇaṃ ca prapannānāṃ trāṇārthaṃ ca kulasya ca
|
uttiṣṭhadhvaṃ naravyāghrāḥ sajjībhavata mā ciram||6||

7arjunaśca yamau caiva tvaṃ ca vīrāparājitaḥ
|
mokṣayadhvaṃ naravyāghrā hriyamāṇaṃ suyodhanam||7||

8ete rathā naravyāghrāḥ sarvaśastrasamanvitāḥ
|
dhṛtarāṣṭrasya putrāṇāṃ vimalāḥ kāñcanadhvajāḥ||8||

9sasvanānadhirohadhvaṃ nityasajjānimān rathān
|
indrasenādibhiḥ sūtaiḥ kṛtaśastrairadhiṣṭhitān||9||

10etānāsthāya vai yattā gandharvān yoddhumāhave
|
suyodhanasya mokṣāya prayatadhvamatandritāḥ||10||

11ya eva kaścidrājanyaḥ śaraṇārthamihāgatam
|
paraṃ śaktyā'bhirakṣeta kimpunastvaṃ vṛkodara||11||

12ka ihāryo bhavet trāṇamabhidhāveti coditaḥ
|
prāñjaliṃ śaraṇāpannaṃ dṛṣṭvā śatrumapi dhruvam||12||

13varapradānaṃ rājyaṃ ca putrajanma ca pāṇḍavāḥ
|
śatrośca mokṣaṇaṃ kleśāntrīṇi caikaṃ ca tatsamam||13||

14kiṃ cāpyadhikametasmādyadāpannaḥ suyodhanaḥ
|
tvadbāhubalamāśritya jīvitaṃ parimārgate||14||

15svayameva pradhāveyaṃ yadi na syādvṛkodara
|
vitato me kraturvīra na hi me'tra vicāraṇā||15||

16sāmnaiva tu yathā bhīma mokṣayethāḥ suyodhanam
|
tathā sarvairupāyaistvaṃ yatethāḥ kurunandana||16||

17na sāmnā pratipadyeta yadi gandharvarāḍasau
|
parākrameṇa mṛdunā mokṣayethāḥ suyodhanam||17||

18athāsau mṛduyuddhena na muñcedbhīma kauravān
|
sarvopāyairvimocyāste nigṛhya paripanthinaḥ||18||

19etāvaddhi mayā śakyaṃ sandeṣṭuṃ vai vṛkodara
|
vaitāne karmaṇi tate vartamāne ca bhārata||19||

20vaiśampāyana uvāca|
ajātaśatrorvacanaṃ tacchrutvā tu dhanañjayaḥ
|
pratijajñe gurorvākyaṃ kauravāṇāṃ vimokṣaṇam||20||

21arjuna uvāca|
yadi sāmnā na mokṣyanti gandharvā dhṛtarāṣṭrajān
|
adya gandharvarājasya bhūmiḥ pāsyati śorṇitam||21||

22arjunasya tu tāṃ śrutvā pratijñāṃ satyavādinaḥ
|
kauravāṇāṃ tadā rājan punaḥ pratyāgataṃ manaḥ||22||
