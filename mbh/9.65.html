Mahābhārata 9.65 (Śalyaparvan)

1sañjaya uvāca|
vārtikāṇāṃ sakāśāttu śrutvā duryodhanaṃ hatam
|
hataśiṣṭāstato rājan kauravāṇāṃ mahārathāḥ||1||

2vinirbhinnāḥ śitairbāṇairgadātomaraśaktibhiḥ
|
aśvatthāmā kṛpaścaiva kṛtavarmā ca sātvataḥ||2||

3tvaritā javanairaśvairāyodhanamupāgaman
|
tatrāpaśyanmahātmānaṃ dhārtarāṣṭraṃ nipātitam||3||

4prabhagnaṃ vāyuvegena mahāśālaṃ yathā vane
|
bhūmau viceṣṭamānaṃ taṃ rudhireṇa samukṣitam||4||

5mahāgajamivāraṇye vyādhena vinipātitam
|
vivartamānaṃ bahuśo rudhiraughapariplutam||5||

6yadṛcchayā nipatitaṃ cakramādityagocaram
|
mahāvātasamutthena saṃśuṣkamiva sāgaram||6||

7pūrṇacandramiva vyomni tuṣārāvṛtamaṇḍalam
|
reṇudhvastaṃ dīrghabhujaṃ mātaṅgamiva vikrame||7||

8vṛtaṃ bhūtagaṇairghoraiḥ kravyādaiśca samantataḥ
|
yathā dhanaṃ lipsamānairbhūtyairnṛpatisattamam||8||

9bhṛkuṭīkṛtavaktrāntaṃ krodhāduvṛttacakṣuṣam
|
sāmarṣaṃ taṃ naravyāghraṃ vyāghraṃ nipatitaṃ yathā||9||

10te taṃ dṛṣṭvā maheṣvāsaṃ bhūtale patitaṃ nṛpam
|
mohamabhyāgamansarve kṛpaprabhṛtayo rathāḥ||10||

11avatīrya rathebhyaśca prādravan rājasannidhau
|
duryodhanaṃ ca samprekṣya sarve bhīmāvupāviśan||11||

12tato drauṇirmahārāja bāṣpapūrṇekṣaṇaḥ śvasan
|
uvāca bharataśreṣṭhaṃ sarvalokeśvareśvaram||12||

13na nūnaṃ vidyate satyaṃ mānuṣe kiñcideva hi
|
yatra tvaṃ puruṣavyāghra śeṣe pāṃsuṣu rūṣitaḥ||13||

14bhūtvā hi nṛpatiḥ pūrvaṃ samājñāpya ca medinīm
|
kathameko'dya rājendra tiṣṭhase nirjane vane||14||

15duḥśāsanaṃ na paśyāmi nāpi karṇaṃ mahāratham
|
nāpi tān suhṛdaḥ sarvān kimidaṃ bharatarṣabha||15||

16duḥkhaṃ nūnaṃ kṛtāntasya gatiṃ jñātuṃ kathañcana
|
lokānāṃ ca bhavānyatra śeṣe pāṃsuṣu rūṣitaḥ||16||

17eṣa mūrdhābhiṣiktānāmagre gatvā parantapaḥ
|
satṛṇaṃ grasate pāṃsuṃ paśya kālasya paryayam||17||

18kva te tadamalaṃ chatraṃ vyajanaṃ kva ca pārthiva
|
sā ca te mahatīṃ senāṃ kva gatā pārthivottamā||18||

19durvijñeyā gatirnūnaṃ kāryāṇāṃ kāraṇāntare
|
yadvai lokagururbhūtvā bhavānetāṃ daśāṃ gataḥ||19||

20adhruvā sarvamartyeṣu śrīrūpālakṣyate bhṛśam
|
bhavato vyasanaṃ dṛṣṭvā śakravispardhino bhṛśam||20||

21tasya tadvacanaṃ śrutvā duḥkhitasya viśeṣataḥ
|
uvāca rājanputraste prāptakālamidaṃ vacaḥ||21||

22vimṛjya netre pāṇibhyāṃ śokajaṃ vāṣpamutsṛjan
|
kṛpādīnsa tadā vīrān sarvāneva narādhipaḥ||22||

23īdṛśo lokadharmo'yaṃ dhātrā nirdiṣṭa ucyate
|
vināśaḥ sarvabhūtānāṃ kālaparyāyamāgataḥ||23||

24so'yaṃ māṃ samanuprāptaḥ pratyakṣaṃ bhavatāṃ hi yaḥ
|
pṛthivīṃ pālayitvā'hametāṃ niṣṭhāmupāgataḥ||24||

25diṣṭyā nāhaṃ parāvṛtto yuddhe kasyāñcidāpadi
|
diṣṭyāhaṃ nihataḥ pāpaiśchalenaiva viśeṣataḥ||25||

26utsāhaśca kṛto nityaṃ mayā diṣṭyā yuyutsatā
|
diṣṭyā cāsminhato yuddhe nihatajñātibāndhavaḥ||26||

27diṣṭyā ca vo'haṃ paśyāmi muktānasmājjanakṣayāt
|
svastiyuktāṃśca kalyāṃśca tanme priyamanuttamam||27||

28mā bhavanto'tra tapyantāṃ sauhṛdānnidhanena me
|
yadi vedāḥ pramāṇaṃ vo jitā lokā mayākṣayāḥ||28||

29manyamānaḥ prabhāvaṃ ca kṛṣṇasyāmitatejasaḥ
|
tena na cyāvitaścāhaṃ kṣatradharmātsvanuṣṭhitāt||29||

30sa mayā samanuprāpto nāsmi śocyaḥ kathañcana
|
kṛtaṃ bhavadbhiḥ sadṛśamanurūpamivātmanaḥ||30||

31yatitaṃ vijaye nityaṃ daivaṃ tu duratikramam
|
etāvaduktvā vacanaṃ bāṣpavyākulalocanaḥ||31||

32tūṣṇīṃ babhūva rājendra rujāsau vihvalo bhṛśam
|
tathā dṛṣṭvā tu rājānaṃ bāṣpaśokasamanvitam||32||

33drauṇiḥ krodhena jajvāla yathā vahnirjagatkṣaye
|
sa ca krodhasamāviṣṭaḥ pāṇau pāṇiṃ nipīḍya ca||33||

34bāṣpavihvalayā vācā rājānamidamabravīt
|
pitā me nihataḥ kṣudraiḥ sunṛśaṃsena karmaṇā||34||

35na tathā tena tapyāmi yathā rājaṃstvayā'dya vai
|
śṛṇu cedaṃ vaco mahyaṃ satyena vadataḥ prabho||35||

36iṣṭāpūrtena dānena dharmeṇa sukṛtena ca
|
adyāhaṃ sarvapañcālānvāsudevasya paśyataḥ||36||

37sarvopāyairhi neṣyāmi pretarājaniveśanam
|
anujñāṃ tu mahārāja bhavānme dātumarhati||37||

38iti śrutvā tu vacanaṃ droṇaputrasya kauravaḥ
|
manasaḥ prītijananaṃ kṛpaṃ vacanamabravīt||38||

39ācārya śīghraṃ kalaśaṃ jalapūrṇaṃ samānaya
|
sa tadvacanamājñāya rājño brāhmaṇasattamaḥ||39||

40kalaśaṃ pūrṇamādāya rājño'ntikamupāgamat
|
tamabravīnmahārāja putrastava viśāmpate||40||

41mamājñayā dvijaśreṣṭha droṇaputro'bhiṣicyatām
|
saināpatyena bhadraṃ te mama cedicchasi priyam||41||

42rājño niyogādyoddhavyaṃ brāhmaṇena viśeṣataḥ
|
vartatā kṣatradharmeṇa hyevaṃ dharmavido viduḥ||42||

43rājñastu vacanaṃ śrutvā kṛpaḥ śāradvatastathā
|
drauṇiṃ rājño niyogena saināpatye'bhyaṣecayat||43||

44so'bhiṣikto mahārāja pariṣvajya nṛpottamam
|
prayayau siṃhanādena diśaḥ sarvā vinādayan||44||

45duryodhano'pi rājendra śoṇitena pariplutaḥ
|
tāṃ niśāṃ pratipede'tha sarvabhūtabhayāvahām||45||

46apakramya tu te tūrṇaṃ tasmādāyodhanānnṛpa
|
śokasaṃvignamanasaścintādhyānaparā'bhavan||46||
