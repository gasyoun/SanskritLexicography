Mahābhārata 7.56 (Droṇaparvan)

1nārada uvāca| suhotraṃ nāma rājānaṃ mṛtaṃ sṛñjaya śuśruma
|
ekavīramaśakyaṃ tamamarairabhivīkṣitum ||1||

2yaḥ prāpya rājyaṃ dharmeṇa ṛtvigbrahmapurohitān
|
apṛcchadātmanaḥ śreyaḥ pṛṣṭvā teṣāṃ mate sthitaḥ ||2||

3prajānāṃ pālanaṃ dharmo dānamijyā dviṣajjayaḥ
|
etatsuhotro vijñāya dharmeṇaicchaddhanāgamam ||3||

4dharmeṇārādhayandevān bāṇaiḥ śatrūn jayaṃstathā
|
sarvāṇyapi ca bhūtāni svaguṇairapyarañjayat ||4||

5yo bhuktvemāṃ vasumatīṃ mlecchāṭavikavarjitām
|
yasmai vavarṣa parjanyo hiraṇyaṃ parivatsarān ||5||

6hairaṇyāstatra vāhinyaḥ svairiṇyo vyavahan purā
|
grāhān karkaṭakāṃścaiva matsyāṃśca vividhān bahūn ||6||

7kāmān varṣati parjanyo rūpāṇi vividhāni ca
|
sauvarṇānyaprameyāṇi vāpyaśca krośasaṃmitāḥ ||7||

8sahasraṃ vāmanān kubjānnakrān makarakacchapān
|
sauvarṇān vihitān dṛṣṭvā tato'smayata vai tadā ||8||

9tat suvarṇamaparyantaṃ rājarṣiḥ kurujāṅgaleḥ
|
ījāno vitate yajñe brāhmaṇebhyo hyamanyata ||9||

10so'śvamedhasahasreṇa rājasūyaśatena ca
|
puṇyaiḥ kṣatriyayajñaiśca prabhūtavaradakṣiṇaiḥ ||10||

11kāmyanaimittikājasrairiṣṭāṃ gatimavāptavān
|
sa cenmamāra sṛñjaya caturbhadratarastvayā ||11||

12putrān puṇyatarastubhyaṃ mā putramanutapyathāḥ
|
ayajvānamadākṣiṇyamabhiśvaityeti vyāharan ||12||
