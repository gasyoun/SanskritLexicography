Mahābhārata 12.295 (Śāntiparvan)

1parāśara uvāca |
eṣa dharmavidhistāta gṛhasthasya prakīrtitaḥ
|
taporvidhiṃ tu vakṣyāmi tanme nigadataḥ śṛṇu ||1||

2prāyeṇa ca gṛhasthasya mamatvaṃ nāma jāyate
|
saṅgāgataṃ naraśreṣṭha bhāvai rājasatāmasaiḥ ||2||

3gṛhāṇyāśritya gāvaśca kṣetrāṇi ca dhanāni ca
|
dārāḥ putrāśca bhṛtyāśca bhavantīha narasya vai ||3||

4evaṃ tasya pravṛttasya nityamevānupaśyataḥ
|
rāgadveṣau vivardhete hyanityatvamapaśyataḥ ||4||

5rāgadveṣābhibhūtaṃ ca naraṃ dravyavaśānugam
|
mohajātā ratirnāma samupaiti narādhipa ||5||

6kṛtārthaṃ bhoginaṃ matvā sarvo ratiparāyaṇaḥ
|
lābhaṃ grāmyasukhādanyaṃ ratito nānupaśyati ||6||

7tato lobhābhibhūtātmā saṃgādvardhayate janam
|
puṣṭyarthaṃ caiva tasyeha janasyārthaṃ cikīrṣati ||7||

8sa jānannapi cākāryamarthārthaṃ sevate naraḥ
|
bālasnehaparītātmā tatkṣayāccānutapyate ||8||

9tato mānena sampanno rakṣannātmaparājayam
|
karoti yena bhogī syāmiti tasmādvinaśyati ||9||

10tathā hi buddhiyuktānāṃ śāśvataṃ brahmavādinām
|
anvicchatāṃ śubhaṃ karma narāṇāṃ tyajatāṃ sukham ||10||

11snehāyatananāśācca dhananāśācca pārthiva
|
ādhivyādhipratāpācca nirvedamupagacchati ||11||

12nirvedādātmasambodhaḥ sambodhācchāstradarśanam
|
śāstrārthadarśanādrājaṃstapa evānupaśyati ||12||

13durlabho hi manuṣyendra naraḥ pratyavamarśavān
|
yo vai priyasukhe kṣīṇastapaḥ kartuṃ vyavasyati ||13||

14tapaḥ sarvagataṃ tāta hīnasyāpi vidhīyate
|
jitendriyasya dāntasya svargamārgapravartakam ||14||

15prajāpatiḥ prajāḥ pūrvamasṛjattapasā vibhuḥ
|
kvacitkvacidbrahmaparo vratānyāsthāya pārthiva ||15||

16ādityā vasavo rudrāstathaivāgnyaśvimārutāḥ
|
viśvedevāstathā sādhyāḥ pitaro'tha marudgaṇāḥ ||16||

17yakṣarākṣasagandharvāḥ siddhāścānye divaukasaḥ
|
saṃsiddhāstapasā tāta ye cānye svargavāsinaḥ ||17||

18ye cādau brāhmaṇāḥ sṛṣṭā brahmaṇā tapasā purā
|
te bhāvayantaḥ pṛthivīṃ vicaranti divaṃ tathā ||18||

19martyaloke ca rājāno ye cānye gṛhamedhinaḥ
|
mahākuleṣu dṛśyante tatsarvaṃ tapasaḥ phalam ||19||

20kauśikāni ca vastrāṇi śubhānyābharaṇāni ca
|
vāhanāsanapānāni tatsarvaṃ tapasaḥ phalam ||20||

21mano'nukūlāḥ pramadā rūpavatyaḥ sahasraśaḥ
|
vāsaḥ prāsādapṛṣṭhe ca tatsarvaṃ tapasaḥ phalam ||21||

22śayanāni ca mukhyāni bhojyāni vividhāni ca
|
abhipretāni sarvāṇi bhavanti śubhakarmiṇām ||22||

23nāprāpyaṃ tapasaḥ kiṃcit trailokye'pi paraṃtapa
|
upabhogaparityāgaḥ phalānyakṛtakarmaṇām ||23||

24sukhito duḥkhito vā'pi naro lobhaṃ parityajet
|
avekṣya manasā śāstraṃ buddhyā ca nṛpasattama ||24||

25asantoṣo'sukhāyeti lobhādindriyasambhramaḥ
|
tato'sya naśyati prajñā vidyevābhyāsavarjitā ||25||

26naṣṭaprajño yadā tu syāttadā nyāyaṃ na paśyati
|
tasmātsukhakṣaye prāpte pumānugraṃ tapaścaret ||26||

27yadiṣṭaṃ tatsukhaṃ prāhurdveṣyaṃ duḥkhamiheṣyate
|
kṛtākṛtasya tapasaḥ phalaṃ paśyasva yādṛśam ||27||

28nityaṃ bhadrāṇi paśyanti viṣayāṃścopabhuñjate
|
prākāśyaṃ caiva gacchanti kṛtvā niṣkalmaṣaṃ tapaḥ ||28||

29apriyāṇyavamānāṃśca duḥkhaṃ bahuvidhātmakam
|
phalārthī tatphalaṃ tyaktvā prāpnoti viṣayātmakam ||29||

30dharme tapasi dāne ca vicikitsā cāsya jāyate
|
sa kṛtvā pāpakānyeva nirayaṃ pratipadyate ||30||

31sukhe tu vartamāno vai duḥkhe vāpi narottama
|
suvṛttādyo na calate śāstracakṣuḥ sa mānavaḥ ||31||

32iṣuprapātamātraṃ hi sparśayoge ratiḥ smṛtā
|
rasane darśane ghrāṇe śravaṇe ca viśāmpate ||32||

33tato'sya jāyate tīvrā vedanā tatkṣayātpunaḥ
|
abudhā na praśaṃsanti mokṣaṃ sukhamanuttamam ||33||

34tataḥ phalārthaṃ sarvasya bhavanti jyāyase guṇāḥ
|
dharmavṛttyā ca satataṃ kāmārthābhyāṃ na hīyate ||34||

35aprayatnāgatāḥ sevyā gṛhasthairviṣayāḥ sadā
|
prayatnenopagamyaśca svadharma iti me matiḥ ||35||

36mānināṃ kulajātānāṃ nityaṃ śāstrārthacakṣuṣām
|
kriyādharmavimuktānāmaśaktyā saṃvṛtātmanām ||36||

37kriyamāṇaṃ yadā karma nāśaṃ gacchati mānuṣam
|
teṣāṃ nānyadṛte loke tapasaḥ karma vidyate ||37||

38sarvātmanā'nukurvīta gṛhasthaḥ karmaniścayam
|
dākṣyeṇa havyakavyārthaṃ svadharme vicarannṛpa ||38||

39yathā nadīnadāḥ sarve sāgare yānti saṃsthitam
|
evamāśramiṇaḥ sarve gṛhasthe yānti saṃsthitim ||39||
