Mahābhārata 1.34 (Ādiparvan)

1garuḍa uvāca|
sakhyaṃ me'stu tvayā deva yathecchasi puraṃdara
|
balaṃ tu mama jānīhi mahaccāsahyameva ca||1||

2kāmaṃ naitatpraśaṃsanti santaḥ svabalasaṃstavam
|
guṇasaṃkīrtanaṃ cāpi svayameva śatakrato||2||

3sakheti kṛtvā tu sakhe pṛṣṭo vakṣyāmyahaṃ tvayā
|
na hyātmastavasaṃyuktaṃ vaktavyamanimittataḥ||3||

4saparvatavanāmurvīṃ sasāgarajalāmimām
|
vahe pakṣeṇa vai śakra tvāmapyatrāvalambinam||4||

5sarvānsaṃpiṇḍitānvāpi lokānsasthāṇujaṅgamān
|
vaheyamapariśrānto viddhīdaṃ me mahadbalam||5||

6sautiruvāca|
ityuktavacanaṃ vīraṃ kirīṭī śrīmatāṃ varaḥ
|
āha śaunaka devendraḥ sarvalokahitaḥ prabhuḥ||6||

7evameva yathāttha tvaṃ sarvaṃ saṃbhāvyate tvayi
|
saṃgṛhyatāmidānīṃ me sakhyamatyantamuttamam||7||

8na kāryaṃ yadi somena mama somaḥ pradīyatām
|
asmāṃste hi prabādheyuryebhyo dadyādbhavānimam||8||

9garuḍa uvāca|
kiṃcitkāraṇamuddiśya somo'yaṃ nīyate mayā
|
na dāsyāmi samāpātuṃ somaṃ kasmaicidapyaham||9||

10yatremaṃ tu sahasrākṣa nikṣipeyamahaṃ svayam
|
tvamādāya tatastṛrṇaṃ harethāstridiveśvara||10||

11śakra uvāca|
vākyenānena tuṣṭo'haṃ yattvayoktamihāṇḍaja
|
yamicchasi varaṃ mattastaṃ gṛhāṇa khagottama||11||

12sautiruvāca|
ityuktaḥ pratyuvācedaṃ kadrūputrānanusmaran
|
smṛtvā caivopadhikṛtaṃ māturdāsyanimittataḥ||12||

13īśo'hamapi sarvasya kariṣyāmi tu te'rthitām
|
bhaveyurbhujagāḥ śakra mama bhakṣyā mahābalāḥ||13||

14tathetyuktvā'nvagacchattaṃ tato dānavasūdanaḥ
|
devadevaṃ mahātmānaṃ yogināmīśvaraṃ harim||14||

15sa cānvamodattaṃ cārthaṃ yathoktaṃ garuḍena vai
|
idaṃ bhūyo vacaḥ prāha bhagavāṃstridaśeśvaraḥ||15||

16hariṣyāmi vinikṣiptaṃ somamityanubhāṣya tam
|
ājagāma tatastūrṇaṃ suparṇo māturantikam||16||

17atha sarpānuvācedaṃ sarvānparamahṛṣṭavat
|
idamānītamamṛtaṃ nikṣepsyāmi kuśeṣu vaḥ||17||

18snātā maṅgalasaṃyuktāstataḥ prāśnīta pannagāḥ
|
bhavadbhiridamāsīnairyaduktaṃ tadvacastadā||18||

19adāsī caiva māteyamadyaprabhṛti cāstu me
|
yathoktaṃ bhavatāmetadvaco me pratipāditam||19||

20tataḥ snātuṃ gatāḥ sarpāḥ pratyuktvā taṃ tathetyuta
|
śakro'pyamṛtamākṣipya jagāma tridivaṃ punaḥ||20||

21athāgatāstamuddeśaṃ sarpāḥ somārthinastadā
|
snātāśca kutajapyāśca prahṛṣṭāḥ kṛtamaṅgalāḥ||21||

22yatraitadamṛtaṃ cāpi sthāpitaṃ kuśasaṃstare
|
tadvijñāya hṛtaṃ sarpāḥ pratimāyā kṛtaṃ ca tat||22||

23somasthānamidaṃ ceti darbhāṃste lilihustadā
|
tato dvidhā kṛtā jihvāḥ sarpāṇāṃ tena karmaṇā||23||

24abhavaṃścāmṛtasparśāddarbhāste'tha pavitriṇaḥ
|
evaṃ tadamṛtaṃ tena hṛtamāhṛtameva ca
|
dvijihvāśca kṛtāḥ sarpā garuḍena mahātmanā||24||

25tataḥ suparṇaḥ paramapraharṣavānvihṛtya mātrā saha tatra kānane
|
bhujaṃgabhakṣaḥ paramārcitaḥ khagairahīnakīrtirvinatāmanandayat|| 25||

26imāṃ kathāṃ yaḥ śṛṇuyānnaraḥ sadā paṭheta vā dvijagaṇamukhyasaṃsadi
|
asaṃśayaṃ tridivamiyātsa puṇyabhāk mahātmanaḥ patagapateḥ prakīrtanāt||26||
