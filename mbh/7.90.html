Mahābhārata 7.90 (Droṇaparvan)

1dhṛtarāṣṭra uvāca|
tasmin prabhagne sainyāgre vadhyamāne kirīṭinā
|
ke tu tatra raṇe vīrāḥ pratyudīyurdhanañjayam ||1||

2āhosvicchakaṭavyūhaṃ praviṣṭā meghaniścayāḥ
|
droṇamāśritya tiṣṭhantaṃ prākāramakutobhayam ||2||

3sañjaya uvāca|
tathā'rjunena saṃbhagne tasmistava bale'nagha
|
hatavīre hatotsāhe palāyanakṛtakṣaṇe ||3||

4pākaśāsaninā'bhīkṣṇaṃ vadhyamāne śarottamaiḥ
|
na tatra kaścitsaṃgrāme śaśākārjunamīkṣitum ||4||

5tatastava suto rājan dṛṣṭvā sainyaṃ tathāgatam
|
duḥśāsano bhṛśaṃ kruddho yuddhāyārjunamabhyagāt ||5||

6sa kāñcanavicitreṇa kavacena samāvṛtaḥ
|
jāmbūnada‌śirastrāṇaḥ śūrastīvraparākramaḥ ||6||

7nāgānīkena mahatā grasanniva mahīmimām
|
duḥśāsano mahārāja savyasācinamāvṛṇot ||7||

8hrādena gajaghaṇṭānāṃ śaṃkhānāṃ ninadena ca
|
jyākṣepaninadaiścaiva virāveṇa ca dantināmā ||8||

9bhūrdiśaścāntarikṣaṃ ca śabdenāsīt samāvṛtam
|
sa muhūrtaṃ pratibhayo dāruṇaḥ samapadyata ||9||

10tān dṛṣṭvā patatastūrṇamaṅkuśairabhicoditān
|
vyālambahastān saṃrabdhān sapakṣāniva parvatān ||10||

11siṃhanādena mahatā narasiṃho dhanañjayaḥ
|
gajānīkamamitrāṇāmabhīto vyadhamaccharaiḥ ||11||

12mahormiṇamivoddhūtaṃ śvasanena mahārṇavam
|
kirīṭī tadgajānīkaṃ prāviśanmakaro yathā ||12||

13kāṣṭhātīta ivādityaḥ pratapan sa yugakṣaye
|
dadṛśe dikṣu sarvāsu pārthaḥ parapurañjayaḥ ||13||

14khuraśabdena cāśvānāṃ nemighoṣeṇa tena ca
|
tena cotkṛṣṭaśabdena jyāninādena tena ca ||14||

15nānāvāditraśabdena pāñcajanyasvanena ca
|
devadattasya ghoṣeṇa gāṇḍīvaninadena ca ||15||

16mandavegā narā nāgā babhūvuste vicetasaḥ
|
śarairāśīviṣasparśairnirbhinnāḥ savyasācinā ||16||

17te gajā viśikhaistīkṣṇairyudhi gāṇḍīvacoditaiḥ
|
anekaśatasāhasraiḥ sarvāṅgeṣu samarpitāḥ ||17||

18ārāvaṃ paramaṃ kṛtvā vadhyamānāḥ kirīṭinā
|
nipeturaniśaṃ bhūmau chinnapakṣā ivādrayaḥ ||18||

19apare dantaveṣṭeṣu kuṃbheṣu ca kaṭeṣu ca
|
śaraiḥ samarpitā nāgāḥ krauñcavadvyanadanmuhuḥ ||19||

20gajaskandhagatānāṃ ca puruṣāṇāṃ kirīṭinā
|
cchidyante cottamāṅgāni bhallaiḥ sannataparvabhiḥ ||20||

21sakuṇḍalānāṃ patatāṃ śirasāṃ dharaṇītale
|
padmānāmiva saṃghātaiḥ pārthaścakre nivedanam ||21||

22yantrabaddhā vikavacā vraṇārtā rudhirokṣitāḥ
|
bhramatsu yudhi nāgeṣu manuṣyā vilalambire ||22||

23kecidekena bāṇena suyuktena supatriṇā
|
dvau trayaśca vinirbhinnā nipeturdharaṇītale ||23||

24atividdhāśca nārācairvamanto rudhiraṃ mukhaiḥ
|
sārohā nyapatan bhūmau drumavanta ivācalāḥ ||24||

25maurvīṃ dhvajaṃ dhanuścaiva yugamīṣāṃ tathaiva ca
|
rathināṃ kuṭṭayāmāsa bhallaiḥ sannataparvabhiḥ ||25||

26na saṃdadhanna cākarṣanna vimuñcanna codvahan
|
maṇḍalenaiva dhanuṣā nutyan pārthaḥ sma dṛśyate ||26||

27atividdhāśca nārācairvamanto rudhiraṃ mukhaiḥ
|
muhūrtānnyapatannanye vāraṇā vasudhātale ||27||

28utthitānyagaṇeyāni kabandhāni samantataḥ
|
adṛśyanta mahārāja tasmin paramasaṃkule ||28||

29sacāpāḥ sāṃgulitrāṇāḥ sakhaḍgāḥ sāṅgadā raṇe
|
adṛśyanta bhujāśchinnā hemābharaṇabhūṣitāḥ ||29||

30sūpaskarairadhiṣṭhānairīṣādaṇḍakabandhuraiḥ
|
cakairvimathitairakṣairbhagnaiśca bahudhā yugaiḥ ||30||

31carmacāpadharaiścaiva vyavakīrṇaistatastataḥ
|
sragbhirābharaṇairvastraiḥ patitaiśca mahādhvajaiḥ ||31||

32nihatairvāraṇairaśvaiḥ kṣatriyaiśca nipātitaiḥ
|
adṛśyata mahī tatra dāruṇapratidarśanā ||32||

33evaṃ duḥśāsanabalaṃ vadhyamānaṃ kirīṭinā
|
saṃprādravanmahārāja vyathitaṃ sahanāyakam ||33||

34tato duḥśāsanastrastaḥ sahānīkaḥ śarārditaḥ
|
droṇaṃ trātāramākāṃkṣan śakaṭavyūhamabhyagāt ||34||
