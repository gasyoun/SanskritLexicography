Mahābhārata 2.3 (Sabhāparvan)

1vaiśampāyana uvāca|
athābravīnmayaḥ pārthamarjunaṃ jayatāṃ varam
|
āpṛcche tvāṃ gamiṣyāmi punareṣyāmi cāpyaham||1||

2uttareṇa tu kailāsaṃ mainākaṃ parvataṃ prati
|
yiyakṣamāṇeṣu purā dānaveṣu mayā kṛtam||2||

3citraṃ maṇimayaṃ bhāṇḍaṃ ramyaṃ bindusaraḥ prati
|
sabhāyāṃ satyasandhasya yadāsīdvṛṣaparvaṇaḥ||3||

4āgamiṣyāmi tadgṛhya yadi tiṣṭhati bhārata
|
tataḥ sabhāṃ kariṣyāmi pāṇḍavasya yaśasvinīm||4||

5manaḥ prahlādinīṃ citrāṃ sarvaratnavibhūṣitām
|
asti bindusārasyugrā gadā ca kurunandana||5||

6nihitā bhāvayāmyevaṃ rājñā hatvā raṇe ripūn
|
suvarṇabindubhiścitrā gurvī bhārasahā dṛḍhā||6||

7sā vai śatasahasrasya sammitā śatrughātinī
|
anurūpā ca bhīmasya gāṇḍīvaṃ bhavato yathā||7||

8vāruṇaśca mahāśaṅkho devadattaḥ sughoṣavān
|
sarvametatpradāsyāmi bhavate nātra saṃśayaḥ||8||

9ityuktvā so'suraḥ pārthaṃ prāgudīcīṃ diśaṃ gataḥ
|
athottareṇa kailāsānmainākaṃ parvataṃ prati||9||

10hiraṇyaśṛṅgaḥ sumahānmahāmaṇimayo giriḥ
|
ramyaṃ bindusaro nāma yatra rājā bhagīrathaḥ||10||

11druṣṭuṃ bhāgīrathīṃ gaṅgāmuvāsa bahulāḥ samāḥ
|
yatreṣṭaṃ sarvabhūtānāmīśvareṇa mahātmanā||11||

12āhṛtāḥ kratavo mukhyāḥ śataṃ bharatasattama
|
yatra yūpā maṇimayāścaityāścāpi hiraṇmayāḥ||12||

13śobhārthaṃ vihitāstatra na tu dṛṣṭāntataḥ kṛtāḥ
|
yatreṣṭvā sa gataḥ siddhiṃ sahasrākṣaḥ śacīpatiḥ||13||

14yatra bhūtapatiḥ sṛṣṭvā sarvānlokānsanātanaḥ
|
upāsyate tigmatejāḥ sthito bhūtaiḥ sahasraśaḥ||14||

15naranārāyaṇau brahmā yamaḥ sthāṇuśca pañcamaḥ
|
upāsate yatra satraṃ sahasrayugaparyaye||15||

16yatreṣṭaṃ vāsudevena satrairvarṣagaṇānbahūn
|
śraddadhānena satataṃ dharmasampratipattaye||16||

17suvarṇamālino yūpānścaityānścāpyatibhāsvarān
|
dadau yatra sahasrāṇi prayutāni ca keśavaḥ||17||

18tatra gatvā sa jagrāha gadāṃ śaṅkhaṃ ca bhārata
|
sphāṭikaṃ ca sabhādravyaṃ yadāsīdvṛṣaparvaṇaḥ||18||

19kiṅkaraiḥ saha rakṣobhiryadarakṣanmahaddhanam
|
tadagṛhṇānmayastatra gatvā sarvaṃ mahāsuraḥ||19||

20tadāhṛtya ca tāṃ cakre so'suro'pratimāṃ sabhām
|
viśrutāṃ triṣu lokeṣu divyāṃ maṇimayīṃ śubhām||20||

21gadāṃ ca bhīmasenāya pravarāṃ pradadau tadā
|
devadattaṃ cārjunāya śaṅkhapravaramuttamam||21||

22yasya śaṅkhasya nādena bhūtāni pracakampire
|
sabhā ca sā mahārāja śātakumbhamayadrumā||22||

23daśakiṣkusahasrāṇi samantādādāyatābhavat
|
yathā vahneryathārkasya somasya ca yathā sabhā||23||

24bhrājamānā tathātyarthaṃ dadhāra paramaṃ vapuḥ
|
abhighnatīva prabhayā prabhāmarkasya bhāsvarām||24||

25prababhau jvalamāneva divyā divyena varcasā
|
navameghapratīkāśā divamākṛtya viṣṭhitā
|
āyatā vipulā ramyā vipāpmā vigataklamā ||25||

26uttamadravyasampannā ratnaprākāratoraṇā
|
bahucitrā bahudhanā sukṛtā viśvakarmaṇā||26||

27na dāśārhī sudharmā vā brahmaṇo vātha tādṛśī
|
sabhā rūpeṇa sampannā yāṃ cakre matimānmayaḥ||27||

28tāṃ sma tatra mayenoktā rakṣanti ca vahanti ca
|
sabhāmaṣṭau sahasrāṇi kiṅkarā nāma rākṣasāḥ||28||

29antarikṣacarā ghorā mahākāyā mahābalāḥ
|
raktākṣāḥ piṅgalākṣāśca śuktikarṇāḥ prahāriṇaḥ||29||

30tasyāṃ sabhāyāṃ nalinīṃ cakārāpratimāṃ mayaḥ
|
vaidūryapatravitatāṃ maṇinālamayāmbujām||30||

31padmasaugandhikavatīṃ nānādvijagaṇāyutām
|
puṣpitaiḥ paṅkajaiścitrāṃ kūrmairmatsyaiśca kāñcanaiḥ
|
citrasphaṭikasopānāṃ niṣpaṅkasalilāṃ śubhām||31||

32mandānilasamuddhūtāṃ muktābindubhirācitām
|
mahāmaṇiśilāpaṭṭabaddhaparyantavedikām||32||

33maṇiratnacitāṃ tāṃ tu kecidabhyetya pārthivāḥ
|
dṛṣṭvāpi nābhyajānanta te'jñānātprapatantyuta ||33||

34tāṃ sabhāmabhito nityaṃ puṣpavanto mahādrumāḥ
|
āsannānāvidhā lolāḥ śītacchāyā manoramāḥ||34||

35kānanāni sugandhīni puṣkariṇyaśca sarvaśaḥ
|
haṃsakāraṇḍavopetāścakravākopaśobhitāḥ||35||

36jalajānāṃ ca padmānāṃ sthalajānāṃ ca sarvaśaḥ
|
māruto gandhamādāya pāṇḍavānsma niṣevate||36||

37īdṛśīṃ tāṃ sabhāṃ kṛtvā māsaiḥ paricaturdaśaiḥ
|
niṣṭhitāṃ dharmarājāya mayo rājannyavedayat||37||
