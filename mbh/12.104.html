Mahābhārata 12.104 (Śāntiparvan)

1yudhiṣṭhira uvāca|
dhārmiko'rthānasamprāpya rājāmātyaiḥ prabādhitaḥ
|
cyutaḥ kośācca daṇḍācca sukhamicchankathaṃ caret||1||

2bhīṣma uvāca|
atrāyaṃ kṣemadarśīya itihāso'nugīyate
|
tatte'haṃ sampravakṣyāmi tannibodha yudhiṣṭhira||2||

3kṣemadarśī nṛpasuto yatra kṣīṇabalaḥ purā
|
muniṃ kālakavṛkṣīyamājagāmeti naḥ śrutam
|
taṃ papracchānusaṃgṛhya kṛcchrāmāpadamāsthitaḥ||3||

4rājovāca
|
artheṣu bhāgī puruṣa īhamānaḥ punaḥ punaḥ
|
alabdhvā madvidho rājyaṃ brahmankiṃ kartumarhati||4||

5anyatra maraṇāddainyādanyatra parasaṃśrayāt
|
kṣudrādanyatra cācārāttanmamācakṣva sattama||5||

6vyādhinā cābhipannasya mānasenetareṇa vā
|
dharmajñaśca kṛtajñaśca tvadvidhaḥ śaraṇaṃ bhavet||6||

7nirvidyati naraḥ kāmānnirvidya sukhamedhate
|
tyaktvā prītiṃ ca śokaṃ ca labdhvā buddhimayaṃ vasu||7||

8sukhamarthāśrayaṃ yeṣāmanuśocāmi tānaham
|
mama hyarthāḥ subahavo naṣṭāḥ svapna ivāgatāḥ||8||

9duṣkaraṃ bata kurvanti mahato'rthāṃstyijanti ye
|
vayaṃ tvetānparityaktumasato'pi na śaknumaḥ||9||

10imāmavasthāṃ samprāptaṃ dīnamārtaṃ śriyā cyutam
|
yadanyatsukhamastīha tadbrahmannanuśādhi mām||10||

11kausalyenaivamuktastu rājaputreṇa dhīmatā
|
muniḥ kālakavṛkṣīyaḥ pratyuvāca mahādyutiḥ||11||

12muniruvāca| purastādeva te buddhiriyaṃ kāryā vijānatā
|
anityaṃ sarvamevaitadahaṃ ca mama cāsti yat||12||

13yatkiṃcinmanyase'stīti sarvaṃ nāstīti viddhi tat
|
evaṃ na vyathate prājñaḥ kṛcchrāmapyāpadaṃ gataḥ||13||

14yaddhi bhūtaṃ bhaviṣyaṃ ca sarvaṃ tanna bhaviṣyati
|
evaṃ viditavedyastvamadharmebhyaḥ pramokṣyase||14||

15yacca pūrvaṃ samāhāre yacca pūrvaṃ pare pare
|
sarvaṃ tannāsti te caiva tajjñātvā ko'nusaṃjvaret||15||

16bhūtvā ca na bhavatyetadabhūtvā ca bhaviṣyati
|
śoke na hyasti sāmarthyaṃ śokaṃ kuryātkathaṃcana||16||

17kva nu te'dya pitā rājan kva nu te'dya pitāmahaḥ
|
na tvaṃ paśyasi tānadya na tvāṃ paśyanti te'pi ca||17||

18ātmano'dhruvatāṃ paśyaṃstāṃstvaṃ kimanuśocasi
|
buddhyā caivānubuddhyasva dhruvaṃ hi na bhaviṣyasi||18||

19ahaṃ ca tvaṃ ca nṛpate suhṛdaḥ śatravaśca te
|
avaśyaṃ na bhaviṣyāmaḥ sarvaṃ ca na bhaviṣyati||19||

20ye tu viṃśativarṣā vai triṃśadvarṣāśca mānavāḥ
|
arvāgeva hi te sarve mariṣyanti śaracchatāt||20||

21api cenmahato vittānna pramucyeta pūruṣaḥ
|
naitanmameti tanmatvā kurvīta priyamātmanaḥ||21||

22anāgataṃ yanna mameti vidyādatikrāntaṃ yanna mameti vidyāt
|
diṣṭaṃ balīya iti manyamānāste paṇḍitāstatsatāṃ sthānamāhuḥ||22||

23anāḍhyāścāpi jīvanti rājyaṃ cāpyanuśāsati
|
buddhipauruṣasampannāstvayā tulyādhikā janāḥ||23||

24na ca tvamiva śocanti tasmāttvamapi mā śucaḥ
|
kiṃ na tvaṃ tairnaraiḥ śreyāṃstulyo vā buddhipauruṣaiḥ||24||

25rājovāca|
yādṛcchikaṃ sarvamāsīttadrājyamiti cintaye
|
hriyate sarvamevedaṃ kālena mahatā dvija||25||

26tasyaiva hriyamāṇasya strotaseva tapodhana
|
phalametatprapaśyāmi yathālabdhena vartayan||26||

27muniruvāca|
anāgatamatītaṃ ca yāthātathyaviniścayāt
|
nānuśoceta kausalya sarvārtheṣu tathā bhava||27||

28avāpyānkāmayannarthānnānavāpyānkadācana
|
pratyutpannānanubhavanmā śucastvamanāgatān||28||

29yathā labdhopapannārthaistathā kausalya raṃsyase
|
kaccicchuddhasvabhāvena śriyā hīno na śocasi||29||

30purastādbhūtapūrvatvāddhīnabhāgyo hi durmatiḥ
|
dhātāraṃ garhate nityaṃ labdhārthaśca na mṛṣyate||30||

31anarhānapi caivānyānmanyate śrīmato janān
|
etasmātkāraṇādetadduḥkhaṃ bhūyo'nuvartate||31||

32īrṣyābhimānasampannā rājanpuruṣamāninaḥ
|
kaccittvaṃ na tathā rājanmatsarī kosalādhipa||32||

33sahasva śriyamanyeṣāṃ yadyapi tvayi nāsti sā
|
anyatrāpi satīṃ lakṣmīṃ kuśalā bhuñjate sadā||33||

34śriyaṃ ca putrapautraṃ ca manuṣyā dharmacāriṇaḥ
|
yogadharmavido dhīrāḥ svayameva tyajantyuta||34||

35bahusaṃkusukaṃ dṛṣṭvā vidhitsāsādhanena ca
|
tathā'nye saṃtyajantyeva matvā paramadurlabham||35||

36ttvaṃ punaḥ prājñarūpaḥ san kṛpaṇaṃ paritapyase
|
akāmyānkāmayāno'rthān parādhīnānupadravān||36||

37tāṃ buddhimupajijñāsustvamevaitānparityaja
|
anarthāścārtharūpeṇa hyarthāścānartharūpiṇaḥ||37||

38arthāyaiva hi keṣāṃciddhananāśo bhavatyuta
|
ānantyaṃ tatsukhaṃ matvā śriyamanyaḥ parīpsati||38||

39ramamāṇaḥ śriyā kaścinnānyacchreyo'bhimanyate
|
tathā tasyehamānasya samārambho vinaśyati||39||

40kṛcchrāllabdhamabhipretaṃ yadi kausalya naśyati
|
tadā nirvidyate so'rthātparibhagnakramo naraḥ||40||

41dharmameke'bhipadyante kalyāṇābhijanā narāḥ
|
paratra sukhamicchanto nirvidyeyuśca laukikāt||41||

42jīvitaṃ saṃtyajantyeke dhanalobhaparā janāḥ
|
na jīvitārthaṃ manyante puruṣā hi dhanādṛte||42||

43paśya teṣāṃ kṛpaṇatāṃ paśya teṣāmabuddhitām
|
adhruve jīvite mohādarthadṛṣṭimupāśritāḥ||43||

44saṃcaye ca vināśānte maraṇānte ca jīvita
|
saṃyoge ca viyogānte ko nu vipraṇayenmanaḥ||44||

45dhanaṃ vā puruṣo rājanpuruṣaṃ vā punardhanam
|
avaśyaṃ prajahātyeva tadvidvānko'nusaṃjvaret||45||

46anyeṣāmapi naśyanti suhṛdaśca dhanāni ca
|
paśya buddhyā manuṣyāṇāṃ rājannāpadamātmanaḥ
|
niyaccha yaccha saṃyaccha indriyāṇi mano giram||46||

47pratiṣeddhā na cāpyeṣu durbaleṣvahiteṣvapi ||47||

48prāptisṛṣṭeṣu bhāveṣu vyapakṛṣṭeṣvasambhave
|
prajñānatṛpto vikrāntastvadvidho nānuśocati||48||

49alpamicchannacapalo mṛdurdāntaḥ suniścitaḥ
|
brahmacaryopapannaśca tvadvidho naiva śocati||49||

50na tveva jālmīṃ kāpālīṃ vṛttimeṣitumarhasi
|
nṛśaṃsavṛttiṃ pāpiṣṭhāṃ duṣṭāṃ kāpuruṣocitām||50||

51api mūlaphalājīvo ramasvaiko mahāvane
|
vāgyata saṃgṛhītātmā sarvabhūtadayānvitaḥ||51||

52sadṛśaṃ paṇḍitasyaitadīṣādantena dantinā
|
yadeko ramate'raṇyeṣvāraṇyenaiva tuṣyati||52||

53mahāhradaḥ saṃkṣubhita ātmanaiva prasīdati
|
etadevaṃ gatasyāhaṃ sukhaṃ paśyāmi jīvitum||53||

54asambhave śriyo rājanhīnasya sacivādibhiḥ
|
daive pratiniviṣṭe ca kiṃ śreyo manyate bhavān||54||
