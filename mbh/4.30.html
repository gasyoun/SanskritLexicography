Mahābhārata 4.30 (Virāṭaparvan)

1vaiśampāyana uvāca|atha rājā trigartānāṃ suśarmā rathayūthapaḥ
|
prāptakālamidaṃ vākyamuvāca tvarito balī ||1||

2asakṛnnikṛtāḥ pūrvaṃ matsyaśālveyakaiḥ prabho
|
sutenaiva ca matsyasya kīcakena punaḥ punaḥ ||2||

3bādhito bandhubhiḥ sārdhaṃ balādbalavatāṃ vibhau
|
sa karṇamabhyudīkṣyātha duryodhanamabhāṣata ||3||

4asakṛtmatsyarājñā me rāṣṭraṃ bādhitamojasā
|
praṇetā kīcakastasya balavānabhavat purā ||4||

5krūro'marṣī sa duṣṭātmā bhuvi prakhyātavikramaḥ
|
nihataḥ sa tu gandharvaiḥ pāpakarmā nṛśaṃsavān ||5||

6tasmin vinihate rājā hatadarpo nirāśrayaḥ
|
bhaviṣyati nirutsāho virāṭa iti me matiḥ ||6||

7tatra yātrā mama matā yadi te rocate'nagha
|
kauravāṇāṃ ca sarveṣāṃ karṇasya ca mahātmanaḥ ||7||

8etat prāptamahaṃ manye kāryamātyayikaṃ hi naḥ
|
rāṣṭraṃ tasyābhiyāsyāmo bahudhānyasamākulam ||8||

9ādadāmo'sya ratnāni vividhāni vasūni ca
|
grāmān rāṣṭrāṇi vā tasya hariṣyāmo vibhāgaśaḥ ||9||

10athavā gosahasrāṇi śubhāni ca bahūni ca
|
vividhāni hariṣyāmaḥ pratipīḍya puraṃ balāt ||10||

11kauravaiḥ saha saṅgatya trigartaiśca viśāmpate
|
gāstasyāpaharāmo'dya sarvaiścaiva susaṃhatāḥ ||11||

12saṃvibhāgena kṛtvā tu nibaghnīmo'sya pauruṣam
|
hatvā cāsya camūṃ kṛtsnāṃ vaśamevānayāmahe ||12||

13taṃ vaśe nyāyataḥ kṛtvā sukhaṃ vatsyāmahe vayam
|
bhavatāṃ balavṛddhiśca bhaviṣyati na saṃśayaḥ ||13||

14tacchrutvā vacanaṃ tasya karṇo rājānamabravīt
|
sūktaṃ suśarmaṇā vākyaṃ prāptakālaṃ hitaṃ ca naḥ ||14||

15tasmāt kṣipraṃ viniryāmo yojayitvā varūthinīm
|
vibhajya cāpyanīkāni yathā vā manyase'nagha ||15||

16prājño vā kuruvṛddho'yaṃ sarveṣāṃ naḥ pitāmahaḥ
|
ācāryaśca yathā droṇaḥ kṛpaḥ śāradvatastathā
|
manyante te yathā sarve tathā yātrā vidhīyatām ||16||

17saṃmantrya cāśu gacchāmaḥ sādhanārthaṃ mahīpateḥ
|
kiṃ ca naḥ pāṇḍavaiḥ kāryaṃ hīnārthabalapauruṣaiḥ ||17||

18atyantaṃ vā pranaṣṭāste prāptā vā'pi yamakṣayam
|
yāmo rājannirudvignā virāṭanagaraṃ vayam ||18||

19ādāsyāmo hi gāstasya vividhāni vasūni ca
|
vaiśampāyana uvāca|tato duryodhano rājā vākyamādāya tasya tat
|
vaikartanasya karṇasya kṣipramājñāpayat svayam ||19||

20śāsane nityasaṃyuktaṃ duḥśāsanamanantaram
|
saha vṛddhaistu saṃmantrya kṣipraṃ yojaya vāhinīm ||20||

21yathoddeśaṃ ca gacchāmaḥ sahitāstatra kauravaiḥ
|
suśarmā ca yathoddiṣṭaṃ deśaṃ yātu mahārathaḥ
|
trigartaiḥ sahito rājā samagrabalavāhanaḥ ||21||

22prāgeva hi susaṃvīto matsyasya viṣayaṃ prati
|
jaghanyato vayaṃ tatra yāsyāmo divasāntare
|
viṣayaṃ matsyarājasya susamṛddhaṃ susaṃhatāḥ ||22||

23te yāntu sahitāstatra virāṭanagaraṃ prati
|
kṣipraṃ gopān samāsādya gṛhṇantu vipulaṃ dhanam ||23||

24gavāṃ śatasahasrāṇi śrīmanti guṇavanti ca
|
vayamapyanugṛhṇīmo dvidhā kṛtvā varūthinīm ||24||

25vaiśampāyana uvāca|te sma gatvā yathoddiṣṭāṃ diśaṃ vahnermahīpate
|
sannaddhā rathinaḥ sarve sapadātā balotkaṭāḥ ||25||

26prati vairaṃ cikīrṣanto goṣu gṛddhā mahābalāḥ
|
ādātuṃ gāḥ suśarmā'tha kṛṣṇapakṣasya saptamīm ||26||

27apare divase sarve rājan saṃbhūya kauravāḥ
|
aṣṭamyāṃ te nyagṛhṇanta gokulāni sahasraśaḥ ||27||
