Mahābhārata 7.50 (Droṇaparvan)

1sañjaya uvāca|
vayaṃ tu pravaraṃ hatvā teṣāṃ taiḥ śarapīḍitāḥ
|
niveśāyābhyupāyāmaḥ sāyāhne rudhirokṣitāḥ||1||

2nirīkṣamāṇāstu vayaṃ pare cāyodhanaṃ śanaiḥ
|
apayātā mahārāja glāniṃ prāptā vicetasaḥ||2||

3tato niśāyā divasasya cāśivaḥ śivārutaiḥ sandhiravartatādbhutaḥ
|
kuśeśayāpīḍanibhe divākare vilambamāne'stamupetya parvatam||3||

4varāsiśaktyṛṣṭivarūthacarmaṇāṃ vibhūṣaṇānāṃ ca samākṣipan prabhāḥ
|
divaṃ ca bhūmiṃ ca samānayanniva priyāṃ tanuṃ bhānurupaiti pāvakam||4||

5mahābhrakūṭācalaśṛṅgasannibhairgajairanekairiva vajrapātitaiḥ
|
sa vaijayantyaṃkuśavarmayantṛbhirnipātitairnaṣṭagatiścitā kṣitiḥ||5||

6hateśvaraiścūrṇitapattyupaskarairhatāśvasūtairvipatākaketubhiḥ
|
mahārathairbhū śuśubhe vicūrṇitaiḥ purairivāmitrahatairnarādhipa||6||

7rathāśvavṛndaiḥ saha sādibhirhataiḥ praviddhabhāṇḍābharaṇaiḥ pṛthagvidhaiḥ
|
nirastajihvādaśanāntralocanairdharā babhau ghoravirūpadarśanā||7||

8praviddhavarmābharaṇāmbarāyudhā vipannahastyaśvarathānugā narāḥ
|
mahārhaśayyāstaraṇocitāstadā kṣitāvanāthā iva śerate hatāḥ||8||

9atīva hṛṣṭāḥ śvaśṛgālavāyasā bakāḥ suparṇāśca vṛkāstarakṣavaḥ
|
vayāṃsyasṛkpānyatha rakṣasāṃ gaṇāḥ piśācasaṅghāśca sudāruṇā raṇe||9||

10tvaco vinirbhidya piban vasāmasṛk tathaiva majjāḥ piśitāni cāśnuvan
|
vapāṃ viluṃpanti hasanti gānti ca prakarṣamāṇāḥ kuṇapānyanekaśaḥ||10||

11śarīrasaṅghātavahā hyasṛgjalā rathoḍupā kuñjaraśailasaṅkaṭā
|
manuṣyaśīrṣopalamāṃsakardamāpraviddhanānāvidhaśastramālinī||11||

12bhayāvahā vaitaraṇīva dustarā pravartitā yodhavaraistadā nadī
|
uvāha madhyena raṇājire bhṛśaṃ bhayāvahā jīvamṛtapravāhinī||12||

13pibanti cāśnanti ca yatra durddaśāḥ piśācasaṃghāstu nadanti bhairavāḥ
|
sunanditāḥ prāṇabhṛtāṃ kṣayaṅkarāḥ samānabhakṣāḥ śvasṛgālapakṣiṇaḥ||13||

14tathā tadāyodhanamugradarśanaṃ niśāmukhe pitṛpatirāṣṭravardhanam
|
nirīkṣamāṇāḥ śanakairjahurnarāḥ samutthitā nṛttakabandhasaṃkulam||14||

15apetavidhvastamahārhabhūṣaṇaṃ nipātitaṃ śakrasamaṃ mahābalam
|
raṇe'bhimanyuṃ dadṛśustadā janā vyapoḍhahavyaṃ sadasīva pāvakam||15||
