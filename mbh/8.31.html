Mahābhārata 8.31 (Karṇaparvan)

1dhṛtarāṣṭra uvāca|
svena cchandena naḥ sarvānavadhīdvyaktamarjunaḥ
|
na hyasya samare mucyedantako'pyātatāyinaḥ ||1||

2pārthaścaiko'haradbhadrāmekaścāgnimatarpayat
|
ekaścemāṃ mahīṃ jitvā cakre balibhṛto nṛpān ||2||

3eko nivātakavacānahanaddivyakārmukaḥ
|
ekaḥ kirātarūpeṇa sthitaṃ śarvamayodhayat ||3||

4eko hyarakṣadbharatāneko bhavamatoṣayat
|
tenaikena jitāḥ sarve mahīpā hyugratejasā ||4||

5na te nindyāḥ praśasyāste yatte cakrurbravīhi tat
|
tato duryodhanaḥ sūta paścātkimakarottadā ||5||

6sañjaya uvāca|
hataprahatavidhvastā vivarmāyudhavāhanāḥ
|
dīnasvarā dūyamānā māninaḥ śatrunirjitāḥ ||6||

7śibirasthāḥ punarmantraṃ matrayanti sma kauravāḥ
|
bhagnadaṃṣṭrā hataviṣāḥ pādākrāntā ivoragāḥ ||7||

8tānabravīttataḥ karṇaḥ kruddhaḥ sarpa iva śvasan
|
karaṃ kareṇa niṣpīḍya prekṣamāṇastavātmajam ||8||

9yatto dṛḍhaśca dakṣaśca dhṛtimānarjunastadā
|
saṃbodhayati cāpyenaṃ yathā kālamadhokṣajaḥ ||9||

10sahasā'stravisargeṇa vayaṃ tenādya vañcitāḥ
|
śvastvahaṃ tasya saṃkalpaṃ sarvaṃ hantā mahīpate ||10||

11evamuktastathetyuktvā so'nujajñe nṛpottamān
|
te'nujñātā nṛpāḥ sarve svāni veśmāni bhejire ||11||

12sukhoṣitāstāṃ rajanīṃ hṛṣṭā yuddhāya niryayuḥ
|
te'paśyanvihitaṃ vyūhaṃ dharmarājena durjayam ||12||

13prayatnātkurumukhyena bṛhaspatyuśanomate
|
atha pratīpakartāraṃ pravīraṃ paravīrahā ||13||

14sasmāra vṛṣabhaskandhaṃ karṇaṃ duryodhanastadā
|
purandarasamaṃ yuddhe marudgaṇasamaṃ bale ||14||

15kārtavīryasamaṃ vīrye karṇe rājño'gamanmanaḥ
|
sarveṣāṃ caiva sainyānāṃ karṇamevāgamanmanaḥ
|
sūtaputraṃ maheṣvāsaṃ bandhumātyayikeṣviva ||15||

16dhṛtarāṣṭra uvāca|
tato duryodhanaḥ sūta paścātkimakarottadā
|
yadvo'gamanmano mandāḥ karṇaṃ vaikartanaṃ prati ||16||

17apyapaśyata rādheyaṃ śītārta iva bhāskaram
|
kṛte'vahāre sainyānāṃ pravṛtte ca raṇe punaḥ ||17||

18kathaṃ vaikartanaḥ karṇastatrāyudhyata sañjaya
|
kathaṃ ca pāṇḍavāḥ sarve yuyudhustatra sūtajam ||18||

19karṇo hyeko mahābāhurhanyātpārthānsasṛṃjayān
|
karṇasya bhujayorvīryaṃ śakraviṣṇusamaṃ yudhi ||19||

20tasya śastrāṇi ghorāṇi vikramaśca mahātmanaḥ
|
karṇamāśritya saṃgrāme matto duryodhano nṛpaḥ ||20||

21duryodhanaṃ tato dṛṣṭvā pāṇḍavena bhṛśārditam
|
parākrāntānpāṇḍusutān dṛṣṭvā cāpi mahārathaḥ ||21||

22karṇamāśritya saṃgrāme mando duryodhanaḥ punaḥ
|
jetumutsahate pārthānsaputrānsaha keśavān ||22||

23aho bata mahadduḥkhaṃ yatra pāṇḍusutānraṇe
|
nātaradrabhasaḥ karṇo 'daivaṃ nūnaṃ parāyaṇam' ||23||

24aho dyutasya niṣṭheyaṃ ghorā saṃprati vartate
|
aho tīvrāṇi duḥkhāni duryodhanakṛtānyaham ||24||

25soḍhā ghorāṇi bahuśaḥ śalyabhūtāni sañjaya
|
saubalaṃ ca tadā tāta nītimāniti manyate ||25||

26karṇaśca rabhaso nitya rājā taṃ cāpyanuvrataḥ
|
yadevaṃ vartamāneṣu mahāyuddheṣu sañjaya ||26||

27aśrauṣaṃ nihatānputrānnityameva vinirjitān
|
na pāṇḍavānāṃ samare kaścidasti nivārakaḥ ||27||

28strīmadhyamiva gāhante 'daivaṃ tu balavattaram'
|
sañjaya uvāca|
rājanpūrvanimittāni dharmiṣṭhāni vicintaya ||28||

29atikrāntaṃ hi yatkāryaṃ paścāccintayate naraḥ
|
taccāsya na bhavetkāryaṃ cintayā ca vinaśyati ||29||

30tadidaṃ tava kāryaṃ tu dūraprāptaṃ vijānatā
|
na kṛtaṃ yattvayā pūrvaṃ prāptāprāptavicāraṇam ||30||

31ukto'si bahudhā rājan mā yudhyasveti pāṇḍavaiḥ
|
gṛhṇīṣe na ca tanmohādvacanaṃ ca viśāṃpate ||31||

32tvayā pāpāni ghorāṇi samācīrṇāni pāṇḍuṣu
|
tvatkṛte vartate ghoraḥ pārthivānāṃ janakṣayaḥ ||32||

33tattvidānīmatikrāntaṃ mā śuco bharatarṣabha
|
śṛṇu sarvaṃ yathāvṛttaṃ ghoraṃ vaiśasamucyate ||33||

34prabhātāyāṃ rajanyāṃ tu karṇo rājānamabhyayāt
|
sametya ca mahābāhurduryodhanamathābravīt ||34||

35karṇa uvāca|
adya rājansameṣyāmi pāṇḍavena yaśasvinā
|
nihaniṣyāmi taṃ vīraṃ sa vā māṃ nihaniṣyati ||35||

36bahutvānmama kāryāṇāṃ tathā pārthasya bhārata
|
nābhūtsamāgamo rājan mama caivārjunasya ca ||36||

37idaṃ tu me yathāprajñaṃ śṛṇu vākyaṃ viśāṃpate
|
anihatya raṇe pārthaṃ nāhameṣyāmi bhārata ||37||

38hatapravīre sainye'sminmayi cāvasthite yudhi
|
abhiyāsyati māṃ pārthaḥ śakraśaktivinākṛtam ||38||

39tataḥ śreyaskaraṃ yacca tannibodha janeśvara
|
āyudhānāṃ ca me vīryaṃ divyānāmarjunasya ca ||39||

40kāryasya mahato bhede lāghave dūrapātane
|
sauṣṭhave cāstrapāte ca savyasācī na matsamaḥ ||40||

41prāṇe śaurye'tha vijñāne vikrame cāpi bhārata
|
nimittajñānayoge ca savyasācī na matsamaḥ||41||

42sarvāyudhamahāmātraṃ vijayaṃ nāma taddhanuḥ
|
indrārthaṃ priyakāmena nirmitaṃ viśvakarmaṇā ||42||

43yena daityagaṇānrājañjitavānvai śatakratuḥ
|
yasya ghoṣeṇa daityānāṃ vyāmuhyanta diśo daśa ||43||

44tadbhārgavāya prāyacchacchakraḥ paramasammatam
|
taddivyaṃ bhārgavo mahyamadadaddhanuruttamam ||44||

45tena yotsye mahābāhumarjunaṃ jayatāṃ varam
|
yathendraḥ samare sarvān daiteyānvai samāgatān ||45||

46dhanurghoraṃ rāmadattaṃ gāṇḍīvāttadviśiṣyate
|
trissaptakṛtvaḥ pṛthivī dhanuṣā yena nirjitā ||46||

47dhanuṣo hyasya karmāṇi divyāni prāha bhārgavaḥ
|
tadrāmo hyadadanmahyaṃ tena yotsyāmi pāṇḍavam ||47||

48adya duryodhanāhaṃ tvāṃ nandayiṣye sabāndhavam
|
nihatya samare vīramarjunaṃ jayatāṃ varam ||48||

49saparvatavanadvīpā hatavīrā sasāgarā
|
putrapautrapratiṣṭhā te bhaviṣyatyadya pārthiva ||49||

50nāśakyaṃ vidyate me'dya tvatpriyārthaṃ viśeṣataḥ
|
samyagdharmānuraktasya siddhirātmavato yathā ||50||

51na hi māṃ samare soḍhuṃ saṃśakto'gniṃ taruryathā
|
avaśyaṃ tu mayā vācyaṃ yena hīno'smi phālgunāt ||51||

52jyā tasya dhanuṣo divyā tathā'kṣayye maheṣudhī
|
sārathistasya govindo mama tādṛṅna vidyate ||52||

53tasya divyaṃ dhanuḥ śreṣṭhaṃ gāṇḍīvamajitaṃ yudhi
|
vijayaṃ ca mahaddivyaṃ mamāpi dhanuruttamam ||53||

54tatrāhamadhikaḥ pārthāddhanuṣā tena pārthiva
|
yena cāpyadhiko vīraḥ pāṇḍavastannibodha me ||54||

55raśmigrāhaśca dāśārhaḥ sarvalokanamaskṛtaḥ
|
agnidattaśca vai divyo rathaḥ kāñcanabhūṣaṇaḥ ||55||

56acchedyaḥ sarvato vīra vājinaśca manojavāḥ
|
dhvajaśca divyo dyutimān vānaro vismayaṅkaraḥ ||56||

57kṛṣṇaśca sraṣṭā jagato rathaṃ tamabhirakṣati
|
etairdravyairahaṃ hīno yoddhumicchāmi pāṇḍavam ||57||

58ayaṃ tu sadṛśaḥ śaureḥ śalyaḥ samitiśobhanaḥ
|
sārathyaṃ yadi me kuryāddhruvaste vijayo bhavet ||58||

59tasya me sārathiḥ śalyo bhavatvasukaraḥ paraiḥ
|
nārācāngārdhrapatrāṃśca śakaṭāni vahantu me ||59||

60rathāśca mukhyā rājendra yuktā vājibhiruttamaiḥ
|
āyāntu paścātsatataṃ māmeva bharatarṣabha ||60||

61evamabhyadhikaḥ pārthādbhaviṣyāmi guṇairaham
|
śalyopyabhyadhikaḥ kṛṣṇādarjunādapi cāpyaham ||61||

62yathā'śvahṛdayaṃ veda dāśārhaḥ paravīrahā
|
tathā śalyo vijānīte hayajñānaṃ mahārathaḥ ||62||

63bāhuvīrye samo nāsti madrarājasya kaścana
|
tathā'stre matsamo nāsti kaścideva dhanurdharaḥ ||63||

64tathā śalyasamo nāsti hayajñāne hi kaścana
|
so'yamabhyadhikaḥ kṛṣṇādbhaviṣyati ratho mama ||64||

65evaṃ kṛte rathastho'haṃ guṇairabhyadhiko'rjunāt
|
bhave yudhi jayeyaṃ ca phālgunaṃ kurusattama ||65||

66samudyātuṃ na śakṣyanti devā api savāsavāḥ
|
etatkṛtaṃ mahārāja tvayecchāmi paraṃtapa ||66||

67kriyatāmeṣa kāmo me mā vaḥ kālo'tyagādayam
|
evaṃ kṛte kṛtaṃ sahyaṃ sarvakāmairbhaviṣyati ||67||

68tato drakṣyasi saṃgrāme yatkariṣyāmi bhārata
|
sarvathā pāṇḍavānsaṃkhye vijeṣye vai samāgatān ||68||

69na hi me samare śaktāḥ samudyātuṃ surāsurāḥ
|
kimu pāṇḍusutā rājan raṇe mānuṣayonayaḥ ||69||

70sañjaya uvāca|
evamuktastava sutaḥ karṇenāhavaśobhinā
|
saṃpūjya saṃprahṛṣṭātmā tato rādheyamabravīt ||70||

71duryodhana uvāca|
evametatkariṣyāmi yathā tvaṃ karṇa manyase
|
sopāsaṅgā rathāḥ sāśvāḥ svanuyāsyanti saṃyuge ||71||

72nārācāngārghrapatrāṃśca śakaṭāni vahantu te
|
anuyāsyāma karṇa tvāṃ vayaṃ sarve ca pārthivāḥ ||72||

73sañjaya uvāca|
evamuktvā mahārāja tava putraḥ pratāpavān
|
abhigamyābravīdrājā madrarājamidaṃ vacaḥ ||73||
