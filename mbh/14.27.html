Mahābhārata 14.27 (Āśvamedhikaparvan)

1brāhmaṇa uvāca|
saṅkalpadaṃśamaśakaṃ śokaharṣahimātapam
|
mohāndhakāratimiraṃ lobhavyādhisarīsṛpam||1||

2viṣayaikātyayādhvānaṃ kāmakrodhavirodhakam
|
tadatītya mahādurgaṃ praviṣṭo'smi mahad vanam||2||

3brāhmaṇyuvāca|
kva tadvanaṃ mahāprājña ke vṛkṣāḥ saritaśca kāḥ
|
girayaḥ parvatāścaiva kiyatyadhvani tadvanam||3||

4brāhmaṇa uvāca|
naitadasti pṛthagbhāvaḥ kiñcidanyattataḥ sukham
|
naitadastyapṛthagbhāvaḥ kiñcidduḥkhataraṃ tataḥ||4||

5tasmāddhrasvataraṃ nāsti na tato'sti mahattaram
|
nāsti tasmātsūkṣmataraṃ nāstyanyattatsamaṃ sukham||5||

6na tatrāviśya śocanti na prahaṣyanti ca dvijāḥ
|
na ca bibhyati keṣāñcittebhyo bibhyati kecana||6||

7tasminvane sapta mahādrumāśca phalāni saptātithayaśca sapta
|
saptāśramāḥ sapta samādhayaśca dīkṣāśca saptaitadaraṇyarūpam||7||

8pañcavarṇāni divyāni puṣpāṇi ca phalāni ca
|
sṛjantaḥ pādapāstatra vyāpya tiṣṭhanti tad vanam||8||

9suvarṇāni dvivarṇāni puṣpāṇi ca phalāni ca
|
sṛjantaḥ pādapāstatra vyāpya tiṣṭhanti tad vanam||9||

10surabhīṇi dvivarṇāni puṣpāṇi ca phalāni ca
|
sṛjantaḥ pādapāstatra vyāpya tiṣṭhanti tad vanam||10||

11surabhīṇyekavarṇāni puṣpāṇi ca phalāni ca
|
sṛjantaḥ pādapāstatra vyāpya tiṣṭhanti tad vanam||11||

12bahūnyavyaktavarṇāni puṣpāṇi ca phalāni ca
|
visṛjantau mahāvṛkṣau tadvanaṃ vyāpya tiṣṭhataḥ||12||

13eko vahniḥ sumanā brāhmaṇo'tra pañcendriyāṇi samidhaścātra santi
|
tebhyo mokṣāḥ sapta phalanti dīkṣā guṇāḥ phalānyatithayaḥ phalāśāḥ||13||

14ātithyaṃ pratigṛhṇanti tatra tatra maharṣayaḥ
|
arciteṣu pralīneṣu teṣvanyadrocate vanam||14||

15prajñāvṛkṣaṃ mokṣaphalaṃ śānticchāyāsamanvitam
|
jñānāśrayaṃ tṛptitoyamantaḥkṣetrajñabhāskaram||15||

16ye'dhigacchanti taṃ santasteṣāṃ nāsti bhayaṃ punaḥ
|
ūrdhvaṃ cādhaśca tiryakca tasya nānto'dhigamyate||16||

17sapta striyastatra vasantui sadyastvavāṅmukhā bhānumatyo janitryaḥ
|
ardhvaṃ rasānādadate prajābhyaḥ sarvān yathā satyamanityatā ca||17||

18tatraiva pratitiṣṭhanti punastatropayanti ca
|
sapta saptarṣayaḥ siddhā vasiṣṭhapramukhaiḥ saha||18||

19yaśo vartho bhagaścaiva vijayaḥ siddhatejasaḥ
|
evamevānuvartante sapta jyotīṃṣi bhāskaram||19||

20girayaḥ parvatāścaiva santi tatra samāsataḥ
|
nadyaśca sarito vāri vahantyo brahmasambhavam||20||

21nadīnāṃ saṅgamaścaiva vaitāne samupahvare
|
svātmatṛptā yato yānti sākṣādeva pitāmaham||21||

22kṛśāśāḥ suvratāśāśca tapasā dagdhakilbiṣāḥ
|
ātmanyātmānamāviśya brahmāṇaṃ samupāsate||22||

23śamamapyatra śaṃsanti vidyāraṇyavido janāḥ
|
tadāraṇyamabhipretya yathādhīrabhijāyata||23||

24etadevedṛśaṃ puṇyamaraṇyaṃ brāhmaṇā viduḥ
|
viditvā cānutiṣṭhanti kṣetrajñenānudarśinā||24||
