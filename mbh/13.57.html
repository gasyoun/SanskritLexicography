Mahābhārata 13.57 (Anuśāsanaparvan)

1yudhiṣṭhira uvāca|
muhyāmīva niśamyādya cintayānaḥ punaḥ punaḥ
|
hīnāṃ pārthivasaṃghātaiḥ śrīmadbhiḥ pṛthivīmimām ||1||

2prāpya rājyāni śataśo mahīṃ jitvā'tha bhārata
|
koṭiśaḥ puruṣānhatvā paritapye pitāmaha ||2||

3kā nu tāsāṃ varastrīṇāṃ samavasthā bhaviṣyati
|
yā hīnāḥ patibhiḥ putrairmātulairbhrātṛbhistathā ||3||

4vayaṃ hi tān kurūnhatvā śātīṃśca suhṛdo'pi vā
|
avākśīrṣāḥ patiṣyāmo narake nātra saṃśayaḥ ||4||

5śarīraṃ yoktumicchāmi tapasogreṇa bhārata
|
upadiṣṭamihecchāmi tattvato'haṃ viśāṃpate ||5||

6vaiśampāyana uvāca|
yudhiṣṭhirasya tadvākyaṃ śrutvā bhīṣmo mahāmanāḥ
|
parīkṣya nipuṇaṃ buddhyā yudhiṣṭhiramabhāṣata ||6||

7rahasyamadbhutaṃ caiva śṛṇu vakṣyāmi yattvayi
|
yā gatiḥ prāpyate yena pretyabhāve viśāṃpate ||7||

8tapasā prāpyate svargastapasā prāpyate yaśaḥ
|
āyuḥ prakarṣo bhogāśca labhyante tapasā vibho ||8||

9jñānaṃ vijñānamārogyaṃ rūpaṃ saṃpattathaiva ca
|
saubhāgyaṃ caiva tapasā prāpyate bharatarṣabha ||9||

10dhanaṃ prāpnoti tapasā maunenājñāṃ prayacchati
|
upabhogāṃstu dānena brahmacaryeṇa jīvitam ||10||

11ahiṃsāyāḥ phalaṃ rūpaṃ dīkṣāyā janma vai kule
|
phalamūlāśināṃ rājyaṃ svargaḥ parṇāśināṃ bhavet ||11||

12payobhakṣo divaṃ yāti dānena draviṇādhikaḥ
|
guruśuśrūṣayā vidyā nityaśrāddhena santatiḥ ||12||

13gavāḍhyaḥ śākadīkṣābhiḥ svargamāhustṛṇāśinām
|
striyastriṣavaṇaṃ snātvā vāyuṃ pītvā kratuṃ labhet ||13||

14nityasnāyī bhaveddakṣaḥ sandhye tu dve japandvijaḥ
|
maruṃ sādhayato rājannākapṛṣṭhamanāśake ||14||

15sthaṇḍile śayamānānāṃ gṛhāṇi śayanāni ca
|
cīravalkalavāsobhirvāsāṃsyābharaṇāni ca ||15||

16śayyāsanāni yānāni yogayukte tapodhane
|
agnipraveśe niyataṃ brahmaloke mahīyate ||16||

17rasānāṃ pratisaṃhārātsaubhāgyamiha vindati
|
āmiṣapratisaṃhārātprajā hyāyuṣmatī bhavet ||17||

18udavāsaṃ vasedyastu sa narādhipatirbhavet
|
satyavādī naraśreṣṭha daivataiḥ saha modate ||18||

19kīrtirbhavati dānena tathā''rogyamahiṃsayā
|
dvijaśuśrūṣayā rājyaṃ dvijatvaṃ cāpi puṣkalam ||19||

20pānīyasya pradānena kīrtirbhavati śāśvatī
|
annasya tu pradānena tṛpyante kāmabhogataḥ ||20||

21sāntvadaḥ sarvabhūtānāṃ sarvaśokairvimucyate
|
devaśuśrūṣayā rājyaṃ divyaṃ rūpaṃ niyacchati ||21||

22dīpālokapradānena cakṣuṣmānbhavate naraḥ
|
prekṣaṇīyapradānena smṛtiṃ medhāṃ ca vindati ||22||

23gandhamālyapradānena kīrtirbhavati puṣkalā
|
keśaśmaśru dhārayatāmagryā bhavati saṃtatiḥ ||23||

24upavāsaṃ ca dīkṣāṃ ca abhiṣekaṃ ca pārthiva
|
kṛtvā dvādaśavarṣāṇi vīrasthānādviśiṣyate ||24||

25dāsīdāsamalaṅkārānkṣetrāṇi ca gṛhāṇi ca
|
brahmadeyāṃ sutāṃ datvā prāpnoti manujarṣabha ||25||

26kratubhiścopavāsaiśca tridivaṃ yāti bhārata
|
labhate ca śivaṃ jñānaṃ phalapuṣpaprado naraḥ ||26||

27suvarṇaśṛṃgaistu virājitānāṃ gavāṃ sahasrasya naraḥ pradānāt
|
prāpnoti puṇyaṃ divi devalokamityevamāhurdivi devasaṃghāḥ ||27||

28prayacchate yaḥ kapilāṃ savatsāṃ kāṃsyopadohāṃ kanakāgraśṛṅgīm
|
taistairguṇaiḥ kāmaduhāsya bhūtvā naraṃ pradātāramupaiti sā gauḥ ||28||

29yāvanti romāṇi bhavanti dhenvāstāvatkālaṃ prāpya sa gopradānāt
|
putrāṃśca pautrāṃśca kulaṃ ca sarvamāsaptamaṃ tārayate paratra ||29||

30sadakṣiṇāṃ kāñcanacāruśṛṅgīṃ kāṃsyopadohāṃ draviṇottarīyām
|
dhenuṃ tilānāṃ dadato dvijāya lokā vasūnāṃ sulabhā bhavanti ||30||

31svakarmabhirmānavaṃ sanniruddhaṃ tīvrāndhakāre narake patantam
|
mahārṇave nauriva vāyuyuktā dānaṃ gavāṃ tārayate paratra ||31||

32yo brahmadeyāṃ tu dadāti kanyāṃ bhūmipradānaṃ ca karoti vipre
|
dadāti cānnaṃ vidhivacca yaśca sa lokamāpnoti purandarasya ||32||

33naiveśikaṃ sarvaguṇopapannaṃ dadāti vai yastu naro dvijāya
|
svādhyāyacāritryaguṇānvitāya tasyāpi lokāḥ kuruṣūttareṣu ||33||

34dhuryapradānena gavāṃ tathā vai lokānavāpnoti naro vasūnām
|
svargāya cāhustu hiraṇyadānaṃ tato viśiṣṭaṃ kanakapradānam ||34||

35chatrapradānena gṛhaṃ variṣṭhaṃ yānaṃ tathopānahasaṃpradāne
|
vastrapradānena phalaṃ svarūpaṃ gandhapradānātsurabhirnaraḥ syāt ||35||

36puṣpopagaṃ vā'tha phalopagaṃ vā yaḥ pādapaṃ sparśayate dvijāya
|
saśrīkamṛddhaṃ bahuratnapūrṇaṃ labhatyayatnopagataṃ gṛhaṃ vai ||36||

37bhakṣyānnapānīyarasapradātā sarvānsamāpnoti rasānprakāmam
|
pratiśrayācchādanasaṃpradātā prāpnoti tānyeva na saṃśayo'tra ||37||

38sragdhūpagandhānanulepanāni snānāni mālyāni ca mānavo yaḥ
|
dadyāddvijebhyaḥ sa bhavedarogastathābhirūpaśca narendraloke ||38||

39bījairaśūnyaṃ śayanairupetaṃ dadyādgṛhaṃ yaḥ puruṣo dvijāya
|
puṇyābhirāmaṃ bahuratnapūrṇaṃ labhatyadhiṣṭhānavaraṃ sa rājan ||39||

40sugandhacitrāstaraṇopadhānaṃ dadyānnaro yaḥ śayanaṃ dvijāya
|
rūpānvitāṃ pakṣavatīṃ manojñāṃ bhāryāmayatnopagatāṃ labhetsaḥ ||40||

41pitāmahasyānavaro vīraśāyī bhavennaraḥ
|
nādhikaṃ vidyate yasmādityāhuḥ paramarṣayaḥ ||41||

42vaiśampāyana uvāca|
tasya tadvacanaṃ śrutvā prītātmā kurunandanaḥ
|
nāśrame'rocayadvāsaṃ vīramārgābhikāṃkṣayā ||42||

43tato yudhiṣṭhiraḥ prāha pāṇḍavānpuruṣarṣabha
|
pitāmahasya yadvākyaṃ tadvo rocatviti prabhuḥ ||43||

44tatastu pāṇḍavāḥ sarve draupadī ca yaśasvinī
|
yudhiṣṭhirasya tadvākyaṃ bāḍhamityabhyapūjayan ||44||
