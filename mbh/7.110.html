Mahābhārata 7.110 (Droṇaparvan)

1dhṛtarāṣṭra uvāca|
bhāradvājaṃ kathaṃ yuddhe yuyudhāno nyavārayat
|
sañjayācakṣva tattvena paraṃ kautūhalaṃ hi me ||1||

2sañjaya uvāca|
śṛṇu rājan mahāprājña saṃgrāmaṃ lomaharṣaṇam
|
droṇasya pāṇḍavaiḥ sārdhaṃ yuyudhānapurogamaiḥ ||2||

3vadhyamānaṃ balaṃ dṛṣṭvā yuyudhānena māriṣa
|
abhyadravatsvayaṃ droṇaḥ sātyakiṃ satyavikramam ||3||

4tamāpatantaṃ sahasā bhāradvājaṃ mahāratham
|
sātyakiḥ pañcaviṃśatyā kṣudrakāṇāṃ samāparyat ||4||

5droṇo'pi yudhi vikrānto yuyudhānaṃ samāhitaḥ
|
avidhyatpañcabhistūrṇaṃ hemapuṃkhaiḥ śaraiḥ śitaiḥ ||5||

6te varma bhittvā sudṛḍhaṃ dviṣatpiśitabhojanāḥ
|
abhyayudharaṇīṃ rājan śvasanta iva pannagāḥ ||6||

7dīrghabāhurabhikruddhastotrārdita iva dvipaḥ
|
droṇaṃ pañcāśatā'vidhyannārācairagnisannibhaiḥ ||7||

8bhāradvājo raṇe viddho yuyudhānena satvaram
|
sātyakiṃ bahubhirbāṇairyatamānamavidhyata ||8||

9tataḥ kruddho maheṣvāso bhūya eva mahābalaḥ
|
sātvataṃ pīḍayāmāsa śareṇānataparvaṇā ||9||

10sa vadhyamānaḥ samare bhāradvājena sātyakiḥ
|
nānvapadyata kartavyaṃ kiñcideva viśāmpate ||10||

11viṣaṇṇavadanaścāpi yuyudhāno'bhavannṛpa
|
bhāradvājaṃ raṇe dṛṣṭvā visṛjantaṃ śitāñśarān ||11||

12taṃ tu saṃprekṣya te putrāḥ saunikāśca viśāṃpate
|
prahṛṣṭamanaso bhūtvā siṃhavadvyanadanmuhuḥ ||12||

13taṃ śrutvā ninadaṃ ghoraṃ pīḍyamānaṃ ca mādhavam
|
yudhiṣṭhiro'bravīdrājā sarvasainyāni bhārata ||13||

14eṣa vṛṣṇivaro vīraḥ sātyakiḥ satyavikramaḥ
|
grasyate yudhi vīreṇa bhānumāniva rāhuṇā ||14||

15abhidravata gacchadhvaṃ sātyakiryatra yudhyate
|
dhṛṣṭadyumnaṃ ca pāñcālyamida‌māha janādhipaḥ ||15||

16abhidrava drutaṃ droṇaṃ kimu tiṣṭhasi pārṣata
|
na paśyasi bhayaṃ droṇāddhoraṃ naḥ samupasthitam ||16||

17asau droṇo maheṣvāso yuyudhānena saṃyuge
|
krīḍate sūtrabaddhena pakṣiṇā bālako yathā ||17||

18tatraiva sarve gacchantu bhīmasenapurogamāḥ
|
tvayaiva sahitāḥ sarvaṃ yuyudhānarathaṃ prati ||18||

19pṛṣṭhato'nugamiṣyāmi tvāmahaṃ sahasainikaḥ
|
sātyakiṃ mokṣayasvādya yamadaṃṣṭrāntaraṃ gatam ||19||

20evamuktvā tato rājā sarvasainyena bhārata
|
abhyadravadraṇe droṇaṃ yuyudhānasya kāraṇāt ||20||

21tatrārāvo mahānāsīddroṇameka yuyutsatām
|
pāṇḍavānāṃ ca bhadraṃ te sṛñjayānāṃ ca sarvaśaḥ ||21||

22te sametya naravyāghrā bhāradvājaṃ mahāratham
|
abhyavarṣan śaraistīkṣṇaiḥ kaṅkabarhiṇavājitaiḥ ||22||

23smayanneva tu tān ‌vīrān droṇaḥ pratyagrahīt svayaṃ
|
atithīnāgatān yadvat salilenāsanena ca ||23||

24tarpitāste śaraistasya bhāradvājasya dhanvinaḥ
|
ātitheyaṃ gṛhaṃ prāpya nṛpate'tithayo yathā ||24||

25bhāradvājaṃ ca te sarve na śekuḥ prativīkṣitum
|
madhyandinamanuprāptaṃ sahastrāṃśumiva prabho ||25||

26tāṃstu sarvānmaheṣvāsān droṇaḥ śasrabhṛtāṃ varaḥ
|
atāpayaccharavrātaigabhastibhirivāṃśumān ||26||

27vadhyamānā mahārāja pāṇḍavāḥ sṛñjayāstathā
|
trātāraṃ nādhyagacchanta paṅkamagnā iva dvipāḥ ||27||

28droṇasya ca vyadṛśyanta visarpanto mahāśarāḥ
|
gabhastaya ivārkasya pratapantaḥ samantataḥ ||28||

29tasmindroṇena nihatāḥ pañcālāḥ pañcaviṃśatiḥ
|
mahārathāḥ samākhyātā dhṛṣṭadyumnasya saṃmatāḥ ||29||

30pāṇḍūnāṃ sarvasainyeṣu pañcālānāṃ tathaiva ca
|
droṇaṃ sma dadṛśuḥ śūraṃ vinighnantaṃ varān varān ||30||

31kekayānāṃ śataṃ hatvā vidrāvya ca samantataḥ
|
droṇastasthau mahārāja vyāditāsya ivāntakaḥ ||31||

32pañcālān sṛñjayān matsyān kekayāṃśca narādhipa
|
droṇo'jayanmahābāhuḥ śataśo'tha sahasraśaḥ ||32||

33teṣāṃ samabhavacchabdo viddhānāṃ droṇasāyakaiḥ
|
vanaukasāmivāraṇye vyāptānāṃ dhūmaketunā ||33||

34tatra devāḥ sagandharvāḥ pitaraścābruvannṛpa
|
ete dravanti pañcālāḥ pāṇḍavāśca sasainikāḥ ||34||

35taṃ tathā samare droṇaṃ nighnantaṃ somakān raṇe
|
na cāpyabhiyayuḥ kecidapare naiva vivyadhuḥ ||35||

36vartamāne tathā raudre tasmin vīravarakṣaye
|
aśśṛṇot sahasā pārthaḥ pāñcajanyasya niḥsvanam ||36||

37pūrito vāsudevena śaṃkharāṭ svanate bhṛśam
|
yudhyamāneṣu vīreṣu saindhavasyābhirakṣiṣu ||37||

38nadatsu dhārtarāṣṭreṣu vijayasya rathaṃ prati
|
gāṇḍīvasya ca nirghoṣe vipranaṣṭe samantataḥ ||38||

39kaśmalābhihato rājā cintayāmāsa pāṇḍavaḥ
|
na nūnaṃ svasti pārthāya yathā nadati śakharāṭ ||39||

40kauravāśca yathā hṛṣṭā vinadanti muhurmuhuḥ
|
evaṃ sa cintayitvā tu vyākulenāntarātmanā ||40||

41ajātaśatruḥ kaunteyaḥ sātvataṃ pratyabhāṣata
|
bāṣpagadgadayā vācā muhyamāno muhurmuhuḥ
|
kṛtyasyānantarāpekṣī śaineyaṃ śinipuṅgavam ||41||

42yudhiṣṭhira uvāca|
yaḥ sa dharmaḥ purā dṛṣṭaḥ sadbhiḥ śaineya śāśvataḥ
|
sāmparāye suhṛtkṛtye tasya kālo'yamāgataḥ ||42||

43sarveṣvapi ca yodheṣu cintayan śinipuṅgava
|
tvattaḥ suhṛttamaṃ kañcinnābhijānāmi sātyake ||43||

44yo hi prītamanā nityaṃ yaśca nityamanuvrataḥ
|
sa kārye sāṃparāye tu niyojya iti me matiḥ ||44||

45yathā ca keśavo nityaṃ pāṇḍavānāṃ parāyaṇam
|
tathā tvamapi vārṣṇeya kṛṣṇatulyaparākramaḥ ||45||

46so'haṃ bhāraṃ samādhāsye tvavi taṃ voḍhumarhasi
|
abhiprāyaṃ ca me nityaṃ na vṛthā kartumarhasi ||46||

47sa tvaṃ bhrāturvayasyasya gurorapi ca saṃyuge
|
kuru kṛcchre sahāyārthamarjunasya nararṣabha ||47||

48tvaṃ hi satyavrataḥ śūro mitrāṇāmabhayaṃkaraḥ
|
loke vikhyāyase vīra karmabhiḥ satyavāgiti ||48||

49yo hi śaineya mitrārthe yudhyamānastyajettanum
|
pṛthivīṃ ca dvijātibhyo yo dadyāt sa samo bhavet ||49||

50śrutāśca bahavo'smābhī rājāno ye divaṃ gatāḥ
|
datvemāṃ pṛthivīṃ kṛtsnāṃ brāhmaṇebhyo yathāvidhi ||50||

51evaṃ tvāmapi dharmātman prayāce'haṃ kṛtāñjaliḥ
|
pṛthivīdānatulyaṃ syādadhikaṃ vā phalaṃ vibho ||51||

52eka eva sadā kṛṣṇo mitrāṇāmabhayaṃkaraḥ
|
raṇe santyajati prāṇān ‌dvitīyastvaṃ ca sātyake ||52||

53vikrāntasya ca vīrasya yuddhe prārthayato yaśaḥ
|
śūra eva sahāyaḥ syānnetaraḥ prākṛto janaḥ ||53||

54īdṛśe tu parāmarśa vartamānasya mādhava
|
tvadanyo hi raṇe goptā vijayasya na vidyate ||54||

55ślāghanneva hi karmāṇi śataśastava pāṇḍavaḥ
|
mama sañjanayan harṣaṃ punaḥ punarakīrtayat ||55||

56laghuhastaścitrayodhī tathā laghuparākramaḥ
|
prājñaḥ sarvāstravicchūro muhyate na ca sayuge ||56||

57mahāskandho mahorasko mahābāhurmahāhanuḥ
|
mahābalo mahāvīryaḥ sa mahātmā mahārathaḥ ||57||

58śiṣyo mama sakhā caiva priyo'syāhaṃ priyaśca me
|
yuyudhānaḥ sahāyo me pramathiṣyati kauravān ||58||

59asmadarthaṃ ca rājendra saṃnohyedyadi keśavaḥ
|
rāmo vāpyaniruddho vā pradyumno vā mahārathaḥ ||59||

60gado vā sāraṇo vāpi sāmbo vā saha vṛṣṇibhiḥ
|
sahāyārthaṃ mahārāja saṃgrāmottamamūrdhani ||60||

61tathāpyahaṃ naravyāghraṃ śaineyaṃ satyavikramam
|
sāhāyye viniyokṣyāmi nāsti me'nyo hi tatsamaḥ ||61||

62iti dvaitavane tāta māmuvāca dhanaṃjayaḥ
|
parokṣe tvad‌guṇāṃstathyān kathayannāryasaṃsadi ||62||

63tasya tvamevaṃ saṃkalpaṃ na vṛthā kartumarhasi
|
dhanañjayasya vārṣṇeya mama bhīmasya cobhayoḥ ||63||

64yaccāpi tīrthāni carannagacchaṃ dvārakāṃ prati
|
tatrāhamapi te bhaktimarjunaṃ prati dṛṣṭavān ||64||

65na tatsauhṛdamanyeṣu mayā śaineya lakṣitam
|
yathā tvamasmān bhajase vartamānānupaplave ||65||

66so'bhijātyā ca bhaktyā ca sakhyasyācāryakasya ca
|
sauhṛdasya ca vīryasya kulīnatvasya mādhava ||66||

67satyasya ca mahābāho anukampārthameva ca
|
anurūpaṃ maheṣvāsa karma tvaṃ kartumarhasi ||67||

68suyodhano hi sahasā gato droṇena daṃśitaḥ
|
pūrvamevānuyātāste kauravāṇāṃ mahārathāḥ ||68||

69sumahānninadaścaiva śrūyate vijayaṃ prati
|
sa śaineya javenāśu gantumarhasi mānada ||69||

70bhīmaseno vayaṃ caiva saṃyattāḥ sahasainikāḥ
|
droṇamāvārayiṣyāmo yadi tvāṃ prati yāsyati ||70||

71paśya śaineya sainyāni dravamāṇāni saṃyuge
|
mahāntaṃ ca raṇe śabdaṃ dīryamāṇāṃ ca bhāratīm ||71||

72mahāmārutavegena samudramiva parvasu
|
dhārtarāṣṭrabalaṃ tāta vikṣiptaṃ savyasācinā ||72||

73rathairviparidhāvadbhirmanuṣyaiśca hayaiśca ha
|
sainyaṃ rajaḥsamuddhūtametat saṃparivartate ||73||

74saṃvṛtaḥ sindhusauvīrairnakharaprāsayodhibhiḥ
|
atyantopacitaiḥ śūraiḥ phālgunaḥ paravīrahā ||74||

75naitadbalamasaṃvāryaṃ śakyo jetuṃ jayadrathaḥ
|
ete hi saindhavasyārthe sarve santyaktajīvitāḥ ||75||

76śaraśaktidhvajavaraṃ hayanāgasamākulam
|
paśyaitaddhārtarāṣṭrāṇāmanīkaṃ sudurāsadam ||76||

77śṛṇu dundubhinirghoṣaṃ śaṅkhaśabdāṃśca puṣkalān
|
siṃhanādaravāṃścaiva rathanemisvanāṃstathā ||77||

78nāgānāṃ śṛṇu śabdaṃ ca pattīnāṃ ca sahasraśaḥ
|
sādināṃ dravatāṃ caiva śṛṇu kaṃpayatāṃ mahīm ||78||

79purastātsaindhavānīkaṃ doṇānīkaṃ ca pṛṣṭhataḥ
|
bahutvāddhi naravyāghra devendramapi pīḍayet ||79||

80aparyante bale magno jahyādapi ca jīvitam
|
tasmiṃśca nihate yuddhe kathaṃ jīveta mādṛśaḥ ||80||

81sarvathā'hamanuprāptaḥ sukṛcchraṃ tvayi jīvati
|
śyāmo yuvā guḍākeśo darśanīyaśca pāṇḍavaḥ ||81||

82laghvastraścitrayādhī ca praviṣṭastāta bhāratīm
|
sūryodaye mahābāhurdivasaścātivartate ||82||

83tanna jānāmi vārṣṇeya yadi jīvati vā navā
|
kurūṇāṃ cāpi tatsainyaṃ sāgarapratimaṃ mahat ||83||

84eka eva ca bībhatsuḥ praviṣṭastāta bhāratīm
|
aviṣahyāṃ mahābāhuḥ surairapi mahāhave ||84||

85na hi me vartate buddhiradya yuddhe kathañcana
|
droṇo'pi rabhaso yuddhe mama pīḍayate balam ||85||

86pratyakṣaṃ te mahābāho yathā'sau carati dvijaḥ
|
yugapacca sametānāṃ kāryāṇāṃ tvaṃ vicakṣaṇaḥ ||86||

87mahārthaṃ laghusaṃyuktaṃ kartumarhasi mānada
|
tasya me sarvakāryeṣu kāryametanmataṃ mahat ||87||

88arjunasya paritrāṇaṃ kartavyamiti saṃyuge
|
nāhaṃ śocāmi dāśārhaṃ goptāraṃ jagataḥ patim ||88||

89sa hi śakto raṇe tāta trīn lokānapi saṅgatān
|
vijetuṃ puruṣavyāghraḥ satyametadbravīmi te ||89||

90kiṃ punardhārtarāṣṭrasya balametat sudurbalam
|
arjunastveṣa vārṣṇeya pīḍito bahubhiryudhi ||90||

91prajahyāt samare prāṇāṃstasmādvindāmi kaśmalam
|
tasya tvaṃ padavīṃ gaccha gaccheyustvādṛśā yathā ||91||

92tādṛśasyedṛśe kāle mādṛśenābhinoditaḥ
|
raṇe vṛṣṇipravīrāṇāṃ dvāvevātirathau smṛtau ||92||

93pradyumnaśca mahābāhustvaṃ ca sātvata viśrutaḥ
|
astre nārāyaṇasamaḥ saṃkarṣaṇasamo bale ||93||

94vīratāyāṃ naravyāghra dhanañjayasamo hyasi
|
bhīṣmadroṇāvatikramya sarvayuddhaviśāradam ||94||

95tvāmeva puruṣavyāghraṃ loke santaḥ pracakṣate
|
nāśakyaṃ vidyate loke sātyakeriti mādhava ||95||

96tattvāṃ yada‌bhivakṣyāmi tatkuruṣva mahābalaḥ
|
saṃbhāvanā hi lokasya mama pārthasya cobhayoḥ ||96||

97nānyathā tāṃ mahābāho saṃprakartumihārhasi
|
parityajya priyān prāṇānraṇe cara vibhītavat ||97||

98na hi śaineya dāśārho raṇe rakṣanti jīvitam
|
ayuddhamanavasthānaṃ saṃgrāme ca palāyanam ||98||

99bhīruṇāmasatāṃ mārgo naiṣa dāśārhasevitaḥ
|
tavārjuno gurustāta dharmātmā śinipuṅgavaḥ ||99||

100vāsudevo guruścāpi tava pārthasya dhīmataḥ
|
kāraṇadvayametaddhi jānaṃstvāmahamabruvam ||100||

101māvamaṃsthā vaco mahyaṃ gurustava gurorhyaham
|
vāsudevamataṃ caiva mama caivārjunasya ca ||101||

102satyametanmayoktaṃ te yāhi yatra dhanañjayaḥ
|
etadvacanamājñāya mama satyaparākrama ||102||

103praviśaitadbalaṃ tāta dhārtarāṣṭrasya durmateḥ
|
praviśya ca yathānyāyaṃ saṃgamya ca mahārathaiḥ
|
yathāhamātmanaḥ karma raṇe sātvata darśaya ||103||
