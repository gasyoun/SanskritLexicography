Mahābhārata 7.186 (Droṇaparvan)

1sañjaya uvāca|
tribhāgamātraśeṣāyāṃ rātryāṃ yuddhamavartata
|
kurūṇāṃ pāṇḍavānāṃ ca saṃmṛṣṭānāṃ viśāṃpate||1||

2atha candraprabhāṃ muṣṇannādityasya puraḥsaraḥ
|
aruṇo'bhyudayāṃcakre tāmrīkurvannivāmbaram||2||

3prācyāṃ diśi sahasrāṃśoraruṇenāruṇīkṛtam
|
tāpanīyaṃ yathā cakraṃ bhrājate ravimaṇḍalam||3||

4tato rathāśvāṃśca manuṣyayānānyutsṛjya sarve kurupāṇḍuyodhāḥ
|
divākarasyābhimukhaṃ japantaḥ sandhyāgatāḥ prāñjalayo babhūvuḥ||4||

5tato dvaidhīkṛte sainye droṇaḥ somakapāṇḍavān
|
abhyadravatsapañcālānduryodhanapurogamaḥ||5||

6dvaidhīkṛtānkurūn dṛṣṭvā mādhavo'rjunamabravīt
|
sapatnānsavyataḥ kṛtvā apasavyamimaṃ kuru||6||

7sa mādhavamanujñāya kuruṣveti dhanañjayaḥ
|
droṇakarṇau maheṣvāsau savyataḥ paryavartata||7||

8abhiprāyaṃ tu kṛṣṇasya jñātvā parapurañjayaḥ
|
ājiśīrṣagataṃ pārthaṃ bhīmaseno'bhyuvāca ha||8||

9bhīmasena uvāca|
arjunārjuna bībhatso śṛṇuṣvaitadvaco mama
|
yadarthaṃ kṣatriyā sūte tasya kālo'yamāgata||9||

10asmiṃścedāgate kāle śreyo na pratipatsyase
|
asaṃbhāvitarūpastvaṃ sunṛśaṃsaṃ kariṣyasi||10||

11satya-śrī-dharma-yaśasāṃ vīryeṇānṛṇyamāpnuhi
|
bhindhyanīkaṃ yudhāṃ śreṣṭha apasavyamimānkuru||11||

12sañjaya uvāca|
sa savyasācī bhīmena coditaḥ keśavena ca
|
karṇadroṇāvatikramya samantātparyavārayat||12||

13tamājiśīrṣamāyāntaṃ dahantaṃ kṣatriyarṣabhān
|
parākrāntaṃ parākramya tataḥ kṣatriyapuṅgavāḥ||13||

14nāśaknuvanvārayituṃ vardhamānamivānalam
|
atha duryodhanaḥ karṇaḥ śakuniścāpi saubalaḥ||14||

15abhyavarṣañcharavrātaiḥ kuntīputraṃ dhanañjayam
|
teṣāmastrāṇi sarveṣāmuttamāstravidāṃ varaḥ||15||

16kadarthī kṛtya rājendra śaravarṣairavākirat
|
astrairastrāṇi saṃvārya laghuhasto jitendriyaḥ||16||

17sarvānavidhyanniśitairdaśabhirdaśabhiḥ śaraiḥ
|
uddhūtā rajaso vṛṣṭiḥ śaravṛṣṭistathaiva ca||17||

18tamaśca ghoraṃ śabdaśca tadā samabhavanmahān
|
na dyaurna bhūmirna diśaḥ prājñāyanta tathāgate||18||

19sainyena rajasā mūḍhaṃ sarvamandhamivābhavat
|
naiva te na vayaṃ rājanprājñāsiṣma parasparam||19||

20uddeśena hi tena sma samayudhyanta pārthivāḥ
|
virathā rathino rājansamāsādya parasparam||20||

21keśeṣu samasajjanta kavaceṣu bhujeṣu ca
|
hatāśvā hatasutāśca niśceṣṭā rathino hatāḥ||21||

22jīvanta iva tatra sma vyadṛśyanta bhayārditāḥ
|
hatān gajānsamāśliṣya parvatāniva vājinaḥ||22||

23gatasattvā vyadṛśyanta tathaiva saha sādibhiḥ
|
tatastvabhyavasṛtyaiva saṃgrāmāduttarāṃ diśam||23||

24ātiṣṭha bāhave droṇo vidhūmo'gniriva jvalan
|
tamājiśīrṣādekāntamapakrāntaṃ niśamya tu||24||

25samakampanta sainyāni pāṇḍavānāṃ viśāṃpate
|
bhrājamānaṃ śriyā yuktaṃ jvalantamiva tejasā||25||

26droṇaṃ dṛṣṭvā pare tresuścerurmamluśca bhārata
|
āhvayantaṃ parānīkaṃ prabhinnamiva vāraṇam||26||

27nainamāśaṃsire jetuṃ dānavā vāsavaṃ yathā
|
kecidāsannirutsāhāḥ kecitkruddhā manasvinaḥ||27||

28vismitāścābhavankecit kecidāsannamarṣitāḥ
|
hastairhastāgramapare pratyapiṃṣannarādhipāḥ||28||

29apare daśanairoṣṭhān daśanakrodhamūcchitāḥ
|
vyākṣipannāyudhānyanye mamṛduścāpare bhujān||29||

30anye cānvapatandroṇaṃ tyaktātmāno mahaujasaḥ
|
pañcālāstu viśeṣeṇa droṇasāyakapīḍitāḥ||30||

31samasajanta rājendra samare bhṛśavedanāḥ
|
tato virāṭadrupadau droṇaṃ prati yayū raṇe||31||

32tathā carantaṃ saṃgrāme bhṛśaṃ samaradurjayam
|
drupadasya tataḥ pautrāstraya eva viśāṃpate||32||

33cedayaśca maheṣvāsā droṇamevābhyayuryudhi
|
teṣāṃ drupada‌pautrāṇāṃ trayāṇāṃ niśitaiḥ śaraiḥ||33||

34tribhirdoṇo'haratprāṇāṃste hatā nyapatanbhuvi
|
tato droṇo'jayadyuddhe cedikaikeyasṛñjayān||34||

35matsyāṃścaivājayatkṛtsnān bhāradvājo mahārathān
|
tatastu drupadaḥ krodhāccharavarṣamavāsṛjat||35||

36droṇaṃ prati mahārāja virāṭaścaiva saṃyuge
|
taṃ nihatyeṣuvarṣaṃ tu droṇaḥ kṣatriyamardanaḥ||36||

37tau śaraiśchādayāmāsa virāṭadrupadāvubhau
|
droṇena cchādyamānau tu kruddhau saṃgrāmamūrdhani||37||

38droṇaṃ śarairvivyadhatuḥ paramaṃ krodhamāsthitau
|
tato droṇo mahārāja krodhāmarṣasamanvitaḥ||38||

39bhallābhyāṃ bhṛśatīkṣṇābhyāṃ ciccheda dhanuṣī tayoḥ
|
tato virāṭaḥ kupitaḥ samare tomarāndaśa||39||

40daśa cikṣepa ca śarāndroṇasya vadhakāṃkṣayā
|
śaktiṃ ca drupado ghorāmāyasīṃ svarṇabhūṣitām||40||

41cikṣepa bhujagendrābhāṃ kruddho droṇarathaṃ prati
|
tato bhallaiḥ suniśitaiśchittvā tāṃstomarāndaśa||41||

42śaktiṃ kanakavaidūryāṃ droṇāściccheda sāyakaiḥ
|
tato droṇaḥ supītābhyāṃ bhallābhyāmarimardanaḥ||42||

43drupadaṃ ca virāṭaṃ ca preṣayāmāsa mṛtyave
|
hate virāṭe drupade kekayeṣu tathaiva ca||43||

44tathaiva cedimatsyeṣu pañcāleṣu tathaiva ca
|
hateṣu triṣu vīreṣu drupadasya ca naptṛṣu||44||

45droṇasya karma taddṛṣṭvā kopaduḥkhasamanvitaḥ
|
śaśāpa rathināṃ madhye dhṛṣṭadyumno mahāmanāḥ||45||

46iṣṭāpūrtāttathā kṣātrādbrāhmaṇyācca sa naśyatu
|
droṇo yasyādya mucyeta yaṃ vā droṇaḥ parābhaveda||46||

47iti teṣāṃ pratiśrutya madhye sarvadhanuṣmatām
|
āyāddroṇaṃ sahānīkaḥ pāñcālyaḥ paravīrahā||47||

48pañcālāstvekato droṇamabhyaghnanpāṇḍavaiḥ saha
|
duryodhanaśca karṇaśca śakuniścāpi saubalaḥ||48||

49sodaryośca yathā mukhyāste'rakṣandroṇamāhave
|
rakṣyamāṇaṃ tathā droṇaṃ sarvaistaistu mahārathaiḥ||49||

50yatamānāstu pañcālā na śekuḥ prativīkṣitum
|
tatrākrudhyadbhīmaseno dhṛṣṭadyumnasya māriṣa||50||

51sa enaṃ vāgbhirugrābhistatakṣa puruṣarṣabhaḥ
|
bhīmasena uvāca|
drupadasya kule jātaḥ sarvāstreṣvastravittamaḥ||51||

52kaḥ kṣatriyo manyamānaḥ prekṣetārimavasthitam
|
pitṛputravadhaṃ prāpya pumānkaḥ paripālayet||52||

53viśeṣatastu śapathaṃ śapitvā rājasaṃsadi
|
eṣa vaiśvānara iva samiddhaḥ svena tejasā||53||

54śaracāpendhano droṇaḥ kṣatraṃ dahati tejasā
|
purā karoti niḥśeṣāṃ pāṇḍavānāmanīkinīm||54||

55sthitāḥ paśyata me karma droṇameva vrajāmyaham
|
ityuktvā prāviśatkruddho droṇānīkaṃ vṛkodaraḥ||55||

56śaraiḥ pūrṇāyatotsṛṣṭairdrāvayaṃstava vāhinīm
|
dhṛṣṭadyumno'pi pāñcālyaḥ praviśya mahatīṃ camūm||56||

57āsasāda raṇe droṇaṃ tadāsīttumulaṃ mahat
|
naiva nastādṛśaṃ yuddhaṃ dṛṣṭapūrvaṃ na ca śrutam||57||

58yathā sūryodaye rājansamutpiñjo'bhavanmahān
|
saṃsaktānyeva cādṛśyan rathavṛndāni māriṣaḥ||58||

59hatāni ca vikīrṇāni śarīrāṇi śarīriṇām
|
kecidanyatra gacchantaḥ pathi cānyairupadrutāḥ||59||

60vimukhāḥ pṛṣṭhataścānye tāḍyante pārśvataḥ pare
|
tathā saṃsaktayuddhaṃ tadabhavadbhṛśadāruṇam
|
atha saṃdhyāgataḥ sūryaḥ kṣaṇena samapadyata||60||
