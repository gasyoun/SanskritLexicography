Mahābhārata 3.288 (Āraṇyakaparvan)

1mārkaṇḍeya uvāca|
tataḥ śrutvā hataṃ saṅkhye kumbhakarṇaṃ sahānugam
|
prahastaṃ ca maheṣvāsaṃ dhūmrākṣaṃ cātitejasam||1||

2putramindrajitaṃ vīraṃ rāvaṇaḥ pratyabhāṣata
|
jahi rāmamamitraghna sugrīvaṃ ca salakṣmaṇam||2||

3tvayā hi mama satputra yaśo dīptamupārjitam
|
jitvā vajradharaṃ saṅkhye sahasrākṣaṃ śacīpatim||3||

4antarhitaḥ prakāśo vā divyairdattavaraiḥ śaraiḥ
|
jahi śatrūnamitraghna mama śastrabhṛtāṃ vara||4||

5rāmalakṣmaṇasugrīvāḥ śarasparśaṃ na te'nagha
|
samarthāḥ pratisoḍhuṃ ca kutastadanuyāyinaḥ||5||

6agatā yā prahastena kumbhakarṇena cānagha
|
kharasyāpacitiḥ saṅkhye tāṃ gaccha tvaṃ mahābhuja||6||

7tvamadya niśitairbāṇairhatvā śatrūn sasainikān
|
pratinandaya māṃ putra purā jitveva vāsavam||7||

8ityuktaḥ sa tathetyuktvā rathamāsthāya daṃśitaḥ
|
prayayāvindrajidrājaṃstūrṇamāyodhanaṃ prati||8||

9tato viśrāvya vispaṣṭaṃ nāma rākṣasapuṅgavaḥ
|
āhvayāmāsa samare lakṣmaṇaṃ śubhalakṣaṇam||9||

10taṃ lakṣmaṇo'bhyadhāvacca pragṛhya saśaraṃ dhanuḥ
|
trāsayaṃstalaghoṣeṇa siṃhaḥ kṣudramṛgān yathā||10||

11tayoḥ samabhavadyuddhaṃ sumahajjayagṛddhinoḥ
|
divyāstraviduṣostīvramanyonyasparthinostadā||11||

12rāvaṇistu yadā naivaṃ viśeṣayati sāyakaiḥ
|
tato gurutaraṃ yatnamātiṣṭhadbalināṃ varaḥ||12||

13tata enaṃ mahāvegairardayāmāsa tomaraiḥ
|
tānāgatān sa ciccheda saumitrirniśitaiḥ śaraiḥ||13||

14te nikṛttāḥ śaraistīkṣṇairnyapatan dharaṇītale
|
tamaṅgado vālisutaḥ śrīmānudyamya pādapam||14||

15abhidrutya mahāvegastāḍayāmāsa mūrdhani
|
tasyendrajidasabhrāntaḥ prāsenorasi vīryavān||15||

16prahartumaicchata taṃ cāsya prāsaṃ ciccheda lakṣmaṇaḥ
|
tamabhyāśagataṃ vīramaṅgadaṃ rāvaṇātmajaḥ||16||

17gadayā'tāḍayat savye pārśve vānarapuṅgavam
|
tamacintyaprahāraṃ sa balavān vālinaḥ sutaḥ||17||

18sasarjendrajitaḥ krodhācchālaskandhaṃ tathā'ṅgadaḥ
|
so'ṅgadena ruṣotsṛṣṭo vadhāyendrajitastaruḥ||18||

19jaghānendrajitaḥ pārtha rathaṃ sāśvaṃ sasārathim
|
tato hatāśvāt praskandya rathāt sa hatasārathiḥ||19||

20tatraivāntardadhe rājan māyayā rāvaṇātmajaḥ
|
antarhitaṃ viditvā taṃ bahumāyaṃ ca rākṣasam||20||

21rāmastaṃ deśamāgamya tat sainyaṃ paryarakṣata
|
sa rāmamuddiśya śaraistato dattavaraistadā||21||

22vivyādha sarvagātreṣu lakṣmaṇaṃ ca mahābalam
|
tamadṛśyaṃ śaraiḥ śūrau māyayā'ntarhitaṃ tadā||22||

23yodhayāmāsaturubhau rāvaṇiṃ rāmalakṣmaṇau
|
sa ruṣā sarvagātreṣu tayoḥ puruṣasiṃhayoḥ||23||

24vyasṛjat sāyakān bhūyaḥ śataśo'tha sahasraśaḥ
|
tamadṛśyaṃ vicinvantaḥ sṛjantamaniśaṃ śarān||24||

25harayo viviśurvyoma pragṛhya mahatīḥ śilāḥ
|
tāṃśca tau cāpyadṛśyaḥ sa śarairvivyādha rākṣasaḥ||25||

26sa bhṛśaṃ tāḍayāmāsa rāvaṇirmāyayā vṛtaḥ
|
tau śarairācitau vīrau bhrātarau rāmalakṣmaṇau
|
petaturgaganādbhūmiṃ sūryācandramasāviva||26||
