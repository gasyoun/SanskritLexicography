Mahābhārata 7.89 (Droṇaparvan)

1arjuna uvāca| codayāśvān hṛṣīkeśa yatra durmarṣaṇaḥ sthitaḥ
|
etadbhittvā gajānīkaṃ pravekṣyāmyarivāhinīm ||1||

2sañjaya uvāca|
evamukto mahābāhuḥ keśavaḥ savyasācinā
|
acodayaddhayāṃstatra yatra durmarṣaṇaḥ sthitaḥ ||2||

3sa saṃprahārastumulaḥ saṃpravṛttaḥ sudāruṇaḥ
|
ekasya ca bahūnāṃ ca rathanāganarakṣayaḥ ||3||

4tataḥ sāyakavarṣeṇa parjanya iva vṛṣṭimān
|
parānavākiran pārthaḥ parvatāniva nīradaḥ ||4||

5te cāpi rathinaḥ sarve tvaritāḥ kṛtahastavat
|
avākiran bāṇajālaistatra kṛṣṇadhanañjayau ||5||

6tataḥ kruddho mahābāhurvāryamāṇaḥ parairyudhi
|
śirāṃsi rathināṃ pārthaḥ kāyebhyo'pāharaccharaiḥ ||6||

7udbhrāntanayanairvaktraiḥ saṃdaṣṭauṣṭhapuṭaiḥ śubhaiḥ
|
sakuṃḍalaśirastrāṇairvasudhā samakīryata ||7||

8puṇḍarīkavanānīva vidhvastāni samaṃtataḥ
|
vinikarṇāni yodhānāṃ vadanāni cakāśire ||8||

9tapanīyatanutrāṇāḥ saṃsiktā rudhireṇa ca
|
saṃsaktā iva dṛśyante meghasaṃghāḥ savidyutaḥ ||9||

10śirasāṃ patatāṃ rājan śabdo'bhūdvasudhātale
|
kālena paripakvānāṃ tālānāṃ patatāmiva ||10||

11tataḥ kabandhaṃ kiñcittu dhanurālambya tiṣṭhati
|
kiṃcit khaḍgaṃ viniṣkṛpya bhujenodyamya tiṣṭhati ||11||

12patitāni na jānanti śirāṃsi puruṣarṣabhāḥ
|
amṛṣyamāṇāḥ saṃgrāme kaunteyaṃ jayagṛddhinaḥ ||12||

13hayānāmuttamāṅgaiśca hastihastaiśca bhedinī
|
bāhubhiśca śirobhiśca vīrāṇāṃ samakīryata ||13||

14ayaṃ pārthaḥ kutaḥ pārtha eṣa pārtha iti prabho
|
tava sainyeṣu yodhānāṃ pārthabhūtamivābhavat ||14||

15anyonyamapi cājaghnurātmānamapi cāpare
|
pārthabhūtamamanyanta jagat kālena mohitāḥ ||15||

16niṣṭanantaḥ sarudhirā visaṃjñā gāḍhavedanāḥ
|
śayānā bahavo vīrāḥ kīrtayantaḥ svabāṃdhavān ||16||

17sabhiṃdipālāḥ saprāsāḥ saśaktyṛṣṭiparaśvadhāḥ
|
sanirvyūhāḥ sanistriṃśāḥ saśarāsanatomarāḥ ||17||

18sabāṇavarmābharaṇāḥ sagadāḥ sāṅgadā raṇe
|
mahābhujagasaṃkāśā bāhavaḥ parighopamāḥ ||18||

19udveṣanti viceṣṭanti saṃceṣṭanti ca sarvaśaḥ
|
vegaṃ kurvanti saṃrabdhā nikṛttāḥ parameṣubhiḥ ||19||

20yo yaḥ sma samare pārthaṃ pratisaṃcarate naraḥ
|
tasya tasyāntako bāṇaḥ śarīramupasarpati ||20||

21nṛtyato rathamārgeṣu dhanurvyāyacchatastathā
|
na kaścittatra pārthasya dadṛśe'ntaramaṇvapi ||21||

22yattasya ghaṭamānasya kṣipraṃ vikṣipataḥ śarān
|
lāghavāt pāṇḍuputrasya vyasmayanta pare janāḥ ||22||

23hastinaṃ hastiyantāramaśvamāśvikameva ca
|
abhinat phālguno bāṇau rathinaṃ ca sasārathim ||23||

24āvartamānamāvṛttaṃ yudhyamānaṃ ca pāṇḍavaḥ
|
pramukhe tiṣṭhamānaṃ ca na kiñcinna nihanti saḥ ||24||

25yathodayan vai gagane sūryo hanti mahattamaḥ
|
tathā'rjuno gajānīkamavadhīt kaṅkapatribhiḥ ||25||

26hastibhiḥ patitairbhinnaistava sainyamadṛśyata
|
antakāle yathā bhūmirvyavakīrṇā mahīdharaiḥ ||26||

27yathā madhyandine sūryo duṣprekṣyaḥ prāṇibhiḥ sadā
|
tathā dhanañjayaḥ kruddho duṣprekṣyo yudhi śatrubhiḥ ||27||

28tattathā tava putrasya sainyaṃ yudhi paraṃtapa
|
prabhagnaṃ drutamāvignamatīva śarapīḍitam ||28||

29māruteneva mahatā meghānīkaṃ vyadīryata
|
prakālyamānaṃ tat sainyaṃ nāśakat prativīkṣitum ||29||

30pratodaiścāpakoṭībhirhuṅkāraiḥ sādhuvāhitaiḥ
|
kaśāpārṣṇyabhighātaiśca vāgbhirugrābhireva ca ||30||

31codayanto hayāṃstūrṇaṃ palāyante sma tāvakāḥ
|
sādino rathinaścaiva pattayaścārjunārditāḥ ||31||

32pārṣṇyaṃguṣṭhāṃkuśairnāgaṃ codayantastathā pare
|
śaraiḥ saṃmohitāścānye tamevābhimukhā yayuḥ || tava yodhā hatotsāhā vibhrāntamanasastadā ||32||
