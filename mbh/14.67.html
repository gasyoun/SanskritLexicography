Mahābhārata 14.67 (Āśvamedhikaparvan)

1vaiśampāyana uvāca|
utthitāyāṃ pṛthāyāṃ tu subhadrā bhrātaraṃ tadā
|
dṛṣṭvā cukrośa duḥkhārtā vacanaṃ cedamabravīt||1||

2puṇḍarīkākṣa paśya tvaṃ pautraṃ pārthasya dhīmataḥ
|
parikṣīṇeṣu kuruṣu parikṣīṇaṃ gatāyuṣam||2||

3iṣīkā droṇaputreṇa bhīmasenārthamudyatā
|
sottarāyāṃ nipatitā vijaye mayi caiva ha||3||

4seyaṃ vidīrṇe hṛdaye mayi tiṣṭhati keśava
|
yanna paśyāmi durdharṣa sahaputraṃ tu taṃ prabho||4||

5kiṃ nu vakṣyati dharmātmā dharmarājo yudhiṣṭhiraḥ
|
bhīmasenārjunau cāpi mādravatyāḥ sutau ca tau||5||

6śrutvābhimanyostanayaṃ jātaṃ ca mṛtameva ca
|
muṣitā iva vārṣṇeya droṇaputreṇa pāṇḍavāḥ||6||

7abhimanyuḥ priyaḥ kṛṣṇa bhrātṝṇāṃ nātra saṃśayaḥ
|
te śrutvā kiṃ nu vakṣyanti droṇaputrāstranirjitāḥ||7||

8bhavitātaḥ paraṃ duḥkhaṃ kiṃ tadanyajjanārdana
|
abhimanyoḥ sutātkṛṣṇa mṛtājjātādarindama||8||

9sāhaṃ prasādaye kṛṣṇa tvāmadya śirasā natā
|
pṛtheyaṃ draupadī caiva tāḥ paśya puruṣottama||9||

10yadā droṇasuto garbhānpāṇḍūnāṃ hanti mādhava
|
tadā kila tvayā drauṇiḥ kruddhanokto'rimardana||10||

11akāmaṃ tvāṃ kariṣyāmi brahmabandho narādhama
|
ahaṃ sañjīvayiṣyāmi kirīṭitanayātmajam||11||

12ityetadvacanaṃ śrutvā jānānāhaṃ balaṃ tava
|
prasādaye tvāṃ durdharṣa jīvatāmabhimanyujaḥ||12||

13yadyetattvaṃ pratiśrutya na karoṣi vacaḥ śubham
|
sakalaṃ vṛṣṇiśārdūla mṛtāṃ māmavadhāraya||13||

14abhimanyoḥ suto vīra na sañjīvati yadyayam
|
jīvati tvayi durdharṣa kiṃ kariṣyāmyahaṃ tvayā||14||

15sañjīvayainaṃ durdharṣa mṛtaṃ tvamabhimanyujam
|
sadṛśākṣasutaṃ vīra sasyaṃ varṣannivāmbudaḥ||15||

16tvaṃ hi keśava dharmātmā satyavānsatyavikramaḥ
|
sa tāṃ vācamṛtāṃ kartumarhasi tvamarindama||16||

17icchannapi hi lokāṃstrīnjīvayethā mṛtānimān
|
kiṃ punardayitaṃ jātaṃ svasrīyasyātmajaṃ mṛtam||17||

18prabhāvajñāsmi te kṛṣṇa tasmāttvāṃ yācayāmyaham
|
kuruṣva pāṇḍuputrāṇāmimaṃ paramanugraham||18||

19svaseti vā mahābāho hataputreti vā punaḥ
|
prapannā māmiyaṃ ceti dayāṃ kartumihārhasi||19||
