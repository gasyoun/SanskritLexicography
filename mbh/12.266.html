Mahābhārata 12.266 (Śāntiparvan)

1yudhiṣṭhira uvāca|
kathaṃ kāryaṃ parīkṣeta śīghraṃ vā'tha cireṇa vā
|
sarvathā kāryadurge'smin bhavānnaḥ paramo guruḥ ||1||

2bhīṣma uvāca|
atrāpyudāharantīmamitihāsaṃ purātanam
|
cirakārestu yatpūrvaṃ vṛttamāṅgirase kule ||2||

3cirakārika bhadraṃ te bhadraṃ te cirakārika
|
cirakārī hi medhāvī nāparādhyati karmasu ||3||

4cirakārī mahāprājño gautamasyābhavatsutaḥ
|
cireṇa sarvakāryāṇi vimṛśyārthānprapadyate ||4||

5ciraṃ sa cintayatyarthāṃściraṃ jāgracciraṃ svapan
|
ciraṃ kāryābhipattiṃ ca cirakārī tathocyate ||5||

6alasagrahaṇaṃ prāpto durmedhāvī tathocyate
|
buddhilāghavayuktena janenādīrghadarśinā ||6||

7vyabhicāre tu kasmiṃścidvyatikramyāparānsutān
|
pitroktaḥ kupitenātha jahīmāṃ jananīmiti ||7||

8ityuktvā sa tadā vipro gautamo japatāṃ varaḥ
|
avimṛśya mahābhāgo vanameva jagāma saḥ ||8||

9sa tatheti cireṇoktvā svabhāvāccirakārikaḥ
|
vimṛśya cirakāritvāccintayāmāsa vai ciram ||9||

10piturājñāṃ kathaṃ kuryāṃ na hanyāṃ mātaraṃ katham
|
kathaṃ dharmacchalenāsminnimajjeyamasādhuvat ||10||

11piturājñā paro dharmaḥ svadharmo mātṛrakṣaṇam
|
asvatantraṃ ca putratvaṃ kiṃ tu māṃ nānupīḍayet ||11||

12striyaṃ hatvā mātaraṃ ca ko hi jātu sukhī bhavet
|
pitaraṃ cāpyavajñāya kaḥ pratiṣṭhāmavāpnuyāt ||12||

13anavajñā pituryuktā dhāraṇaṃ mātṛrakṣaṇam
|
yuktakṣamāvubhāvetau nātivarteta māṃ katham ||13||

14pitā hyātmānamādhatte jāyāyāṃ jajñivāniti
|
śīlacāritragotrasya dhāraṇārthaṃ kulasya ca ||14||

15so'haṃ mātrā svayaṃ pitrā putratve prakṛtaḥ punaḥ
|
vijñānaṃ me kathaṃ na syāddvau buddhye cātmasambhavam ||15||

16jātakarmaṇi yatprāha pitā yaccopakarmaṇi
|
paryāptaḥ sa dṛḍhīkāraḥ piturgauravaniścaye ||16||

17gururagryaḥ paro dharmaḥ poṣaṇādhyāpanānvitaḥ
|
pitā yadāha dharmaḥ sa vedeṣvapi suniścitaḥ ||17||

18prītimātraṃ pituḥ putraḥ sarvaṃ putrasya vai pitā
|
śarīrādīni deyāni pitā tvekaḥ prayacchati ||18||

19tasmātpiturvacaḥ kāryaṃ na vicāryaṃ kadācana
|
pātakānyapi pūyante pituḥ śāsanakāriṇaḥ ||19||

20bhogye bhojye pravacane sarvaṃ lokanidarśane
|
bhartrā caiva samāyoge sīmantonnayane tathā ||20||

21pitā dharmaḥ pitā svargaḥ pitā hi paramaṃ tapaḥ
|
pitari prītimāpanne sarvāḥ prīyanti devatāḥ ||21||

22āśiṣastā bhajantyenaṃ paruṣaṃ prāha yatpitā
|
niṣkṛtiḥ sarvapāpānāṃ pitā yaccābhinandati ||22||

23mucyate bandhanātpuṣpaṃ phalaṃ vṛkṣātpramucyate
|
kliśyannapi sutaṃ snehaiḥ pitā putraṃ na muñcati ||23||

24etadvicintitaṃ tāvat putrasya pitṛgauravam
|
pitā nālpataraṃ sthānaṃ cintayiṣyāmi mātaram ||24||

25yo hyayaṃ mayi saṃghāto martyatve pāñcabhautikaḥ
|
yasya me jananī hetuḥ pāvakasya yathā'raṇiḥ ||25||

26mātā dehāraṇiḥ puṃsāṃ sarvasyārtasya nirvṛtiḥ
|
mātṛlobhe sanāthatvamanāthatvaṃ viparyaye ||26||

27na ca śocati nāpyenaṃ sthāviryamapakarṣati
|
śriyā hīno'pi yo gehamambeti pratipadyate ||27||

28putrapautropapanno'pi jananīṃ yaḥ samāśritaḥ
|
api varṣaśatasyānte sa dvihāyanavaccaret ||28||

29samarthaṃ vā'samarthaṃ vā kṛśaṃ vāpyakṛśaṃ tathā
|
rakṣatyeva sutaṃ mātā nānyaḥ poṣṭā vidhānataḥ ||29||

30tadā sa vṛddho bhavati tadā bhavati duḥkhitaḥ
|
tadā śūnyaṃ jagattasya yadā mātrā viyujyate ||30||

31nāsti mātṛsamā cchāyā nāsti mātṛsamā gatiḥ
|
nāsti mātṛsamaṃ trāṇaṃ nāsti mātṛsamā priyā ||31||

32kukṣisaṃdhāraṇāddhātrī jananājjananī smṛtā
|
aṅgānāṃ vardhanādambā vīrasūtvena vīrasūḥ ||32||

33śiśoḥ śuśrūṣaṇācchuśrūrmātā dehamanantaram
|
cetanāvānnaro hanyādyasya nāsuṣiraṃ śiraḥ ||33||

34dampatyoḥ prāṇasaṃśleṣe yo'bhisaṃdhiḥ kṛtaḥ kila
|
taṃ mātā ca pitā ceti bhūtārtho mātari sthitaḥ ||34||

35mātā jānāti yadgotraṃ mātā jānāti yasya saḥ
|
māturbharaṇamātreṇa prītiḥ snehaḥ pituḥ prajāḥ ||35||

36pāṇibandhaṃ svayaṃ kṛtvā saha dharmamupetya ca
|
yadā yāsyanti puruṣāḥ striyo nārhanti yācyatām ||36||

37bharaṇāddhi striyo bhartā pātyā caiva striyaḥ patiḥ
|
guṇasyāsya nivṛttau tu na bhartā na punaḥ patiḥ ||37||

38evaṃ strī nāparādhnoti nara evāparādhyati
|
vyuccaraṃśca mahādoṣaṃ nara evāparādhyati ||38||

39striyā hi paramo bhartā daivataṃ paramaṃ smṛtam
|
tasyātmanā tu sadṛśamātmānaṃ paramaṃ dadau ||39||

40nāparādho'sti nārīṇāṃ nara evāparādhyati
|
sarvakāryāparādhyatvānnāparādhyanti cāṅganāḥ ||40||

41yaśca nokto'tha nirdeśastriyā maithunatṛptaye
|
tasya smārayato vyaktamadharmo nāsti saṃśayaḥ ||41||

42evaṃ nārīṃ mātaraṃ ca gaurave cādhike sthitām
|
avadhyāṃ tu vijānīyuḥ paśavo'pyavicakṣaṇāḥ ||42||

43devatānāṃ samāvāyamekasthaṃ pitaraṃ viduḥ
|
martyānāṃ devātanāṃ ca snehādabhyeti mātaram ||43||

44evaṃ vimṛśatastasya cirakāritayā bahu
|
dīrghaḥ kālo vyatikrāntastato'syābhyāgamatpitā ||44||

45medhātithirmahāprājño gautamastapasi sthitaḥ
|
vimṛśya tena kālena patnyāḥ saṃsthāvyatikramam ||45||

46so'bravīdbhṛśasantapto duḥkhenāśrūṇi vartayan
|
śrutadhairyaprasādena paścāttāpamupāgataḥ ||46||

47āśramaṃ mama samprāptastrilokeśaḥ puraṃdaraḥ
|
atithivratamāsthāya brāhmaṇaṃ rūpamāsthitaḥ ||47||

48sa mayā sāntvito vāgbhiḥ svāgatenābhipūjitaḥ
|
arghyaṃ pādyaṃ yathānyāyaṃ mayā ca pratipāditaḥ ||48||

49paravānasmi cetyuktaḥ praṇayiṣyati tena ca
|
atra cākuśale jāte striyā nāsti vyatikramaḥ ||49||

50evaṃ na strī na caivāhaṃ nādhvagastridaśeśvaraḥ
|
aparādhyati dharmasya pramādastvaparādhyati ||50||

51īrṣyājaṃ vyasanaṃ prāhustena caivordhvaretasaḥ
|
īrṣyayā tvahamākṣipto magno duṣkṛtasāgare ||51||

52hatvā sādhvīṃ ca nārīṃ ca vyasanitvācca vāsitām
|
bhartavyatvena bhāryāṃ ca ko na māṃ tārayiṣyati ||52||

53antareṇa mayā''jñaptaścirakārītyudāradhīḥ
|
yadyadya cirakārī syātsa māṃ trāyeta pātakāt ||53||

54cirakārika bhadraṃ te bhadraṃ te cirakārika
|
yadyadya cirakārī tvaṃ tato'si cirakārikaḥ ||54||

55trāhi māṃ mātaraṃ caiva tapo yaccārjitaṃ mayā
|
ātmānaṃ pātakebhyaśca bhavādya cirakārikaḥ ||55||

56sahajaṃ cirakāritvamatiprajñatayā tava
|
saphalaṃ tattathā te'stu bhavādya cirakārikaḥ ||56||

57ciramāśaṃsito mātrā ciraṃ garbheṇa dhāritaḥ
|
saphalaṃ cirakāritvaṃ kuru tvaṃ cirakārika ||57||

58cirāyate ca saṃtāpācciraṃ svapiti vāritaḥ
|
āvayościrasaṃtāpādavekṣya cirakārika ||58||

59evaṃ sa duḥkhito rājanmaharṣigautamastadā
|
cirakāriṃ dadarśātha putraṃ sthitamathāntike ||59||

60cirakārī tu pitaraṃ dṛṣṭvā paramaduḥkhitaḥ
|
śastraṃ tyaktvā tato mūrdhnā prasādāyopacakrame ||60||

61gautamastaṃ tato dṛṣṭvā śirasā patitaṃ bhuvi
|
patnīṃ caiva nirākārāṃ parāmabhyāgamanmudam ||61||

62na hi sā tena sambhedaṃ patnī nītā mahātmanā
|
vijane cāśramasthena putraścāpi samāhitaḥ ||62||

63hanyā iti samādeśaḥ śastrapāṇai sute sthite
|
vinīte prasavatyarthe vivāse cātmakarmasu ||63||

64buddhiśāsītsutaṃ dṛṣṭvā pituścaraṇayornatam
|
śastragrahaṇacāpalyaṃ saṃvṛṇoti bhayāditi ||64||

65tataḥ pitrā ciraṃ stutvā ciraṃ cāghrāya mūrdhani
|
ciraṃ dorbhyāṃ pariṣvajya ciraṃ jīvetyudāhṛtaḥ ||65||

66evaṃ sa gautamaḥ putraṃ prītiharṣaguṇairyutaḥ
|
abhinandya mahāprājña idaṃ vacanamabravīt ||66||

67cirakārika bhadraṃ te cirakārī ciraṃ bhava
|
cirāya yadi te saumya ciramasmi na duḥkhitaḥ ||67||

68gāthāścāpyabravīdvidvān gautamo munisattamaḥ
|
cirakāriṣu dhīreṣu guṇoddeśasamāśrayāḥ ||68||

69cireṇa mitraṃ badhnīyāccireṇa ca kṛtaṃ tyajet
|
cireṇa hi kṛtaṃ mitraṃ ciraṃ dhāraṇamarhati ||69||

70rāge darpe ca māne ca drohe pāpe ca karmaṇi
|
apriye caiva kartavye cirakārī praśasyate ||70||

71bandhūnāṃ suhṛdāṃ caiva bhṛtyānāṃ strījanasya ca
|
avyakteṣvaparādheṣu cirakārī praśasyate ||71||

72evaṃ sa gautamastatra prītaḥ putrasya bhārata
|
karmaṇā tena kauravya cirakāritayā tathā ||72||

73evaṃ sarveṣu kāryeṣu vimṛśya puruṣastataḥ
|
cireṇa niścayaṃ kṛtvā ciraṃ na paritapyate ||73||

74ciraṃ dhārayate roṣa ciraṃ karma niyacchati
|
paścāttāpakaraṃ karma na kiṃcidupapadyate ||74||

75ciraṃ vṛddhānupāsīta ciramanvāsya pūjayet
|
ciraṃ dharmaṃ niṣeveta kuryāccānveṣaṇaṃ ciram ||75||

76ciramanvāsya viduṣaściraṃ śiṣṭānniṣevya ca
|
ciraṃ vinīya cātmānaṃ ciraṃ yātyanavajñatām ||76||

77bruvataśca parasyāpi vākyaṃ dharmopasaṃhitam
|
ciraṃ pṛṣṭo'pi ca brūyācciraṃ na paritapyate ||77||

78upāsya bahulāstasminnāśrame sumahātapāḥ
|
samāḥ svargaṃ gato vipraḥ putreṇa sahitastadā ||78||
