Mahābhārata 7.161 (Droṇaparvan)

1sañjaya uvāca|
tato yudhiṣṭhiraścaiva bhīmasenaśca pāṇḍavaḥ
|
droṇaputraṃ mahārāja samantātparyavārayan||1||

2tato duryodhano rājā bhāradvājena saṃvṛtaḥ
|
abhyayātpāṇḍavānsaṃkhye tato yuddhamavartata||2||

3ghorarūpaṃ mahārāja bhīrūṇāṃ bhayavardhanam
|
aṃbaṣṭhānmālavānvaṅgāñchibīṃstraigartakānapi||3||

4prāhiṇonmṛtyulokāya gaṇān kruddho vṛkodaraḥ
|
abhīṣāhāñchūrasenānkṣatriyān yuddhadurmadān||4||

5nikṛtya pṛthivīṃ cakre bhīmaḥ śoṇitakardamām
|
yaudheyānadrijān rājanmadrakānmālavānapi||5||

6prāhiṇonmṛtyulokāya kirīṭī niśitaiḥ śaraiḥ
|
pragāḍhamañjogatibhirnārācairabhitāḍitāḥ||6||

7nipeturdviradā bhūmau dviśṛṅgā iva parvatāḥ
|
nikṛttairhastihastaiśca ceṣṭamānairitastataḥ||7||

8rarāja vasudhā''kīrṇā visarpadbhirivoragaiḥ
|
kṣiptaiḥ kanakacitraiśca nṛpacchatraiḥ kṣitirbabhau||8||

9dyaurivādityacandrādyairgrahaiḥ kīrṇā yugakṣaye
|
hata praharatābhītā vidhyata vyavakṛntata||9||

10ityāsūttumulaḥ śabdaḥ śoṇāśvasya rathaṃ prati
|
droṇastu paramakruddho vāyavyāstreṇa saṃyuge||10||

11vyadhamattānmahāvāyurmeghāniva duratyayaḥ
|
te hanyamānā droṇena pañcālāḥ prādravanbhayāt||11||

12paśyato bhīmasenasya pārthasya ca mahātmanaḥ
|
tataḥ kirīṭī bhīmaśca sahasā saṃnyavartatām||12||

13mahatā rathavaṃśena parigṛhya balaṃ mahat
|
bībhatsurdakṣiṇaṃ pārśvamuttaraṃ tu vṛkodaraḥ||13||

14bhāradvājaṃ śaraughābhyāṃ mahadbhyāmabhyavarṣatām
|
tau tathā sṛñjayāścaiva pañcālāśca mahaujasaḥ||14||

15anvagacchanmahārāja matsyaiśca saha somakaiḥ
|
tathaiva tava putrasya rathodārāḥ prahāriṇaḥ||15||

16mahatyā senayā rājan jagmurdroṇarathaṃ prati
|
tataḥ sā bhāratī senā hanyamānā kirīṭinā||16||

17tamasā nidrayā caiva punareva vyadīryata
|
droṇena vāryamāṇāste svayaṃ tava sutena ca||17||

18nāśakyanta mahārāja yodhā vārayituṃ tadā
|
sā pāṇḍuputrasya śarairdīryamāṇā mahācamūḥ||18||

19tamasā saṃvṛte loke vyadravatsarvatomukhī
|
utsṛjya śataśo vāhāṃstatra kecinnarādhipāḥ
|
prādravanta mahārāja bhayāviṣṭāḥ samantataḥ||19||
