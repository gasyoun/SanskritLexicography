Mahābhārata 8.33 (Karṇaparvan)

1duryodhana uvāca|
bhūya eva tu madreśa yatte vakṣyāmi tacchṛṇu
|
yathā purāvṛttamidaṃ yuddhe devāsure vibho ||1||

2yaduktavān piturmahyaṃ mārkaṇḍeyo mahānṛṣiḥ
|
tadaśeṣeṇa bruvato mama rājarṣisattama ||2||

3nibodha manasā cātra na te kāryā vicāraṇā
|
devānāmasurāṇāṃ ca parasparajigīṣayā||3||

4babhūva prathamo rājan saṃgrāmastārakāmayaḥ
|
nirjitāśca tadā daityā daivatairiti naḥ śrutam||4||

5nirjiteṣu ca daityeṣu tārakasya sutāstrayaḥ
|
tārākṣaḥ kamalākṣaśca vidyunmālī ca pārthiva ||5||

6tapa ugraṃ samāsthāya niyame parame sthitāḥ
|
tapasā karṣayāmāsurdehān svān śatrutāpana ||6||

7damena tapasā caiva niyamena samādhinā
|
teṣāṃ pitāmahaḥ prīto varadaḥ pradadau varam ||7||

8avadhyatvaṃ ca te rājan sarvabhūtasya sarvadā
|
sahitā varayāmāsuḥ sarvalokapitāmaham ||8||

9tānabravīttadā devo lokānāṃ prabhurīśvaraḥ
|
nāsti sarvāmaratvaṃ vai nivartadhvamito'surāḥ ||9||

10anyaṃ varaṃ vṛṇīdhvaṃ vai yādṛśaṃ saṃprarocate
|
tataste sahitā rājan saṃpradhāryāsakṛtprabhum ||10||

11sarvalokeśvara vākyaṃ praṇamyedamathābruvan
|
asmabhyaṃ tvaṃ varaṃ deva saṃprayaccha pitāmaha ||11||

12vayaṃ purāṇi trīṇyeva samāsthāya mahīmimām
|
vicariṣyāma loke'smiṃstvatprasādapuraskṛtāḥ ||12||

13tato varṣasahastre tu sameṣyāmaḥ parasparam
|
ekībhāvaṃ gamiṣyanti purāṇyetāni cānagha ||13||

14samāgatāni caitāni yo hanyādbhagavaṃstadā
|
ekeṣuṇā devavaraḥ sa no mṛtyurbhaviṣyati ||14||

15evamastviti tān devaḥ pratyuktvā prāviśaddivam
|
te tu labdhavarāḥ prītāḥ saṃpradhārya parasparam ||15||

16puratrayavisṛṣṭyarthaṃ vayaṃ vavrurmahāsuram
|
viśvakarmāṇamajaraṃ daityadānavapūjitam ||16||

17tato mayaḥ svatapasā cakre dhīmānpurāṇi ca
|
trīṇi kāñcanamekaṃ vai raupyaṃ kārṇāyasaṃ tathā ||17||

18kāñcanaṃ divi tatrāsīdantarikṣe ca rājatam
|
āyasaṃ cābhavadbhaumaṃ cakrasthaṃ pṛthivīpate ||18||

19ekaikaṃ yojanaśataṃ vistārāyāmataḥ samam
|
gṛhāṭṭālakasaṃyuktaṃ bahuprākāratoraṇam ||19||

20gṛhapravarasaṃbādhamasaṃbādhamahāpatham
|
prāsādairvividhaiścāpi dvāraiścaivopaśobhitam ||20||

21pureṣu cābhavan rājan rājāno vai pṛthak pṛthak
|
kāñcanaṃ tārakākṣasya citramāsīnmahātmanaḥ ||21||

22rājataṃ kamalākṣasya vidyunmālina āyasam
|
trayaste daityarājānastrīlloঁkānastratejasā ||22||

23ākramya tasthurūcuśca kaśca nāma prajāpatiḥ
|
teṣāṃ dānavamukhyānāṃ prayutānyarbudāni ca ||23||

24koṭyaścāprativīrāṇāṃ samājagmustatastataḥ
|
māṃsāśinaḥ sudṛptāśca surairvinikṛtāḥ purā ||24||

25mahadaiśvaryamicchantastripuraṃ durgamāśritāḥ
|
sarveṣāṃ ca punaścaiṣāṃ sarvayogavaho mayaḥ ||25||

26tamāśritya hite sarve vartayante'kutobhayāḥ
|
yo hi yanmanasā kāmaṃ dadhyau tripurasaṃśrayaḥ ||26||

27tasmai kāmaṃ mayastaṃ taṃ vidadhe māyayā tadā
|
tārakākṣasuto vīro harirnāma mahābalaḥ ||27||

28tapastepe paramakaṃ yenātuṣyatpitāmahaḥ
|
santuṣṭamavṛṇoddevaṃ vāpī bhavatu naḥ pure ||28||

29śastrairvinihatā yatra kṣiptāḥ syurbalavattarāḥ
|
sa tu labdhvā varaṃ vīrastārakākṣasuto hariḥ ||29||

30sasṛje tatra vāpīṃ tāṃ mṛtānāṃ jīvinīṃ prabho
|
yena rūpeṇa daityastu yena veṣeṇa caiva ha ||30||

31mṛtastasyāṃ parikṣiptastādṛśenaiva jajñivān
|
tāṃ prāpyate punastāṃstu lokānsarvānbabādhire ||31||

32mahatā tapasā siddhāḥ surāṇāṃ bhayavardhanāḥ
|
na teṣāmabhavadrājan kṣayo yuddhe kadācana ||32||

33tataste lobhamohābhyāmabhibhūtā vicetasaḥ
|
nirhrīkāḥ saṃsthitāḥ sarve sthāpitāḥ samalūlupan ||33||

34vidrāvya sagaṇān devāṃstatra tatra tadā tadā
|
viceruḥ svena kāmena varadānena darpitāḥ ||34||

35devodyānāni sarvāṇi priyāṇi ca divaukasām
|
ṛṣīṇāmāśramānpuṇyān ramyāñjanapadāṃstathā ||35||

36vyanāśayannamaryādā dānavā duṣṭacāriṇaḥ
|
pīḍyamāneṣu lokeṣu tataḥ śakro marudvṛtaḥ ||36||

37purāṇyāyodhayāṃ cakre vajrapātaiḥ samantataḥ
|
nāśakattānyabhedyāni yadā bhettuṃ purandaraḥ ||37||

38purāṇi varadattāni dhātrā tena narādhipa
|
tadā bhītaḥ surapatirmuktvā tāni purāṇyatha ||38||

39taireva vibudhaiḥ sārdhaṃ pitāmaharindama
|
jagāmātha tadākhyātuṃ viprakāraṃ suretaraiḥ ||39||

40te tattvaṃ sarvamākhyāya śirobhiḥ saṃpraṇamya ca
|
vadhopāyamapṛcchanta bhagavantaṃ pitāmaham ||40||

41śrutvā tadbhagavāndevo devānidamuvāca ha
|
mamāpi so'parādhnoti yo yuṣmākamasaumyakṛt ||41||

42asurā hi durātmānaḥ sarva eva suradviṣaḥ
|
aparādhyanti satataṃ ye yuṣmān pīḍayantyuta ||42||

43ahaṃ hi tulyaḥ sarveṣāṃ bhūtānāṃ nātra saṃśayaḥ
|
adhārmikāstu hantavyā iti me vratamāhitam ||43||

44ekeṣuṇā bibhedyāni tāni durgāṇi nānyathā
|
na ca sthāṇumṛte śakto bhettumekeṣuṇā puraḥ ||44||

45te yūyaṃ sthāṇumīśānaṃ jiṣṇumakliṣṭakāriṇam
|
yoddhāraṃ vṛṇutādityāḥ sa tān hantā suretarān ||45||

46iti tasya vacaḥ śrutvā devā śakrapurogamāḥ
|
brahmāṇamagrataḥ kṛtvā vṛṣāṅkaṃ śaraṇaṃ yayuḥ ||46||

47taponiyamamāsthāya gṛṇanto brahma śāśvatam
|
ṛṣibhiḥ saha dharmajñā bhavaṃ sarvātmanā gatāḥ ||47||

48tuṣṭuvurvāgbhirugrābhirbhayeṣvabhayadaṃ nṛpa
|
sarvātmānaṃ mahātmānaṃ yenāptaṃ sarvamātmanā ||48||

49tapoviśeṣairvividhairyogaṃ yo veda cātmanaḥ
|
yaḥ sāṃkhyamātmano vetti yasya cātmā vaśe sadā ||49||

50taṃ te dadṛśurīśānaṃ tejorāśimumāpatim
|
ananyasadṛśaṃ loke bhagavantamakalmaṣam ||50||

51ekaṃ ca bhagavantaṃ te nānārūpamakalpayan
|
ātmanaḥ pratirūpāṇi rūpāṇyatha mahātmani ||51||

52parasparasya cāpaśyan sarve paramavismitāḥ
|
sarvabhūtamayaṃ dṛṣṭvā tamajaṃ jagataḥ patim ||52||

53devā brahmarṣayaścaiva śirobhirdharaṇīṃ gatāḥ
|
tānsvastivādenābhyarcya samutthāpya ca śaṅkaraḥ ||53||

54brūta brūteti bhagavān smayamāno'bhyabhāṣata
|
tryaṃbakeṇābhyanujñātāstataste svasthacetasaḥ ||54||

55namo namo namaste'stu prabho ityabruvanvacaḥ
|
namo devādhidevāya dhanvine vanamāline ||55||

56prajāpatimakhaghnāya prajāpatibhirīḍyate
|
namaḥ stutāya stutyāya stūyamānāya śaṃbhave ||56||

57vilohitāya rudrāya nīlagrīvāya śūline
|
amoghāya mṛgākṣāya pravarāyudhayodhine ||57||

58arhāya caiva śuddhāya kṣayāya krathanāya ca
|
durvāraṇāya krāthāya brahmaṇe brahmacāriṇe ||58||

59īśānāyāprameyāya niyantre carmavāsase
|
taporatāya piṅgāya vratine kṛttivāsase ||59||

60kumārapitre tryakṣāya pravarāyudhadhāriṇe
|
prapannārtivināśāya brahmadviṭsaṃghaghātine ||60||

61vanaspatīnāṃ pataye narāṇāṃ pataye namaḥ
|
gavāṃ ca pataye nityaṃ yajñānāṃ pataye namaḥ ||61||

62namo'stu te sasainyāya tryambakāyāmitaujase
|
manovākkarmabhirdeva tvāṃ prapannān bhajasva naḥ ||62||

63tataḥ prasanno bhagavān svāgatenābhinandya ca
|
provāca vyetu vastrāso brūta kiṃ karavāṇi vaḥ ||63||
