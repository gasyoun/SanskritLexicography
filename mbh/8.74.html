Mahābhārata 8.74 (Karṇaparvan)

1sañjaya uvāca|
sa keśavasya bībhatsuḥ śrutvā bhārata bhāṣitam
|
viśokaḥ saṃprahṛṣṭaśca kṣaṇena samapadyata ||1||

2tato jyāmabhimṛjyāśu vyākṣipadgāṇḍivaṃ dhanuḥ
|
dadhre karṇavināśāya keśavaṃ cābhyabhāṣata ||2||

3tvayā nāthena govinda dhruva eva jayo mama
|
prasanno yasya me'dya tvaṃ loke bhūtabhaviṣyakṛt ||3||

4tvatsahāyo hyahaṃ kṛṣṇa trīnlokānvai samāgatān
|
prāpayeyaṃ paraṃ lokaṃ kimu karṇaṃ mahāhave ||4||

5paśyāmi dravatīṃ senāṃ pañcālānāṃ janārdana
|
paśyāmi karṇaṃ samare vicarantamabhītavat ||5||

6bhārgavāstraṃ ca paśyāmi jvalantaṃ kṛṣṇa sarvaśaḥ
|
sṛṣṭaṃ karṇena vārṣṇeya śakreṇeva yathā'śanim ||6||

7ayaṃ khalu sa saṃgrāmo yatra karṇaṃ mayā hatam
|
kathayiṣyanti bhūtāni yāvadbhūmirdhariṣyati ||7||

8adya kṛṣṇa vikarṇā me karṇaṃ neṣyanti mṛtyave
|
gāṇḍīvamuktāḥ kṣiṇvanto mama hastapracoditāḥ ||8||

9adya rājā dhṛtarāṣṭraḥ svāṃ buddhimavamaṃsyate
|
duryodhanamarājyārhaṃ yayā rājye'bhyaṣecayat ||9||

10adya rājyātsukhāccaiva śriyo rāṣṭrāttathā purāt
|
putrebhyaśca mahābāho dhṛtarāṣṭro vimokṣyati ||10||

11guṇavantaṃ hi yo dveṣṭi nirguṇaṃ kurute prabhum
|
sa śocati nṛpaḥ kṛṣṇa kṣipramevāgate kṣaye ||11||

12yathā ca puruṣaḥ kaścicchittvā cāmravaṇaṃ mahat
|
phalaṃ dṛṣṭvā bhṛśaṃ duḥkhī bhaviṣyati janārdana
|
sūtaputre hate tvadya nirāśo bhavitā prabhuḥ ||12||

13adya duryodhano rājyājjīvitācca nirāśakaḥ
|
bhaviṣyati hate karṇe kṛṣṇa satyaṃ bravīmi te ||13||

14adya dṛṣṭvā mayā karṇaṃ śarairviśakalīkṛtam
|
smaratāṃ tava vākyāni śamaṃ prati janeśvaraḥ ||14||

15adyāsau saubalaḥ kṛṣṇa glahāñjānātu vai śarān
|
durodaraṃ ca gāṇḍīvaṃ maṇḍalaṃ ca rathaṃ prati ||15||

16adya kuntīsutasyāhaṃ dṛḍhaṃ rājñaḥ prajāgaram
|
vyapaneṣyāmi govinda hatvā karṇaṃ śitaiḥ śaraiḥ ||16||

17adya kuntīsuto rājā hate sūtasute mayā
|
suprahṛṣṭamanāḥ prītaściraṃ sukhamavāpsyati ||17||

18adya cāhamanādhṛṣyaṃ keśavāpratimaṃ śaram
|
utsrakṣyāmīha yaḥ karṇaṃ jīvitādbhraṃśayiṣyati ||18||

19yasya caitadvrataṃ mahyaṃ vadhe kila durātmanaḥ
|
pādau na dhāvaye tāvadyāvaddhanyāṃ na phālgunam ||19||

20mṛṣā kṛtvā vrataṃ tasya pāpasya madhusūdana
|
pātayiṣye rathātkāyaṃ śaraiḥ sannataparvabhiḥ ||20||

21yo'sau raṇe naraṃ nānyaṃ pṛthivyāmanumanyate
|
tasyādya sūtaputrasya bhūmiḥ pāsyati śoṇitam ||21||

22'apatirhyasi kṛṣṇe'-ti sūtaputro yadabravīt
|
dhṛtarāṣṭramate karṇaḥ ślāghamānaḥ svakān guṇān ||22||

23anṛtaṃ tatkariṣyanti māmakā niśitāḥ śarāḥ
|
āśīviṣā iva kruddhāstasya pāsyanti śoṇitam ||23||

24mayā hastavatā muktā nārācā vaidyutatviṣaḥ
|
gāṇḍīvasṛṣṭā dāsyanti karṇasya paramāṃ gatim ||24||

25adya tapsyati rādheyaḥ pāñcālīṃ yattadābravīt
|
sabhāmadhye vacaḥ krūraṃ kutsayanpāṇḍavānprati ||25||

26ye vai ṣaṇḍhatilāstatra bhavitāro'dya te tilāḥ
|
hate vaikartane karṇe sūtaputre durātmani ||26||

27ahaṃ vaḥ pāṇḍuputrebhyastrāsyāmīti yadabravīt
|
dhṛtarāṣṭrasutānkarṇaḥ ślāghamānotmano guṇān ||27||

28anṛtaṃ tatkariṣyanti māmakā niśitāḥ śarāḥ
|
udyogaḥ pāṇḍuputrāṇāṃ samāptimupayāsyati ||28||

29hantāhaṃ pāṇḍavānsarvānsaputrāniti yobravīt
|
tamadya karṇaṃ hantāsmi miṣatāṃ sarvadhanvinām ||29||

30yasya vīryaṃ samāśritya dhārtarāṣṭro mahāmanāḥ
|
avāmanyata durbuddhirnityamasmān durātmavān
|
hatvā'haṃ karṇamājau hi toṣayiṣyāmi bhrātaram ||30||

31śarānnānāvidhānmuktvā trāsayiṣyāmi śātravān
|
ākarṇamuktairiṣubhiryamarāṣṭravivardhanaiḥ ||31||

32bhūmiśobhāṃ kariṣyāmi pātitai rathakuñjaraiḥ
|
tatrāhaṃ vai mahāsaṃkhye saṃpannaṃ yuddhadurmadam ||32||

33adya karṇamahaṃ ghoraṃ sūdayiṣyāmi sāyakaiḥ
|
adya karṇe hate kṛṣṇa dhārtarāṣṭrāḥ sarājakāḥ ||33||

34vidravantu diśo bhītāḥ siṃhatrastā mṛgā iva
|
adya duryodhano rājā ātmānaṃ cānuśocatām ||34||

35hate karṇe mayā saṃkhye saputre sasuhṛjjane
|
adya karṇaṃ hataṃ dṛṣṭvā dhārtarāṣṭro'tyamarṣaṇaḥ ||35||

36jānātu māṃ raṇe kṛṣṇa pravaraṃ sarvadhanvinām
|
saputrapautraṃ sāmātyaṃ sabhṛtyaṃ ca nirāśiṣam ||36||

37adya rājye kariṣyāmi dhṛtarāṣṭraṃ janeśvaram
|
adya karṇasya cakrāṅgāḥ kravyādāśca pṛthagvidhāḥ ||37||

38śaraiśchinnāni gātrāṇi vicariṣyanti keśava
|
adya rādhāsutasyāhaṃ saṃgrāme madhusūdana ||38||

39śiraśchetsyāmi karṇasya miṣatāṃ sarvadhanvinām
|
adya tīkṣṇairvipāṭhaiśca kṣuraiśca madhusūdana ||39||

40raṇe chetsyāmi gātrāṇi rādheyasya durātmanaḥ
|
adya rājā mahatkṛcchraṃ santyakṣyati yudhiṣṭhiraḥ ||40||

41santāpaṃ mānasaṃ vīraścirasaṃbhṛtamātmanaḥ
|
adya keśava rādheyamahaṃ hatvā sabāndhavam ||41||

42nandayiṣyāmi rājānaṃ dharmaputraṃ yudhiṣṭhiram
|
adyāhamanugān kṛṣṇa karṇasya kṛpaṇān yudhi ||42||

43hantā jvalanasaṃkāśaiḥ śaraiḥ sarpaviṣopamaiḥ
|
adyāhaṃ hemakavacairābaddhamaṇikuṇḍalaiḥ ||43||

44saṃstariṣyāmi govinda vasudhāṃ vasudhādhipaiḥ
|
adyābhimanyoḥ śatrūṇāṃ sarveṣāṃ madhusudana ||44||

45pramathiṣyāmi gātrāṇi śirāṃsi ca śitaiḥ śaraiḥ
|
adya nirdhārtarāṣṭrāṃ ca bhrātre dāsyāmi medinīm ||45||

46nirarjunāṃ vā pṛthivīṃ keśavānucariṣyasi
|
adyāhamanṛṇaḥ kṛṣṇa bhaviṣyāmi dhanurbhṛtām ||46||

47kopasya ca kurūṇāṃ ca śarāṇāṃ gāṇḍivasya ca
|
adya duḥkhamahaṃ mokṣye trayodaśasamārjitam ||47||

48hatvā karṇaṃ raṇe kṛṣṇa śambaraṃ maghavāniva
|
adya karṇe hate yuddhe somakānāṃ mahārathāḥ ||48||

49kṛtaṃ kāryaṃ ca manyantāṃ mitrakāryepsavo yudhi
|
mama caiva kathaṃ prītiḥ śaineyasyādya mādhava ||49||

50bhaviṣyati hate karṇe mayi cāpi jayādhike
|
ahaṃ hatvā raṇe karṇaṃ putraṃ cāsya mahāratham ||50||

51prītiṃ dāsyāmi bhīmasya yamayoḥ sātyakasya ca
|
dhṛṣṭadyumnaśikhaṇḍibhyāṃ pañcālānāṃ ca mādhava ||51||

52adyānṛṇyaṃ gamiṣyāmi hatvā karṇaṃ mahāhave
|
adya paśyantu saṃgrāme dhanañjayamamarṣaṇam ||52||

53yudhyantaṃ kauravānsaṃkhye ghātayantaṃ ca sūtajam
|
bhavatsakāśe vakṣye ca punarevātmasaṃstavam ||53||

54dhanurvede matsamo nāsti loke parākrame vā mama ko'sti tulyaḥ
|
ko vāpyanyo matsamo'sti kṣamāvāṃstathā krodhe sadṛśo'nyo na me'sti ||54||

55ahaṃ dhanuṣmānsasurāsurāṃśca sarvāṇi bhūtāni ca saṅgatāni
|
svabāhuvīryādgamaye parābhavaṃ matpauruṣaṃ viddhi paraṃ parebhyaḥ ||55||

56śarārciṣā gāṇḍivenāhamekaḥ sarvānkurūnbāhlikāṃścābhihatya
|
himātyaye kakṣagato yathāgnistathā daheyaṃ sagaṇānprasahya ||56||

57pāṇau pṛṣatkā likhitā mamaite dhanuśca divyaṃ vitataṃ sabāṇam
|
pādau ca me sarathau sadhvajau ca na mādṛśaṃ yuddhagataṃ jayanti ||57||

58ityevamuktvā'rjuna ekavīraḥ kṣipraṃ ripughnaḥ kṣatajopamākṣaḥ
|
bhīmaṃ mumukṣuḥ samare prayātaḥ karṇasya kāyācca śiro jihīrṣuḥ ||58||
