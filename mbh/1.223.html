Mahābhārata 1.223 (Ādiparvan)

1vaiśaṃpāyana uvāca|
so'bravīdarjunaṃ caiva vāsudevaṃ ca sātvatam
|
lokapravīrau tiṣṭhantau kāṇḍavasya samīpataḥ||1||

2brāhmaṇo bahubhoktā'smi bhuñje'parimitaṃ sadā
|
bhikṣe vārṣṇeyapārthau vāmekāṃ tṛptiṃ prayacchatam||2||

3evamuktau tamabrūtāṃ tatastau kṛṣṇapāṇḍavau
|
kenānnena bhavāṃstṛpyettasyānnasya yatāvahe||3||

4evamuktaḥ sa bhagavānabravīttāvubhau tataḥ
|
bhāṣamāṇau tadā vīrau kimannaṃ kriyatāmiti||4||

5brāhmaṇa uvāca|
nāhamannaṃ bubhukṣe vai pāvakaṃ māṃ nibodhatam
|
yadannamanurūpaṃ me tadyuvāṃ saṃprayacchatam||5||

6idamindraḥ sadā dāvaṃ khāṇḍavaṃ parirakṣati
|
na ca śaknomyahaṃ dagdhuṃ rakṣyamāṇaṃ mahātmanā||6||

7vasatyatra sakhā tasya takṣakaḥ pannagaḥ sadā
|
sagaṇastatkṛte dāvaṃ parirakṣati vajrabhṛt||7||

8tatra bhūtānyanekāni rakṣyate'sya prasaṅgataḥ
|
taṃ didhakṣurna śaknomi dagdhuṃ śakrasya tejasā||8||

9sa māṃ prajvalitaṃ dṛṣṭvā meghāmbhobhiḥ pravarṣati
|
tato dagdhuṃ na śaknomi didhakṣurdāvamīpsitam||9||

10sa yuvābhyāṃ sahāyābhyāmastravidbhyāṃ samāgataḥ
|
daheyaṃ khāṇḍavaṃ dāvametadannaṃ vṛtaṃ mayā||10||

11yuvāṃ hyudakadhārāstā bhūtāni ca samantataḥ
|
uttamāstravidau samyaksarvato vārayiṣyathaḥ||11||

12janamejaya uvāca|
kimarthaṃ bhagavānagniḥ khāṇḍavaṃ dagdhumicchati
|
rakṣyamāṇaṃ mahendreṇa nānāsatvasamāyutam||12||

13na hyetatkāraṇaṃ brahmannalpaṃ saṃprati bhāti me
|
yaddadāha susaṃkruddhaḥ khāṇḍavaṃ havyavāhanaḥ||13||

14etadvistaraśo brahmanśrotumicchāmi tattvataḥ
|
khāṇḍavasya purā dāho yathā samabhavanmune||14||

15vaiśaṃpāyana uvāca|
śṛṇu me bruvato rājansarvametadyathātatham
|
yannimittaṃ dadāhāgniḥ khāṇḍavaṃ pṛthivīpate||15||

16hanta te kathayiṣyāmi paurāṇīmṛṣisaṃstutām
|
kathāmimāṃ naraśreṣṭha khāṇḍavasya vināśinīm||16||

17paurāṇaḥ śrūyate rājanrājā harihayopamaḥ
|
śvetakirnāma vikhyāto balavikramasaṃyutaḥ||17||

18yajvā dānapatirdhīmānyathā nānyo'sti kaścana
|
īje ca sa mahāyajñaiḥ kratubhiścāptadakṣiṇaiḥ||18||

19tasya nānyā'bhavadbuddhirdivase divase nṛpa
|
satre kriyāsamārambhe dāneṣu vividheṣu ca||19||

20ṛtvigbhiḥ sahito dhīmānevamīje sa bhūmipaḥ
|
tatastu ṛtvijaścāsya dhūmavyākulalocanāḥ||20||

21kālena mahatā khinnāstatyajuste narādhipam
|
tataḥ prasādayāmāsa ṛtvijastānmahīpatiḥ||21||

22cakṣurvikalatāṃ prāptā na prapeduśca te kratum
|
tatasteṣāmanumate tadvipraistu narādhipaḥ||22||

23satraṃ samāpayāmāsa ṛtvigbhiraparaiḥ saha
|
tasyaivaṃ vartamānasya kadācitkālaparyaye||23||

24satramahārtukāmasya saṃvatsaraśataṃ kila
|
ṛtvijo nābhyapadyanta samāhartuṃ mahātmanaḥ||24||

25sa ca rājā'karodyatnaṃ mahāntaṃ sasuhṛjjanaḥ
|
praṇipātena sāntvena dānena ca mahāyaśāḥ||25||

26ṛtvijo'nunayāmāsa bhūyo bhūyastvatandritaḥ
|
te cāsya tamabhiprāyaṃ na cakruramitaujasaḥ||26||

27sa cāśramasthānrājarṣistānuvāca ruṣānvitaḥ
|
yadyahaṃ patito viprāḥ śuśrūṣāṃ yāṃ na ca sthitaḥ||27||

28āśu tyājyo'smi yuṣmābhirbrāhmaṇaiśca jugupsitaḥ
|
tannārhatha kratuśraddhāṃ vyāghātayitumadya tām||28||

29asthāne vā parityāgaṃ kartuṃ me dvijasattamāḥ
|
prapanna eva vo viprāḥ prasādaṃ kartumarhatha||29||

30sāntvadānādibhirvākyaistattvataḥ kāryavattayā
|
prasādayitvā vakṣyāmi yannaḥ kāryaṃ dvijottamāḥ||30||

31athavāhaṃ parityakto bhavadbhirdveṣakāraṇāt
|
ṛtvijo'nyāngamiṣyāmi yājanārthaṃ dvijottamāḥ||31||

32etāvaduktvā vacanaṃ virarāma sa pārthivaḥ
|
yadā na śekū rājānaṃ yājanārthaṃ parantapa||32||

33tataste yājakāḥ kruddhāstamūcurnṛpasattamam
|
tava karmāṇyajasraṃ vai vartante pārthivottama||33||

34tato vayaṃ pariśrāntāḥ satataṃ karmavāhinaḥ
|
śramādasmātpariśrāntānsa tvaṃ nastyaktumarhasi||34||

35buddhimohaṃ samāsthāya tvarāsaṃbhāvito'nagha
|
gaccha rudrasakāśaṃ tvaṃ sahi tvāṃ yājayiṣyati||35||

36sādhikṣepaṃ vacaḥ śrutvā saṃkruddhaḥ śvetakirnṛpaḥ
|
kailāsaṃ parvataṃ gatvā tapa ugraṃ samāsthitaḥ||36||

37ārādhayanmahādevaṃ niyataḥ saṃśitavrataḥ
|
upavāsaparo rājandīrghakālamatiṣṭhata||37||

38kadāciddvādaśe kāle kadācidapi ṣoḍaśe
|
āhāramakarodrājā mūlāni ca phalāni ca||38||

39ūrdhvabāhustvanimiṣastiṣṭhansthāṇurivācalaḥ
|
ṣaṇmāsānabhavadrājā śvetakiḥ susamāhitaḥ||39||

40taṃ tathā nṛpaśārdūlaṃ tapyamānaṃ mahattapaḥ
|
śaṅkaraḥ paramaprītyā darśayāmāsa bhārata||40||

41uvāca cainaṃ bhagavānsnigdhagambhīrayā girā
|
prīto'smi naraśārdūla tapasā te parantapa||41||

42varaṃ vṛṇīṣva bhadraṃ te yaṃ tvamicchasi pārthiva
|
etacchrutvā tu vacanaṃ rudrasyāmitatejasaḥ||42||

43praṇipatya mahātmānaṃ rājarṣiḥ pratyabhāṣata
|
yadi me bhagavānprītaḥ sarvalokanamaskṛtaḥ||43||

44svayaṃ māṃ devadeveśa yājayasva sureśvaraḥ
|
etacchrutvā tu vacanaṃ rājñā tena prabhāṣitam||44||

45uvāca bhagavānprītaḥ smitapūrvamidaṃ vacaḥ
|
nāsmākametadviṣaye vartate yājanaṃ prati||45||

46tvayā ca sumahattaptaṃ tapo rājanvarārthinā
|
yājayiṣyāmi rājaṃstvāṃ samayena parantapa||46||

47rudra uvāca|
samā dvādaśa rājendra brahmacārī samāhitaḥ
|
satataṃ tvājyadhārābhiryadi tarpayase'nalam||47||

48kāmaṃ prārthayase yaṃ tvaṃ mattaḥ prāpsyasi taṃ nṛpa
|
evamuktaśca rudreṇa śvetakirmanujādhipaḥ||48||

49tathā cakāra tatsarvaṃ yathoktaṃ śūlapāṇinā
|
pūrṇe tu dvādaśe varṣe punarāyānmaheśvaraḥ||49||

50dṛṣṭvaiva ca sa rājānaṃ śaṅkaro lokabhāvanaḥ
|
uvāca paramaprītaḥ śvetakiṃ nṛpasattamam||50||

51toṣito'haṃ nṛpaśreṣṭha tvayehādyena karmaṇā
|
yājanaṃ brāhmaṇānāṃ tu vidhidṛṣṭaṃ parantapa||51||

52ato'haṃ tvāṃ svayaṃ nādya yājayāmi parantapa
|
mamāṃśastu kṣititale mahābhāgo dvijottamaḥ||52||

53durvāsā iti vikhyātaḥ sa hi tvāṃ yājayiṣyati
|
manniyogānmahātejāḥ saṃbhārāḥ saṃbhriyantu te||53||

54etacchrutvā tu vacanaṃ rudreṇa samudāhṛtam
|
svapuraṃ punarāgamya saṃbhārānpunarārjayat||54||

55tataḥ saṃbhṛtasaṃbhāro bhūyo rudramupāgamat
|
saṃbhṛtā mama saṃbhārāḥ sarvopakaraṇāni ca||55||

56tvatprasādānamahādeva śvo me dīkṣā bhavediti
|
etacchrutvā tu vacanaṃ tasya rājño mahātmanaḥ||56||

57durvāsasaṃ samāhūya rudro vacanamabravīt
|
eṣa rājā mahābhāgaḥ śvetakirdvijasattama||57||

58enaṃ yājaya viprendra manniyogena bhūmipam
|
bāḍhamityeva vacanaṃ rudraṃ tvṛṣiruvāca ha||58||

59tataḥ satraṃ samabhavattasya rājño mahātmanaḥ
|
yathāvidhi yathākālaṃ yathoktaṃ bahudakṣiṇam||59||

60tasminparisamāpte tu rājñaḥ satre mahātmanaḥ
|
durvāsasā'bhyanujñātā vipratasthuḥ sma yājakāḥ||60||

61ye tatra dīkṣitāḥ sarve sadasyāśca mahaujasaḥ
|
so'pi rājanmahābhāgaḥ svapuraṃ prāviśattadā||61||

62pūjyamāno mahābhāgairbrāhmaṇairvedapāragaiḥ
|
bandibhiḥ stūyamānaśca nāgaraiścābhinanditaḥ||62||

63evaṃ vṛttaḥ sa rājarṣiḥ śvetakirnṛpasattamaḥ
|
kālena mahatā cāpi yayau svargamabhiṣṭutaḥ||63||

64ṛtvigbhiḥ sahitaḥ sarvaiḥ sadasyaiśca samanvitaḥ
|
tasya satre papau vahnirhavirdvādaśavatsarān||64||

65satataṃ cājyadhārābhiraikātmye tatra karmaṇi
|
haviṣā ca tato vahniḥ parāṃ tṛptimagacchata||65||

66na caicchatpunarādātuṃ haviranyasya kasyacit
|
pāṇḍuvarṇo vivarṇaśca na yathāvatprakāśate||66||

67tato bhaghavato vahnervikāraḥ samajāyata
|
tejasā viprahīṇaśca glāniścainaṃ samāviśat||67||

68sa lakṣayitvā cātmānaṃ tejohīnaṃ hutāśanaḥ
|
jagāma sadanaṃ puṇyaṃ brahmaṇo lokapūjitam||68||

69tatra brahmāṇamāsīnamidaṃ vacanamabravīt
|
bhagavanparamā prītiḥ kṛtā me śvetakinā||69||

70aruciścābhavattīvrā tāṃ na śaknomyapohitum
|
tejasā viprahīṇo'smi balena ca jagatpate||70||

71iccheyaṃ tvatprasādena svātmanaḥ prakṛtiṃ sthirām
|
etacchrutvā hutavahādbhagavānsarvalokakṛt||71||

72havyavāhamidaṃ vākyamuvāca prahasanniva
|
tvayā dvādaśa varṣāṇi vasordhārāhutaṃ haviḥ||72||

73upayuktaṃ mahābhāga tena tvāṃ glānirāviśat
|
tejasā viprahīṇatvātsahasā havyavāhana||73||

74māgamastvaṃ yathā vahne prakṛtistho bhaviṣyasi
|
aruciṃ nāśayiṣyehaṃ samayaṃ pratipadyate||74||

75purā devaniyogena yattvayā bhasmasātkṛtam
|
ālayaṃ devaśatrūṇāṃ sughoraṃ khāṇḍavaṃ vanam||75||

76tatra sarvāṇi satvāni nivasanti vibhāvaso
|
teṣāṃ tvaṃ medasā tṛptaḥ prakṛtistho bhaviṣyasi||76||

77gaccha śīghraṃ pradagdhuṃ tvaṃ tato mokṣyasi kilviṣāt
|
etacchutvā tu vacanaṃ parameṣṭhimukhāccyutam||77||

78uttamaṃ javamāsthāya pradudrāva hutāśanaḥ
|
āgamya khāṇḍavaṃ dāvamuttamaṃ vīryamāsthitaḥ
|
sahasā prājvalaccāgniḥ kruddho vāyusamīritaḥ||78||

79pradīptaṃ khāṇḍavaṃ dṛṣṭvā ye syustatra nivāsinaḥ
|
paramaṃ yatnamātiṣṭhanpāvakasya praśāntaye||79||

80karaistu kariṇaḥ śīghraṃ jalamādāya satvarāḥ
|
siṣicuḥ pāvakaṃ kruddhāḥ śataśo'tha sahasraśaḥ||80||

81bahuśīrṣāstato nāgāḥ śirobhirjalasantatim
|
mumucuḥ pāvakābhyāśe satvarāḥ krodhamūrcchitāḥ||81||

82tathaivānyāni satvāni nānāpraharaṇodyamaiḥ
|
vilayaṃ pāvakaṃ śīghramanayanbharatarṣabha||82||

83anena tu prakāreṇa bhūyo bhūyaśca prajvalan
|
saptakṛtvaḥ praśamitaḥ khāṇḍave havyavāhanaḥ||83||
