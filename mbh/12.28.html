Mahābhārata 12.28 (Śāntiparvan)

1vaiśampāyana uvāca|
jñātiśokābhitaptasya prāṇānabhyutsisṛkṣataḥ
|
jyeṣṭhasya pāṇḍuputrasya vyāsaḥ śokamapānudat ||1||

2vyāsa uvāca |
atrāpyudāharantīmamitihāsaṃ purātanam
|
aśmagītaṃ naravyāghra tannibodha yudhiṣṭhira ||2||

3aśmānaṃ brāhmaṇaṃ prājñaṃ vaideho janako nṛpaḥ
|
saṃśayaṃ paripapraccha duḥkhaśokasamanvitaḥ ||3||

4janaka uvāca |
āgame yadi vā'pāye jñātīnāṃ draviṇasya ca
|
nareṇa pratipattavyaṃ kalyāṇaṃ kathamicchatā ||4||

5aśmovāca |
utpannamimamātmānaṃ narasyānantaraṃ tataḥ
|
tāni tānyanuvartante duḥkhāni ca sukhāni ca ||5||

6teṣāmanyatarāpattau yadyadevopapadyate
|
tadasya cetanāmāśu haratyabhramivānilaḥ ||6||

7abhijāto'smi siddho'smi nāsmi kevalamānuṣaḥ
|
ityebhirhetubhistasya tribhiścittaṃ prasicyate ||7||

8samprasaktamanā bhogān visṛjya pitṛsaṃcitān
|
parikṣīṇaḥ parasvānāmādānaṃ sādhu manyate ||8||

9tamatikrāntamaryādamādadānamasāmpratam
|
pratiṣedhanti rājāno lubdhā mṛgamiveṣubhiḥ ||9||

10ye ca viṃśativarṣā vā triṃśadvarṣāśca mānavāḥ
|
pareṇa te varṣaśatānna bhaviṣyanti pārthiva ||10||

11teṣāṃ paramaduḥkhānāṃ buddhyā bhaiṣajyamācaret
|
sarvaprāṇabhṛtāṃ vṛttaṃ prekṣamāṇastatastataḥ ||11||

12mānasānāṃ punaryonirduḥkhānāṃ cittavibhramaḥ
|
aniṣṭopanipāto vā tṛtīyaṃ nopapadyate ||12||

13evametāni duḥkhāni tāni tānīha mānavam
|
vividhānyupavartante tathā saṃsparśajānyapi ||13||

14jarāmṛtyū hi bhūtānāṃ khāditārau vṛkāviva
|
balināṃ durbalānāṃ ca hrasvānāṃ mahatāmapi ||14||

15na kaścijjātvatikrāmejjarāmṛtyū hi mānavaḥ
|
api sāgaraparyantāṃ vijityemāṃ vasundharām ||15||

16sukhaṃ vā yadi vā duḥkhaṃ bhūtānāṃ paryupasthitam
|
prāptavyamavaśaiḥ sarvaṃ parihāro na vidyate ||16||

17pūrve vayasi madhye vā'pyuttare vā narādhipa
|
avarjanīyāste'rthā vai kāṃkṣitā ye tato'nyathā ||17||

18apriyaiḥ saha saṃyogo viprayogaśca supriyaiḥ
|
arthānarthau sukhaṃ duḥkhaṃ vidhānamanuvartate ||18||

19prādurbhāvaśca bhūtānāṃ dehatyāgastathaiva ca
|
prāptirvyāyāmayogaśca sarvametatpratiṣṭhitam ||19||

20gandha-varṇa-rasa-sparśā nivartante svabhāvataḥ
|
tathaiva sukhaduḥkhāni vidhānamanuvartate ||20||

21āsanaṃ śayanaṃ yānamutthānaṃ pānabhojanam
|
niyataṃ sarvabhūtānāṃ kālenaiva bhavatyuta ||21||

22vaidyāścāpyāturāḥ santi balavantaśca durbalāḥ
|
śrīmantaścāpare ṣaṇḍhā vicitraḥ kālaparyayaḥ ||22||

23kule janma, tathā vīryamārogyaṃ rūpameva ca
|
saubhāgyamupabhogaśca bhavitavyena labhyate ||23||

24santi putrāḥ subahavo daridrāṇāmanicchatām
|
nāstiḥ putraḥ samṛddhānāṃ vicitraṃ vidhiceṣṭim ||24||

25vyādhiragnirjalaṃ śastraṃ bubhukṣāścāpado viṣam
|
jvaraśca maraṇaṃ jantoruccācca patanaṃ tathā ||25||

26nirmāṇe yasya yaddiṣṭaṃ tena gacchati setunā
|
dṛśyate nāpyatikrāmanna niṣkrānto'thavā punaḥ ||26||

27dṛśyate cāpyatikrāmanna nigrāhyo'thavā punaḥ
|
dṛśyate hi yuvaiveha vinaśyanvasumānnaraḥ
|
daridraśca parikliṣṭaḥ śatavarṣo jarānvitaḥ ||27||

28akiñcanāśca dṛśyante puruṣāścirajīvinaḥ
|
samṛddhe ca kule jātā vinaśyanti pataṅgavat ||28||

29prāyeṇa śrīmatāṃ loke bhoktuṃ śaktirna vidyate
|
kāṣṭhānyapi hi jīryante daridrāṇāṃ ca sarvaśaḥ ||29||

30ahametatkaromīti manyate kālanoditaḥ
|
yadyadiṣṭamasantoṣāddurātmā pāpamācaret ||30||

31mṛgayā'kṣāḥ striyaḥ pānaṃ prasaṅgā ninditā budhaiḥ
|
dṛśyante puruṣāścātra samprayuktā bahuśrutāḥ ||31||

32iti kālena sarvārthānīpsitānīpsitāniha
|
spṛśanti sarvabhūtāni nimittaṃ nopalabhyate ||32||

33vāyumākāśamagniṃ ca candrādityāvahaḥkṣape
|
jyotīṃṣi saritaḥ śailān kaḥ karoti bibharti ca ||33||

34śītamuṣṇaṃ tathā varṣaṃ kālena parivartate
|
evameva manuṣyāṇāṃ sukhaduḥkhe nararṣabha ||34||

35nauṣadhāni na mantrāśca na homā na punarjapāḥ
|
trāyante mṛtyunopetaṃ jarayā cāpi mānavam ||35||

36yathā kāṣṭhaṃ ca kāṣṭhaṃ ca sameyātāṃ mahodadhau
|
sametya ca vyapeyātāṃ tadvadbhūtasamāgamaḥ ||36||

37ye caiva puruṣāḥ strībhirgītavādyairupasthitāḥ
|
ya cānāthāḥ parānnādā: kālasteṣu samakriyaḥ ||37||

38mātāpitṛsahasrāṇi putradāraśatāni ca
|
saṃsāreṣvanubhūtāni kasya te kasya vā vayam ||38||

39naivāsya kaścidbhavitā nāyaṃ bhavati kasyacit
|
pathi saṅgatamevedaṃ dārabandhusuhṛjjanaiḥ ||39||

40kvāse kva ca gamiṣyāmi ko nvahaṃ kimihāsthitaḥ
|
kasmātkimanuśoceyamityevaṃ sthāpayenmanaḥ ||40||

41anitye priyasaṃvāse saṃsāre cakravadgatau
|
pathi saṅgatamevaitadbhrātā mātā pitā sakhā ||41||

42na dṛṣṭapūrvaṃ pratyakṣaṃ paralokaṃ vidurbudhāḥ
|
āgamāṃstvanatikramya śraddhātavyaṃ bubhūṣatā ||42||

43kurvīta pitṛdaivatyaṃ dharmyāṇi ca samācaret
|
yajecca vidvānvidhivattrivargaṃ cāpyupācaret ||43||

44sannimajjejjagadidaṃ gambhīre kālasāgare
|
jarāmṛtyumahāgrāhe na kaścidavabudhyate ||44||

45āyurvedamadhīyānāḥ kevalaṃ saparigrahāḥ
|
dṛśyante bahavo vaidyā vyādhibhiḥ samabhiplutāḥ ||45||

46te pibantaḥ kaṣāyāṃśca sarpīṃṣi vividhāni ca
|
na mṛtyumativartante velāmiva mahodadhiḥ ||46||

47rasāyanavidaścaiva suprayuktarasāyanāḥ
|
dṛśyante jarayā bhagnā nagā nāgairivottamaiḥ ||47||

48tathaiva tapasopetāḥ svādhyāyābhyasane ratāḥ
|
dātāro yajñaśīlāśca na taranti jarāntakau ||48||

49na hyahāni nivartante na māsā na punaḥ samāḥ
|
jātānāṃ sarvabhūtānāṃ na pakṣā na punaḥ kṣapāḥ ||49||

50so'yaṃ vipulamadhvānaṃ kālena dhruvamadhruvaḥ
|
naro'vaśaḥ samabhyeti sarvabhūtaniṣevitam ||50||

51deho vā jīvato'bhyeti jīvo vābhyeti dehataḥ
|
pathi saṅgamamabhyeti dārairanyaiśca bandhubhiḥ ||51||

52nāyamatyantasaṃvāso labhyate jātu kenacit
|
api svena śarīreṇa kimutānyena kenacit ||52||

53kva nu te'dya pitā rājan kva nu te'dya pitāmahāḥ
|
na tvaṃ paśyasi tānadya na tvāṃ paśyanti te'nagha ||53||

54na caiva puruṣo draṣṭā svargasya narakasya ca
|
āgamastu satāṃ cakṣurnṛpate tamihācara ||54||

55caritabrahmacaryo hi prajāyeta yajeta ca
|
pitṛ-deva-manuṣyāṇāmānṛṇyādanasūyakaḥ ||55||

56sa yajñaśīlaḥ prajane niviṣṭaḥ prāgbrahmacārī praviviktacakṣuḥ
|
ārādhayetsvargamimaṃ ca lokaṃ paraṃ ca muktvā hṛdayavyalīkam ||56||

57samaṃ hi dharmaṃ carato nṛpasya dravyāṇi cābhyāharato yathāvat
|
pravṛttadharmasya yaśo'bhivardhate sarveṣu lokeṣu carācareṣu ||57||

58ityevamājñāya videharājo vākyaṃ samagraṃ paripūrṇahetuḥ
|
aśmānamāmaṃtrya viśuddhabuddhiryayau gṛhaṃ svaṃ prati śāntaśokaḥ ||58||

59tathā tvamapyacyuta muñca śokamuttiṣṭha śakropama harṣamehi
|
kṣātreṇa dharmeṇa mahī jitā te tāṃ bhuṅkṣva kuntīsuta māvamaṃsthāḥ ||59||
