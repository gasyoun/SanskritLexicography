Mahābhārata 12.199 (Śāntiparvan)

1yudhiṣṭhira uvāca|
kālamṛtyuyamānāṃ te ikṣvākorbrāhmaṇasya ca
|
vivādo vyāhṛtaḥ pūrvaṃ tadbhavānvaktumarhati ||1||

2bhīṣma uvāca|
atrāpyudāharantīmamitihāsaṃ purātanam
|
ikṣvākoḥ sūryaputrasya tadvṛttaṃ brāhmaṇasya ca ||2||

3kālasya mṛtyośca tathā yadvṛttaṃ tannibodha me
|
yathā sa teṣāṃ saṃvādo yasminsthāne'pi cābhavat ||3||

4brāhmaṇo jāpakaḥ kaściddharmavṛtto mahāyaśāḥ
|
ṣaḍaṅgavinmahāprājñaḥ paippalādiḥ sa kauśikaḥ ||4||

5tasyāparokṣaṃ vijñānaṃ ṣaḍaṅgeṣu babhūva ha
|
vedeṣu caiva niṣṇāto himavatpādasaṃśrayaḥ ||5||

6sodyaṃ brāhmaṃ tapastepe saṃhitāṃ saṃyato japan
|
tasya varṣasahasraṃ tu niyamena tathā gatam ||6||

7sa devyā darśitaḥ sākṣāt prītā'smīti tadā kila
|
jaṣyamāvartayaṃstūṣṇīṃ na sa tāṃ kiñcidabravīt ||7||

8tasyānukampayā devī prītā samabhavattadā
|
vedamātā tatastasya tajjapyaṃ samapūjayat ||8||

9samāptajapyastūttthāya śirasā pādayostadā
|
papāta devyā dharmātmā vacanaṃ cedamabravīt ||9||

10diṣṭyā devi prasannā tvaṃ darśanaṃ cāgatā mama
|
yadi cāpi prasannāsi japye me ramatāṃ manaḥ ||10||

11sāvitryuvāca |
kiṃ prārthayasi viprarṣe kiṃ ceṣṭaṃ karavāṇi te
|
prabrūhi japatāṃ śreṣṭha sarvaṃ tatte bhaviṣyati ||11||

12ityuktaḥ sa tadā devyā vipraḥ provāca dharmavit
|
japyaṃ prati mameccheyaṃ vardhatviti punaḥ punaḥ ||12||

13manasaśca samādhirme vardhetāharahaḥ śubhe
|
tattatheti tato devī madhuraṃ pratyabhāṣata ||13||

14idaṃ caivāparaṃ prāha devī tatpriyakāmyayā
|
nirayaṃ naiva yātā tvaṃ yatra yātā dvijarṣabhāḥ ||14||

15yāsyasi brahmaṇaḥ sthānamanimittamaninditam
|
sādhaye bhavitā caitadyattvayā'hamihārthitā ||15||

16niyato japa caikāgro dharmastvāṃ samupaiṣyati
|
kālo mṛtyuryamaścaiva samāyāsyanti te'ntikam ||16||

17bhavitā ca vivādo'tra tava teṣāṃ ca dharmataḥ
|
bhīṣma uvāca|
evamuktvā bhagavatī jagāma bhavanaṃ svakam ||17||

18brāhmaṇo'pi japannāste divyaṃ varṣaśataṃ tathā
|
sadā dānto jitakrodhaḥ satyasandho'nasūyakaḥ ||18||

19samāpte niyame tasminnatha viprasya dhīmataḥ
|
sākṣātprītastadā dharmo darśayāmāsa taṃ dvijam ||19||

20dharma uvāca |
dvijāte paśya māṃ dharmamahaṃ tvāṃ draṣṭumāgataḥ
|
japyasyāsya phalaṃ yattatsamprāptaṃ tacca me śṛṇu ||20||

21jitā lokāstvayā sarve ye divyā ye ca mānuṣāḥ
|
devānāṃ nilayānsādho sarvānutkramya yāsyasi ||21||

22prāṇatyāgaṃ kuru mune gaccha lokānyathepsitān
|
tyaktvā''tmanaḥ śarīraṃ ca tato lokānavāpsyasi ||22||

23brāhmaṇa uvāca|
kiṃ nu lokairhi me dharma gaccha tvaṃ ca yathāsukham
|
bahuduḥkhasukhaṃ dehaṃ notsṛjeyamahaṃ vibho ||23||

24dharma uvāca |
avaśyaṃ bhoḥ śarīraṃ te tyaktavyaṃ munipuṅgava
|
svargamāroha bho vipra kiṃ vā vai rocate'nagha ||24||

25brāhmaṇa uvāca|
na rocaye svargavāsaṃ vinā dehamahaṃ vibho
|
gaccha dharma na me śraddhā svargaṃ gantuṃ vinā''tmanā ||25||

26dharma uvāca |
alaṃ dehe manaḥ kṛtvā tyaktvā dehaṃ sukhī bhava
|
gaccha lokānarajaso yatra gatvā na śocasi ||26||

27brāhmaṇa uvāca|
rame japan mahābhāga kiṃ nu lokaiḥ sanātanaiḥ
|
saśarīreṇa gantavyaṃ mayā svargaṃ na vā vibho ||27||

28dharma uvāca |
yadi tvaṃ necchase tyaktuṃ śarīraṃ paśya vai dvija
|
eṣa kālastathā mṛtyuryamaśca tvāmupāgatāḥ ||28||

29bhīṣma uvāca|
atha vaivasvataḥ kālo mṛtyuśca tritayaṃ vibho
|
brāhmaṇaṃ taṃ mahābhāgamupagamyedamabruvan ||29||

30yama uvāca |
tapaso'sya sutaptasya tathā sucaritasya ca
|
phalaprāptistava śreṣṭhā yamo'haṃ tvāmupabruve ||30||

31kāla uvāca |
yathāvadasya japyasya phalaṃ prāptamanuttamam
|
kālaste svargamāroḍhuṃ kālo'haṃ tvāmupāgataḥ ||31||

32mṛtyuruvāca |
mṛtyu māṃ viddhi dharmajña rūpiṇaṃ svayamāgatam
|
kālena codito vipra tvāmito netumadya vai ||32||

33brāhmaṇa uvāca|
svāgataṃ sūryaputrāya kālāya ca mahātmane
|
mṛtyave cātha dharmāya kiṃ kāryaṃ karavāṇi vaḥ ||33||

34bhīṣma uvāca|
ayaṃ pādyaṃ ca dattvā sa tebhyastatra samāgame
|
abravītparamaprītaḥ svaśaktyā kiṃ karomi vaḥ ||34||

35tasminnevātha kāle tu tīrthayātrāmupāgataḥ
|
ikṣvākuragamattatra sametā yatra te vibho ||35||

36sarvāneva tu rājarṣiḥ sampūjyātha praṇamya ca
|
kuśalapraśanamakarotsarveṣāṃ rājasattamaḥ ||36||

37tasmai so'thāsanaṃ dattvā pādyamarghyaṃ tathaiva ca
|
abravīdbrāhmaṇo vākyaṃ kṛtvā kuśalasaṃvidam ||37||

38svāgataṃ te mahārāja brūhi yadyadihecchasi
|
svaśaktyā kiṃ karomīha tadbhavānprabravītu mām ||38||

39rājovāca |
rājā'haṃ brāhmaṇaśca tvaṃ yadā ṣaṭkarmasaṃsthitaḥ
|
dadāni vasu kiṃcitte prathitaṃ tadvadasva me ||39||

40brāhmaṇa uvāca|
dvividhā brāhmaṇā rājan dharmaśca dvividhaḥ smṛtaḥ
|
pravṛttāśca nivṛttāśca nivṛtto'haṃ pratigrahāt ||40||

41tebhyaḥ prayaccha dānāni ye pravṛttā narādhipa
|
ahaṃ na pratigṛhṇāmi kimiṣṭaṃ kiṃ dadāmi te
|
brūhi tvaṃ nṛpatiśreṣṭha tapasā sādhayāmi kim ||41||

42rājovāca |
kṣatriyo'haṃ na jānāmi dehīti vacanaṃ kvacit
|
prayaccha yuddhamityevaṃvādinaḥ smo dvijottama ||42||

43brāhmaṇa uvāca|
tuṣyasi tvaṃ svadharmeṇa tathā tuṣṭā vayaṃ nṛpa
|
anyonyasyāntaraṃ nāsti yadiṣṭaṃ tat samācara ||43||

44rājovāca |
svaśaktyāhaṃ dadānīti tvayā pūrvamudāhṛtam
|
yāce tvāṃ dīyatāṃ mahyaṃ japyasyāsya phalaṃ dvija ||44||

45brāhmaṇa uvāca|
yuddhaṃ mama sadā vāṇī yācatīti vikatthase
|
na ca yuddhaṃ mayā sādhu kimarthaṃ yācase punaḥ ||45||

46rājovāca |
vāgvajrā brāhmaṇāḥ proktāḥ kṣatriyā bāhujīvinaḥ
|
vāgyuddhaṃ tadidaṃ tīvraṃ mama vipra tvayā saha ||46||

47brāhmaṇa uvāca|
saivādyāpi pratijñā me svaśaktyā kiṃ pradīyatām
|
brūhi dāsyāmi rājendra vibhave sati mā ciram ||47||

48rājovāca |
yattadvarṣaśataṃ pūrṇaṃ japyaṃ vai japatā tvayā
|
phalaṃ prāptaṃ tatprayaccha mama ditsurbhavān yadi ||48||

49brāhmaṇa uvāca|
paramaṃ gṛhyatāṃ tasya phalaṃ yajjapitaṃ mayā
|
ardhaṃ tvamavicāreṇa phalaṃ tasya hyavāpnuhi ||49||

50athavā sarvameveha māmakaṃ jāpakaṃ phalam
|
rājanprāpnuhi kāmaṃ tvaṃ yadi sarvamihecchasi ||50||

51rājovāca |
kṛtaṃ sarveṇa bhadraṃ te japyaṃ yadyācitaṃ mayā
|
svasti te'stu gamiṣyāmi kiñca tasya phalaṃ vada ||51||

52brāhmaṇa uvāca|
phalaprāptiṃ na jānāmi dattaṃ yajjapitaṃ mayā
|
ayaṃ dharmaśca kālaśca yamo mṛtyuśca sākṣiṇaḥ ||52||

53rājovāca |
ajñātamasya dharmasya phalaṃ kiṃ me kariṣyati
|
phalaṃ bravīṣi dharmasya na cejjapyakṛtasya mām
|
prāpnotu tatphalaṃ vipro nāhamicche sasaṃśayam ||53||

54brāhmaṇa uvāca|
nādade'paravaktavyaṃ dattaṃ cāsya phalaṃ mayā
|
vākyaṃ pramāṇaṃ rājarṣe mamādya tava caiva hi ||54||

55nābhisandhirmayā japye kṛtapūrvaḥ kadācana
|
jaṣyasya rājaśārdūla kathaṃ vetsyāmyahaṃ phalam ||55||

56dadasveti tvayā coktaṃ dadānīti mayā tathā
|
na vācaṃ dūṣayiṣyāmi satyaṃ rakṣa sthiro bhava ||56||

57athaivaṃ vadato me'dya vacanaṃ na kariṣyasi
|
mahānadharmo bhavitā tava rājanmṛṣā kṛtaḥ ||57||

58na yuktaṃ tu mṛṣā vāṇī tvayā vaktumarindam
|
tathā mayā'pyabhihitaṃ mithyā kartuṃ na śakyate ||58||

59saṃśrutaṃ ca mayā pūrvaṃ dadānītyavicāritam
|
tadgṛhṇīṣvāvicāreṇa yadi satye sthito bhavān ||59||

60ihāgamya hi māṃ rājan jāpyaṃ phalamayācathāḥ
|
tanme nisṛṣṭaṃ gṛhṇīṣva bhava satye sthiro'pi ca ||60||

61nāyaṃ loko'sti na paro na ca pūrvān sa tārayet
|
kuta eva janiṣyāṃstu mṛṣāvādaparāyaṇaḥ ||61||

62na yajñaphaladānāni niyamāstārayanti hi
|
yathā satyaṃ pare loke tatheha puruṣarṣabha ||62||

63tapāṃsi yāni cīrṇāni cariṣyanti ca yattapaḥ
|
śataiḥ śatasahasraiśca taiḥ satyānna viśiṣyate ||63||

64satyamekākṣaraṃ brahma satyamekākṣaraṃ tapaḥ
|
satyamekākṣaro yajñaḥ satyamekākṣaraṃ śrutam ||64||

65satyaṃ vedeṣu jāgarti phalaṃ satye paraṃ smṛtam
|
satyāddharmo damaścaiva sarvaṃ satye pratiṣṭhitam ||65||

66satyaṃ vedāstathāṅgāni satyaṃ vidyāstathā vidhiḥ
|
vratacaryā tathā satyamoṅkāraḥ satyameva ca ||66||

67prāṇināṃ jananaṃ satyaṃ satyaṃ santatireva ca
|
satyena vāyurabhyeti satyena tapate raviḥ ||67||

68satyena cāgnirdahati svargaḥ satye pratiṣṭhitaḥ
|
satyaṃ yajñastapo vedāḥ stobhā mantrāḥ sarasvatī ||68||

69tulāmāropito dharmaḥ satyaṃ caiveti naḥ śrutam
|
samakakṣāṃ tulayato yataḥ satyaṃ tato'dhikam ||69||

70yato dharmastataḥ satyaṃ sarvaṃ satyena vardhate
|
kimarthamanṛtaṃ karma kartuṃ rājaṃstvamicchasi ||70||

71satye kuru sthiraṃ bhāvaṃ mā rājannanṛtaṃ kṛthāḥ
|
kasmāttvamanṛtaṃ vākyaṃ dehīti kuruṣe'śubham ||71||

72yadi japyaphalaṃ dattaṃ mayā naiṣiṣyase nṛpa
|
dharmebhyaḥ samparibhraṣṭo lokānanucariṣyasi ||72||

73saṃśrutya yo na ditseta yācitvā yaśca necchati
|
ubhāvānṛtikāvetau na mṛṣā kartumarhasi ||73||

74rājovāca |
yoddhavyaṃ rakṣitavyaṃ ca kṣatradharmaḥ kila dvija
|
dātāraḥ kṣatriyāḥ proktā gṛhṇīyāṃ bhavataḥ katham ||74||

75brāhmaṇa uvāca|
na cchandayāmi te rājannāpi te gṛhamāvrajam
|
ihā''gamya tu yācitvā na gṛhṇīṣe punaḥ katham ||75||

76dharma uvāca |
avivādo'stu yuvayorvitta māṃ dharmamāgatam
|
dvijo dānaphalairyukto rājā satyaphalena ca ||76||

77svarga uvāca |
svargaṃ māṃ viddhi rājendra rūpiṇaṃ svayamāgatam
|
avivādo'stu yuvayorubhau tulyaphalau yuvām ||77||

78rājovāca |
kṛtaṃ svargeṇa me kāryaṃ gaccha svargaṃ yathāgatam
|
vipro yadīcchate gantuṃ cīrṇaṃ gṛhṇātu me phalam ||78||

79brāhmaṇa uvāca|
bālye yadi syādajñānānmayā hastaḥ prasāritaḥ
|
nivṛttalakṣaṇaṃ dharmamupāse saṃhitāṃ japan ||79||

80nivṛttaṃ māṃ cirādrājanvipralobhayase katham
|
svena kāryaṃ kariṣyāmi tvatto necche phalaṃ nṛpa
|
tapaḥsvādhyāyaśīlo'haṃ nivṛttaśca pratigrahāt ||80||

81rājovāca |
yadi vipra visṛṣṭaṃ te japyasya phalamuttamam
|
āvayoryatphalaṃ kiñcitsahitaṃ nau tadastviha ||81||

82dvijāḥ pratigrahe yuktā dātāro rājavaṃśajāḥ
|
yadi dharmaḥ śruto vipra sahaiva phalamastu nau ||82||

83mā vā bhūtsahabhojyaṃ nau madīyaṃ phalamāpnuhi
|
pratīccha matkṛtaṃ dharmaṃ yadi te mayyanugrahaḥ ||83||

84bhīṣma uvāca|
tato vikṛtaveṣau dvau puruṣau samupasthitau
|
gṛhītvā'nyonyamāveṣṭya kucailāvūcaturvacaḥ ||84||

85na me dhārayasītyeko dhārayāmīti cāparaḥ
|
ihāsti nau vivādo'yamayaṃ rājānuśāsakaḥ ||85||

86satyaṃ bravīmyahamidaṃ na me dhārayate bhavān
|
anṛtaṃ vadasīha tvamṛṇaṃ te dhārayāmyaham ||86||

87tāvubhau subhṛśaṃ taptau rājānamidamūcatuḥ
|
parīkṣya tvaṃ yathā syāvo nāvāmiha vigarhitau ||87||

88virūpa uvāca |
dhārayāmi naravyāghra vikṛtasyeha goḥ phalam
|
dadataśca na gṛhṇāti vikṛto me mahīpate ||88||

89vikṛta uvāca |
na me dhārayate kiñcidvirūpo'yaṃ narādhipa
|
mithyā bravītyayaṃ hi tvāṃ satyābhāsaṃ narādhipa ||89||

90rājovāca |
virūpa kiṃ dhārayate bhavānasya bravītu me
|
śrutvā tathā kariṣye'hamiti me dhīyate manaḥ ||90||

91virūpa uvāca |
śṛṇuṣvāvahito rājan yathaitaddhārayāmyaham
|
vikṛtasyāsya rājarṣe nikhilena narādhipa ||91||

92anena dharmaprāptyarthaṃ śubhā dattā purā'nagha
|
dhenurviprāya rājarṣe tapaḥsvādhyāyaśīline ||92||

93tasyāścāyaṃ mayā rājan phalamabhyetya yācitaḥ
|
vikṛtena ca me dattaṃ viśuddhenāntarātmanā ||93||

94tato me sukṛtaṃ karma kṛtamātmaviśuddhaye
|
gāvau ca kapile krītvā vatsale bahudohane ||94||

95te coñcchavṛttaye rājanmayā samapavarjite
|
yathāvidhi yathāśraddhaṃ tadasyāhaṃ punaḥ prabho ||95||

96ihādyaiva gṛhītvā tu prayacchedviguṇaṃ phalam
|
evaṃ syātpuruṣavyāghra kaḥ śuddhaḥ ko'tra doṣavān ||96||

97evaṃ vivadamānau svastvāmihābhyāgatau nṛpa
|
kuru dharmamadharmaṃ vā vinaye nau samādadha ||97||

98yadi necchati me dānaṃ yathā dattamanena vai
|
bhavānatra sthiro bhūtvā mārge sthāpayitā'dya nau ||98||

99rājovāca |
dīyamānaṃ na gṛhṇāsi ṛṇaṃ kasmāttvamadya vai
|
yathaiva te'bhyanujñātaṃ tathā gṛhṇīṣva mā ciram ||99||

100vikṛta uvāca |
dhārayāmītyanenoktaṃ dadānīti tathā mayā
|
nāyaṃ me dhārayatyadya gacchatāṃ yana vāñchati ||100||

101rājovāca |
dadato'sya na gṛhṇāsi viṣamaṃ pratibhāti me
|
daṇḍyo hi tvaṃ mama mato nāstyatra khalu saṃśayaḥ ||101||

102vikṛta uvāca |
mayā'sya dattaṃ rājarṣe gṛhṇīyāṃ tatkathaṃ punaḥ
|
kāmamatrāparādho me daṇḍamājñāpaya prabho ||102||

103virūpa uvāca |
dīyamānaṃ yadi mayā neṣiṣyasi kathañcana
|
niyaṃsyati tvāṃ nṛpatirayaṃ dharmānuśāsakaḥ ||103||

104vikṛta uvāca |
svaṃ mayā yāciteneha dattaṃ kathamihādya tat
|
gṛhṇīyāṃ gacchatu bhavānabhyanujñāṃ dadāni te ||104||

105brāhmaṇa uvāca|
śrutametattvayā rājannanayoḥ kathitaṃ dvayoḥ
|
pratijñātaṃ mayā yatte tadgṛhāṇāvicāritam ||105||

106rājovāca |
prastutaṃ sumahatkāryamanayorgahvaraṃ yathā
|
jāpakasya dṛḍhīkāraḥ kathametadbhaviṣyati ||106||

107yadi tāvanna gṛhṇāmi brāhmaṇenāpavarjitam
|
kathaṃ na lipyeyamahaṃ pāpena mahatā'dya vai ||107||

108tau covāca sa rājarṣiḥ kṛtakāryau gamiṣyathaḥ
|
nedānīṃ māmihāsādya rājadharmo bhavenmṛṣā ||108||

109svadharmaḥ paripālyastu rājñāmiti viniścayaḥ
|
vipradharmaśca gahano māmanātmānamāviśat ||109||

110brāhmaṇa uvāca|
gṛhāṇa dhāraye'haṃ ca yācitaṃ saṃśrutaṃ mayā
|
na cedgrahīṣyase rājan śapiṣye tvāṃ na saṃśayaḥ ||110||

111rājovāca |
dhigrājadharmaṃ yasyāyaṃ kāryasyeha viniścayaḥ
|
ityarthaṃ me grahītavyaṃ kathaṃ tulyaṃ bhavediti ||111||

112eṣa pāṇirapūrvaṃ me nikṣepārthaṃ prasāritaḥ
|
yanme dhārayase vipra tadidānīṃ pradīyatām ||112||

113brāhmaṇa uvāca|
saṃhitāṃ japatā yāvān guṇaḥ kaścitkṛto mayā
|
tatsarvaṃ pratigṛhṇīṣva yadi kiñcidihāsti me ||113||

114rājovāca |
jalametannipatitaṃ mama pāṇau dvijottama
|
samamastu sahaivāstu pratigṛhṇātu vai bhavān ||114||

115virūpa uvāca |
kāmakrodhau viddhi nau tvamāvābhyāṃ kārito bhavān
|
saheti ca yaduktaṃ te samā lokāstavāsya ca ||115||

116nāyaṃ dhārayate kiñcijjijñāsā tvatkṛte kṛtā
|
kālo dharmastathā mṛtyuḥ kāmakrodhau tathā yuvām ||116||

117sarvamanyonyaniṣkarṣe nighṛṣṭaṃ paśyatastava
|
gaccha lokān jitān svena karmaṇā yatra vāñcchasi ||117||

118bhīṣma uvāca|
jāpakānāṃ phalāvāptirmayā te sampradarśitā
|
gatiḥ sthānaṃ ca lokāśca jāpakena yathā jitāḥ ||118||

119prayāti saṃhitādhyāyī brahmāṇaṃ parameṣṭhinam
|
athavā'gniṃ samāyāti sūryamāviśate'pi vā ||119||
