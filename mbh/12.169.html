Mahābhārata 12.169 (Śāntiparvan)

1bhīṣma uvāca|
tasyāṃ niśāyāṃ vyuṣṭāyāṃ gate tasmin dvijottame
|
niṣkramya gautamo'gacchat samudraṃ prati bhārata ||1||

2sāmudrikānsavaṇijastato'paśyat sthitān pathi
|
sa tena saha sārthena prayayau sāgaraṃ prati ||2||

3sa tu sārtho mahān rājan kasmiṃścidgirigahvare
|
mattena dviradenātha nihataḥ prāyaśo'bhavat ||3||

4sa kathaṃcidbhayāttasmādvimukto brāhmaṇastathā
|
kāṃdigbhūto jīvitārthī pradudrāvottarāṃ diśam ||4||

5sa tu sārthaparibhraṣṭastasmāddeśāt tathā cyutaḥ
|
ekākī vyacarattatra vane kiṃpuruṣo yathā ||5||

6sa panthānamathāsādya samudrābhisaraṃ tadā
|
āsasāda vanaṃ ramyaṃ divyaṃ puṣpitapādapam ||6||

7sarvartukairāmravaṇaiḥ puṣpitairupaśobhitam
|
nandanoddeśasadṛśaṃ yakṣakinnarasevitam ||7||

8śālaistālaistamālaiśca kālāguruvanaistathā
|
candanasya ca mukhyasya pādapairupaśobhitam
|
giriprastheṣu ramyeṣu teṣu teṣu sugandhiṣu ||8||

9samantato dvijaśreṣṭhāstatrākūjanta vai tadā
|
manuṣyavadanāścānye bhāruṇḍā iti viśrutāḥ ||9||

10bhūliṅgaśakunāścānye sāmudrāḥ parvatodbhavāḥ
|
sa tānyatimanojñāni vihagānāṃ rutāni vai ||10||

11śṛṇvan suramaṇīyāni vipro'gacchata gautamaḥ
|
tato'paśyatsuramyeṣu suvarṇasikatācite ||11||

12deśe same sukhe citre svarṇoddeśasame nṛpa
|
śriyā juṣṭaṃ mahāvṛkṣaṃ nyagrodhaṃ ca sumaṇḍalam ||12||

13śākhābhiranurūpābhirbhūyiṣṭhaṃ kṣatrasannibham
|
tasya mūlaṃ ca saṃsiktaṃ varacandanavāriṇā ||13||

14divyapuṣpānvitaṃ śrīmatpitāmahasabhopamam
|
taṃ dṛṣṭvā gautamaḥ prīto manaḥkāntamanuttamam ||14||

15medhyaṃ suragṛhaprakhyaṃ puṣpitaiḥ pādapairvṛtam
|
tamāsādya mudā yuktastasyādhastādupāviśat ||15||

16tatrāsīnasya kaunteya gautamasya sukhaḥ śivaḥ
|
puṣpāṇi samupaspṛśya pravavāvanilaḥ śubhaḥ
|
hlādayan sarvagātrāṇi gautamasya tadā nṛpa ||16||

17sa tu vipraḥ praśāntaśca spṛṣṭaḥ puṇyena vāyunā
|
sukhamāsādya suṣvāpa bhāskaraścāstamabhyayāt ||17||

18tato'staṃ bhāskare yāte sandhyākāla upasthite
|
ājagāma svabhavanaṃ brahmalokātkhagottamaḥ ||18||

19nāḍījaṅgha iti khyāto dayito brahmaṇaḥ sakhā
|
bakarājo mahāprājñaḥ kaśyapasyātmasambhavaḥ ||19||

20rājadharme'tivikhyāto babhūvāpratimo bhuvi
|
devakanyāsutaḥ śrīmān vidvāndevasamaprabhaḥ ||20||

21mṛṣṭābharaṇasampanno bhūṣaṇairarkasannibhaiḥ
|
ṣitaḥ sarvagātreṣu devagarbhaḥ śriyā jvalan ||21||

22tamāgataṃ khagaṃ dṛṣṭvā gautamo vismito'bhavat
|
kṣutpipāsāpariśrānto hiṃsārthī cābhyavekṣata ||22||

23rājadharmovāca |
svāgataṃ bhavato vipra diṣṭyā prāpto'si me gṛham
|
astaṃ ca savitā yātaḥ sandhyeyaṃ samupasthitā ||23||

24mama tvaṃ nilaya prāptaḥ priyātithiraninditaḥ
|
pūjito yāsyasi prātarvidhidṛṣṭena karmaṇā ||24||
