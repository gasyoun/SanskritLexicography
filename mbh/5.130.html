Mahābhārata 5.130 (Udyogaparvan)

1vaiśampāyana uvāca|
tattu vākyamanādṛtya so'rthavanmātṛbhāṣitam
|
punaḥ pratasthe saṃrambhāt sakāśamakṛtātmanām ||1||

2tataḥ sabhāyā nirgamya mantrayāmāsa kauravaḥ
|
saubalena matākṣeṇa rājñā śakuninā saha ||2||

3duryodhanasya karṇasya śakuneḥ saubalasya ca
|
duḥśāsanacaturthānāmidamāsīdviceṣṭitam ||3||

4purā'yamasmān gṛhṇāti kṣiprakārī janārdanaḥ
|
sahito dhṛtarāṣṭreṇa rājñā śāntanavena ca ||4||

5vayameva hṛṣīkeśaṃ nigṛhṇīma balādiva
|
prasahya puruṣavyāghramindro vairocaniṃ yathā ||5||

6śrutvā gṛhītaṃ vārṣṇeyaṃ pāṇḍavā hatacetasaḥ
|
nirutsāhā bhaviṣyanti bhagnadaṃṣṭrā ivoragāḥ ||6||

7ayaṃ hyeṣāṃ mahābāhuḥ sarveṣāṃ śarma varma ca
|
asmin gṛhīte varade ṛṣabhe sarvasātvatām ||7||

8nirudyamā bhaviṣyanti pāṇḍavāḥ somakaiḥ saha
|
tasmādvayamihaivainaṃ keśavaṃ kṣiprakāriṇam ||8||

9krośato dhṛtarāṣṭrasya baddhvā yotsyāmahe ripūn
|
teṣāṃ pāpamabhiprāyaṃ pāpānāṃ duṣṭacetasām ||9||

10iṅgitajñaḥ kaviḥ kṣipramanvabuddhyata sātyakiḥ
|
tadarthamabhiniṣkramya hārdikyena sahāsthitaḥ ||10||

11abravīt kṛtavarmāṇaṃ kṣipraṃ yojaya vāhinīm
|
vyūḍhānīkaḥ sabhādvāramupatiṣṭhasva daṃśitaḥ ||11||

12yāvadākhyāmyahaṃ caitat kṛṣṇāyākliṣṭakāriṇe
|
sa praviśya sabhāṃ vīraḥ siṃho giriguhāmiva ||12||

13ācaṣṭa tamabhiprāyaṃ keśavāya mahātmane
|
dhṛtarāṣṭraṃ tataścaiva viduraṃ cānvabhāṣata ||13||

14teṣāmetamabhiprāyamācacakṣe smayanniva
|
dharmādārthācca kāmācca karma sādhuvigarhitam ||14||

15mandāḥ kartumihecchanti na cāvāpyaṃ kathañcana
|
purā vikurvate mūḍhāḥ pāpātmānaḥ samāgatāḥ ||15||

16dharṣitāḥ kāmamanyubhyāṃ krodhalobhavaśānugāḥ
|
imaṃ hi puṇḍarīkākṣaṃ jighṛkṣantyalpacetasaḥ ||16||

17paṭenāgniṃ prajvalitaṃ yathā bālā yathā jaḍāḥ
|
sātyakestadvacaḥ śrutvā viduro dīrghadarśivān ||17||

18dhṛtarāṣṭraṃ mahābāhumabravīt kurusaṃsadi
|
rājan parītakālāste putrāḥ sarve paraṃtapa ||18||

19aśakyamayaśasyaṃ ca kartuṃ karma samudyatāḥ
|
imaṃ hi puṇḍarīkākṣamabhibhūya prasahya ca ||19||

20nigrahītuṃ kilecchanti sahitā vāsavānujam
|
imaṃ puruṣaśārdūlamapradhṛṣyaṃ durāsadam ||20||

21āsādya na bhaviṣyanti pataṅgā iva pāvakam
|
ayamicchan hi tān sarvān yudhyamānān janārdanaḥ ||21||

22siṃho nāgāniva kruddho gamayedyamasādanam
|
na tvayaṃ ninditaṃ karma kuryāt pāpaṃ kathañcana ||22||

23na ca dharmādapakrāmedacyutaḥ puruṣottamaḥ
|
vidureṇaivamukte tu keśavo vākyamabravīt ||23||

24dhṛtarāṣṭramabhiprekṣya suhṛdāṃ śṛṇvatāṃ mithaḥ
|
rājannete yadi kruddhā māṃ nigṛhṇīyurojasā ||24||

25ete vā māmahaṃ vainānanujānīhi pārthiva
|
etān hi sarvān saṃrabdhānniyantumahamutsahe ||25||

26na tvahaṃ ninditaṃ karma kuryāṃ pāpaṃ kathañcana
|
pāṇḍavārthe hi lubhyantaḥ svārthān hāsyanti te sutāḥ ||26||

27ete cedevamicchanti kṛtakāryo yudhiṣṭhiraḥ
|
adyaiva hyahamenāṃśca ye cainānanu bhārata ||27||

28nigṛhya rājan pārthebhyo dadyāṃ kiṃ duṣkṛtaṃ bhavet
|
idaṃ tu na pravarteyaṃ ninditaṃ karma bhārata ||28||

29sannidhau te mahārāja krodhajaṃ pāpabuddhijam
|
eṣa duryodhano rājan yathecchati tathā'stu tat ||29||

30ahaṃ tu sarvāstanayānanujānāmi te nṛpa
|
etacchrutvā tu viduraṃ dhṛtarāṣṭro'bhyabhāṣata
|
kṣipramānaya taṃ pāpaṃ rājyalubdhaṃ suyodhanam ||30||

31sahamitraṃ sahāmātyaṃ sasodaryaṃ sahānugam
|
śaknuyāṃ yadi panthānamavatārayituṃ punaḥ ||31||

32tato duryodhanaṃ kṣattā punaḥ prāveśayat sabhām
|
akāmaṃ bhrātṛbhiḥ sārdhaṃ rājabhiḥ parivāritam ||32||

33atha duryodhanaṃ rājā dhṛtarāṣṭro'bhyabhāṣata
|
karṇaduḥśāsanābhyāṃ ca rājabhiścāpi saṃvṛtam ||33||

34nṛśaṃsa pāpabhūṣiṣṭha kṣudrakarmasahāyavān
|
pāpaiḥ sahāyaiḥ saṃhatya pāpaṃ karma cikīrṣasi ||34||

35aśakyamayaśasyaṃ ca sadbhiścāpi vigarhitam
|
yathā tvādṛśako mūḍho vyavasyet kulapāṃsanaḥ ||35||

36tvamimaṃ puṇḍarīkākṣamapradhṛṣyaṃ durāsadam
|
pāpaiḥ sahāyaiḥ saṃhatya nigrahītuṃ kilecchasi ||36||

37yo na śakyo balātkartuṃ devairapi savāsavaiḥ
|
taṃ tvaṃ prārthayase manda bālaścandramasaṃ yathā ||37||

38devairmanuṣyairgandharvairasurairuragaiśca yaḥ
|
na soḍhuṃ samare śakyastaṃ na buddhyasi keśavam ||38||

39durgrāhyaḥ pāṇinā vāyurdusparśaḥ pāṇinā śaśī
|
durdharā pṛthivī mūrdhnā durgrāhyaḥ keśavo balāt||39||

40ityukte dhṛtarāṣṭreṇa kṣattā'pi viduro'bravīt
|
duryodhanamabhipretya dhārtarāṣṭramamarṣaṇam ||40||

41vidura uvāca|
duryodhana nibodhedaṃ vacanaṃ mama sāmpratam
|
saubhadvāre vānarendro dvivido nāma nāmataḥ
|
śilāvarṣeṇa mahatā chādayāmāsa keśavam ||41||

42grahītukāmo vikramya sarvayatnena mādhavam
|
gṛhītuṃ nāśakaccainaṃ taṃ tvaṃ prārthayase balāt ||42||

43prāgjyotiṣagataṃ śauriṃ narakaḥ saha dānavaiḥ
|
grahītuṃ nāśakattatra taṃ tvaṃ prārthayase balāt ||43||

44anekayugavarṣāyurnihatya narakaṃ mṛdhe
|
nītvā kanyāsahasrāṇi upayeme yathāvidhi ||44||

45nirmocane ṣaṭsahasrāḥ pāśairbaddhā mahāsurāḥ
|
gṛhītuṃ nāśakaṃścainaṃ taṃ tvaṃ prārthayase balāt ||45||

46anena hi hatā bālye pūtanā śakunī tathā
|
govardhano dhāritaśca gavārthe bharatarṣabha ||46||

47ariṣṭo dhenukaścaiva cāṇūraśca mahābalaḥ
|
aśvarājaśca nihataḥ kaṃsaścāriṣṭamācaran ||47||

48jarāsandhaśca vaktraśca śiśupālaśca vīryavān
|
bāṇaśca nihataḥ saṅkhye rājānaśca niṣūditāḥ ||48||

49varuṇo nirjito rājā pāvakaścāmitaujasā
|
pārijātaṃ ca haratā jitaḥ sākṣācchacīpatiḥ||49||

50ekārṇave ca svapatā nihatau madhukaiṭabhau
|
janmāntaramupāgamya hayagrīvastathā hataḥ ||50||

51ayaṃ kartā na kriyate kāraṇaṃ cāpi pauruṣe
|
yadyadicchedayaṃ śauristattat kuryādayatnataḥ ||51||

52taṃ na buddhyasi govindaṃ ghoravikramamacyutam
|
āśīviṣamiva kruddhaṃ tejorāśimaninditam ||52||

53pradharṣayanmahābāhuṃ kṛṣṇamakliṣṭakāriṇam
|
pataṅgo'gnimivāsādya sāmātyo na bhaviṣyasi ||53||
