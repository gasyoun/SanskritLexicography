Mahābhārata 2.27 (Sabhāparvan)

1vaiśampāyana uvāca|
evamuktaḥ pratyuvāca bhagadattaṃ dhanañjayaḥ
|
anenaiva kṛtaṃ sarvaṃ bhaviṣyatyanujānatā||1||

2taṃ vijitya mahābāhuḥ kuntīputro dhanañjayaḥ
|
prayayāvuttarāṃ tasmāddiśaṃ dhanadapālitām||2||

3antargiriṃ ca kaunteyastathaiva ca bahirgirim
|
tathaivopagiraṃ caiva vijigye puruṣarṣabhaḥ||3||

4vijitya parvatānsarvānye ca tatra narādhipāḥ
|
tānvaśe sthāpayitvā sa dhanānyādāya sarvaśaḥ||4||

5taireva sahitaḥ sarvairanurajya ca tānnṛpān
|
ulūkavāsinaṃ rājanbṛhantamupajagmivān||5||

6mṛdaṅgavaranādena rathanemisvanena ca
|
hastināṃ ca ninādena kampayanvasudhāmimām||6||

7tato bṛhantastvarito balena caturaṅgiṇā
|
niṣkramya nagarāttasmādyodhayāmāsa phālgunam||7||

8sumahānsannipāto'bhūddhanañjayabṛhantayoḥ
|
na śaśāka bṛhantastu soḍhuṃ pāṇḍavavikramam||8||

9so'viṣahyatamaṃ matvā kaunteyaṃ parvateśvaraḥ
|
upāvartata durdharṣo ratnānyādāya sarvaśaḥ||9||

10sa tadrājyamavasthāpya ulūkasahito yayau
|
senābindumatho rājanrājyādāśu samākṣipat||10||

11modāpuraṃ vāmavedaṃ sudāmānaṃ susaṅkulam
|
ulūkānuttarāṃścaiva tāṃśca rājñaḥ samānayat||11||

12tatrasthaḥ puruṣaireva dharmarājasya śāsanāt
|
kirīṭī jitavānrājandeśānpañcagaṇāṃstataḥ||12||

13sa devaprasthamāsādya senābindoḥ puraṃ prati
|
balena caturaṅgeṇa niveśamakarotprabhuḥ||13||

14sa taiḥ parivṛtaḥ sarvairviṣvagaśvaṃ narādhipam
|
abhyagacchanmahātejāḥ pauravaṃ puruṣarṣabha||14||

15vijitya cāhave śūrānpārvatīyānmahārathān
|
jigāya senayā rājanpuraṃ pauravarakṣitam||15||

16pauravaṃ yudhi nirjitya dasyūnparvatavāsinaḥ
|
gaṇānutsavasaṅketānajayatsapta pāṇḍavaḥ||16||

17tataḥ kāśmīrakānvīrānkṣatriyānkṣatriyarṣabhaḥ
|
vyajayallohitaṃ caiva maṇḍalairdaśabhiḥ saha||17||

18tatastrigartāḥ kaunteyaṃ dārvāḥ kokanadāstathā
|
kṣatriyā bahavo rājannupāvartanta sarvaśaḥ||18||

19abhisārīṃ tato ramyāṃ vijigye kurunandanaḥ
|
uragāvāsinaṃ caiva rocamānaṃ raṇe'jayat||19||

20tataḥ siṃhapuraṃ ramyaṃ citrāyudhasurakṣitam
|
prādhamadbalamāsthāya pākaśāsannirāhave||20||

21tataḥ suhyāṃśca colāṃśca kirīṭī pāṇḍavarṣabhaḥ
|
sahitaḥ sarvasainyena prāmathatkurunandanaḥ||21||

22tataḥ paramavikrānto bāhlīkānpākaśāsaniḥ
|
mahatā parimardena vaśe cakre durāsadān||22||

23gṛhītvā tu balaṃ sāraṃ phalgunaḥ pāṇḍunandanaḥ
|
daradānsaha kāmbhojairajayatpākaśāsaniḥ||23||

24prāguttaraṃ diśaṃ ye ca vasantyāśritya dasyavaḥ
|
nivasanti vane ye ca tānsarvānajayatprabhuḥ||24||

25lohānparamakāmbhojānṛṣikānuttarānapi
|
sahitāṃstānmahārāja vyajayatpākaśāsaniḥ||25||

26ṛṣikeṣvapi saṅgrāme babhūvātibhayaṅkaraḥ
|
tārakāmayasaṅkāśaḥ parastvṛṣikapārthayoḥ||26||

27sa vijitya tato rājannṛṣikānraṇamūrdhani
|
śukodarasamāṃstatra hayānaṣṭau samānayat||27||

28mayūrasadṛśānanyānuttarānaparānapi
|
javanānāśugāṃścaiva karārthaṃ samupānayat||28||

29sa vinirjitya saṅgrāme himavantaṃ saniṣkṛṭam
|
śvetaparvatamāsādya nyaviśatpuruṣarṣabhaḥ||29||
