Mahābhārata 1.29 (Ādiparvan)

1sautiruvāca|
tasya kaṇṭhamanuprāpto brāhmaṇaḥ saha bhāryayā
|
dahandīpta ivāṅgārastamuvācāntarikṣagaḥ||1||

2dvijottama vinirgaccha tūrṇamāsyādapāvṛtāt
|
na hi me brāhmaṇo vadhyaḥ pāpeṣvapi rataḥ sadā||2||

3bruvāṇamevaṃ garuḍaṃ brāhmaṇaḥ pratyabhāṣata
|
niṣādī mama bhāryeyaṃ nirgacchatu mayā saha||3||

4garuḍa uvāca|
etāmapi niṣādīṃ tvaṃ parigṛhyāśu niṣpata
|
tūrṇaṃ saṃbhāvayātmānamajīrṇaṃ mama tejasā||4||

5sautiruvāca|
tataḥ sa vipro niṣkrānto niṣādīsahitastadā
|
vardhayitvā ca garuḍamiṣṭaṃ deśaṃ jagāma ha||5||

6sahabhārye viniṣkrānte tasminvipre sa pakṣirāṭ
|
vitatya pakṣāvākāśamutpapāta manojavaḥ||6||

7tato'paśyat sa pitaraṃ pṛṣṭaścākhyātavānpituḥ
|
yathānyāyamameyātmā taṃ covāca mahānṛṣiḥ||7||

8kaśyapa uvāca|
kaccidvaḥ kuśalaṃ nityaṃ bhojane bahulaṃ suta
|
kaccicca mānuṣe loke tavānnaṃ vidyate bahu||8||

9garuḍa uvāca|
mātā me kuśalā śaśvattathā bhrātā tathā hyaham
|
na hi me kuśalaṃ tāta bhojane bahule sadā||9||

10ahaṃ hi sarpaiḥ prahitaḥ somamāhartumuttamam
|
mahāturdāsyavimokṣārthamāhariṣye tamadya vai||10||

11mātrā cātra samādiṣṭo niṣādānbhakṣayeti ha
|
na ca me tṛptirabhavadbhakṣayitvā sahasraśaḥ||11||

12tasmādbhakṣyaṃ tvamaparaṃ bhagavanpradiśasva me
|
yadbhuktvā'mṛtamāhartuṃ samarthaḥ syāmahaṃ prabho||12||

13kṣutpipāsāvighātārthaṃ bhakṣyamākhyātu me bhavān
|
kaśyapa uvāca|
idaṃ saro mahāpuṇyaṃ devaloke'pi viśrutam||13||

14yatra kūrmāgrajaṃ hastī sadā karṣatyavāṅmukhaḥ
|
tayorjanmāntare vairaṃ saṃpravakṣyāmyasoṣataḥ||14||

15tanme tattvaṃ nibodhasya yatpramāṇau ca tāvubhau
|
āsīdvibhāvasurnāma maharṣiḥ kopano bhṛśam||15||

16bhrātā tasyānujaścāsītsupratīko mahātapāḥ
|
sa necchati dhanaṃ bhrātā sahaikasthaṃ mahāmuniḥ||16||

17vibhāgaṃ kīrtayatyeva supratīko hi nityaśaḥ
|
athābravīcca taṃ bhrātā supratīkaṃ vibhāvasuḥ||17||

18vibhāgaṃ bahavo mohātkartumicchanti nityaśaḥ
|
tato vibhaktāstvanyo'nyaṃ vikrudhyante'rthamohitāḥ||18||

19tataḥ svārthaparānmūḍhānpṛthagbhūtānsvakairdhanaiḥ
|
viditvā bhedayantyetānamitrāmitrarūpiṇaḥ||19||

20viditvā cāpare bhinnānantareṣu patantyatha
|
bhinnānāmatulo nāśaḥ kṣiprameva pravartate||20||

21tasmādvibhāgaṃ bhrātṝṇāṃ na praśaṃsanti sādhavaḥ
|
guruśāstre nibaddhānāmanyo'nyenābhiśaṅkinām||21||

22niyantuṃ na hi śakyastvaṃ bhedato dhanamicchasi
|
yasmāttasmātsupratīka hastitvaṃ samavāpsyasi||22||

23kaśyapa uvāca|
śaptastvevaṃ supratīko vibhāvasumathābravīt
|
tvamapyantarjalacaraḥ kacchapaḥ saṃbhaviṣyasi||23||

24evamanyonyaśāpāttau supratīkavibhāvasū
|
gajakacchapatāṃ prāptāvarthārthaṃ mūḍhacetasau||24||

25roṣadoṣānuṣaṅgeṇa tiryagyonigatāvubhau
|
parasparadveṣaratau pramāṇabaladarpitau||25||

26sarasyasminmahākāyau pūrvavairānusāriṇau
|
tayoranyataraḥ śrīmānsamupaiti mahāgajaḥ||26||

27yasya bṛṃhitaśabdena kūrmo'pyantarjaleśayaḥ
|
utthito'sau mahākāyaḥ kṛtsnaṃ vikṣobhayansaraḥ||27||

28taṃ dṛṣṭvā veṣṭitakaraḥ patatyeṣa gajo jalam
|
dantahastāgralāṅgūlapādavegena vīryavān||28||

29vikṣobhayaṃstato nāgaḥ saro bahuruṣākulam
|
kūrmo'pyabhyudyataśirā yuddhāyābhyeti vīryavān||29||

30ṣaḍucchrito yojanāni gajastaddviguṇāyataḥ
|
kūrmastriyojanotsedho daśayojanamaṇḍalaḥ||30||

31tāvubhau yuddhasaṃmattau parasparavadhaiṣiṇau
|
upayujyāśu karmedaṃ sādhaye hitamātmanaḥ||31||

32mahābhraghanasaṃkāśaṃ taṃ bhuktvāmṛtamānaya
|
mahāgirisamaprakhyaṃ ghorarūpaṃ ca hastinam||32||

33sautiruvāca|
ityuktvā garuḍaṃ so'tha māṅgalyamakarottadā
|
yudhyataḥ saha devaiste yuddhe bhavatu maṅgalam||33||

34pūrṇakumbho dvijā gāvo yaccānyatkiṃciduttamam
|
śubhaṃ svastyayanaṃ cāpi bhaviṣyati tavāṇḍajā||34||

35yudhyamānasya saṅgrāme devaiḥ sārdhaṃ mahābala
|
ṛco yajūṃṣi sāmāni pavitrāṇi havīṃṣi ca||35||

36rahasyāni ca sarvāṇi sarve vedāśca te balam
|
ityukto garuḍaḥ pitrā gatastaṃ hradamantikāt||36||

37apaśyannirmalajalaṃ nānāpakṣisamākulam
|
sa tatsmṛtvā piturvākyaṃ bhīmavego'ntarikṣagaḥ||37||

38nakhena gajamekena kūrmamekena cākṣipat
|
sadhutpapāta cākāśaṃ tata uccairvihaṅgamaḥ||38||

39so'lambaṃ tīrthamāsādya devavṛkṣānupāgamat
|
te bhītāḥ samakampanta tasya pakṣānilāhatāḥ||39||

40na no bhañjyāditi tadā divyāḥ kanakaśākhinaḥ
|
pracalāṅgānsa tāndṛṣṭvā manorathaphaladrumān||40||

41anyānatularūpāṅgānupacakrāma khecaraḥ
|
kāñcanai rājataiścaiva phalairvaidūryaśākhinaḥ
|
sāgarāmbuparikṣiptānbhrājamānānmahādrumān||41||

42tamuvāca khagaśreṣṭhaṃ tatra rauhiṇapādapaḥ
|
atipravṛddhaḥ sumahānāpatantaṃ manojavam||42||

43rauhiṇa uvāca|
yaiṣā mama mahāśākhā śatayojanamāyatā
|
etāmāsthāya śākhāṃ tvaṃ khādemau gajakacchapau||43||

44tato drumaṃ patagasahasrasevitaṃ mahīdharapratimavapuḥ prakampayan
|
khagottamo drutamabhipatya vegavān bhañjatāmaviralapatrasaṃcayām||44||
