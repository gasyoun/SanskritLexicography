Mahābhārata 3.231 (Āraṇyakaparvan)

1mārkaṇḍeya uvāca|
yadā skandena mātṝṇāmevametat priyaṃ kṛtam
|
athainamabravīt svāhā mama putrastvamaurasaḥ||1||

2icchāmyahaṃ tvayā dattāṃ prītiṃ paramadurlabhām
|
tāmabravīt tataḥ skandaḥ prītimicchasi kīdṛśīm ||2||

3svāhovāca|
dakṣasyāhaṃ priyā kanyā svāhā nāma mahābhuja
|
bālyātprabhṛti nityaṃ ca jātakāmā hutāśane ||3||

4na sa māṃ kāminīṃ putra samyak jānāti pāvakaḥ
|
icchāmi śāśvataṃ vāsaṃ vastuṃ putra sahāgninā||4||

5skanda uvāca|
havyaṃ kavyaṃ ca yatkiñciddvijānāṃ mantrasaṃstutam
|
hoṣyantyagnau sadā devi svāhetyuktvā samuddhṛtam||5||

6adyaprabhṛti dāsyanti suvṛttāḥ satpathe sthitāḥ
|
evamagnistvayā sārdhaṃ sadā vatsyati śobhane||6||

7mārkaṇḍeya uvāca|
evamuktā tataḥ svāhā tuṣṭā skandena pūjitā
|
pāvakena samāyuktā bhartrā skandamapūjayat||7||

8tato brahmā mahāsenaṃ prajāpatirathābravīt
|
abhigaccha mahādevaṃ pitaraṃ tripurārdanam||8||

9rudreṇāgnniṃ samāviśya svāhāmāviśya cāmayā
|
hitārthaṃ sarvalokānāṃ jātastvamaparājitaḥ||9||

10umāyonyāṃ ca rudreṇa śukraṃ siktaṃ mahātmanā
|
asmin girau nipatitaṃ miñjikāmiñjikaṃ yataḥ||10||

11sambhūtaṃ lohitode tu śukraśeṣamavāpatat
|
sūryaraśmiṣu cāpyanyadanyaccaivāpatadbhuvi ||11||

12āsaktamanyadvṛkṣeṣu tadevaṃ pañcadhā'patat
|
tatra te vividhākārā gaṇā jñeyā manīṣibhiḥ
|
tava pāriṣadā ghorā ya ete piśitāśinaḥ||12||

13evamastviti cāpyuktvā mahāseno maheśvaram
|
apūjayadameyātmā pitaraṃ pitṛvatsalaḥ||13||

14mārkaṇḍeya uvāca|
arkapuṣpaistu te pañca gaṇāḥ pūjyā dhanārthibhiḥ
|
vyādhipraśamanārthaṃ ca teṣāṃ pūjāṃ samācaret||14||

15miñjikāmiñjikaṃ caiva mithunaṃ rudrasambhavam
|
namaskāryaṃ sadaiveha bālānāṃ hitamicchatā||15||

16striyo mānuṣamāṃsādā vṛddhikā nāma nāmataḥ
|
vṛkṣeṣu jātāstā devyo namaskāryāḥ prajārthibhiḥ||16||

17evamete piśācānāmasaṅkhyeyā gaṇāḥ smṛtāḥ
|
ghaṇṭāyāḥ sapatākāyāḥ śṛṇu me sambhavaṃ nṛpa||17||

18airāvatasya ghaṇṭe dve vaijayantyāviti śrute
|
guhasya te svayaṃ datte krameṇānāyya dhīmatā||18||

19ekā tatra viśākhasya ghaṇṭā skandasya cāparā
|
patākā kārtikeyasya viśākhasya ca lohitā||19||

20yāni krīḍanakānyasya devairdattāni vai tadā
|
taireva ramate devo mahāseno mahābalaḥ||20||

21sa saṃvṛtaḥ piśācānāṃ gaṇairdevagaṇaistathā
|
śuśubhe kāñcane śaile dīpyamānaḥ śriyā vṛtaḥ||21||

22tena vīreṇa śuśubhe sa śailaḥ śubhakānanaḥ
|
ādityenevāṃśumatā mandaraścārukandaraḥ||22||

23santānakavanaiḥ phullaiḥ karavīravanairapi
|
pārijātavanaiścaiva japāśokavanaistathā||23||

24kadambataruṣaṇḍaiśca divyairmṛgagaṇairapi
|
divyaiḥ pakṣigaṇaiścaiva śuśubhe śvetaparvataḥ||24||

25tatra devagaṇāḥ sarve sarve devarṣayastathā
|
meghatūryaravāścaiva kṣubdhodadhisamasvanāḥ||25||

26tatra divyāśca gandharvā nṛtyante'psarasastathā
|
hṛṣṭānāṃ tatra bhūtānāṃ śrūyate ninado mahān||26||

27evaṃ sendraṃ jagat sarvaṃ śvetaparvatasaṃsthitam
|
prahṛṣṭaṃ prekṣate skandaṃ na ca glāyati darśanāt||27||

28mārkaṇḍeya uvāca|
yadā'bhiṣikto bhagavān saināpatyena pāvakiḥ
|
tadā samprasthitaḥ śrīmān hṛṣṭo bhadravaṭaṃ haraḥ||28||

29rathenādityavarṇena pārvatyā sahitaḥ prabhuḥ
|
sahasraṃ tasya siṃhānāṃ tasmin yuktaṃ rathottame||29||

30utpapāta divaṃ śubhraṃ kālenābhipracoditam
|
te pibanta ivākāśaṃ trāsayantaścarācarān||30||

31siṃhā nabhasyagacchanta nadantaścārukesarāḥ
|
tasmin rathe paśupatiḥ sthito bhātyumayā saha||31||

32vidyutā sahitaḥ sūryaḥ sendracāpe ghane yathā
|
agratastasya bhagavān dhaneśo guhyakaiḥ saha||32||

33āsthāya tu ciraṃ yāti puṣpakaṃ naravāhanaḥ
|
airāvataṃ samāsthāya śakraścāpi suraiḥ saha||33||

34pṛṣṭhato'nuyayau yāntaṃ varadaṃ vṛṣabhadhvajam
|
jṛmbhakairyakṣarakṣobhiḥ sragvibhiḥ samalaṅkṛtaḥ||34||

35yātyamogho mahāyakṣo dakṣiṇaṃ pakṣamāsthitaḥ
|
tasya dakṣiṇato devā bahavaścitrayodhinaḥ||35||

36gacchanti vasubhiḥ sārdhaṃ rudraiśca saha saṅgatāḥ
|
yamaśca mṛtyunā sārdhaṃ sarvataḥ parivāritaḥ||36||

37ghorairvyādhiśatairyāti ghorarūpavapustathā
|
yamasya pṛṣṭhataścaiva ghorastriśikharaḥ śitaḥ||37||

38vijayo nāma rudrasya yāti śūlaḥ svalaṅkṛtaḥ
|
tamugrapāśo varuṇo bhagavān salileśvaraḥ||38||

39parivārya śanairyāti yādobhirvividhairvṛtaḥ
|
pṛṣṭhato vijayasyāpi yāti rudrasya paṭṭiśaḥ||39||

40gadāmusalaśaktyādyairvṛtaḥ praharaṇottamaiḥ
|
paṭṭiśaṃ tvanvagādrājaṃcchatraṃ raudraṃ mahāprabham||40||

41kamaṇḍaluścāpyanu taṃ maharṣigaṇasevitaḥ
|
tasya dakṣiṇato bhāti daṇḍo gacchan śriyā vṛtaḥ||41||

42bhṛgvaṅgirobhiḥ sahito daivataiścānupūjitaḥ
|
eṣāṃ tu pṛṣṭhato rudro vimale syandane sthitaḥ||42||

43yāti saṃharṣayan sarvāṃstejasā tridivaukasaḥ
|
ṛṣayaścāpi devāśca gandharvā bhujagāstathā||43||

44nadyo hṛdāḥ samudrāśca tathaivāpsarasāṃ gaṇāḥ
|
nakṣatrāṇi grahāścaiva devānāṃ śiśavaśca ye||44||

45striyaśca vividhākārā yānti rudrasya pṛṣṭhataḥ
|
sṛjantyaḥ puṣpavarṣāṇi cārurūpā varāṅganāḥ||45||

46parjanyaścāpyanuyayau namaskṛtya pinākinam
|
chatraṃ ca pāṇḍuraṃ somastasya mūrdhanyadhārayat||46||

47cāmare cāpi vāyuśca gṛhītvā'gniśca dhiṣṭhitau
|
śakraśca pṛṣṭhatastasya yāti rājañchriyā vṛtaḥ||47||

48saha rājarṣibhiḥ sarvaiḥ stuvāno vṛṣaketanam
|
gaurī vidyā'tha gāndhārī keśinī mitrasāhvayā||48||

49sāvitryā saha sarvāstāḥ pārvatyā yānti pṛṣṭhataḥ
|
tatra vidyāgaṇāḥ sarve ye kecit kavibhiḥ kṛtāḥ||49||

50tasya kurvanti vacanaṃ sendrā devāścamūmukhe
|
gṛhītvā tu patākāṃ vai yātyagre rākṣaso grahaḥ||50||

51vyāpṛtastu śmaśāne yo nityaṃ rudrasya vai sakhā
|
piṅgalo nāma yakṣendro lokasyānandadāyakaḥ||51||

52ebhiśca sahito devastatra yāti yathāsukham
|
agrataḥ pṛṣṭhataścaiva na hi tasya gatirdhruvā||52||

53rudraṃ satkarmabhirmartyāḥ pūjayantīha daivatam
|
śivamityeva yaṃ prāhurīśaṃ rudraṃ pitāmaham||53||

54bhāvaistu vividhākāraiḥ pūjayanti maheśvaram
|
devasenāpatistvevaṃ devasenābhirāvṛtaḥ
|
anugacchati deveśaṃ brahmaṇyaḥ kṛttikāsutaḥ||54||

55athābravīnmahāsenaṃ mahādevo bṛhadvacaḥ
|
saptamaṃ mārutaskandhaṃ rakṣa nityamatandritaḥ||55||

56skanda uvāca|
saptamaṃ mārutaskandhaṃ pālayiṣyāmyahaṃ prabho
|
yadanyadapi me kāryaṃ deva tadvada māciram||56||

57rudra uvāca|
kāryeṣvahaṃ tvayā putra sandraṣṭavyaḥ sadaiva hi
|
darśanānmama bhaktyā ca śreyaḥ paramavāpsyasi||57||

58mārkaṇḍeya uvāca|
ityuktvā visasarjainaṃ pariṣvajya maheśvaraḥ
|
visarjite tataḥ skande babhūvautpātikaṃ mahat||58||

59sahasaiva mahārāja devān sarvān pramohayat
|
jajvāla khaṃ sanakṣatraṃ pramūḍhaṃ bhuvanaṃ bhṛśam||59||

60cacāla vyanadaccorvī tamobhūtaṃ jagadbabhau
|
tatastaddāruṇaṃ dṛṣṭvā kṣubhitaḥ śaṅkarastadā||60||

61umā caiva mahābhāgā devāśca samaharṣayaḥ
|
tatasteṣu pramūḍheṣu parvatāmbudasannibham||61||

62nānāpraharaṇaṃ ghoramadṛśyata mahadbalam
|
tadvai ghoramasaṅkhyeyaṃ garjacca vividhā giraḥ||62||

63abhyadravaddraṇe devān bhagavantaṃ ca śaṅkaram
|
tairvisṛṣṭānyanīkeṣu bāṇajālānyanekaśaḥ||63||

64parvatāśca śataghnyaśca prāsāptiparighā gadāḥ
|
nipatadbhiśca tairghorairdevānīkaṃ mahāyudhaiḥ||64||

65kṣaṇena vyadravat sarvaṃ vimukhaṃ cāpyadṛśyata
|
nikṛttayodhanāgāśvaṃ kṛttāyudhamahāratham||65||

66dānavairarditaṃ sainyaṃ devānāṃ vimukhaṃ babhau
|
asurairvadhyamānaṃ tat pāvakairiva kānanam||66||

67apataddagdhabhūyiṣṭhaṃ mahādrumavanaṃ yathā
|
te vibhinnaśirodehāḥ prādravanto divaukasaḥ||67||

68na nāthamadhigacchanti vadhyamānā mahāraṇe
|
atha tadvidrutaṃ sainyaṃ dṛṣṭvā devaḥ purandaraḥ||68||

69āśvāsayannuvācedaṃ balabhiddānavārditam
|
bhayaṃ tyajata bhadraṃ vaḥ śūrāḥ śastrāṇi gṛhṇata||69||

70kurudhvaṃ vikrame buddhiṃ mā vaḥ kācidvyathā bhavet
|
jayatainān sudurvṛttān dānavān ghoradarśanān ||70||

71abhidravata bhadraṃ vo mayā saha mahāsurān
|
śakrasya vacanaṃ śrutvā samāśvastā divaukasaḥ||71||

72dānavān pratyayudhyanta śakraṃ kṛtvā vyapāśrayam
|
tataste tridaśāḥ sarve marutaśca mahābalāḥ||72||

73pratyudyayurmahābhāgāḥ sādhyāśca vasubhiḥ saha
|
tairvisṛṣṭānyanīkeṣu kruddhaiḥ śastrāṇi saṃyuge||73||

74śarāśca daityakāyeṣu pibanti rudhiraṃ bahu
|
teṣāṃ dehān vinirbhidya śarāste niśitāstadā||74||

75nipatanto'bhyadṛśyanta nagebhya iva pannagāḥ
|
tāni daityaśarīrāṇi nirbhinnāni sma sāyakaiḥ ||75||

76apatan bhūtale rājaṃśchinnābhrāṇīva sarvaśaḥ
|
tatastaddānavaṃ sainyaṃ sarvairdevagaṇairyudhi||76||

77trāsitaṃ vividhairbāṇaiḥ kṛtaṃ caiva parāṅmukham
|
athotkruṣṭaṃ tadā hṛṣṭaiḥ sarvairdevairudāyudhaiḥ||77||

78saṃhatāni ca tūryāṇi prāvādyanta hyanekaśaḥ
|
evamanyonyasaṃyuktaṃ yuddhamāsīt sudāruṇam ||78||

79devānāṃ dānavānāṃ ca māṃsaśoṇitakardamam
|
anayo devalokasya sahasaivābhyadṛśyata||79||

80tathāhi dānavā ghorā vinighnanti divaukasaḥ
|
tatastūryapraṇādāśca bherīṇāṃ ca mahāsvanaḥ||80||

81babhūvurdānavendrāṇāṃ siṃhanādāśca dāruṇāḥ
|
atha daityabalāddhorānniṣpapāta mahābalaḥ||81||

82dānavo mahiṣo nāma pragṛhya vipulaṃ girim
|
te taṃ ghanairivādityaṃ dṛṣṭvā samparivāritam||82||

83tamudyatagiriṃ rājan vyadravanta divaukasaḥ
|
athābhidrutya mahiṣo devāṃścikṣepa taṃ girim||83||

84patatā tena giriṇā devasainyasya pārthiva
|
bhīmarūpeṇa nihatamayutaṃ prāpatadbhuvi||84||

85atha tairdānavaiḥ sārdhaṃ mahiṣastrāsayan surān
|
abhyadravadraṇe tūrṇaṃ siṃhaḥ kṣudramṛgāniva||85||

86tamāpatantaṃ mahiṣaṃ dṛṣṭvā sendrā divaukasaḥ
|
vyadravanta raṇe bhītā vikīrṇāyudhaketanāḥ||86||

87tataḥ sa mahiṣaḥ kruddhastūrṇaṃ rudrarathaṃ yayau
|
abhidrutya ca jagrāha rudrasya rathakūbaram||87||

88yadā rudrarathaṃ kruddho mahiṣaḥ sahasā gataḥ
|
resatū rodasī gāḍhaṃ mumuhuśca maharṣayaḥ||88||

89anadaṃśca mahākāyā detyā jaladharopamāḥ
|
āsīcca niścitaṃ teṣāṃ jitamasmābhirityuta||89||

90tathābhūte tu bhagavānahananmahiṣaṃ raṇe
|
sasmāra ca tadā skandaṃ mṛtyuṃ tasya durātmanaḥ||90||

91mahiṣo'pi rathaṃ dṛṣṭvā raudro rudrasya cānadat
|
devān santrāsayaṃścāpi daityāṃścāpi praharṣayan||91||

92tatastasmin bhaye ghore devānāṃ samupasthite
|
ājagāma mahāsenaḥ krodhāt sūrya iva jvalan||92||

93lohitāmbarasaṃvīto lohitastragbhūṣaṇaḥ
|
lohitāśvo mahābāhurhiraṇyakavacaḥ prabhuḥ||93||

94rathamādityasaṅkāśamāsthitaḥ kanakaprabham
|
taṃ dṛṣṭvā daityasenā sā vyadravat sahasā raṇe||94||

95sa cāpi tāṃ prajvalitāṃ mahiṣasya vidāriṇīm
|
mumoca śaktiṃ rājendra mahāseno mahābalaḥ||95||

96sā muktā'bhyaharat tasya mahiṣasya śiro mahat
|
papāta bhinne śirasi mahiṣastyaktajīvitaḥ||96||

97patatā śirasā tena dvāraṃ ṣoḍaśayojanam
|
parvatābhena pihitaṃ tadā'gamyaṃ tato'bhavat||97||

98uttarāḥ kuravastena gacchantyadya yathāsukham
|
kṣiptākṣiptā tu sā śaktirhatvā śatrūn sahastraśaḥ||98||

99skandahastamanuprāptā dṛśyate devadānavaiḥ
|
prāyaḥ śarairvinihatā mahāsenena dhīmatā||99||

100śeṣā daityagaṇā ghorā bhītāstrastā durāsadaiḥ
|
skandapāriṣadairhatvā bhakṣitāśca sahasraśaḥ||100||

101dānavān bhakṣayantaste prapibantaśca śoṇitam
|
kṣaṇānnirdānavaṃ sarvamakārṣurbhṛśaharṣitāḥ||101||

102tamāṃsīva yathā sūryo vṛkṣānagnirdhanān khagaḥ
|
tathā skando'jayacchatrūn svena vīryeṇa kīrtimān||102||

103sampūjyamānastridaśairabhivādya maheśvaram
|
śuśubhe kṛttikāputraḥ prakīrṇāṃśurivāṃśumān||103||

104naṣṭaśatruryadā skandaḥ prayātastu maheśvaram
|
tadā'bravīnmahāsenaṃ pariṣvajya purandaraḥ||104||

105brahmadattavaraḥ skanda tvayā'yaṃ mahiṣo hataḥ
|
devāstṛṇasamā yasya babhūvurjayatāṃ vara||105||

106so'yaṃ tvayā mahābāho śamito devakaṇṭakaḥ
|
śataṃ mahiṣatulyānāṃ dānavānāṃ tvayā raṇe||106||

107nihataṃ devaśatrūṇāṃ yairvayaṃ pūrvatāpitāḥ
|
tāvakairbhakṣitāścānye dānavāḥ śatasaṅghaśaḥ||107||

108ajeyastvaṃ raṇe'rīṇāmumāpatiriva prabhuḥ
|
etat te prathamaṃ deva khyātaṃ karma bhaviṣyati||108||

109triṣu lokeṣu kīrtiśca tavākṣayyā bhaviṣyati
|
vaśagāśca bhaviṣyanti surāstava mahābhuja||109||

110mahāsenamevamuktvā nivṛttaḥ saha daivataiḥ
|
anujñāto bhagavatā tryambakeṇa śacīpatiḥ||110||

111gato bhadravaṭaṃ rudro nivṛttāśca divaukasaḥ
|
uktāśca devā rudreṇa skandaṃ paśyata māmiva||111||

112sa hatvā dānavagaṇān pūjyamāno maharṣibhiḥ
|
ekāhnaivājayat sarvaṃ trailokyaṃ vahninandanaḥ||112||

113skandasya ya idaṃ vipraḥ paṭhejjanma samāhitaḥ
|
sa puṣṭimiha samprāpya skandasālokyamāpnuyāt||113||
