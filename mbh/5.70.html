Mahābhārata 5.70 (Udyogaparvan)

1dhṛtarāṣṭra uvāca|
bhūyo me puṇḍarīkākṣaṃ sañjayācakṣva pṛcchataḥ
|
nāmakarmārthavittāta prāpnuyāṃ puruṣottamam ||1||

2sañjaya uvāca|
śrutaṃ me vāsudevasya nāmanirvacanaṃ śubham
|
yāvattatrābhijāne'hamaprameyo hi keśavaḥ ||2||

3vasanāt sarvabhūtānāṃ vasutvāddevayonitaḥ
|
vāsudevastato vedyo bṛhattvādviṣṇurucyate ||3||

4maunāddhyānācca yogācca viddhi bhārata mādhavam
|
sarvatattvamayatvācca madhuhā madhusūdanaḥ ||4||

5kṛṣirbhūvācakaḥ śabdo ṇaśca nirvṛtivācakaḥ
|
viṣṇustadbhāvayogācca kṛṣṇo bhavati sātvataḥ ||5||

6puṇḍarīkaṃ paraṃ dhāma nityamakṣayamavyayam
|
tadbhāvāt puṇḍarīkākṣo dasyutrāsājjanārdanaḥ ||6||

7yataḥ sattvānna cyavate yacca sattvānna hīyate
|
satvataḥ sātvatastasmādārṣabhādvṛṣabhekṣaṇaḥ ||7||

8na jāyate janitrā'yamajastasmāda‌nīkajit
|
devānāṃ svaprakāśatvāddamāddāmodaro vibhuḥ ||8||

9harṣāt sukhāt sukhaiśvaryāddhṛṣīkeśatvamaśnute
|
bāhubhyāṃ rodasī vibhranmahābāhuriti smṛtaḥ ||9||

10adho na kṣīyate jātu yasmāttasmādadhokṣajaḥ
|
narāṇāmayanāccāpi tato nārāyaṇaḥ smṛtaḥ ||10||

11pūraṇāt sadanāccāpi tato'sau puruṣottamaḥ
|
asataśca sataścaiva sarvasya prabhavāpyayāt ||11||

12sarvasya ca sadā jñānāt sarvametaṃ pracakṣate
|
satye pratiṣṭhitaḥ kṛṣṇaḥ satyamatra pratiṣṭhitam ||12||

13satyātsatyaṃ tu govindastasmātsatyo'pi nāmataḥ
|
viṣṇurvikramaṇāddevo jayanājjiṣṇurucyate ||13||

14śāśvatatvādanantaśca govindo vedanādgavām
|
atattvaṃ kurute tattvaṃ tena mohayate prajāḥ ||14||

15evaṃvidho dharmanityo bhagavān madhusūdanaḥ
|
āgantā hi mahābāhurānṛśaṃsyārthamacyutaḥ ||15||
