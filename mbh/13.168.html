Mahābhārata 13.168 (Anuśāsanaparvan)

1vaiśampāyana uvāca|
evamuktvā kurūnsarvān bhīṣmaḥ śāntanavastadā
|
tūṣṇīṃ babhūva kauravyaḥ sa muhūrtamarindama ||1||

2dhārayāmāsa cātmānaṃ dhāraṇāsu yathākramam
|
tasyordhvamagamanprāṇāḥ sanniruddhā mahātmanaḥ ||2||

3ida‌māścaryamāsīcca madhye teṣāṃ mahātmanām
|
sahitairṛṣibhiḥ sarvaistadā vyāsādibhiḥ prabho ||3||

4yadyanmuñcati gātraṃ hi sa śāntanusutastadā
|
tattadviśalyaṃ bhavati yogayuktasya tasya vai ||4||

5kṣaṇena prekṣatāṃ teṣāṃ viśalyaḥ so'bhavattadā
|
taddṛṣṭvā vismitāḥ sarve vāsudevapurogamāḥ ||5||

6saha tairmunibhiḥ sarvaistadā vyāsādibhirnṛpa
|
sanniruddhastu tenātmā sarveṣvāyataneṣu ca ||6||

7jagāma bhittvā mūrdhānaṃ divamabhyutpapāta ha
|
devadundubhinādaśca puṣpavarṣaiḥ sahābhavat ||7||

8siddhā brahmarṣayaścaiva sādhu sādhviti harṣitāḥ
|
maholkeva ca bhīṣmasya mūrdhadeśājjanādhipa ||8||

9niḥsṛtyākāśamāviśya kṣaṇenāntaradhīyata
|
evaṃ sa rājaśārdūla nṛpaḥ śāntanavastadā ||9||

10samayujyata kālena bharatānāṃ kulodvahaḥ
|
tatastvādāya dārūṇi gandhāṃśca vividhānbahūn ||10||

11citāṃ cakrurmahātmānaḥ pāṇḍavā vidurastathā
|
yuyutsuścāpi kauravyaṃ prekṣakāstvitare'bhavan ||11||

12yudhiṣṭhiraśca gāṅgeyaṃ viduraśca mahāmatiḥ
|
chādayāmāsaturubhau kṣaumermālyaiśca kauravam ||12||

13dhārayāmāsa tasyātha yuyutsuśchatramuttamam
|
cāmaravyajane śubhre bhīmasenārjunāvubhau ||13||

14uṣṇīṣe parigṛhṇītāṃ mādrīputrāvubhau tathā
|
striyaḥ kauravanāthasya bhīṣmaṃ kurukulodvaham ||14||

15tālavṛntānyupādāya paryavījanta sarvaśaḥ
|
tato'sya vidhivaccakruḥ pitṛmedhaṃ mahātmanaḥ ||15||

16yajanaṃ bahuśaścāgnau jaguḥ sāmāni sāmagāḥ
|
tataścandanakāṣṭheśca tathā kālīyakairapi ||16||

17kālāguruprabhṛtibhirgandhaiścoccāvacaistathā
|
samavacchādya gāṅgeyaṃ saṃprajvālya hutāśanam ||17||

18apasavyamakurvanta dhṛtarāṣṭramukhāścitām
|
saṃskṛtya ca kuruśreṣṭhaṃ gāṅgeyaṃ kurusattamāḥ ||18||

19jagmurbhāgīrathīṃ puṇyāmṛṣijuṣṭāṃ kurūdvahāḥ
|
anugamyamānā vyāsena nāradenāsitena ca ||19||

20kṛṣṇena bharatastrībhirye ca paurāḥ samāgatāḥ
|
udakaṃ cakrire caiva gāṅgeyasya mahātmanaḥ ||20||

21vidhivatkṣatriyaśreṣṭhāḥ sa ca sarvo janastadā
|
tato bhāgīrathī devī tanayasyodake kṛte ||21||

22utthāya salilāttasmādrudatī śokavihvalā
|
paridevayatī tatra kauravānabhyabhāṣata ||22||

23nibodhata yathāvṛttamucyamānaṃ mayā'naghāḥ
|
rājavṛttena saṃpannaḥ prajñayā'bhijanena ca ||23||

24satkartā kuruvṛddhānāṃ pitṛbhakto mahāvrataḥ
|
jāmadagnyena rāmeṇa yaḥ purā na parājitaḥ ||24||

25divyairastrairmahāvīryaḥ sa hato'dya śikhaṇḍinā
|
aśmasāramayaṃ nūnaṃ hṛdayaṃ mama pārthivāḥ ||25||

26apaśyantyāḥ priyaṃ putraṃ yanna dīryati me'dya vai
|
sametaṃ pārthivaṃ kṣatraṃ kāśipuryāṃ svayaṃvare ||26||

27vijityaikarathenaiva kanyāścāyaṃ jahāra ha
|
yasya nāsti bale tulyaḥ pṛthivyāmapi kaścana ||27||

28hataṃ śikhaṇḍinā śrutvā na vidīryeta yanmanaḥ
|
jāmadagnyaḥ kurukṣetre yudhi yena mahātmanā ||28||

29pīḍito nātiyatnena sa hato'dya śikhaṇḍinā
|
evaṃvidhaṃ bahu tadā vilapantīṃ mahānadīm ||29||

30āśvāsayāmāsa tadā gaṅgāṃ dāmodaro vibhuḥ
|
samāśvasihi bhadre tvaṃ mā śucaḥ śubhadarśane ||30||

31gataḥ sa paramaṃ lokaṃ tava putro na saṃśayaḥ
|
vasureṣa mahātejāḥ śāpadoṣeṇa śobhane ||31||

32mānuṣatvamanuprāpto nainaṃ śocitumarhasi
|
sa eṣa kṣatradharmeṇa ayudhyata raṇājire ||32||

33dhanañjayena nihato naiṣa devi śikhaṇḍinā
|
bhīṣmaṃ hi kuruśārdūlamudyateṣu mahāraṇe ||33||

34na śaktaḥ saṃyuge hantuṃ sākṣāpi śatakratuḥ
|
svacchandatastava suto gataḥ svargaṃ śubhānane ||34||

35na śaktā vinihantuṃ hi raṇe taṃ sarvadevatāḥ
|
tasmānmā tvaṃ saricchreṣṭhe śocasva kurunandanam
|
vasūneṣa gato devi putraste vijvarā bhava ||35||

36vaiśampāyana uvāca|
ityuktā sā tu kṛṣṇena vyāsena tu saridvarā
|
tyaktvā śokaṃ mahārāja svaṃ vāryavatatāra ha ||36||

37satkṛtya te tāṃ saritaṃ tataḥ kṛṣṇamukhā nṛpa
|
anujñātāstayā sarve nyavartanta janādhipāḥ ||37||
