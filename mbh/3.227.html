Mahābhārata 3.227 (Āraṇyakaparvan)

1mārkaṇḍeya uvāca|
grahāḥ sopagrahāścaiva ṛṣayo mātarastathā
|
hutāśanamukhāścaiva dṛptāḥ pāriṣadāṃ gaṇāḥ||1||

2ete cānye ca bahavo ghorāstridivavāsinaḥ
|
parivārya mahāsenaṃ sthitā mātṛgaṇaiḥ saha||2||

3sandigdhaṃ vijayaṃ dṛṣṭvā vijayepsuḥ sureśvaraḥ
|
āruhyairāvataskandhaṃ prayayau daivataiḥ saha||3||

4ādāya vajraṃ balavān sarvairdevagaṇairvṛtaḥ
|
vijighāṃsurmahāsenamindrastūrṇataraṃ yayau||4||

5ugraṃ taṃ ca mahānādaṃ devānīkaṃ mahāprabham
|
vicitradhvajasannāhaṃ nānāvāhanakārmukam||5||

6pravarāmbarasaṃvītaṃ śriyā juṣṭamalaṅkṛtam
|
vijighāṃsuṃ tamāyāntaṃ kumāraḥ śakramanvayāt||6||

7vinadan pārthadeveśo drutaṃ yāti mahābalaḥ
|
saṃharṣayan devasenāṃ jighāṃsuḥ pāvakātmajam||7||

8sampūjyamānastridaśaistathaiva paramarṣibhiḥ
|
samīpamatha samprāptaḥ kārtikeyasya vāsavaḥ||8||

9siṃhanādaṃ tataścakre deveśaḥ sahitaiḥ suraiḥ
|
guho'pi śabdaṃ taṃ śrutvā vyanadat sāgaro yathā||9||

10tasya śabdena mahatā samudbhūtodadhiprabham
|
babhrāma tatra tatraiva devasainyamacetanam||10||

11jighāṃsūnupasamprāptān devān dṛṣṭvā sa pāvakiḥ
|
visasarja mukhāt kruddhaḥ pravṛddhāḥ pāvakārciṣaḥ||11||

12adahaddevasainyāni vepamānāni bhūtale
|
te pradīptaśirodehāḥ pradīptāyudhavāhanāḥ||12||

13pracyutāḥ sahasā bhānti vyastāstārāgaṇā iva
|
dahyamānāḥ prapannāste śaraṇaṃ pāvakātmajam||13||

14devā vajradharaṃ tyaktvā tataḥ śāntimupāgatāḥ
|
tyakto devaistataḥ skande vajraṃ śakro nyapātayat||14||

15tadvisṛṣṭaṃ jaghānāśu pārśvaṃ skandasya dakṣiṇam
|
bibheda ca mahārāja pārśvaṃ tasya mahātmanaḥ||15||

16vajraprahārāt skandasya sañjātaḥ puruṣo'paraḥ
|
yuvā kāñcanasannāhaḥ śaktidhṛgdivyakuṇḍalaḥ||16||

17yadvajraviśanājjāto viśākhastena so'bhavat
|
sañjātamaparaṃ dṛṣṭvā kālānalasamadyutim||17||

18bhayādindrastu taṃ skandaṃ prāñjaliḥ śaraṇaṃ gataḥ
|
tasyābhayaṃ dadau skandaḥ saha sainyasya sattamaḥ
|
tataḥ prahṛṣṭāstridaśā vāditrāṇyabhyavādayan||18||
