Mahābhārata 4.46 (Virāṭaparvan)

1vaiśampāyana uvāca|uttaraṃ sārathiṃ kṛtvā śamīṃ kṛtvā pradakṣiṇam
|
āyudhaṃ sarvamādāya prayayau pāṇḍavarṣabhaḥ ||1||

2dhvajaṃ siṃhaṃ rathāt tasmādapanīya mahārathaḥ
|
praṇidhāya śamīmūle prāyāduttarasārathiḥ ||2||

3devīṃ māyāṃ rathe yuktāṃ vihitāṃ viśvakarmaṇā
|
kāñcanaṃ siṃhalāṅgūlaṃ dhvajaṃ vānaralakṣaṇam ||3||

4manasā cintayāmāsa prasādaṃ pāvakasya ca
|
sa ca taccintitaṃ jñātvā dhvaje bhūtānyadeśayat ||4||

5sapatākaṃ vicitrāṅgaṃ sopāsaṅgaṃ mahābalam
|
khāt papāta rathe tūrṇaṃ divyarūpaṃ manoramam ||5||

6rathaṃ tamāgataṃ dṛṣṭvā dakṣiṇaṃ prākarot tadā
|
rathamāsthāya bībhatsuḥ kaunteyaḥ śvetavāhanaḥ ||6||

7baddhagodhāṅgulitrāṇaḥ pragṛhītaśarāsanaḥ
|
tataḥ prāyādudīcīṃ ca kapipravaraketanaḥ ||7||

8svanavantaṃ mahāśaṅkhaṃ balavānarimardanaḥ
|
prādhamadbalamāsthāya dviṣatāṃ lomaharṣaṇam ||8||

9tataste javanā dhuryā jānubhyāmagamanmahīm
|
uttaraścāpi santrasto rathopastha upāviśat ||9||

10saṃsthāpya cāśvānkaunteyaḥ samudyamya ca raśmibhiḥ
|
uttaraṃ ca pariṣvajya samāśvāsayadarjunaḥ ||10||

11arjuna uvāca|mā bhaistvaṃ rājaputrāgryaṃ kṣatriyo'si paraṃtapa
|
kathaṃ tu puruṣavyāghra śatrumadhye viṣīdasi ||11||

12śrutāste śaṅkhaśabdāśca bherīśabdāśca puṣkalāḥ
|
kuñjarāṇāṃ ca nadatāṃ vyūḍhānīkeṣu tiṣṭhatām ||12||

13sa tvaṃ kathamihānena śaṅkhaśabdena bhīṣitaḥ
|
vivarṇarūpo vitrastaḥ puruṣaḥ prākṛto yathā ||13||

14uttara uvāca|śrutā me śaṅkhaśabdāśca bherīśabdāśca puṣkalāḥ
|
kuñjarāṇāṃ ninadatāṃ vyūḍhānīkeṣu tiṣṭhatām ||14||

15naivaṃvidhaḥ śaṅkhaśabdaḥ purā jātu mayā śrutaḥ
|
dhvajasya cāpi rūpaṃ me dṛṣṭapūrvaṃ na hīdṛśam ||15||

16dhanuṣaścaiva nirghoṣaḥ śrutapūrvo na me kvacit
|
asya śaṅkhasya śabdena dhanuṣo niḥsvanena ca ||16||

17amānuṣāṇāṃ śabdena bhūtānāṃ dhvajavāsinām
|
rathasya ca ninādena mano muhyati me bhṛśam ||17||

18vyākulāśca diśaḥ sarvā hṛdayaṃ vyathatīva me
|
dhvajena pihitāḥ sarvā diśo na pratibhānti me ||18||

19gāṇḍīvasya ca śabdena karṇau me badhirīkṛtau
|
sa muhūrtaṃ prayātaṃ tu pārtho vairāṭimabravīt ||19||

20arjuna uvāca|ekāntaṃ rathamāsthāya padbhyāṃ tvamavapīḍayan
|
dṛḍhaṃ ca raśmīn saṃyaccha śaṅkhaṃ dhmāsyāmyahaṃ punaḥ||20||

21vaiśampāyana uvāca|tataḥ śaṅkhamupādhmāsīddārayanniva parvatān
|
guhā girīṇāṃ ca tadā diśaḥ śailāṃstathaiva ca ||21||

22uttaraścāpi saṃlīno rathopastha upāviśat
|
tasya śaṅkhasya śabdena rathanemisvanena ca ||22||

23gāṇḍīvasya ca ghoṣeṇa pṛthivī samakampata
|
taṃ samāśvāsayāmāsa punareva dhanañjayaḥ ||23||

24droṇa uvāca|yathā rathasya nirghoṣo yathā megha udīryate
|
kampate ca yathā bhūmirnaiṣo'nyaḥ savyasācinaḥ||24||

25śastrāṇi na prakāśante na prahṛṣyanti vājinaḥ
|
agnayaśca na bhāsante samiddhāstanna śobhanam ||25||

26pratyādityaṃ ca naḥ sarve mṛgā ghorapravādinaḥ
|
dhvajeṣu ca nilīyante vāyasāstanna śobhanam ||26||

27śakunāścāpasavyā no vedayanti mahadbhayam
|
gomāyureṣa senāyāṃ rudanmadhyena dhāvati ||27||

28anāhataśca niṣkrānto mahadvedayate bhayam
|
bhavatāṃ romakūpāṇi prahṛṣṭānyupalakṣaye ||28||

29dhruvaṃ vināśo yuddhena kṣatriyāṇāṃ pradṛśyate
|
jyotīṃṣi na prakāśante dāruṇā mṛgapakṣiṇaḥ ||29||

30utpātā vividhā ghorā dṛśyante kṣatranāśanāḥ
|
viśeṣata ihāsmākaṃ nimittāni vināśane ||30||

31ulkābhiśca pradīptābhirbādhyate pṛtanā tava
|
vāhanānyaprahṛṣṭāni rudantīva viśāmpate ||31||

32upāsate ca sainyāni gṛdhrāstava samantataḥ
|
taṣsyase vāhinīṃ dṛṣṭvā pārthabāṇaprapīḍitām
|
parābhūtā ca vaḥ senā na kaścidyoddhumicchati ||32||

33vivarṇamukhabhūyiṣṭhāḥ sarve yodhā vicetasaḥ
|
gāḥ saṃprasthāpya tiṣṭhāmo vyūḍhānīkāḥ prahāriṇaḥ ||33||
