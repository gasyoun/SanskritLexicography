Mahābhārata 1.220 (Ādiparvan)

1vaiśaṃpāyana uvāca|
tataḥ saṃvadite tasminnanujñāto dhanaṃjayaḥ
|
gatāṃ raivatake kanyāṃ viditvā janamejaya||1||

2vāsudevābhyanujñātaḥ kathatitvetikṛtyatām
|
kṛṣṇasya matamādāya prayayau bharatarṣabhaḥ||2||

3rathena kāñcanāṅgena kalpitena yathāvidhi
|
śaibyasugrīvayuktena kiṅkiṇījālamālinā||3||

4sarvaśastropapannena jīmūtaravanādinā
|
jvalitāgniprakāśena dviṣatāṃ harṣaghātinā||4||

5saṃnaddhaḥ kavacī khaḍgī baddhagodhāṅgulitravān
|
mṛgayāvyapadeśena prayayau puruṣarṣabhaḥ||5||

6subhadrā tvatha śailendramabhyacyaiva hi raivatam
|
daivatāni ca sarvāṇi brāhmaṇānsvasti vācya ca||6||

7pradakṣiṇaṃ gireḥ kṛtvā prayatau dvārakāṃ prati
|
tāmabhidrutya kaunteyaḥ prasahyāropayadratham
|
subhadrāṃ cārisarvāṅgīṃ kāmabāṇaprapīḍitaḥ||7||

8tataḥ sa puruṣavyāghrastāmādāya śucismitām
|
rathenā kāñcanāṅgena prayayau svapuraṃ prati||8||

9hriyamāṇāṃ tu tāṃ dṛṣṭvā subhadrāṃ sainikā janāḥ
|
vikrośanto'dravansarve dvārakāmabhitaḥ purīm||9||

10te samāsādya sahitāḥ sudharmāmabhitaḥ sabhām
|
sabhāpālasya tatsarvamācakhyuḥ pārthavikramam||10||

11teṣāṃ śrutvā sabhāpālo bherīṃ sānnāhikīṃ tataḥ
|
samājaghne mahāghoṣāṃ jāmbūnadapariṣkṛtām||11||

12kṣubdhāstenātha śabdena bhojavṛṣṇyandhakāstadā
|
annapānamapāsyātha samāpetuḥ samantataḥ||12||

13tatra jāmbūnadāṅgāni spardhyāstaraṇavanti ca
|
maṇividrumacitrāṇi jvalitāgniprabhāṇi ca||13||

14bhejire puruṣavyāghrā vṛṣṇyandhakamahārathāḥ
|
siṃhāsanāni śataśo dhiṣṇyānīva hutāśanāḥ||14||

15teṣāṃ samupaviṣṭānāṃ devānāmiva sannaye
|
ācakhyau ceṣṭitaṃ jiṣṇoḥ sabhāpālaḥ sahānugaḥ||15||

16tacchrutvā vṛṣṇivīrāste madasaṃraktalocanāḥ
|
amṛṣyamāṇāḥ pārthasya samutpeturahaṅkṛtāḥ||16||

17yojayadhvaṃ rathānāśu prāsānāharateti ca
|
dhanūṃṣi ca mahārhāṇi kavacāni bṛhanti ca||17||

18sūtānuccukruśuḥ kecidrathānyojayateti ca
|
svayaṃ ca turagānkecidayuñjanhemabhūṣitān||18||

19ratheṣvānīyamāneṣu kavaceṣu dhvajeṣu ca
|
abhikrande nṛvīrāṇāṃ tadāsīttumulaṃ mahat||19||

20vanamālī tataḥ kṣībaḥ kailāsaśikharopamaḥ
|
nīlavāsā madotsikta idaṃ vacanamabravīt||20||

21kimidaṃ kuruthāprajñāstūṣṇīṃbhūte janārdane
|
asya bhāvamavijñāya saṃkruddhā moghagarjitāḥ||21||

22eṣa tāvadabhiprāyamākhyātu svaṃ mahāmatiḥ
|
yadasya ruciraṃ kartuṃ tatkurudhvamatandritāḥ||22||

23tataste tadvacaḥ śrutvā grāhya rūpaṃ halāyudhāt
|
tūṣṇīṃbhūtāstataḥ sarve sādhu sādhviti cābruvan||23||

24samaṃ vaco niśamyaiva baladevasya dhīmataḥ
|
punareva sabhāmadhye sarve te samupāviśan||24||

25tato'bravīdvāsudevaṃ vaco rāmaḥ parantapaḥ
|
kimavāgupaviṣṭo'si prekṣamāṇo janārdana||25||

26satkṛtastvatkṛte pārthaḥ sarvairasmābhiracyuta
|
na ca so'rhati tāṃ pūjāṃ durbuddhiḥ kulapāṃsanaḥ||26||

27ko hi tatraiva bhuktvānnaṃ bhājanaṃ bhettumarhati
|
manyamānaḥ kule jātamātmānaṃ puruṣaḥ kvacit||27||

28icchanneva hi saṃbandhaṃ kṛtaṃ pūrvaṃ ca mānayan
|
ko hi nāma bhavenārthī sāhasena samācaret||28||

29so'vamanya tathā'smākamanādṛtya ca keśavam
|
prasahya hṛtavānadya subhadrāṃ mṛtyumātmanaḥ||29||

30kathaṃ hi śiraso madhye kṛtaṃ tena padaṃ mama
|
marṣayiṣyāmi govinda pādasparśamivoragaḥ||30||

31adya niṣkauravāmekaḥ kariṣyāmi vasundharām
|
na hi me marṣaṇīyo'yamarjunasya vyatikramaḥ||31||

32taṃ tathā garjamānaṃ tu meghadundubhiniḥsvanam
|
anvapadyanta te sarve bhojavṛṣṇyandhakāstadā||32||
