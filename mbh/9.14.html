Mahābhārata 9.14 (Śalyaparvan)

1sañjaya uvāca|
arjuno drauṇinā viddho yuddhe bahubhirāyasaiḥ
|
tasya cānucaraiḥ śūraistrigartānāṃ mahārathaiḥ||1||

2drauṇiṃ vivyādha samare tribhireva śilīmukhaiḥ
|
tathetarānmaheṣvāsān dvābhyāṃ dvābhyāṃ dhanañjayaḥ||2||

3bhūyaścaiva mahārāja śaravarṣairavākirat
|
śarakaṇṭakitāste tu tāvakā bharatarṣabha||3||

4na jahuḥ pārthamāsādya tāḍyamānāḥ śitaiḥ śaraiḥ
|
arjunaṃ rathavaṃśena droṇaputrapurogamāḥ||4||

5ayodhayanta samare parivārya mahārathāḥ
|
taistu kṣiptāḥ śarā rājankārtasvaravibhūṣitāḥ||5||

6arjunasya rathopasthaṃ pūrayāmāsurañjasā
|
tathā kṛṣṇau maheṣvāsau vṛṣabhau sarvadhanvinām||6||

7śarairvīkṣya vinunnāṅgau prahṛṣṭā yuddhadurmadāḥ
|
kūbaraṃ rathacakrāṇi īṣā yoktrāṇi vā vibho||7||

8yugaṃ caivānukarṣaṃ ca śarabhūtamabhūttadā
|
naitādṛśaṃ dṛṣṭapūrvaṃ rājannaiva ca nasa śrutam||8||

9yādṛśaṃ tatra pārthasya tāvakāḥ sampracakrire
|
sa rathaḥ sarvato bhāti citrapuṅkhaiḥ śitaiḥ śaraiḥ||9||

10ulkāśataiḥ sampradīptaṃ vimānamiva bhūtale
|
tato'rjuno mahārāja śaraiḥ sannataparvabhiḥ||10||

11avākirattāṃ pṛtanāṃ magho vṛṣṭyeva parvatam
|
te vadhyamānāḥ samare pārthanāmāṅkitaiḥ śaraiḥ||11||

12pārthabhūtamamanyanta prekṣamāṇāstathāvidham
|
kopoddhṛtaśarajvālo dhanuḥśabdānilo mahān||12||

13sainyendhanaṃ dadāhāśu tāvakaṃ pārthapāvakaḥ
|
cakrāṇāṃ patatāṃ cāpi yugānāṃ ca dharātale||13||

14tūṇīrāṇāṃ patākānāṃ dhvajānāṃ ca rathaiḥ saha
|
īṣāṇāmanukarṣāṇāṃ triveṇūnāṃ ca bhārata||14||

15akṣāṇamatha yoktrāṇāṃ pratodānāṃ ca sarvaśaḥ
|
śirasāṃ patatāṃ cāpi kuṇḍaloṣṇīṣadhāriṇām||15||

16bhujānāṃ ca mahābhāga skandhānāṃ ca samantataḥ
|
chatrāṇāṃ vyajanaiḥ sārdhaṃ mukuṭānāṃ ca rāśayaḥ||16||

17samadṛśyanta pārthasya rathamārgeṣu bhārata
|
tataḥ kruddhasya pārthasya rathamārge viśāmpate||17||

18agamyarūpā pṛthivī māṃsaśoṇitakardamā
|
bhīrūṇāṃ trāsajananī śūrāṇāṃ harṣavardhinī||18||

19babhūva bharataśreṣṭha rudrasyākrīḍanaṃ yathā
|
hatvā tu samare pārthaḥ sahasre dve parantapaḥ||19||

20rathānāṃ savarūthānāṃ vidhūmo'gniriva jvalan
|
yathā hi bhagavānagnirjagaddagdhvā carācaram||20||

21vidhūmo dṛśyate rājaṃstathā pārtho dhanañjayaḥ
|
drauṇistu samare dṛṣṭvā pāṇḍavasya parākramam||21||

22rathenātipatākena pāṇḍavaṃ pratyavārayat
|
tāvubhau puruṣavyāghrau tāvubhau dhanvināṃ varau||22||

23samīyatustadā'nyonyaṃ parasparavadhaiṣiṇau
|
tayorāsīnmahārāja bāṇavarṣaṃ sudāruṇam||23||

24jīmūtayoryathā vṛṣṭistapānte bharatarṣabha
|
anyonyaspardhinau tau tu śaraiḥ sannataparvabhi||24||

25tatakṣatustadā'nyonyaṃ śṛṅgābhyāṃ vṛṣabhāviva
|
tayoryuddhaṃ mahārāja ciraṃ samamivābhavat||25||

26śastrāṇāṃ saṅgamaścaiva ghorastatrābhavatpunaḥ
|
tato'rjunaṃ dvādaśabhī rukmapuṅkhaiḥ sutejanaiḥ||26||

27vāsudevaṃ ca daśabhidrauṇirvivyādha bhārata
|
tataḥ praharṣādbībhatsurvyākṣipadgāṇḍivaṃ dhanuḥ||27||

28mānayitvā muhūrtaṃ tu guruputraṃ mahāhave
|
vyaśvasūtarathaṃ cakre savyasācī parantapaḥ||28||

29mṛdupūrvaṃ tataścainaṃ punaḥ punaratāḍayat
|
hatāśve tu rathe tiṣṭhandroṇaputrastvayasmayam||29||

30musalaṃ pāṇḍuputrāya cikṣepa parighopamam
|
tamāpatantaṃ sahasā hemapaṭṭavibhūṣitam||30||

31ciccheda saptadhā vīraḥ pārthaḥ śatrunibarhaṇaḥ
|
sa cchinnaṃ musalaṃ dṛṣṭvā drauṇiḥ paramakopanaḥ||31||

32ādade parighaṃ ghoraṃ nagendriśikharopamam
|
cikṣepa caiva pārthāya drauṇiyuddhaviśāradaḥ||32||

33tamantakamiva kruddhaṃ parighaṃ prekṣya pāṇḍavaḥ
|
arjunastvarito jaghne pañcabhiḥ sāyakottamaiḥ||33||

34sa cchinnaḥ patito bhūmau pārthabāṇairmahāhave
|
dārayan pṛthivīndrāṇaṃ manāṃsīva ca bhārata||34||

35tato'paraistribhirbhallairdrauṇiṃ vivyādha pāṇḍavaḥ
|
so'tividdho balavatā pārthena sumahātmanā||35||

36nākampata tadā drauṇiḥ pauruṣe sve vyavasthitaḥ
|
surathaṃ ca tato rājan bhāradvājo mahāratham||36||

37avākiraccharavrātaiḥ sarvakṣatrasya paśyataḥ
|
tatastu suratho'pyājau pañcālānāṃ mahārathaḥ||37||

38rathena meghaghoṣeṇa drauṇimevābhyadhāvata
|
vikarṣanvai dhanuḥ śreṣṭhaṃ sarvabhārasahaṃ dṛḍham||38||

39jvalanāśīviṣanibhaiḥ śaraiścainamavākirat
|
surathaṃ taṃ tataḥ kruddhamāpatantaṃ mahāratham||39||

40cukopa samare drauṇirdaṇḍāhata ivoragaḥ
|
triśikhāṃ bhrukuṭīṃ kṛtvā sṛkkiṇī parisaṃlahan||40||

41udvīkṣya surathaṃ roṣāddhanurdhyāmavamṛjya ca
|
mumoca tīkṣṇaṃ nārācaṃ yamadaṇḍopamadyutim||41||

42sa tasya hradayaṃ bhittvā praviveśātivegitaḥ
|
śakrāśanirivotsṛṣṭo vidārya dharaṇītalam||42||

43tataḥ sa patito bhūmau nārācena samāhataḥ
|
vajreṇa ca yathā śṛṅgaṃ parvatasyeva dīryataḥ||43||

44tasminvinihate vīre droṇaputraḥ pratāpavān
|
āruroha rathaṃ tūrṇaṃ tameva rathināṃ varaḥ||44||

45tataḥ sajjo mahārāja drauṇirāhavadurmadaḥ
|
arjunaṃ yodhayāmāsa saṃśaptakavṛto raṇe||45||

46tatra yuddhaṃ mahaccāsīdarjunasya paraiḥ saha
|
madhyandinagate sūrye yamarāṣṭravivardhanam||46||

47tatrāścaryamapaśyāma dṛṣṭvā teṣāṃ parākramam
|
yadeko yugapadvīrānsamayodhayadarjunaḥ||47||

48vimardaḥ sumahānāsīdekasya bahubhiḥ saha
|
śatakraturyathā pūrvaṃ mahatyā daityasenayā||48||
