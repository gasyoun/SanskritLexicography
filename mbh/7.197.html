Mahābhārata 7.197 (Droṇaparvan)

1sañjaya uvāca|
arjunasya vacaḥ śrutvā nocustatra mahārathāḥ
|
apriyaṃ vā priyaṃ vāpi mahārāja dhanañjayam||1||

2tataḥ kruddho mahābāhurbhīmaseno'bhyabhāṣata
|
kutsayanniva kaunteyamarjunaṃ bharatarṣabha||2||

3suniryathā'raṇyagato bhāṣate dharmasaṃhitam
|
nyastadaṇḍo yathā pārtha brāhmaṇaḥ saṃśitavrataḥ||3||

4kṣatatrātā kṣatājjīvan kṣaṃtā strīṣvapi sādhuṣu
|
kṣatriyaḥ kṣitimāpnoti kṣipraṃ dharmaṃ yaśaḥ śriyaḥ||4||

5sa bhavān kṣatriyaguṇairyuktaḥ sarvaiḥ kulodvahaḥ
|
avipaścidyathā vācaṃ vyāharannādya śobhase||5||

6parākramaste kaunteya śakrasyeva śacīpateḥ
|
na cāti vartase dharmaṃ velāmiva mahodadhiḥ||6||

7na pūjayettvāṃ ko nvadya yantrayodaśavārṣikam
|
amarṣaṃ pṛṣṭhataḥ kṛtvā dharmamevābhikāṃkṣase||7||

8diṣṭyā tāta manaste'dya svadharmamanuvartate
|
ānṛśaṃsye ca te diṣṭyā buddhiḥ satatamacyuta||8||

9yattu dharmapravṛttasya hṛtaṃ rājyamadharmataḥ
|
draupadī ca parāmṛṣṭā sabhāmānīya śatrubhiḥ||9||

10vanaṃ pravājitāścāsma valkalājinavāsasaḥ
|
anarhamāṇāstaṃ bhāvaṃ trayodaśa samāḥ paraiḥ||10||

11etānyamarṣasthānāni marṣitāni mayā'nagha
|
kṣatradharmaprasaktena sarvametada‌nuṣṭhitam||11||

12tamadharmamapākṛṣṭaṃ smṛtvā'dya sahitastvayā
|
sānubandhānhaniṣyāmi kṣudrān rājyaharānaham||12||

13tvayā hi kathitaṃ pūrvaṃ yuddhāyābhyāgatā vayam
|
ghaṭāmahe yathāśakti tvaṃ tu no'dya jugupsase||13||

14dharmamanvicchasi jñātuṃ mithyāvacanameva te
|
bhayārditānāmasmākaṃ vācā marmāṇi kṛṃtasi||14||

15vapan vraṇe kṣāramiva kṣatānāṃ śatrukarśana
|
vidīryate me hṛdayaṃ tvayā vākśalyapīḍitam||15||

16adharmamenaṃ vipulaṃ dhārmikaḥ sanna buddhyase
|
yattvamātmānamasmāṃśca praśaṃsyānna praśaṃsasi||16||

17vāsudeve sthite cāpi droṇaputraṃ praśaṃsasi
|
yaḥ kalāṃ ṣoḍaśīṃ pūrṇāṃ dhanañjaya na te'rhati||17||

18svayamevātmano doṣān bruvāṇaḥ kinna lajjase
|
dārayeyaṃ mahīṃ krodhādvikireyaṃ ca parvatān||18||

19āvidhyaitāṃ gadāṃ gurvīṃ bhīmāṃ kāñcanamālinīm
|
giriprakāśān kṣitijān bhañjeyamanilo yathā||19||

20drāvayeyaṃ śaraiścāpi sendrāndevānsamāgatān
|
sarākṣasagaṇānpārtha sāsuroragamānavān||20||

21sa tvamevaṃvidhaṃ jānan bhrātaraṃ māṃ nararṣabha
|
droṇaputrādbhayaṃ kartuṃ nārhasyamitavikrama||21||

22athavā tiṣṭha bībhatso saha sarvaiḥ sahodaraiḥ
|
ahamenaṃ gadāpāṇirjeṣyāmyeko mahāhave||22||

23tataḥ pāñcālarājasya putraḥ pārthamathābravīt
|
saṃkruddhamiva nardantaṃ hiraṇyakaśipurharim||23||

24dhṛṣṭadyumna uvāca|
bībhatso viprakarmāṇi viditāni manīṣiṇām
|
yājanādhyāpane dānaṃ tathā yajñapratigrahau||24||

25ṣaṣṭhamadhyayanaṃ nāma, teṣāṃ kasminpratiṣṭhitaḥ
|
hato droṇo mayā hyevaṃ kiṃ māṃ pārtha vigarhase||25||

26apakrāntaḥ svadharmācca kṣātradharmaṃ vyapāśritaḥ
|
amānuṣeṇa hantyasmānastreṇa kṣudrakarmakṛt||26||

27tathā māyāṃ prayuñjānamasahyaṃ brāhmaṇabruvam
|
māyayaiva nihanyādyo na yuktaṃ pārtha tatra kim||27||

28tasmiṃstathā mayā śaste yadi drauṇāyanī ruṣā
|
kurute bhairavaṃ nādaṃ tatra kiṃ mama hīyate||28||

29na cādbhutamidaṃ manye yaddrauṇiryuddhasaṃjñayā
|
ghātayiṣyati kauravyānparitrātumaśaknuvan||29||

30yacca māṃ dhārmiko bhūtvā bravīṣi gurughātinam
|
tadarthamahamutpannaḥ pāñcālyasya suto'nalāt||30||

31yasya kāryamakāryaṃ vā yudhyataḥ syātsamaṃ raṇe
|
taṃ kathaṃ brāhmaṇaṃ brūyāḥ kṣatriyaṃ vā dhanañjaya||31||

32yo hyanastravido hanyādbrahmāstraiḥ krodhamūrcchitaḥ
|
sarvopāyairna sa kathaṃ vadhyaḥ puruṣasattamaḥ||32||

33vidharmiṇaṃ dharmavidbhiḥ proktaṃ teṣāṃ viṣopamam
|
jānandharmārthatattvajña kiṃ māmarjuna garhase||33||

34nṛśaṃsaḥ sa mayā''kramya ratha eva nipātitaḥ
|
tanmāmaniṃdyaṃ bībhatso kimarthaṃ nābhinandase||34||

35kālānalasamaṃ pārtha jvalanārkaviṣopamam
|
bhīmaṃ droṇaśiraśchinnaṃ na praśaṃsāsa me katham||35||

36yo'sau mamaiva nānyasya bāndhavān yudhi jaghnivān
|
chittvā'pi tasya mūrdhānaṃ naivāsmi vigatajvaraḥ||36||

37tacca me kṛntate marma yanna tasya śiro mayā
|
niṣāda‌viṣaye kṣiptaṃ jayadrathārīro yathā||37||

38athāvadhaśca śatrūṇāmadharmaḥ śrūyate'rjuna
|
kṣatriyasya hi dharmo'yaṃ hanyāddhanyeta vā punaḥ||38||

39sa śatrurnihataḥ saṃkhye mayā dharmeṇa pāṇḍava
|
yathā tvayā hataḥ śūro bhagadattaḥ pituḥ sakhā||39||

40pitāmahaṃ raṇe hatvā manyase dharmamātmanaḥ
|
mayā śatrau hate kasmātpāpe dharmaṃ na manyase||40||

41saṃbandhāvanataṃ pārtha na māṃ tvaṃ vaktumarhasi
|
svagātrakṛtasopānaṃ niṣaṇṇamiva dantinam||41||

42kṣamāmi te sarvameva vāgvyatikramamarjuna
|
draupadyā draupadeyānāṃ kṛte nānyena hetunā||42||

43kulakramāgataṃ vairaṃ mamācāryeṇa viśrutam
|
tathā jānātyayaṃ loko na yūyaṃ pāṇḍunandanāḥ||43||

44nānṛtī pāṇḍavo jyeṣṭho nāhaṃ vā'dhārmiko'rjuna
|
śiṣyadrohī hataḥ pāpo yudhyasva vijayastava||44||
