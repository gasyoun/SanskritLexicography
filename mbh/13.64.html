Mahābhārata 13.64 (Anuśāsanaparvan)

1yudhiṣṭhira uvāca|
śrutaṃ me bhavato vākyamannadānasya yo vidhiḥ
|
nakṣatrayogasyedānīṃ dānakalpaṃ bravīhi me ||1||

2bhīṣma uvāca|
atrāpyudāharantīmamitihāsaṃ purātanam
|
devakyāścaiva saṃvādaṃ maharṣernāradasya ca ||2||

3dvārakāmanusaṃprāptaṃ nāradaṃ devadarśanam
|
papracchedaṃ vacaḥ praśnaṃ devakī dharmadarśanam ||3||

4tasyāḥ saṃpṛcchamānāyā devarṣirnāradastataḥ
|
ācaṣṭa vidhivatsarvaṃ tacchṛṇuṣva viśāṃpate ||4||

5nārada uvāca|
kṛttikāsu mahābhāge pāyasena sasarpiṣā
|
saṃtarpya brāhmaṇānsādhū~llokānāpnotyanuttamān ||5||

6rohiṇyāṃ prasṛtairmārgairmāṃsairannena sarpiṣā
|
payonnapānaṃ dātavyamanṛṇārthaṃ dvijātaye ||6||

7dogdhrīṃ datvā savatsāṃ tu nakṣatre somadaivate
|
gacchanti mānuṣāllokātsvargalokamanuttamam ||7||

8ārdrāyāṃ kṛsaraṃ datvā tilamiśramupoṣitaḥ
|
narastarati durgāṇi kṣuradhārāṃśca parvatān ||8||

9pūpānpunarvasau datvā tathaivānnāni śobhane
|
yaśasvī rūpasaṃpanno bahvanno jāyate kule ||9||

10puṣyeṇa kanakaṃ datvā kṛtaṃ vā kṛtameva ca
|
anālokeṣu lokeṣu somavatsa virājate ||10||

11āśleṣāyāṃ tu yo rūpyamṛṣabhaṃ vā prayacchati
|
sa sarvabhayanirmuktaḥ sambhavānadhitiṣṭhati ||11||

12maghāsu tilapūrṇāni vardhamānāni mānavaḥ
|
pradāya putrapaśumāniha pretya ca modate ||12||

13phalgunīpūrvasamaye brāhmaṇānāmupoṣitaḥ
|
bhakṣyānphāṇitasaṃyuktān datvā saubhāgyamṛcchati ||13||

14ghṛtakṣīrasamāyuktaṃ vidhivatṣaṣṭikaudanam
|
uttarāviṣaye dattvā svargaloke mahīyate ||14||

15yadyatpradīyate dānamuttarāviṣaye naraiḥ
|
mahāphalamanantaṃ tadbhavatīti viniścayaḥ ||15||

16haste hastirathaṃ datvā caturyuktamupoṣitaḥ
|
prāpnoti paramā~llokānpuṇyakāmasamanvitān ||16||

17citrāyāṃ vṛṣabhaṃ datvā puṇyagandhāṃśca bhārata
|
carantyapsarasāṃ loke ramante nandane tathā ||17||

18svātyāmatha dhanaṃ datvā yadiṣṭatamamātmanaḥ
|
prāpnoti lokānsa śubhāniha caiva mahadyaśaḥ ||18||

19viśākhāyāmanaḍvāhaṃ dhenuṃ datvā ca dugdhadām
|
saprāsaṃgaṃ ca śakaṭaṃ sadhānyaṃ vastrasaṃyutam ||19||

20pitṝndevāṃśca prīṇāti pretya cānantyamaśrute
|
na ca durgāṇyavāpnoti svargalokaṃ ca gacchati ||20||

21datvā yathoktaṃ viprebhyo vṛttimiṣṭāṃ sa vindati
|
narakādīṃśca saṃkleśānnāpnotīti viniścayaḥ ||21||

22anurādhāsu prāvāraṃ varānnaṃ samupoṣitaḥ
|
dattvā yugaśataṃ cāpi naraḥ svarge mahīyate ||22||

23kālaśākaṃ tu viprebhyo dattvā martyaḥ samūlakam
|
jyeṣṭhāyāmṛddhimiṣṭāṃ vai gatimiṣṭāṃ sa gacchati ||23||

24mūle mūlaphalaṃ datvā brāhmaṇebhyaḥ samāhitaḥ
|
pitṝnprīṇayate cāpi gatimiṣṭāṃ ca gacchati ||24||

25atha pūrvāsvaṣāḍhāsu dadhipātrāṇyupoṣitaḥ
|
kulavṛttopasaṃpanne brāhmaṇe veda‌pārage ||25||

26puruṣo jāyate pretya kule subahugodhane
|
udamanthaṃ sasarpiṣkaṃ prabhūtamadhiphāṇitam ||26||

27datvottarāsvaṣāḍhāsu sarvakāmānavāpnuyāt
|
dugdhaṃ tvabhijite yoge datvā madhughṛtaplutam
|
dharmanityo manīṣibhyaḥ svargaloke mahīyate ||27||

28śravaṇe kambalaṃ datvā vastrāntaritameva vā
|
śvetena yāti yānena svargalokānasaṃvṛtān ||28||

29goprayuktaṃ dhaniṣṭhāsu yānaṃ datvā samāhitaḥ
|
vastrarāśidhanaṃ sadyaḥ pretya rājyaṃ prapadyate ||29||

30gandhāñchatabhiṣāyoge datvā sāgurucandanān
|
prāpnotyapsarasāṃ saṃghānpretya gandhāṃśca śāśvatān ||30||

31pūrvābhādrapadāyoge rājamāṣānpradāya tu
|
sarvabhakṣaphalopetaḥ sa vai pretya sukhī bhavet ||31||

32aurabhramuttarāyoge yastu māṃsaṃ prayacchati
|
sa pitṝnprīṇayati vai pretya cānantyamaśrute ||32||

33kāṃsyopadohanāṃ dhenuṃ revatyāṃ yaḥ prayacchati
|
sā pretya kāmānādāya dātāramupatiṣṭhati ||33||

34rathamaśvasamāyuktaṃ datvā'śvinyāṃ narottamaḥ
|
hastyaśvarathasaṃpanne varcasvī jāyate kule ||34||

35bharaṇīṣu dvijātibhyastiladhenuṃ pradāya vai
|
gāḥ suprabhūtāḥ prāpnoti naraḥ pretya yaśastathā ||35||

36bhīṣma uvāca|
ityeṣa lakṣaṇoddeśaḥ prokto nakṣatrayogataḥ
|
devakyā nāradeneha sā snuṣābhyo'bravīdidam ||36||
