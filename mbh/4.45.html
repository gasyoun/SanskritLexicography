Mahābhārata 4.45 (Virāṭaparvan)

1uttara uvāca|āsthāya ruciraṃ vīra rathaṃ sārathinā mayā
|
katamaṃ yāsyase'nīkamukto yāsyāmyahaṃ tvayā ||1||

2arjuna uvāca|prīto'smi puruṣavyāghra na bhayaṃ vidyate tava
|
sarvānnudāmi te śatrūn raṇe raṇaviśārada ||2||

3svastho bhava mahābāho paśya māṃ śatrubhiḥ saha
|
yudhyamānaṃ vimarde'smin kurvāṇaṃ bhairavaṃ mahat ||3||

4etānsarvānupāsaṅgān kṣipraṃ badhnīhi me rathe
|
ekaṃ cāhara nistriṃśaṃ jātarūpapariṣkṛtam ||4||

5vaiśampāyana uvāca|arjunasya vacaḥ śrutvā tvarāvānuttarastadā
|
arjunasyāyudhān gṛhya śīghreṇāvātarattataḥ ||5||

6arjuna uvāca|ahaṃ vai kurubhiryotsyāmyavajeṣyāmi te paśūn ||6||

7saṃkalpapakṣavikṣepaṃ bāhuprākāratoraṇam
|
tridaṇḍatūṇasaṃbādhamanekadhvajasaṃkulam ||7||

8vyākṣepaṇaṃ krodhakṛtaṃ nemīninadadundubhi
|
nagaraṃ te mayā guptaṃ rathopasthaṃ bhaviṣyati ||8||

9adhiṣṭhito mayā saṃkhye ratho gāṇḍīvadhanvanā
|
ajeyaḥ śatrusainyānāṃ vairāṭe vyetu te bhayam ||9||

10uttara uvāca|bibhemi nāhameteṣāṃ jānāmi tvāṃ sthiraṃ yudhi
|
keśavenāpi saṅgrāme sākṣādindreṇa vā samam ||10||

11idaṃ tu cintayannevaṃ parimuhyāmi kevalam
|
niścayaṃ cāpi durmedhā na gacchāmi kathaṃcana ||11||

12evaṃ yuktāṅgarūpasya lakṣaṇaiḥ sūcitasya ca
|
kena karmavipākena klībatvamida‌māgatam ||12||

13manye tvāṃ klībaveṣeṇa carantaṃ śūlapāṇinam
|
gandharvarājapratimaṃ devaṃ vā'pi śatakratum ||13||

14arjuna uvāca|bhrāturniyogājjyeṣṭhasya saṃvatsaramidaṃ vratam
|
carāmi vratacaryaṃ ca satyametadbravīmi te ||14||

15nāsmi klībo mahābāho paravān dharmasaṃyutaḥ
|
samāptavratamuttīrṇaṃ viddhi māṃ tvaṃ nṛpātmaja ||15||

16uttara uvāca|paramo'nugraho me'dya yatastarko na me vṛthā
|
na hīdṛśāḥ klībarūpā bhavanti tu narottama ||16||

17sahāyavānasmi raṇe yudhyeyamamarairapi
|
sādhvasaṃ hi pranaṣṭaṃ me kiṃ karomi bravīhi me ||17||

18ahaṃ te saṃgrahīṣyāmi hayān śatrurathārujān
|
śikṣito hyasmi sārathye tīrthataḥ puruṣarṣabha ||18||

19dāruko vāsudevasya yathā śakrasya mātaliḥ
|
tathā māṃ viddhi sārathye śikṣitaṃ narapuṅgava ||19||

20yasya yāte na paśyanti bhūmau kṣiptaṃ padaṃ padam
|
dakṣiṇāṃ yo dhuraṃ yuktaḥ sugrīvasadṛśo hayaḥ ||20||

21yo'yaṃ dhuraṃ dhuryavaro vāmāṃ vahati śobhanaḥ
|
taṃ manye meghapuṣpasya javena sadṛśaṃ hayam ||21||

22yo'yaṃ kāñcanasannāhaḥ pārṣṇiṃ vahati śobhanaḥ
|
samaṃ śaivyasya taṃ manye javena balavattaram ||22||

23yo'yaṃ vahati me pārṣṇiṃ dakṣiṇāmabhitaḥ sthitaḥ
|
balāhakādapi mataḥ sa jave vīryavattaraḥ ||23||

24tvāmevāyaṃ ratho boḍhuṃ saṅgrāme'rhati dhanvinam
|
tvaṃ cemaṃ rathamāsthāya yoddhumarho mato mama ||24||

25vaiśampāyana uvāca|tato vimucya bāhubhyāṃ valayāni sa viryavān
|
citre kāñcanasannāhe pratyamuñcat tadā tale ||25||

26kṛṣṇān bhaṅgimataḥ keśān śvetenodgrathya vāsasā
|
athāsau prāṅmukho bhūtvā śuciḥ prayatamānasaḥ
|
abhidadhyau mahābāhuḥ sarvāstrāṇi rathottame ||26||

27ūcuśca pārthaṃ sarvāṇi prāñjalīni nṛpātmajam
|
ime sma paramodārāḥ kiṃkarāḥ pāṇḍunandana ||27||

28praṇipatya tataḥ pārthaḥ samālabhya ca pāṇinā
|
sarvāṇi mānasānīha bhavatetyabhyabhāṣata ||28||

29pratigṛhya tato'strāṇi prahṛṣṭavadano'bhavat
|
adhijyaṃ tarasā kṛtvā gāṇḍīvaṃ vyākṣipaddhanuḥ ||29||

30tasya vikṣipyamāṇasya dhanuṣo'bhūnmahādhvaniḥ
|
yathā śailasya mahataḥ śailenaivāvajaghnataḥ ||30||

31sa nirghāto'bhavadbhūbhiddikṣu vāyurvavau bhṛśam
|
papāta mahatī colkā diśo na pracakāśire
|
bhrāntadhvajaṃ khaṃ tadāsītprakampitamahādrumam ||31||

32taṃ śabdaṃ kuravo'jānan visphoṭamaśaneriva
|
yadarjuno dhanuḥśreṣṭhaṃ bāhubhyāmākṣipadrathe ||32||

33uttara uvāca|ekastvaṃ pāṇḍavaśreṣṭha bahūnetān mahārathān
|
kathaṃ jeṣyasi saṅgrāme sarvaśastrāstrapāragān ||33||

34asahāyo'si kaunteya sasahāyāśca kauravāḥ
|
ata eva mahābāho bhītastiṣṭhāmi te'grataḥ ||34||

35uvāca pārtho mā bhaiṣīḥ prahasya svanavattadā ||35||

36yudhyamānasya me vīra gandharvaiḥ sumahābalaiḥ
|
sahāyo ghoṣayātrāyāṃ kastadāsīt sakhā mama ||36||

37tathā pratibhaye tasmin devadānavasaṃkule
|
khāṇḍave yudhyamānasya kastadāsīt sakhā mama ||37||

38nivātakavacaiḥ sārdhaṃ paulomaiśca mahābalaiḥ
|
yudhyato devarājārthe kaḥ sahāyastadā'bhavat||38||

39svayaṃvare tu pāñcālyā rājabhiḥ saha saṃyuge
|
yudhyato bahubhistāta kaḥ sahāyastadā'bhavat ||39||

40upajīvya guruṃ droṇaṃ śakraṃ vaiśravaṇaṃ yamam
|
varuṇaṃ pāvakaṃ caiva kṛpaṃ kṛṣṇaṃ ca mādhavam ||40||

41pinākapāṇinaṃ caiva kathametānna yodhaye
|
rathaṃ vāhaya me śīghraṃ vyetu te mānaso jvaraḥ ||41||
