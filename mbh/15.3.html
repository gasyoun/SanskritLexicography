Mahābhārata 15.3 (Āśramavāsikaparvan)

1vaiśampāyana uvāca|
yudhiṣṭhirasya nṛpaterduryodhanapitustadā
|
nāntaraṃ dadṛśū rājye puruṣāḥ praṇayaṃ prati||1||

2yadā tu kauravo rājā putraṃ sasmāra durmatim
|
tadā bhīmaṃ hṛdā rājannapadhyāti sa pārthivaḥ||2||

3tathaiva bhīmaseno'pi dhṛtarāṣṭraṃ janādhipam
|
nāmarṣayata rājendra sadaiva duṣṭavaddhṛdā||3||

4aprakāśānyapriyāṇi cakārāsya vṛkodaraḥ
|
ājñāṃ pratyaharaccāpi kṛtajñaiḥ puruṣaiḥ sadā||4||

5smarandurmantritaṃ tasya vṛttānyapyasya kānicit
|
atha bhīmaḥ suhṛnmadhye bāhuśabdaṃ tathākarot||5||

6saṃśrave dhṛtarāṣṭrasya gāndhāryāścāpyamarṣaṇaḥ
|
smṛtvā duryodhanaṃ śatruṃ karṇaduḥśāsanāvapi||6||

7provācedaṃ susaṃrabdho bhīmaḥ sa paruṣaṃ vacaḥ
|
andhasya nṛpateḥ putrā mayā parighabāhunā||7||

8nītā lokamamuṃ sarve nānāśastrāstrayodhinaḥ
|
imau tau parighaprakhyau bhujau mama durāsadau||8||

9yayorantaramāsādya dhārtarāṣṭrāḥ kṣayaṃ gatāḥ
|
tāvimau candanenāktau candanārhau ca me bhujau||9||

10yābhyāṃ duryodhano nītaḥ kṣayaṃ sasutabāndhavaḥ
|
etāścānyāśca vividhāḥ śalyabhūtā narādhipaḥ||10||

11vṛkodarasya tā vācaḥ śrutvā nirvedamāgamat
|
sā ca buddhimatī devī kālaparyāyavedinī||11||

12gāndhārī sarvadharmajñā tānyalīkāni śuśruve
|
tataḥ pañcadaśe varṣe samatīte narādhipaḥ||12||

13rājā nirvedamāpede bhīmavāgbāṇapīḍitaḥ
|
nānvabudhyata tadrājā kuntīputro yudhiṣṭhiraḥ||13||

14śvetāśvo vātha kuntī vā draupadī vā yaśasvinī
|
mādrīputrau ca dharmajñau cittaṃ tasyānvavartatām||14||

15rājñastu cittaṃ rakṣantau nocatuḥ kiñcidapriyam
|
tataḥ sa mānayāmāsa dhṛtarāṣṭraḥ suhṛjjanam||15||

16bāṣpasandigdhamatyarthamidamāha ca tānbhṛśam
|
dhṛtarāṣṭra uvāca|
viditaṃ bhavatāmetadyathā vṛttaḥ kurukṣayaḥ||16||

17mamāparādhāttatsarvamanujñātaṃ ca kauravaiḥ
|
yo'haṃ duṣṭamatiṃ mando jñātīnāṃ bhayavardhanam||17||

18duryodhanaṃ kauravāṇāmādhipatya'bhyaṣecayam
|
yaccāhaṃ vāsudevasya nāśrauṣaṃ vākyamarthavat||18||

19vadhyatāṃ sādhvayaṃ pāpaḥ sāmātya iti durmatiḥ
|
putrasnehābhibhūtastu hitamukto manīṣibhiḥ||19||

20vidureṇātha bhīṣmeṇa droṇena ca kṛpeṇa ca
|
pade pade bhagavatā vyāsena ca mahātmanā||20||

21sañjayenātha gāndhāryā tadidaṃ tapyate ca mām
|
yaccāhaṃ pāṇḍuputreṣu guṇavatsu mahātmasu||21||

22na dattavān śriyaṃ dīptāṃ pitṛpaitāmahīmimām
|
vināśaṃ paśyamāno hi sarvarājñāṃ gadāgrajaḥ||22||

23etacchreyastu paramamamanyata janārdanaḥ
|
so'hametānyalīkāni nivṛttānyānmanastadā||23||

24hṛdaye śalyabhūtāni dhārayāmi sahasraśaḥ
|
viśeṣatastu paśyāmi varṣe pañcadaro'dya vai||24||

25asya pāpasya śuddhyarthaṃ niyato'smi sudurmatiḥ
|
caturthe niyate kāle kadācidapi cāṣṭame||25||

26tṛṣṇāvinayanaṃ bhuñje gāndhārī veda tanmama
|
karotyāhāramiti māṃ sarvaḥ parijanaḥ sadā||26||

27yudhiṣṭhirabhayādeti bhṛśaṃ tapyati pāṇḍavaḥ
|
bhūmau śaye japyaparo darbheṣvajinasaṃvṛtaḥ||27||

28niyamavyapadeśena gāndhārī ca yaśasvinī
|
hataṃ śataṃ tu putrāṇāṃ yayoryuddhe'palāyinām||28||

29nānutapyāmi taccāhaṃ kṣatradharmaṃ hi te viduḥ
|
ityuktvā dharmarājānamabhyabhāṣata kauravaḥ||29||

30bhadraṃ te yādavīmātarvacaścedaṃ nibodha me
|
sukhamasamyuṣitaḥ putra tvayā suparipālitaḥ||30||

31mahādānāni dattāni śrāddhāni ca punaḥ punaḥ
|
prakṛṣṭaṃ ca yayā putra puṇyaṃ cīrṇaṃ yathābalam||31||

32gāndhārī hataputreyaṃ dhairyeṇodīkṣate ca mām
|
draupadyā hyapakartārastava caiśvaryahāriṇaḥ||32||

33samatītā nṛśaṃsāste svadharmeṇa hatā yudhi
|
na teṣu pratikartavyaṃ paśyāmi kurunandana||33||

34sarve śastrabhṛtāṃ lokān gatāste'bhimukhaṃ hatāḥ
|
ātmanastu hitaṃ puṇyaṃ pratikartavyamadya vai||34||

35gāndhāryāścaiva rājendra tadanujñātumarhasi
|
tvaṃ tu śastrabhṛtāṃ śreṣṭhaḥ satataṃ dharmavatsalaḥ||35||

36rājā guruḥ prāṇabhṛtāṃ tasmādetadbravīmyaham
|
anujñātastvayā vīra saṃśrayeyaṃ vanānyaham||36||

37cīravalkalabhṛdrājan gāndhāryā sahito'nayā
|
tavāśiṣaḥ prayuñjāno bhaviṣyāmi vanecaraḥ||37||

38ucitaṃ naḥ kule tāta sarveṣāṃ bharatarṣabha
|
putreṣvaiśvaryamādhāya vayaso'nte vanaṃ nṛpa||38||

39tatrāhaṃ vāyubhakṣo vā nirāhāro'pi vā vasan
|
patnyā sahānayā vīra cariṣyāmi tapaḥ param||39||

40tvaṃ cāpi phalabhāktāta tapasaḥ pārthivo hyasi
|
phalabhājo hi rājānaḥ kalyāṇasyetarasya vā||40||

41yudhiṣṭhira uvāca|
na māṃ prīṇayate rājyaṃ tvayyevaṃ duḥkhite nṛpa
|
dhiṅnāmastu sudurbuddhiṃ rājyasaktaṃ pramādinam||41||

42yo'haṃ bhavantaṃ duḥkhārtamupavāsakṛśaṃ bhṛśam
|
jitāhāraṃ kṣitiśayaṃ na vinde bhrātṛbhiḥ saha||42||

43aho'smi vañcito mūḍho bhavatā gūḍhabuddhinā
|
viśvāsayitvā pūrvaṃ māṃ yadidaṃ duḥkhamaśnuthāḥ||43||

44kiṃ me rājyena bhogairvā kiṃ yajñaiḥ kiṃ sukhena vā
|
yasya me tvaṃ mahīpāla duḥkhānyetānyavāptavān||44||

45pīḍitaṃ cāpi jānāmi rājyamānmānameva ca
|
anena vacasā tubhyaṃ duḥkhitasya janeśvara||45||

46bhavānpitā bhavānmātā bhavānnaḥ paramo guruḥ
|
bhavatā viprahīṇā vai kva nu tiṣṭhāmahe vayam||46||

47auraso bhavataḥ putro yuyutsurnṛpasattama
|
astu rājā mahārāja yamanyaṃ manyate bhavān||47||

48ahaṃ vanaṃ gamiṣyāmi bhavān rājyaṃ praśāsatu
|
na māmayaśasā dagdhaṃ bhūyastvaṃ dagdhumarhasi||48||

49nāhaṃ rājā bhavān rājā bhavataḥ paravānaham
|
kathaṃ guruṃ tvāṃ dharmajñamanujñātumihotsahe||49||

50na manyurhadi naḥ kaścitsuyodhanakṛte'nagha
|
bhavitavyaṃ tathā taddhi vayaṃ cānye ca mohitāḥ||50||

51vayaṃ putrā hi bhavato yathā duryodhanādayaḥ
|
gāndhārī caiva kuntī ca nirviśeṣe mate mama||51||

52sa māṃ tvaṃ yadi rājendra parityajya gamiṣyasi
|
pṛṣṭhatastvanuyāsyāmi satyamātmānamālabhe||52||

53iyaṃ hi vasusampūrṇā mahī sāgaramekhalā
|
bhavatā viprahīṇasya na me prītikarī bhavet||53||

54bhavadīyamidaṃ sarvaṃ śirasā tvāṃ prasādaye
|
tvadadhīnāḥ sma rājendra vyetu te mānaso jvaraḥ||54||

55bhavitavyamanuprāpto manye tvaṃ vasudhādhipa
|
diṣṭyā śuśrūṣamāṇastvāṃ mokṣiṣye manaso jvaram||55||

56dhṛtarāṣṭra uvāca|
tāpasye me manastāta vartate kurunandana
|
ucitaṃ ca kule'smākamaraṇyagamanaṃ prabho||56||

57ciramasmyuṣitaḥ putra ciraṃ śuśrūṣitastvayā
|
vṛddhaṃ māmapyanujñātumarhasi tvaṃ narādhipa||57||

58vaiśampāyana uvāca|
ityuktvā dharmarājānaṃ vepamānaṃ kṛtāñjalim
|
uvāca vacanaṃ rājā dhṛtarāṣṭro'mbikāsutaḥ||58||

59sañjayaṃ ca mahātmānaṃ kṛpaṃ cāpi mahāratham
|
anunenumihecchāmi bhavadbhirvasudhādhipam||59||

60mlāyate me mano hīdaṃ mukhaṃ ca pariśuṣyati
|
vayasā ca prakṛṣṭena vāgvyāyāmena caiva ha||60||

61ityuktvā sa tu dharmātmā vṛddho rājā kurudvahaḥ
|
gāndhārī śiśriye dhīmān sahasaiva gatāsuvat||61||

62taṃ tu dṛṣṭvā samāsīnaṃ visaṃjñamiva kauravam
|
ārtiṃ rājāgamattīvrāṃ kaunteyaḥ paravīrahā||62||

63yudhiṣṭhira uvāca|
yasya nāgasahasreṇa śatasaṅkhyena vai balam
|
so'yaṃ nārīṃ vyapāśritya śete rājā gatāsuvat||63||

64āyasī pratimā yena bhīmasenasya sā purā
|
cūrṇīkṛtā balavatā so'balāmāśritaḥ striyam||64||

65dhigastu māmadharmajñaṃ dhigbuddhiṃ dhikca me śrutam
|
yatkṛte pṛthivīpālaḥ śete'yamatathocitaḥ||65||

66ahamapyupavatsyāmi yathaivāyaṃ gururmama
|
yadi rājā na bhuṅkte'yaṃ gāndhārī ca yaśasvinī||66||

67vaiśampāyana uvāca|
tato'sya pāṇinā rājan jalaśītena pāṇḍavaḥ
|
uro mukhaṃ ca śanakaiḥ paryamārjata dharmavit||67||

68tena ratnauṣadhimatā puṇyena ca sugandhinā
|
pāṇisparśana rājñaḥ sa rājā saṃjñāmavāpa ha||68||

69dhṛtarāṣṭra uvāca|
spṛśa māṃ pāṇinā bhūyaḥ pariṣvaja ca pāṇḍava
|
jīvāmīvātisaṃsparśāttava rājīvalocana||69||

70mūrdhānaṃ ca tavāghrātumicchāmi manujādhipa
|
pāṇibhyāṃ hi parispraṣṭuṃ prīṇanaṃ hi mahanmama||70||

71aṣṭamo hyadya kālo'yamāhārasya kṛtasya me
|
yenāhaṃ kuruśārdūla śaknomi na viceṣṭitum||71||

72vyāyāmaścāyamatyarthaṃ kṛtastvāmabhiyācatā
|
tato glānamanāstāta naṣṭasaṃjña ivābhavam||72||

73tavāmṛtarasaprakhyaṃ hastasparśamimaṃ prabho
|
labdhā sañjīvito'smīti manye kurukulodvaha||73||

74vaiśampāyana uvāca|
evamuktastu kaunteyaḥ pitrā jyeṣṭhena bhārata
|
pasparśa sarvagātreṣu sauhārdāttaṃ śanaistadā||74||

75upalabhya tataḥ prāṇāndhṛtarāṣṭro mahīpatiḥ
|
bāhubhyāṃ sampariṣvajya mūrdhnyājighrata pāṇḍavam||75||

76vidurādayaśca te sarve rurudurduḥkhitā bhṛśam
|
atiduḥkhāttu rājānaṃ nocuḥ kiñcana pāṇḍavam||76||

77gāndhārī tveva dharmajñā manasodvahatī bhṛśam
|
duḥkhānyadhārayādrājanmaivamityeva cābravīt||77||

78itarāstu striyaḥ sarvāḥ kuntyā saha suduḥkhitāḥ
|
netrairāgatavikledaiḥ parivārya sthitā'bhavan||78||

79athābravītpunarvākyaṃ dhṛtarāṣṭro yudhiṣṭhiram
|
anujānīhi māṃ rājastāpasye bharatarṣabha||79||

80glāyate me manastāta bhūyo bhūyaḥ prajalpataḥ
|
na māmataḥ paraṃ putra parikleṣṭumihārhasi||80||

81tasmiṃstu kauravendre taṃ tathā bruvati pāṇḍavam
|
sarveṣamiva yodhānāmārtanādo mahānabhūt||81||

82dṛṣṭvā kṛśaṃ vivarṇaṃ ca rājānamatathocitam
|
upavāsapariśrāntaṃ tvagasthiparivāraṇam||82||

83dharmaputraḥ svapitaraṃ pariṣvajya mahāprabhum
|
śokajaṃ bāṣpamutsṛjya punarvacanamabravīt||83||

84na kāmaye naraśreṣṭha jīvitaṃ pṛthivīṃ tathā
|
yathā tava priyaṃ rājaṃścikīrṣāmi parantapa||84||

85yadi cāhamanugrāhyo bhavato dayito'pi vā
|
kriyatāṃ tāvadāhārastato vetsyāmyahaṃ param||85||

86tato'bravīnmahātejā dhṛtarāṣṭro yudhiṣṭhiram
|
anujñātastvayā putra bhuñjīyāmiti kāmaye||86||

87iti bruvati rājendre dhṛtarāṣṭre yudhiṣṭhiram
|
ṛṣiḥ satyavatīputro vyāso'bhyetya vaco'bravīt||87||
