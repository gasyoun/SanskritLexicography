Mahābhārata 12.252 (Śāntiparvan)

1vyāsa uvāca |
dvandvāni mokṣajijñāsurarthadharmāvanuṣṭhitaḥ
|
vaktrā guṇavatā śiṣyaḥ śrāvyaḥ pūrvamidaṃ mahat ||1||

2ākāśaṃ māruto jyotirāpaḥ pṛthvī ca pañcamī
|
bhāvābhāvau ca kālaśca sarvabhūteṣu pañcasu ||2||

3antarātmakamākāśaṃ tanmayaṃ śrotramindriyam
|
tasya śabdaṃ guṇaṃ vidyānmūrtiśāstravidhānavit ||3||

4caraṇaṃ mārutātmeti prāṇāpānau ca tanmayau
|
sparśanaṃ cendriyaṃ vidyāttathā sparśaṃ ca tanmayam ||4||

5tāpaḥ pākaḥ prakāśaśca jyotiścakṣuśca pañcamam
|
tasya rūpaṃ guṇaṃ vidyāttāmragaurāsitātmakam ||5||

6prakledaḥ kṣudratā sneha ityapāmupadiśyate
|
asṛṅmajjā ca yaccānyatsnigdhaṃ vidyāttadātmakam ||6||

7rasanaṃ cendriyaṃ jihvā rasaścāpāṃ guṇo mataḥ
|
saṃghātaḥ pārthivo dhāturasthidantanakhāni ca ||7||

8śmaśru roma ca keśāśca śirā snāyu ca carma ca
|
indriyaṃ ghrāṇasaṃjñātaṃ nāsiketyabhisaṃjñitā ||8||

9gandhaścevendriyārtho'yaṃ vijñeyaḥ pṛthivīmayaḥ
|
uttareṣu guṇāḥ santi sarvasattveṣu cottarāḥ ||9||

10pañcānāṃ bhūtasaṃghānāṃ santatiṃ munayo viduḥ
|
mano navamameṣāṃ tu buddhistu daśamī smṛtā ||10||

11ekādaśastvanantātmā sa sarvaḥ para ucyate
|
vyavasāyātmikā buddhirmano vyākaraṇātmakam
|
karmānumānādvijñeyaḥ sa jīvaḥ kṣetrasaṃjñakaḥ ||11||

12ebhiḥ kālātmakairbhāvairyaḥ sarvaiḥ sarvamanvitam
|
paśyatyakaluṣaṃ karma sa mohaṃ nānuvartate ||12||
