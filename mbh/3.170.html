Mahābhārata 3.170 (Āraṇyakaparvan)

1arjuna uvāca|
tato nivātakavacāḥ sarve vegena bhārata
|
abhyadravan māṃ sahitāḥ pragṛhītāyudhā raṇe||1||

2ācchādya rathapanthānamutkrośanto mahārathāḥ
|
āvṛtya sarvatasta māṃ śaravarṣairavākiran||2||

3tato'pare mahāvīryāḥ śūlapaṭṭiśapāṇayaḥ
|
śūlāni ca bhuśuṇḍīśca mumucurdānavā mayi||3||

4tacchulavarṣaṃ sumahadgadāśaktisamākulam
|
aniśaṃ sṛjyamānaṃ tairapatanmadra‌thopari||4||

5anye māmabhyadhāvanta nivātakavacā yudhi
|
jitaśastrāyudhā raudrāḥ kālarūpāḥ prahāriṇaḥ||5||

6tānahaṃ vividhairbāṇaivaigavadbhirajihmagaiḥ
|
gāṇḍīvamuktairabhyaghnamekaikaṃ daśabhirmudhe||6||

7te kṛtā vimukhāḥ sarve matprayuktaiḥ śilāśitaiḥ
|
tato mātalinā tūrṇaṃ hayāste sampracoditāḥ||7||

8mārgān bahuvidhāṃstatra vicerurvātaraṃhasaḥ
|
susaṃyatā mātalinā prāmathnanta diteḥ sutān||8||

9śataṃ śatāste harayastasmin yuktā mahārathe
|
śāntā mātalinā yattā vyacarannalpakā iva||9||

10teṣāṃ caraṇapātena rathanemisvanena ca
|
mama bāṇanipātaiśca hatāste śataśo'surāḥ||10||

11gatāsavastathaivā'nye pragṛhītaśarāsanāḥ
|
hatasārathayastatra vyakṛṣyanta turaṃgamaiḥ||11||

12te diśo vidiśaḥ sarve pratiruthya prahāriṇaḥ
|
abhyaghnan vividhaiḥ śastrastato me vyathitaṃ manaḥ||12||

13tato'haṃ mātalervīryamapaśyaṃ paramādbhutam
|
aśvāṃstathā vegavato yadayatnā‌dadhārayat||13||

14tato'haṃ laghubhiścitrairastraistānasurān raṇe
|
ciccheda sāyudhān rājan śataśo'tha sahasraśaḥ||14||

15evaṃ me caratastatra sarvayatnena śatruhan
|
prītimānabhavadvīro mātaliḥ śakrasārathiḥ||15||

16vadhyamānāstatastaistu hayaistena rathena ca
|
agaman prakṣayaṃ kecinnyavartanta tathā pare||16||

17spardhamānā ivāsmābhirnivātakavacā raṇe
|
śaravarṣaiḥ śarārtaṃ māṃ mahadbhiḥ pratyavārayan||17||

18tato'haṃ laghubhiścitrairbrahmāstraparimantritaiḥ
|
vyadhamaṃ sāyakairāśu śataśo'tha sahasraśaḥ||18||

19tataḥ sampīḍyamānāste krodhāviṣṭā mahārathāḥ
|
apīḍayan māṃ sahitāḥ śaktiśūlāsivṛṣṭibhiḥ||19||

20tato'hamastramātiṣṭhaṃ paramaṃ tigmataijasam
|
dayitaṃ devarājasya mādhavaṃ nāma bhārata||20||

21tataḥ khaḍgatriśūlena tomarāṃśca sahasraśaḥ
|
astravīryeṇa śatadhā tairmuktānahamacchidam||21||

22chittvā praharaṇānyeṣāṃ tatastānapi sarvaśaḥ
|
pratyavidhyamahaṃ roṣāddaśabhirdaśabhiḥ śaraiḥ||22||

23gāṇḍīvāddhi tadā saṃkhye yathā bhramarapaṅktayaḥ
|
niṣpatanti mahābāṇāstanmātalirapūjayat||23||

24teṣāmapi tu bāṇāste tanmātalirapūjayat
|
avākiran māṃ balavattānahaṃ vyadhamaṃ śaraiḥ||24||

25vadhyamānāstataste tu nivātakavacāḥ punaḥ
|
śaravarṣairmahadbhirmāṃ samantāt paryavārayan||25||

26śaravegānnihatyāhamastrairastravighātibhiḥ
|
jvaladbhiḥ paramaiḥ śīghaistānavidhyaṃ sahasraśaḥ||26||

27teṣāṃ chinnāni gātrāṇi visṛjanti sma śoṇitam
|
prāvṛṣīvābhivṛṣṭāni śṛṅgāṇyatha dharābhṛtām||27||

28indrāśanisamasparśairvegavadbhirajihmagaiḥ
|
madbāṇairvadhyamānāste samudvignāḥ sma dānavāḥ||28||

29śatadhā bhinnadehāste kṣīṇapraharaṇaujasaḥ
|
tato nivātakavacā māmayudhyanta māyayā||29||
