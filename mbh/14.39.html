Mahābhārata 14.39 (Āśvamedhikaparvan)

1brahmovāca|
naiva śakyā guṇā vaktuṃ pṛthaktvenaiva sarvaśaḥ
|
avicchinnāni dṛśyante rajaḥ sattvaṃ tamastathā||1||

2anyonyamatha rajyante hyanyonyaṃ cārthajīvinaḥ
|
anyonyamāśrayāḥ sarve tathānyonyānuvartinaḥ||2||

3yāvatsattvaṃ rajastāvadvartate nātra saṃśayaḥ
|
yāvattamaśca sattvaṃ ca rajastāvadihocyate||3||

4saṃhatya kurvate yātrāṃ sahitāḥ saṅghacāriṇaḥ
|
saṅghātavṛttayo hyete vartante hetvahetubhiḥ||4||

5udrekavyatiriktānāṃ teṣāmanyonyavartinām
|
vakṣyate tadyathānyūnaṃ vyatiriktaṃ ca sarvaśaḥ||5||

6vyatiriktaṃ tamo yatra tiryagbhāvagataṃ bhavet
|
alpaṃ tatra rajo jñeyaṃ sattvamalpataraṃ tathā||6||

7udriktaṃ ca rajo yatra madhyasrotogataṃ bhavet
|
alpaṃ tatra tamo jñeyaṃ sattvamalpataraṃ tathā||7||

8udriktaṃ ca yadā sattvamūrdhvasrotogataṃ bhavet
|
alpaṃ tatra tamo jñeyaṃ rajaścālpataraṃ tathā||8||

9sattvaṃ vaikārikī yonirindriyāṇāṃ prakāśikā
|
na hi sattvātparo dharmaḥ kaścidanyo vidhīyate||9||

10ūrdhvaṃ gacchanti sattvasthā madhye tiṣṭhanti rājasāḥ
|
jaghanyaguṇasaṃyuktā yāntyadhastāmasā janāḥ||10||

11tamaḥ śūdre rajaḥ kṣatre brāhmaṇe sattvamuttamam
|
ityevaṃ triṣu varṇeṣu vivartante guṇāstrayaḥ||11||

12dūrādapi hi dṛśyante sahitāḥ saṅghacāriṇaḥ
|
tamaḥ sattvaṃ rajaścaiva pṛthaktve nānuśuśruma||12||

13dṛṣṭvā tvādityamudyantaṃ kucarāṇāṃ bhayaṃ bhavet
|
adhvagāḥ paritapyeyuruṣṇato duḥkhabhāginaḥ||13||

14ādityaḥ sattvamudriktaṃ kucarāstu tathā tamaḥ
|
paritāpo'dhvagānāṃ ca rajaso guṇa ucyate||14||

15prakāśyaṃ sattvamādityaḥ santāpo rajaso guṇaḥ
|
upaplavastu vijñeyastāmasastasya parvasu||15||

16evaṃ jyotiṣṣu sarveṣu nivartante guṇāstrayaḥ
|
paryāyeṇa ca vartante tatra tatra tathā tathā||16||

17sthāvareṣu tu bhāveṣu tiryagbhāvagataṃ tamaḥ
|
rājasāstu vivartante snehabhāvastu sāttvikaḥ||17||

18ahastridhā tu vijñeyaṃ tridhā rātrirvidhīyate
|
māsārdhamāsavarṣāṇi ṛtavaḥ sandhayastathā||18||

19tridhā dānāni dīyante tridhā yajñaḥ pravartate
|
tridhā lokāstridhā devāstridhā vidyāstridhā gatiḥ||19||

20bhūtaṃ bhavyaṃ bhaviṣyaṃ ca dharmo'rthaḥ kāma eva ca
|
prāṇāpānāvudāścāpyeta eva trayo guṇāḥ||20||

21paryāyeṇa pravartante tatra tatra tathā tathā
|
yatkiñcidiha loke'smin sarvamete trayo guṇāḥ||21||

22trayo guṇāḥ pravartante hyavyaktā nityameva tu
|
sattvaṃ rajastamaścaiva guṇasargaḥ sanātanaḥ||22||

23tamo vyaktaṃ śivaṃ dhāma rajo yoniḥ sanātanaḥ
|
prakṛtirvikāraḥ prāyaḥ pradhānaṃ prabhavāpyayau||23||

24anudriktamanūnaṃ vāpyakampamacalaṃ dhruvam
|
sadasaccaiva tatsarvamavyaktaṃ triguṇaṃ smṛtam
|
jñeyāni nāmadheyāni narairadhyātmacintakaiḥ||24||

25avyaktanāmāni guṇāṃśca tattvato yo veda sarvāṇi gatīśca kevalāḥ
|
vimuktadehaḥ pravibhāgatattvavit sa mucyate sarvaguṇairnirāmayaḥ||25||
