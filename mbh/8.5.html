Mahābhārata 8.5 (Karṇaparvan)

1vaiśampāyana uvāca|
iti śrutvā mahārāja dhṛtarāṣṭro'mbikāsutaḥ
|
abravītsañjayaṃ sūtaṃ śokasaṃvignamānasaḥ ||1||

2duṣpraṇītena me tāta putrasyādīrghajīvinaḥ
|
hataṃ vaikartanaṃ śrutvā śoko marmāṇi kṛntati ||2||

3tasya me saṃśayaṃ chindhi duḥkhapāraṃ titīrṣataḥ
|
kurūṇāṃ sṛñjayānāṃ ca ke ca jīvanti ke mṛtāḥ ||3||

4sañjaya uvāca|
hataḥ śāntanavo rājan durādharṣaḥ pratāpavān
|
hatvā pāṇḍavayodhānāmarbudaṃ daśabhirdinaiḥ ||4||

5tathā droṇo maheṣvāsaḥ pañcālānāṃ rathavrajān
|
nihatya yudhi durdharṣaḥ paścādrukmaratho hataḥ ||5||

6hataśeṣasya bhīṣmeṇa droṇena ca mahātmanā
|
ardhaṃ nihatya sainyasya karṇo vaikartano hataḥ ||6||

7viviṃśatirmahārāja rājaputro mahābalaḥ
|
ānartayodhāñśataśo nihatya nihato raṇe ||7||

8tathā putro vikarṇaste kṣatravratamanusmaran
|
kṣīṇavāhāyudhaḥ śūraḥ sthito'bhimukhataḥ parān ||8||

9ghorarūpān parikleśān duryodhanakṛtānbahūn
|
pratijñāṃ smaratā caiva bhīmasenena pātitaḥ ||9||

10vindānuvindāvāvantyau rājaputrau mahārathau
|
kṛtvā tvasukaraṃ karma gatau vaivasvatakṣayam ||10||

11sindhurāṣṭrasukhānīha daśa rāṣṭrāṇi yāniha
|
vaśe tiṣṭhanti vīrasya yaḥ sthitastava śāsane ||11||

12akṣauhiṇīrdaśaikāṃ ca vinirjitya śitaiḥ śaraiḥ
|
arjunena hato rājanmahāvīryo jayadrathaḥ ||12||

13tathā duryodhanasutastarasvī yuddhadurmadaḥ
|
vartamānaḥ pituḥ śāstre saubhadreṇa nipātitaḥ ||13||

14tathā dauḥśāsaniḥ śūro bāhuśālī raṇotkaṭaḥ
|
draupadeyena saṅgamya gamito yamasādanam ||14||

15kirātānāmadhipatiḥ sāgarānūpavāsinām
|
devarājasya dharmātmā priyo bahumataḥ sakhā ||15||

16bhagadatto mahīpālaḥ kṣatradharmarataḥ sadā
|
dhanañjayena vikramya gamito yamasādanam ||16||

17tathā kauravadāyādo nyastaśastro mahāyaśāḥ
|
hato bhūriśravā rājañśūraḥ sātyakinā yudhi ||17||

18śrutāyurapi cāmbaṣṭhaḥ kṣatriyāṇāṃ dhurandharaḥ
|
carannabhītavatsaṃkhye nihataḥ savyasācinā ||18||

19tava putraḥ sadāmarṣī kṛtāstro yuddhadurmadaḥ
|
duḥśāsano mahārāja bhīmasenena pātitaḥ ||19||

20yasya rājan gajānīkaṃ bahusāhastramadbhutam
|
sudakṣiṇaḥ sa saṃgrāme nihataḥ savyasācinā ||20||

21kosalānāmadhipatirhatvā bahumatānparān
|
saubhadreṇeha vikramya gamito yamasādanam ||21||

22bahuśo yodhayitvā tu bhīmasenaṃ mahāratham
|
citrasenastava suto bhīmasenena pātitaḥ||22||

23madrarājātmajaḥ śūraḥ pareṣāṃ bhayavardhanaḥ
|
asi-carmadharaḥ śrīmānsaubhadreṇa nipātitaḥ ||23||

24samaḥ karṇasya samare yaḥ sa karṇasya paśyataḥ
|
vṛṣaseno mahātejāḥ śīghrāstro dṛḍhavikramaḥ ||24||

25abhimanyorvadhaṃ śrutvā pratijñāmapi cātmanaḥ
|
dhanañjayena vikramya gamito yamasādanam ||25||

26nityaṃ prasaktavairo yaḥ pāṇḍavaiḥ pṛthivīpatiḥ
|
viśrāvya vairaṃ pārthena śrutāyuḥ sa nipātitaḥ ||26||

27śalyaputrastu vikrāntaḥ sahadevena māriṣa
|
hato rukmaratho rājan bhrātā mātulajo yudhi ||27||

28rājā bhagīratho vṛddho bṛhatkṣatraśca kekayaḥ
|
parākramantau vikrāntau nihatau vīryavattarau ||28||

29bhagadattasuto rājan kṛtaprajño mahābalaḥ
|
śyenavaccaratā saṃkhye nakulena nipātitaḥ ||29||

30pitāmahastava tathā bāhlīkaḥ saha bāhlīkaiḥ
|
nihato bhīmasenena mahābalaparākramaḥ ||30||

31jayatsenastathā rājañjārāsandhirmahābalaḥ
|
māgadho nihataḥ saṃkhye saubhadreṇa mahātmanā ||31||

32putraste durmukho rājan duḥsahaśca mahārathaḥ
|
gadayā bhīmasenena nihatau śūramāninau ||32||

33durmarṣaṇo durviṣaho durjayaśca mahārathaḥ
|
kṛtvā tvasukaraṃ karma gatā vaivasvatakṣayam ||33||

34ubhau kaliṅgavṛṣakau bhrātarau yuddhadurmadau
|
kṛtvā cāsukaraṃ karma gatau vaivasvatakṣayam ||34||

35sacivo vṛṣavarmā te śūraḥ paramavīryavān
|
bhīmasenena vikramya gamito yamasādanam ||35||

36tathaiva pauravo rājā nāgāyutabalo mahān
|
samare pāṇḍuputreṇa nihataḥ savyasācinā ||36||

37vasātayo mahārāja dvisāhasrāḥ prahāriṇaḥ
|
śūrasenāśca vikrāntāḥ sarve yudhi nipātitāḥ ||37||

38abhīṣāhāḥ kavacinaḥ praharanto raṇotkaṭāḥ
|
śibayaśca rathodārāḥ kāliṅgasahitā hatāḥ||38||

39gokule nityasaṃvṛddhā yuddhe paramakopanāḥ
|
te'pāvṛttakavīrāśca nihatāḥ savyasācinā ||39||

40śreṇayo bahusāhasrāḥ saṃśaptakagaṇāśca ye
|
te sarve pārthamāsādya gatā vaivasvatakṣayam ||40||

41syālau tava mahārāja rājānau vṛṣakācalau
|
tvadarthamativikrāntau nihatau savyasācinā ||41||

42ugrakarmā maheṣvāso nāmataḥ karmatastathā
|
śālvarājo mahābāhurbhīmasenena pātitaḥ ||42||

43oghavāṃśca mahārāja bṛhantaḥ sahitau raṇe
|
parākramantau mitrārthe gatau vaivasvatakṣayam ||43||

44tathaiva rathināṃ śreṣṭhaḥ kṣemadhūrtirviśāṃpate
|
nihato gadayā rājan bhīmasenena saṃyuge ||44||

45tathā rājanmaheṣvāso jalasandho mahābalaḥ
|
sumahatkadanaṃ kṛtvā hataḥ sātyakinā raṇe ||45||

46alambuṣo rākṣasendraḥ kharabandhurayānavān
|
ghaṭotkacena vikramya gamito yamasādanam ||46||

47rādheyaḥ sūtaputraśca bhrātaraśca mahārathāḥ
|
kekayāḥ sarvaśaścāpi nihatāḥ savyasācinā ||47||

48mālavā madrakāścaiva drāviḍāścograkarmiṇaḥ
|
yaudheyāśca lalitthāśca kṣudrakāścāpyuśīnarāḥ ||48||

49māvellakāstuṇḍikerāḥ sāvitrīputrakāśca ye
|
prācyodīcyāḥ pratīcyāśca dākṣiṇātyāśca māriṣa ||49||

50pattīnāṃ nihatāḥ saṅghā hayānāṃ prayutāni ca
|
rathavrajāśca nihatā hatāśca varavāraṇāḥ ||50||

51sadhvajāḥ sāyudhāḥ śūrāḥ savarmāmbarabhūṣaṇāḥ
|
kālena mahatā''yastāḥ kuśalairye ca vardhitāḥ ||51||

52te hatāḥ samare rājan pārthenākliṣṭakarmaṇā
|
anye tathā'mitabalāḥ parasparavadhaiṣiṇaḥ ||52||

53ete cānye ca bahavo rājānaḥ sagaṇā raṇe
|
hatāḥ sahasraśo rājanyanmāṃ tvaṃ paripṛcchasi ||53||

54evameṣa kṣayo vṛttaḥ karṇārjunasamāgame
|
mahendreṇa yathā vṛtro yathā rāmeṇa rāvaṇaḥ ||54||

55yathā kṛṣṇena narako muruśca narakāriṇā
|
kārtavīryaśca rāmeṇa bhārgaveṇa yathā hataḥ ||55||

56sajñātibāndhavaḥ śūraḥ samare yuddhadurmadaḥ
|
raṇe kṛtvā mahadyuddhaṃ ghoraṃ trailokyamohanam ||56||

57yathā skandena mahiṣo yathā rudreṇa cāndhakaḥ
|
tathā'rjunena sa hato dvairathe yuddhadurmadaḥ ||57||

58sāmātyabāndhavo rājan karṇaḥ praharatāṃ varaḥ
|
jayāśā dhārtarāṣṭrāṇāṃ vairasya ca mukhaṃ yataḥ ||58||

59tīrṇastatpāṇḍavo rājan yatpurā nāvabudhyase
|
ucyamāno mahārāja bandhubhirhitakāṅgibhiḥ ||59||

60tadidaṃ samanuprāptaṃ vyasanaṃ sumahātyayam
|
putrāṇāṃ rājyakāmānāṃ tvayā rājan hitaiṣiṇā
|
ahitānyeva cīrṇāni teṣāṃ tatphalamāgatam ||60||
