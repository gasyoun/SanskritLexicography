Mahābhārata 13.63 (Anuśāsanaparvan)

1yudhiṣṭhira uvāca|
kāni dānāni loke'smindātukāmo mahīpatiḥ
|
guṇādhikebhyo viprebhyo dadyādbharatasattama ||1||

2kena tuṣyanti te sadyaḥ kiṃ tuṣṭāḥ pradiśanti ca
|
śaṃsa me tanmahābāho phalaṃ puṇyakṛtaṃ mahat ||2||

3dattaṃ ki phalavadrājanniha loke paratra ca
|
bhavataḥ śrotumicchāmi tanme vistarato vada ||3||

4bhīṣma uvāca|
imamarthaṃ purā pṛṣṭo nārado devadarśanaḥ
|
yaduktavānasau vākyaṃ tanme nigadataḥ śṛṇu ||4||

5nārada uvāca|
annameva praśaṃsanti devā ṛṣigaṇāstathā
|
lokatantraṃ hi saṃjñāśca sarvamanne pratiṣṭhitam ||5||

6annena sadṛśaṃ dānaṃ na bhūtaṃ na bhaviṣyati
|
tasmādannaṃ viśeṣeṇa dātumicchanti mānavāḥ ||6||

7annamūrjaskaraṃ loke prāṇāścānne pratiṣṭhitāḥ
|
annena dhāryate sarvaṃ viśvaṃ jagadidaṃ prabho ||7||

8annādgṛhasthā loke'smin bhikṣavastāpasāstathā
|
annādbhavanti vai prāṇāḥ pratyakṣaṃ nātra saṃśayaḥ ||8||

9kuṭumbine sīdate ca brāhmaṇāya mahātmane
|
dātavyaṃ bhikṣaṣe cānnamātmano bhūtimicchatā ||9||

10brāhmaṇāyābhirūpāya yo dadyādannamarthine
|
vidadhāti nidhiṃ śreṣṭhaṃ pāralaukikamātmanaḥ ||10||

11śrāntamadhvani vartantaṃ vṛddhamarhamupasthitam
|
arcayedbhūtimanvicchan gṛhastho gṛhamāgatam ||11||

12krodhamutpatitaṃ hitvā suśīlo vītamatsaraḥ
|
annadaḥ prāpnute rājan diviḥ ceha ca yatsukham ||12||

13nāvamanyedabhigataṃ na praṇudyātkadācana
|
api śvapāke śuni vā na dānaṃ vipraṇaśyati ||13||

14yo dadyādaparikliṣṭamannamadhvani vartate
|
ārtāyādṛṣṭapūrvāya sa mahaddharmamāpnuyāt ||14||

15pitṝndevānṛṣīnviprānatithīṃśca janādhipa
|
yo naraḥ prīṇayatyanaistasya puṇyaphalaṃ mahat ||15||

16kṛtvā'tipātakaṃ karma yo dadyādannamarthine
|
brāhmaṇāya viśeṣeṇa na sa pāpena muhyate ||16||

17brāhmaṇeṣvakṣayaṃ dānamannaṃ śudre mahāphalam
|
annadānaṃ hi śūdre ca brāhmaṇe ca viśiṣyate ||17||

18na pṛcchedgotracaraṇaṃ svādhyāyaṃ deśameva ca
|
bhikṣito brāhmaṇeneha dadyādannaṃ prayācitaḥ ||18||

19annadasyānnavṛkṣāśca sarvakāmaphalapradāḥ
|
bhavanti ceha cāmutra nṛpaternātra saṃśayaḥ ||19||

20āśaṃsante hi pitaraḥ suvṛṣṭimiva karṣakāḥ
|
asmākamapi putro vā pautro vā'nna pradāsyati ||20||

21brāhmaṇo hi mahadbhūtaṃ svayaṃ dehīti yācati
|
akāmo vā sakāmo vā dattvā puṇyamavāpnuyāt ||21||

22brāhmaṇaḥ sarvabhūtānāmatithiḥ prasṛtāgrabhuk
|
viprā yadadhigacchanti bhikṣamāṇā gṛhaṃ sadā ||22||

23satkṛtāśca nivartante tadatīva pravardhate
|
mahābhāge kule pretya janma cāpnoti bhārata ||23||

24dattvā tvannaṃ naro loke tathā sthānamanuttamam
|
nityaṃ miṣṭānnadāyī tu svarge vasati satkṛtaḥ ||24||

25annaṃ prāṇā narāṇāṃ hi sarvamanne pratiṣṭhitam
|
annadaḥ paśumānputrī dhanavān bhogavānapi ||25||

26prāṇavāṃścāpi bhavati rūpavāṃśca tathā nṛpa
|
annadaḥ prāṇado loke sarvadaḥ procyate tu saḥ ||26||

27annaṃ hi datvā'tithaye brāhmaṇāya yathāvidhi
|
pradātā sukhamāmoti daivataiśvāpi pūjyate ||27||

28brāhmaṇo hi mahadbhūtaṃ kṣetrabhūtaṃ yudhiṣṭhira
|
upyate tatra yadbījaṃ taddhi puṇyaphalaṃ mahat ||28||

29pratyakṣaṃ prītijananaṃ bhokturdāturbhavatyuta
|
sarvāṇyanyāni dānāni parokṣaphalavantyuta ||29||

30annāddhi prasavaṃ yānti ratirannāddhi bhārata
|
dharmārthāvannato viddhi roganāśaṃ tathā'nnataḥ ||30||

31annaṃ hyamṛtamityāha purākalpe prajāpatiḥ
|
annaṃ bhuvaṃ divaṃ khaṃ ca sarvamanne pratiṣṭhitam ||31||

32annapraṇāśe bhidyante śarīre pañca dhātavaḥ
|
balaṃ balavato'pīha praṇaśyatyannahānitaḥ ||32||

33āvāhāśca vivāhāśca yajñāścānnamṛte tathā
|
nivartante naraśreṣṭha brahma cātra pralīyate ||33||

34annataḥ sarvametaddhi yatkiñcitsthāṇujaṃgamam
|
triṣu lokeṣu dharmārthamannaṃ deyamato budhaiḥ ||34||

35annadasya manuṣyasya balamojo yaśāṃsi ca
|
kīrtiśca vardhate śaśvattriṣu lokeṣu pārthiva ||35||

36megheṣūrdhvaṃ sannidhatte prāṇānāṃ pavanaḥ patiḥ
|
tacca meghagataṃ vāri śakro varṣati bhārata ||36||

37ādatte ca rasānbhaumānādityaḥ svagabhastibhiḥ
|
vāyurādityatastāṃśca rasāndevaḥ pravarṣati ||37||

38tadyadā meghato vāri patitaṃ bhavati kṣitau
|
tadā vasumatī devī snigdhā bhavati bhārata ||38||

39tataḥ sasyāni rohanti yena vartayate jagat
|
māṃsamedosthiśukrāṇāṃ prādurbhāvastataḥ punaḥ ||39||

40saṃbhavanti tataḥ śukrātprāṇinaḥ pṛthivīpate
|
agnīṣomau hi tacchukraṃ sṛjataḥ puṣpataśca ha ||40||

41evamannāddhi sūryaśca pavanaḥ śukrameva ca
|
eka eva smṛto rāśistato bhūtāni jajñire ||41||

42prāṇāndadāti bhūtānāṃ tejaśca bharatarṣabha
|
gṛhamabhyāgatāyātha yo dadyādannamarthine ||42||

43bhīṣma uvāca|
nāradenaivamukto'hamadāmannaṃ sadā nṛpa
|
anasūyustvamapyannaṃ tasmāddehi gatajvaraḥ ||43||

44datvā'nnaṃ vidhivadrājanviprebhyastvamiti prabhā
|
yathāvada‌nurūpebhyastataḥ svargamavāpsyasi ||44||

45annadānāṃ hi ye lokāstāṃstvaṃ śṛṇu janādhipa
|
bhavanāni prakāśante divi teṣāṃ mahātmanām ||45||

46tārāsaṃsthāni rūpāṇi nānāstaṃbhānvitāni ca
|
candramaṇḍalaśubhrāṇi kiṃkiṇījālavanti ca ||46||

47taruṇādityavarṇāni sthāvarāṇi carāṇi ca
|
anekaśatabhaumāni sāntarjalacarāṇi ca ||47||

48vaidūryārkaprakāśāni raupyarukmamayāni ca
|
sarvakāmaphalāścāpi vṛkṣā bhavanasaṃsthitāḥ ||48||

49vāpyo vīthyaḥ sabhāḥ kūpā dīrghikāścaiva sarvaśaḥ
|
ghoṣavanti ca yānāni yuktānyatha sahasraśaḥ ||49||

50bhakṣyabhojyamayāḥ śailā vāsāṃsyābharaṇāni ca
|
kṣīraṃ sravanti saritastathā caivānnaparvatāḥ ||50||

51prāsādāḥ pāṇḍurābhrābhāḥ śayyāśca kāṃcanojjvalāḥ
|
tānyannadāḥ prapadyante tasmādannaprado bhava ||51||

52ete lokāḥ puṇyakṛtā annadānāṃ mahātmanām
|
tasmādannaṃ prayatnena dātavyaṃ mānavairbhuvi ||52||
