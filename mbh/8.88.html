Mahābhārata 8.88 (Karṇaparvan)

1sañjaya uvāca|
taddevanāgāsurasiddhayakṣairgandharvarakṣopsarasāṃ ca saṅghaiḥ
|
brahmarṣirājarṣisuparṇajuṣṭaṃ babhau viyadvismayanīyarūpam ||1||

2nānadyamānaṃ ninadairmanojñairvāditra-gīta-stuti-nṛtya-hāsaiḥ
|
sarve'ntarikṣaṃ dadṛśurmanuṣyāḥ khasthāśca tadvismayanīyarūpam ||2||

3tataḥ prahṛṣṭāḥ kurupāṇḍuyodhā vāditraśaṅkhasvanasiṃhanādaiḥ
|
vinādayanto vasudhāṃ diśaśca svanena sarvān dviṣato nijaghnuḥ ||3||

4narāśvamātaṅgarathaiḥ samākulaṃ śarāsiśaktyṛṣṭinipātaduḥsaham
|
abhīrujuṣṭaṃ hatadehasaṃkulaṃ raṇājiraṃ lohitamābabhau tadā ||4||

5babhūva yuddhaṃ kurupāṇḍavānāṃ yathā surāṇāmasuraiḥ sahābhavat
|
tathā pravṛtte tumule sudāruṇe dhanañjayasyādhiratheśca sāyakaiḥ ||5||

6diśaśca sainyaṃ ca śitairajihmagaiḥ parasparaṃ prāvṛṇutāṃ sudaṃśitaiḥ
|
tatastvadīyāśca pare ca sāyakaiḥ kṛte'ndhakāre dadṛśurna kiñcana ||6||

7bhayāturā ekarathau samāśrayaṃstato'bhavattvadbhutameva sarvataḥ
|
tato'stramastreṇa parasparaṃ tau vidhūya vātāviva pūrvapaścimau ||7||

8ghanāndhakāre vitate tamonudau yathoditau tadvadatīva rejatuḥ
|
na cābhisartavyamiti pracoditāḥ pare tvadīyāśca tathā'vatasthire ||8||

9mahārathau tau parivārya sarvataḥ surāsurāḥ śambaravāsavāviva
|
mṛdaṅga-bherī-paṇavānakasvanaiḥ sasiṃhanādairnadaturnarottamau ||9||

10śaśāṅkasūryāviva meghaniḥsvanairvirejatustau puruṣarṣabhau tadā
|
mahādhanurmaṇḍalamadhyagāvubhau suvarcasau bāṇasahasradīdhitī ||10||

11didhakṣamāṇau sacarācaraṃ jagadyugāntasūryāviva duḥsahau raṇe
|
ubhāvajeyāvahitāntakāvubhāvubhau jighāṃsū kṛtinau parasparam ||11||

12mahāhave vītabhayau samīyaturmahendrajambhāviva karṇapāṇḍavau
|
tato mahāstrāṇi mahādhanurdharau vimuñcamānāviṣubhirbhayānakaiḥ ||12||

13narāśvanāgānamitānnijaghnatuḥ parasparaṃ cāpi mahārathau nṛpa
|
tato visasruḥ punararditā narā narottamābhyāṃ kurupāṇḍavāśrayāḥ ||13||

14sanāgapattyaśvarathā diśo daśa tathā yathā siṃhahatā vanaukasaḥ
|
tatastu duryodhanabhojasaubalāḥ kṛpeṇa śāradvatasūnunā saha ||14||

15mahārathāḥ pañca dhanañjayācyutau śaraiḥ śarīrārtikarairatāḍayan
|
dhanūṃṣi teṣāmiṣudhīn dhvajānhayān rathāṃśca sūtāṃśca dhanañjayaḥ śaraiḥ ||15||

16samaṃ pramathyāśu parānsamantataḥ śarottamairdvādaśabhiśca sūtajam
|
athābhyadhāvaṃstvaritāḥ śataṃ rathāḥ śataṃ gajāścārjunamātatāyinaḥ ||16||

17śakāstuṣārā yavanāśca sādinaḥ sahaiva kāmbojavarairjighāṃsavaḥ
|
varāyudhānpāṇigataiḥ śaraiḥ saha kṣurairnyakṛntatprapatan śirāṃsi ca ||17||

18hayāṃśca nāgāṃśca rathāṃśca yudhyato dhanañjayaḥ śatrugaṇān kṣitau kṣiṇot
|
tato'ntarikṣe suratūryaniḥsvanāḥ sasādhuvādā hṛṣitaiḥ samīritāḥ ||18||

19nipeturapyuttamapuṣpavṛṣṭayaḥ sugandhigandhāḥ pavaneritāḥ śubhāḥ
|
tada‌dbhutaṃ devamanuṣyasākṣikaṃ samīkṣya bhūtāni visismiyustadā ||19||

20tavātmajaḥ sūtasutaśca na vyathāṃ na vismayaṃ jagmaturekaniścayau
|
athābravīddroṇasutastavātmajaṃ karaṃ kareṇa pratipīḍya sāntvayan ||20||

21prasīda duryodhana śāmya pāṇḍavairalaṃ virodhena dhigastu vigraham
|
hato gururbrahmasamo mahāstravittathaiva bhīṣmapramukhā mahārathāḥ ||21||

22ahaṃ tvavadhyo mama cāpi mātulaḥ praśādhi rājyaṃ saha pāṇḍavaiściram
|
dhanañjayaḥ śāmyati vārito mayā janārdano naiva virodhamicchati ||22||

23yudhiṣṭhiro bhūtahite rataḥ sadā vṛkodarastadvaśagastathā yamau
|
tvayā tu pārthaiśca kṛte ca saṃvide prajāḥ śivaṃ prāpnuyuricchayā tava ||23||

24vrajantu śeṣāḥ svapurāṇi bāndhavā nivṛttayuddhāśca bhavantu sainikāḥ
|
na cedvacaḥ śroṣyasi me narādhipa dhruvaṃ prataptāsi hato'ribhiryudhi ||24||

25idaṃ ca dṛṣṭaṃ jagatā saha tvayā kṛtaṃ yadekena kirīṭamālinā
|
yathā na kuryādbalabhinna cāntako na cāpi dhātā bhagavānna yakṣarāṭ ||25||

26ato'pi bhūyān svaguṇairdhanañjayo na cātivartiṣyati me vaco'khilam
|
tavānuyātrāṃ ca sadā kariṣyati prasīda rājendra śamaṃ tvamāpnuhi ||26||

27mamāpi mānaḥ paramaḥ sadā tvayi bravīmyatastvāṃ paramācca sauhṛdāt
|
nivārayiṣyāmi ca karṇamapyahaṃ yadā bhavānsapraṇayo bhaviṣyati ||27||

28vadanti mitraṃ sahajaṃ vicakṣaṇāstathaiva sāmnā ca dhanena cārjitam
|
pratāpataścopanataṃ caturvidhaṃ tadasti sarvaṃ tava pāṇḍaveṣu ||28||

29nisargataste tava vīra bāndhavāḥ punaśca sāmnā samavāpnuhi prabho
|
tvayi prasanne yadi mitratāṃ gate hitaṃ kṛtaṃ syājjagatastvayā'tulam ||29||

30sa evamuktaḥ suhṛdā vaco hitaṃ vicintya niḥśvasya ca durmanā'bravīt
|
yathā bhavānāha sakhe tathaivatanmamāpi vijñāpayato vacaḥ śṛṇu ||30||

31nihatya duḥśāsanamuktavānvacaḥ prasahya śārdūlavadeṣa durmatiḥ
|
vṛkodarastaddhṛdaye mama sthitaṃ na tatparokṣaṃ bhavataḥ kutaḥ śamaḥ ||31||

32na cāpi karṇaṃ prasahedraṇe'rjuno mahāgiriṃ merumivogramārutaḥ
|
na cāśvasiṣyanti pṛthātmajā mayi prasahya vairaṃ bahuśo vicintya ||32||

33na cāpi karṇaṃ guruputrasaṃyugādupārametyarhasi vaktumacyuta
|
śrameṇa yukto mahatā'dya phālgunastameṣa karṇaḥ prasabhaṃ haniṣyati ||33||

34tamevamuktvā'pyanunīya cāsakṛttavātmajaḥ svānanuśāsti sainikān
|
samāhitābhidravatāhitānmama sabāṇahastāḥ kimu joṣamāsata ||34||
