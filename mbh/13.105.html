Mahābhārata 13.105 (Anuśāsanaparvan)

1yudhiṣṭhira uvāca|
yathā jyeṣṭhaḥ kaniṣṭheṣu varteta bharatarṣabha
|
kaniṣṭhāśca yathā jyeṣṭhe varteraṃstadbravīhi me ||1||

2bhīṣma uvāca|
jyeṣṭhavattāta vartasva jyeṣṭho'si satataṃ bhavān
|
gurorgarīyasī vṛttiryā ca śiṣyasya bhārata ||2||

3na gurāvakṛtaprajñe śakyaṃ śiṣyeṇa vartitum
|
gurorhi dīrghadarśitvaṃ yattacchiṣyasya bhārata ||3||

4andhaḥ syādandhavelāyāṃ jaḍaḥsyādapi vā budhaḥ
|
parihāreṇa tadbrūyādyasteṣāṃ syādvyatikramaḥ ||4||

5pratyakṣaṃ bhinnahṛdayā bhedayeyuḥ kṛtaṃ narāḥ
|
śriyā'bhitaptāḥ kaunteya bhedakāmāstathārayaḥ ||5||

6jyeṣṭhaḥ kulaṃ vardhayati vināśayati vā punaḥ
|
hanti sarvamapi jyeṣṭhaḥ kulaṃ yatrāvajāyate ||6||

7atha yo vinikurvīta jyeṣṭho bhrātā yavīyasaḥ
|
ajyeṣṭhaḥ syādabhāgaśca niyamyo rājabhiśca saḥ ||7||

8nikṛtī hi naro lokānpāpāngacchatyasaṃśayam
|
vidulasyeva tatpuṣpaṃ moghaṃ janayituḥ smṛtam ||8||

9sarvānarthaḥ kule yatra jāyate pāpapūruṣaḥ
|
akīrtiṃ janayatyeva kīrtimantardadhāti ca ||9||

10sarve cāpi vikarmasthā bhāgaṃ nārhanti sodarāḥ
|
nāpradāya kaniṣṭhebhyo jyeṣṭhaḥ kurvīta yautakam ||10||

11anupaghnanpiturdāyaṃ jaṃghāśramaphalo'dhvagaḥ
|
svayamīhitalabdhaṃ tu nākāmo dātumarhati ||11||

12bhrātṝṇāmavibhaktānāmutthānamapi cetsaha
|
na putrabhāgaṃ viṣamaṃ pitā dadyātkadācana ||12||

13na jyeṣṭho vā'vamanyeta duṣkṛtaḥ sukṛtopi vā
|
yadi strī yadyavarajaḥ śreyaścettattadācaret ||13||

14dharmaṃ hi śreya ityāhuriti dharmavido janāḥ
|
daśācāryānupādhyāya upādhyāyānpitā daśa ||14||

15daśa caiva pitṝnmātā sarvāṃ vā pṛthivīmapi
|
gauraveṇābhibhavati nāsti mātṛsamo guruḥ ||15||

16mātā garīyasī yacca tenaitāṃ manyate janaḥ
|
jyeṣṭho bhrātā pitṛsamo mṛte pitari bhārata ||16||

17sa hyeṣāṃ vṛttidātā syātsa caitānpratipālayet
|
kaniṣṭhāstaṃ namasyeransarve chandānuvartinaḥ ||17||

18tameva copajīveran yathaiva pitaraṃ tathā
|
śarīrametau sṛjataḥ pitā mātā ca bhārata ||18||

19ācāryaśāstā yā jātiḥ sā satyā sā'jarā'marā
|
jyeṣṭhā mātṛsamā cāpi bhaginī bharatarṣabha ||19||

20bhrāturbhāryā ca tadvatsyādyasyā bālye stanaṃ pibet ||20||
