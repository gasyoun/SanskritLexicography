Mahābhārata 6.85 (Bhīṣmaparvan)

1sañjaya uvāca|
sa tāḍyamānastu śarairdhanañjayaḥ padā hato nāga iva śvasan balī
|
bāṇena bāṇena mahārathānāṃ ciccheda cāpāni raṇe prasahya ||1||

2sañchidya cāpāni ca tāni rājñāṃ teṣāṃ raṇe vīryavatāṃ kṣaṇena
|
vivyādha bāṇairyugapanmahātmā niḥśeṣatāṃ teṣvatha manyamānaḥ ||2||

3nipeturājau rudhirapradigdhāste tāḍitāḥ śakrasutena rājan
|
vibhinnagātrāḥ patitottamāṅgā gatāsavaśchinnatanutrakāyāḥ ||3||

4mahīṃ gatāḥ pārthabalābhibhūtā vicitrarūpā yugapadvineśuḥ
|
dṛṣṭvā hatāṃstān yudhi rājaputrāṃstrigartarājaḥ prayayau rathena ||4||

5teṣāṃ rathānāmatha pṛṣṭhagopā dvātriṃśadanye'bhyapatanta pārtham
|
tathaiva te taṃ parivārya pārthaṃ vikṛṣya cāpāni mahāravāṇi ||5||

6avīvṛṣan bāṇamahaudhavṛṣṭyā yathā giriṃ toyadharā jalaughaiḥ
|
saṃpīḍyamānastu śaraughavṛṣṭyā dhanañjayastān yudhi jātaroṣaḥ ||6||

7ṣaṣṭhyā śaraiḥ saṃyati tailadhautairjadhāna tānapyatha pṛṣṭhagopān
|
rathāśca tāṃstānavajitya saṃkhye dhanañjayaḥ prītamanā yaśasvī ||7||

8athātvaradbhīṣmavadhāya jiṣṇurbalāni rājan samare nihatya
|
trigartarājo nihatān samīkṣya mahātmanā tānatha bandhuvargān ||8||

9raṇe puraskṛtya narādhipāṃstān jagāma pārthaṃ tvarito vadhāya
|
abhidrutaṃ cāstrabhṛtāṃ variṣṭhaṃ dhanañjayaṃ vīkṣya śikhaṇḍimukhyāḥ ||9||

10abhyudyayuste śitaśastrahastā rirakṣiṣanto rathamarjunasya
|
pārtho'pi tānāpatataḥ samīkṣya trigartarājñā sahitānnṛvīrān ||10||

11vidhvaṃsayitvā samare dhanuṣmān gāṇḍīvamuktairniśitaiḥ pṛṣatkaiḥ
|
bhīṣmaṃ yiyāsuryudhi saṃdadarśa duryodhanaṃ saindhavādīṃśca rājñaḥ ||11||

12saṃvārayiṣṇūnabhivārayitvā muhūrtamāyodhya balena vīraḥ
|
utsṛjya rājānamanantavīryo jayadrathādīṃśca nṛpān mahaujāḥ ||12||

13yayau tato bhīmabalo manasvī gāṅgeyamājau śaracāpapāṇiḥ
|
yudhiṣṭhiraśca prabalo mahātmā samāyayau tvarito jātakopaḥ ||13||

14madrādhipaṃ samabhityajya saṃkhye svabhāgamāptaṃ tamanantakīrtiḥ
|
sārdhaṃ sa mādrīsutabhīmasenairbhīṣmaṃ yayau śāntanavaṃ raṇāya ||14||

15taiḥ saṃprayuktaiḥ sa mahārathāgryairgaṅgāsutaḥ samare citrayodhī
|
na vivyathe śāntanavo mahātmā samāgataiḥ pāṇḍusutaiḥ samastaiḥ ||15||

16athaitya rājā yudhi satyasandho jayadratho'tyugrabalo manasvī
|
ciccheda cāpāni mahārathānāṃ prasahya teṣāṃ dhanuṣā vareṇa ||16||

17yudhiṣṭhiraṃ bhīmasenaṃ yamau ca pārthaṃ kṛṣṇaṃ yudhi sañjātakopaḥ
|
duryodhanaḥ krodhaviṣo mahātmā jaghāna bāṇairanalaprakāśaiḥ ||17||

18kṛpeṇa śalyena śalena caiva tathā vibho citrasenena cājau
|
viddhāḥ śaraiste'tivivṛddha kopairdevā yathā daityagaṇaiḥ sametaiḥ ||18||

19chinnāyudhaṃ śāntanavena rājā śikhaṇḍinaṃ prekṣya ca jātakopaḥ
|
ajātaśatruḥ samare mahātmā śikhaṇḍinaṃ kruddha uvāca vākyam ||19||

20uktvā tathā tvaṃ pituragrato māmahaṃ haniṣyāmi mahāvrataṃ tam
|
bhīṣmaṃ śaraughairvimalārkavarṇaiḥ satyaṃ vadāmīti kṛtā pratijñā ||20||

21tvayā ca naināṃ saphalāṃ karoṣi devavrataṃ yanna nihaṃsi yuddhe
|
mithyāpratijño bhava mā'tra vīra rakṣa svadharmaṃ svakulaṃ yaśaśca ||21||

22prekṣasva bhīṣmaṃ yudhi bhīmavegaṃ sarvāṃstapantaṃ mama sainyasaṅghān
|
śaraughajālairatitigmavegaiḥ kālaṃ yathā kālakṛtaṃ kṣaṇena ||22||

23nikṛttacāpaḥ samare'napekṣaḥ parājitaḥ śāntanavena cājau
|
vihāya bandhūnatha sodarāṃśca kva yāsyase nānurūpaṃ tavedam ||23||

24dṛṣṭvā hi bhīṣmaṃ tamanantavīryaṃ bhagnaṃ ca sainyaṃ dravamāṇamevam
|
bhīto'si nūnaṃ drupadasya putra tathā hi te mukhavarṇo'prahṛṣṭaḥ ||24||

25ajñāyamāne ca dhanañjaye'pi mahāhave saṃprasakte nṛvīre
|
kathaṃ hi bhīṣmātprathitaḥ pṛthivyāṃ bhayaṃ tvamadya prakaroṣi vīra ||25||

26sa dharmarājasya vaco niśamya rūkṣākṣaraṃ vipralāpānubaddham
|
pratyādeśaṃ manyamāno mahātmā pratatvare bhīṣmavadhāya rājan ||26||

27tamāpatantaṃ mahatā javena śikhaṇḍinaṃ bhīṣmamabhidravantam
|
nivārayāmāsa hi śalya enamastreṇa ghoreṇa sudurjayena ||27||

28sa cāpi dṛṣṭvā samudīryamāṇamastraṃ yugāntāgnisamaprakāśam
|
na saṃmumoha drupadasya putro rājan mahendrapratimaprabhāvaḥ ||28||

29tasthau ca tatraiva mahādhanuṣmān śaraistadastraṃ pratibādhamānaḥ
|
athādade vāruṇamanyadastraṃ śikhaṇḍyathograṃ pratighātamasya ||29||

30tadastramastreṇa vidāryamāṇaṃ svasthāḥ surā dadṛśuḥ pārthivāśca
|
bhīṣmastu rājan samare mahātmā dhanuśca citraṃ dhvajameva cāpi ||30||

31chittvā'nadat pāṇḍusutasya vīro yudhiṣṭhirasyājamīḍhasya rājñaḥ
|
tataḥ samutsṛjya dhanuḥ sabāṇaṃ yudhiṣṭhiraṃ vīkṣya bhayābhibhūtam ||31||

32gadāṃ pragṛhyābhipapāta saṃkhye jayadrathaṃ bhīmasenaḥ padātiḥ
|
tamāpatantaṃ sahasā javena jayadrathaṃ sagadaṃ bhīmasenam ||32||

33vivyādha ghorairyamadaṇḍakalpaiḥ śitaiḥ śaraiḥ pañcaśataiḥ samantāt
|
acintayitvā sa śarāṃstarasvī vṛkodaraḥ krodhaparītacetāḥ ||33||

34jaghāna vāhān samare samantāt pārāvatān sindhurājasya saṃkhye
|
tato'bhivīkṣyāpratimaprabhāvastavātmajastvaramāṇo rathena ||34||

35abhyāyayau bhīmasenaṃ nihantuṃ samudyatāstro surarājakalpaḥ
|
bhīmo'pyathainaṃ sahasā vinadya pratyudyayau gadayā tarjayānaḥ ||35||

36samudyatāṃ tāṃ yamadaṇḍakalpāṃ dṛṣṭvā gadāṃ te kuravaḥ samantāt
|
vihāya sarve tava putramugraṃ pātaṃ gadāyāḥ parihartukāmāḥ ||36||

37apakrāntāstumule saṃpramarde sudāruṇe bhārata mohanīye
|
amūḍhacetāstvatha citraseno mahāgadāmāpatantīṃ nirīkṣya ||37||

38rathaṃ svamutsṛjya padātirājau pragṛhya khaḍgaṃ vipulaṃ ca carma
|
avaplutaḥ siṃha ivācalāgrājjagāmānyaṃ bhūmipa bhūmideśam ||38||

39gadā'pi sā prāpya rathaṃ sucitraṃ sāśvaṃ sasūtaṃ vinihatya saṅkhye
|
jagāma bhūmiṃ jvalitā maholkā bhraṣṭāmbarādgāmiva saṃpatantī ||39||

40āścaryabhūtaṃ sumahattvadīyā dṛṣṭvaiva tadbhārata saṃprahṛṣṭāḥ
|
sarve vineduḥ sahitāḥ samantāt pupūjire tava putrasya śauryam ||40||
