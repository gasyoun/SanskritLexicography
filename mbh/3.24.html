Mahābhārata 3.24 (Āraṇyakaparvan)

1vaiśampāyana uvāca|
tatasteṣu prayāteṣu kaunteyaḥ satyasaṅgaraḥ
|
abhyabhāṣata dharmātmā bhrātṝnsarvānyudhiṣṭhiraḥ||1||

2dvādaśemāḥ samāsmābhirvastavyaṃ nirjane vane
|
samīkṣadhvaṃ mahāraṇye deśaṃ bahumṛgadvijam||2||

3bahupuṣpaphalaṃ ramyaṃ śivaṃ puṇyajanāvṛtam
|
yatremāḥ śaradaḥ sarvāḥ sukhaṃ prativasemahi||3||

4evamukte pratyuvāca dharmarājaṃ dhanañjayaḥ
|
guruvanmānavaguruṃ mānayitvā manasvinam||4||

5arjuna uvāca|
bhavāneva maharṣīṇāṃ vṛddhānāṃ paryupāsitā
|
ajñātaṃ mānuṣe loke bhavato nāsti kiñcana||5||

6tvayā hyupāsitā nityaṃ brāhmaṇā bharatarṣabha
|
dvaipāyanaprabhṛtayo nāradaśca mahātapāḥ||6||

7yaḥ sarvalokadvārāṇi nityaṃ sañcarate vaśī
|
devalokādbrahmalokaṃ gandharvāpsarasāmapi||7||

8anubhāvāṃśca jānāsi brāhmaṇānāṃ na saṃśayaḥ
|
prabhāvāṃścaiva vettha tvaṃ sarveṣāmeva pārthiva||8||

9tvameva rājañjānāsi śreyaḥkāraṇameva ca
|
yatrecchasi mahārāja nivāsaṃ tatra kurmahe||9||

10idaṃ dvaitavanaṃ nāma saraḥ puṇyajalocitam
|
bahupuṣpaphalaṃ ramyaṃ nānādvijaniṣevitam||10||

11yatremā dvādaśasamā viharemeti rocaye
|
yadi te'numataṃ rājankimanyanmanyate bhavān||11||

12yudhiṣṭhira uvāca|
mamāpyetanmataṃ pārtha tvayā yatsamudāhṛtam
|
gacchāmaḥ puṇyavikhyātaṃ mahaddvaitavanaṃ saraḥ||12||

13vaiśampāyana uvāca|
tataste prayayuḥ sarve pāṇḍavā dharmacāriṇaḥ
|
brāhmaṇairbahubhiḥ sārdhaṃ puṇyaṃ dvaitavanaṃ saraḥ||13||

14brāhmaṇāḥ sāgnihotrāśca tathaiva ca niragnayaḥ
|
svādhyāyino bhikṣavaśca tathaiva vanavāsinaḥ||14||

15bahavo brāhmaṇāstatra parivavruryudhiṣṭhiram
|
tapaḥsiddhā mahātmānaḥ śataśaḥ saṃśitavratāḥ||15||

16te yātvā pāṇḍavāstatra brāhmaṇairbahubhiḥ saha
|
puṇyaṃ dvaitavanaṃ ramyaṃ viviśurbharatarṣabhāḥ||16||

17tamālatālāmramadhūkanīpakadambasarjārjunakarṇikāraiḥ
|
tapātyaye puṣpadharairupetaṃ mahāvalaṃ rāṣṭrapatirdadarśa||17||

18mahādrumāṇāṃ śikhareṣu tasthurmanoramāṃ vācamudīrayantaḥ
|
mayūradātyūhacakorasaṅghāstasminvane barhiṇakokilāśca||18||

19kareṇuyūthaiḥ saha yūthapānāṃ madotkaṭānāmacalaprabhāṇām
|
mahānti yūthāni mahādvipānāṃ tasminvane rāṣṭrapatirdadarśa||19||

20manoramāṃ bhogavatīmupetya pūtātmanāṃ cīrajaṭādharāṇām
|
tasminvane dharmabhṛtāṃ nivāse dadarśa siddharṣigaṇānanekān||20||

21tataḥ sa yānādavaruhya rājā sabhrātṛkaḥ sajanaḥ kānanaṃ tat
|
viveśa dharmātmavatāṃ variṣṭhastriviṣṭapaṃ śakra ivāmitaujāḥ||21||

22taṃ satyasandhaṃ sahitābhipeturdidṛkṣavaścāraṇasiddhasaṅghāḥ
|
vanaukasaścāpi narendrasiṃhaṃ manasvinaṃ taṃ parivārya tasthuḥ||22||

23sa tatra siddhānabhivādya sarvānpratyarcito rājavaddevavacca
|
viveśa sarvaiḥ sahito dvijāgryaiḥ kṛtāñjalirdharmabhṛtāṃ variṣṭhaḥ||23||

24sa puṇyaśīlaḥ pitṛvanmahātmā tapasvibhirdharmaparairupetya
|
pratyarcitaḥ puṣpadharasya mūle mahādrumasyopaviveśa rājā||24||

25bhīmaśca kṛṣṇā ca dhanañjayaśca yamau ca te cānucarā narendram
|
vimucya vāhānavaraśāśca sarve tatropatasthurbharataprabarhāḥ||25||

26latāvatānāvanataḥ sa pāṇḍavairmahādrumaḥ pañcabhireva dhanvibhiḥ
|
babhau nivāsopagatairmahātmabhirmahāgirirvāraṇayūthapairiva||26||
