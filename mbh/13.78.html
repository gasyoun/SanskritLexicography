Mahābhārata 13.78 (Anuśāsanaparvan)

1bhīṣma uvāca|
etasminneva kāle tu vasiṣṭhamṛṣisattamam
|
ikṣvākuvaṃśajo rājā saudāso vadatāṃ varaḥ ||1||

2sarvalokacaraṃ siddhaṃ brahmakośaṃ sanātanam
|
purohitamabhipraṣṭumabhivādyopacakrame ||2||

3saudāsa uvāca|
trailokye bhagavankiṃsvitpavitraṃ kathyate'nagha
|
yatkīrtayansadā martyaḥ prāpnuyātpuṇyamuttamam ||3||

4bhīṣma uvāca|
tasmai provāca vacanaṃ praṇatāya hitaṃ tadā
|
gavāmupaniṣadvidvānnamaskṛtya gavāṃ śuciḥ ||4||

5gāvaḥ surabhigandhinyastathā guggulugandhayaḥ
|
gāvaḥ pratiṣṭhā bhūtānāṃ gāvaḥ svastyayanaṃ mahat ||5||

6gāvo bhūtaṃ ca bhavyaṃ ca gāvaḥ puṣṭiḥ sanātanī
|
gāvo lakṣmyāstathā mūlaṃ goṣu dattaṃ na naśyati ||6||

7annaṃ hi paramaṃ gāvo devānāṃ paramaṃ haviḥ
|
svāhākāravaṣaṭkārau goṣu nityaṃ pratiṣṭhitau ||7||

8gāvo yajñasya hi phalaṃ goṣu yajñāḥ pratiṣṭhitāḥ
|
gāvo bhaviṣyaṃ bhūtaṃ ca goṣu yajñāḥ pratiṣṭhitāḥ ||8||

9sāyaṃ prātaśca satataṃ homakāle mahādyute
|
gāvo dadati vai haumyamṛṣibhyaḥ puruṣarṣabha ||9||

10yāni kāni ca durgāṇi duṣkṛtāni kṛtāni ca
|
taranti caiva pāpmānaṃ dhenuṃ ye dadati prabho ||10||

11ekāṃ ca daśagurdadyāddaśa dadyācca gośatī
|
śataṃ sahasragurdadyātsarve tulyaphalā hi te ||11||

12anāhitāgniḥ śatagurayajvā ca sahasraguḥ
|
samṛddho yaśca kīnāśo nārghamarhanti te trayaḥ ||12||

13kapilāṃ ye prayacchanti savatsāṃ kāṃsyadohanām
|
suvratāṃ vastrasaṃvītāmubhau lokau jayanti te ||13||

14yuvānamindriyopetaṃ śatena śatayūthapam
|
gavendraṃ brāhmaṇendrāya bhūriśṛṅgamalaṅkṛtam ||14||

15vṛṣabhaṃ ye prayacchanti śrotriyāya parantapa
|
aiśvaryaṃ te'dhigacchanti jāyamānāḥ punaḥ punaḥ ||15||

16nākīrtayitvā gāḥ supyāttāsāṃ saṃsmṛtya cotpatet
|
sāyaṃprātarnamasyecca gāstataḥ puṣṭimāpnuyāt ||16||

17gavāṃ mūtrapurīṣasya nodvijeta kathañcana
|
na cāsāṃ māṃsamaśnīyādgavāṃ puṣṭiṃ tathāpnuyāt ||17||

18gāśca saṃkīrtayennityaṃ nāvamanyeta tāstathā
|
aniṣṭaṃ svapnamālakṣya gāṃ naraḥ saṃprakīrtayet ||18||

19gomayena sadā snāyātkarīṣe cāpi saṃviśet
|
śleṣmamūtrapurīṣāṇi pratighātaṃ ca varjayet ||19||

20sārdre carmaṇi bhuñjīta nirīkṣedvāruṇīṃ diśam
|
vāgyataḥ sarpiṣā bhūmau gavāṃ puṣṭiṃ sadā'śnute ||20||

21ghṛtena juhuyādagniṃ ghṛtena svasti vācayet
|
ghṛtaṃ dadyā‌dghṛtaṃ prāśedgavāṃ puṣṭiṃ sadā'śnute ||21||

22gomatyā vidyayā dhenuṃ tilānāmabhimantrya yaḥ
|
sarvaratnamayīṃ dadyānna sa śocetkṛtākṛte ||22||

23gāvo māmupatiṣṭhantu hemaśṛṃgyaḥ payomucaḥ
|
surabhyaḥ saurabheyyaśca saritaḥ sāgaraṃ yathā ||23||

24gā vai paśyāmyahaṃ nityaṃ gāvaḥ paśyantu māṃ sadā
|
gāvo'smākaṃ vayaṃ tāsāṃ yato gāvastato vayam ||24||

25evaṃ rātrau divā cāpi sameṣu viṣameṣu ca
|
mahābhayeṣu ca naraḥ kīrtayanmucyate bhayāt ||25||
