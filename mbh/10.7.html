Mahābhārata 10.7 (Sauptikaparvan)

1sañjaya uvāca|
evaṃ sañcintayitvā tu droṇaputro viśāmpate
|
avatīrya rathopasthāddeveśaṃ praṇataḥ sthitaḥ||1||

2drauṇiruvāca|
ugraṃ sthāṇuṃ śivaṃ rudraṃ śarvamīśānamīśvaram
|
giriśaṃ varadaṃ devaṃ bhavabhāvanamīśvaram||2||

3śitikaṇṭhamajaṃ śukraṃ dakṣakratuharaṃ haram
|
viśvarūpaṃ virupākṣaṃ bahurūpamumāpatim||3||

4śmaśānavāsinaṃ dṛptaṃ mahāgaṇapatiṃ vibhum
|
khaṭvāṅgadhāriṇaṃ rudraṃ jaṭilaṃ brahmacāriṇam||4||

5manasā suviśuddhena duṣkareṇālpacetasā
|
so'hamātmopahāreṇa yakṣye tripuraghātinam||5||

6stutaṃ stutyaṃ stūyamānamamoghaṃ kṛttivāsasam
|
vilohitaṃ nīlakaṇṭhamasahyaṃ durnivāraṇam||6||

7śakraṃ brahmasṛjaṃ brahma brahmacāriṇameva ca
|
vratavantaṃ taponiṣṭhamanantaṃ tapatāṃ gatim||7||

8bahurūpaṃ gaṇādhyakṣaṃ tryakṣaṃ pāriṣadapriyam
|
dhanādhyakṣaṃ kṣitimukhaṃ gaurīhṛdayavallabham||8||

9kumārapitaraṃ piṅgaṃ govṛṣottamavāhanam
|
tanuvāsasamatyugramumābhūṣaṇatatparam||9||

10paraṃ parebhyaḥ paramaṃ paraṃ yasmānna vidyate
|
iṣvastrottamabhartāraṃ digantaṃ deśarakṣiṇam||10||

11hiraṇyakavacaṃ devaṃ candramaulivibhūṣaṇam
|
prapadye śaraṇaṃ devaṃ parameṇa samādhinā||11||

12imāṃ cedāpadaṃ ghorāṃ tarāmyadya suduṣkarām
|
sarvabhūtopahāreṇa yakṣye'haṃ śucinā śucim||12||

13iti tasya vyavasitaṃ jñātvā yogātsukarmaṇaḥ
|
purastātkāñcanī vedī prādurāsīnmahātmanaḥ||13||

14tasyāṃ vedyāṃ tadā rājaṃścitrabhānurajāyata
|
sa diśo vidiśaḥ khaṃ ca jvālābhiriva pūrayan||14||

15dīptāsyanayanāścātra naikapādaśirobhujāḥ
|
ratnacitrāṅgadadharāḥ samudyatakarāstathā||15||

16dvīpaśailapratīkāśāḥ prādurāsanmahāgaṇāḥ
|
śvavarāhoṣṭrarūpāśca hayagomāyugomukhāḥ||16||

17ṛkṣamārjāravadanā vyāghradvīpimukhāstathā
|
kākavaktrāḥ plavamukhāḥ śukavaktrāstathaiva ca||17||

18mahājagaravaktrāśca haṃsavaktrāḥ śitaprabhāḥ
|
dārvāghāṭamukhāścāpi cāṣavaktrāśca bhārata||18||

19kūrmanakramukhāścaiva śiśumāramukhāstathā
|
mahāmakaravaktrāśca timivaktrāstathaiva ca||19||

20harivaktrāḥ krauñcamukhāḥ kapotebhamukhāstathā
|
pārāvatamukhāścaiva madguvaktrāstathaiva ca||20||

21pāṇikarṇāḥ sahasrākṣāstathaiva ca mahodarāḥ
|
nirmāṃsāḥ kākavaktrāśca śyenavaktrāśca bhārata||21||

22tathaivāśiraso rājan ṛkṣavaktrāśca bhārata
|
pradīptanetrajihvāśca jvālāvarṇāstathaiva ca||22||

23jvālākeśāśca rājendra jvaladromacaturbhujāḥ
|
meṣavaktrāstathaivānye tathā chāgamukhānṛpa||23||

24śaṅkhābhāḥ śaṅkhavaktrāśca śaṅkhavarṇāstathaiva ca
|
śaṅkhamālāparikarāḥ śaṅkhadhvanisamasvanāḥ||24||

25jaṭādharāḥ pañcaśikhāstathā muṇḍāḥ kṛśodarāḥ
|
caturdaṃṣṭrāścaturjihvāḥ śaṅkukarṇāḥ kirīṭinaḥ||25||

26mauñjīdharāśca rājendra tathā kuñcitamūrdhajāḥ
|
uṣṇīṣiṇo mukuṭinaścāruvaktrāḥ svalaṅkṛtāḥ||26||

27padmotpalāpīḍadharāstathā mukuṭadhāriṇaḥ
|
māhātmyena ca saṃyuktāḥ śataśo'tha sahasraśaḥ||27||

28śataghnīvajrahastāśca tathā musalapāṇayaḥ
|
bhuśuṇḍīpāśahastāśca daṇḍahastāśca bhārata||28||

29pṛṣṭheṣu baddheṣudhayaścitrabāṇotkaṭāstathā
|
sadhvajāḥ sapatākāśca saghaṇṭāḥ saparaśvadhāḥ||29||

30mahāpāśodyatakarāstathā laguḍapāṇayaḥ
|
sthūṇāhastāḥ khaḍgahastāḥ sarpocchritakirīṭinaḥ||30||

31mahāsarpāṅgadadharāścitrābharaṇadhāriṇaḥ
|
rajodhvajāḥ paṅkadigdhāḥ sarve śuklāmbarasrajaḥ||31||

32nīlāṅgāḥ piṅgalāṅgāśca muṇḍavaktrāstathaiva ca
|
bherīśaṅkhamṛdaṅgāṃśca jharjharānakagomukhān||32||

33avādayanpāriṣadāḥ prahṛṣṭāḥ kanakaprabhāḥ
|
gāyamānāstathaivānye nṛtyamānāstathā pare||33||

34laṅghayantaḥ plavantaśca valgantaśca mahārathāḥ
|
dhāvanto javanā muṇḍāḥ pavanoddhūtamūrdhajāḥ||34||

35mattā iva mahānāgā vinadanto muhurmuhuḥ
|
subhīmā ghorarūpāśca śūlapaṭṭiśapāṇayaḥ||35||

36nānāvirāgavasanāścitramālyānulepanāḥ
|
ratnacitrāṅgadadharāḥ samudyatakarāstathā||36||

37hantāro dviṣatāṃ śūrāḥ prasahyāsahyavikramāḥ
|
pātāro'sṛgvasaudhānāṃ māṃsāntrakṛtabhojanāḥ||37||

38cūḍālāḥ karṇikārāśca prahṛṣṭāḥ piṭharodarāḥ
|
atihrasvātidīrghāśca pralambāścātibhairavāḥ||38||

39vikaṭāḥ kālalamboṣṭhā bṛhacchephāṇḍapiṇḍikāḥ
|
mahārhanānāvikaṭā muṇḍāśca jaṭilāḥ pare||39||

40sārkendugrahanakṣatrāṃ dyāṃ kuryuste mahītale
|
utsaheraṃśca ye hantuṃ bhūtagrāmaṃ caturvidham||40||

41ye ca vītabhayā nityaṃ harasya bhrukuṭīsahāḥ
|
kāmakāraka nityaṃ trailokyasyeśvareśvarāḥ||41||

42nityānandapramuditā vāgīśā vītamatsarāḥ
|
prāpyāṣṭaguṇamaiśvaryaṃ ye na yāsyanti vai smayam||42||

43yeṣāṃ vismayate nityaṃ bhagavānkarmabhirharaḥ
|
manovākkarmabhiryuktairnityamārādhitaśca yaiḥ||43||

44manovākkarmabhirbhaktānpāti putrānivaurasān
|
pibanto'sṛgvasāścānye kruddhā brahmadviṣāṃ sadā||44||

45caturvidhātmakaṃ somaṃ ye pibanti ca sarvadā
|
śrutena brahmacaryeṇa tapasā ca damena ca||45||

46ye samārādhya śūlāṅkaṃ bhavasāyujyamāgatāḥ
|
yairātmabhūtairbhagavānpārvatyā ca maheśvaraḥ||46||

47mahābhūtagaṇairbhuṅkte bhūtabhavyabhavatprabhuḥ
|
nānāvāditrahasitakṣveḍitotkruṣṭagarjitaiḥ||47||

48santrāsayantaste viśvamaśvatthāmānamabhyayuḥ
|
saṃstuvanto mahādevaṃ bhāḥ kurvāṇāḥ suvarcasaḥ||48||

49vivardhayiṣavo drauṇermahimānaṃ mahātmanaḥ
|
jijñāsamānāstattejaḥ sauptikaṃ ca didṛkṣavaḥ||49||

50bhīmograparighālātaśūlapaṭṭiśapāṇayaḥ
|
ghorarūpāḥ samājagmurbhūtasaṅghāḥ samantataḥ||50||

51janayeyurbhayaṃ ye sma trailokyasyāpi darśanāt
|
tānprekṣamāṇo'pi vyathāṃ na cakāra mahābalaḥ||51||

52atha drauṇirdhanuṣpāṇirbaddhagodhāṅgulitravān
|
svayamevātmanātmānamupahāramupāharat||52||

53dhanūṃṣi samidhastatra pavitrāṇi sitāḥ śarāḥ
|
havirātmavataścātmā tasmin bhārata karmaṇi||53||

54tataḥ saumyena mantreṇa droṇaputraḥ pratāpavān
|
upahāraṃ mahāmanyurathātmānamupāharat||54||

55taṃ rudraṃ raudrakarmāṇaṃ raudraiḥ karmabhiracyutam
|
abhiṣṭhutya mahātmānamityuvāca kṛtāñjaliḥ||55||

56drauṇiruvāca|
imamātmānamadyāhaṃ jātamāṅgirase kule
|
svagnau juhomi bhagavan pratigṛhṇīṣva māṃ balim||56||

57bhavadbhaktyā mahādeva parameṇa samādhinā
|
asyāmāpadi viśvātmannupākurmi tavāgrataḥ||57||

58tvayi sarvāṇi bhūtāni sarvabhūteṣu cāsi vai
|
guṇānāṃ hi pradhānānāmekatvaṃ tvayi tiṣṭhati||58||

59sarvabhūtāśraya vibho havirbhūtamavasthitam
|
pratigṛhāṇa māṃ deva yadyaśakyāḥ pare mayā||59||

60ityuktvā drauṇirāsthāya tāṃ vedīṃ dīptapāvakām
|
santyajyātmānamāruhya kṛṣṇavartmanyupāviśat||60||

61tamūrdhvabāhuṃ niśceṣṭaṃ dṛṣṭvā havirupasthitam
|
abravīdbhagavānsākṣānmahādevo hasanniva||61||

62satyaśaucārjavatyāgaistapasā niyamena ca
|
kṣāntyā bhaktyā ca dhṛtyā ca buddhyā ca vacasā tathā||62||

63yathāvadahamārāddhaḥ kṛṣṇenākliṣṭakarmaṇā
|
tasmādiṣṭatamaḥ kṛṣṇādanyo mama na vidyate||63||

64kurvatā tātasammānaṃ tvāṃ ca jijñāsatā mayā
|
pāñcālāḥ sahasā guptā māyāśca bahuśaḥ kṛtāḥ||64||

65kṛtastasyaiva sammānaḥ pāñcālān rakṣatā mayā
|
abhibhūtāstu kālena naiṣāmadyāsti jīvitam||65||

66evamuktvā mahātmānaṃ bhagavānātmanastanum
|
āviveśa dadau cāsmai vimalaṃ khaḍgamuttamam||66||

67athāviṣṭo bhagavatā bhūyo jajvāla tejasā
|
vegavāṃścābhavadyuddhe devasṛṣṭena tejasa||67||

68tamadṛśyāni bhūtāni rakṣāṃsi ca samādravan
|
abhitaḥ śatruśibiraṃ yāntaṃ sākṣādiveśvaram||68||
