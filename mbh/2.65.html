Mahābhārata 2.65 (Sabhāparvan)

1śakuniruvāca||
bahuvittaṃ parājaiṣīḥ pāṇḍavānāṃ yudhiṣṭhira
|
ācakṣva vittaṃ kaunteya yadi te'styaparājitam||1||

2yudhiṣṭhira uvāca|
mama vittamasaṅkhyeyaṃ yadahaṃ veda saubala
|
atha tvaṃ śakune kasmādvittaṃ samanupṛcchasi||2||

3ayutaṃ prayutaṃ caiva śaṅkuṃ padmaṃ tathārbudam
|
kharvaṃ śaṅkhaṃ nikharvaṃ ca mahāpadmaṃ ca koṭayaḥ||3||

4madhyaṃ caiva parārdhaṃ ca saparaṃ cātra paṇyatām
|
etanmama dhanaṃ rājaṃstena dīvyāmyahaṃ tvayā||4||

5vaiśampāyana uvāca|
etacchutvā vyavasito nikṛtiṃ samupāśritaḥ
|
jitamityeva śakuniryudhiṣṭhiramabhāṣata||5||

6yudhiṣṭhira uvāca|
gavāśvaṃ bahudhenūkamasaṅkhyeyamajāvikam
|
yatkiñcidanuparṇāśāṃ prāksindhorapi saubala
|
etanmama dhanaṃ sarvaṃ tena dīvyāmyahaṃ tvayā||6||

7vaiśampāyana uvāca|
etacchutvā vyavasito nikṛtiṃ samupāśritaḥ
|
jitamityeva śakuniryudhiṣṭhiramabhāṣata||7||

8yudhiṣṭhira uvāca|
puraṃ janapado bhūmirabrāhmaṇadhanaiḥ saha
|
abrāhmaṇāśca puruṣā rājañcchiṣṭaṃ dhanaṃ mama
|
etadrājanmama dhanaṃ tena dīvyāmyahaṃ tvayā||8||

9vaiśampāyana uvāca|
etacchrutvā vyavasito nikṛtiṃ samupāśritaḥ
|
jitamityeva śakuniryudhiṣṭhiramabhāṣata||9||

10yudhiṣṭhira uvāca|
rājaputrā ime rājanśobhante yairvibhūṣitāḥ
|
kuṇḍalāni ca niṣkāśca sarvaṃ rājavibhūṣaṇam
|
etanmama dhanaṃ rājaṃstena dīvyāmyahaṃ tvayā||10||

11vaiśampāyana uvāca|
etacchrutvā vyavasito nikṛtiṃ samupāśritaḥ
|
jitamityeva śakuniryudhiṣṭhiramabhāṣata||11||

12yudhiṣṭhira uvāca|
śyāmo yuvā lohitākṣaḥ siṃhaskandho mahābhujaḥ
|
nakulo glaha evaikī viddhyetanmama taddhanam||12||

13śakuniruvāca| priyaste nakulo rājanrājaputro yudhiṣṭhira
|
asmākaṃ vaśatāṃ prāpto bhūyaḥ keneha dīvyase||13||

14vaiśampāyana uvāca|
evamuktvā tu tānakṣāñśakuniḥ pratyadīvyata
|
jitamityeva śakuniryudhiṣṭhiramabhāṣata||14||

15yudhiṣṭhira uvāca|
ayaṃ dharmānsahadevo'nuśāsti loke hyasminpaṇḍitākhyāṃ gataśca
|
anarhatā rājaputreṇa tena dīvyāmyahaṃ cāpriyavatpriyeṇa||15||

16vaiśampāyana uvāca|
etacchrutvā vyavasito nikṛtiṃ samupāśritaḥ
|
jitamityeva śakuniryudhiṣṭhiramabhāṣata||16||

17śakuniruvāca| mādrīputrau priyau rājaṃstavemau vijitau mayā
|
garīyāṃsau tu te manye bhīmasenadhanañjayau||17||

18yudhiṣṭhira uvāca|
adharmaṃ carase nūnaṃ yo nāvekṣasi vai nayam
|
yo naḥ sumanasāṃ mūḍha vibhedaṃ kartumicchasi||18||

19śakuniruvāca| garte mattaḥ prapatate pramattaḥ sthāṇumṛcchati
|
jyeṣṭho rājanvariṣṭho'si namaste bharatarṣabha||19||

20svapne tāni na dṛśyante jāgrato vā yudhiṣṭhira
|
kitavā yāni dīvyantaḥ pralapantyutkaṭā iva||20||

21yudhiṣṭhira uvāca|
yo naḥ saṅkhye nauriva pāranetā jetā ripūṇāṃ rājaputrastarasvī
|
anarhatā lokavīreṇa tena dīvyāmyahaṃ śakune phālgunena||21||

22vaiśampāyana uvāca|
etacchrutvā vyavasito nikṛtiṃ samupāśritaḥ
|
jitamityeva śakuniryudhiṣṭhiramabhāṣata||22||

23śakuniruvāca| ayaṃ mayā pāṇḍavānāṃ dhanurdharaḥ parājitaḥ pāṇḍavaḥ savyasācī
|
bhīmena rājandayitena dīvya yatkaitavaṃ pāṇḍava te'vaśiṣṭam||23||

24yudhiṣṭhira uvāca|
yo no netā yo yudhi naḥ praṇetā yathā vajrī dānavaśatrurekaḥ
|
tiryakprekṣī sannatabhrūrmahātmā siṃhaskandho yaśca sadā'tyamarṣī||24||

25balena tulyo yasya pumānna vidyate gadābhṛtāmagrya ihārimardanaḥ
|
anarhatā rājaputreṇa tena dīvyāmyahaṃ bhīmasenena rājan||25||

26vaiśampāyana uvāca|
etacchrutvā vyavasito nikṛtiṃ samupāśritaḥ
|
jitamityeva śakuniryudhiṣṭhiramabhāṣata||26||

27śakuniruvāca| bahuvittaṃ parājaiṣīrbhrātṝṃśca sahayadvipān
|
ācakṣva vittaṃ kaunteya yadi te'styaparājitam||27||

28yudhiṣṭhira uvāca|
ahaṃ viśiṣṭaḥ sarveṣāṃ bhrātṝṇāṃ dayitastathā
|
kuryāmahaṃ jitaḥ karma svayamātmanyupalpute||28||

29vaiśampāyana uvāca|
etacchrutvā vyavasito nikṛtiṃ samupāśritaḥ
|
jitamityeva śakuniryudhiṣṭhiramabhāṣata||29||

30śakuniruvāca| etatpāpiṣṭhamakaroryadātmānaṃ parājayeḥ
|
śiṣṭe sati dhane rājanpāpa ātmaparājayaḥ||30||

31vaiśampāyana uvāca|
evamuktvā matākṣastānglahe sarvānavasthitān
|
parājayallokavīrānuktvā rājñāṃ pṛthakpṛthak||31||

32śakuniruvāca| asti te vai priyā rājanglaha eko'parājitaḥ
|
paṇasva kṛṣṇāṃ pāñcālīṃ tayātmānaṃ punarjaya||32||

33yudhiṣṭhira uvāca|
naiva hrasvā na mahatī na kṛśā nātirohiṇī
|
nīlakuñcitakeśī ca tayā dīvyāmyahaṃ tvayā||33||

34śāradotpalapatrākṣyā śāradotpalagandhayā
|
śāradotpalasevinyā rūpeṇa śrīsamānayā||34||

35tathaiva syādānuśaṃsyāttathā syādrūpasampadā
|
tathā syācchīlasampattyā yāmicchetpuruṣaḥ striyam||35||

36sarvairguṇairhi sampannāmanukūlāṃ priyaṃvadām
|
yādṛśīṃ dharmakāmārthasiddhimicchennaraḥ striyam||36||

37caramaṃ saṃviśati yā prathamaṃ pratibudhyate
|
āgopālāvipālebhyaḥ sarvaṃ veda kṛtākṛtam||37||

38ābhāti padmavadvaktraṃ sasvedaṃ mallikeva ca
|
vedīmadhyā dīrghakeśī tāmrāsyā nātilomaśā||38||

39tayaivaṃvidhayā rājanpāñcālyāhaṃ sumadhyamā
|
glahaṃ dīvyāmi cārvaṅgyā draupadyā hanta saubala||39||

40vaiśampāyana uvāca|
evamukte tu vacane dharmarājena dhīmatā
|
dhigdhigityeva vṛddhānāṃ sabhyānāṃ niḥsṛtā giraḥ||40||

41cukṣubhe sā sabhā rājanrājñāṃ sañjajñire śucaḥ
|
bhīṣmadroṇakṛpādīnāṃ svedaśca samajāyata||41||

42śiro gṛhītvā viduro gatasatva ivābhavat
|
āste dhyāyannadhovaktro niḥśvasanniva pannagaḥ||42||

43dhṛtarāṣṭrastu taṃ hṛṣṭaḥ paryapṛcchatpunaḥ punaḥ
|
kiṃ jitaṃ kiṃ jitamiti hyākāraṃ nābhyarakṣata||43||

44jaharṣa karṇo'tibhṛśaṃ saha duḥśāsanādibhiḥ
|
itareṣāṃ tu sabhyānāṃ netrebhyaḥ prāpatajjalam||44||

45saubalastvabhighāyaiva jitakāśī madotkaṭaḥ
|
jitamityeva tānakṣānpurarevānvapadyata||45||
