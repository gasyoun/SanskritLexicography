Mahābhārata 6.103 (Bhīṣmaparvan)

1sañjaya uvāca|
madhyaṃdine mahārāja saṅgrāmaḥ samapadyata
|
lokakṣayakaro raudro bhīṣmasya saha somakaiḥ ||1||

2gāṅgeyo rathināṃ śreṣṭhaḥ pāṇḍavānāmanīkinīm
|
vyadhamanniśitairbāṇaiḥ śataśo'tha sahasraśaḥ ||2||

3saṃmamarda ca tatsainyaṃ pitā devavratastava
|
dhānyānāmiva lūnānāṃ prakaraṃ gogaṇā iva ||3||

4dhṛṣṭadyumnaḥ śikhaṇḍī ca virāṭo drupadastathā
|
bhīṣmamāsādya samare śarairjaghnurmahāratham ||4||

5dhṛṣṭadyumnaṃ tato vidhvā virāṭaṃ ca śaraistribhiḥ
|
drupadasya ca nārācaṃ preṣayāmāsa bhārata ||5||

6tena viddhā maheṣvāsā bhīṣmeṇāmitrakarṣiṇā
|
cukrudhuḥ samare rājanpādaspṛṣṭā ivoragāḥ ||6||

7śikhaṇḍī taṃ ca vivyādha bharatānāṃ pitāmaham
|
strīmayaṃ manasā dhyātvā nāsmai prāharadacyutaḥ ||7||

8dhṛṣṭadyumnastu samare krodhenāgniriva jvalan
|
pitāmahaṃ tribhirbāṇairbāhvorurasi cārpayat ||8||

9drupadaḥ pañcaviṃśatyā virāṭo daśabhiḥ śaraiḥ
|
śikhaṃḍī pañcaviṃśatyā bhīṣmaṃ vivyādha sāyakaiḥ ||9||

10so'tividdho mahārāja śoṇitaughapariplutaḥ
|
vasante puṣpaśabalo raktāśoka ivābabhau ||10||

11tān pratyavidhyadgāṅgeyastribhistribhirajihmagaiḥ
|
drupadasya ca bhallena dhanuściccheda māriṣa ||11||

12so'nyat kārmukamādāya bhīṣmaṃ vivyādha pañcabhiḥ
|
sārathiṃ ca tribhirbāṇaiḥ suśitai raṇamūrdhani ||12||

13tathā bhīmo mahārāja draupadyāḥ pañca cātmajāḥ
|
kekayā bhrātaraḥ pañca sātyakiścaiva sāttvataḥ ||13||

14abhyadravanta gāṅgeyaṃ yudhiṣṭhirapurogamāḥ
|
rirakṣiṣantaḥ pāñcālyaṃ dhṛṣṭadyumnapurogamāḥ||14||

15tathaiva tāvakāḥ sarve bhīṣmarakṣārthamudyatāḥ
|
pratyudyayuḥ pāṇḍusenāṃ sahasainyā narādhipa ||15||

16tatrāsīt sumahadyuddhaṃ tava teṣāṃ ca saṅkulam
|
narāśvarathanāgānāṃ yamarāṣṭravivardhanam ||16||

17rathī rathinamāsādya prāhiṇodyamasādanam
|
tathetarān samāsādya naranāgāśvasādinaḥ ||17||

18anayan paralokāya śaraiḥ sannataparvabhiḥ
|
śaraiśca vividhairghoraistatra tatra viśāṃpate ||18||

19rathāstu rathibhirhīnā hatasārathayastathā
|
vipradrutāśca samare diśo jagmuḥ samantataḥ ||19||

20mṛdnantaste narān rājan hayāṃśca subahūn raṇe
|
vātāyamānā dṛśyante gandharvanagaropamāḥ ||20||

21rathinaśca rathairhīnā varmiṇastejasā yutāḥ
|
kuṇḍaloṣṇīṣiṇaḥ sarve niṣkāṅgadavibhūṣaṇāḥ ||21||

22devaputrasamāḥ sarve śaurye śakrasamā yudhi
|
ṛddhyā vaiśravaṇaṃ cāti nayena ca bṛhaspatim ||22||

23sarvalokeśvarāḥ śūrāstatra tatra viśāṃpate
|
vipradrutā vyadṛśyanta prākṛtā iva mānavāḥ ||23||

24dantinaśca naraśreṣṭha hīnāḥ paramasādibhiḥ
|
mṛdnantaḥ svānyanīkāni nipetuḥ sarvaśabdagāḥ ||24||

25carmabhiścāmaraiścitraiḥ patākābhiśca māriṣa
|
chatraiḥ sitairhemadaṇḍaiścāmaraiśca samantataḥ ||25||

26viśīrṇairvipradhāvanto dṛśyante sma diśo daśa
|
navabheghapratīkāśā jaladopamaniḥsvanāḥ ||26||

27tathaiva dantibhirhīnā gajārohā viśāṃpate
|
pradhāvanto'nvadṛśyanta tava teṣāṃ ca saṅkule ||27||

28nānādeśasamutthāṃśca turagān hemabhūṣitān
|
vātāyamānānadrākṣaṃ śataśo'tha sahasraśaḥ ||28||

29aśvārohān hatairaśvairgṛhītāsīn samantataḥ
|
dravamāṇānapaśyāma drāvyamāṇāṃśca saṃyuge ||29||

30gajo gajaṃ samāsādya dravamāṇaṃ mahāhave
|
yayau pramṛdya tarasā pādātān vājinastathā ||30||

31tathaiva ca rathān rājan pramamarda raṇe gajaḥ
|
rathāścaiva samāsādya patitāṃsturagānbhuvi ||31||

32vyamṛdnan samare rājaṃsturagāṃśca narān raṇe
|
evaṃ te bahudhā rājanpratyamṛdnanparasparam ||32||

33tasmin raudre tathā yuddhe vartamāne mahābhaye
|
prāvartata nadī ghorā śoṇitāntrataraṅgiṇī ||33||

34asthisaṅghātasambādhā keśaśaivalaśādvalā
|
rathahṛdā śarāvartā hayamīnā durāsadā ||34||

35śīrṣopalasamākīrṇā hastigrāhasamākulā
|
kavacoṣṇīṣaphenaughā dhanurvegāsikacchapā ||35||

36patākādhvajavṛkṣāḍhyā martyakūlāpahāriṇī
|
kravyādahaṃsasaṃkīrṇā yamarāṣṭravivardhanī ||36||

37tāṃ nadīṃ kṣatriyāḥ śūrā rathanāgahayaplavaiḥ
|
praterurbahavo rājan bhayaṃ tyaktvā mahārathāḥ ||37||

38apovāha raṇe bhīrūn kaśmalenābhisaṃvṛtān
|
yathā vaitaraṇī pretān pretarājapuraṃ prati ||38||

39prākrośan kṣatriyāstatra dṛṣṭvā tadvaiśasaṃ mahat
|
duryodhanāparādhena gacchanti kṣatriyāḥ kṣayam ||39||

40guṇavatsu kathaṃ dveṣaṃ dhṛtarāṣṭro janeśvaraḥ
|
kṛtavān pāṇḍuputreṣu pāpātmā lobhamohitaḥ ||40||

41evaṃ bahuvidhā vācaḥ śrūyante sma parasparam
|
pāṇḍavastavasaṃyuktāḥ putrāṇāṃ te sudāruṇāḥ ||41||

42tā niśamya tato vācaḥ sarvayodhairudāhṛtāḥ
|
āgaskṛtsarvalokasya putro duryodhanastava ||42||

43bhīṣmaṃ droṇaṃ kṛpaṃ caiva śalyaṃ covāca bhārata
|
yudhyadhvamanahaṅkārāḥ kiṃ ciraṃ kurutheti ca ||43||

44tataḥ pravavṛte yuddhaṃ kurūṇāṃ pāṇḍavaiḥ saha
|
akṣadyūtakṛtaṃ rājan sughoraṃ vaiśasaṃ tadā ||44||

45yatpurā na nigṛhṇāsi vāryamāṇo mahātmabhiḥ
|
vaicitravīrya tasyedaṃ phalaṃ paśya sudāruṇam ||45||

46na hi pāṇḍusutā rājansasainyāḥ sapadānugāḥ
|
rakṣanti samare prāṇān kauravā vāpi saṃyuge ||46||

47etasmāt kāraṇādghoro vartate svajanakṣayaḥ
|
devādvā puruṣavyāghra tava cāpanayānnṛpa ||47||
