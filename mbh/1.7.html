Mahābhārata 1.7 (Ādiparvan)

1sautiruvāca|
śaptastu bhṛguṇā vahniḥ kruddho vākyamathābravīt
|
kimidaṃ sāhasaṃ brahmankṛtavānasi māṃ prati||1||

2dharme prayatamānasya satyaṃ ca vadataḥ samam
|
pṛṣṭo yadabruvaṃ satyaṃ vyabhicāro'tra ko mama||2||

3pṛṣṭo hi sākṣī yaḥ sākṣyaṃ jānāno'pyanyathā vadet
|
sa pūrvānātmanaḥ sapta kule hanyāttathā'parān||3||

4yaśca kāryārthatattvajño jānāno'pi na bhāṣate
|
so'pi tenaiva pāpena lipyate nātra saṃśayaḥ||4||

5śakto'hamapi śaptuṃ tvāṃ mānyāstu brāhmaṇā mama
|
jānato'pi ca te brahmankathayiṣye nibodha tat||5||

6yogena bahudhātmānaṃ kṛtvā tiṣṭhāmi mūrtiṣu
|
agnihotreṣu satreṣu kriyāsu ca makheṣu ca||6||

7vedoktena vidhānena mayi yaddhūyate haviḥ
|
devatāḥ pitaraścaiva tena tṛptā bhavanti vai||7||

8āpo devagaṇāḥ sarve āpaḥ pitṛgaṇāstathā
|
darśaśca paurṇamāsaśca devānāṃ pitṛbhiḥ saha||8||

9devatāḥ pitarastasmātpitaraścāpi devatāḥ
|
ekībhūtāśca dṛśyante pṛthaktvena ca parvasu||9||

10devatāḥ pitaraścaiva bhuñjate mayi yaddhutam
|
devatānāṃ pitṝṇāṃ ca mukhametadahaṃ smṛtam||10||

11amāvāsyāṃ hi pitaraḥ paurṇamāsyāṃ hi devatāḥ
|
manmukhenaiva hūyante bhuñjate ca hutaṃ haviḥ||11||

12sarvabhakṣaḥ kathaṃ tveṣāṃ bhaviṣyāmi mukhaṃ tvaham
|
sautiruvāca|
cintayitvā tato vahniścakre saṃhāramātmanaḥ||12||

13dvijānāmagnihotreṣu yajñasatrakriyāsu ca
|
niroṃkāravaṣaṭkārāḥ svadhāsvāhāvivarjitāḥ||13||

14vinā'gninā prajāḥ sarvāstata āsansuduḥkhitāḥ
|
atharṣayaḥ samudvignā devān gatvā bruvanvacaḥ||14||

15agnināśātkriyābhrāṃśādbhrāntā lokāstrayo'naghāḥ
|
vidadhvamatra yatkāryaṃ na syātkālātyayo yathā||15||

16atharṣayaśca devāśca brahmāṇamupagamya tu
|
agnerāvedayanśāpaṃ kriyāsaṃhārameva ca||16||

17bhṛguṇā vai mahābhāga śapto'gniḥ kāraṇāntare
|
kathaṃ devamukho bhūtvā yajñabhāgāgrabhuktathā||17||

18hutabhuksarvalokeṣu sarvabhakṣatvameṣyati
|
śrutvā tu tadvacasteṣāmagnimāhūya viśvakṛt||18||

19uvāca vacanaṃ ślakṣṇaṃ bhūtabhāvanamavyayam
|
lokānāmiha sarveṣāṃ tvaṃ kartā cānta eva ca||19||

20tvaṃ dhārayasi lokāṃstrīnkriyāṇāṃ ca pravartakaḥ
|
sa tathā kuru lokeśa nocchidyeranyathā kriyāḥ||20||

21kasmādevaṃ vimūḍhastvamīśvaraḥ san hutāśana
|
tvaṃ pavitraṃ sadā loke sarvabhūtagatiśca ha||21||

22na tvaṃ sarvaśarīreṇa sarvabhakṣatvameṣyasi
|
apāne hyarciṣo yāste sarvaṃ bhakṣyanti tāḥ śikhin||22||

23kravyādā ca tanuryāte sā sarvaṃ bhakṣayiṣyati
|
yathā sūryāṃśubhiḥ spṛṣṭaṃ sarvaṃ śuci vibhāvyate||23||

24tathā tvadarcirnirdagdhaṃ sarvaṃ śuci bhaviṣyati
|
tvamagne paramaṃ tejaḥ svaprabhāvādvinirgatam||24||

25svatejasaiva taṃ śāpaṃ kuru satyamṛṣervibho
|
devānāṃ cātmano bhāgaṃ gṛhāṇa tvaṃ mukhe hutam||25||

26sautiruvāca|
evamastviti taṃ vahniḥ pratyuvāca pitāmaham
|
jagāma śāsanaṃ kartuṃ devasya parameṣṭhinaḥ||26||

27devarṣayaśca muditāstato jagmuryathāgatam
|
ṛṣayaśca yathāpūrvaṃ kriyāḥ sarvāḥ pracakrire||27||

28divi devā mumudire bhūtasaṅghāśca laukikāḥ
|
agniśca paramāṃ prītimavāpa hatakalmaṣaḥ||28||

29evaṃ sa bhagavāñchāpaṃ lebhe'gnirbhṛgutaḥ purā
|
evameṣa purāvṛtta hatihāso'gniśāpajaḥ
|
pulomnaśca vināśo'yaṃ cyavanasya ca saṃbhavaḥ||29||
