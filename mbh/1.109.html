Mahābhārata 1.109 (Ādiparvan)

1vaiśaṃpāyana uvāca|
teṣu triṣu kumāreṣu jāteṣu kurujāṅgalam
|
kuravo'tha kurukṣetraṃ trayametadavardhata||1||

2ūrdhvasasyā'bhavadbhūmiḥ sasyāni phalavanti ca
|
yathartuvarṣī parjanyo bahupuṣpaphalā drumāḥ||2||

3vāhanāni prahṛṣṭāni muditā mṛgapakṣiṇaḥ
|
gandhavanti ca mālyāni rasavanti phalāni ca||3||

4vaṇigbhiścānvakīryanta nagarāṇyatha śilpibhiḥ
|
śūrāśca kṛtavidyāśca santaśca sukhino'bhavan||4||

5nābhavandasyavaḥ kecinnādharmarucayo janāḥ
|
pradeśeṣvapi rāṣṭrāṇāṃ kṛtaṃ yugamavartata||5||

6dharmakriyā yajñaśīlāḥ satyavrataparāyaṇāḥ
|
anyonyaprītisaṃyuktā vyavardhanta prajāstadā||6||

7mānakrodhavihīnāśca narā lobhavivarjitāḥ
|
anyonyamabhyanandanta dharmottaramavartata||7||

8tanmahodadhivatpūrṇaṃ nagaraṃ vai vyarocata
|
dvāratoraṇaniryūhairyuktamabhracayopamaiḥ||8||

9prasādaśatasaṃbādhaṃ mahendrapurasannibham
|
nadīṣu vanakhaṇḍeṣu vāpīpalvalasānuṣu
|
kānaneṣu ca ramyeṣu vijahrurmuditā janāḥ||9||

10uttaraiḥ kurubhiḥ sārdhaṃ dakṣiṇāḥ kuravastathā
|
vispardhamānā vyacaraṃstathā devarṣicāraṇaiḥ||10||

11nābhavatkṛpaṇaḥ kaścinnābhavanvidhavāḥ striyaḥ
|
tasmiñjanapade ramye kurubhirbahulīkṛte||11||

12kūpārāmasabhāvāpyo brāhmaṇāvasathāstathā
|
babhūvuḥ sarvardhiyutāstasminrāṣṭre sadotsavāḥ||12||

13bhīṣmeṇa dharmato rājansarvataḥ parirakṣite
|
babhūva ramaṇīyaśca caityayūpaśatāṅkitaḥ||13||

14sa deśaḥ pararāṣṭrāṇi vimṛjyābhipravardhitaḥ
|
bhīṣmeṇa vihitaṃ rāṣṭre dharmacakramavartata||14||

15kriyamāṇeṣu kṛtyeṣu kumārāṇāṃ mahātmanām
|
paurajānapadāḥ sarve babhūvuḥ paramotsukāḥ||15||

16gṛheṣu kurumukhyānāṃ paurāṇāṃ ca narādhipa
|
dīyatāṃ bhujyatāṃ ceti vāco'śrūyanta sarvaśaḥ||16||

17dhṛtarāṣṭraśca pāṇḍuśca viduraśca mahāmatiḥ
|
janmaprabhṛti bhīṣmeṇa putravatparipālitāḥ||17||

18saṃskāraiḥ saṃskṛtāste tu vratādhyayanasaṃyutāḥ
|
śramavyāyāmakuśalāḥ samapadyanta yauvanam||18||

19dhanurvede ca vede ca gadāyuddhe'si carmaṇi
|
tathaiva gajaśikṣāyāṃ nītiśāstreṣu pāragāḥ||19||

20itihāsapurāṇeṣu nānāśikṣāsu bodhitāḥ
|
vedavedāṅgatattvajñāḥ sarvatra kṛtaniścayāḥ||20||

21pāṇḍurdhanuṣi vikrānto nareṣvabhyadiko'bhavat
|
anyebhyo balavānāsīddhṛtarāṣṭro mahīpatiḥ||21||

22triṣu lokeṣu na tvāsītkaścidvidurasaṃmitaḥ
|
dharmanityastathā rājandharmaṃ ca paramaṃ gataḥ||22||

23pranaṣṭaṃ śāntanorvaṃśaṃ samīkṣya punaruddhṛtam
|
tato nirvacanaṃ loke sarvarāṣṭreṣvavartata||23||

24vīrasūnāṃ kāśisute deśānāṃ kurujāṅgalam
|
sarvadharmavidāṃ bhīṣmaḥ purāṇāṃ gajasāhvayam||24||

25dhṛtarāṣṭrastvacakṣuṣṭvādrajyaṃ na pratyapadyata
|
pārasavatvādviduro rājā pāṇḍurbabhūva ha||25||

26kadācidatha gāṅgeyaḥ sarvanītimatāṃ varaḥ
|
viduraṃ dharmatattvajñaṃ vākyamāha yathocitam||26||
