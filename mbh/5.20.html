Mahābhārata 5.20 (Udyogaparvan)

1vaiśampāyana uvāca|
sa ca kauravyamāsādya drupadasya purohitaḥ
|
satkṛto dhṛtarāṣṭreṇa bhīṣmeṇa vidureṇa ca ||1||

2sarvaṃ kauśalyamuktvā''dau pṛṣṭvā caivamanāmayam
|
sarvasenāpraṇetṝṇāṃ madhye vākyamuvāca ha ||2||

3sarvairbhavadbhirvidito rājadharmaḥ sanātanaḥ
|
vākyopādānahetostu vakṣyāmi vidite sati ||3||

4dhṛtarāṣṭraśca pāṇḍuśca sutāvekasya viśrutau
|
tayoḥ samānaṃ draviṇaṃ paitṛkaṃ nātra saṃśayaḥ ||4||

5dhṛtarāṣṭrasya ye putrāḥ prāptaṃ taiḥ paitṛkaṃ vasu
|
pāṇḍuputrāḥ kathaṃ nāma na prāptāḥ paitṛkaṃ vasu ||5||

6evaṃgate pāṇḍaveyairviditaṃ vaḥ purā yathā
|
na prāptaṃ paitṛkaṃ dravyaṃ dhṛtarāṣṭreṇa saṃvṛtam||6||

7prāṇāntikairapyupāyaiḥ prayatadbhiranekaśaḥ
|
śeṣavanto na śakitā netuṃ vai yamasādanam ||7||

8punaśca vardhitaṃ rājyaṃ svabalena mahātmabhiḥ
|
chadmanā'pahṛtaṃ kṣudrairdhārtarāṣṭraiḥ sasaubalaiḥ ||8||

9tadapyanumataṃ karma yathāyuktamanena vai
|
vāsitāśca mahāraṇye varṣāṇīha trayodaśa ||9||

10sabhāyāṃ kleśitairvīraiḥ sahabhāryaistathā bhṛśam
|
araṇye vividhāḥ kleśāḥ saṃprāptāstaiḥ sudāruṇāḥ||10||

11tathā virāṭanagare yonyantaragatairiva
|
prāptaḥ paramasaṃkleśo yathā pāpairmahātmabhiḥ ||11||

12te sarvaṃ pṛṣṭhataḥ kṛtvā tat sarvaṃ pūrvakilbiṣam
|
sāmaiva kurubhiḥ sardhamicchanti kurupuṅgavāḥ ||12||

13teṣāṃ ca vṛttamājñāya vṛttaṃ duryodhanasya ca
|
anunetumihārhanti dhārtarāṣṭraṃ suhṛjjanāḥ ||13||

14na hi te vigrahaṃ vīrāḥ kurvanti kurubhiḥ saha
|
avināśena lokasya kāṅkṣante pāṇḍavāḥ svakam ||14||

15yaścāpi dhārtarāṣṭrasya hetuḥ syādvigrahaṃ prati
|
sa ca heturna mantavyo balīyāṃsastathā hi te ||15||

16akṣauhiṇyaśca saptaiva dharmaputrasya saṅgatāḥ
|
yuyutsamānāḥ kurubhiḥ pratīkṣante'sya śāsanam ||16||

17apare puruṣavyāghrāḥ sahasrākṣauhiṇīsamāḥ
|
sātyakirbhīmasenaśca yamau ca sumahābalau ||17||

18ekādaśaitāḥ pṛtanā ekataśca samāgatāḥ
|
ekataśca mahābāhurbahurūpī dhanañjayaḥ ||18||

19yathā kirīṭī sarvābhyaḥ senābhyo vyatiricyate
|
evameva mahābāhurvāsudevo mahādyutiḥ ||19||

20bahulatvaṃ ca senānāṃ vikramaṃ ca kirīṭinaḥ
|
buddhimattvaṃ ca kṛṣṇasya buddhvā yudhyeta ko naraḥ ||20||

21te bhavanto yathādharmaṃ yathāsamayameva ca
|
prayacchantu pradātavyaṃ mā vaḥ kālo'tyagādayam ||21||
