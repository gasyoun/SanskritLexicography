Mahābhārata 13.40 (Anuśāsanaparvan)

1bhīṣma uvāca|
evameva mahābāho nātra mithyā'sti kiñcana
|
yathā bravīṣi kauravya nārīṃ prati janādhipa ||1||

2atra te vartayiṣyāmi itihāsaṃ purātanam
|
yathā rakṣā kṛtā pūrvaṃ vipulena mahātmanā ||2||

3pramadāśca yathā sṛṣṭā brahmaṇā bharatarṣabha
|
yadarthaṃ tacca te tāta pravakṣyāmi narādhipa ||3||

4na hi strībhyaḥ paraṃ putra pāpīyaḥ kiñcidasti vai
|
agnirhi pramadā dīpto māyāśca mayajā vibho ||4||

5kṣuradhārā viṣaṃ sarpo vahnirityekataḥ striyaḥ
|
prajā imā mahābāho dhārmikya iti naḥ śrutam ||5||

6svayaṃ gacchanti devatvaṃ tato devāniyādbhayam
|
athābhyagacchan devāste pitāmahamarindama ||6||

7nivedya mānasaṃ cāpi tūṣṇīmāsannadhomukhāḥ
|
teṣāmantargataṃ jñātvā devānāṃ sa pitāmahaḥ ||7||

8mānavānāṃ pramohārthaṃ kṛtyā nāryo'sṛjatprabhuḥ
|
pūrvasarge tu kaunteya sādhvyo nārya ihābhavan ||8||

9āsādhvyastu samutpannāḥ kṛtyāḥ sargātprajāpateḥ
|
tābhyaḥ kāmānyathākāmaṃ prādāddhi sa pitāmahaḥ ||9||

10tāḥ kāmalubdhāḥ pramadāḥ prabādhante narānsadā
|
krodhaṃ kāmasya deveśaḥ sahāyaṃ cāsṛjatprabhuḥ ||10||

11asajjanta prajāḥ sarvāḥ kāmakrodhavaśaṃ gatāḥ
|
na ca strīṇāṃ kriyāḥ kāściditi dharmo vyavasthitaḥ ||11||

12nirindriyā hyaśāstrāśca striyo'nṛtamiti śrutiḥ
|
śayyāsanamalaṃkāramannapānamanāryatām ||12||

13durvāgbhāvaṃ rāteṃ caiva dadau strībhyaḥ prajāpatiḥ
|
na tāsāṃ rakṣaṇaṃ śakyaṃ kartuṃ puṃsā kathaṃcana ||13||

14api viśvakṛtā tāta kutastu puruṣairiha
|
vācā ca vadhabaṃdhairvā kleśairvā vividhaistathā ||14||

15na śakyā rakṣituṃ nāryastā hi nityamasaṃyatāḥ
|
idaṃ tu puruṣavyāghra purastācchrutavānaham ||15||

16yathā rakṣā kutā pūrvaṃ vipulena gurustriyāḥ
|
ṛṣirāsīnmahābhāgo devaśarmeti viśrutaḥ ||16||

17tasya bhāryā rucirnāma rūpeṇā'sadṛśī bhuvi
|
tasyā rūpeṇa sammattā devagandharvadānavāḥ ||17||

18viśeṣeṇa tu rājendra vṛtrahā pākaśāsanaḥ
|
nārīṇāṃ caritajñaśca devaśarmā mahāmuniḥ ||18||

19yathāśakti yathotsāhaṃ bhāryāṃ tāmabhyarakṣata
|
purandaraṃ ca jānīte parastrīkāmacāriṇam ||19||

20tasmādbalena bhāryāyā rakṣaṇaṃ sa cakāra ha
|
sa kadācidṛṣistāta yajñaṃ kartumanāstadā ||20||

21bhāryāsaṃrakṣaṇaṃ kāryaṃ kathaṃ syādityacintayat
|
rakṣāvidhānaṃ manasā sa sañcintya mahātapāḥ ||21||

22āhūya dayitaṃ śiṣyaṃ vipulaṃ prāha bhārgavam
|
devaśarmovāca
|
yajñakāro gamiṣyāmi ruciṃ cemāṃ sureśvaraḥ ||22||

23yataḥ prārthayate nityaṃ tāṃ rakṣasva yathābalam
|
apramattena te bhāvyaṃ sadā prati purandaram ||23||

24sa hi rūpāṇi kurute vividhāni bhṛgūttama
|
bhīṣma uvāca|
ityukto vipulastena tapasvī niyatendriyaḥ ||24||

25sadaivogratapā rājannagnyarkasadṛśadyutiḥ
|
dharmajñaḥ satyavādī ca tatheti pratyabhāṣata ||25||

26punaścedaṃ mahārāja papraccha prasthitaṃ gurum
|
vipula uvāca|
kāni rūpāṇi śakrasya bhavantyāgacchato mune ||26||

27vapustejaśca kīdṛgvai tanme vyākhyātumarhasi
|
bhīṣma uvāca|
tataḥ sa bhagavāṃstasmai vipulāya mahātmane ||27||

28ācacakṣe yathātattvaṃ māyāṃ śakrasya bhārata
|
devaśarmovāca
|
bahumāyaḥ sa viprarṣe bhagavānpākaśāsanaḥ ||28||

29tāṃstānvikurute bhāvānbahūnatha muhurmuhuḥ
|
kirīṭī vajradhṛgdhanvī mukuṭī baddhakuṇḍalaḥ ||29||

30bhavatyatha muhūrtena caṇḍālasamadarśanaḥ
|
śikhī jaṭī cīravāsāḥ punarbhavati putraka ||30||

31bṛhaccharīraśca punaścīravāsāḥ punaḥ kuśaḥ
|
gauraṃ śyāmaṃ ca kṛṣṇaṃ ca varṇaṃ vikurute punaḥ ||31||

32virūpo rūpavāṃścaiva yuvā vṛddhastathaiva ca
|
brāhmaṇaḥ kṣatriyaścaiva vaiśyaḥ śūdrastathaiva ca ||32||

33pratilomo'nulomaśca bhavatyatha śatakratuḥ
|
śukravāyasarūpāṃ ca haṃsakokilarūpavān ||33||

34siṃhavyāghragajānāṃ ca rūpaṃ dhārayate punaḥ
|
daivaṃ daityamatho rājñāṃ vapurdhārayate'pi ca ||34||

35akṛśo vāyubhagnāṅgaḥ śakunirvikṛtastathā
|
catuṣpādbahurūpaśca punarbhavati bāliśaḥ ||35||

36makṣikāmaśakādīnāṃ vapurdhārayate'pi ca
|
na śakyamasya grahaṇaṃ kartuṃ vipula kenacit ||36||

37api viśvakṛtā tāta yena sṛṣṭamidaṃ jagat
|
punarantarhitaḥ śakro dṛśyate jñānacakṣuṣā ||37||

38vāyubhūtaśca sa punardevarājo bhavantyuta
|
evaṃ rūpāṇi satataṃ kurute pākaśāsanaḥ ||38||

39tasmādvipula yatnena rakṣamāṃ tanumadhyamām
|
yathā ruciṃ nāvaliheddevendro bhṛgusattama ||39||

40kratāvupahite nyastaṃ haviḥ śveva durātmavān
|
evamākhyāya sa muniryajñakāro'gamattadā ||40||

41devaśarmā mahābhāgastato bharatasattama
|
vipulastu vacaḥ śrutvā gurościntāmupeyivān ||41||

42rakṣāṃ ca paramāṃ cakre devarājānmahābalāt
|
kiṃ nu śakyaṃ mayā kartuṃ gurudārābhirakṣaṇe ||42||

43māyāvī hi surendrosau durdharṣaśvāpi vīryavān
|
nāpidhāyāśramaṃ śakyo rakṣituṃ pākaśāsanaḥ ||43||

44uṭajaṃ vā tathā hyasya nānāvidhasarūpatā
|
vāyu rūpeṇa vā śakro gurupatnīṃ pradharṣayet ||44||

45tasmādimāṃ saṃpraviśya ruciṃ sthāsyehamadya vai
|
athavā pauruṣeṇeyaṃ na śakyā rakṣituṃ mayā ||45||

46bahurūpo hi bhagavāñchrūyate pākaśāsanaḥ
|
so'haṃ yogabalādenāṃ rakṣiṣye pākaśāsanāt ||46||

47gātrāṇi gātrairasyāhaṃ saṃpravekṣye hi rakṣitum
|
yadyucchiṣṭāmi māṃ pātnīmadya paśyati me guruḥ ||47||

48śapsyatyasaṃśayaṃ kopāddivyajñāno mahātapāḥ
|
na ceyaṃ rakṣituṃ śakyā yathā'nyā pramadā nṛbhiḥ ||48||

49māyāvī hi surendro'sāvaho prāpto'smi saṃśayam
|
avaśyaṃ karaṇīyaṃ hi guroriha hi śāsanam ||49||

50yadi tvetadahaṃ kuryāmāścaryaṃ syātkṛtaṃ mayā
|
yogenātha praveśo hi gurupatnyāḥ kalevare ||50||

51evameva śarīre'syā nivatsyāmi samāhitaḥ
|
aśaktaḥ padmapatrastho jalabinduryathā calaḥ ||51||

52nirmuktasya rajorūpānnāparādho bhavenmama
|
yathā hi śūnyāṃ pathikaḥ sabhāmadhyāvasetpathi ||52||

53tathādyāvāsayiṣyāmi gurupatnyāḥ kalevaram
|
evameva śarīre'syā nivatsyāmi samāhitaḥ ||53||

54ityevaṃ dharmamālokya vedavedāṃśca sarvaśaḥ
|
tapaśca vipulaṃ dṛṣṭvā gurorātmana eva ca ||54||

55iti niścitya manasā rakṣāṃ prati sa bhārgavaḥ
|
anvatiṣṭhatparaṃ yatnaṃ yathā tacchṛṇu pārthiva ||55||

56gurupatnīṃ samāsīno vipulaḥ sa mahātapāḥ
|
upāsīnāmanindyāṅgīṃ yathārthe samalobhayat ||56||

57netrābhyāṃ netrayorasyā raśmiṃ saṃyojyaraśmibhiḥ
|
viveśa vipulaḥ kāyamākāśaṃ pavano yathā ||57||

58lakṣaṇaṃ lakṣaṇenaiva vadanaṃ vadanena ca
|
aviceṣṭannatiṣṭadvai chāyevāntahito muniḥ ||58||

59tato viṣṭabhya vipulo gurupatnyāḥ kalevaram
|
uvāsa rakṣaṇe yukto na ca sā tamabuddhyata ||59||

60yaṃ kālaṃ nāgato rājan gurustasya mahātmanaḥ
|
krartuṃ samāpya svagṛhaṃ taṃ kālaṃ so'bhyarakṣata ||60||
