Mahābhārata 3.180 (Āraṇyakaparvan)

1vaiśampāyana uvāca|
yudhiṣṭhirastamāsādya sarpabhogena veṣṭitam
|
dayitaṃ bhrātaraṃ dhīmānidaṃ vacanamabravīt||1||

2kuntīmātaḥ kathamimāmāpadaṃ tvamavāptavān
|
kaścāyaṃ parvatābhogapratimaḥ pannagottamaḥ||2||

3sa dharmarājamālakṣya bhrātā bhrātaramagrajam
|
kathayāmāsa tat sarvaṃ grahaṇādi viceṣṭitam||3||

4bhīma uvāca|
ayamārya mahāsattvo bhakṣārthaṃ māṃ gṛhītavān
|
nahuṣo nāma rājarṣiḥ prāṇavāniva saṃsthitaḥ||4||

5yudhiṣṭhira uvāca|
mucyatāmayamāyuṣman bhrātā me'mitavikramaḥ
|
vayamāhāramanyaṃ te dāsyāmaḥ kṣunnivāraṇam||5||

6sarpa uvāca|
āhāro rājaputro'yaṃ mayā prāpto mukhāgataḥ
|
gamyatāṃ neha sthātavyaṃ śvo bhavānapi me bhavet||6||

7vratametanmahābāho viṣayaṃ mama yo vrajet
|
sa me bhakṣo bhavet tāta tvaṃ cāpi viṣaye mama||7||

8cireṇādya mayā''hāraḥ prāpto'yamanujastava
|
nāhamenaṃ vimokṣyāmi na cānyamabhikāṅkṣaye||8||

9yudhiṣṭhira uvāca|
devo vā yadi vā daitya urago vā bhavān yadi
|
satyaṃ sarpa vaco brūhi pṛcchati tvāṃ yudhiṣṭhiraḥ
|
kimarthaṃ ca tvayā grasto bhīmaseno bhujaṅgama||9||

10kimāhṛtya viditvā vā prītiste syādbhujaṅgama
|
kimāhāraṃ prayacchāmi kathaṃ muñcedbhavānimam||10||

11sarpa uvāca|
nahuṣo nāma rājā'hamāsaṃ pūrvastavānagha
|
prathitaḥ pañcamaḥ somādāyoḥ putro narādhipa||11||

12kratubhistapasā caiva svādhyāyena damena ca
|
trailokyaiśvaryamavyagraṃ prāpto'haṃ vikrameṇa ca||12||

13tadaiśvaryaṃ samāsādya darpo māmagamattadā
|
sahasraṃ hi dvijātīnāmuvāha śibikāṃ mama||13||

14aiśvaryamadamatto'hamavamanya tato dvijān
|
imāmagastyena daśāmānītaḥ pṛthivīpate||14||

15na tu māmajahāt prajñā yāvadadyeti pāṇḍava
|
tasyaivānugrahādrājannagastyasya mahātmanaḥ||15||

16ṣaṣṭhe kāle mayā''hāraḥ prāpto'yamanujastava
|
nāhamenaṃ vimokṣyāmi na cānyadapi kāmaye||16||

17praśnānuccāritānadya vyāhariṣyāsa cenmama
|
atha paścādvimokṣyāmi bhrātaraṃ te vṛkodaram||17||

18yudhiṣṭhira uvāca|
brūhi sarpa yathākāmaṃ prativakṣyāmi te vacaḥ
|
āpa cecchaknuyāṃ prītimāhartuṃ te bhujaṅgama||18||

19vedyaṃ ca brāhmaṇeneha tadbhavān vetti kevalam
|
sarparāja tataḥ śrutvā prativakṣyāmi te vacaḥ||19||

20sarpa uvāca|
brāhmaṇaḥ ko bhavedrājan vedyaṃ kiṃ ca yudhiṣṭhira
|
bravīhyatimatiṃ tvāṃ hi vākyairanubhimīmahe||20||

21yudhiṣṭhira uvāca|
satyaṃ dānaṃ kṣamā kṣīlamānṛśaṃsyaṃ tapo ghṛṇā
|
dṛśyante yatra nāgendra sa brāhmaṇa iti smṛtaḥ||21||

22vedyaṃ sarpa paraṃ brahma nirduḥkhamasukhaṃ ca yat
|
yatra gatvā na śocanti bhavataḥ kiṃ vivakṣitam||22||

23sarpa uvāca|
cāturvarṇyaṃ pramāṇaṃ ca satyaṃ ca brahma caiva hi
|
śudreṣvapi ca satyaṃ ca dānamakrodha eva ca
|
ānṛśaṃsyamahiṃsā ca ghṛṇā caiva yudhiṣṭhira||23||

24vedyaṃ yaccātra nirduḥkhamasukhaṃ ca narādhipa
|
tābhyāṃ hīnaṃ padaṃ cānyanna tadastīti lakṣaye||24||

25yudhiṣṭhira uvāca|
śūdre tu yadbhavellakṣma dvije tacca na vidyate
|
na vai śūdro bhavecchūdro brāhmaṇo na ca brāhmaṇaḥ||25||

26yatraitallakṣyate sarpa vṛttaṃ sa brāhmaṇaḥ smṛtaḥ
|
yatraitanna bhavet sarpa taṃ śuddhamiti nirdiśet||26||

27yat punarbhavatā proktaṃ na vedyaṃ vidyatīti ca
|
tābhyāṃ hīnamato'nyatra padaṃ nāstīti cedapi||27||

28evametanmataṃ sarpa tābhyāṃ hīnaṃ na vidyate
|
yathā śītoṣṇayormadhye bhavennoṣṇaṃ na śītatā||28||

29evaṃ vai sukhaduḥkhābhyāṃ hīnaṃ nāsti padaṃ kvacit
|
eṣā mama matiḥ sarpa yathā vā manyate bhavān||29||

30sarpa uvāca|
yadi te vṛttato rājan brāhmaṇaḥ prasamīkṣitaḥ
|
vṛthā jātistadā''yuṣman kṛtiryāvanna vidyate||30||

31yudhiṣṭhira uvāca|
jātiratra mahāsarpa manuṣyatve mahāmate
|
saṅkarāt sarvavarṇānāṃ duṣparīkṣyeti me matiḥ||31||

32sarve sarvāsvapatyāni janayanti sadā narāḥ
|
vāṅmaithunamatho janma maraṇaṃ ca samaṃ nṛṇām||32||

33idamārṣaṃ pramāṇaṃ ca ye yajāmaha ityapi
|
tasmācchīlaṃ pradhāneṣṭaṃ vidurye tattvadarśinaḥ||33||

34prāṅnābhivardhanāt puṃso jātakarma vidhīyate
|
tatrāsya mātā sāvitrī pitā tvācārya ucyate||34||

35tāvacchūdrasamo hyeṣa yāvadvede na jāyate
|
tasminnevaṃ matidvaidhe manuḥ svāyambhuvo'bravīt||35||

36kṛtakṛtyāḥ punarvarṇā yadi vṛttaṃ na vidyate
|
saṅkarastvatra nāgendra balavān prasamīkṣitaḥ||36||

37yatredānīṃ mahāsarpa saṃskṛtaṃ vṛttamiṣyate
|
taṃ brāhmaṇamahaṃ pūrvamuktavān bhujagottama||37||

38sarpa uvāca|
śrutaṃ viditavedyasya tava vākyaṃ yudhiṣṭhira
|
bhakṣayeyamahaṃ kasmādbhrātaraṃ te vṛkodaram||38||
