Mahābhārata 1.186 (Ādiparvan)

1dhṛṣṭadyumna uvāca|
duryodhano durviṣaho durmukho duṣpradharṣaṇaḥ
|
viviṃśatirvikarṇaśca saho duḥśāsanastathā||1||

2yuyutsurvāyuvegaśca bhīmavegaravastathā
|
ugrāyudho balākī ca karakāyurvirocanaḥ||2||

3kuṇḍakaścitrasenaśca suvarcāḥ kanakadhvajaḥ
|
nandako bāhuśālī ca tuhuṇḍo vikaṭastathā||3||

4ete cānye ca bahavo dhārtarāṣṭrā mahābalāḥ
|
karṇena sahitā vīrāstvadarthaṃ samupāgatāḥ||4||

5asaṅkhyātā mahātmānaḥ pārthivāḥ kṣatriyarṣabhāḥ
|
śakuniḥ saubalaścaiva vṛṣako'tha bṛhadbalaḥ||5||

6ete gāndhārarājasya sutāḥ sarve samāgatāḥ
|
aśvatthāmā ca bhojaśca sarvaśastrabhṛtāṃ varau||6||

7samavetau mahātmānau tvadarthe samalaṅkṛtau
|
bṛhanto maṇimāṃścaiva daṇḍadhāraśca pārthivaḥ||7||

8sahadevajayatsenau meghasandhiśca pārthivaḥ
|
virāṭaḥ saha putrābhyāṃ śaṅkhenaivottareṇa ca||8||

9vārdhakṣemiḥ suśarmā ca senābinduśca pārthivaḥ
|
suketuḥ saha putreṇa sunāmnā ca suvarcasā||9||

10sucitraḥ sukumāraśca vṛkaḥ satyadhṛtistathā
|
sūryadhvajo rocamāno nīlaścitrāyudhastathā||10||

11aṃśumāṃścekitānaśca śreṇimāṃśca mahābalaḥ
|
samudrasenaputraśca candrasenaḥ pratāpavān||11||

12jalasandhaḥ pitāputrau vidaṇḍo daṇḍa eva ca
|
pauṇḍrako vāsudevaśca bhagadattaśca vīryavān||12||

13kaliṅgastāmraliptaśca pattanādhipatistathā
|
madrarājastathā śalyaḥ sahaputro mahārathaḥ||13||

14rukmāṅgadena vīreṇa tathā rukmarathena ca
|
kauravyaḥ somadattaśca putraścāsya mahārathaḥ||14||

15samavetāstrayaḥ śūrā bhūrirbhūriśravāḥ śalaḥ
|
sudakṣiṇaśca kāmbojo dṛḍhadhanvā ca pauravaḥ||15||

16bṛhadbalaḥ suṣeṇaśca śibirauśīnastathā
|
paṭaccaranihantā ca kārūṣādhipatistathā||16||

17saṅkarṣaṇo vāsudevo raukmiṇeyaśca vīryavān
|
sāmbaśca cārudeṣṇaśca prādyumniḥ sagadastathā||17||

18akrūraḥ sātyakiścaiva uddhavaśca mahāmatiḥ
|
kṛtavarmā ca hārdikyaḥ pṛthurvipṛthureva ca||18||

19vidūrathaśca kaṅkaśca śaṅkuśca sagaveṣaṇaḥ
|
āśāvaho'niruddhaśca samīkaḥ sārimejayaḥ||19||

20vīro vātapatiścaiva jhillī piṇḍārakastathā
|
uśīnaraśca vikrānto vṛṣṇayaste prakīrtitāḥ||20||

21bhagīratho bṛhatkṣatraḥ saindhavaśca jayadrathaḥ
|
bṛhadratho bāhlikaśca śrutāyuśca mahārathaḥ||21||

22ulūkaḥ kaitavo rājā citrāṅgadaśubhāṅgadau
|
vatsarājaśca matimānkosalādhipatistathā||22||

23śiśupālaśca vikrānto jarāsandhastathaiva ca
|
ete cānye ca bahavo nānājanapadeśvarāḥ||23||

24tvadarthamāgatā bhadre kṣatriyāḥ prathitā bhuvi
|
ete bhetsyanti vikrāntāstvadarthe lakṣyamuttamam
|
vidhyate ya idaṃ lakṣyaṃ varayethāḥ śubhe'dya tam||24||
