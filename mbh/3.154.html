Mahābhārata 3.154 (Āraṇyakaparvan)

1bhīma uvāca|
āṇḍavo bhīmaseno'haṃ dharmarājādanantaraḥ
|
viśālāṃ badarīṃ prāpto bhrātṛbhiḥ saha rākṣasāḥ ||1||

2apaśyattatra pāñcālī saugandhikamanuttamam
|
aniloḍhamito nūnaṃ sā bahūni parīpsati ||2||

3tasyā māmanavadyāṅgyā dharmapatnyāḥ priye sthitam
|
puṣpāhāramiha prāptaṃ nibodhata niśācarāḥ || 3||

4rākṣasā ūcuḥ|
ākrīḍo'yaṃ kuberasya dayitaḥ puruṣarṣabha
|
neha śakyaṃ manuṣyeṇa vihartuṃ martyadharmaṇā ||4||

5devarṣayastathā yakṣā devāścātra vṛkodara
|
āmantrya yakṣapravaraṃ pibanti ramayanti ca
|
gandharvāpsarasaścaiva viharantyatra pāṇḍava ||5||

6anyāyeneha yaḥ kaścidavamanya dhaneśvaram
|
vihartumiccheddurvṛttaḥ sa vinaśyenna saṃśayaḥ ||6||

7tamanādṛtya padmāni jihīrṣasi balādṛtaḥ
|
dharmarājasya cātmānaṃ bravīṣi bhrātaraṃ katham ||7||

8āmantrya yakṣarājaṃ vai tataḥ piba harasva ca
|
nāto'nyathā tvayā śakyaṃ kiṃcitpuṣkaramīkṣitum||8||

9bhīmasena uvāca|
rākṣasāstaṃ na paśyāmi dhaneśvaramihāntike
|
dṛṣṭāpi ca mahārājaṃ nāhaṃ yācitumutsahe ||9||

10na hi yācanti rājāna eṣa dharmaḥ sanātanaḥ
|
na cāhaṃ hātumicchāmi kṣātradharmaṃ kathaṃcana ||10||

11iyaṃ ca nalinī ramyā jātā parvatanirjhare
|
neyaṃ bhavanamāsādya kuberasya mahātmanaḥ ||11||

12tulyā hi sarvabhūtānāmiyaṃ vaiśravaṇasya ca
|
evaṃ gateṣu dravyeṣu kaḥ kaṃ yācitumarhati ||12||

13vaiśaṃpāyana uvāca|
ityuktvā rākṣasānsarvānbhīmaseno hyamarṣaṇaḥ
|
vyagāhata mahābāhurnalinīṃ tāṃ mahābalaḥ ||13||

14tataḥ sa rākṣasairvācā pratiṣiddhaḥ pratāpavān
|
mā maivamiti sakrodhairbhartsayadbhiḥ samantataḥ ||14||

15kadarthīkṛtya tu sa tānrākṣasānbhīmavikramaḥ
|
vyagāhata mahāteḥste taṃ sarve nyavārayan ||15||

16gṛhṇīta pradhnīta vikartatemaṃ pacāma khādāma ca bhīmasenam
|
kruddhā bruvanto'bhiyayurdrutaṃ te śastrāṇi codyamya vivṛtatanetrāḥ ||16||

17tataḥ sa gurvīṃ yamadaṇḍakalpāṃ mahāgadāṃ kāñcanapaṭṭanaddhām
|
pragṛhya tānabhyapatattarasvī tato'bravīttiṣṭhata tiṣṭhateti ||17||

18te taṃ tadā tobharapaṭṭiśādyairvyāviddhaśastraiḥ sahasā nipetuḥ
|
jighāṃsavaḥ krodhavaśāḥ subhīmā bhīmaṃ samantāt parivavurugrāḥ ||18||

19ghātena kuntyāṃ balavān sujātaḥ śūrastarasvī dviṣatāṃ nihantā
|
satye ca dharme ca rataḥ sadaiva parākrame śatrubhirapradhṛṣyaḥ ||19||

20teṣāṃ sa mārgān vividhān mahātmā vihanya śastrāṇi ca śātravāṇām
|
yathā pravīrānnijaghāna bhīmaḥ paraṃ śataṃ puṣkariṇīsamīpe ||20||

21te tasya vīryaṃ ca balaṃ ca dṛṣṭvā vidyābalaṃ bāhubalaṃ tathaiva
|
aśaknuvantaḥ sahitaṃ samantāddrutaṃ pravīrāḥ sahasā nivṛttāḥ ||21||

22vidīryamāṇāstata eva hantumākāśamāsthāya vimūḍhasaṃjñāḥ
|
kailāsaśṛṅgāṇyabhidudruvuste bhīmārditāḥ krodhavaśāḥ prabhagnāḥ||22||

23sa śakravaddānavadaityasaṅghān vikramya jitvā ca raṇe'risaṅghān
|
vigāhya tāṃ puṣkariṇīṃ jitāriḥ kāmāya jagrāha tato'mbujāni ||23||

24tataḥ sa pītvā'mṛtakalpamambho bhūyo babhūvottamavīryatejāḥ
|
utpāṭya jagrāha ca so'mbujāni saugandhikānyuttamagandhavanti ||24||

25tatastu te krodhavaśāḥ sametya dhaneśvaraṃ bhīmabalapraṇunnāḥ
|
bhīmasya vīryaṃ ca balaṃ ca saṅkhye yathāvadācakhyuratīva bhītāḥ ||25||

26teṣāṃ vacastattu niśamya devaḥ prahasya rakṣāṃsi tato'bhyuvāca
|
gṛhṇātu bhīmo jalajāni kāmāt kṛṣṇānimittaṃ viditaṃ mamaitat ||26||

27tato'bhyanujñāpya dhaneśvaraṃ te jagmuḥ kurūṇāṃ pravaraṃ viroṣāḥ
|
bhīmaṃ ca tasyāṃ dadṛśurnalinyāṃ yathopajoṣaṃ viharantamekam ||27||
