Mahābhārata 14.63 (Āśvamedhikaparvan)

1janamejaya uvāca|
śrutvaitadvacanaṃ brahman vyāsenoktaṃ mahātmanā
|
aśvamedhaṃ prati tadā kiṃ bhūyaḥ pracakāra ha||1||

2ratnaṃ ca yanmaruttena nihitaṃ vasudhātale
|
tadavāpa kathañceti tanme brūhi dvijottama||2||

3vaiśampāyana uvāca|
śrutvā dvaipāyanavaco dharmarājo yudhiṣṭhiraḥ
|
bhrātṝnsarvānsamānāyya kāle vacanamabravīt||3||

4arjunaṃ bhīmasenaṃ ca mādrīputrau yamāvapi
|
śrutaṃ vo vacanaṃ vīrāḥ sauhṛdādyanmahātmanā||4||

5kurūṇāṃ hitakāmena proktaṃ kṛṣṇena dhīmatā
|
tapovṛddhena mahatā suhṛdāṃ bhūtimicchatā||5||

6guruṇā dharmaśīlena vyāsenādbhutakarmaṇā
|
bhīṣmeṇa ca mahāprājña govindena ca dhīmatā||6||

7saṃsmṛtya tadahaṃ samyakkartumicchāmi pāṇḍavāḥ
|
āyatyāṃ ca tadātve ca sarveṣāṃ taddhi no hitam||7||

8anubandhe ca kalyāṇaṃ yadvaco brahmavādinaḥ
|
iyaṃ hi vasudhā sarvā kṣīṇaratnā kurudvahāḥ||8||

9taccācaṣṭa tadā vyāso maruttasya dhanaṃ nṛpāḥ
|
yadyetadvo bahumataṃ manyadhvaṃ vā kṣamaṃ yadi||9||

10tathā yathā''ha dharmeṇa kathaṃ vā bhīma manyase
|
ityuktavākye nṛpatau tadā kurukulodvaha||10||

11bhīmaseno nṛpaśreṣṭhaṃ prāñjalirvākyamabravīt
|
rocate me mahābāho yadidaṃ bhāṣitaṃ tvayā||11||

12vyāsākhyātasya vittasya samupānayanaṃ prati
|
yadi tatprāpnuyāmeha dhanamāvikṣitaṃ prabho||12||

13kṛtameva mahārāja bhavediti matirmama
|
te vayaṃ praṇipātena girīśasya mahātmanaḥ||13||

14tadānayāma bhadraṃ te samabhyarcya kapardinam
|
tadvittaṃ devadeveśaṃ tasyaivānucarāṃśca tān||14||

15prasādyārthamavāpsyāmo nūnaṃ vāgbuddhikarmabhiḥ
|
rakṣante ye ca taddravyaṃ kinnarā raudradarśanāḥ||15||

16te ca vaśyā bhaviṣyanti prasanne vṛṣabhadhvaje
|
śrutvaivaṃ vadatastasya vākyaṃ bhīmasya bhārata||16||

17prīto dharmātmajo rājā babhūvātīva bhārata
|
arjunapramukhāścāpi tathetyevābruvanvacaḥ||17||

18kṛtvā tu pāṇḍavāḥ sarve ratnāharaṇaniścayam
|
senāmājñāpayāmāsurnakṣatre'hani ca dhruve||18||

19tato yayuḥ pāṇḍusutā brāhmaṇānsvasti vācya ca
|
arcayitvā suraśreṣṭhaṃ pūrvameva maheśvaram||19||

20modakaiḥ pāyasenātha māṃsāpūpaistathaiva ca
|
āśāsya ca mahātmānaṃ prayayurmuditā bhṛśam||20||

21teṣāṃ prayāsyatāṃ tatra maṅgalāni śubhānyatha
|
prāhuḥ prahṛṣṭamanaso dvijāgryā nāgarāśca te||21||

22tataḥ pradakṣiṇīkṛtya śirobhiḥ praṇipatya ca
|
brāhmaṇānagnisahitānprayayuḥ pāṇḍunandanāḥ||22||

23samanujñāpya rājānaṃ putraśokasamāhatam
|
dhṛtarāṣṭraṃ sabhāryaṃ vai pṛthāṃ ca pṛthulocanām||23||

24mūle nikṣipya kauravyaṃ yuyutsuṃ dhṛtarāṣṭrajam
|
sampūjyamānāḥ pauraiśca brāhmaṇaiśca manīṣibhiḥ||24||
