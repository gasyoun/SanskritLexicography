Mahābhārata 3.70 (Āraṇyakaparvan)

1bṛhadaśva uvāca| atha dīrghasya kālasya parṇādo nāma vai dvijaḥ
|
pratyetya nagaraṃ bhaimīmidaṃ vacanamabravīt||1||

2naiṣadhaṃ mṛgayāṇena damayanti mayā nalam
|
ayodhyāṃ nagarīṃ gatvā bhāṅgāsurimupasthitaḥ||2||

3śrāvitaśca mayā vākyaṃ tvadīyaṃ sa mahājane
|
ṛtuparṇo mahābhāgo yathoktaṃ varavarṇini||3||

4tacchrutvā nābravītkiṃcidṛtuparṇo narādhipaḥ
|
na ca pāriṣadaḥ kaścidbhāṣyamāṇo mayā'sakṛt||4||

5anujñātaṃ tu māṃ rājñā vijane kaścidabravīt
|
ṛtuparṇasya puruṣo bāhuko nāma nāmataḥ||5||

6sūtastasya narendrasya virūpo hrasvabāhukaḥ
|
śīghrayāneṣu kuśalo mṛṣṭakartā ca bhojane||6||

7sa viniḥśvasya bahuśo ruditvā ca punaḥpunaḥ
|
kuśalaṃ caiva māṃ pṛṣṭvā paścādidamabhāṣata||7||

8vaiṣamyamapi saṃprāptā gopāyanti kulastriyaḥ
|
ātmānamātmanā satyo jitaḥ svargo na saṃśayaḥ||8||

9rahitā bhartṛbhiścaiva na kupyanti kadācana
|
prāṇāṃścāritrakavacāndhārayanti varastriyaḥ||9||

10viṣamasthena mūḍhena paribhraṣṭasukhena ca
|
yatsā tena parityaktā tatra na kroddhumarhati||10||

11prāṇayātrāṃ pariprepsoḥ śakunairhṛtavāsasaḥ
|
ādhibhirdahyamānasya śyāmā na kroddhumarhati||11||

12satkṛtā'satkṛtā vāpi patiṃ dṛṣṭvā tathāgatam
|
bhraṣṭarājyaṃ śriyāhīnaṃ kṣudhitaṃ vyasanāplutam||12||

13tasya tadvacanaṃ śrutvā tvarito'hamihāgataḥ
|
śrutvā pramāṇaṃ bhavatī rājñaścaiva nivedaya||13||

14etacchrutvāśrupūrṇākṣī parṇādasya viśāṃpate
|
damayantī rahobhyetya mātaraṃ pratyabhāṣata||14||

15ayamartho na saṃvedyo bhīme mātaḥ kadācana
|
tvatsannidhau niyokṣyehaṃ sudevaṃ dvijasattamam||15||

16yathā na nṛpatirbhīmaḥ pratipadyeta me matam
|
tathā tvayā prakartavyaṃ mama cetpriyamicchasi||16||

17yathā cāhaṃ samānītā sudevenāśu bāndhavān
|
tenaiva maṅgalenādya sudevo yātu mā ciram||17||

18samānetuṃ nalaṃ mātarayodhyāṃ nagarīmitaḥ
|
viśrāntaṃ tu tataḥ paścātparṇādaṃ dvijasattamam||18||

19arcayāmāsa vaidarbhī dhanenātīva bhāminī
|
nale cehāgate tatra bhūyo dāsyābhi te vasu||19||

20tvayā hi me bahukṛtaṃ yadanyo na kariṣyati
|
yadbhartrāhaṃ sameṣyāmi śīghrameva dvijottama||20||

21sa evamukto'thāśvāsya āśīrvādaiḥ sumaṅgalaiḥ
|
gṛhānupayayau cāpi kṛtārthaḥ sumahāmanāḥ||21||

22tataḥ sudevamābhāṣya damayantī yudhiṣṭhira
|
abravītsannidau māturduḥkhaśokasamanvitā||22||

23gatvā sudeva nagarīmayodhyāvāsinaṃ nṛpam
|
ṛtuparṇaṃ vaco brūhi saṃpatanniva kāmagaḥ||23||

24āsthāsyati punarbhaimī damayantī svayaṃvaram
|
tatra gacchanti rājāno rājaputrāśca sarvaśaḥ||24||

25tathā ca gaṇitaḥ kālaḥ śvobhūte sa bhaviṣyati
|
yadi saṃbhāvinīyaṃ te gaccha śīghramariṃdama||25||

26sūryodaye dvatīyaṃ sā bhartāraṃ varayiṣyati
|
na hi sa jñāyate vīro nalo jīvati vā na vā||26||

27evaṃ tayā yathokto vai gatvā rājānamabravīt
|
ṛtuparṇaṃ mahārāja sudevo brāhmaṇastadā||27||
