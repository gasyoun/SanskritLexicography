Mahābhārata 1.110 (Ādiparvan)

1bhīṣma uvāca|
guṇaiḥ samuditaṃ samyagidaṃ naḥ prathitaṃ kulam
|
atyanyānpṛthivīpālānpṛthivyāmadhirājyabhāk||1||

2rakṣitaṃ rājabhiḥ pūrvaṃ dharmavidbhirmahātmabhiḥ
|
notsādamagamaccedaṃ kadācidiha naḥ kulam||2||

3mayā ca satyavatyā ca kṛṣṇena ca mahātmanā
|
samavasthāpitaṃ bhūyo yuṣmāsu kulatantuṣu||3||

4taccaitadvardhate bhūyaḥ kulaṃ sāgaravadyathā
|
tathā mayā vidhātavyaṃ tvayā caiva na saṃśayaḥ||4||

5śrūyate yādavī kanyā svanurūpā kulasya naḥ
|
subalasyātmajā caiva tathā madreśvarasya ca||5||

6kulīnā rūpavatyaśca tāḥ kanyāḥ putra sarvaśaḥ
|
ucitāścaiva saṃbandhe te'smākaṃ kṣatriyarṣabhāḥ||6||

7manye varayitavyāstā ityahaṃ dhīmatāṃ vara
|
santānārthaṃ kulasyāsya yadvā vidura manyase||7||

8vidura uvāca|
bhavānpitā bhāvanmātā bhavānnaḥ paramo guruḥ
|
tasmātsvayaṃ kulasyāsya vicārya kuru yaddhitam||8||

9vaiśaṃpāyana uvāca|
atha śuśrāva viprebhyo gāndhārīṃ subalātmajām
|
ārādhya varadaṃ devaṃ bhaganetraharaṃ haram||9||

10gāndhārī kila putrāṇāṃ śataṃ lebhe varaṃ śubhā
|
iti śuśrāva tattvena bhīṣmaḥ kurupitāmahaḥ||10||

11tato gāndhārarājasya preṣayāmāsa bhārata
|
acakṣuriti tatrāsītsubalasya vicāraṇā||11||

12kulaṃ khyātiṃ ca vṛttaṃ ca buddhyā tu prasamīkṣya saḥ
|
dadai tāṃ dhṛtarāṣṭrāya gāndhārīṃ dharmacāriṇīm||12||

13gāndhārī tvatha śuśrāva dhṛtarāṣṭramacakṣuṣam
|
ātmānaṃ dipsitaṃ cāsmai pitrā mātrā ca bhārata||13||

14tataḥ sā paṭṭamādāya kṛtvā bahuguṇaṃ tadā
|
babandha netre sve rājanpativrataparāyaṇā||14||

15nābhyasūyāṃ patimahamityevaṃ kṛtaniścayā
|
tato gāndhārarājasya putraḥ śakunirabhyayāt||15||

16svasāraṃ parayā lakṣmyā yuktāmādāya kauravān
|
tāṃ tadā dhṛtarāṣṭrāya dadau paramasatkṛtām
|
bhīṣmasyānumate caiva vivāhaṃ samakārayat||16||

17dattvā sa bhaginīṃ vīro yathārhaṃ ca paricchadam
|
punarāyātsvanagaraṃ bhīṣmeṇa pratipūjitaḥ||17||

18gāndhāryapi varārohā śīlācāraviciṣeṭitaiḥ
|
tuṣṭiṃ kurūṇāṃ sarveṣāṃ janayāmāsa bhārata||18||

19vṛttenārādhya tānsarvāngurūnpatiparāyaṇā
|
vācāpi puruṣānanyānsuvratā nānvakīrtayat||19||
