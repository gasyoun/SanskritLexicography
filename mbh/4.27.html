Mahābhārata 4.27 (Virāṭaparvan)

1vaiśampāyana uvāca|athābravīnmahāvīryo droṇastattvārthadarśivān
|
na tādṛśā vinaśyanti na prayānti parābhavam ||1||

2śūrāśca kṛtavidyāśca buddhimanto jitendriyāḥ
|
dharmajñāśca kṛtajñāśca dharmarājamanuvratāḥ ||2||

3nītidharmārthatattvajñaṃ pitṛvacca samāhitam
|
dharme sthitaṃ satyadhṛtiṃ jyeṣṭhaṃ jyeṣṭhānuyāyinaḥ ||3||

4anuvratā mahātmānaṃ bhrātaro bhrātaraṃ nṛpa
|
ajātaśatruṃ śrīmantaṃ sarvabhrātṝnanuvratam ||4||

5teṣāṃ tathā vidheyānāṃ nibhṛtānāṃ mahātmanām
|
kimarthaṃ nītimān pārthaḥ śreyo naiṣāṃ kariṣyati ||5||

6tasmādyatnāt pratīkṣante kālasyodayamāgatam
|
na hi te nāśamṛccheyuriti paśyāmyahaṃ dhiyā ||6||

7sāmprataṃ caiva yatkāryaṃ tacca kṣipramakālikam
|
kriyatāṃ sādhu sañcintya vāsaścaiṣāṃ pracintyatām ||7||

8yathāvat pāṇḍuputrāṇāṃ sarvārtheṣu dhṛtātmanām
|
durjñeyāḥ khalu śūrāste durāpāstapasā vṛtāḥ ||8||

9śuddhātmā guṇavān pārthaḥ satyavānnītimān śuciḥ
|
tejorāśirasaṅkhyeyo gṛhṇīyādapi cakṣuṣā ||9||

10vijñāya kriyatāṃ tasmādbhūyaśca mṛgayāmahe
|
brāhmaṇaiścārakaiḥ siddhairye cānye tadvido janāḥ ||10||
