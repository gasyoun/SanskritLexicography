Mahābhārata 5.165 (Udyogaparvan)

1dhṛtarāṣṭra uvāca|
pratijñāte phālgunena vadhe bhīṣmasya saṃyuge
|
kimakurvata me mandāḥ putrā duryodhanādayaḥ ||1||

2hatameva hi paśyāmi gāṅgeyaṃ pitaraṃ raṇe
|
vāsudevasahāyena pārthena dṛḍhadhanvanā ||2||

3sa cāparimitaprajñastacchrutvā pārthabhāṣitam
|
kimuktavān maheṣvāso bhīṣmaḥ praharatāṃ varaḥ ||3||

4saināpatyaṃ ca saṃprāpya kauravāṇāṃ dhurandharaḥ
|
kimaceṣṭata gāṅgeyo mahābuddhiparākramaḥ ||4||

5vaiśampāyana uvāca|
tatastatsañjayastasmai sarvameva nyavedayat
|
yathoktaṃ kuruvṛddhena bhīṣmeṇāmitatejasā ||5||

6sañjaya uvāca|
saināpatyamanuprāpya bhīṣmaḥ śāntanavo nṛpa
|
duryodhanamuvācedaṃ vacanaṃ harṣayanniva ||6||

7namaskṛtya kumārāya senānye śaktipāṇaye
|
ahaṃ senāpatiste'dya bhaviṣyāmi na saṃśayaḥ ||7||

8senākarmaṇyabhijño'smi vyūheṣu vividheṣu ca
|
karma kārayituṃ caiva bhṛtānapyabhṛtāṃstathā ||8||

9yātrāyāne ca yuddhe ca tathā praśamaneṣu ca
|
bhṛśaṃ veda mahārāja yathā veda bṛhaspatiḥ ||9||

10vyūhānāṃ ca samāraṃbhān daivagāndharvamānuṣān
|
tairahaṃ mohayiṣyāmi pāṇḍavān vyetu te jvaraḥ ||10||

11so'haṃ yotsyāmi tattvena pālayaṃstava vāhinīm
|
yathāvacchāstrato rājan vyetu te mānaso jvaraḥ ||11||

12duryodhana uvāca|
vidyate me na gāṅgeya bhayaṃ devāsureṣvapi
|
samasteṣu mahābāho satyametadbravīmi te ||12||

13kiṃ punastvayi durdharṣe saināpatye vyavasthite
|
droṇe ca puruṣavyāghre sthite yuddhābhinandini ||13||

14bhavadbhyāṃ puruṣāgryābhyāṃ sthitābhyāṃ vijaye mama
|
na durlabhaṃ kuruśreṣṭha devarājyamapi dhruvam ||14||

15rathasaṃkhyāṃ tu kārtsnyena pareṣāmātmanastathā
|
tathaivātirathānāṃ ca vettumicchāmi kaurava ||15||

16pitāmaho hi kuśalaḥ peraṣāmātmanastathā
|
śrotumicchāmyahaṃ sarvaiḥ sahaibhirvasudhādhipaiḥ ||16||

17bhīṣma uvāca|
gāndhāre śṛṇu rājendra rathasaṃkhyāṃ svake bale
|
ye rathāḥ pṛthivīpāla tathaivātirathāśca ye ||17||

18bahūnīha sahastrāṇi prayutānyarbudāni ca
|
rathānāṃ tava senāyāṃ yathāmukhyaṃ tu me śrṛṇu ||18||

19bhavānagre rathodāraḥ saha sarvaiḥ sahodaraiḥ
|
duḥśāsanaprabhṛtibhirbhātṛbhiḥ śatasaṃmitaiḥ ||19||

20sarve kṛtapraharaṇāścheda‌bhedaviśāradāḥ
|
rathopasthe gajaskandhe gadāprāsāsicarmaṇi ||20||

21saṃyantāraḥ prahartāraḥ kṛtāstrā bhārasādhanāḥ
|
iṣvastre droṇaśiṣyāśca kṛpasya ca śaradvataḥ ||21||

22ete haniṣyanti raṇe pañcālānyuddhadurmadān
|
kṛtakilviṣāḥ pāṇḍaveyairdhārtarāṣṭrā manasvinaḥ ||22||

23tathā'haṃ bharataśreṣṭha sarvasenāpatistava
|
śatrūn vidhvaṃsayiṣyāmi kadarthīkṛtya pāṇḍavān ||23||

24na tvātmano guṇān vaktumarhāmi vidito'smi te
|
kṛtavarmā tvatiratho bhojaḥ śastrabhṛtāṃ varaḥ ||24||

25arthasiddhiṃ tava raṇe kariṣyati na saṃśayaḥ
|
śastravidbhiranādhṛṣyo dūrapātī dṛḍhāyudhaḥ ||25||

26haniṣyati camūṃ teṣāṃ mahendro dānavāniva
|
madrarājo maheṣvāsaḥ śalyo me'tiratho mataḥ ||26||

27spardhate vāsudevena nityaṃ yo vai raṇe raṇe
|
bhāgineyānnijāṃstyaktvā śalyaste'tiratho mataḥ
|
eṣa yotsyati saṃgrāme pāṇḍavāṃśca mahārathān||27||

28sāgarormisamairbāṇaiḥ plāvayanniva śātravān
|
bhūriśravāḥ kṛtāstraśca tava cāpi hitaḥ suhṛt ||28||

29saumadattirmaheṣvāso rathayūthapayūthapaḥ
|
balakṣayamamitrāṇāṃ sumahāntaṃ kariṣyati ||29||

30sindhurājo mahārāja mato me dviguṇo rathaḥ
|
yotsyate samare rājan vikrānto rathasattamaḥ ||30||

31draupadīharaṇe rājan parikliṣṭaśca pāṇḍavaiḥ
|
saṃsmaraṃstaṃ parikleśaṃ yotsyate paravīrahā ||31||

32etena hi tadā rājaṃstapa āsthāya dāruṇam
|
sudurlabho baro labdhaḥ pāṇḍavān yoddhumāhave ||32||

33sa eṣa rathaśārdūlastadvairaṃ saṃsmaran raṇe
|
yotsyate pāṃḍavaistāta prāṇāṃstyaktvā sudustyajān ||33||
