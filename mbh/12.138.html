Mahābhārata 12.138 (Śāntiparvan)

1yudhiṣṭhira uvāca|
sarvatra buddhiḥ kathitā śreṣṭhā te bharatarṣabha
|
anāgatā tathotpannā dīrghasūtrā vināśinī ||1||

2tadicchāmi parāṃ śrotuṃ buddhiṃ te bharatarṣabha
|
yathā rājā na muhyeta śatrubhiḥ parivāritaḥ ||2||

3dharmārthakuśalo rājā dharmaśāstraviśāradaḥ
|
pṛcchāmi tvāṃ kuruśreṣṭha tanme vyākhyātumarhasi ||3||

4śatrubhirbahubhirgrasto yathā varteta pārthivaḥ
|
etadicchāmyahaṃ śrotuṃ sarvameva yathāvidhi ||4||

5viṣamasthaṃ hi rājānaṃ śatravaḥ paripanthinaḥ
|
bahavo'pyekamuddhartuṃ yatante pūrvatāpitāḥ ||5||

6sarvatra prārthyamānena durbalena mahābalaiḥ
|
ekenaivāsahāyena śakyaṃ sthātuṃ bhavetkatham ||6||

7kathaṃ mitramariṃ cāpi vindate bharatarṣabha
|
ceṣṭitavyaṃ kathaṃ cātra śatrormitrasya cāntare ||7||

8prajñātalakṣaṇe mitre tayaivāmitratāṃ gate
|
kathaṃ tu puruṣaḥ kuryātkṛtvā kiṃ vā sukhī bhavet ||8||

9vigrahaṃ kena vā kuryātsandhiṃ vā kena yojayet
|
kathaṃ vā śatrumadhyastho varteta balavānapi ||9||

10etadvai sarvakṛtyānāṃ paraṃ kṛtyaṃ parantapa
|
naitasya kaścidvaktāsti śrotā vāpi sudurlabhaḥ ||10||

11ṛte śāntanavādbhīṣmāt satyasaṃdhājjitendriyāt
|
tadanviṣya mahābhāga sarvametadvadasva me ||11||

12bhīṣma uvāca|
tvadyukto'yamanupraśno yudhiṣṭhira sukhodayaḥ
|
śṛṇu me putra kārtsnyena guhyamāpatsu bhārata ||12||

13amitro mitratāṃ yāti mitraṃ cāpi praduṣyati
|
sāmarthyayogātkāryāṇāmanityā vai sadā gatiḥ ||13||

14tasmādviśvasitavyaṃ ca vigrahaṃ ca samācaret
|
deśaṃ kālaṃ ca vijñāya kāryākāryaviniścaye ||14||

15saṃdhātavyaṃ budhairnityaṃ vyavasya ca hitārthibhiḥ
|
amitrairapi sandheyaṃ prāṇā rakṣyā hi bhārata ||15||

16yo hyamitrairnaro nityaṃ na sandadhyādapaṇḍitaḥ
|
na so'rthaṃ prāpnuyātkiṃcitphalānyapi ca bhārata ||16||

17yastvamitreṇa sandadhyānmitreṇa ca viruddhyate
|
arthayuktiṃ samālokya sumahadvindate phalam ||17||

18atrāpyudāharantīmamitihāsaṃ purātanam
|
mārjārasya ca saṃvādaṃ nyagrodhe mūṣikasya ca ||18||

19vano mahati kasmiṃścinyagrodhaḥ sumahānabhūt
|
latājālaparicchinno nānādvijagaṇānvitaḥ ||19||

20skandhavānmeghasaṅkāśaḥ śītacchāyo manoramaḥ
|
araṇyamabhito jātaḥ sa tu vyālamṛgākulaḥ ||20||

21tasya mūlaṃ samāśritya kṛtvā śatamukhaṃ bilam
|
vasati sma mahāprājñaḥ palito nāma mūṣikaḥ ||21||

22śākhāṃ tasya samāśritya vasati sma sukhaṃ purā
|
lomaśo nāma mārjāraḥ pakṣisaṃghātakhādakaḥ ||22||

23tatra cāgatya cāṇḍālo śaraṇye kṛtaketanaḥ
|
prayojayati conmāthaṃ nityamastaṃgate ravau ||23||

24tatra snāyumayān pāśān yathāvatsaṃvidhāya saḥ
|
gṛhaṃ gatvā sukhaṃ śete prabhātāmeti śarvarīm ||24||

25tatra sma nityaṃ badhyante naktaṃ bahuvidhā mṛgāḥ
|
kadācidatra mārjārastvapramatto vyabadhyata ||25||

26tasmin badhe mahāprāṇe śatrau nityātatāyini
|
taṃ kālaṃ palito jñātvā pracacāra sunirbhayaḥ ||26||

27tenānucaratā tasminvane viśvastacāriṇā
|
bhakṣyaṃ mṛgayamāṇena cirāddṛṣṭaṃ tadāmiṣam ||27||

28sa tamunmāthamāruhya tadāmiṣamabhakṣayat ||28||

29tasyopari sapatnasya baddhasya manasā hasan
|
āmiṣe tu prasaktaḥ sa kadācidavalokayan ||29||

30apaśyadaparaṃ ghoramātmanaḥ śatrumāgatam
|
śaraprasūnasaṅkāśaṃ mahīvivaraśāyinam ||30||

31nakulaṃ hariṇaṃ nāma capalaṃ tāmralocanam
|
tena mūṣikagandhena tvaramāṇamupāgatam ||31||

32bhakṣyārthaṃ saṃlihānaṃ taṃ bhūmāvūrdhvamukhaṃ sthitam
|
śākhāgatamariṃ cānyamapaśyatkoṭarālayam ||32||

33ulūkaṃ candrakaṃ nāma tīkṣṇatuṇḍaṃ kṣapācaram
|
gatasya viṣayaṃ tatra nakulolūkayostathā ||33||

34athāsyāsīdiyaṃ cintā tatprāpya sumahadbhayam
|
āpadyasyāṃ sukaśayāṃ maraṇe pratyupasthite ||34||

35samantādbhaya utpanne kathaṃ kāryaṃ hitaiṣiṇā
|
sa tathā sarvato ruddhaḥ sarvatra bhayadarśanaḥ ||35||

36abhavadbhayasaṃtaptaścakre ca paramāṃ matim
|
āpadvināśabhūyiṣṭhaṃ gataiḥ kāryaṃ hi jīvitam ||36||

37samantātsaṃśayātsaiṣā tasmādāpadupasthitā
|
gataṃ mā sahasā bhūmiṃ nakulo bhakṣayiṣyati ||37||

38ulūkaśceha tiṣṭhantaṃ mārjāraḥ pāśasaṃkṣayāt
|
na tvevāsmadvidhaḥ prājñaḥ sammohaṃ gantumarhati ||38||

39kariṣye jīvite yatnaṃ yāvadyuktyā pratigrahāt
|
na hi buddhyānvitaḥ prājño nītiśāstraviśāradaḥ ||39||

40nimajjatyāpadaṃ prāpya mahatīṃ dāruṇāmapi ||40||

41na tvanyāmiha mārjārādgatiṃ paśyāmi sāmpratam
|
viṣamastho hyayaṃ śatruḥ kṛtyaṃ cāsya mahanmayā ||41||

42jīvitārthī kathaṃ tvadya śatrubhiḥ prārthitastribhiḥ
|
tasmādenamahaṃ śatru mārjāraṃ saṃśrayāmi vai ||42||

43nītiśāstraṃ samāśritya hitamasyopavarṇaye
|
yenemaṃ śatrusaṃghātaṃ matipūrveṇa vañcaye ||43||

44ayamatyantaśatrurme vaiṣamyaṃ paramaṃ gataḥ
|
mūḍho grāhayituṃ svārthaṃ saṅgatyā yadi śakyate ||44||

45kadācidvyasanaṃ prāpya sandhiṃ kuryānmayā saha
|
balinā sannikṛṣṭasya śatrorapi parigrahaḥ ||45||

46kārya ityāhurācāryā viṣame jīvitārthinā
|
śreṣṭho hi paṇḍitaḥ śatrurna ca mitramapaṇḍitaḥ ||46||

47mama tvamitre mājari jīvitaṃ sampratiṣṭhitam
|
hantāsmai sampravakṣyāmi hetumātmābhirakṣaṇe ||47||

48apīdānīmayaṃ śatruḥ saṅgatyā paṇḍito bhavet
|
evaṃ vicintayāmāsa mūṣikaḥ śatruceṣṭitam ||48||

49tato'rthagatitattvajñaḥ saṃdhivigrahakālavit
|
sāntvapūrvamidaṃ vākyaṃ mārjāraṃ mūṣiko'bravīt ||49||

50sauhṛdenābhibhāṣe tvāṃ kaccinmārjāra jīvasi
|
jīvitaṃ hi tavecchāmi śreyaḥ sādhāraṇaṃ hi nau ||50||

51na te saumya bhayaṃ kāryaṃ jīviṣyasi yathāsukham
|
ahaṃ tvāmuddhariṣyāmi yadi māṃ na jighāṃsasi ||51||

52asmi kaścidupāyo'tra duṣkaraḥ pratibhāti me
|
yena śakyastvayā mokṣaḥ prāptuṃ śreyastathā mayā ||52||

53mayāpyupāyo dṛṣṭo'yaṃ vicārya matimātmanaḥ
|
ātmārthaṃ ca tvadarthaṃ ca śreyaḥ sādhāraṇaṃ hi nau ||53||

54idaṃ hi nakulolūkaṃ pāpabuddhyābhisaṃsthitam
|
na dharṣayati mārjāra tena me svasti sāmpratam ||54||

55kūjaṃścapalanetro'yaṃ kauśiko māṃ nirīkṣate
|
nagaśākhāgragaḥ pāpastasyāhaṃ bhṛśamudvije ||55||

56satāṃ sāptapadaṃ maitraṃ sa sakhā me'si paṇḍitaḥ
|
sāṃvāsyakaṃ kariṣyāmi nāsti te bhayamadya vai ||56||

57na hi śakto'si mārjāra pāśaṃ chettuṃ mayā vinā
|
ahaṃ chetsyāmi pāśāṃste yadi māṃ tvaṃ na hiṃsasi ||57||

58tvamāśrito drumasyāgraṃ mūlaṃ tvahamupāśritaḥ
|
ciroṣitāvubhāvāvāṃ vṛkṣe'smin viditaṃ ca te ||58||

59yasminnāśvāsate kaścidyaśca nāśvasiti kvacit
|
na tau dhīrāḥ praśaṃsanti nityamudvignamānasau ||59||

60tasmādvivardhatāṃ prītirnityaṃ saṃgatamastu nau
|
kālātītamihārthaṃ tu na praśaṃsanti paṇḍitāḥ ||60||

61arthayuktimimāṃ tatra yathābhūtāṃ niśāmaya
|
tava jīvitamicchāmi tvaṃ mamecchasi jīvitam ||61||

62kaścittarati kāṣṭhena sugambhīrāṃ mahānadīm
|
sa tārayati tatkāṣṭhaṃ sa ca kāṣṭhena tāryate ||62||

63īdṛśo nau samāyogo bhaviṣyati suvistaraḥ
|
ahaṃ tvāṃ tārayiṣyāmi māṃ ca tvaṃ tārayiṣyasi ||63||

64evamuktvā tu palitastamarthamubhayorhitam
|
hetumadgrahaṇīyaṃ ca kālāpekṣī nyavekṣya ca ||64||

65atha suvyāhṛtaṃ śrutvā tasya śatrorvicakṣaṇaḥ
|
hetumadgrahaṇīyārthaṃ mārjāro vākyamabravīt ||65||

66buddhimānvākyasampannastadvākyamanuvarṇayan
|
svāmavasthāṃ samīkṣyātha sāmnaiva pratyapūjayat ||66||

67tatastīkṣṇāgradaśano maṇivaidūryalocanaḥ
|
mūṣikaṃ mandamudvīkṣya mārjāro lomaśo'bravīt ||67||

68nandāmi saumya bhadraṃ te yo māṃ jīvitumicchasi
|
śreyaśca yadi jānīṣe kriyatāṃ mā vicāraya ||68||

69ahaṃ hi bhṛśamāpannastvamāpanataro mama
|
dvayorāpannayoḥ sandhiḥ kriyatāṃ mā cirāya ca ||69||

70vidhāsye prāptakālaṃ yatkāryaṃ siddhikaraṃ vibho
|
mayi kṛcchrādvinirmukte na vinaṃkṣyati te kṛtam ||70||

71nyastamāno'smi bhakto'smi śiṣyastvaddhitakṛttathā
|
nideśavaśavartī ca bhavantaṃ śaraṇaṃ gataḥ ||71||

72ityevamuktaḥ palito mārjāraṃ vaśamāgatam
|
vākyaṃ hitamuvācedamabhinītārthamarthavit ||72||

73udāraṃ yadbhavānāha naitaccitraṃ bhavadvidhe
|
vihito yastu mārgo me hitārthaṃ śṛṇu taṃ mama ||73||

74ahaṃ tvā'nupravekṣyāmi nakulānme mahadbhayam
|
trāyasva bho māvadhīstvaṃ śakto'smi tava rakṣaṇe ||74||

75ulūkāccaiva māṃ rakṣa kṣudraḥ prārthayate hi mām
|
ahaṃ chetsyāmi te pāśān sakhe satyena te śape ||75||

76tadvacaḥ saṃgataṃ śrutvā lomaśo yuktamarthavat
|
harṣādudvīkṣya palitaṃ svāgatenābhyapūjayat ||76||

77taṃ sampūjyātha palitaṃ mārjāraḥ sauhṛde sthitaḥ
|
sa vicintyābravīddhīraḥ prītastvarita eva ca ||77||

78śīghramāgaccha bhadraṃ te tvaṃ me prāṇasamaḥ sakhā
|
tava prājña prasādāddhi prāyaḥ prāpsyāmi jīvitam ||78||

79yadyadevaṃ gate nātha śakyaṃ kartuṃ mayā tava
|
tadājñāpaya kartā'smi sandhirevāstu nau sakhe ||79||

80asmāttu saṃkaṭānmuktaḥ samitragaṇabāndhavaḥ
|
sarvakāryāṇi kartā'haṃ priyāṇi ca hitāni ca ||80||

81muktaśca vyasanādasmātsaumyāhamapi nāma te
|
prītimutpādayeyaṃ ca prītikartuśca satkriyām ||81||

82pratyupakurvanbahvapi na bhāti pūrvopakāriṇā tulyaḥ
|
ekaḥ karoti hi kṛte niṣkāraṇameva kurute'nyaḥ ||82||

83bhīṣma uvāca|
grāhayitvā tu taṃ svārthaṃ mārjāraṃ mūṣikastathā
|
praviveśa tu viśramya kroḍamasya kṛtāgasaḥ ||83||

84evamāśvāsito vidvānmārjareṇa sa mūṣikaḥ
|
mārjārorasi vistrabdhaḥ suṣvāpa pitṛmātṛvat ||84||

85līnaṃ tu tasya gātreṣu mārjārasya ca mūṣikam
|
dṛṣṭvā tau nakulolūkau nirāśau pratyapadyatām ||85||

86tathaiva tau susaṃtrastau dṛḍhamāgatatandritau
|
dṛṣṭvā tayoḥ parāṃ prīti vismayaṃ paramaṃ gatau ||86||

87balinau matimantau ca suvṛttau cāpyupāsitau
|
aśaktau tu nayāttasmātsampradharṣayituṃ balāt ||87||

88kāryārthaṃ kṛtasandhī tau dṛṣṭvā mārjāramūṣikau
|
ulūkanakulau tūrṇaṃ jagmatustau svamālayam ||88||

89līnaḥ sa tasya gātreṣu palito deśakālavit
|
ciccheda pāśānnṛpate kālāpekṣī śanaiḥ śanaiḥ ||89||

90atha bandhaparikliṣṭo mārjāro vīkṣya mūṣikam
|
chindantaṃ vai tadā pāśānatvarantaṃ tvarānvitaḥ ||90||

91tamatvarantaṃ palitaṃ pāśānāṃ chedane tathā
|
saṃcodayitumārebhe mārjāro mūṣikaṃ tadā ||91||

92kiṃ saumya nātitvarase kiṃ kṛtārtho'vamanyase
|
chindhi pāśānamitraghna purā śvapaca eti ca ||92||

93ityuktastvaratā tena matimānpalito'bravīt
|
mārjāramakṛtaprajñaṃ pathyamātmahitaṃ vacaḥ ||93||

94tūṣṇīṃ bhava na te saumya tvarā kāryā na sambhramaḥ
|
vayamevātra kālajñā na kālaḥ parihāsyate ||94||

95akāle kṛtyamārabdhaṃ karturnārthāya kalpate
|
tadeva kāla ārabdhaṃ mahate'rthāya kalpate ||95||

96akāle vipramuktānme tvatta eva bhayaṃ bhavet
|
tasmātkālaṃ pratīkṣasva kimiti tvarase sakhe ||96||

97yadā paśyāmi cāṇḍālamāyāntaṃ śastrapāṇinam
|
tataśchetsyāmi te pāśān prāpte sādhāraṇe bhaye ||97||

98tasminkāle pramuktastvaṃ tarumevādhirokṣyase
|
na hi te jīvitādanyatkiṃcitkṛtyaṃ bhaviṣyati ||98||

99tato bhavatyapakrānte traste bhīte ca lomaśa
|
ahaṃ bilaṃ pravekṣyāmi bhavān śākhāṃ bhajiṣyati ||99||

100evamuktastu mārjāro mūṣikeṇātmano hitam
|
vacanaṃ vākyatattvajño jīvitārthī mahāmatiḥ ||100||

101athātmakṛtye tvaritaḥ samyak praśritamācaran
|
uvāca lomaśo vākyaṃ mūṣikaṃ cirakāriṇam ||101||

102na hyevaṃ mitrakāryāṇi prītyā kurvanti sādhavaḥ
|
yathā tvaṃ mokṣitaḥ kṛcchrāttvaramāṇena vai mayā ||102||

103tathā hi tvaramāṇena tvayā kāryaṃ hitaṃ mam
|
yatnaṃ kuru mahāprājña yathā rakṣā''vayorbhavet ||103||

104athavā pūrvavairaṃ tvaṃ smarankālaṃ jihīrṣasi
|
paśya duṣkṛtakarmaṃstvaṃ vyaktamāyuḥ kṣayaṃ tava ||104||

105yadi kiṃcinmayājñānātpurastādduṣkṛtaṃ kṛtam
|
na tanmanasi kartavyaṃ kṣāmaye tvāṃ prasīda me ||105||

106tamevaṃvādinaḥ prājñaḥ śāstrabuddhisamanvitaḥ
|
uvācedaṃ vacaḥ śreṣṭhaṃ mārjāra mūṣikastadā ||106||

107śrutaṃ me tava mārjāra svamarthaṃ parigṛhṇataḥ
|
mamāpi tvaṃ vijānāsi svamarthaṃ parigṛhṇataḥ ||107||

108yanmitraṃ bhītavatsādhyaṃ yanmitraṃ bhayasaṃhitam
|
surakṣitavyaṃ tatkāryaṃ pāṇiḥ sarpamukhādiva ||108||

109kṛtvā balavatā saṃdhimātmanaṃ yo na rakṣati
|
apathyamiva tadbhuktaṃ tasya nārthāya kalpate ||109||

110na kaścitkasyacinmitraṃ na kaścitkasyacitsuhṛt
|
arthatastu nibaddhyante mitrāṇi ripavastathā ||110||

111arthairarthā nibaddhyante gajairvanagajā iva
|
na ca kaścitkṛte kārye kartāraṃ samavekṣate ||111||

112tasmātsarvāṇi kāryāṇi sāvaśeṣāṇi kārayet
|
tasmin kāle'pi ca bhavān divākīrtibhayārditaḥ ||112||

113mama na grahaṇe śaktaḥ palāyanaparāyaṇaḥ
|
chinnaṃ tu tantubāhulyaṃ tantureko'vaśeṣitaḥ ||113||

114chetsyāmyahaṃ tamapyāśu nirvṛto bhava lomaśa
|
tayoḥ saṃvadatorevaṃ tathaivāpannayordvayoḥ ||114||

115kṣayaṃ jagāma sā rātrirlomaśaṃ tvāviśadbhayam
|
tataḥ prabhātasamaye vikṛtaḥ kṛṣṇapiṅgalaḥ ||115||

116sthūlasphigvikṛto rūkṣaḥ śvayūthaparivāritaḥ
|
śaṃkukarṇo mahāvaktro malino ghoradarśanaḥ ||116||

117parigho nāma cāṇḍālaḥ śastrapāṇiradṛśyata
|
taṃ dṛṣṭvā yamadūtābhaṃ mārjārastrastacetanaḥ ||117||

118uvāca vacanaṃ bhītaḥ kimidānīṃ kariṣyasi
|
atha tāvapi saṃtrastau taṃ dṛṣṭvā ghorasaṃkulam ||118||

119kṣaṇena nakulolūkau nairāśyamupajagmatuḥ
|
balinau matimantau ca saṃghāte cāpyupāgatau ||119||

120aśaktau sunayāttasmātsampradharṣayituṃ balāt
|
kāryārthe kṛtasaṃdhānau dṛṣṭvā mārjāramūṣikau ||120||

121ulūkanakulau tatra jagmatuḥ svaṃ svamālayam
|
tataściccheda taṃ pāśaṃ mārjārasya ca mūṣikaḥ ||121||

122vipramukto'tha mārjārastamevābhyapataddumam
|
sa tasmātsambhramāvartānmukto ghoreṇa śatruṇā ||122||

123bilaṃ viveśa palitaḥ śākhāṃ lebhe sa lomaśaḥ
|
unmāthamapyathādāya cāṇḍālo vīkṣya sarvaśaḥ ||123||

124vihatāśaḥ kṣaṇenāste tasmāddeśādapākramat
|
jagāma sa svabhavanaṃ cāṇḍālo bharatarṣabha ||124||

125tatastasmādbhayānmukto durlabhaṃ prāpya jīvitam
|
bilasthaṃ pādapāgrasthaḥ palitaṃ lomaśo'bravīt ||125||

126akṛtvā saṃvidaṃ kāñcitsahasā samavaplutaḥ
|
kṛtajñaṃ kṛtakarmāṇaṃ kaccinmāṃ nābhiśaṃkase ||126||

127gatvā ca mama viśvāsaṃ dattvā ca mama jīvitam
|
mitropabhogasamaye kiṃ māṃ tvaṃ nopasarpasi ||127||

128kṛtvā hi pūrvaṃ mitrāṇi yaḥ paścānnānutiṣṭhati
|
na sa mitrāṇi labhate kṛcchrāsvāpatsu durmatiḥ ||128||

129satkṛto'haṃ tvayā mitra sāmarthyādātmanaḥ sakhe
|
