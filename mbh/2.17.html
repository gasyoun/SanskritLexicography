Mahābhārata 2.17 (Sabhāparvan)

1vāsudeva uvāca|
jātasya bhārate vaṃśe tathā kuntyāḥ sutasya ca
|
yā vai yuktā matiḥ seyamarjunena pradarśitā||1||

2na sma mṛtyuṃ vayaṃ vidma rātrau vā yadi vā divā
|
na cāpi kañcidamaramayuddhenānuśuśruma||2||

3etāvadeva puruṣaiḥ kāryaṃ hṛdayatoṣaṇam
|
nayena vidhidṛṣṭena yadupakramate parān||3||

4sunayasyānapāyasya saṃyoge paramaḥ kramaḥ
|
saṅgatyā jāyate'sāmyaṃ sāmyaṃ ca na bhaveddvayoḥ||4||

5anayasyānupāyasya saṃyuge paramaḥ kṣayaḥ
|
saṃśayo jāyate sāmyājjayaśca na bhaveddvayoḥ||5||

6te vayaṃ nayamāsthāya śatrudeśasamīpagāḥ
|
kathamantaṃ na gacchema vṛkṣasyeva nadīrayāḥ
|
pararandhre parākrāntāḥ svarandhrāvaraṇe sthitāḥ||6||

7vyūḍhānīkairatibalairna yudvyedaribhiḥ saha
|
iti buddhimatāṃ nītistanmamāpīha rocate||7||

8anavadyā hyasambuddhāḥ praviṣṭāḥ śatrusadma tat
|
śatrudeśamupākramya taṃ kāmaṃ prāpnuyāmahe||8||

9eko hyeva śriyaṃ nityaṃ bibharti puruṣarṣabhaḥ
|
antarātmeva bhūtānāṃ tatkṣayaṃ naiva lakṣaye||9||

10athavainaṃ nihatyājau śeṣeṇāpi samāhatāḥ
|
prāpnuyāma tataḥ svargaṃ jñātitrāṇaparāyaṇāḥ||10||

11kṛṣṇa ko'yaṃ jarāsandhaḥ kiṃvīryaḥ kimparākramaḥ
|
yastvāṃ spṛṣṭvā'gnisadṛśaṃ na dagdhaḥ śalabho yathā||11||

12śṛṇu rājañjarāsandho yadvīryo yatparākramaḥ
|
yathā copekṣito'smābhirbahuśaḥ kṛtavipriyaḥ||12||

13akṣauhiṇīnāṃ tisṛṇāṃ patiḥ samaradarpitaḥ
|
rājā bṛhadratho nāma magadhādhipatirbalī||13||

14rūpavānvīryasampannaḥ śrīmānatulavikramaḥ
|
nityaṃ dīkṣāṅkitatanuḥ śatakraturivāparaḥ||14||

15tejasā sūryasaṅkāśaḥ kṣamayā pṛthivīsamaḥ
|
yaścāntakasamaḥ krodhe śriyā vaiśravaṇopamaḥ||15||

16tasyābhijanasaṃyuktairgaṇairbharatasattama
|
vyāpteyaṃ pṛthivī sarvā sūryasyeva gabhastibhiḥ||16||

17sa kāśirājasya sute yamaje bharatarṣabhaḥ
|
upayeme mahāvīryo rūpadraviṇasaṃyute
|
tayoścakāra samayaṃ mithaḥ sa puruṣarṣabhaḥ||17||

18nātivartiṣya ityevaṃ patnībhyāṃ sannidhau tadā
|
sa tābhyāṃ śuśubhe rājā patnībhyāṃ vasudhādhipaḥ||18||

19priyāmbhāmanurūpābhyāṃ kareṇubhyāmiva dvipaḥ
|
tayormadhyagataścāpi rarāja vasudhādhipaḥ||19||

20gaṅgāyamunayormadhye mūrtimāniva sāgaraḥ
|
viṣayeṣu nimagnasya tasya yauvanamabhyagāt||20||

21na ca vaṃśakaraḥ putrastasyājāyata kaścana
|
maṅgalairbahubhirhomaiḥ putrakāmābhiriṣṭibhiḥ
|
nāsasāda nṛpaśreṣṭhaḥ putraṃ kulavivardhanam||21||

22atha kākṣīvataḥ putraṃ gautamasya mahātmanaḥ
|
śuśrāva tapasi śrāntamudāraṃ caṇḍakauśikam||22||

23yadṛcchayāgataṃ taṃ tu vṛkṣamūlamupāśritam
|
patnībhyāṃ sahito rājā sarvayatnairatoṣayat||23||

24tamabravītsatyadhṛtiḥ satyavāgṛṣisattamaḥ
|
parituṣṭo'smi rājendra varaṃ varaya suvrata||24||

25tataḥ sabhāryaḥ praṇatastamuvāca bṛhadrathaḥ
|
putradarśananairāśyādbāṣpasandigdhayā girā||25||

26bhagavan rājyamutsṛjya prasthito'haṃ tapovanam
|
kiṃ vareṇālpabhāgyasya kiṃ rājyenāprajasya me||26||

27kṛṣṇa uvāca|
etacchrutvā munirdhyānamagamatkṣubhitendriyaḥ
|
tasyaiva cāmravṛkṣasya chāyāyāṃ samupāviśata||27||

28tasyopaviṣṭasya munerutsaṅge nipapāta ha
|
avānamaśukādaṣṭamekamāmraphalaṃ kila||28||

29tatpragṛhya muniśreṣṭho hṛdayenābhimantrya ca
|
rājñe dadāvapratimaṃ putrasamprāptikāraṇam||29||

30uvāca ca mahāprājñastaṃ rājānaṃ mahāmuniḥ
|
gaccha rājankṛtārtho'si nivartasva narādhipa||30||

31etacchrutvā munervākyaṃ śirasā praṇipatya ca
|
muneḥ pādau mahāprājñaḥ sa nṛpaḥ svagṛhaṃ gataḥ||31||

32yathāsamayamājñāya tadā sa nṛpasattamaḥ
|
dvābhyāmekaṃ phalaṃ prādātpatnībhyāṃ bharatarṣabha||32||

33te tadāmraṃ dvidhā kṛtvā bhakṣayāmāsatuḥ śubhe
|
bhāvitvādapi cārthasya satyavākyatayā muneḥ||33||

34tayoḥ samabhavadgarbhaḥ phalaprāśanasambhavaḥ
|
te ca dṛṣṭvā sa nṛpatiḥ parāṃ mudamavāpa ha||34||

35atha kāle mahāprājña yathāsamayamāgate
|
prajāyetāmubhe rājañccharīraśakale tadā||35||

36ekākṣibāhucaraṇe ardhodaramukhasphice
|
dṛṣṭvā śarīraśakale pravepaturubhe bhṛśam||36||

37udvigre saha saṃmantrya te bhaginyau tadā'bale
|
sajīve prāṇiśakale tatyajāte suduḥkhite||37||

38tayordhātryau susaṃvīte kṛtvā te garbhasamplave
|
nirgamyāntaḥpuradvārātsamutsṛjyābhijagmatuḥ||38||

39te catuṣpathanikṣipte jarā nāmātha rākṣasī
|
jagrāha manujavyāghra māṃsaśoṇitabhojanā||39||

40kartukāmā sukhavahe śakale sā tu rākṣasī
|
saṃyojayāmāsa tadā vidhānabalacoditā||40||

41te samānītamātre tu śakale puruṣarṣabha
|
ekamūrtidharo vīraḥ kumāraḥ samapadyata||41||

42tataḥ sā rākṣasī rājanvismayotphullalocanā
|
na śaśāka samudvoḍhuṃ vajrasāramayaṃ śiśum||42||

43bālastāmratalaṃ muṣṭiṃ kṛtvā cāsye nidhāya saḥ
|
prākrośadatisaṃrabdhaḥ satoya iva toyadaḥ||43||

44tena śabdena sambhrāntaḥ sahasā'ntaḥpure janaḥ
|
nirjagāma naravyāghra rājñā saha parantapa||44||

45te cābale parimlāne payaḥpūrṇapayodhare
|
nirāśe putralābhāya sahasaivābhyagacchatām||45||

46te ca dṛṣṭvā tathābhūte rājānaṃ ceṣṭasaṃtatim
|
taṃ ca bālaṃ subalinaṃ cintayāmāsa rākṣasī||46||

47nārhāmi viṣaye rājño vasantī putragṛddhinaḥ
|
bālaṃ putramimaṃ hantuṃ dhārmikasya mahātmanaḥ||47||

48sā taṃ bālamupādāya meghalekhena bhāskaram
|
kṛtvā ca mānuṣaṃ rūpamuvāca vasudhādhipam||48||

49rākṣasyuvāca
bṛhadratha sutaste'yaṃ mayā dattaḥ pragṛhyatām
|
tava patnīdvaye jāto dvijātivaraśāsanāt
|
dhātrījanaparityakto mayā'yaṃ parirakṣitaḥ||49||

50kṛṣṇa uvāca|
tataste bharataśreṣṭha kāśirājasute śubhe
|
taṃ bālamabhipadyāśu prasravairabhyaṣiñjatām||50||

51tataḥ sa rājā saṃhṛṣṭaḥ sarvaṃ tadupalabhya ca
|
apṛcchaddhemagarbhābhāṃ rākṣasīṃ tāmarākṣasīm||51||

52rājovāca
|
kā tvaṃ kamalagarbhābhe mama putrapradāyinī
|
kāmaṃ mā brūhi kalyāṇi devatā pratibhāsi me||52||
