Mahābhārata 7.108 (Droṇaparvan)

1sañjaya uvāca|
draupadeyānmaheṣvāsān saumadattirmahāyaśāḥ
|
ekaikaṃ pañcabhirviddhvā punarvivyādha saptabhiḥ ||1||

2te pīḍitā bhṛśaṃ tena raudreṇa sahasā vibho
|
pramūḍhā naiva vividurmṛdhe kṛtyaṃ sma kiṃcana ||2||

3nākuliśca śatānīkaḥ saumadattiṃ nararṣabham
|
dvābhyāṃ viddhvā'nadaddhṛṣṭaḥ śarābhyāṃ śatrukarśanaḥ ||3||

4tathetare raṇe yattāstribhistribhirajihmagaiḥ
|
vivyadhuḥ samare tūrṇaṃ saumada‌ttimamarṣaṇam ||4||

5sa tān prati mahārāja pañca cikṣepa sāyakān
|
ekaikaṃ hṛdi cājaghne ekaikena mahāyaśāḥ ||5||

6tataste bhrātaraḥ pañca śarairviddhā mahātmanā
|
parivārya raṇe vīraṃ vivyadhuḥ sāyakairbhṛśam ||6||

7ārjunistu hayāṃstasya caturbhirniśitaiḥ śaraiḥ
|
preṣayāmāsa saṃkruddho yamasya sadanaṃ prati ||7||

8bhaimasenirdhanuśchitvā saumadattermahātmanaḥ
|
nanāda balavannādaṃ vivyādha ca śitaiḥ śaraiḥ ||8||

9yaudhiṣṭherirdhvajaṃ tasya chittvā bhūmāvapātayat
|
nākuliścātha yantāraṃ rathanīḍāda‌pāharat ||9||

10sāhadevistu taṃ jñātvā bhrātṛbhirvimukhīkṛtam
|
kṣurapreṇa śiro rājannicakarta mahātmanaḥ ||10||

11tacchiro nyapatadbhūmau tapanīyavibhūṣitam
|
bhrājayattaṃ raṇoddeśaṃ bālasūryasamaprabham ||11||

12saumadatteḥ śiro dṛṣṭvā nihataṃ tanmahātmanaḥ
|
vitrastāstāvakā rājan pradudruvuranekadhā ||12||

13alaṃbuṣastu samare bhīmasenaṃ mahābalam
|
yodhayāmāsa saṃkruddho lakṣmaṇaṃ rāvaṇiryathā ||13||

14saṃprayuddhau raṇe dṛṣṭvā tāvubhau nararākṣasau
|
vismayaḥ sarvabhūtānāṃ praharṣaḥ samajāyata ||14||

15ārṣyaśṛṃgiṃ tato bhīmo navabhirniśitaiḥ śaraiḥ
|
vivyādha prahasan rājan rākṣasendramamarṣaṇam ||15||

16tadrakṣaḥ samare viddhaṃ kṛtvā nādaṃ bhayāvaham
|
abhyadravattato bhīmaṃ ye ca tasya padānugāḥ ||16||

17sa bhīmaṃ pañcabhirvidvā śaraḥ sannataparvabhiḥ
|
bhaimān parijaghānāśu rathāṃstriśatamāhave ||17||

18punaścatuḥśatān hatvā bhīmaṃ vivyādha patriṇā
|
so'tividdhastathā bhīmo rākṣasena mahābalaḥ||18||

19nipapāta rathopasthe mūrcchayā'bhipariplutaḥ
|
pratilabhya tataḥ saṃjñāṃ mārutiḥ krodhamūrcchitaḥ ||19||

20vikṛṣya kārmukaṃ ghoraṃ bhārasādhanamuttamam
|
alaṃbuṣaṃ śaraistīkṣṇairardayāmāsa sarvataḥ ||20||

21sa viddho vahubhirbāṇairnīlāñjanacayopamaḥ
|
śuśubhe sarvato rājan praphulla iva kiṃśukaḥ ||21||

22sa vadhyamānaḥ samare bhīmacāpacyutaiḥ śaraiḥ
|
smaran bhrātṛvadhaṃ caiva pāṇḍavena mahātmanā ||22||

23ghoraṃ rūpamatho kṛtvā bhīmasenamabhāṣata
|
tiṣṭhedānīṃ raṇe pārtha paśya me'dya parākramam ||23||

24bako nāma sudurbuddhe rākṣasapravaro balī
|
parokṣaṃ mama taddhṛttaṃ yadbhrātā me hatastvayā ||24||

25evamuktvā tato bhīmamantardhānaṃ gatastadā
|
mahatā śaravarṣeṇa bhṛśaṃ taṃ samavākirat ||25||

26bhīmastu samare rājannadṛśye rākṣase tadā
|
ākāśaṃ pūrayāmāsa śaraiḥ sannataparvabhiḥ ||26||

27sa vadhyamāno bhīmena nimeṣādrathamāsthitaḥ
|
jagāma dharaṇīṃ caiva kṣudraḥ khaṃ sahasā'gamat ||27||

28uccāvacāni rupāṇi cakāra subahūni ca
|
aṇurbṛhatpunaḥ sthūlo nādānmuñcannivāmbudaḥ ||28||

29uccāvacāstathā vāco vyājahāra samaṃtataḥ
|
nipeturgaganāccaiva śaradhārāḥ sahasraśaḥ ||29||

30śaktayaḥ kaṇapāḥ prāsāḥ śūlapaṭṭiśatomarāḥ
|
śataghnyaḥ parighāścaiva bhindipālāḥ paraśvadhāḥ ||30||

31śilāḥ khaḍgā guḍāścaiva ṛṣṭīrvajrāṇi caiva ha
|
sā rākṣasavisṛṣṭā tu śastravṛṣṭiḥ sudāruṇā ||31||

32jaghāna pāṇḍuputrasya sainikān raṇamūrdhani
|
tena pāṇḍavasainyānāṃ suditā yudhi vāraṇāḥ ||32||

33hayāśca bahavo rājan pattayaśca tathā punaḥ
|
rathebhyo rathinaḥ petustasya nunnāḥ sma sāyakaiḥ ||33||

34śoṇitodāṃ rathāvartāṃ hastigrāhasamākulām
|
chatrahaṃsāṃ kardaminīṃ bāhupakṣagasaṃkulām ||34||

35nadīṃ prāvartayāmāsa rakṣogaṇasamākulām
|
vahantīṃ bahudhā rājaṃścedipañcālasṛñjayān ||35||

36taṃ tathā samare rājan vicarantamabhītavat
|
pāṇḍavā bhṛśasaṃvignāḥ prāpaśyaṃstasya vikramam ||36||

37tāvakānāṃ tu sainyānāṃ praharṣaḥ samajāyata
|
vāditraninadaścograḥ sumahān romaharṣaṇaḥ ||37||

38taṃ śrutvā ninadaṃ ghoraṃ tava sainyasya pāṇḍavaḥ
|
nāmṛṣyata yathā nāgastalaśabdaṃ samīritam ||38||

39tataḥ krodhābhitāmrākṣo nirdahanniva pāvakaḥ
|
saṃdadhe tvāṣṭramastraṃ sa svayaṃ tvaṣṭeva mārutiḥ ||39||

40tataḥ śarasahasrāṇi prādurāsan samantataḥ
|
taiḥ śaraistava sainyasya vidravaḥ sumahānabhūt ||40||

41tadastraṃ preritaṃ tena bhīmasenena saṃyuge
|
rākṣasasya mahāmāyāṃ hatvā rākṣasamārdayat ||41||

42sa vadhyamāno bahudhā bhīmasenena rākṣasaḥ
|
santyajya samare bhīmaṃ droṇānīkamupādravat ||42||

43tasmiṃstu nirjite rājan rākṣasendre mahātmanā
|
anādayan siṃhanādaiḥ pāṇḍavāḥ sarvato diśaṃ ||43||

44apūjayan mārutiṃ ca saṃhṛṣṭāste mahābalam
|
prahrādaṃ samare jitvā yathā śakraṃ marudgaṇāḥ ||44||
