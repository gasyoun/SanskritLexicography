Mahābhārata 4.58 (Virāṭaparvan)

1vaiśampāyana uvāca|kṛpe'panīte droṇastu pragṛhya saśaraṃ dhanuḥ
|
abhyadravadanādhṛṣyaḥ śoṇāśvaḥ śvetavāhanam ||1||

2sa tu rukmarathaṃ dṛṣṭvā gurumāyāntamantikāt
|
arjuno jayatāṃ śreṣṭha uttaraṃ vākyamabravīt ||2||

3arjuna uvāca|yatraiṣā kāñcanī vedī dhvaje yasya prakāśate
|
uchritā pravare daṇḍe patākābhiralaṅkṛtā
|
atra māṃ vaha bhadraṃ te droṇānīkāya sārathe ||3||

4aśvāḥ śoṇāḥ prakāśante bṛhantaścāruvāhinaḥ
|
snigdhavidrumasaṃkāśāstāmrāsyāḥ priyadarśanāḥ
|
yuktā rathavare yasya sarvaśikṣāviśāradāḥ ||4||

5dīrghabāhurmahātejā balarūpasamanvitaḥ
|
sarvalokeṣu vikrānto bhāradvājaḥ pratāpavān ||5||

6buddhyā tulyo hyuśanasā bṛhaspatisamo naye
|
vedāstathaiva catvāro brahmacaryaṃ tathaiva ca ||6||

7sasaṃhārāṇi sarvāṇi divyānyastrāṇi māriṣa
|
dhanurvedaśca kārtsnyena yasminnityaṃ pratiṣṭhitaḥ ||7||

8kṣamā damaśca satyaṃ ca ānṛśaṃsyamathārjavam
|
ete cānye ca bahavo yasminnityaṃ dvije guṇāḥ ||8||

9tenāhaṃ yoddhumicchāmi mahābhāgena saṃyuge
|
tasmāt taṃ prāpayācāryaṃ kṣipramuttara vāhaya ||9||

10vaiśampāyana uvāca|arjunenaivamuktastu vairāṭirhemabhūṣaṇān
|
codayāmāsa tānaśvān bhāradvājarathaṃ prati ||10||

11tamāpatantaṃ vegena pāṇḍavaṃ rathināṃ varam
|
droṇaḥ pratyudyayau pārthaṃ matto mattamiva dvipam ||11||

12tataḥ prādhmāpayacchaṅkhaṃ bherīśataninādinam
|
pracukṣubhe balaṃ sarvamuddhūta iva sāgaraḥ ||12||

13atha śoṇān sadaśvāṃstān haṃsavarṇairmanojavaiḥ
|
miśritān samare dṛṣṭvā vyasmayanta raṇe narāḥ ||13||

14tau rathau vīrasaṃpannau dṛṣṭvā saṅgrāmamūrdhani
|
ācāryaśiṣyāvajitau kṛtavidyau manasvinau ||14||

15samāśliṣṭau tadā'nyonyaṃ droṇapārthau mahābalau
|
dṛṣṭvā prākampata muhurbharatānāṃ mahadbalam ||15||

16harṣayuktastataḥ pārthaḥ prahasanniva vīryavān
|
rathaṃ rathena droṇasya samāsādya mahārathaḥ ||16||

17abhivādya mahābāhuḥ sāmapūrvamidaṃ vacaḥ
|
uvāca ślakṣṇayā vācā kaunteyaḥ paravīrahā ||17||

18uṣitāḥ smo vane vāsaṃ pratikarma cikīrṣavaḥ
|
kopaṃ nārhasi naḥ kartuṃ sadā samaradurjaya ||18||

19ahaṃ tu prahṛte pūrvaṃ prahariṣyāmi te'nagha
|
iti me vartate buddhistadbhavān kartumarhati ||19||

20tato'smai prāhiṇoddroṇaḥ śarānadhikāvaṃśatim
|
aprāptāṃścaiva tān pārthaściccheda kṛtahastavat ||20||

21tataḥ śarasahastreṇa rathaṃ pārthasya vīryavān
|
avākirat tato droṇaḥ śīghramastraṃ vidarśayan ||21||

22hayāṃśca rajataprakhyān kaṅkapatraiḥ śilāśitaiḥ
|
avākiradameyātmā pārthaṃ saṃkopayanniva ||22||

23evaṃ pravavṛte yuddhaṃ bhāradvājakirīṭinoḥ
|
samaṃ vimuñcatoḥ saṃkhye viśikhān dīptatejasaḥ ||23||

24tāvubhau khyātakarmāṇāvubhau vāyusamau jave
|
ubhau divyāstraviduṣāvubhāvuttamatejasau
|
kṣipantau śarajālāni mohayāmāsaturnṛpān ||24||

25vyasmayanta tato yodhā ye tatrāsansamāgatāḥ
|
śarānvisṛjatostūrṇaṃ sādhu sādhvityapūjayan ||25||

26droṇaṃ hi samare ko'nyo yoddhumarhati phālgunāt
|
raudraḥ kṣatriyadharmo'yaṃ guruṇā yadayudhyata
|
ityabruvañjanāstatra saṅgrāmaśirasi sthitāḥ ||26||

27vīrau tāvabhisaṃrabdhau saṃnikṛṣṭau mahābhujau
|
chādayetāṃ śaravrātairanyonyamaparājitau ||27||

28visphārya sumahaccāpaṃ hemapṛṣṭhaṃ durāsadam
|
bhāradvājo'tha saṃkruddhaḥ phālgunaṃ pratyavidhyata ||28||

29sa sāyakamayairjālairarjunasya rathaṃ prati
|
bhānumadbhiḥ śilādhautairbhānorācchādayat prabhām ||29||

30pārthaṃ ca sumahābāhurmahāvegairmahārathaḥ
|
vivyādha niśitairbāṇairmedho vṛṣṭyeva parvatam ||30||

31tathaiva divyaṃ gāṇḍīvaṃ dhanurādāya pāṇḍavaḥ
|
śatrughnaṃ vegavān hṛṣṭo bhārasādhanamuttamam ||31||

32visasarja śarāṃścitrān suvarṇavikṛtān bahūn
|
nāśayan śaravarṣāṇi bhāradvājasya vīryavān
|
tūrṇaṃ cāpavinirmuktaistada‌dbhutamivābhavat ||32||

33sa rathena caran pārthaḥ prekṣaṇīyo dhanañjayaḥ
|
yugapaddikṣu sarvāsu sarvato'strāṇyadarśayat ||33||

34ekacchāyamivākāśaṃ bāṇaiścakre samantataḥ
|
nādṛśyata tadā droṇo nīhāreṇeva saṃvṛtaḥ ||34||

35tasyābhavat tadā rūpaṃ saṃvṛtasya śarauttamaiḥ
|
jājvalyamānasya tadā parvatasyeva sarvataḥ ||35||

36dṛṣṭvā tu pārthasya raṇe śaraiḥ svarathamāvṛtam
|
sa visphārya dhanuḥ śreṣṭhaṃ meghastanitaniḥsvanam ||36||

37agnicakropamaṃ ghoraṃ vyakarṣat paramāyudham
|
vyaśātayaccharāṃstāṃstu droṇaḥ samitiśobhanaḥ ||37||

38mahānabhūttataḥ śabdo vaṃśānāmiva dahyatām ||38||

39jāmbūnadamayaiḥ puṅkhaiścitracāpavinirgataiḥ
|
prācchādayada‌meyātmā diśaḥ sūryasya ca prabhām ||39||

40tataḥ kanakapuṅkhānāṃ śarāṇāṃ nataparvaṇām
|
viyaccārāṇāṃ viyati dṛśyante bahavo vrajāḥ ||40||

41droṇasya puṅkhasaktāśca prabhavantaḥ śarāsanāt
|
eko dīrgha ivādṛśyadākāśe saṃhataḥ śaraḥ ||41||

42evaṃ tau svarṇavikṛtān vimuñcantau mahāśarān
|
ākāśaṃ saṃvṛtaṃ vīrāvulkābhiriva cakratuḥ ||42||

43śarāstayostu vibabhuḥ kaṅkabarhiṇavāsasaḥ
|
paṅktyaḥ śaradi svasthānāṃ haṃsānāṃ caratāmiva ||43||

44yuddhaṃ samabhavat tatra susaṃrabdhaṃ mahātmanoḥ
|
droṇapāṇḍavayorghoraṃ vṛtravāsavayoriva ||44||

45tau gajāviva cāsādya viṣāṇāgraiḥ parasparam
|
śaraiḥ pūrṇāyatotsṛṣṭairanyonyamabhijaghnatuḥ ||45||

46tau vyavāharatāṃ yuddhe saṃrabdhau raṇaśobhinau
|
udīrayantau samare divyānyastrāṇi bhāgaśaḥ ||46||

47atha tvācāryamukhyena śarān sṛṣṭān śilāśitān
|
nyavārayacchitairbāṇairarjuno jayatāṃ varaḥ ||47||

48darśayan vīkṣamāṇānāmastramugraparākramaḥ
|
iṣubhistūrṇamākāśaṃ bahubhiśca samāvṛṇot ||48||

49jighāṃsantaṃ naravyāghramarjunaṃ tigmatejasam
|
ācāryamukhyaḥ samare droṇaḥ śastrabhṛtāṃ varaḥ
|
arjunena sahākrīḍaccharaiḥ sannataparvabhiḥ ||49||

50divyānyastrāṇi varṣantaṃ tasmin vai tumule raṇe
|
astrairastrāṇi saṃvārya phālgunaṃ samayodhayat ||50||

51tayorāsīt saṃprahāraḥ kruddhayornarasiṃhayoḥ
|
amarṣiṇostadā'nyonyaṃ devadānavayoriva ||51||

52aindraṃ vāyavyamāgneyamastramastreṇa pāṇḍavaḥ
|
droṇena muktamātraṃ tu grasati sma punaḥ punaḥ ||52||

53evaṃ śūrau maheṣvāsau visṛjantau śitān śarān
|
ekacchāyaṃ cakratustāvākāśaṃ śaravṛṣṭibhiḥ ||53||

54tatrārjunena muktānāṃ patatāṃ vai śarīriṣu
|
parvateṣviva vajrāṇāṃ śarāṇāṃ śrūyate svanaḥ ||54||

55tato nāgā rathāścaiva vājinaśca viśāṃpate
|
śoṇitāktā vyadṛśyanta puṣpitā iva kiṃśukāḥ ||55||

56bāhubhiśca sakeyūrairvicitraiśca mahārathaiḥ
|
suvarṇacitraiḥ kavacairdhvajaiśca vinipātitaiḥ ||56||

57yodhaiśca nihataistatra pārthabāṇaprapīḍitaiḥ
|
balamāsīt samudbhrāntaṃ droṇārjunasamāgame ||57||

58vidhunvānau tu tau tatra dhanuṣī bhārasādhane
|
ācchādayetāmanyonyaṃ tatakṣaturatheṣubhiḥ ||58||

59tayoḥ samabhavadyuddhaṃ tumulaṃ bharatarṣabha
|
droṇakaunteyayostatra balivāsavayoriva ||59||

60atha pūrṇāyatotsṛṣṭaiḥ śaraiḥ sannataparvabhiḥ
|
vyadārayetāmanyonyaṃ prāṇadyūte pravartite ||60||

61athāntarikṣe nādo'bhūddroṇaṃ tatra praśaṃsatām
|
duṣkaraṃ kṛtavāndroṇo yadarjunamayodhayat ||61||

62pramāthinaṃ mahāvīryaṃ dṛḍhamuṣṭiṃ durāsadam
|
jetāraṃ devadaityānāṃ sarveṣāṃ ca mahāratham ||62||

63avibhramaṃ ca śikṣāṃ ca lāghavaṃ dūrapātitām
|
pārthasya samare dṛṣṭvā droṇasyābhūcca vismayaḥ ||63||

64atha gāṇḍīvamudyamya divyaṃ dhanuramarṣaṇaḥ
|
vicakarṣa raṇe pārtho bāhubhyāṃ bharatarṣabha ||64||

65tasya bāṇamayaṃ varṣaṃ śalabhānāmivāyatim
|
dṛṣṭvā te vismitāḥ sarve sādhu sādhvityapūjayan ||65||

66na ca bāṇāntare vāyurasya śaknoti sarpitum
|
aniśaṃ saṃdadhānasya śarānutsṛjatastathā ||66||

67dadarśa nāntaraṃ kaścit pārthasyādada‌to'pi ca ||67||

68tathā śīghrāstrayuddhe tu vartamāneḥ sudāruṇe
|
śīghraṃ śīghrataraṃ pārthaḥ śarānanyānudīrayat ||68||

69tataḥ śatasahastrāṇi śarāṇāṃ nataparvaṇām
|
yugapat prāpataṃstatra droṇasya rathamantikāt ||69||

70kīryamāṇe tadā droṇe śarairgāṇḍīvadhanvanā
|
hāhākāro mahānāsīt sainyānāṃ bharatarṣabha ||70||

71pāṇḍavasya tu śīghrāstraṃ maghavā pratyapūjayat
|
gandharvāpsarasaścaiva ye ca tatra samāgatāḥ ||71||

72tato vṛndena mahatā rathānāṃ rathayūthapaḥ
|
ācāryaputraḥ sahasā pāṇḍavaṃ paryavārayat ||72||

73aśvatthāmā tu tat karma hṛdayena mahātmanaḥ
|
pūjayāmāsa pārthasya kopaṃ cāsyākarodbhṛśam ||73||

74sa manyuvaśamāpannaḥ pārthamabhyadravadraṇe
|
kirañcharasahastrāṇi parjanya iva vṛṣṭimān ||74||

75āvṛtya tu mahābāhuryato drauṇistato hayān
|
antaraṃ pradadau pārtho droṇasya vyapasarpitum ||75||

76sa tu labdhvā'ntaraṃ tūrṇamapāyājjavanairhayaiḥ
|
chinnavarmadhvajaḥ śūro nikṛttaḥ parameṣubhiḥ ||76||
