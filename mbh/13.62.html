Mahābhārata 13.62 (Anuśāsanaparvan)

1yudhiṣṭhira uvāca|
evaṃ deyamidaṃ deyamiti yaṃ śrutirādarāt
|
bahudeyāśca rājānaḥ kiṃsviddānamanuttamam ||1||

2bhīṣma uvāca|
atidānāni sarvāṇi pṛthivī dānamucyate
|
acalā hyakṣayā bhūmirdogdhrī kāmānihottamān ||2||

3dogdhrī vāsāṃsi ratnāni paśūnvrīhiyavāṃstathā
|
bhūmidaḥ sarvabhūteṣu śāśvatīredhate samāḥ ||3||

4yāvadbhūmerāyuriha tāvadbhūmida edhate
|
na bhūmidānādastīha paraṃ kiñcidyudhiṣṭhira ||4||

5apyalpaṃ pradaduḥ sarve pṛthivyā iti naḥ śrutam
|
bhūmimeva daduḥ sarve bhūmiṃ te bhuñjate janāḥ ||5||

6svakarmaiṣopajīvanti narā iha paratra ca
|
bhūmibhūtirmahādevī dātāraṃ kurute priyam ||6||

7ya etāṃ dakṣiṇāṃ dadyādakṣayāṃ rājasattama
|
punarnaratvaṃ saṃprāpya bhavetsa pṛthivīpatiḥ ||7||

8yathā dānaṃ tathā bhoga iti dharmeṣu niścayaḥ
|
saṃgrāme vā tanuṃ jahyāddadyācca pṛthivīmimām ||8||

9ityetatkṣatrabandhūnāṃ vadanti paramāṃ śriyam
|
punāti dattā pṛthivī dātāramiti śuśruma ||9||

10api pāpasamācāraṃ brahmaghnamapi cānṛtam
|
saiva pāpaṃ plāvayati saiva pāpātpramocayet ||10||

11api pāpakṛtāṃ rājñāṃ pratigṛhṇanti sādhavaḥ
|
pṛthivīṃ nānyadicchanti pāvanaṃ jananī yathā ||11||

12nāmāsyāḥ priyada‌tteti guhāṃ devyāḥ sanātanam
|
dānaṃ vā'pyathavā''dānaṃ nāmāsyāḥ prathamapriyam ||12||

13ya etāṃ viduṣe dadyātpṛthivīṃ pṛthivīpatiḥ
|
pṛthivyāmetaddiṣṭaṃ sa rājā rājyamito vrajet ||13||

14punaścāsau janiṃ prāpya rājavat syānna saṃśayaḥ
|
tasmātprāpyaiṣa pṛthivīṃ dadyādviprāya pārthivaḥ||14||

15nābhūmipatinā bhūmiradhiṣṭheyā kathañcana
|
na cāpātreṇa vā prāhyā dattadāne na cācaret ||15||

16ye cānye bhūmimiccheyuḥ kuryurevaṃ na saṃśayaḥ
|
yaḥ sādhorbhūmimādatte na bhūmiṃ vindate tu saḥ ||16||

17bhūmiṃ datvā tu sādhubhyo vindate bhūmimuttamām
|
pretya ceha ca dharmātmā saṃprāpnoti mahadyaśaḥ ||17||

18yasya viprāstu śaṃsanti sādhorbhūmiṃ sadaiva hi
|
na tasya śatrayo rājan praśaṃsanti vasundharām ||18||

19yatkiñcitpuruṣaḥ pāpaṃ kurute vṛttikarśitaḥ
|
api gocarmamātreṇa bhūmidānena pūyate ||19||

20ye'pi saṃkīrṇakarmāṇo rājāno raudrakarmiṇaḥ
|
tebhyaḥ pavitramākhyeyaṃ bhūmidānamanuttamam ||20||

21alpāntaramidaṃ śaśvatpurāṇā menire janāḥ
|
yo yajetāśvamedhena dadyādvā sādhave mahīm ||21||

22api cetsukṛtaṃ kṛtvā śaṅkerannāpi paṇḍitāḥ
|
aśakyamekamevaitadbhūmidānamanuttamam ||22||

23suvarṇaṃ rajataṃ vastraṃ maṇimuktāvasūni ca
|
sarvametanmahāprājño dadāti vasudhāṃ dadat ||23||

24tapo yajñaḥ śrutaṃ śīlamalobhaḥ satyasandhatā
|
gurudaivatapūjā ca etā vartanti bhūmidam ||24||

25bhartṛniḥśreyase yuktāstyaktātmāno raṇe hatāḥ
|
brahmalokagatāḥ siddhā nātikrāmanti bhūmidam ||25||

26yathā janitrī svaṃ putraṃ kṣīreṇa bharate sadā
|
anugṛhṇāti dātāraṃ tathā sarvarasairmahī ||26||

27mṛtyurvai kiṅkaro daṇḍastamo vahniḥ sudāruṇaḥ
|
ghorāśca dāruṇāḥ pāśā nopasarpanti bhūmidam ||27||

28pitṝṃśca pitṛlokasthāndevalokācca devatāḥ
|
santarpayati śāntātmā yo dadāti vasundharām ||28||

29kuśāya priyamāṇāya vṛttiglānāya sīdate
|
bhūmiṃ vṛttikarīṃ datvā satrī bhavati mānavaḥ ||29||

30yathā dhāvati gaurvatsaṃ sravantī vatsalā payaḥ
|
evameva mahābhāga bhūmirbhavati bhūmidam ||30||

31phālakṛṣṭāṃ mahīṃ datvā sabījāṃ saphalāmapi
|
udīrṇaṃ vāpi śaraṇaṃ yathā bhavati kāmadaḥ||31||

32brāhmaṇaṃ vṛttisaṃpannamāhitāgniṃ śucivratam
|
naraḥ pratigrāhya mahīṃ na yāti paramāpadam ||32||

33yathā candramaso vṛddhirahanyahani jāyate
|
tathā bhūmikṛtaṃ dānaṃ sasye sasye vivardhate ||33||

34atra gāthā bhūmigītāḥ kīrtayanti purāvidaḥ
|
yāḥ śrutvā jāmadagnyena dattā bhūḥ kāśyapāya vai ||34||

35māmevādatta māṃ datta māṃ dattvā māmavāpsyatha
|
asmin loke pare caiva taddattaṃ jāyate punaḥ ||35||

36ya imāṃ vyāhṛtiṃ veda brāhmaṇo vedasāṃmitām
|
śrāddhasya kriyamāṇasya brahmabhūyaṃ sa gacchati ||36||

37kṛtyānāmadhiśastānāmariṣṭaśamanaṃ mahat
|
prāyaścittaṃ mahīṃ dattvā punātyubhayato daśa ||37||

38punāti ya idaṃ veda vedavādaṃ tathaiva ca
|
prakṛtiḥ sarvabhūtānāṃ bhūmirvaiśvānarī matā ||38||

39abhiṣicyaiva nṛpatiṃ śrāvayedimamāgamam
|
yathā śrutvā mahīṃ dadyānnādadyātsādhutaśca tām ||39||

40so'yaṃ kṛtsno brāhmaṇārtho rājārthaścāpyasaṃśayaḥ
|
rājā hi dharmakuśalaḥ prathamaṃ bhūtilakṣaṇam ||40||

41atha yeṣāmadharmajño rājā bhavati nāstikaḥ
|
na te sukhaṃ prabudhyanti na sukhaṃ prasvapanti ca ||41||

42sadā bhavanti codvignāstasya duścaritairnarāḥ
|
yogakṣemā hi bahavo rāṣṭraṃ nāsyāviśaṃti tat ||42||

43atha yeṣāṃ punaḥ prājño rājā bhavati dhārmikaḥ
|
sukhaṃ te pratibudhyante susukhaṃ prasvapanti ca ||43||

44tasya rājñaḥ śubhai rājyaiḥ karmabhirnirvṛtā narāḥ
|
yogakṣemeṇa vṛṣṭyā ca vivardhante svakarmabhiḥ ||44||

45sa kulīnaḥ sa puruṣaḥ sa bandhuḥ sa ca puṇyakṛt
|
sa dātā sa ca vikrānto yo dadāti vasuṃdharām ||45||

46ādityā iva dīpyante tejasā bhuvi mānavāḥ
|
dadanti vasudhāṃ sphītāṃ ye veda‌viduṣi dvije ||46||

47yathā sasyāni rohanti prakīrṇāni mahītale
|
tathā kāmāḥ prarohanti bhūmidānasamārjitāḥ ||47||

48ādityo varuṇo viṣṇurbrahmā somo hutāśanaḥ
|
śūlapāṇiśca bhagavān pratinandanti bhūmidam ||48||

49bhūmau jāyanti puruṣā bhūmau niṣṭhāṃ vrajanti ca
|
caturvidho hi loko'yaṃ yo'yaṃ bhūmiguṇātmakaḥ ||49||

50eṣā mātā pitā caiva jagataḥ pṛthivīpate
|
nānayā sadṛśaṃ bhūtaṃ kiñcidasti janādhipa ||50||

51atrāpyudāharantīmamitihāsaṃ purātanam
|
bṛhaspateśca saṃvāda‌mindrasya ca yudhiṣṭhira ||51||

52iṣṭvā kratuśatenātha mahatā dakṣiṇāvatā
|
maghavā vāgvidāṃ śreṣṭhaṃ papracchedaṃ bṛhaspatim ||52||

53maghavovāca |
bhagavan kena dānena svargataḥ sukhamedhate
|
yadakṣayaṃ mahārghaṃ ca tadbrūhi vadatāṃ vara ||53||

54bhīṣma uvāca|
ityuktaḥ sa surendreṇa tato devapurohitaḥ
|
bṛhaspatirbṛhattejāḥ pratyuvāca śatakratum ||54||

55bṛhaspatiruvāca|
suvarṇadānaṃ godānaṃ bhūmidānaṃ ca vṛtrahan
|
dadadetānmahāprājñaḥ sarvapāpaiḥ pramucyate ||55||

56na bhūmidānāddevendra paraṃ kiñciditi prabho
|
viśiṣṭamiti manyāmi yathā prāhurmanīṣiṇaḥ||56||

57ye śūrā nihatā yuddhe svaryātā raṇagṛddhinaḥ
|
sarve te vibudhaśreṣṭha nātikrāmanti bhūmidam ||57||

58bharturniḥśreyase yuktāstyaktātmāno raṇe hatāḥ
|
brahmalokagatā muktā nātikrāmanti bhūmidam||58||

59pañca pūrvā hi puruṣāḥ ṣaḍanye vasudhāṃ gatāḥ
|
ekādaśa dadadbhūmiṃ paritrātīha mānavaḥ ||59||

60ratnopakīrṇāṃ vasudhāṃ yo dadāti purandara
|
sa muktaḥ sarvakaluṣaiḥ svargaloke mahīyate ||60||

61mahīṃ sphītāṃ dadadrājan sarvakāmaguṇānvitām
|
rājādhirājo bhavati taddhi dānamanuttamam ||61||

62sarvakāmasamāyuktāṃ kāśyapīṃ yaḥ prayacchati
|
sarvabhūtāni manyante māṃ dadātīti vāsava ||62||

63sarvakāmadughāṃ dhenuṃ sarvakāmaguṇānvitām
|
dadāti yaḥ sahasrākṣa svargaṃ yāti sa mānavaḥ ||63||

64madhusarpiḥ pravāhiṇyaḥ payodadhivahāstathā
|
saritastarpayantīha surendra vasudhāpradam ||64||

65bhūmipradānānnṛpatirmucyate sarvakilbiṣāt
|
na hi bhūmipradānena dānamanyadviśiṣyate ||65||

66dadāti yaḥ samudrāntāṃ pṛthivīṃ śastranirjitām
|
taṃ janāḥ kathayantīha yāvadbhavati gauriyam ||66||

67puṇyāmṛddhirasāṃ bhūmiṃ yo dadāti puraṃdara
|
na tasya lokāḥ śrīyante bhūmidānaguṇānvitāḥ ||67||

68sarvadā pārthiveneha satataṃ bhūtimicchatā
|
bhūrdeyāḥ vidhivacchakra pātre sukhamabhīpsunā ||68||

69api kṛtvā naraḥ pāpaṃ bhūmiṃ dattvā dvijātaye
|
samutsṛjati tatpāpaṃ jīrṇāṃ tvacamivoragaḥ ||69||

70sāgarānsaritaḥ śailān kānanāni ca sarvaśaḥ
|
sarvametannaraḥ śakra dadāti vasudhāṃ dadat ||70||

71taḍāgānyudapānāni strotāṃsi ca sarāṃsi ca
|
snehānsarvarasāṃścaiva dadāti vasudhāṃ dadat ||71||

72oṣadhīrvīryasaṃpannā nagānpuṣpaphalānvitān
|
kānanopalaśailāṃśca dadāti vasudhāṃ dadat ||72||

73agniṣṭomaprabhṛtibhiriṣṭvā ca svāptadakṣiṇaiḥ
|
na tatphalamavāpnoti bhūmidānādyada‌śnute ||73||

74dātā daśānugṛhṇāti daśa hanti tathā kṣipan
|
pūrvadattāṃ haran bhūmiṃ narakāyopagacchati ||74||

75na dadāti pratiśrutya dattvā'pi ca harettu yaḥ
|
sa baddho vāruṇaiḥ pāśaistapyate mṛtyuśāsanāt ||75||

76āhitāgniṃ sadāyajñaṃ kuśavṛttiṃ priyātithim
|
ye bhajanti dvijaśreṣṭhaṃ nopasarpanti te yamam ||76||

77brāhmaṇeṣvanṛṇībhūtaḥ pārthivaḥ syātpuraṃdara
|
itareṣāṃ tu varṇānāṃ tārayetkṛśadurbalān ||77||

78nācchindyātsparśitāṃ bhūmiṃ pareṇa tridaśādhipa
|
brāhmaṇasya suraśreṣṭha kṛśavṛtteḥ kadācana ||78||

79yathā'śru patitaṃ teṣāṃ dīnānāmatha sīdatām
|
brāhmaṇānāṃ hṛte kṣetre hanyāttripuruṣaṃ kulam ||79||

80bhūmipālaṃ cyutaṃ rāṣṭrādyastu saṃsthāpayennaraḥ
|
tasya vāsaḥ sahasrākṣa nākapṛṣṭhe mahīyate ||80||

81ikṣubhiḥ santatāṃ bhūmiṃ yavagodhūmaśālinīm
|
go'śvavāhanapūrṇāṃ vā bāhuvīryādupārjitām||81||

82nidhigarbhāṃ dadadbhūmiṃ sarvaratnaparicchadām
|
akṣayā~llabhate lokān bhūmisatraṃ hi tasya tat ||82||

83vidhūya kaluṣaṃ sarvaṃ virajāḥ saṃmataḥ satām
|
loke mahīyate sadbhiryo dadāti vasuṃdharām ||83||

84yathā'psu patitaḥ śakra tailabindurvisarpati
|
tathā bhūmikṛtaṃ dānaṃ sasye sasye vivardhate ||84||

85ye raṇāgre mahīpālāḥ śūrāḥ samitiśobhanāḥ
|
vadhyante'bhimukhāḥ śakra brahmalokaṃ vrajanti te||85||

86nṛtyagītaparā nāryo divyamālyavibhūṣitāḥ
|
upatiṣṭhanti devendra tathā bhūmipradaṃ divi ||86||

87modate ca sukhaṃ svarge devagandharvapūjitaḥ
|
yo dadāti mahīṃ samyagvidhineha dvijātaye ||87||

88śatamapsarasaścaiva divyamālyavibhūṣitāḥ
|
upatiṣṭhanti devendra brahmaloke dharāpradam ||88||

89upatiṣṭhanti puṇyāni sadā bhūmipradaṃ naram
|
śaṅkhabhadrāsanaṃ chatraṃ varāśvā varavāhanam ||89||

90bhūmipradānātpuṣpāṇi hiraṇyanicayāstathā
|
ājñā sadā'pratihatā jayaśabdā vasūni ca ||90||

91bhūmidānasya puṇyāni phalaṃ svargaḥ puraṃdara
|
hiraṇyapuṣpāścauṣadhyaḥ kuśakāñcanaśādvalāḥ ||91||

92amṛtaprasavāṃ bhūmiṃ prāpnoti puruṣo dadat
|
nāsti bhūmisamaṃ dānaṃ nāsti mātṛsamo guruḥ ||92||

93nāsti satyasamo dharmo nāsti dānasamo nidhiḥ
|
etadāṅgirasācchrutvā vāsavo vasudhāmimām
|
vasuratnasamākīrṇāṃ dadāvāṅgirase tadā ||93||

94ya idaṃ śrāvayecchrāddhe bhūmidānasya saṃbhavam
|
na tasya rakṣasāṃ bhāgo nāsurāṇāṃ bhavatyuta ||94||

95akṣayaṃ ca bhaveddattaṃ pitṛbhyastanna saṃśayaḥ
|
tasmāchrāddheṣvidaṃ vidvān bhuñjataḥśrāvayeddvijān ||95||

96ityetatsarvadānānāṃ śreṣṭhamuktaṃ tavānagha
|
mayā bharataśārdūla kiṃ bhūyaḥ śrotumicchasi ||96||
