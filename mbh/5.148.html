Mahābhārata 5.148 (Udyogaparvan)

1vāsudeva uvāca|
bhīṣmeṇokte tato droṇo duryodhanamabhāṣata
|
madhye nṛpāṇāṃ bhadraṃ te vacanaṃ vacanakṣamaḥ ||1||

2prātīpaḥ śāntanustāta kulasyārthe yathā sthitaḥ
|
yathā devavrato bhīṣmaḥ kulasyārthe sthito'bhavat ||2||

3tathā pāṇḍurnarapatiḥ satyasandho jitendriyaḥ
|
rājā kurūṇāṃ dharmātmā suvrataḥ susamāhitaḥ ||3||

4jyeṣṭhāya rājyamadadaddhṛtarāṣṭrāya dhīmate
|
yavīyase tathā kṣatre kurūṇāṃ vaṃśavardhanaḥ ||4||

5tataḥ siṃhāsane rājan sthāpayitvainamacyutam
|
vanaṃ jagāma kauravyo bhāryābhyāṃ sahito nṛpaḥ ||5||

6nīcaiḥ sthitvā tu vidura upāste sma vinītavat
|
preṣyavat puruṣavyāghro bālavyajanamutkṣipan ||6||

7tataḥ sarvāḥ prajāstāta dhṛtarāṣṭraṃ janeśvaram
|
anvapadyanta vidhivadyathā pāṇḍuṃ janādhipam ||7||

8visṛjya dhṛtarāṣṭrāya rājyaṃ savidurāya ca
|
cacāra pṛthivīṃ pāṇḍuḥ sarvāṃ parapurañjayaḥ ||8||

9kośasaṃvanane dāne bhṛtyānāṃ cānvavekṣaṇe
|
bharaṇe caiva sarvasya viduraḥ satyasaṅgaraḥ ||9||

10sandhi-vigrahasaṃyukto rājñāṃ saṃvāhanakriyāḥ
|
avaikṣata mahātejā bhīṣmaḥ parapurañjayaḥ ||10||

11siṃhāsanastho nṛpatirdhṛtarāṣṭro mahābalaḥ
|
anvāsyamānaḥ satataṃ vidureṇa mahātmanā ||11||

12kathaṃ tasya kule jātaḥ kulabhedaṃ vyavasyasi
|
sambhūya bhrātṛbhiḥ sārdhaṃ bhuṅkṣva bhogān janādhipa ||12||

13bravīmyahaṃ na kārpaṇyānnārthahetoḥ kathañcana
|
bhīṣmeṇa dattamicchāmi na tvayā rājasattama ||13||

14nāhaṃ tvatto'bhikāṅkṣiṣye vṛttyupāyaṃ janādhipa
|
yato bhīṣmastato droṇo yadbhīṣmastvāha tat kuru ||14||

15dīyatāṃ pāṇḍuputrebhyo rājyārdhamarikarśana
|
samamācāryakaṃ tāta tava teṣāṃ ca me sadā ||15||

16aśvatthāmā yathā mahyaṃ tathā śvetaha‌yo mama
|
bahunā kiṃ pralāpena yato dharmastato jayaḥ ||16||

17vāsudeva uvāca|
evamukte mahārāja droṇenāmitatejasā
|
vyājahāra tato vākyaṃ viduraḥ satyasaṅgaraḥ
|
piturvadanamanvīkṣya parivṛtya ca dharmavit ||17||

18vidura uvāca|
devavrata nibodhedaṃ vacanaṃ mama bhāṣataḥ
|
pranaṣṭaḥ kauravo vaṃśastvayā'yaṃ punaruddhṛtaḥ ||18||

19tanme vilapamānasya vacanaṃ samupekṣase
|
ko'yaṃ duryodhano nāma kule'smin kulapāṃsanaḥ ||19||

20yasya lobhābhibhūtasya matiṃ samanuvartase
|
anāryasyākṛtajñasya lobhena hṛtacetasaḥ ||20||

21atikrāmati yaḥ śāsraṃ piturdharmārthadarśinaḥ
|
ete naśyanti kuravo duryodhanakṛtena vai ||21||

22yathā te na praṇaśyeyurmahārāja tathā kuru
|
māṃ caiva dhṛtarāṣṭraṃ ca pūrvameva mahāmate ||22||

23citrakāra ivālekhyaṃ kṛtvā sthāpitavānasi
|
prajāpatiḥ prajāḥ sṛṣṭvā yathā saṃharate tathā ||23||

24nopekṣasva mahābāho paśyamānaḥ kulakṣayam
|
atha te'dya matirnaṣṭā vināśe pratyupasthite ||24||

25vanaṃ gaccha mayā sārdhaṃ dhṛtarāṣṭreṇa caiva ha
|
baddhvā vā nikṛtiprajñaṃ dhārtarāṣṭraṃ sudurmatim ||25||

26śādhīdaṃ rājyamadyāśu pāṇḍavairabhirakṣitam
|
prasīda rājaśārdūla vināśo dṛśyate mahān ||26||

27pāṇḍavānāṃ kurūṇāṃ ca rājñāmamitatejasām
|
virarāmaivamuktvā tu viduro dīnamānasaḥ
|
pradhyāyamānaḥ sa tadā niḥśvasaṃśca punaḥ punaḥ ||27||

28tato'tha rājñaḥ subalasya putrī dharmārthayuktaṃ kulanāśabhītā
|
duryodhanaṃ pāpamatiṃ nṛśaṃsaṃ rājñāṃ samakṣaṃ sutamāha kopāt ||28||

29ye pārthivā rājasabhāṃ praviṣṭā brahmarṣayo ye ca sabhāsado'nye
|
śṛṇvantu vakṣyāmi tavāparādhaṃ pāpasya sāmātyaparicchadasya ||29||

30rājyaṃ kurūṇāmanupūrvabhojyaṃ kramāgato naḥ kuladharma eṣaḥ
|
tvaṃ pāpabuddhe'tinṛśaṃsakarman rājyaṃ kurūṇāmanayādvihaṃsi ||30||

31rājye sthito dhṛtarāṣṭro manīṣī tasyānujo viduro dīrghadarśī
|
etāvatikramya kathaṃ nṛpatvaṃ duryodhana prārthayase'dya mohāt ||31||

32rājā ca kṣattā ca mahānubhāvau bhīṣme sthite paravantau bhavetām
|
ayaṃ tu dharmajñatayā mahātmā na kāmayedyo nṛvaro nadījaḥ ||32||

33rājyaṃ tu pāṇḍoridamapradhṛṣyaṃ tasyādya putrāḥ prabhavanti nānye
|
rājyaṃ tadetannikhilaṃ pāṇḍavānāṃ paitāmahaṃ putrapautrānugāmi ||33||

34yadvai brūte kurumukhyo mahātmā devavrataḥ satyasandho manīṣī
|
sarvaṃ tadasmābhirahatya kāryaṃ rājyaṃ svadharmān paripālayadbhiḥ ||34||

35anujñayā cātha mahāvratasya brūyānnṛpo'yaṃ vidurastathaiva
|
kāryaṃ bhavettatsuhṛdbhirniyojyaṃ dharmaṃ puraskṛtya sudīrghakālam ||35||

36nyāyāgataṃ rājyamidaṃ kurūṇāṃ yudhiṣṭhiraḥ śāstu vai dharmaputraḥ
|
pracodito dhṛtarāṣṭreṇa rājñā puraskṛtaḥ śāntanavena caiva ||36||
