Mahābhārata 12.339 (Śāntiparvan)

1bhīṣma uvāca|
evaṃ stutaḥ sa bhagavān guhyaistathyaiśca nāmabhiḥ
|
taṃ muniṃ darśayāmāsa nāradaṃ viśvarūpadhṛk||1||

2kiñciccandrādviśuddhātmā kiñciccandrādviśeṣavān
|
kṛśānuvarṇaḥ kiñcicca kiñciddhiṣṇyākṛtiḥ prabhuḥ||2||

3śukapatranibhaḥ kiñcit kiñcitsphaṭikasannibhaḥ
|
nīlāñjanacayaprakhyo jātarūpaprabhaḥ kvacit||3||

4pravālāṅkuravarṇaśca śvetavarṇastathā kvacit
|
kvacitsuvarṇavarṇābho vaidūryasadṛśaḥ kvacit||4||

5nīlavaidūryasadṛśa indranīlanibhaḥ kvacit
|
mayūragrīvavarṇābho muktāhāranibhaḥ kvacit||5||

6etānbahuvidhānvarṇān rūpairbibhratsanātanaḥ
|
sahasranayanaḥ śrīmāñchataśīrṣaḥ sahasrapāt||6||

7sahasrodarabāhuśca avyakta iti ca kvacit
|
oṅkāramudgiranvaktrātsāvitrīṃ ca tadanvayām||7||

8śeṣebhyaścaiva vaktrebhyaścaturvedān giranbahūn
|
āraṇyakaṃ jagau devo harirnārāyaṇo vaśī||8||

9vediṃ kamaṇḍaluṃ śubhrānmaṇīnupānahau kuśān
|
ajinaṃ daṇḍakāṣṭhaṃ ca jvalitaṃ ca hutāśanam||9||

10dhārayāmāsa deveśo hastairyajñapatistadā
|
taṃ prasannaṃ prasannātmā nārado dvijasattamaḥ||10||

11vāgyataḥ praṇato bhūtvā vavande parameśvaram
|
tamuvāca nataṃ mūrdhnā devānāmādiravyayaḥ||11||

12śrībhagavānuvāca |
ekataśca dvitaścaiva tritaścaiva maharṣayaḥ
|
imaṃ deśamanuprāptā mama darśanalālasāḥ||12||

13na ca māṃ te dadṛśire na ca drakṣyati kaścana
|
ṛte hyaikāntikaśreṣṭhāttvaṃ caivaikāntikottama||13||

14mamaitāstanavaḥ śreṣṭhā jātā dharmagṛhe dvija
|
tāstvaṃ bhajasva satataṃ sādhayasva yathāgatam||14||

15vṛṇīṣva ca varaṃ vipra mattastvaṃ yadihecchasi
|
prasanno'haṃ tavādyeha viśvamūrtirihāvyayaḥ||15||

16nārada uvāca|
adya me tapaso deva yamasya niyamasya ca
|
sadyaḥ phalamavāptaṃ vai dṛṣṭo yadbhagavānmayā||16||

17vara eṣa mamātyantaṃ dṛṣṭastvaṃ yatsanātanaḥ
|
bhagavanviśvadṛk siṃhaḥ sarvamūrtirmahānprabhuḥ||17||

18bhīṣma uvāca|
evaṃ saṃdarśayitvā tu nāradaṃ parameṣṭhinam
|
uvāca vacanaṃ bhūyo gaccha nārada mā ciram||18||

19ime hyanindriyāhārā madbhaktāścandravarcasaḥ
|
ekāgrāścintayeyurmāṃ naiṣāṃ vighno bhavediti||19||

20siddhā hyete mahābhāgāḥ purā hyekāntino'bhavan
|
tamorajobhirnirmuktā māṃ pravekṣyantyasaṃśayam||20||

21na dṛśyaścakṣuṣā yo'sau na spṛśyaḥ sparśanena ca
|
na ghreyaścaiva gandhena rasena ca vivarjitaḥ||21||

22sattvaṃ rajastamaścaiva na guṇāstaṃ bhajanti vai
|
yaśca sarvagataḥ sākṣī lokasyātmeti kathyate||22||

23bhūtagrāmaśarīreṣu naśyatsu na vinaśyati
|
ajo nityaḥ śāśvataśca nirguṇo niṣkalastathā||23||

24dvirdvādaśebhyastattvebhyaḥ khyāto yaḥ pañcaviṃśakaḥ
|
puruṣo niṣkriyaścaiva jñānadṛśyaśca kathyate||24||

25yaṃ praviśya bhavantīha muktā vai dvijasattamāḥ
|
sa vāsudevo vijñeyaḥ paramātmā sanātanaḥ||25||

26paśya devasya māhātmyaṃ mahimānaṃ ca nārada
|
śubhāśubhaiḥ karmabhiryo na lipyati kadācana||26||

27sattvaṃ rajastamaśceti guṇānetānpracakṣate
|
yatte sarvaśarīreṣu tiṣṭhanti vicaranti ca||27||

28etānguṇāṃstu kṣetrajño bhuṅkte naibhiḥ sa bhujyate
|
nirguṇo guṇabhukcaiva guṇasraṣṭā guṇādhikaḥ||28||

29jagatpratiṣṭhā devarṣe pṛthivyapsu pralīyate
|
jyotiṣyāpaḥ pralīyante jyotirvāyau pralīyate||29||

30khe vāyuḥ pralayaṃ yāti manasyākāśameva ca
|
mano hi paramaṃ bhūtaṃ tadavyakte pralīyate||30||

31avyaktaṃ puruṣe brahmanniṣkriye saṃpralīyate
|
nāsti tasmātparataraḥ puruṣādvai sanātanāt||31||

32nityaṃ hi nāsti jagati bhūtaṃ sthāvarajaṅgamam
|
ṛte tamekaṃ puruṣaṃ vāsudevaṃ sanātanam||32||

33sarvabhūtātmabhūto hi vāsudevo mahābalaḥ
|
pṛthivī vāyurākāśamāpo jyotiśca pañcamam||33||

34te sametā mahātmānaḥ śarīramiti saṃjñitam
|
tadā viśati yo brahmannadṛśyo laghuvikramaḥ||34||

35utpanna eva bhavati śarīraṃ ceṣṭayanprabhuḥ
|
na vinā dhātusaṃghātaṃ śarīraṃ bhavati kvacit||35||

36na ca jīvaṃ vinā brahmanvāyavaśceṣṭayantyuta
|
sa jīvaḥ parisaṃkhyātaḥ śeṣaḥ saṃkarṣaṇaḥ prabhuḥ||36||

37tasmātsanatkumāratvaṃ yo'labhatsvena karmaṇā
|
yasmiṃśca sarvabhūtāni pralayaṃ yānti saṃkṣayam||37||

38sa manaḥ sarvabhūtānāṃ pradyumnaḥ paripaṭhyate
|
tasmātprasūto yaḥ kartā kāraṇaṃ kāryameva ca||38||

39tasmātsarvaṃ saṃbhavati jagatsthāvarajaṅgamam
|
so'niruddhaḥ sa īśāno vyaktaḥ sa sarvakarmasu||39||

40yo vāsudevo bhagavānkṣetrajño nirguṇātmakaḥ
|
jñeyaḥ sa eva rājendra jīva: saṃkarṣaṇaḥ prabhuḥ||40||

41saṃkarṣaṇācca pradyumno manobhūtaḥ sa ucyate
|
pradyumnādyo'niruddhastu so'haṃkāraḥ sa īśvaraḥ||41||

42mattaḥ sarvaṃ saṃbhavati jagatsthāvarajaṅgamam
|
akṣaraṃ ca kṣaraṃ caiva saccāsaccaiva nārada||42||

43māṃ praviśya bhavantīha muktā bhaktāstu ye mama
|
ahaṃ hi puruṣo jñeyo niṣkriyaḥ pañcaviṃśakaḥ||43||

44nirguṇo niṣkalaścaiva nirdvandvo niṣparigrahaḥ
|
etattvayā na vijñeyaṃ rūpavāniti dṛśyate||44||

45icchanmuhūrtānnaśyeyamīśo'haṃ jagato guruḥ
|
māyā hyeṣā mayā sṛṣṭā yanmāṃ paśyasi nārada||45||

46sarvabhūtaguṇairyuktaṃ naiva tvaṃ jñātumarhasi
|
mayaitatkathitaṃ samyaktava mūrticatuṣṭayam||46||

47ahaṃ hi jīvasaṃjñāto mayi jīvaḥ samāhitaḥ
|
naivaṃ te buddhiratrābhūdṝṣṭo jīvo mayeti vai||47||

48ahaṃ sarvatrago brahmanbhūtagrāmāntarātmakaḥ
|
bhūtagrāmaśarīreṣu naśyatsu na naśāmyaham||48||

49siddhā hi te mahābhāgā narā hyekāntino'bhavan
|
tamorajobhyāṃ nirmuktāḥ pravekṣyanti ca māṃ mune||49||

50hiraṇyagarbho lokādiścaturvaktro'niruktagaḥ
|
brahmā sanātano devo mama bahvarthacintakaḥ||50||

51lalāṭāccaiva me rudro devaḥ krodhādviniḥsṛtaḥ
|
paśyaikādaśa me rudrāndakṣiṇaṃ pārśvamāsthitān||51||

52dvādaśaiva tathā''dityān vāmapārśve samāsthitān
|
agrataścaiva me paśya vasūnaṣṭau surottamān||52||

53nāsatyaṃ caiva dasraṃ ca bhiṣajau paśya pṛṣṭhataḥ
|
sarvānprajāpatīnpaśya paśya sapta ṛṣīṃstathā||53||

54vedānyajñāṃśca śataśaḥ paśyāmṛtamathauṣadhīḥ
|
tapāṃsi niyamāṃścaiva yamānapi pṛthagvidhān||54||

55tathā'ṣṭaguṇamaiśvaryamekasthaṃ paśya mūrtimat
|
śriyaṃ lakṣmīṃ ca kīrtiṃ ca pṛthivīṃ ca kakudminīm||55||

56vedānāṃ mātaraṃ paśya matsthāṃ devīṃ sarasvatīm
|
dhruvaṃ ca jyotiṣāṃ śreṣṭha paśya nārada khecaram||56||

57aṃbhodharānsamudrāṃśca sarāṃsi saritastathā
|
mūrtimantaḥ pitṛgaṇāṃścaturaḥ paśya sattama||57||

58trīṃścaivemānguṇānpaśya matsthānmūrtivivarjitān
|
devakāryādapi mune pitṛkāryaṃ viśiṣyate||58||

59devānāṃ ca pitṝṇāṃ ca pitā hyeko'hamāditaḥ
|
ahaṃ hayaśirā bhūtvā samudre paścimottare||59||

60pibāmi suhutaṃ havyaṃ kavyaṃ ca śraddhayā'nvitam
|
mayā sṛṣṭaḥ purā brahmā māṃ yajñamayajatsvayam||60||

61tatastasminvarānprīto dattvānasamyanuttamān
|
matputratvaṃ ca kalpādau lokādhyakṣatvameva ca||61||

62ahaṃkārakṛtaṃ caiva nāma paryāyavācakam
|
tvayā kṛtāṃ ca maryādā nātikraṃsyati kaścana||62||

63tvaṃ caiva varado brahmanvarepsūnāṃ bhaviṣyasi
|
surāsuragaṇānāṃ ca ṛṣīṇāṃ ca tapodhana||63||

64pitṝṇāṃ ca mahābhāga satataṃ saṃśitavrata
|
vividhānāṃ ca bhūtānāṃ tvamupāsyo bhaviṣyasi||64||

65prādurbhāvagataścāhaṃ surakāryeṣu nityadā
|
anuśāsyastvayā brahmanniyojyaśca suto yathā||65||

66etāṃścānyāṃśca rucirānbrahmaṇe'mitatejase
|
ahaṃ dattvā varānprīto nivṛttiparamo'bhavam||66||

67nirvāṇaṃ sarvadharmāṇāṃ nivṛttiḥ paramā smṛtā
|
tasmānnivṛttimāpannaścaretsarvāṅganirvṛtaḥ||67||

68vidyāsahāyavantaṃ ca ādityasthaṃ samāhitam
|
kapilaṃ prāhurācāryāḥ sāṃkhyaniścitaniścayāḥ||68||

69hiraṇyagarbho bhagavāneṣa cchandasi suṣṭutaḥ
|
so'haṃ yogaratirbrahmanyogaśāstreṣu śabditaḥ||69||

70eṣo'haṃ vyaktimāgatya tiṣṭhāmi divi śāśvataḥ
|
tato yugasahasrānte saṃhariṣye jagatpunaḥ||70||

71kṛtvā''tmasthāni bhūtāni sthāvarāṇi carāṇi ca
|
ekākī vidyayā sārdhaṃ vihariṣye jagatpunaḥ||71||

72tato bhūyo jagatsarvaṃ kariṣyāmīha vidyayā
|
asminmūrtiścaturthī yā sā'sṛjajcheṣamavyayam||72||

73sa hi saṃkarṣaṇaḥ proktaḥ pradyumnaṃ so'pyajījanat
|
pradyumnādaniruddho'haṃ sargo mama punaḥ punaḥ||73||

74aniruddhāttathā brahmā tannābhikamalodbhavaḥ
|
brahmaṇaḥ sarvabhūtāni carāṇi sthāvarāṇi ca||74||

75etāṃ sṛṣṭiṃ vijānīhi kalpādiṣu punaḥ punaḥ
|
yathā sūryasya gaganādudayāstamane iha||75||

76naṣṭe punarbalātkāla ānayatyamitadyutiḥ
|
tathā balādahaṃ pṛthvīṃ sarvabhūtahitāya vai||76||

77sattvairākrāntasarvāṅgāṃ naṣṭāṃ sāgaramekhalām
|
ānayiṣyāmi svasthānaṃ vārāhaṃ rūpamāsthitaḥ||77||

78hiraṇyākṣaṃ vadhiṣyāmi daiteyaṃ balagarvitam
|
nārasiṃhaṃ vapuḥ kṛtvā hiraṇyakaśipuṃ punaḥ||78||

79surakārye haniṣyāmi yajñaghnaṃ ditinandanam
|
virocanasya balavānbaliḥ putro mahāsuraḥ||79||

80avadhyaḥ sarvalokānāṃ sadevāsurarakṣasām
|
bhaviṣyati sa śakraṃ vā svarājyāccyāvayiṣyati||80||

81trailokye'pahṛte tena vimukhe ca śacīpatau
|
adityāṃ dvādaśādityaḥ saṃbhaviṣyāmi kaśyapāt||81||

82tato rājyaṃ pradāsyāmi śakrāyāmitatejase
|
devatāḥ sthāpayiṣyāmi svasvasthāneṣu nārada||82||

83baliṃ caiva kariṣyāmi pātālatalavāsinam
|
dānavaṃ ca baliśreṣṭhamavadhyaṃ sarvadaivataiḥ||83||

84tretāyuge bhaviṣyāmi rāmo bhṛgukulodvahaḥ
|
kṣatraṃ cotsādayiṣyāmi samṛddhabalavāhanam||84||

85saṃdhyāṃśe samanuprāpte tretāyā dvāparasya ca
|
ahaṃ dāśarathī rāmo bhaviṣyāmi jagatpatiḥ||85||

86tritopaghātādvairūpyamekato'tha dvitastathā
|
prāpsyete vānaratvaṃ hi prajāpatisutāvṛṣī||86||

87tayorye tvanvaye jātā bhaviṣyanti vanaukasaḥ
|
mahābalā mahāvīryāḥ śakratulyaparākramāḥ||87||

88te sahāyā bhaviṣyanti surakārye mama dvija
|
tato rakṣaḥpatiṃ ghoraṃ pulastyakulapāṃsanam||88||

89hariṣye rāvaṇaṃ raudraṃ sagaṇaṃ lokakaṇṭakam
|
dvāparasya kaleścaiva sandhau pāryavasānike||89||

90prādurbhāva: kaṃsahetormathurāyāṃ bhaviṣyati
|
tatrāhaṃ dānavānhatvā subahūn devakaṇṭakān||90||

91kuśasthalīṃ kariṣyāmi niveśaṃ dvārakāṃ purīm
|
vasānastatra vai puryāmaditervipriyaṃkaram||91||

92haniṣye narakaṃ bhaumaṃ muraṃ pīṭhaṃ ca dānavam
|
prāgjyotiṣaṃ puraṃ ramyaṃ nānādhanasamanvitam||92||

93kuśasthalīṃ nayiṣyāmi hatvā vai dānavottamam
|
maheśvaramahāsenau bāṇapriyahitaiṣiṇau||93||

94parājeṣyāmyathodyuktau devau lokanamaskṛtau
|
tataḥ sutaṃ balerjitvā bāṇaṃ bāhusahasriṇam||94||

95vināśayiṣyāmi tataḥ sarvānsaubhanivāsinaḥ
|
yaḥ kālayavanaḥ khyāto gargatejo'bhisaṃvṛtaḥ||95||

96bhaviṣyati vadhastasya matta eva dvijottama
|
jarāsandhaśca balavānsarvarājavirodhanaḥ||96||

97bhaviṣyatyasuraḥ sphīto bhūmipālo girivraje
|
mama buddhiparispandādvadhastasya bhaviṣyati||97||

98śiśupālaṃ vadhiṣyāmi yajñe dharmasutasya vai
|
samāgateṣu baliṣu pṛthivyāṃ sarvarājasu||98||

99vāsaviḥ susahāyo vai mama tveko bhaviṣyati
|
yudhiṣṭhiraṃ sthāpayiṣye svarājye bhrātṛbhiḥ saha||99||

100evaṃ lokā vadiṣyanti naranārāyaṇāvṛṣī
|
udyuktau dahataḥ kṣatraṃ lokakāryārthamīśvarau||100||

101kṛtvā bhārāvataraṇaṃ vasudhāyā yathepsitam
|
sarvasātvatamukhyānāṃ dvārakāyāśca sattama||101||

102kariṣye pralayaṃ ghoramātmajñānābhisaṃhitaḥ
|
karmāṇyaparimeyāṇi caturmūrtidharo hyaham||102||

103kṛtvā lokāngamiṣyāmi svānahaṃ brahmasatkṛtān
|
haṃsa: kūrmaśca matsyaśca prādurbhāvāddvijottama||103||

104varāho narasiṃhaśca vāmano rāma eva ca
|
rāmo dāśarathiścaiva sātvataḥ kalkireva ca||104||

105yadā vedaśrutirnaṣṭā mayā pratyāhṛtā punaḥ
|
savedāḥ saśrutīkāśca kṛtāḥ pūrvaṃ kṛte yuge||105||

106atikrāntāḥ purāṇeṣu śrutāste yadi vā kvacit
|
atikrāntāśca bahavaḥ prādurbhāvā mamottamāḥ||106||

107lokakāryāṇi kṛtvā ca punaḥ svāṃ prakṛtiṃ gatāḥ
|
na hyetadbrahmaṇā prāptamīdṛśaṃ mama darśanam||107||

108yattvayā prāptamadyeha ekāntagatabuddhinā
|
etatte sarvamākhyātaṃ brahmanbhaktimato mayā||108||

109purāṇaṃ ca bhaviṣyaṃ ca sarahasyaṃ ca sattama
|
bhīṣma uvāca|
evaṃ sa bhagavāndevo viśvamūrtidharo'vyayaḥ||109||

110etāvaduktvā vacanaṃ tatraivāntardadhe punaḥ
|
nārado'pi mahātejāḥ prāpyānugrahamīpsitam||110||

111naranārāyaṇau draṣṭuṃ badaryāśramamādravat
|
idaṃ mahopaniṣadaṃ caturvedasamanvitam||111||

112sāṃkhyayogakṛtaṃ tena pañcarātrānuśabditam
|
nārāyaṇamukhodgītaṃ nārado'śrāvayatpunaḥ||112||

113brahmaṇaḥ sadane tāta yathā dṛṣṭaṃ yathā śrutam
|
yudhiṣṭhira uvāca|
etadāścaryabhūtaṃ hi māhātmyaṃ tasya dhīmataḥ||113||

114kiṃ vai brahmā na jānīte yataḥ śuśrāva nāradāt
|
pitāmaho'pi bhagavāṃstasmāddevādanantaraḥ||114||

115kathaṃ sa na vijānīyātprabhāvamamitaujasaḥ
|
bhīṣma uvāca|
mahākalpasahasrāṇi mahākalpaśatāni ca||115||

116samatītāni rājendra sargāśca pralayāśca ha
|
sargasyādau smṛto brahmā prajāsargakaraḥ prabhuḥ||116||

117jānāti devapravaraṃ bhūyaścāto'dhikaṃ nṛpa
|
paramātmānamīśānamātmanaḥ prabhavaṃ tathā||117||

118ye tvanye brahmasadane siddhasaṃghāḥ samāgatāḥ
|
tebhyastacchrāvayāmāsa purāṇaṃ vedasaṃmitam||118||

119teṣāṃ sakāśātsūryastu śrutvā vai bhāvitātmanām
|
ātmānugāmināṃ rājan śrāvayāmāsa vai tataḥ||119||

120ṣaṭ ṣaṣṭirhi sahasrāṇi ṛṣīṇāṃ bhāvitātmanām
|
sūryasya tapato lokān nirmitā ye puraḥ sarāḥ||120||

121teṣāmakathayatsūryaḥ sarveṣāṃ bhāvitātmanām
|
sūryānugāmibhistāta ṛṣibhistairmahātmabhiḥ||121||

122merau samāgatā devāḥ śrāvitāścedamuttamam
|
devānāṃ tu sakāśādvai tataḥ śrutvāsito dvijaḥ||122||

123śrāvayāmāsa rājendra pitṝṇāṃ munisattamaḥ
|
mama cāpi pitā tāta kathayāmāsa śāntanuḥ||123||

124tato mayā'pi śrutvā ca kīrtitaṃ tava bhārata
|
surairvā munibhirvāpi purāṇaṃ yairidaṃ śrutam||124||

125sarve te paramātmānaṃ pūjayante samantataḥ
|
idamākhyānamārṣeyaṃ pāraṃparyāgataṃ nṛpa||125||

126nāvāsudevābhaktāya tvayā deyaṃ kathañcana
|
matto'nyāni ca te rājannupākhyānaśatāni vai||126||

127yāni śrutāni sarvāṇi teṣāṃ sāro'yamuddhṛtaḥ
|
surāsurairyathā rājannirmathyāmṛtamuddhṛtam||127||
