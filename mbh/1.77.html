Mahābhārata 1.77 (Ādiparvan)

1vaiśaṃpāyana uvāca|
samāvṛtavrataṃ taṃ tu visṛṣṭaṃ guruṇā tadā
|
prasthitaṃ tridaśāvāsaṃ devayānyabravīdidam||1||

2ṛṣeraṅgirasaḥ pautra vṛttenābhijanena ca
|
bhrājase vidyayā caiva tapasā ca damena ca||2||

3ṛṣiryathāṅgirā mānyaḥ piturmama mahāyaśāḥ
|
tathā prānyaśca pūjyaśca mama bhūyo bṛhaspatiḥ||3||

4evaṃ jñātvā vijānīhi yadbravīmi tapodhana
|
vratasthe niyamopete yathā vartāmyahaṃ tvayi||4||

5sa samāvṛtavidyo māṃ bhaktāṃ bhajitumarhasi
|
gṛhāṇa pāṇiṃ vidhivanmama mantrapuraskṛtam||5||

6kaca uvāca|
pūjyo mānyaśca bhagavānyathā tava pitā mama
|
tathā tvamanavadyāṅgi pūjanīyatarā mama||6||

7prāṇebhyo'pi priyatarā bhārgavasya mahātmanaḥ
|
tvaṃ bhadre dharmataḥ pūjyā guruputrī sadā mama||7||

8yathā mama gururnityaṃ mānyaḥ śukraḥ pitā tava
|
devayāni tathaiva tvaṃ naivaṃ māṃ vaktumarhasi||8||

9devayānyuvāca|
guruputrasya putro vai na tvaṃ putraśca me pituḥ
|
tasmātpūjyaśca mānyaśca mamāpi tvaṃ dvijottama||9||

10asurairhanyamāne ca kaca tvayi punaḥpunaḥ
|
tadā prabhṛti yā prītistāṃ tvamadya smarasva me||10||

11sauhārde cānurāge ca vettha me bhaktimuttamām
|
na māmarhasi dharmajña tyaktuṃ bhaktāmanāgasam||11||

12kaca uvāca|
aniyojye niyoge māṃ niyunaṅkṣi śubhavrate
|
prasīda subhru tvaṃ mahyaṃ gurorgurutarā śubhe||12||

13yatroṣitaṃ viśālākṣi tvayā candranibhānane
|
tatrāhamuṣito bhadre kukṣau kāvyasya bhāmini||13||

14bhaginī dharmato me tvaṃ maivaṃ vocaḥ sumadhyame
|
sukhamasmyuṣito bhadre na manyurvidyate mama|| 14||

15āpṛcche tvāṃ gamiṣyāmi śivamāśaṃsa me pathi
|
avirodhena dharmasya smartavyo'smi kathāntare
|
apramattotthitā nityamārādhaya guruṃ mama||15||

16devayānyuvāca|
yadi māṃ dharmakāmārthe pratyākhyāsyasi yācitaḥ
|
tataḥ kaca na te vidyā siddhimeṣā gamiṣyati||16||

17kaca uvāca|
guruputrīti kṛtvā'haṃ pratyācakṣe na doṣataḥ
|
guruṇā cānanujñātaḥ kāmamevaṃ śapasva mām||17||

18ārṣaṃ dharmaṃ bruvāṇo'haṃ devayāni yathā tvayā
|
śapto nārho'smi śāpasya kāmato'dya na dharmataḥ||18||

19tasmādbhavatyā yaḥ kāmo na tathā sa bhaviṣyati
|
ṛṣiputro na te kaścijjātu pāṇiṃ grahīṣyati||19||

20phaliṣyati na te vidyā yattvaṃ māmāttha tattathā
|
adhyāpayiṣyāmi tu yaṃ tasya vidyā phaliṣyati||20||

21vaiśaṃpāyana uvāca|
evamuktvā dvijaśreṣṭho devayānīṃ kacastadā
|
tridaśeśālayaṃ śīghraṃ jagāma dvijasattamaḥ||21||

22tamāgatamabhiprekṣya devā indrapurogamāḥ
|
bṛhaspatiṃ sabhājyedaṃ kacaṃ vacanamabruvan||22||

23devā ūcuḥ|
yattvayā'smaddhitaṃ karma kṛtaṃ vai paramādbhutam
|
na te yaśaḥ praṇaśitā bhāgabhāktvaṃ bhaviṣyasi||23||
