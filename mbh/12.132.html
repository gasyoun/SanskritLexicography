Mahābhārata 12.132 (Śāntiparvan)

1yudhiṣṭhira uvāca|
hīne paramake dharme sarvalokābhisaṃhite
|
sarvasmindasyusādbhūte pṛthivyāmupajīvane ||1||

2kena svidbrāhmaṇo jīvejjaghanye kāla āgate
|
asaṃtyajanputrapautrānanukrośāt pitāmaha ||2||

3bhīṣma uvāca|
vijñānabalamāsthāya jīvitavyaṃ tathāgate
|
sarvaṃ sādhvarthamevedamasādhvarthaṃ na kiṃcana ||3||

4asādhubhyo'rthamādāya sādhubhyo yaḥ prayacchati
|
ātmānaṃ saṃkramaṃ kṛtvā kṛcchradharmavideva saḥ ||4||

5ākāṅkṣannātmano rājyaṃ rājye sthitimakopayan
|
adattamevādadīta dāturvittaṃ mameti ca ||5||

6vijñānabalapūto yo vartate ninditeṣvapi
|
vṛttivijñānavāndhīraḥ kastaṃ vā vaktumarhati ||6||

7yeṣāṃ balakṛtā vṛttisteṣāmanyā na rocate
|
tejasā'bhipravartante balavanto yudhiṣṭhira ||7||

8yadaiva prākṛtaṃ śāstramaviśeṣeṇa vartate
|
tadaivamabhyasedevaṃ medhāvī vā'pyathottaram ||8||

9ṛtvikpurohitācāryān satkṛtānabhisatkṛtān
|
na brāhmaṇānghātayīta doṣānprāpnoti ghātayan ||9||

10etatpramāṇaṃ lokasya cakṣuretatsanātanam
|
tatpramāṇo'vagāheta tena tatsādhvasādhu vā ||10||

11bahavo grāmavāstavyā roṣādbrūyuḥ parasparam
|
na teṣāṃ vacanādrājā satkuryādghātayīta vā ||11||

12na vācyaḥ parivādo'yaṃ na śrotavyaḥ kathañcana
|
karṇāvathāpidhātavyau prastheyaṃ cānyato bhavet ||12||

13asatāṃ śīlametadvai parivādo'tha paiśunam
|
guṇānāmeva vaktāraḥ santaḥ satsu narādhipa ||13||

14yathā sumadhurau damyau sudāntau sādhuvāhinau
|
dhuramudyamya vahatastathā varteta vai nṛpaḥ ||14||

15yathā yathā'sya bahavaḥ sahāyāḥ syustathā pare
|
ācārameva manyante garīyo dharmalakṣaṇam ||15||

16apare naivamicchanti ye śaṅkhalikhitapriyāḥ
|
mātsaryādathavā lobhānna brūyurvākyamīdṛśam ||16||

17ārṣamapyatra paśyanti vikarmasthasya pātanam
|
na tādṛksadṛśaṃ kiñcitpramāṇaṃ dṛśyate kvacit ||17||

18devatāśca vikarmasthaṃ pātayanti narādhamam
|
vyājena vindanvittaṃ hi dharmātsa parihīyate ||18||

19sarvataḥ satkṛtaḥ sadbhirbhūtipravarakāraṇaiḥ
|
hṛdayenābhyanujñāto yo dharmastaṃ vyavasyati ||19||

20yaścaturguṇasampannaṃ dharmaṃ brūyāt sa dharmavit
|
aheriva hi dharmasva padaṃ duḥkhaṃ gaveṣitum ||20||

21yathā mṛgasya viddhasya padamekapadaṃ nayet
|
lakṣedrudhiralepena tathā dharmapadaṃ nayet ||21||

22evaṃ saddhirvinītena pathā gantavyamityuta
|
rājarṣīṇāṃ vṛttametadevaṃ gaccha yudhiṣṭhira ||22||
