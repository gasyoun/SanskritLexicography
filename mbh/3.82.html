Mahābhārata 3.82 (Āraṇyakaparvan)

1pulastya uvāca|
anena tava dharmajña praśrayeṇa damena ca
|
satyena ca mahābhāga tuṣṭo'smi tava suvrata||1||

2yasyedṛśaste dharmo'yaṃ pitṛbhaktyāśrito'nagha
|
tena paśyasi māṃ putra prītiśca paramā tvayi||2||

3amoghadarśī bhīṣmāhaṃ brūhi kiṃ karavāṇi te
|
yadvakṣyasi kuruśreṣṭha tasya dātāsmi te'nagha||3||

4bhīṣma uvāca|
prīte tvayi mahābhāga sarvalokābhipūjite
|
kṛtamitāvatā manye yadahaṃ dṛṣṭavānprabhum||4||

5yadi tvahamanugrāhyastava dharmabhṛtāṃ vara
|
saṃdehaṃ te pravakṣyāmi tanme tvaṃ chettumarhasi||5||

6asti me hṛdaye kaścittīrthebhyo dharmasaṃśayaḥ
|
tamahaṃ śrotumicchāmi tadbhavānvaktumarhati||6||

7pradakṣiṇāṃ yaḥ pṛthivīṃ karotyamarasannibha
|
kiṃ phalaṃ tasya viprarṣe tanme brūhi suniścitam||7||

8pulastya uvāca|
hanta te kathayiṣyāmi yadṛṣīṇāṃ parāyaṇam
|
tadekāgramanāḥ putra śṛṇu tīrtheṣu yatphalam||8||

9yasya hastau ca pādau ca manaścaiva susaṃyatam
|
vidyā tapaśca kīrtiśca sa tīrthaphalamaśnute||9||

10pratigrahādapāvṛttaḥ saṃtuṣṭo yena kenacit
|
ahaṃkāranivṛttaśca sa tīrthaphalamaśnute||10||

11akalkako nirārambho laghvāhāro jitendriyaḥ
|
vimuktaḥ sarvapāpebhyaḥ sa tīrthaphalamaśnute||11||

12akrodhanaśca rājendra satyaśīlo dṛḍhavrataḥ
|
ātmopamaśca bhūteṣu sa tīrthaphalamaśnute||12||

13ṛṣibhiḥ kratavaḥ proktā deveṣviha yathākramam
|
phalaṃ caiva yathātathyaṃ pretya ceha ca sarvaśaḥ||13||

14na te śakyā daridreṇa yajñāḥ prāptuṃ mahīpate
|
bahūpakaraṇā yajñā nānāsaṃbhāravistarāḥ||14||

15prāpyante pārthivairetaiḥ samṛddhairvā naraiḥ kvacit
|
nārthanyūnairnāvagaṇairekātmabhirasādhanaiḥ||15||

16yo daridrairapi vidhiḥ śakyaḥ prāptuṃ nareśvara
|
tulyo yajñaphalaiḥ puṇyaistaṃ nibodha yudhāṃ vara||16||

17ṛṣīṇāṃ paramaṃ guhyamidaṃ bharatasattama
|
tīrthābhigamanaṃ puṇyaṃ yajñairapi viśiṣyate||17||

18anapoṣya trirātrāṇi tīrthānyanabhigamya ca
|
adattvā kāñcanaṃ gāśca daridro nāma jāyate||18||

19agniṣṭomādibhiryajñairiṣṭvā vipuladakṣiṇaiḥ
|
na tatphalamavāpnoti tīrthābhigamanena yat||19||

20nṛloke devadevasya tīrthaṃ trailokyaviśrutam
|
puṣkaraṃ nāma vikhyātaṃ mahābhāgaḥ samāviśet||20||

21daśakoṭisahasrāṇi tīrthānāṃ vai mahāmate
|
sānnidhyaṃ puṣkare yeṣāṃ trisandhyaṃ kurunandana||21||

22ādityā vasavo rudrāḥ sādhyāśca samarudgaṇāḥ
|
gandharvāpsarasaścaiva nityaṃ sannihitā vibho||22||

23yatra devāstapastaptvā daityā brahmarṣayastathā
|
divyayogā mahārāja puṇyena mahatānvitāḥ||23||

24manasāpyabhikāmasya puṣkarāṇi manasvinaḥ
|
pūyante sarvapāpāni nākapṛṣṭhe ca pūjyate||24||

25tasmiṃstīrthe mahārāja nityameva pitāmahaḥ
|
uvāsa paramaprīto bhagavānkamalāsanaḥ||25||

26puṣkareṣu mahābhāga devāḥ sarṣigaṇāḥ purā
|
siddhiṃ samabhisaṃprāptāḥ puṇyena mahatānvitāḥ||26||

27tatrābhiṣekaṃ yaḥ kuryātpitṛdevārcane rataḥ
|
aśvamedhāddaśaguṇaṃ phalaṃ prāhurmanīṣiṇaḥ||27||

28apyekaṃ bhojayedvipraṃ puṣkarāraṇyamāśritaḥ
|
tenāsau karmaṇā bhīṣma pretya ceha ca modate||28||

29śākairmūlaiḥ phalairvāpi yena vartayate svayam
|
tadvai dadyādbrāhmaṇāya śraddhāvānanasūyakaḥ||29||

30tenaiva prāpnuyātprājño hayamedhaphalaṃ naraḥ
|
brāhmaṇāḥ kṣatriyā vaiśyāḥ śūdrā vā rājasattama||30||

31na vai yonau prajāyante snātāstīrthe mahātmanaḥ
|
kārtikyāṃ tu viśeṣeṇa yo'bhigacchati puṣkaram||31||

32prāpnuyātsa naro lokānbrahmaṇaḥ sadane'kṣayān
|
sāyaṃprātaḥ smaredyastu puṣkarāṇi kṛtāñjaliḥ||32||

33upaspṛṣṭaṃ bhavettena sarvatīrtheṣu bhārata
|
janmaprabhṛti yatpāpaṃ striyā vā puruṣeṇa vā||33||

34puṣkare snātamātrasya sarvameva praṇaśyati
|
yathā surāṇāṃ sarveṣāmādistu madhusūdanaḥ||34||

35tathaiva puṣkaraṃ rājaṃstīrthānāmādirucyate
|
uṣṭvā dvādaśavarṣāṇi puṣkare niyataḥ śuciḥ||35||

36ṛtūnsarvānavāpnoti brahmalokaṃ sa gacchati
|
yastu varṣaśataṃ pūrṇamagnihotramupāsate||36||

37kārtikīṃ vā vasedekāṃ puṣkare samameva tat||37||

38trīṇi śṛṅgāṇi śubhrāṇi trīṇi prasravaṇāni ca
|
puṣkarāṇyādisiddhāni na vidmastatra kāraṇam||38||

39duṣkaraṃ puṣkare gantuṃ duṣkaraṃ puṣkare tapaḥ
|
duṣkaraṃ puṣkare dānaṃ vastuṃ caiva suduṣkaram||39||

40uṣya dvādaśarātraṃ tu niyato niyatāśanaḥ
|
pradakṣiṇamupāvṛtya jambūmārgaṃ samāviśet||40||

41jambūmārgaṃ samāviśya devarṣipitṛsevitam
|
aśvamedhamavāpnoti viṣṇulokaṃ ca gacchati||41||

42tatroṣya rajanīḥ pañca pūtātmā jāyate naraḥ
|
na durgatimavāpnoti siddhiṃ prāpnoti cottamām||42||

43jambūmārgādupāvṛtya gacchettandulikāśramam
|
na durgatimavāpnoti brahmalokaṃ ca gacchati||43||

44āgastyaṃ sara āsādya pitṛdevārcane rataḥ
|
trirātropoṣito rājannagniṣṭomaphalaṃ labhet||44||

45śākavṛttiḥ phalairvāpi kaumāraṃ vindate padam
|
kaṇvāśramaṃ tato gacchecchrījuṣṭaṃ lokapūjitam||45||

46dharmāraṇyaṃ hi tatpuṇyamādyaṃ ca bharatarṣabha
|
yatra praviṣṭamātro vai sarvapāpaiḥ pramucyate||46||

47arcayitvā pitṝndevānniyato niyatāśanaḥ
|
sarvakāmasamṛddhasya yajñasya phalamaśnute||47||

48pradakṣiṇaṃ tataḥ kṛtvā yayātipatanaṃ vrajet
|
hayamedhasya yajñasya phalaṃ prāpnoti tatra vai||48||

49mahākālaṃ tato gacchenniyato niyatāśanaḥ
|
koṭitīrthamupaspṛśya hayamedhaphalaṃ labhet||49||

50tato gaccheta dharmajñaḥ sthāṇostīrthamumāpateḥ
|
nāmnā bhadravaṭaṃ nāma triṣu lokeṣu viśrutam||50||

51tatrābhigamya ceśānaṃ gosahasraphalaṃ labhet
|
mahādevaprasādācca gāṇapatyaṃ ca vindati||51||

52samṛddhamasapatnaṃ ca śriyā yuktaṃ narottamaḥ
|
narmadāṃ sa samāsādya nadīṃ trailokyaviśrutām||52||

53tarpayitvā pitṝndevānagniṣṭomaphalaṃ labhet
|
dakṣimaṃ sindhumāsādya brahmacārī jitendriyaḥ||53||

54agniṣṭomamavāpnoti vimānaṃ cādhirohati
|
carmaṇvatīṃ samāsādya niyato niyatāśanaḥ
|
rantidevābhyanujñātamagniṣṭomaphalaṃ labhet||54||

55tato gaccheta dharmajñaṃ himavatsutamarbudam
|
pṛthivyāṃ yatra vai chidraṃ pūrvamāsīdyudhiṣṭhira||55||

56tatrāśramo vasiṣṭhasya triṣu lokeṣu viśrutaḥ
|
tatroṣya rajanīmekāṃ gosahasraphalaṃ labhet||56||

57piṅgatīrthamupaspṛśya brahmacārī jitendriyaḥ
|
kapilānāṃ naraśreṣṭha śatasya phalamaśnute||57||

58tato gaccheta rājendra prabhāsaṃ tīrthamuttamam
|
tatra saṃnihito nityaṃ svayameva hutāśanaḥ||58||

59devatānāṃ mukhaṃ vīra jvalano'nilasārathiḥ
|
tasmiṃstīrthe naraḥ snātvā śuciḥ prayatamānasaḥ||59||

60agniṣṭomātirātrābhyāṃ phalaṃ prāpnoti mānavaḥ
|
tato gatvā sarasvatyāḥ sāgarasya ca saṃgame||60||

61gosahasraphalaṃ tasya svargalokaṃ ca vindati
|
prabhayā dīpyate nityamagnivadbharatarṣabha||61||

62tīrthe salilarājasya snātvā prayatamānasaḥ
|
trirātramuṣitaḥ snātastarpayetpitṛdevatāḥ||62||

63prabhāsate yathā somaḥ sośvamedhaṃ ca vindati
|
varadānaṃ tato gacchettīrthaṃ bharatasattama||63||

64viṣṇordurvāsasā yatra varo datto yudhiṣṭhira
|
varadāne naraḥ snātvā gosahasraphaṃla labhet||64||

65tato dvāravatīṃ gacchenniyato niyatāśanaḥ
|
piṇḍārake naraḥ snātvā labhedbahu suvarṇakam||65||

66tasmiṃstīrthe mahābhāga padmalakṣaṇalakṣitāḥ
|
adyāpi mudrā dṛśyante tadadbhutamariṃdama||66||

67triśūlāṅkāni padmāni dṛśyante kurunandana
|
mahādevasya sāṃnidhyaṃ tatra vai puruṣarṣabha||67||

68sāgarasya ca sindhośca saṃgamaṃ prāpya durlabham
|
tīrthe salilarājasya snātvā prayatamānasaḥ||68||

69tarpayitvā pitṝndevānṛṣīṃśca bharatarṣabha
|
prāpnoti vāruṇaṃ lokaṃ dīpyamānaṃ svatejasā||69||

70śaṅkukarṇeśvaraṃ devamarcayitvā yudhiṣṭhira
|
aśvamedhāddaśaguṇaṃ pravadanti manīṣiṇaḥ||70||

71pradakṣiṇamupāvṛtya gaccheta bharatarṣabha
|
tīrthaṃ kuruvaraśreṣṭha triṣu lokeṣu viśrutam||71||

72damīti nāmnā vikhyātaṃ sarvapāpapraṇāśanam
|
tatra brahmādayo devā upāsante maheśvaram||72||

73tatra snātvā ca pītvā ca rudraṃ devagaṇairvṛtam
|
janmaprabhṛti yatpāpaṃ tatsnātasya praṇaśyati||73||

74damī cātra naraśreṣṭha sarvadevairabhiṣṭutaḥ
|
tatra snātvā naravyāghra hayamedhamavāpnuyāt||74||

75gatvā yatra mahāprājña viṣṇunā prabhaviṣṇunā
|
purā śaucaṃ kṛtaṃ rājanhatvā daiteyadānavān||75||

76tato gaccheta dharmajña vasordhārāmabhiṣṭutām
|
gamanādeva tasyāṃ hi hayamedhaphalaṃ labhet||76||

77snātvā kuruvaraśreṣṭha prayatātmā samāhitaḥ
|
tarpya devānpitṝṃścaiva viṣṇuloke mahīyate||77||

78tīrthe cātra saraḥ puṇyaṃ vasūnāṃ bharatarṣabha
|
tatra snātvā ca pītvā ca vasūnāṃ saṃmato bhavet||78||

79sindhūttamamiti khyātaṃ sarvapāpapraṇāśanam
|
tatra snātvā naraśreṣṭha labhedbahu suvarṇakam||79||

80bhadratuṅgaṃ samāsādya śuciḥ śīlasamanvitaḥ
|
brahmalokamavāpnoti gatiṃ ca paramāṃ vrajet||80||

81kumārikāṇāṃ śakrasya tīrthaṃ siddhaniṣevitam
|
tatra snātvā naraḥ kṣipraṃ svargalokamavāpnuyāt||81||

82reṇukāyāśca tatraiva tīrthaṃ siddhaniṣevitam
|
tatra snātvā bhavedvipro nirmalaścandramā yathā||82||

83atha pañcanadaṃ gatvā niyato niyatāśanaḥ
|
pañcayajñānavāpnoti kramaśo yenukīrtitāḥ||83||

84tato gaccheta rājendra bhīmāyāḥ sthānamuttamam
|
tatra snātvā na yo'nyāṃ vai naro bharatasattama||84||

85devyāḥ putro bhavedrājaṃstaptakuṇḍalavigrahaḥ
|
gavāṃ śatasahasrasya phalaṃ prāpnoti mānavaḥ||85||

86śrīkuṇḍaṃ tu samāsādya triṣu lokeṣu viśrutam
|
pitāmahaṃ namaskṛtya gosahasraphalaṃ labhet||86||

87tato gaccheta dharmajña vimalaṃ tīrthamuttamam
|
adyāpi yatra dṛśyante matsyāḥ sauvarṇarājatāḥ||87||

88tatra snātvā naraḥ kṣipraṃ vāsavaṃ lokamāpnuyāt
|
sarvapāpaviśuddhātmā gaccheta paramāṃ gatim||88||

89vitastāṃ ca samāsādya saṃtarpya pitṛdevatāḥ
|
naraḥ phalamavāpnoti vājapeyasya bhārata||89||

90kāśmīreṣveva nāgasya bhavanaṃ takṣakasya ca
|
vitastākhyamiti khyātaṃ sarvapāpapramocanam||90||

91tatra snātvā naro nūnaṃ vājapeyamavāpnuyāt
|
sarvapāpaviśuddhātmā gacchecca paramāṃ gatim||91||

92tato gaccheta vaḍavāṃ triṣu lokeṣu viśrutām
|
paścimāyāṃ tu saṃdhyāyāmupaspṛśya yathāvidhi||92||

93caruṃ saptārciṣe rājanyathāśakti nivedayet
|
pitṝṇāmakṣayaṃ dānaṃ pravadanti manīṣiṇaḥ||93||

94ṛṣayaḥ pitaro devā gandharvāpsarasāṃ gaṇāḥ
|
guhyakāḥ kinnarā yakṣāḥ siddhā vidyādharā narāḥ||94||

95rākṣasā ditijā rudrā brahmā ca manujādhipa
|
niyataḥ paramāṃ dīkṣāmāsthāyābdasahasrikīm||95||

96viṣṇoḥ prasādanaṃ kurvaṃścaruṃ ca śrapayaṃstathā
|
saptabhiḥ saptabhiścaiva ṛgbhistuṣṭāva keśavam||96||

97dadāvaṣṭaguṇaiśvaryaṃ teṣāṃ tuṣṭastu keśavaḥ
|
yathābhilaṣitānanyānkāmāndattvā mahīpate||97||

98tatraivāntardadhe devo vidyudabhreṣu vai yathā
|
nāmnā saptacaruṃ tena khyātaṃ lokeṣu bhārata||98||

99gavāṃ śatasahasreṇa rājasūyaśatena ca
|
aśvamedhasahasreṇa śreyānsaptārciṣe caruḥ||99||

100tato nivṛtto rājendra rudraṃ padamathāviśet
|
arcayitvā mahādevamaśvamedhaphalaṃ labhet||100||

101maṇimantaṃ samāsādya brahmacārī samāhitaḥ
|
ekarātroṣito rājannagniṣṭomaphalaṃ labhet||101||

102atha gaccheta rājendra devikāṃ lokaviśrutām
|
prasūtiryatra viprāṇāṃ śrūyate bharatarṣabha||102||

103triśūlapāṇeḥ sthānaṃ ca triṣu lokeṣu viśrutam
|
devikāyāṃ naraḥ snātvā samabhyarcya maheśvaram||103||

104yathāśakti caruṃ tatra nivedya bharatarṣabha
|
sarvakāmasamṛddhasya yajñasya labhate phalam||104||

105kāmākhyaṃ tatra rudrasya tīrthaṃ devaniṣevitam
|
tatra snātvā naraḥ kṣipraṃ siddhiṃ prāpnoti bhārata||105||

106yajanaṃ yājanaṃ caiva tathaiva brahmabālukam
|
puṣpāmbhaśca upaspṛśya na śocenmaraṇaṃ gataḥ||106||

107ardhayojanavistārā pañcayojanamāyatā
|
etāvatī hi devikā tu puṇyā devarṣisevitā||107||

108tato gaccheta dharmajña dīrghasatraṃ yathākramam
|
tatra brahmādayo devāḥ siddhāśca paramarṣapaḥ||108||

109dīrghasatramupāsante dīkṣitā niyatavratāḥ||109||

110gamanādeva rājendra dīrghasatramariṃdama
|
rājasūyāśvamedhābhyāṃ phalaṃ prāpnoti bhārata||110||

111tato vinaśanaṃ gacchenniyato niyatāśanaḥ
|
gacchatyantarhitā yatra merupṛṣṭhe sarasvatī||111||

112camasetha śivodbhede nāgodbhede ca dṛśyate
|
snātvā tu camasodbhede agniṣṭomaphalaṃ labhet||112||

113śivodbhede naraḥ snātvā gosahasraphaṃla labhet
|
nāgodbhede naraḥ snātvā nāgalokamavāpnuyāt||113||

114śaśayānaṃ ca rājendra tīrthamāsādya durlabham
|
śatarūpapraticchannāḥ puṣkarā yatra bhārata||114||

115sarasvatyāṃ mahārāja anusaṃvatsaraṃ ca te
|
dṛśyante bharataśreṣṭha vṛttāṃ vai kārtikīṃ sadā||115||

116tatra snātvā naravyāghra dyotate śaśivatsadā
|
gosahasraphalaṃ caiva prāpnuyādbharatarṣabha||116||

117kumārakoṭīmāsādya niyataḥ kurunandana
|
tatrābhiṣekaṃ kurvīta pitṛdevārcane rataḥ||117||

118gavāmayutamāpnoti kulaṃ caiva samuddharet
|
tato gaccheta dharmajña rudrakoṭiṃ samāhitaḥ||118||

119purā yatra mahārāja munikoṭiḥ samāgatā
|
harṣeṇa mahatāviṣṭā rudradarśanakāṅkṣayā||119||

120ahaṃ pūrvamahaṃ pūrvaṃ drakṣyāmi vṛṣabhadhvajam
|
evaṃ saṃprasthitā rājannṛṣayaḥ kila bhārata||120||

121tato yogīśvareṇāpi yogamāsthāya bhūpate
|
teṣāṃ manyupraṇāśārthamṛṣīṇāṃ bhāvitātmanām||121||

122sṛṣṭā koṭīti rudrāṇāmṛṣīṇāmagrataḥ sthitā
|
mayā pūrvataraṃ dṛṣṭa iti te menire pṛthak||122||

123teṣāṃ tuṣṭo mahādevo munīnāṃ bhāvitātmanām
|
bhaktyā paramayā rājanvaraṃ teṣāṃ pradiṣṭavān||123||

124adya prabhṛti yuṣmākaṃ dharmavṛddhirbhaviṣyati
|
tatra snātvā naravyāghra rudrakoṭyāṃ naraḥ śuciḥ||124||

125aśvamedhamavāpnoti kulaṃ caiva samuddharet
|
tato gaccheta rājendra saṃgamaṃ lokaviśrutam||125||

126sarasvatyā mahāpuṇyaṃ keśavaṃ samupāsate
|
yatra brahmāhayo devā ṛṣayaśca tapodhanāḥ||126||

127abhigacchanti rājendra caitraśuklacaturdaśīm
|
tatra snātvā naravyāghra vindedbahu suvarṇakam
|
sarvapāpaviśuddhātmā brahmalokaṃ ca gacchati||127||

128ṛṣīṇāṃ yatra satrāṇi samāptāni narādhipa
|
tatrāvasānamāsādya gosahasraphalaṃ labhet||128||
