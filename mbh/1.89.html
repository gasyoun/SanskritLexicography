Mahābhārata 1.89 (Ādiparvan)

1yayātiruvāca|
ahaṃ yayātirnahuṣasya putraḥ pūroḥ pitā sarvabhūtāvamānāt
|
prabhraṃśitaḥ surasiddharṣilokātparicyutaḥ prapatāmyalpapuṇyaḥ||1||

2ahaṃ hi pūrvo vayasā bhavadbhyastenābhivādaṃ bhavatāṃ na prayuñje
|
yo vidyayā tapasā janmanā vā vṛddhaḥ sa pūjyo bhavati dvijānām||2||

3aṣṭaka uvāca|
avādīstvaṃ vayasā yaḥ pravṛddhaḥ sa vai rājannābhyadhikaḥ kathyate ca
|
yo vidyayā tapasā saṃpravṛddhaḥ sa eva pūjyo bhavati dvijānām||3||

4yayātiruvāca|
pratikūlaṃ karmaṇāṃ pāpamāhustadvartate'pravaṇe pāpalokyam
|
santo'satāṃ nānuvartanti caitadyathā caiṣāmanukūlāstathā''san||4||

5abhūddhanaṃ me vipulaṃ gataṃ tadviceṣṭamāno nādhigantā tadasmi
|
evaṃ pradhāryātmahite niviṣṭo yo vartate sa vijānāti dhīraḥ||5||

6mahādhano yo yajate suyajñairyaḥ sarvavidyāsu vinītabuddhiḥ
|
vedānadhītya tapasā yojya dehaṃ divaṃ samāyātpuruṣo vītamohaḥ||6||

7na jātahṛṣyenmahatā dhanena vedānadhīyītānahaṃkṛtaḥ syāt
|
nānābhāvā bahavo jīvaloke daivadhīnā naṣṭaceṣṭādhikārāḥ
|
tattatprāpya na vihanyeta dhīro diṣṭaṃ balīya iti matvātmabuddhyā||7||

8sukhaṃ hi janturyadi vā'pi duḥkhaṃ daivādhīnaṃ vindate nātmaśaktyā
|
tasmāddiṣṭaṃ balavanmanyamāno na saṃjvarennāpi hṛṣyetkathaṃcit||8||

9duḥkhairna tapyenna sukhaiḥ prahṛṣyetsamena varteta sadaiva dhīraḥ
|
diṣṭaṃ balīya iti manyamāno na saṃjvarennāpi hṛṣyetkathaṃcit||9||

10bhaye na muhyāmyaṣṭakāhaṃ kadācitsantāpo me mānaso nāsti kaścit
|
dhātā yathā māṃ vidadhīta loke dhruvaṃ tathā'haṃ bhaviteti matvā||10||

11saṃsvedajā aṇḍajāścodbhidaśca sarīsṛpāḥ kṛmayo'thāpsu matsyāḥ
|
tathāśmanastṛṇakāṣṭhaṃ ca sarve diṣṭakṣaye svāṃ prakṛtiṃ bhajanti||11||

12anityatāṃ sukhaduḥsvasya buddhvā kasmātsaṃtāpamaṣṭakāhaṃ bhajeyam
|
kiṃ kuryāṃ vai kiṃ ca kṛtvā na tapye tasmātsantāpaṃ varjayāmyapramattaḥ||12||

13vaiśaṃpāyana uvāca|
evaṃ vruvāṇaṃ nṛpatiṃ yayātimathāṣṭakaḥ punarevānvapṛcchat
|
mātāmahaṃ sarvaguṇopapannaṃ tatra sthitaṃ svargaloke yathāvat||13||

14aṣṭaka uvāca|
ye ye lokāḥ pārthivendrapradhānāstvayā bhuktā yaṃ ca kālaṃ yathāvat
|
tānme rājanbrūhi sarvānyathāvatkṣetrajñavadbhāṣase tvaṃ hi dharmān||14||

15yayātiruvāca|
rājā'hamāsamiha sārvabhaumastato lokānmahataścājayaṃ vai
|
tatrāvasaṃ varṣasahasramātraṃ tato lokaṃ paramasmyabhyupetaḥ||15||

16tataḥ purīṃ puruhūtasya ramyāṃ sahasradvārāṃ śatayojanāyatām
|
adhyāvasaṃ varṣasahasramātraṃ tato lokaṃ paramasmyabhyupetaḥ||16||

17tato divyamajaraṃ prāpya lokaṃ prajāpaterlokapaterdurāpam
|
tatrāvasaṃ varṣasahasramātraṃ tato lokaṃ paramasmyabhyupetaḥ||17||

18sa devadevasya niveśane ca vihṛtya lokānavasaṃ yatheṣṭam
|
saṃpūjyamānastridaśaiḥ samastaistulyaprabhāvadyutirīśvarāṇām||18||

19tathā''vasaṃ nandane kāmarūpī saṃvatsarāṇāmayutaṃ śatānām
|
sahāpsarobhirviharanpuṇyagandhānpaśyannagānpuṣpitāṃścārurūpān||19||

20tatra sthitaṃ māṃ devasukheṣu saktaṃ kāle'tīte mahati tato'timātram
|
dūto devānāmabravīdugrarūpo dhvaṃsetyuccaistriḥplutena svareṇa||20||

21etāvanme viditaṃ rājasiṃha tato bhraṣṭo'haṃ nandanātkṣīṇapuṇyaḥ
|
vāco'śrauṣaṃ cāntarikṣe surāṇāṃ sānukroṃśāḥ śocatāṃ māṃ narendra||21||

22aho kaṣṭaṃ kṣīṇapuṇyo yayātiḥ patatyasau puṇyakṛtpuṇyakīrtiḥ
|
tānabruvaṃ patamānastato'haṃ satāṃ madhye nipateyaṃ kathaṃ nu||22||

23tairākhyātā bhavatāṃ yajñabhūmiḥ samīkṣya cemāṃ tvaritamupāgato'smi
|
havirgandhaṃ deśikaṃ yajñabhūmerdhūmāpāṅgaṃ pratigṛhya pratītaḥ||23||
