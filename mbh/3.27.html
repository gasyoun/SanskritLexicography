Mahābhārata 3.27 (Āraṇyakaparvan)

1vaiśaṃpāyana uvāca|
tato vanagatāḥ pārthāḥ sāyāhne saha kṛṣṇayā
|
upaviṣṭāḥ kathāścakrurduḥkhaśokaparāyaṇāḥ||1||

2priyā ca darśanīyā ca paṇḍitā ca pativratā
|
atha kṛṣṇā dharmarājamidaṃ vacanamabravīt||2||

3draupadyuvāca|
na nūnaṃ tasya pāpasya duḥkhamasmāsu kiñcana
|
vidyate dhārtarāṣṭrasya nṛśaṃsasya durātmanaḥ||3||

4yastvāṃ rājanmayā sārdhamajinaiḥ prativāsitam
|
vanaṃ prasthāpya duṣṭātmā nānvatapyata durmatiḥ||4||

5āyasaṃ hṛdayaṃ nūnaṃ tasya duṣkṛtakarmaṇaḥ
|
yastvāṃ dharmaparaṃ śreṣṭhaṃ rūkṣāṇyaśrāvayattadā||5||

6sukhocitamaduḥkhārhaṃ durātmā sasuhṛdgaṇaḥ
|
īdṛśaṃ duḥkhamānīya modate pāpapūruṣaḥ||6||

7caturṇāmeva pāpānāmastraṃ na patitaṃ tadā
|
tvayi bhārata niṣkrānte vanāyājinavāsasi||7||

8duryodhanasya karṇasya śakuneśca durātmanaḥ
|
durbhrātustasya cograsya rājanduḥśāsanasya ca||8||

9itareṣāṃ tu sarveṣāṃ kurūṇāṃ kurusattama
|
duḥkhenābhiparītānāṃ netrebhyaḥ prāpatajjalam||9||

10idaṃ ca śayanaṃ dṛṣṭvā yaccāsītte purātanam
|
śocāmi tvāṃ mahārāja duḥkhānarhaṃ sukhocitam||10||

11dāntaṃ yacca sabhāmadhya āsanaṃ ratnabhūṣitam
|
dṛṣṭvā kuśabṛsīṃ cemāṃ śoko māṃ rundhayatyayam||11||

12yadapaśyaṃ sabhāyāṃ tvāṃ rājabhiḥ parivāritam
|
tacca rājannapaśyantyāḥ kā śāntirhṛdayasya me||12||

13yā tvāhaṃ candanādigdhamapaśyaṃ sūryavarcasam
|
sā tvāṃ paṅkamalādigdhaṃ dṛṣṭvā muhyāmi bhārata||13||

14yā tvāhaṃ kauśikairvastraiḥ śubhrairācchāditaṃ purā
|
dṛṣṭavatyasmi rājendra sā tvāṃ paśyāmi cīriṇam||14||

15yacca tadrukmapātrībhirbrāhmaṇebhyaḥ sahasraśaḥ
|
hriyate te gṛhādannaṃ saṃskṛtaṃ sārvakāmikam||15||

16yatīnāmagṛhāṇāṃ te tathaiva gṛhamedhinām
|
dīyate bhojanaṃ rājannatīvaguṇavatprabho||16||

17satkṛtāni sahasrāṇi sarvakāmaiḥ purā gṛhe
|
sarvakāmaiḥ suvihitairyadapūjayathā dvijān||17||

18tacca rājannapaśyantyāḥ kā śāntirhṛdayasya me
|
yatte bhrātṝnmahārāja yuvāno mṛṣṭakuṇḍalāḥ||18||

19abhojayanta mṛṣṭānnaiḥ sūdāḥ paramasaṃskṛtaiḥ
|
sarvāṃstānadya paśyāmi vane vanyena jīvinaḥ||19||

20aduḥkhārhānmanuṣyendra nopaśāmyati me manaḥ
|
bhīmasenamimaṃ cāpi duḥkhitaṃ vanavāsinam||20||

21dhyāyataḥ kiṃ na manyuste prāpte kāle vivardhate
|
bhīmasenaṃ hi karmāṇi svayaṃ kurvāṇamacyutam||21||

22sukhārhaṃ duḥkhitaṃ dṛṣṭvā kasmānmanyurna vardhate
|
satkṛtaṃ vividhairyānairvastrairuccāvacaistathā||22||

23taṃ te vanagataṃ dṛṣṭvā kasmānmanyurna vardhate
|
ayaṃ kurūnraṇe sarvānhantumutsahate prabhuḥ||23||

24tvatpratijñāṃ pratīkṣaṃstu sahate'yaṃ vṛkodaraḥ
|
yo'rjunenārjunastulyo dvibāhurbahubāhunā||24||

25śarāvamarde śīghratvātkālāntakayamopamaḥ
|
yasya śastrapratāpena praṇatāḥ sarvapārthivāḥ||25||

26yajñe tava mahārāja brāhmaṇānupatasthire
|
tamimaṃ puruṣavyāghraṃ pūjitaṃ devadānavaiḥ||26||

27dhyāyantamarjunaṃ dṛṣṭvā kasmādrājanna kupyasi
|
dṛṣṭvā vanagataṃ pārthamaduḥkhārhaṃ sukhocitam||27||

28na ca te vardhate manyustena muhyāmi bhārata
|
yo devāṃśca manuṣyāṃśca sarpāṃścaikaratho'jayat||28||

29taṃ te vanagataṃ dṛṣṭvā kasmānmanyurna vardhate
|
yo yānairadbhutākārairhayairnāgaiśca saṃvṛtaḥ||29||

30prasahya vittānyādatta pārthivebhyaḥ parantapa
|
kṣipatyekena vegena pañcabāṇaśatāni yaḥ||30||

31taṃ te vanagataṃ dṛṣṭvā kasmānmanyurna vardhate
|
śyāmaṃ bṛhantaṃ taruṇaṃ carmiṇāmuttamaṃ raṇe||31||

32nakulaṃ te vane dṛṣṭvā kasmānmanyurna vardhate
|
darśanīyaṃ ca śūraṃ ca mādrīputraṃ yudhiṣṭhira||32||

33sahadevaṃ vane dṛṣṭvā kasmātkṣamasi pārthiva
|
nakulaṃ sahadevaṃ ca dṛṣṭvā te duḥkhitāvubhau||33||

34aduḥkhārhau manuṣyendra kasmānmanyurna vardhate
|
drupadasya kule jātāṃ snuṣāṃ pāṇḍormahātmanaḥ||34||

35dhṛṣṭadyumnasya bhaginīṃ vīrapatnīmanuvratām
|
māṃ vai vanagatāṃ dṛṣṭvā kasmātkṣamasi pārthiva||35||

36nūnaṃ ca tava vai nāsti manyurbharatasattama
|
yatte bhrātṝṃśca māṃ caiva dṛṣṭvā na vyathate manaḥ||36||

37na nirmanyuḥ kṣatriyo'sti loke nirvacanaṃ smṛtam
|
tadadya tvayi paśyāmi kṣatriye viparītavat||37||

38yo na darśayate tejaḥ kṣatriyaḥ kāla āgate
|
sarvabhūtāni taṃ pārtha sadā paribhavantyuta||38||

39tattvayā na kṣamā kāryā śatrūnprati kathañcana
|
tejasaiva hi te śakyā nihantuṃ nātra saṃśayaḥ||39||

40tathaiva yaḥ kṣamākāle kṣatriyo nopaśāmyati
|
apriyaḥ sarvabhūtānāṃ so'mutreha ca naśyati||40||
