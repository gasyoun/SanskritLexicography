Mahābhārata 12.178 (Śāntiparvan)

1bhīṣma uvāca|
atrāpyudāharantīmamitihāsaṃ purātanam
|
gītaṃ videharājena janakena praśāmyatā ||1||

2anantamiva me vittaṃ yasya me nāsti kiñcana
|
mithilāyāṃ pradīptāyāṃ na me dahyati kiñcana ||2||

3atraivodāharantīmaṃ bodhyasya padasaṃcayam
|
nirvedaṃ prativinyastaṃ taṃ nibodha yudhiṣṭhira ||3||

4bodhyaṃ śāntamṛṣiṃ rājā nāhuṣaḥ paryapṛcchata
|
nirvedācchāntimāpannaṃ śāstraprajñānatarpitam ||4||

5upadeśaṃ mahāprājña śamasyopadiśasva me
|
kāṃ buddhiṃ samanudhyāya śāntaścarasi nirvṛtaḥ ||5||

6bodhya uvāca |
upadeśena vartāmi nānuśāsmīha kaṃcana
|
lakṣaṇaṃ tasya vakṣye'haṃ tatsvayaṃ parimṛśyatām ||6||

7piṅgalā, kakuraḥ, sarpaḥ, sāraṅgānveṣaṇaṃ vane
|
iṣukāraḥ, kumārī ca ṣaḍete guravo mama ||7||

8bhīṣma uvāca|
āśā balavatī rājannairāśyaṃ paramaṃ sukham
|
āśāṃ nirāśāṃ kṛtvā tu sukhaṃ svapiti piṅgalā ||8||

9sāmiṣaṃ kuraraṃ dṛṣṭvā vadhyamānaṃ nirāmiṣaiḥ
|
āmiṣasya parityāgātkuraraḥ sukhamedhate ||9||

10gṛhārambho hi duḥkhāya na sukhāya kadācana
|
sarpaḥ parakṛtaṃ veśma praviśya sukhamedhate ||10||

11sukhaṃ jīvanti munayo bhaikṣyavṛttiṃ samāśritāḥ
|
adroheṇaiva bhūtānāṃ sāraṅgā iva pakṣiṇaḥ ||11||

12iṣukāro naraḥ kaścidiṣāvāsaktamānasaḥ
|
samīpenāpi gacchantaṃ rājānaṃ nāvabuddhavān ||12||

13bahūnāṃ kalaho nityaṃ dvayoḥ saṃkathanaṃ dhruvam
|
ekākī vicariṣyāmi kumārīśaṃkhako yathā ||13||
