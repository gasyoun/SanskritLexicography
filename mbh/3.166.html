Mahābhārata 3.166 (Āraṇyakaparvan)

1vaiśampāyana uvāca|
tato rajanyāṃ vyuṣṭāyāṃ dharmarājaṃ yudhiṣṭhiram
|
bhrātṛbhiḥ sahitaḥ sarvairavandata dhanañjayaḥ||1||

2etasminneva kāle tu sarvavāditraniḥsvanaḥ
|
babhūva tumulaḥ śabdastvantarikṣe divaukasām||2||

3rathanemisvanaścaiva ghaṇṭāśabdaśca bhārata
|
pṛthagvyālamṛgāṇāṃ ca pakṣiṇāmiva sarvaśaḥ||3||

4te samantādanuyayurgandharvāpsarasāṃ gaṇāḥ
|
vimānaiḥ sūryasaṅkāśairdevarājamarindamam||4||

5tataḥ sa haribhiryuktaṃ jāmbūnadapariṣkṛtam
|
meghanādinamāruhya śriyā paramayā jvalan||5||

6pārthānabhyājagāmātha devarājaḥ purandaraḥ
|
āgatya ca sahasrākṣo rathādavaruroha vai||6||

7taṃ dṛṣṭvaiva mahātmānaṃ dharmarājo yudhiṣṭhiraḥ
|
bhrātṛbhiḥ sahitaḥ śrīmān devarājamupāgamat||7||

8pūjayāmāsa caivā'tha vidhivadbhūridakṣiṇaḥ
|
yathārhamamitātmānaṃ vidhidṛṣṭena karmaṇā||8||

9dhanañjayaśca tejasvī praṇipatya purandam
|
bhṛtyavat praṇatastasthau devarājasamīpataḥ||9||

10āghrāya taṃ mahātejāḥ kuntīputro yudhiṣṭhiraḥ
|
dhanañjayamabhiprekṣya vinītaṃ sthitamantike||10||

11jaṭilaṃ devarājasya tapoyuktamakalmaṣam
|
harṣeṇa mahatā''viṣṭaḥ phālgunasyātha darśanāt||11||

12babhūva paramaprīto devarājaṃ ca pūjayan
|
taṃ tathā'dīnamanasaṃ rājānaṃ harṣasamplutam||12||

13uvāca vacanaṃ dhīmān devarājaḥ purandaraḥ
|
tvamimāṃ pṛthivīṃ rājan praśāsiṣyasi pāṇḍava
|
svasti prāpnuhi kaunteya kāmyakaṃ punarāśramam||13||

14astrāṇi labdhāni ca pāṇḍavena sarvāṇi mattaḥ prayatena rājan
|
kṛtapriyaścāsmi dhanañjayena jetuṃ na śakyastribhireṣa lokaiḥ||14||

15evamuktvā sahasrākṣaḥ kuntīputraṃ yudhiṣṭhiram
|
jagāma tridivaṃ hṛṣṭaḥ stūyamāno maharṣibhiḥ||15||

16dhaneśvaragṛhasthānāṃ pāṇḍavānāṃ samāgamam
|
śakreṇa ya idaṃ vidvānadhīyīta samāhitaḥ||16||

17saṃvatsaraṃ brahmacārī niyataḥ saṃśitavrataḥ
|
sa jīveddhi nirābādhaḥ sa sukhī śaradāṃ śatam||17||
