Mahābhārata 13.99 (Anuśāsanaparvan)

1yudhiṣṭhira uvāca|
śrutaṃ me bharataśreṣṭha puṣpadhūpapradāyinām
|
phalaṃ balividhāne ca tadbhūyo vaktumarhasi ||1||

2dhūpapradānasya phalaṃ pradīpasya tathaiva ca
|
balayaśca kimarthaṃ vai kṣipyante gṛhamedhibhiḥ ||2||

3bhīṣma uvāca|
atrāpyudāharantīmamitihāsaṃ purātanam
|
nahuṣasya ca saṃvāda‌magastyasya bhṛgostathā ||3||

4nahuṣo hi mahārāja rājarṣiḥ sumahātapāḥ
|
devarājyamanuprāptaḥ sukṛteneha karmaṇā ||4||

5tatrāpi prayato rājannahuṣastridive vasan
|
mānuṣīścaiva divyāśca kurvāṇo vividhāḥ kriyāḥ ||5||

6mānuṣyastatra sarvāḥ sma kriyāstasya mahātmanaḥ
|
pravṛttāstridive rājandivyāścaiva sanātanāḥ ||6||

7agnikāryāṇi samidhaḥ kuśāḥ sumanasastathā
|
balayaścānnalājābhirdhūpanaṃ dīpakarma ca ||7||

8sarvaṃ tasya gṛhe rājñaḥ prāvartata mahātmanaḥ
|
japayajñānmanoyajñāṃstridive'pi cakāra saḥ ||8||

9devānabhyarcayaccāpi vidhivatsa sureśvaraḥ
|
sarvāneva yathānyāyaṃ yathāpūrvamarindama ||9||

10athendro'hamiti jñātvā ahaṃkāraṃ samāviśam
|
sarvāścaiva kriyāstasya paryahīyanta bhūpateḥ ||10||

11sa ṛṣīnvāhayāmāsa varadānamadānvitaḥ
|
parihīnakriyaścaiva durbalatvamupeyivān ||11||

12tasya vāhayataḥ kālo munimukhyāṃstapodhanān
|
ahaṃkārābhibhūtasya sumahānabhyavartata ||12||

13atha paryāyaśaḥ sarvānvāhanāyopacakrame
|
paryāyaścāpyagastyasya samapadyata bhārata ||13||

14athāgatya mahātejā bhṛgurbrahmavidāṃ varaḥ
|
agastyamāśramasthaṃ vai samupetyedamabravīt ||14||

15evaṃ vayamasatkāraṃ devendrasyāsya durmateḥ
|
nahuṣasya kimarthaṃ vai marṣayāma mahāmune ||15||

16agastya uvāca|
kathameṣa mayā śakyaḥ śaptuṃ yasya mahāmune
|
varadena varo datto bhavato viditaśca saḥ ||16||

17yo me dṛṣṭipathaṃ gacchetsa me vaśyo bhavediti
|
ityanena varaṃ devo yācito gacchatā divam ||17||

18evaṃ na dagdhaḥ sa mayā bhavatā ca na saṃśayaḥ
|
anyenāpyṛṣimukhyena na dagdho na ca pātitaḥ ||18||

19amṛtaṃ caiva pānāya dattamasmai purā vibho
|
mahātmanā tadarthaṃ ca nāsmābhirvinipātyate ||19||

20prāyacchata varaṃ devaḥ prajānāṃ duḥkhakāraṇam
|
dvijeṣvadharmayuktāni sa karoti narādhamaḥ ||20||

21tatra yatprāptakālaṃ nastadbrūhi vadatāṃ vara
|
bhavāṃścāpi yathā brūyāttatkartā'smi na saṃśayaḥ||21||

22bhṛguruvāca|
pitāmahaniyogena bhavantaṃ so'hamāgataḥ
|
pratikartuṃ balavati nahuṣe daivamohite ||22||

23adya hi tvāṃ sudurbuddhī rathe yokṣyati devarāṭ
|
adyainamahamudvṛttaṃ kariṣye'nindramojasā ||23||

24adyendraṃ sthāpayiṣyāmi paśyataste śatakratum
|
sañcālya pāpakarmāṇamaindrāt sthānātsudurmatim ||24||

25adya cāsau kudevendrastvāṃ padā dharṣayiṣyati
|
daivopahatacittatvādātmanāśāya mandadhīḥ ||25||

26vyutkrāntadharmaṃ tamahaṃ dharṣaṇāmarṣito bhṛśam
|
ahirbhavasveti ruṣā śapsye pāpaṃ dvijadruham ||26||

27tata enaṃ sudurbuddhiṃ dhikśabdābhihatatviṣam
|
dharaṇyāṃ pātayiṣyāmi paśyataste mahāmune ||27||

28nahuṣaṃ pāpakarmāṇamaiśvaryabalamohitam
|
yathā ca rocate tubhyaṃ tathā kartāsmyahaṃ mune ||28||

29evamuktastu bhṛguṇā maitrāvaruṇiravyayaḥ
|
agastyaḥ paramaprīto babhūva vigatajvaraḥ ||29||
