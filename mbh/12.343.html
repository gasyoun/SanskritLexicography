Mahābhārata 12.343 (Śāntiparvan)

1śaunaka uvāca|
saute sumahadākhyānaṃ bhavatā parikīrtitam
|
yacchrutvā munayaḥ sarve vismayaṃ paramaṃ gatāḥ||1||

2sarvāśramābhigamanaṃ sarvatīrthāvagāhanam
|
na tathā phaladaṃ saute nārāyaṇakathā yathā||2||

3pāvitāṅgāḥ sma saṃvṛttāḥ śrutvemāmāditaḥ kathām
|
nārāyaṇāśrayāṃ puṇyāṃ sarvapāpapramocanīm||3||

4durdarśo bhagavāndevaḥ sarvalokanamaskṛtaḥ
|
sabrahmakaiḥ suraiḥ kṛtsnairanyaiścaiva maharṣibhiḥ||4||

5dṛṣṭavānnārado yattu devaṃ nārāyaṇaṃ harim
|
nūnametaddhyanumataṃ tasya devasya sūtaja||5||

6yad dṛṣṭavān jagannāthamaniruddhatanau sthitam
|
yatprādravatpunarbhūyo nārado devasattamau||6||

7naranārāyaṇau draṣṭuṃ kāraṇaṃ tadbravīhi me
|
sautiruvāca |
tasminyajñe vartamāne rājñaḥ pārikṣitasya vai||7||

8karmāntareṣu vidhivadvartamāneṣu śaunaka
|
kṛṣṇadvaipāyanaṃ vyāsamṛrṣi vedanidhiṃ prabhum||8||

9paripapraccha rājendraḥ pitāmahapitāmaham
|
janamejaya uvāca|
śvetadvīpānnivṛttena nāradena surarṣiṇā||9||

10dhyāyatā bhagavadvākyaṃ ceṣṭitaṃ kimataḥ param
|
badaryāśramamāgamya samāgamya ca tāvṛṣī||10||

11kiyantaṃ kālamavasatkāṃ kathāṃ pṛṣṭavāṃśca saḥ
|
idaṃ śatasahasrāddhi bhāratākhyānavistarāt||11||

12āmanthya matimanthena jñānodadhimanuttamam
|
navanītaṃ yathā dadhno malayāccandanaṃ yathā||12||

13āraṇyakaṃ ca vedebhya oṣadhibhyo'mṛtaṃ yathā
|
samuddhṛtamidaṃ brahmankathāmṛtamidaṃ tathā||13||

14taponidhe tvayoktaṃ hi nārāyaṇakathāśrayam
|
sa īśo bhagavāndevaḥ sarvabhūtātmabhāvanaḥ||14||

15aho nārāyaṇaṃ tejo durdarśaṃ dvijasattama
|
yatrāviśanti kalpānte sarve brahmādaya: surāḥ||15||

16ṛṣayaśca sagandharvā yacca kañciccarācaram
|
na tato'sti paraṃ manye pāvanaṃ divi ceha ca||16||

17sarvāśramābhigamanaṃ sarvatīrthāvagāhanam
|
na tathā phaladaṃ cāpi nārāyaṇakathā yathā||17||

18sarvathā pāvitāḥ smeha śrutvemāmāditaḥ kathām
|
hareviśveśvarasyeha sarvapāpapraṇāśanīm||18||

19na citraṃ kṛtavāṃstatra yadāryo me dhanañjayaḥ
|
vāsudevasahāyo yaḥ prāptavāñjayamuttamam||19||

20na cāsya kiñcidaprāpyaṃ manye lokeṣvapi triṣu
|
trailokyanātho viṣṇuḥ sa yathā''sītsāhyakṛtsa vai||20||

21dhanyāśca sarva evāsan brahmaṃste mama pūrvajāḥ
|
hitāya śreyase caiva yeṣāmasījjanārdanaḥ||21||

22tapasā'tha sudṛśyo hi bhagavān lokapūjitaḥ
|
yaṃ dṛṣṭavantaste sākṣācchrīvatsāṅkavibhūṣaṇam||22||

23tebhyo dhanyataraścaiva nāradaḥ parameṣṭhijaḥ
|
na cālpatejasamṛṣiṃ vedmi nāradamavyayam||23||

24śvetadvīpaṃ samāsādya yena dṛṣṭaḥ svayaṃ hariḥ
|
devaprasādānugataṃ vyaktaṃ tattasya darśanam||24||

25tad dṛṣṭavāṃstadā devamaniruddhatanau sthitam
|
badarīmāśramaṃ yattu nāradaḥ prādravatpunaḥ ||25||

26naranārāyaṇau draṣṭuṃ kiṃ tu tatkāraṇaṃ mune
|
śvetadvīpānnivṛttaśca nāradaḥ parameṣṭhijaḥ||26||

27badarīmāśramaṃ prāpya samāgamya ca tāvṛṣī
|
kiyantaṃ kālamavasatpraśnān kān pṛṣṭavāṃśca ha||27||

28śvetadvīpādupāvṛtte tasminvā sumahātmani
|
kimabrūtāṃ mahātmānau naranārāyaṇāvṛṣī||28||

29tadetanme yathātattvaṃ sarvamākhyātumarhasi
|
vaiśampāyana uvāca|
namo bhavagate tasmai vyāsāyāmitatejase||29||

30yasya prasādādvakṣyāmi nārāyaṇakathāmimām
|
prāpya śvetaṃ mahādvīpaṃ dṛṣṭvā ca harimavyayam||30||

31nivṛtto nārado rājaṃstarasā merumāgamat
|
hṛdayenodvahan bhāraṃ yaduktaṃ paramātmanā||31||

32paścādasyābhavadrājannātmanaḥ sādhvasaṃ mahat
|
yadgatvā dūramadhvānaṃ kṣemī punarihāgataḥ||32||

33meroḥ pracakrāma tataḥ parvataṃ gandhamādanam
|
nipapāta ca khāttūrṇaṃ viśālāṃ badarīmanu||33||

34tataḥ sa dadṛśe devau purāṇāvṛṣisattamau
|
tapaścarantau sumahadātmaniṣṭhau mahāvratau||34||

35tejasābhyadhikau sūryātsarvalokavirocanāt
|
śrīvatsalakṣaṇau pūjyau jaṭāmaṇḍaladhāriṇau||35||

36jālapādabhujau tau tu pādayoścakralakṣaṇau
|
vyūḍhoraskau dīrghabhujau tathā muṣkacatuṣkiṇau||36||

37ṣaṣṭidantāvaṣṭadaṃṣṭrau meghaughasadṛśasvanau
|
svāsyau pṛthulalāṭau ca subhrū suhanunāsikau||37||

38ātapatreṇa sadṛśe śirasī devayostayoḥ
|
evaṃ lakṣaṇasaṃpannau mahāpuruṣasaṃjñitau||38||

39tau dṛṣṭvā nārado hṛṣṭastābhyāṃ ca pratipūjitaḥ
|
svāgatenābhibhāṣyātha pṛṣṭaścānāmayaṃ tathā||39||

40babhūvāntargatamatirnirīkṣya puruṣottamau
|
sadogatāstatra ye vai sarvabhūtanamaskṛtāḥ||40||

41śvetadvīpe mayā dṛṣṭāstādṛśāvṛṣisattamau
|
iti sañcintya manasā kṛtvā cābhipradakṣiṇam||41||

42sa copaviviśe tatra pīṭhe kuśamaye śubhe
|
tatastau tapasāṃ vāsau yaśasāṃ tejasāmapi||42||

43ṛṣī śamadamopetau kṛtvā paurvāhnikaṃ vidhim
|
paścānnāradamavyagrau pādyārghyābhyāmathārcataḥ||43||

44pīṭhayoścopaviṣṭau tau kṛtātithyāhnikau nṛpau
|
teṣu tatropaviṣṭeṣu sa deśo'bhivyarājata||44||

45ājyāhutimahājvālairyajñavāṭo yathā'gnibhiḥ
|
atha nārāyaṇastatra nāradaṃ vākyamabravīt||45||

46sukhopaviṣṭaṃ viśrāntaṃ kṛtātithyaṃ sukhasthitam
|
naranārāyaṇāvūcatuḥ
|
apīdānīṃ sa bhagavānparamātmā sanātanaḥ||46||

47śvetadvīpe tvayā dṛṣṭa āvayoḥ prakṛtiḥ parā
|
nārada uvāca|
dṛṣṭo me puruṣaḥ śrīmānviśvarūpadharo'vyayaḥ||47||

48sarve lokā hi tatrasthāstathā devāḥ saharṣibhiḥ
|
adyāpi cainaṃ paśyāmi yuvāṃ paśyansanātanau||48||

49yairlakṣaṇairupetaḥ sa hariravyaktarūpadhṛk
|
tairlakṣaṇairupetau hi vyaktarūpadharau yuvām||49||

50dṛṣṭau yuvāṃ mayā tatra tasya devasya pārśvataḥ
|
ihaiva cāgato'smpadya visṛṣṭaḥ paramātmanā||50||

51ko hi nāma bhavettasya tejasā yaśasā śriyā
|
sadṛśastriṣu lokeṣu ṛte dharmātmajau yuvām||51||

52tena me kathitaḥ kṛtsno dharmaḥ kṣetrajñasaṃjñitaḥ
|
prādurbhāvāśca kathitā bhaviṣyā iha ye yathā||52||

53tatra ye puruṣāḥ śvetāḥ pañcendriyavivarjitāḥ
|
pratibuddhāśca te sarve bhaktāśca puruṣottamam||53||

54te'rcayanti sadā devaṃ taiḥ sārdhaṃ ramate ca saḥ
|
priyabhakto hi bhagavānparamātmā dvijapriyaḥ||54||

55ramate so'rcyamāno hi sadā bhāgavatapriyaḥ
|
viśvabhuk sarvago devo mādhavo bhaktavatsalaḥ||55||

56sa kartā kāraṇaṃ caiva kāryaṃ cātibaladyutiḥ
|
hetuścājñā vidhānaṃ ca tattvaṃ caiva mahāyaśāḥ||56||

57tapasā yojya so''tmānaṃ śvetadvīpātparaṃ hi yat
|
teja ityabhivikhyātaṃ svayaṃbhāsā'vabhāsitam||57||

58śāntiḥ sā triṣu lokeṣu vihitā bhāvitātmanā
|
etayā śubhayā buddhyā naiṣṭhikaṃ vratamāsthitaḥ||58||

59na tatra sūryastapati na somo'bhivirājate
|
na vāyurvāti deveśe tapaścarati duścaram||59||

60vedīmaṣṭanalotsedhāṃ bhūmāvāsthāya viśvakṛt
|
ekapādasthito deva urdhvabāhurudaṅmukha||60||

61sāṅgānāvartayanvedāṃstapastepe suduścaram
|
yadbrahmā ṛṣayaścaiva svayaṃ paśupatiśca yat||61||

62śeṣāśca vibudhaśreṣṭhā daityadānavarākṣasāḥ
|
nāgāḥ suparṇā gandharvāḥ siddhā rājarṣayaśca ye||62||

63havyaṃ kavyaṃ ca satataṃ vidhiyuktaṃ prayuñjate
|
kṛtsnaṃ tu tasya devasya caraṇāvupatiṣṭhataḥ||63||

64yāḥ kriyāḥ saṃprayuktāśca ekāntagatabuddhibhiḥ
|
tāḥ sarvāḥ śirasā devaḥ pratigṛhṇāti vai svayam||64||

65na tasyā'nyaḥ priyataraḥ pratibuddhairmahātmabhiḥ
|
vidyate triṣu lokeṣu tato'syaikāntikaṃ gataḥ||65||

66iha caivāgatastena visṛṣṭaḥ paramātmanā
|
evaṃ me bhagavāndevaḥ svayamākhyātavānhariḥ
|
āsiṣye tatparo bhūtvā yuvābhyāṃ saha nityaśaḥ||66||
