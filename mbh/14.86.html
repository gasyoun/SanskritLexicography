Mahābhārata 14.86 (Āśvamedhikaparvan)

1vaiśampāyana uvāca|
samāgatānvedavido rājñaśca pṛthivīśvarān
|
dṛṣṭvā yudhiṣṭhiro rājā bhīmasenamabhāṣata||1||

2upayātā naravyāghrā ya ete pṛthivīśvarāḥ
|
eteṣāṃ kriyatāṃ pūjā pūjārhā hi narādhipāḥ||2||

3ityuktaḥ sa tathā cakre narendreṇa yaśasvinā
|
bhīmaseno mahātejā yamābhyāṃ saha pāṇḍavaḥ||3||

4athābhyagacchadgovindo vṛṣṇibhiḥ saha dharmajam
|
baladevaṃ puraskṛtya sarvaprāṇabhṛtāṃ varaḥ||4||

5yuyudhānena sahitaḥ pradyumnena gadena ca
|
niśaṭhenātha sāmbena tathaiva kṛtavarmaṇā||5||

6teṣāmapi parāṃ pūjāṃ cakre bhīmo mahārathaḥ
|
viviśuste ca veśmāni ratnavanti ca sarvaśaḥ||6||

7yudhiṣṭhirasamīpe tu kathānte madhusūdanaḥ
|
arjunaṃ kathayāmāsa bahusaṅgrāmakarṣitam||7||

8sa taṃ papraccha kaunteyaḥ punaḥ punararindamam
|
dharmajaḥ śakrajaṃ jiṣṇuṃ samācaṣṭa jagatpatiḥ||8||

9āgamaddvārakāvāsī mamāptaḥ puruṣo nṛpa
|
yo'drākṣītpāṇḍavaśreṣṭha bahusaṅgrāmakarṣitam||9||

10samīpe ca mahābāhumācaṣṭa ca mama prabho
|
kuru kāryāṇi kaunteya hayamedhārthasiddhaye||10||

11ityuktaḥ pratyuvācainaṃ dharmarājo yudhiṣṭhiraḥ
|
diṣṭyā sa kuśalī jiṣṇurupāyāti ca mādhava||11||

12yadidaṃ sandideśāsmin pāṇḍavānāṃ balāgraṇīḥ
|
tadā jñātumihecchāmi bhavatā yadunandana||12||

13ityukto dharmarājena vṛṣṇyandhakapatistadā
|
provācedaṃ vaco vāgmī dharmātmānaṃ yudhiṣṭhiram||13||

14idamāha mahārāja pārthavākyaṃ smarannaraḥ
|
vācyo yudhiṣṭhiraḥ kṛṣṇa kāle vākyamidaṃ mama||14||

15āgamiṣyanti rājānaḥ sarve vai kauravarṣabha
|
prāptānāṃ mahatāṃ pūjā kāryā hyetatkṣamaṃ hi naḥ||15||

16ityetadvacanādrājā vijñāpyo mama mānada
|
yathā cātyayikaṃ na syādyadarghāharaṇe'bhavat||16||

17kartumarhati tadrājā bhavāṃścāpyanumanyatām
|
rājadveṣānna naśyeyurimā rājanpunaḥ prajāḥ||17||

18idamanyacca kaunteya vacaḥ sa puruṣo'bravīt
|
dhanañjayasya nṛpate tanme nigadataḥ śṛṇu||18||

19upāyāsyati yajñaṃ no maṇipūrapatirnṛpaḥ
|
putro mama mahātejā dayito babhruvāhanaḥ||19||

20taṃ bhavānmadapeścārthaṃ vidhivatpratipūjayet
|
sa tu bhakto'nuraktaśca mama nityamiti prabho||20||

21ityetadvacanaṃ śrutvā dharmarājo yudhiṣṭhiraḥ
|
ābhanandyāsya tadvākyamidaṃ vacanamabravīt||21||
