Mahābhārata 1.36 (Ādiparvan)

1śaunaka uvāca|
ākhyātā bhujagāstāta vīryavanto durāsadāḥ
|
śāpaṃ taṃ te'bhivijñāya kṛtavantaḥ kimuttaram||1||

2sautiruvāca|
teṣāṃ tu bhagavāñccheṣaḥ kadrūṃ tyaktvā mahāyaśāḥ
|
ugraṃ tapaḥ samātasthe vāyubhakṣo yatavrataḥ||2||

3gandhamādanamāsādya badaryāṃ ca taporataḥ
|
gokarṇe puṣkarāraṇye tathā himavatastaṭe||3||

4teṣu teṣu ca puṇyeṣu tīrtheṣvāyataneṣu ca
|
ekāntaśīlo niyataḥ satataṃ vijitendriyaḥ||4||

5tapyamānaṃ tapo ghoraṃ taṃ dadarśa pitāmahaḥ
|
saṃśuṣkamāṃsatvaksnāyuṃ jaṭācīradharaṃ munim||5||

6tamabravītsatyadhṛtiṃ tapyamānaṃ pitāmahaḥ
|
kimidaṃ kuruṣe śeṣa prajānāṃ svasti vai kuru||6||

7tvaṃ hi tīvreṇa tapasā prajāstāpayase'nagha
|
brūhi kāmaṃ ca me śeṣa yaste hṛdi vyavasthitaḥ||7||

8śeṣa uvāca|
sodaryā mama sarve hi bhrātaro mandacetasaḥ
|
saha tairnotsahe vastuṃ tadbhavānanumanyatām||8||

9abhyasūyanti satataṃ parasparamamitravat
|
tato'haṃ tapa ātiṣṭhaṃ naitanpaśyeyamityuta||9||

10na marṣayanti sasutāṃ satataṃ vinatāṃ ca te
|
asmākaṃ cāparo bhrātā vainateyo'ntarikṣagaḥ||10||

11taṃ ca dviṣanti satataṃ sa cāpi balavattaraḥ
|
varapradānātsa pituḥ kaśyapasya mahātmanaḥ||11||

12so'haṃ tapaḥ samāsthāya mokṣyāmīdaṃ kalevaram
|
kathaṃ me pretyabhāve'pi na taiḥ syātsaha saṅgamaḥ||12||

13tamevaṃ vādinaṃ śeṣaṃ pitāmaha uvāca ha
|
jānāmi śeṣa sarveṣāṃ bhrātṝṇāṃ te viceṣṭitam||13||

14mātuścāpyaparādhādvai bhrātṝṇāṃ te mahadbhayam
|
kṛto'tra parihāraśca pūrvameva bhujaṅgama||14||

15bhrātṝṇāṃ tava sarveṣāṃ na śokaṃ kartumarhasi
|
vṛṇīṣva ca varaṃ mattaḥ śeṣa yatte'bhikāṅkṣitam||15||

16dāsyāmi hi varaṃ te'dya prītirme paramā tvayi
|
diṣṭyā buddhiśca te dharme niviṣṭā pannagottama
|
bhūyo bhūyaśca te buddhirdharme bhavatu susthirā||16||

17śeṣa uvāca|
eṣa eva varo deva kāṅkṣito me pitāmaha
|
dharme me ramatāṃ buddhiḥ śame tapasi ceśvara||17||

18brahmovāca|
prīto'smyanena te śeṣa damena ca śamena ca
|
tvayā tvidaṃ vacaḥ kāryaṃ manniyogātprajāhitam||18||

19imāṃ mahīṃ śailavanopapannāṃ sasāgaragrāmavihārapattanām
|
tvaṃ śeṣa samyak calitāṃ yathāvatsaṃgṛhya tiṣṭhasva yathā'calā syāt||19||

20śeṣa uvāca|
yathā''ha devo varadaḥ prajāpatirmahīpatirbhūtapatirjagatpatiḥ
|
tathā mahīṃ dhārayitā'smi niścalāṃ prayacchatāṃ me vivaraṃ prajāpate||20||

21brahmovāca|
adho mahīṃ gaccha bhujaṅgamottama svayaṃ tavaiṣā vivaraṃ pradāsyati
|
imāṃ dharāṃ dhārayatā tvayā hi me mahatpriyaṃ śeṣa kṛtaṃ bhaviṣyati||21||

22sautiruvāca|
tathaiva kṛtvā vivaraṃ praviśya sa prabhurbhuvo bhujagavarāgrajaḥ sthitaḥ
|
bibharti devīṃ śirasā mahīmimāṃ samudranemiṃ parigṛhya sarvataḥ||22||

23brahmovāca|
śeṣo'si nāgottama dharmadevo mahīmimāṃ dhārayase yadekaḥ
|
anantabhogaiḥ parigṛhya sarvāṃ yathāhamevaṃ balabhidyathā vā||23||

24sautiruvāca|
adho bhūmau vasatyevaṃ nāgo'nantaḥ pratāpavān
|
dhāsyanvasudhāmekaḥ śāsanādbrahmaṇo vibhoḥ||24||

25suparṇaṃ ca sahāyaṃ vai bhagavānamarottamaḥ
|
prādādanantāya tadā vainateyaṃ pitāmahaḥ||25||
