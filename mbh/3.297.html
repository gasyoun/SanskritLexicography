Mahābhārata 3.297 (Āraṇyakaparvan)

1mārkaṇḍeya uvāca|
atha bhāryāsahāyaḥ sa phalānyādāya vīryavān
|
kaṭhinaṃ pūrayāmāsa tataḥ kāṣṭānyapāṭayat||1||

2tasya pāṭayataḥ kāṣṭhaṃ svedo vai samajāyata
|
vyāyāmena ca tenāsya jajñe śirasi vedanā||2||

3so'bhigamya priyāṃ bhāryāmuvāca śramapīḍitaḥ
|
satyavānuvāca|
vyāyāmena mamānena jātā śirasi vedanā||3||

4aṅgāni caiva sāvitri hṛdayaṃ dūyatīva ca
|
asvasthamiva cātmānaṃ lakṣaye mitabhāṣiṇi||4||

5śūlairiva śiro viddhamidaṃ saṃlakṣayāmyaham
|
tat svaptumicche kalyāṇi na sthātuṃ śaktirasti me||5||

6sā samāsādya sāvitrī bhartāramupagamya ca
|
utsaṅge'sya śiraḥ kṛtvā niṣasāda mahītale||6||

7tataḥ sā nāradavaco vimṛśantī tapasvinī
|
taṃ muhūrtaṃ kṣaṇaṃ velāṃ divasaṃ ca yuyoja ha||7||

8muhūrtādeva cāpaśyat puruṣaṃ raktavāsasam
|
baddhamauliṃ vapuṣmantamādityasamatejasam||8||

9śyāmāvadātaṃ raktākṣaṃ pāśahastaṃ bhayāvaham
|
sthitaṃ satyavataḥ pārśve nirīkṣantaṃ tameva ca||9||

10taṃ dṛṣṭvā sahasotthāya bharturnyasya śanaiḥ śiraḥ
|
kṛtāñjaliruvācārtā hṛdayena pravepatī||10||

11sāvitryuvāca|
daivataṃ tvā'bhijānāmi vapuretaddhyamānuṣam
|
kāmayā brūhi deveśa kastvaṃ kiñca cikīrṣasi||11||

12yama uvāca|
pativratā'si sāvitri tathaiva ca taponvitā
|
atastvāmabhibhāṣāmi viddhi māṃ tvaṃ śubhe yamam ||12||

13ayaṃ te satyavān bhartā kṣīṇāyuḥ pārthivātmajaḥ
|
jeṣyāmi tamahaṃ badhva viddhyetanme cikīrṣitam ||13||

14sāvitryuvāca |
śrūyate bhagavan dūtāstavāgacchanti mānavān
|
netuṃ kila bhavān kasmādāgato'si svayaṃ prabho||14||

15mārkaṇḍeya uvāca|
ityuktaḥ pitṛrājastāṃ bhagavān syacikīrṣitam
|
yathāvat sarvamākhyātuṃ tatpriyārthaṃ pracakrame||15||

16ayaṃ ca dharmasaṃyukto rūpavān guṇasāgaraḥ
|
nārho matpuruṣairnaitumato'smi svayamāgataḥ ||16||

17tataḥ satyavataḥ kāyāt pāśabaddhaṃ vaśaṃ gatam
|
aṅguṣṭhamātraṃ puruṣaṃ niścakarṣa yamo balāt ||17||

18tataḥ samuddhṛtaprāṇaṃ gataśvāsaṃ hataprabham
|
nirviceṣṭaṃ śarīraṃ tadvabhūvāpriyadarśanam ||18||

19yamastu taṃ tato baddhvā prayāto dakṣiṇāmukhaḥ
|
sāvitrī caiva duḥkhārtā yamamevānvagacchata
|
niyamavratasaṃsiddhā mahābhāgā pativratā||19||

20yama uvāca|
nivarta gaccha sāvitri kuruṣvāsyaurdhvadehikam
|
kṛtaṃ bhartustvayā''nuṇyaṃ yāvadgamyaṃ gataṃ tvayā ||20||

21sāvitryuvāca |
yatra me nīyate bhartā svayaṃ vā yatra gacchati
|
mayā ca tatra gantavyameṣa dharmaḥ sanātanaḥ ||21||

22tapasā gurubhaktyā ca bhartuḥ snehādvratena ca
|
tava caiva prasādena na me pratihatā gatiḥ ||22||

23prāhuḥ sāptapadaṃ maitraṃ budhāstatvārthadarśinaḥ
|
mitratāṃ ca puraskṛtya kiñcidvakṣyāmi tacchruṇu||23||

24nānātmavantastu vane caranti dharmaṃ ca vāsaṃ ca pariśramaṃ ca
|
vijñānato dharmamudāharanti tasmāt santo dharmamāhuḥ pradhānam ||24||

25ekasya dharmeṇa satāṃ matena sarve sma taṃ mārgamanuprapannāḥ
|
mā vai dvitīyaṃ mā tṛtīyaṃ ca vāñche tasmāt santo dharmamāhuḥ pradhānam ||25||

26yama uvāca|
nivarta tuṣṭo'smi tavānayā girā svarākṣaravyañjanahetuyuktayā
|
varaṃ vṛṇīṣveha vinā'sya jīvitaṃ dadāni te sarvamanindite varam ||26||

27sāvitryuvāca |
cyutaḥ svarājyādvanavāsamāśrito vinaṣṭacakṣuḥ śvaśuro mamāśrame
|
sa labdhacakṣurbalavān bhavennṛpastava prasādājjvalanārkasannibhaḥ ||27||

28yama uvāca|
dadāni te'ha tamanindite varaṃ yathā tvayoktaṃ bhavitā ca tat tathā
|
tavādhvanā glānimivopalakṣaye nivarta gacchasva na te śramo bhavet ||28||

29sāvitryuvāca |
śramaḥ kuto bhartṛsamīpato hi me yato hi bhartā mama sā gatirdhruvā
|
yataḥ patiṃ neṣyasi tatra me gatiḥ sureśa bhūyaśca vaco nibodha me ||29||

30satāṃ sakṛtsaṅgatamīpsitaṃ paraṃ tataḥ paraṃ mitramiti pracakṣate
|
na cāphalaṃ satpuruṣeṇa saṅgataṃ tataḥ satāṃ sannivaset samāgame ||30||

31yama uvāca|
manonukūlaṃ budhabuddhivardhanaṃ tvayā yaduktaṃ vacanaṃ hitāśrayam
|
vinā punaḥ satyavato'sya jīvitaṃ varaṃ dvitīyaṃ varayasva bhāmini ||31||

32sāvitryuvāca |
hataṃ purā me śvaśurasya dhīmataḥ svameva rājyaṃ labhatāṃ sa pārthivaḥ
|
jahyāt svadharmānna ca me gururyathā dvitīyametadvarayāmi te varam ||32||

33yama uvāca|
svameva rājyaṃ pratipatsyate'cirānna ca svadharmāt parihāsyate nṛpaḥ
|
kṛtena kāmena mayā nṛśatmaje nivarta gacchasva na te śramo bhavet ||33||

34sāvitryuvāca |
prajāstvayaitā niyamena saṃyatā niyamya caitā nayase nikāmayā
|
tato yamatvaṃ tava deva viśrutaṃ nibodha cemāṃ giramīritāṃ mayā ||34||

35adrohaḥ sarvabhūteṣu karmaṇā manasā girā
|
anugrahaśca dānaṃ ca satāṃ dharmaḥ sanātanaḥ ||35||

36evaṃprāyaśca loko'yaṃ manuṣyāḥ śaktipeśalāḥ
|
santastvevāpyamitreṣu dayāṃ prāpteṣu kurvate ||36||

37yama uvāca|
pipāsitasyeva bhavedyathā payastathā tvayā vākyamidaṃ samīritam
|
vinā pujeḥ satyavato'sya jīvitaṃ varaṃ vṛṇīṣveha śubhe yadicchasi ||37||

38sāvitryuvāca |
mamānapatyaḥ pṛthivīpatiḥ pitā bhavet pituḥ putraśataṃ tathorasam
|
kulasya santānakaraṃ ca yadbhavet tṛtīyametadvarayāmi te varam ||38||

39yama uvāca|
kulasya santānakaraṃ suvarcasaṃ śataṃ sutānāṃ piturastu te śubhe
|
kṛtena kāmena narādhipātmaje nivarta dūraṃ hi pathastvamāgatā ||39||

40sāvitryuvāca|
na dūrametanmama bhartṛsannidhau mano hi me dūrataraṃ pradhāvati
|
atha vrajanneva giraṃ samudyatāṃ mayocyamānāṃ śṛṇu bhūya eva ca||40||

41vivasvatastvaṃ tanayaḥ pratāpavāṃstato hi vaivasvata ucyase budhaiḥ
|
samena dharmeṇa caranti tāḥ prajāstatastaveheśvara dharmarājatā||41||

42ātmanyapi na viśvāsastathā bhavati satsu yaḥ
|
tasmāt satsu viśeṣeṇa sarvaḥ praṇayamicchati||42||

43sauhṛdāt sarvabhūtānāṃ viśvāso nāma jāyate
|
tasmāt satsu viśeṣeṇa viśvāsaṃ kurute janaḥ||43||

44yama uvāca|
udāhṛtaṃ te vacanaṃ yadaṅgane śubhe na tādṛk tvadṛte śrutaṃ mayā
|
anena tuṣṭo'smi vinā'sya jīvitaṃ varaṃ caturthaṃ varayasva gaccha ca||44||

45sāvitryuvāca|
mamātmajaṃ satyavatastathaurasaṃ bhavedubhābhyāmiha yat kulodvaham
|
śataṃ sutānāṃ balavīryaśālināmidaṃ caturthaṃ varayāmi te varam||45||

46yama uvāca|
śataṃ sutānāṃ balavīryaśālināṃ bhaviṣyati prītikaraṃ tavābale
|
pariśramaste na bhavenṛpātmaje nivarta dūraṃ hi pathastvamāgatā||46||

47sāvitryuvāca|
satāṃ sadā śāśvatadharmavṛttiḥ santo na sīdanti na ca vyathanti
|
satāṃ sadbhirnāphalaḥ saṅgamo'sti sadbhyo bhayaṃ nānuvartanti santaḥ||47||

48santo hi satyena nayanti sūryaṃ santo bhūmiṃ tapasā dhārayanti
|
santo gatirbhūtabhavyasya rājan satāṃ madhye nāvasīdanti santaḥ||48||

49āryajuṣṭamidaṃ vṛttamiti vijñāya śāśvatam
|
santaḥ parārthaṃ kurvāṇā nāvekṣanti parasparam||49||

50na ca prasādaḥ satpuruṣeṣu mogho na cāpyartho nadayati nāpi mānaḥ
|
yasmādetanniyataṃ satsu nityaṃ tasmāt santo rakṣitāro bhavanti||50||

51yama uvāca|
yathāyathā bhāṣasi dharmasaṃhitaṃ manonukulaṃ supadaṃ mahārthavat
|
tathātathā me tvayi bhaktiruttamā varaṃ vṛṇīṣvāpratimaṃ pativratte||51||

52sāvitryuvāca|
na te'pavargaḥ sukṛtādvinākṛtastathā yathā'nyeṣu vareṣu mānada
|
varaṃ vṛṇe jīvatu satyavānayaṃ yathā mṛtā hyevamahaṃ patiṃ vinā||52||

53na kāmaye bhartṛvinākṛtā sukhaṃ na kāmaye bhartṛvinākṛtā divam
|
na kāmaye bhartṛvinākṛtā śriyaṃ na bhartṛhīnā vyavasāmi jīvitum||53||

54varātisargaḥ śataputratā mama tvayaiva datto hriyate ca me patiḥ
|
varaṃ vṛṇe jīvatu satyavānayaṃ tavaiva satyaṃ vacanaṃ bhaviṣyati||54||

55mārkaṇḍeya uvāca|
tathetyuktvā tu taṃ pāśaṃ muktvā vaivasvato yamaḥ
|
dharmarājaḥ prahṛṣṭātmā sāvitrīmidamabravīt||55||

56eṣa bhadre mayā mukto bhartā te kulanandini
|
arogastava neyaśca siddhārthaḥ sa bhaviṣyati||56||

57caturvarṣaśatāyuśca tvayā sārdhamavāpsyati
|
iṣṭvā yajñaiśca dharmeṇa khyātiṃ loke gamiṣyati||57||

58tvayi putraśataṃ caiva satyavān janayiṣyati
|
te cāpi sarve rājānaḥ kṣatriyāḥ putrapautriṇaḥ||58||

59khyātāstvannāmadheyāśca bhaviṣyantīha śāśvatāḥ
|
pituśca te putraśataṃ bhavitā tava mātari||59||

60mālavyāṃ mālavā nāma śāśvatāḥ putrapautriṇaḥ
|
bhrātaraste bhaviṣyanti kṣatriyāstridaśopamāḥ||60||

61evaṃ tasyai varaṃ datvā dharmarājaḥ pratāpavān
|
nivartayitvā sāvitrīṃ svameva bhavanaṃ yayau||61||

62sāvitryapi yame yāte bhartāraṃ pratilabhya ca
|
jagāma tatra yatrāsyā bhartuḥ śāvaṃ kalevaram||62||

63sā bhūmau prekṣya bhartāramupasṛtyopagṛhya ca
|
utsaṅge śira āropya bhūmāvupaviveśa ha||63||

64saṃjñāṃ ca sa punarlabdhvā sāvitrīmabhyabhāṣata
|
proṣyāgata iva premṇā punaḥ punarudīkṣya vai||64||

65satyavānuvāca|
suciraṃ bata supto'smi kimarthaṃ nāvabodhitaḥ
|
kva cāsau puruṣaḥ śyāmo yo'sau māṃ sañcakarṣa ha||65||

66sāvitryuvāca|
suciraṃ tvaṃ prasupto'si mamāṅke puruṣarṣabha
|
gataḥ sa bhagavān devaḥ prajāsaṃyamano yamaḥ||66||

67viśrānto'si mahābhāga vinidraśca nṛpātmaja
|
yadi śakyaṃ samuttiṣṭha vigāḍhāṃ paśya śarvarīm||67||

68mārkaṇḍeya uvāca|
upalabhya tataḥ saṃjñāṃ sukhasupta ivotthitaḥ
|
diśaḥ sarvā vanāntāṃśca nirīkṣyovāca satyavān||68||

69phalāhāro'smi niṣkrāntastvayā saha sumadhyame
|
tataḥ pāṭayataḥ kāṣṭhaṃ śiraso me rujā'bhavat||69||

70śirobhitāpasantaptaḥ sthātuṃ ciramaśaknuvan
|
tavotsaṅge prasupto'smi iti sarvaṃ smare śubhe||70||

71tvayopagūḍhasya ca me nidrayā'pahṛtaṃ manaḥ
|
tato'paśyaṃ tamo ghoraṃ puruṣaṃ ca mahaujasam||71||

72tadyadi tvaṃ vijānāsi kiṃ tadbrūhi sumadhyame
|
svapno me yadi vā dṛṣṭo yadi vā satyameva tat||72||

73tamuvācātha sāvitrī rajanī vyavagāhate
|
śvaste sarvaṃ yathāvṛttamākhyāsyāmi nṛpātmaja||73||

74uttiṣṭhottiṣṭha bhadraṃ te pitarī paśya suvrata
|
vigāḍhā rajanī ceyaṃ nivṛttaśca divākaraḥ||74||

75naktañcarāścarantyete hṛṣṭāḥ krūrābhibhāṣiṇaḥ
|
śrūyante parṇaśabdāśca mṛgāṇāṃ caratāṃ vane||75||

76etā ghoraṃ śivā nādān diśaṃ dakṣiṇapaścimām
|
āsthāya viruvantyugrāḥ kampayantyo mano mama||76||

77satyavānuvāca|
vanaṃ pratibhayākāraṃ ghanena tamasā vṛtam
|
na vijñāsyasi panthānaṃ gantuṃ caiva na śakṣyasi||77||

78sāvitryuvāca|
asminnadya vane dagdhe śuṣkavṛkṣaḥ sthito jvalan
|
vāyunā dhamyamāno'tra dṛśyate'gniḥ kvacitkvacit||78||

79tato'gnimānayitveha jvālayiṣyāmi sarvataḥ
|
kāṣṭhānīmāni santīha jahi santāpamātmanaḥ||79||

80yadi notsahase gantuṃ sarujaṃ tvāṃ hi lakṣaye
|
na ca jñāsyasi panthānaṃ tamasā saṃvṛte vane||80||

81śvaḥ prabhāte vane dṛśye yāsyāvo'numate tava
|
vasāveha kṣapāmekāṃ rucitaṃ yadi te'nagha||81||

82satyavānuvāca|
śirorujā nivṛttā me svasthānyaṅgāni lakṣaye
|
mātāpitṛbhyāmicchāmi saṅgamaṃ tvatprasādajam||82||

83na kadācidvikālaṃ hi gatapūrvo mayā śramaḥ
|
anāgatāyāṃ sandhyāyāṃ mātā me praruṇaddhi mām||83||

84divā'pi mayi niṣkrānte santapyete gurū mama
|
vicinoti hi māṃ tātaḥ sahaivāśramavāsibhiḥ||84||

85mātrā pitrā ca subhṛśaṃ duḥkhitābhyāmahaṃ purā
|
upālabdhaśca bahuśaścireṇāgacchasīti ha||85||

86kā tvavasthā tayoradya madarthamiti cintaye
|
tayoradṛśye mayi ca mahadduḥkhaṃ bhaviṣyati||86||

87purā māmūcatuścaiva rātrāvastrāyamāṇakau
|
bhṛśaṃ suduḥkhitau vṛddhau bahuśaḥ prītisaṃyuttau||87||

88tvayā hīnau na jīvāva muhūrtamapi putraka
|
yāvaddhariṣyase putra tāvannau jīvitaṃ dhruvam||88||

89vṛddhayorandhayordṛṣṭistvayi vaṃśaḥ pratiṣṭhitaḥ
|
tvayi piṇḍaśca kīrtiśca santānaṃ cāvayoriti||89||

90mātā vṛddhā pitā vṛddhastayoryaṣṭirahaṃ kila
|
tau rātrau māmapaśyantau kāmavasthāṃ gamiṣyataḥ||90||

91nidrāyāścābhyasūyāmi yasyā hetoḥ pitā mama
|
mātā ca saṃśayaṃ prāptā matkṛte'napakāriṇī||91||

92ahaṃ ca saṃśayaṃ prāptaḥ kṛcchrāmāpadamāsthitaḥ
|
mātāpitṛbhyāṃ hi vinā nāhaṃ jīvitumutsahe||92||

93vyaktamākulayā buddhyā prajñācakṣuḥ pitā mama
|
ekaikamasyāṃ velāyāṃ pṛcchatyāśramavāsinam||93||

94nātmānamanuśocāmi yathā'haṃ pitaraṃ śubhe
|
bhartāraṃ cāpyanugatāṃ mātaraṃ paridurbalam||94||

95matkṛte na hi tāvadya santāpaṃ parameṣyataḥ
|
jīvantāvanujīvāmi bhartavyau tau mayeti ha||95||

96tayoḥ priyaṃ me kartavyamiti jānāmi cāpyaham
|
mārkaṇḍeya uvāca|
evamuktvā sa dharmātmā gurubhakto gurupriyaḥ||96||

97ucchritya bāhū duḥkhārtaḥ susvaraṃ praruroda ha
|
tato'bravīt tathā dṛṣṭvā bhartāraṃ śokakarśitam||97||

98pramṛjyāśrūṇi netrābhyāṃ sāvitrī dharmacāriṇī
|
yadi me'sti tapastaptaṃ yadi dattaṃ hutaṃ yadi||98||

99śvaśrūśvaśurabhartṝṇāṃ mama puṇyā'stu śarvarī
|
na smarāmyuktapūrvaṃ vai svaireṣvapyanutāṃ giram||99||

100tena satyena tāvadya dhriyetāṃ śvagurau mama
|
satyavānuvāca|
kāmaye darśanaṃ pitroryāhi sāvitri mā ciram||100||

101purā mātuḥ piturvā'pi yadi paśyāmi vipriyam
|
na jīviṣye varārohe satyenātmānamālabhe||101||

102yadi dharme ca te buddhirmāṃ cejjīvantamicchasi
|
mama priyaṃ vā kartavyaṃ gacchāvāśramamantikāt||102||

103mārkaṇḍeya uvāca|
sāvitrī tata utthāya keśān saṃyamya bhāvinī
|
patimutthāpayāmāsa bāhubhyāṃ parigṛhya vai||103||

104utthāya satyavāṃścāpi pramṛjyāṅgāni pāṇinā
|
sarvā diśaḥ samālokya kaṭhine dṛṣṭimādadhe||104||

105tamuvācātha sāvitrī śvaḥ phalāni hariṣyasi
|
yogakṣemārthametaṃ te neṣyāmi paraśuṃ tvaham||105||

106kṛtvā kaṭhinabhāraṃ sā vṛkṣaśākhāvalāmbinam
|
gṛhītvā paraśuṃ bhartuḥ sakāśe punarāgamat||106||

107vāme skandhe tu vāmorūrbharturbāhuṃ niveśya ca
|
dakṣiṇena pariṣvajya jagāma gajagāminī||107||

108satyavānuvāca|
abhyāsagamanādbhīru panthāno viditā mama
|
vṛkṣāntarālokitayā jyotsnayā cāpi lakṣaye||108||

109āgatau svaḥ pathā yena phalānyavacitāni ca
|
yathāgataṃ śubhe gaccha panthānaṃ mā vicāraya||109||

110palāśakhaṇḍe caitasmin panthā vyāvartate dvidhā
|
tasyottareṇaḥ yaḥ panthāstena gaccha tvarasva ca||110||

111svastho'smi balavānasmi didṛkṣuḥ pitarāvubhau
|
bruvanneva tvarāyuktaḥ samprāyādāśramaṃ prati||111||
