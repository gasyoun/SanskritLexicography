Mahābhārata 2.8 (Sabhāparvan)

1nārada uvāca|
kathayiṣye sabhāṃ yāmyāṃ yudhiṣṭhira nibodha tām
|
vaivasvatasya yāṃ pārtha viśvakarmā cakāra ha||1||

2taijasī sā sabhā rājanbabhūva śatayojanā
|
vistārāyāmasampannā bhūyasī cāpi pāṇḍava||2||

3arkaprakāśā bhrājiṣṇuḥ sarvataḥ kāmarūpiṇī
|
nātiśītā na cātyuṣṇā manasaśca praharṣiṇī||3||

4na śoko na jarā tasyāṃ kṣutpipāse na cāpriyam
|
na ca dainyaṃ klamo vāpi pratikūlaṃ na cāpyuta||4||

5sarve kāmāḥ sthitāstasyāṃ ye divyā ye ca mānuṣāḥ
|
sāravacca prabhūtaṃ ca bhakṣyaṃ bhojyamarindama||5||

6lehyaṃ coṣyaṃ ca peyaṃ ca hṛdyaṃ svādu manoharam
|
puṇyagandhāḥ srajastasya nityaṃ kāmaphalā drumāḥ||6||

7rasavanti ca toyāni śītānyuṣṇāni caiva hi
|
tasyāṃ rājarṣayaḥ puṇyāstathā brahmarṣayo'malāḥ||7||

8yamaṃ vaivasvataṃ tāta prahṛṣṭāḥ paryupāsate
|
yayātirnahuṣaḥ pūrurmāndhātā somako nugaḥ||8||

9trasadasyuśca rājarṣiḥ kṛtavīryaḥ śrutaśravāḥ
|
ariṣṭanemiḥ siddhaśca kṛtavegaḥ kṛtirnimiḥ||9||

10pratardanaḥ śibirmatsyaḥ pṛthulākṣo bṛhadrathaḥ
|
vārto maruttaḥ kuṣikaḥ sāṅkāśyaḥ sāṅkṛtirdhruvaḥ||10||

11caturaśca sadasyormiḥ kārtavīryaśca pārthivaḥ
|
bhārataḥ surathaścaiva sunītho niśaṭho'nalaḥ||11||

12divodāsaśca sumanā ambarīṣo bhagīrathaḥ
|
vyaśvaḥ sadaśvo vaghryaśvaḥ pṛthuvegaḥ pṛthuśravāḥ||12||

13pṛṣadaśvā vasumanāḥ kṣupaśca sumahābalaḥ
|
ruṣadrurvṛṣasenaśca purukutso dhvajī rathī||13||

14ārṣṭiṣeṇo dilīpaśca mahātmā cāpyuśīnaraḥ
|
auśīnariḥ puṇḍarīkaḥ śaryātiḥ śarabhaḥ śuciḥ||14||

15aṅgoriṣṭaśca venaśca duṣyantaḥ sṛñjayo jayaḥ
|
bhāṅgāsuriḥ sunīthaśca niṣadho'tha vahīnaraḥ||15||

16karandhamo bāhlikaśca sudyumno balavānmadhuḥ
|
ailo maruttaśca tathā balavānpṛthivīpatiḥ||16||

17kapotaromā tṛṇakaḥ sahadevārjunau tathā
|
vyaśvaḥ sāśvaḥ kṛśāśvaśca śaśabinduśca pārthivaḥ||17||

18rāmo dāśarathiścaiva lakṣmaṇo'tha pratardanaḥ
|
alarkaḥ kakṣasenaśca gayo gaurāśva eva ca||18||

19jāmadagnyaśca rāmaśca nābhāgasagarau tathā
|
bhūridyumno mahāśvaśca pṛthāśvo janakastathā||19||

20rājā vainyo vārisenaḥ purijijjanamejayaḥ
|
brahmadattastrigartiśca rājoparicarastathā||20||

21indradyumno bhīmajānurgaurapṛṣṭho'nagho layaḥ
|
padmo'tha mucukundaśca bhūridyumnaḥ prasenajit||21||

22ariṣṭanemiḥ sudyumnaḥ pṛthulāśvo'ṣṭakastathā
|
śataṃ matsyā nṛpatayaḥ śataṃ nīpāḥ śataṃ hayāḥ||22||

23dhṛtarāṣṭrāścaikaśatamaśītirjanamejayāḥ
|
śataṃ ca brahmadattānāṃ vīriṇāmīriṇāṃ śatam||23||

24bhīṣṇāṇāṃ dve śatepyatra bhīmānāṃ tu tathā śatam
|
śataṃ ca prativindhyānāṃ śataṃ nāgāḥ śataṃ hayāḥ||24||

25palāśānāṃ śataṃ jñeyaṃ śataṃ kāśakuśādayaḥ
|
śāntanuścaiva rājendra pāṇḍuścaiva pitā tava||25||

26uśaṅgavaḥ śataratho devarājo jayadrathaḥ
|
vṛṣadarbhaśca rājarṣirbuddhimānsaha mantribhiḥ||26||

27athāpare sahasrāṇi ye gatāḥ śaśabindavaḥ
|
iṣṭvāśvamedhairbahubhirmahadbhirbhūridakṣiṇaiḥ||27||

28ete rājarṣayaḥ puṇyāḥ kīrtimanto bahuśrutāḥ
|
tasyāṃ sabhāyāṃ rājendra vaivasvatamupāsate||28||

29agastyo'tha mataṅgaśca kālo mṛtyustathaiva ca
|
yajvānaścaiva siddhāśca ye ca yogaśarīriṇaḥ||29||

30agniṣvāttāśca pitaraḥ phenapāścoṣmapāśca ye
|
sudhāvanto barhiṣado mūrtimantastathā'pare||30||

31kālacakraṃ ca sākṣācca bhagavānhavyavāhanaḥ
|
narā duṣkṛtakarmāṇo dakṣiṇāyanamṛtyavaḥ||31||

32kālasya nayane yuktā yamasya puruṣāśca ye
|
tasyāṃ śiṃśapapālāśāstathā kāśakuśādayaḥ||32||

33upāsate dharmarājaṃ mūrtimanto janādhipa
|
ete cānye ca bahavaḥ pitṛrājasabhāsadaḥ
|
na śakyāḥ parisaṅkhyātuṃ nāmabhiḥ karmabhistathā||33||

34asambādhā hi sā pārtha ramyā kāmagamā sabhā
|
dīrghakālaṃ tapastaptvā nirmitā viśvakarmaṇā||34||

35jvalantī bhāsamānā ca tejasā svena bhārata
|
tāmugratapaso yānti suvratāḥ satyavādinaḥ||35||

36śāntāḥ sanyāsinaḥ śuddhāḥ pūtāḥ puṇyena karmaṇā
|
sarve bhāsvaradehāśca sarve ca virajombarāḥ||36||

37citrāṅgadāścitramālyāḥ sarve jvalitakuṇḍalāḥ
|
sukṛtaiḥ karmabhiḥ puṇyaiḥ pāribarhaiśca bhūṣitāḥ||37||

38gandharvāśca mahātmānaḥ saṅghaśaścāpsarogaṇāḥ
|
vāditraṃ nṛtyagītaṃ ca hāsyaṃ lāsyaṃ ca sarvaśaḥ||38||

39puṇyāśca gandhāḥ śabdāśca tasyāṃ pārtha samantataḥ
|
divyāni caiva mālyāni upatiṣṭhanti nityaśaḥ||39||

40śataṃ śatasahasrāṇi dharmiṇāṃ taṃ prajeśvaram
|
upāsate mahātmānaṃ rūpayuktā manasvinaḥ||40||

41īdṛśī sā sabhā rājanpitṛrājño mahātmanaḥ
|
varuṇasyāpi vakṣyāmi sabhāṃ puṣkaramālinīm||41||
