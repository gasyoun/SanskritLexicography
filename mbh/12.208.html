Mahābhārata 12.208 (Śāntiparvan)

1yudhiṣṭhira uvāca|
ke pūrvamāsanpatayaḥ prajānāṃ bharatarṣabha
|
ke carṣayo mahābhāgā dikṣu pratyekaśaḥ smṛtāḥ ||1||

2bhīṣma uvāca|
śrūyatāṃ bharataśreṣṭha yanmāṃ tvaṃ paripṛcchasi
|
prajānāṃ patayo ye'smin dikṣu ye carṣayaḥ smṛtāḥ ||2||

3ekaḥ svayaṃbhūrbhagavānādyo brahmā sanātanaḥ
|
brahmaṇaḥ sapta va putrā mahātmānaḥ svayaṃbhuvaḥ ||3||

4marīciratryaṅgirasau pulastyaḥ pulahaḥ kratuḥ
|
vasiṣṭhaśca mahābhāgaḥ sadṛśo vai svayaṃbhuvā ||4||

5sapta brahmāṇa ityete purāṇe niścayaṃ gatāḥ
|
ata urdhvaṃ pravakṣyāmi sarvāneva prajāpatīn ||5||

6atrivaṃśasamutpanno brahmayoniḥ sanātanaḥ
|
prācīnabarhirbhagavāṃstasmātprācetaso daśa ||6||

7daśānāṃ tanayastveko dakṣo nāma prajāpatiḥ
|
tasya dve nāmanī loke dakṣaḥ ka iti cocyate ||7||

8marīceḥ kaśyapaḥ putrastasya dve nāmanī smṛte
|
ariṣṭanemirityeke kaśyapetyapare viduḥ ||8||

9atreścaivaurasaḥ śrīmān rājā somaśca vīryavān
|
sahasraṃ yaśca divyānāṃ yugānāṃ paryupāsitā ||9||

10aryamā caiva bhagavān ye cāsya tanayā vibho
|
ete pradeśāḥ kathitā bhuvanānāṃ prabhāvanāḥ ||10||

11śaśabindośca bhāryāṇāṃ sahasrāṇi daśācyuta
|
ekaikasyāṃ sahasraṃ tu tanayānāmabhūttadā ||11||

12evaṃ śatasahasrāṇāṃ śataṃ kasya mahātmanaḥ
|
putrāṇāṃ ca na te kañcidicchantyanyaṃ prajāpatim ||12||

13prajāmācakṣate viprāḥ purāṇāḥ śāśabindavīm
|
sa vṛṣṇivaṃśaprabhavo mahāvaṃśaḥ prajāpateḥ ||13||

14ete prajānāṃ patayaḥ samuddiṣṭā yaśasvinaḥ
|
ataḥ paraṃ pravakṣyāmi devāṃstribhuvaneśvarān ||14||

15bhagoṃ'śaścāryamā caiva mitro'tha varuṇastathā
|
savitā caiva dhātā ca vivasvāṃśca mahābalaḥ ||15||

16tvaṣṭā pūṣā tathaivendro dvādaśo viṣṇurucyate
|
ityete dvādaśādityāḥ kaśyapasyātmasambhavāḥ ||16||

17nāsatyaścaiva dasraśca smṛtau dvāvaśvināvapi
|
mārtaṇḍasyātmajāvetāvaṣṭamasya mahātmanaḥ ||17||

18te ca pūrvaṃ surāśceti dvividhāḥ pitaraḥ smṛtāḥ
|
tvaṣṭaścaivātmajaḥ śrīmān viśvarūpo mahāyaśāḥ ||18||

19ajaikapādahirbudhnyo virūpākṣo'tha raivataḥ
|
haraśca bahurūpaśca tryambakaśca sureśvaraḥ ||19||

20sāvitraśca jayantaśca pinākī cāparājitaḥ
|
pūrvameva mahābhāgā vasavo'ṣṭau prakīrtitāḥ ||20||

21eta evaṃvidhā devā manoreva prajāpateḥ
|
te ca pūrvaṃ surāśceti dvividhāḥ pitaraḥ smṛtāḥ ||21||

22śīla-yauvanatastvanyastathānyaḥ siddhasādhyayoḥ
|
ṛbhavo marutaścaiva devānāṃ codito gaṇaḥ ||22||

23evamete samāmnātā viśvedevāstathāśvinau
|
ādityāḥ kṣatriyāsteṣāṃ viśaśca marutastathā ||23||

24aśvinau tu smṛtau śūdrau tapasyugre samāsthitau
|
smṛtāstvaṅgiraso devā brāhmaṇā iti niścayaḥ ||24||

25ityetatsarvadevānāṃ cāturvarṇyaṃ prakīrtitam
|
etānvai prātarutthāya devān yastu prakīrtayet ||25||

26svajādanyakṛtāccaiva sarvapāpātpramucyate
|
yavakrīto'tha raibhyaśca arvāvasuparāvasū ||26||

27auśijaścaiva kakṣīvān balaścāṅgirasaḥ sutāḥ
|
ṛṣirmedhātitheḥ putraḥ kaṇvo barhiṣadastathā ||27||

28trailokyabhāvanāstāta prācyāṃ saptarṣayastathā
|
unmuco vimucaścaiva svastyātreyaśca vīryavān ||28||

29pramucaścedhmavāhaśca bhagavāṃśca dṛḍhavrataḥ
|
mitrāvaruṇayoḥ putrastathāgastyaḥ pratāpavān ||29||

30ete brahmarṣayo nityamāsthitā dakṣiṇāṃ diśam
|
uṣaṃguḥ kavaṣo dhaumyaḥ parivyādhaśca vīryavān ||30||

31ekataśca dvitaścaiva tritaścaiva maharṣayaḥ
|
atreḥ putraśca bhavāṃstathā sārasvataḥ prabhuḥ ||31||

32ete caiva mahātmanaḥ paścimāmāśritā diśam
|
ātreyaśca vasiṣṭhaśca kaśyapaśca mahānṛṣiḥ ||32||

33gautamo'tha bharadvājo viśvāmitro'tha kauśikaḥ
|
tathaiva putro bhagavānṛcīkasya mahātmanaḥ ||33||

34jamadagniśca saptaite udīcīmāśritā diśam
|
ete pratidiśaṃ sarve kīrtitāstigmatejasaḥ ||34||

35sākṣibhūtā mahātmāno bhuvanānāṃ prabhāvanāḥ
|
evamete mahātmānaḥ sthitāḥ pratyekaśo diśam ||35||

36yasyāṃ yasyāṃ diśi hyete tāṃ diśaṃ śaraṇaṃ gataḥ
|
mucyate sarvapāpebhyaḥ svastimāṃśca gṛhānvrajet ||36||
