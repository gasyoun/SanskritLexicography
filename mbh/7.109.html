Mahābhārata 7.109 (Droṇaparvan)

1sañjaya uvāca|
alaṃbuṣaṃ tathā yuddhe vicarantamabhītavat
|
haiḍimbiḥ prayayau tūrṇaṃ vivyādha niśitaiḥ śaraiḥ ||1||

2tayoḥ pratibhayaṃ yuddhamāsīdrākṣasasiṃhayoḥ
|
kurvatorvividhā māyāḥ śakraśambarayoriva ||2||

3alaṃbuṣo bhṛśaṃ kruddho ghaṭotkacamatāḍayat
|
tayoryuddhaṃ samabhavadrakṣogrāmaṇimukhyayoḥ ||3||

4yādṛgeva purā vṛttaṃ rāmarāvaṇayoḥ prabho
|
ghaṭotkacastu viṃśatyā nārācānāṃ stanāntare ||4||

5alaṃbuṣamatho viddhvā siṃhavadvyanadanmuhuḥ
|
tathaivālaṃbuṣo rājan haiḍimbiṃ yuddhadurmadam ||5||

6viddhvā viddhā'navad‌dhṛṣṭaḥ pūrayan khaṃ samantataḥ
|
tathā tau bhṛśasaṃkruddhau rākṣasendrau mahābalau ||6||

7nirviśeṣamayudhyetāṃ māyābhiritaretaram
|
māyāśatasṛjau nityaṃ mohayantī parasparam ||7||

8māyāyuddheṣu kuśalau māyāyuddhamayudhyatām
|
yāṃ yāṃ ghaṭotkaco yuddhe māyāṃ darśayate nṛpa ||8||

9tāṃ tāmalambuṣo rājanmāyayaiva nijaghnivān
|
taṃ tathā yudhyamānaṃ tu māyāyuddhaviśāradam ||9||

10alambuṣaṃ rākṣasendraṃ dṛṣṭvā'krudhyanta pāṇḍavāḥ
|
ta enaṃ bhṛśasaṃvignāḥ sarvataḥ pravarā rathaiḥ ||10||

11abhyadravanta saṃkruddhā bhīmasenādayo nṛpa
|
ta enaṃ koṣṭhakīkṛtya rathavaṃśena māriṣa ||11||

12sarvato vyakiranbāṇairulkābhiriva kuñjaram
|
sa teṣāmastravegaṃ taṃ pratihatyāstramāyayā ||12||

13tasmādrathavrajanmukto vanadāhādiva dvipaḥ
|
sa visphārya dhanurghoramindrāśanisamasvanam ||13||

14mārutiṃ pañcaviṃśatyā bhaimaseniṃ ca pañcabhiḥ
|
yudhiṣṭhiraṃ tribhirviddhvā sahadevaṃ ca saptabhiḥ ||14||

15nakulaṃ ca trisaptatyā draupadeyāṃśca māriṣa
|
pañcabhiḥ pañcabhirviddhvā ghoraṃ nādaṃ nanāda ha ||15||

16taṃ bhīmaseno navabhiḥ sahadevastu pañcabhiḥ
|
yudhiṣṭhiraḥ śatenaiva rākṣasaṃ pratyavidhyata ||16||

17nakulastu catuḥṣaṣṭyā draupadeyāstribhistribhiḥ
|
haiḍimbo rākṣasaṃ viddhvā yuddhe pañcāśatā śaraiḥ ||17||

18punarvivyādha saptatyā nanāda ca mahābalaḥ
|
tasya nādena mahatā kampiteyaṃ vasundharā ||18||

19saparvatavanā rājan sapāda‌pajalāśayā
|
so'tividdho maheṣvāsaiḥ sarvatastairmahārathaiḥ ||19||

20prativivyādha tānsarvānpañcabhiḥ pañcāmiḥ śaraiḥ
|
taṃ śuddhaṃ rākṣasaṃ yuddhe pratikruddhastu rākṣasaḥ ||20||

21haiḍimbo bharataśreṣṭha śarairvivyādha saptabhiḥ
|
so'tividdho balavatā rākṣasendro mahābalaḥ ||21||

22vyasṛjat sāyakāṃstūrṇaṃ rukmapuṃkhān śilāśitān
|
te śarā nataparvāṇo viviśū rākṣasaṃ tadā ||22||

23ruṣitāḥ pannagā yadvadgiriśṛṅgaṃ mahābalāḥ
|
tataste pāṇḍavā rājansamaṃtānniśitāñśarān ||23||

24preṣayāmāsurudvignā haiḍimbaśca ghaṭotkacaḥ
|
sa vidhyamānaḥ samare pāṇḍavairjitakāśibhiḥ ||24||

25martyadharmamanuprāptaḥ kartavyaṃ nānvapadyata
|
tataḥ samaraśauṇḍo vai bhaisasenirmahābalaḥ ||25||

26samīkṣya tadavasthaṃ taṃ vadhāyāsya mano dadhe
|
vegaṃ cakre mahāntaṃ ca rākṣasendrarathaṃ prati ||26||

27dagdhādrikūṭaśṛṅgābhaṃ bhinnāñjanacayopamam
|
rathādrathamabhidrutya kruddho haiḍimbirākṣipat ||27||

28udvabarha rathāccāpi pannagaṃ garuḍo yathā
|
samutkṣipya ca vāhubhyāmāviddhya ca punaḥ punaḥ ||28||

29niṣpiṣeṣa kṣitau kṣipraṃ pūrṇakumbhamivāśmani
|
balalāghavasampannaḥ sampadmo vikrameṇa ca ||29||

30bhaimasenī raṇe kruddhaḥ sarvasainyānyabhīṣayat
|
sa visphāritasarvāṅgaścūrṇitāsthirvibhīṣaṇaḥ ||30||

31ghaṭotkacena dhīreṇa hataḥ śālakaṭaṅkaṭaḥ
|
tataḥ sumanasaḥ pārthā hate tasminniśācare ||31||

32cukruśuḥ siṃhanādāṃśca vāsāṃsyādudhuvuśca ha
|
tāvakāśca hataṃ dṛṣṭvā rākṣasendraṃ mahābalam ||32||

33alambuṣaṃ tathā śūrā viśīrṇamiva parvatam
|
hāhākāramakārṣuśca sainyāni bharatarṣabha ||33||

34janāśca taddadṛśire rakṣaḥkautūhalānvitāḥ
|
yadṛcchayā nipatitaṃ bhūmāvaṅgārakaṃ yathā ||34||

35ghaṭotkacastu taddhatvā rakṣo balavatāṃ varam
|
mumoca balavannādaṃ balaṃ hatveva vāsavaḥ ||35||

36sa pūjyamānaḥ pitṛbhiḥ sabāndhavairghaṭotkacaḥ karmaṇi duṣkare kṛte
|
ripuṃ nihatyābhinananda vai tadā hyalambuṣaṃ pakvamalambuṣaṃ yathā ||36||

37tato ninādaḥ sumahānsamutthitaḥ saśaṅkhanānāvidhabāṇaghoṣavān
|
niśamya taṃ pratyanadaṃstu pāṇḍavāstato dhvanirbhuvanapathā spṛśadbhṛśam ||37||
