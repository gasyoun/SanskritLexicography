Mahābhārata 3.222 (Āraṇyakaparvan)

1mārkaṇḍeya uvāca|
āpasya muditā bhāryā sahasya paramā priyā
|
bhūpatirbhuvabhartā ca janayat pāvakaṃ param||1||

2bhūtānāṃ sāpi sarveṣāṃ yaṃ prāhuḥ pāvakaṃ patim
|
ātmā bhuvanabharteti sānvayeṣu dvijātiṣu||2||

3mahatāṃ caiva bhūtānāṃ sarveṣāmiha yaḥ patiḥ
|
bhagavān sa mahātejā nityaṃ carati pāvakaḥ||3||

4agnirgṛhapatirnāma nityaṃ yajñeṣu pūjyate
|
hutaṃ vahati yo havyamasya lokasya pāvakaḥ||4||

5apāṃ garbho mahābhāgaḥ sattvabhugyo mahādbhūtaḥ
|
bhūpatirbhuvabhartā ca mahataḥ patirucyate||5||

6dahan mṛtāni bhūtāni tasyāgnirbharato'bhavat
|
agniṣṭome ca niyataḥ kratuśreṣṭho bharasya tu||6||

7sa vahniḥ prathamo nityaṃ devairanviṣyate prabhuḥ
|
āyāntaṃ niyataṃ dṛṣṭvā praviveśārṇavaṃ bhayāt||7||

8devāstatrāpi gacchanti mārgamāṇā yathādiśam
|
dṛṣṭvā tvagniratharvāṇaṃ tato vacanamabravīt||8||

9devānāṃ vaha havyaṃ tvamahaṃ vīra sudurbalaḥ
|
atha tvaṃ gaccha madhvakṣaṃ priyametat kuruṣva me||9||

10preṣya cāgniratharvāṇamanyaṃ deśaṃ tato'gamat
|
matsyāstasya samācakhyuḥ kruddhastānagnirabravīt
|
bhakṣyā vai vividherbhāvairbhaviṣyatha śarīriṇām||10||

11atharvāṇaṃ tathā cāpi havyavāho'bravīdvacaḥ||11||

12anunīyamāno hi bhṛśaṃ devavākyāddhi tena saḥ
|
naicchadvoḍhuṃ haviḥ soḍhuṃ śarīraṃ cāpi so'tyajat||12||

13sa taccharīraṃ santyajya praviveśa dharāṃ tadā
|
bhūmiṃ spṛṣṭvā'sṛjaddhātūn pṛthak pṛthagatīva hi||13||

14pūyāt sa gandhaṃ tejaśca asthibhyo devadāru ca
|
śleṣmaṇaḥ sphāṭikaṃ tasya pittānmārakataṃ tathā||14||

15yakṛt kṛṣṇāyasaṃ tasya tribhireva babhuḥ prajāḥ
|
nakhāstasyābhrapaṭalaṃ śirājālāni vidrumam||15||

16śarīrādvividhāścānye dhātavo'syābhavannṛpa
|
evaṃ tyaktvā śarīraṃ ca parame tapasi sthitaḥ||16||

17bhṛgvaṅgirādibhirbhūyastapasotthāpitastadā
|
bhṛśaṃ jajvāla tejasvī tapasā''pyāyitaḥ śikhī||17||

18dṛṣṭvā ṛṣiṃ bhayāccāpi praviveśa mahārṇavam
|
tasminnaṣṭe jagadbhītamatharvāṇamathāśritam
|
arcayāmāsurevainamatharvāṇaṃ surādayaḥ||18||

19atharvā tvasṛjallokānātmanā''lokya pāvakam
|
miṣatāṃ sarvabhūtānāmunmamātha mahārṇavam||19||

20evamagnirbhagavatā naṣṭaḥ pūrvamatharvaṇā
|
āhūtaḥ sarvabhūtānāṃ havyaṃ vahati sarvadā||20||

21evaṃ tvajanayaddhiṣṇyān vedoktān vibudhān bahūn
|
vicaran vividhān deśān bhramamāṇastu tatra vai||21||

22sindhuṃ nadaṃ pañcanadaṃ devikā'tha sarasvatī
|
gaṅgā ca śatakumbhā ca śarayū gaṇḍasāhvayā||22||

23carmaṇvatī mahī caiva medhyā medhātithistadā
|
tāmravatī vetravatī nadyastisro'tha kauśikī||23||

24tamasā narmadā caiva nadī godāvarī tathā
|
veṇopavegā bhīmā ca vaḍavā caiva bhārata||24||

25bhāratī suprayogā ca kāverī murjurā tathā
|
tuṅgaveṇā kṛṣṇaveṇā kapilā śoṇa eva ca||25||

26etā nadyastu dhiṣṇyānāṃ mātaro yāḥ prakīrtitāḥ
|
adbhutasya priyā bhāryā tasya putro vibhūrasiḥ||26||

27yāvantaḥ pāvakāḥ proktāḥ somāstāvanta eva tu||27||

28atreścāpyanvaye jātā brahmaṇo mānasāḥ prajāḥ
|
atriḥ putrān sraṣṭukāmastānevātmanyadhārayat||28||

29tasya tadbrahmaṇaḥ kāyānnirharanti hutāśanāḥ
|
evamete mahātmānaḥ kīrtitāste'gnayo mayā||29||

30aprameyā yathotpannāḥ śrīmantastimirāpahāḥ
|
adbhutasya tu māhātmyaṃ yathā vedeṣu kīrtitam||30||

31tādṛśaṃ viddhi sarveṣāmeko hyeṣu hutāśanaḥ
|
eka evaiṣa bhagavān vijñeyaḥ prathamo'ṅgirāḥ||31||

32bahudhā niḥsṛtaḥ kāyājjyotiṣṭomaḥ kraturyathā
|
ityeṣa vaṃśaḥ sumahānagnīnāṃ kīrtito mayā
|
yo'rcito vividhairmantrairhavyaṃ vahati dehinām||32||
