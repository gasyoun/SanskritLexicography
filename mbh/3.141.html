Mahābhārata 3.141 (Āraṇyakaparvan)

1yudhiṣṭhira uvāca|
bhīmasenayamau cobhau pāñcāli ca nibodhata
|
nāsti bhūtasya nāśo vai paśyatāsmānvanecarān||1||

2durbalāḥ kleśitāḥ smeti yadbruvāmetaretaram
|
aśakye'pi vrajāmo yaddhanaṃjayadidṛkṣayā||2||

3tanme dahati gātrāṇi tūlarāśimivānalaḥ
|
yacca vīraṃ na paśyāmi dhanaṃjayamupāntikāt||3||

4tasya darśanatṛṣṇaṃ māṃ sānujaṃ vanamāsthitam
|
yājñasenyāḥ parāmarśaḥ sa ca vīra dahatyuta||4||

5nakulātpūrvajaṃ pārthaṃ na paśyāmyamitaujasam
|
ajeyamugradhanvānaṃ tena tapye vṛkodara||5||

6tīrthāni caiva ramyāṇi vanāni ca sarāṃsi ca
|
carāmi saha yuṣmābhistasya darśanakāṅkṣayā||6||

7pañcavarṣāṇyahaṃ vīraṃ satyasandhaṃ dhanaṃjayam
|
yanna paśyāmi bībhatsuṃ tena tapye vṛkodara||7||

8taṃ vai śyāmaṃ guḍākeśaṃ siṃhavikrāntagāminam
|
na paśyāmi mahābāhuṃ tena tapye vṛkodara||8||

9kṛtāstraṃ nipuṇaṃ yuddhe'pratimānaṃ dhanuṣmatām
|
na paśyābhi kuruśreṣṭha tena tapye vṛkodara||9||

10carantamarisaṃgheṣu kāle kruddhamivāntakam
|
prabhinnamiva mātaṅgaṃ siṃhaskandhaṃ dhanaṃjayam||10||

11yaḥ sa śakrādanavaro vīryeṇa draviṇena ca
|
yamayoḥ pūrvajaḥ pārthaḥ śvetāśvo'mitavikramaḥ||11||

12duḥkhena mahatāviṣṭastanna paśyāmi phālgunam
|
ajeyamugradhanvānaṃ tena tapye vṛkodara||12||

13satataṃ yaḥ kṣamāśīlaḥ kṣipyamāṇo'pyaṇīyasā
|
ṛjumārgaprapannasya śarma dātābhayasya ca||13||

14sa tu jihmapravṛttasya māyayābhijighāṃsataḥ
|
api vajradharasyāpi bhavetkālaviṣopamaḥ||14||

15śatrorapi prapannasya so'nṛśaṃsaḥ pratāpavān
|
dātābhayasya bībhatsuramitātmā mahābalaḥ||15||

16sarveṣāmāśrayo'smākaṃ raṇe'rīṇāṃ pramarditā
|
āhartā sarvaratnānāṃ sarveṣāṃ naḥ sukhāvahaḥ||16||

17ratnāni yasya vīryeṇa divyānyāsanpurā mama
|
bahūni bahujātīni yāni prāptaḥ suyodhanaḥ||17||

18yasya bāhubalādvīra sabhā cāsītpurā mama
|
sarvaratnamayī khyātā triṣu lokeṣu pāṇḍava||18||

19vāsudevasamaṃ vīrye kārtavīryasamaṃ yudhi
|
ajeyamamitaṃ yuddhe taṃ na paśyāmi phālgunam||19||

20saṃkarṣaṇaṃ mahāvīryaṃ tvāṃ ca bhīmāparājitam
|
anuyātaḥ svavīryeṇa vāsudevaṃ ca śatruhā||20||

21yasya bāhubale tulyaḥ prabhāve ca puraṃdaraḥ
|
jave vāyurmukhe somaḥ krodhe mṛtyuḥ sanātanaḥ||21||

22te vayaṃ taṃ naravyāghraṃ sarve vīradidṛkṣavaḥ
|
pravekṣyāmo mahābāho parvataṃ gandhamādanam||22||

23viśālā badarī yatra naranārāyaṇāśramaḥ
|
taṃ sadādhyuṣitaṃ yakṣairdrakṣyāmo girimuttamam||23||

24kuberanalinīṃ ramyāṃ rākṣasairabhisevitām
|
padbhireva gamiṣyāmastapyamānā mahattapaḥ||24||

25na ca yānavatā śakyo gantuṃ deśo vṛkodara
|
na nṛśaṃsena lubdhena nāpraśāntena bhārata||25||

26tatra sarve gamiṣyāmo bhīmārjunagaveṣiṇaḥ
|
sāyudhā baddhanistriṃśāḥ sārdhaṃ viprairmahāvrataiḥ||26||

27makṣikādaṃśamaśakānsiṃhānvyāghrānsarīsṛpān
|
prāpnotyaniyataḥ pārtha niyatastānna paśyati||27||

28te vayaṃ niyatātmānaḥ parvataṃ gandhamādanam
|
pravekṣyāmo mitāhārā dhanaṃjayadidṛkṣavaḥ||28||
