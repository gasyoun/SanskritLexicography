Mahābhārata 15.22 (Āśramavāsikaparvan)

1vaiśampāyana uvāca|
evaṃ te puruṣavyāghrāḥ pāṇḍavā mātṛnandanāḥ
|
smaranto mātaraṃ vīrā babhūvurbhṛśaduḥkhitāḥ||1||

2ye rājakāryeṣu purā vyāsaktā nityaśo'bhavan
|
te rājakāryāṇi tadā nākārṣuḥ sarvataḥ pure||2||

3praviṣṭā iva śokena nābhyanandanta kiñcana
|
sambhāṣyamāṇā api te na kiñcitpratyapūjayan||3||

4te sma vīrā durādharṣā gāmbhīrye sāgaropamāḥ
|
śokopahatavijñānā naṣṭasaṃjñā ivābhavan||4||

5acintayaṃśca jananīṃ tataste pāṇḍunandanāḥ
|
kathaṃ nu vṛddhamithunaṃ vahatyatikṛśā pṛthā||5||

6kathaṃ ca sa mahīpālo hataputro nirāśrayaḥ
|
patnyā saha vasatyeko vane śvāpadasevite||6||

7sā ca devī mahābhāgā gāndhārī hatabāndhavā
|
patimandhaṃ kathaṃ vṛddhamanveti vijane vane||7||

8evaṃ teṣāṃ kathayatāmautsukyamabhavattadā
|
gamane cābhavadbuddhirdhṛtarāṣṭradidṛkṣayā||8||

9sahadevastu rājānaṃ praṇipatyedamabravīt
|
aho me bhavato dṛṣṭaṃ hṛdayaṃ gamanaṃ prati||9||

10na hi tvāṃ gauraveṇāhamaśakaṃ vaktumañjasā
|
gamanaṃ prati rājendra tadidaṃ samupasthitam||10||

11diṣṭyā drakṣyāmi tāṃ kuntīṃ vartayantīṃ tapasvinīm
|
jaṭilāṃ tāpasī vṛddhāṃ kuśakāśaparikṣatām||11||

12prāsādaharmyasaṃvṛddhāmatyantasukhabhāginīm
|
kadā tu jananīṃ śrāntāṃ drakṣyāmi bhṛśaduḥkhitām||12||

13anityāḥ khalu martyānāṃ gatayo bharatarṣabha
|
kuntī rājasutā yatra vasatyasukhitā vane||13||

14sahadevavacaḥ śrutvā draupadī yoṣitāṃ varā
|
uvāca devī rājānamabhipūjyābhinandya ca||14||

15kadā drakṣyāmi nāṃ devīṃ yadi jīvati sā pṛthā
|
jīvantyā hyadya me prītirbhaviṣyati janādhipa||15||

16eṣā te'stu matirnityaṃ dharme te ramatāṃ manaḥ
|
yo'dya tvamasmān rājendra śreyasā yojayiṣyasi||16||

17agrapādasthitaṃ cemaṃ viddhi rājanvadhūjanam
|
kāṅkṣantaṃ darśanaṃ kuntyā gāndhāryāḥ śvaśurasya ca||17||

18ityuktaḥ sa nṛpo devyā draupadyā bharatarṣabha
|
senādhyakṣān samānāyyā sarvānidamuvāca ha||18||

19niryātayata me senāṃ prabhūtarathakuñjarām
|
drakṣyāmi vanasaṃsthaṃ ca dhṛtarāṣṭraṃ mahīpatim||19||

20stryadhyakṣāṃścābravīdrājā yānāni vividhāni me
|
sajjīkriyantāṃ sarvāṇi śibikāśca sahasraśaḥ||20||

21śakaṭāpaṇaveśāśca kośaḥ śilpina eva ca
|
niryāntu koṣapālāśca kurukṣetrāśramaṃ prati||21||

22yaśca paurajanaḥ kaściddraṣṭumicchati pārthivam
|
anāvṛtaḥ suvihitaḥ sa ca yātu surakṣitaḥ||22||

23sūdāḥ paurogavāścaiva sarvaṃ caiva mahānasam
|
vividhaṃ bhakṣyabhojyaṃ ca śakaṭairuhyatāṃ mama||23||

24prayāṇaṃ ghuṣyatāṃ caiva śvobhūta iti mā ciram
|
kriyatāṃ pathi cāpyadya veśmāni vividhāni ca||24||

25evamājñāpya rājā sa bhrātṛbhiḥ sahapāṇḍavaḥ
|
śvobhūte niryayau rājan sastrīvṛddhapuraḥsaraḥ||25||

26sa bahirdivasāneva janaughaṃ paripālayan
|
nyavasannṛpatiḥ pañca tato'gacchadvanaṃ prati||26||
