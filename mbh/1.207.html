Mahābhārata 1.207 (Ādiparvan)

1drupada uvāca|
evametanmahāprājña yathāttha vidurādya mām
|
mamāpi paramo harṣaḥ saṃbandhesminkṛte prabho||1||

2gamanaṃ cāpi yuktaṃ syāddṛḍhameṣāṃ mahātmanām
|
na tu tāvanmayā yuktametadvaktuṃ svayaṃ girā||2||

3yadā tu manyate vīraḥ kuntīputro yudhiṣṭhiraḥ
|
bhīmasenārjunau caiva yamau ca puruṣarṣabhau||3||

4rāmakṛṣṇau ca dharmajñau tadā gacchantu pāṇḍavāḥ
|
etau hi puruṣavyāghrāveṣāṃ priyahite ratau||4||

5yudhiṣṭhira uvāca|
paravanto vayaṃ rājaṃstvayi sarve sahānugāḥ
|
yathā vakṣyasi naḥ prītyā tatkariṣyāmahe vayam||5||

6vaiśaṃpāyana uvāca|
tato'bravīdvāsudevo gamanaṃ rocate mama
|
yathā vā manyate rājā drupadaḥ sarvadharmavit||6||

7drupada uvāca|
yathaiva manyate vīro dāśārhaḥ puruṣottamaḥ
|
prāptakālaṃ mahābāhuḥ sā buddhirniścitā mama||7||

8yathaiva hi mahābhāgāḥ kaunteyā mama sāṃpratam
|
tathaiva vāsudevasya pāṇḍuputrā na saṃśayaḥ||8||

9na taddhyāyati kaunteyaḥ pāṇḍuputro yudhiṣṭhiraḥ
|
yathaiṣāṃ puruṣavyāghraḥ śreyo dhyāyati keśavaḥ||9||

10vaiśaṃpāyana uvāca|
tataste samanujñātā drupadena mahātmanā
|
pāṇḍavāścaiva kṛṣṇaśca viduraśca mahīpate||10||

11ādāya draupadīṃ kṛṣṇāṃ kuntīṃ caiva yaśasvinīm
|
savihāraṃ sukhaṃ jagmurnagaraṃ nāgasāhvayam||11||

12śrutvā cāpyāgatānvīrāndhṛtarāṣṭro janeśvaraḥ
|
pratigrahāya pāṇḍūnāṃ preṣayāmāsa kauravān||12||

13vikarṇaṃ ca maheṣvāsaṃ citrasenaṃ ca bhārata
|
droṇaṃ ca parameṣvāsaṃ gautamaṃ kṛpameva ca||13||

14taiste parivṛtā vīrāḥ śobhamānā mahābalāḥ
|
nagaraṃ hāstinapuraṃ śanaiḥ praviviśustadā||14||

15kautūhalena nagaraṃ pūryamāṇamivābhavat
|
tatra te puruṣavyāghrāḥ śokaduḥkhasamanvitāḥ||15||

16tata uccāvacā vācaḥ pauraiḥ priyacikīrṣubhiḥ
|
udīritā aśṛṇvaṃste pāṇḍavā hṛdayaṃgamāḥ||16||

17ayaṃ sa puruṣavyāghraḥ punarāyāti dharmavit
|
yo naḥ svāniva dāyādāndharmeṇa parirakṣati||17||

18adya pāṇḍurmahārājo vanādiva janapriyaḥ
|
āgataḥ priyamasmākaṃ cikīrṣurnātra saṃśayaḥ||18||

19kiṃ nu nādya kṛtaṃ tāta sarveṣāṃ naḥ paraṃ priyam
|
yannaḥ kuntīsutā vīrā nagaraṃ punarāgatāḥ||19||

20yadi dattaṃ yadi hutaṃ vidyate yadi nastapaḥ
|
tena tiṣṭhantu nagare pāṇḍavāḥ śaradāṃ śatam||20||

21tataste dhṛtarāṣṭrasya bhīṣmasya ca mahātmanaḥ
|
anyeṣāṃ ca tadarhāṇāṃ cakruḥ pādābhivandanam||21||

22kṛtvā tu kuśalapraśnaṃ sarveṇa nagareṇa ca
|
nyaviśantātha veśmāni dhṛtarāṣṭrasya śāsanāt||22||

23viśrāntāste mahātmānaḥ kaṃcitkālaṃ mahābalāḥ
|
āhūtā dhṛtarāṣṭreṇa rājñā śāntanavena ca ||23||

24dhṛtarāṣṭra uvāca|
bhrātṛbhiḥ saha kaunteya nibodha gadatā mama
|
punarno vigraho mā bhūtkhāṇḍavaprasthamāviśa||24||

25na ca vo vasatastatra kaścicchaktaḥ prabādhitum
|
saṃrakṣyamāṇānpārthena tridaśāniva vajriṇā||25||

26ardhaṃ rājyasya saṃprāpya khāṇḍavaprasthaṇāviśa
|
vaiśaṃpāyana uvāca|
pratigṛhya tu tadvākyaṃ nṛpaṃ sarve praṇamya ca||26||

27pratasthire tato ghoraṃ vanaṃ tanmanujarṣabhāḥ
|
ardhaṃ rājyasya saṃprāpya khāṇḍavaprasthamāviśan||27||

28tataste pāṇḍavāstatra gatvā kṛṣṇapurogamāḥ
|
maṇḍayāñcakrire tadvai puraṃ svargādivadacyutāḥ||28||

29tataḥ puṇye śive deśe śāntiṃ kṛtvā mahārathāḥ
|
nagaraṃ māpayāmāsurdvaipāyanapurogamāḥ||29||

30sāgarapratirūpābhiḥ parikhābhiralaṅkṛtam
|
prākāreṇa ca saṃpannaṃ divamāvṛtya tiṣṭhatā||30||

31pāṇḍurābhraprakāśena himaraśminibhena ca
|
śuśubhe tatpuraśreṣṭhaṃ nāgairbhogavatīmiva||31||

32dvipakṣagaruḍaprakhyairdvāraiḥ saudhaiśca śobhitam
|
guptamabhracayaprakhyairgopurairmandaropamaiḥ||32||

33vividhairapi nirviddhaiḥ śastropetaiḥ susaṃvṛtaiḥ
|
śaktibhiścāvṛtaṃ taddhi dvijihvairiva pannagaiḥ||33||

34talpaiścābhyāsikairyuktaṃ śuśubhe yodharakṣitam
|
tīkṣṇāṅkuśaśataghnībhiryantrajālaiśca śobhitam||34||

35āyasaiśca mahācakraiḥ śuśubhe tatpurottamam
|
suvibhaktamahārathyaṃ devatābādhavarjitam||35||

36virocamānaṃ vividhaiḥ pāṇḍurairbhavanottamaiḥ
|
tattriviṣṭapasaṃkāśamindraprasthaṃ vyarocata||36||

37meghavṛndamivākāśe viddhaṃ vidyutsamāvṛtam
|
tatra ramye śive deśe kauravyasya niveśanam||37||

38śuśubhe dhanasaṃpūrṇaṃ dhanādhyakṣakṣayopamam
|
tatrāgacchandvijā rājansarvavedavidāṃ varāḥ||38||

39nivāsaṃ rocayanti sma sarvabhāṣāvidastathā
|
vaṇijaścāyayustatra nānādigbhyo dhanārthinaḥ||39||

40sarvaśilpavidastatra vāsāyābhyāgamaṃstadā
|
udyānāni ca ramyāṇi nagarasya samantataḥ||40||

41āmrairāmrātakairnīpairaśokaiścampakaistathā
|
punnāgairnāgapuṣpaiśca lakucaiḥ panasaistathā||41||

42śālatālatamālaiśca bakulaiśca saketakaiḥ
|
manoharaiḥ supuṣpaiśca phalabhārāvanāmitaiḥ||42||

43prācīnāmalakairlodhrairaṅkolaiśca supiṣpitaiḥ
|
jambūbhiḥ pāṭalābhiśca kubjakairatimuktakaiḥ||43||

44karavīraiḥ pārijātairanyaiśca vividhairdrumaiḥ
|
nityapuṣpaphalopetairnānādvijagaṇāyutaiḥ||44||

45mattabarhiṇasaṃghuṣṭakokilaiśca sadāmadaiḥ
|
gṛhairādarśavimalairvividhaiśca latāgṛhaiḥ||45||

46manoharaiścitragṛhaistathā'jagatipravataiḥ
|
vāpībhirvividhābhiśca pūrṇābhiḥ paramāmbhasā||46||

47sarobhiratiramyaiśca padmotpalasugandhibhiḥ
|
haṃsakāraṇḍavayutaiścakravākopaśobhitaiḥ||47||

48ramyāśca vividhāstatra puṣkariṇyo vanāvṛtāḥ
|
taḍāgāni ca ramyāṇi bṛhanti subahūni ca||48||

49teṣāṃ puṇyajanopetaṃ rāṣṭramāviśatāṃ mahat
|
pāṇḍavānāṃ mahārāja śaśvatprītiravardhata||49||

50tatra bhīṣmeṇa rājñā ca dharmapraṇayane kṛte
|
pāṇḍavāḥ samapadyanta khāṇḍavaprasthavāsinaḥ||50||

51pañcabhistairmaheṣvāsairindrakalpaiḥ samāvṛtam
|
śuśubhe tatpuraśreṣṭhaṃ nāgairbhogavatī yathā||51||

52tāṃ niveśya tato vīro rāmeṇa saha keśavaḥ
|
yayau dvāravatīṃ rājanpāṇḍavānumate tadā||52||
