Mahābhārata 12.135 (Śāntiparvan)

1bhīṣma uvāca|
atrāpyudāharantīmamitihāsaṃ purātanam
|
yathā dasyuḥ samaryādaḥ pretyabhāve na naśyati ||1||

2prahartā matimān śūraḥ śrutavān sunṛśaṃsavān
|
rakṣannāśramiṇāṃ dharmaṃ brahmaṇyo gurupūjakaḥ ||2||

3niṣādyāṃ kṣatriyājjātaḥ kṣatradharmānupālakaḥ
|
kāyavyo nāma naiṣādirdasyutvātsiddhimāptavān ||3||

4araṇye sāyaṃ pūrvāhṇe mṛgayūthaprakopitā
|
vidhijño mṛgajātīnāṃ naiṣādānāṃ ca kovidaḥ ||4||

5sarvakālapradeśajñaḥ pāriyātracaraḥ sadā
|
dharmajñaḥ sarvabhūtānāmamogheṣurdṛḍhāyudhaḥ ||5||

6apyanekaśatāṃ senāmeka eva jigāya saḥ
|
sa vṛddhāvandhabadhirau mahāraṇye'bhyapūjayat ||6||

7madhumāṃsairmūlaphalairannaruccāvacairapi
|
satkṛtya bhojayāmāsa mānyānparicacāra ca ||7||

8āraṇyakān pravrajitān brāhmaṇān paripūjayan
|
api tebhyo mṛgān hatvā nināya satataṃ vane ||8||

9ye'smānna pratigṛhṇanti dasyubhojanaśaṅkayā
|
teṣāmāsajya geheṣu kalya eva sa gacchati ||9||

10bahūni ca sahasrāṇi grāmaṇitve'bhivavrire
|
nirmaryādāni dasyūnāṃ niranukrośavartinām ||10||

11dasyava ūcuḥ |
muhūrtadeśakālajñaḥ prājñaḥ śūro dṛḍhavrataḥ
|
grāmaṇīrbhavano mukhyo sarveṣāmeva saṅgataḥ ||11||

12yathā yathā vakṣyasi naḥ kariṣyāmastathā tathā
|
pālayāsmānyathānyāyaṃ yathā mātā yathā pitā ||12||

13kāyavya uvāca |
mā vadhīstvaṃ striyaṃ bhīrūṃ mā śiṣṭuṃ mā tapasvinam
|
nāyuddhyamāno hantavyo na ca grāhyā balāt striyaḥ ||13||

14sarvathā strī na hantavyā sarvasattveṣu kenacit
|
nityaṃ tu brāhmaṇe svasti yoddhavyaṃ ca tadarthataḥ ||14||

15satyaṃ ca nāpi hartavyaṃ sāravighnaṃ ca mā kṛthāḥ
|
pūjyante yatra devāśca pitaro'tithayastathā ||15||

16sarvabhūteṣvapi ca vai brāhmaṇo mokṣamarhati
|
kāryā copacitisteṣāṃ sarvasvenāpi yā bhavet ||16||

17yasya hyete sampraruṣṭā mantrayanti parābhavam
|
na tasya triṣu lokeṣu trātā bhavati kaścana ||17||

18yo brāhmaṇānparivadedvināśaṃ cāpi rocayet
|
sūryodaya iva dhvānte dhruvaṃ tasya parābhavaḥ ||18||

19ihaiva phalamāsīnaḥ pratyākāṅkṣeta sarvaśaḥ
|
ye ye no na pradāsyanti tāṃstāṃstenābhiyāsyasi ||19||

20śiṣṭyarthaṃ vihito daṇḍo na vṛddhyarthaṃ viniścayaḥ
|
ye ca śiṣṭān prabādhante daṇḍasteṣāṃ vadhaḥ smṛtaḥ ||20||

21ye ca rāṣṭroparodhena vṛddhiṃ kurvanti kecana
|
tadaiva te'numāryante kuṇape kṛmayo yathā ||21||

22ye punardharmaśāstreṇa varteranniha dasyavaḥ
|
api te dasyavo bhūtvā kṣipraṃ siddhimavāpnuyuḥ ||22||

23bhīṣma uvāca|
te sarvamevānucakruḥ kāyavyasyānuśāsanam
|
vṛddhiṃ ca lebhire sarve pāpebhyaścāpyupāraman ||23||

24kāyavyaḥ karmaṇā tena mahatīṃ siddhimāptavān
|
sādhūnāmācaran kṣemaṃ dasyūnpāpānnivartayan ||24||

25idaṃ kāyavyacaritaṃ yo nityamanucintayet
|
nāraṇyebhyo hi bhūtebhyo bhayaṃ prāpnoti kiṃcana ||25||

26na bhayaṃ tasya bhūtebhyaḥ sarvebhyaścaiva bhārata
|
nāsato vidyate rājan sa hyaraṇyeṣu gopatiḥ ||26||
