Mahābhārata 1.185 (Ādiparvan)

1vaiśaṃmpāyana uvāca|
evamuktāḥ prayātāste pāṇḍavā janamejaya
|
rājñā dakṣiṇapañcālāndrupadenābhirakṣitān||1||

2tatasteṣu mahātmānaṃ śuddhātmānamakalmaṣam
|
dadṛśuḥ pāṇḍavā vīrāḥ pathi dvaipāyanaṃ tadā||2||

3tasmai yathāvatsatkāraṃ kṛtvā tena ca satkṛtāḥ
|
kathānte cābhyanujñātāḥ prayayurdrupadakṣayam||3||

4paśyanto ramaṇīyāni vanāni ca sarāṃsi ca
|
tatra tatra vasantaśca śanairjagmurmaharāthāḥ||4||

5svādhyāyavantaḥ śucayo madhurāḥ priyavādinaḥ
|
ānupūrvyeṇa saṃprāptāḥ pāñcālānpāṇḍunandanāḥ||5||

6te tu dṛṣṭvā puraṃ tacca skandhāvāraṃ ca pāṇḍavāḥ
|
kumbhakārasya śālāyāṃ nivāsaṃ cakrire tadā||6||

7tatra bhaikṣaṃ samājahrurbrāhmaṇīṃ vṛttimāśritāḥ
|
tānsaṃprāptāṃstathā vīrāñjajñire na narāḥ kvacit||7||

8yajñasenasya kāmastu pāṇḍavāya kirīṭine
|
kṛṣṇāṃ dadyāmiti sadā na catadvivṛṇoti saḥ||8||

9so'nveṣamāṇaḥ kaunteyaṃ pāñcālyo janamejaya
|
dṛḍhaṃ dhanurathānamyaṃ kārayāmāsa bhārata||9||

10yantraṃ vaihāyasaṃ cāpi kārayāmāsa kṛtrimam
|
tena yantreṇa samitaṃ rālā lakṣyaṃ cakāra saḥ||10||

11drupada uvāca|
idaṃ sajyaṃ dhanuḥ kṛtvā sajjairebhiśca sāyakaiḥ
|
atītya lakṣya yo veddhā sa labdhā matsutāmiti||11||

12vaiśaṃpāyana uvāca|
iti sa drupado rājā svayaṃvaramaghoṣayat
|
tacchrutvā pārthivāḥ sarve samīyustatra bhārata||12||

13ṛṣayaśca mahātmānaḥ svayaṃvaradidṛkṣavaḥ
|
duryodhanapurogāśca sakarṇāḥ kuravo nṛpa||13||

14tato'rcitā rājaguṇā drupadena mahātmanā||14||

15upopaviṣṭā mañceṣu draṣṭukāmāḥ svayaṃvaram
|
tataḥ paurajanāḥ sarve sāgaroddhūtaniḥsvanāḥ||15||

16śiśumāraśiraḥ prāpya nyaviśaṃste sma pārthivāḥ
|
prāguttareṇa nagarādbhūmibhāge same śubhe
|
samājavāṭaḥ śuśubhe bhavanaiḥ sarvato vṛtaḥ||16||

17prākāraparikhopeto dvāratoraṇamaṇḍitaḥ
|
vitānena vicitreṇa sarvataḥ samalaṅkṛtaḥ||17||

18tūryaughaśatasaṅkīrṇaḥ parārdhyāgurudhūpitaḥ
|
candanodakasiktaśca mālyadāmopaśobhitaḥ||18||

19kailāsaśikharaprakhyairnabhastalavilekhibhiḥ
|
sarvataḥ saṃvṛtaḥ śubhraiḥ prāsādaiḥ sukṛtocchrayai||19||

20suvarṇajālasaṃvītairmaṇikuṭṭimabhūṣaṇaiḥ
|
sukhārohaṇasaupānairmahāsanaparicchadaiḥ||20||

21sragdāmasamavacchannairagurūttamavāsitaiḥ
|
haṃsāṃśuvarṇairbahubhirāyojanasugandhibhiḥ||21||

22asaṃbādhaśatadvāraiḥ śayanāsanaśobhitaiḥ
|
bahudhā tu pinaddhāṅgairhimavacchikharairiva||22||

23tatra nānāprakāreṣu vimāneṣu svalaṅkṛtāḥ
|
spardhamānāstadā'nyo'nyaṃ niṣeduḥ sarvapārthivāḥ||23||

24tatropaviṣṭāndadṛśurmahāsattvāparākramān
|
rājasiṃhānmahābhāgānkṛṣṇāguruvibhūṣitān||24||

25mahāprasādānbrāhmaṇyānsvarāṣṭraparirakṣiṇaḥ
|
priyānsarvasya lokasya sukṛtaiḥ karmabhiḥ śubhaiḥ||25||

26mañceṣu ca parārdhyeṣu paurajānapadā janāḥ
|
kṛṣṇādarśanasiddhyarthaṃ sarvataḥ samupāviśan||26||

27brāhmaṇaiste ca sahitāḥ pāṇḍavāḥ samupāviśan
|
ṛddhiṃ pañcālarājasya paśyantastāmanuttamām||27||

28tataḥ samājo vavṛdhe sa rājandivasānbahūn
|
ratnapradānabahulaḥ śobhito naṭanartakaiḥ||28||

29vartamāne samāje tu ramaṇīye'hni ṣoḍaśe
|
āplutāṅgī suvasanā sarvābharaṇabhūṣitā||29||

30mālāṃ ca samupādāya kāñcanīṃ samalaṅkṛtām
|
avatīrṇā tato raṅgaṃ draupadī bharatarṣabha||30||

31purohitaḥ somakānāṃ mantravidbrāhmaṇaḥ śuciḥ
|
paristīrya juhāvāgnimājyena vidhivattadā||31||

32saṃtarpayitvā jvalanaṃ brāhmaṇānsvasti vācya ca
|
vārayāmāsa sarvāṇi vāditrāṇi samantataḥ||32||

33niḥśabde tu kṛte tasmindhṛṣṭadyumno viśāṃpate
|
kṛṣṇāmādāya vidhivanmeghadundubhiniḥsvanaḥ||33||

34raṅgamadhyaṃ gatastatra meghagambhīrayā girā
|
vākyamuccairjagādedaṃ ślakṣṇamarthavaduttamam||34||

35idaṃ dhanurlakṣyamime ca bāṇāḥ śṛṇvantu me bhūpatayaḥ sametāḥ
|
chidreṇa yantrasya samarpayadhvaṃ śaraiḥ śitairvyomacarairdaśārdhaiḥ||35||

36etanmahatkarma karoti yo vai kulena rūpeṇa balena yuktaḥ
|
tasyādya bhāryā bhaginī mameyaṃ kṛṣṇā bhavitrī na mṛṣā bravīmi||36||

37tānevamuktvā drupadasya putraḥ paścādidaṃ tāṃ bhaginīmuvāca
|
nāmnā ca gotreṇa ca karmaṇā ca saṃkīrtayanbhūmipatīnsametān||37||
