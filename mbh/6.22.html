Mahābhārata 6.22 (Bhīṣmaparvan)

1sañjaya uvāca|
tato yudhiṣṭhiro rājā svāṃ senāṃ samanodayat
|
prativyūhannanīkāni bhīṣmasya bharatarṣabha ||1||

2yathoddiṣṭānyanīkāni pratyavyūhanta pāṇḍavāḥ
|
svargaṃ paramamicchantaḥ suyuddhena kurūdvahāḥ ||2||

3madhye śikhaṇḍino'nīkaṃ rakṣitaṃ savyasācinā
|
dhṛṣṭadyumnaścarannagre bhīmasenena pālitaḥ ||3||

4anīkaṃ dakṣiṇaṃ rājanyuyudhānena pālitam
|
śrīmatā sātvatāgryeṇa śakreṇeva dhanuṣmatā ||4||

5mahendrayānapratimaṃ rathaṃ tu sopaskaraṃ hāṭakaratnacitram
|
yudhiṣṭhiraḥ kāñcanabhāṇḍayoktraṃ samāsthito nāgapurasya madhye ||5||

6samucchritaṃ dantaśalākamasya supāṇḍuraṃ chatramatīva bhāti
|
pradakṣiṇaṃ cainamupācaranta maharṣayaḥ saṃstutibhirmahendram ||6||

7purohitāḥ śatruvadhaṃ vadanto brahmarṣisiddhāḥ śrutavanta enam
|
japyaiśca mantraiśca mahauṣadhībhiḥ samantataḥ svastyayanaṃ bruvantaḥ ||7||

8tataḥ sa vastrāṇi tathaiva gāśca phalāni puṣpāṇi tathaiva niṣkān
|
kurūttamo brāhmaṇasānmahātmā kurvanyayau śakra ivāmareśaḥ ||8||

9sahasrasūryaḥ śatakiṅkiṇīkaḥ parārdhyajāmbūnadahemacitraḥ
|
ratho'rjunasyāgnirivārcimālī vibhrājate śvetahayaḥ sucakraḥ ||9||

10tamāsthitaḥ keśavasaṅgṛhītaṃ kapidhvajo gāṇḍivabāṇapāṇiḥ
|
dhanurdharo yasya samaḥ pṛthivyāṃ na vidyate no bhavitā kadācit ||10||

11uddhartayiṣyaṃstava putrasenāṃ atīva raudraṃ sa bibharti rūpam
|
anāyudho yaḥ subhujo bhujābhyāṃ narāśvanāgānyudhi bhasma kuryāt ||11||

12sa bhīmasenaḥ sahito yamābhyāṃ vṛkodaro vīrarathasya goptā
|
taṃ tatra siṃharṣabhamattakhelaṃ loke mahendrapratimānakalpam ||12||

13samīkṣya senāgragataṃ durāsadaṃ saṃvivyathuḥ paṅkagatā yathā dvipāḥ
|
vṛkodaraṃ vāraṇājadarpaṃ yodhāstvadīyā bhayavignasattvāḥ ||13||

14anīkamadhye tiṣṭhantaṃ rājaputraṃ durāsadam
|
abravīdbharataśreṣṭhaṃ guḍākeśaṃ janārdanaḥ ||14||

15vāsudeva uvāca|
ya eṣa roṣātpratapanbalastho yo naḥ senāṃ siṃha ivekṣate ca
|
sa eṣa bhīṣmaḥ kuruvaṃśaketuḥ yenāhṛtāstriśataṃ vājimedhāḥ ||15||

16etānyanīkāni mahānubhāvaṃ gūhanti meghā iva raśmimantam
|
etāni hatvā puruṣapravīra kāṅkṣasva yuddhaṃ bharatarṣabheṇa ||16||
