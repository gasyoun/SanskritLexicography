Mahābhārata 5.115 (Udyogaparvan)

1nārada uvāca|
evamuktaḥ suparṇena tathyaṃ vacanamuttamam
|
vimaśyāvahito rājā niścitya ca punaḥ punaḥ ||1||

2yaṣṭā kratusahasrāṇāṃ dātā dānapatiḥ prabhuḥ
|
yayātiḥ sarvakāśīśa idaṃ vacanamabravīt ||2||

3dṛṣṭvā priyasakhaṃ tārkṣyaṃ gālavaṃ ca dvijarṣabham
|
nidarśanaṃ ca tapasā bhikṣāṃ ślāghyāṃ ca kīrtitām ||3||

4atītya ca nṛpānanyānādityakulasaṃbhavān
|
matsakāśamanuprāptāvetāṃ buddhimavekṣya ca ||4||

5adya me saphalaṃ janma tāritaṃ cādya me kulam
|
adyāyaṃ tārito deśo mama tārkṣya tvayā'nagha ||5||

6vaktumicchāmi tu sakhe yathā jānāsi māṃ purā
|
na tathā vittavānasmi kṣīṇaṃ vittaṃ ca me sakhe ||6||

7na ca śakto'smi te kartuṃ moghamāgamanaṃ khaga
|
na cāśāmaraya viprarṣorvitathīkartumutsahe ||7||

8tattu dāsyāmi yat kāryamidaṃ sampādayiṣyati
|
abhigamya hatāśo hi nivṛtto dahate kulam ||8||

9nātaḥ paraṃ vainateya kiñcit pāpiṣṭhamucyate
|
yathāśānāśanālloke dehi nāstīti vā vacaḥ ||9||

10hatāśo hyakṛtārthaḥ san hataḥ saṃbhāvito naraḥ
|
hinasti tasya putrāṃśca pautrāṃścākurvato hitam ||10||

11tasmāccaturṇāṃ vaṃśānāṃ sthāpayitrī sutā mama
|
iyaṃ surasutaprakhyā sarvadharmopacāyinī ||11||

12sadā devamanuṣyāṇāmasurāṇāṃ ca gālava
|
kāṅkṣitā rūpato bālā sutā me pratigṛhyatām ||12||

13asyāḥ śulkaṃ pradāsyanti nṛpā rājyamapi dhruvam
|
kiṃ punaḥ śyāmakarṇānāṃ hayānāṃ dve catuḥśate ||13||

14sa bhavān pratigṛhṇātu mamaitāṃ mādhavīṃ sutām
|
ahaṃ dauhitravān syāṃ vai vara eṣa mama prabho ||14||

15pratigṛhya ca tāṃ kanyāṃ gālavaḥ saha pakṣiṇā
|
punardrakṣyāva ityuktvā pratasthe saha kanyayā ||15||

16upalabdhamidaṃ dvāramaśvānāmiti cāṇḍajaḥ
|
uktvā gālavamāpṛcchaya jagāma bhavanaṃ svakam ||16||

17gate patagarāje tu gālavaḥ saha kanyayā
|
cintayānaḥ kṣamaṃ dāne rājñāṃ vai śulkato'gamat ||17||

18so'gacchanmanasekṣvākuṃ haryaśvaṃ rājasattamam
|
ayodhyāyāṃ mahāvīryaṃ caturaṅgabalānvitam ||18||

19kośadhānyabalopetaṃ priyapauraṃ dvijapriyam
|
prajābhikāmaṃ śāmyantaṃ kurvāṇaṃ tapa uttamam ||19||

20tamupāgamya vipraḥ sa haryaśvaṃ gālavo'bravīt
|
kanyeyaṃ mama rājendra prasavaiḥ kulavardhinī ||20||

21iyaṃ śulkena bhāryārthaṃ haryaśva pratigṛhyatām
|
śulkaṃ te kīrtayiṣyāmi tacchrutvā saṃpradhāryatām ||21||
