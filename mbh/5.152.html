Mahābhārata 5.152 (Udyogaparvan)

1vaiśampāyana uvāca|
tato deśe same snigdhe prabhūtayavasendhane
|
niveśayāmāsa tadā senāṃ rājā yudhiṣṭhiraḥ ||1||

2parihṛtya śmaśānāni devatāyatanāni ca
|
āśramāṃśca maharṣīṇāṃ tīrthānyāyatanāni ca ||2||

3madhurānūṣare deśe śucau puṇye mahāmatiḥ
|
niveśaṃ kārayāmāsa kuntīputro yudhiṣṭhiraḥ ||3||

4tataśca punarutthāya sukhī viśrāntavāhanaḥ
|
prayayau pṛthivīpālairvṛtaḥ śatasahasraśaḥ ||4||

5vidrāvya śataśo gulmān dhārtarāṣṭrasya sainikān
|
paryakrāmat samantācca pārthena saha keśavaḥ ||5||

6śibiraṃ māpayāmāsa dhṛṣṭadyumnaśca pārṣataḥ
|
sātyakiśca rathodāro yuyudhānaśca vīryavān ||6||

7āsādya saritaṃ puṇyāṃ kurukṣetre hiraṇvatīm
|
sūpatīrthāṃ śucijalāṃ śarkarāpaṅkavarjitām ||7||

8khānayāmāsa parikhāṃ keśavastatra bhārata
|
guptyarthamapi cādiśya balaṃ tatra nyaveśayat ||8||

9vidhiryaḥ śibirasyāsītpāṇḍavānāṃ mahātmanām
|
tadvidhāni narendrāṇāṃ kārayāmāsa keśavaḥ ||9||

10prabhūtatarakāṣṭhāni durādharṣatarāṇi ca
|
bhakṣyabhojyānnapānāni śataśo'tha sahasraśaḥ ||10||

11śibirāṇi mahārhāṇi rājñāṃ tatra pṛthakpṛthak
|
vimānānīva rājendra niviṣṭhāni mahītale ||11||

12tatrāsan śilpinaḥ prājñāḥ śataśo dattavetanāḥ
|
sarvopakaraṇairyuktā vaidyāḥ śāstraviśāradāḥ ||12||

13jyādhanurvarmaśastrāṇāṃ tathaiva madhusarpiṣoḥ
|
sasarjarasapāṃsūnāṃ rāśayaḥ parvatopamāḥ ||13||

14bahūdakaṃ suyavasaṃ tuṣāṅgārasamanvitam
|
śibire śibire rājā sañcakāra yudhiṣṭhiraḥ ||14||

15mahāyantrāṇi nārācāstomarāṇi paraśvadhāḥ
|
dhanūṃṣi kavacādīni ṛṣṭayastūṇasaṃyutāḥ ||15||

16gajāḥ kaṇṭakasannāhā lohavarmottaracchadāḥ
|
dṛśyante tatra giryābhāḥ sahasraśatayodhinaḥ ||16||

17niviṣṭān pāṇḍavāṃstatra jñātvā mitrāṇi bhārata
|
abhisasruryathādeśaṃ sabalāḥ sahavāhanāḥ ||17||

18caritabrahmacaryāste somapā bhūridakṣiṇāḥ
|
jayāya pāṇḍuputrāṇāṃ samājagmurmahīkṣitaḥ ||18||
