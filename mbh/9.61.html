Mahābhārata 9.61 (Śalyaparvan)

1dhṛtarāṣṭra uvāca|
hataṃ duryodhanaṃ dṛṣṭvā bhīmasenena saṃyuge
|
pāṇḍavāḥ sṛñjayāścaiva kimakurvata sañjaya||1||

2sañjaya uvāca|
hataṃ duryodhanaṃ dṛṣṭvā bhīmasenena saṃyuge
|
siṃheneva mahārāja mattaṃ vanagajaṃ yathā||2||

3prahṛṣṭamanasastatra kṛṣṇena saha pāṇḍavāḥ
|
pañcālāḥ sṛñjayāścaiva nihate kurunandane||3||

4āviddhyannuttarīyāṇi siṃhanādāṃśca nedire
|
naitānharṣasamāviṣṭāniyaṃ sehe vasundharā||4||

5dhanūṃṣyante vyākṣipanta jyāścāpyanye tathā'kṣipan
|
dadhmuranye mahāśaṅkhānanye jaghnuśca dundubhīn||5||

6cikrīḍuśca tathaivānye jahasuśca tavāhitāḥ
|
abruvaṃścāsakṛdvīrā bhīmasenamidaṃ vacaḥ||6||

7duṣkaraṃ bhavatā karma raṇe'dya sumahatkṛtam
|
kauravendraṃ raṇe hatvā gadayā'tikṛtaśramam||7||

8indreṇeva hi vṛtrasya vadhaṃ paramasaṃyuge
|
tvayā kṛtamamanyanta śatrorvadhamimaṃ janāḥ||8||

9carantaṃ vividhānmārgānmaṇḍalāni ca sarvaśaḥ
|
duryodhanamimaṃ śūraṃ ko'nyo hanyādvṛkodarāt||9||

10vairasya ca gataḥ pāraṃ tvamihānyaiḥ sudurgamam
|
aśakyametadanyena sampādayitumīdṛśam||10||

11kuñjareṇeva mattena vīra saṅgrāmamūrdhani
|
duryodhanaśiro diṣṭyā pādena mṛditaṃ tvayā||11||

12siṃhena mahiṣasyeva kṛtvā saṅgaramuttamam
|
duḥśāsanasya rudhiraṃ diṣṭyā pītaṃ tvayānagha||12||

13ye viprakurvan rājānaṃ dharmātmānaṃ yudhiṣṭhiram
|
mūrdhni teṣāṃ kṛtaḥ pādo diṣṭyā te svena karmaṇā||13||

14amitrāṇāmadhiṣṭhānādvadhādduryodhanasya ca
|
bhīma diṣṭyā pṛthivyāṃ te prathitaṃ sumahadyaśaḥ||14||

15evaṃ nūnaṃ hate vṛtre śakraṃ nandanti bandinaḥ
|
tathā tvāṃ nihatāmitraṃ vayaṃ nandāma bhārata||15||

16duryodhanavadhe yāni romāṇi hṛṣitāni naḥ
|
adyāpi na vikṛṣyante tāni tadviddhi bhārata||16||

17ityabruvan bhīmasenaṃ vātikāstatra saṅgatāḥ
|
tān hṛṣṭānpuruṣavyāghrān pañcālānpāṇḍavaiḥ saha||17||

18bruvato'sadṛśaṃ tatra provāca madhusūdanaḥ
|
na nyāyyaṃ nihataṃ śatruṃ bhūyo hantuṃ narādhipāḥ||18||

19asakṛdvāgbhirugrābhirnihato hyeṣa mandadhīḥ
|
tadaivaiṣa hataḥ pāpo yadaiva nirapatrapaḥ||19||

20lubdhaḥ pāpasahāyaśca suhṛdāṃ śāsanātigaḥ
|
bahuśo viduradroṇakṛpagāṅgeyasañjayaiḥ||20||

21pāṇḍubhyaḥ prārthyamāno'pi pitryamaṃśaṃ na dattavān
|
naiṣa yogyo'dya mitraṃ vā śatrurvā puruṣādhamaḥ||21||

22kimanenātibhugnena vāgbhiḥ kāṣṭhasadharmaṇā
|
ratheṣvārohata kṣipraṃ gacchāmo vasudhādhipāḥ||22||

23diṣṭyā hato'yaṃ pāpātmā sāmātyajñātibāndhavaḥ
|
iti śrutvā tvadhikṣepaṃ kṛṣṇādduryodhano nṛpaḥ||23||

24amarṣavaśamāpanna udatiṣṭhadviśāmpate
|
sphigdeśenopaviṣṭaḥ sa dorbhyāṃ viṣṭabhya medinīm||24||

25dṛṣṭiṃ bhrūsaṅkaṭāṃ kṛtvā vāsudevo nyapātayat
|
ardhonnataśarīrasya rūpamāsīnnṛpasya tu||25||

26kruddhasyāśīviṣasyeva cchinnapucchasya bhārata
|
prāṇāntakariṇīṃ ghorāṃ vedanāmapyacintayan||26||

27duryodhano vāsudevaṃ vāgbhirugrābhirārdayat
|
kaṃsadāsasya dāyāda na te lajjā'styanena vai||27||

28adharmeṇa gadāyuddhe yadahaṃ vinipātitaḥ
|
ūrū bhindhīti bhīmasya smṛtiṃ mithyā prayacchatā||28||

29kiṃ na vijñātametanme yadarjunamavocathāḥ
|
ghātayitvā mahīpālānṛjuyuddhānsahasraśaḥ||29||

30jihmairupāyairbahubhirna te lajjā na te ghṛṇā
|
ahanyahani śūrāṇāṃ kurvāṇaḥ kadanaṃ mahat||30||

31śikhaṇḍinaṃ puraskṛtya ghātitaste pitāmahaḥ
|
aśvatthāmnaḥ sanāmānaṃ hatvā nāgaṃ sudurmate||31||

32ācāryo nyāsitaḥ śastraṃ kiṃ tanna viditaṃ mayā
|
sa cānena nṛśaṃsena dhṛṣṭadyumnena vīryavān||32||

33pātyamānastvayā dṛṣṭo na cainaṃ tvamavārayaḥ
|
vadhārthaṃ pāṇḍaputrasya yācitāṃ śaktimeva ca||33||

34ghaṭotkace vyaṃsayataḥ kastvattaḥ pāpakṛttamaḥ
|
chinnahastaḥ prāyagatastathā bhūriśravā balī||34||

35tvayā'bhisṛṣṭena hataḥ śaineyena mahātmanā
|
kurvāṇaścottamaṃ karma karṇaḥ pārthajigīṣayā||35||

36vyaṃsanenāśvasenasya pannagendrasya vai punaḥ
|
punaśca patite cakre vyasanārtaḥ parājitaḥ||36||

37pātitaḥ samare karṇaścakravyagro'graṇīnṛṇām
|
yadi māṃ cāpi karṇaṃ ca bhīṣmadroṇau ca saṃyutau||37||

38ṛjunā pratiyudhyethā na te syādvijayo dhruvam
|
tvayā punaranāryeṇa jihmamārgeṇa pārthivāḥ||38||

39svadharmamanutiṣṭhanto vayaṃ cānye ca ghātitāḥ
|
vāsudeva uvāca|
hatastvamasi gāndhāre sabhrātṛsutabāndhavaḥ||39||

40sagaṇaḥ sasuhṛccaiva pāpaṃ mārgamanuṣṭhitaḥ
|
tavaiva duṣkṛtairvīrau bhīṣmadroṇau nipātitau||40||

41karṇaśca nihataḥ saṅkhye tava śīlānuvartakaḥ
|
yācyamānaṃ mayā mūḍha pitryamaṃśaṃ na ditsasi||41||

42pāṇḍavebhyaḥ svarājyaṃ ca lobhācchakuniniścayāt
|
viṣaṃ te bhīmasenāya dattaṃ sarve ca pāṇḍavāḥ||42||

43pradīpitā jatugṛhe mātrā saha sudurmate
|
sabhāyāṃ yājñasenī ca kṛṣṭā dyūte rajasvalā||43||

44tadaiva tāvadduṣṭātman vadhyastvaṃ nirapatrapa
|
anakṣajñaṃ ca dharmajñaṃ saubalenākṣavedinā||44||

45nikṛtyā yatparājaiṣīstasmādasi hato raṇe
|
jayadrathena pāpena yatkṛṣṇā kleśitā vane||45||

46yā teṣu mṛgayāṃ caiva tṛṇabindorathāśramam
|
abhimanyuśca yadbāla eko bahubhirāhave||46||

47tvaddoṣairnihataḥ pāpa tasmādasi hato raṇe
|
yānyakāryāṇi cāsmākaṃ kṛtānīti prabhāṣase||47||

48vaiguṇyena tavātyarthaṃ sarvaṃ hi tadanuṣṭhitam
|
bṛhaspateruśanaso nopadeśaḥ śrutastvayā||48||

49vṛddhā nopāsitāścaiva hitaṃ vākyaṃ na te śrutam
|
lobhenātibalena tvaṃ tṛṣṇayā ca vaśīkṛtaḥ||49||

50kṛtavānasya kāryāṇi vipākastasya bhujyatām
|
duryodhana uvāca|
adhītaṃ vidhivaddattaṃ bhūḥ praśāstā sasāgarā||50||

51mūrdhni sthitamamitrāṇāṃ ko nu svantataro mayā
|
yadiṣṭaṃ kṣatrabandhūnāṃ svadharmamanupaśyatām||51||

52tadidaṃ nidhanaṃ prāptaṃ ko nu svantataro mayā
|
devārhā mānuṣā bhogāḥ prāptā asulabhā nṛpaiḥ||52||

53aiśvaryaṃ cottamaṃ prāptaḥ ko nu svantataro mayā
|
sasuhṛtsānugaścaiva svargaṃ gantāhamacyuta||53||

54yūyaṃ nihatasaṅkalpāḥ śocanto vartayiṣyatha
|
sañjaya uvāca|
asya vākyasya nidhane kururājasya dhīmataḥ||54||

55apatatsumahadvarṣaṃ puṣpāṇāṃ puṇyagandhinām
|
avādayanta gandharvā vāditraṃ sumanoharam||55||

56jaguścāpsaraso rājño yaśaḥsambaddhameva ca
|
siddhāśca mumucurvācaḥ sādhu sādhviti pārthiva||56||

57vavau ca surabhirvāyuḥ puṇyagandho mṛduḥ sukhaḥ
|
vyarājaṃśca diśaḥ sarvā nabho vaidūryasaṃnibham||57||

58atyadbhutāni te dṛṣṭvā vāsudevapurogamāḥ
|
duryodhanasya pūjāṃ tu dṛṣṭvā vrīḍāmupāgaman||58||

59hatāṃścādharmataḥ śrutvā śokārtāḥ śuśucurhi te
|
bhīṣmaṃ droṇaṃ tathā karṇaṃ bhūriśravasameva ca||59||

60tāṃstu cintāparān dṛṣṭvā pāṇḍavān dīnacetasaḥ
|
provācedaṃ vacaḥ kṛṣṇo meghadundubhiniḥsvanaḥ||60||

61naiṣa śakyo'tiśīghrāstraste ca sarve mahārathāḥ
|
ṛjuyuddhena vikrāntā hantuṃ yuṣmābhirāhave||61||

62naiṣa śakyaḥ kadācittu hantuṃ dharmeṇa pārthivaḥ
|
te vā bhīṣmamukhāḥ sarve maheṣvāsā mahārathāḥ||62||

63mayānekairūpāyaistu māyāyogena cāsakṛt
|
hatāste sarva evājau bhavatāṃ hitamicchatā||63||

64yadi naivaṃvidhaṃ jātu kuryāṃ jihmamahaṃ raṇe
|
kuto vo vijayo bhūyaḥ kuto rājyaṃ kuto dhanam||64||

65te hi sarve mahātmānaścatvāro'tirathā bhuvi
|
na śakyā dharmato hantuṃ lokapālairapi svayam||65||

66tathaivāyaṃ gadāpāṇirdhārtarāṣṭro gataklamaḥ
|
na śakyo dharmato hantuṃ kālenāpīha daṇḍinā||66||

67na ca vo hṛdi kartavyaṃ yadayaṃ ghātito ripuḥ
|
mithyāvadhyāstathopāyairbahavaḥ śatravo'dhikāḥ||67||

68pūrvairanugato mārgo devairasuraghātibhiḥ
|
saddhiścānugataḥ panthāḥ sa sarvairanugamyate||68||

69kṛtakṛtyāśca sāyāhne nivāsaṃ rocayāmahe
|
sāścanāgarathāḥ sarve viśramāmo narādhipāḥ||69||

70vāsudevavacaḥ śrutvā tadānīṃ pāṇḍavaiḥ saha
|
pañcālā bhṛśasaṃhṛṣṭā vineduḥ siṃhasaṅghavat||70||

71tataḥ prādhyāpayañśaṅkhānpāñcajanyaṃ ca mādhavaḥ
|
hṛṣṭā duryodhanaṃ dṛṣṭvā nihataṃ puruṣarṣabha||71||
