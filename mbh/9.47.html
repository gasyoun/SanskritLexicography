Mahābhārata 9.47 (Śalyaparvan)

1janamejaya uvāca|
atyadbhutamidaṃ brahman śrutavānasmi tattvataḥ
|
abhiṣekaṃ kumārasya vistareṇa yathāvidhi||1||

2yacchrutvā pūtamātmānaṃ vijānāmi tapodhana
|
prahṛṣṭāni ca romāṇi prasannaṃ ca mano mama||2||

3abhiṣekaṃ kumārasya daityānāṃ ca vadhaṃ tathā
|
śrutvā me paramā prītirbhūyaḥ kautūhalaṃ hi me||3||

4apāṃ patiḥ kathaṃ hyasminnabhiṣiktaḥ purā suraiḥ
|
tanme brūhi mahāprājña kuśalo hyasi sattama||4||

5vaiśampāyana uvāca|
śṛṇu rājannidaṃ citraṃ pūrvakalpe yathātatham
|
ādau kṛtayuge rājan vartamāne yathāvidhi||5||

6varuṇaṃ devatāḥ sarvāḥ yametyedapathābruvan
|
yathāsmān surarāṭchakro bhayebhyaḥ pāti sarvadā||6||

7tathā tvamapi sarvāsāṃ saritāṃ vai patirbhava
|
vāsaśca te sadā deva sāgare makarālaye||7||

8samudro'yaṃ tava vaśe bhaviṣyati nadīpatiḥ
|
somena sārdhaṃ ca tava hānivṛddhī bhaviṣyataḥ||8||

9evamastviti tāndevānvaruṇo vākyamabravīt
|
samāgamya tataḥ sarve varuṇaṃ sāgarālayam||9||

10apāṃ patiṃ pracakrurhi vidhidṛṣṭena karmaṇā
|
abhiṣicya tato devā varuṇaṃ yādasāṃ patim||10||

11jagmuḥ svānyeva sthānāni pūjayitvā jaleśvaram
|
abhiṣiktastato devairvaruṇo'pi mahāyaśāḥ||11||

12saritaḥ sāgarāṃścaiva nadāṃścāpi sarāṃsi ca
|
pālayāmāsa vidhinā yathā devāśatakratuḥ||12||

13tatastatrāpyupaspṛśya dattvā ca vividhaṃ vasu
|
agnitīrthaṃ mahāprājño jagāmātha pralambahā||13||

14naṣṭo na dṛśyate yatra śamīgarbhe hutāśanaḥ
|
lokālokavināśe ca prādurbhūte tadānagha||14||

15upatasthuḥ surā yatra sarvalokapitāmaham
|
agniḥ pranaṣṭo bhagavān kāraṇaṃ ca na vidmahe||15||

16sarvabhūtakṣayo rājan sampādaya vibho'nalam
|
janamejaya uvāca|
kimarthaṃ bhagavānagniḥ pranaṣṭo lokabhāvanaḥ||16||

17vijñātaśca kathaṃ devaistanmamācakṣva tattvataḥ
|
vaiśampāyana uvāca|
bhṛgoḥ śāpādbhṛśaṃ bhīto jātavedāḥ pratāpavān||17||

18śamīgarbhamathāsādya nanāśa bhagavāṃstataḥ
|
pranaṣṭe tu tadā vahnau devāḥ sarve savāsavāḥ||18||

19anvaiṣanta tadā naṣṭaṃ jvalanaṃ bhṛśaduḥkhitāḥ
|
tato'gnitīrthamāsādya śamīgarbhasthameva hi||19||

20dadṛśurjvalanaṃ tatra vasamānaṃ yathāvidhi
|
devāḥ sarve naravyāghra bṛhaspatipurogamāḥ||20||

21jvalanaṃ taṃ samāsādya prītā'bhūvan savāsavāḥ
|
punaryathāgataṃ jagmuḥ sarvabhakṣaśca so'bhavat||21||

22bhṛgoḥ śāpānmahābhāga yaduktaṃ brahmavādinā
|
tatrāpyāplutya matimān brahmayoniṃ jagāma ha||22||

23sasarja bhagavān yatra sarvalokapitāmahaḥ
|
tatrāplutya tato brahmā saha devaiḥ prabhuḥ purā||23||

24sasarja tīrthāni tathā devatānāṃ yathāvidhi
|
tatra snātvā ca datvā ca vasūni vividhāni ca||24||

25kauberaṃ prayayau tīrthaṃ tatra taptvā mahattapaḥ
|
dhanādhipatyaṃ samprāpto rājannailabila prabhuḥ||25||

26tatrasthameva taṃ rājan dhanāni nidhayastathā
|
upatasthurnaraśreṣṭha tattīrthaṃ lāṅgalī balaḥ||26||

27gatvā datvā ca vidhivadbrāhmaṇebhyo dhanaṃ dadau
|
dadṛśe tatra tat sthānaṃ kaubere kānanottame||27||

28purā yatra tapastaptaṃ vipulaṃ sumahātmanā
|
yakṣarājñā kubereṇa varā labdhāśca puṣkalāḥ||28||

29dhanādipatyaṃ sakhyaṃ ca rudreṇāmitatejasā
|
suratvaṃ lokapālatvaṃ putraṃ ca nalakūbaram||29||

30yatra lebhe mahābāho dhanādhipatirañjasā
|
abhiṣiktaśca tatraiva samāgamya marudgaṇaiḥ||30||

31vāhanaṃ cāsya taddattaṃ haṃsayuktaṃ manojavam
|
vimānaṃ puṣpakaṃ divyaṃ nairṛtaiścaryameva ca||31||

32tatrāplutya balo rājandattvā dāyāṃśca puṣkalān
|
jagāma tvarito rāmastīrthaṃ śvetānulepanaḥ||32||

33niṣevitaṃ sarvasattvairnāmnā badarapācanam
|
nānartukavanopetaṃ sadā puṣpaphalaṃ śubham||33||
