Mahābhārata 12.194 (Śāntiparvan)

1yudhiṣṭhira uvāca|
adhyātmaṃ nāma yadidaṃ puruṣasyeha cintyate
|
yadadhyātmaṃ yathā caitattanme brūhi pitāmaha ||1||

2kutaḥ sṛṣṭamidaṃ viśvaṃ brahman sthāvarajaṅgamam
|
pralaye kathamabhyeti tanme vaktumihārhasi ||2||

3bhīṣma uvāca|
adhyātmamiti māṃ pārtha yadetadanupṛcchasi
|
tadvyākhyāsyāmi te tāta śreyaskaratamaṃ sukham ||3||

4sṛṣṭipralayasaṃyuktamācāryaiḥ paridarśitam
|
yajjñātvā puruṣo loke prītiṃ saukhyaṃ ca vindati
|
phalalābhaśca tasya syāt sarvabhūtahitaṃ ca tat ||4||

5pṛthivī vāyurākāśamāpo jyotiśca pañcamam
|
mahābhūtāni bhūtānāṃ sarveṣāṃ prabhavāpyayau ||5||

6yataḥ sṛṣṭāni tatraiva tāni yānti punaḥ punaḥ
|
mahābhūtāni bhūtebhyaḥ sāgarasyormayo yathā ||6||

7prasārya ca yathā'ṅgāni kūrmaḥ saṃharate punaḥ
|
tadvadbhūtāni bhūtātmā sṛṣṭāni harate punaḥ ||7||

8mahābhūtāni pañcaiva sarvabhūteṣu bhūtakṛt
|
akarotteṣu vaiṣamyaṃ tattu jīvo na paśyati ||8||

9śabdaḥ śrotraṃ tathā khāni trayamākāśayonijam
|
vāyoḥ sparśastathā ceṣṭā tvakcaiva tritayaṃ smṛtam ||9||

10rūpaṃ cakṣustathā pākastrividhaṃ teja ucyate
|
rasaḥ kledaśca jihvā ca trayo jalaguṇāḥ smṛtāḥ ||10||

11ghreyaṃ ghrāṇaṃ śarīraṃ ca ete bhūmiguṇāstrayaḥ
|
mahābhūtāni pañcaiva ṣaṣṭhaṃ ca mana ucyate ||11||

12indriyāṇi manaścaiva vijñānānyasya bhārata
|
saptamī buddhirityāhuḥ kṣetrajñaḥ punaraṣṭamaḥ ||12||

13cakṣurālocanāyaiva saṃśayaṃ kurute manaḥ
|
buddhiradhyavasānāya kṣetrajñaḥ sākṣivat sthitaḥ ||13||

14urdhvaṃ pādatalābhyāṃ yadarvākcordhvaṃ ca paśyati
|
etena sarvamevedaṃ viddhyabhivyāptamantaram ||14||

15puruṣairindriyāṇīha veditavyāni kṛtsnaśaḥ
|
tamo rajaśca sattvaṃ ca te'pi bhāvāstadāśritāḥ ||15||

16etāṃ buddhvā narā buddhyā bhūtānāmāgatiṃ gatim
|
samavekṣya śanaiścaiva labhate śamamuttamam ||16||

17guṇairnenīyate buddhirbuddherevendriyāṇyapi
|
manaḥṣaṣṭhāni sarvāṇi tadabhāve kuto guṇāḥ ||17||

18iti tanmayamevaitatsarvaṃ sthāvarajaṅgamam
|
pralīyate codbhavati tasmānnirdiśyate tathā ||18||

19yena paśyati taccakṣuḥ śṛṇoti śrotramucyate
|
jighrati ghrāṇamityāhū rasaṃ jānāti jihvayā ||19||

20tvacā sparśayate sparśaṃ buddhirvikriyate sakṛt
|
yena prārthayate kiñcittadā bhavati tanmanaḥ ||20||

21adhiṣṭhānāni buddherhi pṛthagarthāni pañcadhā
|
indriyāṇīti yānyāhustānyadṛśyo'dhitiṣṭhati ||21||

22puruṣe tiṣṭhatī buddhistriṣu bhāveṣu vartate
|
kadācillabhate prītiṃ kadācidanuśocati ||22||

23na sukhena na duḥkhena kadācidapi vartate
|
evaṃ narāṇāṃ manasi triṣu bhāveṣvavasthitā ||23||

24seyaṃ bhāvātmikā bhāvāṃstrīnetānativartate
|
saritāṃ sāgaro bhartā mahāvelāmivormimān ||24||

25atibhāvagatā buddhirbhāve manasi vartate
|
pravartamānaṃ tu rajastadbhāvamanuvartate ||25||

26indriyāṇi hi sarvāṇi pravartayati sā tadā
|
tataḥ sattvaṃ tamobhāvaḥ prītiyogātpravartate ||26||

27prītiḥ sattvaṃ rajaḥ śokastamo mohastu te trayaḥ
|
ye ye ca bhāvā loke'smin sarveṣveteṣu vai triṣu ||27||

28iti buddhigatiḥ sarvā vyākhyātā tava bhārata
|
indriyāṇi ca sarvāṇi vijetavyāni dhīmatā ||28||

29sattvaṃ rajastamaścaiva prāṇināṃ saṃśritāḥ sadā
|
trividhā vedanā caiva sarvasattveṣu dṛśyate ||29||

30sāttvikī rājasī caiva tāmasī ceti bhārata
|
sukhasparśaḥ sattvaguṇo duḥkhasparśo rajoguṇaḥ
|
tamoguṇena saṃyuktau bhavato vyāvahārikau ||30||

31tatra yatprītisaṃyuktaṃ kāye manasi vā bhavet
|
vartate sāttviko bhāva ityācakṣīta tattathā ||31||

32atha yadduḥkhasaṃyuktamaprītikaramātmanaḥ
|
pravṛttaṃ raja ityeva tanna saṃrabhya cintayet ||32||

33atha yanmohasaṃyuktamavyaktaviṣayaṃ bhavet
|
apratarkyamavijñeyaṃ tamastadupadhārayet ||33||

34praharṣaḥ prītirānandaḥ sukhaṃ saṃśāntacittatā
|
kathañcidabhivartanta ityete sāttvikā guṇāḥ ||34||

35atuṣṭiḥ paritāpaśca śoko lobhastathā'kṣamā
|
liṅgāni rajasastāni dṛśyante hetvahetubhiḥ ||35||

36avamānastathā mohaḥ pramādaḥ svapnatandritā
|
kathañcidabhivartante vividhāstāmasā guṇāḥ ||36||

37dūragaṃ bahudhāgāmi prārthanāsaṃśayātmakam
|
manaḥ suniyataṃ yasya sa sukhī pretya ceha ca ||37||

38sattvakṣetrajñayoretadantaraṃ paśya sūkṣmayoḥ
|
sṛjate tu guṇāneka eko na sṛjate guṇān ||38||

39maśakodumbarau vāpi samprayuktau yathā sadā
|
anyonyametau syātāṃ ca samprayogastathā tayoḥ ||39||

40pṛthagbhūtau prakṛtyā tau samprayuktau ca sarvadā
|
yathā matsyau jalaṃ caiva samprayuktau tathaiva tau ||40||

41na guṇā vidurātmānaṃ sa guṇānvetti sarvaśaḥ
|
paridraṣṭā guṇānāṃ tu saṃsṛṣṭānmanyate tathā ||41||

42indriyaistu pradīpārthaṃ kurute buddhisaptamaiḥ
|
nirviceṣṭairajānadbhiḥ paramātmā pradīpavat ||42||

43sṛjate hi guṇān sattvaṃ kṣetrajñaḥ paripaśyati
|
samprayogastayoreṣa sattvakṣetrajñayordhruvaḥ ||43||

44āśrayo nāsti sattvasya kṣetrajñasya ca kaścana
|
sattvaṃ manaḥ saṃsṛjate na guṇānvai kadācana ||44||

45raśmīṃsteṣāṃ sa manasā yadā samyaṅniyacchati
|
tadā prakāśate'syātmā ghaṭe dīpo jvalanniva ||45||

46tyaktvā yaḥ prākṛtaṃ karma nityamātmaratirmuniḥ
|
sarvabhūtātmabhūstasmāt sa gaccheduttamāṃ gatim ||46||

47yathā vāricaraḥ pakṣī salilena na lipyate
|
evameva kṛtaprajño bhūteṣu parivartate ||47||

48evaṃ svabhāvamevaitatsvabuddhyā viharennaraḥ
|
aśocannaprahṛṣyaṃśca samo vigatamatsaraḥ ||48||

49svabhāvayuktyā yuktastu sa nityaṃ sṛjate guṇān
|
urṇanābhiryathā sūtraṃ vijñeyāstantuvadguṇāḥ ||49||

50pradhvastā na nivartante nivṛttirnopalabhyate
|
pratyakṣeṇa parokṣaṃ tadanumānena sidhyati ||50||

51evameke'dhyavasyanti nivṛttiriti cāpare
|
ubhayaṃ sampradhāryaitadvyavasyeta yathāmati ||51||

52itīmaṃ hṛdayagranthiṃ buddhibhedamayaṃ dṛḍham
|
vimucya sukhamāsīta na śocecchinnasaṃśayaḥ ||52||

53malināḥ prāpnuyuḥ siddhiṃ yathā pūrṇāṃ nadīṃ narāḥ
|
avagāhya suvidvāṃso viddhi jñānamidaṃ tathā ||53||

54mahānadyā hi pārajñastapyate na tadanyathā
|
na tu tupyati tattvajñaḥ phale jñāte taratyuta ||54||

55evaṃ ye vidurādhyātmaṃ kevalaṃ jñānamuttamam ||55||

56etāṃ buddhvā naraḥ sarvāṃ bhūtānāmāgatiṃ gatim
|
avekṣya ca śanairbuddhyā labhate śamanaṃ tataḥ ||56||

57trivargo yasya viditaḥ prekṣya yaśca vimuñcati
|
anviṣya manasā yuktastattvadarśī nirutsukaḥ ||57||

58na cātmā śakyate draṣṭumindriyaiśca vibhāgaśaḥ
|
tatra tatra visṛṣṭaiśca durvāryaiścākṛtātmabhiḥ ||58||

59etadbuddhvā bhavedbuddhaḥ kimanyadbuddhalakṣaṇam
|
vijñāya taddhi manyante kṛtakṛtyā manīṣiṇaḥ ||59||

60na bhavati viduṣāṃ tato bhayaṃ yadaviduṣāṃ sumahadbhayaṃ bhavet
|
na hi gatiradhikāsti kasyacit sati hi guṇe pravadantyatulyatām ||60||

61yaḥ karotyanabhisandhipūrvakaṃ tacca nirgudati yatpurākṛtam
|
nāpriyaṃ tadubhayaṃ kutaḥ priyaṃ tasya tajjanayatīha sarvataḥ ||61||

62lokamāturamasūyate janastasya tajjanayatīha sarvataḥ ||62||

63loka āturajanānnirāvaśaṃstattadeva bahu paśya śocataḥ
|
tatra paśya kuśalānaśocato ye vidustadubhayaṃ padaṃ satām ||63||
