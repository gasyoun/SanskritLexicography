Mahābhārata 2.78 (Sabhāparvan)

1yudhiṣṭhira uvāca|
āmantrayāmi bharatāṃstathā vṛddhaṃ pitāmaham
|
rājānaṃ somadattaṃ ca mahārājaṃ ca bāhlikam||1||

2droṇaṃ kṛpaṃ nṛpāṃścānyānaśvatthāmānameva ca
|
viduraṃ dhṛtarāṣṭraṃ ca dhārtarāṣṭrāṃśca sarvaśaḥ||2||

3yuyutsuṃ sañjayaṃ caiva tathaivānyānsabhāsadaḥ
|
sarvānāmantrya gacchāmi draṣṭā'smi punaretya vaḥ||3||

4vaiśampāyana uvāca|
na ca kiñcidathocustaṃ hriyā''sannā yudhiṣṭhiram
|
manobhireva kalyāṇaṃ dadhyuste tasya dhīmataḥ||4||

5vidura uvāca|
āryā pṛthā rājaputrī nāraṇyaṃ gantumarhati
|
sukumārī ca vṛddhā ca nityaṃ caiva sukhocitā||5||

6iha vatsyati kalyāṇī satkṛtā mama veśmani
|
iti pārthā vijānīdhvamagadaṃ vo'stu sarvaśaḥ||6||

7pāṇḍavā ūcuḥ| tathetyuktvā bruvansarve yathā no vadase'nagha
|
tvaṃ pitṛvyaḥ pitṛsamo vayaṃ ca tvatparāyaṇāḥ||7||

8yathā jñāpayase vidvaṃstvaṃ hi naḥ paramo guruḥ
|
yaccānyadapi kartavyaṃ tadvidhatsva mahāmate||8||

9vidura uvāca|
yudhiṣṭhira vijānīhi mamedaṃ bharatarṣabha
|
nādharmeṇa jitaḥ kaścidvyathate vai parājaye||9||

10tvaṃ vai dharmaṃ vijānīṣe yuddhe jetā dhanañjayaḥ
|
hantā'rīṇāṃ bhīmaseno nakulastvarthasaṅgrahī||10||

11saṃyantā sahadevastu dhaumyo brahmaviduttamaḥ
|
dharmārthakuśalā caiva draupadī dharmacāriṇī||11||

12anyonyasya priyāḥ sarve tathaiva priyadarśanāḥ
|
parairabhedyāḥ santuṣṭāḥ ko vo na spṛhayediha||12||

13eṣa vai sarvakalyāṇaḥ samādhistava bhārata
|
nainaṃ śatrurviṣahate śakreṇāpi samo'pyuta||13||

14himavatyanuśiṣṭo'si merusāvarṇinā purā
|
dvaipāyanena kṛṣṇena nagare vāraṇāvate||14||

15bhṛgutuṅge ca rāmeṇa dṛṣṭadvatyāṃ ca śambhunā
|
aśrauṣīrasi tasyāpi maharṣerañjanaṃ prati||15||

16kalmāṣītīrasaṃsthasya gatastvaṃ śiṣyatāṃ bhṛgoḥ
|
draṣṭā sadā nāradaste dhaumyaste'yaṃ purohitaḥ||16||

17mā hāsīḥ sāmparāye tvaṃ buddhiṃ tāmṛṣipūjitām
|
purūravasamailaṃ tvaṃ buddhyā jayasi pāṇḍava||17||

18śaktyā jayasi rājño'nyānṛṣīndharmopasevayā
|
aindre jaye dhṛtamanā yāmye kopavidhāraṇe||18||

19tathā visarge kaubere vāruṇe caiva sayyame
|
ātmapradānaṃ saumyatvamadbhyaścaivopajīvanam||19||

20bhūmeḥ kṣamā ca tejaśca samagraṃ sūryamaṇḍalāt
|
vāyorbalaṃ prāpnuhi tvaṃ bhūtebhyaścātmasampadam||20||

21agadaṃ vo'stu bhadraṃ vo draṣṭā'smi punarāgatān
|
āpaddharmārthakṛcchreṣu sarvakāryeṣu vā punaḥ||21||

22yathāvatpratipadyethāḥ kāle kāle yudhiṣṭhira
|
āpṛṣṭo'sīha kaunteya svasti prāpnuhi bhārata||22||

23kṛtārthaṃ svasmimantaṃ tvāṃ drakṣyāmaḥ punarāgatam
|
na hi vo vṛjinaṃ kiñcidveda kaścitpurā kṛtam||23||

24vaiśampāyana uvāca|
evamuktastathetyuktvā pāṇḍavaḥ satyavikramaḥ
|
bhīṣmadroṇau namaskṛtya prātiṣṭhata yudhiṣṭhiraḥ||24||
