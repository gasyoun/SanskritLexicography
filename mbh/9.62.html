Mahābhārata 9.62 (Śalyaparvan)

1sañjaya uvāca|
tataste prayayuḥ sarve nivāsāya mahīkṣitaḥ
|
śaṅkhān pradhmāpayanto vai hṛṣṭāḥ parighabāhavaḥ||1||

2pāṇḍavān gacchataścāpi śibiraṃ no viśāmpate
|
maheṣvāso'nvagātpaścādyuyutsuḥ sātyakistathā||2||

3dhṛṣṭadyumnaḥ śikhaṇḍī ca draupadeyāśca sarvaśaḥ
|
sarve cānye maheṣvāsāḥ prayayuḥ śibirāṇyuta||3||

4tataste prāviśanpārthā hatatviṭkaṃ hateśvaram
|
duryodhanasya śibiraṃ raṅgavadvisṛte jane||4||

5gatotsavaṃ puramiva hṛtanāgamiva hradam
|
strīvarṣavarabhūyiṣṭhaṃ vṛddhāmātyairadhiṣṭhitam||5||

6tatraitānparyupātiṣṭhan duryodhanapuraḥsarāḥ
|
kṛtāñjalipuṭā rājankāṣāyamalināmbarāḥ||6||

7śibiraṃ samanuprāpya kururājasya pāṇḍavāḥ
|
avaterurmahārāja rathebhyo rathasattamāḥ||7||

8tato gāṇḍīvadhanvānamabhyabhāṣata keśavaḥ
|
sthitaḥ priyahite nityamatīva bharatarṣabha||8||

9avaropaya gāṇḍīvamakṣayau ca maheṣudhī
|
athāhamavarokṣyāmi paścādbharatasattama||9||

10svayaṃ caivāvaroha tvametacchreyastavānagha
|
taccākarottathā vīraḥ pāṇḍuputro dhanañjayaḥ||10||

11atha paścāttataḥ kṛṣṇo raśmīnutsṛjya vājinām
|
avārohata medhāvī rathādgāṇḍīvadhanvanaḥ||11||

12athātavatīrṇe bhūtānāmīśvare sumahātmani
|
kapirantardadhe divyo dhvajo gāṇḍīvadhanvanaḥ||12||

13sa dagdho droṇakarṇābhyāṃ divyairastrairmahārathaḥ
|
athādīpto'gninā hyāśu prajajvāla mahīpate||13||

14sopāsaṅgaḥ saraśmiśca sāśvaḥ sayugabandhuraḥ
|
bhasmībhūto'patadbhūmau ratho gāṇḍīvadhanvanaḥ||14||

15taṃ tathā bhasmabhūtaṃ tu dṛṣṭvā pāṇḍusutāḥ prabho
|
abhavan vismitā rājannarjunaścedamabravīt||15||

16kṛtāñjaliḥ sapraṇayaṃ praṇipatyābhivādya ha
|
govinda kasmādbhagavan ratho dagdho'yamagninā||16||

17kimetanmahadāścaryamabhavadyadunandana
|
tanme brūhi mahābāho śrotavyaṃ yadi manyase||17||

18vāsudeva uvāca|
astrairbahuvidhairdagdhaḥ pūrvamevāyamarjuna
|
madadhiṣṭhittatvātsamare na viśīrṇaḥ parantapa||18||

19idānīṃ tu viśīrṇo'yaṃ dagdho brahmāstratejā
|
mayā vimuktaḥ kaunteya tvayyadya kṛtakarmaṇi||19||

20īṣadutsmayamāstu bhagavānkeśavo'rihā
|
pariṣvajya ca rājānaṃ yudhiṣṭhiramabhāṣata||20||

21diṣṭyā jayasi kaunteya diṣṭyā te śatravo jitāḥ
|
diṣṭyā gāṇḍīvadhanvā ca bhīmasenaśca pāṇḍavaḥ||21||

22tvaṃ cāpi kuśalī rājanmādrīputro ca pāṇḍavau
|
muktā vīrakṣayādasmātsaṅgrāmānnihatadviṣaḥ||22||

23kṣipramuttarakālāni kuru kāryāṇi bhārata
|
upāyatamupaplavyaṃ saha gāṇḍīvadhanvanā||23||

24ānīya madhuparkaṃ māṃ yatpurā tvamavocathāḥ
|
eṣa bhrātā sakhā caiva tava kṛṣṇa dhanañjayaḥ||24||

25rakṣitavyo mahābāho sarvāsvāpatsviti prabho
|
tava caiva bruvāṇasya tathetyevāhamabruvam||25||

26sa savyasācī guptaste vijayī ca janeśvara
|
bhrātṛbhiḥ saha rājendra śūraḥ satyaparākramaḥ||26||

27mukto vīrakṣayādasmātsaṅgrāmāllomaharṣaṇāt
|
evamuktastu kṛṣṇena dharmarājo yudhiṣṭhiraḥ||27||

28hṛṣṭaromā mahārāja pratyuvāca janārdanam
|
yudhiṣṭhira uvāca|
pramuktaṃ droṇakarṇābhyāṃ brahmāstramarimardana||28||

29kastvadanyaḥ sahetsākṣādapi vajrī purandaraḥ
|
bhavatastu prasādena saṃśaptakagaṇā jitāḥ||29||

30mahāraṇagataḥ pārtho yacca nāsītparāṅmukhaḥ
|
tathaiva ca mahābāho paryāyairbahubhirmayā||30||

31karmaṇāmanusantānaṃ tejasaśca gatīḥ śubhāḥ
|
upaplavye mahaṣirme kṛṣṇadvaipāyano'bravīt||31||

32yato dharmastataḥ kṛṣṇo yataḥ kṛṣṇastato jayaḥ
|
ityevamukte te vīrāḥ śibiraṃ tava bhārata||32||

33praviśya pratyapadyanta kośaratnardhisañcayān
|
rajataṃ jātarūpaṃ ca maṇīnatha ca mauktikān||33||

34bhūṣaṇānyatha mukhyāni kambalānyajināni ca
|
dāsīdāsamasaṅkhyeyaṃ rājyopakaraṇāni ca||34||

35te prāpya dhanamakṣayyaṃ tvadīyaṃ bharatarṣabha
|
udakrośanmahābhāgā narendra vijitārayaḥ||35||

36te tu vīrāḥ samāśvasya vāhanānyavamucya ca
|
atiṣṭhanta muhuḥ sarve pāṇḍavāḥ sātyakistathā||36||

37athābravīnmahārāja vāsudevo mahāyaśāḥ
|
asmābhirmaṅgalārthāya vastavyaṃ śibirādbahiḥ||37||

38tathetyuktvā hi te sarve pāṇḍavāḥ sātyakistathā
|
vāsudevena sahitā maṅgalārthaṃ bahiryayuḥ||38||

39te samāsādya saritaṃ puṇyāmoghavatīṃ nṛpa
|
nyavasannatha tāṃ rātriṃ pāṇḍavā hataśatravaḥ||39||

40yudhiṣṭhirastato rājā prāptakālamacintayat
|
tatra te gamanaṃ prāptaṃ rocate tava mādhava||40||

41gāndhāryāḥ krodhadīptāyāḥ praśamārthamarindama
|
hetukāraṇayuktaiśca vākyaiḥ kālasamīritaiḥ||41||

42kṣiprameva mahābhāga gāndhārīṃ praśamiṣyasi
|
pitāmahaśca bhagavān vyāsastatra bhaviṣyati||42||

43tataḥ sampreṣayāmāsuryādavaṃ nāgasāhvayam
|
sa ca prāyājjavenāśu vāsudevaḥ pratāpavān||43||

44dārukaṃ rathamāropya yena rājāmbikāsutaḥ
|
tamūcuḥ samprayāsyantaṃ śaibyasugrīvavāhanam||44||

45pratyāśvāsaya gāndhārīṃ hataputrāṃ yaśasvinīm
|
sa prāyātpāṇḍavairuktastatpuraṃ sātvatāṃ varaḥ
|
āsasāda tataḥ kṣipraṃ gāndhārīṃ nihatātmajām||45||
