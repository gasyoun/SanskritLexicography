Mahābhārata 14.49 (Āśvamedhikaparvan)

1ṛṣaya ūcuḥ|
ko vā svidiha dharmāṇāmanuṣṭheyatamo mataḥ
|
vyāhatāmiva paśyāmo dharmasya vividhāṃ gatim||1||

2ūrdhvaṃ dehādvadantyeke naitadastīti cāpare
|
kecitsaṃśayitaṃ sarvaṃ niḥsaṃśayamathāpare||2||

3anityaṃ nityamityeke nāstyastītyapi cāpare
|
ekarūpaṃ dvidhetyeke vyāmiśramiti cāpare||3||

4manyante brāhmaṇā eva brahmajñāstattvadarśinaḥ
|
ekameke pṛthakcānye bahutvamiti cāpare||4||

5deśakālāvubhau kecinnaitadastīti cāpare
|
jaṭājinadharāścānye muṇḍāḥ kecidasaṃvṛtāḥ||5||

6asnānaṃ kecidicchanti snānamapyapare janāḥ
|
manyante brāhmaṇā devā brahmajñāstattvadarśinaḥ||6||

7āhāraṃ kecidicchanti keciccānaśane ratāḥ
|
karma kecitpraśaṃsanti praśāntiṃ cāpare janāḥ||7||

8kecinmokṣaṃ praśaṃsanti kecidbhogānpṛthagvidhān
|
dhanāni kecidicchanti nirdhanatvamathāpare
|
upāsya sādhanaṃ tveke naitadastīti cāpare||8||

9ahiṃsāniratāścānye keciddhiṃsāparāyaṇāḥ
|
puṇyena yaśasā cānye naitadastīti cāpare||9||

10sadbhāvaniratāścānye kecit saṃśayite sthitāḥ
|
duḥkhādanye sukhādanye dhyānamityapare janāḥ||10||

11yajñamityapare viprāḥ pradānamiti cāpare
|
tapastvanye praśaṃsanti svādhyāyamapare janāḥ||11||

12jñānaṃ saṃnyāsamityeke svabhāvaṃ bhūtacintakāḥ
|
sarvameke praśaṃsanti na sarvamiti cāpare||12||

13evaṃ vyutthāpite dharme bahudhā viprabodhite
|
niścayaṃ nādhigacchāmaḥ sammūḍhāḥ surasattama||13||

14idaṃ śreya idaṃ śreya ityevaṃ vyusthito janaḥ
|
yo hi yasmin rato dharme sa taṃ pūjayate sadā||14||

15tena no'vihitā prajñā manaśca bahulīkṛtam
|
etadākhyātamicchāmaḥ śreyaḥ kimiti sattama||15||

16ataḥ paraṃ tu yadguhyaṃ tadbhavānvaktumarhati
|
sattvakṣetrajñayoścāpi sambandhaḥ kena hetunā||16||

17evamuktaḥ sa tairviprairbhagavā~llokabhāvanaḥ
|
tebhyaḥ śaśaṃsa dharmātmā yāthātathyena buddhimān||17||
