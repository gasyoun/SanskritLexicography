Mahābhārata 12.363 (Śāntiparvan)

1sūrya uvāca|
naiṣa devo'nilasakho nāsuro na ca pannagaḥ
|
uñcavṛttivrate siddho munireva divaṃ gataḥ||1||

2eṣa mūlaphalāhāraḥ śīrṇaparṇāśanastathā
|
abbhakṣo vāyubhakṣaśca āsīdvipraḥ samāhitaḥ||2||

3bhavaścānena vipreṇa saṃhitābhirabhiṣṭutaḥ
|
svargadvāre kṛtodyogo yenāsau tridivaṃ gataḥ||3||

4asaṃgatiranākāṅkṣī nityamucchaśilāśanaḥ
|
sarvabhūtahite yukta eṣa vipro bhujaṅgamāḥ||4||

5na hi devā na gandharvā nāsurā na ca pannagāḥ
|
prabhavantīha bhūtānāṃ prāptānāmuttamāṃ gatim||5||

6etadevaṃvidhaṃ dṛṣṭamāścaryaṃ tatra me dvija
|
saṃsiddho mānuṣa: kāmaṃ yo'sau siddhagatiṃ gataḥ
|
sūryeṇa sahito brahmanpṛthivīṃ parivartate||6||
