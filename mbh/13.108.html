Mahābhārata 13.108 (Anuśāsanaparvan)

1yudhiṣṭhira uvāca|
yadvaraṃ sarvatīrthānāṃ tanme brūhi pitāmaha
|
yatra caiva paraṃ śaucaṃ tanme vyākhyātumarhasi ||1||

2bhīṣma uvāca|
sarvāṇi khalu tīrthāni guṇavanti manīṣiṇaḥ
|
yattu tīrthaṃ ca śaucaṃ ca tanme śṛṇu samāhitaḥ ||2||

3agādhe vimale śuddhe satyatoye dhṛtihrade
|
snātavyaṃ mānase tīrthe sattvamālaṃvya śāśvatam ||3||

4tīrthaśaucamanarthitvamārjavaṃ satyamārdavam
|
ahiṃsā sarvabhūtānāmānṛśaṃsyaṃ damaḥ śamaḥ ||4||

5nirmamā nirahaṃkārā nirdvandvā niṣparigrahāḥ
|
śucayastīrthabhūtāste ye bhaikṣyamupabhuñjate ||5||

6tattvavittvanahaṃbuddhistīrthapravaramucyate
|
śaucalakṣaṇametatte sarvatraivānvavekṣataḥ ||6||

7rajastamaḥ satvamatho yeṣāṃ nirdhotamātmanaḥ
|
śaucāśaucasamāyuktāḥ svakāryaparimārgiṇaḥ ||7||

8sarvatyāgeṣvabhiratāḥ sarvajñāḥ samadarśinaḥ
|
śaucena vṛttaśaucārthāste tīrthāḥ śucayaśca ye ||8||

9nodakaklinnagātrastu snāta ityabhidhīyate
|
sa snāto yo damasnātaḥ sa nāhyābhyantaraḥ śuciḥ ||9||

10atīteṣvanapekṣā ye prāpteṣvartheṣu nirmamāḥ
|
śaucameva paraṃ teṣāṃ yeṣāṃ notpadyate spṛhā ||10||

11prajñānaṃ śaucameveha śarīrasya viśeṣataḥ
|
tathā niṣkiñcanatvaṃ ca manasaśca prasannatā ||11||

12vṛttaśaucaṃ manaḥśaucaṃ tīrthaśaucamataḥ param
|
jñānotpannaṃ ca yacchaucaṃ tacchaucaṃ paramaṃ smṛtam ||12||

13manasā ca pradīptena brahmajñānajalena ca
|
snāti yo mānase tīrthe tatsnānaṃ tattvadarśinaḥ ||13||

14samāropitaśaucastu nityaṃ bhāvasamāhitaḥ
|
kevalaṃ guṇasaṃpannaḥ śucireva naraḥ sadā ||14||

15śarīrasthāni tīrthāni proktānyetāni bhārata
|
pṛthivyāṃ yāni tīrthāni puṇyāni śṛṇu tānyapi ||15||

16śarīrasya yathoddeśāḥ śucayaḥ parikīrtitāḥ
|
tathā pṛthivyā bhāgāśca puṇyāni salilāni ca ||16||

17kīrtanāccaiva tīrthasya snānācca pitṛtarpaṇāt
|
dhunanti pāpaṃ tīrtheṣu te prayānti sukhaṃ divam ||17||

18parigrahācca sādhūnāṃ pṛthivyāścaiva tejasā
|
atīva puṇyabhāgāste salilasya ca tejasā ||18||

19manasaśca pṛthivyāśca puṇyāstīrthāstathāpare
|
ubhayoreva yaḥ snāyātsa siddhiṃ śīghramāpnuyāt ||19||

20yathā balaṃ kriyāhīnaṃ kriyā vā balavarjitā
|
neha sādhayate kāryaṃ samāyuktā tu sidhyati ||20||

21evaṃ śarīraśaucena tīrthaśaucena cānvitaḥ
|
śuciḥ siddhimavāpnoti dvividhaṃ śaucamuttamam ||21||
