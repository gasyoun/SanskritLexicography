Mahābhārata 13.83 (Anuśāsanaparvan)

1bhīṣma uvāca|
ye ca gāṃ saṃprayacchanti hutaśiṣṭāśinaśca ye
|
teṣāṃ satrāṇi yajñāśca nityameva yudhiṣṭhira ||1||

2ṛte dadhighṛteneha na yajñaḥ saṃpravartate
|
tena yajñasya yajñatvamato mūlaṃ ca kathyate ||2||

3dānānāmapi sarveṣāṃ gavāṃ dānaṃ praśasyate
|
gāvaḥ śreṣṭhāḥ pavitrāśca pāvanaṃ hyetaduttamam ||3||

4puṣṭyarthametāḥ seveta śāntyarthamapi caiva ha
|
payodadhighṛtaṃ cāsāṃ sarvapāpapramocanam ||4||

5gāvastejaḥ paraṃ proktamiha loke paratra ca
|
na gobhyaḥ paramaṃ kiñcitpavitraṃ bharatarṣabha ||5||

6atrāpyudāharantīmamitihāsaṃ purātanam
|
pitāmahasya saṃvāda‌mindrasya ca yudhiṣṭhira ||6||

7parābhūteṣu daityeṣu śakrastribhuvaneśvaraḥ
|
prajāḥ samuditāḥ sarvāḥ satyadharmaparāyaṇāḥ ||7||

8atharṣayaḥ sagandharvāḥ kinnaroragarākṣasāḥ
|
devāsurasuparṇāśca prajānāṃ patayastathā ||8||

9paryupāsanta kaunteya kadācidvai pitāmaham
|
nāradaḥ parvataścaiva viśvāvasurhahāhuhūḥ||9||

10divyatāneṣu gāyantaḥ paryupāsanta taṃ prabhum
|
tatra divyāni puṣpāṇi prāvahatpavanastadā ||10||

11ājarhrurṛtavaścāpi sugandhīni pṛthak pṛthak
|
tasmindevasamāvāye sarvabhūtasamāgame ||11||

12divyavāditrasaṃghuṣṭe divyastrīcāraṇāvṛte
|
indraḥ papraccha deveśamabhivādya praṇamya ca ||12||

13devānāṃ bhagavankasmāllokeśānāṃ pitāmaha
|
upariṣṭādgavāṃ loka etadicchāmi veditum ||13||

14kiṃ tapo brahmacaryaṃ vā gobhiḥ kṛtamaheśvara
|
devānāmupariṣṭādyadvasantyarajasaḥ sukham ||14||

15tataḥ provāca brahmā taṃ śakraṃ balaniṣūdanam
|
avajñātāstvayā nityaṃ gāvo balaniṣūdana ||15||

16tena tvamāsāṃ māhātmyaṃ na vetsi śṛṇu yatprabho
|
gavāṃ prabhāvaṃ paramaṃ māhātmyaṃ ca surarṣabha ||16||

17yajñāṅgaṃ kathitā gāvo yajña eva ca vāsava
|
etābhiśca vinā yajño na varteta kathañcana ||17||

18dhārayanti prajāścaiva payasā haviṣā tathā
|
etāsāṃ tanayāścāpi kṛṣiyogamupāsate ||18||

19janayanti ca dhānyāni bījāni vividhāni ca
|
tato yajñāḥ pravartante havyaṃ kavyaṃ ca sarvaśaḥ ||19||

20payoda‌dhighṛtaṃ caiva puṇyāścaitāḥ surādhipa
|
vahanti vividhānbhārānkṣuttṛṣṇāparipīḍitāḥ||20||

21munīṃśca dhārayantīha prajāścaivāpi karmaṇā
|
vāsavākūṭavāhinyaḥ karmaṇā sukṛtena ca ||21||

22upariṣṭāttato'smākaṃ vasantyetāḥ sadaiva hi
|
evaṃ te kāraṇaṃ śakra nivāsakṛtamadya vai ||22||

23gavāṃ devopariṣṭāddhi samākhyātaṃ śatakrato
|
etā hi varadattāśca varadāścāpi vāsava ||23||

24surabhyaḥ puṇyakarmiṇyaḥ pāvanāḥ śubhalakṣaṇāḥ
|
yadarthaṃ gāṃ gatāścaiva surabhyaḥ surasattama ||24||

25tacca me śṛṇu kārtsnyena vadato balasūdana
|
purā devayuge tāta devendreṣu mahātmasu ||25||

26trīঁঁllokānanuśāsatsu viṣṇau garbhatvamāgate
|
adityāstapyamānāyāstapo ghoraṃ suduścaram ||26||

27putrārthamamaraśreṣṭha pādenaikena nityadā
|
tāṃ tu dṛṣṭvā mahādevīṃ tapyamānāṃ mahattapaḥ ||27||

28dakṣasya duhitā devī surabhī nāma nāmataḥ
|
atapyata tapo ghoraṃ hṛṣṭā dharmaparāyaṇā ||28||

29kailāsaśikhare ramye devagandharvasevite
|
vyatiṣṭhadekapādena paramaṃ yogamāsthitā ||29||

30daśavarṣasahasrāṇi daśavarṣaśatāni ca
|
santaptāstapasā tasyā devāḥ sarṣimahoragāḥ ||30||

31tatra gatvā mayā sārdhaṃ paryupāsanta tāṃ śubhām
|
athāhamabruvaṃ tatra devīṃ tāṃ tapasānvitām ||31||

32kimarthaṃ tapyase devi tapo ghoramanindite
|
prītaste'haṃ mahābhāge tapasā'nena śobhane ||32||

33varayasva varaṃ devi dātāsmīti purandara ||33||

34surabhyuvāca|
vareṇa bhagavanmahyaṃ kṛtaṃ lokapitāmaha
|
eṣa eva varo me'dya yatprīto'si mamānagha ||34||

35brahmovāca|
tāmevaṃ bruvatīṃ devīṃ surabhi trida‌śeśvara
|
pratyabruvaṃ yaddevendra tannibodha śacīpate ||35||

36alobhakāmyayā devi tapasā ca śubhānane
|
prasanno'haṃ varaṃ tasmādamaratvaṃ dadāmi te ||36||

37trayāṇāmapi lokānāmupariṣṭānnivatsyasi
|
matprasādācca vikhyāto golokaḥ saṃbhaviṣyati ||37||

38mānuṣeṣu ca kurvāṇāḥ prajāḥ karma śubhāstava
|
nivatsyanti mahābhāge sarvā duhitaraśca te ||38||

39manasā cintitā bhogāstvayā vai divyamānuṣāḥ
|
yacca sarve sukhaṃ devi tatte sampatsyate śubhe ||39||

40tasyā lokāḥ sahasrākṣa sarvakāmasamanvitāḥ
|
na tatra kramate mṛtyurna jarā na ca pāvakaḥ ||40||

41na daivaṃ nāśubhaṃ kiñcidvidyate tatra vāsava
|
tatra divyānyaraṇyāni divyāni bhavanāni ca ||41||

42vimānāni suyuktāni kāmagāni ca vāsava
|
brahmacaryeṇa tapasā yatnena ca damena ca ||42||

43dānaiśca vividhaiḥ puṇyaistathā tīrthānusevanāt
|
tapasā mahatā caiva sukṛtena ca karmaṇā ||43||

44śakyaḥ samāsādayituṃ golokaḥ puṣkarekṣaṇa
|
etatte sarvamākhyātaṃ mayā śakrānupṛcchate ||44||

45na te paribhavaḥ kāryo gavāmasurasūdana ||45||

46bhīṣma uvāca|
etacchrutvā sahasrākṣaḥ pūjayāmāsa nityadā
|
gāścakre vahumānaṃ ca tāsu nityaṃ yudhiṣṭhira ||46||

47etatte sarvamākhyātaṃ pāvanaṃ ca mahādyute
|
pavitraṃ paramaṃ cāpi gavāṃ māhātmyamuttamam ||47||

48kīrtitaṃ puruṣavyāghra sarvapāpavimocanam
|
ya idaṃ kathayennityaṃ brāhmaṇebhyaḥ samāhitaḥ ||48||

49havyakavyeṣu yajñeṣu pitṛkāryeṣu caiva ha
|
sārvakāmikamakṣayyaṃ pitṝṃstasyopatiṣṭhate ||49||

50goṣu bhaktaśca labhate yadyadicchati mānavaḥ
|
striyo'pi bhaktā yā goṣu tāśca kāmamavāpnuyuḥ ||50||

51putrārthī labhate putraṃ kanyārthī tāmavāpnuyāt
|
dhanārthī labhate vittaṃ dharmārthī dharmamāpnuyāt ||51||

52vidyārthī cāpnuyādvidyāṃ sukhārthī prāpnuyātsukham
|
na kiñciddurlabhaṃ caiva gavāṃ bhaktasya bhārata ||52||
