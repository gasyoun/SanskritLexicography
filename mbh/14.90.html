Mahābhārata 14.90 (Āśvamedhikaparvan)

1janamejaya uvāca|
pitāmahasya me yajñe dharmarājasya dhīmataḥ
|
yadāścaryamabhūtkiñcittadbhavān vaktumarhati||1||

2vaiśampāyana uvāca|
śrūyatāṃ rājaśārdūla mahadāścaryamuttamam
|
aśvamedhe mahāyajñe nivṛtte yadabhūtprabho||2||

3tarpiteṣu dvijāgryeṣu jñātisambandhibandhuṣu
|
dīnāndhakṛpaṇe vāpi tadā bharatasattama||3||

4ghuṣyamāṇe mahādāne dikṣu sarvāsu bhārata
|
patatsu puṣpavarṣeṣu dharmarājasya mūrdhani||4||

5nīlākṣastatra nakulo rukmapārśvastadānagha
|
vajrāśanisamaṃ nādamamuñcadvasudhādhipa||5||

6sakṛdutsṛjya tannādaṃ trāsayāno mṛgadvijān
|
mānuṣaṃ vacanaṃ prāha dhṛṣṭo vilaśayo mahān||6||

7saktuprasthena vo nāyaṃ yajñastulyo narādhipāḥ
|
ucchavṛttervadānyasya kurukṣetranivāsinaḥ||7||

8tasya tadvacanaṃ śrutvā nakulasya viśāmpate
|
vismayaṃ paramaṃ jagmuḥ sarve te brāhmaṇarṣabhāḥ||8||

9tataḥ sametya nakulaṃ paryapṛcchanta te dvijāḥ
|
kutastvaṃ samanuprāpto yajñaṃ sādhusamāgamam||9||

10kiṃ balaṃ paramaṃ tubhyaṃ kiṃ śrutaṃ kiṃ parāyaṇam
|
kathaṃ bhavantaṃ vidyāma yo no yajñaṃ vigarhase||10||

11avilupyāgamaṃ kṛtsnaṃ vividhairyajñiyaiḥ kṛtam
|
yathāgamaṃ yathānyāyaṃ kartavyaṃ ca tathā kṛtam||11||

12pūjārhāḥ pūjitāścātra vidhivacchāstradarśanāt
|
mantrāhutihutaścāgnirdattaṃ deyamamatsaram||12||

13tuṣṭā dvijātayaścātra dānairbahuvidhairapi
|
kṣatriyāśca suyuddhena śrāddhaiścāpi pitāmahāḥ||13||

14pālanena viśastuṣṭāḥ kāmaistuṣṭā varastriyaḥ
|
anukrośaistathā śūdrā dānaśeṣaiḥ pṛthagjanāḥ||14||

15jñātisambandhinastuṣṭāḥ śaucena ca nṛpasya naḥ
|
devā havirbhiḥ puṇyaiśca rakṣaṇaiḥ śaraṇāgatāḥ||15||

16yadatra tathyaṃ tadbrūhi satyaṃ satyaṃ dvijātiṣu
|
yathāśrutaṃ yathādṛṣṭaṃ pṛṣṭo brāhmaṇakāmyayā||16||

17śraddheyavākyaḥ prājñastvaṃ divyaṃ rūpaṃ bibharṣi ca
|
samāgataśca vipraistvaṃ tadbhavānvaktumarhati||17||

18iti pṛṣṭo dvijaistaiḥ sa prahasannakulo'bravīt
|
naiṣā mṛṣā mayā vāṇī proktā darpeṇa vā dvijāḥ||18||

19yanmayoktamidaṃ vākyaṃ yuṣmābhiścāpyupaśrutam
|
saktuprasthena vo nāyaṃ yajñastulyo dvijarṣabhāḥ||19||

20ityavaśyaṃ mayaitadvo vaktavyaṃ dvijasattamāḥ
|
śṛṇutāvyagramanasaḥ śaṃsato me yathātatham||20||

21anubhūtaṃ ca dṛṣṭaṃ ca yanmayādutamuttamam
|
ucchavṛttervadānyasya kurukṣetranivāsinaḥ||21||

22svargaṃ yena dvijāḥ prāptaḥ sabhāryaḥ sasutasnuṣaḥ
|
yathā cārdhaṃ śarīrasya mamedaṃ kāñcanīkṛtam||22||

23nakula uvāca|
hanta vo vartayiṣyāmi dānasya phalamuttamam
|
nyāyalabdhasya sūkṣmasya vipradattasya yaddvijāḥ||23||

24dharmakṣetre kurukṣetre dharmajñairbahubhirvṛte
|
ucchavṛttirdvijaḥ kaścitkāpotirabhavattadā||24||

25sabhāryaḥ saha putreṇa sasnuṣastapasi sthitaḥ
|
babhūva śulvavṛttaḥ sa dharmātmā niyatendriyaḥ||25||

26ṣaṣṭhe kāle sadā vipro bhuṅkte taiḥ saha suvrataḥ
|
ṣaṣṭhe kāle kadācittu tasyāhāro na vidyate||26||

27bhuṅkte'nyasminkadācitsa ṣaṣṭhe kāle dvijottamaḥ
|
kadāciddharmiṇastasya durbhikṣe sati dāruṇe||27||

28nāvidyata tadā viprāḥ sañcayastannibodhata
|
kṣīṇauṣadhisamāveśe dravyahīno'bhavat tadā||28||

29kāle kāle'sya samprāpte naiva vidyeta bhojanam
|
kṣudhāparigatāḥ sarve prātiṣṭhanta tadā tu te||29||

30ucchaṃ tadā śuklapakṣe madhyaṃ tapati bhāskare
|
uṣṇārtaśca kṣudhārtaśca viprastapasi saṃsthitaḥ||30||

31ucchamaprāptavāneva brāhmaṇaḥ kṣucchramānvitaḥ
|
sa tathaiva kṣudhāviṣṭaḥ sārdhaṃ parijanena ha||31||

32kṣapayāmāsa taṃ kālaṃ kṛcchraprāṇo dvijottamaḥ
|
atha ṣaṣṭhe gate kāle yavaprasthamupārjayan||32||

33yavaprasthaṃ tu taṃ saktūnakurvanta tapasvinaḥ
|
kṛtajapyāhnikāste tu hutvā cāgniṃ yathāvidhi||33||

34kuḍavaṃ kuḍavaṃ sarve vyabhajanta tapasvinaḥ
|
athāgacchaddvijaḥ kaścidatithirbhuñjatāṃ tadā||34||

35te taṃ dṛṣṭvātithiṃ prāptaṃ prahṛṣṭamanaso'bhavan
|
te'bhivādya sukhapraśnaṃ pṛṣṭvā tamatithiṃ tadā||35||

36viśuddhamanaso dāntāḥ śraddhādamasamanvitāḥ
|
anasūyavo vikrodhāḥ sādhavo vītamatsarāḥ||36||

37tyaktamānamadakrodhā dharmajñā dvijasattamāḥ
|
sa brahmacaryaṃ gotraṃ te tasya khyātvā parasparam||37||

38kuṭīṃ praveśayāmāsuḥ kṣudhārtamatithiṃ tadā
|
idamarghyaṃ ca pādyaṃ ca vṛsī ceyaṃ tavānagha||38||

39śucayaḥ saktavaśceme niyamopārjitāḥ prabho
|
pratigṛhṇīṣva bhadraṃ te mayā dattā dvijarṣabha||39||

40ityuktaḥ pratigṛhyātha saktūnāṃ kuḍavaṃ dvijaḥ
|
bhakṣayāmāsa rājendra na ca tuṣṭiṃ jagāma saḥ||40||

41sa ucchavṛttistaṃ prekṣya kṣudhāparigataṃ dvijam
|
āhāraṃ cintayāmāsa kathaṃ tuṣṭo bhavediti||41||

42tasya bhāryābravīdvākyaṃ madbhāgo dīyatāmiti
|
gacchatveṣa yathākāmaṃ parituṣṭo dvijottamaḥ||42||

43iti bruvantīṃ tāṃ sādhvīṃ bhāryāṃ sa dvijasattamaḥ
|
kṣudhāparigatāṃ jñātvā tānsaktūnnābhyanandata||43||

44ātmānumāyo vidvānsa tu viprarṣabhastadā
|
jānan vṛddhāṃ kṣudhārtāṃ ca śrāntāṃ glānāṃ tapasvinīm||44||

45tvagasthibhūtāṃ vepantīṃ tato bhāryāmuvāca ha
|
api kīṭapataṅgānāṃ mṛgāṇāṃ caiva śobhane||45||

46striyo rakṣyāśca poṣayāśca na tvevaṃ vaktumarhasi
|
anukampyo naraḥ patnyā puṣṭo rakṣita eva ca||46||

47prapatedyaśase dīptātsa ca lokānna cāpnuyāt
|
dharmakāmārthakāryāṇi śuśrūṣā kulasantatiḥ||47||

48dāreṣvadhīno dharmaśca pitṝṇāmātmanastathā
|
na vetti karmato bhāryārakṣaṇe yo'kṣamaḥ pumān||48||

49ayaśo mahadāpnoti narakāṃścaiva gacchati
|
ityuktā sā tataḥ prāha dharmāthau nau samau dvija||49||

50saktuprasthacaturbhāgaṃ gṛhāṇemaṃ prasīda me
|
satyaṃ ratiśca dharmaśca svargaśca guṇanirjitaḥ||50||

51strīṇāṃ patisamādhīnaṃ kāṃkṣitaṃ ca dvijarṣabha
|
ṛturmātuḥ piturbījaṃ daivataṃ paramaṃ patiḥ||51||

52bhartuḥ prasādānnārīṇāṃ ratiputraphalaṃ tathā
|
pālanāddhi patistvaṃ me bhartāsi bharaṇācca me||52||

53putrapradānādvaradastasmātsaktūnprayaccha me
|
jarāparigato vṛddhaḥ kṣudhārto durbalo bhṛśam||53||

54upavāsapariśrānto yadā tvamapi karśitaḥ
|
ityuktaḥ sa tayā saktūnpragṛhyedaṃ vaco'bravīt||54||

55dvija saktūnimānbhūyaḥ pratigṛhṇīṣva sattama
|
sa tānpragṛhya bhuktvā ca na tuṣṭimagamaddvijaḥ
|
tamucchavṛttirālakṣya tataścintāparo'bhavat||55||

56putra uvāca|
saktūnimānpragṛhya tvaṃ dehi viprāya sattama
|
ityeva sukṛtaṃ manye tasmādetatkaromyaham||56||

57bhavānhi paripālyo me sarvadaiva prayatnataḥ
|
sādhūnāṃ kāṃkṣitaṃ yasmātpiturvṛddhasya pālanam||57||

58putrārtho vihito hyeṣa vārdhake paripālanam
|
śrutireṣā hi viprarṣe triṣu lokeṣu śāśvatī||58||

59prāṇadhāraṇamātreṇa śakyaṃ kartuṃ tapastvayā
|
prāṇo hi paramo dharmaḥ sthito deheṣu dehinām||59||

60pitovāca|
api varṣasahasrī tvaṃ bāla eva mato mama
|
utpādya putraṃ hi pitā kṛtakṛtyo bhavetsutāt||60||

61bālānāṃ kṣudbalavatī jānāmyetadahaṃ prabho
|
vṛddho'haṃ dhārayiṣyāmi tvaṃ balī bhava putrakā||61||

62jīrṇena vayasā putra na māṃ kṣudbādhate'pi ca
|
dīrghakālaṃ tapastaptaṃ na me maraṇato bhayam||62||

63putra uvāca|
apatyamasmi te puṃsastrāṇātputra iti smṛtaḥ
|
ātmā putraḥ smṛtastasmātbāhyātmānamihātmanā||63||

64pitovāca|
rūpeṇa sadṛśastvaṃ me śīlena ca damena ca
|
parīkṣitaśca bahudhā saktūnādahyi te suta||64||

65ityuktvā''dāya tānsaktūn prītātmā dvijasattamaḥ
|
prahasanniva viprāya sa tasmai pradadau tadā||65||

66bhuktvā tānapi saktūtsa naiva tuṣṭo babhūva ha
|
ucchavṛttistu dharmātmā vrīḍāmanujagāma ha||66||

67taṃ vai vadhūḥ sthitā sādhvī brāhmaṇapriyakāmyayā
|
saktūnādāya saṃhṛṣṭā śvaśuraṃ vākyamabravīt||67||

68santānāttava santānaṃ mama vipra bhaviṣyati
|
saktūnimānatithaye gṛhītvā samprayaccha me||68||

69tava prasādānnirvṛttā mama lokāḥ kilākṣayāḥ
|
putreṇa tānavāpnoti yatra gatvā na śocati||69||

70dharmādyā hi yathā tretā vahnitretā tathaiva ca
|
tathaiva putrapautrāṇāṃ svargastretā kilākṣayaḥ||70||

71pitṝn ṛṇāttārayati putra ityanuśuśruma
|
putrapautreśca niyataṃ sādhulokānupāśnute||71||

72śvaśura uvāca|
vātātapaviśīrṇāṅgī tvāṃ vivarṇāṃ nirīkṣya vai
|
karṣitāṃ suvratācāre kṣudhāvihvalacetasam||72||

73kathaṃ saktūn grahaṣyāmi bhūtvā dharmopaghātakaḥ
|
kalyāṇavṛtte kalyāṇi naivaṃ tvaṃ vaktumarhasi||73||

74ṣaṣṭhe kāle vratavatī śaucaśīlatapo'nvitām
|
kṛcchravṛttiṃ nirāhārāṃ drakṣyāmi tvāṃ kathaṃ śubhe||74||

75bālā kṣudhārtā nārī ca rakṣyā tvaṃ satataṃ mayā
|
upavāsapariśrāntā tvaṃ hi bāndhavanandinī||75||

76snuṣovāca|
gurormama gurustvaṃ vai yato daivatadaivatam
|
devātidevastasmāttvaṃ saktūnādatsva me prabho||76||

77dehaḥ prāṇaśca dharmaśca śuśrūṣārthamidaṃ guroḥ
|
tava vipra prasādena lokānprāpsyāmahe śubhān||77||

78avekṣyā iti kṛtvāhaṃ dṛḍhabhakteti vā dvija
|
cintyā mameyamiti vā saktūnādātumarhasi||78||

79śvaśura uvāca|
anena nityaṃ sādhvī tvaṃ śīlavṛttena śobhase
|
yā tvaṃ dharmavratopetā guruvṛttimavekṣase||79||

80tasmātsaktūn grahīṣyāmi vadhu nārhasi vañcanām
|
gaṇayitvā mahābhāge tvāṃ hi dharmabhṛtāṃ vare||80||

81ityuktvā tānupādāya saktūnprādāddvijātaye
|
tatastuṣṭo'bhavadviprastasya sādhormahātmanaḥ||81||

82prītātmā sa tu taṃ vākyamidamāha dvijarṣabham
|
vāgmī tadā dvijaśreṣṭho dharmaḥ puruṣavigrahaḥ||82||

83śuddhena tava dānena nyāyopāttena dharmataḥ
|
yathāśakti visṛṣṭena prīto'smi dvijasattama
|
aho dānaṃ ghuṣyate te svarge svarganivāsibhiḥ||83||

84gaganātpuruṣavarṣaṃ ca paśyedaṃ patitaṃ bhuvi
|
surarṣidevagandharvā ye ca devapuraḥsarāḥ||84||

85stuvanto devadūtāśca sthitā dānena vismitāḥ
|
brahmarṣayo vimānasthā brahmalokacarāśca ye||85||

86kāṃkṣante darśanaṃ tubhyaṃ divaṃ vraja dvijarṣabha
|
pitṛlokagatāḥ sarve tāritāḥ pitarastvayā||86||

87anāgatāśca bahavaḥ subahūni yugānyuta
|
brahmacaryeṇa dānena yajñena tapasā tathā||87||

88asaṅkareṇa dharmeṇa tasmādgaccha divaṃ dvija
|
śraddhayā parayā yastvaṃ tapaścarasi suvrata||88||

89tasmāddevāśca dānena prītā brāhmaṇasattama
|
sarvametaddhi yasmātte dattaṃ śuddhena cetasā||89||

90kṛcchrakāle tataḥ svargo vijitaḥ karmaṇā tvayā
|
kṣudhā nirṇudati prajñāṃ dharmabuddhiṃ vyapohati||90||

91kṣudhāparigatajñāno dhṛtiṃ tyajati caiva ha
|
bubhukṣāṃ jayate yastu sa svargaṃ jayate dhruvam||91||

92yadā dānaruciḥ syādvai tadā dharmo na sīdati
|
anavekṣya sutasnehaṃ kalatrasnehameva ca||92||

93dharmameva guruṃ jñātvā tṛṣṇā na gaṇitā tvayā
|
dravyāgamo nṛṇāṃ sūkṣmaḥ pātre dānaṃ tataḥ param||93||

94kālaḥ parataro dānācchraddhā caiva tataḥ parā
|
svargadvāraṃ susūkṣmaṃ hi narairmohānna dṛśyate||94||

95svargārgalaṃ lobhabījaṃ rāmaguptaṃ durāsadam
|
taṃ tu paśyanti puruṣā jitakrodhā jitendriyāḥ||95||

96brāhmaṇāstapasā yuktā yathāśakti pradāyinaḥ
|
sahasraśaktiśca śataṃ śataśaktirdaśāpi ca||96||

97dadyādapaśca yaḥ śaktyā sarve tulyaphalāḥ smṛtāḥ
|
rantidevo hi nṛpatirapaḥ prādādakiñcanaḥ||97||

98śuddhena manasā vipra nākapṛṣṭhaṃ tato gataḥ
|
na dharmaḥ prīyate tāta dānairdattairmahāphalaiḥ||98||

99nyāyalabdhairyathā sūkṣmaiḥ śraddhāpūtaiḥ sa tuṣyati
|
gopradānasahasrāṇi dvijebhyo'dānnṛgo nṛpaḥ||99||

100ekāṃ dattvā sa pārakyāṃ narakaṃ samapadyata
|
ātmamāṃsapradānena śibirauśīnaro nṛpaḥ||100||

101prāpya puṇyakutāllokānmodate divi suvrataḥ
|
vibhavo na nṛṇāṃ puṇyaṃ svaśaktyā svarjitaṃ satām||101||

102na yajñairvividhairvipra yathānyāyena sañcitaiḥ
|
krodhāddānaphalaṃ hanti lobhātsvargaṃ na gacchati||102||

103nyāyavṛttirhi tapasā dānavitsvargamaśnute
|
na rājasūyairbahubhiriṣṭvā vipuladakṣiṇaiḥ||103||

104na cāśvamedhairbahubhiḥ phalaṃ samamidaṃ tava
|
saktuprasthena vijito brahmalokastvayākṣayaḥ||104||

105virajo brahmasadanaṃ gaccha vipra yathāsukham
|
sarveṣāṃ vo dvijaśreṣṭha divyaṃ yānamupasthitam||105||

106ārohata yathākāmaṃ dharmo'smi dvija paśya mām
|
tārito hi tvayā deho loke kīrtiḥ sthirā ca te||106||

107sabhāryaḥ sahaputraśca sasnuṣaśca divaṃ vraja
|
ityuktavākye dharme tu yānamāruhya sa dvijaḥ||107||

108sadāraḥ sasutaścaiva sasnuṣaśca divaṃ gataḥ
|
tasminvipre gate svargaṃ sasute sasnuṣe tadā||108||

109bhāryācaturthe dharmajñe tato'haṃ niḥsṛto bilāt
|
tatastu saktugandhena kledena salilasya ca||109||

110divyapuṣpavimardācca sādhordānalavaiśca taiḥ
|
viprasya tapasā tasya śiro me kāñcanīkṛtam||110||

111tasya satyābhisandhasya saktudānena caiva ha
|
śarīrārdhaṃ ca me viprāḥ śātakumbhamayaṃ kṛtam||111||

112paśyatemaṃ suvipulaṃ tapasā tasya dhīmataḥ
|
kathamevaṃvidhaṃ syādvai pārśvamanyaditi dvijāḥ||112||

113tapovanāni yajñāṃśca hṛṣṭo'bhyemi punaḥ punaḥ
|
yajñaṃ tvahamimaṃ śrutvā kururājasya dhīmataḥ||113||

114āśayā parayā prāpto na cāhaṃ kāñcanīkṛtaḥ
|
tato mayoktaṃ tadvākyaṃ prahasya brāhmaṇarṣabhāḥ||114||

115saktuprasthena yajño'yaṃ sammito neti sarvathā
|
saktuprasthalavaistairhi tadāha kāñcanīkṛtaḥ||115||

116na hi yajño mahāneṣa sadṛśastairmato mama
|
ityuktvā nakulaḥ sarvānyajñe dvijavarāṃstadā||116||

117jagāmādarśanaṃ teṣāṃ viprāste ca yayurgrahān||117||

118vaiśampāyana uvāca|
etatte sarvamākhyātaṃ mayā parapurañjaya
|
yadāścaryamabhūttatra vājimedhe mahākratau||118||

119na vismayaste nṛpate yajñe kāryaḥ kathañcana
|
ṛṣikoṭisahasrāṇi tapobhirye divaṃ gatāḥ||119||

120adrohaḥ sarvabhūteṣu santoṣaḥ śīlamārjavam
|
tapo damaśca satyaṃ ca pradānaṃ ceti sammitam||120||
