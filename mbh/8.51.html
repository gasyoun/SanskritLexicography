Mahābhārata 8.51 (Karṇaparvan)

1dhṛtarāṣṭra uvāca|
suduṣkaramidaṃ karma kṛtaṃ bhīmena sañjaya
|
yena karṇo mahābāhū rathopasthe nipātitaḥ ||1||

2karṇo hyeko raṇe hantā pāṇḍavānsṛñjayaiḥ saha
|
iti duryodhanaḥ sūta prābravīnmāṃ muhurmuhuḥ ||2||

3parājitaṃ tu rādheyaṃ dṛṣṭvā bhīmena saṃyuge
|
tataḥ paraṃ kimakarotputro duryodhano mama ||3||

4sañjaya uvāca|
vimukhaṃ prekṣya rādheyaṃ sūtaputraṃ mahāhave
|
putrastava mahārāja sodaryānsamabhāṣata ||4||

5śīghraṃ gacchata bhadraṃ vo rādheyaṃ parirakṣata
|
bhīmasenabhayāgādhe majjantaṃ vyasanārṇave ||5||

6te tu rājñā samādiṣṭā bhīmasenaṃ jighāṃsavaḥ
|
abhyavartanta saṃkruddhāḥ pataṅgāḥ pāvakaṃ yathā ||6||

7śrutarvā durdharaḥ krātho vivitsurvikaṭaḥ samaḥ
|
niṣaṅgī kavacī pāśī tathā nandopananda‌kau ||7||

8duṣpradharṣaḥ subāhuśca vātavegasuvarcasau
|
dhanurgrāho durmadaśca jalasandhaḥ śalaḥ sahaḥ ||8||

9ete rathaiḥ parivṛtā vīryavanto mahābalāḥ
|
bhīmasenaṃ samāsādya samantātparyavārayan ||9||

10te vyamuñcaccharavrātānnānāliṃgānsamantataḥ
|
sa tairabhyardyamānastu bhīmaseno mahābalaḥ ||10||

11teṣāmāpatatāṃ kṣipraṃ sutānāṃ te janādhipa
|
rathaiḥ pañcadaśaiḥ sārdhaṃ pañcāśadahanadrathān ||11||

12vivitsostu tataḥ kruddho bhallenāpāharacchiraḥ
|
bhīmaseno mahārāja tat papāta hataṃ bhuvi ||12||

13sakuṇḍalaśirastrāṇaṃ pūrṇacandropamaṃ tathā
|
taṃ dṛṣṭvā nihataṃ śūraṃ bhrātaraḥ sarvataḥ prabho ||13||

14abhyadravanta samare bhīmaṃ bhīmaparākramam
|
tato'parābhyāṃ bhallābhyāṃ putrayoste mahāhave ||14||

15jahāra samare prāṇān bhīmo bhīmaparākramaḥ
|
tau dharāmanvapadyetāṃ vātarugṇāviva drumau ||15||

16vikaṭaśca sahaścobhau devaputropamau nṛpa
|
tatastu tvarito bhīmaḥ krāthaṃ ninye yamakṣayam ||16||

17nārācena sutīkṣṇena sa hato nyapatadbhuvi
|
hāhākārastatastīvraḥ saṃbabhūva janeśvara ||17||

18vadhyamāneṣu vīreṣu tava putreṣu dhanviṣu
|
teṣāṃ sululite sainye punarbhīmo mahābalaḥ ||18||

19nandopanandau samare praiṣayadyamasādanam
|
tataste prādravan bhītāḥ putrāste vihvalīkṛtāḥ ||19||

20bhīmasenaṃ raṇe dṛṣṭvā kālāntakayamopamam
|
putrāṃste nihatāndṛṣṭvā sūtaputraḥ sudurmanāḥ ||20||

21haṃsavarṇān hayānbhūyaḥ praiṣayadyatra pāṇḍavaḥ
|
te preṣitā mahārāja madrarājena vājinaḥ ||21||

22bhīmasenarathaṃ prāpya samasajjanta vegitāḥ
|
sa sannipātastumulo ghorarūpo viśāṃpate ||22||

23āsīdraudro mahārāja karṇapāṇḍavayormṛdhe
|
dṛṣṭvā mama mahārāja tau sametau mahārathau ||23||

24āsīdbuddhiḥ kathaṃ yuddhametadadya bhaviṣyati
|
tato bhīmo raṇaślāghī chāda‌yāmāsa patribhiḥ ||24||

25karṇaṃ raṇe mahārāja putrāṇāṃ tava paśyatām
|
tataḥ karṇo bhṛśaṃ kruddho bhīmaṃ navabhirāyasaiḥ ||25||

26vivyādha paramāstrajño bhallaiḥ sannataparvabhiḥ
|
āhataḥ sa mahābāhurbhīmo bhīmaparākramaḥ ||26||

27ākarṇapūrṇairviśikhaiḥ karṇaṃ vivyādha saptabhiḥ
|
tataḥ karṇo mahārāja āśīviṣa iva śvasan ||27||

28śaravarṣeṇa mahatā chādayāmāsa pāṇḍavam
|
bhīmo'pi taṃ śaravrātaiśchādayitvā mahāratham ||28||

29paśyatāṃ kauraveyāṇāṃ vinanarda mahābalaḥ
|
tataḥ karṇo bhṛśaṃ kruddho dṛḍhamādāya kārmukam ||29||

30bhīmaṃ vivyādha daśabhiḥ kaṅkapatraiḥ śilāśitaiḥ
|
kārmukaṃ cāsya ciccheda bhallena niśitena ca ||30||

31tato bhīmo mahābāhurhemapaṭṭavibhūṣitam
|
parighaṃ ghoramādāya mṛtyudaṇḍamivāparam ||31||

32karṇasya nidhanākāṃkṣī cikṣepātibalo nadan
|
tamāpatantaṃ parighaṃ vajrāśanisamasvanam ||32||

33ciccheda bahudhā karṇaḥ śarairāśīviṣopamaiḥ
|
tataḥ kārmukamādāya bhīmo dṛḍhataraṃ tadā ||33||

34chādayāmāsa viśikhaiḥ karṇaṃ parabalārdanam
|
tato yuddhamabhūddhoraṃ karṇapāṇḍavayormṛdhe ||34||

35harīndrayoriva muhuḥ parasparavadhaiṣiṇoḥ
|
tataḥ karṇo mahārāja bhīmasenaṃ tribhiḥ śaraiḥ ||35||

36ākarṇamūlaṃ vivyādha dṛḍhamāyamya kārmukam
|
so'tividdho maheṣvāsaḥ karṇena balināṃ varaḥ ||36||

37ghoramādatta viśikhaṃ karṇakāyāvadāraṇam
|
tasya bhittvā tanutrāṇaṃ bhittvā kāyaṃ ca sāyakaiḥ ||37||

38prāviśaddharaṇīṃ rājanvalmīkamiva pannagaḥ
|
sa tenātiprahāreṇa vyathito vihvalanniva ||38||

39saṃcacāla rathe karṇaḥ kṣitikampe yathā'calaḥ
|
tataḥ karṇo mahārāja roṣāmarṣasamanvitaḥ ||39||

40pāṇḍavaṃ pañcaviṃśatyā nārācānāṃ samārpayat
|
ājaghne bahubhirbāṇairdhvajamekeṣuṇā'hanat ||40||

41sārathiṃ cāsya bhallena preṣayāmāsa mṛtyave
|
chittvā ca kārmukaṃ tūrṇaṃ pāṇḍavasyāśu patriṇā ||41||

42tato muhūrtādrājendra nātikṛcchrāddhasanniva
|
virathaṃ bhīmakarmāṇaṃ bhīmaṃ karṇaścakāra ha ||42||

43viratho bharataśreṣṭha prahasannanilopamaḥ
|
gadāṃ gṛhya mahābāhurapatatsyandanottamāt ||43||

44avaplutya ca vegena tava sainyaṃ viśāṃpate
|
vyadhamadgadayā bhīmaḥ śaranmeghānivānilaḥ ||44||

45nāgānsaptaśatānrājannīṣādantānprahāriṇaḥ
|
vyadhamatsahasā bhīmaḥ kruddharūpaḥ paraṃtapaḥ ||45||

46dantaveṣṭeṣu netreṣu kumbheṣu ca kaṭeṣu ca
|
marmasvapi ca marmajñastānnāgānavadhīdbalī ||46||

47tataste prādravanbhītāḥ pratīpaṃ prahitāḥ punaḥ
|
mahāmātraistamāvavrurmedhā iva divākaram ||47||

48tān sa saptaśatānnāgān sārohāyudhaketanān
|
bhūmiṣṭho ga‌dayā jaghne vajreṇendra ivācalān ||48||

49tataḥ subalaputrasya nāgānatibalānpunaḥ
|
pothayāmāsa kaunteyo dvipañcāśadariṃdamaḥ ||49||

50tathā rathaśataṃ sāgraṃ pattīṃśca śataśo'parān
|
nyahanatpāṇḍavo yuddhe tāpayaṃstava vāhinīm ||50||

51pratāpyamānaṃ sūryeṇa bhīmena ca mahātmanā
|
tava sainyaṃ saṃcukoca carmāgnāvāhitaṃ yathā ||51||

52te bhīmabhayasaṃtrastāstāvakā bharatarṣabha
|
vihāya samare bhīmaṃ dudruvurvai diśo diśa ||52||

53rathāḥ pañcaśatāścānye hrādinaścarmavarmiṇaḥ
|
bhīmamabhyadravan ghnantaḥ śarapūgaiḥ samantataḥ ||53||

54tānsa pañcaśatānvīrānsapatākadhvajāyudhān
|
pothayāmāsa gadayā bhīmo viṣṇurivāsurān ||54||

55tataḥ śakuninirdiṣṭāḥ sādinaḥ śūrasaṃmatāḥ
|
trisāhasrābhyayurbhīmaṃ śaktyṛṣṭiprāsapāṇayaḥ ||55||

56pratyudgamya javenāśu sāśvārohāṃstadā'rihā
|
vividhānvicaranmārgān gadayā samapothayat ||56||

57teṣāmāsīnmahāñchabdastāḍitānāṃ ca sarvaśaḥ
|
aśmabhirvidhyamānānāṃ nagānāmiva bhārata ||57||

58evaṃ subalaputrasya trisāhasrān hayottamān
|
hatvā'nyaṃ rathamāsthāya kruddho rādheyamabhyayāt ||58||

59karṇo'pi samare rājan dharmaputramarindamam
|
sa śaraiśchāda‌yāmāsa sārathiṃ cāpyapātayat ||59||

60tataḥ sa pradrutaḥ saṃkhye rathaṃ dṛṣṭvā mahārathaḥ
|
anvadhāvatkiranbāṇaiḥ kaṅkapatrairājahmagaiḥ ||60||

61rājānamabhidhāvantaṃ śarairāvṛtya rodasī
|
kruddhaḥ pracchāda‌yāmāsa śarajālena mārutiḥ||61||

62sannivṛttastatastūrṇaṃ rādheyaḥ śatrukarśanaḥ
|
bhīmaṃ pracchāda‌yāmāsa samantānniśitaiḥ śaraiḥ ||62||

63bhīmasenarathavyagraṃ karṇaṃ bhārata sātyakiḥ
|
abhyardayadameyātmā pārṣṇigrahaṇakāraṇāt ||63||

64abhyavartata karṇastamardito'pi śarairbhṛśam
|
tāvanyonyaṃ samāsādya vṛṣabhau sarvadhanvinām ||64||

65visṛjantau śarāndīptān vibhrājetāṃ manasvinau
|
tābhyāṃ viyati rājendra vitataṃ bhīmadarśanam ||65||

66krauñcapṛṣṭhāruṇaṃ raudraṃ bāṇajālaṃ vyadṛśyata
|
naiva sūryaprabhā rājanna diśaḥ pradiśastathā ||66||

67prājñāsiṣma vayaṃ te vā śarairmuktaiḥ sahasraśaḥ
|
madhyāhne tapato rājan bhāskarasya mahāprabhāḥ ||67||

68hṛtāḥ sarvāḥ śaraughaistaiḥ karṇapāṇḍavayostadā
|
saubalaṃ kṛtavarmāṇaṃ drauṇimādhirathiṃ kṛpam ||68||

69saṃsaktānpāṇḍavairdṛṣṭvā nivṛttāḥ kuravaḥ punaḥ
|
teṣāmāpatatāṃ śabdastīvra āsīdviśāṃpate ||69||

70udvṛttānāṃ yathā vṛṣṭyā sāgarāṇāṃ bhayāvahaḥ
|
te sene bhṛśasaṃsakte dṛṣṭvā'nyonyaṃ mahāhave ||70||

71harṣeṇa mahatā yukte parigṛhya parasparam
|
tataḥ pravavṛte yuddhaṃ madhyaṃ prāpte divākare ||71||

72tādṛśaṃ na kadāciddhi dṛṣṭapūrvaṃ na ca śrutam
|
balaughastu samāsādya balaughaṃ sahasā raṇe ||72||

73upāsarpata vegena vāryogha iva sāgaram
|
āsīnninādaḥ sumahānbāṇaughānāṃ parasparam ||73||

74garjatāṃ sāgaraughāṇāṃ yathā syānniḥsvano mahān
|
te tu sene samāsādya vegavatyau parasparam ||74||

75ekībhāvamanuprāpte nadyāviva samāgame
|
tataḥ pravavṛte yuddhaṃ ghorarūpaṃ viśāṃpate ||75||

76kurūṇāṃ pāṇḍavānāṃ ca lipsatāṃ sumahadyaśaḥ
|
śūrāṇāṃ garjatāṃ tatra hyavicchedakṛtā giraḥ ||76||

77śrūyante vividhā rājannāmānyuddiśya bhārata
|
yasya yaddhi raṇe vyaṅgaṃ pitṛto mātṛto'pi vā ||77||

78karmataḥ śīlato vā'pi sa tacchrāvayate yudhi
|
tāndṛṣṭvā samare śūrāṃstarjamānānparasparam ||78||

79abhavanme matī rājannaiṣāmastīti jīvitam
|
teṣāṃ dṛṣṭvā tu kruddhānāṃ vapūṃṣyamitatejasām ||79||

80abhavanme bhayaṃ tīvraṃ kathametadbhaviṣyati
|
tataste pāṇḍavā rājankauravāśca mahārathāḥ
|
tatakṣuḥ sāyakaistīkṣṇairnighnanto hi parasparam ||80||
