Mahābhārata 3.250 (Āraṇyakaparvan)

1karṇa uvāca|
rājannādyāvagacchāmi taveha laghusattvatām
|
kimatra citraṃ yadvīra mokṣitaḥ pāṇḍavairasi||1||

2sadyo vaśaṃ samāpannaḥ śatrūṇāṃ śatrukarśana
|
senājīvaiśca kauravya tathā viṣayavāsibhiḥ||2||

3ajñātairyadi vā jñātaiḥ kartavyaṃ nṛpateḥ priyam
|
prāyaḥ pradhānāḥ puruṣāḥ kṣobhayantyarivāhinīm||3||

4nigṛhyante ca yuddheṣu mokṣyante caiva sainikaiḥ
|
senājīvāśca ye rājñāṃ viṣaye santi mānavāḥ||4||

5taiḥ saṅgamya nṛpārthāya yatitavyaṃ yathātatham
|
yadyevaṃ pāṇḍavai rājan bhavadviṣayavāsibhiḥ||5||

6yadṛcchayā mokṣito'si tatra kā paridevanā
|
na caitat sādhu yadrājan pāṇḍavāstvāṃ nṛpottamam||6||

7svasenayā samprayāntaṃ nānuyānti sma pṛṣṭhataḥ
|
śūrāśca balavantaśca saṃyugeṣvapalāyinaḥ||7||

8bhavataste sahāyā vai preṣyatāṃ pūrvamāgatāḥ
|
pāṇḍaveyāni ratnāni tvamadyāpyupabhuñjase||8||

9sattvasthān pāṇḍavān paśya na te prāyamupāviśan
|
uttiṣṭha rājan bhadraṃ te na ciraṃ kartumarhasi||9||

10avaśyameva nṛpate rājño viṣayavāsibhiḥ
|
priyāṇyācaritavyāni tatra kā paridevanā||10||

11madvākyametadrājendra yadyevaṃ na kariṣyasi
|
sthāsyāmīha bhavatpādau śuśrūṣannarimardana||11||

12notsahe jīvitumahaṃ tvadvihīno nararṣabha
|
prāyopaviṣṭastu nṛpa rājñāṃ hāsyo bhaviṣyasi||12||

13vaiśampāyana uvāca|
evamuktastu karṇena rājā duryodhanastadā
|
naivotthātuṃ manaścakre svargāya kṛtaniścayaḥ||13||
