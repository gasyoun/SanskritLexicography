Mahābhārata 4.60 (Virāṭaparvan)

1arjuna uvāca|karṇa yat te sabhāmadhye bahu vācā vikatthitam
|
na me yudhi samo'stīti tadidaṃ samupasthitam ||1||

2so'dya karṇa mayā sārdhaṃ vyavahṛtya mahāmṛdhe
|
jñāsyasyabalamātmānaṃ na cānyānavamanyase ||2||

3avocaḥ paruṣā vāco dharmamṛtsṛjya kevalam
|
idaṃ tu duṣkaraṃ manye yadidaṃ te cikīrṣitam ||3||

4yat tvayā kathitaṃ pūrvaṃ māmanāsādya kiṃcana
|
tadadya kuru rādheya kurumadhye mayā saha ||4||

5yatsabhāyāṃ sa pāñcālīṃ kliśyamānāṃ durātmabhiḥ
|
dṛṣṭavānasi tasyādya phalamāpnuhi kevalam ||5||

6dharmapāśanibaddhena yanmayā marṣitaṃ purā
|
tasya rādheya kopasya vijayaṃ paśya me mṛdhe ||6||

7vane dvādaśa varṣāṇi yāni soḍhāni durmate
|
tasyādya pratikopasya phalaṃ prāpnuhi saṃprati ||7||

8ehi karṇa mayā sārdhaṃ pratiyudhyasva saṅgare
|
prekṣakāḥ kuravaḥ sarve bhavantu tava sainikāḥ ||8||

9karṇa uvāca|bravīṣi vācā yat pārtha karmaṇā tat samācara
|
atiśete hi te vākyaṃ karmaitat prathitaṃ bhuvi ||9||

10yat tvayā marṣitaṃ pūrvaṃ tadaśaktena marṣitam
|
ito gṛhṇīmahe pārtha tava dṛṣṭvā parākramam ||10||

11dharmapāśanibaddhena yat tvayā marṣitaṃ purā
|
tathaiva baddhamātmānamabaddhamiva manyase ||11||

12yadi tāvadvane vāso yathoktaścaritastvayā
|
tattvaṃ dharmārthavitkliṣṭaḥ sa mayā yoddhumicchasi ||12||

13yadi śakraḥ svayaṃ pārtha yudhyate tava kāraṇāt
|
tathā'pi na vyathā kācinmama syādvikramiṣyataḥ ||13||

14ayaṃ kaunteya kāmaste na cirāt samupasthitaḥ
|
yotsyase hi mayā sārdhamadya drakṣyasi me balam ||14||

15arjuna uvāca|idānīmeva tāvattvamapayāto raṇānmama
|
tena jīvasi rādheya nihatastvanujastava ||15||

16bhrātaraṃ ghātayitvā kastyaktvā raṇaśiraśca kaḥ
|
tvadanyaḥ kaḥ pumān satsu brūyādevaṃ vyavasthitaḥ ||16||

17vaiśampāyana uvāca|iti karṇaṃ bruvanneva bībhatsuraparājitaḥ
|
abhyayādvisṛjan bāṇān kāyāvaraṇabhedinaḥ ||17||

18pratijagrāha taṃ karṇaḥ prīyamāṇo mahārathaḥ
|
mahatā śaravarṣeṇa varṣamāṇamivāmbudam ||18||

19utpetuḥ śarajālāni ghorarūpāṇi sarvaśaḥ
|
avidhyadaśvān bāhvośca hastāvāpaṃ pṛthak pṛthak ||19||

20so'mṛṣyamāṇaḥ karṇasya niṣaṅgasyāvalambanam
|
ciccheda niśitāgreṇa śareṇa nataparvaṇā ||20||

21upāsaṅgādupādāya karṇo bāṇānathāparān
|
vivyādha pāṇḍavaṃ haste tasya muṣṭiraśīryata ||21||

22tataḥ pārtho mahābāhuḥ karṇasya dhanuracchinat
|
sa śaktiṃ prāhiṇot tasmai tāṃ pārtho vyadhamaccharaiḥ ||22||

23tato'nupeturbahavo rādheyasya padānugāḥ
|
tāṃśca gāṇḍīvanirmuktaiḥ prāhiṇodyamasādanam ||23||

24tato'syāśvān śaraistīkṣṇairbībhatsurbhārasādhanaiḥ
|
ākarṇamuktairabhyaghnaṃste hatāḥ prāpatan bhuvi ||24||

25athāpareṇa bāṇena jvalitena mahaujasā
|
vivyādha karṇaṃ kaunteyastīkṣṇenorasi vīryavān ||25||

26tasya bhittvā tanutrāṇaṃ kāyamabhyagamaccharaḥ
|
tataḥ sa tamasā''viṣṭo na sma kiṃcitprajajñivān ||26||

27sa gāḍhavedano hitvā raṇaṃ prāyādudaṅmukhaḥ
|
tato'rjuna uda‌krośaduttaraśca mahārathaḥ ||27||
