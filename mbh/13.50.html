Mahābhārata 13.50 (Anuśāsanaparvan)

1yudhiṣṭhira uvāca|
darśane kīdṛśaḥ snehaḥ saṃvāse ca pitāmaha
|
mahābhāgyaṃ gavāṃ caiva tanme vyākhyātumarhasi ||1||

2bhīṣma uvāca|
hanta te kathayiṣyāmi purāvṛttaṃ mahādyute
|
nahuṣasya ca saṃvādaṃ maharṣeścyavanasya ca ||2||

3purā maharṣiścyavano bhārgavo bharatarṣama
|
udavāsakṛtārambho babhūva sa mahāvrataḥ ||3||

4nihatya mānaṃ kodhaṃ ca praharṣaṃ śokameva ca
|
varṣāṇi dvādaśa munirjalavāse dhṛtavrataḥ ||4||

5ādadhatsarvabhūteṣu viśrambhaṃ paramaṃ śubham
|
jalecareṣu sarveṣu śītaraśmiriva prabhuḥ ||5||

6sthāṇubhūtaḥ śucirbhūtvā daivatebhyaḥ praṇamya ca
|
gaṅgāyamunayormadhye jalaṃ saṃpraviveśa ha ||6||

7gaṅgāyamunayoryegaṃ subhīmaṃ bhīmaniḥsvanam
|
pratijagrāha śirasā vātavegasamaṃ jave ||7||

8gaṅgā ca yamunā caiva saritaśca sarāṃsi ca
|
pradakṣiṇamṛṣiṃ cakrurna cainaṃ paryapīḍayan ||8||

9antarjaleṣu suṣvāpa kāṣṭhabhūto mahāmuniḥ
|
tataścordhvāsthito dhīmānabhavadbharatarṣabha ||9||

10jalaukasāṃ sa sattvānāṃ babhūva priyadarśanaḥ
|
upājighranta ca tadā tasyoṣṭhaṃ hṛṣṭamānasāḥ ||10||

11tatra tasyāsataḥ kālaḥ samatīto'bhavanmahān
|
tataḥ kadācitsamaye kasmiṃścinmatsyajīvinaḥ||11||

12taṃ deśaṃ samupājagmurjālahastā mahādyute
|
niṣādā bahavastatra matsyoddharaṇaniścayāḥ ||12||

13vyāyatā balinaḥ śūrāḥ salileṣvanivartinaḥ
|
abhyāyayuśca taṃ deśaṃ niścitā jālakarmaṇi ||13||

14jālaṃ te yojayāmāsurniśeṣeṇa janādhipa
|
matsyodakaṃ samāsādya tadā bharatasattama ||14||

15tataste bahubhiryogaiḥ kaivartā matsyakāṃkṣiṇaḥ
|
gaṅgāyamunayorvāri jālairabhyakiraṃstataḥ ||15||

16jālaṃ suvitataṃ teṣāṃ navasūtrakṛtaṃ tathā
|
vistārāyāmasampannaṃ yattatra salile'kṣipan||16||

17tataste sumahaccaiva balavacca suvartitam
|
avatīrya tataḥ sarve jālaṃ cakṛṣire tadā ||17||

18abhītarūpāḥ saṃhṛṣṭā anyonyavaśavartinaḥ
|
babandhustatra matsyāṃśca tathā'nyān‌ jalacāriṇaḥ||18||

19tathā matsyaiḥ parivṛtaṃ cyavanaṃ bhṛgunandanam
|
ākarṣayanmahārāja jālenātha yadṛcchayā ||
|
19||

20nadīśaivaladigdhāṅgaṃ hariśmaśrujaṭādharam
|
lagnaiḥ śaṅkhanakhairgātre kroḍaiścitrairivārpitam||20||

21taṃ jālenoddhṛtaṃ dṛṣṭvā te tadā vedapāragam
|
sarve prāñjalayo dāśāḥ śirobhiḥ prāpatanbhuvi||21||

22parisvedaparitrāsājjālasyākarṣaṇena ca
|
matsyā babhūvurvyāpannāḥ sthalasaṃsparśanena ca||22||

23sa sunistattadā dṛṣṭvā matsyānāṃ kadanaṃ kṛtam
|
babhūva kṛpayāviṣṭo niḥśvasaṃśca punaḥ punaḥ||23||

24niṣādā ūcuḥ |
ajñānādyatkṛtaṃ pāpaṃ prasādaṃ tatra naḥ kuru
|
karavāma priyaṃ kiṃ te tanno brūhi mahāmune ||24||

25ityukto matsyamadhyasthaścyavano vākyamabravīt
|
yo me'dya paramaḥ kāmastaṃ śṛṇudhvaṃ samāhitāḥ||25||

26prāṇotsargaṃ visargaṃ vā matsyairyāsyāmyahaṃ saha
|
saṃvāsānotsahe tyaktuṃ salile'dhyuṣitānaham||26||

27ityuktāste niṣādāstu subhṛśaṃ bhayakampitāḥ
|
sarve vivarṇavadanā nahuṣāya nyavedayan ||27||
