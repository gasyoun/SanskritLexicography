Mahābhārata 13.112 (Anuśāsanaparvan)

1yudhiṣṭhira uvāca|
adharmasya gatirbrahmankathitā me tvayā'nagha
|
dharmasya tu gatiṃ śrotumicchāmi vadatāṃ vara ||1||

2kṛtvā karmāṇi pāpāni kathaṃ yānti śubhāṃ gatim
|
karmaṇā ca kṛteneha kena yānti śubhāṃ gatim ||2||

3bṛhaspatiruvāca|
kṛtvā pāpāni karmāṇi adharmavaśamāgataḥ
|
manasā viparītena nirayaṃ pratipadyate ||3||

4mohādadharmaṃ yaḥ kṛtvā punaḥ samanutapyate
|
manaḥsamādhisaṃyukto na sa seveta duṣkṛtam ||4||

5yathā yathā manastasya duṣkṛtaṃ karma garhate
|
tathā tathā śarīraṃ tu tenādharmeṇa mucyate ||5||

6yadi vyāharate rājanviprāṇāṃ dharmavādinām
|
tato'dharmakṛtāt kṣipramapavādāt pramucyate ||6||

7yathā yathā naraḥ samyagadharmamanubhāṣate
|
samāhitena manasā vimucyeta tathā tathā ||7||

8bhujaṅga iva nirmokātpūrvabhuktājjarānvitāt
|
dattvā viprasya dānāni vividhāni samāhitaḥ ||8||

9manaḥsamādhisaṃyuktaḥ sugatiṃ pratipadyate
|
pradānāni tu vakṣyāmi yāni datvā yudhiṣṭhira ||9||

10naraḥ kṛtvāpyakāryāṇi tato dharmeṇa yujyate
|
sarveṣāmeva dānānāmannaṃ śreṣṭhamudāhṛtam
|
pūrvamannaṃ pradātavyamṛjunā dharmamicchatā ||10||

11prāṇā hyannaṃ manuṣyāṇāṃ tasmājjantuśca jāyate
|
anne pratiṣṭhito lokastasmādannaṃ praśasyate ||11||

12annameva praśaṃsanti devarṣipitṛmānavāḥ
|
annasya hi pradānena rantidevo divaṃ gataḥ ||12||

13nyāyalabdhaṃ pradātavyaṃ dvijātibhyo'nnamuttamam
|
svādhyāyaṃ samupetebhyaḥ prahṛṣṭenāntarātmanā ||13||

14yasya hyannamupāśnanti brāhmaṇānāṃ śataṃ daśa
|
hṛṣṭena manasā dattaṃ na sa tiryaggatirbhavet ||14||

15brāhmaṇānāṃ sahasrāṇi daśa bhojya nararṣabha
|
naro'dharmātpramucyeta yogeṣvabhirataḥ sadā ||15||

16bhaikṣyeṇānnaṃ samāhṛtya vipro vedapuraskṛtaḥ
|
svādhyāyanirate vipre datveha sukhamedhate ||16||

17ahiṃsanbrāhmaṇasvāni nyāyena paripālya ca
|
kṣatriyastarasā prāptamannaṃ yo vai prayacchati ||17||

18dvijebhyo vedavṛddhebhyaḥ prayataḥ susamāhitaḥ
|
tenāpohati dharmātman duṣkṛtaṃ karma pāṇḍava ||18||

19ṣaḍbhāgapariśuddhaṃ ca kṛṣerbhāgamupārjitam
|
vaiśyo dadaddvijātibhyaḥ pāpebhyaḥ parimucyate ||19||

20avāpya prāṇasandehaṃ kārkaśyena samārjitam
|
annaṃ datvā dvijātibhyaḥ śūdraḥ pāpātpramucyate ||20||

21aurasena balenānnamarjayitvā'vihiṃsakaḥ
|
yaḥ prayacchati viprebhyo na sa durgāṇi paśyati ||21||

22nyāyenaivāptamannaṃ tu naro harṣasamanvitaḥ
|
dvijebhyo vedavṛddhebhyo datvā pāpātpramucyate ||22||

23annamūrjaskaraṃ loke datvorjasvī bhavennaraḥ
|
satāṃ panthānamāvṛtya sarvapāpaiḥ pramucyate ||23||

24dānavadbhiḥ kṛtaḥ panthā yena yānti manīṣiṇaḥ
|
te hi prāṇasya dātārastebhyo dharmaḥ sanātanaḥ ||24||

25sarvāvasthaṃ manuṣyeṇa nyāyenānnamupārjitam
|
kāryaṃ pātrāgataṃ nityamannaṃ hi paramā gatiḥ ||25||

26annasya hi pradānena naro raudraṃ na sevate
|
tasmādannaṃ pradātavyamanyāyaparivarjitam ||26||

27yatedbrāhmaṇapūrvaṃ hi bhoktumannaṃ gṛhī sadā
|
avandhyaṃ divasaṃ kuryādannadānena mānavaḥ ||27||

28bhojayitvā daśaśataṃ naro vedavidāṃ nṛpa
|
nyāyaviddharmaviduṣāmitihāsavidāṃ tathā ||28||

29na yāti narakaṃ ghoraṃ saṃsārāṃśca na sevate
|
sarvakāmasamāyuktaḥ pretya cāpyaśnute sukham ||29||

30evaṃ khalu samāyukto ramate vigatajvaraḥ
|
rūpavān kīrtimāṃścaiva dhanavāṃścopapadyate ||30||

31etatte sarvamākhyātamannadānaphalaṃ mahat
|
mūlametattu dharmāṇāṃ pradānānāṃ ca bhārata ||31||
