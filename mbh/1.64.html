Mahābhārata 1.64 (Ādiparvan)

1janamejaya uvāca|
ya ete kīrtitā brahmanye cānye nānukīrtitāḥ
|
samyaktāñśrotumicchāmirājñaścānyānsahasraśaḥ||1||

2yadarthamiha saṃbhūtā devakalpā mahārathāḥ
|
bhuvi tanme mahābhāga samyagākhyātumarhasi||2||

3vaiśampāyana uvāca|
rahasyaṃ khalvidaṃ rājandevānāmiti naḥ śrutam
|
tattu te kathayiṣyāmi namaskṛtvā svayaṃbhuve||3||

4triḥsaptakṛtvaḥ pṛthivīṃ kṛtvā niḥkṣatriyāṃ purā
|
jāmadagnyastapastepe mahendre parvatottame||4||

5tadā niḥkṣatriye loke bhārgaveṇa kṛte sati
|
brāhmaṇānkṣatriyā rājansutārthinyo'bhicakramuḥ||5||

6tābhiḥ saha samāpeturbrāhmaṇāḥ saṃśitavratāḥ
|
ṛtāvṛtau naravyāghra na kāmānnānṛtau tathā||6||

7tebhyaśca lebhire garbhaṃ kṣatriyāstāḥ sahasraśaḥ
|
tataḥ suṣuvire rājankṣatriyānvīryavattarān||7||

8kumārāṃśca kumārīśca punaḥ kṣatrābhivṛddhaye
|
evaṃ tadbrāhmaṇaiḥ kṣatraṃ kṣatriyāsu tapasvibhiḥ||8||

9jātaṃ vṛddhaṃ ca dharmeṇa sudīrgeṇāyuṣānvitam
|
catvāro'pi tato varṇā babhūvurbrāhmaṇottarāḥ||9||

10abhyagacchannṛtau nārīṃ na kāmānnānṛtau tathā
|
tathaivānyāni bhūtāni tiryagyonigatānyapi||10||

11ṛtau dārāṃśca gacchanti tattathā bharatarṣabha
|
tato vardhanta dharmeṇa sahasraśatajīvinaḥ||11||

12tāḥ prajāḥ pṛthivīpāla dharmavrataparāyaṇāḥ
|
ādhibhirvyādhibhiścaiva vimuktāḥ sarvaśo narāḥ||12||

13athemāṃ sāgarāpāṅgīṃ gāṃ gajendragatākhilām
|
adhyatiṣṭhatpunaḥ kṣatraṃ saśailavanapattanām||13||

14praśāsati punaḥ kṣatre dharmeṇemāṃ vasundharām
|
brāhmaṇādyāstato varṇā lebhire mudamuttamām||14||

15kāmakrodhodbhavāndoṣānnirasya ca narādhipāḥ
|
dharmeṇa daṇḍaṃ daṇḍeṣu praṇayanto'nvapālayan||15||

16tathā dharmapare kṣatre sahasrākṣaḥ śatakratuḥ
|
svādu deśe ca kāle ca varṣeṇāpālayatprajāḥ||16||

17na bāla eva mriyate tadā kaścijjanādhipa
|
na ca striyaṃ prajānāti kaścidaprāptayauvana:||17||

18evamāyuṣmatībhistu prajābhirbharatarṣabha
|
iyaṃ sāgaraparyantā sasāpūryata medinī||18||

19ījire ca mahāyajñaiḥ kṣatriyā bahudakṣiṇaiḥ
|
sāṅgopaniṣadānvedānviprāścādhīyate tadā||19||

20na ca vikrīṇate brahma brāhmaṇāśca tadā nṛpa
|
na ca śūdrasamābhyāse vedānuccārayantyuta||20||

21kārayantaḥ kṛṣiṃ gobhistathā vaiśyāḥ kṣitāviha
|
yuñjate dhuri no gāśca kṛśāṅgāṃścāpyajīvayan||21||

22phenapāṃśca tathā vatsānna duhanti sma mānavāḥ
|
na kūṭamānairvaṇijaḥ paṇyaṃ vikrīṇate tadā||22||

23karmāṇi ca naravyāghra dharmopetāni mānavāḥ
|
dharmamevānupaśyantaścakrurdharmaparāyaṇāḥ||23||

24svakarmaniratāścāsansarve varṇā narādhipa
|
evaṃ tadā naravyāghra dharmo na hrasate kvacit||24||

25kāle gāvaḥ prasūyante nāryaśca bharatarṣabha
|
bhavantyṛtuṣu vṛkṣāṇāṃ puṣpāṇi ca phalāni ca||25||

26evaṃ kṛtayuge samyagvartamāne tadā nṛpa
|
āpūryata mahī kṛtsnā prāṇibhirbahubhirbhṛśam||26||

27evaṃ samudite loke mānuṣe bharatarṣabha
|
asurā jajñire kṣetre rājñāṃ tu manujeśvara||27||

28ādityairhi tadā daityā bahuśo nirjitā yudhi
|
aiśvaryādbhraṃśitāḥ svargātsaṃbabhūvuḥ kṣitāviha||28||

29iha devatvamicchanto mānuṣeṣu tapasvinaḥ
|
jajñire bhuvi bhūteṣu teṣu teṣvasurā vibho||29||

30goṣvaśveṣu ca rājendra kharoṣṭramahiṣeṣu ca
|
kravyātsu caiva bhūteṣu gajeṣu ca mṛgeṣu ca||30||

31jātairiha mahīpāla jāyamānaiśca tairmahī
|
na śaśākātmanātmānamiyaṃ dhārayituṃ dharā||31||

32atha jātā mahīpālāḥ kecidbahumadānvitāḥ
|
diteḥ putrā danoścaiva tadā loka ihācyutāḥ||32||

33vīryavanto'valiptāste nānārūpadharā mahīm
|
imāṃ sāgaraparyantāṃ parīyurarimardanāḥ||33||

34brāhmaṇānkṣatriyānvaiśyāñśūdrāṃścaivāpyapīḍayan
|
anyāni caiva sattvāni pīḍayāmāsurojasā||34||

35trāsayanto'bhinighnantaḥ sarvabhūtagaṇāṃśca te
|
viceruḥ sarvaśo rājanmahīṃ śatasahasraśaḥ||35||

36āśramasthānmaharṣīṃśca dharṣayantastatastataḥ
|
abrahmaṇyā vīryamadā mattā madabalena ca||36||

37evaṃ vīryabalotsiktairbhūriyaṃ tairmahāsuraiḥ
|
pīḍyamānā mahī rājanbrahmāṇamupacakrame||37||

38na hyamī bhūtasatvaughāḥ pannagāḥ sanagāṃ mahīm
|
tadā dhārayituṃ śekuḥ saṃkrāntāṃ dānavairbalāt||38||

39tato mahī mahīpāla bhārārtā bhayapīḍitā
|
jagāma śaraṇaṃ devaṃ sarvabhūtapitāmaham||39||

40sā saṃvṛtaṃ mahābhāgairdevadvijamaharṣibhiḥ
|
dadarśa devaṃ brahmāṇaṃ lokakartāramavyayam||40||

41gandharvairapsarobhiśca daivakarmasu niṣṭhitaiḥ
|
vandyamānaṃ mudopatairvavande cainametya sā||41||

42atha vijñāpayāmāsa bhūmistaṃ śaraṇārthinī
|
sannidhau lokapālānāṃ sarveṣāmeva bhārata||42||

43tatpradhānātmanastasya bhūmeḥ kṛtyaṃ svayaṃbhuvaḥ
|
pūrvamevābhavadrājanviditaṃ parameṣṭhinaḥ||43||

44sraṣṭā hi jagataḥ kasmānna saṃbudhyeta bhārata
|
sasurāsuralokānāmaśeṣeṇa manogatam||44||

45tāmuvāca mahārāja bhūmiṃ bhūmipatiḥ prabhuḥ
|
prabhavaḥ sarvabhūtānāmīśaḥ śaṃbhuḥ prajāpatiḥ||45||

46brahmovāca|
yadarthamabhisaṃprāptā matsakāśaṃ vasundhare
|
tadarthaṃ sanniyokṣyāmi sarvāneva divaukasaḥ||46||

47vaiśaṃpāyana uvāca|
ityuktvā sa mahīṃ devo brahmā rājanvisṛjya ca
|
ādideśa tadā sarvānvibudhānbhūtakṛtsvayam||47||

48asyā bhūmernirasituṃ bhāraṃ bhāgaiḥ pṛthakpṛthak
|
asyāmeva prasūyadhvaṃ tirodhāyeti cābravīt||48||

49tathaiva ca samānīya gandharvāpsarasāṃ gaṇān
|
uvāca bhagavānsarvānidaṃ vacanamarthavat||49||

50brahmovāca|
svaiḥ svairaṃśaiḥ prasūyadhvaṃ yatheṣṭaṃ māneṣeṣu ca
|
atha śakrādayaḥ sarve śrutvā suragurorvacaḥ
|
tathyamarthyaṃ ca pathyaṃ ca tasya te jagṛhustadā||50||

51atha te sarvaśoṃśaiḥ svairgantuṃ bhūmiṃ kṛtakṣaṇāḥ
|
nārāyaṇamamitraghnaṃ vaikuṇṭhamupacakramuḥ||51||

52yaḥ sa cakragadāpāṇiḥ pītavāsāḥ śitiprabhaḥ
|
padmanābhaḥ surārighnaḥ pṛthucārvañcitekṣaṇaḥ||52||

53prajāpatipatirdevaḥ suranātho mahābalaḥ
|
śrīvatsāṅko hṛṣīkeśaḥ sarvadaivatapūjitaḥ||53||

54taṃ bhuvaḥ śodhanāyendra uvāca puruṣottamam
|
aṃśenāvataretyevaṃ tathetyāha ca taṃ hariḥ||54||
