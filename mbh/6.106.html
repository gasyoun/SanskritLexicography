Mahābhārata 6.106 (Bhīṣmaparvan)

1sañjaya uvāca|
tataḥ pitā tava kruddho niśitaiḥ sāyakottamaiḥ
|
ājaghāna raṇe pārthān sahasenānsamantataḥ ||1||

2bhīmaṃ dvādaśabhirviddhvā sātyakiṃ navabhiḥ śaraiḥ
|
nakulaṃ ca tribhirviddhvā sahadevaṃ ca saptabhiḥ ||2||

3yudhiṣṭhiraṃ dvādaśabhirbāhvorurasi cārpayat
|
dhṛṣṭadyumnaṃ tato viddhvā nanāda sumahābalaḥ ||3||

4taṃ dvādaśākhyairnakulo mādhavaśca tribhiḥ śaraiḥ
|
dhṛṣṭadyumnaśca saptatyā bhīmasenaśca saptabhiḥ ||4||

5yudhiṣṭhiro dvādaśabhiḥ pratyavidhyatpitāmaham
|
droṇastu sātyakiṃ viddhvā bhīmasenamavidhyata ||5||

6ekaikaṃ pañcabhirbāṇairyamadaṇḍopamaiḥ śitaiḥ
|
tau ca taṃ pratyavidhyetāṃ tribhistribhirajihmagaiḥ ||6||

7totrairiva mahānāgaṃ droṇaṃ brāhmaṇapuṅgavam
|
sauvīrāḥ kitavāḥ prācyāḥ pratīcyodīcyamālavāḥ ||7||

8abhīṣāhāḥ śūrasenāḥ śivayo'tha vasātayaḥ
|
saṅgrāme nājahurbhīṣmaṃ vadhyamānāḥ śitaiḥ śaraiḥ ||8||

9tathaivānye mahīpālā nānādeśasamāgatāḥ
|
pāṇḍavānabhyavartanta vividhāyudhapāṇayaḥ ||9||

10tathaiva pāṇḍavā rājan parivavruḥ pitāmaham
|
sa samantāt parivṛto rathaughairaparājitaḥ ||10||

11gahane'gnirivotsṛṣṭaḥ prajajvāla dahanparān
|
rathāgnyagāraścāpārcirasiśaktigadendhanaḥ ||11||

12śarasphuliṅgo bhīṣmāgnirdadāha kṣatriyarṣabhān
|
suvarṇapuṅkhairiṣubhirgārdhrapakṣaiḥ sutejanaiḥ ||12||

13karṇinālīkanārācaiśchādayāmāsa tadbalam
|
apātayaddhvajāṃścaiva rathinaśca śitaiḥ śaraiḥ ||13||

14muṇḍatālavanānīva cakāra sa rathavrajān
|
nirmanuṣyān rathān rājan gajānaśvāṃśca saṃyuge ||14||

15akarot sa mahābāhuḥ sarvaśastrabhṛtāṃ varaḥ
|
tasya jyātalanirghoṣaṃ visphūrjitamivāśaneḥ||15||

16niśamya sarvabhūtāni samakampanta bhārata
|
amoghā hyapatan bāṇāḥ pituste bharatarṣabha ||16||

17nāsajjanta tanutreṣu bhīṣmacāpacyutāḥ śarāḥ
|
hatavīrān rathān rājan saṃyuktān javanairhayaiḥ ||17||

18apaśyāma mahārāja hriyamāṇān raṇājire
|
cedikāśikarūṣāṇāṃ sahasrāṇi caturdaśa ||18||

19mahārathāḥ samākhyātāḥ kulaputrāstanutyajaḥ
|
aparāvartinaḥ sarve suvarṇavikṛtadhvajāḥ ||19||

20saṅgrāme bhīṣmamāsādya vyāditāsyamivāntakam
|
nimagnāḥ paralokāya savājirathakuñjarāḥ ||20||

21bhagnākṣopaskarānkāṃścidbhagnacakrāṃśca bhārata
|
apaśyāma mahārāja śataśo'tha sahasraśaḥ ||21||

22savarūthai rathairbhagnai rathibhiśca nipātitaiḥ
|
śaraiḥ sukavacaiśchinaiḥ paṭṭiśaiśca viśāṃpate ||22||

23gadābhirbhindipālaiśca niśitaiśca śilīmukhaiḥ
|
anukarṣairupāsaṅgeścakrairbhagnaiśca māriṣa ||23||

24bāhubhiḥ kārmukaiḥ khaḍgaiḥ śirobhiśca sakuṇḍalaiḥ
|
talatrairaṅgulitraiśca dhvajaiśca vinipātitaiḥ ||24||

25cāpaiśca bahudhā cchinnaiḥ samāstīryata medinī
|
hatārohā gajā rājanhayāśca hayasādinaḥ ||25||

26nyapatanta gataprāṇāḥ śataśo'tha sahasraśaḥ
|
yatamānāśca te vīrā dravamāṇānmahārathān ||26||

27nāśaknuvan vārayituṃ bhīṣmabāṇaprapīḍitān
|
mahendrasamavīryeṇa vadhyamānā mahācamūḥ ||27||

28abhajyata mahārāja na ca dvau saha dhāvataḥ
|
āviddharathanāgāśvaṃ patitadhvajasaṅkulam ||28||

29anīkaṃ pāṇḍuputrāṇāṃ hāhābhūtamacetanam
|
jaghānātra pitā putraṃ putraśca pitaraṃ tathā ||29||

30priyaṃ sakhāyaṃ cākrande sakhā daivabalāt kṛtaḥ
|
vimucya kavacānanye pāṇḍuputrasya sainikāḥ ||30||

31prakīrya keśān dhāvantaḥ pratyadṛśyanta sarvaśaḥ
|
tadgokulamivodbhrāntamudbhāntarathakūbaram ||31||

32dadṛśe pāṇḍuputrasya sainyamārtasvaraṃ tadā
|
prabhajyamānaṃ sainyaṃ tu dṛṣṭvā yādavanandanaḥ ||32||

33uvāca pārthaṃ bībhatsuṃ nigṛhya rathamuttamam
|
ayaṃ sa kālaḥ saṃprāptaḥ pārtha yaḥ kāṅkṣitastava ||33||

34praharāsminnaravyāghra na cenmohādvimuhyase
|
yatpurā kathitaṃ vīra rājñāṃ teṣāṃ samāgame ||34||

35virāṭanagare tāta sañjayasya samīpataḥ
|
bhīṣmadroṇamukhānsarvāndhārtarāṣṭrasya sainikān ||35||

36sānubandhān haniṣyāmi ye māṃ yotsyanti saṅgare
|
iti tatkuru kaunteya satyaṃ vākyamarindama ||36||

37kṣatradharmamanusmṛtya yudhyasva vigatajvaraḥ
|
ityukto vāsudevena tiryagdṛṣṭiradhomukhaḥ ||37||

38akāma iva bībhatsuridaṃ vacanamabravīt
|
avadhyānāṃ vadhaṃ kṛtvā rājyaṃ vā narakottaram ||38||

39duḥkhāni vanavāse vā kiṃ nu me sukutaṃ bhavet
|
codayāśvānyato bhīṣmaḥ kariṣye vacanaṃ tava ||39||

40pātayiṣyāmi durdharṣaṃ bhīṣmaṃ kurupitāmaham
|
sa cāśvān rajataprakhyāṃ codayāmāsa mādhavaḥ ||40||

41yato bhīṣmastato rājan duṣprekṣyo raśmivāniva
|
tatastat punaravṛttaṃ yudhiṣṭhirabalaṃ mahat ||41||

42dṛṣṭvā pārthaṃ mahābāhuṃ bhīṣmāyodyatamāhave
|
tato bhīṣmaḥ kuruśreṣṭhaḥ siṃhavadvinadanmuhuḥ ||42||

43dhanañjayarathaṃ śīghraṃ śaravarṣairavākirat
|
kṣaṇena sa rathastasya sahayaḥ sahasārathiḥ ||43||

44śaravarṣeṇa mahatā na prājñāyata bhārata
|
vāsudevastvasaṃbhrānto dhairyamāsthāya satvaraḥ ||44||

45codayāmāsa tānaśvānvinunnānbhīṣmasāyakaiḥ
|
tataḥ pārtho dhanurguhya divyaṃ jaladaniḥ svanam ||45||

46pātayāmāsa bhīṣmasya dhanuśchitvā śitaiḥ śaraiḥ
|
sa cchinnadhanvā kauravyaḥ punaranyanmahaddhanuḥ ||46||

47nimeṣāntaramātreṇa sajyaṃ cakre pitā tava
|
cakarṣa ca tato dorbhyāṃ dhanurjaladaniḥ svanam ||47||

48athāsya tadapi kruddhaściccheda dhanurarjunaḥ
|
tasya tatpūjayāmāsa lāghavaṃ śāntanoḥ sutaḥ ||48||

49gāṅgeyastvabravīt pārthaṃ dhanviśreṣṭhamarindama
|
sādhu sādhu mahābāho sādhu kuntīsuteti ca ||49||

50samābhāṣyaivamaparaṃ pragṛhya ruciraṃ dhanuḥ
|
mumoca samare bhīṣmaḥ śarān pārtharathaṃ prati ||50||

51adarśayadvāsudevo hayayāne paraṃ balam
|
modhān kurvan śarāṃstasya maṇḍalāni nidarśayan ||51||

52śuśubhāte naravyāghrau tau bhīṣmaśaravikṣatau
|
govṛṣāviva saṃrabdhau viṣāṇollikhitāṅkitau ||52||

53vāsudevastu saṃprekṣya pārthasya mṛduyuddhatām
|
bhīṣmaṃ ca śaravarṣāṇi sṛjantamaniśaṃ yudhi ||53||

54pratapantamivādityaṃ madhyamāsādya senayoḥ
|
varānvarān vinighnantaṃ pāṇḍuputrasya sainikān ||54||

55yugāntamiva kurvāṇaṃ bhīṣmaṃ yaudhiṣṭhire bale
|
nāmṛṣyata mahābāhurmādhavaḥ paravīrahā ||55||

56utsṛjya rajataprakhyān hayānpārthasya māriṣa
|
vāsudevastato yogī pracaskanda mahārathāt ||56||

57abhidudrāva bhīṣmaṃ sa bhujapraharaṇo balī
|
pratodapāṇistejasvī siṃhavadvinadanmuhuḥ ||57||

58dārayanniva padbhyāṃ sa jagatīṃ jagadīśvaraḥ
|
krodhatāmrekṣaṇaḥ kṛṣṇo jighāṃsuramitadyutiḥ ||58||

59grasanta iva cetāṃsi tāvakānāṃ mahāhave
|
dṛṣṭvā mādhavamākrande bhīṣmāyodyatamantike ||59||

60hato bhīṣmo hato bhīṣmastatra tatra vaco mahat
|
aśrūyata mahārāja vāsudevabhayāttadā ||60||

61pītakauśeyasaṃvīto maṇiśyāmo janārdanaḥ
|
śuśume vidravan bhīṣmaṃ vidyunmālī yathāṃ'budaḥ ||61||

62sa siṃha iva mātaṅgaṃ yūtharṣabha ivarṣabham
|
abhidudrāva vegena vinadanyādavarṣabhaḥ ||62||

63tamāpatantaṃ saṃprekṣya puṇḍarīkākṣamāhave
|
asaṃbhramaṃ raṇe bhīṣmo vicakarṣa mahaddhanuḥ ||63||

64uvāca caiva govindamasaṃbhrāntena cetasā
|
ehyehi puṇḍarīkākṣa devadeva namo'stu te ||64||

65māmadya sātvataśreṣṭha pātayasva mahāhave
|
tvayā hi deva saṅgrāme hatasyāpi mamānagha ||65||

66śreya eva paraṃ kṛṣṇa loke bhavati sarvataḥ
|
saṃbhāvito'smi govinda trailokyenādya saṃyuge ||66||

67praharasva yatheṣṭaṃ vai dāso'smi tava cānagha
|
anvageva tataḥ pārthaḥ samabhidrutya keśavam ||67||

68nijagrāha mahābāhurbāhubhyāṃ parigṛhya vai
|
nigṛhyamāṇaḥ pārthena kṛṣṇo rājīvalocanaḥ ||38||

69jagāmaivainamādāya vegena puruṣottamaḥ
|
pārthastu viṣṭabhya balāccaraṇau paravīrahā ||69||

70nijagrāha hṛṣīkeśaṃ kathañciddaśame pade
|
tata evamuvācārtaḥ krodhaparyākulekṣaṇam ||70||

71niḥśvasantaṃ yathā nāgamarjunaḥ praṇayātsakhā
|
nivartasva mahābāho nānṛtaṃ kartumarhasi ||71||

72yattvayā kathitaṃ pūrvaṃ na yotsyāmīti keśava
|
mithyāvādīti lokāstvāṃ kathayiṣyanti mādhava ||72||

73mamaiṣa bhāraḥ sarvo hi haniṣyāmi pitāmaham
|
śape keśava śastreṇa satyena sukṛtena ca ||73||

74antaṃ yathā gamiṣyāmi śatrūṇāṃ śatrusudana
|
adyaiva paśya durdharṣaṃ pātyamānaṃ mahāratham ||74||

75tārāpatimivāpūrṇamantakāle yadṛcchayā
|
mādhavastu vacaḥ śrutvā phālgunasya mahātmanaḥ ||75||

76na kiñcidudvā sakrodha āruroha rathaṃ punaḥ
|
tau rathasthau naravyāghrau bhīṣmaḥ śāntanavaḥ punaḥ||76||

77vavarṣa śaravarṣeṇa megho vṛṣṭyā yathā'calau
|
prāṇānādatta yodhānāṃ pitā devavratastava ||77||

78gabhastibhirivādityastejāṃsi śiśirātyaye
|
yathā kurūṇāṃ sainyāni babhañjuryudhi pāṇḍavāḥ ||78||

79tathā pāṇḍavasainyāni babhañja yudhi te pitā
|
hatavidrutasainyāstu nirutsāhā vicetasaḥ ||79||

80nirīkṣituṃ na śekuste bhīṣmamapratimaṃ raṇe
|
madhyaṃgatamivādityaṃ pratapantaṃ svatejasā ||80||

81te vadhyamānā bhīṣmeṇa śataśo'tha sahasraśaḥ
|
kurvāṇaṃ samare karmāṇyatimānuṣavikramam ||81||

82vīkṣāṃ cakrurmahārāja pāṇḍavā bhayapīḍitāḥ
|
tathā pāṇḍavasainyāni drāvyamāṇāni bhārata ||82||

83trātāraṃ nādhyagacchanta gāvaḥ paṅkagatā iva
|
pipīlikā iva kṣuṇṇā durbalā balinā raṇe ||83||

84mahārathaṃ bhārataduṣprakampaṃ śaraugheṇaṃ pratapantaṃ narendrān
|
bhīṣmaṃ na śekuḥ prativīkṣituṃ te śarārciṣaṃ sūryamivātapantam ||84||

85vimṛdnatastasya tu pāṇḍusenāmastaṃ jagāmātha sahasraraśmiḥ
|
tato balānāṃ śramakarśitānāṃ mano'vahāraṃ prati saṃbabhūva ||85||
