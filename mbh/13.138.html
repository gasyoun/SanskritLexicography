Mahābhārata 13.138 (Anuśāsanaparvan)

1yudhiṣṭhira uvāca|
śrutaṃ me bhavatastāta satyavrataparākrama
|
dānadharmeṇa mahatā ye prāptāstridivaṃ nṛpāḥ ||1||

2imāṃstu śrotumicchāmi dharmāndharmabhṛtāṃ vara
|
dānaṃ katividhaṃ deyaṃ kiṃ tasya ca phalaṃ labheta ||2||

3kathaṃ kebhyaśca dharmyaṃ ca dānaṃ dātavyamiṣyate
|
kaiḥ kāraṇaiḥ katividhaṃ śrotumicchāmi tattvataḥ||3||

4bhīṣma uvāca|
śṛṇu tattvena kaunteya dānaṃ prati mamānagha
|
yathā dānaṃ pradātavyaṃ sarvavarṇeṣu bhārata ||4||

5dharmādarthādbhayātkāmātkāruṇyāditi bhārata
|
dānaṃ pañcavidhaṃ jñeyaṃ kāraṇairyairnibodha tat ||5||

6iha kīrtimavāpnoti pretya cānuttamaṃ sukham
|
iti dānaṃ pradātavyaṃ brāhmaṇebhyo'nasūyatā ||6||

7dadāti vā dāsyati vā mahyaṃ dattamanena vā
|
ityarthibhyo niśamyaiva sarvaṃ dātavyamarthine ||7||

8nāsyāhaṃ na madīyo'yaṃ pāpaṃ kuryādvimānitaḥ
|
iti dadyādbhayādeva dṛḍhaṃ mūḍhāya paṇḍitaḥ ||8||

9priyo me'yaṃ priyosyāhamiti saṃprekṣya buddhimān
|
vayasyāyaivamakliṣṭaṃ dānaṃ dadyādatandritaḥ ||9||

10dīnaśca yācate cāyamalpenāpi hi tuṣyati
|
iti dadyāddaridrāya kāruṇyāditi sarvathā ||10||

11iti pañcavidhaṃ dānaṃ puṇyakīrtivivardhanam
|
yathāśaktyā pradātavyamevamāha prajāpatiḥ ||11||
