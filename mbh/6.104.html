Mahābhārata 6.104 (Bhīṣmaparvan)

1sañjaya uvāca|
arjunastānnaravyāghraḥ suśarmānucarānnṛpān
|
anayat pretarājasya sadanaṃ sāyakaiḥ śitaiḥ ||1||

2suśarmā'pi tato bāṇaiḥ pārthaṃ vivyādha saṃyuge
|
vāsudevaṃ ca saptatyā pārthaṃ ca navabhiḥ punaḥ ||2||

3taṃ nivārya śaraugheṇa śakrasūnurmahārathaḥ
|
suśarmaṇo raṇe yodhānprāhiṇodyamasādanam ||3||

4te vadhyamānāḥ pārthena kāleneva yugakṣaye
|
vyadravanta raṇe rājan bhaye jāte mahārathāḥ ||4||

5utsṛjya turagān kecidrathān kecicca māriṣa
|
gajānanye samutsṛjya prādravanta diśo daśa ||5||

6apare tu tadā''dāya vājināgarathān raṇe
|
tvarayā parayā yuktāḥ prādravanta viśāṃpate ||6||

7pādātāścāpi śastrāṇi samutsṛjya mahāraṇe
|
nirapekṣā vyadhāvanta tena tena sma bhārata ||7||

8vāryamāṇāḥ subahuśastraigartena suśarmaṇā
|
tathā'nyaiḥ pārthivaśreṣṭhairna vyatiṣṭhanta saṃyuge ||8||

9tadbalaṃ pradrutaṃ dṛṣṭvā putro duryodhanastava
|
puraskṛtya raṇe bhīṣmaṃ sarvasainyapuraskṛtaḥ ||9||

10sarvodyogena mahatā dhanañjayamupādravat
|
trigartādhipaterarthe jīvitasya viśāmpate ||10||

11sa ekaḥ samare tasthau kiran bahuvidhān śarān
|
bhrātṛbhiḥ sahitaḥ sarvaiḥ śeṣā hi pradrutā narāḥ ||11||

12tathaiva pāṇḍavā rājan sarvodyogena daṃśitāḥ
|
prayayuḥ phālgunārthāya yatra bhīṣmo vyatiṣṭhata ||12||

13jñāyamānā raṇe vīryaṃ ghoraṃ gāṇḍīvadhanvanaḥ
|
hāhākārakṛtotsāhā bhīṣmaṃ jagmuḥ samantataḥ ||13||

14tatastāladhvajaḥ śūraḥ pāṇḍavānāṃ varūthinī
|
chādayāsa samare śaraiḥ sannataparvabhiḥ ||14||

15ekībhūtāstataḥ sarve kuravaḥ sahaḥ pāṇḍavaiḥ
|
ayudhyanta mahārāja madhyaṃ prāpte divākare ||15||

16sātyakiḥ kṛtavarmāṇaṃ vidhvā pañcabhirāśugaiḥ
|
atiṣṭhadāhave śūraḥ kiranbāṇān sahasraśaḥ ||16||

17tathaiva drupado rājā droṇaṃ viddhvā śitaiḥ śaraiḥ
|
punarvivyādha saptatyā sārathiṃ cāsya pañcabhiḥ ||17||

18bhīmasenastu rājānaṃ bāhlīkaṃ prapitāmaham
|
viddhvā nadanmahānādaṃ śārdūla iva kānane ||18||

19ārjuniścitrasenena viddho bahubhirāśugaiḥ
|
atiṣṭhadāhave śūraḥ kiranbāṇān sahasraśaḥ ||19||

20citrasenaṃ tribhirbāṇairvivyādha samare bhṛśam
|
samāgatau tau tu raṇe mahāmātrau vyarocatām ||20||

21yathā divi mahāghorau rājanbudhaśanaiścarau
|
tasyāśvāṃścaturo hatvā sūtaṃ ca navabhiḥ śaraiḥ ||21||

22nanāda balavānnādaṃ saubhadraḥ paravīrahā
|
hatāśvāttu rathāt tūrṇaṃ so'vaplutya mahārathaḥ ||22||

23āruroha rathaṃ tūrṇaṃ durmukhasya viśāmpate
|
droṇaśca drupadaṃ bhittvā śaraiḥ sannataparvabhiḥ ||23||

24sārathiṃ cāsya vivyādha tvaramāṇaḥ parākramī
|
pīḍyamānastato rājā drupado vāhinīmukhe ||24||

25apāyājjavanairaśvaiḥ pūrvavairamanusmaran
|
bhīmasenastu rājānaṃ muhūrtādiva bāhlikam ||25||

26vyaśvasūtarathaṃ cakre sarvasainyasya paśyataḥ
|
sasaṃbhramo mahārāja saṃśayaṃ paramaṃ gataḥ ||26||

27avaplutya tato vāhādbahlīkaḥ puruṣottamaḥ
|
āruroha rathaṃ tūrṇaṃ lakṣmaṇasya mahāraṇe ||27||

28sātyakiḥ kṛtavarmāṇaṃ vārayitvā mahāraṇe
|
śarairbahuvidhai rājannāsasāda pitāmaham ||28||

29sa vidhvā bhārataṃ ṣaṣṭyā niśitairlomavāhibhiḥ
|
nṛtyanniva rathopasthe vidhunvāno mahaddhanuḥ ||29||

30tasyāyasīṃ mahāśaktiṃ cikṣepātha pitāmahaḥ
|
hemacitrāṃ mahāvegāṃ nāgakanyopamāṃ śubhām ||30||

31tāmāpatantīṃ sahasā mṛtyukalpāṃ sudurjayām
|
vyaṃsayāmāsa vārṣṇeyo lāghavena mahāyaśāḥ ||31||

32anāsādya tu vārṣṇeyaṃ śaktiḥ paramadāruṇā
|
nyapataddharaṇīpṛṣṭhe maholkeva mahāprabhā ||32||

33vārṣṇeyastu tato rājan svāṃ śaktiṃ kanakaprabhām
|
vegavagṛhya cikṣepa pitāmaharathaṃ prati ||33||

34vārṣṇeyabhujavegena praṇunnā sā mahāhave
|
abhidudrāva vegena kālarātriryathā naram ||34||

35tāmāpatantīṃ sahasā dvidhā ciccheda bhārataḥ
|
kṣuraprābhyāṃ sutīkṣṇābhyāṃ sā vyaśīryata medinīm ||35||

36chittvā śaktiṃ tu gāṅgeyaḥ sātyakiṃ navabhiḥ śaraiḥ
|
ājaghānorasi kruddhaḥ prahasañchatrukarśanaḥ ||36||

37tataḥ sarathanāgāśvāḥ pāṇḍavāḥ pāṇḍupūrvajāḥ
|
parivabrū raṇe bhīṣmaṃ mādhavatrāṇakāraṇāt ||37||

38tataḥ pravavṛte yuddhaṃ tumulaṃ lomaharṣaṇam
|
pāṇḍavānāṃ kurūṇāṃ ca samare vijayaiṣiṇām ||38||
