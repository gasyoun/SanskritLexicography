Mahābhārata 12.134 (Śāntiparvan)

1bhīṣma uvāca|
atra dharmānuvacanaṃ kīrtayanti purāvidaḥ
|
pratyakṣāveva dharmārthau kṣatriyasya vijānataḥ ||1||

2tatra na vyavadhātavyaṃ parokṣā dharmayāpanā
|
adharmo dharma ityetadyathā vṛkapadaṃ tathā ||2||

3dharmādharmaphale jātu dadarśeha na kaścana
|
bubhūṣedbalamevaitatsarvaṃ balavato vaśe ||3||

4śriyo balamamātyāṃśca balavāniha vindati
|
yo hyanāḍhyaḥ sa patitastaducchiṣṭaṃ yadalpakam ||4||

5bahvapathyaṃ balavati na kiṃcitkriyate bhayāt
|
ubhau satyādhikārasthau trāyete mahato bhayāt ||5||

6atidharmādbalaṃ manye balāddharmaḥ pravartate
|
bale pratiṣṭhito dharmo dharaṇyāmiva jaṅgamam ||6||

7dhūmo vāyoriva vaśe balaṃ dharmo'nuvartate
|
anīśvaro bale dharmo drume vallīva saṃśritā ||7||

8vaśe balavatāṃ dharmaḥ sukhaṃ bhogavatāmiva
|
nāstyasādhyaṃ balavatāṃ sarvaṃ balavatāṃ śuci ||8||

9durācāraḥ kṣīṇabalaḥ paritrāṇaṃ na gacchati
|
atha tasmādudvijate sarvo loko vṛkādiva ||9||

10apadhvasto hyavamato duḥkhaṃ jīvati jīvitam
|
jīvitaṃ yadapakruṣṭaṃ yathaiva maraṇaṃ tathā ||10||

11yadevamāhuḥ pāpena cāritreṇa vivarjitaḥ
|
subhṛśaṃ tapya tenaiva vākśalyena parikṣataḥ ||11||

12atraitadāhurācāryāḥ pāpasya parimokṣaṇe
|
trayīṃ vidyāmavekṣeta tathopāsīta vai dvijān ||12||

13prasādayeccakṣuṣā ca vācā cāpyatha karmaṇā
|
mahāmanāścāpi bhavedvivahecca mahākule ||13||

14ityasmīti vadedevaṃ pareṣāṃ kīrtayed guṇān
|
japedudakaśīlaḥ syātpeśalo nātijalpakaḥ ||14||

15brahmakṣatraṃ sampraviśedbahu kṛtvā suduṣkaram
|
ucyamāno hi lokena bahukṛttadacintayan ||15||

16apāpo hyevamācāraḥ kṣipraṃ bahumato bhavet
|
sukhaṃ ca citraṃ bhuñjīta kṛtenaikena gopayet ||16||

17loke ca labhate pūjāṃ paratreha mahatphalam ||17||
