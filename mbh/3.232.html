Mahābhārata 3.232 (Āraṇyakaparvan)

1yudhiṣṭhira uvāca|
bhagavan śrotumicchāmi nāmānyasya mahātmanaḥ
|
triṣu lokeṣu yānyasya vikhyātāni dvijottama||1||

2vaiśampāyana uvāca|
ityuktaḥ pāṇḍaveyena mahātmā ṛṣisannidhau
|
uvāca bhagavāṃstatra mārkaṇḍeyo mahātapāḥ||2||

3mārkaṇḍeya uvāca|
āgneyaścaiva skandaśca dīptakīrtiranāmayaḥ
|
mayūraketurdharmātmā bhūteśo mahiṣārdanaḥ||3||

4kāmajit kāmadaḥ kāntaḥ satyavāgbhuvaneśvaraḥ
|
śiśuḥ śīghraḥ śuciścaṇḍo dīptavarṇaḥ śubhānanaḥ||4||

5amoghastvanagho raudraḥ priyaścandrānanastathā
|
dīptaśaktiḥ praśāntātmā bhadrakṛt kūṭamohanaḥ||5||

6ṣaṣṭhīpriyaśca dharmātmā pavitro mātṛvatsalaḥ
|
kanyābhartā vibhaktaśca svāheyo revatīsutaḥ||6||

7prabhurnetā viśākhaśca naigameyaḥ suduścaraḥ
|
suvrato lalitaścaiva bālakrīḍanakapriyaḥ||7||

8khacārī brahmacārī ca śūraḥ śaravaṇodbhavaḥ
|
viśvāmitrapriyaścaiva devasenāpriyastathā||8||

9vāsudevapriyaścaiva priyaḥ priyakṛdeva tu
|
nāmānyetāni divyāni kārtikeyasya yaḥ paṭhet
|
svargaṃ kīrtiṃ dhanaṃ caiva sa labhennātra saṃśayaḥ||9||

10mārkaṇḍeya uvāca|
stoṣyāmi devairṛṣibhiśca juṣṭaṃ śaktyā guhaṃ nāmabhiraprameyam
|
ṣaḍānanaṃ śaktidharaṃ suvīraṃ nibodha caitāni kurupravīra||10||

11brahmaṇyo vai brahmajo brahmavicca brahmeśayo brahmavatāṃ variṣṭhaḥ
|
brahmapriyo brāhmaṇasavratī tvaṃ brahmajño vai brāhmaṇānāṃ ca netā||11||

12svāhā svadhā tvaṃ paramaṃ pavitraṃ mantrastutastvaṃ prathitaḥ ṣaḍarciḥ
|
saṃvatsarastvamṛtavaśca ṣaḍvai māsārdhamāsāvayanaṃ diśaśca||12||

13tvaṃ puṣkarākṣastvaravindavaktraḥ sahasravaktro'si sahastrabāhuḥ
|
tvaṃ lokapālaḥ paramaṃ haviśca tvaṃ bhāvanaḥ sarvasurāsurāṇām||13||

14tvameva senādhipatiḥ pracaṇḍaḥ prabhurvibhuścāpyatha śatrujetā
|
sahasrabhūstvaṃ dharaṇī tvameva sahasratuṣṭiśca sahasrabhukca||14||

15sahasraśīrṣastvamanantarūpaḥ sahasrapāt tvaṃ guhaśaktidhārī
|
gaṅgāsutastvaṃ svamatena deva svāhāmahīkṛttikānāṃ tathaiva||15||

16tvaṃ krīḍase ṣaṇmukha kukkuṭena yatheṣṭanānāvidhakāmarūpī
|
dīkṣā'si somo marutaḥ sadaiva dharmo'si vāyuracalendra indraḥ||16||

17sanātanānāmapi śāśvatastvaṃ prabhuḥ prabhūṇāmapi cogradhanvā
|
ṛtasya kartā ditijāntakastvaṃ jetā ripūṇāṃ pravaraḥ surāṇām||17||

18sūkṣmaṃ tapastatparamaṃ tvameva parāvarajño'si parāvarastvam
|
dharmasya kāmasya parasya caiva tvattejasā kṛtsnamidaṃ mahātman||18||

19vyāptaṃ jagat sarva surapravīra śaktyā mayā saṃstuta lokanātha
|
namo'stu te dvādaśanetrabāho ataḥ paraṃ vedmi gatiṃ na te'ham||19||

20skandasya ya idaṃ vipraḥ paṭhejjanma samāhitaḥ
|
śrāvayedbrāhmaṇebhyo yaḥ śṛṇuyādvā dvijeritam||20||

21dhanamāyuryaśo dīptaṃ putrān śatrujayaṃ tathā
|
sa puṣṭituṣṭī samprāpya skandasālokyamāpnuyāt||21||
