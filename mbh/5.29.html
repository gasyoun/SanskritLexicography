Mahābhārata 5.29 (Udyogaparvan)

1vāsudeva uvāca|
avināśaṃ sañjaya pāṇḍavānāmicchāmyahaṃ bhūtimeṣāṃ priyaṃ ca
|
tathā rājño dhṛtarāṣṭrasya sūta samāśaṃse bahuputrasya vṛddhim ||1||

2kāmo hi me sañjaya nityameva nānyadbrūyāṃ tānprati śāmyateti
|
rājñaśca hi priyametacchṛṇomi manye caitatpāṇḍavānāṃ samakṣam ||2||

3suduṣkarastatra śamo hi nūnaṃ pradarśitaḥ sañjaya pāṇḍavena
|
yasmin gṛddho dhṛtarāṣṭraḥ saputraḥ kasmādeṣāṃ kalaho nāvamūrcchet ||3||

4na tvaṃ dharmaṃ vicaraṃ sañjayeha mattaśca jānāsi yudhiṣṭhirācca
|
atho kasmāt sañjaya pāṇḍavasya utsāhinaḥ pūrayataḥ svakarma ||4||

5yathākhyātamāvamataḥ kuṭumbe purā kasmāt sādhuvilopamāttha
|
asmin vidhau vartamāne yathāvaduccāvacā matayo brāhmaṇānām ||5||

6karmaṇā''huḥ siddhimeke paratra hitvā karma vidyayā siddhimeke
|
nābhuñjāno bhakṣyabhojyasya tṛpyedvidvānapīha vihitaṃ brāhmaṇānām ||6||

7yā vai vidyāḥ sādhayantīha karma tāsāṃ phalaṃ vidyate netarāsām
|
tatreha vai dṛṣṭaphalaṃ tu karma pītvodakaṃ śāmyati tṛṣṇayā''rtaḥ ||7||

8so'yaṃ vidhirvihitaḥ karmaṇaiva saṃvartate sañjaya tatra karma
|
tatra yo'nyat karmaṇaḥ sādhu manyenmoghaṃ tasyālapitaṃ durbalasya ||8||

9karmaṇā'mī bhānti devāḥ paratra karmaṇaiveha plavate mātariśvā
|
ahorātre vidadhat karmaṇaiva atandrito nityamudeti sūryaḥ ||9||

10māsārdhamāsānatha nakṣatrayogānatandritaścandramāścābhyupaiti
|
atandrito dahate jātavedāḥ samiddhyamānaḥ karma kurvan prajābhyaḥ ||10||

11atandritā bhāramimaṃ mahāntaṃ bibharti devī pṛthivī balena
|
atandritāḥ śīghramapo vahanti santarpayantyaḥ sarvabhūtāni nadyaḥ ||11||

12atandrito varṣati bhūritejāḥ sannādayannantarikṣaṃ diśaśca
|
atandrito brahmacaryaṃ cacāra śreṣṭhatvamicchan balabhiddevatānām ||12||

13hitvā sukhaṃ manasaśca priyāṇi tena śakraḥ karmaṇā śraiṣṭhyamāpa
|
satyaṃ dharmaṃ pālayannapramatto damaṃ titikṣāṃ samatāṃ priyaṃ ca ||13||

14etāni sarvāṇyupasevamāno sa devarājyaṃ maghavān prāpa mukhyam
|
bṛhaspatirbrahmacaryaṃ cacāra samīhitaḥ saṃśimātmā yathāvat ||14||

15hitvā sukhaṃ pratirudhyendriyāṇi tena devānāmagamadgauravaṃ saḥ
|
tathā nakṣatrāṇi karmaṇā'mutra bhānti rudrādityā vasavo'thāpi viśve ||15||

16yamo rājā vaiśravaṇaḥ kubero gandharvayakṣāpsarasaśca sūta
|
brahmavidyāṃ brahmacaryaṃ kriyāṃ ca niṣevamāṇā ṛṣayo'mutra bhānti ||16||

17jānannimaṃ sarvalokasya dharmaṃ viprendrāṇāṃ kṣatriyāṇāṃ viśāṃ ca
|
sa kasmāttvaṃ jānatāṃ jñānavān san vyāyacchase sañjaya kauravārthe ||17||

18āmnāyeṣu nityasaṃyogamasya tathā'śvamedhe rājasūye ca viddhi
|
saṃyujyate dhanuṣā varmaṇā ca hastyaśvādyai rathaśastraiśca bhūyaḥ ||18||

19te cedime kauravāṇāmupāyamavagaccheyuravadhenaiva pārthāḥ
|
dharmatrāṇaṃ puṇyameṣāṃ kṛtaṃ syādārye vṛtte bhīmasenaṃ nigṛhya ||19||

20te cetpitrye karmaṇi vartamānā āpadyeran diṣṭavaśena mṛtyum
|
yathāśaktyā pūrayantaḥ svakarma tadapyeṣāṃ nidhanaṃ syāt praśastam ||20||

21utāho tvaṃ manyase śāmyameva rājñāṃ yuddhe vartate dharmatantram
|
ayuddhe vā vartate dharmatantraṃ tathaiva te vācamimāṃ śrṛṇomi ||21||

22cāturvarṇyasya prathamaṃ saṃvibhāgamavekṣya tvaṃ sañjaya svaṃ ca karma
|
niśamyātho pāṇḍavānāṃ ca karma praśaṃsa vā ninda vā yā matiste||22||

23adhīyīta brāhmaṇo vai yajeta dadyādīyāttīrthamukhyāni caiva
|
adhyāpayedyājayeccāpi yājyān pratigrahān vā vihitān pratīcchet ||23||

24tathā rājanyo rakṣaṇaṃ vai prajānāṃ kṛtvā dharmeṇāpramatto'tha dattvā
|
yajñairiṣṭvā sarvavedānadhītya dārān kṛtvā puṇyakṛdāvasedgṛhān ||24||

25sa dharmātmā dharmamadhītya puṇyaṃ yadṛcchayā vrajati brahmalokam
|
vaiśyo'dhītya kṛṣigorakṣapaṇyairvittaṃ cinvan pālayannapramattaḥ ||25||

26priyaṃ kurvan brāhmaṇakṣatriyāṇāṃ dharmaśīlaḥ puṇyakṛdāvasedgṛhān
|
paricaryā vandanaṃ brāhmaṇānāṃ nādhīyīta pratiṣiddho'sya yajñaḥ
|
nityotthito bhūtaye'tandritaḥ syādevaṃ smṛtaḥ śūdradharmaḥ purāṇaḥ ||26||

27etān rājā pālayannapramatto niyojayan sarvavarṇān svadharme
|
akāmātmā samavṛttiḥ prajāsu nādhārmikānanurudhyeta kāmān ||27||

28śreyāṃstasmādyadi vidyeta kaścidabhijñātaḥ sarvadharmopapannaḥ
|
sa taṃ draṣṭumanuśiṣyan prajānāṃ na caitadbudhyediti tasminnasādhuḥ ||28||

29yadā gṛdhyet parabhūtau nṛśaṃso vidhiprakopādbalamādadānaḥ
|
tato rājñāmabhavadyuddhametattatra jātaṃ varma śastraṃ dhanuśca||29||

30indreṇaitaddasyuvadhāya karma utpāditaṃ varma śastraṃ dhanuśca ||30||

31tatra puṇyaṃ dasyuvadhena labhyate so'yaṃ doṣaḥ kurubhistīvrarūpaḥ
|
adharmajñairdharmamabudhyamānaiḥ prādurbhūtaḥ sañjaya sādhu tanna ||31||

32tatra rājā dhṛtarāṣṭraḥ saputro dharmyaṃ haret pāṇḍavānāmakasmāt
|
nāvekṣante rājadharmaṃ purāṇaṃ tadanvayāḥ kuravaḥ sarva eva ||32||

33steno haredyatra dhanaṃ hyadṛṣṭaḥ prasahya vā yatra hareta dṛṣṭaḥ
|
ubhau garhyau bhavataḥ sañjayaitau kiṃ vai pṛthaktvaṃ dhṛtarāṣṭrasya putre ||33||

34so'yaṃ lobhānmanyate dharmametaṃ yamicchati krodhavaśānugāmī
|
bhāgaḥ punaḥ pāṇḍavānāṃ niviṣṭastaṃ naḥ kasmādādadīran pare vai ||34||

35asmin pade yuddhyatāṃ no vadho'pi ślāghyaḥ pitryaṃ pararājyādviśiṣṭam
|
etān dharmān kauravāṇāṃ purāṇānācakṣīthāḥ sañjaya rājamadhye ||35||

36ete madānmṛtyuvaśābhipannāḥ samānītā dhārtarāṣṭreṇa mūḍhāḥ
|
idaṃ punaḥ karma pāpīya eva sabhāmadhye paśya vattaṃ kurūṇām ||36||

37priyāṃ bhāryāṃ draupadīṃ pāṇḍavānāṃ yaśasvinīṃ śīlavṛttopapannām
|
yadupaikṣanta kuravo bhīṣmamukhyāḥ kāmānugenoparuddhāṃ vrajantīm ||37||

38tāṃ cettadā te sakumāravṛddhā avārayiṣyan kuravaḥ sametāḥ
|
mama priyaṃ dhṛtarāṣṭro'kariṣyat putrāṇāṃ ca kṛtamasyābhaviṣyat ||38||

39duḥśāsanaḥ pratilomyānnināya sabhāmadhye śvaśurāṇāṃ ca kṛṣṇām
|
sā tatra nītā karuṇaṃ vyapekṣya nānyaṃ kṣatturnāthamavāpa kiṃcit ||39||

40kārpaṇyādeva sahitāstatra bhūpā nāśaknuvan prativaktuṃ sabhāyām
|
ekaḥ kṣattā dharmyamarthaṃ bruvāṇo dharmabuddhyā pratyuvācālpabuddhim ||40||

41abuddhvā tvaṃ dharmametaṃ sabhāyāmathecchase pāṇḍavasyopadeṣṭum
|
kṛṣṇā tvetat karma cakāra śuddhaṃ suduṣkaraṃ tatra sabhāṃ sametya ||41||

42yena kṛcchrāt pāṇḍavānujjahāra tathā''tmānaṃ nauriva sāgaraughāt
|
yatrābravīt sūtaputraḥ sabhāyāṃ kṛṣṇāṃ sthitāṃ śvaśurāṇāṃ samīpe ||42||

43na te gatirvidyate yājñaseni prapadya dāsī dhārtarāṣṭrasya veśma
|
parājitāste patayo na santi patiṃ cānyaṃ bhāmini tvaṃ vṛṇīṣva ||43||

44yo bībhatsorhṛdaye prota āsīdasthi chindanmarmaghātī sughoraḥ
|
karṇāccharo vāṅbhayastigmatejāḥ pratiṣṭhito hṛdaye phālgunasya ||44||

45kṛṣṇājināni paridhitsamānān duḥśāsanaḥ kaṭukānyabhyabhāṣat
|
ete sarve ṣaṇḍhatilā vinaṣṭāḥ kṣayaṃ gatā narakaṃ dīrghakālam ||45||

46gāndhārarājaḥ śakunirnikṛtyā yadabravīt dyūtakāle sa pārtham
|
parājito nandanaḥ kiṃ tavāsti kṛṣṇayā tvaṃ dīvya vai yājñasenyā||46||

47jānāsi tvaṃ sañjaya sarvametat dyūte vākyaṃ garhyamevaṃ yathoktam
|
svayaṃ tvahaṃ prārthaye tatra gantuṃ samādhātuṃ kāryametadvipannam||47||

48ahāpayitvā yadi pāṇḍavārthaṃ śamaṃ kurūṇāmapi cecchakeyam
|
puṇyaṃ ca me syāccaritaṃ mahodayaṃ mucyeraṃśca kuravo mṛtyupāśāt ||48||

49api me vācaṃ bhāṣamāṇasya kāvyāṃ dharmārāmāmarthavatīmahiṃsrām
|
avekṣeran dhārtarāṣṭrāḥ samakṣaṃ māṃ ca prāptaṃ kuravaḥ pūjayeyuḥ ||49||

50ato'nyathā rathinā phālgunena bhīmena caivāhavadaṃśitena
|
parāsiktān dhārtarāṣṭrāṃśca viddhi pradahyamānān karmaṇā svena pāpān ||50||

51parājitān pāṇḍaveyāṃstu vāco raudrā rūkṣā bhāṣate dhārtarāṣṭraḥ
|
gadāhasto bhīmaseno'pramatto duryodhanaṃ smārayitā hi kāle ||51||

52suyodhano manyumayo mahādrumaḥ skandhaḥ karṇaḥ śakunistasya śākhā
|
duḥśāsanaḥ puṣpaphale samṛddhe mūlaṃ rājā dhṛtarāṣṭro'manīṣī ||52||

53yudhiṣṭhiro dharmamayo mahādrumaḥ skandho'rjuno bhīmaseno'sya śākhā
|
mādrīputrau puṣpaphale samṛddhe mūlaṃ tvahaṃ brahma ca brāhmaṇāśca
|
vanaṃ rājā dhṛtarāṣṭraḥ saputro vyāghraste vai sañjaya pāṇḍuputrāḥ||53||

54mā vanaṃ chindhi savyāghraṃ mā vyāghrā'nīnaśanvanāt ||54||

55nirvano vadhyate vyāghro nirvyāghraṃ chidyate vanam
|
tasmādvyāghro vanaṃ rakṣedvanaṃ vyāghraṃ ca pālayet ||55||

56latādharmā dhārtarāṣṭrāḥ śālāḥ sañjaya pāṇḍavāḥ
|
na latā vardhate jātu mahādrumamanāśritā ||56||

57sthitāḥ śuśrūṣituṃ pārthāḥ sthitā yoddhumarindamāḥ
|
yatkṛtyaṃ dhṛtarāṣṭrasya tatkarotu narādhipaḥ ||57||

58sthitāḥ śame mahātmānaḥ pāṇḍavā dharmacāriṇaḥ
|
yodhāḥ samarthāstadvidvannācakṣīthā yathātatham ||58||
