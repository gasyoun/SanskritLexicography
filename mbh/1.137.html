Mahābhārata 1.137 (Ādiparvan)

1vaiśaṃpāyana uvāca|
tataḥ srastottarapaṭaḥ saprasvedaḥ savepathuḥ
|
viveśādhiratho raṅgaṃ yaṣṭiprāṇo hvayanniva||1||

2tamālokya dhanustyaktvā pitṛgauravayantritaḥ
|
karṇo'bhiṣekārdraśirāḥ śirasā samavandata||2||

3tataḥ pādāvavacchādya paṭāntena sasaṃbhramaḥ
|
putreti paripūrṇārthamabravīdrathasārathim||3||

4pariṣvajya ca tasyātha mūrdhānaṃ snehaviklavaḥ
|
aṅgarājyābhiṣekārdramaśrubhiḥ siṣice punaḥ||4||

5taṃ dṛṣṭvā sūtaputro'yamiti saṃcintya pāṇḍavaḥ
|
bhīmasenastadā vākyamabravītprahasanniva||5||

6na tvamarhasi pārthena sūtaputra raṇe vadham
|
kulasya sadṛśastūrṇaṃ pratodo gṛhyatāṃ tvayā||6||

7aṅgarājyaṃ ca nārhastvamupabhoktuṃ narādhama
|
śvā hutāśasamīpasthaṃ puroḍāśamivādhvare||7||

8evamuktastataḥ karṇaḥ kiṃcitprasphuritādharaḥ
|
gaganasthaṃ viniḥśvasya divākaramudaikṣata||8||

9tato duryodhanaḥ kopādutpapāta mahābalaḥ
|
bhrātṛpadmavanāttasmānmadotkaṭa iva dvipaḥ||9||

10so'bravīdbhīmakarmāṇaṃ bhīmasenamavasthitam
|
vṛkodara na yuktaṃ te vacanaṃ vaktumīdṛśam||10||

11kṣatriyāṇāṃ balaṃ jyaṣṭhaṃ yoktavyaṃ kṣatrabandhunā
|
śūrāṇāṃ ca nadīnāṃ ca durvidāḥ prabhavaḥ kila||11||

12salilādutthito vahniryena vyāptaṃ carācaram
|
dadhīcasyāsthito vajraṃ kṛtaṃ dānavasūdanam||12||

13āgneyaḥ kṛttikāputro raudro gāṅgeya ityapi
|
śrūyate bhagavāndevaḥ sarvaguhyamayo guhaḥ||13||

14kṣatriyebhyaśca ye jātā brāhmaṇāste ca te śrutāḥ
|
viśvāmitraprabhṛtayaḥ prāptā brahmatvamavyayam||14||

15ācāryaḥ kalaśājjāto droṇaḥ śastrabhṛtāṃ varaḥ
|
gautamasyānvavāye ca śarastambācca gautamaḥ||15||

16bhavatāṃ ca yathā janma tadapyāgamitaṃ mayā
|
sakuṇḍalaṃ sakavacaṃ sarvalakṣaṇalakṣitam
|
kathamādityasadṛśaṃ mṛgī vyāghraṃ janiṣyati||16||

17pṛthivīrājyamarho'yaṃ nāṅgarājyaṃ nareśvaraḥ
|
anena bāhuvīryeṇa mayā cājñānuvartinā||17||

18yasya vā manujasyedaṃ na kṣāntaṃ madviceṣṭitam
|
rathamāruhya padbhyāṃ sa vināmayatu kārmukam||18||

19tataḥ sarvasya raṅgasya hāhākāro mahānabhūt
|
sādhuvādānusaṃbaddhaḥ sūryaścāstamupāgamat||19||

20tato duryodhanaḥ karṇamālambyāgrakare nṛpaḥ
|
dīpikāgnikṛtālokastasmādraṅgādviniryayau||20||

21pāṇḍavāśca sahadroṇāḥ sakṛpāśca viśāṃpate
|
bhīṣmeṇa sahitāḥ sarve yayuḥ svaṃ svaṃ niveśanam||21||

22arjuneti janaḥ kaścitkaścitkarṇeti bhārata
|
kaścidduryodhanetyevaṃ bruvantaḥ prasthitāstadā||22||

23kuntyāśca pratyabhijñāya divyalakṣaṇasūcitam
|
putramaṅgeśvaraṃ snehācchannā prītirajāyata||23||

24duryodhanasyāpi tadā karṇamāsādya pārthiva
|
bhayamarjunasaṃjātaṃ kṣipramantaradhīyata||24||

25sa cāpi vīraḥ kṛtaśastraniśramaḥ pareṇa sāmnā'bhyavadatsuyodhanam
|
yudhiṣṭhirasyāpyabhavattadā matirna karṇatulyo'sti dhanurdharaḥ kṣitau||25||
