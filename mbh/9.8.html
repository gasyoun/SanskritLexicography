Mahābhārata 9.8 (Śalyaparvan)

1sañjaya uvāca|
vyatītāyāṃ rajanyāṃ tu rājā duryodhanastadā
|
abravīttāvakānsarvānsannahyantāṃ mahārathāḥ||1||

2rājñaśca matamājñāya samanahyata sā camūḥ
|
ayojayan rathāṃstūrṇaṃ paryadhāvaṃstathā pare||2||

3akalpyanta ca mātaṅgāḥ samanahyanta pattayaḥ
|
rathānāstaraṇopetāṃścakruranye sahasraśaḥ||3||

4vāditrāṇāṃ ca ninadaḥ prādurāsīdviśāmpate
|
āyodhanārthaṃ yodhānāṃ balānāṃ cāpyudīryatām||4||

5tato balāni sarvāṇi senāśiṣṭāni bhārata
|
prasthitāni vyadṛśyanta mṛtyuṃ kṛtvā nivartanam||5||

6śalyaṃ senāpatiṃ kṛtvā madrarājaṃ mahārathāḥ
|
pravibhajya balaṃ sarvamanīkeṣu vyavasthitāḥ||6||

7tataḥ sarve samāgamya putreṇa tava sainikāḥ
|
kṛpaśca kṛtavarmā ca drauṇiḥ śalyo'tha saubalaḥ||7||

8anye ca pārthivāḥ śeṣāḥ samayaṃ cakrurādṛtāḥ
|
na na ekena yoddhavyaṃ kathañcidapi pāṇḍavaiḥ||8||

9yo hyekaḥ pāṇḍavairyudhyedyo vā yudhyantamutsṛjet
|
sa pañcabhirbhavedyuktaḥ pātakaicopapātakaiḥ||9||

10anyonyaṃ parirakṣadbhiryoddhavyaṃ sahitaiśca ha
|
evaṃ te samayaṃ kṛtvā sarve tatra mahārathāḥ||10||

11madrarājaṃ puraskṛtya tūrṇamabhyadravan parān
|
tathaiva pāṇḍavā rājan vyūhya sainyaṃ mahāraṇe||11||

12abhyayuḥ kauravān rājan yotsyamānāḥ samantataḥ
|
tadbalaṃ bharataśreṣṭha kṣubdhārṇavasamasvanam||12||

13samuddhūtārṇavākāramuddhūtarathakuñjaram
|
dhṛtarāṣṭra uvāca|
droṇasya caiva bhīṣmasya rādheyasya ca me śrutam||13||

14pātanaṃ śaṃsa me bhūyaḥ śalyasyātha sutasya me
|
kathaṃ raṇe hataḥ śalyo dharmarājena sañjaya||14||

15bhīmena ca mahābāhuḥ putro duryodhano mama
|
sañjaya uvāca|
kṣayaṃ manuṣyadehānāṃ tathā nāgāśvasaṃkṣayam||15||

16śṛṇu rājan sthiro bhūtvā saṅgrāmaṃ śaṃsato mama
|
āśā balavatī rājan putrāṇāṃ te'bhavattadā||16||

17hate droṇe ca bhīṣme ca sūtaputre ca pātite
|
śalyaḥ pārthānraṇe sarvānnihaniṣyati māriṣa||17||

18tāmāśāṃ hṛdaye kṛtvā samāśvasya ca bhārata
|
madrarājaṃ ca samare samāśritya mahāratham||18||

19nāthavantaṃ tadā''tmānamamanyanta sutāstava
|
yadā karṇe hate pārthāḥ siṃhanādaṃ pracakrire||19||

20tadā tu tāvakān rājannāviveśa mahadbhayam
|
tānsamāśvāsya yodhāstu madrarājaḥ pratāpavān||20||

21vyūhya vyūhaṃ mahārāja sarvatobhadramṛddhimat
|
pratyudyayau raṇe pārthānmadrarājaḥ pratāpavān||21||

22vidhunvankārmukaṃ citraṃ bhāraghnaṃ vegavattaram
|
rathapravaramāsthāya saindhavāśvaṃ mahārathaḥ||22||

23tasya sūto mahārāja rathastho'śobhayadratham
|
sa tena saṃvṛto vīro rathenāmitrakarṣaṇaḥ||23||

24tasthau śūro mahārāja putrāṇāṃ te bhayapraṇut
|
prayāṇe madrarājo'bhūnmukhaṃ vyūhasya daṃśitaḥ||24||

25madrakaiḥ sihato vīraiḥ karṇaputraiśca durjayaiḥ
|
savye'bhūtkṛtavarmā ca trigartaiḥ parivāritaḥ||25||

26gautamo dakṣiṇe pārśve śakaiśca yavanaiḥ saha
|
aśvatthāmā pṛṣṭhato'bhūtkāmbojaiḥ parivāritaḥ||26||

27duryodhano'bhavanmadhye rakṣitaḥ kurupuṅgavaiḥ
|
hayānīkena mahatā saubalaścāpi saṃvṛtaḥ||27||

28prayayau sarvasainyena kaitavyaśca mahārathaḥ
|
pāṇḍavāśca maheṣvāsā vyūhya sainyamarindamāḥ||28||

29tridhā bhūtā mahārāja tava sainyamupādravan
|
dhṛṣṭadyumnaḥ śikhaṇḍī ca sātyakiśca mahārathaḥ||29||

30śalyasya vāhinīṃ hantumabhidudruvurāhave
|
tato yudhiṣṭhiro rājā svenānīkena saṃvṛtaḥ||30||

31śalyamevābhidudrāva jighāṃsurbharatarṣabhaḥ
|
hārdikyaṃ ca maheṣvāsasamarjunaḥ śatrusainyahā||31||

32saṃśaptakagaṇāṃścaiva vegito'bhividudruve
|
gautamaṃ bhīmaseno vai somakāśca mahārathāḥ||32||

33abhyadravanta rājendra jighāṃsantaḥ parān yudhi
|
mādrīputrau tu śakunimulūkaṃ ca mahāratham||33||

34sasainyau sahasainyau tāvupatasthaturāhave
|
tathaivāyutaśo yodhāstāvakāḥ pāṇḍavān raṇe||34||

35abhyavartanta saṅkruddhā vividhāyudhapāṇayaḥ
|
dhṛtarāṣṭra uvāca|
hate bhīṣme maheṣvāse droṇe karṇe mahārathe||35||

36kuruṣvalpāvaśiṣṭeṣu pāṇḍaveṣu ca saṃyuge
|
susaṃrabdheṣu pārtheṣu parākrānteṣu sañjaya||36||

37māmakānāṃ pareṣāṃ ca kiṃ śiṣṭamabhavadbalam
|
sañjaya uvāca|
yathā vayaṃ pare rājan yuddhāya samupasthitāḥ||37||

38yāvaccāsīdbalaṃ śiṣṭaṃ saṅgrāme tannibodha me
|
ekādaśa sahasrāṇi rathānāṃ bharatarṣabha||38||

39daśa dantisahasrāṇi sapta caiva śatāni ca
|
pūrṇe śatasahasre dve hayānāṃ tatra bhārata||39||

40pattikoṭyastathā tisro balametattavābhavat
|
rathānāṃ ṣaṭsahasrāṇi ṣaṭsahasrāśca kuñjarāḥ||40||

41daśa cāśvasahasrāṇi pattikoṭī ca bhārata
|
etadbalaṃ pāṇḍavānāmabhavaccheṣamāhave||41||

42eta eva samājagmuryuddhāya bharatarṣabha
|
evaṃ vibhajya rājendra madrarājavaśe sthitāḥ||42||

43pāṇḍavānpratyudīyuste jayagṛddhāḥ pramanyavaḥ
|
tathaiva pāṇḍavāḥ śūrāḥ samare jitakāśinaḥ||43||

44upayātā naravyāghrāḥ pañcālāśca yaśasvinaḥ
|
ime te ca balaughena parasparavadhaiṣiṇaḥ||44||

45upayātā naravyāghrāḥ pūrvāṃ sandhyāṃ prati prabho
|
tataḥ pravarvṛte yuddhaṃ ghorarūpaṃ bhayānakam
|
tāvakānāṃ pareṣāṃ ca nighnatāmitaretaram||45||
