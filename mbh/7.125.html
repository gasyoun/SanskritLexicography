Mahābhārata 7.125 (Droṇaparvan)

1sañjaya uvāca|
aparāhṇe mahārāja saṃgrāmaḥ sumahānabhūt
|
parjanyasamanirghoṣaḥ punardroṇasya somakaiḥ ||1||

2śoṇāśvaṃ rathamāsthāya naravīraḥ samāhitaḥ
|
samare'bhyadravatpāṇḍūñjavamāsthāya madhyamam ||2||

3tava priyahite yukto maheṣvāso mahābalaḥ
|
citrapuṃkhaiḥ śitairbāṇaiḥ phalaśottamasaṃbhavaḥ ||3||

4varānvarānhi yodhānāṃ vicinvanniva bhārata
|
ākrīḍata raṇe rājan bhāradvājaḥ pratāpavān ||4||

5tamabhyayādbṛhatkṣatraḥ kekayānāṃ mahārathaḥ
|
bhrātṝṇāṃ nṛpapañcānāṃ śreṣṭhaḥ samarakarkaśaḥ ||5||

6vimuñcanviśikhāṃstīkṣṇānācāryaṃ bhṛśamārdayat
|
mahāmegho yathā varṣaṃ vimuñcangandhamādane ||6||

7tasya droṇo mahārāja svarṇapuṃkhāñchilāśitān
|
preṣayāmāsa saṃkruddhaḥ sāyakāndaśa pañca ca ||7||

8tāṃstu droṇavinirmuktān kuddhāśīviṣasannibhān
|
ekaikaṃ pañcabhirbāṇairyudhi ciccheda hṛṣṭavat ||8||

9tadasya lāghavaṃ dṛṣṭvā prahasya dvijapuṅgavaḥ
|
preṣayāmāsa viśikhānaṣṭau sannataparvaṇaḥ ||9||

10tān dṛṣṭvā patatastūrṇaṃ droṇacāpacyutāñśarān
|
avārayaccharaireva tāvadbhirniśitairmṛdhe ||10||

11tato'bhavanmahārāja tava sainyasya vismayaḥ
|
bṛhatkṣatreṇa tat karma kṛtaṃ dṛṣṭvā suduṣkaram ||11||

12tato droṇo mahārāja bṛhatkṣatraṃ viśeṣayan
|
prāduścakre raṇe divyaṃ brāhmamastraṃ sudurjayam ||12||

13kaikeyo'straṃ samālokya muktaṃ droṇena saṃyuge
|
brahmāstreṇaiva rājendra brāhmamastramaśātayat ||13||

14tato'stre nihate brāhme bṛhatkṣatrastu bhārata
|
vivyādha brāhmaṇaṃ ṣaṣṭyā svarṇapuṅkhaiḥ śilāśitaiḥ ||14||

15taṃ droṇo dvipadāṃ śreṣṭho nārācena samārpayat
|
sa tasya kavacaṃ bhittvā prāviśaddharaṇītalam ||15||

16kṛṣṇasarpo yathā mukto valmīkaṃ nṛpasattama
|
tathā'tyagānmahīṃ bāṇo bhittvā kaikeyamāhave ||16||

17so'tividdho mahārāja kaikeyo droṇasāyakaiḥ
|
krodhena mahatā''viṣṭo vyāvṛtya nayane śubhe ||17||

18droṇaṃ vivyādha saptatyā svarṇapuṅkhaiḥ śilāśitaiḥ
|
sārathiṃ cāsya bāṇena bhṛśaṃ marmasvatāḍayat ||18||

19droṇastu bahubhirviddho bṛhatkṣatreṇa māriṣa
|
asṛjadviśikhāṃstīkṣṇān kaikeyasya rathaṃ prati ||19||

20vyākulīkṛtya taṃ droṇo bṛhatkṣatraṃ mahāratham
|
aśvāṃścaturbhirnyavadhīccaturo'sya patatribhiḥ ||20||

21sūtaṃ caikena bāṇena rathanīḍāda‌pātayat
|
dvābhyāṃ dhvajaṃ ca cchatraṃ ca cchitvā bhūmāvapātayat ||21||

22tataḥ sādhuvisṛṣṭena nārācena dvijarṣabhaḥ
|
hṛdyavidhyadbṛhatkṣatraṃ sa cchinnahṛda‌yo'patat ||22||

23bṛhatkṣatre hate rājan kekayānāṃ mahārathe
|
śaiśupālirabhikruddho yantāramidamabravīt ||23||

24sārathe yāhi yatraiṣa droṇastiṣṭhati daṃśitaḥ
|
vinighnan kekayān sarvān pañcālānāṃ ca vāhinīm ||24||

25tasya tadvacanaṃ śrutvā sārathī rathināṃ varam
|
droṇāya prāpayāmāsa kāmbojairjavanairhayaiḥ ||25||

26dhṛṣṭaketuśca cedīnāmṛṣabho'tibaloditaḥ
|
vadhāyābhyadravaddroṇaṃ pataṅga iva pāvakam ||26||

27so'vidhyata tadā droṇaṃ ṣaṣṭyā sāśvarathadhvajam
|
punaścānyaiḥ śaraistīkṣṇaiḥ suptaṃ vyāghraṃ tuda‌nniva ||27||

28tasya droṇo dhanurmadhye kṣurapreṇa śitena ca
|
cakarta gārdhrapatreṇa yatamānasya śuṣmiṇaḥ ||28||

29athānyaddhanurādāya śaiśupālirmahārathaḥ
|
vivyādha sāyakairdroṇaṃ kaṅkabarhiṇavājitaiḥ ||29||

30tasya droṇo hayān hatvā caturbhiścaturaḥ śaraiḥ
|
sāratheśca śiraḥ kāyāccakarta prahasanniva ||30||

31athainaṃ pañcaviṃśatyā sāyakānāṃ samārpayat
|
avaplutya rathāccaidyo gadāmādāya satvaraḥ ||31||

32bhāradvājāya cikṣepa ruṣitāmiva pannagīm
|
tāmāpatantīmālokya kālarātrimivodyatām ||32||

33aśmasāramayīṃ gurvīṃ tapanīyavibhūṣitām
|
śarairanekasāhasrairbhāradvājo'cchinacchitaiḥ ||33||

34sā chinnā bahubhirbāṇairbhāradvājena māriṣa
|
gadā papāta kauravya nādayantī dharātalam ||34||

35gadāṃ vinihatāṃ dṛṣṭvā dhṛṣṭaketuramarṣaṇaḥ
|
tomaraṃ vyasṛjadvīraḥ śaktiṃ ca kanakojjvalām ||35||

36tomaraṃ pañcabhirbhittvā śaktiṃ ciccheda pañcabhiḥ
|
tau jagmaturmahīṃ chinnau sarpāviva garutmatā ||36||

37tato'sya viśikhaṃ tīkṣṇaṃ vadhāya vadhakāṃkṣiṇaḥ
|
preṣayāmāsa samare bhāradvājaḥ pratāpavān ||37||

38sa tasya kavacaṃ bhittvā hṛdayaṃ cāmitaujasaḥ
|
abhyagāddharaṇīṃ bāṇo haṃsaḥ padmavanaṃ yathā ||38||

39pataṅgaṃ hi graseccāṣo yathā kṣudraṃ bubhukṣitaḥ
|
tathā droṇo'prasacchūro dhṛṣṭaketuṃ mahāhave ||39||

40nihate cedirāje tu tat khaṇḍaṃ pitryamāviśat
|
amarṣavaśamāpannaḥ putro'sya paramāstravit ||40||

41tamapi prahasan droṇaḥ śarairnitye yamakṣayam
|
mahāvyāghro mahāraṇye mṛgaśāvaṃ yathā balī ||41||

42teṣu prakṣīyamāṇeṣu pāṇḍaveyeṣu bhārata
|
jarāsandhasuto vīraḥ svayaṃ droṇamupādravat ||42||

43sa tu droṇaṃ mahābāhuḥ śaradhārābhirāhave
|
adṛśyamakarottūrṇaṃ jalado bhāskaraṃ yathā ||43||

44tasya tallāghavaṃ dṛṣṭvā droṇaḥ kṣatriyamardanaḥ
|
vyasṛjat sāyakāṃstūrṇaṃ śataśo'tha sahasraśaḥ ||44||

45chādayitvā raṇe droṇo rathasvaṃ rathināṃ varam
|
jārāsandhiṃ jaghānāśu miṣatāṃ sarvadhanvinām ||45||

46yo yaḥ sma nīyate tatra taṃ droṇo hyantakopamaḥ
|
ādatta sarvabhūtāni prāpte kāle yathā'ntakaḥ ||46||

47tato droṇo mahārāja nāma viśrāvya saṃyuge
|
śarairanekasāhasraiḥ pāṇḍaveyān samāvṛṇot ||47||

48te tu nāmāṅkitā bāṇā droṇenāstāḥ śilāśitāḥ
|
narānnāgān hayāṃścaiva nijaghnuḥ śataśo mṛdhe ||48||

49te vadhyamānā droṇena śakreṇeva mahāsurāḥ
|
samakampanta pañcālā gāvaḥ śītārditā iva ||49||

50tato niṣṭhānako ghoraḥ pāṇḍavānāmajāyata
|
droṇena vadhyamāneṣu sainyeṣu bharatarṣabha ||50||

51pratāpyamānāḥ sūryeṇa hanyamānāśca sāyakaiḥ
|
anvapadyanta pañcālāstadā santrastacetasaḥ ||51||

52mohitā bāṇajālena bhāradvājena saṃyuge
|
ūrugrāhagṛhītānāṃ pañcālānāṃ mahārathāḥ ||52||

53cedayaśca mahārāja suñjayāḥ kāśikosalāḥ
|
abhyadravanta saṃhṛṣṭā bhāradvājaṃ yuyutsayā ||53||

54bruvantaśca raṇe'nyonyaṃ cedipañcālasṛñjayāḥ
|
hata droṇaṃ hata droṇamiti te droṇamabhyayuḥ ||54||

55yatantaḥ puruṣavyāghrāḥ sarvaśaktyā mahādyutim
|
ninīṣavo raṇe droṇaṃ yamasya sadanaṃ prati ||55||

56yatamānāṃstu tānvīrānbhāradvājaḥ śilīmukhaiḥ
|
yamāya preṣayāmāsa cedimukhyān viśeṣataḥ ||56||

57teṣu prakṣīyamāṇeṣu cedimukhyeṣu sarvaśaḥ
|
pañcālāḥ samakampanta droṇasāyakapīḍitāḥ ||57||

58prākrośan bhīmasenaṃ te dhṛṣṭadyumnaṃ ca bhārata
|
dṛṣṭvā droṇasya karmāṇi tathā rūpāṇi māriṣa ||58||

59brāhmaṇena tapo nūnaṃ caritaṃ duścaraṃ mahat
|
tathā hi yudhi saṃkruddho dahati kṣatriyarṣabhān ||59||

60dharmo yuddhaṃ kṣatriyasya brāhmaṇasya parantapaḥ
|
tapasvī kṛtavidyaśca prekṣitenāpi nirdahet ||60||

61droṇāgnimastrasaṃsparśaṃ praviṣṭāḥ kṣatriyarṣabhāḥ
|
bahavo dustaraṃ ghoraṃ yatrādahyanta bhārata ||61||

62yathābalaṃ yathotsāhaṃ yathāsattvaṃ mahādyutiḥ
|
mohayan sarvabhūtāni droṇo hanti balāni naḥ ||62||

63teṣāṃ tadvacanaṃ śrutvā kṣatradharmā vyavasthitaḥ
|
ardhacandreṇa ciccheda kṣatradharmā mahābalaḥ ||63||

64krodhasaṃvignamanaso droṇasya saśaraṃ dhanuḥ
|
sa saṃrabdhataro bhūtvā droṇaḥ kṣatriyamardanaḥ ||64||

65anyatkārmukamādāya bhāsvaraṃ vegavattaram
|
tatrādhāya śaraṃ tīkṣṇaṃ parānīkaviśātanam ||65||

66ākarṇapūrṇamācāryo balavānabhyavāsṛjat
|
sa hatyā kṣatradharmāṇaṃ jagāma dharaṇītalam ||66||

67sa bhinnahṛdayo vāhānnyapatanmedinītale
|
tataḥ sainyānyakampanta dhṛṣṭadyumnasute hate ||67||

68atha droṇaṃ samārohaccekitāno mahābalaḥ
|
sa droṇaṃ daśabhirviddhvā pratyaviddhyatstanāntare ||68||

69caturbhiḥ sārathiṃ cāsya caturbhiścaturo hayān
|
tamācāryastribhirbāṇairbāhvorurasi cārpayat ||69||

70dhvajaṃ saptabhirunmathya yantāramavadhīttribhiḥ
|
tasya sūte hate te'śvā rathamādāya vidrutāḥ ||70||

71samare śarasaṃvātā bhāradvājena māriṣa
|
cekitānarathaṃ dṛṣṭvā hatāśvaṃ hatasārathim ||71||

72tān sametān raṇe śūrāṃścedipañcālasṛñjayān
|
samantāddrāvayan droṇo bahvaśobhata māriṣa ||72||

73ākarṇapalitaḥ śyāmo vayasā'śītipañcakaḥ
|
raṇe paryacaraddroṇo vṛddhaḥ ṣoḍaśavarṣayat ||73||

74atha droṇaṃ mahārāja vicarantamabhītavat
|
vajrahastamamanyanta śatravaḥ śatrusūdanam ||74||

75tato'bravīnmahābāhurdrupado buddhimānnṛpa
|
lubdho'yaṃ kṣatriyān hanti vyāghraḥ kṣudramṛgāniva ||75||

76kṛcchrān duryodhano lokān pāpaḥ prāpsyati durmatiḥ
|
yasya lobhādvinihatāḥ samare kṣatriyarṣabhāḥ ||76||

77śataśaḥ śerate bhūmau nikṛttā govṛṣā iva
|
rudhireṇa parītāṅgāḥ śvaśṛgālādanīkṛtāḥ ||77||

78evamuktvā mahārāja drupado'kṣauhiṇīpatiḥ
|
puraskṛtya raṇe pārthān droṇamabhyaddravaddrutam ||78||
