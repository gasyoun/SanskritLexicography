Mahābhārata 2.40 (Sabhāparvan)

1vaiśampāyana uvāca|
tataḥ sāgarasaṅkāśaṃ dṛṣṭvā nṛpatimaṇḍalam
|
saṃvartavātābhihataṃ bhīmaṃ kṣubdhamivārṇavam||1||

2roṣātpracalitaṃ sarvamidamāha yudhiṣṭhiraḥ
|
bhīṣmaṃ matimatāṃ mukhyaṃ vṛddhaṃ kurupitāmaham
|
bṛhaspatiṃ bṛhattejāḥ puruhūta ivārihā||2||

3asau roṣātpracalito mahānnṛpatisāgaraḥ
|
atra yatpratipattavyaṃ tanme brūhi pitāmaha||3||

4yajñasya ca na vighnaḥ syātprajānāṃ ca hitaṃ bhavet
|
yathā sarvatra tatsarvaṃ brūhi me'dya pitāmaha||4||

5ityuktavati dharmajñe dharmarāje yudhiṣṭhire
|
uvācedaṃ vaco bhīṣmastataḥ kurupitāmahaḥ||5||

6mā bhaistvaṃ kuruśārdūla śvā siṃhaṃ hantumarhati
|
śivaḥ panthāḥ sunīto'tra mayā pūrvataraṃ vṛtaḥ||6||

7prasupte hi yathā siṃhe śvānastasminsamāgatāḥ
|
bhaṣeyuḥ sahitāḥ sarve tatheme vasudhādhipāḥ||7||

8vṛṣṇisiṃhasya suptasya tathā'mī pramuke sthitāḥ
|
bhaṣante tāta saṅkruddhāḥ śvānaḥ siṃhasya sannidhau||8||

9na hi sambudhyate yāvatsuptaḥ siṃha ivācyutaḥ
|
tena siṃhīkarotyetānnṛsiṃhaścedipuṅgavaḥ||9||

10pārthivānpārthivaśreṣṭhaḥ śiśupālo'pyacetanaḥ
|
sarvānsarvātmanā tāta netukāmo yamakṣayam||10||

11nūnametatsamādātuṃ punaricchatyadhokṣajaḥ
|
yadasya śiśupālasya tejastiṣṭhati bhārata||11||

12viplutā cāsya bhadraṃ te buddhirbuddhimatāṃ vara
|
cedirājasya kaunteya sarveṣāṃ ca mahīkṣitam||12||

13ādātuṃ ca naravyāghro yaṃ yamicchatyayaṃ tadā
|
tasya viplavate buddhirevaṃ cedipateryathā||13||

14caturvidhānāṃ bhūtānāṃ triṣu lokeṣu mādhavaḥ
|
prabhavaścaiva sarveṣāṃ nidhanaṃ ca yudhiṣṭhira||14||

15vaiśampāyana uvāca|
iti tasya vacaḥ śrutvā tataścedipatirnṛpaḥ
|
bhīṣmaṃ rūkṣākṣarā vācaḥ śrāvayāmāsa bhārata||15||
