Mahābhārata 3.279 (Āraṇyakaparvan)

1mārkaṇḍeya uvāca|
sakhā daśarathasyāsījjaṭāyuraruṇātmajaḥ
|
gṛdhrarājo mahāvīraḥ sampātiryasya sodaraḥ||1||

2sa dadarśa tadā sītāṃ rāvaṇāṅkagatāṃ snuṣām
|
sakrodho'bhyadravat pakṣī rāvaṇaṃ rākṣaseśvaram||2||

3athainamabravīdgṛdhro muñca muñcasva maithilīm
|
dhriyamāṇe mayi kathaṃ hariṣyasi niśācara||3||

4na hi me mokṣyase jīvan yadi notsṛjyase vadhūm
|
uktvaivaṃ rākṣasendraṃ taṃ cakarta nakharairbhṛśam||4||

5pakṣatuṇḍaprahāraiśca śataśo jarjarīkṛtam
|
cakṣāra rudhiraṃ bhūri giriḥ prastravaṇairiva||5||

6sa vadhyamāno gṛdhreṇa rāmapriyahitaiṣiṇā
|
khaḍgamādāya ciccheda bhujau tasya patattriṇaḥ||6||

7nihatya gṛdhrarājaṃ sa bhinnābhraśikharopamam
|
ūrdhvamācakrame sītāṃ gṛhītvā'ṅkena rākṣasaḥ||7||

8yatra yatra tu vaidehī paśyatyāśramamaṇḍalam
|
saro vā sarito vā'pi tatra muñcati bhūṣaṇam||8||

9sā dadarśa giriprasthe pañca vānarapuṅgavān
|
tatra vāso mahaddivyamutsasarja manasvinī||9||

10tat teṣāṃ vānarendrāṇāṃ papāta pavanoddhatam
|
madhye supītaṃ pañcānāṃ vidyunmeghāntare yathā||10||

11acireṇāticakrāma khecaraḥ khe caranniva
|
dadarśātha purīṃ ramyāṃ bahudvārāṃ manoramām||11||

12prākāravaprasambādhāṃ nirmitāṃ viśvakarmaṇā
|
praviveśa purīṃ laṅkāṃ sasīto rākṣaseśvaraḥ||12||

13evaṃ hṛtāyāṃ vaidehyāṃ rāmo hatvā mahāmṛgam
|
nivṛtto dadṛśe dhīmān bhrātaraṃ lakṣmaṇaṃ tathā||13||

14kathamutsṛjya vaidehīṃ vane rākṣasasevite
|
iti taṃ bhrātaraṃ dṛṣṭvā prāpto'sīti vyagarhayat||14||

15mṛgarūpadhareṇātha rakṣasā sopakarṣaṇam
|
bhrāturāgamanaṃ caiva cintayan paryatapyata||15||

16garhayanneva rāmastu tvaritastaṃ samāsadat
|
api jīvati vaidehīmiti paśyāmi lakṣmaṇa||16||

17tasya tat sarvamācakhyau sītāyā lakṣmaṇo vacaḥ
|
yaduktavatyasadṛśaṃ vaidehī paścimaṃ vacaḥ||17||

18dahyamānena tu hṛdā rāmo'bhyapatadāśramam
|
sa dadarśa tadā gṛdhraṃ nihataṃ parvatopamam||18||

19rākṣasaṃ śaṅkamānastaṃ vikṛṣya balavaddhanuḥ
|
abhyadhāvata kākutsthastatastaṃ sahalakṣmaṇaḥ||19||

20sa tāvuvāca tejasvī sahitau rāmalakṣmaṇau
|
gṛdhrarājo'smi bhadraṃ vāṃ sakhā daśarathasya vai||20||

21tasya tadvacanaṃ śrutvā sa gṛhya dhanuṣī śubhe
|
ko'yaṃ pitaramasmākaṃ nāmnā''hetyūcatuśca tau||21||

22tato dadṛśatustau taṃ chinnapakṣadvayaṃ khagam
|
tayoḥ śaśaṃsa gṛdhrastu sītārthe rāvaṇādvadham||22||

23apṛcchadrāghavo gṛdhraṃ rāvaṇaḥ kāṃ diśaṃ gataḥ
|
tasya gṛdhraḥ śiraḥ kampairācacakṣe mamāra ca||23||

24dakṣiṇāmiti kākutstho viditvā'sya tadiṅgitam
|
satkāraṃ lambhayāmāsa sakhāyaṃ pūjayan pituḥ||24||

25tato dṛṣṭvā''śramapadaṃ vyapaviddhabṛsīmaṭham
|
vidhvastakalaśaṃ śūnyaṃ gomāyuśatasaṅkulam||25||

26duḥkhaśokasamāviṣṭau vaidehīharaṇārditau
|
jagmaturdaṇḍakāraṇyaṃ dakṣiṇena parantapau||26||

27vane mahati tasmiṃstu rāmaḥ saumitriṇā saha
|
dadarśa mṛgayūthāni dravamāṇāni sarvaśaḥ||27||

28śabdaṃ ca ghoraṃ satvānāṃ dāvāgneriva vardhataḥ
|
apaśyetāṃ muhūrtācca kabandhaṃ ghoradarśanam||28||

29meghaparvatasaṃkāśaṃ śālaskandhaṃ mahābhujam
|
urogataviśālākṣaṃ mahodaramahāmurkha:||29||

30yadṛcchayā'tha tadrakṣaḥ kare jagrāha lakṣmaṇam
|
viṣādamagamat sadyaḥ saumitriratha bhārata||30||

31sarāmamabhisamprekṣya kṛṣyate yena tanmukham
|
viṣaṇṇaścābravīdrāmaṃ paśyāvasthāmimāṃ mama||31||

32haraṇaṃ caiva vaidehyā mama cāyasupaplavaḥ
|
rājyabhraṃśaśca bhavatastātasya maraṇaṃ tathā||32||

33nāhaṃ tvāṃ saha vaidehyā sametaṃ kosalāgatam
|
drakṣyāmi pṛthivī rājye pitṛpaitāmahe sthitam||33||

34drakṣyantyāryasya dhanyā ye kuśalājaśamīdalaiḥ
|
abhiṣiktasya vadanaṃ somaṃ śāntaghanaṃ yathā||34||

35evaṃ bahuvidhaṃ dhīmān vilalāpa salakṣmaṇaḥ
|
tamuvācātha kākutsthaḥ sambhrameṣvapyasambhramaḥ||35||

36mā viṣīda naravyāghra naiṣa kaścinmayi sthite
|
chindhyasya dakṣiṇaṃ bāhuṃ chinnaḥ savyo mayā bhujaḥ||36||

37ityevaṃ vadatā tasya bhujo rāmeṇa pātitaḥ
|
khaḍgena bhṛśatīkṣṇena nikṛttastilakāṇḍavat||37||

38tato'sya dakṣiṇaṃ bāhuṃ khaḍgenājaghnivān balī
|
saumitrirapi samprekṣya bhrātaraṃ rāghavaṃ sthitam||38||

39punarjaghāna pārśve vai tadrakṣo lakṣmaṇo bhṛśam
|
gatāsurapatadbhūmau kabandhaḥ sumahāṃstataḥ||39||

40tasya dehādviniḥsṛtya puruṣo divyadarśanaḥ
|
dadṛśe divamāsthāya divi sūrya iva jvalan||40||

41papraccha rāmastaṃ vāgmī kastvaṃ prabūhi pṛcchataḥ
|
kāmayā kimidaṃ citramāścaryaṃ pratibhāti me||41||

42tasyācacakṣe gandharvo viśvāvasurahaṃ nṛpa
|
prāpto brāhmaṇaśāpena yoniṃ rākṣasasevitām||42||

43rāvaṇena hṛtā sītā rājñā laṅkādhivāsinā
|
sugrīvamabhigacchasva sa te sāhyaṃ kariṣyati||43||

44eṣā pampā śivajalā haṃsakāraṇḍavāyutā
|
ṛṣyamūkasya śailasya sannikarṣe taṭākinī||44||

45vasate tatra sugrīvaścaturbhiḥ sacivaiḥ saha
|
bhrātā vānararājasya vālino hemamālinaḥ||45||

46tena tvaṃ saha saṅgamya duḥkhamūlaṃ nivedaya
|
samānaśīlo bhavataḥ sāhāyyaṃ sa kariṣyati||46||

47etāvacchakyamasmābhirvaktuṃ draṣṭā'si jānakīm
|
dhruvaṃ vānararājasya vidito rāvaṇālayaḥ||47||

48ityuktvā'ntarhito divyaḥ puruṣaḥ sa mahāprabhaḥ
|
vismayaṃ jagmatuścobhau pravīrau rāmalakṣmaṇau||48||
