Mahābhārata 6.120 (Bhīṣmaparvan)

1dhṛtarāṣṭra uvāca|
kathamāsaṃstadā yodhā hīno bhīṣmeṇa sañjaya
|
balinā devakalpena gurvarthe brahmacāriṇā ||1||

2tadaiva nihatān manye kurūnanyāṃśca pāṇḍavaiḥ
|
na prāharadyadā bhīṣmo ghṛṇitvāddupadātmajam ||2||

3tato duḥkhataraṃ manye kimanyat prabhaviṣyati
|
adyāhaṃ pitaraṃ śrutvā nihataṃ sma sudurmatiḥ ||3||

4aśmasāramayaṃ nūnaṃ hṛdayaṃ mama sañjaya
|
śrutvā vinihataṃ bhīṣmaṃ śatadhā yanna daryite ||4||

5yadanyannihatenājau bhīṣmeṇa jayamicchatā
|
ceṣṭitaṃ kurusiṃhena tanme kathaya suvrata ||5||

6punaḥpunarna mṛṣyāmi hataṃ devavrataṃ raṇe
|
na hato jāmadagnyena divyairaṃstrairayaṃ purā ||6||

7sa hato draupadeyena pāñcālyena śikhaṇḍinā
|
sañjaya uvāca|sāyāhne nihato bhūmau dhārtarāṣṭrān viṣādayan ||7||

8pañcālānāṃ dadau harṣaṃ bhīṣmaḥ kurupitāmahaḥ
|
sa śete śaratalpastho medinīmaspṛśaṃstadā ||8||

9bhīṣme rathāt prapatite pracyute dharaṇītale
|
hāheti tumulaḥ śabdo bhūtānāṃ samapadyata ||9||

10sīmāvṛkṣe nipatine kurūṇāṃ samitiñjaye
|
senayorubhayo rājan kṣatriyān bhayamāviśat ||10||

11bhīṣmaṃ śāntanavaṃ dṛṣṭvā viśīrṇakavacadhvajam
|
kuravaḥ paryavartanta pāṇḍavāśca viśāṃpate ||11||

12khaṃ tamaḥ saṃvṛtamabhūdāsīdbhānurgataprabhaḥ
|
rarāsa pṛthivī caiva bhīṣme śāntanave hate ||12||

13ayaṃ brahmavidāṃ śreṣṭho hyayaṃ brahmavidāṃ varaḥ
|
ityabhāṣanta bhūtāni śayānaṃ puruṣarṣabham ||13||

14ayaṃ pitaramājñāya kāmārtaṃ śantanuṃ purā
|
ūrdhvaretasamātmānaṃ cakāra puruṣarṣabhaḥ ||14||

15iti sma śaratalpasthaṃ bharatānāṃ mahattamam
|
ṛṣayastvabhyabhāṣanta sahitāḥ siddhacāraṇaiḥ ||15||

16hate śāntanave bhīṣme bharatānāṃ pitāmahe
|
na kiñcitpratyapadyanta putrāstava hi māriṣa ||16||

17viṣaṇṇavadanāścāsan hataśrīkāśca bhārata
|
atiṣṭhan vrīḍitāścaiva hriyā yuktā hyadhomukhāḥ ||17||

18pāṇḍavāśca jayaṃ labdhvā saṃgrāmaśirasi sthitāḥ
|
sarve dadhmurmahāśaṃkhān hemajālapariṣkṛtān ||18||

19harṣāt tūryasahastreṣu vādyamāneṣu cānagha
|
apaśyāma mahārāja bhīmasenaṃ mahābalam ||19||

20vikrīḍamānaṃ kaunteyaṃ harṣeṇa mahatā yutam
|
nihatya tarasā śatruṃ mahābalasamanvitam ||20||

21saṃmohaścāpi tumulaḥ kurūṇāprabhavat tataḥ
|
karṇaduyādhanau cāpi niḥśvasetāṃ muharmuhuḥ ||21||

22tathā nipatite bhīṣme kauravāṇāṃ pitāmahe
|
hāhābhūtamabhūt sarvaṃ nirmaryādamavartata ||22||

23dṛṣṭvā ca patitaṃ bhīṣmaṃ putro duḥśāsanastava
|
uttamaṃ javamāsthāya droṇānīkamupādravat ||23||

24bhrātrā prasthāpito vīraḥ svenānīkena daṃśitaḥ
|
prayayau puruṣavyāghraḥ svasainyaṃ sa viṣādayan ||24||

25tamāyāntamabhiprekṣya kuravaḥ paryavārayan
|
duḥśāsanaṃ mahārāja kimayaṃ vakṣyatīti ca ||25||

26tato droṇāya nihataṃ bhīṣmamācaṣṭa kauravaḥ
|
droṇastatrāpriyaṃ śrutvā mumoha bharatarṣabha ||26||

27sa saṃjñāmupalabhyāśu bhāradvājaḥ pratāpavān
|
nivārayāmāsa tadā svānyanīkāni māriṣa ||27||

28vinivṛttān kurūn dṛṣṭvā pāṇḍavā'pi svasainikān
|
dūtaiḥ śīghrāśvasaṃyuktaiḥ samantāt paryavārayan ||28||

29nivṛtteṣu ca sainyeṣu pāramparyeṇa sarvaśaḥ
|
nirmuktakavacāḥ sarve bhīṣmamīyurnarādhipāḥ ||29||

30vyuparamya tato yuddhādyodhāḥ śatasahasraśaḥ
|
upatasthurmahātmānaṃ prajāpatimivāmarāḥ ||30||

31te tu bhīṣmaṃ samāsādya śayānaṃ bharatarṣabham
|
abhivādyāvatiṣṭhanta pāṇḍavāḥ kurubhiḥ saha ||31||

32atha pāṇḍūn kurūṃścaiva praṇipatyāgrataḥ sthitān
|
abhyabhāṣata dharmātmā bhīṣmaḥ śāntanavastadā ||32||

33svāgataṃ vo mahābhāgāḥ svāgataṃ vo mahārathāḥ
|
tuṣyāmi darśanāccāhaṃ yuṣmākamamaropamāḥ ||33||

34abhimantryātha tānevaṃ śirasā lambatā'bravīt
|
śiro me lambate'tyarthamupadhānaṃ pradīyatām ||34||

35tato nṛpāḥ samājahrustanūni ca mṛdūni ca
|
upadhānāni mukhyāni naicchattāni pitāmahaḥ ||35||

36athābravīnnaravyāghraḥ prahasanniva tānnṛpān
|
naitāni vīraśayyāsu yuktarūpāṇi pārthivāḥ ||36||

37tato vīkṣya naraśreṣṭhamabhyabhāṣata pāṇḍavam
|
dhanañjayaṃ dīrghabāhuṃ sarvalokamahāratham ||37||

38dhanañjaya mahābāho śiro me tāta lambate
|
dīyatāmupadhānaṃ vairyaduktamiha manyase ||38||

39sañjaya uvāca|
samāropya mahaccāpamabhivādya pitāmaham
|
netrbhyāmaśrupūrṇābhyāmidaṃ vacanamabravīt ||39||

40ājñāpaya kuruśreṣṭha sarvaśastrabhṛtāṃ vara
|
prepyo'haṃ tava durdharṣa kriyatāṃ kiṃ pitāmaha ||40||

41tamabravīcchāntanavaḥ śiro me tātaḥ lambate
|
upadhānaṃ kuruśreṣṭha phālgunopadadhatsva me ||41||

42śayanasyānurūpaṃ vai śīghraṃ vīra prayaccha me
|
tvaṃ hi pārtha samartho vai śreṣṭhaḥ sarvadhanuṣmatām ||42||

43kṣatradharmasya vettā ca buddhisattvaguṇānvitaḥ
|
phālguno'pi tathetyuktvā vyavasāyamarocayat ||43||

44gṛhyānumantrya gāṇḍīvaṃ śarān sannataparvaṇaḥ
|
anumānya mahātmānaṃ bharatānāṃ mahāratham ||44||

45tribhistīkṣṇairmahāvegairanvagṛhvācchiraḥ śaraiḥ
|
abhiprāye tu vidite dharmātmā saṃvyasācinā ||45||

46atuṣyadbharataśreṣṭho bhīṣmo dharmārthatattvavit
|
upadhānena dattena pratyanandaddhanañjayam ||46||

47prāha sarvān samudvīkṣya bharatān bhārataṃ prati
|
kuntīputraṃ yudhāṃ śreṣṭhaṃ suhṛdāṃ prītivardhanam ||47||

48śayanasyānurūpaṃ me pāṇḍavopahitaṃ tvayā
|
yadyanyathā prapadyethāḥ śapeyaṃ tvāmahaṃ ruṣā ||48||

49evameva mahābāho dharmeṣu paritiṣṭhata
|
svaptavyaṃ kṣatriyeṇājau śaratalpagatena vai ||49||

50evamuktvā tu bībhatsuṃ sarvāṃstānabravīdvacaḥ
|
rājñaśca rājaputrāṃśca pāṇḍavānabhisaṃsthitān ||50||

51paśyadhvamupadhānaṃ me pāṇḍavenābhisaṃdhitam
|
śiśye'hamasyāṃ śayyāyāṃ yāvadāvartanaṃ raveḥ ||51||

52ye tadā māṃ gamiṣyanti te ca prekṣyanti māṃ nṛpāḥ
|
diśaṃ vaiśravaṇākrāntāṃ yadā gantā divākaraḥ ||52||

53nūnaṃ saptāśvayuktena rathenottamatejasā
|
vimokṣye'haṃ tadā prāṇān suhṛdaḥ supriyāniva ||53||

54parikhāḥ khanyatāmatra mamāvasadane nṛpāḥ
|
upāsiṣye vivasvantamevaṃ śaraśatācitaḥ ||54||

55upāramadhvaṃ saṃgrāmādvairamutsṛjya pārthivāḥ
|
sañjaya uvāca|upātiṣṭhannatho vaidyāḥ śalyoddharaṇakovidāḥ ||55||

56sarvopakaraṇairyuktāḥ kuśalaiḥ sādhu śikṣitāḥ
|
tān‌ dṛṣṭvā jāhnavīputraḥ provāca tanayaṃ tava ||56||

57dhanaṃ dattvā visṛjyantāṃ pūjayitvā cikitsakāḥ
|
evaṃgate mayedānīṃ vaidyaiḥ kāryabhihāsti kim ||57||

58kṣatradharme praśastāṃ hi prāpto'smi paramāṃ gatim
|
naiṣa dharmo mahīpālāḥ śaratalpagatasya me ||58||

59ebhireva śaraiścāhaṃ dagdhavyo'smi narādhipāḥ
|
tacchrutvā vacanaṃ tasya putro duryodhanastava ||59||

60vaidyān visarjayāmāsa pūjayitvā yathārhataḥ
|
tataste vismayaṃ jagmurnānājanapadeśvaroḥ ||60||

61sthitiṃ dharme parāṃ dṛṣṭvā bhīṣmasyāmitatejasaḥ
|
upadhānaṃ tato dattvā pituste manujeśvarāḥ ||61||

62sahitāḥ pāṇḍavāḥ sarve kuravaśca mahārathāḥ
|
upagamya mahātmānaṃ śayānaṃ śayane śubhe ||62||

63te'bhivādya tato bhīṣmaṃ kṛtvā ca triḥ pradakṣiṇam
|
vidhāya rakṣāṃ bhīṣmasya sarva eva samantataḥ ||63||

64vīrāḥ svaśibirāṇyeva dhyāyantaḥ paramāturāḥ
|
niveśāyābhyuṣāṃgacchansāyāhne rudhirokṣitāḥ ||64||

65niviṣṭānpāṇḍavāṃścaiva prīyamāṇānmahārathān
|
bhīṣmasya patane hṛṣṭānupagamya mahābalaḥ ||65||

66uvāca mādhavaḥ kāle dharmaputraṃ yudhiṣṭhiram
|
diṣṭyā jayasi kauravya diṣṭyā bhīṣmo nipātitaḥ ||66||

67avadhyo mānuṣaireva satyasandho mahārathaḥ
|
athavā daivataiḥ sārdhaṃ sarvaśāstrasya pāragaḥ ||67||

68tvāṃ tu cakṣurhaṇaṃ prāpya dagdho ghoreṇa cakṣuṣā
|
evamukto dharmarājaḥ pratyuvāca janārdanam ||68||

69tava prasādādvijayaḥ krodhāt tava parājayaḥ
|
tvaṃ hi naḥ śaraṇaṃ kṛṣṇa bhaktānāmabhayaṅkaraḥ ||69||

70anāścaryo jayasteṣāṃ yeṣāṃ tvamasi keśava
|
rakṣitā samare nityaṃ nityaṃ cāpi hite rataḥ ||70||

71sarvathā tvāṃ samāsādya nāścaryamiti me matiḥ
|
evamuktaḥ pratyuvāca smayamāno janārdanaḥ
|
tavaivaitadyuktarūpaṃ vacanaṃ pārthivottama ||71||
