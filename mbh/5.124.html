Mahābhārata 5.124 (Udyogaparvan)

1dhṛtarāṣṭra uvāca|
bhagavannevamevaitadyathā vadasi nārada
|
icchāmi cāhamapyevaṃ na tvīśo bhagavannaham ||1||

2vaiśampāyana uvāca|
evamuktvā tataḥ kṛṣṇamabhyabhāṣata kauravaḥ
|
svargyaṃ lokyaṃ ca māmāttha dharmyaṃ nyāyyaṃ ca keśava ||2||

3na tvahaṃ svavaśastāta kriyamāṇaṃ na me priyam
|
aṅga duryodhanaṃ kṛṣṇa mandaṃ śāstrātigaṃ mama ||3||

4anunetuṃ mahābāho yatasva puruṣottama
|
na śṛṇoti mahābāho vacanaṃ sādhubhāṣitam ||4||

5gāndhāryāśca hṛṣīkeśa vidurasya ca dhīmataḥ
|
anyeṣāṃ caiva suhṛdāṃ bhīṣmādīnāṃ hitaiṣiṇām ||5||

6sa tvaṃ pāpamatiṃ krūraṃ pāpacittamacetanam
|
anuśādhi durāt māna svayaṃ duryodhanaṃ nṛpam ||6||

7suhṛtkāryaṃ tu sumahat kṛtaṃ te syājjanārdana
|
tato'bhyāvṛtya vārṣṇeyo duryodhanamamarṣaṇam ||7||

8abravīnmadhurāṃ vācaṃ sarvadharmārthatattvavit
|
duryodhana nibodhedaṃ madvākyaṃ kurusattama ||8||

9śarmārthaṃ te viśeṣeṇa sānubandhasya bhārata
|
mahāprajña kule jātaḥ sādhvetat kartumarhasi ||9||

10śrutavṛttopasampannaḥ sarvaiḥ samudito guṇaiḥ
|
dauṣkuleyā durātmāno nuśaṃsā nirapatrapāḥ ||10||

11ta etadīdṛśaṃ kuryuryathā tvaṃ tāta manyase
|
dharmārthayuktā loke'smin pravṛttirlakṣyate satām ||11||

12asatāṃ viparītā tu lakṣyate bharatarṣabha
|
viparītā tviyaṃ vṛttirasakṛllakṣyate tvayi ||12||

13adharmaścānubandho'tra ghoraḥ prāṇaharo mahān
|
aniṣṭaścānimittaśca na ca śakyaśca bhārata ||13||

14tamanarthaṃ pariharannātmaśreyaḥ kariṣyasi
|
bhrātṝṇāmatha bhṛtyānāṃ mitrāṇāṃ ca parantapa ||14||

15adharmyādayaśasyācca karmaṇastvaṃ pramokṣyase
|
prājñaiḥ śūrairmahotsāhairātmavadbhibahuśrutaiḥ ||15||

16sandhatsva puruṣavyāghra pāṇḍavairbharatarṣabha
|
taddhitaṃ ca priyaṃ caiva dhṛtarāṣṭrasya dhīmataḥ ||16||

17pitāmahasya droṇasya vidurasya mahāmateḥ
|
kṛpasya somadattasya bāhlīkasya ca dhīmataḥ ||17||

18aśvatthāmno vikarṇasya sañjayasya viviṃśateḥ
|
jñātīnāṃ caiva bhūyiṣṭhaṃ mitrāṇāṃ ca paraṃtapa ||18||

19śame śarma bhavettata sarvasya jagatastathā
|
hrīmānasi kule jātaḥ śrutavānanṛśaṃsavān
|
tiṣṭha tāta pituḥ śāstre mātuśca bharatarṣabha ||19||

20etacchreyo hi manyante pitā yacchāsti bhārata
|
uttamāpadgataḥ sarvaḥ pituḥ smarati śāsanam ||20||

21rocate te pitustāta pāṇḍavaiḥ saha saṅgamaḥ
|
samātyasya kuruśreṣṭha tattubhyaṃ tāta rocatām ||21||

22śrutvā yaḥ suhṛdāṃ śāstraṃ martyo na pratipadyate
|
vipākānte dahatyenaṃ kiṃpākamiva bhakṣitam ||22||

23yastu niḥśreyasaṃ vākyaṃ mohānna pratipadyate
|
sa dīrghasūtro hīnārthaḥ paścāttāpena yujyate ||23||

24yastu niḥśreyasaṃ śrutvā prāktadevābhipadyate
|
ātmano matamutsṛjya sa loke sukhamedhate ||24||

25yo'rthakāmasya vacanaṃ prātikūlyāṃnna mṛṣyate
|
śṛṇoti pratikūlāni dviṣatāṃ vaśameti saḥ ||25||

26satāṃ matamatikramya yo'satāṃ vartate mate
|
śocante vyasane tasya suhṛdo na cirādiva ||26||

27mukhyānamātyānutsṛjya yo nihīnānniṣevate
|
sa ghorāmāpadaṃ prāpya nottāramadhigacchati ||27||

28yo'satsevī vṛthācāro na śrotā suhṛdāṃ satām
|
parān vṛṇīte svān dveṣṭi taṃ gaustyajati bhārata ||28||

29sa tvaṃ virudhya tairvīrairanyebhyastrāṇamicchasi
|
aśiṣṭebhyo'samarthebhyo mūḍhebhyo bharatarṣabha ||29||

30ko hi śakrasamān jñātīnatikramya mahārathān
|
anyebhyastrāṇamāśaṃsettvadanyo bhuvi mānavaḥ ||30||

31janmaprabhṛti kaunteyā nityaṃ vinikṛtāstvayā
|
na ca te jātu kupyanti dharmātmāno hi pāṇḍavāḥ ||31||

32mithyopacaritāstāta janmaprabhṛti bāndhavāḥ
|
tvayi samyaṅmahābāho pratipannā yaśakhinaḥ ||32||

33tvayā'pi pratipattavyaṃ tathaiva bharatarṣama
|
sveṣu bandhuṣu mukhyeṣu mā manyuvaśamanvagāḥ ||33||

34trivargayuktaḥ prājñānāmārambho bharatarṣabha
|
dharmārthāvanurudhyante trivargāsambhave narāḥ ||34||

35pṛthakca viniviṣṭānāṃ dharmaṃ dhīro'nurudhyate
|
madhyamo'rthaṃ kaliṃ bālaḥ kāmamevānurudhyate ||35||

36indriyaiḥ prākṛto lobhāddharmaṃ viprajahāti yaḥ
|
kāmārthāvanupāyena lipsamāno vinaśyati ||36||

37kāmārthau lipsamānastu dharmamevāditaścaret
|
na hi dharmādapaityarthaḥ kāmo vā'pi kadācana ||37||

38upāyaṃ dharmamevāhustrivargasya viśāmpate
|
lipyamāno hi tenāśu kakṣe'gniriva vardhate ||38||

39sa tvaṃ tātānupāyena lipsyase bharatarṣabha
|
ādhirājyaṃ mahaddīpaṃ prathitaṃ sarvarājasu ||39||

40ātmānaṃ takṣati hyeṣa vanaṃ paraśunā yathā
|
yaḥ samyagvartamāneṣu mithyā rājan pravartate ||40||

41na tasya hi matiṃ chindyādyasya necchet parābhavam
|
avicchinnamaterasya kalyāṇe dhīyate matiḥ
|
ātmavānnāvamanyeta triṣu lokeṣu bhārata ||41||

42apyanyaṃ prākṛtaṃ kiñcit kimutān pāṇḍavarṣabhān
|
amarṣavaśamāpanno na kiñcidbudhyate janaḥ ||42||

43chiyate hyātataṃ sarvaṃ pramāṇaṃ paśya bhārata
|
śreyaste durjanāttāta pāṇḍavaiḥ saha saṅgatam ||43||

44tairhi saṃprīyamāṇastvaṃ sarvān kāmānavāpsyasi
|
pāṇḍavairnirmitāṃ bhūmiṃ bhuñjāno rājasattama ||44||

45pāṇḍavān pṛṣṭhataḥ kṛtvā trāṇamāśaṃsase'nyataḥ
|
duḥśāsane durviṣahe karṇe cāpi sasaubale ||45||

46eteṣvaiśvaryamādhāya bhūtimicchasi bhārata
|
na caite tava paryāptā jñāne dharmārthayostathā ||46||

47vikrame cāpyaparyāptāḥ pāṇḍavān prati bhārata
|
na hīme sarvarājānaḥ paryāptāḥ sahitāstvayā ||47||

48kruddhasya bhīmasenasya prekṣituṃ mukhamāhave
|
idaṃ sannihitaṃ tāta samagraṃ pārthivaṃ balam ||48||

49ayaṃ bhīṣmastathā droṇaḥ karṇaścāyaṃ tathā kṛpaḥ
|
bhūriśravāḥ saumadattiraśvatthāmā jayadrathaḥ ||49||

50aśaktāḥ sarva evaite pratiyoddhuṃ dhanañjayam
|
ajeyo hyarjunaḥ saṅkhye sarvairapi surāsuraiḥ
|
mānuṣairapi gandharvairmā yuddhe ceta ādhithāḥ ||50||

51dṛśyatāṃ vā pumān kaścit samagre pārthive bale
|
yo'rjunaṃ samare prāpya svastimānāvrajedgṛhān ||51||

52kiṃ te janakṣayeṇeha kṛtena bharatarṣama
|
yasmiñjite jitaṃ tatsyāt pumānekaḥ sa dṛśyatām ||52||

53yaḥ sa devān sagandharvān sayakṣāsurapannagān
|
ajayat khāṇḍavaprasthe kastaṃ yudhyeta mānavaḥ ||53||

54tathā virāṭanagare śrūyate mahadadbhutam
|
ekasya ca bahūnāṃ ca paryāptaṃ tannidarśanam ||54||

55yuddhe yena mahādevaḥ sākṣāt santoṣitaḥ śivaḥ
|
tamajeyamanādhṛṣyaṃ vijetuṃ jiṣṇumacyutam
|
āśaṃsasīha samare vīrimarjunamūrjitam ||55||

56maddvitīyaṃ punaḥ pārthaṃ kaḥ prārthāyitumarhati
|
yuddhe pratīpamāyāntamapi sākṣāt purandaraḥ ||56||

57bāhubhyāmudvahedbhūmiṃ dahetkruddha imāḥ prajāḥ
|
pātayettridivāddevān yo'rjunaṃ samare jayet ||57||

58paśya putrāṃstathā bhrātṝn jñātīn sambandhinastathā
|
tvatkṛte na vinaśyeyurime bharatasattamāḥ ||58||

59astu śeṣaṃ kauravāṇāṃ mā parābhūdidaṃ kulam
|
kulaghna iti nocyethā naṣṭakīrtirnarādhipa ||59||

60tvāmeva sthāpayiṣyanti yauvarājye mahārathāḥ
|
mahārājye'pi pitaraṃ dhṛtarāṣṭraṃ janeśvaram ||60||

61mā tāta śriyamāyāntīmavamaṃsthāḥ samudyatām
|
ardhaṃ pradāya pārthebhyo mahatīṃ śriyamāpnuhi ||61||

62pāṇḍavaiḥ saṃśamaṃ kṛtvā kṛtvā ca suhṛdāṃ vacaḥ
|
saṃprīyamāṇo mitraiśca ciraṃ bhadrāṇyavāpsyasi ||62||
