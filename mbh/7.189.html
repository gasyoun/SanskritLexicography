Mahābhārata 7.189 (Droṇaparvan)

1sañjaya uvāca|
tasmistathā vartamāne gajāśvanarasaṃkṣaye
|
duḥśāsano mahārāja dhṛṣṭadyumnamayodhayat||1||

2sa tu rukmarathāsakto duḥśāsanaśarārditaḥ
|
amarṣāttava putrasya śarairvāhānavākirata||2||

3kṣaṇena sa rathastasya sadhvajaḥ sahasārathiḥ
|
nādṛśyata mahārāja pārṣatasya śaraiścitaḥ||3||

4duḥśāsanastu rājendra pāñcālyasya mahātmanaḥ
|
nāśakatpramukhe sthātuṃ śarajālaprapīḍitaḥ||4||

5sa tu duḥśāsanaṃ bāṇairvimukhīkṛtya pārṣataḥ
|
kirañcharasahasrāṇi droṇamevābhyayādraṇe||5||

6abhyapadyata hārdikyaḥ kṛtavarmā tvanantaram
|
sodaryāṇāṃ trayaścaiva ta enaṃ paryavārayan||6||

7taṃ yamau pṛṣṭhato'nvaitāṃ rakṣantau puruṣarṣabhau
|
droṇāyābhimukhaṃ yāntaṃ dīpyamānamivānalam||7||

8saṃprahāramakurvaṃste sarve ca sumahārathāḥ
|
amarṣitāḥ sattvavantaḥ kṛtvā maraṇamagrataḥ||8||

9śuddhātmānaḥ śuddhavṛttā rājan svargapuraskṛtāḥ
|
āryaṃ yuddhamakurvanta parasparajigīṣavaḥ||9||

10śuklābhijanakarmāṇo matimanto janādhipa
|
dharmayuddhamayudhyanta prepsanto gatimuttamām||10||

11na tatrāsīdadharmiṣṭhamaśastaṃ yuddhameva ca
|
nātra karṇī na nālīko na lipto na ca bastikaḥ||11||

12na sūcī kapiśo naiva na gavāsthirgajāsthijaḥ
|
iṣurāsīnna saṃśliṣṭo na pūtirna ca jihmagaḥ||12||

13ṛjūnyeka viśuddhāni sarve śastrāṇyadhārayan
|
suyuddhena parā~llokānīpsantaḥ kīrtimeva ca||13||

14tadāsīttumulaṃ yuddhaṃ sarvadoṣavivarjitam
|
caturṇāṃ tava yodhānāṃ taistribhiḥ pāṇḍavaiḥ saha||14||

15dhṛṣṭadyumnastu tān dṛṣṭvā tava rājan ratharṣabhān
|
yamābhyāṃ vāritānvīrāñchīghrāstro droṇamabhyayāt||15||

16nivāritāstu te vīrāstayoḥ puruṣasiṃhayoḥ
|
samasajjanta catvāro vātāḥ parvatayoriva||16||

17dvābhyāṃ dvābhyāṃ yamau sārdhaṃ rathābhyāṃ rathapuṅgavau
|
samāsaktau tato droṇaṃ dhṛṣṭadyumno'bhyavartata||17||

18dṛṣṭvā droṇāya pāñcālyaṃ vrajantaṃ yuddhadurmadam
|
yamābhyāṃ tāṃśca saṃsaktāṃstadantaramupādravat||18||

19duryodhano mahārāja kirañchoṇitabhojanān
|
taṃ sātyakiḥ śīghrataraṃ punarevābhyavartata||19||

20tau parasparamāsādya samīpe kurumādhavau
|
hasamānau nṛśārdūlāvabhītau samasajjatām||20||

21bālyavṛttāni sarvāṇi prīyamāṇau vicintya tau
|
anyonyaṃ prekṣamāṇau ca smayamānau punaḥ punaḥ||21||

22atha duryodhano rājā sātyakiṃ samabhāṣata
|
priyaṃ sakhāyaṃ satataṃ garhayan vṛttamātmanaḥ||22||

23dhik krodhaṃ dhiksakhe lobhaṃ ghiṅmohaṃ dhigamarṣitam
|
dhigastu kṣātramācāraṃ dhigastu balamaurasam||23||

24yatra māmabhisaṃdhatse tvāṃ cāhaṃ śinipuṅgava
|
tvaṃ hi prāṇaiḥ priyataro mamāhaṃ ca sadā tava||24||

25smarāmi tāni sarvāṇi bālyavṛttāni yāni nau
|
tāni sarvāṇi jīrṇāni sāṃprataṃ no raṇājire||25||

26kimanyatkrodhalobhābhyāṃ yuddhamevādya sātvata
|
taṃ tathāvādinaṃ tatra sātyakiḥ pratyabhāṣata||26||

27prahasanviśikhāṃstīkṣṇānudyamya paramāstraviva
|
neyaṃ sabhā rājaputra nācāryasya niveśanam||27||

28yatra krīḍitamasmābhistadā rājansamāgataiḥ
|
duryodhana uvāca|
kka sā krīḍā gatā'smākaṃ bālye vai śinipuṅgava||28||

29kva ca yuddhamidaṃ bhūyaḥ 'kālo hi duratikramaḥ'
|
kiṃnu no vidyate kṛtyaṃ dhanena dhanalipsayā||29||

30yatra yudhyāmahe sarve dhanalobhātsamāgatāḥ
|
sañjaya uvāca|
taṃ tathāvādinaṃ tatra rājānaṃ mādhavo'bravīt||30||

31evaṃ vṛttaṃ sadā kṣātraṃ yudhyantīha gurūnapi
|
yadi te'haṃ priyo rājan jahi māṃ mā ciraṃ kṛthāḥ||31||

32tvatkṛte sukṛtāṃllokān gaccheyaṃ bharatarṣabha
|
yā te śaktirbalaṃ yacca tatkṣipraṃ mayi darśaya||32||

33necchāmi tadahaṃ draṣṭuṃ mitrāṇāṃ vyasanaṃ mahat
|
ityevaṃ vyaktamābhāṣya pratibhāṣya ca sātyakiḥ||33||

34abhyayāttūrṇamavyagro dayāṃ nākurutātmani
|
tamāyāntaṃ mahābāhuṃ pratyagṛhṇāttavātmajaḥ||34||

35śaraiścāvākiradrājañśaineyaṃ tanayastava
|
tataḥ pravavṛte yuddhaṃ kurumādhavasiṃhayoḥ||35||

36anyonyaṃ kruddhayorghoraṃ yathā dviradasiṃhayoḥ
|
tataḥ pūrṇāyatotsṛṣṭaiḥ sātvataṃ yuddhadurmadam||36||

37duryodhanaḥ pratyavidhyatkupito daśabhiḥ śaraiḥ
|
taṃ sātyakiḥ pratyavidhyattathaivāvākiraccharaiḥ||37||

38yañcāśatā punaścājau triṃśatā daśabhiśca ha
|
sātyakiṃ tu raṇe rājanprahasaṃstanayastava||38||

39ākarṇapūrṇairniśitairvivyādha triṃśatā śaraiḥ
|
tato'sya saśaraṃ cāpaṃ kṣurapreṇa dvidhā'cchinat||39||

40so'nyatkārmukamādāya laghuhastastato dṛḍham
|
sātyakirvyasṛjaccāpi śaraśreṇīṃ sutasya te||40||

41tāmāpatantīṃ sahasā śaraśreṇīṃ jighāṃsayā
|
ciccheda bahudhā rājā tata uccukruśurjanāḥ||41||

42sātyakiṃ ca trisaptatyā pīḍayāmāsa vegitaḥ
|
svarṇapuṅkhaiḥ śilādhautairākarṇāpūrṇaniḥsṛtaiḥ||42||

43tasya saṃdadhataśceṣu saṃhiteṣu ca kārmukam
|
ācchinatsātyakistūrṇaṃ śaraiścaivāpyavīvidhat||43||

44sa gāḍhaviddho vyathitaḥ pratyapāyādrathāntare
|
duryodhano mahārāja dāśārhaśarapīḍitaḥ||44||

45samāśvasya tu putraste sātyakiṃ punarabhyayāt
|
visṛjanniṣujālāni yuyudhānarathaṃ prati||45||

46tathaiva sātyakirbāṇānduryodhanarathaṃ prati
|
satataṃ visṛjan rājaṃstatsaṃkulamavartata||46||

47tatreṣubhiḥ kṣipyamāṇaiḥ patadbhiśca śarīriṣu
|
agneriva mahākakṣaiḥ śabdaḥ samabhavanmahān||47||

48tayoḥ śarasahasaiśca saṃchannaṃ vasudhātalam
|
agamyarūpaṃ ca śarairākāśaṃ samapadyata||48||

49tatrāpyadhikamālakṣya mādhavaṃ rathasattamam
|
kṣipramabhyapatatkarṇaḥ parīpsaṃstanayaṃ tava||49||

50na tu taṃ marṣayāmāsa bhīmaseno mahābalaḥ
|
sobhyayāttvaritaḥkarṇaṃ visṛjansāyakānbahūn||50||

51tasya karṇaḥ śitānbāṇānpratihanya hasanniva
|
dhanuḥ śarāṃśca ciccheda sutaṃ cābhyahanaccharaiḥ||51||

52bhīmasenastu saṃkruddho gadāmādāya pāṇḍavaḥ
|
dhvajaṃ dhanuśca sūtaṃ ca saṃmamardāhave ripoḥ||52||

53rathacakraṃ ca karṇasya babhañja sa mahābalaḥ
|
bhagnacakre rathe'tiṣṭhadakampaḥ śailarāḍiva||53||

54ekacakraṃ rathaṃ tasya tamūhuḥ suciraṃ hayāḥ
|
ekacakramivārkasya rathaṃ sapta hayā yathā||54||

55amṛṣyamāṇaḥ karṇastu bhīmasenamayudhyata
|
vividhairipujālaiśca nānāśastraiśca saṃyuge||55||

56bhīmasenastu saṃkruddhaḥ sūtaputramayodhayat
|
tasmiṃstathā vartamāne kruddho dharmasuto'bravīt||56||

57pañcālānāṃ naravyāghrānmatsyāṃśca puruṣarṣabhān
|
ye naḥ prāṇāḥ śiro ye ca ye no yodhā mahārathāḥ||57||

58ta ete dhārtarāṣṭreṣu viṣaktāḥ puruṣarṣabhāḥ
|
kiṃ tiṣṭhata yathā mūḍhāḥ sarve vigatacetasaḥ||58||

59tatra gacchata yatraite yudhyante māmakā rathāḥ
|
kṣatradharmaṃ puraskṛtya sarva eva gatajvarāḥ||59||

60jayanto vadhyamānāśca gatimiṣṭāṃ gamiṣyatha
|
jitvā vā bahubhiryajñairyajadhvaṃ bhūridakṣiṇaiḥ||60||

61hatā vā devasādbhūtvā lokānprāpsyatha puṣkalān
|
te rājñā coditā vīrā yotsyamānā mahārathāḥ||61||

62kṣātradharmaṃ puraskṛtya tvaritā droṇamabhyayuḥ
|
pañcālāstvekato droṇamabhyaghnanniśitaiḥ śaraiḥ||62||

63bhīmasenapurogāścāpyekataḥ paryavārayan
|
āsaṃstu pāṇḍuputrāṇāṃ trayo jihmā mahārathāḥ||63||

64yamau ca bhīmasenaśca prākrośaṃste dhanañjayam
|
abhidravārjuna kṣipraṃ kurūndroṇādapānuda||64||

65tata enaṃ haniṣyanti pañcālā hatarakṣiṇam
|
kauraveyāṃstataḥ pārthaḥ sahasā samupādravat||65||

66pañcālāneva tu droṇo dhṛṣṭadyumnapurogamān
|
mamardustarasā vīrāḥ pañcame'hani bhārata||66||
