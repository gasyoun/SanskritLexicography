Mahābhārata 13.53 (Anuśāsanaparvan)

1yudhiṣṭhira uvāca|
tasminnantarhite vipre rājā kimakarottadā
|
bhāryā cāsya mahābhāgā tanme brūhi pitāmaha ||1||

2bhīṣma uvāca|
adṛṣṭvā sa mahīpālastamṛṣiṃ saha bhāryayā
|
pariśrānto nivavṛte vrīḍito naṣṭacetanaḥ ||2||

3sa praviśya purīṃ dīno nābhyabhāṣata kiñcana
|
tadeva cintayāmāsa cyavanasya viceṣṭitam ||3||

4atha śūnyena manasā praviśya svagṛhaṃ nṛpaḥ
|
dadarśa śayane tasmin śayānaṃ bhṛgunandanam ||4||

5vismitau tamṛṣiṃ dṛṣṭvā tadāścaryaṃ vicintya ca
|
darśanāttasya tu tadā viśrāntau sambabhūvatuḥ||5||

6yathāsthānaṃ ca tau sthitvā bhūyastaṃ saṃvavāhatuḥ
|
athāpareṇa pārśvena suṣvāpa sa mahāmuniḥ ||6||

7tenaiva ca sa kālena pratyabuddhyata vīryavān
|
na ca tau cakratuḥ kiñcidvikāraṃ bhayaśaṅkitau ||7||

8pratibuddhastu sa munistau provāca viśāmpate
|
tailābhyaṅgo dīyatāṃ me snāsye'hamiti bhārata ||8||

9tau tatheti pratiśrutya kṣudhitau śramakarśitau
|
śatapākena tailena mahārheṇopatasthatuḥ ||9||

10tataḥ sukhāsīnamṛṣiṃ vāgyatau saṃvavāhatuḥ
|
na ca paryāptamityāha bhārgavaḥ sumahātapāḥ ||10||

11yadā tau nirvikārau tu lakṣayāmāsa bhārgavaḥ
|
tata utthāya sahasā snānaśālāṃ viveśa ha ||11||

12klṛptameva tu tatrāsītsnānīyaṃ pārthivocitam
|
asatkṛtya ca tatsarvaṃ tatraivāntaradhīyata ||12||

13sa muniḥ punarevātha nṛpateḥ paśyatastadā
|
nāsūyāṃ cakratustau ca dampatī bharatarṣabha ||13||

14atha snātaḥ sa bhagavānsiṃhāsanagataḥ prabhuḥ
|
darśayāmāsa kuśikaṃ sabhāryaṃ kurunandana ||14||

15saṃhṛṣṭavada‌no rājā sabhāryaḥ kuśiko munim
|
siddhamannamiti prahvo nirvikāro nyavedayat ||15||

16ānīyatāmiti munistaṃ covāca narādhipam
|
sa rājā samupājahre tadannaṃ saha bhāryayā ||16||

17māṃsaprakārānvividhān śākāni vividhāni ca
|
vesavāravikārāṃśca pānakāni laghūni ca ||17||

18rasālāpūpakāṃścitrānmodakānatha khāṇḍavān
|
rasānnānāprakārāṃśca vanyaṃ ca munibhojanam ||18||

19phalāni ca vicitrāṇi rājabhojyāni bhūriśaḥ
|
badareṅguda‌kāśmaryabhallātakaphalāni ca ||19||

20gṛhasthānāṃ ca yadbhojyaṃ yaccāpi vanavāsinām
|
sarvamāhārayāmāsa rājā śāpabhayāttataḥ ||20||

21atha sarvamupanyastamagrataścyavanasya tat
|
tataḥ sarvaṃ samānīya tacca śayyāsanaṃ muniḥ ||21||

22vastraiḥ śubhairavacchādya bhojanopaskaraiḥ saha
|
sarvamādīpayāmāsa cyavano bhṛgunandanaḥ ||22||

23na ca tau cakratuḥ krodhaṃ dampatī sumahāmatī
|
tayoḥ saṃprekṣatoreva punarantarhito'bhavat ||23||

24tathaiva ca sa rājarṣistasthau tāṃ rajanīṃ tadā
|
sabhāryo vāgyataḥ śrīmānna ca kopaṃ samāviśada ||24||

25nityasaṃskṛtamannaṃ tu vividhaṃ rājaveśmani
|
śayanāni ca mukhyāni pariṣekāśca puṣkalāḥ ||25||

26vastraṃ ca vividhākāramabhavatsamupārjitam
|
na śaśāka tato draṣṭumantaraṃ cyavanastadā ||26||

27punareva ca viprarṣiḥ provāca kuśikaṃ nṛpam
|
sabhāryo māṃ rathenāśu vaha yatra bravīmyaham ||27||

28tatheti ca prāha nṛpo nirviśaṅkastapodhanam
|
krīḍāratho'stu bhagavannuta sāṃgrāmiko rathaḥ ||28||

29ityuktaḥ sa munī rājñā tena hṛṣṭena tadvacaḥ
|
cyavanaḥ pratyuvācedaṃ hṛṣṭaḥ parapurañjayam ||29||

30sajjīkuru rathaṃ kṣipraṃ yaste sāṃgrāmiko mataḥ
|
sāyudhaḥ sapatākaśca śaktīkanakayaṣṭimān ||30||

31kiṅkiṇīsvananirghoṣo yuktastoraṇakalpanaiḥ
|
jāmbūnada‌nibaddhaśca parameṣuśatānvitaḥ ||31||

32tataḥ sa taṃ tathetyuktvā kalpayitvā mahāratham
|
bhāryāṃ vāme dhuri tadā cātmānaṃ dakṣiṇe tathā ||32||

33tridaṇḍaṃ vajrasūcyagraṃ pratodaṃ tatra cādadhat
|
sarvametattathā datvā nṛpo vākyamathābravīt ||33||

34bhagavankva ratho yātu bravītu bhṛgunandana
|
yatra vakṣyasi viprarṣe tatra yāsyati te rathaḥ ||34||

35evamuktastu bhagavānpratyuvācātha taṃ nṛpam
|
itaḥ prabhṛti yātavyaṃ padakaṃ padakaṃ śanaiḥ ||35||

36śramo mama yathā na syāttathā macchaṃdacāriṇau
|
susukhaṃ caiva voḍhavyo janaḥ sarvaśca paśyatu ||36||

37notsāryāḥ pathikāḥ kecittebhyo dāsye vasuhyaham
|
brāhmaṇebhyaśca ye kāmānarthayiṣyanti māṃ pathi ||37||

38sarvāndāsyāmyaśeṣeṇa dhanaṃ ratnāni caiva hi
|
kriyatāṃ nikhilenaitanmā vicāraya pārthiva ||38||

39tasya tadvacanaṃ śrutvā rājā bhṛtyāṃstathā'bravīt
|
yadyadbrūyānmunistattatsarvaṃ deyamaśaṅkitaiḥ ||39||

40tato ratnanyanekāni striyo yugyamajāvikam
|
kṛtākṛtaṃ ca kanakaṃ gajendrāścācalopamāḥ ||40||

41anvagacchanta tamṛṣiṃ rājāmātyāśca sarvaśaḥ
|
hāhābhūtaṃ ca tatsarvamāsīnnagaramārtavat ||41||

42tau tīkṣṇāgreṇa sahasā pratodena pratoditau
|
pṛṣṭhe viddhau kaṭe caiva nirvikārau tamūhatuḥ ||42||

43vepamānau nirāhārau pañcāśadrātrakarśitau
|
kathañcidūhaturvīrau dampatī taṃ rathottamam ||43||

44bahuśo bhṛśaviddhau tau sravantau ca kṣatodbhavam
|
dadṛśāte mahārāja puṣpitāviva kiṃśukau ||44||

45tau dṛṣṭvā pauravargastu bhṛśaṃ śokasamākulaḥ
|
abhiśāpabhayatrasto na ca kiñciduvāca ha ||45||

46dvandvaśaścāvruvansarve paśyadhvaṃ tapaso balam
|
kruddhā api muniśreṣṭhaṃ vīkṣituṃ neha śaknumaḥ ||46||

47aho bhagavato vīryaṃ maharṣerbhāvitātmanaḥ
|
rājñaścāpi sabhāryasya dhairyaṃ paśyata yādṛśam ||47||

48śrāntāvapi hi kṛcchreṇa rathamenaṃ samūhatuḥ
|
na caitayorvikāraṃ vai dadarśa bhṛgunandanaḥ ||48||

49bhīṣma uvāca|
tataḥ sa nirvikārau tu dṛṣṭvā bhṛgukulodvaham
|
vasu viśrāṇayāmāsa yathā vaiśravaṇastathā ||49||

50tatrāpi rājā prītātmā yathādiṣṭamathākarot
|
tato'sya bhagavānprīto babhūva munisattamaḥ ||50||

51avatīrya rathaśreṣṭhāddampatī tau mumoca ha
|
vimocyaṃ caitau vidhivattato vākyamuvāca ha ||51||

52snigdhagambhīrayā vācā bhārgavaḥ suprasannayā
|
dadāni vāṃ varaṃ śreṣṭhaṃ taṃ brūtāmiti bhārata ||52||

53sukumārau ca tau viddhau karābhyāṃ munisattamaḥ
|
pasparśāmṛtakalpābhyāṃ snehādbharatasattama ||53||

54athābravīnnṛpo vākyaṃ śramo nāstyāvayoriha
|
viśrāntau ca prabhāvātte ūcatustau tu bhārgavam ||54||

55atha tau bhagavānprāha prahṛṣṭaścyavanastadā
|
na vṛthā vyāhṛtaṃ pūrvaṃ yanmayā tadbhaviṣyati ||55||

56ramaṇīyaḥ samuddeśo gaṅgātīramidaṃ śubham
|
kiñcitkālaṃ vrataparo nivatsyāmīha pārthiva ||56||

57gamyatāṃ svapuraṃ putra viśrāntaḥ punareṣyasi
|
ihasthaṃ māṃ sabhāryastvaṃ draṣṭāsi śvo narādhipa
|
na ca manyustvayā kāryaḥ śreyaste samupasthitam ||57||

58yatkāṃkṣitaṃ hṛdisthaṃ te tatsarvaṃ hi bhaviṣyati
|
ityevamuktaḥ kuśikaḥ prahṛṣṭenāntarātmanā ||58||

59provāca muniśārdūlamidaṃ vacanamarthavat
|
na me manyurmahābhāga pūtrau svo bhagavaṃstvayā ||59||

60saṃvṛtau yauvanasthau svo vapuṣmaṃtau balānvitau
|
pratodena vraṇā ye me sabhāryasya tvayā kṛtāḥ ||60||

61tānna paśyāmi gātreṣu svastho'smi saha bhāryayā
|
imāṃ ca devīṃ paśyāmi vapuṣā'psarasopamām ||61||

62śriyā paramayā yuktā tathā dṛṣṭā purā mayā
|
tava prasādasaṃvṛttamidaṃ sarvaṃ mahāmune ||62||

63naitaccitraṃ tu bhagavaṃstvayi satyaparākrama
|
ityuktaḥ pratyuvācainaṃ kuśikaṃ cyavanastadā ||63||

64āgacchethāḥ sabhāryaśca tvamiheti narādhipa
|
ityuktaḥ samanujñāto rājarṣirabhivādya tam ||64||

65prayayau vapuṣā yukto nagaraṃ devarājavat
|
tata enamupājagmuramātyāḥ sapurohitāḥ ||65||

66balasthā gaṇikāyuktāḥ sarvāḥ prakṛtayastathā
|
tairvṛtaḥ kuśiko rājā śriyā paramayā jvalan ||66||

67praviveśa puraṃ hṛṣṭaḥ pūjyamāno'tha baṃdibhiḥ
|
tataḥ praviśya nagaraṃ kṛtvā paurvāhṇikīḥ kriyāḥ
|
bhuktvā sabhāryo rajanīmuvāsa sa mahādyutiḥ ||67||

68tatastu tau navamabhivīkṣya yauvanaṃ parasparaṃ vigatarujāvivāmarau
|
nanandatuḥ śayanagatau vapurdharau śriyā yutau dvijavaradattayā tadā ||68||

69athāpyṛṣirbhṛgukulakīrtivardhanastapodhano vanamabhirāmamṛddhimat
|
manīṣayā bahuvidharatnabhūṣitaṃ sasarja yanna puri śatakratorapi ||69||
