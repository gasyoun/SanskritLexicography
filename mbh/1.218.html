Mahābhārata 1.218 (Ādiparvan)

1vaiśaṃpāyana uvāca|
so'parānteṣu tīrthāni puṇyānyāyatanāni ca
|
sarvāṇyevānupūrvyeṇa jagāmāmitavikramaḥ||1||

2samudre paścime yāni tīrthānyāyatanāni ca
|
tāni sarvāṇi gatvā sa prabhāsamupajagmivān||2||

3prabhāsadeśaṃ saṃprāptaṃ bībhatsumaparājitam
|
supuṇyaṃ ramaṇīyaṃ ca śuśrāva madhusūdanaḥ||3||

4tato'bhyagacchatkaunteyaṃ sakhāyaṃ tatra mādhavaḥ
|
dadṛśāte tadānyonyaṃ prabhāse kṛṣṇapāṇḍavau||4||

5tāvanyonyaṃ samāśliṣya pṛṣṭvā ca kuśalaṃ vane
|
āstāṃ priyasakhāyau tau naranārāyaṇāvṛṣī||5||

6tatorjunaṃ vāsudevastāṃ caryāṃ paryapṛcchata
|
kimarthaṃ pāṇḍavaitāni tīrthānyanucarasyuta||6||

7tato'rjano yathāvṛttaṃ sarvamākhyātavāṃstadā
|
śrutvovāca ca vārṣṇeya evametaditi prabhuḥ||7||

8tau vihṛtya yathākāmaṃ prabhāse kṛṣṇapāṇḍavau
|
mahīdharaṃ raivatakaṃ vāsāyaivābhijagmatuḥ||8||

9pūrvameva tu kṛṣṇasya vacanāttaṃ mahīdharam
|
puruṣā maṇḍayāñcakrurupajahraśca bhojanam||9||

10pratigṛhyārjunaḥ sarvamupabhujya ca pāṇḍavaḥ
|
sahaiva vāsudevena dṛṣṭavānnaṭanartakān||10||

11abhyanujñāya tānsarvānarcayitvā ca pāṇḍavaḥ
|
satkṛtaṃ śayanaṃ divyamabhyagacchanmahāmatiḥ||11||

12tatastatra mahābāhuḥ śayānaḥ śayane śubhe
|
tīrthānāṃ palvalānāṃ ca parvatānāṃ ca darśanam
|
āpagānāṃ vanānāṃ ca kathayāmāsa sātvate||12||

13evaṃ sa kathayanneva nidrayā janamejaya
|
kaunteyopi hṛtastasmiñśayane svargasannibhe||13||

14madhureṇaiva gītena vīṇāśabdena caiva ha
|
prabodhyamāno bubudhe stutibhirmaṅgalaistathā||14||

15sa kṛtvā'vaśyakāryāṇi vārṣṇeyenābhinanditaḥ
|
rathena kāñcanāṅgena dvārakāmabhijagmivān||15||

16alaṅkṛtā dvārakā tu babhūva janamejaya
|
kuntīputrasya pūjārthamapi niṣkuṭakeṣvapi||16||

17didṛkṣantaśca kaunteyaṃ dvārakāvāsino janāḥ
|
narendramārgamājagmustūrṇaṃ śatasahasraśaḥ||17||

18avalokeṣu nārīṇāṃ sahasrāṇi śatāni ca
|
bhojavṛṣṇyandhakānāṃ ca samavāyo mahānabhūt||18||

19sa tathā satkṛtaḥ sarvairbhojavṛṣṇyandhakātmajaiḥ
|
abhivādyābhivādyāṃśca sarvaiśca pratinanditaḥ||19||

20kumāraiḥ sarvaśo vīraḥ satkāreṇābhicoditaḥ
|
samānavayasaḥ sarvānāśliṣya sa punaḥpunaḥ||20||

21kṛṣṇasya svabhavane ramye ratnabhojyasamāvṛte
|
upavāsa saha kṛṣṇena bahulāstatra śarvarīḥ||21||
