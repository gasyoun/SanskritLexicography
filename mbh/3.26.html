Mahābhārata 3.26 (Āraṇyakaparvan)

1vaiśampāyana uvāca|
vasatsu vai dvaitavane pāṇḍaveṣu mahātmasu
|
anukīrṇaṃ mahāraṇyaṃ brāhmaṇaiḥ samapadyata||1||

2īryamāṇena satataṃ brahmaghoṣeṇa sarvaśaḥ
|
brahmalokasamaṃ puṇyamāsīddvaitavanaṃ saraḥ||2||

3yajuṣāmṛcāṃ sāmnāṃ ca gadyānāṃ caiva sarvaśaḥ
|
āsīduccāryamāṇānāṃ niḥsvano hṛdayaṅgamaḥ||3||

4jyāghoṣaścaiva pārthānāṃ brahmaghoṣaśca dhīmatām
|
saṃsṛṣṭaṃ brahmaṇā kṣatraṃ bhūya eva vyarocata||4||

5athābravīdbako dālbhyo dharmarājaṃ yudhiṣṭhiram
|
sandhyāṃ kaunteyamāsīnamṛṣibhiḥ parivāritam||5||

6paśya dvaitavane pārtha brāhmaṇānāṃ tapasvinām
|
homavelāṃ kuruśreṣṭha samprajvalitapāvakām||6||

7caranti dharmaṃ puṇye'smiṃstvayā guptā dhṛtavratāḥ
|
bhṛgavo'ṅgirasaścaiva vāsiṣṭhāḥ kāśyapaiḥ saha||7||

8āgastyāśca mahābhāgā ātreyāścottamavratāḥ
|
sarvasya jagataḥ śreṣṭhā brāhmaṇāḥ saṅgatāstvayā||8||

9idaṃ tu vacanaṃ pārtha śṛṇuṣva gadato mama
|
bhrātṛbhiḥ saha kaunteya yattvāṃ vakṣyāmi kaurava||9||

10brahma kṣatreṇa saṃsṛṣṭaṃ kṣatraṃ ca brahmaṇā saha
|
udīrṇe dahataḥ śatrūnvanānīvāgnimārutau||10||

11nābrāhmaṇastāta ciraṃ bubhūṣedicchannimaṃ lokamamuṃ ca jetum
|
vinītadharmārthamapetamohaṃ labdhvā dvijaṃ nudati nṛpaḥ sapatnān||11||

12carannaiḥśreyasaṃ dharmaṃ prajāpālanakāritam
|
nādhyagacchadbalirloke tīrthamanyatra vai dvijāt||12||

13anūnamāsīdasurasya kāmairvairocaneḥ śrīrapi cākṣayāsīt
|
labdhvā mahīṃ brāhmaṇasamprayogātteṣvācaranduṣṭamatho vyanaśyat||13||

14nābrāhmaṇaṃ bhūmiriyaṃ sabhūtirvarṇaṃ dvitīyaṃ bhajate cirāya
|
samudranemirnamate tu tasmai yaṃ brāhmaṇaḥ śāsti nayairvinītam||14||

15kuñjarasyeva saṅgrāme parigṛhyāṅkuśagraham
|
brāhmaṇairviprahīnasya kṣatrasya kṣīyate balam||15||

16brāhmaṇyanupamā dṛṣṭiḥ kṣātramapratimaṃ balam
|
tau yadā carataḥ sārdhaṃ tadā lokaḥ prasīdati||16||

17yathā hi sumahānagniḥ kakṣaṃ dahati sānilaḥ
|
tathā dahati rājanyo brāhmaṇena samaṃ ripum||17||

18brāhmaṇeṣveva medhāvī buddhiparyeṣaṇaṃ caret
|
alabdhasya ca lābhāya labdhasya parivṛddhaye||18||

19alabdhalābhāya ca labdhavṛddhaye yathārhatīrthapratipādanāya
|
yaśasvinaṃ vedavidaṃ vipaścitaṃ bahuśrutaṃ brāhmaṇameva vāsaya||19||

20brāhmaṇeṣūttamā vṛttistava nityaṃ yudhiṣṭhira
|
tena te sarvalokeṣu dīpyate prathitaṃ yaśaḥ||20||

21vaiśaṃpāyana uvāca|
tataste brāhmaṇāḥ sarve bakaṃ dālbhyamapūjayan
|
yudhiṣṭhire stūyamāne bhūyaḥ sumanaso'bhavan||21||

22dvaipāyano nāradaśca jāmadagnyaḥ pṛthuśravāḥ
|
indradyumno bhālukiśca kṛtacetāḥ sahasrapāt||22||

23karṇaśravāśca muñjaśca lavaṇāśvaśca kāśyapaḥ
|
hārītaḥ sthūṇakarṇaśca agniveśyo'tha śaunakaḥ||23||

24kṛtavākca suvākcaiva bṛhadaśvo vibhāvasuḥ
|
ūrdhvaretā vṛṣāmitraḥ suhotro hotravāhanaḥ||24||

25ete cānye ca bahavo brāhmaṇāḥ saṃśitavratāḥ
|
ajātaśatrumānarcuḥ puraṃdaramivarṣayaḥ||25||
