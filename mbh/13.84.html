Mahābhārata 13.84 (Anuśāsanaparvan)

1yudhiṣṭhira uvāca|
uktaṃ pitāmahenedaṃ gavāṃ dānamanuttamam
|
viśeṣeṇa narendrāṇāmiha dharmamavekṣatām ||1||

2rājyaṃ hi satataṃ duḥkhaṃ durdharaṃ cākṛtātmabhiḥ
|
bhūyiṣṭhaṃ ca narendrāṇāṃ vidyate na śubhā gatiḥ ||2||

3pūyante tatra niyataṃ prayacchanto vasundharām
|
sarve ca kathitā dharmāstvayā me kurunandana ||3||

4evameva gavāmuktaṃ pradānaṃ te nṛgeṇa ha
|
ṛṣiṇā nāciketena pūrvameva nidarśitam ||4||

5vedopaniṣadaścaiva sarvakarmasu dakṣiṇāḥ
|
sarvakratuṣu coddiṣṭaṃ bhūmirgāvo'tha kāñcanam ||5||

6tatra śrutistu paramā suvarṇaṃ dakṣiṇeti vai
|
etadicchāmyahaṃ śrotuṃ pitāmaha yathātatham ||6||

7kiṃ suvarṇaṃ kathaṃ jātaṃ kasminkāle kimātmakam
|
kiṃ daivaṃ kiṃ phalaṃ caiva kasmācca paramucyate ||7||

8kasmāddānaṃ suvarṇasya pūjayanti manīṣiṇaḥ
|
kasmācca dakṣiṇārthaṃ tadyajñakarmasu śasyate ||8||

9kasmācca pāvanaṃ śreṣṭhaṃ bhūmergobhyaśca kāñcanam
|
paramaṃ dakṣiṇārthe ca tadbravīhi pitāmaha ||9||

10bhīṣma uvāca|
śṛṇu rājannavahito bahukāraṇavistaram
|
jātarūpasamutpattimanubhūtaṃ ca yanmayā ||10||

11pitā mama mahātejāḥ śāntanurnidhanaṃ gataḥ
|
tasya ditsurahaṃ śrāddhaṃ gaṅgādvāramupāgamam ||11||

12tatrā''gamya pituḥ putra śrāddhakarma samārabham
|
mātā me jāhnavī cātra sāhāyyamakarottadā ||12||

13tato'gratastataḥ siddhānupaveśya bahūnṛṣīn
|
toyapradānātprabhṛti kāryāṇyahamathārabham ||13||

14tatsamāpya yathoddiṣṭaṃ pūrvakarma samāhitaḥ
|
dātuṃ nirvapaṇaṃ samyagyathāvadahamārabham ||14||

15tatastaṃ darbhavinyāsaṃ bhittvā surucirāṅgadaḥ
|
pralambābharaṇo bāhuruda‌tiṣṭhadviśāmpate ||15||

16tamutthitamahaṃ dṛṣṭvā paraṃ vismayamāgamam
|
pratigrahītā sākṣānme piteti bharatarṣabha ||16||

17tato me punarevāsītsaṃjñā sañcintya śāstrataḥ
|
nāyaṃ vedeṣu vihito vidhirhasta iti prabho ||17||

18piṇḍo deyo nareṇeha tato matirabhūnmama
|
sākṣānneha manuṣyasya piṇḍaṃ hi pitaraḥ kvacit ||18||

19gṛhṇanti vihitaṃ cetthaṃ piṇḍo deyaḥ kuśeṣviti
|
tato'haṃ tadanādṛtya piturhastanidarśanam ||19||

20śāstraprāmāṇyasūkṣmaṃ tu vidhiṃ piṇḍasya saṃsmaran
|
tato darbheṣu tatsarvamadadaṃ bharatarṣabha ||20||

21śāstramārgānusāreṇa tadviddhi manujarṣabha
|
tataḥ sontarhito bāhuḥ piturmama janādhipa ||21||

22tato māṃ darśayāmāsuḥ svapnānte pitarastathā
|
prīyamāṇāstu māmūcuḥ prītāḥ sma bharatarṣabha||22||

23vijñānena tavānena yanna muhyasi dharmataḥ
|
tvayā hi kurvatā śāstraṃ pramāṇamiha pārthiva ||23||

24ātmā dharmaḥ śrutaṃ vedāḥ pitaraścarṣibhiḥ saha
|
sākṣātpitāmaho brahmā guravo'tha prajāpatiḥ||24||

25pramāṇamupanītā vai sthitāśca na vicālitāḥ
|
tadidaṃ samyagārabdhaṃ tvayā'dya bharatarṣabha ||25||

26kiṃ tu bhūmergavāṃ cārthe suvarṇaṃ dīyatāmiti
|
evaṃ vayaṃ ca dharmajña sarve cāsmatpitāmahāḥ ||26||

27pāvitā vai bhaviṣyanti pāvanaṃ hi paraṃ hi tat
|
daśapūrvāndaśaivānyāṃstathāsantārayanti te ||27||

28suvarṇaṃ ye prayacchanti evaṃ matpitaro'bruvan
|
tato'haṃ vismito rājanpratibuddho viśāmpate ||28||

29suvarṇadāne'karavaṃ matiṃ ca bharatarṣabha
|
itihāsamimaṃ cāpi śṛṇu rājanpurātanam ||29||

30jāmadagnyaṃ prati vibho dhanyamāyuṣyameva ca
|
jāmadagnyena rāmeṇa tīvraroṣānvitena vai ||30||

31triḥ saptakṛtvaḥ pṛthivī kṛtā niḥkṣatriyā purā
|
tato jitvā mahīṃ kṛtsnāṃ rāmo rājīvalocanaḥ ||31||

32ājahāra kratuṃ vīro brahmakṣatreṇa pūjitam
|
vājimedhaṃ mahārāja sarvakāmasamanvitam ||32||

33pāvanaṃ sarvabhūtānāṃ tejodyutivivardhanam
|
vipāpmā ca sa tejasvī tena ṛtuphalena ca ||33||

34naivātmano'tha laghutāṃ jāmadagnyo'dhyagacchata
|
sa tu kratuvareṇeṣṭvā mahātmā dakṣiṇāvatā ||34||

35papracchāgama sampannānṛṣīndevāṃśca bhārgava
|
pāvanaṃ yatparaṃ nṝṇāmugre karmaṇi vartatām ||35||

36taducyatāṃ mahābhāgā iti jātaghṛṇo'bravīt
|
ityuktā vedaśāstrajñāstamūcuste maharṣayaḥ ||36||

37rāma viprāḥ satkriyantāṃ veda‌prāmāṇyadarśanāt
|
bhūyaśca viprarṣigaṇāḥ praṣṭavyāḥ pāvanaṃ prati ||37||

38te yadbrūyurmahāprājñāstaccaiva samudācara
|
tato vasiṣṭhaṃ devarṣimagastyamatha kāśyapam ||38||

39tamevārthaṃ mahātejāḥ papraccha bhṛgunandanaḥ
|
jātā matirme viprendrā kathaṃ pūyeyamityuta ||39||

40kena vā karmayogena pradāneneha kena vā
|
yadi vo'nugrahakṛtā buddhirmāṃ prati sattamāḥ
|
prabūta pāvanaṃ kiṃ me bhavediti tapodhanāḥ ||40||

41ṛṣaya ūcuḥ|
gāśca bhūmiṃ ca vittaṃ ca datveha bhṛgunandana
|
pāpakṛtpūyate martya iti bhārgava śuśruma ||41||

42anyaddānaṃ tu viprarṣe śrūyatāṃ pāvanaṃ mahat
|
divyamatyadbhutākāramapatyaṃ jātavedasaḥ ||42||

43dagdhvā lokānpurā vīryātsambhūtamiha śuśruma
|
suvarṇamiti vikhyātaṃ taddadatsiddhimeṣyasi ||43||

44tato'bravīdvasiṣṭhastaṃ bhagavānsaṃśitavrataḥ
|
śṛṇu rāma yathotpannaṃ suvarṇamanalaprabham ||44||

45phalaṃ dāsyati te yattu dāne paramihocyate
|
suvarṇaṃ yacca yasmācca yathā ca guṇavattamam ||45||

46tannibodha mahābāho sarvaṃ nigadato mama
|
agnīṣomātmakamidaṃ suvarṇaṃ viddhi niścaye ||46||

47ajo'gnirvaruṇo meṣaḥ sūryo'śva iti darśanam
|
kuñjarāśca mṛgā nāgā mahiṣāścāsurā iti ||47||

48kukkuṭāśca varāhāśca rākṣasā bhṛgunandana
|
iḍā gāvaḥ payaḥ somo bhūmirityeva ca smṛtiḥ||48||

49jagatsarvaṃ ca nirmithya tejorāśiḥ samutthitaḥ
|
suvarṇamebhyo viprarṣe ratnaṃ paramamuttamam ||49||

50etasmātkāraṇāddevā gandharvoragarākṣasāḥ
|
manuṣyāśca piśācāśca prayatā dhārayanti tat ||50||

51mukuṭairaṅgadayutairalaṅkāraiḥ pṛthagvidhaiḥ
|
suvarṇavikṛtaistatra virājante bhṛgūttama ||51||

52tasmātsarvapavitrebhyaḥ pavitraṃ paramaṃ smṛtam
|
bhūmergobhyo'tha ratnebhyastadviddhi manujarṣabha ||52||

53pṛthivīṃ gāśca datveha yaccānyadapi kiñcana
|
viśiṣyate suvarṇasya dānaṃ paramakaṃ vibho ||53||

54akṣayaṃ pāvanaṃ caiva suvarṇamamaradyute
|
prayaccha dvijamukhyebhyaḥ pāvanaṃ hyetaduttamam ||54||

55suvarṇameva sarvāsu dakṣiṇāsu vidhīyate
|
suvarṇaṃ ye prayacchanti sarvadāste bhavantyuta ||55||

56devatāste prayacchanti ye suvarṇaṃ dadatyatha
|
agnirhi devatāḥ sarvāḥ suvarṇaṃ ca tadātmakam ||56||

57tasmātsuvarṇaṃ dadatā dattāḥ sarvāḥ sma devatāḥ
|
bhavanti puruṣavyāghra na hyataḥ paramaṃ viduḥ ||57||

58bhūya eva ca māhātmyaṃ suvarṇasya nibodha me
|
gadato mama viprarṣe sarvaśastrabhṛtāṃ vara ||58||

59mayā śrutamidaṃ pūrvaṃ purāṇe bhṛgunandana
|
prajāpateḥ kathayato yathānyāyaṃ tu tasya vai ||59||

60śūlapāṇerbhagavato rudrasya ca mahātmanaḥ
|
girau himavati śreṣṭhe tadā bhṛgukulodvaha ||60||

61devyā vivāhe nirvṛtte rudrāṇyā bhṛgunandana
|
samāgame bhagavato devyā saha mahātmanaḥ ||61||

62tataḥ sarve samudvignā devā rudramupāgaman
|
te mahādevamāsīnaṃ devīṃ ca varadāmumām ||62||

63prasādya śirasā sarve rudramūcurbhṛgūdvaha
|
ayaṃ samāgamo devo devyā saha tavānagha ||63||

64tapasvinastapasvinyā tejasvinyā'titejasaḥ
|
amoghatejāstvaṃ deva devī ceyamumā tathā ||64||

65apatyaṃ yuvayordeva balavadbhavitā vibho
|
tannūnaṃ triṣu lokeṣu na kiñciccheṣayiṣyati ||65||

66tadebhyaḥ praṇatebhyastvaṃ devebhyaḥ pṛthulocana
|
varaṃ prayaccha lokeśa trailokyahitakāmyayā ||66||

67apatyārthaṃ nigṛhṇīṣva tejaḥ paramakaṃ vibho
|
trailokyasārau hi yuvāṃ lokaṃ santāpayiṣyathaḥ||67||

68tadapatyaṃ hi yuvayordevānabhibhaveddhruvam
|
na hi te pṛthivī devī na ca dyaurna divaṃ vibho ||68||

69nedaṃ dhārayituṃ śaktāḥ samastā iti me matiḥ
|
tejaḥprabhāvanirdagdhaṃ tasmātsarvamidaṃ jagat ||69||

70tasmātprasādaṃ bhagavankartumarhasi naḥ prabho
|
na devyāṃ sambhavetputro bhavataḥ surasattama
|
dhairyādeva nigṛhṇīṣva tejo jvalitamuttamam ||70||

71iti teṣāṃ kathayatāṃ bhagavānvṛṣabhadhvajaḥ
|
evamastviti devāṃstānviprarṣe pratyabhāṣata ||71||

72ityuktvā cordhvamanayadreto vṛṣabhavāhanaḥ
|
ūrdhvaretāḥ samabhavattataḥ prabhṛti cāpi saḥ ||72||

73rudrāṇīti tataḥ kruddhā prajocchede tadā kṛte
|
devānathābravīttatra strībhāvātparuṣaṃ vacaḥ ||73||

74yasmādapatyakāmo vai bhartā me vinivartitaḥ
|
tasmātsarve surā yūyamanapatyā bhaviṣyatha ||74||

75prajocchedo mama kṛto yasmādyuṣmābhiradya vai
|
tasmātprajā vaḥ khagamāḥ sarveṣāṃ na bhaviṣyati ||75||

76pāvakastu na tatrāsīcchāpakāle bhṛgūdvaha
|
devā devyāstathā śāpādanapatyāstato'bhavan ||76||

77rudrastu tejo'pratimaṃ dhārayāmāsa vai tadā
|
praskannaṃ tu tatastasmātkiñcittatrāpatadbhuvi ||77||

78utpapāta tadā vahnau vavṛdhe cādbhutopamam
|
tejastejasi saṃyuktamātmayonitvamāgatam ||78||

79etasminneva kāle tu devāḥ śakrapurogamāḥ
|
asurastārako nāma tena santāpitā bhṛśam||79||

80ādityā vasavo rudrā maruto'thāśvināvapi
|
sādhyāśca sarve saṃtrastā daiteyasya parākramāt ||80||

81sthānāni devatānāṃ hi vimānāni purāṇi ca
|
ṛṣīṇāṃ cāśramāścaiva babhūvurasurairhṛtāḥ ||81||

82te dīnamanasaḥ sarve devatā ṛṣayaśca ye
|
prajagmuḥ śaraṇaṃ devaṃ brahmāṇamajaraṃ vibhum ||82||
