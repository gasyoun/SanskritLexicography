Mahābhārata 9.28 (Śalyaparvan)

1sañjaya uvāca|
tasmin pravṛtte saṅgrāme gajavājinarakṣaye
|
śakuniḥ saubalo rājan sahadevaṃ samabhyayāt||1||

2tato'syāpatatastūrṇaṃ sahadevaḥ pratāpavān
|
śaraughān preṣayāmāsa pataṅgāniva śīghragān||2||

3ulūkaśca raṇe bhīmaṃ vivyādha daśabhiḥ śaraiḥ
|
śakuniśca mahārāja bhīmaṃ viddhvā tribhiḥ śaraiḥ||3||

4sāyakānāṃ navatyā vai sahadevamavākirat
|
te śūrāḥ samare rājan samāsādya parasparam||4||

5vivyadhurniśitairbāṇaiḥ kaṅkabarhiṇavājitaiḥ
|
svarṇapuṅgaiḥ śilādhauterākarṇaprahitaiḥ śaraiḥ||5||

6teṣāṃ cāpabhujotsṛṣṭā śaravṛṣṭiviśāmpate
|
ācchādayaddiśaḥ sarvā dhārā iva payomucaḥ||6||

7tataḥ kruddho raṇe bhīmaḥ sahadevaśca bhārata
|
ceratuḥ kadanaṃ saṅkhye kurvantau sumahābalau||7||

8tābhyāṃ śaraśataiśchannaṃ tadbalaṃ tava bhārata
|
sāndhakāramivākāśamabhavattatra tatra ha||8||

9aśvairviparidhāvadbhiḥ śaracchannairviśāmpate
|
tatra tatra vṛto mārgo vikarṣadbhirhatān bahūn||9||

10nihatānāṃ hayānāṃ ca sahaiva hayasādibhiḥ
|
varmabhirvinikṛttaiśca prāsaiśchinnaiśca māriṣa||10||

11ṛṣṭibhiḥ śaktibhiścaiva sāsiprāsaparaśvadhaiḥ
|
sañchannā pṛthivī jajñe kusumaiḥ śabalā iva||11||

12yodhāstatra mahārāja samāsādya parasparam
|
vyacaranta raṇe kruddhā vinighnantaḥ parasparam||12||

13udvattanayanai roṣātsandaṣṭauṣṭhapuṭairmukhaiḥ
|
sakuṇḍalairmahī cchannā padmakiñjalkasannibhaiḥ||13||

14bhujaiśchinnairmahārāja nāgarājakaropamaiḥ
|
sāṅgadaiḥ satanutraiśca sāsiprāsaparaśvadhaiḥ||14||

15kabandhairutthitaiśchinnairnṛtyadbhiścāparairyudhi
|
kravyādagaṇasañchannā ghorābhūtpṛthivī vibho||15||

16alpāvaśiṣṭe sainye tu kauraveyānmahāhave
|
prahṛṣṭāḥ pāṇḍavā bhūtvā ninyire yamasādanam||16||

17etasminnantare śūraḥ saubaleyaḥ pratāpavān
|
prāsena sahadevasya śirasi prāharadbhṛśam||17||

18sa vihvalo mahārāja rathopastha upāviśat
|
sahadevaṃ tathā dṛṣṭvā bhīmasenaḥ pratāpavān||18||

19sarvasainyāni saṅkruddho vārayāmāsa bhārata
|
nirbibheda ca nārācaiḥ śataśo'tha sahasraśaḥ||19||

20vinirbhidyākaroccaiva siṃhanādamarindamaḥ
|
tena śabdena vitrastāḥ sarve sahayavāraṇāḥ||20||

21prādravansahasā bhītāḥ śakuneśca padānugāḥ
|
prabhagnānatha tāndṛṣṭvā rājā duryodhano'bravīt||21||

22nivartadhvamadharmajñā yudhyadhvaṃ kiṃ sṛtena vaḥ
|
iha kīrtiṃ samādhāya pretya lokānsamaśnute||22||

23prāṇāñjahāti yo dhīro yuddhe pṛṣṭhamadarśayan
|
evamuktāstu te rājñā saubalasya padānugāḥ||23||

24pāṇḍavānabhyavartanta mṛtyuṃ kṛtvā nivartanam
|
dravadbhistatra rājendra kṛtaḥ śabdo'tidāruṇaḥ||24||

25kṣubdhasāgarasaṅkāśāḥ kṣubhitāḥ sarvato'bhavan
|
tāṃstathā purato dṛṣṭvā saubalasya padānugān||25||

26pratyudyayurmahārāja pāṇḍavā vijayodyatāḥ
|
pratyāśvasya ca durdharṣaḥ sahadevo viśāmpate||26||

27śakuniṃ daśabhirviddhvā hayāṃścāsya tribhiḥ śaraiḥ
|
dhanuściccheda ca śaraiḥ saubalasya hasanniva||27||

28athānyaddhanurādāya śakuniryuddhadurmadaḥ
|
vivyādha nakulaṃ ṣaṣṭyā bhīmasenaṃ ca saptabhiḥ||28||

29ulūko'pi mahārāja bhīmaṃ vivyādha saptabhiḥ
|
sahadevaṃ ca saptatyā parīpsanpitaraṃ raṇe||29||

30taṃ bhīmasenaḥ samare vivyādha navabhiḥ śaraiḥ
|
śakuniṃ ca catuḥṣaṣṭyā pārśvasthāṃśca tribhistribhiḥ||30||

31te hanyamānā bhīmena nārācaistailapāyitaiḥ
|
sahadevaṃ raṇe kruddhāśchādayañśaravṛṣṭibhiḥ||31||

32parvataṃ vāridhārābhiḥ savidyuta ivāmbudāḥ
|
tato'syāpatataḥ śūrāḥ sahadevaḥ pratāpavān||32||

33ulūkasya mahārāja bhallenāpāharacchiraḥ
|
sa jagāma rathādbhūmiṃ sahadevena pātitaḥ||33||

34rudhirāplutasarvāṅgo nandayanpāṇḍavān yudhi
|
putraṃ tu nihataṃ dṛṣṭvā śakunistatra bhārata||34||

35sāśrukaṇṭho viniḥśvasya kṣatturvākyamanusmaran
|
cintayitvā muhūrtaṃ sa bāṣpapūrṇekṣaṇaḥ śvasan||35||

36sahadevaṃ samāsādya tribhirvivyādha sāyakaiḥ
|
tānapāsya śarānmuktāśarasaṅdhaiḥ pratāpavān||36||

37sahadevo mahārāja dhanuściccheda saṃyuge
|
chinne dhanuṣi rājendra śakuniḥ saubalastadā||37||

38pragṛhya vipulaṃ khaḍgaṃ sahadevāya prāhiṇot
|
tamāpatantaṃ sahasā ghorarūpaṃ viśāmpate||38||

39dvidhā ciccheda samare saubalasya hasanniva
|
asiṃ dṛṣṭvā tathā cchinnaṃ pragṛhya mahatīṃ gadām||39||

40prāhiṇotsahadevāya sā moghā nyapatadbhuvi
|
tataḥ śaktiṃ mahāghorāṃ kālarātrīmivodyatām||40||

41preṣayāmāsa saṅkruddhaḥ pāṇḍavaṃ prati saubalaḥ
|
tāmāpatantīṃ sahasā śaraiḥ kanakabhūṣaṇaiḥ||41||

42tridhā ciccheda samare sahadevo hasanniva
|
sā papāta tridhā cchinnā bhūmau kanakabhūṣaṇā||42||

43śīryamāṇā yathā dīptā gaganādvai śatahradā
|
śaktiṃ vinihatāṃ dṛṣṭvā saubalaṃ ca bhayārditam||43||

44dudruvustāvakāḥ sarve bhaye jāte sasaubalāḥ
|
athotkruṣṭaṃ mahaccāsītpāṇḍavairjitakāśibhiḥ||44||

45dhārtarāṣṭrāstataḥ sarve prāyaśo vimukhābhavan
|
tānvai vimanaso dṛṣṭvā mādrīputraḥ pratāpavān||45||

46śarairanekasāhasrairvārayāmāsa saṃyuge
|
tato gāndhārakairguptaṃ puṣṭairaśvairjaye dhṛtam||46||

47āsasāda raṇe yāntaṃ sahadevo'tha saubalam
|
svamaṃśamavaśiṣṭaṃ taṃ saṃsmṛtya śakuni nṛpa||47||

48rathena kāñcanāṅgena sahadevaḥ samabhyayāt
|
adhijyaṃ balavatkṛtvā vyākṣipansumahaddhanuḥ||48||

49sa saubalamabhidutya gārdhrapatraiḥ śilāśitaiḥ
|
bhṛśamabhyahanatkruddhastotrairiva mahādvipam||49||

50uvāca cainaṃ medhāvī vigṛhya smārayanniva
|
kṣatradharme sthiro bhūtvā yudhyasva puruṣo bhava||50||

51yattadā hṛṣyase mūḍha glahannakṣaiḥ sabhātale
|
phalamadya prapaśyasva karmaṇastasya durmate||51||

52nihattāste durātmāno ye'smānavahasanpurā
|
duryodhanaḥ kulāṅgāraḥ śiṣṭastvaṃ cāsya mātulaḥ||52||

53adya te nihanaṣyāmi kṣureṇonmathitaṃ śiraḥ
|
vṛkṣātphalamivāviddhaṃ laguḍena pramāthinā||53||

54evamuktvā mahārāja sahadevo mahābalaḥ
|
saṅkruddho raṇaśārdūlo vegenābhijagāma tam||54||

55abhigamya sudurdharṣaḥ sahadevo yudhāṃ patiḥ
|
vikṛṣya balavaccāpaṃ krodhena prajvalanniva||55||

56śakuniṃ daśabhirviddhvā caturbhiścāsya vājinaḥ
|
chatraṃ dhvajaṃ dhanuścāsya cchittvā siṃha ivānadat||56||

57chinnadhvajadhanuśchatraḥ sahadevena saubalaḥ
|
kṛto viddhaśca bahubhiḥ sarvamarmasu sāyakaiḥ||57||

58tato bhūyo mahārāja sahadevaḥ pratāpavān
|
śakuneḥ preṣayāmāsa śaravṛṣṭiṃ durāsadām||58||

59tatastu kruddhaḥ subalasya putro mādrīsutaṃ sahadevaṃ vimarde
|
prāsena jāmbūnadabhūṣaṇena jighāṃsureko'bhipapāta śīghram||59||

60mādrīsutastasya samudyataṃ taṃ prāsaṃ suvṛttau ca bhujau raṇāgre
|
bhallaistribhiryugapatsañcakarta nanāda coccaistarasā''jimadhye||60||

61tasyāśukārī susamāhitena suvarṇapuṅkhena dṛḍhāyasena
|
bhallena sarvāvaraṇātigena śiraḥ śarīrātpramamātha bhūyaḥ||61||

62śareṇa kārtasvarabhūṣitena divākarābheṇa susaṃhitena
|
hṛtottamāṅgo yudhi pāṇḍavena papāta bhūmau subalasya putraḥ||62||

63sa tacchiro vegavatā śareṇa suvarṇapuṅkhena śirāśitena
|
prāverayatkupitaḥ pāṇḍuputro yattatkurūṇāmanayasya mūlam||63||

64bhujau suvṛttau pracakarta vīraḥ paścātkabandhaṃ rudhirāvasiktam
|
vispandamānaṃ nipapāta ghoraṃ rathottamātpārthiva pārthivasya||64||

65hṛtottamāṅjaṃ śakuniṃ samīkṣya bhūmau śayānaṃ rudhirārdragātram
|
yodhāstvadīyā bhayanaṣṭasattvā diśaḥ prajagmuḥ pragṛhītaśastrāḥ||65||

66pravidrutāḥ śuṣkamukhā visaṃjñā gāṇḍīvaghoṣeṇa samāhatāśca
|
bhayārditā bhagnarathāśvanāgāḥ padātayaścaiva sadhārtarāṣṭrāḥ||66||

67tato rathācchukuniṃ pātayitvā mudānvitā bhārata pāṇḍaveyāḥ
|
śaṅkhānpradadhmuḥ samare'tihṛṣṭāḥ sakeśavāḥ sainikānharṣayantaḥ||67||

68taṃ cāpi sarve pratipūjayanto dṛṣṭvā bruvāṇāḥ sahadevamājau
|
diṣṭyā hato naikṛtiko mahātmā sahātmajo vīra raṇe tvayeti||68||
