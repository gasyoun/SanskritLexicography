Mahābhārata 12.242 (Śāntiparvan)

1śuka uvāca |
kṣarātprabhṛti yaḥ sargaḥ saguṇānīndriyāṇi ca
|
buddhyaiśvaryātisargo'yaṃ pradhānaścātmanaḥ śrutam ||1||

2bhūya eva tu loke'smin sadvṛttiṃ kālahaitukīm
|
yayā santaḥ pravartante tadicchāmyanuvartitum ||2||

3vede vacanamuktaṃ tu kuru karma tyajeti ca
|
kathametadvijānīyāṃ tacca vyākhyātumarhasi ||3||

4lokavṛttāntatattvajñaḥ pūto'haṃ guruśāsanāt
|
kṛtvā buddhiṃ vimuktātmā drakṣyāmyātmānamavyayam ||4||

5vyāsa uvāca |
yathā vai vihitā vṛttiḥ purastādbrahmaṇā svayam
|
eṣā pūrvataraiḥ sadbhirācīrṇā paramarṣibhiḥ ||5||

6brahmacaryeṇa vai lokān jayanti paramarṣayaḥ
|
ātmanaśca tataḥ śreyāṃsyanvicchanmanasā''tmani ||6||

7vane mūlaphalāśī ca tapyansuvipulaṃ tapaḥ
|
puṇyāyatanacārī ca bhūtānāmavihiṃsakaḥ ||7||

8vidhūme sannamusale vānaprasthapratiśraye
|
kāle prāpte caranbhaikṣyaṃ kalpate brahmabhūyase ||8||

9niḥstutinirmamaskāraḥ parityajya śubhāśubhe
|
araṇye vicaraikākī yena kenacidāśitaḥ ||9||

10śuka uvāca |
yadidaṃ vedavacanaṃ lokavāde virudhyate
|
pramāṇe vā'pramāṇe ca viruddhe śāstratā kutaḥ ||10||

11ityetacchrotumicchāmi pramāṇaṃ tūbhayaṃ katham
|
karmaṇāmavirodhena kathaṃ mokṣaḥ pravartate ||11||

12bhīṣma uvāca|
ityuktaḥ pratyuvācedaṃ gandhavatyāḥ sutaḥ sutam
|
ṛṣistatpūjayanvākyaṃ putrasyāmitatejasaḥ ||12||

13vyāsa uvāca |
brahmacārī gṛhasthaśca vānaprastho'tha bhikṣukaḥ
|
yathoktacāriṇaḥ sarve gacchanti paramāṃ gatim ||13||

14eko vā'pyāśramānetānyo'nutiṣṭhedyathāvidhi
|
akāmadveṣasaṃyuktaḥ sa paratra vidhīyate ||14||

15catuṣpadī hi niḥśreṇī brahmaṇyeṣā pratiṣṭhitā
|
etāmāruhya niḥśreṇīṃ brahmaloke mahīyate ||15||

16āyuṣastu caturbhāgaṃ brahmacāryanasūyakaḥ
|
gurau vā guruputre vā vaseddharmārthakovidaḥ ||16||

17jaghanyaśāyī pūrvaṃ syādutthāya guruveśmani
|
yacca śiṣyeṇa kartavyaṃ kāryaṃ dāsena vā punaḥ ||17||

18kṛtamityeva tatsarvaṃ kṛtvā tiṣṭheta pārśvataḥ
|
kiṃkaraḥ sarvakārī syātsarvakarmasu kovidaḥ ||18||

19karmātiśeṣeṇa gurāvadhyetavyaṃ bubhūṣatā
|
dakṣiṇo'napavādī syādāhūto gurumāśrayet ||19||

20śucirdakṣo guṇopeto brūyādiṣṭamivāntarā
|
cakṣuṣā gurumavyagro nirīkṣeta jitendriyaḥ ||20||

21nābhuktavati cāśnīyādapītavati no pibet
|
nātiṣṭhati tathā''sīta nāsupte prasvapeta ca ||21||

22uttānābhyāṃ ca pāṇibhyāṃ pādāvasya mṛdu spṛśet
|
dakṣiṇaṃ dakṣiṇenaiva savyaṃ savyena pīḍayet ||22||

23abhivādya guruṃ brūyādadhīṣva bhagavanniti
|
idaṃ kariṣye bhagavannidaṃ cāpi kṛtaṃ mayā ||23||

24brahmaṃstadapi kartā'smi yadbhavānvakṣyate punaḥ
|
iti sarvamanujñāpya nivedya ca yathāvidhi ||24||

25kuryātkṛtvā ca tatsarvamākhyeyaṃ gurave punaḥ
|
yāṃstu gandhān rasān vāpi brahmacārī na sevate ||25||

26seveta tānsamāvṛtya iti dharmeṣu niścayaḥ
|
ye kecidvistareṇoktā niyamā brahmacāriṇaḥ ||26||

27tānsarvānācarennityaṃ bhaveccānapago guroḥ
|
sa evaṃ gurave prītimupahatya yathābalam ||27||

28āśramādāśrameṣveva śiṣyo varteta karmaṇā
|
vedavratopavāsena caturthe cāyuṣo gate ||28||

29gurave dakṣiṇāṃ dattvā samāvartedyathāvidhi ||29||

30dharmalabdhairyuto dārairagnīnutpādya yatnataḥ
|
dvitīyamāyuṣo bhāgaṃ gahamedhī bhavedvratī ||30||
