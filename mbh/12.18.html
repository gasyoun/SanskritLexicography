Mahābhārata 12.18 (Śāntiparvan)

1vaiśampāyana uvāca|
tūṣṇīṃbhūtaṃ tu rājānaṃ punarevārjuno'bravīt
|
saṃtaptaḥ śokaduḥkhābhyāṃ rājavākśalyapīḍitaḥ ||1||

2arjuna uvāca |
kathayanti purāvṛttamitihāsamimaṃ janāḥ
|
videharājñaḥ saṃvādaṃ bhāryayā saha bhārata ||2||

3utsṛjya rājyaṃ bhikṣārthaṃ kṛtabuddhiṃ nareśvaram
|
videharājamahiṣī duḥkhitā ya‌dabhāṣata ||3||

4dhanānyapatyaṃ dārāśca ratnāni vividhāni ca
|
panthānaṃ pāvakaṃ hitvā janako mauḍhyamāsthitaḥ ||4||

5taṃ dadarśa priyā bhāryā bhaikṣyavṛttimakiñcanam
|
dhānāmuṣṭimupāsīnaṃ nirīhaṃ gatamatsaram ||5||

6tamuvāca samāgatya bhartāramakutobhayam
|
kruddhā manasvinī bhāryā vivikte hetumadvacaḥ ||6||

7kathamutsṛjya rājyaṃ svaṃ dhanadhānyasamanvitam
|
kāpālīṃ vṛttimāsthāya dhānāmuṣṭirna te varaḥ ||7||

8pratikṣā te'nyathā rājan viceṣṭhā cānyathā tava
|
yadrājyaṃ mahadutsṛjya svalpe tuṣyasi pārthiva ||8||

9naitenātithayo rājandevarṣipitarastathā
|
adya śakyāstvayā bhartuṃ moghaste'yaṃ pariśramaḥ ||9||

10devatātithibhiścaiva pitṛbhiścaiva pārthiva
|
sarvairetaiḥ parityaktaḥ parivrajasi niṣkriyaḥ ||10||

11yastvaṃ traividyavṛddhānāṃ brāhmaṇānāṃ sahasraśaḥ
|
bhartā bhūtvā ca lokasya so'dya tairbhṛtimicchasi ||11||

12śriyaṃ hitvā pradīptāṃ tvaṃ śvavatsamprati vīkṣyase
|
aputrā jananī te'dya kausalyā cāpatistvayā ||12||

13amī ca dharmakāmāstvāṃ kṣatriyāḥ paryupāsate
|
tvadāśāmabhikāṅkṣantaḥ kṛpaṇāḥ phalahetukāḥ ||13||

14tāṃśca tvaṃ viphalānkurvan kaṃ nu lokaṃ gamiṣyasi
|
rājansaṃśayite mokṣe paratantreṣu dehiṣu ||14||

15naiva te'sti paro loko nāparaḥ pāpakarmaṇaḥ
|
dharmyāndārānparityajya yastvamicchasi jīvitum ||15||

16srajo gandhānalaṅkārān vāsāṃsi vividhāni ca
|
kimarthamabhisaṃtyajya parivrajasi niṣkrayaḥ ||16||

17nipānaṃ sarvabhūtānāṃ bhūtvā tvaṃ pāvanaṃ mahat
|
āḍhyo vanaspatirbhūtvā so'nyāṃstvaṃ paryupāsase ||17||

18khāda‌nti hastinaṃ nyāsaiḥ kravyādā bahavo'pyuta
|
bahavaḥ kṛmayaścaiva kiṃ punastvāmanarthakam ||18||

19ya imāṃ kuṇḍikāṃ bhindyāttriviṣṭabdhaṃ ca yo haret
|
vāsaścāpi harettasmin kathaṃ te mānasaṃ bhavet ||19||

20yastvayaṃ sarvamutsṛjya dhānāmuṣṭeranugrahaḥ
|
yadā'nena samaṃ sarvaṃ kimidaṃ hyavasīyase ||20||

21dhanāmuṣṭerihārthaścetpratijñā te vinaśyati
|
kā vā'haṃ tava ko me tvaṃ kaśca te mayyanugrahaḥ ||21||

22praśādhi pṛthivīṃ rājan yadi te'nugraho bhavet
|
prāsādaṃ śayanaṃ yānaṃ vāsāṃsyābharaṇāni ca ||22||

23śriyā vihīnairadhanaistyaktamitrairakiñcanaiḥ
|
saukhikaiḥ saṃbhṛtānarthān yaḥ saṃtyajati kiṃ nu tat ||23||

24yo'tyantaṃ pratigṛhṇīyādyaśca dadyātsadaiva hi
|
tayostvamantaraṃ viddhi śreyāṃstābhyāṃ ka ucyate ||24||

25sadaiva yācamāneṣu tathā dambhānviteṣu ca
|
eteṣu dakṣiṇā dattā dāvāgnāviva durhutam ||25||

26jātavedā yathā rājannādagdhyaivopaśāmyati
|
sadaiva yācamāno hi tathā śāmyati vai dvijaḥ ||26||

27satāṃ vai dadato'nnaṃ ca loke'sminprakṛtirdhruvā
|
na cedrājā bhaveddātā kutaḥ syurmokṣakāṅkṣiṇaḥ ||27||

28annādgṛhasthā loke'smin bhikṣavastata eva ca
|
annātprāṇaḥ prabhavati annadaḥ prāṇado bhavet ||28||

29gṛhasthebhyo'pi nirmuktā gṛhasthāneva saṃśritāḥ
|
prabhavaṃ ca pratiṣṭhāṃ ca dāntā vindanta āsate ||29||

30tyāgānna bhikṣukaṃ vindyānna mauḍhyānna ca yācanāt
|
ṛjustu yo'rthaṃ tyajati na sukhaṃ viddhi bhikṣukam ||30||

31asaktaḥ saktavadgacchanniḥsaṅgo muktabandhanaḥ
|
samaḥ śatrau ca mitre ca sa vai mukto mahīpate ||31||

32parivrajanti dānārthaṃ muṇḍāḥ kāṣāyavāsasaḥ
|
sitā bahuvidhaiḥ pāśaiḥ sañcinvanto vṛthāmiṣam ||32||

33trayīṃ ca nāma vārtāṃ ca tyaktvā putrānvrajanti ye
|
triviṣṭabdhaṃ ca vāsaśca pratigṛhṇantyabuddhayaḥ ||33||

34aniṣkaṣāye kāṣāyamīhārthamiti viddhi tam
|
dharmadhvajānāṃ muṇḍānāṃ vṛttyarthamiti me mitiḥ ||34||

35kāṣāyairajinaiścīrairnagnānmuṇḍāñjaṭādharān
|
bibhratsādhūnmahārāja jaya lokāñjitendriyaḥ ||35||

36agnyādheyāni gurvarthaṃ kratūnapi sudakṣiṇān
|
dadātyaharahaḥ pūrvaṃ ko nu dharmaratastataḥ ||36||

37arjuna uvāca |
tattvajño janako rājā loke'sminniti gīyate
|
so'pyāsīnmohasaṃpanno mā mohavaśamanvagāḥ ||37||

38evaṃ dharmamanukrāntāḥ sada dānatapaḥparā
|
ānṛśaṃsyaguṇopetāḥ kāma-krodhavivarjitāḥ||38||

39prajānāṃ pālane yuktā dānamuttamamāsthitāḥ
|
iṣṭā~llokānavāpsyāmo guruvṛddhopacāyinaḥ ||39||

40devatātithibhūtānāṃ nirvapanto yathāvidhi
|
sthānamiṣṭamavāpsyāmo brahmaṇyāḥ satyavādinaḥ ||40||
