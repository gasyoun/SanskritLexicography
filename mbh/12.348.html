Mahābhārata 12.348 (Śāntiparvan)

1janamejaya uvāca|
aho hokantinaḥ sarvānprīṇāti bhagavānhariḥ
|
vidhiprayuktāṃ pūjāṃ ca gṛhṇāti bhagavānsvayam||1||

2ye tu dagdhendhanā loke puṇyapāpavivarjitāḥ
|
teṣāṃ tvayā'bhinirdiṣṭā pāraṃparyāgatā gatiḥ||2||

3caturthyāṃ caiva te gatyāṃ gacchanti puruṣottamam
|
ekāntinastu puruṣā gacchanti paramaṃ padam||3||

4nūnamekāntadharmo'yaṃ śreṣṭho nārāyaṇapriyaḥ
|
agatvā gatayastisro yadgacchatyavyayaṃ harim||4||

5sahopaniṣadānvedānye viprāḥ samyagāsthitāḥ
|
paṭhanti vidhimāsthāya ye cāpi yatidharmiṇaḥ||5||

6tebhyo viśiṣṭāṃ jānāmi gatimekāntināṃ nṛṇām
|
kenaiṣa dharmaḥ kathito devena ṛṣiṇā'pi vā||6||

7ekāntināṃ ca kā caryā kadā cotpāditā vibho
|
etanme saṃśayaṃ chindhi paraṃ kautūhalaṃ hi me||7||

8vaiśampāyana uvāca|
samupoḍheṣvanīkeṣu kurupāṇḍavayormṛdhe
|
arjune vimanaske ca gītā bhagavatā svayam||8||

9agatiśca gatiścaiva pūrvaṃ te kathitā mayā
|
gahano hyeṣa dharmo vai durvijñeyo'kṛtātmabhiḥ||9||

10saṃmitaḥ samāvedena puraivādiyuge kṛtaḥ
|
dhāryate svayamīśena rājannārāyaṇena ca||10||

11etadarthaṃ mahārāja pṛṣṭaḥ pārthena nāradaḥ
|
ṛṣimadhye mahābhāgaḥ śṛṇvatoḥ kṛṣṇabhīṣmayoḥ||11||

12guruṇā ca mayā'pyeṣa kathito nṛpasattama
|
yathā tatkathitaṃ tatra nāradena tathā śṛṇu||12||

13yadāsīnmānasaṃ janma nārāyaṇamukhodgatam
|
brahmaṇaḥ pṛthivīpāla tadā nārāyaṇaḥ svayam||13||

14tena dharmeṇa kṛtavāndaivaṃ pitryaṃ ca bhārata
|
phenapā ṛṣayaścaiva taṃ dharmaṃ pratipedire||14||

15vaikhānasāḥ phenapebhyo dharmaṃ taṃ pratipedire
|
vaikhānasebhyaḥ somastu tataḥ so'ntardadhe punaḥ||15||

16yadāsīccākṣuṣaṃ janma dvitīyaṃ brahmaṇo nṛpa
|
tadā pitāmahenaiva somāddharmaḥ pariśrutaḥ||16||

17nārāyaṇātmako rājanrudrāya pradadau ca tam
|
tato yogasthito rudraḥ purā kṛtayuge nṛpa||17||

18bālakhilyānṛṣīnsarvāndharmametadapāṭhayat
|
antardadhe tato bhūyastasya devasya māyayā||18||

19tṛtīyaṃ brahmaṇo janma yadāsīdvācikaṃ mahat
|
tatraiṣa dharmaḥ saṃbhūtaḥ svayaṃ nārāyaṇānnṛpa||19||

20suparṇo nāma tamṛṣiḥ prāptavānpuruṣottamāt
|
tapasā vai sutaptena damena niyamena ca||20||

21triḥparikrāntavānetatsuparṇo dharmamuttamam
|
yasmāttasmādvrataṃ hyetattrisauparṇamihocyate||21||

22ṛgvedapāṭhapaṭhitaṃ vratametaddhi duścaram
|
suparṇāccāpyadhigato dharma eṣa sanātanaḥ||22||

23vāyunā dvipadāṃ śreṣṭha kathito jagadāyuṣā
|
vāyoḥ sakāśātprāptaśca ṛṣibhirvighasāśibhiḥ||23||

24tato mahodadhiścaiva prāptavāndharmamuttamam
|
antardadhe tato bhūyo nārāyaṇasamāhitaḥ||24||

25yadā bhūyaḥ śravaṇajā sṛṣṭirāsīnmahātmanaḥ
|
brahmaṇaḥ puruṣavyāghra tatra kīrtayataḥ śṛṇu||25||

26jagatsraṣṭumanā devo harinārāyaṇaḥ svayam
|
cintayāmāsa puruṣaṃ jagatsargakaraṃ prabhum||26||

27atha cintayatastasya karṇābhyāṃ puruṣaḥ smṛtaḥ
|
prajāsargakaro brahmā tamuvāca jagatpatiḥ||27||

28sṛja prajāḥ putra sarvā mukhataḥ pādatastathā
|
śreyastava vidhāsyāmi balaṃ tejaśca suvrata||28||

29dharmaṃ ca matto gṛhṇīṣva sātvataṃ nāma nāmataḥ
|
tena sṛṣṭaṃ kṛtayugaṃ sthāpayasva yathāvidhi||29||

30tato brahmā namaścakre devāya harimedhase
|
dharmaṃ cāgryaṃ sa jagrāha sarahasyaṃ sasaṃgraham||30||

31āraṇyakena sahitaṃ nārāyaṇamukhodbhavam
|
upadiśya tato dharmaṃ brahmaṇe'mitatejase||31||

32tvaṃ kartā yugadharmāṇāṃ nirāśī:karmasaṃjñitam
|
jagāma tamasaḥ pāraṃ yatrāvyaktaṃ vyavasthitam||32||

33tato'tha varado devo brahmā lokapitāmahaḥ
|
asṛjatsa tato lokānkṛtsnānsthāvarajaṅgamān||33||

34tataḥ prāvartata tadā ādau kṛtayugaṃ śubham
|
tato hi sātvato dharmo vyāpya lokānavasthitaḥ||34||

35tenaivādyena dharmeṇa brahmā lokavisargakṛt
|
pūjayāmāsa deveśaṃ hari nārāyaṇaṃ prabhum||35||

36dharmapratiṣṭhāhetośca manuṃ svārociṣaṃ tataḥ
|
adhyāpayāmāsa tadā lokānāṃ hitakāmyayā||36||

37tataḥ svarocivaḥ putraṃ svayaṃ śaṃkhapadaṃ nṛpa
|
adhyāpayatpurā'vyagraḥ sarvalokapatirvibhuḥ||37||

38tataḥ śaṅkhapadaścāpi putramātmajamaurasam
|
diśāṃ pālaṃ suvarṇābhamadhyāpayata bhārata
|
so'ntardadhe tato bhūyaḥ prāpte tretāyuge punaḥ||38||

39nāsatye janmani purā brahmaṇaḥ pārthivottama
|
dharmamataṃ svayaṃ devo harirnārāyaṇaḥ prabhuḥ||39||

40tajjagādāravindākṣo brahmaṇaḥ paśyatastadā
|
sanatkumāro bhagavāṃstata: prādhītavānnṛpa||40||

41sanatkumārādapi ca vīraṇo vai prajāpatiḥ
|
kṛtādau kuruśārdūla dharmametadadhītavān||41||

42vīraṇaścāpyadhītyainaṃ raibhyāya munaye dadau
|
raibhyaḥ putrāya śuddhāya suvratāya sumedhase||42||

43kukṣināmne sa pradadau diśāṃ pālāya dharmiṇe
|
tato'pyantardadhe bhūyo nārāyaṇamukhodbhavaḥ||43||

44aṇḍaje janmani punarbrahmaṇe hariyonaye
|
eṣa dharmaḥ samudbhūto nārāyaṇamukhātpunaḥ||44||

45gṛhīto brahmaṇā rājanprayuktaśca yathāvidhi
|
adhyāpitāśca munayo nāmnā barhiṣado nṛpa||45||

46barhiṣadbhyaśca saṃprāptaḥ sāmavedāntagaṃ dvijam
|
jyeṣṭhaṃ nāmābhivikhyātaṃ jyeṣṭhasāmavrato hariḥ||46||

47jyeṣṭhāccāpyanusaṃkrānto rājānamavikampanam
|
antardadhe tato rājanneṣa dharmaḥ prabho hareḥ||47||

48yadidaṃ saptamaṃ janma padmajaṃ brahmaṇo nṛpa
|
tatraiva dharmaḥ kathitaḥ svayaṃ nārāyaṇena ha||48||

49pitāmahāya śuddhāya yugādau lokadhāriṇe
|
pitāmahaśca dakṣāya dharmametaṃ purā dadau||49||

50tato jyeṣṭhe tu dauhitre prādāddakṣo nṛpottama
|
āditye saviturjyeṣṭhe vivasthāñjagṛhe tataḥ||50||

51tretāyugādau ca tato vivasvānmanave dadau
|
manuśca lokabhūtyarthaṃ sutāyekṣvākave dadau||51||

52ikṣvākunā ca kathito vyāpya lokānavasthitaḥ
|
gamiṣyati kṣayānte ca punarnārāyaṇaṃ nṛpa||52||

53yatīnāṃ cāpi yo dharmaḥ sa te pūrvaṃ nṛpottama
|
kathito harigītāsu samāsavidhikalpitaḥ||53||

54nāradena susaṃprāptaḥ sarahasyaḥ sasaṃgrahaḥ
|
eṣa dharmo jagannāthātsākṣānnārāyaṇānnṛpa||54||

55evameṣa mahāndharma ādyo rājansanātanaḥ
|
durvijñeyo duṣkaraśca sātvatairdhāryate sadā||55||

56dharmajñānena caitena suprayuktena karmaṇā
|
ahiṃsādharmayuktena prīyate harirīśvaraḥ||56||

57ekavyūhavibhāgo vā kvaciddvirvyūhasaṃjñitaḥ
|
trirvyūhaścāpi saṃkhyātaścaturvyūhaśca dṛśyate||57||

58harireva hi kṣetrajño nirmamo niṣkalastathā
|
jīvaśca sarvabhūteṣu pañcabhūtaguṇātigaḥ||58||

59manaśca prathitaṃ rājanpañcendriyasamīraṇam
|
eṣa lokavidhirdhīmāneṣa lokavisargakṛt||59||

60akartā caiva kartā ca kāryaṃ kāraṇameva ca
|
yathecchati tathā rājankrīḍate puruṣo'vyayaḥ||60||

61eṣa ekāntadharmaste kīrtito nṛpasattama
|
mayā guruprasādena durvijñeyo'kṛtātmabhiḥ||61||

62ekāntino hi puruṣā durlabhā bahavo nṛpa
|
yadyekāntibhirākīrṇaṃ jagatsyātkurunandana||62||

63ahiṃsakairātmavidbhiḥ sarvabhūtahite rataiḥ
|
bhavetkṛtayugaprāptirāśī: karmavivarjitā||63||

64evaṃ sa bhagavānvyāso gururmama viśāṃpate
|
kathayāmāsa dharmajño dharmarājñe dvijottamaḥ||64||

65ṛṣīṇāṃ saṃnidhau rājan śṛṇvatoḥ kṛṣṇabhīṣmayoḥ
|
tasyāpyakathayatpūrvaṃ nāradaḥ sumahātapāḥ||65||

66devaṃ paramakaṃ brahma śvetaṃ candrābhamacyutam
|
yatra caikāntino yānti nārāyaṇaparāyaṇāḥ||66||

67janamejaya uvāca|
evaṃ bahuvidhaṃ dharmaṃ pratibuddhairniṣevitam
|
na kurvanti kathaṃ viprā anye nānāvrate sthitāḥ||67||

68vaiśampāyana uvāca|
tisraḥ prakṛtayo rājandehabandheṣu nirmitāḥ
|
sāttvikī rājasī caiva tāmasī caiva bhārata||68||

69dehabandheṣu puruṣaḥ śreṣṭhaḥ kurukulodvaha
|
sāttvikaḥ puruṣavyāghra bhavenmokṣāya niścitaḥ||69||

70atrāpi sa vijānāti puruṣaṃ brahmavittamam
|
nārāyaṇaparo mokṣastato vai sāttvikaḥ smṛtaḥ||70||

71manīṣitaṃ ca prāpnoti cintayanpuruṣottamam
|
ekāntabhaktiḥ satataṃ nārāyaṇaparāyaṇaḥ||71||

72manīṣiṇo hi ye kecidyatayo mokṣadharmiṇaḥ
|
teṣāṃ vicchinnatṛṣṇānāṃ yogakṣemavaho hariḥ||72||

73jāyamānaṃ hi puruṣaṃ yaṃ paśyenmadhusūdanaḥ
|
sāttvikastu sa vijñeyo bhavenmokṣe ca niścitaḥ||73||

74sāṃkhyayogena tulyo hi dharma ekāntasevitaḥ
|
nārāyaṇātmake mokṣe tato yānti parāṃ gatim||74||

75nārāyaṇena dṛṣṭastu pratibuddho bhavetpumān
|
evamātmecchayā rājanpratibuddho na jāyate||75||

76rājasī tāmasī caiva vyāmiśre prakṛtī smṛte
|
tadātmakaṃ hi puruṣaṃ jāyamānaṃ viśāṃpate||76||

77pravṛttilakṣaṇairyuktaṃ nāvekṣati hariḥ svayam
|
paśyatyenaṃ jāyamānaṃ brahmā lokapitāmahaḥ||77||

78rajasā tamasā caiva mānasaṃ samabhiplutam
|
kāmaṃ devā ṛṣayaśca sattvasthā nṛpasattama||78||

79hīnāḥ sattvena sīkṣmeṇa tato vaikārikāḥ smṛtāḥ
|
janamejaya uvāca|
kathaṃ vaikāriko gacchetpuruṣaḥ puruṣottamam||79||

80vada sarvaṃ yathādṛṣṭaṃ pravṛttiṃ ca yathākramam
|
vaiśampāyana uvāca|
susūkṣmaṃ sattvasaṃyuktaṃ saṃyuktaṃ tribhirakṣaraiḥ||80||

81puruṣaḥ puruṣaṃ gacchenniṣkriyaḥ pañcaviṃśakaḥ
|
evamekaṃ sāṃkhyayogaṃ vedāraṇyakameva ca||81||

82parasparāṅgānyetāni pāñcarātraṃ ca kathyate
|
eṣa ekāntināṃ dharmo nārāyaṇaparātmakaḥ||82||

83yathā samudrātprasṛtā jalaughāstameva rājan punarāviśanti
|
ime tathā jñānamahājalaughānārāyaṇaṃ vai punarāviśanti||83||

84eṣa te kathito dharmaḥ sātvataḥ kurunandana
|
kururuṣvainaṃ yathānyāyaṃ yadi śakto'si bhārat||84||

85evaṃ hi sa mahābhāgo nārado gurave mama
|
śvetānāṃ yatināṃ cāha ekāntagatimavyayām||85||

86vyāsaścakathayatprītyā dharmaputrāya dhīmate
|
sa evāyaṃ mayā tubhyamākhyātaḥ prasṛto guroḥ||86||

87itthaṃ hi duścaro dharma eṣa pārthivasattama
|
yathaiva tvaṃ tathaivānye bhavantīha vimohitāḥ||87||

88kṛṣṇa eva hi lokānāṃ bhāvano mohanastathā
|
saṃhārakārakaścaiva kāraṇaṃ ca viśāṃpate||88||
