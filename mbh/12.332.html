Mahābhārata 12.332 (Śāntiparvan)

1bhīṣma uvāca|
giriśṛṅgaṃ samāruhya suto vyāsasya bhārata
|
same deśe vivikte sa niḥśalāka upāviśat||1||

2dhārayāmāsa cātmānaṃ yathāśāstraṃ yathāvidhi
|
pādaprabhṛtigātreṣu krameṇa kramayogavit||2||

3tataḥ sa prāṅmukho vidvānādityenācirodite
|
pāṇipādaṃ samādāya vinītavadupāviśat||3||

4na tatra pakṣisaṃghāto na śabdo nātidarśanam
|
yatra vaiyāsakirdhīmān yoktuṃ samupacakrame||4||

5sa dadarśa tadā''tmānaṃ sarvasaṃgaviniḥsṛtam
|
prajahāsa tato hāsaṃ śukaḥ saṃprekṣya tatparam||5||

6sa punaryogamāsthāya mokṣamārgopalabdhaye
|
mahāyogeśvaro bhūtvā so'tyakāmadvihāyasam||6||

7tataḥ pradakṣiṇaṃ kṛtvā devarṣiṃ nāradaṃ tataḥ
|
nivedayāmāsa ca taṃ svaṃ yogaṃ paramarṣaye||7||

8śuka uvāca |
dṛṣṭo mārgaḥ pravṛtto'smi svasti te'stu tapodhana
|
tvatprasādādgamiṣyāmi gatimiṣṭāṃ mahādyute||8||

9nāradenābhyanujñāta: śuko dvaipāyanātmajaḥ
|
abhivādya punaryogamāsthāyākāśamāviśat||9||

10kailāsapṛṣṭhādutpatya sa papāta divaṃ tadā
|
antarikṣacaraḥ śrīmānvāyubhūtaḥ suniścitaḥ||10||

11tamudyantaṃ dvijaśreṣṭhaṃ vainateyasamadyutim
|
dadṛśuḥ sarvabhūtāni manomārutaraṃhasam||11||

12vyavasāyena lokāṃstrīn sarvān so'tha vicintayan
|
āsthito dīrghamadhvānaṃ pāvakārkasamaprabhaḥ||12||

13tamekamanasaṃ yāntamavyagramakutobhayam
|
dadṛśuḥ sarvabhūtāni jaṅgamāni carāṇi ca||13||

14yathāśakti yathānyāyaṃ pūjāṃ vai cakrire tadā
|
puṣpavarṣaiśca divyaistamavacakrurdivaukasaḥ||14||

15taṃ dṛṣṭvā vismitāḥ sarve gandharvāpsarasāṃ gaṇāḥ
|
ṛṣayaścaiva saṃsiddhāḥ paraṃ vismayamāgatāḥ||15||

16antarikṣagataḥ ko'yaṃ tapasā siddhimāgataḥ
|
adha:kāyordhvavaktraśca netraiḥ samabhirajyate||16||

17tataḥ paramadharmātmā triṣu lokeṣu viśrutaḥ
|
bhāskaraṃ samudīkṣansa prāṅmukho vāgyato'gamat||17||

18śabdenākāśamakhilaṃ pūrayanniva sarvaśaḥ
|
tamāpatantaṃ sahasā dṛṣṭvā sarvāpsarogaṇāḥ||18||

19saṃbhrāntamanaso rājannāsanparamavismitāḥ
|
pañcacūḍāprabhṛtayo bhṛśamutphullalocanāḥ ||19||

20daivataṃ katamaṃ hyetaduttamāṃ gatimāsthitam
|
suniścitamihāyāti vimuktamiva niḥspṛham||20||

21tataḥ samabhicakrāma malayaṃ nāma parvatam
|
urvaśī pūrvacittiśca yaṃ nityamupasevataḥ||21||

22tasya brahmarṣiputrasya vismayaṃ yayatuḥ param
|
aho buddhisamādhānaṃ vedābhyāsarate dvije||22||

23acireṇaiva kālena nabhaścarati candravat
|
pitṛśuśrūṣayā buddhiṃ saṃprāpto'yamanuttamām||23||

24pitṛbhakto dṛḍhatapā: pituḥ sudayitaḥ sutaḥ
|
ananyamanasā tena kathaṃ pitrā visarjitaḥ||24||

25urvaśyā vacanaṃ śrutvā śukaḥ paramadharmavit
|
udaikṣata diśaḥ sarvā vacane gatamānasaḥ||25||

26so'ntarikṣaṃ mahīṃ caiva saśailavanakānanām
|
vilokayāmāsa tadā sarāṃsi saritastathā||26||

27tato dvaipāyanasutaṃ bahumānātsamantataḥ
|
kṛtāñjalipuṭāḥ sarvā nirīkṣante sma devatāḥ||27||

28abravīttāstadā vākyaṃ śukaḥ paramadharmavit
|
pitā yadyanugacchenmāṃ krośamānaḥ śuketi vai||28||

29tataḥ prativaco deyaṃ sarvaireva samāhitaiḥ
|
etanme snehataḥ sarve vacanaṃ kartumarhatha||29||

30śukasya vacanaṃ śrutvā diśaḥ sarvāḥ sakānanāḥ
|
samudrāḥ saritaḥ śailāḥ pratyūcustaṃ samantataḥ||30||

31yathā''jñāpayase vipra bāḍhamevaṃ bhaviṣyati
|
ṛṣervyāharato vākyaṃ prativakṣyāmahe vayam||31||
