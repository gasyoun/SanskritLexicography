Mahābhārata 12.47 (Śāntiparvan)

1janamejaya uvāca|
śaratalpe śayānastu bharatānāṃ pitāmahaḥ
|
kathamutsṛṣṭavāndehaṃ kaṃ ca yogamadhārayat ||1||

2vaiśampāyana uvāca|
śṛṇuṣvāvahito rājañśucirbhūtvā samāhitaḥ
|
bhīṣmasya kuruśārdūla dehotsargaṃ mahātmanaḥ ||2||

3nivṛttamātre tvayana uttare vai divākare
|
samāveśayadātmānamātmanyeva samāhitaḥ ||3||

4vikīrṇāṃśurivādityo bhīṣmaḥ śaraśataiścitaḥ
|
śuśubhe parayā lakṣyā vṛto brāhmaṇasattamaiḥ ||4||

5vyāsena vedaviduṣā nāradena surarṣiṇā
|
devasthānena vātsyena tathā'śmakasumantunā ||5||

6tathā jaimininā caiva pailena ca mahātmanā
|
śāṇḍilyadevalābhyāṃ ca maitreyeṇa ca dhīmatā ||6||

7asitena vasiṣṭhena kauśikena mahātmanā
|
hārītalomaśābhyāṃ ca tathā''treyeṇa dhīmatā ||7||

8bṛhaspatiśca śukraśca cyavanaśca mahāmuniḥ
|
sanatkumāraḥ kapilo vālmīkistumburuḥ kuruḥ ||8||

9maudgalyo bhārgavo rāmastṛṇabindurmahāmuniḥ
|
pippalādo'tha vāyuśca saṃvartaḥ pulahaḥ kacaḥ ||9||

10kāśyapaśca pulastyaśca kraturdakṣaḥ parāśaraḥ
|
marīciraṅgirāḥ kāśyo gautamo gālavo muniḥ ||10||

11dhaumyo vibhāṇḍo māṇḍavyo dhaumraḥ kṛṣṇānubhautikaḥ
|
ulūkaḥ paramo vipro mārkaṇḍeyo mahāmuniḥ ||11||

12bhāskariḥ pūraṇaḥ kṛṣṇaḥ sūtaḥ paramadhārmikaḥ
|
etaiścānyairmunigaṇairmahābhāgairmahātmabhiḥ ||12||

13śraddhā-dama-śamopetairvṛtaścandra iva grahaiḥ
|
bhīṣmastu puruṣavyāghraḥ karmaṇā manasā girā ||13||

14śaratalpagataḥ kṛṣṇaṃ pradadhyau prāñjaliḥ śuciḥ
|
svareṇa hṛṣṭapuṣṭena tuṣṭāva madhusūdanam ||14||

15yogeśvaraṃ padmanābhaṃ viṣṇuṃ jiṣṇuṃ jagatpatim
|
kṛtāñjalipuṭo bhūtvā vāgvidāṃ pravaraḥ prabhuḥ
|
bhīṣmaḥ paramadharmātmā vāsudevamathāstuvat ||15||

16bhīṣma uvāca|
ārirādhayiṣuḥ kṛṣṇaṃ vācaṃ jigadiṣāmiyām
|
tayā vyāsasamāsinyā prīyatāṃ puruṣottamaḥ ||16||

17śuciṃ śucipadaṃ haṃsaṃ tatpadaṃ parameṣṭhinam
|
muktvā sarvātmanā''tmānaṃ taṃ prapadye prajāpatim ||17||

18anādyantaṃ paraṃ brahma na devā narṣayo viduḥ
|
eko'yaṃ veda bhagavān dhātā nārāyaṇo hariḥ ||18||

19nārāyaṇādṛṣigaṇāstathā siddhamahoragāḥ
|
devā devarṣayaścaiva yaṃ viduḥ paramavyayam ||19||

20devadānavagandharvā yakṣarākṣasapannagāḥ
|
yaṃ na jānanti ko hyeṣa kuto vā bhagavāniti ||20||

21yasminviśvāni bhūtāni tiṣṭhanti ca viśanti ca
|
guṇabhūtāni bhūteśe sūtre maṇigaṇā iva ||21||

22yasminnitye tate tantau dṛḍhe sragiva tiṣṭhati
|
sadasadgrathitaṃ viśvaṃ viśvāṅge viśvakarmaṇi ||22||

23hariṃ sahasraśirasaṃ sahasracaraṇekṣaṇam
|
sahasrabāhumukuṭaṃ sahasravadanojjvalam ||23||

24prāhurnārāyaṇaṃ devaṃ yaṃ viśvasya parāyaṇam
|
aṇīyasāmaṇīyāṃsaṃ sthaviṣṭhaṃ ca sthavīyasām ||24||

25garīyasāṃ gariṣṭhaṃ ca śreṣṭhaṃ ca śreyasāmapi ||25||

26yaṃ vākeṣvanuvākeṣu niṣatsūpaniṣatsu ca
|
gṛṇanti satyakarmāṇaṃ satyaṃ satyeṣu sāmasu ||26||

27caturbhiścaturātmānaṃ sattvasthaṃ sātvatāṃ patim
|
yaṃ divyairdevamarcanti guhyaiḥ paramanāmabhiḥ ||27||

28yasminnityaṃ tapastaptaṃ yadaṅgeṣvanutiṣṭhati
|
sarvātmā sarvavitsarvaḥ sarvajñaḥ sarvabhāvanaḥ ||28||

29yaṃ devaṃ devakī devī vasudevādajījanat
|
bhaumasya brahmaṇo guptyai dīptamagnimivāraṇiḥ ||29||

30yamananyo vyapetāśīrātmānaṃ vītakalmaṣam
|
dṛṣṭyānantyāya govindaṃ paśyatyātmānamātmani ||30||

31ativāyvindrakarmāṇamatisūryātitejasam
|
atibuddhīndriyātmānaṃ taṃ prapadye prajāpatim ||31||

32purāṇe puruṣaṃ proktaṃ brahma proktaṃ yugādiṣu
|
kṣaye saṅkarṣaṇaṃ proktaṃ tamupāsyamupāsmahe ||32||

33yamekaṃ bahudhā''tmānaṃ prādurbhūtamadhokṣajam
|
nānyabhaktāḥ kriyāvanto yajante sarvakāmadam ||33||

34yamāhurjagataḥ kośaṃ yasminsannihitāḥ prajāḥ
|
yasmillokāḥ sphurantīme jale śakunayo yathā ||34||

35ṛtamekākṣaraṃ brahma yattatsadasatoḥ param
|
anādimadhyaparyantaṃ na devā narṣayo viduḥ
|
yaṃ surāsuragandharvāḥ siddhā ṛṣimahoragāḥ ||35||

36prayatā nityamarcanti paramaṃ duḥkhabheṣajam
|
anādinidhanaṃ devamātmayoniṃ sanātanam ||36||

37aprekṣyamanabhijñeyaṃ hariṃ nārāyaṇaṃ prabhum
|
yaṃ vai viśvasya kartāraṃ jagatastasthuṣāṃ patim
|
vadanti jagato'dhyakṣamakṣaraṃ paramaṃ padam ||37||

38atha bhīṣmastavarājaḥ
|
hiraṇyavarṇaṃ yaṃ garbhamaditerdaityanāśanam
|
ekaṃ dvādaśadhā jajñe tasmai sūryātmane namaḥ ||38||

39śukle devānpitṝnkṛṣṇe tarpayatyamṛtena yaḥ
|
yaśca rājā dvijātīnāṃ tasmai somātmane namaḥ ||39||

40mahatastamasaḥ pāre puruṣaṃ hyatitejasam
|
yaṃ jñātvā mṛtyumatyeti tasmai jñeyātmane namaḥ ||40||

41yaṃ bṛhantaṃ bṛhatyukthe yamagnau yaṃ mahādhvare
|
yaṃ viprasaṃghā gāyanti tasmai vedātmane namaḥ ||41||

42ṛgyajuḥsāmadhāmānaṃ daśārdhahavirātmakam
|
yaṃ saptatantuṃ tanvanti tasmai yajñātmane namaḥ ||42||

43caturbhiśca caturbhiśca dvābhyāṃ pañcabhireva ca
|
hūyate ca punardvābhyāṃ tasmai homātmane namaḥ ||43||

44yaḥ suparṇo yajurnāma cchandogātrastrivṛcchirāḥ
|
rathantaraṃ vṛhatsāma tasmai stotrātmane namaḥ ||44||

45yaḥ sahasrasame satre jajñe viśvasṛjāmṛṣiḥ
|
hiraṇyapakṣaḥ śakunistasmai haṃsātmane namaḥ ||45||

46pādāṅgaṃ sandhiparvāṇaṃ svaravyañjanabhūṣaṇam
|
yamāhurakṣaraṃ divyaṃ tasmai vāgātmane namaḥ ||46||

47yajñāṅgo yo varāho vai bhūtvā gāmujjahāra ha
|
lokatrayahitārthāya tasmai vīryātmane namaḥ ||47||

48yaḥ śete yogamāsthāya paryaṅke nāgabhūṣite
|
phaṇāsahasraracite tasmai nidrātmane namaḥ ||48||

49yastanoti satāṃ setumṛtenāmṛtayoninā
|
dharmārthavyavahārāṅgaistasmai satyātmane namaḥ ||49||

50yaṃ pṛthagdharmacaraṇāḥ pṛthagdharmaphalaiṣiṇaḥ
|
pṛthagdharmaiḥ samarcanti tasmai dharmātmane namaḥ ||50||

51yataḥ sarvaṃ prasūyante hyanaṅgātmāṅgadehinaḥ
|
unmādaḥ sarvabhūtānāṃ tasmai kāmātmane namaḥ ||51||

52yaṃ ca vyaktasthamavyaktaṃ vicinvanti maharṣayaḥ
|
kṣetre kṣetrajñamāsīnaṃ tasmai kṣetrātmane namaḥ ||52||

53yaṃ tridhātmānamātmasthaṃ vṛtaṃ ṣoḍaśabhirguṇaiḥ
|
prāhuḥ saptadaśaṃ sāṅkhyāstasmai sāṅkhyātmane namaḥ ||53||

54yaṃ vinidrā jitaśvāsāḥ sattvasthāḥ saṃyatendriyāḥ
|
jyotiḥ paśyanti yuñjānāstasmai yogātmane namaḥ ||54||

55apuṇyapuṇyoparame yaṃ punarbhavanirbhayāḥ
|
śāntāḥ saṃnyāsino yānti tasmai mokṣātmane namaḥ ||55||

56yo'sau yugasahasrānte pradīptārcirvibhāvasuḥ
|
sambhakṣayati bhūtāni tasmai ghorātmane namaḥ ||56||

57sambhakṣya sarvabhūtāni kṛtvā caikārṇavaṃ jagat
|
bālaḥ svapiti yaścaikastasmai māyātmane namaḥ ||57||

58tadyasya nābhyāṃ sambhūtaṃ yasminviśvaṃ pratiṣṭhitam
|
puṣkare puṣkarākṣasya tasmai padmātmane namaḥ ||58||

59sahasraśirase caiva puruṣāyāmitātmane
|
catuḥ samudraparyāyayoganidrātmane namaḥ ||59||

60yasya keśeṣu jīmūtā nadyaḥ sarvāṅgasandhiṣu
|
kukṣau samudrāścatvārastasmai toyātmane namaḥ ||60||

61yasmātsarvāḥ prasūyante sargapralayavikriyāḥ
|
yasmiṃścaiva pralīyante tasmai hetvātmane namaḥ ||61||

62yo niṣaṇṇo bhavedrātrau divā bhavati viṣṭhitaḥ
|
iṣṭāniṣṭasya ca draṣṭā tasmai draṣṭrātmane namaḥ ||62||

63akuṇṭhaṃ sarvakāryeṣu dharmakāryārthamudyatam
|
vaikuṇṭhasya ca tadrūpaṃ tasmai kāryātmane namaḥ ||63||

64triḥsaptakṛtvo yaḥ kṣatraṃ dharmavyutkrāntagauravam
|
kruddho nijaghne samare tasmai krauryātmane namaḥ ||64||

65vibhajya pañcadhā''tmānaṃ vāyurbhūtvā śarīragaḥ
|
yaśceṣṭayati bhūtāni tasmai vāyvātmane namaḥ ||65||

66yugeṣvāvartate yogairmāsartvayanahāyanaiḥ
|
sargapralayayoḥ kartā tasmai kālātmane namaḥ ||66||

67brahma vaktraṃ bhujau kṣatraṃ kṛtsnamūrūdaraṃ viśaḥ
|
pādau yasyāśritāḥ śūdrāstasmai varṇātmane namaḥ ||67||

68yasyāgnirāsyaṃ dyaurmūrdhā khaṃ nābhiścaraṇau kṣitiḥ
|
sūryaścakṣurdiśaḥ śrotre tasmai lokātmane namaḥ ||68||

69paraḥ kālātparo yajñātparātparataraśca yaḥ
|
anādirādirviśvasya tasmai viśvātmane namaḥ ||69||

70viṣaye vartamānānāṃ yaṃ taṃ vaiśeṣikairguṇaiḥ
|
prāhurviṣayagoptāraṃ tasmai goptrātmane namaḥ ||70||

71annapānendhanamayo rasaprāṇavivardhanaḥ
|
yo dhārayati bhūtāni tasmai prāṇātmane namaḥ ||71||

72prāṇānāṃ dhāraṇārthāya yo'nnaṃ bhuṅkte caturvidham
|
antarbhūtaḥ pacatyagnistasmai pākātmane namaḥ ||72||

73piṅgekṣaṇasaṭaṃ yasya rūpaṃ daṃṣṭrānakhāyudham
|
dānavendrāntakaraṇaṃ tasmai dṛptātmane namaḥ ||73||

74yaṃ na devā na gandharvā na daityā na ca dānavāḥ
|
tattvato hi vijānanti tasmai sūkṣmātmane namaḥ ||74||

75rasātalagataḥ śrīmānananto bhagavānvibhuḥ
|
jagaddhārayate kṛtsnaṃ tasmai vīryātmane namaḥ ||75||

76yo mohayati bhūtāni snehapāśānubandhanaiḥ
|
sargasya rakṣaṇārthāya tasmai mohātmane namaḥ ||76||

77ātmajñānamidaṃ jñānaṃ jñātvā pañcasvavasthitam
|
yaṃ jñānenābhigacchanti tasmai jñānātmane namaḥ ||77||

78aprameyaśarīrāya sarvato buddhicakṣuṣe
|
anantaparimeyāya tasmai divyātmane namaḥ ||78||

79jaṭine daṇḍine nityaṃ lambodaraśarīriṇe
|
kamaṇḍaluniṣaṅgāya tasmai brahmātmane namaḥ ||79||

80śūline tridaśeśāya tryambakāya mahātmane
|
bhasmadigdhordhvaliṅgāya tasmai rudrātmane namaḥ ||80||

81candrādhakṛtaśīrṣāya vyālayajñopavītine
|
pinākaśūlahastāya tasmai ugrātmane namaḥ ||81||

82sarvabhūtātmabhūtāya bhūtādinidhanāya ca
|
akrodhadrohamohāya tasmai śāntātmane namaḥ ||82||

83yasminsarvaṃ yataḥ sarvaṃ yaḥ sarvaṃ sarvataśca yaḥ
|
yaśca sarvamayo nityaṃ tasmai sarvātmane namaḥ ||83||

84viśvakarmannamaste'stu viśvātmanviśvasambhava
|
apavargasthabhūtānāṃ pañcānāṃ parataḥ sthitaḥ ||84||

85namaste triṣu lokeṣu namaste paratastriṣu
|
namaste dikṣu sarvāsu tvaṃ hi sarvamayo nidhiḥ ||85||

86namaste bhagavanviṣṇo lokānāṃ prabhavāpyaya
|
tvaṃ hi kartā hṛṣīkeśa saṃhartā cāparājitaḥ ||86||

87na hi paśyāmi te bhāvaṃ divyaṃ hi triṣu vartmasu
|
tvāṃ tu paśyāmi tattvena yatte rūpaṃ sanātanam ||87||

88divaṃ te śirasā vyāptaṃ padbhyāṃ devī vasundharā
|
vikrameṇa trayo lokāḥ puruṣo'si sanātanaḥ ||88||

89diśo bhujā raviścakṣurvīrye śukraḥ pratiṣṭhitaḥ
|
saptamārgā niruddhāste vāyoramitatejasaḥ ||89||

90atasīpuṣpasaṃkāśaṃ pītavāsasamacyutam
|
ye namasyanti govindaṃ na teṣāṃ vidyate bhayam ||90||

91eko'pi kṛṣṇasya kṛtaḥ praṇāmo daśāśvamedhāvabhṛthena tulyaḥ
|
daśāśvamedhī punareti janma kṛṣṇapraṇāmī na punarbhavāya ||91||

92kṛṣṇavratāḥ kṛṣṇamanusmaranto rātrau ca kṛṣṇaṃ punarutthitā ye
|
te kṛṣṇadehāḥ praviśanti kṛṣṇamājyaṃ yathā mantrahutaṃ hutāśe ||92||

93namo narakasaṃtrāsarakṣāmaṇḍalakāriṇe
|
saṃsāranimnagāvartatarikāṣṭhāya viṣṇave ||93||

94namo brahmaṇyadevāya gobrāhmaṇahitāya ca
|
jagaddhitāya kṛṣṇāya govindāya namo namaḥ ||94||

95prāṇakāntārapātheyaṃ saṃsārocchedabheṣajam
|
duḥkhaśokaparitrāṇaṃ harirityakṣaradvayam ||95||

96yathā viṣṇumayaṃ satyaṃ tathā viṣṇumayaṃ jagat
|
yathā viṣṇumayaṃ sarvaṃ pāpmā me naśyatāṃ tathā ||96||

97tvāṃ prapannāya bhaktāya gatimiṣṭāṃ jigīṣave
|
yacchreyaḥ puṇḍarīkākṣa taddhyāyasva surottama ||97||

98iti vidyātapoyonirayonirviṣṇurīḍitaḥ
|
vāgyajñenārcito devaḥ prīyatāṃ me janārdanaḥ ||98||

99nārāyaṇaḥ paraṃ brahma nārāyaṇaparaṃ tapaḥ
|
nārāyaṇaḥ paro devaḥ sarvaṃ nārāyaṇaḥ sadā ||99||

100vaiśampāyana uvāca|
etāvaduktvā vacanaṃ bhīṣmastadgatamānasaḥ
|
nama ityeva kṛṣṇāya praṇāmamakarottadā ||100||

101abhigamya tu yogena bhaktiṃ bhīṣmasya mādhavaḥ
|
trailokyadarśanaṃ jñānaṃ divyaṃ dattvā yayau hariḥ ||101||

102tasminnuparate śabde tataste brahmavādinaḥ
|
bhīṣmaṃ vāgbhirbāṣpakaṇṭhāstamānarcurmahāmatim ||102||

103te stuvantaśca viprāgryāḥ keśavaṃ puruṣottamam
|
bhīṣmaṃ ca śanakaiḥ sarve praśaśaṃsuḥ punaḥ punaḥ ||103||

104viditvā bhaktiyogaṃ tu bhīṣmasya puruṣottamaḥ
|
sahasotthāya saṃhṛṣṭo yānamevānvapadyata ||104||

105keśavaḥ sātyakiścāpi rathenaikena jagmatuḥ
|
apareṇa mahātmānau yudhiṣṭhiradhanañjayau ||105||

106bhīmaseno yamau cobhau rathamekaṃ samāśritāḥ
|
kṛpo yuyutsuḥ sūtaśca sañjayaśca parantapaḥ ||106||

107te sthairnagarākāraiḥ prayātāḥ puruṣarṣabhāḥ
|
nemighoṣeṇa mahatā kampayanto vasundharām ||107||

108tato giraḥ puruṣavarastavānvitā dvijeritāḥ pathi sumanāḥ sa śuśruve
|
kṛtāñjaliṃ praṇatamathāparaṃ janaṃ sa keśihā muditamanā'bhyanandata ||108||
