Mahābhārata 6.88 (Bhīṣmaparvan)

1sañjaya uvāca|
bhīṣmaṃ tu samare kruddhaṃ pratapantaṃ samantataḥ
|
na śekuḥ pāṇḍavā draṣṭuṃ tapantamiva bhāskaram ||1||

2tataḥ sarvāṇi sainyāni dharmaputrasya śāsanāt
|
abhyadravanta gāṅgeyaṃ mardayantaṃ śitaiḥ śaraiḥ ||2||

3sa tu bhīṣmo raṇaślādhī somakānsahasṛñjayān
|
pañcālāṃśca maheṣvāsān pātayāmāsa sāyakaiḥ ||3||

4te vadhyamānā bhīṣmeṇa pañcālāḥ somakaiḥ saha
|
bhīṣmamevābhyayustūrṇaṃ tyaktvā mṛtyukṛtaṃ bhayam ||4||

5sa teṣāṃ rathināṃ vīro bhīṣmaḥ śāṃtanavo yudhi
|
ciccheda sahasā rājan ‌bāhūnatha śirāṃsi ca ||5||

6virathān rathinaścakre pitā devavratastava
|
patitānyuttamāṅgāni hayebhyo hayasādinām ||6||

7nirmanuṣyāṃśca mātaṅgān śayānān parvatopamān
|
apaśyāma mahārāja bhīṣmāstreṇa pramohitān ||7||

8na tatrāsīt pumān‌ kaścitpāṇḍavānāṃ viśāṃpate
|
anyatra rathināṃ śreṣṭhādbhīmasenānmahābalāt ||8||

9sa hi bhīṣmaṃ samāsādya tāḍayāmāsa saṃyuge
|
tato niṣṭhānako ghoro bhīṣmabhīmasamāgame ||9||

10babhūva sarvasainyānāṃ ghorarūpo bhayānakaḥ
|
tathaiva pāṇḍavā hṛṣṭāḥ siṃhanādamathānadan ||10||

11tato duryodhano rājā sodaryaiḥ parivāritaḥ
|
bhīṣmaṃ jugopa samare vartamāne janakṣaye ||11||

12bhīmastu sārathiṃ hatvā bhīṣmasya rathināṃ varaḥ
|
pradrutāśve rathe tasmin dravamāṇe samantataḥ ||12||

13sunābhasya śareṇāśu śiraściccheda bhārata
|
kṣurapreṇa sutīkṣṇena sa hato nyapatadbhuvi ||13||

14hate tasmin mahārāja tava putre mahārathe
|
nāmṛṣyanta raṇe śūrāḥ sodarāḥ sa saṃyuge ||14||

15ādityaketurbahvāśī kuṇḍadhāro mahodaraḥ
|
aparājitaḥ paṇḍitako viśālākṣaḥ sudurjayaḥ ||15||

16pāṇḍavaṃ citrasannāhā vicitrakavacadhvajāḥ
|
abhyadravanta saṃgrāme yoddhukāmārimardanāḥ ||16||

17mahodarastu samare bhīmaṃ vivyādha patribhiḥ
|
navabhirvajrasaṅkāśairnamuciṃ vṛtrahā yathā ||17||

18ādityaketuḥ saptatyā bahvāśī cāpi pañcabhiḥ
|
navatyā kuṇḍadhāraśca viśālākṣaśca pañcabhiḥ ||18||

19aparājito mahārāja parājiṣṇurmahāratham
|
śarairbahubhirānarcchadbhīmasenaṃ mahābalam ||19||

20raṇe paṇḍitakaścainaṃ tribhirbāṇaiḥ samārpayat
|
sa tanna mamṛṣe bhīmaḥ śatrubhirvadhamāhave ||20||

21dhanuḥ prapīḍya vāmena kareṇābhitrakarśanaḥ
|
śiraściccheda samare śareṇānataparvaṇā ||21||

22aparājitasya sunasaṃ tava putrasya saṃyuge
|
parājitasya bhīmena nipapāta śiro mahīm ||22||

23athāpareṇa bhallena kuṇḍadhāraṃ mahāratham
|
prāhiṇonmṛtyulokāya sarvalokasya paśyataḥ||23||

24tataḥ punarameyātmā prasaṃdhāya śilīmukham
|
preṣayāmāsa samare paṇḍitaṃ prati bhārata ||24||

25sa śaraḥ paṇḍitaṃ hatvā viveśa dharaṇītalam
|
yathā naraṃ nihatyāśu bhujagaḥ kālacoditaḥ ||25||

26viśālākṣaśiraśchitvā pātayāmāsa bhūtale
|
tribhiḥ śarairadīnātmā smaran kleśaṃ purātanam ||26||

27mahodaraṃ maheṣvāsaṃ nārācena stanāntare
|
vivyādha samare rājan sa hato nyapatadbhuvi ||27||

28ādityaketoḥ ketuṃ ca chitvā bāṇena saṃyuge
|
bhallena bhṛśatīkṣṇena śiraściccheda bhārata ||28||

29bahvāśinaṃ tato bhīmaḥ śareṇānataparvaṇā
|
preṣayāmāsa saṃkruddho yamasya sadanaṃ prati ||29||

30pradudruvustataste'nye putrāstava viśāṃpate
|
manyamānā hi tatsatyaṃ sabhāyāṃ tasya bhāṣitam ||30||

31tato duryodhano rājā bhrātṛvyasanakarśitaḥ
|
abravīttāvakānyodhānbhīmo'yaṃ yudhi vadhyatām ||31||

32evamete maheṣvāsāḥ putrāstava viśāṃpate
|
bhrātṝnsaṃdṛśya nihatān prāsmaraṃste hi tadvacaḥ ||32||

33yaduktavānmahāprājñaḥ kṣattā hitamanāmayam
|
tadidaṃ samanuprāptaṃ vacanaṃ divyadarśinaḥ ||33||

34lobhamohasamāviṣṭaḥ putraprītyā janādhipa
|
na budhyase purā yattattathyamuktaṃ vaco mahat ||34||

35tathaiva ca vadhārthāya putrāṇāṃ pāṇḍavo balī
|
nūnaṃ jāto mahābāhuryathā hanti sma kauravān ||35||

36tato duryodhano rājā bhīṣmamāsādya saṃyuge
|
duḥkhena mahatā''viṣṭo vilalāpa suduḥkhitaḥ ||36||

37nihatā bhrātaraḥ śūrā bhīmasenena me yudhi
|
yatamānāstathā'nye'pi hanyante sarvasainikāḥ ||37||

38bhavāṃśca madhyasthatayā nityamasmānupekṣate
|
so'haṃ kupathamārūḍhaḥ paśya daivamidaṃ mama ||38||

39etacchrutvā vacaḥ krūraṃ pitā devavratastava
|
duryodhanamidaṃ vākyamabravītsāśrulocanaḥ ||39||

40uktametanmayā pūrvaṃ droṇena vidureṇa ca
|
gāndhāryā ca yaśasvinyā tattvaṃ tāta na buddhavān ||40||

41samayaśca mayā pūrvaṃ kṛto vaḥ śatrukarśana
|
nāhaṃ yudhi niyoktavyo nāpyācāryaḥ kathañcana ||41||

42yaṃ yaṃ hi dhārtarāṣṭrāṇāṃ bhīmo drakṣyati saṃyuge
|
haniṣyati raṇe nityaṃ satyametadbravīmi te ||42||

43sa tvaṃ rājansthiro bhūtvā raṇe kṛtvā dṛḍhāṃ matim
|
yodhayasva raṇe pārthān svargaṃ kṛtvā parāyaṇam ||43||

44na śakyāḥ pāṇḍavā jetuṃ sendrairapi surāsuraiḥ
|
tasmādyuddhe sthirāṃ kṛtvā matiṃ yuddhyasva bhārata ||44||
