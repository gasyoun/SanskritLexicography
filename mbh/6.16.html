Mahābhārata 6.16 (Bhīṣmaparvan)

1sañjaya uvāca|
tato rajanyāṃ vyuṣṭāyāṃ śabdaḥ samabhavanmahān
|
krośatāṃ bhūmipalānāṃ yujyatāṃ yujyatāmiti ||1||

2śaṅkhadundubhighoṣaiśca siṃhanādaiśca bhārata
|
hayaheṣitanādaiśca rathanemisvanaistathā ||2||

3gajānāṃ bṛhatāṃ caiva yodhānāṃ cāpi garjatām
|
kṣvelitāsphoṭitotkruṣṭaistumulaṃ sarvato'bhavat ||3||

4udatiṣṭhanmahārāja sarvaṃ yuktamaśeṣataḥ
|
sūryodaye mahatsainyaṃ kurupāṇḍavasenayoḥ ||4||

5rājendra tava putrāṇāṃ pāṇḍavānāṃ tathaiva ca
|
duṣpradhṛṣyāṇi cāstrāṇi saśasrakavacāni ca ||5||

6tataḥ prakāśe sainyāni samadṛśyanta bhārata
|
tvadīyānāṃ pareṣāṃ ca śastravanti mahānti ca ||6||

7tatra nāgā rathāścaiva jāmbūnadapariṣkṛtāḥ
|
vibhrājamānā dṛśyante meghā iva savidyutaḥ ||7||

8rathānīkānyadṛśyanta nagarāṇīva bhūriśaḥ
|
atīva śuśubhe tatra pitā te pūrṇacandravat ||8||

9dhanurbhirṛṣṭibhiḥ khaṅgairgadābhiḥ śaktitomaraiḥ
|
yodhāḥ praharaṇaiḥ śubhraisteṣvanīkeṣvavasthitāḥ ||9||

10gajāḥ padātā rathinasturagāśca viśāmpate
|
vyatiṣṭhanvāgurākārāḥ śataśo'tha sahasraśaḥ ||10||

11dhvajā bahuvidhākārā vyadṛśyanta samucchritāḥ
|
sveṣāṃ caiva pareṣāṃ ca dyutimantaḥ sahasraśaḥ ||11||

12kāñcanā maṇicitrāṅgā jvalanta iva pāvakāḥ
|
arciṣmanto vyarocanta gajārohāḥ sahasraśaḥ ||12||

13mahendraketavaḥ śubhrā mahendrasadaneṣviva
|
sannaddhāste pravīrāśca dadṛśuryuddhakāṅkṣiṇaḥ ||13||

14udyatairāyudhaiścitrāstalabaddhāḥ kalāpinaḥ
|
ṛṣabhākṣā manuṣyendrāścamūmukhagatā babhuḥ ||14||

15śakuniḥ saubalaḥ śalya āvantyo'tha jayadrathaḥ
|
vindānuvindau kaikeyāḥ kāmbhojaśca sudakṣiṇaḥ ||15||

16śrutāyudhaśca kāliṅgo jayatsenaśca pārthivaḥ
|
bṛhadbalaśca kauśalyaḥ kṛtavarmā ca sātvataḥ ||16||

17daśaite puruṣavyāghrāḥ śūrāḥ parighabāhavaḥ
|
akṣauhiṇīnāṃ patayo yajvāno bhūridakṣiṇāḥ ||17||

18ete cānye ca bahavo duryodhanavaśānugāḥ
|
rājāno rājaputrāśca nītimanto mahārathāḥ ||18||

19sannaddhāḥ samadṛśyanta sveṣvanīkeṣvavasthitāḥ
|
baddhakṛṣṇājināḥ sarve balino yuddhaśālinaḥ ||19||

20hṛṣṭā duryodhanasyārthe brahmalokāya dīkṣitāḥ
|
samarthā daśa vāhinyaḥ parigṛhya vyavasthitāḥ ||20||

21ekādaśī dhārtarāṣṭrī kauravāṇāṃ mahācamūḥ
|
agrataḥ sarvasainyānāṃ yatra śāntanavo'graṇīḥ ||21||

22śvetoṣṇīṣaṃ śvetahayaṃ śvetavarmāṇamacyutam
|
apaśyāma mahārāja bhīṣmaṃ candramivoditam ||22||

23hematāladhvajaṃ bhīṣmaṃ rājate syandate sthitam
|
śvetābhra iva tīkṣṇāṃśuṃ dadṛśuḥ kurupāṇḍavāḥ ||23||

24sṛñjayāśca maheṣvāsā dhṛṣṭadyumnapurogamāḥ
|
jṛmbhamāṇaṃ mahāsiṃhaṃ dṛṣṭvā kṣudramṛgā yathā ||24||

25dhṛṣṭadyumnamukhāḥ sarve samudvivijire muhuḥ
|
ekādaśaitāḥ śrījuṣṭā vāhinyastava pārthiva ||25||

26pāṇḍavānāṃ tathā sapta mahāpuruṣapālitāḥ
|
unmattamakarāvartau mahāgrāhasamākulau ||26||

27yugānte samavetau dvau dṛśyete sāgarāviva
|
naiva nastādṛśo rājandṛṣṭapūrvo na ca śrutaḥ
|
anīkānāṃ sametānāṃ kauravāṇāṃ tathāvidhaḥ ||27||
