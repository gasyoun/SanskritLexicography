Mahābhārata 12.204 (Śāntiparvan)

1manuruvāca |
yathā vyaktamidaṃ śete svapne carati cetanam
|
jñānamindriyasaṃyuktaṃ tadvatpretya bhavābhavau ||1||

2yathāmbhasi prasanne tu rūpaṃ paśyati cakṣuṣā
|
tadvatprasannendriyatvājjñeyaṃ jñānena paśyati ||2||

3sa eva lulite tasmin yathā rūpaṃ na paśyati
|
tathendriyākulībhāve jñeyaṃ jñānena paśyati ||3||

4abuddhirajñānakṛtā abuddhyā kṛṣyate manaḥ
|
duṣṭasya manasaḥ pañca sampraduṣyanti mānasāḥ ||4||

5ajñānatṛpto viṣayeṣvavagāḍho na tṛpyate
|
adṛṣṭavacca bhūtātmā viṣayebhyo nivartate ||5||

6tarṣacchedo na bhavati puruṣasyeha kalmaṣāt
|
nivartate tadā tarṣaḥ pāpamantagataṃ yadā ||6||

7viṣayeṣu tu saṃsargācchāśvatasya tu saṃśrayāt
|
manasā cānyathā kāṅkṣanparaṃ pratipadyate ||7||

8jñānamutpadyate puṃsāṃ kṣayātpāpasya karmaṇaḥ
|
yathā''darśatale prakhye paśyatyātmānamātmani ||8||

9prasṛtairindriyairduḥkhī taireva niyataiḥ sukhī
|
tasmādindriyarūpebhyo yacchedātmānamātmanā ||9||

10indriyebhyo manaḥ pūrvaṃ buddhiḥ paratarā tataḥ
|
buddheḥ parataraṃ jñānaṃ jñānātparataraṃ mahat ||10||

11avyaktātprasṛtaṃ jñānaṃ tato buddhistato manaḥ
|
manaḥśrotrādibhiryuktaṃ śabdādīn sādhu paśyati ||11||

12yastāṃstyajati śabdādīn sarvāśca vyaktastathā
|
vimuñcetprākṛtāngrāmāṃstānmuktvāmṛtamaśnute ||12||

13udyanhi savitā yadvatsṛjate raśmimaṇḍalam
|
sa evāstamapāgacchaṃstadevātmani yacchati ||13||

14antarātmā tathā dehamāviśyendriyaraśmibhiḥ
|
prāpyendriyaguṇānpañca so'stamāvṛtya gacchati ||14||

15praṇītaṃ karmaṇā mārgaṃ nīyamānaḥ punaḥ punaḥ
|
prāpnotyayaṃ karmaphalaṃ pravṛttaṃ dharmamāptavān ||15||

16viṣayā vinivartante nirāhārasya dehinaḥ
|
rasavarjaṃ raso'pyasya paraṃ dṛṣṭvā nivartate ||16||

17buddhiḥ karmaguṇairhīnā yadā manasi vartate
|
tadā sampadyate brahma tatraiva pralayaṃ gatam ||17||

18asparśanamaśṛṇvānamanāsvādamadarśanam
|
aghrāṇamavitarkaṃ ca sattvaṃ praviśate param ||18||

19manasyākṛtayo magnā manastvabhigataṃ matim
|
matistvabhigatā jñānaṃ jñānaṃ cābhigataṃ param ||19||

20indriyairmanasaḥ siddhirna buddhiṃ buddhyate manaḥ
|
na buddhirbuddhyate vyaktaṃ sūkṣmaṃ tvetāni paśyati ||20||
