Mahābhārata 13.89 (Anuśāsanaparvan)

1bhīṣma uvāca|
yamastu yāni śrāddhāni provāca śaśabindave
|
tāni me śṛṇu kāmyāni nakṣatreṣu pṛthak pṛthak ||1||

2śrāddhaṃ yaḥ kṛttikāyoge kurvīta satataṃ naraḥ
|
agnīnādhāya sāpatyo yajeta vigatajvaraḥ ||2||

3apatyakāmo rohiṇyāṃ tejaskāmo mṛgottame
|
krūrakarmā dadacchrāddhamārdrāyāṃ mānavo bhavet ||3||

4dhanakāmo bhavenmartyaḥ kurvañchrāddhaṃ punarvasau
|
puṣṭikāmo'tha puṣyeṇa śrāddhamīheta mānavaḥ ||4||

5āśleṣāyāṃ dadacchrāddhaṃ dhīrānputrānprajāyate
|
jñātīnāṃ tu bhavecchreṣṭho maghāsu śrāddhamāvapan ||5||

6phalgunīṣu dadacchrāddhaṃ subhagaḥ śrāddhado bhavet
|
apatyabhāguttarāsu hastena phalabhāgbhavet ||6||

7citrāyāṃ tu dadat śrāddhaṃ labhedrūpavataḥ sutān
|
svātiyoge pitṝnarcya vāṇijyamupajīvati ||7||

8bahuputro viśākhāsu putramīhanbhavennaraḥ
|
anurādhāsu kurvāṇo rājacakraṃ pravartayet ||8||

9ādhipatyaṃ vrajenmartyo jyeṣṭhāyāmapavarjayan
|
naraḥ kurukulaśreṣṭha ṛddho damapuraḥsaraḥ ||9||

10mūle tvārogyamṛccheta yaśo''ṣāḍhāsu cottamam
|
uttarāsu tvaṣāḍhāsu vītaśokaścarenmahīm ||10||

11śrāddhaṃ tvabhijitā kurvanbhiṣaksiddhimavāpnuyāt
|
śravaṇeṣu dadacchrāddhaṃ pretya gacchetsa tadgatim ||11||

12rājyabhāgī dhaniṣṭhāyāṃ bhaveta niyataṃ naraḥ
|
nakṣatre vāruṇe kurvan bhiṣaksiddhimavāpnuyāt ||12||

13pūrvaproṣṭhapadāḥ kurvan bahūnvindatyajāvikān
|
uttarāsu prakurvāṇo vindate gāḥ sahasraśaḥ ||13||

14bahukupyakṛtaṃ vittaṃ vindate revatīṃ śritaḥ
|
aśvinīṣvaśvānvindeta bharaṇīṣvāyuruttamam ||14||

15imaṃ śrāddhavidhiṃ śrutvā śaśabindustathākarot
|
akleśenājayaccāpi mahīṃ so'nuśaśāsa ha ||15||
