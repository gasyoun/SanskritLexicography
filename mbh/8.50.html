Mahābhārata 8.50 (Karṇaparvan)

1sañjaya uvāca|
tānabhidravato dṛṣṭvā pāṇḍavāṃstāvakaṃ balam
|
duryodhano mahārāja vārayāmāsa sarvaśaḥ ||1||

2yodhāṃśca svabalaṃ caiva samantādbharatarṣabha
|
krośatastava putrasya na sma rājanyavartata ||2||

3tataḥ pakṣaḥ prapakṣaśca śakuniścāpi saubalaḥ
|
tadā saśastrāḥ kuravo bhīmamabhyadravanraṇe ||3||

4karṇo'pi dṛṣṭvā dravato dhārtarāṣṭrānsarājakān
|
madrarājamuvācedaṃ yāhi bhīmarathaṃ prati ||4||

5evamuktaśca karṇena śalyo madrādhipastadā
|
haṃsavarṇānhayānagryānpraiṣīdyatra vṛkodaraḥ ||5||

6te preritā mahārāja śalyenāhavaśobhinā
|
bhīmasenarathaṃ prāpya samasajjanta vājinaḥ ||6||

7dṛṣṭvā karṇaṃ samāyāntaṃ bhīmaḥ krodhasamanvitaḥ
|
matiṃ cakre vināśāya karṇasya bharatarṣabha ||7||

8so'bravītsātyakiṃ vīraṃ dhṛṣṭadyumnaṃ ca pārṣatam
|
yūyaṃ rakṣata rājānaṃ dharmātmānaṃ yudhiṣṭhiram ||8||

9saṃśayānmahato muktaṃ kathañcitprekṣato mama
|
agrato me kṛto rājā chinnasarvaparicchadaḥ ||9||

10duryodhanasya prītyarthaṃ rādheyena durātmanā
|
antamadya gamiṣyāmi tasya duḥkhasya pārṣata ||10||

11hantāsmyadya raṇe karṇaṃ sa vā māṃ nihaniṣyati
|
saṃgrāmeṇa sughoreṇa satyabhetadbravīmi te ||11||

12rājānamadya bhavatāṃ nyāsabhūtaṃ dadāni vai
|
tasya saṃrakṣaṇe sarve yatadhvaṃ vigatajvarāḥ ||12||

13evamuktvā mahābāhuḥ prāyādādhirathiṃ prati
|
siṃhanādena mahatā sarvāḥ sannādayandiśaḥ ||13||

14dṛṣṭvā tvaritamāyāntaṃ bhīmaṃ yuddhābhinandinam
|
sūtaputramathovāca madrāṇāmaśviro vibhuḥ ||14||

15śalya uvāca|
paśya karṇa mahābāhuṃ saṃkruddhaṃ pāṇḍunandanam
|
dīrghakālārjitaṃ krodhaṃ moktukāmaṃ tvayi dhruvam ||15||

16īdṛśaṃ nāsya rūpaṃ me dṛṣṭapūrvaṃ kadācana
|
abhimanyau hate karṇa rākṣase ca ghaṭotkace ||16||

17trailokyasya samastasya śaktaḥ kruddho nivāraṇe
|
bibharti sadṛśaṃ rūpaṃ yugāntāgnisamaprabham ||17||

18sañjaya uvāca|
iti bruvati rādheyaṃ madrāṇāmīśvare nṛpa
|
abhyavartata vai karṇaṃ krodhadīpto vṛkodaraḥ ||18||

19athāgataṃ tu saṃprekṣya bhīmaṃ yuddhābhinandinam
|
abravīdvacanaṃ śalyaṃ rādheyaḥ prahasanniva ||19||

20yaduktaṃ vacanaṃ me'dya tvayā madrajaneśvara
|
bhīmasenaṃ prati vibho tatsatyaṃ nātra saṃśayaḥ ||20||

21eṣa śūraśca vīraśca krodhanaśca vṛkodaraḥ
|
nirapekṣaḥ śarīre ca prāṇataśca balādhikaḥ ||21||

22ajñātavāsaṃ vasatā virāṭanagare tadā
|
draupadyāḥ priyakāmena kevalaṃ bāhusaṃśrayāt ||22||

23gūḍhabhāvaṃ samāśritya kīcakaḥ sagaṇo hataḥ
|
so'dya saṃgrāmaśirasi sannaddhaḥ krodhamūrchitaḥ ||23||

24kiṃ karodyatadaṇḍena mṛtyunā'pi vrajedraṇam
|
cirakālābhilaṣito mamāyaṃ tu manorathaḥ ||24||

25arjunaṃ samare hanyāṃ māṃ vā hanyāddhanañjayaḥ
|
sa me kadācida‌dyaiva bhavedbhīmasamāgamāt ||25||

26nihate bhīmasene vā yadi vā virathīkṛte
|
abhiyāsyati māṃ pārthastanme sādhu bhaviṣyati ||26||

27atra yanmanyase prāptaṃ tacchīghraṃ saṃpradhāraya
|
etacchrutvā tu vacanaṃ rādheyasyāmitaujasaḥ ||27||

28uvāca vacanaṃ śalyaḥ sūtaputraṃ tathāgatam
|
abhiyāhi mahābāho bhīmasenaṃ mahābalam ||28||

29nirasya bhīmasenaṃ tu tataḥ prāpsyasi phālgunam
|
yaste kāmo'bhilaṣitaścirātprabhṛti hṛdgataḥ ||29||

30sa vai saṃpatsyate karṇa satyametadbravīmi te
|
evamukte tataḥ karṇaḥ śalyaṃ punarabhāṣata ||30||

31hantā'hamarjunaṃ saṃkhye māṃ vā hanyāddhanañjayaḥ
|
yuddhe manaḥ samādhāya yāhi yatra vṛkodaraḥ ||31||

32sañjaya uvāca|
tataḥ prāyādrathenāśu śalyastatra viśāṃpate
|
yatra bhīmo maheṣvāso vyadrāvayata vāhinīm ||32||

33tatastūryaninādaśca bherīṇāṃ ca mahāsvanaḥ
|
uda‌tiṣṭhacca rājendra karṇabhīmasamāgame ||33||

34bhīmaseno'tha saṃkruddhastasya sainyaṃ durāsadam
|
nārācairvimalaistīkṣṇairdiśaḥ prādrāvayadbalī ||34||

35sa sannipātastumulo ghorarūpo viśāṃpate
|
āsīdraudro mahārāja karṇapāṇḍavayormṛdhe ||35||

36tato muhūrtādrājendra pāṇḍavaḥ karṇamādravat
|
samāpatantaṃ saṃprekṣya karṇo vaikartano vṛṣaḥ ||36||

37ājaghāna susaṃkuddho nārācena stanāntare
|
punaścainamameyātmā śaravarṣairavākirat ||37||

38sa viddhaḥ sūtaputreṇa cchāda‌yāmāsa patribhiḥ
|
vivyādha niśitaiḥ karṇaṃ navabhirnataparvabhiḥ ||38||

39tasya karṇo dhanurmadhye dvidhā ciccheda patribhiḥ
|
athainaṃ chinnadhanvānaṃ pratyavidhyatstanāntare ||39||

40nārācena sutīkṣṇena sarvāvaraṇabhedinā
|
so'nyatkārmukamādāya sūtaputraṃ vṛkodaraḥ ||40||

41rājanmarmasu marmajño vivyādha niśitaiḥ śaraiḥ
|
nanāda balavannādaṃ kampayanniva rodasī ||41||

42taṃ karṇaḥ pañcaviṃśatyā nārācena samārpayat
|
madotkaṭaṃ vane dṛptamulkābhiriva kuñjaram ||42||

43tataḥ sāyakabhinnāṅgaḥ pāṇḍavaḥ krodhamūrcchitaḥ
|
saṃraṃbhāmarṣatāmrākṣaḥ sūtaputravadhepsayā ||43||

44sa kārmuke mahāvegaṃ bhārasādhanamuttamam
|
girīṇāmapi bhettāraṃ sāyakaṃ samayojayat ||44||

45vikṛṣya balavaccāpamākarṇādatimārutiḥ
|
taṃ mumoca maheṣvāsaḥ kuddhaḥ karṇajighāṃsayā ||45||

46sa visṛṣṭo balavatā bāṇo vajrāśanisvanaḥ
|
adārayadraṇe karṇaṃ vajravego yathācalam ||46||

47sa bhīmasenābhihataḥ sūtapūtraḥ kurūdvaha
|
niṣasāda rathopasthe visaṃjñaḥ pṛtanāpatiḥ ||47||

48tato madrādhipo dṛṣṭvā visaṃjñaṃ sūtanandanam
|
apovāha rathenājau karṇamāhavaśobhinam ||48||

49tataḥ parājite karṇe dhārtarāṣṭrīṃ mahācamūm
|
vyadrāvayadbhīmaseno yathendro dānavān purā ||49||
