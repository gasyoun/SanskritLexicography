Mahābhārata 6.6 (Bhīṣmaparvan)

1dhṛtarāṣṭra uvāca|
ukto dvīpasya saṅkṣepo vidhivadbuddhimaṃstvayā
|
tattvajñaścosi sarvasya vistāraṃ brūhi sañjaya ||1||

2yāvānbhūmyavakāśo'yaṃ dṛśyate śaśalakṣaṇe
|
tasya pramāṇaṃ prabrūhi tato vakṣyasi pippalam ||2||

3vaiśampāyana uvāca|
evaṃ rājñā sa pṛṣṭastu sañjayo vākyamabravīt
|
sañjaya uvāca|
prāgāyatā mahārāja ṣaḍete varṣaparvatāḥ
|
avagāḍhā hyubhayataḥ samudrau pūrvapaścimau ||3||

4himavānhemakūṭaśca niṣadhaśca nagottamaḥ
|
nīlaśca vaidūryamayaḥ śvetaśca śaśisannibhaḥ ||4||

5sarvadhātuvicitraśca śrṛṅgavānnāma parvataḥ
|
ete vai parvatā rājansiddhacāraṇasevitāḥ ||5||

6eṣāmantaraviṣkambhā yojanāni sahasraśaḥ
|
tatra puṇyā janapadāstāni varṣāṇi bhārata ||6||

7vasanti teṣu satvāni nānājātīni sarvaśaḥ
|
idaṃ tu bhārataṃ varṣaṃ tato haimavataṃ param ||7||

8hemakūṭātparaṃ caiva harivarṣaṃ pracakṣate
|
dakṣiṇena tu nīlasya niṣadhasyottareṇa tu ||8||

9prāgāyato mahābhāga mālyavānnāma parvataḥ
|
tataḥ paraṃ mālyavataḥ parvato gandhamādanaḥ ||9||

10parimaṇḍalastayormadhye meruḥ kanakaparvataḥ
|
ādityataruṇābhāso vidhūma iva pāvakaḥ ||10||

11yojanānāṃ sahasrāṇi caturaśītirucchritaḥ
|
adhastāccaturaśītiryojanānāṃ mahīpate ||11||

12ūrdhvaṃmadhaśca tiryakva lokānāvṛtya tiṣṭhati
|
tasya pārśveṣvamī dvīpāścatvāraḥ saṃsthitā vibho ||12||

13bhadrāśvaḥ ketumālaśca jambūdvīpaśca bhārata
|
uttarāścaiva kuravaḥ kṛtapuṇyapratiśrayāḥ ||13||

14vihagaḥ sumukho yastu suparṇasyātmajaḥ kila
|
sa vai vicintayāmāsa sauvarṇānvīkṣya vāyasān ||14||

15meruruttamamadhyānāmadhamānāṃ ca pakṣiṇām
|
aviśeṣakaro yasmāttasmādenaṃ tyajāmyaham ||15||

16tamādityo'nuparyeti satataṃ jyotiṣāṃ varaḥ
|
candramāśca sanakṣatro vāyuścaiva pradakṣiṇaḥ ||16||

17sa parvato mahārāja divyapuṣpaphalānvitaḥ
|
bhavanairāvṛtaḥ sarvairjāmbūnadapariṣkṛtaiḥ ||17||

18tatra devagaṇā rājangandharvāsurarākṣasāḥ
|
apsarogaṇasaṃyuktāḥ śaile krīḍanti sarvadā ||18||

19tatra brahmā ca rudraśca śakraścāpi sureśvaraḥ
|
sametya vividhairyajñairyajante'nekadakṣiṇaiḥ ||19||

20tumbururnāradaścaiva viśvāvasurhahā huhūḥ
|
abhigamyāmaraśreṣṭhāṃstuṣṭuvurvividhaiḥ stavaiḥ ||20||

21saptarṣayo mahātmānaḥ kaśyapaśca prajāpatiḥ
|
tatra gacchanti bhadraṃ te sadā parvaṇi parvaṇi ||21||

22tasyaiva mūrdhanyuśanāḥ kāvyo daityairmahīpate
|
imāni tasya ratnāni tasyeme ratnaparvatāḥ ||22||

23tasmātkubero bhagavāṃścaturthaṃ bhāgamaśrute
|
tataḥ kalāṃśaṃ vittasya manuṣyebhyaḥ prayacchati ||23||

24pārśve tasyottare divyaṃ sarvartukusumaiścitam
|
karṇikāravanaṃ ramyaṃ śilājālasamudgatam ||24||

25tatra sākṣātpaśupatirdivyairbhūtaiḥ samāvṛtaḥ
|
umāsahāyo bhagavānramate bhūtabhāvanaḥ ||25||

26karṇikāramayīṃ mālāṃ bibhratpādāvalambinīm
|
tribhirnetraiḥ kṛtodyotastribhiḥ sūryairivoditaiḥ ||26||

27tamugratapasaḥ siddhāḥ suvratāḥ satyavādinaḥ
|
paśyanti nahi durvṛttaiḥ śakyo druṣṭuṃ maheśvaraḥ ||27||

28tasya śailasya śikharātkṣīradhārā nareśvara
|
viśvarūpā parimitā bhīmanirghātaniḥsvanā ||28||

29puṇyā puṇyatamairjuṣṭā gaṅgā bhāgīrathī śubhā
|
plavantīva pravegena hrade candramasaḥ śubhe ||29||

30tayā hyutpāditaḥ puṇyaḥ sa hradaḥ sāgaropamaḥ
|
tāṃ dhārayāmāsa tadā durdharāṃ parvatairapi ||30||

31śataṃ varṣasahasrāṇāṃ śirasaiva maheśvaraḥ
|
merostu paścime pārśve ketumālo mahīpate ||31||

32jambūkhaṇḍe tu tatraiva mahājanapado nṛpa
|
āyurdaśasahasrāṇi varṣāṇāṃ tatra bhārata ||32||

33suvarṇavarṇāśca narāḥ striyaścāpsarasopamāḥ
|
anāmayā vītaśokā nityaṃ muditamānasāḥ ||33||

34jāyante mānavāstatra niṣṭaptakanakaprabhāḥ
|
gandhamādanaśrṛṅgeṣu kuberaḥ saha rākṣasaiḥ ||34||

35saṃvṛto'psarasāṃ saṅghairmodate guhyakādhipaḥ
|
gandhamādanapārśve tu pare tvaparagaṇḍikāḥ ||35||

36ekādaśasahasrāṇi varṣāṇāṃ paramāyuṣaḥ
|
tatra hṛṣṭā narā rājaṃstejoyuktā mahābalāḥ
|
striyaścotpalavarṇābhāḥ sarvāḥ supriyadarśanāḥ ||36||

37nīlātparataraṃ śvetaṃ śvetāddhairaṇyakaṃ param
|
varṣamairātaṃ rājannānājanapadāvṛtam ||37||

38dhanuḥsaṃsthe mahārāja dve varṣe dakṣiṇottare
|
ilāvṛttaṃ madhyamaṃ tu pañca varṣāṇi caiva hi ||38||

39uttarottarametebhyo varṣamudricyate guṇaiḥ
|
āyuḥpramāṇamārogyaṃ dharmataḥ kāmato'rthataḥ ||39||

40samanvitāni bhūtāni teṣu varṣeṣu bhārata
|
evameṣā mahārāja parvataiḥ pṛthivī citā ||40||

41hemakūṭastu sumahān kailāso nāma parvataḥ
|
yatra vaiśravaṇo rājan guhyakaiḥ saha modate ||41||

42astyuttareṇa kailāsaṃ mainākaṃ parvataṃ prati
|
hiraṇyaśrṛṅgaḥ sumahāndivyo maṇimayo giriḥ ||42||

43tasya pārśve mahaddivyaṃ śubhraṃ kāñcanavālukam
|
ramyaṃ bindusaro nāma yatra rājā bhagīrathaḥ ||43||

44dṛṣṭvā bhāgīrathīṃ gaṅgāmuvāsa bahulāḥ samāḥ
|
yūpā maṇimayāstatra caityāścāpi hiraṇmayāḥ ||44||

45tatreṣṭvā tu gataḥ siddhiṃ sahasrākṣo mahāyaśāḥ
|
sraṣṭā bhūtapatiryatra sarvalokaiḥ sanātanaḥ ||45||

46upāsyate tigmatejā yatra bhūtaiḥ samantataḥ
|
naranārāyaṇau brahmā manuḥ sthāṇuśca pañcamaḥ ||46||

47tatra divyā tripathagā prathamaṃ tu pratiṣṭhitā
|
brahmalokādapakrāntā saptadhā pratipadyate ||47||

48vasvaukasārā nalinī pāvanī ca sarasvatī
|
jambūnadī ca sītā ca gaṅgā sindhuśca saptamī ||48||

49acintyā divyasaṅkāśā prabhoreṣaiva saṃvidhiḥ
|
upāsate yatra satraṃ sahasrayugaparyaye ||49||

50dṛśyā'dṛśyā ca bhavati tatra tatra sarasvatī
|
etā divyā saptagaṅgāstriṣu lokeṣu viśrutāḥ ||50||

51rakṣāṃsi vai himavati hemakūṭe tu guhyakāḥ
|
sarpā nāgāśca niṣadhe gokarṇaṃ ca tapovanam ||51||

52devāsurāṇāṃ sarveṣāṃ śvetaparvata ucyate
|
gandharvā niṣadhe nityaṃ nīle brahmarṣayastathā
|
śrṛṅgavāṃstu mahārāja devānāṃ pratisañcaraḥ ||52||

53ityetāni mahārāja sapta varṣāṇi bhāgaśaḥ
|
bhūtānyupaniviṣṭāni gatimanti dhruvāṇi ca ||53||

54teṣāmṛddhirbahuvidhā dṛśyate daivamānuṣī
|
aśakyā parisaṅkhyātuṃ śraddheyā tu bubhūṣatā ||54||

55yāṃ tu pṛcchasi māṃ rājandivyāmetāṃ śaśākṛtim
|
pārśve śaśasya dve varṣe ukte ye dakṣiṇottare
|
karṇau tu nagadvīpaśca kāśyapadvīpa eva ca ||55||

56tāmraparṇaśilo rājañchrīmānmalayaparvataḥ
|
etaddvitīyaṃ dvīpasya dṛśyate śaśasaṃsthitam ||56||
