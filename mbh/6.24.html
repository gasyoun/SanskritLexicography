Mahābhārata 6.24 (Bhīṣmaparvan)

1dhṛtarāṣṭra uvāca|
keṣāṃ prahṛṣṭāstatrāgre yodhā yudhyanti sañjaya
|
udagramanasaḥ ke vā ke vā dīnā vicetasaḥ ||1||

2ke pūrvaṃ prāharaṃstatra yuddhe hṛdayakampane
|
māmakāḥ pāṇḍaveyā vā tanmamācakṣva sañjaya ||2||

3kasya senāsamudaye gandhamālyasamudbhavaḥ
|
vāyuḥ pradakṣiṇaścaiva yodhānāmabhigarjatām ||3||

4sañjaya uvāca|
ubhayoḥ senayostatra yodhā jahṛṣire tadā
|
srajaḥ samāḥ sugandhānāmubhayatra samudbhavaḥ ||4||

5saṃhatānāmanīkānāṃ vyūḍhānāṃ bharatarṣabha
|
saṃsargātsamudīrṇānāṃ vimardaḥ sumahānabhūt ||5||

6vāditraśabdastumulaḥ śaṅkhabherīvimiśritaḥ
|
śūrāṇāṃ raṇaśūrāṇāṃ garjatāmitaretaram ||6||

7ubhayoḥ senayo rājanmahānvyatikaro'bhavat
|
anyonyaṃ vīkṣamāṇānāṃ yodhānāṃ bharatarṣabha
|
kuñjarāṇāṃ ca nadatāṃ sainyānāṃ ca prahṛṣyatām ||7||
