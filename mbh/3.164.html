Mahābhārata 3.164 (Āraṇyakaparvan)

1vaiśampāyana uvāca|
tasminnagendre vasatāṃ tu teṣāṃ mahātmanāṃ saṅgatamāsthitānām
|
ratiḥ pramodaśca babhūva teṣāmākāṅkṣatāṃ darśanamarjunasya||1||

2tān vīryayuktān suviśuddhakāmāṃstejasvinaḥ satyadhṛtipradhānān
|
samprīyamāṇā bahavo'bhijagmurgandharvasaṅghāśca maharṣayaśca||2||

3taṃ pādapaiḥ puṣpadharairupetaṃ nagottamaṃ prāpya mahārathānām
|
manaḥprasādaḥ paramo babhūva yathā divaṃ prāpya marudgaṇānām||3||

4mayūrahaṃsasvananāditāni puṣpopakīrṇāni mahācalasya
|
śṛṅgāṇi sānūni ca paśyamānā gireḥ paraṃ harṣamavāpya tasthuḥ||4||

5sākṣāt kubereṇa kṛtāśca tasmin nagottame saṃvṛtakūlarodhasaḥ
|
kādambakāraṇḍavahaṃsajuṣṭāḥ padmākulāḥ puṣkariṇīrapaśyan||5||

6krīḍāpradeśāṃśca samṛddharūpān sucitramālyāvṛtajātaśobhān
|
maṇiprakīrṇāṃśca manoramāṃśca yathā bhaveyurdhanadasya rājñaḥ||6||

7anekavarṇaiśca sugandhibhiśca mahādrumaiḥ santatamabhrajālaiḥ
|
tapaḥpradhānāḥ satataṃ carantaḥ śṛṅgaṃ gireścintayituṃ na śekuḥ||7||

8svatejasā tasya nagottamasya mahauṣadhīnāṃ ca tathā prabhāvāt
|
vibhaktabhāvo na babhūva kaścidahoniśānāṃ puruṣapravīra||8||

9yamāsthitaḥ sthāvarajaṅgamāni vibhāvasurbhāvayate'mitaujāḥ
|
tasyodayaṃ cāstamanaṃ ca rvārāstatra sthitāste dadṛśurnṛsiṃhāḥ||9||

10ravestamistrāgamanirgamāṃste tathodayaṃ cāstamanaṃ ca vīrāḥ
|
samāvṛtāḥ prekṣya tamonudasya gabhastijālaiḥ pradiśo diśaśca||10||

11svādhyāyavantaḥ satatakriyāśca dharmapradhānāśca śucivratāśca
|
satye sthitāstasya mahārathasya satyavratasyāgamanapratīkṣāḥ||11||

12ihaiva harṣo'stu samāgatānāṃ kṣipraṃ kṛtāstreṇa dhanañjayena
|
iti bruvantaḥ paramāśiṣaste pārthāstapoyogaparā babhūvuḥ||12||

13dṛṣṭvā vicitrāṇi girau vanāni kirīṭinaṃ cintayantāmabhīkṣṇam
|
babhūva rātrirdivasaśca teṣāṃ saṃvatsareṇaiva samānarūpaḥ||13||

14yadaiva dhaumyānumate mahātmā kṛtvā jaṭāṃ pravrajitaḥ sa jiṣṇuḥ
|
tadaiva teṣāṃ na babhūva harṣaḥ kuto ratistadgatamānasānām||14||

15bhrāturniyogāttu yudhiṣṭhirasya vanādasau vāraṇamattagāmī
|
yatkāmyakāt pravrajitaḥ sa jiṣṇustadaiva te śokahatā babhūvuḥ||15||

16tathaiva taṃ cintayatāṃ sitāśvamastrārthinaṃ vāsavamabhyupetam
|
māso'tha kṛcchreṇa tadā vyatītastasminnage bhārata bhāratānām||16||

17uṣittvā pañcavarṣāṇi sahasrākṣaniveśane
|
avāpya divyānyastrāṇi sarvāṇi vibudheśvarāt||17||

18āgneyaṃ vāruṇaṃ saumyaṃ vāyavyamatha vaiṣṇavam
|
aindraṃ pāśupataṃ brāhmaṃ pārameṣṭhyaṃ prajāpateḥ||18||

19yamasya dhātuḥ savitustvaṣṭurvaiśravaṇasya ca
|
tāni prāpya sahastrākṣāda‌bhivādya śatakratum||19||

20anujñātastadā tena kṛtvā cāpi pradakṣiṇam
|
āgacchadarjunaḥ prītaḥ prahṛṣṭo gandhamādanam||20||
