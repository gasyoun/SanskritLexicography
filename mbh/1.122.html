Mahābhārata 1.122 (Ādiparvan)

1vaiśaṃpāyana uvāca|
evamuktastayā rājā tāṃ devīṃ punarabravīt
|
dharmaviddharmasaṃyuktamidaṃ vacanamuttamam||1||

2pāṇḍuruvāca|
evametatpurā kunti vyuṣitāśvaścakāra ha
|
yathā tvayoktaṃ kalyāṇi sa hyāsīdamaropamaḥ||2||

3atha tvidaṃ pravakṣyāmi dharmatattvaṃ nibodha me
|
purāṇamṛṣibhirdṛṣṭaṃ dharmavidbhirmahātmabhiḥ||3||

4anāvṛtāḥ kila purā striya āsanvarānane
|
kāmacāravihāriṇyaḥ svatantrāścāruhāsini||4||

5tāsāṃ vyuccaramāṇānāṃ kaumārātsubhage patīn
|
nādharmo'bhūdvarārohe sa hi dharmaḥ purā'bhavat||5||

6taṃ caiva dharmaṃ paurāṇaṃ tiryagyonigatāḥ prajāḥ
|
adyāpyanuvidhīyante kāmakrodhavivarjitāḥ||6||

7pramāṇadṛṣṭo dharmo'yaṃ pūjyate ca maharṣibhiḥ
|
uttareṣu ca rambhoru kuruṣvadyāpi pūjyate||7||

8strīṇāmanugrahakaraḥ sa hi dharmaḥ sanātanaḥ
|
asmiṃstu loke na cirānmaryādeyaṃ śucismite||8||

9sthāpitā yena yasmācca tanme vistarataḥ śṛṇu
|
babhūvoddālako nāma maharṣiriti naḥ śrutam||9||

10śvetaketuriti khyātaḥ putrastasyābhavanmuniḥ
|
maryādeyaṃ kṛtā tena dharmyā vai śvetaketunā||10||

11kopātkamalapatrākṣi yadarthaṃ tannibodha me
|
śvetaketoḥ kila purā samakṣaṃ mātaraṃ pituḥ||11||

12jagrāha brāhmaṇaḥ pāpau gacchāva iti cābravīt
|
ṛṣiputrastadā kopaṃ cakārāmarṣitastadā||12||

13mātaraṃ tāṃ tathā dṛṣṭvā nīyamānāṃ balādiva
|
kruddhaṃ taṃ tu pitā dṛṣṭvā śvetaketumuvāca ha||13||

14mā tāta kopaṃ kārṣīstvameṣa dharmaḥ sanātanaḥ
|
anāvṛtā hi sarveṣāṃ varṇānāmaṅganā bhuvi||14||

15yathā gāvaḥ sthitāstāta sve sve varṇe tathā prajāḥ
|
ṛṣiputro'tha taṃ dharmaṃ śvetaketurna cakṣame||15||

16cakāra caiva maryādāmimāṃ strīpuṃsayorbhuvi
|
mānuṣeṣu mahābhāge na tvevānyeṣu jantuṣu||16||

17tadāprabhṛti maryādā sthiteyamiti naḥ śrutam
|
vyuccarantyāḥ patiṃ nāryā adyaprabhṛti pātakam||17||

18bhrūṇahatyāsamaṃ ghoraṃ bhaviṣyatyasukhāvaham
|
bhāryāṃ tathā vyuccarataḥ kaumārabrahmacāriṇīm||18||

19pativratāmetadeva bhavitā pātakaṃ bhuvi
|
patyā niyuktā yā caiva patnī putrārthameva ca||19||

20na kariṣyati tasyāśca bhaviṣyati tadeva hi
|
iti tena purā bhīru maryādā sthāpitā balāt||20||

21uddālakasya putreṇa dharmyā vai śvetaketunā
|
saudāsena ca rambhoru niyuktā putrajanmani||21||

22madayantī jagāmarṣiṃ vasiṣṭhamiti naḥ śrutam
|
tasmāllebhe ca sā putramaśmakaṃ nāma bhāminī||22||

23evaṃ kṛtavatī sāpi bhartuḥ priyacikīrṣayā
|
asmākamapi te janma viditaṃ kamalekṣaṇe||23||

24kṛṣṇadvaipāyanādbhīru kurūṇāṃ vaṃśavṛddhaye
|
ata etāni sarvāṇi kāraṇāni samīkṣya vai||24||

25mamaitadvacanaṃ dharmyaṃ kartumarhasyanindite
|
ṛtāvṛtau rājaputri striyā bhartā pativrate||25||

26nātivartavya ityevaṃ dharmaṃ dharmavido viduḥ
|
śeṣeṣvanyeṣu kāleṣu svātantryaṃ strī kilārhati||26||

27dharmamevaṃ janāḥ santaḥ purāṇaṃ paricakṣate
|
bhartā bhāryāṃ rājaputri dharmyaṃ vā'dharmyameva vā||27||

28yadbrūyāttattathā kāryamiti vedavido viduḥ
|
viśeṣataḥ putragṛddhī hīnaḥ prajananātsvayam||28||

29yathā'hamanavadyāṅgi putradarśanalālasaḥ
|
tathā raktāṅgulinibhaḥ padmapatranibhaḥ śubhe||29||

30prasādanārthaṃ mayā te'yaṃ śirasyabhyudyato'ñjaliḥ
|
manniyogātsukeśānte dvijātestapasā'dhikāt||30||

31putrānguṇasamāyuktānutpādayitumarhasi
|
tvatkṛte'haṃ pṛthuśroṇi gaccheyaṃ putriṇāṃ gatim||31||

32vaiśaṃpāyana uvāca|
evamuktā tataḥ kuntī pāṇḍuṃ parapurañjayam
|
pratyuvāca varārohā bhartuḥ priyahite ratā||32||

33pitṛveśmanyahaṃ bālā niyuktā'tithipūjane
|
ugraṃ paryacaraṃ tatra brāhmaṇaṃ saṃśitavratam||33||

34nigūḍhaniścayaṃ dharme yaṃ taṃ durvāsasaṃ viduḥ
|
tamahaṃ saṃśitātmānaṃ sarvayatnairatoṣayam||34||

35same'bhicārasaṃyuktamācaṣṭa bhagavānvaram
|
mantraṃ tvimaṃ ca me prādādabravīccaiva māmidam||35||

36yaṃ yaṃ devaṃ tvametena mantreṇāvāhayiṣyasi
|
akāmo vā sakāmo vā vaśaṃ te samupaiṣyati||36||

37tasya tasya prasādātte rājñi putro bhaviṣyati
|
ityuktā'haṃ tadā tena pitṛveśmani bhārata||37||

38brāhmaṇasya vacastathyaṃ tasya kālo'yamāgataḥ
|
anujñātā tvayā devamāhvayeyamahaṃ nṛpa||38||

39tena mantreṇa rājarṣe yathāsyānnau prajā hitā
|
āvāhayāmi kaṃ devaṃ brūhi satyavatāṃ vara||
tvatto'nujñāpratīkṣāṃ māṃ viddhyāsminkarmaṇi sthitām||39||

40pāṇḍuruvāca|
adyaiva tvaṃ vārarohe prayatasva yathāvidhi
|
dharmamāvāhaya śubhe sa hi lokeṣu puṇyabhāk||40||

41adharmeṇa na no dharmaḥ saṃyujyati kathaṃcana
|
lokaścāyaṃ varārohe dharmo'yamiti manyate||41||

42dhārmikaśca kurūṇāṃ sa bhaviṣyati na saṃśayaḥ
|
dharmeṇa cāpi dattasya nādharme raṃsyate manaḥ||42||

43tasmāddharmaṃ puruskṛtya niyatā tvaṃ śucismite
|
upacārābhicārābhyāṃ dharmamāvāhayasva vai||43||

44vaiśaṃpāyana uvāca|
sā tathoktā tathetyuktvā tena bhartrā varāṅganā
|
abhivādyābhyanujñātā pradakṣiṇamavartate||44||
