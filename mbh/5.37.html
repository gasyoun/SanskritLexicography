Mahābhārata 5.37 (Udyogaparvan)

1vidura uvāca|
saptadaśemān rājendra manuḥ svāyaṃbhuvo'bravīt
|
vaicitravīrya puruṣānākāśaṃ muṣṭibhirghnataḥ ||1||

2tānevendrasya ca dhanuranāmyaṃ namato'bravīt
|
atho marīcinaḥ pādānagrāhyān gṛhṇatastathā ||2||

3yaścāśiṣyaṃ śāsti vai yaśca tuṣyedyaścātivelaṃ bhajate dviṣantam
|
striyaśca yo rakṣati bhadramaśnute yaścāyācyaṃ yācate katthate vā ||3||

4yaścābhijātaḥ prakarotyakāryaṃ yaścābalo balinā nityavairī
|
aśraddadhānāya ca yo bravīti yaścākāmyaṃ kāmayate narendra ||4||

5vadhvā'vahāsaṃ śvaśuro manyate yo vadhvā'vasannabhayo mānakāmaḥ
|
parakṣetre nirvapati yaśca bījaṃ striyaṃ ca yaḥ parivadate'tivelam ||5||

6yaścāpi labdhvā na smarāmīti vādī dattvā ca yaḥ katthati yācyamānaḥ
|
yaścāsataḥ sāntvamupānayīta etānnayanti nirayaṃ pāśahastāḥ ||6||

7yasmin yathā vartate yo manuṣyastasmiṃstathā vartitavyaṃ sa dharmaḥ
|
māyācāro māyayā vartitavyaḥ sādhvācāraḥ sādhunā pratyupeyaḥ ||7||

8jarā rūpaṃ harati hi dhairyamāśā mṛtyuḥ prāṇān dharmacaryāmasūyā
|
kāmo hriyaṃ vṛttamanāryasevā krodhaḥ śriyaṃ sarvamevābhimānaḥ ||8||

9dhṛtarāṣṭra uvāca|
śatāyuruktaḥ puruṣaḥ sarvavedeṣu vai yadā
|
nāpnotyatha ca tatsarvamāyuḥ keneha hetunā ||9||

10vidura uvāca|
atimāno'tivādaśca tathā'tyāgo narādhipa
|
krodhaścātmavidhitsā ca mitradrohaśca tāni ṣaṭ ||10||

11eta evāsayastīkṣṇāḥ kṛntantyāyūṃṣi dehinām
|
etāni mānavān ghnanti na mṛtyurbhadramastu te ||11||

12viśvastasyaiti yo dārān yaścāpi gurutalpagaḥ
|
vṛṣalīpatirdvijo yaśca pānapaścaiva bhārata ||12||

13ādeśakṛdvṛttihantā dvijānāṃ preṣakaśca yaḥ
|
śaraṇāgatahā caiva sarve brahmahaṇaḥ samāḥ
|
etaiḥ sametya kartavyaṃ prāyaścittamiti śrutiḥ ||13||

14gṛhītavākyo nayavidvadānyaḥ śeṣānnabhoktā hyavihiṃsakaśca
|
nānarthakṛtyākulitaḥ kṛtajñaḥ satyo mṛduḥ svargamupaiti vidvān ||14||

15sulabhāḥ puruṣā rājan satataṃ priyavādinaḥ
|
apriyasya tu pathyasya vaktā śrotā ca durlabhaḥ ||15||

16yo hi dharmaṃ samāśritya hitvā bhartuḥ priyāpriye
|
apriyāṇyāha pathyāni tena rājā sahāyavān ||16||

17tyajet kulārthe puruṣaṃ grāmasyārthe kulaṃ tyajet
|
grāmaṃ janapadasyārthe ātmārthe pṛthivīṃ tyajet ||17||

18āpadarthe dhanaṃ rakṣeddārān rakṣeddhanairapi
|
ātmānaṃ satataṃ rakṣeddārairapi dhanairapi ||18||

19dyūtametat purā kalpe dṛṣṭaṃ vairakaraṃ nṛṇām
|
tasmāt dyūtaṃ na seveta hāsyārthamapi buddhimān ||19||

20uktaṃ mayā dyūtakāle'pi rājannedaṃ yuktaṃ vacanaṃ prātipeya
|
tadauṣadhaṃ pathyamivāturasya na rocate tava vaicitravīrya ||20||

21kākairimāṃścitrabarhān mayūrān parājayethāḥ pāṇḍavān dhārtarāṣṭraiḥ
|
hitvā siṃhān kroṣṭukān gṛhamānaḥ prāpte kāle śocitā tvaṃ narendraḥ ||21||

22yastāta na kruddhyati sarvakālaṃ bhṛtyasya bhaktasya hite ratasya
|
tasmin bhṛtyā bhartari viśvasanti na cainamāpatsu parityajanti ||22||

23na bhṛtyānāṃ vṛttisaṃrodhanena rājyaṃ dhanaṃ sañjighṛkṣedapūrvam
|
tyajanti hyenaṃ vañcitā vai viruddhāḥ snigdhā hyamātyā parihīnabhogāḥ ||23||

24kṛtyāni pūrvaṃ parisaṅkhyāya sarvāṇyāyavyaye cānurūpāṃ ca vṛttim
|
saṅgṛhṇīyādanurūpān sahāyān sahāyasādhyāni hi duṣkarāṇi ||24||

25abhiprāyaṃ yo viditvā tu bhartuḥ sarvāṇi kāryāṇi karotyatandrī
|
vaktā hitānāmanurakta āryaḥ śaktijña ātmeva hi so'nukampyaḥ ||25||

26vākyaṃ tu yo nādriyate'nuśiṣṭaḥ pratyāha yaścāpi niyujyamānaḥ
|
prajñābhimānī pratikūlavādī tyājyaḥ sa tādṛk tvarayaiva bhṛtyaḥ ||26||

27astabdhamaklībamadīrghasūtraṃ sānukrośaṃ ślakṣṇamahāryamanyaiḥ
|
arogajātīyamudāravākyaṃ dūtaṃ vadantyaṣṭaguṇopapannam ||27||

28na viśvāsājjātu parasya gehe gacchennaraścetayāno vikāle
|
na catvare niśi tiṣṭhennigūḍho na rājakāmyāṃ yoṣitaṃ prārthayīta ||28||

29na ninhavaṃ mantragatasya gacchet saṃsṛṣṭamantrasya kusaṅgatasya
|
na ca brūyānnāśvasimi tvayīti sakāraṇaṃ vyapadeśaṃ tu kuryāt ||29||

30ghṛṇī rājā puṃścalī rājabhṛtyaḥ putro bhrātā vidhavā bālaputrā
|
senājīvī coddhṛtabhūrireva vyavahāreṣu varjanīyāḥ syurete ||30||

31aṣṭau guṇāḥ puruṣaṃ dīpayanti prajñā ca kaulyaṃ ca śrutaṃ damaśca
|
parākramaścābahubhāṣitā ca dānaṃ yathāśakti kṛtajñatā ca ||31||

32etān guṇāṃstāta mahānubhāvāneko guṇaḥ saṃśrayate prasahya
|
rājā yadā satkurute manuṣyaṃ sarvān guṇaneṣa guṇo bibharti ||32||

33guṇā daśa snānaśīlaṃ bhajante balaṃ rūpaṃ svaravarṇapraśuddhiḥ
|
sparśaśca gandhaśca viśuddhatā ca śrīḥ saukumāryaṃ pravarāśca nāryaḥ ||33||

34guṇāśca ṣaṇmitabhuktaṃ bhajante ārogyamāyuśca balaṃ sukhaṃ ca
|
anāvilaṃ cāsya bhavatyapatyaṃ na cainamādyūna iti kṣipanti ||34||

35akarmaśīlaṃ ca mahāśanaṃ ca lokadviṣṭaṃ bahumāyaṃ nṛśaṃsam
|
adeśakālajñamaniṣṭaveṣametān gṛhe na prativāsayeta ||35||

36kadaryamākrośakamaśrutaṃ ca vanaukasaṃ dhūrtamamānyamāninam
|
niṣṭhūriṇaṃ kṛtavairaṃ kṛtaghnametān bhṛśārto'pi na jātu yācet ||36||

37saṃkliṣṭakarmāṇamatipramādaṃ nityānṛtaṃ cādṛḍhabhaktikaṃ ca
|
visṛṣṭarāgaṃ paṭumāninaṃ cāpyetānna seveta narādhamān ṣaṭ ||37||

38sahāyabandhanā hyarthāḥ sahāyāścārthabandhanāḥ
|
anyonyabandhanāvetau vinā'nyonyaṃ na siddhyataḥ ||38||

39utpādya putrānanṛṇāṃśca kṛtvā vṛttiṃ ca tebhyo'nuvidhāya kāñcit
|
sthāne kumārīḥ pratipādya sarvā araṇyasaṃstho'tha munirbubhūṣet ||39||

40hitaṃ yat sarvabhūtānāmātmanaśca sukhāvaham
|
tatkuryādīśvaro hyetanmūlaṃ sarvārthasiddhaye ||40||

41vṛddhiḥ prabhāvastejaśca sattvamutthānameva ca
|
vyavasāyaśca yasya syāttasyāvṛttibhayaṃ kutaḥ ||41||

42paśya doṣān pāṇḍavairvigrahe tvaṃ yatra vyatheyurapi devāḥ saśakrāḥ
|
putrairvairaṃ nityamudvignavāso yaśaḥpraṇāśo dviṣataśca harṣaḥ||42||

43bhīṣmasya kopastava caivendrakalpa droṇasya rājñaśca yudhiṣṭhirasya
|
utsādayellokamimaṃ pravṛddhaḥ śveto grahastiryagivāpatan khe ||43||

44tava putraśataṃ caiva karṇaḥ pañca ca pāṇḍavāḥ
|
pṛthivīmanuśāseyurakhilāṃ sāgarāmbarām ||44||

45dhārtarāṣṭrā vanaṃ rājan vyāghrāḥ pāṇḍusutā matāḥ
|
mā vanaṃ chindhi savyāghraṃ mā vyāghrā nīnaśanvanāt ||45||

46na syādvanamṛte vyāghrān vyāghrā na syurṛte vanam
|
vanaṃ hi rakṣyate vyāghrairvyāghrān rakṣati kānanam ||46||

47na tathecchanti kalyāṇān pareṣāṃ vedituṃ guṇān
|
yathaiṣāṃ jñātumicchanti nairguṇyaṃ pāpacetasaḥ ||47||

48arthasiddhiṃ parāmicchan dharmamevāditaścaret
|
na hi dharmādapaityarthaḥ svargalokādivāmṛtam ||48||

49yasyātmā virataḥ pāpāt kalyāṇe ca niveśitaḥ
|
tena sarvamidaṃ buddhaṃ prakṛtirvikṛtiśca yā ||49||

50yo dharmamarthaṃ kāmaṃ ca yathākālaṃ niṣevate
|
dharmārthakāmasaṃyogaṃ so'mutreha ca vindati ||50||

51sanniyacchati yo vegamutthitaṃ krodhaharṣayoḥ
|
sa śriyo bhājanaṃ rājan yaścāpatsu na muhyati ||51||

52balaṃ pañcavidhaṃ nityaṃ puruṣāṇāṃ nibodha me
|
yattu bāhubalaṃ nāma kaniṣṭhaṃ balamucyate ||52||

53amātyalābho bhadraṃ te dvitīyaṃ balamucyate
|
tṛtīyaṃ dhanalābhaṃ tu balamāhurmanīṣiṇaḥ ||53||

54yattvasya sahajaṃ rājan pitṛpaitāmahaṃ balam
|
abhijātabalaṃ nāma taccaturthaṃ balaṃ smṛtam ||54||

55yena tvetāni sarvāṇi saṅgṛhītāni bhārata
|
yadbalānāṃ balaṃ śreṣṭhaṃ tatprajñābalamucyate ||55||

56mahate yo'pakārāya narasya prabhavennaraḥ
|
tena vairaṃ samāsajya dūrastho'smīti nāśvaset ||56||

57strīṣu rājasu sarpeṣu svādhyāyaprabhuśatruṣu
|
bhogeṣvāyuṣi viśvāsaṃ kaḥ prājñaḥ kartumarhati ||57||

58prajñāśareṇābhihatasya jantościkitsakāḥ santi na cauṣadhāni
|
na homamantrā na ca maṅgalāni nātharvaṇā nāpyagadāḥ susiddhāḥ ||58||

59sarpaścāgniśca siṃhaśca kulaputraśca bhārata
|
nāvajñeyā manuṣyeṇa sarve hyete'titejasaḥ ||59||

60agnistejo mahalloke gūḍhastiṣṭhati dāruṣu
|
na copayuṅkte taddāru yāvannoddīpyate paraiḥ ||60||

61sa eva khalu dārubhyo yadā nirmathya dīpyate
|
taddāru ca vanaṃ cānyannirdahatyāśu tejasā ||61||

62evameva kule jātāḥ pāvakopamatejasaḥ
|
kṣamāvanto nirākārāḥ kāṣṭhe'gniriva śerate ||62||

63latādharmā tvaṃ saputraḥ śālāḥ pāṇḍusutā matāḥ
|
na latā vardhate jātu mahādrumamanāśritā ||63||

64vanaṃ rājaṃstava putro'mbikeya siṃhān vane pāṇḍavāṃstāta viddhi
|
siṃhairvihīnaṃ hi vanaṃ vinaśyet siṃhā vinaśyeyurha ṛte vanena ||64||
