Mahābhārata 12.136 (Śāntiparvan)

1bhīṣma uvāca|
atra gāthā brahmagītāḥ kīrtayanti purāvidaḥ
|
yena mārgeṇa rājā vai kośaṃ saṃjanayatyuta ||1||

2na dhanaṃ yajñaśīlānāṃ hāryaṃ devasvameva ca
|
dasyūnāṃ niṣkriyāṇāṃ ca kṣatriyo hartumarhati ||2||

3imāḥ prajāḥ kṣatriyāṇāṃ rājyabhogāśca bhārata
|
dhanaṃ hi kṣatriyasyaiva dvitīyasya na vidyate ||3||

4tadasya syādbalārtha vā dhanaṃ yajñārthameva ca
|
abhogyāścauṣadhīśchittvā bhogyā eva pacantyuta ||4||

5yo vai na devānna pitṝnna mārtyānhaviṣā'rcati
|
anarthakaṃ dhanaṃ taḥ prāhurdharmavido janāḥ ||5||

6hareta draviṇaṃ rājan dhārmikaḥ pṛthivīpatiḥ
|
tataḥ prīṇayate lokaṃ na kośaṃ tadvidhaṃ nṛpaḥ ||6||

7asādhubhyo'rthamādāya sādhubhyo yaḥ prayacchati
|
ātmānaṃ saṃkramaṃ kṛtvā kṛtsnadharmavideva saḥ ||7||

8tathā tathā jayellokān śaktyā caiva yathā yathā
|
udbhijjā jantavo yadvacchuklajīvā yathā yathā ||8||

9animittātsambhavanti tathā'yajñaḥ prajāyate ||9||

10yathaiva daṃśamaśakaṃ yathā cāṇḍapipīlikam
|
saiva vṛttirayajñeṣu yathā dharmo vidhīyate ||10||

11yathā hyakasmādbhavati bhūmau pāṃsurvilolitaḥ
|
tathaiveha bhaveddharmaḥ sūkṣmaḥ sūkṣmatarastathā ||11||
