Mahābhārata 3.21 (Āraṇyakaparvan)

1vāsudeva uvāca|
evaṃ sa puruṣavyāghraḥ śālvarājo mahāripuḥ
|
yudhyamāno mayā saṅkhye viyadabhyagamatpunaḥ||1||

2tataḥ śataghnīśca mahāgadāśca dīptāṃśca śūlānmusalānasīṃśca
|
cikṣepa roṣānmayi mandabuddhiḥ śālvo mahārāja jayābhikāṅkṣī||2||

3tānāśugairāpatato'hamāśu nivārya hantuṃ khagamānkha eva
|
dvidhā tridhā cācchinamāśu muktaistato'ntarikṣe ninado babhūva||3||

4tataḥ śatasahasreṇa śarāṇāṃ nataparvaṇām
|
dārukaṃ vājinaścaiva rathaṃ ca samavākirat||4||

5tato māmabravīdvīra dāruko vihvalanniva
|
sthātavyamiti tiṣṭhāmi śālvabāṇaprapīḍitaḥ
|
avasthātuṃ na śaknomi aṅgaṃ me vyavasīdati||5||

6iti tasya niśamyāhaṃ sāratheḥ karuṇaṃ vacaḥ
|
avekṣamāṇo yantāramapaśyaṃ śarapīḍitam||6||

7na tasyorasi no mūrdhni na kāye na bhujadvaye
|
antaraṃ pāṇḍavaśreṣṭha paśyāmyanicitaṃ śaraiḥ||7||

8sa tu bāṇavarotpīḍādvisravatyasṛgulbaṇam
|
abhivṛṣṭe yathā meghe girirgairikadhātumān||8||

9abhīṣu hastaṃ taṃ dṛṣṭvā sīdantaṃ sārathiṃ raṇe
|
astaṃ bhayaṃ mahābāho śālvabāṇaprapīḍitam||9||

10atha māṃ puruṣaḥ kaściddvārakānilayo'bravīt
|
tvarito rathamāropya sauhṛdādiva bhārata||10||

11āhukasya vaco vīra tasyaiva paricārakaḥ
|
viṣaṇṇaḥ sannakaṇṭhena tannibodha yudhiṣṭhira||11||

12dvārakādhipatirvīra āha tvāmāhuko vacaḥ
|
keśavaihi vijānīṣva yattvāṃ pitṛsakho'bravīt||12||

13upāyāyādya śālvena dvārakāṃ vṛṣṇinandana
|
viṣakte tvayi durdharṣa hataḥ śūrasuto balāt||13||

14tadalaṃ sādhu yuddhena nivartasva janārdana
|
dvārakāmeva rakṣasva kāryametanmahattava||14||

15ityahaṃ tasya vacanaṃ śrutvā paramadurmanāḥ
|
niścayaṃ nādhigacchāmi kartavyasyetarasya ca||15||

16sātyakiṃ baladevaṃ ca pradyumnaṃ ca mahāratham
|
jagarhe manasā vīra tacchrutvā mahadapriyam||16||

17ahaṃ hi dvārakāyāśca pituśca kurunandana
|
teṣu rakṣāṃ samādhāya prayātaḥ saubhapātane||17||

18baladevo mahābāhuḥ kaccijjīvati śatruhā
|
sātyakī raukmiṇeyaśca cārudeṣṇaśca vīryavān||18||

19sāmbaprabhṛtayaścaivetyahamāsaṃ sudurmanāḥ
|
eteṣu hi naravyāghra jīvatsu na kathañcana||19||

20śakyaḥ śūrasuto hantumapi vajrabhṛtā svayam
|
hataḥ śūrasuto vyaktaṃ vyaktaṃ caite parāsavaḥ||20||

21baladevamukhāḥ sarva iti me niścitā matiḥ
|
so'haṃ sarvavināśaṃ taṃ cintayāno muhurmuhuḥ
|
suvihvalo mahārāja punaḥ śālvamayodhayam||21||

22tato'paśyaṃ mahārāja prapatantamahaṃ tadā
|
saubhācchūrasutaṃ vīra tato māṃ moha āviśat||22||

23tasya rūpaṃ prapatataḥ piturmama narādhipa
|
yayāteḥ kṣīṇapuṇyasya svargādiva mahītalam||23||

24viśīrṇamalinoṣṇīṣaprakīrṇāmbaramūrdhajaḥ
|
prapatandṛśyate ha sma kṣīṇapuṇya iva grahaḥ||24||

25tataḥ śārṅgadhanuḥ śreṣṭhaṃ karātprapatitaṃ mama
|
mohāpannaśca kaunteya rathopastha upāviśam||25||

26tato hāhākṛtaṃ sarvaṃ sainyaṃ me gatacetanam
|
māṃ dṛṣṭvā rathanīḍasthaṃ gatāsumiva bhārata||26||

27prasārya bāhū patataḥ prasārya caraṇāvapi
|
rūpaṃ piturme vibabhau śakuneḥ patato yathā||27||

28taṃ patantaṃ mahābāho śūlapaṭṭiśapāṇayaḥ
|
abhighnanto bhṛśaṃ vīrā mama ceto hyakampayan||28||

29tato muhūrtātpratilabhya saṃjñāmahaṃ tadā vīra mahāvimarde
|
na tatra saubhaṃ na ripuṃ na śālvaṃ paśyāmi vṛddhaṃ pitaraṃ na cāpi||29||

30tato mamāsīnmanasi māyeyamiti niścitam
|
prabuddho'smi tato bhūyaḥ śataśo'vākiraccharān||30||
