Mahābhārata 12.151 (Śāntiparvan)

1bhīṣma uvāca|
evamuktaḥ pratyuvāca taṃ muniṃ janamejayaḥ
|
garhyaṃ bhavān garhayate nindyaṃ nindati māṃ punaḥ ||1||

2dhikkāryaṃ māṃ dhikkurute tasmāttvā'haṃ prasādaye
|
sarvaṃ hīdaṃ duṣkṛtaṃ me jvalāmyagnāvivāhitaḥ ||2||

3svakarmāṇyabhisandhāya nābhinandati me manaḥ
|
prāpya ghoraṃ bhayaṃ nūnaṃ mayā vaivasvatādapi ||3||

4tattu śalyamanirhatya kathaṃ śakṣyāmi jīvitum
|
sarvaṃ manyuṃ vinīya tvamabhi māṃ vada śaunaka ||4||

5mahānāsaṃ brāhmaṇānāṃ bhūyo vakṣyāmi sāmpratam
|
astu śeṣaṃ kulasyāsya mā parābhūdidaṃ kulam ||5||

6na hi no brahmaśaptānāṃ śeṣaṃ bhavitumarhati
|
stutīralabhamānānāṃ saṃvidaṃ vedaniścitān ||6||

7nirvidyamānaḥ subhṛśaṃ bhūyo vakṣyāmi śāśvatam
|
bhūyaścaivābhirakṣantu nirdhanān nirjanā iva ||7||

8na hyayajñā amuṃ lokaṃ prāpnuvanti kathañcana
|
āpātānpratitiṣṭhanti pulindaśabarā iva ||8||

9avijñāyaiva me prajñā bālasyeva sa paṇḍitaḥ
|
brahman piteva putrasya prītimān bhava śaunaka ||9||

10śaunaka uvāca|
kimāścaryaṃ tataḥ prājño bahu kuryādasāmpratam
|
iti vai paṇḍito bhūtvā bhūtānāṃ nānukupyate ||10||

11prajñāprāsādamāruhya aśocyaḥ śocate janān
|
jagatīsthānivādristhaḥ prajñayā pratipatsyati ||11||

12na copalabhyate tena na cāścaryāṇi kurvate
|
niviṇṇātmā parokṣo vā dhikkṛtaḥ pūrvasādhuṣu ||12||

13viditaṃ bhavato vīryaṃ māhātmyaṃ veda āgame
|
kuruṣveha yathāśānti brahmā śaraṇamastu te ||13||

14tadvai pāratrikaṃ tāta brāhmaṇānāmakupyatām
|
athavā tapyase pāpe dharmamevānupaśya vai ||14||

15janamejaya uvāca|
anutapye ca pāpena na ca dharmaṃ vilopaye
|
bubhūṣuṃ bhajamānaṃ ca prītimān bhava śaunaka ||15||

16śaunaka uvāca|
chittvā dambhaṃ ca mānaṃ ca prītimicchāmi te nṛpa
|
sarvabhūtahitaṃ tiṣṭha dharmaṃ caiva prati smaran ||16||

17na bhayānna ca kārpaṇyānna lobhāttvāmupāhvaye
|
tāṃ me daivīṃ giraṃ satyāṃ śṛṇu tvaṃ brāhmaṇaiḥ saha ||17||

18so'haṃ na kenaciccārthī tvāṃ ca dharmādupāhvaye
|
krośatāṃ sarvabhūtānāṃ hāhā dhigiti jalpatām ||18||

19vakṣyanti māmadharmajñaṃ tyakṣyanti suhṛdo janāḥ
|
tā vācaḥ suhṛdaḥ śrutvā saṃjvariṣyanti me bhṛśam ||19||

20kecideva mahāprājñāḥ pratijñāsyanti tattvataḥ
|
jānīhi matkṛtaṃ tāta brāhmaṇān prati bhārata ||20||

21yathā te matkṛte kṣemaṃ labhante te tathā kuru
|
pratijānīhi cādrohaṃ brāhmaṇānāṃ narādhipa ||21||

22janamejaya uvāca|
naiva vācā na manasā punarjātu na karmaṇā
|
drogdhāsmi brāhmaṇānvipra caraṇāvapi te spṛśe ||22||
