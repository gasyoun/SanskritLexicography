Mahābhārata 14.30 (Āśvamedhikaparvan)

1pitara ūcuḥ|
atrāpyudāharantīmamitihāsaṃ purātanam
|
śrutvā ca tattathā kāryaṃ bhavatā dvijasattama||1||

2alarko nāma rājarṣirabhavatsumahātapāḥ
|
dharmajñaḥ satyavādī ca mahātmā sudṛḍhavrataḥ||2||

3sasāgarāntāṃ dhanuṣā vinirjitya mahīmimām
|
kṛtvā suduṣkaraṃ karma manaḥ sūkṣme samādadhe||3||

4sthitasya vṛkṣamūleṣu tasya cintā babhūva ha
|
utsṛjya sumahatkarma sūkṣmaṃ prati mahāmate||4||

5alarka uvāca|
manaso me balaṃ jātaṃ mano jitvā dhruvo jayaḥ
|
anyatra bāṇāndhāsyāmi śatrubhiḥ parivāritaḥ||5||

6yadidaṃ cāpalātkarma sarvānmartyāṃścikīrṣati
|
manaḥprati sutīkṣṇāgrānahaṃ mokṣyāmi sāyakān||6||

7mana uvāca|
neme bāṇāstariṣyanti māmalarka kathañcana
|
tavaiva marma bhetsyanti bhinnamarmā mariṣyasi||7||

8anyān bāṇānsamīkṣasva yaistvaṃ māṃ sūdayiṣyasi
|
tacchutvāpyavicintyātha tato vacanamabravīt||8||

9alarka uvāca|
āghrāya subahūn gandhāṃstāneva pratigṛdhyati
|
tasmād ghrāṇaṃ prati śarān pratimokṣyāmyahaṃ śitān||9||

10ghrāṇa uvāca|
neme bāṇāstariṣyanti māmalarka kathañcana
|
tavaiva marma bhetsyanti bhinnamarmā mariṣyasi||10||

11anyānbāṇānsamīkṣasva yaistvaṃ māṃ sūdayiṣyasi
|
tacchutvā sa vicintyātha tato vacanamabravīt||11||

12alarka uvāca|
iyaṃ svādūn rasān bhuktvā tāneva pratigṛdhyati
|
tasmājjihvāṃ prati śarān pratimokṣyāmyahaṃ śitān||12||

13jihvovāca|
neme bāṇāstariṣyanti māmalarka kathañcana
|
tavaiva marma bhetsyanti bhinnamarmā mariṣyasi||13||

14anyānbāṇānsamīkṣasva yaistvaṃ māṃ sūdayiṣyasi
|
tacchutvā sa vicintyātha tato vacanamabravīt||14||

15alarka uvāca|
spṛṣṭvā tvagvividhān sparśāṃstāneva pratigṛdhyati
|
tasmāttvacaṃ pāṭayiṣye vividhaiḥ kaṅkapatribhiḥ||15||

16tvaguvāca|
neme bāṇāstariṣyanti māmalarka kathañcana
|
tavaiva marma bhetsyanti bhinnamarmā mariṣyasi||16||

17anyānbāṇānsamīkṣasva yaistvaṃ māṃ sūdayiṣyasi
|
tacchutvā sa vicintyātha tato vacanamabravīt||17||

18alarka uvāca|
śrutvā tu vividhāñśabdāṃstāneva pratigṛdhyati
|
tasmācchrotraṃ prati śarān pratimuñcāmyahaṃ śitān||18||

19śrotramuvāca|
neme bāṇāstariṣyanti māmalarka kathañcana
|
tavaiva marma bhetsyanti tato hāsyasi jīvitam||19||

20anyānbāṇānsamīkṣasva yaistvaṃ māṃ sūdayiṣyasi
|
tacchutvā sa vicintyātha tato vacanamabravīt||20||

21alarka uvāca|
dṛṣṭvā rūpāṇi bahuśastānyeva pratigṛdhyati
|
tasmāccakṣurhaniṣyāmi niśitaiḥ sāyakairaham||21||

22cakṣuruvāca|
neme bāṇāstariṣyanti māmalarka kathañcana
|
tavaiva marma bhetsyanti bhinnamarmā mariṣyasi||22||

23anyānbāṇānsamīkṣasva yaistvaṃ māṃ sūdayiṣyasi
|
tacchutvā sa vicintyātha tato vacanamabravīt||23||

24alarka uvāca|
iyaṃ niṣṭhā bahuvidhā prajñayā tvadhyavasyati
|
tasmādbuddhiṃ prati śarānpratimokṣyāmyahaṃ śitān||24||

25buddhiruvāca|
neme bāṇāstariṣyanti māmalarka kathañcana
|
tavaiva marma bhetsyanti bhinnamarmā mariṣyasi
|
anyānbāṇānsamīkṣasva yaistvaṃ māṃ sūdayiṣyasi||25||

26brāhmaṇa uvāca|
tato'larkastapo ghoraṃ tatraivāsthāya duṣkaram
|
nādhyagacchat paraṃ śaktyā bāṇameteṣu saptasu||26||

27susamāhitacetāstu sa tato'cintayatprabhuḥ
|
sa vicintya ciraṃ kālamalarko dvijasattama||27||

28nādhyagacchatparaṃ śreyo yogānmatimatāṃ varaḥ
|
sa ekāgraṃ manaḥ kṛtvā niścalo yogamāsthitaḥ||28||

29indriyāṇi jaghānāśu bāṇenaikena vīryavān
|
yogenātmānamāviśya siddhiṃ paramikāṃ gataḥ||29||

30vismitaścāpi rājarṣirimāṃ gāthāṃ jagāda ha
|
aho kaṣṭaṃ yadasmābhiḥ sarvaṃ bāhyamanuṣṭhitam||30||

31bhogatṛṣṇāsamāyuktaiḥ pūrvaṃ rājyamupāsitam
|
iti paścānmayā jñātaṃ yogānnāsti paraṃ sukham||31||

32iti tvamanujānīhi rāma mā kṣatriyān jahi
|
tapo ghoramupātiṣṭha tataḥ śreyo'bhipatsyase||32||

33ityuktaḥ sa tapo ghoraṃ jāmadagnyaḥ pitāmahaiḥ
|
āsthitaḥ sumahābhāgo yayau siddhiṃ ca durgamām||33||
