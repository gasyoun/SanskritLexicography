Mahābhārata 4.31 (Virāṭaparvan)

1vaiśampāyana uvāca|tatasteṣāṃ mahārāja tatraivāmitatejasām
|
chadmaliṅgapraviṣṭānāṃ pāṇḍavānāṃ mahātmanām ||1||

2vyatītaḥ samayaḥ samyagvasatāṃ vai purottame
|
kurvatāṃ tasya karmāṇi virāṭasya mahīpateḥ ||2||

3kīcake tu hate rājā virāṭaḥ paravīrahā
|
parāṃ saṃbhāvanāṃ cakre kuntīputre yudhiṣṭhire ||3||

4tatastrayodaśasyānte tasya varṣasya bhārata
|
suśarmaṇā gṛhītaṃ tadgodhanaṃ tarasā bahu ||4||

5tato javena mahatā gopaḥ puramathāvrajat
|
sa dṛṣṭvā matsyarājaṃ ca rathāt praskandya kuṇḍalī ||5||

6śūraiḥ parivṛtaṃ yodhaiḥ kuṇḍalāṅgadadhāribhiḥ
|
saṃvṛtaṃ mantribhiḥ sārdhaṃ pāṇḍavaiśca mahātmabhiḥ ||6||

7taṃ sabhāyāṃ mahārājamāsīnaṃ rāṣṭravardhanam
|
so'bravīdupasaṃgamya virāṭaṃ praṇatastadā ||7||

8asmān yudhi vinirjitya paribhūya sabāndhavān
|
gavāṃ śatasahasrāṇi trigartāḥ kālayanti te ||8||

9tān parīpsasva rājendra mā neśuḥ paśavastava
|
tacchrutvā nṛpatiḥ senāṃ matsyānāṃ samayojayat ||9||

10rathanāgāśvakalilāṃ pattidhvajasamākulām
|
rājāno rājaputrāśca tanutrāṇyatha bhejire ||10||

11bhānumanti vicitrāṇi śūrasevyāni bhāgaśaḥ
|
savajrāyasagarbhaṃ tu kavacaṃ tatra kāñcanam ||11||

12virāṭasya priyo bhrātā śatānīko'bhyahārayat
|
sarvapārasavaṃ varma kalyāṇapaṭalaṃ dṛḍham ||12||

13śatānīkādavarajo madirākṣo'bhyahārayat
|
śatasūryaṃ śatāvartaṃ śatabindu śatākṣimat ||13||

14abhedyakalpaṃ matsyānāṃ rājā kavacamāharat
|
utsedhe yasya padmāni śataṃ saugandhikāni ca ||14||

15suvarṇapṛṣṭhaṃ sūryābhaṃ sūryadatto'bhyahārayat
|
dṛḍhamāyasagarbhaṃ ca śvetaṃ varma śatākṣimat ||15||

16virāṭasya suto jyeṣṭho vīraḥ śaṅkho'bhyahārayat
|
śataśaśca tanutrāṇi yathāsvaṃ te mahārathāḥ ||16||

17yotsyamānā anahyanta devarūpāḥ prahāriṇaḥ
|
sūpaskareṣu śubhreṣu mahatsu ca mahārathāḥ ||17||

18pṛthak kāñcanasannāhān ratheṣvaśvānayojayan
|
sūryacandrapratīkāśe rathe divye hiraṇmaye ||18||

19mahānubhāvo matsyasya dhvaja ucchiśriye tadā
|
athānyān vividhākārāndhvajān hemapariṣkṛtān ||19||

20yathāsvaṃ kṣatriyāḥ śūrā ratheṣu samayojayan
|
atha matsyo'bravīdrājā śatānīkaṃ jaghanyajam ||20||

21kaṅkaballavagopālā dāmagranthiśca vīryavān
|
yuddhyeyuriti me buddhirvartate nātra saṃśayaḥ ||21||

22eteṣāmapi dīyantāṃ rathā dhvajapatākinaḥ
|
kavacāni ca citrāṇi dṛḍhāni ca mṛdūni ca ||22||

23pratimuñcantu gātreṣu dīyantāmāyudhāni ca
|
vīrāṅgarūpāḥ puruṣā nāgarājakaropamāḥ ||23||

24neme jātu na yudhyeranniti me dhīyate matiḥ
|
etacchrutvā tu nṛpatervākyaṃ tvaritamānasaḥ
|
śatānīkastu pārthebhyo rathān rājan samādiśat ||24||

25sahadevāya rājñe ca bhīmāya nakulāya ca
|
tān prahṛṣṭāṃstataḥ sūtā rājabhaktipuraskṛtāḥ ||25||

26nirdiṣṭā naradevena rathān śīghramayojayan
|
kavacāni vicitrāṇi mṛdūni ca dṛḍhāni ca ||26||

27virāṭaḥ prādiśadyāni teṣāmakliṣṭakarmaṇām
|
tānyāmucya śarīreṣu daṃśitāste parantapāḥ ||27||

28rathān hayaiḥ susaṃpannānāsthāya ca narottamāḥ
|
niryayurmuditāḥ pārthāḥ śatrusaṅghāvamardinaḥ ||28||

29tarasvinaśchannarūpāḥ sarve yuddhaviśāradāḥ
|
rathān hemaparicchannānāsthāya ca mahārathāḥ ||29||

30virāṭamanvayuḥ pārthāḥ sahitāḥ kurupuṅgavāḥ
|
catvāro bhrātaraḥ śūrāḥ pāṇḍavāḥ satyavikramāḥ ||30||

31bhīmāśca mattamātaṅgā prabhinnakaraṭāmukhāḥ
|
kṣarantaścaiva nāgendrāḥ sudantāḥ ṣaṣṭihāyanāḥ ||31||

32svārūḍhā yuddhakuśalaiḥ śikṣitā hastisādibhiḥ
|
rājānamanvayuḥ paścāccalanta iva parvatāḥ ||32||

33viśāradānāṃ mukhyānāṃ hṛṣṭānāṃ cārujīvinām
|
aṣṭau rathasahasrāṇi daśa nāgaśatāni ca ||33||

34ṣaṣṭiścāśvasahastrāṇi matsyānāmabhiniryayuḥ
|
tadanīkaṃ virāṭasya śuśubhe bharatarṣabha ||34||

35saṃprayātaṃ tadā rājannirīkṣantaṃ gavāṃ padam
|
tadbalāgryaṃ virāṭasya saṃprasthitamaśobhata
|
dṛḍhāyudhajanākīrṇaṃ gajāśvarathasaṅkulam ||35||
