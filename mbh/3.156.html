Mahābhārata 3.156 (Āraṇyakaparvan)

1vaiśampāyana uvāca|
tasminnivasamāno'tha dharmarājo yudhiṣṭhiraḥ
|
kṛṣṇayā sahitān bhrātṝnityuvāca sahadvijān||1||

2dṛṣṭāni tīrthānyasmābhiḥ puṇyāni ca śivāni ca
|
manaso hlādanīyāni vanāni ca pṛthakpṛthak||2||

3devaiḥ pūrvaṃ vicīrṇāni munibhiśca mahātmabhiḥ
|
yathākramaviśeṣeṇa dvijaiḥ saṃpūjitāni ca||3||

4ṛṣīṇāṃ pūrvacaritaṃ tathā karma viceṣṭitam
|
rājarṣīṇāṃ ca caritaṃ kathāśca vividhāḥ śubhāḥ||4||

5śṛṇvānāstatra tatra sma āśrameṣu śiveṣu ca
|
abhiṣekaṃ dvijaiḥ sārdhaṃ kṛtavanto viśeṣataḥ||5||

6arcitāḥ satataṃ devāḥ puṣpairadbhiḥ sadā ca vaḥ
|
yathālabdhairmūlaphalaiḥ pitaraścāpi tarpitāḥ||6||

7parvateṣu ca ramyeṣu sarveṣu ca sarassu ca
|
udadhau ca mahāpuṇye sūpaspṛṣṭaṃ mahātmabhiḥ||7||

8ilā sarasvatī sindhuryamunā narmadā tathā
|
nānātīrtheṣu ramyeṣu sūpaspṛṣṭaṃ saha dvijaiḥ||8||

9gaṅgādvāramatikramya bahavaḥ parvatāḥ śubhāḥ
|
himavān parvataścaiva nānādvijagaṇāyutaḥ||9||

10viśālā badarī dṛṣṭā naranārāyaṇāśramaḥ
|
divyā puṣkariṇī dṛṣṭā siddhadevarṣipūjitā||10||

11yathākramaviśeṣeṇa sarvāṇyāyatanāni ca
|
darśitāni dvijaśreṣṭhā lomaśena mahātmanā||11||

12imaṃ vaiśravaṇāvāsaṃ puṇyaṃ siddhaniṣevitam
|
kathaṃ bhīma gamiṣyāmo gatiranptaradhīyatām||12||

13vaiśampāyana uvāca|
evaṃ bruvati rājendre vāguvācāśarīriṇī
|
na śakyo durgamo gantumito vaiśravaṇāśramāt||13||

14anenaiva pathā rājan pratigaccha yathāgatam
|
naranārāyaṇasthānaṃ badarītyabhiviśrutam||14||

15tasmādyāsyasi kaunteya siddhacāraṇasevitam
|
bahupuṣpaphalaṃ ramyamāśramaṃ vṛṣaparvaṇaḥ||15||

16atikramya ca taṃ pārtha tvārṣṭiṣeṇāśrame vaseḥ
|
tato drakṣyasi kaunteya niveśaṃ dhanadasya ca||16||

17etasminnantare vāyurdivyagandhavahaḥ śuciḥ
|
sukhaprahlādanaḥ śītaḥ puṇyavarṣaṃ vavarṣa ca||17||

18śrutvā tu divyāmākāśādvācaṃ sarve visismiyuḥ
|
ṛṣīṇāṃ brāhmaṇānāṃ ca pārthivānāṃ viśeṣataḥ||18||

19śrutvā tanmahadāścaryaṃ dvijo dhaumyo'bravīttadā
|
na śakyamuttaraṃ vaktumevaṃ bhavatu bhārata||19||

20tato yudhiṣṭhiro rājā pratijagrāha tadvacaḥ
|
pratyāgamya punastaṃ tu naranārāyaṇāśramam||20||

21bhīmasenādibhiḥ sarvairbhrātṛbhiḥ parivāritaḥ
|
pāñcālyā brāhmaṇāścaiva nyavasanta sukhaṃ tadā||21||
