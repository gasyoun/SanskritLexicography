Mahābhārata 12.5 (Śāntiparvan)

1nārada uvācaḥ
|
āviṣkṛtabalaṃ karṇaṃ śrutvā rājā sa māgadhaḥ
|
āhvayaddvairathenājau jarāsandho mahīpatiḥ ||1||

2tayoḥ samabhavadyuddhaṃ divyāstraviduṣordvayoḥ
|
yudhi nānāpraharaṇairanyonyamabhivarṣatoḥ ||2||

3kṣīṇabāṇau vidhanuṣau bhagnakhaḍgau mahīṃ gatau
|
bāhubhiḥ samasajjetāmubhāvapi balānvitau ||3||

4bāhukaṇṭakayuddhena tasya karṇo'tha yudhyataḥ
|
bibheda sandhiṃ dehasya jarayā śleṣitasya hi ||4||

5sa vikāraṃ śarīrasya dṛṣṭvā nṛpatirātmanaḥ
|
prīto'smītyabravītkarṇaṃ vairamutsṛjya dūrataḥ ||5||

6prītyā dadau sa karṇāya mālinīṃ nagarīmatha
|
aṃgeṣu naraśārdūla sa rājā''sītsapatnajit ||6||

7pālayāmāsa campāṃ ca karṇaḥ parabalārdanaḥ
|
duryodhanasyānumate tavāpi viditaṃ tathā ||7||

8evaṃ śastrapratāpena prathitaḥ so'bhavatkṣitau
|
tvaddhitārthaṃ surendreṇa bhikṣito varmakuṇḍale ||8||

9sa divye sahaje prādātkuṇḍale paramārjite
|
sahajaṃ kavacaṃ cāpi mohito devamāyayā ||9||

10vimuktaḥ kuṇḍalābhyāṃ ca sahajena ca varmaṇā
|
nihato vijayenājau vāsudevasya paśyataḥ ||10||

11brāhmaṇasyābhiśāpena rāmasya ca mahātmanaḥ
|
kuntyāśca varadānena māyayā ca śatakratoḥ ||11||

12bhīṣmāvamānātsaṃkhyāyāṃ rathasyārdhānukīrtanāt
|
śalyāttejovadhāccāpi vāsudevanayena ca ||12||

13rudrasya devarājasya yamasya varuṇasya ca
|
kuveradroṇayoścaiva kṛpasya ca mahātmanaḥ ||13||

14astrāṇi divyānyādāya yudhi gāṇḍīvadhanvanā
|
hato vaikartanaḥ karṇo divākarasamadyutiḥ ||14||

15evaṃ śaptastava bhrātā bahubhiśvāpi vañcitaḥ
|
na śocyaḥ puruṣavyāghra yuddhena nidhanaṃ gataḥ ||15||
