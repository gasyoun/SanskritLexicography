Mahābhārata 3.57 (Āraṇyakaparvan)

1bṛhadaśva uvāca| atha kāle śubhe prāpte tithau puṇye kṣaṇe tathā
|
ājuhāva mahīpālānbhīmo rājā svayaṃvare ||1||

2tacchrutvā pṛthivīpālāḥ sarve hṛcchayapīḍitāḥ
|
tvaritāḥ samapājagmurdamayantīmabhīpsavaḥ||2||

3kanakastambharuciraṃ toraṇena virājitam
|
viviśuste nṛpā raṅgaṃ mahāsiṃha ivācalam||3||

4tatrāsaneṣu vividheṣvāsīnāḥ pṛthivīkṣitaḥ
|
surabhisragdharāḥ sarve pramṛṣṭamaṇikuṇḍalāḥ||4||

5tāṃ rājasamitiṃ puṇyāṃ nāgairbhogavatīmiva
|
sampūrṇāṃ puruṣavyāghrairvyāghrairgiriguhāmiva||5||

6tatra sma pīnā dṛśyante bāhavaḥ parighopamāḥ
|
ākāravarṇasuślakṣṇāḥ pañcaśīrṣā ivoragāḥ||6||

7sukeśāntāni cārūṇi sunāsākṣibhruvāṇi ca
|
mukhāni rājñāṃ śobhante nakṣatrāṇi yathā divi||7||

8damayantī tato raṅgaṃ praviveśa śubhānanā
|
muṣṇantī prabhayā rājñāṃ cakṣūṃṣi ca manāṃsi ca||8||

9tasyā gātreṣu patitā teṣāṃ dṛṣṭirmahātmanām
|
tatra tatraiva saktā'bhūnna cacāla ca paśyatām||9||

10tataḥ saṅkīrtyamāneṣu rājñāṃ nāmasu bhārata
|
dadarśa bhaimī puruṣānpañcatulyākṛtīni ha||10||

11tānsamīkṣya tataḥ sarvānnirviśeṣākṛtīnsthitān
|
sandehādatha vedarbhī nābhyajānānnalaṃ nṛpam||11||

12yaṃ yaṃ hi dadṛśe teṣāṃ taṃ taṃ mene nalaṃ nṛpam
|
sā cintayantī buddhyā'tha tarkayāmāsa bhāminī||12||

13kathaṃ nu devāñjānīyāṃ kathaṃ vidyāṃ nalaṃ nṛpam
|
evaṃ sañcintayantī sā vaidarbhī bhṛśaduḥkhitā||13||

14śrutāni devaliṅgāni tarkayāmāsa bhārata
|
devānāṃ yāni liṅgāni sthavirebhyaḥ śrutāni me||14||

15tānīha tiṣṭhatāṃ bhūmāvekasyāpi na lakṣaye
|
sā viniścitya bahudhā vicārya ca punaḥ punaḥ||15||

16śaraṇaṃ prati devānāṃ prāptakālamamanyata
|
vācā ca manasā caiva namaskāraṃ prayujya sā||16||

17devebhyaḥ prāñjalirbhūtvā vepamānedamabravīt
|
haṃsānāṃ vacanaṃ śrutvā yathā me naiṣadho vṛtaḥ
|
patitve tena satyena devāstaṃ pradiśantu me||17||

18manasā vacasā caiva yathā nābhicarāmyaham
|
tena satyena vibudhāstameva pradiśantu me||18||

19yathā devaiḥ sa me bhartā vihito niṣadhādhiṣaḥ
|
tena satyena me devāstameva pradiśantu me||19||

20yathedaṃ vratamārabdhaṃ nalasyārādhane mayā
|
tena satyena me devāstameva pradiśantu me||20||

21svaṃ caiva rūpaṃ kurvantu lokapālā maheśvarāḥ
|
yathā'hamabhijānīyāṃ puṇyaślokaṃ narādhipam||21||

22niśamya damayantyāstatkaruṇaṃ pratidevitam
|
niścayaṃ paramaṃ tathyamanurāgaṃ ca naiṣadhe||22||

23manoviśuddhiṃ buddhiṃ ca bhaktiṃ rāgaṃ ca naiṣadhe
|
yathoktaṃ cakrire devāḥ sāmarthyaṃ liṅgadhāraṇe||23||

24sā'paśyadvibudhānsarvānasvedānstabdhalocanān
|
hṛṣitasragrajohīnānsthitānaspṛśataḥ kṣitim||24||

25chāyādvitīyo mlānasragrajaḥsvedasamanvitaḥ
|
bhūmiṣṭho naiṣadhaścaiva nimeṣeṇa ca sūcitaḥ||25||

26sā samīkṣya tu tāndevānpuṇyaślokaṃ ca bhārata
|
naiṣadhaṃ varayāmāsa bhaimī dharmeṇa pāṇḍava||26||

27vilajjamānā vastrāntaṃ jagrāhāyatalocanā
|
skandhadeśe'sṛjattasya srajaṃ paramaśobhanām||27||

28varayāmāsa caivainaṃ patitve varavarṇinī
|
tato hāheti sahasā muktaḥ śabdo narādhipaiḥ||28||

29devairmaharṣibhistatra sādhu sādhviti bhārata
|
vismitairīritaḥ śabdaḥ praśaṃsadbhirnalaṃ nṛpam||29||

30damayantīṃ tu kauravya vīrasenasuto nṛpaḥ
|
āśvāsayadvarārohāṃ prahṛṣṭenāntarātmanā||30||

31yattvaṃ bhajasi kalyāṇi pumāṃsaṃ devasannidhau
|
tasmānmāṃ viddhi bhartārametatte vacane ratam||31||

32yāvacca me dhariṣyanti prāṇā dehe śucismite
|
tāvattvayi bhaviṣyāmi satyametadbravīmi te||32||

33damayantī tathā vāgbhirabhinandya kṛtāñjaliḥ
|
tau parasparataḥ prītau dṛṣṭvā tvagnipurogamān||33||

34tāneva śaraṇaṃ devāñjagmaturmanasā tadā
|
vṛte tu naiṣadhe bhaimyā lokapālā mahaujasaḥ||34||

35prahṛṣṭamanasaḥ sarve nalāyāṣṭau varāndaduḥ
|
pratyakṣadarśanaṃ yajñe gatiṃ cānuttamāṃ śubhām||35||

36naiṣadhāya dadau śakraḥ prīyamāṇaḥ śacīpatiḥ
|
agnirātmabhavaṃ prādādyatra vāñcchati naiṣadhaḥ||36||

37lokānātmaprabhāṃścaiva dadau tasmai hutāśanaḥ
|
yamastvannarasaṃ prādāddharme ca paramāṃ sthitim||37||

38apāṃ patirapāṃ bhāvaṃ yatra vāñcchati naiṣadhaḥ
|
srajaścottamagandhāḍhyāḥ sarve ca mithunaṃ daduḥ ||38||

39varānevaṃ pradāyāsya devāste tridivaṃ gatāḥ
|
pārthivāścānubhūyāsya vivāhaṃ vismayānvitāḥ||39||

40damayantyāśca muditāḥ pratijagmuryathāgatam
|
gateṣu pārthivendreṣu bhīmaḥ prīto mahāmanāḥ||40||

41vivāhaṃ kārayāmāsa damayantyā nalasya ca
|
uṣya tatra tathākāmaṃ naiṣadho dvipadāṃ varaḥ||41||

42bhīmena samanujñāto jagāma nagaraṃ svakam
|
avāpya nārīratnaṃ tu puṇyaśloko'pi pārthivaḥ||42||

43reme saha tayā rājañcchacyeva balavṛtrahā
|
atīva mudito rājā bhrājamānoṃ'śumāniva||43||

44arañjayatprajā vīro dharmeṇa paripālayan
|
īje cāpyaśvamedhena yayātiriva nāhuṣaḥ||44||

45anyaiśca bahubhirdhīmānkratubhiścāptadakṣiṇaiḥ
|
pugaśca ramaṇīyeṣu vaneṣūpavaneṣu ca||45||

46damayantyā saha nalo vijahārāmaropamaḥ
|
janayāmāsa ca tato damayantyāṃ mahāmanāḥ
|
indrasenaṃ sutaṃ cāpi indrasenāṃ ca kanyakām||46||

47evaṃ sa yajamānaśca viharaṃśca narādhipaḥ
|
rarakṣa vasusaṃpūrṇāṃ vasudhāṃ vasudhādhipaḥ||47||
