Mahābhārata 12.211 (Śāntiparvan)

1bhīṣma uvāca|
caturvidhāni bhūtāni sthāvarāṇi carāṇi ca
|
avyaktaprabhavānyāhuravyaktanidhanāni ca
|
avyaktalakṣaṇaṃ vidyādavyaktātmātmakaṃ manaḥ ||1||

2yathā'śvatthakaṇīkāyāmantarbhūto mahādrumaḥ
|
niṣpanno dṛśyate vyaktamavyaktātsambhavastathā ||2||

3abhidravatyayaskāntamayo niścetanaṃ yathā
|
svabhāvahetujā bhāvā yadvadanyadapīdṛśam ||3||

4tadvadavyaktajā bhāvāḥ kartuḥ kāraṇalakṣaṇāḥ
|
acetanāścetayituḥ kāraṇādabhisaṃhatā ||4||

5na bhūrna khaṃ dyaurbhūtāni narṣayo na surāsurāḥ
|
nānyadāsīdṛte jīvamāsedurna tu saṃhatam ||5||

6pūrvaṃ nityaṃ sarvagataṃ manohetumalakṣaṇam
|
ajñānakarmanirdiṣṭametatkāraṇalakṣaṇam ||6||

7tatkāraṇairhi saṃyuktaṃ kāryasaṃgrahakārakam
|
yenaitadvartate cakramanādinidhanaṃ mahat ||7||

8avyaktanābhaṃ vyaktāraṃ vikāraparimaṇḍalam
|
śretrajñādhiṣṭhitaṃ cakraṃ snigdhākṣaṃ vartate dhruvam ||8||

9snigdhatvāttilavatsarvaṃ cakre'sminpīḍyate jagat
|
tilapīḍairivākramya bhogairajñānasambhavaiḥ ||9||

10karma tatkurute tarṣādahaṃkāraparigrahāt
|
kāryakāraṇasaṃyoge sa heturupapāditaḥ ||10||

11nābhyeti kāraṇaṃ kāryaṃ na kāryaṃ kāraṇaṃ tathā
|
kāryavyaktena karaṇe kālo bhavati hetumān ||11||

12hetuyuktāḥ prakṛtayo vikārāśca parasparam
|
anyonyamabhivartante puruṣādhiṣṭhitāḥ sadā ||12||

13rājasaistāmasairbhāvaiścyuto hetubalānvitaḥ
|
kṣetrajñamevānuyāti pāṃsurvāterito yathā ||13||

14na ca taiḥ spṛśyate bhāvairna te tena mahātmanā
|
sarajasko'rajaskaśca naiva vāyurbhavedyathā ||14||

15tathaitadantaraṃ vidyātsattvakṣetrajñayorbudhaḥ
|
abhyāsātsa tathā yukto na gacchetprakṛtiṃ punaḥ ||15||

16sandehametamutpannamacchinadbhagavānṛṣiḥ
|
tathā vārtāṃ samīkṣeta kṛtalakṣaṇasammitām ||16||

17bījānyagnyupadagdhāni na rohanti yathā punaḥ
|
jñānadagdhaistathā kleśairnātmā sampadyate punaḥ ||17||
