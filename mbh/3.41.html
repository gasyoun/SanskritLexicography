Mahābhārata 3.41 (Āraṇyakaparvan)

1vaiśaṃpāyana uvāca|
tasya saṃpaśyatastveva pinākī vṛṣabhadhvajaḥ
|
jagāmādarśanaṃ bhānurlokasyevāstamīyivān||1||

2tato'rjunaḥ paraṃ cakre vismayaṃ paravīrahā
|
mayā sākṣānmahādevo dṛṣṭa ityeva bhārata||2||

3dhanyo'smyanugṛhīto'smi yanmayā tryambako haraḥ
|
pinākī varado rūpī dṛṣṭaḥ spṛṣṭaśca pāṇinā||3||

4kṛtārthaṃ cāvagacchāmi paramātmānamāhave
|
śatrūṃśca vijitānsarvānnirvṛttaṃ ca prayojanam||4||

5ityevaṃ cintayānasya pārthasyāmitatejasaḥ
|
tato vaidūryavarṇābho bhāsayansarvato diśaḥ
|
yādogaṇavṛtaḥ śrīmānājagāma jaleśvaraḥ||5||

6nāgairnadairnadībhiśca daityaiḥ sādhyaiśca daivataiḥ
|
varuṇo yādasāṃ bhartā vaśī taṃ deśamāgamat||6||

7atha jāmbūnadavapurvimānena mahārciṣā
|
kuveraḥ samanuprāpto yakṣairanugataḥ prabhuḥ||7||

8vidyotayannivākāśamadbhutopamadarśanaḥ
|
dhanānāmīśvaraḥ śrīmānarjunaṃ draṣṭumāgataḥ||8||

9tathā lokāntakṛcchrīmānyamaḥ sākṣātpratāpavān
|
martyamūrtidharaiḥ sārdhaṃ pitṛbhirlokabhāvanaiḥ||9||

10daṇḍapāṇiracintyātmā sarvabhūtavināśakṛt
|
vaivasvato dharmarājo vimānenāvamāsayan||10||

11trīṃllokānguhyakāṃścaiva gandharvāṃśca sapannagān
|
dvitīya iva mārtaṇḍo yugānte samupasthite||11||

12te bhānumanti citrāṇi śikharāṇi mahāgireḥ
|
samāsthāyārjunaṃ tatra dadṛśustapasānvitam||12||

13tato muhūrtādbhagavānairāvataśirogataḥ
|
ājagāma sahendrāṇyā śakraḥ suragaṇairvṛtaḥ||13||

14pāṇḍureṇātapatreṇa dhriyamāṇena mūrdhani
|
śuśubhe tārakārājaḥ sitamabhramiva sthitaḥ||14||

15saṃstūyamāno gandharvairṛṣibhiśca tapodhanaiḥ
|
śṛṅgaṃ gireḥ samāsādya tastho sūrya ivoditaḥ||15||

16atha meghasvano dhīmānvyājahāra śubhāṃ giram
|
yamaḥ paramadharmajño dakṣiṇāṃ diśamāsthitaḥ||16||

17arjunārjuna paśyāsmāṃllokapālānsamāgatān
|
dṛṣṭiṃ te vitarāmodya bhavānarhati darśanam||17||

18pūrvarṣiramitātmā tvaṃ naro nāma mahābalaḥ
|
niyogādbrahmaṇastāta martyatāṃ samupāgataḥ||18||

19tvayā ca vasusaṃbhūto mahāvīryaḥ pitāmahaḥ
|
bhīṣmaḥ paramadharmātmā saṃsādhyaśca raṇe'nagha||19||

20kṣatraṃ cāgnisamasparśaṃ bhāradvājena rakṣitam
|
dānavāśca mahāvīryā ye manuṣyatvamāgatāḥ||20||

21nivātakavacāñcaiva dānavāḥ kurunandana
|
piturmamāṃśo devasya sarvalokapratāpinaḥ||21||

22karṇaśca sumahāvīryastvayā vadhyo dhanañjaya
|
aṃśāśca kṣitisamprāptā devadānavarakṣasām||22||

23tvayā nipātitā yuddhe svakarmaphalanirjitām
|
gatiṃ prāpsyanti kaunteya yathā svāmarikarṣaṇa||23||

24akṣayā tava kīrtiśca loke sthāsyati phālguna
|
tvayā sākṣānmahādevastoṣito hi mahāmṛdhe||24||

25laghvī vasumatī cāpi kartavyā viṣṇunā saha
|
gṛhāṇāstraṃ mahābāho daṇḍamaprativāraṇam
|
anenāstreṇa sumahattvaṃ hi karma kariṣyasi||25||

26vaiśaṃpāyana uvāca|
pratijagrāha tatpārtho vidhivatkurunandanaḥ
|
samantraṃ sopacāraṃ ca samokṣavinivartanam||26||

27tato jaladharaśyāmo varuṇo yādasāṃ patiḥ
|
paścimāṃ diśamāsthāya giramuccārayanprabhuḥ||27||

28pārtha kṣatriyamukhyastvaṃ kṣatradharme vyavasthitaḥ
|
paśya māṃ pṛthutāmrākṣa varuṇo'smi jaleśvara||28||

29mayā samudyatānpāśānvāruṇānanivāritān
|
pratigṛhṇīṣva kaunteya sarahasyanivartanam||29||

30ebhistadā mayā vīra saṅgrāme tārakāmaye
|
daiteyānāṃ sahasrāṇi saṃyatāni mahātmanām||30||

31tasmādimānmahāsattva matprasādasamutthitān
|
gṛhāṇa na hi te mucyedantako'pyātatāyinaḥ||31||

32anena tvaṃ yadāstreṇa saṅgrāme vicariṣyasi
|
tadā niḥkṣatriyā bhūmirbhaviṣyati na saṃśayaḥ||32||

33vaiśaṃpāyana uvāca|
tataḥ kailāsanilayo dhanādhyakṣo'bhyabhāṣata
|
datteṣvastreṣu divyeṣu varuṇena yamena ca||33||

34prīto'hamapi te prājña pāṇḍaveya mahābala
|
tvayā saha samāgamya ajitena tathaiva ca||34||

35savyasācinmahābāho pūrvadeva satātana
|
sahāsmābhirbhavāñcchrāntaḥ purākalpeṣu nityaśaḥ||35||

36darśanātte tvidaṃ divyaṃ pradiśāmi nararṣabha
|
amanuṣyānmahābāho durjayānapi jeṣyasi||36||

37mattaścaiva bhavānāśu gṛhṇātvastramanuttamam
|
anena tvamanīkāni dhārtarāṣṭrasya dhakṣyasi||37||

38tadidaṃ pratigṛhṇīṣva antardhānaṃ priyaṃ mama
|
ojastejodyutikaraṃ prasvāpanamarātinut||38||

39mahātmanā śaṅkareṇa tripuraṃ nihataṃ yadā
|
tadaitadastraṃ nirmuktaṃ yena dagdhā mahāsurāḥ||39||

40tvadarthamudyataṃ cedaṃ mayā satyaparākrama
|
tvamarho dhāraṇe cāsya merupratimagaurava||40||

41tato'rjuno mahābāhurvidhivatkurunandanaḥ
|
kauberamadhijagrāha divyamastraṃ mahābalaḥ||41||

42tato'bravīddevarājaḥ pārthamakliṣṭakāriṇam
|
sāntvayañcchlakṣṇayā vācā meghadundubhiniḥsvanaḥ||42||

43kuntīmātarmahābāho tvamīśānaḥ purātanaḥ
|
parāṃ siddhimanuprāptaḥ sākṣāddevagatiṃ gataḥ||43||

44devakāryaṃ tu sumahattvayā kāryamarindama
|
āroḍhavyastvayā svargaḥ sajjībhava mahādyute||44||

45ratho mātalisaṃyukta āgantā tvatkṛte mahīm
|
tatra te'haṃ pradāsyāmi divyānyastrāṇi kaurava||45||

46tāndṛṣṭvā lokapālāṃstu sametāngirimūrdhani
|
jagāma vismayaṃ dhīmānkuntīputro dhanañjayaḥ||46||

47tato'rjuno mahātejā lokapālānsamāgatān
|
pūjayāmāsa vidhivadvāgbhiradbhiḥ phalairapi||47||

48tataḥ pratiyayurdevāḥ pratimānya dhanañjayam
|
yathāgatena vibudhāḥ sarve kāmamanojavāḥ||48||

49tato'rjuno mudaṃ lebhe labdhāstraḥ puruṣarṣabhaḥ
|
kṛtārthamatha cātmānaṃ sa mene pūrṇamānasam||49||
