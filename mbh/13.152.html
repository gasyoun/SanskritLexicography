Mahābhārata 13.152 (Anuśāsanaparvan)

1yudhiṣṭhira uvāca|
kāṃ tu brāhmaṇapūjāyāṃ vyuṣṭiṃ dṛṣṭvā janādhipa
|
kaṃ vā karmodayaṃ matvā tānarcasi mahāmate ||1||

2bhīṣma uvāca|
atrāpyudāharantīmamitihāsaṃ purātanam
|
pavanasya ca saṃvādamarjunasya ca bhārata||2||

3sahasrabhujabhṛcchrīmānkāravīryo'bhavatprabhuḥ
|
asya lokasya sarvasya māhiṣmatyāṃ mahābalaḥ||3||

4sa tu ratnākaravatīṃ sadvīpāṃ sāgarambarām
|
śaśāsa pṛthivīṃ sarvāṃ haihayaḥ satyavikramaḥ||4||

5svavittaṃ tena dattaṃ tu dattātreyāya kāraṇe
|
kṣatradharmaṃ puraskṛtya vinayaṃ śrutameva ca ||5||

6ārādhayāmāsa ca taṃ kṛtavīryātmajo munim
|
nyamantrayata santuṣṭo dvijaścainaṃ varaistribhiḥ ||6||

7sa varaiśchanditastena nṛpo vacanamabravīt
|
sahasrabāhurbhūyāṃ vai camūmadhye gṛhe'nyathā ||7||

8mama bāhusahasraṃ tu paśyatāṃ sainikā raṇe
|
vikrameṇa mahīṃ kṛtsnāṃ jayeyaṃ saṃśitavrata ||8||

9tāṃ ca dharmeṇa saṃprāpya pālayeyamatandritaḥ
|
caturthaṃ tu varaṃ yāce tvāmahaṃ dvijasattama ||9||

10taṃ mamānugrahakṛte dātumarhasyanindita
|
anuśāsantu māṃ santo mithyodvṛttaṃ tvadāśrayam ||10||

11ityuktaḥ sa dvijaḥ prāha tathāstviti narādhipam
|
evaṃ samabhavaṃstasya varāste dīptatejasaḥ ||11||

12tataḥ sa rathamāsthāya jvalanārkasamadyutim
|
abravīdvīryasaṃmohātko vāsti sadṛśo mama ||12||

13dhairyairvīryairyaśaḥśauryairvikrameṇaujasāpi vā
|
tadvākyānte cāntarikṣe vāguvācāśarīriṇī ||13||

14na tvaṃ mūḍha vijānīṣe brāhmaṇaṃ kṣatriyādvaram
|
sahito brāhmaṇeneha kṣatriyaḥ śāsti vai prajāḥ||14||

15arjuna uvāca|
kuryāṃ bhūtāni tuṣṭo'haṃ kruddho nāśaṃ tathā'naye
|
karmaṇā manasā vācā na matto'sti varo dvijaḥ||15||

16pūrvo brahmottaro vādo dvitīyaḥ kṣatriyottaraḥ
|
tvayoktau hetuyuktau tau viśeṣastatra dṛśyate ||16||

17brāhmaṇāḥ saṃśritāḥ kṣatraṃ na kṣatraṃ brāhmaṇāśritam
|
śritā brahmopadhā viprāḥ khādanti kṣatriyān bhuvi ||17||

18kṣatriyeṣvāśrito dharmaḥ prajānāṃ paripālanam
|
kṣatrādvṛttirbrāhmaṇānāṃ taiḥ kathaṃ brāhmaṇo varaḥ ||18||

19sarvabhūtapradhānāṃstānbhaikṣavṛttīnahaṃ sadā
|
ātmasaṃbhāvitānviprānsthāpayāmyātmano vaśe ||19||

20kathitaṃ tvanayā'satyaṃ gāyatryā kanyayā divi
|
vijeṣyāmyavaśānsarvānbrāhmaṇāṃścarmavāsasaḥ ||20||

21na ca māṃ cyāvayedrāṣṭrāntriṣu lokeṣu kaścana
|
devo vā mānuṣo vā'pi tasmājjyeṣṭho dvijādaham ||21||

22adya brahmottaraṃ lokaṃ kariṣye kṣatriyottaram
|
na hi me saṃyuge kaścitsoḍhumutsahate balam ||22||

23arjunasya vacaḥ śrutvā vitrastā'bhūnniśācarī
|
athainamantarikṣasthastato vāyurabhāṣata ||23||

24tyajainaṃ kaluṣaṃ bhāvaṃ brāhmaṇebhyo namaskuru
|
eteṣāṃ kurvataḥ pāpaṃ rāṣṭrakṣobho bhaviṣyati ||24||

25athavā tvāṃ mahīpāla śamayiṣyanti vai dvijāḥ
|
nirasiṣyanti te rāṣṭrāddhatotsāhā mahābalāḥ||25||

26taṃ rājā kastvamityāha tatastaṃ prāha mārutaḥ
|
vāyurvai devadūtosmi hitaṃ tvāṃ prabravīmyaham ||26||

27arjuna uvāca|
aho tvayā'yaṃ vipreṣu bhaktirāgaḥ pradarśitaḥ
|
yādṛśaṃ pṛthivībhūtaṃ tādṛśaṃ brūhi me dvijam ||27||

28vāyorvā sadṛśaṃ kiñcidbrūhi tvaṃ brāhmaṇottamam
|
apāṃ vai sadṛśaṃ vahneḥ sūryasya nabhasopi vā ||28||
