Mahābhārata 3.17 (Āraṇyakaparvan)

1vāsudeva uvāca|
evamuktvā raukmiṇeyo yādavānbharatarṣabha
|
daṃśitairharibhiryuktaṃ rathamāsthāya kāñcanam||1||

2ucchritya makaraṃ ketuṃ vyāttānanamivāntakam
|
utpatadbhirivākāśaṃ tairhayairanvayātparān||2||

3vikṣipannādayaṃścāpi dhanuḥśreṣṭhaṃ mahābalaiḥ
|
tūṇakhaḍgadharaḥ śūro baddhagodhāṅgulitravān||3||

4sa vidyucchuritaṃ cāpaṃ viharanvai talāttalam
|
mohayāmāsa daiteyānsarvānsaubhanivāsinaḥ||4||

5tasya vikṣipataścāpaṃ saṃdadhānasya cāsakṛt
|
nāntaraṃ dadṛśe kaścinnighnataḥ śātravānraṇe||5||

6mukhasya varṇo na vikalpate'sya celuśca gātrāṇi na cāpi tasya
|
siṃhonnataṃ cāpyabhigarjato'sya śuśrāva loko'dbhutavīryamagryam||6||

7jalecaraḥ kāñcanayaṣṭisaṃstho vyāttānanaḥ sarvatimipramāthī
|
vitrāsayanrājati vāhamukhye śālvasya senāpramukhe dhvajāgryaḥ||7||

8tatastūrṇaṃ viniṣpatya pradyumnaḥ śatrukarṣaṇaḥ
|
śālvamevābhidudrāva vidhitsuḥ kalahaṃ nṛpa||8||

9abhiyānaṃ tu vīreṇa pradyumnena mahāraṇe
|
nāmarṣayata saṅkruddhaḥ śālvaḥ kurukulodvaha||9||

10sa roṣamadamatto vai kāmagādavaruhya ca
|
pradyumnaṃ yodhayāmāsa śālvaḥ parapurañjayaḥ||10||

11tayoḥ sutumulaṃ yuddhaṃ śālvavṛṣṇipravīrayoḥ
|
sametā dadṛśurlokā balivāsavayoriva||11||

12tasya māyāmayo vīra ratho hemapariṣkṛtaḥ
|
sapatākaḥ sadhvajaśca sānukarṣaḥ satūṇavān||12||

13sa taṃ rathavaraṃ śrīmānsamāruhya kila prabho
|
mumoca bāṇānkauravya pradyumnāya mahābalaḥ||13||

14tato bāṇamayaṃ varṣaṃ vyasṛjattarasā raṇe
|
pradyumno bhujavegena śālvaṃ saṃmohayanniva||14||

15sa tairabhihataḥ saṅkhye nāmarṣayata saubharāṭ
|
śarāndīptāgnisaṅkāśānmumoca tanaye mama||15||

16tānāpatata bāṇaughānsa ciccheda mahābalaḥ
|
tataścānyāñccharāndīptānpracikṣepa sute mama||16||

17sa śālvabāṇai rājendra viddho rukmiṇinandanaḥ
|
mumoca bāṇaṃ tvarito marmabhedinamāhave||17||

18tasya varma vibhidyāśu sa bāṇo matsuteritaḥ
|
vivyādha hṛdayaṃ patrī sa mumoha papāta ca||18||

19tasminnipatite vīre śālvarāje vicetasi
|
samprādravandānavendrā dārayanto vasundharām||19||

20hāhākṛtamabhūtsainyaṃ śālvasya pṛthivīpate
|
naṣṭasañjñe nipatite tadā saubhapatau nṛpe||20||

21tata utthāya kauravya pratilabhya ca cetanām
|
mumoca bāṇānsahasā pradyumnāya mahābalaḥ||21||

22taiḥ sa viddho mahābāhuḥ pradyumnaḥ samare sthitaḥ
|
jatrudeśe bhṛśaṃ vīro vyavāsīdadrathe tadā||22||

23taṃ sa viddhvā mahārāja śālvo rukmiṇinandanam
|
nanāda siṃhanādaṃ vai nādenāpūrayanmahīm||23||

24tato mohaṃ samāpanne tanaye mama bhārata
|
mumoca bāṇāṃstvaritaḥ punaranyāndurāsadān||24||

25sa tairabhihato bāṇairbahubhistena mohitaḥ
|
niśceṣṭaḥ kauravaśreṣṭha pradyumno'bhūdraṇājire||25||
