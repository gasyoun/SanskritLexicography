Mahābhārata 3.215 (Āraṇyakaparvan)

1mārkaṇḍeya uvāca|
gurū nivedya viprāya tau mātāpitarāvubhau
|
punareva sa dharmātmā vyādho brāhmaṇamabravīt||1||

2pravṛttacakṣurjāto'smi sampaśya tapaso balam
|
yadarthamukto'si tayā gaccha tvaṃ mithilāmiti||2||

3patiśuśrūṣaparayā dāntayā satyaśīlayā
|
mithilāyāṃ vasedvyādhaḥ sa te dharmān pravakṣyati||3||

4brāhmaṇa uvāca|
pativratāyāḥ satyāyāḥ śīlāḍhyāyā yatavrata
|
saṃsmṛtya vākyaṃ dharmajña guṇavānasi me mataḥ||4||

5vyādha uvāca|
yat tadā tvaṃ dvijaśreṣṭha tayokto māṃ prati prabho
|
dṛṣṭameva tayā samyagekapatnyā na saṃśayaḥ||5||

6tvadanugrahabuddhyā tu vipraitaddarśitaṃ mayā
|
vākyaṃ ca śṛṇu me tāta yat te vakṣye hitaṃ dvija||6||

7tvayā vinikṛtā mātā pitā ca dvijasattama
|
anisṛṣṭo'si niṣkrānto gṛhāt tābhyāmanindita||7||

8vedoccāraṇakāryārthamayuktaṃ tat tvayā kṛtam
|
tava śokena vṛddhau tāvandhībhūtau tapasvinau||8||

9tau prasādayituṃ gaccha mā tvāṃ dharmo'tyagādayam
|
tapasvī tvaṃ mahātmā ca dharme ca nirataḥ sadā||9||

10sarvametadapārthaṃ te kṣipraṃ tau samprasādaya
|
śraddadhasva mama brahman nānyathā kartumarhasi
|
gamyatāmadya viprarṣe śreyaste kathayāmyaham||10||

11brāhmaṇa uvāca|
yadetaduktaṃ bhavatā sarvaṃ satyamasaṃśayam
|
prīto'smi tava bhadraṃ te dharmācāraguṇānvita||11||

12vyādha uvāca|
daivatapratimo hi tvaṃ yastvaṃ dharmamanuvrataḥ
|
purāṇaṃ śāśvataṃ divyaṃ duṣprāpyamakṛtātmabhiḥ||12||

13mātāpitroḥ sakāśaṃ hi gatvā tvaṃ dvijasattama
|
atandritaḥ kuru kṣipraṃ mātāpitrorhi pūjanam
|
ataḥ paramahaṃ dharmaṃ nānyaṃ paśyāmi kañcana||13||

14brāhmaṇa uvāca|
ihāhamāgato diṣṭyā diṣṭyā me saṅgataṃ tvayā
|
īdṛśā durlabhā loke narā dharmapradarśakāḥ||14||

15eko narasahasreṣu dharmavidvidyate na vā
|
prīto'smi tava satyena bhadraṃ te puruṣarṣabha||15||

16patamāno'dya narake bhavatā'smi samuddhṛtaḥ
|
bhavitavyamathaicaṃ ca yaddṛṣṭo'si mayā'nagha||16||

17rājā yayātirdauhitraiḥ patitastārito yathā
|
sadbhiḥ puruṣaśārdūla tathā'haṃ bhavatā dvijaḥ||17||

18mātāpitṛbhyāṃ śuśrūṣāṃ kariṣye vacanāt tava
|
nākṛtātmā vedayati dharmādharmaviniścayam||18||

19durjñeyaḥ śāśvato dharmaḥ śūdrayonau hi vartate
|
na tvāṃ śūdramahaṃ manye bhavitavyaṃ hi kāraṇam||19||

20yena karmaviśeṣeṇa prāpteyaṃ śūdratā tvayā
|
etadicchāmi vijñātuṃ tattvena hi mahāmate
|
kāmayā brūhi me sarvaṃ satyena prayatātmanā||20||

21vyādha uvāca|
anatikramaṇīyā vai brāhmaṇā me dvijottama
|
śṛṇu sarvamidaṃ vṛttaṃ pūrvadehe mamānagha||21||

22ahaṃ hi brāhmaṇaḥ pūrvamāsaṃ dvijavarātmajaḥ
|
vedādhyāyī sukuśalo vedāṅgānāṃ ca pāragaḥ||22||

23ātmadoṣakṛtairbrahmannavasthāmāptavānimām
|
kaścidrājā mama sakhā dhanurvedaparāyaṇaḥ||23||

24saṃsargāddhanuṣi śreṣṭhastato'hamabhavaṃ dvija
|
etasminneva kāle tu mṛgayāṃ nirgato nṛpaḥ||24||

25sahito yodhamukhyaiśca mantribhiśca susaṃvṛtaḥ
|
tato'bhyahan mṛgāṃstatra subahūnāśramaṃ prati||25||

26atha kṣiptaḥ śaro ghoro mayā'pi dvijasattama
|
tāḍitaśca ṛṣistena śareṇānataparvaṇā||26||

27bhūmau nipatito brahmannuvāca pratinādayan
|
nāparādhyāmyahaṃ kiñcit kena pāpamidaṃ kṛtam||27||

28manvānastaṃ mṛgaṃ cāhaṃ samprāptaḥ sahasā prabho
|
apaśyantamṛṣiṃ viddhaṃ śareṇānataparvaṇā||28||

29akāryakāraṇāccāpi bhṛśaṃ me vyathitaṃ manaḥ
|
tamugratapasaṃ vipraṃ niṣṭavantaṃ mahītale||29||

30ajānatā kṛtamidaṃ mayetyahamathābruvam
|
kṣantumarhasi me sarvamiti cokto mayā muniḥ||30||

31tataḥ pratyabravīdvākyamṛṣirmāṃ krodhamūrcchitaḥ
|
vyādhastvaṃ bhavitā krūra śūdrayonāviti dvija||31||
