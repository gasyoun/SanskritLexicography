Mahābhārata 15.10 (Āśramavāsikaparvan)

1vaiśampāyana uvāca|
evamuktāstu te tena paurajānapadā janāḥ
|
vṛddhena rājñā kauravya naṣṭasaṃjñā ivābhavan||1||

2tūṣṇīmbhūtāṃstatastāṃstu bāṣpakaṇṭhānmahīpatiḥ
|
dhṛtarāṣṭro mahīpālaḥ punarevābhyabhāṣata||2||

3vṛddhaṃ ca hataputraṃ ca dharmapatnyā sahānayā
|
vilapantaṃ bahuvidhaṃ kṛpaṇaṃ caiva sattamāḥ||3||

4pitrā svayamanujñātaṃ kṛṣṇadvaipāyanena vai
|
vanavāsāya dharmajñā dharmajñena nṛpeṇa ha||4||

5so'haṃ punaḥ punaścaiva śirasāvanato'naghāḥ
|
gāndhāryā sahitaṃ tanmāṃ samanujñātumarhatha||5||

6vaiśampāyana uvāca|
tacchrutvā kururājasya vākyāni karuṇāni te
|
ruruduḥ sarvaśo rājansametāḥ kurujāṅgalāḥ||6||

7uttarīyaiḥ karaiścāpi sañcchādya vadanāni te
|
ruruduḥ śokasantaptā muhūrtaṃ pitṛmātṛvat||7||

8hṛdayaiḥ śūnyabhūtaiste dhṛtarāṣṭrapravāsajam
|
duḥkhaṃ sandhārayanto hi naṣṭasaṃjñā ivābhavan||8||

9te vinīya tamāyāsaṃ dhṛtarāṣṭraviyogajam
|
śanaiḥ śanaistadānyonyamabruvansammatānyuta||9||

10tataḥ sandhāya te sarve vākyānyatha samāsataḥ
|
ekasminbrāhmaṇe rājanniveśyocurnarādhipam||10||

11tataḥ svācaraṇo vipraḥ sammato'rthaviśāradaḥ
|
sāmbākhyo bahvṛco rājanvaktuṃ samupacakrame||11||

12anumānya mahārājaṃ tatsadaḥ samprasādya ca
|
vipraḥ pragalbho medhāvī sa rājānamuvāca ha||12||

13rājanvākyaṃ janasyāsya mayi sarvaṃ samarpitam
|
vakṣyāmi tadahaṃ vīra tajjuṣasva narādhipa||13||

14yathā vadasi rājendra sarvametattathā vibho
|
nātra mithyā vacaḥ kiñcitsuhṛttvaṃ naḥ parasparam||14||

15na jātvasya ca vaṃśasya rājñāṃ kaścitkadācana
|
rājā''sīdyaḥ prajāpālaḥ prajānāmapriyo'bhavat||15||

16pitṛvadbhrātṛvaccaiva bhavantaḥ pālayanti naḥ
|
na ca duryodhanaḥ kiñcidayuktaṃ kṛtavānnṛpaḥ||16||

17yathā bravīti dharmātmā muniḥ satyavatīsutaḥ
|
tathā kuru mahārāja sa hi naḥ paramo guruḥ||17||

18tyaktā vayaṃ tu bhavatā duḥkhaśokaparāyaṇāḥ
|
bhaviṣyāmaściraṃ rājan bhavadguṇaśatairyutāḥ||18||

19yathā śāntanunā guptā rājñā citrāṅgadena ca
|
bhīṣmavīryopagūḍhena pitrā tava ca pārthiva||19||

20bhavadudvīkṣaṇāccaiva pāṇḍunā pṛthivīkṣitā
|
tathā duryodhanenāpi rājñā suparipālatāḥ||20||

21na svalpamapi putraste vyalīkaṃ kṛtavānnṛpa
|
pitarīva suviśvastāstasminnapi narādhipe||21||

22vayamāsma yathā samyak bhavato viditaṃ tathā
|
tathā varṣasahasrāṇi kuntīputreṇa dhīmatā||22||

23pālyamānā dhṛtimatā sukhaṃ vindāmahe nṛpa
|
rājarṣīṇāṃ purāṇānāṃ bhavatāṃ puṇyakarmaṇām||23||

24kurusaṃvaraṇādīnāṃ bharatasya ca dhīmataḥ
|
vṛttaṃ samanuyātyeṣa dharmātmā bhūridakṣiṇaḥ||24||

25nātra vācyaṃ mahārāja susūkṣmamapi vidyate
|
uṣitāḥ sma sukhaṃ nityaṃ bhavatā paripālitāḥ||25||

26susūkṣmaṃ ca vyalīkaṃ te saputrasya na vidyate
|
yattu jñātivimarde'sminnāttha duryodhanaṃ prati||26||

27bhavantamanuneṣyāmi tatrāpi kurunandana
|
brāhmaṇa uvāca|
na tadduryodhanakṛtaṃ na ca tadbhavatā kṛtam||27||

28na karṇasaubalābhyāṃ ca kuravo yat kṣayaṃ gatāḥ
|
daivaṃ tattu vijānīmo yanna śakyaṃ pravādhitum||28||

29daivaṃ puruṣakāreṇa na śakyamapi bādhitum
|
akṣauhiṇyo mahārāja daśāṣṭau ca samāgatāḥ||29||

30aṣṭādaśāhena hatāḥ kurubhiryodhapuṅgavaiḥ
|
bhīṣmadroṇakṛpādyaiśca karṇena ca mahātmanā||30||

31yuyudhānena vīreṇa dhṛṣṭadyumnena caiva ha
|
caturbhiḥ pāṇḍuputraiśca bhīmārjunayamaistathā||31||

32na ca kṣayo'yaṃ nṛpate ṛte daivabalādabhūt
|
avaśyameva saṅgrāme kṣatriyeṇa viśeṣataḥ||32||

33kartavyaṃ nidhanaṃ kāle martavyaṃ kṣatrabandhunā
|
tairiyaṃ puruṣavyāghrairvidyā bāhubalānvitaiḥ||33||

34pṛthivī nihatā sarvā sahayā sarathadvipā
|
na sa rājñāṃ vadhe sūnuḥ kāraṇaṃ te mahātmanām||34||

35na bhavānna ca te bhṛtyā na karṇo na sa saubalaḥ
|
yadviśastāḥ kuruśreṣṭha rājānaśca sahasraśaḥ||35||

36sarvaṃ daivakṛtaṃ viddhi ko'tra kiṃ vaktumarhati
|
gururmato bhavānasya kṛtsnasya jagataḥ prabhuḥ||36||

37dharmātmānamatastubhyamanujānīmahe sutam
|
labhatāṃ vīralokaṃ sa sasahāyo narādhipaḥ||37||

38dvijāgryaiḥ samanujñātastridive modatāṃ sukham
|
prāpsyate ca bhavānpuṇyaṃ dharme ca paramāṃ sthitim||38||

39vedadharmaṃ ca kṛtsnena samyak tvaṃ bhava suvrataḥ
|
dṛṣṭipradānamapi te pāṇḍavānprati no vṛthā||39||

40samarthāstridivasyāpi pālane kiṃ punaḥ kṣiteḥ
|
anuvartsyanti vā dhīmansameṣu viṣameṣu ca||40||

41prajāḥ kurukulaśreṣṭha pāṇḍavān śīlabhūṣaṇān
|
brahmadeyāgrahārāṃśca pāribarhāṃśca pārthivaḥ||41||

42pūrvarājābhipannāṃśca pālayatyeva pāṇḍavaḥ
|
dīrghadarśī mṛdurdāntaḥ sadā vaiśravaṇo yathā||42||

43akṣudrasacivaścāyaṃ kuntīputro mahāmanāḥ
|
atha mitre dayāvāṃśca śuciśca bharatarṣabhaḥ||43||

44ṛju paśyati medhāvī putravatpālitaḥ sadā
|
vipriyaṃ ca janasyāsya saṃsargāddharmajasya vai||44||

45na kariṣyanti rājarṣe tathā bhīmārjunādayaḥ
|
mandā mṛduṣu kauravya tīkṣṇeṣvāśīviṣopamāḥ||45||

46vīryavanto mahātmānaḥ paurāṇāṃ ca hite ratāḥ
|
na kuntī na ca pāñcālī na colūpī na sātvatī||46||

47asmin jane kariṣyanti pratikūlāni karhicit
|
bhavatkṛtamimaṃ snehaṃ yudhiṣṭhiravivardhitam||47||

48na pṛṣṭhataḥ kariṣyanti paurā jānapadā janāḥ
|
adharmiṣṭhānapi sataḥ kuntīputrā mahārathāḥ||48||

49mānavānpālayiṣyanti bhūtvā dharmaparāyaṇāḥ
|
sa rājanmānasaṃ duḥkhamapanīya yudhiṣṭhirāt||49||

50kuru kāryāṇi dharmyāṇi namaste puruṣarṣabha
|
vaiśampāyana uvāca|
tasya tadvacanaṃ dharmyamanumānya guṇottaram||50||

51sādhu sādhviti sarvaḥ sa janaḥ pratigṛhītavān
|
dhṛtarāṣṭraśca tadvākyamabhipūjya punaḥ punaḥ||51||

52visarjayāmāsa tadā prakṛtīstu śanaiḥ śanaiḥ
|
sa taiḥ sampūjito rājā śivenāvekṣitastathā||52||

53prāñjaliḥ pūjayāmāsa taṃ janaṃ bharatarṣabha
|
tato viveśa bhavanaṃ gāndhāryā sahito nijam
|
vyuṣṭāyāṃ caiva śarvaryāṃ yaccakāra nibodha tat||53||
