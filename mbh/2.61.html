Mahābhārata 2.61 (Sabhāparvan)

1yudhiṣṭhira uvāca|
matta kaitakenaiva yajjito'smi durodare
|
śakune hanta dīvyāmo glahamānāḥ parasparam||1||

2santi niṣkasahasrasya bhāṇḍinyo bharitāḥ śubhāḥ
|
kośo hiraṇyamakṣayyaṃ jātarūpamanekaśaḥ
|
etadrājanmama dhanaṃ tena dīvyāmyahaṃ tvayā||2||

3vaiśampāyana uvāca|
kauravāṇāṃ kulakaraṃ jyeṣṭhaṃ pāṇḍavamacyutam
|
ityuktaḥ śakuniḥ prāha jitamityeva taṃ nṛpam||3||

4yudhiṣṭhira uvāca|
ayaṃ sahasrasamito vaiyāghraḥ supratiṣṭhitaḥ
|
sucakropaskaraḥ śrīmānkiṅkiṇījālamaṇḍitaḥ||4||

5saṃhrādano rājaratho ya ihāsmānupāvahat
|
jaitro rathavaraḥ puṇyo meghasāgaraniḥsvanaḥ||5||

6aṣṭau yaṃ kuraracchāyāḥ sadaśvā rāṣṭrasaṃmatāḥ
|
vahanti naiṣāṃ mucyeta padādbhūmimupaspṛśan
|
etadrājandhanaṃ mahyaṃ tena dīvyāmyahaṃ tvayā||6||

7vaiśampāyana uvāca|
evaṃ śrutvā vyavasito nikṛtiṃ samupāśritaḥ
|
jitamityeva śakuniryudhiṣṭhiramabhāṣata||7||

8yudhiṣṭhira uvāca|
śataṃ dāsīsahasrāṇi taruṇyo hemabhadrikāḥ
|
kambukeyūradhāriṇyo niṣkakaṇṭhyaḥ svalaṅkṛtāḥ||8||

9mahārhamālyābharaṇāḥ suvastrāścandanokṣitāḥ
|
maṇīnhema ca bibhratyaścatuḥṣaṣṭiviśāradāḥ||9||

10anusevāṃ carantīmāḥ kuśalā nṛtyasāmasu
|
snātakānāmamātyānāṃ rājñāṃ ca mama śāsanāt
|
etadrājanmama dhanaṃ tena dīvyāmyahaṃ tvayā||10||

11vaiśampāyana uvāca|
etacchutvā vyavasito nikṛtiṃ samupāśritaḥ
|
jitamityeva śakuniryudhiṣṭhiramabhāṣata||11||

12yudhiṣṭhira uvāca|
etāvanti ca dāsānāṃ sahasrāṇyuta santi me
|
pradakṣiṇānulomāśca prāvāravasanāḥ sadā||12||

13prājñā medhāvino dāntā yuvāno mṛṣṭakuṇḍalāḥ
|
pātrīhastā divārātramatithīnbhojayantyuta
|
etadrājanmama dhanaṃ tena dīvyāmyahaṃ tvayā||13||

14vaiśampāyana uvāca|
etacchrutvā vyavasito nikṛtiṃ samupāśritaḥ
|
jitamityeva śakuniryudhiṣṭhirabhāṣata||14||

15yudhiṣṭhira uvāca|
sahasrasaṅkhyā nagā me mattāstiṣṭhanti saubala
|
hemakakṣāḥ kṛtāpīḍāḥ padmino hemamālinaḥ||15||

16sudāntā rājavahanāḥ sarvaśabdakṣamā yudhi
|
īṣādantā mahākāyāḥ sarve cāṣṭakareṇavaḥ||16||

17sarve ca purabhettāro navameghanibhā gajāḥ
|
etadrājanmama dhanaṃ tena dīvyāmyahaṃ tvayā||17||

18vaiśampāyana uvāca|
ityevaṃvādinaṃ pārthaṃ prahasanniva saubalaḥ
|
jitamityeva śakuniryudhiṣṭhiramabhāṣata||18||

19yudhiṣṭhira uvāca|
rathāstāvanta eveme hemadaṇḍāḥ patākinaḥ
|
hayairvinītaiḥ sampannā rathibhiścitrayodhibhiḥ||19||

20ekaiko hyatra labhate sahasraparamāṃ bhṛtim
|
yudhyato'yudhyato vāpi vetanaṃ māsakālikam
|
etadrājanmama dhanaṃ tena dīvyāmyahaṃ tvayā||20||

21vaiśampāyana uvāca|
ityevamukte vacane kṛtavairo durātmavān
|
jitamityeva śakuniryudhiṣṭhiramabhāṣata||21||

22yudhiṣṭhira uvāca|
aśvāṃstittirikalmāṣāngāndharvānhemamālinaḥ
|
dadau citrarathastuṣṭo yāṃstāngāṇḍīvadhanvane||22||

23yuddhe jitaḥ parābhūtaḥ prītipūrvamarindamaḥ
|
etadrājanmama dhanaṃ tena dīvyāmyahaṃ tvayā||23||

24vaiśampāyana uvāca|
etacchrutvā vyavasito nikṛtiṃ samupāśritaḥ
|
jitamityeva śakuniryudhiṣṭhiramabhāṣata||24||

25yudhiṣṭhira uvāca|
rathānāṃ śakaṭānāṃ ca śreṣṭhānāṃ cāyutāni me
|
yuktānyeva hi tiṣṭhanti vāhairuccāvacaistathā||25||

26evaṃ varṇasya varṇasya samuccīya sahasraśaḥ
|
yathā samuditā vīrāḥ sarve vīraparākramāḥ||26||

27kṣīraṃ pibantastiṣṭhanti bhuñjānāḥ śālitaṇḍulān
|
ṣaṣṭistāni sahasrāṇi sarve vipulavakṣasaḥ
|
etadrājanmama dhanaṃ tena dīvyāmyahaṃ tvayā||27||

28vaiśampāyana uvāca|
etacchratvā vyavasito nikṛtiṃ samupāśritaḥ
|
jitamityeva śakuniryudhiṣṭhiramabhāṣata||28||

29yudhiṣṭhira uvāca|
tāmralohaiḥ parivṛtā nidhayo ye catuḥ śatāḥ
|
pañcadrauṇika ekaikaḥ suvarṇasyāhatasya vai||29||

30jātarūpasya mukhyasya anargheyasya bhārata
|
etadrājanmama dhanaṃ tena dīvyāmyahaṃ tvayā||30||

31vaiśampāyana uvāca|
etacchrutvā vyavasito nikṛtiṃ samupāśritaḥ
|
jitamityeva śakuniryudhiṣṭhiramabhāṣata||31||
