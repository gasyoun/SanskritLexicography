Mahābhārata 5.125 (Udyogaparvan)

1vaiśampāyana uvāca|
tataḥ śāntanavo bhīṣmo duryodhanamamarṣaṇam
|
keśavasya vacaḥ śrutvā provāca bharatarṣama ||1||

2kṛṣṇena vākyamukto'si suhṛdāṃ śamamicchatā
|
anvapadyasva tattāta mā manyuvaśamanvagāḥ ||2||

3akṛtvā vacanaṃ tāta keśavasya mahātmanaḥ
|
śreyo na jātu na sukhaṃ na kalyāṇamavāpsyasi ||3||

4dharmyamarthyaṃ mahābāhurāha tvāṃ tāta keśavaḥ
|
tadarthamabhipadyasva mā rājannīnaśaḥ prajāḥ ||4||

5jvalitāṃ tvamimāṃ lakṣmīṃ bhāratīṃ sarvarājasu
|
jīvato dhṛtarāṣṭrasya daurātmyādbhraṃśayiṣyasi ||5||

6ātmānaṃ ca sahāmātyaṃ saputrabhrātṛbāndhavam
|
ahamityanayā buddhyā jīvitādbhraṃśayiṣyasi ||6||

7atikrāman keśavasya tathyaṃ vacanamarthavat
|
pituśca bharataśreṣṭha vidurasya ca dhīmataḥ ||7||

8mā kulaghnaḥ kupuruṣo durmatiḥ kāpathaṃ gamaḥ
|
mātaraṃ pitaraṃ caiṣa mā majjīḥ śokasāgare ||8||

9atha droṇo'bravīttatra duryodhanāmadaṃ vacaḥ
|
amarṣavaśamāpannaṃ niḥśvasantaṃ punaḥpunaḥ ||9||

10dharmārthayuktaṃ vacanamāha tvāṃ tāta keśavaḥ
|
tathā bhīṣmaḥ śāntanavastajjuṣasva narādhipa ||10||

11prājñau medhāvinau dāntāvarthakāmau bahuśrutau
|
āhatustvāṃ hitaṃ vākyaṃ tajjuṣasva narādhipa ||11||

12anutiṣṭha mahāprājña kṛṣṇabhīṣmau yadūcatuḥ
|
mādhavaṃ buddhimohena mā'vamaṃsthāḥ paraṃtapa ||12||

13ye tvāṃ protsāhayantyete naite kṛtyāya kārhicit
|
vairaṃ pareṣāṃ grīvāyāṃ pratimokṣyanti saṃyuge ||13||

14mā jīghanaḥ prajāḥ sarvāḥ putrān bhrātṝṃstathaiva ca
|
vāsudevārjunau yatra viddhyajeyānalaṃ hi tān ||14||

15etaccaiva mataṃ satyaṃ suhṛdoḥ kṛṣṇabhīṣmayoḥ
|
yadi nādāsyase tāta paścāttapsyasi bhārata ||15||

16yathoktaṃ jāmadagnyena bhūyāneṣa tato'rjunaḥ
|
kṛṣṇo hi devakīputro devairapi suduḥsahaḥ
|
kiṃ te sukhapriyeṇeha proktena bharatarṣabha ||16||

17etatte sarvamākhyātaṃ yathecchasi tathā kuru
|
na hi tvāmutsahe vaktuṃ bhūyo bharatasattama ||17||

18vaiśampāyana uvāca|
tasminvākyāntare vākyaṃ kṣattāpi viduro'bravīt
|
duryodhanamabhiprekṣya dhārtarāṣṭramamarṣaṇam ||18||

19duryodhana na śocāmi tvāmahaṃ bharatarṣabha
|
imau tu vṛddhī śocāmi gāndhārīṃ pitaraṃ ca te ||19||

20yāvānarthau cariṣyete tvayā nāthena durhṛdā
|
hatamitrau hatāmātyau lūnapakṣāvivāṇḍajau ||20||

21bhikṣukau vicariṣyeta śocantau pṛthivīmimām
|
kulaghnamīdṛśaṃ pāpaṃ janayitvā kupūruṣam ||21||

22atha duryodhanaṃ rājā dhṛtarāṣṭro'bhyabhāṣata
|
āsīnaṃ bhrātṛbhiḥ sārdhaṃ rājabhiḥ parivāritam ||22||

23duryodhana nibodhedaṃ śauriṇoktaṃ mahātmanā
|
ādatsva śivamatyantaṃ yogakṣemavadavyayam ||23||

24anena hi sahāyena kṛṣṇenākliṣṭakarmaṇā
|
iṣṭān sarvānabhiprāyān prāpsyāmaḥ sarvarājasu ||24||

25susaṃhataḥ keśavena tāta gaccha yudhiṣṭhiram
|
cara svastyayanaṃ kṛtsnaṃ bharatānāmanāmayam ||25||

26vāsudevena tīrthena tāta gacchasva saṃśamam
|
kālaprāptamidaṃ manye mā tvaṃ duryodhanātigāḥ ||26||

27śamaṃ cedyācamānaṃ tvaṃ pratyākhyāsyasi keśavam
|
tvadarthamabhijalpantaṃ na tavāstyaparābhavaḥ ||27||
