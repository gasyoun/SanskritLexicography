Mahābhārata 5.77 (Udyogaparvan)

1śrībhagavānuvāca
|
bhāvaṃ jijñāsamāno'haṃ praṇayādidamabruvam
|
ja cākṣepānna pāṇḍityānna krodhānna vivakṣayā ||1||

2vedāhaṃ tava māhātmyamuta te veda yadbalam
|
uta te veda karmāṇi na tvāṃ paribhavāmyaham ||2||

3yathā cātmani kalyāṇaṃ sambhāvayasi pāṇḍava
|
sahastraguṇamapyetattvayi sambhāvayāmyaham ||3||

4yādṛśe ca kule janma sarvarājābhipūjite
|
bandhubhiśca suhṛdbhiśca bhīma tvamasi tādṛśaḥ ||4||

5jijñāsanto hi dharmasya sandigdhasya vṛkodara
|
paryāyaṃ nādhyavasyanti devamānuṣayorjanāḥ ||5||

6sa eva heturbhūtvā hi puruṣasyārthasiddhiṣu
|
vināśe'pi sa evāsya sandigdhaṃ karma pauruṣam ||6||

7anyathā paridṛṣṭāni kavibhirdoṣadarśibhiḥ
|
anyathā parivartante vegā iva nabhasvataḥ ||7||

8sumantritaṃ sunītaṃ ca nyāyataścopapāditam
|
kṛtaṃ mānuṣyakaṃ karma daivenāpi virudhyate ||8||

9daivamapyakṛtaṃ karma pauruṣeṇa vihanyate
|
śītamuṣṇaṃ tathā varṣaṃ kṣutpipāse ca bhārata ||9||

10yadanyaddiṣṭabhāvasya puruṣasya svayaṃ kṛtam
|
tasmādanuparodhaśca vidyate tatra lakṣaṇam ||10||

11lokasya nānyato vṛttiḥ pāṇḍavānyatra karmaṇaḥ
|
evaṃbuddhiḥ pravarteta phalaṃ syādubhayānvaye ||11||

12ya evaṃ kṛtabuddhiḥ sa karmasveva pravartate
|
nāsiddhau vyathate tasya na siddhau harṣamaśnute ||12||

13tatreyamanumātrā me bhīmasena vivakṣitā
|
naikāntasiddhirvaktavyā śatrubhiḥ saha saṃyuge ||13||

14nātiprahīṇaraśmiḥ syāttathā bhāvaviparyaye
|
viṣādamarchedglāniṃ vā'pyetamarthaṃ bravīmi te ||14||

15śvobhūte dhṛtarāṣṭrasya samīpaṃ prāpya pāṇḍava
|
yatiṣye praśamaṃ kartuṃ yuṣmadarthamahāpayan ||15||

16śamaṃ cette kariṣyanti tato'nantaṃ yaśo mama
|
bhavatāṃ ca kṛtaḥ kāmasteṣāṃ ca śreya uttamam ||16||

17te cedabhinivekṣyante nābhyupaiṣyanti me vacaḥ
|
kuravo yuddhamevātra ghoraṃ karma bhaviṣyati ||17||

18asminyuddhe bhīmasena tvayi bhāraḥ samāhitaḥ
|
dhūrarjunena dhāryā syādvoḍhavya itaro janaḥ ||18||

19ahaṃ hi yantā bībhatsorbhavitā saṃyuge sati
|
dhanañjayasyaiṣa kāmo na hi yuddhaṃ na kāmaye ||19||

20tasmādāśaṅkamāno'haṃ vṛkodara matiṃ tava
|
gadataḥ klībayā vācā tejaste samadīdipam ||20||
