Mahābhārata 11.14 (Strīparvan)

1vaiśampāyana uvāca|dhṛtarāṣṭrābhyanujñātāstataste kurupuṅgavāḥ
|
abhyayurbhrātaraḥ sarve gāndhārīṃ sahakeśavāḥ||1||

2tato jñātvā hatāmitraṃ yudhiṣṭhiramupāgamat
|
gāndhārī putraśokārtā śaptumaicchadaninditā||2||

3tasyāḥ pāpamabhiprāyaṃ viditvā pāṇḍavānprati
|
ṛṣiḥ satyavatīputraḥ prāgeva samabudhyata||3||

4sa gaṅgāyāmupaspṛśya puṇyagandhi payaḥ śuciḥ
|
taṃ deśamupasampede paramarṣirmanojavaḥ||4||

5divyena cakṣuṣā paśyan manasā tadgatena ca
|
sarvaprāṇabhṛtāṃ bhāvaṃ sa tatra samabudhyata||5||

6sa snuṣāmabravītkāle kalyāṇāni mahātapāḥ
|
śāpakālamavākṣipya śamakālamudīrayan||6||

7na kopaḥ pāṇḍave kāryo gāndhāri śamamāpnuhi
|
rajo nigṛhyatāṃ caiva śṛṇu cedaṃ vaco mama||7||

8uktā'syaṣṭādaśāhāni putreṇa jayamicchatām
|
śivamāśāsva me mātaryudhyamānasya śatrubhiḥ||8||

9sā tathā yācyamānā tvaṃ kāle kāle jayaiṣiṇā
|
uktavatyasi gāndhāri yato dharmastato jayaḥ||9||

10na cā'pyatītāṃ gāndhārivācaṃ te vitathāmaham
|
smarāmi toṣamāṇāyāstathā praṇihitā hyasi||10||

11vigrahe tumule rājñāṃ gatvā pāramasaṃśayam
|
jitaṃ pāṇḍusutairyuddhe nūnaṃ dharmastato'dhikaḥ||11||

12kṣamāśīlā purā bhūtvā sā'dya na kṣamase katham
|
adharmaṃ jahi dharmajñe yato dharmastato jayaḥ||12||

13svaṃ ca dharmaṃ parismṛtya vācaṃ coktāṃ manasvini
|
kopaṃ saṃyaccha gāndhāri maivaṃ bhūḥ satyavādini||13||

14gāndhāryuvāca|bhagavannābhyasūyāmi naitānicchāmi naśyataḥ
|
putraśokena tu balānmano vihvalatīva me||14||

15yathaiva kuntyā kaunteyā rakṣitavyāstathā mayā
|
tathaiva dhṛtarāṣṭreṇa rakṣitavyā yathā tvayā||15||

16duryodhanāparādhena śakuneḥ saubalasya ca
|
karṇaduḥśāsanābhyāṃ ca kṛto'yaṃ kṛrusaṃkṣayaḥ||16||

17nāparādhyati bībhatsurna ca pārtho vṛkodaraḥ
|
nakulaḥ sahadevaśca naiva jātu yudhiṣṭhiraḥ||17||

18yudhyamānā hi kauravyāḥ kṛtamānāḥ parasparam
|
nihatāḥ sahitāścānyaistacca nāstyapriyaṃ mama||18||

19kiṃ tu karmākarodbhīmo vāsudevasya paśyataḥ
|
duryodhanaṃ samāhūya gadāyuddhe mahāmanāḥ||19||

20śikṣayā'bhyadhikaṃ jñātvā carantaṃ bahudhā raṇe
|
adho nābhyāḥ prahṛtavāṃstanme kopamavardhayat||20||

21kathaṃ nu dharmaṃ dharmajñaiḥ samuddiṣṭaṃ mahātmabhiḥ
|
tyajeyurāhave śūrāḥ prāṇahetoḥ kathañcanaḥ||21||
