Mahābhārata 12.152 (Śāntiparvan)

1śaunaka uvāca|
tasmātte'haṃ pravakṣyāmi dharmamāvṛtacetase
|
śrīmānmahābalastuṣṭaḥ svayaṃ dharmamavekṣase ||1||

2purastāddāruṇo bhūtvā sucitratarameva tat
|
anugṛhṇāti bhūtāni svena vṛttena pārthivaḥ ||2||

3kṛtsnaṃ nūnaṃ sa dahati iti loko vyavasyati
|
yatra tvaṃ tādṛśo bhūtvā dharmamevānupaśyasi ||3||

4hitvā tu suciraṃ bhakṣyaṃ bhojyāṃśca tapa āsthitaḥ
|
ityetadabhibhūtānāmadbhutaṃ janamejaya ||4||

5yo'durlabho bhaveddātā kṛpaṇo vā tapodhanaḥ
|
anāścaryaṃ tadityāhurnātidūreṇa vartate ||5||

6etadeva hi kārpaṇyaṃ samagramasamīkṣitam
|
yaccetsamamīkṣayaiva syādbhavettasmiṃstato guṇaḥ ||6||

7yajño dānaṃ dayā vedāḥ satyaṃ ca pṛthivīpate
|
pañcaitāni pavitrāṇi ṣaṣṭhaṃ sucaritaṃ tapaḥ ||7||

8tadeva rājñāṃ paramaṃ pavitraṃ janamejaya
|
tena samyaggṛhītena śreyāṃsaṃ dharmamāpsyasi ||8||

9puṇyadeśābhigamanaṃ pavitraṃ paramaṃ smṛtam
|
atrāpyudāharantīmāṃ gāthāṃ gītāṃ yayātinā ||9||

10yo martyaḥ pratipadyeta āyurjīvitamātmanaḥ
|
yajñamekāntataḥ kṛtvā tatsaṃnyasya tapaścaret ||10||

11puṇyamāhuḥ kurukṣetraṃ kurukṣetrātsarasvatīm
|
sarasvatyāśca tīrthāni tīrthebhyaśca pṛthūdakam ||11||

12yatrāvagāhya pītvā ca nainaṃ śvomaraṇaṃ tapet
|
mahāsaraḥ puṣkarāṇi prabhāsottaramānase ||12||

13kālodakaṃ ca gantāsi labdhāyurjīvite punaḥ
|
sarasvatīdṛṣadvatyoḥ saṃgamo mānasaḥ saraḥ ||13||

14svādhyāyaśīlaḥ sthāneṣu sarveṣvevamupaspṛśet
|
tyāgadharmaḥ pavitrāṇāṃ saṃnyāsaṃ manurabravīt ||14||

15atrāpyudāharantīmā gāthāḥ satyavatā kṛtāḥ
|
yathā kumāraḥ satyo vai naiva puṇyo na pāpakṛt ||15||

16na hyasti sarvabhūteṣu duḥkhamasminkutaḥ sukham
|
evaṃ prakṛtibhūtānāṃ sarvasaṃsargayāyinām ||16||

17tyajatāṃ jīvitaṃ śreyo nivṛtte puṇyapāpake
|
yattveva rājño jyāyiṣṭhaṃ kāryāṇāṃ tadbravīmi te ||17||

18balena saṃvibhāgaiśca jaya svargaṃ janeśvara
|
yasyaiva balamojaśca sa dharmasya prabhurnaraḥ ||18||

19brāhmaṇārthaṃ sukhārthaṃ hi tvaṃ pāhi vasudhāṃ nṛpa
|
yathaivaitānpurā''kṣepsīstathaivaitānprasādaya ||19||

20api vikriyamāṇo'pi tyajyamāno'pyanekadhā
|
ātmano darśanādviprānna hantāsmīti mārgaya
|
ghaṭamānaḥ svakāryeṣu kuru niḥśreyasaṃ param ||20||

21himāgnighorasadṛśo rājā bhavati kaścana
|
lāṃgalāśanikalpo vā bhavedanyaḥ paraṃtapa ||21||

22na viśeṣeṇa gantavyamavicchinnena vā punaḥ
|
na jātu nāhamasmīti suprasaktamasādhuṣu ||22||

23vikarmaṇā tapyamānaḥ pāpādviparimucyate
|
naitatkāryaṃ punariti dvitīyātparimucyate ||23||

24kariṣye dharmameveti tṛtīyātparimucyate
|
śucistīrthānyanucaran bahutvātparimucyate ||24||

25kalyāṇamanukartavyaṃ puruṣeṇa bubhūṣatā
|
ye sugandhīni sevante tathāgandhā bhavanti te ||25||

26ye durgandhīni sevante tathāgandhā bhavanti te
|
tapaścaryāparaḥ sadyaḥ pāpādviparimucyate ||26||

27saṃvatsaramupāsyāgnimabhiśastaḥ pramucyate
|
trīṇi varṣāṇyupāsyāgniṃ bhrūṇahā vipramucyate ||27||

28mahāsaraḥ puṣkarāṇi prabhāsottaramānase
|
abhyetya yojanaśataṃ bhrūṇahā vipramucyate ||28||

29yāvataḥ prāṇino hanyāttajjātīyāṃstu tāvataḥ
|
pramīyamānānunmocya prāṇihā vipramucyate ||29||

30api cāpsu nimajjeta japaṃstriraghamarṣaṇam
|
yathāśvamedhāvabhṛthastathā tanmanurabravīt ||30||

31tatkṣipraṃ nudate pāpaṃ satkāraṃ labhate yathā
|
api cainaṃ prasīdanti bhūtāni jaḍamūkavat ||31||

32bṛhaspatiṃ devaguruṃ surāsurāḥ sarve sametyābhyanuyujya rājan
|
dharmyaṃ phalaṃ vettha phalaṃ maharṣe tathaiva tasminnarake pāralokye ||32||

33ubhe tu yasya sadṛśe bhavetāṃ kiṃsvittayostatra jayo'tha na syāt
|
ācakṣva naḥ puṇyaphalaṃ maharṣe kathaṃ pāpaṃ nudate dharmaśīlaḥ ||33||

34bṛhaspatiruvāca| kṛtvā pāpaṃ pūrvamabuddhipūrvaṃ puṇyāni cetkurute buddhipūrvam
|
sa tatpāpaṃ nudate karmaśīlo vāso yathā malinaṃ kṣārayuktam ||34||

35pāpaṃ kṛtvā'bhimanyeta nāhamasmīti pūruṣaḥ
|
taccikīrṣati kalyāṇaṃ śraddadhāno'nasūyakaḥ ||35||

36chidrāṇi vivṛtānyeva sādhūnāṃ cāvṛṇoti yaḥ
|
yaḥ pāpaṃ puruṣaḥ kṛtvā kalyāṇamabhipadyate ||36||

37yathā''dityaḥ prātarudyaṃstamaḥ sarvaṃ vyapohati
|
kalyāṇamācarannevaṃ sarvapāpaṃ vyapohati ||37||

38bhīṣma uvāca|
evamuktvā tu rājānamindroto janamejayam
|
yājayāmāsa vidhivadvājimedhena śaunakaḥ ||38||

39tataḥ sa rājā vyapanītakalmaṣaḥ śreyovṛtaḥ prajvalitāgnirūpavān
|
viveśa rājyaṃ svamamitrakarṣaṇo yathā divaṃ pūrṇavapurniśākaraḥ ||39||
