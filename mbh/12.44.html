Mahābhārata 12.44 (Śāntiparvan)

1vaiśampāyana uvāca|
tato visarjayāmāsa sarvāḥ prakṛtayo nṛpaḥ
|
viviśuścābhyanujñātā yathāsvāni gṛhāṇi te ||1||

2tato yudhiṣṭhiro rājā bhīmaṃ bhīmaparākramam
|
sāntvayannabravīcchrīmānarjunaṃ yamajau tathā ||2||

3śatrubhirvividhaiḥ śastraiḥ kṣatadehā mahāraṇe
|
śrāntā bhavantaḥ subhṛśaṃ tāpitāḥ śokamanyubhiḥ ||3||

4araṇye duḥkhavasatīrmatkṛte bharatarṣabhāḥ
|
bhavadbhiranubhūtā hi yathā kupuruṣaistathā ||4||

5yathāsukhaṃ yathājoṣaṃ jayo'yamanubhūyatām
|
viśrāntāllaṃbyavijñānān śvaḥ sametā'smi vaḥ punaḥ ||5||

6tato duryodhanagṛhaṃ prāsādairupaśobhitam
|
bahuratnasamākīrṇaṃ dāsīdāsasamākulam ||6||

7dhṛtarāṣṭrābhyanujñātaṃ bhrātrā dattaṃ vṛkodaraḥ
|
pratipede mahābāhurmandiraṃ maghavāniva ||7||

8yathā duryodhanagṛhaṃ tathā duḥśāsanasya tu
|
prāsādamālāsaṃyuktaṃ hematoraṇabhūṣitam ||8||

9dāsīdāsasusampūrṇaṃ prabhūtadhanadhānyavat
|
pratipede mahābāhurarjuno rājaśāsanāt ||9||

10durmarṣaṇasya bhavanaṃ duḥśāsanagṛhādvaram
|
kuberabhavanaprakhyaṃ maṇihemavibhūṣitam ||10||

11nakulāya varārhāya karśitāya mahāvane
|
dadau prītā mahārāja dharmaputro yudhiṣṭhiraḥ ||11||

12durmukhasya ca veśmāgryaṃ śrīmatkanakabhūṣaṇam
|
pūrṇapadmadalākṣīṇāṃ strīṇāṃ śayanasaṅkulam ||12||

13pradadau sahadevāya santataṃ priyakāriṇe
|
mumude tacca labdhvā'sau kailāsaṃ dhanado yathā ||13||

14yuyutsurviduraścaiva sañjayaśca viśāmpate
|
sudharmā caiva dhaumyaśca yathāsvān jagmurālayān ||14||

15saha sātyakinā śaurirarjunasya niveśanam
|
viveśa puruṣavyāghro vyāghro giriguhāmiva ||15||

16tatra bhakṣyānnapānaiste muditāḥ susukhoṣitāḥ
|
sukhaprabuddhā rājānamupatasthuryudhiṣṭhiram ||16||
