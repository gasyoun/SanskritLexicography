Mahābhārata 18.6 (Svargārohaṇaparvan)

1vyāsaṃ vasiṣṭhanaptāraṃ śakteḥ pautramakalmaṣam
|
parāśarātmajaṃ vande śukatātaṃ taponidhim||1||

2vyāsāya viṣṇurūpāya vyāsarūpāya viṣṇave
|
namo vai brahmanidhaye vāsiṣṭhāya namonamaḥ||2||

3acaturvadano brahmā dvibāhuraparo hariḥ
|
aphālalocanaḥ śaṃbhurbhagavān bādarāyaṇaḥ||3||

4muniṃ snigdhāṃbujābhāsaṃ vedavyāsamakalmaṣam
|
vedavyāsaṃ sarasvatyāvāsaṃ vyāsaṃ namāmyaham||4||

5sañjaya uvāca| dvāravatyāṃ sthitaṃ kṛṣṇaṃ cintayāmāsa vai purā
|
sandhyarthaṃ preṣayāmāsa kurūṇāṃ paṇḍavaiḥ saha||5||

6pāṇḍavānāṃ hitārthāya śīghraṃ kṛṣṇena gamyatām
|
śrīkṛṣṇo ratha vegena gatvā vai hastināpurīm||6||

7vidurasya gṛham gatvā dṛṣṭastena janārdanaḥ
|
viduraścāgataṃ dṛṣṭvā idaṃ vacanamabravit||7||

8bhavaddarśanamātreṇa kṛtakṛtyo'smyahaṃ prabho
|
adya me saphalam janma adya me saphalam tapaḥ
|
adya me pitarastuṣṭā govinde gṛhamāgate||8||

9adyāṣṭamī ca navamī ca caturdaśī ca
|
adyāyanam ca viṣuvaṃ ca dinatrayam ca
|
adyaiva piṇḍapitṛyajñamakhasya kālo
|
dāmodareṇa sahasā gṛhamāgatena||9||

10śrībhagavānuvāca| sādhu sādhu mahāprājña sarvaśāstraviśārada
|
tuṣṭo'smi ca varam brūhi dāsyāmi kurunandana||10||

11vidura uvāca| bhojanaṃ viprasaṃkīrṇaṃ bandhusaṃkīrṇamandiram
|
śayanaṃ sutasaṃkīrṇaṃ dehi me madhusūdana||11||

12sañjaya uvāca| kṛṣṇasyāgamanaṃ śrutvā rājarājaḥ suyodhanaḥ
|
vidurasya gṛhaṃ gatvā idaṃ vacanamabravīt||12||

13duryodhana uvāca| bhīṣmadroṇau parityajya māṃ caiva madhusūdanaḥ
|
kimarthaṃ punḍarīkākṣa kṛtaṃ vṛṣalibhojanam||13||

14śrībhagavānuvāca| na śūdrā bhagavadbhaktā viprā bhāgavatāḥ smṛtāḥ
|
sarvavarṇeṣu te śūdrā ye hyabhaktā janārdane||14||

15śuddhaṃ bhāgavatasyānnaṃ śuddhaṃ bhāgīrathījalam
|
śuddhaṃ viṣṇupadaṃ divyaṃ śuddhamekādaśīvratam||15||

16cāṇḍālaṃ mama bhaktam vā nāvamanyeta buddhimān
|
yo'vamanyeta mūḍhātmā rauravaṃ narakaṃ vrajet||16||

17kasya doṣa kule nāsti vyādhinā ko na pīḍitaḥ
|
vyasanaṃ kairna saṃprāptam kasya saukhyaṃ nirantaram||17||

18bhojanaṃ pṛcchase rājannādaraṃ kiṃ na pṛcchasi
|
bhojanaṃ gatajīrṇaṃ syādādarastyajarāmaraḥ||18||

19ādareṇopanītāni śākānnāni suyodhana
|
prīṇanti mama gātrāṇi nāmṛtam mānavarjitam||19||

20saṃprītibhojyānyannāni āpadbhojyāni vā punaḥ
|
na ca saṃprīyase rājan na vai cāpadgatā vayam||20||

21dviṣadannaṃ na bhoktavyaṃ dviṣantam naiva bhojayet
|
pāṇḍavāndveṣṭi bho rājan mama prāṇā hi pāṇḍavāḥ||21||

22mama vākyaṃ kuruśreṣṭhaḥ śāntimiccha suyodhana
|
rājyaṃ teṣāṃ samaṃ dattvā yūyaṃ pañcottaraṃ śatam||22||

23gotrakṣayo na kartavyo rājñām bandhujanaiḥ saha
|
kurvanto te hitaṃ vākyaṃ mama bodhaṃ vibodhaya||23||

24vane dvādaśa varṣāṇi ajñātaṃ ca trayodaśam
|
pañca grāmārthino rājan pāṇḍavā dharmacāriṇaḥ||24||

25duryodhana uvāca| yantrasya guṇadoṣo'sti yantriṇaḥ puruṣottama
|
ahaṃ yantro bhavān yantrī mama doṣo na vidyate||25||

26śrībhagavānuvāca| indraprastham yamaprasthamavantīm vāruṇāpurī
|
dehi me caturo grāmānpañcamaṃ hastināpurīm||26||

27duryodhana uvāca| indraprasthaṃ gurordattaṃ yamaprasthaṃ kṛpasya ca
|
vāruṇāvatakaṃ bhīṣme avantī sūryanandane||27||

28hastināpuramasmākaṃ pañca grāmānanukramāt
|
evaṃ vyavasthitāngrāmān śṛṇu devakinandana||28||

29sūcyagreṇa sutīkṣṇena yavadbhidyati medinī
|
tāvanna hi pradāsyāmi vinā yuddhena keśava||29||

30śrībhagavānuvāca| dvāvimau puruṣau mūrkhau duryodhanadaśānanau
|
gograham vanabhaṅgam ca dṛṣṭvā yuddhaṃ punaḥ punaḥ||30||

31yadā yadā paśyati vānaradhvajam dhanurdharaṃ pāṇḍavamadhyamaṃ raṇe
|
gadāprahāraṃ balinaṃ vṛkodaram tadā tadā dāsyasi sarvamedinīm||31||

32yadā yadā droṇavikarṇakarṇaiḥ saṃkṣiptamātre khalu bhīṣmaśalyau
|
kṛpaśca yodhāḥ patitā raṇāṅgaṇe tadā tadā dāsyasi sarvamedinīm||32||

33duryodhana uvāca| hiraṇyavarṇaṃ paripūrṇagātraṃ meghonnataṃ mattagajendratulyam
|
ādityaputraṃ bahuśatrunāśaṃ paśyāmi karṇaṃ rathamāruhantam||33||

34śrībhagavānuvāca| nare catuṣkaṃ turage ca ṣoḍaśaṃ gaje śataṃ pancaśataṃ ratheṣu
|
dṛṣṭvā'rjuno muñcati bāṇavarṣaṃ svātīgataḥ śukra ivātivṛṣṭim||34||

35ekadhā daśadhā caiva śatadhā ca sahasradhā
|
raṇe pārthaśarā vṛṣṭirdānaṃ brahmavido yathā||35||

36kiṃ karṇena sahasreṇa duryodhanaśatairapi
|
śaragarjitameghena vṛdhā karṇena garjitam||36||

37ekākī pādacāreṇa yadi nāyāsi kaurava
|
dharmaśāstrapravartakāro manvādyā madyapāyinaḥ||37||

38dhṛtarāṣṭra uvāca| brūhi sañjaya yadvṛttaṃ yuddhe teṣāṃ mahātmanām
|
pāṇḍavānāṃ kurūṇāṃ ca saṃpravṛtte mahākṣaye||38||

39ke tatra pramukhā yodhāḥ ke ca tatra mahārathāḥ
|
mahābalāśca ke tatra kathaṃ te vinipātitāḥ||39||

40bhīṣmadroṇau kathaṃ bhagnau karṇaśalyau kathaṃ hatau
|
kathaṃ duryodhano rāja bhīmasenena pātitaḥ||40||

41sañjaya uvāca| medinībhāranirhāram pārthasārathimacyutam
|
praṇamāmi hṛṣīkeśaṃ durlabhaṃ cakrapāṇinam||41||

42durlabhā vipragoṣṭī ca durlabhā bhāratī kathā
|
durlabhā haribhaktiḥ ca gaṅgāsnānaṃ ca durlabham||42||

43sadbhiśca sahavāsena jāhnavyā darśanena ca
|
viṣṇoḥ smaraṇamātreṇa sarvapāpaiḥ pramucyate||43||

44arjunaḥ sātyakiścaiva dhṛṣṭadyumno ghaṭotkacaḥ
|
śikhaṇḍiścābhimanyuśca vāyuputro mahābalaḥ||44||

45nakulaḥ sahadevaśca dharmarājo yudhiṣṭhiraḥ
|
virāṭaścottaraścaiva drupadaśca mahārathāḥ||45||

46pāṇḍavānāṃ bale yodhāḥ sarve viṣṇuparākramāḥ
|
kauravānāṃ bale yodhāḥ sarve saṃkarṣaṇaprabhāḥ||46||

47śakuniḥ saubalo bhīṣmaḥ kṛtavarmā jayadradhaḥ
|
bhūriśravāśca bāhliko bhagadattastathaiva ca||47||

48ulūkaḥ somadattaśca śaśibinduśca pārthivaḥ
|
droṇo droṇiḥ kṛpaḥ śalyo vṛṣaseno halāyudhaḥ||48||

49vaikartano vikarṇaśca kaliṅgastu tathaiva ca
|
duḥśāsanaśca karṇaśca rājā duryodhanastathā||49||

50ete dvāviṃśatiḥ proktā bharateṣu mahārathāḥ
|
kauravāḥ pāṇḍavāścaiva ete yuddhaviśāradāḥ||50||

51bhīṣma uvāca| arjunaḥ saha putreṇa droṇaḥ saha sutena ca
|
ahaṃ bhūriśravāścaiva ṣaḍete'tirathāḥ smṛtāḥ||51||

52kṛpaśca kṛtavarmā ca madrarājo yudhiṣṭhiraḥ
|
virāṭo bhīmasenaśca ṣaḍete ca mahārathāḥ||52||

53sātyakiśca śikhaṇḍiśca dhṛṣṭadyumno virāṭajaḥ
|
śakunī rājaputraśca ete samarathāḥ smṛtāḥ||53||

54duḥśāsanaśca karṇaśca vṛṣaseno'pi saindhavaḥ
|
nakulaḥ sahadevaśca ṣaḍete'rtharathāḥ smṛtāḥ||54||

55ahamekaṃ tribhirdroṇaḥ pañcabhiḥ sūryanandanaḥ
|
nimeṣaṃ droṇaputrastu nimuṣārdhaṃ dhanañjayaḥ||55||

56caturviṃśatirete vai (vīrā) bhāratasattamāḥ
|
nānāśastrapraharaṇāḥ sarve yuddhaviśāradāḥ||56||

57ādiparva sabhāparva parva āraṇyakaṃ tathā
|
virāṭaparva vijñeyaṃ caturthaṃ tadanantaram||57||

58udyogaṃ pañcamaṃ parva bhīṣmaparva ataḥ param
|
saptamaṃ droṇaparva tu karṇaparva athāṣṭamam||58||

59navamaṃ śalyaparva ca gadāparva ataḥ param
|
sauṣuptikam tadā parva garbhapātanameva ca||59||

60trayodaśaṃ tu strīparva pradānamudakasya ca
|
śānti parva ataḥ proktamāśvamedikameva ca||60||

61svargārohaṇa parva tu harivaṃśastathaiva ca
|
ityaṣṭādaśaparvāṇi saṃkhyā dvaipāyanena tu||61||

62bhāti sarveṣu vedeṣu ratiḥ sarveṣu jantuṣu
|
taraṇaṃ sarvapāpānāṃ yasmādbhāratamucyate||62||

63bhāratasya samudrasya merornārāyaṇasya ca
|
aprameyāṇi catvāri puṇyaṃ toyaṃ guhāguṇāḥ||63||

64hemante prathame māse śuklapakṣe trayodaśī
|
pravṛttaṃ bhārataṃ yuddhaṃ nakṣatram yamadaivatam||64||

65phālgunyāṃ nihato bhīṣmaḥ kṛṣṇa pakṣe ca saptamī
|
aṣṭamyāṃ caiva saubhadro navamyāṃ ca jayadrathaḥ||65||

66daśamyāṃ bhagadattastu mahāyuddhe nipātitāḥ
|
ekādaśyāmardharātrau hato vīro ghaṭotkacaḥ||66||

67tataḥ prabhātasamaye virāṭadrupadau hatau
|
dvādaśyāṃ caiva madhyāhne droṇācāryo raṇe hataḥ||67||

68trayodaśyāṃ tu madhyāhne vṛṣaseno nipātitaḥ
|
caturdaśyām tu pūrvāhṇe raṇe duḥśāsano hataḥ||68||

69tasminneva mahāyuddhe vartamāne caturdaśī
|
dhanañjayena madhyāhne karṇo vaikartano hataḥ||69||

70niḥśabdatūryaṃ hata yodhavīram praśāntadarpaṃ dhṛtarāṣṭrasainyam
|
na śobhate sūryasutena hīnam vṛndaṃ grahāṇāmiva candrahīnam||70||

71mukhaṃ kamalapatrākṣaṃ yathā śravaṇavarjitam
|
tathā tat kauravaṃ sainyaṃ karṇahīnaṃ na śobhate||71||

72vyūḍhoraskaṃ kamalanayanaṃ taptahemāvabhāsam
|
putraṃ dṛṣṭvā bhuvanatilakaṃ pārthabāṇāvasaktam
|
pāṃsugrastaṃ malinavasanaṃ putramanvīkṣya taṃ ca
|
mandam mandam mṛditavadanaṃ medinī mandarāśiḥ||72||

73śrīkṛṣṇa uvāca| sva (yā) mayā ca kuntyā ca dharaṇyā vāsavena ca
|
jāmadagnyena rāmeṇa ṣaḍbhiḥ karṇo nipātitaḥ||73||

74sanjaya uvāca| amāyāṃ dharmaputreṇa śalyo madrādhipo hataḥ
|
ulūkaḥ śakuniścaiva yamābhyāṃ vinipātitau||74||

75amāyāmardharātre tu rājā duryodhano hataḥ
|
bhīmasenasya gadāyā tāḍito vinipātitaḥ||75||

76abhavattādṛśaṃ yuddhaṃ kṣatriyāṇāṃ manasvinām
|
anyathā bhāṣitaṃ yuddhaṃ karmaṇā kṛtamanyathā||76||

77amāyāmeva yāminyāṃ droṇinā nihatastadā
|
dhṛṣṭādyumnaḥ śikhaṇḍī ca draupadyāḥ pañca cātmajāḥ||77||

78aṣṭau rathasahasrāṇi nava danti śatāni ca
|
rājaputrasahasram ca aśvatthāma nivartate||78||

79dināni daśa bhīṣmeṇa bhāradvājena pañca ca
|
dinadvaye tu karṇena śalyenāardhadinam tathā||79||

80dinārdhaṃ tu gadāyuddhametadbhāratamucyate
|
evamaṣṭādaśaṃ hanti akṣauhiṇyāṃ dinakramāt||80||

81dharmakṣetre kṣayakṣetre kurukṣetre mahātmanā
|
pārthenārohayansvargam rājaputrā yaśaśvinaḥ||81||

82navanāgasahasreṣu nāge nāge śataṃ rathāḥ
|
rathe rathe śataṃ cāśvā aśve aśve śataṃ narāḥ||82||

83raṇayajñe mahīyajñe dīkṣito'yaṃ yudhiṣṭhiraḥ
|
vediṃ kṛtvā kurukṣetraṃ yūpaṃ kṛtvā janārdanam||83||

84hotāramarjunaṃ kṛtvā yajamāno yudhiṣṭhiraḥ
|
pāñcālīmaraṇiṃ kṛtvā vahniṃ kṛtvā vṛkodaram||84||

85ājyaṃ kṛtvārkatanayaṃ jayadrathamukhānnṛpān
|
duryodhanaṃ paśuṃ kṛtvā bhīṣmadroṇau mahahaviḥ||85||

86ayājñikamidaṃ dravyaṃ bhayamohavivarjitam
|
gāṇḍīvena sruveṇaiva hūyamāneṣu rājasu||86||

87mātāpitṛsahasrāṇi putradāraśatāni ca
|
saṃsāreṣvanubhūtāni yānti yāsyanti cāpare||87||

88harṣasthānasahasrāṇi bhayasthānaśatāni ca
|
divase divase mūḍhaṃ āviśanti na paṇḍitam||88||

89ūrdhvabāhurviraumyeṣa na ca kaścicchṛṇoti me
|
dharmādarthaśca kāmaśca sa kimarthaṃ na sevyate||89||

90na jātu kāmān na bhayānna lobhāt dharmaṃ tyajejjīvitasyāpi hetoḥ
|
nityo dharmaḥ sukhaduḥkhe tvanitye jīvo nityaḥ heturasya tvanityaḥ||90||
