Mahābhārata 1.8 (Ādiparvan)

1sautiruvāca|
sa cāpi cyavano brahmanbhārgavo janayatsutam
|
sukanyāyāṃ mahātmānaṃ pramatiṃ dīptatejasam||1||

2pramatistu ruruṃ nāma ghṛtācyāṃ samajījanat
|
ruruḥ pramadvarāyāṃ tu śunakaṃ samajījanam||2||

3śunakastu mahāsattvaḥ sarvaṃ bhārgavanandanaḥ
|
jātastapasi tīvre ca sthitaḥ sthirayaśāstataḥ||3||

4tasya brahmanruroḥ sarvaṃ caritaṃ bhūritejasaḥ
|
vistareṇa pravakṣyāmi tacchṛṇu tvamaśeṣataḥ||4||

5ṛṣirāsīnmahānpūrvaṃ tapovidyāsamanvitaḥ
|
sthūlakeśa iti khyātaḥ sarvabhūtahite rataḥ||5||

6etasminneva kāle tu menakāyāṃ prajajñivān
|
gandharvarājo viprarṣe viśvāvasuriti smṛtaḥ||6||

7apsarā menakā tasya taṃ garbhaṃ bhṛgunandana
|
utsasarja yathākālaṃ sthūlakeśāśramaṃ prati||7||

8utsṛjya caiva taṃ garbhaṃ nadyāstīre jagāma sā
|
apsarā menakā brahmannirdayā nirapatrapā||8||

9kanyāmamaragarbhābhāṃ jvalantīmiva ca śriyā
|
tāṃ dadarśa samutsṛṣṭāṃ nadītīre mahānṛṣiḥ||9||

10sthūlakeśaḥ sa tejasvī vijane bandhuvarjitām
|
sa tāṃ dṛṣṭvā tadā kanyāṃ sthūlakeśo mahādvijaḥ||10||

11jagrāha ca muniśreṣṭhaḥ kṛpāviṣṭaḥ pupoṣa ca
|
vavṛdhe sā varārohā tasyāśramapade śubhe||11||

12jātakādyāḥ kriyāścāsyā vidhipūrvaṃ yathākramam
|
sthūlakeśo mahābhāgaścakāra sumahānṛṣiḥ||12||

13pramadābhyo varā sā tu sattvarūpaguṇānvitā
|
tataḥ pramadvaretyasyā nāma cakre mahānṛṣiḥ||13||

14tāmāśramapade tasya rururdṛṣṭvā pramadvarām
|
babhūva kila dharmātmā madanopahatastadā||14||

15pitaraṃ sakhibhiḥ so'tha śrāvayāmāsa bhārgavam
|
pramatiścābhyayācattāṃ sthūlakeśaṃ yaśasvinam||15||

16tataḥ prādātpitā kanyāṃ rurave tāṃ pramadvarām
|
vivāhaṃ sthāpayitvā'gre nakṣatre bhagadaivate||16||

17tataḥ katipayāhasya vivāhe samupasthite
|
sakhībhiḥ krīḍatī sārdhaṃ sā kanyā varavarṇinī||17||

18nāpaśyatsaṃprasuptaṃ vai bhujaṃgaṃ tiryagāyatam
|
padā cainaṃ samākrāmanmumūrṣuḥ kālacoditā||18||

19sa tasyāḥ saṃpramattāyāścoditaḥ kāladharmaṇā
|
viṣopaliptāndaśanānbhṛśamaṅge nyapātayat||19||

20sā daṣṭā tena sarpeṇa papāta sahasā bhuvi
|
vivarṇā vigataśrīkā bhraṣṭābharaṇacetanā||20||

21nirānandakarī teṣāṃ bandhūnāṃ muktamūrdhajā
|
vyasuraprekṣaṇīyā sā prekṣaṇīyatamā'bhavat|| 21||

22prasupte vābhavaccāpi bhuvi sarpaviṣārditā
|
bhūyo manoharatarā babhūva tanumadhyamā||22||

23dadarśa tāṃ pitā caiva ye caivānye tapasvinaḥ
|
viceṣṭamānāṃ patitāṃ bhūtale padmavarcasam||23||

24tataḥ sarve dvijatarāḥ samājagmuḥ kṛpānvitāḥ
|
svastyātreyo mahājānuḥ kuśikaḥ śaṅkhamekhalaḥ||24||

25uddālakaḥ kaṭhaścaiva śvetaścaiva mahāyaśāḥ
|
bharadvājaḥ kauṇakṛtsya ārṣṭiṣeṇo'tha gautamaḥ||25||

26pramatiḥ saha putreṇa tathā'nye vanavāsinaḥ
|
tāṃ te kanyāṃ vyasuṃ dṛṣṭvā bhujaṃgasya viṣārditām||26||

27ruruduḥ kṛpayā'viṣṭā rurustvārto bahiryayau
|
te ca sarve dvijaśreṣṭhāstatraivopāviśaṃstadā|| 27||
