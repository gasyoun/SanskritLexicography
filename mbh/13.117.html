Mahābhārata 13.117 (Anuśāsanaparvan)

1yudhiṣṭhira uvāca|
akāmāśca sakāmāśca ye hatāḥ sma mahāmṛdhe
|
kāṃ gatiṃ pratipannāste tanme brūhi pitāmaha ||1||

2duḥkhaṃ prāṇaparityāgaḥ puruṣāṇāṃ mahāmṛdhe
|
jānāsi tvaṃ mahāprājña prāṇatyāgaṃ suduṣkaram ||2||

3samṛddhau vā'samṛddhau vā śubhe vā yadi vā'śubhe
|
kāraṇaṃ tatra me brūhi sarvajño hyasi me mataḥ ||3||

4bhīṣma uvāca|
samṛddhau vā'samṛddhau vā śubhe vā yadi vā'śubhe
|
saṃsāre'sminsamāyātāḥ prāṇinaḥ pṛthivīpate ||4||

5niratā yena bhāvena tatra me śṛṇu kāraṇam
|
samyakcāyamanupraśnastvayoktastu yudhiṣṭhira ||5||

6atra te vartayiṣyāmi purāvṛttamidaṃ nṛpa
|
dvaipāyanasya saṃvādaṃ kīṭasya ca yudhiṣṭhira ||6||

7brahmabhūtaścaranvipraḥ kṛṣṇadvaipāyanaḥ purā
|
dadarśa kīṭaṃ dhāvantaṃ śīghraṃ śakaṭavartmani ||7||

8gatijñaḥ sarvabhūtānāṃ bhāṣājñaśca śarīriṇām
|
sarvajñaḥ sa tadā dṛṣṭvā kīṭaṃ vacanamabravīt ||8||

9vyāsa uvāca|
kīṭa saṃtrastarūpo'si tvaritaścaiva lakṣyase
|
kva dhāvasi tadācakṣva kutaste bhayamāgatam ||9||

10kīṭa uvāca|
śakaṭasyāsya mahato ghoṣaṃ śrutvā bhayaṃ mama
|
āgataṃ vai mahābuddhe svana eṣa hi dāruṇaḥ ||10||

11śrūyate na ca māṃ hanyāditi hyasmādapakrame
|
śvasatāṃ ca śṛṇomyenaṃ goputrāṇāṃ pratodyatām ||11||

12vahatāṃ sumahābhāraṃ sannikarṣe svanaṃ prabho
|
nṛṇāṃ ca saṃvāhayatāṃ śrūyate vividhaḥ svanaḥ ||12||

13śrotumasmadvidhenaiṣa na śakyaḥ kīṭayoninā
|
tasmādatikramāmyeṣa bhayādasmātsudāruṇāt ||13||

14duḥkhaṃ hi mṛtyurbhūtānāṃ jīvitaṃ ca sudurlabham
|
ato bhītaḥ palāyāmi gaccheyaṃ nāsukhaṃ sukhāt ||14||

15bhīṣma uvāca|
ityuktaḥ sa tu taṃ prāha kutaḥ kīṭa sukhaṃ tava
|
maraṇaṃ te sukhaṃ manye tiryagyonau tu vartase ||15||

16śabdaṃ sparśaṃ rasaṃ gandhaṃ bhogāṃścoccāvacānbahun
|
nābhijānāsi kīṭa tvaṃ śreyo maraṇameva te ||16||

17kīṭa uvāca|
sarvatra nirato jīva itaścāpi sukhaṃ mama
|
cintayāmi mahāprājña tasmādicchāmi jīvitum ||17||

18ihāpi viṣayaḥ sarvo yathādehaṃ pravartitaḥ
|
mānuṣāḥ sthairyajāścaiva pṛthagbhogā viśeṣataḥ ||18||

19ahamāsaṃ manuṣyo vai śūdro bahudhanaḥ prabho
|
abrahmaṇyo nṛśaṃsaśca ka‌daryo vṛddhijīvanaḥ ||19||

20vāktīkṣṇo nikṛtiprajño dveṣṭā viśvasya sarvaśaḥ
|
mithyākṛto'pi vidhinā parasvaharaṇe rataḥ ||20||

21bhṛtyātithijanaścāpi gṛhe paryaśito mayā
|
mātsaryātsvādukāmena nṛśaṃsena bubhukṣatā ||21||

22devārthaṃ pitṛyajñārthamannaṃ śraddhāhṛtaṃ mayā
|
na dattamarthakāmena deyamannaṃ purā kila ||22||

23guptaṃ śaraṇamāśritya bhayeṣu śaraṇāgatāḥ
|
akasmātte mayā tyaktā na trātā abhayaiṣiṇaḥ||23||

24dhanaṃ dhānyaṃ priyāndārānyānaṃ vāsastathādbhutam
|
śriyaṃ dṛṣṭvā manuṣyāṇāmasūyāmi nirarthakam ||24||

25īrṣyuḥ parasukhaṃ dṛṣṭvā anyasya na bubhūṣakaḥ
|
trivargahantā cānyeṣāmātmakāmānuvartakaḥ ||25||

26nṛśaṃsaguṇabhūyiṣṭhaṃ purā karma kṛtaṃ mayā
|
smṛtvā tadanutapye'haṃ hitvā priyamivātmajam ||26||

27śubhānāṃ nābhijānāmi kṛtānāṃ karmaṇāṃ phalam
|
mātā ca pūjitā vṛddhā brāhmaṇaścārcito mayā||27||

28sakṛjjātiguṇopetaḥ saṅgatyā gṛhamāgataḥ
|
atithiḥ pūjito brahmastena māṃ nājahātsmṛtiḥ||28||

29karmaṇā punarevāhaṃ sukhamāgāmi lakṣaye
|
tacchrotumahamicchāmi tvattaḥ śreyastapodhana ||29||
