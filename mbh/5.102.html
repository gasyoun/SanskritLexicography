Mahābhārata 5.102 (Udyogaparvan)

1nārada uvāca|
idaṃ rasātalaṃ nāma saptamaṃ pṛthivītalam
|
yatrāste surabhirmātā gavāmamṛtasaṃbhavā ||1||

2kṣarantī satataṃ kṣīraṃ pṛthivīsārasaṃbhavam
|
ṣaṇṇāṃ rasānāṃ sāreṇa rasamekamanuttamam ||2||

3amṛtenābhitṛptasya sāramudgirataḥ purā
|
pitāmahasya vada‌nāduda‌tiṣṭhada‌ninditā ||3||

4yasyāḥ kṣīrasya dhārāyā nipatantyā mahītale
|
hradaḥ kṛtaḥ kṣīranidhiḥ pavitraṃ paramucyate ||4||

5puṣpitasyeva phenena paryantamanuveṣṭitam
|
pibanto nivasantyatra phenapā munisattamāḥ ||5||

6phenapā nāma te khyātāḥ phenāhārāśca mātale
|
ugre tapasi vartante yeṣāṃ bibhyati devatāḥ ||6||

7asyāścatasro dhenvo'nyā dikṣu sarvāsu mātale
|
nivasanti diśāṃ pālyo dhārayanto diśaḥ sma tāḥ ||7||

8pūrvāṃ diśaṃ dhārayate surūpā nāma saurabhī
|
dakṣiṇāṃ haṃsikā nāma dhārayatyaparāṃ diśam ||8||

9paścimā vāruṇī dikca dhāryate vai sumadra‌yā
|
mahānubhāvayā nityaṃ mātale viśvarūpayā ||9||

10sarvakāmadughā nāma dhenurdhārayate diśam
|
uttarāṃ mātale dharmyāṃ tathailavilasaṃjñitām ||10||

11āsāṃ tu payasā miśraṃ payo nirmathya sāgare
|
manthānaṃ mandaraṃ kṛtvā devairasurasaṃhitaiḥ ||11||

12uddhṛtā vāruṇī lakṣmīramṛtaṃ cāpi mātale
|
uccaiḥśravāścāśvarājo maṇiratnaṃ ca kaustubham ||12||

13sudhāhāreṣu ca sudhāṃ svadhābhojiṣu ca svadhām
|
amṛtaṃ cāmṛtāśeṣu surabhī kṣarate payaḥ ||13||

14atra gāthā purā gītā rasātalanivāsibhiḥ
|
paurāṇī śrūyate loke gīyate yā manīṣibhiḥ ||14||

15na nāgaloke na svarge na vimāne triviṣṭape
|
parivāsaḥ sukhastādṛk rasātalatale yathā ||15||
