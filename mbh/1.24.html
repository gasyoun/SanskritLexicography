Mahābhārata 1.24 (Ādiparvan)

1sautiruvāca|
sa śrutvā'thātmano dehaṃ suparṇaḥ prekṣya ca svayam
|
śarīrapratisaṃhāramātmanaḥ saṃpracakrame||1||

2suparṇa uvāca|
na me sarvāṇi bhūtāni vibhiyurdehadarśanāt
|
bhīmarūpātsamudvigrāstasmāttejastu saṃhare||2||

3sautiruvāca|
tataḥ kāmagamaḥ pakṣī kāmavīryo vihaṃgamaḥ
|
aruṇaṃ cātmanaḥ pṛṣṭhamāropya sa piturgṛhāt||3||

4māturantikamāgacchatparaṃ tīraṃ mahodadheḥ
|
tatrāruṇaśca nikṣipto diśaṃ pūrvāṃ mahādyutiḥ||4||

5sūryastejobhiratyugrairlokāndagdhumanā yadā
|
rururuvāca|
kimarthaṃ bhagavānsūryo lokāndagdhumanāstadā|| 5||

6kimastāpahṛtaṃ devairyenemaṃ manyurāviśat
|
pramatiruvāca|
candrādityairyadā rāhurākhyāto hyamṛtaṃ piban||6||

7vairānubandhaṃ kṛtavāṃścandrādityau tadā'nagha
|
bādhyamānaṃ graheṇātha hyāditye manyurāviśat||7||

8surārthāya samutpanno roṣo rāhostu māṃ prati
|
bahvanarthakaraṃ pāpameko'haṃ samavāpnuyām|| 8||

9sahāya eva kāryeṣu na ca kṛcchreṣu dṛśyate
|
paśyanti grasyamānaṃ māṃ sahante vai divaukasaḥ|| 9||

10tasmāllokavināśārthaṃ hyavatiṣṭhe na saṃśayaḥ
|
evaṃ kṛtamatiḥ sūryo hyastamabhyagamadgirim||10||

11tasmāllokavināśāya saṃtāpayata bhāskaraḥ
|
tato devānupāgamya procurevaṃ maharṣayaḥ|| 11||

12ādyārdharātrasamaye sarvalokabhayāvahaḥ
|
utpatsyate mahāndāhastrailokyasya vināśanaḥ||12||

13tato devāḥ sarṣigaṇā upagamya pitāmaham
|
abruvankimivehādya mahaddāhakṛtaṃ bhayam|| 13||

14na tāvaddṛśyate sūryaḥ kṣayo'yaṃ pratibhāti ca
|
udite bhagavanbhānau kathanetadbhaviṣyati||14||

15pitāmaha uvāca|
eṣa lokavināśāya ravirudyantumudyataḥ
|
dṛśyanneva hi lokānsa bhasmarāśīkariṣyati||15||

16tasya pratividhānaṃ ca vihitaṃ pūrvameva hi
|
kaśyapasya suto dhīmānaruṇetyabhiviśrutaḥ|| 16||

17mahākāyo mahātejāḥ sa sthāsyati puro raveḥ
|
kariṣyati ca sārathyaṃ tejaścāsya hariṣyati||17||

18lokānāṃ svasti caivaṃ syādṛṣīṇāṃ ca divaukasām
|
pramatiruvāca|
tataḥ pitāmahājjñātaḥ sarvaṃ cakre tadā'ruṇaḥ||18||

19uditaścaiva savitā hyaruṇena samāvṛtaḥ
|
etatte sarvamākhyātaṃ yatsūryaṃ manyurāviśat||19||

20aruṇaśca yathaivāsya sārathyamakarotprabhuḥ
|
bhūya evāparaṃ praśnaṃ śṛṇu pūrvamudāhṛtam||20||
