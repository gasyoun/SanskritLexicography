Mahābhārata 12.182 (Śāntiparvan)

1yudhiṣṭhira uvāca|
kutaḥ sṛṣṭamidaṃ viśvaṃ jagatsthāvarajaṅgamam
|
pralaye ca kamabhyeti tanme brūhi pitāmaha ||1||

2sasāgaraḥ sagagana saśailaḥ sabalāhakaḥ
|
sabhūmiḥ sāgnipavano loko'yaṃ kena nirmitaḥ ||2||

3kathaṃ sṛṣṭāni bhūtāni kathaṃ varṇavibhaktayaḥ
|
śaucaśaucaṃ kathaṃ teṣāṃ dharmādharmavidhiḥ katham ||3||

4kīdṛśo jīvatāṃ jīvaḥ kva vā gacchanti ye mṛtāḥ
|
asmāllokādamuṃ lokaṃ sarvaṃ śaṃsatu no bhavān ||4||

5bhīṣma uvāca|
atrāpyudāharantīmamitihāsaṃ purātanam
|
bhṛguṇā'bhihitaṃ śāstraṃ bharadvājāya pṛcchate ||5||

6kailāsaśikhare dṛṣṭvā dīpyamānaṃ mahaujasam
|
bhṛguṃ maharṣimāsīnaṃ bharadvājo'nvapṛcchata ||6||

7sasāgaraḥ sagaganaḥ saśailaḥ sabalāhakaḥ
|
sabhūmiḥ sāgnipavano loko'yaṃ kena nirmitaḥ ||7||

8kathaṃ sṛṣṭāni bhūtāni kathaṃ varṇavibhaktayaḥ
|
śaucāśaucaṃ kathaṃ teṣāṃ dharmādharmavidhiḥ katham ||8||

9kīdṛśo jīvatāṃ jīvaḥ kva vā gacchanti ye mṛtāḥ
|
paralokamimaṃ cāpi sarvaṃ śaṃsitumarhasi ||9||

10evaṃ sa bhagavānpṛṣṭo bharadvājena saṃśayam
|
brahmarṣirbrahmasaṃkāśaḥ sarvaṃ tasmai tato'bravīt ||10||

11bhṛguruvāca |
mānaso nāma yaḥ pūrvo viśruto vai maharṣibhiḥ
|
anādinidhano devastathā'bhedyo'jarāmaraḥ ||11||

12avyakta iti vikhyātaḥ śāśvato'thākṣayo'vyayaḥ
|
yataḥ sṛṣṭāni bhūtāni jāyante ca mriyanti ca ||12||

13so'sṛjatprathamaṃ devo mahāntaṃ nāma nāmataḥ
|
mahānsasarjāhaṃkāraṃ sa cāpi bhagavānatha ||13||

14ākāśamiti vikhyātaṃ sarvabhūtadharaḥ prabhuḥ
|
ākāśādabhavadvāri salilādagnimārutau
|
agnimārutasaṃyogāt tataḥ samabhavanmahī ||14||

15tatastejomayaṃ divyaṃ padmaṃ sṛṣṭaṃ svayambhuvā
|
tasmātpadmātsamabhavadbrahmā vedamayo nidhiḥ ||15||

16ahaṃkāra iti khyātaḥ sarvabhūtātmabhūtakṛt
|
brahmā vai sa mahātejā ya ete pañca dhātavaḥ ||16||

17śailāstasyāsthisaṃjñāstu medo māṃsaṃ ca medinī
|
samudrāstasya rudhiramākāśamudaraṃ tathā ||17||

18pavanaścaiva niḥśvāsastejo'gnirnimnagāḥ śirāḥ
|
agnīṣomau tu candrārkau nayane tasya viśrute ||18||

19nabhaścordhvaṃ śirastasya kṣitiḥ pādau bhujau diśaḥ
|
durvijñeyo hyacintyātmā siddhairapi na saṃśayaḥ ||19||

20sa eṣa bhagavānviṣṇurananta iti viśrutaḥ
|
sarvabhūtātmabhūtastho durvijñeyo kṛtātmabhiḥ ||20||

21ahaṃkārasya yaḥ sraṣṭā sarvabhūtabhavāya vai
|
yataḥ samabhavadviśvaṃ pṛṣṭo'haṃ yadiha tvayā ||21||

22bharadvāja uvāca |
gaganasya diśāṃ caiva bhūtalasyānilasya vā
|
kānyatra parimāṇāni saṃśayaṃ chindhi tattvataḥ ||22||

23bhṛguruvāca| anantametadākāśaṃ siddhadaivatasevitam
|
ramyaṃ nānāśrayākīrṇaṃ yasyānto nādhigamyate ||23||

24urdhvaṃ gateradhastāttu candrādityau na dṛśyataḥ
|
tatra devāḥ svayaṃ dīptā bhāsvarābhāgnivarcasaḥ ||24||

25te cāpyantaṃ na paśyanti nabhasaḥ prathitaujasaḥ
|
durgamatvādanantatvāditi me viddhi mānada ||25||

26upariṣṭopariṣṭāttu prajvaladbhiḥ svayaṃprabhaiḥ
|
niruddhametadākāśamaprameyaṃ surairapi ||26||

27pṛthivyante samudrāstu samudrānte tamaḥ smṛtam
|
tamaso'nte jalaṃ prāhurjalasyānte'gnireva ca ||27||

28rasātalānte salilaṃ jalānte pannagādhipāḥ
|
tadante punarākāśamākāśānte punarjalam ||28||

29evamantaṃ bhagavataḥ pramāṇaṃ salilasya ca
|
agnimārutatoyebhyo durjñeyaṃ daivatairapi ||29||

30agnimārutatoyānāṃ varṇāḥ kṣititalasya ca
|
ākāśādavagṛhyante bhidyante tattvadarśanāt ||30||

31paṭhanti caiva munayaḥ śāstreṣu vividheṣu ca
|
trailokyasāgare caiva pramāṇaṃ vihitaṃ yathā ||31||

32adṛśyāya tvagamyāya kaḥ pramāṇamudāharet
|
siddhānāṃ devatānāṃ ca yadā parimitā gatiḥ ||32||

33tadā gauṇamanantasya nāmānanteti viśrutam
|
nāmadheyānurūpasya mānasasya mahātmanaḥ ||33||

34yadā tu divyaṃ yadrūpaṃ hrasate vardhate punaḥ
|
ko'nyastadvedituṃ śakto yo'pi syāttadvidho'paraḥ ||34||

35tataḥ puṣkarataḥ sṛṣṭaḥ sarvajño mūrtimānprabhuḥ
|
brahmā dharmamayaḥ pūrvaḥ prajāpatiranuttamaḥ ||35||

36bharadvāja uvāca |
puṣkarādyadi sambhūto jyeṣṭhaṃ bhavati puṣkaram
|
brahmāṇaṃ pūrvajaṃ cāha bhavānsaṃdeha eva me ||36||

37bhṛguruvāca |
mānasasyeha yā mūrtirbrahmatvaṃ samupāgatā
|
tasyāsanavidhānārthaṃ pṛthivī padmamucyate ||37||

38karṇikātasya padmasya merurgaganamucchritaḥ
|
tasya madhye sthito lokānsṛjate jagataḥ prabhuḥ ||38||
