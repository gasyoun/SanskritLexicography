Mahābhārata 14.37 (Āśvamedhikaparvan)

1brāhmaṇa uvāca|
rajo'haṃ vaḥ pravakṣyāmi yāthātathyena sattamāḥ
|
nibodhata mahābhāgā guṇavṛttaṃ ca rājasam||1||

2santāpo rūpamāyāsa: sukhaduḥkhe himātapau
|
aiśvaryaṃ vigrahaḥ sandhirhetuvādo'ratiḥ kṣamā||2||

3balaṃ śauryaṃ mado roṣo vyāyāmakalahāvapi
|
īrṣyepsā piśunaṃ yuddhaṃ mamatvaṃ paripālanam||3||

4vadhabandhaparikleśāḥ krayo vikraya eva ca
|
nikṛnta chindhi bhindhīti paravarmāvakartanam||4||

5ugraṃ dāruṇamākrośaḥ paracchidrānuśāsanam
|
lokacintānucintā ca matsaraḥ paripālanam||5||

6mṛṣā vādo mṛṣā dānaṃ vikalpaḥ paribhāṣaṇam
|
nindā stutiḥ praśaṃsā ca pratāpaḥ paridharṣaṇam||6||

7paricaryānuśuśrūṣā sevā tṛṣṇā vyāpāśrayaḥ
|
vyūho nayaḥ pramādaśca parivādaḥ parigrahaḥ||7||

8saṃskārā ye ca lokeṣu pravartante pṛthakpṛthak
|
nṛṣu nārīṣu bhūteṣu dravyeṣu śaraṇeṣu ca||8||

9santāpo'pratyayaścaiva vratāni niyamāśca ye
|
āśīryuktāni karmāṇi paurtāni vividhāni ca||9||

10svāhākāro namaskāraḥ svadhākāro vaṣaṭkriyā
|
yājanādhyāpane cobhe yajanādhyayane api||10||

11dānaṃ pratigrahaścaiva prāyaścittāni maṅgalam
|
idaṃ me syādidaṃ me syātsneho guṇasamudbhavaḥ||11||

12abhidrohastathā māyā nikṛtirmāna eva ca
|
stainyaṃ hiṃsā jugupsā ca paritāpaḥ prajāgaraḥ||12||

13dambho darpo'tha rāgaśca bhaktiḥ prītiḥ pramodanam
|
dyūtaṃ ca janavādaśca sambandhāḥ strīkṛtāśca ye||13||

14nṛtyavāditragītānāṃ prasaṅgā yeca kecana
|
sarva ete guṇā viprā rājasāḥ samprakīrtitāḥ||14||

15bhūtabhavyabhaviṣyāṇāṃ bhāvānāṃ bhuvi bhāvanāḥ
|
trivarganiratā nityaṃ dharmo'rthaḥ kāma ityapi||15||

16kāmavṛttāḥ pramodante sarvakāmasamṛddhibhiḥ
|
arvāksrotasa ityete manuṣyā rajasā vṛtāḥ||16||

17asmi~lloke pramodante jāyamānāḥ punaḥ punaḥ
|
pretya bhāvikamīhante aihalaukikameva ca
|
dadati pratigṛhṇanti tarpayantyatha juhvati||17||

18rajoguṇā vo bahudhānukīrtitā yathāvaduktaṃ guṇavṛttameva ca
|
naro'pi yo veda guṇānimānsadā sa rājasaiḥ sarvaguṇarvimucyate||18||
