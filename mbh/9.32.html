Mahābhārata 9.32 (Śalyaparvan)

1dhṛtarāṣṭra uvāca|
evaṃ santarjyamānastu mama putro mahīpatiḥ
|
prakṛtyā manyumānvīraḥ kathamāsītparantapaḥ||1||

2na hi santarjanā tena śrutapūrvā kathañcana
|
rājabhāvena mānyaśca sarvalokasya so'bhavat||2||

3yasyātapatracchāyāpi svakā bhānostathā prabhā
|
khedāyaivābhimānitvātsahetsaivaṃ kathaṃ giraḥ||3||

4iyaṃ ca pṛthivī sarvā samlecchāṭavikā bhṛśam
|
prasādādbhiyate yasya pratyakṣaṃ tava sañjaya||4||

5sa tathā tarjyamānastu pāṇḍuputrairviśeṣataḥ
|
vihīnaśca svakairbhṛtyairnirjane cāvṛto bhṛśam||5||

6sa śrutvā kaṭukā vāco jayayuktāḥ punaḥ punaḥ
|
kimabravītpāṇḍaveyāṃstatanmamācakṣva sañjaya||6||

7sañjaya uvāca
tarjyamānastadā rājannudakasthastavātmajaḥ
|
yudhiṣṭhireṇa rājendra bhrātṛbhiḥ sahitena ha||7||

8śrutvā sa kaṭukā vāco viṣamastho narādhipaḥ
|
dīrghamuṣṇaṃ ca niḥśvasya salilasthaḥ punaḥ punaḥ||8||

9salilāntargato rājā dhunvanhastau punaḥ punaḥ
|
manaścakāra yuddhāya rājānaṃ cābhyabhāṣata||9||

10yūyaṃ sasuhṛdaḥ pārthāḥ sarve sarathavāhanāḥ
|
ahamekaḥ paridyūno viratho hatavāhanaḥ||10||

11āttaśastrai rathopetairbahubhiḥ parivāritaḥ
|
kathamekaḥ padātiḥ sannaśastro yoddhamutsahe||11||

12ekaikena tu māṃ yūyaṃ yodhayadhvaṃ yudhiṣṭhira
|
na hyeko bahubhirvīrairnyāyyo yodhayituṃ yudhi||12||

13viśeṣato vikavacaḥ śrāntaścāpatsamāśritaḥ
|
bhṛśaṃ vikṣatagātraśca śrāntavāhanasainikaḥ||13||

14na me tvatto bhayaṃ rājanna ca pārthādvṛkodarāt
|
phālgunādvāsudevādvā pañcālebhyo'thavā punaḥ||14||

15yamābhyāṃ yuyudhānādvā ye cānye tava sainikāḥ
|
ekaḥ sarvānahaṃ kruddho vārayiṣye yudhi sthitaḥ||15||

16dharmamūlā satāṃ kīrtirmanuṣyāṇāṃ janādhipa
|
dharmaṃ caiveha kīrtiṃ ca pālayanprabravīmyaham||16||

17ahamutthāya sarvānvai pratiyotsyāmi saṃyuge
|
anugamyāgatānsarvānṛtūnsaṃvatsaro yathā||17||

18adyaḥ vaḥ sarathān sāśvānaśastro viratho'pi san
|
nakṣatrāṇīva sarvāṇi savitā rātrisaṃkṣaye||18||

19tejasā nāśayiṣyāmi sthirībhavata pāṇḍavāḥ
|
adyānṛṇyaṃ gamiṣyāmi kṣatriyāṇāṃ yaśasvinām||19||

20bāhlīkadroṇabhīṣmāṇāṃ karṇasya ca mahātmanaḥ
|
jayadrathasya śūrasya bhagadattasya cobhayoḥ||20||

21madrarājasya śalyasya bhūriśravasa eva ca
|
putrāṇāṃ bharataśreṣṭha śakuneḥ saubalasya ca||21||

22mitrāṇāṃ suhṛdāṃ caiva bāndhavānāṃ tathaiva ca
|
ānṛṇyamadya gacchāmi hatvā tvāṃ bhrātṛbhiḥ saha||22||

23etāvaduktvā vacanaṃ virarāma janādhipaḥ
|
yudhiṣṭhira uvāca|
diṣṭyā tvamapi jānīṣe kṣatradharmaṃ suyodhana||23||

24diṣṭyā te vartate buddhiryuddhāyaiva mahābhuja
|
diṣṭyā śūro'si kauravya diṣṭyā jānāsi saṅgaram||24||

25yastvameko hi naḥ sarvānsaṅgare yoddhumicchasi
|
eka ekena saṅgamya yatte sammatamāyudham||25||

26tattvamādāya yudhvasva prekṣakāste vayaṃ sthitāḥ
|
svayamiṣṭaṃ ca te kāmaṃ vīra bhūyo dadāmyaham||26||

27hatvaikaṃ bhavato rājyaṃ hato vā svargamāpnuhi
|
duryodhana uvāca|
ekaścedyoddhamākrande śūro'dya mama dīyatām||27||

28āyudhānāmiyaṃ cāpi vṛtā tvatsammate gadā
|
hantaikaṃ bhavatāmekaḥ śakyaṃ māṃ yo'bhimanyate||28||

29padātirgadayā saṅkhye sa yudhyatu mayā saha
|
vṛttāni rathayuddhāni vicitrāṇi pade pade||29||

30idamekaṃ gadāyuddhaṃ bhavatvadyādbhutaṃ mahat
|
astrāṇāmapi paryāyaṃ kartumicchanti mānavāḥ||30||

31yuddhānāmapi paryāyo bhavatvanumate tava
|
gadayā tvāṃ mahābāho vijeṣyāmi sahānujam||31||

32pañcālān sañjayāṃścaiva ye cānye tava sainikāḥ
|
na hi me sambhramo jātu śakrādapi yudhiṣṭhira||32||

33yudhiṣṭhira uvāca|
uttiṣṭhottiṣṭha gāndhāre māṃ yodhaya suyodhana
|
eka ekena saṅgamya saṃyuge gadayā balī
|
||33||

34puruṣo bhava gāndhāre yudhyasva susamāhitaḥ
|
adya te jīvitaṃ nāsti yadīndro'pi tavāśrayaḥ||34||

35sañjaya uvāca|
etatsa naraśārdūlo nāmṛṣyata tavātmajaḥ
|
salilāntargataḥ śvabhre mahānāga iva śvasan||35||

36tathāsau vākpratodena tudyamānaḥ punaḥ punaḥ
|
vaco na mamṛṣe rājannuttamāśvaḥ kaśāmiva||36||

37saṃkṣobhya salilaṃ vegādgadāmādāya vīryavān
|
adrisāramayīṃ gurvīṃ kāñcanāṅgadabhūṣaṇām||37||

38antarjalātsamuttasthau nāgendra iva niḥśvasan
|
sa bhittvā stambhitaṃ toyaṃ skandhe kṛtvā''yasīṃ gadām||38||

39udatiṣṭhata putraste pratapan raśmivāniva
|
tataḥ śaikyāyasīṃ gurvīṃ jātarūpapariṣkṛtām||39||

40gadāṃ parāmṛśaddhīmān dhārtarāṣṭro mahābalaḥ
|
gadāhastaṃ tu taṃ dṛṣṭvā saśṛṅgamiva parvatam||40||

41prajānāmiva saṅkruddhaṃ śūlapāṇimiva sthitam
|
sagado bhārato bhāti pratapan bhāskaro yathā||41||

42tamuttīrṇaṃ mahābāhuṃ gadāhastamarindamam
|
menire sarvabhūtāni daṇḍapāṇimivāntakam||42||

43vajrahastaṃ yathā śakraḥ śūlahastaṃ yathā haram
|
dadṛśuḥ sarvapañcālāḥ putraṃ tava janādhipa||43||

44tamuttīrṇaṃ tu samprekṣya samahaṣyanta sarvaśaḥ
|
pañcālāḥ pāṇḍaveyāśca te'nyonyasya talāndaduḥ||44||

45avahāsaṃ tu taṃ matvā putro duryodhanastava
|
uddhṛtya nayane kruddho didhakṣuriva pāṇḍavān||45||

46triśikhāṃ bhrukuṭīṃ kṛtvā sandaṣṭadaśanacchadaḥ
|
pratyuvāca tatastānvai pāṇḍavān sahakeśavān||46||

47duryodhana uvāca|
asyāvahāsasya phalaṃ pratibhokṣyatha pāṇḍavāḥ
|
gamiṣyatha hatāḥ sadyaḥ sapañcālā yamakṣayam||47||

48sañjaya uvāca|
utthitaśca jalāttasmātputro duryodhanastava
|
atiṣṭhata gadāpāṇī rudhireṇa samukṣitaḥ||48||

49tasya śoṇitadigdhasya salilena samukṣitam
|
śarīraṃ sma tadā bhāti sravanniva mahīdharaḥ||49||

50tamudyatagadaṃ vīraṃ menire tatra pāṇḍavāḥ
|
vaivasvatamiva kruddhaṃ śūlapāṇimiva sthitam||50||

51sa meghaninado harṣānnardanniva na govṛṣaḥ
|
ājuhāva tataḥ pārthān gadayā yudhi vīryavān||51||

52duryodhana uvāca|
ekaikena ca māṃ yūyamāsīta yudhiṣṭhira
|
na hyeko bahubhirnyāyyo vīro yodhayituṃ yudhi||52||

53nyastavarmā viśeṣeṇa śrāntaścāpsu pariplutaḥ
|
bhṛśaṃ vikṣatagātraśca hatavāhanasainikaḥ||53||

54avaśyameva yoddhavyaṃ sarvaireva mayā saha
|
yuktaṃ tvayuktamityetadvetsi tvaṃ caiva sarvadā||54||

55yudhiṣṭhira uvāca|
mā bhūdiyaṃ tava prajñā kathamevaṃ suyodhana
|
yadābhimanyuṃ bahavo jaghnuryudhi mahārathāḥ||55||

56kṣatradharmaṃ bhṛśaṃ krūraṃ nirapekṣaṃ sunighṛṇam
|
anyathā tu kathaṃ hanyurabhimanyuṃ tathāgatam||56||

57sarve bhavanto dharmajñāḥ sarve śūrāstanutyajaḥ
|
nyāyena yudhyatāṃ proktā śakralokagatiḥ parā||57||

58yadyekastu na hantavyo bahubhirdharma eva tu
|
tadābhimanyuṃ bahavo nijaghnustvanmate katham||58||

59sarvo vimṛśate jantuḥ kṛcchrastho dharmadarśanam
|
padasthaḥ pihitaṃ dvāraṃ paralokasya paśyati||59||

60āmuñca kavacaṃ vīra mūrdhajān yamayasva ca
|
yacānyadapi te nāsti tadapyādatsva bhārata||60||

61imamekaṃ ca te kāmaṃ vīra bhūyo dadāmyaham
|
pañcānāṃ pāṇḍaveyānāṃ yenaṃ tvaṃ yoddhumicchasi||61||

62taṃ hatvā vai bhavān rājā hato vā svargamāpnuhi
|
ṛte ca jīvitādvīra yuddhe kiṃ karma te priyam||62||

63sañjaya uvāca|
tatastava suto rājan varma jagrāha kāñcanam
|
vicitraṃ ca śirastrāṇaṃ jāmbūnadapariṣkṛtam||63||

64so'vabaddhaśirastrāṇaḥ śubhakāñcanavarmabhṛt
|
rarāja rājanputraste kāñcanaḥ śailarāḍiva||64||

65sannaddhaḥ sagado rājan sajjaḥ saṅgrāmamūrdhani
|
abravītpāṇḍavānsarvānputro duryodhanastava||65||

66bhrātṝṇāṃ bhavatāmeko yudhyatāṃ gadayā mayā
|
sahadevena vā yotsye bhīmena nakulena vā||66||

67athavā phālgunenādya tvayā vā bharatarṣabha
|
yotsye'haṃ saṅgaraṃ prāpya vijeṣye ca raṇājire||67||

68ahamadya gamiṣyāmi vairasyāntaṃ sudurgamam
|
gadayā puruṣavyāghra hemapaṭṭanibaddhayā||68||

69gadāyuddhe na me kaścitsadṛśo'stīti cintaye
|
gadayā vo haniṣyāmi sarvāneva samāgatān||69||

70na me samarthāḥ sarve vai yoddhuṃ nyāyena kecana
|
na yuktamātmanā vaktumevaṃ garvoddhataṃ vacaḥ
|
athavā saphalaṃ hyetatkariṣye bhavatāṃ puraḥ||70||

71asmin muhūrte satyaṃ vā mithyā vaitadbhaviṣyati
|
gṛhṇātu ca gadāṃ yo vai yotsyate'dya mayā saha||71||
