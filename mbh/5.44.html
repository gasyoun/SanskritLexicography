Mahābhārata 5.44 (Udyogaparvan)

1dhṛtarāṣṭra uvāca|
sanatsujāta yāmimāṃ parāṃ tvaṃ brāhmīṃ vācaṃ vadase viśvarūpām
|
parāṃ hi kāmeṣu sudurlabhāṃ kathāṃ prabrūhi me vākyamidaṃ kumāra ||1||

2sanatsujāta uvāca|
naitadbrahma tvaramāṇena labhyaṃ yanmāṃ pṛcchannatihṛṣyasyatīva
|
buddhau vilīne manasi pracintyā vidyā hi sā brahmacaryeṇa labhyā||2||

3dhṛtarāṣṭra uvāca|
atyantavidyāmiti yatsanātanīṃ bravīṣi tvaṃ brahmacaryeṇa siddhām
|
anārabhyāṃ vasatīha kāryakāle kathaṃ brāhmaṇyamamṛtatvaṃ labheta||3||

4sanatsujāta uvāca|
avyaktavidyāmabhidhāsye purāṇīṃ buddhyā ca teṣāṃ brahmacaryeṇa siddhām
|
yāṃ prāpyaiva martyaloke tyajanti yā vai vidyā guruvṛddheṣu nityā||4||

5dhṛtarāṣṭra uvāca|
brahmacaryeṇa yā vidyā śakyā veditumañjasā
|
tatkathaṃ brahmacaryaṃ syādetadbrahman bravīmi te||5||

6sanatsujāta uvāca|
ācāryayonimiha ye praviśya bhūtvā garbhe brahmacaryaṃ caranti
|
ihaiva te śāstrakārā bhavanti prahāya dehaṃ paramaṃ yānti yogam ||6||

7asmi~lloke vai jayantīha kāmān brāhmīṃ sthitiṃ hyanutitikṣamāṇāḥ
|
ta ātmānaṃ nirharantīha dehānmuñjādiṣīkāmiva sattvasaṃsthāḥ||7||

8śārīrametau kurutaḥ pitā mātā ca bhārata
|
ācāryaśāstā yā jātiḥ sā puṇyā sājarā'marā||8||

9yaḥ prāvṛṇotyavitathena varṇānṛtaṃ kurvannamṛtaṃ saṃprayacchan
|
taṃ manyeta pitaraṃ mātaraṃ ca tasmai na druhyet kṛtamasya jānan ||9||

10guruṃ śiṣyo nityamabhivādayīta svādhyāyamicchecchucirapramattaḥ
|
mānaṃ na kuryānnādadhīta roṣameṣa prathamo brahmacaryasya pādaḥ ||10||

11śiṣyavṛttikrameṇaiva vidyāmāpnoti yaḥ śuciḥ
|
brahmacaryavratasyāsya prathamaḥ pāda ucyate||11||

12ācāryasya priyaṃ kuryātprāṇairapi dhanairapi
|
karmaṇā manasā vācā dvitīyaḥ pāda ucyate ||12||

13samā gurau yathā vṛttirgurupatnyāṃ tathā''caret
|
tatputre ca tathā kurvan dvitīyaḥ pāda ucyate||13||

14ācāryeṇātmakṛtaṃ vijānan jñātvā cārthaṃ bhāvito'smītyanena
|
yanmanyate taṃ prati hṛṣṭabuddhiḥ sa vai tṛtīyo brahmacaryasya pādaḥ||14||

15nācāryasyānapākṛtya pravāsaṃ prājñaḥ kurvīta naitadahaṃ karomi
|
itīva manyeta na bhāṣayeta sa vai caturtho brahmacaryasya pādaḥ ||15||

16kālena pādaṃ labhate tathā'rthaṃ tataśca pādaṃ guruyogataśca
|
utsāhayogena ca pādamṛcchecchāstreṇa pādaṃ ca tato'bhiyāti||16||

17dharmādayo dvādaśa yasya rūpamanyāni cāṅgāni tathā balaṃ ca
|
ācāryayoge phalatīti cāhurbrahmārthayogena ca brahmacaryam||17||

18evaṃ pravṛtto yadupālabheta vai dhanamācāryāya tadanu prayacchet
|
satāṃ vṛttiṃ bahuguṇāmevameti guroḥ putre bhavati ca vṛttireṣā ||18||

19evaṃ vasan sarvato vardhatīha bahūn putrān labhate ca pratiṣṭhām
|
varṣanti cāsmai pradiśo diśaśca vasantyasmin brahmacarye janāśca ||19||

20etena brahmacaryeṇa devā devatvamāpnuvan
|
ṛṣayaśca mahābhāgā brahmalokaṃ manīṣiṇaḥ ||20||

21gandharvāṇāmanenaiva rūpamapsarasāmabhūt
|
etena brahmacaryeṇa sūryo'pyahnāya jāyate ||21||

22ākāṅkṣyārthasya saṃyogādrasabhedārthināmiva
|
evaṃ hyete samājñāya tādṛgbhāvaṃ gatā ime||22||

23ya āśrayet pāvayeccāpi rājan sarvaṃ śarīraṃ tapasā tapyamānaḥ
|
etena vai bālyamatyeti vidvān mṛtyuṃ tathā sa jayatyantakāle ||23||

24antavantaḥ kṣatriya te jayanti lokān janāḥ karmaṇā nirmalena
|
brahmaiva vidvāṃstena cābhyeti sarvaṃ nānyaḥ panthā ayanāya vidyate ||24||

25dhṛtarāṣṭra uvāca|
ābhāti śuklamiva lohitamivātho kṛṣṇamathāñjanaṃ kādravaṃ vā
|
sadbrahmaṇaḥ paśyati yo'tra vidvān kathaṃ rūpaṃ tadamṛtamakṣaraṃ padam ||25||

26sanatsujāta uvāca|
ābhāti śuklamiva lohitamivātho kṛṣṇamāyasamarkavarṇam
|
na pṛthivyāṃ tiṣṭhati nāntarikṣe naitatsamudre salilaṃ bibharti ||26||

27na tārakāsu na ca vidyudāśritaṃ na cābhreṣu dṛśyate rūpamasya
|
na cāpi vāyau na ca devatāsu naitaccandre dṛśyate nota sūrye ||27||

28naivarkṣu tanna yajuṣṣu nāpyatharvasu na dṛśyate vai vimaleṣu sāmasu
|
rathantare bārhadrathe vā'pi rājan mahāvratenaiva dṛśyet dhruvaṃ tat ||28||

29apāraṇīyaṃ tamasaḥ parastāt tadantako'pyeti vināśakāle
|
aṇīyo rūpaṃ kṣuradhārayā samaṃ mahacca rūpaṃ tadvai parvatebhyaḥ ||29||

30sā pratiṣṭhā tadamṛtaṃ lokāstadbrahma tadyaśaḥ
|
bhūtāni jajñire tasmātpralayaṃ yānti tatra hi ||30||

31anāmayaṃ tanmahadudyataṃ yaśo vāco vikāraṃ kavayo vadanti
|
yasmin jagat sarvamidaṃ pratiṣṭhitaṃ ye tadviduramṛtāste bhavanti ||31||
