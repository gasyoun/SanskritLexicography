Mahābhārata 6.114 (Bhīṣmaparvan)

1sañjaya uvāca|
arjunastu raṇe śalyaṃ yatamānaṃ mahāratham
|
chā‌dayāmāsa samare śaraiḥ sannataparvabhiḥ ||1||

2suśarmāṇaṃ kṛpaṃ caiva tribhistribhiravidhyata
|
prāgjyotiṣaṃ ca samare saindhavaṃ ca jayadratham ||2||

3citrasenaṃ vikarṇaṃ ca kṛtavarmāṇameva ca
|
durmarṣaṇaṃ ca rājendra hyāvantyau ca mahārathau ||3||

4ekaikaṃ tribhirānarcchat kaṅkabarhiṇavājitaiḥ
|
śarairatiratho yuddhe pīḍayan vāhinīṃ tava ||4||

5jayadratho raṇe pārthaṃ viddhvā bhārata sāyakaiḥ
|
bhīmaṃ vivyādha tarasā citrasenarathe sthitaḥ ||5||

6śalyaśca samare jiṣṇuṃ kṛpaśca rathināṃ varaḥ
|
vivyadhāte mahārāja bahudhā marmabhedibhiḥ ||6||

7citrasenādayaścaiva putrāstava viśāṃpate
|
pañcabhiḥ pañcabhistūrṇaṃ saṃyuge niśitaiḥ śaraiḥ ||7||

8ājaghnurarjunaṃ saṃkhye bhīmasenaṃ ca māriṣa
|
tau tatra rathināṃ śreṣṭhau kaunteyau bharatarṣabhau ||8||

9apīḍayetāṃ samare trigartānāṃ mahadbalam
|
suśarmā'pi raṇe pārthaṃ śarairnavabhirāśugaiḥ ||9||

10nanāda balavannādaṃ trāsayāno mahadbalam
|
anye ca rathinaḥ śūrā bhīmasenadhanañjayau ||10||

11vivyadhurniśitairbāṇai rukmapuṅkhairajihmagaiḥ
|
teṣāṃ ca rathināṃ madhye kaunteyau bharatarṣabhau ||11||

12krīḍamānau rathodārau citrarūpau vyadṛśyatām
|
āmiṣepsū gavāṃ madhye siṃhāviva madotkaṭau ||12||

13chittvā dhanūṃṣi śūrāṇāṃ śarāṃśca bahudhā raṇe
|
pātayāmāsaturvīrau śirāṃsi śataśo nṛṇām ||13||

14rathāśca bahavo bhagnā hayāśca śataśo hatāḥ
|
gajāśca sagajārohāḥ petururvyāṃ mahāhave ||14||

15rathinaḥ sādinaścāpi tatra tatra niṣūditāḥ
|
dṛśyante bahavo rājan vepamānāḥ samaṃtataḥ ||15||

16hatairgajapadātyoghairvājibhiśca niṣūditaiḥ
|
rathaiśca bahudhā bhagnaiḥ samāstīryata medinī ||16||

17chatraiśca bahudhā chinnairdhvajaiśca vinipātitaiḥ
|
aṃkuśairapaviddhaiśca paristomaiśca bhārata ||17||

18keyūrairaṅgadairhārai rāṅkavairmṛditaistathā
|
uṣṇīṣairṛṣṭibhiścaiva cāmaravyajanairapi ||18||

19tatra tatrāpaviddhaiśca bāhubhiścandanokṣitaiḥ
|
ūrubhiśca narendrāṇāṃ samāstīryata medinī ||19||

20tatrādbhutamapaśyāma raṇe pārthasya vikramam
|
śaraiḥ saṃvārya tān vīrān yajjaghāna mahābalaḥ ||20||

21putrastu tava taṃ dṛṣṭvā bhīmārjunaparākramam
|
gāṅgeyasya rathābhyāśamupajagme mahābalaḥ ||21||

22kṛpaśca kṛtavarmā ca saindhavaśca jayadrathaḥ
|
vindānuvindāvāvantyau nājahuḥ saṃyugaṃ tadā ||22||

23tato bhīmo maheṣvāsaḥ phālgunaśca mahārathaḥ
|
kauravāṇāṃ camūṃ ghorāṃ bhṛśaṃ dudruvat raṇe ||23||

24tato barhiṇavājānāmayutānyarbudāni ca
|
dhanañjayarathe tūrṇaṃ pātayanti sma bhūmipāḥ ||24||

25tatastān śarajālena sannivārya mahārathān
|
pārthaḥ samantāt samare preṣayāmāsa mṛtyave ||25||

26śalyastu samare jiṣṇuṃ krīḍanniva mahārathaḥ
|
ājaghānorasi kruddho bhallaiḥ sannataparvabhiḥ ||26||

27tasya pārtho dhanuśchitvā hastāvāpaṃ ca pañcabhiḥ
|
athainaṃ sāyakaistīkṣṇairbhṛśaṃ vivyādha marmaṇi ||27||

28athānyaddhanurādāya samare bhārasādhanam
|
madreśvaro raṇe jiṣṇuṃ tāḍayāmāsa roṣitaḥ ||28||

29tribhiḥ śarairmahārāja vāsudevaṃ ca pañcabhiḥ
|
bhīmasenaṃ ca navabhirbāhvorurasi cārpayat ||29||

30tato droṇo mahārāja māgadhaśca mahārathaḥ
|
duryodhanasamādiṣṭau taṃ deśamupajagmatuḥ ||30||

31yatra pārtho mahārāja bhīmasenaśca pāṇḍavaḥ
|
kauravyasya mahāsenāṃ jaghnatuḥ sumahārathau ||31||

32jayatsenastu samare bhīmaṃ bhīmāyudhaṃ yudhi
|
vivyādha niśitairbāṇairaṣṭabhirbharatarṣabha ||32||

33taṃ bhīmo daśabhirviddhvā punarvivyādha pañcabhiḥ
|
sārathiṃ cāsya bhallena rathanīḍā‌dapātayat ||33||

34udbhrāntaisturagaiḥ so'tha dravamāṇaiḥ samantataḥ
|
māgadho'pasṛto rājā sarvasainyasya paśyataḥ ||34||

35droṇaśca vivaraṃ dṛṣṭvā bhīmasenaṃ śilīmukhaiḥ
|
vivyādha bāṇairniśitaiḥ pañcaṣaṣṭibhirāyasaiḥ ||35||

36taṃ bhīmaḥ samaraślāghī guruṃ pitṛsamaṃ raṇe
|
vivyādha pañcabhirbhallaistathā ṣaṣṭyā ca bhārata ||36||

37arjunastu suśarmāṇaṃ viddhvā bahubhirāyasaiḥ
|
vyadhamattasya tatsainyaṃ mahābhrāṇi yathā'nilaḥ ||37||

38tato bhīṣmaśca rājā ca kausalyaśca bṛhadbalaḥ
|
samavatanta saṃkruddhā bhīmasenadhanañjayau ||38||

39tathaiva pāṇḍavāḥ śūrā dhṛṣṭadyumnaśca pārṣataḥ
|
abhyadravanraṇe bhīṣmaṃ vyāditāsyamivāntakam ||39||

40śikhaṇḍī tu samāsādya bharatānāṃ pitāmaham
|
abhyadravata saṃhṛṣṭo bhayaṃ tyaktvā mahārathāt ||40||

41yudhiṣṭhiramukhāḥ pārthāḥ puraskṛtya śikhaṇḍinam
|
ayodhayan raṇe bhīṣmaṃ sahitāḥ sarvasṛñjayaiḥ ||41||

42tathaiva tāvakāḥ sarve puraskṛtya yatavratam
|
śikhaṇḍipramukhān pārthānyodhayanti sma saṃyuge ||42||

43tataḥ pravavṛte yuddhaṃ kauravāṇāṃ bhayāvaham
|
tatra pāṇḍusutaiḥ sārdhaṃ bhīṣmasya vijayaṃ prati ||43||

44tāvakānāṃ jaye bhīṣmo glaha āsīdviśāṃpate
|
tatra hi dyūtamāsaktaṃ vijayāyetarāya vā ||44||

45dhṛṣṭadyumnastu rājendra sarvasainyānyacodayat
|
abhyadravata gāṃgeyaṃ mā bhaiṣṭa rathasattamāḥ ||45||

46senāpativacaḥ śrutvā pāṇḍavānāṃ varūthinī
|
bhīṣmaṃ samabhyayāt tūrṇaṃ prāṇāṃstyaktvā mahāhave ||46||

47bhīṣmo'pi rathināṃ śreṣṭhaḥ pratijagrāha tāṃ camūm
|
āpatantīṃ mahārāja velāmiva mahodadhiḥ ||47||
