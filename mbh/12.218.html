Mahābhārata 12.218 (Śāntiparvan)

1yudhiṣṭhira uvāca|
kena vṛttena vṛttajña janako mithilādhipaḥ
|
jagāma mokṣaṃ mokṣajño bhogānutsṛjya mānuṣān ||1||

2bhīṣma uvāca|
atrāpyudāharantīmamitihāsaṃ purātanam
|
yena vṛttena dharmajñaḥ sa jagāma mahatsukham ||2||

3janako janadevastu mithilāyāṃ janādhipaḥ
|
aurdhvadehikadharmāṇāmāsīdyukto vicintane ||3||

4tasya sma śatamācāryā vasanti satataṃ gṛhe
|
darśayantaḥ pṛthagdharmānnānāśramanivāsinaḥ ||4||

5sa teṣāṃ pretya bhāve ca pretya jātau viniścaye
|
āgamasthaḥ sa bhūyiṣṭhamātmatattve na tuṣyati ||5||

6tatra pañcaśikho nāma kāpileyo mahāmuniḥ
|
paridhāvanmahīṃ kṛtsnāṃ jagāma mithilāmatha ||6||

7sarvasaṃnyāsadharmāṇāṃ tattvajñānaviniścaye
|
suparyavasitārthaśca nirdvandvo naṣṭasaṃśayaḥ ||7||

8ṛṣīṇāmāhurekaṃ taṃ yaṃ kāmānāvṛtaṃ nṛṣu
|
śāśvataṃ sukhamatyantamanvicchantaṃ sudurlabham ||8||

9yamāhuḥ kapilaṃ sāṃkhyāḥ paramarṣiṃ prajāpatim
|
sa manye tena rūpeṇa vismāpayati hi svayam ||9||

10āsureḥ prathamaṃ śiṣyaṃ yamāhuścirajīvinam
|
pañcasrotasi yaḥ satramāste varṣasahasrikam ||10||

11yatra cāsīnamāgamya kāpilaṃ maṇḍalaṃ mahat
|
pañcasrotasi niṣṇātaḥ pañcarātriviśāradaḥ ||11||

12pañcajñaḥ pañcakṛtpañcaguṇaḥ pañcaśikhaḥ smṛtaḥ
|
puruṣāvasthamavyaktaṃ paramārthaṃ nyavedayat ||12||

13iṣṭasatreṇa saṃpṛṣṭo bhūyaśca tapasā''suriḥ
|
kṣetrakṣetrajñayorvyaktiṃ bubudhe devadarśanaḥ ||13||

14yattadekākṣaraṃ brahma nānārūpaṃ pradṛśyate
|
āsurirmaṇḍale tasminpratipede tadavyayam ||14||

15tasya pañcaśikhaḥ śiṣyo mānuṣyā payasā bhṛtaḥ
|
brāhmaṇī kapilā nāma kācidāsītkuṭumbinī ||15||

16tasyāḥ putratvamāgamya striyāḥ sa pibati stanau
|
tataḥ sa kāpileyatvaṃ lebhe buddhiṃ ca naiṣṭhikīm ||16||

17etanme bhagavānāha kāpileyasya sambhavam
|
tasya tatkāpileyatvaṃ sarvavittvamanuttam ||17||

18sāmānyaṃ janakaṃ jñātvā dharmajño jñānamuttamam
|
upetya śatamācāryānmohayāmāsa hetubhiḥ ||18||

19janakastvabhisaṃraktaḥ kāpileyānudarśanāt
|
utsṛjya śatamācāryānpṛṣṭhato'nujagāma tam||19||

20tasmai paramakalyāya praṇatāya ca dharmataḥ
|
abravītparamaṃ mokṣaṃ yattatsāṃkhye'bhidhīyate||20||

21jātinirvedamuktvā sa karmanirvedamabravīt
|
karmanirvedamuktvā ca sarvanirvedamabravīt ||21||

22yadarthaṃ dharmasaṃsargaḥ karmaṇāṃ ca phalodayaḥ
|
tamanāśvāsikaṃ mohaṃ vināśi calamadhruvam ||22||

23dṛśyamāne vināśe ca pratyakṣe lokasākṣike
|
āgamātparamastīti bruvannapi parājitaḥ ||23||

24anātmā hyātmano mṛtyuḥ kleśo mṛtyurjarāmayaḥ
|
ātmānaṃ manyate mohāttadasamyak paraṃ matam ||24||

25atha cedevamapyasti yalloke nopapadyate
|
ajaro'yamamṛtyuśca rājā'sau manyate yathā ||25||

26asti nāstīti cāpyetattasminnasati lakṣaṇe
|
kimadhiṣṭhāya tadbrūyāllokayātrāviniścayam ||26||

27pratyakṣaṃ hyetayormūlaṃ kṛtāntaitihyayorapi
|
pratyakṣeṇāgamo bhinnaḥ kṛtānto vā na kiñcana ||27||

28yatra yatrānumāne'smin kṛtaṃ bhāvayato'pi ca
|
nānyo jīvaḥ śarīrasya nāstikānāṃ mate sthitaḥ ||28||

29reto vaṭakaṇīkāyāṃ ghṛtapākādhivāsanam
|
jātiḥ smṛtirayaskāntaḥ sūryakānto'mbubhakṣaṇam ||29||

30pretībhūte'tyayaścaiva devatādhupayācanam
|
mṛte karmanivṛttiśca pramāṇamiti niścayaḥ ||30||

31nanvete hetavaḥ santi ye kecinmūrtisaṃsthitāḥ
|
amūrtasya hi mūrtena sāmānyaṃ nopapadyate ||31||

32avidyākarmaceṣṭānāṃ kecidāhuḥ punarbhave
|
kāraṇaṃ lobhamohau tu doṣāṇāṃ tu niṣevaṇam ||32||

33avidyāṃ kṣetramāhurhi karmabījaṃ tathā kṛtam
|
tṛṣṇāsaṃjananaṃ sneha eṣa teṣāṃ punarbhavaḥ ||33||

34tasmin gūḍhe ca dagdhe ca bhinne maraṇadharmiṇi
|
anyo'smājjāyate dehastamāhuḥ sattvasaṃkṣayam ||34||

35yadā svarūpataścānyo jātitaḥ śubhato'rthataḥ
|
kathamasminsa ityevaṃ sarvaṃ vā syādasaṃhitam ||35||

36evaṃ sati ca kā prītirdāna-vidyā-tapo-balaiḥ
|
yadasyācaritaṃ karma sarvamanyatprapadyate ||36||

37api hyayamihaivānyaiḥ prākkṛtairduḥkhito bhavet
|
sukhito duḥkhito vā'pi dṛśyādṛśyavinirṇayaḥ ||37||

38tathāhi musalairhanyuḥ śarīraṃ tatpunarbhavet
|
pṛthagjñānaṃ yadanyacca yenaitannopapadyate ||38||

39ṛtusaṃvatsarau tiṣyaḥ śītoṣṇe'tha priyāpriye
|
yathā'tītāni paśyanti tādṛśaḥ sattvasaṃkṣayaḥ ||39||

40jarayābhiparītasya mṛtyunā ca vināśinā
|
durbalaṃ durbalaṃ pūrvaṃ gṛhasyeva vinaśyati ||40||

41indriyāṇi mano vāyuḥ śoṇitaṃ māṃsamasthi ca
|
ānupūrvyā vinaśyanti svaṃ dhātumupayānti ca ||41||

42lokayātrāvighātaśca dānadharmaphalāgame
|
tadarthaṃ vedaśabdāśca vyavahārāśca laukikāḥ ||42||

43iti samyaṅmanasyate bahavaḥ santi hetavaḥ
|
etadastīdamastīti na kiñcitpratidṛśyate ||43||

44teṣāṃ vimṛśatāmeva tattatsamabhidhāvatām
|
kvacinniviśate buddhistatra jīryati vṛkṣavat ||44||

45evamarthairanarthaiśca duḥkhitāḥ sarvajantavaḥ
|
āgamairapakṛṣyante hastipairhastino yathā ||45||

46arthāṃstathā'tyantasukhāvahāṃśca lipsanta ete bahavo viśuṣkāḥ
|
mahattaraṃ duḥkhamanuprapannā hitvā''miṣaṃ mṛtyuvaśaṃ prayānti ||46||

47vināśino hyadhruvajīvitasya kiṃ bandhabhirbhinnaparigrahaiśca
|
vihāya yo gacchati sarvameva kṣaṇena gatvā na nivartate ca ||47||

48bhūvyomatoyānalavāyavo'pi sadā śarīraṃ pratipālayanti
|
itīdamālakṣya ratiḥ kuto bhavedvināśino'pyasya na śarma vidyate ||48||

49idamanupadhivākyamacchalaṃ paramanirāmayamātmasākṣikam
|
narapatirabhivīkṣya vismitaḥ punaranuyoktumidaṃ pracakrame ||49||
