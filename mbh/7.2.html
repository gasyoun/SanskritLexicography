Mahābhārata 7.2 (Droṇaparvan)

1sañjaya uvāca|
hataṃ bhīṣmamathādhirathirviditvā bhinnāṃ nāvamivātyagādhe kurūṇām
|
sodaryavadvyasanāt sūtaputraḥ santārayiṣyaṃstava putrasya senām||1||

2śrutvā tu karṇaḥ puruṣendramacyutaṃ nipātitaṃ śāntanavaṃ mahāratham
|
athopayāyāt sahasā'rikarṣaṇo dhanurdharāṇāṃ pravarastadā nṛpa||2||

3hate tu bhīṣme rathasattame parairnimajjatīṃ nāvamivārṇave kurūn
|
piteva putrāṃstvarito'bhyayāt tataḥ saṃtārayiṣyaṃstava putrasya senām||3||

4karṇa uvāca|
yasmin dhṛtirbuddhiparākramaujaḥ satyaṃ smṛtirvīraguṇāśca sarve
|
astrāṇi divyānyatha saṃnatirhrīḥ priyā ca vāganasūyā ca bhīṣme||4||

5sadā kṛtajñe dvijaśatrughātake sanātanaṃ candramasīva lakṣma
|
sa cetpraśāntaḥ paravīrahantā manye hatāneva ca sarvavīrān||5||

6neha dhruvaṃ kiṃcana jātu vidyate loke hyasmin karmaṇo'nityayogāt
|
sūryodaye ko hi vimuktasaṃśayo bhāvaṃ kurvītārya mahāvrate hate||6||

7vasuprabhāve vasuvīryasaṃbhave gate vasūneva vasundharādhipe
|
vasūni putrāṃśca vasundharāṃ tathā kurūṃśca śocadhvamimāṃ ca vāhinīm||7||

8sañjaya uvāca|
mahāprabhāve varade nipātite lokeśvare śāstari cāmitaujasi
|
parājiteṣu bharateṣu durmanāḥ karṇo bhṛśaṃ nyaśvasadaśru vartayan||8||

9idaṃ ca rādheyavaco niśamya sutāśca rājaṃstava sainikāśca ha
|
parasparaṃ cukruśurārtijaṃ muhustadāśru netrairmumucuśca śabdavat||9||

10pravartamāne tu punarmahāhave vigāhyamānāsu camūṣu pārthivaiḥ
|
athābravīddharṣakaraṃ tadā vaco ratharṣabhān sarvamahāratharṣabhaḥ||10||

11jagatyanitye satataṃ pradhāvati pracintayannasthiramadya lakṣaye
|
bhavatsu tiṣṭhatsviha pātito mṛdhe giriprakāśaḥ kurupuṅgavaḥ katham||11||

12nipātite śāntanave mahārathe divākare bhūtalamāsthite yathā
|
na pārthivāḥ soḍhumalaṃ dhanañjayaṃ giripravoḍhāramivānilaṃ drumāḥ||12||

13hatapradhānaṃ tvidamārtarūpaṃ parairhatotsāhamanāthamadya vai
|
mayā kurūṇāṃ paripālyamāhave balaṃ yathā tena mahātmanā tathā||13||

14samāhitaṃ cātmani bhāramīdṛśaṃ jagat tathā nityamidaṃ ca lakṣaye
|
nipātitaṃ cāhavaśauṇḍamāhave kathaṃ nu kuryāmahamīdṛśe bhayam||14||

15ahaṃ tu tān kuruvṛṣabhānajihmagaiḥ praveśayan yamasadanaṃ caran raṇe
|
yaśaḥ paraṃ jagati vibhāvya vartitā parairhato bhuvi śayitā'thavā punaḥ||15||

16yudhiṣṭhiro dhṛtimatisatyasattvavān vṛkodaro gajaśatatulyavikramaḥ
|
tathā'rjunastridaśavarātmajo yuvā na tadbalaṃ sujayamihāmarairapi||16||

17yamau raṇe yatra yamopamau bale sasātyakiryatra ca devakīsutaḥ
|
na tadbalaṃ kāpuruṣo'bhyupeyivānnivartate mṛtyumukhānna cāsubhṛt||17||

18tapo'bhyudīrṇaṃ tapasaiva bādhyate balaṃ balenaiva tathā manasvibhiḥ
|
manaśca me śatrunivāraṇe dhruvaṃ svarakṣaṇe cācalavadvyavasthitam||18||

19evaṃ caiṣāṃ bādhamānaḥ prabhāvaṃ gatvaivāhaṃ tāñjayāmyadya sūta
|
mitradroho marṣaṇīyo na me'yaṃ bhagne sainye yaḥ sameyāt sa mitram||19||

20kartāsmyetat satpuruṣāryakarma tyaktvā prāṇānanuyāsyāmi bhīṣmam
|
sarvān saṅkhye śatrusaṅghān haniṣye hatastairvā vīralokaṃ prapatsye||20||

21saṃprākruṣṭe ruditastrīkumāre parāhate pauruṣe dhārtarāṣṭre
|
mayā kṛtyamiti jānāmi sūta tasmādrājñastvadya śatrūn vijeṣye||21||

22kurūn rakṣan pāṇḍuputrān jighāṃsaṃstyaktvā prāṇān ghorarūpe raṇe'smin
|
sarvān saṅkhye śatrusaṅghānnihatya dāsyāmyahaṃ dhārtarāṣṭrāya rājyam||22||

23nibadhyatāṃ me kavacaṃ vicitraṃ haimaṃ śubhraṃ maṇiratnāvabhāsi
|
śirastrāṇaṃ cārkasamānabhāsaṃ dhanuḥśarāṃścāgniviṣāhikalpān||23||

24upāsaṅgān ṣoḍaśa yojayantu dhanūṃṣi divyāni tathā''harantu
|
asīṃśca śaktīśca gadāśca gurvīḥ śaṅkhaṃ ca jāmbūnadacitranālam||24||

25imāṃ raukmīṃ nāgakakṣyāṃ vicitrāṃ dhvajaṃ citraṃ divyamindīvarāṅkam
|
ślakṣṇairvastrairvipramṛjyānayantu citrāṃ mālāṃ cārubaddhāṃ salājām||25||

26aśvānagryān pāṇḍurābhraprakāśān puṣṭān snātān mantrapūtābhiradbhiḥ
|
taptairbhāṇḍaiḥ kāñcanairabhyupetān śīghrāñśīghraṃ sūtaputrānayasva||26||

27rathaṃ cāgryaṃ hemamālāvanaddhaṃ ratnaiścitraṃ sūryacandraprakāśaiḥ
|
dravyairyuktaṃ saṃprahāropapannairvāhairyuktaṃ tūrṇamāvartayasva||27||

28citrāṇi cāpāni ca vegavanti jyāścottamāḥ sannahanopapannāḥ
|
tūṇāṃśca pūrṇānmahataḥ śarāṇāmāsādya gātrāvaraṇāni caiva||28||

29prāyātrikaṃ cānayatāśu sarvaṃ dadhnā pūrṇaṃ vīra kāṃsyaṃ ca haimam
|
ānīya mālāmavabadhya cāṅge pravādayantvāśu jayāya bherīḥ||29||

30prayāhi sūtāśu yataḥ kirīṭī vṛkodaro dharmasuto yamau ca
|
tān vā haniṣyāmi sametya saṅkhye bhīṣmāya gacchāmi hato dviṣadbhiḥ||30||

31yasmin rājā satyadhṛtiryudhiṣṭhiraḥ samāsthito bhīmasenārjunau ca
|
vāsudevaḥ sātyakiḥ sṛñjayāśca manye balaṃ tadajayyaṃ mahīpaiḥ||31||

32taṃ cenmṛtyuḥ sarvaharo'bhirakṣet sadā'pramattaḥ samare kirīṭinam
|
tathā'pi hantā'smi sametya saṅkhye yāsyāmi vā bhīṣmapathā yamāya||32||

33na tvevāhaṃ na gamiṣyāmi teṣāṃ madhye śūrāṇāṃ tatra cāhaṃ bravīmi
|
mitradruho durbalabhaktayo ye pāpātmāno na mamaite sahāyāḥ||33||

34sañjaya uvāca|
samṛddhimantaṃ rathamuttamaṃ dṛḍhaṃ sakūbaraṃ hemapariṣkṛtaṃ śubham
|
patākinaṃ vātajavairhayottamairyuktaṃ samāsthāya yayau jayāya||34||

35saṃpūjyamānaḥ kurubhirmahātmā ratharṣabho devagaṇairyathendraḥ
|
yayau tadāyodhanamugradhanvā yatrāvasānaṃ bharatarṣabhasya||35||

36varūthinā mahatā sadhvajena suvarṇamuktāmaṇiratnamālinā
|
sadaśvayuktena rathena karṇo meghasvanenārka ivāmitaujāḥ||36||

37hutāśanābhaḥ sa hutāśanaprabhe śubhaḥ śubhe vai svarathe dhanurdharaḥ
|
sthito rarājādhirathirmahārathaḥ svayaṃ vimāne surarāḍivāsthitaḥ||37||
