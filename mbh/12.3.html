Mahābhārata 12.3 (Śāntiparvan)

1nārada uvāca|
karṇasya bāhuvīryeṇa praṇayena damena ca
|
tutoṣa bhṛguśārdūlo guruśuśrūṣayā tathā ||1||

2tasmai sa vidhivatkṛtsnaṃ brahmāstraṃ sanivartanam
|
provācākhilamavyagraṃ tapasvī tattapasvine ||2||

3viditāstrastataḥ karṇo ramamāṇo''śrame bhṛgoḥ
|
cakāra vai dhanurvede yatnamadbhutavikramaḥ ||3||

4tataḥ kadācidrāmastu carannāśramamantikāt
|
karṇena sahito dhīmānupavāsena karśitaḥ ||4||

5suṣvāpa jāmadagnyastu viśraṃbhotpannasauhṛdaḥ
|
karṇasyotsaṅga ādhāya śiraḥ klāntamanā guruḥ ||5||

6atha kṛmiḥ śleṣma-medo-māṃsa-śoṇitabhojanaḥ
|
dāruṇo dāruṇasparśaḥ karṇasyābhyāśamāgataḥ ||6||

7sa tasyorumathāsādya bibheda rudhirāśanaḥ
|
na cainamaśakatkṣeptuṃ hantuṃ vāpi gurorbhayāt ||7||

8saṃdaśyamānastu tathā kṛmiṇā tena bhārata
|
guroḥ prabodhanāśaṅkī tamupaikṣata sūryajaḥ||8||

9karṇastu vedanāṃ dhairyādasahyāṃ vinigṛhya tām
|
akampayannavyathayan dhārayāmāsa bhārgavam ||9||

10yadā'sya rudhireṇāṅgaṃ parispṛṣṭaṃ bhṛgūdvahaḥ
|
tadā'buddhyata tejasvī saṃtrastaścedamabravīt ||10||

11ahosmyaśucitāṃ prāptaḥ kimidaṃ kriyate tvayā
|
kathayasva bhayaṃ tyaktvā yāthātathyamidaṃ mama ||11||

12tasya karṇastadā''caṣṭa kṛmiṇā paribhakṣaṇam
|
dadarśa rāmastaṃ cāpi kṛmiṃ sūkarasannibham ||12||

13aṣṭapādaṃ tīkṣṇadaṃṣṭraṃ sucībhiriva saṃvṛtam
|
romabhiḥ sanniruddhāṅgamalarkaṃ nāma nāmataḥ ||13||

14sa dṛṣṭamātro rāmeṇa kṛmiḥ prāṇānavāsṛjat
|
tasminnevāsṛji klinnastada‌dbhutamivābhavat ||14||

15tato'ntarikṣe dadṛśe viśvarūpaḥ karālavān
|
rākṣaso lohitagrīvaḥ kṛṣṇāṅgo meghavāhanaḥ ||15||

16sa rāmaṃ prāñjalirbhūtvā babhāṣe pūrṇamānasaḥ
|
svasti te bhṛguśārdūla gamiṣye'haṃ yathāgatam ||16||

17mokṣito narakādasmādbhavatā munisattama
|
bhadraṃ tavāstu vande tvāṃ priyaṃ me bhavatā kṛtam ||17||

18tamuvāca mahābāhurjāmadagnyaḥ pratāpavān
|
kastvaṃ kasmācca narakaṃ pratipanno bravīhi tat ||18||

19so'bravīda‌‌hamāsaṃ prāk daṃśo nāma mahāsuraḥ
|
purā devayuge tāta bhṛgostulyavayā iva ||19||

20so'haṃ bhṛgoḥ suda‌yitāṃ bhāryāmapaharaṃ balāt
|
maharṣerabhiśāpena kṛmibhūto'pataṃ bhuvi ||20||

21abravīddhi sa māṃ kruddhastava pūrvapitāmahaḥ
|
mūtraśleṣmāśanaḥ pāpa nirayaṃ pratipatsyase ||21||

22śāpasyānto bhavedbrahmannityevaṃ tamathābruvam
|
bhavitā bhārgavādrāmāditi māmabravīdbhṛguḥ||22||

23so'hamenāṃ gatiṃ prāpto yathā nakuśalaṃ tathā
|
tvayā sādho samāgamya vimuktaḥ pāpayonitaḥ ||23||

24evamuktvā namaskṛtya yayau rāmaṃ mahāsuraḥ
|
rāmaḥ karṇaṃ ca sakrodhamidaṃ vacanamabravīt ||24||

25atiduḥkhamidaṃ mūḍha na jātu brāhmaṇaḥ sahet
|
kṣatriyasyeva te dhairyaṃ kāmayā satyamucyatām ||25||

26tamuvāca tataḥ karṇaḥ śāpādbhītaḥ prasādayan
|
brahmakṣatrāntare jātaṃ sutaṃ māṃ viddhi bhārgava ||26||

27rādheyaḥ karṇa iti māṃ pravadanti janā bhuvi
|
prasādaṃ kuru me brahmannastralubdhasya bhārgava ||27||

28pitā gururna saṃdeho vedavidyāpradaḥ prabhuḥ
|
ato bhārgava ityuktaṃ mayā gotraṃ tavāntike ||28||

29tamuvāca bhṛguśreṣṭhaḥ saroṣaḥ pradahanniva
|
bhūmau nipatitaṃ dīnaṃ vepamānaṃ kṛtāñjalim ||29||

30yasmānmithyopacarito hyastralobhādiha tvayā
|
tasmādetanna te mūḍha brahmāstraṃ pratibhāsyati ||30||

31anyatra vadhakālātte sadṛśena samīyuṣaḥ
|
abrāhmaṇena hi brahma dhruvaṃ tiṣṭhetkadācana ||31||

32gacchedānīṃ na te sthānamanṛtasyeha vidyate
|
na tvayā sadṛśo yuddhe bhavitā kṣatriyo bhuvi ||32||

33evamuktaḥ sa rāmeṇa nyāyenopajagāma ha
|
duryodhanamupāgamya kṛtāstro'smīti cābravīt ||33||
