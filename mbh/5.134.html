Mahābhārata 5.134 (Udyogaparvan)

1vidulovāca
|
athaitasyāmavasthāyāṃ pauruṣaṃ hātumicchasi
|
nihīnasevitaṃ mārgaṃ gamiṣyasyacirādiva ||1||

2yo hi tejo yathāśakti na darśayati vikramāt
|
kṣatriyo jīvitākāṅkṣī stena ityeva taṃ viduḥ ||2||

3arthavantyupapannāni vākyāni guṇavanti ca
|
naiva saṃprāpnuvanti tvāṃ mumūrṣumiva bhaiṣajam ||3||

4santi vai sindhurājasya santuṣṭā na tathā janāḥ
|
daurbalyādāsate mūḍhā vyasanaughapratīkṣiṇaḥ ||4||

5sahāyopacitiṃ kṛtvā vyavasāyya tatastataḥ
|
anuduṣyeyurapare paśyantastava pauruṣam ||5||

6taiḥ kṛtvā saha saṅghātaṃ giridurgālayaṃ cara
|
kāle vyasanamākāṅkṣannaivāyamajarāmaraḥ ||6||

7sañjayo nāmataśca tvaṃ na ca paśyāmi tattvayi
|
anvarthanāmā bhava me putra mā vyarthanāmakaḥ ||7||

8samyagdṛṣṭirmahāprājño bālaṃ tvāṃ brāhmaṇo'bravīt
|
ayaṃ prāpya mahatkṛcchraṃ punarvṛddhiṃ gamiṣyati ||8||

9tasya smarantī vacanamāśaṃse vijayaṃ tava
|
tasmāttāta bravīmi tvāṃ vakṣyāmi ca punaḥ punaḥ ||9||

10yasya hyarthābhinirvṛttau bhavantyāpyāyitāḥ pare
|
tasyārthasiddhirniyatā nayeṣvarthānusāriṇaḥ ||10||

11samṛddhirasamṛddhirvā pūrveṣāṃ mama sañjaya
|
evaṃ vidvānyuddhamanā bhava mā pratyupāharaḥ ||11||

12nātaḥ pāpīyasīṃ kāñcidavasthāṃ śambaro'bravīt
|
yatra naivādya na prātarbhojanaṃ pratidṛśyate ||12||

13patiputravadhādetat paramaṃ duḥkhamabravīt
|
dāridryamiti yatproktaṃ paryāyamaraṇaṃ hi tat ||13||

14ahaṃ mahākule jātā hradāddhradamivāgatā
|
īśvarī sarvakalyāṇī bhartrā paramapūjitā ||14||

15mahārhamālyābharaṇāṃ sumṛṣṭāmbaravāsasam
|
purā hṛṣṭaḥ suhṛdvargo māmapaśyat suhṛdgatām ||15||

16yadā māṃ caiva bhāryāṃ ca draṣṭā'si bhṛśadurbalām
|
na tadā jīvitenārtho bhavitā tava sañjaya ||16||

17dāsakarmakarān bhṛtyānācāryartvikpurohitān
|
avṛttyā'smān prajahato dṛṣṭvā kiṃ jīvitena te ||17||

18yadi kṛtyaṃ na paśyāmi tavādyāhaṃ yathā purā
|
ślāghanīyaṃ yaśasyaṃ ca kā śāntirhṛdayasya me ||18||

19neti cedbrāhmaṇaṃ brūyāṃ dīryeta hṛdayaṃ mama
|
na hyahaṃ na ca me bhartā neti brāhmaṇamuktavān ||19||

20vayamāśrayaṇīyāḥ sma na śrotāraḥ parasya ca
|
sā'nyamāsādya jīvantī parityakṣyāmi jīvitam ||20||

21apāre bhava naḥ pāramaplave bhava naḥ plavaḥ
|
kuruṣva sthānamasthāne mṛtān sañjīvayasva naḥ||21||

22sarve te śatravaḥ śakyā na cejjīvitumarhasi
|
atha cedīdṛśīṃ vṛttiṃ klībāmabhyupapadyase ||22||

23nirviṇṇātmā hatamanā muñcaitāṃ pāpajīvikām
|
ekaśatruvadhenaiva śūro gacchati viśrutim ||23||

24indro vṛtravadhenaiva mahendraḥ samapadyata
|
māhedraṃ ca gṛhaṃ lebhe lokānāṃ ceśvaro'bhavat ||24||

25nāma viśrāvya vai saṃkhye śatrūnāhūya daṃśitān
|
senāgraṃ cāpi vidrāvya hatvā vā puruṣaṃ varam ||25||

26yadaiva labhate vīraḥ suyuddhena mahadyaśaḥ
|
yadaiva pravyathante'sya śatravo vinamanti ca ||26||

27tyaktvā''tmānaṃ raṇe dakṣaṃ śūraṃ kāpuruṣā janāḥ
|
avaśāstarpayanti sma sarvakāmasamṛddhibhiḥ ||27||

28rājyaṃ cāpyugravibhraṃśaṃ saṃśayo jīvitasya vā
|
na labdhasya hi śatrorvai śeṣaṃ kurvanti sādhavaḥ ||28||

29svargadvāropamaṃ rājyamathavā'pyamṛtopamam
|
ruddhamekāyanaṃ matvā patolmuka ivāriṣu ||29||

30jahi śatrūnraṇe rājan svadharmamanupālaya
|
mā tvādṛśaṃ sukṛpaṇaṃ śatrūṇāṃ bhayavardhanam ||30||

31asmadīyaiśca śocadbhirnadadbhiśca parairvṛtam
|
api tvāṃ nānupaśyeyaṃ dīnāddīnamivāsthitam ||31||

32hṛṣya sauvīrakanyābhiḥ ślāghasvārthairyathā purā
|
mā ca saindhavakanyānāmavasanno vaśaṃ gamaḥ ||32||

33yuvā rūpeṇa saṃpanno vidyayā'bhijanena ca
|
yattvādṛśo vikurvīta yaśasvī lokaviśrutaḥ ||33||

34adhuryavacca voḍhavye manye maraṇameva tat
|
yadi tvāmanupaśyāmi parasya priyavādinam ||34||

35pṛṣṭhato'nuvrajantaṃ vā kā śāntirhṛdayasya me
|
nāsmin jātu kule jāto gacchedyo'nyasya pṛṣṭhataḥ ||35||

36na tvaṃ parasyānucarastāta jīvitumarhasi
|
ahaṃ hi kṣatrahṛdayaṃ veda yat pariśāśvatam ||36||

37pūrvaiḥ pūrvataraiḥ proktaṃ paraiḥ paratarairapi
|
śāśvataṃ cāvyayaṃ caiva prajāpativinirmitam ||37||

38yo vai kaścidihājātaḥ kṣatriyaḥ kṣatrakarmavit
|
bhayādvṛttisamīkṣo vā na namediha kasyacit ||38||

39udyacchedeva na namedudyamo hyeva pauruṣam
|
apyaparvaṇi bhajyeta na nameteha kasyacit ||39||

40mātaṅgo matta iva ca parīyāt sa mahāmanāḥ
|
brāhmaṇebhyo namennityaṃ dharmāyaiva ca sañjaya ||40||

41niyacchannitarān varṇān vinighnan sarvaduṣkṛtaḥ
|
sasahāyo'sahāyo vā yāvajjīvaṃ tathā bhavet ||41||
