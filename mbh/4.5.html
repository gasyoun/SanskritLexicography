Mahābhārata 4.5 (Virāṭaparvan)

1vaiśampāyana uvāca|te vīrā baddhanistriṃśāstathā baddhakalāpinaḥ
|
baddhagodhāṅgulitrāṇāḥ kālindīmabhito yayuḥ ||1||

2tataste dakṣiṇaṃ tīramanvagacchan padātayaḥ
|
nivṛttavanavāsā hi svarāṣṭraṃ prepsavastadā
|
vasanto giridurgeṣu vanadurgeṣu dhanvinaḥ ||2||

3vidhyanto mṛgajātāni maheṣvāsā mahābalāḥ
|
uttareṇa daśārṇāṃste pañcālān dakṣiṇena ca ||3||

4antareṇa yakṛllomān śūrasenāṃśca pāṇḍavāḥ
|
lubdhā bruvāṇā matsyasya viṣayaṃ prāviśan vanāt ||4||

5dhanvino baddhanistriṃśā vivarṇāḥ śmaśrudhāriṇaḥ
|
tato janapadaṃ prāpya kṛṣṇā rājānamabravīt ||5||

6paśyaikapadyo dṛśyante kṣetrāṇi vividhāni ca
|
vyaktaṃ dūre virāṭasya rājadhānī bhaviṣyati
|
vasāmehāparāṃ rātri balavān me pariśramaḥ ||6||

7yudhiṣṭhira uvāca|dhanañjaya samudyamya pāñcālīṃ vaha bhārata
|
rājadhānyāṃ nivatsyāmo vimuktāśca vanāditaḥ ||7||

8vaiśampāyana uvāca|tāmādāyārjunastūrṇaṃ draupadīṃ gajarāḍiva
|
saṃprāpya nagarābhyāśamavatārayadarjunaḥ ||8||

9sa rājadhānīṃ saṃprāpya kaunteyo'rjunamabravīt
|
kvāyu‌dhāni samāsajjya pravekṣyāmaḥ puraṃ vayam ||9||

10sāyudhāśca pravekṣyāmo vayaṃ tāta puraṃ yadi
|
samudvegaṃ janasyāsya kariṣyāmo na saṃśayaḥ ||10||

11gāṇḍīvaṃ ca mahadgāḍhaṃ loke ca viditaṃ nṛṇām
|
taccedāyudhamādāya gacchāmo nagaraṃ vayam
|
kṣipramasmānvijānīyurmanuṣyā nātra saṃśayaḥ ||11||

12tato dvādaśa varṣāṇi praveṣṭavyaṃ vane punaḥ
|
ekasminnapi vijñāte pratijñātaṃ hi nastathā ||12||

13arjuna uvāca|iyaṃ kūṭe manuṣyendra gahanā mahatī śamī
|
bhīmaśākhā durārohā śmaśānasya samīpataḥ ||13||

14na cāpi vidyate kaścinmanuṣya iti me matiḥ
|
yo'smānnidadhato draṣṭā bhavecchastrāṇi pāṇḍavāḥ ||14||

15utpathe hi vane jātā mṛgavyālaniṣevite
|
samīpe ca śmaśānasya gahanasya viśeṣataḥ ||15||

16samādhāyāyudhaṃ śamyāṃ gacchāmo nagaraṃ prati
|
evamatra yathāyogaṃ vihariṣyāma bhārata ||16||

17vaiśampāyana uvāca|evamuktvā sa rājānaṃ dharmarājaṃ yudhiṣṭhiram
|
pracakrame nidhānāya śastrāṇāṃ bharatarṣabha ||17||

18yena devān manuṣyāṃśca sarvāṃścaikaratho'jayat
|
sphītān janapadāṃścānyānajayat kurupuṅgavaḥ ||18||

19tadudāraṃ mahāghoṣaṃ saṃpannabalasūdanam
|
apajyamakarot pārtho gāṇḍīvaṃ subhayaṅkaram ||19||

20yena vīraḥ kurukṣetramabhyarakṣat parantapaḥ
|
amuñcaddhanuṣastasya jyāmakṣayyāṃ yudhiṣṭhiraḥ ||20||

21pāñcālān yena saṅgrāme bhīmaseno'jayat prabhuḥ
|
pratyaṣedhadbahūnekaḥ sapatnāṃścaiva digjaye ||21||

22niśamya yasya visphāraṃ vyadravanta raṇāt pare
|
parvatasyeva dīrṇasya visphoṭamaśaneriva ||22||

23saindhavaṃ yena rājānaṃ paryāmṛṣitavānatha
|
jyāpāśaṃ dhanuṣastsya bhīmaseno'vatārayat ||23||

24ajayat paścimāmāśāṃ dhanuṣā yena pāṇḍavaḥ
|
mādrīputro mahābāhustāmrāsyo mitabhāṣitā ||24||

25tasya morvīmapākarṣacchūraḥ saṃkrandano yudhi
|
kule nāsti samo rūpe yasyeti nakulaḥ smṛtaḥ ||25||

26dakṣiṇāṃ dakṣiṇācāro diśaṃ yenājayat prabhuḥ
|
apajyamakarodvīraḥ sahadevastadāyudham ||26||

27khaḍgāṃśca dīptān dīrghāṃśca kalāpāṃśca mahādhanān
|
vipāṭhān kṣuradhārāṃśca dhanurbhirnidadhuḥ saha ||27||

28vaiśampāyana uvāca|athānvaśāsannakulaṃ kuntīputro yudhiṣṭhiraḥ
|
āruhyemāṃ śamīṃ vīra dhanūṃṣyetāni nikṣipa ||28||

29tāmupāruhya nakulo dhanūṃṣi nidadhe svayam
|
yāni tasyā vakāśāni divyarūpāṇyamanyata ||29||

30yatra cāpaśyata sa vai tirovarṣāṇi varṣati
|
tatra tāni dṛḍhaiḥ pāśaiḥ sugāḍhaṃ paryabandhata ||30||

31śarīraṃ ca mṛtasyaikaṃ samabadhnanta pāṇḍavāḥ
|
vivarjayiṣyanti narā dūrādeva śamīmimām ||31||

32ābaddhaṃ śavamaveti gandhamāghrāya pūtikam
|
aśītiśatavarṣeyaṃ mātā na iti vādinaḥ ||32||

33kuladharmo'yamasmākaṃ pūrvairācarito'pi vā
|
samāsajjyātha vṛkṣe'sminniti vai vyāharanti te ||33||

34āgopālāvipālebhya ācakṣāṇāḥ paraṃtapāḥ
|
ājagmurnagarābhyāśaṃ pārthāḥ śatrunibarhaṇāḥ ||34||

35jayo jayanto vijayo jayatseno jayadvalaḥ
|
iti guhyāni nāmāni cakre teṣāṃ yudhiṣṭhiraḥ ||35||

36tato yathāpratijñābhiḥ prāviśannagaraṃ mahat
|
ajñātacaryāṃ vatsyanto rāṣṭre varṣaṃ trayodaśam ||36||
