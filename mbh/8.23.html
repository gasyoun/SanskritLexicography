Mahābhārata 8.23 (Karṇaparvan)

1sañjaya uvāca|
sahadevaṃ tathā kruddhaṃ dahantaṃ tava vāhinīm
|
duḥśāsano mahārāja bhrātā bhrātaramabhyayāt ||1||

2tau sametau mahāyuddhe dṛṣṭvā tatra mahārathāḥ
|
siṃhanādaravāṃścakrurvāsāṃsyādudhuvuśca ha ||2||

3tato bhārata kruddhena tava putreṇa dhanvinā
|
pāṇḍuputrastribhirbāṇairvakṣasyabhihato balī ||3||

4sahadevastato rājannārācena tavātmajam
|
viddhvā vivyādha saptatyā sārathiṃ ca tribhiḥ śaraiḥ ||4||

5duḥśāsanastataścāpaṃ chittvā rājanmahāhave
|
sahadevaṃ trisaptatyā bāhvorurasi cārpayat ||5||

6sahadevastu saṃkruddhaḥ khaḍgaṃ gṛhya mahāhave
|
āvidhya prāsṛjattūrṇaṃ tava putrarathaṃ prati ||6||

7samārgaṇaguṇaṃ cāpaṃ chittvā tasya mahānasiḥ
|
nipapāta tato bhūmau cyutaḥ sarpa ivāmbarāt ||7||

8athānyaddhanurādāya sahadevaḥ pratāpavān
|
duḥśāsanāya cikṣepa bāṇamantakaraṃ tataḥ ||8||

9tamāpatantaṃ viśikhaṃ yamadaṇḍopamatviṣam
|
khaḍgena śitadhāreṇa dvidhā ciccheda kauravaḥ ||9||

10tatastaṃ niśitaṃ khaḍgamāvidhya yudhi satvaraḥ
|
dhanuścānyatsamādāya śaraṃ jagrāha vīryavān ||10||

11tamāpatantaṃ sahasā nistriṃśaṃ niśitaiḥ śaraiḥ
|
pātayāmāsa samare sahadevo hasanniva ||11||

12tato bāṇāṃścatuḥṣaṣṭiṃ tava putro mahāraṇe
|
sahadevarathaṃ tūrṇaṃ preṣayāmāsa bhārata ||12||

13tāñcharānsamare rājan vegenāpatato bahūn
|
ekaikaṃ pañcabhirbāṇaiḥ sahadevo nyakṛntata ||13||

14sannivārya mahābāṇāṃstava putreṇa preṣitān
|
athāsmai subahūnbāṇānpreṣayāmāsa saṃyuge ||14||

15tānbāṇāṃstava putro'pi chittvekaikaṃ tribhiḥ śaraiḥ
|
nanāda sumahānādaṃ dārayāṇo vasundharām ||15||

16tato duḥśāsano rājan viddhvā pāṇḍusutaṃ raṇe
|
sārathiṃ navabhirbāṇairmādreyasya samārpayat ||16||

17tataḥ kruddho mahārāja sahadevaḥ pratāpavān
|
samādhatta śaraṃ ghoraṃ mṛtyukālāntakopamam ||17||

18vikṛṣya balavaccāpaṃ tava putrāya so'sṛjat
|
sa taṃ nirbhidya vegena bhittvā ca kavacaṃ mahat ||18||

19prāviśaddharaṇīṃ rājan valmīkamiva pannagaḥ
|
tataḥ saṃmumuhe rājaṃstava putro mahārathaḥ ||19||

20mūḍhaṃ cainaṃ samālokya sārathistvarito ratham
|
apovāha bhṛśaṃ trasto vadhyamānaḥ śitaiḥ śaraiḥ ||20||

21parājitya raṇe taṃ tu kauravyaṃ pāṇḍunandanaḥ
|
duryodhanabalaṃ dṛṣṭvā pramamātha samantataḥ ||21||

22pipīlikapuṭaṃ rājanyathā mṛdūnannaro ruṣā
|
tathā sā kauravī senā mṛditā tena bhārata ||22||
