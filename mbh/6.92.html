Mahābhārata 6.92 (Bhīṣmaparvan)

1sañjaya uvāca|
tatastadbāṇavarṣaṃ tu duḥsahaṃ dānavairapi
|
dadhāra yudhi rājendro yathā varṣaṃ mahādvipaḥ ||1||

2tataḥ krodhasamāviṣṭo niḥśvasanniva pannagaḥ
|
saṃśayaṃ paramaṃ prāptaḥ putraste bharatarṣabha ||2||

3mumoca niśistāṃstīkṣṇānnārācān pañcaviṃśatim
|
te'patansahasā rājaṃstasmin rākṣasapuṅgave ||3||

4āśīviṣā iva kruddhāḥ parvate gandhamādane
|
sa tairviddhaḥ sravan raktaṃ prabhinna iva kuñjaraḥ ||4||

5dadhre matiṃ vināśāya rājñaḥ sa piśitāśanaḥ
|
jagrāha ca mahāśaktiṃ girīṇāmapi dāriṇīm ||5||

6saṃpradīptāṃ maholkābhāmaśaniṃ jvalitāmiva
|
samudyacchan mahābāhurjighāṃsustanayaṃ tava ||6||

7tāmudyatāmabhiprekṣya vaṅgānāmadhipastvaran
|
kuñjaraṃ girisaṅkāśaṃ rākṣasaṃ pratyacodayat ||7||

8sanāgapravareṇājau balinā śīghragāminā
|
yato duryodhanarathastaṃ mārgaṃ pratyavartata ||8||

9rathaṃ ca vārayāmāsa kuñjareṇa sutasya te
|
mārgamāvāritaṃ dṛṣṭvā rājñā vaṅgena dhīmatā ||9||

10ghaṭotkaco mahārāja krodhasaṃraktalocanaḥ
|
udyatāṃ tāṃ mahāśaktiṃ tasmiṃścikṣepa vāraṇe ||10||

11sa tayā'bhihato rājaṃstena bāhupramuktayā
|
sañjātarudhirotpīḍaḥ papāta ca mamāra ca ||11||

12patatyatha gaje cāpi vaṅgānāmīśvaro balī
|
javena samabhidrutya jagāma dharaṇītalam ||12||

13duryodhano'pi saṃprekṣya patitaṃ varavāraṇam
|
prabhagnaṃ ca balaṃ dṛṣṭvā jagāma paramāṃ vyathām ||13||

14kṣatradharmaṃ puraskṛtya ātmanaścābhimānitām
|
prāpte'pakramaṇe rājā tasthau giririvācalaḥ ||14||

15saṃdhāya ca śitaṃ bāṇaṃ kālāgnisamatejasam
|
mumoca paramakruddhastasmin ghore niśācare ||15||

16tamāpatantaṃ saṃprekṣya bāṇamindrāśaniprabham
|
lāghavānmocayāmāsa mahātmā vai ghaṭotkacaḥ||16||

17bhūyaśca vinanādograṃ krodhasaṃraktalocanaḥ
|
trāsayāmāsa sainyāni yugānte jalado yathā ||17||

18taṃ śrutvā ninadaṃ ghoraṃ tasya bhīmasya rakṣasaḥ
|
ācāryamupasaṅgamya bhīṣmaḥ śāntanavo'bravīt ||18||

19yathaiṣa ninado ghoraḥ śrūyate rākṣaseritaḥ
|
haiḍimbo yudhyate nūna rājñā duryodhanena ha ||19||

20naiṣa śakyo hi saṅgrāme jetuṃ bhūtena kenacit
|
tatra gacchata bhadraṃ vo rājānaṃ parirakṣata ||20||

21abhidruto mahābhāgo rākṣasena mahātmanā
|
etaddhi vaḥ paraṃ kṛtyaṃ sarveṣāṃ naḥ paraṃtapāḥ ||21||

22pitāmahavacaḥ śrutvā tvaramāṇā mahārathāḥ
|
uttamaṃ javamāsthāya prayayuryatra kauravaḥ ||22||

23droṇaśca somadattaśca bāhlīko'tha jayadrathaḥ
|
kṛpo bhūriśravāḥ śalya āvantyaḥ sa bṛhadbalaḥ ||23||

24aśvatthāmā vikarṇaśca citraseno viviṃśatiḥ
|
rathāścānekasāhasrā ye teṣāmanuyāyinaḥ ||24||

25abhidrutaṃ parīpsantaḥ putraṃ duryodhanaṃ tava
|
tadanīkamanādhṛṣyaṃ pālitaṃ tu mahārathaiḥ ||25||

26ātatāyinamāyāntaṃ prekṣya rākṣasasattamaḥ
|
nākampata mahābāhurmaināka iva parvataḥ ||26||

27pragṛhya vipulaṃ cāpaṃ jñātibhiḥ parivāritaḥ
|
śūlamudgarahastaiśca nānāpraharaṇairapi ||27||

28tataḥ samabhavadyuddhaṃ tumulaṃ lomaharṣaṇam
|
rākṣasānāṃ ca mukhyasya duryodhanabalasya ca ||28||

29dhanuṣāṃ kūjatāṃ śabdaḥ sarvatastumulo raṇe
|
aśrūyata mahārāja vaṃśānāṃ dahyatāmiva ||29||

30astrāṇāṃ pātyamānānāṃ kavaceṣu śarīriṇām
|
śabdaḥ samabhavadrājan girīṇāmiva bhidyatām ||30||

31vīrabāhuvisṛṣṭānāṃ tomarāṇāṃ viśāṃpate
|
rūpamāsīdviyatsthānāṃ sarpāṇāmiva sarpatām ||31||

32tataḥ paramasaṃkruddho visphārya sumahaddhanuḥ
|
rākṣasendro mahābāhurvinadan bhairavaṃ ravam ||32||

33ācāryasyārdhacandreṇa kruddhaściccheda kārmukam
|
somadattasya bhallena dhvajaṃ conmathya cānadat ||33||

34bāhlīkaṃ ca tribhirbāṇaiḥ pratyavidhyatstanāntare
|
kṛpamekena vivyādha citrasenaṃ tribhiḥ śaraiḥ ||34||

35pūrṇāyatavisṛṣṭena samyak praṇihitena ca
|
jatrudeśe samāsādya vikarṇaṃ samatāḍayat ||35||

36nyaṣīdat svarathopasthe śoṇitena pariplutaḥ
|
tataḥ punarameyātmā nārācān daśa pañca ca ||36||

37bhūriśravasi saṃkruddhaḥ prāhiṇodbharatarṣabha
|
te varma bhittvā tasyāśu viviśurdharaṇītalam ||37||

38viviṃśateśca drauṇeśca yantārau samatāḍayat
|
tau petatū rathopasthe raśmīnutsṛjya vājinām ||38||

39sindhurājño'rdhacandreṇa vārāhaṃ svarṇabhūṣitam
|
unmamātha mahārāja dvitīyenācchinaddhanuḥ ||39||

40caturbhiratha nārācairāvantyasya mahātmanaḥ
|
jaghāna caturo vāhān krodhasaṃraktalocanaḥ ||40||

41pūrṇāyatavisṛṣṭena pītena niśitena ca
|
nirbibheda mahārāja rājaputraṃ bṛhadbalam ||41||

42sa gāḍhaviddho vyathito rathopastha upāviśat
|
bhṛśaṃ krodhena cāviṣṭo rathastho rākṣasādhipaḥ ||42||

43cikṣepa niśitāṃstīkṣṇāñcharānāśīviṣopamān
|
bibhiduste mahārāja śalyaṃ yuddhaviśāradam ||43||
