Mahābhārata 3.101 (Āraṇyakaparvan)

1lomaśa uvāca|
tataḥ sa vajrī balibhirdaivatairabhirakṣitaḥ
|
āsasāda tato vṛtraṃ sthitamāvṛtya rodasī||1||

2kālakeyairmahākāyaiḥ samantādabhirakṣitam
|
samudyatapraharaṇaiḥ saśṛṅgairiva parvataiḥ||2||

3tato yuddhaṃ samabhavaddevānāṃ dānavaiḥ saha
|
muhūrtaṃ bharataśreṣṭha lokatrāsakaraṃ mahat||3||

4udyatapratipiṣṭānāṃ khaḍgānāṃ vīrabāhubhiḥ
|
āsītsutumulaḥ śabdaḥ śarīreṣvabhipātyatām||4||

5śirobhiḥ prapatadbhiścāpyantarikṣānmahītalam
|
tālairiva mahārāja vṛntādbhraṣṭairadṛśyata||5||

6te hemakavacā bhūtvā kāleyāḥ parighāyudhāḥ
|
tridaśānabhyavartanta dāvadagdhā ivādrayaḥ||6||

7teṣāṃ vegavatāṃ vegaṃ sābhimānaṃ pradhāvatām
|
na śekustridaśāḥ soḍhuṃ te bhagnāḥ prādravanbhayāt||7||

8tāndṛṣṭvā dravato bhītānsahasrākṣaḥ puraṃdaraḥ
|
vṛtre vivardhamāne ca kaśmalaṃ mahadāviśat||8||

9kāleyabhayasaṃtrasto devaḥ sākṣātpurandaraḥ
|
jagāma śaraṇaṃ śīghraṃ taṃ tu nārāyaṇaṃ prabhum||9||

10taṃ śakraṃ kaśmalāviṣṭaṃ dṛṣṭvā viṣṇuḥ sanātanaḥ
|
svatejo vyadadhacchakre balamasya vivardhayan||10||

11viṣṇunā gopitaṃ śakraṃ dṛṣṭvā devagaṇāstataḥ
|
sarve tejaḥ samādadhyustathā brahmarṣayo'malāḥ||11||

12sa samāpyāyitaḥ śakro viṣṇunā daivataiḥ saha
|
ṛṣibhiśca mahābhāgairbalavānsamapadyata||12||

13jñātvā balasthaṃ tridaśādhipaṃ tu nanāda vṛtro mahato ninādān
|
tasya praṇādena dharā diśaśca khaṃ dyaurnagāścāpi cacāla sarvam||13||

14tato mahendraḥ paramābhitaptaḥ śrutvā ravaṃ ghorarūpaṃ mahāntam
|
bhaye nimagnastvarito mumoca vajraṃ mahattasya vadhāya rājan||14||

15sa śakravajrābhihataḥ papāta mahāsuraḥ kāñcanamālyadhārī
|
yathā mahāśailavaraḥ purastātsa mando viṣṇukarādvimuktaḥ||15||

16tasminhate daityavare bhayārtaḥ śakraḥ pradudrāva saraḥ praveṣṭum
|
vajraṃ sa mene na karādvimuktaṃ vṛtraṃ bhayāccāpi hataṃ na mene||16||

17sarve ca devā muditāḥ prahṛṣṭā maharṣayaścendramabhiṣṭuvantaḥ
|
sarvāṃśca daityāṃstvaritāḥ sametya jaghnuḥ surā vṛtravadhābhitaptān||17||

18taistrāsyamānāstridaśaiḥ sametaiḥ samudramevāviviśurbhayārtāḥ
|
praviśya caivodadhimaprameyaṃ jhaṣākulaṃ nakrasamākulaṃ ca||18||

19tadā sma mantraṃ sahitāḥ pracakrustrailokyanāśārthamabhismayantaḥ
|
tatra sma kecinmatiniścayajñāstāṃstānupāyānupavarṇayanti||19||

20teṣāṃ tu tatra kramakālayogāddhorā matiścintayatāṃ babhūva
|
ye santi vidyātapasopapannāsteṣāṃ vināśaḥ prathamaṃ tu kāryaḥ||20||

21lokā hi sarve tapasā dhriyante tasmāttvaradhvaṃ tapasaḥ kṣayāya
|
ye santi kecicca vasuṃdharāyāṃ tapasvino dharmavidaśca tajjñāḥ||21||

22teṣāṃ vadhaḥ kriyatāṃ kṣiprameva teṣu pranaṣṭeṣu jagatpranaṣṭam
|
evaṃ hi sarve gatabuddhibhāvā jagadvināśe paramaprahṛṣṭāḥ||22||

23durgaṃ samāśritya mahormimantaṃ ratnākaraṃ varuṇasyālayaṃ sma||23||
