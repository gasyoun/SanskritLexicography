Mahābhārata 7.83 (Droṇaparvan)

1sañjaya uvāca|
tato yudhiṣṭhiro rājā pratinandya janārdanam
|
uvāca paramaprītaḥ kaunteyo devakīsutam ||1||

2sukhena rajanī vyuṣṭā kaccitte madhusūdana
|
kaccijjñānāni sarvāṇi prasannāni tavācyuta ||2||

3vāsudevo'pi tadyuktaṃ paryapṛcchadyudhiṣṭhiram
|
tataśca prakṛtīḥ kṣattā nyavedayadupasthitāḥ ||3||

4anujñātaśca rājñā sa prāveśayata taṃ janam
|
virāṭaṃ bhīmasenaṃ ca dhṛṣṭadyumnaṃ ca sātyakim ||4||

5cedipaṃ dhṛṣṭaketuṃ ca drupadaṃ ca mahāratham
|
śikhaṇḍinaṃ yamau caiva cekitānaṃ sakekayam ||5||

6yuyutsuṃ caiva kauravyaṃ pāñcālyaṃ cottamaujasam
|
yudhāmanyuṃ subāhuṃ ca draupadeyāṃśca sarvaśaḥ ||6||

7ete cānye ca bahavaḥ kṣatriyāḥ kṣatriyarṣabham
|
upatasthurmahātmānaṃ viviśuścāsane śubhe ||7||

8ekasminnāsane vīrāvupaviṣṭau mahābalau
|
kṛṣṇaśca yuyudhānaśca mahātmānau mahādyutī ||8||

9tato yudhiṣṭhirasteṣāṃ śṛṇvatāṃ madhusūdanam
|
abravīt puṇḍarīkākṣamābhāṣya madhuraṃ vacaḥ ||9||

10ekaṃ tvāṃ vayamāśritya sahasrākṣamivāmarāḥ
|
prārthayāmo jayaṃ yuddhe śāśvatāni sukhāni ca ||10||

11tvaṃ hi rājyavināśaṃ ca dviṣadbhiśca nirākriyām
|
kleśāṃśca vividhān kṛṣṇa sarvāṃstānapi veda naḥ ||11||

12tvayi sarveśa sarveṣāmasmākaṃ bhaktavatsala
|
sukhamāyattamatyarthaṃ yātrā ca madhusūdana ||12||

13sa tathā kuru vārṣṇeya yathā tvayi mano mama
|
arjunasya yathā satyā pratijñā syāccikīrṣitā ||13||

14sa bhavāṃstārayatvasmādduḥkhāmarṣamahārṇavāt
|
pāraṃ titīrṣatāmadya plavo no bhava mādhava ||14||

15na hi tat kurute saṃkhye rathī ripuvadhodyataḥ
|
yathā vai kurute kṛṣṇa sārathiryatnamāsthitaḥ ||15||

16yathaiva sarvāsvāpatsu pāsi vṛṣṇīñjanārdana
|
tathaivāsmān mahābāho vṛjināttrāturhasi ||16||

17tvamagādhe'plave magnān pāṇḍavān kurusāgare
|
samuddhara plavo bhūtvā śaṃkha-cakra-gadādhara ||17||

18namaste devadeveśa sanātana viśātana
|
viṣṇo jiṣṇo hare kṛṣṇa vaikuṇṭha puruṣottama ||18||

19nāradastvāṃ samācakhyau purāṇamṛṣisattamam
|
varadaṃ śārṅgiṇaṃ śreṣṭhaṃ tat satyaṃ kuru mādhava ||19||

20ityuktaḥ puṇḍarīkākṣo dharmarājena saṃsadi
|
toyameghasvano vāgmī pratyuvāca yudhiṣṭhiram ||20||

21vāsudeva uvāca| sāmareṣyapi lokeṣu sarveṣu na tathāvidhaḥ
|
śarāsanadharaḥ kaścidyathā pārtho dhanañjayaḥ ||21||

22vīryavānastrasaṃpannaḥ parākrānto mahābalaḥ
|
yuddhaśauṇḍaḥ sadāmarṣī tejasā paramo nṛṇām ||22||

23sa yuvā vṛṣabhaskandho dīrghabāhurmahābalaḥ
|
siṃharṣabhagatiḥ śrīmān dviṣataste haniṣyati ||23||

24ahaṃ ca tatkariṣyāmi yathā kuntīsuto'rjunaḥ
|
dhārtarāṣṭrasya sainyāni dhakṣyatyagnirivendhanam ||24||

25adya taṃ pāpakarmāṇaṃ kṣudraṃ saubhadraghātinam
|
apunardarśanaṃ mārgamiṣubhiḥ kṣepsyate'rjunaḥ ||25||

26tasyādya gṛdhrāḥ śyenāśca caṇḍayomāyavastathā
|
bhakṣayiṣyanti māṃsāni ye cānye puruṣādakāḥ ||26||

27yadyasya devā goptāraḥ sendrāḥ sarve tathā'pyasau
|
rājadhānīṃ yamasyādya hataḥ prāpsyati saṃkule ||27||

28nihatya saindhavaṃ jiṣṇuradya tvāmupayāsyati
|
viśoko vijvaro rājan bhava bhūtipuraskṛtaḥ ||28||
