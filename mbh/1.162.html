Mahābhārata 1.162 (Ādiparvan)

1vaiśaṃpāyana uvāca|
kariṣya iti bhīmena pratijñāte'tha bhārata
|
ājagmuste tataḥ sarve bhaikṣamādāya pāṇḍavāḥ||1||

2ākāreṇaiva taṃ jñātvā pāṇḍuputro yudhiṣṭhiraḥ
|
rahaḥ samupaviśyaikastataḥ papraccha mātaram||2||

3yudhiṣṭhira uvāca|
kiṃ cikīrṣatyayaṃ karma bhīmo bhamaparākramaḥ
|
bhavatyanumate kaccitsvayaṃ vā kartumicchati||3||

4kuntyuvāca|
mamaiva vacanādeṣa kariṣyati parantapaḥ
|
brāhmaṇārthe mahatkṛtyaṃ mokṣāya nagarasya ca||4||

5yudhiṣṭhira uvāca|
kimidaṃ sāhasaṃ tīkṣṇaṃ bhavatyā duṣkaraṃ kṛtam
|
parityāgaṃ hi putrasya na praśaṃsanti sādhavaḥ||5||

6kathaṃ parasutasyārthe svasutaṃ tyaktumicchasi
|
lokavedaviruddhaṃ hi putratyāgātkṛtaṃ tvayā||6||

7yasya bāhū samāśritya sukhaṃ sarve śayāmahe
|
rājyaṃ cāpahṛtaṃ kṣudrairājihīrṣāmahe punaḥ||7||

8yasya duryodhano vīryaṃ cintayannamitaujasaḥ
|
na śete rajanīḥ sarvā duḥkhācchakuninā saha||8||

9yasya vīrasya vīryeṇa muktā jatugṛhādvayam
|
anyebhyaścaiva pāpebhyo nihataśca purocanaḥ||9||

10yasya vīryaṃ samāśritya vasupūrṇāṃ vasundharām
|
imāṃ manyāmahe prāptāṃ nihatya dhṛtarāṣṭrajān||10||

11tasya vyavasitastyāgo buddhimāsthāya kāṃ tvayā
|
kaccinnu duḥkhairbuddhiste viluptā gatacetasaḥ||11||

12kuntyuvāca|
yudhiṣṭhira na saṃtāpastvayā kāryo vṛkodare
|
na cāyaṃ buddhidaurbalyādvyavasāyaḥ kṛto mayā||12||

13iha viprasya bhavane vayaṃ putra sukhoṣitāḥ
|
ajñātā dhārtarāṣṭrāṇāṃ satkṛtā vītamanyavaḥ||13||

14tasya pratikriyā pārtha mayeyaṃ prasamīkṣitā
|
etāvāneva puruṣaḥ kṛtaṃ yasminna naśyati||14||

15yāvacca kuryādanyo'sya kuryādbahugumaṃ tataḥ
|
dṛṣṭvā bhīmasya vikrāntaṃ tadā jatugṛhe mahat
|
hiḍimbasya vadhāccaivaṃ viśvāso me vṛkodare||15||

16bāhvorbalaṃ hi bhīmasya nāgāyutasamaṃ mahat
|
yena yūyaṃ gajaprakhyā nirvyūḍhā vāraṇāvatāt||16||

17vṛkodareṇa sadṛśo balenānyo na vidyate
|
yo vyatīyādyudhi śreṣṭhamapi cakradharaṃ svayam||17||

18jātamātraḥ purā caiva mamāṅkātpatito girau
|
śarīragauravādasya śilā gātrairvicūrṇitā||18||

19tadahaṃ prajñayā jñātvā balaṃ bhīmasya pāṇḍava
|
pratikārye ca viprasya tataḥ kṛtavatī matim||19||

20nedaṃ lobhānna cājñānānna ca mohādviniścitam
|
buddhipūrvaṃ tu dharmasya vyavasāyaḥ kṛto mayā||20||

21arthau dvāvapi niṣpannau yudhiṣṭhira bhaviṣyataḥ
|
pratīkāraśca vāsasya dharmaśca carito mahān||21||

22yo brāhmaṇasya sāhāyyaṃ kuryādartheṣu karhicit
|
kṣatriyaḥ sa śubhā~llokānāpnuyāditi me matiḥ||22||

23kṣatriyasyaiva kurvāṇaḥ kṣatriyo vadhamokṣaṇam
|
vipulāṃ kīrtimāpnoti loke'smiṃśca paratra ca||23||

24vaiśyasyārthe ca sāhāyyaṃ kurvāṇaḥ kṣatriyo bhuvi
|
sa sarveṣvapi lokeṣu prajā rañjayate dhruvam||24||

25śūdraṃ tu mocayedrājā śaraṇārthinamāgatam
|
prāpnotīha kule janma saddravye rājapūjite||25||

26evaṃ māṃ bhagavānvyāsaḥ purā pauravanandana
|
provācāsukaraprajñastasmādevaṃ cikīrṣitam||26||
