Mahābhārata 3.183 (Āraṇyakaparvan)

1vaiśampāyana uvāca|
kāmyakaṃ prāpya kauravya yudhiṣṭhirapurogamāḥ
|
kṛtātithyā munigaṇairniṣeduḥ saha kṛṣṇayā||1||

2tatastān pariviśvastān vasataḥ pāṇḍunandanān
|
brāhmaṇā bahavastatra samantāt paryavārayan||2||

3athā'bravīddvijaḥ kaścidarjunasya priyaḥ sakhā
|
sa eṣyati mahābāhurvaśī śaurirudāradhīḥ||3||

4viditā hi hareryūyamihāyātāḥ kurūdvahāḥ
|
sadā hi darśanākāṅkṣī śreyo'nveṣī ca vo hariḥ||4||

5bahuvatsarajīvī ca mārkaṇḍeyo mahātapāḥ
|
svādhyāyatapasā yuktaḥ kṣipraṃ yuṣmān sameṣyati||5||

6tathaiva bruvatastasya pratyadṛśyata keśavaḥ
|
śaibyasugrīvayuktena rathena rathināṃ varaḥ||6||

7maghavāniva paulomyā sahitaḥ satyabhāmayā
|
upāyāddevakīputro didṛkṣuḥ kurusattamān||7||

8avatīrya rathāt kṛṣṇo dharmarājaṃ yathāvidhi
|
vavande mudito dhīmān bhīmaṃ ca balināṃ varam||8||

9pūjayāmāsa dhaumyaṃ ca yamābhyāmabhivāditaḥ
|
pariṣvajya guḍākeśaṃ draupadīṃ paryasāntvayat||9||

10sa dṛṣṭvā phālgunaṃ vīraṃ cirasya priyamāgatam
|
paryaṣvajata dāśārhaḥ punaḥ punararindamaḥ||10||

11tathaiva satyabhāmā'pi draupadīṃ pariṣasvaje
|
pāṇḍavānāṃ priyāṃ bhāryāṃ kṛṣṇasya mahiṣī priyā||11||

12tataste pāṇḍavāḥ sarve sabhāryāḥ sapurohitāḥ
|
ānarcuḥ puṇḍarīkākṣaṃ parivavruśca sarvaśaḥ||12||

13kṛṣṇastu pārthena sametya vidvān dhanañjayenāsuratarjanena
|
babhau yathā bhūtapatirmahātmā sametya sākṣāt bhagavān guhena||13||

14tataḥ samastāni kirīṭamālī vaneṣu vṛttāni gadāgrajāya
|
uktvā yathāvat punaranvapṛcchat kathaṃ subhadrā ca sa cābhimanyuḥ||14||

15sa pūjayitvā madhuhā yathāvat pārthaṃ ca kṛṣṇāṃ ca purohitaṃ ca
|
uvāca rājānabhipraśaṃsan yudhiṣṭhiraṃ tatra sahopaviśya||15||

16dharmaḥ paraḥ pāṇḍavarājyalābhāt tasyārthamāhustapa eva rājan
|
satyārjavābhyāṃ caratā svadharmaṃ jitastvayā'yaṃ ca paraśca lokaḥ||16||

17adhītamagre caratā vratāni samyagdhanurvedamavāpya kṛtsnam
|
kṣātreṇa dharmeṇa vasūni labdhvā sarve hyavāptāḥ kratavaḥ purāṇāḥ||17||

18na grāmyadharmeṣu ratistavāsti kāmānna kiñcit kuruṣe narendra
|
na cārthalobhāt prajahāsi dharmaṃ tasmāt prabhāvādasi dharmarājaḥ||18||

19dānaṃ ca satyaṃ ca tapaśca rājan śraddhā ca buddhiśca kṣamā dhṛtiśca
|
avāpya rāṣṭrāṇi vasūni bhogāneṣā parā pārtha sadā ratiste||19||

20yadā janaughaḥ kurujāṅgalānāṃ kṛṣṇāṃ sabhāyāmavaśāmapaśyat
|
apetadharmavyavahāravṛttaṃ saheta tat pāṇḍava kastvadanyaḥ||20||

21asaṃśayaṃ sarvasamṛddhakāmaḥ kṣipraṃ prajāḥ pālayitā'si samyak
|
ime vayaṃ nigrahaṇe kurūṇāṃ yadi pratijñā bhavataḥ samāptā||21||

22dhaumyaṃ ca bhīmaṃ ca yudhiṣṭhiraṃ ca yamau ca kṛṣṇāṃ ca daśārhasiṃhaḥ
|
uvāca diṣṭyā bhavatāṃ śivena prāptaḥ kirīṭī muditaḥ kṛtāstraḥ||22||

23provāca kṛṣṇāmapi yājñasenīṃ daśārhabhartā sahitaḥ suhṛdbhiḥ
|
diṣṭyā samagrā'si dhanaṃjayena samāgatetyevamuvāca kṛṣṇaḥ||23||

24kṛṣṇe dhanurvedaratipradhānāstavātmajāste śiśavaḥ suśīlāḥ
|
sadbhiḥ sadaivācaritaṃ suhṛdbhiścaranti putrāstava yājñaseni||24||

25rājyena rāṣṭraiśca nimantryamāṇāḥ pitrā ca kṛṣṇe tava sodaraiśca
|
na yajñasenasya na mātulānāṃ gṛheṣu bālā ratimāpnuvanti||25||

26ānartamevābhimukhāḥ śivena gatvā dhanurvedaratipradhānāḥ
|
tavātmajā vṛṣṇipuraṃ praviśya na daivatebhyaḥ spṛhayanti kṛṣṇe||26||

27yathā tvamevārhasi teṣu vṛttaṃ prayoktumāryā ca yathaiva kuntī
|
teṣvapramādena tathā karoti tathaiva bhūyaśca tathā subhadrā||27||

28yathā'niruddhasya yathā'bhimanyoryathā sunīthasya yathaiva bhānoḥ
|
tathā vinetā ca gatiśca kṛṣṇe tavātmajānāmapi raukmiṇeyaḥ||28||

29gadāsicarmagrahaṇeṣu śūrānastreṣu śikṣāsu rathāśvayāne
|
samyagvinetā vinayedatandrīstāṃścābhimanyuḥ satataṃ kumāraḥ||29||

30sa cāpi samyak praṇidhāya śikṣāṃ śastrāṇi caiṣāṃ vidhivat pradāya
|
tavātmajānāṃ ca tathā'bhimanyoḥ parākramaistuṣyati raukmiṇeyaḥ||30||

31yathā vihāraṃ prasamīkṣamāṇāḥ prayānti putrāstava yājñaseni
|
ekaikameṣāmanuyānti tatra rathāśca yānāni ca dantinaśca||31||

32athābravīddharmarājaṃ tu kṛṣṇo daśārhayodhāḥ kukurāndhakāśca
|
ete nideśaṃ tava pālayantastiṣṭhantu yatrecchasi tatra rājan||32||

33āvartatāṃ kārmukavegavātā halāyudhapragrahaṇā madhūnām
|
senā tavārtheṣu narendra yattā sasādipattyaśvarathā sanāgā||33||

34prasthāpyatāṃ pāṇḍava dhārtarāṣṭraḥ suyodhanaḥ pāpakṛtāṃ variṣṭhaḥ
|
sa sānubandhaḥ sa suhṛdgaṇaśca bhaumasya saubhādhipateśca mārgam||34||

35kāmaṃ tathā tiṣṭha narendra tasmin yathā kṛtaste samayaḥ sabhāyām
|
dāśārhayodhaistu hatāriyodhaṃ pratīkṣatāṃ nāgapuraṃ bhavantam||35||

36vyapetamanyurvyapanītapāpmā vihṛtya yatrecchasi tatra kāmam
|
tataḥ prasiddhaṃ prathamaṃ viśokaḥ prapatsyase nāgapuraṃ sarāṣṭram||36||

37tatastadājñāya mataṃ mahātmā yathāvaduktaṃ puruṣottamena
|
praśasya viprekṣya ca dharmarājaḥ kṛtāñjaliḥ keśavamityuvāca||37||

38asaṃśayaṃ keśava pāṇḍavānāṃ bhavān gatistvaccharaṇā hi pārthāḥ
|
kālodaye tacca tataśca bhūyaḥ kartā bhavān karma na saṃśayo'sti||38||

39yathāpratikṣaṃ vihṛtaśca kālaḥ sarvāḥ samā dvādaśa nirjaneṣu
|
ajñātacaryāṃ vidhivat samāpya bhavadgatāḥ keśava pāṇḍaveyāḥ||39||

40eṣaiva buddhirjuṣatāṃ sadā tvāṃ satye sthitāḥ keśava pāṇḍaveyāḥ
|
sadānadharmāḥ sajanāḥ sadārāḥ sabādhavāstvaccharaṇā hi pārthāḥ||40||

41vaiśampāyana uvāca|
tathā vadati vārṣṇeye dharmarāje ca bhārata
|
atha paścāt tapovṛddho bahuvarṣasahasradhṛk||41||

42pratyadṛśyata dharmātmā mārkaṇḍeyo mahātapāḥ
|
ajaraścāmaraścaiva rūpaudāryaguṇānvitaḥ||42||

43vyadṛśyata tathā yukto yathā syāt pañcaviṃśakaḥ
|
tamāgatamṛṣiṃ vṛddhaṃ bahuvarṣasahastriṇam||43||

44ānarcurbāhmaṇāḥ sarve kṛṣṇaśca saha pāṇḍavaiḥ
|
tamarcitaṃ suviśvastamāsīnamṛṣisattamam
|
brāhmaṇānāṃ matenāha pāṇḍavānāṃ ca keśavaḥ||44||

45kṛṣṇa uvāca|
śuśrūṣavaḥ pāṇḍavāste brāhmaṇāśca samāgatāḥ
|
draupadī satyabhāmā ca tathā'haṃ paramaṃ vacaḥ||45||

46purāvṛttāḥ kathāḥ puṇyāḥ sadācārān sanātanān
|
rājñāṃ strīṇāmṛṣīṇāṃ ca mārkaṇḍeya vicakṣva naḥ||46||

47vaiśampāyana uvāca|
teṣu tatropaviṣṭeṣu devarṣirapi nāradaḥ
|
ājagāma viśuddhātmā pāṇḍavānavalokakaḥ||47||

48tamapyatha mahātmānaṃ sarve te puruṣarṣabhāḥ
|
pādyārghyābhyāṃ yathānyāyamupatasthurmanīṣiṇaḥ||48||

49nāradastvatha devarṣirjñātvā tāṃstu kṛtakṣaṇān
|
mārkaṇḍeyasya vadatastāṃ kathāmanvamodata||49||

50uvāca cainaṃ kālajñaḥ smayanniva sanātanaḥ
|
brahmarṣe kathyatāṃ yat te pāṇḍaveṣu vivakṣitam||50||

51evamuktaḥ pratyuvāca mārkaṇḍeyo mahātapāḥ
|
kṣaṇaṃ kurudhvaṃ vipulamākhyātavyaṃ bhaviṣyati||51||

52evamuktāḥ kṣaṇaṃ cakruḥ pāṇḍavāḥ saha tairdvijaiḥ
|
madhyandine yathā''dityaṃ prekṣantaste mahāmunim||52||

53vaiśampāyana uvāca|
taṃ vivakṣantamālakṣya kururājo mahāmunim
|
kathāsañjananārthāya codayāmāsa pāṇḍavaḥ||53||

54bhavān daivatadaityānāmṛṣīṇāṃ ca mahātmanām
|
rājarṣīṇāṃ ca sarveṣāṃ caritajñaḥ purātanaḥ||54||

55sevyaścopāsitavyaśca mato naḥ kāṅkṣitaściram
|
ayaṃ ca devakīputraḥ prāpto'smānavalokakaḥ||55||

56bhavatyeva hi me buddhirdṛṣṭvā''tmānaṃ sukhāccyutam
|
dhārtarāṣṭrāṃśca durvṛttānṛdhyataḥ prekṣya sarvaśaḥ||56||

57karmaṇaḥ puruṣaḥ kartā śubhasyāpyaśubhasya vā
|
sa phalaṃ tadupāśnāti kathaṃ kartā svidīśvaraḥ||57||

58kuto vā sukhaduḥkheṣu nṛṇāṃ brahmavidāṃ vara
|
iha vā kṛtamanveti paradehe'tha vā punaḥ||58||

59dehī ca dehaṃ santyajya mṛgyamāṇaḥ śubhāśubhaiḥ
|
kathaṃ saṃyujyate pretya iha vā dvijasattama||59||

60aihalaukikameveha utāho pāralaukikam
|
kva ca karmāṇi tiṣṭhanti jantoḥ pretasya bhārgava||60||

61mārkaṇḍeya uvāca|
tvadyukto'yamanupraśno yathāvadvadatāṃ vara
|
viditaṃ veditavyaṃ te sthityarthaṃ tvaṃ tu pṛcchasi||61||

62atra te kathayiṣyāmi tadihaikamanāḥ śṛṇu
|
yathehāmutra ca naraḥ sukhaduḥkhamupāśnute||62||

63nirmalāni śarīrāṇi viśuddhāni śarīriṇām
|
sasarja dharmatantrāṇi pūrvotpannaḥ prajāpatiḥ||63||

64amoghaphalasaṅkalpāḥ suvratāḥ satyavādinaḥ
|
brahmabhūtā narāḥ puṇyāḥ purāṇāḥ kurusattama||64||

65sarve devaiḥ samāyānti svacchandena nabhastalam
|
tataśca punarāyānti sarve svacchandacāriṇaḥ||65||

66svacchandamaraṇāścāsan narāḥ svacchandacāriṇaḥ
|
alpabādhā nirātaṅkāḥ siddhārthā nirupadravāḥ||66||

67draṣṭāro devasaṅghānāmṛṣīṇāṃ ca mahātmanām
|
pratyakṣāḥ sarvadharmāṇāṃ dāntā vigatamatsarāḥ||67||

68āsan varṣasahastrīyāstathā putrasahasriṇaḥ
|
tataḥ kālāntare'nyasmin pṛthivītalacāriṇaḥ||68||

69kāmakrodhābhibhūtāste māyāvyājopajīvinaḥ
|
lobhamohābhibhūtāśca tyaktā dehaistato narāḥ||69||

70aśubhaiḥ karmabhiḥ pāpāstiryaṅnirayagāminaḥ
|
saṃsāreṣu vicitreṣu pacyamānāḥ punaḥ punaḥ||70||

71mogheṣṭā moghasaṅkalpā moghajñānā vicetasaḥ
|
sarvābhiśaṅkinaścaiva saṃvṛttāḥ kleśadāyinaḥ||71||

72aśubhaiḥ karmabhiścāpi prāyaśaḥ paricihnitāḥ
|
dauṣkulyā vyādhibahulā durātmāno'pratāpinaḥ||72||

73bhavantyalpāyuṣaḥ pāpā raudrakarmaphalodayāḥ
|
nāthantaḥ sarvakāmānāṃ nāstikā bhinnacetasaḥ||73||

74jantoḥ pretasya kauntaya gatiḥ svairiha karmabhiḥ
|
prājñasya hīnabuddheśca karmakośaḥ kva tiṣṭhati||74||

75kvasthastat samupāśnāti sukṛtaṃ yadi vetarat
|
iti te darśanaṃ yacca tatrāpyanunayaṃ śṛṇu||75||

76ayamādiśarīreṇa devasṛṣṭena mānavaḥ
|
śubhānāmaśubhānāṃ ca kurute sañcayaṃ mahat||76||

77āyuṣo'nte prahāyedaṃ kṣīṇaprāyaṃ kalevaram
|
sambhavatyeva yugapadyonau nāstyantarā'bhavaḥ||77||

78tatrāsya svakṛtaṃ karma chāyevānugataṃ sadā
|
phalatyatha sukhārho vā duḥkhārho vā'tha jāyate||78||

79kṛtāntavidhisaṃyuktaḥ sa janturlakṣaṇaiḥ śubhaiḥ
|
aśubhairvā nirādāno lakṣyate jñānadṛṣṭibhiḥ||79||

80eṣā tāvadabuddhīnāṃ gatiruktā yudhiṣṭhira
|
ataḥ paraṃ jñānavatāṃ nibodha gatimuttamām||80||

81manuṣyāstaptatapasaḥ sarvāgamaparāyaṇāḥ
|
sthiravratāḥ satyaparā guruśuśrūṣaṇe ratāḥ||81||

82suśīlāḥ śuklajātīyā kṣāntā dāntāḥ sutejasaḥ
|
śuciyonyantaragatāḥ prāyaśaḥ śubhalakṣaṇāḥ||82||

83jitendriyatvādvaśinaḥ śuklatvānmandarogiṇaḥ
|
alpābādhaparitrāsādbhavanti nirupadravāḥ||83||

84cyavantaṃ jāyamānaṃ ca garbhasthaṃ caiva sarvaśaḥ
|
svamātmānaṃ paraṃ caiva budhyante jñānacakṣuṣā||84||

85ṛṣayaste mahātmānaḥ pratyakṣāgamabuddhayaḥ
|
karmabhūmimimāṃ prāpya punaryānti surālayam||85||

86kiñciddaivāddhaṭhāt kiñcit kiñcideva svakarmabhiḥ
|
prāpnuvanti narā rājan mā te'stvanyā vicāraṇā||86||

87imāmatropamāṃ cāpi nibodha vadatāṃ vara
|
manuṣyaloke yacchreyaḥ paraṃ manye yudhiṣṭhira||87||

88iha vaikasya nāmutra amutraikasya no iha
|
iha vā'mutra caikasya nāmutraikasya no iha||88||

89dhanāni yeṣāṃ vipulāni santi nityaṃ ramante suvibhūṣitāṅgāḥ
|
teṣāmayaṃ śatruvaraghnaloko nāsau sadā dehasukhe ratānām||89||

90ye yogayuktāstapasi prasaktāḥ svādhyāyaśīlā jarayanti dehān
|
jitendriyāḥ prāṇivadhe nivṛttāsteṣāmasau nāyamarighnalokaḥ||90||

91ye dharmameva prathamaṃ caranti dharmeṇa labdhvā ca dhanāni kāle
|
dārānavāpya kratubhiryajante teṣāmayaṃ caiva paraśca lokaḥ||91||

92ye naiva vidyāṃ na tapo na dānaṃ na cāpi mūḍhāḥ prajane yatanti
|
na cānugacchanti sukhānnabhogāṃsteṣāmayaṃ caiva paraśca lokaḥ||92||

93sarve bhavantastvativīryasattvā divyaujasaḥ saṃhananopapannāḥ
|
lokādamuṣmādavaniṃ prapannāḥ svadhītavidyāḥ surakāryahetoḥ||93||

94kṛtvaiva karmāṇi mahānti śūrāstapodamācāravihāraśīlāḥ
|
devānṛṣīn pretagaṇāṃśca sarvān santarpayitvā vidhinā pareṇa||94||

95svargaṃ paraṃ puṇyakṛto nivāsaṃ krameṇa samprāpsyatha karmabhiḥ svaiḥ
|
mābhūdviśaṅkā tava kauravendra dṛṣṭvā''tmanaḥ kleśamimaṃ sukhārham||95||
