Mahābhārata 7.143 (Droṇaparvan)

1sañjaya uvāca|
sa bāhurnyapatadbhūmau sakhaḍgaḥ saśubhāṅgadaḥ
|
ādadhajjīvalokasya duḥkhamadbhutamuttamaḥ||1||

2prahariṣyanhṛto bāhuradṛśyena kirīṭinā
|
vegena nyapatadbhūmau pañcāsya iva pannagaḥ||2||

3sa moghaṃ kṛtamātmānaṃ dṛṣṭvā pārthena kauravaḥ
|
utsṛjya sātyakiṃ krodhādgarhayāmāsa pāṃḍavam||3||

4bhūriśravā uvāca|
nṛśaṃsaṃ bata kaunteya karmedaṃ kṛtavānasi
|
apaśyato viṣaktasya yanme bāhumacicchidaḥ||4||

5kiṃ nu vakṣyasi rājānaṃ dharmaputraṃ yudhiṣṭhiram
|
kiṃ kurvāṇo mayā saṃkhye hato bhūriśravā raṇe||5||

6idamindreṇa te sākṣādupadiṣṭaṃ mahātmanā
|
astraṃ rudreṇa vā pārtha droṇenātha kṛpeṇa vā ||6||

7nanu nāmāstradharmajñastvaṃ loke'bhyadhikaḥ paraiḥ
|
so'yudhyamānasya kathaṃ raṇe prahṛtavānasi||7||

8na pramattāya bhītāya virathāya prayācate
|
vyasane vartamānāya praharanti manasvinaḥ||8||

9idaṃ tu nīcācaritamasatpuruṣasevitam
|
kathamācaritaṃ pārtha pāpakarma suduṣkaram||9||

10āryeṇa sukaraṃ tvāhurāryakarma dhanañjaya
|
anāryakarma tvāryeṇa suduṣkaratamaṃ bhuvi||10||

11yeṣu yeṣu naravyāghra yatra yatra ca vartate
|
āśu tacchīlatāmeti tadidaṃ tvayi dṛśyate||11||

12kathaṃ hi rājavaṃśyastvaṃ kauraveyo viśeṣataḥ
|
kṣatradharmādapakrāntaḥ suvṛttaścaritavrataḥ||12||

13idaṃ tu yadatikṣudraṃ vārṣṇeyārthe kṛtaṃ tvayā
|
vāsudevamataṃ nūnaṃ naitattvayyupapadyate||13||

14ko hi nāma pramattāya pareṇa saha yuddhyate
|
īdṛśaṃ vyasanaṃ dadyādyo na kṛṣṇasakhā bhavet||14||

15vrātyāḥ saṃkliṣṭakarmāṇaḥ prakṛtyaiva ca garhitāḥ
|
vṛṣṇyandhakāḥ kathaṃ pārtha pramāṇaṃ bhavatā kṛtāḥ||15||

16evamukto raṇe pārtho bhūriśravasamabravīt
|
arjuna uvāca|
vyaktaṃ hi jīryamāṇo'pi buddhiṃ jarayate naraḥ||16||

17anarthakamidaṃ sarvaṃ yattvayā vyāhataṃ prabho
|
jānanneva hṛṣīkeśaṃ garhase māṃ ca pāṇḍavam||17||

18saṃgrāmāṇāṃ hi dharmajñaḥ sarvaśāstrārthapāragaḥ
|
na cādharmamahaṃ kuryāṃ jānaṃścaiva hi muhyase||18||

19yuddhyanti kṣatriyāḥ śatrūn svaiḥ svaiḥ parivṛtā narāḥ
|
bhrātṛbhiḥ pitṛbhiḥ putraistathā saṃbandhibāndhavaiḥ||19||

20vayasyairatha mitraiśca te ca bāhuṃ samāśritāḥ
|
sa kathaṃ sātyakiṃ śiṣyaṃ sukhasambandhimeva ca||20||

21asmadarthe ca yuddhyantaṃ tyaktvā prāṇānsudustyajān
|
mama bāhuṃ raṇe rājan dakṣiṇaṃ yuddhadurmadam||21||

22na cātmā rakṣitavyo vai rājan raṇagatena hi
|
yo yasya yujyate'rtheṣu sa vai rakṣyo narādhipa||22||

23tai rakṣyamāṇaiḥ sa nṛpo rakṣitavyo mahāmṛdhe
|
yadyahaṃ sātyakiṃ paśye vadhyamānaṃ mahāraṇe||23||

24tatastasya viyogena pāpaṃ me'narthato bhavet
|
rakṣitaśca mayā yasmāttasmātkrudhyasi kiṃ mayi||24||

25yacca me garhase rājannanyena saha saṅgatam
|
ahaṃ tvayā vinikṛtastatra me buddhivibhramaḥ||25||

26kavacaṃ dhunvatastubhyaṃ rathaṃ cārohataḥ svayam
|
dhanurjyāṃ karṣataścaiva yudhyataḥ saha śatrubhiḥ||26||

27evaṃ rathagajākīrṇe hayapattisamākule
|
siṃhanādoddhatarave gaṃbhīre sainyasāgare ||27||

28svaiḥ paraiśca sametebhyaḥ sātvatena ca saṅgame
|
ekasyaikena hi kathaṃ saṃgrāmaḥ saṃbhaviṣyati||28||

29bahubhiḥ saha saṃgamya nirjitya ca mahārathān
|
śrāntaśca śrāntavāhaśca vimanāḥ śastrapīḍitaḥ||29||

30īdṛśaṃ sātyakiṃ saṃkhye nirjitya ca mahāratham
|
adhikatvaṃ vijānīṣe svavīryavaśamāgatam||30||

31yadicchasi śiraścāsya asinā hantumāhave
|
tathā kṛcchragataṃ caiva sātyakiṃ kaḥ kṣamiṣyati||31||

32tvaṃ vai vigarhayātmānamātmānaṃ yo na rakṣasi
|
kathaṃ kariṣyase vīra yo vā tvāṃ saṃśrayejjanaḥ||32||

33sañjaya uvāca|
evamukto mahābāhuryūpaketurmahāyaśāḥ
|
yuyudhānaṃ samutsṛjya raṇe prāyamupāviśat||33||

34śarānāstīryaṃ savyena pāṇinā puṇyalakṣaṇaḥ
|
yiyāsurbrahmalokāya prāṇānprāṇeṣvathājuhot||34||

35sūrye cakṣuḥ samādhāya prasannaṃ salile manaḥ
|
dhyāyanmahopaniṣadaṃ yogayukto'bhavanmuniḥ||35||

36tataḥ sa sarvasenāyāṃ janaḥ kṛṣṇadhanañjayau
|
garhayāmāsa taṃ cāpi śaśaṃsa puruṣarṣabham||36||

37nindyamānau tathā kṛṣṇau nocatuḥ kiṃcidapriyam
|
tataḥ praśasyamānaśca nāhṛṣyadyūpaketanaḥ||37||

38tāṃstathā vādino rājanputrāṃstava dhanañjayaḥ
|
amṛṣyamāṇo manasā teṣāṃ tasya ca bhāṣitam||38||

39asaṃkruddhamanā vācaḥ smārayanniva bhārata
|
uvāca pāṇḍutanayaḥ sākṣepamiva phālgunaḥ||39||

40mama sarve'pi rājāno jānantyeva mahāvratam
|
na śakyo māmako haṃtuṃ yo me syādbāṇagocare||40||

41yūpaketo nirīkṣyaitanna māmahasi garhitum
|
na hi dharmamavijñāya yuktaṃ garhayituṃ param ||41||

42āttaśastrasya hi raṇe vṛṣṇivīraṃ jighāṃsataḥ
|
yadahaṃ bāhumacchaitsaṃ na sa dharmo vigarhitaḥ||42||

43nyastaśastrasya bālasya virathasya vivarmaṇaḥ
|
abhimanyorvadhaṃ tāta dhārmikaḥ ko na pūjayet||43||

44evamuktaḥ sa pārthena śirasā bhūmimaspṛśat
|
pāṇinā caiva savyena prāhiṇodasya dakṣiṇam||44||

45etatpārthasya tu vacastataḥ śrutvā mahādyutiḥ
|
yūpaketurmahārāja tūṣṇīmāsīdavāṅmukhaḥ||45||

46arjuna uvāca|
yā prītirdharmarāje me bhīme ca balināṃ vare
|
nakule sahadeve ca sā me tvayi śalāgraja||46||

47mayā tvaṃ samanujñātaḥ kṛṣṇena ca mahātmanā
|
gaccha puṇyakṛtāṃllokāñchibirauśīnaro yathā||47||

48vāsudeva uvāca|
ye lokā mama vimalāḥ sakṛdvibhātā brahmādyaiḥ suravṛṣabhairapīṣyamāṇāḥ
|
tān kṣipraṃ vraja satatāgnihotrayājin mattulyo bhava garuḍottamāṅgayānaḥ||48||

49sañjaya uvāca|
utthitaḥ sa tu śaineyo vimuktaḥ saumadattinā
|
khaḍgamādāya cicchitsuḥ śirastasya mahātmanaḥ||49||

50nihataṃ pāṇḍuputreṇa prasaktaṃ bhūridakṣiṇam
|
iyeṣa sātyakirhantuṃ śalāgrajamakalmaṣam||50||

51nikṛttabhujamāsīnaṃ chinnahastamiva dvipam
|
krośatāṃ sarvasainyānāṃ nindyamānaḥ sudurmanāḥ||51||

52vāryamāṇaḥ sa kṛṣṇena pārthena ca mahātmanā
|
bhīmena cakrarakṣābhyāmaśvatthāmnā kṛpeṇa ca||52||

53karṇena vṛṣasenena saindhavena tathaiva ca
|
vikrośatāṃ ca sainyānāmavadhīttaṃ dhṛtavratam||53||

54prāyopaviṣṭāya raṇe pārthena chinnabāhave
|
sātyakiḥ kauraveyāya khaḍgenāpāharacchiraḥ||54||

55nābhyanandanta sainyāni sātyakiṃ tena karmaṇā
|
arjunena hataṃ pūrvaṃ yajaghāna kurūdvaham||55||

56sahasrākṣasamaṃ caiva siddhacāraṇamānavāḥ
|
bhūriśravasamālokya yuddhe prāyagataṃ hatam||56||

57apūjayanta taṃ devā vismitāste'sya karmabhiḥ
|
pakṣavādāṃśca subahūnprāvadaṃstava sainikāḥ||57||

58na vārṣṇeyasyāparādho bhavitavyaṃ hi tattathā
|
tasmānmanyurna vaḥ kāryaḥ krodho duḥkhataro nṛṇām||58||

59hantavyaścaiva vīreṇa nātra kāryā vicāraṇā
|
vihito hāsya dhātraiva mṛtyuḥ sātyakirāhave||59||

60sātyakiruvāca| na hantavyo na hantavya iti yanmāṃ prabhāṣata
|
dharmavādairadharmiṣṭhā dharmakañcukamāsthitāḥ||60||

61yadā bālaḥ sumadrāyāḥ sutaḥ śastravinā kṛtaḥ
|
yuṣmābhirnihato yuddhe tadā dharmaḥ kva vo gataḥ||61||

62mayā tvetatpratijñātaṃ kṣepe kasmiścideva hi
|
yo māṃ niṣpiṣya saṃgrāme jīvanhanyātpadā ruṣā||62||

63sa me vadhyo bhavecchatruryadyapi syānmunivrataḥ
|
ceṣṭamānaṃ pratīghāte sabhujaṃ māṃ sacakṣuṣaḥ||63||

64manyadhvaṃ mṛta ityevametadvo buddhilāghavam
|
yukto hāsya pratīghātaḥ kṛto me kurupuṅgavāḥ||64||

65yattu pārthena māṃ dṛṣṭvā pratijñāmabhirakṣatā
|
sakhaḍgo'sya hṛto bāhuretenaivāsmi vañcitaḥ||65||

66bhavitavyaṃ hi yadbhāvi devaṃ ceṣṭayatīva ca
|
so'yaṃ hato vimarde'sminkimatrādharmaceṣṭitam||66||

67api cāyaṃ purā gītaḥ śloko vālmīkinā bhuvi
|
na hantavyāḥ striya iti yadbravīṣi plavaṅgama||67||

68sarvakālaṃ manuṣyeṇa vyavasāyavatā sadā
|
pīḍākaramamitrāṇāṃ yatsyātkartavyameva tat ||68||

69sañjaya uvāca|
evamukte mahārāja sarve kauravapuṅgavāḥ
|
na sma kiñcidabhāṣanta manasā samapūjayan||69||

70mantrābhipūtasya mahādhvareṣu yaśasvino bhūrisahastradasya ca
|
munerivāraṇyagatasya tasya na tatra kaścidvadhamabhyanandat||70||

71sunīlakeśaṃ varadasya tasya śūrasya pārāvatalohitākṣam
|
aśvasya medhyasya śiro nikṛttaṃ nyastaṃ havirdhānamivāntareṇa||71||

72sa tejasā śastrakṛtena pūto mahāhave dehavaraṃ visṛjya
|
ākrāmadūrdhvaṃ varado varārho vyāvṛttya dharmeṇa pareṇa rodasī||72||
