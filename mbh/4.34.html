Mahābhārata 4.34 (Virāṭaparvan)

1vaiśampāyana uvāca|evamukte tu savrīḍaḥ suśarmā''sīda‌dhomukhaḥ
|
sa mukto'bhyetya rājānamabhivādya pratasthivān ||1||

2visṛjya tu suśarmāṇaṃ pāṇḍavāste hatadviṣaḥ
|
svabāhubalasaṃpannā hīniṣevā yatavratāḥ
|
saṃgrāmaśiraso madhye tāṃ rātriṃ sukhino'vasan ||2||

3tato virāṭaḥ kaunteyānatimānuṣavikramān
|
arcayāmāsa vittena mānena ca mahārathān ||3||

4virāṭa uvāca|yathaiva mama ratnāni yuṣmākaṃ tāni vai tathā
|
kāryaṃ kuruta vai sarve yathākāmaṃ yathāsukham ||4||

5dadāmyalaṅkṛtāḥ kanyā vasūni vividhāni ca
|
manasaścāpyabhipretaṃ yuddhe śatrunibarhaṇāḥ ||5||

6yuṣmākaṃ vikramādadya mukto'haṃ svastimāniha
|
tasmādbhavanto matsyānāmīśvarāḥ sarva eva hi ||6||

7vaiśampāyana uvāca| tathetivādinaṃ matsyaṃ kauraveyāḥ pṛthak pṛthak
|
ūcuḥ prāñjalayaḥ sarve yudhiṣṭhirapurāgamāḥ ||7||

8pratinandāma te vākyaṃ sarvaṃ caiva viśāmpate
|
etenaiva pratītāḥ sma yattvaṃ mukto'dya śatrubhiḥ ||8||

9tato'bravīt pratimanā matsyarājo yudhiṣṭhiram
|
punareva mahābāhurvirāṭo rājasattamaḥ ||9||

10ehi tvāmabhiṣekṣyāmi matsyarājastu no bhavān
|
manasaścāpyabhipretaṃ yatheṣṭaṃ bhuvi durlabham
|
tatte'haṃ sampradāsyāmi sarvamarhati no bhavān ||10||

11ratnāni gāḥ suvarṇaṃ ca maṇimuktamathāpi ca
|
vaiyāghrapadya viprendra sarvathaiva namo'stu te ||11||

12tvatkṛte hyadya paśyāmi rājyaṃ santānameva ca
|
yataśca jātasaṃrambho na ca śatruvaśaṃ gataḥ ||12||

13tato yudhiṣṭhiro matsyaṃ punarevābhyabhāṣata
|
pratinandāmi te vākyaṃ manojñaṃ matsya bhāṣase ||13||

14ānṛśaṃsyaparo nityaṃ susukhī satataṃ bhava
|
gacchantu dūtāstvaritaṃ nagaraṃ tava pārthiva ||14||

15suhṛdāṃ priyamākhyātuṃ ghoṣayantu ca te jayam
|
tatastadvacanānmatsyo dūtānrājā samādiśat ||15||

16ācakṣadhvaṃ puraṃ gatvā saṃgrāmavijayaṃ mama
|
kumāryaḥ samalaṃkṛtya paryāgacchantu me purāt ||16||

17vāditrāṇi ca sarvāṇi gaṇikāśca svalaṃkṛtāḥ
|
etāṃ cājñāṃ tataḥ śrutvā rājñā matsyena noditāḥ
|
tāmājñāṃ śirasā kṛtvā prasthitā hṛṣṭamānasāḥ ||17||

18te gatvā tatra tāṃ rātrimatha sūryodayaṃ prati
|
virāṭasya purābhyāśe dūtā jayamaghoṣayan ||18||
