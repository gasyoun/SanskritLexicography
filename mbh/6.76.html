Mahābhārata 6.76 (Bhīṣmaparvan)

1dhṛtarāṣṭra uvāca|
evaṃ bahuguṇaṃ sainyamevaṃ bahuvidhaṃ purā
|
vyūḍhamevaṃ yathāśāstramamoghaṃ caiva sañjaya ||1||

2hṛṣṭamasmākamatyantamabhikāmaṃ ca naḥ sadā
|
prahvamavyasanopetaṃ purastāddṛṣṭavikramam ||2||

3nātivṛddhamabālaṃ ca na kṛśaṃ na ca pīvaram
|
laghuvṛttāyataprāyaṃ sārayodhamanāmayam ||3||

4āttasannāhaśastraṃ ca bahuśastraparigraham
|
asiyuddhe niyuddhe ca gadāyuddhe ca kovidam ||4||

5prāsarṣṭitomareṣvājau parigheṣvāyaseṣu ca
|
bhindipāleṣu śaktīṣu musaleṣu ca sarvaśaḥ ||5||

6kampaneṣu ca cāpeṣu kaṇapeṣu ca sarvaśaḥ
|
kṣepaṇīyeṣu citreṣu muṣṭiyuddheṣu ca kṣamam ||6||

7aparokṣaṃ ca vidyāsu vyāyāme ca kṛtaśramam
|
śastragrahaṇavidyāsu sarvāsu pariniṣṭhitam ||7||

8ārohe paryavaskande saraṇe sāntaraplute
|
samyakpraharaṇe yāne vyapayāne ca kovidam ||8||

9nāgāśvarathayāneṣu bahuśaḥ suparīkṣitam
|
parīkṣya ca yathānyāyaṃ vetanenopapāditam ||9||

10na goṣṭhyā nopakāreṇa na ca bandhunimittataḥ
|
na sauhṛdabalairvāpi nākulīnaparigrahaiḥ ||10||

11samṛddhajanamāryaṃ ca tuṣṭasambandhibāndhavam
|
kṛtopakārabhūyiṣṭhaṃ yaśasvi ca manasvi ca ||11||

12svajanaistu narairmukhyairbahuśo dṛṣṭakarmabhiḥ
|
lokapālopamaistāta pālitaṃ lokaviśrutam ||12||

13bahubhiḥ kṣatriyairguptaṃ pṛthivyāṃ lokasaṃmataiḥ
|
asmānabhigataiḥ kāmāt sabalaiḥ sapadānugaiḥ ||13||

14mahodadhimivāpūrṇamāpagābhiḥ samantataḥ
|
apakṣaiḥ pakṣisaṅkāśai rathairnāgaiśca saṃvṛtam ||14||

15nānāyodhajalaṃ bhīmaṃ vāhanormitaraṅgiṇam
|
kṣepaṇyasigadāśaktiśaraprāsasamākulam ||15||

16dhvajabhūṣaṇasaṃbādhaṃ ratnapaṭṭasusañcitam
|
paridhāvadbhiraśvaiśca vāyuvegavikampitam ||16||

17apāramiva garjantaṃ sāgarapratimaṃ mahat
|
droṇabhīṣmābhisaṃguptaṃ guptaṃ ca kṛtavarmaṇā ||17||

18kṛpaduḥśāsanābhyāṃ ca jayadrathamukhaistathā
|
bhagadattavikarṇābhyāṃ drauṇisaubalabāhlikaiḥ ||18||

19guptaṃ pravīrairlokaiśca sāravadbhirmahātmabhiḥ
|
yadahanyata saṅgrāme daivamatra purātanam ||19||

20naitādṛśaṃ samudyogaṃ dṛṣṭavanto hi mānuṣāḥ
|
ṛṣayo vā mahābhāgāḥ purāṇā bhuvi sañjaya ||20||

21īdṛśo'pi balaughastu saṃyuktaḥ śastrasaṃpadā
|
vadhyate yatra saṅgrāme kimanyadbhāgadheyataḥ ||21||

22viparītamidaṃ sarvaṃ pratibhāti hi sañjaya
|
yatredṛśaṃ balaṃ ghoraṃ pāṇḍavānnātaradraṇe ||22||

23pāṇḍavārthāya niyataṃ devāstatra samāgatāḥ
|
yudhyante māmakaṃ sainyaṃ yathā'vadhyata sañjaya ||23||

24ukto hi vidureṇāhaṃ hitaṃ pathyaṃ ca nityaśaḥ
|
na ca jagrāha tanmandaḥ putro duryodhano mama ||24||

25tasya manye matiḥ pūrvaṃ sarvajñasya mahātmanaḥ
|
āsīdyathāgataṃ tāta yena dṛṣṭamidaṃ purā ||25||

26athavā bhāvyamevaṃ hi sañjayaitena sarvathā
|
purā dhātrā yathā sṛṣṭaṃ tattathā naitadanyathā ||26||
