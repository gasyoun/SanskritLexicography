Mahābhārata 3.92 (Āraṇyakaparvan)

1lomaśa uvāca| dhanaṃjayena cāpyuktaṃ yattacchṛṇu yudhiṣṭhira
|
yudhiṣṭhiraṃ bhrātaraṃ me yojayerdharmyayā dhiyā||1||

2tvaṃ hi dharmānparānvettha tapāṃsi ca tapodhana
|
śrīmatāṃ cāpi jānāsi dharmaṃ rājñāṃ sanātanam||2||

3sa bhavānparamaṃ veda pāvanaṃ puruṣaṃ prati
|
tena saṃyojayethāstvaṃ tīrthapuṇyena pāṇḍavān||3||

4yathā tīrthāni gaccheta gāśca dadyātsa pārthivaḥ
|
tathā sarvātmanā kāryamiti māmarjuno'bravīt||4||

5bhavatā cānugupto'sau carettīrthāni sarvaśaḥ
|
rakṣobhyo rakṣitavyaśca durgeṣu viṣameṣu ca||5||

6dadhīca iva devendraṃ yathā cāpyaṅgirā ravim
|
tathā rakṣasva kaunteyānrākṣasebhyo dvijottama||6||

7yātudhānā hi bahavo rākṣasāḥ parvatopamāḥ
|
tvayābhiguptaṃ kaunteyaṃ na vivarteyurantikam||7||

8so'hamindrasya vacanānniyogādarjunasya ca
|
rakṣamāṇo bhayebhyastvāṃ cariṣyāmi tvā saha||8||

9dvistīrthāni mayā pūrvaṃ dṛṣṭāni kurunandana
|
idaṃ tṛtīyaṃ drakṣyāmi tānyeva bhavatā saha||9||

10iyaṃ rājarṣibhiryātā puṇyakṛdbhiryudhiṣṭhira
|
manvādibhirmahārāja tīrthayātrā bhayāpahā||10||

11nānṛjurnākṛtātmā ca nāvidyo na ca pāpakṛt
|
snāti tīrtheṣu kauravya na ca vakramatirnaraḥ||11||

12tvaṃ tu dharmamatirnityaṃ dharmajñaḥ satyasaṃgaraḥ
|
vimuktaḥ sarvapāpebhyo bhūya eva bhaviṣyasi||12||

13yathā bhagīratho rājā rājānaśca gayādayaḥ
|
yathā yayātiḥ kaunteya tathā tvamapi pāṇḍava||13||

14yudhiṣṭhira uvāca| na harṣātsaṃprapaśyāmi vākyasyāsyottaraṃ kvacit
|
sareddhi devarājo'yaṃ ko nāmābhyadhikastataḥ||14||

15bhavatā saṃgamo yasya bhrātā caiva dhanaṃjayaḥ
|
vāsavaḥ smarate yasya ko nāmābhyadhikastataḥ||15||

16yacca māṃ bhagavānāha tīrthānāṃ darśanaṃ prati
|
dhaumyasya vacanādeṣā buddhiḥ pūrvaṃ kṛtaiva me||16||

17tadyadā manyase brahmangamanaṃ tīrthadarśane
|
tadaiva gantāsmi tīrthānneṣa me niścayaḥ paraḥ||17||

18vaiśaṃpāyana uvāca| gamane kṛtabuddhiṃ tu pāṇḍavaṃ lomaśo'bravīt
|
laghurbhava mahārāja laghuḥ svairaṃ gamiṣyasi||18||

19yudhiṣṭhira uvāca| bhikṣābhujo nivartantāṃ brāhmaṇā yatayaśca ye
|
kṣuttṛḍadhvaśramāyāsaśītārtimasahiṣṇavaḥ||19||

20te sarve vinivartantāṃ ye ca miṣṭabhujo dvijāḥ
|
pakvānnalehyapānānāṃ māṃsānāṃ ca vikalpakāḥ||20||

21te'pi sarve nivartantāṃ ye ca sūdānuyāyinaḥ
|
mayā yathocitājīvyaiḥ saṃvibhaktāśca vṛttibhiḥ||21||

22ye cāpyanugatāḥ paurā rājabhaktipuraḥsarāḥ
|
dhṛtarāṣṭraṃ mahārājamabhigacchantu te ca vai||22||

23sa dāsyati yathākālamucitā yasya yā bhṛtiḥ
|
sa cedyathocitāṃ vṛttiṃ na dadyānmanujeśvaraḥ||23||

24asmatpriyahitārthāya pāñcālyo vaḥ pradāsyati||24||

25vaiśaṃpāyana uvāca| tato bhūyiṣṭhaśaḥ paurā gurubhāraprapīḍitāḥ
|
viprāśca yatayo mukhyā jagmurnāgapuraṃ prati||25||

26tānsarvāndharmarājasya premṇā rājāmbikāsutaḥ
|
pratijagrāha vidhivaddhanaiśca samatarpayat||26||

27tataḥ kuntīsuto rājā laghubhirbrāhmaṇaiḥ saha
|
lomaśena ca suprītastrirātraṃ kāmyake'vasat||27||
