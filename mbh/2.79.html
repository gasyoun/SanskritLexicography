Mahābhārata 2.79 (Sabhāparvan)

1vaiśaṃpāyana uvāca|
tasminsaṃprasthite kṛṣṇāṃ pṛthāṃ prāpya yaśasvinīm
|
apṛcchamdaśaduḥkhārtā yāścānyāstatra yoṣitaḥ||1||

2yathārhaṃ vandanāśleṣānkṛtvā gantumiyeṣa sā
|
tato ninādaḥ sumahānpāṇḍavāntaḥpurebhavat||2||

3kuntī ca bhṛśasaṃtaptā draupadīṃ prekṣya gacchatīm
|
śokavihvalayā vācā kṛcchrādvacanamabravīt||3||

4vatse śoko na te kāryaḥ prāpyedaṃ vyasanaṃ mahat
|
strīdharmāṇāmabhijñāsi śīlācāravatī tathā||4||

5na tvāṃ saṃdeṣṭumahami bhartṝnprati śucismite
|
sādhvī guṇasamāpannā bhūṣitaṃ te kuladvayam||5||

6sabhāgyāḥ kuravaśceme yena dagdhāstvayānaghe
|
ariṣṭaṃ vraja panthānaṃ madanudhyānabṛṃhitā||6||

7bhāvinyarthe hi satstrīṇāṃ vaikṛtaṃ nopajāyate
|
gurudharmābhiguptā ca śreyaḥ kṣipramavāpsyasi||7||

8sahadevaśca me putraḥ sadāvekṣyo vane vasan
|
yathedaṃ vyasanaṃ prāpya nāyaṃ sīdenmahāmatiḥ||8||

9tathetyuktvā tu sā devī sravannetrajalāvilā
|
śoṇitāktaikavasanā muktakeśī viniryayau||9||

10tāṃ krośantīṃ pṛthā duḥkhādanuvavrāja gacchatīm
|
athāpaśyansutānsarvānhṛtābharaṇavāsasaḥ||10||

11rurucarmāvṛtatanūnhriyā kiṃcidavāṅmukhān
|
paraiḥ parītānsaṃhṛṣṭaiḥ suhṛdbhiścānuśocitān||11||

12tadavasthānsutānsarvānupasṛtyātivatsalā
|
svajamānāvadacchokāttattadvilapatī bahu||12||

13kuntyuvāca|
kathaṃ saddharmacāritrānvṛttasthitivibhūṣitān
|
akṣudrāndṛḍhabhaktāṃśca daivatejyāparānsadā||13||

14vyasanaṃ vaḥ samabhyāgātkoyaṃ vidhiviparyayaḥ
|
kasyāpadhyānajaṃ cedamāgaḥ paśyāmi vo dhiyā ||14||

15syātu madbhāgyadoṣoyaṃ yāhaṃ yuṣmānajījanam
|
duḥkhāyāsabhujotyarthaṃ yuktānapyuttamairguṇaiḥ||15||

16kathaṃ vatsyatha durgeṣu vane ṛddhivinākṛtāḥ
|
vīryasattvabalotsāhatejābhirakṛśāḥ kṛśāḥ||16||

17yadyetadevamajñāsyaṃ vane vāso hi vo dhruvam
|
śataśṛṅgānmṛte pāṇḍau nāgamiṣyaṃ gajāhvayam ||17||

18dhanyaṃ vaḥ pitaraṃ manye tapomedhānvitaṃ tathā
|
yaḥ putrādhimasaṃprāpya svargecchāmakarotpriyām||18||

19dhanyāṃ cātīndriyajñānāmimāṃ prāptāṃ parāṃ gatim
|
manye tu mādrīṃ dharmajñāṃ kalyāṇīṃ sarvathaiva tu||19||

20ratyā matyā ca gatyā ca yayāhamabhisandhitā
|
jīvitapriyatāṃ mahyaṃ dhiṅmāṃ saṅkleśabhāginīm||20||

21putrakā na vihāsye vaḥ kṛchralabdhānpriyānsataḥ
|
sāhaṃ yāsyāmi hi vanaṃ hā kṛṣṇe kiṃ jahāsi mām||21||

22antavantyasudharmesmindhātrā kiṃ nu pramādataḥ
|
mamānto naiva vihitastenāyurna jahāti mām||22||

23hā kṛṣṇa dvārakāvāsinkvāsi saṅkarṣaṇānuja
|
kasmānna trāyase duḥkhānmāṃ cemāṃśca narottamān||23||

24anādinidhanaṃ ye tvāmanudhyāyanti vai narāḥ
|
tāstvaṃ pāsītyayaṃ vādaḥ sa gato vyarthatāṃ katham||24||

25ime saddharmamāhātmyayaśovīryānuvartinaḥ
|
nārhanti vyasanaṃ bhoktuṃ nanveṣāṃ kriyatāṃ dayā||25||

26seyaṃ nītyarthavijñeṣu bhīṣmadroṇakṛpādiṣu
|
sthiteṣu kulanātheṣu kathamāpadupāgatā||26||

27hā pāṇḍo hā mahārāja kvāsi kiṃ samupekṣase
|
putrānvivāsyataḥ sādhūnaribhirdyūtanirjitān||27||

28sahadeva nivartasva nanu tvamasi me priyaḥ
|
śarīrādapi mādreya māṃ mā tyākṣīḥ kuputravat||28||

29vrajantu bhrātarastemī yadi satyābhisandhinaḥ
|
matparitrāṇajaṃ dharmamihaiva tvamavāpnuhi||29||

30vaiśaṃpāyana uvāca|
evaṃ vilapatīṃ kuntīmabhivādya praṇamya ca
|
pāṇḍavā vigatānandā vanāyaiva pravavrajuḥ||30||

31viduraścāpi tāmārtī kuntīmāśvāsya hetubhiḥ
|
prāveśayadgṛhaṃ kṣattā svayamārtataraḥ śanaiḥ||31||

32dhārtarāṣṭrastriyastāśca nikhilenopalabhya tat
|
gamanaṃ parikarṣe ca kṛṣṇāyā dyūtamaṇḍale||32||

33ruruduḥ susvanaṃ sarvā vinidantyaḥ kurūnbhṛśam
|
dadhyuśca suciraṃ kālaṃ karāsaktamukhāmbujāḥ||33||

34rājā ca dhṛtarāṣṭrastu putrāṇāmanayaṃ tadā
|
dhyāyannudvignahṛdayo na śāntimadhijagmivān||34||

35sa cintayannanekāgraḥ śokavyākulacetanaḥ
|
kṣattuḥ saṃpreṣayāmāsa śīghramāgamyatāmiti||35||

36tato jagāma viduro dhṛtarāṣṭraniveśanam
|
taṃ paryapṛcchatsaṃvigno dhṛtarāṣṭro janādhipaḥ||36||
