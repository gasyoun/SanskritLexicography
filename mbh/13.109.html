Mahābhārata 13.109 (Anuśāsanaparvan)

1yudhiṣṭhira uvāca|
sarveṣāmupavāsānāṃ yacchreyaḥ sumahatphalam
|
yaccāpyasaṃśayaṃ loke tanme tvaṃ vaktumarhasi ||1||

2bhīṣma uvāca|
śṛṇu rājanyathā gītaṃ svayameva svayaṃbhuvā
|
yatkṛtvā nirvṛto bhūyātpuruṣo nātra saṃśayaḥ ||2||

3dvādaśyāṃ mārgaśīrṣe tu ahorātreṇa keśavam
|
arcyāśvamedhaṃ prāpnoti duṣkṛtaṃ cāsya naśyati ||3||

4tathaiva pauṣamāse tu pūjyo nārāyaṇeti ca
|
vājapeyamavāpnoti siddhiṃ ca paramāṃ vrajet ||4||

5ahorātreṇa dvādaśyāṃ māghamāse tu mādhavam
|
rājasūyamavāpnoti kulaṃ caiva samuddharet ||5||

6tathaiva phālgune māsi govindeti ca pūjayan
|
atirātramavāpnoti somalokaṃ ca gacchati ||6||

7ahorātreṇa dvādaśyāṃ caitre viṣṇuriti smaran
|
pauṇḍarīkamavāpnoti devalokaṃ ca gacchati ||7||

8vaiśākhamāse dvādaśyāṃ pūjayanmadhusūdanam
|
agniṣṭomamavāpnoti somalokaṃ ca gacchati ||8||

9ahorātreṇa dvādaśyāṃ jyeṣṭhe māsi trivikramam
|
gavāṃ medhamavāpnoti apsarobhiśca modate ||9||

10āṣāḍhe māsi dvādaśyāṃ vāmaneti ca pūjayan
|
naramedhamavāpnoti puṇyaṃ ca labhate mahat ||10||

11ahorātreṇa dvādaśyāṃ śrāvaṇe māsi śrīdharam
|
pañcayajñānavāpnoti vimānasthaśca modate ||11||

12tathā bhādrapadre māsi hṛṣīkeśeti pūjayan
|
sautrāmaṇimavāpnoti pūtātmā bhavate ca hi ||12||

13dvādaśyāmāśvine māsi padmanābheti cārcayan
|
gosahasraphalaṃ puṇyaṃ prāpnuyānnātra saṃśayaḥ ||13||

14dvādaśyāṃ kārtike māsi pūjya dāmodareti ca
|
gavāṃ yajñamavāpnoti pumān strī vā na saṃśayaḥ ||14||

15arcayetpuṇḍarīkākṣamevaṃ saṃvatsaraṃ tu yaḥ
|
jātismaratvaṃ prāpnoti vindyadbahu suvarṇakam ||15||

16ahanyahani tadbhāvamupendraṃ yo'dhigacchati
|
samāpte bhojayedviprānathavā dāpayeddhṛtam ||16||

17ataḥ paraṃ nopavāso bhavatīti viniścayaḥ
|
uvāca bhagavānviṣṇuḥ svayameva purātanam ||17||
