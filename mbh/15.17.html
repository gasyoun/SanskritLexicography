Mahābhārata 15.17 (Āśramavāsikaparvan)

1kuntyuvāca|
evametanmahābāho yathā vadasi pāṇḍava
|
kṛtamuddharṣaṇaṃ pūrvaṃ mayā vaḥ sīdatāṃ nṛpāḥ||1||

2dyūtāpahṛtarājyānāṃ patitānāṃ sukhādapi
|
jñātibhiḥ paribhūtānāṃ kṛtamuddharṣaṇaṃ mayā||2||

3kathaṃ pāṇḍorna naśyeta santatiḥ puruṣarṣabhāḥ
|
yaśaśca vo na naśyeta iti coddharṣaṇaṃ kṛtam||3||

4yūyamindrasamāḥ sarve devatulyaparākramāḥ
|
mā pareṣāṃ mukhaprekṣāḥ sthetyevaṃ tatkṛtaṃ mayā||4||

5kathaṃ dharmabhṛtāṃ śreṣṭho rājā tvaṃ vāsavopamaḥ
|
punarvane na duḥkhī syā iti coddharṣaṇaṃ kṛtam||5||

6nāgāyutasamaprāṇaḥ khyātavikramapauruṣaḥ
|
nāyaṃ bhīmo'tyayaṃ gacchediti cauddharṣaṇaṃ kṛtam||6||

7bhīmasenādavarajastathāyaṃ vāsavopamaḥ
|
vijayo nāvasīdeta iti coddharṣaṇaṃ kṛtam||7||

8nakulaḥ sahadevaśca tathemau guruvartinau
|
kṣudhā kathaṃ na sīdetāmiti coddharṣaṇaṃ kṛtam||8||

9iyaṃ ca bṛhatī śyāmā tathātyāyatalocanā
|
vṛthā sabhātale kliṣṭā mā bhūditi ca tatkṛtam||9||

10prekṣatāmeva vo bhīma vepantīṃ kadalīmiva
|
strīdharmiṇīmariṣṭāṅgī tathā dyūtaparājitām||10||

11duḥśāsano yadā maurkhyāddāsīvatparyakarṣata
|
tadaiva viditaṃ mahyaṃ parābhūtamidaṃ kulam||11||

12niṣaṇṇāḥ kuravaścaiva tadā me śvaśurādayaḥ
|
sā daivaṃ nāthamicchantī vyalapatkurarī yathā||12||

13keśapakṣe parāmṛṣṭā pāpena hatabuddhinā
|
yadā duḥśāsanenaiṣā tadā muhyāmyahaṃ nṛpāḥ||13||

14yuṣmattejovivṛddhyarthaṃ mayā hyuddharṣaṇaṃ kṛtam
|
tadānīṃ vidulāvākyairiti tadvitta putrakāḥ||14||

15kathaṃ na rājavaṃśo'yaṃ naśyetprāpya sutānmama
|
pāṇḍoriti mayā putrāstasmāduddharṣaṇaṃ kṛtam||15||

16na tasya putrāḥ pautrā vā kṣatavaṃśasya pārthiva
|
labhante sukṛtā~llokān yasmādvaṃśaḥ praṇaśyati||16||

17bhuktaṃ rājyaphalaṃ putrā bharturme vipulaṃ purā
|
mahādānāni dattāni pītaḥ somo yathāvidhi||17||

18nāhamātmaphalārthaṃ vai vāsudevamacūcudam
|
vidulāyāḥ pralāpaistaiḥ pālanārthaṃ ca tatkṛtam||18||

19nāhaṃ rājyaphalaṃ putrāḥ kāmaye putranirjitam
|
patilokānahaṃ puṇyānkāmaye tapasā vibho||19||

20śvaśrūśvaśurayoḥ kutvā śuśrūṣāṃ vanavāsinoḥ
|
tapasā śoṣayiṣyāmi yudhiṣṭhira kalevaram||20||

21nivartasva kuruśreṣṭa bhīmasenādibhiḥ saha
|
dharme te dhīyatāṃ buddhirmanastu mahadastu ca||21||
