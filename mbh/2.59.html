Mahābhārata 2.59 (Sabhāparvan)

1vaiśampāyana uvāca|
praviśya tāṃ sabhāṃ pārthā yudhiṣṭhirapurogamāḥ
|
sametya pārthivānsarvānpūjārhānabhipūjya ca||1||

2yathāvayaḥ sameyānā upaviṣṭā yathārhataḥ
|
āsaneṣu vicitreṣu sparddhyāstaraṇavatsu ca||2||

3teṣu tatropaviṣṭeṣu sarveṣvatha nṛpeṣu ca
|
śakuniḥ saubalastatra yudhiṣṭhiramabhāṣata||3||

4śakuniruvāca| upastīrṇā sabhā rājansarve tvayi kṛtakṣaṇāḥ
|
akṣānuptvā devanasya samayo'stu yudhiṣṭhira||4||

5yudhiṣṭhira uvāca|
nikṛtirdevanaṃ pāpaṃ na kṣātro'tra parākramaḥ
|
na ca nītirdhruvā rājankiṃ tvaṃ dyūtaṃ praśaṃsasi||5||

6na hi mānaṃ praśaṃsanti nikṛtau kitavasya hi
|
śakune maivaṃ no'jaiṣīramārgeṇa nṛśaṃsavat||6||

7śakuniruvāca| yo vetti saṅkhyāṃ nikṛtau vidhijñaśceṣṭāsvakhinnaḥ kitavo'kṣajāsu
|
mahāmatiryaśca jānāti dyūtaṃ sa vai sarvaṃ sahate prakriyāsu||7||

8akṣaglahaḥ so'bhibhavetparaṃ nastenaiva doṣo bhavatīha pārtha
|
dīvyāmahe pārthiva mā viśaṅkāṃ kuruṣva pāṇaṃ ca ciraṃ ca mā kṛthāḥ||8||

9yudhiṣṭhira uvāca|
evamāhāyamasito devalo munisattamaḥ
|
imāni lokadvārāṇi yo vai bhrāmyati sarvadā||9||

10idaṃ vai devanaṃ pāpaṃ nikṛtyā kitavaiḥ saha
|
dharmeṇa tu jayo yuddhe tatparaṃ na tu devanam||10||

11nāryā mlecchanti bhāṣābhirmāyayā na carantyuta
|
ajihyamaśaṭhaṃ yuddhametatsatpuruṣavratam||11||

12śaktito brāhmaṇārthāya śikṣituṃ prayatāmahe
|
tadvai vittaṃ mātidevīrmā jaiṣīḥ śakune parān||12||

13nikṛtyā kāmaye nāhaṃ sukhānyuta dhanāni vā
|
kitavasyeha kṛtino vṛttametanna pūjyate||13||

14śakuniruvāca| śrotriyaḥ śrotriyāneti nikṛtyaiva yudhiṣṭhira
|
vidvānaviduṣo'bhyeti nāhustāṃ nikṛtiṃ janāḥ||14||

15akṣairhi śikṣito'bhyeti nikṛtyaiva yudhiṣṭhira
|
vidvānaviduṣo'bhyeti nāhustāṃ nikṛtiṃ janāḥ||15||

16akṛtāstraṃ kṛtāsraśca durbalaṃ balavattaraḥ
|
evaṃ karmasu sarveṣu nikṛtyaiva yudhiṣṭhiraḥ
|
vidvānaviduṣobhyeti nāhustāṃ nikṛtiṃ janāḥ||16||

17evaṃ tvaṃ māmihābhyetya nikṛtiṃ yadi manyase
|
devanādvinivartasva yadi te vidyate bhayam||17||

18yudhiṣṭhira uvāca|
āhūto na nivarteyamiti me vratamāhitam
|
vidhiśca balavānrājandiṣṭasyāsmi vaśe sthitaḥ||18||

19asminsamāgame kena devanaṃ me bhaviṣyati
|
pratipāṇaśca ko'nyosti tato dyūtaṃ pravartatām||19||

20duryodhana uvāca|
ahaṃ dātāsmi ratnānāṃ dhanānāṃ ca viśāmpate
|
madarthe devitā cāyaṃ śakunirmātulo mama||20||

21yudhiṣṭhira uvāca|
anyenānyasya vai dyūtaṃ viṣamaṃ pratibhāti me
|
etadvidvinnupādatsva kāmamevaṃ pravartatām||21||
