Mahābhārata 9.27 (Śalyaparvan)

1sañjaya uvāca|
duryodhano mahārāja sudarśaścāpi te sutaḥ
|
hataśeṣau tadā saṅkhye vājimadhye vyavasthitau||1||

2tato duryodhanaṃ dṛṣṭvā vājimadhye vyavasthitam
|
uvāca devakīputraḥ kuntīputraṃ dhanañjayam||2||

3śatravo hatabhūyiṣṭhā jñātayaḥ paripālitāḥ
|
gṛhītvā sañjayaṃ cāsau nivṛttaḥ śinipuṅgavaḥ||3||

4pariśrāntaśca nakulaḥ sahadevaśca bhārata
|
yodhayitvā raṇe pāpāndhārtarāṣṭrānsahānugān||4||

5duryodhanamabhityajya traya ete vyavasthitāḥ
|
kṛpaśca kṛtavarmā ca drauṇiścaiva mahārathaḥ||5||

6asau tiṣṭhati pāñcālyaḥ śriyā paramayā yutaḥ
|
duryodhanabalaṃ hatvā saha sarvaiḥ prabhadrakaiḥ||6||

7asau duryodhanaḥ pārtha vājimadhye vyavasthitaḥ
|
chatreṇa dhriyamāṇena prekṣamāṇo muhurmuhuḥ||7||

8prativyūhya balaṃ sarvaṃ raṇamadhye vyavasthitaḥ
|
enaṃ hatvā śitairbāṇaiḥ kṛtakṛtyo bhaviṣyasi||8||

9gajānīkaṃ hataṃ dṛṣṭvā tvāṃ ca prāptamarindama
|
yāvanna vidravantyate tāvajjahi suyodhanam||9||

10yātu kaścittu pāñcālyaṃ kṣipramāgamyatāmiti
|
pariśrāntabalastāta naiṣa mucyeta kilviṣī||10||

11hatvā tava balaṃ sarvaṃ saṅgrāme dhṛtarāṣṭrajaḥ
|
jitānpāṇḍusutānmatvā rūpaṃ dhārayate mahat||11||

12nihataṃ svabalaṃ dṛṣṭvā pīḍitaṃ cāpi pāṇḍavaiḥ
|
dhruvameṣyati saṅgrāme vadhāyaivātmano nṛpaḥ||12||

13evamuktaḥ phalgunastu kṛṣṇaṃ vacanamabravīta
|
dhṛtarāṣṭrasutāḥ sarve hatā bhīmena mādhava||13||

14yāvetāvāsthitau kṛṣṇa tāvadya na bhaviṣyataḥ
|
hato bhīṣmo hato droṇaḥ karṇo vaikartano hataḥ||14||

15madrarājo hataḥ śalyo hataḥ kṛṣṇa jayadrathaḥ
|
hayāḥ pañcaśatāḥ śiṣṭāḥ śakuneḥ saubalasya ca||15||

16rathānāṃ tu śate śiṣṭe dve eva tu janārdana
|
dantināṃ ca śataṃ sāgraṃ trisāhasrāḥ padātayaḥ||16||

17aśvatthāmā kṛpaścaiva trigartādhipatistathā
|
ulūkaḥ śakuniścaiva kṛtavarmā ca sātvataḥ||17||

18etadbalamabhūccheṣaṃ dhārtarāṣṭrasya mādhava
|
mokṣo na nūnaṃ kālāttu vidyute bhuvi kasyacit||18||

19tathā vinihate sainye paśya duryodhanaṃ sthitam
|
adyāhnā hi mahārājo hatāmitro bhaviṣyati||19||

20na hi me mokṣyate kaścitpareṣāmiha cintaye
|
tvadya samaraṃ kṛṣṇa na hāsyanti madotkaṭāḥ||20||

21tānvai sarvānhaniṣyāmi yadyapi syurna mānuṣāḥ
|
adya yuddhe susaṅkruddho dīrghaṃ rājñaḥ pramāgaram||21||

22apaneṣyāmi gāndhāraṃ ghātayitvā śitaiḥ śaraiḥ
|
nikṛtyā vai durācāro yāni ratnāni saubalaḥ||22||

23sabhāyāmaharaddyūte punastānyāharāmyaham
|
adya tā api rotsyanti sarvā nāgapure striyaḥ||23||

24śrutvā patīṃśca putrāṃśca pāṇḍavairnihatānyudhi
|
samāptamadya vai karma sarvaṃ kṛṣṇa bhaviṣyati||24||

25adya duryodhano dīptāṃ śriyaṃ prāṇāṃśca mokṣyati
|
nāpayāti bhayātkṛṣṇaṃ saṅgrāmādyadi cenmama||25||

26nihataṃ viddhi vārṣṇeya dhārtarāṣṭraṃ subāliśam
|
mama hyetadaśaktaṃ vai vājivṛndamarindama||26||

27soḍhuṃ jyātalanirghoṣaṃ yāhi yāvannihanmyaham
|
evamuktastu dāśārhaḥ pāṇḍavena yaśasvinā||27||

28acodayaddhayān rājan duryodhanabalaṃ prati
|
tadanīkamabhiprekṣya trayaḥ sajjā mahārathāḥ||28||

29bhīmaseno'rjunaścaiva sahadevaśca māriṣa
|
prayayuḥ siṃhanādena duryodhanajighāṃsayā||29||

30tānprekṣya sahitānsarvāñjavenodyatakārmukān
|
saubalo'bhyadravadyuddhe pāṇḍavānātatāyinaḥ||30||

31sudarśanastava suto bhīmasenaṃ samabhyayāt
|
suśarmā śakuniścaiva yuyudhāte kirīṭinā||31||

32sahadevaṃ tava suto hayapṛṣṭhagato'bhyayāt
|
tato hi yatnataḥ kṣipraṃ tava putro janādhipa||32||

33prāsena sahadevasya śirasi prāharadbhṛśam
|
sopāviśadrathopasthe tava putreṇa tāḍitaḥ||33||

34rudhirāplutasarvāṅga āśīviṣa iva śvasan
|
pratilabhya tataḥ saṃjñā sahadevo viśāmpate||34||

35duryodhanaṃ śaraistīkṣṇaiḥ saṅkruddhaḥ samavākirat
|
pārtho'pi yudhi vikramya kuntīputro dhanañjayaḥ||35||

36śūrāṇāmaśvapṛṣṭhebhyaḥ śirāṃsi nicakarta ha
|
tadanīkaṃ tadā pārtho vyadhamadbahubhiḥ śaraiḥ||36||

37pātayitvā hayān sarvāstrigartānāṃ rathān yayau
|
tataste sahitā bhūtvā trigartānāṃ mahārathāḥ||37||

38arjunaṃ vāsudevaṃ ca śaravarṣairavākiran
|
satyakarmāṇamākṣipya kṣurapreṇa mahāyaśāḥ||38||

39tato'sya syandanasyeṣāṃ cicchide pāṇḍunandanaḥ
|
śilāśitena ca vibho kṣurapreṇa mahāyaśāḥ||39||

40śiraściccheda sahasā taptakuṇḍalabhūṣaṇam
|
satyeṣumadha cādatta yodhānāṃ miṣatāṃ tataḥ||40||

41yathā siṃho vane rājan mṛgaṃ paribubhukṣitaḥ
|
taṃ nihatya tataḥ pārthaḥ suśarmāṇaḥ tribhiḥ śaraiḥ||41||

42viddhvā tānahanat sarvān rathān rukmavibhūṣitān
|
tataḥ prāyāt tvaranpārtho dīrghakālaṃ susaṃvṛtam||42||

43muñcan krodhaviṣaṃ tīkṣṇaṃ prasthalādhipatiṃ prati
|
tamarjunaḥ pṛṣatkānāṃ śatena bharatarṣabha||43||

44pūrayitvā tato vāhān prāharattasya dhanvinaḥ
|
tataḥ śaraṃ samādāya yamadaṇḍopamaṃ tadā||44||

45suśarmāṇaṃ samuddiśya cikṣepāśu hasanniva
|
sa śaraḥ preṣitastena krodhadīptena dhanvinā||45||

46suśarmāṇa samāsādya bibheda hṛdayaṃ raṇe
|
sa gatāsumahārāja papāta dharaṇītale||46||

47nandayanpāṇḍavān sarvān vyathayaṃścāpi tāvakān
|
śuśarmāṇaṃ raṇe hatvā putrānasya mahārathān||47||

48sapta cāṣṭau ca triṃśacca sāyakairanayat kṣamam
|
tato'sya niśitairbāṇaiḥ sarvān hatvā padānugān||48||

49abhyagādbhāratī senāṃ hataśeṣāṃ mahārathaḥ
|
bhīmastu samare kruddhaḥ putraṃ tava janādhipa||49||

50sudarśanamadṛśyaṃ taṃ śaraiścakre hasanniva
|
tato'sya prahasan kruddhaḥ śiraḥ kāyādapāharat||50||

51kṣurapreṇa sutīkṣṇena sa hataḥ prāpatadbhuvi
|
tasmiṃstu nihate vīra tatastasya padānugāḥ||51||

52parivavū raṇe bhīmaṃ kiranto vividhāśarān
|
tatastu niśitairbāṇaistavānīkaṃ vṛkodaraḥ||52||

53indrāśanisamasparśaḥ samantātparyavākirat
|
tataḥ kṣaṇena tadbhīmo nyahanadbharatarṣabha||53||

54teṣu tūtsādyamāneṣu senādhyakṣā mahārathāḥ
|
bhīmasenaṃ samāsādya tato'yuddhyanta bhārata||54||

55sa tānsarvāśaraighorairavākirata pāṇḍavaḥ
|
tathaiva tāvakā rājanpāṇḍaveyān mahārathān||55||

56śaravarṣeṇa mahatā samantāt paryavārayan
|
vyākulaṃ tadabhūtsarvaṃ pāṇḍavānāṃ paraiḥ saha||56||

57tāvakānāṃ ca samare pāṇḍaveyairyuyutsatām
|
tatra yodhāstadā petuḥ parasparasamāhatāḥ
|
ubhayoḥ senayo rājansaṃśocantaḥ sma bāndhavān||57||
