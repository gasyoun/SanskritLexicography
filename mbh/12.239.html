Mahābhārata 12.239 (Śāntiparvan)

1bhīṣma uvāca|
ityukto'bhipraśasyaitatparamarṣestu śāsanam
|
mokṣadharmārthasaṃyuktamidaṃ praṣṭuṃ pracakrame ||1||

2śuka uvāca |
prajñāvān śrotriyo yajvā kṛtaprajño'nasūyakaḥ
|
anāgatāmanaiticaṃ kathaṃ brahmādhigacchati ||2||

3tavayasa brahmacaryeṇa sarvatyāgena medhayā
|
sāṃkhye vā yadi vā yoga etatpṛṣṭo vadasva me ||3||

4manasaścendriyāṇāṃ ca yathaikāgryamavāpyate
|
yenopāyena puruṣaistattvaṃ vyākhyātumarhasi ||4||

5vyāsa uvāca |
nānyatra vidyātapasornānyatrendriyanigrahāt
|
nānyatra sarvasantyāgātsiddhiṃ vindati kaścana ||5||

6mahābhūtāni sarvāṇi pūrvasṛṣṭiḥ svayambhuvaḥ
|
bhūyiṣṭhaṃ prāṇabhṛdgrāme niviṣṭāni śarīriṣu ||6||

7bhūmerdeho jalātsneho jyotiṣaścakṣuṣī smṛte
|
prāṇāpānāśrayo vāyuḥ kheṣvākāśaṃ śarīriṇām ||7||

8krānte viṣṇurbale śakraḥ koṣṭhe'gnirbhoktumicchati
|
karṇayoḥ pradiśaḥ śrotraṃ jihvāyāṃ vāk sarasvatī ||8||

9karṇau tvak cakṣuṣī jihvā nāsikā caiva pañcamī
|
darśanīyendriyoktāni dvārāṇyāhārasiddhaye ||9||

10śabdaḥ sparśastathā rūpaṃ, raso, gandhaśca pañcamaḥ
|
indriyārthānpṛthagvidyādindriyebhyastu nityadā ||10||

11indriyāṇi mano yuṅkte vaśyānyanteva vājinaḥ
|
panaścāpi sadā yuṅkte bhūtātmā hṛdayāśritaḥ ||11||

12indriyāṇāṃ tathaivaiṣāṃ sarveṣāmīśvaraṃ manaḥ
|
niyame ca visarge ca bhūtātmā mānasastathā ||12||

13indriyāṇīndriyārthāśca svabhāvaścetanā manaḥ
|
prāṇāpānau ca jīvaśca nityaṃ deheṣu dehinām ||13||

14āśrayo nāsti sattvasya guṇāḥ śabdo na cetanā
|
sattvaṃ hi tejaḥ sṛjati na guṇānvai kathaṃcana ||14||

15evaṃ saptadaśaṃ dehe vṛtaṃ ṣoḍaśabhirguṇaiḥ
|
manīṣī manasā vipraḥ paśyatyātmānamātmani ||15||

16na hyayaṃ cakṣuṣā dṛśyo na ca sarvairapīndriyaiḥ
|
manasā tu pradīpena mahānātmā prakāśate ||16||

17aśabdasparśarūpaṃ tadarasāgandhamavyayam
|
aśarīraṃ śarīreṣu nirīkṣeta nirindriyam ||17||

18avyaktaṃ sarvadeheṣu martyeṣu paramāśritam
|
yo'nupaśyati sa pretya kalpate brahmabhūyase ||18||

19vidyābhijanasampanne brāhmaṇe gavi hastini
|
śuci caiva śvapāke ca paṇḍitāḥ samadarśinaḥ ||19||

20sa hi sarveṣu bhūteṣu jaṅgameṣu dhruveṣu ca
|
vasatyeko mahānātmā yena sarvamidaṃ tatam ||20||

21sarvabhūteṣu cātmānaṃ sarvabhūtāni cātmani
|
yadā paśyati bhūtātmā brahma sampadyate tadā ||21||

22yāvānātmani vedātmā tāvānātmā parātmani
|
ya evaṃ satataṃ veda so'mṛtatvāya kalpate ||22||

23sarvabhūtātmabhūtasya vibhorbhūtahitasya ca
|
devā'pi mārge muhyanti apadasya padaiṣiṇaḥ ||23||

24śakuntānāmivākāśe matsyānāmiva codake
|
yathā gatirna dṛśyeta tathā jñānavidāṃ gatiḥ ||24||

25kālaḥ pacati bhūtāni sarvāṇyevātmanātmani
|
yasmiṃstu pacyate kālastaṃ vedeha na kaścana ||25||

26na tadūrdhvaṃ na tiryak ca nādho na ca punaḥ punaḥ
|
na madhye pratigṛhṇīte naiva kiṃcitkutaścana ||26||

27sarve'ntaḥsthā ime lokā bāhmameṣāṃ na kiṃcana
|
yadyajasraṃ samāgacchedyathā bāṇo guṇacyutaḥ ||27||

28naivāntaṃ kāraṇasyeyādyadyapi syānmanojavaḥ
|
tasmātsūkṣmātsūkṣmataraṃ nāsti sthūlataraṃ tataḥ ||28||

29sarvataḥ pāṇipādaṃ tatsarvato'kṣiśiromukham
|
sarvataḥ śrutimalloke sarvamāvṛtya tiṣṭhati ||29||

30tadevāṇoraṇutaraṃ tanmahadbhyo mahattaram
|
tadantaḥ sarvabhūtānāṃ dhruvaṃ tiṣṭhanna dṛśyate ||30||

31akṣaraṃ ca kṣaraṃ caiva dvaidhībhāvo'yamātmanaḥ
|
kṣaraḥ sarveṣu bhūteṣa divyaṃ tamṛtamakṣaram ||31||

32navadvāraṃ puraṃ gatvā haṃso hi niyato vaśī
|
īśaḥ sarvasya bhūtasya sthāvarasya carasya ca ||32||

33hānibhaṅgavikalpānāṃ navānāṃ saṃcayena ca
|
śarīrāṇāmajasyāhurhaṃsatvaṃ pāradarśinaḥ ||33||

34haṃsoktaṃ cākṣaraṃ caiva kūṭasthaṃ yattadakṣaram
|
tadvidvānakṣaraṃ prāpya jahāti prāṇajanmanī ||34||
