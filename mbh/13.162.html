Mahābhārata 13.162 (Anuśāsanaparvan)

1vaiśampāyana uvāca|
ityuktavati vākyaṃ tu kṛṣṇe devakinandane
|
bhīṣmaṃ śāntanavaṃ bhūyaḥ paryapṛcchadyudhiṣṭhiraḥ ||1||

2nirṇaye vā mahābuddhe sarvadharmavidāṃ vara
|
pratyakṣamāgamo veti kiṃ tayoḥ kāraṇaṃ bhavet ||2||

3bhīṣma uvāca|
nāstyatra saṃśayaḥ kaściditi me vartate matiḥ
|
śṛṇu vakṣyāmi te prājña samyak tvaṃ me'nupṛcchasi ||3||

4saṃśayaḥ sugamastatra durgamastasya nirṇayaḥ
|
dṛṣṭaṃ śrutamanantaṃ hi yatra saṃśayadarśanam ||4||

5pratyakṣaṃ kāraṇaṃ dṛṣṭvā haitukāḥ prājñamāninaḥ
|
nāstītyevaṃ vyavasyanti satyaṃ saṃśayameva ca ||5||

6tadayuktaṃ vyavasyanti bālāḥ paṇḍitamāninaḥ
|
atha cenmanyase caikaṃ kāraṇaṃ kiṃ bhavediti ||6||

7śakyaṃ dīrdheṇa kālena yuktenātandritena ca
|
prāṇayātrāmanekāṃ ca kalpamānena bhārata ||7||

8tatpareṇaiva nānyena śakyaṃ hyetasya darśanam
|
hetūnāmantamāsādya vipulaṃ jñānamuttamam ||8||

9jyotiḥ sarvasya lokasya vipulaṃ pratipadyate
|
na tveva gamanaṃ rājanhetuto gamanaṃ tathā
|
agrāhyamanibaddhaṃ ca vācā saṃparivarjayet ||9||

10yudhiṣṭhira uvāca|
pratyakṣaṃ lokataḥ siddhirlokaścāgamapūrvakaḥ
|
śiṣṭācāro bahuvidhastanme brūhi pitāmaha ||10||

11bhīṣma uvāca|
dharmasya hriyamāṇasya balavadbhirdurātmabhiḥ
|
saṃsthā yatnairapi kṛtā kālena pratibhidyate ||11||

12adharmo dharmarūpeṇa tṛṇaiḥ kūpa ivāvṛtaḥ
|
tatastairbhidyate vṛttaṃ śṛṇu caiva yudhiṣṭhira ||12||

13avṛttā ye tu bhindanti śrutityāgaparāyaṇāḥ
|
dharmavidveṣiṇo mandā ityuktasteṣu saṃśayaḥ ||13||

14atṛpyantastu sādhūnāṃ ya evāgamabuddhayaḥ
|
paramityeva santuṣṭāstānupāsva ca pṛccha ca ||14||

15kāmārthai pṛṣṭhataḥ kṛtvā lobhamohānusāriṇau
|
dharma ityeva sambuddhastānupāsva ca pṛccha ca ||15||

16na teṣāṃ bhidyate vṛttaṃ yajñāḥ svādhyāyakarma ca
|
ācāraḥ kāraṇaṃ caiva dharmaścaikastrayaṃ punaḥ ||16||

17yudhiṣṭhira uvāca|
punareva hi me buddhiḥ saṃśaye parimuhyati
|
apāre mārgamāṇasya paraṃ tīramapaśyataḥ ||17||

18vedaḥ pratyakṣamācāraḥ pramāṇaṃ tattrayaṃ yadi
|
pṛthaktvaṃ labhyate caiṣāṃ dharmaścaikastrayaṃ katham ||18||

19bhīṣma uvāca|
dharmasya hriyamāṇasya balavadbhirdurātmabhiḥ
|
yadyevaṃ manyase rājaṃstridhā dharmavicāraṇā ||19||

20eka eveti jānīhi tridhā dharmasya darśanam
|
pṛthaktve ca na me buddhistrayāṇāmapi vai tathā ||20||

21ukto mārgastrayāṇāṃ ca tattathaiva samācara
|
jijñāsā na tu kartavyā dharmasya paritarkaṇāt ||21||

22sadaiva bharataśreṣṭha mā te'bhūdatra saṃśayaḥ
|
andho jaḍa ivāśaṅkī yadbravīmi tadācara ||22||

23ahiṃsā satyamakrodho dānametaccatuṣṭayam
|
ajātaśatro sevasva dharma eṣa sanātanaḥ ||23||

24brāhmaṇeṣu ca vṛttiryā pitṛpaitāmahocitā
|
tāmanvehi mahābāho dharmasyaite hi deśikāḥ ||24||

25pramāṇamapramāṇaṃ vai yaḥ kuryāda‌budho janaḥ
|
na sa pramāṇatāmarho vivādajanano hi saḥ ||25||

26brāhmaṇāneva sevasva satkṛtya bahumanya ca
|
eteṣveva tvime lokāḥ kṛtsnā iti nibodha tān ||26||

27yudhiṣṭhira uvāca|
ye ca dharmamasūyante ye cainaṃ paryupāsate
|
bravītu me bhavānetat kva te gacchanti tādṛśāḥ ||27||

28bhīṣma uvāca|
rajasā tamasā caiva samavastīrṇacetasaḥ
|
narakaṃ pratipadyante dharmavidveṣiṇo janāḥ ||28||

29ye tu dharmaṃ mahārāja satataṃ paryupāsate
|
satyārjavaparāḥ santaste vai svargabhujo narāḥ ||29||

30dharma eva gatisteṣāmācāryopāsanādbhavet
|
devalokaṃ prapadyante ye dharmaṃ paryupāsate ||30||

31manuṣyā yadi vā devāḥ śarīramupatāpya vai
|
dharmiṇaḥ sukhamedhante lobhadveṣavivarjitāḥ ||31||

32prathamaṃ brahmaṇaḥ putraṃ dharmamāhurmanīṣiṇaḥ
|
dharmiṇaḥ paryupāsante phalaṃ pakkamivāśayaḥ ||32||

33yudhiṣṭhira uvāca|
asatāṃ kīdṛśaṃ rūpaṃ sādhavaḥ kiṃ ca kurvate
|
bravītu me bhavānetatsanto'santaśca kīdṛśāḥ ||33||

34bhīṣma uvāca|
durācārāśca durdharṣā durmukhāścāpyasādhavaḥ
|
sādhavaḥ śīlasaṃpannāḥ śiṣṭācārasya lakṣaṇam ||34||

35rājamārge gavāṃ madhye dhānyamadhye ca dharmiṇaḥ
|
nopasevanti rājendra sargaṃ sūtrapurīṣayoḥ ||35||

36pañcānāmaśanaṃ datvā śeṣamaśnanti sādhavaḥ
|
na jalpanti ca bhuñjānā na nidrāntyārdrapāṇayaḥ ||36||

37citrabhānumanaḍvāhaṃ devaṃ goṣṭhaṃ catuṣpatham
|
brāhmaṇaṃ dhārmikaṃ vṛddhaṃ ye kurvanti pradakṣiṇam ||37||

38vṛddhānāṃ bhārataptānāṃ strīṇāṃ cakradharasya ca
|
brāhmaṇānāṃ gavāṃ rājñāṃ panthānaṃ dadate ca ye ||38||

39atithīnāṃ ca sarveṣāṃ preṣyāṇāṃ svajanasya ca
|
tathā śaraṇakāmānāṃ goptā syātsvāgatapradaḥ ||39||

40sāyaṃprātarmanuṣyāṇāmaśanaṃ devanirmitam
|
nāntarā bhojanaṃ dṛṣṭamupavāsavidhirhi saḥ ||40||

41homakāle yathā vahniḥ kālameva pratīkṣate
|
ṛtukāle tathā nārī ṛtumeva pratīkṣate ||41||

42nānyadā gacchate yastu brahmacaryaṃ ca tatsmṛtam
|
amṛtaṃ brāhmaṇā gāva ityetattrayamekataḥ ||42||

43tasmādgobrāhmaṇaṃ nityamarcayeta yathāvidhi
|
yajuṣā saṃskṛtaṃ māṃsamupabhuñjanna duṣyati
|
pṛṣṭhamāṃsaṃ vṛthāmāṃsaṃ putramāṃsaṃ ca tatsamam ||43||

44svadeśe paradeśe vāpyatithiṃ nopavāsayet
|
karma vai saphalaṃ kṛtvā gurūṇāṃ pratipādayet ||44||

45gurubhyastvāsanaṃ deyamabhivādyābhipūjya ca
|
gurumabhyarcya vardhante āyuṣā yaśasā śriyā ||45||

46vṛddhānnābhibhavejjātu na caitānpreṣayediti
|
nāsīnaḥ syātsthiteṣvevamāyurasya na riṣyate ||46||

47na nagnāmīkṣate nārīṃ na nagnānpuruṣānapi
|
maithunaṃ satataṃ guptamāhāraṃ ca samācaret ||47||

48tīrthānāṃ guravastīrthaṃ cokṣāṇāṃ hṛdayaṃ śuci
|
darśanānāṃ paraṃ jñānaṃ santoṣaḥ paramaṃ sukham ||48||

49sāyaṃ prātaśca vṛddhānāṃ śṛṇuyātpuṣkalā giraḥ
|
śrutamāpnoti hi naraḥ satataṃ vṛddhasevayā ||49||

50svādhyāye bhojane caiva dakṣiṇaṃ pāṇimuddharet
|
yacchedvāṅmanasī nityamindriyāṇi tathaiva ca ||50||

51saṃskṛtaṃ pāyasaṃ nityaṃ yavāgūṃ kṛsaraṃ haviḥ
|
aṣṭakāḥ pitṛdaivatyā grahāṇāmabhipūjanam ||51||

52śmaśrukarmaṇi maṅgalyaṃ śrutānāmabhinandanam
|
vyādhitānāṃ ca sarveṣāmāyuṣāmabhinandanam ||52||

53na jātu tvamiti brūyādāpannopi mahattaram
|
tvaṃkāro vā vadho veti vidvatsu na viśiṣyate ||53||

54avarāṇāṃ samānānāṃ śiṣyāṇāṃ ca samācaret
|
pāpamācakṣate nityaṃ hṛdayaṃ pāpakarmiṇaḥ ||54||

55jñānapūrvakṛtaṃ karma cchādayante hyasādhavaḥ
|
jñānapūrvaṃ vinaśyanti gūhamānā mahājane ||55||

56na māṃ manuṣyāḥ paśyanti na māṃ paśyanti devatāḥ
|
pāpenābhihitaḥ pāpaḥ pāpamevābhijāyate ||56||

57yathā vārdhuṣiko vṛddhiṃ dinabhede pratīkṣate
|
dharmeṇa pihitaṃ pāpaṃ dharmamevābhivardhayet ||57||

58yathā lavaṇamaṃbhobhirāplutaṃ pravilīyate
|
prāyaścittahataṃ pāpaṃ tathā sadyaḥ praṇaśyati ||58||

59tasmātpāpaṃ na gūheta gūhamānaṃ vivardhayet
|
kṛtvā tatsādhuṣvākhyeyaṃ te tatpraśamayantyuta ||59||

60āśayā sañcitaṃ dravyaṃ kālenaivopabhujyate
|
anye caitatprapadyante viyoge tasya dehinaḥ ||60||

61mānasaṃ sarvabhūtānāṃ dharmamāhurmanīṣiṇaḥ
|
tasmātsarvāṇi bhūtāni dharmameva samāsate ||61||

62eka eva careddharmaṃ na dharmadhvajiko bhavet
|
dharmavāṇijakā hyete ye dharmamupabhuñjate ||62||

63arceddevānadambhena sevetāmāyayā gurūn
|
nidhiṃ nidadhyātpāratryaṃ yātrārthaṃ dānaśabditama ||63||
