Mahābhārata 12.346 (Śāntiparvan)

1vaiśampāyana uvāca|
śrutvaitannārado vākyaṃ naranārāyaṇeritam
|
atyantaṃ bhaktimāndeve ekāntitvamupeyivān||1||

2proṣya varṣasahasraṃ tu naranārāyaṇāśrame
|
śrutvā bhagavadākhyānaṃ dṛṣṭvā ca harimavyayam||2||

3himavantaṃ jagāmāśu yatrāsya svaka āśramaḥ
|
tāvapi khyātatapasau naranārāyaṇāvṛṣī||3||

4tasminnevāśrame ramye tepatustapa uttamam
|
tvamapyamitavikrāntaḥ pāṇḍavānāṃ kulodvahaḥ||4||

5pāvitātmādya saṃvṛttaḥ śrutvemāmādita: kathām
|
naiva tasyāparo loko nāyaṃ pārthivasattama||5||

6karmaṇā manasā vācā yo dviṣyādviṣṇumavyayam
|
majjanti pitarastasya narake śāśvatī: samā:||6||

7yo dviṣyādvibudhaśreṣṭhaṃ devaṃ nārāyaṇaṃ harim
|
kathaṃ nāma bhaveddveṣya ātmā lokasya kasyacit||7||

8ātmā hi puruṣavyāghra jñeyo viṣṇuriti sthitiḥ
|
ya eṣa gururasmākamṛṣirgandhavatīsutaḥ||8||

9tenaitatkathitaṃ tāta māhātmyaṃ paramavyayam
|
tasmācchrutaṃ mayā cedaṃ kathitaṃ ca tavānagha||9||

10nāradena tu saṃprāptaḥ sarahasya: saṃsagrahaḥ
|
eṣa dharmo jagannāthātsākṣānnārāyaṇānnṛpa||10||

11evameṣa mahāndharmaḥ sa te pūrvaṃ nṛpottama
|
kathito harigītāsu samāsavidhikalpitaḥ||11||

12kṛṣṇadvaipāyanaṃ vyāsaṃ viddhi nārāyaṇaṃ bhuvi
|
ko hyanyaḥ puruṣavyāghra mahābhāratakṛdbhavet||12||

13dharmānnānāvidhāṃścaiva ko brūyāttamṛte prabhum||13||

14vartatāṃ te mahāyajño yathā saṃkalpitastvayā
|
saṃkalpitāśvamedhastvaṃ śrutadharmaśca tattvataḥ||14||

15sautiruvāca |
etattu mahadākhyānaṃ śrutvā pārthivasattamaḥ
|
tato yajñasamāptyarthaṃ kriyāḥ sarvāḥ samārabhat||15||

16nārāyaṇīyamākhyānametatte kathitaṃ mayā
|
pṛṣṭena śaunakādyeha naimiṣāraṇyavāsiṣu||16||

17nāradena purā yadvai gurave tu niveditam
|
ṛṣīṇāṃ pāṇḍavānāṃ ca śṛṇvatoḥ kṛṣṇabhīṣmayo||17||

18sa hi paramarṣirjanabhuvanapatiḥ pṛthudharaṇidharaḥ śrutivinayanidhiḥ
|
śamaniyamanidhiryamaniyamaparo dvijavarasahitastava ca bhavatugatirhariramarahitaḥ||18||

19asuravadhakarastapasāṃ nidhiḥ sumahatāṃ yaśasāṃ ca bhājanam
|
madhukaiṭabhahā kṛtadharmavidāṃgatido'bhayadomakhabhāgaharo'stu śaraṇaṃ sa te||19||

20triguṇo viguṇaścaturātmadharaḥ pūrteṣṭayośca phalabhāgaharaḥ
|
vidadhātu nityamajito'ticalo gatimātmagāṃ sukṛtināmṛṣīṇām||20||

21taṃ lokasākṣiṇamajaṃ puruśaṃ purāṇaṃ ravivarṇamīśvaraṃ gatiṃ bahuśaḥ
|
praṇamadhvamekamanaso yataḥ salilodbhavo'pi tamṛṣiṃ praṇataḥ||21||

22sa hi lokayoniramṛtasya padaṃ sūkṣmaṃ parāyaṇamacalaṃ hi padam
|
tatsāṃkhyogibhirudāravṛtaṃ buddhyā yatātmabhiridaṃ sanātanam||22||
