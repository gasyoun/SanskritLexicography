Mahābhārata 5.106 (Udyogaparvan)

1janamejaya uvāca|
anarthe jātanirbandhaṃ parārthe lobhamohitam
|
anāryakeṣvabhirataṃ maraṇe kṛtaniścayam ||1||

2jñātīnāṃ duḥkhakartāraṃ bandhūnāṃ śokavardhanam
|
suhṛdāṃ kleśadātāraṃ dviṣatāṃ harṣavardhanam ||2||

3kathaṃ nainaṃ vimārgasthaṃ vārayantīha bāndhavāḥ
|
sauhṛdādvā suhṛt snigdho bhagavān vā pitāmahaḥ ||3||

4vaiśampāyana uvāca|
uktaṃ bhagavatā vākyamuktaṃ bhīṣmeṇa yatkṣamam
|
uktaṃ bahuvidhaṃ caiva nāradenāpi tacchṛṇu ||4||

5nārada uvāca|
durlabho vai suhṛcchrotā durlabhaśca hitaḥ suhṛt
|
tiṣṭhate hi suhṛdyatra na bandhustatra tiṣṭhate ||5||

6śrotavyamapi paśyāmi suhṛdāṃ kurunandana
|
na kartavyaśca nirbandho nirbandho hi sudāruṇaḥ ||6||

7atrāpyudāharantīmamitihāsaṃ purātanam
|
yathā nirbandhataḥ prāpto gālavena parājayaḥ ||7||

8viśvāmitraṃ tapasyantaṃ dharmo jijñāsayā purā
|
abhyagacchatsvayaṃ bhūtvā vasiṣṭho bhagavānṛṣiḥ ||8||

9saptarṣīṇāmanyatamaṃ veṣamāsthāya bhārata
|
bubhukṣuḥ kṣubhito rājannāśramaṃ kauśikasya tu ||9||

10viśvāmitro'tha saṃbhrāntaḥ śrapayāmāsa vai carum
|
paramānnasya yatnena na ca taṃ pratyapālayat ||10||

11annaṃ tena yadā bhuktamanyairdattaṃ tapasvibhiḥ
|
atha gṛhyānnamatyuṣṇaṃ viśvāmitro'pyupāgamat ||11||

12bhuktaṃ me tiṣṭha tāvattvamityuktvā bhagavān yayau
|
viśvāmitrastato rājan sthita eva mahādyutiḥ ||12||

13bhaktaṃ pragṛhya mūrdhnā vai bāhubhyāṃ saṃśitavrataḥ
|
sthitaḥ sthāṇurivābhyāśe niśceṣṭo mārutāśanaḥ ||13||

14tasya śuśrūṣaṇe yatnamakarodgālavo muniḥ
|
gauravādbahumānācca hārdena priyakāmyayā ||14||

15atha varṣaśate pūrṇe dharmaḥ punarupāgamat
|
vāsiṣṭhaṃ veṣamāsthāya kauśikaṃ bhojanepsayā ||15||

16sa dṛṣṭvā śirasā bhaktaṃ dhriyamāṇaṃ maharṣiṇā
|
tiṣṭhatā vāyubhakṣeṇa viśvāmitreṇa dhīmatā ||16||

17pratigṛhya tato dharmastathaivoṣṇaṃ tathā navam
|
bhuktvā prīto'smi viprarṣe tamuktvā sa munirgataḥ ||17||

18kṣatrabhāvādapagato brāhmaṇatvamupāgataḥ
|
dharmasya vacanāt prīto viśvāmitrastathā'bhavat ||18||

19viśvāmitrastu śiṣyasya gālavasya tapasvinaḥ
|
śuśrūṣayā ca bhaktyā ca prītimānityuvāca ha ||19||

20anujñāto mayā vatsa yatheṣṭaṃ gaccha gālava
|
ityuktaḥ pratyuvācedaṃ gālavo munisattamam ||20||

21prīto madhurayā vācā viśvāmitraṃ mahādyutim
|
dakṣiṇāḥ kāḥ prayacchāmi bhavate gurukarmaṇi ||21||

22dakṣiṇābhirupetaṃ hi karma siddhyati mānada
|
dakṣiṇānāṃ hi dātā vai apavargeṇa yujyate ||22||

23svarge ṛtuphalaṃ taddhi dakṣiṇā śāntirucyate
|
kimāharāmi gurvarthaṃ bravītu bhagavāniti ||23||

24jānānastena bhagavān jitaḥ śuśrūṣaṇena vai
|
viśvāmitrastamasakṛdgaccha gacchetyacodayat ||24||

25asakṛdgaccha gaccheti viśvāmitreṇa bhāṣitaḥ
|
kiṃ dadānīti bahuśo gālavaḥ pratyabhāṣata ||25||

26nirbandhatastu bahuśo gālavasya tapasvinaḥ
|
kiñcidāgatasaṃrambho viśvāmitro'bravīdidam ||26||

27ekataḥ śyāmakarṇānāṃ hayānāṃ candravarcasām
|
aṣṭau śatāni me dehi gaccha gālava mā ciram ||27||
