Mahābhārata 5.153 (Udyogaparvan)

1janamejaya uvāca|
yudhiṣṭhiraṃ sahānīkamupāyāntaṃ yuyutsayā
|
sanniviṣṭaṃ kurukṣetre vāsudevena pālitam ||1||

2virāṭadrupadābhyāṃ ca saputrābhyāṃ samanvitam
|
kekayairvṛṣṇibhiścaiva pārthivaiḥ śataśo vṛtam ||2||

3mahendramiva cādityairabhiguptaṃ mahārathaiḥ
|
śrutvā duryodhano rājā kiṃ kāryaṃ pratyapadyata ||3||

4etadicchāmyahaṃ śrotuṃ vistareṇa mahāmate
|
sambhrame tumule tasmin yadāsīt kurujāṅgale ||4||

5vyathayeyurime devān sendrānapi samāgame
|
pāṇḍavā vāsudevaśca virāṭadrupadau tathā ||5||

6dhṛṣṭadyumnaśca pāñcālyaḥ śikhaṇḍī ca mahārathaḥ
|
yudhāmanyuśca vikrānto devairapi durāsadaḥ ||6||

7etadicchāmyahaṃ śrotuṃ vistareṇa tapodhana
|
kurūṇāṃ pāṇḍavānāṃ ca yadyadāsīdviceṣṭitam ||7||

8vaiśampāyana uvāca|
pratiyāte tu dāśārhe rājā duryodhanastadā
|
karṇaṃ duḥśāsanaṃ caiva śakuniṃ cābravīdidam ||8||

9akṛtenaiva kāryeṇa gataḥ pārthānadhokṣajaḥ
|
sa enānmanyunāviṣṭo dhruvaṃ dhakṣyatyasaṃśayam ||9||

10iṣṭo hi vāsudevasya pāṇḍavairmama vigrahaḥ
|
bhīmasenārjunau caiva dāśārhasya mate sthitau ||10||

11ajātaśatruratyarthaṃ bhīmasenavaśānugaḥ
|
nikṛtaśca mayā pūrvaṃ saha sarvaiḥ sahodaraiḥ ||11||

12virāṭadrupadau caiva kṛtavairau mayā saha
|
tau ca senāpraṇetārau vāsudevavaśānugau ||12||

13bhavitā vigrahaḥ so'yaṃ tumulo lomaharṣaṇaḥ
|
tasmāt sāṅgrāmikaṃ sarvaṃ kārayadhvamatandritāḥ ||13||

14śibirāṇi kurukṣetre kriyantāṃ vasudhādhipāḥ
|
svaparyāptāvakāśāni durādeyāni śatrubhiḥ||14||

15āsannajalakoṣṭhāni śataśo'tha sahasraśaḥ
|
acchedyāhāramārgāṇi bandhocchrayacitāni ca ||15||

16vividhāyudhapūrṇāni patākādhvajavanti ca
|
samāśca teṣāṃ panthānaḥ kriyantāṃ nagarādbahiḥ ||16||

17prayāṇaṃ ghuṣyatāmadya śvobhūta iti mā ciram
|
te tatheti pratijñāya śvobhūte cakrire tathā ||17||

18hṛṣṭarūpā mahātmāno nivāsāya mahīkṣitām
|
tataste pārthivāḥ sarve tacchrutvā rājaśāsanam ||18||

19āsanebhyo mahārhebhya udatiṣṭhannamarṣitāḥ
|
bāhūn parighasaṅkāśān saṃspṛśantaḥ śanaiḥ śanaiḥ ||19||

20kāñcanāṅgadadīptāṃśca candanāgurubhūṣitān
|
uṣṇīṣāṇi niyacchantaḥ puṇḍarīkanibhaiḥ karaiḥ
|
antarīyottarīyāṇi bhūṣaṇāni ca sarvaśaḥ ||20||

21te rathān rathinaḥ śreṣṭhā hayāṃśca hayakovidāḥ
|
sajjayanti sma nāgāṃśca nāgaśikṣāsvanuṣṭhitāḥ ||21||

22atha varmāṇi citrāṇi kāñcanāni bahūni ca
|
vividhāni ca śastrāṇi cakruḥ sarvāṇi sarvaśaḥ ||22||

23padātayaśca puruṣāḥ śastrāṇi vividhāni ca
|
upājahruḥ śarīreṣu hemacitrāṇyanekaśaḥ ||23||

24tadutsava ivodagraṃ saṃprahṛṣṭanarāvṛtam
|
nagaraṃ dhārtarāṣṭrasya bhāratāsītsamākulam ||24||

25janaughasalilāvarto rathanāgāśvamīnavān
|
śaṅkhadundubhinirghoṣaḥ kośasañcayaratnavān ||25||

26citrābharaṇavarmormiḥ śastranirmalaphenavān
|
prāsāda‌mālādrivṛto rathyāpaṇamahāhradaḥ ||26||

27yodhacandrodayodbhūtaḥ kururājamahārṇavaḥ
|
vyadṛśyata tadā rājaṃścandrodaya ivodadhiḥ ||27||
