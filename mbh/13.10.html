Mahābhārata 13.10 (Anuśāsanaparvan)

1yudhiṣṭhira uvāca|
mitrasauhārdayogena upadeśaṃ karoti yaḥ
|
jātyā'dharasya rājarṣe doṣastasya bhavenna vā ||1||

2etadicchāmi tattvena vyākhyātuṃ vai pitāmaha
|
sūkṣmā gatirhi dharmasya yatra muhyanti mānavāḥ ||2||

3bhīṣma uvāca|
atra te vartayiṣyāmi śṛṇu rājan yathākramam
|
ṛṣīṇāṃ vadatāṃ pūrvaṃ śrutamāsīt yathā purā ||3||

4upadeśo na kartavyo jātihīnasya kasyacit
|
upadeśe mahān doṣa upādhyāyasya bhāṣyate ||4||

5nidarśanamidaṃ rājan śruṇu me bharatarṣabha
|
duruktavacane rājan yathāpūrvaṃ yudhiṣṭhira ||5||

6brahmāśramapade vṛttaṃ pārśve himavataḥ śubhe
|
tatrāśramapadaṃ puṇyaṃ nānāvṛkṣagaṇāyutam ||6||

7nānāgulmalatākīrṇaṃ mṛgadvijaniṣevitam
|
siddhacāraṇasaṃyuktaṃ ramyaṃ puṣpitakānanam ||7||

8vratibhirbahubhiḥ kīrṇaṃ tāpasairupasevitam
|
brāhmaṇaiśca mahābhāgaiḥ sūryajvalanasannibhaiḥ ||8||

9niyamavratasampannaiḥ samākīrṇaṃ tapasvibhiḥ
|
dīkṣitairbharataśreṣṭha yatāhāraiḥ kṛtātmabhiḥ ||9||

10tapodhyayanaghoṣaiśca nāditaṃ bharatarṣabha
|
vālakhilyaiśca bahubhiryatibhiśca niṣevitam ||10||

11tatra kaścitsamutsāhaṃ kṛtvā śudro dayānvitaḥ
|
āgato hyāśramapadaṃ pūjitaśca tapasvibhiḥ ||11||

12tāṃstu dṛṣṭvā munigaṇāndevakalpānmahaujasaḥ
|
vividhāṃ vahato dīkṣāṃ samprāhṛṣyata bhārata ||12||

13athāsya buddhirabhavattapasye bharatarṣabha
|
tato'bravītkulapatiṃ pādau saṅgṛhya bhārata ||13||

14bhavatprasādādicchāmi dharmaṃ vaktuṃ dvijarṣabha
|
tanmāṃ tvaṃ bhagavanvaktuṃ pravrājayitumarhasi ||14||

15varṇāvaro'haṃ bhagavan śūdro jātyāsmi sattama
|
śuśrūṣāṃ kartumicchāmi prapannāya prasīda me ||15||

16kulapatiruvāca|
na śakyamiha śūdreṇa liṅgamāśritya vartitum
|
āsyatāṃ yadi te buddhiḥ śuśrūṣānirato bhava ||16||

17śuśrūṣayā parā~llokānavāpsyasi na saṃśayaḥ ||17||

18bhīṣma uvāca|
evamuktastu muninā sa śūdro cintayannṛpa
|
kathamatra mayā kāryaṃ śraddhā dharmaparā ca me ||18||

19vijñātamevaṃ bhavatu kariṣye priyamātmanaḥ
|
gatvā''śramapadāddūramuṭajaṃ kṛtavāṃstu saḥ ||19||

20tatra vedīṃ ca bhūmiṃ ca devatāyatanāni ca
|
niveśya bharataśreṣṭha niyamastho'bhavanmuniḥ ||20||

21abhiṣekāṃśca niyamān devatāyataneṣu ca
|
baliṃ ca kṛtvā hutvā ca devatāṃ cāpyapūjayat ||21||

22saṅkalpaniyamopetaḥ phalāhāro jitendriyaḥ
|
nityaṃ sannihitābhistu oṣadhībhiḥ phalaistathā ||22||

23atithīnpūjayāmāsa yathāvatsamupāgatān
|
evaṃ hi sumahānkālo vyatyakrāmata tasya vai ||23||

24athāsya munirāgacchatsaṅgatyā vai tamāśramam
|
sampūjya svāgatenarṣiṃ vidhivatsamatoṣayat ||24||

25anukūlāḥ kathāḥ kṛtvā yathāgatamapṛcchata
|
ṛṣiḥ paramatejasvī dharmātmā saṃśitavrataḥ ||25||

26evaṃ subahuśastasya śūdrasya bharatarṣabha
|
so'gacchadāśramamṛṣiḥ śūdraṃ draṣṭuṃ nararṣabha ||26||

27atha taṃ tāpasaṃ śūdraḥ so'bravīdbharatarṣabha
|
pitṛkāryaṃ kariṣyāmi tatra me'nugrahaṃ kuru ||27||

28bāḍhamityeva taṃ vipra uvāca bharatarṣabha
|
śucirbhūtvā sa śudrastu tasyarṣeḥ pādyamānayat ||28||

29atha darbhāṃśca vanyāṃśca oṣadhīrbharatarṣabha
|
pavitramāsanaṃ caiva bṛsīṃ ca samupānayat ||29||

30atha dakṣiṇamāvṛtya bṛsīṃ caramaśairṣikīm
|
kṛtāmanyāyato dṛṣṭvā taṃ śūdramṛṣirabravīt ||30||

31kuruṣvaitāṃ pūrvaśīrṣāṃ bhavāṃścodaṅmukhaḥ śuciḥ
|
sa ca tatkṛtavān śūdraḥ sarvaṃ yadṛṣirabravīt ||31||

32yathopadiṣṭaṃ medhāvī darbhārghādi yathātatham
|
havyakavyavidhiṃ kṛtsnamuktaṃ tena tapasvinā ||32||

33ṛṣiṇā pitṛkārye ca sa ca dharmapathe sthitaḥ
|
pitṛkārye kṛte cāpi visṛṣṭaḥ sa jagāma ha ||33||

34atha dīrghasya kālasya sa tapyan śūdratāpasaḥ
|
vane pañcatvamagamatsukṛtena ca tena vai ||34||

35ajāyata mahārāja vaṃśe sa ca mahādyutiḥ ||35||

36tathaiva sa ṛṣistāta kāladharmamavāpa ha ||36||

37purohatakule vipra ājāto bharatarṣabha
|
evaṃ tau tatra sambhūtāvubhau śūdramunī tadā ||37||

38krameṇa vardhitau cāpi vidyāsu kuśalāvubhau
|
atharvavede vede ca babhūvarṣiḥ suniṣṭhitaḥ
|
kalpaprayoge cotpanne jyotiṣe ca paraṃ gataḥ ||38||

39sāṃkhye caiva parā prītistasya caivaṃ vyavardhata
|
pitaryuparate cāpi kṛtaśaucastu pārthivaḥ ||39||

40abhiṣiktaḥ prakṛtibhī rājaputraḥ sa pārthivaḥ
|
abhiṣiktena sa ṛṣirabhiṣiktaḥ purohitaḥ ||40||

41sa taṃ purodhāya sukhamavasadbharatarṣabha
|
rājyaṃ śaśāsa dharmeṇa prajāśca paripālayan ||41||

42puṇyāhavācane nityaṃ dharmakāryeṣu cāsakṛt
|
utsmayanprāhasaccāpi dṛṣṭvā rājā purohitam ||42||

43evaṃ sa bahuśo rājan purodhasamupāhasat
|
lakṣayitvā purodhāstu bahuśastaṃ narādhipam ||43||

44utsmayantaṃ ca satataṃ dṛṣṭvā'sau manyumāviśat
|
atha śūnye purodhāstu saha rājñā samāgataḥ ||44||

45kathābhiranukūlābhī rājānaṃ cābhyarocayata
|
tato'bravīnnarendraṃ sa purodhā bharatarṣabha ||45||

46varamicchāmyahaṃ tvekaṃ tvayā dattaṃ mahādyute ||46||

47rājovāca
|
varāṇāṃ te śataṃ dadyāṃ kiṃ bataikaṃ dvijottama
|
snehācca bahumānācca nāstyadeyaṃ hi me tava ||47||

48purohita uvāca|
ekaṃ vai varamicchāmi yadi tuṣṭo'si pārthiva
|
pratijānīhi tāvattvaṃ satyaṃ yadvada nānṛtam ||48||

49bhīṣma uvāca|
bāḍhamityeva taṃ rājā pratyuvāca yudhiṣṭhira
|
yadi jñāsyāmi vakṣyāmi ajānanna tu saṃvade ||49||

50purohita uvāca|
puṇyāhavācane nityaṃ dharmakṛtyeṣu cāsakṛt
|
śāntihomeṣu ca sadā kiṃ tvaṃ hasasi vīkṣya mām ||50||

51savrīḍaṃ vai bhavati hi mano me hasatā tvayā
|
kāmayā śāpito rājannānyathā vaktumarhasi ||51||

52suvyaktaṃ kāraṇaṃ hyatra na te hāsyamakāraṇam
|
kautūhalaṃ me subhṛśaṃ tattvena kathayasva me ||52||

53rājovāca
|
evamukte tvayā vipra yadavācyaṃ bhavedapi
|
avaśyameva vaktavyaṃ śṛṇuṣvaikamanā dvija ||53||

54pūrvadehe yathā vṛttaṃ tannibodha dvijottama
|
jātiṃ smarāmyahaṃ brahmannavadhānena me śruṇu ||54||

55śūdro'hamabhavaṃ pūrvaṃ tāpaso bhṛśasaṃyutaḥ
|
ṛṣirugratapāstvaṃ ca tadā'bhūrdvijasattama ||55||

56prīyatā hi tadā brahmanmamānugrahabuddhinā
|
pitṛkārye tvayā pūrvamupadeśaḥ kṛto'nagha ||56||

57bṛsyāṃ darbheṣu havye ca kavye ca munisattama
|
etena karmadoṣeṇa purodhāstvamajāyathāḥ ||57||

58ahaṃ rājā ca viprendra paśya kālasya paryayam
|
matkṛtasyopadeśasya tvayā''vāptamidaṃ phalam ||58||

59etasmātkāraṇādbrahmanprahase tvāṃ dvijottama
|
na tvāṃ paribhavanbrahmanprahasāmi gururbhavān ||59||

60viparyayeṇa me manyustena santapyate manaḥ
|
jātiṃ smarāmyahaṃ tubhyamatastvāṃ prahasāmi vai ||60||

61evaṃ tavograṃ hi tapa upadeśena nāśitam
|
purohitatvamutsṛjya yatasva tvaṃ punarbhave ||61||

62itastvamadhamāmanyāṃ mā yoniṃ prāpsyase dvija
|
gṛhyatāṃ draviṇaṃ vipra pūtātmā bhava sattama ||62||

63bhīṣma uvāca|
tato visṛṣṭo rājñā tu vipro dānānyanekaśaḥ
|
brāhmaṇebhyo dadau vittaṃ bhūmiṃ grāmāṃśca sarvaśaḥ ||63||

64kṛcchrāṇi cīrtvā ca tato yathoktāni dvijottamaiḥ
|
tīrthāni cāpi gatvā vai dānāni vividhāni ca ||64||

65datvā gāścaiva viprebhyaḥ pūtātmā'bhavadātmavān
|
tameva cāśramaṃ gattvā cacāra vipulaṃ tapaḥ ||65||

66tataḥ siddhiṃ parāṃ prāpto brāhmaṇo rājasattama
|
sammataścābhavatteṣāmāśrame tannivāsinām ||66||

67evaṃ prāpto mahatkṛcchramṛṣiḥ sannṛpasattama
|
brāhmaṇena na vaktavyaṃ tasmādvarṇāvare jane ||67||

68brāhmaṇāḥ kṣatriyā vaiśyāstrayo varṇā dvijātayaḥ
|
eteṣu kathayanrājanbrāhmaṇo na praduṣyati ||68||

69tasmātsadbhirna vaktavyaṃ kasyacitkiñcidagrataḥ
|
sūkṣmā gatirhi dharmasya durjñeyā hyakṛtātmabhiḥ ||69||

70tasmānmaunena munayo dīkṣāṃ kurvanti cādṛtāḥ
|
duruktasya bhayādrājannābhāṣante ca kiñcana ||70||

71dhārmikā guṇasampannāḥ satyārjavasamanvitāḥ
|
duruktavācābhihitaiḥ prāpnuvantīha duṣkṛtam ||71||

72upadeśo na kartavyaḥ kadācidapi kasyacit
|
upadeśāddhi tatpāpaṃ brāhmaṇaḥ samavāpnuyāt ||72||

73vimṛśya tasmātprājñena vaktavyaṃ dharmamicchatā
|
satyānṛtena hi kṛta upadeśī hinasti hi ||73||

74vaktavyamiha pṛṣṭena viniścitya viniścayam
|
sa copadeśaḥ kartavyo yena dharmamavāpnuyāt ||74||

75etatte sarvamākhyātamupadeśakṛte mayā
|
mahān kleśo hi bhavati tasmānnopadiśediha ||75||
