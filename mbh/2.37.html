Mahābhārata 2.37 (Sabhāparvan)

1śiśupāla uvāca|
nāyamarhati vārṣṇeyastiṣṭhatsviha mahātmasu
|
mahīpatiṣu kauravya rājavatpārthivārhaṇam||1||

2nāyaṃ yuktaḥ samācāraḥ pāṇḍaveṣu mahātmasu
|
yatkāmātpuṇḍarīkākṣaṃ pāṇḍavārcitavānasi||2||

3bālā yūyaṃ na jānīdhvaṃ dharmaḥ sūkṣmo hi pāṇḍavāḥ
|
ayaṃ ca smṛtyatikrānto hyāpageyo'lpadarśanaḥ||3||

4tvādṛśo dharmayukto hi kurvāṇaḥ priyakāmyayā
|
bhavatyabhyadhikaṃ bhīṣmo lokeṣvavamataḥ satām||4||

5kathaṃ hyarājā dāśārho madhye sarvamahīkṣitām
|
arhaṇāmarhati tathā yathā yuṣmābhirarcitaḥ||5||

6atha vā manyase kṛṣṇaṃ sthaviraṃ kurupuṅgavaḥ
|
vasudeve sthite vṛddhe kathamarhati tatsutaḥ||6||

7atha vā vāsudevo'pi priyakāmo'nuvṛttavān
|
drupade tiṣṭhati kathaṃ mādhavo'rhati pūjanam||7||

8ācāryaṃ manyase kṛṣṇamatha vā kurunandana
|
droṇe tiṣṭhati vārṣṇeyaṃ kasmādarcitavānasi||8||

9ṛtvijaṃ manyase kṛṣṇamatha vā kurunandana
|
dvaupāyane sthite vṛddhe kathaṃ kṛṣṇo'rcitastvayā||9||

10bhīṣme śāntanave rājansthite puruṣasattame
|
svacchandamṛtyuke rājankathaṃ kṛṣṇo'rcitastvayā||10||

11aśvatthāmni sthite vīre sarvaśāstraviśārade
|
kathaṃ kṛṣṇastvayā rājannarcitaḥ kurunandana||11||

12duryodhane ca rājendre sthite puruṣasattame
|
kṛpe ca bhāratācārye kathaṃ kṛṣṇastvayā'rcitaḥ||12||

13drumaṃ kiṃpuruṣācāryamatikramya tathā'rcitaḥ
|
bhīṣmake caiva durdharṣe pāṇḍuvatkṛtalakṣaṇe||13||

14nṛpe ca rukmiṇi śreṣṭhe ekalavye tathaiva ca
|
śalye madrādhipe caiva kathaṃ kṛṣṇastvayārcitaḥ||14||

15ayaṃ ca sarvarājñāṃ vai balaślāghī mahābalaḥ
|
jāmadagnyasya dayitaḥ śiṣyo viprasya bhārata||15||

16yenātmabalamāśritya rājāno yudhi nirjitāḥ
|
taṃ ca karṇamatikramya kathaṃ kṛṣṇastvayārcitaḥ||16||

17naivartviṅnaiva cācāryo na rājā madhusūdanaḥ
|
arcitaśca kuruśreṣṭha kimanyatpriyakāmyayā||17||

18athavā'bhyarcanīyo'yaṃ yuṣmākaṃ madhusūdanaḥ
|
kiṃ rājabhirihānītairavamānāya bhārata||18||

19vayaṃ tu na bhayādasya kaunteyasya mahātmanaḥ
|
prayacchāmaḥ karānsarve na lobhānna ca sāntvanāt||19||

20asya dharmapravṛttasya pārthivatvaṃ cikīrṣataḥ
|
karānasmai prayacchāmaḥ so'yamasmānna manyate||20||

21kimanyadavamanānāddhe yadenaṃ rājasaṃsadi
|
aprāptalakṣaṇaṃ kṛṣṇamarghyeṇārcitavānasi||21||

22akasmāddharmaputrasya dharmātmeti yaśo gatam
|
ko hi dharmacyute pūjāmevaṃ yuktāṃ niyojayet||22||

23yoyaṃ vṛṣṇikule jāto rājānaṃ hatavānpurā
|
jarāsandhaṃ mahātmānamanyāyena durātmavān||23||

24adya dharmātmatā caiva vyapakṛṣṭā yudhiṣṭhirāt
|
darśitaṃ kṛpaṇatvaṃ ca kṛṣṇe'rghyasya nivedanāt||24||

25yadi bhītāśca kaunteyāḥ kṛpaṇāśca tapasvinaḥ
|
nanu tvayā'pi boddhavyaṃ yāṃ pūjāṃ mādhavārhasi||25||

26athavā kṛpaṇairetāmupanītāṃ janārdana
|
pūjāmanarhaḥ kasmāttvamabhyanujñātavānasi||26||

27ayuktāmātmanaḥ pūjāṃ tvaṃ punarbahu manyase
|
haviṣaḥ prāpya niṣyandaṃ prāśitā śveva nirjane||27||

28na tvaṃ pārthivendrāṇāmapamānaḥ prayujyate
|
tvāmeva kuravo vyaktaṃ pralambhante janārdana||28||

29klībe dārakriyā yādṛgandhe vā rūpadarśanam
|
arājño rājavatpūjā tathā te madhusūdana||29||

30dṛṣṭo yudhiṣṭhiro rājā dṛṣṭo bhīṣmaśca yādṛśaḥ
|
vāsudevo'pyayaṃ dṛṣṭaḥ sarvametadyathātatham||30||

31ityuktvā śiśupālastānutthāya paramāsanāt
|
niryayau sadasastasmātsahito rājabhistadā||31||
