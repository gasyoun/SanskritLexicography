Mahābhārata 7.102 (Droṇaparvan)

1vāsudeva uvāca|
duryodhanamatikrāntametaṃ paśya dhanañjaya
|
atyadbhutamimaṃ manye nāstyasya sadṛśo rathaḥ ||1||

2dūrapātī maheṣvāsaḥ kṛtāstro yuddhadurmadaḥ
|
dṛḍhāstraścitrayodhī ca dhārtarāṣṭro mahābalaḥ ||2||

3atyantasukhasaṃvṛddho mānitaśca mahārathaḥ
|
kṛtī ca satataṃ pārtha nityaṃ dveṣṭi ca bāndhavān ||3||

4tena yuddhamahaṃ manye prāptakālaṃ tavānagha
|
atra vo dyūtamāyattaṃ vijayāyetarāya vā ||4||

5atra krodhaviṣaṃ pārtha vimuñca cirasaṃbhṛtam
|
eṣa mūlamanarthānāṃ pāṇḍavānāṃ mahārathaḥ ||5||

6so'yaṃ prāptastavākṣepaṃ paśya sāphalyamātmanaḥ
|
kathaṃ hi rājā rājyārthī tvayā gaccheta saṃyugam ||6||

7diṣṭyā tvidānīṃ saprāpta eṣa te bāṇagocaram
|
yathā'yaṃ jīvitaṃ jahyāt tathā kuru dhanañjaya ||7||

8aiśvaryamadasaṃmūḍho naiṣa duḥkhamupeyivān
|
na ca te saṃyuge vīryaṃ jānāti puruṣarṣabha ||8||

9tvāṃ hi lokāstrayaḥ pārtha sasurāsuramānuṣāḥ
|
notsahante raṇe jetuṃ kimutaikaḥ suyodhanaḥ ||9||

10sa diṣṭyā samanuprāptastava pārtha rathāntikam
|
jahyenaṃ tvaṃ mahābāho yathā vṛtraṃ purandaraḥ ||10||

11eṣa hyanarthe satataṃ parākrāntastavānagha
|
nikṛtyā dharmarājaṃ ca dyūte vañcitavānayam ||11||

12bahūni sunṛśaṃsāni kṛtānyetena mānada
|
yuṣmāsu pāpamatinā apāpeṣveva nityadā ||12||

13tamanāryaṃ sadā kruddhaṃ puruṣaṃ kāmarūpiṇam
|
āryāṃ yuddhe matiṃ kṛtvā jahi pārthāvicārayan ||13||

14nikṛtyā rājyaharaṇaṃ vanavāsaṃ ca pāṇḍava
|
parikleśaṃ ca kṛṣṇāyā hṛdi kṛtvā parākramam ||14||

15diṣṭyaiṣa tava bāṇānāṃ gocare parivartate
|
pratighātāya kāryasya diṣṭyā ca yatate'grataḥ ||15||

16diṣṭyā jānāti saṃgrāme yoddhavyaṃ hi tvayā saha
|
diṣṭyā ca saphalāḥ pārtha sarve kāmā hyakāmitāḥ ||16||

17tasmājjahi raṇe pārtha dhārtarāṣṭraṃ kulādhamam
|
yathendreṇa hataḥ pūrvaṃ jaṃbho devāsure mṛdhe ||17||

18asmin hate tvayā sainyamanāthaṃ bhidyatāmidam
|
vairasyāsyāstvavabhṛtho mūlaṃ chindhi durātmanām ||18||

19sañjaya uvāca|
taṃ tathetyabravīt pārthaḥ kṛtyarūpamidaṃ mama
|
sarvamanyadanādṛtya gaccha yatra suyodhanaḥ ||19||

20yenaitaddīrghakālaṃ no bhuktaṃ rājyamakaṇṭakam
|
apyasya yudhi vikramya chindyāṃ mūrdhānamāhave ||20||

21api tasya hyanarhāyāḥ parikleśasya mādhava
|
kṛṣṇāyāḥ śaknuyāṃ gantuṃ padaṃ keśapradharṣaṇe ||21||

22ityevaṃvādinau kṛṣṇau hṛṣṭau śvetānhayottamān
|
preṣayāmāsatuḥ saṃkhye prepsantau taṃ narādhipam ||22||

23tayoḥ samīpaṃ saṃprāpya putraste bharatarṣabha
|
na cakāra bhayaṃ prāpte bhaye mahati māriṣa ||23||

24tadasya kṣatriyāstatra sarva evābhyapūjayan
|
yadarjuna hṛṣīkeśau pratyudyātau nyavārayat ||24||

25tataḥ sarvasya sainyasya tāvakasya viśāṃpate
|
mahānādo hyabhūttatra dṛṣṭvā rājānamāhave ||25||

26tasmin janasamunnāde pravṛtte bhairave sati
|
kadarthīkṛtya te putraḥ pratyabhitramavārayat ||26||

27āvāritastu kaunteyastava putreṇa dhanvinā
|
saṃraṃbhamagamadbhūyaḥ sa ca tasmin parantapaḥ ||27||

28tau dṛṣṭvā pratisaṃrabdhau duryodhanadhanañjayau
|
abhyavaikṣanta rājāno bhīmarūpāḥ samantataḥ ||28||

29dṛṣṭvā tu pārthaṃ saṃrabdhaṃ vāsudevaṃ ca māriṣa
|
prahasanneva putraste yoddhukāmaḥ samāhvayat ||29||

30tataḥ prahṛṣṭo dāśārhaḥ pāṇḍavaśca dhanañjayaḥ
|
vyakrośetāṃ mahānādaṃ dadhmatuścāṃbujottamau ||30||

31tau hṛṣṭarūpau saṃprekṣya kauraveyāstu sarvaśaḥ
|
nirāśāḥ samapadyanta putrasya tava jīvite ||31||

32śokamāpuḥ pare caiva kuravaḥ sarva eva te
|
amanyanta ca putraṃ te vaiśvānaramukhe hutam ||32||

33tathā tu dṛṣṭvā yodhāste prahṛṣṭau kṛṣṇapāṇḍavau
|
hato rājā hato rājetyūcire ca bhayārditāḥ ||33||

34janasya sanninādaṃ tu śrutvā duryodhano'bravīt
|
vyetu vo bhīrahaṃ kṛṣṇau preṣayiṣyāmi mṛtyave ||34||

35ityuktvā sainikānsarvān jayāpekṣī narādhipaḥ
|
pārthamābhāṣya saṃraṃbhādidaṃ vacanamabravīt ||35||

36pārtha yacchikṣitaṃ te'straṃ divyaṃ pārthivameva ca
|
taddarśaya mayi kṣipraṃ yadi jāto'si pāṇḍunā ||36||

37yadbalaṃ tava vīryaṃ ca keśavasya tathaiva ca
|
tatkuruṣva mayi kṣipraṃ paśyāmastava pauruṣam ||37||

38asmatparokṣaṃ karmāṇi kṛtāni pravadanti te
|
svāmisatkārayuktāni yāni tānīha darśaya ||38||
