Mahābhārata 11.5 (Strīparvan)

1dhṛtarāṣṭra uvāca|yadidaṃ dharmagahanaṃ buddhyā samanugamyate
|
taddhi vistarataḥ sarvaṃ buddhimārgaṃ praśaṃsa me||1||

2vidura uvāca|atra te vartayiṣyāmi namaskṛtvā svayambhuve
|
yathā saṃsāragahanaṃ vadanti paramarṣayaḥ||2||

3kaścinmahati kāntāre vartamāno dvijaḥ kila
|
mahaddurgamanuprāpto vanaṃ kravyādasaṅkulam||3||

4siṃhavyāghragajarkṣaughairatighoraṃ mahāsvanaiḥ
|
piśitādairatibhayairmahogrākṛtibhistathā||4||

5samantātsamparikṣiptaṃ yat sma dṛṣṭvā trasedyamaḥ
|
tadasya dṛṣṭvā hṛdayamudvegamagamatparam||5||

6abhyucchrayaṃ ca romṇāṃ vai vikriyāśca parantapa
|
sa tadvanaṃ vyanusaransampradhāvannitastataḥ||6||

7vīkṣamāṇo diśaḥ sarvāḥ śaraṇaṃ kva bhavediti
|
sa teṣāṃ chidramanvicchanpradruto bhayapīḍitaḥ||7||

8na ca niryāti vai dūraṃ na vā tairvipramocyate
|
athāpaśyadvanaṃ ghoraṃ samantādvāgurāvṛtam||8||

9bāhubhyāṃ samparikṣiptaṃ striyā paramaghorayā
|
pañcaśīrṣadharairnāgaiḥ śailairiva samunnataiḥ||9||

10nabhaḥspṛśairmahāvṛkṣaiḥ parikṣiptaṃ mahāvanam
|
vanamadhye ca tatrābhūdudapānaḥ samāvṛtaḥ||10||

11vallībhistṛṇacchannābhirdṛḍhābhirabhisaṃvṛtaḥ
|
papāta sa dvijastatra nigūḍhe salilāśaye||11||

12vilagnaścābhavattasminlatāsantānasaṅkule
|
panasasya yathā jātaṃ vṛntabaddhaṃ mahāphalam||12||

13sa tathā lambate tatra hyūrdhvapādo hyadhaḥśirāḥ
|
atha tatrāpi cānyo'sya bhūyo jāta upadravaḥ||13||

14kūpamadhye mahānāgamapaśyata mahābalam
|
kūpavīnāhavelāyāmapaśyata mahāgajam||14||

15ṣaḍvaktraṃ kṛṣṇaśuklaṃ ca dviṣaṭkapadacāriṇam
|
krameṇa parisarpantaṃ vallīvṛkṣasamāvṛtam||15||

16tasya cāpi praśākhāsu vṛkṣaśākhāvalambinaḥ
|
nānārūpā madhukarā ghorarūpā bhayāvahāḥ||16||

17āsate madhu saṃvṛtya pūrvameva niketajāḥ
|
bhūyo bhūyaḥ samīhante madhūni bharatarṣabha||17||

18svādanīyāni bhūtānāṃ yairbālo viprakṛṣyate
|
teṣāṃ madhūnāṃ bahudhā dhārā prasravate tadā||18||

19ālambamānaḥ sa pumāndhārāṃ pibati sarvadā
|
na cāsya tṛṣṇā viratā pibamānasya saṅkaṭe||19||

20abhīpsati tadā nityamatṛptaḥ sa punaḥ punaḥ
|
na cāsya jīvite rājannirvedaḥ samajāyata||20||

21tatraiva ca manuṣyasya jīvitāśā pratiṣṭhitā
|
kṛṣṇāḥ śvetāśca taṃ vṛkṣaṃ nikṛntanti sma mūṣikāḥ||21||

22vyālaiśca vanadurgānte striyā ca paramograyā
|
kūpādhastācca nāgena vīnāhe kuñjareṇa ca||22||

23vṛkṣaprapātācca bhayaṃ mūṣikebhyaśca pañcamam
|
madhulobhānmadhukaraiḥ ṣaṣṭhamāhurmahadbhayam||23||

24evaṃ sa vasate tatra kṣiptaḥ saṃsārasāgare
|
na caiva jīvitāśāyāṃ nirvedamupagacchati||24||
