Mahābhārata 15.34 (Āśramavāsikaparvan)

1sautiruvāca|
etacchutvā nṛpo vidvān hṛṣṭo'bhūjjanamejayaḥ
|
pitāmahānāṃ sarveṣāṃ gamanāgamanaṃ tadā||1||

2abravīcca mudā yuktaḥ punarāgamanaṃ prati
|
kathaṃ nu tyaktadehānāṃ punastadrūpadarśanam||2||

3ityuktaḥ sa dvijaśreṣṭho vyāsaśiṣyaḥ pratāpavān
|
provāca vadatāṃ śreṣṭhastaṃ nṛpaṃ janamejayam||3||

4vaiśampāyana uvāca|
avipraṇāśaḥ sarveṣāṃ karmaṇāmiti niścayaḥ
|
karmajāni śarīrāṇi tathaivākṛtayo nṛpa||4||

5mahābhūtāni nityāni bhūtādhipatisaṃśrayāt
|
teṣāṃ ca nityasaṃvāso na vināśo viyujyatām||5||

6anāyāsakṛtaṃ karma satyaḥ śreṣṭhaḥ phalāgamaḥ
|
ātmā caibhiḥ samāyuktaḥ sukhaduḥkhamupāśnute||6||

7avināśyastathāyuktaḥ śretrajña iti niścayaḥ
|
bhūtānāmātmako bhāvo yathāsau na viyujyate||7||

8yāvanna kṣīyate karma tāvattasya svarūpatā
|
kṣīṇakarmā naro loke rūpānyatvaṃ niyacchati||8||

9nānābhāvāstathaikatvaṃ śarīraṃ prāpya saṃhatāḥ
|
bhavanti te tathā nityāḥ pṛthagbhāvaṃ vijānatām||9||

10aśvamedhe śrutiśceyamaśvasaṃjñapanaṃ prati
|
lokāntaragatā nityaṃ prāṇā nityaṃ śarīriṇām||10||

11ahaṃ hitaṃ vadāmyetatpriyaṃ cettava pārthiva
|
devayānā hi panthānaḥ śrutāste yajñasaṃstare||11||

12āhṛto yatra yajñaste tatra devā hitāstava
|
yadā samanvitā devāḥ paśūnāṃ gamaneśvarāḥ||12||

13gatimantaśca teneṣṭvā nānye nityā bhavantyuta
|
nitye'sminpañcake varge nitye cātmani pūruṣaḥ||13||

14asya nānāsamāyogaṃ yaḥ paśyati vṛthāmatiḥ
|
viyoge śocate'tyarthaṃ sa bāla iti me matiḥ||14||

15viyoge doṣadarśī yaḥ saṃyogaṃ sa visarjayet
|
asaṅge saṅgamo nāsti duḥkhaṃ bhuvi viyogajam||15||

16parāparajñastvaparo nābhimānādudīritaḥ
|
aparajñaḥ parāṃ buddhiṃ jñātvā mohādvimucyate||16||

17adarśanādāpatitaḥ punaścādarśanaṃ gataḥ
|
nāhaṃ taṃ vedmi nāsau māṃ na ca me'sti virāgatā||17||

18yena yena śarīreṇa karotyayamanīśvaraḥ
|
tena tena śarīreṇa tadavaśyamupāśnute
|
mānasaṃ manasā''pnoti śarīraṃ ca śarīravān||18||
