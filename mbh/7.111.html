Mahābhārata 7.111 (Droṇaparvan)

1sañjaya uvāca|
prītiyuktaṃ ca hṛdyaṃ ca madhurākṣarameva ca
|
kālayuktaṃ ca citraṃ ca nyāyyaṃ yaccāpi bhāṣitum||1||

2dharmarājasya tadvākyaṃ niśamya śinipuṅgavaḥ
|
sātyakirbharataśreṣṭha pratyuvāca yudhiṣṭhiram||2||

3śrutaṃ te gadato vākyaṃ sarvametanmayā'cyuta
|
nyāyayuktaṃ ca citraṃ ca phālgunārthe yaśaskaram||3||

4evaṃvidhe tathā kāle mādṛśaṃ prekṣya saṃmatam
|
vaktumarhasi rājendra yathā pārthaṃ tathaiva mām||4||

5na me dhanañjayasyārthe prāṇā rakṣyāḥ kathañcana
|
tvatprayuktaḥ punarahaṃ kiṃ na kuryāṃ mahāhave ||5||

6lokatraye yodhayeyaṃ sadevāsuramānuṣam
|
tvatprayukto narendreha kimutaitatsudurbalam ||6||

7suyodhanabalaṃ tvadya yodhayiṣye samantataḥ
|
vijeṣye ca raṇe rājan satyametadbravīmi te||7||

8kuśalyahaṃ kuśalinaṃ samāsādya dhanañjayam
|
hate jayadrathe rājan punareṣyāmi te'ntikam‌||8||

9avaśyaṃ tu mayā sarvaṃ vijñāpyastvaṃ narādhipa
|
vāsudevasya yadvākyaṃ phālgunasya ca dhīmataḥ ||9||

10dṛḍhaṃ tvabhiparīto'hamarjunena punaḥ punaḥ
|
madhye sarvasya sainyasya vāsudevasya śṛṇvataḥ ||10||

11adya mādhava rājānamapramatto'nupālaya
|
āryāṃ yuddhe matiṃ kṛtvā yāvaddhanmi jayadratham||11||

12tvayi cāhaṃ mahābāho pradyumne vā mahārathe
|
nṛpaṃ nikṣipya gaccheyaṃ nirapekṣo jayadratham ||12||

13jānīṣe hi raṇe droṇaṃ kuruṣu śreṣṭhasammatam
|
pratijñātaṃ hi tenedaṃ paśyamānena vai prabho ||13||

14grahaṇe dharmarājasya bhāradvājo'pi gṛdhyati
|
śaktaścāpi raṇe droṇo nigrahītuṃ yudhiṣṭhiram ||14||

15evaṃ tvayi samādhāya dharmarājaṃ narottamam
|
ahamadya gamiṣyāmi saindhavasya vadhāya hi||15||

16jayadrathaṃ ca hatvā'haṃ drutameṣyāmi mādhava
|
dharmarājaṃ na ceddroṇo nigṛhṇīyādraṇe balāt ||16||

17nigṛhīte naraśreṣṭhe bhāradvājena mādhava
|
saindhavasya vadho na syānmamāprītistathā bhavet||17||

18evaṃgate naraśreṣṭhe pāṇḍave satyavādini
|
asmākaṃ gamanaṃ vyaktaṃ vanaṃ prati bhavet punaḥ ||18||

19so'yaṃ mama jayo vyaktaṃ vyartha eva bhaviṣyati
|
yadi droṇo raṇe kruddho nigṛhṇīyādyudhiṣṭhiram ||19||

20sa tvamadya mahābāho priyārthaṃ mama mādhava
|
jayārthaṃ ca yaśo'rthaṃ ca rakṣa rājānamāhave||20||

21sa bhavānmayi nikṣepo nikṣiptaḥ savyasācinā
|
bhāradvājādbhayaṃ nityaṃ manyamānena vai prabho ||21||

22tasyāpi ca mahābāho nityaṃ paśyāmi saṃyuge
|
nānyaṃ hi pratiyoddhāraṃ raukmiṇeyādṛte prabho||22||

23māṃ cāpi manyate yuddhe bhāradvājasya dhīmataḥ
|
so'haṃ saṃbhāvanāṃ caitāmācāryavacanaṃ ca tat||23||

24pṛṣṭhato notsahe kartuṃ tvāṃ vā tyaktuṃ mahīpate
|
ācāryo laghuhastatvādabhedyakavacāvṛtaḥ ||24||

25upalabhya raṇe krīḍedyathā śakuninā śiśuḥ
|
yadi kārṣṇirdhanuṣpāṇiriha syānmakaradhvajaḥ||25||

26tasmai tvāṃ visṛjeyaṃ vai sa tvāṃ rakṣedyathārjunaḥ
|
kuru tvamātmano guptiṃ kaste goptā gate mayi||26||

27yaḥ pratīyādraṇe droṇaṃ yāvadgacchāmi pāṇḍavam
|
mā ca te bhayamadyāstu rājannarjunasaṃbhavam||27||

28na sa jātu mahābāhurbhāramudyamya sīdati
|
ye ca sauvīrakā yodhāstathā saindhavapauravāḥ||28||

29udīcyā dākṣiṇātyāśca ye cānye'pi mahārathāḥ
|
ye ca karṇamukhā rājan rathodārāḥ prakīrtitāḥ ||29||

30ete'rjunasya kruddhasya kalāṃ nārhanti ṣoḍaśīm
|
udyuktā pṛthivī sarvā sasurāsuramānuṣā ||30||

31sarākṣasagaṇā rājan sakinnaramahoragā
|
jaṃgamāḥ sthāvarāḥ sarve nālaṃ pārthasya saṃyuge ||31||

32evaṃ jñātvā mahārāja vyetu te bhīrdhanañjaye
|
yatra vīrau maheṣvāsau kṛṣṇau satyaparākramau||32||

33na tatra karmaṇo vyāpat kathañcidāpe vidyate
|
daivaṃ kṛtāstratāṃ yogamamarṣamapi cāhave ||33||

34kṛtajñatāṃ dayāṃ caiva bhrātustvamanucintaya
|
mayi cāpyapayāte vai gaccha māne'rjunaṃ prati ||34||

35droṇe citrāstratāṃ saṃkhye rājaṃstvamanucintaya
|
ācāryo hi bhṛśaṃ rājannigrahe tava gṛdhyati ||35||

36pratijñāmātmano rakṣan satyāṃ kartuṃ ca bhārata
|
kuruṣvādyātmano guptiṃ kaste goptā gate mayi||36||

37yasyāhaṃ pratyayāt pārtha gaccheyaṃ phālgunaṃ prati
|
na hyahaṃ tvāṃ mahārāja anikṣipya mahāhave ||37||

38kaccidyāsyāmi kauravya satyametadbravīmi te
|
etadvicārtha bahuśo buddhyā buddhimatāṃ vara||38||

39dṛṣṭvā śreyaḥ paraṃ buddhyā tato rājanpraśādhi mām||39||

40yudhiṣṭhira uvāca|
evametanmahābāho yathā vadasi mādhava
|
na tu me śuddhyate bhāvaḥ śvetāśvaṃ prati māriṣa||40||

41kariṣye paramaṃ yatnamātmano rakṣaṇe hyaham
|
gaccha tvaṃ samanujñāto yatra yāto dhanañjayaḥ||41||

42ātmasaṃrakṣaṇaṃ saṃkhthe gamanaṃ cārjunaṃ prati
|
vicāryaitatsvayaṃ buddhyā gamanaṃ tatra rocaya ||42||

43sa tvamātiṣṭha yānāya yatra yāto dhanañjayaḥ
|
mamāpi rakṣaṇaṃ bhīmaḥ kariṣyati mahābalaḥ ||43||

44pārṣataśca sasodaryaḥ pārthivāśca mahābalāḥ
|
draupadeyāśca māṃ tāta rakṣiṣyanti na saṃśayaḥ||44||

45kekayā bhrātaraḥ paṃca rākṣasaśca ghaṭotkacaḥ
|
virāṭo drupadaścaiva śikhaṇḍī ca mahārathaḥ ||45||

46dhṛṣṭraketuśca balavān kuntibhojaśca mātulaḥ
|
nakulaḥ sahadevaśca pañcālāḥ sṛñjayāstathā||46||

47ete samāhitāstāta rakṣiṣyanti na saṃśayaḥ
|
na droṇaḥ saha sainyena kṛtavarmā ca saṃyuge ||47||

48samāsādayituṃ śakto na ca māṃ dharṣayiṣyati
|
dhṛṣṭadyumnaśca samare droṇaṃ kruddhaṃ parantapaḥ ||48||

49vārayiṣyati vikramya veleva makarālayam
|
yatra sthāsyati saṃgrāme pārvataḥ paravīrahā ||49||

50droṇo na sainyaṃ balavatkāmet tatra kathañcana
|
eṣa droṇavināśāya samutpanno hutāśanāt ||50||

51kavacī sa śarī khaḍgī dhanvī ca varabhūṣaṇaḥ
|
viśrabdhaṃ gaccha śaineya mā kārṣīrmayi saṃbhramam
|
dhṛṣṭadyumno raṇe kuddhaṃ droṇamāvāraviṣyati ||51||
