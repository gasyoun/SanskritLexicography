Mahābhārata 12.168 (Śāntiparvan)

1yudhiṣṭhira uvāca|
pitāmaha mahāprājña kurūṇāṃ prītivardhana
|
praśnaṃ kañcitpravakṣyāmi tanme vyākhyātumarhasi ||1||

2kīdṛśā mānavāḥ saumyāḥ kaiḥ prītiḥ paramā bhavet
|
āyatyāṃ ca tadātve ca ke kṣamāstānvadasva me ||2||

3na hi tatra dhanaṃ sphītaṃ na ca sambandhibāndhavāḥ
|
tiṣṭhanti yatra suhṛdastiṣṭhantīti matirmama ||3||

4durlabho hi suhṛcchrotā durlabhaśca hitaḥ suhṛt
|
etaddharmabhṛtāṃ śreṣṭha sarvaṃ vyākhyātumarhasi ||4||

5bhīṣma uvāca|
sandheyān puruṣān rājannasandheyāṃśca tattvataḥ
|
vadato me nibodha tvaṃ nikhilena yudhiṣṭhira ||5||

6lubdhaḥ krūrastyaktadharmā nikṛtiḥ śaṭha eva ca
|
kṣudraḥ pāpasamācāraḥ sarvaśaṅkī tathā'lasaḥ ||6||

7dīrghasūtro'nṛjuḥ kruṣṭo gurudārapradharṣakaḥ
|
vyasane yaḥ parityāgī durātmā nirapatrapaḥ ||7||

8sarvataḥ pāpadarśī ca nāstiko vedanindakaḥ
|
samprakīrṇendriyo loke yaḥ kāmaṃ nirataścaret ||8||

9asatyo lokavidviṣṭaḥ samaye cānavasthitaḥ
|
piśuno'thākṛtaprajño matsarī pāpaniścayaḥ ||9||

10duḥśīlo'thākṛtātmā ca nṛśaṃsaḥ kitavastathā
|
mitrairapakṛtirnityamicchate'rthaṃ parasya yaḥ ||10||

11dadataśca yathāśakti yo na tuṣyati mandadhīḥ
|
adhairyamapi yo yuṅkte sadā mitraṃ nararṣabha ||11||

12asthānakrodhano'yukto yaścākasmādvirudhyate
|
suhṛdaścaiva kalyāṇānāśu tyajati kilbiṣī ||12||

13alpe'pyapakṛte mūḍhastathājñānātkṛte'pi ca
|
kāryasevī ca mitreṣu mitradveṣī narādhipa ||13||

14śatrurmitramukho yaśca jihmaprekṣī vilocanaḥ
|
na virajyati kalyāṇe yaḥ kuryāttādṛśaṃ naram ||14||

15pānapo dveṣaṇaḥ krodhī nirghṛṇaḥ paruṣastathā
|
paropatāpī mitradhruk tathā prāṇivadhe rataḥ ||15||

16kṛtaghnaścādhamo loke na sandheyaḥ kadācana
|
chidrānveṣī hyasandheyaḥ sandheyānapi me śṛṇu ||16||

17kulīnā vākyasampannā jñānavijñānakovidāḥ
|
rūpavanto guṇopetāstathā'lubdhā jitaśramāḥ ||17||

18sanmitrāśca kṛtajñāśca sarvajñā lobhavarjitāḥ
|
mādhuryaguṇasampannāḥ satyasandhā jitendriyāḥ ||18||

19vyāyāmaśīlāḥ satataṃ kulaputrāḥ kulodvahāḥ
|
doṣaiḥ pramuktāḥ prathitāste grāhyāḥ pārthivairnarāḥ ||19||

20yathāśakti samācārāḥ sampratuṣyanti hi prabho
|
nāsthāne krodhavantaśca na cākasmādvirāgiṇaḥ
|
viraktaśca na duṣyanti manasā'pyarthakovidāḥ ||20||

21ātmānaṃ pīḍayitvā'pi suhṛtkāryaparāyaṇāḥ
|
virajyanti na mitrebhyo vāso raktamivāvikam ||21||

22krodhācca lobhamohābhyāṃ nānarthe yuvatīṣu ca
|
na darśayanti suhṛdo viśvastā dharmavatsalāḥ ||22||

23loṣṭhakāñcanatulyārthāḥ suhṛtsu dṛḍhabuddhayaḥ
|
ye carantyabhimānāni sṛṣṭārthamanuṣaṅgiṇaḥ ||23||

24saṃgṛhṇantaḥ parijanaṃ svāmyarthaparamāḥ sadā
|
īdṛśaiḥ puruṣaśreṣṭhairyaḥ sandhiṃ kurute nṛpaḥ ||24||

25tasya vistīryate rājyaṃ jyotsnāgrahapateriva
|
śāstranityā jitakrodhā balavanto raṇe sadā ||25||

26janmaśīlaguṇopetāḥ sandheyāḥ puruṣottamāḥ
|
ye ca doṣasamāyuktā narāḥ proktā mayā'nagha ||26||

27teṣāmapyadhamā rājan kṛtaghnā mitraghātakāḥ
|
tyaktavyāstu durācārāḥ sarveṣāmiti niścayaḥ ||27||

28yudhiṣṭhira uvāca|
vistareṇātha sambandhaṃ śrotumicchāmi tattvataḥ
|
mitradrohī kṛtaghnaśca yaḥ proktastadvadasva me ||28||

29bhīṣma uvāca|
hanta te vartayiṣye'hamitihāsaṃ purātanam
|
udīcyāṃ diśi yadvṛttaṃ mleccheṣu manujādhipa ||29||

30brāhmaṇo madhyadeśīyaḥ kaścidvai brahmavarjitaḥ
|
grāmaṃ vṛddhiyutaṃ vīkṣya prāviśadbhaikṣyakāsayā ||30||

31tatra dasyurdhanayutaḥ sarvavarṇaviśeṣavit
|
brahmaṇyaḥ satyasandhaśca dāne ca nirato'bhavat ||31||

32tasya kṣayamupāgamya tato bhikṣāmayācata
|
pratiśrayaṃ ca vāsārthaṃ bhikṣāṃ caivātha vārṣikīm ||32||

33prādāttasmai sa viprāya vastraṃ ca sadṛśaṃ navam
|
nārīṃ cāpi vayopetāṃ bhartrā virahitāṃ tathā ||33||

34etatsamprāpya hṛṣṭātmā dasyoḥ sarvaṃ dvijastathā
|
tasmin gṛhavare rājaṃstayā reme sa gautamaḥ ||34||

35kuṭumbārthaṃ ca dāsyāśca sāhāyyaṃ cāpyathākarot
|
tatrāvasatsa varṣāṃśca samṛddhe śabarālaye ||35||

36bāṇavedhe paraṃ yatnamakaroccaiva gautamaḥ
|
cakrāṅgānsa ca nityaṃ vai sarvato vanagocarān ||36||

37jaghāna gautamo rājan yathā dasyugaṇāstathā
|
hiṃsāpaṭurghṛṇāhīnaḥ sadā prāṇivadhe rataḥ ||37||

38gautamaḥ sannikarṣeṇa dasyubhiḥ samatāmiyāt
|
tathā tu vasatastasya dasyugrāme sukhaṃ tadā ||38||

39agamanbahavo māsā nighnataḥ pakṣiṇo bahūn
|
tataḥ kadācidaparo dvijastaṃ deśamāgataḥ ||39||

40jaṭācīrājinadharaḥ svādhyāyaparamaḥ śuciḥ
|
vinīto niyatāhāro brahmaṇyo vedapāragaḥ ||40||

41sa brahmacārī taddeśyaḥ sakhā tasyaiva supriyaḥ
|
taṃ dasyugrāmamagamadyatrāsau gautamo'vasat ||41||

42sa tu vipragṛhānveṣī śūdrānnaparivarjakaḥ
|
grāme dasyusamākīrṇe vyacaratsarvatodiśam ||42||

43tataḥ sa gautamagṛhaṃ praviveśa dvijottamaḥ
|
gautamaścāpi samprāptastāvanyonyena saṃgatau ||43||

44cakrāṅgabhāraskandhaṃ taṃ dhanuṣpāṇiṃ dhṛtāyudham
|
rudhireṇāvasiktāṅgaṃ gṛhadvāramupāgatam ||44||

45taṃ dṛṣṭvā puruṣādābhamapadhvastaṃ kṣayāgatam
|
abhijñāya dvijo vrīḍatridaṃ vākyamathābravīt ||45||

46kimidaṃ kuruṣe mohādviprastvaṃ hi kulodvahaḥ
|
madhyadeśaparijñāto dasyubhāvaṃ gataḥ katham ||46||

47pūrvān smara dvija jñātīn prakhyātānvedapāragān
|
teṣāṃ vaṃśe'bhijātastvamīdṛśaḥ kulapāṃsanaḥ ||47||

48avabudhyātmanā''tmānaṃ sa tvaṃ śīlaṃ śrutaṃ damam
|
anukrośaṃ ca saṃsmṛtya tyaja vāsamimaṃ dvija ||48||

49sa evamuktaḥ suhṛdā tena tatra hitaiṣiṇā
|
pratyuvāca tato rājan viniścitya tadārtavat ||49||

50nirdhano'smi dvijaśreṣṭha nāpi vedavidapyaham
|
vittārthamiha samprāptaṃ viddhi māṃ dvijasattama ||50||

51tvaddarśanāttu viprendra kṛtārtho'smyadya vai dvija
|
āvāṃ hi saha yāsyāvaḥ śvo vasasvādya śarvarīm ||51||

52sa tatra nyavasadvipro ghṛṇī kiñcidasaṃspṛśan
|
kṣudhitaśchandyamāno'pi bhojanaṃ nābhyanandata ||52||
