Mahābhārata 7.132 (Droṇaparvan)

1dhṛtarāṣṭra uvāca|
svayaṃ śiṣyo maheśasya bhṛgūttamadhanurdharaḥ
|
śiṣyatvaṃ prāptavān karṇastasya tulyo'stravidyayā ||1||

2tadviśiṣṭo'pi vā karṇaḥ śiṣyaḥ śiṣyaguṇairyutaḥ
|
kuntīputreṇa bhīmena nirjitaḥ sa tu līlayā||2||

3yasmiñjayāśā mahartā putrāṇāṃ mama sañjaya
|
taṃ bhīmādvimukhaṃ dṛṣṭvā kinnu duryodhano'bravīt||3||

4kathaṃ ca yuyudhe bhīmo vīryaślāghī mahābalaḥ
|
karṇo vā samare tāta kimakārṣīttataḥ param
|
bhīmasenaṃ raṇe dṛṣṭvā jvalantamiva pāvakam||4||

5sañjaya uvāca|
rathamanyaṃ samāsthāya vidhivatkalpitaṃ punaḥ
|
abhyayātpāṇḍavaṃ karṇo vātoddhūta ivārṇavaḥ||5||

6kruddhamādhirathiṃ dṛṣṭvā putrāstava viśāṃpate
|
bhīmasenamamanyanta vaiśvānaramukhe hutam ||6||

7cāpaśabdaṃ tataḥ kṛtvā talaśabdaṃ ca bhairavam
|
abhyadravata rādheyo bhīmasenarathaṃ prati ||7||

8punareva tayo rājan ghora āsīta samāgamaḥ
|
vaikartanasya śūrasya bhīmasya ca mahātmanaḥ||8||

9saṃrabdhau hi mahābāhū parasparavadhaiṣiṇau
|
anyonyamīkṣāṃ cakrāte dahantāviva locanaiḥ||9||

10krodharaktekṣaṇau tīvrau niḥśvasantāvivoragau
|
śūrāvanyonyamāsādya tatakṣaturarindamau||10||

11vyāghrāviva susaṃrabdhau śyenāviva ca śīghragau
|
śarabhāviva saṃkruddhau yuyudhāte parasparam ||11||

12tato bhīmaḥ smarankleśānakṣadyūte vane'pi ca
|
virāṭanagare caiva duḥkhaṃ prāptamarindamaḥ||12||

13rāṣṭrāṇāṃ sphītaratnānāṃ haraṇaṃ ca tavātmajaiḥ
|
satataṃ ca parikleśānsaputreṇa tvayā kṛtān ||13||

14dagdhumaicchacca yaḥ kuntīmaputrāṃ tvamanāgasam
|
kṛṣṇāyāśca parikleśaṃ sabhāmadhye durātmabhiḥ||14||

15keśapakṣagrahaṃ caiva duḥśāsanakṛtaṃ tathā
|
paruṣāṇi ca vākyāni karṇenoktāni bhārata||15||

16patimanyaṃ parīpsasva na santi patayastava
|
patitā narake pārthāḥ sarve ṣaṇḍhatilopamāḥ||16||

17samakṣaṃ tava kauravya yadūcuḥ kauravāstadā
|
dāsībhāvena kṛṣṇāṃ ca bhoktukāmāḥ sutāstava||17||

18yaccāpi tān pravrajataḥ kṛṣṇājinanivāsinaḥ
|
paruṣāṇyuktavān karṇaḥ sabhāyāṃ sannidhau tava||18||

19tṛṇīkṛtya yathā pārthāṃstava putro vavalga ha
|
viṣamasthānsamastho hi saṃrabdho gatacetanaḥ||19||

20bālyātprabhṛti cārighnaḥ svāni duḥkhāni cintayan
|
niravidyata dharmātmā jīvitena vṛkodaraḥ||20||

21tato visphārya sumahaddhemapṛṣṭhaṃ durāsadam
|
cāpaṃ bharataśārdūlastyaktātmā karṇamabhyayāt ||21||

22sa sāyakamayairjālaurbhīmaḥ karṇarathaṃ prati
|
bhānumadbhiḥ śilādhautairbhānoḥ prācchādayatprabhām||22||

23tataḥ prahasyādhirathistūrṇamasya śilāśitaiḥ
|
vyadhamadbhīmasenasya śarajālāni patribhiḥ||23||

24mahāratho mahābāhurmahābāṇairmahābalaḥ
|
vivyādhādhirathirbhīmaṃ navabhirniśitaistadā ||24||

25sa totrairiva mātaṅgo vāryamāṇaḥ patatribhiḥ
|
abhyadhāvadasaṃbhrāntaḥ sūtaputraṃ vṛkodaraḥ ||25||

26tamāpatantaṃ vegena rabhasaṃ pāṇḍavarṣabham
|
karṇaḥ pratyudyayau yuddhe matto mattamiva dvipam||26||

27tataḥ pradhmāpya jalajaṃ bherīśatasamasvanam
|
akṣubhyata balaṃ harṣāduddhūta iva sāgaraḥ ||27||

28tadudbhūtaṃ balaṃ dṛṣṭvā nāgāśvarathapattimat
|
bhīmaḥ karṇaṃ samāsādya cchādayāmāsa sāyakaiḥ||28||

29aśvānṛkṣasavarṇāṃśca haṃsavarṇairhayottamaiḥ
|
vyāmiśrayadraṇe karṇaḥ pāṇḍavaṃ chādayañcharaiḥ||29||

30ṛkṣavarṇānhayānkarkairmiśrānmārutaraṃhasaḥ
|
nirīkṣya tava putrāṇāṃ hāhākṛtamabhūdbalam||30||

31te hayā bahvaśobhanta miśritā vātaraṃhasaḥ
|
sitāsitā mahārāja yathā vyomni balāhakāḥ ||31||

32saṃrabdhau krodhatāmrākṣau prekṣya karṇavṛkodarau
|
saṃtrastāḥ samakampanta tvadīyānāṃ mahārathāḥ||32||

33yamarāṣṭropamaṃ ghoramāsīdāyodhanaṃ tayoḥ
|
durdarśaṃ bharataśreṣṭha pretarājapuraṃ yathā||33||

34samājamiva taccitraṃ prekṣamāṇā mahārathāḥ
|
nālakṣayañjayaṃ vyaktamekasyaiva mahāraṇe ||34||

35tayoḥ praikṣanta saṃmardaṃ sannikṛṣṭaṃ mahāstrayoḥ
|
tava durmantrite rājan saputrasya viśāṃpate ||35||

36chādayantau hi śatrughnāvanyonyaṃ sāyakaiḥ śitaiḥ
|
śarajālāvṛtaṃ vyoma cakrāte'dbhutavikramau ||36||

37tāvanyonyaṃ jighāṃsantau śaraistīkṣṇairmahārathau
|
prekṣaṇīyatarāvāstāṃ vṛṣṭimantāvivāmbudau||37||

38suvarṇavikṛtānbāṇānvimuṃcantāvarinda‌mau
|
bhākharaṃ vyoma cakrāte maholkābhiriva prabho||38||

39tābhyāṃ muktāḥ śarā rājan ‌gārdhrapatrāścakāśire
|
śreṇyaḥ śaradi mattānāṃ sārasānāmivāmbare||39||

40saṃsaktaṃ sūtaputreṇa dṛṣṭvā bhīmamarindamam
|
atibhāramamanyetāṃ bhīme kṛṣṇacanañjayau ||40||

41tatrādhirathibhīmābhyāṃ śarairmuktairdṛḍhaṃ hatāḥ
|
iṣupātamatikramya peturaśva-nara-dvipāḥ ||41||

42patadbhiḥ patitaiścānyairgatāsubhiranekaśaḥ
|
kṛto rājanmahārāja putrāṇāṃ te janakṣayaḥ||42||

43manuṣyāśva-gajānāṃ ca śarīrairgatajīvitaiḥ
|
kṣaṇena bhūmiḥ saṃjajñe saṃvṛtā bharatarṣabha ||43||
