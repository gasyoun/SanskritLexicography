Mahābhārata 7.138 (Droṇaparvan)

1dhṛtarāṣṭra uvāca|
mahānapanayaḥ sūta mamaivātra viśeṣataḥ
|
sa idānīmanuprāpto manye sañjaya śocataḥ ||1||

2yadgataṃ tadgatamiti mamāsīnmanasi sthitam
|
idānīmatra kiṃ kāryaṃ prakariṣyāmi sañjaya ||2||

3yathā hyeṣa kṣayo vṛtto mamāpanayasaṃbhavaḥ
|
vīrāṇāṃ tanmamācakṣva sthirībhūto'smi sañjaya ||3||

4sañjaya uvāca|
karṇabhīmau mahārāja parākrāntau mahābalau
|
bāṇavarṣāṇyasṛjatāṃ vṛṣṭimantāvivāmbudau ||4||

5bhīmanāmāṃkitābāṇāḥ svarṇapuṃkhāḥ śilāśitāḥ
|
viviśuḥ karṇamāsādya cchindanta iva jīvitam ||5||

6tathaiva karṇanirmuktāḥ śarā barhiṇavāsasaḥ
|
chādayāṃcakrire vīraṃ śataśo'tha sahasraśaḥ ||6||

7tayoḥ śarairmahārāja saṃpatadbhiḥ samantataḥ
|
babhūva tatra sainyānāṃ saṃkṣobhaḥ sāgarottaraḥ ||7||

8bhīmacāpacyutairbāṇaistava sainyamarindama
|
avadhyata camūmadhye ghorairāśīviṣopamaiḥ ||8||

9vāraṇaiḥ patitai rājanvājibhiśca naraiḥ saha
|
adṛśyata mahī kīrṇā vātabhagnairiva drumaiḥ ||9||

10te vadhyamānāḥ samare bhīmacāpacyutaiḥ śaraiḥ
|
prādravaṃstāvakā yodhāḥ kimetaditi cābruvan ||10||

11tato vyudastaṃ tatsainyaṃ sindhusauvīrakauravam
|
protsāritaṃ mahāvegaiḥ karṇapāṇḍavayoḥ śaraiḥ ||11||

12te śūrā hatabhūyiṣṭhā hatāśva-ratha-vāraṇāḥ
|
utsṛjya bhīmakarṇau ca sarvato vyadravandiśaḥ ||12||

13nūnaṃ pārthārthamevāsmānmohayanti divaukasaḥ
|
yatkarṇabhīmaprabhavairvadhyate no balaṃ śaraiḥ ||13||

14evaṃ bruvāṇā yodhāste tāvakā bhayapīḍitāḥ
|
śarapātaṃ samutsṛjya sthitā yuddhadidṛkṣavaḥ ||14||

15tataḥ prāvartata nadī ghorarūpā raṇājire
|
śūrāṇāṃ harṣajananī bhīrūṇāṃ bhayavardhinī ||15||

16vāraṇāśvamanuṣyāṇāṃ rudhiraughasamudbhavā
|
saṃvṛttā gatasattvaiśca manuṣyagajavājibhiḥ ||16||

17sānukarṣapatākaiśca dvipāśva-ratha-bhūṣaṇaiḥ
|
syandanairapaviddhaiśca bhagnacakākṣakūbaraiḥ ||17||

18jātarūpapariṣkārairdhanurbhiḥ sumahāsvanaiḥ
|
suvarṇapuṅkhairiṣubhirnārācaiśca sahasraśaḥ ||18||

19karṇapāṇḍavanirmuktairnirmuktairiva pannagaiḥ
|
prāsa-tomarasaṃghātaiḥ khaḍgaiśca saparaśvadhaiḥ||19||

20suvarṇavikṛtaiścāpi gadā- musala-paṭṭiśaiḥ
|
dhvajaiśca vividhākāraiḥ śaktibhiḥ parighairapi ||20||

21śataghnībhiśca citrābhirbabhau bhārata medinī
|
kanakāṅgadahāraiśca kuṇḍalairmukuṭaistathā ||21||

22valayairapaviddhaiśca tatraivāṅguliveṣṭakaiḥ
|
cūḍāmaṇibhiruṣṇīṣaiḥ svarṇasūtraiśca māriṣa ||22||

23tanutraiḥ satalatraiśca hārairniṣkaiśca bhārata
|
vastraiśchatraiśca vidhvastaiścāmaravyajanairapi ||23||

24gajāśvamanujairbhinnaiḥ śoṇitāktaiśca patribhiḥ
|
taistaiśca vividhairbhinnaistatra tatra vasuṃdharā ||24||

25patitairapaviddhaiśca vibabhau dyoriva grahaiḥ
|
acintyamadbhutaṃ caiva tayoḥ karmātimānuṣam ||25||

26dṛṣṭvā cāraṇasiddhānāṃ vismayaḥ samajāyata
|
agnervāyusahāyasya gatiḥ kakṣa ivāhave ||26||

27āsīdbhīmasahāyasya raudramādhirathergatam
|
nipātitadhvajarathaṃ hatavāji- nara- dvipam ||27||

28gajābhyāṃ saṃprayuktābhyāmāsīnnalavanaṃ yathā
|
meghajālanibhaṃ sainyamāsīttava narādhipa
|
vimardaḥ karṇabhīmābhyāmāsīcca paramo raṇe ||28||
