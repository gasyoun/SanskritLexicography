Mahābhārata 9.64 (Śalyaparvan)

1dhṛtarāṣṭra uvāca|
adhiṣṭhitaḥ padā mūrdhni bhagnasaktho mahīṃ gataḥ
|
śauṭīryamānī putro me kimabhāṣata sañjaya||1||

2atyarthaṃ kopano rājā jātavairaśca pāṇḍuṣu
|
vyasanaṃ paramaṃ prāptaḥ kimāha paramāhave||2||

3sañjaya uvāca|
śṛṇu rājanpravakṣyāmi yathāvṛttaṃ narādhipa
|
rājñā yaduktaṃ bhagnena tasminvyasana āgate||3||

4bhagnasaktho nṛpo rājan pāṃsunā so'vaguṇṭhitaḥ
|
yamayanmūrdhajāstatra vīkṣya caiva diśo daśa||4||

5keśānniyamya yatnena niḥśvasannurago yathā
|
saṃrambhāśruparītābhyāṃ netrābhyāmabhivīkṣya mām||5||

6bāhū dharaṇyāṃ niṣyiṣya sudurmatta iva dvipaḥ
|
prakīrṇānmūrdhajāndhunvan dantairdantānupaspṛśan||6||

7garhayanpāṇḍavaṃ jyeṣṭhaḥ niḥśvasyedamathābravīt
|
bhīṣme śāntanave nāthe karṇe śastrabhṛtāṃ vare||7||

8gautame śakunau cāpi droṇe cāstrabhṛtāṃ vare
|
aśvatthāmni tathā śalye śūre ca kṛtavarmaṇi||8||

9imāmavasthāṃ prāpto'smi kālo hi duratikramaḥ
|
ekādaśacamūbhartā so'hametāṃ daśāṃ gataḥ||9||

10kālaṃ prāpya mahābāho na kaścidativartate
|
ākhyātavyaṃ madīyānāṃ ye'smiñjīvanti saṃyuge||10||

11yathāhaṃ bhīmasenena vyutkramya samayaṃ hataḥ
|
bahūni sunṛśaṃsāni kṛtāni khalu pāṇḍavaiḥ||11||

12bhūriśravasi karṇe ca bhīṣme droṇe ca śrīmati
|
idaṃ cākīrtijaṃ karma nṛśaṃsaiḥ pāṇḍavaiḥ kṛtam||12||

13yena te satsu nirvedaṃ gamiṣyanti hi me matiḥ
|
kā prītiḥ sattvayuktasya kṛtvopādhikṛtaṃ jayam||13||

14ko vā samayabhettāraṃ budhaḥ sammantumarhati
|
adharmeṇa jayaṃ labdhvā ko na hṛṣyeta paṇḍitaḥ||14||

15yathā saṃhṛṣyate pāpaḥ pāṇḍuputro vṛkodaraḥ
|
kinnu citramitastvadya bhagnasakthasya yanmama||15||

16kruddhena bhīmasenena pādena mṛditaṃ śiraḥ
|
pratapantaṃ śriyā juṣṭaṃ vartamānaṃ ca bandhuṣu||16||

17evaṃ kuryānnaro yo hi sa vai sañjaya pūjitaḥ
|
abhijñau yuddhadharmasya mama mātā pitā ca me||17||

18tau hi sañjaya duḥkhārtau vijñāpyau vacanāddhi me
|
iṣṭaṃ bhṛtyā bhṛtāḥ samyagbhūḥ praśāstā sasāgarā||18||

19mūrdhni sthitamamitrāṇāṃ jīvatāmeva sañjaya
|
dattā dāyā yathāśakti mitrāṇāṃ ca priyaṃ kṛtam||19||

20amitrā bādhitāḥ sarve ko nu svantataro mayā
|
mānitā bāndhavāḥ sarve vaśyaḥ sampūjito janaḥ||20||

21tritayaṃ sevitaṃ sarvaṃ ko nu svantataro mayā
|
ājñaptaṃ nṛpamukhyeṣu mānaḥ prāptaḥ sudurlabhaḥ||21||

22ājāneyaistathā yātaṃ ko nu svantataro mayā
|
yātāni pararāṣṭrāṇi nṛpā bhuktāśca dāsavat||22||

23priyebhyaḥ prakṛtaṃ sādhu ko nu svantataro mayā
|
adhītaṃ vidhivaddattaṃ prāptamāyurnirāmayam||23||

24svadharmeṇa jitā lokāḥ ko nu svantataro mayā
|
diṣṭyā nāhaṃ jitaḥ saṅkhye parānpreṣyavadāśritaḥ||24||

25diṣṭyā me vipulā lakṣmīrmṛte tvanyagatā vibho
|
yadiṣṭaṃ kṣatrabandhūnāṃ svadharmamanutiṣṭhatām||25||

26nidhanaṃ tanmayā prāptaṃ ko na svantataro mayā
|
diṣṭyā nāhaṃ parāvṛtto vairātprākṛtavajjitaḥ||26||

27diṣṭyā na vimatiṃ kāñcidbhajitvā tu parājitaḥ
|
suptaṃ vātha pramattaṃ vā yathā hanyādviṣeṇa vā||27||

28evaṃ vyutkrāntadharmeṇa vyutkramya samayaṃ hataḥ
|
aśvatthāmā mahābhāgaḥ kṛtavarmā ca sātvataḥ||28||

29kṛpaḥ śāradvataścaiva vaktavyā vacanānmama
|
adharmeṇa pravṛttānāṃ pāṇḍavānāmanekaśaḥ||29||

30viśvāsaṃ samayaghnānāṃ na yūyaṃ gantumarhatha
|
vārtikāṃścābravīdrājā putraste satyavikramaḥ||30||

31adharmād bhīmasenena nihato'haṃ yathā raṇe
|
so'haṃ droṇaṃ svargagataṃ karṇaśalyāvubhau tathā||31||

32vṛṣasenaṃ mahāvīryaṃ śakuniṃ cāpi saubalam
|
jalasandhaṃ mahāvīryaṃ bhagadattaṃ ca pārthivam||32||

33somadattaṃ maheṣvāsaṃ saindhavaṃ ca jayadratham
|
duḥśāsanapurogāṃśca bhrātṝnātmasamāṃstathā||33||

34dauḥśāsaniṃ ca vikrāntaṃ lakṣmaṇaṃ cātmajāvubhau
|
etāṃścānyāṃśca subahūn madīyāṃśca sahasraśaḥ||34||

35pṛṣṭhato'nugamiṣyāmi sārthahīno yathā'dhvagaḥ
|
kathaṃ bhrātṝn hatāñśrutvā bhartāraṃ ca svasā mama||35||

36rorūyamāṇā duḥkhārtā duḥśalā sā bhaviṣyati
|
snuṣābhiḥ prasnuṣābhiśca vṛddho rājā pitā mama||36||

37gāndhārīsahitaścaiva kāṃ gatiṃ pratipatsyati
|
nūnaṃ lakṣmaṇamātāpi hataputrā hateśvarā||37||

38vināśaṃ yāsyati kṣipraṃ kalyāṇī pṛthalocanā
|
yadi jānāti cārvākā parivāḍvāgviśāradaḥ||38||

39kariṣyati mahābhāgo dhruvaṃ cāpacitiṃ mama
|
samantapañcake puṇye triṣu lokeṣu viśrute||39||

40ahaṃ nidhanamāsādya lokānprāpsyāmi śāśvatān
|
tato janasahasrāṇi bāṣpapūrṇāni māriṣa||40||

41pralāpaṃ nṛpateḥ śrutvā vyadravanta diśo daśa
|
sasāgaravanā ghorā pṛthivī sacarācarā||41||

42cacālātha sanirhrādā diśaścaivāvilābhavan
|
te droṇaputramāsādya yathāvṛttaṃ nyavedayan||42||

43vyavahāraṃ gadāyuddhe pārthivasya ca pātanam
|
tadākhyāya tataḥ sarve droṇaputrasya bhārata
|
dhyātvā ca suciraṃ kālaṃ jagmurārtā yathāgatam||43||
