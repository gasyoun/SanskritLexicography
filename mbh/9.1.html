Mahābhārata 9.1 (Śalyaparvan)

1janamejaya uvāca|
evaṃ nipātite karṇe samare savyasācinā
|
alpāvaśiṣṭāḥ kuravaḥ kimakurvata vai dvija||1||

2udīryamāṇaṃ ca balaṃ dṛṣṭvā rājā suyodhanaḥ
|
pāṇḍavaiḥ prāptakālaṃ ca kiṃ prāpadyata kauravaḥ||2||

3etadicchāmyahaṃ śrotuṃ tadācakṣva dvijottama
|
na hi tṛpyāmi pūrveṣāṃ śṛṇvānaścaritaṃ mahat||3||

4vaiśampāyana uvāca|tataḥ karṇe hate rājan dhārtarāṣṭraḥ suyodhanaḥ
|
bhṛśaṃ śokārṇave magno nirāśaḥ sarvato'bhavat||4||

5hā karṇa hā karṇa iti śocamānaḥ punaḥ punaḥ
|
kṛcchrātsvaśibiraṃ prāpto hataśeṣairnṛpaiḥ saha||5||

6sa samāśvāsyamāno'pi hetubhiḥ śāstraniścitaiḥ
|
rājabhirnālabhaccharma sūtaputravadhaṃ smaran||6||

7sa daivaṃ balavanmatvā bhavitavyaṃ ca pārthivaḥ
|
saṅgrāme niścayaṃ kṛtvā punaryuddhāya niryayau||7||

8śalyaṃ senāpatiṃ kṛtvā vidhivadrājapuṅgavaḥ
|
raṇāya niryayau rājā hataśaiṣairnṛpaiḥ saha||8||

9tataḥ sutumulaṃ yuddhaṃ kurupāṇḍavasenayoḥ
|
babhūva bharataśreṣṭha devāsuraraṇopamam||9||

10tataḥ śalyo mahārāja kṛtvā kadanamāhave
|
sasainyo'tha sa madhyāhne dharmarājena ghātitaḥ||10||

11tato duryodhano rājā hatabandhū raṇājirāt
|
apasṛtya hradaṃ ghoraṃ viveśa ripujādbhayāt||11||

12athāparāhne tasyāhnaḥ parivārya suyodhanaḥ
|
hradādāhūya yuddhāya bhīmasenena patitaḥ||12||

13tasminhate maheṣvāse hataśiṣṭāstrayo rathāḥ
|
saṃrambhānniśi rājendra jaghnuḥ pāñcālasomakān||13||

14tataḥ pūrvāhnasamaye śibirādetya sañjayaḥ
|
praviveśa purīṃ dīno duḥkhaśokasamanvitaḥ||14||

15sa praviśya purīṃ sūto bhujāvucchritya dukhitaḥ
|
vepamānastato rājñaḥ praviveśa niketanam||15||

16ruroda ca naravyāghraṃ hā rājanniti dukhitaḥ
|
aho bata vinaṣṭāḥ sma nidhanena mahātmanaḥ||16||

17vidhiśca balavānatra pauruṣaṃ tu nirarthakam
|
śakratulyabalāḥ sarve yathāvadhyanta pāṇḍavaiḥ||17||

18dṛṣṭvaiva ca pure rājañjanaḥ sarvaḥ sa sañjayam
|
kleśena mahatā yuktaṃ sarvato rājasattama||18||

19ruroda ca bhṛśodvigno hā rājanniti visvaram
|
ākumāraṃ naravyāghra tatra tatra samantataḥ||19||

20ārtanādaṃ tataścakre śrutvā vinihataṃ nṛpam
|
dhāvataścāpyapaśyāmastatra tān puruṣarṣabhān||20||

21naṣṭacittānivonmattān śokena bhṛśapīḍitān
|
tathā sa vihvalaḥ sūtaḥ praviśya nṛpatikṣayam||21||

22dadarśa nṛpatiśreṣṭhaṃ prajñācakṣuṣamīśvaram
|
tathā cāsīnamanaghaṃ samantātparivāritam||22||

23snuṣābhirbharataśreṣṭha gāndhāryā vidureṇa ca
|
tathānyaiśca suhṛddhiśca jñātibhiśca hitaiṣibhiḥ||23||

24tameva cārthaṃ dhyāyantaṃ karṇasya nidhanaṃ prati
|
rudannevābravīdvākyaṃ rājānaṃ janamejaya||24||

25nātihaṣṭamanāḥ sūto vākyasandigdhayā girā
|
sañjayo'haṃ naravyāghra namaste bharatarṣabha||25||

26madrādhipo hataḥ śalyaḥ śakuniḥ saubalastathā
|
ulūkaḥ puruṣavyāghra kaitavyo dṛḍhavikramaḥ||26||

27saṃśaptakā hatāḥ sarve kāmbojāśca śakaiḥ saha
|
mlecchāśca parvatīyāśca yavanā vinipātitāḥ||27||

28prācyā hatā mahārāja dākṣiṇātyāśca sarvaśaḥ
|
udīcyāśca hatāḥ sarve pratīcyāśca narottamāḥ||28||

29rājāno rājaputrāśca sarve te nihatā nṛpa
|
duryodhano hato rājā yathoktaṃ pāṇḍavena ha||29||

30bhagnasaktho mahārāja śete pāṃsuṣu rūṣitaḥ
|
dhṛṣṭadyumno mahārāja śikhaṇḍī cāparājitaḥ||30||

31uttamaujā yudhāmanyustathā rājan prabhadrakāḥ
|
pāñcālāśca naravyāghra cedayaśca niṣūditāḥ||31||

32tava putrā hatāḥ sarve draupadeyāśca bhārata
|
karṇaputro hataḥ śūro vṛṣasenaḥ pratāpavān||32||

33narā vinihatāḥ sarve gajāśca vinipātitāḥ
|
rathinaśca naravyāghra hayāśca nihatā yudhi||33||

34kiñciccheṣaṃ ca śibiraṃ tāvakānāṃ kṛtaṃ prabho
|
pāṇḍavānāṃ kurūṇāṃ ca samāsādya parasparam||34||

35prāyaḥ strīśeṣamabhavajjagatkālena mohitam
|
sapta pāṇḍavataḥ śeṣā dhārtarāṣṭrastrayo rathāḥ||35||

36te caiva bhrātaraḥ pañca vāsudevo'tha sātyakiḥ
|
kṛpaśca kṛtavarmā ca drauṇiśca jayatāṃ varaḥ||36||

37tathāpyete mahārāja rathino nṛpasattama
|
akṣauhiṇīnāṃ sarvāsāṃ sametānāṃ janeśvara||37||

38ete śeṣā mahārāja sarve'nye nidhanaṃ gatāḥ
|
kālena nihataṃ sarvaṃ jagadvai bharatarṣabha||38||

39duryodhanaṃ vai purataḥ kṛtvā vairaṃ ca bhārata
|
vaiśampāyana uvāca|
etacchrutvā vacaḥ krūraṃ dhṛtarāṣṭro janeśvaraḥ||39||

40nipapāta sa rājendro gatasattvo mahītale
|
tasminnipatite bhūmau viduro'pi mahāyaśāḥ||40||

41nipapāta mahārāja śokavyasanakarṣitaḥ
|
gāndhārī ca nṛpaśreṣṭha sarvāṃśca kuruyoṣitaḥ||41||

42patitāḥ sahasā bhūmau śrutvā krūraṃ vacastadā
|
niḥsaṃjñaṃ patitaṃ bhūmau tadāsīdrājamaṇḍalam||42||

43pralāpayuktaṃ mahati citranyastaṃ paṭe yathā
|
kṛcchreṇa tu tato rājā dhṛtarāṣṭro mahīpatiḥ||43||

44śanairalabhata prāṇān putravyasanakarśitaḥ
|
labdhvā tu sa nṛpaḥ saṃjñāṃ vepamānaḥ suduḥkhitaḥ||44||

45udīkṣya ca diśaḥ sarvāḥ kṣattāraṃ vākyamabravīt
|
vidvan kṣattarmahāprājña tvaṃ gatirbharatarṣabha||45||

46mamānāthasya subhṛśaṃ putrairhīnasya sarvaśaḥ
|
evamuktvā tato bhūyo visaṃjño nipapāta ha||46||

47taṃ tathā patitaṃ dṛṣṭvā bāndhavā ye'sya kecana
|
śītaiste siṣicustoyairvivyajurvyajanairapi||47||

48sa tu dīrgheṇa kālena pratyāśvasto narādhipaḥ
|
tūṣṇīṃ dadhyau mahīpālaḥ putravyasanakarśitaḥ||48||

49niḥśvasañjihmaga iva kumbhakṣipto viśāmpate
|
sañjayo'pyarudat tatra dṛṣṭvā rājānamāturam||49||

50tathā sarvāḥ striyaścaiva gāndhārī ca yaśasvinī
|
tato dīrgheṇa kālena viduraṃ vākyamabravīt||50||

51dhṛtarāṣṭro naraśreṣṭha muhyamāno muhurmuhuḥ
|
gacchantu yoṣitaḥ sarvā gāndhārī ca yaśasvinī||51||

52tatheme suhṛdaḥ sarve bhrāmyate me mano bhṛśam
|
evamuktastataḥ kṣattā tāḥ striyo bharatarṣabha||52||

53visarjayāmāsa śanairvepamānaḥ punaḥ punaḥ
|
niścakramustataḥ sarvāḥ striyo bharatasattama||53||

54suhṛdaśca tathā sarve dṛṣṭvā rājānamāturam
|
tato narapatiṃ tūrṇaṃ labdhasaṃjñaṃ parantapa||54||

55avaikṣatsañjayo dīnaṃ rodamānaṃ bhṛśāturam
|
prāñjalirniśvasantaṃ ca taṃ narendraṃ muhurmuhuḥ
|
samāśvāsayata kṣattā vacasā madhureṇa ca||55||
