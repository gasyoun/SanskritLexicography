Mahābhārata 13.94 (Anuśāsanaparvan)

1bhīṣma uvāca|
atraivodāharantīmamitihāsaṃ purātanam
|
yadvṛttaṃ tīrthayātrāyāṃ śapathaṃ prati tacchṛṇu ||1||

2puṣkarārthaṃ kṛtaṃ stainyaṃ purā bharatasattama
|
rājarṣibhirmahārāja tathaiva ca dvijārṣabhiḥ ||2||

3ṛṣayaḥ sametāḥ paścime vai prabhāse samāgatā mantramamantrayanta
|
carāma sarvāṃ pṛthivīṃ puṇyatīrthāṃ tannaḥ kāmaṃ haṃta gacchāma sarve ||3||

4śukro'ṅgirāścaiva kaviśca vidvāṃstathā hyagastyo nāradaparvatau ca
|
bhṛgurvasiṣṭhaḥ kaśyapo gautamaśca viśvāmitro jamada‌gniśca rājan ||4||

5ṛṣistathā gālavo'thāṣṭakaśca bharadvājo'rundhatī vālakhilyāḥ
|
śibirdilīpo nahuṣo'mbarīṣo rājā yayātirdhundhumāro'tha pūruḥ ||5||

6jagmuḥ puraskṛtya mahānubhāvaṃ śatakratuṃ vṛtrahaṇaṃ narendrāḥ
|
tīrthāni sarvāṇi paribhramanto mādhyāṃ yayuḥ kauśikīṃ puṇyatīrthām ||6||

7sarveṣu tīrtheṣvavadhūtapāpā jagmustato brahmasaraḥ supuṇyam
|
devasya tīrthe jalamagnikalpā vigāhya te bhuktabisaprasūnāḥ ||7||

8kecidbisānyakhanaṃstatra rājannanye mṛṇālānyakhanaṃstatra viprāḥ
|
athāpaśyanpuṣkaraṃ te hriyantaṃ hradādagastyena samuddhṛtaṃ tat ||8||

9tānāha sarvānṛṣimukhyānagastyaḥ kenāda‌ttaṃ puṣkaraṃ me sujātam
|
yuṣmānśaṅke puṣkaraṃ dīyatāṃ me na vai bhavanto hartumarhanti padmam ||9||

10śṛṇomi kālo hiṃsate dharmavīryaṃ so'yaṃ prāpto vartate dharmapīḍā
|
purā'dharmo vartate neha yāvattāvadgacchāmaḥ suralokaṃ cirāya ||10||

11purā vedānbrāhmaṇā grāmamadhye ghuṣṭasvarā vṛṣalānśrāvayanti
|
purā rājā vyavahāreṇa dharmānpaśyatyahaṃ paralokaṃ vrajāmi ||11||

12purāvarānpratyavarān garīyaso yāvannarā nāvamaṃsyanti sarve
|
tamottaraṃ yāvadidaṃ na vartate tāvadvrajāmi paralokaṃ cirāya ||12||

13purā prapaśyāmi pareṇa martyān balīyasā durbalānbhujyamānān
|
tasmādyāsyāmi paralokaṃ cirāya na hyutsahe draṣṭumiha jīvalokam ||13||

14tamāhurārtā ṛṣayo maharṣiṃ na te vayaṃ puṣkaraṃ corayāmaḥ
|
mithyābhiṣaṅgo bhavatā na kāryaḥ śapāma tīkṣṇaiḥ śapathairmaharṣe ||14||

15te niścitāstatra maharṣayastu saṃpaśyanto dharmametaṃ narendrāḥ
|
tato'śapanta śapathānparyayeṇa sahaiva te pārthiva putrapautraiḥ ||15||

16bhṛguruvāca|
pratyākrośedihākruṣṭastāḍitaḥ pratitāḍayet
|
khādecca pṛṣṭhamāṃsāni yaste harati puṣkaram ||16||

17vasiṣṭha uvāca|
asvādhyāyaparo loke śvānaṃ ca parikarṣatu
|
pure ca bhikṣurbhavatu yaste harati puṣkaram ||17||

18kaśyapa uvāca|
sarvatra sarvaṃ paṇatu nyāse lobhaṃ karotu ca
|
kūṭasākṣitvamabhyetu yaste harati puṣkaram ||18||

19gautama uvāca|
jīvatvahaṃkṛto buddhyā viṣameṇāsamena saḥ
|
karṣako matsarī cāstu yaste harati puṣkaram ||19||

20aṅgirā uvāca|
aśucirbrahmakūṭo'stu śvānaṃ ca parikarṣatu
|
brahmahā'nikṛtiścāstu yaste harati puṣkaram ||20||

21dhundhumāra uvāca|
akṛtajñastu mitrāṇāṃ śūdrāyāṃ ca prajāyatu
|
ekaḥ sampannamaśnātu yaste harati puṣkaram ||21||

22pūruruvāca|
cikitsāyāṃ pracaratu bhāryayā caiva puṣyatu
|
śvaśurāttasya vṛttiḥ syādyaste harati puṣkaram ||22||

23dilīpa uvāca|
uda‌pānaplave grāme brāhmaṇo vṛṣalīpatiḥ
|
tasya lokānsa vrajatu yaste harati puṣkaram ||23||

24śukra uvāca|
vṛthāmāṃsaṃ samaśnātu divā gacchatu maithunam
|
preṣyo bhavatu rājñaśca yaste harati puṣkaram ||24||

25jamada‌gniruvāca|
anadhyāyeṣvadhīyīta mitraṃ śrāddhe ca bhojayet
|
śrāddhe śūdrasya cāśnīyādyaste harati puṣkaram ||25||

26śibiruvāca|
anāhitāgnirmriyatāṃ yajñe vighnaṃ karotu ca
|
tapasvibhirvirudhyecca yaste harati puṣkaram ||26||

27yayātiruvāca|
anṛtau ca vratī caiva bhāryāyāṃ sa prajāyatu
|
nirākarotu vedāṃśca yaste harati puṣkaram ||27||

28nahuṣa uvāca|
atithirgṛhasaṃstho'stu kāmavṛttastu dīkṣitaḥ
|
vidyāṃ prayacchatu bhuto yaste harati puṣkaram ||28||

29ambarīṣa uvāca|
nṛśaṃsastyaktadharmo'stu strīṣu jñātiṣu goṣu ca
|
nihantu brāhmaṇaṃ cāpi yaste harati puṣkaram ||29||

30nārada uvāca|
gṛhajñānī bahiḥśāstraṃ paṭhatāṃ visvaraṃ padam
|
garīyaso'vajānātu yaste harati puṣkaram ||30||

31nābhāga uvāca|
anṛtaṃ bhāṣatu sadā sadbhiścaiva virudhyatu
|
śulkena tu dadatkanyāṃ yaste harati puṣkaram ||31||

32kaviruvāca|
padbhyāṃ sa gāṃ tāḍayatu sūryaṃ ca pratimehatu
|
śaraṇāgataṃ saṃtyajatu yaste harati puṣkaram ||32||

33viśvāmitra uvāca|
karotu bhṛtako'varṣāṃ rājñaścāstu purohitaḥ
|
ṛtvigastu hyayājyasya yaste harati puṣkaram ||33||

34parvata uvāca|
grāme cādhikṛtaḥ so'stu kharayānena gacchatu
|
śunaḥ karṣatu vṛttyarthe yaste harati puṣkaram ||34||

35bharadvāja uvāca|
sarvapāpasamādānaṃ nṛśaṃse cānṛte ca yat
|
tattasyāstu sadā pāpaṃ yaste harati puṣkaram ||35||

36aṣṭaka uvāca|
sa rājāstvakṛtaprajñaḥ kāmavṛttaśca pāpakṛt
|
adharmeṇābhiśāstūrvīṃ yaste harati puṣkaram ||36||

37gālava uvāca|
pāpiṣṭhebhyo hyanarghārhaḥ sa narostu svapāpakṛt
|
datvā dānaṃ kīrtayatu yaste harati puṣkaram ||37||

38arundhatyuvāca
|
śvaśvāpavādaṃ vadatu bharturbhavatu durmanāḥ
|
ekā svādu samaśnātu yā te harati puṣkaram ||38||

39vālakhilyā ūcuḥ |
ekapādena vṛttyarthaṃ grāmadvāre sa tiṣṭhatu
|
dharmajñastyaktadharmā'stu yaste harati puṣkaram ||39||

40śunaḥsakha uvāca|
agnihotramanādṛtya sa sukhaṃ svapatu dvijaḥ
|
parivrāṭ kāmavṛtto'stu yaste harati puṣkaram ||40||

41surabhyuvāca
|
bālajena nidānena kāṃsyaṃ bhavatu dohanam
|
duhyeta paravatsena yā te harati puṣkaram ||41||

42bhīṣma uvāca|
tatastu taiḥ śapathaiḥ śapyamānairnānāvidhairbahubhiḥ kauravendra
|
sahastrākṣo devarāṭ saṃprahṛṣṭaḥsamīkṣya taṃ kopanaṃ vipramukhyam ||42||

43athābravīnmaghavā pratyayaṃ svaṃ samābhāṣya tamṛṣiṃ jātaroṣam
|
brahmarṣirdevarṣinṛparṣimadhye yaṃ taṃ nibodheha mamādya rājan ||43||

44śakra uvāca|
adhvaryave duhitaraṃ dadātuchandoge vā caritabrahmacarye
|
atharvaṇaṃ vedamadhītya vipraḥsnāyīta yaḥ puṣkaramādadāti ||44||

45sarvānvedānadhīyīta puṇyaśīlo'stu dhārmikaḥ
|
brahmaṇaḥ sadanaṃ yātu yaste harati puṣkaram ||45||

46agastya uvāca|
āśīrvādastvayā proktaḥ śapatho balasūdana
|
dīyatāṃ puṣkaraṃ mahyameṣa dharmaḥ sanātanaḥ ||46||

47indra uvāca|
na mayā bhagavan lokāddhṛtaṃ puṣkaramadya vai
|
dharmāṃstu śrotukāmena hṛtaṃ na kroddhumarhasi ||47||

48dharmaśrutisamutkarṣo dharmaseturanāmayaḥ
|
ārṣo vai śāśvato nityamavyayo'yaṃ mayā śrutaḥ ||48||

49tadidaṃ gṛhyatāṃ vidvanpuṣkaraṃ dvijasattama
|
atikramaṃ me bhagavan kṣantumarhasyanindita ||49||

50ityuktaḥ sa mahendreṇa tapasvī kopano bhṛśam
|
jagrāha puṣkaraṃ dhīmānprasannaścābhavanmuniḥ ||50||

51prayayuste tato bhūyastīrthāni vanagocarāḥ
|
puṇyeṣu tīrtheṣu tathā gātrāṇyāplāvayanta te||51||

52ākhyānaṃ ya idaṃ yuktaḥ paṭhetparvaṇi parvaṇi
|
na mūrkhaṃ janayetputraṃ na bhavecca nirākṛtiḥ ||52||

53na tamāpatspṛśetkācidvijvaro na jarāvahaḥ
|
virajāḥ śreyasā yuktaḥ pretya svargamavāpnuyāt ||53||

54yaśca śāstramadhīyīta ṛṣibhiḥ paripālitam
|
sa gacchedbrahmaṇo lokamavyayaṃ ca narottama ||54||
