Mahābhārata 2.53 (Sabhāparvan)

1āryāstu ye vai rājānaḥ satyasandhā mahāvratāḥ
|
paryāptavidyā vaktāro vedāntāvabhṛthaplutāḥ||1||

2dhṛtimanto hrīniṣevā dharmātmāno yaśasvinaḥ
|
mūrdhābhiṣiktāste cainaṃ rājānaḥ paryupāsate||2||

3dakṣiṇārthaṃ samānītā rājabhiḥ kāṃsyadohanāḥ
|
āraṇyā bahusāhasrā apaśyaṃstatra tatragāḥ||3||

4ājahrastatra satkṛtya svayamudyamya bhārata
|
abhiṣekārthamavyagrā bhāṇḍamuccāvacaṃ nṛpāḥ||4||

5bāhlīko rathamāhārṣījjāmbūnadavibhūṣitam
|
sudakṣiṇastu yuyuje śvetaiḥ kāmbojajairhayaiḥ||5||

6sunīthaḥ prītimāṃścaiva hyanukarṣaṃ mahābalaḥ
|
dhvajaṃ cedipatiścaivamahārṣītsvayamudyatam||6||

7dākṣiṇātyaḥ sannahanaṃ sraguṣṇīṣe ca māgadhaḥ
|
vasudāno maheṣvāso gajendraṃ ṣaṣṭihāyanam||7||

8matsyastvakṣānhemanaddhānekalavya upānahau
|
āvantyastvabhiṣekārthamapo bahuvidhāstathā||8||

9cekitāna upāsaṅgaṃ dhanuḥ kāśya upāharat
|
asiṃ ca sutsaruṃ śalyaḥ śaikyaṃ kāñcanabhūṣaṇam||9||

10abhyapiñcattato dhaumyo vyāsaśca sumahātapāḥ
|
nāradaṃ ca puraskṛtya devalaṃ cāsitaṃ munim||10||

11prītimanta upātiṣṭhannabhiṣekaṃ maharṣayaḥ
|
jāmadagnyena sahitāstathānye vedapāragāḥ||11||

12abhijagmarmahātmāno mantravadbhūridakṣiṇam
|
mahendramiva devendraṃ divi saptarṣayo yathā||12||

13adhārayacchatramasya sātyakiḥ satyavikramaḥ
|
dhanañjayaśca vyajane bhīmasenaśca pāṇḍavaḥ||13||

14cāmare cāpi śuddhe dve yamau jagṛhatustathā
|
upāgṛhṇādyamindrāya purākalpe prajāpatiḥ||14||

15tamasmai śaṅkhamāhārṣīdvāruṇaṃ kalaśodadhiḥ
|
śaikyaṃ niṣkasahasreṇa sukṛtaṃ viśvakarmaṇā||15||

16tenābhiṣiktaḥ kṛṣṇena tatra me kaśmalo'bhavat
|
gacchanti pūrvādaparaṃ samudraṃ cāpi dakṣiṇam||16||

17uttaraṃ tu gacchanti vinā tāta patatribhiḥ
|
tatra sma dadhmuḥ śataśaḥ śaṅkhānmaṅgalakārakān||17||

18prāṇadanta samādhmātāstato romāṇi me'hṛṣan
|
prāpatanbhūmipālāśca ye tu hīnāḥ svatejasā||18||

19dhṛṣṭadyumnaḥ pāṇḍavāśca sātyakiḥ keśavo'ṣṭamaḥ
|
satvasthā vīryasampannā hyanyonyapriyadarśanāḥ||19||

20visañjñānbhūmipāndṛṣṭvā māṃ ca te prāhasaṃstadā
|
tataḥ prahṛṣṭo bībhatsuḥ prādāddhemaviṣāṇinām||20||

21śatānyanaḍuhāṃ pañca dvijamukhyeṣu bhārata
|
na rantidevo nābhāgo yauvanāśvo manurna ca||21||

22na ca rājā pṛthurvainyo na cāpyāsīdbhagīrathaḥ
|
yayātirnahuṣo vāpi yathā rājā yudhiṣṭhiraḥ||22||

23yathā'timātraṃ kaunteyaḥ śriyā paramayā yutaḥ
|
rājasūyamavāpyaivaṃ hariścandra iva prabhuḥ||23||

24etāndṛṣṭvā śriyaṃ pārthe hariścandre yathā vibho
|
kathaṃ tu jīvitaṃ śreyo mama paśyasi bhārata||24||

25andheneva yugaṃ naddhaṃ viparyastaṃ narādhipa
|
kanīyāṃso vivardhante jyeṣṭhā hīyanta eva ca||25||

26evaṃ dṛṣṭvā nābhivindāmi śarma samīkṣamāṇo'pi kurupravīra
|
tenāhamevaṃ kṛśatāṃ gataśca vivarṇatāṃ caiva saśokatāṃ ca||26||
