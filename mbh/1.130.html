Mahābhārata 1.130 (Ādiparvan)

1janamejaya uvāca|
kṛpasyāpi mama brahmansaṃbhavaṃ vaktumarhasi
|
śarastambātkathaṃ jajñe kathaṃ vā'strāṇyavāptavān||1||

2vaiśaṃpāyana uvāca|
maharṣergautamasyāsīccharadvānnāma gautamaḥ
|
putraḥ kila mahārāja jātaḥ saha śarairvibho||2||

3na tasya vedādhyayane tathā buddhirajāyata
|
yathāsya buddhirabhavaddhanurvede parantapa||3||

4adhijagmuryathā vedāṃstapasā brahmacāriṇaḥ
|
tathā sa tapasopetaḥ sarvāṇyastrāṇyavāpa ha||4||

5dhanurvedaparatvācca tapasā vipulena ca
|
bhṛśaṃ santāpayāmāsa devarājaṃ sa gautamaḥ||5||

6tato jālavatīṃ nāma devakanyāṃ sureśvaraḥ
|
prāhiṇottapaso vighnaṃ kuru tasyeti kaurava||6||

7sā hi gatvāśramaṃ tasya ramaṇīyaṃ śaradvataḥ
|
dhanurbāṇadharaṃ bālā lobhayāmāsa gautamam||7||

8tāmekavasanāṃ dṛṣṭvā gautamo'psarasaṃ vane
|
loke'pratimasaṃsthānāṃ protphullanayano'bhavat||8||

9dhanuśca hi śarāstasya karābhyāmapatanbhuvi
|
vepathuścāpi tāṃ dṛṣṭvā śarīre samajāyata||9||

10sa tu jñānagarīyastvāttapasaśca samarthanāt
|
avatasthe mahāprājño dhairyeṇa parameṇa ha||10||

11yastasya sahasā rājanvikāraḥ samadṛśyata
|
tena susrāva reto'sya sa ca tannānvabudhyata||11||

12dhanuśca saśaraṃ tyaktvā tathā kṛṣṇājināni ca
|
sa vihāyāśramaṃ taṃ ca tāṃ caivāpsarasaṃ muniḥ||12||

13jagāma retastattasya śarastambe papāta ca
|
śarastambe ca patitaṃ dvidhā tadabhavannṛpa||13||

14tasyātha mithuṃ jajñe gautamasya śaradvataḥ
|
mṛgayāṃ carato rājñaḥ śāntanostu yadṛcchayā||14||

15kaścitsenācaro'raṇye mithunaṃ tadapaśyata
|
dhanuśca saśaraṃ dṛṣṭvā tathā kṛṣṇājināni ca||15||

16jñātvā dvijasya cāpatye dhanurvedāntagasya ha
|
sa rājñe darśayāmāsa mithunaṃ saśaraṃ dhanuḥ||16||

17sa tadādāya mithunaṃ rājā ca kṛpayānvitaḥ
|
ājagāma gṛhāneva mama putrāviti bruvan||17||

18tataḥ saṃvardhayāmāsa saṃskāraiścāpyayojayat
|
prātipeyo naraśreṣṭho mithunaṃ gautamasya tat|| 18||

19gautamo'pi tato'bhyetya dhanurvedaparo'bhavat
|
kṛpayā yanmayā bālāvimau saṃvardhitāviti||19||

20tasmāttayornāma cakre tadeva sa mahīpatiḥ
|
gopitau gautamastatra tapasā samavandita||20||

21āgatya tasmai gotrādi sarvamākhyātavāṃstadā
|
caturvidhaṃ dhanurvedaṃ śāstrāṇi vividhāni ca||21||

22niśilenāsya tatsarvaṃ guhyamākhyātavāṃstadā
|
so'cireṇaiva kālena paramācāryatāṃ gataḥ||22||

23tato'dhijagmuḥ sarve te dhanurvedaṃ mahārathāḥ
|
dhṛtarāṣṭrātmajāścaiva pāṇḍavāḥ saha yādavaiḥ||23||

24vṛṣṇayaśca nṛpāścānye nānādeśasamāgatāḥ
|
vaiśaṃpāyana uvāca|
viśeṣārthī tato bhīṣmaḥ pautrāṇāṃ vinayepsayā||24||

25iṣvastrajñānparyapṛcchadācāryānvīryasaṃmatān
|
nālpadhīrnā mahābhāgastathā nānastrakovidaḥ||25||

26nādevasatvo vinayetkurūnastre mahāvalān
|
iti saṃcintya gāṅgeyaḥ sadā bharatasattamaḥ||26||

27droṇāya vedaviduṣe bhāradvājāya dhīmate
|
pāṇḍavānkauravāṃścaiva dadau śiṣyānnararṣabha||27||

28śāstrataḥ pūjitaścaiva samyaktena mahātmanā
|
sa bhīṣmeṇa mahābhāgastuṣṭo'straviduṣāṃ varaḥ||28||

29pratijagrāha tānsarvāñśiṣyatvena mahāyaśāḥ
|
śikṣayāmāsa ca droṇo dhanurvedamaśeṣataḥ||29||

30te'cireṇaiva kālena sarvaśastraviśāradāḥ
|
babhūvuḥ kauravā rājanpāṇḍavāścāmitaujasaḥ||30||

31janamejaya uvāca|
kathaṃ samabhavaddroṇaḥ kathaṃ cāstrāṇyavāptavān
|
kathaṃ cāgātkurūnbrahmankasya putraḥ sa vīryavān||31||

32kathaṃ cāsya suto jātaḥ so'śvatthāmāstravittamaḥ
|
etadicchāmyahaṃ śrotuṃ vistareṇa prakīrtaya||32||

33vaiśaṃpāyana uvāca|
gaṅgādvāraṃ prati mahānbabhūva bhagavānṛṣiḥ
|
bharadvāja iti khyātaḥ satataṃ saṃśitavrataḥ||33||

34so'bhiṣektuṃ gato gaṅgāṃ pūrvamevāgamannadīm
|
maharṣibhirbharadvājo havirdhāne caranpurā||34||

35dadarśāpsarasaṃ sākṣāddhṛtācīmāplutāmṛṣiḥ
|
rūpayauvanasaṃpannāṃ madadṛptāṃ madālasām||35||

36tasyā punarnadītīra vasanaṃ paryavartata
|
vyapakṛṣṭāmbarāṃ dṛṣṭvā tāmṛṣiścakame tataḥ||36||

37tatra saṃsaktamanaso bharadvājasya dhīmataḥ
|
tato'sya retaścaskanda tadṛṣirdroṇa ādadhe||37||

38tataḥ samabhavaddroṇaḥ kalaśe tasya dhīmataḥ
|
adhyagīṣṭa sa vedāṃśca vedāṅgāni ca sarvaśaḥ||38||

39agniveśyaṃ mahābhāgaṃ bharadvājaḥ pratāpavān
|
pratyapādayadāgneyamastramastravidāṃ varaḥ||39||

40agnestu jātaḥ sa munistato bharatasattama
|
bhāradvājaṃ tadāgneyaṃ mahāstraṃ pratyapādayat||40||

41bharadvājasakhā cāsītpṛṣato nāma pārthivaḥ
|
tasyāpi drupado nāma tathā samabhavatsutaḥ||41||

42sa nityamāśramaṃ gatvā droṇena saha pārthivaḥ
|
cikrīḍādhyayanaṃ caiva cakāra kṣatriyarṣabhaḥ||42||

43tato vyatīte pṛṣate sa rājā drupado'bhavat
|
pañcāleṣu mahābāhuruttareṣu nareśvaraḥ||43||

44bharadvājo'pi bhagavānāruroha divaṃ tadā
|
tatraiva ca vasandroṇastapastepe mahātapāḥ||44||

45vedavedāṅgavidvānsa tapasā dagdhakilbiṣaḥ
|
tataḥ pitṛniyuktātmā putralobhānmahāyaśāḥ||45||

46śāradvatīṃ tato bhāryāṃ kṛpīṃ droṇo'nvavindata
|
agnihotre ca dharme ca dame ca satataṃ ratām||46||

47alabhadgautamī putramaśvatthāmānameva ca
|
sa jātamātro vyanadadyathaivoccaiḥśravā hayaḥ||47||

48tacchrutvāntarhitaṃ bhūtamantarikṣasthamabravīt
|
aśvasyevāsya yatsthāma nadataḥ pradiśo gatam||48||

49aśvatthāmaiva bālo'yaṃ tasmānnāmnā bhaviṣyati
|
sutena tena suprīto bhāradvājastato'bhavat||49||

50tatraiva ca vasandhīmāndhanurvedaparo'bhavat
|
sa śuśrāva mahātmānaṃ jāmadagnyaṃ paraṃtapam||50||

51sarvajñānavidaṃ vipraṃ sarvaśastrabhṛtāṃ varam
|
brāhmaṇebhyastadā rājanditsantaṃ vasu sarvaśaḥ||51||

52sa rāmasya dhanurvedaṃ divyānyastrāṇi caiva ha
|
śrutvā teṣu manaścakre nītiśāstre tathaiva ca||52||

53tataḥ sa vratibhiḥ śiṣyaistapoyuktairmahātapāḥ
|
vṛtaḥ prāyānmahāvāhurmahendraṃ parvatottamam||53||

54tato mahendramāsādya bhāradvājo mahātapāḥ
|
kṣantaṃ dāntamamitraghnamapaśyadbhṛgunandanam||54||

55tato droṇo vṛtaḥ śiṣyairupagamya bhṛgūdvaham
|
ācakhyāvātmano nāma janma cāṅgirasaḥ kule||55||

56nivedya śirasā bhūmau pādau caivābhyavādayat
|
tatastaṃ sarvamutsṛjya vanaṃ jigamiṣuṃ tadā||56||

57jāmadagnyaṃ mahātmānaṃ bhāradvājo'bravīdidam
|
bharadvājātsamutpannaṃ tathā tvaṃ māmayonijam||57||

58āgataṃ vittakāmaṃ māṃ viddhi droṇaṃ dvijottama
|
tamabravīnmahātmā sa sarvakṣatriyamardanaḥ||58||

59svāgataṃ te dvijaśreṣṭha yadicchasi vadasva me
|
evamuktastu rāmeṇa bhāradvājo'bravīdvacaḥ||59||

60rāmaṃ praharatāṃ śreṣṭhaṃ ditsantaṃ vividhaṃ vasu
|
ahaṃ dhanamanantaṃ hi prārthaye vipulavrata||60||

61rāma uvāca|
hiraṇyaṃ mama yaccānyadvasu kiṃcidiha sthitam
|
brāhmaṇebhyo mayā dattaṃ sarvametattapodhana||61||

62tathaiveyaṃ dharā devī sāgarāntā sapattanā
|
kaśyapāya mayā dattā kṛtsnā nagaramālinī||62||

63śarīramātramevādya mamedamavaśeṣitam
|
śastrāṇi ca mahārhāṇi śastrāṇi vividhāni ca||63||

64astrāṇi vā śarīraṃ vā varayaitanmayodyatam
|
vṛṇīṣva kiṃ prayacchāmi tubhyaṃ droṇa vadāśu tat||64||

65droṇa uvāca|
astrāṇi me samagrāṇi sasaṃhārāṇi bhārgava
|
sa prayogarahasyāni dātumarhasyaśeṣataḥ||65||

66tathetyuktvā tatastasmai prādādastrāṇi bhārgavaḥ
|
sarahasyavrataṃ caiva dhanurvedamaśeṣataḥ||66||

67pratigṛhya tu tatsarvaṃ kṛtāstre dvijasattamaḥ
|
priyaṃ sakhāyaṃ suprīto jagāma drupadaṃ prati||67||
