Mahābhārata 12.355 (Śāntiparvan)

1atithiruvāca|
upadeśaṃ tu te vipra kariṣye'haṃ yathākramam
|
guruṇā me yathākhyātamarthatattvaṃ tu me śṛṇu||1||

2yatra pūrvābhisarge vai dharmacakra pravartitam
|
naimiṣe gomatītīre tatra nāgāhvayaṃ puram||2||

3samagraistridaśaistatra iṣṭamāsīddvijarṣabha
|
yatrendrātikramaṃ cakre māndhātā rājasattamaḥ||3||

4kṛtādhivāso dharmātmā tatra cakṣuḥśravā mahān
|
padmanābho mahānāgaḥ padma ityeva viśrutaḥ||4||

5sa vācā karmaṇā caiva manasā ca dvijarṣabha
|
prasādayati bhūtāni trividhe vartmani sthitaḥ||5||

6sāmnā bhedena dānena daṇḍeneti caturvidham
|
viṣamasthaṃ samasthaṃ ca cakṣurdhyānena rakṣati||6||

7tamatikramya vidhinā praṣṭumarhasi kāṃkṣitam
|
sa te paramakaṃ dharmaṃ na mithyā darśayiṣyati||7||

8sa hi sarvātithirnāgo buddhiśāstraviśāradaḥ
|
guṇairanupamairyuktaḥ samastairābhikāmikaiḥ||8||

9prakṛtyā nityasalilo nityamadhyayane rataḥ
|
tapodamābhyāṃ saṃyukto vṛttenānavareṇa ca||9||

10yajvā dānapatiḥ kṣānto vṛtte ca parame sthitaḥ
|
satyavāganasūyuśca śīlavānniyatendriyaḥ||10||

11śeṣānnabhoktā vacanānukūlo hitārjavotkṛṣṭakṛtākṛtajñaḥ
|
avairakṛdbhūtahite niyukto gaṅgāhradāṃbho'bhijanopapannaḥ||11||
