Mahābhārata 14.18 (Āśvamedhikaparvan)

1brāhmaṇa uvāca|
śubhānāmaśubhānāṃ ca neha nāśo'sti karmaṇām
|
prāpya prāpyānupacyante kṣetraṃ kṣetra tathā tathā||1||

2yathā prasūyamānastu phalī dadyātphalaṃ bahu
|
tathā syādvipulaṃ puṇyaṃ śuddhena manasā kṛtam||2||

3pāpaṃ cāpi tathaiva syāt pāpena manasā kṛtam
|
purodhāya mano hīdaṃ karmaṇyātmā pravartate||3||

4yathā karmasamāviṣṭaḥ kāmamanyusamāvṛtaḥ
|
naro garbhaṃ praviśati taccāpi śṛṇu cottaram||4||

5śukraṃ śoṇitasaṃsṛṣṭaṃ striyā garbhāśayaṃ gatam
|
kṣetraṃ karmajamāpnoti śubhaṃ vā yadi vāśubham||5||

6saukṣmyādavyaktabhāvācca na ca kvacana sajjati
|
samprāpya brāhmaṇaḥ kāmaṃ tasmāt tadbrahma śāśvatam||6||

7tadbījaṃ sarvabhūtānāṃ tena jīvanti jantavaḥ
|
sa jīvaḥ sarvagātrāṇi garbhasyāviśya bhāgaśaḥ||7||

8dadhāti cetasā sadyaḥ prāṇasthāneṣvavasthitaḥ
|
tata: spandayate'ṅgāni sa garbhaścetanānvitaḥ||8||

9yathā lohasya ni:syando niṣikto bimbavigraham
|
upaiti tadvijānīhi garbhe jīvapraveśanam||9||

10lohapiṇḍaṃ yathā vahniḥ praviśya hyatitāpayet
|
tathā tvamapi jānīhi garbhe jīvāpapādanam||10||

11yathā ca dīpaḥ śaraṇe dīpyamānaḥ prakāśate
|
evameva śarīrāṇi prakāśayati cetanā||11||

12yadyacca kurute karma śubhaṃ vā yadi vāśubham
|
pūrvadehakṛtaṃ sarvamavaśyamupabhujyate||12||

13tatastu kṣīyate caiva punaścānyatpracīyate
|
yāvattanmokṣayāgasthaṃ dharmaṃ naivāvabudhyate||13||

14tatra karma pravakṣyāmi sukhī bhavati yena vai
|
āvartamāno jātīṣu yathānyonyāsu sattama||14||

15dānaṃ vrataṃ brahmacaryaṃ yathoktaṃ brahmadhāraṇam
|
damaḥ praśāntatā caiva bhūtānāṃ cānukampanam||15||

16saṃyamāścānṛśaṃsyaṃ ca parasvādānavarjanam
|
vyalīkānāmakaraṇaṃ bhūtānāṃ manasā bhuvi||16||

17mātāpitrośca śuśrūṣā devatātithipūjanam
|
gurupūjā ghṛṇā śaucaṃ nityamindriyasaṃyamaḥ||17||

18pravartanaṃ śubhānāṃ ca tatsatāṃ vṛttamucyate
|
tato dharmaḥ prabhavati yaḥ prajāḥ pāti śāśvatīḥ||18||

19evaṃ satsu sadā paśyet tatrāpyeṣā dhruvā sthitiḥ
|
ācāro dharmamācaṣṭe yasmiśāntā vyavasthitāḥ||19||

20teṣu tatkarma nikṣiptaṃ yaḥ sa dharmaḥ sanātanaḥ
|
yastaṃ samabhipadyeta na sa durgatimāpnuyāt||20||

21ato niyamyate lokaḥ pracyavandharmavartmasu
|
yaśca yogī ca muktaśca sa etebhyo viśiṣyate||21||

22vartamānasya dharmeṇa śubhaṃ yatra yathā tathā
|
saṃsāratāraṇaṃ hyasya kālena mahatā bhavet||22||

23evaṃ pūrvakṛtaṃ karma nityaṃ jantu: prapadyate
|
sarvaṃ tatkāraṇaṃ yena vikṛto'yamihāgataḥ||23||

24śarīragrahaṇaṃ cāsya kena pūrvaṃ prakalpitam
|
ityevaṃ saṃśayo loke tacca vakṣyāmyata: param||24||

25śarīramātmanaḥ kṛtvā sarvalokapitāmahaḥ
|
trailokyamasṛjad brahmā kṛtsnaṃ sthāvarajaṅgamam||25||

26tataḥ pradhānamasṛjat prakṛtiṃ sa śarīriṇām
|
yayā sarvamidaṃ vyāptaṃ yāṃ loke paramāṃ viduḥ||26||

27idaṃ tatkṣaramityuktaṃ paraṃ tvamṛtamakṣaram
|
trayāṇāṃ mithunaṃ sarvamekaikasya pṛthak pṛthak||27||

28asṛjatsarvabhūtāni pūrvadṛṣṭaḥ prajāpatiḥ
|
sthāvarāṇi ca bhūtāni ityeṣā paurvikī śrutiḥ||28||

29tasya kālaparīmāṇamakarotsa pitāmahaḥ
|
bhūteṣu parivṛttiṃ ca punarāvṛttimeva ca||29||

30yathātra kaścinmedhāvī dṛṣṭātmā pūrvajanmani
|
yat pravakṣyāmi tatsarvaṃ yathāvadupapadyate||30||

31sukhaduḥkhe yathā samyaganitye yaḥ prapaśyati
|
kāyaṃ cāmedhyasaṅghātaṃ vināśaṃ karmasaṃhitam||31||

32yacca kiñcitsukhaṃ tacca duḥkhaṃ sarvamiti smaran
|
saṃsārasāgaraṃ ghoraṃ tariṣyati sudustaram||32||

33jātīmaraṇaraugaiśca samāviṣṭaḥ pradhānavit
|
cetanāvatsu caitanyaṃ samaṃ bhūteṣu paśyati||33||

34nirvidyate tataḥ kṛtsnaṃ mārgamāṇaḥ paraṃ padam
|
tasyopadeśaṃ vakṣyāmi yāthātathyena sattama||34||

35śāśvatasyāvyayasyātha yadasya jñānamuttamam
|
prācyamānaṃ mayā vipra nibodhedamaśeṣataḥ||35||
