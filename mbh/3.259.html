Mahābhārata 3.259 (Āraṇyakaparvan)

1vaiśampāyana uvāca|
vane nivasatāṃ teṣāṃ pāṇḍavānāṃ mahātmanām
|
varṣāṇyekādaśātīyuḥ kṛcchreṇa bharatarṣabha||1||

2phalamūlāśanāste hi sukhārhā duḥkhamuttamam
|
prāptakālamanudhyāntaḥ sehire varapūrupāḥ||2||

3yudhiṣṭhirastu rājarṣirātmakarmāparādhajam
|
cintayan sa mahābāhurbhrātṝṇāṃ duḥkhamuttamam||3||

4na suṣvāpa sukhaṃ rājā hṛdi śalyairivārpitaiḥ
|
daurātmyamanupaśyaṃstat kāle dyūtodbhavasya hi||4||

5saṃsmaran paruṣā vācaḥ sūtaputrasya pāṇḍavaḥ
|
niḥśvāsaparamo dīno bibhrat kopaviṣaṃ mahat||5||

6arjuno yamajau cobhau draupadī ca yaśasvinī
|
sa ca bhīmo mahātejāḥ sarveṣāmuttamo balī||6||

7yudhiṣṭhiramudīkṣantaḥ sehurduḥkhamanuttamam
|
avaśiṣṭamalpakālaṃ manvānāḥ puruṣarṣabhāḥ||7||

8vapuranyadivākārṣurutsāhāmarṣaceṣṭitaiḥ
|
kasyacit tvatha kālasya vyāsaḥ satyavatīsutaḥ||8||

9ājagāma mahāyogī pāṇḍavānavalokakaḥ
|
tamāgatamabhiprekṣya kuntīputro yudhiṣṭhiraḥ||9||

10pratyudgamya mahātmānaṃ pratyagṛhṇādyathāvidhi
|
tamāsīnamupāsīnaḥ śuśrūṣurniyatendriyaḥ||10||

11toṣayan praṇipātena vyāsaṃ pāṇḍavanandanaḥ
|
tānavekṣya kṛśān pautrān vane vanyena jīvataḥ||11||

12maharṣiranukampārthamabravīdbāṣpagadgadam
|
yudhiṣṭhira mahābāho śṛṇu dharmabhṛtāṃ vara||12||

13nātaptatapaso loke prāpnuvanti mahāsukham
|
sukhaduḥkhe hi puruṣaḥ paryāyeṇopasevate||13||

14na hyanantaṃ sukhaṃ kaścit prāpnoti puruṣarṣabha
|
prajñāvāṃstveva puruṣaḥ saṃyuktaḥ parayā dhiyā||14||

15udayāstamanajño hi na hṛṣyati na śocati
|
sukhamāpatitaṃ sevedduḥkhamāpatitaṃ vehat||15||

16kālaprāptamupāsīta sasyānāmiva karṣakaḥ
|
tapaso hi paraṃ nāsti tapasā vindate mahat||16||

17nāsādhyaṃ tapasaḥ kiñciditi buddhyasva bhārata
|
satyamārjavamakrodhaḥ saṃvibhāgo damaḥ śamaḥ||17||

18anasūyā'vihiṃsā ca śaucamindriyasaṃyamaḥ
|
pāvanāni mahārāja narāṇāṃ puṇyakarmaṇām||18||

19adharmarucayo mūḍhāstiryaggatiparāyaṇāḥ
|
kṛcchrāṃ yonimanuprāptā na sukhaṃ vindate janāḥ||19||

20iha yat kriyate karma tat paratropayujyate
|
tasmāccharīraṃ yuñjīta tapasā niyamena ca||20||

21yathāśakti prayaccheta sampūjyābhipraṇamya ca
|
kāle prāpte ca hṛṣṭātmā rājan vigatamatsaraḥ||21||

22satyavādī labhetāyuranāyāsamathārjavam
|
akrodhano'nasūyaśca nirvṛtiṃ labhate parām||22||

23dāntaḥ śamaparaḥ śaśvat parikleśaṃ na vindati
|
na ca tapyati dāntātmā dṛṣṭvā paragatāṃ śriyam||23||

24saṃvibhaktā ca dātā ca bhogavān sukhavānnaraḥ
|
bhavatyahiṃsakaścaiva paramārogyamaśnute||24||

25mānyamānayitā janma kule mahati vindati
|
vyasanairna tu saṃyogaṃ prāpnoti vijitendriyaḥ||25||

26śubhānuśayabuddhirhi saṃyuktaḥ kāladharmaṇā
|
prādurbhavati tadyogāt kalyāṇamatireva saḥ||26||

27yudhiṣṭhira uvāca|
bhagavan dānadharmāṇāṃ tapaso vā mahāmune
|
kiṃ svidbahuguṇaṃ pretya kiṃ vā duṣkaramucyate||27||

28vyāsa uvāca|
dānānna duṣkaraṃ tāta pṛthivyāmasti kiñcana
|
arthe ca mahatī tṛṣṇā sa ca duḥkhena labhyate||28||

29parityajya priyān prāṇān dhanārthaṃ hi mahāmate
|
praviśanti narā vīrāḥ samudramaṭavīṃ tathā||29||

30kṛṣigorakṣyamityeke pratipadyanti mānavāḥ
|
puruṣāḥ preṣyatāmeke nirgacchanti dhanārthinaḥ||30||

31tasmādduḥkhārjitasyaiva parityāgaḥ suduṣkaraḥ
|
na duṣkarataraṃ dānāt tasmāddānaṃ mataṃ mama||31||

32viśeṣastvatra vijñeyo nyāyenopārjitaṃ dhanam
|
pātre kāle ca daśe ca sādhubhyaḥ pratipādayet||32||

33anyāyāt samupāttena dānadharmo dhanena yaḥ
|
kriyate na sa kartāraṃ trāyate mahato bhayāt||33||

34pātre dānaṃ svalpamapi kāle dattaṃ yudhiṣṭhira
|
manasā hi viśuddhena pretyānantaphalaṃ smṛtam||34||

35atrāpyudāharantīmamitihāsaṃ purātanam
|
vrīhidroṇaparityāgādyat phalaṃ prāpa mudgalaḥ||35||
