Mahābhārata 3.11 (Āraṇyakaparvan)

1kirmīrasya vadhaṃ kṣattaḥ śrotumicchāmi kathyatām
|
rakṣasā bhīmasenasya kathamāsītsamāgamaḥ||1||

2vidura uvāca|
śṛṇu bhīmasya karmedamatimānuṣakarmaṇaḥ
|
śrutapūrvaṃ mayā teṣāṃ kathānteṣu punaḥpunaḥ||2||

3itaḥ prayātā rājendra pāṇḍavā dyūtanirjitāḥ
|
jagmustribhirahorātraiḥ kāmyakaṃ nāma tadvanam||3||

4rātrau niśīthe tvābhīle gate'rdhasamaye nṛpa
|
pracāre puruṣādānāṃ rakṣasāṃ ghorakarmaṇām||4||

5tadvanaṃ tāpasā nityaṃ gopāśca vanacāriṇaḥ
|
dūrātpariharanti sma puruṣādabhayātkila||5||

6teṣāṃ praviśatāṃ tatra mārgamāvṛtya bhārata
|
dīptākṣaṃ bhīṣaṇaṃ rakṣaḥ solmukaṃ pratyapadyata||6||

7bāhū mahāntau kṛtvā ca tathāsyaṃ ca bhayānakam
|
sthitamāvṛtya panthānaṃ yena yānti kurūdvahāḥ||7||

8spaṣṭāṣṭadaṃṣṭraṃ tāmrākṣaṃ pradīptordhvaśiroruham
|
sārkaraśmitaḍiccakraṃ sabalākamivāmbudam||8||

9sṛjantaṃ rākṣasīṃ māyāṃ mahānādanināditam
|
muñcantaṃ vipulānnādānsato'yamiva toyadam||9||

10tasya nādena santrastāḥ pakṣiṇaḥ sarvato diśam
|
vimuktanādāḥ sampetuḥ sthalajā jalajaiḥ saha||10||

11sampradrutamṛgadvīpimahiṣarkṣasamākulam
|
tadvanaṃ tasya nādena samprasthitamivābhavat||11||

12tasyoruvātābhihatāstāmrapallavabāhavaḥ
|
vidūrajātāśca latāḥ samāśliṣyanti pādapān||12||

13tasminkṣaṇe'tha pravavau māruto bhṛśadāruṇaḥ
|
rajasā saṃvṛtaṃ tena naṣṭajyotirabhūnnabhaḥ||13||

14pañcānāṃ pāṇḍuputrāṇāmavijñāto mahāripuḥ
|
pañcānāmindriyārthānāṃ śokāveśa ivātulaḥ||14||

15sa dṛṣṭvā pāṇḍavāndūrātkṛṣṇājinasamāvṛtān
|
āvṛṇottadvanadvāraṃ maināka iva parvataḥ||15||

16taṃ samāsādya vitrastā kṛṣṇā kamalalocanā
|
adṛṣṭapūrvaṃ santrāsānnyamīlayata locane||16||

17duḥśāsanakarotsṛṣṭaviprakīrṇaśiroruhā
|
pañcaparvatamadhyasthā nadīvākulatāṃ gatā||17||

18momuhyamānāṃ tāṃ tatra jagṛhuḥ pañca pāṇḍavāḥ
|
indriyāṇi prasaktāni viṣayeṣu yathā ratim||18||

19atha tāṃ rākṣasīṃ māyāmutthitāṃ ghoradarśanām
|
rakṣoghnairvividhairmantrairdhaumyaḥ samyakprayojitaiḥ||19||

20paśyatāṃ pāṇḍuputrāṇāṃ nāśayāmāsa vīryavān
|
sa naṣṭamāyo'tibalaḥ krodhavisphāritekṣaṇaḥ||20||

21kāmamūrtidharaḥ krūraḥ kālakalpo vyadṛśyata
|
tamuvāca tato rājā dīrghaprajño yudhiṣṭhiraḥ||21||

22ko bhavānkasya vā kiṃ te kriyatāṃ kāryamucyatām
|
pratyuvācātha tadrakṣo dharmarājaṃ yudhiṣṭhiram||22||

23ahaṃ bakasya vai bhrātā kirmīra iti viśrutaḥ
|
vane'sminkāmyake śūnye nivasāmi gatajvaraḥ||23||

24yudhi nirjitya puruṣānāhāraṃ nityamācaran
|
ke yūyamabhisamprāptā bhakṣyabhūtā mamāntikam
|
yudhi nirjitya vaḥ sarvānbhakṣayiṣye gatajvaraḥ||24||

25vaiśaṃpāyana uvāca|
yudhiṣṭhirastu tacchrutvā vacastasya durātmanaḥ
|
ācacakṣe tataḥ sarvaṃ gotranāmādi bhārata||25||

26yudhiṣṭhira uvāca|
pāṇḍavo dharmarājo'haṃ yadi te śrotramāgataḥ
|
sahito bhrātṛbhiḥ sarvairbhīmasenārjunādibhiḥ||26||

27hṛtarājyo vane vāsaṃ vastuṃ kṛtamatistataḥ
|
vanamabhyāgato ghoramidaṃ tava parigraham||27||

28vidura uvāca|
kirmīrastvabravīdenaṃ diṣṭyā devairidaṃ mama
|
upapāditamadyeha cirakālānmanogatam||28||

29bhīmasenavadhārthaṃ hi nityamabhyudyatāyudhaḥ
|
carāmi pṛthivīṃ kṛtsnāṃ nainaṃ cāsādayāmyaham||29||

30so'yamāsādito diṣṭyā bhrātṛhā kāṅkṣitaściram
|
anena hi mama bhrātā bako vinihataḥ priyaḥ||30||

31vaitrakīyavane rājanbrāhmaṇacchadmarūpiṇā
|
vidyābalamupāśritya na hyastyasyaurasaṃ balam||31||

32hiḍimbaśca sakhā mahyaṃ dayito vanagocaraḥ
|
hato durātmanānena svasā cāsya hṛtā purā||32||

33so'yamabhyāgato mūḍho mamedaṃ gahanaṃ vanam
|
pracārasamaye'smākamardharātre sthite same||33||

34adyāsya pātayiṣyāmi tadvairaṃ cirasambhṛtam
|
tarpayiṣyāmi ca bakaṃ rudhireṇāsya bhūriṇā||34||

35adyāhamanṛṇo bhūtvā bhrātuḥ sakhyustathaiva ca
|
śāntiṃ labdhāsmi paramāṃ hatva rākṣasakaṇṭakam||35||

36yadi tena purā mukto bhīmaseno bakena vai
|
adyainaṃ bhakṣayiṣyāmi paśyataste yudhiṣṭhira||36||

37enaṃ hi vipulaprāṇamadya hatvā vṛkodaram
|
sambhakṣya jarayiṣyāmi yathāgastyo mahāsuram||37||

38evamuktastu dharmātmā satyasandho yudhiṣṭhiraḥ
|
naitadastīti sakrodho bhartsayāmāsa rākṣasam||38||

39tato bhīmo mahābāhurārujya tarasā drumam
|
daśavyāmamathodviddhaṃ niṣpatramakarottadā||39||

40cakāra sajyaṃ gāṇḍīvaṃ vajraniṣpeṣagauravam
|
nimeṣāntaramātreṇa tathaiva vijayo'rjunaḥ||40||

41nivārya bhīmo jiṣṇuṃ taṃ tadrakṣo meghaniḥsvanam
|
abhidrutyābravīdvākyaṃ tiṣṭha tiṣṭheti bhārata||41||

42ityuktvainamatikruddhaḥ kakṣyāmutpīḍya pāṇḍavaḥ
|
niṣpiṣya pāṇinā pāṇiṃ saṃdaṣṭoṣṭhapuṭo balī||42||

43tamabhyadhāvadvegena bhīmo vṛkṣāyudhastadā
|
yamadaṇḍapratīkāśaṃ tatastaṃ tasya mūrdhani||43||

44pātayāmāsa vegena kuliśaṃ maghavāniva
|
asambhrāntaṃ tu tadrakṣaḥ samare pratyadṛśyata||44||

45cikṣepa colmukaṃ dīptamaśaniṃ jvalitāmiva
|
tadudastamalātaṃ tu bhīmaḥ praharatāṃ varaḥ||45||

46padā savyena cikṣepa tadrakṣaḥ punarāvrajat
|
kirmīraścāpi sahasā vṛkṣamutpāṭya pāṇḍavam||46||

47daṇḍapāṇiriva kruddhaḥ samare pratyadhāvata
|
tadvṛkṣayuddhamabhavanmahīruhavināśanam||47||

48vālisugrīvayorbhrātroryathā strīkāṅkṣiṇoḥ purā
|
śīrṣayoḥ patitā vṛkṣā bibhidurnaikadhā tayoḥ||48||

49yathaivotpalamālāni mattayordvipayostathā
|
muñjavajjarjarībhūtā bahavastatra pādapāḥ||49||

50cīrāṇīva vyudastāni rejustatra mahāvane
|
tadvṛkṣayuddhamabhavanmuhūrtaṃ bharatarṣabha
|
rākṣasānāṃ ca mukhyasya narāṇāmuttamasya ca||50||

51tataḥ śilāṃ samutkṣipya bhīmasya yudhi tiṣṭhataḥ
|
prāhiṇodrākṣasaḥ kruddho bhīmaśca na cacāla ha||51||

52taṃ śilātāḍanajaḍaṃ paryadhāvata rākṣasaḥ
|
bāhuvikṣiptakiraṇaḥ svarbhānuriva bhāskaram||52||

53tāvanyonyaṃ samāśliṣya prakarṣantau parasparam
|
ubhāvapi cakāśete prayuddhau vṛṣabhāviva||53||

54tayorāsītsutumulaḥ samprahāraḥ sudāruṇaḥ
|
nagadaṃṣṭrāyudhavatorvyāghrayoriva dṛptayoḥ||54||

55duryodhananikārācca bāhuvīryācca darpitaḥ
|
kṛṣṇānayanadṛṣṭaśca vyavardhata vṛkodaraḥ||55||

56abhipadya ca bāhubhyāṃ pratyagṛhṇādamarṣitaḥ
|
mātaṅgamiva mātaṅgaḥ prabhinnakaraṭāmukham||56||

57sa cāpyenaṃ tato rakṣaḥ pratijagrāha vīryavān
|
tamākṣipadbhīmaseno balena balināṃ varaḥ||57||

58tayorbhujaviniṣpeṣādubhayorbalinostadā
|
śabdaḥ samabhavadghoro veṇusphoṭasamo yudhi||58||

59athainamākṣipya balādgṛhya madhye vṛkodaraḥ
|
dhunayāmāsa vegena vāyuścaṇḍa iva drumam||59||

60sa bhīmena parāmṛṣṭo durbalo balinā raṇe
|
vyaspandata yathāprāṇaṃ vicakarṣa ca pāṇḍavam||60||

61tata enaṃ pariśrāntamupalakṣya vṛkodaraḥ
|
yoktrayāmāsa bāhubhyāṃ paśuṃ raśanayā yathā||61||

62vinadantaṃ mahānādaṃ bhinnabherīsvanaṃ balī
|
bhrāmayāmāsa suciraṃ visphurantamacetasam||62||

63taṃ viṣīdantamājñāya rākṣasaṃ pāṇḍunandanaḥ
|
pragṛhya tarasā dorbhyāṃ paśumāramamārayat||63||

64ākramya ca kaṭīdeśe jānunā rākṣasādhamam
|
pīḍayāmāsa pāṇibhyāṃ tasya kaṇṭhaṃ tasya vṛkodaraḥ||64||

65atha jarjarasarvāṅgaṃ vyāviddhanayanāmbaram
|
bhūtale pātayāmāsa vākyaṃ cedamuvāca ha||65||

66hiḍimbabakayoḥ pāpa na tvamaśrupramārjanam
|
kariṣyasi gataścāpi yamasya sadanaṃ prati||66||

67ityevamuktvā puruṣapravīrastaṃ rākṣasaṃ krodhaparītacetāḥ
|
visrastavastrābharaṇaṃ sphurantamudbhrāntacittaṃ vyasumutsasarja||67||

68tasminhate toyadatulyarūpe kṛṣṇāṃ puraskṛtya narendraputrāḥ
|
bhīmaṃ praśasyātha guṇairanekairhṛṣṭāstato dvaitavanāya jagmuḥ||68||

69vidura uvāca|
evaṃ vinihataḥ saṅkhye kirmīro manujādhipa
|
bhīmena vacanāttasya dharmarājasya kaurava||69||

70tato niṣkaṇṭakaṃ kṛtvā vanaṃ tadaparājitaḥ
|
draupadyā saha dharmajño vasatiṃ tāmuvāsa ha||70||

71samāśvāsya ca te sarve draupadīṃ bharatarṣabhāḥ
|
prahṛṣṭamanasaḥ prītyā praśaśaṃsurvṛkodaram||71||

72bhīmabāhubalotpiṣṭe vinaṣṭe rākṣase tataḥ
|
viviśuste vanaṃ vīrāḥ kṣemaṃ nihatakaṇṭakam||72||

73sa mayā gacchatā mārge vinikīrṇo bhayāvahaḥ
|
vane mahati duṣṭātmā dṛṣṭo bhīmabalāddhataḥ||73||

74tatrāśrauṣamahaṃ caitatkarma bhīmasya bhārata
|
brāhmaṇānāṃ kathayatāṃ ye tatrāsansamāgatāḥ||74||

75vaiśampāyana uvāca|
evaṃ vinihataṃ saṅkhye kirmīraṃ rakṣasāṃ varam
|
śrutvā dhyānaparo rājā niśaśvāsārtavattadā||75||
