Mahābhārata 7.115 (Droṇaparvan)

1sañjaya uvāca|
śṛṇuṣvaikamanā rājan yanmāṃ tvaṃ paripṛcchasi
|
drāvyamāṇe bale tasmin hārdikyena mahātmanā ||1||

2lajjayā'vanate cāpi prahṛṣṭaiścāpi tāvakaiḥ
|
dvīpo ya āsīt pāṃḍūnāmagādhe gādhamicchatām||2||

3śrutvā sa ninadaṃ bhīmaṃ tāvakānāṃ mahāhave
|
śaineyastvarito rājan kṛtavarmāṇamabhyayāt ||3||

4uvāca sārathiṃ tatra krodhāmarṣasamanvitaḥ
|
hārdikyābhimukhaṃ sūta kuru me rathamuttamam ||4||

5kurute kadanaṃ paśya pāṇḍusainye hyamarṣitaḥ
|
enaṃ jitvā punaḥ sūta yāsyāmi vijayaṃ prati||5||

6evamukte tu vacane sūtastasya mahāmate
|
nimeṣāntaramātreṇa kṛtavarmāṇamabhyayāt ||6||

7kṛtavarmā tu hārdikyaḥ śaineyaṃ niśitaiḥ śaraiḥ
|
avākiratsusaṃkruddhastato'kruddhyatsa sātyakiḥ||7||

8athāśu niśitaṃ bhallaṃ śaineyaḥ kṛtavarmaṇaḥ
|
preṣayāmāsa samare śarāṃśca caturo'parān ||8||

9te tasya jāghnire vāhān bhallenāsyācchiddhanuḥ
|
pṛṣṭharakṣaṃ tathā sūtamavidhyaniśitaiḥ śaraiḥ ||9||

10tatastaṃ virathaṃ kṛtvā sātyakiḥ satyavikramaḥ
|
senāmasyārdayāmāsa śaraiḥ sannataparvabhiḥ ||10||

11abhajyatātha pṛtanā śaineyaśarapīḍitā
|
tataḥ prāyātsa tvaritaḥ sātyakiḥ satyavikramaḥ||11||

12śṛṇu rājan yadakarot taba sainyeṣu vīryavān
|
atītya sa mahārāja droṇānīkamahārṇavam ||12||

13parājitya tu saṃhṛṣṭaḥ kṛtavarmāṇamāhave
|
yantāramabravīcchūraḥ śanairyāhītyasaṃbhramam||13||

14dṛṣṭvā tu tava tat sainyaṃ rathāśvadvipasaṃkulam
|
padātijanasaṃpūrṇamabravīt sārathiṃ punaḥ ||14||

15yadetanmedhasaṃkāśaṃ droṇānīkasya savyataḥ
|
sumahat kuñjarānīkaṃ yasya rukmaratho sukham ||15||

16ete hi bahavaḥ sūta durnivārāśca saṃyuge
|
duryodhanasamādiṣṭā madarthe tyaktajīvitāḥ ||16||

17rājaputrā maheṣvāsāḥ sarve vikrāntayodhinaḥ
|
trigartānāṃ rathodārāḥ suvarṇavikṛtadhvajāḥ ||17||

18māmevābhimukhā vīrā yotsyamānā vyavasthitāḥ
|
atra māṃ prāpaya kṣipramaśvāṃcodaya sārathe ||18||

19trigartaiḥ saha yotsyāmi bhāradvājasya paśyataḥ
|
tataḥ prāyācchanaiḥ sūtaḥ sātvatasya mate sthitaḥ||19||

20rathenādityavarṇena bhāsvareṇa patākinā
|
tamūhuḥ sārathervaśyā valgamānā hayottamāḥ||20||

21vāyuvegasamāḥ saṃkhye kundendurajataprabhāḥ
|
āpatantaṃ raṇe taṃ tu śaṃkhavarṇairhayottamaiḥ||21||

22parivavrustataḥ śūrā gajānīkena sarvataḥ
|
kiranto vividhāṃstīkṣṇān sāyakān laghuvedhinaḥ ||22||

23sātvato niśitairbāṇairgajānīkamayodhayat
|
parvatāniva varṣeṇa tapānte jalado mahān ||23||

24vajrāśanisamasparśarvadhyamānāḥ śarairgajāḥ
|
prādravan raṇamutsṛjya śinivīrasamīritaiḥ||24||

25śīrṇadantā virudhirā bhinnamastakapiṇḍikāḥ
|
viśīrṇakarṇāsyakarā viniyantṛpatākinaḥ ||25||

26saṃbhinnamarmaghaṇṭāśca vinikṛttamahādhvajāḥ
|
hatārohā diśo rājan bhejire bhraṣṭakaṃbalāḥ||26||

27ruvanto vividhānnādān jaladopamaniḥsvanāḥ
|
nārācairvatsadantaiśca bhallairañjalikaistathā ||27||

28kṣuraprairardhacandraiśca sātvatena vidāritāḥ
|
kṣaranto'sṛkra tathā sūtraṃ purīṣaṃ ca pradudruvuḥ ||28||

29babhramuścaskhaluścānye peturmamlustathā'pare
|
evaṃ tat kuñjarānīkaṃ yuyudhānena pīḍitam ||29||

30śarairagnyarkasaṃkāśaiḥ pradudrāva samantataḥ
|
tasminhate gajānīke jalasandho mahābalaḥ||30||

31yattaḥ saṃprāpayannāgaṃ rajatāśvarathaṃ prati
|
rukmavarmadharaḥ śūrastapanīyāṃgadaḥ śuciḥ ||31||

32kuṇḍalī mukuṭī khaḍgī raktacandanarūpitaḥ
|
śirasā dhārayan dīptāṃ tapanīyamayīṃ srajam||32||

33urasā dhārayanniṣkaṃ kaṇṭhasūtraṃ ca bhāsvaram
|
cāpaṃ ca rukmavikṛtaṃ vidhunvan gajamūrdhani ||33||

34aśobhata mahārāja savidyādeva toyadaḥ
|
tamāpatantaṃ sahasā māgadhasya gajottamam||34||

35sātyakirvārayāmāsa veleva makarālayam
|
nāgaṃ nivāritaṃ dṛṣṭvā śaineyasya śarottamaiḥ||35||

36akruddhyata raṇe rājan jalasaṃdho mahābalaḥ
|
tataḥ kruddho mahārāja mārgaṇairbhārasādhanaiḥ||36||

37avidhyata śineḥ pautraṃ jalasandho mahorasi
|
tato'pareṇa bhallena pītena niśitena ca||37||

38asyato vṛṣṇivīrasya nicakarta śarāsanam
|
sātyakiṃ chinnadhanvānaṃ prahasanniva bhārata ||38||

39avidhyanmāgadho vīraḥ pañcabhirniśitaiḥ śaraiḥ
|
sa viddho bahubhirbāṇairjalasandhena vīryavān||39||

40nākampata mahābāhustada‌dbhutamivābhavat
|
acintayan vai sa śarānnātyarthaṃ sambhramādbalī||40||

41dhanuranyat samādāya tiṣṭha tiṣṭhetyuvāca ha
|
etāvaduktvā śaineyo jalasandhaṃ mahorasi||41||

42vivyādha ṣaṣṭyā sabhṛśaṃ śarāṇāṃ prahasanniva
|
kṣurapreṇa sutīkṣṇena muṣṭideśe mahaddhanuḥ||42||

43jalasaṃdhasya ciccheda vivyādha ca tribhiḥ śaraiḥ
|
jalasaṃdhastu tattyaktvā sāraṃ vai śarāsanam ||43||

44tomaraṃ tyasṛjattūrṇaṃ sātyakiṃ prati māriṣa
|
sa nirbhidya bhujaṃ savyaṃ mādhavasya mahāraṇe ||44||

45abhyagāddharaṇīṃ ghoraḥ śvasanniva mahoragaḥ
|
nirbhinne tu bhuje savye sātyakiḥ satyavikramaḥ||45||

46triṃśadbhirviśikhaistīkṣṇairjalasandhamatāḍayat
|
pragṛhya tu tataḥ khaḍgaṃ jalasandho mahābalaḥ||46||

47ārṣabhaṃ carma ca mahacchatacandrakasaṃṅkulam
|
āvidhya ca tataḥ khaḍgaṃ sātvatāyotsasarja ha||47||

48śaineyastha dhanuśchittvā sa khaṅgo nyapatanmahīm
|
alātacakravaccaiva vyarocata mahīṃ gataḥ ||48||

49athānyaddhanurādāya sarvakāyāvadāraṇam
|
śālaskandhapratīkāśamindrāśanisamasvanam||49||

50visphārya vivyadhe kruddho jalasandhaṃ śareṇa ha
|
tataḥ sābharaṇau bāhū kṣurābhyāṃ mādhavottamaḥ ||50||

51sātyakirjalasandhasya ciccheda prahasanniva
|
tau bāhū parighaprakhyau petaturgajasattamāt||51||

52vasundharādharādbhraṣṭau pañcaśīrṣāvivoragau
|
tataḥ sudaṃṣṭraṃ sumahaccārukuṇḍalamaṇḍitam||52||

53kṣureṇāsya tṛrtāyena śiraściccheda sātyakiḥ
|
tatpātitaśirobāhukabandhaṃ bhīmadarśanam ||53||

54dviradaṃ jalasandhasya rudhireṇābhyaṣiñcata
|
jalasandhaṃ nihatyājau tvaramāṇastu sātvataḥ||54||

55vimānaṃ pātayāmāsa gajaskandhādviśāṃpate
|
rudhireṇāvasiktāṃgo jalasandhasya kuñjaraḥ||55||

56vilambamānamavahat saṃśliṣṭaṃ paramāsanam
|
śarārditaḥ sātvatena mardamānaḥ svavāhinīm||56||

57ghoramārtasvaraṃ kṛtvā vidudrāva mahāgajaḥ
|
hāhākāro mahānāsīttava sainyasya māriṣa||57||

58jalasandhaṃ hataṃ dṛṣṭvā vṛṣṇīnāmṛṣabheṇa tu
|
vimukhāścābhyadhāvanta tava yodhāḥ samantataḥ||58||

59palāyanakṛtotsāhā nirutsāhā dviṣajjaye
|
etasminnantare rājan droṇaḥ śastrabhṛtāṃ varaḥ||59||

60abhyayājjavanairaśvairyuyudhānaṃ mahāratham
|
tamudīrṇaṃ tathā dṛṣṭvā śaineyaṃ narapuṅgavāḥ||60||

61droṇenaiva saha kruddhāḥ sātyakiṃ samupādravan
|
tataḥ pravavṛte yuddhaṃ kurūṇāṃ sāttvatasya ca
|
droṇasya ca raṇe rājan ghoraṃ devāsuropamam||61||
