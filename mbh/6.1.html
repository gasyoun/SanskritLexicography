Mahābhārata 6.1 (Bhīṣmaparvan)

1janamejaya uvāca|
kathaṃ yuyudhire vīrāḥ kurupāṇḍavasomakāḥ pārthivāḥ sumahātmāno nānādeśasamāgatāḥ ||1||

2vaiśampāyana uvāca|
yathā yuyudhire vīrāḥ kurupāṇḍavasomakāḥ
|
kurukṣetre tapaḥkṣetre śṛṇu tvaṃ pṛthivīpate ||2||

3te'vatīrya kurukṣetraṃ pāṇḍavāḥ sahasomakāḥ
|
kauravāḥ samavartanta jigīṣanto mahābalāḥ ||3||

4vedādhyayanasampannāḥ sarve yuddābhinandinaḥ
|
āśaṃsanto jayaṃ yuddhe balenābhimukhā raṇe ||4||

5abhiyāya ca durdharṣāṃ dhārtarāṣṭrasya vāhinīm
|
prāṅmukhāḥ paścime bhāge nyavaśanta sasainikāḥ ||5||

6samantapañcakādbāhyaṃ śibirāṇi sahasraśaḥ
|
kārayāmāsa vidhivatkuntīputro yudhiṣṭhiraḥ ||6||

7śūnyā ca pṛthivī sarvā bālavṛddhāvaśeṣitā
|
niraśvapuruṣevāsīdrathakuñjaravarjitā ||7||

8yāvattapati suryo hi jambudvīpasya maṇḍalam
|
tāvadeva samāvṛttaṃ balaṃ pārthivasattama ||8||

9ekasthāḥ sarvavarṇāste maṇḍalaṃ bahuyojanam
|
paryākrāmanta deśāṃśca nadīḥ śailānvanāni ca ||9||

10teṣāṃ yudhiṣṭhiro rājā sarveṣāṃ puruṣarṣabha
|
vyādideśa sabāhyānāṃ bhakṣyabhojyamanuttamam ||10||

11śayyāśca vividhāstāta teṣāṃ rātrau yudhiṣṭhiraḥ
|
evaṃvedī veditavyaḥ pāṇḍaveyo'yamityuta ||11||

12abhijñānāni sarveṣāṃ saṃjñāścābharaṇāni ca
|
yojayāmāsa kauravyo yuddhakāla upasthite ||12||

13dṛṣṭvā dhvajāgraṃ pārthasya dhārtarāṣṭro mahāmanāḥ
|
saha sarvairmahīpālaiḥ pratyavyūhata pāṇḍavam ||13||

14pāṇḍureṇātapatreṇa dhriyamāṇena mūrdhani
|
madhye nāgasahasrasya bhrātṛbhiḥ parivāritaḥ ||14||

15dṛṣṭvā duryodhanaṃ hṛṣṭāḥ pañcālā yuddhanandinaḥ
|
dadhmuḥ prītā mahāśaṅkhānbheryaśca madhurasvanāḥ ||15||

16tataḥ prahṛṣṭāṃ tāṃ senāmabhivīkṣyātha pāṇḍavāḥ
|
babhūvurhṛṣṭamanaso vāsudevaśca vīryavān ||16||

17tato harṣaṃ samāgamya vāsudevadhanañjayau
|
dadhmatuḥ puruṣavyāghrau divyau śaṅkhau rathe sthitau ||17||

18pāñcajanyasya nirghoṣaṃ devadattasya cobhayoḥ
|
śrutvā tu ninadaṃ yodhāḥ śakṛnmūtraṃ prasusruvuḥ ||18||

19yathā siṃhasya nadataḥ svanaṃ śrutvetare mṛgāḥ
|
trayeyurninadaṃ śrutvā tathā'sīdata tadbalam ||19||

20udatiṣṭhadrajo bhaumaṃ na prājñāyata kiñcana
|
astaṅgata ivāditye sainyena sahasāvṛte ||20||

21vavarṣa tatra parjanyo māṃsaśoṇitavṛṣṭimān
|
dikṣu sarvāṇi sainyāni tadadbhutamivābhavat ||21||

22vāyustataḥ prādūrabhūnnīcaiḥ śarkarakarṣaṇaḥ
|
vinighnaṃstānyanīkāni śataśo'tha sahasraśaḥ ||22||

23ubhe sainye ca rājendra yuddhāya mudite bhṛśam
|
kurukṣetre sthite yatte sāgarakṣubhitopame ||23||

24tayostu senayorāsīdadbhutaḥ sa tu saṅgamaḥ
|
yugānte samanuprāpte dvayoḥ sāgarayoriva ||24||

25śūnyā''sītpṛthivī sarvā vṛddhabālāvaśeṣitā
|
niraśvapuruṣevāsīdrathakuñjaravarjitā ||25||

26tena senāsamūhena samānītena kauravaiḥ
|
tataste samayaṃ cakruḥ kurupāṇḍavasomakāḥ ||26||

27dharmān saṃsthāpayāmāsuryuddhānāṃ bharatarṣabha
|
nivṛtte vihite yuddhe syātprītirnaḥ parasparam ||27||

28yathāparaṃ yathāyogaṃ na ca syāt kasyacit punaḥ
|
vācā yuddhapravṛttānāṃ vācaiva pratiyodhanam
|
niṣkrāntāḥ pṛtanāmadhyānna hantavyāḥ kadācana ||28||

29rathī ca rathinā yodhyo gajena gajadhūrgataḥ
|
aśvenāśvī padātiśca pādātenaiva bhārata ||29||

30yathāyogaṃ yathākāmaṃ yathotsāhaṃ yathābalam
|
samābhāṣya prahartavyaṃ na viśvaste na vihvale ||30||

31ekena saha saṃyuktaḥ prapanno vimukhastathā
|
kṣīṇaśastro vivarmā ca na hantavyaḥ kadācana ||31||

32na sūteṣu na dhuryeṣu na ca śastropanāyiṣu
|
na bherīśaṅkhavādeṣu prahartavyaṃ kathañcana ||32||

33evaṃ te samayaṃ kṛtvā kurupāṇḍavasomakāḥ
|
vismayaṃ paramaṃ jagmuḥ prekṣamāṇāḥ parasparam ||33||

34nirviśya ca mahātmānastataste puruṣarṣabhāḥ
|
hṛṣṭarūpāḥ sumanaso babhūvuḥ sahasainikāḥ ||34||
