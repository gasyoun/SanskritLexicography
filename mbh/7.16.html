Mahābhārata 7.16 (Droṇaparvan)

1sañjaya uvāca|
tadbalaṃ sumahaddīrṇaṃ tvadīyaṃ prekṣya vīryavān
|
dadhāraiko raṇe rājan vṛṣaseno'stramāyayā||1||

2śarā daśa diśo muktā vṛṣasenena saṃyuge
|
viceruste vinirbhidya naravājirathadvipān||2||

3tasya dīptā mahābāṇā viniśceruḥ sahasraśaḥ
|
bhānoriva mahārāja gharmakāle marīcayaḥ||3||

4tenārditā mahārāja rathinaḥ sādinastathā
|
nipetururvyāṃ sahasā vātabhagnā iva drumāḥ||4||

5hayaughāṃśca rathaughāṃśca gajaughāṃśca mahārathaḥ
|
apātayadraṇe rājan śataśo'tha sahasraśaḥ||5||

6dṛṣṭvā tamekaṃ samare vicarantamabhītavat
|
sahitāḥ sarvarājānaḥ parivavruḥ samantataḥ||6||

7nākulistu śatānīko vṛṣasenaṃ samabhyayāt
|
vivyādha cainaṃ daśabhirnārācairmarmabhedibhiḥ||7||

8tasya karṇātmajaścāpaṃ chittvā ketumapātayat
|
taṃ bhrātaraṃ parīpsanto draupadeyāḥ samabhyayuḥ||8||

9karṇātmajaṃ śaravrātairadṛśyaṃ cakrurañjasā
|
tānnadanto'bhyadhāvanta droṇaputramukhā rathāḥ||9||

10chādayanto mahārāja draupadeyān mahārathān
|
śarairnānāvidhaistūrṇaṃ parvatān jaladā iva||10||

11tān pāṇḍavāḥ pratyagṛhṇaṃstvaritāḥ putragṛddhinaḥ
|
pañcālāḥ kekayā matsyāḥ sṛñjayāścodyatāyudhāḥ||11||

12tadyuddhamabhavaddhoraṃ sumahallomaharṣaṇam
|
tvadīyaiḥ pāṇḍuputrāṇāṃ devānāmiva dānavaiḥ||12||

13evaṃ yuyudhire vīrāḥ saṃrabdhāḥ kurupāṇḍavāḥ
|
parasparamudīkṣantaḥ parasparakṛtāgasaḥ||13||

14teṣāṃ dadṛśire kopādvapūṃṣyamitatejasām
|
yuyutsūnāmivākāśe patattrivarabhoginām||14||

15bhīma-karṇa-kṛpa-droṇa-drauṇi-pārṣata-sātyakaiḥ
|
babhāse sa raṇoddeśaḥ kālasūrya ivoditaḥ||15||

16tadāsīt tumulaṃ yuddhaṃ nighnatāmitaretaram
|
mahābalānāṃ balibhirdānavānāṃ yathā suraiḥ||16||

17tato yudhiṣṭhirānīkamuddhṛtārṇavaniḥsvanam
|
tvadīyamavadhīt sainyaṃ saṃpradrutamahāratham||17||

18tat prabhagnaṃ valaṃ dṛṣṭvā śatrubhirbhṛśamarditam
|
alaṃ drutena vaḥ śūrā iti droṇo'bhyabhāṣata||18||

19tataḥ śoṇahayaḥ kruddhaścaturdanta iva dvipaḥ
|
praviśya pāṇḍavānīkaṃ yudhiṣṭhiramupādravat||19||

20tamāvidhyacchitairbāṇaiḥ kaṅkapatrairyudhiṣṭhiraḥ
|
tasya droṇo dhanuśchittvā taṃ drutaṃ samupādravat||20||

21cakrarakṣaḥ kumārastu pañcālānāṃ yaśaskaraḥ
|
dadhāra droṇamāyāntaṃ veleva saritāṃ patim||21||

22droṇaṃ nivāritaṃ dṛṣṭvā kumāreṇa dvijarṣabham
|
siṃhanādaravo hyāsīt sādhu sādhviti bhāṣitam||22||

23kumārastu tato droṇaṃ sāyakena mahāhave
|
vivyādhorasi saṃkruddhaḥ siṃhavacca nadanmuhuḥ||23||

24saṃvārya caraṇe droṇaṃ kumārastu mahābalaḥ
|
śarairanekasāhastraiḥ kṛtahasto jitaśramaḥ||24||

25taṃ śūramāryavratinaṃ mantrāstreṣu kṛtaśramam
|
cakrarakṣaṃ parāmṛdnāt kumāraṃ dvijapuṅgavaḥ||25||

26sa madhyaṃ prāpya sainyānāṃ sarvāḥ pravicaran diśaḥ
|
tava sainyasya goptā''sīdbhāradvājo dvijarṣabhaḥ||26||

27śikhaṇḍinaṃ dvādaśabhirviṃśatyā cottamaujasam
|
nakulaṃ pañcabhirviddhvā sahadevaṃ ca saptabhiḥ||27||

28yudhiṣṭhiraṃ dvādaśabhirdraupadeyāṃstribhistribhiḥ
|
sātyakiṃ pañcabhirviddhvā matsyaṃ ca daśabhiḥ śaraiḥ||28||

29vyakṣobhayadraṇe yodhān yathā mukhyamabhidravan
|
abhyavartata saṃprepsuḥ kuntīputraṃ yudhiṣṭhiram||29||

30yugandharastato rājan bhāradvājaṃ mahāratham
|
vārayāmāsa saṃkruddhaṃ vātoddhatamivārṇavam||30||

31yudhiṣṭhiraṃ sa viddhvā tu śaraiḥ sannataparvabhiḥ
|
yugandharaṃ tu bhallena rathanīḍādapātayat||31||

32tato virāṭadrupadau kekayāḥ sātyakiḥ śibiḥ
|
yāghrada‌ttaśca pāñcālyaḥ siṃhasenaśca vīryavān||32||

33ete cānye ca bahavaḥ parīpsanto yudhiṣṭhiram
|
āvavrustasya panthānaṃ kirantaḥ sāyakān bahūn||33||

34vyāghradattastu pāñcālyo droṇaṃ vivyādha mārgaṇaiḥ
|
pañcāśatā śitai rājaṃstata uccukruśurjanāḥ||34||

35tvaritaṃ siṃhasenastu droṇaṃ viddhvā mahāratham
|
prāhasat sahasā hṛṣṭastrāsayan vai mahārathān||35||

36tato visphārya nayane dhanurjyāmavamṛjya ca
|
talaśabdaṃ mahatkṛtvā droṇastaṃ samupādravat||36||

37tatastu siṃhasenasya śiraḥ kāyāt sakuṇḍalam
|
vyāghradattasya cākramya bhallābhyāmāharadbalī||37||

38tān pramṛjya śaravrātaiḥ pāṇḍavānāṃ mahārathān
|
yudhiṣṭhirarathābhyāśe tasthau mṛtyurivāntakaḥ||38||

39tato'bhavanmahāśabdo rājan yaudhiṣṭhire bale
|
hato rājeti yodhānāṃ samīpasthe yatavrate||39||

40abruvan sainikāstatra dṛṣṭvā droṇasya vikramam
|
adya rājā dhārtarāṣṭraḥ kṛtārtho vai bhaviṣyati||40||

41asmin mahūrte droṇastu pāṇḍavaṃ gṛhya harṣitaḥ
|
āgamiṣyati no nūnaṃ dhārtarāṣṭrasya saṃyuge||41||

42evaṃ sañjalpatāṃ teṣāṃ tāvakānāṃ mahārathaḥ
|
āyājjavena kaunteyo rathaghoṣeṇa nādayan||42||

43śoṇitodāṃ rathāvartāṃ kṛtvā viśasane nadīm
|
śūrāsthicayasaṃkīrṇāṃ pretakūlāpahāriṇīm||43||

44tāṃ śaraughamahāphenāṃ prāsamatsyasamākulām
|
nadīmuttīrya vegena kurūn vidrāvya pāṇḍavaḥ||44||

45tataḥ kirīṭī sahasā droṇānīkamupādravat
|
chādayanniṣujālena mahatā mohayanniva||45||

46śīghramabhyasyato bāṇān saṃdadhānasya cāniśam
|
nāntaraṃ dadṛśe kaścit kaunteyasya yaśakhinaḥ||46||

47na diśo nāntarikṣaṃ ca na dyornaiva ca medinī
|
adṛśyanta mahārāja bāṇabhūtā ivābhavan||47||

48nādṛśyata tadā rājaṃstatra kiṃcana saṃyuge
|
bāṇāndhakāre mahati kṛte gāṇḍīvadhanvanā||48||

49sūrye cāstamanuprāpte tamasā cābhisaṃvṛte
|
nājñāyata tadā śatrurna suhṛnna ca kaścana||49||

50tato'vahāraṃ cakruste droṇaduryodhanādayaḥ
|
tān viditvā punastrastānayuddhamanasaḥ parān||50||

51svānyanīkāni bībhatsuḥ śanakairavahārayat
|
tato'bhituṣṭuvuḥ pārthaṃ prahṛṣṭāḥ pāṇḍusṛñjayāḥ||51||

52pañcālāśca manojñābhirvāgbhiḥ sūryabhivarṣayaḥ
|
evaṃ svaśibiraṃ prāyājjitvā śatrūn dhanañjayaḥ||52||

53pṛṣṭhataḥ sarvasainyānāṃ mudito vai sa keśavaḥ||53||

54masāragalvarkasuvarṇarūpairvajrapravālasphaṭikaiśca mukhyaiḥ
|
citre rathe pāṇḍusuto babhāse nakṣatracitre viyatīva candraḥ||54||
