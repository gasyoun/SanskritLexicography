Mahābhārata 12.327 (Śāntiparvan)

1bhīṣma uvāca|
etacchrutvā tu vacanaṃ kṛtātmā kṛtaniścayaḥ
|
ātmanā''tmānamāsthāya dṛṣṭvā cātmānamātmanā||1||

2kṛtakāryaḥ sukhī śāntastūṣṇīṃ prāyādudaṅmukha
|
śaiśiraṃ girimuddiśya sadharmā mātariśvanaḥ||2||

3etasminneva kāle tu devarṣirnāradastathā
|
himavantamiyāddraṣṭuṃ siddhacāraṇasevitam||3||

4tamapsarogaṇākīrṇaṃ śāntasvananināditam
|
kinnarāṇāṃ sahasraiśca bhṛṅgarājaistathaiva ca||4||

5madgubhiḥ khañjarīṭaiśca vicitrairjīvajīvakaiḥ||5||

6citravarṇairmayūraiśca kekāśatavirājitaiḥ
|
rājahaṃsasamūhaiśca kṛṣṇaiḥ parabhṛtaistathā||6||

7pakṣirājo garutmāṃśca yaṃ nityamadhitiṣṭhati
|
catvāro lokapālāśca devāḥ sarṣigaṇāstathā||7||

8tatra nityaṃ samāyānti lokasya hitakāmyayā
|
viṣṇunā yatra putrārthe tapastaptaṃ mahātmanā||8||

9tatraiva ca kumāreṇa bālye kṣiptā divaukasaḥ
|
śaktirnyastā kṣititale trailokyamavamanya vai||9||

10tatrovāca jagatskandaḥ kṣipanvākyamidaṃ tadā
|
yo'nyo'sti matto'bhyadhiko viprā yasyādhikaṃ priyāḥ||10||

11yo brahmaṇyo dvitīyo'sti triṣu lokeṣu vīryavān
|
so'bhyuddharattvimāṃ śaktimathavā kampayatviti||11||

12tacchrutvā vyathitā lokāḥ ka imāmuddharediti
|
atha devagaṇaṃ sarvaṃ saṃbhrāntendriyamānasam||12||

13apaśyadbhagavānviṣṇuḥ kṣiptaṃ sāsurarākṣasam
|
kintvatra sukṛtaṃ kāryaṃ bhavediti vicintayan||13||

14anāmṛṣya tataḥ kṣepamavaikṣata ca pāvakim
|
saṃpragṛhya viśuddhātmā śaktiṃ prajvalitāṃ tadā||14||

15kampayāmāsa savyena pāṇinā puruṣottamaḥ
|
śaktyāṃ tu kampyamānāyāṃ viṣṇunā balinā tadā||15||

16medinī kampitā sarvā saśailavanakānanā
|
śaktenāpi samuddhartuṃ kampitā sā'bhavattadā||16||

17rakṣitā skandarājasya dharṣaṇā prabhaviṣṇunā
|
tāṃ kampayitvā bhagavānprahlādamidamabravīt||17||

18paśya vīryaṃ kumārasya naitadanyaḥ kariṣyati
|
so'mṛṣyamāṇastadvākyaṃ samuddharaṇaniścitaḥ||18||

19jagrāha tāṃ tadā śaktiṃ na caināṃ sa vyakampayat
|
nādaṃ mahāntaṃ muktvā sa mūrcchito girimūrdhani||19||

20vihvala: prāpatadbhūmau hiraṇyakaśipoḥ sutaḥ
|
tatrottarāṃ diśaṃ gatvā śailarājasya pārśvataḥ||20||

21tapo'tapyata durdharṣaṃ tāta nityaṃ vṛṣadhvajaḥ
|
pāvakena parikṣiptaṃ dīpyatā yasya cāśramam||21||

22ādityaparvataṃ nāma durdharṣamakṛtātmabhiḥ
|
na tatra śakyate gantuṃ yakṣarākṣasadānavaiḥ||22||

23daśayojanavistāramagnijvālāsamāvṛtam
|
bhagavānpāvakastatra svayaṃ tiṣṭhati vīryavān||23||

24sarvānvighnānpraśamayanmahādevasya dhīmataḥ
|
divyaṃ varṣasahasraṃ hi pādenaikena tiṣṭhataḥ||24||

25devānsaṃtāpayaṃstatra mahādevo mahāvrataḥ
|
aindrīṃ tu diśamāsthāya śailarājasya dhīmataḥ||25||

26vivikte parvatataṭe pārāśaryo mahātapāḥ
|
vedānadhyāpayāmāsa vyāsa: śiṣyānmahātapāḥ||26||

27sumantuṃ ca mahābhāgaṃ vaiśampāyanameva ca
|
jaiminīṃ ca mahāprājñaṃ pailaṃ cāpi tapasvinam||27||

28yatra śiṣyaiḥ parivṛto vyāsa āste mahātapāḥ
|
tatrāśramapadaṃ ramyaṃ dadarśa pituruttamam||28||

29āraṇeyo viśuddhātmā nabhasīva divākaraḥ
|
atha vyāsa: parikṣiptaṃ jvalantamiva pāvakam||29||

30dadṛśe sutamāyāntaṃ divākarasamaprabham
|
asajjamānaṃ vṛkṣeṣu śaileṣu viṣayeṣu ca
|
yogayuktaṃ mahātmānaṃ yathā bāṇaṃ guṇacyutam||30||

31so'bhigamya pituḥ pādāvagṛhṇādaraṇīsutaḥ
|
yathopajoṣaṃ taiścāpi samāgacchanmahāmuniḥ||31||

32tato nivedayāmāsa pitre sarvamaśeṣataḥ
|
śuko janakarājena saṃvādaṃ prītamānasaḥ||32||

33evamadhyāpayanśiṣyānvyāsaḥ putraṃ ca vīryavān
|
uvāsa himavatpṛṣṭhe pārāśaryo mahāmuniḥ||33||

34tataḥ kadācicchiṣyāstaṃ parivāryāvatasthire
|
vedādhyayanasampannāḥ śāntātmāno jitendriyāḥ||34||

35vedeṣu niṣṭhāṃ samprāpya sāṅgeṣvapi tapasvinaḥ
|
athocuste tadā vyāsaṃ śiṣyāḥ prāñjalayo gurum||35||

36śiṣyā ūcuḥ| mahatā tejasā yuktā yaśasā cāpi vardhitāḥ
|
ekaṃ tvidānīmicchāmo guruṇā'nugrahaṃ kṛtam||36||

37iti teṣāṃ vacaḥ śrutvā brahmarṣistānuvāca ha
|
ucyatāmiti tadvatsā yadvaḥ kāryaṃ priyaṃ mayā||37||

38etadvākyaṃ guroḥ śrutvā śiṣyāste haṣṭamānasāḥ
|
punaḥ prāñjalayo bhūtvā praṇamya śirasā gurum||38||

39ūcuste sahitā rājannidaṃ vacanamuttamam
|
yadi prīta upādhyāyo dhanyāḥ smo munisattama||39||

40kāṅkṣāmastu vayaṃ sarve varaṃ dātuṃ maharṣiṇā
|
ṣaṣṭhaḥ śiṣyo na te khyātiṃ gacchedatra prasīda naḥ||40||

41catvāraste vayaṃ śiṣyā guruputraśca pañcamaḥ
|
iha vedāḥ pratiṣṭheranneṣaḥ naḥ kāṃkṣito varaḥ||41||

42śiṣyāṇāṃ vacanaṃ śrutvā vyāso vedārthatattvavit
|
parāśarātmajo dhīmānparalokārthacintakaḥ||42||

43uvāca śiṣyāndharmātmā dharmyaṃ naiḥśreyasaṃ vacaḥ
|
brāhmaṇāya sadā deyaṃ brahma śuśrūṣave tathā||43||

44brahmaloke nivāsaṃ yo dhruvaṃ samabhikāṃkṣate
|
bhavanto bahulāḥ santu vedo vistāryatāmayam||44||

45nāśiṣye saṃpradātavyo nāvrate nākṛtātmani
|
ete śiṣyaguṇā: sarve vijñātavyā yathārthataḥ||45||

46nāparīkṣitacāritre vidyā deyā kathaṃcana
|
yathā hi kanakaṃ śuddhaṃ tāpacchedanikarṣaṇaiḥ||46||

47parīkṣeta tathā śiṣyānīkṣetkulaguṇādibhiḥ
|
na niyojyāśca vaḥ śiṣyā aniyoge mahābhaye||47||

48yathāmati yathāpāṭhaṃ tathā vidyā phaliṣyati
|
sarvastaratu durgāṇi sarvo bhadrāṇi paśyatu||48||

49śrāvayeccaturo varṇānkṛtvā brāhmaṇamagrataḥ
|
vedasyādhyayanaṃ hīdaṃ tacca kāryaṃ mahatsmṛtam||49||

50stutyarthamiha devānāṃ vedāḥ sṛṣṭāḥ svayaṃbhuvā
|
yo nirvadeta saṃmohādbrāhmaṇaṃ vedapāragam||50||

51so'bhidhyānādbrāhmaṇasya parābhūyādasaṃśayam
|
yaścādharmeṇa vibrūyādyaścādharmeṇa pṛcchati||51||

52tayoranyataraḥ praiti vidveṣaṃ cādhigacchati
|
etadvaḥ sarvamākhyātaṃ svādhyāyasya vidhiṃ prati
|
upakuryācca śiṣyāṇāmetacca hṛdi vo bhavet||52||
