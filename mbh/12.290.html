Mahābhārata 12.290 (Śāntiparvan)

1yudhiṣṭhira uvāca|
ataḥ paraṃ mahābāho yacchreyastadbravīhi me
|
na tṛpyāmyamṛtasyeva vacasaste pitāmaha ||1||

2kiṃ karma puruṣaḥ kṛtvā śubhaṃ puruṣasattama
|
śreyaḥ paramavāpnoti pretya ceha ca tadvada ||2||

3bhīṣma uvāca|
atra te vartayiṣyāmi yathāpūrvaṃ mahāyaśāḥ
|
parāśaraṃ mahātmānaṃ papraccha janako nṛpaḥ ||3||

4kiṃ śreyaḥ sarvabhūtānāmasmi~lloke paratra ca
|
yadbhavetpratipattavyaṃ tadbhavānprabravītu me ||4||

5tataḥ sa tapasā yuktaḥ sarvadharmavidhānavit
|
nṛpāyānugrahamanā munirvākyamathābravīt ||5||

6parāśara uvāca |
dharma eva kṛtaḥ śreyāniha loke paratra ca
|
tasmāddhi paramaṃ nāsti yathā prāhurmanīṣiṇaḥ ||6||

7pratipadya naro dharmaṃ svargaloke mahīyate
|
dharmātmakaḥ karmavidhirdehināṃ nṛpasattama ||7||

8tasminnāśramiṇaḥ santaḥ svakarmāṇīha kurvate ||8||

9caturvidhā hi loke'sminyātrā tāta vidhīyate
|
martyā yatrāvatiṣṭhante sā ca kāmātpravartate ||9||

10sukṛtāsukṛtaṃ karma niṣevya vividhaiḥ kramaiḥ
|
daśārdhapravibhaktānāṃ bhūtānāṃ bahudhā gatiḥ ||10||

11sauvarṇaṃ rājataṃ cāpa yathā bhāṇḍaṃ niṣicyate
|
tathā niṣicyate jantuḥ pūrvakarmavaśānugaḥ ||11||

12nābījājjāyate kiṃcinnākṛtvā sukhamedhate
|
sukṛtairvindate saukhyaṃ prāpya dehakṣayaṃ naraḥ ||12||

13daivaṃ tāta na paśyāmi nāsti daivasya sādhanam
|
svabhāvato hi saṃsiddhā devagandharvadānavāḥ ||13||

14pretya yāntyakṛtaṃ karma na smaranti sadā janāḥ
|
te vai tasya phalaprāptau karma cāpi caturvidham ||14||

15lokayātrāśrayaścaiva śabdo vedāśrayaḥ kṛtaḥ
|
śāntyarthaṃ manasastāta naitadvṛddhānuśāsanam ||15||

16cakṣuṣā manasā vācā karmaṇā ca caturvidham
|
kurute yādṛśaṃ karma tādṛśaṃ pratipadyate ||16||

17nirantaraṃ ca miśraṃ ca labhate karma pārthiva
|
kalyāṇaṃ yadi vā pāpaṃ na tu nāśo'sya vidyate ||17||

18kadācitsukṛtaṃ tāta kūṭasthamiva tiṣṭhati
|
majjamānasya saṃsāre yāvadduḥkhādvimucyate ||18||

19tato duḥkhakṣayaṃ kṛtvā sukṛtaṃ karma sevate
|
sukṛtakṣayācca duṣkṛtaṃ tadviddhi manujādhipa ||19||

20damaḥ kṣamā dhṛtistejaḥ saṃtoṣaḥ satyavāditā
|
hrīrahiṃsā'vyasanitā dākṣyaṃ ceti sukhāvahāḥ ||20||

21duṣkṛte sukṛte cāpi na janturniyato bhavet
|
nityaṃ manaḥsamādhāne prayateta vicakṣaṇaḥ ||21||

22nāyaṃ parasya sukṛtaṃ duṣkṛtaṃ cāpi sevate
|
karoti yādṛśaṃ karma tādṛśaṃ pratipadyate ||22||

23sukhaduḥkhe samādhāya pumānanyena gacchati
|
anyenaiva janaḥ sarvaḥ saṃgato yaśca pārthivaḥ ||23||

24pareṣāṃ yadasūyeta na tatkuryātsvayaṃ naraḥ
|
yo hyasūyustathāyuktaḥ so'vahāsaṃ niyacchati ||24||

25bhīrū rājanyo brāhmaṇaḥ sarvabhakṣyo vaiśyo'nīhāvānhīnavarṇo'lasaśca
|
vidvāṃścāśīlo vṛttahīnaḥ kulīnaḥ satyādvibhraṣṭo brāhmaṇastrī ca duṣṭā ||25||

26rāgī yuktaḥ pacamano''tmahetormūrkho vaktā nṛpahīnaṃ ca rāṣṭram
|
ete sarve śocyatāṃ yānti rājan yaścāyuktaḥ snehahīnaḥ prajāsu ||26||
