Mahābhārata 7.200 (Droṇaparvan)

1sañjaya uvāca|
bhīmasenaṃ samākīrṇaṃ dṛṣṭvā'streṇa dhanañjayaḥ
|
tejasaḥ pratighātārthaṃ vāruṇena samāvṛṇot||1||

2nālakṣayata tat kaścidvāruṇāstreṇa saṃvṛtam
|
arjunasya laghutvācca saṃvṛtatvācca tejasaḥ||2||

3sāśvasūtaratho bhīmo droṇaputrāstrasaṃvṛtaḥ
|
agnāvagniriva nyasto jvālāmālī sudurdṛśaḥ||3||

4yathā rātrikṣaye rājan jyotīṃṣyastagiraṃ prati
|
samāpetustathā bāṇā bhīmasenarathaṃ prati||4||

5sa hi bhīmo rathaścāsya hayāḥ sutaśca māriṣa
|
saṃvṛtā droṇaputreṇa pāvakāntargatā'bhavan||5||

6yathā jagdhvā jagatkṛtsnaṃ samaye sacarācaram
|
gacchedvanhirvibhorāsyaṃ tathā'straṃ bhīmamāvṛṇot||6||

7sūryamagniḥ praviṣṭaḥ syādyathā cāgniṃ divākaraḥ
|
tathā praviṣṭaṃ tattejo na prājñāyata pāṇḍavam||7||

8vikīrṇamastraṃ tadṛṣṭvā tathā bhīmarathaṃ prati
|
udīryamāṇaṃ drauṇiṃ ca niṣpratidvandvamāhave||8||

9sarvasainyaṃ ca pāṇḍūnāṃ nyastaśastramacetanam
|
yudhiṣṭhirapurogāṃśca vimukhāṃstānmahārathān||9||

10arjuno vāsudevaśca tvaramāṇau mahādyutī
|
avaplutya rathādvīrau bhīmamādravatāṃ tataḥ||10||

11tatastaddroṇaputrasya tejo'strabalasaṃbhavam
|
vigāhya tau subalinau māyayā'viśatāṃ tathā||11||

12nyastaśastrau tatastau tu nādahatsostrajo'nalaḥ
|
vāruṇāstraprayogācca vīryavattvācca kṛṣṇayoḥ||12||

13tataścakṛṣaturbhīmaṃ sarvaśastrāyudhāni ca
|
nārāyaṇāstraśāntyarthaṃ naranārāyaṇau balāt||13||

14ākṛṣyamāṇaḥ kauntayo nadatyeva mahāravam
|
vardhate caiva tadghoraṃ drauṇerastraṃ sudurjayam||14||

15tamabravīdvāsudevaḥ kimidaṃ pāṇḍunandana
|
vāryamāṇo'pi kaunteya yadyuddhānna nivartase||15||

16yadi yuddhena jeyāḥ syurime kauravanandanāḥ
|
vayamapyatra yudhyema tathā ceme nararṣabhāḥ||16||

17rathebhyastvavatīrṇāḥ sma sarva eva hi tāvakāḥ
|
tasmāttvamapi kaunteya rathāttūrṇamapākrama||17||

18evamuktvā tu taṃ kṛṣṇo rathādbhūmimavartayat
|
niḥśvasantaṃ yathā nāgaṃ krodhasaṃraktalocanam||18||

19yadā'pakṛṣṭaḥ sa rathānnyāsitaścāyudhaṃ bhuvi
|
tato nārāyaṇāstraṃ tat praśāntaṃ śatrutāpanam||19||

20sañjaya uvāca|
tasminpraśānte vidhinā tena tejasi duḥsahe
|
babhūvurvimalāḥ sarvā diśaḥ pradiśa eva ca||20||

21pravavuśca śivā vātāḥ praśāntā mṛgapakṣiṇaḥ
|
vāhanāni ca hṛṣṭāni praśānte'stre sudurjaye||21||

22vyapoḍhe ca tato ghore tasmiṃstejasi bhārata
|
babhau bhīmo niśāpāye dhīmānsūrya ivoditaḥ||22||

23hataśeṣaṃ balaṃ tattu pāṇḍavānāmatiṣṭhata
|
astravyuparamāddhṛṣṭaṃ tava putrajighāṃsayā||23||

24vyavasthite bale tasminnastre pratihate tathā
|
duryodhano mahārāja droṇaputramathābravīt||24||

25aśvatthāmanpunaḥ śīghramastrametatprayojaya
|
avasthitā hi pañcālāḥ punarete jayaiṣiṇaḥ||25||

26aśvatthāmā tathoktastu tava putreṇa māriṣa
|
sudīnamabhiniḥśvasya rājānamidamabravīt||26||

27naitadāvartate rājannastraṃ dvirnopapadyate
|
āvṛtaṃ hi nivarteta prayoktāraṃ na saṃśayaḥ||27||

28eṣa cāstrapratīghātaṃ vāsudevaḥ prayuktavān
|
anyathā vihitaḥ saṃkhye vadhaḥ śatrorjanādhipa||28||

29parājayo vā mṛtyurvā śreyānmṛtyurna nirjayaḥ
|
vijitāścārayo hyete śastrotsargānmṛtopamāḥ||29||

30duryodhana uvāca|
ācāryaputra yadyetaddvirastraṃ na prayujyate
|
anyairgurughnā vadhyantāmastrairastravidāṃ vara||30||

31tvayi śastrāṇi divyāni tryambake cāmitaujasi
|
icchato na hi te mucyet saṃkruddho hi purandaraḥ||31||

32dhṛtarāṣṭra uvāca|
tasminnastre pratihate droṇe copadhinā hate
|
tathā duryodhanenokto drauṇiḥ kimakarotpunaḥ||32||

33dṛṣṭvā pārthāṃśca saṃgrāme yuddhāya samupasthitān
|
nārāyaṇāstranirmuktāṃścarataḥ pṛtanāmukhe||33||

34sañjaya uvāca|
jānanpituḥ sa nidhanaṃ siṃhalāṅgulaketanaḥ
|
sakrodho bhayamutsṛjya so'bhidudrāva pārṣatam||34||

35abhidrutya ca viṃśatyā kṣudrakāṇāṃ nararṣabha
|
pañcabhiścātivegena vivyādha puruṣarṣabhaḥ||35||

36dhṛṣṭadyumnastato rājan jvalantamiva pāvakam
|
droṇaputraṃ triṣaṣṭyā tu rājanvivyādha patriṇām||36||

37sārathiṃ cāsya viṃśatyā svarṇapuṅkhaiḥ śilāśitaiḥ
|
hayāṃśca caturo'vidhyaccaturbhirniśitaiḥ śaraiḥ||37||

38viddhvā viddhvā'nadaddrauṇiṃ kampayanniva medinīm
|
ādade sarvalokasya prāṇāniva mahāraṇe||38||

39pārṣatastu balī rājankṛtāstraḥ kṛtaniścayaḥ
|
drauṇimevābhidudrāva mṛtyuṃ kṛtvā nivartanam||39||

40tato bāṇamayaṃ varṣaṃ droṇaputrasya mūrdhani
|
avāsṛjada‌meyātmā pāñcālyo rathināṃ varaḥ||40||

41taṃ drauṇiḥ samare kruddhaṃ chādayāmāsa patribhiḥ
|
vivyādha cainaṃ daśabhiḥ piturvadhamanusmaran||41||

42dvābhyāṃ ca suvisṛṣṭābhyāṃ kṣurābhyāṃ dhvajakārmuke
|
chittvā pāñcālarājasya drauṇiranyaiḥ samārdayat||42||

43vyaśvasutarathaṃ cainaṃ drauṇiścakre mahāhave
|
tasya cānucarānsarvān kruddhaḥ prādrāvayaccharaiḥ||43||

44tataḥ pradudruve sainyaṃ pañcālānāṃ viśāṃpate
|
saṃbhrāntarūpamārtaṃ ca na parasparamaikṣata||44||

45dṛṣṭvā tu vimukhānyodhāndhṛṣṭadyumnaṃ ca pīḍitam
|
śaineyo'codayattūrṇaṃ rathaṃ drauṇirathaṃ prati||45||

46aṣṭabhirniśitairbāṇairaśvatthāmānamārdayat
|
viṃśatyā punarāhatya nānārūpairamarṣaṇaḥ||46||

47vivyādha ca tathā sūtaṃ caturbhiścaturo hayān
|
dhanurdhvajaṃ ca saṃyattaściccheda kṛtahastavat||47||

48sa sāśvaṃ vyadhamaccāpi rathaṃ hemapariṣkṛtam
|
hṛdi vivyādha samare triṃśatā sāyakairbhṛśam||48||

49evaṃ sa pīḍito rājannaśvatthāmā mahābalaḥ
|
śarajālaiḥ parivṛtaḥ kartavyaṃ nānvapadyata||49||

50evaṃ gate guroḥ putre tava putro mahārathaḥ
|
kṛpakarṇādibhiḥ sārdhaṃ śaraiḥ sātvatamāvṛṇot||50||

51duryodhanastu viṃśatyā kṛpaḥ śāradvatastribhiḥ
|
kṛtavarmā'tha daśabhiḥ karṇaḥ pañcāśatā śaraiḥ||51||

52duḥśāsanaḥ śatenaiva vṛṣasenaśca saptabhiḥ
|
sātyakiṃ vivyadhustūrṇaṃ samantānniśitaiḥ śaraiḥ||52||

53tataḥ sa sātyakī rājansarvāneva mahārathān
|
virathānvimukhāṃścaiva kṣaṇenaivākaronnṛpa||53||

54aśvatthāmā tu saṃprāpya cetanāṃ bharatarṣabha
|
cintayāmāsa duḥkhārto niḥśvasaṃśca punaḥ punaḥ||54||

55atho rathāntaraṃ drauṇiḥ samāruhya paraṃtapaḥ
|
sātyakiṃ vārayāmāsa kirañśaraśatānbahūn||55||

56tamāpatantaṃ saṃprekṣya bhāradvājasutaṃ raṇe
|
virathaṃ vimukhaṃ caiva punaścakre mahārathaḥ||56||

57tataste pāṇḍavā rājan dṛṣṭvā sātyakivikramam
|
śaṃkhaśabdānbhṛśaṃ cakruḥ siṃhanādāṃśca nedire||57||

58evaṃ taṃ virathaṃ kṛtvā sātyakiḥ satyavikramaḥ
|
jaghāna vṛṣasenasya trisāhastrānmahārathān||58||

59ayutaṃ dantināṃ sārdhaṃ kṛpasya nijaghāna saḥ
|
pañcāyutāni cāśvānāṃ śakunernijaghāna ha||59||

60tato drauṇirmahārāja rathamāruhya vīryavān
|
sātyakiṃ pratisaṃkruddhaḥ prayayau tadvadhepsayā||60||

61punastamāgataṃ dṛṣṭvā śaineyo niśitaiḥ śaraiḥ
|
adārayatkrūrataraiḥ punaḥ punarariṃdama||61||

62so'tividdho maheṣvāso nānāliṅgairamarṣaṇaḥ
|
yuyudhānena vai drauṇiḥ prahasanvākyamabravīt||62||

63śaineyābhyupapattiṃ te jānāmyācāryaghātini
|
na cainaṃ trāsyasi mayā grastamātmānameva ca||63||

64śape''tmanā'haṃ śaineya satyena tapasā tathā
|
ahatvā sarvapañcālānyadi śāntimahaṃ labhe||64||

65yadbalaṃ pāṇḍaveyānāṃ vṛṣṇīnāmapi yadbalam
|
kriyatāṃ sarvameveha nihaniṣyāmi somakān||65||

66evamuktvārkaraśmyābhaṃ sutīkṣṇaṃ taṃ śarottamam
|
vyasṛjatsātvate drauṇirvajraṃ vṛtre yathā hariḥ||66||

67sa taṃ nirbhidya tenāstaḥ sāyakaḥ saśarāvaram
|
viveśa vasudhāṃ bhittvā śvasanbilamivoragaḥ||67||

68sa bhinnakavacaḥ śūrastotrārdita iva dvipaḥ
|
vimucya saśaraṃ cāpaṃ bhūrivraṇaparisravaḥ||68||

69sīdan rudhirasiktaśca rathopastha upāviśat
|
sūtenāpahṛtastūrṇaṃ droṇaputrādrathāntaram||69||

70athānyena supuṅkhena śareṇānataparvaṇā
|
ājaghāna bhruvormadhye dhṛṣṭadyumnaṃ parantapaḥ||70||

71sa pūrvamatividdhaśca bhṛśaṃ paścācca pīḍitaḥ
|
sasādātha ca pāñcālyo vyapāśrayata ca dhvajam||71||

72taṃ nāgamiva siṃhena dṛṣṭvā rājañśarārditam
|
javenābhyadravañchūrāḥ pañca pāṇḍavato rathāḥ||72||

73kirīṭī bhīmasenaśca vṛddhakṣatraśca pauravaḥ
|
yuvarājaśca cedīnāṃ mālavaśca sudarśanaḥ||73||

74ete hāhākṛtāḥ sarve pragṛhītaśarāsanāḥ
|
vīraṃ drauṇāyaniṃ vīrāḥ sarvataḥ paryavārayan||74||

75te viṃśatipade yattā guruputramamarṣaṇam
|
pañcabhiḥ pañcabhirbāṇairabhyaghnansarvataḥ samam||75||

76āśīviṣābhairviṃśatyā pañcabhistu śitaiḥ śaraiḥ
|
ciccheda yugapaddrauṇiḥ pañcaviṃśatisāyakān||76||

77saptabhistu śitairbāṇaiḥ pauravaṃ drauṇirārdayat
|
mālavaṃ tribhirekana pārthaṃ ṣaḍbirvṛkodaram||77||

78tataste vivyadhuḥ sarve drauṇiṃ rājanmahārathāḥ
|
yugapacca pṛthakcaiva rukmapuṅkhaiḥ śilāśitaiḥ||78||

79yuvarājaśca viṃśatyā drauṇiṃ vivyādha patribhiḥ
|
pārthaśca punaraṣṭābhistathā sarve tribhistribhiḥ||79||

80tato'rjunaṃ ṣaḍbhirathājaghāna drauṇāyanirdaśabhirvāsudevam
|
bhīmaṃ daśārdhairyuvarājaṃ caturbhirdvābhyāṃ dvābhyāṃ mālavaṃ pauravaṃ ca||80||

81sūtaṃ viddhvā bhīmasenasya ṣaḍbhirdvābhyāṃ viddhvā kārmukaṃ ca dhvajaṃ ca
|
punaḥ pārthaṃ śaravarṣeṇa viddhvā drauṇirghoraṃ siṃhanādaṃ nanāda||81||

82tasyāsyatastānniśitānpītadhārān drauṇeḥ śarānpṛṣṭhataścāgrataśca
|
dharā viyaddyauḥ pradiśo diśaśca cchannā bāṇairabhavanghorarūpaiḥ||82||

83āsannasya svarathaṃ tīvratejāḥ sudarśanasyendraketuprakāśau
|
bhujau śiraścendrasamānavīryastribhiḥ śarairyugapatsaṃcakarta||83||

84sa pauravaṃ rathaśaktyā nihatya chittvā rathaṃ tilaśaścāsya bāṇaiḥ
|
chittvā ca bāhū varacandanāktau bhallena kāyācchira uccakarta||84||

85yuvānamindīvaradāmavarṇaṃ cediprabhuṃ yuvarājaṃ prasahya
|
bāṇaistvarāvānprajvalitāgnikalpairviddhvā prādānmṛtyave sāśvasūtam||85||

86mālavaṃ pauravaṃ caiva yuvarājaṃ ca cedipam
|
dṛṣṭvā samakṣaṃ nihataṃ droṇaputreṇa pāṇḍavaḥ||86||

87bhīmaseno mahābāhuḥ krodhamāhārayatparam
|
tataḥ śaraśataistīkṣṇaiḥ saṃkruddhāśīviṣopamaiḥ||87||

88chādayāmāsa samare droṇaputraṃ paraṃtapaḥ
|
tato drauṇirmahātejāḥ śaravarṣaṃ nihatya tam||88||

89vivyādha niśitairbāṇairbhīmasenamamarṣaṇaḥ
|
tato bhīmo mahābāhurdrauṇeryudhi mahābalaḥ||89||

90kṣurapreṇa dhanuśchittvā drauṇiṃ vivyādha patriṇā
|
ta‌dapāsya dhanuśchinnaṃ droṇaputro mahāmanāḥ||90||

91anyatkārmukamādāya bhīmaṃ vivyādha patribhiḥ
|
tau drauṇibhīmau samare parākrāntau mahābalau||91||

92avarṣatāṃ śaravarṣaṃ vṛṣṭimantāvivāmbudau
|
bhīmanāmāṅkitā bāṇāḥ svarṇapuṅkhāḥ śilāśitāḥ||92||

93drauṇiṃ saṃchādayāmāsurghanaughā iva bhāskaram
|
tathaiva drauṇinirmuktairbhīmaḥ sannataparvabhiḥ||93||

94avākīryata sa kṣipraṃ śaraiḥ śatasahasraśaḥ
|
sa cchādyamānaḥ samare drauṇinā raṇaśālinā||94||

95na vivyathe mahārāja tada‌dbhutamivābhavat
|
tato bhīmo mahābāhuḥ kārtasvaravibhūṣitān||95||

96nārācāndaśa saṃpraiṣīdyamadaṇḍanibhāñchitān
|
te jatrudeśamāsādya droṇaputrasya māriṣa||96||

97nirbhidya viviśustūrṇaṃ valmīkamiva pannagāḥ
|
so'tividdho bhṛśaṃ drauṇiḥ pāṃḍavena mahātmanā||97||

98dhvajayaṣṭiṃ samāsādya nyamīlayata locane
|
sa muhūrtātpunaḥ saṃjñāṃ labdhvā drauṇirnarādhipa||98||

99krodhaṃ paramamātasthau samare rudhirokṣitaḥ
|
dṛḍhaṃ so'bhihatastena pāṇḍavena mahātmanā||99||

100vegaṃ cakre mahābāhurbhīmasenarathaṃ prati
|
tata ākarṇapūrṇānāṃ śarāṇāṃ tigmatejasām||100||

101śatamāśīviṣābhānāṃ preṣayāmāsa bhārata
|
bhīmo'pi samaraślādhī tasya vīryamacintayat||101||

102tūrṇaṃ prāsṛjadugrāṇi śaravarṣāṇi pāṇḍavaḥ
|
tato drauṇirmahārāja chittvā'sya viśikhairdhanuḥ||102||

103ājaghānorasi kruddhaḥ pāṇḍavaṃ niśitaiḥ śaraiḥ
|
tato'nyaddhanurādāya bhīmaseno hyamarṣaṇaḥ||103||

104vivyādha niśitairbāṇairdrauṇiṃ pañcabhirāhave
|
jīmūtāviva gharmānte tau śaraughapravarṣiṇau||104||

105anyonyakrodhatāmrākṣau chādayāmāsaturyudhi
|
talaśabdaistato ghoraistrāsayantau parasparam||105||

106ayudhyetāṃ susaṃrabdhau kṛtapratikṛtaiṣiṇau
|
tato visphārya sumahaccāpaṃ rukmavibhūṣitam||106||

107bhīmaṃ praikṣata sa drauṇiḥ śarānasyantamantikāt
|
śaradyaharmadhyagato dīptārciriva bhāskaraḥ||107||

108ādadānasya viśikhānsaṃdadhānasya cāśugān
|
vikarṣato muñcataśca nāntaraṃ dadṛśurjanāḥ||108||

109alātacakrapratimaṃ tasya maṇḍalamāyudham
|
drauṇerāsīnmahārāja bāṇānvisṛjatastadā||109||

110dhanuścyutāḥ śarāstasya śataśo'tha sahasraśaḥ
|
ākāśe pratyadṛśyanta śalabhānāmivāyatīḥ||110||

111te tu drauṇivinirmuktāḥ śarā hemavibhūṣitāḥ
|
ajasramanvakīryanta ghorā bhīmarathaṃ prati||111||

112tatrādbhutamapaśyāma bhīmasenasya vikramam
|
balaṃ vīryaṃ prabhāvaṃ ca vyavasāyaṃ ca bhārata||112||

113tāṃ sa meghādivodbhūtāṃ bāṇavṛṣṭiṃ samantataḥ
|
jalavṛṣṭiṃ mahāghorāṃ tapānta iva cintayan||113||

114droṇaputravadhaprepsurbhīmo bhīmaparākramaḥ
|
amuñcaccharavarṣāṇi prāvṛṣīva balāhakaḥ||114||

115tadrukmapṛṣṭhaṃ bhīmasya dhanurghoraṃ mahāraṇe
|
vikṛṣyamāṇaṃ vibabhau śakracāpamivāparam||115||

116tasmāccharāḥ prādurāsañchataśo'tha sahasraśaḥ
|
saṃchādayantaḥ samare drauṇimāhavaśobhinam||116||

117tayorvisṛjatorevaṃ śarajālāni māriṣa
|
vāyurapyantarā rājannāśaknotpratisarpitum||117||

118tathā drauṇirmahārāja śarānhemavibhūṣitān
|
tailadhautānprasannāgrānprāhiṇodvadhakāṅkṣayā||118||

119tānantarikṣe viśikhaistridhaikaikamaśātayat
|
viśeṣayandroṇasutaṃ tiṣṭha tiṣṭheti cābravīt||119||

120punaśca śaravarṣāṇi ghorāṇyugrāṇi pāṇḍavaḥ
|
vyasṛjadbalavān kruddho droṇaputravadhepsayā||120||

121tato'stramāyayā tūrṇaṃ śaravṛṣṭiṃ nivārya tām
|
dhanuściccheda bhīmasya droṇaputro mahāstravit||121||

122śaraiścainaṃ subahubhiḥ kruddhaḥ saṅkhye parābhinat
|
sa chinnadhanvā balavān rathaśaktiṃ sudāruṇām||122||

123vegenāvidhya cikṣepa droṇaputrarathaṃ prati
|
tāmāpatantīṃ sahasā maholkābhāṃ śitaiḥ śaraiḥ||123||
