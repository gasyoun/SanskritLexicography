Mahābhārata 13.156 (Anuśāsanaparvan)

1bhīṣma uvāca|
ityuktastvarjunastūṣṇīmabhūdvāyustamabravīt
|
śṛṇu me haihayaśreṣṭha karmātreḥ sumahātmanaḥ ||1||

2ghore tamasyayudhyanta sahitā devadānavāḥ
|
avidhyata śaraistatra svarbhānuḥ somabhāskarau ||2||

3atha te tamasā grastā nihanyante sma dānavaiḥ
|
devā nṛpatiśārdūla sahaiva balibhistadā ||3||

4asurairvadhyamānāste kṣīṇaprāṇā divaukasaḥ
|
apaśyanta tapasyantamātriṃ vipraṃ tapodhanam ||4||

5athainamabruvandevāḥ śāntakrodhaṃ jitendriyam
|
asurairiṣubhirviddhau candrādityāvimāvubhau ||5||

6vayaṃ vadhyāmahe cāpi śatrubhistamasāvṛte
|
nādhigacchāmi śāntiṃ ca bhayāttrāyasva naḥ prabho ||6||

7atriruvāca|
kathaṃ rakṣāmi bhavataste'bruvaṃścandramā bhava
|
timiraghnaśca savitā dasyuhantā ca no bhava ||7||

8evamuktastadā'trirvai tamonudabhavacchaśī
|
apaśyatsaumyabhāvācca somavatpriyadarśanaḥ ||8||

9dṛṣṭvā nātiprabhaṃ somaṃ tathā sūryaṃ ca pārthiva
|
prakāśamakarodatristapasā svena saṃyuge ||9||

10jagadvitimiraṃ cāpi pradīptamakarottadā ||10||

11vyajayacchatrusaṃghāṃśca devānāṃ svena tejasā
|
atriṇā dahyamānāṃstāndṛṣṭvā devā mahāsurān ||11||

12parākramaiste'pi tadā vyaghnannatrisurakṣitāḥ
|
udbhāsitaśca savitā devāstrātā hatāsurāḥ ||12||

13atriṇā tvatha sāmarthyaṃ kṛtamuttamatejasā
|
dvijenāgnidvitīyena japatā carmavāsasā ||13||

14phalabhakṣeṇa rājarṣe paśya karmātriṇā kṛtam
|
tasyāpi vistareṇoktaṃ karmātreḥ sumahātmanaḥ
|
bravīmyahaṃ brūhi vā tvamatritaḥ kṣatriyaṃ varam ||14||

15ityuktastvarjunastūṣṇīmabhūdvāyustato'bravīt
|
śṛṇu rājan mahatkarma cyavanasya mahātmanaḥ ||15||

16aśvinoḥ pratisaṃśrutya cyavanaḥ pākaśāsanam
|
provāca sahito devaiḥ somapāvaśvinau kuru ||16||

17indra uvāca|
asmābhirninditāvetau bhavetāṃ somapau katham
|
devairna saṃmitāvetau tasmānmaivaṃ vadasva naḥ ||17||

18aśvibhyāṃ saha necchāmaḥ somaṃ pātuṃ mahāvrata
|
yadanyadvakṣyase vipra tatkariṣyāma te vacaḥ ||18||

19cyavana uvāca|
pibetāmaśvinau somaṃ bhavadbhiḥ sahitāvimau
|
ubhāvetāvapi surau sūryaputrau sureśvara ||19||

20kriyatāṃ madvaco devā yathā vai samudāhṛtam
|
etadvaḥ kurvatāṃ śreyo bhavennaitadakurvatām ||20||

21indra uvāca|
aśvibhyāṃ saha somaṃ vai na pāsyāmi dvijottama
|
pibantvanye yathākāmaṃ nāhaṃ pātumihotsahe ||21||

22cyavana uvāca|
na cetkariṣyasi vaco mayoktaṃ balasūdana
|
mayā pramathitaḥ sadyaḥ somaṃ pāsyasi vai makhe ||22||

23vāyuruvāca|
tataḥ karma samārabdhaṃ hitāya sahasāśvinoḥ
|
cyavanena tato mantrairabhibhūtāḥ surā'bhavan ||23||

24tattu karma samārabdhaṃ dṛṣṭvendraḥ krodhamūrcchitaḥ
|
udyamya vipulaṃ śailaṃ cyavanaṃ samupādravat ||24||

25tathā vajreṇa bhagavānamarṣākulalocanaḥ
|
tamāpatantaṃ dṛṣṭvaiva cyavanastapasānvitaḥ ||25||

26adbhiḥ siktvā'staṃbhayattaṃ savajraṃ sahaparvatam
|
athendrasya mahāghoraṃ so'sṛjacchatrumeva hi ||26||

27madaṃ nāmāhutimayaṃ vyāditāsyaṃ mahāmuniḥ
|
tasya dantasahasraṃ tu babhūva śatayojanam ||27||

28dviyojanaśatāstasya daṃṣṭrāḥ paramadāruṇāḥ
|
hanustasyābhavadbhūmāvāsyaṃ cāsyāspṛśaddivam ||28||

29jihvāmūle sthitāstasya sarve devāḥ savāsavāḥ
|
timerāsyamanuprāptā yathā matsyā mahārṇave ||29||

30te saṃmantrya tato devā madasyāsya samīpagāḥ
|
abruvansahitāḥ śakraṃ praṇamāsmai dvijātaye ||30||

31aśvibhyāṃ saha somaṃ ca pibāma vigatajvarāḥ
|
tataḥ sa praṇataḥ śakraścakāra cyavanasya tat ||31||

32cyavanaḥ kṛtavānetāvaśvinau somapāyinau
|
tataḥ pratyāharatkarma madaṃ ca vyabhajanmuniḥ ||32||

33akṣeṣu mṛgayāyāṃ ca pāne strīṣu ca vīryavān
|
etairdoṣairnarā rājan kṣayaṃ yānti na saṃśayaḥ
|
tasmādetānnaro nityaṃ dūrataḥ parivarjayet ||33||

34etatte cyavanasyāpi karma rājanprakīrtitam
|
bravīmyahaṃ brūhi vā tvaṃ kṣatriyaṃ brāhmaṇādvaram ||34||
