Mahābhārata 5.111 (Udyogaparvan)

1suparṇa uvāca|
yasmāduttāryate pāpādyasmānniḥśreyaso'śnute
|
asmāduttāraṇabalāduttaretyucyate dvija ||1||

2uttarasya hiraṇyasya parivāpaśca gālava
|
mārgaḥ paścimapūrvābhyāṃ digbhyāṃ vai madhyamaḥ smṛtaḥ ||2||

3asyāṃ diśi variṣṭhāyāmuttarāyāṃ dvijarṣabha
|
nāsaumyo nāvidheyātmā nādharmo vasate janaḥ ||3||

4atra nārāyaṇaḥ kṛṣṇo jiṣṇuścaiva narottamaḥ
|
badaryāmāśramapade tathā brahmā ca śāśvataḥ ||4||

5atra vai himavatpṛṣṭhe nityamāste maheśvaraḥ
|
prakṛtyā puruṣaḥ sārdhaṃ yugāntāgnisamaprabhaḥ ||5||

6na sa dṛśyo munigaṇaistathā devaiḥ savāsavaiḥ
|
gandharvayakṣasiddhairvā naranārāyaṇādṛte ||6||

7atra viṣṇuḥ sahasrākṣaḥ sahasracaraṇo'vyayaḥ
|
sahasraśirasaḥ śrīmānekaḥ paśyati māyayā ||7||

8atra rājyena viprāṇāṃ candramāścābhyaṣicyata
|
atra gaṅgāṃ mahādevaḥ patantīṃ gaganāccyutām ||8||

9pratigṛhya dadau loke mānuṣe brahmavittama
|
atra devyāṃ tapastaptaṃ maheśvaraparīpsayā ||9||

10atra kāmaśca roṣaśca śailaścomā ca sambabhuḥ
|
atra rākṣasayakṣāṇāṃ gandharvāṇāṃ ca gālava ||10||

11ādhipatyena kailāse dhanado'pyabhiṣecitaḥ
|
atra caitrarathaṃ ramyamatra vaikhānasāśramaḥ ||11||

12atra mandākinī caiva mandaraśca dvijarṣabha
|
atra saugandhikavanaṃ nairṛtairabhirakṣyate ||12||

13śādvalaṃ kadalīskandhamatra santānakā nagāḥ
|
atra saṃyamanityānāṃ siddhānāṃ svairacāriṇām ||13||

14vimānānyanurūpāṇi kāmabhogyāni gālava
|
atra te ṛṣayaḥ sapta devī cārundhatī tathā ||14||

15atra tiṣṭhati vai svātiratrāsyā udayaḥ smṛtaḥ
|
atra yajñaṃ samāsādya dhruvaṃ sthātā pitāmahaḥ ||15||

16jyotīṃṣi candrasūryau ca parivartanti nityaśaḥ
|
atra gaṅgāṃ mahādvāraṃ rakṣanti dvijasattama ||16||

17dhāmā nāma mahātmāno munayaḥ satyavādinaḥ
|
na teṣāṃ jñāyate mūrtirnākṛtirna tapaścitam ||17||

18parivartaḥ sahasrāṇi kāmabhojyāni gālava
|
yathā yathā praviśati tasmāt parataraṃ naraḥ ||18||

19tathā tathā dvijaśreṣṭha pravilīyati gālava
|
naitat kenacidanyena gatapūrvaṃ dvijarṣabha ||19||

20ṛte nārāyaṇaṃ devaṃ naraṃ vā jiṣṇumavyayam
|
atra kailāsamityuktaṃ sthānamailavilasya tat ||20||

21atra vidyutprabhā nāma jajñire'psaraso daśa
|
atra viṣṇupadaṃ nāma kramatā viṣṇunā kṛtam ||21||

22trilokavikrame brahmannuttarāṃ diśamāśritam
|
atra rājñā maruttena yajñeneṣṭaṃ dvijottama ||22||

23uśīrabīje viprarṣe yatra jāmbūnadaṃ saraḥ
|
jīmūtasyātra viprarṣerupatasthe mahātmanaḥ ||23||

24sākṣāddhaimavataḥ puṇyo vimalaḥ kanakākaraḥ
|
brāhmaṇeṣu ca yatkṛtsnaṃ svantaṃ kṛtvā dhanaṃ mahat ||24||

25vavre dhanaṃ maharṣiḥ sa jaimūtaṃ taddhanaṃ tataḥ
|
atra nityaṃ diśāṃpālāḥ sāyaṃ prātardvijarṣabha ||25||

26kasya kāryaṃ kimiti vai parikrośanti gālava
|
evameṣā dvijaśreṣṭha guṇairanyairdiguttarā ||26||

27uttareti parikhyātā sarvakarmasu cottarā
|
etā vistaraśastāta tava saṅkīrtitā diśaḥ ||27||

28catasraḥ kramayogena kāmāśāṃ gantumicchasi
|
udyato'haṃ dvijaśreṣṭha tava darśayituṃ diśaḥ
|
pṛthivīṃ cākhilāṃ brahmaṃstasmādāroha māṃ dvija ||28||
