Mahābhārata 12.73 (Śāntiparvan)

1bhīṣma uvāca|
rājñā purohitaḥ kāryo bhavedvidvānbahuśrutaḥ
|
ubhau samīkṣya dharmārthāvaprameyāvanantaram||1||

2dharmātmā mantravidyeṣāṃ rājñāṃ rājanpurohitaḥ
|
rājā caivaṃguṇo yeṣāṃ kuśalaṃ teṣu sarvaśaḥ||2||

3ubhau prajā vardhayato devānsarvānsutānpitṝn
|
bhaveyātāṃ sthitau dharme śraddheyau sutapasvinau||3||

4parasparasya suhṛdau vihitau samacetasau
|
brahmakṣatrasya sammānātprajā sukhamavāpnuyāt||4||

5vimānanāttayoreva prajā naśyeyureva hi
|
brahmakṣatraṃ hi sarveṣāṃ varṇānāṃ mūlamucyate||5||

6atrāpyudāharantīmamitihāsaṃ purātanam
|
ailakaśyapasaṃvādaṃ tannibodha yudhiṣṭhira||6||

7aila uvāca| yadā hi brahma prajahāti kṣatraṃ kṣatraṃ yadā vā prajahāti brahma
|
anvagbalaṃ katame'sminbhajante tathā varṇāḥ katame'smindhriyante||7||

8kaśyapa uvāca| viddhaṃ rāṣṭraṃ kṣatriyasya bhavati brahmakṣatraṃ yatra viruddhyatīha
|
anvagbalaṃ dasyavastadbhajante tathā varṇaṃ tatra vidanti santaḥ||8||

9naiṣāṃ brahma ca vardhate nota putrā na gargaro mathyate no jayante
|
naiṣāṃ putrā vedamadhīyate ca yadā brahma kṣatriyāḥ saṃtyajanti||9||

10naiṣāmartho vardhate jātu gehe nādhīyate suprajā no yajante
|
apadhvastā dasyubhūtā bhavanti ye brāhmaṇānkṣatriyāḥ saṃtyajanti||10||

11etau hi nityaṃ saṃyuktāvitaretaradhāraṇe
|
kṣatraṃ vai brahmaṇo yoniryoniḥ kṣatrasya vai dvijāḥ||11||

12ubhāvetau nityamabhiprapannau samprāpaturmahatīṃ sampratiṣṭhām
|
tayoḥ saṃdhirbhidyate cetpurāṇastataḥ sarvaṃ bhavati hi sampramūḍham||12||

13nātra pāraṃ labhate pāragāmī mahāgādhaṃ gauriva samprapannā
|
cāturvarṇyaṃ bhavati hi sampramūḍhaṃ prajāstataḥ kṣayasaṃsthā bhavanti||13||

14brahmavṛkṣo rakṣyamāṇo madhu hema ca varṣati
|
arakṣyamāṇaḥ satatamaśru pāpaṃ ca varṣati||14||

15na brahmacārī caraṇādayeto yadā brahma brahmaṇi trāṇamicchet
|
āścaryato varṣati tatra devastatrābhīkṣṇaṃ duḥsahāścāviśanti||15||

16striyaṃ hatvā brāhmaṇaṃ vā'pi pāpaḥ sabhāyāṃ yatra labhate'nuvādam
|
rājñaḥ sakāśe na bibheti cāpi tato bhayaṃ vidyate kṣatriyasyā||16||

17pāpaiḥ pāpe kriyamāṇe hi caila tato rudro jāyate deva eṣaḥ
|
pāpaiḥ pāpāḥ saṃjanayanti rudraṃ tataḥ sarvānsādhvasādhūnhinasti||17||

18aila evāca
|
kuto rudraḥ kīdṛśo vā'pi rudraḥ sattvaiḥ sattvaṃ dṛśyate vadhyamānam
|
etat sarvaṃ kaśyapa me pracakṣva kuto rudro jāyate deva eṣaḥ||18||

19kaśyapa uvāca| ātmā rudro hṛdayo mānavānāṃ svaṃ svaṃ dehaṃ paradehaṃ na hanti
|
vātotpātaiḥ sadṛśaṃ rudramāhurdevairjīmūtaiḥ sadṛśaṃ rūpamasya||19||

20aila uvāca| na vai vātaḥ parivṛṇoti kaścinna jīmūto varṣati nāpi devaḥ
|
tathāyukto dṛśyate mānuṣeṣu kāmadveṣādbadhyate muhyate ca||20||

21kaśyapa uvāca| yathaikagehe jātavedāḥ pradīptaḥ kṛtsnaṃ grāmaṃ dahate catvaraṃ vā
|
vimohanaṃ kurute deva eṣa tataḥ sarvaṃ spṛśyate puṇyapāpaiḥ||21||

22aila uvāca| yadi daṇḍaḥ spṛśate'puṇyapāpaṃ pāpaṃ pāpe kriyamāṇe viśeṣāt
|
kasya hetoḥ sukṛtaṃ nāma kuryādduṣkṛtaṃ vā kasya hetorna kuryāt||22||

23kaśyapa uvāca| asaṃtyāgātpāpakṛtāmapāpāṃstulyo daṇḍaḥ spṛśate miśrabhāvāt
|
śuṣkeṇārdraṃ dahyate miśrabhāvānna miśraḥ syātpāpakṛdbhiḥ kathaṃcit||23||

24aila uvāca| sādhvasādhūndhārayatīha bhūmiḥ sādhvasādhūṃstāpayatīha sūryaḥ
|
sādhvasādhūṃścāpi vātīha vāyurāpastathā sādhvasādhūnpunanti||24||

25kaśyapa uvāca| evamasminvartate loka eva nāmutraṃ vartate rājaputra
|
pretyaitayorantarāvānviśeṣo yo vai puṇyaṃ carate yaśca pāpam||25||

26puṇyasya loko madhumānghṛtārcirhiraṇyajyotiramṛtasya nābhiḥ
|
tatra pretya modate brahmacārī na tatra mṛtyurna jarā nota duḥkham||26||

27pāpasya loko nirayo'prakāśo nityaṃ duḥkhaṃ śokabhūyiṣṭhameva
|
tatrātmānaṃ śocati pāpakarmā bahvīḥ samāḥ pratapannapatiṣṭhaḥ||27||

28mitho bhedādbrāhmaṇakṣatriyāṇāṃ prajā duḥkhaṃ duḥsahaṃ cāviśanti
|
evaṃ jñātvā kārya eveha nityaṃ purohito naikavidyo nṛpeṇā||28||

29taṃ caivānvabhiṣiñceta tathā dharmo vidhīyate
|
agraṃ hi brāhmaṇaḥ proktaṃ sarvasyaiveha dharmataḥ||29||

30pūrvaṃ hi brāhmaṇaḥ sṛṣṭiriti brahmavido viduḥ
|
jyeṣṭhenābhijanenāsya prāptaṃ pūrvaṃ yaduttaram||30||

31tasmānmānyaśca pūjyaśca brāhmaṇaḥ prasṛtāgrabhuk
|
sarvaṃ śreṣṭhaṃ viśiṣṭaṃ ca nivedyaṃ tasya dharmataḥ||31||

32avaśyameva kartavyaṃ rājñā balavatā'pi hi
|
brahma vardhayati kṣatraṃ kṣatrato brahma vardhate
|
evaṃ rājñā viśeṣeṇa pūjyā vai brāhmaṇāḥ sadā||32||
