Mahābhārata 1.182 (Ādiparvan)

1arjuna uvāca|
rājñā kalmāṣapādena gurau brahmavidāṃ vare
|
kāraṇaṃ kiṃ puraskṛtya bhāryā vai sanniyojitā||1||

2jānatā vai paraṃ dharmaṃ vasiṣṭhena mahātmanā
|
agamyāgamanaṃ kasmātkṛtaṃ tena maharṣiṇā||2||

3adharmiṣṭhaṃ vasiṣṭhena kṛtaṃ cāpi purā sakhe
|
etanme saṃśayaṃ sarvaṃ chettumarhasi pṛcchataḥ||3||

4gandharva uvāca|
dhanañjaya nibodheyaṃ yanmāṃ tvaṃ paripṛcchasi
|
vasiṣṭhaṃ prati durdharṣa tathā mitrasahaṃ nṛpam||4||

5kathitaṃ te mayā sarvaṃ yathā śaptaḥ sa pārthivaḥ
|
śaktinā bharataśreṣṭha vāsiṣṭhena mahātmanā||5||

6sa tu śāpavaśaṃ prāptaḥ krodhaparyākulekṣaṇaḥ
|
nirjagāma purādrājā sahadāraḥ parantapaḥ||6||

7araṇyaṃ nirjanaṃ gatvā sadāraḥ paricakrame
|
nānāmṛgagaṇākīrṇaṃ nānāsatvasamākulam||7||

8nānāgulmalatācchannaṃ nānādrumasamāvṛtam
|
araṇyaṃ ghorasannādaṃ śāpagrastaḥ paribhraman||8||

9sa kadācitkṣudhāviṣṭo mṛgayanbhakṣyamātmanaḥ
|
dadarśa suparikliṣṭaḥ kasmiṃścinnirjane vane||9||

10brāhmaṇaṃ brāhmaṇīṃ caiva mithunāyopasaṃgatau
|
tau taṃ vīkṣya suvitrastāvakṛtārthau pradhāvitau||10||

11tayoḥ pradravatorvipraṃ jagrāha nṛpatirbalāt
|
dṛṣṭvā gṛhītaṃ bhartāramatha brāhmaṇyabhāṣata||11||

12śṛṇu rājanmama vaco yattvāṃ vakṣyāmi suvrata
|
ādityavaṃśaprabhavastvaṃ hi loke pariśrutaḥ||12||

13apramattaḥ sthito dharme guruśuśrūṣaṇe rataḥ
|
śāpopahatadurdharṣa na pāpaṃ kartumarhasi||13||

14ṛtukāle tu saṃprāpte bhartṛvyasanakarśitā
|
akṛtārthā hyahaṃ bhartrā prasavārthaṃ samāgatā||14||

15prasīda nṛpatiśreṣṭha bhartā'yaṃ me visṛjyatām
|
evaṃ vikrośamānāyāstasyāstu na nṛśaṃsavat||15||

16bhartāraṃ bhakṣayāmāsa vyāghro mṛgamivepsitam
|
tasyāḥ krodhābhibhūtāyā yānyaśrūṇyapatanbhuvi||16||

17so'gniḥ samabhavaddīptastaṃ ca deśaṃ vyadīpayat
|
tataḥ sā śokasaṃtaptā bhartṛvyasanakarśitā||17||

18kalmāṣapādaṃ rājarṣimaśapadbrāhmaṇī ruṣā
|
yasmānmamākṛtārthāyāstvayā kṣudra nṛśaṃsavat||18||

19prekṣantyā bhakṣito me'dya priyo bhartā mahāyaśāḥ
|
tasmāttvamapi durbuddhe macchāpaparivikṣataḥ||19||

20patnīmṛtāvanuprāpya sadyastyakṣyasi jīvitam
|
yasya carṣervasiṣṭhasya tvayā putrā vināśitāḥ||20||

21tena saṃgamyate bhāryā tanayaṃ janayiṣyati
|
sate vaṃśakaraḥ putro bhaviṣyati nṛpādhama||21||

22evaṃ śaptvā tu rājānaṃ sā tamāṅgirasī śubhā
|
tasyaiva sannidhau dīptaṃ praviveśa hutāśanam||22||

23vasiṣṭhaśca mahābhāgaḥ sarvametadavaikṣata
|
jñānayogena mahatā tapasā ca parantapa||23||

24muktaśāpaśca rājarṣiḥ kālena mahatā tataḥ
|
ṛtukāle'bhipatito madayantyā nivāritaḥ||24||

25na hi sasmāra sa nṛpastaṃ śāpaṃ kāmamohitaḥ
|
devyāḥ so'tha vacaḥ śrutvā saṃbhrānto nṛpasattamaḥ||25||

26taṃ śāpamanusaṃsmṛtya paryatapyadbhṛśaṃ tadā
|
etasmātkāraṇādrājā vasiṣṭhaṃ saṃnyayojayat
|
svadāreṣu naraśreṣṭha śāpadoṣasamanvitaḥ||26||
