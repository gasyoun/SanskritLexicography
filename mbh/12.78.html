Mahābhārata 12.78 (Śāntiparvan)

1yudhiṣṭhira uvāca|
vyākhyātā rājadharmeṇa vṛttirāpatsu bhārata
|
kathaṃ svidvaiśyadharmeṇa saṃjīvedbrāhmaṇo na vā||1||

2bhīṣma uvāca|
aśaktaḥ kṣatradharmeṇa vaiśyadharmeṇa vartayet
|
kṛṣigorakṣyamāsthāya vyasane vṛttisaṃkṣaye||2||

3yudhiṣṭhira uvāca|
kāni paṇyāni vikrīya svargalokānna hīyate
|
brāhmaṇo vaiśyadharmeṇa vartayanbharatarṣabha||3||

4bhīṣma uvāca|
surā lavaṇamityeva tilānkesariṇaḥ paśūn
|
vṛṣabhānmadhumāṃsaṃ ca kṛtānnaṃ ca yudhiṣṭhira||4||

5sarvāsvavasthāsvetāni brāhmaṇaḥ parivarjayet
|
eteṣāṃ vikrayāttāta brāhmaṇo narakaṃ vrajet||5||

6ajo'gnirvaruṇo meṣaḥsūryo'śvaḥ pṛthivī virāṭ
|
dhenuryajñaśca somaśca na vikreyāḥ kathaṃcana||6||

7pakvenāmasya nimayaṃ na praśaṃsanti sādhavaḥ
|
nimayetpakvamāmena bhojanārthāya bhārata||7||

8vayaṃ siddhamaśiṣyāmo bhavānsādhayatāmidam
|
evaṃ saṃvīkṣya niyamennādharmo'sti kathaṃcana||8||

9atra te vartayiṣyāmi purādharmaḥ sanātanaḥ
|
vyavahārapravṛttānāṃ tannibodha yudhiṣṭhira||9||

10bhavate'haṃ dadānīdaṃ bhavānetatprayacchatu
|
rucito vartate dharmo na balātsampravartate||10||

11ityevaṃ sampravartante vyavahārāḥ purātanāḥ
|
ṛṣīṇāmitareṣāṃ ca sādhu caitadasaṃśayam||11||

12yudhiṣṭhira uvāca|
atha tāta yadā sarvāḥ śastramādadate prajāḥ
|
vyutkrāmanti svadharmebhyaḥ kṣatrasya kṣīyate balam||12||

13rājā trātā tu lokasya kathaṃ ca syātparāyaṇam
|
etanme saṃśayaṃ brūhi vistareṇa narādhipa||13||

14bhīṣma uvāca|
dānena tapasā yajñairadroheṇa damena ca
|
brāhmaṇapramukhā varṇāḥ kṣemamiccheyurātmanaḥ||14||

15teṣāṃ ye vedabalinaste'bhyutthāya samantataḥ
|
rājño balaṃ vardhayeyurmahendrasyeva devatāḥ||15||

16rājño'pi kṣīyamāṇasya brahmaivāhuḥ parāyaṇam
|
tasmādbrahmabalenaiva samuttheyaṃ vijānatā||16||

17yadā bhuvi jayī rājā kṣemaṃ rāṣṭre'bhisaṃdadhet
|
tadā varṇā yathādharmaṃ niviśeyuḥ kathaṃcana||17||

18unmaryāde pravṛtte tu dasyubhiḥ saṃkare kṛte
|
sarve varṇā na duṣyeyuḥ śastravanto yudhiṣṭhira||18||

19yudhiṣṭhira uvāca|
atha cetsarvataḥ kṣatraṃ praduṣyedbrāhmaṇaṃ prati
|
kastasya brāhmaṇastrātā ko dharmaḥ kiṃ parāyaṇam||19||

20bhīṣma uvāca|
tapasā brahmacaryeṇa śastreṇa ca balena ca
|
amāyayā māyayā ca niyantavyaṃ tadā bhavet||20||

21kṣatriyasyātivṛttasya brāhmaṇeṣu viśeṣataḥ
|
brahmaiva saṃniyantṛ syāt kṣatraṃ hi brahmasambhavam||21||

22adbhyo'gnirbrahmataḥ kṣatramaśmano lohamutthitam
|
teṣāṃ sarvatragaṃ tejaḥ svāsu yoniṣu śāmyati||22||

23yadā chinattyayo'śmānagniścāpo'bhigacchati
|
kṣatraṃ ca brāhmaṇaṃ dveṣṭi tadā naśyanti te trayaḥ||23||

24tasmādbrahmaṇi śāmyanti kṣatriyāṇāṃ yudhiṣṭhira
|
samudīrṇānyajeyāni tejāṃsi ca balāni ca||24||

25brahmavīrye mṛdubhūte kṣatravīrye ca durbale
|
duṣṭeṣu sarvavarṇeṣu brāhmaṇānprati sarvaśaḥ||25||

26ye tatra yuddhaṃ kurvanti tyaktvā jīvitamātmanaḥ
|
brāhmaṇānparirakṣanto dharmāmātmānameva ca||26||

27manasvino manyumantaḥ puṇyaślokā bhavanti te
|
brāhmaṇārthaṃ hi sarveṣāṃ śastragrahaṇamiṣyate||27||

28atisviṣṭamadhītānāṃ lokānatitapasvinām
|
anāśanāgnyorviśatāṃ śūrā yānti parāṃ gatim||28||

29brāhmaṇastriṣu varṇeṣu śastraṃ gṛhṇanna duṣyati
|
evamevātmanastyāgannānyaṃ dharmaṃ vidurjanāḥ||29||

30tebhyo namaśca bhadraṃ ca ye śarīrāṇi juhvate
|
brahmadviṣo niyacchantasteṣāṃ no'stu salokatā
|
brahmalokajitaḥ svargyānvīrāṃstānmanurabravīt||30||

31yathāśvamedhāvabhṛthe snātāḥ pūtā bhavantyuta
|
duṣkṛtasya praṇāśena tataḥ śastrahatā raṇe||31||

32bhavatyadharmo dharmo hi dharmādharmāvubhāvapi
|
kāraṇāddeśakālasya deśakālaḥ sa tādṛśaḥ||32||

33maitrāḥ krūrāṇi kurvanto jayanti svargamuttamam
|
dharmyāḥ pāpāni kurvāṇā gacchanti paramāṃ gatim||33||

34brāhmaṇastriṣu kāleṣu śastraṃ gṛhṇanna duṣyati
|
ātmatrāṇe varṇadoṣe durdamyaniyameṣu ca||34||

35yudhiṣṭhira uvāca|
abhyutthite dasyubale kṣatrārthe varṇasaṃkare
|
sampramūḍheṣu kṣatreṣu yadyanyo'bhibhavedbalī||35||

36brāhmaṇo yadi vā vaiśyaḥ śūdro vā rājasattama
|
dasyubhyo'tha prajā rakṣeddaṇḍaṃ dharmeṇa dhārayan||36||

37kāryaṃ kuryānna vā kuryātsa vāryo vā bhavenna vā
|
tasmācchastraṃ grahītavyamanyatra kṣatrabandhutaḥ||37||

38bhīṣma uvāca|
apāre yo bhavetpāramaplave yaḥ plavo bhavet
|
śūdro vā yadi vā'pyanyaḥ sarvathā mānamarhati||38||

39yamāśritya narā rājanvartayeyuryathāsukham
|
anāthāḥ parikālyanto dasyubhiḥ paripīḍitāḥ||39||

40tameva pūjayeyuste prītyā svamiva bāndhavam
|
abhīrabhīkṣṇaṃ kauravya kartā sanmānamarhati||40||

41kiṃ tairye'naḍuho nohyāḥ kiṃ dhenvā vā'pyadugdhayā
|
vandhyayā bhāryayā ko'rthaḥ ko'rtho rājñā'pyarakṣatā||41||

42yathā dārumayo hastī yathā carmamayo mṛgaḥ
|
yathā hyanarthaḥ ṣaṇḍho vā pārtha kṣetraṃ yathoṣaram||42||

43evaṃ vipro'nadhīyāno rājā yaśca na rakṣitā
|
megho na varṣate yaśca sarvathā te nirarthakāḥ||43||

44nityaṃ yastu sato rakṣedasataśca nivartayet
|
sa eva rājā kartavyastena sarvamidaṃ dhṛtam||44||
