Mahābhārata 14.61 (Āśvamedhikaparvan)

1vaiśampāyana uvāca|
kathayanneva tu sadā vāsudevaḥ pratāpavān
|
mahābhāratayuddhaṃ tatkathānte pituragrataḥ||1||

2abhimanyorvadhaṃ vīraḥ so'tyakrāmanmahāmatiḥ
|
apriyaṃ vasudevasya mā bhūditi mahāmatiḥ||2||

3mā dauhitravadhaṃ śrutvā vasudevo mahātyayam||3||

4duḥkhaśokābhisantapto bhavediti mahāmatiḥ
|
subhadrā tu tamutkrāntamātmajasya vadhaṃ raṇe||4||

5ācakṣva kṛṣṇa saubhadravamityapatadbhuvi
|
tāmapaśyannipatitāṃ vasudevaḥ kṣitau tadā||5||

6dṛṣṭvaiva ca papātorvyāṃ so'pi duḥkhena mūrchitaḥ
|
tataḥ sa dauhitravadhaduḥkhaśokasamāhataḥ||6||

7vasudevo mahārāja kṛṣṇaṃ vākyamathābravīt
|
nanu tvaṃ puṇḍarīkākṣa satyavāgbhuvi viśrutaḥ||7||

8yaddauhitravadhaṃ me'dya na khyāpayasi śatruhan
|
tadbhāgineyanidhanaṃ tattvenācakṣva me prabho||8||

9sadṛśākṣastava kathaṃ śatrubhirnihato raṇe
|
durmaraṃ bata vārṣṇeya kāle'prāpte nṛbhiḥ saha||9||

10yatra me hṛdayaṃ duḥkhācchatadhā na vidīryate
|
kimabravīttvāṃ saṅgrāme subhadrāṃ mātaraṃ prati||10||

11māṃ cāpi puṇḍarīkākṣa capalākṣaḥ pribo mama
|
āhavaṃ pṛṣṭhataḥ kṛtvā kaccinna nihataḥ paraiḥ||11||

12kaccinmukhaṃ na govinda tenājau vikṛtaṃ kṛtam
|
sa hi kṛṣṇa mahātejāḥ ślāghanniva mamāgrataḥ||12||

13bālabhāvena vinayamātmano'kathayat prabhuḥ
|
kaccinna nikṛto bālo droṇakarṇakṛpādibhiḥ||13||

14dharaṇyāṃ nihataḥ śete tanmamācakṣva keśava
|
sa hi droṇaṃ ca bhīṣmaṃ ca karṇaṃ ca balināṃ varam||14||

15spardhate sma raṇe nityaṃ duhituḥ putrako mama
|
evaṃvidhaṃ bahu tadā vilapantaṃ suduḥkhitam||15||

16pitaraṃ duḥkhitataro govindo vākyamabravīt
|
na tena vikṛtaṃ vaktraṃ kṛtaṃ saṅgrāmamūrdhani||16||

17na pṛṣṭhata: kṛtaścāpi saṅgrāmastena dustaraḥ
|
nihatya pṛthivīpālānsahasraśatasaṅghaśaḥ||17||

18khedito droṇakarṇābhyāṃ dauḥśāsanivaśaṃ gataḥ
|
eko hyekena satataṃ yudhyamāne yadi prabho||18||

19na sa śakyeta saṅgrāme nihantumapi vajriṇā
|
samāhṛte ca saṅgrāmātpārthe saṃśaptakaistadā||19||

20paryavāryata saṅkruddhaiḥ sa droṇādibhirāhave
|
tataḥ śatruvadhaṃ kṛtvā sumahāntaṃ raṇe pitaḥ||20||

21dauhitrastava vārṣṇeya dauḥśāsanivaśaṃ gataḥ
|
nūnaṃ ca sa gataḥ svargaṃ jahi śokaṃ mahāmate||21||

22na hi vyasanamāsādya sīdanti kṛtabuddhayaḥ
|
droṇakarṇaprabhṛtayo yena pratisamāsitāḥ||22||

23raṇe mahendrapratimāḥ sa kathaṃ nāpnuyāddivam
|
sa śokaṃ jahi durdharṣa mā ca manyuvaśaṃ gamaḥ||23||

24śastrapūtāṃ hi sa gatiṃ gataḥ parapurañjayaḥ
|
tasmiṃstu nihate vīre subhadreyaṃ svasā mama||24||

25duḥkhārtādho sutaṃ prāpya kurarīva nanāda ha
|
draupadīṃ ca samāsādya paryapṛcchata duḥkhitā||25||

26ārye kva dārakāḥ sarve draṣṭumicchāmi tānaham
|
asyāstu vacanaṃ śrutvā sarvāstāḥ kuruyoṣitaḥ||26||

27bhujābhyāṃ parigṛhyaināṃ cukruśuḥ paramārtavat||27||

28uttarāṃ cābravīdbhadre bhartā sa kva nu te gataḥ
|
kṣipramāgamanaṃ mahyaṃ tasya tvaṃ vedayasva ha||28||

29nanu nāmādya vairāṭi śrutvā mama giraṃ sadā
|
bhavanānniṣpatatyāśu kasmānnābhyeti te patiḥ||29||

30abhimanyo kuśalino mātulāste mahārathāḥ
|
kuśalaṃ cābruvansarve tvāṃ yuyutsumihāgatam||30||

31ācakṣva me'dya saṅgrāmaṃ yathāpūrvamarindama
|
kasmādevaṃ vilapatīṃ nāgheha pratibhāṣase||31||

32evamādi tu vārṣṇeyyāstasyāstatparidevitam
|
śrutvā pṛthā suduḥkhārtā śanairvākyamathābravīt||32||

33subhadre vāsudevena tathā sātyakinā raṇe
|
pitrā ca lālito bālaḥ sa hataḥ kāladharmaṇā||33||

34īdṛśo martyadharmo'yaṃ mā śuco yadunandini
|
putro hi tava durgharṣaḥ samprāptaḥ paramāṃ gatim||34||

35kule mahati jātāsi kṣatriyāṇāṃ mahātmanām
|
mā śucaścapalākṣaṃ tvaṃ padmapatranibhekṣaṇe||35||

36uttarāṃ tvamavekṣasva gurviṇī mā śucaḥ śubhe
|
putrameṣā hi tasyāśu janayiṣyati bhāvinī||36||

37evamāśvāsayitvaināṃ kuntī yadukulodvaha
|
vihāya śokaṃ durdharṣe śrāddhamasya hyakalpayat||37||

38samanujñāpya dharmajñaṃ rājānaṃ bhīmameva ca
|
yamau yamopamau caiva dadau dānānyanekaśaḥ||38||

39tataḥ pradāya bahvīrgā brāhmaṇāya yadūdvaha
|
samāhṛṣya tu vārṣṇeyī vairāṭīmabravīdidam||39||

40vairāṭi neha santāpastvayā kāryo hyanindite
|
bhartāraṃ prati suśroṇi garbhasthaṃ rakṣa vai śiśum||40||

41evamuktvā tataḥ kuntī virarāma mahādyute
|
tāmanujñāpya caivemāṃ subhadrāṃ samupānayam||41||

42evaṃ sa nidhanaṃ prāpto dauhitrastava mānada
|
santāpaṃ tyaja dudharṣa mā ca śoke manaḥ kṛthāḥ||42||
