Mahābhārata 7.13 (Droṇaparvan)

1sañjaya uvāca|
sāntare tu pratijñāte rājño droṇena nigrahe
|
tataste sainikāḥ śrutvā taṃ yudhiṣṭhiranigraham||1||

2siṃhanādaravāṃścakrubārhuśabdāṃśca kṛtsnaśaḥ
|
tacca sarvaṃ yathānyāyaṃ dharmarājena bhārata||2||

3āptairāśu parijñātaṃ bhāradvājacikīrṣitam
|
tataḥ sarvān samānāyya bhrātṝnanyāṃśca sarvaśaḥ||3||

4abravīddharmarājastu dhanañjayamidaṃ vacaḥ
|
śrutaṃ te puruṣavyāghra droṇasyādya cikīrṣitam||4||

5yathā tanna bhavet satyaṃ tathā nītirvidhīyatām
|
sāntaraṃ hi pratijñātaṃ droṇenāmitrakārṣiṇā||5||

6taccāntaraṃ maheṣvāsa tvayi tena samāhitam
|
sa tvamadya mahābāho yudhyasva madanantaram ||6||

7yathā duryodhanaḥ kāmaṃ nemaṃ droṇādavāpnuyāt
|
arjuna uvāca|
yathā me na vadhaḥ kārya ācāryasya kadācana||7||

8tathā tava parityāgo na me rājaṃścikīrṣitaḥ
|
apyevaṃ pāṇḍava prāṇānutsṛjeyamahaṃ yudhi||8||

9pratīpo nāhamācārye bhaveyaṃ vai kathañcana
|
tvāṃ nigṛhyāhave rājyaṃ dhārtarāṣṭro'yamicchati||9||

10na sa taṃ jīvaloke'smin kāmaṃ prāpyet kathañcana
|
prapatet dyauḥ sanakṣatrā pṛthivī śakalī bhavet||10||

11na tvāṃ droṇo nigṛhṇīyājjīvamāne mayi dhruvam
|
yadi tasya raṇe sāhyaṃ kurute vajrabhṛt svayam||11||

12viṣṇurvā sahito devairna tvāṃ prāpsyatyasau mṛdhe
|
mayi jīvati rājendra na bhayaṃ kartumarhasi||12||

13droṇādastrabhṛtāṃ śreṣṭhāt sarvaśastrabhṛtāmapi
|
anyacca brūyāṃ rājendra pratijñāṃ mama niścalām||13||

14na smarāmyanṛtaṃ tāvanna smarāmi parājayam
|
na smarāmi pratiśrutya kiñcidapyanṛtaṃ kṛtam||14||

15sañjaya uvāca|
tataḥ śaṃkhāśca bheryaśca mṛdaṃgāścānakaiḥ saha
|
prāvādyanta mahārāja pāṇḍavānāṃ niveśane||15||

16siṃhanādaśca sañjajñe pāṇḍavānāṃ mahātmanām
|
dhanurjyātalaśabdaśca gaganaspṛksubhairavaḥ||16||

17śrutvā śaṃkhasya nirghoṣaṃ pāṇḍavasya mahaujasaḥ
|
tvadīyeṣvapyanīkeṣu vāditrāṇyabhijaghnire||17||

18tato vyūḍhānyanīkāni tava teṣāṃ ca bhārata
|
śanairupeyuranyonyaṃ yodhyamānāni saṃyuge||18||

19tataḥ pravavṛte yuddhaṃ tumulaṃ lomaharṣaṇam
|
pāṇḍavānāṃ kurūṇāṃ ca droṇapāñcālyayorapi||19||

20yatamānāḥ prayatnena droṇānīkaviśātane
|
na śekuḥ sṛñjayā yuddhe taddhi droṇena pālitam||20||

21tathaiva tava putrasya rathodārāḥ prahāriṇaḥ
|
na śekuḥ pāṇḍavīṃ senāṃ pālyamānāṃ kirīṭinā||21||

22āstāṃ te stimite sene rakṣamāṇe parasparam
|
saṃprasupte yathā naktaṃ vanarājyau supuṣpite ||22||

23tato rukmaratho rājannarkeṇeva virājatā
|
varūthinā viniṣpatya vyacarat pṛtanāmukhe||23||

24tamudyataṃ rathenaikamāśukāriṇamāhave
|
anekamiva saṃtrāsānmenire pāṇḍusṛñjayāḥ||24||

25tena suktāḥ śarā ghorā viceruḥ sarvatodiśam
|
trāsayanto mahārāja pāṇḍaveyasya vāhinīm||25||

26madhyaṃ dinamanuprāpto gabhastiśatasaṃvṛtaḥ
|
yathā dṛśyeta gharmāṃśustathā droṇo'pyadṛśyata||26||

27na cainaṃ pāṇḍaveyānāṃ kaścicchaknoti bhārata
|
vīkṣituṃ samare kuddhaṃ mahendramiva dānavaḥ||27||

28mohayitvā tataḥ sainyaṃ bhāradvājaḥ pratāpavān
|
dhṛṣṭadyumnabalaṃ tūrṇaṃ vyadhamanniśitaiḥ śaraiḥ||28||

29sa diśaḥ sarvato rudhvā saṃvṛtya khamajihmagaiḥ
|
pārṣato yatra tatraiva mamṛde pāṇḍuvāhinīm||29||
