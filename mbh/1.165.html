Mahābhārata 1.165 (Ādiparvan)

1janamejaya uvāca|
te tathā puruṣavyāghrā nihatya bakarākṣasam
|
ata ūrdhvaṃ tato brahmankimakurvata pāṇḍavāḥ||1||

2vaiśaṃpāyana uvāca|
tatraiva nyavasanrājannihatya bakarākṣasam
|
adhīyānāḥ paraṃ brahma brāhmaṇasya niveśane||2||

3tataḥ katipayāhasya brāhmaṇaḥ saṃśitavrataḥ
|
pratiśrayārthī tadveśma brāhmaṇasyājagāma ha||3||

4sa myakpūjayitvā taṃ vipraṃ viprarṣabhastadā
|
dadau pratiśrayaṃ tasmai sadā sarvātithivrataḥ||4||

5tataste pāṇḍavāḥ sarve saha kuntyā nararṣabhāḥ
|
upāsāñcakrire vipraṃ kathayantaṃ kathāḥ śubhāḥ||5||

6kathayāmāsa deśāṃśca tīrthāni saritastathā
|
rājñaśca vividhāścaryāndeśāṃścaiva purāṇi ca||6||

7sa tatrākathayadvipraḥ kathānte janamejaya
|
pañcāleṣvadbhutākāraṃ yājñasenyāḥ svayaṃvaram||7||

8dhṛṣṭadyumnasya cotpattimutpattiṃ ca śikhaṇḍinaḥ
|
ayonijatvaṃ kṛṣṇāyā drupadasya mahāmakhe||8||

9tadadbhutatamaṃ śrutvā loke tasya mahātmanaḥ
|
vistareṇaiva papracchuḥ kathāṃte puruṣarṣabhāḥ||9||

10pāṇḍavā ūcuḥ|
kathaṃ drupadaputrasya dhṛṣṭadyumnasya pāvakāt
|
vedīmadhyācca kṛṣṇāyāḥ saṃbhavaḥ kathamadbhutaḥ||10||

11kathaṃ droṇānmaheṣvāsātsarvāṇyastrāṇyaśikṣata
|
kathaṃ vipra sakhāyau tau bhinnau kasya kṛtena vā||11||

12vaiśaṃpāyana uvāca|
evaṃ taiścodito rājansa vipraḥ puruṣarṣabhaiḥ
|
kathayāmāsa tatsarvaṃ draupadīsaṃbhavaṃ tadā||12||
