Mahābhārata 12.173 (Śāntiparvan)

1bhīṣma uvāca|
tataścitāṃ bakapateḥ kārayāmāsa rākṣasaḥ
|
ratnairgandhaiśca bahubhirvastraiśca samalaṃkṛtām ||1||

2tataḥ prajvālya nṛpatirbakarāja pratāpavān
|
pretakāryāṇi vidhivadrākṣasendraścakāra ha ||2||

3tasminkāle ca surabhirdevī dākṣāyaṇī śubhā
|
upariṣṭāttatastasya sā babhūva payasvinī ||3||

4tasyā vaktrāccyutaḥ phenaḥ kṣīramiśrastadā'nagha
|
so'patadvai tatastasyāṃ citāyāṃ rājadharmaṇaḥ ||4||

5tataḥ saṃjīvitastena bakarājastadā'nagha
|
utpatya ca samīyāya virūpākṣaṃ bakādhipaḥ ||5||

6tato'bhyagāddevarājo virūpākṣapuraṃ tadā
|
prāha cedaṃ virūpākṣaṃ diṣṭyā saṃjīvitastvayā ||6||

7śrāvayāmāsa cendrastaṃ virūpākṣaṃ purātanam
|
yathā śāpaḥ purā datto brahmaṇā rājadharmaṇaḥ ||7||

8yadā bakapatī rājan brahmāṇaṃ nopasarpati
|
tato roṣādidaṃ prāha khagendrāya pitāmahaḥ ||8||

9yasmānmūḍho mama sabhāṃ nāgato'sau bakādhamaḥ
|
tasmādvadhaṃ sa duṣṭātmā na cirātsamavāpsyati ||9||

10tadayaṃ tasya vacanānnihato gautamena vai
|
tenaivāmṛtasiktaśca punaḥ sañjīvito bakaḥ ||10||

11rājadharmā bakaḥ prāha praṇipatya purandaram
|
yadi te'nugrahakṛtā mayi buddhiḥ sureśvara ||11||

12sakhāyaṃ me sudayitaṃ gautamaṃ jīvayetyuta
|
tasya vākyaṃ samādāya vāsavaḥ puruṣarṣabha ||12||

13siktvā'mṛtena taṃ vipraṃ gautamaṃ jīvayattadā
|
sabhāṇḍopaskaraṃ rājaṃstamāsādya bakādhipaḥ ||13||

14sampariṣvajya suhṛdaṃ prītyā paramayā yutaḥ
|
atha taṃ pāpakarmāṇaṃ rājadharmā bakādhipaḥ ||14||

15visarjayitvā sadhanaṃ praviveśa svamālayam
|
yathocitaṃ ca sa bako yayau brahmasadastathā ||15||

16brahmā cainaṃ mahātmānamātithyenābhyapūjayat
|
gautamaścāpi samprāpya punastaṃ śabarālayam
|
śūdrāyāṃ janayāmāsa putrān duṣkṛtakāriṇaḥ ||16||

17śāpaśca sumahāṃstasya dattaḥ suragaṇaistadā
|
kukṣau punarbhvāḥ pāpo'yaṃ janayitvā cirātsutān ||17||

18nirayaṃ prāpsyati mahat kṛtaghno'yamiti prabho
|
etatprāha purā sarvaṃ nārado mama bhārata ||18||

19saṃsmṛtya cāpi sumahadākhyānaṃ bharatarṣabha
|
mayā'pi bhavate sarvaṃ yathāvadanuvarṇitam ||19||

20kutaḥ kṛtaghnasya yaśaḥ kutaḥ sthānaṃ kutaḥ sukham
|
aśraddheyaḥ kṛtaghno hi kṛtaghne nāsti niṣkṛtiḥ ||20||

21mitradroho na kartavyaḥ puruṣeṇa viśeṣataḥ
|
mitradhruṅnarakaṃ ghoramanantaṃ pratipadyate ||21||

22kṛtajñena sadā bhāvyaṃ mitrakāmena caiva ha
|
mitrācca labhate sarvaṃ mitrātpūjāṃ labheta ca ||22||

23mitrādbhogāṃśca bhuñjīta mitreṇāpatsu mucyate
|
satkārairuttamairmitraṃ pūjayeta vicakṣaṇaḥ ||23||

24parityājyo budhaiḥ pāpaḥ kṛtaghno nirapatrapaḥ
|
mitradrohī kulāṅgāraḥ pāpakarmā narādhamaḥ ||24||

25eṣa dharmabhṛtāṃ śreṣṭha proktaḥ pāpo mayā tava
|
mitradrohī kṛtaghno vai kiṃ bhūyaḥ śrotumicchasi ||25||

26vaiśampāyana uvāca|
etacchrutvā tadā vākyaṃ bhīṣmeṇoktaṃ mahātmanā
|
yudhiṣṭhiraḥ prītamanā babhūva janamejaya ||26||
