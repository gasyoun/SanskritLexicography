Mahābhārata 6.32 (Bhīṣmaparvan)

1arjuna uvāca —
kiṃ tadbrahma kimadhyātmaṃ kiṃ karma puruṣottama
|
adhibhūtaṃ ca kiṃ proktamadhidaivaṃ kimucyate ||1||

2adhiyajñaḥ kathaṃ ko'tra dehe'sminmadhusūdana
|
prayāṇakāle ca kathaṃ jñeyo'si niyatātmabhiḥ ||2||

3śrībhagavānuvāca —
akṣaraṃ brahma paramaṃ svabhāvo'dhyātmamucyate
|
bhūtabhāvodbhavakaro visargaḥ karmasaṃjñitaḥ ||3||

4adhibhūtaṃ kṣaro bhāvaḥ puruṣaścādhidaivatam
|
adhiyajño'hamevātra dehe dehabhṛtāṃ vara ||4||

5antakāle ca māmeva smaranmuktvā kalebaram
|
yaḥ prayāti sa madbhāvaṃ yāti nāstyatra saṃśayaḥ ||5||

6yaṃ yaṃ vāpi smaranbhāvaṃ tyajatyante kalebaram
|
taṃ tamevaiti kaunteya sadā tadbhāvabhāvitaḥ ||6||

7tasmātsarveṣu kāleṣu māmanusmara yudhya ca
|
mayyarpitamanobuddhirmāmevaiṣyasyasaṃśayaḥ ||7||

8abhyāsayogayuktena cetasā nānyagāminā
|
paramaṃ puruṣaṃ divyaṃ yāti pārthānucintayan ||8||

9kaviṃ purāṇamanuśāsitāramaṇoraṇīyāṃsamanusmaredyaḥ
|
sarvasya dhātāramacintyarūpamādityavarṇaṃ tamasaḥ parastāt ||9||

10prayāṇakāle manasācalena bhaktyā yukto yogabalena caiva
|
bhruvormadhye prāṇamāveśya samya ksa taṃ paraṃ puruṣamupaiti divyam ||10||

11yadakṣaraṃ vedavido vadanti viśanti yadyatayo vītarāgāḥ
|
yadicchanto brahmacaryaṃ caranti tatte padaṃ saṅgraheṇa pravakṣye ||11||

12sarvadvārāṇi saṃyamya mano hṛdi nirudhya ca
|
mūrdhnyādhāyātmanaḥ prāṇamāsthito yogadhāraṇām ||12||

13omityekākṣaraṃ brahma vyāharanmāmanusmaran
|
yaḥ prayāti tyajandehaṃ sa yāti paramāṃ gatim ||13||

14ananyacetāḥ satataṃ yo māṃ smarati nityaśaḥ
|
tasyāhaṃ sulabhaḥ pārtha nityayuktasya yoginaḥ ||14||

15māmupetya punarjanma duḥkhālayamaśāśvatam
|
nāpnuvanti mahātmānaḥ saṃsiddhiṃ paramāṃ gatāḥ ||15||

16ā brahmabhuvanāllokāḥ punarāvartino'rjuna
|
māmupetya tu kaunteya punarjanma na vidyate ||16||

17sahasrayugaparyantamaharyadbrahmaṇo viduḥ
|
rātriṃ yugasahasrāntāṃ te'horātravido janāḥ ||17||

18avyaktādvyaktayaḥ sarvāḥ prabhavantyaharāgame
|
rātryāgame pralīyante tatraivāvyaktasaṃjñake ||18||

19bhūtagrāmaḥ sa evāyaṃ bhūtvā bhūtvā pralīyate
|
rātryāgame'vaśaḥ pārtha prabhavatyaharāgame ||19||

20parastasmāttu bhāvo'nyo'vyakto'vyaktātsanātanaḥ
|
yaḥ sa sarveṣu bhūteṣu naśyatsu na vinaśyati ||20||

21avyakto'kṣara ityuktastamāhuḥ paramāṃ gatim
|
yaṃ prāpya na nivartante taddhāma paramaṃ mama ||21||

22puruṣaḥ sa paraḥ pārtha bhaktyā labhyastvananyayā
|
yasyāntaḥsthāni bhūtāni yena sarvamidaṃ tatam ||22||

23yatra kāle tvanāvṛttimāvṛttiṃ caiva yoginaḥ
|
prayātā yānti taṃ kālaṃ vakṣyāmi bharatarṣabha ||23||

24agnirjyotirahaḥ śuklaḥ ṣaṇmāsā uttarāyaṇam
|
tatra prayātā gacchanti brahma brahmavido janāḥ ||24||

25dhūmo rātristathā kṛṣṇaḥ ṣaṇmāsā dakṣiṇāyanam
|
tatra cāndramasaṃ jyotiryogī prāpya nivartate ||25||

26śuklakṛṣṇe gatī hyete jagataḥ śāśvate mate
|
ekayā yātyanāvṛttimanyayāvartate punaḥ ||26||

27naite sṛtī pārtha jānanyogī muhyati kaścana
|
tasmātsarveṣu kāleṣu yogayukto bhavārjuna ||27||

28vedeṣu yajñeṣu tapaḥsu caiva dāneṣu yatpuṇyaphalaṃ pradiṣṭam
|
atyeti tatsarvamidaṃ viditvā yogī paraṃ sthānamupaiti cādyam ||28||
