Mahābhārata 7.10 (Droṇaparvan)

1vaiśampāyana uvāca|
etat pṛṣṭvā sūtaputraṃ hṛcchokenārdito bhṛśam
|
jaye nirāśaḥ putrāṇāṃ dhṛtarāṣṭro'patat kṣitau||1||

2taṃ visaṃjñaṃ nipatitaṃ siṣicuḥ paricārikāḥ
|
jalenātyarthaśītena vījantyaḥ puṇyagandhinā ||2||

3patitaṃ cainamālokya samantādbharatastriyaḥ
|
parivavrurmahārājamaspṛśaṃścaiva pāṇibhiḥ||3||

4utthāpya cainaṃ śanakai rājānaṃ pṛthivītalāt
|
āsanaṃ prāpayāmāsurbāṣpakaṇṭhyo varānanāḥ ||4||

5āsanaṃ prāpya rājā tu mūrchayā'bhipariplutaḥ
|
niśceṣṭo'tiṣṭhata tadā vījyamānaḥ samantataḥ||5||

6sa labdhvā śanakaiḥ saṃjñāṃ vepamāno mahīpatiḥ
|
punargāvalgaṇiṃ sūtaṃ paryapṛcchadyathātatham ||6||

7dhṛtarāṣṭra uvāca|
yaḥ sa udyannivādityo jyotiṣā praṇudaṃstamaḥ
|
ajātaśatrumāyāntaṃ kastaṃ droṇādavārayat ||7||

8prabhinnamiva mātaṅgaṃ yathā kruddhaṃ tarasvinam
|
prasannavadanaṃ dṛṣṭvā pratidviradagāminam ||8||

9vāsitāsaṃgame yadvadajayyaṃ prati yūthapaiḥ
|
nijaghāna raṇe vīrān vīraḥ puruṣasattamaḥ ||9||

10yo hyeko hi mahāvīryo nirdaheddhoracakṣuṣā
|
kṛtsnaṃ duryodhanabalaṃ dhṛtimān satyasaṃgaraḥ||10||

11cakṣurhaṇaṃ jaye saktamiṣvāsadharamacyutam
|
dāntaṃ bahumataṃ loke ke śūrāḥ paryavārayan||11||

12ke duṣpradharṣaṃ rājānamiṣvāsadharamacyutam
|
samāsedurnaravyāghraṃ kaunteyaṃ tatra māmakāḥ||12||

13tarasaivābhipadyātha yo vai droṇamupādravat
|
yaḥ karoti mahat karma śatrūṇāṃ vai mahābalaḥ||13||

14mahākāyo mahotsāho nāgāyutasamo bale
|
taṃ bhīmasenamāyāntaṃ ke śūrāḥ paryavārayan||14||

15yadā''yājjaladaprakhyo rathaḥ paramavīryavān
|
parjanya iva bībhatsustumulāmaśanīṃ sṛjan||15||

16visṛjañcharajālāni varṣāṇi maghavāniva
|
avasphūrjan diśaḥ sarvāstalanemisvanena ca||16||

17cāpavidyutprabho ghoro rathagulmacalāhakaḥ
|
sa nemighoṣastanitaḥ śaraśabdātibandhuraḥ||17||

18roṣanirjitajīmūto manobhiprāyaśīghragaḥ
|
marmātigo bāṇadharastumulaḥ śoṇitodakaiḥ||18||

19saṃplāvayan diśaḥ sarvā mānavairāstaran mahīm
|
bhīmaniḥ svanito raudro duryodhanapurogamān||19||

20yuddhe'bhyaṣiñcadvijayo gārdhrapatraiḥ śilāśitaiḥ
|
gāṇḍīvaṃ dhārayan dhīmān kīdṛśaṃ vo manastadā||20||

21iṣusaṃbādhamākāśaṃ kurvan kapivaradhvajaḥ
|
yadāyāt kathamāsīt tadā pārthaṃ samīkṣatām||21||

22kaccidgāṇḍīvaśabdena na praṇaśyati vai balam
|
yadvaḥ sabhairavaṃ kurvannarjuno bhṛśamanvayāt||22||

23kaccinnāpānudat prāṇāniṣubhirvo dhanañjayaḥ
|
vāto vegādivāvidhyanmeghān śaragaṇairnṛpān||23||

24ko hi gāṇḍīvadhanvānaṃ raṇe soḍhuṃ naro'rhati
|
yamupaśrutya senāgre janaḥ sarvo vidīryate ||24||

25yatsenāḥ samakampanta yadvīrānaspṛśadbhayam
|
ke tatra nājahurdroṇaṃ ke kṣudrāḥ prādravan bhayāt||25||

26ke vā tatra tanūstyaktvā pratīpaṃ mṛtyumāvrajan
|
amānuṣāṇāṃ jetāraṃ yuddheṣvapi dhanañjayam||26||

27na ca vegaṃ sitāśvasya visahiṣyanti māmakāḥ
|
gāṇḍīvasya ca nirghoṣaṃ prāvṛḍjaladaniḥsvanam||27||

28viṣvakseno yasya yantā yasya yoddhā dhanañjayaḥ
|
aśakyaḥ sa ratho jetuṃ manye devāsurairapi||28||

29sukumāro yuvā śūro darśanīyaśca pāṇḍavaḥ
|
medhāvī nipuṇo dhīmān yudhi satyaparākramaḥ||29||

30ārāvaṃ vipulaṃ kurvan vyathayan sarvasainikān
|
yadā''yānnakulo droṇaṃ ke śūrāḥ paryavārayan||30||

31āśīviṣa iva kruddhaḥ sahadevo yadābhyayāt
|
kadanaṃ kariṣyan śatrūṇāṃ tejasā durjayo yudhi||31||

32āryavratamamogheṣuṃ hrīmantamaparājitam
|
sahadevaṃ tamāyāntaṃ ke śūrāḥ paryavārayan||32||

33yastu sauvīrarājasya pramathya mahatīṃ camūm
|
ādatta mahiṣīṃ bhojāṃ kāmyāṃ sarvāṅgaśobhanām||33||

34satyaṃ dhṛtiśca śauryaṃ ca brahmacaryaṃ ca kevalam
|
sarvāṇi yuyudhāne'sminnityāni puruṣarṣabhe||34||

35balinaṃ satyakarmāṇamadīnamaparājitam
|
vāsudevasamaṃ yuddhe vāsudevādanantaram||35||

36dhanañjayopadeśena śreṣṭhamiṣvastrakarmaṇi
|
pārthena samamastreṣu kastaṃ droṇādavārayat||36||

37vṛṣṇīnāṃ pravaraṃ vīraṃ śūraṃ sarvadhanuṣmatām
|
rāmeṇa samamastreṣu yaśasā vikrameṇa ca||37||

38satyaṃ dhṛtirmatiḥ śauryaṃ brāhmaṃ cāstramanuttamam
|
sātvate tāni sarvāṇi trailokyamiva keśave||38||

39tamevaṃguṇasampannaṃ durvāramapi daivataiḥ
|
samāsādya maheṣvāsaṃ ke śūrāḥ paryavārayan||39||

40pañcāleṣūttamaṃ vīramuttamābhijanapriyam
|
nityamuttamakarmāṇamuttamaujasamāhave||40||

41yuktaṃ dhanañjayahite mamānarthārthamutthitam
|
yamavaiśravaṇādityamahendra varuṇopamam ||41||

42mahārathaṃ samākhyātaṃ droṇāyodyatamāhave
|
tyajantaṃ tumule prāṇān ke śūrāḥ samavārayan||42||

43ekopasṛtya cedibhyaḥ pāṇḍavānyaḥ samāśritaḥ
|
dhṛṣṭaketuṃ samāyāntaṃ droṇaṃ kastaṃ nyavārayat||43||

44yo'vadhīt ketumān vīro rājaputraṃ durāsadam
|
aparāntagiridvāre droṇāt kastaṃ nyavārayat||44||

45strīpuṃsayornaravyāghro yaḥ sa veda guṇāguṇān
|
śikhaṇḍinaṃ yājñasenimamlānamanasaṃ yudhi||45||

46devavratasya samare hetuṃ mṛtyormahātmanaḥ
|
droṇāyābhimukhaṃ yāntaṃ ke śūrāḥ paryavārayan||46||

47yasminnabhyadhikā vīre guṇāḥ sarve dhanañjayāt
|
yasminnastrāṇi satyaṃ ca brahmacaryaṃ ca sarvadā||47||

48vāsudevasamaṃ vīrye dhanañjayasamaṃ bale
|
tejasā''dityasadṛśaṃ bṛhaspatisamaṃ matau||48||

49abhimanyuṃ mahātmānaṃ vyāttānanamivāntakam
|
droṇāyābhimukhaṃ yāntaṃ ke śūrāḥ samavārayan||49||

50taruṇastaruṇaprajñaḥ saubhadraḥ paravīrahā
|
yadābhyadhāvadvai droṇaṃ tadāsīdvo manaḥ katham||50||

51draupadeyā naravyāghrāḥ samudramiva sindhavaḥ
|
yaddroṇamādravan saṃkhye ke śūrāstānyavārayan||51||

52ete dvādaśa varṣāṇi krīḍāmutsṛjya bālakāḥ
|
astrārthamavasan bhīṣme bibhrato vratamuttamam||52||

53kṣatraṃjayaḥ kṣatradevaḥ kṣatravarmā ca mānadaḥ
|
dhṛṣṭadyumnātmajā vīrāḥ ke tān droṇādavārayan||53||

54śatādviśiṣṭaṃ yaṃ yuddhe samamanyanta vṛṣṇayaḥ
|
cekitānaṃ maheṣvāsaṃ kastaṃ droṇādavārayat||54||

55vārdhakṣebhiḥ kaliṅgānāṃ yaḥ kanyāmāharadyudhi
|
anādhṛṣṭiradīnātmā kastaṃ droṇādavārayat||55||

56bhrātaraḥ pañca kaikeyā dhārmikāḥ satyavikramāḥ
|
indragopakasaṅkāśā raktavarmāyudhadhvajāḥ||56||

57mātṛṣvasuḥ sutā vīrāḥ pāṇḍavānāṃ jayārthinaḥ
|
tān droṇaṃ hantumāyātān ke vīrāḥ paryavārayan||57||

58yaṃ yodhayanto rājāno nājayan vāraṇāvate
|
ṣaṇmāsānapi saṃrabdhā jighāṃsanto yudhāṃpatim||58||

59dhanuṣmatāṃ varaṃ śūraṃ satyasandhaṃ mahābalam
|
droṇāt kastaṃ naravyāghraṃ yuyutsuṃ paryavārayat||59||

60yaḥ putraṃ kāśirājasya vārāṇasyāṃ mahāratham
|
samare strīṣu gṛdhyantaṃ bhallenāpāharadrathāt||60||

61dhṛṣṭadyumnaṃ maheṣvāsaṃ pārthānāṃ mantradhāriṇam
|
yuktaṃ duryodhanānarthe sṛṣṭaṃ droṇavadhāya ca||61||

62nirdahantaṃ raṇe yodhān dārayantaṃ ca sarvataḥ
|
droṇābhimukhamāyāntaṃ ke śūrāḥ paryavārayan||62||

63utsaṅga iva saṃvṛddhaṃ drupadasyāstravittamam
|
śaikhaṇḍinaṃ śastraguptaṃ ke ca droṇādavārayan||63||

64ya imāṃ pṛthivīṃ kṛtsnāṃ carmavat samaveṣṭayat
|
mahatā rathaghoṣeṇa mukhyārighno mahārathaḥ||64||

65daśāśvamedhānājahre svannapānāptadākṣiṇān
|
nirargalānsarvamedhān putravat pālayan prajāḥ||65||

66gaṅgāsrotasi yāvatyaḥ sikatā apyaśeṣataḥ
|
tāvatīrgā dadau vīra uśīnarasuto'dhvare||66||

67na pūrve nāpare cakruridaṃ kecana mānavāḥ
|
itīdaṃ cukruśurdevāḥ kṛte karmaṇi duṣkare||67||

68paśyāmastriṣu lokeṣu na taṃ saṃ sthāsnucāriṣu
|
jātaṃ cāpi janiṣyantaṃ dvitīyaṃ cāpi sāṃpratam||68||

69anyamauśīnarācchaibyāddhuro voḍhāramityuta
|
gatiṃ yasya na yāsyanti mānuṣā lokavāsinaḥ||69||

70tasya naptāramāyāntaṃ śaibyaṃ kaḥ samavārayat
|
droṇāyābhimukhaṃ yattaṃ vyāttānanamivāntakam||70||

71virāṭasya rathānīkaṃ matsyasyāmitraghātinaḥ
|
prepsantaṃ sumare droṇaṃ ke vīrāḥ paryavārayan||71||

72sadyo vṛkodarājjāto mahābalaparākramaḥ
|
māyāvī rākṣaso vīro yasmānmama mahadbhayam||72||

73pārthānāṃ jayakāmaṃ taṃ putrāṇāṃ mama kaṇṭakam
|
ghaṭotkacaṃ mahātmānaṃ kastaṃ droṇādavārayat||73||

74ete cānye ca bahavo yeṣāmarthāya sañjaya
|
tyaktāraḥ saṃyuge prāṇān kiṃ teṣāmajitaṃ yudhi||74||

75yeṣāṃ ca puruṣavyāghraḥ śārṅgadhanvā vyapāśrayaḥ
|
hitārthī cāpi pārthānāṃ kathaṃ teṣāṃ parājayaḥ||75||

76lokānāṃ gururatyarthaṃ lokanāthaḥ sanātanaḥ
|
nārāyaṇo raṇe nātho divyo divyātmakaḥ prabhuḥ||76||

77yasya divyāni karmāṇi pravadanti manīṣiṇaḥ
|
tānyahaṃ kīrtayiṣyāmi bhaktyā sthairyārthamātmanaḥ||77||
