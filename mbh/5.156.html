Mahābhārata 5.156 (Udyogaparvan)

1vaiśampāyana uvāca|
tataḥ śāntanavaṃ bhīṣmaṃ prāñjalirdhṛtarāṣṭrajaḥ
|
saha sarvairmahīpālairidaṃ vacanamabravīt ||1||

2ṛte senāpraṇetāraṃ pṛtanā sumahatyapi
|
dīryate yuddhamāsādya pipīlikapuṭaṃ yathā ||2||

3na hi jātu dvayorbuddhiḥ samā bhavati karhicit
|
śauryaṃ ca balanetṝṇāṃ spardhate ca parasparam ||3||

4śrūyate ca mahāprājña haihayānamitaujasaḥ
|
abhyayurbāhmaṇāḥ sarve samucchritakuśadhvajāḥ ||4||

5tānabhyayustadā vaiśyāḥ śūdrāścaiva pitāmaha
|
ekatastu trayo varṇā ekataḥ kṣatriyarṣabhāḥ||5||

6tato yuddheṣvabhajyanta trayo varṇāḥ punaḥ punaḥ
|
kṣatriyāśca jayantyeva bahulaṃ caikato balam ||6||

7tataste kṣatriyāneva papracchurdvijasattamāḥ
|
tebhyaḥ śaśaṃsurdharmajñā yāthātathyaṃ pitāmaha ||7||

8vayamekasya śṛṇvānā mahābuddhimato raṇe
|
bhavantastu pṛthaksarve svabuddhivaśavartinaḥ ||8||

9tataste brāhmaṇāścakrurekaṃ senāpatiṃ dvijam
|
naye sukuśalaṃ śūramajayan kṣatriyāṃstataḥ ||9||

10evaṃ ye kuśalaṃ śūraṃ hitepsitamakalmaṣam
|
senāpatiṃ prakurvanti te jayanti raṇe ripūn ||10||

11bhavānuśanasā tulyo hitaiṣī ca sadā mama
|
asaṃhāryaḥ sthito dharme sa naḥ senāpatirbhava ||11||

12raśmivatāmivādityo vīrudhāmiva candramāḥ
|
kubera iva yakṣāṇāṃ devānāmiva vāsavaḥ ||12||

13parvatānāṃ yathā meruḥ suparṇaḥ pakṣiṇāṃ yathā
|
kumāra iva devānāṃ vasūnāmiva havyavāṭ ||13||

14bhavatā hi vayaṃ guptāḥ śakreṇeva divaukasaḥ
|
anādhṛṣyā bhaviṣyāmastridaśānāmapi dhruvam ||14||

15prayātu no bhavānagre devānāmiva pāvakiḥ
|
vayaṃ tvāmanuyāsyāmaḥ saurabheyā ivarṣabham ||15||

16bhīṣma uvāca|
evametanmahābāho yathā vadasi bhārata
|
yathaiva hi bhavanto me tathaiva mama pāṇḍavāḥ ||16||

17api caiva mayā śreyo vācyaṃ teṣāṃ narādhipa
|
saṃyoddhavyaṃ tavārthāya yathā me samayaḥ kṛtaḥ ||17||

18na tu paśyāmi yoddhāramātmanaḥ sadṛśaṃ bhuvi
|
ṛte tasmānnaravyāghrāt kuntīputrāddhanañjayāt ||18||

19sa hi veda mahābuddhirdivyānyastrāṇyanekaśaḥ
|
na tu māṃ vivṛto yuddhe jātu yudhyeta pāṇḍavaḥ ||19||

20ahaṃ caiva kṣaṇenaiva nirmanuṣyamidaṃ jagat
|
kuryāṃ śastrabalenaiva sasurāsurarākṣasam ||20||

21na tvevotsādanīyā me pāṇḍoḥ putrā janādhipa
|
tasmādyodhān haniṣyāmi prayogeṇāyutaṃ sadā ||21||

22evameṣāṃ kariṣyāmi nidhanaṃ kurunandana
|
na cette māṃ haniṣyanti pūrvameva samāgame ||22||

23senāpatistvahaṃ rājan samaye nāpareṇa te
|
bhaviṣyāmi yathākāmaṃ tanme śrotumihārhasi ||23||

24karṇo vā yudhyatāṃ pūrvamahaṃ vā pṛthivīpate
|
spardhate hi sadā'tyarthaṃ sūtaputro mayā raṇe ||24||

25karṇa uvāca|
nāhaṃ jīvati gāṅgeye rājan yotsye kathañcana
|
hate bhīṣme tu yotsyāmi saha gāṇḍīvadhanvanā ||25||

26vaiśampāyana uvāca|
tataḥ senāpatiṃ cakre vidhivadbhūridakṣiṇam
|
dhṛtarāṣṭrātmajo bhīṣmaṃ so'bhiṣikto vyarocata ||26||

27tato bherīśca śaṅkhāṃśca śataśo'tha sahasraśaḥ
|
vādayāmāsuravyagrā vādakā rājaśāsanāt ||27||

28siṃhanādāśca vividhā vāhanānāṃ ca niḥsvanāḥ
|
prādurāsannanabhre ca varṣaṃ rudhirakardamam ||28||

29nirghātāḥ pṛthivīkampā gajabṛṃhitaniḥsvanāḥ
|
āsaṃśca sarvayodhānāṃ pātayanto manāṃsyuta ||29||

30vācaścāpyaśarīriṇyo divaścolkāḥ prapedire
|
śivāśca bhayavedinyo nedurdīptatarā bhṛśam ||30||

31saināpatye yadā rājā gāṅgeyamabhiṣiktavān
|
tadaitānyugrarūpāṇi babhūvuḥ śataśā nṛpa ||31||

32tataḥ senāpatiṃ kṛtvā bhīṣmaṃ parabalārdanam
|
vācayitvā dvijaśreṣṭhān gobhirniṣkaiśca bhūriśaḥ ||32||

33vardhamāno jayāśīrbhirniryayau sainikairvṛtaḥ
|
āpageyaṃ puraskṛtya bhrātṛbhiḥ sahitastadā ||33||

34skandhāvāreṇa mahatā kurukṣetraṃ jagāma ha ||34||

35parikramya kurukṣetraṃ karṇena saha kauravaḥ
|
śibiraṃ māpayāmāsa same deśe janādhipa ||35||

36madhurānūṣare deśe prabhūtayavasendhane
|
yathaiva hāstinapuraṃ tadvacchibiramābabhau ||36||
