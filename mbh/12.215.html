Mahābhārata 12.215 (Śāntiparvan)

1bhīṣma uvāca|
duranteṣvindriyārtheṣu saktāḥ sīdanti jantavaḥ
|
ye tvasaktā mahātmānaste yānti paramāṃ gatim ||1||

2janmamṛtyujarāduḥkhairvyādhibhirmānasaklamaiḥ
|
dṛṣṭvaiva saṃtataṃ lokaṃ ghaṭenmokṣāya buddhimān ||2||

3vāṅmanobhyāṃ śarīreṇa śuciḥ syādanahaṃkṛtaḥ
|
praśānto jñānavān bhikṣurnirapekṣaścaretsukham ||3||

4athavā manasaḥ saṅgaṃ paśyedbhūtānukampayā
|
tatrāpyupekṣāṃ kurvīta jñātvā karmaphalaṃ jagat ||4||

5yatkṛtaṃ syācchubhaṃ karma pāpaṃ vā yadi vā'śnute
|
tasmācchubhāni karmāṇi kuryādvā buddhikarmabhiḥ ||5||

6ahiṃsā satyavacanaṃ sarvabhūteṣu cārjayam
|
kṣamā caivāpramādaśca yasyaite sa sukhī bhavet ||6||

7tasmātsamāhitaṃ buddhyā mano bhūteṣu dhārayet
|
yaścainaṃ paramaṃ dharmaṃ sarvabhūtasukhāvaham ||7||

8duḥkhānniḥsaraṇaṃ veda sarvajñaḥ sa sukhī bhavet
|
nāpadhyāyenna spṛhayennābaddhaṃ cintayedasat ||8||

9athāmodhaprayatnena mano jñāne niveśayat
|
vācā moghaprayāsena manojñaṃ tatpravartate ||9||

10vivakṣatā ca sadvākyaṃ dharmaṃ sūkṣmamavekṣatā
|
satyāṃ vācamahiṃsrāṃ ca vadedanapavādinīm ||10||

11kalkāpetāmaparuṣāmanṛśaṃsāmapaiśunām
|
īdṛgalpaṃ ca vaktavyamavikṣiptena cetasā ||11||

12vākprabaddho hi saṃsāro virāgādvyāharedyadi
|
buddhyāpyanugṛhītena manasā karma tāmasam ||12||

13rajobhūtairhi karaṇaiḥ karmaṇi pratipadyate
|
sa duḥkhaṃ prāpya loke'sminnarakāyopapadyate
|
tasmānmanovākśarīrairācareddhairyamātmanaḥ ||13||

14prakīrṇameṣa bhāraṃ hi yadvaddhāryeta dasyubhiḥ
|
pratilomāṃ diśaṃ buddhvā saṃsāramabudhāstathā ||14||

15tameva ca yathā dasyuḥ kṣiptvā gacchecchivāṃ diśam
|
tathā rajastamaḥkarmāṇyutsṛjya prāpnuyācchubham ||15||

16niḥsaṃdigdhamanīho vai muktaḥ sarvaparigrahaiḥ
|
viviktacārī laghvāśī tapasvī niyatendriyaḥ ||16||

17jñānadagdhaparikleśaḥ prayogaratirātmavān
|
niṣpracāreṇa manasā paraṃ tadadhigacchati ||17||

18dhṛtimānātmavān buddhiṃ nigṛhṇīyādasaṃśayam
|
mano buddhyā nigṛhṇīyādviṣayānmanasā''tmanaḥ ||18||

19nigṛhītendriyasyāsya kurvāṇasya mano vaśe
|
devatāstatprakāśante hṛṣṭā yānti tamīśvaram ||19||

20tābhiḥ saṃyuktamanaso brahma tatsamprakāśate
|
śanaiścopagate sattve brahmabhūyāya kalpate ||20||

21athavā na pravarteta yogatantrairupakramet
|
yena tantrayatastantraṃ vṛttiḥ syāttattadācaret ||21||

22kaṇa-kulmāṣa-piṇyāka-śāka-yāvaka-saktavaḥ
|
tathā mūlaphalaṃ bhakṣyaṃ paryāyeṇopayojayet ||22||

23āhāraniyamaṃ caiva deśe kāle ca sāttvikam
|
tatparīkṣyānuvarteta tatpravṛttyanupūrvakam ||23||

24pravṛttaṃ noparundheta śanairagnimivendhayet
|
jñānānvitaṃ tathā jñānamarkavatsamprakāśate ||24||

25jñānādhiṣṭhānamajñānaṃ trī~llokānadhitiṣṭhati
|
vijñānānugataṃ jñānamajñānenāpakṛṣyate ||25||

26pṛthaktvātsamprayogācca nāsūyurveda śāśvatam
|
sa tayorapavargajño vītarāgo vimucyate ||26||

27vayotīto jarāmṛtyū jitvā brahma sanātanam
|
amṛtaṃ tadavāpnoti yattadakṣaramavyayam ||27||
