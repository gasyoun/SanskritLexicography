Mahābhārata 4.37 (Virāṭaparvan)

1vaiśampāyana uvāca|sā prādravatkāñcanamālyadhāriṇī jyeṣṭhena bhrātrā prahitā yaśasvinī
|
sudakṣiṇā vedivilagnamadhyā sā padmapatrābhanibhā śikhaṇḍinī ||1||

2tanvī śubhāṅgī maṇicitramekhalā matsyasya rājño duhitā śriyā vṛtā
|
tannartanāgāramarālapakṣmā śatahradā meghamivānvapadyata ||2||

3sā hastihastopamasaṃhitorūḥ svaninditā cārudatī sumadhyamā
|
āsādya taṃ vai varamālyadhāriṇī pārthaṃ śubhā nāgavadhūriva dvipam ||3||

4sā ratnabhūtā manasaḥ priyā'rcitā sutā virāṭasya yathendralakṣmīḥ
|
sudarśanīyā pramukhe yaśasvinī prītyā'bravīdarjunamāyatekṣaṇā ||4||

5susaṃhatoruṃ kanakojjvalattvacaṃ pārthaḥ kumārīṃ sa tadā'bhyabhāṣata
|
kimāgamaḥ kāñcanamālyadhāriṇi mṛgākṣi kiṃ tvaṃ tvariteva bhāmini
|
kiṃ te mukhaṃ sundari na prasannamācakṣva tattvaṃ mama śīghramaṅgane ||5||

6vaiśampāyana uvāca|sa tāṃ dṛṣṭvā viśālākṣīṃ rājaputrīṃ sakhīṃ tathā
|
prahasannabravīdrājan kimāgamanamityuta ||6||

7tamabravīdrājaputrī samupetya nararṣabham
|
praṇayaṃ bhāvayantī sā sakhīmadhya idaṃ vacaḥ ||7||

8gāvo rāṣṭrasya kurubhiḥ kālyante no bṛhannale
|
tā vijetuṃ mama bhrātā prayāsyati dhanurdharaḥ ||8||

9nāciraṃ nihatastasya saṃgrāme rathasārathiḥ
|
tena nāsti samaḥ sūto yo'sya sārathyamācaret ||9||

10tasmai prayatamānāya sārathyarthaṃ bṛhannale
|
ācacakṣe hayajñāne sairandhrī kauśalaṃ tava ||10||

11arjunasya kilāsīstvaṃ sārathirdayitaḥ purā
|
tvayā'jayatsahāyena pṛthivīṃ pāṇḍavarṣabhaḥ ||11||

12sā sārathyaṃ mama bhrātuḥ kuru sādhu bṛhanna
|
purā dūrataraṃ gāvo hriyante kurubhirhi naḥ ||12||

13athaitadvacanaṃ me'dya niyuktā na kariṣyasi
|
praṇayāducyamānā tvaṃ parityakṣyāmi jīvitam||13||

14evamuktastu suśroṇyā tayā sakhyā paraṃtapaḥ
|
jagāma rājaputrasya sakāśamamitaujasaḥ ||14||

15tamāvrajantaṃ tvaritaṃ prabhinnamiva kuñjaram
|
anvagacchadviśālākṣī śiśuṃ gajavadhūriva ||15||

16dūrādeva tu tāṃ prekṣya rājaputrā'bhyabhāṣata
|
tvayā sārathinā pārthaḥ khāṇḍave'gnimatarpayet ||16||

17pṛthivīmajayat kṛtsnāṃ kuntīputro dhanañjayaḥ
|
sairandhrī tvāṃ samācaṣṭe sā hi jānāti pāṇḍavān ||17||

18saṃyaccha māmakānaśvāṃstathaiva tvaṃ bṛhannale
|
kurubhiryotsyamānasya godhanāni parīpsataḥ ||18||

19arjunasya kilāsīstvaṃ sārathirdayitaḥ purā
|
tvayā'jayat sahāyena pṛthivīṃ pāṇḍavarṣabhaḥ ||19||

20evamuktā pratyuvāca rājaputraṃ bṛhannalā
|
kā śaktirmama sārathyaṃ kartuṃ saṅgrāmamūrdhani ||20||

21gītaṃ vā yadi vā nṛtyaṃ vāditraṃ vā pṛthagvidham
|
tat kariṣyāmi bhadraṃ te sārathyaṃ tu kuto mama ||21||

22uttara uvāca|bṛhannale gāyano vā nartano vā punarbhava
|
kṣipraṃ me rathamāsthāya nigṛhṇīṣva hayottamān ||22||

23vaiśampāyana uvāca|sa tatra narmasaṃyuktamakarot pāṇḍavo bahu
|
uttarāyāḥ pramukhataḥ sarvaṃ jānannarindamaḥ ||23||

24ūrdhvamutkṣipya kavacaṃ śarīre pratyamuñcata
|
kumāryastatra taṃ dṛṣṭvā prāhasan pṛthulocanāḥ ||24||

25sa tu dṛṣṭvā vimuhyantaṃ svayamevottarastataḥ
|
kavacena mahārheṇa samanahyadbṛhannalām ||25||

26sa bibhratkavacaṃ cāgryaṃ svayamapyaṃśumatprabham
|
dhvajaṃ ca siṃhamucchritya sārathye samakalpayat ||26||

27dhanūṃṣi ca mahārhāṇi bāṇāṃśca rucirān bahūn
|
ādāya prayayau vīraḥ sa bṛhannalasārathiḥ ||27||

28athottarā ca kanyāśca sakhyastāmabruvaṃstadā
|
bṛhannale ānayethā vāsāṃsi rucirāṇi ca ||28||

29pāñcālikārthaṃ citrāṇi sūkṣmāṇi ca mṛdūni ca
|
vijitya saṃgrāmagatān bhīṣmadroṇamukhān kurūn ||29||

30evaṃ tā bruvatīḥ kanyāḥ sahitāḥ pāṇḍunandanaḥ
|
pratyuvāca hasan pārtho meghadundubhiniḥsvanaḥ ||30||

31bṛhannalovāca
|
yadyuttaro'yaṃ saṃgrāme vijeṣyati mahārathān
|
athāhariṣye vāsāṃsi divyāni rucirāṇi ca ||31||

32vaiśampāyana uvāca|evamuktvā tu bībhatsustataḥ prācodayaddhayān
|
kurūnabhimukhaḥ śūro nānādhvajapatākinaḥ ||32||

33tamuttaraṃ vīkṣya rathottame sthitaṃ bṛhannalāyāḥ sahitaṃ mahābhujam
|
striyaśca kanyāśca dvijāśca suvratāḥ pradakṣiṇaṃ cakrurathocuraṅganāḥ ||33||

34yadarjunasyarṣabhatulyagāminaḥ purā'bhavat khāṇḍavadāhamaṅgalam
|
kurūn samāsādya raṇe bṛhannale sahottareṇādya tadastu maṅgalam ||34||
