Mahābhārata 4.33 (Virāṭaparvan)

1vaiśampāyana uvāca|tamasā'bhiplute loke rajasā caiva bhārata
|
atiṣṭhan vai muhūrtaṃ tu vyūḍhānīkāḥ prahāriṇaḥ ||1||

2tato'ndhakāraṃ praṇudannudatiṣṭhata candramāḥ
|
kurvāṇo vimalāṃ rātriṃ nandayan kṣatriyān yudhi ||2||

3tataḥ prakāśamāsādya punaryuddhamavartata
|
ghorarūpaṃ tataste sma nāvaikṣanta parasparam ||3||

4tataḥ suśarmā traigartaḥ saha bhrātrā yavīyasā
|
abhyadravanmatsyarājaṃ rathavrātena sarvaśaḥ ||4||

5tato rathābhyāṃ praskandya bhrātarau kṣatriyarṣabhau
|
gadāpāṇī susaṃrabdhau samabhyadravatāṃ rathān ||5||

6tathaiva teṣāṃ tu balāni tāni kruddhānyathānyonyamabhidravanti
|
gadāsikhaḍgaiśca paraśvadhaiśca prāsaiśca tīkṣṇāgrasupītadhāraiḥ ||6||

7balaṃ tu matsyasya balena rājā sarvaṃ trigartādhipatiḥ suśarmā
|
pramathya jitvā ca prasahya matsyaṃ virāṭamojasvinamabhyadhāvat ||7||

8tau nihatya pṛthak dhuryāvubhau tau pārṣṇisārathī
|
virathaṃ matsyarājānaṃ jīvagrāhamagṛhṇatām ||8||

9tamunmathya suśarmā'tha yuvatīmiva kāmukaḥ
|
syandanaṃ svaṃ samāropya prayayau śīghravāhanaḥ ||9||

10tasmin gṛhīte virathe virāṭe balavattare
|
prādravanta bhayānmatsyāstrigatairarditā bhṛśam ||10||

11teṣu santrasyamāneṣu kuntīputro yudhiṣṭhiraḥ
|
pratyabhāṣanmahābāhuṃ bhīmasenamarindamam ||11||

12matsyarājaḥ parāmṛṣṭastrigartena suśarmaṇā
|
taṃ mocaya mahābāho na gaccheddviṣatāṃ vaśam ||12||

13uṣitāḥ sma sukhaṃ sarve sarvakāmaiḥ supūjitāḥ
|
bhīmasena tvayā kāryā tasya vāsasya niṣkṛtiḥ||13||

14bhīmasena uvāca|ahamenaṃ paritrāsye śāsanāt tava pārthiva
|
paśya me sumahat karma yudhyataḥ saha śatrubhiḥ ||14||

15svabāhubalamāśritya tiṣṭha tvaṃ bhrātṛbhiḥ saha
|
ekāntamāśrito rājan paśya me'dya parākramam ||15||

16suskandho'yaṃ mahāvṛkṣo gadārūpa iva sthitaḥ
|
ahamenamapārujya drāvayiṣyāmi śātravān ||16||

17vaiśampāyana uvāca|taṃ mattamiva mātaṅgaṃ vīkṣamāṇaṃ vanaspatim
|
abravīdbhrātaraṃ vīraṃ dharmarājo yudhiṣṭhiraḥ ||17||

18mā bhīma sāhasaṃ kārṣīstiṣṭhatveṣa vanaspatiḥ
|
mā tvaṃ vṛkṣeṇa karmāṇi kurvāṇamatimānuṣam ||18||

19janāḥ samavabudhyeran bhīmo'yamiti bhārata
|
anyadevāyudhaṃ kiñcit pratipadyasva mānuṣam ||19||

20cāpaṃ vā yadi vā śaktiṃ nistriṃśaṃ vā paraśvadham
|
yadeva mānuṣaṃ bhīma bhavedanyairalakṣitam ||20||

21tadevāyudhamādāya mokṣayāśu mahīpatim
|
yamau ca cakrarakṣau te bhavitārau mahābalau ||21||

22sahitāḥ samare tatra matsyarājaṃ parīpsata
|
vaiśampāyana uvāca|evamuktastu vegena bhīmaseno mahābalaḥ ||22||

23gṛhītvā tu dhanuḥ śreṣṭhaṃ javena sumahājavaḥ
|
vyamuñcaccharavarṣāṇi satoya iva toyadaḥ ||23||

24taṃ bhīmo bhīmakarmāṇaṃ suśarmāṇamathādravat
|
virāṭaṃ samavīkṣyainaṃ tiṣṭha tiṣṭheti cāvadat ||24||

25suśarmā cintayāmāsa kālāntakayamopamam
|
tiṣṭha tiṣṭheti bhāṣantaṃ pṛṣṭhato rathapuṅgavaḥ ||25||

26paśyatāṃ sumahat karma mahadyuddha‌mupasthitam
|
parāvṛtto dhanurguhya suśarmā bhrātṛbhiḥ saha
|
nimeṣāntaramātreṇa bhīmasenena te rathāḥ ||26||

27rathānāṃ ca gajānāṃ ca vājināṃ ca sasādinām
|
sahasraśatasaṅghātāḥ śūrāṇāmugradhanvinām ||27||

28pātitā bhīmasenena virāṭasya samīpataḥ
|
pattayo nihatāsteṣāṃ gadāṃ gṛhya mahātmanā ||28||

29taddṛṣṭvā tādṛśaṃ yuddhaṃ suśarmā yuddhadurmadaḥ
|
cintayāmāsa manasā kiṃ śeṣaṃ hi balasya me
|
aparo dṛśyate sainye purā magno mahābale ||29||

30ākarṇapūrṇena tadā dhanuṣā pratyadṛśyata
|
suśarmā sāyakāṃstīkṣṇān kṣipate ca punaḥ punaḥ ||30||

31tataḥ samastāste sarve turagānabhyacodayan
|
divyamastraṃ vikurvāṇāstrigartān pratyamarṣaṇāḥ ||31||

32tānnivṛttarathān dṛṣṭvā pāṇḍavān sā mahācamūḥ
|
vairāṭiḥ paramakruddho yuyudhe paramādbhutam ||32||

33sahastramavadhīttatra kuntīputro yudhiṣṭhiraḥ
|
bhīmaḥ sapta sahasrāṇi yamalokamadarśayat ||33||

34nakulaścāpi saptaiva śatāni prāhiṇoccharaiḥ
|
śatāni trīṇi śūrāṇāṃ sahadevaḥ pratāpavān ||34||

35yudhiṣṭhirasamādiṣṭo nijaghne puruṣarṣabhaḥ
|
tato'bhyapatadatyugraḥ suśarmāṇamudāyudhaḥ ||35||

36hatvā tāṃ mahatīṃ senāṃ trigartānāṃ mahārathaḥ
|
tato yudhiṣṭhiro rājā tvaramāṇo mahārathaḥ ||36||

37abhipatya suśarmāṇaṃ śarairabhyāhanadbhṛśam
|
suśarmā'pi susaṃrabdhastvaramāṇo yudhiṣṭhiram ||37||

38avidhyannavabhirbāṇaiścaturbhiścaturo hayān
|
tato rājannāśukārī kuntīputro vṛkodaraḥ ||38||

39samāsādya suśarmāṇamaśvānasya vyapothayat
|
pṛṣṭhagopāṃśca tasyātha hatvā paramasāyakaiḥ ||39||

40athāsya sārathiṃ kruddho rathopasthādapātayat
|
cakrarakṣaśca śūro vai madirākṣo'tiviśrutaḥ ||40||

41samāyādvirathaṃ dṛṣṭvā trigartaṃ prāharat tadā
|
tato virāṭaḥ praskandya rathādatha suśarmaṇaḥ ||41||

42gadāṃ tasya parāmṛśya tamevābhyadravadbalī
|
sa cacāra gadāpāṇirvṛddho'pi taruṇo yathā ||42||

43palāyamānaṃ traigartaṃ dṛṣṭvā bhīmo'bhyabhāṣata
|
rājaputra nivartasva na te yuktaṃ palāyanam ||43||

44anena vīryeṇa kathaṃ gāstvaṃ prārthayase balāt
|
kathaṃ cānucarāṃstyaktvā śatrumadhye viṣīdasi ||44||

45ityuktaḥ sa tu pārthena suśarmā rathayūthapaḥ
|
tiṣṭha tiṣṭheti bhīmaṃ sa sahasā'bhyadravadvalī ||45||

46bhīmastu bhīmasaṃkāśo rathāt praskandya pāṇḍavaḥ
|
prādravattūrṇamavyagro jīvitepsuḥ suśarmaṇaḥ ||46||

47taṃ bhīmaseno dhāvantamabhyadhāvata vīryavān
|
trigartarājamādātuṃ siṃhaḥ kṣudramṛgaṃ yathā ||47||

48abhidrutya suśarmāṇaṃ keśapakṣe parāmṛśat
|
samudyamya tu roṣāt taṃ niṣpipeṣa mahītale ||48||

49padā mūrdhni mahābāhuḥ prāharadvilapiṣyataḥ
|
tasya jānuṃ dadau bhīmo jaghne cainamaratninā
|
sa mohamagamadrājā prahāravarapīḍitaḥ ||49||

50tasmin gṛhīte virathe trigartānāṃ mahārathe
|
abhajyata balaṃ sarvaṃ traigartaṃ tadbhayāturam ||50||

51nivartya gāstataḥ sarvāḥ pāṇḍuputrā mahārathāḥ
|
avajitya suśarmāṇaṃ dhanaṃ cādāya sarvaśaḥ ||51||

52svabāhubalasampannā hrīniṣevā yatavratāḥ
|
virāṭasya mahātmānaḥ parikleśavināśanāḥ ||52||

53sthitāḥ samakṣaṃ te sarve tvatha bhīmo'bhyabhāṣata||53||

54nāyaṃ pāpasamācāro matto jīvitumarhati
|
kiṃ tu śakyaṃ mayā kartuṃ yadrājā satataṃ ghṛṇī ||54||

55gale gṛhītvā rājānamānīya vivaśaṃ vaśam
|
tata enaṃ viceṣṭantaṃ badhvā pārtho vṛkodaraḥ ||55||

56rathamāropayāmāsa visaṃjñaṃ pāṃsuguṇṭhitam
|
abhyetya raṇamadhyasthamabhyagacchadyudhiṣṭhiram ||56||

57darśayāmāsa bhīmastu suśarmāṇaṃ narādhipam
|
provāca puruṣavyāghro bhīmamāhavaśobhinam ||57||

58taṃ rājā prāhasat dṛṣṭvā mucyatāṃ vai narādhamaḥ
|
evamukto'bravīdbhīmaḥ suśarmāṇaṃ mahābalam ||58||

59bhīma uvāca|jīvituṃ cecchase mūḍha hetuṃ me gadataḥ śṛṇu
|
dāso'smīti tvayā vācyaṃ saṃsatsu ca sabhāsu ca ||59||

60evaṃ te jīvitaṃ dadyāmeṣa yuddhajito vidhiḥ
|
tamuvāca tato jyeṣṭho bhrātā sapraṇayaṃ vacaḥ ||60||

61yudhiṣṭhira uvāca|muñca muñcādhamācāraṃ pramāṇaṃ yadi te vayam
|
dāsabhāvaṃ gato hyeṣa virāṭasya mahīpateḥ
|
adāso gaccha mukto'si maivaṃ kārṣīḥ kadācana ||61||
