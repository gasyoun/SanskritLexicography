Mahābhārata 16.3 (Mausalaparvan)

1vaiśampāyana uvāca|
kālīstrī pāṇḍurairdantaiḥ praviśya hasatī niśi
|
striyaḥ svapneṣu muṣṇantī dvārakāṃ paridhāvati||1||

2agrihotraniketeṣu vāstumadhyeṣu veśmasu
|
vṛṣṇyandhakānakhādanta svapne gṛdhrā bhayānakāḥ||2||

3alaṅkārāśca chatraṃ ca dhvajāśca kavacāni ca
|
hriyamāṇānyadṛśyanta rakṣobhiḥ subhayānakaiḥ||3||

4taccāgnidattaṃ kṛṣṇasya vajranābhamayomayam
|
divamācakrame cakraṃ vṛṣṇīnāṃ paśyatāṃ tadā||4||

5yuktaṃ rathaṃ divyamādityavarṇaṃ hayā haranpaśyato dārukasya
|
te sāgarasyopariṣṭādavartanmanojavāścaturo vājimukhyāḥ||5||

6tālaḥ suparṇaśca mahādhvajau tau supūjitau rāmajanārdanābhyām
|
uccairjahrurapsaraso divāniśaṃ vācaścocurgamyatāṃ tīrthayātrā||6||

7tato jigamiṣantaste vṛṣṇyandhakamahārathāḥ
|
sāntaḥpurāstadā tīrthayātrāmaicchannararṣabhāḥ||7||

8tato bhojyaṃ ca bhakṣyaṃ ca peyaṃ cāndhakavṛṣṇayaḥ
|
bahu nānāvidhaṃ cakrurmadya māṃsamanekaśaḥ||8||

9tataḥ sainikavargāśca niryayurnagarādbahiḥ
|
yānairaśvairgajaiścaiva śrīmantastigmatejasaḥ||9||

10tataḥ prabhāse nyavasanyathoddiṣṭaṃ yathāgṛham
|
prabhūtabhakṣyapeyāste sadārā yādavāstadā||10||

11niviṣṭhāṃstānniśamyātha samudrānte sa yogavit
|
jagāmāmantrya tānvīrānuddhavorthaviśāradaḥ||11||

12taṃ prasthitaṃ mahātmānamabhivādya kṛtāñjalim
|
jānanvināśaṃ vṛṣṇīnāṃ naicchadvārayituṃ hariḥ||12||

13tataḥ kālaparītāste vṛṣṇyandhakamahārathāḥ
|
apaśyannuddhavaṃ yātaṃ tejasā''vṛtya rodasī||13||

14brāhmaṇārtheṣu yatsiddhamannaṃ teṣāṃ mahātmanām
|
tadvānarebhyaḥ pradaduḥ surāgandhasamanvitam||14||

15tatastūryaśatākīrṇaṃ naṭanartakasaṅkulam
|
avartata mahāpānaṃ prabhāse tigmatejasām||15||

16kṛṣṇasya sannidhau rāmaḥ sahitaḥ kṛtavarmaṇā
|
apibadyuyudhānaśca gado babhrustathaiva ca||16||

17tataḥ pariṣado madhye yuyudhāno madotkaṭaḥ
|
abravītkṛtavarmāṇamavahāsyāvamanya ca||17||

18kaḥ kṣatriyo hanyamānaḥ suptānhanyānmṛtāniva
|
tanna mṛṣyanti hārdikya yādavā yattvayā kṛtam||18||

19ityukte yuyudhānena pūjayāmāsa tadvacaḥ
|
pradyumro rathināṃ śreṣṭho hārdikyamavamanya ca||19||

20tataḥ paramasaṅkruddhaḥ kṛtavarmā tamabravīt
|
nirdiśanniva sāvajñaṃ tadā savyena pāṇinā||20||

21bhūriśravāśchinnabāhuryuddhe prāyagatastvamā
|
vadhena sunṛśaṃsena kathaṃ vīreṇa pātitaḥ||21||

22iti tasya vacaḥ śrutvā keśavaḥ paravīrahā
|
tiryaksaroṣayā dṛṣṭyā vīkṣāñcakre sa manyumān||22||

23maṇiḥ syamantakaścaiva yaḥ sa satrājito'bhavat
|
tāṃ kathāṃ śrāvayāmāsa sātyakirmadhusūdanam||23||

24tacchrutvā keśavasyāṅkamagamadrudatī tadā
|
satyabhāmā prakupitā kopayantī janārdanam||24||

25tata utthāya sakrodhaḥ sātyakirvākyamabravīt
|
pañcānāṃ draupadeyānāṃ dhṛṣṭadyumnaśikhaṇḍinoḥ||25||

26eṣa gacchāmi padavīṃ satyena ca tathā śape
|
sauptike ye ca nihatāḥ suptā yena durātmanā||26||

27droṇaputrasahāyena pāpena kṛtavarmaṇā
|
samāptamāyurasyādya yaśaścaiva sumadhyame||27||

28ityevamuktvā khaḍgena keśavasya samīpataḥ
|
abhidrutya śiraḥ kruddhaściccheda kṛtavarmaṇaḥ||28||

29tathā'nyānapi nighnantaṃ yuyudhānaṃ samantataḥ
|
abhyadhāvaddhṛṣīkeśo vinivārayituṃ tadā||29||

30ekībhūtāstataḥ sarve kālaparyāyacoditāḥ
|
bhojāndhakā mahārāja śaineyaṃ paryavārayan||30||

31tāndṛṣṭvā patatastūrṇamabhikruddhājjanārdanaḥ
|
na cukrodha mahātejā jānankālasya paryayam||31||

32te tu pānamadāviṣṭāścoditāḥ kāladharmaṇā
|
yuyudhānamathābhyaghnannucchiṣṭairbhājanaistadā||32||

33hanyamāne tu śaineye kruddho rukmiṇinandanaḥ
|
tadanantaramāgacchanmokṣayiṣyan śineḥ sutam||33||

34sa bhojaiḥ saha saṃyuktaḥ sātyakiścāndhakaiḥ saha
|
vyāyacchamānau tau vīrau bāhudraviṇaśālinau||34||

35bahutvānnihatau tatra ubhau kṛṣṇasya paśyataḥ
|
hataṃ dṛṣṭvā ca śaineyaṃ putraṃ ca yadunandanaḥ||35||

36erakānāṃ tato muṣṭiṃ kopājjagrāha keśavaḥ
|
tadabhūnmusalaṃ ghoraṃ vajrakalpamayomayam||36||

37jaghāna kṛṣṇastāṃstena ye ye pramukhato'bhavan
|
tato'ndhakāśca bhojāśca śaineyā vṛṣṇayastathā||37||

38jaghnuranyonyamākrande musalaiḥ kālacoditāḥ
|
yasteṣāmerakāṃ kaścijjagrāha kupito nṛpa||38||

39vajrabhūteva sā rājannadṛśyata tadā vibho
|
tṛṇaṃ ca musalībhūtamapi tatra vyadṛśyata||39||

40brahmadaṇḍakṛtaṃ sarvamiti tadviddhi pārthiva
|
avidhyān vidhya te rājan prakṣipanti sma yattṛṇam||40||

41tadvajrabhūtaṃ musalaṃ vyadṛśyata tadā dṛḍham
|
avadhītpitaraṃ putraḥ pitā putraṃ ca bhārata||41||

42mattāḥ paripatanti sma yodhayantaḥ parasparam
|
pataṃgā iva cāgnau te nipetuḥ kukurāndhakāḥ||42||

43nāsītpalāyane buddhirvadhyamānasya kasyacit
|
tatrāpaśyanmahābāhurjānankālasya paryayam||43||

44musalaṃ samavaṣṭabhya tasthau sa madhusūdanaḥ
|
sāmbaṃ ca nihataṃ dṛṣṭvā cārudeṣṇaṃ ca mādhavaḥ||44||

45pradyumnaṃ cāniruddhaṃ ca tataścukrodha bhārata
|
gadaṃ vīkṣya śayānaṃ ca bhṛśaṃ kopasamanvitaḥ||45||

46sa niḥśeṣaṃ tadā cakre śārṅgacakragadādharaḥ
|
tannighnantaṃ mahātejā babhruḥ parapurañjayaḥ||46||

47dārukaścaiva dāśārhamūcaturyannibodha tat
|
bhagavannihatāḥ sarve tvayā bhūyiṣṭhaśo narāḥ
|
rāmasya padamanviccha tatra gacchāma yatra saḥ||47||
