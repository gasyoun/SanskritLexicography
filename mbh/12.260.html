Mahābhārata 12.260 (Śāntiparvan)

1yudhiṣṭhira uvāca|
sūkṣmaṃ sādhu samāddiṣṭaṃ bhavatā dharmalakṣaṇam
|
pratibhā tvasti me kācittāṃ brūyāmanumānataḥ ||1||

2bhūyāṃso hṛdaye ye me praśnāste vyāhṛtāstvayā
|
idaṃ tvanyatpravakṣyāmi na rājannigrahādiva ||2||

3imāni hi prāṇayanti sṛjantyuttārayanti ca
|
na dharmaḥ paripāṭhena śakyo bhārata veditum ||3||

4anyo dharmaḥ samasthasya viṣamasthasya cāparaḥ
|
āpadastu kathaṃ śakyāḥ paripāṭhena veditum ||4||

5sadācāro mato dharmaḥ santastvācāralakṣaṇāḥ
|
sādhyāsādhyaṃ kathaṃ śakyaṃ sadācāro hyalakṣaṇaḥ ||5||

6dṛśyate hi dharmārūpeṇādharmaṃ prākṛtaścaran
|
dharmaṃ cādharmarūpeṇa kaścidaprākṛtaścaran ||6||

7punarasya pramāṇaṃ hi nirdiṣṭaṃ śāstrakovidaiḥ
|
vedavādāścānuyugaṃ hrasantītīha naḥ śrutam ||7||

8anye kṛtayuge dharmāstretāyāṃ dvāpare pare
|
anye kaliyuge dharmā yathāśakti kṛtā iva ||8||

9āmnāyavacanaṃ satyamityayaṃ lokasaṃgrahaḥ
|
āmnāyebhyaḥ punarvedāḥ prasṛtāḥ sarvatomukhāḥ ||9||

10te cetsarvapramāṇaṃ vai pramāṇaṃ hyatra vidyate
|
pramāṇe'pyapramāṇena viruddha śāstratā kutaḥ ||10||

11dharmasya kriyamāṇasya balavadbhirdurātmabhiḥ
|
yā yā vikriyate saṃsthā tataḥ sāpi praṇaśyati ||11||

12vidma caivaṃ na vā vidma śakyaṃ vā vedituṃ na vā
|
aṇīyān kṣuradhārāyā garīyānapi parvatāt ||12||

13gandharvanagarākāraḥ prathamaṃ sampradṛśyate
|
anvīkṣyamāṇaḥ kavibhiḥ punargacchatyadarśanam ||13||

14nipānānīva gobhyo'pi kṣetre kulye ca bhārata
|
smṛtirhi śāśvate dharmo viprahīṇo na dṛśyate ||14||

15kāmādanyecchayā cānye kāraṇairaparaistathā
|
asanto'pi vṛthācāraṃ bhajante bahavo'pare ||15||

16dharmo bhavati sa kṣipraṃ pralāpastveva sādhuṣu
|
athaitānāhurunmattānapi cāvahasantyuta ||16||

17mahājanā hyupāvṛttā rājadharmaṃ samāśritāḥ
|
na hi sarvahitaḥ kaścidācāraḥ sampravartate ||17||

18tevaivānyaḥ prabhavati so'paraṃ bādhate punaḥ
|
dṛśyate caiva sa punastulyarūpo yadṛcchayā ||18||

19yenaivānyaḥ prabhavati so'parānapi bādhate
|
ācārāṇāmanaikāgryaṃ sarveṣāmupalakṣayet ||19||

20cirābhipannaḥ kavibhiḥ pūrvaṃ dharma udāhṛtaḥ
|
tenācāreṇa pūrveṇa saṃsthā bhavati śāśvatī ||20||
