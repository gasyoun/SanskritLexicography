Mahābhārata 3.16 (Āraṇyakaparvan)

1vāsudeva uvāca|
tāṃ tūpayāto rājendra śālvaḥ saubhapatistadā
|
prabhūtanaranāgena balenopaviveśa ha||1||

2same niviṣṭā sā senā prabhūtasalilāśaye
|
caturaṅgabalopetā śālvarājābhipālitā||2||

3varjayitvā śmaśānāni devatāyatanāni ca
|
valmīkāṃścaityavṛkṣāṃśca tanniviṣṭamabhūdbalam||3||

4anīkānāṃ vibhāgena panthānaḥ saṃvṛtā bhavan
|
pravaṇāya ca naivāsañcchālvasya śibire nṛpa||4||

5sarvāyudhasamopetaṃ sarvaśastraviśāradam
|
rathanāgāśvakalilaṃ padātidhvajasaṅkulam||5||

6tuṣṭapuṣṭabalopetaṃ vīralakṣaṇalakṣitam
|
vicitradhvajasannāhaṃ vicitrarathakārmukam||6||

7saṃniveśya ca kauravya dvārakāyāṃ nararṣabha
|
abhisārayāmāsa tadā vegena patagendravat||7||

8tadāpatantaṃ saṃdṛśya balaṃ śālvapatestadā
|
niryāya yodhayāmāsuḥ kumārā vṛṣṇinandanāḥ||8||

9asahanto'bhiyānaṃ tacchālvarājasya kaurava
|
cārudeṣṇaśca sāmbaśca pradyumnaśca mahārathaḥ||9||

10te rathairdaṃśitāḥ sarve vicitrābharaṇadhvajāḥ
|
saṃsaktāḥ śālvarājasya bahubhiryodhapuṅgavaiḥ||10||

11gṛhītvā kārmukaṃ sāmbaḥ śālvasya sacivaṃ raṇe
|
yodhayāmāsa saṃhṛṣṭaḥ kṣemavṛddhiṃ camūpatim||11||

12tasya bāṇamayaṃ varṣaṃ jāmbavatyāḥ suto mahat
|
mumoca bharataśreṣṭha yathā varṣaṃ sahasradṛk||12||

13tadbāṇavarṣaṃ tumulaṃ viṣehe sa camūpatiḥ
|
kṣemavṛddhirmahārāja himavāniva niścalaḥ||13||

14tataḥ sāmbāya rājendra kṣemavṛddhirapi svayam
|
mumoca māyāvihitaṃ śarajālaṃ mahattaram||14||

15tato māyāmayaṃ jālaṃ māyayaiva vidīrya saḥ
|
sāmbaḥ śarasahasreṇa rathamasyābhyavarṣata||15||

16tataḥ sa viddhaḥ sāmbena kṣemavṛddhiścamūpatiḥ
|
apāyājjavanairaśvaiḥ sāmbabāṇaprapīḍitaḥ||16||

17tasminvipradrute krūre śālvasyātha camūpatau
|
vegavānnāma daiteyaḥ sutaṃ me'bhyadravadbalī||17||

18abhipannastu rājendra sāmbo vṛṣṇikulodvahaḥ
|
vegaṃ vegavato rājaṃstasthau vīro vidhārayan||18||

19sa vegavati kaunteya sāmbo vegavatīṃ gadām
|
cikṣepa tarasā vīro vyāvidhdyaḥ satyavikramaḥ||19||

20tayā tvabhihato rājanvegavānnyapatadbhuvi
|
vātarugṇa iva kṣuṇṇo jīrṇamūlo vanaspatiḥ||20||

21tasminvinihate vīre gadānunne mahāsure
|
praviśya mahatīṃ senāṃ yodhayāmāsa me sutaḥ||21||

22cārudeṣṇena saṃsakto vivindhyo nāma dānavaḥ
|
mahārathaḥ samājñāto mahārāja mahādhanuḥ||22||

23tataḥ sutumulaṃ yuddhaṃ cārudeṣṇavivindhyayoḥ
|
vṛtravāsavayo rājanyathā pūrvaṃ tathābhavat||23||

24anyonyasyābhisaṅkruddhāvanyonyaṃ jaghnatuḥ śaraiḥ
|
vinadantau mahārāvānsiṃhāviva mahābalau||24||

25raukmiṇeyastato bāṇamagnyarkopamavarcasam
|
abhimantrya mahāstreṇa saṃdadhe śatrunāśanam||25||

26sa vivindhyāya sakrodhaḥ samāhūya mahārathaḥ
|
cikṣepa me suto rājansa gatāsurathāpatat||26||

27vivindhyaṃ nihataṃ dṛṣṭvā tāṃ ca vikṣobhitāṃ camūm
|
kāmagena sasaubhena śālvaḥ punarupāgamat||27||

28tato vyākulitaṃ sarvaṃ dvārakāvāsi tadbalam
|
dṛṣṭvā śālvaṃ mahābāho saubhasthaṃ nṛpate tadā||28||

29tato niryāya kauravyam avasthāpya ca tadbalam
|
ānartānāṃ mahārāja pradyumno vākyamabravīt||29||

30sarve bhavantastiṣṭhantu sarve paśyantu māṃ yudhi
|
nivārayantaṃ saṅgrāme balātsaubhaṃ sarājakam||30||

31ahaṃ saubhapateḥ senāmāyasairbhujagairiva
|
dhanurbhujavinirmuktairnāśayāmyadya yādavāḥ||31||

32āśvasadhvaṃ na bhīḥ kāryā saubharāḍadya naśyati
|
mayābhipanno duṣṭātmā sasaubho vinaśiṣyati||32||

33evaṃ bruvati saṃhṛṣṭe pradyumne pāṇḍunandana
|
dhiṣṭitaṃ tadbalaṃ vīra yuyudhe ca yathāsukham||33||
