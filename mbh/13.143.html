Mahābhārata 13.143 (Anuśāsanaparvan)

1umovāca
|
bhagavan bhaganetraghna pūṣṇo dantanipātana
|
dakṣakratuhara tryakṣa saṃśayo me mahānayam ||1||

2cāturvarṇyaṃ bhagavatā pūrvaṃ sṛṣṭaṃ svayaṃbhuvā
|
kena karmavipākena vaiśyo gacchati śūdratām ||2||

3vaiśyo vā kṣatriyaḥ kena dvijo vā kṣatriyo bhavet
|
pratilomaḥ kathaṃ deva śakyo dharmo nivartitum ||3||

4kena vā karmaṇā vipraḥ śūdrayonau prajāyate
|
kṣatriyaḥ śūdratāmeti kena vā karmaṇā vibho ||4||

5etanme saṃśayaṃ deva vada bhūtapate'nagha
|
trayo varṇāḥ prakṛtyeha kathaṃ brāhmaṇyamāpnuyuḥ ||5||

6maheśvara uvāca|
brāhmaṇyaṃ devi duṣprāpaṃ nisargādbrāhmaṇaḥ śubhe
|
kṣatriyo vaiśyaśūdro vā nisargāditi me matiḥ ||6||

7karmaṇā duṣkṛteneha sthānādbhraśyati vai dvijaḥ
|
jyeṣṭhaṃ varṇamanuprāpya tasmādrakṣeta vai dvijaḥ ||7||

8sthito brāhmaṇadharmeṇa brāhmaṇyamupajīvati
|
kṣatriyo vā'tha vaiśyo vā brahmabhūyaṃ sa gacchati ||8||

9yastu vipratvamutsṛjya kṣātraṃ dharmaṃ niṣevate
|
brāhmaṇyātsa paribhraṣṭaḥ kṣatrayonau prajāyate ||9||

10vaiśyakarma ca yo vipro lobhamohavyapāśrayaḥ
|
brāhmaṇyaṃ durlabhaṃ prāpya karotyalpamatiḥ sadā ||10||

11sa dvijo vaiśyatāmeti vaiśyo vā śūdratāmiyāt
|
svadharmātpracyuto viprastataḥ śūdrattvamāpnute ||11||

12tatrāsau nirayaṃ prāpto varṇabhraṣṭo bahiṣkṛtaḥ
|
brahmalokātparibhraṣṭaḥ śūdraḥ samupajāyate ||12||

13kṣatriyo vā mahābhāge vaiśyo vā dharmacāriṇi
|
svāni karmāṇyapāhāya śūdrakarma niṣevate ||13||

14svasthānātsa paribhraṣṭo varṇasaṅkaratāṃ gataḥ
|
brāhmaṇaḥ kṣatriyo vaiśyaḥ śūdratvaṃ yāti tādṛśaḥ ||14||

15yastu buddhaḥ svadharmeṇa jñānavijñānavān śuciḥ
|
dharmajño dharmanirataḥ sa dharmaphalamaśnute ||15||

16idaṃ caivāparaṃ devi brahmaṇā samudāhṛtam
|
adhyātmaṃ naiṣṭhikaṃ sadbhirdharmakāmairniṣevyate ||16||

17ugrānnaṃ garhitaṃ devi gaṇānnaṃ śrāddhasūtakam
|
duṣṭānnaṃ naiva bhoktavyaṃ śūdrānnaṃ naiva karhicit ||17||

18śūdrānnaṃ garhitaṃ devi sadā devairmahātmabhiḥ
|
pitāmahasukhotsṛṣṭaṃ pramāṇamiti me matiḥ ||18||

19śūdrānnenāvaśeṣeṇa jaṭhare yo mriyeddvijaḥ
|
āhitāgnistathā yajvā sa śūdragatibhāgbhavet ||19||

20tena śūdrānnaśeṣeṇa brahmasthānāda‌pākṛtaḥ
|
brāhmaṇaḥ śūdratāmeti nāsti tatra vicāraṇā ||20||

21yasyānnenāvaśeṣeṇa jaṭhare yo mriyeddvijaḥ
|
tāṃ tāṃ yoniṃ vrajedvipro yasyānnamupajīvati ||21||

22brāhmaṇatvaṃ śubhaṃ prāpya durlabhaṃ yo'vamanyate
|
abhojyānnāni cāśnāti sa dvijatvātpateta vai ||22||

23surāpo brahmahā kṣudraścoro bhagnavrato'śuciḥ
|
svādhyāyavarjitaḥ pāpo lubdho naikṛtikaḥ śaṭhaḥ ||23||

24avratī vṛṣalībhartā kuṃḍāśī somavikrayī
|
nihīnasevī vipro hi patati brahmayonitaḥ ||24||

25gurutalpī gurudrohī gurukutsāratiśca yaḥ
|
brahmaviccāpi patati brāhmaṇo brahmayonitaḥ ||25||

26ebhistu karmabhirdevi śubhairācaritaistathā
|
śūdro brāhmaṇatāṃ yāti vaiśyaḥ kṣatriyatāṃ vrajet ||26||

27śūdrakarmāṇi sarvāṇi yathānyāyaṃ yathāvidhi
|
śuśrūṣāṃ paricaryāṃ ca jyeṣṭhe varṇe prayatnataḥ ||27||

28kuryādavimanāḥ śūdraḥ satataṃ satpathe sthitaḥ
|
devadvijātisatkartā sarvātithyakṛtavrataḥ ||28||

29ṛtukālābhigāmī ca niyato niyatāśanaḥ
|
cokṣaścokṣajanānveṣī śeṣānnakṛtabhojanaḥ ||29||

30vṛthāmāṃsaṃ na bhuñjīta śūdro vaiśyatvamṛcchati
|
ṛtavāganahaṃvādī nirdvandvaḥ śamakovidaḥ ||30||

31yajate nityayajñaiśca svādhyāyaparamaḥ śuciḥ
|
dānto brāhmaṇasatkartā sarvavarṇabubhūṣakaḥ ||31||

32gṛhasthavratamātiṣṭhan dvikālakṛtabhojanaḥ
|
śeṣāśī vijitāhāro niṣkāmo nirahaṃvadaḥ ||32||

33agnihotramupāsaṃśca juhvānaśca yathāvidhi
|
sarvātithyamupātiṣṭhan śeṣānnakṛtabhojanaḥ ||33||

34tretāgnimantravihito vaiśyo bhavati vai dvijaḥ
|
sa vaiśyaḥ kṣatriyakule śucau mahati jāyate ||34||

35sa vaiśyaḥ kṣatriyo jāto janmaprabhṛti saṃskṛtaḥ
|
upanīto vrataparo dvijo bhavati satkṛtaḥ ||35||

36dadāti yajate yajñaiḥ samṛddhairāptada‌kṣiṇaiḥ
|
adhītya svargamanvicchaṃstretāgniśaraṇaḥ sadā ||36||

37ārtahastaprado nityaṃ prajā dharmeṇa pālayan
|
satyaḥ satyāni kurute nityaṃ yaḥ sukhadarśanaḥ ||37||

38dharmadaṇḍo na nirdaṇḍo dharmakāryānuśāsakaḥ
|
yantritaḥ kāryakaraṇaiḥ ṣaḍ‌bhāgakṛtalakṣaṇaḥ ||38||

39grāmyadharmaṃ na seveta svacchandenārthakovidaḥ
|
ṛtukāle tu dharmātmā patnīmupaśayetsadā ||39||

40sadopavāsī niyataḥ svādhyāyanirataḥ śuciḥ
|
barhiṣkāntarite nityaṃ śayāno'gnigṛhe sadā ||40||

41sarvātithyaṃ trivargasya kurvāṇaḥ sumanāḥ sadā
|
śūdrāṇāṃ cānnakāmānāṃ nityaṃ siddhamiti bruvan ||41||

42arthādvā yadi vā kāmānna kiñcidupalakṣayet
|
pitṛdevātithikṛte sādhanaṃ kurute ca yaḥ ||42||

43svaveśmani yathānyāyamupāste bhaikṣyameva ca
|
trikālamagnihotraṃ ca juhvāno vai yathāvidhi ||43||

44gobrāhmaṇahitārthāya raṇe cābhisukho hataḥ
|
tretāgnimantrapūtātmā samāviśya dvijo bhavet ||44||

45jñānavijñānasaṃpannaḥ saṃskṛto vedapāragaḥ
|
vipro bhavati dharmātmā kṣatriyaḥ svena karmaṇā ||45||

46etaiḥ karmaphalairdevi nyūnajātikulodbhavaḥ
|
śūdropyāgamasaṃpanno dvijo bhavati saṃskṛtaḥ ||46||

47brāhmaṇo vāpyasadvṛttaḥ sarvasaṅkarabhojanaḥ
|
brāhmaṇyaṃ sa samutsṛjya śūdro bhavati tādṛśaḥ ||47||

48karmabhiḥ śucibhirdevi śuddhātmā vijitendriyaḥ
|
śūdro'pi dvijavatsevya iti brahmā'bravītsvayam ||48||

49svabhāvaḥ karma ca śubhaṃ yatra śūdre'pi tiṣṭhati
|
viśiṣṭaḥ sa dvijātervai viśeya iti me matiḥ ||49||

50na yonirnāpi saṃskāro na śrutaṃ na ca santatiḥ
|
kāraṇāni dvijatvasya vṛttameva tu kāraṇam ||50||

51sarvo'yaṃ brāhmaṇo loke vṛttena tu vidhīyate
|
vṛtte sthitastu śūdropi brāhmaṇatvaṃ niyacchati ||51||

52brāhmaḥ svabhāvaḥ suśroṇi samaḥ sarvatra me matiḥ
|
nirguṇaṃ nirmalaṃ brahma yatra tiṣṭhati sa dvijaḥ ||52||

53ete yoniphalā devi sthānabhāganidarśakāḥ
|
svayaṃ ca varadenoktā brahmaṇā sṛjatā prajāḥ ||53||

54brāhmaṇo'pi mahatkṣetraṃ loke carati pādavat
|
yattatra bījaṃ vapati sā kṛṣiḥ pretya bhāvini ||54||

55vighasāśinā sadā bhāvyaṃ satpathālaṃbinā tathā
|
brāhyaṃ hi mārgamākramya vartitavyaṃ bubhūṣatā ||55||

56saṃhitādhyāyinā bhāvyaṃ gṛhe vai gṛhamedhinā
|
nityaṃ svādhyāyinā bhāvyaṃ na cādhyayanajīvinā ||56||

57evaṃbhūto hi yo vipraḥ satpathaṃ satpathe sthitaḥ
|
āhitāgniradhīyāno brahmabhūyāya kalpate ||57||

58brāhmaṇyaṃ devi saṃprāpya rakṣitavyaṃ yatātmanā
|
yonipratigrahādānaiḥ karmabhiśca śucismite ||58||

59etatte guhyamākhyātaṃ yathā śūdro bhavaddvijaḥ
|
brāhmaṇo vā cyuto dharmādyathā śūdratvamāpnute ||59||
