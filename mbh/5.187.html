Mahābhārata 5.187 (Udyogaparvan)

1bhīṣma uvāca|
tataste tāpasāḥ sarve tapase dhṛtaniścayām
|
dṛṣṭvā nyavartayaṃstāta kiṃ kāryamiti cābruvan ||1||

2tānuvāca tataḥ kanyā tapovṛddhānṛṣīṃstadā
|
nirākṛtā'smi bhīṣmeṇa bhraṃśitā patidharmataḥ ||2||

3vadhārthaṃ tasya dīkṣā me na lokārthaṃ tapodhanāḥ
|
nihatya bhīṣmaṃ gaccheyaṃ śāntimityeva niścayaḥ||3||

4yatkṛte duḥkhavasatimimāṃ prāptā'smi śāśvatīm
|
patilokādvihīnā ca naiva strī na pumāniha ||4||

5nāhatvā yudhi gāṅgeyaṃ nivartiṣye tapodhanāḥ
|
eṣa me hṛdi saṃkalpo yadidaṃ kathitaṃ mayā ||5||

6strībhāve parinirviṇṇā puṃstvārthe kṛtaniścayā
|
bhīṣme praticikīrṣāmi nāsmi vāryeti vai punaḥ ||6||

7tāṃ devo darśayāmāsa śūlapāṇirumāpatiḥ
|
madhye teṣāṃ maharṣīṇāṃ svena rūpeṇa tāpasīm ||7||

8chandyamānā vareṇātha sā vavre matparājayam
|
haniṣyasīti tāṃ devaḥ pratyuvāca manasvinīm ||8||

9tataḥ sā punarevātha kanyā rudramuvāca ha
|
upapadyet kathaṃ deva striyā yudhi jayo mama ||9||

10strībhāvena ca me gāḍhaṃ manaḥ śāntamumāpate
|
pratiśrutaśca bhūteśa tvayā bhīṣmaparājayaḥ ||10||

11yathā sa satyo bhavati tathā kuru vṛṣadhvaja
|
yathā hanyāṃ samāgamya bhīṣmaṃ śāntanavaṃ yudhi ||11||

12tāmuvāca mahādevaḥ kanyāṃ kila vṛṣadhvajaḥ
|
na me vāganṛtaṃ prāha satyaṃ bhadre bhaviṣyati ||12||

13haniṣyasi raṇe bhīṣmaṃ puruṣatvaṃ ca lapsyase
|
smariṣyasi ca tat sarvaṃ dehamanyaṃ gatā satī ||13||

14drupadasya kule jātā bhaviṣyasi mahārathaḥ
|
śīghrāstraścitrayodhī ca bhaviṣyasi susaṃmataḥ ||14||

15yathoktameva kalyāṇi sarvametadbhaviṣyati
|
bhaviṣyasi pumān paścātkasmāccitkālaparyayāt ||15||

16evamuktvā mahādevaḥ kapardī vṛṣabhadhvajaḥ
|
paśyatāmeva viprāṇāṃ tatraivāntaradhīyata ||16||

17tataḥ sā paśyatāṃ teṣāṃ maharṣīṇāmaninditā
|
samāhṛtya vanāttasmāt kāṣṭhāni varavarṇinī ||17||

18citāṃ kṛtvā sumahatīṃ pradāya ca hutāśanam
|
pradīpte'gnau mahārāja roṣadīptena cetasā ||18||

19uktvā bhīṣmavadhāyeti praviveśa hutāśanam
|
jyeṣṭhā kāśisutā rājan yamunāmabhito nadīm ||19||
