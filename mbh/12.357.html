Mahābhārata 12.357 (Śāntiparvan)

1bhīṣma uvāca|
sa vanāni vicitrāṇi tīrthāni ca sarāṃsi ca
|
abhigacchan krameṇa sma kañcinmunimupasthitaḥ||1||

2taṃ sa tena yathoddiṣṭaṃ nāgaṃ vipreṇa brāhmaṇaḥ
|
paryapṛcchadyathānyāyaṃ śrutvaiva ca jagāma saḥ||2||

3so'bhigamya yathānyāyaṃ nāgāyatanamarthavit
|
proktavānahamasmīti bhoḥśabdālaṃkṛtaṃ vacaḥ||3||

4tattasya vacanaṃ śrutvā rūpiṇī dharmavatsalā
|
darśayāmāsa taṃ vipraṃ nāgapatnī pativratā||4||

5sā tasmai vidhivatpūjāṃ cakre dharmaparāyaṇā
|
svāgatenāgataṃ kṛtvā kiṃ karomīti cābravīt||5||

6brāhmaṇa uvāca|
viśrānto'bhyarcitaścāsmi bhavatyā ślakṣṇayā girā
|
draṣṭumicchāmi bhavati devaṃ nāgamanuttamam||6||

7etaddhi paraṃ kāryametānme paramepsitam
|
anena cārthenāsmyadya saṃprāpta: pannāgāśramam||7||

8nāgabhāryovāca |
āryaḥ sūryarathaṃ voḍhuṃ gato'sau māsacārikaḥ
|
saptāṣṭabhirdinairvipra darśayiṣyatyasaṃśayam||8||

9etadviditamāryasya vivāsakaraṇaṃ tava
|
bharturbhavatu kiñcānyat kriyatāṃ tadvadasva me||9||

10brāhmaṇa uvāca|
anena niścayenāhaṃ sādhvi saṃprāptavāniha
|
pratīkṣannāgamaṃ devi vatsyāpyasminmahāvane||10||

11saṃprāptasyaiva cāvyagramāvedyo'hamihāgataḥ
|
mamābhigamanaṃ prāpto vācyaśca vacanaṃ tvayā||11||

12ahamapyatra vatsyāmi gomatyāḥ puline śubhe
|
kālaṃ parimitāhāro yathoktaṃ paripālayan||12||

13tataḥ sa viprastāṃ nāgīṃ samādhāya punaḥ punaḥ
|
tadeva pulinaṃ nadyāḥ prayayau brāhmaṇarṣabhaḥ||13||
