Mahābhārata 7.41 (Droṇaparvan)

1sañjaya uvāca|
so'tigarjan dhanuṣpāṇirjyāṃ vikarṣan punaḥ punaḥ
|
tayormahātmanostūrṇaṃ rathāntaramavāpatat||1||

2so'vidhyaddaśabhirbāṇairabhimanyuṃ durāsadam
|
sacchatradhvajayantāraṃ sāśvamāśu smayanniva||2||

3pitṛpaitāmahaṃ karma kurvāṇamatimānuṣam
|
dṛṣṭvārditaṃ śaraiḥ kārṇīṃ tvadīyā hṛṣitā bhavan||3||

4tasyābhimanyurāyamya smayannekena patriṇā
|
śiraḥ pracyāvayāmāsa tadrathāt prāpatadbhuvi||4||

5karṇikāramivādhūtaṃ vātenāpatitaṃ nagāt
|
bhrātaraṃ nihataṃ dṛṣṭvā rājan karṇo vyathāṃ yayau||5||

6vimukhīkṛtya karṇaṃ tu saubhadraḥ kaṅkapatribhiḥ
|
anyānapi maheṣvāsāṃstūrṇamevābhidudruve||6||

7tatastadvitatataṃ sainyaṃ hastyaśvarathapattimat
|
kruddho'bhimanyurabhinattigmatejā mahārathaḥ||7||

8karṇastu bahubhirbāṇairardyamāno'bhimanyunā
|
apāyājjavanairaśvaistato'nīkamabhajyata||8||

9śalabhairiva cākāśe dhārābhiriva cāvṛte
|
abhimanyoḥ śarai rājanna prājñāyata kiṃcana||9||

10tāvakānāṃ tu yodhānāṃ vadhyatāṃ niśitaiḥ śaraiḥ
|
anyatra saindhavādrājanna sma kaścidatiṣṭhata||10||

11saubhadrastu tataḥ śaṃkhaṃ pradhmāpya puruṣarṣabhaḥ
|
śīghramabhyapatat senāṃ bhāratīṃ bharatarṣabha||11||

12sa kakṣe'gnirivotsṛṣṭo nirdahaṃstarasā ripūn
|
madhyaṃ bhāratasainyānāmārjuniḥ paryavartata||12||

13rathanāgāśvamanujānardayanniśitaiḥ śaraiḥ
|
saṃpraviśyākarodbhūmiṃ kabandhagaṇasaṃkulām||13||

14saubhadracāpaprabhavairnikṛttāḥ parameṣubhiḥ
|
svānevābhimukhān ghnantaḥ prādravan jīvitārthinaḥ||14||

15te ghorā raudrakarmāṇo vipāṭhā bahavaḥ śitāḥ
|
nighnanto rathanāgāśvān jagmurāśu vasuṃdharām||15||

16sāyudhāḥ sāṃgulitrāṇāḥ sagadāḥ sāṅgadā raṇe
|
dṛśyante bāhavaśchinnā hemābharaṇabhūṣitāḥ||16||

17śarāścāpāni khaḍgāśca śarīrāṇi śirāṃsi ca
|
sakuṇḍalāni sragvīṇi bhūmāvāsan sahasraśaḥ||17||

18sopaskarairadhiṣṭhānairīṣādaṇḍaiśca bandhuraiḥ
|
akṣairvimathitaiścakrairbahudhā patitairyugaiḥ||18||

19śakticāpāsibhiścaiva patitaiśca mahādhvajaiḥ
|
carmacāpaśaraiścaiva vyavakīrṇaiḥ samantataḥ||19||

20nihataiḥ kṣatriyairaśvairvāraṇaiśca viśāṃpate
|
agamyarūpā pṛthivī kṣaṇenāsīt sudāruṇā||20||

21vadhyatāṃ rājaputrāṇāṃ krandatāmitaretaram
|
prādurāsīnmahāśabdo bhīrūṇāṃ bhayavardhanaḥ||21||

22sa śabdo bharataśreṣṭha diśaḥ sarvā vyanādayat
|
saubhadraścādravat senāṃ ghnan varāśvarathadvipān||22||

23kakṣamagnirivotsṛṣṭo nirdahaṃstarasā ripūn
|
madhye bhāratasainyānāmārjuniḥ pratyadṛśyata||23||

24vicarantaṃ diśaḥ sarvāḥ pradiśaścāpi bhārata
|
taṃ tadā nānupaśyāmaḥ sainye ca rajasā''vṛte||24||

25ādadānaṃ gajāśvānāṃ nṛṇāṃ cāyūṃṣi bhārata
|
kṣaṇena bhūyaḥ paśyāmaḥ sūryaṃ madhyaṃdine yathā||25||

26abhimanyuṃ mahārāja pratapantaṃ dviṣadgaṇān
|
sa vāsavasamaḥ saṃkhye vāsavasyātmajātmajaḥ
|
abhimanyurmahārāja sainyamadhye vyarocata||26||
