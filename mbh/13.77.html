Mahābhārata 13.77 (Anuśāsanaparvan)

1vaiśampāyana uvāca|
tato yudhiṣṭhiro rājā bhūyaḥ śāntanavaṃ nṛpam
|
godānavistaraṃ dharmānpapraccha vinayānvitaḥ ||1||

2yudhiṣṭhira uvāca|
gopradānaguṇānsamyak punarme brūhi bhārata
|
na hi tṛpyāmyahaṃ vīra śṛṇvāno'mṛtamīdṛśam ||2||

3vaiśampāyana uvāca|
ityukto dharmarājena tadā śāntanavo nṛpaḥ
|
samyagāha guṇāṃstasmai gopradānasya kevalān ||3||

4bhīṣma uvāca|
vatsalāṃ guṇasaṃpannāṃ taruṇīṃ vastrasaṃyutām
|
datvedṛśīṃ gāṃ viprāya sarvapāpaiḥ pramucyate ||4||

5asuryā nāma te lokā gāṃ dattvā tānna gacchati
|
pītodakāṃ jagdhatṛṇāṃ naṣṭakṣīrāṃ nirindriyām ||5||

6jarārogopasaṃpannāṃ jīrṇāṃ vāpīmivājalām
|
dattvā tamaḥ praviśati dvijaṃ kleśena yojayet ||6||

7ruṣṭā duṣṭā vyādhitā durbalā vā no dātavyā yāśca mūlyairadattaiḥ
|
kleśairvipraṃ yo'phalaiḥ saṃyunakti tasyāvīryāścāphalāścaiva lokāḥ ||7||

8balānvitāḥ śīlavayopapannāḥ sarve praśaṃsanti sugandhavatyaḥ
|
yathā hi gaṅgā saritāṃ variṣṭhā tathārjunīnāṃ kapilā variṣṭhā ||8||

9yudhiṣṭhira uvāca|
kasmātsamāne bahulāpradāne sadbhiḥ praśastaṃ kapilāpradānam
|
viśeṣamicchāmi mahāprabhāvaṃ śrotuṃ samartho'smi bhavānpravaktum ||9||

10bhīṣma uvāca|
vṛddhānāṃ bruvatāṃ tāta śrutaṃ me yatpurātanam
|
vakṣyāmi tadaśeṣeṇa rohiṇyo nirmitā yathā ||10||

11prajā sṛjeti cādiṣṭaḥ pūrvaṃ dakṣaḥ svayaṃbhuvā
|
asṛjadvṛttimevāgre prajānāṃ hitakāmyayā ||11||

12yathā hyamṛtamāśritya vartayanti divaukasaḥ
|
tathā vṛttiṃ samāśritya vartayanti prajā vibho ||12||

13acarebhyaśca bhūtebhyaścarāḥ śreṣṭhāḥ sadā narāḥ
|
brāhmaṇāśca tataḥ śreṣṭhāsteṣu yajñāḥ pratiṣṭhitāḥ ||13||

14yajñairavāpyate somaḥ sa ca goṣu pratiṣṭhitaḥ
|
tato devāḥ pramodante pūrvaṃ vṛttistataḥ prajāḥ ||14||

15prajātānyeva bhūtāni prākrośanvṛttikāṃkṣayā
|
vṛttidaṃ cānvapadyanta tṛṣitāḥ pitṛmātṛvat ||15||

16itīdaṃ manasā gatvā prajāsargārthamātmanaḥ
|
prajāpatistu bhagavānamṛtaṃ prāpibattadā ||16||

17sa gatastasya tṛptiṃ tu gandhaṃ surabhimudgiran
|
dadarśodgārasaṃvṛttāṃ surabhiṃ musvajāṃ sutām ||17||

18sā'sṛjatsaurabheyīstu surabhirlokamātṛkāḥ
|
suvarṇavarṇāḥ kapilāḥ prajānāṃ vṛttidhenavaḥ ||18||

19tāsāmamṛtavarṇānāṃ kṣarantīnāṃ samantataḥ
|
babhūvāmṛtajaḥ phenaḥ sravantīnāmivormijaḥ ||19||

20sa vatsamukhavibhraṣṭo bhavasya bhuvi tiṣṭhataḥ
|
śirasyavāpa tatkruddhaḥ sa tadaikṣata ca prabhuḥ ||20||

21lalāṭaprabhaveṇākṣṇā rohiṇīṃ pradahanniva
|
tattejastu tato raudraṃ kapilāstā viśāṃpate ||21||

22nānāvarṇatvamanayanmeghāniva divākaraḥ
|
yāstu tasmādapakramya somamevābhisaṃśritāḥ ||22||

23yathotpannāḥ svavarṇāsthāstā nītāścānyavarṇatām
|
atha kruddhaṃ mahādevaṃ prajāpatirabhāṣata ||23||

24amṛtenāvasiktastvaṃ nocchiṣṭaṃ vidyate gavām
|
yathā hyamṛtamādāya somo visyandate punaḥ ||24||

25tathā kṣīraṃ kṣarantyetā rohiṇyo'mṛtasaṃbhavam
|
na duṣyatyanilo nāgnirna suvarṇaṃ na codadhiḥ ||25||

26nāmṛtenāmṛtaṃ pītaṃ vatsapītā na vatsalā
|
imānlokānbhariṣyanti haviṣā prasraveṇa ca ||26||

27āsāmaiśvaryamicchanti sarve'mṛtamayaṃ śubham
|
vṛṣabhaṃ ca dadau tasmai saha gobhiḥ prajāpatiḥ ||27||

28prasādayāmāsa manastena rudrasya bhārata
|
prītaścāpi mahādevaścakāra vṛṣabhaṃ tadā ||28||

29dhvajaṃ ca vāhanaṃ caiva tasmātsa vṛṣabhadhvajaḥ
|
tato devairmahādevastadā paśupatiḥ kṛtaḥ
|
īśvaraḥ sa gavāṃ madhye vṛṣabhāṅkaḥ prakīrtitaḥ ||29||

30evamavyagravarṇānāṃ kapilānāṃ mahaujasām
|
pradāne prathamaḥ kalpaḥ sarvāsāmeva kīrtitaḥ ||30||

31lokajyeṣṭhā lokavṛttipravṛttā rudropetāḥ somaviṣyandabhūtāḥ
|
saumyāḥ puṇyāḥ kāmadāḥ prāṇadāśca gā vai dattvā sarvakāmapradaḥ syāt ||31||

32idaṃ gavāṃ prabhavavidhānamuttamaṃ paṭhansadā'śucirapi maṅgalapriyaḥ
|
vimucyate kalikaluṣeṇa mānavaḥ śriyaṃ sutān dhanapaśumāpnuyātsadā ||32||

33havyaṃ kavyaṃ tarpaṇaṃ śāntikarma yānaṃ vāso vṛddhabālasya tuṣṭiḥ
|
etānsarvān gopradāne guṇānvai dātā rājannāpnuyādvai sadaiva ||33||

34vaiśampāyana uvāca|
pitāmahasyātha niśamya vākyaṃ rājā saha bhrātṛbhirājamīḍhaḥ
|
suvarṇavarṇānaḍuhastathā gāḥ pārtho dadau brāhmaṇasattamebhyaḥ ||34||

35tathaiva tebhyo'pi dadau dvijebhyo gavāṃ sahasrāṇi śatāni caiva
|
yajñānsamuddiśya ca dakṣiṇārthe lokānvijetuṃ paramāṃ ca kīrtim ||35||
