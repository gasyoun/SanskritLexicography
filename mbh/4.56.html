Mahābhārata 4.56 (Virāṭaparvan)

1vaiśaṃpāyana uvāca|tānyanīkānyadṛśyanta kurūṇāmugradhanvinām
|
saṃsarpante yathā meghā gharmānte mandamārutāḥ ||1||

2abhyāśe vājinastasthuḥ samārūḍhāḥ prahāriṇaḥ
|
bhīmarūpāśca mātaṅgāstomarāṅkuśanoditāḥ
|
mahāmātraiḥ samārūḍhā vicitrakavacojjvalāḥ ||2||

3tataḥ śakraḥ suragaṇaiḥ samāruhya sudarśanam
|
sahopāyāt tadā rājanviśvāśvimarutāṃ gaṇaiḥ ||3||

4taddevayakṣagandharvamahoragasamākulam
|
śuśubhe'bhravinirmuktaṃ grahāṇāmiva maṇḍalam ||4||

5astrāṇāṃ ca balaṃ teṣāṃ mānuṣeṣu prayuñjatām
|
tacca bhīmaṃ mahadyuddhaṃ kṛpārjunasamāgame
|
draṣṭumabhyāgatā devāḥ svavimānaiḥ pṛthak pṛthak ||5||

6śataṃ śatasahasrāṇāṃ yatra sthūṇā hiraṇmayī
|
maṇiratnamayī cānyā prāsādaṃ tadadhārayat ||6||

7tataḥ kāmagamaṃ divyaṃ sarvaratnavibhūṣitam
|
vimānaṃ devarājasya śuśubhe khecaraṃ tadā ||7||

8tatra devāstrayastriṃśattiṣṭhanti sahavāsavāḥ
|
gandharvā rākṣasāḥ sarpāḥ pitaraśca maharṣibhiḥ ||8||

9tathā rājā vasumanā balākṣaḥ supratardanaḥ
|
aṣṭakaśca śibiścaiva yayātirnahuṣo gayaḥ ||9||

10manuḥ pūrū raghurbhānuḥ kṛśāśvaḥ sagaro nalaḥ
|
vimāne devarājasya samadṛśyanta suprabhāḥ ||10||

11agnerīśasya somasya varuṇasya prajāpateḥ
|
tathā dhāturvidhātuśca kuberasya yamasya ca ||11||

12alambuṣograsenānāṃ gandharvasya ca tumburoḥ
|
yathāmānaṃ yathoddeśaṃ vimānāni cakāśire ||12||

13sarvadevanikāyāśca siddhāśca paramarṣayaḥ
|
arjunasya kurūṇāṃ ca draṣṭuṃ yuddhamupāgatāḥ ||13||

14divyānāṃ sarvamālyānāṃ gandhaḥ puṇyo'tha sarvaśaḥ
|
prasasāra vasantāgre vanānāmiva bhārata ||14||

15tatra ratnāni devānāṃ samadṛśyanta tiṣṭhatām
|
ātapatrāṇi vāsāṃsi srajaśca vyajanāni ca ||15||

16upāśāmyadrajo bhaumaṃ sarvaṃ vyāptaṃ marīcibhiḥ
|
divyagandhānupādāya vāyuryodhānasevata ||16||

17prabhāsitamivākāśaṃ citrarūpamalaṅkṛtam
|
saṃpatadbhiḥ sthitaiścāpi nānāratnavibhāsitaiḥ ||17||

18vimānairvividhaiścitrairupānītaiḥ surottamaiḥ
|
vajrabhṛcchuśubhe tatra vimānasthaiḥ surairvṛtaḥ ||18||

19bibhraṃnmālāṃ mahātejāḥ padmotpalasamāyutām
|
viprekṣyamāṇo bahubhirnātṛpyatsumahāhavam ||19||
