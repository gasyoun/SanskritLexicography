Mahābhārata 13.69 (Anuśāsanaparvan)

1yudhiṣṭhira uvāca|
bhūya eva kuruśreṣṭha dānānāṃ vidhimuttamam
|
kathayasva mahāprājña bhūmidānaṃ viśeṣataḥ ||1||

2pṛthivīṃ kṣatriyo dadyādbrāhmaṇāyeṣṭikarmiṇe
|
vidhivatpratigṛhṇīyānna tvanyo dātumarhati ||2||

3sarvavarṇaistu yacchakyaṃ pradātuṃ phalakāṃkṣibhiḥ
|
vede vā yatsamākhyātaṃ tanme vyākhyātumarhasi ||3||

4bhīṣma uvāca|
tulyanāmāni deyāni trīṇi tulyaphalāni ca
|
sarvakāmaphalānīha gāvaḥ pṛthvī sarasvatī ||4||

5yo brūyāccāpi śiṣyāya dharmyāṃ brāhmīṃ sarasvatīm
|
pṛthivīgopradānābhyāṃ tulyaṃ sa phalamaśrute ||5||

6tathaiva gāḥ praśaṃsanti na tu deyaṃ tataḥ param
|
sannikṛṣṭaphalāstā hi laghvarthāśca yudhiṣṭhira ||6||

7mātaraḥ sarvabhūtānāṃ gāvaḥ sarvasukhapradāḥ
|
vṛddhimākāṃkṣatā nityaṃ gāvaḥ kāryāḥ pradakṣiṇāḥ ||7||

8saṃtāḍyā na tu pādena gavāṃ madhye na ca vrajet
|
maṅgalāyatanaṃ devyastasmātpūjyāḥ sadaiva hi ||8||

9pracodanaṃ devakṛtaṃ gavāṃ karmasu vartatām
|
pūrvamevākṣaraṃ cānya‌dabhidheyaṃ tataḥ param ||9||

10pracāre vā nivāte vā budho nodvejayeta gāḥ
|
tṛṣitā hyabhivīkṣantyo naraṃ hanyuḥ sabāndhavam ||10||

11pitṛsadmāni satataṃ devatāyatanāni ca
|
pūyante śakṛtā yāsāṃ pūtaṃ kimadhikaṃ tataḥ ||11||

12ghāsamuṣṭiṃ paragave dadyātsaṃvatsaraṃ tu yaḥ
|
akṛtvā svayamāhāraṃ vrataṃ tatsārvakāmikam ||12||

13sa hi putrānyaśorthaṃ ca śriyaṃ cāpyadhigacchati
|
nāśayatyaśubhaṃ caiva duḥsvapnaṃ cāpyapohati ||13||

14yudhiṣṭhira uvāca|
deyāḥ kiṃlakṣaṇā gāvaḥ kāścāpi parivarjayet
|
kīdṛśāya pradātavyā na deyāḥ kīdṛśāya ca ||14||

15bhīṣma uvāca|
asadvṛttāya pāpāya lubdhāyānṛtavādine
|
havyakavyavyapetāya na deyā gauḥ kathañcana ||15||

16bhikṣave bahuputrāya śrotriyāyāhitāgnaye
|
dattvā daśagavāṃ dātā lokānāpnotyanuttamān ||16||

17yaścaiva dharmaṃ kurute tasya dharmaphalaṃ ca yat
|
sarvasyaivāṃśabhāgdātā taṃ nimittaṃ pravṛttayaḥ ||17||

18yaścainamutpādayate yaścainaṃ trāyate bhayāt
|
yaścāsya kurute vṛttiṃ sarve te pitarastrayaḥ ||18||

19kalmaṣaṃ guruśuśrūṣā hanti māno mahadyaśaḥ
|
aputratāṃ trayaḥ putrā avṛttiṃ daśa ghenavaḥ ||19||

20vedāntaniṣṭhasya bahuśrutasya prajñānatṛptasya jitendriyasya
|
śiṣṭasya dāntasya yatasya caiva bhūteṣu nityaṃ priyavādinaśca ||20||

21yaḥ kṣudbhayādvai na vikarma kuryānmṛduśca śāntau hyatithipriyaśca
|
vṛttiṃ dvijāyātisṛjeta tasmai yastulyaśīlaśca saputradāraḥ ||21||

22śubhe pātre ye guṇā gopradāne tāvāndoṣo brāhmaṇasvāpahāre
|
sarvāvasthaṃ brāhmaṇasvāpahāro dārāścaiṣāṃ dūrato varjanīyāḥ ||22||
