Mahābhārata 13.8 (Anuśāsanaparvan)

1yudhiṣṭhira uvāca|
ke pūjyāḥ ke namaskāryāḥ kānnamasyasi bhārata
|
etanme sarvamācakṣva yebhyaḥ spṛhayase nṛpa ||1||

2uttamāpadgatasyāpi yatra te vartate manaḥ
|
manuṣyaloke sarvasmin yadamutreha cāpyuta ||2||

3bhīṣma uvāca|
spṛhayāmi dvijātibhyo yeṣāṃ brahma paraṃ dhanam
|
yeṣāṃ svapratyayaḥ svargastapaḥsvādhyāyasādhanam ||3||

4yeṣāṃ bālāśca vṛddhāśca pitṛpaitāmahīṃ dhuram
|
udvahanti na sīdanti tebhyo vai spṛhayāmyaham ||4||

5vidyāsvabhivinītānāṃ dāntānāṃ mṛdubhāṣiṇām
|
śrutavṛttopapannānāṃ sadākṣaravidāṃ satām ||5||

6saṃsatsu vadatāṃ tāta haṃsānāmiva saṅghaśaḥ
|
maṅgalyarūpā rucirā divyajīmūtaniḥsvanāḥ ||6||

7samyaguccaritā vācaḥ śrūyante hi yudhiṣṭhira
|
śuśrūṣamāṇe nṛpatau pretya ceha sukhāvahāḥ ||7||

8ye cāpi teṣāṃ śrotāraḥ sadā sada‌si sammatāḥ
|
vijñānaguṇasampannāstebhyaśca spṛhayāmyaham ||8||

9susaṃskṛtāni prayatāḥ śucīni guṇavanti ca
|
dadatyannāni tṛptyarthaṃ brāhmaṇebhyo yudhiṣṭhira ||9||

10ye cāpi satataṃ rājaṃstebhyaśca spṛhayāmyaham
|
śakyaṃ hyevāhave yoddhuṃ na dātumanasūyitam ||10||

11śūrā vīrāśca śataśaḥ santi loke yudhiṣṭhira
|
yeṣāṃ saṃkhyāyamānānāṃ dānaśūro viśiṣyate ||11||

12dhanyaḥ syāṃ yadyahaṃ bhūyaḥ saumya brāhmaṇako'pi vā
|
kule jāto dharmagatistapovidyāparāyaṇaḥ ||12||

13na me tvattaḥ priyataro loke'smin pāṇḍunandana
|
tvattaścāpi priyatarā brāhmaṇā bharatarṣabha ||13||

14yathā mama priyatamāstvatto viprāḥ kurūttama
|
tena satyena gaccheyaṃ lokānyatra sa śāntanuḥ ||14||

15na me pitā priyataro brāhmaṇebhyastathā'bhavat
|
na me pituḥ pitā vāpi ye cānye'pi suhṛjjanāḥ ||15||

16na hi me vṛjinaṃ kiñcidvidyate brāhmaṇeṣviha
|
aṇu vā yadi vā sthūlaṃ vidyate sādhu karmasu ||16||

17karmaṇā manasā vāpi vācā vāpi parantapa
|
yanme kṛtaṃ brāhmaṇebhyastenādya na tapāmyaham ||17||

18brahmaṇya iti māmāhustayā vācā'smi toṣitaḥ
|
etadeva pavitrebhyaḥ sarvebhyaḥ paramaṃ smṛtam ||18||

19paśyāmi lokānamalāñchucīnbrāhmaṇayāyinaḥ
|
teṣu me tāta gantavyamahnāya ca cirāya ca ||19||

20yathā bhartrāśrayo dharmaḥ strīṇāṃ loke yudhiṣṭhira
|
sa devaḥ sā gatirnānyā kṣatriyasya tathā dvijāḥ ||20||

21kṣatriyaḥ śatavarṣī ca daśavarṣī dvijottamaḥ
|
pitāputrau ca vijñeyau tayorhi brāhmaṇo guruḥ ||21||

22nārī tu patyabhāve vai devaraṃ kurute patim
|
pṛthivī brāhmaṇālāme kṣatriyaṃ kurute patim ||22||

23putravacca tato rakṣyā upāsyā guruvacca te
|
agnivaccopacaryā vai brāhmaṇāḥ kurusattama ||23||

24ṛjūnsataḥ satyaśīlānsarvabhūtahite ratān
|
āśīviṣāniva kruddhān dvijānparicaretsadā ||24||

25tejasastapasaścaiva nityaṃ bibhyedyudhiṣṭhira
|
ubhe caite parityājye tejaścaiva tapastathā ||25||

26vyavasāyastayoḥ śīghramubhayoreva vidyate
|
hanyuḥ kruddhā mahārāja brāhmaṇā ye tapasvinaḥ ||26||

27bhūyaḥ syādubhayaṃ dattaṃ brāhmaṇādyada‌kopanāt
|
kuryādubhayataḥ śeṣaṃ dattaśeṣaṃ na śeṣayet ||27||

28daṇḍapāṇiryathā goṣu pālo nityaṃ hi rakṣayet
|
brāhmaṇā brahma ca tathā kṣatriyaḥ paripālayet ||28||

29piteva putrān rakṣethā brāhmaṇān dharmacetasaḥ
|
gṛhe caiṣāmavekṣethāḥ kiṃsvidastīti jīvanam ||29||
