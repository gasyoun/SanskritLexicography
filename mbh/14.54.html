Mahābhārata 14.54 (Āśvamedhikaparvan)

1uttaṅka uvāca|
brūhi keśava tattvena tvamadhyātmamaninditam
|
śrutvā śreyo'bhidhāsyāmi śāpaṃ vā te janārdana||1||

2vāsudeva uvāca
tamo rajaśca satvaṃ ca viddhi bhāvānmadāśrayān
|
tathā rudānvasūnvāpi viddhi matprabhavāndvija||2||

3mayi sarvāṇi bhūtāni sarvabhūteṣu cāpyaham
|
sthita ityabhijānīhi mā te'bhūdatra saṃśayaḥ||3||

4tathā daityagaṇānsarvānyakṣagandharvarākṣasān
|
nāgānapsarasaścaiva viddhi matprabhavāndvija||4||

5sadasaccaiva yatprāhuravyaktaṃ vyaktameva ca
|
akṣaraṃ ca kṣaraṃ caiva sarvametanmadātmakam||5||

6ye cāśrameṣu vai dharmāścaturdhā viditā mune
|
vaidikāni ca sarvāṇi viddhi sarvaṃ madātmakam||6||

7asacca sadasaccaiva yadviśvaṃ sadasatparam
|
mattaḥ parataraṃ nāsti devadevātsanātanāt||7||

8oṅkārapramukhānvedānviddhi māṃ tvaṃ bhṛgūdvaha
|
yūpaṃ somaṃ caruṃ homaṃ tridaśāpyāyanaṃ makhe||8||

9hotāramapi havyaṃ ca viddhi māṃ bhṛgunandana
|
adhvaryuḥ kalpakaścāpi haviḥ paramasaṃskṛtam||9||

10udgātā cāpi māṃ stauti gītaghoṣairmahādhvare
|
prāyaścitteṣu māṃ brahman śāntimaṅgalavācakāḥ||10||

11stuvanti viśvakarmāṇaṃ satataṃ dvijasattama
|
mama viddhi sutaṃ dharmamagrajaṃ dvijasattama||11||

12mānasaṃ dayitaṃ vipra sarvabhūtadayātmakam
|
tatrāhaṃ vartamānaiśca nivṛttaiścaiva mānavaiḥ||12||

13bahvīḥ saṃsaramāṇo vai yonīrvāmi sattama
|
dharmasaṃrakṣaṇārthāya dharmasaṃsthāpanāya ca||13||

14taistairveṣaiśca rūpaiśca triṣu lokeṣu bhārgava
|
ahaṃ viṣṇurahaṃ brahmā śakro'tha prabhavāpyayaḥ||14||

15bhūtagrāmasya sarvasya sraṣṭā saṃhāra eva ca
|
adharme vartamānānāṃ sarveṣāmahamacyutaḥ||15||

16dharmasya setuṃ badhnāmi calite calite yuge
|
tāstā yonīḥ praviśyāhaṃ prajānāṃ hitakāmyayā||16||

17yadā tvahaṃ devayonau vartāmi bhṛgunandana
|
tadāhaṃ devavatsarvamācarāmi na saṃśayaḥ||17||

18yadā gandharvayonau vā vartāmi bhṛgunandana
|
tadā gandharvavatsarvamācarāmi na saṃśayaḥ||18||

19nāgayonau yadā caiva tathā vartāmi nāgavat
|
yakṣarākṣasayonyostu yathāvadvicarāmyaham||19||

20mānuṣye vartamāne tu kṛpaṇaṃ yācitā mayā
|
na ca te jānasammohā vaco'gṛhṇanta me hitam||20||

21bhayaṃ ca mahaduddiśya trāsitāḥ kuravo mayā
|
kruddhena bhūtvā tu punaryathāvadanudarśitāḥ||21||

22te'dharmeṇeha saṃyuktāḥ parītāḥ kāladharmaṇā
|
dharmeṇa nihatā yuddhe gatāḥ svargaṃ na saṃśayaḥ||22||

23lokeṣu pāṇḍavāścaiva gatāḥ khyātiṃ dvijottama
|
etatte sarvamākhyātaṃ yanmāṃ tvaṃ paripṛcchasi||23||
