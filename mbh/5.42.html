Mahābhārata 5.42 (Udyogaparvan)

1vaiśampāyana uvāca|
tato rājā dhṛtarāṣṭro manīṣī saṃpūjya vākyaṃ vidureritaṃ tat
|
sanatsujātaṃ rahite mahātmā papraccha buddhiṃ paramāṃ bubhūṣan ||1||

2dhṛtarāṣṭra uvāca|
sanatsujāta yadimaṃ śṛṇomi na mṛtyurastīti tava pravādam
|
devāsurā hyācaran bahmacaryamamṛtyave tatkatarannu satyam ||2||

3sanatsujāta uvāca|
amṛtyuṃ karmaṇā yacca mṛtyurnāstīti cāparam
|
śṛṇu me bruvato rājan yathaitanmā viśaṅkithāḥ ||3||

4ubhe satye kṣatriyaitasya viddhi mohānmṛtyuḥ saṃmato'yaṃ kavīnām
|
pramādaṃ vai mṛtyumahaṃ bravīmi tathā'pramādamamṛtatvaṃ bravīmi ||4||

5pramādādvai asurāḥ parābhavannapramādādbrahmabhūtā bhavanti
|
naiva mṛtyurvyāghra ivātti jantanna hyasya rūpamupalabhyate hi ||5||

6yamaṃ tveke mṛtyumato'nyamāhurātmāvāsannamamṛtaṃ brahmacaryam
|
pitṛloke rājyamanuśāsti devaḥ śivaḥ śivānāmaśivo'śivānām ||6||

7asyādeśānniḥsarate narāṇāṃ krodhaḥ pramādo lobharūpaśca mṛtyuḥ
|
ahaṃgatenaiva caranvimārgānna cātmano yogamupaiti kaścit ||7||

8te mohitāstadvaśe vartamānā itaḥ pretāstatra punaḥ patanti
|
tatastān devā anuviplavante ato mṛtyurmaraṇākhyāmupaiti ||8||

9karmodaye karmaphalānurāgāstatrānu te yānti na taranti mṛtyum
|
sadarthayogānavagamāt samantāt pravartate bhogayogena dehī ||9||

10tadvai mahāmohanamindriyāṇāṃ mithyārthayogasya gatirhi nityā
|
mithyārthayogābhihatāntarātmā smarannupāste viṣayān samantāt ||10||

11abhidhyā vai prathamaṃ hanti lokān kāmakrodhāvanugṛhyāśu paścāt
|
ete bālān mṛtyave prāpayanti dhīrāstu dhairyeṇa taranti mṛtyum ||11||

12so'bhidhyāyannutpatitānnihanyādanādareṇāpratibudhyamānaḥ
|
nainaṃ mṛtyurmṛtyurivātti bhūtvā evaṃ vidvānyo vinihanti kāmān ||12||

13kāmānusārī puruṣaḥ kāmānanu vinaśyati
|
kāmān vyudasya dhunute yatkiñcitpuruṣo rajaḥ ||13||

14tamo'prakāśo bhūtānāṃ narako'yaṃ pradṛśyate
|
muhyanta iva dhāvanti gacchantaḥ śvabhravatsukham||14||

15amūḍhavṛtteḥ puruṣasyeha kuryāt kiṃ vai mṛtyustārṇa ivāsya vyāghraḥ
|
amanyamānaḥ kṣatriya kiñcidanyannādhīyīta nirṇudannivāsya cāyuḥ||15||

16sa krodhalobhau mohavānantarātmā sa vai mṛtyustvaccharīre ya eṣaḥ
|
evaṃ mṛtyuṃ jāyamānaṃ viditvā jñāne tiṣṭhanna bibhetīha mṛtyoḥ
|
vinaśyate viṣaye tasya mṛtyurmṛtyoryathā viṣayaṃ prāpya martyaḥ ||16||

17dhṛtarāṣṭra uvāca|
yānevāhurijyayā sādhulokān dvijātīnāṃ puṇyatamān sanātanāt
|
teṣāṃ parārthaṃ kathayantīha vedā etadvidvānnopaiti kathaṃ nu karma ||17||

18sanatsujāta uvāca|
evaṃ hyavidvānupayāti tatra tatrārthajātaṃ ca vadanti vedāḥ
|
saveha āyāti paraṃ parātmā prayāti mārgeṇa nihatya mārgān ||18||

19dhṛtarāṣṭra uvāca|
ko'sau niyuṅkte tamajaṃ purāṇaṃ sa cedidaṃ sarvamanukrameṇa
|
kiṃ vā'sya kāryamathavā sukhaṃ ca tanme vidvan brūhi sarvaṃ yathāvat ||19||

20sanatsujāta uvāca|
doṣo mahānatra vibhedayoge hyanādiyogena bhavanti nityāḥ
|
tathā'sya nādhikyamapaiti kiñcidanādiyogena bhavanti puṃsaḥ ||20||

21ya etadvā bhagavān sa nityo vikārayogena karoti viśvam
|
tathā ca tacchaktiriti sma manyate tathā'rthayoge ca bhavanti vedāḥ ||21||

22dhṛtarāṣṭra uvāca|
ye'smin dharmānnācarantīha kecittathā dharmān kecidihācaranti
|
dharmaḥ pāpena pratihanyate svidutāho dharmaḥ pratihanti pāpam ||22||

23sanatsujāta uvāca|
ubhayameva tatropayujyate phalaṃ dharmasyaivetarasya ca ||23||

24tasmin sthitau vā'pyubhayaṃ hi nityaṃ jñānena vidvān pratihanti siddham
|
tathā'nyathā puṇyamupaiti dehī tathāgataṃ pāpamupaiti siddham ||24||

25gatvobhayaṃ karmaṇā yujyate sthiraṃ śubhasya pāpasya sa cāpi karmaṇā
|
dharmeṇa pāpaṃ praṇudatīha vidvān dharmo balīyāniti tasya siddhiḥ ||25||

26dhṛtarāṣṭra uvāca|
yānihāhuḥ svasya dharmasya lokān dvijātīnāṃ puṇyakṛtāṃ sanātanān
|
teṣāṃ kramān kathaya tato'pi cānyānnaitadvidvan vettumicchāmi karma ||26||

27sanatsujāta uvāca|
yeṣāṃ vrate'tha vispardhā bale balavatāmiva
|
te brāhmaṇā itaḥ pretya brahmalokaprakāśakāḥ||27||

28yeṣāṃ dharme ca vispardhā teṣāṃ tajjñānasādhanam
|
te brāhmaṇā ito muktāḥ svargaṃ yānti triviṣṭapam ||28||

29tasya samyak samācāramāhurvedavido janāḥ
|
nainaṃ manyeta bhūyiṣṭhaṃ bāhyamābhyantaraṃ janam ||29||

30yatra manyeta bhūyiṣṭhaṃ prāvṛṣīva tṛṇolapam
|
annaṃ pānaṃ brāhmaṇasya tajjīvennānusañjvaret ||30||

31yatrākathayamānasya prayacchatyaśivaṃ bhayam
|
atiriktamivākurvan sa śreyānnetaro janaḥ ||31||

32yo vā kathayamānasya hyātmānaṃ nānusañjvaret
|
brahmasvaṃ nopabhuñjīta tadannaṃ saṃmataṃ satām||32||

33yathāsvaṃ vāntamaśnāti śvā vai nityamabhūtaye
|
evaṃ te vāntamaśnanti svavīryasyopasevanāt ||33||

34nityamajñātacaryā me iti manyeta brāhmaṇaḥ
|
jñātīnāṃ tu vasan madhye taṃ vidurbrāhmaṇaṃ budhāḥ||34||

35ko hyanantaramātmānaṃ brāhmaṇo hantumarhati
|
nirliṅgamacalaṃ śuddhaṃ sarvadvaitavivarjitam ||35||

36tasmāddhi kṣatriyasyāpi brahmā vasati paśyati||36||

37yo'nyathā santamātmānamanyathā pratipadyate
|
kiṃ tena na kṛtaṃ pāpaṃ caureṇātmāpahāriṇā ||37||

38aśrāntaḥ syādanādātā saṃmato nirupadravaḥ
|
śiṣṭo na śiṣṭavatsa syādbrāhmaṇo brahmavitkaviḥ ||38||

39anāḍhyā mānuṣe vitte āḍhyā daive tathā kratau
|
te durdharṣā duṣprakampyāstānvidyādbrahmaṇastanum ||39||

40sarvānsviṣṭakṛto devān vidyādya iha kaścana
|
na samāno brāhmaṇasya tasmin prayatate svayam ||40||

41yamaprayatamānaṃ tu mānayanti sa mānitaḥ
|
na mānyamāno manyeta na mānyamabhisañjvaret ||41||

42lokaḥ svabhāvavṛttirhi nimeṣonmeṣavatsadā
|
vidvāṃso mānayantīha iti manyeta mānitaḥ ||42||

43adharmanipuṇā mūḍhā loke māyāviśāradāḥ
|
na mānyaṃ mānayiṣyanti mānyānāmavamāninaḥ ||43||

44na vai mānaṃ ca maunaṃ ca sahitau vasataḥ sadā
|
ayaṃ hi loko mānasya asau maunasya tadviduḥ ||44||

45śrīḥ sukhasyeha saṃvāsaḥ sā cāpi paripanthinī
|
brāhmī sudurlabhā śrīrhi prajñāhīnena kṣatriya ||45||

46dvārāṇi tasyeha vadanti santo bahuprakārāṇi durādharāṇi
|
satyārjave hrīrdamaśaucavidyā yathā na mohapratibandhanāni ||46||
