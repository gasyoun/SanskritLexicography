Mahābhārata 4.35 (Virāṭaparvan)

1vaiśampāyana uvāca|yāte trigartānmatsye tu paśūṃstānvai parīpsati
|
duryodhanaḥ sahāmātyo virāṭamupayādatha ||1||

2bhīṣmo droṇaśca karṇaśca kṛpaśca paramāstravit
|
drauṇiśca saubalaścaiva tathā duḥśāsanaḥ prabho ||2||

3viviṃśatirvikarṇaśca citrasenaśca vīryavān
|
durmukho duḥśalaścaiva ye caivānye mahārathāḥ ||3||

4ete matsyānupāgamya virāṭasya mahīpateḥ
|
ghoṣān vidrāvya tarasā godhanaṃ jahrurojasā ||4||

5ṣaṣṭiṃ gavāṃ sahasrāṇi kuravaḥ kālayanti ca
|
mahatā rathavaṃśena parivārya samantataḥ ||5||

6gopālānāṃ tu ghoṣasya hanyatāṃ tairmahārathaiḥ
|
ārāvaḥ sumahānāsīt saṃprahāre bhayaṅkare ||6||

7gopādhyakṣo bhayatrasto rathamāsthāya satvaraḥ
|
jagāma nagarāyaiva parikrośaṃstadārtavat ||7||

8sa praviśya puraṃ rājño nṛpaveśmābhyayāt tataḥ
|
avatīrya rathāt tūrṇamākhyātuṃ praviveśa ha ||8||

9dṛṣṭvā bhūmiñjayaṃ nāma putraṃ matsyasya māninam
|
tasmai tat sarvamācaṣṭa rāṣṭrasya paśukarṣaṇam ||9||

10ṣaṣṭiṃ gavāṃ sahastrāṇi kuravaḥ kālayanti te
|
tadvijetuṃ samuttiṣṭha godhanaṃ rāṣṭravardhana ||10||

11rājaputra hitaprepsuḥ kṣipraṃ niryāhi ca svayam
|
tvāṃ hi matsyo mahīpālaḥ śūnyapālamihākarot ||11||

12tvayā pariṣado madhye ślāghate sa narādhipaḥ
|
putro mamānurūpaśca śūraśceti kulodvahaḥ ||12||

13iṣvastre nipuṇo yodhaḥ sadāvīraśca me sutaḥ
|
tasya tatsatyamevāstu manuṣyendrasya bhāṣitam ||13||

14āvartaya kurūn jitvā paśūn paśumatāṃ vara
|
nirdahaiṣāmanīkāni bhīmena śaratejasā ||14||

15dhanuścyutai rukmapuṅkhaiḥ śaraiḥ sannataparvabhiḥ
|
dviṣatāṃ bhindhyanīkāni gajānāmiva yūthapaḥ ||15||

16pāśopadhānāṃ jyātantrīṃ cāpadaṇyāṃ mahāsvanām
|
śaravarṇāṃ dhanurvīṇāṃ śatrumadhye pravādaya ||16||

17śvetā rajatasaṅkāśā rathe yujyantu te hayāḥ
|
dhvajaṃ ca siṃhaṃ sauvarṇamuchrayantu tava prabho ||17||

18rukmapuṅkhāḥ prasannāgrā muktā hastavatā tvayā
|
chādayantu śarāḥ sūryaṃ rājñāṃ mārganirodhakāḥ ||18||

19raṇe jitvā kurūn sarvān vajrapāṇirivāsurān
|
yaśo mahadavāpya tvaṃ praviśedaṃ puraṃ punaḥ ||19||

20tvaṃ hi rāṣṭrasya paramā gatirmatsyapateḥ sutaḥ
|
yathā hi pāṇḍuputrāṇāmarjuno jayatāṃ varaḥ ||20||

21evameva gatirnūnaṃ bhavān viṣayavāsinām
|
gatimanto vayaṃ tvadya sarve viṣayavāsinaḥ ||21||

22vaiśampāyana uvāca|strīmadhya uktastenāsau tadvākyamabhayaṃkaram
|
antaḥpure ślāghamāna idaṃ vacanamabravīt ||22||
