Mahābhārata 8.34 (Karṇaparvan)

1duryodhana uvāca|
pitṛdevarṣisaṃghebhyo'bhaye datte mahātmanā
|
satkṛtya śaṅkaraṃ prāha brahmā lokahitaṃ vacaḥ ||1||

2tavātisargāddeveśa prājāpatyamidaṃ padam
|
mayā'dhitiṣṭhatā datto dānavebhyo mahānvaraḥ ||2||

3tānatikrāntamaryādān nānyaḥ saṃhartumarhati
|
tvāmṛte bhūtabhavyeśa tvaṃ hyeṣāṃ pratyarirvadhe ||3||

4sa tvaṃ deva prapannānāṃ yācatāṃ ca divaukasām
|
kuru prasādaṃ deveśa dānavāñjahi śaṅkara ||4||

5tvatprasādājjagatsarvaṃ sukhamaidhata mānada
|
śaraṇyastvaṃ hi lokeśa te vayaṃ śaraṇaṃ gatāḥ ||5||

6sthāṇuruvāca | hantavyāḥ śatravaḥ sarve yuṣmākamiti me matiḥ
|
na tveka utsahe hantuṃ balasthā hi suradviṣaḥ ||6||

7te yūyaṃ saṃhatāḥ sarve madīyenārdhatejasāḥ
|
jayadhvaṃ yudhi tāñśatrūn saṃhatā hi mahābalāḥ ||7||

8devā ūcuḥ | asmattejobalaṃ yāvattāva‌ddviguṇamāhave
|
teṣāmiti hi manyāmo dṛṣṭatejobalā hi te ||8||

9sthāṇuruvāca | vadhyāste sarvataḥ pāpā ye yuṣmāsvaparādhinaḥ
|
mama tejobalārdhena sarvānnighnata śātravān ||9||

10devā ūcuḥ | bibhartuṃ bhavato'rdhaṃ tu na śakṣyāmo maheśvara
|
sarveṣāṃ no balārdhena tvameva jahi śātravān ||10||

11sthāṇuruvāca | yadi śaktirna vaḥ kācidbibhartuṃ māmakaṃ balam
|
ahametān haniṣyāmi yuṣmattejordhabṛṃhitaḥ ||11||

12tatastatheti deveśastairukto rājasattama
|
ardhamādāya sarveṣāṃ tejasā'bhyadhiko'bhavat ||12||

13sa tu devo balenāsītsarvebhyo balavattaraḥ
|
mahādeva iti khyātastataḥprabhṛti śaṃkaraḥ ||13||

14tato'bravīnmahādevo dhanurbāṇadharo hyaham
|
haniṣyāmi rathenājau tān ripūnvo divaukasaḥ ||14||

15te yūyaṃ me rathaṃ caiva dhanurbāṇaṃ tathaiva ca
|
paśyadhvaṃ yāvadadyaitān pātayāmi mahītale ||15||

16devā ūcuḥ | mūrtīḥ sarvāḥ samādhāya trailokyasya tatastataḥ
|
rathaṃ te kalpayiṣyāmo deveśvara suvarcasam||16||

17tathaiva buddhyā vihitaṃ viśvakarmakṛtaṃ śubham
|
tato vibudhaśārdūlāste rathaṃ samakalpayan ||17||

18viṣṇuṃ somaṃ hutāśaṃ ca tasyeṣuṃ samakalpayan
|
śṛṅgamagnirbabhūvāsya bhallaḥ somo viśāṃpate ||18||

19kuḍmalaścābhavadviṣṇustasminniṣuvare tadā
|
rathaṃ vasundharāṃ devīṃ viśālapuramālinīm ||19||

20saparvatavanadvīpāṃ cakrurbhūtadharāṃ tadā
|
mandaraḥ parvataścākṣo jaṅghā tasya mahānadī ||20||

21diśaśca pradiśaścaiva parivāro rathasya tu
|
īṣā nakṣatravaṃśaśca yugaḥ kṛtayugo'bhavat ||21||

22kūbaraśca rathasyāsīdvāsukirbhujagottamaḥ
|
apaskaramadhiṣṭhāne himavān vindhyaparvataḥ
|
udayāstāvadhiṣṭhāne girī cakruḥ surottamāḥ ||22||

23samudramakṣamasṛjandānavālayamuttamam
|
saptarṣimaṇḍalaṃ caiva rathasyāsītpariṣkaraḥ ||23||

24gaṅgā sarasvatī sindhurdhuramākāśameva ca
|
upaskaro rathasyāsannāpaḥ sarvāśca nimnagāḥ ||24||

25ahorātraṃ kalāścaiva kāṣṭhāśca ṛtavastathā
|
anukarṣaṃ grahā dīptā varūthaṃ cāpi tārakāḥ ||25||

26dharmārthakāmaṃ saṃyuktaṃ triveṇuṃ dāru bandhuram
|
oṣadhīrvīrudhaścaiva ghaṇṭāḥ puṣpaphalopagāḥ ||26||

27sūryācandramasau kṛtvā cakre rathavarottame
|
pakṣau pūrvāparau tatra kṛte rātryahanī śubhe ||27||

28daśa nāgapatīnīṣāṃ dhṛtarāṣṭramukhāṃstadā
|
yoktrāṇi cakrurnāgāṃśca niḥśvasaṃto mahoragān ||28||

29dyāṃ yugaṃ yugacarmāṇi saṃvartakabalāhakān
|
kālapṛṣṭho'tha nahuṣaḥ karkoṭakadhanañjayau ||29||

30itare cābhavannāgā hayānāṃ bālabandhanāḥ
|
diśaśca pradiśaścaiva raśmayo rathavājinām ||30||

31sandhyāṃ dhṛtiṃ ca medhāṃ ca sthitiṃ sannatimeva ca
|
graha-nakṣatra-tārābhiścarma citraṃ nabhastalam ||31||

32surāmbupretavittānāṃ patīlloঁkeśvarān hayān
|
sinīvālīmanumatiṃ kuhūṃ rākāṃ ca suvratām ||32||

33yoktrāṇi cakrurvāhānāṃ rohakāṃstatra kaṇṭakān
|
dharmaḥ satyaṃ tapo'rthaśca vihitāstatra raśmayaḥ ||33||

34adhiṣṭhānaṃ manaścāsītparirathyā sarasvatī
|
nānāvarṇāśca citrāśca patākāḥ pavaneritāḥ ||34||

35vidyudindradhanurnaddhaṃ rathaṃ dīptaṃ vyadīpayan
|
vaṣa‌ṭkāraḥ pratodo'bhūdgāyatrī śīrṣabandhanā ||35||

36yo yajñe vihitaḥ pūrvamīśānasya mahātmanaḥ
|
saṃvatsaro dhanustadvai sāvitrī jyā mahāsvanā ||36||

37divyaṃ ca varma vihitaṃ mahārhaṃ ratnabhūṣitam
|
abhedyaṃ virajaskaṃ vai kālacakrabahiṣkṛtam ||37||

38dhyajayaṣṭirabhūnmeruḥ śrīmān kanakaparvataḥ
|
patākāścābhavanmeghāstaḍidbhiḥ samalaṅkṛtāḥ ||38||

39rejuradhvaryumadhyasthā jvalanta iva pāvakāḥ
|
klaptaṃ tu taṃ rathaṃ dṛṣṭvā vismitā devatā'bhavan ||39||

40sarvalokasya tejāṃsi dṛṣṭvaikasthāni māriṣa
|
yuktaṃ nivedyāmāsurdevāstasmai mahātmane ||40||

41evaṃ tasminmahārāja kalpite rathasattame
|
devairmanujaśārdūla dviṣatāmabhimardane ||41||

42svānyāyudhāni mukhyāni nyadadhācchaṅkaro rathe
|
dhvajayaṣṭiṃ viyatkṛtvā sthāpayāmāsa govṛṣam ||42||

43brahmadaṇḍaḥ kāladaṇḍo rudradaṇḍastathā jvaraḥ
|
pariskandā rathasyāsan sarvato diśamudyatāḥ ||43||

44atharvāṅgirasāvāstāṃ cakrarakṣau mahātmanaḥ
|
ṛgvedaḥ sāmavedaśca purāṇaṃ ca puraḥsarāḥ ||44||

45itihāsayajurvedau pṛṣṭharakṣau babhūvatuḥ
|
divyā vācaśca vidyāśca paripārśvacarāḥ sthitāḥ ||45||

46stotrādayaśca rājendra vaṣaṭ‌kārastathaiva ca
|
oṅkāraśca mukhe rājannatiśobhākaro'bhavat ||46||

47vicitramṛtubhiḥ ṣaḍbhiḥ kṛtvā saṃvatsaraṃ dhanuḥ
|
chāyāmevātmanaścakre dhanurjyāmakṣayāṃ raṇe ||47||

48kālo hi bhagavān rudrastasya saṃvatsaro dhanuḥ
|
tasmādraudrī kālarātrirjyā kṛtā dhanuṣo jarā ||48||

49iṣuścāpyabhavadviṣṇurjvalanaḥ soma eva ca
|
agnīṣomau jagatkṛtsnaṃ vaiṣṇavaṃ cocyate jagat ||49||

50viṣṇuścātmā bhagavato bhavasyāmitatejasaḥ
|
tasmāddhanurjyāsaṃsparśaṃ na viṣehurharasya te ||50||

51tasmiñśare tigmamanyuṃ mumocāsahyamīśvaraḥ
|
bhṛgvaṅgiromanyubhavaṃ krodhāgnimatiduḥsaham ||51||

52sa nīlalohito dhūmraḥ kṛttivāsā'bhayaṅkaraḥ
|
ādityāyutasaṅkāśastejojvālāvṛto jvalan||52||

53duścyāvacyāvano jetā hantā brahmadviṣāṃ haraḥ
|
nityaṃ trātā ca hantā ca dharmādharmāśritānnarān ||53||

54pramāthibhirbhīmabalairbhīmarūpairmanojavaiḥ
|
vibhāti bhagavān sthāṇustairevātmaguṇairvṛtaḥ ||54||

55tasyāṅgāni samāśritya sthitaṃ viśvamidaṃ jagat
|
jaṅgamājaṅgamaṃ rājañśuśubhe'dbhutadarśanam ||55||

56dṛṣṭvā tu taṃ rathaṃ yuktaṃ kavacī sa śarāsanī
|
bāṇamādāya taṃ divyaṃ somaviṣṇvagnisaṃbhavam ||56||

57tasya rājaṃstadā devāḥ kalpayāñcakrire prabho
|
puṇyagandhavahaṃ rājañśvasanaṃ devasattamam ||57||

58tamāsthāya mahādevastrāsayandaivatānyapi
|
āruroha tadā yattaḥ kampayanniva medinīm ||58||

59tamārurucaṃ deveśaṃ tuṣṭuvuḥ paramarṣayaḥ
|
gandharvā devasaṅghāśca tathaivāpsarasāṃ gaṇāḥ ||59||

60brahmarṣibhiḥ stūyamāno vandyamānaśca bandibhiḥ
|
tathaivāpsarasāṃ vṛndairnṛtyadbhirnṛtyakovidaiḥ ||60||

61sa śobhamāno varadaḥ khaḍgī bāṇī śarāsanī
|
hasannivābravīddevānsārathiḥ ko bhaviṣyati ||61||

62tamabruvan devagaṇā yaṃ bhavānsanniyokṣyate
|
sa bhaviṣyati deveśa sārathiste na saṃśayaḥ ||62||

63tānabravītpunardevo mattaḥ śreṣṭhataro hi yaḥ
|
taṃ sārathiṃ kurudhvaṃ me svayaṃ sañcintya mā-ciram ||63||

64etacchrutvā tato devā vākyamuktaṃ mahātmanā
|
gatvā pitāmahaṃ devāḥ prasādyedaṃ vaco'bruvan ||64||

65yathā tvatkathitaṃ deva tridaśārivinigrahe
|
tathā ca kṛtamasmābhiḥ prasanno no vṛṣadhvajaḥ ||65||

66rathaśca vihito'smābhirvicitrāyudhasaṃvṛtaḥ
|
sārathiṃ ca na jānīmaḥ kaḥ syāttasmin rathottame ||66||

67tasmādvidhīyatāṃ kaścitsārārthardevasattama
|
saphalāṃ tāṃ giraṃ deva kartumarhasi no vibho ||67||

68evamasmāsu hi purā bhagavannuktavānasi
|
hitakartā'smi bhavatāmiti tatkartumarhasi ||68||

69sa deva yukto rathasattamo no durādharo drāvaṇaḥ śātravāṇām
|
pinākapāṇirvihito'tra yoddhā vibhīṣayan dānavānudyato'sau ||69||

70tathaiva vedāścaturo hayāgryā dharā saśailā ca ratho mahātmanaḥ
|
nakṣatravaṃśānugato varūthī haro yoddhā sārathirnābhilakṣyaḥ ||70||

71tatra sārathireṣṭavyaḥ sarvairetairviśeṣavān
|
tatpratiṣṭho ratho deva hayā yoddhā tathaiva ca ||71||

72kavacāni saśastrāṇi kārmukaṃ ca pitāmaha
|
tvāmṛte sārathiṃ tatra nānyaṃ paśyāmahe vayam ||72||

73tvaṃ hi sarvaguṇairyukto daivatebhyo'dhikaḥ prabho
|
sa rathaṃ tūrṇamāruhya saṃyaccha paramān hayān ||73||

74jayāya tridiveśānāṃ vadhāya tridaśadviṣām
|
iti te śirasā gatvā trilokeśaṃ pitāmaham ||74||

75devāḥ prasādyāmāsuḥ sārathyāyeti naḥ śrutam
|
pitāmaha uvāca|
nātra kiñcinmṛṣā vākyaṃ yaduktaṃ tridivaukasaḥ
|
saṃyacchāmi hayāneṣa yudhyato vai kapardinaḥ ||75||

76tataḥ sa bhagavān devo lokasraṣṭā pitāmahaḥ
|
sārathye kalpito devairīśānasya mahātmanaḥ ||76||

77tasminnārohati kṣipraṃ syandane lokapūjite
|
śirobhiragaman bhūmiṃ te hayā vātaraṃhasaḥ
|
āruhya bhagavāndevo dīpyamānaḥ svatejasā ||77||

78abhīṣūn hi pratodaṃ ca saṃjagrāha pitāmahaḥ
|
tata utthāya bhagavāṃstān hayānanilopamān ||78||

79babhāṣe ca tadā sthāṇumāroheti surottamaḥ
|
tatastamiṣumādāya viṣṇusomāgnisaṃbhavam ||79||

80āruroha tadā sthāṇurdhanuṣā kampayanparān
|
tamārūḍhaṃ tu deveśaṃ tuṣṭuvuḥ paramarṣayaḥ ||80||

81gandharvā devasaṃghāśca tathaivāpsarasāṃ gaṇāḥ
|
sa śobhamāno varadaḥ khaḍgī bāṇī śarāsanī ||81||

82pradīpayan rathe tasthau trīlloঁkān svena tejasā
|
tato bhūyo'bravīddevo devānindrapurogamān ||82||

83na hanyāditi kartavyo na śoko vaḥ kathañcana
|
hatānityeva jānīta bāṇenānena cāsurān ||83||

84te devāḥ satyamityāhurnihatā iti cābruvan
|
na ca tadvacanaṃ mithyā yadā''ha bhagavān prabhuḥ ||84||

85iti sañcintya vai devāḥ parāṃ tuṣṭimavāpnuvan
|
tataḥ prayāto deveśaḥ sarvairdevagaṇairvṛtaḥ ||85||

86rathena mahatā rājannupamā nāsti yasya ha
|
svaiśca pāriṣadairdevaḥ pūjyamāno mahāyaśāḥ ||86||

87nṛtyadbhiraparaiścaiva māṃsabhakṣairdurāsadaiḥ
|
dhāvamānaiḥ samantācca tarjamānaiḥ parasparam ||87||

88ṛṣayaśca mahābhāgāstapoyuktā mahāguṇāḥ
|
āśaṃsurvai janā devā mahādevasya sarvaśaḥ ||88||

89evaṃ prayāte deveśe lokānāmabhayaṅkare
|
tuṣṭamāsījjagatsarvaṃ devatāśca narottama ||89||

90ṛṣayastatra deveśaṃ stuvanto bahubhiḥ stavaiḥ
|
tejaścāsmai vardhayanto rājannāsanpunaḥ punaḥ ||90||

91gandharvāṇāṃ sahasrāṇi prayutānyarbudāni ca
|
vādayanti prayāṇe'sya vādyāni vividhāni ca ||91||

92tato'dhirūḍhe varade prayāte cāsurānprati
|
sādhu sādhviti viśveśaḥ smayamāno'bhyabhāṣata ||92||

93yāhi deva yato daityāścodayāśvānatandritaḥ
|
paśya bāhvorbalaṃ me'dya nighnataḥ śātravānraṇe ||93||

94tato'śvāṃśco‌dayāmāsa manomārutaraṃhasaḥ
|
yena tattripuraṃ rājan daityadānavarakṣitam ||94||

95pibadbhiriva cākāśaṃ tairhayairlokapūjitaiḥ
|
jagāma bhagavān kṣipraṃ jayāya tridivaukasām ||95||

96prayāte rathamāsthāya tripurābhimukhe bhave
|
nanāda sumahānādaṃ vṛṣabhaḥ pūrayandiśaḥ ||96||

97vṛṣabhasyāsya ninadaṃ śrutvā bhayakaraṃ mahat
|
vināśamagamaṃstatra tārakāḥ suraśatravaḥ ||97||

98apare'vasthitāstatra yuddhāyābhimukhāstadā
|
tataḥ sthāṇurmahārāja śūladhṛk krodhamūrchitaḥ ||98||

99trastāni sarvabhūtāni trailokyaṃ bhūḥ prakampate
|
nimittāni ca ghorāṇi tatra sandadhataḥ śaram ||99||

100tasminsomāgniviṣṇūnāṃ kṣobheṇa brahmarudrayoḥ
|
sa ratho dhanuṣaḥ kṣobhādatīva hyavasīdati ||100||

101tato nārāyaṇastasmāccharabhāgādviniḥsṛtaḥ
|
vṛṣarūpaṃ samāsthāya ujjahāra mahāratham ||101||

102sīda‌māne rathe caiva nardamāneṣu śatruṣu
|
sa saṃbhramāttu bhagavānnādaṃ cakre mahābalaḥ ||102||

103vṛṣabhasya sthito mūrdhni hayapṛṣṭhe ca mānada
|
tadā sa bhagavān rudro niraikṣaddānavaṃ puram ||103||

104vṛṣabhasyāsthito rudro hayasya ca narottama
|
stanāṃstadā'śātayata khurāṃścaiva dvidhā'karot ||104||

105tataḥprabhṛti bhadraṃ te gavāṃ dvaidhīkṛtāḥ khurāḥ
|
hayānāṃ ca stanā rājaṃstadāprabhṛti nābhavan ||105||

106pīḍitānāṃ balavatā rudreṇādbhutakarmaṇā
|
athādhijyaṃ dhanuḥ kṛtvā śarvaḥ saṃdhāya taṃ śaram ||106||

107yuktvā pāśupatāstreṇa tripuraṃ samacintayat
|
tasmin sthite mahārāja rudre vidhṛtakārmuke ||107||

108purāṇi tāni kālena jagmurevaikatāṃ tadā
|
ekībhāvaṃ gate caiva tripuratvamupāgate ||108||

109babhūva tumulo harṣo devatānāṃ mahātmanām
|
tato devagaṇāḥ sarve siddhāśca paramarṣayaḥ ||109||

110jayeti vāco mumucuḥ saṃstuvanto maheśvaram
|
tato'grataḥ prādurabhūttripuraṃ nighnato'surān ||110||

111anirdeśyo'gravapuṣo devasyāsahyatejasaḥ
|
sa tadvikṛṣya bhagavān divyaṃ lokeśvaro dhanuḥ ||111||

112trailokyasāraṃ tamiṣuṃ mumoca tripuraṃ prati
|
utsṛṣṭe vai mahābhāga tasminniṣuvare tadā ||112||

113mahānārtasvaro hyāsītpurāṇāṃ patatāṃ bhuvi
|
tān so'suragaṇān dagdhvā prākṣipatpaścimārṇave ||113||

114evaṃ tu tripuraṃ dagdhaṃ dānavāścāpyaśeṣataḥ
|
maheśvareṇa kruddhena trailokyasya hitaiṣiṇā ||114||

115sa cātmakrodhajo vahnirhāhetyuktvā nivāritaḥ
|
mā kārṣīrbhasmasāllokāniti tryakṣo'bravīcca tam ||115||

116tataḥ prakṛtimāpannā devā lokāstvatharṣayaḥ
|
tuṣṭuvurvāgbhiragryābhiḥ sthāṇumapratimaujasam ||116||

117te'nujñātā bhagavatā jagmuḥ sarve yathāgatam
|
kṛtakāmāḥ prayatnena prajāpatimukhāḥ surāḥ ||117||

118evaṃ sa bhagavān devo lokasraṣṭā maheśvaraḥ
|
devāsuragaṇādhyakṣo lokānāṃ vidadheḥ śivam ||118||

119yathaiva bhagavān brahmā lokadhātā pitāmahaḥ
|
sārathyamakarottatra rudrasya paramo'vyayaḥ ||119||

120tathā bhavānapi kṣipraṃ rudrasyeva pitāmahaḥ
|
saṃyacchatu hayānasya rādheyasya mahātmanaḥ ||120||

121tvaṃ hi kṛṣṇācca karṇācca phālgunācca viśeṣataḥ
|
viśiṣṭo rājaśārdūla nāsti tatra vicāraṇā ||121||

122yuddhe hyayaṃ rudrakalpastvaṃ ca brahmasamo naye
|
tasmācchakto bhavāñjetuṃ macchatrūṃstānivāsurān ||122||

123yathā śalyādya karṇo'yaṃ śvetāśvaṃ kṛṣṇasārathim
|
pramathya hanyātkaunteyaṃ tathā śīghraṃ vidhīyatām ||123||

124tvayi madreśarājyāśā jīvitāśā tathaiva ca
|
vijayaśca tathaivādya karṇasācivyakāritaḥ ||124||

125tvayi karṇaśca rājyaṃ ca vayaṃ caiva pratiṣṭhitāḥ
|
vijayaścaiva saṃgrāme saṃyacchādya hayottamān ||125||

126imaṃ cāpyaparaṃ bhūya itihāsaṃ nibodha me
|
piturmama sakāśe yadbrahmaṇaḥ prāha dharmavit ||126||

127śrutvā caitadvacaścitraṃ hetukāryārthasaṃhitam
|
kuru śalya viniścitya mābhūdatra vicāraṇā ||127||

128bhārgavāṇāṃ kule jāto jamada‌gnirmahāyaśāḥ
|
tasya rāmetivikhyātaḥ putrastejoguṇānvitaḥ ||128||

129sa tīvraṃ tapa āsthāya prasāda‌yitavān bhavam
|
astrahetoḥ prasannātmā niyataḥ saṃyatendriyaḥ ||129||

130tasya tuṣṭo mahādevo bhaktyā ca praśamena ca
|
hṛdgataṃ cāsya vijñāya darśayāmāsa śaṅkaraḥ ||130||

131maheśvara uvāca|
rāma tuṣṭo'smi bhadraṃ te viditaṃ me tavepsitam
|
kuruṣva pūtamātmānaṃ sarvametadavāpsyasi ||131||

132dāsyāmi te tadāstrāṇi yadā pūto bhaviṣyasi
|
apātramasamarthaṃ ca dahantyatrāṇi bhārgava ||132||

133ityukto jāmadagnyastu devadevena śūlinā
|
pratyuvāca mahātmānaṃ śirasāvanataḥ prabhum ||133||

134yadā jānāti deveśaḥ pātraṃ māmastradhāraṇe
|
tadā śuśrūṣave'strāṇi bhavānme dātumarhati ||134||

135duryodhana uvāca|
tataḥ sa tapasā caiva damena niyamena ca
|
pūjopahārabalibhirhomamantrapuraskṛtaiḥ ||135||

136ārādhayitavān śarvaṃ bahūnvarṣagaṇāṃstadā
|
prasannaśca mahādevo bhārgavasya mahātmanaḥ||136||

137abravīttasya bahuśo guṇāndevyāḥ samīpataḥ
|
bhaktimāneṣa satataṃ mayi rāmo dṛḍhavrataḥ ||137||

138evaṃ tasya guṇānprīto bahuśo'kathayatprabhuḥ
|
devatānāṃ pitṝṇāṃ ca samakṣamarisūdana ||138||

139etasminneva kāle tu daityā hyāsanmahābalāḥ
|
taistadā darpamohādyairabādhyanta divaukasaḥ ||139||

140tataḥ saṃbhūya vibudhāstān hantuṃ kṛtaniścayāḥ
|
cakruḥ śatruvadhe yatnaṃ na śekurjetumeva tān ||140||

141abhigamya tato devā maheśvaramumāpatim
|
prāsādayaṃstadā bhaktyā jahi śatrugaṇāniti ||141||

142pratijñāya tato devo devatānāṃ ripukṣayam
|
rāmaṃ bhārgavamāhūya so'bhyabhāṣata śaṅkaraḥ||142||

143ripūn bhārgava devānāṃ jahi sarvānsamāgatān
|
lokānāṃ hitakāmārthaṃ matprītyarthaṃ tathaiva ca ||143||

144evamuktaḥ pratyuvāca tryaṃbakaṃ varadaṃ prabhum
|
rāma uvāca|
kā śaktirmama deveśa akṛtāstrasya saṃyuge ||144||

145nihantuṃ dānavānsarvān kṛtāstrān yuddhadurmadān
|
maheśvara uvāca|
gaccha tvaṃ mada‌nujñāto nihaniṣyasi śātravān ||145||

146vijitya ca ripūnsarvān guṇānprāpsyasi puṣkalān
|
etacchrutvā tu vacanaṃ pratigṛhya ca sarvaśaḥ ||146||

147rāmaḥ kṛtasvastyayanaḥ prayayau dānavānprati
|
abravīddevaśatrūṃstān mahādarpabalānvitān ||147||

148mama yuddhaṃ prayacchadhvaṃ daityā yuddhamadotkaṭāḥ
|
preṣito devadevena vo vijetuṃ mahāsurāḥ ||148||

149ityuktā bhārgaveṇātha daityā yuddhaṃ pracakramuḥ
|
sa tānnihatya samare daityān bhārgavanandanaḥ ||149||

150vajrāśanisamaspararśaiḥ prahāraireva bhārgavaḥ
|
sa dānavaiḥ kṣatatanurjāmadagnyo dvijottamaḥ ||150||

151saṃspṛṣṭaḥ sthāṇunā sadyo nirvraṇaḥ samajāyata
|
prītaśca bhagavāndevaḥ karmaṇā tena tasya vai ||151||

152varān prādādbahuvidhāna bhārgavāya mahātmane
|
uktaśca devadevena prītiyuktena śūlinā ||152||

153nipātāttava śastrāṇāṃ śarīre yā'bhavadrujā
|
tayā te mānuṣaṃ karma vyapoḍhaṃ bhṛgunandana ||153||

154gṛhāṇāstrāṇi divyāni matsakāśādyathepsitam
|
duryodhana uvāca|
tato'strāṇi samastāni varāṃśca manasepsitān ||154||

155labdhvā bahuvidhānrāmaḥ praṇamya śirasā bhavam
|
anujñāṃ prāpya deveśājjagāma sa mahātapāḥ||155||

156evametatpurāvṛttaṃ tadā kathitavānṛṣiḥ
|
bhārgavo'pi dadau divyaṃ dhanurvedaṃ mahātmane ||156||

157karṇāya puruṣavyāghra suprītenāntarātmanā
|
vṛjinaṃ hi bhavetkiñcidyadi karṇasya pārthiva ||157||

158nāsmai hyastrāṇi divyāni prādāsyadbhṛgunandanaḥ
|
nāpi sūtakule jātaṃ karṇaṃ manye kathañcana ||158||

159devaputramahaṃ manye kṣatriyāṇāṃ kulodbhavam
|
visṛṣṭamavabodhārthaṃ kulasyeti matirmama ||159||

160sarvathā na hyayaṃ śalya karṇaḥ sūtakulodbhavaḥ
|
sakuṇḍalaṃ sakavacaṃ dīrghabāhuṃ mahāratham ||160||

161kathamādityasadṛśaṃ mṛgī vyāghraṃ janiṣyati
|
yathā hyasya bhujau pīnau nāgarājakaropamau ||161||

162vakṣaḥ paśya viśālaṃ ca sarvaśatrunibarhaṇam
|
na tveṣa prākṛtaḥ kaścit karṇo vaikartano vṛṣaḥ
|
mahātmā hyeṣa rājendra rāmaśiṣyaḥ pratāpavān ||162||
