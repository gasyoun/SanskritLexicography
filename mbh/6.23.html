Mahābhārata 6.23 (Bhīṣmaparvan)

1sañjaya uvāca|
dhārtarāṣṭrabalaṃ dṛṣṭvā yuddhāya samupasthitam
|
arjunasya hitārthāya kṛṣṇo vacanamabravīt ||1||

2śrībhagavānuvāca |
śucirbhūtvā mahābāho saṅgrāmābhimukhe sthitaḥ
|
parājayāya śatrūṇāṃ durgāstotramudīraya ||2||

3sañjaya uvāca|
evamukto'rjunaḥ saṅkhye vāsudevena dhīmatā
|
avatīrya rathātpārthaḥ stotramāha kṛtāñjaliḥ ||3||

4arjuna uvāca|
namaste siddhasenāni ārye mandaravāsini
|
kumāri kāli kāpāli kapile kṛṣṇapiṅgale ||4||

5bhadrakāli namastubhyaṃ mahākāli namo'stu te
|
caṇḍi caṇḍe namastubhyaṃ tāriṇi varavarṇini ||5||

6kātyāyani mahābhāge karāli vijaye jaye
|
śikhipicchadhvajadhare nānābharaṇabhūṣite ||6||

7aṭṭaśūlapraharaṇe svaṅgakheṭakadhāriṇi
|
gopendrasyānuje jyeṣṭhe nandagopakulodbhave ||7||

8mahiṣāsṛkpriye nityaṃ kauśiki pītavāsini
|
aṭṭahāse kokamukhe namaste'stu raṇapriye ||8||

9ubhe śākambhari śvete kṛṣṇe kaiṭabhanāśini
|
hiraṇyākṣi virūpākṣi sudhūmrākṣi namo'stu te ||9||

10vedaśruti mahāpuṇye brahmaṇye jātavedasi
|
jambūkaṭakacaityeṣu nityaṃ sannihitālaye ||10||

11tvaṃ brahmavidyā vidyānāṃ mahānidrā ca dehinām
|
skandamātarbhagavati durge kāntāravāsini ||11||

12svāhākāraḥ svadhā caiva kalā kāṣṭhā sarasvatī
|
sāvitrī vedamātā ca tathā vedānta ucyate ||12||

13stutāsi tvaṃ mahādevi viśuddhenāntarātmanā
|
jayo bhavatu me nityaṃ tvatprasādādraṇājire ||13||

14kāntārabhayadurgeṣu bhaktānāṃ cālayeṣu ca
|
nityaṃ vasasi pātāle yuddhe jayasi dānavān ||14||

15tvaṃ jambhanī mohinī ca māyā hrīḥ śrīstathaiva ca
|
sandhyā prabhāvatī caiva sāvitrī jananī tathā ||15||

16tuṣṭiḥ puṣṭirdhṛtirdīptiścandrādityavivardhinī
|
bhūtirbhūtimatāṃ saṅkhye vīkṣyase siddhacāraṇaiḥ ||16||

17sañjaya uvāca|
tataḥ pārthasya vijñāya bhaktiṃ mānavavatsalā
|
antarikṣagatovāca govindasyāgrataḥ sthitā ||17||

18devyuvāca |
svalpenaiva tu kālena śatrūñjeṣyasi pāṇḍava
|
narastvamasi durdharṣa nārāyaṇasahāyavān ||18||

19ajeyastvaṃ raṇe'rīṇāmapi vajrabhṛtaḥ svayam
|
ityevamuktvā varadā kṣaṇenāntaradhīyata ||19||

20labdhvā varaṃ tu kaunteyo mene vijayamātmanaḥ
|
āruroha tataḥ pārtho rathaṃ paramasammatam ||20||

21kṛṣṇārjunāvekarathau divyau śaṅkhauḥ pradadhmatuḥ
|
ya idaṃ paṭhate stotraṃ kalya utthāya mānavaḥ ||21||

22yakṣarakṣaḥpiśācebhyo na bhayaṃ vidyate sadā
|
na cāpi ripavastebhyaḥ sarpādyā ye ca daṃṣṭriṇaḥ ||22||

23na bhayaṃ vidyate tasya sadā rājakulādapi
|
vivāde jayamāpnoti baddho mucyati bandhanāt ||23||

24durgaṃ tarati cāvaśyaṃ tathā corairvimucyate
|
saṅgrāme vijayennityaṃ lakṣmīṃ prāpnoti kevalām ||24||

25ārogyabalasampanno jīvedvarṣaśataṃ tathā
|
etaddṛṣṭaṃ prasādāttu mayā vyāsasya dhīmataḥ ||25||

26mohādetau na jānanti naranārāyaṇāvṛṣī
|
tava putrā durātmānaḥ sarve manyuvaśānugāḥ ||26||

27prāptakālamidaṃ vākyaṃ kālapāśena kuṇṭhitāḥ
|
dvaipāyano nāradaśca kaṇvo rāmastathānaghaḥ
|
avārayaṃstava sutaṃ na cāsau tadgṛhītavān ||27||

28yatra dharmo dyutiḥ kāntiryatra hrīḥ śrīstathā matiḥ
|
yato dharmastataḥ kṛṣṇo yataḥ kṛṣṇastato jayaḥ ||28||
