Mahābhārata 6.87 (Bhīṣmaparvan)

1sañjaya uvāca|
pariṇāmya niśāṃ tāṃ tu sukhaṃ prāptā janeśvarāḥ
|
kuravaḥ pāṇḍavāścaiva punaryuddhāya niryayuḥ ||1||

2tataḥ śabdo mahānāsīt sainyayorubhayornṛpa
|
nirgacchamānayoḥ saṃkhye sāgarapratimo mahān ||2||

3tato duryodhano rājā citraseno viviṃśatiḥ
|
bhīṣmaśca rathināṃ śreṣṭho bhāradvājaśca vai nṛpa ||3||

4ekībhūtāḥ susaṃyattāḥ kauravāṇāṃ mahācamūm
|
vyūhāya vidadhū rājan pāṇḍavān prati daṃśitāḥ ||4||

5bhīṣmaḥ kṛtvā mahāvyūhaṃ pitā tava viśāṃpate
|
sāgarapratimaṃ ghoraṃ vāhanormitaraṅgiṇam ||5||

6agrataḥ sarvasainyānāṃ bhīṣmaḥ śāntanavo yayau
|
mālavairdākṣiṇātyaiśca āvantyaiśca samanvitaḥ ||6||

7tato'nantaramevāsīdbhāradvājaḥ pratāpavān
|
pulindaiḥ pāradaiścaiva tathā kṣudrakamālavaiḥ ||7||

8droṇādanantaraṃ yatto bhagadattaḥ pratāpavān
|
magadhaiśca kaliṅgaiśca piśācaiśca viśāṃpate ||8||

9prāgjyotiṣādanu nṛpaḥ kausalyo'tha bṛhadbalaḥ
|
mekalaiḥ kuruvindaiśca traipuraiśca samanvitaḥ ||9||

10bṛhadbalāt tataḥ śūrastrigartaḥ prasthalādhipaḥ
|
kāmbojairbahubhiḥ sārdhaṃ yavanaiśca sahasraśaḥ ||10||

11drauṇistu rabhasaḥ rastraigartāda bhārata
|
prayayau siṃhanādena nādayāno dharātalam ||11||

12tathā sarveṇa sainyena rājā duryodhanastadā
|
drauṇeranantaraṃ prāyāt sodaryaiḥ parivāritaḥ ||12||

13duryodhanādanu tataḥ kṛpaḥ śāradvato yayau
|
evameṣa mahāvyūhaḥ prayayau sāgaropamaḥ ||13||

14rejustatra patākāśca śvetacchatrāṇi vā vibho
|
aṅgadānyatra citrāṇi mahārhāṇi dhanūṃṣi ca ||14||

15taṃ tu dṛṣṭvā mahāvyūhaṃ tāvakānāṃ mahārathaḥ
|
yudhiṣṭhiro'bravīt tūrṇaṃ pārṣataṃ pṛtanāpatim ||15||

16paśya vyūhaṃ maheṣvāsa nirmitaṃ sāgaropamam
|
prativyūhaṃ tvamapi hi kuru pārṣata satvaram ||16||

17tataḥ sa pārṣataḥ krūro vyūhaṃ cakre sudāruṇam
|
śṛṅgāṭakaṃ mahārāja paravyūhavināśanam ||17||

18śṛṅgābhyāṃ bhīmasenaśca sātyakiśca mahārathaḥ
|
rathairanekasāhasraistathā hayapadātibhiḥ ||18||

19tābhyāṃ babhau naraśreṣṭhaḥ śvetāśvaḥ kṛṣṇasārathiḥ
|
madhye yudhiṣṭhiro rājā mādrīputrau ca pāṇḍavau ||19||

20athottare maheṣvāsāḥ sahasainyā narādhipāḥ
|
vyūha taṃ pūrayāmāsurvyūhaśāstraviśāradāḥ ||20||

21abhimanyustataḥ paścādvirāṭaśca mahārathaḥ
|
draupadeyāśca saṃhṛṣṭā rākṣasaśca ghaṭotkacaḥ ||21||

22evametaṃ mahāvyūhaṃ vyūhya bhārata pāṇḍavāḥ
|
atiṣṭhan samare śūrā yoddhukāmā jayaiṣiṇaḥ ||22||

23bherīśabdaiśca vimalairvibhiśraiḥ śaṅkhaniḥsvanaiḥ
|
kṣveḍitāsphoṭitotkṛṣṭairnāditāḥ sarvato diśaḥ ||23||

24tataḥ śūrāḥ samāsādya samare te parasparam
|
netrairanimiṣai rājannavaikṣanta parasparam ||24||

25nāmabhiste manuṣyendra pūrvaṃ yodhāḥ parasparam
|
yuddhāya samavartanta samāhūyetaretaram ||25||

26tataḥ pravavṛte yuddhaṃ ghorarūpaṃ bhayāvaham
|
tāvakānāṃ pareṣāṃ ca nighnatāmitaretaram ||26||

27nārācā niśitāḥ saṅkhye saṃpatanti sma bhārata
|
vyāttānanā bhayakarā uragā iva saṅghaśaḥ ||27||

28niṣpeturvimalāḥ śaktyastailadhautāḥ sutejanāḥ
|
ambudebhyo yathā rājan bhrājamānāḥ śatahradāḥ ||28||

29gadāśca vimalaiḥ paṭṭaiḥ pinaddhāḥ svarṇabhūṣitaiḥ
|
patantyastatra dṛśyante giriśṛṅgopamāḥ śubhāḥ ||29||

30nistriṃśāśca vyadṛśyanta vimalāmbarasannibhāḥ
|
ārṣabhāṇi vicitrāṇi śatacandrāṇi bhārata ||30||

31aśobhanta raṇe rājan pātyamānāni sarvaśaḥ
|
te'nyonyaṃ samare sene yudhyamāne narādhipa ||31||

32aśobhetāṃ yathā devadaityasene samudyate
|
abhyadravanta samare te'nyonyaṃ vai samantataḥ ||32||

33rathāstu rathibhistūrṇaṃ preṣitāḥ paramāhave
|
yugairyugāni saṃśliṣya yuyudhuḥ pārthivarṣabhāḥ ||33||

34daṃtināṃ yudhyamānānāṃ saṃgharṣāt pāvako'bhavat
|
danteṣu bharataśreṣṭha sadhūmaḥ sarvatodiśam ||34||

35prāsairabhihatāḥ kecidgajayodhāḥ samantataḥ
|
patamānāḥ sma dṛśyante giriśṛṅgānnagā iva ||35||

36pādātāścāpyadṛśyanta nighnanto'tha parasparam
|
citrarūpadharāḥ śūrā nakharaprāsayodhinaḥ ||36||

37anyonyaṃ te samāsādya kurupāṇḍavasainikāḥ
|
astrairnānāvidhairghorai raṇe ninyuryamakṣayam ||37||

38tataḥ śāntanavo bhīṣmo rathaghoṣeṇa nādayan
|
abhyāgamadraṇe pārthān dhanuḥśabdena mohayan ||38||

39pāṇḍavānāṃ rathāścāpi nadanto bhairavaṃ svanam
|
abhyadravanta saṃyattā dhṛṣṭadyumnapurogamāḥ ||39||

40tataḥ pravavṛte yuddhaṃ tava teṣāṃ ca bhārata
|
narāśvarathanāgānāṃ vyatiṣaktaṃ parasparam ||40||
