Mahābhārata 13.49 (Anuśāsanaparvan)

1yudhiṣṭhira uvāca|
brūhi tāta kuruśreṣṭha varṇānāṃ tvaṃ pṛthak pṛthakū
|
kīdṛśyāṃ kīdṛśāścāpi putrāḥ kasya ca ke ca te||1||

2vipravādāḥ subahavaḥ śrūyante putrakāritāḥ
|
atra no muhyatāṃ rājansaṃśayaṃ chettumarhasi ||2||

3bhīṣma uvāca|
ātmā putraśca vijñeyastasyānantarajaśca yaḥ
|
niruktajaśca vijñeyaḥ sutaḥ prasṛtajastathā ||3||

4patitasya tu bhāryāyā bhartrā susamavetayā
|
tathā dattakṛtau putrāvadhyūḍhaśca tathā'paraḥ ||4||

5ṣaḍapadhvaṃsajāścāpi kānīnāpasadāstathā
|
ityete vai samākhyātāstānvijānīhi bhārata||5||

6yudhiṣṭhira uvāca|
ṣaḍapadhvaṃsajāḥ ke syuḥ ke vāpyapasadāstathā
|
etatsarvaṃ yathātattvaṃ vyākhyātuṃ me tvamarhasi ||6||

7bhīṣma uvāca|
triṣu varṇeṣu ye putrā brāhmaṇasya yudhiṣṭhira
|
varṇayośca dvayoḥ syātāṃ yau rājanyasya bhārata ||7||

8eko viḍvarṇa evātha tathā'traivopalakṣitaḥ
|
ṣaḍapadhvaṃsajāste hi tathaivāpasadān śṛṇu ||8||

9cāṇḍālo vrātyavaidyau ca brāhmaṇyāṃ kṣatriyāsu ca
|
vaiśyāyāṃ caiva śudrasya lakṣyante'pasadāstrayaḥ ||9||

10māgadho vāmakaścaiva dvau vaiśyasyopalakṣitau
|
brāhmaṇyāṃ kṣatriyāyāṃ ca kṣatriyasyaika eva tu ||10||

11brāhmaṇyāṃ lakṣyate sūta ityete'pasadāḥ smṛtāḥ
|
putrā hyete na śakyante mithyākartuṃ narādhipa||11||

12yudhiṣṭhira uvāca|
kṣetrajaṃ kecidevāhuḥ sutaṃ kecittu śukrajam
|
tulyāvetau sutau kasya tanme brūhi pitāmaha ||12||

13bhīṣma uvāca|
retajo vā bhavetputrastyakto vā kṣetrajo bhavet
|
adhyūḍhaḥ samayaṃ bhittvetyetadeva nibodha me ||13||

14yudhiṣṭhira uvāca|
retajaṃ vidma vai putraṃ kṣetrajasyāgamaḥ katham
|
adhyūḍhaṃ vidma vai putraṃ bhittvā tu samayaṃ katham ||14||

15bhīṣma uvāca|
ātmajaṃ putramutpādya yastyajetkāraṇāntare
|
na tatra kāraṇaṃ retaḥ sa kṣetrasvāmino bhavet ||15||

16putrakāmo hi putrārthe yāṃ vṛṇīte viśāṃpate
|
kṣetrajaṃ tu pramāṇaṃ syānna ve tatrātmajaḥ sutaḥ||16||

17anyatra kṣetrajaḥ putro lakṣyate bharatarṣabha
|
na hyātmā śakyate hantuṃ dṛṣṭāntopagato hyasau||17||

18kvacicca kṛtakaḥ putraḥ saṃgrahādeva lakṣyate
|
na tatra retaḥ kṣetraṃ vā yatra lakṣyeta bhārata ||18||

19yudhiṣṭhira uvāca|
kīdṛśaḥ kṛtakaḥ putraḥ saṃgrahādeva lakṣyate
|
śukra kṣetraṃ pramāṇaṃ vā yatra lakṣyaṃ na bhārata ||19||

20bhīṣma uvāca|
mātāpitṛbhyāṃ yastyaktaḥ pathi yastaṃ prakalpayet
|
na cāsya mātāpitarau jñāyetāṃ sa hi kṛtrimaḥ ||20||

21asvāmikasya svāmitvaṃ yasminsaṃprati lakṣyate
|
yo varṇaḥ poṣayettaṃ ca tadvarṇastasya jāyate ||21||

22yudhiṣṭhira uvāca|
kathamasya prayoktavyaḥ saṃskāraḥ kasya vā katham
|
deyā kanyā kathaṃ ceti tanme brūhi pitāmaha ||22||

23bhīṣma uvāca|
ātmavattasya kurvīta saṃskāraṃ svāmivattathā
|
tyakto mātāpitṛbhyāṃ yaḥ savarṇaṃ pratipadyate||23||

24tadgotrabandhujaṃ tasya kuryātsaṃskāramacyuta
|
atha deyā tu kanyā syāttadvarṇasya yudhiṣṭhira ||24||

25saṃskartuṃ varṇagotraṃ ca mātṛvarṇaviniścaye
|
kānīnādhyūḍhajau vāpi vijñeyau putra kilbiṣau ||25||

26tāvapi svāviva sutau saṃskāryāviti niścayaḥ
|
kṣetrajo vāpyapasado ye'dhyūḍhāsteṣu cāpyuta||26||

27ātmavadvai prayuñjīransaṃskārānbrāhmaṇādayaḥ
|
dharmaśāstreṣu varṇānāṃ niścayo'yaṃ pradṛśyate ||27||

28etatte sarvamākhyātaṃ kiṃ bhūyaḥ śrotumicchasi||28||
