Mahābhārata 9.6 (Śalyaparvan)

1sañjaya uvāca|
atha haimavate prasthe sthitvā yuddhābhinandinaḥ
|
sarva eva mahāyodhāstatra tatra samāgatāḥ||1||

2śalyaśca citrasenaśca śakuniśca mahārathaḥ
|
aśvatthāmā kṛpaścaiva kṛtavarmā ca sātvataḥ||2||

3suṣeṇo'riṣṭasenaśca dhṛtasenaśca vīryavān
|
jayatsenaśca rājānaste rātrimuṣitāstataḥ||3||

4raṇe karṇe hate vīre trāsitā jitakāśibhiḥ
|
nālabhaśarma te putrā himavantamṛte girim||4||

5te'bruvansahitāstatra rājānaṃ śalyasannidhau
|
kṛtayatnā raṇe rājansampūjya vidhivattadā||5||

6kṛtvā senāpraṇetāraṃ parāṃstvaṃ yodbhumarhasi
|
yenābhiguptāḥ saṅgrāme jayemāsuhṛdo vayam||6||

7tato duryodhanaḥ sthitvā rathe rathavarottamam
|
sarvayuddhavibhāvajñamantakapratimaṃ yudhi||7||

8svaṅgaṃ pracchannaśirasaṃ kambugrīvaṃ priyaṃvadam
|
vyākośapadmapatrākṣaṃ vyāghrāsyaṃ merugauravam||8||

9sthāṇovṛṣasya sadṛśaṃ skandhanetragatisvaraiḥ
|
puṣṭaśliṣṭāyatabhujaṃ suvistīrṇavarorasam||9||

10bale jave ca sadṛśamaruṇānujavātayoḥ
|
ādityasyārciṣā tulyaṃ buddhyā cośanasā samam||10||

11kāntirūyamukhaiśvaryaistribhiścandramasā samam
|
kāñcanopalasaṅghātaiḥ sadṛśaṃ śliṣṭasandhikam||11||

12suvṛttorukaṭījaṅghaṃ supādaṃ svaṅgulīnakham
|
smṛtvā smṛtvaiva tu guṇāndhātrā yatnādvinirmitam||12||

13sarvalakṣaṇasampannaṃ nipuṇaṃ śrutisāgaram
|
jetāraṃ tarasārīṇāmajeyamaribhirbalāt||13||

14daśāṅgaṃ yaścatuṣpādamiṣvastraṃ veda tattvataḥ
|
sāṅgāṃstu caturo vedānsamyagākhyānapañcamān||14||

15ārādhya tryambakaṃ yatnādvratairugrairmahātapāḥ
|
ayonijāyāmutpanno droṇenāyonijena yaḥ||15||

16tamapratimakarmāṇaṃ rūpeṇāpratimaṃ bhuvi
|
pāragaṃ sarvavidyānāṃ guṇārṇavamaninditam||16||

17tamabhyetyātmajastubhyamaśvatthāmānamabravīt
|
yaṃ puraskṛtya sahitā yudhi jeṣyāma pāṇḍavān||17||

18guruputro'dya sarveṣāmasmākaṃ paramā gatiḥ
|
bhavāṃstasmānniyogātte ko'stu senāpatirmama||18||

19drauṇiruvāca|
ayaṃ kulena rūpeṇa tejasā yaśasā śriyā
|
sarvairguṇaiḥ samuditaḥ śalyo no'stu camūpatiḥ||19||

20bhāgineyānnijāṃstyaktvā kṛtajño'smānupāgataḥ
|
mahāseno mahābāhurmahāsena ivāparaḥ||20||

21enaṃ senāpatiṃ kṛtvā nṛpatiṃ nṛpasattama
|
śakyaḥ prāptuṃ jayo'smābhirdevaiḥ skandamivājitam||21||

22tathokte droṇaputreṇa sarva eva narādhipāḥ
|
parivārya sthitāḥ śalyaṃ jayaśabdāṃśca cakrire||22||

23yuddhāya ca matiṃ cakrurāveśaṃ ca paraṃ yayuḥ
|
tato duryodhano bhūmau sthitvā rathavare sthitam||23||

24uvāca prāñjalirbhūtvā droṇabhīṣmasamaṃ raṇe
|
ayaṃ sa kālaḥ samprāpto mitrāṇāṃ mitravatsala||24||

25yatra mitramamitraṃ vā parīkṣante budhā janāḥ
|
sa bhavānastu naḥ śūraḥ praṇetā vāhinīmukhe||25||

26raṇaṃ yāte ca bhavati pāṇḍavā mandacetasaḥ
|
bhaviṣyanti sahāmātyaḥ pañcālāśca nirudyamāḥ||26||

27duryodhanavacaḥ śrutvā śalyo madrādhipastadā
|
uvāca vākyaṃ vākyajño rājānaṃ rājasannidhau||27||

28yattu māṃ manyase rājan kururāja karomi tat
|
tvatpriyārthaṃ hi me sarvaṃ prāṇā rājyaṃ dhanāni ca||28||

29duryodhana uvāca|
saināpatyena varaye tvāmahaṃ mātulātulam
|
so'smānpāhi yudhāṃ śreṣṭha skando devānivāhave||29||

30abhiṣicyasva rājendra devānāmiva pāvakiḥ
|
jahi śatrūn raṇe vīra mahendro dānavāniva||30||
