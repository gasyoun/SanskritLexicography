Mahābhārata 1.157 (Ādiparvan)

1janamejaya uvāca|
ekacakrāṃ gatāste tu kuntīputrā mahārathāḥ
|
ata ūrdhvaṃ dvijaśreṣṭha kimakurvata pāṇḍavāḥ||1||

2vaiśaṃpāyana uvāca|
ekacakrāṃ gatāste tu kantīputrā mahārathāḥ
|
ūṣurnāticiraṃ kālaṃ brāhmaṇasya niveśane||2||

3ramaṇīyāni paśyanto vanāni vividhāni ca
|
pārthivānapi coddeśānsaritaśca sarāṃsi ca||3||

4cerurbhaiśraṃ tadā te tu sarva eva viśāṃpate
|
babhūvurnāgarāṇāṃ ca svairguṇaiḥ saṃpriyadarśanāḥ||4||

5nivedayanti sma tadā kuntyā bhaikṣyaṃ sadā niśi
|
tayā vibhaktānbhāgāṃste bhuñjate sma pṛthakpṛthak||5||

6ardhaṃ te bhuñjate vīrāḥ saha mātrā parantapāḥ
|
ardhaṃ sarvasya bhaikṣasya bhīmo bhuṅkte mahābalaḥ||6||

7tathā tu teṣāṃ vasatāṃ tasminrāṣṭre mahātmanām
|
aticakrāma sumahānkālo'tha bharatarṣabha||7||

8tataḥ kadācidbhaikṣāya gatāste puruṣarṣabhāḥ
|
saṃgatyā bhīmasenastu tatrāste pṛthayā saha||8||

9athārtijaṃ mahāśabdaṃ brāhmaṇasya niveśane
|
bhṛśamutpatitaṃ ghoraṃ kuntī śuśrāva bhārata||9||

10roruyamāṇāṃstāndṛṣṭvā paridevayataśca sā
|
kāruṇyātsādhubhāvācca kuntī rājanna cakṣame||10||

11mathyamāneva duḥkhena hṛdayena pṛthā tadā
|
uvāca bhīmaṃ kalyāṇī kṛpānvitamidaṃ vacaḥ||11||

12vasāma susukhaṃ putra brāhmaṇasya niveśane
|
ajñātā dhārtarāṣṭrasya satkṛtā vītamanyavaḥ||12||

13sā cintaye sadā putra brāhmaṇasyāsya kiṃ nvaham
|
priyaṃ kuryāmiti gṛhe yatkuryuruṣitāḥ sukham||13||

14etāvānpuruṣastāta kṛtaṃ yasminna naśyati
|
yāvacca kuryādanyo'sya kuryādabhyadhikaṃ tataḥ||14||

15tadidaṃ brāhmaṇasyāsya duḥkhamāpatitaṃ dhruvam
|
tatrāsya yadi sāhāyyaṃ kuryāmupakṛtaṃ bhavet||15||

16bhīmasena uvāca|
jñāyatāmasya yadduḥkhaṃ yataścaiva samutthitam
|
viditvā vyavasiṣyāmi yadyapi syātsuduṣkaram||16||

17vaiśaṃpāyana uvāca|
evaṃ tau kathayantau ca bhūyaḥ suśruvatuḥ svanam
|
ārtijaṃ tasya viprasya sabhāryasya viśāṃpate||17||

18antaḥpuraṃ tatastasya brāhmaṇasya mahātmanaḥ
|
viveśa tvaritā kuntī baddhavatseva saurabhī||18||

19tatastaṃ brāhmaṇaṃ tatra bhāryayā ca sutena ca
|
duhitrā caiva sahitaṃ dadarśavanatānanam||19||

20brāhmaṇa uvāca|
dhigidaṃ jīvitaṃ loke gatasāramanarthakam
|
duḥkhamūlaṃ parādhīnaṃ bhṛśamapriyabhāgi ca||20||

21jīvite paramaṃ duḥkhaṃ jīvite paramo jvaraḥ
|
jīvite vartamānasya dvandvānāmāgamo dhruvaḥ||21||

22ātmāhyeko hi dharmārthau kāmaṃ caiva niṣevate
|
etaiśca viprayogo'pi duḥkhaṃ paramanantakam||22||

23āhuḥ kecitparaṃ mokṣaṃ sa ca nāsti kathaṃcana
|
arthaprāptau tu narakaḥ kṛtsna evopapadyate||23||

24arthepsutā paraṃ duḥkhamarthaprāptau tato'dhikam
|
jātasnehasya cārtheṣu viprayoge mahattaram||24||

25na hi yogaṃ prapaśyāmi yena mucyeyamāpadaḥ
|
putradāreṇa vā sārdhaṃ prādraveyamanāmayam||25||

26yatitaṃ vai mayā pūrvaṃ vettha brāhmaṇi tattathā
|
kṣemaṃ yatastato gantuṃ tvayā tu mama na śrutam||26||

27iha jātā vivṛddhā'smi pitā mātā mameti vai
|
uktavatyasi durmedhe yācyamānā mayā'sakṛt||27||

28svargato'pi pitā vṛddhastathā mātā ciraṃ tava
|
bāndhavā bhūtapūrvāśca tatra vāse tu kā ratiḥ||28||

29so'yaṃ te bandhukāmāyā aśṛṇvantyā vaco mama
|
bandhupraṇāśaḥ saṃprāpto bhṛśaṃ duḥkhakaro mama||29||

30athavā madvināśo'yaṃ na hi śakṣyāmi kaṃcana
|
parityaktumahaṃ bandhuṃ svayaṃ jīvannṛśaṃsavat||30||

31sahadharmacarīṃ dāntāṃ nityaṃ mātṛsamāṃ mama
|
sakhāyaṃ vihitāṃ devairnityaṃ paramikāṃ gatim||31||

32pitrā mātrā ca vihitāṃ sadā gārhasthyabhāginīm
|
varayitvā yathānyāyaṃ mantravatpariṇīya ca||32||

33kulīnāṃ śīlasaṃpannāmapatyajananīmapi
|
tvāmahaṃ jīvitasyārthe sādhvīmanapakāriṇīm||33||

34parityaktuṃ na śakṣyāmi bhāryāṃ nityamanuvratām
|
kuta eva parityaktuṃ sutāṃ śakṣyāmyahaṃ svayam||34||

35bālāmaprāptavayasamajātavyañjanākṛtim
|
bharturarthāya nikṣiptāṃ nyāsaṃ dhātrā mahātmanā||35||

36yayā dauhitrajā~llokānāśaṃse pitṛbhiḥ saha
|
svayamutpādya tāṃ bālāṃ kathamutsraṣṭumutsahe||36||

37manyante kecidadhikaṃ snehaṃ putre piturnarāḥ
|
kanyāyāṃ kecidapare mama tulyāvubhau smṛtau||37||

38yasyāṃ lokāḥ prasūtiśca sthitā nityamatho sukham
|
apāpāṃ tāmahaṃ bālāṃ kathamutsraṣṭumutsahe||38||

39ātmānamapi cotsṛjya tapsyāmi paralokagaḥ
|
tyaktā hyete mayā vyaktaṃ neha śakṣyanti jīvitum||39||

40eṣāṃ cānyatamatyāgo nṛśaṃso garhito budhaiḥ
|
ātmatyāge kṛte ceme mariṣyanti mayā vinā||40||

41sa kṛcchrāmahamāpanno na śaktastartumāpadam
|
aho dhikkāṃ gatiṃ tvadya gamiṣyāmi sabāndhavaḥ
|
sarvaiḥ saha mṛtaṃ śreyo na ca me jīvituṃ kṣamam||41||
