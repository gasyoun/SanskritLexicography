Mahābhārata 8.68 (Karṇaparvan)

1sañjaya uvāca|
śrutvā karṇaṃ kalpamudāravīryaṃ kruddhaḥ pārthaḥ phālgunasyāmitaujāḥ
|
dhanañjayaṃ vākyamuvāca cedaṃ yudhiṣṭhiraḥ karṇaśarābhitaptaḥ ||1||

2vipradrutā tāta camūstvadīyātiraskṛtā cādya yathā na sādhu
|
bhīto bhīmaṃ tyajya cāyāstathā tvaṃ yannāśakaḥ karṇamatho nihantum ||2||

3snehastvayā pārtha kṛtaḥ pṛthāyā garbhaṃ samāviśya yathā na sādhu
|
tyaktvā raṇe yadapāyāḥ sa bhīmaṃ yannāśakaḥ sūtaputraṃ nihantum ||3||

4yattadvākyaṃ dvaitavane tvayoktaṃ karṇaṃ hantā'smyekarathena satyam
|
tyaktvā taṃ vai kathamadyāpayātaḥ karṇādbhīto bhīmasenaṃ vihāya ||4||

5idaṃ yadi dvaitavane'pyacakṣaḥ karṇaṃ yoddhuṃ na praśakṣye nṛpeti
|
vayaṃ tataḥ prāptakālaṃ ca sarve kṛtyānyupaiṣyāma tathaiva pārtha ||5||

6mayi pratiśrutya vadhaṃ hi tasya na vai kṛtaṃ tacca tathaiva vīra
|
ānīya naḥ śatrumadhyaṃ sa kasmātsamutkṣipya sthaṇḍile pratyarpiṣṭhāḥ ||6||

7apyāśiṣma vayamarjuna tvayi yiyāsavo bahukalyāṇamiṣṭam
|
tannaḥ sarvaṃ viphalaṃ rājaputra phalārthināṃ viphala ivātipuṣpaḥ ||7||

8pracchāditaṃ baḍiśamivābhiṣeṇa saṃcchāditaṃ garalamivāśanena
|
anarthakaṃ me darśitavānasi tvaṃ rājyārthino rājyarūpaṃ vināśam ||8||

9trayodaśemā hi samāḥ sadā vayaṃ tvāmanvajīviṣma dhanañjayāśayā
|
kāle varṣaṃ devamivoptabījaṃ tannaḥ sarvānnarake tvaṃ nyamajjaḥ ||9||

10yattatpṛthāṃ vāguvācāntarikṣe saptāhajāte tvayi mandabuddhau
|
jātaḥ putro vāsavavikramo'yaṃ sarvān śūrān śātravāñjeṣyatīti ||10||

11ayaṃ jetā khāṇḍave devasaṅghān sarvāṇi bhūtānyapi cottamaujāḥ
|
ayaṃ jetā madra-kaliṅga-kekayānayaṃ kurūnrājamadhye nihantā ||11||

12asmātparo no bhavitā dhanurdharo nainaṃ bhūtaṃ kiñcana jātu jetā
|
icchannayaṃ sarvabhūtāni kuryādvaśe vaśī sarvasamāptavidyaḥ ||12||

13kāntyā śaśāṅkasya javena vāyoḥ sthairyeṇa meroḥ kṣamayā pṛthivyāḥ
|
sūyasya bhāsā dhanadasya lakṣmyā śauryeṇa śakrasya balena viṣṇoḥ ||13||

14tulyo mahātmā tava kuntiputro jāto'diterviṣṇurivārihantā
|
sveṣāṃ jayāya dviṣatāṃ vadhāya khyāto'mitaujāḥ kulatantukartā ||14||

15ityantarikṣe śataśṛṅgamūrdhni tapasvināṃ śṛṇvatāṃ vāguvāca
|
evaṃvidhaṃ tacca nābhūttathā ca devā'pi nūnamanṛtaṃ vadanti ||15||

16tathā pareṣāmṛṣisattamānāṃ śrutvā giraḥ pūjayatāṃ sadā tvām
|
na saṃnatiṃ praimi suyodhanasya na tvāṃ jānāmyādhiratherbhayārtam ||16||

17pūrvaṃ yaduktaṃ hi suyodhanena na phālgunaḥ pramukhe sthāsyatīti
|
karṇasya yuddhe hi mahābalasya maurkhyāttu tannāvabuddhaṃ mayā''sīt ||17||

18tenādya tapsye bhṛśamaprameyaṃ yacchatruvarge narakaṃ praviṣṭaḥ
|
tadaiva vācyo'smi nanu tvayā'haṃ na yotsye'haṃ sūtaputraṃ kathañcit ||18||

19tato nāhaṃ sṛñjayānkekayāṃśca samānayeyaṃ suhṛdo raṇāya
|
evaṃ gate kiñca mayā'dya śakyaṃ kāryaṃ kartuṃ vigrahe sūtajasya ||19||

20tathaiva rājñaśca suyodhanasya ye vāpi māṃ yoddhukāmāḥ sametāḥ
|
dhigastu majjīvitamadya kṛṣṇa yo'haṃ vaśaṃ sūtaputrasya yātaḥ ||20||

21madhye kurūṇāṃ suhṛdāṃ ca madhye ye cāpyanye yoddhukāmāḥ sametāḥ
|
yadi sma jīvet sa bhavennihantā mahārathānāṃ pravaro rathottamaḥ
|
tavābhimanyustanayo'dya pārtha na cāsmi gantā samare parābhavam ||21||

22athāpi jīvetsamare ghaṭotkacastathāpi nāhaṃ samare parāṅmukhaḥ
|
mama hyabhāgyāni purā kṛtāni pāpāni nūnaṃ balavanti yuddhe ||22||

23tṛṇaṃ ca kṛtvā samare bhavantaṃ tato'hamevaṃ nikṛto durātmanā
|
vaikartanenaiva tathā kṛto'haṃ yathā hyaśaktaḥ kriyate hyabāndhavaḥ ||23||

24āpadgataṃ kaścana yo vimokṣetsa bāndhavaḥ snehayuktaṃ suhṛcca
|
evaṃ purāṇā munayo vadanti dharmaḥ sadā sadbhiranuṣṭhitaśca ||24||

25tvaṣṭrā kṛtaṃ vāhamakūjanākṣaṃ śubhaṃ samāsthāya kapidhvajaṃ tam
|
khaḍgaṃ gṛhītvā hemapaṭṭānubaddhaṃ dhanuścedaṃ gāṇḍivaṃ tālamātram ||25||

26sa keśavenohyamānaḥ kathaṃ tvaṃ karṇādbhīto vyapayāto'si pārtha
|
dhanuśca tat keśavāya prayaccha yantā bhaviṣyastvaṃ raṇe keśavasya ||26||

27tadā haniṣyatkeśavaḥ karṇamugraṃ marutpatirvṛtramivāttavajraḥ
|
rādheyametaṃ yadi nādya śaktaścarantamugraṃ pratibādhanāya ||27||

28prayacchānyasmai gāṇḍivametadadya tvatto yo'strairabhyadhiko vā narendraḥ
|
asmānnaivaṃ putradārairvihīnānsukhādbhraṣṭān rājyanāśācca bhūyaḥ ||28||

29draṣṭā lokaḥ patitānapyagādhe pāpairjuṣṭe narake pāṇḍaveya
|
māse'patiṣyaḥ pañcame tvaṃ sukṛcchre na vā garbhe ābhaviṣyaḥ pṛthāyāḥ ||29||

30tatte śreyo rājaputrābhaviṣyanna cetsaṃgrāmādapayānaṃ durātman
|
dhiggāṇḍīvaṃ dhik ca te bāhuvīryamasaṃkhyeyān bāṇagaṇāṃśca dhikte
|
dhikte ketuṃ kesariṇaḥ sutasya kṛśānudattaṃ ca rathaṃ ca dhikte ||30||
