Mahābhārata 5.73 (Udyogaparvan)

1śrībhagavānuvāca
|
sañjayasya śrutaṃ vākyaṃ bhavataśca śrutaṃ mayā
|
sarvaṃ jānāmyabhiprāyaṃ teṣāṃ ca bhavataśca yaḥ ||1||

2tava dharmāśritā buddhisteṣāṃ vairāśrayā matiḥ
|
yadayuddhena labhyeta tatte bahumataṃ bhavet ||2||

3na caivaṃ naiṣṭhikaṃ karma kṣatriyasya viśāmpate
|
āhurāśramiṇaḥ sarve na bhaikṣaṃ kṣatriyaścaret ||3||

4jayo vadho vā saṃgrāme dhātrā''diṣṭaḥ sanātanaḥ
|
svadharmaḥ kṣatriyasyaiṣa kārpaṇyaṃ na praśasyate ||4||

5na hi kārpaṇyamāsthāya śakyā vṛttiryudhiṣṭhira
|
vikramasva mahābāho jahi śatrūn parantapa ||5||

6atigṛddhāḥ kṛtasnehā dīrghakālaṃ sahoṣitāḥ
|
kṛtamitrāḥ kṛtabalā dhārtarāṣṭrāḥ paraṃtapa ||6||

7na paryāyo'sti yatsāmyaṃ tvayi kuryurviśāṃpate
|
balavattāṃ hi manyante bhīṣmadroṇakṛpādibhiḥ ||7||

8yāvacca mārdavenaitānrājannupacariṣyasi
|
tāvadete hariṣyanti tava rājyamarindame ||8||

9nānukrośānna kārpaṇyānna ca dharmārthakāraṇāt
|
alaṅkartuṃ dhārtarāṣṭrāstava kāmamarindama ||9||

10etadeva nimittaṃ te pāṇḍavāstu yathā tvayi
|
nānvatapyanta kaupīnaṃ tāvatkṛtvā'pi duṣkaram ||10||

11pitāmahasya droṇasya vidurasya ca dhīmataḥ
|
brāhmaṇānāṃ ca sādhūnāṃ rājñaśca nagarasya ca ||11||

12paśyatāṃ kurumukhyānāṃ sarveṣāmeva tattvataḥ
|
dānaśīlaṃ mṛduṃ dāntaṃ dharmaśīlamanuvratam ||12||

13yattvāmupadhinā rājan dyūte vañcitavāṃstadā
|
na cāpatrapate tena nṛśaṃsaḥ svena karmaṇā ||13||

14tathā śīlasamācāre rājan mā praṇayaṃ kṛthāḥ
|
vadhyāste sarvalokasya kiṃ punastava bhārata ||14||

15vāgbhistvapratirūpābhiratudattvāṃ sahānujam
|
ślāghamānaḥ prahṛṣṭaḥ san bhrātṛbhiḥ saha bhāṣate ||15||

16etāvat pāṇḍavānāṃ hi nāsti kiñcidiha svakam
|
nāmadheyaṃ ca gotraṃ ca tadapyeṣāṃ na śiṣyate ||16||

17kālena mahatā caiṣāṃ bhaviṣyati parābhavaḥ
|
prakṛtiṃ te bhajiṣyanti naṣṭaprakṛtayo mayi ||17||

18duḥśāsanena pāpena tadā dyūte pravartite
|
anāthavattadā devī draupadī sudurātmanā ||18||

19ākṛṣya keśe rudatī sabhāyāṃ rājasaṃsadi
|
bhīṣmadroṇapramukhato gauriti vyāhṛtā muhuḥ ||19||

20bhavatā vāritāḥ sarve bhrātaro bhīmavikramāḥ
|
dharmapāśanibaddhāśca na kiñcit pratipedire ||20||

21etāścānyāśca paruṣā vācaḥ sa samudīrayan
|
ślāghate jñātimadhye sma tvayi pravrajite vanam ||21||

22ye tatrāsan samānītāste dṛṣṭvā tvāmanāgasam
|
aśrukaṇṭhā rudantaśca sabhāyāmāsate sadā ||22||

23na cainamabhyanandaṃste rājāno brāhmaṇaiḥ saha
|
sarve duryodhanaṃ tatra nindanti sma sabhāsadaḥ ||23||

24kulīnasya ca yā nindā vadho vā'mitrakarśana
|
mahāguṇo vadho rājanna tu nindā kujīvikā ||24||

25tadaiva nihato rājan yadaiva nirapatrapaḥ
|
ninditaśca mahārāja pṛthivyāṃ sarvarājabhiḥ ||25||

26īṣatkāryo vadhastasya yasya cāritramīdṛśam
|
praskundena pratistabdhaśchinnamūla iva drumaḥ ||26||

27vadhyaḥ sarpa ivānāryaḥ sarvalokasya durmatiḥ
|
jahyenaṃ tvamamitraghna mā rājan vicikitsithāḥ ||27||

28sarvathā tvatkṣamaṃ caitadrocate ca mamānagha
|
yattvaṃ pitari bhīṣme ca praṇipātaṃ samācareḥ ||28||

29ahaṃ tu sarvalokasya gatvā chetsyāmi saṃśayam
|
yeṣāmasti dvidhābhāvo rājan duryodhanaṃ prati ||29||

30madhye rājñāmahaṃ tatra prātipauruṣikān guṇān
|
tava saṅkīrtayiṣyāmi ye ca tasya vyatikramāḥ ||30||

31bruvatastatra me vākyaṃ dharmārthasahitaṃ hitam
|
niśamya pārthivāḥ sarve nānājanapadeśvarāḥ ||31||

32tvayi sampratipatsyante dharmātmā satyavāgiti
|
tasiṃścādhigamiṣyanti yathā lobhādavartata ||32||

33garhayiṣyāmi caivainaṃ paurajānapadeṣvapi
|
vṛddhabālānupādāya cāturvarṇye samāgate ||33||

34śamaṃ vai yācamānastvaṃ nādharmaṃ tatra lapsyase
|
kurūn vigarhayiṣyanti dhṛtarāṣṭraṃ ca pārthivāḥ ||34||

35tasmin lokaparityakte kiṃ kāryamavaśiṣyate
|
hate duryodhane rājan yadanyatkriyatāmiti ||35||

36yātvā cāhaṃ kurūn sarvān yuṣmadarthamahāpayan
|
yatiṣye praśamaṃ kartuṃ lakṣayiṣye ca ceṣṭitam ||36||

37kauravāṇāṃ pravṛttiṃ ca gatvā yuddhādhikārikām
|
niśamya vinivartiṣye jayāya tava bhārata ||37||

38sarvathā yuddhamevāhamāśaṃsāmi paraiḥ saha
|
nimittāni hi sarvāṇi tathā prādurbhavanti me ||38||

39mṛgāḥ śakuntāśca vadanti ghoraṃ hastyaśvamukhyeṣu niśāmukheṣu
|
ghorāṇi rūpāṇi tathaiva cāgnirvarṇān bahūn puṣyati ghorarūpān ||39||

40manuṣyalokakṣayakṛt sughoro no cedanuprāpta ihāntakaḥ syāt
|
śastrāṇi yantraṃ kavacā rathāṃśca nāgān hayāṃśca pratipādayitvā ||40||

41yodhāśca sarve kṛtaniścayāste bhavantu hastyaśvaratheṣu yattāḥ
|
sāṅgrāmikaṃ te yadupārjanīyaṃ sarvaṃ samagraṃ kuru tannarendra ||41||

42duryodhano na hyalamadya dātuṃ jīvaṃstavaitannṛpate kathañcit
|
yatte purastādabhavatsamṛddhaṃ dyūte hṛtaṃ pāṇḍavamukhya rājyam ||42||
