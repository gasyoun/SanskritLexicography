Mahābhārata 13.56 (Anuśāsanaparvan)

1cyavana uvāca|
avaśyaṃ kathanīyaṃ me tavaitannarapuṅgava
|
yadarthaṃ tvāhamucchettuṃ saṃprāpto manujādhipa ||1||

2bhṛgūṇāṃ kṣatriyā yājyā nityametajjanādhipa
|
te ca bhedaṃ gamiṣyanti daivayuktena hetunā ||2||

3kṣatriyāśca bhṛgūnsarvānvadhiṣyanti narādhipa
|
ā garbhādanukṛntanto daivadaṇḍanipīḍatāḥ ||3||

4tata utpatsyate'smākaṃ kule gotravivardhanaḥ
|
ūrvo nāma mahātejā jvalanārkasamadyutiḥ ||4||

5sa trailokyavināśāya kopāgniṃ janayiṣyati
|
mahīṃ saparvatavānāṃ yaḥ kariṣyati bhasmasāt ||5||

6kañcitkālaṃ tu vahniṃ ca sa eva śamayiṣyati
|
samudre vaḍavāvaktre prakṣipya munisattamaḥ ||6||

7putraṃ tasya mahārājā ṛcīkaṃ bhṛgunandanam
|
sākṣātkṛtsno dhanurvedaḥ samupasthāsyate'nagha ||7||

8kṣatriyāṇāmabhāvāya dauvayuktena hetunā
|
sa tu taṃ pratigṛhyaiva putre saṃkrāmayiṣyati ||8||

9jamadagnau mahābhāge tapasā bhāvitātmani
|
sa cāpi bhṛguśārdulastaṃ vedaṃ dhārayiṣyati ||9||

10kulāttu tava dharmātmankanyāṃ so'dhigamiṣyati
|
udbhāvanārthaṃ bhavato vaṃśasya bharatarṣabha||10||

11gādherduhitaraṃ prāpya pautrīṃ tava mahātapāḥ
|
brāhmaṇaṃ kṣatradharmāṇaṃ putramutpāda‌yiṣyati ||11||

12kṣatriyaṃ viprakarmāṇaṃ bṛhaspatimivaujasā
|
viśvāmitraṃ tava kule gādheḥ putraṃ sudhārmikam ||12||

13tapasā mahatā yuktaṃ pradāsyati mahādyute
|
striyau tu kāraṇaṃ tatra parivarte bhaviṣyataḥ ||13||

14pitāmahaniyogādvai nānyathaitadbhaviṣyati
|
tṛtīye puruṣe tubhyaṃ brāhmaṇatvamupaiṣyati
|
bhavitā tvaṃ ca sambandhī bhṛgūṇāṃ bhāvitātmanām ||14||

15bhīṣma uvāca|
kuśikastu munervākyaṃ cyavanasya mahātmanaḥ
|
śrutvā hṛṣṭo'bhavadrājā vākyaṃ ceda‌muvāca ha ||15||

16evamastviti dharmātmā tadā bharatasattama
|
cyavanastu mahātejāḥ punareva narādhipam ||16||

17varārthaṃ codayāmāsa tamuvāca sa pārthivaḥ
|
bāḍhamevaṃ kariṣyāmi kāmaṃ tvatto mahāmune ||17||

18brahmabhūtaṃ kulaṃ me'stu dharme cāsya mano bhavet ||18||

19evamuktastathetyevaṃ pratyuktvā cyavano muniḥ
|
abhyanujñāya nṛpatiṃ tīrthayātrāṃ yayau tadā ||19||

20etatte kathitaṃ sarvamaśeṣeṇa mayā nṛpa
|
bhṛgūṇāṃ kuśikānāṃ ca abhisambandhakāraṇam ||20||

21yathoktamṛṣiṇā cāpi tadā tadabhavannṛpa
|
janma rāmasya ca munerviśvāmitrasya caiva hi ||21||
