Mahābhārata 12.175 (Śāntiparvan)

1yudhiṣṭhira uvāca|
atikrāmati kāle'smin sarvabhūtakṣayāvahe
|
kiṃ śreyaḥ pratipadyeta tanme brūhi pitāmaha ||1||

2bhīṣma uvāca|
atrāpyudāharantīmamitihāsaṃ purātanam
|
pituḥ putreṇa saṃvādaṃ taṃ nibodha yudhiṣṭhira ||2||

3dvijāteḥ kasyacitpārtha svādhyāyaniratasya vai
|
babhūva putro medhāvī medhāvī nāma nāmataḥ ||3||

4so'bravītpitaraṃ putraḥ svādhyāyakaraṇe ratam
|
mokṣadharmārthakuśalo lokatattvavicakṣaṇaḥ ||4||

5putra uvāca |
dhīraḥ kiṃsvittāta kuryātprajānan kṣipraṃ hyāyurbhraśyate mānavānām
|
pitastadācakṣva yathārthayogaṃ māmanupūvyāṃ yena dharmaṃ careyam ||5||

6pitovāca |
vedānadhītya brahmacaryeṇa putra putrānicchetpāvanārthaṃ pitṝṇām
|
agnīnādhāya vivivacceṣṭayajño vanaṃ praviśyātha munirbubhūṣet ||6||

7putra uvāca |
evamabhyāhate loke samantātparivārite
|
amoghāsu patantīṣu kiṃ dhīra iva bhāṣase ||7||

8pitovāca |
kathamabhyāhato lokaḥ kena vā parivāritaḥ
|
amoghāḥ kāḥ patantīha kiṃ nu bhīṣayasīva mām ||8||

9putra uvāca |
mṛtyunā'bhyāhato loko jarayā parivāritaḥ
|
ahorātrāḥ patantyete nanu kasmānna budhyase ||9||

10amoghā rātrayaścāpi nityamāyānti yānti ca
|
yadā'hametajjānāmi na mṛtyustiṣṭhatīti ha
|
so'haṃ kathaṃ pratīkṣiṣye jñānenāpihitaścaran ||10||

11rājyāṃ rātryāṃ vyatītāyāmāyuralpataraṃ yadā
|
tadaiva vandhyaṃ divasamiti vidyādvicakṣaṇaḥ ||11||

12gādhodake matsya iva sukhaṃ vindeta kastadā
|
anavāpteṣu kāmeṣu mṛtyurabhyeti mānavam ||12||

13puṣpāṇīva vicinvantamanyatragatamānasam
|
vṛkīvoraṇamāsādya mṛtyurādāya gacchati ||13||

14adyaiva kuru yacchreyo mā tvāṃ kālo'tyagādayam
|
akṛteṣveva kāryeṣu mṛtyurvai samprakarṣati ||14||

15śvaḥ kāryamadya kurvīta pūrvāhṇe cāparāhṇikam
|
na hi pratīkṣate mṛtyuḥ kṛtamasya na vā kṛtam ||15||

16ko hi nājāti kasyādya mṛtyukālo bhaviṣyati
|
yuvaiva dharmaśīlaḥ syādanityaṃ khalu jīvitam
|
kṛte dharme bhavetkīrtiriha pretya ca vai sukham ||16||

17mohena hi samāviṣṭaḥ putradārārthamudyataḥ
|
kṛtvā kāryamakāryaṃ vā puṣṭimeṣāṃ prayacchati ||17||

18taṃ putrapaśusampannaṃ vyāsaktamanasaṃ naram
|
suptaṃ vyāghro mṛgamiva mṛtyurādāya gacchati ||18||

19sañcinvānakamevainaṃ kāmānāmavitṛptakam
|
vyāghraḥ paśumivādāya mṛtyurādāya gacchati ||19||

20idaṃ kṛtamidaṃ kāryamidamanyatkṛtākṛtam
|
evamīhāsukhāsaktaṃ kṛtāntaḥ kurute vaśe ||20||

21kṛtānāṃ phalamaprāptaṃ karmaṇāṃ karmasaṃjñitam
|
kṣetrāpaṇagṛhāsaktaṃ mṛtyurādāya gacchati ||21||

22durbalaṃ balavantaṃ ca śūraṃ bhīrūṃ jaḍaṃ kavim
|
aprāptaṃ sarvakāmārthānmṛtyurādāya gacchati ||22||

23mṛtyurjarā ca vyādhiśca duḥkhaṃ cānekakāraṇam
|
anuṣaktaṃ yadā dehe kiṃ svastha iva tiṣṭhasi ||23||

24jātamevāntako'ntāya jarā cānveti dehinam
|
anuṣaktā dvayenaite bhāvāḥ sthāvarajaṅgamāḥ ||24||

25mṛtyorvā mukhametadvai yā grāme vasato ratiḥ
|
devānāmeṣa vai goṣṭho yadaraṇyamiti śrutiḥ ||25||

26nibandhanī rajjureṣā yā grāme vasato ratiḥ
|
chittvaitāṃ sukṛto yānti naināṃ chindanti duṣkṛtaḥ ||26||

27na hiṃsayati yo jantūnmanovākkāyahetubhiḥ
|
jīvitārthāpanayanaiḥ prāṇibhirna sa hiṃsyate ||27||

28na mṛtyusenāmāyāntīṃ jātu kaścitprabādhate
|
ṛte satyamasattyājyaṃ satye hyamṛtamāśritam ||28||

29tasmātsatyavratācāraḥ satyayogaparāyaṇaḥ
|
satyāgamaḥ sadā dāntaḥ satyenaivāntakaṃ jayet ||29||

30amṛtaṃ caiva mṛtyuśca dvayaṃ dehe pratiṣṭhitam
|
mṛtyumāpadyate mohāt satyenāpadyate'mṛtam ||30||

31so'haṃ hyahiṃsraḥ satyārthī kāmakrodhabahiṣkṛtaḥ
|
samaduḥkhasukhaḥ kṣemī mṛtyuṃ hāsyāmyamartyavat ||31||

32śāntiyajñarato dānto brahmayajñe sthito muniḥ
|
vāṅmanaḥ karmayajñaśca bhaviṣyāmyudagāyane ||32||

33paśuyajñaiḥ kathaṃ hiṃsrairmādṛśo yaṣṭumarhati
|
antavadbhiriva prājñaḥ kṣetrayajñaiḥ piśācavat ||33||

34yasya vāṅmanasī syātāṃ samyak praṇihite sadā
|
tapastyāgaśca satyaṃ ca sa vai sarvamavāpnuyāt ||34||

35nāsti vidyāsamaṃ cakṣurnāsti satyasamaṃ tapaḥ
|
nāsti rāgasamaṃ duḥkhaṃ nāsti tyāgasamaṃ sukham ||35||

36ātmanyevātmanā jāta ātmaniṣṭho'prajo'pi vā
|
ātmanyeva bhaviṣyāmi na māṃ tārayati prajā ||36||

37naitādṛśaṃ brāhmaṇasyāsti vittaṃ yathaikatā samatā satyatā ca
|
śīlaṃ sthitirdaṇḍanidhānamārjavaṃ tatastataścoparamaḥ kriyābhyaḥ ||37||

38kiṃ te dhanairbāndhavairvā'pi kiṃ te kiṃ te dārairbrāhmaṇa yo mariṣyasi
|
ātmānamanviccha guhāṃ praviṣṭaṃ pitāmahāste kva gatāḥ pitā ca ||38||

39bhīṣma uvāca|
putrasyaitadvacaḥ śrutvā yathā'kārṣītpitā nṛpa
|
tathā tvamapi vartasva satyadharmaparāyaṇaḥ ||39||
