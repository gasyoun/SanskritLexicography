Mahābhārata 7.149 (Droṇaparvan)

1sañjaya uvāca|
tato rājānamabhyetya dharmaputraṃ yudhiṣṭhiram
|
vavande sa prahṛṣṭātmā hate pārthena saindhave||1||

2diṣṭyā vardhasi rājendra hataśatrurnarottama
|
diṣṭyā nistīrṇavāṃścaiva pratijñāmanujastava||2||

3sa tvevamuktaḥ kṛṣṇena hṛṣṭaḥ parapuraṃjayaḥ
|
tato yudhiṣṭhiro rājā rathādāplutya bhārata||3||

4paryaṣvajattadā kṛṣṇāvānandāśrupariplutaḥ
|
pramṛjya vadanaṃ śubhraṃ puṇḍarīkasamaprabham||4||

5abravīdvāsudevaṃ ca pāṇḍavaṃ ca dhanañjayam
|
priyametadupaśrutya tvattaḥ puṣkaralocana||5||

6nāntaṃ gacchāmi harṣasya titīrṣurudadheriva
|
atyadbhutamidaṃ kṛṣṇa kṛtaṃ pārthena dhīmatā||6||

7diṣṭyā paśyāmi saṃgrāme tīrṇabhārau mahārathau
|
diṣṭyā vinihataḥ pāpaḥ saindhavaḥ puruṣādhamaḥ||7||

8kṛṣṇa diṣṭyā mama prītirmahatī pratipāditā
|
tvayā guptena govinda ghnatā pāpaṃ jayadratham||8||

9kintu nātyadbhutaṃ teṣāṃ yeṣāṃ nastvaṃ samāśrayaḥ
|
na teṣāṃ duṣkṛtaṃ kiñcittriṣu lokeṣu vidyate||9||

10sarvalokagururyeṣāṃ tvaṃ nātho madhusūdana
|
tvatprasādāddhi govinda vayaṃ jeṣyāmahe ripūn||10||

11sthitaḥ sarvātmanā nityaṃ priyeṣu ca hiteṣu ca
|
tvāṃ caivāsmābhirāśritya kṛtaḥ śastrasamudyamaḥ||11||

12surairivāsuravadhe śakraṃ śakrānujāhave
|
asaṃbhāvyamidaṃ karma devairapi janārdana||12||

13tvadbuddhibalavīryeṇa kṛtavāneṣu phālgunaḥ
|
bālyātprabhṛti te kṛṣṇa karmāṇi śrutavānaham||13||

14amānuṣāṇi divyāni mahānti ca bahūni ca
|
tadaivājñāsiṣaṃ śatrūnhatānprāptāṃ ca medinīm||14||

15tvatprasādasamutthena vikrameṇārisūdana
|
sureśatvaṃ gataḥ śakro hatvā daityānsahasraśaḥ||15||

16tvatprasādāddhṛṣīkeśa jagatsthāvarajagaṅgamam
|
svavartmani sthitaṃ vīra japahomeṣu vartate||16||

17ekārṇavamidaṃ pūrvaṃ sarvamāsīttamomayam
|
tvatprasādānmahābāho jagatprāptaṃ narottama||17||

18sraṣṭāraṃ sarvalokānāṃ paramātmānamavyayam
|
ye paśyanti hṛṣīkeśaṃ na te muhyanti karhicit||18||

19purāṇaṃ paramaṃ devaṃ devadevaṃ sanātanam
|
ye prapannāḥ suraguruṃ na te muhyanti karhicit||19||

20anādinidhanaṃ devaṃ lokakartāramavyayam
|
ye bhaktāstvāṃ hṛṣīkeśa durgāṇyatitaranti te||20||

21paraṃ purāṇaṃ puruṣaṃ parāṇāṃ paramaṃ ca yat
|
prapadyatastatparamaṃ parā bhūtirvidhīyate||21||

22gāyanti caturo vedā yaśca vedeṣu gīyate
|
taṃ prapadya mahātmānaṃ bhūtimaśnāmyanuttamām||22||

23parameśa pareśeśa tiryagīśa nareśvara
|
sarveśvareśvareśeśa namaste puruṣottama||23||

24tvamīśeśeśvareśāna prabho vardhasva mādhava
|
prabhavāpyaya sarvasya sarvātmanpṛthulocana||24||

25dhanañjayasakhā yaśca dhanañjayahitaśca yaḥ
|
dhanañjayasya goptā taṃ prapadya sukhamedhate||25||

26mārkaṇḍeyaḥ purāṇarṣiścaritajñastavānagha
|
māhātmyamanubhāvaṃ ca purā kīrtitavānmuniḥ||26||

27asito devalaścaiva nāradaśca mahātapāḥ
|
pitāmahaśca me vyāsastvāmāhurvidhimuttamam||27||

28tvaṃ tejastvaṃ paraṃ brahma tvaṃ satyaṃ tvaṃ mahattapaḥ
|
tvaṃ śreyastvaṃ yaśaścāgryaṃ kāraṇaṃ jagatastathā
|
tvayā sṛṣṭamidaṃ sarvaṃ jagatsthāvarajaṅgamam||28||

29pralaye samanuprāpte tvāṃ vai niviśate punaḥ
|
anādinidhanaṃ devaṃ viśvasyeśaṃ jagatpate||29||

30dhātāramajamavyaktamāhurvedavido janāḥ
|
bhūtātmānaṃ mahātmānamanantaṃ viśvatomukham||30||

31api devā na jānanti guhyamādyaṃ jagatpatim
|
nārāyaṇaṃ paraṃ devaṃ paramātmānamīśvaram||31||

32jñānayoniṃ hariṃ viṣṇuṃ mumukṣūṇāṃ parāyaṇam
|
paraṃ purāṇaṃ puruṣaṃ purāṇānāṃ paraṃ ca yat||32||

33evamādiguṇānāṃ te karmaṇāṃ divi ceha ca
|
atītabhūtabhavyānāṃ saṃkhyātā'tra na vidyate||33||

34sarvato rakṣaṇīyāḥ sma śakreṇeva divaukasaḥ
|
yaistvaṃ sarvaguṇopetaḥ suhṛnna upapāditaḥ||34||

35ityevaṃ dharmarājena harirukto mahāyaśāḥ
|
anurūpamidaṃ vākyaṃ pratyuvāca janārdanaḥ||35||

36bhavatā tapasogreṇa dharmeṇa parameṇa ca
|
sādhutvādārjavāccaiva hataḥ pāpo jayadrathaḥ||36||

37ayaṃ ca puruṣavyāghra tvadanudhyānasaṃvṛtaḥ
|
hatvā yodhasahasrāṇi nyahan jiṣṇurjayadratham||37||

38kṛtitve bāhuvīrye ca tathaivāsaṃbhrame'pi ca
|
śīghratā'moghabuddhitve nāsti pārthasamaḥ kvacit||38||

39tadayaṃ bharataśreṣṭha bhrātā te'dya yadarjunaḥ
|
sainyakṣayaṃ raṇe kṛtvā sindhurājaśiroharat||39||

40tato dharmasuto jiṣṇuṃ pariṣvajya viśāṃpate
|
pramṛjya vadanaṃ tasya paryāśvāsayata prabhuḥ||40||

41atīva sumahatkarma kṛtavānasi phālguna
|
asahyaṃ cāviṣahyaṃ ca daiverapi savāsavaiḥ||41||

42diṣṭyā nistīrṇabhāro'si hatāraścāsi śatruhan
|
diṣṭyā satyā pratijñeyaṃ kṛtā hatvā jayadratham||42||

43evamuktvā guḍākeśaṃ dharmarājo mahāyaśāḥ
|
pasparśa puṇyagandhena pṛṣṭhe hastena pārthivaḥ||43||

44evamuktau mahātmānāvubhau keśavapāṇḍavau
|
tāvabrūtāṃ tadā kṛṣṇau rājānaṃ pṛthivīpatim||44||

45tava kopāgninā dagdhaḥ pāpo rājā jayadrathaḥ
|
uttīrṇaṃ cāpi sumahaddhārtarāṣṭrabalaṃ raṇe||45||

46hanyante nihatāścaiva vinakṣyanti ca bhārata
|
tava krodhahatā hyete kauravāḥ śatrusūdana||46||

47tvāṃ hi cakṣurhaṇaṃ vīraṃ kopayitvā suyodhanaḥ
|
samitrabandhuḥ samare prāṇāṃstyakṣyati durmatiḥ||47||

48tava krodhahataḥ pūrvaṃ devairapi sudurjayaḥ
|
śaratalpagataḥ śete bhīṣmaḥ kurupitāmahaḥ||48||

49durlabho vijayasteṣāṃ saṃgrāme ripughātinām
|
yātā mṛtyuvaśaṃ te vai yeṣāṃ kruddho'si pāṇḍava||49||

50rājyaṃ prāṇāḥ śriyaḥ putrāḥ saukhyāni vividhāni ca
|
acirāttasya naśyanti yeṣāṃ kruddho'si mānada||50||

51vinaṣṭān kauravānmanye saputrapaśubāndhavān
|
rājadharmapare nityaṃ tvayi kruddhe parantapa||51||

52tato bhīmo mahābāhuḥ sātyakiśca mahārathaḥ
|
abhivādya guruṃ jyeṣṭhaṃ mārgaṇaiḥ kṣatavikṣatau||52||

53kṣitāvāstāṃ maheṣvāsau pāñcālyaparivāritau
|
tau dṛṣṭvā muditau vīrau prāñjalī cāgrataḥ sthitau||53||

54abhyanandata kaunteyastāvubhau bhīmasātyakī
|
diṣṭyā paśyāmi vāṃ śūrau vimuktau sainyasāgarāt||54||

55droṇagrāhadurādharṣāddhārdikyamakarālayāt
|
diṣṭyā vinirjitāḥ saṃkhye pṛthivyāṃ sarvapārthivāḥ||55||

56yuvāṃ vijayinau cāpi diṣṭyā paśyāmi saṃyuge
|
diṣṭyā droṇo jitaḥ saṃkhye hārdikyaśca mahābalaḥ||56||

57diṣṭyā vikarṇibhiḥ karṇo raṇe nītaḥ parābhavam
|
vimukhaśca kṛtaḥ śalyo yuvābhyāṃ puruṣarṣabhau||57||

58diṣṭyā yuvāṃ kuśalinau saṃgrāmātpunarāgatau
|
paśyāmi rathināṃ śreṣṭhāvubhau yuddhaviśāradau||58||

59mama vākyakarau vīrau mama gauravayantritau
|
sainyārṇavaṃ samuttīrṇau diṣṭyā paśyāmi vāmaham||59||

60samaraślāghinau vīrau samareṣvaparājitau
|
mama vākyasamau caiva diṣṭyā paśyāmi vāmaham||60||

61ityuktvā pāṇḍavo rājan yuyudhānavṛkodarau
|
sasvaje puruṣavyāghrau harṣādbāṣpaṃ mumoca ha||61||

62tataḥ pramuditaṃ sarvaṃ balamāsīdviśāmpate
|
pāṇḍavānāṃ raṇe hṛṣṭaṃ yuddhāya tu mano dadhe||62||
