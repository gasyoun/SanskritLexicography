Mahābhārata 6.59 (Bhīṣmaparvan)

1dhṛtarāṣṭra uvāca|
pratijñāte tatastasmin yuddhe bhīṣmeṇa dāruṇe
|
krodhito mama putreṇa duḥkhitena viśeṣataḥ ||1||

2bhīṣmaḥ kimakarot tatra pāṇḍaveyeṣu bhārata
|
pitāmahe vā pañcālāstanmamācakṣva sañjaya ||2||

3sañjaya uvāca|gatapūrvāhṇabhūyiṣṭhe tasminnahani bhārata
|
paścimāṃ diśamāsthāya sthite cāpi divākare ||3||

4jayaṃ prāpteṣu hṛṣṭeṣu pāṇḍaveṣu mahātmasu
|
sarvadharmaviśeṣajñaḥ pitā devavratastava ||4||

5abhyayājjavanairaśvaiḥ pāṇḍavānāmanīkinīm
|
mahatyā senayā guptastava putraiśca sarvaśaḥ ||5||

6prāvartata tato yuddhaṃ tumulaṃ lomaharṣaṇam
|
asmākaṃ pāṇḍavaiḥ sārdhamanayāttava bhārata ||6||

7dhanuṣāṃ kūjatāṃ tatra talānāṃ cābhihanyatām
|
mahān samabhavacchabdo girīṇāmiva dīryatām ||7||

8tiṣṭha sthito'smi viddhyainaṃ nivartasva sthiro bhava
|
sthiro'smi praharasveti śabdo'śrūyata sarvaśaḥ ||8||

9kāñcaneṣu tanutreṣu kirīṭeṣu dhvajeṣu ca
|
śilānāmiva śaileṣu patitānāmabhūddhvaniḥ ||9||

10patitānyuttamāṅgāni bāhavaśca vibhūṣitāḥ
|
vyaceṣṭanta mahīṃ prāpya śataśo'tha sahasraśaḥ ||10||

11hṛtottamāṅgāḥ kecittu tathaivodyatakārmukāḥ
|
pragṛhītāyudhāścāpi tasthuḥ puruṣasattamāḥ ||11||

12prāvartata mahāvegā nadī rudhiravāhinī
|
mātaṅgāṅgaśilā raudrā māṃsaśoṇitakardamā ||12||

13varāśvanaranāgānāṃ śarīraprabhavā tadā
|
paralokārṇavamukhī gṛdhragomāyumodinī ||13||

14na dṛṣṭaṃ na śrutaṃ vāpi yuddhametādṛśaṃ nṛpa
|
yathā tava sutānāṃ ca pāṇḍavānāṃ ca bhārata ||14||

15nāsīdrathapathastatra yodhairyudhi nipātitaiḥ
|
gajaiśca patitairnīlairgiriśṛṅgairivāvṛtaḥ ||15||

16vikīrṇaiḥ kavacaiścitraiḥ śirastrāṇaiśca māriṣa
|
śuśubhe tadraṇasthānaṃ śaradīva nabhastalam ||16||

17vinirbhinnāḥ śaraiḥ kecidantrāpīḍaprakarṣiṇaḥ
|
abhītāḥ samare śatrūnabhyadhāvanta darpitāḥ ||17||

18tāta bhrātaḥ sakhe bandho vayasya mama mātula
|
mā māṃ parityajetyanye cukruśuḥ patitā raṇe ||18||

19athābhyehi tvamāgaccha kiṃ bhīto'si kva yāsyasi
|
sthito'haṃ samare mā bhairiti cānye vicukruśuḥ ||19||

20tatra bhīṣmaḥ śāntanavo nityaṃ maṇḍalakārmukaḥ
|
mumoca bāṇān dīptāgrānahīnāśīviṣāniva ||20||

21śarairekāyanīkurvan diśaḥ sarvā yatavrataḥ
|
jaghāna pāṇḍavarathānādiśya bharatarṣabha ||21||

22sa nṛtyanvai rathopasthe darśayan pāṇilāghavam
|
alātacakravadrājaṃstatra tatra sma dṛśyate ||22||

23anekaśatasāhasraṃ samapaśyanta lāghavāt ||23||

24māyākṛtātmānamiva bhīṣmaṃ tatra sma menire
|
pūrvasyāṃ diśi taṃ dṛṣṭvā pratīcyāṃ dadṛśurjanāḥ ||24||

25udīcyāṃ caivamālokya dakṣiṇasyāṃ punaḥ prabho
|
evaṃ sa samare śūro gāṅgeyaḥ pratyadṛśyata ||25||

26na caivaṃ pāṇḍaveyānāṃ kaścicchaknoti vīkṣitum
|
viśikhāneva paśyanti bhīṣmacāpacyutānbahūn ||26||

27kurvāṇaṃ samare karma sūdayānaṃ ca vāhinīm
|
vyākrośanta raṇe tatra narā bahuvidhā bahu ||27||

28amānuṣeṇa rūpeṇa carantaṃ pitaraṃ tava
|
śalabhā iva rājānaḥ patanti vidhicoditāḥ ||28||

29bhīṣmāgnimabhisaṃkruddhaṃ vināśāya sahasraśaḥ
|
na hi moghaḥ śaraḥ kaścidāsīdbhīṣmasya saṃyuge ||29||

30naranāgāśvakāyeṣu bahutvāllaghuyodhinaḥ
|
bhinattyekena bāṇena sumukhena patattriṇā ||30||

31gajakaṇṭakasannaddhaṃ vajreṇeva śiloccayam
|
dvau trīnapi gajārohānpiṇḍitānvarmitānapi ||31||

32nārācena sumuktena nijaghāna pitā tava
|
yo yo bhīṣmaṃ naravyāghramabhyeti yudhi kaścana ||32||

33muhūrtadṛṣṭaḥ sa mayā patito bhuvi dṛśyate
|
evaṃ sā dharmarājasya vadhyamānā mahācamūḥ ||33||

34bhīṣmeṇātulavīryeṇa vyaśīryata sahasradhā
|
prākampata mahāsenā śaravarṣeṇa tāpitā ||34||

35paśyato vāsudevasya pārthasyātha śikhaṇḍinaḥ
|
vartamānā'pi te vīrā dravamāṇānmahārathān ||35||

36nāśaknuvan vārayituṃ bhīṣmabāṇaprapīḍitān
|
mahendrasamavīryeṇa vadhyamānā mahācamūḥ ||36||

37abhajyata mahārāja na ca dvau saha dhāvataḥ
|
āviddhanaranāgāśvaṃ patitadhvajakūbaram ||37||

38anīkaṃ pāṇḍuputrāṇāṃ hāhābhūtamacetanam
|
jaghānātra pitā putraṃ putraśca pitaraṃ tathā ||38||

39priyaṃ sakhāyaṃ cākrande sakhā daivabalātkṛtaḥ
|
vimucya kavacānyanye pāṇḍuputrasya sainikāḥ ||39||

40vimuktakeśā dhāvantaḥ pratyadṛśyanta bhārata
|
tadgokulamivodbhrāntamudbhrāntarathayūthapam ||40||

41dadṛśe pāṇḍuputrasya sainyamārtasvaraṃ tadā
|
prabhajyamānaṃ sainyaṃ tu dṛṣṭvā yādavanandanaḥ ||41||

42uvāca pārthaṃ bībhatsuṃ nigṛhya rathamuttamam
|
ayaṃ sa kālaḥ saṃprāptaḥ pārtha yaste'bhikāṅkṣitaḥ ||42||

43praharasva naravyāghra na cenmohādvimuhyase
|
yattvayā kathitaṃ vīra purā rājñāṃ samāgame ||43||

44bhīṣmadroṇamukhānsarvān dhārtarāṣṭrasya sainikān
|
sānubandhān haniṣyāmi ye māṃ yotsyanti saṃyuge ||44||

45iti tat kuru kaunteya satyaṃ vākyamarindama
|
bībhatso paśya sainyaṃ svaṃ bhajyamānaṃ tatastataḥ ||45||

46dravataśca mahīpālān paśya yaudhiṣṭhire bale
|
dṛṣṭvā hi bhīṣmaṃ samare vyāttānanamivāntakam ||46||

47bhayārtāḥ prapalāyante siṃhāt kṣudramṛgā iva
|
evamuktaḥ pratyuvāca vāsudevaṃ dhanañjayaḥ ||47||

48nodayāśvān yato bhīṣmo vigāhaitadbalārṇavam
|
pātayiṣyāmi durdharṣaṃ vṛddhaṃ kurupitāmaham ||48||

49sañjaya uvāca|tato'śvān rajataprakhyānnodayāmāsa mādhavaḥ
|
yato bhīṣmaratho rājan duṣprekṣyo raśmivāniva ||49||

50tatastat punarāvṛttaṃ yudhiṣṭhirabalaṃ mahat
|
dṛṣṭvā pārthaṃ mahābāhuṃ bhīṣmāyodyatamāhave ||50||

51tato bhīṣmaḥ kuruśreṣṭha siṃhavadvinadanmuhuḥ
|
dhanañjayarathaṃ śīghraṃ śaravarṣairavākirat ||51||

52kṣaṇena sa rathastasya sahayaḥ sahasārathiḥ
|
śaravarṣeṇa mahatā saṃchanno na prakāśate ||52||

53vāsudevastvasaṃbhrānto dhairyamāsthāya sattvavān
|
codayāmāsa tānaśvān vicitān bhīṣmasāyakaiḥ ||53||

54tataḥ pārtho dhanurguhya divyaṃ jaladaniḥsvanam
|
pātayāmāsa bhīṣmasya dhanuśchitvā tribhiḥ śaraiḥ ||54||

55sa cchinnadhanvā kauravyaḥ punaranyanmahaddhanuḥ
|
nimiṣāntaramātreṇa sajyaṃ cakre pitā tava ||55||

56vicakarṣa tato dorbhyāṃ dhanurjaladaniḥsvanam
|
athāsya tadapi kruddhaściccheda dhanurarjunaḥ ||56||

57tasya tat pūjayāmāsa lāghavaṃ śāntanoḥ sutaḥ
|
sādhu pārtha mahābāho sādhu bho pāṇḍunandana ||57||

58tvayyevaitadyuktarūpaṃ mahat karma dhanañjaya
|
prīto'smi subhṛśaṃ putra kuru yuddhaṃ mayā saha ||58||

59iti pārthaṃ praśasyātha pragṛhyānyanmahaddhanuḥ
|
mumoca samare vīraḥ śarān pārtharathaṃ prati ||59||

60adarśayadvāsudevo hayayāne paraṃ balam
|
moghān kurvan śarāṃstasya maṇḍalānyācarallaghu ||60||

61tathā bhīṣmastu sudṛḍhaṃ vāsudevadhanañjayau
|
vivyādha niśitairbāṇaiḥ sarvagātreṣu bhārata ||61||

62śuśubhāte naravyāghrau tau bhīṣmaśaravikṣatau
|
govṛṣāviva saṃrabdhau viṣāṇairlikhitāṅkitau ||62||

63punaścāpi susaṃrabdhaḥ śaraiḥ śatasahasraśaḥ
|
kṛṣṇayoryudhi saṃrabdho bhīṣmo'thāvārayaddiśaḥ ||63||

64vārṣṇeyaṃ ca śaraistīkṣṇaiḥ kampayāmāsa roṣitaḥ
|
muhurabhyardayan bhīṣmaḥ prahasya svanavattadā ||64||

65tatastu kṛṣṇaḥ samare dṛṣṭvā bhīṣmaparākramam
|
saṃprekṣya ca mahābāhuḥ pārthasya mṛduyuddhatām ||65||

66bhīṣmaṃ ca śaravarṣāṇi sṛjantamaniśaṃ yudhi
|
pratapantamivādityaṃ madhyamāsādya senayoḥ ||66||

67varān varānvinighnantaṃ pāṇḍuputrasya sainikān
|
yugāntamiva kurvāṇaṃ bhīṣmaṃ yaudhiṣṭhire bale ||67||

68amṛṣyamāṇo bhagavān keśavaḥ paravīrahā
|
acintayadameyātmā nāsti yaudhiṣṭhiraṃ balam ||68||

69ekāhnā hi raṇe bhīṣmo nāśayeddevadānavān
|
kiṃ nu pāṇḍusutān yuddhe sabalān sapadānugān ||69||

70dravate ca mahāsainyaṃ pāṇḍavasya mahātmanaḥ
|
ete ca kauravāstūrṇaṃ prabhagnān vīkṣya somakān ||70||

71prādravanti raṇe dṛṣṭvā harṣayantaḥ pitāmaham
|
so'haṃ bhīṣmaṃ nihanmyadya pāṇḍavārthāya daṃśitaḥ ||71||

72bhārametaṃ vineṣyāmi pāṇḍavānāṃ mahātmanām
|
arjuno hi śaraistīkṣṇairvadhyamāno'pi saṃyuge ||72||

73kartavyaṃ nābhijānāti raṇe bhīṣmasya gauravāt
|
tathā cintayatastasya bhūya eva pitāmahaḥ
|
preṣayāmāsa saṃkruddhaḥ śarān pārtharathaṃ prati ||73||

74teṣāṃ bahutvāttu bhṛśaṃ śarāṇāṃ diśaśca sarvāḥ pihitā babhūvuḥ
|
na cāntarikṣaṃ na diśo na bhūmirnabhāskaro'dṛśyata raśmimālī ||74||

75vavuśca vātāstumulāḥ sadhūmā diśaśca sarvāḥ kṣubhitā babhūvuḥ
|
droṇo vikarṇo'tha jayadrathaśca bhūriśravāḥ kṛtavarmā kṛpaśca ||75||

76śrutāyurambaṣṭhapatiśca rājā vindānuvindau ca sudakṣiṇaśca
|
prācyāśca sauvīragaṇāśca sarve vasātayaḥ kṣudrakamālavāśca ||76||

77kirīṭinaṃ tvaramāṇā'bhisasrurnideśagāḥ śāntanavasya rājñaḥ
|
taṃ vājipādātarathaughajālairanekasāhastraśatairdadarśa ||77||

78kirīṭinaṃ saṃparivāryamāṇaṃ śinernaptā vāraṇayūthapaiśca
|
tatastu dṛṣṭvā'rjunavāsudevau padātināgāśvarathaiḥ samantāt ||78||

79abhidrutau śastrabhṛtāṃ variṣṭhau śinipravīro'bhisasāra tūrṇam
|
sa tānyanīkāni mahādhanuṣmān śinipravīraḥ sahasā'bhipatya ||79||

80cakāra sāhāyyamathārjunasya viṣṇuryathā vṛtraniṣūdanasya
|
viśīrṇanāgāśvarathadhvajaughaṃ bhīṣmeṇa vitrāsitasarvayodham ||80||

81yudhiṣṭhirānīkamabhidravantaṃ provāca saṃdṛśya śinipravīraḥ
|
kva kṣatriyā yāsyatha naiṣa dharmaḥ satāṃ purastāt kathitaḥ purāṇaiḥ ||81||

82mā svāṃ pratijñāṃ tyajata pravīrāḥ svaṃ vīradharmaṃ paripālayadhvam
|
tānvāsavānantarajo niśāmya narendramukhyān dravataḥ samantāt ||82||

83pārthasya dṛṣṭvā mṛduyuddhatāṃ ca bhīṣmaṃ ca saṃkhye samudīryamāṇam
|
amṛṣyamāṇaḥ sa tato mahātmā yaśasvinaṃ sarvadaśārhabhartā ||83||

84uvāca śaineyamabhipraśaṃsan dṛṣṭvā kurūnāpatataḥ samagrān
|
ye yānti te yāntu śinipravīra ye'pi sthitāḥ sātvana te'pi yāntu ||84||

85bhīṣmaṃ rathāt paśya nipātyamānaṃ droṇaṃ ca saṅkhye sagaṇaṃ mayā'dya
|
na me rathī sātvatakauravāṇāṃ kruddhasya mucyeta raṇe'dya kaścit ||85||

86tasmādahaṃ gṛhya rathāṅgamugraṃ prāṇaṃ hariṣyāmi mahāvratasya
|
nihatya bhīṣmaṃ sagaṇaṃ tathā''jau droṇaṃ ca śaineyarathapravīrau ||86||

87prītiṃ kariṣyāmi dhanañjayasya rājñaśca bhīmasya tathā'śvinośca
|
nihatya sarvān dhṛtarāṣṭraputrāṃstatpakṣiṇo ye ca narendramukhyāḥ ||87||

88rājyena rājānamajātaśatruṃ sampādayiṣyāmyahamadya hṛṣṭaḥ
|
tataḥ sunābhaṃ vasudevaputraḥ sūyaprabhaṃ vajrasamaprabhāvam ||88||

89kṣurāntamudyamya bhujena cakraṃ rathādavaplutya visṛjya vāhān
|
saṃkampayan gāṃ caraṇairmahātmā vegena kṛṣṇaḥ prasasāra bhīṣmam ||89||

90madāndhamājo samudīrṇadarpaṃ siṃho jighāṃsanniva vāraṇendram
|
so'bhidravan bhīṣmamanīkamadhye kruddho mahendrāvarajaḥ pramāthī ||90||

91vyālambipītāntapaṭaścakāśe ghano yathā khe taḍitāvanaddhaḥ
|
sudarśanaṃ cāsya rarāja śorestaccakrapadmaṃ subhujorunālam ||91||

92yathādipadmaṃ taruṇārkavarṇaṃ rarāja nārāyaṇanābhijātam
|
tatkṛṣṇakopodayasūryabuddhaṃ kṣurāntatīkṣṇāgrasujātapatram ||92||

93tasyaiva dehorusaraḥ prarūḍhaṃ rarāja nārāyaṇabāhunālam
|
tamāttacakraṃ praṇadantamuccaiḥ kuddhaṃ mahendrāvarajaṃ samīkṣya ||93||

94sarvāṇi bhūtāni bhṛśaṃ vineduḥ kṣayaṃ kurūṇāmiva cintayitvā
|
sa vāsudevaḥ pragṛhītacakraḥ saṃvartayiṣyanniva sarvalokam ||94||

95abhyutpatan lokagururbabhāse bhūtāni dhakṣyanniva dhūmaketuḥ
|
tamādravantaṃ pragṛhītacakraṃ dṛṣṭvā devaṃ śāntanavastadānīm ||95||

96asaṃbhramaṃ tadvicakarṣa dorbhyāṃ mahādhanurgāṇḍivatulyaghoṣam
|
uvāca bhīṣmastamanantapauruṣaṃ govindamājāvavimūḍhacetāḥ ||96||

97ehyehi deveśa jagannivāsa namo'stu te mādhava cakrapāṇe
|
prasahya māṃ pātaya lokanātha rathottamāt sarvaśaraṇyasaṃkhye ||97||

98tvayā hatasyāpi mamādya kṛṣṇa śreyaḥ parasminniha caiva loke
|
saṃbhāvito'smyandhakavṛṣṇinātha lokaistribhirvīra tavābhiyānāt ||98||

99rathādavaplutya tatastvarāvān pārtho'pyanudrutya yadupravīram
|
jagrāha pīnottamalambabāhuṃ bāhvorhariṃ vyāyatapīnabāhuḥ ||99||

100nigṛhyamāṇaśca tadā''didevo bhṛśaṃ saroṣaḥ kila cātmayogī
|
ādāya vegena jagāma viṣṇurjiṣṇuṃ mahāvāta ivaikavṛkṣam ||100||

101pārthastu viṣṭabhya balena pādau bhīṣmāntikaṃ tūrṇamabhidravantam
|
balānnijagrāha hariṃ kirīṭī pade'tha rājan daśame kathañcit ||101||

102avasthitaṃ ca praṇipatya kṛṣṇaṃ prīto'rjunaḥ kāñcanacitramālī
|
uvāca kopaṃ pratisaṃhareti gatirbhavān keśava pāṇḍavānām ||102||

103na hāsyate karma yathāpratijñaṃ putraiḥ śape keśava sodaraiśca
|
antaṃ kariṣyāmi yathā kurūṇāṃ tvayā'hamindrānujasaṃprayuktaḥ ||103||

104tataḥ pratijñāṃ samayaṃ ca tasya janārdanaḥ prītamanā niśamya
|
sthitaḥ priye kauravasattamasya rathaṃ sacakraḥ punarāruroha ||104||

105sa tānabhīṣūn punarādadānaḥ pragṛhya śaṅkhaṃ dviṣatāṃ nihantā
|
vinādayāmāsa tato diśaśca sa pāñcajanyasya raveṇa śauriḥ ||105||

106vyāviddhaniṣkāṅgadakuṇḍalaṃ taṃ rajovikīrṇāñcitapadmanetram
|
viśuddhadaṃṣṭraṃ pragṛhītaśaṅkhaṃ vicukruśuḥ prekṣya kurupravīrāḥ ||106||

107mṛdaṅgabherīpaṇavapraṇādā nemisvanā dundubhiniḥsvanāśca
|
sasiṃhanādāśca babhūvurugrāḥ sarveṣvanīkeṣu tataḥ kurūṇām ||107||

108gāṇḍīvaghoṣaḥ stanayitnukalpo jagāma pārthasya nabho diśaśca
|
jagmuśca bāṇā vimalāḥ prasannāḥ sarvā diśaḥ pāṇḍavacāpamuktāḥ ||108||

109taṃ kauravāṇāmadhipo javena bhīṣmeṇa bhūriśravasā ca sārdham
|
abhyudyayāvudyatabāṇapāṇiḥ kakṣaṃ didhakṣanniva dhūmaketuḥ ||109||

110athārjunāya prajighāya bhallān bhūriśravāḥ sapta suvarṇapuṅkhān
|
duryodhanastomaramugravegaṃ śalyo gadāṃ śāntanavaśca śaktim ||110||

111sa saptabhiḥ saptaśarapravekān saṃvārya bhūriśravasā visṛṣṭān
|
śitena duryodhanabāhumuktaṃ kṣureṇa tattomaramunmamātha ||111||

112tataḥ śubhāmāpatatīṃ sa śaktiṃ vidyutprabhāṃ śāntanavena muktām
|
gadāṃ ca madrādhipabāhumuktāṃ dvābhyāṃ śarābhyāṃ nicakarta vīraḥ ||112||

113tato bhujābhyāṃ balavadvikṛṣya citraṃ dhanurgāṇḍivamaprameyam
|
māhendramastraṃ vidhivatsughoraṃ prāduścakārādbhutamantarikṣe ||113||

114tenottamāstreṇa tato mahātmā sarvāṇyanīkāni mahādhanuṣmān
|
śaraughajālairvimalāgnivarṇairnivārayāmāsa kirīṭamālī ||114||

115śilīmukhāḥ pārthadhanuḥpramuktā rathān dhvajāgrāṇi dhanūṃṣi bāhūn
|
nikṛtya dehānviviśuḥ pareṣāṃ narendranāgendraturaṅgamāṇām ||115||

116tato diśaḥ so'nudiśaśca pārthaḥ śaraiḥ sudhāraiḥ samare vitatya
|
gāṇḍīvaśabdena manāṃsi teṣāṃ kirīṭamālī vyathayāñcakāra ||116||

117tasmiṃstathā ghoratame pravṛtte śaṅkhasvanā dundubhiniḥsvanāśca
|
antarhitā gāṇḍivaniḥsvanena babhūvurugrāśvarathapraṇādāḥ ||117||

118gāṇḍīvaśabdaṃ tamatho viditvā virāṭarājapramukhāḥ pravīrāḥ
|
pāñcālarājo drupadaśca vīrastaṃ deśamājagmuradīnasattvāḥ||118||

119sarvāṇi sainyāni tu tāvakāni yato yato gāṇḍivajaḥ praṇādaḥ
|
tatastataḥ sannatimeva jagmurna taṃ pratīpo'bhisasāra kaścit ||119||

120tasmin sughore nṛpasaṃprahāre hatāḥ pravīrāḥ sarathāśvasutāḥ
|
gajāśca nārācanipātataptā mahāpatākāḥ śubharukmakakṣyāḥ ||120||

121parītasattvāḥ sahasā nipetuḥ kirīṭinā bhinnatanutrakāyāḥ
|
dṛḍhaṃ hatāḥ patribhirugravegaiḥ pārthena bhallairvimalaiḥ śitāgraiḥ ||121||

122nikṛttayantrā nihatendrakīlā dhvajā mahānto dhvajinīmukheṣu
|
padātisaṅghāśca rathāśca saṃkhye hayāśca nāgāśca dhanañjayena ||122||

123bāṇāhatāstūrṇamapetasattvā viṣṭabhya gātrāṇi nipetururvyām
|
aindreṇa tenāstravareṇa rājan mahāhave bhinnatanutradehāḥ ||123||

124tataḥ śaraughairniśitaiḥ kirīṭinā nṛdehaśastrakṣatalohitodā
|
nadī sughorā naramedaphenā pravartitā tatra raṇājire vai ||124||

125vegena sā'tīva pṛthupravāhā paretanāgāśvaśarīrarodhā
|
narendramajjocchritamāṃsapaṅkā prabhūtarakṣogaṇabhūtasevitā ||125||

126śiraḥkapālākulakeśaśādvalā śarīrasaṅghātasahastravāhinī
|
viśīrṇanānākavacormisaṃkulā narāśvanāgāsthinikṛttaśarkarā ||126||

127śva-kaṅka-śālāvṛka-gṛdhra-kākaiḥ kravyādasaṅghaiśca tarakṣubhiśca
|
upetakūlāṃ dadṛśurmanuṣyāḥ krūrāṃ mahāvaitaraṇīprakāśām ||127||

128pravartitāmarjunabāṇasaṅghairmedovasāsṛkpravahāṃ subhīmām
|
hatapravīrāṃ ca tathaiva dṛṣṭvā senāṃ kurūṇāmatha phālgunena ||128||

129te cedi-pāñcāla-karū-ṣamatsyāḥ pārthāśca sarve sahitāḥ praṇeduḥ
|
jayapragalbhāḥ puruṣapravīrāḥ saṃtrāsayantaḥ kuruvīrayodhān ||129||

130hatapravīrāṇi balāni dṛṣṭvā kirīṭinā śatrubhayāvahena
|
vitrāsya senāṃ dhvajinīpatīnāṃ siṃho mṛgāṇāmiva yūthasaṅghān ||130||

131vinedatustāvatiharṣayuktau gāṇḍīvadhanvā ca janārdanaśca
|
tato raviṃ saṃvṛtaraśmijālaṃ dṛṣṭvā bhṛśaṃ śastraparikṣatāṅgāḥ ||131||

132tadaindramastraṃ vitataṃ ca ghoramasahyamudvīkṣya yugāntakalpam
|
athāpayānaṃ kuravaḥ sabhīṣmāḥ sadroṇaduryodhanabāhlikāśca ||132||

133cakrurniśāṃ sandhigatāṃ samīkṣya vibhāvasorlohitarāgayuktām
|
avāpya kīrtiṃ ca yaśaśca loke vijitya śatrūṃśca dhanañjayo'pi ||133||

134yayau narendraiḥ saha sodaraiśca samāptakarmā śibiraṃ niśāyām
|
tataḥ prajajñe tumulaḥ kurūṇāṃ niśāmukhe ghoratamaḥ praṇādaḥ ||134||

135raṇe rathānāmayutaṃ nihatya hatā gajāḥ saptaśatārjunena
|
prācyāśca sauvīragaṇāśca sarve nipātitāḥ kṣudrakamālavāśca ||135||

136mahat kṛtaṃ karma dhanañjayena kartuṃ yathā nārhati kaścidanyaḥ
|
śrutāyurambaṣṭhapatiśca rājā tathaiva durmarṣaṇacitrasenau ||136||

137droṇaḥ kṛpaḥ saindhavabāhlikau ca bhūriśravāḥ śalyaśalau ca rājan
|
anye ca yodhāḥ śataśaḥ sametāḥ kruddhena pārthena raṇasya madhye ||137||

138svabāhuvīryeṇa jitāḥ sabhīṣmāḥ kirīṭinā lokamahārathena
|
iti bruvantaḥ śibirāṇi jagmuḥ sarve gaṇā bhārata ye tvadīyāḥ ||138||

139ulkāsahastraiśca susaṃpradīptairvibhrājamānaiśca tathā pradīpaiḥ
|
kirīṭivitrāsitasarvayodhā cakre niveśaṃ dhvajinī kurūṇām ||139||
