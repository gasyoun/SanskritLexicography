Mahābhārata 4.65 (Virāṭaparvan)

1vaiśampāyana uvāca|bhīṣme tu saṅgrāmaśiro vihāya palāyamāne dhṛtarāṣṭraputraḥ
|
utsṛjya ketuṃ vinadanmahātmā dhanurvigṛhyārjunamāsasāda ||1||

2sa bhīmadhanvānamudagravīryaṃ dhanañjayaṃ śatrugaṇe carantam
|
ākarṇapūrṇāyatacoditena vivyādha bhallena lalāṭamadhye ||2||

3sa tena bāṇena samarpitena jāmbūnadāgreṇa susaṃhitena
|
rarāja rājanmahanīyakarmā yathaikaparvā ruciraikaśṛṅgaḥ ||3||

4athāsya bāṇena vidāritasya prādurbabhūvāsṛgajastramuṣṇam
|
sa tasya jāmbūnadapuṅkhacitro bhittvā lalāṭaṃ suvirājate sma ||4||

5duryodhanaścāpi tamugratejāḥ pārthaśca duryodhanamekavīraḥ
|
anyonyamājau puruṣapravīrau samau samājagmaturājamīḍhau ||5||

6tataḥ prabhinnena mahāgajena mahīdharābhena punarvikarṇaḥ
|
rathaiścaturbhirgajapādarakṣaiḥ kuntīsutaṃ jiṣṇumathābhyadhāvat ||6||

7tamāpatantaṃ tvaritaṃ gajendraṃ dhanañjayaḥ kumbhavibhāgamadhye
|
ākarṇapūrṇena mahāyasena bāṇena vivyādha mahājavena ||7||

8pārthena sṛṣṭaḥ sa tu gārdhrapatra āpuṅkhadeśāt praviveśa nāgam
|
vidārya śailapravaraṃ prakāśaṃ yathā'śaniḥ parvatamindrasṛṣṭaḥ ||8||

9śaraprataptaḥ sa tu nāgarājaḥ pravepitāṅgo vyathitāntarātmā
|
saṃsīdamāno nipapāta mahyāṃ vajrāhataṃ śṛṅgamivācalasya ||9||

10nipātite dantivare pṛthivyāṃ trāsādvikarṇaḥ sahasā'vatīrya
|
tūrṇaṃ padānyaṣṭaśatāni gatvā viviṃśateḥ syandanamāruroha ||10||

11nihatya nāgaṃ tu śareṇa tena vajropamenādrivarāmbudābham
|
tathāvidhenaiva śareṇa pārtho duryodhanaṃ vakṣasi nirbibheda ||11||

12tato gaje rājani caiva bhinne bhagne vikarṇe ca sapādarakṣe
|
gāṇḍīvamuktairviśikhaiḥ praṇunnāste yodhamukhyāḥ sahasā'pajagmuḥ ||12||

13dṛṣṭvaiva pārthena hataṃ ca nāgaṃ yodhāṃśca sarvāndravato niśamya
|
rathaṃ samāvṛtya kurupravīro raṇātpradudrāva yato na pārthaḥ ||13||

14taṃ bhīmarūpaṃ tvaritaṃ dravantaṃ duryodhanaṃ śatrusaho'bhiṣaṅgāt
|
prāsphoṭayadyoddhumanāḥ kirīṭī bāṇena viddhaṃ rudhiraṃ vamantam ||14||

15arjuna uvāca|vihāya kīrtiṃ vipulaṃ yaśaśca yuddhātparāvṛtya palāyase kim
|
na te'dya tūryāṇi samāhatāni tathaiva rājyādavaropitasya ||15||

16yudhiṣṭhirasyāsmi nideśakārī pārthastṛtīyo yudhi saṃsthito'smi
|
tadarthamāvṛtya mukhaṃ prayaccha narendra vṛttaṃ smara dhārtarāṣṭra ||16||

17moghaṃ tavedaṃ bhuvi nāmadheyaṃ duryodhanetīha kṛtaṃ purastāt
|
na hīha duryodhanatā tavāsti palāyamānasya raṇaṃ vihāya ||17||

18na te purastādya pṛṣṭhato vā paśyāmi duryodhana rakṣitāram
|
apehi yuddhāt puruṣapravīra prāṇānpriyānpāṇḍavato'dya rakṣa ||18||
