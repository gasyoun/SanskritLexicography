Mahābhārata 14.25 (Āśvamedhikaparvan)

1brāhmaṇa uvāca|
atrāpyudāharantīmamitihāsaṃ purātanam
|
cāturhotravidhānasya vidhānamiha yādṛśam||1||

2tasya sarvasya vidhivadvidhānamupadiśyate
|
śṛṇu me gadato bhadre rahasyamidamadbhutam||2||

3karaṇaṃ karma kartā ca mokṣa ityeva bhāvini
|
catvāra ete hotāro yairidaṃ jagadāvṛtam||3||

4hetūnāṃ sādhanaṃ caiva śṛṇu sarvamaśeṣataḥ
|
ghrāṇaṃ jihvā ca cakṣuśca tvakca śrotraṃ ca pañcamam
|
mano buddhiśca saptaite vijñeyā guṇahetavaḥ||4||

5gandho rasaśca rūpaṃ ca śabdaḥ sparśaśca pañcamaḥ
|
mantavyamatha boddhavyaṃ saptaite karmahetavaḥ||5||

6ghrātā bhakṣayitā draṣṭā vaktā śrotā ca pañcamaḥ
|
mantā boddhā ca saptaite vijñeyāḥ kartṛhetavaḥ||6||

7svaguṇaṃ bhakṣayantyete guṇavantaḥ śubhāśubham
|
ahaṃ ca nirguṇo'nantaḥ saptaite mokṣahetavaḥ||7||

8viduṣāṃ budhyamānānāṃ svaṃ svaṃ sthānaṃ yathāvidhi
|
guṇāste devatābhūtāḥ satataṃ bhuñjate haviḥ||8||

9adannannānyatho'vidvān mamatvenopapadyate
|
ātmārthaṃ pācayannannaṃ mamatvenopahanyate||9||

10abhakṣyabhakṣaṇaṃ caiva madyapānaṃ ca hanti tam
|
sa cānnaṃ hanti taṃ cānnaṃ sa hatvā hanyate punaḥ||10||

11hantā hyannamidaṃ vidvān punarjanayatīśvaraḥ
|
na cānnājjāyate tasmin sūkṣmo nāma vyatikramaḥ||11||

12manasā gamyate yacca yacca vācā nigadyate
|
śrotreṇa śrūyate yacca cakṣuṣā yacca dṛśyate||12||

13sparśena spṛśyate yacca ghrāṇena ghrāyate ca yat
|
manaḥṣaṣṭhāni saṃyamya havīṃṣyetāni sarvaśaḥ||13||

14guṇavatpāvako mahyaṃ dīvyate'ntaḥśarīragaḥ
|
yogayajñaḥ pravṛtte me jñānavahnipradodbhavaḥ
|
prāṇastotro'pānaśastraḥ sarvatyāgasudakṣiṇaḥ||14||

15kartānumantā brahmātmā hotādhvaryuḥ kṛtastutiḥ
|
ṛtaṃ praśāstā tacchastramapavargo'sya dakṣiṇā||15||

16ṛcaścāpyatra śaṃsanti nārāyaṇavido janāḥ
|
nārāyaṇāya devāya yadavindanpaśūnpurā||16||

17tatra sāmāni gāyanti tatra cāhurnidarśanam
|
devaṃ nārāyaṇaṃ bhīru sarvātmānaṃ nibodha tam||17||

18ṛcaścāpyatra śaṃsanti nārāyaṇavido janāḥ
|
nārāyaṇāya devāya yadavindan paśūn purā||18||

19tatra sāmāni gāyanti tatra cāhurnidarśanam
|
devaṃ nārāyaṇaṃ bhīru sarvātmānaṃ nibodha tam||19||
