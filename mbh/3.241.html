Mahābhārata 3.241 (Āraṇyakaparvan)

1vaiśampāyana uvāca|
tataste sahitāḥ sarve duryodhanamupāgaman
|
abruvaṃśca mahārāja yadūcuḥ kauravaṃ prati||1||

2gandharvairvārite sainye dhārtarāṣṭraḥ pratāpavān
|
amarṣapūrṇaḥ sainyāni pratyabhāṣata bhārata||2||

3śāsatainānadharmajñān mama vipriyakāriṇaḥ
|
yadi prakrīḍate sarvairdevaiḥ saha śatakratuḥ||3||

4duryodhanavacaḥ śrutvā dhārtarāṣṭrā mahābalāḥ
|
sarva evābhisannaddhā yodhāścāpi sahasraśaḥ||4||

5tataḥ pramathya sarvāṃstāṃstadvanaṃ viviśurbalāt
|
siṃhanādena mahatā pūrayanto diśo daśa||5||

6tato'parairavāryanta gandharvaiḥ kurusainikāḥ
|
te vāryamāṇā gandharvaiḥ sāmnaiva vasudhādhipa||6||

7tānanādṛtya gandharvāṃstadvanaṃ viviśurmahat
|
yadā vācā na tiṣṭhanti dhārtarāṣṭraḥ sarājakāḥ||7||

8tataste khecarāḥ sarve citrasene nyavedayan
|
gandharvarājastān sarvānabravīt kauravān prati||8||

9anāryān śāsatetyetāṃścitraseno'tyamarṣaṇaḥ
|
anujñātāśca gandharvāścitrasenena bhārata||9||

10pragṛhītāyudhāḥ sarve dhārtarāṣṭrānabhidravan
|
tān dṛṣṭvā patataḥ śīghrān gandharvānudyatāyudhān||10||

11prādravaṃste diśaḥ sarve dhārtarāṣṭrasya paśyataḥ
|
tān dṛṣṭvā dravataḥ sarvān dhārtarāṣṭrān parāṅmukhān||11||

12rādheyastu tadā vīro nāsīt tatra parāṅmukhaḥ
|
āpatantīṃ tu samprekṣya gandharvāṇāṃ mahācamūm||12||

13mahatā śaravarṣeṇa rādheyaḥ pratyavārayat
|
kṣuraprairviśikhairbhallairvatsadantaistathā''yasaiḥ||13||

14gandharvān śataśo'bhighnaṃllaghutvāt sūtanandanaḥ
|
pātayannuttamāṅgāni gandharvāṇāṃ mahārathaḥ||14||

15kṣaṇena vyadhamat sarvāṃ citrasenasya vāhinīm
|
te vadhyamānā gandharvāḥ sūtaputreṇa dhīmatā||15||

16bhūya evābhyavartanta śataśo'tha sahasraśaḥ
|
gandharvabhūtā pṛthivī kṣaṇena samapadyata||16||

17āpatadbhirmahāvegaiścitrasenasya sainikaiḥ
|
atha duryodhano rājā śakuniścāpi saubalaḥ||17||

18duḥśāsano vikarṇaśca ye cānye dhṛtarāṣṭrajāḥ
|
nyahanaṃstat tadā sainyaṃ rathairgaruḍaniḥsvanaiḥ||18||

19bhūyaśca yodhayāmāsuḥ kṛtvā karṇamathāgrataḥ
|
mahatā rathasaṅgena rathacāreṇa cāpyuta||19||

20vaikartanaṃ parīpsanto gandharvān samavākiran
|
tataḥ sannyapatan sarve gandharvāḥ kauravaiḥ saha||20||

21tadā sutumulaṃ yuddhamabhavallomaharṣaṇam
|
tataste mṛdavo'bhūvan gandharvāḥ śarapīḍitāḥ||21||

22uccukruśuśca kauravyā gandharvān prekṣya pīḍitān
|
gandharvāṃstrāsitān dṛṣṭvā citraseno hyamarpaṇaḥ||22||

23utpapātāsanāt kruddho vadhe teṣāṃ samāhitaḥ
|
tato māyāstramāsthāya yuyudhe citramārgavit
|
tayā'muhyanta kauravyāścitrasenasya māyayā||23||

24ekaikasya tadā yodhā dhārtarāṣṭrasya bhārata
|
paryavartanta gandharvairdaśabhirdaśabhiḥ saha||24||

25tataḥ sampīḍyamānāste balena mahatā tadā
|
prādravanta raṇe bhītā ye ca rājan jijīṣavaḥ||25||

26bhajyamāneṣvanīkeṣu dhārtarāṣṭreṣu sarvaśaḥ
|
karṇo vaikartano rājaṃstasthau giririvācalaḥ||26||

27duryodhanaśca karṇaśca śakuniścāpi saubalaḥ
|
gandharvān yodhayāmāsuḥ samare bhṛśavikṣatāḥ||27||

28sarva eva tu gandharvāḥ śataśo'tha sahasraśaḥ
|
jighāṃsamānāḥ sahitāḥ karṇamabhyadravan raṇe||28||

29asibhiḥ paṭṭiśaiḥ śūlairgadābhiśca mahābalāḥ
|
sūtaputraṃ jighāṃsantaḥ samantāt paryavākiran||29||

30anye'sya yugamacchindan dhvajamanye nyapātayan
|
īṣāmanye hayānanye sūtamanye nyapātayan||30||

31anye chatraṃ varūthaṃ ca bandhuraṃ ca tathā'pare
|
gandharvā bahusāhasrāstilaśo vyadhaman ratham||31||

32tato rathādavaplutya sūtaputro'sicarmabhṛt
|
vikarṇarathamāsthāya mokṣāyāśvānacodayat||32||
