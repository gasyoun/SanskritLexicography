Mahābhārata 1.170 (Ādiparvan)

1vaiśaṃpāyana uvāca|
gate bhagavati vyāse pāṇḍavā hṛṣṭamānasāḥ
|
te pratasthuḥ puraskṛtya mātaraṃ puruṣarṣabhāḥ||1||

2āmantrya brāhmaṇaṃ pūrvamabhivādyanumānya ca
|
samairudaṅmukhairmārgairyathoddiṣṭaṃ parantapāḥ||2||

3ta tvagacchannahorātrā tīrthaṃ somāśrayāyaṇam
|
āseduḥ puruṣavyāghrā gaṅgāyāṃ pāṇḍunandanāḥ||3||

4ulmukaṃ tu samudyamya teṣāmagre dhanañjayaḥ
|
prakāśārthaṃ yayau tatra rakṣārthaṃ ca mahārathaḥ||4||

5tatra gaṅgājale ramye vivikte krīḍayan striyaḥ
|
īrṣyargandharvarājo vai lajakrīḍāmupāgataḥ||5||

6śabdaṃ teṣāṃ sa śuśrāva nadīṃ samupasarpatām
|
tena śabdena cāviṣṭaścukrodha balavadbalī||6||

7sa dṛṣṭvā pāṇḍavāṃstatra saha mātrā parantapān
|
viṣphārayandhanurghoramidaṃ vacanamabravīt||7||

8sandhyā saṃrajyate ghorā pūrvarātrāgameṣu yā
|
aśītibhirlavairhīnaṃ tanmuhūrtaṃ pracakṣate||8||

9vihitaṃ kāmacārāṇāṃ yakṣagandharvarakṣasām
|
śeṣamanyanmanuṣyāṇāṃ karmacāreṣu vai smṛtam||9||

10lobhātpracāraṃ caratastāsu velāsu vai narān
|
upakrāntā nigṛhṇīmo rākṣasaiḥ saha bāliśān||10||

11ato rātrau prāpnuvato jalaṃ brahmavido janāḥ
|
garhayanti narānsarvānbalasthānnṛpatīnapi||11||

12ārāttiṣṭhata māmahyaṃ samīpamupasarpata
|
kasmānmāṃ nābhijānīta prāptaṃ bhāgīrathījalam||12||

13aṅgāraparṇaṃ gandharvaṃ vitta māṃ svabalāśrayam
|
ahaṃ hi mānī cerṣyuśca kuberasya priyaḥ sakhā||13||

14aṅgāraparṇamityevaṃ khyātaṃ cedaṃ vanaṃ mama
|
anugaṅgaṃ carankāmāṃścitraṃ yatra ramāmyaham||14||

15na kauṇapāḥ śṛṅgiṇo vā na devā na ca mānuṣāḥ
|
idaṃ samupasarpanti tatkiṃ samanusarpatha||15||

16arjuna uvāca|
samudre himavatpārśve nadyāmasyā ca durmate
|
rātrāvahani sandhyāyāṃ kasya klṛptaḥ parigrahaḥ||16||

17bhukto vāpyathavā'bhukto rātrāvahani khecara
|
na kālaniyamo hyasti gaṅgā prāpya saridvarām||17||

18vayaṃ ca śaktisampannā akāle tvāmadhṛṣṇuma
|
aśaktā hi raṇe krūra yuṣmānarcanti mānavāḥ||18||

19purā himavataścaiṣā hemaśṛṅgādviniḥsṛtā
|
gaṅgā gatvā samudrāmbhaḥ saptadhā samapadyata||19||

20gaṅgāṃ ca yamunāṃ caiva plakṣajātāṃ sarasvatīm
|
rathasthāṃ sarayūṃ caiva gomatīṃ gaṇḍakīṃ tathā||20||

21aparyuṣitapāpāste nadīḥ sapta pibanti ye
|
iyaṃ bhūtvā caikavaprā śucirākāśagā punaḥ||21||

22deveṣu gaṅgā gandharva prāpnotyalakanandatām
|
tathā pitṝnvaitaraṇī dustarā pāpakarmabhiḥ
|
gaṅgā bhavati vai prāpya kṛṣṇadvaipāyano'bravīt||22||

23asambādhā devanadī svargasaṃpādanī śubhā
|
kathamicchasi tāṃ roddhuṃ naiṣa dharmaḥ sanātanaḥ||23||

24anivāryamasambādhaṃ tava vācā kathaṃ vayam
|
na spṛśema yathākāmaṃ puṇyaṃ bhāgīrathījalam||24||

25vaiśaṃpāyana uvāca|
aṅgāraparṇastacchrutvā kruddha ānamya kārmukam
|
mumoca bāṇānniśitānahīnāśīviṣāniva||25||

26ulmukaṃ bhrāmayaṃstūrṇaṃ pāṇḍavaścarma cottaram
|
vyapohata śarāṃstasya sarvāneva dhanañjayaḥ||26||

27arjuna uvāca|
bibhīṣikā vai gandharva nāstrajñeṣu prayujyate
|
astrajñeṣu prayukteyaṃ phenavatpravilīyate||27||

28mānuṣānatigandharvānsarvāngandharva lakṣaye
|
tasmādastreṇa divyena yotsye'haṃ na tu māyayā||28||

29purā'stramimāgneyaṃ prādātkila bṛhaspatiḥ
|
bharadvājāya gandharva gururmānyaḥ śatakratoḥ||29||

30bharadvajādagniveśya agniveśyādgururmama
|
sādhvidaṃ mahyamadadaddroṇo brāhmaṇasattamaḥ||30||

31vaiśaṃpāyana uvāca|
ityuktvā pāṇḍavaḥ kruddho gandharvāya mumoca ha
|
pradīptamastramāgneyaṃ dadāhāsya rathaṃ tu tat||31||

32virathaṃ viplutaṃ taṃ tu sa gandharvaṃ mahābalaḥ
|
astratejaḥpramūḍhaṃ ca prapatantamavāṅmukham||32||

33śiroruheṣu jagrāha mālyavatsu dhanañjayaḥ
|
bhrātṝnprati cakarṣātha so'strapātādacetasam||33||

34yudhiṣṭhiraṃ tasya bhāryā prapede śaraṇārthinī
|
nāmnā kumbhīnasī nāma patitrāṇamabhīpsatī||34||

35gandharvyuvāca
|
trāyasva māṃ mahābhāga patiṃ cemaṃ vimuñca me
|
gandharvī śaraṇaṃ prāptā nāmnā kumbhīnasī prabho||35||

36yudhiṣṭhira uvāca|
yuddhe jitaṃ yaśohīnaṃ strīnāthamaparākramam
|
ko nihanyādripuṃ tāta muñcemaṃ ripusūdana||36||

37arjuna uvāca|
jīvitaṃ pratipadyasva gaccha gandharva mā śucaḥ
|
pradiśatyabhayaṃ te'dya kururājo yudhiṣṭhiraḥ||37||

38gandharva uvāca|
jito'haṃ pūrvakaṃ nāma muñcāmyaṅgāraparṇatām
|
na ca ślāghe balenāṅga na nāmnā janasaṃsadi||38||

39sādhvimaṃ labdhavā~llābhaṃ yo'haṃ divyāstradhāriṇam
|
gāndharvyā māyayecchāmi saṃyojayitumarjunam||39||

40astrāgninā vicitro'yaṃ dagdho me ratha uttamaḥ
|
so'haṃ citraratho bhūtvā nāmnā dagdharatho'bhavam||40||

41saṃbhṛtā caiva vidyeyaṃ tapaseha mayā purā
|
nivedayiṣye tāmadya prāṇadāya mahātmane||41||

42saṃstambhayitvā tarasā jitaṃ śaraṇamāgatam
|
yo ripuṃ yojayetprāṇaiḥ kalyāṇaṃ kiṃ na so'rhati||42||

43cākṣuṣī nāma vidyeyaṃ yāṃ somāya dadau manuḥ
|
dadau sa viśvāvasave mama viśvāvasurdadau||43||

44seyaṃ kāpuruṣaṃ prāptā gurudattā praṇaśyati
|
āgamo'syā mayā prokto vīryaṃ pratinibodha me||44||

45yaccakṣuṣā draṣṭumicchettriṣu lokeṣu kiṃcana
|
tatpaśyedyādṛśaṃ cecchettādṛśaṃ draṣṭumarhati||45||

46ekapādena ṣaṇmāsān sthito vidyāṃ labhedimām
|
anuneṣyāmyahaṃ vidyāṃ svayaṃ tubhyaṃ vrate kṛte||46||

47vidyayā hyanayā rājanvayaṃ nṛbhyo viśeṣitāḥ
|
aviśiṣṭāśca devānāmanubhāvapradarśinaḥ||47||

48gandharvajānāmaśvānāmahaṃ puruṣasattama
|
bhrātṛbhyastava tubhyaṃ ca pṛthagdātā śataṃ śatam||48||

49devagandharvavāhāste divyavarṇā manojavāḥ
|
kṣīṇākṣīṇābhavantyete na hīyante ca raṃhasaḥ||49||

50purā kṛtaṃ mahendrasya vajraṃ vṛtranibarhaṇam
|
daśadhā śatadhā caiva tacchīrṇaṃ vṛtramūrdhani||50||

51tato bhāgīkṛto devairvajrabhāga upāsyate
|
loke yaśodhanaṃ kiṃcitsaiva vajratanuḥ smṛtā||51||

52vajrapāṇirbrāhmaṇaḥ syātkṣatraṃ vajrarathaṃ smṛtam
|
vaiśyā vai dānavajrāśca karmavajrā yavīyasaḥ||52||

53kṣatravajrasya bhāgena avadhyā vājinaḥ smṛtāḥ
|
rathāṅgavaḍavā sūte śūrāścāśveṣu ye matāḥ||53||

54kāmavarṇāḥ kāmajavāḥ kāmataḥ samupasthitāḥ
|
iti gandharvajāḥ kāmaṃ pūrayiṣyanti me hayāḥ||54||

55arjuna uvāca|
yadi prītena me dattaṃ saṃśaye jīvitasya vā
|
vidyādhanaṃ śrutaṃ vā'pi na tadgandharva rocaye||55||

56gandharva uvāca|
saṃyogo vai prītikaro mahatsu pratidṛśyate
|
jīvitasya pradānena prīto vidyāṃ dadāmi te||56||

57tvatto'pyahaṃ grahīṣyāmi astramāgneyamuttamam
|
tathaiva yogyaṃ bībhatso cirāya maratarṣabha||57||

58arjuna uvāca|
tvatto'streṇa vṛṇomyaśvānsaṃyogaḥ śāsvato'stunau
|
sakhe tadbrūhi gandharva yuṣmabhyo yadbhayaṃ bhavet||58||

59kāraṇaṃ brūhi gandharva kiṃ tadyena sma dharṣitāḥ
|
yānto vedavidaḥ sarve santo rātrāvarindamāḥ||59||

60gandharva uvāca|
anagnayo'nāhutayo na ca viprapuraskṛtāḥ
|
yūyaṃ tato dharṣitāḥ stha mayā vai pāṇḍunandanāḥ||60||

61yakṣarākṣasagandharvāḥ piśācoragadānavāḥ
|
vistaraṃ kuruvaṃśasya dhīmantaḥ kathayanti te||61||

62nāradaprabhṛtīnāṃ tu devarṣīṇāṃ mayā śrutam
|
guṇānkathayatāṃ vīra pūrveṣāṃ tava dhīmatām||62||

63svayaṃ cāpi mayā dṛṣṭaścaratā sāgarāmbarām
|
imāṃ vasumatīṃ kṛtsnāṃ prabhāvaḥ sukulasya te||63||

64vede dhanuṣi cācāryamabhijānāmi te'rjuna
|
viśrutaṃ triṣu lokeṣu bhāradvājaṃ yaśasvinam||64||

65dharmaṃ vāyuṃ ca śakraṃ ca vijānāmyaśvinau tathā
|
pāṇḍuṃ ca kuruśārdūla ṣaḍetānkuruvardhanān
|
pitṝnetānahaṃ pārtha devamānuṣasattamān||65||

66divyātmāno mahātmānaḥ sarvaśastrabhṛtāṃ varāḥ
|
bhavanto bhrātaraḥ śūrāḥ sarve sucaritavratāḥ||66||

67uttamāṃ ca manobuddhiṃ bhavatāṃ bhāvitātmanām
|
jānannapi ca vaḥ pārtha kṛtavāniha dharṣaṇām||67||

68strīsakāśe ca kauravya na pumānkṣantumarhati
|
dharṣaṇāmātmanaḥ paśyanbrāhudraviṇamāśritaḥ||68||

69naktaṃ ca balamasmākaṃ bhūya evābhivardhate
|
yatastato māṃ kaunteya sadāraṃ manyurāviśat||69||

70so'haṃ tvayeha vijitaḥ saṅkhye tāpatyavardhana
|
yena teneha vidhinā kīrtyamānaṃ nibodha me||70||

71brahmacaryaṃ paro dharmaḥ sa cāpi niyatastvayi
|
yasmāttasmādahaṃ pārtha raṇe'smi vijitastvayā||71||

72yastu syātkṣatriyaḥ kaścitkāmavṛttaḥ parantapa
|
naktaṃ ca yudhi yudhyeta na sa jīvetkathaṃcana||72||

73yastu syātkāmavṛtto'pi pārtha brahmapuraskṛtaḥ
|
jayennaktañcarānsarvānsapurohitadhūrgataḥ||73||

74tasmāttāpatya yatkiṃcinnṛṇāṃ śreya ihepsitam
|
tasminkarmaṇi yoktavyā dāntātmānaḥ purohitāḥ||74||

75vede ṣaḍaṅge niratāḥ śucayaḥ satyavādinaḥ
|
dharmātmānaḥ kṛtātmānaḥ syurnṛpāṇāṃ purohitāḥ||75||

76jayaśca niyato rājñaḥ svargaśca tadanantaram
|
yasya syāddharmavidvāgmī purodhāḥ śīlavān śuciḥ||76||

77lābhaṃ labdhumalabdhaṃ vā labdhaṃ vā parirakṣitum
|
purohitaṃ prakurvīta rājā guṇasamanvitam||77||

78purohitamate tiṣṭhedya icchedbhūtimātmanaḥ
|
prāptuṃ vasumatīṃ sarvāṃ sarvaśaḥ sāgarāmbarām||78||

79na hi kevalaśauryeṇa tāpatyābhijanena ca
|
jayedabrāhmaṇaḥ kaścidbhūmiṃ bhūmipatiḥ kvacit||79||

80tasmādevaṃ vijānīhi kurūṇāṃ vaṃśavardhana
|
brāhmaṇapramukhaṃ rājyaṃ śakyaṃ pālayituṃ ciram||80||
