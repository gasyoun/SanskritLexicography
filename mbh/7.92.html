Mahābhārata 7.92 (Droṇaparvan)

1sañjaya uvāca|
sanniruddhastu taiḥ pārtho mahābalaparākramaḥ
|
drutaṃ samanuyātaśca droṇena rathināṃ varaḥ ||1||

2kiranniṣugaṇāṃstīkṣṇān sa raśmīniva bhāskaraḥ
|
tāpayāmāsa tat sainyaṃ dehaṃ vyādhigaṇo yathā ||2||

3aśvo viddho rathaśchinnaḥ sārohaḥ pātito gajaḥ
|
chatrāṇi cāpaviddhāni rathāścakrairvinā kutāḥ ||3||

4vidrutāni ca sainyāni śarārtāni samantataḥ
|
ityāsīttumulaṃ yuddhaṃ na prājñāyata kiñcana ||4||

5teṣāṃ saṃyacchatāṃ saṅkhye parasparamajihmagaiḥ
|
arjuno dhvajinīṃ rājannabhīkṣṇaṃ samakampayat ||5||

6satyāṃ cikīrṣamāṇastu pratijñāṃ satyasaṅgaraḥ
|
abhyadravadrathaśreṣṭhaṃ śoṇāśvaṃ śvetavāhanaḥ ||6||

7taṃ droṇaḥ pañcaviṃśatyā marmabhidbhirajihmagaiḥ
|
antevāsinamācāryo maheṣvāsaṃ samārpayat ||7||

8taṃ tūrṇamiva bībhatsuḥ sarvaśastrabhṛtāṃ varaḥ
|
abhyadhāvadiṣūnasyanniṣuvegavighātakān ||8||

9tasyāśukṣiptān bhallāhni bhallaiḥ sannataparvabhiḥ
|
pratyavidhyadameyātmā brahmāstraṃ samudīrayan ||9||

10tada‌dbhutamapaśyāma droṇasyācāryakaṃ yudhi
|
yatamāno yuvā nainaṃ pratyavidhyadyadarjunaḥ ||10||

11kṣaranniva mahāmegho vāridhārāḥ sahasraśaḥ
|
droṇameghaḥ pārthaśailaṃ vavarṣa śaravṛṣṭibhiḥ ||11||

12arjunaḥ śaravarṣaṃ tadbrahmāstreṇaiva māriṣa
|
pratijagrāha tejasvī bāṇairbāṇānniśātayan ||12||

13droṇastu pañcaviṃśatyā śvetavāhanamārdayat
|
vāsudevaṃ ca saptatyā bāhvorurasi cāśugaiḥ ||13||

14pārthastu prahasan dhīmānācāryaṃ saśaraughiṇam
|
visṛjantaṃ śitān‌ bāṇānavārayata taṃ yudhi ||14||

15atha tau vadhyamānau tu droṇena rathasattamau
|
āvarjayetāṃ durdharṣaṃ yugāntāgnimivotthitam ||15||

16varjayanniśitānbāṇāndroṇacāpaviniḥsṛtān
|
kirīṭamālī kaunteyo bhojānīkaṃ vyaśātayat ||16||

17sontarā kṛtavarmāṇaṃ kāmbojaṃ ca sudakṣiṇam
|
abhyayādvarjayan droṇaṃ mainākamiva parvatam ||17||

18tato bhojo naravyāghro durdharṣaṃ kurusattamam
|
avidhyat tūrṇamavyagro daśabhiḥ kaṅkapatribhiḥ ||18||

19tamarjunaḥ śatenājau rājan vivyādha patriṇāṃ
|
punaścānyaistribhirbāṇairmohayanniva sātvatam ||19||

20bhojastu prahasan pārthaṃ vāsudevaṃ ca mādhavam
|
ekaikaṃ pañcaviṃśatyā sāyakānāṃ samārpayat ||20||

21tasyārjuno dhanuśchittvā vivyādhainaṃ trisaptabhiḥ
|
śarairagniśikhākāraiḥ kruddhāśīviṣasannibhaiḥ ||21||

22athānyaddhanurādāya kṛtavarmā mahārathaḥ
|
pañcabhiḥ sāyakaistūrṇaṃ vivyādhorasi bhārata ||22||

23punaśca niśitairbāṇaiḥ pārthaṃ vivyādha pañcabhiḥ
|
taṃ pārtho navabhirbāṇairājaghāna stanāntare ||23||

24dṛṣṭvā viṣaktaṃ kaunteyaṃ kṛtavarmarathaṃ prati
|
cintayāmāsa vārṣṇeyo na naḥ kālātyayo bhavet ||24||

25tataḥ kṛṣṇo'bravīt pārthaṃ kṛtavarmaṇi mā dayām
|
kuru sambandhakaṃ hitvā pramathyainaṃ viśātaya ||25||

26tataḥ sa kṛtavarmāṇaṃ mohayitvā'rjunaḥ śaraiḥ
|
abhyagājjavanairaśvaiḥ kāmbojānāmanīkinīm ||26||

27amarṣitastu hārdikyaḥ praviṣṭe śvetavāhane
|
vidhunvansaśaraṃ cāpaṃ pāñcālyābhyāṃ samāgataḥ ||27||

28cakrarakṣau tu pāñcālyāvarjunasya padānugau
|
paryavārayadāyāntau kṛtavarmā ratheṣubhiḥ ||28||

29tāvavidhyattato bhojaḥ kṛtavarmā śitaiḥ śaraiḥ
|
tribhireva yudhāmanyuṃ caturbhiścottamaujasam ||29||

30tāvapyenaṃ vividhaturdaśabhirdaśabhiḥ śaraiḥ
|
tribhireva yudhāmanyuruttamaujāstribhistathā ||30||

31sañcicchidaturapyasya dhvajaṃ kārmukameva ca
|
athānyaddhanurādāya hārdikyaḥ krodhamūrchitaḥ ||31||

32kṛtvā vidhanuṣau vīrau śaravarṣairavākirat
|
tāvanye dhanuṣī sajye kṛtvā bhojaṃ vijaghnatuḥ ||32||

33tenāntareṇa bībhatsurviveśāmitravāhinīm
|
na lebhāte tu tau dvāraṃ vāritau kṛtavarmaṇā ||33||

34dhārtarāṣṭreṣvanīkeṣu yatamānau nararṣabhau
|
anīkānyardayan yuddhe tvaritaḥ śvetavāhanaḥ ||34||

35nāvadhīt kṛtavarmāṇaṃ prāptamapyariṣūdanaḥ
|
taṃ dṛṣṭvā tu tathā yāntaṃ śūro rājā śrutāyudhaḥ ||35||

36abhyadravatsusaṅkruddho vidhunvāno mahaddhanuḥ
|
sa pārthaṃ tribhirānarchat saptatyā ca janārdanam ||36||

37kṣurapreṇa sutīkṣṇena pārthaketumatāḍayat
|
tato'rjuno navatyā tu śarāṇāṃ nataparvaṇām ||37||

38ājaghāna bhṛśaṃ kruddhastotrairiva mahādvipam
|
sa tanna mamṛṣe rājan pāṇḍaveyasya vikramam ||38||

39athainaṃ saptasaptatyā nārācānāṃ samārpayat
|
tasyārjuno dhanuśchitvā śarāvāpaṃ nikṛtya ca ||39||

40ājaghānorasi kruddhaḥ saptabhirnataparvabhiḥ
|
athānyaddhanurādāya sa rājā krodhamūrcchitaḥ ||40||

41vāsaviṃ navabhirbāṇairbāhvorurasi cārpayat
|
tato'rjunaḥ smayanneva śrutāyudhamarindamaḥ ||41||

42śarairanekasāhasraiḥ pīḍayāmāsa bhārata
|
aśvāṃścāsyāvadhīstūrṇaṃ sārathiṃ ca mahārathaḥ ||42||

43vivyādha cainaṃ saptatyā nārācānāṃ mahābalaḥ
|
hatāśvaṃ rathamutsṛjya sa tu rājā śrutāyudhaḥ ||43||

44abhyadravadraṇe pārthaṃ gadāmudyamya vīryavān
|
varuṇasyātmajo vīraḥ sa tu rājā śrutāyudhaḥ ||44||

45parṇāśā jananī yasya śītatoyā mahānadī
|
tasya mātā'bravīdrājan varuṇaṃ putrakāraṇāt ||45||

46avadhyo'yaṃ bhavelloke śatrūṇāṃ tanayo mama
|
varuṇastvabravīt prīto dadāmyasmai varaṃ hitam ||46||

47divyamastraṃ sutaste'yaṃ yenāvadhyo bhaviṣyati
|
nāsti cāpyamaratvaṃ vai manuṣyasya kathañcana ||47||

48sarveṇāvaśyamartavyaṃ jātena saritāṃ vare
|
durdharṣastveṣa śatrūṇāṃ raṇeṣu bhavitā sadā ||48||

49astrasyāsya prabhāvādvai vyetu te mānaso jvaraḥ
|
ityuktvā varuṇaḥ prādādgadāṃ mantrapuraskṛtām ||49||

50yāmāsādya durādharṣaḥ sarvaloke śrutāyudhaḥ
|
uvāca cainaṃ bhagavān punareva jaleśvaraḥ ||50||

51ayudhyati na moktavyā sā tvayyeva patediti
|
hanyādeṣā pratīpaṃ hi prayoktāramapi prabho ||51||

52na cākarot sa tadvākyaṃ prāpte kāle śrutāyudhaḥ
|
sa tayā vīraghātinyā janārdanamatāḍayat ||52||

53pratijagrāha tāṃ kṛṣṇaḥ pīnenāṃsena vīryavān
|
nākampayata śauriṃ sā vindhyaṃ girimivānilaḥ ||53||

54pratyudyāntī tamevaiṣā kṛtyeva duradhiṣṭhitā
|
jaghāna cāsthitaṃ vīraṃ śrutāyudhamamarṣaṇam ||54||

55hatvā śrutāyudhaṃ vīraṃ dharaṇīmanvapadyata
|
gadāṃ nivartitāṃ dṛṣṭvā nihataṃ ca śrutāyudham ||55||

56hāhākāro mahāṃstatra sainyānāṃ samajāyata
|
svenāstreṇa hataṃ dṛṣṭvā śrutāyudhamarindamam ||56||

57ayudhyamānāya tataḥ keśavāya narādhipa
|
kṣiptā śrutāyudhenātha tasmāttamavadhīdgadā ||57||

58yathoktaṃ varuṇenājau tathā sa nidhanaṃ gataḥ
|
vyasuścāpyapatadbhūmau prekṣatāṃ sarvadhanvinām ||58||

59patamānastu sa babhau parṇāśāyāḥ priyaḥ sutaḥ
|
sa bhagna iva vātena bahuśākho vanaspatiḥ||59||

60tataḥ sarvāṇi sainyāni senāmukhyāśca sarvaśaḥ
|
prādravanta hataṃ dṛṣṭvā śrutāyudhamarindamam ||60||

61tataḥ kāmbojarājasya putraḥ śūraḥ sudakṣiṇaḥ
|
abhyayājjavanairaśvaiḥ phālgunaṃ śatrusūdanam ||61||

62tasya pārthaḥ śarān sapta preṣayāmāsa bhārata
|
te taṃ śūraṃ vinirbhidya prāviśan dharaṇītalam ||62||

63so'tividdhaḥ śaraistīkṣṇairgāṇḍīvapreṣitairmṛdhe
|
arjunaṃ prativivyādha daśabhiḥ kaṅkapatribhiḥ ||63||

64vāsudevaṃ tribhirviddhvā punaḥ pārthaṃ ca pañcabhiḥ
|
tasya pārtho dhanuśchittvā ketuṃ ciccheda māriṣa ||64||

65bhallābhyāṃ bhṛśatīkṣṇābhyāṃ taṃ ca vivyādha pāṇḍavaḥ
|
sa tu pārthaṃ tribhirviddhvā siṃhanāda‌mathānadat ||65||

66sarvapāraśavīṃ caiva śaktiṃ śūraḥ sudakṣiṇaḥ
|
sa ghaṇṭāṃ prāhiṇoddhorāṃ kruddho gāṇḍīvadhanvane ||66||

67sā jvalantī maholkeva tamāsādya mahāratham
|
savisphuliṅgā nirbhidya nipapāta mahītale ||67||

68śaktyā tvabhihato gāḍhaṃ mūrcchayā'bhipariplutaḥ
|
samāśvāsya mahātejāḥ sṛkkiṇī parilelihan ||68||

69taṃ caturdaśabhiḥ pārtho nārācaiḥ kaṅkapatribhiḥ
|
sāśvadhvajadhanuḥsūtāṃ vivyādhācintyavikramaḥ ||69||

70rathaṃ cānyaiḥ subahubhiścakre viśakalaṃ śaraiḥ
|
sudakṣiṇaṃ taṃ kāmbojaṃ moghasaṅkalpavikramam ||70||

71bibheda hṛdi bāṇena pṛthudhāreṇa pāṇḍavaḥ
|
sa bhinnavarmā srastāṅgaḥ prabhraṣṭamukuṭāṅgadaḥ ||71||

72papātābhimukhaḥ śūro yantramukta iva dhvajaḥ
|
gireḥ śikharajaḥ śrīmān suśākhaḥ supratiṣṭhitaḥ ||72||

73nirbhagna iva vātena karṇikāro himātyaye
|
śete sma nihato bhūmau kāmbojāstaraṇocitaḥ ||73||

74mahārhābharaṇopetaḥ sānumāniva parvataḥ
|
sudarśanīyastāmrākṣaḥ karṇinā sa sudakṣiṇaḥ ||74||

75putraḥ kāmbojarājasya pārthena vinipātitaḥ
|
dhārayannagnisaṅkāśāṃ śirasā kāñcanīṃ srajam ||75||

76aśobhata mahābāhurvyasurbhūmau nipātitaḥ
|
tataḥ sarvāṇi sainyāni vyadravanta sutasya te
|
hataṃ śrutāyudhaṃ dṛṣṭvā kāmbojaṃ ca sudakṣiṇam‌ ||76||
