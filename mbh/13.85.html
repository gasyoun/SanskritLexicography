Mahābhārata 13.85 (Anuśāsanaparvan)

1devā ūcuḥ |
asurastārako nāma tvayā dattavaraḥ prabho
|
surānṛṣīṃśca kliśnāti vadhastasya vidhīyatām ||1||

2tasmādbhayaṃ samutpannamasmākaṃ vai pitāmaha
|
paritrāyasva no deva na hyanyā gatirasti naḥ ||2||

3brahmovāca|
samo'haṃ sarvabhūtānāmadharmaṃ neha rocaye
|
hanyatāṃ tārakaḥ kṣipraṃ surarṣigaṇabādhitā ||3||

4vedā dharmāśca nocchedaṃ gaccheyuḥ surasattamāḥ
|
vihitaṃ pūrvamevātra mayā vai vyetu vo jvaraḥ ||4||

5devā ūcuḥ |
varadānādbhagavato daiteyo balagarvitaḥ
|
devairna śakyate hantuṃ sa kathaṃ praśamaṃ vrajet ||5||

6sa hi naiva sma devānāṃ nāsurāṇāṃ na rakṣasām
|
vadhyaḥ syāmiti jagrāha varaṃ tvattaḥ pitāmaha ||6||

7devāśca śaptā rudrāṇyā prajocchede purākṛte
|
na bhaviṣyati vo'patyamiti sarve jagatpate ||7||

8brahmovāca|
hutāśano na tatrāsīcchāpakāle surottamāḥ
|
sa utpādayitā'patyaṃ vadhāya tridaśadviṣām ||8||

9tadvai sarvānatikramya devadānavarākṣasān
|
mānuṣānatha gandharvānnāgānatha ca pakṣiṇaḥ ||9||

10astreṇāmoghapātena śaktyā taṃ ghātayiṣyati
|
yato vo bhayamutpannaṃ ye cānye suraśatravaḥ ||10||

11sanātano hi saṅkalpaḥ kāma ityabhidhīyate
|
rudrasya tejaḥ praskannamagnau nipatitaṃ ca yat ||11||

12tattejo'gnirmahadbhūtaṃ dvitīyamiti pāvakam
|
vadhārthaṃ devaśatrūṇāṃ gaṅgāyāṃ janayiṣyati ||12||

13sa tu nāvāpa taṃ śāpaṃ naṣṭaḥ sa hutabhuk tadā
|
tasmādvo bhayahṛddevāḥ samutpatsyati pāvakiḥ ||13||

14anviṣyatāṃ vai jvalanastathā cādya niyujyatām
|
tārakasya vadhopāyaḥ kathito vai mayā'naghāḥ ||14||

15na hi tejasvināṃ śāpāstejaḥsu prabhavanti vai
|
balānyativalaṃ prāpya durbalāni bhavanti vai ||15||

16hanyādavadhyānvaradānapi caiva tapasvinaḥ
|
saṅkalpābhiruciḥ kāmaḥ sanātanatamo'bhavat ||16||

17jagatpatiranirdeśyaḥ sarvagaḥ sarvabhāvanaḥ
|
hṛcchayaḥ sarvabhūtānāṃ jyeṣṭho rudrādapi prabhuḥ ||17||

18anviṣyatāṃ sa tu kṣipraṃ tejorāśihutāśanaḥ
|
sa vo manogataṃ kāmaṃ devaḥ sampādayiṣyati ||18||

19etadvākyamupaśritya tato devā mahātmanaḥ
|
jagmuḥ saṃsiddhasaṅkalpāḥ paryeṣanto vibhāvasum ||19||

20tatastrailokyamṛṣayo vyacinvanta suraiḥ saha
|
kāṃkṣanto darśanaṃ vahneḥ sarve tadgatamānasāḥ ||20||

21pareṇa tapasā yuktāḥ śrīmanto lokaviśrutāḥ
|
lokānanvacaransiddhāḥ sarva eva bhṛgūttama ||21||

22naṣṭamātmani saṃlīnaṃ nābhijagmurhutāśanam
|
tataḥ sañjātasaṃtrāsānagnidarśanalālasān ||22||

23jalecaraḥ klāntamanāstejasā'gneḥ pradīpitaḥ
|
uvāca devānmaṇḍūko rasātalatalotthitaḥ ||23||

24rasātalatale devā vasatyagniriti prabho
|
santāpādiha samprāptaḥ pāvakaprabhavādaham ||24||

25sa saṃsupto jale devā bhagavānhavyavāhanaḥ
|
apaḥ saṃsṛjya tejobhistena santāpitā vayam ||25||

26tasya darśanamiṣṭaṃ vo yadi devā vibhāvasoḥ
|
tatraivamadhigacchadhvaṃ kāryaṃ vo yadi vahninā ||26||

27gamyatāṃ sādhayiṣyāmo vayaṃ hyagnibhayātsurāḥ
|
etāvaduktvā maṇḍūkastvarito jalamāviśat ||27||

28hutāśanastu bubudhe maṇḍūkasya ca paiśunam
|
śaśāpa sa tamāsādya na rasānvetsyasīti vai ||28||

29taṃ vai saṃyujya śāpena maṇḍūka tvarito yayau
|
anyatra vāsāya vibhurna cātmānamadarśayat ||29||

30devāstvanugrahaṃ cakrurmaṇḍūkānāṃ bhṛgūttama
|
yattacchṛṇu mahābāho gadato mama sarvaśaḥ ||30||

31devā ūcuḥ |
agniśāpādajihvāpi rasajñānabahiṣkṛtāḥ
|
sarasvatīṃ bahuvidhāṃ yūyamuccārayiṣyatha ||31||

32bilavāsaṃ gatāṃścaiva nirāhārānacetasaḥ
|
gatāsūnapi saṃśuṣkān bhūmiḥ saṃtārayiṣyati ||32||

33tamoghanāyāmapi vai niśāyāṃ vicariṣyatha
|
ityuktvā tāṃstato devāḥ punareva mahīmimām ||33||

34parīyurjvalanasyārthe na cāvindan hutāśanam
|
atha tāndviradaḥ kaścitsurendradviradopamaḥ ||34||

35aśvatthastho'gnirityevamāha devān bhṛgūdvaha
|
śaśāpa jvalanaḥ sarvāndviradānkrodhamūrcchitaḥ||35||

36pratīpā bhavatāṃ jihvā bhavitrīti bhṛgūdvaha
|
ityuktvā niḥsṛtośvatthādagnirvāraṇasūcitaḥ
|
praviveśa śamīgarbhamatha vahniḥ suṣupsayā ||36||

37anugrahaṃ tu nāgānāṃ yaṃ cakruḥ śṛṇu taṃ prabho
|
devā bhṛgukulaśreṣṭha prītyā satyaparākramāḥ ||37||

38devā ūcuḥ |
pratīpayā jihvayāpi sarvāhāraṃ kariṣyatha
|
vācaṃ coccārayiṣyadhvamuccairavyañjitākṣarām ||38||

39ityuktvā punarevāgnimanusasrurdivaukasaḥ
|
aśvatthānniḥsṛtaścāgniḥ śamīgarbhamupāviśat ||39||

40śukena khyāpito vipra taṃ devāḥ samupādravan
|
śaśāpa śukamagnistu vāgvihīno bhaviṣyasi ||40||

41jihvāmāvartayāmāsa tasyāpi hutabhuktathā
|
dṛṣṭvā tu jvalanaṃ devāḥ śukamūcurdayānvitāḥ ||41||

42bhavitā na tvamatyantaṃ śukatve naṣṭavāgiti
|
āvṛttajihvasya sato vākyaṃ kāntaṃ bhaviṣyati||42||

43bālasyeva pravṛddhasya kalamavyaktamadbhutam
|
ityuktvā taṃ śamīgarbhe vahnimālakṣya devatāḥ ||43||

44tadevāyatanaṃ cakruḥ puṇyaṃ sarvakriyāsvapi
|
tataḥ prabhṛti cāpyagniḥ śamīgarbheṣu dṛśyate ||44||

45utpādane tathopāyamabhijagmuśca mānavāḥ
|
āpo rasātale yāstu saṃspṛṣṭāścitrabhānunā ||45||

46tāḥ parvataprasravaṇairūṣmāṃ muñcati bhārgava
|
pāvakenādhiśayatā saṃtaptāstasya tejasā ||46||

47athāgnirdevatā dṛṣṭvā babhūva vyathitastadā
|
kimāgamanamityevaṃ tānapṛcchata pāvakaḥ ||47||

48tamūcurvibudhāḥ sarve te caiva paramarṣayaḥ ||48||

49tvāṃ niyokṣyāmahe kārye tadbhavānkartumarhati
|
kṛte ca tasmin bhavitā tavāpi sumahānguṇaḥ ||49||

50agniruvāca|
brūta yadbhavatāṃ kāryaṃ kartāsmi tadahaṃ surāḥ
|
bhavatāṃ tu niyojyo'smi mā votrāstuvicāraṇā ||50||

51devā ūcuḥ |
asurastārako nāma brahmaṇo varadarpitaḥ
|
asmānprabādhate vīryādvadhastasya vidhīyatām ||51||

52imāndevagaṇāṃstāta prajāpatigaṇāṃstathā
|
ṛṣīṃścāpi mahābhāga paritrāyasva pāvaka ||52||

53apatyaṃ tejasā yuktaṃ pravīraṃ janaya prabho
|
yadbhayaṃ no'surāttasmānnāśayeddhavyavāhana ||53||

54śaptānāṃ no mahādevyā nānyadasti parāyaṇam
|
anyatra bhavato vīryaṃ tasmātrāyasva naḥ prabho ||54||

55ityuktaḥ sa tathetyuktvā bhagavānhavyavāhanaḥ
|
jagāmātha durādharṣo gaṅgāṃ bhāgīrathīṃ prati ||55||

56tayā cāpyabhavanmiśro garbhaṃ cāsyādadhe tadā
|
vavṛdhe sa tadā garbhaḥ kakṣe kṛṣṇagatiryathā ||56||

57tejasā tasya devasya gaṅgā vihvalacetanā
|
santāpamagamattīvraṃ soḍhuṃ sā na śaśāka ha ||57||

58āhite jvalanenātha garbhe tejaḥsamanvite
|
gaṅgāyāmasuraḥ kaścidbhairavaṃ nāda‌mānadat ||58||

59abuddhipatitenātha nādena vipulena sā
|
vitrastodbhrāntanayanā gaṅgā visrutalocanā ||59||

60visaṃjñā nāśakadgarbhaṃ voḍhumātmānameva ca
|
sā tu tejaḥparītāṅgī kampayantīva jāhnavī ||60||

61uvāca jvalanaṃ vipra tadā garbhabaloddhutā
|
te na śaktāsmi bhagavaṃstejaso'sya vidhāraṇe||61||

62vimūḍhā'smi kṛtānena na me svāsthyaṃ yathā purā
|
vihvalā cāsmi bhagavaṃśceto naṣṭaṃ ca me'nagha ||62||

63dhāraṇe nāsya śaktā'haṃ garbhasya tapatāṃ vara
|
utsrakṣyehamimaṃ duḥkhānna tu kāmātkathañcana ||63||

64na tejaso'sti saṃsparśo mama deva vibhāvaso
|
āpadarthe hi sambandhaḥ susukṣmo'pi mahādyute ||64||

65yadatra guṇasampannamitaradvā hutāśana
|
tvayyeva tadahaṃ manye dharmādharmau ca kevalau ||65||

66tāmuvāca tato vahnirdhāryatāṃ dhāryatāmiti
|
garbho mattejasā yukto mahāguṇaphalodayaḥ ||66||

67śaktā hyasi mahīṃ kṛtsnāṃ vāḍhuṃ dhārayituṃ tathā
|
na hi te kiñcidaprāpyamanyato dhāraṇādṛte ||67||

68sā vahninā vāryamāṇā devairapi saridvarā
|
samutsasarja taṃ garbhaṃ merau girivare tadā ||68||

69samarthā dhāraṇe cāpi rudratejaḥpradharṣitā
|
nāśakattaṃ tadā garbhaṃ saṃdhārayitumojasā ||69||

70sā samutsṛjya taṃ duḥkhāddīptavaiśvānaraprabham
|
darśayāmāsa cāgnistaṃ tadā gaṅgāṃ bhṛbhūdvaha ||70||

71papraccha saritāṃ śreṣṭhāṃ kañcidgarbhaḥ sukhodayaḥ
|
kīdṛgvaṇarṇo'pi vā devi kīdṛgrūpaśca dṛdayate
|
tejasā kena vā yuktaḥ sarvametadbravīhi me ||71||

72gaṅgovāca
|
jātarūpaḥ sa garbho vai tejasā tvamivānagha
|
suvarṇo vimalo dīptaḥ parvataṃ cāvabhāsayat ||72||

73padmotpalavimiśrāṇāṃ hradānāmiva śītalaḥ
|
gandho'sya sa kadambānāṃ tulyo vai tapatāṃ vara ||73||

74tejasā tasya garbhasya bhāskarasyeva raśmibhiḥ
|
yaddravyaṃ parisaṃsṛṣṭaṃ pṛthivyāṃ parvateṣu ca ||74||

75tatsarvaṃ kāñcanībhūtaṃ samantātpratyadṛśyata
|
paryadhāvata śailāṃśca nadīḥ prasravaṇāni ca ||75||

76vyādīpayaṃstejasā ca trailokyaṃ sacarācaram
|
evaṃrūpaḥ sa bhagavānputraste havyavāhana
|
sūryavaiśvānarasamaḥ kāntyā soma ivāparaḥ ||76||

77evamuktvā tu sā devī tatraivāntaradhīyata
|
pāvakaścāpi tejasvī kṛtvā kāryaṃ divaukasām ||77||

78jagāmeṣṭaṃ tato deśaṃ tadā bhārgavanandana
|
etaiḥ karmaguṇairloke nāmāgneḥ parigīyate ||78||

79hiraṇyaretā iti vai ṛṣibhirvibudhaistathā
|
pṛthivī ca tadā devī khyātā vasumatīti vai ||79||

80sa tu garbho mahātejā gāṅgeyaḥ pāvakodbhavaḥ
|
divyaṃ śaravaṇaṃ prāpya vavṛdhe'dbhutadarśanaḥ ||80||

81dadṛśuḥ kṛttikāstaṃ tu bālārkasadṛśadyutim
|
putraṃ ve tāśca taṃ bālaṃ pupuṣuḥ stanyavisravaiḥ ||81||

82tataḥ sa kārtikeyatvamavāpa paramadyutiḥ
|
skannatvātskandatāṃ cāpi guhāvāsādguho'bhavat ||82||

83evaṃ suvarṇamutpannamapatyaṃ jātavedasaḥ
|
tatra jāmbūnadaṃ śreṣṭhaṃ devānāmapi bhūṣaṇam ||83||

84tataḥ prabhṛti cāpyetajjātarūpamudāhṛtam
|
ratnānāmuttamaṃ ratnaṃ bhūṣaṇānāṃ tathaiva ca ||84||

85pavitraṃ ca pavitrāṇāṃ maṅgalānāṃ ca maṅgalam
|
yatsuvarṇaṃ sa bhagavānagnirīśaḥ prajāpatiḥ ||85||

86pavitrāṇāṃ pavitraṃ hi kanakaṃ dvijasattamāḥ
|
agnīṣomātmakaṃ caiva jātarūpamudāhṛtam ||86||

87vasiṣṭha uvāca|
api cedaṃ purā rāma śrutaṃ me brahmadarśanam
|
pitāmahasya yadvṛttaṃ brahmaṇaḥ paramātmanaḥ ||87||

88devasya mahatastāta vāruṇīṃ vibhratastanum
|
aiśvarye vāruṇe rāma rudrasyeśasya vai prabho ||88||

89ājagmurmunayaḥ sarve devāścāgnipurogamāḥ
|
yajñāṅgāni ca sarvāṇi vaṣaṭkāraśca mūrtimān ||89||

90mūrtimanti ca sāmāni yajūṃṣi ca sahasraśaḥ
|
ṛgveda‌ścāgamattatra padakramavibhūṣitaḥ ||90||

91lakṣaṇāni svarā stobhā niruktaṃ surapaṃktayaḥ
|
oṅkāraścāvasannetre nigrahapragrahau tathā ||91||

92vedāśca sopaniṣado vidyā sāvitryathāpi ca
|
bhūtaṃ bhavyaṃ bhaviṣyaṃ ca dadhāra bhagavānśivaḥ||92||

93sañjuhāvātmanā''tmānaṃ svayameva tadā prabho
|
yaśaṃ ca śobhayāmāsa bahurūpaṃ pinākadhṛt ||93||

94dyaurnabhaḥ pṛthivī khaṃ ca tathā caivaiṣa bhūpatiḥ
|
sarvavidyeśvaraḥ śrīmāneṣa cāpi vibhāvasuḥ ||94||

95eṣa brahmā śivo rudro varuṇo'gniḥ prajāpatiḥ
|
kīrtyate bhagavāndevaḥ sarvabhūtapatiḥ śivaḥ ||95||

96tasya yajñaḥ paśupatestapaḥ kratava eva ca
|
dīkṣā dīptavatā devī diśaśca sadigīśvarāḥ||96||

97devapatnyaśca kanyāśca devānāṃ caiva mātaraḥ
|
ājagmuḥ sahitāstatra tadā bhṛgukulodvaha ||97||

98yajñaṃ paśupateḥ prītā varuṇasya mahātmanaḥ
|
svayaṃbhuvastu tā dṛṣṭvā retaḥ samapatadbhuvi ||98||

99tasya śukrasya vispandānpāṃsunsaṃgṛhya bhūmitaḥ
|
prāsyatpūṣā karābhyāṃ vai tasminneva hutāśane ||99||

100tatastasminsampravṛtte satre jvālatapāvake
|
brahmaṇo juhvatastatra prādurbhāvo babhūva ha ||100||

101skannamātraṃ ca tacchukraṃ sruveṇa parigṛhya saḥ
|
ājyavanmantrataścāpi so'juhodbhṛgunandana ||101||

102tataḥ sa janayāmāsa bhūtagrāmaṃ ca vīryavān
|
tasya tattejasastasmājjajñe lokeṣu taijasam ||102||

103tamasastāmasā bhāvā vyāpi sattvaṃ tathobhayam
|
sa guṇastejaso nityastasya cākāśameva ca ||103||

104sarvabhūteṣu ca tathā sattvaṃ tejastathottamam
|
śukre hute'gnau tasmiṃstu prādurāsaṃstrayaḥ prabho ||104||

105puruṣā vapuṣā yuktāḥ svaiḥ svaiḥ prasavajairguṇaiḥ
|
bhṛgityeva bhṛguḥ pūrvamaṅgārebhyo'ṅgirā'bhavat ||105||

106aṅgārasaṃśrayāccaiva kavirityaparo'bhavat
|
saha jvālābhirutpanno bhṛgustasmādbhṛguḥ smṛtaḥ ||106||

107marīcibhyo marīcistu mārīcaḥ kaśyapo hyabhūt
|
aṅgārebhyo'ṅgirāstāta vālakhilyāḥ kuśoccayāt ||107||

108atraivātreti ca vibho jātamatriṃ vadantyapi
|
tathā bhasmavyapohebhyo brahmarṣigaṇasammatāḥ ||108||

109vaikhānasāḥ samutpannāstapaḥ śrutaguṇepsavaḥ
|
aśruto'sya samutpannāvaśvinau rūpasammatau ||109||

110śeṣāḥ prajānāṃ patayaḥ srotobhyastasya jajñire
|
ṛṣayo romakūpebhyaḥ svedācchando balānmanaḥ ||110||

111etasmātkāraṇādāhuragniḥ sarvāstu devatāḥ
|
ṛṣayaḥ śrutasampannā veda‌prāmāṇyadarśanāt ||111||

112yāni dārūṇi niryāsāste māsāḥ pakṣasaṃjñitāḥ
|
ahorātrā muhūrtāśca pittaṃ jyotiśca dāruṇam ||112||

113raudraṃ lohitamityāhurlohitātkanakaṃ smṛtam
|
tanmaitrāmati vijñeyaṃ dhūmācca vasavaḥ smṛtāḥ ||113||

114arciṣo yāśca te rudrāstathā''dityā mahāprabhāḥ
|
uddiṣṭāste tathāṅgārā ye dhiṣṇyeṣu divi sthitāḥ ||114||

115ādikartā ca lokasya tatparaṃ brahma taddhruvam
|
sarvakāma‌damityāhustadrahasyamuvāca ha ||115||

116tato'bravīnmahādevo varuṇaḥ pavanātmakaḥ
|
mama satramidaṃ divyamahaṃ gṛhapatistviha ||116||

117trīṇi pūrvāṇyapatyāni mama tāni na saṃśayaḥ
|
iti jānīta khagamā mama yajñaphalaṃ hi tat ||117||

118agniruvāca|
madaṅgebhyaḥ prasūtāni madāśrayakṛtāni ca
|
mamaiva tānyapatyāni varuṇo hyavaśātmakaḥ ||118||

119athābravīllokagururbrahmā lokapitāmahaḥ
|
mamaiva tānyapatyāni mama śukraṃ hutaṃ hi tat ||119||

120ahaṃ kartā hi satrasya hotā śukrasya caiva ha
|
yasya bījaṃ phalaṃ tasya śukraṃ cetkāraṇaṃ matam ||120||

121tato'bruvandevagaṇāḥ pitāmahamupetya vai
|
kṛtāñjalipuṭāḥ sarve śirobhirabhivandya ca ||121||

122vayaṃ ca bhagavansarve jagacca sacarācaram
|
tavaiva prasavāḥ sarve tasmāda‌gnirvibhāvasuḥ ||122||

123varuṇaśceśvaro devo labhatāṃ kāmamīpsitam
|
nisargādbrahmaṇaścāpi varuṇo yādasāmpatiḥ ||123||

124jagrāha vai bhṛguṃ pūrvamapatyaṃ sūryavarcasam
|
īśvaro'ṅgirasaṃ cāgnerapatyārthamakalpayat ||124||

125pitāmahastvapatyaṃ vai kaviṃ jagrāha tattvavit
|
tadā sa vāruṇaḥ khyāto bhṛguḥ prasravakarmakṛt ||125||

126āgneyastvaṃgirāḥ śrīmānkavirbrāhmo mahāyaśāḥ
|
bhārgavāṅgirasau loke lokasantānalakṣaṇau ||126||

127ete hi prastravāḥ sarve prajānāṃ patayastrayaḥ
|
sarvaṃ santānameteṣāmidamityupadhāraya ||127||

128bhṛgostu putrāḥ saptāsansarve tulyā bhṛgorguṇaiḥ
|
cyavano vajraśīrṣaśca śuciraurvastathaiva ca ||128||

129śukro vareṇyaśca vibhuḥ savanaśceti sapta te
|
bhārgavā vāruṇāḥ sarve yeṣāṃ vaṃśe bhavānapi ||129||

130aṣṭau cāṅgirasaḥ putrā vāruṇāstepyudāhṛtāḥ
|
bṛhaspatirutathyaśca payasyaḥ śāntireva ca ||130||

131ghoro virūpaḥ saṃvartaḥ sudhanvā cāṣṭamaḥ smṛtaḥ
|
ete'ṣṭau vahnijāḥ sarve jñānaniṣṭhā nirāmayāḥ ||131||

132brahmaṇastu kaveḥ putrā vāruṇāste'pyudāhṛtāḥ
|
aṣṭau prasavajairyuktā guṇairbrahmavidaḥ śubhāḥ ||132||

133kaviḥ kāvyaśca dhṛṣṇuśca buddhimānuśanāstathā
|
bhṛguśca virajāścaiva kāśī cograśca dharmavit ||133||

134aṣṭau kavisutā hyate sarvamebhirjagattatam
|
prajāpataya ete hi prajābhāgauraha prajāḥ ||134||

135evamaṅgirasaścaiva kaveśca prasavānvayaiḥ
|
bhṛgośca bhṛguśārdūla vaṃśajaiḥ satataṃ jagat ||135||

136varuṇaścādito vipra jagrāha prabhurīśvaraḥ
|
kaviṃ tāta bhṛguṃ cāpi tasmāttau vāruṇau smṛtau ||136||

137jagrāhāṅgirasaṃ devaḥ śikhī tasmāddhutāśanaḥ
|
tasmādāṅgirasā jñeyāḥ sarva eva tadanvayāḥ ||137||

138brahmā pitāmahaḥ pūrvaṃ devatābhiḥ prasāditaḥ
|
ime naḥ santariṣyanti prajābhirjagatīśvarāḥ ||138||

139sarve prajānāṃ patayaḥ sarve cātitapasvinaḥ
|
tvatprasādādimaṃ lokaṃ tārayiṣyanti sāmpratam ||139||

140tathaiva vaṃśakartārastava tejovivardhanāḥ
|
bhaveyurveda‌viduṣaḥ sarve ca kṛtinastathā ||140||

141devapakṣacarāḥ saumyāḥ prājāpatyā maharṣayaḥ
|
āpnuvanti tapaścaiva brahmacaryaṃ paraṃ tathā ||141||

142sarve hi vayamete ca tavaiva prasavaḥ prabho
|
devānāṃ brāhmaṇānāṃ ca tvaṃ hi kartā pitāmaha||142||

143mārīcamāditaḥ kṛtvā sarve caivātha bhārgavāḥ
|
apatyānīti samprekṣya kṣamayāma pitāmaha ||143||

144te tvanenaiva rūpeṇa prajaniṣyanti vai prajāḥ
|
sthāpayiṣyanti cātmānaṃ yugādinidhane tathā ||144||

145ityuktaḥ sa tadā taistu brahmā lokapitāmahaḥ
|
tathetyevābravītprītaste'pi jagmuryathāgatam ||145||

146evametatpurā vṛttaṃ tasya yajñe mahātmanaḥ
|
devaśreṣṭhasya lokādau vāruṇīṃ bibhratastanum ||146||

147agnirbrahmā paśupatiḥ śarvo rudraḥ prajāpatiḥ
|
agnerapatyametadvai suvarṇamiti dhāraṇā ||147||

148agnyabhāve ca kurute vahnisthāneṣu kāñcanam
|
jāmadagnyaḥ pramāṇajño vedaśrutinidarśanāt ||148||

149kuśastambe juhotyagniṃ suvarṇe tatra ca sthite
|
valmīkasya vapāyāṃ ca karṇe vā'jasya dakṣiṇe ||149||

150śakaṭorvyāṃ parasyāpsu brāhmaṇasya kare tathā
|
hute prītikarīmṛddhiṃ bhagavāṃstatra manyate ||150||

151tasmādagniparāḥ sarve devatā iti śuśruma
|
brahmaṇo hi prabhūto'gniragnerapi ca kāñcanam ||151||

152tasmādye vai prayacchanti suvarṇaṃ dharmadārśanaḥ
|
devatāste prayacchanti samastā iti naḥ śrutam ||152||

153tasya cātamaso lokā gacchataḥ paramāṃ gatim
|
svarloke rājarājyena so'bhiṣicyeta bhārgava ||153||

154ādityodayasamprāpte vidhimantrapuraskṛtam
|
dadāti kāñcanaṃ yo vai duḥsvapnaṃ pratihanti saḥ ||154||

155dadātyuditamātre yastasya pāpmā vidhūyate
|
madhyāhne dadato rukmaṃ hanti pāpamanāgatam ||155||

156dadāti paścimāṃ sandhyāṃ yaḥ suvarṇaṃ yatavrataḥ
|
brahmavāyvagnisomānāṃ sālokyamupayāti saḥ ||156||

157sendreṣu caiva lokeṣu pratiṣṭhāṃ vindate śubhām
|
iha loke yaśaḥ prāpya śāntapāpmā ca modate ||157||

158tataḥ sampadyate'nyeṣu lokeṣvapratimaḥ sadā
|
anāvṛtagatiścaiva kāmacāro bhavatyuta ||158||

159na ca kṣarati tebhyaśca yaśaścaivāpnute mahat
|
suvarṇamakṣayaṃ dattvā lokāṃścāpnoti puṣkalān ||159||

160yastu sañjanayitvāgnimādityodayanaṃ prati
|
dadyādvai vratamuddiśya sarvakāmānsamaśnute ||160||

161agnimityeva tatprāhuḥ pradānaṃ ca sukhāvaham
|
yatheṣṭaguṇasaṃvṛttaṃ pravartakamiti smṛtam ||161||

162eṣā suvarṇasyotpattiḥ kathitā te mayā'nagha
|
kārtikeyasya ca vibho tadviddhi bhṛgunandana ||162||

163kārtikeyastu saṃvṛddhaḥ kālena mahatā tadā
|
devaiḥ senāpatitvena vṛtaḥ sendrairbhṛgūdvaha ||163||

164jaghāna tārakaṃ cāpi daityamanyāṃstathāsurān
|
tridaśendrājñayā brahmaঁঁllokānāṃ hitakāmyayā ||164||

165suvarṇadāne ca mayā kathitāste guṇā vibho
|
tasmātsuvarṇaṃ viprebhyaḥ prayaccha dadatāṃ vara ||165||

166bhīṣma uvāca|
ityuktaḥ sa vasiṣṭhena jāmadagnyaḥ pratāpavān
|
dadau suvarṇaṃ viprebhyo vyamucyata ca kilbiṣāt ||166||

167etatte sarvamākhyātaṃ suvarṇasya mahīpate
|
pradānasya phalaṃ caiva janma cāsya yudhiṣṭhira ||167||

168tasmāttvamapi viprebhyaḥ prayaccha kanakaṃ bahu
|
dadatsuvarṇaṃ nṛpate kilbiṣādvipramokṣyasi ||168||
