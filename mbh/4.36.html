Mahābhārata 4.36 (Virāṭaparvan)

1uttara uvāca|adyāhamanugaccheyaṃ dṛḍhadhanvā gavāṃ padam
|
yadi me sārathiḥ kaścidbhavedaśveṣu kovidaḥ ||1||

2taṃ tvahaṃ nāvagacchāmi yo me yantā bhavennaraḥ
|
paśyadhvaṃ sārathiṃ kṣipraṃ mama yuktaṃ prayāsyataḥ ||2||

3aṣṭāviṃśatirātraṃ vā māsaṃ vā nūnamantataḥ
|
yattadāsīnmahadyuddhaṃ tatra me sārathirhataḥ ||3||

4sa labheyaṃ yadā tvanyaṃ hayayānavidaṃ naram
|
tvarāvānadya yātvāhaṃ samucchritamahādhvajam ||4||

5vigāhya tatparānīkaṃ gajavājirathākulam
|
śastrapratāpanirvīryān kurūn jitvā''naye paśūn ||5||

6duryodhanaṃ śāntanavaṃ karṇaṃ vaikartanaṃ kṛpam
|
droṇaṃ ca sahaputreṇa maheṣvāsān samāgatān ||6||

7vitrāsayitvā saṅgrāme dānavāniva vajrabhṛt
|
anenaiva muhūrtena punaḥ pratyānaye paśūn ||7||

8śūnyamāsādya kuravaḥ prayāntyādāya godhanam
|
kiṃ tu śakyaṃ mayā kartuṃ yadahaṃ tatra nābhavam ||8||

9paśyeyuradya me vīryaṃ kuravaste samāgatāḥ
|
kiṃ nu pārtho'rjunaḥ sākṣādayamasmān prabādhate ||9||

10vaiśampāyana uvāca|śrutvā tadarjuno vākyaṃ rājñaḥ putrasya bhāṣataḥ
|
atītasamaye kāle priyāṃ bhāryāmaninditām ||10||

11drupadasya sutāṃ tanvīṃ pāñcālīṃ pāvakātmajām
|
satyārjavaguṇopetāṃ bhartuḥ priyahite ratām ||11||

12uvāca rahasi prītaḥ kṛṣṇāṃ sarvārthakovidaḥ
|
uttaraṃ brūhi kalyāṇi kṣipraṃ madvacanādidam ||12||

13ayaṃ vai pāṇḍavasyāsīt sārathiḥ saṃmato dṛḍhaḥ
|
mahāyuddheṣu saṃsiddhaḥ sa te yantā bhaviṣyati ||13||

14vaiśampāyana uvāca|tasya tadvacanaṃ strīṣu bhāṣataśca punaḥ punaḥ
|
na sā'marṣata pāñcālī bībhatsoḥ parikīrtanam ||14||

15athainamupasaṃgamya strīmadhyāt sā tapasvinī
|
vrīḍamāneva śanakairidaṃ vacanamabravīt ||15||

16yo'sau bṛhadvāraṇābho yuvā supriyadarśanaḥ
|
bṛhannaleti vikhyātaḥ pārthasyāsīt sa sārathiḥ||16||

17dhanuṣyanavaraścāsīt tasya śiṣyo mahātmanaḥ
|
dṛṣṭapūrvo mayā vīra carantyā pāṇḍavān prati ||17||

18yadā tatpāvako dāvamadahat khāṇḍavaṃ mahat
|
arjunasya tadā'nena saṃgṛhītā hayottamāḥ ||18||

19tena sārathinā pārthaḥ sarvabhūtāni sarvaśaḥ
|
ajayat khāṇḍavaprasthena hi yantā'sti tādṛśaḥ ||19||

20uttara uvāca|sairandhri jānāsi tathā yuvānaṃ napuṃsako naiva bhavedyathā'sau
|
ahaṃ na śaknomi bṛhannalāṃ śubhe vaktuṃ svayaṃ yaccha hayān mameti vai ||20||

21draupadyuvāca
|
yeyaṃ kumārī suśroṇī bhaginī te yavīyasī
|
asyāḥ sā vīra vacanaṃ kariṣyati na saṃśayaḥ ||21||

22yadi vai sārathiḥ sa syāt kurūn sarvānna saṃśayaḥ
|
jitvā gāśca samādāya dhruvamāgamanaṃ bhavet ||22||

23evamuktaḥ sa sairandhryā bhaginīṃ pratyabhāṣata
|
gaccha tvamanavadyāṅgi tāmānaya bṛhannalām ||23||

24sā bhrātrā preṣitā śīghramagacchannartanāgṛham
|
yatrāste sa mahābāhuśchannaḥ satreṇa pāṇḍavaḥ ||24||
