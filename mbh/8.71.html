Mahābhārata 8.71 (Karṇaparvan)

1sañjaya uvāca|
dharmarājasya tacchrutvā prītiyuktaṃ vacastataḥ
|
pārthaṃ provāca dharmātmā govindo yadunandanaḥ ||1||

2iti sma kṛṣṇavacanāt pratyuccārya yudhiṣṭhiram
|
babhūva vimanāḥ pārthaḥ kiñcitkṛtveva pātakam ||2||

3tato'bravīdvāsudevaḥ prahasanniva pāṇḍavam
|
kathaṃ nāma bhavedetadyadi tvaṃ pārtha dharmajam ||3||

4asinā tīkṣṇadhāreṇa hanyā dharme vyavasthitam
|
tvamityuktvā'tha rājānamevaṃ kaśmalamāviśaḥ ||4||

5hatvā tu nṛpatiṃ pārtha ākariṣyaḥ kimuttaram
|
evaṃ hi durvido dharmo mandaprajñairviśeṣataḥ ||5||

6sa bhavān dharmabhīrutvāddhruvamaiṣyanmahattamaḥ
|
narakaṃ ghorarūpaṃ ca bhrāturjyeṣṭhasya vai vadhāt ||6||

7sa tvaṃ dharmabhṛtāṃ śreṣṭhaṃ rājānaṃ dharmasaṃhitam
|
prasādaya kuruśreṣṭhametadatra mataṃ mama ||7||

8prasādya bhaktyā rājānaṃ prīte caiva yudhiṣṭhire
|
prayāvastvaritau yoddhuṃ sūtaputrarathaṃ prati ||8||

9hatvā tu samare karṇaṃ tvamadya niśitaiḥ śaraiḥ
|
vipulāṃ prītimādhatsva dharmaputrasya mānada ||9||

10etadatra mahābāho prāptakālaṃ mataṃ mama
|
evaṃ kṛte kṛtaṃ caiva tava kāryaṃ bhaviṣyati ||10||

11tato'rjuno mahārāja lajjayā vai samanvitaḥ
|
dharmarājasya caraṇau prapadya śirasā nataḥ ||11||

12uvāca bharataśreṣṭhaṃ prasīdeti punaḥ punaḥ
|
kṣamasva rājan yat proktaṃ dharmakāmena bhīruṇā ||12||

13dṛṣṭvā tu patitaṃ padbhyāṃ dharmarājo yudhiṣṭhiraḥ
|
dhanañjayamamitraghnaṃ rudantaṃ bharatarṣabha ||13||

14utthāpya bhrātaraṃ rājā dharmarājo dhanañjayam
|
samāśliṣya ca sasnehaṃ praruroda mahīpatiḥ ||14||

15ruditvā suciraṃ kālaṃ bhrātarau sumahādyutī
|
kṛtaśaucau mahārāja prītimantau babhūvatuḥ ||15||

16tata āśliṣya taṃ premṇā mūrdhni cāghrāya pāṇḍavaḥ
|
prītyā paramayā yukto vismayaṃśca punaḥ punaḥ
|
abravīttaṃ maheṣvāsaṃ dharmarājo dhanañjayam ||16||

17karṇena me mahābāho sarvasainyasya paśyataḥ
|
kavacaṃ ca dhvajaṃ caiva dhanuḥ śaktirhayāḥ śarāḥ ||17||

18śaraiḥ kṛttā maheṣvāsa yatamānasya saṃyuge
|
so'haṃ jñātvā raṇe tasya karma dṛṣṭvā ca phālguna ||18||

19vyavasīdāmi duḥkhena na ca me jīvitaṃ priyam
|
na cedadya hi taṃ vīraṃ nihaniṣyasi saṃyuge ||19||

20prāṇāneva parityakṣye jīvitārtho hi ko mama
|
evamuktaḥ pratyuvāca vijayo bharatarṣabha ||20||

21satyena te śape rājanprasādena tathaiva ca
|
bhīmena ca naraśreṣṭha yamābhyāṃ ca mahīpate ||21||

22yathādya samare karṇaṃ haniṣyāmi hato'pi vā
|
mahītale patiṣyāmi satyenāyudhamālabhe ||22||

23evamābhāṣya rājānamabravīnmādhavaṃ vacaḥ
|
adya karṇaṃ raṇe kṛṣṇa sū‌dayiṣye na saṃśayaḥ ||23||

24tava buddhyā hi bhadraṃ te vadhastasya durātmanaḥ
|
evamukto'bravītpārthaṃ keśavo rājasattama ||24||

25śakto'si bharataśreṣṭha hantuṃ karṇaṃ mahābalam
|
eṣa cāpi hi me kāmo nityameva mahāratha ||25||

26kathaṃ bhavānraṇe karṇaṃ nihanyāditi sattama
|
bhūyaścovāca matimān mādhavo dharmanandanam ||26||

27yudhiṣṭhiremaṃ bībhatsuṃ tvaṃ sāntvayitumarhasi
|
anujñātuṃ ca karṇasya vadhāyādya durātmanaḥ ||27||

28śrutvā hyahamayaṃ caiva tvāṃ karṇaśarapīḍitam
|
pravṛttiṃ jñātumāyātāvihāvāṃ pāṇḍunandana ||28||

29diṣṭyā'si rājanna hato diṣṭyā na grahaṇaṃ gataḥ
|
parisāntvaya bībhatsuṃ jayamāśādhi cānagha ||29||

30yudhiṣṭhira uvāca|
ehyehi pārtha bībhatso māṃ pariṣvajya pāṇḍava
|
vaktavyamukto'smi hitaṃ tvayā kṣāntaṃ ca tanmayā ||30||

31ahaṃ tvāmanujānāmi jahi karṇaṃ dhanañjaya
|
manyuṃ ca mā kṛthāḥ pārtha yanmayokto'si dāruṇam ||31||

32sañjaya uvāca|
tato dhanañjayo rājañśirasā praṇatastadā
|
pādau jagrāha pāṇibhyāṃ bhrāturjyeṣṭhasya māriṣa ||32||

33tamutthāpya tato rājā pariṣvajya ca pīḍitam
|
mūrdhnyupāghrāya caivainamidaṃ punaruvāca ha ||33||

34dhanañjaya mahābāho mānito'smi dṛḍhaṃ tvayā
|
māhātmyaṃ vijayaṃ caiva bhūyaḥ prāpnuhi śāśvatam ||34||

35arjuna uvāca|
adya taṃ pāpakarmāṇaṃ sānubandhaṃ raṇe śaraiḥ
|
nayāmyantaṃ samāsādya rādheyaṃ balagarvitam ||35||

36yena tvaṃ pīḍito bāṇairdṛḍhamāyamya kārmukam
|
tasyādya karmaṇaḥ karṇaḥ phalamāpsyati dāruṇam ||36||

37adya tvāmanupaśyāmi karṇaṃ hatvā mahīpate
|
sabhājayitumākrandāditi satyaṃ bravīmi te ||37||

38nāhatvā vinivartiṣye karṇamadya raṇājirāt
|
iti satyena te pādau spṛśāmi jagatīpate ||38||

39sañjaya uvāca|
iti bruvāṇaṃ sumanāḥ kirīṭinaṃ yudhiṣṭhiraḥ prāha vaco bṛhattaram
|
yaśo'kṣayaṃ jīvitamīpsitaṃ te jayaṃ sadā vīryamarikṣayaṃ tadā ||39||

40prayāhi vṛddhiṃ ca diśantu devatā yathā'hamicchāmi tavāstu tattathā || prayāhi śīghraṃ jahi karṇamāhave purandaro vṛtramivātmavṛddhaye ||40||
