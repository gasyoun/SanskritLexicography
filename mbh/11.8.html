Mahābhārata 11.8 (Strīparvan)

1vaiśampāyana uvāca|vidurasya tu tadvākyaṃ niśamya kurusattamaḥ
|
putraśokābhisantaptaḥ papāta bhuvi mūrchitaḥ||1||

2taṃ tathā patitaṃ bhūmau niḥsaṃjñaṃ prekṣya bāndhavāḥ
|
kṛṣṇadvaipāyanaścaiva kṣattā ca vidurastathā||2||

3sañjayaḥ suhṛdaścānye dvāḥsthā ye cāsya sammatāḥ
|
jalena sukhaśītena tālavṛntaiśca bhārata||3||

4pasparśuśca karairgātraṃ vījyamānaśca yatnataḥ
|
āśvāsya tu ciraṃ kālaṃ dhṛtarāṣṭraṃ tathāgatam||4||

5atha dīrghasya kālasya labdhasañjño mahīpatiḥ
|
vilalāpa ciraṃ kālaṃ putrādhibhirabhiplutaḥ||5||

6dhigastu khalu mānuṣyaṃ mānuṣyeṣu parigrahe
|
yatomūlāni duḥkhāni sambhavanti muhurmuhuḥ||6||

7putranāśe'rthanāśe ca jñātisambandhināmatha
|
prāpyate sumahadduḥkhaṃ viṣāgnipratimaṃ vibho||7||

8yena dahyanti gātrāṇi yena prajñā vinaśyati
|
yenābhibhūtaḥ puruṣo maraṇaṃ pratipadyate||8||

9tadidaṃ vyasanaṃ prāptaṃ mayā bhāgyaviparyayāt
|
tasyāntaṃ nādhigacchāmi ṛte prāṇavimokṣaṇāt||9||

10tathaivāhaṃ kariṣyāmi adyaiva dvijasattama
|
ityuktvā tu mahātmānaṃ pitaraṃ brahmavittamam||10||

11dhṛtarāṣṭro'bhavanmūḍhaḥ sa śokaṃ paramaṃ gataḥ
|
abhūcca tūṣṇīṃ rājā'sau dhyāyamāno mahīpatiḥ||11||

12tasya tadvacanaṃ śrutvā kṛṣṇadvaipāyanaḥ prabhuḥ
|
putraśokābhisantaptaṃ putraṃ vacanamabravīt||12||

13vyāsa uvāca|dhṛtarāṣṭra mahābāho yattvāṃ vakṣyāmi tacchṛṇu
|
śrutavānasi medhāvī dharmārthakuśalaḥ prabho||13||

14na te'styaviditaṃ kiñcidveditavyaṃ parantapa
|
anityatāṃ hi martyānāṃ vijānāsi na saṃśayaḥ||14||

15adhruve jīvaloke ca sthāne vā śāśvate sati
|
jīvite maraṇānte ca kasmācchocasi bhārata||15||

16pratyakṣaṃ tava rājendra vairasyāsya samudbhavaḥ
|
putraṃ te kāraṇaṃ kṛtvā kālayogena kāritaḥ||16||

17avaśyaṃ bhavitavye ca kurūṇāṃ vaiśase nṛpa
|
kasmācchocasi tāñśūrāngatānparamikāṃ gatim||17||

18jānatā ca mahābāho vidureṇa mahātmanā
|
yatitaṃ sarvayatnena śamaṃ prati janeśvara||18||

19na ca daivakṛto mārgaḥ śakyo bhūtena kenacit
|
ghaṭatāpi ciraṃ kālaṃ niyantumiti me matiḥ||19||

20devatānāṃ hi yatkāryaṃ mayā pratyakṣataḥ śrutam
|
tatte'haṃ sampravakṣyāmi yathā sthairyaṃ bhavettava||20||

21purāhaṃ tvarito yātaḥ sabhāmaindrīṃ jitaklamaḥ
|
apaśyaṃ tatra ca tadā samavetāndivaukasaḥ||21||

22nāradapramukhāścāpi sarve devarṣayo'nagha
|
tatra cāpi mayā dṛṣṭā pṛthivī pṛthivīpate||22||

23kāryārthamupasamprāptā devatānāṃ samīpataḥ
|
upagamya tadā dhātrī devānāha samāgatān||23||

24yatkāryaṃ mama yuṣmābhirbrahmaṇaḥ sadane tadā
|
pratijñātaṃ mahābhāgāstacchīghraṃ saṃvidhīyatām||24||

25tasyāstadvacanaṃ śrutvā viṣṇurlokanamaskṛtaḥ
|
uvāca vākyaṃ prahasanpṛthivīṃ devasaṃsadi||25||

26dhṛtarāṣṭrasya putrāṇāṃ yastu jyeṣṭhaḥ śatasya vai
|
duryodhana iti khyātaḥ sa te kāryaṃ kariṣyati||26||

27taṃ ca prāpya mahīpālaṃ kṛtakṛtyā bhaviṣyasi
|
tasyārthe pṛthivīpālāḥ kurukṣetraṃ samāgatāḥ||27||

28anyonyaṃ ghātayiṣyanti dṛḍhaiḥ śastraiḥ prahāriṇaḥ
|
tataste bhavitā devi bhārasya yudhi nāśanam||28||

29gaccha śīghraṃ svakaṃ sthānaṃ lokāndhāraya śobhane
|
sa eṣa te suto rāja~llokasaṃhārakāraṇāt||29||

30kaleraṃśaḥ samutpanno gāndhāryā jaṭhare nṛpa
|
amarṣī capalaścāpi krodhano duṣprasādhanaḥ||30||

31daivayogātsamutpannā bhrātarastasya tādṛśāḥ
|
śakunirmātulaścaiva karṇaśca paramaḥ sakhā||31||

32samutpannā vināśārthaṃ pṛthivyāṃ sahitā nṛpāḥ
|
yādṛśo jāyate rājā tādṛśo'sya jano bhavet||32||

33adharmo dharmatāṃ yāti svāmī ceddhārmiko bhavet
|
svāmino guṇadoṣābhyāṃ bhṛtyāḥ syurnātra saṃśayaḥ||33||

34duṣṭaṃ rājānamāsādya gatāste tanayā nṛpa
|
etamarthaṃ mahābāho nārado veda tattvavit||34||

35ātmāparādhātputrāste vinaṣṭāḥ pṛthivīpate
|
mā tāñśocasva rājendra na hi śoke'sti kāraṇam||35||

36na hi te pāṇḍavāḥ svalpamaparādhyanti bhārata
|
putrāstava durātmāno yairiyaṃ ghātitā mahī||36||

37nāradena ca bhadraṃ te pūrvameva na saṃśayaḥ
|
yudhiṣṭhirasya samitau rājasūye niveditam||37||

38pāṇḍavāḥ kauravāḥ sarve samāsādya parasparam
|
na bhaviṣyanti kaunteya yatte kṛtyaṃ tadācara||38||

39nāradasya vacaḥ śrutvā tadā'śocanta pāṇḍavāḥ
|
evaṃ te sarvamākhyātaṃ devaguhyaṃ sanātanam||39||

40kathaṃ te śokanāśaḥ syātprāṇeṣu ca dayā prabho
|
snehaśca pāṇḍuputreṣu jñātvā daivakṛtaṃ vidhim||40||

41eṣa cārtho mahābāho pūrvameva mayā śrutaḥ
|
kathito dharmarājasya rājasūye kurūttame||41||

42yatitaṃ dharmaputreṇa mayā guhye nivedite
|
avigrahe kauravāṇāṃ daivaṃ tu balavattaram||42||

43anatikramaṇīyo hi vidhī rājankathañcana
|
kṛtāntasya tu bhūtena sthāvareṇa careṇa ca||43||

44bhavāndharmaparo yatra buddhiśreṣṭhaśca bhārata
|
muhyate prāṇināṃ jñātvā gatiṃ cāgatimeva ca||44||

45tvāṃ tu śokena santaptaṃ muhyamānaṃ muhurmuhuḥ
|
jñātvā yudhiṣṭhiro rājā prāṇānapi parityajet||45||

46kṛpālurnityaśo vīrastiryagyonigateṣvapi
|
sa kathaṃ tvayi rājendra kṛpāṃ naiva kariṣyati||46||

47mama caiva niyogena vidheścāpyanivartanām
|
pāṇḍavānāṃ ca kāruṇyātprāṇāndhāraya bhārata||47||

48evaṃ te vartamānasya loke kīrtirbhaviṣyati
|
dharmārthaḥ sumahāṃstāta taptaṃ syācca tapaścirāt||48||

49putraśokaṃ samutpannaṃ hutāśaṃ jvalitaṃ yathā
|
prajñāmbhasā mahārāja nirvāpaya sadā sadā||49||

50vaiśampāyana uvāca|tacchrutvā tasya vacanaṃ vyāsasyāmitatejasaḥ
|
muhūrtaṃ samanudhyāyandhṛtarāṣṭro'bhyabhāṣata||50||

51mahatā śokajālena praṇunno'smi dvijottama
|
nātmānamavabudhyāmi muhyamāno muhurmuhuḥ||51||

52idaṃ tu vacanaṃ śrutvā tava devaniyogajam
|
dhārayiṣyāmyahaṃ prāṇānghaṭiṣye na tu śocitum||52||

53etacchrutvā tu vacanaṃ vyāsaḥ satyavatīsutaḥ
|
dhṛtarāṣṭrasya rājendra tatraivāntaradhīyata||53||
