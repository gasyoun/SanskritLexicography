Mahābhārata 5.33 (Udyogaparvan)

1vaiśampānaya uvāca|
dvāḥsthaṃ prāha mahāprājño dhṛtarāṣṭro mahīpatiḥ
|
viduraṃ draṣṭumicchāmi tamihānaya māciram ||1||

2prahito dhṛtarāṣṭreṇa dūtaḥ kṣattāramabravīt
|
īśvarastvāṃ mahārājo mahāprājña didṛkṣati ||2||

3evamuktastu viduraḥ prāpya rājaniveśanam
|
abravīddhṛtarāṣṭrāya dvāḥsthaṃ māṃ prativedaya ||3||

4dvāḥstha uvāca|
viduro'yamanuprāpto rājendra tava śāsanāt
|
druṣṭumicchati te pādau kiṃ karotu praśādhi mām ||4||

5dhṛtarāṣṭra uvāca|
praveśaya mahāprajñaṃ viduraṃ dīrghadarśinam
|
ahaṃ hi vidurasyāsya nākalpo jātu darśane ||5||

6dvāḥstha uvāca|
praviśāntaḥpuraṃ kṣattarmahārājasya dhīmataḥ
|
na hi te darśane kalpo jātu rājā'bravīddhi mām ||6||

7vaiśampāyana uvāca|
tataḥ praviśya viduro dhṛtarāṣṭraniveśanam
|
abravīt prāñjalirvākyaṃ cintayānaṃ narādhipam ||7||

8viduro'haṃ mahāprājña saṃprāptastava śāsanāt
|
yadi kiñcana kartavyamayamasmi praśādhi mām ||8||

9dhṛtarāṣṭra uvāca|
sañjayo vidura prāpto garhayitvā ca māṃ gataḥ
|
ajātaśatroḥ śvo vākyaṃ sabhāmadhye sa vakṣyati ||9||

10tasyādya kuruvīrasya na vijñātaṃ vaco mayā
|
tanme dahati gātrāṇi tadakārṣīt prajāgaram ||10||

11āgrato dahyamānasya śreyo yadanupaśyasi
|
tadbrūhi tvaṃ hi nastāta dharmārthakuśalo hyasi ||11||

12yataḥ prāptaḥ sañjayaḥ pāṇḍavebhyo na me yathāvanmanasaḥ praśāntiḥ
|
sarvendriyāṇyaprakṛtiṃ gatāni kiṃ vakṣyatītyeva me'dya pracintā ||12||

13vidura uvāca|
abhiyuktaṃ balavatā durbalaṃ hīnasādhanam
|
hṛtasvaṃ kāminaṃ coramāviśanti prajāgarāḥ ||13||

14kaccidetairmahādoṣairna spṛṣṭo'si narādhipa
|
kaccicca paravitteṣu gṛddhyanna paritapyase ||14||

15dhṛtarāṣṭra uvāca|
śrotumicchāmi te dharmyaṃ paraṃ naiśreyasaṃ vacaḥ
|
asmin rājarṣivaṃśe hi tvamekaḥ prājñasaṃmataḥ ||15||

16niṣevate praśastāni ninditāni na sevate
|
anāstikaḥ śraddadhāna etat paṇḍitalakṣaṇam ||16||

17krodho harṣaśca darpaśca hrīḥstambho mānyamānitā
|
yamarthānnāpakarṣanti sa vai paṇḍita ucyate ||17||

18yasya kṛtyaṃ na jānanti mantraṃ vā mantritaṃ pare
|
kṛtamevāsya jānanti sa vai paṇḍita ucyate ||18||

19yasya kṛtyaṃ na vighnanti śītamuṣṇaṃ bhayaṃ ratiḥ
|
samṛddhirasamṛddhirvā sa vai paṇḍita ucyate||19||

20yasya saṃsāriṇī prajñā dharmārthāvanuvartate
|
kāmādarthaṃ vṛṇīte yaḥ sa vai paṇḍita ucyate ||20||

21yathāśakti cikīrṣanti yathāśakti ca kurvate
|
na kiñcidavamanyante narāḥ paṇḍitabuddhayaḥ ||21||

22kṣipraṃ vijānāti ciraṃ śrṛṇoti vijñāya cāryaṃ bhajate na kāmāt
|
nāsaṃpṛṣṭo vyupayuṅkte parārthe tatprajñānaṃ prathamaṃ paṇḍitasya ||22||

23nāprāpyamabhivāñchanti naṣṭaṃ necchanti śocitum
|
āpatsu ca na muhyanti narāḥ paṇḍitabuddhayaḥ ||23||

24niścitya yaḥ prakramate nāntarvasati karmaṇaḥ
|
avandhyakālo vaśyātmā sa vai paṇḍita ucyate||24||

25āryakarmaṇi rajyante bhūtikarmāṇi kurvate
|
hitaṃ ca nābhyasūyanti paṇḍitā bharatarṣabha ||25||

26na hṛṣyatyātmasaṃmāne nāvamānena tapyate
|
gāṅgo hrada ivākṣobhyo yaḥ sa paṇḍita ucyate ||26||

27tattvajñaḥ sarvabhūtānāṃ yogajñaḥ sarvakarmaṇām
|
upāyajño manuṣyāṇāṃ naraḥ paṇḍita ucyate ||27||

28pravṛttavāk citrakatha ūhavān pratibhānavān
|
āśu granthasya vaktā ca yaḥ sa paṇḍita ucyate ||28||

29śrutaṃ prajñānugaṃ yasya prajñā caiva śrutānugā
|
asaṃbhinnāryamaryādaḥ paṇḍitākhyāṃ labheta saḥ ||29||

30aśrutaśca samunnaddho daridraśca mahāmanāḥ
|
arthāṃścākarmaṇā prepsurmūḍha ityucyate budhaiḥ ||30||

31svamarthaṃ yaḥ parityajya parārthamanutiṣṭhati
|
mithyācarati mitrārthe yaśca mūḍhaḥ sa ucyate ||31||

32akāmān kāmayati yaḥ kāmayānān parityajet
|
balavantaṃ ca yo dveṣṭi tamāhurmūḍhacetasam ||32||

33amitraṃ kurute mitraṃ mitraṃ dveṣṭi hinasti ca
|
karma cārabhate duṣṭaṃ tamāhurmūḍhacetasam ||33||

34saṃsārayati kṛtyāni sarvatra vicikitsate
|
ciraṃ karoti kṣiprārthe sa mūḍho bharatarṣabha ||34||

35śrāddhaṃ pitṛbhyo na dadāti daivatāni na cārcati
|
suhṛnmitraṃ na labhate tamāhurmūḍhacetasam ||35||

36anāhūtaḥ praviśati apṛṣṭo bahu bhāṣate
|
aviśvaste viśvasiti mūḍhacetā narādhamaḥ||36||

37paraṃ kṣipati doṣeṇa vartamānaḥ svayaṃ tathā
|
yaśca krudhyatyanīśānaḥ sa ca mūḍhatamo naraḥ ||37||

38ātmano balamājñāya dharmārthaparivarjitam
|
alabhyamicchannaiṣkarmyānmūḍhabuddhirihocyate ||38||

39aśiṣyaṃ śāsti yo rājan yaśca śūnyamupāsate
|
kadaryaṃ bhajate yaśca tamāhurmūḍhacetasam ||39||

40arthaṃ mahāntamāsādya vidyāmaiśvaryameva vā
|
vicaratyasamunnaddho yaḥ sa paṇḍita ucyate||40||

41ekaḥ saṃpannamaśnāti vaste vāsaśca śobhanam
|
yo'saṃvibhajya bhṛtyebhyaḥ ko nṛśaṃsatarastataḥ ||41||

42ekaḥ pāpāni kurute phalaṃ bhuṅkte mahājanaḥ
|
bhoktāro vipramucyante kartā doṣeṇa lipyate ||42||

43ekaṃ hanyānna vā hanyādiṣurmukto dhanuṣmatā
|
buddhirbuddhimatotsṛṣṭā hanyādrāṣṭraṃ sarājakam ||43||

44ekayā dve viniścitya trīṃścaturbhirvaśe kuru
|
pañca jitvā viditvā ṣaṭ sapta hitvā sukhī bhava||44||

45ekaṃ viṣaraso hanti śastreṇaikaśca vadhyate
|
sarāṣṭraṃ saprajaṃ hanti rājānaṃ mantraviplavaḥ ||45||

46ekaḥ svādu na bhuñjīta ekaścārthānna cintayet
|
eko na gacchedadhvānaṃ naikaḥ supteṣu jāgṛyāt ||46||

47ekamevādvitīyaṃ tadyadrājannāvabudhyase
|
satyaṃ svargasya sopānaṃ pārāvārasya nauriva ||47||

48ekaḥ kṣamāvatāṃ doṣo dvitīyo nopapadyate
|
yadenaṃ kṣamayā yuktamaśaktaṃ manyate janaḥ ||48||

49so'sya doṣo na mantavyaḥ kṣamā hi paramaṃ balam
|
kṣamā guṇo hyaśaktānāṃ śaktānāṃ bhūṣaṇaṃ kṣamā ||49||

50kṣamā vaśīkṛtirloke kṣamayā kiṃ na sādhyate
|
śāntikhaḍgaḥ kare yasya kiṃ kariṣyati durjanaḥ ||50||

51atṛṇe patito vahniḥ svayamevopaśāmyati
|
akṣamāvān paraṃ doṣairātmānaṃ caiva yojayet ||51||

52eko dharmaḥ paraṃ śreyaḥ kṣamaikā śāntiruttamā
|
vidyaikā paramā tṛptirahiṃsaikā sukhāvahā ||52||

53dvāvimau grasate bhūmiḥ sarpo balaśayāniva
|
rājānaṃ cāviroddhāraṃ brāhmaṇaṃ cāpravāsinam ||53||

54dve karmaṇī naraḥ kurvannasmi~lloke virocate
|
abruvan paruṣaṃ kiñcidasato'narcayaṃstathā ||54||

55dvāvimau puruṣavyāghra parapratyayakāriṇau
|
striyaḥ kāmitakāminyo lokaḥ pūjitapūjakāḥ ||55||

56dvāvimau kaṇṭakau tīkṣṇau śarīrapariśoṣiṇau
|
yaścādhanaḥ kāmayate yaśca kupyatyanīśvaraḥ ||56||

57dvāveva na virājete viparītena karmaṇā
|
gṛhasthaśca nirārambhaḥ kāryavāṃścaiva bhikṣukaḥ ||57||

58dvāvimau puruṣau rājan svargasyopari tiṣṭhataḥ
|
prabhuśca kṣamayā yukto daridraśca pradānavān ||58||

59nyāyāgatasya dravyasya boddhavyau dvāvatikramau
|
apātre pratipattiśca pātre cāpratipādanam ||59||

60dvāvambhasi niveṣṭavyau gale baddhvā dṛḍhāṃ śilām
|
dhanavantamadātāraṃ daridraṃ cātapasvinam ||60||

61dvāvimau puruṣavyāghra sūryamaṇḍalabhedinau
|
parivrāḍyogayuktaśca raṇe cābhimukho hataḥ ||61||

62trayopāyā manuṣyāṇāṃ śrūyante bharatarṣabha
|
kanīyānmadhyamaḥ śreṣṭha iti vedavido viduḥ ||62||

63trividhāḥ puruṣā rājannuttamādhamamadhyamāḥ
|
niyojayedyathāvattāṃstrividheṣveva karmasu ||63||

64traya evādhanā rājan bhāryā dāsastathā sutaḥ
|
yatte samadhigacchanti yasya te tasya taddhanam ||64||

65haraṇaṃ ca parasvānāṃ paradārābhimarśanam
|
suhṛdaśca parityāgastrayo doṣāḥ kṣayāvahāḥ ||65||

66trividhaṃ narakasyedaṃ dvāraṃ nāśanamātmanaḥ
|
kāmaḥ krodhastathā lobhastasmādetattrayaṃ tyajet ||66||

67varapradānaṃ rājyaṃ ca putrajanma ca bhārata
|
śatrośca mokṣaṇaṃ kṛcchrāttrīṇi caikaṃ ca tatsamam ||67||

68bhaktaṃ ca bhajamānaṃ ca tavāsmīti ca vādinam
|
trīnetāñcharaṇaṃ prāptān viṣame'pi na santyajet ||68||

69catvāri rājñā tu mahābalena varjyānyāhuḥ paṇḍitastāni vidyāt
|
alpaprajñaiḥ saha mantraṃ na kuryānna dīrghasūtrairabhasaiścāraṇaiśca ||69||

70catvāri te tāta gṛhe vasantu śriyā'bhijuṣṭasya gṛhasthadharme
|
vṛddho jñātiravasannaḥ kulīnaḥ sakhā daridro bhaginī cānapatyā ||70||

71catvāryāha mahārāja sādyaskāni bṛhaspatiḥ
|
pṛcchate tridaśendrāya tānīmāni nibodha me ||71||

72devatānāṃ ca saṅkalpamanubhāvaṃ ca dhīmatām
|
vinayaṃ kṛtavidyānāṃ vināśaṃ pāpakarmaṇām ||72||

73catvāri karmāṇyabhayaṃkarāṇi bhayaṃ prayacchantyayathākṛtāni
|
mānāgnihotramuta mānamaunaṃ mānenādhītamuta mānayajñaḥ ||73||

74pañcāgnayo manuṣyeṇa paricaryāḥ prayatnataḥ
|
pitā mātā'gnirātmā ca guruśca bharatarṣabha ||74||

75pañcaiva pūjaya~lloke yaśaḥ prāpnoti kevalam
|
devānpitṝn manuṣyāṃśca bhikṣūnatithipañcamān ||75||

76pañca tvā'nugamiṣyanti yatra yatra gamiṣyasi
|
mitrāṇyamitrā madhyasthā upajīvyopajīvinaḥ||76||

77pañcendriyasya martyasya chidraṃ cedekamindriyam
|
tato'sya sravati prajñā dṛteḥ pātrādivodakam ||77||

78ṣaḍdoṣāḥ puruṣeṇeha hātavyā bhūtimicchatā
|
nidrā tandrī bhayaṃ krodha ālasyaṃ dīrghasūtratā||78||

79ṣaḍimān puruṣo jahyādbhinnāṃ nāvamivārṇave
|
apravaktāramācāryamanadhīyānamṛtvijam ||79||

80arakṣitāraṃ rājānaṃ bhāryāṃ cāpriyavādinīm
|
grāmakāmaṃ ca gopālaṃ vanakāmaṃ ca nāpitam ||80||

81ṣaḍeva tu guṇāḥ puṃsā na hātavyāḥ kadācana
|
satyaṃ dānamanālasyamanasūyā kṣamā dhṛtiḥ ||81||

82arthāgamo nityamarogitā ca priyā ca bhāryā priyavādinī ca
|
vaśyaśca putro'rthakarī ca vidyā ṣaḍjīvalokasya sukhāni rājan ||82||

83ṣaṇṇāmātmani nityānāmaiśvaryaṃ yo'dhigacchati
|
na sa pāpaiḥ kuto'narthairyujyate vijitendriyaḥ ||83||

84ṣaḍime ṣaṭsu jīvanti saptamo nopalabhyate
|
corāḥ pramatte jīvanti vyādhiteṣu cikitsakāḥ ||84||

85pramadāḥ kāmayāneṣu yajamāneṣu yājakāḥ
|
rājā vivadamāneṣu nityaṃ mūrkheṣu paṇḍitāḥ ||85||

86ṣaḍimāni vinaśyanti muhūrtamanavekṣaṇāt
|
gāvaḥ sevā kṛṣirbhāryā vidyā vṛṣalasaṅgatiḥ ||86||

87ṣaḍete hyavamanyante nityaṃ pūrvopakāriṇam
|
ācāryaṃ śikṣitāḥ śiṣyāḥ kṛtadārāśca mātaram ||87||

88nārīṃ vigatakāmāstu kṛtārthāśca prayojakam
|
nāvaṃ nistīrṇakāntārā āturāśca cikitsakam ||88||

89ārogyamānṛṇyamavipravāsaḥ sadbhirmanuṣyaiḥ saha saṃprayogaḥ
|
svapratyayā vṛttirabhītavāsaḥ ṣaṭ jīvalokasya sukhāni rājan ||89||

90īrṣurdhṛṇī nasantuṣṭaḥ krodhano nityaśaṅkitaḥ
|
parabhāgyopajīvī ca ṣaḍete nityaduḥkhitāḥ ||90||

91sapta doṣāḥ sadā rājñā hātavyā vyasanodayāḥ
|
prāyaśo yairvinaśyanti kṛtamūlā apīśvarāḥ||91||

92striyo'kṣā mṛgayā pānaṃ vākpāruṣyaṃ ca pañcamam
|
mahacca daṇḍapāruṣyamarthadūṣaṇameva ca||92||

93aṣṭau pūrvanimittāni narasya vinaśiṣyataḥ
|
brāhmaṇān prathamaṃ dveṣṭi brāhmaṇaiśca virudhyate||93||

94brāhmaṇasvāni cādatte brāhmaṇāṃśca jighāṃsati
|
ramate nindayā caiṣāṃ praśaṃsāṃ nābhinandati ||94||

95nainān smarati kṛtyeṣu yācitaścābhyasūyati
|
etān doṣānnaraḥ prājño buddhyedbudhvā visarjayet ||95||

96aṣṭāvimāni harṣasya navanītāni bhārata
|
vartamānāni dṛśyante tānyeva svasukhānyapi ||96||

97samāgamaśca sakhibhirmahāṃścaiva dhanāgamaḥ
|
putreṇa ca pariṣvaṅgaḥ sannipātaśca maithune ||97||

98samaye ca priyālāpaḥ svayūthyeṣu samunnatiḥ
|
abhipretasya lābhaśca pūjā ca janasaṃsadi ||98||

99aṣṭau gumāḥ puruṣaṃ dīpayanti prajñā ca kaulyaṃ ca damaḥ śrutaṃ ca
|
parākramaścābahubhāṣitā ca dānaṃ yathāśakti kṛtajñatā ca ||99||

100navadvāramidaṃ veśma tristhūṇaṃ pañcasākṣikam
|
kṣetrajñādhiṣṭhitaṃ vidvān yo veda sa paraḥ kaviḥ ||100||

101daśa dharmaṃ na jānanti dhṛtarāṣṭra nibodha tān
|
mattaḥ pramatta unmattaḥ śrāntaḥ kruddho bubhukṣitaḥ||101||

102tvaramāṇaśca lubdhaśca bhītaḥ kāmī ca te daśa
|
tasmādeteṣu bhāveṣu na prasajjeta paṇḍitaḥ||102||

103atraivodāharantīmamitihāsaṃ purātanam
|
putrārthamasurendreṇa gītaṃ caiva sudhanvanā ||103||

104yaḥ kāmamanyū prajahāti rājā pātre pratiṣṭhāpayate dhanaṃ ca
|
viśeṣavicchrutavān kṣiprakārī taṃ sarvalokaḥ kurute pramāṇam ||104||

105jānāti viśvāsayituṃ manuṣyān vijñātadoṣeṣu dadhāti daṇḍam
|
jānāti mātrāṃ ca tathā kṣamāṃ ca taṃ tādṛśaṃ śrīrjuṣate samagrā ||105||

106sudurbalaṃ nāvajānāti kañcidyukto ripuṃ sevate buddhipūrvam
|
na vigrahaṃ rocayate balasthaiḥ kāle ca yo vikramate sa dhīraḥ ||106||

107prāpyāpadaṃ na vyathate kadācidudyogamanvicchati cāpramattaḥ
|
duḥkhaṃ ca kāle sahate mahātmā dhurandharastasya jitāḥ sapatnāḥ ||107||

108anarthakaṃ vipravāsaṃ gṛhebhyaḥ pāpaiḥ sandhiṃ paradārābhimarśam
|
dambhaṃ stainyaṃ paiśunaṃ madyapānaṃ na sevate yaḥ sa sukhī sadaiva ||108||

109na saṃrambheṇārabhate trivargamākāritaḥ śaṃsati tattvameva
|
na mitrārthe rocayate vivādaṃ nāpūjitaḥ kupyati cāpyamūḍhaḥ ||109||

110na yo'bhyasūyatyanukampate ca na durbalaḥ prātibhāvyaṃ karoti
|
nātyāha kiñcit kṣamate vivādaṃ sarvatra tādṛglabhate praśaṃsām||110||

111yo noddhataṃ kurute jātu veṣaṃ na pauruṣeṇāpi vikatthate'nyān
|
na mūrcchitaḥ kaṭukānyāha kiñcit priyaṃ sadā taṃ kurute jano hi ||111||

112na vairamuddīpayati praśāntaṃ na darpamārohati nāstimeti
|
na durgato'smīti karotyakāryaṃ tamāryaśīlaṃ paramāhurāryāḥ ||112||

113na sve sukhe vai kurute praharṣaṃ nānyasya duḥkhe bhavati prahṛṣṭaḥ
|
dattvā na paścāt kurute na tāpaṃ sa kathyate satpuruṣāryaśīlaḥ ||113||

114deśācārān samayān jātidharmān bubhūṣate yaḥ sa parāvarajñaḥ
|
sa yatra tatrābhigataḥ sadaiva mahājanasyādhipatyaṃ karoti ||114||

115dambhaṃ mohaṃ matsaraṃ pāpakṛtyaṃ rājadviṣṭaṃ paiśunaṃ pūgavairam
|
mattonmattairdurjanaiścāpi vādaṃ yaḥ prajñāvān varjayet sa pradhānaḥ ||115||

116dānaṃ mohaṃ daivataṃ maṅgalāni prāyaścittān vividhā~llokavādān
|
etāni yaḥ kurute naityakāni tasyotthānaṃ devatā rādhayanti ||116||

117samairvivāhaṃ kurute na hīnaiḥ samaiḥ sakhyaṃ vyavahāraṃ kathāṃ ca
|
guṇairviśiṣṭāṃśca puro dadhāti vipaścitastasya nayāḥ sunītāḥ ||117||

118mitaṃ bhuṅkte saṃvibhajyāśritebhyo mitaṃ svapityamitaṃ karma kṛtvā
|
dadātyamitreṣvapi yācitaḥ saṃstamātmavantaṃ prajahatyanarthāḥ ||118||

119cikīrṣitaṃ viprakṛtaṃ ca yasya nānye janāḥ karma jānanti kiñcit
|
mantre gupte samyaganuṣṭhite ca nālpo'pyasya cyavate kaścidarthaḥ ||119||

120yaḥ sarvabhūtapraśame niviṣṭaḥ satyo mṛdurmānakṛcchuddhabhāvaḥ
|
atīva sa jñāyate jñātimadhye mahāmaṇirjātya iva saprannaḥ ||120||
