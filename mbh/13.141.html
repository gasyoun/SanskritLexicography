Mahābhārata 13.141 (Anuśāsanaparvan)

1śrībhagavānuvāca
|
tilottamā nāma purā brahmaṇā yoṣiduttamā
|
tilaṃ tilaṃ samuddhṛtya ratnānāṃ nirmitā śubhā ||1||

2sā'bhyagacchata māṃ devi rūpeṇāpratimā bhuvi
|
pradakṣiṇaṃ lobhayantī māṃ śubhe rucirānanā ||2||

3yatā yataḥ sā sudatī māmupādhāvadantike
|
tatastatāṃ mukhaṃ cāru mama devi vinirgatam ||3||

4tāṃ didṛkṣurahaṃ yogāccaturmūrtitvamāgataḥ
|
caturmukhaśca saṃvṛtto darśayanyogamuttamam ||4||

5pūrveṇa vadanenāhamindratvamanuśāsmi ha
|
uttareṇa tvayā sārdhaṃ ramāmyahamanindite ||5||

6paścimaṃ me mukhaṃ saumyaṃ sarvaprāṇisukhāvaham
|
dakṣiṇaṃ bhīmasaṃkāśaṃ raudraṃ saṃharati prajāḥ ||6||

7jaṭilo brahmacārī ca lokānāṃ hitakāmyayā
|
devakāryārthasiddhyarthaṃ pinākaṃ me kare sthitam ||7||

8indreṇa ca purā vajraṃ kṣiptaṃ śrīkāṃkṣiṇā mama
|
dagdhvā kaṇṭhaṃ tu tadyātaṃ tena śrīkaṇṭhatā mama ||8||

9umovāca
|
vāhaneṣvatra sarveṣu śrīmatsvanyeṣu sattama
|
kathaṃ ca vṛṣabho deva vāhanatvamupāgataḥ ||9||

10maheśvara uvāca|
surabhīmasṛjadbrahmā devadhenuṃ payomucam
|
sā sṛṣṭā bahudhā jātā kṣaramāṇā payo'mṛtam ||10||

11tasyā vatsamukhotsṛṣṭaḥ pheno madgātramāgataḥ
|
tato dagdhā mayā gāvo nānāvarṇatvamāgatāḥ ||11||

12tato'haṃ lokaguruṇā śamaṃ nīto'rthavedinā
|
vṛṣaṃ cainaṃ dhvajārthaṃ me dadau vāhanameva ca ||12||

13umovāca
|
nivāsā bahurūpāste divi sarvaguṇānvitāḥ
|
tāṃśca saṃtyajya bhagavan śmaśāne ramase katham ||13||

14keśāsthikalile bhīme kapālaghaṭasaṃkule
|
gṛdhragomāyubahule citāgniśatasaṃkule ||14||

15aśucau māṃsakalile vasāśoṇitakardame
|
vikīrṇāntrāsthinicaye śivānādavinādite ||15||

16maheśvara uvāca|
medhyānveṣī mahīṃ kṛtsnāṃ vicarāmyaniśaṃ sadā
|
na ca medhyataraṃ kiñcit śmaśānādiha lakṣyate ||16||

17tena me sarvavāsānāṃ śmaśāne ramate manaḥ
|
nyagrodhaśākhāsaṃchanne nirbhugnasragvibhūṣite ||17||

18tatra caiva ramantīme bhūtasaṃghāḥ śucismite
|
na ca bhūtagaṇairdevi vinā'haṃ vastumutsahe ||18||

19eṣa vāso hi me medhyaḥ svargīyaśca mataḥ śubhe
|
puṇyaḥ paramakaścaiva medhyakāmairupāsyate ||19||

20umovāca
|
bhagavan sarvabhūteśa sarvadharmavidāṃ vara
|
pinākapāṇe varada saṃśayo me mahānayam ||20||

21ayaṃ munigaṇaḥ sarvastapastepa iti prabho
|
tapoveṣakaro loke bhramate vividhākṛtiḥ ||21||

22asya caivarṣisaṃghasya mama ca priyakāmyayā
|
etaṃ mameha sandehaṃ vaktumarhasyarindama ||22||

23dharmaḥ kiṃlakṣaṇaḥ proktaḥ kathaṃ vā carituṃ naraiḥ
|
śakyo dharmamavindadbhirdharmajña vada me prabho ||23||

24nārada uvāca|
tato munigaṇaḥ sarvastāṃ devīṃ pratyapūjayat
|
vāgbhirṛgbhūṣitārthābhiḥ stavaiścārthaviśāradaiḥ ||24||

25maheśvara uvāca|
ahiṃsā satyavacanaṃ sarvabhūtānukaṃpanam
|
śamo dānaṃ yathāśakti gārhasthyo dharma uttamaḥ ||25||

26paradāreṣvasaṃsargo nyāsastrīparirakṣaṇam
|
adattādānaviramo madhumāṃsasya varjanam ||26||

27eṣa pañcavidho dharmo bahuśākhaḥ sukhodayaḥ
|
dehibhirdharmaparamaiścartavyo dharmasaṃbhavaḥ ||27||

28umovāca
|
bhagavan saṃśayaḥ pṛṣṭastanme śaṃsitumarhasi
|
cāturvarṇyasya yo dharmaḥ sve sve varṇe guṇāvahaḥ ||28||

29brāhmaṇe kīdṛśo dharmaḥ kṣatriye kīdṛśo'bhavat
|
vaiśye kiṃlakṣaṇo dharmaḥ śūdre kiṃlakṣaṇo bhavet ||29||

30maheśvara uvāca|
nyāyataste mahābhāge sarvaśaḥ samudīritaḥ
|
bhūmidevā mahābhāgāḥ sadā loke dvijātayaḥ ||30||

31upavāsaḥ sadā dharmo brāhmaṇasya na saṃśayaḥ
|
sa hi dharmārthasaṃpanno brahmabhūyāya kalpate ||31||

32tasya dharmakriyā devi brahmacaryā ca nyāyataḥ
|
vratopanayanaṃ caiva dvijo yenopapadyate ||32||

33gurudaivatapūjārthaṃ svādhyāyābhyasanātmakaḥ
|
dehibhirdharmaparamaiścartavyo dharmasaṃbhavaḥ ||33||

34umovāca
|
bhagavan saṃśayo me'sti tanme vyākhyātumarhasi
|
cāturvarṇyasya dharmaṃ vai naipuṇyena prakīrtaya ||34||

35maheśvara uvāca|
rahasyaśravaṇaṃ dharmo vedavrataniṣevaṇam
|
agnikāryaṃ tathā dharmo gurukāryaprasādhanam ||35||

36bhaikṣacaryā paro dharmo nityayajñopavītitā
|
nityaṃ svādhyāyitā dharmo brahmacaryāśramastathā ||36||

37guruṇā cābhyanujñātaḥ samāvarteta vai dvijaḥ
|
vindetānantaraṃ bhāryāmanurūpāṃ yathāvidhi ||37||

38śūdrānnavarjanaṃ dharmastathā satpathasevanam
|
dharmo nityopavāsitvaṃ brahmacaya tathaiva ca ||38||

39āhitāgniradhīyāno juhvānaḥ saṃyatendriyaḥ
|
vighasāśī yatāhāro gṛhasthaḥ satyavāk‌śuciḥ ||39||

40atithivratatā dharmo dharmastretāgnidhāraṇam
|
iṣṭīśca paśubandhāṃśca vidhipūrvaṃ samācaret ||40||

41yajñaśca paramo dharmastathā'hiṃsā ca dehiṣu
|
apūrvabhojanaṃ dharmo vighasāśitvameva ca ||41||

42bhuṅkte parijane paścādbhojanaṃ dharma ucyate
|
brāhmaṇasya gṛhasthasya śrotriyasya viśeṣataḥ ||42||

43dampatyoḥ samaśīlatvaṃ dharmaḥ syādgṛhamedhinaḥ
|
gṛhyāṇāṃ caiva devānāṃ nityapuṣpabalikriyā ||43||

44nityopalepanaṃ dharmastathā nityopavāsitā
|
susaṃmṛṣṭopalipte ca sājyadhūmo bhavedgṛhe ||44||

45eṣa dvijajane dharmo gārhastho lokadhāraṇaḥ
|
dvijānāṃ ca satāṃ nityaṃ sadaivaiṣa pravartate ||45||

46yastu kṣatragato devi mayā dharma udīritaḥ
|
tamahaṃ te pravakṣyāmi tanme śṛṇu samāhitā ||46||

47kṣatriyasya smṛto dharmaḥ prajāpālanamāditaḥ
|
nirdiṣṭaphalabhoktā hi rājā dharmeṇa yujyate ||47||

48prajāḥ pālayate yo hi dharmeṇa manujādhipaḥ
|
tasya dharmārjitā lokāḥ prajāpālanasaṃcitāḥ ||48||

49tasya rājñaḥ paro dharmo damaḥ svādhyāya eva ca
|
agnihotraparispando dānādhyayanameva ca ||49||

50yajñopavītadharaṇaṃ yajño dharmakriyāstathā
|
bhṛtyānāṃ bharaṇaṃ dharmaḥ kṛte karmaṇyamoghatā ||50||

51samyagdaṇḍe sthitirdharmo dharmo vedakratukriyāḥ
|
vyavahārasthitirdharmaḥ satyavākyaratistathā ||51||

52ārtahastaprado rājā pretya ceha mahīyate
|
gobrāhmaṇārthe vikrāntaḥ saṃgrāme nidhanaṃ gataḥ ||52||

53aśvamedhajitā~llokānāpnoti tridivālaye ||53||

54vaiśyasya satataṃ dharmaḥ pāśupālyaṃ kṛṣistathā
|
agnihotraparispando dānādhyayanameva ca ||54||

55vāṇijyaṃ satpathasthānamātithyaṃ praśamo damaḥ
|
viprāṇāṃ svāgataṃ tyāgo vaiśyadharmaḥ sanātanaḥ ||55||

56tilān gandhān rasāṃścaiva vikrīṇīyānna caiva hi
|
vaṇikpathamupāsīno vaiśyaḥ satpathamāśritaḥ ||56||

57sarvātithyaṃ trivargasya yathāśakti yathārhataḥ
|
śūdradharmaḥ paro nityaṃ śuśrūṣā ca dvijātiṣu ||57||

58sa śūdraḥ saṃśitatapāḥ satyavādī jitendriyaḥ
|
śuśrūṣuratithiṃ prāptaṃ tapaḥ saṃcinute mahat ||58||

59nityaṃ sa hi śubhācāro devatādvijapūjakaḥ
|
śūdro dharmaphalairiṣṭaiḥ saṃprayujyeta buddhimān ||59||

60etatte sarvamākhyātaṃ cāturvarṇyasya śobhane
|
ekaikasyeha subhage kimanyacchrotumicchasi ||60||

61umovāca
|
uktastvayā pṛthagdharmaścāturvarṇyahitaḥ śubhaḥ
|
sarvavyāpī tu yo dharmo bhagavaṃstadbravīhi me ||61||

62maheśvara uvāca|
brāhmaṇā lokasāreṇa sṛṣṭā dhātrā guṇārthinā
|
lokāṃstārayituṃ kṛtsnānmartyeṣu kṣitidevatāḥ ||62||

63teṣāmapi pravakṣyāmi dharmakarmaphalodayam
|
brāhmaṇeṣu hi yo dharmaḥ sa dharmaḥ paramo mataḥ ||63||

64ime te lokadharmārthaṃ trayaḥ sṛṣṭāḥ svayambhuvā
|
pṛthivyāṃ sarjane nityaṃ sṛṣṭāṃstānapi me śṛṇu ||64||

65vedoktaḥ paramo dharmaḥ smṛtiśāstragato'paraḥ
|
śiṣṭācīrṇo paraḥ proktastrayo dharmāḥ sanātanāḥ ||65||

66traividyo brāhmaṇo vidvānna cādhyayanajīvakaḥ
|
trikarmā triparikrānto maitra eṣa smṛto dvijaḥ ||66||

67ṣaḍimāni tu karmāṇi provāca bhuvaneśvaraḥ
|
vṛttyarthaṃ brāhmaṇānāṃ vai śṛṇu dharmānsanātanān ||67||

68yajanaṃ yājanaṃ caiva tathā dānapratigrahau
|
adhyāpanaṃ cādhyayanaṃ ṣaṭkarmā dharmabhāgdvijaḥ ||68||

69nityaḥ svādhyāyitā dharmo dharmo yajñaḥ sanātanaḥ
|
dānaṃ praśasyate cāsya yathāśakti yathāvidhi ||69||

70śamastūparamo dharmaḥ pravṛttaḥ satsu nityaśaḥ
|
gṛhasthānāṃ viśuddhānāṃ dharmasya nicayo mahān ||70||

71pañcayajñaviśuddhātmā satyavāganasūyakaḥ
|
dātā brāhmaṇasatkartā susaṃsṛṣṭaniveśanaḥ ||71||

72amānī ca sadā'jihmaḥ snigdhavāṇīpradastathā
|
atithyabhyāgataratiḥ śeṣānnakṛtabhojanaḥ ||72||

73pādyamarghyaṃ yathānyāyamāsanaṃ śayanaṃ tathā
|
dīpaṃ pratiśrayaṃ caiva yo dadāti sa dhārmikaḥ ||73||

74prātarutthāya cācamya bhojanenopamantrya ca
|
satkṛtyānuvajedyastu tasya dharmaḥ sanātanaḥ ||74||

75sarvātithyaṃ trivargasya yathāśakti niśāniśam
|
śūdradharmaḥ samākhyātastrivargaparicāraṇam ||75||

76pravṛttilakṣaṇo dharmo gṛhastheṣu vidhīyate
|
tamahaṃ vartayiṣyāmi sarvabhūtahitaṃ śubham ||76||

77dātavyamasakṛcchaktyā yaṣṭavyamasakṛttathā
|
puṣṭikarmavidhānaṃ ca kartavyaṃ bhūtimicchatā ||77||

78dharmeṇārthaḥ samāhāryo dharmalabdhaṃ tridhā dhanam
|
kartavyaṃ dharmaparamaṃ mānavena prayatnataḥ ||78||

79ekenāṃśena dharmārthau kartavyau bhūtimicchatā
|
ekenāṃśena kāmārtha ekamaṃśaṃ vivardhayet ||79||

80nivṛttilakṣaṇastvanyo dharmo mokṣāya tiṣṭhati
|
tasya vṛttiṃ pravakṣyāmi śṛṇu me devi tattvataḥ ||80||

81sarvabhūtadyā dharmo na caikagrāmavāsitā
|
āśāpāśavimokṣaśca śasyate mokṣakāṃkṣiṇām ||81||

82na kuṭyāṃ nodake saṅgo na vāsasi na cāsane
|
na tridaṇḍe na śayane nāgnau na śaraṇālaye ||82||

83adhyātmagatticitto yastanmanāstatparāyaṇaḥ
|
yukto yogaṃ prati sadā pratisaṃkhyānameva ca ||83||

84vṛkṣamūlaparo nityaṃ śūnyāgāraniveśanaḥ
|
nadīpulinaśāyī ca nadītīraratiśca yaḥ ||84||

85vimuktaḥ sarvasaṅgeṣu snehabandheṣu ca dvijaḥ
|
ātmanyevātmano bhāvaṃ samāsajjeta vai dvijaḥ ||85||

86sthāṇubhūto nirāhāro mokṣadṛṣṭena karmaṇā
|
parivrajati yo yuktastasya dharmaḥ sanātanaḥ ||86||

87na caikatra samāsakto na caikagrāmagocaraḥ
|
mukto hyaṭati nirmukto na caikapulineśayaḥ ||87||

88eṣa mokṣavidāṃ dharmo vedoktaḥ satpathaḥ satām
|
yo mārgamanuyātīmaṃ padaṃ tasya ca vidyate ||88||

89caturvidhā bhikṣavaste kuṭīcakabahūda‌kau
|
haṃsaḥ paramahaṃsaśca yo yaḥ paścātsa uttamaḥ ||89||

90ataḥ parataraṃ nāsti nāvaraṃ na tirogrataḥ
|
aduḥkhamasukhaṃ saumyamajarāmaramadhyayam ||90||

91umovāca
|
gārhasthyo mokṣadharmaśca sajjanācaritastvayā
|
bhāṣito jīvalokasya mārgaḥ śreyaskaro mahān ||91||

92ṛṣidharmaṃ tu dharmajña śrotumicchāmyataḥ param
|
spṛhā bhavati me nityaṃ tapovananivāsiṣu ||92||

93ājyadhūmodbhavo gandho ruṇaddhīva tapovanam
|
taṃ dṛṣṭvā me manaḥ prītaṃ maheśvara sadā bhavet ||93||

94etanme saṃśayaṃ deva sunidharmakṛtaṃ vibho
|
sarvadharmārthatattvajña devadeva vadasva me
|
nikhilena mayā pṛṣṭaṃ mahādeva yathātatham ||94||

95śrībhagavānuvāca|
hanta te'haṃ pravakṣyāmi munidharmamanuttamam
|
yaṃ kṛtvā munayo yānti siddhiṃ svatapasā śubhe ||95||

96phenapānāmṛṣīṇāṃ yo dharmo dharmavidāṃ satām
|
tanme śṛṇu mahābhāge dharmajñe dharmamāditaḥ ||96||

97uṃcchanti satataṃ ye te brāhmyaṃ phenotkaraṃ śubham
|
amṛtaṃ brahmaṇā pītamadhvare prasṛtaṃ divi ||97||

98eṣa teṣāṃ viśuddhānāṃ phenapānāṃ tapodhane
|
dharmacaryākṛto mārgo vālakhilyagaṇaiḥ śṛṇu ||98||

99vālakhilyāstapaḥ siddhā munayaḥ sūryamaṇḍale
|
uṃcche tiṣṭhanti dharmajñāḥ śākunīṃ vṛttimāsthitāḥ ||99||

100mṛganimarmokavasanāścīravalkalavāsasaḥ
|
nirdvandvāḥ satpathaṃ prāptā vālakhilyāstapodhanāḥ ||100||

101aṅ‌guṣṭhaparvamātrā ye bhūtvā sve sve vyavasthitāḥ
|
tapaścaraṇamīhante teṣāṃ dharmaphalaṃ mahat ||101||

102te suraiḥ samatāṃ yānti surakāryārthasiddhaye
|
dyotayanti diśaḥ sarvāstapasā dagdhakilviṣāḥ ||102||

103ye tvanye śuddhamanaso dayādharmaparāyaṇāḥ
|
santaścakracarāḥ puṇyāḥ somalokacarāśca ye ||103||

104pitṛlokasamīpasthāsta uṃcchanti yathāvidhi
|
saṃprakṣālāśmakuṭṭāśca dantolūkhalikāśca te ||104||

105somapānāṃ ca devānāmūṣmapāṇāṃ tathaiva ca
|
uṃcchanti ye samīpasthāḥ sadārā niyatendriyāḥ||105||

106teṣāmagniparisyandaḥ pitṝṇāṃ cārcanaṃ tathā
|
yajñānāṃ caiva pañcānāṃ yajanaṃ dharma ucyate ||106||

107eṣa cakracarairdevi devalokacarairdvijaiḥ
|
ṛṣidharmaḥ sadā cīrṇo yo'nyastamapi me śṛṇu ||107||

108sarveṣvevarṣidharmeṣu jñeyo''tmā saṃyatendriyaiḥ
|
kāmakrodhau tataḥ paścājjetavyāviti me matiḥ ||108||

109agnihotraparispando dharmarātrisamāsanam
|
somayajñābhyanujñānaṃ pañcamī yajñadakṣiṇā||109||

110nityaṃ yajñakriyā dharmaḥ pitṛdevārcane ratiḥ
|
sarvātithyaṃ ca kartavyamannenoṃcchārjitena vai ||110||

111nivṛttirupabhogeṣu gorasānāṃ śame ratiḥ
|
sthaṃḍile śayane yogaḥ śākaparṇaniṣevaṇam ||111||

112phalamūlāśanaṃ vāyurāpaḥ śaivalabhakṣaṇam
|
ṛṣīṇāṃ niyamā hyete yairjayantyajitāṃ gatim ||112||

113vidhūme sannamusale vyaṅgāre bhuktavajjane
|
atītapātrasañcāre kāle vigatabhikṣuke ||113||

114atithiṃ kāṃkṣamāṇo vai śeṣānnakṛtabhojanaḥ
|
satyadharmarataḥ śānto munidharmeṇa yujyate ||114||

115na stambhī na ca mānī syānnāprasanno na vismitaḥ
|
mitrāmitrasamo maitro yaḥ sa dharmaviduttamaḥ ||115||
