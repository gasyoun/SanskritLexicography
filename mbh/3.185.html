Mahābhārata 3.185 (Āraṇyakaparvan)

1mārkaṇḍeya uvāca|
bhūya eva mahābhāgyaṃ brāhmaṇānāṃ nibodha me
|
vainyo nāmeha rājarṣiraśvamedhāya dīkṣitaḥ||1||

2tamatrirgantumārebhe vittārthamiti naḥ śrutam
|
bhūyo'rthaṃ nānurudhyat sa dharmavyaktinidarśanāt||2||

3sa vicintya mahātejā vanamevānvarocayat
|
dharmapatnīṃ samāhūya putrāṃścedamuvāca ha||3||

4prāpsyāmaḥ phalamatyantaṃ bahulaṃ nirupadravam
|
araṇyagamanaṃ kṣipraṃ rocatāṃ vo guṇādhikam||4||

5taṃ bhāryā pratyuvācātha dharmamevānutanvatī
|
vainyaṃ gatvā mahātmānamarthayasva dhanaṃ bahu||5||

6sa te dāsyati rājarṣiryajamāno'rthito dhanam
|
tata ādāya viprarṣe pratigṛhya dhanaṃ bahu||6||

7bhṛtyān sutān saṃvibhajya tato vraja yathepsitam
|
eṣa vai paramo dharmo dharmavidbhirudāhṛtaḥ||7||

8atriruvāca|
kathito me mahābhāge gautamena mahātmanā
|
vainyo dharmārthasaṃyuktaḥ satyavratasamanvitaḥ||8||

9kintvasti tatra dveṣṭāro vivasanti hi me dvijāḥ
|
yathā me gautamaḥ prāha tato na vyavasāmyaham||9||

10tatra sma vācaṃ kalyāṇīṃ dharmakāmārthasaṃhitām
|
mayoktāmanyathā brūyustataste vai nirarthikām||10||

11gamiṣyāmi mahāprājñe rocate me vacastava
|
gāśca me dāsyate vainyaḥ prabhūtaṃ cārthasañcayam||11||

12evamuktvā jagāmāśu vainyayajñaṃ mahātapāḥ
|
gatvā ca yajñāyatanamatristuṣṭāva taṃ nṛpam||12||

13vākyairmaṅgalasaṃyuktaiḥ pūjayāno'bravīdvacaḥ
|
atriruvāca|
rājan dhanyastvamīśaśca bhuvi tvaṃ prathamo nṛpaḥ||13||

14stuvanti tvāṃ munigaṇāstvadanyo nāsti dharmavit
|
tamabravīdṛṣiḥ kruddho vacanaṃ vai mahātapāḥ||14||

15gautama uvāca|
maivamatre punarbrūyā na te prajñā samāhitā
|
atra naḥ prathamaṃ sthātā mahendro vai prajāpatiḥ||15||

16athātrirapi rājendra gautamaṃ pratyabhāṣata
|
ayameva vidhātā hi yathaivendraḥ prajāpatiḥ
|
tvameva muhyase mohānna prajñānaṃ tavāsti ha||16||

17gautama uvāca|
jānāmi nāhaṃ muhyāmi tvamevātra vimuhyase
|
stauṣi tvaṃ darśanaprepsū rājānaṃ janasaṃsadi||17||

18na vettha paramaṃ dharmaṃ na cāvaiṣi prayojanam
|
bālastvamasi mūḍhaśca vṛddhaḥ kenāpi hetunā||18||

19vivadantau tathā tau tu munīnāṃ darśane sthitau
|
ye tasya yajñe saṃvṛttāste'pṛcchanta kathaṃ tvimau||19||

20praveśaḥ kena datto'yamubhayorvainyasaṃsadi
|
uccaiḥ samabhibhāṣantau kena kāryeṇa dhiṣṭhitau||20||

21tataḥ paramadharmātmā kāśyapaḥ sarvadharmavit
|
vivādināvanuprāptau tāvubhau pratyavedayat||21||

22athābravīt sadasyāṃstu gautamo munisattamān
|
āvayorvyāhṛtaṃ praśnaṃ śṛṇuta dvijasattamāḥ||22||

23vainyaṃ vidhātetyāhātriratra nau saṃśayo mahān
|
śrutvaiva tu mahātmāno munayo'bhyadravan drutam||23||

24sanatkumāraṃ dharmajñaṃ saṃśayacchedanāya vai
|
sa ca teṣāṃ vacaḥ śrutvā yathātattvaṃ mahātapāḥ
|
pratyuvācātha tānevaṃ dharmārthasahitaṃ vacaḥ||24||

25sanatkumāra uvāca|
brahma kṣatreṇa sahitaṃ kṣatraṃ ca brahmaṇā saha
|
saṃyuktau dahataḥ śatrūn vanānīvāgnimārutau||25||

26rājā vai prathito dharmaḥ prajānāṃ patireva ca
|
sa eva śakraḥ śukraśca sa dhātā ca bṛhaspatiḥ||26||

27prajāpatirvirāṭ samrāṭ kṣatriyo bhūpatirnupaḥ
|
ya ebhiḥ stūyate śabdaiḥ kastaṃ nārcitumarhati||27||

28purāyoniryudhājicca abhiyā mudito bhavaḥ
|
svarṇetā sahajidbabhruriti rājā'bhidhīyate||28||

29satyayoniḥ purāvicca satyadharmapravartakaḥ
|
adharmādṛṣayo bhītā balaṃ kṣatre samādadhan||29||

30ādityo divi deveṣu tamo nudati tejasā
|
tathaiva nṛpatirbhūmāvadharmānnudate bhṛśam||30||

31tato rājñaḥ pradhānatvaṃ śāstraprāmāṇyadarśanāt
|
uttaraḥ siddhyate pakṣo yena rājeti bhāṣitam||31||

32mārkaṇḍeya uvāca|
tataḥ sa rājā saṃhṛṣṭaḥ siddhe pakṣe mahāmanāḥ
|
tamatrimabravīt prītaḥ pūrvaṃ yenābhisaṃstutaḥ||32||

33yasmāt pūrvaṃ manuṣyeṣu jyāyāṃsaṃ māmihābravīḥ
|
sarvadevaiśca viprarṣe sammitaṃ śreṣṭhameva ca||33||

34tasmātte'haṃ pradāsyāmi vividhaṃ vasu bhūri ca
|
dāsīsahasraṃ śyāmānāṃ suvastrāṇāmalaṅkṛtam||34||

35daśakoṭīrhiraṇyasya rukmabhārāṃstathā daśa
|
etaddadāmi viprarṣe sarvajñastvaṃ mato hi me||35||

36tadatrirnyāyataḥ sarvaṃ pratigṛhyābhisatkṛtaḥ
|
pratyujjagāma tejasvī gṛhāneva mahātapāḥ||36||

37pradāya ca dhanaṃ prītaḥ putrebhyaḥ prayatātmavān
|
tapaḥ samabhisandhāya vanamevānvapadyata||37||
