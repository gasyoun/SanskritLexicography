Mahābhārata 7.137 (Droṇaparvan)

1sañjaya uvāca|
bhīmasenasya rādheyaḥ śrutvā jyātalaniḥsvanaṃ
|
nāmṛṣyata yathā matto gajaḥ pratigajasvanam ||1||

2so'pakramya muhūrtaṃ tu bhīmasenasya gocarāt
|
putrāṃstava dadarśātha bhīmasenena pātitān ||2||

3tānavekṣya naraśreṣṭha vimanā duḥkhitastadā
|
niḥśvasandīrghamuṣṇaṃ ca punaḥ pāṇḍavamabhyayāt ||3||

4sa tāmranayanaḥ krodhācchvasanniva mahoragaḥ
|
babhau karṇaḥ śarānasyan raśmīniva divākaraḥ ||4||

5kiraṇairiva sūryasya mahīdhro bharatarṣabha
|
karṇacāpacyutairbāṇaiḥ prācchādyata vṛkodaraḥ ||5||

6te karṇacāpaprabhavāḥ śarā barhiṇavāsasaḥ
|
viviśuḥ sarvataḥ pārthaṃ vāsāyevāṇḍajā drumam ||6||

7karṇacāpacyutā bāṇāḥ saṃpatantastatastataḥ
|
rukmapuṅkhā vyarājanta haṃsāḥ śreṇīkṛtā iva ||7||

8cāpadhvajopaskarebhyaśchatrādīṣāmukhādyugāt
|
prabhavanto vyadṛśyanta rājannadhiratheḥ śarāḥ ||8||

9khaṃ pūrayanmahāvegāt khagamān gṛdhravāsasaḥ
|
suvarṇavikṛtāṃścitrānmucocādhirathiḥ śarān ||9||

10tamantakamivāyastamāpatantaṃ vṛkodaram
|
tyaktvā prāṇānatikramya vivyādha niśitaiḥ śaraiḥ ||10||

11tasya vegamasahyaṃ sa dṛṣṭvā karṇasya pāṇḍavaḥ
|
mahataśca śaraughāṃstānnyavārayata vīryavān ||11||

12tato vidhamyādhiratheḥ śarajālāni pāṇḍavaḥ
|
vivyādha karṇaṃ viṃśatyā punaranyaiḥ śilāśitaiḥ ||12||

13yathaiva hi sa karṇena pārthaḥ pracchāditaḥ śaraiḥ
|
tathaiva sa raṇe karṇaṃ chādayāmāsa pāṇḍavaḥ ||13||

14dṛṣṭvā tu bhīmasenasya vikramaṃ yudhi bhārata
|
abhyanandaṃstvadīyāśca saṃprahṛṣṭāśca cāraṇāḥ ||14||

15bhūriśravāḥ kṛpo drauṇirmadrarājo jayadrathaḥ
|
uttamaujā yudhāmanyuḥ sātyakiḥ keśavārjunau ||15||

16kurupāṇḍavapravarā daśa rājanmahārathāḥ
|
sādhu sādhviti vegena siṃhanādamathānadan ||16||

17tasminsamutthite śabde tumule lomaharṣaṇe
|
abhyabhāṣata putraste rājanduryodhanastvaran ||17||

18rājñaḥ saṃrājaputrāṃśca sodaryāṃśca viśeṣataḥ
|
karṇaṃ gacchata bhadraṃ vaḥ parīpsanto vṛkodarāt ||18||

19purā nighnanti rādheyaṃ bhīmacāpacyutāḥ śarāḥ
|
te yatadhvaṃ maheṣvāsāḥ sūtaputrasya rakṣaṇe ||19||

20duryodhanasamādiṣṭāḥ sodaryāḥ sapta bhārata
|
bhīmasenamabhidrutya saṃrabdhāḥ paryavārayan ||20||

21te samāsādya kaunteyamāvṛṇvañśaravṛṣṭibhiḥ
|
parvataṃ vāridhārābhiḥ prāvṛṣīva balāhakāḥ ||21||

22te'pīḍayan bhīmasenaṃ kruddhāḥ sapta mahārathāḥ
|
prajāsaṃharaṇe rājansomaṃ sapta grahā iva ||22||

23tato vegena kaunteyaḥ pīḍayitvā śarāsanam
|
muṣṭinā pāṇḍavo rājan dṛḍhena supariṣkṛtam ||23||

24manuṣyasamatāṃ jñātvā sapta saṃdhāya sāyakān
|
tebhyo vyasṛjadāyastaḥ sūryaraśminibhānprabhuḥ ||24||

25nirasyanniva dehebhyastanayānāmasūṃstava
|
bhīmaseno mahārāja pūrvavairamanusmaran ||25||

26te kṣiptā bhīmasenena śarā bhārata bhāratān
|
vidārya khaṃ samutpetuḥ svarṇapuṃkhāḥ śilāśitāḥ ||26||

27teṣāṃ vidārya cetāṃsi śarā hemavibhūṣitāḥ
|
vyarājanta mahārāja suparṇā iva khecarāḥ ||27||

28śoṇitā digdhavājāgrāḥ sapta hemapariṣkṛtāḥ
|
putrāṇāṃ tava rājendra pītvā śoṇitamudgatāḥ||28||

29te śarairbhinnamarmāṇo rathebhyaḥ prāpatan kṣitau
|
girisānuruhā bhagnā dvipeneva mahādrumāḥ ||29||

30śatruñjayaḥ śatrusahaścitraścitrāyudho dṛḍhaḥ
|
citraseno vikarṇaśca saptaite vinipātitāḥ ||30||

31putrāṇāṃ tava sarveṣāṃ nihatānāṃ vṛkodaraḥ
|
śocatyatibhṛṃśaṃ duḥkhādvikarṇaṃ pāṇḍavaḥ priyam ||31||

32pratijñeyaṃ mayā vṛttā nihantavyāstu saṃyuge
|
vikarṇa tenāsi hataḥ pratijñā rakṣitā mayā ||32||

33tvamāgāḥ samaraṃ vīra kṣātradharmamanusmaran
|
tato vinihataḥ saṃkhye yuddhadharmo hi niṣṭhuraḥ ||33||

34viśeṣato hi nṛpatestathā'smākaṃ hite rataḥ
|
nyāyato'nyāyato vā'pi hataḥ śete mahādyutiḥ ||34||

35agādhabuddhirgāṅgeyaḥ kṣitau suraguroḥ samaḥ
|
tyājitaḥ samare prāṇāṃstasmādyuddhaṃ hi niṣṭhuraṃ ||35||

36sañjaya uvāca|
tānnihatya mahābāhū rādheyasyaiva paśyataḥ
|
siṃhanādaravaṃ ghoramasṛjatpāṇḍunandanaḥ ||36||

37sa ravastasya śūrasya dharmarājasya bhārata
|
ācakhyāviva tadyuddhaṃ vijayaṃ cātmano mahat ||37||

38taṃ śrutvā tu mahānādaṃ bhīmasenasya dhanvinaḥ
|
babhūva paramā prītirdharmarājasya dhīmataḥ ||38||

39tato hṛṣṭamanā rājanvāditrāṇāṃ mahāsvanaiḥ
|
siṃhanādaravaṃ bhrātuḥ pratijagrāha pāṇḍavaḥ ||39||

40harṣeṇa mahatā yuktaḥ kṛtasaṃjño vṛkodare
|
abhyayātsamare droṇaṃ sarvaśastrabhṛtāṃ varaḥ ||40||

41ekatriṃśanmahārāja putrāṃstava nipātitān
|
hatān duryodhano dṛṣṭvā kṣattuḥ sasmāra tadvacaḥ||41||

42tadidaṃ samanuprāptaṃ kṣatturniḥśreyasaṃ vacaḥ
|
iti sañcintya te putro nottaraṃ pratyapadyata ||42||

43yadyūtakāle durbuddhirabravīttanayastava
|
sabhāmānāyyapāñcālīṃ karṇena sahitolpadhīḥ ||43||

44yacca karṇo'bravītkṛṣṇāṃ sabhāyāṃ paruṣaṃ vacaḥ
|
pramukhe pāṇḍuputrāṇāṃ tava caiva viśāṃpate ||44||

45śṛṇvatastava rājendra kauravāṇāṃ ca sarvaśaḥ
|
vinaṣṭāḥ pāṇḍavāḥ kṛṣṇe śāśvataṃ narakaṃ gatāḥ
|
patimanyaṃ vṛṇīṣveti tasyedaṃ phalamāgatam ||45||

46yacca ṣaṇḍhatilādīni paruṣāṇi tavātmajaiḥ
|
śrāvitāste mahātmānaḥ pāṇḍavāḥ kopayiṣṇubhiḥ ||46||

47taṃ bhīmasenaḥ krodhāgniṃ trayodaśa samāḥ sthitam
|
udgiraṃstava putrāṇāmantaṃ gacchati pāṇḍavaḥ ||47||

48vilapaṃśca bahu kṣattā śamaṃ nālabhata tvayi
|
saputro bharataśreṣṭha tasya bhuṃkṣva phalodayam ||48||

49tvayā vṛddhena dhīreṇa kāryatattvārthadarśinā
|
na kṛtaṃ suhṛdāṃ vākyaṃ daivamatra parāyaṇam ||49||

50tanmā śuco naravyāghra tavaivāpanayo mahān
|
vināśahetuḥ putrāṇāṃ bhavāneva mato mama ||50||

51hato vikarṇo rājendra citrasenaśca vīryavān
|
pravarāścātmajānāṃ te sutāścānye mahārathāḥ ||51||

52yānanyān dadṛśe bhīmaścakṣurviṣayamāgatān
|
putrāṃstatha mahārāja tvarayā tāñjaghāna ha ||52||

53tvatkṛte hyahamadrākṣaṃ dahyamānāṃ varūthinīm
|
sahasraśaḥ śarairmuktaiḥ pāṇḍavena vṛṣeṇa ca ||53||
