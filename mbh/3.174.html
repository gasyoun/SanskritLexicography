Mahābhārata 3.174 (Āraṇyakaparvan)

1arjuna uvāca|
tato mamitaviśvastaṃ saṃrūḍhaśaravikṣatam
|
devarājo vigṛhyedaṃ kāle vacanamabravīt||1||

2divyānyastrāṇi sarvāṇi tvayi tiṣṭhanti bhārata
|
na tvā'bhibhavituṃ śakto mānuṣo bhuvi kaścana||2||

3bhīṣmo droṇaḥ kṛpaḥ karṇaḥ śakuniḥ saha rājabhiḥ
|
saṅgrāmasthasya te putra kalāṃ nārhanti ṣoḍaśīm||3||

4idaṃ ca me tanutrāṇaṃ prāyacchanmaghavān prabhuḥ
|
abhedyaṃ kavacaṃ divyaṃ srajaṃ caiva hiraṇmayīm||4||

5devadattaṃ ca me śaṅkhaṃ punaḥ prādānmahāravam
|
divyaṃ cedaṃ kirīṭaṃ me svayamindro yuyoja ha||5||

6tato divyāni vastrāṇi divyānyābharaṇāni ca
|
prādācchakro mamaitāni rucirāṇi bṛhanti ca||6||

7evaṃ sampūjitastatra sukhamasmyuṣito nṛpa
|
indrasya bhavane puṇye gandhavaṃśiśubhiḥ saha||7||

8tato māmabravīcchakraḥ prītimānamaraiḥ saha
|
samayo'rjuna gantuṃ te bhrātaro hi smaranti te||8||

9evamindrasya bhatrane pañca varṣāṇi bhārata
|
uṣitāni mayā rājan smaratā dyūtajaṃ kalim||9||

10tato bhavantamadrākṣaṃ bhrātṛbhiḥ parivāritam
|
gandhamādanapādasya parvatasyāsya mūrdhani||10||

11yudhiṣṭhira uvāca|
diṣṭyā dhanañjayāstrāṇi tvayā prāptāni bhārata
|
diṣṭyā cārādhito rājā devānāmīśvaraḥ prabhuḥ||11||

12diṣṭyā ca bhagavān sthāṇurdevyā saha parantapa
|
sākṣāddṛṣṭaḥ svayuddhena toṣitaśca tvayā'nagha||12||

13diṣṭyā ca lokapālaistvaṃ sameto bharatarṣabha
|
diṣṭyā vardhāmahe pārtha diṣṭyā'si punarāgataḥ||13||

14adya kṛtsnāṃ mahīṃ devīṃ vijitāṃ puramālinīm
|
manye ca dhṛtarāṣṭrasya putrānapi vaśīkṛtān||14||

15icchāmi tāni cāstrāṇi draṣṭuṃ divyāni bhārata
|
yaistathā vīryavantaste nivātakavacā hatāḥ||15||

16arjuna uvāca|
śvaḥ prabhāte bhavān draṣṭā divyānyastrāṇi sarvaśaḥ
|
nivātakavacā ghorā yairmayā vinipātitāḥ||16||

17vaiśampāyana uvāca|
evamāgamanaṃ tatra kathayitvā dhanañjayaḥ
|
bhrātṛbhiḥ sahitaḥ sarvai rajanīṃ tāmuvāsa ha||17||
