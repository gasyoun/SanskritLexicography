Mahābhārata 8.43 (Karṇaparvan)

1sañjaya uvāca|
tataḥ punarmahārāja madrarājamarindamaḥ
|
abhyabhāṣata rādheyaḥ sannivāryottaraṃ vacaḥ ||1||

2yattvaṃ nidarśanārthaṃ māṃ śalya jalpitavānasi
|
nāhaṃ śakyastvayā vācā bibhīṣayitumāhave ||2||

3yadi māṃ devatāḥ sarvā yodhayeyuḥ savāsavāḥ
|
tathāpi me bhayaṃ na syātkimu pārthātsakeśavāt ||3||

4nāhaṃ bhīṣayituṃ śakyo vāṅmātreṇa kathañcana
|
anyaṃ jānīhi yaḥ śakyastvayā bhīṣayituṃ raṇe ||4||

5nīcasya balametāvatpāruṣyaṃ yattvamāttha mām
|
aśakto madguṇān vaktuṃ valgase bahu durmate ||5||

6na hi karṇaḥ samudbhūto bhayārthamiha madraka
|
vikramārthamahaṃ jāto yaśorthaṃ ca tathā''tmanaḥ ||6||

7sakhibhāvena sauhārdānmitrabhāvena caiva hi
|
kāraṇaistribhiretaistvaṃ śalya jīvasi sāṃpratam ||7||

8rājñaśca dhārtarāṣṭrasya kāryaṃ sumahadudyatam
|
mayi taccāhitaṃ śalya tena jīvasi me kṣaṇam ||8||

9kutaśca samayaḥ pūrvaṃ kṣantavyaṃ vipriyaṃ tava
|
ṛte śalyasahastreṇa vijayeyamahaṃ parān
|
mitradrohastu pāpīyāniti jīvasi sāṃpratam ||9||
