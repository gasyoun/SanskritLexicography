Mahābhārata 13.25 (Anuśāsanaparvan)

1yudhiṣṭhira uvāca|
tīrthānāṃ darśanaṃ śreyaḥ snānaṃ ca bharatarṣabha
|
śravaṇaṃ ca mahāprājña śrotumicchāmi tattvataḥ ||1||

2pṛthivyāṃ yāni tīrthāni puṇyāni bharatarṣabha
|
vaktumarhasi me tāni śrotāsmi niyataṃ prabho ||2||

3bhīṣma uvāca|
imamaṃgirasā proktaṃ tīrthavaṃśaṃ mahādyute
|
śrotumarhasi bhadraṃ te prāpsyase dharmamuttamam ||3||

4tapovanagataṃ vipramabhigamya mahāmunim
|
papracchāṃgirasaṃ dhīraṃ gautamaḥ saṃśitavrataḥ ||4||

5asti me bhagavankaścittīrthebhyo dharmasaṃśayaḥ
|
tatsarvaṃ śrotumicchāmi tanme śaṃsa mahāmune ||5||

6upaspṛśya phalaṃ kiṃ syātteṣu tīrtheṣu vai mune
|
pretyabhāve mahāprājña tadyathā'sti tathā vada ||6||

7aṅgirā uvāca|
saptāhaṃ candrabhāgāṃ vai vitastāmūrmimālinīm
|
vigāhya vai nirāhāro nirmalo munivadbhavet ||7||

8kāśmīramaṇḍale nadyo yāḥ patanti mahānadam
|
tā nadīḥ sindhumāsādya śīlavānsvargamāpnuyāt ||8||

9puṣkaraṃ ca prabhāsaṃ ca naimiṣaṃ sāgarodakam
|
devikāmindramārgaṃ ca svarṇabinduṃ vigāhya ca ||9||

10vibodhyate vimānasthaḥ so'psarobhirabhiṣṭutaḥ
|
hiraṇyabinduṃ vikṣobhya prayatnaścābhivādya ca ||10||

11kuśeśayaṃ ca devaṃ taṃ dhūyate tasya kilviṣam
|
indratoyāṃ samāsādya gandhamādanasannidhau ||11||

12karatoyāṃ kuraṅge ca trirātropoṣito naraḥ
|
aśvamedhamavāpnoti vigāhya prayataḥ śuciḥ ||12||

13gaṅgādvāre kuśāvarte bilvake nīlaparvate
|
tathā kanakhale snātvā dhūtapāpmā divaṃ vrajet ||13||

14apāṃ hrada upaspṛśya vājimedhaphalaṃ labhet
|
brahmacārī jitakrodhaḥ satyasandhastvahiṃsakaḥ ||14||

15yatra bhāgīrathī gaṅgā patate diśamuttarām
|
maheśvarasya tristhāne yo narastvabhiṣicyate ||15||

16ekamāsaṃ nirāhāraḥ sa paśyati hi devatāḥ
|
saptagaṅge trigaṅge ca indramārge ca tarpayan ||16||

17sudhāṃ vai labhate bhoktuṃ yo naro jāyate punaḥ
|
mahāśrama upaspṛśya yo'gnihotraparaḥ śuciḥ ||17||

18ekamāsaṃ nirāhāraḥ siddhiṃ māsena sa vrajet
|
mahāhrada upaspṛśya bhṛgutuṅge tvalolupaḥ ||18||

19trirātropoṣito bhūtvā mucyate brahmahṛtyayā
|
kanyākūpa upaspṛśya balākāyāṃ kṛtodakaḥ ||19||

20deveṣu labhate kīrtiṃ yaśasā ca virājate ||20||

21devikāyāmupaspṛśya tathā sundarikāhrade
|
aśvinyāṃ rūpavarcaskaṃ pretya vai labhate naraḥ ||21||

22mahāgaṅgāmupaspṛśya kṛttikāṅgārake tathā
|
pakṣamekaṃ nirāhāraḥ svargamāpnoti nirmalaḥ ||22||

23vaimānika upaspṛśya kiṅkiṇīkāśrame tathā
|
nivāse'psarasāṃ divye kāmacārī mahīyate ||23||

24kālikāśramamāsādya vipāśāyāṃ kṛtodakaḥ
|
brahmacārī jitakrodhastrirātraṃ mucyate bhavāt ||24||

25āśrame kṛttikānāṃ tu snātvā yastarpayetpitṝn
|
toṣayitvā mahādevaṃ nirmalaḥ svargamāpnuyāt ||25||

26mahāpura upaspṛśya trirātropoṣitaḥ śuciḥ
|
trasānāṃ sthāvarāṇāṃ ca dvipadāṃnāṃ bhayaṃ tyajet ||26||

27devadāruvane snātvā dhūtapāpmā kṛtodakaḥ
|
devalokamavāpnoti saptarātroṣitaḥ śuciḥ ||27||

28śarastambe kuśastambe droṇaśarmapade tathā
|
apāṃ prapatanāsevī sevyate so'psarogaṇaiḥ ||28||

29citrakūṭe janasthāne tathā mandākinījale
|
vigāhya vai nirāhāro rājalakṣmyā niṣevyate||29||

30śyāmāyāstvaśramaṃ gatvā upitvā cābhiṣicya ca
|
ekapakṣaṃ nirāhārastvantardhānaphalaṃ labhet ||30||

31kauśikīṃ tu samāsādya vāyubhakṣastvalolupaḥ
|
ekaviṃśatirātreṇa svargamārohate naraḥ ||31||

32mataṅgavāpyāṃ yaḥ snāyādekarātreṇa siddhyati
|
vigāhati hyanālambamandhakaṃ vai sanātanam ||32||

33naimiṣe svargatīrthe ca upaspṛśya jitendriyaḥ
|
phalaṃ puruṣamedhasya labhenmāsaṃ kṛtodakaḥ ||33||

34gaṅgāhrada upaspṛśya tathā caivotpalāvane
|
aśvamedhamavāpnoti tatra māsaṃ kṛtodakaḥ ||34||

35gaṅgāyāmunayostīrthe tathā kālañjare girau
|
daśāśvamedhānāpnoti tatra māsaṃ kṛtodakaḥ ||35||

36ṣaṣṭihrada upaspṛśya cānnadānādviśiṣyate
|
daśatīrthasahasrāṇi tisraḥ koṭyastathā parāḥ ||36||

37samāgacchanti māghyāṃ tu prayāge bharatarṣabha
|
māghamāsaṃ prayāge tu niyataḥ saṃśitavrataḥ ||37||

38snātvā tu bharataśreṣṭha nirmalaḥ svargamāpnuyāt
|
marudgaṇa upaspṛśya pitṝṇāmāśrame śuciḥ ||38||

39vaivasvatasya tīrthe ca tīrthabhūto bhavennaraḥ
|
tathā brahmasaro gatvā bhāgīrathyāṃ kṛtodakaḥ ||39||

40ekamāsaṃ nirāhāraḥ somalokamavāpnuyāt ||40||

41utpātake naraḥ snātvā aṣṭāvakre kṛtodakaḥ
|
dvādaśāhaṃ nirāhāro naramedhaphalaṃ labhet ||41||

42aśmapṛṣṭhe gayāyāṃ ca niravinde ca parvate
|
tṛtīyāṃ krauñcapadyāṃ ca brahmahatyā viśudhyate ||42||

43kalaviṅka upaspṛśya vidyācca bahuśo jalam
|
agneḥ pure naraḥ snātvā agnikanyāpure vaset ||43||

44karavīrapure snātvā viśālāyāṃ kṛtodakaḥ
|
devahrada upaspṛśya brahmabhūto virājate ||44||

45punarāvartanandāṃ ca mahānandāṃ ca sevya vai
|
nandane sevyate dāntastvapsarobhirahiṃsakaḥ ||45||

46urvaśīṃ kṛttikāyoge gatvā caiva samāhitaḥ
|
lauhitye vidhivatsnātvā puṇḍarīkaphalaṃ labhet ||46||

47rāmahrada upaspṛśya vipāśāyāṃ kṛtodakaḥ
|
dvādaśāhaṃ nirāhāraḥ kalmaṣādvipramucyate ||47||

48mahāhrada upaspṛśya śuddhena manasā naraḥ
|
ekamāsaṃ nirāhāro jamadagnigatiṃ labhet ||48||

49vindhye santāpya cātmānaṃ satyasandhastvahiṃsakaḥ
|
vinayāttapa āsthāya māsenaikena siddhyati ||49||

50narmadāyāmupaspṛśya tathā śūrpārakodake
|
ekapakṣaṃ nirāhāro gajaputro vidhīyate ||50||

51jambūmārge tribhirmāsaiḥ saṃyataḥ susamāhitaḥ
|
ahorātreṇa caikena siddhiṃ samadhigacchati ||51||

52kokāmukhe vigāhyātha gatvā cāñjālikāśramam
|
śākabhakṣaścīravāsāḥ kumārīrvindate daśa ||52||

53vaivasvatasya sadanaṃ na sa gacchetkadācana
|
yasya kanyāhrade vāso devalokaṃ sa gacchati ||53||

54prabhāse tvekarātreṇa amāvāsyāṃ samāhitaḥ
|
siddhyate tu mahābāho yo naro jāyate'maraḥ ||54||

55ujjānaka upaspṛśya ārṣṭiṣeṇasya cāśrame
|
piṅgāyāścāśrame snātvā sarvapāpaiḥ pramucyate ||55||

56kulyāyāṃ samupaspṛśya japtvā caivāghamarṣaṇam
|
aśvamedhamavāpnoti trirātropoṣito naraḥ ||56||

57piṃḍāraka upaspṛśya ekarātroṣito naraḥ
|
agniṣṭomamavāpnoti prabhātāṃ śarvarīṃ śuciḥ ||57||

58tathā brahmasaro gatvā dharmāraṇyopaśobhitam
|
puṇḍarīkamavāpnoti upaspṛśya naraḥ śuciḥ ||58||

59maināke parvate snātvā tathā sandhyāmupāsya ca
|
kāmaṃ jitvā ca vai māsaṃ sarvayajñaphalaṃ labhet ||59||

60kālodakaṃ nandikuṇḍaṃ tathā cottaramānasayam
|
abhyetya yojanaśatādbhrūṇahā vipramucyate ||60||

61nandīśvarasya mūrtiṃ tu dṛṣṭā mucyeta kilbiṣaiḥ
|
svargamārge naraḥ snātvā brahmalokaṃ sa gacchati ||61||

62vikhyāto himavānpuṇyaḥ śaṅkaraśvaśuro giriḥ
|
ākaraḥ sarvaratnānāṃ siddhacāraṇasevitaḥ ||62||

63śarīramutsṛjettatra vidhipūrvamanāśake
|
adhruvaṃ jīvitaṃ jñātvā yo vai vedāntago dvijaḥ ||63||

64abhyarcya devatāstatra namaskṛtya munīṃstathā
|
tataḥ siddho divaṃ gacchedbrahmalokaṃ sanātanam ||64||

65kāmaṃ krodhaṃ ca lobhaṃ ca yo jitvā tīrthamāvaset
|
na tena kiñcinna prāptaṃ tīrthābhigamanādbhavet ||65||

66yānyagamyāni tīrthāni durgāṇi viṣamāṇi ca
|
manasā tāni gamyāni sarvatīrthasamīkṣayā ||66||

67idaṃ medhyamidaṃ puṇyamidaṃ svargyamanuttamam
|
idaṃ rahasyaṃ vedānāmāplāvyāṃ pāvanaṃ tathā ||67||

68idaṃ dadyāddvijātīnāṃ sādhorātmahitasya ca
|
suhṛdāṃ ca japetkarṇe śiṣyasyānugatasya ca ||68||

69dattavān gautamasyaitadaṅgirā vai mahātapāḥ
|
aṅgirāḥ samanujñātaḥ kāśyapena ca śrīmatā ||69||

70maharṣīṇāmidaṃ japyaṃ pāvanānāṃ tathottamam
|
japaṃścābhyutthitaḥ śaśvannirmalaḥ svargamāpnuyāt ||70||

71idaṃ yaścāpi śṛṇuyādrahasyaṃ tvaṅgiromatam
|
uttame ca kule janma labhejjātīśca saṃsmaret ||71||
