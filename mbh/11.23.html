Mahābhārata 11.23 (Strīparvan)

1gāndhāryuvāca|eṣa śalyo hataḥ śete sākṣānnakulamātulaḥ
|
dharmajñena hatastāta dharmarājena saṃyuge||1||

2yastvayā spardhate nityaṃ sarvatra puruṣarṣabha
|
sa eṣa nihataḥ śete madrarājo mahābalaḥ||2||

3yena saṅgṛhṇatā tāta rathamādhiratheryudhi
|
jayārthaṃ pāṇḍuputrāṇāṃ tadā tejovadhaḥ kṛtaḥ||3||

4aho dhikpaśya śalyasya pūrṇacandrasudarśanam
|
mukhaṃ pdmapalāśākṣaṃ kākairādaṣṭamavraṇam||4||

5asya cāmīkarābhasya taptakāñcanasaprabhā
|
āsyādviniḥ sṛtā jihvā bhakṣyate kṛṣṇa pakṣibhiḥ||5||

6yudhiṣṭhireṇa nihataṃ śalyaṃ samitiśobhanam
|
rudantyaḥ paryupāsante madrarājaṃ kulāṅganāḥ||6||

7etāḥ susūkṣmavasanā madrarājaṃ nararṣabham
|
krośantyo'tha samāsādya kṣatriyāḥ kṣatriyarṣabham||7||

8śalyaṃ nipatitaṃ nāryaḥ parivāryābhitaḥ sthitāḥ
|
vāsitā gṛṣṭayaḥ paṅke parimagnamivarṣabham||8||

9śalyaṃ śaraṇadaṃ śūraṃ paśyemaṃ vṛṣṇinandana
|
śayānaṃ vīraśayane śarairviśakalīkṛtam||9||

10eṣa śailālayo rājā bhagadattaḥ pratāpavān
|
gajāṅkuśadharaḥ śrīmāñśete bhuvi nipātitaḥ||10||

11yasya rukmamayī mālā śirasyeṣā virājate
|
śvāpadairbhakṣyamāṇasya śobhayantīva mūrdhajān||11||

12etena kila pārthasya yuddhamāsītsudāruṇam
|
romaharṣaṇamatyugraṃ śakrasya tvahinā yathā||12||

13yodhayitvā māhābāhureṣa pārthaṃ dhanañjayam
|
saṃśayaṃ gamayitvā ca kuntīputreṇa pātitaḥ||13||

14yasya nāsti samo loke śaurye vīrye ca kaścana
|
sa eṣa nihataḥ śete bhīṣmo bhīṣmakṛdāhave||14||

15paśya śāntanavaṃ kṛṣṇa śayānaṃ sūryavarcasam
|
yugānta iva kālena pātitaṃ sūryamambarāt||15||

16eṣa taptvā raṇe śatrūñśastratāpena vīryavān
|
narasūryo'stamabhyeti sūryo'stamiva keśava||16||

17śaratalpagataṃ bhīṣmamūrdhvaretasamacyutam
|
śayānaṃ vīraśayane paśya śūraniṣevite||17||

18karṇinālīkanārācairāstīrya śayanottamam
|
āviśya śete bhagavānskandaḥ śaravaṇaṃ yathā||18||

19atūlapūrṇaṃ gāṅgeyastribhirbāṇaiḥ samanvitam
|
upadhāyopadhānāgryaṃ dattaṃ gāṇḍīvadhanvanā||19||

20pālayānaḥ pituḥ śāstramūrdhvaretā mahāyaśāḥ
|
eṣa śāntanavaḥ śete mādhavāpratimo yudhi||20||

21dharmātmā tāta sarvajñaḥ pārāvaryeṇa nirṇaye
|
amartya iva martyaḥ sanneṣa prāṇānadhārayat||21||

22nāsti yuddhe kṛtī kaścinna vidvānna parākramī
|
yatra śāntanavo bhīṣmaḥ śete'dya nihataḥ paraiḥ||22||

23svayametena śūreṇa pṛcchayamānena pāṇḍavaiḥ
|
dharmajñenāhave mṛtyurādiṣṭaḥ satyavādinā||23||

24pranaṣṭaḥ kuruvaṃśaśca punaryena samudvṛtaḥ
|
sa gataḥ kurubhiḥ sārdhaṃ mahābuddhiḥ parābhavam||24||

25dharmeṣu kuravaḥ kaṃ nu pariprakṣyanti mādhava
|
gate devavrate svargaṃ devakalpe nararṣabhe||25||

26arjunasya vinetāramācāryaṃ sātyakestathā
|
taṃ paśya patitaṃ droṇaṃ kurūṇāṃ gurumuttamam||26||

27astraṃ caturvidhaṃ veda yathaiva tridaśeśvaraḥ
|
bhārgavo vā mahāvīryastathā droṇo'pi mādhava||27||

28yasya prasādādbībhatsuḥ pāṇḍavaḥ karma duṣkaram
|
cakāra sa hataḥ śete nainamastrāṇyapālayan||28||

29yaṃ purodhāya kurava āhūyanti sma pāṇḍavān
|
so'yaṃ śastrabhṛtāṃ śreṣṭho droṇaḥ śastraiḥ parikṣataḥ||29||

30yasya nirdahataḥ senāṃ gatiragnerivābhavat
|
sa bhūmau nihataḥ śete śāntārciriva pāvakaḥ||30||

31dhanurmuṣṭiraśīrṇaśca hastāvāpaśca mādhava
|
droṇasya nihatasyāpi dṛśyate jīvato yathā||31||

32vedā yasmācca catvāraḥ sarvāṇyastrāṇi keśava
|
anapetāni vai śūrādyathaivādau prajāpateḥ||32||

33vandanārhāvimau tasya bandibhirvanditau śubhau
|
gomāyavo vikarṣanti pādau śiṣyaśatārcitau||33||

34droṇaṃ drupadaputreṇa nihataṃ madhusūdana
|
kṛpī kṛpaṇamanvāste duḥkhopahatacetanā||34||

35tāṃ paśya rudatīmārtāṃ muktakeśīmadhomukhīm
|
hataṃ patimupāsantīṃ droṇaṃ śastrabhṛtāṃ varam||35||

36bāṇairbhinnatanutrāṇaṃ dhṛṣṭadyumnena keśava
|
upāste vai mṛdhe droṇaṃ jaṭilā brahmacāriṇī||36||

37pretakṛtyaṃ ca yatate kṛpī kṛpaṇamāturā
|
hatasya samare bhartuḥ sukumārī yaśasvinī||37||

38agnīnādhāya vidhivaccitāṃ prajvālya sarvataḥ
|
droṇamādhāya gāyanti trīṇi sāmāni sāmagāḥ||38||

39kurvanti ca citāmete jaṭilā brahmacāriṇaḥ
|
dhanurbhiḥ śaktibhiścaiva rathanīḍaiśca mādhava||39||

40śaraiśca vividhairanyairdhakṣyante bhūritejasam
|
iti droṇaṃ samādāya śaṃsanti ca rudanti ca||40||

41sāmabhistribhirantasthairanuśaṃsanti cāpare
|
agnāvagniṃ samādhāya droṇaṃ hutvā hutāśane||41||

42gacchantyabhimukhā gaṅgāṃ droṇaśiṣyā dvijātayaḥ
|
apasavyāṃ citiṃ kṛtvā puraskṛtya kṛpīṃ ca te||42||
