Mahābhārata 12.334 (Śāntiparvan)

1yudhiṣṭhira uvāca|
gṛhastho brahmacārī vā vānaprastho'tha bhikṣukaḥ
|
ya icchetsiddhimāsthātuṃ devatāṃ kāṃ yajeta saḥ||1||

2kuto hyasya dhruvaḥ svargaḥ kuto naiḥśreyasaṃ param
|
vidhinā kena juhuyāddaiva pitryaṃ tathaiva ca||2||

3muktaśca kāṃ gatiṃ gacchenmokṣaścaiva kimātmakaḥ
|
svargataścaiva kiṃ kuryādyena na cyavate divaḥ||3||

4devatānāṃ ca ko devaḥ pitṝṇāṃ ca pitā tathā
|
tasmātparataraṃ yacca tanme brūhi pitāmaha||4||

5bhīṣma uvāca|
gūḍhaṃ māṃ praśnavitpraśnaṃ pṛcchase tvamihānagha
|
na hyetattarkayā śakyaṃ vaktuṃ varṣaśatairapi||5||

6ṛte devaprasādādvā rājan jñānāgamena vā
|
gahanaṃ hyetadākhyānaṃ vyākhyātavyaṃ tavārihan||6||

7atrāpyudāharantīmamitihāsaṃ purātanam
|
nāradasya ca saṃvādamṛṣernārāyaṇasya ca||7||

8nārāyaṇo hi viśvātmā caturmūrtiḥ sanātanaḥ
|
dharmātmajaḥ saṃbabhūva pitaivaṃ me'bhyabhāṣata||8||

9kṛte yuge mahārāja purā svāyaṃbhūve'ntare
|
naro nārāyaṇaścaiva hariḥ kṛṣṇaḥ svayaṃbhuvaḥ||9||

10teṣāṃ nārāyaṇanarau tapastepaturavyayau
|
badaryāśramamāsādya śakaṭe kanakāmaye||10||

11aṣṭacakraṃ hi tadyānaṃ bhūtayuktaṃ manoramam
|
tatrādyau lokanāthau tau kṛśau dhamanisantatau||11||

12tapasā tejasā caiva durnirīkṣyau surairapi
|
yasya prasādaṃ kurvāte sa devau draṣṭumarhati||12||

13nūnaṃ tayoranumate hṛdi hṛcchayacoditaḥ
|
mahāmerogireḥ śṛṅgātpracyuto gandhamādanam||13||

14nāradaḥ sumahadbhūtaṃ sarvalokānacīcarat
|
taṃ deśamagamadrājanbadaryāśramamāśugaḥ||14||

15tayorāhnikavelāyāṃ tasya kautūhalaṃ tvabhūt
|
idaṃ tadāspadaṃ kṛtsnaṃ yasminlokāḥ pratiṣṭhitāḥ||15||

16sadevāsuragandharvāḥ sakinnaramahoragāḥ
|
ekā mūrtiriyaṃ pūrvaṃ jātā bhūyaścaturvidhā||16||

17dharmasya kulasantāne dharmādebhirvivardhitaḥ
|
aho hyanugṛhīto'dya dharma ebhiḥ surairiha||17||

18naranārāyaṇābhyāṃ ca kṛṣṇena hariṇā tathā
|
atra kṛṣṇo hariścaiva kasmiṃścitkāraṇāntare||18||

19sthitau dharmottarau hyetau tathā tapasi dhiṣṭhitau
|
etau hi paramaṃ dhāma kā'nayorāhnikakriyā||19||

20pitarau sarvabhūtānāṃ daivataṃ ca yaśasvinau
|
kāṃ devatāṃ tu yajataḥ pitṝnvā kānmahāmatī||20||

21iti sacintya manasā bhaktyā nārāyaṇasya tu
|
sahasā prādurabhavatsamīpe devayostadā||21||

22kṛte daive ca pitrye ca tatastābhyāṃ nirīkṣitaḥ
|
pūjitaścaiva vidhinā yathāproktena śāstrataḥ||22||

23tad dṛṣṭvā mahadāścaryamapūrvaṃ vidhivistaram
|
upopaviṣṭaḥ suprīto nārado bhagavānṛṣiḥ||23||

24nārāyaṇaṃ saṃnirīkṣya prasannenāntarātmanā
|
namaskṛtvā mahādevamidaṃ vacanamabravīt||24||

25nārada uvāca|
vedeṣu sapurāṇeṣu sāṅgopāṅgeṣu gīyase
|
tvamajaḥ śāśvato dhātā mātā'mṛtamanuttamam||25||

26pratiṣṭhitaṃ bhūtabhavyaṃ tvayi sarvamidaṃ jagat
|
catvāro hyāśramā deva sarve gārhasthyamūlakāḥ||26||

27yajante tvāmaharaharnānāmūrtisamāsthitam
|
pitā mātā ca sarvasya jagataḥ śāśvato guruḥ
|
kaṃ tvadya yajase devaṃ pitaraṃ kaṃ na vidmahe||27||

28śrī bhagavānuvāca |
avācyametadvaktavyamātmaguhyaṃ sanātanam
|
tava bhaktimato brahmanvakṣyāmi tu yathātatham||28||

29yattatsūkṣmamavijñeyamavyaktamacalaṃ dhruvam
|
indriyairindriyārthaiśca sarvabhūtaiśca varjitam||29||

30sa hyantarātmā bhūtānāṃ kṣetrajñaśceti kathyate
|
triguṇavyatirikto vai puruṣaśceti kalpitaḥ||30||

31tasmādavyaktamutpannaṃ triguṇaṃ dvijasattama
|
avyaktā vyaktabhāvasthā yā sā prakṛtiravyayā||31||

32tāṃ yonimāvayorviddhi yo'sau sadasadātmakaḥ
|
āvābhyāṃ pūjyate so hi daive pitrye ca kalpyate||32||

33nāsti tasmātparo'nyo hi pitā devo'thavā dvija
|
ātmā hi naḥ sa vijñeyastatastaṃ pūjayāvahe||33||

34tenaiṣā prathitā brahmanmaryādā lokabhāvinī
|
daivaṃ pitryaṃ ca kartavyamiti tasyānuśāsanam||34||

35brahmā sthāṇurmanurdakṣo bhṛgurdharmastathā yamaḥ
|
marīciraṅgirā'triśca pulastyaḥ pulahaḥ kratuḥ||35||

36vasiṣṭhaḥ parameṣṭhī ca vivasvānsoma eva ca
|
kardamaścāpi yaḥ proktaḥ krodho vikrīta eva ca||36||

37ekaviṃśatirutpannāste prajāpatayaḥ smṛtāḥ
|
tasya devasya maryādāṃ pūjayantaḥ sanātanīm||37||

38daivaṃ pitryaṃ ca satataṃ tasya vijñāya tattvataḥ
|
ātmaprāptāni ca tataḥ prāpnuvanti dvijottamāḥ||38||

39svargasthā api ye kecittānnamasyanti dehinaḥ
|
te tatprasādādgacchanti tenādiṣṭaphalāṃ gatim||39||

40ye hīnāḥ saptadaśabhirguṇaiḥ karmabhireva ca
|
kalā:pañcadaśa tyaktvāste muktā iti niścayaḥ||40||

41muktānāṃ tu gatirbrahmankṣetrajña iti kalpitā
|
sa hi sarvaguṇaścaiva nirguṇaścaiva kathyate||41||

42dṛśyate jñānayogena āvāṃ ca prasṛtau tataḥ
|
evaṃ jñātvā tamātmānaṃ pūjayāvaḥ sanātanam||42||

43taṃ vedāścāśramāścaiva nānāmatasamāsthitāḥ
|
bhaktyā saṃpūjayantyāśu gatiṃ caiṣāṃ dadāti saḥ||43||

44ye tu tadbhāvitā loke hyekāntitvaṃ samāsthitāḥ
|
etadabhyadhikaṃ teṣāṃ yatte taṃ praviśantyut||44||

45iti guhyasamuddeśastava nārada kīrtitaḥ
|
bhaktyā premṇā ca viprarṣe asmadbhaktyā ca te śrutaḥ||45||
