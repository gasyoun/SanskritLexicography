Mahābhārata 3.135 (Āraṇyakaparvan)

1lomaśa uvāca|
eṣā madhuvilā rājansamaṅgā saṃprakāśate
|
etatkardamilaṃ nāma bharatasyābhiṣecanam||1||

2alakṣmyā kila saṃyukto vṛtraṃ hatvā śacīpatiḥ
|
āplutaḥ sarvapāpebhyaḥ samaṅgāyāṃ vyamucyata||2||

3etadvinaśanaṃ kukṣau mainākasya nararṣabha
|
aditiryatra putrārthaṃ tadannamapacatpurā||3||

4enaṃ parvatarājānamāruhya bharatarṣabhāḥ
|
ayaśasyāmasaṃśabdyāmalakṣmīṃ vyapanotsyatha||4||

5ete kanakhalā rājannṛṣīṇāṃ dayitā nagāḥ
|
eṣā prakāśate gaṅgā yudhiṣṭhira mahānadī||5||

6sanatkumāro bhagavānatra siddhimagātpurā
|
ājamīḍhāvagāhyaināṃ sarvapāpaiḥ pramokṣyase||6||

7apāṃ hradaṃ ca puṇyākhyaṃ bhṛgutuṅgaṃ ca parvatam
|
uṣṇīgaṅge ca kaunteya sāmātyaḥ samupaspṛśa||7||

8āśramaḥ sthūlaśiraso ramaṇīyaḥ prakāśate
|
atra mānaṃ ca kaunteya krodhaṃ caiva vivarjaya||8||

9eṣa raibhyāśramaḥ śrīmānpāṇḍaveya prakāśate
|
bhāradvājo yatra kaviryavakrīto vyanaśyata||9||

10yudhiṣṭhira uvāca|
kathaṃ yukto'bhavadṛṣirbharadvājaḥ pratāpavān
|
kimarthaṃ ca yavakrītaḥ putro'naśyata vai muneḥ||10||

11etatsarvaṃ yathāvṛttaṃ śrotumicchāmi tattvataḥ
|
karmabhirdevakalpānāṃ kīrtyamānairbhṛśaṃ rame||11||

12lomaśa uvāca|
bharadvājaśca raibhyaśca sakhāyau saṃbabhūvatuḥ
|
tāvūṣaturihātyantaṃ prīyamāṇāvanantaram||12||

13raibhyasya tu sutāvāstāmarvāvasuparāvasū
|
āsīdyavakrīḥ putrastu bharadvājasya bhārata||13||

14raibhyo vidvānsahāpatyastapasvī cetaro'bhavat
|
tayoścāpyatulā kīrtirbālyātprabhṛti bhārata||14||

15yavakrīḥ pitaraṃ dṛṣṭvā tapasvinamasatkṛtam
|
dṛṣṭvā ca satkṛtaṃ viprai raibhyaṃ putraiḥ sahānaghaḥ||15||

16paryatapyata tejasvī manyunābhipariplutaḥ
|
tapastepe tato ghoraṃ vedajñānāya pāṇḍava||16||

17sa samiddhe mahatyagnau śarīramupatāpayan
|
janayāmāsa saṃtāpamindrasya sumahātapāḥ||17||

18tata indro yavakrītamupagamya yudhiṣṭhira
|
abravītkasya hetostvamāsthitastapa uttamam||18||

19yavakrīta uvāca|
dvijānāmanadhītā vai vedāḥ suragaṇārcita
|
pratibhāntviti tapye'hamidaṃ paramakaṃ tapaḥ||19||

20svādhyāyārthaṃ samārambho mamāyaṃ pākaśāsana
|
tapasā jñātumicchāmi sarvajñānāni kauśika||20||

21kālena mahatā vedāḥ śakyā gurumukhādvibho
|
prāptuṃ tasmādayaṃ yatnaḥ paramo me samāsthitaḥ||21||

22indra uvāca|
amārga eṣa viprarṣe yena tvaṃ yātumicchasi
|
kiṃ vighātena te vipra gacchādhīhi gurormukhāt||22||

23lomaśa uvāca|
evamuktvā gataḥ śakro yavakrīrapi bhārata
|
bhūya evākarodyatnaṃ tapasyamitavikramaḥ||23||

24ghoreṇa tapasā rājaṃstapyamāno mahattapaḥ
|
saṃtāpayāmāsa bhṛśaṃ devendramiti naḥ śrutam||24||

25taṃ tathā tapyamānaṃ tu tapastīvraṃ mahāmunim
|
upetya balabhiddevo vārayāmāsa vai punaḥ||25||

26aśakyo'rthaḥ samārabdho naitadbuddhikṛtaṃ tava
|
pratibhāsyanti vai vedāstava caiva pituśca te||26||

27yavakrīta uvāca|
na caitadevaṃ kriyate devarāja mamepsitam
|
mahatā niyamenāhaṃ tapsye ghorataraṃ tapaḥ||27||

28samiddhe'gnāvupakṛtyāṅgamaṅgaṃ hoṣyāmi vā maghavaṃstannibodha
|
yadyetadevaṃ na karoṣi kāmaṃ mamepsitaṃ devarājeha sarvam||28||

29lomaśa uvāca|
niścayaṃ tamabhijñāya munestasya mahātmanaḥ
|
prativāraṇahetvarthaṃ buddhyā saṃcintya buddhimān||29||

30tata indro'karodrūpaṃ brāhmaṇasya tapasvinaḥ
|
anekaśatavarṣasya durbalasya sayakṣmaṇaḥ||30||

31yavakrītasya yattīrthamucitaṃ śaucakarmaṇi
|
bhāgīrathyāṃ tatra setuṃ vālukābhiścakāra saḥ||31||

32yadāsya vadato vākyaṃ na sa cakre dvijottamaḥ
|
vālukābhistataḥ śakro gaṅgāṃ samabhipūrayan||32||

33vālukāmuṣṭimaniśaṃ bhāgīrathyāṃ vyasarjayat
|
setumabhyārabhacchakro yavakrītaṃ nidarśayan||33||

34taṃ dadarśa yavakrīto yatnavantaṃ nibandhane
|
prahasaṃścābravīdvākyamidaṃ sa munipuṅgavaḥ||34||

35kimidaṃ vartate brahmankiṃ ca te ha cikīrṣitam
|
atīva hi mahānyatnaḥ kriyate'yaṃ nirarthakaḥ||35||

36indra uvāca|
bandhiṣye setunā gaṅgāṃ sukhaḥ panthā bhaviṣyati
|
kliśyate hi janastāta taramāṇaḥ punaḥ punaḥ||36||

37yavakrīta uvāca|
nāyaṃ śakyastvayā baddhuṃ mahānoghastapodhana
|
aśakyādvinivartasva śakyamarthaṃ samārabha||37||

38indra uvāca|
yathaiva bhavatā cedaṃ tapo vedārthamudyatam
|
aśakyaṃ tadvadasmābhirayaṃ bhāraḥ samāhitaḥ||38||

39yavakrīta uvāca|
yathā tava nirartho'yamārambhastridaśeśvara
|
tathā yadi mamāpīdaṃ manyase pākaśāsana||39||

40kriyatāṃ yadbhavecchakyaṃ tvayā suragaṇeśvara
|
varāṃśca me prayacchānyānyairanyānbhavitāsmyati||40||

41lomaśa uvāca|
tasmai prādādvarānindra uktavānyānmahātapāḥ
|
pratibhāsyanti te vedāḥ pitrā saha yathepsitāḥ||41||

42yaccānyatkāṅkṣase kāmaṃ yavakrīrgamyatāmiti
|
sa labdhakāmaḥ pitaraṃ sametyātheda‌mabravīt||42||

43yavakrīta uvāca|
pratibhāsyanti vai vedā mama tātasya cobhayoḥ
|
ati cānyānbhaviṣyāvo varā labdhāstathā mayā||43||

44bharadvāja uvāca|
darpaste bhavitā tāta varā~llabdhvā yathepsitān
|
sa darpapūrṇaḥ kṛpaṇaḥ kṣiprameva vinaṅkhyāsa||44||

45atrāpyudāharantīmā gāthā devairudāhṛtāḥ
|
munirāsītpurā putra bāladhirnāma vīryavān||45||

46sa putraśokādudvignastapastepe suduṣkaram
|
bhavenmama suto'martya iti taṃ labdhavāṃśca saḥ||46||

47tasya prasādo vai devaiḥ kṛto na tvamaraiḥ samaḥ
|
nāmartyo vidyate martyo nimittāyurbhaviṣyati||47||

48bāladhiruvāca|
yatheme parvatāḥ śaśvattiṣṭhanti surasattamāḥ
|
akṣayāstannimittaṃ me sutasyāyurbhaviṣyati||48||

49bharadvāja uvāca|
tasya putrastadā jajñe medhāvī krodhanastadā
|
sa tacchrutvākaroddarpamṛṣīṃścaivāvamanyata||49||

50vikurvāṇo munīnāṃ ca vyacaratsa mahīmimām
|
āsasāda mahāvīryaṃ dhanuṣākṣaṃ manīṣiṇam||50||

51tasyāpacakre medhāvī taṃ śaśāpa sa vīryavān
|
bhava bhasmeti coktaḥ sa na bhasma samapadyata||51||

52dhanuṣākṣastu taṃ dṛṣṭvā medhāvinamanāmayam
|
nimittamasya mahiṣairbhedayāmāsa vīryavān||52||

53sa nimitte vinaṣṭe tu mamāra sahasā śiśuḥ
|
taṃ mṛtaṃ putramādāya vilalāpa tataḥ pitā||53||

54lālapyamānaṃ taṃ dṛṣṭvā munayaḥ paramārtavat
|
ūcurvedavidaḥ sarve gāthāṃ yāṃ tāṃ nibodha me||54||

55na diṣṭamarthamatyetumīśo martyaḥ kathaṃcana
|
mahiṣairbhedayāmāsa dhanuṣākṣo mahīdharān||55||

56evaṃ labdhvā varānbālā darpapūrṇāstapasvinaḥ
|
kṣiprameva vinaśyanti yathā na syāttathā bhavān||56||

57eṣa raibhyo mahāvīryaḥ putrau cāsya tathāvidhau
|
taṃ yathā putra nābhyeṣi tathā kuryāstvatandritaḥ||57||

58sa hi kruddhaḥ samarthaśca putraṃ pīḍayituṃ ruṣā
|
raibhyaścāpi tapasvī ca kopanaśca mahānṛṣiḥ||58||

59yavakrīta uvāca|
evaṃ kariṣye mā tāpaṃ tāta kārṣīḥ kathaṃcana
|
yathā hi me bhavānmānyastathā raibhyaḥ pitā mama||59||

60lomaśa uvāca|
uktvā sa pitaraṃ ślakṣṇaṃ yavakrīrakutobhayaḥ
|
viprakurvanṛṣīnanyānatuṣyatparayā mudā||60||
