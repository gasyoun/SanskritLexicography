Mahābhārata 7.160 (Droṇaparvan)

1sañjaya uvāca|
duryodhanenaivamukto drauṇirāhavadurmadaḥ
|
cakārārivadhe yatnamindro daityavadhe yathā
|
pratyuvāca mahābāhustava putramidaṃ vacaḥ||1||

2satyametanmahābāho yathā vadasi kaurava
|
priyā hi pāṇḍavā nityaṃ mama cāpi pituśca me||2||

3tathaivāvāṃ priyau teṣāṃ na tu yuddhe kurūdvaha
|
śaktitastāta yudhyāmastyaktvā prāṇānabhītavat||3||

4ahaṃ karṇaśca śalyaśca kṛpo hārdikya eva ca
|
nimeṣātpāṇḍavīṃ senāṃ kṣapayema nṛpottama||4||

5te cāpi kauravīṃ senāṃ nimeṣārdhātkurūdvaha
|
kṣapayeyurmahābāho na syāma yadi saṃyuge||5||

6yudhyatāṃ pāṇḍavāñchaktyā teṣāṃ cāsmānyuyutsatām
|
tejastejaḥ samāsādya praśamaṃ yātu bhārata||6||

7aśakyā tarasā jetuṃ pāṇḍavānāmanīkinī
|
jīvatsu pāṇḍuputreṣu taddhi satyaṃ bravīmi te||7||

8ātmārthaṃ yudhyamānāste samarthāḥ pāṃḍunandanāḥ
|
kimarthaṃ tava sainyāni na haniṣyanti bhārata||8||

9tvaṃ tu lubdhatamo rājannikṛtijñaśca kaurava
|
sarvābhiśaṃkī mānī ca tato'smānabhiśaṃkase||9||

10manye tvaṃ kutsito rājanpāpātmā pāpapūruṣaḥ
|
anyānapi sa naḥ kṣudra śaṃkase pāpabhāvitaḥ||10||

11ahaṃ tu yatnamāsthāya tvadarthe tyaktajīvitaḥ
|
eṣa gacchāmi saṃgrāmaṃ tvatkṛte kurunandana||11||

12yotsye'haṃ śatrubhiḥ sārdhaṃ jeṣyāmi ca varānvarān
|
pañcālaiḥ saha yotsyāmi somakaiḥ kekayaistathā||12||

13pāṇḍaveyaiśca saṃgrāme tvatpriyārthamarindama
|
adya madbāṇanirdagdhāḥ pañcālāḥ somakāstathā||13||

14siṃhenevārditā gāvo vidraviṣyanti sarvaśaḥ
|
adya dharmasuto rājā dṛṣṭvā mama parākramam||14||

15aśvatthāmamayaṃ lokaṃ maṃsyate saha somakaiḥ
|
āgamiṣyati nirvedaṃ dharmaputro yudhiṣṭhiraḥ||15||

16dṛṣṭvā vinihatānsaṃkhye pañcālānsomakaiḥ saha
|
ye māṃ yuddhe'bhiyotsyanti tānhaniṣyāmi bhārata||16||

17na hi te vīra mokṣyante madbāhvantaramāgatāḥ
|
evamuktvā mahābāhuḥ putraṃ duryodhanaṃ tava||17||

18abhyavartata yuddhāya trāsayansarvadhanvinaḥ
|
cikīrṣustava putrāṇāṃ priyaṃ prāṇabhṛtāṃ varaḥ||18||

19tato'bravītsakaikeyān pañcālān gautamīsutaḥ
|
praharadhvamitaḥ sarve mama gātre mahārathāḥ||19||

20sthirībhūtāśca yuddhyadhvaṃ darśayanto'stralāghavam
|
evamuktāstu te sarve śastravṛṣṭīrapātayan||20||

21drauṇiṃ prati mahārāja jalaṃ jaladharā iva
|
tānnihatya śarāndrauṇirdaśa vīrānapothayat||21||

22pramukhe pāṇḍuputrāṇāṃ dhṛṣṭadyumnasya ca prabho
|
te hanyamānāḥ samare pañcālāḥ somakāstathā||22||

23parityajya raṇe drauṇiṃ vyadravanta diśo daśa
|
tāndṛṣṭvā dravataḥ śūrānpañcālānsahasomakān||23||

24dhṛṣṭadyumno mahārāja drauṇimabhyadravadraṇe
|
tataḥ kāñcanacitrāṇāṃ sajalāṃbudanādinām||24||

25vṛtaḥ śatena śūrāṇāṃ rathānāmanivartinām
|
putraḥ pāñcālarājasya dhṛṣṭadyumno mahārathaḥ||25||

26drauṇimityabravīdvākyaṃ dṛṣṭvā yodhānnipātitān
|
ācāryaputra durbuddhe kimanyairnihataistava||26||

27samāgaccha mayā sārdhaṃ yadi śūro'si saṃyuge
|
ahaṃ tvāṃ nihaniṣyāmi tiṣṭhedānīṃ mamāgrataḥ||27||

28tatastamācāryasutaṃ dhṛṣṭadyumnaḥ pratāpavān
|
marmabhidbhiḥ śaraistīkṣṇairjaghāna bharatarṣabha||28||

29te tu paṃktīkṛtā drauṇiṃ śarā viviśurāśugāḥ
|
rukmapuṅkhāḥ prasannāgrāḥ sarvakāyāvadāraṇāḥ||29||

30madhvarthina ivoddāmā bhramarāḥ puṣpitaṃ drumam
|
so'tividdho bhṛśaṃ kruddhaḥ padākrānta ivoragaḥ||30||

31mānī drauṇirasaṃbhrānto bāṇapāṇirabhāṣata
|
dhṛṣṭadyumna sthiro bhūtvā muhūrtaṃ pratipālaya||31||

32yāvattvāṃ niśitairbāṇaiḥ preṣayāmi yamakṣayam
|
drauṇirevamathābhāṣya pārṣataṃ paravīrahā||32||

33chādayāmāsa bāṇaughaiḥ samantāllaghuhastavat
|
sa bādhyamānaḥ samare drauṇinā yuddhadurmadaḥ||33||

34drauṇiṃ pāñcālatanayo vāgbhirātarjayattadā
|
na jānīṣe pratijñāṃ me viprotpattiṃ tathaiva ca||34||

35droṇaṃ hatvā kila mayā hantavyastvaṃ sudurmate
|
tatastvā'haṃ na hanmyadya droṇe jīvati saṃyuge||35||

36imāṃ tu rajanīṃ prāptāmaprabhātāṃ sudurmate
|
nihatya pitaraṃ te'dya tatastvāmapi saṃyuge||36||

37neṣyāmi pretalokāya hyetanme manasi sthitam
|
yaste pārtheṣu vidveṣo yā bhaktiḥ kauraveṣu ca||37||

38tāṃ darśaya sthiro bhūtvā na me jīvanvimokṣyase
|
yo hi brāhmaṇyamutsṛjya kṣatradharmarato dvijaḥ||38||

39sa vadhyaḥ sarvalokasya yathā tvaṃ puruṣādhamaḥ
|
ityuktaḥ paruṣaṃ vākyaṃ pārṣatena dvijottamaḥ||39||

40krodhamāhārayattīvraṃ tiṣṭha tiṣṭheti cābravīt
|
nirdahanniva cakṣurbhyāṃ pārṣataṃ so'bhyavaikṣata||40||

41chādayāmāsa ca śarairniḥśvasanpannago yathā
|
sa cchādyamānaḥ samare drauṇinā rājasattama||41||

42sarvapāñcālasenābhiḥ saṃvṛto rathasattamaḥ
|
nākaṃpata mahābāhuḥ svavīryaṃ samupāśritaḥ||42||

43sāyakāṃścaiva vividhānaśvatthāmni mumoca ha
|
tau punaḥ saṃnyavartetāṃ prāṇadyūtapaṇe raṇe||43||

44nipīḍayantau bāṇaughaiḥ parasparamamarṣiṇau
|
utsṛjantau maheṣvāsau śaravṛṣṭīḥ samantataḥ||44||

45drauṇipārṣatayoryuddhaṃ ghorarūpaṃ bhayānakam
|
dṛṣṭvā saṃpūjayāmāsuḥ siddhacāraṇavātikāḥ||45||

46śaraughaiḥ pūrayantau tāvākāśaṃ ca diśastathā
|
alakṣyau samayudhyetāṃ mahatkṛtvā śaraistamaḥ||46||

47nṛtyamānāviva raṇe maṇḍalīkṛtakārmukau
|
parasparavadhe yattau sarvabhūtabhayaṅkarau||47||

48ayudhyetāṃ mahābāhū citraṃ laghu ca suṣṭhu ca
|
saṃpūjyamānau samare yodhamukhyaiḥ sahasraśaḥ||48||

49tau prabuddhau raṇe dṛṣṭvā vane vanyau gajāviva
|
ubhayoḥ senayorharṣastumulaḥ samapadyata||49||

50siṃhanādaravāścāsandadhmuḥ śaṅkhāṃśca sainikāḥ
|
vāditrāṇyabhyavādyanta śataśo'tha sahasraśaḥ||50||

51tasmiṃstu tumule yuddhe bhīrūṇāṃ bhayavardhane
|
muhūrtamapi tadyuddhaṃ samarūpaṃ tadā'bhavat||51||

52tato drauṇirmahārāja pārṣatasya mahātmanaḥ
|
dhvajaṃ dhanustathā chatramubhau ca pārṣṇisārathī||52||

53sūtamaśvāṃśca caturo nihatyābhyadravadraṇe
|
pañcālāṃścaiva tānsarvānbāṇaiḥ sannataparvabhiḥ||53||

54vyadrāvayadameyātmā śataśo'tha sahasraśaḥ
|
tatastu vivyathe senā pāṇḍavī bharatarṣabha||54||

55dṛṣṭvā drauṇermahatkarma vāsavasyeva saṃyuge
|
śatena ca śataṃ hatvā pañcālānāṃ mahārathaḥ||55||

56tribhiśca niśitairbāṇairhatvā trīnvai mahārathān
|
drauṇirdupadaputrasya phālgunasya ca paśyataḥ||56||

57nāśayāmāsa pañcālān bhūyiṣṭhaṃ ye vyavasthitāḥ
|
te vadhyamānāḥ pañcālāḥ samare saha sṛñjayaiḥ||57||

58agacchandrauṇimutsṛjya viprakīrṇarathadhvajāḥ
|
sa jitvā samare śatrūndroṇaputro mahārathaḥ||58||

59nanāda sumahānādaṃ tapānte jalado yathā
|
sa nihatya bahūñchūrānaśvatthāmā vyarocata
|
yugānte sarvabhūtāni bhasma kṛtveva pāvakaḥ||59||

60saṃpūjyamāno yudhi kauraveyairnirjitya saṃkhye'rigaṇānsahasraśaḥ
|
vyarocata droṇasutaḥ pratāpavān yathā surendro'rigaṇānnihatya vai||60||
