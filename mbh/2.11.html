Mahābhārata 2.11 (Sabhāparvan)

1nārada uvāca|pitāmahasabhāṃ tāta kathyamānāṃ nibodha me
|
śakyate yā na nirdeṣṭumevaṃrūpeti bhārata||1||

2purā devayuge rājannādityo bhagavāndivaḥ
|
āgacchanmānuṣaṃ lokaṃ didṛkṣurvigataklamaḥ||2||

3caranmānuṣarūpeṇa sabhāṃ dṛṣṭvā svayambhuvaḥ
|
sa tāmakathayanmahyaṃ dṛṣṭvā tattvena pāṇḍava||3||

4aprameyāṃ sabhāṃ divyāṃ mānasīṃ bharatarṣabha
|
anirdeśyāṃ prabhāveṇa sarvabhūtamanoramām||4||

5śrutvā guṇānahaṃ tasyāḥ sabhāyāḥ pāṇḍavarṣabha
|
darśanepsustathā rājannādityamidamabruvam||5||

6bhagavandraṣṭumicchāmi pitāmahasabhāṃ śubhām
|
yena vā tapasā śakyā karmaṇā vā'pi gopate||6||

7auṣadhairyā tathā yuktairuttamā pāpanāśinī
|
tanmamācakṣva bhagavanpaśyeyaṃ tāṃ sabhāṃ yathā||7||

8sa tanmama vacaḥ śrutvā sahasrāṃśurdivākaraḥ
|
provāca bhārataśreṣṭha vrataṃ varṣasahasrakam||8||

9brahmavratamupāssva tvaṃ prayatenāntarātmanā
|
tato'haṃ himavatpṛṣṭhe samārabdho mahāvratam||9||

10tataḥ sa bhagavānsūryo māmupādāya vīryavān
|
āgacchattāṃ sabhāṃ brāhmīṃ vipāpmā vigataklamaḥ ||10||

11evaṃrūpeti sā śakyā na nirdeṣṭuṃ narādhipa
|
kṣaṇena hi bibhartyanyadanirdeśyaṃ vapustathā||11||

12na veda parimāṇaṃ vā saṃsthānaṃ cāpi bhārata
|
na ca rūpaṃ mayā tādṛk dṛṣṭapūrvaṃ kadācana||12||

13susukhā sā sadā rājanna śītā na ca gharmadā
|
na kṣutpipāse na glāniṃ prāpya tāṃ prāptuvantyuta ||13||

14nānārūpairiva kṛtā maṇibhiḥ sā subhāsvaraiḥ
|
stambhairna ca dhṛtā sā tu śāśvatī na ca sā kṣarā ||14||

15divyairnānāvidhairbhāvairbhāsadbhiramitaprabhaiḥ||15||

16aticandraṃ ca sūryaṃ ca śikhinaṃ ca svayamprabhā
|
dīpyate nākapṛṣṭhasthā bhartsayantīva bhāskaram||16||

17tasyāṃ sa bhagavānāste vidadhaddevamāyayā
|
svayamekoniśaṃ rājansarvalokapitāmahaḥ||17||

18upatiṣṭhanti cāpyenaṃ prajānāṃ patayaḥ prabhum
|
dakṣaḥ pracetāḥ pulaho marīciḥ kaśyapaḥ prabhuḥ||18||

19bhṛguratrirvasiṣṭhaśca gautamo'tha tathāṅgirāḥ
|
pulastyaśca katuścaiva prahlādaḥ kardamastathā||19||

20atharvāṅgirasaścaiva vālakhilyā marīcipāḥ
|
mano'ntarikṣaṃ vidyāśca vāyustejo jalaṃ mahī||20||

21śabdasparśau tathā rūpaṃ raso gandhaśca bhārata
|
prakṛtiśca vikāraśca yaccānyatkāraṇaṃ bhuvaḥ||21||

22agastyaśca mahātejā mārkaṇḍeyaśca vīryavān
|
jamadagnirbharadvājaḥ saṃvartaścyavanastathā||22||

23durvāsāśca mahābhāga ṛṣyaśṛṅgaśca dhārmikaḥ
|
sanatkumāro bhagavānyogācāryo mahātapāḥ||23||

24asito devalaścaiva jaigīṣavyaśca tattvavit
|
ṛṣabho jitaśatruśca mahāvīryastathā maṇiḥ||24||

25āyurvedastathāṣṭāṅgo dehavāṃstatra bhārata
|
candramāḥ saha nakṣatrairādityaśca gabhastimān||25||

26vāyavaḥ kratavaścaiva saṅkalpaḥ prāṇa eva ca
|
mūrtimanto mahātmāno mahāvrataparāyaṇāḥ||26||

27ete cānye ca bahavo brahmāṇaṃ samupasthitāḥ
|
artho dharmaśca kāmaśca harṣo dveṣastapo damaḥ||27||

28āyānti tasyāṃ sahitā gandharvāpsarasāṃ gaṇāḥ
|
viṃśatiḥ sapta caivānye lokapālāśca sarvaśaḥ||28||

29śukro bṛhaspatiścaiva budho'ṅgāraka eva ca
|
śanaiścaraśca rāhuśca grahāḥ sarve tathaiva ca||29||

30mantro rathantaraṃ caiva harimānvasumānapi
|
ādityāḥ sādhirājāno nāmadvandvairudāhṛtāḥ||30||

31maruto viśvakarmā ca vasavaścaiva bhārata
|
tathā pitṛgaṇāḥ sarve sarvāṇi ca havīṃṣyatha||31||

32ṛgvedaḥ sāmavedaśca yajurvedaśca pāṇḍava
|
atharvavedaśca tathā sarvaśāstrāṇi caiva ha||32||

33itihāsopavedāśca vedāṅgāni ca sarvaśaḥ
|
grahā yajñāśca somaśca devatāścāpi sarvaśaḥ||33||

34sāvitrī durgataraṇī vāṇī saptavidhā tathā
|
medhā dhṛtiḥ śrutiścaiva prajñā buddhiryaśaḥ kṣamā||34||

35sāmāni stutiśastrāṇi gāthāśca vividhāstathā
|
bhāṣyāṇi tarkayuktāni dehavanti viśāṃpate||35||

36nāṭakā vividhāḥ kāvyāḥ kathākhyāyikakārikāḥ
|
tatra tiṣṭhanti te puṇyā ye cānye gurupūjakāḥ||36||

37kṣaṇā lavā muhūrtāśca divā rātristathaiva ca
|
ardhamāsāśca māsāśca ṛtavaḥ ṣaṭ ca bhārata||37||

38saṃvatsarāḥ pañcayugamahorātraścaturvidhaḥ
|
kālacakraṃ ca taddivyaṃ nityamakṣayamavyayam||38||

39dharmacakraṃ tathā cāpi nityamāste yudhiṣṭhira
|
aditirditirdanuścaiva surasā vinatā irā||39||

40kālikā surabhī devī saramā cātha gautamī||40||

41prabhā kadrūśca vai devyau devatānāṃ ca mātaraḥ
|
rudrāṇī śrīśca lakṣmīśca bhadrā ṣaṣṭhī tathā'parā||41||

42pṛthivī gāṃ gatā devī hrīḥ svāhā kīrtireva ca
|
surā devī śacī caiva tathā puṣṭirarundhatī||42||

43saṃvṛttirāśā niyatiḥ sṛṣṭirdevī ratistathā
|
etāścānyāśca vai devya upatasthuḥ prajāpatim||43||

44ādityā vasavo rudrā marutaścāśvināvapi
|
viśvedevāśca sādhyāśca pitaraśca manojavāḥ||44||

45pitṝṇāṃ ca gaṇānviddhi saptaiva puruṣarṣabha
|
mūrtimanto vai catvārastrayaścāpi śarīriṇaḥ||45||

46vairājaśca mahābhāgā agniṣvāttāśca bhārata
|
gārhapatyā nākacarāḥ pitaro lokaviśrutāḥ||46||

47somapā ekaśṛṅgāśca caturvedāḥ kalāstathā
|
ete caturṣu varṇeṣu pūjyante pitaro nṛpa||47||

48etairāpyāyitaiḥ purvaṃ somaścāpyāyyate punaḥ
|
ta ete pitaraḥ sarve prajāpatimupasthitāḥ||48||

49upāsate ca saṃhṛṣṭā brahmāṇamamitaujasam
|
rākṣasāśca piśācāśca dānavā guhyakāstathā||49||

50nāgāḥ suparṇāḥ paśavaḥ pitāmahamupāsate
|
sthāvarā jaṅgamāścaiva mahābhūtāstathā'pare||50||

51purandaraśca devendro varuṇo dhanado yamaḥ
|
mahādevaḥ sahomo'tra sadā gacchati sarvaśaḥ||51||

52mahāsenaśca rājendra sadopāste pitāmaham
|
devo nārāyaṇastasyāṃ tathā devarṣayaśca ye||52||

53ṛṣayo vālakhilyāśca yonijā'yonijāstathā
|
yacca kiñcitriloke'smindṛśyate sthāṇu jaṅgamam
|
sarvaṃ tasyāṃ mayā dṛṣṭamiti viddhi narādhipa||53||

54aṣṭāśītisahasrāṇi ṛṣīṇāmūrdhvaretasām
|
prajāvatāṃ ca pañcāśadṛṣīṇāmapi pāṇḍava||54||

55te sma tatra yathākāmaṃ dṛṣṭvā sarve divaukasaḥ
|
praṇamya śirasā tasmai sarve yānti yathāgamam||55||

56atithīnāgatāndevāndaityānnāgāṃstathā dvijān
|
yakṣānsuparṇānkāleyāngandharvāpsarasastathā||56||

57mahābhāgānamitadhīrbrahmā lokapitāmahaḥ
|
dayāvānsarvabhūteṣu yathārhaṃ pratipadyate||57||

58pratigṛhya tu viśvātmā svayambhūramitadyutiḥ
|
sāntvamānārthasambhogairyunakti manujādhipa||58||

59tathā tairupayātaiśca pratiyadbhiśca bhārata
|
ākulā sā sabhā tāta bhavati sma sukhapradā||59||

60sarvatejomayī divyā brahmarṣigaṇasevitā
|
brāhayā śriyā dīpyamānā śuśubhe vigataklamā||60||

61sā sabhā tādṛśī dṛṣṭā mayā lokeṣu durlabhā
|
sabheyaṃ rājaśārdūla manuṣyeṣu yathā tava||61||

62etā mayā dṛṣṭapūrvāḥ sabhā deveṣu bhārata
|
sabheyaṃ mānuṣe loke sarvaśreṣṭhatamā tava||62||
