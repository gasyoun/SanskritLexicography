Mahābhārata 12.268 (Śāntiparvan)

1yudhiṣṭhira uvāca|
avirodhena bhūtānāṃ yogaḥ ṣāḍguṇyakārakaḥ
|
yaḥ syādubhayabhāgdharmastanme brūhi pitāmaha ||1||

2gārhasthyasya ca dharmasya yogadharmasya cobhayoḥ
|
adūrasamprasthitayoḥ kiṃsvicchreyaḥ pitāmaha ||2||

3bhīṣma uvāca|
ubhau dharmau mahābhāgāvubhau paramaduścarau
|
ubhau mahāphalau tau tu sadbhirācaritāvubhau ||3||

4atra te vartayiṣyāmi prāmāṇyamubhayostayoḥ
|
śṛṇuṣvaikamanāḥ pārtha cchinnadharmārthasaṃśayam ||4||

5atrāpyudāharantīmamitihāsaṃ purātanam
|
kapilasya gośca saṃvādaṃ tannibodha yudhiṣṭhira ||5||

6āmnāyamanupaśyanhi purāṇaṃ śāśvataṃ dhruvam
|
nahuṣaḥ pūrvamālebhe tvaṣṭurgāmiti naḥ śrutam ||6||

7tāṃ niyuktāmadīnātmā sattvasthaḥ saṃyame rataḥ
|
jñānavānniyatāhāro dadarśa kapilastathā ||7||

8sa buddhimuttamāṃ prāpto naiṣṭhikīmakutobhayām
|
satīmaśithilā satyāṃ vedā3 ityabravīt sakṛt ||8||

9tāṃ gāmṛṣiḥ syūmaraśmiḥ praviśya yatimabravīt
|
haṃho vedā3 yadi matā dharmāḥ kenāpare matāḥ ||9||

10tapasvino dhṛtimantaḥ śrutivijñānacakṣuṣaḥ
|
sarvamārṣaṃ hi manyante vyāhṛtaṃ viditātmanaḥ ||10||

11tasyaivaṃ gatatṛṣṇasya vijvarasya nirāśiṣaḥ
|
kā vivakṣā'sti vedeṣu nirārambhasya sarvataḥ ||11||

12kapila uvāca |
nāhaṃ vedānvinindāmi na vivakṣyāmi karhicit
|
pṛthagāśramiṇāṃ karmāṇyekārthānīti naḥ śrutam ||12||

13gacchatyeva parityāgī vānaprasthaśca gacchati
|
gṛhastho brahmacārī ca ubhau tāvapi gacchataḥ ||13||

14devayānā hi panthānaścatvāraḥ śāśvatā matāḥ
|
eṣāṃ jyāyaḥ kanīyastvaṃ phaleṣūktaṃ balābalam ||14||

15evaṃ viditvā sarvārthānārabheteti vaidikam
|
nārabheteti cānyatra naiṣṭhikī śrūyate śrutiḥ ||15||

16anārambhe hyadoṣaḥ syādārāmbhe doṣa uttamaḥ
|
evaṃ sthitasya śāstrasya durvijñeyaṃ balābalam ||16||

17yadyatra kiñcitpratyakṣamahiṃsāyāḥ paraṃ matam
|
ṛte tvāgamaśāstrebhyo brūhi tadyadi paśyasi ||17||

18syūmaraśmiruvāca |
svargakāmo yajeteti satataṃ śrūyate śrutiḥ
|
phalaṃ prakalpya pūrvaṃ hi tato yajñaḥ pratāyate ||18||

19ajaścāśvaśca meṣaśca gauśca pakṣigaṇāśca ye
|
grāmyāraṇyāścauṣadhayaḥ prāṇasyānnamiti śrutiḥ ||19||

20tathaivānnaṃ hyaharahaḥ sāyaṃprātarnirūpyate
|
paśavaścātha dhānyaṃ ca yajñasyāṅgamiti śrutiḥ ||20||

21etāni saha yajñena prajāpatikalpayat
|
tena prajāpatirdevān yajñenāyajata prabhuḥ ||21||

22tadanyonyavarāḥ sarve prāṇinaḥ sapta saptadhā
|
yajñeṣūpākṛtaṃ viśvaṃ prāhuruttamasaṃjñitam ||22||

23etaccaivābhyanujñātaṃ pūrvaiḥ pūrvataraistathā
|
ko jātu na vicinvīta vidvānsvāṃ śaktimātmanaḥ ||23||

24paśavaśca manuṣyāśca drumāścauṣadhibhiḥ saha
|
svargamevābhikāṅkṣante na ca svargastato makhāt ||24||

25oṣadhyaḥ paśavo vṛkṣā vīrudājyaṃ payo dadhi
|
havirbhūmirdiśaḥ śraddhā kālaścaitāni dvādaśa ||25||

26ṛco yajūṃṣi sāmāni yajamānaśca ṣoḍaśa
|
agnirjñeyo gṛhapatiḥ sa saptadaśa ucyate ||26||

27aṅgānyetāni yajñasya yajño mūlamiti śrutiḥ
|
ājyena payasā dadhnā śakṛtā''mikṣayā tvacā ||27||

28vālaiḥ śṛṅgeṇa pādena sambhavatyeva gaurmakham
|
evaṃ pratyekaśaḥ sarvaṃ yadyadasya vidhīyate ||28||

29yajñaṃ vahanti sambhūya sahartvigbhiḥ sadakṣiṇaiḥ
|
saṃhṛtyaitāni sarvāṇi yajñaṃ nivartayantyuta ||29||

30yajñārthāni hi sṛṣṭāni yathārthā śrūyate śrutiḥ
|
evaṃ pūrvatarāḥ sarve pravṛttāścaiva mānavāḥ ||30||

31na hinasti nārabhate nābhidruhyati kiṃcana
|
yajño yaṣṭavya ityeva yo yajatyaphalepsayā ||31||

32yajñāṅgānyapi caitāni yajñoktānyanupūrvaśaḥ
|
vidhinā vidhiyuktāni dhārayanti parasparam ||32||

33āmnāyamārṣaṃ paśyāmi yasminvedāḥ pratiṣṭhitāḥ
|
taṃ vidvāṃso'nupaśyanti brāhmaṇasyānudarśanāt ||33||

34brāhmaṇaprabhavo yajño brāhmaṇārpaṇa eva ca
|
anuyajñaṃ jagatsarvaṃ yajñaścānujagatsadā ||34||

35omiti brahmaṇo yonirnamaḥ svāhā svadhā vaṣaṭ
|
yasyaitāni prayujyante yathāśakti kṛtānyapi ||35||

36na tasya triṣu lokeṣu paralokabhayaṃ viduḥ
|
iti vedā vadantīha siddhāśca paramarṣayaḥ ||36||

37ṛco yajūṃṣi sāmāni stobhāśca vidhicoditāḥ
|
yasminnetāni sarvāṇi bhavantīha sa vai dvijaḥ ||37||

38agnyādheye yadbhavati yacca some sute dvija
|
yaccetarairmahāyajñairveda tadbhagavān punaḥ ||38||

39tasmādbrahmanyajeccaiva yājayeccāvicārayan
|
yajataḥ svargavidhinā pretya svargaphalaṃ mahat ||39||

40nāyaṃ loko'styayajñānāṃ paraśceti viniścayaḥ
|
vedavādavidaścaiva pramāṇamubhayaṃ tadā ||40||
