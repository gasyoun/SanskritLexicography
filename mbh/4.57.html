Mahābhārata 4.57 (Virāṭaparvan)

1vaiśampāyana uvāca|dṛṣṭvā vyūḍhānyanīkāni kurūṇāṃ kurunandana
|
tatra vairādimāmantrya pārtho vacanamabravīt ||1||

2jāmbūnadamayī vedī dhvaje yasya pradṛśyate
|
tasya dakṣiṇato yāhi kṛpaḥ śāradvato yataḥ ||2||

3vaiśampāyana uvāca|dhanañjayavacaḥ śrutvā vairāṭistvaritastataḥ
|
hayān rajatasaṃkāśān hemabhāṇḍānacodayat ||3||

4ānupūrvyāttu tat sarvamāsthāya javamuttamam
|
prāhiṇoccandrasaṃkāśān kupitāniva tān hayān ||4||

5sa gatvā kurusenāyāḥ samīpaṃ hayakovidaḥ
|
punarāvartayāmāsa tān hayān vātaraṃhasaḥ ||5||

6pradakṣiṇamupāvṛtya maṇḍalaṃ savyameva ca
|
kurūn saṃmohayāmāsa matsyo yānena tattvavit ||6||

7kṛpasya rathamāsthāya vairāṭirakutobhayaḥ
|
pradakṣiṇamupāvṛtya tasthau tasyāgrato balī ||7||

8tato'rjunaḥ śaṅkhavaraṃ devadattaṃ mahāravam
|
pradadhmau balamāsthāya nāma viśrāvya cātmanaḥ ||8||

9tasya śabdo mahānāsīddhamyamānasya jiṣṇunā
|
tathā vīryavatā saṃkhye parvatasyeva dīryataḥ ||9||

10pūjayāñcakrire śaṅkhaṃ kuravaḥ sahasainikāḥ
|
arjunena tathā ghmātaḥ śatadhā yanna dīryate ||10||

11divamāvṛtya śabdastu nivṛttaḥ śuśruve punaḥ
|
sṛṣṭo maghavatā vajraḥ prapatanniva parvate ||11||

12etasminnantare vīro balavīryasamanvitaḥ
|
arjunaṃ prati saṃrabdhaḥ kṛpaḥ paramadurjayaḥ
|
amṛṣyamāṇastaṃ śabdaṃ kṛpaḥ śāradvatastadā ||12||

13arjunaṃ prati saṃrabdho yuddhārthī sa mahārathaḥ
|
mahodadhijamādāya dadhmau vegena vīryavān ||13||

14sa tu śabdena lokāṃstrīnāvṛtya rathināṃ varaḥ
|
dhanurādāya sumahajjyāśabdamakarot tadā ||14||

15tau rathau sūryasaṃkāśau yotsyamānau mahābalau
|
śāradāviva jīmūtau vyarocetāṃ vyavasthitau ||15||

16tataḥ śāradvatastūrṇaṃ pārthaṃ daśabhirāśugaiḥ
|
vivyādha paravīraghnaṃ niśitairmarmabhedibhiḥ ||16||

17pārtho'pi viśrutaṃ loke gāṇḍīvaṃ paramāyudham
|
vikṛṣya cikṣepa bahun nārācān marmabhedinaḥ ||17||

18tānaprāptān śitairbāṇairnārācān raktabhojanān
|
kṛpaściccheda pārthasya śataśo'tha sahasraśaḥ ||18||

19tataḥ pārthastu saṃkruddhaścitrān mārgān pradarśayan
|
diśaḥ saṃchādayan bāṇaiḥ pradiśaśca mahārathaḥ
|
ekacchāyamivākāśamakarot sarvataḥ prabhuḥ ||19||

20prācchāda‌meyātmā pārthaḥ śaraśataiḥ kṛpam
|
sa śarairarditaḥ kuddhaḥ śitairagniśikhopamaiḥ ||20||

21tūrṇaṃ daśasahastreṇa pārthamapratimaujasam
|
ardayitvā mahātmānaṃ nanarda samare kṛpaḥ ||21||

22tataḥ kanakaparvāgrairvīraḥ sannataparvabhiḥ
|
tvaran gāṇḍīvanirmuktairarjunastasya vājinaḥ ||22||

23caturbhiścaturastīkṣṇairavidhyat parameṣubhiḥ
|
te hayā niśitairbāṇairjvaladbhiriva pannagaiḥ
|
utpetuḥ sahasā sarve kṛpaḥ sthānādathācyavat ||23||

24cyutaṃ tu gautamaṃ sthānāt samīkṣya kurunandanaḥ
|
nāvidhyat paravīraghno rakṣamāṇo'sya gauravam ||24||

25sa tu labdhvā punaḥ sthānaṃ gautamaḥ savyasācinam
|
vivyādha daśabhirbāṇaistvaritaḥ kaṅkapatribhiḥ ||25||

26tataḥ pārtho dhanustasya bhallena niśitena ha
|
cicchedaikena bhūyaśca hastāvāpamathāharat ||26||

27athāsya kavacaṃ bāṇairniśitairmarmabhedibhiḥ
|
vyadhamanna ca pārtho'sya śarīramavapīḍayat ||27||

28tasya nirmucyamānasya kavacāt kāya ābabhau
|
samaye mucyamānasya sarpasyeva tanuryathā ||28||

29chinne dhanuṣi pārthena so'nyadādāya kārmukam
|
cakāra gautamaḥ sajyaṃ tada‌dbhutamivābhavat ||29||

30sa tadapyasya kaunteyaściccheda nataparvaṇā
|
evamanyāni cāpāni bahūni kṛtahastavat ||30||

31śāradvatasya ciccheda pāṇḍavaḥ paravīrahā
|
sa chinnadhanurādāya rathaśaktiṃ pratāpavān ||31||

32prāhiṇot pāṇḍuputrāya pradīptāmaśanīmiva
|
tāmarjunastadā''yāntīṃ śaktiṃ hemavibhūṣitām ||32||

33viyadgatāṃ maholkābhāṃ ciccheda daśabhiḥ śaraiḥ
|
sā'pataddaśadhā chinnā bhūmau pārthena dhīmatā ||33||

34yugapaccaiva bhallaistu tataḥ sajyadhanuḥ kṛpaḥ
|
tamāśu niśitaiḥ pārthaṃ bibheda daśabhiḥ śaraiḥ ||34||

35tataḥ pārtho mahātejā viśikhānagnitejasaḥ
|
cikṣepa samare kruddhastrayodaśa śilāśitān ||35||

36athāsya yugamekena caturbhiścaturo hayān
|
ṣaṣṭhena ca śiraḥ kāyācchareṇa rathasāratheḥ ||36||

37tribhistriveṇuṃ samare dvābhyāmakṣaṃ mahārathaḥ
|
dvādaśena tu bhallena cakartāsya dhvajaṃ tadā ||37||

38tato vajranikāśena phālgunaḥ prahasanniva
|
trayodaśenendrasamaḥ kṛpaṃ vakṣasyavidhyata ||38||

39sa chinnadhanvā viratho hatāśvo hatasārathiḥ
|
gadāpāṇiravaplutya tūrṇaṃ cikṣepa tāṃ gadām ||39||

40sā ca muktā gadā gurvī kṛpeṇa supariṣkṛtā
|
arjunena śarairnunnā pratimārgamathāgamat ||40||

41taṃ tu yodhāḥ parīpsantaḥ śāradvatamamarṣaṇam
|
sarvataḥ samare pārthaṃ śaravarṣairavākiran ||41||

42tato virāṭasya suto savyamāvṛtya vājinaḥ
|
yamakaṃ maṇḍalaṃ kṛtvā tān yodhān pratyavārayat ||42||

43tataḥ kṛpamupādāya virathaṃ te nararṣabhāḥ
|
apajahrurmahāvegāḥ kuntīputrāddhanañjayāt ||43||
