Mahābhārata 7.39 (Droṇaparvan)

1dhṛtarāṣṭra uvāca|
dvaidhībhavati me cittaṃ hriyā tuṣṭyā ca sañjaya
|
mama putrasya yatsainyaṃ saubhadraḥ samavārayat||1||

2vistareṇaiva me śaṃsa sarvaṃ gāvalgaṇe punaḥ
|
vikrīḍitaṃ kumārasya skandasyevāsuraiḥ saha||2||

3sañjaya uvāca|
hanta te saṃpravakṣyāmi vimardamatidāruṇam
|
ekasya ca bahūnāṃ ca yathā''sīt tumulo raṇaḥ||3||

4abhimanyuḥ kṛtotsāhaḥ kṛtotsāhānarindamān
|
rathastho rathinaḥ sarvāṃstāvakānabhyavarṣayat||4||

5droṇaṃ karṇaṃ kṛpaṃ śalyaṃ drauṇiṃ bhojaṃ bṛhadbalam
|
duryodhanaṃ saumadattiṃ śakuniṃ ca mahābalam||5||

6nānānṛpānnṛpasutān sainyāni vividhāni ca
|
alātacakravatsarvāṃścaran bāṇaiḥ samārpayat||6||

7nighnannamitrān saubhadraḥ paramāstraiḥ pratāpavān
|
adarśayata tejasvī dikṣu sarvāsu bhārata||7||

8taddṛṣṭvā caritaṃ tasya saubhadrasyāmitaujasaḥ
|
samakampanta sainyāni tvadīyāni sahasraśaḥ||8||

9athābravīnmahāprājño bhāradvājaḥ pratāpavān
|
harṣeṇotphullanayanaḥ kṛpamābhāṣya satvaram||9||

10ghaṭayanniva marmāṇi putrasya tava bhārata
|
abhimanyuṃ raṇe dṛṣṭvā tadā raṇaviśāradam||10||

11eṣa gacchati saubhadraḥ pārthānāṃ prathito yuvā
|
nandayan suhṛdaḥ sarvān rājānaṃ ca yudhiṣṭhiram||11||

12nakulaṃ sahadevaṃ ca bhīmasenaṃ ca pāṇḍavam
|
bandhūn sambandhinaścānyān madhyasthān suhṛdastathā||12||

13nāsya yuddhe samaṃ manye kañcidanyaṃ dhanurdharam
|
icchan hanyādimāṃ senāṃ kimarthamapi necchati||13||

14droṇasya prītisaṃyuktaṃ śrutvā vākyaṃ tavātmajaḥ
|
ārjuniṃ prati saṃkruddho droṇaṃ dṛṣṭvā smayanniva||14||

15atha duryodhanaḥ karṇamabravīdbāhlikaṃ nṛpaḥ
|
duḥśāsanaṃ madrarājaṃ tāṃstathā'nyān mahārathān||15||

16sarvamūrdhābhiṣiktānāmācāryo brahṛvittamaḥ
|
arjunasya sutaṃ mūḍhaṃ nāyaṃ hantumihecchati||16||

17na hyasya samare yuddhyedantako'pyātatāyinaḥ
|
kimaṅga punarevānyo martyaḥ satyaṃ bravīmi vaḥ||17||

18arjunasya sutaṃ tveṣa śiṣyatvā‌dabhirakṣati
|
śiṣyāḥ putrāśca dayitāstadapatyaṃ ca dharmiṇām||18||

19saṃrakṣyamāṇo droṇena manyate vīryamātmanaḥ
|
ātmasaṃbhāvito mūḍhastaṃ pramathnīta mā ciram||19||

20evamuktāstu te rājñā sātvatīputramabhyayuḥ
|
saṃrabdhāste jighāṃsanto bhāradvājasya paśyataḥ||20||

21duḥśāsanastu tacchrutvā duryodhanavacastadā
|
abravīt kuruśārdūla duryodhanamidaṃ vacaḥ||21||

22ahamenaṃ haniṣyāmi mahārāja bravīmi te
|
miṣatāṃ pāṇḍuputrāṇāṃ pañcālānāṃ ca paśyatām||22||

23grasiṣyāmyadya saubhadraṃ yathā rāhurdivākaram
|
utkruśya cābravīdvākyaṃ kururājamidaṃ punaḥ||23||

24śrutvā kṛṣṇau mayā grastaṃ saubhadramatimāninau
|
gamiṣyataḥ pretalokaṃ jīvalokānna saṃśayaḥ||24||

25tau ca śrutvā mṛtau vyaktaṃ pāṇḍoḥ kṣetrodbhavāḥ sutāḥ
|
ekāhnā sasuhṛdvargāḥ klaibyāddhāsyanti jīvitam||25||

26tasmādasmin hate rātrau hatāḥ sarve'hitāsta
|
śivena māṃ dhyāhi rājanneṣa hanmi ripūstava||26||

27evamuktvā'nadadrājan putro duḥśāsanastava
|
saubhadramabhyayāt kruddhaḥ śaravarṣairavākiran||27||

28tamatikruddhamāyāntaṃ tava putramarindamaḥ
|
abhimanyuḥ śaraistīkṣṇaiḥ ṣaḍviṃśatyā samārpayat||28||

29duḥśāsanastu saṃkruddhaḥ prabhinna iva kuñjaraḥ
|
ayodhayata saubhadramabhimanyuśca taṃ raṇe||29||

30tau maṇḍalāni citrāṇi rathābhyāṃ savyadakṣiṇam
|
caramāṇāvayudhyetāṃ rathaśikṣāviśāradau||30||

31atha paṇavamṛdaṅgadundubhīnāṃ krakacamahānakabherijharjharāṇām
|
ninadamatibhṛśaṃ narāḥ pracakrurlavaṇajalodbhavasiṃhanādamiśram||31||
