Mahābhārata 7.114 (Droṇaparvan)

1dhṛtarāṣṭra uvāca|
evaṃ bahuguṇaṃ sainyamevaṃ pravicitaṃ balam
|
vyūḍhamevaṃ yathānyāyamevaṃ bahu ca sañjaya ||1||

2nityaṃ pūjitamasmābhirabhikāmaṃ ca naḥ sadā
|
prauḍhamatyadbhutākāraṃ purastād dṛṣṭavikramam ||2||

3nātivṛddhamabālaṃ ca nākṛśaṃ nātipīvaram
|
laghuvṛttāyataprāyaṃ sāragātramanāmayam ||3||

4āttasannāhasaṃchannaṃ bahuśastraparicchadam
|
śastragrahaṇavidyāsu bahvīṣu pariniṣṭhitam ||4||

5ārohe paryavaskande saraṇe sāntaraplute
|
samyak praharaṇe yāne vyapayāne ca kovidam ||5||

6nāgebhyaśveṣu bahuśo ratheṣu ca parīkṣitam
|
parīkṣya ca yathānyāyaṃ vetanenopapāditam ||6||

7na goṣṭhyā nopakāreṇa na saṃbandhanimittataḥ
|
nānāhūtaṃ nāpyabhṛtaṃ mama sainyaṃ babhūva ha ||7||

8kulīnāryajanopetaṃ tuṣṭapuṣṭamanuddhatam
|
kṛtamānopacāraṃ ca yaśasvi ca manasvi ca ||8||

9sacivaiścāparairmukhyairbahubhiḥ puṇyakarmabhiḥ
|
lokapālopamaistāta pālitaṃ narasattamaiḥ ||9||

10bahubhiḥ pārthivairguptamasmatpriyacikīrṣubhiḥ
|
asmānabhisṛtaiḥ kāmān sabalaiḥ sapadānugaiḥ||10||

11mahodadhimivāpūrṇamāpagābhiḥ samantataḥ
|
apakṣaiḥ pakṣisaṃkāśai rathairaśvaiśca savṛttam ||11||

12prabhinnakaraṭaiścaiva dviradairāvṛtaṃ mahat
|
yadahanyata me sainyaṃ kimanyadbhāgadheyataḥ||12||

13yodhākṣayyajalaṃ bhīmaṃ vāhanormitaraṅgiṇam
|
kṣepaṇyasigadāśaktiśaraprāsajhaṣākulam||13||

14dhvajabhūṣaṇasaṃbādharatnopalasusaṃcitam
|
vāhanairabhidhāvadbhirvāyuvegavikaṃpitam ||14||

15droṇagaṃbhīrapātālaṃ kṛtavarma mahāhradam
|
jalasandhamahāgrāhaṃ karṇacandrodayoddhatam ||15||

16gate sainyārṇavaṃ bhittvā tarasā pāṇḍavarṣabhe
|
sañjayaikarathenaiva yuyudhāne ca māmakam ||16||

17tatra śeṣaṃ na paśyāmi praviṣṭe savyasācini
|
sātvate va rathodāre mama sainyasya sañjaya ||17||

18tau tatra samatikrāntau dṛṣṭvātīva tarasvinau
|
sindhurājaṃ tu saṃprekṣya gāṇḍīvasyeṣugocare ||18||

19kiṃ nu vā kuravaḥ kṛtyaṃ vida‌dhuḥ kālacoditāḥ
|
dāruṇaikāyane'kāle kathaṃ vā pratipedire ||19||

20grastān hi kauravān manye mṛtyunā tāta saṃgatān
|
vikramo'pi raṇe teṣāṃ na tathā dṛśyate hi vai||20||

21akṣatau saṃyuge tatra praviṣṭau kṛṣṇapāṇḍavau
|
na ca vārayitā kaścittayorastīha sañjaya ||21||

22bhṛtāśca bahavo yodhāḥ parīkṣyaiva mahārathāḥ
|
vetanena yathāyogaṃ priyavādena cāpare ||22||

23asatkārabhūtastāta mama sainye na vidyate
|
karmaṇā hyanurūpeṇa labhyate bhaktavetanam ||23||

24na cāyodho'bhavat kaścinmama sainye tu sañjaya
|
alpadānabhṛtastāta tathā cābhṛtako naraḥ ||24||

25pūjito hi yathāśaktyā dānamānāsanairmayā
|
tathā putraiśca me tāta jñātibhiśca sabāndhavaiḥ||25||

26te ca prāpyaiva saṃgrāme nirjitāḥ savyasācinā
|
śainayana parāmṛṣṭāḥ kimanyadbhāgadheyataḥ ||26||

27rakṣyate yaśca saṃgrāme ye ca sañjaya rakṣiṇaḥ
|
ekaḥ sādhāraṇaḥ panthā rakṣyasya saha rakṣibhiḥ||27||

28arjunaṃ samare dṛṣṭvā saindhavasyāgrataḥ sthitam
|
putro mama bhṛśaṃ mūḍhaḥ kiṃ kāryaṃ pratyapadyata ||28||

29sātyakiṃ ca raṇe dṛṣṭvā praviśantamabhītavat
|
kiṃ nu duryodhanaḥ kṛtyaṃ prāptakālamamanyata ||29||

30sarvaśastrātigau senāṃ praviṣṭau rathisattamau
|
dṛṣṭvā kāṃ vai dhṛtiṃ yuddhe pratyapadyanta māmakāḥ ||30||

31dṛṣṭvā kṛṣṇaṃ tu dāśārhamarjunārthe vyavasthitam
|
śinīnāmṛṣabhaṃ caiva manye śocānti putrakāḥ ||31||

32dṛṣṭvā senāṃ vyatikrāntāṃ sātvatenārjunena ca
|
palāyamānāṃśca kurūn manye śocanti putrakāḥ||32||

33vidrutān rathino dṛṣṭvā nirutsāhān dviṣajjaye
|
palāyanakṛtotsāhānmanye śocanti putrakāḥ||33||

34śūnyān kṛtānrathopasthānsātvatenārjunena ca
|
hatāṃśca yodhān saṃdṛśya manye śocanti putrakāḥ||34||

35vyaśvanāgarathān dṛṣṭvā tatra vīrān sahasraśaḥ
|
dhāvamānānraṇevyagrānmanye śocanti putrakāḥ||35||

36mahānāgān vidravato dṛṣṭvā'rjunaśarāhatān
|
patitānpatataścānyānmanye śocanti putrakāḥ||36||

37vihīnāṃśca kṛtānaśvān virathāṃścā kṛtānnarān
|
tatra sātyakipārthābhyāṃ manye śocanti putrakāḥ||37||

38hayaughānnihatān dṛṣṭvā dravamāṇāṃstatastataḥ
|
raṇe mādhavapārthābhyāṃ manye śocanti putrakāḥ||38||

39pattisaṃghān raṇe dṛṣṭvā dhāvamānāṃśca sarvaśaḥ
|
nirāśā vijaye sarve manye śocanti putrakāḥ||39||

40droṇasya samatikrāntāvanīkamaparājitau
|
kṣaṇena dṛṣṭvā tau vīrau manye śocanti putrakāḥ||40||

41saṃmūḍho'smi bhṛśaṃ tāta śrutvā kṛṣṇadhanañjayau
|
praviṣṭau māmakaṃ sainyaṃ sātvatena sahācyutau||41||

42tasmin praviṣṭe pṛtanāṃ śinīnāṃ pravare rathe
|
bhojānīkaṃ vyatikrānte kimakurvata kauravāḥ||42||

43tathā droṇena samare nigṛhīteṣu pāṇḍuṣu
|
kathaṃ yuddhamabhūt tatra tanmamācakṣva sañjaya ||43||

44droṇo hi balavān śreṣṭhaḥ kṛtāstro yuddhadurmadaḥ
|
pañcālāṃste maheṣvāsaṃ pratyavidhyan kathaṃ raṇe||44||

45baddhavairāstato droṇe dhanañjayajayaiṣiṇaḥ
|
bhāradvājasutasteṣu dṛḍhavairo mahārathaḥ ||45||

46arjunaścāpi yaccakre sindhurājavadhaṃ prati
|
tanme sarvaṃ samācakṣva kuśalo hyasi sañjaya ||46||

47sañjaya uvāca|
ātmāparādhāt saṃbhūtaṃ vyasanaṃ bharatarṣabha
|
prāpya prākṛtavadvīra na tvaṃ śocitumarhasi||47||

48purā yaducyase prājñaiḥ sahadbhirvidurādibhiḥ
|
mā hārṣīḥ pāṇḍavān rājanniti tanna tvayā śrutam ||48||

49suhṛdāṃ hitakāmānāṃ vākyaṃ yo na śṛṇoti ha
|
sa mahadvyasanaṃ prāpya śocate vai yathā bhavān ||49||

50yācito'si purā rājan dāśārheṇa śamaṃ prati
|
na ca taṃ labdhavān kāmaṃ tvattaḥ kṛṣṇo mahāyaśāḥ ||50||

51tava nirguṇatāṃ jñātvā pakṣapātaṃ suteṣu ca
|
dvaidhībhāvaṃ tathā dharme pāṇḍaveṣu ca matsaram ||51||

52tava jihmamabhiprāyaṃ viditvā pāṇḍavān prati
|
ārtapralāpāṃśca bahūnmanujādhipasattama ||52||

53sarvalokasya tattvajñaḥ sarvalokeśvaraḥ prabhuḥ
|
vāsudevastato yuddhaṃ kurūṇāmakaronmahat||53||

54ātmāparādhāt sumahānprāptaste vipulaḥ kṣayaḥ
|
nainaṃ duryodhane doṣaṃ kartumarhasi mānada ||54||

55na hi te sukṛtaṃ kiñcidādau madhye ca bhārata
|
dṛśyate pṛṣṭhataścaiva tvanmūlo hi parājayaḥ||55||

56tasmādavasthito bhūtvā jñātvā lokasya nirṇayam
|
śruṇu yuddhaṃ yathāvṛttaṃ ghoraṃ devāsuropamam ||56||

57praviṣṭe tava sainyaṃ tu śaineye satyavikrame
|
bhīmasenamukhāḥ pārthāḥ pratīyurvāhinīṃ tava||57||

58āgacchatastān sahasā kruddharūpān sahānugān
|
dadhāraiko raṇe pāṇḍūn kṛtavarmā mahārathaḥ ||58||

59yathoddhṛttaṃ vārayate velā vai salilārṇavam
|
pāṇḍusainyaṃ tathā saṃkhye hārdikyaḥ sapnavārayat||59||

60tatrādbhutamapaśyāma hārdikyasya parākramam
|
yadenaṃ sahitāḥ pārthā nāticakramurāhave||60||

61tato bhīmastribhirviddhvā kṛtavarmāṇamāśugaiḥ
|
śaṅkhaṃ dadhmau mahābāhurharṣayan sarvapāṇḍavān ||61||

62sahadevastu viṃśatyā dharmarājaśca pañcabhiḥ
|
śatena nakulaścāpi hārdikyaṃ samavidhyata||62||

63draupadeyāstrisaptatyā saptabhiśca ghaṭotkacaḥ
|
dhṛṣṭadyumnastribhiścāpi kṛtavarmāṇamārdayat ||63||

64virāṭo drupada‌ścaiva yājñaseniśca pañcabhiḥ
|
śikhaṃḍī caiva hārdikyaṃ viddhvā pañcabhirāśugaiḥ||64||

65punarvivyādha viṃśatyā sāyakānāṃ hasanniva
|
kṛtavarmā tato rājan sarvatastān mahārathān||65||

66ekaikaṃ pañcabhirviddhvā bhīmaṃ vivyādha saptabhiḥ
|
dhanurdhvajaṃ cāsya tadā rathādbhūmāvapātayat||66||

67athainaṃ chinnadhanvānaṃ tvaramāṇo mahārathaḥ
|
ājaghānorasi kruddhaḥ saptatyā niśitaiḥ śaraiḥ||67||

68sa gāḍhaviddho balavān hārdikyasya śarottamaiḥ
|
cacāla rathamadhyasthaḥ kṣitikampe yathā'calaḥ||68||

69bhīmasenaṃ tathā dṛṣṭvā dharmarājapurogamāḥ
|
visṛjantaḥ śarān rājan kṛtavarmāṇamārdayan||69||

70taṃ tathā koṣṭhakīkṛtya rathavaṃśena māriṣa
|
vivyadhuḥ sāyakairhṛṣṭā rakṣārthaṃ mārutermṛdhe||70||

71pratilabhya tataḥ saṃjñāṃ bhīmaseno mahābalaḥ
|
śaktiṃ jagrāha samare hemadaṇḍāmayasmayīm||71||

72cikṣepa ca rathāttūrṇaṃ kṛtavarmarathaṃ prati
|
sā bhīmabhujanirmuktā nirmuktoragasannibhā||72||

73kṛtavarmāṇamabhitaḥ prajajvāla sudāruṇā
|
tāmāpatantīṃ sahasā yugāntāgnisamaprabhām||73||

74dvābhyāṃ śarābhyāṃ hārdikyo nijaghāna dvidhā tadā
|
sā chinnā patitā bhūmau śaktiḥ kanakabhūṣaṇā ||74||

75dyotayantī diśo rājanmaholkeva nabhaścyutā
|
śaktiṃ vinihatāṃ dṛṣṭvā bhīmaścukrodha vai bhṛśam||75||

76tato'nyaddhanurādāya vegavat sumahāsvanam
|
bhīmaseno raṇe kruddho hārdikyaṃ samavārayat ||76||

77athainaṃ pañcabhirbāṇairājaghāna stanāntare
|
bhīmo bhīmabalo rājaṃstava durmantritena ca ||77||

78bhojastu kṣatasarvāṅgo bhīmasenena māriṣa
|
raktāśoka ivotphullo vyabhrājata raṇājire||78||

79tataḥ kruddhastribhirbāṇairbhīmasenaṃ hasanniva
|
abhihatya dṛḍhaṃ yuddhe tān sarvān pratyavidhyata||79||

80tribhistribhirmaheṣvāso yatamānānmahārathān
|
te'pi taṃ pratyavidhyanta saptabhiḥ saptabhiḥ śaraiḥ||80||

81śikhaṇḍinastataḥ kruddhaḥ kṣurapreṇa mahārathaḥ
|
dhanuściccheda samare prahasanniva sātvataḥ||81||

82śikhaṇḍī tu tataḥ kruddhaśchinne dhanuṣi satvaraḥ
|
asiṃ jagrāha samare śatacandraṃ ca bhāsvaram ||82||

83bhramayitvā mahaccarma cāmīkaravibhūṣitam
|
tamasiṃ preṣayāmāsa kṛtavarmarathaṃ prati ||83||

84sa tasya saśaraṃ cāpaṃ chittvā rājan mahānasiḥ
|
abhyagāddharaṇīṃ rājaṃścyutaṃ jyotirivāṃbarāt||84||

85etasminneva kāle tu tvaramāṇaṃ mahārathāḥ
|
vivyadhuḥ sāyakairgāḍhaṃ kṛtavarmāṇamāhave||85||

86athānyaddhanurādāya tyaktvā tacca mahaddhanuḥ
|
viśīrṇaṃ bharataśreṣṭha hārdikyaḥ paravīrahā ||86||

87vivyādha pāṇḍavān yuddhe tribhistribhirajihmagaiḥ
|
śikhaṇḍinaṃ ca vivyādha tribhiḥ pañcabhireva ca ||87||

88dhanuranyat samādāya śikhaṇḍī tu mahāyaśāḥ
|
avārayat kūrmanakhairāśugairhṛdikātmajam||88||

89tataḥ kruddho raṇe rājan hṛdikasyātmasaṃbhavaḥ
|
abhidudrāva vegena yājñaseniṃ mahāratham ||89||

90bhīṣmasya samare rājan mṛtyorhetuṃ mahātmanaḥ
|
vidarśayan balaṃ śūraḥ śārdūla iva kuñjaram ||90||

91tau diśāṃ gajasaṃkāśau jvalitāviva pāvakau
|
samāpetaturanyonyaṃ śarasaṅghairarinda‌mau||91||

92vidhunvānau dhanuḥśreṣṭhe saṃdadhānau ca sāyakān
|
visṛjantau ca śataśo gabhastīniva bhāskarau||92||

93tāpayantau śaraistīkṣṇairanyonyaṃ tau mahārathau
|
yugāntapratimau vīrau rejaturbhāskarāviva ||93||

94kṛtavarmā ca samare yājñaseniṃ mahāratham
|
viddhveṣubhistrisaptatyā punarvivyādha saptabhiḥ||94||

95sa gāḍhaviddho vyathito rathopastha upāviśat
|
visṛjya saśaraṃ cāpaṃ mūrcchayā'bhipariplutaḥ||95||

96taṃ viṣaṇṇaṃ raṇe dṛṣṭvā tāvakāḥ puruṣarṣabha
|
hārdikyaṃ pūjayāmāsurvāsāṃsyādudhuvuśca ha||96||

97śikhaṇḍinaṃ tathā jñātvā hārdikyaśarapīḍitam
|
apovāha raṇādyantā tvaramāṇo mahāratham||97||

98sāditaṃ tu rathopasthe dṛṣṭvā pārthāḥ śikhaṇḍinam
|
parivavrū rathaistūrṇaṃ kṛtavarmāṇamāhave||98||

99tatrādbhutaṃ paraṃ cakre kṛtavarmā mahārathaḥ
|
yadekaḥ samare pārthān vārayāmāsa sānugān||99||

100pārthāñjitvā'jayaccedīn pañcālān sṛñjayānapi
|
kekayāṃśca mahāvīryān kṛtavarmā mahārathaḥ||100||

101te vadhyamānāḥ samare hārdikyena sma pāṇḍavāḥ
|
itaścetaśca dhāvanto naiva cakrurdhṛtiṃ raṇe ||101||

102jitvā pāṇḍusutān yuddhe bhīmasenapurogamān
|
hārdikyaḥ samare'tiṣṭhadvidhūma iva pāvakaḥ ||102||

103te drāvyamāṇāḥ samare hārdikyena mahārathāḥ
|
vimukhāḥ samapadyanta śaravṛṣṭibhirārditāḥ ||103||
