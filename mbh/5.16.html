Mahābhārata 5.16 (Udyogaparvan)

1bṛhaspatiruvāca| tvamagre sarvadevānāṃ mukhaṃ tvamasi havyavāṭ
|
tvamantaḥ sarvabhūtānāṃ gūḍhaścarasi sākṣivat||1||

2tvāmāhurekaṃ kavayastvāmāhustrividhaṃ punaḥ
|
tvayā tyaktaṃ jagaccedaṃ sadyo naśyeddhutāśana ||2||

3kṛtvā tubhyaṃ namo viprāḥ svakarmavijitāṃ gatim
|
gacchanti saha patnībhiḥ sutairapi ca śāśvatīm ||3||

4tvamevāgne havyavāhastvameva paramaṃ haviḥ
|
yajanti satraistvāmeva yajñaiśca paramādhvare ||4||

5sṛṣṭvā lokāṃstrīnimān havyavāha prāpte kāle pacasi punaḥ samiddhaḥ
|
tvaṃ sarvasya bhuvanasya prasūtistvamevāgne bhavasi punaḥ pratiṣṭhā ||5||

6tvāmagne jaladānāhurvidyutaśca manīṣiṇaḥ
|
vahanti sarvabhūtāni tvatto niṣkramya hetayaḥ ||6||

7tvayyāpo nihitāḥ sarvāstvayi sarvamidaṃ jagat
|
na te'styaviditaṃ kiñcit triṣu lokeṣu pāvaka ||7||

8svayoniṃ bhajate sarvo viśasvāpo'viśaṅkitaḥ
|
ahaṃ tvāṃ vardhayiṣyāmi brāhmairmantraiḥ sanātanaiḥ ||8||

9evaṃ stuto havyavāṭ sa bhagavān kaviruttamaḥ
|
bṛhaspatimathovāca prītimān vākyamuttamam||9||

10darśayiṣyāmi te śakraṃ satyametadbravīmi te| śalya uvāca|
praviśyāpastato vahniḥ sasamudrāḥ sapalvalāḥ
|
āsasāda sarastacca gūḍho yatra śatakratuḥ ||10||

11atha tatrāpi padmāni vicinvan bharatarṣabha
|
apaśyat sa tu devendraṃ visamadhyagataṃ tadā ||11||

12āgatya ca tatastūrṇaṃ tamācaṣṭa bṛhaspateḥ
|
aṇumātreṇa vapuṣā padmatantvāśritaṃ prabhum ||12||

13gatvā devarṣigandharvaiḥ sahito'tha bṛhaspatiḥ
|
purāṇaiḥ karmabhirdevaṃ tuṣṭāva balasūdanam ||13||

14mahāsuro hataḥ śakra namucirdāruṇastvayā
|
śambaraśca balaścaiva tathobhau ghoravikramau ||14||

15śatakrato vivardhasva sarvān śatrūnniṣūdaya
|
uttiṣṭha śakra saṃpaśya devarṣīṃśca samāgatān ||15||

16mahendra dānavān hatvā lokāstrātāstvayā vibho
|
apāṃ phenaṃ samāsādya viṣṇutejo'tibṛṃhitam
|
tvayā vṛtro hataḥ pūrvaṃ devarāja jagatpate ||16||

17tvaṃ sarvabhūteṣu śaraṇya īḍyastvayā samaṃ vidyate neha bhūtam
|
tvayā dhāryante sarvabhūtāni śakta tvaṃ devānāṃ mahimānaṃ cakartha ||17||

18pāhi sarvāṃśca lokāṃśca mahendrabalamāpnuhi
|
evaṃ saṃstūyamānaśca so'vardhata śanaiḥ śanaiḥ ||18||

19svaṃ caiva vapurāsthāya babhūva sa balānvitaḥ
|
abravīcca guraṃ devo bṛhaspatimavasthitam ||19||

20kiṃ kāryamavaśiṣṭaṃ vo hatastvāṣṭro mahāsuraḥ
|
vṛtraśca sumahākāyo yo vai lokānanāśayat ||20||

21bṛhaspatiruvāca| mānuṣo nahuṣo rājā devarṣigaṇatejasā
|
devarājyamanuprāptaḥ sarvānno bādhate bhṛśam ||21||

22indra uvāca|
kathaṃ ca nahuṣo rājyaṃ devānāṃ prāpa durlabham
|
tapasā kena vā yuktaḥ kiṃvīryo vā bṛhaspate ||22||

23bṛhaspatiruvāca| devā bhītāḥ śakramakāmayanta tvayā tyaktaṃ mahadaindraṃ padaṃ tat
|
tadā devāḥ pitaro'tharṣayaśca gandharvamukhyāśca sametya sarve ||23||

24gatvā'bruvannahuṣaṃ tatra śakra tvaṃ no rājā bhava bhuvanasya goptā
|
tānabravīnnahuṣo nāsmi śakta āpyāyadhvaṃ tapasā tejasā mām ||24||

25evamuktairvardhitaścāpi devai rājā'bhavannahuṣo ghoravīryaḥ
|
trailokye ca prāpya rājyaṃ maharṣīn kṛtvā vāhān yāti lokān durātmā ||25||

26tejoharaṃ dṛṣṭiviṣaṃ sughoraṃ mā tvaṃ paśyernahuṣaṃ vai kadācit
|
devāśca sarve nahuṣaṃ bhṛśārtā na paśyante gūḍharūpāścarantaḥ ||26||

27śalya uvāca|
evaṃ vadatyaṅgirasāṃ variṣṭhe bṛhaspatau lokapālaḥ kuberaḥ
|
vaivasvataścaiva yamaḥ purāṇo daveśca somo varuṇaścājagāma||27||

28te vai samāgamya mahendramūcurdiṣṭyā tvāṣṭro nihataścaiva vṛtraḥ
|
diṣṭyā ca tvāṃ kuśalinamakṣataṃ ca paśyāmo vai nihatāriṃ ca śakra ||28||

29sa tān yathāvacca hi lokapālān sametya vai prītamanā mahendraḥ
|
uvāca cainān pratibhāṣya śakraḥ saṃcodayiṣyannahuṣasyāntareṇa ||29||

30rājā devānāṃ nahuṣo ghorarūpastatra sāhyaṃ dīyatāṃ me bhavadbhiḥ
|
te cābruvannahuṣo ghorarūpo dṛṣṭīviṣastasya bibhīma īśa ||30||

31tvaṃ cedrājānaṃ nahuṣaṃ parājayestato vayaṃ bhāgamarhāma śakra
|
indro'bravīdbhavatu bhavānapāṃ patiryamaḥ kuberaśca mayābhiṣekam ||31||

32saṃprāpnuvantvadya sahaiva daivatai ripuṃ jayāma taṃ nahuṣaṃ ghoradṛṣṭim
|
tataḥ śakraṃ jvalano'pyāha bhāgaṃ prayaccha mahyaṃ tava sāhyaṃ kariṣye
|
tamāha śakro bhavitā'gne tavāpi cendrāgnyorvai bhāga eko mahākratau||32||

33śalya uvāca|
evaṃ sañcintya bhagavān mahendraḥ pākaśāsanaḥ
|
kuberaṃ sarvayakṣāṇāṃ dhanānāṃ ca prabhuṃ tathā ||33||

34vaivasvataṃ pitṝṇāṃ ca varuṇaṃ cāpyapāṃ tathā
|
ādhipatyaṃ dadau śakraḥ sañcintya varadastathā ||34||
