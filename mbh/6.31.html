Mahābhārata 6.31 (Bhīṣmaparvan)

1śrībhagavānuvāca —
mayyāsaktamanāḥ pārtha yogaṃ yuñjanmadāśrayaḥ
|
asaṃśayaṃ samagraṃ māṃ yathā jñāsyasi tacchṛṇu ||1||

2jñānaṃ te'haṃ savijñānamidaṃ vakṣyāmyaśeṣataḥ
|
yajjñātvā neha bhūyo'nyajjñātavyamavaśiṣyate ||2||

3manuṣyāṇāṃ sahasreṣu kaścidyatati siddhaye
|
yatatāmapi siddhānāṃ kaścinmāṃ vetti tattvataḥ ||3||

4bhūmirāpo'nalo vāyuḥ khaṃ mano buddhireva ca
|
ahaṅkāra itīyaṃ me bhinnā prakṛtiraṣṭadhā ||4||

5apareyamitastvanyāṃ prakṛtiṃ viddhi me parām
|
jīvabhūtāṃ mahābāho yayedaṃ dhāryate jagat ||5||

6etadyonīni bhūtāni sarvāṇītyupadhāraya
|
ahaṃ kṛtsnasya jagataḥ prabhavaḥ pralayastathā ||6||

7mattaḥ parataraṃ nānyatkiñcidasti dhanañjaya
|
mayi sarvamidaṃ protaṃ sūtre maṇigaṇā iva ||7||

8raso'hamapsu kaunteya prabhāsmi śaśisūryayoḥ
|
praṇavaḥ sarvavedeṣu śabdaḥ khe pauruṣaṃ nṛṣu ||8||

9puṇyo gandhaḥ pṛthivyāṃ ca tejaścāsmi vibhāvasau
|
jīvanaṃ sarvabhūteṣu tapaścāsmi tapasviṣu ||9||

10bījaṃ māṃ sarvabhūtānāṃ viddhi pārtha sanātanam
|
buddhirbuddhimatāmasmi tejastejasvināmaham ||10||

11balaṃ balavatāṃ cāhaṃ kāmarāgavivarjitam
|
dharmāviruddho bhūteṣu kāmo'smi bharatarṣabha ||11||

12ye caiva sāttvikā bhāvā rājasāstamasāśca ye
|
matta eveti tānviddhi na tvahaṃ teṣu te mayi ||12||

13tribhirguṇamayairbhāvairebhiḥ sarvamidaṃ jagat
|
mohitaṃ nābhijānāti māmebhyaḥ paramavyayam ||13||

14daivī hyeṣā guṇamayī mama māyā duratyayā
|
māmeva ye prapadyante māyāmetāṃ taranti te ||14||

15na māṃ duṣkṛtino mūḍhāḥ prapadyante narādhamāḥ
|
māyayāpahṛtajñānā āsuraṃ bhāvamāśritāḥ ||15||

16caturvidhā bhajante māṃ janāḥ sukṛtino'rjuna
|
ārto jijñāsurarthārthī jñānī ca bharatarṣabha ||16||

17teṣāṃ jñānī nityayukta ekabhaktirviśiṣyate
|
priyo hi jñānino'tyarthamahaṃ sa ca mama priyaḥ ||17||

18udārāḥ sarva evaite jñānī tvātmaiva me matam
|
āsthitaḥ sa hi yuktātmā māmevānuttamāṃ gatim ||18||

19bahūnāṃ janmanāmante jñānavānmāṃ prapadyate
|
vāsudevaḥ sarvamiti sa mahātmā sudurlabhaḥ ||19||

20kāmaistaistairhṛtajñānāḥ prapadyante'nyadevatāḥ
|
taṃ taṃ niyamamāsthāya prakṛtyā niyatāḥ svayā ||20||

21yo yo yāṃ yāṃ tanuṃ bhaktaḥ śraddhayārcitumicchati
|
tasya tasyācalāṃ śraddhāṃ tāmeva vidadhāmyaham ||21||

22sa tayā śraddhayā yuktastasyā rādhanamīhate
|
labhate ca tataḥ kāmānmayaiva vihitānhi tān ||22||

23antavattu phalaṃ teṣāṃ tadbhavatyalpamedhasām
|
devāndevayajo yānti madbhaktā yānti māmapi ||23||

24avyaktaṃ vyaktimāpannaṃ manyante māmabuddhayaḥ
|
paraṃ bhāvamajānanto mamāvyayamanuttamam ||24||

25nāhaṃ prakāśaḥ sarvasya yogamāyāsamāvṛtaḥ
|
mūḍho'yaṃ nābhijānāti loko māmajamavyayam ||25||

26vedāhaṃ samatītāni vartamānāni cārjuna
|
bhaviṣyāṇi ca bhūtāni māṃ tu veda na kaścana ||26||

27icchādveṣasamutthena dvandvamohena bhārata
|
sarvabhūtāni saṃmohaṃ sarge yānti parantapa ||27||

28yeṣāṃ tvantagataṃ pāpaṃ janānāṃ puṇyakarmaṇām
|
te dvandvamohanirmuktā bhajante māṃ dṛḍhavratāḥ ||28||

29jarāmaraṇamokṣāya māmāśritya yatanti ye
|
te brahma tadviduḥ kṛtsnamadhyātmaṃ karma cākhilam ||29||

30sādhibhūtādhidaivaṃ māṃ sādhiyajñaṃ ca ye viduḥ
|
prayāṇakāle'pi ca māṃ te viduryuktacetasaḥ ||30||
