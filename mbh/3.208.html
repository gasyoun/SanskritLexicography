Mahābhārata 3.208 (Āraṇyakaparvan)

1mārkaṇḍeya uvāca|
sa tu vipramathovāca dharmavyādho yudhiṣṭhira
|
yadahamācare karma ghorametadasaṃśayam||1||

2vidhistu balavān brahman dustaraṃ hi purā kṛtam
|
purā kṛtasya pāpasya karmadoṣo bhavatyayam||2||

3doṣasyaitasya vai brahman vighāte yatnavānaham
|
vidhinā hi hate pūrvaṃ nimittaṃ ghātako bhavet||3||

4nimittabhūtā hi vayaṃ karmaṇo'sya dvijottama
|
yeṣāṃ hatānāṃ māṃsāni vikrīṇāmīha vai dvija||4||

5teṣāmapi bhaveddharma upayogena bhakṣaṇe
|
devatātithibhṛtyānāṃ pitṝṇāṃ cāpi pūjanam||5||

6oṣadhyo vīrudhaścaiva paśavo mṛgapakṣiṇaḥ
|
annādyabhūtā lokasya ityapi śrūyate śrutiḥ||6||

7ātmamāṃsaprasādena śibirauśīnaro nṛpaḥ
|
svargaṃ sudurgamaṃ prāptaḥ kṣamāvān dvijasattama||7||

8rājño mahānase pūrvaṃ rantidevasya vai dvija
|
dve sahastre tu vadhyete paśūnāmanvahaṃ tadā||8||

9ahanyahani vadhyete dve sahasre gavāṃ tathā
|
samāṃsaṃ dadato hyannaṃ rantidevasya nityaśaḥ||9||

10atulā kīrtirabhavannṛpasya dvijasattama
|
cāturmāsye ca paśavo vadhyanta iti nityaśaḥ||10||

11agnayo māṃsakāmāśca ityapi śrūyate śrutiḥ
|
yajñeṣu paśavo brahman vadhyante satataṃ dvijaiḥ||11||

12saṃskṛtāḥ kila mantraiśca te'pi svargamavāpnuvan
|
yadi naivāgnayo brahman māṃsakāmā'bhavan purā||12||

13bhakṣyaṃ naivābhavanmāṃsaṃ kasyacciddvijasattama||13||

14atrāpi vidhiruktaśca munibhirmāṃsabhakṣaṇe
|
devatānāṃ pitṝṇāṃ ca bhuṅkte datvā'pi yaḥ sadā
|
yathāvidhi yathāśraddhaṃ na praduṣyati bhakṣaṇāt||14||

15amāṃsāśī bhavatyevamityapi śrūyate śrutiḥ
|
bhāryāṃ gacchan brahmacārī ṛtau bhavati brāhmaṇaḥ||15||

16satyānṛte viniścitya atrāpi vidhirucyate
|
saudāsena tadā rājñā mānuṣā bhakṣitā dvija
|
śāpābhibhūtena bhṛśamatra kiṃ pratibhāti me||16||

17svadharma iti kṛtvā tu na tyajāmi dvijottama
|
purā kṛtamiti jñātvā jīvāmyetena karmaṇā||17||

18svakarma tyajato brahmannadharma iha dṛśyate
|
svakarmanirato yastu dharmaḥ sa iti niścayaḥ||18||

19pūrvaṃ hi vihitaṃ karma dehinaṃ na vimuñcati
|
dhātrā vidhirayaṃ dṛṣṭo bahudhā karmanirṇaye||19||

20draṣṭavyā tu bhavet prajñā krūre karmaṇi vartatā
|
kathaṃ karma śubhaṃ kuryāṃ kathaṃ mucye parābhavāt||20||

21karmaṇastasya ghorasya bahudhā nirṇayo bhavet
|
dāne ca satyavākye ca guruśuśrūṣaṇe tathā||21||

22dvijātipūjane cāhaṃ dharme ca nirataḥ tadā
|
abhimānātivādābhyāṃ nivṛtto'smi dvijottama||22||

23kṛṣiṃ sādhvīti manyante tatra hiṃsā parā smṛtā
|
karṣanto lāṅgalaiḥ puṃso ghnanti bhūmiśayān bahūn||23||

24jīvānanyāṃśca bahuśastatra kiṃ pratibhāti me
|
dhānyabījāni yānyāhurvrīhyādīni dvijottama||24||

25sarvāṇyetāni jīvāni tatra kiṃ pratibhāti me
|
adhyākramya paśūṃścāpi ghnanti vai bhakṣayanti ca||25||

26vṛkṣāṃstathauṣadhīścāpi chindanti puruṣā dvija
|
jīvā hi bahavo brahman vṛkṣeṣu ca phaleṣu ca||26||

27udake bahavaścāpi tatra kiṃ pratibhāti me
|
sarvaṃ vyāptamidaṃ brahman prāṇiniḥ prāṇijīvanaiḥ||27||

28matsyān grasante matsyāśca tatra kiṃ pratibhāti me
|
satvaiḥ sattvāni jīvanti bahudhā dvijasattama||28||

29prāṇino'nyonyabhakṣāśca tatra kiṃ pratibhāti me
|
caṅkramyamāṇā jīvāṃśca dharaṇīsaṃśritān bahūn||29||

30padbhyāṃ ghnanti narā vipra tatra kiṃ pratibhāti me
|
upaviṣṭāḥ śayānāśca ghnanti jīvānanekaśaḥ||30||

31jñānavijñānavantaśca tatra kiṃ pratibhāti me
|
jīvairgrastamidaṃ sarvamākāśaṃ pṛthivī tathā||31||

32avijñānācca hiṃsanti tatra kiṃ pratibhāti me
|
ahiṃseti yaduktaṃ hi puruṣairvismitaiḥ purā||32||

33ke na hiṃsānti jīvān vai loke'smin dvijasattama
|
bahu sañcitya iti vai nāsti kaścidahiṃsakaḥ||33||

34ahiṃsāyāṃ tu niratā yatayo dvijasattama
|
kurvantyeva hi hiṃsāṃ te yatnādalpatarā bhavet||34||

35ālakṣyāścaiva puruṣāḥ kule jātā mahāguṇāḥ
|
mahāghorāṇi karmāṇi kṛtvā lajjanti vai na ca||35||

36sahṛdaḥ suhṛdo'nyāṃśca durhṛdaścāpi durhṛdaḥ
|
samyakpravṛttān puruṣānnasamyaganupaśyataḥ||36||

37samṛddhaiśca na nandanti bāndhavā bāndhavairapi
|
gurūṃścaiva vinindanti mūḍhāḥ paṇḍitamāninaḥ||37||

38bahu loke viparyastaṃ dṛśyate dvijasattama
|
dharmayuktamadharmaṃ ca tatra kiṃ pratibhāti me||38||

39vaktuṃ bahuvidhaṃ śakyaṃ dharmādharmeṣu karmasu
|
svakarmanirato yo hi sa yaśaḥ prāpnuyānmahat||39||
