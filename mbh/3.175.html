Mahābhārata 3.175 (Āraṇyakaparvan)

1vaiśampāyana uvāca|
tasyāṃ rātryāṃ vyatītāyāṃ dharmarājo yudhiṣṭhiraḥ
|
utthāyāvaśyakāryāṇi kṛtavān bhrātṛbhiḥ saha||1||

2tataḥ sañcodayāmāsa sorjunaṃ mātṛnandanam
|
darśayāstrāṇi kaunteya yairjitā dānavāstvayā||2||

3tato dhanañjayo rājan devairdattāni pāṇḍavaḥ
|
astrāṇi tāni divyāni darśayāmāsa bhārata||3||

4yathānyāyaṃ mahātejāḥ śaucaṃ paramamāsthitaḥ
|
girikūbarapādākṣaṃ śubhaveṇu triveṇumat||4||

5pārthivaṃ rathamāsthāya śobhamāno dhanañjayaḥ
|
divyena saṃvṛtastena kavacena suvarcasā||5||

6dhanurādāya gāṇḍīvaṃ devadattaṃ sa vārijam
|
śośubhyamānaḥ kaunteya ānupūrvyānmahābhujaḥ||6||

7astrāṇi tāni divyāni darśanāyopacakrame
|
atha prayokṣyamāṇeṣu divyeṣvastreṣu teṣu vai||7||

8samākrāntā mahī padbhyāṃ samakampata sadrumā
|
kṣubhitāḥ saritaścaiva tathaiva ca mahodadhiḥ||8||

9śailāścāpi vyadīryanta na vavau ca samīraṇaḥ
|
na babhāse sahasrāṃśurna jajvāla ca pāvakaḥ||9||

10na vedāḥ pratibhānti sma dvijātīnāṃ kathañcana
|
antarbhūmigatā ye ca prāṇino janamejaya||10||

11pīḍyamānāḥ samutthāya pāṇḍavaṃ paryavārayan
|
vepamānāḥ prāñjalayaste sarve vikṛtānanāḥ||11||

12dahyamānāstadā'straiste yācanti sma dhanañjayam
|
tato brahmarṣayaścaiva siddhā ye ca maharṣayaḥ||12||

13jaṅgamāni ca bhūtāni sarvāṇyevāvatasthire
|
devarṣayaśca pravarāstathaiva ca divaukasaḥ||13||

14yakṣarākṣasagandharvāstathaiva ca patattriṇaḥ
|
khecarāṇi ca bhūtāni sarvāṇyevāvatasthire||14||

15tataḥ pitāmahaścaiva lokapālāśca sarvaśaḥ
|
bhagavāṃśca mahādevaḥ sagaṇo'bhyāyayau tadā||15||

16tato vāyurmahārāja divyairmālyaiḥ samanvitaḥ
|
abhitaḥ pāṇḍavaṃ citrairavacakre samantataḥ||16||

17jaguśca gāthā vividhā gandharvāḥ suracoditāḥ
|
nanṛtuḥ saṅghaśaścaiva rājannapsarasāṃ gaṇāḥ||17||

18tasmiṃśca tādṛśe kāle nāradaścoditaḥ suraiḥ
|
āgamyāha vacaḥ pārthaṃ śravaṇīyamidaṃ nṛpa||18||

19arjunārjuna mā yuṅkṣva divyānyastrāṇi bhārata
|
naitāni niradhiṣṭhāne prayujyante kathañcana||19||

20adhiṣṭhāne na vā'nārtaḥ prayuñjīta kadācana
|
prayogeṣu mahān doṣo hyastrāṇāṃ kurunandana||20||

21etāni rakṣyamāṇāni dhanañjaya yathāgamam
|
balavanti sukhārhāṇi bhaviṣyanti na saṃśayaḥ||21||

22arakṣyamāṇānyetāni trailokyasyāpi pāṇḍava
|
bhavanti sma vināśāya maivaṃ bhūyaḥ kṛthāḥ kvacit||22||

23ajātaśatro tvaṃ caiva drakṣyase tāni saṃyuge
|
yojyamānāni pārthena dviṣatāmavamardane||23||

24vaiśampāyana uvāca|
nivāryātha tataḥ pārthaṃ sarve devā yathāgatam
|
jagmuranye ca ye tatra samājagmurnararṣabha||24||

25teṣu sarveṣu kauravya pratiyāteṣu pāṇḍavāḥ
|
tasminneva vane hṛṣṭāsta ūṣuḥ saha kṛṣṇayā||25||
