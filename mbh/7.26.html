Mahābhārata 7.26 (Droṇaparvan)

1dhṛtarāṣṭra uvāca|
teṣvevaṃ sannivṛtteṣu pratyudyāteṣu bhāgaśaḥ
|
kathaṃ yuyudhire pārthā māmakāśca tarasvinaḥ||1||

2kimarjunaścāpyakarot saṃśaptakabalaṃ prati
|
saṃśaptakā vā pārthasya kimakurvata sañjaya||2||

3sañjaya uvāca|
tathā teṣu nivṛtteṣu pratyudyāteṣu bhāgaśaḥ
|
svayamabhyadravadbhīmaṃ nāgānīkena te sutaḥ||3||

4sa nāga iva nāgena govṛṣeṇeva govṛṣaḥ
|
samāhūtaḥ svayaṃ rājñā nāgānīkamupādravat||4||

5sa yuddhakuśalaḥ pārtho bāhurvāryeṇa cānvitaḥ
|
abhinat kuñjarānīkamacireṇaiva māriṣa||5||

6te gajā girisaṃkāśāḥ kṣarantaḥ sarvato madam
|
bhīmasenasya nārācairvimukhā vimadīkṛtāḥ||6||

7vidhamedabhrajālāni yathā vāyuḥ samuddhataḥ
|
vyadhamattānyanīkāni tathaiva pavanātmajaḥ||7||

8sa teṣu visṛjanbāṇān bhīmo nāgeṣvaśobhata
|
bhuvaneṣviva sarveṣu gabhastīnudito raviḥ||8||

9te bhīmabāṇābhihatāḥ saṃsyūtā vibabhurgajāḥ
|
gabhastibhirivārkasya vyomni nānābalāhakāḥ||9||

10tathā gajānāṃ kadanaṃ kurvāṇamanilātmajam
|
kruddho duryodhanobhyetya pratyavidhyacchitaiḥ śaraiḥ||10||

11tataḥ kṣaṇena kṣitipaṃ kṣatajapratimekṣaṇaḥ
|
kṣayaṃ ninīṣurniśitairbhīmo vivyādha patribhiḥ||11||

12sa śarācitasarvāṅgaḥ kruddho vivyādha pāṇḍavam
|
nārācairarkaraśmyābhairbhīmasenaṃ smayannivaḥ||12||

13tasya nāgaṃ maṇimayaṃ ratnacitradhvaje sthitam
|
mallābhyāṃ kārmukaṃ caiva kṣipraṃ ciccheda pāṇḍavaḥ||13||

14duryodhanaṃ pīḍyamānaṃ dṛṣṭvā bhīmena māriṣa
|
cukṣobhayiṣurabhyāgādaṅgo mātaṅgamāsthitaḥ||14||

15tamāpatantaṃ nāgendramambudapratimasvanam
|
kuṃbhāntare bhāmaseno nārācairārdayadbhṛśam||15||

16tasya kāyaṃ vinirbhidya nyamajjaddharaṇītale
|
tataḥ papāta dvirado vajrāhata ivācalaḥ||16||

17tasyāvarjitanāgasya mlecchasyādhaḥ patiṣyataḥ
|
śiraściccheda bhallena kṣiprakārī vṛkodaraḥ||17||

18tasminnipatite vīre saṃprādravata sācamūḥ
|
saṃbhrāntāśvadviparathā padātīnavamṛdnatī||18||

19teṣvanīkeṣu bhagneṣu vidravatsu samaṃtataḥ
|
prāgjyotiṣastato bhīmaṃ kuñjareṇa samādravat||19||

20yena nāgena maghavānajayaddaityadānavān
|
tadanvayena nāgena bhīmasenamupādravat||20||

21sa nāgapravaro bhīmaṃ sahasā samupādravat
|
caraṇābhyāmatho dvābhyāṃ saṃhatena kareṇa ca||21||

22vyāvṛttanayanaḥ kuddhaḥ pramathanniva pāṇḍavam
|
vṛkodararathaṃ sāśvamaviśeṣamacūrṇayat||22||

23padbhyāṃ bhīmo'pyatho dhāvaṃstasya gātreṣvalīyata
|
jānannañjalikāvedhaṃ nāpākrāmata pāṇḍavaḥ||23||

24gātrābhyantarago bhūtvā kareṇātāḍayanmuhuḥ
|
lālayāmāsa taṃ nāgaṃ vadhākāṅkṣiṇamavyayam||24||

25kulālacakravannāgastadā tūrṇamathābhramat
|
nāgāyutabalaḥ śrīmān kālayāno vṛkodaram||25||

26bhīmo'pi niṣkramya tataḥ supratīkāgrato'bhavat
|
bhīmaṃ kareṇāvanamya jānubhyāmabhyatāḍayat||26||

27grīvāyāṃ veṣṭayitvainaṃ sa gajo hantumaihata
|
karaveṣṭaṃ bhīmaseno bhramaṃ dattvā vyamocayat||27||

28punargātrāṇi nāgasyaḥ praviveśa vṛkodaraḥ
|
yāvatpratigajāyātaṃ svabale pratyavaikṣata||28||

29bhīmo'pi nāgagātrebhyo viniḥsṛtyāpayājjavāt
|
tataḥ sarvasya sainyasya nādaḥ samabhavanmahān||29||

30aho dhiḍihato bhīmaḥ kuñjareṇeti māriṣa
|
tena nāgena santrastā pāṇḍavānāmanīkinī||30||

31sahasā'bhyadravadrājan yatra tasthau vṛkodaraḥ
|
tato yudhiṣṭhiro rājā hataṃ matvā vṛkodaram||31||

32bhagadattaṃ sapāñcālyaḥ sarvataḥ samavārayat
|
taṃ rathaṃ rathināṃ śreṣṭhāḥ parivārya paraṃtapāḥ||32||

33avākiran śaraistīkṣṇaiḥ śataśo'tha sahasraśaḥ
|
savighātaṃ pṛṣatkānāmaṅkuśena samāharan||33||

34gajena pāṇḍupañcālān vyadhamat parvateśvaraḥ
|
tada‌dbhutamapaśyāma bhagada‌ttasya saṃyuge||34||

35tathā vṛddhasya caritaṃ kuñjareṇa viśāṃpate
|
tato rājā daśārṇānāṃ prāgjyotiṣamupādravat||35||

36tiryagyātena nāgena samadenāśugāminā
|
tayoryuddhaṃ samabhavannāgayorbhīmarūpayoḥ||36||

37sapakṣayoḥ parvatayoryathā sadrumayoḥ purā
|
prāgjyotiṣapaternāgaḥ sannivṛtyāpasṛtya ca||37||

38pārśve daśārṇādhipaterbhittvā nāgamapātayat
|
tomaraiḥ sūryaraśmyābhairbhagadatto'tha saptabhiḥ||38||

39jaghāna dviradasthaṃ taṃ śatruṃ pracalitāsanam
|
vyavacchidya tu rājānaṃ bhagadattaṃ yudhiṣṭhiraḥ||39||

40rathānīkena mahatā sarvataḥ paryavārayat
|
sa kuñjarastho rathibhiḥ śuśubhe sarvato vṛtaḥ||40||

41parvataṃ vanamadhyastho jvalanniva hutāśanaḥ
|
maṇḍalaṃ sarvataḥ śliṣṭaṃ rathināmugradhanvinām||41||

42kiratāṃ śaravarṣāṇi sa nāgaḥ paryavartata
|
tataḥ prāgjyotiṣo rājā parigṛhya mahāgajam||42||

43preṣayāmāsa sahasā yuyudhānarathaṃ prati
|
śineḥ pautrasya tu rathaṃ parigṛhya mahādvipaḥ||43||

44abhicikṣepa vegena yuyudhānastvapākramat
|
bṛhataḥ saindhavānaśvān samutthāpyātha sārathiḥ||44||

45tasthau sātyakimāsādya saṃplatastaṃ rathaṃ prati
|
sa tu labdhvāntaraṃ nāgastvarito rathamaṇḍalāt||45||

46niścakrāma tataḥ sarvān paricikṣepa pārthivān
|
te tvāśugatinā tena trāsyamānā nararṣabhāḥ||46||

47tamekaṃ dviradaṃ saṃkhye menire śataśo dvipān
|
te gajasthena kālyante bhagadattena pāṇḍavāḥ||47||

48airāvatasthena yathā devarājena dānavāḥ
|
teṣāṃ pradravatāṃ bhīmaḥ pañcālānāmitastataḥ||48||

49gajavājikṛtaḥ śabdaḥ sumahān samajāyata
|
bhagadattena samare kālyamāneṣu pāṇḍuṣu||49||

50prāgjyotiṣamabhikruddhaḥ punarbhīmaḥ samabhyayāt
|
tasyābhidravato vāhān hastamuktena vāriṇā||50||

51siktvā vyatrāsayannāgaste pārthamaharaṃstataḥ
|
tatastamabhyayāt tūrṇaṃ ruciparvā kṛtī sutaḥ||51||

52samaghnañcharavarṣeṇa rathastho'ntakasannibhaḥ
|
tataḥ sa ruciparvāṇaṃ śareṇānataparvaṇā||52||

53suparvā parvatapatirninye vaivasvatakṣayam
|
tasminnipatite vīre saubhadro draupadīsutaḥ||53||

54cekitāno dhṛṣṭaketuryuyutsuścārdayan dvipam
|
ta enaṃ śaradhārābhirdhārābhiriva toyadāḥ||54||

55siṣicurbhairavānnādān vinadanto jighāṃsavaḥ
|
tataḥ pārṣṇyaṃkuśāṅguṣṭhaiḥ kṛtinā codito dvipaḥ||55||

56prasāritakaraḥ prāyāt stabdhakarṇekṣaṇo drutam
|
so'dhiṣṭhāya padā vāhān yuyutsoḥ sūtamārujat||56||

57yuyutsustu rathādrājannapātrāmat tvarānvitaḥ
|
tataḥ pāṇḍavayodhāste nāgarājaṃ śarairdutam||57||

58siṣicurbhairavānnādān vinadanto jighāṃsavaḥ
|
putrastu tava saṃbhrāntaḥ saubhadrasyāpluto ratham||58||

59sa kuñjarastho visṛjanniṣūnariṣu pārthivaḥ
|
babhau raśmīnivādityo bhuvaneṣu samutsṛjan||59||

60tamārjunirdvādaśabhiryuyutsurdaśabhiḥ śaraiḥ
|
tribhistribhirdaupadeyā dhṛṣṭaketuśca vivyadhuḥ||60||

61so'tiyatnārpitairbāṇairācito dvirado babhau
|
saṃsyūta iva sūryasya raśmibhirjalado mahān||61||

62niyantuḥśilpayatnābhyāṃ prerito'riśarārditaḥ
|
paricikṣepa tānnāgaḥ sa ripūn savyadakṣiṇam||62||

63gopāla iva daṇḍena yathā paśugaṇān vane
|
āveṣṭayata tāṃ senāṃ bhagadattastathā muhuḥ||63||

64kṣipraṃ śyenābhipannānāṃ vāyasānāmiva svanaḥ
|
babhūva pāṇḍaveyānāṃ bhṛśaṃ vidravatāṃ svanaḥ||64||

65sa nāgarājaḥ pravarāṅkuśāhataḥ purā sapakṣo'drivaro yathā nṛpa
|
bhayaṃ tadā ripuṣu samādadhadbhṛśaṃ vaṇigjanānāṃ kṣubhito yathārṇavaḥ||65||

66tato dhvanirdviradarathāśvapārthivairbhayāddravadbhirjanito'tibhairavaḥ
|
kṣitiṃ viyad dyāṃ vidiśo diśastathā samāvṛṇot pārthiva saṃyuge tataḥ||66||

67sa tena nāgapravareṇa pārthivo bhṛśaṃ jagāhe dviṣatāmanīkinīm
|
purā suguptāṃ vibudhairivāhave virocano devavarūthinīmiva||67||

68bhṛśaṃ vavau jvalanasakhoviyadrajaḥ samāvṛṇonmuhurapi caiva sainikān
|
tamekanāgaṃ gaṇaśo yathā gajān samantato drutamatha menire janāḥ||68||
