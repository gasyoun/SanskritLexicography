Mahābhārata 13.114 (Anuśāsanaparvan)

1vaiśampāyana uvāca|
tato yudhiṣṭhiro rājā śaratalpe pitāmaham
|
punareva mahātejāḥ papraccha vadatāṃ varaḥ ||1||

2yudhiṣṭhira uvāca|
ṛṣayo brāhmaṇā devāḥ praśaṃsanti mahāmate
|
ahiṃsālakṣaṇaṃ dharmaṃ vedaprāmāṇyadarśanāt ||2||

3karmaṇā manujaḥ kurvan hiṃsāṃ pārthivasattama
|
vācā ca manasā caiva kathaṃ duḥkhāt pramucyate ||3||

4bhīṣma uvāca|
caturvidheyaṃ nirdiṣṭā hyahiṃsā brahmavādibhiḥ
|
ekaikato'pi vibhraṣṭā na bhavatyarisūdana ||4||

5yathā sarvaścatuṣpādvai tribhiḥ pādairna tiṣṭhati
|
tathaiveyaṃ mahīpāla kāraṇaiḥ procyate tribhiḥ ||5||

6yathā nāgapade'nyāni padāni pada‌gāminām
|
sarvāṇyevāpidhīyante padajātāni kauñjare ||6||

7evaṃ lokeṣvahiṃsā tu nirdiṣṭā dharmataḥ purā
|
karmaṇā lipyate janturvācā ca manasāpi ca ||7||

8pūrvaṃ tu manasā tyaktvā tathā vācā'tha karmaṇā
|
na bhakṣayati yo māṃsaṃ trividhaṃ sa vimucyate ||8||

9trikāraṇaṃ tu nirdiṣṭaṃ śrūyate brahmavādibhiḥ
|
mano vāci tathā''svāde doṣā hyeṣu pratiṣṭhitāḥ ||9||

10na bhakṣayantyato māṃsaṃ tapoyuktā manīṣiṇaḥ
|
doṣāṃstu bhakṣaṇe rājanmāṃsasyeha nibodha me ||10||

11putramāṃsopamaṃ jānan khādate yo vicakṣaṇaḥ
|
māṃsaṃ mohasamāyuktaḥ puruṣaḥ so'dhamaḥ smṛtaḥ ||11||

12pitṛmātṛsamāyoge putratvaṃ jāyate yathā
|
hiṃsāṃ kṛtvāvaśaḥ pāpo bhūyiṣṭhaṃ jāyate tathā ||12||

13rasaṃ ca pratijihvāyā jñānaṃ prajñāyate yathā
|
tathā śāstreṣu niyataṃ rāgo hyāsvāditādbhavet ||13||

14saṃskṛtāsaṃskṛtāḥ pakvā lavaṇālavaṇāstathā
|
prajāyante yathā bhāvāstathā cittaṃ nirudhyate ||14||

15bherīmṛdaṅgaśabdāṃśca tantrīśabdāṃśca puṣkalān
|
niṣeviṣyanti vai mandā māṃsabhakṣāḥ kathaṃ narāḥ ||15||

16acintitamanirdiṣṭamasaṅkalpitameva ca
|
rasagṛddhyā'bhibhūtā ye praśaṃsanti phalārthinaḥ ||16||

17praśaṃsā hyeva māṃsasya doṣakarmaphalānvitā
|
jīvitaṃ hi parityajya bahavaḥ sādhavo janāḥ
|
svamāṃsaiḥ paramāṃsāni paripālya divaṃ gatāḥ ||17||

18evameṣā mahārāja caturbhiḥ kāraṇairvṛtā
|
ahiṃsā tava nirdiṣṭā sarvadharmānusaṃhitā ||18||
