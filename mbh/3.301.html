Mahābhārata 3.301 (Āraṇyakaparvan)

1sūrya uvāca|
mā'hitaṃ karṇa kārṣīstvamātmanaḥ suhṛdāṃ tathā
|
putrāṇāmatha bhāryāṇāmatho māturatho pituḥ||1||

2śarīrasthāvirodhena prāṇināṃ prāṇabhṛdvara
|
iṣyate yaśasaḥ prāptiḥ kīrtiśca tridive sthirā||2||

3yastvaṃ prāṇavirodhena kīrtimicchasi śāśvatīm
|
sā te prāṇān samādāya gamiṣyati na saṃśayaḥ||3||

4jīvatāṃ kurute kāryaṃ pitā mātā sutāstathā
|
ye cānye bāndhavāḥ kecilloke'smin puruṣarṣabha||4||

5rājānaśca naravyāghra pauruṣeṇa nibodha tat
|
kīrtiśca jīvataḥ sādhvī puruṣasya mahādyute||5||

6mṛtasya kīrtyā kiṃ kāryaṃ bhasmībhūtasya dehinaḥ
|
mṛtaḥ kīrtiṃ na jānīte jīvan kīrtiṃ samaśnute||6||

7mṛtasya kīrtirmartyasya yathā mālā gatāyuṣaḥ
|
ahaṃ tu tvāṃ bravīmyetadbhakto'sīti hitepsayā||7||

8bhaktimanto hi me rakṣyā ityetenāpi hetunā
|
bhakto'yaṃ parayā bhaktyā māmityeva mahābhuja||8||

9mamāpi bhaktirutpannā sa tvaṃ kuru vaco mama
|
asti cātra paraṃ kiñcidadhyātmaṃ devanirmitam
|
ataśca tvāṃ bravīmyetat kriyatāmaviśaṅkayā||9||

10devaguhyaṃ tvayā jñātuṃ na śakyaṃ puruṣarṣabha
|
tasmānnākhyāmi te guhyaṃ kāle vetsyati tadbhavān||10||

11punaruktaṃ ca vakṣyāmi tvaṃ rādheya nibodha tat
|
mā'smai te kuṇḍale dadyā bhikṣate vajrapāṇinā||11||

12śobhase kuṇḍalābhyāṃ ca rucirābhyāṃ mahādyute
|
viśākhayormadhyagataḥ śaśīva vimale divi||12||

13kīrtiśca jīvataḥ sādhvī puruṣasyeti viddhi tat
|
pratyākhyeyastvayā tāta kuṇḍalārthe sureśvaraḥ||13||

14śakyā bahuvidhairvākyaiḥ kuṇḍalepsā tvayā'nagha
|
vihantuṃ devarājasya hetuyuktaiḥ punaḥpunaḥ||14||

15hetumadupapannārthairmādhuryakṛtabhūṣaṇaiḥ
|
purandarasya karṇa tvaṃ buddhimetāmapānuda||15||

16tvaṃ hi nityaṃ naravyāghra spardhase savyasācinā
|
savyasācī tvayā ceha yudhi śūraḥ sameṣyati||16||

17na tu tvāmarjunaḥ śaktaḥ kuṇḍalābhyāṃ samanvitam
|
vijetuṃ yudhi yadyasya svayāmindraḥ sakhā bhavet||17||

18tasmānna deye śakrāya tvayaite kuṇḍale śubhe
|
saṅgrāme yadi nirjetuṃ karṇa kāmayase'rjunam||18||
