Mahābhārata 6.57 (Bhīṣmaparvan)

1sañjaya uvāca|
tato vyūḍheṣvanīkeṣu tāvakeṣu pareṣu ca
|
dhanañjayo rathānīkamavadhīt tava bhārata ||1||

2śarairatiratho yuddhe dārayan rathayūthapān
|
te vadhyamānāḥ pārthena kāleneva yugakṣaye ||2||

3dhārtarāṣṭrā raṇe yatnāt pāṇḍavān pratyayodhayan
|
prārthayānā yaśo dīptaṃ mṛtyuṃ kṛtvā nivartanam ||3||

4ekāgramanaso bhūtvā pāṇḍavānāṃ varūthinīm
|
babhañjurbahuśo rājaṃste cāsajjanta saṃyuga ||4||

5dravadbhiratha bhagnaiśca parivartadbhireva ca
|
pāṇḍavaiḥ kauraveyaiśca na prājñāyata kiñcana ||5||

6udatiṣṭhadrajo bhaumaṃ chāda‌yānaṃ divākaram
|
na diśaḥ pradiśo vā'pi tatra hanyuḥ kathaṃ narāḥ ||6||

7anumānena saṃjñābhirnāmagotraiśca saṃyuge
|
vartate ca tathā yuddhaṃ tatra tatra viśāṃpate ||7||

8na vyūho bhidyate tatra kauravāṇāṃ kathañcana
|
rakṣitaḥ satyasandhena bhāradvājena saṃyuge ||8||

9tathaiva pāṇḍavānāṃ ca rakṣitaḥ savyasācinā
|
nābhidyata mahāvyūho bhīmena ca surakṣitaḥ ||9||

10senāgrādapi niṣpatya prāyudhyaṃstatra mānavāḥ
|
ubhayoḥ senayo rājan vyatiṣaktarathadvipāḥ ||10||

11hayārohairhayārohāḥ pātyante sma mahāhave
|
ṛṣṭibhirvimalābhiśca prāsairapi ca saṃyuge ||11||

12rathī rathinamāsādya śaraiḥ kanakabhūṣaṇaiḥ
|
pātayāmāsa samare tasminnatibhayaṅkare ||12||

13gajārohā gajārohānnārācaśaratomaraiḥ
|
saṃsaktān pātayāmāsustava teṣāṃ ca sarvaśaḥ ||13||

14kaścidutpatya samare varavāraṇamāsthitaḥ
|
keśapakṣe parāmpṛśya jahāra samare śiraḥ ||14||

15anye dviradadantāgranirbhinnahṛdayā raṇe
|
vemuśca rudhiraṃ vīrā niḥśvasantaḥ samantataḥ ||15||

16kaścitkariviṣāṇastho vīro raṇaviśāradaḥ
|
prāvepacchaktinirbhinno gajaśikṣāstravedinā ||16||

17pattisaṅghā raṇe pattīn bhindipālaparaśvadhaiḥ
|
nyapātayanta saṃhṛṣṭāḥ parasparakṛtāgasaḥ ||17||

18rathī ca samare rājannāsādya gajayūthapam
|
sagajaṃ pātayāmāsa gajī ca rathināṃ varam ||18||

19rathinaṃ ca hayārohaḥ prāsena bharatarṣabha
|
pātayāmāsa samare rathī ca hayasādinam ||19||

20padātī rathinaṃ saṃkhye rathī cāpi padātinam
|
nyapātayacchitaiḥ śastraiḥ senayorubhayorapi ||20||

21gajārohā hayārohān pātayāñcakrire tadā
|
hayārohā gajasthāṃśca tada‌dbhutamivābhavat ||21||

22gajārohavaraiścāpi tatra tatra padātayaḥ
|
pātitāḥ samadṛśyanta taiścāpi gajayodhinaḥ ||22||

23pattisaṅghā hayārohaiḥ sādisaṅghāśca pattibhiḥ
|
pātyamānā vyadṛśyanta śataśo'tha sahasraśaḥ ||23||

24dhvajaistatrāpaviddhaiśca kārmukaistomaraistathā
|
prāsaistathā gadābhiśca parighaiḥ kampanaistathā ||24||

25śaktibhiḥ kavacaiścitraiḥ kaṇapairaṅkuśairapi
|
nistriṃśairvimalaiścāpi svarṇapuhṇaiḥ śaraistathā ||25||

26paristomaiḥ kuthābhiśca kambalaiśca mahādhanaiḥ
|
bhūrbhāti bharataśreṣṭha sragdāmairiva citritā ||26||

27narāśvakāyaiḥ patitairdantibhiśca mahāhave
|
agamyarūpā pṛthivī māṃsaśoṇitakardamā ||27||

28praśaśāma rajo bhaumaṃ vyukṣitaṃ raṇaśoṇitaiḥ
|
diśaśca vimalāḥ sarvāḥ sambabhūvurjaneśvara ||28||

29utthitānyagaṇeyāni kabandhāni samantataḥ
|
cihnabhūtāni jagato vināśārthāya bhārata ||29||

30tasmin yuddhe mahāraudre vartamāne sudāruṇe
|
pratyadṛśyanta rathito dhāvamānāḥ samantataḥ ||30||

31tato bhīṣmaśca droṇaśca saindhavaśca jayadrathaḥ
|
purumitro jayo bhojaḥ śalyaścāpi sasaubalaḥ ||31||

32ete samaradurdharṣāḥ siṃhatulyaparākramāḥ
|
pāṇḍavānāmanīkāni babhañjuḥ sma punaḥ punaḥ ||32||

33tathaiva bhīmaseno'pi rākṣasaśca ghaṭotkacaḥ
|
sātyakiścekitānaśca draupadeyāśca bhārata ||33||

34tāvakāṃstava putrāṃśca sahitān sarvarājabhiḥ
|
drāvayāmāsurājau te tridiśā dānavāniva ||34||

35tathā te samare'nyonyaṃ nighnantaḥ kṣatriyarṣabhāḥ
|
raktokṣitā ghorarūpā virejurdānavā iva ||35||

36vinirjitya ripūn vīrāḥ senayorubhayorapi
|
vyadṛśyanta mahāmātrā grahā iva nabhastale ||36||

37tato rathasahasreṇa putro duryodhanastava
|
abhyayāt pāṇḍavaṃ yuddhe rākṣasaṃ ca ghaṭotkacam ||37||

38tathaiva pāṇḍavāḥ sarve mahatyā senayā saha
|
droṇabhīṣmau raṇe yattau pratyudyayurarindamau ||38||

39kiriṭī ca yayau kruddhaḥ samantātpārthivottamān
|
ārjuniḥ sātyakiścaiva yayatuḥ saubalaṃ balam ||39||

40tataḥ pravavṛte bhūyaḥ saṅgrāmo lomaharṣaṇaḥ
|
tāvakānāṃ pareṣāṃ ca samare vijayaiṣiṇām ||40||
