Mahābhārata 2.33 (Sabhāparvan)

1vaiśampāyana uvāca|
rakṣaṇāddharmarājasya satyasya paripālanāt
|
śatrūṇāṃ kṣapaṇāccaiva svakarmaniratāḥ prajāḥ||1||

2balīnāṃ samyagādānāddharmataścānuśāsanāt
|
nikāmavarṣī parjanyaḥ sphīto janapado'bhavat||2||

3sarvārambhāḥ supravṛttā gorakṣā karṣaṇaṃ vaṇik
|
viśeṣātsarvamevaitatsañjajñe rājakarmaṇaḥ||3||

4dasyubhyo vañcakebhyo vā rājanprati parasparam
|
rājavallabhataścaiva nāśrūyanta mṛṣā giraḥ||4||

5avarṣaṃ cātivarṣaṃ ca vyādhipāvakamūrchanam
|
sarvametattadā nāsīddharmanitye yudhiṣṭhire||5||

6priyaṃ kartumupasthātuṃ balikarma svabhāvajam
|
abhihartuṃ nṛpā jagmurnānyaiḥ kāryaiḥ kathañcana||6||

7dharmairdhanāgamaistasya vavṛdhe nicayo mahān
|
kartuṃ yasya na śakyeta kṣayo varṣaśatairapi||7||

8svakoṣṭhasya parīmāṇaṃ kośasya ca mahīpatiḥ
|
vijñāya rājā kaunteyo yajñāyaiva mano dadhe||8||

9suhṛdaścaiva ye sarve pṛthakca sahitā bruvan
|
yajñakālastava vibho kriyatāmatra sāmpratam||9||

10athaivaṃ bruvatāmeva teṣāmabhyāyayau hariḥ
|
ṛṣiḥ purāṇo vedātmā'dṛśyaścaiva vijānatām||10||

11jagatastasthuṣāṃ śreṣṭhaḥ prabhavaścāpyayaśca ha
|
bhūtabhavyabhavannāthaḥ keśavaḥ keśisūdanaḥ||11||

12prākāraḥ sarvavṛṣṇīnāmāpatsvabhayado'rihā
|
balādhikāre nikṣipya samyagānakadundubhim||12||

13uccāvacamupādāya dharmarājāya mādhavaḥ
|
dhanaughaṃ puruṣavyāghro balena mahatā vṛtaḥ||13||

14taṃ dhanaughamaparyantaṃ ratnasāgaramakṣayam
|
nādayanrathaghoṣeṇa praviśeśa purottamam||14||

15pūrṇamāpūrayaṃsteṣāṃ dviṣacchokāvaho'bhavat
|
asūryamiva sūryeṇa nivātamiva vāyunā
|
kṛṣṇena samupetena jahṛṣe bhārataṃ puram||15||

16taṃ mudā'bhisamāgamya satkṛtya ca yathāvidhi
|
sa pṛṣṭvā kuśalaṃ caiva sukhāsīnaṃ yudhiṣṭhiraḥ||16||

17dhaumyadvaipāyanamukhairṛtvigbhiḥ puruṣarṣabha
|
bhīmārjunayamaiścaiva sahitaḥ kṛṣṇamabravīt||17||

18yudhiṣṭhira uvāca|
tvatkṛte pṛthivī sarvā madvaśe kṛṣṇa vartate
|
dhanaṃ ca bahu vārṣṇeya tvatprasādādupārjitam||18||

19so'hamicchāmi tatsarvaṃ vidhivaddevakīsuta
|
upayoktuṃ dvijāgryebhyo havyavāhe ca mādhava||19||

20tadahaṃ yaṣṭumicchāmi dāśārha sahitastvayā
|
anujaiśca mahābāho tanmā'nujñātumarhasi||20||

21taddīkṣāpaya govinda tvamātmānaṃ mahābhuja
|
tvayīṣṭavati dāśārha vipāpmā bhavitā hyaham||21||

22māṃ vāpyabhyanujānīha sahaibhiranujairvibho
|
anujñātastvayā kṛṣṇa prāpnuyāṃ kratumuttamam||22||

23vaiśampāyana uvāca|
taṃ kṛṣṇaḥsa pratyuvācedaṃ bahūktvā guṇavistaram
|
tvameva rājaśārdūla saṃmrāḍarho mahākratum
|
samprāpnuhi tvayā prāpte kṛtakṛtyāstato vayam||23||

24yadasvābhīpsitaṃ yajñaṃ mayi śreyasyavasthite
|
niyuṅkṣva tvaṃ ca māṃ kṛtye sarvaṃ kartāsmi te vacaḥ||24||

25yudhiṣṭhira uvāca|
saphalaḥ kṛṣṇa saṅkalpaḥ siddhiśca niyatā mama
|
yasyame tvaṃ hṛṣīkeśa yathepsitamupasthitaḥ||25||

26vaiśampāyana uvāca|
anujñātastu kṛṣṇena pāṇḍavo bhrātṛbhiḥ saha
|
ījituṃ rājasūyena sādhanānyupacakrame||26||

27tatastvājñāpayāmāsa pāṇḍavo'rinibarhaṇaḥ
|
sahadevaṃ yudhāṃ śreṣṭhaṃ mantriṇaścaiva sarvaśaḥ||27||

28asminkratau yathoktāni yajñāṅgāni dvijātibhiḥ
|
yathopakaraṇaṃ sarvaṃ maṅgalāni ca sarvaśaḥ||28||

29adhiyajñāṃśca sambhārāndhaumyoktānkṣiprameva hi
|
samānayantu puruṣā yathāyogaṃ yathākramam||29||

30indraseno viśokaśca pūruścārjunasārathiḥ
|
annādyāharaṇe yuktāḥ santu matpriyakāmyayā||30||

31sarvakāmāśca kāryantāṃ rasagandhasamanvitāḥ
|
manorathaprītikarā dvijānāṃ kurusattama||31||

32tadvākyasamakālaṃ ca kṛtaṃ sarvaṃ nyavedayat
|
sahadevo yudhāṃ śreṣṭho dharmarājo yudhiṣṭhire||32||

33tato dvaipāyano rājannṛtvijaḥ samupānayat
|
vedāniva mahābhāgānsākṣānmūrtimato dvijān||33||

34svayaṃ brahmatvamakarottasya satyavatīsutaḥ
|
dhanañjayānāmṛṣabhaḥ susāmā sāmago'bhavat||34||

35yājñavalkyo babhūvātha brahmiṣṭho'dhvaryusattamaḥ
|
pailo hotā vasoḥ putro dhaumyena sahito'bhavat||35||

36eteṣāṃ putravargāśca śiṣyāśca bharatarṣabha
|
babhūvurhotragāḥ sarve vedavedāṅgapāragāḥ||36||

37te vācayitvā puṇyāhamūhayitvā ca taṃ vidhim
|
śāstroktaṃ pūjayāmāsustaddevayajanaṃ mahat||37||

38tatra cakruranujñātāḥ śaraṇānyuta śilpinaḥ
|
gandhavanti viśālāni veśmānīva divaukasām||38||

39tata ājñāpayāmāsa sa rājā rājasattamaḥ
|
sahadevaṃ tadā sadyo mantriṇaṃ puruṣarṣabhaḥ||39||

40āmantraṇārthaṃ dūtāṃstvaṃ preṣayasvāśugāndrutam
|
upaśrutya vaco rājñaḥ sa dūtānprāhiṇottadā||40||

41āmantrayadhvaṃ rāṣṭreṣu brāhmaṇānbhūmipānatha
|
viśaśca mānyānśūdrāṃśca sarvānānayateti ca||41||

42vaiśampāyana uvāca|
samājñaptāstato dūtāḥ pāṇḍaveyasya śāsanāt
|
āmantrayāmbabhūvuśca ānayaṃścāparāndrutam
|
tathā parānapi narānātmanaḥ śīghragāminaḥ||42||

43tataste tu yathākālaṃ kuntīputraṃ yudhiṣṭhiram
|
dīkṣayāñcakrire viprā rājasūyāya bhārata||43||

44dīkṣitaḥ sa tu dharmātmā dharmarājo yudhiṣṭhiraḥ
|
jagāma yajñāyatanaṃ vṛto vipraiḥ sahasraśaḥ||44||

45bhātṛbhirjñātibhiścaiva suhṛdbhiḥ sacivaiḥ saha
|
kṣatriyaiśca manuṣyendrairnānādeśasamāgataiḥ||45||

46amātyaiśca naraśreṣṭho dharmo vigrahavāniva
|
ājagmurbrāhmaṇāstatra viṣayebhyastatastataḥ||46||

47sarvavidyāsu niṣṇātā vedavedāṅgapāragāḥ
|
teṣāmāvasathāṃścakrurdharmarājasya śāsanāt||47||

48bahvannācchādanairyuktānsagaṇānāṃ pṛthakpṛthak
|
sarvartuguṇasampannānśilpino'tha sahasraśaḥ||48||

49teṣu te nyavasanrājanbrāhmaṇā nṛpasatkṛtāḥ
|
kathayantaḥ kathā bahvīḥ paśyanto naṭanartakān||49||

50bhuñjatāṃ caiva viprāṇāṃ vadatāṃ ca mahāsvanaḥ
|
aniśaṃ śrūyate tatra muditānāṃ mahātmanām||50||

51dīyatāṃ dīyatāmeṣāṃ bhujyatāṃ bhujyatāmiti
|
evaṃprakārāḥ sañjalpāḥ śrūyante smātra nityaśaḥ||51||

52gavāṃ śatasahasrāṇi śayanānāṃ ca bhārata
|
rukmasya yoṣitāṃ caiva dharmarājaḥ pṛthagdadau||52||

53prāvartataivaṃ yajñaḥ sa pāṇḍavasya mahātmanaḥ
|
pṛthivyāmekavīrasya śakrasyeva triviṣṭape||53||

54tato yudhiṣṭhiro rājā preṣayāmāsa pāṇḍavam
|
nakulaṃ hāstinapuraṃ bhīṣmāya puruṣarṣabhaḥ||54||

55droṇāya dhṛtarāṣṭrāya vidurāya kṛpāya ca
|
bhrātṝṇāṃ caiva sarveṣāṃ yenuraktā yudhiṣṭhire ||55||
