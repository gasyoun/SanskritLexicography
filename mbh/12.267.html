Mahābhārata 12.267 (Śāntiparvan)

1yudhiṣṭhira uvāca|
kathaṃ rājā prajā rakṣenna ca kiṃcitpraghātayet
|
pṛcchāmi tvāṃ satāṃ śreṣṭha tanme brūhi pitāmaha ||1||

2bhīṣma uvāca|
atrāpyudāharantīmamitihāsaṃ purātanam
|
dyumatsenasya saṃvādaṃ rājñā satyavatā saha ||2||

3avyāhṛtaṃ vyājahāra satyavāniti naḥ śrutam
|
vadhāyonnīyamāneṣu piturevānuśāsanāt ||3||

4adharmatāṃ yāti dharmo yātyadharmaśca dharmatām
|
vadho nāma bhaveddharmo naitadbhavitumarhati ||4||

5dyumatsena uvāca |
atha cedavadho dharmo dharmaḥ ko jātu cidbhavet
|
dasyavaścenna hanyeran satyavan saṃkaro bhavet ||5||

6mamedamiti nāsyaitatpravarteta kalau yuge
|
lokayātrā na caiva syādatha cedvettha śaṃsa naḥ ||6||

7satyavānuvāca |
sarva ete trayo varṇāḥ kāryā brāhmaṇabandhanāḥ
|
dharmapāśanibaddhānāmanyo'pyevaṃ cariṣyati ||7||

8yo yasteṣāmapacarettamācakṣīta vai dvijaḥ
|
ayaṃ me na śṛṇotīti tasminrājā pradhārayet ||8||

9tattvābhedena yacchāstraṃ tatkāryaṃ nānyathāvidham
|
asamīkṣyaiva karmāṇi nītiśāstraṃ yathāvidhi ||9||

10dasyūnnihanti vai rājā bhūyaṃso vāpyanāgasaḥ
|
bhāryā mātā pitā putro hanyante puruṣeṇa te
|
pareṇāpakṛto rājā tasmātsamyak pradhārayet ||10||

11asādhuścaiva puruṣo labhate śīlamekadā
|
sādhoścāpi hyasādhubhyaḥ śobhanā jāyate prajā ||11||

12na mūlaghātaḥ kartavyo naiṣa dharmaḥ sanātanaḥ
|
api svalpavadhenaiva prāyaścittaṃ vidhīyate ||12||

13udvejanena bandhena virūpakaraṇena ca
|
vadhadaṇḍena te kliśyā na purohitasaṃsadi ||13||

14yadā purohitaṃ vā te paryeyuḥ śaraṇaiṣiṇaḥ
|
kariṣyāmaḥ punarbrahmanna pāpamiti vādinaḥ ||14||

15tadā visargamarhāḥ syuritīdaṃ dhātṛśāsanam
|
bibhraddaṇḍājinaṃ muṇḍo brāhmaṇo'rhati śāsanam ||15||

16garīyāṃso garīyāṃsamaparādhe punaḥ punaḥ
|
tadā visargamarhanti na yathā prathame tathā ||16||

17dyumatsena uvāca |
yatra yatraiva śakyeransaṃyantuṃ samaye prajāḥ
|
sa tāvānprocyate dharmo yāvanna pratilaṃghyate ||17||

18ahanyamāneṣu punaḥ sarvameva parābhavet
|
pūrve pūrvatare caiva suśāsyā hyabhavan janāḥ ||18||

19mṛdavaḥ satyabhūyiṣṭhā alpadrohālpamanyavaḥ
|
purā dhigdaṇḍa evāsīdvāgdaṇḍastadanantaram ||19||

20āsīdādānadaṇḍo'pi vadhadaṇḍo'dya vartate
|
vadhenāpi na śakyante niyantumapare janāḥ ||20||

21naiva dasyurmanuṣyāṇāṃ na devānāmiti śrutiḥ
|
na gandharvapitṝṇāṃ ca kaḥ kasyeha na kaścana ||21||

22padmaṃ śmaśānādādatte piśācāccāpi daivatam
|
teṣu yaḥ samayaṃ kaścitkurvīta hatabuddhiṣu ||22||

23satyavānuvāca |
tānna śaknoṣi cetsādhūn paritrātumahiṃsayā
|
kasyacidbhūtabhavyasya lābhenāntaṃ tathā kuru ||23||

24rājāno lokayātrārthaṃ tapyante paramaṃ tapaḥ
|
te'patrapanti tādṛgbhyastathāvṛttā bhavanti ca ||24||

25vitrāsyamānāḥ sukṛto na kāmāt ghnanti duṣkṛtīn
|
sukṛtenaiva rājāno bhūyiṣṭhaṃ śāsate prajāḥ ||25||

26śreyasaḥ śreyaso'pyevaṃ vṛttaṃ loko'nuvartate
|
sadaiva hi gurorvṛttamanuvartanti mānavāḥ ||26||

27ātmānamasamādhāya samādhitsati yaḥ parān
|
viṣayeṣvindriyavaśaṃ mānavāḥ prahasanti tam ||27||

28yo rājño dambhamohena kiñcitkuryādasāmpratam
|
sarvopayairniyamyaḥ sa tathā pāpānnivartate ||28||

29ātmaivādau niyantavyo duṣkṛtaṃ sanniyacchatā
|
daṇḍayecca mahādaṇḍairapi bandhūnanantarān ||29||

30yatra vai pāpakṛnnaco na mahadduḥkhamarcchati
|
vardhante tatra pāpāni dharmo hrasati ca dhruvam ||30||

31iti kāruṇyaśīlastu vidvānvai brāhmaṇo'nvaśāt
|
iti caivānuśiṣṭo'smi pūrvaistāta pitāmahaiḥ ||31||

32āśvāsayadbhiḥ subhṛśamanukrośāttathaiva ca
|
etatprathamakalpena rājā kṛtayuge yajet ||32||

33pādonenāpi dharmeṇa gacchettretāyuge tathā
|
dvāpare tu dvipādena pādena tvadhare yuge ||33||

34tathā kaliyuge prāpte rājño duścaritena ha
|
bhavetkālaviśeṣaṇa kalā dharmasya ṣoḍaśī ||34||

35atha prathamakalpena satyavansaṃkaro bhavet
|
āyuḥ śaktiṃ ca kālaṃ ca nirdiśya tapa ādiśet ||35||

36satyāya hi yathā neha jahyāddharmaphalaṃ mahat
|
bhūtānāmanukampārthaṃ manuḥ svāyambhuvo'bravīt ||36||
