Mahābhārata 1.100 (Ādiparvan)

1vaiśaṃpāyana uvāca|
sa rājā śāntanurdhīmāndevarājarṣisatkṛtaḥ
|
dharmātmā sarvalokeṣu satyavāgiti viśrutaḥ||1||

2damo dānaṃ kṣamā buddhirhrīrdhṛtisteja uttamam
|
nityānyāsanmahāsatve śāntanau puruṣarṣabhe||2||

3evaṃ sa guṇasaṃpanno dharmārthakuśalo nṛpaḥ
|
āsīdbharatavaṃśasya goptā sarvajanasya ca||3||

4kambugrīvaḥ pṛthuvyaṃso mattavāraṇavikramaḥ
|
anvitaḥ paripūrṇārthaiḥ sarvairnṛpatilakṣaṇaiḥ||4||

5tasya kīrtimato vṛttamavekṣya satataṃ narāḥ
|
dharma eva paraḥ kāmādarthācceti vyavasthitaḥ||5||

6etānyāsanmahāsattve śāntanau puruṣarṣabhe
|
na cāsya sadṛśaḥ kaściddharmataḥ pārthivo'bhavat||6||

7vartamānaṃ hi dharmeṣu sarvadharmabhṛtāṃ varam
|
taṃ mahīpā mahīpālaṃ rājarājye'bhyaṣecayan||7||

8vītaśokabhayābādhāḥ sukhasvapnanibodhanāḥ
|
patiṃ bhāratagoptāraṃ samapadyanta bhūmipāḥ||8||

9tena kīrtimatā śiṣṭāḥ śakrapratimatejasā
|
yajñadānakriyāśīlāḥ samapadyanta bhūmipāḥ||9||

10śāntanupramukhairgupte loke nṛpatibhistadā
|
niyamātsarvavarṇānāṃ dharmottaramavartata||10||

11brahma paryacaratkṣatraṃ viśaḥ kṣatramanuvratāḥ
|
brahmakṣatrānuraktāśca śūdrāḥ paryacaranviśaḥ||11||

12sa hāstinapure ramye kurūṇāṃ puṭabhedane
|
vasansāgaraparyantāmanvaśāsadvasundharām||12||

13sa devarājasadṛśo dharmajñaḥ satyavāgṛjuḥ
|
dānadharmatapoyogācchriyā paramayā yutaḥ||13||

14arāgadveṣasaṃyuktaḥ somavatpriyadarśanaḥ
|
tejasā sūryakalpo'bhūdvāyuvegasamo jave
|
antakapratimaḥ kope kṣamayā pṛthivīsamaḥ||14||

15vadhaḥ paśuvarāhāṇāṃ tathaiva mṛgapakṣiṇām
|
śāntanau pṛthivīpāle nāvartata tathā nṛpa||15||

16brahmadharmottare rājye śāntanurvinayātmavān
|
samaṃ śaśāsa bhūtāni kāmarāgavivarjitaḥ||16||

17devarṣipitṛyajñārthamārabhyanta tadā kriyāḥ
|
na cādharmeṇa keṣāṃcitprāṇināmabhavadvadhaḥ||17||

18asukhānāmanāthānāṃ tiryagyoniṣu vartatām
|
sa eva rājā sarveṣāṃ bhūtānāmabhavatpitā||18||

19tasminkurupatiśreṣṭhe rājarājeśvare sati
|
śritā vāgabhavatsatyaṃ dānadharmāśritaṃ manaḥ||19||

20sa samāḥ ṣoḍaśāṣṭau ca catasro'ṣṭau tathā'parāḥ
|
ratimaprāpnuvanstrīṣu babhūva vanagocaraḥ||20||

21tathārūpastathācārastathāvṛttastathāśrutaḥ
|
gāṅgeyastasya putro'bhūnnāmnā devavrato vasuḥ||21||

22sarvāstreṣu sa niṣṇātaḥ pārthiveṣvitareṣu ca
|
mahābalo mahāsatvo mahāvīryo mahārathaḥ||22||

23sa kadācinmṛgaṃ viddhvā gaṅgāmanusarannadīm
|
bhāgīrathīmalpajalāṃ śāntanurdṛṣṭavānnṛpaḥ||23||

24tāṃ dṛṣṭvā cintayāmāsa śāntanuḥ puruṣarṣabhaḥ
|
syandate kiṃ nviyaṃ nādya saricchreṣṭhā yathā purā||24||

25tato nimittamanvicchandadarśa sa mahāmanāḥ
|
kumāraṃ rūpasaṃpannaṃ bṛhantaṃ cārudarśanam||25||

26divyamastraṃ vikurvāṇaṃ yathā devaṃ purandaram
|
kṛtsnāṃ gaṅgāṃ samāvṛtya śaraistīkṣṇairavasthitam||26||

27tāṃ śarairācitāṃ dṛṣṭvā nadīṃ gaṅgāṃ tadantike
|
abhavadvismito rājā dṛṣṭvā kramātirmānuṣam||27||

28jātamātraṃ purā dṛṣṭaṃ taṃ putraṃ śāntanustadā
|
nopalebhe smṛtiṃ dhīmānabhijñātuṃ tamātmajam||28||

29sa tu taṃ pitaraṃ dṛṣṭvā mohayāmāsa māyayā
|
saṃmohya tu tataḥ kṣipraṃ tatraivāntaradhīyata||29||

30tadadbutaṃ tato dṛṣṭvā tatra rājā sa śāntanuḥ
|
saṅkamānaḥ sutaṃ gaṅgāmabravīddarśayeti ha||30||

31darśayāmāsa taṃ gaṅgā bibhratī rūpamuttamam
|
gṛhītvā dakṣiṇe pāṇau taṃ kumāramalaṅkṛtam||31||

32alaṅkṛtāmābharaṇairvirajombarasaṃvṛttam
|
dṛṣṭapūrvāmapi sa tāṃ nābhyajānātsa śāntanuḥ||32||

33gaṅgovāca
|
yaṃ putramaṣṭamaṃ rājaṃstvaṃ purā mayyavindathāḥ
|
sa cāyaṃ puruṣavyāghra sarvāstravidanuttamaḥ||33||

34gṛhāṇemaṃ mahārāja mayā saṃvardhitaṃ sutam
|
ādāya puruṣavyāghra nayasvainaṃ gṛhaṃ vibho||34||

35vedānadhijage sāṅgānvasiṣṭhādeṣa vīryavān
|
kṛtāstraḥ parameṣvāso devarājasamo yudhi||35||

36surāṇāṃ saṃmato nityamasurāṇāṃ ca bhārata
|
uśanā vedayacchāstramayaṃ tadveda sarvaśaḥ||36||

37tathaivāṅgirasaḥ putraḥ surasuranamaskṛtaḥ
|
yadveda śāstraṃ taccāpi kṛtsnamasminpratiṣṭhitam||37||

38tava putre mahābāhau sāṅgopāṅgaṃ mahātmani
|
ṛṣiḥ parairanādhṛṣyo jāmadagnyaḥ pratāpavān||38||

39yadastraṃ veda rābhaśca tadetasminpratiṣṭhitam
|
maheṣvāsamimaṃ rājanrājadharmārthakovidam||39||

40mayā dattaṃ nijaṃ putraṃ vīraṃ vīra gṛhaṃ naya
|
vaiśaṃpāyana uvāca|
tayaivaṃ samanujñātaḥ putramādāya śāntanuḥ||40||

41bhrājamānaṃ yathā'dityamāyayau svapuraṃ prati
|
pauravastu purīṃ gatvā purandarapuropamām||41||

42sarvakāmasamṛddhārthaṃ mene sotmānamātmanā
|
pauraveṣu tataḥ putraṃ rājyārthamabhayapradam||42||

43guṇavantaṃ mahātmānaṃ yauvarājye'bhyaṣecayat
|
pauravāñśāntanoḥ putraḥ pitaraṃ ca mahāyaśāḥ||43||

44rāṣṭraṃ ca rañjayāmāsa vṛttena bharatarṣabha
|
sa tathā saha putreṇa ramamāṇo mahīpatiḥ||44||

45vartayāmāsa varṣāṇi catvāryamitavikramaḥ
|
sa kadācidvanaṃ yāto yamunāmabhito nadīm||45||

46mahīpatiranirdeśyamājighradgandhamuttamam
|
tasya prabhavamanvicchanvicacāra samantataḥ||46||

47sa dadarśa tadā kanyāṃ dāśānāṃ devarūpiṇīm
|
tāmapṛcchatsa dṛṣṭvaiva kanyāmasitalocanām||47||

48kasya tvamasi kā cāsi kiṃ ca bhīru cikīrṣasi
|
sā'bravīddāśakanyā'smi dharmārthaṃ vāhaye tarim||48||

49piturniyogādbhadraṃ te dāśarājño mahātmanaḥ
|
rūpamādhuryagandhaistāṃ saṃyuktāṃ devarūpiṇīm||49||

50samīkṣya rājā dāśeyīṃ kāmayāmāsa śāntanuḥ
|
sa gatvā pitaraṃ tasyā varayāmāsa tāṃ tadā||50||

51paryapṛcchattatastasyāḥ pitaraṃ sotmakāraṇāt
|
sa ca taṃ pratyuvācedaṃ dāśarājo mahīpatim||51||

52jātamātraiva me deyā varāya varavarṇinī
|
hṛdi kāmastu me kaścittaṃ nibodha janeśvara||52||

53yadīmāṃ dharmapatnīṃ tvaṃ mattaḥ prārthayase'nagha
|
satyavāgasi satyena samayaṃ kuru me tataḥ||53||

54samayena pradadyāṃ te kanyāmahamimāṃ nṛpa
|
na hi me tvatsamaḥ kaścidvaro jātu bhaviṣyati||54||

55śāntanuruvāca|
śrutvā tava varaṃ dāśa vyavasyeyamahaṃ tava
|
dātavyaṃ cetpradāsyāmi na tvadeyaṃ kathaṃcana||55||

56dāśa uvāca|
asyāṃ jāyeta yaḥ putraḥ sa rājā pṛthivīpate
|
tvadūrdhvamabhiṣektavyo nānyaḥ kaścana pārthiva||56||

57vaiśaṃpāyana uvāca|
nākāmayata taṃ dātuṃ varaṃ dāśāya śāntanuḥ
|
śarīrajena tīvreṇa dahyamāno'pi bhārata||57||

58sa cintayanneva tadā dāśakanyāṃ mahīpatiḥ
|
pratyayāddhāstinapuraṃ kāmopahatacetanaḥ||58||

59tataḥ kadācicchocantaṃ śāntanuṃ dhyānamāsthitam
|
putro devavrato'bhyetya pitaraṃ vākyamabravīt||59||

60sarvato bhavataḥ kṣemaṃ vidheyāḥ sarvapārthivāḥ
|
tatkimarthamihābhīkṣṇaṃ pariśocasi duḥkhitaḥ||60||

61dhyāyanniva ca māṃ rājannābhibhāṣasi kiṃcana
|
na cāśvena viniryāsi vivarṇo hariṇaḥ kṛśaḥ||61||

62vyādhimicchāmi te jñātuṃ pratikuryāṃ hi tatra vai
|
evamuktaḥ sa putreṇa śāntanuḥ pratyabhāṣata||62||

63asaṃśayaṃ dhyānaparo yathā vatsa tathā śṛṇu
|
apatyaṃ nastvamevaikaḥ kule mahati bhārata||63||

64śastranityaśca satataṃ pauruṣe paryavasthitaḥ
|
anityatāṃ ca lokānāmanuśocāmi putraka||64||

65kathaṃcittava gāṅgeya vipattau nāsti naḥ kulam
|
asaṃśayaṃ tvamevaikaḥ śatādapi varaḥ sutaḥ||65||

66na cāpyahaṃ vṛthā bhūyo dārānkartumihotsahe
|
saṃtānasyāvināśāya kāmaye bhadramastu te||66||

67anapatyataikaputratvamityāhurdharmavādinaḥ
|
agnihotraṃ trayī vidyā saṃtānamapi cākṣayam||67||

68sarvāṇyetānyapatyasya kalāṃ nārhanti ṣoḍaśīm
|
evametanmanuṣyeṣu tacca sarvaṃ prajāsviti||68||

69yadapatyaṃ mahāprājña tatra me nāsti saṃśayaḥ
|
eṣā trayī purāṇānāṃ devatānāṃ ca śāśvatī||69||

70tvaṃ ca śūraḥ sadā'marṣī śastranityaśca bhārata
|
nānyatra yuddhāttasmātte nidhanaṃ vidyate kvacit||70||

71so'smi saṃśayamāpannastvayi śānte kathaṃ bhavet
|
iti te kāraṇaṃ tāta duḥkhasyoktamaśeṣataḥ||71||

72vaiśaṃpāyana uvāca|
tatastatkāraṇaṃ rājño jñātvā sarvamaśeṣataḥ
|
devavrato mahābuddhiḥ prajñayā cānvacintayat||72||

73abhyagacchattadaivāśu vṛddhāmātyaṃ piturhitam
|
tamapṛcchattadā'bhyetya pitustacchokakāraṇam||73||

74tasmai sa kurumukhyāya yathāvatparipṛcchate
|
varaṃ śaśaṃsa kanyāṃ tāmuddiśya bharatarṣabha ||74||

75tato devavrato vṛddhaiḥ kṣatriyaiḥ sahitastadā
|
abhigamya dāśarājaṃ kanyāṃ vavre pituḥ svayam||75||

76taṃ dāśaḥ pratijagrāha vidhivatpratipūjya ca
|
abravīccainamāsīnaṃ rājasaṃsadi bhārata||76||

77tvameva nāthaḥ paryāptaḥ śāntanorbharatarṣabha
|
putraḥ śastrabhṛtāṃ śreṣṭhaḥ kiṃ nu vakṣyāmi te vacaḥ||77||

78kohi saṃbandhakaṃ ślāghyamīpsitaṃ yaunamīdṛśam
|
atikrāmanna tapyeta sākṣādapi śatakratuḥ||78||

79apatyaṃ caitadāryasya yo yuṣmākaṃ samo guṇaiḥ
|
yasya śukrātsatyavatī saṃbhūtā varavarṇinī||79||

80tena me bahuśastāta pitā te parikīrtitaḥ
|
arhaḥ satyavatīṃ voḍhuṃ dharmajñaḥ sa narādhipaḥ||80||

81asito hyapi devarṣiḥ pratyākhyātaḥ purā mayā
|
satyavatyā bhṛśaṃ hyarthī sa āsīdṛṣisattamaḥ||81||

82kanyāpitṛtvātkiṃcittu vakṣyāmi tvāṃ narādhipa
|
balavatsapatnatāmatra doṣaṃ paśyāmi kevalam||82||

83yasya hi tvaṃ sapatnaḥ syā gandharvasyāsurasya vā
|
na sa jātu ciraṃ jīvettvayi kruddhe parantapa||83||

84etāvānatra doṣo hi nānyaḥ kaścana pārthiva
|
etajjānīhi bhadraṃ te dānādāne parantapa||84||

85vaiśaṃpāyana uvāca|
evamuktastu gāṅgeyastadyuktaṃ pratyabhāṣata
|
śṛṇvatāṃ bhūmipālānāṃ piturarthāya bhārata||85||

86idaṃ me vratamādatsva satyaṃ satyavatāṃ vara
|
naiva jāto na vājāta īdṛśaṃ vaktumutsahet||86||

87evametatkariṣyāmi yathā tvamanubhāṣase
|
yo'syāṃ janiṣyate putraḥ sa no rājā bhaviṣyati||87||

88ityuktaḥ punarevātha taṃ dāśaḥ pratyabhāṣata
|
cikīrṣurduṣkaraṃ karma rājyārthe bharatarṣabha||88||

89tvameva nāthaḥ saṃprāptaḥ śāntanoramitadyute
|
kanyāyāścaiva dharmātmanprabhurdānāya ceśvaraḥ||89||

90idaṃ tu vacanaṃ saumya kāryaṃ caiva nibodha me
|
kaumārikāṇāṃ śīlena vakṣyāmyahamarindama||90||

91yattvayā satyavatyarthe satyadharmaparāyaṇa
|
rājamadhye pratijñātamanurūpaṃ tavaiva tat||91||

92nānyathā tanmahābāho saṃśayo'tra na kaścana
|
tavāpatyaṃ bhavedyattu tatra naḥ saṃśayo mahān||92||

93vaiśaṃpāyana uvāca|
tasyaitanmatamājñāya satyadharmaparāyaṇaḥ
|
pratyajānāttadā rājanpituḥ priyacikīrṣayā||93||

94gāṅgeya uvāca|
dāśarāja nibodhedaṃ vacanaṃ me nṛpottama
|
śṛṇvatāṃ bhūmipālānāṃ yadbravīmi pituḥ kṛte||94||

95rājyaṃ tāvatpūrvameva mayā tyaktaṃ narādhipāḥ
|
apatyahetorapi ca kariṣye'dya viniścayam||95||

96adyaprabhṛti me dāśa brahmacaryaṃ bhaviṣyati
|
aputrasyāpi me lokā bhaviṣyantyakṣayā divi||96||

97vaiśaṃpāyana uvāca|
tasya tadvacanaṃ śrutvā saṃprahṛṣṭatanūruhaḥ
|
dadānītyeva taṃ dāśo dharmātmā pratyabhāṣata||97||

98tatontarikṣe'psaraso devāḥ sarṣigaṇāstadā
|
abhyavarṣanta kusumairbhīṣmo'yamiti cābruvan||98||

99tataḥ sa piturarthāya tāmuvāca yaśasvinīm
|
adhiroha rathaṃ mātargacchāvaḥ svagṛhāniti||99||

100vaiśaṃpāyana uvāca|
evamuktvā tu bhīṣmastāṃ rathamāropya bhāminīṃ||
āgamya hāstinapuraṃ śāntanoḥ saṃnyavedayat||100||

101tasya tadduṣkaraṃ karma praśaśaṃsurnārādhipāḥ
|
sametāśca pṛthakcaiva bhīṣmoyamiti cābruvan||101||

102tacchrutvā duṣkaraṃ karma kṛtaṃ bhīṣmeṇa śāntanuḥ
|
svacchandamaraṇaṃ tuṣṭo dadau tasmai mahātmane||102||

103na te mṛtyuḥ prabhavitā yāvajjīvitumicchasi
|
tvatto hyanujñāṃ saṃprāpya mṛtyuḥ prabhavitā'nagha||103||
