Mahābhārata 8.78 (Karṇaparvan)

1dhṛtarāṣṭra uvāca|
tato bhagneṣu sainyeṣu bhīmasenena saṃyuge
|
duryodhano'bravītkiṃ nu saubalo vāpi sañjaya ||1||

2karṇo vā jayatāṃ śreṣṭho yodhā vā māmakā yudhi
|
kṛpo vā kṛtavarmā vā drauṇirduḥśāsano'pi vā ||2||

3atyadbhutamahaṃ manye pāṇḍaveyasya vikramam
|
yadekaḥ samare sarvān yodhayāmāsa māmakān ||3||

4yathāpratijñaṃ yodhānāṃ rādheyaḥ kṛtavānapi
|
kurūṇāmatha sarveṣāṃ karṇaḥ śatruniṣūdanaḥ ||4||

5śarma varma pratiṣṭhā ca jīvitāśā ca sañjaya
|
tatprabhagnaṃ balaṃ dṛṣṭvā kaunteyenāmitaujasā ||5||

6rādheyo vāpyādhirathiḥ karṇaḥ kimakarodyudhi
|
putrā vā mama durdharṣā rājāno vā mahārathāḥ
|
etanme sarvamācakṣva kuśalo hyasi sañjaya ||6||

7sañjaya uvāca|
aparāhṇe mahārāja sūtaputraḥ pratāpavān
|
jaghāna somakānsarvānbhīmasenasya paśyataḥ ||7||

8bhīmo'pyatibalaṃ sainyaṃ dhārtarāṣṭraṃ vyapothayat
|
atha karṇo'bravīcchalyaṃ pañcālānprāpayasva mām ||8||

9drāvyamāṇaṃ balaṃ dṛṣṭvā bhīmasenena dhīmatā
|
yantāramabravītkarṇaḥ pañcālāneva māṃ vaha ||9||

10madrarājastataḥ śalyaḥ śvetānaśvānmahājavān
|
prāhiṇoccedipañcālānkarūṣāṃśca mahābalaḥ ||10||

11praviśya ca mahatsainyaṃ śalyaḥ parabalārdanaḥ
|
nyayacchatturagān hṛṣṭo yatra yatraicchadagraṇīḥ ||11||

12taṃ rathaṃ meghasaṃkāśaṃ vaiyāghraparivāraṇam
|
saṃdṛśya pāṇḍupañcālāstrastā hyāsanviśāṃpate ||12||

13tato rathasya ninadaḥ prādurāsīnmahāraṇe
|
parjanyasamanirghoṣaḥ parvatasyeva dīryataḥ ||13||

14tataḥ śaraśataistīkṣṇaiḥ karṇa ākarṇaniḥsṛtaiḥ
|
jaghāna pāṇḍavabalaṃ śataśo'tha sahasraśaḥ ||14||

15taṃ tathā samare karma kurvāṇamaparājitam
|
parivavrurmaheṣvāsāḥ pāṇḍavānāṃ mahārathāḥ ||15||

16taṃ śikhaṇḍī ca bhīmaśca dhṛṣṭadyumnaśca pārṣataḥ
|
nakulaḥ sahadevaśca draupadeyāśca sātyakiḥ ||16||

17parivavrurjighāṃsanto rādheyaṃ śaravṛṣṭibhiḥ
|
sātyakistu tadā karṇaṃ viṃśatyā niśitaiḥ śaraiḥ ||17||

18atāḍayadraṇe śūro jatrudeśe narottamaḥ
|
śikhaṇḍī pañcaviṃśatyā dhṛṣṭadyumnaśca saptabhiḥ ||18||

19draupadeyāścatuḥṣaṣṭyā sahadevaśca saptabhiḥ
|
nakulaśca śatenājau karṇaṃ vivyādha sāyakaiḥ ||19||

20bhīmasenastu rādheyaṃ navatyā nataparvaṇām
|
vivyādha samare kruddho jatrudeśe mahābalaḥ ||20||

21atha prahasyādhirathirvyākṣipaddhanuruttamam
|
mumoca niśitānbāṇān pīḍayansumahābalaḥ ||21||

22tānpratyavidhyadrādheyaḥ pañcabhiḥ pañcabhiḥ śaraiḥ
|
sātyakestu dhanuśchittvā dhvajaṃ ca bharatarṣabha ||22||

23taṃ tathā navabhirbāṇairājaghāna stanāntare
|
bhīmasenaṃ tataḥ kuddho vivyādha triṃśatā śaraiḥ ||23||

24sahadevasya bhallena dhvajaṃ ciccheda māriṣa
|
sārathiṃ ca tribhirbāṇairājaghāna parantapaḥ ||24||

25virathāndraupadeyāṃśca cakāra bharatarṣabha
|
akṣṇornimeṣamātreṇa tadadbhutamivābhavat ||25||

26vimukhīkṛtya tānsarvāñśaraiḥ sannataparvabhiḥ
|
pañcālānahanacchūrāṃścedīnāṃ ca mahārathān ||26||

27te vadhyamānāḥ samare cedimatsyā viśāṃpate
|
karṇamekamabhidrutya śarasaṅghaiḥ samārpayan ||27||

28tāñjaghāna śitairbāṇaiḥ sūtaputro mahārathaḥ
|
te vadhyamānāḥ samare cedimatsyā viśāṃpate ||28||

29prādravanta raṇe bhītāḥ siṃhatrastā mṛgā iva
|
etadatyadbhutaṃ karma dṛṣṭavānasmi bhārata ||29||

30yadekaḥ samare śūrānsūtaputraḥ pratāpavān
|
yatamānānparaṃ śaktyā yodhayānāṃśca dhanvinaḥ ||30||

31pāṇḍaveyānmahārāja śarairvāritavān raṇe
|
tatra bhārata karṇasya lāghavena mahātmanaḥ ||31||

32tutuṣurdevatāḥ sarvāḥ siddhāśca saha cāraṇaiḥ
|
apūjayanmaheṣvāsā dhārtarāṣṭrā narottamam ||32||

33karṇaṃ rathavaraśreṣṭhaṃ śreṣṭhaṃ sarvadhanuṣmatām
|
tataḥ karṇo mahārāja dadāha ripuvāhinīm ||33||

34kakṣamiddho yathā vahnirnidāghe jvalito mahān
|
te vadhyamānāḥ karṇena pāṇḍaveyāstatastataḥ ||34||

35prādravanta raṇe bhītāḥ karṇaṃ dṛṣṭvā mahāratham
|
tatrākrando mahānāsītpañcālānāṃ mahāraṇe ||35||

36vadhyatāṃ sāyakaistīkṣṇaiḥ karṇacāpavaracyutaiḥ
|
tena śabdena vitrastā pāṇḍavānāṃ mahācamūḥ ||36||

37karṇamekaṃ raṇe yodhaṃ menire tatra śātravāḥ
|
tatrādbhutaṃ punaścakre rādheyaḥ śatrukarśanaḥ ||37||

38yadenaṃ pāṇḍavāḥ sarve na śekurabhivīkṣitum
|
yathaughaḥ parvataśreṣṭhamāsādyābhipradīryate ||38||

39tathā tatpāṇḍavaṃ sainyaṃ karṇamāsādya dīryate
|
karṇo'pi samare rājanvidhūmo'gniriva jvalan ||39||

40dahaṃstasthau mahābāhuḥ pāṇḍavānāṃ mahācamūm
|
śirāṃsi ca mahārāja karṇāṃścaiva sakuṇḍalān ||40||

41bāhūṃśca vīro vīrāṇāṃ ciccheda laghu ceṣubhiḥ
|
hastidantatsarūn khaḍgān dhvajāñśaktīrhayān gajān ||41||

42rathāṃśca vividhānrājanpatākā vyajanāni ca
|
akṣaṃ ca yugayoktrāṇi cakrāṇi vividhāni ca ||42||

43ciccheda bahudhā karṇo yodhavratamanuṣṭhitaḥ
|
tatra bhārata karṇena nihatairgajavājibhiḥ ||43||

44agamyarūpā pṛthivī māṃsaśoṇitakardamā
|
viṣamaṃ ca samaṃ caiva hatairaśvapadātibhiḥ ||44||

45rathaiśca kuñjaraiścaiva na prājñāyata kiñcana
|
nāpi sve na pare yodhāḥ prājñāyanta parasparam ||45||

46ghore śarāndhakāre tu karṇāstre ca vijṛmbhite
|
rādheyacāpanirmuktaiḥ śaraiḥ kāñcanabhūṣaṇaiḥ ||46||

47saṃchāditā mahārāja pāṇḍavānāṃ mahārathāḥ
|
te pāṇḍaveyāḥ samare rādheyena punaḥpunaḥ ||47||

48abhajyanta mahārāja yatamānā mahārathāḥ
|
mṛgasaṅghān yathā kruddhaḥ siṃho drāvayate vane ||48||

49pañcālānāṃ rathaśreṣṭhāndrāvayavañśātravāṃstathā
|
karṇastu samare yodhāṃstrāsayansumahāyaśāḥ ||49||

50kālayāmāsa tatsainyaṃ yathā paśugaṇān vṛkaḥ
|
dṛṣṭvā tu pāṇḍavīṃ senāṃ dhārtarāṣṭrāḥ parāṅmukhīm ||50||

51tatrājagmurmaheṣvāsā ruvanto bhairavānravān
|
duryodhano hi rājendra mudā paramayā yutaḥ ||51||

52vādayāmāsa saṃhṛṣṭo nānāvādyāni sarvaśaḥ
|
pañcālāpi maheṣvāsā bhagnāstatra narottamāḥ ||52||

53nyavartanta yathā śūraṃ mṛtyuṃ kṛtvā nivartanam
|
tānnivṛttānraṇe śūrānrādheyaḥ śatrutāpanaḥ ||53||

54anekaśo mahārāja babhañja puruṣarṣabhaḥ
|
tatra bhārata karṇena pañcālā viṃśatī rathāḥ ||54||

55nihatāḥ sāyakaiḥ krodhāccedayaśca paraṃśatāḥ
|
kṛtvā śūnyānrathopasthānvājipṛṣṭhāṃśca bhārata ||55||

56nirmanuṣyān gajaskandhān pādātāṃścaiva vidrutān
|
āditya iva madhyāhne durnirīkṣyaḥ parantapaḥ ||56||

57kālāntakavapuḥ śūraḥ sūtaputro'bhyarājata
|
evametanmahārāja naravājirathadvipān ||57||

58hatvā tasthau maheṣvāsaḥ karṇo'rigaṇasūdanaḥ
|
yathā bhūtagaṇānhatvā kālastiṣṭhenmahābalaḥ ||58||

59tathā sa somakānhatvā tasthāveko mahārathaḥ
|
tatrādbhutamapaśyāma pañcālānāṃ parākramam ||59||

60vadhyamānā'pi yatkarṇaṃ nājahū raṇamūrdhani
|
rājā duḥśāsanaścaiva kṛpaḥ śāradvatastathā ||60||

61aśvatthāmā kṛtavarmā śakuniśca mahābalaḥ
|
nyahananpāṇḍavīṃ senāṃ śataśo'tha sahasraśaḥ ||61||

62karṇaputrau tu rājendra bhrātarau satyavikramau
|
nijaghnāte balaṃ kruddhau pāṇḍavānāmitastataḥ ||62||

63tatra yuddhaṃ mahaccāsītkrūraṃ viśasanaṃ mahat
|
tathaiva pāṇḍavāḥ śūrā dhṛṣṭadyumnaśikhaṇḍinau ||63||

64draupadeyāśca saṃkruddhā abhyaghnaṃstāvakaṃ balam
|
evameṣa kṣayo vṛttaḥ pāṇḍavānāṃ tatastataḥ
|
tāvakānāmapi raṇe bhīmaṃ prāpya mahābalam ||64||
