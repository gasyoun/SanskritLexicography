Mahābhārata 12.234 (Śāntiparvan)

1vyāsa uvāca |
bhūtagrāme niyuktaṃ yattadetatkīrtitaṃ mayā
|
brāhmaṇasya tu yatkṛtyaṃ tatte vakṣyāmi tacchṛṇu ||1||

2jātakarmaprabhṛtyasya karmaṇāṃ dakṣiṇāvatām
|
kriyā syādāsamāvṛtterācārye vedapārage ||2||

3adhītya vedānakhilān guruśuśrūṣaṇe rataḥ
|
gurūṇāmanṛṇo bhūtvā samāvarteta yajñavit ||3||

4ācāryeṇābhyanujñātaścaturṇāmekamāśramam
|
āvimokṣāccharīrasya so'vatiṣṭhedyathāvidhi ||4||

5prajāsargeṇa dāraiśca brahmacaryeṇa vā punaḥ
|
vane, gurusakāśe, vā yatidharmeṇa vā punaḥ ||5||

6gṛhasthastveṣa dharmāṇāṃ sarveṣāṃ mūlamucyate
|
yatra pakvakaṣāyo hi dāntaḥ sarvatra sidhyati ||6||

7prajāvān śrotriyo yajvā mukta eva ṛṇaistribhiḥ
|
athānyānāśramānpaścātpūto gaccheta karmabhiḥ ||7||

8yatpṛthivyāṃ puṇyatamaṃ vidyātsthānaṃ tadāvaset
|
yateta tasminprāmāṇyaṃ gantuṃ yaśasi cottame ||8||

9tapasā vā sumahatā vidyānāṃ pāraṇena vā
|
ijyayā vā pradānairvā viprāṇāṃ vardhate yaśaḥ ||9||

10yāvadasya bhavatyasminkīrtirloke yaśaskarī
|
tāvatpuṇyakṛtā lokānanantānpuruṣo'śnute ||10||

11adhyāpayedadhīyīta yājayeta yajeta vā
|
na vṛthā pratigṛhṇīyānna ca dadyātkathaṃcana ||11||

12yājyataḥ śiṣyato vā'pi kanyāyā vā dhanaṃ mahat
|
yadā''gacchedyajeddadyānnako'śnīyātkathaṃcana ||12||

13gṛhamāvasato hyasya nānyattīrthaṃ pratigrahāt
|
devarṣipitṛgurvarthaṃ vṛddhāturabubhukṣatām ||13||

14antarhitāritaptānāṃ yathāśakti bubhūṣatām
|
devānāmatiśaktyā'pi deyameṣā kṛtādapi ||14||

15arhatāmanurūpāṇāṃ nādeya hyasti kiṃcana
|
ucaiḥśravasamapyaśvaṃ prāpaṇīyaṃ satāṃ viduḥ ||15||

16anunīya yathākāmaṃ satyasandho mahāvrataḥ
|
svaiḥ prāṇairbrāhmaṇaprāṇānparitrāya divaṃ gataḥ ||16||

17rantidevaśca sāṃkṛtyo vasiṣṭhāya mahātmane
|
apaḥ pradāya śītoṣṇā nākapṛṣṭhe mahīyate ||17||

18ātreyaścendradamano hyarhate vividhaṃ dhanam
|
dattvā lokānyayau dhīmānanantānsa mahīpatiḥ ||18||

19śibirauśīnaro'ṅgāni sutaṃ ca priyamaurasam
|
brāhmaṇārthamupāhṛtya nākapṛṣṭhamito gataḥ ||19||

20pratardanaḥ kāśipatiḥ pradāya nayane svake
|
brāhmaṇāyātulāṃ kīrtimiha cāmutra cāśnute ||20||

21divyamaṣṭaśalākaṃ tu sauvarṇaṃ paramardhimat
|
chatraṃ devāvṛdho dattvā sarāṣṭro'bhyapataddivam ||21||

22sāṃkṛtiśca tathā''treyaḥ śiṣyebhyo brahma nirguṇam
|
upadiśya mahātejā gato lokānanuttamān ||22||

23ambarīṣo gavāṃ dattvā brāhmaṇebhyaḥ pratāpavān
|
arbudāni daśaikaṃ ca sarāṣṭro'bhyapataddivam ||23||

24sāvitrī kuṇḍale divye śarīraṃ janamejayaḥ
|
brāhmaṇārthe parityajya jagmaturlokamuttamam ||24||

25sarvaratnaṃ vṛṣādarbhiryuvanāśvaḥ priyāḥ striyaḥ
|
ramyamāvasathaṃ caiva datvā svarlokamāsthitaḥ ||25||

26nimī rāṣṭraṃ ca vaideho jāmadagnyo vasundharām
|
brāhmaṇebhyo dadau cāpi gayaścorvīṃ sapattanām ||26||

27avarṣati ca parjanye sarvabhūtāni bhūtakṛt
|
vasiṣṭho jīvayāmāsa prajāpatiriva prajāḥ ||27||

28karandhamasya putrastu kṛtātmā marutastathā
|
kanyāmaṅgirase dattvā divamāśu jagāma ha ||28||

29brahmadattaśca pāñcālyo rājā buddhimatāṃ varaḥ
|
nidhi śaṅkha dvijāgrebhyo dattvā lokānavāptavān ||29||

30rājā mitrasahaścāpi vasiṣṭhāya mahātmane
|
madayantīṃ priyāṃ dattvā tayā saha divaṃ gataḥ ||30||

31sahasrajicca rājarṣiḥ prāṇāniṣṭān mahāyaśāḥ
|
brāhmaṇārthaṃ parityajya gato lokānanuttamān ||31||

32sarvakāmaiśca sampūrṇaṃ dattvā veśma hiraṇmayam
|
mudgalāya gataḥ svargaṃ śatadyumno mahīpatiḥ ||32||

33nāmnā ca dyutimānnāma śālvarājaḥ pratāpavān
|
dattvā rājyamṛcīkāya gato lokānanuttamān ||33||

34lomapādaśca rājarṣiḥ śāntāṃ dattvā sutāṃ prabhuḥ
|
ṛṣyaśṛṅgāya vipulaiḥ sarvakāmairayujyata ||34||

35madirāśvaśca rājarṣirdattvā kanyāṃ sumadhyamām
|
hiraṇyahastāya gato lokāndevairabhiṣṭutān ||35||

36dattvā śatasahasraṃ tu gavāṃ rājā prasenajit
|
savatsānāṃ mahātejā gato lokānanuttamān ||36||

37ete cānye ca bahavo dānena tapasaiva ca
|
mahātmāno gatāḥ svargaṃ śiṣṭātmāno jitendriyāḥ ||37||

38teṣāṃ pratiṣṭhitā kīrtiryāvatsthāsyati medinī
|
dānayajñaprajāsargairete hi divamāpnuvan ||38||
