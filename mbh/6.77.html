Mahābhārata 6.77 (Bhīṣmaparvan)

1sañjaya uvāca|
ātmadoṣāttvayā rājan prāptaṃ vyasanamīdṛśam
|
na hi duryodhanastāni paśyate bharatarṣabha ||1||

2yāni tvaṃ dṛṣṭavān rājan dharmasaṅkarakārite
|
tava doṣāt purā vṛttaṃ dyūtameva viśāṃpate ||2||

3tava doṣeṇa yuddhaṃ ca pravṛttaṃ saha pāṇḍavaiḥ
|
tvamevādya phalaṃ bhuṅkṣu kṛtvā kilbiṣamātmanā ||3||

4ātmanaiva kṛtaṃ karma ātmanaivopabhujyate
|
iha ca pretya vā rājaṃstvayā prāptaṃ yathātatham ||4||

5tasmādrājan sthiro bhūtvā prāpyedaṃ vyasanaṃ mahat
|
śṛṇu yuddhaṃ yathāvṛttaṃ śaṃsato me narādhipa ||5||

6bhīmasenaḥ suniśitairbāṇairbhittvā mahācamūm
|
āsasāda tato vīraḥ sarvān duryodhanānujān ||6||

7duḥśāsanaṃ durviṣahaṃ duḥsahaṃ durmadaṃ jayam
|
jayatsenaṃ vikarṇaṃ ca citrasenaṃ sudarśanam ||7||

8cārucitraṃ suvarmāṇaṃ duṣkarṇaṃ karṇameva ca
|
etāṃścānyāṃśca subahūn samīpasthān mahārathān ||8||

9dhārtarāṣṭrān susaṅkruddhān dṛṣṭvā bhīmo mahārathaḥ
|
bhīṣmeṇa samare guptāṃ praviveśa mahācamūm ||9||

10athālokya praviṣṭaṃ tamūcuste sarva eva tu
|
jīvagrāhaṃ nigṛhṇīmo vayamenaṃ narādhipāḥ ||10||

11sa taiḥ parivṛtaḥ pārtho bhrātṛbhiḥ kṛtaniścayaiḥ
|
prajāsaṃharaṇe sūryaḥ krūrairiva mahāgrahaiḥ ||11||

12saṃprāpya madhyaṃ sainyasya na bhīḥ pāṇḍavamāviśat
|
yathā devāsure yuddhe mahendraṃ prāpya dānavān ||12||

13tataḥ śatasahasrāṇi rathināṃ sarvaśaḥ prabho
|
udyatāni śaraistīvraistamekaṃ parivavrire ||13||

14sa teṣāṃ pravarān yodhān hastyaśvarathasādinaḥ
|
jaghāna samare śūro dhārtarāṣṭrānacintayan ||14||

15teṣāṃ vyavasitaṃ jñātvā bhīmaseno jighṛkṣatām
|
samastānāṃ badhe rājan matiṃ cakre mahāmanāḥ ||15||

16tato rathaṃ samutsṛjya gadāmādāya pāṇḍavaḥ
|
jaghāna dhārtarāṣṭrāṇāṃ taṃ balaughaṃ mahārṇavam ||16||

17bhīmasene praviṣṭe tu dhṛṣṭadyumno'pi pārṣataḥ
|
droṇamutsṛjya tarasā prayayau yatra saubalaḥ ||17||

18nivārya mahatīṃ senāṃ tāvakānāṃ nararṣabhaḥ
|
āsasāda rathaṃ śūnyaṃ bhīmasenasya saṃyuge ||18||

19dṛṣṭvā viśokaṃ samare bhīmasenasya sārathim
|
dhṛṣṭadyumno mahārāja durmanā gatacetanaḥ ||19||

20apṛcchadbāṣpasaṃruddho niḥśvasan vācamīrayan
|
mama prāṇaiḥ priyatamaḥ kva bhīma iti duḥkhitaḥ ||20||

21viśokastamuvācedaṃ dhṛṣṭadyumnaṃ kṛtāñjaliḥ
|
saṃsthāpya māmiha balī pāṇḍaveyaḥ parākramī ||21||

22praviṣṭo dhārtarāṣṭrāṇāmetadbalamahārṇavam
|
māmuktvā puruṣavyāghraḥ prītiyuktamidaṃ vacaḥ ||22||

23pratipālaya māṃ sūta niyamyāśvān muhūrtakam
|
yāvadetānnihanmyadya ya ime madvadhodyatāḥ ||23||

24tato dṛṣṭvā pradhāvantaṃ gadāhastaṃ mahābalam
|
sarveṣāmeva sainyānāṃ saṃharṣaḥ samajāyata ||24||

25tasmin sutumule yuddhe vartamāne bhayānake
|
bhittvā rājan mahāvyūhaṃ praviveśa vṛkodaraḥ ||25||

26viśokasya vacaḥ śrutvā dhṛṣṭadyumno'tha pārṣataḥ
|
pratyuvāca tataḥ sūtaṃ raṇamadhye mahābalaḥ ||26||

27na hi me jīvitenāpi vidyate'dya prayojanam
|
bhīmasenaṃ raṇe hitvā snehamutsṛjya pāṇḍavaiḥ ||27||

28yadi yāmi vinā bhīmaṃ kiṃ māṃ kṣatraṃ vadiṣyati
|
ekāyanagate bhīme mayi cāvasthite yudhi ||28||

29asvasti tasya kurvanti devāḥ śakrapurogamāḥ
|
yaḥ sahāyān parityajya svastimānāvrajedgṛham ||29||

30mama bhīmaḥ sakhā caiva sambandhī ca mahābalaḥ
|
bhakto'smān bhaktimāṃścāhaṃ tamapyariniṣūdanam ||30||

31so'haṃ tatra gamiṣyāmi yatra yāto vṛkodaraḥ
|
nighnantaṃ māṃ ripūn paśya dānavāniva vāsavam ||31||

32evamuktvā tato vīro yayau madhyena bhārata
|
bhīmasenasya mārgeṣu gadāpramathitairgajaiḥ ||32||

33sa dadarśa tadā bhīmaṃ dahantaṃ ripuvāhinīm
|
vāto vṛkṣāniva balāt prabhañjantaṃ raṇe ripūn ||33||

34te vadhyamānāḥ samare rathinaḥ sādinastathā
|
pādātā dantinaścaiva cakrarārtasvaraṃ mahat ||34||

35hāhākāraśca sañjajñe tava sainyasya māriṣa
|
vadhyato bhīmasenena kṛtinā citrayodhinā ||35||

36tataḥ kṛtāstrāste sarve parivārya vṛkodaram
|
abhītāḥ samavartanta śastravṛṣṭyā parantapa ||36||

37abhidrutaṃ śastrabhṛtāṃ variṣṭhaṃ samantataḥ pāṇḍavaṃ lokavīraḥ
|
sainyena ghoreṇa susaṃhitena dṛṣṭvā balī pārṣato bhīmasenam ||37||

38athopagacchaccharavikṣatāṅgaṃ padātinaṃ krodhaviṣaṃ vamantam
|
āśvāsayan pārṣato bhīmasenaṃ gadāhastaṃ kālamivāntakāle ||38||

39viśalyamenaṃ ca cakāra tūrṇamāropayaccātmarathe mahātmā
|
bhṛśaṃ pariṣvajya ca bhīmasenamāśvāsayāmāsa sa śatrumadhye ||39||

40bhrātṝnathopetya tavāpi putrastasminvimarde mahati pravṛtte
|
ayaṃ durātmā drupadasya putraḥ samāgato bhīmasenena sārdham ||40||

41taṃ yāma sarve mahatā balena mā vo ripuḥ prārthayatāmanīkam
|
śrutvā tu vākyaṃ tamamṛṣyamāṇā jyeṣṭhājñayā noditā dhārtarāṣṭrāḥ ||41||

42vadhāya niṣpeturudāyudhāste yugakṣaye ketavo yadvadugrāḥ
|
pragṛhya cāstrāṇi dhanūṃṣi vīrā jyāṃ nemighoṣaiḥ pravikampayantaḥ ||42||

43śarairavarṣan drupadasya putraṃ yathā'mbudā bhūdharaṃ vārijālaiḥ
|
nihatya tāṃścāpi śaraiḥ sutīkṣṇairna vivyathe samare citrayodhī ||43||

44samabhyudīrṇāṃśca tavātmajāṃstathā niśamya vīrānabhitaḥ sthitān raṇe
|
jighāṃsurugraṃ drupadātmajo yuvā pramohanāstraṃ yuyuje mahārathaḥ ||44||

45kruddho bhṛśaṃ tava putreṣu rājan daityeṣu yadvatsamare mahendraḥ
|
tato vyamuhyanta raṇe nṛvīrāḥ pramohanāstrāhatabuddhisattvāḥ ||45||

46pradudruvuḥ kuravaścaiva sarve savājināgāḥ sarathāḥ samantāt
|
parītakālāniva naṣṭasaṃjñān mohopetāṃstava putrānniśamya ||46||

47etasminneva kāle tu droṇaḥ śastrabhṛtāṃ varaḥ
|
drupadaṃ tribhirāsādya śarairvivyādha dāruṇaiḥ ||47||

48so'tividdhastato rājan raṇe droṇena pārthivaḥ
|
apāyāddrupado rājan pūrvaveramanusmaran ||48||

49jitvā tu drupadaṃ droṇaḥ śaṅkhaṃ dadhmau pratāpavān
|
tasya śaṅkhasvanaṃ śrutvā vitresuḥ sarvasomakāḥ ||49||

50atha śuśrāva tejasvī droṇaḥ śastrabhṛtāṃ varaḥ
|
pramohanāstreṇa raṇe mohitānātmajāṃstava ||50||

51tato droṇo mahārāja tvarito'bhyāyayau raṇāt
|
tatrāpaśyanmaheṣvāso bhāradvājaḥ pratāpavān ||51||

52dhṛṣṭadyumnaṃ ca bhīmaṃ ca vicaranto mahāraṇe
|
mohāviṣṭāṃśca te putrānapaśyat sa mahārathaḥ ||52||

53tataḥ prajñāstramādāya mohanāstraṃ vyanāṃśayat
|
atha pratyāgataprāṇāstava putrā mahārathāḥ ||53||

54punaryuddhāya samare prayayurbhīmapārṣatau
|
tato yudhiṣṭhiraḥ prāha samāhūya svasainikān ||54||

55gacchantu padavīṃ śaktyā bhīmapārṣatayoryudhi
|
saubhadrapramukhā vīrā rathā dvādaśa daṃśitāḥ ||55||

56pravṛttimadhigacchantu na hi śuddhyati me manaḥ
|
ta evaṃ samanujñātāḥ śūrā vikrāntayodhinaḥ ||56||

57bāḍhamityevamuktvā tu sarve puruṣamāninaḥ
|
madhyandinagate sūrye prayayuḥ sarva eva hi ||57||

58kekayā draupadeyāśca dhṛṣṭaketuśca vīryavān
|
abhimanyuṃ puraskṛtya mahatyā senayā vṛtāḥ ||58||

59te kṛtvā samaravyūhaṃ sūcīmukhamarindamāḥ
|
bibhidurdhārtarāṣṭrāṇāṃ tadrathānīkamāhave ||59||

60tānprayātānmaheṣvāsānabhimanyupurogamān
|
bhīmasenabhayāviṣṭā dhṛṣṭadyumnavimohitā ||60||

61na saṃvārayituṃ śaktā tava senā janādhipa
|
madamūrcchānvitātmā vai pramadevādhvani sthitā ||61||

62te'bhijātā maheṣvāsāḥ suvarṇavikṛtadhvajāḥ
|
parīpsanto'bhyadhāvanta dhṛṣṭadyumnavṛkodarau ||62||

63tau ca dṛṣṭvā maheṣvāsāvabhimanyupurogamān
|
babhūvaturmudā yuktau nighnantau tava vāhinīm ||63||

64dṛṣṭvā tu sahasā yāntaṃ pāñcālyo gurumātmanaḥ
|
nāśaṃsata vadhaṃ vīraḥ putrāṇāṃ tava bhārata ||64||

65tato rathaṃ samāropya kaikeyasya vṛkodaram
|
abhyadhāvat susaṃkrudro droṇamiṣvastrapāragam ||65||

66tasyābhipatatastūrṇaṃ bhāradvājaḥ pratāpavān
|
kruddhaściccheda bāṇena dhanuḥ śatrunibarhaṇaḥ ||66||

67anyāṃśca śataśo bāṇān preṣayāmāsa pārṣate
|
duryodhanahitārthāya bhartṛpiṇḍamanusmaran ||67||

68athānyaddhanurādāya pārṣataḥ paravīrahā
|
droṇaṃ vivyādha viṃśatyā rukmapuṅkhaiḥ śilāśitaiḥ ||68||

69tasya droṇaḥ punaścāpaṃ cicchedāmitrakarśanaḥ
|
hayāṃśca caturastūrṇaṃ caturbhiḥ sāyakottamaiḥ ||69||

70vaivasvatakṣayaṃ ghoraṃ preṣayāmāsa bhārata
|
sārathiṃ cāsya bhallena preṣayāmāsa mṛtyave ||70||

71hatāśvāt sa rathāttūrṇamavaplutya mahārathaḥ
|
āruroha mahābāhurabhimanyormahāratham ||71||

72tataḥ sarathanāgāśvā samakampata vāhinī
|
paśyato bhīmasenasya pārṣatasya ca paśyataḥ ||72||

73tatprabhagnaṃ balaṃ dṛṣṭvā droṇenāmitatejasā
|
nāśaknuvan vārayituṃ samastāste mahārathāḥ ||73||

74vadhyamānaṃ tu tatsainyaṃ droṇena niśitaiḥ śaraiḥ
|
vyabhramattatra tatraiva kṣobhyamāṇa ivārṇavaḥ ||74||

75tathā dṛṣṭvā ca tatsainyaṃ jahṛṣe tāvakaṃ balam
|
dṛṣṭvā''cāryaṃ susaṃkruddhaṃ patantaṃ ripuvāhinīm
|
cukruśuḥ sarvato yodhāḥ sādhu sādhviti bhārata ||75||
