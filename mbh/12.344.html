Mahābhārata 12.344 (Śāntiparvan)

1naranārāyaṇāvūcatuḥ
|
dhanyo'syanugṛhīto'si yatte dṛṣṭaḥ svayaṃ prabhuḥ
|
na hi taṃ dṛṣṭavānkaścitpadmayonirapi svayam||1||

2avyaktayonirbhagavāndurdarśaḥ puruṣottamaḥ
|
nāradaitaddhi nau satyaṃ vacanaṃ samudāhṛtam||2||

3nāsya bhaktātpriyataro loke kaścana vidyate
|
tataḥ svayaṃ darśitavānsvamātmānaṃ dvijottama||3||

4tapo hi tapyatastasya yatsthāna paramātmanaḥ
|
na tatsaṃprāpnute kaścidṛte hyāvāṃ dvijottama||4||

5yā hi sūryasahasrasya samastasya bhaveddyutiḥ
|
sthānasya sā bhavettasya svayaṃ tena virājatā||5||

6tasmāduttiṣṭhate vipra devādviśvabhuvaḥ pateḥ
|
kṣamā kṣamāvatāṃ śreṣṭha yayā bhūmistu yujyate||6||

7tasmāccottiṣṭhate devātsarvabhūtahitādrasaḥ
|
āpo hi tena yujyante dravatvaṃ prāpnuvanti ca||7||

8tasmādeva samudbhūtaṃ tejo rūpaguṇātmakam
|
yena saṃyujyate sūryastato loke virājate||8||

9tasmāddevātsamudbhūtaḥ sparśastu puruṣottamāt
|
yena sma yujyate vāyustato lokānvivātyasau||9||

10tasmāccottiṣṭhate śabdaḥ sarvalokeśvarātprabhoḥ
|
ākāśaṃ yujyate yena tatastiṣṭhatyasaṃvṛtam||10||

11tasmāccottiṣṭhate devātsarvabhūtagataṃ manaḥ
|
candramā yena saṃyuktaḥ prakāśaguṇadhāraṇaḥ||11||

12sadbhūtotpādakaṃ nāma tatsthānaṃ vedasaṃjñitam
|
vidyāsahāyo yatrāste bhagavānhavyakavyabhuk||12||

13ye hi niṣkaluṣā loke puṇyapāpavivarjitāḥ
|
teṣāṃ vai kṣemamadhvānaṃ gacchatāṃ dvijasattama||13||

14sarvaloke tamo hantā ādityo dvāramucyate
|
ādityadagdhasarvāṅgā adṛśyāḥ kenacitkvacit||14||

15paramāṇubhūtā bhūtvā tu taṃ devaṃ praviśantyuta
|
tasmādapi ca nirmuktā aniruddhatanau sthitāḥ||15||

16manobhūtāstato bhūtvā pradyumnaṃ praviśantyuta
|
pradyumnāccāpi nirmuktā jīvaṃ saṃkarṣaṇaṃ tataḥ||16||

17viśanti viprapravarā: sāṃkhyā bhāgavataiḥ saha
|
tatastraiguṇyahīnāste paramātmānamañjasā||17||

18praviśanti dvijaśreṣṭhāḥ kṣetrajñaṃ nirguṇātmakam
|
sarvāvāsaṃ vāsudevaṃ kṣetrajñaṃ viddhiṃ tattvataḥ||18||

19samāhitamanaskāśca niyatāḥ saṃyatendriyāḥ
|
ekāntabhāvopagatā vāsudevaṃ viśanti te||19||

20āvāmapi ca dharmasya gṛhe jātau dvijottama
|
ramyāṃ viśālāmāśritya tapa ugraṃ samāsthitau||20||

21ye tu tasyaiva devasya prādurbhāvāḥ surapriyāḥ
|
bhaviṣyanti trilokasthāsteṣāṃ svatītyatho dvija||21||

22vidhinā svena yuktābhyāṃ yathāpūrvaṃ dvijottama
|
āsthitābhyāṃ sarvakṛcchraṃ vrataṃ samyaganuttamam||22||

23āvābhyāmapi dṛṣṭastvaṃ śvetadvīpe tapodhana
|
samāgato bhagavatā saṃkalpaṃ kṛtavāṃstathā||23||

24sarvaṃ hi nau svaṃ viditaṃ trailokye sacarācare
|
yadbhaviṣyati vṛttaṃ vā vartate vā śubhāśubham
|
sarvaṃ sa te kathitavāndevadevo mahāmune||24||

25vaiśampāyana uvāca|
etacchrutvā tayorvākyaṃ tapasyugre ca vartatoḥ
|
nāradaḥ prāñjalirbhūtvā nārāyaṇaparāyaṇaḥ||25||

26jajāpa vidhivanmantrānnārāyaṇagatānbahūn
|
divyaṃ varṣasahasraṃ hi naranārāyaṇāśrame||26||

27avasatsa mahātejā nārado bhagavānṛṣiḥ
|
tamevābhyarcayandevaṃ naranārāyaṇau ca tau||27||
