Mahābhārata 7.156 (Droṇaparvan)

1sañjaya uvāca|
prāyopaviṣṭe tu hate putre sātyakinā tadā
|
somada‌tto bhṛśaṃ kruddhaḥ sātyakiṃ vākyamabravīt||1||

2kṣatradharmaḥ purā dṛṣṭo yastu devairmahātmabhiḥ
|
taṃ tvaṃ sātvata santyajya dasyudharme kathaṃ rataḥ||2||

3parāṅmukhāya dīnāya nyastaśastrāyaṃ sātyake
|
kṣatradharmarataḥ prājñaḥ kathaṃ nu praharedraṇe||3||

4dvāveva kila vṛṣṇīnāṃ tatra khyātau mahārathau
|
pradyumnaśca mahābāhustvaṃ caiva yudhi sātvata||4||

5kathaṃ prāyopaviṣṭāya pārthena chinnabāhave
|
nṛśaṃsaṃ patanīyaṃ ca tādṛśaṃ kṛtavānasi||5||

6karmaṇastasya durvṛtta phalaṃ prāpnuhi saṃyuge
|
adya cchetsyāmi te mūḍha śiro vikramya patriṇā||6||

7śape sātvataputrābhyāmiṣṭena sukṛtena ca
|
anatītāmimāṃ rātriṃ yadi tvāṃ vīramāninam||7||

8arakṣyamāṇaṃ pārthena jiṣṇunā sasutānujam
|
na hanyānnarake ghore pateyaṃ vṛṣṇipāṃsana||8||

9evamuktvā susaṃkruddhaḥ somadatto mahābalaḥ
|
dadhmau śaṅkhaṃ ca tāreṇa siṃhanādaṃ nanāda ca||9||

10tataḥ kamalapatrākṣaḥ siṃhadaṃṣṭro durāsadaḥ
|
sātyakirbhṛśasaṃkruddhaḥ somadattamathābravīt||10||

11kauraveya na me trāsaḥ kathaṃcidapi vidyate
|
tvayā sārdhamathānyaiśca yudhyato hṛdi kaścana||11||

12yadi sarveṇa sainyena gupto māṃ yodhayiṣyasi
|
tathāpi na vyathā kācittvayi syānmama kaurava||12||

13yuddhasāreṇa vākyena asatāṃ saṃmatena ca
|
nāhaṃ bhīṣayituṃ śakyaḥ kṣatravṛtte sthitastvayā||13||

14yadi te'sti yuyutsā'dya mayā saha narādhipa
|
nirdayo niśitairbāṇaiḥ prahara praharāmi te||14||

15hato bhūriśravā vīrastava putro mahārathaḥ
|
śalaścaiva mahārāja bhrātṛvyasanakarṣitaḥ||15||

16tvāṃ cāpyadya vadhiṣyāmi sahaputraṃ sabāndhavam
|
tiṣṭhedānīṃ raṇe yattaḥ kauravo'si mahārathaḥ||16||

17yasmindānaṃ damaḥ śaucamahiṃsā hrīrdhṛtiḥ kṣamā
|
anapāyāni sarvāṇi nityaṃ rājñiyudhiṣṭhire||17||

18mṛdaṅgaketostasya tvaṃ tejasā nihataḥ purā
|
sakarṇasaubalaḥ saṃkhye vināśasupayāsyasi||18||

19śape'haṃ kṛṣṇacaraṇairiṣṭāpūrtena caiva ha
|
yadi tvāṃ sasutaṃ pāpaṃ na hanyāṃ yudhi roṣitaḥ||19||

20apayāsyasi cetyuktvā raṇaṃ mukto bhaviṣyasi
|
evamābhāṣya cānyonyaṃ krodhasaṃraktalocanau||20||

21pravṛttau śarasaṃpātaṃ kartuṃ puruṣasattamau
|
tato rathasahastreṇa nāgānāmayutena ca||21||

22duryodhanaḥ somadattaṃ parivārya samantataḥ
|
śakuniśca susaṃkruddhaḥ sarvaśastrabhṛtāṃ varaḥ||22||

23putrapautraiḥ parivṛto bhrātṛbhiścendravikramaiḥ
|
syālastava mahābāhurvajrasaṃhanano yuvā||23||

24sāgraṃ śatasahasraṃ tu hayānāṃ tasya dhīmataḥ
|
somadattaṃ maheṣvāsaṃ samantātparyarakṣata||24||

25rakṣyamāṇaśca balibhiśchādayāmāsa sātyakim
|
taṃ chādyamānaṃ viśikhairdṛṣṭvā sannataparvabhiḥ||25||

26dhṛṣṭadyumno'bhyayātkruddhaḥ pragṛhya mahatīṃ camūm
|
caṇḍavātābhisṛṣṭānāmudadhīnāmiva svanaḥ||26||

27āsīdrājanbalaughānāmanyonyamabhinighnatām
|
vivyādha somadattastu sātvataṃ navabhiḥ śaraiḥ||27||

28sātyakirnavabhiścainamavadhītkurupuṅgavam
|
so'tividdho balavatā samare dṛḍhadhanvinā||28||

29rathopasthaṃ samāsādya mumoha gatacetanaḥ
|
taṃ vimūḍhaṃ samālakṣya sārathistvarayā yutaḥ||29||

30apovāha raṇādvīraṃ somadattaṃ mahāratham
|
taṃ visaṃjñaṃ samālakṣya yuyudhānaśarārditam||30||

31abhyadravattato droṇo yaduvīrajighāṃsayā
|
tamāyāntamabhiprekṣya yudhiṣṭhirapurogamāḥ||31||

32parivavrurmahātmānaṃ parīpsanto yadūttamam
|
tataḥ pravavṛte yuddhaṃ droṇasya saha pāṇḍavaiḥ||32||

33baleriva suraiḥ pūrvaṃ trailokyajayakāṅkṣayā
|
tataḥ sāyakajālena pāṇḍavānīkamāvṛṇot||33||

34bhāradvājo mahātejā vivyādha ca yudhiṣṭhiram
|
sātyakiṃ daśabhirbāṇairviṃśatyā pārṣataṃ śaraiḥ||34||

35bhīmasenaṃ ca navabhirnakulaṃ pañcabhistathā
|
sahadevaṃ tathā'ṣṭābhiḥ śatena ca śikhaṇḍinam||35||

36draupadeyānmahābāhuḥ pañcabhiḥ pañcabhiḥ śaraiḥ
|
virāṭaṃ matsyamaṣṭābhirdrupadaṃ daśabhiḥ śaraiḥ||36||

37yudhāmanyuṃ tribhiḥ ṣaḍbhiruttamaujasamāhave
|
anyāṃśca sainikānviddhvā yudhiṣṭhiramupādravat||37||

38te vadhyamānā droṇena pāṇḍuputrasya sainikāḥ
|
prādravanvai bhayādrājan sārtanādā diśo daśa||38||

39kālyamānaṃ tu tatsainyaṃ dṛṣṭvā droṇena phālgunaḥ
|
kiñcidāgatasaṃraṃbho guruṃ pārtho'bhyayāddrutam||39||

40dṛṣṭvā droṇastu bībhatsumabhidhāvantamāhave
|
saṃnyavartata tatsainyaṃ punaryaudhiṣṭhiraṃ balam||40||

41tato yuddhamabhūdbhūyo bhāradvājasya pāṇḍavaiḥ
|
droṇastava sutai rājansarvataḥ parivāritaḥ||41||

42vyadhamatpāṇḍusainyāni tūlarāśimivānalaḥ
|
taṃ jvalantamivādityaṃ dīptānalasamadyutim||42||

43rājannaniśamatyantaṃ dṛṣṭvā droṇaṃ śarārciṣam
|
maṇḍalīkṛtadhanvānaṃ tapantamiva bhāskaram||43||

44dahantamahitānsainye nainaṃ kaścidavārayat
|
yo yo hi pramukhe tasya tasthau droṇasya pūruṣaḥ||44||

45tasya tasya śiraśchittvā yayurdoṇaśarāḥ kṣitim
|
evaṃ sā pāṇḍavī senā vadhyamānā mahātmanā||45||

46pradudrāva punarbhītā paśyataḥ savyasācinaḥ
|
saṃprabhagnaṃ calaṃ dṛṣṭvā droṇena niśi bhārata||46||

47govindamabravījjiṣṇurgaccha droṇarathaṃ prati
|
tato rajata-rajata-gokṣīra-kundendusadṛśaprabhān||47||

48codayāmāsa dāśārho hayāndroṇarathaṃ prati
|
bhīmaseno'pi taṃ dṛṣṭvā yāntaṃ droṇāya phālgunam||48||

49svasārathimuvācedaṃ droṇānīkāya māṃ vaha
|
so'pi tasya vacaḥ śrutvā viśoko vāhayaddhayān||49||

50pṛṣṭhataḥ satyasandhasya jiṣṇorbharatasattama
|
tau dṛṣṭvā bhrātarau yattau droṇānīkamabhidrutau ||50||

51pañcālāḥ sṛñjayā matsyāścedikārūṣakosalāḥ
|
anvagacchanmahārāja kekayāśca mahārathāḥ||51||

52tato rājannabhūdghoraḥ saṃgrāmo lomaharṣaṇaḥ
|
bībhatsurdakṣiṇaṃ pārśvamuttaraṃ ca vṛkodaraḥ||52||

53mahadbhyāṃ rathavṛndābhyāṃ balaṃ jagṛhatustava
|
tau dṛṣṭvā puruṣavyāghrau bhīmasenadhanañjayau||53||

54dhṛṣṭadyumno'bhyayādrājan sātyakiśca mahābalaḥ
|
caṇḍavātābhipannānāmudadhīnāmiva svanaḥ||54||

55āsīdrājanbalaughānāṃ tadā'nyonyamabhighnatām
|
saumadattivadhātkruddho dṛṣṭvā sātyakimāhave||55||

56drauṇirabhyadravadrājanvadhāya kṛtaniścayaḥ
|
tamāpatantaṃ saṃprekṣya śaineyasya rathaṃ prati||56||

57bhaimaseniḥ susaṃkruddhaḥ pratyabhitramavārayat
|
kārṣṇāyasaṃ mahāghoramṛkṣacarmaparicchadam||57||

58mahāntaṃ rathamāsthāya triṃśannalvāṃtarāntaram
|
vikṣiptayantrasannāhaṃ mahāmeghaughaniḥsvanam||58||

59yuktaṃ gajanibhairvāhairna hayairnāpi vāraṇaiḥ
|
vikṣiptapakṣacaraṇavivṛtākṣeṇa kūjatā||59||

60dhvajenocchritadaṇḍena gṛdhrarājena rājitam
|
lohitārdrapatākaṃ tu antramālāvibhūṣitam||60||

61aṣṭacakrasamāyuktamāsthāya vipulaṃ ratham
|
śūlamudgaradhāriṇyā śailapādapahastayā||61||

62rakṣasāṃ ghorarūpāṇāmakṣauhiṇyā samāvṛtaḥ
|
tamudyatamahācāpaṃ niśamya vyathitā nṛpāḥ||62||

63yugāntakālasamaye daṇḍahastamivāntakam
|
tatastaṃ giriśṛṅgābhaṃ bhīmarūpaṃ bhayāvaham||63||

64daṃṣṭrākarālogramukhaṃ śaṃkukarṇaṃ mahāhanum
|
ūrdhvakeśaṃ virūpākṣaṃ dīptāsyaṃ nimnitodaram||64||

65mahāśvabhragaladvāraṃ kirīṭacchannamūrdhajam
|
trāsanaṃ sarvabhūtānāṃ vyāttānanamivāntakam||65||

66vīkṣya dīptamivāyāntaṃ ripuvikṣobhakāriṇam
|
tamudyatamahācāpaṃ rākṣasendraṃ ghaṭotkacam||66||

67bhayārditā pracukṣobha putrasya tava vāhinī
|
vāyunā kṣobhitāvartā gaṅgevordhvataraṅgiṇī||67||

68ghaṭotkacaprayuktena siṃhanādena bhīṣitāḥ
|
prasusruvurgajā mūtraṃ vivyathuśca narā bhṛśam||68||

69tato'śmavṛṣṭiratyarthamāsīttatra samantataḥ
|
sandhyākālādhikabalaiḥ prayuktā rākṣasaiḥ kṣitau||69||

70āyasāni ca cakrāṇi bhuśuṇḍyaḥ prāsa-tomarāḥ
|
patantyaviratāḥ śūlāḥ śataghnyaḥ paṭṭiśāstathā||70||

71tadugramatiraudraṃ ca dṛṣṭvā yuddhaṃ narādhipāḥ
|
tanayāstava karṇaśca vyathitāḥ prādravandiśaḥ||71||

72tatraiko'stravalaślāghī drauṇirmānī na vivyathe
|
vyadhamacca śarairmāyāṃ ghaṭotkacavinirmitām||72||

73vihatāyāṃ tu māyāyāmamarṣī sa ghaṭotkacaḥ
|
visasarja śarānghorāṃsteśvatthāmānamāviśan||73||

74bhujaṅgā iva vegena valmīkaṃ krodhamūrcchitāḥ
|
te śarā rudhirāktāṅgā bhittvā śāradvatīsutam||74||

75viviśurdharaṇīṃ śīghrā rukmapuṅkhāḥ śilāśitāḥ
|
aśvatthāmā tu saṃkruddho laghuhastaḥ pratāpavān||75||

76ghaṭotkacamabhikruddhaṃ bibheda daśabhiḥ śaraiḥ
|
ghaṭotkaco'tividdhastu droṇaputreṇa marmasu||76||

77cakraṃ śatasahastrāramagṛhṇādvyathito bhṛśam
|
kṣurāntaṃ bālasūryābhaṃ maṇivajravibhūṣitam||77||

78aśvatthāmni sa cikṣepa bhaimasenirjighāṃsayā
|
vegena mahatā gacchadvikṣiptaṃ drauṇinā śaraiḥ||78||

79abhāgyasyeva saṃkalpastanmoghamapatadbhuvi
|
ghaṭotkacastatastūrṇaṃ dṛṣṭvā cakraṃ nipātitam||79||

80drauṇiṃ prācchādayadbāṇaiḥ svarbhānuriva bhāskaram
|
ghaṭotkacasutaḥ śrīmān bhinnāñjanacayopamaḥ||80||

81rurodha drauṇimāyāntaṃ prabhañjanamivādrirāṭ
|
pautreṇa bhīmasenasya śarairañjanaparvaṇā||81||

82babhau meghena dhārābhirgirirmerurivāvṛtaḥ
|
aśvatthāmā tvasaṃbhrānto rudropendrendravikramaḥ||82||

83dhvajamekena bāṇena cicchedāñjanaparvaṇaḥ
|
dvābhyāṃ tu rathayantārau tribhiścāsya triveṇukam||83||

84dhanurekena ciccheda caturbhiścaturo hayān
|
virathasyodyataṃ hastāddhemabindubhirācitam||84||

85viśikhena sutīkṣṇena khaḍgamasya dvidhā'karot
|
gadāṃ hemāṅgadāṃ rājaṃstūrṇaṃ haiḍimbisūnunā||85||

86bhrāmyotkṣiptā śaraiḥ sā'pi drauṇinābhyāhatā'patat
|
tato'ntarikṣamutplutya kālamegha ivonnadan||86||

87vavarṣāñjanaparvā sa drumavarṣaṃ nabhastalāt
|
tato māyādharaṃ drauṇirghaṭotkacasutaṃ divi||87||

88mārgaṇairabhivivyādha ghanaṃ sūrya ivāṃśubhiḥ
|
so'vatīrya purastasthau rathe hemavibhūṣite||88||

89mahīgata ivātyugraḥ śrīmānañjanaparvataḥ
|
tamayasmayavarmāṇaṃ drauṇirbhīmātmajātmajam||89||

90jaghānāñjanaparvāṇaṃ maheśvara ivāndhakam
|
atha dṛṣṭvā hataṃ putramaśvatthāmnā mahābalam||90||

91drauṇeḥ sakāśamabhyetya roṣātprajvalitāṅgadaḥ
|
prāha vākyamasaṃbhrānto vīraṃ śāradvatīsutam||91||

92dahantaṃ pāṇḍavānīkaṃ vanamagnimivocchritam
|
ghaṭotkaca uvāca|
tiṣṭha tiṣṭha na me jīvandroṇaputra gamiṣyasi||92||

93tvāmadya nihaniṣyāmi krauñcamagnisuto yathā
|
aśvatthāmovāca
|
gaccha vatsa sahānyaistvaṃ yudhyasvāmaravikrama||93||

94na hi putreṇa haiḍimbe pitā nyāyyaḥ prabādhitum
|
kāmaṃ khalu na roṣo me haiḍimbe vidyate tvayi||94||

95kiṃ tu roṣānvito janturhanyādātmānamapyuta
|
sañjaya uvāca|
śrutvaitatkrodhatāmrākṣaḥ putraśokasamanvitaḥ||95||

96aśvatthāmānamāyasto bhaimasenirabhāṣata
|
kimahaṃ kātaro drauṇe pṛthagjana ivāhave||96||

97yanmāṃ bhīṣayase vāgbhirasadetadvacastava
|
bhīmātkhalu samutpannaḥ kurūṇāṃ vipule kule||97||

98pāṇḍavānāmahaṃ putraḥ samareṣvanivartinām
|
rakṣasāmadhirājo'haṃ daśagrīvasamo bale||98||

99tiṣṭha tiṣṭha na me jīvandroṇaputra gamiṣyasi
|
yuddhaśraddhāmahaṃ te'dya vineṣyāmi raṇājire||99||

100ityuktvā krodhatāmrākṣo rākṣasaḥ sumahābalaḥ
|
drauṇimabhyadravatkruddho gajendramiva kesarī||100||

101rathākṣamātrairiṣubhirabhyavarṣaddhaṭotkacaḥ
|
rathināmṛṣabhaṃ drauṇiṃ dhārābhiriva toyadaḥ||101||

102śaravṛśṭiṃ śarairdrauṇiraprāptāṃ tāṃ vyaśātayat
|
tatontarikṣe bāṇānāṃ saṃgrāmo'nya ivābhavat||102||

103athāstrasaṃmardakṛtairvisphuliṅgaistadā babhau
|
vibhāvarīmukhe vyoma khadyotairiva citritam||103||

104niśāmya nihatāṃ māyāṃ drauṇinā raṇamāninā
|
ghaṭotkacastato māyāṃ sasarjāntarhitaḥ punaḥ||104||

105so'bhavadgiriratyuccaḥ śikharaistarusaṅkaṭaiḥ
|
śūlaprāsāsimusalajalaprassravaṇo mahān||105||

106tamañjanagiriprakhyaṃ drauṇirdṛṣṭvā mahīdharam
|
prapatadbhiśca bahubhiḥ śastrasaṅghairna vivyathe||106||

107tato hasanniva drauṇirvajramastramudairayat
|
sa tenāstreṇa śailendraḥ kṣiptaḥ kṣipraṃ vyanaśyata||107||

108tataḥ sa toyado bhūtvā nīlaḥ sendrāyudho divi
|
aśmavṛṣṭibhiratyugro drauṇimācchādayadraṇe||108||

109atha sandhāya vāyavyamastramastravidāṃ varaḥ
|
vyadhamaddroṇatanayo nīlameghaṃ samutthitam||109||

110sa mārgaṇagaṇairdrauṇirdiśaḥ pracchādya sarvaśaḥ
|
śataṃ rathasahastrāṇāṃ jaghāna dvipadāṃ varaḥ||110||

111sa dṛṣṭvā punarāyāntaṃ rathenāyatakārmukam
|
ghaṭotkacamasaṃbhrāntaṃ rākṣasairbahubhirvṛtam||111||

112siṃhaśārdūlasadṛśairmattadvirada‌vikramaiḥ
|
gajasthaiśca rathasthaiśca vājipṛṣṭhagatairapi||112||

113vikṛtāsyaśirogrīvairhiḍimbānucaraiḥ saha
|
paulastyairyātudhānaiśca tāmasaiścendravikramaiḥ||113||

114nānāśastradharairvīrairnānākavacabhūṣaṇaiḥ
|
mahābalairbhīmaravaiḥ saṃraṃbhodvṛttalocanaiḥ||114||

115upasthitaistato yuddhe rākṣasairyuddhadurmadaiḥ
|
viṣaṇṇamabhisaṃprekṣya putraṃ te drauṇirabravīt||115||

116tiṣṭha duryodhanādya tvaṃ na kāryaḥ saṃbhramastvayā
|
sahaibhirbhātṛbhirvīraiḥ pārthivaiścendravikramaiḥ||116||

117nihaniṣyāmyamitrāṃste na tavāsti parājayaḥ
|
satyaṃ te pratijānāmi paryāśvāsaya vāhinīm||117||

118duryodhana uvāca|
na tvetada‌dbhutaṃ manye yatte mahadidaṃ manaḥ
|
asmāsu ca parā bhaktistava gautaminandana||118||

119sañjaya uvāca|
aśvatthāmānamuktvaivaṃ tataḥ saubalamabravīt
|
vṛtaṃ rathasahasreṇa hayānāṃ raṇaśobhinām||119||

120ṣaṣṭhyā rathasahastraiśca prayāhi tvaṃ dhanañjayam
|
karṇaśca vṛṣasenaśca kṛpo nīlastathaiva ca||120||

121udīcyāḥ kṛtavarmā ca purumitraḥ sutāpanaḥ
|
duḥśāsano nikuṃbhaśca kuṇḍabhedī parākramaḥ||121||

122purañjayo dṛḍharathaḥ patākī hemakampanaḥ
|
śalyāruṇīṃdrasenāśca sañjayo vijayo jayaḥ||122||

123kamalākṣaḥ parakrāthī jayavarmā sudarśanaḥ
|
ete tvāmanuyāsyanti pattīnāmayutāni ṣaṭ||123||

124jahi bhīmaṃ yamau cobhau dharmarājaṃ ca mātula
|
asurāniva devendro jayāśā me tvayi sthitā||124||

125dāritāndrauṇinā bāṇairbhṛśaṃ vikṣatavigrahān
|
jahi mātula kaunteyānasurāniva pāvakiḥ||125||

126evamukto yayau śīghraṃ putreṇa tava saubalaḥ
|
piprīṣuste sutān rājandidhakṣuścaiva pāṇḍavān||126||

127atha pravavṛte yuddhaṃ drauṇirākṣasayormṛdhe
|
vibhāvaryāṃ sutumulaṃ śakraprahlāda‌dayoriva||127||

128tato ghaṭotkaco bāṇairdaśabhirgautamīsutam
|
jaghānorasi saṃkruddho viṣāgnipratimairdṛḍhaiḥ||128||

129sa tairabhyāhato gāḍhaṃ śarairbhīmasuteritaiḥ
|
cacāla rathamadhyastho vātodbhūta iva drumaḥ||129||

130bhūyaścāñjalikenātha mārgaṇena mahāprabham
|
drauṇihastasthitaṃ cāpaṃ cicchedāśu ghaṭotkacaḥ||130||

131tato'nyaddrauṇirādāya dhanurbhārasahaṃ mahat
|
vavarṣa viśikhāṃstīkṣṇān vāridhārā ivāṃbudaḥ||131||

132tataḥ śāradvatīputraḥ preṣayāmāsa bhārata
|
suvarṇapuṃkhāñchatrughnān khacarān khacaraṃ prati||132||

133tadbāṇairarditaṃ yūthaṃ rakṣasāṃ pīnavakṣasām
|
siṃhairiva babhau mattaṃ gajānāmākulaṃ kulam||133||

134vidhamya rākṣasānbāṇaiḥ sāśvasūtarathadvipān
|
dadāha bhagavānvahnirbhūtānīva yugakṣaye||134||

135sa dagdhvā'kṣauhiṇīṃ bāṇairnairṛtīṃ ruruce nṛpa
|
pureva tripuraṃ dagdhvā divi devo maheśvaraḥ||135||

136yugānte sarvabhūtāni dagdhveva vasurulbaṇaḥ
|
rarāja jayatāṃ śreṣṭho droṇaputrastavāhitān||136||

137tato ghaṭotkacaḥ kruddho rakṣasāṃ bhīmakarmaṇām
|
drauṇiṃ hateti mahatīṃ codayāmāsa tāṃ camūm||137||

138ghaṭotkacasya tāmājñāṃ pratigṛhyātha rākṣasāḥ
|
daṃṣṭrojjvalā mahāvaktrā ghorarūpā bhayānakāḥ||138||

139vyāttānanā ghorajihvāḥ krodhatāmrekṣaṇā bhṛśam
|
siṃhanādena mahatā nādayanto vasundharām||139||

140hantumabhyadravandrauṇiṃ nānāpraharaṇāyudhāḥ
|
śaktīḥ śataghnīḥ paridhānaśanīḥ śūlapaṭṭiśān||140||

141khaḍgān gadābhindipālān musalāni paraśvadhān
|
prāsānasīṃstomarāṃśca kaṇapānkampanāñchitān||141||

142sthūlān bhuśuṇḍyaśmagadāsthūṇān kārṣṇāyasāṃstathā
|
mudgarāṃśca mahāghorān samare śatrudāraṇān||142||

143drauṇimūrdhanyasaṃtrastā rākṣasā bhīmavikramāḥ
|
cikṣipuḥ krodhatāmrākṣāḥ śataśotha sahasraśaḥ||143||

144tacchastravarṣaṃ sumahaddroṇaputrasya mūrdhani
|
patamānaṃ samīkṣyātha yodhāste vyathitā'bhavan||144||

145droṇaputrastu vikrāntastadvarṣaṃ ghoramucchritam
|
śarairvidhvaṃsayāmāsa vajrakalpaiḥ śilāśitaiḥ||145||

146tato'nyaurviśikhaistūrṇaṃ svarṇapuṃkhairmahāmanāḥ
|
nijaghne rākṣasāndrauṇirdivyāstrapratimantritaiḥ||146||

147tadbāṇairarditaṃ yūthaṃ rakṣasāṃ pīnavakṣasām
|
siṃhairiva babhau mattaṃ gajānāmākulaṃ kulam||147||

148te rākṣasāḥ susaṃkruddhā droṇaputreṇa tāḍitāḥ
|
kruddhāḥ sma prādravandrauṇiṃ jighāṃsanto mahābalāḥ||148||

149tatrādbhutamimaṃ drauṇirdarśayāmāsa vikramam
|
aśakyaṃ kartumanyena sarvabhūteṣu bhārata||149||

150yadeko rākṣasīṃ senāṃ kṣaṇāddrauṇirmahāstravit
|
dadāha jvalitairbāṇai rākṣasendrasya paśyataḥ||150||

151sa hatvā rākṣasānīkaṃ rarāja drauṇirāhave
|
yugānte sarvabhūtāni saṃvartaka ivānalaḥ||151||

152taṃ dahantamanīkāni śarairāśīviṣopamaiḥ
|
teṣu rājasahastreṣu pāṇḍaveyeṣu bhārata||152||

153nainaṃ nirīkṣituṃ kaścidaśaknoddrauṇimāhave
|
ṛte ghaṭotkacādvīrādrākṣasendrānmahābalāt||153||

154sa punarbharataśreṣṭha krodhādudbhrāntalocanaḥ
|
talaṃ talena saṃhatya saṃdaśya daśanacchadam||154||

155svaṃ sūtamabravītkruddho droṇaputrāya māṃ vaha
|
sa yayau ghorarūpeṇa supatākena bhāsvatā||155||

156dvairathaṃ droṇaputreṇa punarapyarisūdanaḥ
|
sa vinadya mahānādaṃ siṃhavadbhīmavikramaḥ||156||

157cikṣepāvidhya saṃgrāme droṇaputrāya rākṣasaḥ
|
aṣṭaghaṇṭāṃ mahāghorāmaśaniṃ devanirmitām||157||

158tāmavaplutya jagrāha drauṇirnyasya rathe dhanuḥ
|
cikṣepa caināṃ tasyaiva syandanātso'vapupluve||158||

159sāśvasūtadhvajaṃ yānaṃ bhasma kṛtvā mahāprabhā
|
viveśa vasudhāṃ bhittvā sā'śanirbhṛśadāruṇā||159||

160drauṇestatkarma dṛṣṭvā tu sarvabhūtānyapūjayan
|
yadavaplutya jagrāha ghorāṃ śaṅkaranirmitām||160||

161dhṛṣṭadyumnarathaṃ gatvā bhaimasenistato nṛpa
|
dhanurghoraṃ samādāya mahadindrāyudhopamam
|
mumoca niśitānbāṇānpunardrauṇermahorasi||161||

162dhṛṣṭadyumnastvasaṃbhrānto mumocāśīviṣopamān
|
suvarṇapuṃkhānviśikhāndroṇaputrasya vakṣasi||162||

163tato mumoca nārācāndrauṇistāṃśca sahasraśaḥ
|
tāvapyagniśikhaprakhyairjaghnatustasya mārgaṇān||163||

164atitīvraṃ mahadyuddhaṃ tayoḥ puruṣasiṃhayoḥ
|
yodhānāṃ prītijananaṃ drauṇeśca bharatarṣabha||164||

165tato rathasahastreṇa dviradānāṃ śataistribhiḥ
|
ṣaḍbhirvājisahastraiśca bhīmastaṃ deśamāgamat||165||

166tato bhīmātmajaṃ rakṣo dhṛṣṭadyumnaṃ ca sānugam
|
ayodhayata dharmātmā drauṇirakliṣṭavikramaḥ||166||

167tatrādbhutatamaṃ drauṇirdarśayāmāsa vikramam
|
aśakyaṃ kartumanyena sarvabhūteṣu bhārata||167||

168nimeṣāntaramātreṇa sāśvasūtarathadvipām
|
akṣauhiṇīṃ rākṣasānāṃ śitairbāṇairaśātayat||168||

169miṣato bhīmasenasya haiḍimbeḥ pārṣatasya ca
|
yamayordharmaputrasya vijayasyācyutasya ca||169||

170pragāḍhamañjogatibhirnārācairabhitāḍitāḥ
|
nipeturdviradā bhūmau saśṛṅgā iva parvatāḥ||170||

171nikṛttairhastihastaiśca vicaladbhiritastataḥ
|
rarāja vasudhā kīrṇā visarpadbhirivoragaiḥ||171||

172kṣiptaiḥ kāñcanadaṇḍaiśca nṛpacchatraiḥ kṣitirbabhau
|
dyaurivoditacandrārkā grahākīrṇā yugakṣaye||172||

173pravṛddhadhvajamaṇḍūkāṃ bherīvistīrṇakacchapām
|
chatrahaṃsāvalījuṣṭāṃ phenacāmaramālinīm||173||

174kaṅkagṛdhramahāgrāhāṃ naikāyudhajhaṣākulām
|
vistīrṇagajapāṣāṇāṃ hatāśvamakarākulām||174||

175rathakṣiptamahāvaprāṃ patākāruciradrumām
|
śaramīnāṃ mahāraudrāṃ prāsaśaktyṛṣṭiḍuṇḍubhām||175||

176majjāmāṃsamahāpaṅkāṃ kabandhāvarjitoḍupām
|
keśaśaivalakalmāṣāṃ bhīrūṇāṃ kaśmalāvahām||176||

177nāgendrahayayodhānāṃ śarīravyayasaṃbhavām
|
śoṇitaughamahāghorāṃ drauṇiḥ prāvartayannadīm||177||

178yodhārtaravanirghoṣāṃ kṣatajormisamākulām
|
śvāpadātimahāghorāṃ yamarāṣṭramahodadhim||178||

179nihatya rākṣasānbāṇairdrauṇirhaiḍimbimārdayat
|
punarapyatisaṃkruddhaḥ savṛkodarapārṣatān||179||

180sa nārācagaṇaiḥ pārthāndrauṇirviddhvā mahābalaḥ
|
jaghāna surathaṃ nāma drupadasya sutaṃ vibhuḥ||180||

181punaḥ śatruñjayaṃ nāma drupadasyātmajaṃ raṇe
|
balānīkaṃ jayānīkaṃ jayāśvaṃ cābhijaghnivān||181||

182śrutāhvayaṃ ca rājānaṃ drauṇirninye yamakṣayam
|
tribhiścānyaiḥ śaraistīkṣṇaiḥ supuṅkhairhemamālinam||182||

183jaghāna sa pṛṣadhraṃ ca candrasenaṃ ca māriṣa
|
kuntibhojasutāṃścāsau daśabhirdaśa jaghnivān||183||

184aśvatthāmā susaṃkruddhaḥ sandhāyogramajihmagam
|
mumocākarṇapūrṇena dhanuṣā śaramuttamam||184||

185yamadaṇḍopamaṃ ghoramuddiśyāśu ghaṭotkacam
|
sa bhittvā hṛdayaṃ tasya rākṣasasya mahāśaraḥ||185||

186viveśa vasudhāṃ śīghraṃ sapuṅkhaḥ pṛthivīpate
|
taṃ hataṃ patitaṃ jñātvā dhṛṣṭadyumno mahārathaḥ||186||

187drauṇeḥ sakāśādrājendra vyapaninye rathottamam
|
tataḥ parāṅmukhanṛpaṃ sainyaṃ yaudhiṣṭhiraṃ nṛpa||187||

188parājitya raṇe vīro droṇaputro nanāda ha
|
pūjitaḥ sarvabhūteṣu tava putraiśca bhārata||188||

189atha śaraśatabhinnakṛttadehairhatapatitaiḥ kṣaṇadācaraiḥ samantāt
|
nidhanamupagatairmahī kṛtā'bhūdgiriśikharairiva durgamā'tiraudrā||189||

190taṃ siddhagandharvapiśācasaṃghā nāgāḥ suparṇāḥ pitaro vayāṃsi
|
rakṣogaṇā bhūtagaṇāśca drauṇimapūjayannapsarasaḥ surāśca||190||
