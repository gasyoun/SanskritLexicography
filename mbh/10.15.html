Mahābhārata 10.15 (Sauptikaparvan)

1vaiśampāyana uvāca|
dṛṣṭvaiva naraśārdūla tāvagnisamatejasau
|
sañjahāra śaraṃ divyaṃ tvaramāṇo dhanañjayaḥ||1||

2uvāca bharataśreṣṭha tāvṛṣī prāñjalistadā
|
pramuktamastramastreṇa śāmyatāmiti vai mayā||2||

3saṃhṛte paramāstre'sminsarvānasmānaśeṣataḥ
|
pāpakarmā dhruvaṃ drauṇiḥ pradhakṣyatyastratejasā||3||

4yadatra hitamasmākaṃ lokānāṃ caiva sarvathā
|
bhavantau devasaṅkāśau tathā sammantumarhataḥ||4||

5ityuktvā sañjahārāstraṃ punareva dhanañjayaḥ
|
saṃhāro duṣkarastasya devairapi hi saṃyuge||5||

6visṛṣṭasya raṇe tasya paramāstrasya saṅgrahe
|
aśaktaḥ pāṇḍavādanyaḥ sākṣādapi śatakratuḥ||6||

7brahmatejodbhavaṃ taddhi visṛṣṭamakṛtātmanā
|
na śakyamāvartayituṃ brahmacārivratādṛte||7||

8acīrṇabrahmacaryo yaḥ sṛṣṭvā vartayate punaḥ
|
tadastraṃ sānubandhasya mūrdhānaṃ tasya kṛntati||8||

9brahmacārī vratī cāpi duravāpamavāpya tat
|
paramavyasanārto'pi nārjuno'straṃ vyamuñcata||9||

10satyavratadharaḥ śūro brahmacārī ca pāṇḍavaḥ
|
guruvartī ca tenāstraṃ sañjahārārjunaḥ punaḥ||10||

11drauṇirapyatha samprekṣya tāvṛṣī purataḥ sthitau
|
na śaśāka punarghoramastraṃ saṃhartumojasā||11||

12aśaktaḥ pratisaṃhāre paramāstrasya saṃyuge
|
drauṇirdīnamanā rājan dvaipāyanamabhāṣata||12||

13uttamavyasanārtena prāṇatrāṇamabhīpsunā
|
mayaitadastramutsṛṣṭaṃ bhīmasenabhayānmune||13||

14adharmaśca kṛto'nena dhārttarāṣṭraṃ jighāṃsatā
|
mithyācāreṇa bhagavan bhīmasenena saṃyuge||14||

15ataḥ sṛṣṭamidaṃ brahmanmayā'stramakṛtātmanā
|
tasya bhūyo'dya saṃhāraṃ kartuṃ nāhamihotsahe||15||

16visṛṣṭaṃ hi mayā divyametadastraṃ durāsadam
|
apāṇḍavāyeti mune vahnitejo'numantrya vai||16||

17tadidaṃ pāṇḍaveyānāmantakāyābhisaṃhitam
|
adya pāṇḍusutānsarvān jīvitādbhraṃśayiṣyati||17||

18kṛtaṃ pāpamidaṃ brahman roṣāviṣṭena cetasā
|
vadhamāśāsya pārthānāṃ mayā'straṃ sṛjatā raṇe||18||

19vyāsa uvāca|
astraṃ brahmaśirastāta vidvānpārtho dhanañjayaḥ
|
utsṛṣṭavānna roṣeṇa na nāśāya tavāhave||19||

20astramastreṇa tu raṇe tava saṃśamayiṣyatā
|
visṛṣṭamarjunenedaṃ punaśca pratisaṃhṛtam||20||

21brahmāstramapyavāpyaitadupadeśātpitustava
|
kṣatradharmānmahābāhurnākampata dhanañjayaḥ||21||

22evaṃ dhṛtimataḥ sādhoḥ sarvāstraviduṣaḥ sataḥ
|
sabhrātṛbandhoḥ kasmāttvaṃ vadhamasya cikīrṣasi||22||

23astraṃ brahmaśiro yatra paramāstreṇa vadhyate
|
samā dvādaśa parjanyastadrāṣṭraṃ nābhivarṣati||23||

24etadarthaṃ mahābāhuḥ śaktimānapi pāṇḍavaḥ
|
na vihanyāttadastraṃ tu prajāhitacikīrṣayā||24||

25pāṇḍavāstvaṃ ca rāṣṭraṃ ca sadā saṃrakṣyameva hi
|
tasmātsaṃhara divyaṃ tvamastrametanmahābhuja||25||

26aroṣastava caivāstu pārthāḥ santu nirāmayāḥ
|
na hyadharmeṇa rājarṣiḥ pāṇḍavo jetumicchati||26||

27maṇiṃ caiva prayacchādya yaste śirasi tiṣṭhati
|
etadādāya te prāṇānpratidāsyanti pāṇḍavāḥ||27||

28drauṇiruvāca|
pāṇḍavairyāni ratnāni yaccānyatkauravairdhanam
|
avāptamiha tebhyo'yaṃ maṇirmama viśiṣyate||28||

29yamābadhya bhayaṃ nāsti śastravyādhikṣudhāśrayam
|
devebhyo dānavebhyo vā nāgebhyo vā kathañcana||29||

30na ca rakṣogaṇabhayaṃ na taskarabhayaṃ tathā
|
evaṃvīryo maṇirayaṃ na me tyājyaḥ kathañcana||30||

31yattu me bhagavānāha tanme kāryamanantaram
|
ayaṃ maṇirayaṃ cāhamīṣikā tu patiṣyati||31||

32garbheṣu pāṇḍaveyānāmamoghaṃ caitaduttamam
|
na ca śakto'smi bhagavansaṃhartuṃ punarudyatam||32||

33etadastramataścaiva garbheṣu visṛjāmyaham
|
na ca vākyaṃ bhagavato na kariṣye mahāmune||33||

34vyāsa uvāca|
evaṃ kuru na cānyā tu buddhiḥ kāryātvayā'nagha
|
garbheṣu pāṇḍaveyānāṃ visṛjyaitadupārama||34||

35vaiśampāyana uvāca|
tataḥ paramamastraṃ tu drauṇirudyatamāhave
|
dvaipāyanavacaḥ śrutvā garbheṣu pramumoca ha||35||
