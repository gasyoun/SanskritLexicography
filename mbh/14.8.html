Mahābhārata 14.8 (Āśvamedhikaparvan)

1saṃvarta uvāca|
girerhimavataḥ pṛṣṭhe muñjavān nāma parvataḥ
|
tapyate yatra bhagavāṃstapo nityamumāpatiḥ||1||

2vanaspatīnāṃ mūleṣu śṛṅgeṣu viṣameṣu ca
|
guhāsu śailarājasya yathākāmaṃ yathāsukham||2||

3umāsahāyo bhagavān yatra nityaṃ maheśvaraḥ
|
āste śūlī mahātejā nānābhūtagaṇāvṛtaḥ||3||

4tatra rudrāśca sādhyāśca viśve'tha vasavastathā
|
yamaśca varuṇaścaiva kuberaśca sahānugaḥ||4||

5bhūtāni ca piśācāśca nāsatyāvapi cāśvinau
|
gandharvāpsarasaścaiva yakṣā devarṣayastathā||5||

6ādityā marutaścaiva yātudhānāśca sarvaśaḥ
|
upāsante mahātmānaṃ bahurūpamumāpatim||6||

7ramate bhagavāṃstatra kuberānucaraiḥ saha
|
vikṛtairvikṛtākāraiḥ krīḍadbhiḥ pṛthivīpate||7||

8śriyā jvalan dṛśyate vai bālādityasamadyutiḥ
|
na rūpaṃ śakyate tasya saṃsthānaṃ vā kadācana||8||

9nirdeṣṭuṃ prāṇibhiḥ kaiścit prākṛtairmāṃsalocanaiḥ
|
noṣṇaṃ na śiśiraṃ tatra na vāyurna ca bhāskaraḥ||9||

10na jarā kṣutpipāse vā na mṛtyurna bhayaṃ nṛpa
|
tasya śailasya pārśveṣu sarveṣu jayatāṃ vara||10||

11dhātavo jātarūpasya raśmayaḥ savituryathā
|
rakṣyante te kuberasya sahāyairudyatāyudhaiḥ||11||

12cikīrṣadbhiḥ priyaṃ rājan kuberasya mahātmanaḥ
|
tasmai bhagavate kṛtvā namaḥ śarvāya vedhase||12||

13rudrāya śitikaṇṭhāya puruṣāya suvarcase
|
kapardine karālāya haryakṣṇe varadāya ca||13||

14tryakṣṇe pūṣṇo dantabhide vāmanāya śivāya ca
|
yāmyāyāvyaktarūpāya savṛtte śaṅkarāya ca||14||

15kṣemyāya harikeśāya sthāṇave puruṣāya ca
|
harinetrāya muṇḍāya kruddhāyottaraṇāya ca||15||

16bhāskarāya sutīrthāya devadevāya raṃhase
|
uṣṇīṣiṇe suvaktrāya sahasrākṣāya mīḍhuṣe||16||

17giriśāya praśāntāya yataye cīravāsase
|
bilvadaṇḍāya siddhāya sarvadaṇḍadharāya ca||17||

18mṛgavyādhāya mahate dhanvine'tha bhavāya ca
|
varāya somavaktrāya siddhamantrāya cakṣuṣe||18||

19hiraṇyabāhave rājannugrāya pataye diśām
|
lelihānāya goṣṭhāya siddhamantrāya vṛṣṇaye||19||

20paśūnāṃ pataye caiva bhūtānāṃ pataye namaḥ
|
vṛṣāya mātṛbhaktāya senānye madhyamāya ca||20||

21suvahastāya pataye dhanvine bhārgavāya ca
|
ajāya kṛṣṇanetrāya virūpākṣāya caiva ha||21||

22tīkṣṇadaṃṣṭrāya tīkṣṇāya vaiśvānaramukhāya ca
|
mahādyutaye'naṅgāya sarvāya pataye viśām||22||

23vilohitāya dīptāya dīptākṣāya mahaujase
|
vasuretaḥ suvapuṣe pṛthave kṛttivāsase||23||

24kapālamāline caiva suvarṇamukuṭāya ca
|
mahādevāya kṛṣṇāya tryambakāyānaghāya ca||24||

25krodhanāyānṛśaṃsāya mṛdave bāhuśāline
|
daṇḍine taptapase tathaivākrūrakarmaṇe||25||

26sahasraśirase caiva sahasracaraṇāya ca
|
namaḥ svadhāsvarūpāya bahurūpāya daṃṣṭriṇe||26||

27pinākinaṃ mahādevaṃ mahāyoginamavyayam
|
triśūlahastaṃ varadaṃ tryambakaṃ bhuvaneśvaram||27||

28tripuraghnaṃ trinayanaṃ trilokeśaṃ mahaujasam
|
prabhavaṃ sarvabhūtānāṃ dhāraṇaṃ dharaṇīdharam||28||

29īśānaṃ śaṅkaraṃ sarve śivaṃ viśveśvaraṃ bhavam
|
umāpatiṃ paśupatiṃ viśvarūpaṃ maheśvaram||29||

30virūpākṣaṃ daśabhujaṃ divyagovṛṣabhadhvajam
|
ugraṃ sthāṇuṃ śivaṃ raudraṃ śarvaṃ gaurīśamīśvaram||30||

31śitikaṇṭhamajaṃ śukraṃ pṛthu pṛthuharaṃ varam
|
viśvarūpaṃ virūpākṣaṃ bahurūpamumāpatim||31||

32praṇamya śirasā devamanaṅgāṅgaharaṃ haram
|
śaraṇyaṃ śaraṇaṃ yāhi mahādevaṃ caturmukham||32||

33evaṃ kṛtvā namastasmai mahādevāya raṃhase
|
mahātmane kṣitipate tatsuvarṇamavāpsyasi||33||

34suvarṇamāhariṣyantastatra gacchantu te narāḥ
|
ityuktaḥ sa vacastena cakre kārandhamātmajaḥ||34||

35tato'timānuṣaṃ sarvaṃ cakre yajñasya saṃvidhim
|
sauvarṇāni ca bhāṇḍāni sañcakrustatra śilpinaḥ||35||

36bṛhaspatistu tāṃ śrutvā maruttasya mahīpateḥ
|
samṛddhipatidevebhya: santāpamakarod bhṛśam||36||

37sa tapyamāno vaivarṇyaṃ kṛśatvaṃ cāgamatparam
|
bhaviṣyati hi me śatruḥ saṃvarto vasumāniti||37||

38taṃ śrutvā bhṛśasantaptaṃ devarājo bṛhaspatim
|
adhigamyāmaravṛtaḥ provācedaṃ vacastadā||38||
