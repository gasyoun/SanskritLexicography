Mahābhārata 6.117 (Bhīṣmaparvan)

1sañjaya uvāca|
śikhaṇḍī tu raṇe bhīṣmamāsādya puruṣarṣabham
|
daśabhirniśitairbhallairājaghāna stanāntare ||1||

2śikhaṇḍinaṃ tu gāṅgeyaḥ krodhadīptena cakṣuṣā
|
saṃpraikṣata kaṭākṣeṇa nirdahanniva bhārata ||2||

3strītvaṃ tasya smaran rājansarvalokasya paśyataḥ
|
nājaghāna raṇe bhīṣmaḥ sa ca tannāvabuddhavān ||3||

4arjunastu mahārāja śikhaṇḍinamabhāṣata
|
abhidravasva tvaritaṃ jahi cainaṃ pitāmaham ||4||

5kiṃ te vivakṣayā vīra jahi bhīṣmaṃ mahāratham
|
na hyanyamanupaśyāmi kañcidyaudhiṣṭhire bale ||5||

6yaḥ śaktaḥ samare bhīṣmaṃ pratiyoddhumihāhave
|
ṛte tvāṃ puruṣavyāghra satyametadbravīmi te ||6||

7evamuktastu pārthena śikhaṇḍī bharatarṣabha
|
śarairnānāvidhaistūrṇaṃ pitāmahamavākirat ||7||

8acintayitvā tān bāṇān pitā devavratastava
|
arjunaṃ samare kruddhaṃ vārayāmāsa sāyakaiḥ ||8||

9tathaiva ca camūṃ sarvāṃ pāṇḍavānāṃ mahārathaḥ
|
apraiṣīt sa śaraistīkṣṇaiḥ paralokāya māriṣa ||9||

10tathaiva pāṇḍavā rājan sainyena mahatā vṛtāḥ
|
bhīṣmaṃ saṃchādayāmāsurmeghā iva divākaram ||10||

11sa samantāt parivṛto bhārato bharatarṣabha
|
nirdadāha raṇe śūrān vane vahniriva jvalan ||11||

12tatrādbhutamapaśyāma tava putrasya pauruṣam
|
ayodhayacca yat pārthaṃ jugopa ca pitāmaham ||12||

13karmaṇā tena samare tava putrasya dhanvinaḥ
|
duḥśāsanasya tutuṣuḥ sarve lokā mahātmanaḥ ||13||

14yadekaḥ samare pārthān sārjunān samayodhayat
|
na cainaṃ pāṇḍavā yuddhe vārayāmāsurulbaṇam ||14||

15duḥśāsanena samare rathino virathīkṛtāḥ
|
sādinaśca maheṣvāsā hastinaśca mahābalāḥ ||15||

16vinirbhinnāḥ śaraistīkṣṇairnipeturvasudhātale
|
śarāturāstathaivānye dantino vidrutā diśaḥ ||16||

17yathāgnirindhanaṃ prāpya jvaleddīptārcirulbaṇam
|
tathā jajvāla putraste pāṇḍusenāṃ vinirdahan||17||

18taṃ bhāratamahāmātraṃ pāṇḍavānāṃ mahārathaḥ
|
jetuṃ notsahate kaścinnābhyudyātuṃ kathañcana ||18||

19ṛte mahendratanayācchvetāśvāt kṛṣṇasāratheḥ
|
sa hi taṃ samare rājannirjitya vijayo'rjunaḥ ||19||

20bhīṣmamevābhidudrāva sarvasainyasya paśyataḥ
|
vijitastava putro'pi bhīṣmabāhuvyapāśrayaḥ ||20||

21punaḥ punaḥ samāśvasya prāyudhyata madotkaṭaḥ
|
arjunastu raṇe rājan yodhayan saṃvyarājata ||21||

22śikhaṇḍī tu raṇe rājan vivyādhaiva pitāmaham
|
śarairaśanisaṃsparśaistathā sarpaviṣopamaiḥ ||22||

23na ca sma te rujaṃ cakruḥ pitustava janeśvara
|
smayamānastu gāṅgeyastān bāṇān jagṛhe tadā ||23||

24uṣṇārto hi naro yadvajjaladhārāḥ pratīcchati
|
tathā jagrāha gāṅgeyaḥ śaradhārāḥ śikhaṇḍinaḥ ||24||

25taṃ kṣatriyā mahārāja dadṛśurghoramāhave
|
bhīṣmaṃ dahantaṃ sainyāni pāṇḍavānāṃ mahātmanām ||25||

26tato'bravīttava sutaḥ sarvasainyāni māriṣa
|
abhidravata saṃgrāme phālgunaṃ sarvato raṇe ||26||

27bhīṣmo vaḥ samare sarvān pālayiṣyati dharmavit
|
te bhayaṃ sumahattyaktvā pāṇḍavān prati yudhyata ||27||

28hematālena mahatā bhīṣmastiṣṭhati pālayan
|
sarveṣāṃ dhārtarāṣṭrāṇāṃ samare śarma varma ca ||28||

29tridaśā'pi samudyuktā nālaṃ bhīṣmaṃ samāsitum
|
kimu pārthā mahātmānaṃ martyabhūtā mahābalāḥ ||29||

30tasmāddravata mā yodhāḥ phālgunaṃ prāpya saṃyuge
|
ahamadya raṇe yatto yodhayiṣyāmi pāṇḍavam ||30||

31sahitaḥ sarvato yattairbhavadbhirvasudhādhipaiḥ
|
tacchrutvā tu vaco rājaṃstava putrasya dhanvinaḥ||31||

32sarve yodhāḥ susaṃrabdhā balavanto mahābalāḥ
|
te videhāḥ kaliṅgāśca dāserakagaṇāśca ha ||32||

33abhipeturniṣādāśca sauvīrāśca mahāraṇe
|
bāhlīkā daradāścaiva pratīcyodīcyamālavāḥ ||33||

34abhīṣāhāḥ śūrasenāḥ śivayo'tha vasātayaḥ
|
śālvāḥ śakāstrigartāśca ambaṣṭhāḥ kekayaiḥ saha ||34||

35abhipetū raṇe pārthaṃ pataṅgā iva pāvakam
|
śalabhā iva rājendra pārthamapratimaṃ raṇe
|
etān sarvān sahānīkān mahārāja mahārathān ||35||

36divyānyastrāṇi sañcintya prasaṃdhāya dhanañjayaḥ
|
sa tairastrairmahāvegairdadāha sumahābalaḥ ||36||

37śarapratāpairbībhatsuḥ pataṅgāniva pāvakaḥ
|
tasya bāṇasahasrāṇi sṛjato dṛḍhadhanvinaḥ ||37||

38dīpyamānamivākāśe gāṇḍīvaṃ samadṛśyata
|
te śarārtā mahārāja viprakīrṇamahādhvajāḥ ||38||

39nābhyavartanta rājānaḥ sahitā vānaradhvajam
|
sadhvajā rathinaḥ peturhayārohā hayaiḥ saha ||39||

40sagajāśca gajārohāḥ kirīṭiśaratāḍitāḥ
|
tato'rjunabhujotsṛṣṭairāvṛtā''sīdvasundharā ||40||

41vidravadbhiśca bahudhā balai rājñāṃ samantataḥ
|
atha pārtho mahārāja drāvayitvā varūthinīm ||41||

42duḥśāsanāya subahūn preṣayāmāsa sāyakān
|
te tu bhittvā tava sutaṃ duḥśāsanamayomukhāḥ ||42||

43dharaṇīṃ viviśuḥ sarve valmīkamiva pannagāḥ
|
hayāṃścāsya tato jaghne sārathiṃ ca nyapātayat ||43||

44viviṃśatiṃ ca viṃśatyā virathaṃ kṛtavān prabhuḥ
|
ājaghāna bhṛśaṃ caiva pañcabhirnataparvabhiḥ ||44||

45kṛpaṃ vikarṇaṃ śalyaṃ ca viddhvā bahubhirāyasaiḥ
|
cakāra virathāṃścaiva kaunteyaḥ śvetavāhanaḥ ||45||

46evaṃ te virathāḥ sarve kṛpaḥ śalyaśca māriṣa
|
duḥśāsano vikarṇaśca tathaiva ca viviṃśatiḥ ||46||

47saṃprādravanta samare nirjitāḥ savyasācinā
|
pūrvāhṇe bharataśreṣṭha parājitya mahārathān ||47||

48prajajvāla raṇe pārtho vidhūma iva pāvakaḥ
|
tathaiva śaravarṣeṇa bhāskaro raśmivāniva ||48||

49anyānapi mahārāja tāpayāmāsa pārthivān
|
parāṅmukhīkṛtya tathā śaravarṣairmahārathān ||49||

50prāvartayata saṃgrāme śoṇitodāṃ mahānadīm
|
madhyena kurusainyānāṃ pāṇḍavānāṃ ca bhārata ||50||

51gajāśca rathasaṅghāśca bahudhā rathibhirhatāḥ
|
rathāśca nihatā nāgairhayāścaiva padātibhiḥ ||51||

52aṃtarācchidyamānāni śarīrāṇi śirāṃsi ca
|
nipeturdikṣu sarvāsu gajāśvarathayodhinām ||52||

53channamāyodhanaṃ rājan kuṇḍalāṅgadadhāribhiḥ
|
patitaiḥ pātyamānaiśca rājaputrairmahārathaiḥ ||53||

54rathaneminikṛttaiśca gajaiścaivāvapothitaiḥ
|
pādātāścāpyadhāvanta sāśvāśca hayayodhinaḥ ||54||

55gajāśca rathayodhāśca paripetuḥ samantataḥ
|
vikīrṇāśca rathā bhūmau bhagnacakrayugadhvajāḥ ||55||

56tadgajāśvarathaughānāṃ rudhireṇa samukṣitam
|
channamāyodhanaṃ reje raktābhramiva śāradam ||56||

57śvānaḥ kākāśca gṛdhrāśca vṛkā gomāyubhiḥ saha
|
praṇedurbhakṣyamāsādya vikṛtāśca mṛgadvijāḥ ||57||

58vavurbahuvidhāścaiva dikṣu sarvāsu mārutāḥ
|
dṛśyamāneṣu rakṣaḥsu bhūteṣu ca nadatsu ca ||58||

59kāñcanāni ca dāmāni patākāśca mahādhanāḥ
|
dhūyamānā vyadṛśyanta sahasā māruteritāḥ ||59||

60śvetacchatrasahasrāṇi sadhvajāśca mahārathāḥ
|
vikīrṇāḥ samadṛśyanta śataśo'tha sahasraśaḥ ||60||

61sapatākāśca mātaṅgā diśo jagmuḥ śarāturāḥ
|
kṣatriyāśca manuṣyendra gadāśaktidhanurdharāḥ ||61||

62samantataśca dṛśyante patitā dharaṇītale
|
tato bhīṣmo mahārāja divyamastramudīrayan ||62||

63abhyadhāvata kaunteyaṃ miṣatāṃ sarvadhanvinām
|
taṃ śikhaṇḍī raṇe yāntamabhyadravata daṃśitaḥ ||63||

64tataḥ samāharadbhīṣmastadastraṃ pāvakopamam
|
tvaritaḥ pāṇḍavo rājan madhyamaḥ śvetavāhanaḥ
|
nijaghne tāvakaṃ sainyaṃ mohayitvā pitāmaham ||64||
