Mahābhārata 3.31 (Āraṇyakaparvan)

1yudhiṣṭhira uvāca|
valgu citrapadaṃ ślakṣṇaṃ yājñaseni tvayā vacaḥ
|
uktaṃ tacchrutamasmābhinīstekyaṃ tu prabhāṣase||1||

2nāhaṃ karmaphalānveṣī rājaputri carāmyuta
|
dadāmi deyamityeva yaje yaṣṭavyamityuta||2||

3astu vātra phalaṃ mā vā kartavyaṃ puruṣeṇa yat
|
gṛhe vā vasatā kṛṣṇe yathāśakti karomi tat||3||

4dharmaṃ carāmi suśroṇi na dharmaphalakāraṇāt
|
āgamānanatikramya satāṃ vṛttamavekṣya ca||4||

5dharma eva manaḥ kṛṣṇe svabhāvāccaiva me dhṛtam
|
gharmavāṇijyako hīno jaghanyo dharmavādinām||5||

6na dharmaphalamāpnoti yo dharmaṃ dogdhumicchati
|
yaścainaṃ śaṅkate kṛtvā nāstikyātpāpacetanaḥ||6||

7ativādādvadāmyeṣa mā dharmamabhiśaṅkithāḥ
|
dharmābhiśaṅkī puruṣastiryaggatiparāyaṇaḥ||7||

8dharmoṃ yasyābhiśaṅkyaḥ syādārṣaṃ vā durbalātmanaḥ | vedācchūdra ivāpeyātsa lokādajarāmarāt||8||

9vedādhyāyī dharmaparaḥ kule jāto manasvini
|
sthavireṣu sa yoktavyo rājarṣirdharmacāribhiḥ||9||

10pāpīyānsa hi śūdrebhyastaskarebhyo viśiṣyate
|
śāstrātigo mandabuddhiryo dharmamabhiśaṅkate||10||

11pratyakṣaṃ hi tvayā dṛṣṭa ṛṣirgacchanmahātapāḥ
|
mārkaṇḍeyo'prameyātmā dharmeṇa cirajīvitā||11||

12vyāso vasiṣṭho maitreyo nārado lomaśaḥ śukaḥ
|
anye ca ṛṣayaḥ sarve dharmeṇaiva sucetasaḥ||12||

13pratyakṣaṃ paśyasi hyetāndivyayogasamanvitān
|
śāpānugrahaṇe śaktāndevebhyo'pi garīyasaḥ||13||

14ete hi dharmamevādau varṇayanti sadānaghe
|
kartavyamamaraprakhyāḥ pratyakṣāgamabuddhayaḥ||14||

15ato nārhasi kalyāṇi dhātāraṃ dharmameva ca
|
rājñī mūḍhena manasā kṣeptuṃ śaṅkitumeva ca||15||

16unmattānmanyate bālaḥ sarvānāgataniścayān
|
dharmābhiśaṅkī nānyasmātpramāṇamadhigacchati||16||

17ātmapramāṇa unnaddhaḥ śreyaso hyavamanyakaḥ
|
indriyaprītisambaddhaṃ yadidaṃ lokasākṣikam
|
etāvanmanyate bālo mohamanyatra gacchati||17||

18prāyaścittaṃ na tasyāsti yo dharmamabhiśaṅkate
|
dhyāyansa kṛpaṇaḥ pāpo na lokānpratipadyate||18||

19pramāṇāddhi nivṛtto hi vedaśāstrārthanindakaḥ
|
kāmalobhātigo mūḍho narakaṃ pratipadyate||19||

20yastu nityaṃ kṛtamatirdharmamevābhipadyate
|
aśaṅkamānaḥ kalyāṇi so'mutrānantyamaśnute||20||

21ārṣaṃ pramāṇamutkramya dharmaṃ na pratipālayan
|
sarvaśāstrātigo mūḍhaḥ śaṃ janmasu na vindati||21||

22yasya nārṣaṃ pramāṇaṃ syācchiṣṭācāraśca bhāvini
|
na vai taraya paro loko nāyamastīti niścayaḥ||22||

23śiṣṭairācaritaṃ dharmaṃ kṛṣṇe māsmābhiśaṅkithāḥ
|
purāṇamṛṣibhiḥ proktaṃ sarvajñaiḥ sarvadarśibhiḥ||23||

24dharma eva plavo nānyaḥ khargaṃ draupadi gacchatām
|
saiva nauḥ sāgarasyeva vaṇijaḥ pāramicchataḥ||24||

25aphalo yadi dharmaḥ syāccarito dharmacāribhiḥ
|
apratiṣṭhe tamasyetajjaganmajjedanindite||25||

26nirvāṇaṃ nādhigaccheyurjīveyuḥ paśujīvikām
|
vidyāṃ te naiva yujyeyurna cārthaṃ kecidāpnuyuḥ||26||

27tapaśca brahmacaryaṃ ca yajñaḥ svādhyāya eva ca
|
dānamārjavametāni yadi syuraphalāni vai||27||

28nācariṣyanpare dharmaṃ pare paratare ca ye
|
vipralambho'yamatyantaṃ yadi syuraphalāḥ kriyāḥ||28||

29ṛṣayaścaiva devāśca gandharvāsurarākṣasāḥ
|
īśvarāḥ kasya hetoste careyurdharmamādṛtāḥ||29||

30phaladaṃ tviha vijñāya dhātāraṃ śreyasi dhruvam
|
dharmaṃ te vyacarankṛṣṇe taddhi śreyaḥ sanātanam||30||

31sa nāyamaphalo dharmoṃ nādharmo'phalavānapi
|
dṛśyante'pi hi vidyānāṃ phalāni tapasāṃ tathā||31||

32tvamātmano vijānīhi janma kṛṣṇe yathā śrutam
|
vettha cāpi yathā jāto dhṛṣṭadyumnaḥ pratāpavān||32||

33etāvadeva paryāptamupamānaṃ śucismite
|
karmaṇāṃ phalamāpnoti dhīro'lpenāpi tuṣyati||33||

34bahunā hyapi vidvāṃso naiva tuṣyantyabuddhayaḥ
|
teṣāṃ na dharmajaṃ kiñcitpretya śarmāsti vā punaḥ||34||

35karmaṇāṃ śrutapuṇyānāṃ pāpānāṃ ca phalodayaḥ
|
prabhavaścātyayaścaiva deva guhyāni bhāvini||35||

36etāni veda yaḥ kaścinmuhyante'tra prajā imāḥ
|
api kalpasahasreṇa na sa śreyo'dhigacchati||36||

37rakṣyāṇyetāni devānāṃ gūḍhamāyā hi devatāḥ
|
kṛtāśāśca vratāśāśca tapasā dagdhakilbiṣāḥ
|
prasādairmānasairyuktāḥ paśyantyetāni vai dvijāḥ||37||

38na phalādarśanāddharmaḥ śaṅkitavyo na devatāḥ
|
yaṣṭavyaṃ ca prayatnena dātavyaṃ cānasūyatā||38||

39karmaṇāṃ phalamastīha tathaitaddharmaśāśvatam
|
brahmā provāca putrāṇāṃ yadṛṣirveda kaśyapaḥ||39||

40tasmātte saṃśayaḥ kṛṣṇe nīhāra iva naśyatu
|
vyavasya sarvamastīti nāstikyaṃ bhāvamutsṛja||40||

41īśvaraṃ cāpi bhūtānāṃ dhātāraṃ mā ca vai kṣipa
|
śikṣasvainaṃ namasvainaṃ mā te bhūdbuddhirīdṛśī||41||

42yasya prasādāttadbhakto martyo gacchatyamartyatām
|
uttamāṃ devatāṃ kṛṣṇe māvamaṃsthāḥ kathañcana||42||
