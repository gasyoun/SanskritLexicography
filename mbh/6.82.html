Mahābhārata 6.82 (Bhīṣmaparvan)

1sañjaya uvāca|
tathā pravṛtte saṃgrāme nivṛtte ca suśarmaṇi
|
bhagneṣu cāpi vīreṣu pāṇḍavena mahātmanā ||1||

2kṣubhyamāṇe bale tūrṇaṃ sāgarapratime tava
|
pratyudyāte ca gāṅgeye tvaritaṃ vijayaṃ prati ||2||

3dṛṣṭvā duryodhano rājā raṇe pārthasya vikramam
|
tvaramāṇaḥ samabhyetya sarvāṃstānabravīnnṛpān ||3||

4teṣāṃ tu pramukhe śūraṃ suśarmāṇaṃ mahābalam
|
madhye sarvasya sainyasya bhṛśaṃ saṃharṣayanniva ||4||

5eṣa bhīṣmaḥ śāntanavo yoddhukāmo dhanañjayam
|
sarvātmanā kuruśreṣṭhastyaktvā jīvitamātmanaḥ ||5||

6taṃ prayāntaṃ raṇe vīraṃ sarvasainyena bhāratam
|
saṃyattāḥ samare sarve pālayadhvaṃ pitāmaham ||6||

7bāḍhamityevamuktvā tu tānyanīkāni sarvaśaḥ
|
narendrāṇāṃ mahārāja samājagmuḥ pitāmaham ||7||

8tataḥ prayātaḥ sahasā bhīṣmaḥ śāntanavo'rjunam
|
raṇe bhāratamāyāntamāsasāda mahābalaḥ ||8||

9mahāśvetāśvayuktena bhīmavānaraketunā
|
mahatā meghanādena rathenātivirājatā ||9||

10samare sarvasainyānāmupayāntaṃ dhanañjayam
|
abhavattumulo nādo bhayāddṛṣṭvā kirīṭinam ||10||

11abhīṣuhastaṃ kṛṣṇaṃ ca dṛṣṭvā''dityamivāparam
|
madhyaṃdinagataṃ saṃkhye na śekuḥ prativīkṣitum ||11||

12tathā śāntanavaṃ bhīṣmaṃ śvetāśvaṃ śvetakārmukam
|
na śekuḥ pāṇḍavā draṣṭuṃ śvetaṃ grahamivoditam ||12||

13sa sarvataḥ parivṛtastrigartaiḥ sumahātmabhiḥ
|
bhrātṛbhiḥ saha putraiśca tathānyaiśca mahārathaiḥ ||13||

14bhāradvājastu samare matsyaṃ vivyādha patriṇā
|
dhvajaṃ cāsya śareṇājau dhanuścaikena cicchide ||14||

15tadapāsya dhanuśchinnaṃ virāṭo vāhinīpatiḥ
|
anyadādatta vegena dhanurbhārasahaṃ dṛḍham ||15||

16śarāṃścāśīviṣākārān jvalitān pannagāniva
|
droṇaṃ tribhiśca vivyādha caturbhiścāsya vājinaḥ ||16||

17dhvajamekena vivyādha sārathiṃ cāsya pañcabhiḥ
|
dhanurekeṣuṇā'vidhyattatrākrudhyaddvijarṣabhaḥ ||17||

18tasya droṇo'vadhīdaśvān śaraiḥ sannataparvabhiḥ
|
aṣṭābhirbharataśreṣṭha sūtamekena patriṇā ||18||

19sa hatāśvāda‌vaplutya syandanāddhatasārathiḥ
|
āruroha rathaṃ tūrṇaṃ putrasya rathināṃ varaḥ ||19||

20tatastu tau pitāputrau bhāradvājaṃ rathe sthitau
|
mahatā śaravarṣeṇa vārayāmāsaturbalāt ||20||

21bhāradvājastataḥ kuddhaḥ śaramāśīviṣopamam
|
cikṣepa samare tūrṇaṃ śaṅkhaṃ prati janeśvaraḥ ||21||

22sa tasya hṛdayaṃ bhittvā pītvā śoṇitamāhave
|
jagāma dharaṇīṃ bāṇo lohitārdravaracchadaḥ ||22||

23sa papāta raṇe tūrṇaṃ bhāradvājaśarāhataḥ
|
dhanustyaktvā śarāṃścaiva pitureva samīpataḥ ||23||

24hataṃ tamātmajaṃ dṛṣṭvā virāṭaḥ prādravadbhayāt
|
utsṛjya samare droṇaṃ vyāttānanamivāntakam ||24||

25bhāradvājastatastūrṇaṃ pāṇḍavānāṃ mahācamūm
|
dārayāmāsa samare śataśo'tha sahasraśaḥ ||25||

26śikhaṇḍī tu mahārāja drauṇimāsādya saṃyuge
|
ājaghāna bhravormadhye nārācaistribhirāśugaiḥ ||26||

27sa babhau rathaśārdūlo lalāṭe saṃsthitaistribhiḥ
|
śikharaiḥ kāñcanamayairmerustribhirivocchritaiḥ ||27||

28aśvatthāmā tataḥ kruddho nimeṣārdhācchitkhaṇḍinaḥ
|
dhvajaṃ sūtamatho rājaṃsturagānāyudhāni ca ||28||

29śarairbahubhirācchidya pātayāmāsa saṃyuge
|
sa hatāśvādavaplutya rathādvai rathināṃ varaḥ ||29||

30khaḍgamādāya suśitaṃ vimalaṃ ca śarāvaram
|
śyenavadvyacaratkruddhaḥ śikhaṇḍī śatrutāpanaḥ ||30||

31sakhaḍgasya mahārāja caratastasya saṃyuge
|
nāntaraṃ dadṛśe drauṇistada‌dbhutamivābhavat ||31||

32tataḥ śarasahastrāṇi bahūni bharatarṣabha
|
preṣayāmāsa samare drauṇiḥ paramakopanaḥ ||32||

33tāmāpatantīṃ samare śaravṛṣṭiṃ sudāruṇām
|
asinā tīkṣṇadhāreṇa ciccheda balināṃ varaḥ||33||

34tato'sya vimalaṃ drauṇiḥ śatacandraṃ manoramam
|
carmācchinada‌siṃ cāsya khaṇḍayāmāsa saṃyuge ||34||

35śitaistu bahuśo rājan taṃ ca vivyādha pattribhiḥ
|
śikhaṇḍī tu tataḥ khaḍgaṃ khaṇḍitaṃ tena sāyakaiḥ ||35||

36āvidhya vyasṛjattūrṇaṃ jvalantamiva pannagam
|
tamāpatantaṃ sahasā kālānalasamaprabham ||36||

37ciccheda samare drauṇirdarśayan pāṇilāghavam
|
śikhaṇḍinaṃ ca vivyādhaḥ śaraibahubhirāyasaiḥ ||37||

38śikhaṇḍī tu bhṛśaṃ rājastāḍyamānaḥ śitaiḥ śaraiḥ
|
āruroha rathaṃ tūrṇaṃ mādhavasya mahātmanaḥ ||38||

39sātyakiścāpi saṃkruddho rākṣasaṃ krūramāhave
|
alaṃbuṣaṃ śaraistīkṣṇairvivyādha balināṃ varaḥ ||39||

40rākṣasendrastatastasya dhanuściccheda bhārata
|
ardhacandreṇa samare taṃ ca vivyādha sāyakaiḥ ||40||

41māyāṃ ca rākṣasīṃ kṛtvā śaravarṣairavākirat
|
tatrādbhutamapaśyāma śaineyasya parākramam ||41||

42asaṃbhramastu samare vadhyamānaḥ śitaiḥ śaraiḥ
|
aindramastraṃ ca vārṣṇeyo yojayāmāsa bhārata ||42||

43vijayādyadanuprāptaṃ mādhavena yaśasvinā
|
tadastraṃ bhasmasāt kṛttvā māyāṃ tāṃ rākṣasīṃ tadā ||43||

44alaṃbuṣaṃ śarairanyairabhyākirata sarvataḥ
|
parvataṃ vāridhārābhiḥ prāvṛṣīva balāhakaḥ ||44||

45tat tathā pīḍitaṃ tena mādhavena yaśasvinā
|
pradudrāva bhayādrakṣastyaktvā sātyakimāhave ||45||

46tamajeyaṃ rākṣasendraṃ saṃkhye maghavatā api
|
śaineyaḥ prāṇa‌dajjitvā yodhānāṃ tava paśyatām ||46||

47nyahanattāvakāṃścāpi sātyakiḥ satyavikramaḥ
|
niśitairbahubhirbāṇaiste'dravanta bhayārditāḥ ||47||

48etasminneva kāle tu drupadasyātmajo balī
|
dhṛṣṭadyumno mahārāja putraṃ tava janeśvaram ||48||

49chādayāmāsa samare śaraiḥ sannataparvabhiḥ
|
sa cchādyamāno viśikhairdṛṣṭadyumneta bhārata ||49||

50vivyathe na ca rājendra tava putro janeśvara
|
dhṛṣṭadyumnaṃ ca samare tūrṇaṃ vivyādha patribhiḥ ||50||

51ṣaṣṭhyā ca triṃśatā caiva tada‌dbhutamivābhavan
|
tasya senāpatiḥ kruddho dhanuściccheda māriṣa ||51||

52hayāṃśca caturaḥ śīghraṃ nijaghāna mahābalaḥ
|
śaraiścainaṃ suniśitaiḥ kṣipraṃ vivyādha saptabhiḥ ||52||

53sa hatāśvānmahābāhuravaplutya rathādbalī
|
padātirasimudyamya prādravat pārṣataṃ prati ||53||

54śakunistaṃ samabhyetya rājagṛddhī mahābalaḥ
|
rājānaṃ sarvalokasya rathamāropayan svakam ||54||

55tato nṛpaṃ parājitya pārṣataḥ paravīrahā
|
nyahanattāvakaṃ sainyaṃ vajrapāṇirivāsurān ||55||

56kṛtavarmā raṇe bhīmaṃ śarairārchanmahārathaḥ
|
pracchā‌dayāmāsa ca taṃ mahāmegho raviṃ yathā ||56||

57tataḥ prahasya samare bhīmasenaḥ parantapaḥ
|
preṣayāmāsa saṃkruddhaḥ sāyakān kṛtavarmaṇe ||57||

58tairardyamāno'tirathaḥ sāttvataḥ satyakovidaḥ
|
nākampata mahārāja bhīmaṃ cārchacchitaiḥ śaraiḥ ||58||

59tasyāśvāṃścaturo hatvā bhīmaseno mahārathaḥ
|
sārathiṃ pātayāmāsa sadhvajaṃ supariṣkṛtam ||59||

60śarairbahuvidhaiścainamācinot paravīrahā
|
śakalīkṛtasarvāṅgo hatāśvaḥ pratyadṛśyata ||60||

61hatāśvaśca tatastūrṇaṃ vṛṣakasya rathaṃ yayau
|
syālasya te mahārāja tava putrasya paśyataḥ ||61||

62bhīmaseno'pi saṅkruddhastava sainyamupādravat
|
nijaghāna ca saṃkruddho daṇḍapāṇirivāntakaḥ ||62||
