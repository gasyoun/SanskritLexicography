Mahābhārata 13.107 (Anuśāsanaparvan)

1yudhiṣṭhira uvāca|
pitāmahena vidhivadyajñāḥ proktā mahātmanā
|
guṇāścaiṣāṃ yathātathyaṃ pretya ceha ca sarvaśaḥ ||1||

2na te śakyā daridreṇa yajñāḥ prāptuṃ pitāmaha
|
bahūpakaraṇā yajñā nānāsaṃbhāravistarāḥ ||2||

3pārthivai rājaputrairvā śakyāḥ prāptuṃ pitāmaha
|
nārthanyūnairavaguṇairekātmabhirasaṃhataiḥ ||3||

4yo daridrairapi vidhiḥ śakyaḥ prāptuṃ sadā bhavet
|
arthanyūnairavaguṇairekātmabhirasaṃhataiḥ ||4||

5tulyo yajñaphalairetaistanme brūhi pitāmaha
|
bhīṣma uvāca|
ida‌maṅgirasā proktamupavāsaphalātmakam ||5||

6vidhiṃ yajñaphalaistulyaṃ tannibodha yudhiṣṭhira
|
yastu kalyaṃ tathā sāyaṃ bhuñjāno nāntarā pibet ||6||

7ahiṃsānirato nityaṃ juhvāno jātavedasam
|
ṣaḍbhireva sa varṣaistu sidhyate nātra saṃśayaḥ ||7||

8taptakāñcanavarṇaṃ ca vimānaṃ labhate naraḥ
|
devastrīṇāmadhīvāse nṛtyagītaninādite ||8||

9prājāpatye vasetpadmaṃ varṣāṇāmagnisaṃnibhe
|
trīṇi varṣāṇi yaḥ prāśetsatataṃ tvekabhojanam ||9||

10dharmapatnīrato nityamagniṣṭomaphalaṃ labhet
|
yajñaṃ bahusuvarṇaṃ vā vāsavapriyamācaret ||10||

11satyavāndānaśīlaśca brahmaṇyaścānasūyakaḥ
|
kṣānto dānto jitakrodhaḥ sa gacchati parāṃ gatim ||11||

12pāṇḍurābhrapratīkāśe vimāne haṃsalakṣaṇe
|
dve samāpte tataḥ padme sopsarobhirvasetsaha ||12||

13dvitīye divase yastu prāśnīyādekabhojanam
|
sadā dvādaśamāsāṃstu juhvāno jātavedasam ||13||

14agnikāryaparo nityaṃ nityaṃ kalyaprabodhanaḥ
|
agniṣṭomasya yajñasya phalaṃ prāpnoti mānavaḥ ||14||

15haṃsasārasayuktaṃ ca vimānaṃ labhate naraḥ
|
indraloke ca vasate varastrībhiḥ samāvṛtaḥ ||15||

16tṛtīye divase yastu prāśnīyādekabhojanam
|
sadā dvādaśamāsāṃstu juhvāno jātavedasam ||16||

17agnikāryaparo nityaṃ nityaṃ kalyaprabodhanaḥ
|
atirātrasya yajñasya phalaṃ prāpnotyanuttamam ||17||

18mayūrahaṃsayuktaṃ ca vimānaṃ labhate naraḥ
|
saptarṣīṇāṃ sadā loke sopsarobhirvasetsaha ||18||

19nivartanaṃ ca tatrāsya trīṇi padmāni vai vibhuḥ
|
divase yaścaturthe tu prāśnīyādekabhojanam ||19||

20sadā dvādaśamāsānvai juhvāno jātavedasam
|
vājapeyasya yajñasya phalaṃ prāpnotyanuttamam ||20||

21indrakanyābhirūḍhaṃ ca vimānaṃ labhate naraḥ
|
sāgarasya ca paryante vāsavaṃ lokamāvaset ||21||

22devarājasya ca krīḍāṃ nityakālamavekṣate
|
divase pañcame yastu prāśnīyādekabhojanam ||22||

23sadā dvādaśamāsāṃstu juhvāno jātavedasam
|
alubdhaḥ satyavādī ca brahmaṇyaścāvihiṃsakaḥ ||23||

24anasūyurapāpastho dvādaśāha phalaṃ labhet
|
jāmbūnadamayaṃ divyaṃ vimānaṃ haṃsalakṣaṇam ||24||

25sūryamālāsamābhāsamārohetpāṇḍuraṃ gṛham
|
āvartanāni catvāri tathā padmāni dvādaśa ||25||

26śarāgniparimāṇaṃ ca tatrāsau vasate sukham
|
divase yastu ṣaṣṭhe vai muniḥ prāśeta bhojanam ||26||

27sadā dvādaśamāsānvai juhvāno jātavedasam
|
sadā triṣavaṇasnāyī brahmacāryanasūyakaḥ ||27||

28gavāṃ medhasya yajñasya phalaṃ prāpnotyanuttamam
|
agnijvālāsamābhāsaṃ haṃsabarhiṇasevitam ||28||

29śātakuṃbhasamāyuktaṃ sādhayedyānamuttamam
|
tathaivāpsarasāmaṅke pratisuptaḥ prabodhyate ||29||

30nūpurāṇāṃ ninādena mekhalānāṃ ca niḥsvanaiḥ
|
koṭīsahasraṃ varṣāṇāṃ trīṇi koṭiśatāni ca ||30||

31padmānyaṣṭādaśa tathā patāke dve tathaiva ca
|
ayutāni ca pañcāśadṛkṣacarmaśatasya ca ||31||

32lomnāṃ pramāṇena samaṃ brahmaloke mahīyate
|
divase saptame yastu prāśnīyādekabhojanam ||32||

33sadā dvādaśamāsānvai juhvāno jātavedasam
|
sarasvatīṃ gopayāno brahmacaryaṃ samācaran ||33||

34sumanovarṇakaṃ caiva madhumāṃsaṃ ca varjayan
|
puruṣo marutāṃ lokamindralokaṃ ca gacchati ||34||

35tatra tatra hi siddhārtho devakanyābhirarcyate
|
phalaṃ bahusuvarṇasya yajñasya labhate naraḥ ||35||

36saṃkhyāmatiguṇāṃ cāpi teṣu lokeṣu modate
|
yastu saṃvatsaraṃ kṣānto bhuṅkte'hanyaṣṭame naraḥ ||36||

37devakāryaparo nityaṃ juhvāno jātavedasam
|
pauṇḍarīkasya yajñasya phalaṃ prāpnotyanuttamam ||37||

38padmavarṇanibhaṃ caiva vimānamadhirohati
|
kṛṣṇāḥ kanakagauryaśca nāryaḥ śyāmāstathāparāḥ ||38||

39vayorūpavilāsinyo labhate nātra saṃśayaḥ
|
yastu saṃvatsaraṃ bhuṃkte navame navame'hani ||39||

40sadā dvādaśamāsānvai juhvāno jātavedasam
|
aśvamedhasahasrasya phalaṃ prāpnotyanuttamam ||40||

41puṇḍarīkaprakāśaṃ ca vimānaṃ labhate naraḥ
|
dīptasūryāgnitejobhirdivyamālābhireva ca ||41||

42nīyate rudrakanyābhiḥ sontarikṣaṃ sanātanam
|
aṣṭādaśa sahastrāṇi varṣāṇāṃ kalpameva ca ||42||

43koṭīśatasahasraṃ ca teṣu lokeṣu modate
|
yastu saṃvatsaraṃ bhuṃkte daśāhe vai gate gate ||43||

44sadā dvādaśamāsānvai juhvāno jātavedasam
|
brahmakanyānivāse ca sarvabhūtamanohare ||44||

45aśvamedhasahasrasya phalaṃ prāpnotyanuttamam
|
rūpavatyaśca taṃ kanyā ramayanti sanātanam ||45||

46nīlotpalanibhairvarṇai raktotpalanibhastathā
|
vimānaṃ maṇḍalāvartamāvartagahanākulam ||46||

47sāgarormipratīkāśaṃ sa labhedyānamanuttamam
|
vicitramaṇimālābhirnāditaṃ śaṅkhaniḥsvanaiḥ ||47||

48sphāṭikairvajrasāraiśca stambhaiḥ sukṛtavedikam
|
ārohati mahadyānaṃ haṃsasārasanāditam ||48||

49ekādaśe tu divase yaḥ prāpte prāśate haviḥ
|
sadā dvādaśamāsāṃstu juhvāno jātavedasam ||49||

50parastriyaṃ nābhilaṣedvācātha manasāpi vā
|
anṛtaṃ ca na bhāṣeta mātāpitroḥ kṛtepi vā ||50||

51abhigacchenmahādevaṃ vimānasthaṃ mahābalam
|
aśvamedhasahasrasya phalaṃ prāpnotyanuttamam ||51||

52svāyaṃbhuvaṃ ca paśyeta vimānaṃ samupasthitam
|
kumāryaḥ kāñcanābhāsā rūpavatyo nayanti tam ||52||

53rudrāṇāṃ tamadhīvāsaṃ divi divyaṃ manoharam
|
varṣāṇyaparimeyāni yugāntāgnisamaprabhaḥ ||53||

54koṭīśatasahasraṃ ca daśakoṭiśatāni ca
|
rudraṃ nityaṃ praṇamate devadānavasaṃmatam ||54||

55sa tasmai darśanaṃ prāpto divase divase bhavet
|
divase dvādaśe yastu prāpte vai prāśate haviḥ ||55||

56sadā dvādaśamāsānvai sarvamedhaphalaṃ labhet
|
ādityaṃ dvādaśe tasya vimānaṃ saṃvidhīyate ||56||

57maṇimuktāpravālaiśca mahārhairupaśobhitam
|
haṃsabhāsāparikṣiptaṃ nāgavīthīsamākulam ||57||

58mayūraiścakravākaiśca kūjadbhirupaśobhitam
|
aṭṭairmahadbhiḥ saṃyuktaṃ brahmaloke pratiṣṭhitam ||58||

59nityamāvasathaṃ rājannaranārīsamāvṛtam
|
ṛṣirevaṃ mahābhāgastvaṅgirā prāha dharmavit ||59||

60trayodaśe tu divase prāpte yaḥ prāśate haviḥ
|
sadā dvādaśamāsānvai devasatraphalaṃ labhet ||60||

61raktapadmodayaṃ nāma vimānaṃ sādhayennaraḥ
|
jātarūpaprayuktaṃ ca ratnasañcayabhūṣitam ||61||

62devakanyābhirākīrṇaṃ divyābharaṇabhūṣitam
|
puṇyagaṃdhodayaṃ divyaṃ vāyavyairupaśobhitam ||62||

63tatra śaṃkupatāke dve yugāntaṃ kalpameva ca
|
ayutāyutaṃ tathā padmaṃ samudraṃ ca tathā vaset ||63||

64gītagaṃdharvaghoṣaiśca bherīpaṇavaniḥsvanaiḥ
|
sadā prahlāditastābhirdevakanyābhirijyate ||64||

65caturdaśe tu divase yaḥ pūrṇe prāśate haviḥ
|
sadā dvādaśamāsāṃstu mahāmedhaphalaṃ labhet ||65||

66anirdeśyavayorūpā devakanyāḥ svalaṃkṛtāḥ
|
mṛṣṭataptāṅgadadharā vimānairupayānti tam ||66||

67kalahaṃsavinirghoṣairnūpurāṇāṃ ca niḥsvanaiḥ
|
kāñcīnāṃ ca samutkarṣaistatra tatra nibodhyate ||67||

68devakanyānivāse ca tasminvasati mānavaḥ
|
jāhnavīvālukākīrṇaṃ pūrṇaṃ saṃvatsaraṃ naraḥ ||68||

69yastu pakṣe gate bhuṃkte ekabhaktaṃ jitendriyaḥ
|
sadā dvāda‌śamāsāṃstu juhvāno jātavedasam ||69||

70rājasūyasahasrasya phalaṃ prāpnotyanuttamam
|
yānamārohate divyaṃ haṃsabarhiṇasevitam ||70||

71maṇimaṇḍalakaiścitraṃ jātarūpasamāvṛtam
|
divyābharaṇaśobhābhirvarastrībhiralaṃkṛtam ||71||

72ekastambhaṃ caturdvāraṃ saptabhaumaṃ sumaṅgalam
|
vaijayantīsahastraiśca śobhitaṃ gītaniḥsvanaiḥ ||72||

73divyaṃ divyaguṇopetaṃ vimānamadhirohati
|
maṇimuktāpravālaiśca bhūṣitaṃ vaidyutaprabham ||73||

74vasedyugasahasraṃ ca khaḍgakuñjaravāhanaḥ
|
ṣoḍaśe divase prāpte yaḥ kuryādekabhojanam ||74||

75sadā dvādaśamāsānvai somayajñaphalaṃ labhet
|
somakanyānivāseṣu so'dhyāvasati nityaśaḥ ||75||

76saumyagaṃdhānuliptaśca kāmakāragatirbhavet
|
sudarśanābhirnārībhirmadhurābhistathaiva ca ||76||

77arcyate vai vimānasthaḥ kāmabhogaiśca sevyate
|
phalaṃ padmaśataprakhyaṃ mahākalpaṃ daśādhikam ||77||

78āvartanāni catvāri sādhayeccāpyasau naraḥ
|
divase saptadaśame yaḥ prāpte prāśate haviḥ ||78||

79sadā dvādaśamāsānvai juhvāno jātavedasam
|
sthānaṃ vāruṇamaindraṃ ca raudraṃ vāpyadhigacchati ||79||

80mārutauśanase caiva brahmalokaṃ sa gacchati
|
tatra daivatakanyābhirāsanenopacaryate ||80||

81bhūrbhuvaṃ cāpi devarṣiṃ viśvarūpamavekṣate
|
tatra devādhidevasya kumāryo ramayanti tam ||81||

82dvātriṃśadrūpadhāriṇyo madhurāḥ samalaṃkṛtāḥ
|
caṃdrādityāvubhau yāvadgagane carataḥ prabho ||82||

83tāvaccaratyasau dhīraḥ sudhāmṛtarasāśanaḥ
|
aṣṭādaśe yo divase prāśnīyādekabhojanam ||83||

84sadā dvādaśamāsānvai saptalokānsa paśyati
|
rathaiḥ sanandighoṣaiśca pṛṣṭhataḥ so'nugamyate ||84||

85devakanyādhirūḍhaistu bhrājamānaiḥ svalaṃkṛtaiḥ
|
vyāghrasiṃhaprayuktaṃ ca meghasvananināditam ||85||

86vimānamuttamaṃ divyaṃ susukhī hyadhirohati
|
tatra kalpasahasraṃ sa kanyābhiḥ saha modate ||86||

87sudhārasaṃ ca bhuñjīta amṛtopamamuttamam
|
ekonaviṃśatidine yo bhuṅkte ekabhojanam ||87||

88sadā dvādaśamāsānvai saptalokānsa paśyati
|
uttamaṃ labhate sthānamapsarogaṇasevitam ||88||

89gandharvairupagītaṃ ca vimānaṃ sūryavarcasam
|
tatrāmaravarastrībhirmodate vigatajvaraḥ ||89||

90divyāmbaradharaḥ śrīmānayutānāṃ śataṃ śatam
|
pūrṇe'tha viṃśe divase yo bhuṅkte hyekabhojanam ||90||

91sadā dvādaśamāsāṃstu satyavādī dhṛtavrataḥ
|
amāṃsāśī brahmacārī sarvabhūtahite rataḥ ||91||

92sa lokānvipulān ramyānādityānāmupāśnute
|
gandharvairapsarobhiśca divyamālyānulepanaiḥ ||92||

93vimānaiḥ kāñcanairhṛdyaiḥ pṛṣṭhataścānugamyate
|
ekaviṃśe tu divase yo bhuṅkte hyekabhojanam ||93||

94sadā dvādaśamāsānvai juhvāno jātavedasam
|
lokamauśanasaṃ divyaṃ śakralokaṃ ca gacchati ||94||

95aśvinormarutāṃ caiva sukheṣvabhirataḥ sadā
|
anabhijñaśca duḥkhānāṃ vimānavaramāsthitaḥ ||95||

96sevyamāno varastrībhiḥ krīḍatyamaravatprabhuḥ
|
dvāviṃśe divase prāpte yo bhuṅkte hyekabhojanam ||96||

97sadā dvādaśamāsānvai juhvāno jātavedasam
|
ahiṃsānirato dhīmān satyavāganasūyakaḥ ||97||

98lokānvasūnāmāpnoti divākarasamaprabhaḥ
|
kāmacārī sudhāhāro vimānavaramāsthitaḥ ||98||

99ramate devakanyābhirdivyābharaṇabhūṣitaḥ
|
trayoviṃśe tu divase prāśedyastvekabhojanam ||99||

100sadā dvādaśamāsāṃstu mitāhāro jitondrayaḥ
|
vāyoruśanasaścaiva rudralokaṃ ca gacchati ||100||

101kāmacārī kāmagamaḥ pūjyamānopsarogaṇaiḥ
|
anekaguṇaparyantaṃ vimānavaramāsthitaḥ ||101||

102ramate devakanyābhirdivyābharaṇabhūṣitaḥ
|
caturviṃśe tu divase yaḥ prāpte prāśate haviḥ ||102||

103sadā dvādaśamāsāṃśca juhvāno jātavedasam
|
ādityānāmadhīvāse modamāno vasecciram ||103||

104divyamālyāmbaradharo divyagandhānulepanaḥ
|
vimāne kāñcane divye haṃsayukte manorame ||104||

105ramate devakanyānāṃ sahasrairayutaistathā
|
pañcaviṃśe tu divase yaḥ prāśedekabhojanam ||105||

106sadā dvādaśamāsāṃstu puṣkalaṃ yānamāruhet
|
siṃhavyāghraprayuktaistu meghaniḥsvananāditaiḥ ||106||

107sa rathairnandighoṣaiśca pṛṣṭhato hyanugamyate
|
devakanyāsamārūḍhaiḥ kāñcanairvimalaiḥ śubhaiḥ ||107||

108vimānamuttamaṃ divyamāsthāya sumanoharam
|
tatra kalpasahasraṃ vai vasate strīśatāvṛte ||108||

109sudhārasaṃ copajīvannamṛtopamamuttamam
|
ṣaḍviṃśe divase yastu prakuryādekabhojanam ||109||

110sadā dvādaśamāsāṃstu niyato niyatāśanaḥ
|
jitendriyo vītarāgo juhvāno jātavedasam ||110||

111sa prāpnoti mahābhāgaḥ pūjyamāno'psarogaṇaiḥ
|
saptānāṃ marutāṃ lokānvasūnāṃ cāpi sośnute ||111||

112vimānaiḥ sphāṭikairdivyaiḥ sarvaratnairalaṅkṛtaiḥ
|
gandharvairapsarobhiśca pūjyamānaḥ pramodate ||112||

113dve yugānāṃ sahastre tu divye divyena tejasā
|
saptaviṃśe'tha divase yaḥ kuryādekabhojanam ||113||

114sadā dvādaśamāsāṃstu juhvāno jātavedasam
|
phalaṃ prāpnoti vipulaṃ devaloke ca pūjyate ||114||

115amṛtāśī vasaṃstatra sa vitṛṣṇaḥ pramodate
|
devarṣicaritaṃ rājan rājarṣibhiranuṣṭhitam ||115||

116adhyāvasati divyātmā vimānavaramāsthitaḥ
|
strībhirmanobhirāmābhī ramamāṇo madotkaṭaḥ ||116||

117yugakalpasahasrāṇi trīṇyāvasati vai sukham
|
yo'ṣṭāviṃśe tu divase prāśnīyādekabhojanam ||117||

118sadā dvādaśamāsāṃstu jitātmā virjitendriyaḥ
|
phalaṃ devarṣicaritaṃ vipulaṃ samupāśnute ||118||

119bhogavāṃstejasā bhāti sahasrāṃśurivāmalaḥ
|
sukumāryaśca nāryastaṃ ramamāṇāḥ suvarcasaḥ ||119||

120pīnastanorujaghanā divyābharaṇabhūṣitāḥ
|
ramayanti manaḥkānte vimāne sūryasannibhe ||120||

121sarvakāmagame divye kalpāyutaśataṃ samāḥ
|
ekonatriṃśe divase yaḥ prāśedekabhojanam ||121||

122sadā dvādaśamāsānvai satyavrataparāyaṇaḥ
|
tasya lokāḥ śubhā divyā devarājarṣipūjitāḥ ||122||

123vimānaṃ sūryacandrābhaṃ divyaṃ samadhigacchati
|
jātarūpamayaṃ yuktaṃ sarvaratnasamanvitam ||123||

124apsarogaṇasaṃpūrṇaṃ gandharvairabhināditam
|
tatra cainaṃ śubhā nāryo divyābharaṇabhūṣitāḥ ||124||

125manobhirāmā madhurā ramayanti madotkaṭāḥ
|
bhogavāṃstejasā yukto vaiśvānarasamaprabhaḥ ||125||

126divyo divyena vapuṣā bhrājamāna ivāmaraḥ
|
vasūnāṃ marutāṃ caiva sādhyānāmaśvinostathā ||126||

127rudrāṇāṃ ca tathā lokaṃ brahmalokaṃ ca gacchati
|
yastu māse gate bhuṅkte ekabhaktaṃ śamātmakaḥ ||127||

128sadā dvādaśamāsānvai brahmalokamavāpnuyāt
|
sudhārasakṛtāhāraḥ śrīmānsarvamanoharaḥ ||128||

129tejasā vapuṣā lakṣmyā bhrājate raśmivāniva
|
divyamālyāmbaradharo divyagandhānulepanaḥ ||129||

130sukheṣvabhirato bhogī duḥkhānāmavijānakaḥ
|
svayaṃprabhābhirnārībhirvimānastho mahīyate ||130||

131rudradevarṣikanyābhiḥ satataṃ cābhipūjyate
|
nānāramaṇarūpābhirnānārāgābhireva ca ||131||

132nānāmadhurabhāṣābhirnānāratibhireva ca
|
vimāne gaganākāre sūryavaidūryasannibhe ||132||

133pṛṣṭhataḥ somasaṃkāśe udarke cābhrasānnibhe
|
dakṣiṇāyāṃ tu raktābhe adhastānnīlamaṇḍale ||133||

134ūrdhvaṃ vicitrasaṃkāśe naiko vasati pūjitaḥ
|
yāvadvarṣasahasraṃ vai jambudvīpe pravarṣati ||134||

135tāvatsaṃvatsarāḥ proktā brahmaloke'sya dhīmataḥ
|
vipruṣaścaiva yāvantyo nipatanti nabhastalāt ||135||

136varṣāsu varṣatastāvannivasatyamaraprabhaḥ
|
