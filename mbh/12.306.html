Mahābhārata 12.306 (Śāntiparvan)

1janaka uvāca |
nānātvaikatvamityuktaṃ tvayaitadṛṣisattama
|
paśyāmyetaddhi saṃdigdhametayorve nidarśanam||1||

2tathā buddhaprabuddhābhyāṃ buddhyamānasya cānagha
|
sthūlabuddhyā na paśyāmi tattvametanna saṃśayaḥ||2||

3akṣarakṣarayoruktaṃ tvayā yadapi kāraṇam
|
tadapyasthirabuddhitvātpraṇaṣṭamiva me'nagha||3||

4tadetacchrotumicchāmi nānātvaikatvadarśanam
|
buddhaṃ cāpratibuddhaṃ ca budhyamānaṃ ca tattvataḥ||4||

5vidyāvidye ca bhagavānnakṣaraṃ kṣarameva ca
|
sāṅkhyaṃ yogaṃ ca kārsnyena pṛthakcaivāpṛthakca ha||5||

6vasiṣṭha uvāca |
hanta te saṃpravakṣyāmi yadetadanupṛcchasi
|
yogakṛtyaṃ mahārāja pṛthageva śṛṇuṣva me||6||

7yogakṛtyaṃ tu yogānāṃ dhyānameva paraṃ balam
|
taccāpi dvividhaṃ dhyānamāhurvidyāvido janāḥ||7||

8ekāgratā ca manasaḥ prāṇāyāmastathaiva ca
|
prāṇāyāmastu saguṇo nirguṇo manasastathā||8||

9mūtrotsargapurīṣe ca bhojane ca narādhipa
|
trikālaṃ nābhiyuñjīta śeṣaṃ yuñjīta tatparaḥ||9||

10indriyāṇīndriyārthebhyo nivartya manasā śuciḥ
|
daśadvādaśabhirvāpi caturviṃśāt paraṃ tataḥ||10||

11sañcodanābhirmatimānātmānaṃ codayedatha
|
tiṣṭhantamajaraṃ taṃ tu yattaduktaṃ manīṣibhiḥ||11||

12taiścātmā satataṃ jñeya ityevamanuśuśruma
|
vrataṃ hyahīnamanaso nānyatheti viniścayaḥ||12||

13vimuktaḥ sarvasaṅgebhyo laghvāhāro jitendriyaḥ
|
pūrvarātre pararātre dhārayīta mano''tmani||13||

14sthirīkṛtyendriyagrāma manasā mithileśvara
|
mano buddhyā sthiraṃ kṛtvā pāṣāṇa iva niścalaḥ||14||

15sthāṇuvaccāpyakampaḥ syādgirivaccāpi niścalaḥ
|
buddhyā vidhividhānajñāstadā yuktaṃ pracakṣate||15||

16na śṛṇoti na cāghrāti na raṃsyati na paśyati
|
na ca sparśaṃ vijānāti na saṃkalpayate manaḥ||16||

17na cābhimanyate kiṃcinna na budhyati kāṣṭhavat
|
tadā prakṛtimāpannaṃ yuktamāhurmanīṣiṇaḥ||17||

18nirvāte hi yathā dīpyandīpastadvatprakāśate
|
nirliṅgo'vicalaścordhvaṃ na tiryaggatimāpnuyāt||18||

19tadā tamanupaśyeta yasmindṛṣṭe na kathyate
|
hṛdayastho'ntarātmeti jñeyo jñastāta madvidhaiḥ ||19||

20vidhūma iva saptārcirāditya iva raśmimān
|
vaidyuto'gnirivākāśe dṛśyate''tmā tathā''tmani||20||

21ye paśyanti mahātmāno dhṛtimanto manīṣiṇaḥ
|
brāhmaṇā brahmayonisthā hyayonimamṛtātmakam||21||

22tadevāhuraṇubhyo'ṇu tanmahadbhyo mahattaram
|
tattattvaṃ sarvabhūteṣu dhruvaṃ tiṣṭhanna dṛśyate||22||

23buddhidravyeṇa dṛśyeta manodīpena lokakṛt
|
mahatastamasastāta pāre tiṣṭhanna tāmasaḥ||23||

24sa tamonuda ityuktaḥ sarvajñairvedapāragaiḥ
|
vimalo vitamaskaśca nirliṅgo'liṅgasaṃjñitaḥ||24||

25yoga eṣa hi yogānāṃ kimanyadyogalakṣaṇam
|
evaṃ paśyaṃ prapaśyanti ātmānamajaraṃ param||25||

26yogadarśanametāvaduktaṃ te tattvato mayā
|
sāṃkhyajñānaṃ pravakṣyāmi parisaṃkhyānadarśanam||26||

27avyaktamāhuḥ prakṛtiṃ parāṃ prakṛtivādinaḥ
|
tasmānmahatsamutpannaṃ dvitīyaṃ rājasattama||27||

28ahaṅkārastu mahatastṛtīyamiti naḥ śrutam
|
pañcabhūtānyahaṅkārādāhuḥ sāṃkhyātmadarśinaḥ||28||

29etāḥ prakṛtayaścāṣṭau vikārāścāpi ṣoḍaśa
|
pañca caiva viśeṣā vai tathā pañcendriyāṇi ca||29||

30etāvadeva tattvānāṃ sāṃkhyamāhurmanīṣiṇaḥ
|
sāṃkhye vidhividhānajñā nityaṃ sāṃkhyapathe ratāḥ||30||

31yasmādyadabhijāyeta tattatraiva pralīyate
|
līyante pratilomāni sṛjyante cāntarātmanā||31||

32anulomena jāyante līyante pratilomataḥ
|
guṇā guṇeṣu satataṃ sāgarasyormayo yathā||32||

33sargapralaya etāvānprakṛternṛpasattama
|
ekatvaṃ pralaye cāsya bahutvaṃ ca yadā'sṛjat||33||

34evameva ca rājendra vijñeyaṃ jñānakovidaiḥ
|
adhiṣṭhātāramavyaktamasyāpyetannidarśanam||34||

35ekatvaṃ ca bahutvaṃ ca prakṛterarthatattvavān
|
ekatvaṃ pralaye cāsya bahutvaṃ ca pravartanāt||35||

36bahudhā''tmā prakurvīta prakṛtiṃ prasavātmikām
|
tacca kṣetraṃ mahānātmā pañcaviṃśo'dhitiṣṭhati||36||

37adhiṣṭhāteti rājendra procyate yatisattamaiḥ
|
adhiṣṭhānādadhiṣṭhātā kṣetrāṇāmiti naḥ śrutam||37||

38kṣetraṃ jānāti cāvyaktaṃ kṣetrajña iti cocyate
|
avyaktike praviśate puruṣaśceti kathyate||38||

39anyadeva ca kṣetraṃ syādanyaḥ kṣetrajña ucyate
|
kṣetramavyaktamityuktaṃ jñātāraṃ pañcaviṃśakam||39||

40anyadeva ca jñānaṃ syādanyajjñeyaṃ taducyate
|
jñānamavyaktamityuktaṃ jñeyo vai pañcaviṃśakaḥ||40||

41avyaktaṃ kṣetramityuktaṃ tathā sattvaṃ tatheśvaraḥ
|
anīśvaramatattvaṃ ca tattvaṃ tatpañcaviṃśakam||41||

42sāṃkhyadarśanametāvatparisaṃkhyānudarśanam
|
sāṃkhyāḥ prakurvate caiva prakṛtiṃ ca pracakṣate||42||

43tattvāni ca caturviṃśatparisaṃkhyāya tattvataḥ
|
sāṃkhyāḥ saha prakṛtyā tu nistattvaḥ pañcaviṃśakaḥ||43||

44pañcaviṃśo'prakṛtyātmā budhyamāna iti smṛtaḥ
|
yadā tu budhyate''tmānaṃ tadā bhavati kevalaḥ||44||

45samyagdarśanametāvadbhāṣitaṃ tava tattvataḥ
|
evametadvijānantaḥ sāmyatāṃ prati yāntyuta||45||

46samyaṅnidarśanaṃ nāma pratyakṣaṃ prakṛtestathā
|
guṇatattvādyathaitāni nirguṇebhyastathā bhavet||46||

47na tvevaṃ vartamānānāmāvṛttirvidyate punaḥ
|
vidyate'kṣarabhāvatvādaparaṃ paramavyayam||47||

48paśyerannaikamatayo na samyakteṣu darśanam
|
te'vyaktaṃ pratipadyante punaḥ punararindama||48||

49sarvametadvijānanto na sarvasya prabodhanāt
|
vyaktībhūtā bhaviṣyanti vyaktasya vaśavartinaḥ||49||

50sarvamavyaktamityuktamasarvaḥ pañcaviṃśakaḥ
|
ya enamabhijānanti na bhayaṃ teṣu vidyate||50||
