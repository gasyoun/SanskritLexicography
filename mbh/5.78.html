Mahābhārata 5.78 (Udyogaparvan)

1arjuna uvāca|
uktaṃ yudhiṣṭhireṇaiva yāvadvācyaṃ janārdana
|
tava vākyaṃ tu me śrutvā pratibhāti paraṃtapa ||1||

2naiva praśamamatra tvaṃ manyase sukaraṃ prabho
|
lobhādvā dhṛtarāṣṭrasya dainyādvā samupasthitāt ||2||

3aphalaṃ manyase vā'pi puruṣasya parākramam
|
na cāntareṇa karmāṇi pauruṣeṇa balodayaḥ ||3||

4tadidaṃ bhāṣitaṃ vākyaṃ tathācana tathaiva tat
|
na caitadevaṃ draṣṭavyamasādhyamapi kiñcana ||4||

5kiṃ caitanmanyase kṛcchramasmākamavasādakam
|
kurvanti teṣāṃ karmāṇi yeṣāṃ nāsti phalodayaḥ ||5||

6saṃpādyamānaṃ samyakca syāt karma saphalaṃ prabho
|
sa tathā kṛṣṇa vartasva yathā śarma bhavetparaiḥ ||6||

7pāṇḍavānāṃ kurūṇāṃ ca bhavānnaḥ prathamaḥ suhṛt
|
surāṇāmasurāṇāṃ ca yathā vīra prajāpatiḥ ||7||

8kurūṇāṃ pāṇḍavānāṃ ca pratipatsva nirāmayam
|
asmaddhitamanuṣṭhānaṃ manye tava na duṣkaram ||8||

9evaṃ ca kāryatāmeti kāryaṃ tava janārdana
|
gamanādevameva tvaṃ kariṣyasi janārdana ||9||

10cikīrṣitamathānyatte tasmin vīra durātmani
|
bhaviṣyati ca tatsarvaṃ yathā tava cikīrṣitam ||10||

11śarma taiḥ saha vā no'stu tava vā yaccikīrṣitam
|
vicāryamāṇo yaḥ kāmastava kṛṣṇa sa no guruḥ
|
na sa nārhati duṣṭātmā vadhaṃ sasutabāndhavaḥ ||11||

12yena dharmasute dṛṣṭā na sā śrīrupamarṣitā
|
yaccāpyapaśyatopāyaṃ dharmiṣṭhaṃ madhusūdana ||12||

13upāyena nṛśaṃsena hṛtā durdyūtadevinā
|
kathaṃ hi puruṣo jātaḥ kṣatriyeṣu dhanurdharaḥ ||13||

14samāhūto nivarteta prāṇatyāge'pyupasthite
|
adharmeṇa jitān dṛṣṭvā vane pravrajitāṃstathā ||14||

15vadhyatāṃ mama vārṣṇeya nirgato'sau suyodhanaḥ
|
na caitada‌dbhutaṃ kṛṣṇa mitrārthe yaccikīrṣasi
|
kriyā kathaṃ ca mukhyā syānmṛdunā cetareṇa vā ||15||

16athavā manyase jyāyān vadhasteṣāmanantaram
|
tadeva kriyatāmāśu na vicāryamatastvayā ||16||

17jānāsi hi yathaitena draupadī pāpabuddhinā
|
parikliṣṭā sabhāmadhye tacca tasyopamarṣitam ||17||

18sa nāma samyagvarteta pāṇḍaveṣviti mādhava
|
na me saṃjāyate buddhirbījamuptamivoṣare ||18||

19tasmādyanmanyase yuktaṃ pāṇḍavānāṃ hitaṃ ca yat
|
tathā''śu kuru vārṣṇeya yannaḥ kāryamanantaram ||19||
