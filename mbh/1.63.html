Mahābhārata 1.63 (Ādiparvan)

1vaiśaṃpāyana uvāca|
rājoparicaro nāma dharmanityo mahīpatiḥ
|
babhūva mṛgayāṃ gantuṃ sadā kila dhṛtavrataḥ||1||

2sa cediviṣayaṃ ramyaṃ vasuḥ pauravanandanaḥ
|
indropadeśājjagrāha ramaṇīyaṃ mahīpatiḥ||2||

3tamāśrame nyastaśastraṃ nivasantaṃ taponidhim
|
devāḥ śakrapurogā vai rājānamupatasthire||3||

4indratvamarho rājā'yaṃ tapasetyanucintya vai
|
taṃ sāntvena nṛpaṃ sākṣāttapasaḥ saṃnyavartayan||4||

5devā ūcuḥ|
na saṃkīryeta dharmo'yaṃ pṛthivyāṃ pṛthivīpate
|
tvayā hi dharmo vidhṛtaḥ kṛtsnaṃ dhārayate jagat||5||

6indra uvāca|
loke dharmaṃ pālaya tvaṃ nityayuktaḥ samāhitaḥ
|
dharmayuktastato lokānpuṇyānprāpsyasi śāśvatān||6||

7diviṣṭhasya bhuviṣṭhastvaṃ sakhā bhūto mama priyaḥ
|
ramya pṛthivyāṃ yo deśastamāvasa narādhipa||7||

8paśavyaścaiva puṇyaśca prabhūtadhanadhānyavān
|
svā rakṣyaścaiva saumyaśca bhogyairbhūmiguṇairyutaḥ||8||

9arthavāneṣa deśo hi dhanaratnādibhiryutaḥ
|
vasupūrṇā ca vasudhā vasa cediṣu cedipa||9||

10dharmaśīlā janapadāḥ susaṃtoṣāśca sādhavaḥ
|
na ca mithyāpralāpo'tra svaireṣvapi kuto'nyathā||10||

11na ca pitrā vibhajyante putrā guruhite ratāḥ
|
yuñjate dhuri no gāśca kṛśānsaṃdhukṣayanti ca||11||

12sarve varṇāḥ svadharmasthāḥ sadā cediṣu mānada
|
na te'styaviditaṃ kiṃcittriṣu lokeṣu yadbhavet||12||

13daivopabhogyaṃ divyaṃ tvāmākāśe sphāṭikaṃ mahat
|
ākāśagaṃ tvāṃ maddattaṃ vimānamupapatsyate||13||

14tvamekaḥ sarvamartyeṣu vimānavaramāsthitaḥ
|
cariṣyasyuparistho hi devo vigrahavāniva||14||

15dadāmi te vaijayantīṃ mālāmamlānapaṅkajām
|
dhārayiṣyati saṅgrāme yā tvāṃ śastrairavikṣatam||15||

16lakṣaṇaṃ caitadeveha bhavitā te narādhipa
|
indramāleti vikhyātaṃ dhanyamapratimaṃ mahat||16||

17yaṣṭiṃ ca vaiṇavīṃ tasmai dadau vṛtraniṣūdanaḥ
|
iṣṭapradānamuddiśya śiṣṭānāṃ pratipālinīm||17||

18tasyāḥ śakrasya pūjārthaṃ bhūmau bhūmipatistadā
|
praveśaṃ kārayāmāsa sarvotsavavaraṃ tadā||18||

19tataḥprabhṛti cādyāpi yaṣṭeḥ kṣitipasattamaiḥ
|
praveśaḥ kriyate rājanyathā tena pravartitaḥ||19||

20aparedyustatastasyāḥ kriyate'tyucchrayo nṛpaiḥ
|
alaṅkṛtāyā piṭakairgandhamālyaiśca bhūṣaṇaiḥ||20||

21mālyadāmaparikṣiptā vidhivatkriyate'pi ca
|
bhagavānpūjyate cātra haṃsarūpeṇa ceśvaraḥ||21||

22svayameva gṛhītena vasoḥ prītyā mahātmanaḥ
|
sa tāṃ pūjāṃ mahendrastu dṛṣṭvā devaḥ kṛtāṃ śubhām||22||

23vasunā rājamukhyena prītimānabravītprabhuḥ
|
ye pūjayiṣyanti narā rājānaśca mahaṃ mama||23||

24kārayiṣyanti ca mudā yathā cedipatirnṛpaḥ
|
teṣāṃ śrīrvijayaścaiva sarāṣṭrāṇāṃ bhaviṣyati||24||

25tathā sphīto janapado muditaśca bhaviṣyati
|
evaṃ mahātmanā tena mahendreṇa narādhipa||25||

26vasuḥ prītyā maghavatā mahārājo'bhisatkṛtaḥ
|
utsavaṃ kārayiṣyanti sadā śakrasya ye narāḥ||26||

27bhūmiratnādibhirdānaistathā pūjyā bhavanti te
|
varadānamahāyajñaistathā śakrotsavena ca||27||

28saṃpūjito maghavatā vasuścedīśvaro nṛpaḥ
|
pālayāmāsa dharmeṇa cedisthaḥ pṛthivīmimām||28||

29indrapītyā cedipatiścakārendramahaṃ vasuḥ
|
putrāścāsya mahāvīryāḥ pañcāsannamitaujasaḥ||29||

30nānārājyeṣu ca sutānsa samrāḍabhyaṣecayat
|
mahāratho māgadhānāṃ viśruto yo bṛhadrathaḥ||30||

31pratyagrahaḥ kuśāmbaśca yamāhurmaṇivāhanam
|
māvellaśca yaduścaiva rājanyaścāparājitaḥ||31||

32ete tasya sutā rājanrājarṣerbhūritejasaḥ
|
nyaveśayannāmabhiḥ svaiste deśāṃśca purāṇi ca||32||

33vāsavāḥ pañca rājānaḥ pṛthagvaṃśāśca śāśvatāḥ
|
vasantamindraprāsāde ākāśe sphāṭike ca tam||33||

34upatasthurmahātmānaṃ gandharvāpsaraso nṛpam
|
rājoparicaretyevaṃ nāma tasyātha viśrutam||34||

35puropavāhinīṃ tasya nadīṃ śuktamatīṃ giriḥ
|
arautsīccetanāyuktaḥ kāmātkolāhalaḥ kila||35||

36giriṃ kolāhalaṃ taṃ tu padā vasuratāḍayat
|
niścakrāma tatastena prahāravivareṇa sā||36||

37tasyāṃ nadyāṃ sa janayanmithunaṃ parvataḥ svayam
|
tasmādvimokṣaṇātprītā nadī rājñe nyavedayat||37||

38yaḥ pumānabhavattatra taṃ sa rājarṣisattamaḥ
|
vasurvasupradaścakre senāpatimarindamaḥ||38||

39cakāra patnīṃ kanyāṃ tu tathā tāṃ girikāṃ nṛpaḥ
|
vasoḥ patnī tu girikā kāmakālaṃ nyavedayat||39||

40ṛtukālamanuprāptā snātā puṃsavane śuciḥ
|
tadahaḥ pitaraścainamūcurjahi mṛgāniti||40||

41taṃ rājasattamaṃ prītāstadā matimatāṃ varaḥ
|
sa pitṝṇāṃ niyogena tamanatikramya pārthivaḥ||41||

42cakāra mṛgayāṃ kāmī girikāmeva saṃsmaran
|
atīvarūpasaṃpannāṃ sākṣācchriyamivāparām||42||

43aśokaiścampakaiścūtairanekairatimuktakaiḥ
|
punnāgaiḥ karṇikāraiśca bakulairdivyapāṭalaiḥ||43||

44pāṭasairnārikelaiśca candanaiścārjunaistathā
|
etai ramyairmahāvṛkṣaiḥ puṇyaiḥ svāduphalairyutam||44||

45kokilākulasannādaṃ mattabhramaranāditam
|
vasantakāle tattasya vanaṃ caitrarathopamam||45||

46mathābhiparītātmā nāpaśyadgirikāṃ tadā
|
apaśyankāmasaṃtaptaścaramāṇo yadṛcchayā||46||

47puṣpasaṃchannaśākhāgraṃ pallavairupaśobhitam
|
aśokaṃ stabakaiśchannaṃ ramaṇīyamapaśyata||47||

48adhastāttasya cchāyāyāṃ sukhāsīno narādhipaḥ
|
madhugandhaśca saṃyuktaṃ puṣpagandhamanoharam||48||

49vāyunā preryamāṇastu dhūmrāya mudamanvagāt
|
tasya retaḥ pracaskanda carato gahane vane||49||

50skannamātraṃ ca tadreto vṛkṣapatreṇa bhūmipaḥ
|
pratijagrāha mithyā me na patedreta ityuta||50||

51idaṃ mithyā pariskannaṃ reto me na bhavediti
|
ṛtuśca tasyāḥ patnyā me na moghaḥ syāditi prabhuḥ ||51||

52saṃcintyaivaṃ tadā rājā vicārya ca punaḥpunaḥ
|
amoghatvaṃ ca vijñāya retaso rājasattamaḥ||52||

53śukraprasthāpane kālaṃ mahiṣyā prasamīkṣya vai
|
abhimantryātha tacchukramārāttiṣṭhantamāśugam||53||

54sūkṣmadharmārthatattvajño gatvā śyenaṃ tato'bravīt
|
matpriyārthamidaṃ saumya śukraṃ mama gṛhaṃ naya||54||

55girikāyāḥ prayacchāśu tasyā hyārtavamadya vai
|
gṛhītvā tattadā śyenastūrṇamutpatya vegavān||55||

56javaṃ paramamāsthāya pradudrāva vihaṃgamaḥ
|
tamapaśyadathāyāntaṃ śyenaṃ śyenastathā'paraḥ||56||

57abhyadravacca taṃ sadyo dṛṣṭvaivāmiṣaśaṅkayā
|
tuṇḍayuddhamathākāśe tāvubhau saṃpracakratuḥ||57||

58yuddhyatorapatadretastaccāpi yamunāmbhasi
|
tatrādriketi vikhyātā brahmaśāpādvarāpsarā||58||

59mīnabhāvamanuprāptā babhūva yamunācarī
|
śyenapādaparibhraṣṭaṃ tadvīryamatha vāsavam||59||

60jagrāha tarasopetya sā'drikā matsyarūpiṇī
|
kadācidapi matsīṃ tāṃ babandhurmatsyajīvinaḥ||60||

61māse ca daśame prāpte tadā bharatasattama
|
ujjahrurudarāttasyāḥ strīṃ pumāṃsaṃ ca mānuṣam||61||

62āścaryabhūtaṃ tadgatvā rājñe'tha pratyavedayan
|
kāye matsyā imau rājansaṃbhūtau mānuṣāviti||62||

63tayoḥ pumāṃsaṃ jagrāha rājoparicarastadā
|
sa matsyo nāma rājāsīddhārmikaḥ satyasaṅgaraḥ||63||

64sā'psarā muktaśāpā ca kṣaṇena samapadyata
|
yā puroktā bhagavatā tiryagyonigatā śubhā||64||

65mānuṣau janayitvā tvaṃ śāpamokṣamavāpsyasi
|
tataḥ sā janayitvā tau viśastā matsyaghātibhiḥ||65||

66saṃtyajya matsyarūpaṃ sā divyaṃ rūpamavāpya ca
|
siddharṣicāraṇapathaṃ jagāmātha varāpsarāḥ||66||

67sā kanyā duhitā tasyā matsyā matsyasagandhinī
|
rājñā dattā ca dāśāya kanyeyaṃ te bhavatviti||67||

68rūpasatvasamāyuktā sarvaiḥ samuditā guṇaiḥ
|
sā tu satyavatī nāma matsyaghātyabhisaṃśrayāt||68||

69āsītsā matsyagandhaiva kaṃcitkālaṃ śucismitā
|
śuśrūṣārthaṃ piturnāvaṃ vāhayantīṃ jale ca tām||69||

70tīrthayātrāṃ parikrāmannapaśyadvai parāśaraḥ
|
atīvarūpasaṃpannāṃ siddhānāmapi kāṅkṣitām||70||

71dṛṣṭvaiva sa ca tāṃ dhīmāṃścakame cāruhāsinīm
|
divyāṃ tāṃ vāsavīṃ kanyāṃ rambhorūṃ munipuṅgavaḥ||71||

72saṃgamaṃ mama kalyāṇi kuruṣvetyabhyabhāṣata
|
sābravītpaśya bhagavanparapāre sthitānṛṣīn||72||

73āvayordṛṣṭayorebhiḥ kathaṃ nu syātsamāgamaḥ
|
evaṃ tayokto bhagavānnīhāramasṛjatprabhuḥ||73||

74yena deśaḥ sa sarvastu tamobhūta ivābhavat
|
dṛṣṭvā sṛṣṭaṃ tu nīhāraṃ tatastaṃ paramarṣiṇā||74||

75vismitā sābhavatkanyā vrīḍitā ca tapasvinī
|
satyavatyuvāca
|
viddhi māṃ bhagavankanyāṃ sadā pitṛvaśānugām||75||

76tvatsaṃyogācca duṣyeta kanyābhāvo mamā'nagha
|
kanyātve dūṣite vāpi kathaṃ śakṣye dvijottama||76||

77gṛhaṃ gantumṛṣe cāhaṃ dhīmanna sthātumutsahe
|
etatsaṃcintya bhagavanvidhatsva yadanantaram||77||

78evamuktavatīṃ tāṃ tu prītimānṛṣisattamaḥ
|
uvāca matpriyaṃ kṛtvā kanyaiva tvaṃ bhaviṣyati||78||

79vṛṇīṣva ca varaṃ bhīru yaṃ tvamicchasi bhāmini
|
vṛthā hi na prasādo me bhūtapūrvaḥ śucismite||79||

80evamuktā varaṃ vavre gātrasaugandhyamuttamam
|
sa cāsyai bhagavānprādānmanaḥ kāṅkṣitaṃ bhuvi||80||

81tato labdhavarā prītā strībhāvaguṇabhūṣitā
|
jagāma saha saṃsargamṛṣiṇā'dbhutakarmaṇā||81||

82tasyāstu yojanādgandhamājighranta narā bhuvi||82||

83tasyā yojanagandheti tato nāmāparaṃ smṛtam
|
iti satyavatī hṛṣṭā labdhvā varamanuttamam||83||

84parāśareṇa saṃyuktā sadyo garbhaṃ suṣāva sā
|
jajñe ca yamunādvīpe pārāśaryaḥ sa vīryavān||84||

85sa mātaramanujñāpya tapasyeva mano dadhe
|
smṛto'haṃ darśayiṣyāmi kṛtyeṣviti ca so'bravīt||85||

86evaṃ dvaipāyano jajñe satyavatyāṃ parāśarāt
|
nyasto dvīpe yadbālastasmāddvaipāyanaḥ smṛtaḥ||86||

87pādāpasāriṇaṃ dharmaṃ sa tu vidvānyuge yuge
|
āyuḥ śaktiṃ ca martyānāṃ yugāvasthāmavekṣyaca||87||

88brahmaṇo brāhmaṇānāṃ ca tathānugrahakāṅkṣayā
|
vivyāsa vedānyasmatsa tasmādvyāsa iti smṛtaḥ||88||

89vedānadhyāpayāmāsa mahābhāratapañcamān
|
sumantuṃ jaiminiṃ pailaṃ śukaṃ caiva svamātmajam||89||

90prabhurvariṣṭho varado vaiśaṃpāyanameva ca
|
saṃhitāstaiḥ pṛthaktvena bhāratasya prakāśitāḥ||90||

91tathā bhīṣmaḥ śāntanavo gaṅgāyāmamitadyutiḥ
|
vasuvīryātsamabhavanmahāvīryo mahāyaśāḥ||91||

92vaidārthavicca bhagavānṛṣirvipro mahāyaśāḥ
|
śūle protaḥ purāṇarṣiracauraścoraśaṅkayā||92||

93aṇīmāṇḍavya ityevaṃ vikhyātaḥ sa mahāyaśāḥ
|
sa dharmamāhūya purā maharṣiridamuktavān||93||

94iṣīkayā mayā bālyādviddhā hyekā śakuntikā
|
tatkilbiṣaṃ smare dharma nānyatpāpamahaṃ smare||94||

95tanme sahasramamitaṃ kasmānnehājayattapaḥ
|
garīyānbrāhmaṇavadhaḥ sarvabhūtavadhādyataḥ||95||

96tasmāttvaṃ kilbiṣī dhārma śūdrayonau janiṣyasi
|
tena śāpena dharmo'pi śūdrayonāvajāyata||96||

97vidvānvidurarūpeṇa dhārmī tanurakilbiṣī
|
saṃjayo munikalpastu jajñe sūto gavalgaṇāt||97||

98sūryācca kuntikanyāyā jajñe karṇo mahābalaḥ
|
sahajaṃ kavacaṃ bibhratkuṇḍalo dyotitānanaḥ||98||

99anugrahārthaṃ lokānāṃ viṣṇurlokanamaskṛtaḥ
|
vasudevāttu devakyāṃ prādurbhūto mahāyaśāḥ||99||

100anādinidhano devaḥ sa kartā jagataḥ prabhuḥ
|
avyaktamakṣaraṃ brahma pradhānaṃ triguṇātmakam||100||

101ātmānamavyayaṃ caiva prakṛtiṃ prabhavaṃ prabhum
|
puruṣaṃ viśvakarmāṇaṃ satvayogaṃ dhruvākṣaram||101||

102anantamacalaṃ devaṃ haṃsaṃ nārāyaṇaṃ prabhum
|
dhātāramajamavyaktaṃ yamāhuḥ paramavyayam||102||

103kaivalyaṃ nirguṇaṃ viśvamanādimajamavyayam
|
puruṣaḥ sa vibhuḥ kartā sarvabhūtapitāmahaḥ||103||

104dharmasaṃvardhanārthāya prajajñe'ndhakavṛṣṇiṣu
|
astrajñau tu mahāvīryau sarvaśāstraviśāradau||104||

105sātyakiḥ kṛtavarmā ca nārāyaṇamanuvratau
|
satyakāddhṛdikāccaiva jajñāte'stra viśāradau||105||

106bharadvājasya ca skannaṃ droṇyāṃ śukramavardhata
|
maharṣerugratapasastasmāddroṇo vyajāyata||106||

107gautamānmithunaṃ jajñe śarastambāccharadvataḥ
|
aśvatthāmnaśca jananī kṛpaścaiva mahābalaḥ||107||

108aśvatthāmā tato jajñe droṇādeva mahābalaḥ
|
tathaiva dhṛṣṭadyumno'pi sākṣādagnisamadyutiḥ||108||

109vaitāne karmaṇi tate pāvakātsamajāyata
|
vīro droṇavināśāya dhanurādāya vīryavān||109||

110tathaiva vedyāṃ kṛṣṇāpi jajñe tejasvinī śubhā
|
vibhrājamānā vapuṣā bibhratī rūpamuttamam||110||

111prahrādaśiṣyo nagnajitsubalaścābhavattataḥ
|
tasya prajā dharmahantrī jajñe devaprakopanāt||111||

112gāndhārarājaputro'bhūcchakuniḥ saubalastathā
|
duryodhanasya jananī jajñāte'rthaviśāradau||112||

113kṛṣṇadvaipāyanājjajñe dhṛtarāṣṭro janeśvaraḥ
|
kṣetre vicitravīryasya pāṇḍuścaiva mahābalaḥ||113||

114dharmārthakuśalo dhīmānmedhāvī dhūtakalmaṣaḥ
|
viduraḥ śūdrayonau tu jajñe dvaipāyanādapi||114||

115pāṇḍostu jajñire pañca putrā devasamāḥ pṛthak
|
dvayoḥ striyorguṇajyeṣṭhasteṣāmāsīdyudhiṣṭhiraḥ||115||

116dharmādyudhiṣṭhiro jajñe mārutācca vṛkodaraḥ
|
indrāddhanaṃjayaḥ śrīmānsarvaśastrabhṛtāṃ varaḥ||116||

117jajñāte rūpasaṃpannāvaśvibhyāṃ ca yamāvapi
|
nakulaḥ sahadevaśca guruśuśrūṣaṇe ratau||117||

118tathā putraśataṃ jajñe dhṛtarāṣṭrasya dhīmataḥ
|
duryodhanaprabhṛtayo yuyutsuḥ karaṇastathā||118||

119tato duḥśāsanaścaiva duḥsahaścāpi bhārata
|
durmarṣaṇo vikarṇaśca citraseno viviṃśatiḥ||119||

120jayaḥ satyavrataścaiva purumitraśca bhārata
|
vaiśyāputro yuyutsuśca ekādaśa mahārathāḥ||120||

121abhimanyuḥ subhadrāyāmarjunādabhyajāyata
|
svasrīyo vāsudevasya pautraḥ pāṇḍormahātmanaḥ||121||

122pāṇḍavebhyo hi pāñcālyāṃ draupadyāṃ pañca jajñire
|
kumārā rūpasaṃpannāḥ sarvaśāstraviśāradāḥ||122||

123prativindhyo yudhiṣṭhirātsutasomo vṛkodarāt
|
arjunācchrutakīrtistu śatānīkastu nākuliḥ||123||

124tathaiva sahadevācca śrutasenaḥ pratāpavān
|
hiḍimbāyāṃ ca bhīmena vane jajñe ghaṭotkacaḥ||124||

125śikhaṇḍī drupadājjajñe kanyā putratvamāgatā
|
yāṃ yakṣaḥ puruṣaṃ cakre sthūṇaḥ priyacikīrṣayā||125||

126kurūṇāṃ vigrahe tasminsamāgacchanbahūnyathā
|
rājñāṃ śatasahasrāṇi yotsyamānāni saṃyuge||126||

127teṣāmaparimeyānāṃ nāmadheyāni sarvaśaḥ
|
na śakyāni samākhyātuṃ varṣāṇāmayutairapi
|
ete tu kīrtitā mukhyā yairākhyānamidaṃ tatam||127||
