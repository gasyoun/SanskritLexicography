Mahābhārata 12.232 (Śāntiparvan)

1vyāsa uvāca |
brahma tejomayaṃ śukraṃ yasya sarvamidaṃ jagat
|
ekasya brahmabhūtasya dvayaṃ sthāvarajaṅgamam ||1||

2aharmukhe vivuddhaḥ san sṛjate'vidyayā jagat
|
agra eva mahadbhūtamāśu vyaktātmakaṃ manaḥ ||2||

3abhibhūyeha cārciṣmadvyasṛjatsapta mānasān
|
dūragaṃ bahudhāgāmi prārthanāsaṃśayātmakam ||3||

4manaḥ sṛṣṭiṃ vikurute codyamānaṃ sisṛkṣayā
|
ākāśaṃ jāyate tasmāttasya śabdaṃ guṇaṃ viduḥ ||4||

5ākāśāttu vikurvāṇātsarvagandhavahaḥ śuciḥ
|
balavān jāyate vāyustasya sparśo guṇo mataḥ ||5||

6vāyorapi vikurvāṇājjyotirbhavati bhāsvaram
|
rociṣṇu jāyate śukraṃ tadrūpaguṇamucyate ||6||

7jyotiṣo'pi vikurvāṇādbhavantyāpo rasātmikāḥ
|
adbhyo gandhavahā bhūmiḥ sarveṣāṃ sṛṣṭirucyate ||7||

8guṇāḥ sarvasya pūrvasya prāpnuvantyuttarottaram
|
teṣāṃ yāvadyathā yacca tattattāvadguṇaṃ smṛtam ||8||

9upalabhyāpsu cedgandhaṃ kecidbrūyuranaipuṇāt
|
pṛthivyāmeva taṃ vidyādapāṃ vāyośca saṃśritam ||9||

10ete saptavidhātmāno nānāvīryāḥ pṛthak pṛthak
|
nāśaknuvanprajāḥ sraṣṭumasamāgamya kṛtsnaśaḥ ||10||

11te sametya mahātmāno hyanyonyamabhisaṃśritāḥ
|
śarīrāśrayaṇaṃ prāptāstataḥ puruṣa ucyate ||11||

12śarīraṃ śrayaṇādbhavati mūrtimat ṣoḍaśātmakam
|
tamāviśanti bhūtāni mahānti saha karmaṇā ||12||

13sarvabhūtānyupādāya tapasaścaraṇāya hi
|
ādikartā sa bhūtānāṃ tamevāhuḥ prajāpatim ||13||

14sa vai sṛjati bhūtāni sthāvarāṇi carāṇi ca
|
tataḥ sa sṛjati brahmā devarṣipitṛmānavān ||14||

15lokānnadīḥ samudrāṃśca diśaḥ śailānvanaspatīn
|
narakinnararakṣāṃsi vayaḥpaśumṛgoragān
|
avyayaṃ ca vyayaṃ caiva dvayaṃ sthāvarajaṅgamam ||15||

16teṣāṃ ye yāni karmāṇi prāksṛṣṭyāṃ pratipedire
|
tānyeva pratipādyante sṛjyamānāḥ punaḥ punaḥ ||16||

17hiṃsrāhiṃsre mṛdukrūre dharmādharmāvṛtānṛte
|
tadbhāvitāḥ prapadyante tasmāttattasya rocate ||17||

18mahābhūteṣu nānātvamindriyārtheṣu mūrtiṣu
|
viniyogaṃ ca bhūtānāṃ dhātaiva vidadhātyuta ||18||

19kecitpuruṣakāraṃ tu prāhuḥ karmasu mānavāḥ
|
daivamityapare viprāḥ svabhāvaṃ bhūtacintakāḥ ||19||

20pauruṣaṃ karma daivaṃ ca phalavṛttisvabhāvataḥ
|
traya ete'pṛthagbhūtā na vivekaṃ tu kecana ||20||

21etameva ca naivaṃ ca na cobhe nānubhe na ca
|
karmasthā viṣayaṃ brūyuḥ sattvasthāḥ samadarśinaḥ ||21||

22tapo niḥśreyasaṃ jantostasya mūlaṃ śamo damaḥ
|
tena sarvānavāpnoti yānkāmānmanasecchati ||22||

23tapasā tadavāpnoti yadbhūtaṃ sṛjate jagat
|
sa tadbhūtaśca sarveṣāṃ bhūtānāṃ bhavati prabhuḥ ||23||

24ṛṣayastapasā vedānadhyaiṣanta divāniśam
|
anādinidhanā vidyā vāgutsṛṣṭā svayambhuvā ||24||

25ṛṣīṇā nāmadheyāni yāśca vedeṣu sṛṣṭayaḥ
|
nānārūpaṃ ca bhūtānāṃ karmaṇāṃ ca pravartanam ||25||

26vedaśabdebhya evādau nirmimīte sa īśvaraḥ
|
nāmadheyāni carṣīṇāṃ yāśca vedeṣu sṛṣṭayaḥ ||26||

27śarvaryante sujātānāmānyebhyo vidadhātyajaḥ
|
nāmabhedatapaḥkarmayajñākhyā lokasiddhayaḥ ||27||

28ātmasiddhistu vedeṣu procyate daśabhiḥ kramaiḥ
|
yaduktaṃ vedavādeṣu gahanaṃ vedadarśibhiḥ
|
tadanteṣu yathāyuktaṃ kramayogena lakṣyate ||28||

29karmajo'yaṃ pṛthagbhāvo dvandvayukto'pi dehinaḥ
|
tamātmasiddhirvijñānājjahāti puruṣo balāt ||29||

30dve brahmaṇī veditavye śabdabrahma paraṃ ca yat
|
śabdabrahmaṇi niṣṇātaḥ paraṃ brahmādhigacchati ||30||

31ālambhayajñāḥ kṣatrāśca haviryajñā viśaḥ smṛtāḥ
|
paricārayajñāḥ śūdrāstu tapoyajñā dvijātayaḥ ||31||

32tretāyuge vidhistveṣa yajñānāṃ na kṛte yuge
|
dvāpare viplavaṃ yānti yajñāḥ kaliyuge tathā ||32||

33apṛthagdharmiṇo martyā ṛksāmāni yajūṃṣi ca
|
kāmyā iṣṭīḥ pṛthag dṛṣṭvā tapobhistapa eva ca ||33||

34tretāyāṃ tu samastā samastā ye prādurāsanmahābalāḥ
|
saṃyantāraḥ sthāvarāṇāṃ jaṅgamānāṃ ca sarvaśaḥ ||34||

35tretāyāṃ saṃhatā vedā yajñā varṇāśramāstathā
|
saṃrodhādāyuṣastvete bhraśyante dvāpare yuge ||35||

36dṛśyante na ca dṛśyante vedāḥ kaliyuge'khilāḥ
|
utsīdante sayajñāśca kevalā dharmapīḍitāḥ ||36||

37kṛte yuge yastu dharmo brāhmaṇeṣu pradṛśyate
|
ātmavatsu tapovatsu śrutavatsu pratiṣṭhitaḥ ||37||

38sadharmavratasaṃyogaṃ yathādharmaṃ yuge yuge
|
vikriyante svadharmasthā vedavādā yathāgamam ||38||

39yathā viśvāni bhūtāni vṛṣṭyā bhūyāṃsi prāvṛṣi
|
sṛjyante jaṅgamasthāni tathā dharmā yuge yuge ||39||

40yathartuṣvṛtuliṅgāni nānārūpāṇi paryaye
|
dṛśyante tāni tānyeva tathā brahmaharādiṣu ||40||

41vihitaṃ kālanānātvamanādinidhanaṃ tathā
|
kīrtitaṃ tatpurastātte tatsūte cātti ca prajāḥ ||41||

42dadhāti bhavati sthānaṃ bhūtānāṃ samayo matam
|
svabhāvenaiva vartante dvandvayuktāni bhūriśaḥ ||42||

43sargakālakriyā vedāḥ kartā kāryaṃ kriyāphalam
|
proktaṃ te putra sarvaṃ vai yanmāṃ tvaṃ paripṛcchasi ||43||
