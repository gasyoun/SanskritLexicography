Mahābhārata 7.120 (Droṇaparvan)

1sañjaya uvāca|
jitvā yavanakāmbojānyuyudhānastato'rjunam
|
jagāma tava sainyasya madhyena rathināṃ varaḥ||1||

2cārudaṃṣṭro naravyāghro vicitrakavacadhvajaḥ
|
mṛgaṃ vyāghra ivājighraṃstava sainyamabhīṣayat ||2||

3sa rathena caranmārgān dhanurabhrāmayadbhṛśam
|
rukmapṛṣṭhaṃ mahāvegaṃ rukmacandrakasaṃkulam ||3||

4rukmāṅgadaśirastrāṇo rukmavarmasamāvṛtaḥ
|
rukmadhvajadhanuḥ śūro meruśṛṅgamivābabhau||4||

5sadhanurmaṇḍalaḥ saṃkhye tejobhāskararaśmivān
|
śaradīvoditaḥ sūryo nṛsūryo virarāja ha ||5||

6vṛṣabhaskandhavikrānto vṛṣabhākṣo nararṣabhaḥ
|
tāvakānāṃ babhau madhye gavāṃ madhye yathā vṛṣaḥ||6||

7mattadviradasaṃkāśaṃ mattadvirada‌gāminam
|
prabhinnamiva mātaṅgaṃ yūthamadhye vyavasthitam ||7||

8vyāghrā iva jighāṃsantastvadīyāḥ samupādravan
|
droṇānīkamatikrāntaṃ bhojānīkaṃ ca dustaram||8||

9jalasandhārṇavaṃ tīrtvā kāmbojānāṃ ca vāhinīm
|
hārdikyamakarānmuktaṃ tīrṇaṃ vai sainyasāgaram ||9||

10parivavruḥ susaṃkruddhāstvadīyāḥ sātyakiṃ rathāḥ
|
duryodhanaścitraseno duḥśāsanaviviṃśatī||10||

11śakunirduḥsahaścaiva yuvā durdharṣaṇaḥ krathaḥ
|
anye ca bahavaḥ śūrāḥ śastravanto durāsadāḥ||11||

12pṛṣṭhataḥ sātyakiṃ yāntamanvadhāvannamarṣiṇaḥ
|
atha śabdo mahānāsīttava sainyasya māriṣa||12||

13mārutoddhūtavegasya sāgarasyeva parvaṇi
|
tānabhidravataḥ sarvān samīkṣya śinipuṅgavaḥ||13||

14śanairyāhīti yantāramabravīt prahasanniva
|
idametatsamudbhūtaṃ dhārtarāṣṭrasya yadbalam||14||

15māmevābhimukhaṃ tūrṇaṃ gajāśvarathapattimat
|
nādayan vai diśaḥ sarvā rathaghoṣeṇa sārathe||15||

16pṛthivīṃ cāntarikṣaṃ ca kampayan sāgarānapi
|
etadbalārṇavaṃ sūta vārayiṣye mahāraṇe||16||

17paurṇamāsyāmivoddhūtaṃ veleva makarālayam
|
paśya me sūta vikrāntamindrasyeva mahāmṛdhe||17||

18eṣa sainyāni śatrūṇāṃ vidhamāmi śitaiḥ śaraiḥ
|
nihatānāhave paśya padātyaśvarathadvipān ||18||

19maccharairagnisaṃkāśairviddhadehān sahasraśaḥ
|
ityevaṃ bruvatastasya sātyakeramitaujasaḥ||19||

20samīpe sainikāste tu śīghramīyuryuyutsavaḥ
|
jahyādravasva tiṣṭheti paśya paśyeti vādinaḥ||20||

21tānevaṃ bruvato vīrān sātyakirniśitaiḥ śaraiḥ
|
jaghāna triśatānaśvān kuñjarāṃśca catuḥśatān||21||

22sa saṃprahārastumulastasya teṣāṃ ca dhanvinām
|
devāsuraraṇaprakhyaḥ prāvartata janakṣayaḥ ||22||

23meghajālanibhaṃ sainyaṃ tava putrasya māriṣa
|
pratyagṛhṇācchineḥ pautraḥ śarairāśīviṣopamaiḥ||23||

24pracchādyamānaḥ samare śarajālaiḥ sa vīryavān
|
asaṃbhraman mahārāja tāvakānavadhīdbahūn||24||

25āścaryaṃ tatra rājendra sumahaddṛṣṭavānaham
|
na moghaḥ sāyakaḥ kaścitsātyakerabhavatprabho||25||

26rathanāgāśvakalilaḥ padātyūrmisamākulaḥ
|
śaineyavelāmāsādya sthitaḥ sainyamahārṇavaḥ||26||

27saṃbhrāntanaranāgāśvamāvartata muhurmuhuḥ
|
tat sainyamiṣubhistena vadhyamānaṃ samantataḥ||27||

28babhrāma tatra tatraiva gāvaḥ śītārditā iva
|
padātinaṃ rathaṃ nāgaṃ sādinaṃ turagaṃ tathā ||28||

29aviddhaṃ tatra nādrākṣaṃ yuyudhānasya sāyakaiḥ
|
na tādṛkkadanaṃ rājan kṛtavāṃstatra phālgunaḥ||29||

30yādṛk kṣayamanīkānāmakarot sātyakirnṛpa
|
atyarjunaṃ śineḥpautro yudhyate puruṣarṣabhaḥ||30||

31vītabhīrlāghavopetaḥ kṛtitvaṃ saṃpradarśayan
|
tato duryodhano rājā sātvatasya tribhiḥ śaraiḥ||31||

32vivyādha sūtaṃ niśitaiścaturbhiścaturo hayān
|
sātyakiṃ ca tribhirviddhvā punaraṣṭābhireva ca||32||

33duḥśāsanaḥ ṣoḍaśabhirvivyādha śinipuṅgavam
|
śakuniḥ pañcaviṃśatyā citrasenaśca pañcabhiḥ||33||

34duḥsahaḥ pañcadaśabhirvivyādhorasi sātyakim
|
utsmayan vṛṣṇiśārdūlastathā bāṇaiḥ samāhataḥ||34||

35tānavidhyanmahārāja sarvāneva tribhistribhiḥ
|
gāḍhaviddhānarīnkṛtvā mārgaṇaiḥ so'titejanaiḥ||35||

36śaineyaḥ śyenavatsaṃkhye vyacarallaghuvikramaḥ
|
saubalasya dhanuśchitvā hastāvāpaṃ nikṛtya ca||36||

37duryodhanaṃ tribhirbāṇairabhyavidhyat stanāntare
|
citrasenaṃ śatenaiva daśabhirduḥsahaṃ tathā ||37||

38duḥśāsanaṃ tu viṃśatyā vivyādha śinipuṅgavaḥ
|
athānyaddhanurādāya śyālastava viśāṃpate||38||

39aṣṭābhiḥ sātyakiṃ viddhvā punarvivyādha pañcabhiḥ
|
duḥśāsanaśca daśabhirduḥsahaśca tribhiḥ śaraiḥ||39||

40durmukhaśca dvādaśabhī rājan vivyādha sātyakim
|
duryodhanastrisaptatyā viddhvā bhārata mādhavam||40||

41tato'sya niśitairbāṇaistribhirvivyādha sārathim
|
tānsarvānsahitāñśūrānyatamānānmahārathān ||41||

42pañcabhiḥ pañcabhirbāṇaiḥ punarvivyādha sātyakiḥ
|
tataḥ sa rathināṃ śreṣṭhastava putrasya sārathim ||42||

43ājaghānāśu bhallena sa hato nyapatadbhuvi
|
patite sārathau tasmiṃstava putrarathaḥ prabho ||43||

44vātāyamānaistairaśvairapānīyata sagarāt
|
tatastava suto rājan sainikāśca viśāṃpate ||44||

45rājño rathamabhiprekṣya vidrutāḥ śataśo'bhavan
|
vidrutaṃ tatra tat sainyaṃ dṛṣṭvā bhārata sātyakiḥ||45||

46avākiraccharaistīkṣṇai rukmapuṃkhaiḥ śilāśitaiḥ
|
vidrāvya sarvasainyāni tāvakāni sahasraśaḥ||46||

47prayayau sātyakī rājañśvetāśvasya rathaṃ prati
|
taṃ śarānādadānaṃ ca rakṣamāṇaṃ ca sārathim
|
ātmānaṃ pālayānaṃ ca tāvakāḥ samapūjayan||47||
