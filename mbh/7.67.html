Mahābhārata 7.67 (Droṇaparvan)

1nārada uvāca| sāṅkṛtiṃ rantidevaṃ ca mṛtaṃ sṛñjaya śuśruma
|
yasya dviśatasāhasrā āsan sūdā mahātmanaḥ ||1||

2gṛhānabhyāgatān viprānatithīn pariveṣakāḥ
|
paddāpakvaṃ divārātraṃ varānnamamṛtopamam ||2||

3nyāyenādhigataṃ vittaṃ brāhmaṇebhyo hyamanyata
|
vedānadhītya dharmeṇa yaścakre dviṣato vaśe ||3||

4upasthitāśca paśavaḥ svayaṃ yaṃ śaṃsitavratam
|
bahavaḥ svargamicchanto vidhivat satrayājinam ||4||

5nadī mahānasādyasya pravṛttā carmarāśitaḥ
|
tasmāccarmaṇvatī pūrvamagnihotre'bhavat purā ||5||

6brāhmaṇebhyo'dadanniṣkānsauvarṇānsa prabhāvataḥ
|
tubhyaṃ niṣkaṃ tubhyaṃ niṣkamiti ha sma prabhāṣate ||6||

7tubhyaṃ tubhyamiti prādānniṣkānniṣkān sahasraśaḥ
|
tataḥ punaḥ samāśvāsya niṣkāneva prayacchati ||7||

8alpaṃ dattaṃ mayā'dyeti niṣkakoṭiṃ sahasraśaḥ
|
ekāhnā dāsyati punaḥ ko'nyastat sapradāsyati ||8||

9dvijapāṇiviyogena duḥkhaṃ me śāśvataṃ mahat
|
bhaviṣyati na sandeha evaṃ rājā'dadadvasu ||9||

10sahasraśaśca sauvarṇān vṛṣabhān gośatānugān
|
sāṣṭaṃ śataṃ suvarṇānāṃ niṣkamāhurdhanaṃ tathā ||10||

11adhyardhamāsamadadadbrāhmaṇebhyaḥ śataṃ samāḥ
|
agnihotropakaraṇaṃ yajñopakaraṇaṃ ca yat ||11||

12ṛṣibhyaḥ karakān kumbhān sthālīḥ piṭharameva ca
|
śayanāsanayānāni prāsādāṃśca gṛhāṇi ca ||12||

13vṛkṣāṃśca vividhān dadyādannāni ca dhanāni ca
|
sarvaṃ sauvarṇamevāsīdra‌ntidevasya dhīmataḥ ||13||

14tatrāsya gāthā gāyanti ye purāṇavido janāḥ
|
rantidevasya tāṃ dṛṣṭvā samṛddhimatimānuṣīm ||14||

15naitādṛśaṃ dṛṣṭapūrvaṃ kuberasadaneṣvapi
|
dhanaṃ ca pūryamāṇaṃ naḥ kiṃ punarmanujeṣviti ||15||

16vyaktaṃ vasvokasāreyamityūcustatra vismitāḥ
|
sāṅkṛte rantidevasya yāṃ rātrimatithirvaset ||16||

17ālabhyanta tadā gāvaḥ sahasrāṇyekaviṃśatiḥ
|
tatra sma sūdāḥ krośanti supṛṣṭamaṇikuṇḍalāḥ ||17||

18sūpaṃ bhūyiṣṭhamaśnīdhvaṃ nādya māṃsaṃ yathā purā
|
rantidevasya yatkiñcit sauvarṇamabhavat tadā ||18||

19tat sarvaṃ vitate yajñe brāhmaṇebhyo hyamanyata
|
pratyakṣaṃ tasya havyāni pratigṛhṇanti devatāḥ ||19||

20kavyāni pitaraḥ kāle sarvakāmān dvijottamāḥ
|
sa cenmamāra sṛñjaya caturbhadratarastvayā ||20||

21putrāt puṇyatarastubhyaṃ mā putramanutapyathāḥ
|
ayajvānamadākṣiṇyamabhiśvaityeti vyāharan ||21||
