Mahābhārata 5.157 (Udyogaparvan)

1janamejaya uvāca|
āpageyaṃ mahātmānaṃ bhīṣmaṃ śastrabhṛtāṃ varam
|
pitāmahaṃ bhāratānāṃ dhvajaṃ sarvamahīkṣitām ||1||

2bṛhaspatisamaṃ buddhyā kṣamayā pṛthivīsamam
|
samudra‌miva gāṃbhīrye himavantamiva sthiram ||2||

3prajāpatimivaudārye tejasā bhāskaropamam
|
mahendramiva śatrūṇāṃ dhvaṃsanaṃ śaravṛṣṭibhiḥ ||3||

4raṇayajñe pravitate subhīme lomaharṣaṇe
|
dīkṣitaṃ cirarātrāya śrutvā tatra yudhiṣṭhiraḥ ||4||

5kimabravīnmahābāhuḥ sarvaśastrabhṛtāṃ varaḥ
|
bhīmasenārjunau vā'pi kṛṣṇo vā pratyabhāṣata ||5||

6vaiśampāyana uvāca|
āpaddharmārthakuśalo mahābuddhiryudhiṣṭhiraḥ
|
sarvān bhrātṝn samānīya vāsudevaṃ ca śāśvatam ||6||

7uvāca vadatāṃ śreṣṭhaḥ sāntvapūrvamidaṃ vacaḥ
|
paryākrāmata sainyāni yattāstiṣṭhata daṃśitāḥ ||7||

8pitāmahena vo yuddhaṃ pūrvameva bhaviṣyati
|
tasmāt saptasu senāsu praṇetṝn mama paśyata ||8||

9kṛṣṇa uvāca|
yathā'rhati bhavān vaktumasmin kāle hyupasthite
|
tathedamarthavadvākyamuktaṃ te bharatarṣabha ||9||

10rocate me mahābāho kriyatāṃ yadanantaram
|
nāyakāstava senāyāṃ kriyantāmiha sapta vai ||10||

11vaiśampāyana uvāca|
tato drupadamānāyya virāṭaṃ śinipuṅgavam
|
dhṛṣṭadyumnaṃ ca pāñcālyaṃ dhṛṣṭaketuṃ ca pārthiva ||11||

12śikhaṇḍinaṃ ca pāñcālyaṃ sahadevaṃ ca māgadham
|
etān sapta mahābhāgān vīrān yuddhābhikāṃkṣiṇaḥ ||12||

13senāpraṇetṝn vidhivadabhyaṣiñcadyudhiṣṭhiraḥ
|
sarvasenāpatiṃ cātra dhṛṣṭadyumnaṃ cakāra ha ||13||

14droṇāntahetorutpanno ya iddhājjātavedasaḥ
|
sarveṣāmeva teṣāṃ tu samastānāṃ mahātmanām ||14||

15senāpatipatiṃ cakre guḍākeśaṃ dhanañjayam
|
arjunasyāpi netā ca saṃyantā caiva vājinām ||15||

16saṅkarṣaṇānujaḥ śrīmānmahābuddhirjanārdanaḥ
|
taddṛṣṭvopasthitaṃ yuddhaṃ samāsannaṃ mahātyayam ||16||

17prāviśadbhavanaṃ rājan pāṇḍavānāṃ halāyudhaḥ
|
sahākrūraprabhṛtibhirgadasāmboddhavādibhiḥ ||17||

18raukmiṇeyāhukasutaiścārudeṣṇapurogamaiḥ
|
vṛṣṇimukhyairadhigatairvyāghrairiva balotkaṭaiḥ ||18||

19abhigupto mahābāhurmarudbhiriva vāsavaḥ
|
nīlakauśeyavasanaḥ kailāsaśikharopamaḥ ||19||

20siṃhakhelagatiḥ śrīmān madaraktāntalocanaḥ
|
taṃ dṛṣṭvā dharmarājaśca keśavaśca mahādyutiḥ ||20||

21uda‌tiṣṭhattataḥ pārtho bhīmakarmā vṛkodaraḥ
|
gāṇḍīvadhanvā ye cānye rājānastatra kecana ||21||

22pūjayāñcakrire te vai samāyāntaṃ halāyudham
|
tatastaṃ pāṇḍavo rājā kare pasparśa pāṇinā ||22||

23vāsudevapurogāstaṃ sarva evābhyavādayan
|
virāṭadrupadau vṛddhāvabhivādya halāyudhaḥ ||23||

24yudhiṣṭhireṇa sahita upāviśadarindamaḥ
|
tatasteṣūpaviṣṭeṣu pārthiveṣu samantataḥ
|
vāsudevamabhiprekṣya rauhiṇeyo'bhyabhāṣata ||24||

25bhavitā'yaṃ mahāraudro dāruṇaḥ puruṣakṣayaḥ
|
diṣṭametatdhruvaṃ manye na śakyamativartitum ||25||

26tasmādyuddhāt samuttīrṇānapi vaḥ sasuhṛjjanān
|
arogānakṣartairdehairdraṣṭā'smīti matirmama ||26||

27sametaṃ pārthivaṃ kṣatraṃ kālapakvamasaṃśayam
|
vimardaśca mahān bhāvī māṃsaśoṇitakardamaḥ ||27||

28ukto mayā vāsudevaḥ punaḥ punarupahvare
|
sambandhiṣu samāṃ vṛttiṃ vartasva madhusūdana ||28||

29pāṇḍavā hi yathā'smākaṃ tathā duryodhano nṛpaḥ
|
tasyāpi kriyatāṃ sāhyaṃ sa paryeti punaḥpunaḥ ||29||

30tacca me nākarodvākyaṃ tvadarthe madhusūdanaḥ
|
nirviṣṭaḥ sarvabhāvena dhanañjayamavekṣya ha ||30||

31dhruvo jayaḥ pāṇḍavānāmiti me niścitā matiḥ
|
tathā hyabhiniveśo'yaṃ vāsudevasya bhārata ||31||

32na cāhamutsahe kṛṣṇamṛte lokamudīkṣitum
|
tato'hamanuvartāmi keśavasya cikīrṣitam ||32||

33ubhau śiṣyau hi me vīrau gadāyuddhaviśāradau
|
tulyasneho'smyato bhīme tathā duryodhane nṛpe ||33||

34tasmādyāsyāmi tīrthāni sarasvatyā niṣevitum
|
na hi śakṣyāmi kauravyānnaśyamānānupekṣitum ||34||

35evamuktvā mahābāhuranujñātaśca pāṇḍavaiḥ
|
tīrthayātrāṃ yayau rāmo nirvartya madhusūdanam ||35||
