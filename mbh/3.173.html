Mahābhārata 3.173 (Āraṇyakaparvan)

1arjuna uvāca|
nivartamānena mayā mahaddṛṣṭaṃ tato'param
|
puraṃ kāmacaraṃ divyaṃ pāvakārkasamaprabham||1||

2ratnadrumamayaiścitraiḥ susvaraiśca patattribhiḥ
|
paulomaiḥ kālakañjaiśca nityahṛṣṭairadhiṣṭhitam||2||

3gopurāṭṭālakopetaṃ caturdvāraṃ durāsadam
|
sarvaratnamayaṃ divyamadbhutopamadarśanam||3||

4drumaiḥ puṣpaphalopetaiḥ sarvaratnamayairvṛtam
|
tathā patattribhirdivyairupetaṃ sumanoharaiḥ||4||

5asurairnityamuditaiḥ śūlarṣṭimusalāyudhaiḥ
|
cāpamudgarahastaiśca stragvibhiḥ sarvato vṛtam||5||

6tadahaṃ prekṣya daityānāṃ puramadbhutadarśanam
|
apṛcchaṃ mātaliṃ rājan kimidaṃ vartate'dbhutam||6||

7mātaliruvāca|
pulomā nāma daiteyī kālakā va mahāsurī
|
divyaṃ varṣasahasraṃ te ceratuḥ paramaṃ tapaḥ||7||

8tapaso'nte tatastābhyāṃ svayambhūradadadvaram
|
agṛhṇītāṃ varaṃ te tu sutānāmalpaduḥkhatām||8||

9avadhyatāṃ ca rājendra surarākṣasapannagaiḥ
|
puraṃ suramaṇīyaṃ ca khacaraṃ sumahāprabham||9||

10sarvaratnaiḥ samuditaṃ durdharṣamamarairapi
|
maharṣi-yakṣa-gandharva-pannagāsurarākṣasaiḥ||10||

11sarvakāmaguṇopetaṃ vītaśokamanāmayam
|
brahmaṇā bharataśreṣṭha kālakeyakṛte kṛtam||11||

12tadetan khapuraṃ divyaṃ caratyamaravarjitam
|
paulomādhyuṣitaṃ vīra kālakañjaiśca dānavaiḥ||12||

13hiraṇyapuramityevaṃ khyāyate nagaraṃ mahat
|
rakṣitaṃ kālakeyaiśca paulomaiśca mahāsuraiḥ||13||

14ta ete muditā rājannavadhyāḥ sarvadaivataiḥ
|
nivasantyatra rājendra gatodvegā nirutsukāḥ||14||

15mānuṣānmṛtyureteṣāṃ nirdiṣṭo brahmaṇā purā
|
etānapi raṇe pārtha kālakañjān durāsadān
|
vajrāstreṇa nayasvāśu vināśaṃ sumahābalān||15||

16arjuna uvāca|
surāsurairavadhyaṃ tadahaṃ jñātvā viśāmpate
|
abruvaṃ mātaliṃ hṛṣṭo yāhyetat puramañjasā||16||

17tridaśeśadviṣo yāvat kṣayamastrairnayāmyaham
|
na kathañciddhi me pāpā na vadhyā ye suradviṣaḥ||17||

18uvāha māṃ tataḥ śīghraṃ hiraṇyapuramantikāt
|
rathena tena divyena hariyuktena mātaliḥ||18||

19te māmālakṣya daiteyā vicitrābharaṇāmbarāḥ
|
samutpeturmahāvegā rathānāsthāya daṃśitāḥ||19||

20tato nālīkanārācairbhallaiḥ śaktyṛṣṭitomaraiḥ
|
pratyaghnan dānavendrā māṃ kruddhāstīvraparākramāḥ||20||

21tadahaṃ śaravarṣeṇa mahatā pratyavārayam
|
śastravarṣaṃ mahadrājan vidyābalamupāśritaḥ||21||

22vyāmohayaṃ ca tān sarvān rathamārgaiścaran raṇe
|
te'nyo'nyamabhisammūḍhāḥ pātayanti sma dānavān||22||

23teṣāmevaṃ vimūḍhānāmanyo'nyamabhidhāvatām
|
śirāṃsi viśikhairdīptairnyahanaṃ śatasaṅghaśaḥ||23||

24te vadhyamānā daiteyāḥ puramāsthāya tatpunaḥ
|
khamutpetuḥ sanagarā māyāmāsthāya dānavīm||24||

25tato'haṃ śaravarṣeṇa mahatā kurunandana
|
mārgamāvṛtya daityānāṃ gatiṃ caiṣāmavārayam||25||

26tatpuraṃ khacaraṃ divyaṃ kāmagaṃ sūryasaprabham
|
daiteyairvaradānena dhāryate sma yathāsukham||26||

27antarbhūmau nipatati punarūrdhvaṃ pratiṣṭhate
|
punastiryak prayātyāśu punarapsu nimajjati||27||

28amarāvatisaṅkāśaṃ tat puraṃ kāmagaṃ mahat
|
ahamastrairbahuvidhaiḥ pratyagṛhṇaṃ parantapa||28||

29tato'haṃ śarajālena divyāstranuditena ca
|
vyagṛhṇaṃ saha daiteyaistatpuraṃ puruṣarṣabha||29||

30vikṣataṃ cāyasairbāṇairmatprayuktairajihmagaiḥ
|
mahīmabhyapatadrājan prabhagnaṃ puramāsuram||30||

31te vadhyamānā madbāṇairvajravegairayasmayaiḥ
|
paryabhramanta vai rājannasurāḥ kālacoditāḥ||31||

32tato mātalirāruhya purastānnipatanniva
|
mahīmavātarat kṣipraṃ rathenā''dityavarcasā||32||

33tato rathasahasrāṇi ṣaṣṭisteṣāmamarṣiṇām
|
yuyutsūnāṃ mayā sārdhaṃ paryavartanta bhārata
|
tānyahaṃ niśitairbāṇairvyadhamaṃ gārdharājitaiḥ||33||

34te yuddhe sannyavartanta samudrasya yathormayaḥ
|
neme śakyā mānuṣeṇa yuddheneti pracintya tat||34||

35tato'hamānupūrvyeṇa divyānyastrāṇyayojayam
|
tatastāni sahastrāṇi rathināṃ citrayodhinām||35||

36astrāṇi mama divyāni pratyaghnan śanakairiva
|
rathamārgān vicitrāṃste vicaranto mahābalāḥ||36||

37pratyadṛśyanta saṅgrāme śataśo'tha sahasraśaḥ
|
vicitramukuṭāpīḍā vicitrakavacadhvajāḥ||37||

38vicitrābharaṇāścaiva nandayantīva me manaḥ
|
ahaṃ tu śaravarṣaistānastrapracuditai raṇe||38||

39nāśaknuvaṃ pīḍayituṃ te tu māṃ pratyapīḍayan
|
taiḥ pīḍyamāno bahubhiḥ kṛtāstraiḥ kuśalairyudhi||39||

40vyathito'smi mahāyuddhe bhayaṃ cāgānmahanmama
|
tato'haṃ devadevāya rudrāya prayato raṇe||40||

41svasti bhūtebhya ityuktvā mahāstraṃ samacodayam
|
yattadraudramiti khyātaṃ sarvāmitravināśanam||41||

42tato'paśyaṃ triśirasaṃ puruṣaṃ navalocanam
|
trimukhaṃ ṣaḍbhujaṃ dīptamarkajvalanamūrdhajam||42||

43lelihānairmahānāgaiḥ kṛtacīramamitrahan
|
vibhīstatastadastraṃ tu ghoraṃ raudraṃ sanātanam||43||

44dṛṣṭvā gāṇḍīvasaṃyogamānīya bharatarṣabha
|
namaskṛtvā trinetrāya śarvāyāmitatejase||44||

45muktavān dānavendrāṇāṃ parābhāvāya bhārata
|
muktamātre tatastasmin rūpāṇyālan sahasraśaḥ||45||

46mṛgāṇāmatha siṃhānāṃ vyāghrāṇāṃ ca viśāmpate
|
ṛkṣāṇāṃ mahiṣāṇāṃ ca pannagānāṃ tathā gavām||46||

47śaramāṇāṃ gajānāṃ ca vānarāṇāṃ ca saṅghaśaḥ
|
ṛṣabhāṇāṃ varāhāṇāṃ mārjārāṇāṃ tathaiva ca||47||

48śālāvṛkāṇāṃ pretānāṃ bhuruṇḍānāṃ ca sarvaśaḥ
|
gṛdhrāṇāṃ garuḍānāṃ ca camarāṇāṃ tathaiva ca||48||

49devānāṃ ca ṛṣīṇāṃ ca gandharvāṇāṃ ca sarvaśaḥ
|
piśācānāṃ sayakṣāṇāṃ tathaiva ca suradviṣām||49||

50guhyakānāṃ ca saṅgrāme nairṛtānāṃ tathaiva ca
|
jhaṣāṇāṃ gajavaktrāṇāmulūkānāṃ tathaiva ca||50||

51mīnavājisarūpāṇāṃ nānāśastrāsipāṇinām
|
tathaiva yātudhānānāṃ gadāmudgaradhāriṇām||51||

52etaiścānyaiśca bahubhirnānārūpadharaistathā
|
sarvamāsījjagadvyāptaṃ tāsmannastre visarjite||52||

53triśirobhiścaturdaṃṣṭreścaturāsyaiścaturbhujaiḥ
|
anekarūpasaṃyuktairmāṃsamedovasāsthibhiḥ||53||

54abhīkṣṇaṃ vadhyamānāste dānavā nāśamāgatāḥ
|
arkajvalanatejobhirvajrāśanisamaprabhaiḥ||54||

55adrisāramayaiścānyairbāṇairapi nibarhaṇaiḥ
|
nyahanaṃ dānavān sarvān muhūrtenaiva bhārata||55||

56gāṇḍīvāstrapraṇunnāṃstān gatāsūnnabhasaścyutān
|
dṛṣṭvā'haṃ prāṇamaṃ bhūyastripuraghnāya vedhase||56||

57tathā raudrāstraniṣpiṣṭān divyābharaṇabhūṣitān
|
niśamya paramaṃ harṣamagamaddevasārathiḥ||57||

58tadasahyaṃ kṛtaṃ karma devairapi durāsadam
|
dṛṣṭvā māṃ pūjayāmāsa mātaliḥ śakrasārathiḥ||58||

59uvāca vacanaṃ cedaṃ prīyamāṇaḥ kṛtāñjaliḥ
|
surāsurairasahyaṃ hi karma yat sādhitaṃ tvayā||59||

60na hyetat saṃyuge kartumapi śaktaḥ sureśvaraḥ
|
surāsurairavadhyaṃ hi purametat khagaṃ mahat||60||

61tvayā vimāthataṃ vīra svavīryatapaso balāt
|
vidhvaste khapure tasmin dānaveṣu hateṣu ca||61||

62vinadantyaḥ striyaḥ sarvā niṣpeturnagarādbahiḥ
|
prakīrṇakeśyo vyathitāḥ kurarya iva duḥkhitāḥ||62||

63petuḥ putrān pitṝn bhrātṝn śocamānā mahītale
|
rudatyo dīnakaṇṭhyastu ninadantyo hateśvarāḥ||63||

64urāṃsi parinighnantyo visrastasragvibhūṣaṇāḥ
|
tacchokayuktamaśrīkaṃ duḥkhadainyasamāhatam||64||

65na babhau dānavapuraṃ hatatviṭkaṃ hateśvaram
|
gandharvanagarākāraṃ hṛtanāgamiva hṛdam||65||

66śuṣkavṛkṣamivāraṇyamadṛśyamabhavat puram
|
māṃ tu saṃhṛṣṭamanasaṃ kṣipraṃ mātalirānayat||66||

67devarājasya bhavanaṃ kṛtakarmāṇamāhavāt
|
hiraṇyapuramutsṛjya nihatya ca mahāsurān||67||

68nivātakavacāṃścaiva tato'haṃ śakramāgamam
|
mama karma ca devendraṃ mātalirvistareṇa tat||68||

69sarvaṃ viśrāvayāmāsa yathābhūtaṃ mahādyute
|
hiraṇyapuradhātaṃ ca māyānāṃ ca nivāraṇam||69||

70nivātakavacānāṃ ca vadhaṃ saṃkhye mahaujasām
|
tatśrutvara bhagavān prītaḥ sahasrākṣaḥ purandaraḥ||70||

71marudbhiḥ sahitaḥ śrīmān sādhu sādhvityathābravīt
|
tato māṃ devarājo vai samāśvāsya punaḥ punaḥ||71||

72abravīdvibudhaiḥ sārdhamidaṃ sa madhuraṃ vacaḥ
|
atidevāsuraṃ karma kṛtameva tvayā raṇe||72||

73gurvarthaśca kṛtaḥ pārtha mahāśatrūn ghnatā mama
|
evameva sadā bhāvyaṃ sthireṇājau dhanañjaya||73||

74asammūḍhena cāstrāṇāṃ kartavyaṃ pratipādanam
|
aviṣahyo raṇe hi tvaṃ devadānavarākṣasaiḥ||74||

75sayakṣāsuragandharvaiḥ sapakṣigaṇapannagaiḥ
|
vasudhāṃ cāpi kaunteya tvadbāhubalanirjitām
|
pālayiṣyati dharmātmā kuntīputro yudhiṣṭhiraḥ||75||
