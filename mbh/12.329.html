Mahābhārata 12.329 (Śāntiparvan)

1bhīṣma uvāca|
etasminnantare śūnye nāradaḥ samupāgamat
|
śukaṃ svādhyāyanirataṃ vedārthānpraṣṭumīpsayā||1||

2devarṣiṃ tu śuko dṛṣṭvā nāradaṃ samupasthitam
|
arghapūrveṇa vidhinā vedoktenābhyapūjayat||2||

3nārado'thābravītprīto brūhi dharmabhṛtāṃ vara
|
kena tvāṃ śreyasā vatsa yojayāmīti hṛṣṭavat||3||

4nāradasya vacaḥ śrutvā śukaḥ provāca bhārata
|
asmilloঁke hitaṃ yatsyāttena māṃ yoktumarhasi||4||

5nārada uvāca|
tattvaṃ jijñāsatāṃ pūrvamṛṣīṇāṃ bhāvitātmanām
|
sanatkumāro bhagavānidaṃ vacanamabravīt||5||

6nāsti vidyāsamaṃ cakṣurnāsti satyasamaṃ tapaḥ
|
nāsti rāgasamaṃ duḥkhaṃ nāsti tyāgasamaṃ sukham||6||

7nivṛttiḥ karmaṇaḥ pāpātsatataṃ puṇyaśīlatā
|
sadvṛttiḥ samudācāraḥ śreya etadanuttamam||7||

8mānuṣyamasukhaṃ prāpya yaḥ sajjati sa muhyati
|
nālaṃ sa duḥkhamokṣāya saṃyogo duḥkhalakṣaṇam||8||

9saktasya buddhiścalati mohajālavivardhanī
|
mohajālāvṛto duḥkhamiha cāmutra so'śnute||9||

10sarvopāyāttu kāmasya krodhasya ca vinigrahaḥ
|
kāryaḥ śreyorthinā tau hi śreyoghātārthamudyatau||10||

11nityaṃ krodhāttapo rakṣecchriyaṃ rakṣecca matsarāt
|
vidyāṃ mānāvamānābhyāmātmānaṃ tu pramādataḥ||11||

12ānṛśaṃsyaṃ paro dharmaḥ kṣamā ca paramaṃ balam
|
ātmajñānaṃ paraṃ jñānaṃ na satyādvidyate param||12||

13satyasya vacanaṃ śreyaḥ satyādapi hitaṃ vadet
|
yadbhūtahitamatyantametatsatyaṃ mataṃ mama||13||

14sarvārambhaparityāgī nirāśīrniṣparigrahaḥ
|
yena sarvaṃ parityaktaṃ sa vidvān sa ca paṇḍitaḥ||14||

15indriyairindriyārthānyaścaratyātmavaśairiha
|
asajjamānaḥ śāntātmā nirvikāraḥ samāhitaḥ||15||

16ātmabhūtairatadbhūtaḥ saha caiva vinaiva ca
|
sa vimuktaḥ paraṃ śreyo na cireṇādhitiṣṭhati||16||

17adarśanamasaṃsparśastathā saṃbhāṣaṇaṃ sadā
|
yasya bhūtaiḥ saha mune sa śreyo vindate param||17||

18na hiṃsyātsarvabhūtāni maitrāyaṇagataścaret
|
nedaṃ janma samāsādya vairaṃ kurvīta kenacit||18||

19ākiñcanyaṃ susaṃtoṣo nirāśīstvamacāpalam
|
etadāhuḥ paraṃ śreya ātmajñasya jitātmanaḥ||19||

20parigrahaṃ parityajya bhava tāta jitendriyaḥ
|
aśokaṃ sthānamātiṣṭha iha cāmutra cābhayam||20||

21nirāmiṣā na śocanti tyajedāmiṣamātmanaḥ
|
parityajyāmiṣaṃ saumya duḥkhatāpādvimokṣyase||21||

22taponityena dāntena muninā saṃyatātmanā
|
ajitaṃ jetukāmena bhāvyaṃ saṅgeṣvasaṅginā||22||

23guṇasaṅgeṣvanāsakta ekacaryārataḥ sadā
|
brāhmaṇo nacirādeva sukhamāyātyanuttamam||23||

24dvandvārāmeṣu bhūteṣu ya eko ramate muniḥ
|
viddhi prajñānatṛptaṃ taṃ jñānatṛpto na śocati||24||

25śubhairlabhati devatvaṃ vyāmiśrairjanma mānuṣam
|
aśubhaiścāpyadho janma karmabhirlabhate'vaśaḥ||25||

26tatra mṛtyujarāduḥkhaiḥ satataṃ samabhidrutaḥ
|
saṃsāre pacyate jantustatkathaṃ nāvabuddhyase||26||

27ahite hitasaṃjñastvamadhruve dhruvasaṃjñakaḥ
|
anarthe cārthasaṃjñastvaṃ kimarthaṃ nāvabuddhyase||27||

28saṃveṣṭyamānaṃ bahubhirmohāttantubhirātmajaiḥ
|
koṣakāra ivātmānaṃ veśyannāvabudhyase||28||

29alaṃ parigraheṇeha doṣavān hi parigrahaḥ
|
kṛmirhi koṣakārastu badhyate sa parigrahāt||29||

30putradārakuṭumbeṣu saktāḥ sīdanti jantavaḥ
|
sara:paṅkārṇave magnā jīrṇā vanagajā iva||30||

31mahājālasamākṛṣṭān sthale matsyānivoddhṛtān
|
snehajālasamākṛṣṭān paśya jantūnsuduḥkhitān||31||

32kuṭumbaṃ putradārāṃśca śarīraṃ sañcayāśca ye
|
pārakyamadhruvaṃ sarvaṃ kiṃ svaṃ sukṛtaduṣkṛtam||32||

33yadā sarvānparityajya gantavyamavaśena te
|
anarthe kiṃ prasaktastvaṃ samarthaṃ nānutiṣṭhasi||33||

34aviśrāntamanālambamapātheyamadaiśikam
|
tamaḥkāntāramadhvānaṃ kathameko gamiṣyasi||34||

35na hi tvāṃ prasthitaṃ kaścitpṛṣṭhato'nugamiṣyati
|
sukṛtaṃ duṣkṛtaṃ ca tvāṃ yāsyantamanuyāsyati||35||

36vidyā karma ca śaucaṃ ca jñānaṃ ca bahuvistaram
|
arthārthamanusāryante siddhārthaśca vimucyate||36||

37nibandhanī rajjureṣā yā grāme vasato ratiḥ
|
chittvaitāṃ sukṛto yānti naināṃ chindanti duṣkṛtaḥ||37||

38rūpakūlāṃ manaḥsrotāṃ sparśadvīpāṃ rasāvahām
|
gandhapaṅkāṃ śabdajalāṃ svargamārgadurāvahām||38||

39kṣamāritrāṃ satyamayīṃ dharmasthairyavaṭārakām
|
tyāgavātādhvagāṃ śīghrāṃ nautāryāṃ tāṃ nadī taret||39||

40tyaja dharmamadharmaṃ ca tathā satyānṛte tyaja
|
ubhe satyānṛte tyaktvā yena tyajasi taṃ tyaja||40||

41tyaja dharmamasaṅkalpādadharmaṃ cāpyalipsayā
|
ubhe satyānṛte buddhyā buddhiṃ paramaniścayāt||41||

42asthisthūṇaṃ snāyuyutaṃ māṃsaśoṇitalepanam
|
carmāvanaddhaṃ durgandhiṃ pūrṇaṃ mūtrapurīṣayoḥ||42||

43jarāśokasamāviṣṭaṃ rogāyatanamāturam
|
rajasvalamanityaṃ ca bhūtāvāsamimaṃ tyaja||43||

44idaṃ viśvaṃ jagatsarvamajagaccāpi yadbhavet
|
mahābhūtātmakaṃ sarvaṃ mahadyatparamāśrayāt||44||

45indriyāṇi ca pañcaiva tamaḥ sattvaṃ rajastathā
|
ityeṣa saptadaśako rāśiravyaktasaṃjñakaḥ||45||

46sarvairihendriyārthaiśca vyaktāvyaktairhi saṃhitaḥ
|
caturviṃśaka ityeṣa vyaktāvyaktamayo gaṇaḥ||46||

47etaiḥ sarvaiḥ samāyuktaḥ pumānityabhidhīyate
|
trivargaṃ tu sukhaṃ duḥkhaṃ jīvitaṃ maraṇaṃ tathā||47||

48ya idaṃ veda tattvena sa veda prabhavāpyayau
|
pāraṃparyeṇa boddhavyaṃ jñānānāṃ yacca kiñcana||48||

49indriyairgṛhyate yadyattattadvyaktamiti sthitiḥ
|
avyaktamiti vijñeyaṃ liṅgagrāhyamatīndriyam||49||

50indriyairniyatairdehī dhārābhiriva tarpyate
|
loke vitatamātmānaṃ lokāṃścātmani paśyati||50||

51parāvaradṛśaḥ śaktirjñānamūlā na naśyati
|
paśyataḥ sarvabhūtāni sarvāvasthāsu sarvadā||51||

52sarvabhūtasya saṃyogo nāśubhenopapadyate
|
jñānena vividhān kleśānativṛttasya mohajān||52||

53loke buddhiprakāśena lokamārgo na riṣyate
|
anādinidhanaṃ jantumātmani sthitamavyayam||53||

54akartāramamūrtaṃ ca bhagavānāha tīrthavit
|
yo jantuḥ svakṛtaistaistaiḥ karmabhirnityaduḥkhitaḥ||54||

55sa duḥkhapratighātārthaṃ hanti jantūnanekadhā
|
tataḥ karma samādatte punaranyannavaṃ bahu||55||

56tapyate'tha punastena bhuktvāpathyamivāturaḥ
|
ajasrameva mohāndho duḥkheṣu sukhasaṃjñitaḥ||56||

57vadhyate mathyate caiva karmabhirmanthavatsadā
|
tato nibaddhaḥ svāṃ yoniṃ karmaṇāmudayādiha||57||

58paribhramati saṃsāraṃ cakravadbahuvedanaḥ
|
sa tvaṃ nivṛttabandhustu nivṛttaścāpi karmataḥ||58||

59sarvavitsarvajitsiddho bhava bhāvavivarjitaḥ
|
saṃyamena navaṃ bandhaṃ nivartya tapaso balāt
|
saṃprāptā bahavaḥ siddhimapyabādhāṃ sukhodayām||59||
