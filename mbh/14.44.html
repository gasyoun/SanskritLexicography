Mahābhārata 14.44 (Āśvamedhikaparvan)

1brahmovāca|
yadādimadhyaparyantaṃ grahaṇopāyameva ca
|
nāmalakṣaṇasaṃyuktaṃ sarvaṃ vakṣyāmi tattvataḥ||1||

2ahaḥ pūrvaṃ tato rātrirmāsāḥ śuklādayaḥ smṛtāḥ
|
śravaṇādīni ṛkṣāṇi ṛtavaḥ śiśirādayaḥ||2||

3bhūmirādistu gandhānāṃ rasānāmāpa eva ca
|
rūpāṇāṃ jyotirādityaḥ sparśānāṃ vāyurucyate||3||

4śabdasyādistathā''kāśameṣa bhūtakṛto guṇaḥ
|
ataḥ paraṃ pravakṣyāmi bhūtānāmādimuttamam||4||

5ādityo jyotiṣāmādiragnirbhūtādirucyate
|
sāvitrī sarvavidyānāṃ devatānāṃ prajāpatiḥ||5||

6oṅkāraḥ sarvavedānāṃ vacasāṃ prāṇa eva ca
|
yadasminniyataṃ loke sarvaṃ sāvitrirucyate||6||

7gāyatrī cchandasāmādiḥ prajānāṃ sarga ucyate
|
gāvaścatuṣpadāmādirmanuṣyāṇāṃ dvijātayaḥ||7||

8śyenaḥ patatriṇāmādiryajñānāṃ hutamuttamam
|
sarīsṛpāṇāṃ sarveṣāṃ jyeṣṭhaḥ sarpo dvijottamāḥ||8||

9kṛtamādiryugānāṃ ca sarveṣāṃ nātra saṃśayaḥ
|
hiraṇyaṃ sarvaratnāmoṣadhīnāṃ yavāstathā||9||

10sarveṣāṃ bhakṣyabhojyānāmannaṃ paramamucyate
|
dravāṇāṃ caiva sarveṣāṃ peyānāmāpa uttamāḥ||10||

11sthāvarāṇāṃ tu bhūtānāṃ sarveṣāmaviśeṣataḥ
|
brahmakṣetraṃ sadā puṇyaṃ plakṣaḥ prathamataḥ smṛtaḥ||11||

12ahaṃ prajāpatīnāṃ ca sarveṣāṃ nātra saṃśayaḥ
|
mama viṣṇuracintyātmā svayambhūriti sa smṛtaḥ||12||

13parvatānāṃ mahāmeruḥ sarveṣāmagrajaḥ smṛtaḥ
|
diśāṃ ca pradiśāṃ cordhvaṃ dikpūrvā prathamā tathā||13||

14tathā tripathagā gaṅgā nadīnāmagrajā smṛtā
|
tathā sarodapānānāṃ sarveṣāṃ sāgaro'grajaḥ||14||

15devadānavabhūtānāṃ piśācoragarakṣasām
|
narakinnarayakṣāṇāṃ sarveṣāmīśvaraḥ prabhuḥ||15||

16ādirviśvasya jagato viṣṇurbrahmamayo mahān
|
bhūtaṃ parataraṃ yasmāttrailokye neha vidyate||16||

17āśramāṇāṃ ca sarveṣāṃ gārhasthyaṃ nātra saṃśayaḥ
|
lokānāmādiravyaktaṃ sarvasyāntastadeva ca||17||

18ahānyastamayāntāni udayāntā ca śarvarī
|
sukhasyāntaṃ sadā duḥkhaṃ duḥkhasyāntaṃ sadā sukham||18||

19sarve kṣayāntā nicayāḥ patanāntāḥ samucchrayāḥ
|
saṃyogāśca viyogāntā maraṇāntaṃ ca jīvitam||19||

20sarvaṃ kṛtaṃ vināśāntaṃ jātasya maraṇaṃ dhruvam
|
aśāśvataṃ hi loke'smin sadā sthāvarajaṅgamam||20||

21iṣṭaṃ dattaṃ tapo'dhītaṃ vratāni niyamāśca ye
|
sarvametadvināśāntaṃ jñānasyānto na vidyate||21||

22tasmājjñānena śuddhena praśāntātmā jitendriyaḥ
|
nirmamo nirahaṅkāro mucyate sarvapāpmabhiḥ||22||
