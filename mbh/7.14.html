Mahābhārata 7.14 (Droṇaparvan)

1sañjaya uvāca|
tataḥ sa pāṇḍavānīke janayansumahadbhayam
|
vyacarat pṛtanāṃ droṇo dahan kakṣamivānalaḥ||1||

2nirdahantamanīkāni sākṣādagnimivotthitam
|
dṛṣṭvā rukmarathaṃ kruddhaṃ samakampanta sṛñjayāḥ||2||

3satataṃ kṛṣyataḥ saṃkhye dhanuṣo'syāśukāriṇaḥ
|
jyāghoṣaḥ śuśruvetyarthaṃ visphūrjitamivāśaneḥ||3||

4rathinaḥ sādinaścaiva nāgānaśvān padātinaḥ
|
raudrā hastavatā muktāḥ saṃmṛdnanti sma sāyakāḥ||4||

5nānadyamānaḥ parjanyaḥ pravṛddhaḥ śucisaṃkṣaye
|
aśmavarṣamivāvarṣat pareṣāmāvahadbhayam||5||

6vicaran sa tadā rājan senāṃ saṃkṣobhayan prabhuḥ
|
vardhayāmāsa saṃtrāsaṃ śātravāṇāmamānuṣam ||6||

7tasya vidyudivābhreṣu cāpaṃ hemapariṣkṛtam
|
bhramadrathāmbude cāsmin dṛśyate sma punaḥ punaḥ||7||

8sa vīraḥ satyavān prājño dharmanityaḥ sadā punaḥ
|
yugāntakālavaddhorāṃ raudrāṃ prāvartayannadīm||8||

9amarṣavegaprabhavāṃ kravyādagaṇasaṃkulām
|
balaughaiḥ sarvataḥ pūrṇāṃ dhvajavṛkṣāpahāriṇīm||9||

10śoṇitodāṃ rathāvartāṃ hastyaśvakṛtarodhasam
|
kavacoḍupasaṃyuktāṃ māṃsapaṅkasamākulām||10||

11medomajjāsthisikatāmuṣṇīṣacayaphenilām
|
saṃgrāmajaladāpūrṇāṃ prāsamatsyasamākulām||11||

12naranāgāśvakalilāṃ śaravegaughavāhinīm
|
śarīradārusaṃghaṭṭāṃ rathakacchapasaṃkulām||12||

13uttamāṅgaiḥ paṅkajinīṃ nistriṃśajhaṣasaṃkulām
|
rathanāgahradopetāṃ nānābharaṇabhūṣitām||13||

14mahārathaśatāvartāṃ bhūmireṇūrmimālinīm
|
mahāvīryavatāṃ saṃkhye sutarāṃ bhīrudustarām||14||

15śarīraśatasambādhāṃ gṛdhrakaṅkaniṣevitām
|
mahārathasahastrāṇi nayantīṃ yamasādanam||15||

16śūlavyālasamākīrṇāṃ prāṇivājiniṣevitām
|
chinnakṣatramahāhaṃsāṃ mukuṭāṇḍajasevitām||16||

17cakrakūrmāṃ gadānakrāṃ śarakṣudrajhaṣākulām
|
bakagṛdhrasṛgālānāṃ ghorasaṃghairniṣevitām||17||

18nihatān prāṇinaḥ saṃkhye droṇena balinā raṇe
|
vahantī pitṛlokāya śataśo rājasattamam||18||

19śarīraśatasaṃbādhāṃ keśaśaivalaśādvalām
|
nadīṃ prāvartayadrājan bhīrūṇāṃ bhayavardhinīm||19||

20tarjayantamanīkāni tāni tāni mahāratham
|
sarvato'bhyadravan droṇaṃ yudhiṣṭhirapurogamāḥ||20||

21tānabhidravataḥ śūrāṃstāvakā dṛḍhavikramāḥ
|
sarvataḥ pratyagṛhṇanta tadabhūllomaharṣaṇam||21||

22śatamāyastu śakuniḥ sahadevaṃ samādravat
|
saniyantṛdhvajarathaṃ vivyādha niśitaiḥ śaraiḥ||22||

23tasya mādrīsutaḥ ketuṃ dhanuḥ sūtaṃ hayānapi
|
nātikruddhaḥ śaraiśchittvā ṣaṣṭyā vivyādha saubalam||23||

24saubalastu gadāṃ gṛhya pracaskanda rathottamāt
|
sa tasya gadayā rājan rathāt sūtamapātayat||24||

25tatastau virathau rājan gadāhastau mahābalau
|
cikrīḍatū raṇe śūrau saśṛṅgāviva parvatau||25||

26droṇaḥ pāñcālarājānaṃ viddhvā daśabhirāśugaiḥ
|
bahubhistena cābhyastastaṃ vivyādha tato'dhikaiḥ||26||

27viviṃśatiṃ bhīmaseno viṃśatyā niśitaiḥ śaraiḥ
|
viddhvā nākampayadvīrastadadbhutamivābhavat||27||

28viviṃśatastu sahasā vyaśvaketuśarāsanam
|
bhīmaṃ cakre mahārāja tataḥ sainyānyapūjayan||28||

29sa tanna mamṛṣe vīraḥ rātrorvikramamāhave
|
tato'sya gadayā dāntān hayān sarvānapātayat||29||

30hatāśvāt sarathādrājan gṛhya carma mahābalaḥ
|
abhyāyādbhīmasenaṃ tu matto mattamiva dvipam||30||

31śalyastu nakulaṃ vīraḥ svastrīyaṃ priyamātmanaḥ
|
vivyādha prahasan bāṇairlālayan kopayanniva||31||

32tasyāśvānātapatraṃ ca dhvajaṃ sūtamatho dhanuḥ
|
nipātya nakulaḥ saṃkhye śaṅkhaṃ dadhmau pratāpavān||32||

33dhṛṣṭaketuḥ kṛpeṇāstān chittvā bahuvidhāñcharān
|
kṛpaṃ vivyādha saptatyā lakṣma cāsyāharastribhiḥ||33||

34taṃ kṛpaḥ śaravarṣeṇa mahatā samavārayat
|
vivyādha caraṇe vipro dhṛṣṭaketumamarṣaṇam||34||

35sātyakiḥ kṛtavarmāṇaṃ nārācena stanāntare
|
viddhvā vivyādha saptatyā punaranyaiḥ smayanniva||35||

36taṃ bhojaḥ saptasaptatyā viddhvāśu niśitaiḥ śaraiḥ
|
nākampayata śaineyaṃ śīghro vāyurivācalam||36||

37senāpatiḥ suśarmāṇaṃ bhṛśaṃ marmasvatāḍayat
|
sa cāpi taṃ tomareṇa janudeśe'bhyatāḍayat||37||

38vaikartanaṃ tu samare virāṭaḥ pratyavārayat
|
saha matsyairmahāvīryaistadadbhutamivābhavat||38||

39tat pauruṣamabhūt tatra sūtaputrasya dāruṇam
|
yat sainyaṃ vārayāmāsa śaraiḥ sannataparvabhiḥ||39||

40drupadastu svayaṃ rājā bhagada‌ttena saṅgataḥ
|
tayoryuddhaṃ mahārāja citrarūpamivābhavat||40||

41bhagadattastu rājānaṃ drupadaṃ nataparvabhiḥ
|
saniyantṛdhvajarathaṃ vivyādha puruṣarṣabhaḥ||41||

42drupadastu tataḥ kruddho bhagadattaṃ mahāratham
|
ājaghānorasi kṣipraṃ śareṇānataparvaṇā||42||

43yuddhaṃ yodhavarau loke saumadattiśikhaṇḍinau
|
bhūtānāṃ trāsajananaṃ cakrāte'straviśāradau||43||

44bhūriśravā raṇe rājan yājñaseniṃ mahāratham
|
mahatā sāyakaughena chādayāmāsa vīryavān||44||

45śikhaṇḍī tu tataḥ kruddhaḥ saumadattiṃ viśāṃpate
|
navatyā sāyakānāṃ tu kampayāmāsa bhārata||45||

46rākṣasau raudrakarmāṇau haiḍimbālaṃbuṣāvubhau
|
cakrāte'tyadbhutaṃ yuddhaṃ parasparajayaiṣiṇau||46||

47māyāśatasṛjau dṛptau māyābhiritaretaram
|
antarhitau ceratustau bhṛśaṃ vismayakāriṇau||47||

48cekitāno'nuvindena yuyudhe cātibhairavam
|
yathā devāsure yuddhe balaśakrau mahābalau||48||

49lakṣmaṇaḥ kṣatradevena vimardamakarodbhṛśam
|
yathā viṣṇuḥ purā rājan hiraṇyākṣeṇa saṃyuge||49||

50tataḥ pracalitāśvena vidhivatkalpitena ca
|
rathenābhyapatadrājan saubhadraṃ pauravo nadan||50||

51tato'bhyayāt satvarito yuddhākāṃkṣī mahābalaḥ
|
tena cakre mahadyuddhamabhimanyurarindamaḥ||51||

52pauravastvatha saubhadraṃ śaravrātairavākirat
|
tasyārjunirdhvajaṃ chatraṃ dhanuścorvyāmapātayat||52||

53saubhadraḥ pauravaṃ tvanyairviddhvā saptabhirāśugaiḥ
|
pañcabhistasya vivyādha hayānsūtaṃ ca sāyakaiḥ||53||

54tataḥ praharṣayan senāṃ siṃhavadvinadanmuhuḥ
|
samāda‌ttārjunistūrṇaṃ pauravāntakaraṃ śaram||54||

55taṃ tu sandhitamājñāya sāyakaṃ ghoradarśanam
|
dvābhyāṃ śarābhyāṃ hārdikyaściccheda saśaraṃ dhanuḥ||55||

56tadutsṛjya dhanuśchinnaṃ saubhadraḥ paravīrahā
|
udbabarha sitaṃ khaḍgamādadānaḥ śarāvaram||56||

57sa tenānekatāreṇa carmaṇā kṛtahastavat
|
bhrāntāsinā caranmārgāndarśayanvīryamātmanaḥ||57||

58bhramitaṃ punarudbhrāntamādhūtaṃ punarutthitam
|
carmanistriṃśayo rājannirviśeṣamadṛśyata||58||

59sa pauravarathasyeṣāmāplutya sahasā nadan
|
pauravaṃ rathamāsthāya keśapakṣe parāmṛśat||59||

60jaghānāsya padā sūtamasinā pātayaddhvajam
|
vikṣobhyāmbhonidhiṃ tārkṣyastaṃ nāgamiva cākṣipat||60||

61tamāgalitakeśāntaṃ dadṛśuḥ sarvapārthivāḥ
|
ukṣāṇamiva siṃhena pātyamānamacetasam||61||

62tamārjunivaśaṃ prāptaṃ kṛṣyamāṇamanāthavat
|
pauravaṃ pātitaṃ dṛṣṭvā nāmṛṣyata jayadrathaḥ||62||

63sa barhibarhāvatataṃ kiṃkiṇīśatajālavat
|
carma cādāya khaḍgaṃ ca nadan paryapatadrathāt||63||

64tataḥ saindhavamālokya kārṣṇirutsṛjya pauravam
|
utpapāta rathāt tūrṇaṃ śyenavannipapāta ca||64||

65prāsapachi nistriṃśāñchatrubhiḥ saṃpracoditān
|
cicche cāsinā kārṣṇiścarmaṇā saṃrurodha ca||65||

66sa daśāyatvā sainyānāṃ svabāhubalamātmanaḥ
|
tamudyamya mahākhaḍgaṃ carma cātha punarbalī||66||

67vṛddhakṣatrasya dāyādaṃ pituratyantavairiṇam
|
sasārābhimukhaḥ śūraḥ śārdūla iva kuñjaram||67||

68tau parasparamāsādya khaḍgadantanakhāyudhau
|
hṛṣṭavatsaṃprajahrāte vyāghrakesariṇāviva||68||

69saṃpāteṣvabhighāteṣu nipāteṣvasicarmaṇoḥ
|
na tayorantaraṃ kaściddadarśa narasiṃhayoḥ||69||

70avakṣepo'sinirhrādaḥ śastrāntaranidarśanam
|
bāhyāntaranipātaśca nirviśeṣamadṛśyata||70||

71bāhyamābhyantaraṃ caiva carantau mārgamuttamam
|
dadṛśāte mahātmānau sapakṣāviva parvatau||71||

72tato vikṣipataḥ khaḍgaṃ saubhadrasya yaśasvinaḥ
|
śarāvaraṇapakṣānte prajahāra jayadrathaḥ||72||

73rukmapatrāntare saktastasmiṃścarmaṇi bhāsvare
|
sindhurājabaloddhūtaḥ so'bhajyata mahānasiḥ||73||

74bhagnamājñāya nistriṃśamavaplutya padāni ṣaṭ
|
adṛśyata nimeṣeṇa svarathaṃ punarāsthitaḥ||74||

75taṃ kārṣṇiṃ samarānmuktamāsthitaṃ rathamuttamam
|
sahitāḥ sarvarājānaḥ parivavruḥ samantataḥ||75||

76tataścarma ca khaḍgaṃ ca samutkṣipya mahābalaḥ
|
nanādārjunadāyādaḥ prekṣamāṇo jayadratham||76||

77sindhurājaṃ parityajya saubhadraḥ paravīrahā
|
tāpayāmāsa tat sainyaṃ bhuvanaṃ bhāskaro yathā||77||

78tasya sarvāyasīṃ śaktiṃ śalyaḥ kanakabhūṣaṇām
|
cikṣepa samare ghorāṃ dīptāmagniśikhāmiva||78||

79tāmavaplutya jagrāha vikośaṃ cākarodasim
|
vainateyo yathā kārṣṇiḥ patantamuragottamam||79||

80tasya lāghavamājñāya sattvaṃ cāmitatejasaḥ
|
sahitāḥ sarvarājānaḥ siṃhanādamathānadan||80||

81tatastāmeva śalyasya saubhadraḥ paravīrahā
|
mumoca bhujavīryeṇa vaidūryavikṛtāṃ śitām ||81||

82sā tasya rathamāsādya nirmuktabhujagopamā
|
jaghāna sūtaṃ śalyasya rathāccainamapātayat||82||

83tato virāṭadrupadau dhṛṣṭaketuryudhiṣṭhiraḥ
|
sātyakiḥ kekayā bhīmo dhṛṣṭadyumnaśikhaṇḍinau||83||

84yamau ca draupadeyāśca sādhu sādhviti cukruśuḥ
|
bāṇaśabdāśca vividhāḥ siṃhanādāśca puṣkalāḥ||84||

85prādurāsan harṣayantaḥ saubhadramapalāyinam
|
tannāmṛṣyanta putrāste śatrorvijayalakṣaṇam||85||

86athainaṃ sahasā sarve samantānniśitaiḥ śaraiḥ
|
abhyākiranmahārāja jaladā iva parvatam||86||

87teṣāṃ ca priyamanvicchan sūtasya ca parābhavam
|
ārtāyaniramitraghnaḥ kruddhaḥ saubhadramabhyayāt||87||
