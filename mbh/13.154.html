Mahābhārata 13.154 (Anuśāsanaparvan)

1vāyuruvāca|
imāṃ bhūmiṃ dvijātibhyo ditsurvai dakṣiṇāṃ purā
|
aṅgo nāma nṛpo rājaṃstataścintāṃ mahī yayau ||1||

2dhāriṇīṃ sarvabhūtānāmayaṃ prāpya varo nṛpaḥ
|
kathamicchati māṃ dātuṃ dvijebhyo brahmaṇaḥ sutām ||2||

3sā'haṃ tyaktvā gamiṣyāmi bhūmitvaṃ brahmaṇaḥ padam
|
ayaṃ sarāṣṭro nṛpatirmābhūditi tato'gamat ||3||

4tatastāṃ kaśyapo dṛṣṭvā vrajantīṃ pṛthivīṃ tadā
|
praviveśa mahīṃ sadyo muktvā''tmānaṃ samāhitaḥ||4||

5ṛddhā sā sarvato jajñe tṛṇauṣadhisamanvitā
|
dharmottarā naṣṭabhayā bhūmirāsīttato nṛpa ||5||

6evaṃ varṣasahasrāṇi divyāni vipulavrataḥ
|
triṃśataḥ kaśyapo rājan bhūmirāsīdatandritaḥ ||6||

7athāgamya mahārāja namaskṛtya ca kaśyapam
|
pṛthivī kāśyapī jajñe sutā tasya mahātmanaḥ ||7||

8eṣa rājannīdṛśo vai brāhmaṇaḥ kaśyapo'bhavat
|
anyaṃ prabrūhi vā tvaṃ ca kaśyapātkṣatriyaṃ varam ||8||

9tūṣṇīṃ babhūva nṛpatiḥ pavanastvabravītpunaḥ
|
śṛṇu rājannutathyasya jātasyāṅgirase kule ||9||

10bhadrā somasya duhitā rūpeṇa paramā matā
|
tasyāstulyaṃ patiṃ soma utathyaṃ samapaśyata ||10||

11sā ca tīvraṃ tapastepe mahābhāgā yaśasvinī
|
utathyārthe tu cārvaṅgī paraṃ niyamamāsthitā ||11||

12tata āhūya sotathyaṃ dadāvatriryaśasvinīm
|
bhāryārthe sa ca jagrāha vidhivadbhūridakṣiṇaḥ||12||

13tāṃ tvakāmayata śrīmānvaruṇaḥ pūrvameva ha
|
sa cāgamya vanaprasthaṃ yamunāyāṃ jahāra tām ||13||

14jaleśvarastu hṛtvā tāmanayatsvaṃ puraṃ prati
|
paramādbhutasaṃkāśaṃ ṣaṭsahasraśatahradam ||14||

15na hi ramyataraṃ kiñcittasmādanyatpurottamam
|
prāsādairapsarobhiśca divyaiḥ kāmaiśca śobhitam ||15||

16tatra devastayā sārdhaṃ reme rājan jaleśvaraḥ
|
athākhyātamutathyāya tataḥ patnyavamardanam ||16||

17tacchrutvā nāradātsarvamutathyo nāradaṃ tadā
|
provāca gaccha brūhi tvaṃ varuṇaṃ paruṣaṃ vacaḥ ||17||

18madvākyānmuñca me bhāryāṃ kasmāttāṃ hṛtavānasi
|
lokapālosi lokānāṃ na lokasya vilopakaḥ||18||

19somena dattā bhāryā me tvayā cāpahṛtā'dya vai
|
ityukto vacanāttasya nāradena jaleśvaraḥ ||19||

20muñca bhāryāmutathyasya kasmāttvaṃ hṛtavānasi
|
iti śrutvā vacastasya so'tha taṃ varuṇo'bravīt ||20||

21mamaiṣā supriyā bhāryā naināmutsraṣṭumutsahe
|
ityukto varuṇenātha nāradaḥ prāpya taṃ munim
|
utathyamabravīdvākyaṃ nātihṛṣṭamanā iva ||21||

22gale gṛhītvā kṣiptosmi varuṇena mahāmune
|
na prayacchati te bhāryāṃ yatte kāryaṃ kuruṣva tat ||22||

23nāradasya vacaḥ śrutvā kruddhaḥ prājvaladaṅgirāḥ
|
apibattejasā vāri viṣṭabhya sumahātapāḥ||23||

24pīyamāne tu sarvasmiṃstoye'pi salileśvaraḥ
|
suhṛdbhirbhikṣamāṇo'pi naivāmuñcata tāṃ tadā ||24||

25tataḥ kruddho'bravīdbhūmimutathyo brāhmaṇottamaḥ
|
darśayasva sthalaṃ bhadre ṣaṭsahasraśatahradam ||25||

26tatastadīriṇaṃ jātaṃ samudrasyāpasarpataḥ
|
tasmāddeśānnadīṃ caiva provācāsau dvijottamaḥ ||26||

27adṛśyā gaccha bhīru tvaṃ sarasvati marūnprati
|
apuṇya eṣa bhavatu deśastyaktastvayā śubhe ||27||

28tasmin saṃśoṣite deśe bhadrāmādāya vāripaḥ
|
adadāccharaṇaṃ gatvā bhāryāmāṅgirasāya vai ||28||

29pratigṛhya tu tāṃ bhāryāmutathyaḥ sumanā'bhavat
|
mumoca ca jagadduḥkhādvaruṇaṃ caiva haihaya ||29||

30tataḥ sa labdhvā tāṃ bhāryāṃ varuṇaṃ prāha dharmavit
|
utathyaḥ sumahātejā yattacchṛṇu narādhipa ||30||

31mayaiṣā tapasā prāptā krośataste jalādhipa
|
ityuktvā tāmupādāya svameva bhavanaṃ yayau ||31||

32eṣa rājannīdṛśo vai utathyo brāhmaṇarṣabhaḥ
|
bravīmyahaṃ brūhi vā tvamutathyātkṣatriyaṃ varam ||32||
