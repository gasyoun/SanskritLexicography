Mahābhārata 3.224 (Āraṇyakaparvan)

1kanyovāca|
ahaṃ prajāpateḥ kanyā devaseneti viśrutā
|
bhaginī me daityasenā sā pūrvaṃ keśinā hṛtā||1||

2sadaivāvāṃ bhaginyau tu sakhibhiḥ saha mānasam
|
āgacchāveha ratyarthamanujñāpya prajāpatim||2||

3nityaṃ cāvāṃ prārthayate hartuṃ keśī mahāsuraḥ
|
icchatyenaṃ daityasenā na cāhaṃ pākaśāsana||3||

4sā hṛtā'nena bhagavan muktā'haṃ tvadbalena tu
|
tvayā devendra nirdiṣṭaṃ patimicchāmi durjayam||4||

5indra uvāca|
mama mātṛṣvaseyī tvaṃ mātā dākṣāyaṇī mama
|
ākhyātuṃ tvahamicchāmi svayamātmabalaṃ tvayā||5||

6kanyovāca|
abalā'haṃ mahābāho patistu balavān mama
|
varadānāt piturbhāvī surāsuranamaskṛtaḥ||6||

7indra uvāca|
kīdṛśaṃ tu balaṃ devi patyustava bhaviṣyati
|
etadicchāmyahaṃ śrotuṃ tava vākyamanindite||7||

8kanyovāca|
devadānavayakṣāṇāṃ kinnaroragarakṣasām
|
jetā yo duṣṭadaityānāṃ mahāvīryo mahābalaḥ||8||

9yastu sarvāṇi bhūtāni tvayā saha vijeṣyati
|
sa hi me bhavitā bhartā brahmaṇyaḥ kīrtivardhanaḥ||9||

10mārkaṇḍeya uvāca|
indrastasyā vacaḥ śrutvā duḥkhito'cintayadbhṛśam
|
asyā devyāḥ patirnāsti yādṛśaṃ samprabhāṣate||10||

11athāpaśyat sa udaye bhāskaraṃ bhāskaradyutiḥ
|
somaṃ caiva mahābhāgaṃ viśamānaṃ divākaram||11||

12amāvāsyāṃ pravṛttāyāṃ muhūrte raudra eva tu
|
devāsuraṃ ca saṅgrāmaṃ so'paśyadudaye girau||12||

13lohitaiśca ghanairyuktāṃ pūrvāṃ sandhyāṃ śatakratuḥ
|
apaśyallohitodaṃ ca bhagavān varuṇālayam||13||

14bhṛgubhiścāṅgirobhyaśca hutaṃ mantraiḥ pṛthagvidhaiḥ
|
havyaṃ gṛhītvā vahniṃ ca praviśantaṃ divākaram||14||

15parva caiva caturviṃśaṃ tadā sūryamupasthitam
|
tathā dharmagataṃ raudraṃ somaṃ sūryagataṃ ca tam||15||

16samālokyaikatāmeva śaśino bhāskarasya ca
|
samavāyaṃ tu taṃ raudraṃ dṛṣṭvā śakro'nvacintayat||16||

17sūryācandramasorghoraṃ dṛśyate pariveṣaṇam
|
etasminneva rātryante mahadyuddhaṃ tu śaṃsati||17||

18saritsindhurapīyaṃ tu pratyasṛgvāhinī bhṛśam
|
śṛgālinyagnivakrā ca pratyādityaṃ virāviṇī||18||

19eṣa raudraśca saṅghāto mahān yuktaśca tejasā
|
somasya vahnisūryābhyāmadbhuto'yaṃ samāgamaḥ||19||

20janayedyaṃ sutaṃ somaḥ so'syā devyāḥ patirbhavet
|
agniścaitairguṇairyuktaḥ sarvairagniśca devatā||20||

21eṣa cejjanayedgarbhaṃ so'syā devyāḥ patirbhavet
|
evaṃ sañcintya bhagavān brahmalokaṃ tadā gataḥ||21||

22gṛhītvā devasenāṃ tāmavadat sa pitāmaham
|
uvāca cāsyā devyāstvaṃ sādhuśūraṃ patiṃ diśa||22||

23brahmovāca|
mayaitaccintitaṃ kāryaṃ tvayā dānavasūdana
|
tathā sa bhavitā garbho balavānuruvikramaḥ||23||

24sa bhaviṣyati senānīstvayā saha śatakrato
|
asyā devyāḥ patiścaiva sa bhaviṣyati vīryavān||24||

25etacchrutvā namastasmai kṛtvā'sau saha kanyayā
|
tatrābhyagacchaddevendro yatra devarṣayo'bhavan||25||

26vasiṣṭhapramukhā mukhyā viprendrāḥ sumahābalāḥ
|
bhāgārthaṃ tapaso dhātuṃ teṣāṃ somaṃ tathā'dhvare||26||

27pipāsavo yayurdevāḥ śatakratupurogamāḥ
|
iṣṭiṃ kṛtvā yathānyāyaṃ susamiddhe hutāśane||27||

28juhuvuste mahātmāno havyaṃ sarvadivaukasām
|
samāhūto hutavahaḥ so'dbhutaḥ sūryamaṇḍalāt||28||

29viniḥsṛtya yayau vahnirvāmyato vidhivat prabhuḥ
|
āgamyāhavanīyaṃ vai tairdvijairmantrato hutam||29||

30sa tatra vividhaṃ havyaṃ pratigṛhya hutāśanaḥ
|
ṛṣibhyo bharataśreṣṭha prāyacchata divaukasām||30||

31niṣkāmaṃścāpyapaśyat sa patnīsteṣāṃ mahātmanām
|
sveṣvāsaneṣūpaviṣṭāḥ svapantīśca tathā sukham||31||

32rukmavedinibhāstāstu candralekhā ivāmalāḥ
|
hutāśanārcipratimāḥ sarvāstārā ivādbhutāḥ||32||

33sa tatra tena manasā babhūva kṣubhitendriyaḥ
|
patnīrdṛṣṭvā dvijendrāṇāṃ vahniḥ kāmavaśaṃ yayau||33||

34bhūyaḥ sañcintayāmāsa na nyāyyaṃ kṣubhito hyaham
|
sādhvyaḥ patnyo dvijendrāṇāmakāmāḥ kāmayāmyaham||34||

35naitāḥ śakyā mayā draṣṭuṃ spraṣṭuṃ vāpyanimittataḥ
|
gārhapatyaṃ samāviśya tasmāt paśyāmyabhīkṣṇaśaḥ||35||

36mārkaṇḍeya uvāca|
saṃspṛśanniva sarvāstāḥ śikhābhiḥ kāñcanaprabhāḥ
|
paśyamānaśca mumude gārhapatyaṃ samāśritaḥ||36||

37nirupya tatra suciramevaṃ vahnirvaśaṃ gataḥ
|
manastāsu vinikṣipya kāmayāno varāṅganāḥ||37||

38kāmasantaptahṛdayo dehatyāgaviniścitaḥ
|
alābhe brāhmaṇastrīṇāmagnirvanamupāgamat||38||

39svāhā taṃ dakṣaduhitā prathamaṃ kāmayat tadā
|
sā tasya chidramanvaicchaccirātprabhṛtibhāvinī||39||

40apramattasya devasya na ca paśyatyaninditā
|
sā taṃ jñātvā yathāvat tu vahniṃ vanamupāgatam||40||

41tattvataḥ kāmasantaptaṃ cintayāmāsa bhāvinī
|
ahaṃ saptarṣipatnīnāṃ kṛtvā rūpāṇi pāvakam||41||

42kāmayiṣyāmi kāmārtā tāsāṃ rūpeṇa mohitam
|
evaṃ kṛte prītirasya kāmāvātiśca me bhavet||42||
