Mahābhārata 5.113 (Udyogaparvan)

1nārada uvāca|
ṛṣabhasya tataḥ śṛṅgaṃ nipatya dvijapakṣiṇau
|
śāṇḍilīṃ brāhmaṇīṃ tatra dadṛśāte taponvitām ||1||

2abhivādya suparṇastu gālavaścābhipūjya tām
|
tayā ca svāgatenoktau viṣṭhare sanniṣīdatuḥ ||2||

3siddhamannaṃ tayā dattaṃ balimantropabṛṃhitam
|
bhuktvā tṛptāvubhau bhūmau suptau tāvanumohitau ||3||

4muhūrtāt pratibuddhastu suparṇo gamanepsayā
|
atha bhraṣṭatanūjāṅgamātmānaṃ dadṛśe khagaḥ ||4||

5māṃsapiṇḍopamo'bhūtsa mukhapādānvitaḥ khagaḥ
|
gālavastaṃ tathā dṛṣṭvā vimanāḥ paryapṛcchata ||5||

6kimidaṃ bhavatā prāptamihāgamanajaṃ phalam
|
vāso'yamiha kālaṃ tu kiyantaṃ nau bhaviṣyati ||6||

7kiṃ nu te manasā dhyātamaśubhaṃ dharmadūṣaṇam
|
na hyayaṃ bhavataḥ svalpo vyabhicāro bhaviṣyati ||7||

8suparṇo'thābravīdvipraṃ pradhyātaṃ vai mayā dvija
|
imāṃ siddhāmito netuṃ tatra yatra prajāpatiḥ ||8||

9yatra devo mahādevo yatra viṣṇuḥ sanātanaḥ
|
yatra dharmaśca yajñaśca tatreyaṃ nivasediti ||9||

10so'haṃ bhagavatīṃ yāce praṇataḥ priyakāmyayā
|
mayaitannāma pradhyātaṃ manasā śocatā kila ||10||

11tadevaṃ bahumānātte mayehānīpsitaṃ kṛtam
|
sukṛtaṃ duṣkṛtaṃ vā tvaṃ māhātmyāt kṣantumarhasi ||11||

12sā tau tadā'bravīttuṣṭā patagendradvijarṣabhau
|
na bhetavyaṃ suparṇo'si suparṇa tyaja saṃbhramam ||12||

13ninditā'smi tvayā vatsa na ca nindāṃ kṣamāmyaham
|
lokebhyaḥ sapadi bhraśyedyo māṃ nindeta pāpakṛt ||13||

14hīnayā'lakṣaṇaiḥ sarvaistathā'ninditayā mayā
|
ācāraṃ pratigṛhṇantyā siddhiḥ prāpteyamuttamā ||14||

15ācāraḥ phalate dharmamācāraḥ phalate dhanam
|
ācārācchriyamāpnoti ācāro hantyalakṣaṇam ||15||

16tadāyuṣman khagapate yatheṣṭaṃ gamyatāmitaḥ
|
na ca te garhaṇīyā'haṃ garhitavyāḥ striyaḥ kvacit ||16||

17bhavitāsi yathāpūrvaṃ balavīryasamanvitaḥ
|
babhūvatustatastasya pakṣau draviṇavattarau ||17||

18anujñātastu śāṇḍilyā yathāgatamupāgamat
|
naiva cāsāda‌yāmāsa tathārūpāṃsturaṅgamān ||18||

19viśvāmitro'tha taṃ dṛṣṭvā gālavaṃ cādhvani sthitaḥ
|
uvāca vadatāṃ śreṣṭho vainateyasya sannidhau ||19||

20yastvayā svayamevārthaḥ pratijñāto mama dvija
|
tasya kālo'pavargasya yathā vā manyate bhavān ||20||

21pratīkṣiṣyāmyahaṃ kālametāvantaṃ tathā param
|
yathā saṃsidhyate vipra sa mārgastu niśamyatām ||21||

22suparṇo'thābravīddīnaṃ gālavaṃ bhṛśaduḥkhitam
|
pratyakṣaṃ khalvidānīṃ me viśvāmitro yaduktavān ||22||

23tadāgaccha dvijaśreṣṭha mantrayiṣyāva gālava
|
nādatvā gurave śakyaṃ kṛtsnamarthaṃ tvayāsitum ||23||
