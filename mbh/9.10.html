Mahābhārata 9.10 (Śalyaparvan)

1sañjaya uvāca|
tatprabhagnaṃ balaṃ dṛṣṭvā madrarājaḥ pratāpavān
|
uvāca sārathiṃ tūrṇaṃ codayāśvānmahājavān||1||

2eṣa tiṣṭhati vai rājā pāṇḍuputro yudhiṣṭhiraḥ
|
chatreṇa dhriyamāṇena pāṇḍureṇa virājatā||2||

3atra māṃ prāpaya kṣipraṃ paśya me sārathe balam
|
na samartho hi me pārthaḥ sthātumadya puro yudhi||3||

4evamuktastataḥ prāyānmadrarājasya sārathiḥ
|
yatra rājā satyasandho dharmaputro yudhiṣṭhiraḥ||4||

5prāpatattacca sahasā pāṇḍavānāṃ mahadbalam
|
dadhāraiko raṇe śalyo velovṛttamivārṇavam||5||

6pāṇḍavānāṃ balaughastu śalyamāsādya māriṣa
|
vyatiṣṭhata tadā yuddhe sindhorvega ivācalam||6||

7madrarājaṃ tu samare dṛṣṭvā yuddhāya dhiṣṭhitam
|
kuravaḥ sannyavartanta mṛtyuṃ kṛtvā nivartanam||7||

8teṣu rājan nivṛtteṣu vyūḍhānīkeṣu bhāgaśaḥ
|
prāvartata mahāraudraḥ saṅgrāmaḥ śoṇitodakaḥ||8||

9samārchaccitrasenaṃ tu nakulo yuddhadurmadaḥ
|
tau parasparamāsādya citrakārmukadhāriṇau||9||

10moghāviva yathodvṛttau dakṣiṇottaravarṣiṇau
|
śaratoyaiḥ siṣicatustau parasparamāhave||10||

11nāntaraṃ tatra paśyāmi pāṇḍavasyetarasya ca
|
ubhau kṛtāstrau balinau rathacaryāviśāradau||11||

12parasparavadhe yattau chidrānveṣaṇatatparau
|
citrasenastu bhallena pītena niśitena ca||12||

13nakulasya mahārāja muṣṭideśe'cchinaddhanuḥ
|
athainaṃ chinnadhanvānaṃ rukmapuṅkhaḥ śilāśitaiḥ||13||

14tribhiḥ śarairasambhrānto lalāṭe vai samārpayat
|
hayāṃścāsya śaraistīkṣṇaiḥ preṣayāmāsa mṛtyave||14||

15tathā dhvajaṃ sārathiṃ ca tribhistribhirapātayat
|
sa śatrubhujanirmuktairlalāṭasthaistribhiḥ śaraiḥ||15||

16nakulaḥ śuśubhe rājaṃstriśṛṅga iva parvataḥ
|
sa cchinnadhanvā virathaḥ khaḍgamādāya carma ca||16||

17rathādavātaradvīraḥ śailāgrādiva kesarī
|
padbhyāmāpatatastasya śaravṛṣṭiṃ samāsṛjat||17||

18nukalo'pyagrasattāṃ vai carmaṇā laghuvikramaḥ
|
citrasenarathaṃ prāpya citrayodhī jitaśramaḥ||18||

19āruroha mahābāhuḥ sarvasainyasya paśyataḥ
|
sakuṇḍalaṃ samukuṭaṃ sumanasaṃ svāyatekṣaṇam||19||

20citrasenaśiraḥ kāyādapāharata pāṇḍavaḥ
|
sa papāta rathopasthe divākarasamadyutiḥ||20||

21citrasenaṃ viśastaṃ tu dṛṣṭvā tatra mahārathāḥ
|
sādhuvādasvanāṃścakruḥ siṃhanādāṃśca puṣkalān||21||

22viśastaṃ bhrātaraṃ dṛṣṭvā karṇaputrau mahārathau
|
suṣeṇaḥ satyasenaśca muñcantau vividhāśarān||22||

23tato'bhyadhāvatāṃ tūrṇaṃ pāṇḍavaṃ rathināṃ varam
|
jighāṃsantau yathā nāgaṃ vyāghrau rājanmahāvane||23||

24tāvabhyadhāvatāṃ tīkṣṇau dvāvapyenaṃ mahāratham
|
śaraughānsamyagasyantau jīmūtau salilaṃ yathā||24||

25sa śaraiḥ sarvato viddhaḥ prahṛṣṭa iva pāṇḍavaḥ
|
anyatkārmukamādāya rathamāruhya vegavān||25||

26atiṣṭhata raṇe vīraḥ kruddharūpa ivāntakaḥ
|
tasya tau bhrātarau rājañśaraiḥ sannataparvabhiḥ||26||

27rathaṃ viśakalīkartuṃ samārabdhau viśāmpate
|
tataḥ prahasya nakulaścaturbhiścaturo raṇe||27||

28jaghāna niśitairbāṇaiḥ satyasenasya vājinaḥ
|
tataḥ sandhāya nārācaṃ rukmapuṅkha śilāśitam||28||

29dhanuściccheda rājendra satyasenasya pāṇḍavaḥ
|
athānyaṃ rathamāsthāya dhanurādāya cāparam||29||

30satyasenaḥ suṣeṇaśca pāṇḍavaṃ paryadhāvatām
|
avidhyattāvasambhrāntau mādrīputraḥ pratāpavān||30||

31dvābhyāṃ dvābhyāṃ mahārāja śarābhyāṃ raṇamūrdhani
|
suṣeṇastu tataḥ kruddhaḥ pāṇḍavasya mahaddhanuḥ||31||

32ciccheda prahasan yuddhe kṣurapreṇa mahārathaḥ
|
athānyaddhanurādāya nakulaḥ krodhamūrchitaḥ||32||

33suṣeṇaṃ pañcabhirviddhvā dhvajamekena cicchide
|
satyasenasya ca dhanurhastāvāpaṃ ca māriṣa||33||

34ciccheda tarasā yuddhe tata uccukruśurjanāḥ
|
athānyaddhanurādāya vegaghnaṃ bhārasādhanam||34||

35śaraiḥ sañchādayāmāsa samantātpāṇḍunandanam
|
sannivārya tu tānbāṇānnakulaḥ paravīrahā||35||

36satyasenaṃ suṣeṇaṃ ca dvābhyāṃ dvābhyāmavidhyata
|
tāvenaṃ pratyavidhyetāṃ pṛthak pṛthagajihmagaiḥ||36||

37sārathiṃ cāsya rājendra śitairvivyadhatuḥ śaraiḥ
|
satyaseno ratheṣāṃ tu nakulasya dhanustathā||37||

38pṛthakcharābhyāṃ ciccheda kṛtahastaḥ pratāpavān
|
sa rathe'tirathastiṣṭhan rathaśaktiṃ parāmṛśat||38||

39svarṇadaṇḍāmakuṇṭhāgrāṃ tailadhautāṃ sunirmalām
|
lelihānāmiva vibho nāgakanyāṃ mahāviṣām||39||

40samudyamya ca cikṣepa satyasenasya saṃyuge
|
sā tasya hṛdayaṃ saṅkhye bibheda ca tathā nṛpa||40||

41sa papāta rathādbhūmiṃ gatasattvo'lpacetanaḥ
|
bhrātaraṃ nihataṃ dṛṣṭvā suṣeṇaḥ krodhamūrcchitaḥ||41||

42abhyavarṣaccharaistūrṇaṃ pādātaṃ pāṇḍunandanam
|
caturbhiścaturo vāhān dhvajaṃ chitvā ca pañcabhiḥ||42||

43tribhirvai sārathiṃ hatvā karṇaputro nanāda ha
|
nakulaṃ virathaṃ dṛṣṭvā draupadeyo mahāratham||43||

44sutasomo'bhidudrāva parīpsan pitaraṃ raṇe
|
tato'dhiruhya nukalaḥ sutasomasya taṃ ratham||44||

45śuśubhe bharataśreṣṭho giristha iva kesarī
|
anyatkārmukamādāya suṣeṇaṃ samayodhayat||45||

46tāvubhau śaravarṣābhyāṃ samāsādya parasparam
|
parasparavadhe yatnaṃ cakratuḥ sumahārathau||46||

47suṣeṇastu tataḥ kruddhaḥ pāṇḍavaṃ viśikhaistribhiḥ
|
sutasomaṃ tu viṃśatyā bāhvorurasi cārpayat||47||

48tataḥ kruddho mahārāja nakulaḥ paravīrahā
|
śaraistasya diśaḥ sarvāśchādayāmāsa vīryavān||48||

49tato gṛhītvā tīkṣṇāgramardhacandraṃ sutejanam
|
suvegavantaṃ cikṣepa karṇaputrāya saṃyuge||49||

50tasya tena śiraḥ kāyājjahāra nṛpasattama
|
paśyatāṃ sarvasainyānāṃ tadadbhutamivābhavat||50||

51sa hataḥ prāpatadrājannakulena mahātmanā
|
nadīvegādivārugṇastīrajaḥ pādapo mahān||51||

52karṇaputravadhaṃ dṛṣṭvā nakulasya ca vikramam
|
pradudrāva bhayātsenā tāvakī bharatarṣabha||52||

53tāṃ tu senāṃ mahārāja madrarājaḥ pratāpavān
|
apālayadraṇe śūraḥ senāpatirarindamaḥ||53||

54vibhīstasthau mahārāja vyavasthāpya ca vāhinīm
|
siṃhanādaṃ bhṛśaṃ kṛtvā dhanuḥśabdaṃ ca dāruṇam||54||

55tāvakāḥ samare rājan rakṣitā dṛḍhadhanvanā
|
pratyudyayuśca tāṃste tu samantādvigatavyathāḥ||55||

56madrarājaṃ maheṣvāsaṃ parivārya samantataḥ
|
sthitā rājanmahāsenā yoddhukāmā samantataḥ||56||

57sātyakirbhīmasenaśca mādrīputrau ca pāṇḍavau
|
yudhiṣṭhiraṃ puraskṛtya hrīniṣevamarindamam||57||

58parivārya raṇe vīrāḥ siṃhanādaṃ pracakrire
|
bāṇaśaṅkharavāṃstīvrān kṣveḍāśca vividhā dadhuḥ||58||

59tathaiva tāvakāḥ sarve madrādhipatimañjasā
|
parivārya susaṃrabdhāḥ punayuddhamarocayan||59||

60tataḥ pravavṛte yuddhaṃ bhīrūṇāṃ bhayavardhanam
|
tāvakānāṃ pareṣāṃ ca mṛtyuṃ kṛtvā nivartanam||60||

61yathā devāsuraṃ yuddhaṃ pūrvamāsīdviśāmpate
|
abhītānāṃ tathā rājan yamarāṣṭravivardhanam||61||

62tataḥ kapidhvajo rājanhatvā saṃśaptakān raṇe
|
abhyadravata tāṃ senāṃ kauravīṃ pāṇḍunandanaḥ||62||

63tathaiva pāṇḍavāḥ sarve dhṛṣṭadyumnaparogamāḥ
|
abhyadhāvanta tāṃ senāṃ visṛjantaḥ śitāñśarān||63||

64pāṇḍavairavakīrṇānāṃ sammohaḥ samajāyata
|
na ca jajñustvanīkāni diśo vā vidiśastathā||64||

65āpūryamāṇā niśitaiḥ śaraiḥ pāṇḍavacoditaiḥ
|
hatapravīrā vidhvastā vāryamāṇā samantataḥ||65||

66kauravyavadhyata camūḥ pāṇḍuputrairmahārathaiḥ
|
tathaiva pāṇḍavaṃ sainyaṃ śarai rājansamantataḥ||66||

67raṇe'hanyata putraiste śataśo'tha sahasraśaḥ
|
te sene bhṛśasantapte vadhyamāne parasparam||67||

68vyākule samapadyetāṃ varṣāsu saritāviva
|
āviveśa tatastīvraṃ tāvakānāṃ mahadbhayam
|
pāṇḍavānāṃ ca rājendra tathābhūte mahāhave||68||
