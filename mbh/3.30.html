Mahābhārata 3.30 (Āraṇyakaparvan)

1draupadyuvāca|
namo dhātre vidhātre ca yau mohaṃ cakratustava
|
pitṛpaitāmahe vṛtte voḍhavye te'nyathā matiḥ||1||

2karmabhiścintito loke gatyāṃ gatyāṃ pṛthagvidhaḥ
|
tasmātkarmāṇi nityāni lobhānmokṣaṃ yiyāsati||2||

3neha dharmānṛśaṃsyābhyāṃ na kṣāntyā nārjavena ca
|
puruṣaḥ śriyamāpnoti na ghṛṇitvena karhicit||3||

4tvāṃ ca vyasanamabhyāgādidaṃ bhārata duḥsaham
|
yattvaṃ nārhasi nāpīme bhrātaraste mahaujasaḥ||4||

5na hi te'dhyagamañjātu tadānīṃ nādya bhārata
|
dharmātpriyataraṃ kiñcidapi cejjīvitādiha||5||

6dharmārthameva te rājyaṃ dharmārthaṃ jīvitaṃ ca te
|
brāhmaṇā guravaścaiva jānantyapi ca devatāḥ||6||

7bhīmasenārjunau cobhau mādreyau ca mayā saha
|
tyajestvamiti me buddhirna tu dharmaṃ parityajeḥ||7||

8rājānaṃ dharmagoptāraṃ dharmo rakṣati rakṣitaḥ
|
iti me śrutamāryāṇāṃ tvāṃ tu manye na rakṣati||8||

9ananyā hi naravyāghra nityadā dharmameva te
|
buddhiḥ satatamanveticchāyeva puruṣaṃ nijā||9||

10nāvamasthā hi sadṛśānnāvarāñcchreyasaḥ kutaḥ
|
avāpya pṛthivīṃ kṛtsnāṃ na te śṛṅgamavardhata||10||

11svāhākāraiḥ svadhābhiśca pūjābhirapi ca dvijān
|
daivatāni pitṝṃścaiva satataṃ pārtha sevase||11||

12brāhmaṇāḥ sarvakāmaiste satataṃ pārtha tarpitāḥ
|
yatayo mokṣiṇaścaiva gṛhasthāścaiva bhārata||12||

13bhuñjate rukmapātrībhiryatrāhaṃ paricārikā
|
āraṇyakebhyo lauhāni bhājanāni prayacchasi
|
nādeyaṃ brāhmaṇebhyaste gṛhe kiñcana vidyate||13||

14yadidaṃ vaiśvadevaṃ te śāntaye kriyate gṛhe
|
taddattvātithibhūtebhyo rājañcchiṣṭena jīvasi||14||

15iṣṭayaḥ paśubandhāśca kāmyanaimittikāśca ye
|
vartante pākayajñāśca yajñakarma ca nityadā||15||

16asminnapi mahāraṇye vijane dasyusevite
|
rāṣṭrādapetya vasato dhārmaste nāvasīdati||16||

17aśvamedho rājasūyaḥ puṇḍarīko'tha gosavaḥ
|
etairapi mahāyajñairiṣṭaṃ te bhūridakṣiṇaiḥ||17||

18rājanparītayā buddhyā viṣame'kṣaparājaye
|
rājyaṃ vasūnyāyudhāni bhrātṝnmāṃ cāsi nirjitaḥ||18||

19ṛjormṛdorvadānyasya hrīmataḥ satyavādinaḥ
|
kathamakṣavyasanajā buddhirāpatitā tava||19||

20atīva mohamāyāti manaśca paribhūyate
|
niśāmyate duḥkhamidamimāṃ cāpadamīdṛśīm||20||

21atrāpyudāharantīmamitihāsaṃ purātanam
|
īśvarasya vaśe lokāstiṣṭhante nātmano yathā||21||

22dhātaiva khalu bhūtānāṃ sukhaduḥkhe priyāpriye
|
dadhāti sarvamīśānaḥ purastācchukramuccaran||22||

23yathā dārumayī yoṣā naravīra samāhitā
|
īrayatyaṅgamaṅgāni tathā rājannimāḥ prajāḥ||23||

24ākāśa iva bhūtāni vyāpya sarvāṇi bhārata
|
īśvaro vidadhātīha kalyāṇaṃ yacca pāpakam||24||

25śakunistantubaddho vā niyato'yamanīśvaraḥ
|
īśvarasya vaśe tiṣṭhennānyeṣāṃ nātmanaḥ prabhuḥ||25||

26maṇiḥ sūtra iva proto nasyota iva govṛṣaḥ
|
srotaso madhyamāpannaḥ kūlādvṛkṣa iva cyutaḥ||26||

27dhāturādeśamanveti tanmayo hi tadarpaṇaḥ
|
nātmādhīno manuṣyo'yaṃ kālaṃ bhajati kañcana||27||

28ajño janturanīśo'yamātmanaḥ sukhaduḥkhayoḥ
|
īśvaraprerito gacchetsvargaṃ narakameva ca||28||

29yathā vāyostṛṇāgrāṇi vaśaṃ yānti balīyasaḥ
|
dhāturevaṃ vaśaṃ yānti sarvabhūtāni bhārata||29||

30ārye karmaṇi yuñjānaḥ pāpe vā punarīśvaraḥ
|
vyāpya bhūtāni carate na cāyamiti lakṣyate||30||

31hetumātramidaṃ dhātuḥ śarīraṃ kṣetrasaṃjñitam
|
yena kārayate karma śubhāśubhaphalaṃ vibhuḥ||31||

32paśya māyāprabhāvo'yamīśvareṇa yathā kṛtaḥ
|
yo hanti bhūtairbhūtāni mohayitvātmamāyayā||32||

33anyathā paridṛṣṭāni munibhirvedadarśibhiḥ
|
anyathā parivartante vegā iva nabhasvataḥ||33||

34anyathaiva hi manyante puruṣāstāni tāni ca
|
anyathaiva prabhustāni karoti vikaroti ca||34||

35yathā kāṣṭhena vā kāṣṭhamaśmānaṃ cāśmanā punaḥ
|
ayasā cāpyayaśchindyānnirviceṣṭamacetanam||35||

36evaṃ sa bhagavāndevaḥ svayambhūḥ prapitāmahaḥ
|
hinasti bhūtairbhūtāni cchadma kṛtvā yudhiṣṭhira||36||

37samprayojya viyojyāyaṃ kāmakārakaraḥ prabhuḥ
|
krīḍate bhagavanbhūtairbālaḥ krīḍanakairiva||37||

38na mātṛpitṛvadrājandhātā bhūteṣu vartate
|
roṣādiva pravṛtto'yaṃ yathāyamitaro janaḥ||38||

39āryāñcchīlavato dṛṣṭvā hrīmato vṛttikarṣitān
|
anāryānsukhinaścaiva vihvalāniva cintayā||39||

40tavemāmāpadaṃ dṛṣṭvā samṛddhiṃ ca suyodhane
|
dhātāraṃ garhaye pārtha viṣamaṃ yo'nupaśyati||40||

41āryaśāstrātige krūre lubdhe dharmāpacāyini
|
dhārtarāṣṭre śriyaṃ dattvā dhātā kiṃ phalamaśnute||41||

42karma cetkṛtamanveti kartāraṃ nānyamṛcchati
|
karmaṇā tena pāpena lipyate nūnamīśvaraḥ||42||

43atha karmakṛtaṃ pāpaṃ na cetkartāramṛcchati
|
kāraṇaṃ balameveha janāñcchocāmi durbalān||43||
