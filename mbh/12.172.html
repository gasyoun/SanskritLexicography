Mahābhārata 12.172 (Śāntiparvan)

1bhīṣma uvāca|
atha tatra mahārciṣmānanalo vātasārathiḥ
|
tasyāvidūre rakṣārthaṃ khagendreṇa kṛto'bhavat ||1||

2sa cāpi pārśve suṣvāpa viśvasto bakarāṭ tadā
|
kṛtaghnastu sa duṣṭātmā taṃ jighāṃsurathāgrataḥ ||2||

3tato'lātena dīptena niḥśvastaṃ nijaghāna tam
|
nihatya ca mudā yuktaḥ so'nubandhaṃ na dṛṣṭavān ||3||

4sa taṃ vipakṣaromāṇaṃ kṛtvā'gnāvapacattadā
|
taṃ gṛhītvā suvarṇaṃ va yayau drutataraṃ dvijaḥ ||4||

5tato'nyasmin gate cāhni virupākṣo'bravītsutam
|
na prekṣe rājadharmāṇamadya putra khagottamam ||5||

6sa pūrvasaṃdhyāṃ brahmāṇaṃ vandituṃ yāti sarvadā
|
māṃ vā dṛṣṭvā kadācitsa na gacchati gṛhaṃ khagaḥ ||6||

7ubhe dvirātrisaṃdhye vai nābhyagātsa mamālayam
|
tasmānna śuddhyate bhāvo mama sa jñāyatāṃ suhṛt ||7||

8svādhyāyena viyukto hi brahmavarcasavarjitaḥ
|
tadvratastatra me śaṃkā hanyāttaṃ sa dvijādhamaḥ ||8||

9durācārastu durbuddhiriṅgitairlakṣito mayā
|
niṣkṛto dāruṇākāro duṣṭo dasyurivādhamaḥ ||9||

10gautamaḥ sa gatastatra tenodvignaṃ mano mama
|
putra śīghramito gatvā rājadharmaniveśanam ||10||

11jñāyatāṃ sa viśuddhātmā yadi jīvati mā ciram
|
sa evamuktastvarito rakṣobhiḥ sahito yayau ||11||

12nyagrodhaṃ tatra cāpaśyat kaṅkālaṃ rājadharmaṇaḥ
|
sa rudannagamatputro rākṣasendrasya dhīmataḥ ||12||

13tvaramāṇaḥ paraṃ śaktyā gautamagrahaṇāya vai
|
tato'vidūre jagṛhurgautamaṃ rākṣasāstadā ||13||

14rājadharmaśarīraṃ ca pakṣāsthi caraṇojjhitam
|
tamādāyātha rakṣāṃsi drutaṃ meruvrajaṃ yayuḥ ||14||

15rājñaśca darśayāmāsuḥ śarīraṃ rājadharmaṇaḥ
|
kṛtaghnaṃ paruṣaṃ taṃ ca gautamaṃ pāpakāriṇam ||15||

16ruroda rājā taṃ dṛṣṭvā sāmātyaḥ sapurohitaḥ
|
ārtanādaśca sumahānabhūttasya niveśane ||16||

17sastrīkumāraṃ ca puraṃ babhūvāsvasthamānasam
|
athābravīnnṛpaḥ putraṃ pāpo'yaṃ vadhyatāmiti ||17||

18asya māṃsairime sarve viharantu yatheṣṭataḥ
|
pāpācāraḥ pāpakarmā pāpātmā pāpasādhanaḥ ||18||

19hantavyo'yaṃ mama matirbhavadbhiriti rākṣasāḥ
|
ityuktā rākṣasendreṇa rākṣasā ghoravikramāḥ ||19||

20naicchanta taṃ bhakṣayituṃ pāpakarmāṇamityuta
|
dasyūnāṃ dīyatāmeṣa sādhvadya puruṣādhamaḥ ||20||

21ityūcuste mahārāja rākṣasendraṃ niśācarāḥ
|
śirobhiḥ praṇatāḥ sarve vyāharan rākṣasādhipam ||21||

22na dātumarhasi tvaṃ no bhakṣaṇāyāsya kilbiṣam
|
evamastviti tānāha rākṣasendro niśācarān ||22||

23dasyūnāṃ dīyatāmeṣa kṛtaghno'dyaiva rākṣasāḥ
|
ityuktā rākṣasāstena śūlapaṭṭiśapāṇayaḥ ||23||

24kṛtvā taṃ khaṇḍaśaḥ pāpaṃ dasyubhyaḥ pradadustadā
|
dasyavaścāpi naicchanta tamattuṃ pāpakāriṇam
|
kravyādā api rājendra kṛtaghnaṃ nopabhuñjate ||24||

25brahmaghne ca surāpe ca caure bhagnavrate tathā
|
niṣkṛtirvihitā rājan kṛtaghne nāsti niṣkṛtiḥ ||25||

26mitradrohī kṛtaghnaśca nṛśaṃsaśca narādhamaḥ
|
kravyādaiḥ kṛmibhiścaiva na bhujyante hi tādṛśāḥ ||26||
