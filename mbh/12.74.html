Mahābhārata 12.74 (Śāntiparvan)

1bhīṣma uvāca|
yogakṣemo hi rāṣṭrasya rājanyāyatta ucyate
|
yogakṣemo hi rājño hi samāyattaḥ purohite||1||

2yatrādṛṣṭaṃ bhayaṃ brahma prajānāṃ śamayatyuta
|
dṛṣṭaṃ ca rājā bāhubhyāṃ tadrājyaṃ sukhamedhate||2||

3atrāpyudāharantīmamitihāsaṃ purātanam
|
mucukundasya saṃvādaṃ rājño vaiśravaṇasya ca||3||

4mucukundo vijityemāṃ pṛthivīṃ pṛthivīpatiḥ
|
jijñāsamānaḥ svabalamupeyādalakādhipam||4||

5tato vaiśravaṇo rājā rākṣasānasṛjattadā
|
te balānyavamṛdganta mucukundasya nairṛtāḥ||5||

6sa hanyamāne sainye sve mucukundo narādhipaḥ
|
garhayāmāsa vidvāṃsaṃ purohitamarindamaḥ||6||

7tata ugraṃ tapastaptvā vasiṣṭho dharmavittamaḥ
|
rakṣāṃsyupāvadhīttasya panthānaṃ cāpyavindata||7||

8tato vaiśravaṇo rājā mucukundamadarśayat
|
vadhyamāneṣu sainyeṣu vacanaṃ cedamabravīt||8||

9dhanada uvāca| balavantastvayā pūrve rājānaḥ sapurohitāḥ
|
na caivaṃ samavartanta yathā tvamiha vartase||9||

10te khalvapi kṛtāstrāśca balavantaśca bhūmipāḥ
|
āgamya paryupāsante māmīśaṃ sukhaduḥkhayoḥ||10||

11yadyasti bāhuvīryaṃ te taddarśayitumarhasi
|
kiṃ brāhmaṇabalena tvamatimātraṃ pravartase||11||

12mucukundastataḥ kruddhaḥ pratyuvāca dhaneśvaram
|
nyāyapūrvamasaṃrabdhamasambhrāntamidaṃ vacaḥ||12||

13brahmakṣatramidaṃ sṛṣṭamekayoni svayambhuvā
|
pṛthagbalavidhānaṃ tanna lokaṃ paripālayet||13||

14tapo mantrabalaṃ nityaṃ brāhmaṇeṣu pratiṣṭhitam
|
astrabāhubalaṃ nityaṃ kṣatriyeṣu pratiṣṭhitam||14||

15tābhyāṃ sambhūya kartavyaṃ prajānāṃ paripālanam
|
tathā ca māṃ pravartantaṃ kiṃ garhasyalakādhipa||15||

16tato'bravīdvaiśravaṇo rājānaṃ sapurohitam
|
nāhaṃ rājyamanirdiṣṭaṃ kasmaicidvidadhāmyuta||16||

17nācchinde cāpyanirdiṣṭamiti jānīhi pārthiva
|
praśādhi pṛthivīṃ kṛtsnāṃ maddattāmakhilāmimām
|
evamuktaḥ pratyuvāca mucukundo mahīpatiḥ||17||

18mucukunda uvāca| nāhaṃ rājyaṃ bhavaddattaṃ bhoktumicchāmi pārthiva
|
bāhuvīryārjitaṃ rājyamaśnīyāmiti kāmaye||18||

19bhīṣma uvāca|
tato vaiśravaṇo rājā vismayaṃ paramaṃ yayau
|
kṣatradharme sthitaṃ dṛṣṭvā mucukundamasambhramam||19||

20tato rājā mucukundaḥ so'nvaśāsadvasundharām
|
bāhuvīryārjitāṃ samyakkṣatradharmamanuvrataḥ||20||

21evaṃ yo dharmavidrājā brahmapūrvaṃ pravartate
|
jayatyavijitāmurvīṃ yaśaśca mahadaśnute||21||

22nityodakī brāhmaṇaḥ syānnityaśastraśca kṣatriyaḥ
|
tayorhi sarvamāyattaṃ yatkiñcijjagatīgatam||22||
