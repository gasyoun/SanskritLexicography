Mahābhārata 5.89 (Udyogaparvan)

1vaiśampāyana uvāca|
prātarutthāya kṛṣṇastu kṛtavān sarvamāhnikam
|
brāhmaṇairabhyanujñātaḥ prayayau nagaraṃ prati ||1||

2taṃ prayāntaṃ mahābāhumanujñāpya mahābalam
|
paryavartanta te sarve vṛkasthalanivāsinaḥ ||2||

3dhārtarāṣṭrāstamāyāntaṃ pratyujjagmuḥ svalaṅkṛtāḥ
|
duryodhanādṛte sarve bhīṣmadroṇakṛpādayaḥ ||3||

4paurāśca bahulā rājan hṛṣīkeśaṃ didṛkṣavaḥ
|
yānairbahuvidhairanyaiḥ padbhireva tathā pare ||4||

5sa vai pathi samāgamya bhīṣmeṇākliṣṭakarmaṇā
|
droṇena dhārtarāṣṭraiśca tairvṛto nagaraṃ yayau ||5||

6kṛṣṇasammānanārthaṃ ca nagaraṃ samalaṅkṛtam
|
babhūva rājamārgaśca bahuratnasamācitaḥ ||6||

7na ca kaścidgṛhe rājaṃstadā''sīdbharatarṣabha
|
na strī na vṛddho na śiśurvāsudevadidṛkṣayā ||7||

8rājamārge narāstasmin saṃstuvantyavaniṃ gatāḥ
|
tasmin kāle mahārāja hṛṣīkeśapraveśane ||8||

9āvṛtāni varastrībhirgṛhāṇi sumahāntyapi
|
pracalantīva bhāreṇa dṛśyante sma mahītale ||9||

10tathā ca gatimantaste vāsudevasya vājinaḥ
|
pranaṣṭagatayo'bhūvan rājamārge narairvṛte ||10||

11sa gṛhaṃ dhṛtarāṣṭrasya prāviśacchatrukarśanaḥ
|
pāṇḍuraṃ puṇḍarīkākṣaḥ prāsādairupaśobhitam ||11||

12tisraḥ kakṣyā vyatikramya keśavo rājaveśmanaḥ
|
vaicitravīryaṃ rājānamabhyagacchadarindamaḥ ||12||

13abhyāgacchati dāśārhe prajñācakṣurnarādhipaḥ
|
sahaiva droṇabhīṣmābhyāmudatiṣṭhanmahāyaśāḥ ||13||

14kṛpaśca somadattaśca mahārājaśca bāhlikaḥ
|
āsanebhyo'calan sarve pūjayanto janārdanam ||14||

15tato rājānamāsādya dhṛtarāṣṭraṃ yaśasvinam
|
sa bhīṣmaṃ pūjayāmāsa vārṣṇeyo vāgbhirañjasā ||15||

16teṣu dharmānupūrvīṃ tāṃ prayujya madhusūdanaḥ
|
yathāvayaḥ samīyāya rājabhiḥ saha mādhavaḥ ||16||

17atha droṇaṃ sabāhlīkaṃ saputraṃ ca yaśasvinam
|
kṛpaṃ ca somadattaṃ ca samīyāya janārdanaḥ ||17||

18tatrāsīdūrjitaṃ mṛṣṭaṃ kāñcanaṃ mahadāsanam
|
śāsanāddhṛtarāṣṭrasya tatropāviśadacyutaḥ ||18||

19atha gāṃ madhuparkaṃ cāpyudakaṃ ca janārdane
|
upajahruryathānyāyaṃ dhṛtarāṣṭrapurohitāḥ ||19||

20kṛtātithyastu govindaḥ sarvān parihasan kurūn
|
āste sāmbandhikaṃ kurvan kurubhiḥ parivāritaḥ ||20||

21so'rcito dhṛtarāṣṭreṇa pūjitaśca mahāyaśāḥ
|
rājānaṃ samanujñāpya nirakrāmadarindamaḥ ||21||

22taiḥ sametya yathānyāyaṃ kurubhiḥ kurusaṃsadi
|
vidurāvasathaṃ ramyamupātiṣṭhata mādhavaḥ ||22||

23viduraḥ sarvakalyāṇairabhigamya janārdanam
|
arcayāmāsa dāśārhaṃ sarvakāmairupasthitam ||23||

24yā me prītiḥ puṣkarākṣa tvaddarśanasamudbhavā
|
sā kimākhyāyate tubhyamantarātmā'si dehinām ||24||

25kṛtātithyaṃ tu govindaṃ viduraḥ sarvadharmavit
|
kuśalaṃ pāṇḍuputrāṇāmapṛcchanmadhusūdanam ||25||

26prīyamāṇasya suhṛdo viduro buddhisattamaḥ
|
dharmārthanityasya sato gataroṣasya dhīmataḥ ||26||

27tasya sarvaṃ savistāraṃ pāṇḍavānāṃ viceṣṭitam
|
kṣatturācaṣṭa dāśārhaḥ sarvaṃ pratyakṣadarśivān ||27||
