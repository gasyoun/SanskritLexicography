Mahābhārata 14.11 (Āśvamedhikaparvan)

1vaiśampāyana uvāca|
ityukte nṛpatau tasmin vyāsenādbhutakarmaṇā
|
vāsudevo mahātejāstato vacanamādade||1||

2taṃ nṛpaṃ dīnamanasaṃ nihatajñātibāndhavam
|
upaplutamivādityaṃ sadhūmamiva pāvakam||2||

3nirviṇṇamanasaṃ pārthaṃ jñātvā vṛṣṇikulodvahaḥ
|
āśvāsayan dharmasutaṃ pravaktumupacakrame||3||

4vāsudeva uvāca|
sarvaṃ jihyaṃ mṛtyupadamārjavaṃ brahmaṇaḥ padam
|
etāvān jñānaviṣayaḥ kiṃ pralāpaḥ kariṣyati||4||

5naiva te niṣṭhitaṃ karma naiva te śatravo jitāḥ
|
kathaṃ śatruṃ śarīrasthamātmano nāvabudhyase||5||

6atra te vartayiṣyāmi yathādharmaṃ yathāśrutam
|
indrasya saha vṛtreṇa yathā yuddhamavartata||6||

7vṛtreṇa pṛthivī vyāptā purā kila narādhipa
|
dṛṣṭvā sa pṛthivīṃ vyāptāṃ gandhasya viṣaye hate||7||

8dharāharaṇadurgandho viṣayaḥ samapadyata
|
śatakratuścukopātha gandhasya viṣaye hṛte||8||

9vṛtrasya sa tataḥ kruddho ghoraṃ vajramavāsṛjat
|
sa vadhyamāno vajreṇa subhṛśaṃ bhūritejasā||9||

10viveśa sahasā toyaṃ jagrāha viṣayaṃ tataḥ
|
apsu vṛtragṛhītāsu rase ca viṣaye hṛte||10||

11śatakraturatikruddhastatra vajramavāsṛjat
|
sa vadhyamāno vajreṇa tasminnamitatejasā||11||

12viveśa sahasā jyotirjagrāha viṣayaṃ tataḥ
|
vyāpte jyotiṣi vṛtreṇa rūpe'tha viṣaye hṛte||12||

13śatakraturatikruddhastatra vajramavāsṛjat
|
sa vadhyamāno vajreṇa tasminnamitatejā||13||

14viveśa sahasā vāyuṃ jagrāha viṣayaṃ tataḥ
|
vyāpte vāyau tu vṛtreṇa sparśe'tha viṣaye hṛte||14||

15śatakraturatikruddhastatra vajramavāsṛjat
|
sa vadhyamāno vajreṇa tasminnamitatejasā||15||

16ākāśamabhidudrāva jagrāha viṣayaṃ tataḥ
|
ākāśe vṛtrabhūte'tha śabde ca viṣaye hṛte||16||

17śatakraturabhikruddhastatra vajramavāsṛjat
|
sa vadhyamāno vajreṇa tasminnamitatejasā||17||

18viveśa sahasā śakraṃ jagrāha viṣayaṃ tataḥ
|
tasya vṛtragṛhītasya mohaḥ samabhavanmahān||18||

19rathantareṇa taṃ tāta vasiṣṭhaḥ pratyabodhayat
|
tato vṛtraṃ śarīrasthaṃ jaghāna bharatarṣabha
|
śatakraturadṛśyena vajreṇetīha naḥ śrutam||19||

20idaṃ dharmyaṃ rahasyaṃ vai śakreṇoktaṃ maharṣiṣu
|
ṛṣibhiśca mama proktaṃ tannibodha janādhipa||20||
