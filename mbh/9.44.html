Mahābhārata 9.44 (Śalyaparvan)

1janamejaya uvāca|
sarasvatyāḥ prabhāvo'yamuktaste dvijasattama
|
kumārasyābhiṣekaṃ tu brahman vyākhyātumarhasi||1||

2yasmindeśe ca kāle ca yathā ca vadatāṃ vara
|
yaiścābhiṣikto bhagavānvidhinā yena ca prabhuḥ||2||

3skando yathā ca daityānāmakarotkadanaṃ mahat
|
tathā me sarvāmācakṣva paraṃ kautūhalaṃ hi me||3||

4vaiśampāyana uvāca|
kuruvaṃśasya sadṛśaṃ kautūhalamidaṃ tava
|
harṣamutpādayatyeva vaco me janamejaya||4||

5hanta te kathayiṣyāmi śṛṇvānasya narādhipa
|
abhiṣekaṃ kumārasya prabhāvaṃ ca mahātmanaḥ||5||

6tejo māheśvaraṃ skannamagnau prapatitaṃ purā
|
tatsarvabhakṣo bhagavānnāśakaddagdhumakṣayam||6||

7tenāsīdatitejasvī dīptimān havyavāhanaḥ
|
na caiva dhārayāmāsa garbhaṃ tejomayaṃ tadā||7||

8sa gaṅgāmabhisaṅgamya niyogādbrahmaṇaḥ prabhuḥ
|
garbhamāhitavān divyaṃ bhāskaropamatejasam||8||

9atha gaṅgāpi taṃ garbhamasahantī vidhāraṇe
|
utsasarja girau ramye himavatmarārcite||9||

10sa tatra vavṛdhe lokānāvṛtya jvalanātmajaḥ
|
dadṛśurjvalanākāraṃ taṃ garbhamatha kṛttikāḥ||10||

11śarastambe mahātmānamanalātmajamīśvaram
|
mamāyamiti tāḥ sarvāḥ putrārthinyo'bhicukruśuḥ||11||

12tāsāṃ viditvā bhāvaṃ taṃ mātṝṇāṃ bhagavānprabhuḥ
|
prasnutānāṃ payaḥ ṣaḍbhirvadanairapibattadā||12||

13taṃ prabhāvaṃ samālakṣya tasya bālasya kṛttikāḥ
|
paraṃ vismayamāpannā devyo divyavapurdharāḥ||13||

14yatrotsṛṣṭaḥ sa bhagavān gaṅgayā girimūrdhani
|
sa śailakāñcanaḥ sarvaḥ sambabhau kurusattama||14||

15vardhatā caiva garbheṇa pṛthivī tena rañjitā
|
ataśca sarve saṃvṛttā girayaḥ kāñcanākarāḥ||15||

16kumāraḥ sumahāvīryaḥ kārtikeya iti smṛtaḥ
|
gāṅgeyaḥ pūrvamabhavanmahāyogabalānvitaḥ||16||

17śamena tapasā caiva vīryeṇa ca samanvitaḥ
|
vavṛdhe'tīva rājendra candravatpriyadarśanaḥ||17||

18sa tasminkāñcane divye śarastambhe śriyā vṛtaḥ
|
stūyamānaḥ sadā śete gandharvairmunibhistathā||18||

19tathaitamanvanṛtyantaḥ devakanyāḥ sahasraśaḥ
|
divyavāditranṛtyajñāḥ stuvantyaścārudarśanāḥ||19||

20anvāste ca nadī devaṃ gaṅgā vai saritāṃ varā
|
dadhāra pṛthivī cainaṃ bibhratī rūpamuttamam||20||

21jātakarmādikāstatra kriyāścakre bṛhaspatiḥ
|
vedaścainaṃ caturmūrtirupatasthe kṛtāñjaliḥ||21||

22dhanurvedaścatuṣpādaḥ śastragrāmaḥ sasaṅgrahaḥ
|
tatrainaṃ samupātiṣṭhatsākṣādvāṇī ca kevalā||22||

23sa dadarśa mahāvīryaṃ devadevamumāpatim
|
śailaputryā samāsīnaṃ bhūtasaṅghaśatairvṛtam||23||

24nikāyā bhūtasaṅghānāṃ paramādbhutadarśanāḥ
|
vikṛtā vikṛtākārā vikṛtābharaṇadhvajāḥ||24||

25vyāghrasiṃharkṣavadanā biḍālamakarānanāḥ
|
vṛṣadaṃśamukhāścānye gajoṣṭravadanāstathā||25||

26ulūkavadanāḥ kecidgṛdhragomāyudarśanāḥ
|
krauñcapārāvatanibhairvadanai rāṅkavairapi||26||

27śvāvicchalyakagodhānāmajaiḍakagavāṃ tathā
|
sadṛśāni vapūṃṣyanye tatra tatra vyadhārayan||27||

28kecicchailāmbudaprakhyāścakrodyatagadāyudhāḥ
|
kecidañjanapuñjābhāḥ kecicchvetācalaprabhāḥ||28||

29sapta mātṛgaṇāścaiva samājagmurviśāmpate
|
sādhyā viśve'tha maruto vasavaḥ pitarastathā||29||

30rudrādityāstathā siddhā bhujagā dānavāḥ khagāḥ
|
brahmā svayambhūbhagavān saputraḥ saha viṣṇunā||30||

31śakrastathābhyayāddraṣṭuṃ kumāravaramacyutam
|
nāradapramukhāścāpi devagandharvasattamāḥ||31||

32devarṣayaśca siddhāśca bṛhaspatipurogamāḥ
|
pitaro jagataḥ śreṣṭhā devānāmapi devatāḥ||32||

33te'pi tatra samājagmuryāmā dhāmāśca sarvaśaḥ
|
sa tu bālo'pi balavānmahāyogabalānvitaḥ||33||

34abhyājagāma deveśaṃ śūlahastaṃ pinākinam
|
tamāvrajantamālakṣya śivasyāsīnmanogatam||34||

35yugapacchailaputryāśca gaṅgāyāḥ pāvakasya ca
|
kaṃ nu pūrvamayaṃ bālo gauravādabhyupaiṣyati||35||

36api māmiti sarveṣāṃ teṣāmāsīnmanogatam
|
teṣāmetamabhiprāyaṃ caturṇāmupalakṣya saḥ||36||

37yugapadyogamāsthāya sasarja vividhāstanūḥ
|
tato'bhavaccaturmūtiḥ kṣaṇena bhagavānprabhuḥ||37||

38tasya śākho viśākhaśca naigameyaśca pṛṣṭhataḥ
|
evaṃ sa kṛtvā hyātmānaṃ caturdhā bhagavānprabhuḥ||38||

39yato rudrastataḥ skando gajāmādbhutadarśanaḥ
|
viśākhastu yayau yena devī girīvarātmajā||39||

40śākho yayau sa bhagavān vāyumūrtirvibhāvasum
|
naigameyo'gamadgaṅgāṃ kumāraḥ pāvakaprabhaḥ||40||

41sarve bhāsuradehāste catvāraḥ samarūpiṇaḥ
|
tānsamabhyayuravyagrāstadadbhutamivābhavat||41||

42hāhākāro mahānāsīddevadānavarakṣasām
|
taddṛṣṭvā mahadāścaryamadbhūtaṃ lomaharṣaṇam||42||

43tato rudraśca devīṃ ca pāvakaśca pitāmaham
|
gaṅgayā sahitāḥ sarve praṇipeturjagatpatim||43||

44praṇipatya tataste tu vidhivadrājapuṅgava
|
idamūcurvaco rājan kārtikeyapriyepsayā||44||

45asya bālasya bhagavannādhipatyaṃ yathepsitam
|
asmātpriyārthaṃ deveśa sadṛśaṃ dātumarhasi||45||

46tataḥ sa bhagavān dhīmān sarvalokapitāmahaḥ
|
manasā cintayāmāsa kimayaṃ labhatāmiti||46||

47aiśvaryāṇi ca sarvāṇi devagandharvarakṣasām
|
bhūtayakṣavihaṅgānāṃ pannagānāṃ ca sarvaśaḥ||47||

48pūrvamevādideśāsau nikāyeṣu mahātmanām
|
samarthaṃ ca tamaiśvarye mahāmatiramanyata||48||

49tato muhūrtaṃ sa dhyātvā devānāṃ śreyasi sthitaḥ
|
saināpatyaṃ dadau tasmai sarvabhūteṣu bhārata||49||

50sarvadevanikāyānāṃ ye rājānaḥ pariśrutāḥ
|
tān sarvānvyādideśāsmai sarvabhūtapitāmahaḥ||50||

51tataḥ kumāramādāya devā brahmapurogamāḥ
|
abhiṣekārthamājagmuḥ śailendraṃ sahitāstataḥ||51||

52puṇyāṃ haimavatīṃ devīṃ saricchreṣṭhāṃ sarasvatīm
|
samantapañcake yā vai triṣu lokeṣu viśrutā||52||

53tatra tīre sarasvatyāḥ puṇye sarvaguṇānvite
|
niṣedurdevagandharvāḥ sarve sampūrṇamānasāḥ||53||
