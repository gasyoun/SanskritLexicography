Mahābhārata 2.22 (Sabhāparvan)

1jarāsandha uvāca|
na smarāmi kadā vairaṃ kṛtaṃ yuṣmābhirityuta
|
cintayaṃśca na paśyāmi bhavatāṃ prati vaikṛtam||1||

2vaikṛte vā sati kathaṃ manyadhvaṃ māmanāgasam
|
ariṃ vai brūta he viprāḥ satāṃ samaya eṣa hi||2||

3atha dharmopaghātāddhi manaḥ samupatapyate
|
yo'nāgasi prasajati kṣatriyo hi na saṃśayaḥ||3||

4ato'nyathā cara~lloke dharmajñaḥ sanmahārathaḥ
|
vṛjināṃ gatimāpnoti śreyaso'pyupahanti ca||4||

5trailokye kṣatradharmo hi śreyānvai sādhucāriṇām
|
nānyaṃ dharmaṃ praśaṃsanti ye ca dharmavido janāḥ||5||

6tasya medya sthitasyeha svadharme niyatātmanaḥ
|
anāgasaṃ prajānāṃ ca pramādādiva jalpatha||6||

7kṛṣṇa uvāca|
kulakāryaṃ mahābāho kaścidekaḥ kulodvahaḥ
|
vahate yastanniyogādvayamabhyudyatāstvayi||7||

8tvayā copahṛtā rājankṣatriyā lokavāsinaḥ
|
tadāgaḥ krūramutpādya manyase kimanāgasam||8||

9rājā rājñaḥ kathaṃ sādhūnhiṃsyānnṛpatisattama
|
tadrājñaḥ sannigṛhya tvaṃ rudrāyopajihīrṣasi||9||

10asmāṃstadeno gaccheddhi kṛtaṃ bārhadratha tvayā
|
vayaṃ hi śaktā dharmasya rakṣaṇe dharmacāriṇaḥ||10||

11manuṣyāṇāṃ samālambho na ca dṛṣṭaḥ kadācana
|
sa kathaṃ mānuṣairdevaṃ yaṣṭumicchasi śaṅkaram||11||

12savarṇo hi savarṇānāṃ paśusañjñāṃ kariṣyasi
|
ko'nyaṃ evaṃ yathā hi tvaṃ jarāsandha vṛthāmatiḥ||12||

13yasyāṃ yasyāmavasthāyāṃ yadyatkarma karoti yaḥ
|
tasyāṃ tasyāmavasthāyāṃ tatphalaṃ samavāpnuyāt||13||

14te tvāṃ jñātikṣayakaraṃ vayamārtānusāriṇaḥ
|
jñātivṛddhinimittārthaṃ vinihantumihāgatāḥ||14||

15nāsti loke pumānanyaḥ kṣatriyeṣviti caiva tat
|
manyase sa ca te rājansumahānbuddhiviplavaḥ||15||

16ko hi jānannabhijanamātmavānkṣatriyo nṛpa
|
nāviśetsvargamatulaṃ raṇānantaramavyayam||16||

17svargaṃ hyeva samāsthāya raṇayajñeṣu dīkṣitāḥ
|
jayanti kṣatriyā lokāṃstadviddhi manujarṣabha||17||

18svargayonirmahadbrahma svargayonirmahadyaśaḥ
|
svargaṃ yonistapo yuddhe mṛtyuḥ so'vyabhicāravān||18||

19eṣa hyaindro vaijayanto guṇairnityaṃ samāhitaḥ
|
yenāsurānparājitya jagatpāti śatakratuḥ||19||

20svargamārgāya kasya syādvigraho vai yathā tava
|
māgadhairvipulaiḥ sainyairbāhulyabaladarpitaḥ||20||

21māvamaṃsthāḥ parānrājannāsti vīryaṃ nare nare
|
samaṃ tejastvayā caiva viśiṣṭaṃ vā nareśvara||21||

22yāvadetadasambuddhaṃ tāvadeva bhavettava
|
viṣahyametadasmākamato rājanbravīmi te||22||

23jahi tvaṃ sadṛśeṣveva mānaṃ darpaṃ ca māgadha
|
māgamaḥ sasutāmātyaḥ sabalaśca yamakṣayam||23||

24dambhodbhavaḥ kārtavīrya uttaraśca bṛhadrathaḥ
|
śreyaso hyavamanyeha vineśuḥ sabalā nṛpāḥ||24||

25yuyukṣamāṇāstvatto hi na vayaṃ brāhmaṇā dhruvam
|
śaurirasmi hṛṣīkeśo nṛvīrau pāṇḍavāvimau
|
anayormātuleyaṃ ca kṛṣṇaṃ māṃ viddhi te ripum||25||

26tvāmāhvayāmahe rājansthiro yudhvasva māgadha
|
muñca vā nṛpatīnsarvān gaccha vā tvaṃ yamakṣayam||26||

27jarāsandha uvāca|
nājitānvai narapatīnahamādadmi kāṃścana
|
ajitaḥ paryavasthātā ko'tra yo na mayā jitaḥ||27||

28kṣatriyasyaitadevāhurdharmyaṃ kṛṣṇopajīvanam
|
vikramya vaśamānīya kāmato yatsamācaret||28||

29devātārthamupāhṛtya rājñaḥ kṛṣṇa kathaṃ bhayāt
|
ahamadya vimucyeyaṃ kṣātraṃ vratamanusmaran||29||

30sainyaṃ sainyena vyūḍhena eka ekena vā punaḥ
|
dvābhyāṃ tribhirvā yotsye'haṃ yugatpṛthageva vā||30||

31vaiśampāya uvāca|
evamuktvā jarāsandhaḥ sahadevābhiṣecanam
|
ājñāpayattadā rājā yayutsurbhīmakarmabhiḥ||31||

32sa tu senāpatiṃ rājā sasmāra bharatarṣabha
|
kauśikaṃ citrasenaṃ ca tasminyuddha upasthite||32||

33yayoste nāmanī rājanhaṃseti ḍimbhaketi ca
|
pūrvaṃ saṅkathitaṃ pumbhirnṛloke lokasatkṛte||33||

34taṃ tu rājanvibhuḥ śaurī rājānaṃ balināṃ varam
|
smṛtvā puruṣaśārdūlaḥ śārdūlasamavikramam||34||

35satyasandho jarāsandhaṃ bhuvi bhīmaparākramam
|
bhāgamanyasya nirdiṣṭamavadhyaṃ madhurbhirmṛdheḥ||35||

36nātmanā''tmavatāṃ mukhya iyeṣa madhusūdanaḥ
|
brāhmīmājñāṃ puraskṛtya hantuṃ haladharānujaḥ||36||
