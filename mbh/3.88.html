Mahābhārata 3.88 (Āraṇyakaparvan)

1dhaumya uvāca| dakṣiṇasyāṃ tu puṇyāni śṛṇu tīrthāni bhārata
|
vistareṇa yathābuddhi kīrtyamānāni tāni vai||1||

2yasyāmākhyāyate puṇyā diśi godāvarī nadī
|
bahvārāmā bahujalā tāpasācaritā śivā||2||

3veṇā bhīmarathī caiva nadyau pāpabhayāpahe
|
mṛgadvijasamākīrṇe tāpasālayabhūṣite||3||

4rājarṣestasya ca sarinnṛgasya bharatarṣabha
|
ramyatīrthā bahujalā payoṣṇī dvijasevitā||4||

5api cātra mahāyogī mārkaṇḍeyo mahāyaśāḥ
|
anuvaṃśyāṃ jagau gāthāṃ nṛgasya dharaṇīpateḥ||5||

6nṛgasya yajamānasya pratyakṣamiti naḥ śrutam
|
amādyadindraḥ somena dakṣiṇābhirdvijātayaḥ||6||

7payoṣṇyāṃ yajamānasya vārāhe tīrtha uttame
|
uddhṛtaṃ bhūtalasthaṃ vā vāyunā samudīritam
|
payoṣṇyā harate toyaṃ pāpamāmaraṇāntikam||7||

8svargāduttuṅgamamalaṃ viṣāṇaṃ yatra śūlinaḥ
|
svamātmavihitaṃ dṛṣṭvā martyaḥ śivapuraṃ vrajet||8||

9ekataḥ saritaḥ sarvā gaṅgādyāḥ saliloccayāḥ
|
payoṣṇī caikataḥ puṇyā tīrthebhyo hi matā mama||9||

10māṭharasya vanaṃ puṇyaṃ bahumūlaphalaṃ śivam
|
yūpaśca bharataśreṣṭha varuṇasrotase girau||10||

11praveṇyuttaramārge tu puṇye kaṇvāśrame tathā
|
tāpasānāmaraṇyāni kīrtitāni yathāśruti||11||

12vedī śūrpārake tāta jamadagnermahātmanaḥ
|
ramyā pāṣāṇatīrthā ca puraścandrā ca bhārata||12||

13aśokatīrthaṃ tatraiva kaunteya bahulāśramam
|
agastyatīrthaṃ pāṇḍyeṣu vāruṇaṃ ca yudhiṣṭhira||13||

14kumāryaḥ kathitāḥ puṇyāḥ pāṇḍyeṣveva nararṣabha
|
tāmraparṇīṃ tu kaunteya kīrtayiṣyāmi tāṃ śṛṇu||14||

15yatra devaistapastaptaṃ mahadicchadbhirāśrame
|
gokarṇa iti vikhyātastriṣu lokeṣu bhārata||15||

16śītatoyo bahujalaḥ puṇyastāta śivaḥ śubhaḥ
|
hradaḥ paramaduṣprāpo mānuṣairakṛtātmabhiḥ||16||

17tatra vṛkṣatṛṇādyaiśca saṃpannaḥ phalamūlavān
|
āśramo'gastyaśiṣyasya puṇyo devasamo giriḥ||17||

18vaiḍhūryaparvatastatra śrīmānmaṇimayaḥ śivaḥ
|
agastyasyāśramaścaiva bahumūlaphalodakaḥ||18||

19surāṣṭreṣvapi vakṣyāmi puṇyānyāyatanāni ca
|
āśramānsaritaścaiva sarāṃsi ca narādhipa||19||

20camasodbhedanaṃ viprāstatrāpi kathayantyuta
|
prabhāsaṃ codadhau tīrthaṃ tridaśānāṃ yudhiṣṭhira||20||

21tatra piṇḍārakaṃ nāma tāpasācaritaṃ śivam
|
ujjayantaśca śikharī kṣipraṃ siddhikaro mahān||21||

22tatra devarṣivīreṇa nāradenānukīrtitaḥ
|
purāṇaḥ śrūyate ślokastaṃ nibodha yudhiṣṭhira||22||

23puṇye girau surāṣṭreṣu mṛgapakṣiniṣevite
|
ujjayante sma taptāṅgo nākapṛṣṭhe mahīyate||23||

24puṇyā dvāravatī tatra yatrāsau madhusūdanaḥ
|
sākṣāddevaḥ purāṇosau sa hi dharmaḥ sanātanaḥ||24||

25ye ca vedavido viprā ye cādhyātmavido janāḥ
|
te vadanti mahātmānaṃ kṛṣṇaṃ dharmaṃ sanātanam||25||

26pavitrāṇāṃ hi govindaḥ pavitraṃ paramucyate
|
puṇyānāmapi puṇyo'sau maṅgālānāṃ ca maṅgalam
|
trailokye puṇḍarīkākṣo devadevaḥ sanātanaḥ||26||

27avyayātmā vyayātmā ca kṣetrajñaḥ parameśvaraḥ
|
āste hariracintyātmā tatraiva madhusūdanaḥ||27||
