Mahābhārata 8.85 (Karṇaparvan)

1sañjaya uvāca|
nakulamatha viditvā chinnabāṇāsanāsiṃ virathamariśarārtaṃ karṇaputrāstrabhagnam
|
pavanadhutapatākāhrādino valgitāśvā varapuruṣaniyuktāste rathaiḥ śīghramīyuḥ ||1||

2drupadasutavariṣṭhāḥ pañca śaineyaṣaṣṭhā drupadaduhitṛputrāḥ pañca cāmitrasāhāḥ
|
dviradarathanarāśvānsūdayantastvadīyān bhujagapatinikāśairmārgaṇairāttaśastrāḥ ||2||

3atha tava rathamukhyāstānpratīyustvarantaḥ kṛpahṛdikasutau ca drauṇiduryodhanau ca
|
śakunisutavṛkau ca krāthadevāvṛdhau ca dviradajala‌ghoṣaiḥ syandanaiḥ kārmukaiśca ||3||

4tava nṛparathivīrāstāndaśaikaṃ ca vīrānnṛvaraśaravarāgraistāḍayanto'bhyarundhan
|
navajaladasavarṇairhastibhistānudīyurgiriśikharanikāśairbhīmavegaiḥ kulindāḥ ||4||

5sukalpitā haimavatā madotkaṭā raṇābhikāmaiḥ kṛtibhiḥ samāsthitāḥ
|
suvarṇajālairvitatā babhurgajāstathā yathā khe jaladāḥ savidyutaḥ ||5||

6kulindaputro daśabhirmahāyasaiḥ kṛpaṃ sasūtāśvamapīḍayadbhṛśam
|
tataḥ śaradvatsutasāyakairhataḥ sahaiva nāgena papāta bhūtale ||6||

7kulindaputrāvarajastu tomarairdivākarāṃśupratimairayasmayaiḥ
|
rathaṃ ca vikṣobhya nanāda nardatastato'sya gāndhārapatiḥ śiro'harat ||7||

8tataḥ kulindeṣu hateṣu teṣvatha prahṛṣṭarūpāstava te mahārathāḥ
|
bhṛśaṃ pradadhmurlavaṇāmbusaṃbhavān parāṃśca bāṇāsanapāṇayo'bhyayuḥ ||8||

9athābhavadyuddhamatīva dāruṇaṃ punaḥ kurūṇāṃ saha pāṇḍusṛñjayaiḥ
|
śarāsiśaktyṛṣṭigadāparaśvadhairnarāśvanāgāsuharaṃ bhṛśākulam ||9||

10rathāśvamātaṅgapadātibhistataḥ parasparaṃ viprahatāḥ patan kṣitau
|
yathā savidyutstanitā balāhakāḥ samāhatā digbhya ivogramārutaiḥ ||10||

11tataḥ śatānīkamatān mahāgajāṃstathā rathānpattigaṇāṃśca tān bahūn
|
jaghāna bhojastu hayānathāpatan kṣaṇādviśastāḥ kṛtavarmaṇaḥ śaraiḥ ||11||

12athāpare drauṇihatā mahādvipāstrayaḥ sasarvāyudhayodhaketanāḥ
|
nipetururvyāṃ vyasavo nipātitāstathā yathā vajrahatā mahācalāḥ ||12||

13kulindarājāvarajādanantaraḥ stanāntare patrivarairatāḍayat
|
tavātmajaṃ tasya tavātmajaḥ śaraiḥ śitaiḥ śarīraṃ vyahanaddvipaṃ ca tam ||13||

14sa nāgarājaḥ saha rājasūnunā papāta raktaṃ bahu sarvataḥ kṣaran
|
mahendravajraprahato'mbudāgame yathā jalaṃ gairikaparvatastathā ||14||

15kulindaputraprahito'paro dvipaḥ krāthaṃ sasūtāśvarathaṃ vyapothayat
|
tato'patatkrāthaśarābhighātitaḥ saheśvaro vajrahato yathā giriḥ ||15||

16rathī dvipasthena hato'pataccharaiḥ krāthādhipaḥ parvatajena durjayaḥ
|
savājisūteṣvasanadhvajastathā yathā mahāvātahato mahādrumaḥ ||16||

17vṛko dvipasthaṃ girirājavāsinaṃ bhṛśaṃ śarairdvādaśabhiḥ parābhinat
|
tato vṛkaṃ sāśvarathaṃ mahādvipo drutaṃ caturbhiścaraṇairvyapothayat ||17||

18sa nāgarājaḥ saniyantṛko'patattathā hato babhrusuteṣubhirbhṛśam
|
sa cāpi devāvṛdhasūnurarditaḥ papāta nunnaḥ sahadevasūnunā ||18||

19viṣāṇagātrāvarayodhapātinā gajena hantuṃ śakuniṃ kulindajaḥ
|
jagāma vegena bhṛśārdayaṃśca taṃ tato'sya gāndhārapatiḥ śiro'harat ||19||

20tataḥ śatānīkahatā mahāgajā hayā rathāḥ pattigaṇāśca tāvakāḥ
|
suparṇavātaprahatā yathoragāstathāgatā gāṃ vivaśā vicūrṇitāḥ ||20||

21tato'bhyaviddhyadbahubhiḥ śitaiḥ śaraiḥ kaliṅgaputro nakulātmajaṃ smayan
|
tato'sya kopādvicakarta nākuliḥ śiraḥ kṣureṇāmbujasannibhānanam ||21||

22tataḥ śatānīkamavidhyadāyasaistribhiḥ śaraiḥ karṇasuto'rjunaṃ tribhiḥ
|
tribhiśca bhīmaṃ nakulaṃ ca saptabhirjanārdanaṃ dvādaśabhiśca sāyakaiḥ ||22||

23tadasya karmātimanuṣyakarmaṇaḥ samīkṣya hṛṣṭāḥ kuravo'bhyapūjayan
|
parākramajñāstu dhanañjayasya ye huto'yamagnāviti te tu menire ||23||

24tataḥ kirīṭī paravīraghātī hatāśvamālokya narapravīraḥ
|
mādrīsutaṃ nakulaṃ lokamadhye samīkṣya kṛṣṇaṃ bhṛśavikṣataṃ ca ||24||

25samabhyadhāvadvṛṣasenamāhave sa sūtajasya pramukhe sthitastadā
|
tamāpatantaṃ naravīramugraṃ mahāhave bāṇasahasradhāriṇam ||25||

26abhyāpatatkarṇasuto mahārathaṃ yathā mahendraṃ namuciḥ purā tathā
|
tato drutaṃ caikaśareṇa pārthaṃ śitena viddhvā yudhi karṇaputraḥ ||26||

27nanāda nādaṃ sumahānubhāvo viddhveva śakraṃ namuciḥ sa vīraḥ
|
punaḥ sa pārthaṃ vṛṣasena ugrairbāṇairaviddhyadbhujamūle tu savye ||27||

28tathaiva kṛṣṇaṃ navabhiḥ samārdayat punaśca pārthaṃ daśabhirjaghāna
|
pūrvaṃ yathā vṛṣasenaprayuktairabhyāhataḥ śvetahayaḥ śaraistaiḥ ||28||

29saṃrambhamīṣadgamito vadhāya karṇātmajasyātha manaḥ pradadhre
|
tataḥ kirīṭī raṇamūrdhni kopāt kṛtvā triśākhāṃ bhrukuṭiṃ lalāṭe ||29||

30mumoca tūrṇaṃ viśikhānmahātmā vadhe dhṛtaḥ karṇasutasya saṃkhye
|
āraktanetro'ntakaśatruhantā uvāca karṇaṃ bhṛśamutsmayaṃstadā ||30||

31duryodhanaṃ drauṇimukhāṃśca sarvānahaṃ raṇe vṛṣasenaṃ tamugram
|
saṃpaśyataḥ karṇaṃ tavādya saṃkhye nayāmi lokaṃ niśitaiḥ pṛṣatkaiḥ ||31||

32ūnaṃ ca tāvaddhi janā vadanti sarvairbhavadbhirmama sūnurhato'sau
|
eko ratho madvihīnastarasvī ahaṃ haniṣye bhavatāṃ samakṣam ||32||

33saṃrakṣyatāṃ rathasaṃsthāḥ suto'yamahaṃ haniṣye vṛṣasenamugram
|
paścādvadhiṣye tvāmapi saṃpramūḍhamahaṃ haniṣye'rjuna ājimadhye ||33||

34tamadya mūlaṃ kalahasya saṃkhye duryodhanāpāśrayajātadarpam
|
tvāmadya hantā'smi raṇe prasahya asyaiva hantā yudhi bhīmasenaḥ ||34||

35duryodhanasyādhamapūruṣasya yasyānayādeṣa mahān kṣayo'bhavat
|
sa evamuktvā vinimṛjya cāpaṃ lakṣyaṃ hi kṛtvā vṛṣasenamājau ||35||

36sasarja bāṇānviśikhānmahātmā vadhāya rājan karṇasutasya saṃkhye
|
vivyādha cainaṃ daśabhiḥ pṛṣatkairmarmasvaśaṅkaṃ prahasankirīṭī ||36||

37ciccheda cāsyeṣvasanaṃ bhujau ca kṣuraiścaturbhirniśitaiḥ śiraśca
|
sa pārthabāṇābhihataḥ papāta rathādvibāhurviśirā dharāyām ||37||

38supuṣpito vṛkṣavaro'tikāyo vāteritaḥ śāla ivādriśṛṅgāt
|
saṃprekṣya bāṇābhihataṃ patantaṃ rathātsutaṃ sūtajaḥ kṣiprakārī ||38||

39rathaṃ rathenāśu jagāma roṣāt kirīṭinaḥ putravadhābhitaptaḥ
|
tataḥ samakṣaṃ svasutaṃ vilokya karṇo hataṃ śvetahayena saṃkhye
|
saṃrambhamāgamya paraṃ mahātmā kṛṣṇārjunau sahasaivābhyadhāvat ||39||
