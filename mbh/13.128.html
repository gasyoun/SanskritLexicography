Mahābhārata 13.128 (Anuśāsanaparvan)

1vāyuruvāca|
kiñciddharmaṃ pravakṣyāmi mānuṣāṇāṃ sukhāvaham
|
sarahasyāśca ye doṣāstān śṛṇudhvaṃ samāhitāḥ||1||

2agnikāryaṃ ca kartavyaṃ paramānnena bhojanam
|
dīpakaścāpi kartavyaḥ pitṝṇāṃ satilodakaḥ||2||

3etena vidhinā martyaḥ śraddadhānaḥ samāhitaḥ
|
caturo vārṣikān māsān yo dadāti tilodakam ||3||

4bhojanaṃ ca yathāśaktyā brāhmaṇe vedapārage
|
paśubandhaśatasyeha phalaṃ prāpnoti puṣkalam||4||

5idaṃ caivāparaṃ guhyamapraśastaṃ nibodhata
|
agnestu vṛṣalo netā havirmūḍhāśca yoṣitaḥ ||5||

6manyate dharma eveti sa cādharmeṇa lipyate
|
agnayastasya kupyanti śūdrayoniṃ sa gacchati||6||

7pitaraśca na tuṣyanti saha devairviśeṣataḥ
|
prāyaścittaṃ tu yattatra bruvatastannibodha me ||7||

8yatkṛtvā tu naraḥ samyak sukhī bhavati vijvaraḥ
|
gavāṃ mūtrapurīṣeṇa payasā ca ghṛtena ca ||8||

9agnikāryaṃ tryahaṃ kuryānnirāhāraḥ samāhitaḥ
|
tataḥ saṃvatsare pūrṇe pratigṛhṇanti devatāḥ ||9||

10hṛṣyanti pitaraścāsya śrāddhakāla upasthite
|
eṣa hyadharmo dharmaśca sarahasyaḥ prakīrtataḥ ||10||

11martyānāṃ svargakāmānāṃ pretya svargasukhāvahaḥ ||11||
