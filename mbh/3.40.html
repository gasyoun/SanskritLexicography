Mahābhārata 3.40 (Āraṇyakaparvan)

1devadeva uvāca|
narastvaṃ pūrvadehe vai nārāyaṇasahāyavān
|
badaryāṃ taptavānugraṃ tapo varṣāyutānbahūn||1||

2tvayi vā paramaṃ tejo viṣṇau vā puruṣottame
|
yuvābhyāṃ puruṣāgryābhyāṃ tejasā dhāryate jagat||2||

3śakrābhiṣeke sumahaddhanurjaladaniḥsvanam
|
pragṛhya dānavāḥ śastāstvayā kṛṣṇena ca prabho||3||

4tadetadeva gāṇḍīvaṃ tava pārtha karocitam
|
māyāmāsthāya yadgrastaṃ mayā puruṣasattama||4||

5tūṇau cāpyakṣayau bhūyastava pārtha yathocitau
|
bhaviṣyati śarīraṃ ca nīrujaṃ kurunandana||5||

6prītimānasmi te pārtha bhavānsatyaparākramaḥ
|
gṛhāṇa varamasmattaḥ kāṅkṣitaṃ puruṣottama||6||

7na tvayā puruṣaḥ kaścitpumānmartyeṣu mānada
|
divi vā vartate kṣatraṃ tvatpradhānamarindama||7||

8arjuna uvāca|
bhagavandadāsi cenmahyaṃ kāmaṃ prītyā vṛṣadhvaja
|
kāmaye divyamastraṃ tadghoraṃ pāśupataṃ prabho||8||

9yattadbrahmāśaro nāma raudraṃ bhīmaparākramam
|
yugānte dāruṇe prāpte kṛtsnaṃ saṃharate jagat||9||

10karṇabhīṣmakṛpadroṇairbhavitā tu mahāhavaḥ
|
tvatprasādānmahādeva jayeyaṃ tānyathā yudhi||10||

11daheyaṃ yena saṅgrāme dānavānrākṣasāṃstathā
|
bhūtāni ca piśācāṃśca gandharvānatha pannagān||11||

12yasmiñcchūlasahastrāṇi gadāścograpradarśanāḥ
|
śarāścāśīviṣākārāḥ sambhavantyanumantrite||12||

13yudhyeyaṃ yena bhīṣmeṇa droṇena ca kṛpeṇa ca
|
sūtaputreṇa ca raṇe nityaṃ kaṭukabhāṣiṇā||13||

14eṣa me prathamaḥ kāmo bhagavanbhaganetrahan
|
tvatprasādādvinirvṛttaḥ samarthaḥ syāmahaṃ yathā||14||

15bhava uvāca|
dadāmi te'straṃ dayitamahaṃ pāśupataṃ vibho
|
saṃmartho dhāraṇe mokṣe saṃhāre cāsi pāṇḍava||15||

16naitadveda mahendro'pi na yamo na ca yakṣarāṭ
|
varuṇo'pyathavā vāyuḥ kuto vetsyanti mānavāḥ||16||

17na tvetatsahasā pārtha moktavyaṃ puruṣe kvacit
|
jagadvināśayetsarvamalpatejasi pātitam||17||

18avadhyo nāma nāstyatra trailokye sacarācare
|
manasā cakṣuṣā vācā dhanuṣā ca nipātayet||18||

19vaiśaṃpāyana uvāca|
tacchrutvā tvaritaḥ pārthaḥ śucirbhūtvā samāhitaḥ
|
upasaṅgamya viśveśamadhīṣvetyatha so'bravīt||19||

20tatastvadhyāpayāmāsa sarahasyanivartanam
|
tadastraṃ pāṇḍavaśreṣṭhaṃ mūrtimantamivāntakam||20||

21upatasthe ca tatpārthaṃ yathā tryakṣamumāpatim
|
pratijagrāha taccāpi prītimānarjunastadā||21||

22tataścacāla pṛthivī saparvatavanadrumā
|
sasāgaravanoddeśā sagrāmanagarākarā||22||

23śaṅkhadundubhighoṣāśca bherīṇāṃ ca sahasraśaḥ
|
tasminmuhūrte saṃprāpte nirghātāścāpatanmuhuḥ||23||

24athāstraṃ jājvaladghoraṃ pāṇḍavasyāmitaujasaḥ
|
mūrtimadvai sthitaṃ pārśve dadṛśurdevadānavāḥ||24||

25spaṣṭasya tryambakeṇātha phālgunasyāmitaujasaḥ
|
yatkiñcidaśubhaṃ dehe tatsarvaṃ nāśamīyivat||25||

26svargaṃ gacchetyanujñātastryambakeṇa tadārjunaḥ
|
praṇamya śirasā rājanprāñjalirdevamaikṣata||26||

27tataḥ prabhustridivanivāsināṃ vaśī mahāmatirgiriśa umāpātiḥ śivaḥ
|
ghanurmahadditijapiśācasūdanaṃ dadau bhavaḥ puruṣavarāya gāṇḍivam||27||

28tataḥ śubhaṃ girivaramīśvarastadā sahomayā sitataṭasānukandaram
|
vihāya taṃ patagamaharṣisevitaṃ jagāma svaṃ puruṣavarasya paśyataḥ||28||
