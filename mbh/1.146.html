Mahābhārata 1.146 (Ādiparvan)

1vaiśaṃpāyana uvāca|
tataḥ sarvāḥ prakṛtayo nagarādvāraṇāvatāt
|
sarvamaṅgalasaṃyuktā yathāśāstramatandritāḥ||1||

2śrutvāgatānpāṇḍuputrānnānāyānaiḥ sahasraśaḥ
|
abhijagmurnaraśreṣṭhān śrutvaiva parayā mudā||2||

3te samāsādya kaunteyānvāraṇāvatakā janāḥ
|
kṛtvā jayāśiṣaḥ sarve parivāryāvatasthire||3||

4tairvṛtaḥ puruṣavyāghro dharmarājo yudhiṣṭhiraḥ
|
vibabhau devasaṅkāśo vajrapāṇirivāmaraiḥ||4||

5satkṛtāścaiva pauraiste paurānsatkṛtya cānagha
|
alaṅkṛtaṃ janākīrṇaṃ viviśurvāraṇāvatam||5||

6te praviśya purīṃ vīrāstūrṇaṃ jagmuratho gṛhān
|
brāhmaṇānāṃ mahīpāla ratānāṃ sveṣu karmasu||6||

7nagarādhikṛtānāṃ ca gṛhāṇi rathināṃ tadā
|
upatasthurnaraśreṣṭhā vaiśyaśūdragṛhāṇyapi||7||

8arcitāśca naraiḥ pauraiḥ pāṇḍavā bharatarṣabha
|
jagmurāvasathaṃ paścātpurocanapuraḥsarāḥ||8||

9tebhyo bhakṣyāṇi pānāni śayanāni śubhāni ca
|
āsanāni ca mukhyāni pradadau sa purocanaḥ||9||

10tatra te satkṛtāstena sumahārhaparicchadāḥ
|
upāsyamānāḥ puruṣairūṣuḥ puranivāsibhiḥ||10||

11daśarātroṣitānāṃ tu tatra teṣāṃ purocanaḥ
|
nivedayāmāsa gṛhaṃ śivākhyamaśivaṃ tadā||11||

12tatra te puruṣavyāghrā viviśuḥ saparicchadāḥ
|
purocanasya vacanātkailāsamiva guhyakāḥ||12||

13taccāgāramabhiprekṣya sarvadharmabhṛtāṃ varaḥ
|
uvācāgneyamityevaṃ bhīmasenaṃ yudhiṣṭhiraḥ||13||

14yudhiṣṭhira uvāca|
jighrāṇo'sya vasāgandhaṃ sarpirjatuvimiśritam
|
kṛtaṃ hi vyaktamāgneyamidaṃ veśma parantapa||14||

15śaṇasarjarasaṃvyaktamānīya gṛhakarmaṇi
|
muñjabalvajavaṃśādi dravyaṃ sarvaṃ ghṛtokṣitam||15||

16śilpibhiḥ sukṛtaṃ hyāptairvinītairveśmakarmaṇi
|
viśvastaṃ māmayaṃ pāpo dagdhukāmaḥ purocanaḥ||16||

17tathā hi vartate mandaḥ suyodhanavaśe sthitaḥ
|
imāṃ tu tāṃ mahābuddhirviduro dṛṣṭavāṃstathā||17||

18āpadaṃ tena māṃ pārtha sa saṃbodhitavānpurā
|
te vayaṃ bodhitāstena nityamasmaddhitaiṣiṇā||18||

19pitrā kanīyasā snehādbuddhimanto'śivaṃ gṛham
|
anāryaiḥ sukṛtaṃ gūḍhairduryodhanavaśānugaiḥ||19||

20bhīmasena uvāca|
yadīdaṃ gṛhāmāgneyaṃ vihitaṃ manyate bhavān
|
tatraiva sādhu gacchāmo yatra pūrvoṣitā vayam||20||

21yudhiṣṭhira uvāca|
iha yattairnirākārairvastavyamiti rocaye
|
apramattairvicinvadbhirgatimiṣṭāṃ dhruvāmitaḥ||21||

22yadi vindeta cākāramasmākaṃ sa purocanaḥ
|
kṣiprakārī tato bhūtvā prasahyāpi daheta naḥ||22||

23nāyaṃ bibhetyupakrośādadharmādvā purocanaḥ
|
tathā hi vartate mandaḥ suyodhanavaśe sthitaḥ||23||

24api cāyaṃ pradagdheṣu bhīṣmo'smāsu pitāmahaḥ
|
kopaṃ kuryātkimarthaṃ vā kauravānkopayīta saḥ||24||

25athavāpīha dagdheṣu bhīṣmo'smākaṃ pitāmahaḥ
|
dharma ityeva kupyeranye cānye kurupuṅgavāḥ||25||

26vayaṃ tu yadi dāhasya bibhyataḥ pradravemahi
|
spaśairnirghātayetsarvānrājyalubdhaḥ suyodhanaḥ||26||

27apadasthānpade tiṣṭhannapakṣānpakṣasaṃsthitaḥ
|
hīnakośānmahākośaḥ prayogairghātayeddhruvam||27||

28tadasmābhirimaṃ pāpaṃ taṃ ca pāpaṃ suyodhanam
|
vañcayadbhirnivastavyaṃ channāvāsaṃ kvacitkvacit||28||

29te vayaṃ mṛgayāśīlāścarāma vasudhāmimām
|
tathā no viditā mārgā bhaviṣyanti palāyatām||29||

30bhaumaṃ ca bilamadyaiva karavāma susaṃvṛtam
|
gūḍhaśvāsānna nastatra hutāśaḥ saṃpradhakṣyati||30||

31vāsato'tra yathā cāsmānna budhyeta purocanaḥ
|
pauro vāpi janaḥ kaścittathā kāryamatandritaiḥ||31||
