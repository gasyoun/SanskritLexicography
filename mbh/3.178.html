Mahābhārata 3.178 (Āraṇyakaparvan)

1janamejaya uvāca|
kathaṃ nāgāyutaprāṇo bhīmo bhīmaparākramaḥ
|
bhayamāhārayattīvraṃ tasmādajagarānmune||1||

2paulastyaṃ dhanadaṃ yuddhe ya āhvayati darpitaḥ
|
nalinyāṃ kadanaṃ kṛtvā nihantā yakṣarakṣasām||2||

3taṃ śaṃsasi bhayāviṣṭamāpannamarisūdanam
|
etadicchāmyahaṃ śrotuṃ paraṃ kautūhalaṃ hi me||3||

4vaiśampāyana uvāca|
bahvāścarye vane teṣāṃ vasatāmugradhanvinām
|
prāptānāmāśramādrājan rājarṣervṛṣaparvaṇaḥ||4||

5yadṛcchayā dhanuṣpāṇirbaddhakhaḍgo vṛkodaraḥ
|
dadarśa tadvanaṃ ramyaṃ davegandharvasevitam||5||

6sa dadarśa śubhān deśān girerhimavatastadā
|
devarṣisiddhacaritānapsarogaṇasevitān||6||

7cakorairupacakraiśca pakṣibhirjīvajīvakaiḥ
|
kokilairbhṛṅgarājaiśca tatra tatra nināditān||7||

8nityapuṣpaphalairvṛkṣairhimasaṃsparśakomalaiḥ
|
upetān bahulacchāyairmanonayananandanaiḥ||8||

9sa sampaśyan girinadīrvaidūryamaṇisannibhaiḥ
|
salilairhimasaṅkāśairhaṃsakāraṇḍavāyutaiḥ||9||

10vanāni devadārūṇāṃ medhānāmiva vāgurāḥ
|
haricandanamiśrāṇi tuṅgakālīyakānyapi||10||

11mṛgayāṃ paridhāvan sa sameṣu marudhanvasu
|
vidhyan mṛgān śaraiḥ śudvaiścacāra sa mahābalaḥ||11||

12bhīmasenastu vikhyāto mahāntaṃ daṃṣṭriṇaṃ balāt
|
nighnan nāgaśataprāṇo vane tasmin mahābalaḥ||12||

13mṛgāṇāṃ sa varāhāṇāṃ mahiṣāṇāṃ mahābhujaḥ
|
vinighnaṃstatra tatraiva bhīmo bhīmaparākramaḥ||13||

14sa mātaṅgaśataprāṇo manuṣyaśatavāraṇaḥ
|
siṃhaśārdūlavikrānto vane tasmin mahābalaḥ||14||

15vṛkṣānutpāṭayāmāsa tarasā vai babhañja ca
|
pṛthivyāśca pradeśān vai nādayaṃstu vanāni ca||15||

16parvatāgrāṇi vai mṛdūnan nādayānaśca vijvaraḥ
|
prakṣipan pādapāṃścāpi nādenāpūrayan mahīm||16||

17vegena nyapatadbhīmo nirbhayaśca punaḥ punaḥ
|
āsphoṭayan kṣveḍayaṃśca talatālāṃśca vādayan||17||

18cirasambaddhadarpastu bhīmaseno vane tadā
|
gajendrāśca mahāsattvā mṛgendrāśca mahābalāḥ||18||

19bhīmasenasya nādena vyamuñcanta guhā bhayāt
|
kvacit pradhāvaṃstiṣṭhaṃśca kvaciccopaviśaṃstathā||19||

20mṛgaprepsurmahāraudre vane carati nirbhayaḥ
|
sa tatra manujavyāghro vane vanacaropamaḥ||20||

21padbhyāmabhisamāpede bhīmaseno mahābalaḥ
|
sa praviṣṭo mahāraṇye nādān nadati cādbhutān||21||

22trāsayan sarvabhūtāni mahāsattvaparākramaḥ
|
tato bhīmasya śabdena bhītāḥ sarpā guhāśayāḥ||22||

23atikrāntāstu vegena jagāmānusṛtaḥ śanaiḥ
|
tato'maravaraprakhyo bhīmaseno mahābalaḥ||23||

24sa dadarśa mahākāyaṃ bhujaṅgaṃ lomaharṣaṇam
|
giridurge samāpannaṃ kāyenāvṛtya kandaram||24||

25parvatābhogavarṣmāṇamatikāyaṃ mahābalam
|
citrāṅgamaṅgajaiścitrairharidrāsadṛśacchavim||25||

26guhākāreṇa vaktreṇa caturdaṃṣṭreṇa rājatā
|
dīptākṣeṇātitāmreṇa lihānaṃ sṛkkiṇī muhuḥ||26||

27trāsanaṃ sarvabhūtānāṃ kālāntakayamopamam
|
niḥśvāsakṣveḍanādena bhartsayantamiva sthitam||27||

28sa bhīmaṃ sahasā'bhyetya pṛdākuḥ kupito bhṛśam
|
jagrāhājagaro grāho bhujayorubhayorbalāt||28||

29tena saṃspṛṣṭagātrasya bhīmasenasya vai tadā
|
saṃjñā mumoha sahasā varadānena tasya hi||29||

30daśanāgasahasrāṇi dhārayanti hi yadbalam
|
tadbalaṃ bhīmasenasya bhujayorasamaṃ paraiḥ||30||

31sa tejasvī tathā tena bhujagena vaśīkṛtaḥ
|
visphuran śanakairbhīmo na śaśāka viceṣṭitum||31||

32nāgāyutasamaprāṇaḥ siṃhaskandho mahābhujaḥ
|
gṛhīto vyajahāt sattvaṃ varadānavimohitaḥ||32||

33sa hi prayatnamakarottīvramātmavimokṣaṇe
|
na cainamaśakadvīraḥ kathañcit pratibādhitum||33||
