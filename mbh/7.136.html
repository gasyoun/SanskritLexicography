Mahābhārata 7.136 (Droṇaparvan)

1sañjaya uvāca|
tavātmajāṃstu patitān dṛṣṭvā karṇaḥ pratāpavān
|
krodhena mahatā''viṣṭo nirviṇṇo'bhūtsa jīvitāt ||1||

2āgaskṛtamivātmānaṃ mene cādhirathistadā
|
yatpratyakṣaṃ tava sutā bhīmena nihatā raṇe ||2||

3bhīmasenastataḥ kruddhaḥ karṇasya niśitāñśarān
|
nicasvāna sa saṃbhrāntaḥ pūrvavairamanusmaran ||3||

4sa bhīmaṃ pañcabhirviddhvā rādheyaḥ prahasanniva
|
punarvivyādha saptatyā svarṇapuṅkhaiḥ śilāśitaiḥ||4||

5avicintyātha tān bāṇān karṇenāstān vṛkodaraḥ
|
raṇe vivyādha rādheyaṃ śatenānataparvaṇām ||5||

6punaśca viśikhaistīkṣṇairviddhvā marmasu pañcabhiḥ
|
dhanuściccheda bhallena sūtaputrasya māriṣa ||6||

7athānyaddhanurādāya karṇo bhārata durmanāḥ
|
iṣubhiśchādayāmāsa bhīmasenaṃ paraṃtapaḥ ||7||

8tasya bhīmo hayānhatvā vinihatya ca sārathim
|
prajahāsa mahāhāsaṃ kṛte pratikṛte punaḥ ||8||

9iṣubhiḥ kārmukaṃ cāsya cakarta puruṣarṣabhaḥ
|
tat papāta mahārāja svarṇapṛṣṭhaṃ mahāsvanam ||9||

10avārohadrathāttasmādatha karṇo mahārathaḥ
|
gadāṃ gṛhītvā samare bhīmāya prāhiṇodruṣā ||10||

11tāmāpatantīmālakṣya bhīmaseno mahāgadām
|
śarairavārayadrājansarvasainyasya paśyataḥ ||11||

12tato bāṇasahasrāṇi preṣayāmāsa pāṇḍavaḥ
|
sūtaputravadhākāṃkṣī tvaramāṇaḥ parākramī ||12||

13tāniṣūniṣubhiḥ karṇo vārayitvā mahāmṛdhe
|
kavacaṃ bhīmasenasya pāṭayāmāsa sāyakaiḥ ||13||

14athainaṃ pañcaviṃśatyā nārācānāṃ samārpayat
|
paśyatāṃ sarvasainyānāṃ tadadbhutamivābhavat ||14||

15tato bhīmo mahābāhurnavabhirnataparvabhiḥ
|
preṣayāmāsa saṃkruddhaḥ sūtaputrasya māriṣa ||15||

16te tasya kavacaṃ bhittvā tathā bāhuṃ ca dakṣiṇam
|
abhyayurdharaṇīṃ tīkṣṇā valmīkamiva pannagāḥ ||16||

17sa cchādyamāno bāṇaughairbhīmasenadhanuścyutaiḥ
|
punarevābhavatkarṇo bhīmasenātparāṅmukhaḥ ||17||

18taṃ parāṅmukhamālokya padātiṃ sutanandanam
|
kaunteyaśarasaṃchannaṃ rājā duryodhano'bravīt ||18||

19tvaradhvaṃ sarvato yattā rādheyasya rathaṃ prati
|
tatastava sutā rājañchrutvā bhrāturvaco drutam ||19||

20abhyayuḥ pāṇḍavaṃ yuddhe visṛjantaḥ śilīmukhān
|
citropacitraścitrākṣaścārucitraḥ śarāsanaḥ ||20||

21citrāyudhaścitravarmā samare citrayodhinaḥ
|
tānāpatata evāśu bhīmaseno mahārathaḥ ||21||

22ekaikena śareṇājau pātayāmāsa te sutān
|
te hatā nyapatanbhūmau vātarugṇā iva drumāḥ ||22||

23dṛṣṭvā vinihatānputrāṃstava rājanmahārathān
|
aśrupūrṇamukhaḥ karṇaḥ kṣattuḥ sasmāra tadvacaḥ ||23||

24rathaṃ cānyaṃ samāsthāya vidhivatkalpitaṃ punaḥ
|
abhyayātpāṇḍavaṃ yuddhe tvaramāṇaḥ parākramī ||24||

25tāvanyonyaṃ śarairbhittvā svarṇapuṃkhaiḥ śilāśitaiḥ
|
vyabhrājetāṃ yathā meghau saṃsyūtau sūryaraśmibhiḥ ||25||

26ṣaṭtriṃśadbhistato bhallairniśitaistigmatejanaiḥ
|
vyadhamatkavacaṃ kruddhaḥ sūtaputrasya pāṇḍavaḥ ||26||

27sūtaputro'pi kaunteyaṃ śaraiḥ sannataparvabhiḥ
|
pañcāśatā mahābāhurvivyādha bharatarṣabham ||27||

28raktacandanadigdhāṅgau śaraiḥ kṛtamahāvraṇau
|
śoṇitāktau vyarājetāṃ candrasūryāvivoditau ||28||

29tau śoṇitokṣitairgātraiḥ śaraiśchinnatanucchadau
|
karṇabhīmau vyarājetāṃ nirmuktāviva pannagau ||29||

30vyāghrāviva naravyāghrau daṃṣṭrābhiritaretaram
|
śaradhārāsṛjau vīrau meghāviva vavarṣatuḥ ||30||

31vāraṇāviva cānyonyaṃ viṣāṇābhyāmarindamau
|
nirbhindantau svagātrāṇi sāyakaiścāru rejatuḥ ||31||

32nādayantau praharṣantau vikrīḍantau parasparam
|
maṇḍalāni vikurvāṇau rathābhyāṃ rathiṣūttamau ||32||

33vṛṣāvivātha nardantau balinau vāsitāntare
|
siṃhāviva parākrāntau narasiṃhau mahābalau ||33||

34parasparaṃ vīkṣamāṇau krodhasaṃraktalocanau
|
yuyudhāte mahāvīryau śakravairocanī yathā ||34||

35tato bhīmo mahābāhurbāhubhyāṃ vikṣipandhanuḥ
|
vyarājata raṇe rājansavidyudiva toyadaḥ ||35||

36sa nemighoṣastanitaścāpavidyuccharāmbubhiḥ
|
bhīmasenamahāmeghaḥ karṇaparvatamāvṛṇot ||36||

37tataḥ śarasahasreṇa samyagastena bhārata
|
pāṇḍavo vyakiratkarṇaṃ bhīmo bhīmaparākramaḥ ||37||

38tatrāpaśyaṃstava sutā bhīmasenasya vikramam
|
supuṅkhaiḥ kaṅkavāsobhiryatkarṇaṃ chādayañśaraiḥ||38||

39sa nandayan raṇe pārthaṃ keśavaṃ ca yaśasvinam
|
sātyakiṃ cakrarakṣau ca bhīmaḥ karṇamayodhayat ||39||

40vikramaṃ bhujayorvīryaṃ dhairyaṃ ca viditātmanaḥ
|
putrāstava mahārāja dṛṣṭvā vimanaso'bhavan ||40||
