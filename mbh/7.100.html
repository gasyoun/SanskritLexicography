Mahābhārata 7.100 (Droṇaparvan)

1sañjaya uvāca|
salile janite tasmin kaunteyena mahātmanā
|
nistārite dviṣatsainye kṛte ca śaraveśmani ||1||

2vāsudevo rathāt tūrṇamavatīrya mahādyutiḥ
|
mocayāmāsa turagān vinunnān kaṅkapatribhiḥ ||2||

3adṛṣṭapūrvaṃ taddṛṣṭvā sādhuvādo mahānabhūt
|
siddhacāraṇasaṅghānāṃ sainikānāṃ ca sarvaśaḥ ||3||

4padātinaṃ tu kaunteyaṃ yudhyamānaṃ mahārathāḥ
|
nāśaknuvan vārayituṃ tada‌dbhutamivābhavat ||4||

5āpatatsu rathaugheṣu prabhūtagajavājiṣu
|
nāsambhramattadā pārthastadasya puruṣānati ||5||

6vyasṛjanta śaraughāṃste pāṇḍavaṃ prati pārthivāḥ
|
na cāvyathata dharmātmā vāsaviḥ paravīrahā ||6||

7sa tāni śarajālāni gadāḥ prāsāṃścavīryavān
|
āgatānagrasat pārthaḥ saritaḥ sāgaro yathā ||7||

8astravegena mahatā pārtho bāhubalena ca
|
sarveṣāṃ pārthivendrāṇāmagrasattān śarottamān ||8||

9tattu pārthasya vikrāntaṃ vāsudevasya cobhayoḥ
|
apūjayan mahārāja kauravā mahada‌dbhutam ||9||

10kimadbhutatamaṃ loke bhavitā'pyathavā hyabhūt
|
yadaśvān pārthagovindau mocayāmāsatū raṇe ||10||

11bhayaṃ vipulamasmāsu tāvadhattāṃ narottamau
|
tejo vidadhatuścograṃ visrabdhau raṇamūrdhani ||11||

12atha smayan hṛṣīkeśaḥ strīmadhya iva bhārata
|
arjunena kṛte saṅkhye śaragarbhagṛhe tathā ||12||

13upāvartayadavyagrastānaśvān puṣkarekṣaṇaḥ
|
miṣatāṃ sarvasainyānāṃ tvadīyānāṃ viśāmpate ||13||

14teṣāṃ śramaṃ ca glāniṃ ca vamathuṃ vepathuṃ vraṇān
|
sarvaṃ vyapānudat kṛṣṇaḥ kuśalo hyaśvakarmaṇi ||14||

15śalyānuvṛtya pāṇibhyāṃ parimṛjya ca tān hayān
|
upāvartya yathānyāyaṃ pāyayāmāsa vāri saḥ ||15||

16sa tā~llabdhodakān snātān jagdhānnān vigataklamān
|
yojayāmāsa saṃhṛṣṭaḥ punareva rathottame ||16||

17sa taṃ rathavaraṃ śauriḥ sarvaśastrabhṛtāṃ varaḥ
|
samāsthāya mahātejāḥ sārjunaḥ prayayau drutam ||17||

18rathaṃ rathavarasyājau yuktaṃ labdhoda‌kairhayaiḥ
|
dṛṣṭvā kurubalaśreṣṭhāḥ punarvimanaso'bhavan ||18||

19viniḥśvasantaste rājan bhagnadaṃṣṭrā ivoragāḥ
|
dhigaho dhiggataḥ pārthaḥ kṛṣṇaścetyabruvan pṛthak ||19||

20tatsainyaṃ sarvato dṛṣṭvā lomaharṣaṇamadbhutam
|
tvaradhvamiti cākrandannaitadastīti cābruvan ||20||

21sarvakṣatrasya miṣato rathenaikena daṃśitau
|
bālaḥ krīḍanakeneva kadarthīkṛtya no balam ||21||

22krośatāṃ yatamānānāmasaṃsaktau parantapau
|
darśayitvātmano vīryaṃ prayātau sarvarājasu ||22||

23tau prayātau punardṛṣṭvā tadā'nye sainikā'bruvan
|
tvaradhvaṃ kuravaḥ sarve vadhe kṛṣṇakirīṭinoḥ ||23||

24rathayukto hi dāśārho bhiṣatāṃ sarvadhanvinām
|
jayadrathāya yātyeṣa kadarthīkṛtya no raṇe ||24||

25tatra kecinmitho rājan samabhāṣanta bhūmipāḥ
|
adṛṣṭapūrvaṃ saṅgrāme taddṛṣṭvā mahadadbhutam ||25||

26sarvasainyāni rājā ca dhṛtarāṣṭro'tyayaṃ gataḥ
|
duryodhanāparādhena kṣatraṃ kṛtsnā ca medinī ||26||

27vilayaṃ samanuprāptā tacca rājā na budhyate
|
ityevaṃ kṣatriyāstatra bruvantyanye ca bhārata||27||

28sindhurājasya yatkṛtyaṃ gatasya yamasādanam
|
tat karotu vṛthādṛṣṭirdhārtarāṣṭro'nupāyavit ||28||

29tataḥ śīghrataraṃ prāyāt pāṇḍavaḥ saindhavaṃ prati
|
vivartamāne tigmāṃśau hṛṣṭaiḥ pītodakairhayaiḥ ||29||

30taṃ prayāntaṃ mahābāhuṃ sarvaśastrabhṛtāṃ varam
|
nāśaknuvan vārayituṃ yodhāḥ kruddhamivāntakam ||30||

31vidrāvya tu tataḥ sainyaṃ pāṇḍavaḥ śatrutāpanaḥ
|
yathā mṛgagaṇān siṃhaḥ saindhavārthe vyaloḍayat ||31||

32gāhamānastvanīkāni tūrṇamaśvānacodayat
|
balākābhaṃ tu dāśārhaḥ pāñcajanyaṃ vyanādayat ||32||

33kaunteyenāgrataḥ sṛṣṭā nyapatan pṛṣṭhataḥ śarāḥ
|
tūrṇāt tūrṇataraṃ hyaśvāḥ prāvahan vātaraṃhasaḥ ||33||

34tato nṛpatayaḥ kruddhāḥ parivavrurdhanañjayam
|
kṣatriyā bahavaścānye jayadrathavadhaiṣiṇam ||34||

35sainyeṣu viprayāteṣu dhiṣṭhitaṃ puruṣarṣabham
|
duryodhano'nvayātpārthaṃ tvaramāṇo mahāhave ||35||

36vātoddhūtapatākaṃ taṃ rathaṃ jaladaniḥsvanam
|
ghoraṃ kapidhvajaṃ dṛṣṭvā viṣaṇṇā rathino'bhavan ||36||

37divākare'tha rajasā sarvataḥ saṃvṛte bhṛśam
|
śarārtāśca raṇe yodhāḥ śekuḥ kṛṣṇau na vīkṣitum ||37||
