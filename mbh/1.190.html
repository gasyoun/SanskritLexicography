Mahābhārata 1.190 (Ādiparvan)

1ajināni vidhunvantaḥ karakāśca dvijarṣabhāḥ
|
ūcuste bhīrna kartavyā vayaṃ yotsyāmahe parān||1||

2tānevaṃ vadato viprānarjunaḥ prahasanniva
|
uvāca prekṣakā bhūtvā yūyaṃ tiṣṭhata pārśvataḥ||2||

3ahamenānajihmāgraiḥ śataśo vikirañcharaiḥ
|
vārayiṣyāmi saṃkruddhānmantrairāśīviṣāniva||3||

4iti taddhanurānamya śulkāvāptaṃ mahābalaḥ
|
bhrātrā bhīmena sahitastasthau giririvācalaḥ||4||

5tataḥ karṇamukhāndṛṣṭvā kṣatriyānyuddhadurmadān
|
saṃpetaturabhītau tau gajau pratigajāniva||5||

6ūcuśca vācaḥ paruṣāste rājāno yuyutsavaḥ
|
āhave hi dvijasyāpi vadho dṛṣṭo yuyutsataḥ||6||

7ityevamuktvā rājānaḥ sahasā dudruvurdvijān
|
tataḥ karṇo mahātejā jiṣṇuṃ prati yayau raṇe||7||

8yuddhārthī vāsitāhetorgajaḥ pratigajaṃ yathā
|
bhīmasenaṃ yayau śalyo madrāṇāmīśvaro balī||8||

9duryodhanādayaḥ sarve brāhmaṇaiḥ saha saṃgatāḥ
|
mṛdupūrvamayatnena pratyayudhyaṃstadāhave||9||

10tato'rjunaḥ pratyavidhyadāpatantaṃ śitaiḥ śaraiḥ
|
karṇaṃ vaikartanaṃ śrīmānvikṛṣya balavaddhanuḥ||10||

11teṣāṃ śarāṇāṃ vegena śitānāṃ tigmatejasām
|
vimuhyamāno rādheyo yatnāttamanudhāvati||11||

12tāvubhāvapyanirdeśyau lāghavājjayatāṃ varau
|
ayudhyetāṃ susaṃrabdhāvanyonyavijigīṣiṇau||12||

13kṛte pratikṛtaṃ paśya paśya bāhubalaṃ ca me
|
iti śūrārthavacanairabhāṣetāṃ parasparam||13||

14tato'rjunasya bhujayorvīryamapratimaṃ bhuvi
|
jñātvā vaikartanaḥ karṇaḥ saṃrabdhaḥ samayodhayat||14||

15arjunena prayuktāṃstānbāṇānvegavatastadā
|
pratihatya nanādoccaiḥ sainyāni tadapūjayan||15||

16karṇa uvāca|
tuṣyāmi te vipramukhya bhujavīyarsya saṃyuge
|
aviṣādasya caivāsya śastrāstravijayasya ca||16||

17kiṃ tvaṃ sākṣāddhanurvedo rāmo vā viprasattama
|
atha sākṣāddharihayaḥ sākṣādvā viṣṇuracyutaḥ||17||

18ātmapracchādanārthaṃ vai bāhuvīryamupāśritaḥ
|
viprarūpaṃ vidhāyedaṃ manye māṃ pratiyudhyase||18||

19na hi māmāhave kruddhamanyaḥ sākṣācchacīpateḥ
|
pumānyodhayituṃ śaktaḥ pāṇḍavādvā kirīṭinaḥ||19||

20tamevaṃvādinaṃ tatra phālgunaḥ pratyabhāṣata
|
nāsmi karṇa dhanurvedo nāsmi rāmaḥ pratāpavān||20||

21brāhmaṇo'smi yudhāṃ śreṣṭhaḥ sarvaśastrabhṛtāṃ varaḥ
|
brāhme paurandare cāstre niṣṭhitoguruśāsanāt||21||

22sthito'smyadya raṇe jetuṃ tvāṃ vai vīra sthiro bhava
|
vaiśaṃpāyana uvāca|
evamuktastu rādheyo yuddhātkarṇo nyavartata||22||

23brāhmaṃ tejastadā'jayyaṃ manyamāno mahārathaḥ
|
aparasminvanoddeśe vīrau śalyavṛkodarau||23||

24balinau yuddhasaṃpannau vidyayā ca balena ca
|
anyonyamāhvayantau tu mattāviva mahāgajau||24||

25muṣṭibhirjānubhiścaiva nighnantāvitaretaram
|
prakarṣaṇākarṣaṇayorabhyākarṣavikarṣaṇaiḥ||25||

26ācakarṣaturanyonyaṃ muṣṭibhiścāpi jaghnatuḥ
|
tataścaṭacaṭāśabdaḥ sughoro hyabhavattayoḥ||26||

27pāṣāṇasaṃpātanibhaiḥ prahārairabhijaghnatuḥ
|
muhūrtaṃ tau tadā'nyonyaṃ samare paryakarṣatām||27||

28tato bhīmaḥ samutkṣipya bāhubhyāṃ śalyamāhave
|
apātayatkuruśreṣṭho brāhmaṇā jahasustadā||28||

29tatrāścaryaṃ bhīmasenaścakāra puruṣarṣabhaḥ
|
yacchalyaṃ pātitaṃ bhūmau nāvadhīdbalinaṃ balī||29||

30pātite bhīmasenena śalye karṇe ca śaṅkite
|
śaṅkitāḥ sarvarājānaḥ parivavrurvṛkodaram||30||

31ūcuśca sahitāstatra sādhvimau brāhmaṇarṣabhau
|
vijñāyetāṃ kva janmānau kva nivāsau tathaiva ca||31||

32ko hi rādhāsutaṃ karṇaṃ śakto yodhayituṃ raṇe
|
anyatra rāmāddroṇādvā pāṇḍavādvā kirīṭinaḥ||32||

33kṛṣṇādvā devakīputrātkṛpādvāpi śaradvataḥ
|
ko vā duryodhanaṃ śaktaḥ pratiyothayituṃ raṇe||33||

34tathaiva madrādhipatiṃ śalyaṃ balavatāṃvaram
|
baladevādṛte vīrātpāṇḍavādvā vṛkodarāt||34||

35vīrādduryodhanādvā'nyaḥ śaktaḥ pātayituṃ raṇe
|
kriyatāmavahāro'smādyuddhādbrāhmaṇasaṃvṛtāt||35||

36|
brāhmaṇā hi sadā rakṣyāḥ sāparādhā'pi nityadā
|
athainānupalabhyeha punaryotsyāma hṛṣṭavat||36||

37tāṃstathāvādinaḥ sarvānprasamīkṣya kṣitīśvarān
|
athānyānpuruṣāṃścāpi kṛtvā tatkarma saṃyuge||37||

38vaiśaṃpāyana uvāca|
tatkarma bhīmasya samīkṣya kṛṣṇaḥ kuntīsutau tau pariśaṅkamānaḥ
|
nivārayāmāsa mahīpatīṃstān dharmeṇa labdhetyanunīya sarvān||38||

39evaṃ te vinivṛttāstu yuddhādyuddhaviśāradāḥ
|
yathāvāsaṃ yayuḥ sarve vismitā rājasattamāḥ||39||

40vṛtto brahmottaro raṅgaḥ pāñcālī brāhmaṇairvṛtā
|
iti bruvantaḥ prayayurye tatrāsansamāgatāḥ||40||

41brāhmaṇaistu praticchannau rauravājinavāsibhiḥ
|
kṛcchreṇa jagmatustau tu bhīmasenadhanañjayau||41||

42vimukto janasaṃbādhācchatrubhiḥ parivikṣatau
|
kṛṣṇayānugatau tatra nṛvīrau tau virejatuḥ||42||

43paurṇamāsyāṃ ghanairmuktau candrasūryāvivoditau
|
teṣāṃ mātā bahuvidhaṃ vināśaṃ paryacintayat||43||

44anāgacchatsu putreṣu bhaikṣakāle'bhigacchati
|
dhārtarāṣṭrairhatā na syurvijñāya kurupuṅgavāḥ||44||

45māyānvitairvā rakṣobhiḥ sughorairdṛḍhavairibhiḥ
|
viparītaṃ mataṃ jātaṃ vyāsasyāpi mahātmanaḥ||45||

46ityevaṃ cintayāmāsaṃ sutasnehāvṛtā pṛthā
|
tataḥ suptajanaprāye durdine meghasaṃplute||46||

47mahatyathāparāhṇe tu ghanaiḥ sūrya ivāvṛtaḥ
|
brāhmaṇaiḥ prāviśattatra jiṣṇurbhārgavaveśma tat||47||
