Mahābhārata 8.13 (Karṇaparvan)

1sañjaya uvāca|
tataḥ karṇo maheṣvāsaḥ pāṇḍavānāmanīkinīm
|
jaghāna samare śūraḥ śaraiḥ sannataparvabhiḥ ||1||

2tathaiva pāṇḍavā rājaṃstava putrasya vāhinīm
|
karṇasya pramukhe kruddhā nijaghnuste mahārathāḥ ||2||

3karṇo'pi rājansamare vyahanatpāṇḍavīṃ camūm
|
nārācairarkaraśmyābhaiḥ karmāraparimārjitaiḥ ||3||

4tatra bhārata karṇena nārācaistāḍitā gajāḥ
|
neduḥ seduśca mamluśca babhramuśca diśo daśa ||4||

5vadhyamāne bale tasminsūtaputreṇa māriṣa
|
nakulo'bhyadravattūrṇaṃ sūtaputraṃ mahāraṇe ||5||

6bhīmasenastathā drauṇiṃ kurvāṇaṃ karma duṣkaram
|
vindānuvindau kaikeyau sātyakiḥ samavārayat ||6||

7śrutakarmāṇamāyāntaṃ citraseno mahīpatiḥ
|
prativindhyastathā citraṃ citraketanakārmukam ||7||

8duryodhanastu rājānaṃ dharmaputraṃ yudhiṣṭhiram
|
saṃśaptakagaṇān kruddho hyabhyadhāvaddhanañjayaḥ ||8||

9dhṛṣṭadyumnaḥ kṛpeṇātha tasminvīravarakṣaye
|
śikhaṇḍī kṛtavarmāṇaṃ samāsādayadacyutam ||9||

10śrutakīrtistathā śalyaṃ mādrīputraḥ sutaṃ tava
|
duḥśāsanaṃ mahārāja sahadevaḥ pratāpavān ||10||

11kaikeyau sātyakiṃ yuddhe śaravarṣeṇa bhāsvatā
|
sātyakiḥ kekayau cāpi cchādayāmāsa bhārata ||11||

12tāvenaṃ bhrātarau vīrau jaghnaturhṛdaye bhṛśam
|
viṣāṇābhyāṃ yathā nāgau pratināgaṃ mahāvane ||12||

13śarasaṃbhinnavarmāṇau tāvubhau bhrātarau raṇe
|
sātyakiṃ satyakarmāṇaṃ rājanvivyadhatuḥ śaraiḥ ||13||

14tau sātyakirmahārāja prahasan sarvatodiśaḥ
|
chādayañcharavarṣeṇa vārayāmāsa bhārata ||14||

15vāryamāṇau tatastau hi śaineyaśaravṛṣṭibhiḥ
|
śaineyasya rathaṃ tūrṇaṃ chādayāmāsatuḥ śaraiḥ ||15||

16tayostu dhanuṣī citre chittvā śaurirmahāyaśāḥ
|
atha tau sāyakaistīkṣṇairvārayāmāsa saṃyuge ||16||

17athānye dhanuṣī citre pragṛhya ca mahāśarān
|
sātyakiṃ chādayantau tau ceraturlaghu suṣṭhu ca ||17||

18tābhyāṃ muktā mahābāṇāḥ kaṅkabarhiṇavāsasaḥ
|
dyotayanto diśaḥ sarvāḥ saṃpetuḥ svarṇabhūṣaṇāḥ ||18||

19bāṇāndhakāramabhavattayo rājanmahāmṛdhe
|
anyonyasya dhanuścaiva cicchiduste mahārathāḥ ||19||

20tataḥ kruddho mahārāja sātvato yuddhadurmadaḥ
|
dhanuranyatsamādāya sajyaṃ kṛtvā ca saṃyuge ||20||

21kṣurapreṇa sutīkṣṇena anuvindaśiro'harat
|
apatattacchiro rājan kuṇḍalopacitaṃ mahat ||21||

22śambarasya śiro yadvannihatasya mahāraṇe
|
śocayankekayānsarvāñjagāmāśu vasundharām ||22||

23taṃ dṛṣṭvā nihataṃ śūraṃ bhrātā tasya mahārathaḥ
|
sajyamanyaddhanuḥ kṛtvā śaineyaṃ paryavārayat ||23||

24sa ṣaṣṭyā sātyakiṃ viddhvā svarṇapuṅkhaiḥ śilāśitaiḥ
|
nanāda balavannādaṃ tiṣṭha tiṣṭheti cābravīt ||24||

25sātyakiṃ ca tatastūrṇaṃ kekayānāṃ mahārathaḥ
|
śarairanekasāhasrairbāhvorurasi cārpayat ||25||

26sa śaraiḥ kṣatasarvāṅgaḥ sātyakiḥ satyavikramaḥ
|
rarāja samare rājan sapuṣpa iva kiṃśukaḥ ||26||

27sātyakiḥ samare viddhaḥ kaikeyena mahātmanā
|
kaikeyaṃ pañcaviṃśatyā vivyādha prahasanniva ||27||

28tāvanyonyasya samare saṃcchidya dhanuṣī śubhe
|
hatvā ca sārathī tūrṇaṃ hayāṃśca rathināṃ varau ||28||

29virathāvasiyuddhāya samājagmaturāhave
|
śatacandracite gṛhya carmaṇī subhujau tathā ||29||

30virocetāṃ mahāraṅge nistriṃśavaradhāriṇau
|
yathā devāsure yuddhe jambhaśakrau mahābalau ||30||

31maṇḍalāni tatastau tu vicarantau mahāraṇe
|
anyonyamabhitastūrṇaṃ samājagmaturāhave ||31||

32anyonyasya vadhe caiva cakraturyatnamuttamam
|
kaikeyasya dvidhā carma tataściccheda sātvataḥ ||32||

33sātyakestu tathaivāsau carma ciccheda pārthivaḥ
|
carma cchittvā tu kaikeyastārāgaṇaśatairvṛtam ||33||

34cacāra maṇḍalānyeva gatapratyāgatāni ca
|
taṃ carantaṃ mahāraṅge nistriṃśavaradhāriṇam ||34||

35apahastena ciccheda śaineyastvarayā'nvitaḥ
|
savarmā kekayo rājandvidhā cchinno mahāraṇe ||35||

36nipapāta maheṣvāso vajrāhata ivācalaḥ
|
taṃ nihatya raṇe śūraḥ śaineyo rathasattamaḥ ||36||

37yudhāmanyurathaṃ tūrṇamāruroha paraṃtapaḥ
|
tato'nyaṃ rathamāsthāya vidhivatkalpitaṃ punaḥ ||37||

38kekayānāṃ mahatsainyaṃ vyadhamatsātyakiḥ śaraiḥ
|
sā vadhyamānā samare kekayānāṃ mahācamūḥ
|
tamutsṛjya raṇe śatruṃ pradudrāva diśo daśa ||38||
