Mahābhārata 7.66 (Droṇaparvan)

1nārada uvāca|gayaṃ cāmūrtarayasaṃ mṛtaṃ sṛñjaya śuśruma
|
yo vai varṣaśataṃ rājā hutaśiṣṭāśano'bhavat ||1||

2tasmai hyagnirvaraṃ prādāt tato vavre varaṃ gayaḥ
|
tapasā brahmacaryeṇa vratena niyamena ca ||2||

3gurūṇāṃ ca prasādena vedānicchāmi veditum
|
svadharmeṇāvihiṃsyānyān dhanamicchāmi cākṣayam ||3||

4vipreṣu dadataścaiva śraddhā bhavatu nityaśaḥ
|
ananyāsu savarṇāsu putrajanma ca me bhavet ||4||

5annaṃ me dadataḥ śraddhā dharme me ramatāṃ manaḥ
|
avighnaṃ cāstu me nityaṃ dharmakāryeṣu pāvaka ||5||

6tathā bhaviṣyatītyuktvā tatraivāntaradhīyata
|
gayo hyavāpya tatsarvaṃ dharmeṇārīnajījayat ||6||

7sa darśapaurṇamāsībhyāṃ kāleṣvāgrayaṇena ca
|
cāturmāsyaiśca vividhairyajñaiścāvāptadakṣiṇaiḥ ||7||

8ayajacchraddhayā rājā parisaṃvatsarān śatam
|
gavāṃ śatasahasrāṇi śatamaśvaśatāni ca ||8||

9śataṃ niṣkasahasrāṇi gavāṃ cāpyayutāni ṣaṭ
|
utthāyotthāya sa prādāt parisaṃvatsarān śatam ||9||

10nakṣatreṣu ca sarveṣu dadannakṣatradakṣiṇāḥ
|
īje ca vividhairyajñairyathā somo'ṅgirā yathā ||10||

11sauvarṇāṃ pṛthivīṃ kṛtvā ya imāṃ maṇiśarkarām
|
viprebhyaḥ prādadadrājā so'śvamedhe mahāmakhe ||11||

12jāmbūnadamayā yūpāḥ sarve ratnaparicchadāḥ
|
gayasyāsan samṛddhāstu sarvabhūtamanoharāḥ ||12||

13sarvakāmasamṛddhaṃ ca prādādannaṃ gayastadā
|
brāhmaṇebhyaḥ prahṛṣṭebhyaḥ sarvabhūtebhya eva ca ||13||

14sa samudravanadvīpanadīnadavaneṣu ca
|
nagareṣu ca rāṣṭreṣu divi vyomni ca ye'vasan ||14||

15bhūtagrāmāśca vividhāḥ santṛptā yajñasampadā
|
gayasya sadṛśo yajño nāstyanya iti te'bruvan ||15||

16ṣaṭtriṃśadyojanāyāmā triṃśadyojanamāyatā
|
paścāt puraścaturviṃśadvedī hyāsīddhiraṇmayī ||16||

17gayasya yajamānasya muktā vajramaṇistṛtā
|
prādāt sa brāhmaṇebhyo'tha vāsāṃsyābharaṇāni ca ||17||

18yathoktā dakṣiṇāścānyā viprebhyo bhūridakṣiṇaḥ
|
yatra bhojanaśiṣṭasya parvatāḥ pañcaviṃśatiḥ||18||

19kulyāḥ kuśalavāhinyo rasānāmabhavaṃstadā
|
vastrābharaṇagandhānāṃ rāśayaśca pṛthagvidhāḥ ||19||

20yasya prabhāvācca gayastriṣu lokeṣu viśrutaḥ
|
vaṭaścākṣayyakaraṇaḥ puṇyaṃ brahmasaraśca tat ||20||

21sa cenmamāra sṛñjaya caturbhadratarastvayā
|
putrāt puṇyatarastubhyaṃ mā putramanutapyathāḥ
|
ayajvānamadākṣiṇyamabhiśvaityeti vyāharan ||21||
