Mahābhārata 12.111 (Śāntiparvan)

1yudhiṣṭhira uvāca|
asaumyāḥ saumyarūpeṇa saumyāścāsaumyadarśanāḥ
|
īdṛśānpuruṣāṃstāta kathaṃ vidyāmahe vayam||1||

2bhīṣma uvāca|
atrāpyudāharantīmamitihāsaṃ purātanam
|
vyāghra-gomāyusaṃvādaṃ taṃ nibodha yudhiṣṭhira||2||

3purikāyāṃ puri purā śrīmatyāṃ pauriko nṛpaḥ
|
parahiṃsāratiḥ krūro babhūva puruṣādhamaḥ||3||

4sa tvāyuṣi parikṣīṇe jagāmānīpsitāṃ gatim
|
gomāyutvaṃ ca samprāpto dūṣitaḥ pūrvakarmaṇā||4||

5saṃsmṛtya pūrvabhūtiṃ ca nirvedaṃ paramaṃ gataḥ
|
na bhakṣayati māṃsāni parairupahṛtānyapi||5||

6ahiṃsraḥ sarvabhūteṣu satyavāk sudṛḍhavrataḥ
|
sa cakāra yathākālamāhāraṃ patitaiḥ phalaiḥ||6||

7śmaśāne tasya cāvāso gomāyoḥ sammato'bhavat
|
janmabhūmyanurodhācca nānyavāsamarocayat||7||

8tasya śaucamamṛṣyantaste sarve sahajātayaḥ
|
cālayanti sma tāṃ buddhiṃ vacanaiḥ praśrayottaraiḥ||8||

9vasanpitṛvane raudre śauce vartitumicchasi
|
iyaṃ vipratipattiste yadā tvaṃ piśitāśanaḥ||9||

10tatsamāno bhavāsmābhirbhojyaṃ dāsyāmahe vayam
|
bhuṃkṣva śaucaṃ parityajya yaddhi bhuktaṃ sadāstu te||10||

11iti teṣāṃ vacaḥ śrutvā pratyuvāca samāhitaḥ
|
madhuraiḥ prasṛtairvākyairhetumadbhiraniṣṭhuraiḥ||11||

12apramāṇā prasūtirme śīlataḥ kriyate kulam
|
prārthayāmi ca tatkarma yena vistīryate yaśaḥ||12||

13śmaśāne yadi me vāsaḥ samādhirme niśamyatām
|
ātmā phalati karmāṇi nāśramo dharmakāraṇam||13||

14āśrame yo dvijaṃ hanyādgāṃ vā dadyādanāśrame
|
kiṃ tu tatpātakaṃ na syāttadvā dattaṃ vṛthā bhavet||14||

15bhavantaḥ svārthalobhena kevalaṃ bhakṣaṇe ratāḥ
|
anubandhe trayo doṣāstānna paśyanti mohitāḥ||15||

16apratyayakṛtāṃ gārhyāmarthāpanayadūṣitām
|
iha cāmutra cāniṣṭāṃ tasmādvṛttiṃ na rocaye||16||

17taṃ śuciṃ paṇḍitaṃ matvā śārdūlaḥ khyātavikramaḥ
|
kṛtvātmasadṛśīṃ pūjāṃ sācivye'varayatsvayam||17||

18śārdūla uvāca| saumya vijñātarūpastvaṃ gaccha yātrāṃ mayā saha
|
vriyantāmīpsitābhogāḥ parihāryāśca puṣkalāḥ||18||

19tīkṣṇā iti vayaṃ khyātā bhavantaṃ jñāpayāmahe
|
mṛdupūrvaṃ hitaṃ caiva śreyaścādhigamiṣyasi||19||

20atha sampūjya tadvākyaṃ mṛgendrasya mahātmanaḥ
|
gomāyuḥ saṃśritaṃ vākyaṃ babhāṣe kiṃcidānataḥ||20||

21gomāyuruvāca| sadṛśaṃ mṛgarājaitattava vākyaṃ madantare
|
yatsahāyānmṛgayase dharmārthakuśalān śucīn||21||

22na śakyaṃ hyanamātyena mahattvamanuśāsitum
|
duṣṭāmātyena vā vīra śarīraparipanthinā||22||

23sahāyānanuraktāṃśca nayajñānupasaṃhitān
|
parasparamasaṃsṛṣṭānvijigīṣūnalolupān||23||

24anatītopadhānprājñān hite yuktānmanasvinaḥ
|
pūjayethā mahābhāga yathā''cāryānyathā pitṝn||24||

25na tveva mama santoṣādrocate'nyanmṛgādhipa
|
na kāmaye sukhānbhogānaiśvaryaṃ ca tadāśrayam||25||

26na yokṣyati hi me śīlaṃ tava bhṛtyaiḥ purātanaiḥ
|
te tvāṃ vibhedayiṣyanti duḥśīlāśca madantare||26||

27na saṃśrayaḥ ślāghanīyo'hameṣāmapi bhāsvatām
|
kṛtātmā sumahābhāgaḥ pāpakeṣvapyadāruṇaḥ||27||

28dīrghadarśī mahotsāhaḥ sthūlalakṣyo mahābalaḥ
|
kṛtī cāmoghakartāsmi bhāgyaiśca samalaṃkṛtaḥ||28||

29na khalpenāsmi saṃtuṣṭo duḥkhavṛttiranuṣṭhitā
|
sevāyāṃ cāpi nābhijñaḥ svacchandena vanacaraḥ||29||

30rājopakrośadoṣāśca sarve saṃśrayavāsinām
|
vratacaryā tu ni:saṃgā nirbhayā vanavāsinām||30||

31nṛpeṇāhūyamānasya yattiṣṭhati bhayaṃ hṛdi
|
na tattiṣṭhati tuṣṭānāṃ vane mūlaphalāśinām||31||

32pānīyaṃ vā nirāyāsaṃ svādvannaṃ vā'bhayottaram
|
vicārya khalu paśyāmi tatsukhaṃ yatra nirvṛtiḥ||32||

33aparādhairna tāvanto bhṛtyāḥ śiṣṭā narādhipaiḥ
|
upaghātairyathā bhṛtyā dūṣitā nidhanaṃ gatāḥ||33||

34yadi tvetanmayā kāryaṃ mṛgendra yadi manyase
|
samayaṃ kṛtamicchāmi vartitavyaṃ yathā mayi||34||

35madīyā mānanīyāste śrotavyaṃ ca hitaṃ vacaḥ
|
kalpittā yā ca me vṛttiḥ sā bhavettvayi susthirā||35||

36na mantrayeyamanyaiste sacivaiḥ saha karhicit
|
nītimantaḥ parīpsanto vṛthā brūyuḥ pare mayi||36||

37eka ekena saṃgamya raho brūyāṃ hitaṃ vacaḥ
|
na ca te jñātikāryeṣu praṣṭavyo'haṃ hitāhite||37||

38mayā sammantrya paścācca na hiṃsyāḥ sacivāstvayā
|
madīyānāṃ ca kupito mā tvaṃ daṇḍaṃ nipātayeḥ||38||

39evamastviti tenāsau mṛgendreṇābhipūjitaḥ
|
prāptavānmatisācivyaṃ gomāyurvyāghrayonitaḥ||39||

40taṃ tathā sukṛtaṃ dṛṣṭvā pūjyamānaṃ svakarmasu
|
prādviṣankṛtasaṃghātā pūrvabhṛtyā muhurmuhuḥ||40||

41mitrabuddhyā ca gomāyuṃ sāntvayitvā prasādya ca
|
doṣaistu samatāṃ netumaicchannaśubhabuddhayaḥ||41||

42anyathā hyuṣitāḥ pūrvaṃ paradravyābhihāriṇaḥ
|
aśaktāḥ kiñcidādātuṃ dravyaṃ gomāyuyantritāḥ||42||

43vyutthānaṃ ca vikāṅkṣadbhiḥ kathābhiḥ pratilobhyate
|
dhanena mahatā caiva buddhirasya vilobhyate||43||

44na cāpi sa mahāprājñastasmāddhairyāccacāla ha
|
athāsya samayaṃ kṛtvā vināśāya tathā'pare||44||

45īpsitaṃ tu mṛgendrasya māṃsaṃ yattatra saṃskṛtam
|
apanīya svayaṃ taddhi tairnyastaṃ tasya veśmani||45||

46yadarthaṃ cāpyapahṛtaṃ yena taccaiva mantritam
|
tasya tadviditaṃ sarvaṃ kāraṇārthaṃ ca marṣitam||46||

47samayo'yaṃ kṛtastena sācivyamupagacchatā
|
nopaghātastvayā kāryo rājanmaitrīmihecchatā||47||

48bhīṣma uvāca|
kṣudhitasya mṛgendrasya bhoktumabhyutthitasya ca
|
bhojanāyopahartavyaṃ tanmāṃsaṃ nopadṛśyate||48||

49mṛgarājena cājñaptaṃ dṛśyatāṃ cora ityuta
|
kṛtakaiścāpi tanmāṃsaṃ mṛgendrāyopavarṇitam||49||

50sacivenāpanītaṃ te viduṣā prājñamāninā
|
saroṣastvatha śārdūlaḥ śrutvā gomāyucāpalam||50||

51babhūvāmarṣito rājā vadhaṃ cāsya vyarocayat
|
chidraṃ tu tasya taddṛṣṭvā procuste pūrvamantriṇaḥ||51||

52sarveṣāmeva so'smākaṃ vṛttibhaṅge pravartate
|
niścityaiva punastasya te karmāṇyapi varṇayan||52||

53idaṃ tasyedṛśaṃ karma kiṃ tena na kṛtaṃ bhavet
|
śrutaśca svāminā pūrvaṃ yādṛśo naiva tādṛśaḥ||53||

54vāṅmātreṇaiva dharmiṣṭhaḥ svabhāvena tu dāruṇaḥ
|
dharmacchadmā hyayaṃ pāpo vṛthācāraparigrahaḥ||54||

55kāryārthaṃ bhojanārtheṣu vrateṣu kṛtavān śramam
|
yadi vipratyayo hyeṣa tadidaṃ darśayāma te||55||

56tanmāṃsaṃ caiva gomāyostaiḥ kṣaṇādāśu ḍhaukitam
|
māṃsāpanayanaṃ jñātvā vyāghraḥ śrutvā ca tadvacaḥ||56||

57ājñāpayāmāsa tadā gomāyurvadhyatāmiti
|
śārdūlasya vacaḥ śrutvā śārdūlajananī tataḥ||57||

58mṛgarājaṃ hitairvākyaiḥ sambodhayitumāgamat
|
putra naitat tvayā grāhyaṃ kapaṭārambhasaṃyutam||58||

59karmasaṃgharṣajairdoṣairduṣyetāśucibhiḥ śuciḥ
|
nocchritaṃ sahate kaścitprakriyā vairakārikā||59||

60śucerapi hi yuktasya doṣa eva nipātyate
|
munerapi vanasthasya svāni karmāṇi kurvataḥ||60||

61utpādyante trayaḥ pakṣā mitrodāsīnaśatravaḥ
|
lubdhānāṃ śucayo dveṣyāḥ kātarāṇāṃ tarasvinaḥ||61||

62mūrkhāṇāṃ paṇḍitā dveṣyā daridrāṇāṃ mahādhanāḥ
|
adhārmikāṇāṃ dharmiṣṭhā virūpāṇāṃ surūpiṇaḥ||62||

63bahavaḥ paṇḍitā mūrkhā lubdhā māyopajīvinaḥ
|
kuryurdoṣamadoṣasya bṛhaspatimaterapi||63||

64śūnyāttacca gṛhānmāṃsaṃ yadyapyapahṛtaṃ tava
|
necchate dīyamānaṃ ca sādhu tāvadvimṛśyatām||64||

65asabhyāḥ sabhyasaṃkāśāḥ sabhyāścāsabhyadarśanāḥ
|
dṛśyante vividhā bhāvāsteṣu yuktaṃ parīkṣaṇam||65||

66talavaddṛśyate vyoma khadyoto havyavāḍiva
|
na caivāsti talaṃ vyomni khadyote na hutāśanaḥ||66||

67tasmātpratyakṣadṛṣṭo'pi yukto hyarthaḥ parīkṣitum
|
parīkṣya jñāpayannarthānna paścātparitapyate||67||

68na duṣkaramidaṃ putra yatprabhurghātayetparam
|
ślāghanīyā yaśasyā ca loke prabhavatāṃ kṣamā||68||

69sthāpito'yaṃ tvayā putra sāmanteṣvapi viśrutaḥ
|
duḥkhenāsādyate pātraṃ dhāryatāmeṣa te suhṛt||69||

70dūṣitaṃ paradoṣairhi gṛhṇīte yo'nyathā śucim
|
svayaṃ saṃdūṣitāmātyaḥ kṣiprameva vinaśyati||70||

71tasmādapyarisaṃghātādgomāyoḥ kaścidāgataḥ
|
dharmātmā tena cākhyātaṃ yathaitatkapaṭaṃ kṛtam||71||

72tato vijñātacaritaḥ satkṛtya sa vimokṣitaḥ
|
pariṣvaktaśca sasnehaṃ mṛgendreṇa punaḥ punaḥ||72||

73anujñāpya mṛgendraṃ tu gomāyurnītiśāstravit
|
tenāmarṣeṇa saṃtaptaḥ prāyamāsitumaicchata||73||

74śārdūlastaṃ tu gomāyuṃ snehātprotphullalocanaḥ
|
avārayatsa dharmiṣṭhaṃ pūjayā pratipūjayan||74||

75taṃ sa gomāyurālokya snehādāgatasambhramam
|
uvāca praṇato vākyaṃ bāṣpagadgadayā girā||75||

76pūjito'haṃ tvayā pūrvaṃ paścāccaiva vimānitaḥ
|
pareṣāmāspadaṃ nīto vastuṃ nārhāmyahaṃ tvayi||76||

77asaṃtuṣṭāścyutāḥ sthānānmānātpratyavaropitāḥ
|
svayaṃ cāpahṛtā bhṛtyā ye cāpyupahitāḥ paraiḥ||77||

78parikṣīṇāśca lubdhāśca kruddhā bhītāḥ pratāritāḥ
|
hatasvā mānino ye ca tyaktādānā mahepsavaḥ||78||

79saṃtāpitāśca ye kecidvyasanaughapratīkṣiṇaḥ
|
antarhitāḥ sopahitāste sarve'parasādhanāḥ||79||

80avamānena yuktasya sthānabhraṣṭasya vā punaḥ
|
kathaṃ yāsyasi viśvāsamahaṃ tiṣṭhāmi vā katham||80||

81samartha iti saṃgṛhya sthāpayitvā parīkṣitaḥ
|
kṛtaṃ ca samayaṃ bhittvā tvayā'hamavamānitaḥ||81||

82prathamaṃ yaḥ samākhyātaḥ śīlavāniti saṃsadi
|
na vācyaṃ tasya vaiguṇyaṃ pratijñāṃ parirakṣatā||82||

83evaṃ cāvamatasyeha viśvāsaṃ me na yāsyasi
|
tvayi cāpetaviśvāse mamodvego bhaviṣyati||83||

84śaṃkitastvamahaṃ bhītaḥ paracchidrānudarśinaḥ
|
asnigdhāścaiva dustoṣāḥ karma caitadbahucchalam||84||

85duḥkhena śliṣyate bhinnaṃ śliṣṭaṃ duḥkhena bhidyate
|
bhinnā śliṣṭā tu yā prītirna sā snehena vartate||85||

86kaścideva hite bharturdṛśyate na parātmanoḥ
|
kāryāpekṣā hi vartante bhāvasnigdhāḥ sudurlabhāḥ||86||

87sudu:khaṃ puruṣajñānaṃ cittaṃ hyeṣāṃ calācalam
|
samartho vāpyaśaṅko vā śateṣveko'dhigamyate||87||

88akasmātprakriyā nṝṇāmakasmāccāpakarṣaṇam
|
śubhāśubhe mahattvaṃ ca prakartuṃ buddhilāghavam||88||

89evaṃvidhaṃ sāntvamuktvā dharmakāmārthahetumat
|
prasādayitvā rājānaṃ gomāyurvanamabhyagāt||89||

90agṛhyānunayaṃ tasya mṛgendrasya ca buddhimān
|
gomāyuḥ prāyamāsthāya tyaktvā dehaṃ divaṃ yayau||90||
