Mahābhārata 5.176 (Udyogaparvan)

1bhīṣma uvāca|
tataste tāpasāḥ sarve kāryavanto'bhavaṃstadā
|
tāṃ kanyāṃ cintayantaste kiṃ kāryamiti dharmiṇaḥ ||1||

2kecidāhuḥ piturveśma nīyatāmiti tāpasāḥ
|
kecidasmadupālambhe matiṃ cakrurhi tāpasāḥ ||2||

3kecicchālvapatiṃ gatvā niyojyamiti menire
|
neti kecidvyavasyanti pratyākhyātā hi tena sā ||3||

4evaṃ gate tu kiṃ śakyaṃ bhadre kartuṃ manīṣibhiḥ
|
punarūcuśca tāṃ sarve tāpasāḥ saṃśitavratāḥ ||4||

5alaṃ pravrajiteneha bhadre śṛṇu hitaṃ vacaḥ
|
ito gacchasva bhadraṃ te pitureva niveśanam ||5||

6pratipatsyati rājā sa pitā te yadanantaram
|
tatra vatsyasi kalyāṇi sukhaṃ sarvaguṇānvitā ||6||

7na ca te'nyā gatirnyāyyā bhavedbhadre yathā pitā
|
patirvā'pi gatirnāryāḥ pitā vā varavarṇini ||7||

8gatiḥ patiḥ samasthāyā viṣame ca pitā gatiḥ
|
pravrajyā hi suduḥkheyaṃ sukumāryā viśeṣataḥ ||8||

9rājaputryāḥ prakṛtyā ca kumāryāstava bhāmini
|
bhadre doṣā hi vidyante bahavo varavarṇini ||9||

10āśrame vai vasantyāste na bhaveyuḥ piturguhe
|
tatastvanye'bruvan vākyaṃ tāpasāstāṃ tapasvinīm ||10||

11tvāmihaikākinīṃ dṛṣṭvā nirjane gahane vane
|
prārthayiṣyanti rājānastasmānmaivaṃ manaḥ kṛthāḥ ||11||

12ambovāca
|
na śakyaṃ kāśinagaraṃ punargantuṃ piturguhān
|
avajñātā bhaviṣyāmi bāndhavānāṃ na saṃśayaḥ ||12||

13uṣitā'smi tathā bālye piturveśmani tāpasāḥ
|
nāhaṃ gamiṣye bhadraṃ vastatra yatra pitā mama
|
tapastaptumabhīpsāmi tāpasaiḥ parirakṣitā ||13||

14yathā pare'pi me loke na syādevaṃ mahātyayaḥ
|
daurbhāgyaṃ tāpasaśreṣṭhāstasmāttapsyāmyahaṃ tapaḥ ||14||

15bhīṣma uvāca|
ityevaṃ teṣu vipreṣu cintayatsu yathātatham
|
rājarṣistadvanaṃ prāptastapasvī hotravāhanaḥ ||15||

16tataste tāpasāḥ sarve pūjayanti sma taṃ nṛpam
|
pūjābhiḥ svāgatādyābhirāsanenodakena ca ||16||

17tasyopaviṣṭasya sato viśrāntasyopaśṛṇvataḥ
|
punareva kathāṃ cakruḥ kanyāṃ prati vanaukasaḥ ||17||

18ambāyāstāṃ kathāṃ śrutvā kāśirājñaśca bhārata
|
rājarṣiḥ sa mahātejā babhūvodvignamānasaḥ ||18||

19tāṃ tathāvādinīṃ śrutvā dṛṣṭvā ca sa mahātapāḥ
|
rājarṣiḥ kṛpayā''viṣṭo mahātmā hotravāhanaḥ ||19||

20sa vepamāna utthāya mātustasyāḥ pitā tadā
|
tāṃ kanyāmaṅkamāropya paryaśvāsayata prabho ||20||

21sa tāmapṛcchat kārsnyena vyasanotpattimāditaḥ
|
sā ca tasmai yathāvṛttaṃ vistareṇa nyavedayat ||21||

22tataḥ sa rājarṣirabhūdduḥkhaśokasamanvitaḥ
|
kāryaṃ ca pratipede tanmanasā sumahātapāḥ ||22||

23abravīdvepamānaśca kanyāmārtāṃ suduḥkhitaḥ
|
mā gāḥ piturgṛhaṃ bhadre mātuste janako hyaham ||23||

24duḥkhaṃ chindyāmahaṃ te vai mayi vartasva putrike
|
paryāptaṃ te mano vatse yadevaṃ pariśuṣyasi ||24||

25gaccha madvacanādrāmaṃ jāmadagnyaṃ tapasvinam
|
rāmaste sumahadduḥkhaṃ śokaṃ caivāpaneṣyati ||25||

26haniṣyati raṇe bhīṣmaṃ na kariṣyati cedvacaḥ
|
taṃ gaccha bhārgavaśreṣṭhaṃ kālāgnisamatejasam ||26||

27pratiṣṭhāpayitā sa tvāṃ sa me pathi mahātapāḥ
|
tatastu susvaraṃ bāṣpamutsṛjantī punaḥ punaḥ ||27||

28abravīt pitaraṃ mātuḥ sā tadā hotravāhanam
|
abhivādayitvā śirasā gamiṣye tava śāsanāt ||28||

29api nāmādya paśyeyamāryaṃ taṃ lokaviśrutam
|
kathaṃ ca tīvraṃ duḥkhaṃ me nāśayiṣyati bhārgavaḥ
|
etadicchāmyahaṃ jñātuṃ yathā yāsyāmi tatra vai ||29||

30hotravāhana uvāca|
rāmaṃ drakṣyasi bhadre tvaṃ jāmadagnyaṃ mahāvane
|
ugre tapasi vartantaṃ satyasandhaṃ mahābalam ||30||

31mahendraṃ vai giriśreṣṭhaṃ rāmo nityamupāsti ha
|
ṛṣayo vedavidvāṃso gandharvāpsarasastathā ||31||

32tatra gacchasva bhadraṃ te brūyāścainaṃ vaco mama
|
abhivādya ca taṃ mūrdhnā tapovṛddhaṃ dṛḍhavratam ||32||

33brūyāścainaṃ punarbhadre yatte kāryaṃ manīṣitam
|
mayi saṃkīrtite rāmaḥ sarvaṃ tatte kariṣyati ||33||

34mama rāmaḥ sakhā vatse prītiyuktaḥ suhṛcca me
|
jamadagnisuto vīraḥ sarvaśastrabhṛtāṃ varaḥ ||34||

35evaṃ bruvati kanyāṃ tu pārthive hotravāhane
|
akṛtavraṇaḥ prādurāsīdrāmasyānucaraḥ priyaḥ ||35||

36tataste munayaḥ sarve samuttasthuḥ sahasraśaḥ
|
sa ca rājā vayovṛddhaḥ sṛñjayo hotravāhanaḥ ||36||

37tato dṛṣṭvā kṛtātithyamanyonyaṃ te vanaukasaḥ
|
sahitā bharataśreṣṭha niṣeduḥ parivārya tam ||37||

38tataste kathayāmāsuḥ kathāstāstā manoramāḥ
|
dhanyā divyāśca rājendra prītiharṣamudā yutāḥ ||38||

39tataḥ kathānte rājarṣirmahātmā hotravāhanaḥ
|
rāmaṃ śreṣṭhaṃ maharṣīṇāmapṛcchadakṛtavraṇam ||39||

40kva saṃprati mahābāho jāmadagnyaḥ pratāpavān
|
akṛtavraṇa śakyo vai draṣṭuṃ vedavidāṃ vara ||40||

41akṛtavraṇa uvāca|
bhavantameva satataṃ rāmaḥ kīrtayati prabho
|
sṛñjayo me priyasakho rājarṣiriti pārthiva ||41||

42iha rāmaḥ prabhāte śvo bhaviteti matirmama
|
draṣṭā'syenamihāyāntaṃ tava darśanakāṅkṣayā ||42||

43iyaṃ ca kanyā rājarṣe kimarthaṃ vanamāgatā
|
kasya ceyaṃ tava ca kā bhavatīcchāmi veditum ||43||

44hotravāhana uvāca|
dauhitrīyaṃ mama vibho kāśirājasutā priyā
|
jyeṣṭhā svayaṃvare tasthau bhaginībhyāṃ sahānagha ||44||

45iyamambeti vikhyātā jyeṣṭhā kāśipateḥ sutā
|
ambikāmbālike kanye kanīyasyau tapodhana ||45||

46sametaṃ pārthivaṃ kṣatraṃ kāśipuryāṃ tato'bhavat
|
kanyānimittaṃ viprarṣe tatrāsīdutsavo mahān ||46||

47tataḥ kila mahāvīryo bhīṣmaḥ śāntanavo nṛpān
|
adhikṣipya mahātejāstistraḥ kanyā jahāra tāḥ ||47||

48nirjitya pṛthivīpālānatha bhīṣmo gajāhvayam
|
ājagāma viśuddhātmā kanyābhiḥ saha bhārataḥ ||48||

49satyavatyai nivedyātha vivāhaṃ samanantaram
|
bhrāturvicitravīryasya samājñāpayata prabhuḥ ||49||

50taṃ tu vaivāhikaṃ dṛṣṭvā kanyeyaṃ samupārjitam
|
abravīttatra gāṅgeyaṃ mantrimadhye dvijarṣabha ||50||

51mayā śālvapatirvīro manasā'bhivṛtaḥ patiḥ
|
na māmarhasi dharmajña dātuṃ bhrātre'nyamānasām ||51||

52tacchrutvā vacanaṃ bhīṣmaḥ saṃmantrya saha mantribhiḥ
|
niścitya visasarjemāṃ satyavatyā mate sthitaḥ ||52||

53anujñātā tu bhīṣmeṇa śālvaṃ saubhapatiṃ tataḥ
|
kanyeyaṃ muditā tatra kāle vacanamabravīt ||53||

54visarjitā'smi bhīṣmeṇa dharmaṃ māṃ pratipādaya
|
manasā'bhivṛtaḥ pūrvaṃ mayā tvaṃ pārthivarṣabha ||54||

55pratyācakhyau ca śālvo'syāścāritrasyābhiśaṅkitaḥ
|
seyaṃ tapovanaṃ prāptā tāpasye'bhiratā bhṛśam ||55||

56mayā ca pratyabhijñātā vaṃśasya parikīrtanāt
|
asya duḥkhasya cotpattiṃ bhīṣmameveha manyate ||56||

57ambovāca
|
bhagavannevameveha yathā''ha pṛthivīpatiḥ
|
śarīrakartā māturme sṛñjayo hotravāhanaḥ ||57||

58na hyutsahe svanagaraṃ pratiyātuṃ tapodhana
|
apamānabhayāccaiva vrīḍayā ca mahāmune ||58||

59yattu māṃ bhagavān rāmo vakṣyati dvijasattama
|
tanme kāryatamaṃ kāryamiti me bhagavanmatiḥ ||59||
