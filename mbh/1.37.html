Mahābhārata 1.37 (Ādiparvan)

1sautiruvāca|
mātuḥ sakāśāttaṃ śāpaṃ śrutvā vai pannagottamaḥ
|
vāsukiścintayāmāsa śāpo'yaṃ na bhavetkatham||1||

2tataḥ sa mantrayāmāsa bhrātṛbhiḥ saha sarvaśaḥ
|
airāvataprabhṛtibhiḥ sarvairdharmaparāyaṇaiḥ||2||

3vāsukiruvāca|
ayaṃ śāpo yathoddiṣṭo viditaṃ vastathā'naghāḥ
|
tasya śāpasya mokṣārthaṃ mantrayitvā yatāmahe||3||

4sarveṣāmeva śāpānāṃ pratighāto hi vidyate
|
na tu mātrā'bhiśaptānāṃ mokṣaḥ kvacana vidyate||4||

5avyayasyāprameyasya satyasya ca tathāgrataḥ
|
śaptā ityeva me śrutvā jāyate hṛdi vepathuḥ||5||

6nūnaṃ sarvavināśo'yamasmākaṃ samupāgataḥ
|
na hyetāṃ so'vyayo devaḥ śapatnīṃ pratyaṣedhayat||6||

7tasmātsaṃmantrayāmo'dya bhujaṅgānāmanāmayam
|
yathā bhaveddhi sarveṣāṃ mā naḥ kālo'tyagādayam||7||

8sarva eva hi na stāvadbuddhimanto vicakṣaṇāḥ
|
api mantrayamāṇā hi hetuṃ paśyāma mokṣaṇe||8||

9yathā naṣṭaṃ purā devā gūḍhamagniṃ guhāgatam
|
yathā sa yajño na bhavedyathā vā'pi parābhavaḥ
|
janamejayasya sarpāṇāṃ vināśakaraṇāya vai||9||

10sautiruvāca|
tathetyuktvā tataḥ sarve kādraveyāḥ samāgatāḥ
|
samayaṃ cakrire tatra mantrabuddhiviśāradāḥ||10||

11eke tatrābruvannāgā vayaṃ bhūtvā dvijarṣabhāḥ
|
janamejayaṃ tu bhikṣāmo yajñastena bhavediti||11||

12apare tvabruvannāgāstatra paṇḍitamāninaḥ
|
mantriṇo'sya vayaṃ sarve bhaviṣyāmaḥ susaṃmatāḥ||12||

13sa naḥ prakṣyati sarveṣu kāryeṣvarthaviniścayam
|
tatra buddhiṃ pradāsyāmo yathā yajño nivartsyati||13||

14sa no bahumatānrājā buddhyā buddhimatāṃ varaḥ
|
yajñārthaṃ prakṣyati vyaktaṃ neti vakṣyāmahe vayam||14||

15darśayanto bahūndoṣānpretya ceha ca dāruṇān
|
hetubhiḥ kāraṇaiścaiva yathā yajño bhavenna saḥ||15||

16athavā'yamupādhyāyaḥ kratostasya bhaviṣyati
|
sarpasatravidhānajño rājakāryahite rataḥ||16||

17taṃ gatvā daśatāṃ kaścidbhujaṅgaḥ sa mariṣyati
|
tasminmṛte yajñakāre kratuḥ sa na bhaviṣyati||17||

18ye cānye sarpasatrajñā bhaviṣyantyasya cartvijaḥ
|
tāṃśca sarvāndaśiṣyāmaḥ kṛtamevaṃ bhaviṣyati||18||

19apare tvabruvannāgā dharmātmāno dayālavaḥ
|
abuddhireṣā bhavatāṃ brahmahatyā na śobhanam||19||

20samyaksaddharmamūlā vai vyasane śāntiruttamā
|
adharmottaratā nāma kṛtsnaṃ vyāpādayejjagat||20||

21apare tvabruvannāgāḥ samiddhaṃ jātavedasam
|
varṣairnirvāpayiṣyāmo meghā bhūtvā savidyutaḥ||21||

22srugbhāṇḍaṃ niśi gatvā ca apare bhujagottamāḥ
|
pramattānāṃ harantvāśu vighna evaṃ bhaviṣyati|| 22||

23yajñe vā bhujagāstasmiñcchataśo'tha sahasraśaḥ
|
janāndaśantu vai sarve naivaṃ trāso bhaviṣyati||23||

24athavā saṃskṛtaṃ bhojyaṃ dūṣayantu bhujaṅgamāḥ
|
svena mūtrapurīṣeṇa sarvabhojyavināśinā||24||

25apare tvabruvaṃstatra ṛtvijo'sya bhavāmahe
|
yajñavighnaṃ kariṣyāmo dīyatāṃ dakṣiṇā iti||25||

26vaśyatāṃ ca gato'sau naḥ kariṣyati yathepsitam
|
apare tvabruvaṃstatra jale prakrīḍitaṃ nṛpam||26||

27gṛhamānīya badhnīmaḥ kraturevaṃ bhavenna saḥ
|
apare tvabruvaṃstatra nāgāḥ paṇḍitamāninaḥ||27||

28daśāmastaṃ pragṛhyāśu kṛtapevaṃ bhaviṣyati
|
chinnaṃ mūlamanarthānāṃ mṛte tasminbhaviṣyati||28||

29eṣā no naiṣṭhikī buddhiḥ sarveṣāmīkṣaṇaśravaḥ
|
atha yanmanyase rājandrutaṃ tatsaṃvidhīyatām||29||

30ityuktvā samudaikṣanta vāsukiṃ pannagottamam
|
vāsukiścāpi saṃcintya tānuvāca bhujaṅgamān||30||

31naiṣā vo naiṣṭhikī buddhirmatā kartuṃ bhujaṅgamāḥ
|
sarveṣāmeva me buddhiḥ pannagānāṃ na rocate||31||

32kiṃ tatra saṃvidhātavyaṃ bhavatāṃ syāddhitaṃ tu yat
|
śreyaḥ prasādanaṃ manye kaśyapaśya mahātmanaḥ||32||

33jñātivargasya sauhārdādātmanaśca bhujaṅgamāḥ
|
na ca jānāti me buddhiḥ kiṃcitkartuṃ vaco hivaḥ||33||

34mayā hīdaṃ vidhātavyaṃ bhavatāṃ yaddhitaṃ bhavet
|
anenāhaṃ bhṛśaṃ tapye guṇadoṣau madāśrayau||34||
