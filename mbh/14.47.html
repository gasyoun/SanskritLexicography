Mahābhārata 14.47 (Āśvamedhikaparvan)

1brahmovāca|
saṃnyāsaṃ tapa ityāhurvṛddhā niścitavādinaḥ
|
brāhmaṇā brahmayonisthā jñānaṃ brahma paraṃ viduḥ||1||

2atidūrātmakaṃ brahma vedavidyāvyapāśrayam
|
nirdvandvaṃ nirguṇaṃ nityamacintyaguṇamuttamam||2||

3jñānena tapasā caiva dhīrāḥ paśyanti tatparam
|
nirṇiktamanasaḥ pūtā vyutkrāntarajaso'malāḥ||3||

4tapasā kṣemamadhvānaṃ gacchanti parameśvaram
|
saṃnyāsaniratā nityaṃ ye ca brahmavido janāḥ||4||

5tapaḥ pradīpa ityāhurācāro dharmasādhakaḥ
|
jñānaṃ vai paramaṃ vidyātsaṃnyāsaṃ nṛpa uttamam||5||

6yastu veda nirādhāraṃ jñānaṃ nattvaviniścayāt
|
sarvabhūnasthamātmānaṃ sa sarvagatiriṣyate||6||

7yo vidvānsahavāsaṃ ca vivāsaṃ caiva paśyati
|
tathaivaikatvanānātve sa duḥkhātpratimucyate||7||

8yo na kāmayate kiñcinna kiñcidavamanyate
|
ihalokastha evaiṣa brahmabhūyāya kalpate||8||

9pradhānaguṇatattvajñaḥ sarvabhūtapradhānavit
|
nirmamo nirahaṅkāro mucyate nātra saṃśayaḥ||9||

10nirdvandvo nirnamaskāro niḥsvadhākāra eva ca
|
nirguṇaṃ nityamadvandvaṃ praśamenaiva gacchati||10||

11hitvā guṇamayaṃ sarvaṃ karma jantuḥ śubhāśubham
|
ubhe satyānṛte hitvā mucyate nātra saṃśayaḥ||11||

12avyaktayoniprabhavo buddhiskandhamayo mahān
|
mahāhaṅkāraviṭapa indriyāṅkarakoṭaraḥ||12||

13mahābhūtaviśālaśca viśeṣayati śākhinaḥ
|
sadāpatraḥ sadāpuṣpaḥ śubhāśubhaphalodayaḥ||13||

14ājīvyaḥ sarvabhūtānāṃ brahmavṛkṣaḥ sanātanaḥ
|
enaṃ chittvā ca bhittvā ca tattvajñānāsinā budhaḥ||14||

15hitvā saṅgamayānpāśānmṛtyujanmajarodayān
|
nirmamo nirahaṅkāro mucyate nātra saṃśayaḥ||15||

16dvāvimau pakṣiṇau nityau saṃkṣepau cāpyacetanau
|
etābhyāṃ tu paro yo'yaścetanāvānsa ucyate||16||

17acetanaḥ sattvasaṅkhyāvimuktaḥ sattvātparaṃ cetayate'ntarātmā
|
sakṣetravitsarvasaṅkhyātabuddhirguṇātigo mucyate sarvapāpaiḥ||17||
