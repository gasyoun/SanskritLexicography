Mahābhārata 7.91 (Droṇaparvan)

1sañjaya uvāca|
duḥśāsanabalaṃ hatvā savyasācī mahārathaḥ
|
sindhurājaṃ parīpsan vai droṇānīkamupādravat ||1||

2sa tu droṇaṃ samāsādya vyūhasya pramukhe sthitam
|
kṛtāñjaliridaṃ vākyaṃ kṛṣṇasyānumate'bravīt ||2||

3śivena dhyāhi māṃ brahman svasti caiva vadasva me
|
bhavatprasādādicchāmi praveṣṭuṃ durbhidāṃ camūm ||3||

4bhavān pitṛsamo mahyaṃ dharmarājasamo'pi ca
|
tathā kṛṣṇasamaścaiva satyametadbravīmi te ||4||

5aśvatthāmā yathā tāta rakṣaṇīyastvayā'nagha
|
tathāhamapi te rakṣyaḥ sadaiva dvijasattama ||5||

6tava prasādādiccheyaṃ sindhurājānamāhave
|
nihantuṃ dvipadāṃ śreṣṭha pratijñāṃ rakṣa me prabho ||6||

7sañjaya uvāca|
evamuktastadācāryaḥ pratyuvāca smayanniva
|
māmajitvā na bībhatso śakyo jetuṃ jayadrathaḥ ||7||

8etāvaduktvā taṃ droṇaḥ śaravrātairavākirat
|
sarathāśvadhvajaṃ tīkṣṇaiḥ prahasanvai sasārathim ||8||

9tato'rjunaḥ śaravrātān droṇasyāvārya sāyakaiḥ
|
droṇamabhyadravadbāṇairghorarūpairmahattaraiḥ ||9||

10vivyādha caraṇe droṇamanumānya viśāmpate
|
kṣatradharmaṃ samāsthāya navabhiḥ sāyakaiḥ punaḥ ||10||

11tasyeṣūniṣubhiśchitvā droṇo vivyādha tāvubhau
|
viyagnijvalitaprakhyairiṣubhiḥ kṛṣṇapāṇḍavau ||11||

12iyeṣa pāṇḍavastasya bāṇaiśchettuṃ śarāsanam
|
tasya cintayatastvevaṃ phālgunasya mahātmanaḥ ||12||

13droṇaḥ śarairasambhrānto jyāṃ cicchedāśu vīryavān
|
vivyādha ca hayānasya dhvajaṃ sārathimeva ca ||13||

14arjunaṃ ca śarairvīraḥ smayamāno'bhyavākirat
|
etasminnantare pārthaḥ sajyaṃ kṛtvā mahaddhanuḥ ||14||

15viśeṣayiṣyannācāryaṃ sarvāstraviduṣāṃ varaḥ
|
mumoca ṣaṭśatān bāṇān gṛhītvaikamiva drutam ||15||

16punaḥ saptaśatānanyān sahasraṃ cānivartinaḥ
|
cikṣepāyutaśaścānyāṃste ghnan droṇasya tāṃ camūm ||16||

17taiḥ samyagastairbalinā kṛtinā citrayodhinā
|
manuṣyavājimātaṅgā viddhāḥ peturgatāsavaḥ ||17||

18visūtāśvadhvajāḥ petuḥ sañchinnāyudhajīvitāḥ
|
rathino rathamukhyebhyaḥ sahasā śarapīḍitāḥ ||18||

19cūrṇitākṣiptadagdhānāṃ vajrānilahutāśanaiḥ
|
tulyarūpā gajāḥ peturgiryagrāmbudaveśmanām ||19||

20peturaśvasahasrāṇi prahatānyarjuneṣubhiḥ
|
haṃsā himavataḥ pṛṣṭhe vāridhiprahatā iva ||20||

21rathāśvadvipapattyodhāḥ salilaughā ivādbhutāḥ
|
yugāntādityaraśmyābhaiḥ pāṇḍavāstraśarairhatāḥ ||21||

22taṃ pāṇḍavādityaśarāṃśujālaṃ kurupravīrān yudhi niṣṭapantam
|
sa droṇameghaḥ śaravṛṣṭivegaiḥ prācchādayanmegha ivārkaraśmīn ||22||

23athātyarthaṃ visṛṣṭena dviṣatāmasubhojinā
|
ājaghne vakṣasi droṇo nārācena dhanañjayam ||23||

24sa vihvalitasarvāṅgaḥ kṣitikampe yathācalaḥ
|
dhairyamālambya bībhatsurdroṇaṃ vivyādha patribhiḥ ||24||

25droṇastu pañcabhirbāṇairvāsudevamatāḍayat
|
arjunaṃ ca trisaptatyā dhvajaṃ cāsya tribhiḥ śaraiḥ ||25||

26viśeṣayiṣyan śiṣyaṃ ca droṇo rājan parākramī
|
adṛśyamarjunaṃ cakre nimeṣāccharavṛṣṭibhiḥ ||26||

27prasaktān patato'drākṣma bhāradvājasya sāyakān
|
maṇḍalīkṛtamevāsya dhanuścādṛśyatādbhutam ||27||

28te'bhyayuḥ samare rājan vāsudevadhanañjayau
|
droṇasṛṣṭāḥ subahavaḥ kaṅkapatraparicchadāḥ ||28||

29taddṛṣṭvā tādṛśaṃ yuddhaṃ droṇapāṇḍavayostadā
|
vāsudevo mahābuddhiḥ kāryavattāmacintayat ||29||

30tato'bravīdvāsudevo dhanañjayamidaṃ vacaḥ
|
pārtha pārtha mahābāho na naḥ kālātyayo bhavet ||30||

31droṇamutsṛjya gacchāmaḥ kṛtyametanmahattaram
|
pārthaścāpyabravīt kṛṣṇaṃ yatheṣṭamiti keśavam ||31||

32tataḥ pradakṣiṇaṃ kṛtvā droṇaṃ prāyānmahābhujam
|
parivṛttaśca bībhatsuragacchadvisṛjañśarān ||32||

33tato'bravīt svayaṃ droṇaḥ kvedaṃ pāṇḍava gamyate
|
nanu nāma raṇe śatrumajitvā na nivartase ||33||

34arjuna uvāca|
gururbhavānna me śatruḥ śiṣyaḥ putrasamo'smi te
|
na cāsti sa pumā~lloke yastvāṃ yudhi parājayet ||34||

35sañjaya uvāca|
evaṃ bruvāṇo bībhatsurjayadrathavadhotsukaḥ
|
tvarāyukto mahābāhustvatsainyaṃ samupādravat ||35||

36taṃ cakrarakṣau pāñcālyau yudhāmanyūttamaujasau
|
anvayātāṃ mahātmānau viśantaṃ tāvakaṃ balam ||36||

37tato jayo mahārāja kṛtavarmā ca sātvataḥ
|
kāmbojaśca śrutāyuśca dhanañjayamavārayan ||37||

38teṣāṃ daśa sahasrāṇi rathānāmanuyāyinām
|
abhīṣāhāḥ śūrasenāḥ śibayo'tha vasātayaḥ ||38||

39māvellakā lalitthāśca kekayā madrakāstathā
|
nārāyaṇāśca gopālāḥ kāmbojānāṃ ca ye gaṇāḥ ||39||

40karṇena vijitāḥ pūrvaṃ saṅgrāme śūrasaṃmatāḥ
|
bhāradvājaṃ puraskṛtya hṛṣṭātmāno'rjunaṃ prati ||40||

41putraśokābhisaṃtaptaṃ kruddhaṃ mṛtyumivāntakam
|
tyajantaṃ tumule prāṇān sannaddhaṃ citrayodhinam ||41||

42gāhamānamanīkāni mātaṅgamiva yūthapam
|
maheṣvāsaṃ parākrāntaṃ naravyāghramavārayan ||42||

43tataḥ pravavṛte yuddhaṃ tumulaṃ lomaharṣaṇam
|
anyonyaṃ vai prārthayatāṃ yodhānāmarjunasya ca ||43||

44jayadrathavadhaprepsumāyāntaṃ puruṣarṣabham
|
nyavārayanta sahitāḥ kriyā vyādhimivotthitam ||44||
