Mahābhārata 5.114 (Udyogaparvan)

1nārada uvāca|
athāha gālavaṃ dīnaṃ suparṇaḥ patatāṃ varaḥ
|
nirmitaṃ vahninā bhūmau vāyunā śodhitaṃ tathā
|
yasmāddhiraṇmayaṃ sarvaṃ hiraṇyaṃ tena cocyate ||1||

2dhatte dhārayate cedametasmāt kāraṇāddhanam
|
tadetattriṣu lokeṣu dhanaṃ tiṣṭhati śāśvatam ||2||

3nityaṃ proṣṭhapadābhyāṃ ca śukre dhanapatau tathā
|
manuṣyebhyaḥ samādatte śukraścittārjitaṃ dhanam ||3||

4ajaikapāda‌hirbudhnyai rakṣyate dhanadane ca
|
evaṃ na śakyate labdhumalabdhavyaṃ dvijarṣabha
|
ṛte ca dhanamaśvānāṃ nāvāptirvidyate tava ||4||

5sa tvaṃ yācātra rājānaṃ kañcidrājarṣivaṃśajam
|
apīḍya rājā paurān hi yo nau kuryāt‌ kṛtārthinau ||5||

6asti somānvavāye me jātaḥ kaścinnṛpaḥ sakhā
|
abhigacchāvahe taṃ vai tasyāsti vibhavo bhuvi ||6||

7yayātirnāma rājarṣirnāhuṣaḥ satyavikramaḥ
|
sa dāsyati mayā cokto bhavatā cārthitaḥ svayam ||7||

8vibhavaścāsya sumahānāsīddhanapateriva
|
evaṃ gurudhanaṃ vidvan dānenaiva viśodhaya ||8||

9tathā tau kathayantau ca cintayantau ca yatkṣamam
|
pratiṣṭhāne narapatiṃ yayātiṃ pratyupasthitau ||9||

10pratigṛhya ca satkārarairdhyapādyādikaṃ varam
|
pṛṣṭaścāgamane hetumuvāca vinatāsutaḥ ||10||

11ayaṃ me nāhuṣa sakhā gālavastapaso nidhiḥ
|
viśvāmitrasya śiṣyo'bhūdvarṣāṇyayutaśo nṛpa ||11||

12so'yaṃ tenābhyanujñāta upakārepsayā dvijaḥ
|
tamāha bhagavān kāle dadāni gurudakṣiṇām ||12||

13asakṛttena coktena kiñcidāgatamanyunā
|
ayamuktaḥ prayaccheti jānatā vibhavaṃ laghu ||13||

14ekataḥ śyāmakarṇānāṃ śubhrāṇāṃ śuddhajanmanām
|
aṣṭau śatāni me dehi hayānāṃ candravarcasām ||14||

15gurvartho dīyatāmeṣa yadi gālava manyase
|
ityevamāha sakrodho viśvāmitrastapodhanaḥ ||15||

16so'yaṃ śokena mahatā tapyamāno dvijarṣabhaḥ
|
aśaktaḥ pratikartuṃ tadbhavantaṃ śaraṇaṃ gataḥ ||16||

17pratigṛhya naravyāghra tvatto bhikṣāṃ gatavyathaḥ
|
kṛtvā'pavargaṃ gurave cariṣyati mahattapaḥ ||17||

18tapasaḥ saṃvibhāgena bhavantamapi yokṣyate
|
svena rājarṣitapasā pūrṇaṃ tvāṃ pūrayiṣyati ||18||

19yāvanti romāṇi haye bhavantīha nareśvara
|
tāvanto vājino lokān prāpnuvanti mahīpate ||19||

20pātraṃ pratigrahasyāyaṃ dātuṃ pātraṃ tathā bhavān
|
śaṅkhe kṣīramivāsiktaṃ bhavatvetattathopamam ||20||
