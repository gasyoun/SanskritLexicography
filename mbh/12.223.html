Mahābhārata 12.223 (Śāntiparvan)

1yudhiṣṭhira uvāca|
yathā buddhyā mahīpālo bhraṣṭaśrīrvicarenmahīm
|
kāladaṇḍaviniṣpiṣṭastanme brūhi pitāmaha ||1||

2bhīṣma uvāca|
atrāpyudāharantīmamitihāsaṃ purātanam
|
vāsavasya ca saṃvādaṃ balervairocanasya ca ||2||

3pitāmahamupāgamya praṇipatya kṛtāñjaliḥ
|
sarvānevāsurān jitvā baliṃ papraccha vāsavaḥ ||3||

4yasya sma dadato vittaṃ na kadācana hīyate
|
taṃ baliṃ nādhigacchāmi brahmannācakṣva me balim ||4||

5sa vāyurvaruṇaścaiva sa raviḥ sa ca candramāḥ
|
so'gnistapati bhūtāni jalaṃ ca sa bhavatyuta ||5||

6taṃ baliṃ nādhigacchāmi brahmannācakṣva me balim
|
sa eva hyastamayate sa sma vidyotate diśaḥ ||6||

7sa varṣati sma varṣāṇi yathākālamatandritaḥ
|
taṃ baliṃ nādhigacchāmi brahmannācakṣva me balim ||7||

8brahmovāca|
naitatte sādhu maghavanyadenamanupṛcchasi
|
pṛṣṭastu nānṛtaṃ brūyāttasmādvakṣyāmi te balim ||8||

9uṣṭeṣu yadi vā goṣu khareṣvaśveṣu vā punaḥ
|
variṣṭho bhavitā jantuḥ śūnyāgāre śacīpate ||9||

10śakra uvāca |
yadi sma balinā brahman śūnyāgāre sameyivān
|
hanyāmenaṃ na vā hanyāṃ tadbrahmannanuśādhi mām ||10||

11brahmovāca|
mā sma śakra baliṃ hiṃsīrna balirvadhamarhati
|
nyāyastu śakra praṣṭavyastvayā vāsava kāmyayā ||11||

12bhīṣma uvaca
|
evamukto bhagavatā mahendraḥ pṛthivīṃ tadā
|
cacārairāvataskandhamadhiruhya śriyā vṛtaḥ ||12||

13tato dadarśa sa baliṃ kharaveṣeṇa saṃvṛtam
|
yathā''khyātaṃ bhagavatā śūnyāgārakṛtālayam ||13||

14śakra uvāca |
kharayonimanuprāptastuṣabhakṣo'si dānava
|
iyaṃ te yoniradhamā śocasyāho na śocasi ||14||

15adṛṣṭaṃ bata paśyāmi dviṣatāṃ vaśāmāgatam
|
śriyā vihīnaṃ mitraiśca bhraṣṭavīrya-parākramam ||15||

16yattadyānasahasraistvaṃ jñātibhiḥ parivāritaḥ
|
lokānpratāpayansarvānyāsyasmānavitarkayan ||16||

17tvanmukhāścaiva daiteya vyatiṣṭhaṃstva śāsane
|
akṛṣṭapacyā ca mahī tavaiśvarye babhūva ha ||17||

18idaṃ ca te'dya vyasanaṃ śocasyāho na śocasi
|
yadā''tiṣṭhaḥ samudrasya pūrvakūle vilelihan ||18||

19jñātīn vibhajato vittaṃ tadāsītte manaḥ katham
|
yatte sahasrasamitā nanṛturdevayoṣitaḥ ||19||

20bahūni varṣapūgāni vihāre dīpyataḥ śriyā
|
sarvāḥ puṣkaramālinyaḥ sarvāḥ kāñcanasaprabhāḥ ||20||

21kathamadya tadā caiva manaste dānaveśvara
|
chatraṃ tavāsītsumahatsauvarṇaṃ ratnabhūṣitam ||21||

22nanṛtustatra gandharvāḥ ṣaṭ sahasrāṇi saptadhā
|
yūpastavāsītsumahānyajataḥ sarvakāñcanaḥ ||22||

23yatrādadaḥ sahasrāṇi ayutānāṃ gavāṃ daśa
|
anantaraṃ sahasreṇa tadā''sīddainya kā matiḥ ||23||

24yadā ca pṛthivīṃ sarvāṃ yajamāno'nuparyagāḥ
|
śamyākṣepeṇa vidhinā tadā''sītkiṃ tu te hṛdi ||24||

25na te paśyāmi bhṛṅgāraṃ na cchatraṃ vyajane na ca
|
brahmadattāṃ ca te mālāṃ na paśyāmyasurādhipa ||25||

26baliruvāca |
na tvaṃ paśyasi bhṛṅgāraṃ na cchatraṃ vyajane na ca
|
brahmadattāṃ ca me mālāṃ na tvaṃ drakṣyasi vāsava ||26||

27guhāyāṃ nihitāni tvaṃ mama ratnāni pṛcchasi
|
yadā me bhavitā kālastadā tvaṃ yāni drakṣyasi ||27||

28na tvetadanurūpaṃ te yaśaso vā kulasya ca
|
samṛddhārtho samṛddhārthaṃ yanmāṃ katthitumicchasi ||28||

29na hi duḥkheṣu śocante na prahṛṣyanti cardhiṣu
|
kṛtaprajñā jñānatṛptāḥ kṣāntāḥ santo manīṣiṇaḥ ||29||

30tvaṃ tu prākṛtayā buddhyā purandara vikatthase
|
yadā'hamiva bhāvī syāstadā naivaṃ vadiṣyasi ||30||
