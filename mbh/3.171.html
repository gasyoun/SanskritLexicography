Mahābhārata 3.171 (Āraṇyakaparvan)

1arjuna uvāca|
tato'śmavarṣaṃ sumahat prādurāsīt samantataḥ
|
nagamātraiḥ śilākhaṇḍaistanmāṃ dṛḍhamapīḍayat||1||

2tadahaṃ vajrasaṅkāśairmahendrāstrapracoditaiḥ
|
acūrṇayaṃ vegavadbhiḥ śarajālairmahāhave||2||

3cūrṇyamāne'śmavarṣe tu pāvakaḥ samajāyata
|
tatrāśmacūrṇānyapatan pāvakaprakarā iva||3||

4tato'śmavarṣe vihate jalavarṣaṃ mahattaram
|
dhārābhirakṣamātrābhiḥ prādurāsīnmamāntike||4||

5nabhasaḥ pracyutā dhārāstigmavīryāḥ sahasraśaḥ
|
āvṛṇvan sarvato vyoma diśaścopadiśastathā||5||

6dhārāṇāṃ ca nipātena vāyorvisphūrjitena ca
|
garjitena ca daityānāṃ na prājñāyata kiñcana||6||

7dhārā divi ca saṃbaddhā vasudhāyāṃ ca sarvaśaḥ
|
vyāmohayanta māṃ tatra nipatantyo'niśaṃ bhuvi||7||

8tatropadiṣṭamindreṇa divyamastraṃ viśoṣaṇam
|
dīptaṃ prāhiṇavaṃ ghoramaśuṣyattena tajjalam||8||

9hate'śmavarṣe ca mayā jalavarṣe ca śoṣite
|
mumucurdānavā māyāmagniṃ vāyuṃ ca bhārata||9||

10tato'hamagniṃ vyadhamaṃ salilāstreṇa sarvaśaḥ
|
śailena ca mahāstreṇa vāyorvegamadhārayam||10||

11tasyāṃ pratihatāyāṃ te dānavā yuddhadurmadāḥ
|
prākurvan vividhāṃ māyāṃ yaugapadyena bhārata||11||

12tato varṣaṃ prādurabhūt sumahallomaharṣaṇam
|
astrāṇāṃ ghorarūpāṇāmagnervāyostathā'śmanām||12||

13sā tu māyāmayī vṛṣṭiḥ pīḍayāmāsa māṃ yudhi
|
atha ghoraṃ tamastīvraṃ prādurāsīt samantataḥ||13||

14tamasā saṃvṛte loke ghoreṇa paruṣeṇa ca
|
harayo vimukhāścāsan prāskhalaccāpi mātaliḥ||14||

15hastāddhi raśmayaścāsya pratodaḥ prāpatadbhuvi
|
asakṛccāha māṃ bhītaḥ kvāsīti bharatarṣabha||15||

16māṃ ca bhīrāviśattīvrā tasmin vigatacetasi
|
sa ca māṃ vigatajñānaḥ santrastamidamabravīt||16||

17surāṇāmasurāṇāṃ ca saṅgrāmaḥ sumahānabhūt
|
amṛtārthaṃ purā pārtha sa ca dṛṣṭo mayā'nagha||17||

18śambarasya vadhe ghoraḥ saṅgrāmaḥ sumahānabhūt
|
sārathyaṃ devarājasya tatrāpi kṛtavānaham||18||

19tathaiva vṛtrasya vadhe saṅgṛhītā hayā mayā
|
vairocanermahāyuddhaṃ dṛṣṭuṃ cāpi sudāruṇam||19||

20ete mayā mahāghorāḥ saṅgrāmāḥ paryupāsitāḥ
|
na cāpi vigatajñāno bhūtapūrvo'smi pāṇḍava||20||

21pitāmahena saṃhāraḥ prajānāṃ vihito dhruvam
|
na hi yuddhamidaṃ yuktamanyatra jagataḥ kṣayāt||21||

22tasya tadvacanaṃ śrutvā saṃstabhyātmānamātmanā
|
mohayiṣyan dānavānāmahaṃ māyābalaṃ mahat||22||

23abruvaṃ mātaliṃ bhītaṃ paśya me bhujayorbalam
|
astrāṇāṃ ca prabhāvaṃ vai dhanuṣo gāṇḍivasya ca||23||

24adyāstramāyayaiteṣāṃ māyāmetāṃ sudāruṇām
|
vinihanmi tamaścograṃ mā bhaiḥ sūta sthiro bhava||24||

25evamuktvā'hamasṛjamastramāyāṃ narādhipa
|
mohanīṃ sarvabhūtānāṃ hitāya tridivaukasām||25||

26pīḍyamānāsu māyāsu tāsu tāsvasurottamāḥ
|
punarbahuvidhā māyāḥ prākurvannamitaujasaḥ||26||

27punaḥ prakāśamabhavattamasā grasyate punaḥ
|
bhavatyadarśano lokaḥ punarapsu nimajjati||27||

28susaṅgṛhītairharibhiḥ prakāśe sati mātaliḥ
|
vyacarat syandanāgryeṇa saṅgrāme lomaharṣaṇe||28||

29tataḥ paryapatannugrā nivātakavacā mayi
|
tānahaṃ vivaraṃ dṛṣṭvā prāhiṇvaṃ yamasādanam||29||

30vartamāne tathā yuddhe nivātakavacāntake
|
nāpaśyaṃ sahasā sarvān dānavān māyayā''vṛtān||30||
