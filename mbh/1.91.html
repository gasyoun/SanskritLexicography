Mahābhārata 1.91 (Ādiparvan)

1aṣṭaka uvāca|
carangṛhasthaḥ kathameti dharmānkathaṃ bhikṣuḥ kathamācāryakarmā
|
vānaprasthaḥ satpathe sanniviṣṭo bahūnyasminsaṃprati vedayanti||1||

2yayātiruvāca|
āhūtādhyāyī gurukarmasvacodyaḥ pūrvotthāyī caramaṃ copaśāyī
|
mṛdurdānto dhṛtimānapramattaḥ svādhyāśīlaḥ sidhyati brahmacārī||2||

3dharmāgataṃ prāpya dhanaṃ yajeta dadyātsadaivātithīnbhojayecca
|
anādadānaśca parairadattaṃ saiṣā gṛhasthopaniṣatpurāṇī||3||

4svavīryajīvī vṛjinānnivṛtto dātā parebhyo na paropatāpī
|
tādṛṅmuniḥ siddhimupaiti mukhyāṃ vasannaraṇye niyatāhāraceṣṭaḥ||4||

5aśilpajīvī guṇavāṃścaiva nityaṃ jitendriyaḥ sarvato viprayuktaḥ
|
anokaśāyī laghuralpaprasāraścarandeśānekacaraḥ sa bhikṣuḥ||5||

6rātryā yayā vā'bhijitāśca lokā bhavanti kāmābhijitāḥ sukhāśca
|
tāmeva rātriṃ prayateta vidvānaraṇyasaṃstho bhavituṃ yatātmā||6||

7daśaiva pūrvāndaśa cāparāṃśca jñātīnathātmānamathaikaviṃśam
|
araṇyavāsī sukṛte dadhāti vimucyāraṇye svaśarīradhātūn||7||

8aṣṭaka uvāca|
katisvideva munayaḥ kati maunāni cāpyuta
|
bhavantīti tadācakṣva śrotumicchāmahe vayam||8||

9yayātiruvāca|
araṇye vasato yasya grāmo bhavati pṛṣṭhataḥ
|
grāme vā vasato'raṇyaṃ sa muniḥ syājjanādhipa||9||

10aṣṭaka uvāca|
kathaṃsvidvasato'raṇye grāmo bhavati pṛṣṭhataḥ
|
grāme vā vasato'raṇyaṃ kathaṃ bhavati pṛṣṭhataḥ||10||

11yayātiruvāca|
na grāmyamupayuñjīta ya āraṇyo munirbhavet
|
tathāsya vasato'raṇye grāmo bhavati pṛṣṭhataḥ||11||

12anagniraniketaścāpyagotracaraṇo muniḥ
|
kaupīnācchādanaṃ yāvattāvadicchecca cīvaram||12||

13yāvatprāṇābhisandhānaṃ tāvadicchecca bhojanam
|
tathā'sya vasato grāme'raṇyaṃ bhavati pṛṣṭhataḥ||13||

14yastu kāmānparityajya tyaktakarmā jitendriyaḥ
|
ātiṣṭhecca munirmaunaṃ sa loke siddhimāpnuyāt||14||

15dhautadantaṃ kṛttanakhaṃ sadā snātamalaṅkṛtam
|
asitaṃ sitakarmāṇaṃ kastamarhati nārcitum||15||

16tapasā karśitaḥ kṣāmaḥ kṣīṇamāṃsāsthiśoṇitaḥ
|
sa ca lokamimaṃ jitvā lokaṃ vijayate param||16||

17yadā bhavati nirdvandvo munirmaunaṃ samāsthitaḥ
|
atha lokamimaṃ jitvā lokaṃ vijayate param||17||

18āsyena tu yadā'hāraṃ govanmṛgayate muniḥ
|
athāsya lokaḥ sarvo'yaṃ so'mṛtatvāya kalpate||18||
