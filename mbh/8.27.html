Mahābhārata 8.27 (Karṇaparvan)

1sañjaya uvāca|
śvetāśvo'tha mahārāja vyadhamattāvakaṃ balam
|
yathā vāyuḥ samāsādya tūlarāśiṃ samantataḥ ||1||

2pratyudyayustrigartāstaṃ śibayaḥ kauravaiḥ saha
|
śālvāḥ saṃśaptakāścaiva nārāyaṇabalaṃ ca tat ||2||

3satyasenaścandradevo mitradevaḥ sutañjayaḥ
|
sauśrutiścitrasenaśca mitravarmā ca bhārata ||3||

4trigartarājaḥ samare bhrātṛbhiḥ parivāritaḥ
|
putraiścaiva maheṣvāsairnānāśastraviśāradaiḥ ||4||

5vyasṛjanta śaravrātān kiranto'rjunamāhave
|
abhyavartanta sahasā vāryoghā iva sāgaram ||5||

6te tvarjunaṃ samāsādya yodhāḥ śatasahasraśaḥ
|
agacchan vilayaṃ sarve tārkṣyaṃ dṛṣṭveva pannagāḥ ||6||

7te hanyamānāḥ samare nājahuḥ pāṇḍavaṃ raṇe
|
hanyamānā mahārāja śalabhā iva pāvakam ||7||

8satyasenastribhirbāṇairvivyādha yudhi pāṇḍavam
|
mitradevastriṣaṣṭyā tu candrasenastu saptabhiḥ ||8||

9mitravarmā trisaptatyā sauśrutiścāpi saptabhiḥ
|
śatruñjayastu viṃśatyā suśarmā navabhiḥ śaraiḥ ||9||

10sa viddho bahubhiḥ saṃkhye prativivyādha tānnṛpān
|
sauśrutiṃ saptabhirviddhvā satyasenaṃ tribhiḥ śaraiḥ ||10||

11śatruñjayaṃ ca viṃśatyā candradevaṃ tathā'ṣṭabhiḥ
|
mitradevaṃ śatenaiva śrutasenaṃ tribhiḥ śaraiḥ ||11||

12navabhirmitravarmāṇaṃ suśarmāṇaṃ tathā'ṣṭabhiḥ
|
śatruñjayaṃ ca rājānaṃ hatvā tatra śilāśitaiḥ ||12||

13sauśruteḥ saśirastrāṇaṃ śiraḥ kāyādapāharat
|
tvaritaścandradevaṃ ca śarairninye yamakṣayam ||13||

14tathetarānmahārāja yatamānānmahārathān
|
pañcabhiḥ pañcabhirbāṇairekaikaṃ pratyavārayat ||14||

15satyasenastu saṃkruddhastomaraṃ vyasṛjanmahat
|
samuddiśya raṇe kṛṣṇaṃ siṃhanādaṃ nānāda ca ||15||

16sa nirbhidya bhujaṃ savyaṃ mādhavasya mahātmanaḥ
|
ayasmayo hemadaṇḍo jagāma dharaṇīṃ tadā ||16||

17mādhavasya tu viddhasya tomareṇa mahāraṇe
|
pratodaḥ prāpataddhastādraśmayaśca viśāṃpate ||17||

18vāsudevaṃ vibhinnāṅgaṃ dṛṣṭvā pārtho dhanañjayaḥ
|
krodhamāhārayattīvraṃ kṛṣṇaṃ ceda‌muvāca ha ||18||

19prāpayāśvānmahābāho satyasenaṃ prati prabho
|
yāvadenaṃ śaraistīkṣṇairnayāmi yamasādanam ||19||

20pratodaṃ gṛhya so'nyattu raśmīnapi yathā purā
|
vāhayāmāsa tānaśvānsatyasenarathaṃ prati ||20||

21viṣvaksenaṃ tu nirbhinnaṃ dṛṣṭvā pārtho dhanañjayaḥ
|
satyasenaṃ śaraistatīkṣṇairvārayitvā mahārathaḥ ||21||

22tataḥ suniśitairbhallai rājñastasya mahacchiraḥ
|
kuṇḍalopacitaṃ kāyāccakarta pṛtanāntare ||22||

23tannikṛtya śitairbāṇairmitravarmāṇamākṣipat
|
vatsadantena tīkṣṇena sārathiṃ cāsya māriṣa||23||

24tataḥ śaraśatairbhūyaḥ saṃśaptakagaṇānbalī
|
pātayāmāsa saṃkruddhaḥ śataśo'tha sahasraśaḥ ||24||

25tato rajatapuṅkhena rājañśīrṣaṃ mahātmanaḥ
|
mitrasenasya ciccheda kṣurapreṇa mahārathaḥ ||25||

26suśarmāṇaṃ susaṃkruddho jatrudeśe samāhanat
|
tataḥ saṃśaptakāḥ sarve parivārya dhanañjayam ||26||

27śastraughairmamṛduḥ kruddhā nādayanto diśo daśa
|
abhyarditastu tajjiṣṇuḥ śakratulyaparākramaḥ ||27||

28aindramastramameyātmā prāduścakre mahārathaḥ
|
tataḥ śarasahasrāṇi prādurāsanviśāṃpate ||28||

29dhvajānāṃ chidyamānānāṃ kārmukāṇāṃ ca māriṣa
|
rathānāṃ sapatākānāṃ tūṇīrāṇāṃ yugaiḥ saha ||29||

30akṣāṇāmatha cakrāṇāṃ yoktrāṇāṃ raśmibhiḥ saha
|
kūbarāṇāṃ varūthāṇāṃ pṛṣatkānāṃ ca saṃyuge ||30||

31aśvānāṃ patatāṃ cāpi prāsānāmṛṣṭibhiḥ saha
|
gadānāṃ parighanāṃ ca śakti-tomara-paṭṭiśaiḥ ||31||

32śataghnīnāṃ sacakrāṇāṃ bhujānāṃ corubhiḥ saha
|
kaṇṭhasūtrāṅgadānāṃ ca keyūrāṇāṃ ca māriṣa ||32||

33hārāṇāmatha niṣkāṇāṃ tanutrāṇāṃ ca bhārata
|
chatrāṇāṃ vyajanānāṃ ca śirasāṃ mukuṭaiḥ saha ||33||

34aśrūyata mahāñśabdastatra tatra viśāṃpate
|
sakuṇḍalāni svakṣīṇi pūrṇacandranibhāni ca ||34||

35śirāṃsyurvyāmadṛśyanta tārājālamivāmbare
|
susragvīṇi suvāsāṃsi candanenokṣitāni ca ||35||

36śarīrāṇi vyadṛśyanta nihatānāṃ mahītale
|
gandharvanagarākāraṃ ghoramāyodhanaṃ tadā ||36||

37nihatai rājaputraiśca kṣatriyaiśca mahābalaiḥ
|
hastibhiḥ patitaiścaiva turaṅgaiścābhavanmahī ||37||

38agamyarūpā samare viśīrṇairiva parvataiḥ
|
nāsīccakrapathastatra pāṇḍavasya mahātmanaḥ ||38||

39nighnataḥ śātravān bhallairhastyaśvaṃ cāsyato mahat
|
svānugā iva sīdanti rathacakrāṇi māriṣa
|
caratastasya saṃgrāme tasmi~llohitakardame
|
sīdamānāni cakrāṇi samūhusturagā bhṛśam ||39||

40śrameṇa mahatā yuktā manomārutaraṃhasaḥ
|
vadhyamānaṃ tu tatsainyaṃ pāṇḍuputreṇa dhanvinā ||40||

41prāyaśo vimukhaṃ sarvaṃ nāvatiṣṭhata bhārata
|
tāñjitvā samare jiṣṇuḥ saṃśaptakagaṇānbahūn
|
virarāja tadā pārtho vidhūmo'gniriva jvalan ||41||
