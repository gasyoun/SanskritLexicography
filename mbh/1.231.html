Mahābhārata 1.231 (Ādiparvan)

1jaritovāca|
asmādbilānniṣpatitamākhuṃ śyeno jahāra tam
|
kṣudraṃ padbhyāṃ gṛhītvā ca yāto nātra bhayaṃ hi vaḥ||1||

2śārṅgakā ūcuḥ|
na hṛtaṃ taṃ vayaṃ vidmaḥ śyenenākhuṃ kathaṃcana
|
anye'pi bhitāro'tra tebhyo'pi bhameva naḥ||2||

3saṃśayo vahnirāgaccheddṛṣṭaṃ vāyornivartanam
|
mṛtyurno bilavāsibhyo bile syānnātra saṃśayaḥ||3||

4niḥsaṃśayātsaṃśayito mṛtyurmātarviśiṣyate
|
cara khe tvaṃ yathānyāyaṃ putrānāpsyasi śobhanān||4||

5jaritovāca
|
ahaṃ vegena taṃ yāntamadrākṣaṃ patatāṃ varam
|
bilādākhuṃ samādāya śyenaṃ putrā mahābalam||5||

6taṃ patantaṃ mahāvegā tvaritā pṛṣṭhato'nvagām
|
āśiṣo'sya prayuñjānā harato mūṣikaṃ bilāt||6||

7yo no dveṣṭāramādāya śyenarāja pradhāvasi
|
bhava tvaṃ divamāsthāya niramitro hiraṇmayaḥ||7||

8sa yadā bhakṣitastena śyenenākhuḥ patatriṇā
|
tadāhaṃ tamanujñāpya pratyupāyāṃ punargṛham||8||

9praviśadhvaṃ bilaṃ putrā viśrabdhā nāsti vo bhayam
|
śyenena mama paśyantyā hṛta ākhurmahātmanā||9||

10śārṅgakā ūcuḥ|
na vidmahe hṛtaṃ mātaḥ śyenainākhuṃ kathaṃcana
|
avijñāya na śakyāmaḥ praveṣṭuṃ vivaraṃ bhuvaḥ||10||

11jaritovāca
|
ahaṃ tamabhijānāmi hṛtaṃ śyenena mūṣikam
|
nāsti votra bhayaṃ putrāḥ kriyatāṃ vacanaṃ mama||11||

12śārṅgakā ūcuḥ|
na tvaṃ mithyopacāreṇa mokṣayethā bhayāddhi naḥ
|
samākuleṣu jñāneṣu na buddhikṛtameva tat||12||

13na copakṛtamasmābhirna cāsmānvettha ye vayam
|
pīḍyamānā bibharṣyasmānkā satī ke vayaṃ tava||13||

14taruṇī darśīyā'si samarthā bhartureṣaṇe
|
anugaccha patiṃ mātaḥ putrānāpsyasi śomanān||14||

15vayamagniṃ samāviśya lokānāpsyāma śobhanān
|
athāsmānna dahedagnirāyāstvaṃ punareva naḥ||15||

16vaiśaṃpāyana uvāca|
evamuktā tataḥ śārṅgī putrānutsṛjya khāṇḍave
|
jagāma tvaritā deśaṃ kṣemamagneranāmayam||16||

17tatastīkṣṇārcirabhyāgāttvarito havyavāhanaḥ
|
yatra śārṅgā babhūvuste mandapālasya putrakāḥ||17||

18tatastaṃ jvalitaṃ dṛṣṭvā jvalanaṃ te vihaṅgamāḥ
|
jaritāristato vākyaṃ śrāvayāmāsa pāvakam||18||
