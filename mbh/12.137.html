Mahābhārata 12.137 (Śāntiparvan)

1bhīṣma uvāca|
anāgatavidhātā ca pratyutpannamatiśca yaḥ
|
dvāveva sukhamedhete dīrghasūtrī vinaśyati ||1||

2atraiva cedamavyagraṃ śṛṇuṣvākhyānamuttamam
|
dīrghaṃ sūtramupāśritya kāryākāryaviniścaye ||2||

3nātigādhe jalādhāre suhṛdaḥ kuśalāstrayaḥ
|
prabhūtamatsye kaunteya babhūvuḥ sahacāriṇaḥ ||3||

4tatraiko dīrghakālajña utpannapratibho'paraḥ
|
dīrghasūtraśca tatraikastrayāṇāṃ sahacāriṇām ||4||

5kadācit taṃ jalasthāyaṃ matsyabandhāḥ samantataḥ
|
nistrāvayāmāsuratho nimneṣu vividhairmukhaiḥ ||5||

6prakṣīyamāṇaṃ taṃ dṛṣṭvā jalasthāyaṃ bhayāgame
|
abravīddīrghadarśī tu tāvubhau suhṛdau tadā ||6||

7iyamāpatsamutpannā sarveṣāṃ salilaukasām
|
śīghramanyatra gacchāmaḥ panthā yāvanna duṣyati ||7||

8anāgatamanarthaṃ hi sunayairyaḥ prabādhayet
|
sa na saṃśayamāpnoti rocatāṃ bho vrajāmahe ||8||

9dīrghasūtrastu yastatra so'bravītsamyagucyate
|
na ta kāryā tvarā tāvaditi me niścitā matiḥ ||9||

10atha sampratipattijñaḥ prābravīddīrghadarśinam
|
prāpte kāle na me kiñcinyāyataḥ parihāsyate ||10||

11evaṃ śrutvā nirākramya dīrghadarśī mahāmatiḥ
|
jagāma srotasā tena gambhīraṃ salilāśayam ||11||

12tataḥ prasṛtatoyaṃ taṃ prasamīkṣya jalāśayam
|
babandhurvividhairyogairmatsyānmatsyopajīvinaḥ ||12||

13viloḍyamāne tasmiṃstu srutatoye jalāśaye
|
agacchadbandhanaṃ tatra dīrghasūtraḥ sahāparaiḥ ||13||

14udyāne kriyamāṇe tu matsyānāṃ tatra rajjubhiḥ
|
praviśyāntarameteṣāṃ sthitaḥ sampratipattimān ||14||

15gṛhyameva tadudyānaṃ gṛhītvā taṃ tathaiva saḥ
|
sarvāneva ca tāṃstatra te vidurgrathitāniti ||15||

16tataḥ prakṣālyamāneṣu matsyeṣu vipule jale
|
muktvā rajjuṃ pramukto'sau śīghraṃ sampratipattimān ||16||

17dīrghasūtrastu mandātmā hīnabuddhiracetanaḥ
|
maraṇaṃ prāptavān mūḍho yathaivopahatendriyaḥ ||17||

18evaṃ prāptatamaṃ kālaṃ yo mohānnāvabuddhyate
|
sa vinaśyati vai kṣipraṃ dīrghasūtro yathā jhaṣaḥ ||18||

19ādau na kurute śreyaḥ kuśalo'smīti yaḥ pumān
|
sa saṃśayamavāpnoti yathā sampratipattimān ||19||

20anāgatavidhātā ca pratyutpannamatiśca yaḥ
|
dvāveva sukhamedhete dīrghasūtro vinaśyati ||20||

21kāṣṭhāḥ kalā muhūrtāśca divā rātristathā lavāḥ
|
māsāḥ pakṣāḥ ṣaḍ ṛtavaḥ kalpaḥ saṃvatsarāstathā ||21||

22pṛthivī deśa ityuktaḥ kālaḥ sa ca na dṛśyate
|
abhipretārthasiddhyarthaṃ dhyāyate yacca tattathā ||22||

23etau dharmārthaśāstreṣu mokṣaśāstreṣu carṣibhiḥ
|
pradhānāviti nirdiṣṭau kāme cābhimatau nṛṇām ||23||

24parīkṣyakārī yuktaśca sa samyagupapādayet
|
deśakālāvabhipretau tābhyāṃ phalamavāpnuyāt ||24||
