Mahābhārata 3.33 (Āraṇyakaparvan)

1vaiśaṃpāyana uvāca|
yājñasenyā vacaḥ śrutvā bhīmaseno hyamarṣaṇaḥ
|
niśvasannupasaṅgamya kruddho rājānamabravīt||1||

2rājyasya padavīṃ dharmyāṃ vraja satpuruṣocitām
|
dharmakāmārthahīnānāṃ kiṃ no vastuṃ tapovane||2||

3naiva dharmeṇa tadrājyaṃ nārjavena na caujasā
|
akṣakūṭamadhiṣṭhāya hṛtaṃ duryodhanena vai||3||

4gomāyuneva siṃhānāṃ durbalena balīyasām
|
āmiṣaṃ vighasāśena tadvadrājyaṃ hi no hṛtam||4||

5dharmaleśapraticchannaḥ prabhavaṃ dharmakāmayoḥ
|
arthamutsṛjya kiṃ rājanduḥkheṣu paritapyase||5||

6bhavato'navadhānena rājyaṃ naḥ paśyatāṃ hṛtam
|
ahāryamapi śakreṇa guptaṃ gāṇḍīvadhanvanā||6||

7kuṇīnāmiva bilvāni paṅgūnāmiva dhenavaḥ
|
hṛtamaiśvaryamasmākaṃ jīvatāṃ bhavataḥ kṛte||7||

8bhavataḥ priyamityevaṃ mahadvyasanamīdṛśam
|
dharmakāme pratītasya pratipannāḥ sma bhārata||8||

9karṣayāmaḥ svamitrāṇi nandayāmaśca śātravān
|
ātmānaṃ bhavatāṃ śāstrairniyamya bharatarṣabha||9||

10yadvayaṃ na tadaivaitāndhārtarāṣṭrānnihanmahi
|
bhavataḥ śāstramādāya tannastapati duṣkṛtam||10||

11athaināmanvavekṣasva mṛgacaryāmivātmanaḥ
|
durbalācaritāṃ rājannabalasthairniṣevitām||11||

12yāṃ na kṛṣṇo na bībhatsurnābhimanyurna sṛñjayāḥ
|
na cāhamabhinandāmi na ca mādrīsutāvubhau||12||

13bhavāndharmo dharma iti satataṃ vratakarśitaḥ
|
kaccidrājanna nirvedādāpannaḥ klībajīvikām||13||

14durmanuṣyā hi nirvedamaphalaṃ svārthaghātakam
|
aśaktāḥ śriyamāhartumātmanaḥ kurvate priyam||14||

15sa bhavāndṛṣṭimāñcchaktaḥ paśyannasmāsu pauruṣam
|
ānṛśaṃsyaparo rājannānarthamavabudhyase||15||

16asmānamī dhārtarāṣṭrāḥ kṣamamāṇānalaṃ sataḥ
|
aśaktāniva manyante tadduḥkhaṃ nāhave vadhaḥ||16||

17tatra cedyudhyamānānāmajihmamanivartinām
|
sarvaśo hi vadhaḥ śreyānpretya lokānlabhemahi||17||

18athavā vayamevaitānnihatya bharatarṣabha
|
ādadīmahi gāṃ sarvāṃ tathāpi śreya eva naḥ||18||

19sarvathā kāryametannaḥ svadharmamanutiṣṭhatām
|
kāṅkṣatāṃ vipulāṃ kīrtiṃ vairaṃ praticikīrṣatām||19||

20ātmārthaṃ yudhyamānānāṃ vidite kṛtyalakṣaṇe
|
anyairapi hṛte rājye praśaṃsaiva na garhaṇā||20||

21karṣaṇārthe hi yo dharmo mitrāṇāmātmanastathā
|
vyasanaṃ nāma tadrājanna dharmaḥ sa kudharmatat||21||

22sarvathā dharmanityaṃ tu puruṣaṃ dharmadurbalam
|
tyajatastāta dharmārthau pretaṃ duḥkhasukhe yathā||22||

23yasya dharmo hi dharmārthaṃ kleśabhāṅga sa paṇḍitaḥ
|
na sa dharmasya vedārthaṃ sūryasyāndhaḥ prabhāmiva||23||

24yasya cātmārthamevārthaḥ saca nārthasya kovidaḥ
|
rakṣeta bhṛtakoraṇye yathā gāstādṛgeva saḥ||24||

25ativelaṃ hi yo'rthārthī netarāvanutiṣṭhati
|
sa vadhyaḥ sarvabhūtānāṃ brahmaheva jugupsitaḥ||25||

26satataṃ yaśca kāmārthī netarāvanutiṣṭhati
|
mitrāṇi tasya naśyanti dharmārthābhyāṃ ca hīyate||26||

27tasya dharmārthahīnasya kāmānte nidhanaṃ dhruvam
|
kāmato ramamāṇasya mīnasyevāmbhasaḥ kṣaye||27||

28tasmāddharmārthayornityaṃ na pramādyanti paṇḍitāḥ
|
prakṛtiḥ sā hi kāmasya pāvakasyāraṇiryathā||28||

29sarvathā dharmamūlo'rtho dharmaścārthaparigrahaḥ
|
itaretarayornītau viddhi meghodadhī yathā||29||

30dravyārthasparśasaṃyoge yā prītirupajāyate
|
sa kāmaścittasaṅkalpaḥ śarīraṃ nāsya dṛśyate||30||

31arthārthī puruṣo rājanbṛhantaṃ dharmamicchati
|
arthamicchati kāmārthī na kāmādanyamicchati||31||

32na hi kāmena kāmo'nyaḥ sādhyate phalameva tat
|
upayogātphalasyaiva kāṣṭhādbhasmeva paṇḍitaiḥ||32||

33imāñcchakunakānrājanhanti vaitaṃsiko yathā
|
etadrūpamadharmasya bhūteṣu hi vihiṃsatā||33||

34kāmāllobhācca dharmasya prakṛtiṃ yo na paśyati
|
sa vadhyaḥ sarvabhūtānāṃ pretya ceha ca durmatiḥ||34||

35vyaktaṃ te vidito rājannārtho dravyaparigrahaḥ
|
prakṛtiṃ cāpi vetthāsya vikṛtiṃ cāpi bhūyasīm||35||

36tasya nāśe vināśe vā jarayā maraṇena vā
|
anartha iti manyante so'yamasmāsu vartate||36||

37indriyāṇāṃ ca pañcānāṃ manaso hṛdayasya ca
|
viṣaye vartamānānāṃ yā prītirupajāyate||37||

38sa kāma iti me buddhiḥ karmaṇāṃ phalamuttamam
|
evameva pṛthagdṛṣṭvā dharmārthau kāmameva ca||38||

39na dharmapara eva syānna cārthaparamo naraḥ
|
na kāmaparamo vā syātsarvānseveta sarvadā||39||

40dharmaṃ pūrve dhanaṃ madhye jaghanye kāmamācaret
|
ahanyanucaredevameṣa śāstrakṛto vidhiḥ||40||

41kāmaṃ pūrve dhanaṃ madhye jaghanye dharmamācaret
|
vayasyanucaredevameṣa śāstrakṛto vidhiḥ||41||

42dharmaṃ cārthaṃ ca kāmaṃ ca yathāvadvadatāṃ vara
|
vibhajya kāle kālajñaḥ sarvānseveta paṇḍitaḥ||42||

43mokṣo vā paramaṃ śreya eṣa rājansukhārthinām
|
prāptiṃ vā buddhimāsthāya sopāyāṃ kurunandana||43||

44tadvāśu kriyatāṃ rājanprāptiṃ vāpyadhigamyatām
|
jīvitaṃ hyāturasyeva duḥkhamantaravartinaḥ||44||

45viditaścaiva me dharmaḥ satataṃ caritaśca te
|
jānantastvayi śaṃsanti suhṛdaḥ karmacodanām||45||

46dānaṃ yajñāḥ satāṃ pūjā vedadhāraṇamārjavam
|
eṣa dharmaḥ paro rājanbalavānpretya ceha ca||46||

47eṣa nārthavihīnena śakyo rājānniṣevitum
|
akhilāḥ puruṣavyāghra guṇāḥ syuryadyapītare||47||

48dharmamūlaṃ jagadrājannānyaddharmādviśiṣyate
|
dharmaścārthena mahatā śakyo rājanniṣevitum||48||

49na cārtho bhaikṣyacaryeṇa nāpi klaibyena karhicit
|
vettuṃ śakyaḥ sadā rājankevalaṃ dharmabuddhinā||49||

50pratiṣiddhā hi te yāñcā yayā siddhyati vai dvijaḥ
|
tejasaivārthalipsāyāṃ yatasva puruṣarṣabha||50||

51bhaikṣyacaryā na vihitā na ca viṭśūdrajīvikā
|
kṣatriyasya viśeṣeṇa dharmastu balamaurasam||51||

52svadharmaṃ pratipadyasva jahi śatrūnsamāgatān
|
dhārtarāṣṭravanaṃ pārtha mayā pārthena nāśaya||52||

53udārameva vidvāṃso dharmaṃ prāhurmanīṣiṇaḥ
|
udāraṃ pratipadyasva nāvare sthātumarhasi||53||

54anubudhyasva rājendra vettha dharmānsanātanān
|
krūrakarmābhijāto'si yasmādudvijate janaḥ||54||

55prajāpālanasambhūtaṃ phalaṃ tava na garhitam
|
eṣa te vihito rājandhātrā dharmaḥ sanātanaḥ||55||

56tasmādapacitaḥ pārtha loke hāsyaṃ gamiṣyasi
|
svadharmāddhi manuṣyāṇāṃ calanaṃ na praśasyate||56||

57sa kṣātraṃ hṛdayaṃ kṛtvā tyaktvedaṃ śithilaṃ manaḥ
|
vīryamāsthāya kauravya dhuramudvaha dhuryavat||57||

58na hi kevaladharmātmā pṛthivīṃ jātu kaścana
|
pārthivo vyajayadrājanna bhūtiṃ na punaḥ śriyam||58||

59jihvāṃ dattvā bahūnāṃ hi kṣudrāṇāṃ lubdhacetasām
|
nikṛtyā labhate rājyamāhāramiva śalyakaḥ||59||

60bhrātaraḥ pūrvajātāśca susamṛddhāśca sarvaśaḥ
|
nikṛtyā nirjitā devairasurāḥ pārthivarṣabha||60||

61evaṃ balavataḥ sarvamiti buddhvā mahīpate
|
jahi śatrūnmahābāho parāṃ nikṛtimāsthitaḥ||61||

62na hyarjunasamaḥ kaścidyudhi yodho dhanurdharaḥ
|
bhavitā vā pumānkaścinmatsamo vā gadādharaḥ||62||

63sattvena kurute yuddhaṃ rājansubalavānapi
|
na pramāṇena notsāhātsatvastho bhava pāṇḍava||63||

64sattvaṃ hi mūlamarthasya vitathaṃ yadato'nyathā
|
na tu prasaktaṃ bhavati vṛkṣacchāyeva haimanī||64||

65arthatyāgo'pi kāryaḥ syādarthaṃ śreyāṃsamicchatā
|
bījaupamyena kaunteya mā te bhūdatra saṃśayaḥ||65||

66arthena tu samo'nartho yatra labhyeta nodayaḥ
|
na tatra vipaṇaḥ kāryaḥ kharakaṇḍūyanaṃ hi tat||66||

67evameva manuṣyendra dharmaṃ tyaktvālpakaṃ naraḥ
|
bṛhantaṃ dharmamāpnoti sa buddha iti niścitam||67||

68amitraṃ mitrasampannaṃ mitrairbhindanti paṇḍitāḥ
|
minnairmitraiḥ parityaktaṃ yuvānaṃ kurute vaśam||68||

69sattvena kurute yuddhaṃ rājansubalavānapi
|
nodyamena na hotrābhiḥ sarvāḥ svīkurute prajāḥ||69||

70sarvathā saṃhataireva durbalairbalavānapi
|
amitraḥ śakyate hantuṃ madhuhā bhramarariva||70||

71yathā rājanprajāḥ sarvāḥ sūryaḥ pāti gabhastibhiḥ
|
atti caiva tathaiva tvaṃ sadṛśaḥ saviturbhava||71||

72etaccāpi tapo rājanpurāṇamiti naḥ śrutam
|
vidhinā pālanaṃ bhūmeryatkṛtaṃ naḥ pitāmahaiḥ||72||

73na tathā tapasā rāja~llokānprāpnoti kṣatriyaḥ
|
yathā sṛṣṭena yuddhena vijayenetareṇa vā ||73||

74apeyātkila bhā sūryāllakṣmīścandramasastathā
|
iti loko vyavasito dṛṣṭvemāṃ bhavato vyathām||74||

75bhavataśca praśaṃsābhirnindābhiritarasya ca
|
kathā yuktāḥ pariṣadaḥ pṛthagrājansamāgatāḥ||75||

76idamabhyadhikaṃ rājanbrāhmaṇāḥ kuravaśca te
|
sametāḥ kathayantīha muditāḥ satyasandhatām||76||

77yanna mohānna kārpaṇyānna lobhānna bhayādapi
|
anṛtaṃ kiñciduktaṃ te na kāmānnārthakāraṇāt||77||

78yadenaḥ kurute kiñcidrājā bhūmimavāpnuvan
|
sarvaṃ tannudate paścādyajñairvipuladakṣiṇaiḥ||78||

79brāhmaṇebhyo dadadgrāmāngāśca rājansahasraśaḥ
|
mucyate sarvapāpebhyastamobhya iva candramāḥ||79||

80paurajānapadāḥ sarve prāyaśaḥ kurunandana
|
savṛddhabālasahitāḥ śaṃsanti tvāṃ yudhiṣṭhira||80||

81śvadṛtau kṣīramāsaktaṃ brahma vā vṛṣale yathā
|
satyaṃ stene balaṃ nāryāṃ rājyaṃ duryodhane tathā||81||

82iti loke nirvacanaṃ puraścarati bhārata
|
api caitāḥ striyo bālāḥ khādhyāyamadhikurvate||82||

83imāmavasthāṃ ca gate sahāsmābhirarindama
|
hanta naṣṭāḥ sma sarve vai bhavatopadrave sati||83||

84sa bhavānrathamāsthāya sarvopakaraṇānvitam
|
tvaramāṇo'bhiniryātu viprebhyoryavibhāvakaḥ||84||

85vācayitvā dvijaśreṣṭhānadyaiva gajasāhvayam
|
astravidbhiḥ parivṛto bhrātṛbhirdṛḍhadhanvibhiḥ||85||

86āśīviṣasamairvīrairmarudbhiriva vṛtrahā
|
amitrāṃstejasā mṛdgannasurāniva vṛtrahā
|
śriyamādatsva kaunteya dhārtarāṣṭrānmahābala||86||

87na hi gāṇḍīvamuktānāṃ śarāṇāṃ gārdhravāsasām
|
sparśamāśīviṣābhānāṃ martyaḥ kaścana saṃsahet||87||

88na sa vīro na mātaṅgo na ca so'śvo'sti bhārata
|
yaḥ saheta gadāvegaṃ mama kruddhasya saṃyuge||88||

89sṛṃñjayaiḥ saha kaikeyairvṛṣṇīnāṃ vṛṣabheṇa ca
|
kathaṃ svidyudhi kaunteya na rājyaṃ prāpnuyāmahe||89||

90śatruhastagatāṃ rājankathaṃsvinnāharermahīm
|
iha yatnamupāhṛtya balena mahatānvitaḥ||90||
