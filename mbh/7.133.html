Mahābhārata 7.133 (Droṇaparvan)

1dhṛtarāṣṭra uvāca|
atyadbhutamahaṃ manye bhīmasenasya vikramam
|
yatkarṇaṃ yodhayāmāsa samare laghuvikramam ||1||

2tridaśānapi vā yuktānsarvaśastradharānyudhi
|
vārayedyo raṇe karṇaḥ sayakṣāsuramānuṣān ||2||

3sa kathaṃ pāṇḍavaṃ yuddhe bhrājamānamiva śriyā
|
nātaratsaṃyuge pārthaṃ tanmamācakṣva sañjaya ||3||

4kathaṃ ca yuddhaṃ saṃbhūtaṃ tayoḥ prāṇadurodare
|
atra manye samāyatto jayo vā'jaya eva ca ||4||

5karṇaṃ prāpya raṇe sūta mama putraḥ suyodhanaḥ
|
jetumutsahate pārthānsagoviṃdānsasātvatān||5||

6śrutvā tu nirjitaṃ karṇamasakṛdbhīmakarmaṇā
|
bhīmasenena samare moha āviśatīya mām ||6||

7vinaṣṭānkauravānmanye mama putrasya durnayaiḥ
|
na hi karṇo maheṣvāsānpārthāñjeṣyati sañjaya ||7||

8kṛtavānyāni yuddhāni karṇaḥ pāṇḍusutaiḥ saha
|
sarvatra pāṇḍavāḥ karṇamajayanta raṇājire ||8||

9ajeyāḥ pāṇḍavāstāta devairapi savāsavaiḥ
|
na ca tadbudhyate mandaḥ putro duryodhano mama ||9||

10dhanaṃ dhaneśvarasyeva hṛtvā pārthasya me sutaḥ
|
madhuprepsurivābuddhiḥ prapātaṃ nāvabudhyate ||10||

11nikṛtyā nikṛtiprajño rājyaṃ hatvā mahātmanām
|
jitamityeva manvānaḥ pāṇḍavānavamanyate ||11||

12putrasnehābhibhūtena mayā cāpyakṛtātmanā
|
dharme sthitā mahātmāno nikṛtāḥ pāṇḍunandanāḥ||12||

13śamakāmaḥ sasodaryo dīrghaprekṣī yudhiṣṭhiraḥ
|
aśakta iti matvā tu mama putrairnirākṛtaḥ||13||

14tāni duḥkhānyanekāni viprakārāṃśca sarvaśaḥ
|
hṛdi kṛtvā mahābāhurbhīmo'yudhyata sūtajam||14||

15tasmānme sañjaya brūhi karṇabhīmau yathā raṇe
|
ayudhyetāṃ yudhi śreṣṭhau parasparavadhaiṣiṇau ||15||

16sañjaya uvāca|
śṛṇu rājanyathāvṛttaṃ saṃgrāmaṃ karṇabhīmayoḥ
|
parasparavadhaprepsorvanakuñjarayoriva ||16||

17rājanvaikartano bhīmaṃ kruddhaḥ kruddhamaridamam
|
parākrāntaṃ parākramya vivyādha triṃśatā śaraiḥ||17||

18mahāvegaiḥ prasannāgraiḥ śātakuṃbhapariṣkṛtaiḥ
|
ahanadbharataśreṣṭha bhīmaṃ vaikartanaḥ śaraiḥ ||18||

19tasyāsyato dhanurbhīmaścakarta niśitaistribhiḥ
|
rathanīḍācca yantāraṃ bhallenāpātayatkṣitau||19||

20sa kāṃkṣan bhīmasenasya vadhaṃ vaikartano bhṛśam
|
śaktiṃ kanakavaidūryacitradaṇḍāṃ parāmṛśat ||20||

21pragṛhya ca mahāśaktiṃ kālaśaktimivāparām
|
samutkṣipya ca rādheyaḥ sandhāya ca mahābalaḥ||21||

22cikṣepa bhīmasenāya jīvitāntakarīmiva
|
śaktiṃ visṛjya rādheyaḥ purandara ivāśanim ||22||

23nanāda sumahānādaṃ balavānsūtanandanaḥ
|
taṃ ca nādaṃ tataḥ śrutvā putrāste harṣitā'bhavan||23||

24tāṃ karṇabhujanirmuktāmarkavaiśvānaraprabhām
|
śaktiṃ viyati ciccheda bhīmaḥ saptābhirāśugaiḥ||24||

25chittvā śaktiṃ tato bhīmo nirmuktoragasannibhām
|
mārgamāṇa iva prāṇānsūtaputrasya māriṣa||25||

26prāhiṇotkṛtasaṃraṃbhaḥ śarānbarhiṇavāsasaḥ
|
svarṇapuṃkhāñśilādhautān yamadaṇḍopamānmṛdhe||26||

27karṇo'pyanyaddhanurguhya hemapṛṣṭhaṃ durāsadan
|
vikṛṣya tanmahaccāpaṃ vyasṛjatsāyakāṃstadā||27||

28tānpāṇḍuputraściccheda navabhirnataparvabhiḥ
|
vasuṣeṇena nirmuktānnava rājanmahāśarān ||28||

29chittvā bhīmo mahārāja nādaṃ siṃha ivānadat
|
tau vṛṣāviva nardantau balinau vāsitāntare||29||

30śārdūlāviva cānyonyamāmiṣārthe'bhyagarjatām
|
anyonyaṃ prajihīrṣantāvanyonyasyāntaraiṣiṇau||30||

31anyonyamabhivīkṣantī goṣṭheṣviva maharṣabhau
|
mahāgajāvivāsādya viṣāṇāgraiḥ parasparam ||31||

32śaraiḥ pūrṇāyatotsṛṣṭairanyonyamabhijaghnatuḥ
|
nirdahantau mahārāja śastravṛṣṭyā parasparam ||32||

33anyonyamabhivīkṣantau kopādvivṛtalocanau
|
prahasantau tathā'nyonyaṃ bhartsayantau muhurmuhuḥ||33||

34śaṃkhaśabdaṃ ca kurvāṇau yuyudhāte parasparam
|
tasya bhīmaḥpunaścāpaṃ muṣṭau ciccheda māriṣa||34||

35śaṃkhavarṇāṃśca tānaśvānbāṇairninye yamakṣayam
|
sārathiṃ ca tathāpyasya rathanīḍā‌dapātayat ||35||

36tato vaikartanaḥ karṇaścintāṃ prāpa duratyayām
|
sa cchādyamānaḥ samare hatāśvo hatasārathiḥ ||36||

37mohitaḥ śarajālena kartavyaṃ nābhyapadyata
|
tathā kṛcchragataṃ dṛṣṭvā karṇaṃ duryodhano nṛpaḥ ||37||

38vepamāna iva krodhādvyādideśātha durjayam
|
gaccha durjaya rādheyaṃ puro grasati pāṇḍavaḥ||38||

39jahi tūbarakaṃ kṣipraṃ karṇasya balamādadhat
|
evamuktastathetyuktvā tava putraṃ tavātmajaḥ||39||

40abhyadravadbhīmasenaṃ vyāsaktaṃ vikirañcharaiḥ
|
sa bhīmaṃ navabhirbāṇairaśvānaṣṭabhirārpayat ||40||

41ṣaḍbhiḥ sūtaṃ tribhiḥ ketuṃ punastaṃ cāpi saptabhiḥ
|
bhīmaseno'pi saṃkruddhaḥ sāśvayantāramāśugaiḥ||41||

42durjayaṃ bhinnamarmāṇamanayadyamasādanam
|
svalaṃkṛtaṃ kṣitau kṣuṇṇaṃ ceṣṭamānaṃ yathoragam||42||

43rudannārtastava sutaṃ karṇaścakre pradakṣiṇam
|
sa tu taṃ virathaṃ kṛtvā smayannatyantavairiṇam ||43||

44samācinodbāṇagaṇaiḥ śataghnībhiśca śaṅkubhiḥ
|
tathā'pyatirathaḥ karṇo bhidyamāno'sya sāyakaiḥ ||44||

45na jahau samare bhīmaṃ kruddharūpaṃ parantapaḥ ||45||
