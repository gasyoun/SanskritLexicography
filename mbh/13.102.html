Mahābhārata 13.102 (Anuśāsanaparvan)

1yudhiṣṭhira uvāca|
eke lokāḥ sukṛtinaḥ sarve tvāho pitāmaha
|
tatra tatrāpi bhinnāste tanme brūhi pitāmaha ||1||

2bhīṣma uvāca|
karmabhiḥ pārtha nānātvaṃ lokānāṃ yānti mānavāḥ
|
puṇyānpuṇyakṛto yānti pāpānpāpakṛto narāḥ ||2||

3atrāpyudāharantīmamitihāsaṃ purātanam
|
gautamasya munestāta saṃvādaṃ vāsavasya ca ||3||

4brāhmaṇo gautamaḥ kaścinmṛdurdānto jitendriyaḥ
|
mahāvane hastiśiśuṃ paridyunamamātṛkam ||4||

5taṃ dṛṣṭvā jīvayāmāsa sānukrośo dhṛtavrataḥ
|
sa tu dīrgheṇa kālena babhūvātibalo mahān ||5||

6taṃ prabhinnaṃ mahānāgaṃ prasrutaṃ parvatopamam
|
dhṛtarāṣṭrasya rūpeṇa śakro jagrāha hastinam ||6||

7hriyamāṇaṃ tu taṃ dṛṣṭvā gautamaḥ saṃśitavrataḥ
|
abhyabhāṣata rājānaṃ dhṛtarāṣṭraṃ mahātapāḥ ||7||

8mā me'hārṣīrhastinaṃ putramenaṃ duḥkhātpuṣṭaṃ dhṛtarāṣṭrākṛtajña
|
maitraṃ satāṃ saptapadaṃ vadanti mitradroho maiva rājan spṛśettvām ||8||

9idhmodakapradātāraṃ śūnyapālaṃ mamāśrame
|
vinītamācāryakule suyuktaṃ gurukarmaṇi ||9||

10śiṣṭaṃ dāntaṃ kṛtajñaṃ ca priyaṃ ca satataṃ mama
|
na me vikrośato rājan hartumarhasi kuñjaram ||10||

11dhṛtarāṣṭra uvāca|
gavāṃ sahasraṃ bhavate dadāni dāsīśataṃ niṣkaśatāni pañca
|
anyacca vittaṃ vividhaṃ maharṣe kiṃ brāhmaṇasyeha gajena kṛtyam ||11||

12gautama uvāca|
tavaiva gāvo hi bhavantu rājan dāsyaḥ saniṣkā vividhaṃ ca ratnam
|
anyacca vittaṃ vividhaṃ narendra kiṃ brāhmaṇasyeha dhanena kṛtyam ||12||

13dhṛtarāṣṭra uvāca|
brāhmaṇānāṃ hastibhirnāsti kṛtyaṃ rājanyānāṃ nāgakulāni vipra
|
svaṃ vāhanaṃ nayato nāstyadharmo nāgaśreṣṭhaṃ gautamāsmānnivarta ||13||

14gautama uvāca|
yatra preto nandati puṇyakarmā yatra pretaḥ śocate pāpakarmā
|
vaivasvatasya sadane mahātmaṃstatra tvāhaṃ hastinaṃ yātayiṣye ||14||

15dhṛtarāṣṭra uvāca|
ye niṣkriyā nāstikāḥ śraddadhānāḥ pāpātmāna indriyārthe niviṣṭāḥ
|
yamasya te yātanāṃ prāpnuvanti paraṃ gantā dhṛtarāṣṭro na tatra ||15||

16gautama uvāca|
vaivasvatī saṃyamanī janānāṃ yatrānṛtaṃ nocyate yatra satyam
|
yatrābalā balinaṃ yātayanti tatra tvāhaṃ hastinaṃ yātayiṣye ||16||

17dhṛtarāṣṭra uvāca|
jyeṣṭhāṃ svasāraṃ pitaraṃ mātaraṃ ca yathā śatruṃ madamattāścaranti
|
tathāvidhānāmeṣa loko maharṣeḥ paraṃ gantā dhṛtarāṣṭro na tatra ||17||

18gautama uvāca|
mandākinī vaiśravaṇasya rājño mahābhāgā bhogijanapraveśyā
|
gandharvayakṣairapsarobhiśca juṣṭā tatra tvāhaṃ hastinaṃ yātayiṣye ||18||

19dhṛtarāṣṭra uvāca|
atithivratāḥ suvratā ye janā vai pratiśrayaṃ dadati brāhmaṇebhyaḥ
|
śiṣṭāśinaḥ saṃvibhajyāśritāṃśca mandākinīṃ te'pi vibhūṣayanti ||19||

20gautama uvāca|
meroragre yadvanaṃ bhāti ramyaṃ supuṣpitaṃ kinnarīgītajuṣṭam
|
sudarśanā yatra jambūrviśālā tatra tvāhaṃ hastinaṃ yātayiṣye ||20||

21dhṛtarāṣṭra uvāca|
ye brāhmaṇā mṛdavaḥ satyaśīlā bahuśrutāḥ sarvabhūtābhirāmāḥ
|
ye'dhīyate setihāsaṃ purāṇaṃ madhvāhulyā juhvati vai dvijebhyaḥ ||21||

22tathāvidhānāmeṣa loko maharṣe paraṃ gantā dhṛtarāṣṭro na tatra
|
yadvidyate viditaṃ sthānamasti tadbrūhi tvaṃ tvarito hyeṣa yāmi ||22||

23gautama uvāca|
supuṣpitaṃ kiṃ nararāja juṣṭaṃ priyaṃ vanaṃ nandanaṃ nāradasya
|
gandharvāṇāmapsarasāṃ ca śaśvattatra tvāhaṃ hastinaṃ yātayiṣye ||23||

24dhṛtarāṣṭra uvāca|
ye nṛtyagīte kuśalā janāḥ sadā hyayācamānāḥ sahitāścaranti
|
tathāvidhānāmeṣa loko maharṣe paraṃ gantā dhṛtarāṣṭro na tatra ||24||

25gautama uvāca|
yatrottarāḥ kuravo bhānti ramyā devaiḥ sārdhaṃ moda‌mānā narendra
|
yatrāgniyaunāśca vasanti lokā abyonayaḥ parvatayonayaśca ||25||

26yatra śakro varṣati sarvakāmānyatra striyaḥ kāmacārā bhavanti
|
yatra cerṣyā nāsti nārīnarāṇāṃ tatra tvāhaṃ hastinaṃ yātayiṣye ||26||

27dhṛtarāṣṭra uvāca|
ye sarvabhūteṣu nivṛttakāmā amāṃsādā nyastadaṇḍāścaranti
|
na hiṃsanti sthāvaraṃ jaṅgamaṃ ca bhūtānāṃ ye sarvabhūtātmabhūtāḥ ||27||

28nirāśiṣo nirmamā vītarāgā lābhālābhe tulyanindāpraśaṃsāḥ
|
tathāvidhānāmeṣa loko maharṣe paraṃ gantā dhṛtarāṣṭro na tatra ||28||

29gautama uvāca|
tato'pare bhānti lokāḥ sanātanāḥ supuṇyagandhā virajā vītaśokāḥ
|
somasya rājñaḥ sadane mahātmanastatra tvā'haṃ hastinaṃ yātayiṣye ||29||

30dhṛtarāṣṭra uvāca|
ye dānaśīlān pratigṛhṇate sadā na cāpyarthāṃścādadate parebhyaḥ
|
yeṣāmadeyamarhate nāsti kiṃcitsarvātithyāḥ suprasādā janāśca ||30||

31ye kṣantāro nābhijalpanti cānyān satrībhūtāḥ satataṃ puṇyaśīlāḥ
|
tathāvidhānāmeṣa loko maharṣe paraṃ gantā dhṛtarāṣṭro na tatra ||31||

32gautama uvāca|
tato'pare bhānti lokāḥ sanātanā virajaso vitamaskā viśokāḥ
|
ādityadevasya padaṃ mahātmanastatra tvāhaṃ hastinaṃ yātayiṣye ||32||

33dhṛtarāṣṭra uvāca|
svādhyāyaśīlā guruśuśrūṣaṇe ratāstapasvinaḥ suvratāḥ satyasandhāḥ
|
ācāryāṇāmapratikūlabhāṣiṇo nityotthitā gurukarmasvacodyāḥ||33||

34tathāvidhānāmeṣa loko maharṣe viśuddhānāṃ bhāvito vāgyatānām
|
satye sthitānāṃ vedavidāṃ mahātmanāṃ paraṃ gantā dhṛtarāṣṭro na tatra ||34||

35gautama uvāca|
tato'pare bhānti lokāḥ sanātanāḥ supuṇyagandhā virajā viśokāḥ
|
varuṇasya rājñaḥ sadane mahātmanastatra tvāhaṃ hastinaṃ yātayiṣye ||35||

36dhṛtarāṣṭra uvāca|
cāturmāsyairye yajante janāḥ sadā tatheṣṭīnāṃ daśaśataṃ prāpnuvanti
|
ye cāgnihotraṃ juhvati śraddadhānā yathāmnāyaṃ trīṇi varṣāṇi viprāḥ ||36||

37sudhāriṇāṃ dharmasure mahātmanāṃ yathodite vartmani susthitānām
|
dharmātmanāmudvahatāṃ gatiṃ tāṃ paraṃ gantā dhṛtarāṣṭro na tatra ||37||

38gautama uvāca|
indrasya lokā virajā viśokā duranvayāḥ kāṃkṣitā mānavānām
|
tasyāhaṃ te bhavane bhūritejaso rājannimaṃ hastinaṃ yātayiṣye ||38||

39dhṛtarāṣṭra uvāca|
śatavarṣajīvī yaśca śūro manuṣyo vedādhyāyī yaśca yajvā'pramattaḥ
|
ete sarve śakralokaṃ vrajanti paraṃ gantā dhṛtarāṣṭro na tatra ||39||

40gautama uvāca|
prājāpatyāḥ santi lokā mahānto nākasya pṛṣṭhe puṣkalā vītaśokāḥ
|
manīṣitāḥ sarvalokodbhavānāṃ tatra tvāhaṃ hastinaṃ yātayiṣye ||40||

41dhṛtarāṣṭra uvāca|
ye rājāno rājasūyābhiṣiktā dharmātmāno rakṣitāraḥ prajānām
|
ye cāśvamedhāvabhṛthe plutāṅgāsteṣāṃ lokā dhṛtarāṣṭro na tatra ||41||

42gautama uvāca|
tataḥ paraṃ bhānti lokāḥ sanātanāḥ supuṇyagandhā virajā vītaśokāḥ
|
tasminnahaṃ durlabhe cāpyadhṛṣye gavāṃ loke hastinaṃ yātayiṣye ||42||

43dhṛtarāṣṭra uvāca|
yo gosahasrī śatadaḥ samāṃ samāṃ gavāṃ śatī daśa dadyācca śaktyā
|
tathā daśabhyo yaśca dadyādihaikāṃ pañcabhyo vā dānaśīlastathaikām ||43||

44ye jīryante brahmacaryeṇa viprā brāhmīṃ vācaṃ parirakṣanti caiva
|
manasvinastīrthayātrāparāyaṇāste tatra modanti gavāṃ nivāse ||44||

45prabhāsaṃ mānasaṃ tīrthaṃ puṣkarāṇi mahatsaraḥ
|
puṇyaṃ ca naimiṣaṃ tīrthaṃ bāhudāṃ karatoyinīm ||45||

46gayāṃ gayaśiraścaiva vipāśāṃ sthūlavālukām
|
kṛṣṇāṃ gaṅgāṃ pañcanadaṃ mahāhradamathāpi ca ||46||

47gomatīṃ kauśikiṃ paṃpāṃ mahātmāno dhṛtavratāḥ
|
sarasvatīdṛṣadvatyau yamunāṃ ye tu yānti ca ||47||

48tatra te divyasaṃsthānā divyamālyadharāḥ śivāḥ
|
prathānti puṇyagandhāḍhyā dhṛtarāṣṭro na tatra vai ||48||

49gautama uvāca|
yatra śītabhayaṃ nāsti na coṣṇabhayamaṇvapi
|
na kṣutpipāse na glānirna duḥkhaṃ na sukhaṃ tathā ||49||

50na dveṣyo na priyaḥ kaścinna bandhurna ripustathā
|
na jarāmaraṇe tatra na puṇyaṃ na ca pātakam ||50||

51tasminvirajasi sphīte prajñāsattvavyavasthite
|
svayaṃbhubhavane puṇye hastinaṃ me pradāsyasi ||51||

52dhṛtarāṣṭra uvāca|
nirmuktāḥ sarvasaṅgairye kṛtātmāno yatavratāḥ
|
adhyātmayogasaṃsthānairyuktāḥ svargagatiṃ gatāḥ ||52||

53te brahmabhavanaṃ puṇyaṃ prāpnuvantīha sāttvikāḥ
|
na tatra dhṛtarāṣṭraste śakyo draṣṭuṃ mahāmune ||53||

54gautama uvāca|
rathantaraṃ yatra bṛhacca gīyate yatra vedī puṇḍarīkaistṛṇoti
|
yatropayāti haribhiḥ somapīthī tatra tvāhaṃ hastinaṃ yātayiṣye ||54||

55budhyāmi tvāṃ vṛtrahaṇaṃ śatakratuṃ vyatikramantaṃ bhuvanāni viśvā
|
kaccinna vācā vṛjinaṃ kadācidakārṣaṃ te manasobhiṣaṅgāt ||55||

56śatakraturuvāca|
maghavā'haṃ lokapathaṃ prajānāmanvāgamaṃ parivāde gajasya
|
tasmādbhavānpraṇataṃ mā'nuśāstu bravīṣi yattatkaravāṇi sarvam ||56||

57gautama uvāca|
śvetaṃ kareṇuṃ mama putraṃ hi nāgaṃ yaṃ me'hārṣīrdaśavarṣāṇi bālam
|
yo me vane vasato'bhūddvitīyastameva me dehi surendra nāgam ||57||

58śatakraturuvāca|
ayaṃ sutaste dvijamukhya nāga āgacchati tvāmabhivīkṣamāṇaḥ
|
pādau ca te nāsikayopajighrate śreyo mamādhyāhi namaśca te'stu ||58||

59gautama uvāca|
śivaṃ sadaiveha surendra tubhyaṃ dhyāyāmi pūjāṃ ca sadā prayuñje
|
mamāpi tvaṃ śakra śivaṃ dadasva tvayā dattaṃ pratigṛhṇāmi nāgam ||59||

60śatakraturuvāca|
yeṣāṃ vedā nihitā vai guhāyāṃ manīṣiṇāṃ satyavatāṃ mahātmanām
|
teṣāṃ tvayaikena mahātmanā'smi vṛddhastasmātprītimāṃste'hamadya ||60||

61hantaihi brāhmaṇa kṣipraṃ saha putreṇa hastinā
|
tvaṃ hi prāptuṃ śubhānlokānahnāya ca cirāya ca ||61||

62sa gautamaṃ puraskṛtya saha putreṇa hastinā
|
divamācakrame vajrī sadbhiḥ saha durāsadam ||62||

63idaṃ yaḥ śṛṇuyānnityaṃ yaḥ paṭhedvā jitendriyaḥ
|
sa yāti brahmaṇo lokaṃ brāhmaṇo gautamo yathā ||63||
