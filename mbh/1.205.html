Mahābhārata 1.205 (Ādiparvan)

1vidura uvāca|
rājanniḥsaṃśayaṃ śreyo vācyastvamasi bāndhavaiḥ
|
na tvaśuśrūṣamāṇe vai vākyaṃ saṃprati tiṣṭhati||1||

2priyaṃ hitaṃ ca tadvākyamuktavānkurusattamaḥ
|
bhīṣmaḥ śāntanavo rājanpratigṛhṇāsi tanna ca||2||

3tathā droṇena bahudhā bhāṣitaṃ hitamuttamam
|
tacca rādhāsutaḥ karṇo manyate na hitaṃ tava||3||

4cintayaṃśca na paśyāmi rājaṃstava suhṛttamam
|
ābhyāṃ puruṣasiṃhābhyāṃ yo vā syātprajñayādhikaḥ||4||

5imau hi vṛddhau vayasā prajñayā ca śrutena ca
|
samau ca tvayi rājendra tathā pāṇḍusuteṣu ca||5||

6dharme cānavarau rājansatyatāyāṃ ca bhārata
|
rāmāddāśaratheścaiva gayāccaiva na saṃśayaḥ||6||

7na coktavantāvaśreyaḥ purastādapi kiṃcana
|
na cāpyapakṛtaṃ kiṃcidanayorlakṣyate tvayi||7||

8tāvubhau puruṣavyāghrāvanāgasi nṛpe tvayi
|
na mantrayetāṃ tvacchreyaḥ kathaṃ satyaparākramau||8||

9prajñāvantau naraśreṣṭhāvasmi~lloke narādhipa
|
tvannimittamato nemau kiṃcijjihmaṃ vadiṣyataḥ||9||

10iti me naiṣṭhikī buddhirvartate kurunandana
|
na cārthahetordharmajñau vakṣyataḥ pakṣasaṃśritam||10||

11etaddhi paramaṃ śreyo manye'haṃ tava bhārata
|
duryodhanaprabhṛtayaḥ putrā rājanyathā tava||11||

12tathaiva pāṇḍaveyāste putrā rājanna saṃśayaḥ
|
teṣu cedahitaṃ kiṃcinmantrayeyuratadvidaḥ||12||

13mantriṇaste na ca śreyaḥ prapaśyanti viśeṣataḥ
|
atha te hṛdaye rājanviśeṣaḥ sveṣu vartate
|
antarasthaṃ vivṛṇvānāḥ śreyaḥ kuryurna te dhruvam||13||

14etadarthamimau rājanmahātmānau mahādyutī
|
nocaturvivṛtaṃ kiṃcinna hyeṣa tava niścayaḥ||14||

15yaccāpyaśakyatāṃ teṣāmāhatuḥ puruṣarṣabhau
|
tattathā puruṣavyāghra tava tadbhadramastu te||15||

16kathaṃ hi pāṇḍavaḥ śrīmānsavyasācī dhanañjayaḥ
|
śakyo vijetuṃ saṃgrāme rājanmaghavatāpi hi||16||

17bhīmaseno mahābāhurnāgāyutabalo mahān
|
kathaṃ sma yudhi śakyeta vijetumamarairapi||17||

18tathaiva kṛtinau yuddhe yamau yamasutāviva
|
kathaṃ vijetuṃ śakyau tau raṇe jīvitumicchatā||18||

19yasmindhṛtiranukrośaḥ kṣamā satyaṃ parākramaḥ
|
nityāni pāṇḍave jyeṣṭhe sa jīyeta raṇe katham||19||

20yeṣāṃ pakṣadharo rāmo yeṣāṃ mantrī janārdanaḥ
|
kiṃ nu tairajitaṃ saṅkhye yeṣāṃ pakṣe ca sātyakiḥ||20||

21drupadaḥ śvaśuro yeṣāṃ yeṣāṃ syālāśca pārṣatāḥ
|
dhṛṣṭadyumnamukhā vīrā bhrātaro drupadātmajāḥ||21||

22so'śakyatāṃ ca vijñāya teṣāmagre ca bhārata
|
dāyādyatāṃ ca dharmeṇa samyakteṣu samācara||22||

23idaṃ nirdiṣṭamayaśaḥ purocanakṛtaṃ mahat
|
teṣāmanugraheṇādya rājanprakṣālayātmanaḥ||23||

24teṣāmanugrahaścāyaṃ sarveṣāṃ caiva naḥ kule
|
jīvitaṃ ca paraṃ śreyaḥ kṣatrasya ca vivardhanam||24||

25drupado'pi mahānrājā kṛtavairaśca naḥ purā
|
tasya saṃgrahaṇaṃ rājansvapakṣasya vivardhanam||25||

26balavantaśca dāśārhā bahavaśca viśāṃpate
|
yataḥ kṛṣṇastataḥ sarve yataḥ kṛṣṇastato jayaḥ||26||

27yacca sāmnaiva śakyeta kāryaṃ sādhayituṃ nṛpa
|
ko daivaśaptastatkāryaṃ vigraheṇa samācaret||27||

28śrutvā ca jīvataḥ pārthānpaurajānapadā janāḥ
|
balavaddarśane hṛṣṭāsteṣāṃ rājanpriyaṃ kuru||28||

29duryodhanaśca karṇaśca śakuniścāpi saubalaḥ
|
adharmayuktā duṣprajñā bālā maiṣāṃ vacaḥ kṛthāḥ||29||

30uktametatpurā rājanmayā guṇavatastava
|
duryodhanāparādhena prajeyaṃ vai vinaṅkṣyati||30||
