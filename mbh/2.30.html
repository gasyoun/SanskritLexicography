Mahābhārata 2.30 (Sabhāparvan)

1vaiśampāyana uvāca|
tataḥ kumāraviṣaye śreṇimantamathājayat
|
kosalādhipatiṃ caiva bṛhadbalamarindamaḥ||1||

2ayodhyāyāṃ tu dharmajñaṃ dīrghayajñaṃ mahābalam
|
ajayatṇaṇḍavaśreṣṭho nātitīvreṇa karmaṇā||2||

3tato gopālakakṣaṃ ca sottarānapi kosalān
|
mallānāmadhipaṃ caiva pārthivaṃ cājayatprabhuḥ||3||

4tato himavataḥ pārśvaṃ samabhyetya jalodbhavam
|
sarvamalpena kālena deśaṃ cakre vaśaṃ balī||4||

5evaṃ bahuvidhāndeśānvijigye bharatarṣabhaḥ
|
bhallāṭamabhito jigye śuktimantaṃ ca parvatam||5||

6pāṇḍavaḥ sumahāvīryo balena balināṃ varaḥ
|
sa kāśirājaṃ samare subāhumanivartinam||6||

7vaśe cakre mahābāhurbhīmo bhīmaparākramaḥ
|
tataḥ supārśvamabhitastathā rājapatiṃ kratham||7||

8yudhyamānaṃ bālatsaṅkhye vijigye pāṇḍavarṣabhaḥ
|
tato matsyānmahātejā maladāṃśca mahābalān||8||

9anaghānabhayāṃścaiva paśubhūmiṃ ca sarvaśaḥ
|
nivṛtya ca mahābāhurmadadhāraṃ mahīdharam||9||

10somadheyāṃśca nirjitya pratyayāvuttarāmukhaḥ
|
vatsabhūmiṃ ca kaunteyo vijigye balavānbalāt||10||

11bhargāṇāmadhipaṃ caiva niṣādādhipatiṃ tathā
|
vijigye bhūmipālāṃśca maṇimatpramukhānbahūn||11||

12tato dakṣiṇamallāṃśca bhogavantaṃ ca parvatam
|
tarasaivājayadbhīmo nātitīvreṇa karmaṇā||12||

13śarmakānvarmakāṃścaiva vyajayatsāntvapūrvakam
|
vaidehakaṃ ca rājānaṃ janakaṃ jagatīpatim||13||

14vijigye puruṣavyāghro nātitīvreṇa karmaṇā
|
śakāṃśca barbarāṃścaiva ajayacchadmapūrvakam||14||

15vaidehasthastu kaunteya indraparvatamantikāt
|
kirātānāmadhipatīnajayatsapta pāṇḍavaḥ||15||

16tataḥ suhmānprasuhmāṃśca sapakṣānativīryavān
|
vijitya yudhi kaunteyo māgadhānabhyadhādbalī||16||

17daṇḍaṃ ca daṇḍadhāraṃ ca vijitya pṛthivīpatīn
|
taireva sahitaiḥ sarvairgirivrajamupādravat||17||

18jārāsandhiṃ sāntvayitvā kare ca viniveśya ha
|
taireva sahitaiḥ sarvaiḥ karṇamabyadravadbalī||18||

19sa kampayanniva mahīṃ balena catuṅgiṇā
|
yuyudhe pāṇḍavaśreṣṭhaḥ karṇenāmitraghātinā||19||

20sa karṇaṃ yudhi nirjitya vaśe kṛtvā ca bhārata
|
tato vijigye balavānrājñaḥ parvatavāsinaḥ||20||

21atha modāgirau caiva rājānaṃ balavattaram
|
pāṇḍavo bāhuvīryeṇa nijaghāna mahāmṛdhe||21||

22tataḥ puṇḍrādhipaṃ vīraṃ vāsudevaṃ mahābalam
|
kauśikīkacchanilayaṃ rājānaṃ ca mahaujasam||22||

23ubhau balabhṛtau vīrāvumau tīvraparākramau
|
nirjityājau mahārāja vaṅgarājamupādravat||23||

24samudrasena nirjitya candrasenaṃ ca pārthivam
|
tāmraliptaṃ ca rājānaṃ karvaṭādhipatiṃ tathā||24||

25suhyānāmadhipaṃ caiva ye ca sāgaravāsinaḥ
|
sarvānmlecchagaṇāṃścaiva vijigye bharatarṣabhaḥ||25||

26evaṃ bahuvidhāndeśānvijitya pavanātmajaḥ
|
vasu tebhya upādāya lauhityamagadbalī||26||

27sa sarvānmlecchanṛpatīnsāgarānūpavāsinaḥ
|
karamāhārayāmāsa ratnāni vividhāni ca||27||

28candanāguruvastrāṇi maṇimauktikakambalam
|
kāñcanaṃ rajataṃ caiva vidrumaṃ ca mahādhanam||28||

29te koṭīśatasaṅkhyena kaunteyaṃ mahatā tadā
|
abhyavarṣanmahātmānaṃ dhanavarṣeṇa pāṇḍavam||29||

30indraprasthamupāgamya bhīmo bhīmaparākramaḥ
|
nivedayāmāsa tadā dharmarājāya taddhanam||30||
