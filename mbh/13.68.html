Mahābhārata 13.68 (Anuśāsanaparvan)

1tilānāṃ kidṛśam dānamatha dīpasya caiva hi
|
annānāṃ vāsasāṃ caiva bhūya eva bravīhi me ||1||

2bhīṣma uvāca|
atrāpyudāharantīmamitihāsaṃ purātanam
|
brāhmaṇasya ca saṃvādaṃ yamasya ca yudhiṣṭhira ||2||

3madhyadeśe mahāngrāmo brāhmaṇānāṃ babhūva ha
|
gaṃgāyamunayormadhye yāmunasya gireradhaḥ ||3||

4parṇaśāleti vikhyāto ramaṇīyo narādhipa
|
vidvāṃsastatra bhūyiṣṭhā brāhmaṇāścāvasaṃstathā ||4||

5atha prāha yamaḥ kaṃcitpuruṣa kṛṣṇavāsasam
|
raktākṣamūrdhvaromāṇaṃ kākajaṃghākṣināsikam ||5||

6gaccha tvaṃ brāhmaṇagrāmaṃ tato gatvā tamānaya
|
agastyaṃ gotrataścāpi nāmataścāpi śarmiṇam ||6||

7śame niviṣṭaṃ vidvāṃsamadhyāpakamanāvṛtam
|
mā cānyamānayethāstvaṃ sagotraṃ tasya pārśvataḥ ||7||

8sa hi tādṛgguṇastena tulyo'dhyayanajanmanā
|
apatyeṣu tathā vṛtte samastenaiva dhīmatā ||8||

9tamānaya yathoddiṣṭaṃ pūjā kāryā hi tasya vai
|
sa gatvā pratikūlaṃ taccakāra yamaśāsanam ||9||

10tamākramyānayāmāsa pratiṣiddho yamena yaḥ
|
tasmai yamaḥ samutthāya pūjāṃ kṛtvā ca vīryavān ||10||

11provāca nīyatāmeṣa so'nya ānīyatāmiti
|
evamukte tu vacane dharmarājena sa dvijaḥ ||11||

12uvāca dharmarājānaṃ nirviṇṇo'dhyayanena vai
|
yo me kālo bhaveccheṣastaṃ vaseyamihācyuta ||12||

13yama uvāca|
nāhaṃ kālasya vihitaṃ prāpnomīha kathaṃcana
|
yo hi dharmaṃ carati vai taṃ tu jānāmi kevalam ||13||

14gaccha vipra tvamadyaiva ālayaṃ sva mahādyute
|
brūhi sarvaṃ yathā svairaṃ karavāṇi kimacyuta ||14||

15brāhmaṇa uvāca|
yattatra kṛtvā sumahatpuṇyaṃ syāttadbravīhi me
|
sarvasya hi pramāṇaṃ tvaṃ trailokyasyāpi sattama ||15||

16yama uvāca|
śṛṇu tattvena viprarṣe pradānavidhimuttamam
|
tilāḥ paramakaṃ dānaṃ puṇyaṃ caiveha śāśvatam ||16||

17tilāśca saṃpradātavyā yathāśakti dvijarṣabha
|
nityadānātsarvakāmāṃstilā nirvartayantyuta ||17||

18tilān śrāddhe praśaṃsanti dānametaddhyanuttamam
|
tānprayacchasva viprebhyo vidhidṛṣṭena karmaṇā ||18||

19vaiśākhyāṃ paurṇamāsyāṃ tu tilāndadyāddvijātiṣu
|
tilā bhakṣayitavyāśca sadā tvālaṃbhanaṃ ca taiḥ ||19||

20kāryaṃ satatamicchadbhiḥ śreyaḥ sarvātmanā gṛhe
|
tathā''paḥ sarvadā deyāḥ peyāścaiva na saṃśayaḥ ||20||

21puṣkariṇyastaḍāgāni kūpāṃścaivātra khānayet
|
etatsudurlabhataramihaloke dvijottama ||21||

22āpo nityaṃ pradeyāste puṇyaṃ hyetadanuttamam
|
prapāśca kāryā dānārthaṃ nityaṃ te dvijasattama
|
bhukte'pyannaṃ pradeyaṃ tu pānīyaṃ vai viśeṣataḥ ||22||

23bhīṣma uvāca|
ityukte sa tadā tena yamadūne vai gṛhān
|
nītaśca kārayāmāsa sarvaṃ tadyamaśāsanam ||23||

24nītvā taṃ yamadūto'pi gṛhītvā śarmiṇaṃ tadā
|
yayau sa dharmarājāya nyavedayata cāpi tam ||24||

25taṃ dharmarājo dharmajñaṃ pūjayitvā pratāpavān
|
kṛtvā ca saṃvidaṃ tena visasarja yathāgatam ||25||

26tasyāpi ca yamaḥ sarvamupadeśaṃ cakārā ha
|
pretyaitya ca tataḥ sarvaṃ cakrāroktaṃ yamena tat ||26||

27tathā praśaṃsate dīpānyamaḥ pitṛhitepsayā
|
tasmāddīpaprado nityaṃ santārayati pitṝn ||27||

28dātavyāḥ satataṃ dīpāstasmādbharatasattama
|
devatānāṃ pitṝṇāṃ ca cakṣuṣyaṃ cātmanāṃ vibho ||28||

29ratnadānaṃ caḥ sumahatpuṇyamuktaṃ janādhipa
|
yastān vikrīya yajate brāhmaṇo hyabhayaṃkaram ||29||

30yadvai dadāti viprebhyo brāhmaṇaḥ pratigṛhya vai
|
ubhayoḥ syāttadakṣayyaṃ dāturādātureva ca ||30||

31yo dadāti sthitaḥ sthityāṃ tādṛśāya pratigraham
|
ubhayorakṣayaṃ dharmaṃ taṃ manuḥ prāha dharmavit ||31||

32vāsasāṃ saṃpradānena svadāranirato naraḥ
|
suvastraśca suveṣaśca bhavatītyanuśuśruma ||32||

33gāvaḥ suvarṇaṃ ca tathā tilāścevānuvarṇitāḥ
|
bahuśaḥ puruṣavyāghra veda‌prāmāṇyadarśanāt ||33||

34vivāhāṃścaiva kurvīta putrānutpādayate ca
|
putralābho hi kauravya sarvalābhādviśiṣyate ||34||
