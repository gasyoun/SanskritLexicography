Mahābhārata 12.49 (Śāntiparvan)

1vāsudeva uvāca |
śṛṇu kaunteya rāmasya prabhāvo yo mayā śrutaḥ
|
maharṣīṇāṃ kathayatāṃ vikramaṃ tasya janma ca ||1||

2yathā ca jāmadagnyena koṭiśaḥ kṣatriyā hatāḥ
|
udbhūtā rājavaṃśeṣu ye bhūyo bhārate hatāḥ ||2||

3jahnorajastu tanayo balākāśvastu tatsutaḥ
|
kuśiko nāma dharmajñastasya putro mahīpate ||3||

4agryaṃ tapaḥ samātiṣṭhatsahasrākṣasamo bhuvi
|
putraṃ labheyamajitaṃ trilokeśvaramityuta ||4||

5tamugratapasaṃ dṛṣṭvā sahasrākṣaḥ purandaraḥ
|
samarthaṃ putrajanane svayamevānvapadyata ||5||

6putratvamagamadrājaṃstasya lokeśvareśvaraḥ
|
gādhirnāmā'bhavatputraḥ kauśikaḥ pākaśāsanaḥ ||6||

7tasya kanyā'bhavadrājannāmnā satyavatī prabho
|
tāṃ gādhirbhuguputrāya sarcīkāya dadau prabhuḥ ||7||

8tasyāḥ prītaḥ sa śaucena bhārgavaḥ kurunandana
|
putrārthaṃ śrapayāmāsa caruṃ gādhestathaiva ca ||8||

9āhūyovāca tāṃ bhāryāṃ sarcīko bhārgavastadā
|
upabhojyaścarurayaṃ tvayā mātrā'pyayaṃ tava ||9||

10tasyā janiṣyate putro dīptimānkṣatriyarṣabhaḥ
|
ajayyaḥ kṣatriyairloke kṣatriyarṣabhasūdanaḥ ||10||

11tavāpi putraṃ kalyāṇi dhṛtimantaṃ śamātmakam
|
tapo'nvitaṃ dvijaśreṣṭhaṃ carureṣa vidhāsyati ||11||

12ityevamuktvā tāṃ bhāryāṃ sarcīko bhṛgunandanaḥ
|
tapasyabhirataḥ śrīmāñjagāmāraṇyameva hi ||12||

13etasminneva kāle tu tīrthayātrāparo nṛpaḥ
|
gādhiḥ sadāraḥ samprāptaḥ sarcīkasyāśramaṃ prati ||13||

14carudvayaṃ gṛhītvā ca rājansatyavatī tadā
|
bharturvākyaṃ tadā vyagrā mātre hṛṣṭā nyavedayat ||14||

15mātā tu tasyāḥ kaunteya duhitre svaṃ caruṃ dadau
|
tasyāścarumathājñānādātmasaṃsthaṃ cakāra ha ||15||

16atha satyavatī garbhaṃ kṣatriyāntakaraṃ tadā
|
dhārayāmāsa dīptena vapuṣā ghoradarśanam ||16||

17tāmṛcīkastadā dṛṣṭvā tasyā garbhagataṃ dvijam
|
abravīdbhṛguśārdūlaḥ svāṃ bhāryāṃ devarūpiṇīm ||17||

18mātrā'sivyaṃsitā bhadre caruvyatyāsahetunā
|
bhaviṣyati hi te putraḥ krūrakarmā'tyamarṣaṇaḥ ||18||

19utpatsyati ca te bhrātā brahmabhūtastaporataḥ
|
viśvaṃ hi brahma sumahaccarau tava samāhitam ||19||

20kṣatravīryaṃ ca sakalaṃ tava mātre samarpitam
|
viparyayeṇa te bhadre naitadevaṃ bhaviṣyati ||20||

21mātuste brāhmaṇo bhūyāttava ca kṣatriyaḥ sutaḥ
|
saivamuktā mahābhāgā bhartrā satyavatī tadā ||21||

22papāta śirasā tasmai vepantī cābravīdidam
|
nārho'si bhagavannadya vaktumevaṃvidhaṃ vacaḥ
|
brāhmaṇāpasadaṃ putraṃ prāpsyasīti hi māṃ prabho ||22||

23ṛcīka uvāca |
naiṣa saṃkalpitaḥ kāmo mayā bhadre tathā tvayi
|
ugrakarmā samutpannaścaruvyatyāsahetunā ||23||

24satyavatyuvāca |
icchan lokānapi mune sṛjethāḥ kiṃ punaḥ sutam
|
śāmātmakamṛjuṃ putraṃ dātumarhasi me prabho ||24||

25ṛcīka uvāca |
noktapūrvānṛtaṃ bhadre svaireṣvapi kadācana
|
kimutāgniṃ samādhāya mantravaccarusādhane ||25||

26dṛṣṭametatpurā bhadre jñātaṃ ca tapasā mayā
|
brahmabhūtaṃ hi sakalaṃ pitustava kulaṃ bhavet ||26||

27satyavatyuvāca |
kāmamevaṃ bhavetpautro mameha tava ca prabho
|
śamātmakamahaṃ putraṃ labheyaṃ japatāṃ vara ||27||

28ṛcīka uvāca |
putre nāsti viśeṣo me pautre ca varavarṇini
|
yathā tvayoktaṃ vacanaṃ tathā bhadre bhaviṣyati ||28||

29vāsudeva uvāca |
tataḥ satyavatī putraṃ janayāmāsa bhārgavam
|
tapasyabhirataṃ śāntaṃ jamadagniṃ yatavratam ||29||

30viśvāmitraṃ ca dāyādaṃ gādhiḥ kuśikanandanaḥ
|
yaḥ prāpa brahmasamitaṃ viśvairbrahmaguṇairyutam ||30||

31ṛcīko janayāmāsa jamadagniṃ taponidhim
|
so'pi putraṃ hyajanayajjamadagniḥ sudāruṇam ||31||

32sarvavidyāntagaṃ śreṣṭhaṃ dhanurvedasya pāragam
|
rāmaṃ kṣatriyahantāraṃ pradīptamiva pāvakam ||32||

33toṣayitvā mahādevaṃ parvate gandhamādane
|
astrāṇi varayāmāsa paraśuṃ cātitejasam ||33||

34sa tenākuṇṭhadhāreṇa jvalitānalavarcasā
|
kuṭhāreṇāprameyeṇa lokeṣvapratimo'bhavat ||34||

35etasminneva kāle tu kṛtavīryātmajo balī
|
arjuno nāma tejasvī kṣatriyo haihayādhipaḥ ||35||

36dattātreyaprasādena rājā bāhusahasravān
|
cakravartī mahātejā viprāṇāmāśvamedhike ||36||

37dadau sa pṛthivīṃ sarvāṃ saptadvīpāṃ saparvatām
|
svabāhvastrabalenājau jitvā paramadharmavit ||37||

38tṛṣitena ca kaunteya bhikṣitaścitrabhānunā
|
sahasrabāhurvikrāntaḥ prādādbhikṣāmathāgnaye ||38||

39grāmānpurāṇi rāṣṭrāṇi ghoṣāṃścaiva tu vīryavān
|
jajvāla tasya bāṇāgrāccitrabhānurdidhakṣayā ||39||

40sa tasya puruṣendrasya prabhāveṇa mahaujasaḥ
|
dadāha kārtavīryasya śailānatha vanaspatīn ||40||

41sa śūnyamāśramaṃ ramyamāpavasya mahātmanaḥ
|
dadāha pavaneneddhaścitrabhānuḥ sahaihayaḥ ||41||

42āpavastu tato roṣācchaśāpārjunamacyuta
|
dagdhe śrame mahābāho kārtavīryeṇa vīryavān ||42||

43tvayā na varjitaṃ yasmānmamedaṃ hi mahadvanam
|
dagdhaṃ tasmādraṇe rāmo bāhūṃste cchetsyate'rjuna ||43||

44arjunastu mahātejā balī nityaṃ śamātmakaḥ
|
brahmaṇyaśca śaraṇyaśca dātā śūraśca bhārata ||44||

45nācintayattadā śāpaṃ tena dattaṃ mahātmanā
|
tasya putrāstu balinaḥ śāpenāsanpiturvadhe ||45||

46nimittādavaliptā vai nṛśaṃsāścaiva sarvadā
|
jamadagnidhenvāste vatsamāninyurbharatarṣabha ||46||

47ajñātaṃ kārtavīryeṇa haiheyendreṇa dhīmatā
|
tannimittamabhūdyuddhaṃ jāmadagnermahātmanaḥ ||47||

48tato'rjunasya bāhūṃstāṃśchittvā rāmo ruṣānvitaḥ
|
taṃ bhramantaṃ tato vatsaṃ jāmadagnyaḥ svamāśramam ||48||

49pratyānayata rājendra teṣāmantaḥpurātprabhuḥ
|
arjunasya sutāste tu sambhūyābuddhayastadā ||49||

50gatvā''śramamasambuddhā jamadagnermahātmanaḥ
|
apātayanta bhallāgraiḥ śiraḥ kāyānnarādhipa ||50||

51samitkuśārthaṃ rāmasya niryātasya yaśasvinaḥ
|
tataḥ pitṛvadhāmarṣādrāmaḥ paramamanyumān ||51||

52niḥkṣatriyāṃ pratiśrutya mahīṃ śastramagṛhvata
|
tataḥ sa bhṛguśārdūlaḥ kārtavīryasya vīryavān ||52||

53vikramya nijaghānāśu putrānpautrāṃśca sarvaśaḥ
|
sa haihayasahasrāṇi hatvā paramamanyumān ||53||

54cakāra bhārgavo rājan mahīṃ śoṇitakardamām
|
sa tathā''śu mahātejāḥ kṛtvā niḥkṣatriyāṃ mahīm ||54||

55kṛpayā parayā''viṣṭo vanameva jagāma ha
|
tato varṣasahasreṣu samatīteṣu keṣucit ||55||

56kṣepaṃ samprāptavāṃstatra prakṛtyā kopana: prabhuḥ
|
viśvāmitrasya pautrastu raibhyaputro mahātapāḥ ||56||

57parāvasurmahārāja kṣiptvā''ha janasaṃsadi
|
ye te yayātipatane yajñe santaḥ samāgatāḥ ||57||

58pratardanaprabhṛtayo rāma kiṃ kṣatriyā na te ||58||

59mithyāpratijño rāma tvaṃ katthase janasaṃsadi
|
bhayātkṣatriyavīrāṇāṃ parvataṃ samupāśritaḥ ||59||

60sā punaḥ kṣatriyaśataiḥ pṛthivī sarvataḥ stṛtā
|
parāvasorvacaḥ śrutvā śastraṃ jagrāha bhārgavaḥ ||60||

61tato ye kṣatriyā rājan śataśastena varjitāḥ
|
te vivṛddhā mahāvīryāḥ pṛthivīpatayo'bhavan ||61||

62sa punastāñjaghānāśu bālānapi narādhipa
|
garbhasthaistu mahī vyāptā punarevābhavattadā ||62||

63jātaṃ jātaṃ sa garbhaṃ tu punareva jaghāna ha
|
arakṣaṃśca sutān kāṃścittadā kṣatriyayoṣitaḥ ||63||

64triḥsaptakṛtvaḥ pṛthivīṃ kṛtvā niḥkṣatriyāṃ prabhuḥ
|
dakṣiṇāmaśvamedhānte kaśyapāyādadattataḥ ||64||

65sa kṣatriyāṇāṃ śeṣārthaṃ kareṇoddiśya kaśyapaḥ
|
srukpragrahavatā rājaṃstato vākyamathābravīt ||65||

66gaccha tīraṃ samudrasya dakṣiṇasya mahāmune
|
na te madviṣaye rāma vastavyamiha karhicit ||66||

67tataḥ śūrpārakaṃ deśaṃ sāgarastasya nirmame
|
sahasā jāmadagnyasya so'parāntamahītalam ||67||

68kaśyapastāṃ mahārāja pratigṛhya vasundharām
|
kṛtvā brāhmaṇasaṃsthāṃ vai praviṣṭaḥ sumahadvanam ||68||

69tataḥ śūdrāśca vaiśyāśca yathā svairapracāriṇaḥ
|
avartanta dvijāgryāṇāṃ dāreṣu bharatarṣabha ||69||

70arājake jīvaloke durbalā balavattaraiḥ
|
pīḍyante na hi vipreṣu prabhutvaṃ kasyacittadā ||70||

71tataḥ kālena pṛthivī pīḍyamānā durātmabhiḥ
|
viparyayeṇa tenāśu praviveśa rasātalam ||71||

72arakṣyamāṇā vidhivatkṣatriyairdharmarakṣibhiḥ
|
tāṃ dṛṣṭvā dravatīṃ tatra saṃtrāsātsa mahāmanāḥ ||72||

73ūruṇā dhārayāmāsa kaśyapaḥ pṛthivīṃ tataḥ
|
dhṛtā tenoruṇā yena tenorvīti mahī smṛtā ||73||

74rakṣaṇārthaṃ samuddiśya yayāce pṛthivī tadā
|
prasādya kaśyapaṃ devī varayāmāsa bhūmipam ||74||

75pṛthivyuvāca |
santi brahmanmayā guptāḥ strīṣu kṣatriyapuṅgavāḥ
|
haihayānāṃ kule jātāste saṃrakṣantu māṃ mune ||75||

76asti pauravadāyādo vidūrathasutaḥ prabho
|
ṛkṣaiḥ saṃvardhito vipra ṛkṣavatyatha parvate ||76||

77tathā'nukampamānena yajvanā'thāmitaujasā
|
parāśareṇa dāyādaḥ saudāsasyābhirakṣitaḥ ||77||

78sarvakarmāṇi kurute śūdravattasya sa dvijaḥ
|
sarvakarmetyabhikhyātaḥ sa māṃ rakṣatu pārthivaḥ ||78||

79śibiputro mahātejā gopatirnāma nāmataḥ
|
vane saṃvardhito gobhiḥ so'bhirakṣatu māṃ mune ||79||

80pratardanasya putrastu vatso nāma mahābalaḥ
|
vatsaiḥ saṃvardhito goṣṭhe sa māṃ rakṣatu pārthivaḥ ||80||

81dadhivāhanapautrastu putro divirathasya ca
|
guptaḥ sa gautamenāsīdgaṅgākūle'bhirakṣitaḥ ||81||

82bṛhadratho mahātejā bhūribhūtipariṣkṛtaḥ
|
golāṅgūlairmahābhāgo gṛdhrakūṭe'bhirakṣitaḥ ||82||

83maruttasyānvavāye ca rakṣitā: kṣatriyātmajāḥ
|
marutpatisamā vīrye samudreṇābhirakṣitāḥ ||83||

84ete kṣatriyadāyādāstatra tatra pariśrutāḥ
|
dyokārahemakārādijātiṃ nityaṃ samāśritāḥ ||84||

85yadi māmabhirakṣanti tataḥ sthāsyāmi niścalā
|
eteṣāṃ pitaraścaiva tathaiva ca pitāmahāḥ ||85||

86madarthaṃ nihatā yuddhe rāmeṇākliṣṭarmaṇā
|
teṣāmapacitiścaiva mayā kāryā mahāmune ||86||

87na hyahaṃ kāmaye nityamatikrāntena rakṣaṇam
|
vartamānena varteyaṃ tatkṣipraṃ saṃvidhīyatām ||87||

88vāsudeva uvāca |
tataḥ pṛthivyā nirdiṣṭāṃstānsamānīya kaśyapaḥ
|
abhyaṣiñcanmahīpālānkṣatriyānvīryasammatān ||88||

89teṣāṃ putrāśca pautrāśca yeṣāṃ vaṃśāḥ pratiṣṭhitāḥ
|
evametatpurāvṛttaṃ yanmāṃ pṛcchasi pāṇḍava ||89||

90vaiśampāyana uvāca|
evaṃ bruvaṃstaṃ ca yadupravīro yudhiṣṭhiraṃ dharmabhṛtāṃ variṣṭham
|
rathena tenāśu yayau mahātmā diśaḥ prakāśanbhagavānivārkaḥ ||90||
