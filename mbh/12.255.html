Mahābhārata 12.255 (Śāntiparvan)

1bhīṣma uvāca|
bhūtānāṃ parisaṃkhyānaṃ bhūyaḥ putra niśāmaya
|
dvaipāyanamukhādbhraṣṭaṃ ślāghayā parayā'nagha ||1||

2dīptānalanibhaḥ prāha bhagavāndhūmavarcase
|
tato'hamapi vakṣyāmi bhūyaḥ putra nidarśanam ||2||

3bhūmeḥ sthairyaṃ gurutvaṃ ca kāṭhinyaṃ prasavārthatā
|
gandho gurutvaṃ śaktiśca saṃghātaḥ sthāpanā dhṛtiḥ ||3||

4apāṃ śaityaṃ rasaḥ kledo dravatvaṃ snehasaumyatā
|
jihvā visyandanaṃ cāpi bhaumānāṃ śrapaṇaṃ tathā ||4||

5agnerdurdharṣatā jyotistāpaḥ pākaḥ prakāśanam
|
śoko rāgo laghustaikṣṇyaṃ satataṃ cordhvabhāsitā ||5||

6vāyoraniyamasparśo vādasthānaṃ svatantratā
|
balaṃ śaighryaṃ ca mokṣaṃ ca karma ceṣṭā''tmatā bhavaḥ ||6||

7ākāśasya guṇaḥ śabdo vyāpitvaṃ cchidratāpi ca
|
anāśrayamanālambamavyaktamavikāritā ||7||

8apratīghātitā caiva bhūtatvaṃ vikṛtāni ca
|
guṇāḥ pañcāśataṃ proktāḥ pañcabhūtātmabhāvitāḥ ||8||

9dhairyopapattirvyaktiśca visargaḥ kalpanā kṣamā
|
sadasaccāśutā caiva manaso nava vai guṇāḥ ||9||

10iṣṭāniṣṭavipattiśca vyavasāyaḥ samādhitā
|
saṃśayaḥ pratipattiśca buddheḥ pañca guṇānviduḥ ||10||

11yudhiṣṭhira uvāca|
kathaṃ pañcaguṇā buddhiḥ kathaṃ pañcendriyā guṇāḥ
|
etanme sarvamācakṣva sūkṣmajñānaṃ pitāmaha ||11||

12bhīṣma uvāca|
āhuḥ ṣaṣṭiṃ buddhiguṇānvai bhūtaviśiṣṭā nityaviṣaktāḥ
|
bhūtavibhūtīścākṣarasṛṣṭāḥ putra na nityaṃ tadiha vadanti ||12||

13tatputra cintākalilaṃ taduktamanāgataṃ vai tava sampratīha
|
bhūtārthatattvaṃ tadavāpya sarvaṃ bhūtaprabhāvādbhava śāntabuddhiḥ ||13||
