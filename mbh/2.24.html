Mahābhārata 2.24 (Sabhāparvan)

1vaiśampāyana uvāca|
bhīmasenastataḥ kṛṣṇamuvāca yadunandanam
|
buddhimāsthāya vipulāṃ jarāsandhavadhopsayā||1||

2nāyaṃ pāpo mayā kṛṣṇa yuktaḥ syādanurodhitum
|
prāyeṇa yaduśārdūla baddhakakṣeṇa vāsasā||2||

3evamuktastataḥ kṛṣṇaḥ pratyuvāca vṛkodaram
|
tvarayanpuruṣavyāghro jarāsandhavadhepsayā||3||

4yatte daivaṃ paraṃ satvaṃ yacca te mātariśvanaḥ
|
balaṃ bhīma jarāsandhe darśayāśu tadadya vai||4||

5evamuktastadā bhīmo jarāsandhamarindamaḥ
|
utkṣipya bhrāmayāmāsa balavantaṃ mahābalaḥ||5||

6bhrāmayitvā śataguṇaṃ jānubhyāṃ bharatarṣabha
|
babhañja pṛṣṭhaṃ saṅkṣipya niṣpiṣya vinanāda ca||6||

7kare gṛhītvā caraṇaṃ dvedhā cakre mahābalaḥ||7||

8tasya niṣpiṣyamāṇasya pāṇḍavasya ca garjataḥ
|
abhavattumulo nādaḥ sarvaprāṇibhayaṅkaraḥ||8||

9vitresurmāgadhāḥ sarve strīṇāṃ garbhāśca susruvuḥ
|
bhīmasenasya nādena jarāsandhasya caiva ha||9||

10kiṃ nu syāddhimavānbhinnaḥ kiṃ nu sviddīryate mahī
|
iti vai māgadhā jajñurbhīmasenasya niḥsvanāt||10||

11tato rājñaḥ kuladvāri prasuptamiva taṃ nṛpam
|
rātrau gatāsumutsṛjya niścakramurarindamāḥ||11||

12jarāsandharathaṃ kṛṣṇo yojayitvā bāndhavān
|
āropya bhrātarau caiva mokṣayāmāsa bāndhavān||12||

13te vai ratnubhujaṃ kṛṣṇaṃ ratnārhāḥ pṛthivīśvarāḥ
|
rājānaścakrurāsādya mokṣitā mahato bhayāt||13||

14akṣataḥ śastrasaṃpanno jitāriḥ saha rājabhiḥ
|
rathamāsthāya taṃ divyaṃ nirjagāma girivrajāt||14||

15yaḥ sa sodaryavānnāma dviyodhī kṛṣṇasārathiḥ
|
abhyāsaghātī saṃdṛśyo durjayaḥ sarvarājabhiḥ||15||

16bhīmārjunābhyāṃ yodhābhyāmāsthitaḥ kṛṣṇasārathiḥ
|
śuśubhe rathavaryo'sau durjayaḥ sarvadhanvibhiḥ||16||

17śakraviṣṇū hi saṅgrāme ceratustārakāmaye
|
rathena tena vai kṛṣṇa upāruhya yayau tadā||17||

18taptacāmīkarābheṇa kiṅkiṇījālamālinā
|
meghanirghoṣanādena jaitreṇāmitraghātinā||18||

19yena śakro dānavānāṃ jaghāna navatīrnava
|
taṃ prāpya samahṛṣyanta rathaṃ te puruṣarthabhāḥ||19||

20tataḥ kṛṣṇaṃ mahābāhuṃ bhrātṛbhyāṃ sahitaṃ tadā
|
rathasthaṃ māgadhā dṛṣṭvā samapadyanta vismitāḥ||20||

21hayairdivyaiḥ samāyukto ratho vāyusamo jave
|
adhiṣṭhitaḥ sa śuśubhe kṛṣṇenātīva bhārata||21||

22asaṅgo devavihitastasminrathavare dhvajaḥ
|
yojanāddadṛśe śrīmānindrāyudhasamaprabhaḥ||22||

23cintayāmāsa kṛṣṇo'tha garutmantaṃ sa cābhyayāt
|
kṣaṇe tasminsa tenāsīccaityavṛkṣa ivotthitaḥ||23||

24vyāditāsyairmahānādaiḥ saha bhūtairdhvajālayaiḥ
|
tasminrathavare tasthau gurutmānpannagāśanaḥ||24||

25durnirīkṣyo hi bhūtānāṃ tejasā''bhyadhikaṃ babhau
|
āditya iva madhyāhne sahasrakiraṇāvṛtaḥ||25||

26na sa sajjati vṛkṣeṣu śastraiścāpi na riṣyate
|
divyo dhvajavaro rājandṛśyate ceha mānuṣaiḥ||26||

27tamāsthāya rathaṃ divyaṃ parjanyasamaniḥ svanam
|
niryayau puruṣavyāghraḥ pāṇḍavābhyāṃ sahācyutaḥ||27||

28yaṃ lebhe vāsavādrājā vasustasmādbṛhadrathaḥ
|
bṛhadrathātkrameṇaiva prāpto bārhadrathaṃ nṛpa||28||

29sa niryāya mahābāhuḥ puṇḍarīkekṣaṇastataḥ
|
girivrajādbahistasthau samadeśe mahāyaśāḥ||29||

30tatrainaṃ nāgarāḥ sarve satkāreṇābhyayustadā
|
brāhmaṇapramukhā rājanvidhidṛṣṭena karmaṇā||30||

31bandhanādvipramuktāśca rājāno madhusūdanam
|
pūjayāmāsurūcuśca stutipūrvamidaṃ vacaḥ||31||

32naitaccitraṃ mahābāho tvayi devakinandane
|
bhīmārjunabalopete dharmasya pratipālanam||32||

33jarāsandhahrade ghore duḥkhapaṅke nimajjatām
|
rājñāṃ samabhyuddharaṇaṃ yadidaṃ kṛtamadya vai||33||

34viṣṇo samavasannānāṃ giridurge sudāruṇe
|
diṣṭyā mokṣādyaśo dīptamāptaṃ te yadunandana||34||

35kiṃ karmaḥ puruṣavyāghra śādhi naḥ praṇatisthitān
|
kṛtamityeva tadviddhi nṛpairyadyapi duṣkaram||35||

36tānuvāca hṛṣīkeśaḥ samāśvāsya mahāmanāḥ
|
yudhiṣṭhiro rājasūyaṃ kratumārhatumicchati||36||

37tasya dharmapravṛttasya pārthivatvaṃ cikīrṣataḥ
|
sarvairbhavadbhirvijñāya sāhāyyaṃ kriyatāmiti||37||

38tataḥ suprītamanasaste nṛpā nṛpasattama
|
tathetyevābruvansarve pratigṛhyāsya tāṃ giram||38||

39ratnabhājaṃ ca dāśārhaṃ cakruste pṛthivīśvarāḥ
|
kṛcchrājjagrāha govindasteṣāṃ tadanukampayā||39||

40jarāsandhātmajaścaiva sahadevo mahāmanāḥ
|
niryayau sajanāmātyaḥ puraskṛtya purohitam||40||

41sa nīcaiḥ praṇato bhūtvā bahuratnapurogamaḥ
|
sahadevo nṛṇāṃ devaṃ vāsudevamupasthitaḥ||41||

42bhayārtāya tatastasmai kṛṣṇo dattvā'bhayaṃ tadā
|
ādadesya mahārhāṇi ratnāni puruṣottamaḥ||42||

43abhyarṣiñcata tatraiva jarāsandhātmajaṃ mudā
|
gatvaikatvaṃ ca kṛṣṇena pārthābhyāṃ caiva satkṛtaḥ||43||

44viveśa rājā dyutimānbārhadrathapuraṃ nṛpa
|
abhiṣikto mahābāhurjārāsandhirmahātmabhiḥ||44||

45kṛṣṇastu saha pārthābhyāṃ śriyā paramayā yutaḥ
|
ratnānyādāya bhūrīṇi prayayau puruṣarṣabhaḥ||45||

46indraprasthamupāgamya pāṇḍavābhyāṃ sahācyutaḥ
|
sametya dharmarājānaṃ prīyamāṇo'bhyabhāṣata||46||

47diṣṭyā bhīmena balavāñjarāsandho nipātitaḥ
|
rājāno mokṣitāścaiva bandhanānnṛpasattama||47||

48diṣṭyā kuśalinau cemau bhīmasenadhanañjayau
|
punaḥ svanagaraṃ prāptāvakṣatāviti bhārata||48||

49tato yudhiṣṭhiraḥ kṛṣṇaṃ pūjayitvā yathārhataḥ
|
bhīmasenārjunau caiva prahṛṣṭaḥ pariṣasvaje||49||

50tataḥ kṣīṇe jarāsandhe bhrātṛbhyāṃ vihitaṃ jayam
|
ajātaśatrurāsādya mumude bhrātṛbhiḥ saha||50||

51yathāvayaḥ samāgamya bhrātṛbhiḥ saha pāṇḍavaḥ
|
satkṛtya pūjayitvā ca visasarja narādhipān||51||

52yudhiṣṭhirābhyanujñātāste nṛpā hṛṣṭamānasāḥ
|
jagmuḥ svadeśāṃstvaritā yānairuccāvacaistataḥ||52||

53evaṃ puruṣaśārdūlo mahābuddhirjanārdanaḥ
|
pāṇḍavairghātayāmāsa jarāsandhamariṃ tadā||53||

54ghātayitvā jarāsandhaṃ buddhipūrvamarindamaḥ
|
dharmarājamanujñāpya pṛthāṃ kṛṣṇāṃ ca bhārata||54||

55subhadrāṃ bhīmasenaṃ ca phālgunaṃ yamajau tathā
|
dhaumyamāmantrayitvā ca prayayau svāṃ purīṃ prati||55||

56tenaiva rathamukhyena manasastulyagāminā
|
dharmarājavisṛṣṭena divyenānādayandiśaḥ||56||

57tato yudhiṣṭhiramukhāḥ pāṇḍavā bharatarṣabha
|
pradakṣiṇamakurvanta kṛṣṇamakliṣṭakāriṇam||57||

58tato gate bhagavati kṛṣṇe devakinandane
|
jayaṃ labdhvā suvipulaṃ rājñāṃ dattvā'bhayaṃ tadā||58||

59saṃvardhitaṃ yaśo bhūyaḥ karmaṇā tena bhārata
|
draupadyāḥ pāṇḍavā rājanparāṃ prītimavardhayan||59||

60tasminkāle tu yadyuktaṃ dharmakāmārthasaṃhitam
|
tadrājā dharmataścakre prajāpālanakīrtanam||60||
