Mahābhārata 3.32 (Āraṇyakaparvan)

1draupadyuvāca
|
nāvamanye na garhe ca dharmaṃ pārtha kathañcana
|
īśvaraṃ kuta evāhamavamaṃsye prajāpatim||1||

2ārtāhaṃ pralapāmīdamiti māṃ viddhi bhārata
|
bhūyaśca vilapiṣyāmi sumanāstvaṃ nibodha me||2||

3karma khalviha kartavyaṃ jānatā mitrakarśana
|
akarmāṇo hi jīvanti sthāvarā netare janāḥ||3||

4yāvadgostanapānācca yāvacchāyopasevanāt
|
jantavaḥ karmaṇā vṛttimāpnuvanti yudhiṣṭhira||4||

5jaṅgameṣu viśeṣeṇa manuṣyā bharatarṣabha
|
icchanti karmaṇā vṛttimavāptuṃ pretya ceha ca||5||

6utthānamabhijānanti sarvabhūtāni bhārata
|
pratyakṣaṃ phalamaśnanti karmaṇāṃ lokasākṣikam||6||

7sarve hi svaṃ samutthānamupajīvanti jantavaḥ
|
api dhātā vidhātā ca yathāyamudake bakaḥ||7||

8akarmaṇāṃ vai bhūtānāṃ vṛttiḥ syānna hi kācana
|
tadevābhiprapadyeta na vihanyātkadācana||8||

9sa karma kuru mā glāsīḥ karmaṇā bhava daṃśitaḥ
|
kṛtaṃ hi yo'bhijānāti sahasre so'sti nāsti ca||9||

10tasya cāpi bhavetkāryaṃ vivṛddhau rakṣaṇe tathā
|
bhakṣyamāṇo hyanādānātkṣīyeta himavānapi||10||

11utsīderanprajāḥ sarvā na kuryuḥ karma cedbhuvi
|
tathā hyetā na vardherankarma cedaphalaṃ bhavet||11||

12api cāpyaphalaṃ karma paśyāmaḥ kurvato janān
|
nānyathā hyapi gacchanti vṛttiṃ lokāḥ kathañcana||12||

13yaśca diṣṭaparo loke yaścāpi haṭhavādikaḥ
|
ubhāvapi śaṭhāvetau karmabuddhiḥ praśasyate||13||

14yo hi diṣṭamupāsīno nirviceṣṭaḥ sukhaṃ śayet
|
avasīdetsa durbuddhirāmo ghaṭa ivodake||14||

15tathaiva haṭhadurbuddhiḥ śaktaḥ karmaṇyakarmakṛt
|
āsīta na ciraṃ jīvedanātha iva durbalaḥ||15||

16akasmādiha yaḥ kaścidarthaṃ prāpnoti pūruṣaḥ
|
taṃ haṭheneti manyante sa hi yatno na kasyacit||16||

17yaccāpi kiñcitpuruṣo diṣṭaṃ nāma bhajatyuta
|
daivena vidhinā pārtha taddaivamiti niścitam||17||

18yatsvayaṃ karmaṇā kiñcitphalamāpnoti pūruṣaḥ
|
pratyakṣametallokeṣu tatpauruṣamiti śrutam||18||

19svabhāvataḥ pravṛtto yaḥ prāpnotyarthaṃ na kāraṇāt
|
tatsvabhāvātmakaṃ viddhi phalaṃ puruṣasattama||19||

20evaṃ haṭhācca daivācca svabhāvātkarmaṇastathā
|
yāni prāpnoti puruṣastatphalaṃ pūrvakarmaṇām||20||

21dhātāpi hi svakarmaiva taistairhetubhirīśvaraḥ
|
vidadhāti vibhajyeha phalaṃ pūrvakṛtaṃ nṛṇām||21||

22yadhdyāyaṃ puruṣaḥ kiñcitkurute vai śubhāśubham
|
taddhātṛvihitaṃ viddhi pūrvakarmaphalodayam||22||

23kāraṇaṃ tasya deho'yaṃ dhātuḥ karmaṇi vartate
|
sa yathā prerayatyenaṃ tathāyaṃ kurute vaśaḥ||23||

24teṣu teṣu hi kṛtyeṣu viniyoktā maheśvaraḥ
|
sarvabhūtāni kaunteya kārayatyavaśānyapi||24||

25manasārthānviniścitya paścātprāpnoti karmaṇā
|
buddhipūrvaṃ svayaṃ vīra puruṣastatra kāraṇam||25||

26saṅkhyātuṃ naiva śakyāni karmāṇi puruṣarṣabha
|
agāranagarāṇāṃ hi siddhiḥ puruṣahaitukī||26||

27tile tailaṃ gavi kṣīraṃ kāṣṭhe pāvakamantataḥ
|
dhiyā dhīro vijānīyādupāyaṃ cāsya siddhaye||27||

28tataḥ pravartate paścātkāraṇaistasya siddhaye
|
tāṃ siddhimupajīvanti karmajāmiha jantavaḥ||28||

29kuśalena kṛtaṃ karma kartrā sādhu svanuṣṭhitam
|
idaṃ tvakuśaleneti viśeṣādupalabhyate||29||

30iṣṭāpūrtaphalaṃ na syānna śiṣyo na gururbhavet
|
puruṣaḥ karmasādhyeṣu syāccedayamakāraṇam||30||

31kartṛtvādeva puruṣaḥ karmasiddhau praśasyate
|
asiddhau nindyate cāpi karmanāśātkathaṃ tviha||31||

32sarvameva haṭhenaike daivenaike vadantyuta
|
puṃsaḥ prayatnajaṃ kecittredhametannirucyate||32||

33na caivaitāvatā kāryaṃ manyanta iti cāpare
|
asti sarvamadṛśyaṃ tu diṣṭaṃ caiva tathā haṭhaḥ||33||

34dṛśyate hi haṭhāccaiva diṣṭāccārthasya santatiḥ
|
kiñciddaivāddhaṭhātkiñcitkiñcideva svabhāvataḥ||34||

35puruṣaḥ phalamāpnoti caturthaṃ nātra kāraṇam
|
kuśalāḥ pratijānanti ye vai tattvavido janāḥ||35||

36tathaiva dhātā bhūtānāmiṣṭāniṣṭaphalapradaḥ
|
yadi na syānna bhūtānāṃ kṛpaṇo nāma kaścana||36||

37yaṃ yamarthamabhiprepsuḥ kurute karma pūruṣaḥ
|
tattatsaphalameva syādyadi na syātpurā kṛtam||37||

38tridvārāmarthasiddhiṃ tu nānupaśyanti ye narāḥ
|
tathaivānarthasiddhiṃ ca yathaivātmā tathaiva te||38||

39kartavyameva karmeti manoreṣa viniścayaḥ
|
ekāntena hyanīho'yaṃ parābhavati pūruṣaḥ||39||

40kurvato hi bhavatyeva prāyeṇe'ha yudhiṣṭhira
|
ekāntaphalasiddhiṃ tu na vindatyalasaḥ kvacit||40||

41asambhave tvasya hetuḥ prāyaścittaṃ tu lakṣayet
|
kṛte karmaṇi rājendra tathānṛṇyamavāpnute||41||

42alakṣmīrāviśatyenaṃ śayānamalasaṃ naram
|
niḥsaṃśayaṃ phalaṃ labdhvā dakṣo bhūtimupāśnute||42||

43anarthāḥ saṃśayāvasthāḥ siddhyante muktasaṃśayāḥ
|
dhīrā narāḥ karmaratā nanu niḥsaṃśayāḥ kvacit||43||

44ekāntena hyanartho'yaṃ vartate'smāsu sāmpratam
|
satu niḥsaṃśayaṃ na syāttvayi karmaṇyavasthite||44||

45athavā'siddhireva syādabhimānaṃ tadeva te
|
vṛkodarasya bībhatsorbhrātrośca yamayorapi||45||

46anyeṣāṃ karmasaphalamasmākamapi vā punaḥ
|
viprakarṣeṇa budhyeta kṛtakarmā yathā phalam||46||

47pṛthivīṃ lāṅgaleneha bhittvā bījaṃ vapatyuta
|
āstetha karṣakastūṣṇīṃ parjanyastatra kāraṇam||47||

48vṛṣṭiścennānugṛhṇīyādanenāstatra karṣakaḥ
|
yadanyaḥ puruṣaḥ kuryāttatkṛtaṃ saphalaṃ mayā||48||

49taccedaṃ phalamasmākamaparādho na me kvacit
|
iti dhīro'nvavekṣyaiva nātmānaṃ tatra garhayet||49||

50kurvato nārthasiddhirme bhavatīti ha bhārata
|
nirvedo nātra kartavyo dvāvanyau hyatra kāraṇam ||50||

51siddhirvāpyathavā'siddhirapravṛttirato'nyathā
|
bahūnāṃ samavāye hi bhāvānāṃ karmasiddhayaḥ||51||

52guṇābhāve phalaṃ nyūnaṃ bhavatyaphalameva ca
|
anārambhe hi na phala na guṇo dṛśyate kvacit||52||

53deśakālāvupāyāṃśca maṅgalaṃ svastivṛddhaye
|
yunakti medhayā dhīro yathāśakti yathābalam||53||

54apramattena tatkāryamupadeṣṭā parākramaḥ
|
bhūyiṣṭhaṃ karmayogeṣu dṛśyameva parākramaḥ||54||

55yatra dhīmānavekṣeta śreyāṃsaṃ bahubhirguṇaiḥ
|
sāmnaivārthaṃ tato lipsetkarma cāsme prayojayet||55||

56vyasanaṃ vāsya kāṅkṣeta vivāsaṃ vā yudhiṣṭhira
|
api sindhorgirervāpi kiṃ punarmartyadharmiṇaḥ||56||

57utthānayuktaḥ satataṃ pareṣāmantaraiṣaṇe
|
ānṛṣyamāpnoti naraḥ parasyātmana eva ca||57||

58na tvevātmāvamantavyaḥ puruṣeṇa kadācana
|
na hyātmaparibhūtaraya bhūtirbhavati śobhanā||58||

59evaṃ saṃsthitikā siddhiriyaṃ lokasya bhārata
|
tatra siddhirgatiḥ proktā kālāvasthāvibhāgataḥ||59||

60brāhmaṇaṃ me pitā pūrvaṃ vāsayāmāsa paṇḍitam
|
so'pi sarvāmimāṃ prāha pitre me bharatarṣabha||60||

61nītiṃ bṛhaspatiproktāṃ bhrātṝnme'grāhayatpurā
|
teṣāṃ sakāśāda‌śrauṣamahametā tadā gṛhe||61||

62sa māṃ rājankarmavatīmāgatāmāha sāntvayan
|
śuśrūṣamāṇāmāsīnāṃ pituraṅke yudhiṣṭhira||62||
