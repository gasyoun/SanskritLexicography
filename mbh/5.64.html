Mahābhārata 5.64 (Udyogaparvan)

1vidura uvāca|
śakunīnāmihārthāya pāśaṃ bhūmāvayojayat
|
kaścicchākunikastāta pūrveṣāmiti śuśruma ||1||

2tasmiṃstau śakunau baddhau yugapatsahacāriṇau
|
tāvupādāya taṃ pāśaṃ jagmatuḥ khacarāvubhau ||2||

3tau vihāya samākrāntau dṛṣṭvā śākunikastadā
|
anvadhāvada‌nirviṇṇo yena yena sma gacchataḥ ||3||

4tathā tamanudhāvantaṃ mṛgayuṃ śakunārthinam
|
āśramastho muniḥ kaściddadarśātha kṛtāhnikaḥ ||4||

5tāvantarikṣagau śīghramanuyāntaṃ mahīcaram
|
ślokenānena kauravya papraccha sa munistadā ||5||

6vicitramidamāścaryaṃ mṛgahan pratibhāti me
|
plavamānau hi khacarau padātiranudhāvasi ||6||

7śākunika uvāca|
pāśamekamubhāvetau sahitau harato mama
|
yatra vai vivadiṣyete tatra me vaśameṣyataḥ ||7||

8vidura uvāca|
tau vivādamanuprāptau śakunau mṛtyusandhitau
|
vigṛhya ca sudurbuddhī pṛthivyāṃ sannipetatuḥ ||8||

9tau yudhyamānau saṃrabdhau mṛtyupāśavaśānugau
|
upasṛtyāparijñāto jagrāha mṛgahā tadā ||9||

10evaṃ ye jñātayo'rtheṣu mitho gacchanti vigraham
|
te'mitravaśamāyānti śakunāviva vigrahāt ||10||

11sambhojanaṃ saṅkathanaṃ saṃpraśno'tha samāgamaḥ
|
etāni jñātikāryāṇi na virodhaḥ kadācana ||11||

12ye sma kāle sumanasaḥ sarve vṛddhānupāsate
|
siṃhaguptamivāraṇyamapradhṛṣyā bhavanti te ||12||

13ye'rthaṃ santatamāsādya dīnā iva samāsate
|
śriyaṃ te saṃprayacchanti dviṣadbhyo bharatarṣabha ||13||

14dhūmāyante vyapetāni jvalanti sahitāni ca
|
dhṛtarāṣṭrolmukānīva jñātayo bharatarṣabha ||14||

15idamanyat pravakṣyāmi yathā dṛṣṭaṃ girau mayā
|
śrutvā tadapi kauravya yathā śreyastathā kuru ||15||

16vayaṃ kirātaiḥ sahitā gacchāmo girimuttaram
|
brāhmaṇairdevakalpaiśca vidyājambhakavārtikaiḥ ||16||

17kuñjabhūtaṃ giriṃ sarvamabhito gandhamādanam
|
dīpyamānauṣadhigaṇaṃ siddhagandharvasevitam ||17||

18tatrāpaśyāma vai sarve madhupītakamākṣikam
|
maruprapāte viṣame niviṣṭaṃ kumbhasammitam ||18||

19āśīviṣai rakṣyamāṇaṃ kuberadayitaṃ bhṛśam
|
yatprāpya puruṣo matyartho'pyamaratvaṃ niyacchati ||19||

20acakṣurlabhate cakṣurvṛddho bhavati vai yuvā
|
iti te kathayanti sma brāhmaṇā jambhasādhakāḥ ||20||

21tataḥ kirātāstaddṛṣṭvā prārthayanto mahīpate
|
vineśurviṣame tasmin sasarpe girigahvare ||21||

22tathaiva tava putro'yaṃ pṛthivīmeka icchati
|
madhu paśyati sammohāt prapātaṃ nānupaśyati ||22||

23duryodhano yoddhamanāḥ samare savyasācinā
|
na ca paśyāmi tejo'sya vikramaṃ vā tathāvidham ||23||

24ekena rathamāsthāya pṛthivī yena nirjitā
|
bhīṣmadroṇaprabhṛtayaḥ santrastāḥ sādhuyāyinaḥ ||24||

25virāṭanagare bhagnāḥ kiṃ tatra tava dṛśyatām
|
pratīkṣamāṇo yo vīraḥ kṣamate vīkṣitaṃ tava ||25||

26drupadro matsyarājaśca saṃkruddhaśca dhanañjayaḥ
|
na śeṣayeyuḥ samare vāyuyuktā ivāgnayaḥ ||26||

27aṅke kuruṣva rājānaṃ dhṛtarāṣṭra yudhiṣṭhiram
|
yudhyatorhi dvayoryuddhe naikāntena bhavejjayaḥ ||27||
