Mahābhārata 7.18 (Droṇaparvan)

1sañjaya uvāca|
tataḥ saṃśaptakā rājan same deśe vyavasthitāḥ
|
vyūhyānīkaṃ rathaireva candrākāraṃ mudā yutāḥ||1||

2te kirīṭinamāyāntaṃ dṛṣṭvā harṣeṇa māriṣa
|
udakrośannaravyāghrāḥ śabdena mahatā tadā||2||

3sa śabdaḥ pradiśaḥ sarvā diśaḥ khaṃ ca samāvṛṇot
|
āvṛtatvācca lokasya nāsīt tatra pratisvanaḥ||3||

4so'tīva saṃprahṛṣṭāṃstānupalabhya dhanañjayaḥ
|
kiṃcidabhyutsmayan kṛṣṇamidaṃ vacanamabravīt||4||

5paśyaitān devakīmātarmumūrṣūnadya saṃyuge
|
bhrātṝṃstraigartakānevaṃ roditavye praharṣitān||5||

6athavā harṣakālo'yaṃ traigartānāmasaṃśayam
|
kunarairduravāpān hi lokān prāpsyantyanuttamān||6||

7evamuktvā mahābāhuhṛṣīkeśaṃ tato'rjunaḥ
|
āsasāda raṇe vyūḍhāṃ trigartānāmanīkinīm||7||

8sa devadattamādāya śaṃkhaṃ hemapariṣkṛtam
|
dadhmau vegena mahatā ghoṣaṇāpūrayan diśaḥ||8||

9tena śabdena vitrastā saṃśaptakavarūthinī
|
viceṣṭā'vasthitā saṃkhye hyaśmasāramayī yathā||9||

10vāhāsteṣāṃ vivṛttākṣāḥ stabdhakarṇaśirodharāḥ
|
viṣṭabdhacaraṇā mūtraṃ rudhiraṃ ca prasusruvuḥ||10||

11upalabhya tataḥ saṃjñāmavasthāpya ca vāhinīm
|
yugapat pāṇḍuputrāya cikṣipuḥ kaṅkapatriṇaḥ||11||

12tānyarjunaḥ sahasrāṇi daśapañcabhirāśugaiḥ
|
anāgatānyeva śaraiścicchedāśu parākramī||12||

13tato'rjunaṃ śitairbāṇairdaśabhirdaśabhiḥ punaḥ
|
prāvidhyanta tataḥpārthastānavidhyastribhistribhiḥ||13||

14ekaikastu tataḥ pārthaṃ rājan vivyādha pañcabhiḥ
|
sa ca tān prativivyādha dvābhyāṃ dvābhyāṃ parākramī||14||

15bhūya eva tu saṃkruddhāstvarjunaṃ saha keśavam
|
āpūrayan śaraistīkṣṇaistaḍāgamiva vṛṣṭibhiḥ||15||

16tataḥ śarasahasrāṇi prāpatannarjunaṃ prati
|
bhramarāṇāmiva vrātāḥ phullaṃ drumagaṇaṃ vane||16||

17tataḥ subāhustriṃśadbhiradrisāramayaiḥ śaraiḥ
|
avidhyadiṣubhirgāḍhaṃ kirīṭe savyasācinam||17||

18taiḥ kirīṭī kirīṭasthairhemapuṅkhairajihmagaiḥ
|
śātakuṃbhamayāpīḍo babhau sūrya ivotthitaḥ||18||

19hastāvāpaṃ subāhostu bhallena yudhi pāṇḍavaḥ
|
ciccheda taṃ caiva punaḥ śaravarṣairavākirat||19||

20tataḥ suśarmā daśabhiḥ surathastu kirīṭinam
|
sudharmā sudhanuścaiva subāhuśca samārpayat||20||

21tāṃstu sarvān pṛthagbāṇairvānarapravaradhvajaḥ
|
pratyavidhyaddhvajāṃścaiṣāṃ bhallaiściccheda sāyakān||21||

22sudhanvano dhanuśchittvā hayāṃścāsyāvadhīccharaiḥ
|
athāsya saśirastrāṇaṃ śiraḥ kāyādapātayat||22||

23tasminnipatite vīre trastāstasya padānugāḥ
|
vyadravanta bhayādbhītā yatra dauryodhanaṃ balam||23||

24tato jaghāna saṃkruddho vāsavistāṃ mahācamūm
|
śarajālairavicchinnaistamaḥ sūrya ivāṃśubhiḥ||24||

25tato bhagne bale tasmin vipralīne samaṃtataḥ
|
savyasācini saṃkruddhe traigartān bhayamāviśat||25||

26te vadhyamānāḥ pārthena śaraiḥ sannataparvabhiḥ
|
amuhyāstatra tatraiva trastā mṛgagaṇā iva||26||

27tatastrigartarāṭ kruddhastānuvāca mahārathān
|
alaṃ drutena vaḥ śūrānabhayaṃ kartumarhatha||27||

28śaptvātha śapathān ghorānsarvasainyasya paśyataḥ
|
gatvā dauryodhanaṃ sainyaṃ kiṃ vai vakṣyatha mukhyaśaḥ||28||

29nāvahāsyāḥ kathaṃ loke karmaṇānena saṃyuge
|
bhavema sahitāḥ sarve nivartadhvaṃ yathābalam||29||

30evamuktāstu te rājannudakrośanmuhurmuhuḥ
|
śaṃkhāṃśca dadhmire vīrā harṣayantaḥ parasparam||30||

31tataste saṃnyavartanta saṃśaptakagaṇāḥ punaḥ
|
nārāyaṇāśca gopālā mṛtyuṃ kṛtvā nivartanam||31||
