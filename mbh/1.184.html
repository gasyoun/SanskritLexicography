Mahābhārata 1.184 (Ādiparvan)

1vaiśaṃpāyana uvāca|
tataste naraśārdūlā bhrātaraḥ pañca pāṇḍavāḥ
|
prayayurdraupadīṃ draṣṭuṃ taṃ ca deśaṃ mahotsavam||1||

2te prayātā naravyāghrāḥ saha mātrā paraṃtapāḥ
|
brāhmaṇāndadṛśurmārge gacchataḥ saṃgatānbahūn||2||

3ta ūcurbrāhmaṇā rajanpāṇḍavānbrahmacāriṇaḥ
|
bhavanto gamiṣyanti kuto vā'bhyāgatā iha||3||

4yudhiṣṭhira uvāca|
āgatānekacakrāyāḥ sodaryānekacāriṇaḥ
|
bhavanto vai vijānantu saha mātrā dvijarṣabhāḥ|| 4||

5brāhmaṇā ūcuḥ|
gacchatādyaiva pañcālāndrupadasya niveśane
|
svayaṃvaro mahāṃstatra bhāvitā sumahādhanaḥ||5||

6ekasārthaprayātāḥ sma vayaṃ tatraiva gāminaḥ
|
tatra hyadbhutasaṃkāśī bhāvitā sumahotsavaḥ||6||

7yajñasenasya duhitā drupadasya mahātmanaḥ
|
vedīmadhyātsamutpannā pañcapatrānibhekṣaṇā ||7||

8darśanīyānavadyāṅgī sukumārī manasvinī
|
dhṛṣṭadyumnasya bhaginī droṇaśatroḥ pratāpinaḥ||8||

9yo jātaḥ kavacī khaḍgī saśaraḥ saśarāsanaḥ
|
susamiddhe mahābāhuḥ pāvake pāvakopamaḥ||9||

10svasā tasyānavadyāṅgī draupadī tanumadhyamā
|
nīlotpalasamo gandho yasyāḥ krośātpravāti vai||10||

11yajñasenasya ca sutāṃ svayaṃvarakṛtakṣaṇām
|
gacchāmo vai vayaṃ draṣṭuṃ taṃ ca divyaṃ mahotsavam||11||

12rājāno rājaputrāśca yajvāno bhūridākṣiṇāḥ
|
svādhyāyavantaḥ śucayo mahātmāno yatavratāḥ ||12||

13taruṇā daśenīyāśca nānādeśasamāgatāḥ
|
mahārathā kṛtāstrāśca samupeṣyanti bhūmipāḥ||13||

14te tatra vividhāndāyānvijayārthaṃ nareśvarāḥ
|
pradāsyanti ghanaṃ gāśca bhakṣyaṃ bhojyaṃ ca sarveśaḥ||14||

15pratigṛhya ca tatsarvaṃ dṛṣṭvā caiva svayaṃvaram‌
|
anubhūyotsavaṃ caiva gamiṣyāmo yathepsitam||15||

16narā vaitāchikāstatra nartakāḥ sūtamāgadhāḥ
|
niyoghakāśca deśebhyaḥ sameṣyanti mahābalāḥ ||16||

17evaṃ kautūhalaṃ kṛtvā dṛṣṭvā ca pratigrahya ca
|
sahāsmābhirmahātmānaḥ punaḥ pratinivartsyatha ||17||

18darśanīyāṃśca vaḥ savondevarūpānavasthitān‌
|
samīkṣya kṛṣṇā varayetsaṃgatyaikatamaṃ varam‌ ||18||

19ayaṃ bhrātā tava śrīmāndarśanīyo mahāsujaḥ
|
niyujyamāno vijaye saṃgatyā draviṇaṃ bahu
|
āhariṣyannayaṃ nūnaṃ prītiṃ vo vardhayiṣyāti||19||

20yudhiṣṭhira uvāca| paramaṃ bho gamiṣyāmo draṣṭaṃ caiva mahotsavam‌
|
bhavādbhiḥ sahitāḥ sarve kanyāyāstaṃ svayaṃvaram ||20||
