Mahābhārata 12.229 (Śāntiparvan)

1yudhiṣṭhira uvāca|
kiṃśīlaḥ kiṃsamācāraḥ kiṃvidyaḥ kiṃparākramaḥ
|
prāpnoti brahmaṇaḥ sthānaṃ yatparaṃ prakṛterdhruvam ||1||

2bhīṣma uvāca|
mokṣadharmeṣu niyato laghvāhāro jitendriyaḥ
|
prāpnoti brahmaṇaḥ sthānaṃ tatparaṃ prakṛterdhruvam ||2||

3atrāpyudāharantīmamitihāsaṃ purātanam
|
jaigīṣavyasya saṃvādamasitasya ca bhārata ||3||

4jaigīṣavyaṃ mahāprajñaṃ dharmāṇāmāgatāgamam
|
akrudhyantamahṛṣyantamasito devalo'bravīt ||4||

5devala uvāca |
na prīyase vaṃdyamāno nindyamāno na kupyase
|
kā te prajñā kutaścaiṣā kiṃ te tasyāḥ parāyaṇam ||5||

6bhīṣma uvāca|
iti tenānuyuktaḥ sa tamuvāca mahātapāḥ
|
mahadvākyamasaṃdigdhaṃ puṣkalārthapadaṃ śuci ||6||

7jaigīṣavya uvāca |
yā gatiryā parā kāṣṭhā yā śāntiḥ puṇyakarmaṇām
|
tāṃ te'haṃ sampravakṣyāmi mahatīmṛṣisattama ||7||

8nindatsu ca samā nityaṃ praśaṃsatsu ca devala
|
nihnavanti ca ye teṣāṃ samayaṃ sukṛtaṃ ca yat ||8||

9uktāśca na vadiṣyanti vaktāramahite hitam
|
pratihantuṃ na cecchanti hantāraṃ vai manīṣiṇaḥ ||9||

10nāprāptamanuśocanti prāptakālāni kurvate
|
na cātītāni śocanti na caiva pratijānate ||10||

11samprāptānāṃ ca pūjyānāṃ kāmādartheṣu devala
|
yathopapattiṃ kurvanti śaktimantaḥ kṛtavratāḥ ||11||

12pakvavidyā mahāprājñā jitakrodhā jitendriyāḥ
|
manasā karmaṇā vācā nāparādhyanti karhicit ||12||

13anīrṣavo na cānyonyaṃ vihiṃsanti kadācana
|
na ca jātūpatapyante dhīrāḥ parasamṛddhibhiḥ ||13||

14nindāpraśaṃse cātyarthaṃ na vadanti parasya ye
|
na ca nindāpraśaṃsābhyāṃ vikriyante kadācana ||14||

15sarvataśca praśāntā ye sarvabhūtahite ratāḥ
|
na kruddhyanti na dṛṣyanti nāparādhyanti karhicit ||15||

16vimucya hṛdayagranthiṃ caṃkramanti yathāsukham
|
na yeṣāṃ bāndhavāḥ santi ye cānyeṣāṃ na bāndhavāḥ ||16||

17amitrāśca na santyeṣāṃ ye cāmitrā na kasyacit
|
ya evaṃ kurvate mārtyāḥ sukhaṃ jīvanti sarvadā ||17||

18ye dharmaṃ cānuruddhyante dharmajñā dvijasattama
|
ye hyato vicyutā mārgātte hṛṣyantyudvijanti ca ||18||

19āsthitastamahaṃ mārgamasūyiṣyāmi kaṃ katham
|
nindyamānaḥ praśasto vā hṛṣye'haṃ kena hetunā ||19||

20yadyadicchanti tattasmādapi gacchantu mānavāḥ
|
na me nindāpraśaṃsābhyāṃ hrāsavṛddhī bhaviṣyataḥ ||20||

21amṛtasyeva saṃtṛpyedavamānasya tattvavit
|
viṣasyevodvijennityaṃ sammānasya vicakṣaṇaḥ ||21||

22avajñātaḥ sukhaṃ śete iha cāmutra cābhayam
|
vimuktaḥ sarvadoṣebhyo yo'vamantā sa badhyate ||22||

23parāṃ gatiṃ ca ye kecitprārthayanti manīṣiṇaḥ
|
etadvrataṃ samāhṛtya sukhamedhanti te janāḥ ||23||

24sarvataśca samāhṛtya kratūn sarvān jitendriyaḥ
|
prāpnoti brahmaṇaḥ sthānaṃ yatparaṃ prakṛterdhruvam ||24||

25nāsya devā na gandharvā na piśācā na rākṣasāḥ
|
padamanvavarohanti prāptasya paramāṃ gatim ||25||
