Mahābhārata 3.2 (Āraṇyakaparvan)

1vaiśampāyana uvāca|
prabhātāyāṃ tu śarvaryāṃ teṣāmakliṣṭakarmaṇām
|
vanaṃ yiyāsatāṃ viprāstasthurbhikṣābhujo'grataḥ||1||

2tānuvāca tato rājā kuntīputro yudhiṣṭhiraḥ
|
vayaṃ hi hṛtasarvasvā hṛtarājyā hṛtaśriyaḥ||2||

3phalamūlāmiṣāhārā vanaṃ gacchāma duḥkhitāḥ
|
vanaṃ ca doṣahulaṃ bahuvyālasarīsṛpam||3||

4parikleśaśca vo manye dhruvaṃ tatra bhaviṣyati
|
brāhmaṇānāṃ parikleśo daivatānyapi sādayet
|
kiṃ punarmāmito viprā nivartadhvaṃ yatheṣṭataḥ||4||

5brāhmaṇā ūcuḥ| gatiryā bhavatāṃ rājaṃstāṃ vayaṃ gantumudyatāḥ
|
nārhasyasmānparityaktuṃ bhaktānsaddharmadarśinaḥ||5||

6anukampāṃ hi bhakteṣu devatā hyapi kurvate
|
viśeṣato brāhmaṇeṣu sadācarāvalambiṣu||6||

7yudhiṣṭhira uvāca|
mamāpi paramā bhaktirbrāhmaṇeṣu sadā dvijāḥ
|
sahāyaviparibhraṃśastvayaṃ sādayatīva mām||7||

8āhareyurime ye'pi phalamūlamṛgāṃstathā
|
ta ime śokajairduḥkhairbhrātaro me vimohitāḥ||8||

9draupadyā viprakarṣeṇa rājyāpaharaṇena ca
|
duḥkhārditānimānkleśairnāhaṃ yoktumihotsahe||9||

10brāhmaṇā ūcuḥ| asmatpoṣaṇajā cintā mābhūtte hṛdi pārthiva
|
svayamāhṛtya cānnāni tvānuyāsyāmahe vayam||10||

11anudhyānena japyena vidhāsyāmaḥ śivaṃ tava
|
kathābhiścābhiramyābhiḥ saha raṃsyāmahe vayam||11||

12yudhiṣṭhira uvāca|
evametanna sandeho rame'haṃ satataṃ dvijaiḥ
|
nyūnabhāvāttu paśyāmi pratyādeśamivātmanaḥ||12||

13kathaṃ drakṣyāmi vaḥ sarvānvayamāhṛtya bhojinaḥ
|
madbhaktyā kliśyato'narhāndhikpāpāndhṛtarāṣṭrajān||13||

14vaiśampāyana uvāca|
ityuktvā sa nṛpaḥ śocanniṣasāda mahītale
|
tamadhyātmarato vidvāñcchaunako nāma vai dvijaḥ||14||

15yoge sāṅkhye ca kuśalo rājānamidamabravīt||15||

16śokasthānasahasrāṇi bhayasthānaśatāni ca
|
divase divase mūḍhamāviśanti na paṇḍitam||16||

17na hi jñānaviruddheṣu bahudoṣeṣu karmasu
|
śreyoghātiṣu sajjante buddhimanto bhavadvidhāḥ||17||

18aṣṭāṅgāṃ buddhimāhuryāṃ sarvā śreyo'bhighātinīm
|
śrutismṛtisamāyuktāṃ rājansā tvayyavasthitā||18||

19arthakṛcchreṣu durgeṣu vyāpatsu svajanasya ca
|
śārīramānasairduḥkhairna sīdanti bhavadvidhāḥ||19||

20śrūyatāṃ ca cābhidhāsyāmi janakena yathā purā
|
ātmavyavasthānakarā gītāḥ ślokā mahātmanā||20||

21manodehasamutthābhyāṃ duḥkhābhyāmarditaṃ jagat
|
tayorvyāsasamāsābhyāṃ śamopāyamimaṃ śṛṇu||21||

22vyādheraniṣṭasaṃsparśācchramādiṣṭavivarjanāt | duḥkhaṃ caturbhiḥ śārīraṃ kāraṇaiḥ sampravartate||22||

23tadā tatpratikārācca satataṃ cāvicintanāt
|
ādhivyādhipraśamanaṃ kriyāyogadvayena tu||23||

24matimanto hyato vaidyāḥ śamaṃ prāgeva kurvate
|
mānasasya priyākhyānaiḥ sambhogopanayairnṛṇām||24||

25mānasena hi duḥkhena śarīramupatapyate
|
ayaḥpiṇḍena taptena kumbhasaṃsthamivodakam||25||

26mānasaṃ śamayettasmājjñānenāgnimivāmbunā
|
praśānte mānase hyasya śārīramupaśāmyati||26||

27manaso duḥkhamūlaṃ tu sneha ityupalabhyate
|
snehāttu sajjate janturduḥkhayogamupaiti ca||27||

28snehamūlāni duḥkhāni snehajāni bhayāni ca
|
śokaharṣau tathāyāsaḥ sarvaṃ snehātpravartate||28||

29snehādbhāvo'nurāgaśca prajajñe viṣaye tathā
|
aśreyaskāvubhāvetau pūrvastatra guruḥ smṛtaḥ||29||

30koṭarāgniryathā'śeṣaṃ samūlaṃ pādapaṃ dahet
|
dharmārthau tu tathālpo'pi rāgadoṣo vināśayet||30||

31viprayogana tu tyāgī doṣadarśī samāgame
|
virāgaṃ bhajate janturnirvairo niravagrahaḥ||31||

32tasmātsnehaṃ na lipseta mitrebhyo dhanasañcayāt
|
svaśarīrasamutthaṃ ca jñānena vinivartayet||32||

33jñānānviteṣu yukteṣu śāstrajñeṣu kṛtātmasu
|
na teṣu sajjate snehaḥ padmapatreṣvivodakam||33||

34rāgābhibhūtaḥ puruṣaḥ kāmena parikṛṣyate
|
icchā sañjāyate tasya tatastṛṣṇā vivardhate||34||

35tṛṣṇā hi sarvapāpiṣṭhā nityodvegakarī smṛtā
|
adharmabahulā caiva ghorā pāpānubandhinī||35||

36yā dustyajā durmatibhiryā na jīryati jīryataḥ
|
yo'sau prāṇāntiko rogastāṃ tṛṣṇāṃ tyajataḥ sukham||36||

37anādyantā tu sā tṛṣṇā antardehagatā nṛṇām
|
vināśayati bhūtāni ayonija ivānalaḥ||37||

38yathaidhaḥ svasamutthena vahninā nāśamṛcchati
|
tathā'kṛtātmā lobhena sahajena vinaśyati||38||

39rājataḥ salilādagneścorataḥ svajanādapi
|
bhayamarthavatāṃ nityaṃ mṛtyoḥ prāṇabhṛtāmiva||39||

40tathā hyāmiṣamākāśe pakṣibhiḥ śvāpadairbhuvi
|
bhakṣyate salile matsyaistathā sarvatra vittavān||40||

41artha eva hi keṣāñcidanarthaṃ bhajate nṛṇām
|
arthaśreyasi cāsakto na śreyo vindate naraḥ||41||

42tasmādarthāgamāḥ sarve mano mohavivardhanāḥ
|
kārpaṇyaṃ darpamānau ca bhayamudvega eva ca||42||

43arthajāni viduḥ prājñā duḥkhānyetāni dehinām
|
arthasyotpādane caiva pālane ca tathā kṣaye||43||

44sahanti ca mahadduḥkhaṃ ghnanti caivārthakāraṇāt
|
arthā duḥkhaṃ parityaktuṃ pālitāścaiva śatravaḥ||44||

45duḥkhena cādhigamyante tasmānnāśaṃ na cintayet
|
asantoṣaparā mūḍhāḥ santoṣaṃ yānti paṇḍitāḥ||45||

46anto nāsti pipāsāyāḥ santoṣaḥ paramaṃ sukham
|
tasmātsantoṣameveha paraṃ paśyanti paṇḍitāḥ||46||

47anityaṃ yauvanaṃ rūpaṃ jīvitaṃ ratnasañcayaḥ
|
aiśvaryaṃ priyasaṃvāso gṛdhyettatra na paṇḍitaḥ||47||

48tyajeta sañcayāṃstasmāttajjānkleśānsaheta ca
|
na hi sañcayavānkaściddṛśyate nirupadravaḥ
|
ataśca dhārmikaiḥ pumbhiranīhārthaḥ praśasyate||48||

49dharmārthaṃ yasya vittehā varaṃ tasya nirīhatā
|
prakṣālanāddhi paṅkasya śreyo na sparśanaṃ nṛṇām||49||

50yudhiṣṭhiraivaṃ sarveṣu na spṛhāṃ kartumarhasi
|
dharmeṇa yadi te kāryaṃ vimukteccho bhavārthataḥ||50||

51yudhiṣṭhira uvāca|
nārthopabhogalipsārthamiyamarthepsutā mama
|
bharaṇārthaṃ tu viprāṇāṃ brahmankāṅkṣe na lobhataḥ||51||

52kathaṃ tvasmadvidho brahmanvartamāno gṛhāśrame
|
bharaṇaṃ pālanaṃ cāpi na kuryādanuyāyinām||52||

53saṃvibhāgo hi bhūtānāṃ sarveṣāmeva dṛśyate
|
tathaivāpacamānebhyaḥ pradeyaṃ gṛhamedhinā||53||

54tṛṇāni bhūmirudakaṃ vākcaturthī ca sūnṛtā
|
satāmetāni geheṣu nocchidyante kadācana||54||

55deyamārtasya śayanaṃ sthitaśrāntasya cāsanam
|
tṛṣitasya ca pānīyaṃ kṣudhitasya ca bhojanam||55||

56cakṣurdadyānmano dadyādvācaṃ dadyātsubhāṣitām
|
utthāya cāsanaṃ dadyādeṣa dharmaḥ sanātanaḥ
|
pratyutthāyābhigamanaṃ kuryānnyāyena cārcanam||56||

57agnihotramanaḍvāṃśca jñātayo'tithibāndhavāḥ
|
putrā dārāśca bhṛtyāśca nirdaheyurapūjitāḥ||57||

58ātmārthaṃ pācayennānnaṃ na vṛthā ghātayetpaśūn
|
na ca tatsvayamaśnīyādvidhivadyanna nirvapet||58||

59śvabhyaśca śvapacebhyaśca vayobhyaścāvapedbhuvi
|
vaiśvadevaṃ hi nāmaitatsāyaṃ prātaśca dīyate||59||

60vighasāśī bhavettasmānnityaṃ cāmṛtabhojanaḥ
|
vighaso bhuktaśeṣaṃ tu yajñaśeṣaṃ tathā'mṛtam||60||

61cakṣurdadyānmano dadyādvācaṃ dadyācca sūnṛtām
|
anuvrajedupāsīta sa yajñaḥ pañcadakṣiṇaḥ||61||

62yo dadyādaparikliṣṭamannamadhvani vartate
|
śrāntāyādṛṣṭapūrvāya tasya puṇyaphalaṃ mahat||62||

63evaṃ yo vartate vṛttiṃ vartamāno gṛhāśrame
|
tasya dharmaṃ paraṃ prāhuḥ kathaṃ vā vipra manyase||63||

64śaunaka uvāca|
aho bata mahatkaṣṭaṃ viparītamidaṃ jagat
|
yenāpatrapate sādhurasādhustena tuṣyati||64||

65śiśnodarakṛte'prājñaḥ karoti vighasaṃ bahu
|
moharāgavaśākrānta indriyārthavaśānugaḥ||65||

66hriyate budhyamāno'pi naro hāribhirindriyaiḥ
|
vimūḍhasañjño duṣṭāśvairudbhrāntairiva sārathiḥ||66||

67ṣaḍindriyāṇi viṣayaṃ samāgacchanti vai yadā
|
tadā prādurbhavatyeṣāṃ pūrvasaṅkalpajaṃ manaḥ||67||

68mano yasyendriyasyeha viṣayānyāti sevitum
|
tasyautsukyaṃ sambhavati pravṛttiścopajāyate||68||

69tataḥ saṅkalpabījena kāmena viṣayeṣubhiḥ
|
viddhaḥ patati lobhāgnau jyotirlobhātpataṅgavat||69||

70tato vihārairāhārairmohitaśca yathepsayā
|
mahāmohe sukhe magno nātmānamavabudhyate||70||

71evaṃ patati saṃsāre tāsu tāsvihayoniṣu | avidyākarmatṛṣṇābhirbhrāmyamāṇo'tha cakravat||71||

72brahmādiṣu tṛṇānteṣu bhūteṣu parivartate
|
jale bhuvi tathākāśe jāyamānaḥ punaḥ punaḥ||72||

73abudhānāṃ gatistveṣā budhānāmapi me śṛṇu
|
ye dharme śreyasi ratā vimokṣaratayo janāḥ||73||

74tadidaṃ vedavacanaṃ kuru karma tyajeti ca
|
tasmāddharmānimānsarvānnābhimānātsamācaret||74||

75ijyādhyayanadānāni tapaḥ satyaṃ kṣamā damaḥ
|
alobha iti mārgo'yaṃ dharmasyāṣṭavidhaḥ smṛtaḥ||75||

76atra pūrvaścaturvargaḥ pitṛyāṇapathe sthitaḥ
|
kartavyamiti yatkāryaṃ nābhimānātsamācaret||76||

77uttaro devayānastu sadbhirācaritaḥ sadā
|
aṣṭāṅgenaiva mārgeṇa viśuddhātmā samācaret||77||

78samyaksaṅkalpasambandhātsamyakcendriyanigrahāt
|
samyagvrataviśeṣācca samyakca gurusevanāt||78||

79samyagāhārayogācca samyakcādhyayanāgamāt
|
samyakkarmopasaṃnyāsātsamyakcittanirodhanāt||79||

80evaṃ karmāṇi kurvanti saṃsāravijigīṣavaḥ
|
rāgadveṣavinirmuktā aiśvaryaṃ devatā gatāḥ||80||

81rudrāḥ sādhyāstathādityā vasavo'tha tathāśvinau
|
yogaiśvaryeṇa saṃyuktā dhārayanti prajā imāḥ||81||

82tathā tvamapi kaunteya śamamāsthāya puṣkalam
|
tapasā siddhimanviccha yogasiddhiṃ ca bhārata||82||

83pitṛmātramayī siddhiḥ prāptā karmamayī ca te
|
tapasā siddhimanviccha dvijānāṃ bharaṇāya vai||83||

84siddhā hi yadyadicchanti kurvate tadanugrahāt
|
tasmāttapaḥ samāsthāya kuruṣvātmamanoratham||84||
