Mahābhārata 6.112 (Bhīṣmaparvan)

1sañjaya uvāca|
atha vīro maheṣvāso mattavāraṇavikramaḥ
|
samādāya mahaccāpaṃ mattavāraṇavāraṇam ||1||

2vidhunvāno naraśreṣṭho drāvayāṇo varūthinīm
|
pṛtanāṃ pāṇḍaveyānāṃ gāhamāno mahābalaḥ ||2||

3nimittāni nimittajñaḥ sarvato vīkṣya vīryavān
|
pratapantamanīkāni droṇaḥ putramabhāṣata ||3||

4ayaṃ hi divasastāta yatra pārtho mahābalaḥ
|
jighāṃsuḥ samare bhīṣmaṃ paraṃ yatnaṃ kariṣyati ||4||

5utpatanti hi me bāṇā dhanuḥ prasphuratīva ca
|
yogamastrāṇi gacchānta krūre me vartate matiḥ ||5||

6dikṣvaśāntāni ghorāṇi vyāharanti mṛgadvijāḥ
|
nīcairgṛdhrā nilīyante bhāratānāṃ camūṃ prati ||6||

7naṣṭaprabha ivādityaḥ sarvato lohitā diśaḥ
|
rasate vyathate bhūmiḥ kampatīva ca sarvaśaḥ ||7||

8kaṅkagṛdhrā balākāśca vyāharanti muhurmuhuḥ
|
śivāścaivāśivā ghorā vedayantyo mahadbhayam ||8||

9papāta mahatī colkā madhyenādityamaṇḍalāt
|
sakabandhaśca parigho bhānumāvṛtya tiṣṭhati ||9||

10pariveṣastathā ghoraścandrabhāskarayorabhūt
|
vedayāno bhayaṃ ghoraṃ rājñāṃ dehāvakartanam ||10||

11devatāyatanasthāśca kauravendrasya devatāḥ
|
kampante ca hasante ca nṛtyanti ca rudanti ca ||11||

12apasavyaṃ grahāścakruralakṣmāṇaṃ divākaram
|
āvākśirāśca bhagavānupātiṣṭhata candramāḥ ||12||

13vapūṃṣi ca narendrāṇāṃ vigatābhāni lakṣaye
|
dhārtarāṣṭrasya sainyeṣu na ca bhrājanti daṃśitāḥ ||13||

14senayorubhayoścāpi samantācchrūyate mahān
|
pāñcajanyasya nirghoṣo gāṇḍīvasya ca niḥsvanaḥ ||14||

15dhruvamāsthāya bībhatsuruttamāstrāṇi saṃyuge
|
apāsyānyān raṇe yodhānabhyeṣyati pitāmaham ||15||

16hṛṣyanti romakūpāṇi sīdatīva ca me manaḥ
|
cintayitvā mahābāho bhīṣmārjunasamāgamam ||16||

17taṃ ceha nikṛtiyajñaṃ pāñcālyaṃ pāpacetasam
|
puraskṛtya raṇe pārtho bhīṣmasyāyodhanaṃ gataḥ ||17||

18abravīcca purā bhīṣmo nāhaṃ hanyāṃ śikhaṇḍinam
|
strī hyeṣā vihitā dhātrā daivācca sa punaḥ pumān ||18||

19amaṅgalyadhvajaścaiva yājñasenirmahābalaḥ
|
na cāmaṅgalike tasmin praharedāpagāsutaḥ ||19||

20etadvicintayānasya majjā sīdati me bhṛśam
|
abhyudyato raṇe pārthaḥ kuruvṛddhamupādravat ||20||

21yudhiṣṭhirasya ca krodho bhīṣmaścārjunasaṅgataḥ
|
mama cāstrasamārambhaḥ prajānāmaśivaṃ dhruvam ||21||

22manasvī balavān śūraḥ kṛtāstro laghuvikramaḥ
|
dūrapātī dṛḍheṣuśca nimittajñaśca pāṇḍavaḥ ||22||

23ajeyaḥ samare cāpi devairapi savāsavaiḥ
|
balavānbuddhimāṃścaiva jitakleśo yudhāṃ varaḥ ||23||

24vijayī ca raṇe nityaṃ bhairavāstraśca pāṇḍavaḥ
|
tasya mārgaṃ pariharan drutaṃ gaccha yatavrata ||24||

25paśyādyatanmahāghore saṃyuge vaiśasaṃ mahat
|
hemacitrāṇi śūrāṇāṃ mahānti ca śubhāni ca ||25||

26kavacānyavadīryante śaraiḥ sannataparvabhiḥ
|
chidyante ca dhvajāgrāṇi tomarāśca dhanūṃṣi ca ||26||

27prāsāśca vimalāstīkṣṇāḥ śaktyaśca kanakojjvalāḥ
|
vaijayantyaśca nāgānāṃ saṃkruddhena kirīṭinā ||27||

28nāyaṃ saṃrakṣituṃ kālaḥ prāṇān putropajīvibhiḥ
|
yāhi svargaṃ puraskṛtya yaśase vijayāya ca ||28||

29rathanāgahayāvartāṃ mahāghorāṃ sudurgamām
|
rathena saṃgrāmanadīṃ taratyeṣa kapidhvajaḥ ||29||

30brahmaṇyatā damo dānaṃ tapaśca caritaṃ mahat
|
ihaiva dṛśyate pārthe bhrātā yasya dhanañjayaḥ ||30||

31bhīmasenaśca balavān mādrīputrau ca pāṇḍavau
|
vāsudevaśca vārṣṇeyo yasya nātho vyavasthitaḥ ||31||

32tasyaiṣa manyuprabhavo dhārtarāṣṭrasya durmateḥ
|
tapodagdhaśarīrasya kopo dahati bhāratīm ||32||

33eṣa saṃdṛśyate pārtho vāsudevavyapāśrayaḥ
|
dārayan sarvasainyāni dhārtarāṣṭrāṇi sarvaśaḥ||33||

34etadālokyate sainyaṃ kṣobhyamāṇaṃ kirīṭinā
|
mahorminaddhaṃ sumahattimineva mahājalam ||34||

35hāhā vilavilāśabdāḥ śrūyante ca camṛmukhe
|
yāhi pāñcāladāyādamaha yāsye yudhiṣṭhiram ||35||

36durgamaṃ hyantaraṃ rājño vyūhasyāmitatejasaḥ
|
samudrakukṣipratimaṃ sarvato'tirathaiḥ sthitaiḥ ||36||

37sātyakiścābhimanyuśca dhṛṣṭadyumnavṛkodarau
|
paryarakṣanta rājānaṃ yamau ca manujeśvaram ||37||

38upendrasadṛśaḥ śyāmo mahāśāla ivodgataḥ
|
eṣa gacchatyanīkāgre dvitīya iva phālgunaḥ ||38||

39uttamāstrāṇi cādhatsva gṛhītvā ca mahaddhanuḥ
|
pārṣataṃ yāhi rājānaṃ yudhyasva ca vṛkodaram ||39||

40kohi necchetpriyaṃ putraṃ jīvantaṃ śāśvatīḥ samāḥ
|
kṣatradharmaṃ tu saṃprekṣya tatastvāṃ niyunajmyaham ||40||

41eṣa cātiraṇe bhīṣmo dahate vai mahācamūm
|
yuddheṣu sadṛśastāta yamasya varuṇasya ca ||41||
