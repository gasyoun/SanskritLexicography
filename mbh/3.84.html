Mahābhārata 3.84 (Āraṇyakaparvan)

1pulastya uvāca|
tato gacchenmahārāja dharmatīrthamanuttamam
|
yatra dharmo mahābhāgastaptavānuttamaṃ tapaḥ||1||

2tena tīrthaṃ kṛtaṃ puṇyaṃ svena nāmnā ca viśrutam
|
tatra snātvā naro rājandharmaśīlaḥ samāhitaḥ||2||

3āsaptamaṃ kulaṃ caiva punīte nātra saṃśayaḥ
|
tato gaccheta rājendra jñānapāvanamuttamam||3||

4agniṣṭomamavāpnoti munilokaṃ ca gacchati
|
saugandhikavanaṃ rājaṃstato gaccheta mānavaḥ||4||

5tatra brahmādayo devā ṛṣayaśca tapodhanāḥ
|
siddhacāraṇagandharvāḥ kinnarāśca mahoragāḥ||5||

6tadvanaṃ praviśanneva sarvapāpaiḥ pramucyate
|
tataścāpi saricchreṣṭhā nadīnāmuttamā nadī||6||

7plakṣādevī smṛtā rājanpuṇyādevī sarasvatī
|
tatrābhiṣekaṃ kurvīta valmīkānniḥsṛte jale||7||

8arcayitvā pitṝndevānaśvamedhaphalaṃ labhet
|
īśānādhyuṣitaṃ nāma tatra tīrthaṃ sudurlabham||8||

9ṣaṭsu śamyānipāteṣu valmīkāditi niścayaḥ
|
kapilānāṃ sahasraṃ ca vājimedhaṃ ca vindati||9||

10tatra snātvā naravyāghra dṛṣṭametatpurātane
|
sugandhāṃ śatakumbhāṃ ca pañcayakṣāṃ ca bhārata||10||

11abhigamya naraśreṣṭha svargaloke mahīyate
|
triśūlakhātaṃ tatraiva tīrthamāsādya bhārata||11||

12tatrābhiṣekaṃ kurvīta pitṛdevārcane rataḥ
|
gāṇapatyaṃ ca labhate dehaṃ tyaktvā na saṃśayaḥ||12||

13tato gaccheta rājendra devyāḥ sthānaṃ sudurlabham
|
śākambharīti vikhyātā triṣu lokeṣu viśrutā||13||

14divyaṃ varṣasahasraṃ hi śākena kila suvratā
|
āhāraṃ sā kṛtavatī māsi māsi narādhipa||14||

15ṛṣayobhyāgatāstatra devyā bhaktyā tapodhanāḥ
|
ātithyaṃ ca kṛtaṃ teṣāṃ śākena kila bhārata||15||

16tataḥ śākambharītyeva nāma tasyāḥ pratiṣṭhitam
|
śākambharīṃ samāsādya brahmacārī samāhitaḥ||16||

17trirātramuṣitaḥ śākaṃ bhakṣayitvā naraḥ śuciḥ
|
śākāhārasya yatkiṃcidvarṣairdvādaśabhiḥ kṛtam||17||

18tatphalaṃ tasya bhavati devyāśchandena bhārata
|
tato gacchetsuvarṇākhyaṃ triṣu lokeṣu viśrutam||18||

19tatra viṣṇuḥ prasādārthaṃ rudramārādhayatpurā
|
varāṃśca subahūṃllebhe daivateṣu sudurlabhān||19||

20uktaśca tripuraghnena parituṣṭena bhārata
|
api ca tvaṃ priyataro loke kṛṣṇa bhaviṣyasi||20||

21tvanmukhaṃ ca jagatsarvaṃ bhaviṣyati na saṃśayaḥ
|
tatrābhigamya rājendra pūjayitvā vṛṣadhvajam||21||

22aśvamedhamavāpnoti gāṇapatyaṃ ca vindati
|
dhūmāvatīṃ tato gacchetrirātropoṣito naraḥ||22||

23manasā prārthitānkāmāṃllabhate nātra saṃśayaḥ
|
devyāstu dakṣiṇārdhena rathāvarto narādhipa||23||

24tatrāroheta dharmajña śraddadhāno jitendriyaḥ
|
mahādevaprasādāddhi gaccheta paramāṃ gatim||24||

25pradakṣiṇamupāvṛtya gaccheta bharatarṣabha
|
dhārāṃ nāma mahāprājñaḥ sarvapāpapramocanīm||25||

26tatra snātvā naravyāghra na śocati narādhipa
|
tato gaccheta dharmajña namaskṛtya mahāgirim||26||

27svargadvāreṇa yattulyaṃ gaṅgādvāraṃ na saṃśayaḥ
|
tatrābhiṣekaṃ kurvīta koṭitīrthe samāhitaḥ||27||

28puṇḍarīkamavāpnoti kulaṃ caiva samuddharet
|
uṣyaikāṃ rajanīṃ tatra gosahasraphalaṃ labhet||28||

29saptagaṅge trigaṅge ca śakrāvarte ca tarpayan
|
devānpitṝṃśca vidhivatpuṇye loke mahīyate||29||

30tataḥ kanakhale snātvā trirātropoṣito naraḥ
|
aśvamedhamavāpnoti svargalokaṃ ca gacchati||30||

31kapilāvaṭaṃ tato gacchettīrthasevī narādhipa
|
upoṣya rajanīṃ tatra gosahasraphalaṃ labhet||31||

32nāgarājasya rājendra kapilasya mahātmanaḥ
|
tīrthaṃ kuruvaraśreṣṭha sarvalokeṣu viśrutam||32||

33tatrābhiṣekaṃ kurvīta nāgatīrthe narādhipa
|
kapilānāṃ sahasrasya phalaṃ vindati mānavaḥ||33||

34tato lalitakaṃ gacchecchantanostīrthamuttamam
|
tatra snātvā naro rājanna durgatimavāpnuyāt||34||

35gaṅgāyamunayormadhye snāti yaḥ saṃgame naraḥ
|
daśāśvamedhānāpnoti kulaṃ caiva samuddharet||35||

36tato gaccheta rājendra sugandhaṃ lokaviśrutam
|
sarvapāpaviśuddhātmā brahmaloke mahīyate||36||

37rudrāvartaṃ tato gacchettīrthasevī narādhipa
|
tatra snātvā naro rājansvargaloke ca gacchati||37||

38gaṅgāyāśca naraśreṣṭha sarasvatyāśca saṃgame
|
snātvāśvamedhaṃ prāpnoti svargalokaṃ ca gacchati||38||

39bhadrakarṇeśvaraṃ gatvā devamarcya yathāvidhi
|
na durgatimavāpnoti nākapṛṣṭhe ca pūjyate||39||

40tataḥ kubjāmrake gacchettīrthasevī narādhipa
|
gosahasramavāpnoti svargalokaṃ ca nacchati||40||

41arundhatīvaṭaṃ gacchettīrthasevī narādhipa
|
sāmudrakamupaspṛśya brahmacārī samāhitaḥ||41||

42aśvamedhamavāpnoti trirātropoṣito naraḥ
|
gosahasraphalaṃ vidyātkulaṃ caiva samuddharet||42||

43brahmāvartaṃ tato gacchedbrahmacārī samāhitaḥ
|
aśvamedhamavāpnoti somalokaṃ ca gacchati||43||

44yamunāprabhavaṃ gatvā samupaspṛśya yāmunam
|
aśvamedhaphalaṃ labdhvā svargaloke mahīyate||44||

45darvīsaṃkramaṇaṃ prāpya tīrthaṃ trailokyapūjitam
|
aśvamedhamavāpnoti svargalokaṃ ca gacchati||45||

46sindhośca prabhavaṃ gatvā siddhagandharvasevitam
|
tatroṣya rajanīḥ pañca vindedbahu suvarṇakam||46||

47atha vedīṃ samāsādya naraḥ paramadurgamām
|
aśvamedhamavāpnoti svargalokaṃ ca gacchati||47||

48ṛṣikulyāṃ samāsādya vāsiṣṭhaṃ caiva bhārata
|
vāsiṣṭhīṃ samatikramya sarve varṇā dvijātayaḥ||48||

49ṛṣikulyāṃ samāsādya naraḥ snātvā vikalmaṣaḥ
|
devānpitṝṃścārcayitvā ṛṣilokaṃ prapadyate||49||

50yadi tatra vasenmāsaṃ śākāhāro narādhipa
|
bhṛgutuṅgaṃ samāsādya vājimedhaphalaṃ labhet||50||

51gatvā vīrapramokṣaṃ ca sarvapāpaiḥ pramucyate
|
kṛttikāmaghayoścaiva tīrthamāsādya bhārata||51||

52agniṣṭomātirātrābhyāṃ phalamāpnoti mānavaḥ
|
tatra saṃdhyāṃ samāsādya vidyātīrthamanuttamam||52||

53upaspṛśya ca vai vidyāṃ yatra tatropapadyate
|
mahāśrame vasedrātriṃ sarvapāpapramocane||53||

54ekakālaṃ nirāhāro lokānāvasate śubhān
|
ṣaṣṭhakālopavāsena māsamuṣya mahālaye||54||

55sarvapāpaviśuddātmā vindedbahusuvarṇakam
|
daśāparāndaśapūrvānnarānuddharate kulam||55||

56atha vetasikāṃ gatvā pitāmahaniṣevitām
|
aśvamedhamavāpnoti gacchedauśanasīṃ gatim||56||

57atha sundarikātīrthaṃ prāpya siddhaniṣevitam
|
rūpasya bhāgī bhavati dṛṣṭametatpurātanaiḥ||57||

58tato vai brāhmaṇīṃ gatvā brahmacārī jitendriyaḥ
|
padmavarṇena yānena brahmalokaṃ prapadyate||58||

59tatastu naimiṣaṃ gacchetpuṇyaṃ siddhaniṣevitam
|
tatra nityaṃ nivasati brahmā devagaṇaiḥ saha||59||

60naimiṣaṃ mṛgayānasya pāpasyārdhaṃ praṇaśyati
|
praviṣṭamātrastu naraḥ sarvapāpaiḥ pramucyate||60||

61tatra māsaṃ vaseddhīro naimiṣe tīrthatatparaḥ
|
pṛthivyāṃ yāni tīrthāni tāni tīrthāni naimiṣe||61||

62kṛtābhiṣekastatraiva niyato niyatāśanaḥ
|
gavāṃ medhasya yajñasya phalaṃ prāpnoti bhārata||62||

63punātyāsaptamaṃ caiva kulaṃ bharatasattama
|
yastyajennaimiṣe prāṇānupavāsaparāyaṇaḥ||63||

64sa modetsarvalokeṣu evamāhurmanīṣiṇaḥ
|
nityaṃ medhyaṃ ca puṇyaṃ ca naimiṣaṃ nṛpasattama||64||

65gaṅgodbhedaṃ samāsādya trirātropoṣito naraḥ
|
vājapeyamavāpnoti brahmabhūto bhavetsadā||65||

66sarasvatīṃ samāsādya tarpayetpitṛdevatāḥ
|
sārasvateṣu lokeṣu modate nātra saṃśayaḥ||66||

67tataśca bāhudāṃ gacchedbrahmacārī samāhitaḥ
|
tatroṣya rajanīmekāṃ svargaloke mahīyate||67||

68devasatrasya yajñasya phalaṃ prāpnoti kaurava
|
tataḥ kṣīravatīṃ gacchetpuṇyāṃ puṇyatarairvṛtām||68||

69pitṛdevārcanaparo vājapeyamavāpnuyāt
|
vimalāṃśokamāsādya brahmacārī samāhitaḥ||69||

70tatroṣya rajanīmekāṃ svargaloke mahīyate
|
gopratāraṃ tato gacchetsarayvātīrthamuttamam||70||

71yatra rāmo gataḥ svargaṃ sabhṛtyabalavāhanaḥ
|
dehaṃ tyaktvā mahārāja tasya tīrthasya tejasā||71||

72rāmasya ca prasādena vyavasāyācca bhārata
|
tasmiṃstīrthe naraḥ snātvā gopratāre narādhipa||72||

73sarvapāpaviśuddhātmā svargaloke mahīyate
|
rāmatīrthe naraḥ snātvā gomatyāṃ kurunandana||73||

74aśvamedhamavāpnoti punāti ca kulaṃ naraḥ
|
śatasāhasrakaṃ tīrthaṃ tatraiva bharatarṣabha||74||

75tatropasparśanaṃ kṛtvā niyato niyatāśanaḥ
|
gosahasraphalaṃ puṇyaṃ prāpnoti bharatarṣabha||75||

76tato gaccheta rājendra bhartṛsthānamanuttamam
|
aśvamedhasya yajñasya phalaṃ prāpnoti mānavaḥ||76||

77koṭitīrthe naraḥ snātvā arcayitvā guhaṃ nṛpa
|
gosahasraphaṃla vidyāttejasvī ca bhavennaraḥ||77||

78tato vārāṇasīṃ gatvā arcayitvā vṛṣadhvajam
|
kapilāhrade naraḥ snātvā rājasūyamavāpnuyāt||78||

79avimuktaṃ samāsādya tīrthasevī kurūdvaha
|
darśanāddevadevasya mucyate brahmahatyayā||79||

80prāṇānutsṛjya tatraiva mokṣaṃ prāpnoti mānavaḥ
|
mārkaṇḍeyasya rājendra tīrthamāsādya durlabham||80||

81gomatīgaṅgayoścaiva saṃgame lokaviśrute
|
agniṣṭomamavāpnoti kulaṃ caiva samuddharet||81||

82tato gayāṃ samāsādya brahmacārī samāhitaḥ
|
aśvamedhamavāpnoti kulaṃ caiva samuddharet||82||

83tatrākṣayavaṭo nāma triṣu lokeṣu viśrutaḥ
|
tatra dattaṃ pitṛbhyastu bhavatyakṣayamucyate||83||

84mahānadyāmupaspṛśya tapayetpitṛdevatāḥ
|
akṣayānprāpnuyāllokānkulaṃ caiva samuddharet||84||

85tato brahmasaro gatvā dharmāraṇyopaśobhitam
|
brahmalokamavāpnoti prabhātāmeva śarvarīm||85||

86brahmaṇā tatra sarasi yūpaśreṣṭhaḥ samucchritaḥ
|
yūpaṃ pradakṣiṇīkṛtya vājapeyaphalaṃ labhet||86||

87tato gaccheta rājendra dhenukaṃ lokaviśrutam
|
ekarātroṣito rājanprayacchettiladhenukām||87||

88sarvapāpaviśuddhātmā somalokaṃ vrajeddhruvam
|
tatra cihniṃ mahadrājannadyāpi sumahadbhṛśam||88||

89kapilāyāḥ savatsāyāścarantyāḥ parvate kṛtam
|
savatsāyāḥ padāni sma dṛśyante'dyāpi bhārata||89||

90teṣūpaspṛśya rājendra padeṣu nṛpasattama
|
yatkiṃcidaśubhaṃ karma tatpraṇaśyati bhārata||90||

91tato gṛdhravaṭaṃ gacchetsthānaṃ devasya dhīmataḥ
|
snāyīta bhasmanā tatra abhigamya vṛṣadhvajam||91||

92brāhmaṇena bhaveccīrṇaṃ vrataṃ dvādaśavārṣikam
|
itareṣāṃ tu varṇānāṃ sarvapāpaṃ praṇaśyati||92||

93udyantaṃ ca tato gacchetparvataṃ gītanāditam
|
sāvitryāstu padaṃ tatra dṛśyate bharatarṣabha||93||

94tatra saṃdhyāmupāsīta brāhmaṇaḥ saṃśitavrataḥ
|
tena hyupāstā bhavati saṃdhyā dvādaśavārṣikī||94||

95yonidvāraṃ ca tatraiva viśrutaṃ bharatarṣabha
|
tatrābhigamya mucyeta puruṣo yonisaṃkarāt||95||

96kṛṣṇaśuklāvubhau pakṣau gayāyāṃ yo vasennaraḥ
|
punātyāsaptamaṃ rājankulaṃ nāstyatra saṃśayaḥ||96||

97eṣṭavyā bahavaḥ putrā yadyekopi gayāṃ vrajet
|
yajeta vāśvamedhena nīlaṃ vā vṛṣamutsṛjet||97||

98tataḥ phalguṃ vrajedrājaṃstīrthasevī narādhipa
|
aśvamedhamavāpnoti siddhiṃ ca mahatīṃ vrajet||98||

99tato gaccheta rājendra dharmaprasthaṃ samāhitaḥ
|
tatra dharmo mahārāja nityamāste yudhiṣṭhira||99||

100tatra kūpodakaṃ kṛtvā tena snātaḥ śucistathā
|
pitṝndevāṃstu saṃtarpya muktapāpo divaṃ vrajet||100||

101mataṅgasyāśramastatra maharṣerbhāvitātmanaḥ
|
taṃ praviśyāśramaṃ śrīmacchramaśokavināśanam||101||

102gavāmayanayajñasya phalaṃ prāpnoti mānavaḥ
|
dharmaṃ tatrābhisaṃspṛśya vājimedhamavāpnuyāt||102||

103tato gaccheta rājendra brahmasthānamanuttamam
|
tatrābhigamya rājendra brahmāṇaṃ puruṣarṣabha||103||

104rājasūyāśvamedhābhyāṃ phalaṃ vindati mānavaḥ
|
tato rājagṛhaṃ gacchettīrthasevī narādhipa||104||

105upaspṛśya tatastatra kakṣīvāniva modate
|
yakṣiṇyā naityakaṃ tatra prāśnīta puruṣaḥ śuciḥ||105||

106yakṣiṇyāstu prasādena mucyate brahmahatyayā
|
maṇināgaṃ tato gatvā gosahasraphalaṃ labhet||106||

107tairthikaṃ bhuñjate yastu maṇināgasya bhārata
|
daṣṭasyāśīviṣeṇāpi na tasya kramate viṣam||107||

108tatroṣya rajanīmekāṃ gosahasraphalaṃ labhet
|
tato gaccheta brahmarṣergautamasya vanaṃ priyam||108||

109ahalyāyā hrade snātvā vrajeta paramāṃ gatim
|
abhigamyāśramaṃ rājanvindate śriyamātmanaḥ||109||

110tatrodapānaṃ dharmajña triṣu lokeṣu viśrutam
|
tatrābhiṣekaṃ kṛtvā tu vājimedhamavāpnuyāt||110||

111janakasya tu rājarṣeḥ kūpastridaśapūjitaḥ
|
tatrābhiṣekaṃ kṛtvā tu viṣṇulokamavāpnuyāt||111||

112tato vinaśanaṃ gacchetsarvapāpapramocanam
|
vājapeyamavāpnoti somalokaṃ ca gacchati||112||

113gaṇḍakīṃ tu samāsādya sarvatīrthajalodbhavām
|
vājapeyamavāpnoti sūryalokaṃ ca gacchati||113||

114katato viśalyāmāsādya nadīṃ trailokyaviśrutām
|
agniṣṭomamavāpnoti svargalokaṃ ca gacchati||114||

115tatodhivaṅgaṃ dharmajña samāviśya tato vanam
|
guhyakeṣu mahārāja modate nātra saṃśayaḥ||115||

116kampanāṃ tu samāsadya nadīṃ siddhaniveṣitām
|
puṇḍarīkamavāpnoti svargalokaṃ ca gacchati||116||

117atha māheśvarīṃ dhārāṃ samāsādya dharādhipa
|
aśvamedhamavāpnoti kulaṃ caiva samuddharet||117||

118divaukasāṃ puṣkariṇīṃ samāsādya narādhipa
|
na durgatimavāpnoti vājimedhaṃ ca vindati||118||

119atha somapadaṃ gacchedbrahmacārī samāhitaḥ
|
māheśvarapade snātvā vājimedhaphalaṃ labhet||119||

120tatra koṭī tu tīrthānāṃ viśrutā bharatarṣabha
|
kūrmarūpeṇa rājendra hyasureṇa durātmanā||120||

121hriyamāṇā hṛtā rājanviṣṇunā prabhaviṣṇunā
|
tatrābhiṣekaṃ kurvīta tīrthakoṭyāṃ yudhiṣṭhira||121||

122puṇḍarīkamavāpnoti viṣṇulokaṃ ca gacchati
|
tato gaccheta rājendrasthānaṃ nārāyaṇasya ca||122||

123sadā sannihito yatra viṣṇurvasati bhārata
|
yatra brahmādayo devā ṛṣayaśca tapodhanāḥ||123||

124ādityā vasavo rudrā janārdanamupāsate
|
śālagrāma iti khyāto viṣṇuradbhutakarmakaḥ||124||

125abhigamya trilokeśaṃ varadaṃ viṣṇumavyayam
|
aśvamedhamavāpnoti viṣṇulokaṃ ca gacchati||125||

126tatropadānaṃ dharmajña sarvapāpapramocanam
|
samudrāstatra catvāraḥ kūpe saṃnihitāḥ sadā||126||

127tatropaspṛśya rājendra na durgatimavāpnuyāt
|
abhigamya mahādevaṃ varadaṃ rudramavyayam||127||

128virājati yathā somo meghairmukto narādhipa
|
jātismaramupaspṛśya śuciḥ prayatamānasaḥ||128||

129jātismaratvamāpnoti snātvā tatra na saṃśayaḥ
|
māheśvarapuraṃ gatvā arcayitvā vṛṣadhvajam||129||

130īpsitā~llabhate kāmānupavāsānna saṃśayaḥ
|
tatastu vāmanaṃ gatvā sarvapāpapramocanam||130||

131abhigamya hariṃ devaṃ na durgatimavāpnuyāt
|
bharatasyāśramaṃ gacchetsarvapāpapramocanam||131||

132kauśikīṃ tatra gaccheta mahāpāpapraṇāśinīm
|
rājasūyasya yajñasya phalaṃ prāpnoti mānavaḥ||132||

133tato gaccheta rājendra campakāraṇyamuttamam
|
tatroṣya rajanīmekāṃ gosahasraphalaṃ labhet||133||

134atha jeṣṭhilamāsādya tīrthaṃ paramadurlabham
|
tatroṣya rajanīmekāṃ gosahasraphalaṃ labhet||134||

135tatra viśveśvaraṃ dṛṣṭvā devyā saha mahādyutim
|
mitrāvaruṇayorlokānāpnoti puruṣarṣabha||135||

136trirātropoṣitastatra agniṣṭomaphalaṃ labhet
|
kanyāsaṃvedyamāsādya niyato nayitāśanaḥ||136||

137manoḥ prajāpaterlokānāpnoti puruṣarṣabha
|
kanyāyāṃ ye prayacchanti dānamaṇvapi bhārata||137||

138tadakṣayyamiti prāhurṛṣayaḥ saṃśitavratāḥ
|
tato nirvīramāsādya triṣu lokepu viśrutam||138||

139aśvamedhamavāpnoti viṣṇulokaṃ ca gacchati
|
ye tu dānaṃ prayacchanti nirvīrāsaṃgame narāḥ||139||

140te yānti naraśārdūla śakralokamanāmayam
|
tatrāśramo vasiṣṭhasya triṣu lokeṣu viśrutaḥ||140||

141tatrābhiṣekaṃ kurvāṇo vājapeyamavāpnuyāt
|
devakūṭaṃ samāsādya brahmarṣigaṇasevitam||141||

142aśvamedhamavāpnoti kulaṃ caiva samuddharet
|
tato gaccheta rājendra kauśikasya munerhradam||142||

143yatra siddhiṃ parāṃ prāpto viśvāmitrotha kauśikaḥ
|
tatra māsaṃ vasedvīra kauśikyāṃ bharatarṣabha||143||

144aśvamedhasya yatpuṇyaṃ tanmāsenādhigacchati
|
sarvatīrthavare caiva yo vaseta mahāhrade||144||

145na durgatimavāpnoti vindyādbahu suvarṇakam
|
kumāramabhigamyātha vīrāśramanivāsinam||145||

146aśvamedhamavāpnoti naro nāstyatra saṃśayaḥ
|
agnidhārāṃ samāsādya triṣu lokeṣu viśrutām||146||

147tatrābhiṣekaṃ kurvāṇo hyagniṣṭomamavāpnuyāt
|
abhigamya mahādevaṃ varadaṃ viṣṇumavyayam||147||

148pitāmahasaro gatvā śailarājasamīpataḥ
|
tatrābhiṣekaṃ kurvāṇo hyagniṣṭomamavāpnuyāt||148||

149pitāmahasya sarasaḥ prasrutā lokapāvanī
|
kumāradhārā tatraiva triṣu lokeṣu viśrutā||149||

150yatra snātvā kṛtārtho'smītyātmānamavagacchati
|
ṣaṣṭhakālopavāsena mucyate brahmahatyayā||150||

151tato gaccheta dharmajña tīrthasevanatatparaḥ
|
śikharaṃ vai mahādevyā gauryāstrailokyaviśrutam||151||

152samāruhya naraśreṣṭha stanakuṇḍeṣu saṃviśet
|
stanakuṇḍamupaspṛśya vājapeyaphalaṃ labhet||152||

153tatrābhiṣekaṃ kurvāṇaḥ pitṛdevārcane rataḥ
|
hayamedhamavāpnoti śakralokaṃ ca gacchati||153||

154tāmrāruṇaṃ samāsādya brahmacārī samāhitaḥ
|
aśvamedhavāpnoti brahmalokaṃ ca gacchati||154||

155nandinyāṃ ca samāsādya kūpaṃ devaniṣevitam
|
naramedhasya yatpuṇyaṃ tadāpnoti narādhipa||155||

156kālikāsaṃgame snātvā kauśikyaruṇayorgataḥ
|
trirātropoṣito rājansarvapāpaiḥ pramucyate||156||

157urvaśītīrthamāsādya tataḥ somāśramaṃ budhaḥ
|
kumbhakarṇāśramaṃ gatvā pūjyate bhuvi mānavaḥ||157||

158kokāmukhamupaspṛśya brahmacārī yatavrataḥ
|
jātismaratvamāpnoti dṛṣṭametatpurātanaiḥ||158||

159prāṅnadīṃ ca samāsādya kṛtātmā bhavati dvijaḥ
|
sarvapāpaviśuddhātmā śakralokaṃ ca gacchati||159||

160ṛṣabhadvīpamāsādya medhyaṃ krauñcaniṣūdanam
|
sarasvatyāmupaspṛśya vimānastho virājate||160||

161auddālakaṃ mahārāja tīrthaṃ muniniṣevitam
|
tatrābhiṣekaṃ kṛtvā vai sarvapāpaiḥ pramucyate||161||

162dharmatīrthaṃ samāsādya puṇyaṃ brahmarṣisevitam
|
vājapeyamavāpnoti vimānasthaśca pūjyate||162||

163atha campāṃ samāsādya bhāgīrathyāṃ kṛtodakaḥ
|
daṇḍārtamabhigatvā tu gosahasraphalaṃ labhet||163||
