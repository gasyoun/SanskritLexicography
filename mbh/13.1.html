Mahābhārata 13.1 (Anuśāsanaparvan)

1yudhiṣṭhira uvāca|
śamo bahuvidhākāraḥ sūkṣma uktaḥ pitāmaha
|
na ca me hṛdaye śāntirasti śrutvedamīdṛśam ||1||

2asminnarthe bahuvidhā śāntiruktā pitāmaha
|
svakṛte kā nu śāntiḥ syācchamādbahuvidhādapi ||2||

3śarācitaśarīraṃ hi tīvravraṇamudīkṣya ca
|
śarma nopalabhe vīra duṣkṛtānyeva cintayan ||3||

4rudhireṇāvasiktāṅgaṃ prasravantaṃ yathācalam
|
tvāṃ dṛṣṭvā puruṣavyāghra sīde varṣāsvivāṃbujam ||4||

5ataḥ kaṣṭataraṃ kiṃ nu matkṛte yatpitāmahaḥ
|
imāmavasthāṃ gamitaḥ pratyamitrai raṇājire ||5||

6tathā cānye nṛpatayaḥ sahaputrāḥ sabāndhavāḥ
|
matkṛte nidhanaṃ prāptāḥ kiṃ nu kaṣṭataraṃ tataḥ ||6||

7vayaṃ hi dhārtarāṣṭrāśca kālamanyuvaśaṅgatāḥ
|
kṛtvedaṃ ninditaṃ karma prāpsyāmaḥ kāṃ gatiṃ nṛpa ||7||

8idaṃ tu dhārtarāṣṭrasya śreyo manye janādhipa
|
imāmavasthāṃ saṃprāptaṃ yadasau tvāṃ na paśyati ||8||

9so'haṃ tava hyantakaraḥ suhṛdvadhakarastathā
|
na śāntimadhigacchāmi paśyaṃstvāṃ duḥkhitaṃ kṣitau ||9||

10duryodhano hi samare sahasainyaḥ sahānujaḥ
|
nihataḥ kṣatradharme'smindurātmā kulapāṃsanaḥ ||10||

11na sa paśyati duṣṭātmā tvāmadya patitaṃ kṣitau
|
ataḥ śreyo mṛtaṃ manye neha jīvitamātmanaḥ ||11||

12ahaṃ hi samare vīra gamitaḥ śatrubhiḥ kṣayam
|
abhaviṣyaṃ yadi purā saha bhrātṛbhiracyuta ||12||

13na tvāmevaṃ suduḥkhārtamadrākṣaṃ sāyakārditam
|
nūnaṃ hi pāpakarmāṇo dhātrā sṛṣṭāḥ sma he nṛpa ||13||

14anyasminnapi loke vai yathā mucyema kilbiṣāt
|
tathā praśādhi māṃ rājan mama cedicchasi priyam ||14||

15bhīṣma uvāca|
paratantraṃ kathaṃ hetumātmānamanupaśyasi
|
karmaṇāṃ hi mahābhāga sūkṣmaṃ hyetadatīndriyam ||15||

16atrāpyudāharantīmamitihāsaṃ purātanam
|
saṃvādaṃ mṛtyugautamyoḥ kālalubdhakapannagaiḥ ||16||

17gautamī nāma kaunteya sthavirā śamasaṃyutā
|
sarpeṇa daṣṭaṃ svaṃ putramapaśyadgatacetanam ||17||

18atha taṃ snāyupāśena baddhvā sarpamamarṣitaḥ
|
lubdhako'rjunako nāma gautamyāḥ samupānayat ||18||

19sa cābravīdayaṃ te sa putrahā pannagādhamaḥ
|
brūhi kṣipraṃ mahābhāge vadhyatāṃ kena hetunā ||19||

20agnau prakṣipyatāmeṣa cchidyatāṃ khaṇḍaśo'pi vā
|
na hyayaṃ bālahā pāpaściraṃ jīvitumarhati ||20||

21gautamyuvāca|
visṛjainamabuddhistvamavadhyo'rjunaka tvayā
|
ko hyātmānaṃ guruṃ kuryāt prāptavyamavicintayan ||21||

22plavante dharmalaghavo loka'mbhasi yathā plavāḥ
|
majjanti pāpaguravaḥ śastraṃ skannamivodake ||22||

23hatvā cainaṃ nāmṛtaḥ syādayaṃ me jīvatyasminko'tyayaḥ syādayaṃ te
|
asyotsarge prāṇayuktasya jantormṛtyorlokaṃ ko nu gacchedanantam ||23||

24lubdhaka uvāca|
jānāmyahaṃ devi guṇāguṇajñe sarvārtiyuktā guravo bhavanti
|
svasthasyaite tūpadeśā bhavanti tasmātkṣudraṃ sarpamenaṃ haniṣye ||24||

25śamārthinaḥ kālagatiṃ vadanti sadyaḥ śucaṃ tvarthavidastyajanti
|
śreyaḥkṣayaṃ śocati nityamohāttasmācchucaṃ muñca hate bhujaṅge ||25||

26gautamyuvāca|
ārtirnaivaṃ vidyate'smadvidhānāṃ dharmātmānaḥ sarvadā sajjanā hi
|
nityāyasto bālako'pyasya tasmādīśe nāhaṃ pannagasya pramāthe ||26||

27na brāhmaṇānāṃ kopo'sti kutaḥ kopācca yātanām
|
mārdavātkṣamyatāṃ sādho mucyatāmeṣa pannagaḥ ||27||

28lubdhaka uvāca|
hatvā lābhaḥ śreya evāvyayaḥ syāllabhyo lābhyaḥ syādbalibhyaḥ praśastaḥ
|
kālāllābho yastu satyo bhaveta śreyolābhaḥ kutsite'sminna te syāt ||28||

29gautamyuvāca|
kā nu prāptirgṛhya śatruṃ nihatya kā kāmāptiḥ prāpya śatruṃ na muktvā
|
kasmātsaumyāhaṃ na kṣame no bhujaṅge mokṣārthaṃ vā kasya hetorna kuryām ||29||

30lubdhaka uvāca|
asmādekādbahavo rakṣitavyā naiko bahubhyo gautami rakṣitavyaḥ
|
kṛtāgasaṃ dharmavidastyajanti sarīsṛpaṃ pāpamimaṃ jahi tvam ||30||

31gautamyuvāca|
nāsmin hate pannage putrako me saṃprāpsyate lubdhaka jīvitaṃ vai
|
guṇaṃ cānyaṃ nāsya vadhe prapaśye tasmātsarpaṃ lubdhaka muñca jīvam ||31||

32lubdhaka uvāca|
vṛtraṃ hatvā devarāṭ śreṣṭhabhāgvai yajñaṃ hatvā bhāgamavāpa caiva
|
śūlī devo devavṛttaṃ cara tvaṃ kṣipraṃ sarpaṃ jahi mā bhūtte viśaṅkā ||32||

33bhīṣma uvāca|
asakṛtprocyamānā'pi gautamī bhujagaṃ prati
|
lubdhakena mahābhāgā pāpe naivākaronmatim ||33||

34īṣaducchvasamānastu kṛcchrātsaṃstabhya pannagaḥ
|
utsasarjaṃ giraṃ mandāṃ mānuṣīṃ pāśapīḍitaḥ ||34||

35sarpa uvāca|
ko nvarjunaka doṣo'tra vidyate mama bāliśa
|
asvatantraṃ hi māṃ mṛtyurvivaśaṃ yadacūcudat ||35||

36tasyāyaṃ vacanāddaṣṭo na kopena na kāmyayā
|
tasya tatkilbiṣaṃ lubdha vidyate yadi kilbiṣaṃ ||36||

37lubdhaka uvāca|
yadyanyavaśagenedaṃ kṛtaṃ te pannagāśubham
|
kāraṇaṃ vai tvamapyatra tasmāttvamapi kilbiṣī ||37||

38mṛtpātrasya kriyāyāṃ hi daṇḍacakrādayo yathā
|
kāraṇatve prakalpyante tathā tvamapi pannaga ||38||

39kilbiṣī cāpi me vadhyaḥ kilbiṣī cāsi pannaga
|
ātmānaṃ kāraṇaṃ hyatra tvamākhyāsi bhujaṅgama ||39||

40sarpa uvāca|
sarva ete hyasvavaśā daṇḍacakrāda‌yo yathā
|
tathā'hamapi tasmānme naiṣa doṣo matastava ||40||

41athavā matametatte te'pyanyonyaprayojakāḥ
|
kāryakāraṇasandeho bhavatyanyonyacodanāt ||41||

42evaṃ sati na doṣo me nāsmi vadhyo na kilbiṣī
|
kilbiṣaṃ samavāye syānmanyase yadi kilbiṣam ||42||

43lubdhaka uvāca|
kāraṇaṃ yadi na syādvai na kartā syāstvamapyuta
|
vināśakāraṇaṃ tvaṃ ca tasmādvadhyo'si me mataḥ ||43||

44asatyapi kṛte kārye neha pannaga lipyate
|
tasmānnātraiva hetuḥ syādvadhyaḥ kiṃ bahu manyase ||44||

45sarpa uvāca|
kāryābhāve kriyā na syātsatyasatyapi kāraṇe
|
tasmātsame'sminhetau me vācyo heturviśeṣataḥ ||45||

46yadyahaṃ kāraṇatvena mato lubdhaka tattvataḥ
|
anyaḥ prayoge syādatra kilbiṣī jantunāśane ||46||

47lubdhaka uvāca|
vadhyastvaṃ mama durbuddhe bālaghātī nṛśaṃsakṛt
|
bhāṣase kiṃ bahu punarvadhyaḥ sanpannagādhama ||47||

48sarpa uvāca|
yathā havīṃṣi juhvānā mukhe vai lubdhakartvijaḥ
|
na phalaṃ prāpnuvantyatra phalayoge tathā hyaham ||48||

49bhīṣma uvāca|
tathā bruvati tasmiṃstu pannage mṛtyucodite
|
ājagāma tato mṛtyuḥ pannagaṃ cābravīdidam ||49||

50mṛtyuruvāca|
pracodito'haṃ kālena pannaga tvāmacūcudam
|
vināśaheturnāsya tvamahaṃ na prāṇinaḥ śiśoḥ ||50||

51yathā vāyurjaladharānvikarṣati tatastataḥ
|
tadvajjaladavatsarpa kālasyāhaṃ vaśānugaḥ ||51||

52sāttvikā rājasāścaiva tāmasā ye ca kecana
|
bhāvāḥ kālātmakāḥ sarve pravartante ha jantuṣu ||52||

53jaṅgamāḥ sthāvarāścaiva divi vā yadi vā bhuvi
|
sarve kālātmakāḥ sarpa kālātmakamidaṃ jagat ||53||

54pravṛttayaśca loke'smiṃstathaiva ca nivṛttayaḥ
|
tāsāṃ vikṛtayā yāśca sarvaṃ kālātmakaṃ smṛtam ||54||

55ādityaścandramā viṣṇurāpo vāyuḥ śatakratuḥ
|
agniḥ khaṃ pṛthivī mitraḥ parjanyo vasavo'ditiḥ ||55||

56saritaḥ sāgarāścaiva bhāvābhāvau ca pannaga
|
sarve kālena sṛjyante hīyante ca punaḥ punaḥ ||56||

57evaṃ jñātvā kathaṃ māṃ tvaṃ sadoṣaṃ sarpa manyase
|
atha caivaṃgate doṣe mayi tvamapi doṣavān ||57||

58sarpa uvāca|
nirdoṣaṃ doṣavantaṃ vā na tvāṃ mṛtyo bravīmyaham
|
tvayā'haṃ codita iti bravīmyetāvadeva tu ||58||

59yadi kāle tu doṣo'sti yadi tatrāpi neṣyate
|
doṣo naiva parīkṣyo me na hyatrādhikṛtā vayam ||59||

60nirmokṣastvasya doṣasya mayā kāryā yathā tathā
|
mṛtyorapi na doṣaḥ syāditi me'tra prayojanam ||60||

61bhīṣma uvāca|
sarpo'thārjunakaṃ prāha śrutaṃ te mṛtyubhāṣitam
|
nānāgasaṃ māṃ pāśena santāpayitumarhasi ||61||

62lubdhaka uvāca|
mṛtyoḥ śrutaṃ me vacanaṃ tava caiva bhujaṅgama
|
naiva tāvadadoṣatvaṃ bhavati tvayi pannaga ||62||

63mṛtyustvaṃ caiva heturhi bālasyāsya vināśane
|
ubhayaṃ kāraṇaṃ manye na kāraṇamakāraṇam ||63||

64dhiṅmṛtyuṃ ca durātmānaṃ krūraṃ duḥkhakaraṃ satām
|
tvāṃ caivāhaṃ vadhiṣyāmi pāpaṃ pāpasya kāraṇam ||64||

65mṛtyuruvāca|
vivaśau kālavaśagāvāvāṃ nirdiṣṭakāriṇau
|
nāvāṃ doṣeṇa gantavyau yadi samyakprapaśyasi ||65||

66lubdhaka uvāca|
yuvāmubhau kālavaśau yadi me mṛtyupannagau
|
harṣakrodhau yathā syātāmetadicchāmi veditum ||66||

67mṛtyuruvāca|
yā kācideva ceṣṭā spātsarvā kālapracoditā
|
pūrvamevaitaduktaṃ hi mayā lubdhaka kālataḥ ||67||

68tasmādubhau kālavaśāvāvāṃ nirdiṣṭakāriṇau
|
nāvāṃ doṣeṇa gantavyau tvayā lubdhaka karhicit ||68||

69bhīṣma uvāca|
athopagamya kālastu tasmin dharmārthasaṃśaye
|
abravītpannagaṃ mṛtyuṃ lubdhaṃ cārjunakaṃ tathā ||69||

70kāla uvāca|
na hyahaṃ nāvyayaṃ mṛtyurnāyaṃ lubdhaka pannagaḥ
|
kilbiṣī jantumaraṇe na vayaṃ hi prayojakāḥ ||70||

71akarodyadayaṃ karma tanno'rjunaka codakam
|
vināśaheturnānyo'sya vadhyate'yaṃ svakarmaṇā ||71||

72yadanena kṛtaṃ karma tenāyaṃ nidhanaṃ gataḥ
|
vināśahetuḥ karmāsya sarve karmavaśā vayam ||72||

73karmadāyādavā~llokaḥ karmasambandhalakṣaṇaḥ
|
karmāṇi codayantīha yathā'nyonyaṃ tathā vayam ||73||

74yathā mṛtpiṇḍataḥ kartā kurute yadyadicchati
|
evamātmakṛtaṃ karma mānavaḥ pratipadyate ||74||

75yathā cchāyātapau nityaṃ susambaddhau nirantaram
|
tathā karma ca kartā ca sambaddhāvātmakarmabhiḥ ||75||

76evaṃ nāhaṃ na vai mṛtyurna sarpo na tathā bhavān
|
na ceyaṃ brāhmaṇī vṛddhā śiśurevātra kāraṇam ||76||

77tasmiṃstathā buvāṇe tu brāhmaṇī gautamī nṛpa
|
svakarmapratyayā~llokānmatvā'rjunakamabravīt ||77||

78gautamyuvāca|
naiva kālo na bhujago na mṛtyuriha kāraṇam
|
svakarmabhirayaṃ bālaḥ kālena nidhanaṃ gataḥ ||78||

79mayā ca tatkṛtaṃ karma yenāyaṃ me mṛtaḥ sutaḥ
|
yātu kālastathā mṛtyurmuñcārjunaka pannagam ||79||

80bhīṣma uvāca|
tato yathāgataṃ jagmurmṛtyuḥ kālo'tha pannagaḥ
|
abhūdviśoko'rjunako viśokā caiva gautamī ||80||

81etacchrutvā śamaṃ gaccha mā bhūḥ śokaparo nṛpa
|
svakarmapratyayā~llokān sarve gacchanti vai nṛpa ||81||

82naiva tvayā kṛtaṃ karma nāpi duryodhanena vai
|
kālenaitatkṛtaṃ viddhi nihatā yena pārthivāḥ ||82||

83vaiśampāyana uvāca|
ityetadvacanaṃ śrutvā babhūva vigatajvaraḥ
|
yudhiṣṭhiro mahātejāḥ papracchedaṃ ca dharmavit ||83||
