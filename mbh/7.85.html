Mahābhārata 7.85 (Droṇaparvan)

1dhṛtarāṣṭra uvāca|
śvobhūte kimakārṣuste duḥkhaśokasamanvitāḥ
|
abhimanyau hate tatra ke vā'yudhyanta māmakāḥ ||1||

2jānantastasya karmāṇi kuravaḥ savyasācinaḥ
|
kathaṃ tatkilbiṣaṃ kṛtvā nirbhayā brūhi māmakāḥ ||2||

3putraśokābhisaṃtaptaṃ kruddhaṃ mṛtyumivāntakam
|
āyāntaṃ puruṣavyāghraṃ kathaṃ dadṛśurāhave ||3||

4kapirājadhvajaṃ saṃkhye vidhunvānaṃ mahaddhanuḥ
|
dṛṣṭvā putraparidyūnaṃ kimakurvata māmakāḥ ||4||

5kiṃ nu sañjaya saṃgrāme vṛttaṃ duryodhanaṃ prati
|
paridevo mahānadya śruto me nābhinandanam ||5||

6babhūvurye manogrāhyāḥ śabdāḥ śrutisukhāvahāḥ
|
na śrūyante'dya sarve te saindhavasya niveśane ||6||

7stuvatāṃ nādya śrūyante putrāṇāṃ śibire mama
|
sūtamāgadhasaṅghānāṃ nartakānāṃ ca sarvaśaḥ ||7||

8śabdena nāditā'bhīkṣṇamabhavadyatra me śrutiḥ
|
dīnānāmadya taṃ śabdaṃ na śṛṇomi samīritam ||8||

9niveśane satyadhṛteḥ somada‌ttasya sañjaya
|
āsīno'haṃ purā tāta śabda‌maśrauṣamuttamam ||9||

10tadadya puṇyahīno'hamārtasvaranināditam
|
niveśanaṃ gatotsāhaṃ putrāṇāṃ mama lakṣaye ||10||

11viviṃśaterdurmukhasya citrasenavikarṇayoḥ
|
anyeṣāṃ ca sutānāṃ me na tathā śrūyate dhvaniḥ ||11||

12brāhmaṇāḥ kṣatriyā vaiśyā yaṃ śiṣyāḥ paryupāsate
|
droṇaputraṃ maheṣvāsaṃ putrāṇāṃ me parāyaṇam ||12||

13vitaṇḍālāpasaṃlāpairdrutavāditravāditaiḥ
|
gītaiśca vividhairiṣṭai ramate yo divāniśam ||13||

14upāsyamāno bahubhiḥ kurupāṇḍavasātvataiḥ
|
sūta tasya gṛhe śabdo nādya drauṇeryathā purā ||14||

15droṇaputraṃ maheṣvāsaṃ gāyanā nartakāśca ye
|
atyarthamupatiṣṭhanti teṣāṃ na śrūyate dhvaniḥ ||15||

16vindānuvindayoḥ sāyaṃ śibire yo mahādhvaniḥ ||16||

17śrūyate so'dya na tathā kekayānāṃ ca veśmasu
|
nityaṃ pramuditānāṃ ca tālagītasvano mahān ||17||

18nṛtyatāṃ śrūyate tāta gaṇānāṃ so'dya na svanaḥ
|
saptatantūn vitanvānā yājakā yamupāsate ||18||

19saumadattiṃ śrutanidhiṃ teṣāṃ na śrūyate dhvaniḥ
|
jyāghoṣo brahmaghoṣaśca tomarāsirathadhvaniḥ ||19||

20droṇasyāsīdavirato gṛhe taṃ na śṛṇomyaham
|
nānādeśasaṃmutthānāṃ gītānāṃ yo'bhavatsvanaḥ ||20||

21vāditranāditānāṃ ca so'dya na śrūyate mahān
|
yadā prabhṛtyupaplavyācchāntimicchan janārdanaḥ ||21||

22āgataḥ sarvabhūtānāmanukampārthamacyutaḥ
|
tato'hamabruvaṃ sūtaṃ mandaṃ duryodhanaṃ tadā ||22||

23vāsudevena tīrthena putra saṃśāmya pāṇḍavaiḥ
|
kālaprāptamahaṃ manye mā tvaṃ duryodhanātigāḥ ||23||

24śamaṃ cedyācamānaṃ tvaṃ pratyākhyāsyasi keśavam
|
hitārthamabhijalpantaṃ na tavāsti raṇe jayaḥ ||24||

25pratyācaṣṭa sa dāśārhamṛṣabhaṃ sarvadhanvinām
|
anuneyāni jalpantamanayānnānvapadyata ||25||

26tato duḥśāsanasyaiva karṇasya ca mataṃ dvayoḥ
|
anvavartata māṃ hitvā kṛṣṭaḥ kālena durmatiḥ ||26||

27na hyahaṃ dyūtamicchāmi viduro na praśaṃsati
|
saindhavo necchati dyūtaṃ bhīṣmo na dyūtamicchati ||27||

28śalyo bhūriśravāścaiva purumitro jayastathā
|
aśvatthāmā kṛpo droṇo dyūtaṃ necchanti sañjaya ||28||

29eteṣāṃ matamādāya yadi varteta putrakaḥ
|
sajñātimitraḥ sasuhṛcciraṃ jīvedanāmayaḥ ||29||

30ślakṣṇā madhurasaṃbhāṣā jñātibandhupriyaṃvadāḥ
|
kulīnāḥ saṃmatāḥ prājñāḥ sukhaṃ prāpsyanti pāṇḍavāḥ ||30||

31dharmāpekṣī naro nityaṃ sarvatra labhate sukham
|
pretyabhāve ca kalyāṇaṃ prasādaṃ pratipadyate ||31||

32arhāste pṛthivīṃ bhoktuṃ samarthāḥ sādhane'pi ca
|
teṣāmapi samudrāntā pitṛpaitāmahī mahī ||32||

33niyujyamānāḥ sthāsyanti pāṇḍavā dharmavartmani
|
santi me jñātayastāta yeṣāṃ śriṣyanti pāṇḍavāḥ ||33||

34śalyasya somadattasya bhīṣmasya ca mahātmanaḥ
|
droṇasyātha vikarṇasya bālhīkasya kṛpasya ca ||34||

35anyeṣāṃ caiva vṛddhānāṃ bharatānāṃ mahātmanām
|
tvadarthaṃ bruvatāṃ tāta kariṣyanti vaco hi te ||35||

36kaṃ vā tvaṃ manyase teṣāṃ yastān brūyādato'nyathā
|
kṛṣṇo na dharmaṃ sañjahyāt sarve te hi tadanvayāḥ ||36||

37mayā'pi coktāste vīrā vacanaṃ dharmasaṃhitam
|
nānyathā prakariṣyanti dharmātmāno hi pāṇḍavāḥ ||37||

38ityahaṃ vilapan sūta bahuśaḥ putramuktavān
|
na ca me śrutabān mūḍho manye kālasya paryayam ||38||

39vṛkodarārjunau yatra vṛṣṇivīraśca sātyakiḥ
|
uttamaujāśca pāñcālyo yudhāmanyuśca durjayaḥ ||39||

40dhṛṣṭadyumnaśca durdharṣaḥ śikhaṇḍī cāparājitaḥ
|
aśmakāḥ kekayāścaiva kṣatradharmā ca saumakiḥ ||40||

41caidyaśca cekinātaśca putraḥ kāśyasya cābhibhūḥ
|
draupadeyā virāṭaśca drupadaśca mahārathaḥ ||41||

42yamau ca puruṣavyātaghrau mantrī ca madhusūdanaḥ
|
ka etān jātu yudhyeta loke'smin vai jijīviṣuḥ ||42||

43divyamastraṃ vikurvāṇān prasahedvā parān mama
|
anyo duryodhanāt karṇācchakuneścāpi saubalāt ||43||

44duḥśāsanacaturthānāṃ nānyaṃ paśyāmi pañcamam
|
yeṣāmabhīṣuhastaḥ syādviṣvakseno rathe sthitaḥ ||44||

45sannaddhaścārjuno yoddhā teṣāṃ nāsti parājayaḥ
|
teṣāmatha vilāpānāṃ nāyaṃ duryodhanaḥ smaret ||45||

46hatau hi puruṣavyāghrau bhīṣmadroṇau tvamāttha vai
|
teṣāṃ viduravākyānāṃ muktānāṃ dīrghadarśanāt ||46||

47dṛṣṭvemāṃ phalanirvṛttiṃ manye śocanti putrakāḥ
|
senāṃ dṛṣṭvā'bhibhūtā me śainaiyenārjunena ca ||47||

48śunyān dṛṣṭvā rathopasthān manye śocanti putrakāḥ
|
himātyaye yathā kakṣaṃ śuṣkaṃ vāterito mahān ||48||

49agnirdahettathā senāṃ māmikāṃ sa dhanañjayaḥ
|
ācakṣva mama tatsarvaṃ kuśalo hyasi sañjaya ||49||

50yadupāyāta sāyāhne kṛtvā pārthasya kilbiṣam
|
abhimanyau hate tāta kathamāsīnmano hi vaḥ ||50||

51na jātu tasya karmāṇi yudhi gāṇḍīvadhanvanaḥ
|
apakṛtya mahattāta soḍhuṃ śakṣyanti māmakāḥ ||51||

52kinnu duryodhanaḥ kṛtyaṃ karṇaḥ kṛtyaṃ kimabravīt
|
duḥśāsanaḥ saubalaśca teṣāmevaṃ gateṣvapi ||52||

53sarveṣāṃ samavetānāṃ putrāṇāṃ mama sañjaya
|
yadvṛttaṃ tāta saṃgrāme mandasyāpanayairbhṛśam ||53||

54lobhānugasya durbuddheḥ krodhena vikṛtātmanaḥ
|
rājyakāmasya mūḍhasya rāgopahatacetasaḥ
|
durnītaṃ vā sunītaṃ vā tanmamācakṣva sañjaya ||54||
