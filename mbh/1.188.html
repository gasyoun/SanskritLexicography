Mahābhārata 1.188 (Ādiparvan)

1vaiśaṃpāyana uvāca|
yadā nivṛttā rājāno dhanuṣaḥ sajyakarmaṇaḥ
|
athodatiṣṭhadviprāṇāṃ madhyājjiṣṇurudāradhīḥ||1||

2udakrośanvipramukhyā vidhunvanto'jināni ca
|
dṛṣṭvā saṃprasthitaṃ pārthamindraketusamaprabham||2||

3kecidāsanvimanasaḥ kecidāsanmudānvitāḥ
|
āhuḥ parasparaṃ kecinnipuṇā buddhijīvinaḥ||3||

4yatkarṇaśalyapramukhaiḥ kṣatriyairlokaviśrutaiḥ
|
nānataṃ balavadbhirhi dhanurvedaparāyaṇaiḥ||4||

5tatkathaṃ tvakṛtāstreṇa prāṇato durbalīyasā
|
vaṭumātreṇa śakyaṃ hi sajyaṃ kartuṃ dhanurdvijāḥ||5||

6avahāsyā bhaviṣyanti brāhmaṇāḥ sarvarājasu
|
karmaṇyasminnasaṃsiddhe cāpalādaparīkṣite||6||

7yadyeṣa darpāddharṣādvāpyatha brāhmaṇacāpalāt
|
prasthito dhanurāyantuṃ vāryatāṃ sādhu mā gamat||7||

8brāhmaṇā ūcuḥ|
nāvahāsyā bhaviṣyāmo na ca lāghavamāsthitāḥ
|
na ca vidviṣṭatāṃ loke gamiṣyāmo mahīkṣitām||8||

9kecidāhuryuvā śrīmānnāgarājakaropamaḥ
|
pīnaskandhorubāhuśca dhairyeṇa himavāniva||9||

10siṃhakhelagatiḥ śrīmānmattanāgendravikramaḥ
|
saṃbhāvyamasminkarmedamutsāhāccānumīyate||10||

11śaktirasya mahotsāhā na hyaśaktaḥ svayaṃ vrajet
|
na ca tadvidyate kiṃcitkarma lokeṣu yadbhavet||11||

12brāhmaṇānāmasādhyaṃ ca nṛṣu saṃsthānacāriṣu
|
abbhakṣā vāyubhakṣāśca phalāhārā dṛḍhavratāḥ||12||

13durbalā api viprā hi balīyāṃsaḥ svetajasā
|
brāhmaṇo nāvamantavyaḥ sadasadvā samācaran||13||

14sukhaṃ duḥkhaṃ mahaddhrasvaṃ karma yatsamupāgatam
|
jāmadagnyena rāmeṇa nirjitāḥ kṣatriyā yudhi||14||

15pītaḥ samudro'gastyena agādho brahmatejasā
|
tasmādbruvantu sarve'tra vaṭureṣa dhanurmahān||15||

16āropayatu śīghraṃ vai tathetyūcurdvijarṣabhāḥ
|
evaṃ teṣāṃ vilapatāṃ viprāṇāṃ vividhā giraḥ||16||

17arjuno dhanuṣo'bhyāśe tasthau giririvācalaḥ
|
sa taddhanuḥ parikramya pradakṣiṇamathākarot||17||

18praṇamya śirasā devamīśānaṃ varadaṃ prabhum
|
kṛṣṇaṃ ca manasā kṛtvā jagṛhe cārjuno dhanuḥ||18||

19yatpārthivai rukmasunīthavaktraiḥ rādheyaduryodhanaśalyasālvaiḥ
|
tadā dhanurvedaparairnṛsiṃhaiḥ kṛtaṃ na sajyaṃ mahato'pi yatnāt||19||

20tadarjuno vīryavatāṃ sadarpastadaindririndrāvarajaprabhāvaḥ
|
sajyaṃ ca cakre nimiṣāntareṇa śarāṃśca jagrāha daśārdasaṅkhyān||20||

21vivyādha lakṣyaṃ nipapāta tacca chidreṇa bhūmau sahasātividdham
|
tato'ntarikṣe ca babhūva nādaḥ samājamadhye ca mahānninādaḥ||21||

22puṣpāṇi divyāni vavarṣa devaḥ pārthasya mūrdhni dviṣatāṃ nihantuḥ||22||

23cailāni vivyadhustatra brāhmaṇāśca sahasraśaḥ
|
vilakṣitāstataścakrurhāhākārāṃśca sarvaśaḥ
|
nyapataṃścātra nabhasaḥ samantātpuṣpavṛṣṭayaḥ||23||

24śatāṅgāni ca tūryāṇi vādakāḥ samavādayan
|
sūtamāgadhasaṅghāścāpyastuvaṃstatra susvarāḥ||24||

25taṃ dṛṣṭvā drupadaḥ prīto babhūva ripusūdanaḥ
|
saha sainyaiśca pārthasya sāhāyyārthamiyeṣa saḥ||25||

26tasmiṃstu śabde mahati pravṛddhe yudhiṣṭhiro dharmabhṛtāṃ variṣṭhaḥ
|
āvāsamevopajagāma śīghraṃ sārdhaṃ yamābhyāṃ puruṣottamābhyām||26||

27viddhaṃ tu lakṣyaṃ prasamīkṣya kṛṣṇā pārthaṃ ca śakrapratimaṃ nirīkṣya
|
ādāya śuklāmbaramālyadāma jāgāma kuntīsutamutsmayantī||27||

28sa tāmupādāya vijitya raṅge dvijātibhistairabhipūjyamānaḥ
|
raṅgānnirakrāmadacintyakarmā patnyā tayā cāpyanugamyamānaḥ||28||
