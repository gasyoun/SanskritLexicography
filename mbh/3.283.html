Mahābhārata 3.283 (Āraṇyakaparvan)

1mārkaṇḍeya uvāca|
tatastatraiva rāmasya samāsīnasya taiḥ saha
|
samājagmuḥ kapiśreṣṭhāḥ sugrīvavacanāt tadā||1||

2vṛtaḥ koṭisahasreṇa vānarāṇāṃ tarasvinām
|
śvaśuro vālinaḥ śrīmān suṣeṇo rāmamabhyayāt||2||

3koṭīśatavṛto vā'pi gajo gavaya eva ca
|
vānarendrau mahāvīryau pṛthak pṛthagadṛśyatām||3||

4ṣaṣṭikoṭisahastrāṇi prakarṣan pratyadṛśyata
|
golāṅgūlo mahārāja gavākṣo bhīmadarśanaḥ||4||

5gandhamādanavāsī tu prathito gandhamādanaḥ
|
koṭī śatasasrāṇi harīṇāṃ samakarṣata||5||

6panaso nāma medhāvī vānaraḥ sumahābalaḥ
|
koṭīrdaśa dvādaśa ca triṃśatpañca prakarṣati||6||

7śrīmān dadhimukho nāma harivṛddho'tivīryavān
|
pracakarṣa mahāsainyaṃ harīṇāṃ bhīmatejasām||7||

8kṛṣṇānāṃ mukhapuṇḍrāṇāmṛkṣāṇāṃ bhīmakarmaṇām
|
koṭīśatasahasreṇa jāmbavān pratyadṛśyata||8||

9ete cānye ca bahavo hariyūthapayūthapāḥ
|
asaṅkhyeyā mahārāja samīyū rāmakāraṇāt||9||

10girikūṭanibhāṅgānāṃ siṃhānāmiva garjatām
|
śrūyate tumulaḥ śabdastatra tatra pradhāvatām||10||

11girikūṭanibhāḥ kecit kecinmahiṣasannibhāḥ
|
śaradabhrapratīkāśāḥ keciddhiṅgulakānanāḥ||11||

12utpatantaḥ patantaśca plavamānāśca vānarāḥ
|
uddhunvanto'pare reṇūn samājagmuḥ samantataḥ||12||

13sa vānaramahāsainyaḥ pūrṇasāgarasannibhaḥ
|
niveśamakarot tatra sugrīvānumate tadā||13||

14tatasteṣu harīndreṣu samāvṛtteṣu sarvaśaḥ
|
tithau praśaste nakṣatre muhūrte cābhipūjite||14||

15tena vyūḍhena sainyena lokānuddhartayanniva
|
prayayau rāghavaḥ śrīmān sugrīvasahitastadā||15||

16mukhamāsīt tu sainyasya hanūmān mārutātmajaḥ
|
jadhanaṃ pālayāmāsa saumitrirakutobhayaḥ||16||

17baddhagodhāṅgulitrāṇau rāghavau tatra jagmatuḥ
|
vṛtau harimahāmātraiścandrasūryau grahairiva||17||

18pravabhau harisainyaṃ tat sālatālaśilāyudham
|
sumahacchālibhavanaṃ yathā sūryodayaṃ prati||18||

19nalanīlāṅgadakrāthamaindadvividapālitā
|
yayau sumahatī senā rāghavasyārthasiddhaye||19||

20vividheṣu praśasteṣu bahumūlaphaleṣu ca
|
prabhūtamadhumāṃseṣu vārimatsu śiveṣu ca||20||

21nivasantī nirābādhā tathaiva girisānuṣu
|
upāyāddharisenā sā kṣārodamatha sāgaram||21||

22dvitīyasāgaranibhaṃ tadbalaṃ bahuladhvajam
|
velāvanaṃ samāsādya nivāsamakarot tadā||22||

23tato dāśarathiḥ śrīmān sugrīvaṃ pratyabhāṣata
|
madhye vānaramukhyānāṃ prāptakālamidaṃ vacaḥ||23||

24upāyaḥ ko nu bhavatāṃ mataḥ sāgaralaṅghane
|
iyaṃ hi mahatī senā sāgaraścātidustaraḥ||24||

25tatrānye vyāharanti sma vānarā bahumāninaḥ
|
samarthā laṅghane sindhorna tu tatkṛtsnakārakam||25||

26kecinnaubhirvyavasyanti kecicca vividhaiḥ plavaiḥ
|
neti rāmastu tān sarvān sāntvayan pratyabhāṣata||26||

27śatayojanavistāraṃ na śaktāḥ sarvavānarāḥ
|
krāntuṃ toyanidhiṃ vīrā naiṣā vo naiṣṭhikī matiḥ||27||

28nāvo na santi senāyā bavhyastārayituṃ tathā
|
vaṇijāmupaghātaṃ ca kathamasmadvidhaścaret||28||

29vistīrṇaṃ caiva naḥ sainyaṃ hanyācchidreṇa vai paraḥ
|
plavoḍupapratāraśca naivātra mama rocate||29||

30ahaṃ tvimaṃ jalanidhiṃ samārapsyāmyupāyataḥ
|
pratiśeṣyāpyupavasan darśayiṣyati māṃ tataḥ||30||

31na ceddarśayitā mārgaṃ dhakṣyāmyenamahaṃ tataḥ
|
mahāstrairapratihatairatyagnipavanojjvalaiḥ||31||

32ityuktvā saha saumitrirupaspṛśyātha rāghavaḥ
|
pratiśiśye jalanidhiṃ vidhivat kuśasaṃstare||32||

33sāgarastu tataḥ svapne darśayāmāsa rāghavam
|
devo nadanadībhartā śrīmān yādogaṇairvṛtaḥ||33||

34kausalyāmātarityevamābhāṣya madhuraṃ vacaḥ
|
idamityāha ratnānāmākaraiḥ śataśo vṛtaḥ||34||

35brūhi kiṃ te karomyatra sāhāyyaṃ puruṣarṣabha
|
aikṣvāko hyasmi te jñātiriti rāmastamabravīt||35||

36mārgamicchāmi sainyasya dattaṃ nadanadīpate
|
yena gatvā daśagrīvaṃ hanyāṃ paulastyapāṃsanam||36||

37yadyevaṃ yācato mārgaṃ na pradāsyati me bhavān
|
śaraistvāṃ śoṣayiṣyāmi divyāstrapratimantritaiḥ||37||

38ityevaṃ bruvataḥ śrutvā rāmasya varuṇālayaḥ
|
uvāca vyathito vākyamiti baddhāñjaliḥ sthitaḥ||38||

39necchāmi pratighātaṃ te nāsmi vighnakarastava
|
śṛṇu cedaṃ vaco rāma śrutvā kartavyamācara||39||

40yadi dāsyāmi te mārgaṃ sainyasya vrajato''jñayā
|
anye'pyājñāpayiṣyanti māmevaṃ dhanuṣo balāt||40||

41asti tvatra nalo nāma vānaraḥ śilpisammataḥ
|
tvaṣṭurdevasya tanayo balavān viśvakarmaṇaḥ||41||

42sa yat kāṣṭhaṃ tṛṇaṃ vāpi śilāṃ vā kṣepsyate mayi
|
sarvaṃ taddhārayiṣyāmi sa te seturbhaviṣyati||42||

43ityuktvā'ntarhite tasmin rāmo nalamuvāca ha
|
kuru setuṃ samudre tvaṃ śakto hyasi mato mama||43||

44tenopāyena kākutsthaḥ setubandhamakārayat
|
daśayojanavistāramāyataṃ śatayojanam||44||

45nalaseturitikhyāto yo'dyāpi prathito bhuvi
|
rāmasyājñāṃ puraskṛtya niryāto girisannibhaḥ||45||

46tatrasthaṃ sa tu dharmātmā samāgacchadvibhīṣaṇaḥ
|
bhrātā vai rākṣasendrasya caturbhiḥ sacivaiḥ saha||46||

47pratijagrāha rāmastaṃ svāgatena mahāmanāḥ
|
sugrīvasya tu śaṅkā'bhūt praṇidhiḥ syāditi sma ha||47||

48rāghavaḥ satyaceṣṭābhiḥ samyakca cariteṅgitaiḥ
|
yadā tattvena tuṣṭo'bhūt tata enamapūjayat||48||

49sarvarākṣasarājye cāpyabhyaṣiñcadvimīṣaṇam
|
cakre ca mantrasacivaṃ suhṛdaṃ lakṣmaṇasya ca||49||

50vibhīṣaṇamate caiva so'tyakrāmanmahārṇavam
|
sasainyaḥ setunā tena māsenaiva narādhipa||50||

51tato gatvā samāsādya laṅkodyānānyanekaśaḥ
|
bhedayāmāsa kapibhirmahānti ca bahūni ca||51||

52tatastau rāvaṇāmātyau mantriṇau śukasāraṇau
|
carau vānararūpeṇa tau jagrāha vibhīṣaṇaḥ||52||

53pratipannau yadā rūpaṃ rākṣasaṃ tau niśācarau
|
darśayitvā tataḥ sainyaṃ rāmaḥ paścādavāsṛjat||53||

54niveśyopavane sainyaṃ tatpuraḥ prājñavānaram
|
preṣayāmāsa dautyena rāvaṇasya tato'ṅgadam||54||
