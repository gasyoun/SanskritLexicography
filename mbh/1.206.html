Mahābhārata 1.206 (Ādiparvan)

1dhṛtarāṣṭra uvāca|
bhīṣmaḥ śāntanavo vidvāndroṇaśca bhagavānṛṣiḥ
|
hitaṃ ca paramaṃ vākyaṃ tvaṃ ca satyaṃ bravīṣi mām||1||

2yathaiva pāṇḍoste vīrāḥ kuntīputrā mahārathāḥ
|
tathaiva dharmataḥ sarve mama putrā na saṃśayaḥ||2||

3yathaiva mama putrāṇāmidaṃ rājyaṃ vidhīyate
|
tathaiva pāṇḍuputrāṇāmidaṃ rājyaṃ na saṃśayaḥ||3||

4kṣattarānaya gacchaitānsaha mātrā susatkṛtān
|
tayā ca devarūpiṇyā kṛṣṇayā saha bhārata||4||

5diṣṭyā jīvanti te pārthā diṣṭyā jīvati sā pṛthā
|
diṣṭyā drupadakanyāṃ ca labdhavanto mahārathāḥ||5||

6diṣṭyā vardhāmahe sarve diṣṭyā śāntaḥ purocanaḥ
|
diṣṭyā mama paraṃ duḥkhamapanītaṃ mahādyute||6||

7vaiśaṃpāyana uvāca|
tato jagāma viduro dhṛtarāṣṭrasya śāsanāt
|
sakāśaṃ yajñasenasya pāṇḍavānāṃ ca bhārata||7||

8samupādāya ratnāni vasūni vividhāni ca
|
draupadyāḥ pāṇḍavānāṃ ca yajñasenasya caiva ha||8||

9tatra gatvā sa dharmajñaḥ sarvaśāstraviśāradaḥ
|
drupadaṃ nyāyato rājansaṃyuktamupatasthivān||9||

10sa cāpi pratijagrāha dharmeṇa viduraṃ tataḥ
|
cakratuśca yathānyāyaṃ kuśalapraśnasaṃvidam||10||

11dadarśa pāṇḍavāṃstatra vāsudevaṃ ca bhārata
|
snehātpariṣvajya sa tānpapracchānāmayaṃ tataḥ||11||

12taiścāpyamitabuddiḥ sa pūjito hi yathākramam
|
vacanāddhṛtarāṣṭrasya snehayuktaṃ punaḥpunaḥ||12||

13papracchānāmayaṃ rājaṃstatastānpāṇḍunandanān
|
pradadau cāpi ratnāni vividhāni vasūni ca||13||

14pāṇḍavānāṃ ca kuntyāśca draupadyāśca viśāṃpate
|
drupadasya ca putrāṇāṃ yathā dattāni kauravaiḥ||14||

15provāca cāmitamatiḥ praśritaṃ vinayānvitaḥ
|
drupadaṃ pāṇḍuputrāṇāṃ sannidhau keśavasya ca||15||

16vidura uvāca|
rājañcchṛṇu sahāmātyaḥ saputraśca vaco mama
|
dhṛtarāṣṭraḥ saputrastvāṃ sahāmātyaḥ sabāndhavaḥ||16||

17abravītkuśalaṃ rājanprīyamāṇaḥ punaḥpunaḥ
|
prītimāṃste dṛḍhaṃ cāpi saṃbandhena narādhipa||17||

18tathā bhīṣmaḥ śāntanavaḥ kauravaiḥ saha sarvaśaḥ
|
kuśalaṃ tvāṃ mahāprājñaḥ sarvataḥ paripṛcchati||18||

19bhāradvājo mahāprājño droṇaḥ priyasakhastava
|
samāśleṣamupetya tvāṃ kuśalaṃ paripṛcchati||19||

20dhṛtarāṣṭraśca pāñcālya tvayā saṃbandhameyivān
|
kṛtārthaṃ manyatetmānaṃ tathā sarve'pi kauravāḥ||20||

21na tathā rājyasaṃprāptisteṣāṃ prītikarī matā
|
yathā saṃbandhakaṃ prāpya yajñasena tvayā saha||21||

22etadviditvā tu bhavānprasthāpayatu pāṇḍavān
|
draṣṭuṃ hi pāṇḍuputrāṃśca tvaranti kuravo bhṛśam||22||

23viproṣitā dīrghakālamete cāpi nararṣabhāḥ
|
utsukā nagaraṃ draṣṭuṃ bhaviṣyanti tathā pṛthā||23||

24kṛṣṇāmapi ca pāñcālīṃ sarvāḥ kuruvarastriyaḥ
|
draṣṭukāmāḥ pratīkṣte puraṃ ca viṣayāśca naḥ||24||

25sa bhavānpāṇḍuputrāṇāmājñāpayatu mā ciram
|
gamanaṃ sahadārāṇāmetadatra mataṃ mama||25||

26nisṛṣṭeṣu tvayā rājanpāṇḍaveṣu mahātmasu
|
tato'haṃ preṣayiṣyāmi dhṛtarāṣṭrasya śīghragān
|
āgamiṣyanti kaunteyāḥ kuntī ca saha kṛṣṇayā||26||
