Mahābhārata 5.117 (Udyogaparvan)

1gālaya uvāca|
mahāvīryo mahīpālaḥ kāśīnāmīśvaraḥ prabhuḥ
|
divodāsa iti khyāto bhaimasenirnarādhipaḥ ||1||

2tatra gacchāvahe bhadre śanairāgaccha mā śucaḥ
|
dhārmikaḥ saṃyame yuktaḥ satye caiva janeśvaraḥ ||2||

3nārada uvāca|
tamupāgamya sa munirnyāyatastena satkṛtaḥ
|
gālavaḥ prasavasyārthe taṃ nṛpaṃ pratyacodayat ||3||

4divodāsa uvāca|
śrutametanmayā pūrvaṃ kimuktvā vistaraṃ dvija
|
kāṅkṣito hi mayaiṣo'rthaḥ śrutvaiva dvijasattama ||4||

5etacca me bahumataṃ yadutsṛjya narādhipān
|
māmevamupayāto'si bhāvi caitadasaṃśayam ||5||

6sa eva vibhavo'smākamaśvānāmapi gālava
|
ahamapyekamevāsyāṃ janayiṣyāmi pārthivam ||6||

7tathetyuktvā dvijaśreṣṭhaḥ prādāt kanyāṃ mahīpateḥ
|
vidhipūrvāṃ ca tāṃ rājā kanyāṃ pratigṛhītavān ||7||

8reme sa tasyāṃ rājarṣiḥ prabhāvatyāṃ yathā raviḥ
|
svāhāyāṃ ca yathā vahniryathā śacyāṃ ca vāsavaḥ ||8||

9yathā candraśca rohiṇyāṃ yathā dhūmorṇayā yamaḥ
|
varuṇaśca yathā gauryāṃ yathā carddhyāṃ dhaneśvaraḥ ||9||

10yathā nārāyaṇo lakṣmyāṃ jāhnavyāṃ ca yathodadhiḥ
|
yathā rudraśca rudrāṇyāṃ yathā vedyāṃ pitāmahaḥ ||10||

11adṛśyantyāṃ ca vāsiṣṭho vasiṣṭhaścākṣamālayā
|
cyavanaśca sukanyāyāṃ pulastyaḥ sandhyayā yathā ||11||

12agastyaścāpi vaidarbhyāṃ sāvitryāṃ satyavān yathā
|
yathā bhṛguḥ pulomāyāmadityāṃ kaśyapo yathā ||12||

13reṇukāyāṃ yathārcīko haimavatyāṃ ca kauśikaḥ
|
bṛhaspatiśca tārāyāṃ śukraśca śataparvaṇā ||13||

14yathā bhūmyāṃ bhūmipatirurvaśyāṃ ca purūravāḥ
|
ṛcīkaḥ satyavatyāṃ ca sarasvatyāṃ yathā manuḥ||14||

15śakuntalāyāṃ duṣyanto dhṛtyāṃ dharmaśca śāśvataḥ
|
damayantyāṃ nalaścaiva satyavatyāṃ ca nāradaḥ ||15||

16jaratkārurjaratkārvāṃ pulastyaśca pratīcyayā
|
menakāyāṃ yathorṇāyustumburuścaiva rambhayā ||16||

17vāsukiḥ śataśīrṣāyāṃ kumāryāṃ ca dhanañjayaḥ
|
vaidehyāṃ ca yathā rāmo rukmiṇyāṃ ca janārdanaḥ ||17||

18tathā tu ramamāṇasya divodāsasya bhūpateḥ
|
mādhavī janayāmāsa putramekaṃ pratardanam ||18||

19athājagāma bhagavān divodāsaṃ sa gālavaḥ
|
samaye samanuprāpte vacanaṃ cedamabravīt ||19||

20niryātayatu me kanyāṃ bhavāṃstiṣṭhantu vājinaḥ
|
yāvadanyatra gacchāmi śulkārthaṃ pṛthivīpate ||20||

21divodāso'tha dharmātmā samaye gālavasya tām
|
kanyāṃ niryātayāmāsa sthitaḥ satye mahīpatiḥ ||21||
