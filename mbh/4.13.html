Mahābhārata 4.13 (Virāṭaparvan)

1janamejaya uvāca|evaṃ te matsyanagare pracchannāḥ kurunandanāḥ
|
ata ūrdhvaṃ mahāvīryāḥ kimakurvata vai dvija ||1||

2vaiśampāyana uvāca|evaṃ matsyasya nagare pracchannāḥ kurunandanāḥ
|
ārādhayanto rājānaṃ yadakurvata tacchṛṇu ||2||

3tṛṇabinduprasādācca dharmasya ca mahātmanaḥ
|
ajñātavāsamevaṃ tu virāṭanagare'vasan ||3||

4yudhiṣṭhiraḥ sabhāstāro matsyānāmabhavat priyaḥ
|
tathaiva ca virāṭasya saputrasya viśāmpate ||4||

5sa hyakṣahṛdayajñastān krīḍayāmāsa pāṇḍavaḥ
|
akṣavatyāṃ yathākāmaṃ sūtrabaddhāniva dvijān ||5||

6ajñātaṃ ca virāṭasya vijitya vasu dharmarāṭ
|
bhrātṛbhyaḥ puruṣavyāghro yathārhaṃ saṃprayacchati ||6||

7bhīmaseno'pi māṃsāni bhakṣyāṇi vividhāni ca
|
atisṛṣṭāni matsyena vikrīṇīte yudhiṣṭhire ||7||

8vāsāṃsi parijīrṇāni labdhānyantaḥpure'rjunaḥ
|
vikrīṇānaśca sarvebhyaḥ pāṇḍavebhyaḥ prayacchati ||8||

9sahadevo'pi gopānāṃ veṣamāsthāya pāṇḍavaḥ
|
dadhi kṣīraṃ ghṛtaṃ caiva pāṇḍavebhyaḥ prayacchati ||9||

10nakulo'pi dhanaṃ labdhvā kṛte karmaṇi vājinām
|
tuṣṭe tasminnarapatau pāṇḍavebhyaḥ prayacchati ||10||

11kṛṣṇā tu sarvān bhartṝṃstānnirīkṣantī tapasvinī
|
yathā punaravijñātā tathā carati bhāminī ||11||

12evaṃ sampādayantaste tadā'nyonyaṃ mahārathāḥ
|
virāṭanagare ceruḥ punargarbhadhṛtā iva ||12||

13sāśaṅkā dhārtarāṣṭrasya bhayāt pāṇḍusutāstadā
|
prekṣamāṇāstadā kṛṣṇāmūṣuśchannā narādhipa ||13||

14atha māse caturthe tu brahmaṇaḥ sumahotsavaḥ
|
āsīt samṛddho matsyeṣu puruṣāṇāṃ susaṃmataḥ ||14||

15tatra mallāḥ samāpeturdigbhyo rājan sahasraśaḥ
|
samāje brahmaṇo rājan yathā paśupateriva ||15||

16mahākāyā mahāvīryāḥ kālakhañjā ivāsurāḥ
|
vīryonmattā valodagrā rājñā samabhipūjitāḥ ||16||

17siṃhaskandhakaṭigrīvāḥ svavadātā manasvinaḥ
|
asakṛllabdhalakṣāste raṅge pārthivasannidhau ||17||

18teṣāmeko mahānāsīt sarvamallānathāhvayat
|
āvalgamānaṃ taṃ raṅge nopatiṣṭhati kaścana ||18||

19yadā sarve vimanasaste mallā hatacetasaḥ
|
atha sūdena taṃ mallaṃ yodhayāmāsa matsyarāṭ ||19||

20nodyamānastadā bhīmo duḥkhenaivākaronmatim
|
na hi śaknoti vivṛte pratyākhyātuṃ narādhipam ||20||

21tataḥ sa puruṣavyāghraḥ śārdūlaśithilaścaran
|
praviveśa mahāraṅgaṃ virāṭamabhipūjayan ||21||

22babandha kakṣāṃ kaunteyastataḥ saṃharṣayan janam
|
tatastu vṛtrasaṅkāśaṃ bhīmo mallaṃ samāhvayat ||22||

23jīmūtaṃ nāma taṃ tatra mallaṃ prakhyātavikramam
|
tāvubhau sumahotsāhāvubhau bhīmaparākramau ||23||

24mattāviva mahākāyau vāraṇau ṣaṣṭihāyanau
|
tatastau naraśārdūlau bāhuyuddhaṃ samīyatuḥ ||24||

25vīrau paramasaṃhṛṣṭāvanyonyajayakāṅkṣiṇau
|
āsīt subhīmaḥ saṃpāto vajraparvatayoriva ||25||

26ubhau paramasaṃhṛṣṭau balenātibalāvubhau
|
anyonyasyāntaraṃ prepsū parasparajayaiṣiṇau ||26||

27ubhau paramasaṃhṛṣṭau mattāviva mahāgajau
|
kṛtapratikṛtaiścitrairbāhubhiśca susaṅkaṭaiḥ
|
sannipātāvadhūtaiśca pramāthonmathanaistathā ||27||

28kṣepaṇairmuṣṭibhiścaiva varāhoddhūtaniḥsvanaiḥ
|
talairvajranipātaiśca prasṛṣṭābhistathaiva ca ||28||

29śalākānakhapātaiśca pādoddhūtaiśca dāruṇaiḥ
|
jānubhiścāśmanirghoṣaiḥ śirobhiścāvaghaṭṭa‌naiḥ ||29||

30tadyuddhamabhavaddhoramaśastraṃ bāhutejasā
|
balaprāṇena śūrāṇāṃ samājotsavasannidhau ||30||

31arajyata janaḥ sarvaḥ sotkṛṣṭaninadotthitaḥ
|
bālanoḥ saṃyuge rājan vṛtravāsavayoriva ||31||

32prakarṣaṇākarṣaṇayorabhyākarṣavikarṣaṇaiḥ
|
ākarṣaturathānyonyaṃ jānubhiścāpi jaghnatuḥ ||32||

33tataḥ śabdena mahatā bhartsayantau parasparam
|
vyūḍhoraskau dīrghabhujau niyuddhakuśalāvubhau
|
bāhubhiḥ samasajjetāmāyasaiḥ parighairiva ||33||

34cakarṣa dorjyāmutpātya bhīmo mallamamitrahā
|
ninadantamabhikrośan śārdūla iva vāraṇam ||34||

35samudyamya mahābāhurbhrāmayāmāsa vīryavān
|
tato mallāśca matsyāśca vismayaṃ cakrire param ||35||

36bhrāmayittvā śataguṇaṃ gatasattvamacetanam
|
pratyarpiṣanmahābāhurmallaṃ bhuvi vṛkodaraḥ ||36||

37tasmin vinihate vīre jīmūte lokaviśrute
|
virāṭaḥ paramaṃ harṣamagacchadbāndhavaiḥ saha ||37||

38praharṣāt pradadau vittaṃ bahu rājā mahāmanāḥ
|
ballavāya mahāraṅge yathā vaiśravaṇastathā ||38||

39evaṃ sa subahūn mallān puruṣāṃśca mahābalān
|
vinighnan matsyarājasya prītimāharaduttamām ||39||

40yadāsya tulyaḥ puruṣo na kaścittatra vidyate
|
tato vyāghraiśca siṃhaiśca dviradaiścāpyayodhayat ||40||

41punarantaḥpuragataḥ strīṇāṃ madhye vṛkodaraḥ
|
yodhyate sa virāṭena siṃhairmattairmahābalaiḥ ||41||

42bībhatsurapi gītena svanṛtyena ca pāṇḍavaḥ
|
virāṭaṃ toṣayāmāsa sarvāścāntaḥpurastriyaḥ ||42||

43aśvairvinītairjavanaistatra tatra samāgataiḥ
|
toṣayāmāsa rājānaṃ nakulo nṛpasattamam ||43||

44tasmai pradeyaṃ prāyacchat prīto rājā dhanaṃ bahu
|
vinītān vṛṣabhān dṛṣṭvā sahadevasya cābhitaḥ
|
dhanaṃ dadau vahuvidhaṃ virāṭaḥ puruṣarṣabhaḥ ||44||

45draupadī prekṣya tānsarvān kliśyamānānmahārathān
|
nātiprītamanā rājanniḥśvāsaparamā'bhavat ||45||

46evaṃ te nyavasaṃstatra pracchannāḥ puruṣarṣabhāḥ
|
karmāṇi tasya kurvāṇā virāṭanṛpatestadā ||46||
