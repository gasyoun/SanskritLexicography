Mahābhārata 8.55 (Karṇaparvan)

1sañjaya uvāca|
drauṇiryudhiṣṭhiraṃ dṛṣṭvā śaineyenābhirakṣitam
|
draupadeyaistathā śūrairabhyavartata hṛṣṭavat ||1||

2kiranniṣugaṇān ghorān svarṇapuṅkhāñśilāśitān
|
darśayan vividhān mārgān śikṣāśca laghuhastavat ||2||

3tataḥ khaṃ pūrayāmāsa śarairdivyāstramantritaiḥ
|
yudhiṣṭhiraṃ ca samare parivārya mahāstravit ||3||

4drauṇāyaniśaracchannaṃ na prājñāyata kiñcana
|
bāṇabhūtamabhūtsarvamāyodhanaśiro mahat ||4||

5bāṇajālaṃ divi cchannaṃ svarṇajālavibhūṣitam
|
śuśubhe bharataśreṣṭha vitānamiva dhiṣṭhitam ||5||

6tena cchanna nabho rājanbāṇajālena bhāsvatā
|
abhracchāyeva sañjajñe bāṇaruddhe nabhastale ||6||

7tatrāścaryamapaśyāma bāṇabhūte tathāvidhe
|
na sma saṃpatate bhūtaṃ kiñcidevāntarikṣagam ||7||

8sātyakiryatamānastu dharmarājaśca pāṇḍavaḥ
|
tathetarāṇi sainyāni na sma cakruḥ parākramam ||8||

9lāghavaṃ droṇaputrasya dṛṣṭvā tatra mahārathāḥ
|
vyasmayanta mahārāja na cainaṃ pratyudīkṣitum ||9||

10śekuste sarvarājānastapantamiva bhāskaram
|
vadhyamāne tataḥ sainye draupadeyā mahārathāḥ ||10||

11sātyakirdharmarājaśca pañcālāścāpi saṃgatāḥ
|
tyaktvā mṛtyubhayaṃ ghoraṃ drauṇāyanimupādravan ||11||

12sātyakiḥ saptaviṃśatyā drauṇiṃ viddhvā śilīmukhaiḥ
|
punarvivyādha nārācaiḥ saptabhiḥ svarṇabhūṣitaiḥ ||12||

13yudhiṣṭhirastrisaptatyā prativiṃdhyaśca saptabhiḥ
|
śrutakarmā tribhirbāṇaiḥ śrutakīrtiśca saptabhiḥ ||13||

14sutasomastu navabhiḥ śatānīkaśca saptabhiḥ
|
anye ca bahavaḥ śūrā vivyadhustaṃ samantataḥ ||14||

15sa tu kruddhastato rājannāśīviṣa iva śvasan
|
sātyakiṃ pañcaviṃśatyā prāvidhyata śilīmukhaiḥ ||15||

16śrutakīrtiṃ ca navabhiḥ sutasomaṃ ca pañcabhiḥ
|
aṣṭabhiḥ śrutakarmāṇaṃ prativiṃdhyaṃ tribhiḥ śaraiḥ ||16||

17śatānīkaṃ ca navabhirdharmaputraṃ ca pañcabhiḥ
|
tathetarāṃstataḥ śūrāndvābhyāṃ dvābhyāmatāḍayat ||17||

18śrutakīrtestathā cāpaṃ ciccheda niśitaiḥ śaraiḥ
|
athānyaddhanurādāya śrutikīrtirmahārathaḥ ||18||

19drauṇāyaniṃ tribhirviddhyā vivyādhānyaiḥ śitaiḥ śaraiḥ
|
tato drauṇirmahārāja śaravarṣeṇa māriṣa ||19||

20chādayāmāsa tat sainyaṃ samantādbharatarṣabha
|
tataḥ punarameyātmā dharmarājasya kārmukam ||20||

21drauṇiściccheda vihasanvivyādha ca śaraistribhiḥ
|
tato dharmasuto rājanpragṛhyānyanmahaddhanuḥ ||21||

22drauṇiṃ vivyādha saptatyā bāhvorurasi cārpayat
|
sātyakistu tataḥ kruddho drauṇeḥ praharato raṇe ||22||

23ardhacandreṇa tīkṣṇena dhanuśchittvā'nadadbhṛśam
|
chinnadhanvā tato drauṇiḥ śaktyā śaktimatāṃ varaḥ ||23||

24sārathiṃ pātayāmāsa śaineyasya rathāddrutam
|
athānyaddhanurādāya droṇaputraḥ pratāpavān ||24||

25śaineyaṃ śaravarṣeṇa cchādayāmāsa bhārata
|
tasyāśvāḥ pradrutāḥ saṃkhye patite rathasārathau ||25||

26tatra tatraiva dhāvantaḥ samadṛśyanta bhārata
|
yudhiṣṭhirapurogāstu drauṇiṃ śastrabhṛtāṃ varam ||26||

27abhyavarṣanta vegena visṛjantaḥ śitāñcharān
|
āgacchamānāṃstāndṛṣṭvā kruddharūpānparaṃtapaḥ ||27||

28prahasanpratijagrāha droṇaputro mahāraṇe
|
tataḥ śaraśatajvālaḥ senākakṣaṃ mahārathaḥ ||28||

29drauṇirdadāha samare kakṣamagniryathā vane
|
tadbalaṃ pāṇḍuputrasya droṇaputrapratāpitam ||29||

30cukṣubhe bharataśreṣṭha timineva nadīmukham
|
dṛṣṭvā caiva mahārāja droṇaputraparākramam ||30||

31nihatānmenire sarvānpāṇḍūn droṇasutena vai
|
yudhiṣṭhirastu tvarito droṇaśiṣyo mahārathaḥ ||31||

32abravīddroṇaputrāya rāṣāmarṣasamanvitaḥ
|
naiva nāma tava prītirnaiva nāma kṛtajñatā ||32||

33yatastvaṃ puruṣavyāghra māmevādya jighāṃsasi
|
brāhmaṇena tapaḥ kāryaṃ dānamadhyayanaṃ tathā ||33||

34kṣatriyeṇa dhanurnāmyaṃ sa bhavānbrāhmaṇabruvaḥ
|
miṣataste mahābāho yudhi jeṣyāmi kauravān ||34||

35kuruṣva samare karma brahmabandhurasi dhruvam
|
evamukto mahārāja droṇaputraḥ smayanniva ||35||

36yuktaṃ tattvaṃ ca saṃcintya nottaraṃ kiñcidabravīt
|
anuktvā ca tataḥ kiñciccharavarṣeṇa pāṇḍavam ||36||

37chādayāmāsa samare kruddho'ntaka iva prajāḥ
|
sa cchādyamānastu tadā droṇaputreṇa māriṣa ||37||

38pārtho'payātaḥ śīghraṃ vai vihāya mahatīṃ camūm
|
apayāte tatastasmin dharmaputre yudhiṣṭhire ||38||

39droṇaputrastato rājanpratyagātsa mahāmanāḥ
|
tato yudhiṣṭhiro rājaṃstyaktvā drauṇiṃ mahāhave
|
prayayau tāvakaṃ sainyaṃ yuktaḥ krūrāya karmaṇe ||39||
