Mahābhārata 10.2 (Sauptikaparvan)

1kṛpa uvāca|
śrutaṃ te vacanaṃ sarvaṃ yadyaduktaṃ tvayā vibho
|
mamāpi tu vacaḥ kiñcicchṛṇuṣvādya mahābhuja||1||

2ābaddhā mānuṣāḥ sarve nibaddhāḥ karmaṇordvayoḥ
|
daive puruṣakāre ca paraṃ tābhyāṃ na vidyate||2||

3na hi daivena sidhyanti kāryāṇyekena sattama
|
na cāpi karmaṇaikena dvābhyāṃ siddhistu yogataḥ||3||

4tābhyāmubhābhyāṃ sarvārthā nibaddhā adhamottamāḥ
|
pravṛttāścaiva dṛśyante nivṛttāścaiva sarvaśaḥ||4||

5parjanyaḥ parvate varṣankinnu sādhayate phalam
|
kuṣṭe kṣetre tathā varṣankinna sādhayate phalam||5||

6utthānaṃ cāpi devasya hyanutthānaṃ ca daivatam
|
vyarthaṃ bhavati sarvatra pūrvastatra viniścayaḥ||6||

7suvṛṣṭe ca yathā deve samyak kṣetre ca karṣite
|
bījaṃ mahāguṇaṃ bhūyāttathā siddhirhi mānuṣī||7||

8tayordaivaṃ viniścitya svayaṃ caiva pravartate
|
prājñāḥ puruṣakāretu vartante dākṣyamāśritāḥ||8||

9tābhyāṃ sarve hi kāryārthā manuṣyāṇāṃ nararṣabha
|
viceṣṭantaḥ sma dṛśyante nivṛttāstu tathaiva ca||9||

10kṛtaḥ puruṣakāraśca so'pi daivena sidhyati
|
tathā'sya karmaṇaḥ kartturabhinirvarttate phalam||10||

11utthānaṃ ca manuṣyāṇāṃ dakṣāṇāṃ daivavarjitam
|
aphalaṃ dṛśyate loke samyagapyupapāditam||11||

12tatrālasā manuṣyāṇāṃ ye bhavantyamanasvinaḥ
|
utthānaṃ te vigarhanti prājñānāṃ tanna rocate||12||

13prāyaśo hi kṛtaṃ karma nāphalaṃ dṛśyate bhuvi
|
akṛtvā ca punarduḥkhaṃ karma paśyenmahāphalam||13||

14ceṣṭāmakurvan labhate yadi kiñcidyadṛcchayā
|
yo vā na labhate kṛtvā durdarśau tāvubhāvapi||14||

15śaknoti jīvituṃ dakṣo nālasaḥ sukhamedhate
|
dṛśyante jīvaloke'smin dakṣāḥ prāyo hitaiṣiṇaḥ||15||

16yadi dakṣaḥ samārambhāt karmaṇo nāśrute phalam
|
nāsya vācyaṃ bhavetkiñcillabdhavyaṃ vā'dhigacchati||16||

17akṛtvā karma yo lokaṃ phalaṃ vindanti dhiṣṭhitaḥ
|
sa tu vaktavyatāṃ yāti dveṣyo bhavati bhūyaśaḥ||17||

18evametadanādṛtya vartate yastvato'nyathā
|
sa karotyātmano'narthāneṣa buddhimatāṃ nayaḥ||18||

19hīnaṃ puruṣakāreṇa yadi daivena vā punaḥ
|
kāraṇābhyāmathaitābhyāmutthānamaphalaṃ bhavet||19||

20hīnaṃ puruṣakāreṇa karma tviha na siddhyati
|
daivatebhyo namaskṛtya yastvarthānsamyagīhate||20||

21dakṣo dākṣiṇyasampanno na samoghairvihanyate
|
samyagīhā punariyaṃ yo vṛddhānupasevate||21||

22āpṛcchati ca yacchreyaḥ karoti ca hitaṃ vacaḥ
|
utthāyotthāya hi sadā praṣṭavyā vṛddhasammatāḥ||22||

23te sma yoge paraṃ mūlaṃ tanmūlā siddhirucyate
|
vṛddhānāṃ vacanaṃ śrutvā yo'bhyutthānaṃ prayojayet||23||

24utthānasya phalaṃ samyaktadā sa labhate'cirāt
|
rāgātkrodhādbhayāllobhādyo'rthānīhati mānavaḥ||24||

25anīśaścāvamānī ca sa śīghraṃ bhraśyate śriyaḥ
|
so'yaṃ duryodhanenārtho lubdhenādīrghadarśinā||25||

26asamarthaḥ samārabdho mūḍhatvādavicintitaḥ
|
hitabuddhīnanādṛtya sammantryāsādhubhiḥ saha||26||

27vāryamāṇo'karodvairaṃ pāṇḍavairguṇavattaraiḥ
|
pūrvamapyatiduḥśīlo na dhairyaṃ kartumarhati||27||

28tapatyarthe vipanne hi mitrāṇāṃ na kṛtaṃ vacaḥ
|
anuvarttāmahe yattu taṃ vayaṃ pāpapūruṣam||28||

29asmānapyanayastasmātprāptoyaṃ dāruṇo mahān
|
anena tu mamādyāpi vyasanenopatāpitā||29||

30budhdiścintayate kiñcit svaṃ śreyo nāvabuddhyate
|
muhyatā tu manuṣyeṇa praṣṭavyāḥ suhṛdo janāḥ||30||

31tatrāsya buddhirvinayastatra śreyaśca paśyati
|
tatosya mūlaṃ kāryāṇāṃ buddhyā niścitya vai budhāḥ||31||

32te'tra pṛṣṭā yathā brūyustatkartavyaṃ tathā bhavet
|
te vayaṃ dhṛtarāṣṭraṃ ca gāndhārīṃ ca sametya ha||32||

33upapṛcchāmahe gatvā viduraṃ ca mahāmatim
|
te pṛṣṭāstu vadeyuryacchreyo naḥ samanantaram||33||

34tadasmābhiḥ punaḥ kāryamiti me naiṣṭhikī matiḥ
|
anārambhāttu kāryāṇāṃ nārthaḥ sampadyate kvacit||34||

35kṛte puruṣakāre tu yeṣāṃ kāryaṃ na siddhyati
|
daivenāpahatāste tu nātra kāryā vicāraṇā||35||
