Mahābhārata 7.73 (Droṇaparvan)

1yudhiṣṭhira uvāca| tvayi yāte mahābāho saṃśaptakabalaṃ prati
|
prayatnamakarot tīvramācāryo grahaṇe mama ||1||

2vyūḍhānīkā vayaṃ droṇaṃ vārayāmaḥ sma sarvaśaḥ
|
prativyūhya rathānīkaṃ yatamānaṃ tathā raṇe ||2||

3sa vāryamāṇo rathibhirmayi cāpi surakṣite
|
asmānabhijagāmāśu pīḍayanniśitaiḥ śaraiḥ ||3||

4te pīḍyamānā droṇena droṇānīkaṃ na śaknumaḥ
|
prativīkṣitumapyājau bhettuṃ tat kuta eva tu ||4||

5vayaṃ tvapratimaṃ vīrye sarve saubhadramātmajam
|
uktavantaḥ sma taṃ tāta bhindhyanīkamiti prabho ||5||

6sa tathā nodito'smābhiḥ sadaśva iva vīryavān
|
asahyamapi taṃ bhāraṃ voḍhumevopacakrame ||6||

7sa tavāstropadeśena vīryeṇa ca samanvitaḥ
|
prāviśattadbalaṃ bālaḥ suparṇa iva sāgaram ||7||

8te'nuyātā vayaṃ vīraṃ sātvatīputramāhave
|
praveṣṭukāmāstenaiva yena prāviśaccamūm ||8||

9tataḥ saindhavako rājā kṣudrastāta jayadrathaḥ
|
varadānena rudrasya sarvānnaḥ samavārayat ||9||

10tato droṇaḥ kṛpaḥ karṇo drauṇiḥ kausalya eva ca
|
kṛtavarmā ca saubhadraṃ ṣaḍrathāḥ paryavārayan ||10||

11parivārya tu taiḥ sarvairyudhi bālo mahārathaiḥ
|
yatamānaḥ paraṃ śaktyā bahubhirvirathīkṛtaḥ ||11||

12tato dauḥśāsaniḥ kṣipraṃ tathā tairvirathīkṛtam
|
saṃśayaṃ paramaṃ prāpya diṣṭāntenābhyayojayat ||12||

13sa tu hatvā sahasrāṇi narāśvaratha‌dantinām
|
aṣṭau rathasahasrāṇi nava dantiśatāni ca ||13||

14rājaputrasahasre dve vīrāṃścālakṣitān bahūn
|
bṛhadbalaṃ ca rājānaṃ svargeṇājau prayojya ha ||14||

15tataḥ paramadharmātmā diṣṭāntamupajagmivān
|
etāvadeva nirvṛttamasmākaṃ śokavardhanam ||15||

16sa caivaṃ puruṣavyāghra svargalokamavāptavān
|
tato'rjuno vacaḥ śrutvā dharmarājeṇa bhāṣitam ||16||

17hā putra iti niḥśvasya vyathito nyapatadbhuvi
|
viṣaṇṇavadanāḥ sarve parivārya dhanañjayam ||17||

18netrairanimiṣairdīnāḥ pratyavaikṣan parasparam
|
pratilabhya tataḥ saṃjñāṃ vāsaviḥ krodhamūrcchitaḥ ||18||

19kampamāno jvareṇeva niḥśvasaṃśca muhurmuhuḥ
|
pāṇiṃ pāṇau viniṣpiṣya śvasamāno'śrunetravān ||19||

20unmatta iva viprekṣannidaṃ vacanamabravīt
|
arjuna uvāca| satyaṃ vaḥ pratijānāmi śvo'smi hantā jayadratham
|
na cedvadhabhayādbhīto dhārtarāṣṭrān prahāsyati ||20||

21na cāsmān śaraṇaṃ gacchet kṛṣṇaṃ vā puruṣottamam
|
bhavantaṃ vā mahārāja śvo'smi hantā jayadratham ||21||

22dhārtarāṣṭrapriyakaraṃ mayi vismṛtasauhṛdam
|
pāpaṃ bālavadhe hetuṃ śvo'smi hantā jayadratham ||22||

23rakṣamāṇāśca taṃ saṅkhye ye māṃ yotsyanti kecana
|
api droṇakṛpau rājan chādayiṣyāmi tāñcharaiḥ ||23||

24yadyetadevaṃ saṃgrāme na kuryāṃ puruṣarṣabhāḥ
|
mā sma puṇyakṛtāṃ lokānprāpnuyāṃ śūrasaṃmatān ||24||

25ye lokā mātṛhaṃtṝṇāṃ ye cāpi pitṛghātinām
|
gurudāragatānāṃ ye piśunānāṃ ca ye sadā ||25||

26sādhūnasūyatāṃ ye ca ye cāpi parivādinām
|
ye ca nikṣepahartṝṇāṃ ye ca viśvāsaghātinām ||26||

27bhuktapūrvāṃ striyaṃ ye ca vindatāmaghaśaṃsinām
|
brahmaghnānāṃ ca ye lokā ye ca goghātināmapi ||27||

28pāyasaṃ vā yavānnaṃ vā śākaṃ kṛsarameva vā
|
saṃyāvāpūpamāṃsāni ye ca lokā vṛthāśnatām ||28||

29tānanhāyādhigaccheyaṃ na ceddhanyāṃ jayadratham
|
vedādhyāyinamatyarthaṃ saṃśitaṃ vā dvijottamam ||29||

30avamanyamāno yān yāti vṛddhān sādhūn guruṃstathā
|
spṛśato brāhmaṇaṃ gāṃ ca pādenāgniṃ ca yā bhavet ||30||

31yā'psu śleṣma purīṣaṃ ca mūtraṃ vā muñcatāṃ gatiḥ
|
tāṃ gaccheyaṃ gatiṃ kaṣṭāṃ na ceddhanyāṃ jayadratham ||31||

32nagnasya snāyamānasya yā ca vandhyātithergatiḥ
|
utkocināṃ mṛṣoktīnāṃ vañcakānāṃ ca yā gatiḥ ||32||

33ātmāpahāriṇāṃ yā ca yā ca mithyābhiśaṃsinām
|
bhṛtyaiḥ saṃdiśyamānānāṃ putradārāśritaistathā ||33||

34asaṃvibhajya kṣudrāṇāṃ yā gatirmiṣṭamaśnatām
|
tāṃ gaccheyaṃ gātiṃ ghorāṃ na ceddhanyāṃ jayandratham ||34||

35saṃśritaṃ cāpi yastyaktvā sādhuṃ tadvacane ratam
|
na bibharti nṛśaṃsātmā nindate copakāriṇam ||35||

36arhate prātiveśyāya śraddhaṃ yo na dadāti ca
|
anarhebhyaśca yo dadyādvṛṣallīpataye tathā ||36||

37madyapo bhinnamaryādaḥ kṛtaghno bhartṛnindakaḥ
|
teṣāṃ gatimiyāṃ kṣipraṃ na ceddhanyāṃ jayadratham ||37||

38bhuñjānānāṃ tu savyena utsaṅge cāpi khādatām
|
pālāśamāsanaṃ caica tindukairdantadhāvanam ||38||

39ye cāvarjayatāṃ lokāḥ svapatāṃ ca tathoṣasi
|
śītabhītāśca ye viprā raṇabhītāśca kṣatriyāḥ ||39||

40ekakūpodakagrāme vedadhvanivivarjite
|
ṣaṇmāsaṃ tatra vasatāṃ tathā śāstraṃ vinindatām ||40||

41divāmaithunināṃ cāpi divaseṣu ca śerate
|
agāradāhināṃ caiva garadānāṃ ca ye matāḥ ||41||

42agnyātithyavihīnāśca gopāneṣu ca vighnadāḥ
|
rajasvalāṃ sevayantaḥ kanyāṃ śulkena dāyinaḥ ||42||

43yā ca vai bahuyājināṃ brāhmaṇānāṃ śvavṛttinām
|
āsyamaithunikānāṃ ca ye divā maithune ratāḥ ||43||

44brāhmaṇasya pratiśrutya yo vai lobhāddadāti na
|
teṣāṃ gatiṃ gamiṣyāmi śvo na hanyāṃ jayadratham ||44||

45dharmādapetā ye cānye mayā nātrānukīrtitāḥ
|
ye cānukīrtitāsteṣāṃ gatiṃ kṣipramavāpnuyām ||45||

46yadi vyuṣṭāmimāṃ rātriṃ śvo na hanyāṃ jayadratham
|
imāṃ cāpyaparāṃ bhūyaḥ pratikṣāṃ me nibodhata ||46||

47yadyasminnahate pāpe sūryo'stamupayāsyati
|
ihaiva saṃpraveṣṭā'haṃ jvalitaṃ jātavedasam ||47||

48asurasuramanuṣyāḥ pakṣiṇo voragā vā pitṛjanicarā vā brahmadevarṣayo vā
|
caramacaramapīdaṃ yatparaṃ cāpi tasmāttadapi mama ripuṃ taṃ rakṣituṃ naiva śaktāḥ ||48||

49yadi viśati rasātalaṃ tadagryaṃ viyadapi devapuraṃ diteḥ puraṃ vā
|
tadapi śaraśatairahaṃ prabhāte bhṛśamabhimanyuripoḥ śiro'bhihartā ||49||

50evamuktvā vicikṣepa gāṇḍīvaṃ savyada‌kṣiṇam
|
tasya śabdamatikamya dhanuḥśabdo'spṛśaddivam ||50||

51arjunena pratijñāte pāñcajanyaṃ janārdanaḥ
|
pradadhmau tatra saṃkruddho devadattaṃ ca phālgunaḥ ||51||

52sa pāñcajanyo'cyutavaktravāyunā bhṛśaṃ supūrṇodaraniḥsṛtadhvaniḥ
|
jagat sapātālaviyaddigīśvaraṃ prakampayāmāsa yugātyaye yathā ||52||

53tato vāditraghoṣāśca prādurāsan sahasraśaḥ
|
siṃhanādaśca pāṇḍūnāṃ pratijñāte mahātmanā ||53||
