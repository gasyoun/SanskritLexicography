Mahābhārata 1.227 (Ādiparvan)

1vaiśaṃpāyana uvāca|
tasyātha varṣato vāri pāṇḍavaḥ pratyavārayat
|
śaravarṣeṇa bībhatsuruttamāstrāṇi darśayan||1||

2khāṇḍavaṃ ca vanaṃ sarvaṃ pāṇḍavo bahubhiḥ śaraiḥ
|
ācchādayadameyātmā nīhāreṇeva candramāḥ||2||

3na ca sma kiṃcicchaknoti bhūtaṃ niścarituṃ tataḥ
|
saṃcchādyamāne khe bāṇairasyatā savyasācinā||3||

4takṣakastu na tatrāsīnnāgarājo mahābalaḥ
|
dahyamāne vane tasminkurukṣetraṃ gato hi saḥ||4||

5aśvaseno'bhavattatra takṣakasya suto balī
|
sa yatnamakarottīvraṃ mokṣārthaṃ jātavedasaḥ||5||

6na śaśāka sa nirgantuṃ niruddhorjunapatribhiḥ
|
mokṣayāmāsa taṃ mātā nigīrya bhujagātmajā||6||

7tasya pūrvaṃ śiro grastaṃ pucchamasya nigīryate
|
nigīryamāṇā sākrāmatsutaṃ nāgī mumukṣayā||7||

8tasyāḥ śareṇa tīkṣṇena pṛthudhāreṇa pāṇḍavaḥ
|
śiraściccheda gacchantyāstāmapaśyacchacīpatiḥ||8||

9taṃ mumocayiṣurvajrī vātavarṣeṇa pāṇḍavam
|
mohayāmāsa tatkālamaśvasenastvamucyata||9||

10tāṃ ca māyāṃ tadā dṛṣṭvā ghorāṃ nāgena vañcitaḥ
|
dvidhā tridhā ca khagatānprāṇinaḥ pāṇḍavocchinat||10||

11śaśāpa taṃ ca saṃkruddho bībhatsurjihmagāminam
|
pāvako vāsudevaścāpyapratiṣṭho bhaviṣyasi||11||

12tato jiṣṇuḥ sahasrākṣaṃ khaṃ vitatyāśugaiḥ śaraiḥ
|
yodhayāmāsa saṃkruddho vañcanāṃ tāmanusmaran||12||

13devarājo'pi taṃ dṛṣṭvā saṃrabdhaṃ samare'rjunam
|
svamastramasṛjattīvraṃ chādayitvā'khilaṃ nabhaḥ||13||

14tato vāyurmahāghoṣaḥ kṣobhayansarvasāgarān
|
viyatstho janayanmeghāñjaladhārāsamākulān||14||

15tato'śanimuco ghorāṃstaḍitstanitaniḥsvanān
|
tadvighātārthamasṛjadarjuno'pyastramuttamam||15||

16vāyavyamabhimantryātha pratipattiviśāradaḥ
|
tenendrāśanimeghānāṃ vīryaujastadvināśitam||16||

17jaladhārāśca tāḥ śoṣaṃ jagmurneśuśca vidyutaḥ
|
kṣaṇena cābhavadvyoma saṃpraśāntarajastamaḥ||17||

18sukhaśītānilavahaṃ prakṛtisthārkamaṇḍalam
|
niṣpratīkārahṛṣṭaśca hutabhugvividhākṛtiḥ||18||

19sicyamāno vasaughaistaiḥ prāṇināṃ dehaniḥsṛtaiḥ
|
prajajvālātha so'rciṣmānsvanādaiḥ pūrayañjagat||19||

20kṛṣṇābhyāṃ rakṣitaṃ dṛṣṭvā taṃ ca dāvamahaṅkṛtāḥ
|
khamutpeturmahārāja suparṇādyāḥ patattriṇaḥ||20||

21garutmānvajrasadṛśaiḥ pakṣatuṇḍanakhaistathā
|
prahartukāmo nyapatadākāśātkṛṣṇapāṇḍavau||21||

22tathaivoragasaṅghātāḥ pāṇḍavasya samīpataḥ
|
utsṛjanto viṣaṃ ghoraṃ nipeturjvalitānanāḥ||22||

23tāṃścakarta śaraiḥ pārthaḥ saroṣāgnisamukṣitaiḥ
|
viviśuścāpi taṃ dīptaṃ dehābhāvāya pāvakam||23||

24tato'surāḥ sagandharvā yakṣarākṣasapannagāḥ
|
utpeturnādamatulamutsṛjanto raṇārthinaḥ||24||

25ayaḥkaṇapacakrāśmabhuśuṇḍyudyatabāhavaḥ
|
kṛṣṇapārthau jighāṃsantaḥ krodhasaṃmūrcchitaujasaḥ||25||

26teṣāmativyāharatāṃ śastravarṣaṃ pramuñcatām
|
pramamāthottamāṅgāni bībhatsurniśitaiḥ śaraiḥ||26||

27kṛṣṇaśca sumahātejāścakreṇārivināśanaḥ
|
daityadānavasaṅghānāṃ cakīra kadanaṃ mahat||27||

28athāpare śarairviddhāścakravegeritāstathā
|
velāmiva samāsādya vyatiṣṭhannamitaujasaḥ||28||

29tataḥ śakro'tisaṃkruddhastridaśānāṃ maheśvaraḥ
|
pāṇḍuraṃ gajamāsthāya tāvubhau samupādravat||29||

30vegenāśanimādāya vajramastraṃ ca so'sṛjat
|
hatāvetāviti prāha surānasurasūdanaḥ||30||

31tataḥ samudyatāṃ dṛṣṭvā devendreṇa mahāśanim
|
jagṛhuḥ sarvaśastrāṇi svāni svāni surāstathā||31||

32kāladaṇḍaṃ yamo rājan gadāṃ caiva dhaneśvaraḥ
|
pāśāṃśca tatra varuṇo vicitrāṃ ca tathāśanim||32||

33skandaḥ śaktiṃ samādāya tasthau merurivācalaḥ
|
oṣadhīrdīpyamānāśca jagṛhāte'śvināvapi||33||

34jagṛhe ca dhanurdhātā musalaṃ tu jayastathā
|
parvataṃ cāpi jagrāha kruddhastvaṣṭā mahābalaḥ||34||

35aṃśastu śaktiṃ jagrāha mṛtyurdevaḥ paraśvadham
|
pragṛhya parighaṃ ghoraṃ vicacārāryamā api||35||

36mitraśca kṣuraparyantaṃ cakramādāya tasthivān
|
pūṣā bhagaśca saṃkruddhaḥ savitā ca viśāṃpate||36||

37āttakārmukanistriṃśāḥ kṛṣṇāpārthau pradudruvuḥ
|
rudrāśca vasavaścaiva marutaśca mahābalāḥ||37||

38viśvedevāstathā sādhyā dīpyamānāḥ svatejasā
|
ete cānye ca bahavo devāstau puruṣottamau||38||

39kṛṣṇapārthau jighāṃsantaḥ pratīyurvividhāyudhāḥ
|
tatrādbhutānyadṛśyanta nimittāni mahāhave||39||

40yugāntasamarūpāṇi bhūtasaṃmohanāni ca
|
tathā dṛṣṭvā susaṃrabdhaṃ śakraṃ devaiḥ sahācyutau||40||

41abhītau yudhi durdharṣau tasthatuḥ sajjakārmukau
|
āgacchatastato devānubhau yuddhaviśāradau||41||

42vyatāḍayetāṃ saṃkruddhau śarairvajropamaistadā
|
asakṛdbhagnasaṃkalpāḥ surāśca bahuśaḥ kṛtāḥ||42||

43bhayādraṇaṃ parityajya śakramevābhiśiśriyuḥ
|
dṛṣṭvā nivāritāndevānmādhavenārjunena ca||43||

44āścaryamagamaṃstatra munayo nabhasi sthitāḥ
|
śakraścāpi tayorvīryamupalabhyāsakṛdraṇe||44||

45babhūva paramaprīto bhūyaścaitāvayodhayat
|
tato'śmavarṣaṃ sumahadvyasṛjatpākaśāsanaḥ||45||

46bhūya eva tadā vīryaṃ jijñāsuḥ savyasācinaḥ
|
taccharairarjuno varṣaṃ pratijaghne'tyamarṣitaḥ||46||

47viphalaṃ kriyamāṇaṃ tatsamavekṣya śatakratuḥ
|
bhūyaḥ saṃvardhayāmāsa tadvarṣaṃ pākaśāsanaḥ||47||

48so'śmavarṣaṃ mahāvegairiṣubhiḥ pākaśāsaniḥ
|
vilayaṃ gamayāmāsa harṣayanpitaraṃ tathā||48||

49tata utpāṭya pāṇibhyāṃ mandarācchikharaṃ mahat
|
sa drumaṃ vyasṛjacchakro jighāṃsuḥ pāṇḍunandanam||49||

50tato'rjuno vegavadbhirjvalitāgrairajihmagaiḥ
|
śarairvidhvaṃsayāmāsa gireḥ śṛṅgaṃ sahasradhā||50||

51girerviśīryamāṇasya tasya rūpaṃ tadā babhau
|
sārkacandragrahasyeva nabhasaḥ pariśīryataḥ||51||

52tenābhipatatā dāvaṃ śailena mahatā bhṛśam
|
śṛṅgeṇa nihatāstatra prāṇinaḥ khāṇḍavālayāḥ||52||
