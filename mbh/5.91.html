Mahābhārata 5.91 (Udyogaparvan)

1vaiśampāyana uvāca|
pṛthāmāmantrya govindaḥ kṛtvā cābhiprada‌kṣiṇam
|
duryodhanagṛhaṃ śaurirabhyagacchadarindamaḥ ||1||

2lakṣmyā paramayā yuktaṃ purandaragṛhopamam
|
vicitrairāsanairyuktaṃ praviveśa janārdanaḥ ||2||

3tasya kakṣyā vyatikramya tisro dvāḥsthairavāritaḥ
|
tato'bhraghanasaṃkāśaṃ girikūṭamivocchritam ||3||

4śriyā jvalantaṃ prāsādamāruroha mahāyaśāḥ
|
tatra rājasahastraiśca kurubhiścābhisaṃvṛtam ||4||

5dhārtarāṣṭra mahābāhuṃ dadarśāsīnamāsane
|
duḥśāsanaṃ ca karṇaṃ ca śakuniṃ cāpi saubalam ||5||

6duryodhanasamīpe tānāsanasthān dadarśa saḥ
|
abhyāgacchati dāśārhe dhārtarāṣṭro mahāyaśāḥ ||6||

7uda‌tiṣṭhat sahāmātyaḥ pūjayanmadhusūdanam
|
sametya dhārtarāṣṭreṇa sahāmātyena keśavaḥ ||7||

8rājabhistatra vārṣṇeyaḥ samāgacchadyathāvayaḥ
|
tatra jāmbūnadamayaṃ paryaṅkaṃ supariṣkṛtam ||8||

9vividhāstaraṇāstīrṇamabhyupāviśadacyutaḥ
|
tasmin gāṃ madhuparkaṃ cāpyudakaṃ ca janārdane ||9||

10nivedayāmāsa tadā gṛhān rājyaṃ ca kauravaḥ
|
tatra govindamāsīnaṃ prasannādityavarcasam ||10||

11upāsāñcakrire sarve kuravo rājabhiḥ saha
|
tato duryodhano rājā vārṣṇeyaṃ jayatāṃ varam ||11||

12nyamantrayadbhojanena nābhyanandacca keśavaḥ
|
tato duryodhanaḥ kṛṣṇamabravīt kurusaṃsadi ||12||

13mṛdupūrvaṃ śaṭhodarkaṃ karṇamābhāṣya kauravaḥ
|
kasmādannāni pānāni vāsāṃsi śayanāni ca ||13||

14tvadarthamupanītāni nāgṛhīstvaṃ janārdana
|
ubhayośca dadatsāhyamubhayośca hite rataḥ ||14||

15sambadhī dayitaścāsi dhṛtarāṣṭrasya mādhava
|
tvaṃ hi govinda dharmārthau vettha tattvena sarvaśaḥ
|
tatra kāraṇamicchāmi śrotuṃ cakragadādhara ||15||

16vaiśampāyana uvāca|
sa evamukto govindaḥ pratyuvāca mahāmanāḥ
|
udyanmeghasvanaḥ kāle pragṛhya vipulaṃ bhujam ||16||

17alaghūkṛtamagrastamanirastamasaṃkulam
|
rājīvanetro rājānaṃ hetumadvākyamuttamam ||17||

18kṛtārthā bhuñjate dūtāḥ pūjāṃ gṛhṇanti caiva ha
|
kṛtārthaṃ māṃ sahāmātyaṃ samarciṣyasi bhārata ||18||

19evamuktaḥ pratyuvāca dhārtarāṣṭro janārdanam
|
na yuktaṃ bhavatā'smāsu pratipattumasāmpratam ||19||

20kṛtārthaṃ vā'kṛtārthaṃ ca tvāṃ vayaṃ madhusūdana
|
yatāmahe pūjayituṃ dāśārha na ca śaknumaḥ ||20||

21na ca tatkāraṇaṃ vidmo yasminno madhusūdana
|
pūjāṃ kṛtāṃ prīyamāṇairnāmaṃsthāḥ puruṣottama ||21||

22vairaṃ no nāsti bhavatā govinda na ca vigrahaḥ
|
sa bhavān prasamīkṣyaitannedṛśaṃ vaktumarhati ||22||

23vaiśampāyana uvāca|
evamuktaḥ pratyuvāca dhārtarāṣṭraṃ janārdanaḥ
|
abhivīkṣya sahāmātyaṃ dāśārhaḥ prahasanniva ||23||

24nāhaṃ kāmānna saṃraṃbhānna dveṣānnārthakāraṇāt
|
na hetuvādāllobhādvā dharmaṃ jahyāṃ kathañcana ||24||

25saṃprītibhojyānyannāni āpadbhojyāni vā punaḥ
|
na ca saṃprīyase rājanna caivāpadgatā vayam ||25||

26akasmāddveṣṭi vai rājan janmaprabhṛti pāṇḍavān
|
priyānuvartino bhrātṝn sarvaiḥ samuditān guṇaiḥ||26||

27akasmāccaiva pārthānāṃ dveṣaṇaṃ nopapadyate
|
dharme sthitāḥ pāṇḍaveyāḥ kastān kiṃ vaktumarhati ||27||

28yastān dveṣṭi sa māṃ dveṣṭi yastānanu sa māmanu
|
aikātmyaṃ māṃ gataṃ viddhi pāṇḍavairdharmacāribhiḥ ||28||

29kāmakrodhānuvartī hi yo mohādvirurutsati
|
guṇavantaṃ ca yo dveṣṭi tamāhuḥ puruṣādhamam ||29||

30yaḥ kalyāṇaguṇān jātīn mohāllobhāddidṛkṣate
|
so'jitātmā'jitakrodho na ciraṃ tiṣṭhati śriyam ||30||

31atha yo guṇasaṃpannān hṛdayasyāpriyānapi
|
priyeṇa kurute vaśyāṃściraṃ yaśasi tiṣṭhati ||31||

32sarvametanna bhoktavyamannaṃ duṣṭāmisaṃhitam
|
kṣatturekasya bhoktavyamiti me dhīyate matiḥ ||32||

33evamuktvā mahābāhurduryodhanamamarṣaṇam
|
niścakrāma tataḥ śubhrāddhārtarāṣṭraniveśanāt ||33||

34niryāya ca mahābāhurvāsudevo mahāmanāḥ
|
niveśāya yayau veśma vidurasya mahātmanaḥ ||34||

35tamabhyagacchaddroṇaśca kṛpo bhīṣmo'tha bāhlikaḥ
|
kuravaśca mahābāhuṃ vidurasya gṛhe sthitam ||35||

36ta ūcurmādhavaṃ vīraṃ kuravo madhusūdanam
|
nivedayāmo vārṣṇeya saratnāṃste gṛhānvayam ||36||

37tānuvāca mahātejāḥ kauravān madhusūdanaḥ
|
sarve bhavanto gacchantu sarvā me'pacitiḥ kṛtā||37||

38yāteṣu kuruṣu kṣattā dāśārhamaparājitam
|
abhyarcayāmāsa tadā sarvakāmaiḥ prayatnavān ||38||

39tataḥ kṣattā'nnapānāni śucīni guṇavanti ca
|
upāharadanekāni keśavāya mahātmane ||39||

40taistarpayitvā prathamaṃ brāhmaṇān madhusūdanaḥ
|
vedavidbhyo dadau kṛṣṇaḥ prathamaṃ draviṇānyavi ||40||

41tato'nuyāyibhiḥ sārdhaṃ marudbhiriva vāsavaḥ
|
vidurānnāni bubhuje śucīni guṇavanti ca ||41||
