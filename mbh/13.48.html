Mahābhārata 13.48 (Anuśāsanaparvan)

1yudhiṣṭhira uvāca|
arthāllobhādvā kāmādvā varṇānāṃ cāpyaniścayāt
|
ajñānādvāpi varṇānāṃ jāyate varṇasaṃkaraḥ ||1||

2teṣāmetena vidhinā jātānāṃ varṇasaṃkare
|
ko dharmaḥ kāni karmāṇi tanme brūhi pitāmaha||2||

3bhīṣma uvāca|
cāturvarṇyasya karmāṇi cāturvarṇyaṃ ca kevalam
|
asṛjatsa hi yajñārthe pūrvameva prajāpatiḥ ||3||

4bhāryāścatasro viprasya dvayorātmā prajāyate
|
ānupūrvyādvayorhīnau mātṛjātyau prasūyataḥ||4||

5paraṃśavādbrāhmaṇasyaiva putraḥ śūdrāputraṃ pāraśavaṃ tamāhuḥ
|
śuśrūṣakaḥ svasya kulasya sa syātsvacāritraṃ nityamatho na jahyāt ||5||

6sarvānupāyānatha saṃpradhārya samuddharet svasya kulasya tantram
|
jyeṣṭho yavīyānapi yo dvijasya śuśrūṣayā dānaparāyaṇaḥ syāt ||6||

7tisraḥ kṣatriyasambandhāddvayorātmā'sya jāyate
|
hīnavarṇāstṛtīyāyāṃ śūdrā ugrā iti smṛtiḥ||7||

8dve cāpī bhārye vaiśyasya dvayorātmā'sya jāyate
|
śūdrā śūdrasya cāpyekā śūdrameva prajāyate ||8||

9ato'pi śiṣṭastvadhamo gurudārapradharṣakaḥ
|
bāhyaṃ varṇaṃ janayati cāturvarṇyavigarhitam ||9||

10viprāyāṃ kṣatriyo bāhyaṃ sūtaṃ stomakriyāparam
|
vaiśyo vaidehakaṃ cāpi maudgalyamapavarjitam||10||

11śūdraścāṃḍālamatyugraṃ vadhyaghnaṃ bāhyavāsinam
|
brāhmaṇyāṃ saṃprajāyanta ityete kulapāṃsanāḥ
|
ete matimatāṃ śreṣṭhe varṇasaṃkarajāḥ prabho ||11||

12bandī tu jāyate vaiśyānmāgadho vākyajīvanaḥ
|
śūdrānniṣādo matsyaghnaḥ kṣatriyāyāṃ vyatikramāt ||12||

13śūdrādāyogavaścāpi vaiśyāyāṃ grāmyadharmiṇaḥ
|
brāhmaṇairapratigrāhyastakṣā svadhanajīvanaḥ ||13||

14ete'pi sadṛśān varṇān janayanti svayoniṣu
|
mātṛjātyāḥ prasūyante hyavarā hīnayoniṣu ||14||

15yathā caturṣu varṇeṣu dvayorātmāsya jāyate
|
ānantaryātprajāyante tathā bāhyāḥ pradhānataḥ||15||

16te cāpi sadṛśaṃ varṇaṃ janayanti svayoniṣu
|
parasparasya dāreṣu janayanti vigarhitān ||16||

17yathā śūdro'pi brāhmaṇyāṃ jantuṃ bāhyaṃ prasūyate
|
evaṃ bāhyatarādbāhyaścāturvarṇyatprajāyate ||17||

18pratilomaṃ tu vardhante bāhyādbāhyatarātpunaḥ
|
hīnāddhīnāḥ prasūyante varṇāḥ paṃcadaśaiva tu||18||

19agamyāgamanāccaiva jāyate varṇasaṅkaraḥ
|
bāhyānāmanujāyante sairandhryāṃ māgadheṣu ca
|
prasādhanopacārajñamadāsaṃ dāsajīvanam ||19||

20ataścāyogavaṃ sūte vāgurābandhajīvanam
|
maireyakaṃ ca vaidehaḥ saṃprasūte'tha mādhukam ||20||

21niṣādo madguraṃ sūte dāsaṃ nāvopajīvinam
|
mṛtapaṃ cāpi cāṇḍālaḥ śvapākamiti viśrutam||21||

22caturo māgadhī sute krūrānmāyopajīvinaḥ
|
māṃsaṃ svādukaraṃ kṣaudraṃ saugandhamiti viśrutam||22||

23vaidehakācca pāpiṣṭhā krūraṃ māyopajīvinam
|
niṣādānmadranābhaṃ ca kharayānaprayāyinam ||23||

24cāṃḍālātpulkasaṃ cāpi kharāśvagajabhojinam
|
mṛtacailapraticchannaṃ bhinnabhājanabhojinam ||24||

25āyogavīṣu jāyante hīnavarṇāstu te trayaḥ
|
kṣudro vaidehakādandhro bahirgrāmapratiśrayaḥ||25||

26kārāvaro niṣādyāṃ tu carmakāraḥ prasūyate
|
cāṃḍālātpāṃḍusaupākastvaksāravyavahāravān ||26||

27āhiṇḍako niṣādena vaidehyāṃ saṃprasūyate
|
caṇḍālena tu saupākaścaṇḍālasamavṛttimān||27||

28niṣādī cāpi cāṇḍālātputramantevasāyinam
|
śmaśānagocaraṃ sūte bāhyairapi bahiṣkṛtam ||28||

29ityete saṃkare jātāḥ pitṛmātṛvyatikramāt
|
pracchannā vā prakāśā vā veditavyāḥ svakarmabhiḥ||29||

30caturṇāmeva varṇānāṃ dharmo nānyasya vidyate
|
varṇānāṃ dharmahīneṣu saṃkhyā nāstīha kasyacit||30||

31yadṛcchayopasaṃpannairyajñasādhubahiṣkṛtaiḥ
|
bāhyā bāhyaiśca jāyante yathāvṛtti yathāśrayam||31||

32catuṣpathaśmaśānāni śailāṃścānyānvanaspatīn
|
kārṣṇāyasamalaṃkāraṃ parigṛhya ca nityaśaḥ||32||

33vaseyurete vijñātā vartayantaḥ svakarmabhiḥ
|
yuñjanto vāpyalaṃkārāṃstathopakaraṇāni ca||33||

34gobrāhmaṇāya sāhāyyaṃ kurvāṇā vai na saṃśayaḥ
|
ānṛśaṃsyamanukrośaḥ satyavākyaṃ tathā kṣamā||34||

35svaśarīrairāpi trāṇaṃ bāhyānāṃ siddhikāraṇam
|
bhavanti manujavyāghra tatra me nāsti saṃśayaḥ ||35||

36yathopadeśaṃ parikīrtitāsu naraḥ prajāyate vicārya buddhimān
|
nihīnayonirhi suto'vasādayettitīrṣamāṇaṃ hi yathopalo jale ||36||

37avidvāṃsamalaṃ loke vidvāṃsamapi vā punaḥ
|
nayanti hyapathaṃ nāryaḥ kāmakrodhavaśānugam ||37||

38svabhāvaścaiva nārīṇāṃ narāṇāmiha dūṣaṇam
|
atyarthaṃ na prasajjante pramadāsu vipaścitaḥ ||38||

39yudhiṣṭhira uvāca|
varṇāpetamavijñāya naraṃ kaluṣayonijam
|
āryarūpamivānāryaṃ kathaṃ vidyāmahe vayam ||39||

40bhīṣma uvāca|
yonisaṃkaluṣe jātaṃ nānābhāvasamanvitam
|
karmabhiḥ sajjanācīrṇairvijñeyā yoniśuddhatā||40||

41anāryatvamanācāraḥ krūratvaṃ niṣkriyātmatā
|
puruṣaṃ vyañjayantīha loke kaluṣayonijam ||41||

42pitryaṃ vā bhajate śīlaṃ mātṛjaṃ vā tathobhayam
|
na kathaṃcana saṃkīrṇaḥ prakṛtiṃ svāṃ niyacchati||42||

43yathaiva sadṛśo rūpe mātāpitrorhi jāyate
|
vyāghraścitraistathā yoniṃ puruṣaḥ svāṃ niyacchati||43||

44kule srotasi saṃcchanne yasya syādyonisaṃkaraḥ
|
saṃśrayatyeva tacchīlaṃ naro'lpamathavā bahu ||44||

45āryarūpasamācāraṃ carantaṃ kṛtake pathi
|
suvarṇamanyavarṇaṃ vā svaśīlaṃ śāsti niścaye||45||

46nānāvṛtteṣu bhūteṣu nānākarmarateṣu ca
|
janmavṛttasamaṃ loke suśliṣṭaṃ na virajyate ||46||

47śarīramiha sattvena na tasya parikṛṣyate
|
jyeṣṭhamadhyāvaraṃ sattvaṃ tulyasattvaṃ pramodate ||47||

48jyāyāṃsamapi śīlena vihīnaṃ naiva pūjayet
|
api śūdraṃ ca dharmajñaṃ sadvṛttamabhipūjayet ||48||

49ātmānamākhyāti hi karmabhirnaraḥ suśīlacāritrakulaiḥ śubhāśubhaiḥ
|
pranaṣṭamapyāśu kulaṃ tathā naraḥ punaḥ prakāśaṃ kurute svakarmataḥ ||49||

50yoniṣvetāsu sarvāsu saṃkīrṇāsvitarāsu ca
|
yatrātmānaṃ na janayedbudhastāṃ parivarjayet ||50||
