Mahābhārata 4.39 (Virāṭaparvan)

1vaiśampāyana uvāca|taṃ dṛṣṭvā klībaveṣeṇa rathasthaṃ narapuṃgavam
|
śamīmabhimukhaṃ yāntaṃ rathamāropya cottaram ||1||

2bhīṣmadroṇamukhāstatra kuravo rathisattamāḥ
|
vitrastamanasaḥ sarvai dhanañjayakṛtādbhayāt ||2||

3tānavekṣya hatotsāhānutpātānapi cādbhutān
|
guruḥ śastrabhṛtāṃ śreṣṭho bhāradvājo'bhyabhāṣata ||3||

4caṇḍāśca vātāḥ saṃvānti rūkṣāḥ śarkaravarṣiṇaḥ
|
bhasmavarṇaprakāśena tamasā saṃvṛtaṃ nabhaḥ ||4||

5rūkṣavarṇāśca jaladā dṛśyante'dbhutadarśanāḥ
|
niḥsaranti ca kośebhyaḥ śastrāṇi vividhāni ca ||5||

6śivāśca vinadantyetā dīptāyāṃ diśi dāruṇāḥ
|
hayāścāśrūṇi muñcanti dhvajāḥ kampantyakampitāḥ ||6||

7yādṛśānyatra rūpāṇi sandṛśyante bahūni ca
|
yattā bhavantastiṣṭhantu sādhvasaṃ samupasthitam ||7||

8rakṣadhvamapi cātmānaṃ vyūhadhvaṃ vāhinīmapi
|
vaiśasaṃ ca pratīkṣadhvaṃ rakṣadhvaṃ cāpi godhanam ||8||

9eṣa vīro maheṣvāsaḥ sarvaśastrabhṛtāṃ varaḥ
|
āgataḥ klībaveṣeṇa pārtho nāstyatra saṃśayaḥ ||9||

10nadīja laṅkeśavanāriketurnagāhṇayo nāma nagārisūnuḥ
|
eṣo'ṅganāveṣadharaḥ kirīṭī jitvā'va yaṃ neṣyati cādya gā vaḥ ||10||

11sa eṣa pārtho vikrāntaḥ savyasācī paraṃtapaḥ
|
nāyuddhena nivarteta sarvairapi surāsuraiḥ ||11||

12kleśitaśca vane śūro vāsavenāpi śikṣitaḥ
|
amarṣavaśamāpanno vāsavapratimo yudhi
|
nehāsya pratiyoddhāramahaṃ paśyāmi kauravāḥ ||12||

13mahādevo'pi pārthena śrūyate yudhi toṣitaḥ
|
kirātaveṣapracchanno girau himavati prabhuḥ ||13||

14karṇa uvāca|sadā bhavān phālgunasya guṇairasmān vikatthase
|
na cārjunaḥ kalāpūrṇo mama duryodhanasya ca ||14||

15duryodhana uvāca|yadyeṣa pārtho rādheya kṛtaṃ kāryaṃ bhavenmama
|
jñātāḥ punaścariṣyanti dvādaśābdān viśāṃpate ||15||

16athaiṣa kaścidevānyaḥ klībaveṣeṇa mānavaḥ
|
śarairenaṃ suniśitaiḥ pātayiṣyāmi bhūtale ||16||

17vaiśampāyana uvāca|tasmin bruvati tadvākyaṃ dhārtarāṣṭre paraṃtapa
|
bhīṣmo droṇaḥ kṛpo drauṇiḥ pauruṣaṃ tadapūjayan ||17||
