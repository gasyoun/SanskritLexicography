Mahābhārata 13.146 (Anuśāsanaparvan)

1nārada uvāca|
evamuktvā mahādevaḥ śrotukāmaḥ svayaṃprabhuḥ
|
anukūlāṃ priyāṃ bhāryāṃ pārśvasthāṃ samabhāṣata ||1||

2maheśvara uvāca|
parāvarajñe dharmajñe tapovananivāsini
|
sādhvi subhru sukeśānte himavatparvatātmaje||2||

3dakṣe śamadamopeta nirmame dharmacāriṇi
|
pṛcchāmi tvāṃ varārohe pṛṣṭā vada mamepsitam ||3||

4sāvitrī brahmaṇaḥ sādhvī kauśikasya śacī satī
|
mārkaṇḍeyasya dhūmorṇā ṛddhirvaiśravaṇasya ca ||4||

5varuṇasya tathā gaurī sūryasya ca suvarcalā
|
rohiṇī śaśinaḥ sādhvī svāhā caiva vibhāvasoḥ ||5||

6aditiḥ kaśyapasyātha sarvāstāḥ patidevatāḥ
|
pṛṣṭāścopāsitāścaiva tāstvayā devi nityaśaḥ ||6||

7tena tvāṃ paripṛcchāmi dharmajñe dharmavādini
|
strīdharmaṃ śrotumicchāmi tvayodāhṛtamāditaḥ ||7||

8sadharmacāriṇī me tvaṃ samaśīlā samavratā
|
samānasāravīryā ca tapastīvraṃ kṛtaṃ ca te ||8||

9tvayā hyukto viśeṣeṇa guṇavānsa bhaviṣyati
|
loke caiva tvayā devi pramāṇatvamupaiṣyati ||9||

10striyaścaiva viśeṣeṇa strījanasya gatiḥ parā
|
gauryāṃ gacchati suśroṇi lokeṣveṣā gatiḥ sadā ||10||

11mama cārdhaṃ śarīrasya tava cārdhena nirmitam
|
surakāryakarī ca tvaṃ lokasantānakāriṇī ||11||

12tava sarvaḥ suviditaḥ strīdharmaḥ śāśvataḥ śubhe
|
tasmādaśeṣato brūhi svadharmaṃ vistareṇa me ||12||

13umovāca
|
bhagavansarvabhūteśa bhūtabhavyabhavottama
|
tvatprabhāvādiyaṃ deva vākcaiva pratibhāti me ||13||

14imāstu nadyo deveśa sarvatīrthoda‌kairyutāḥ
|
upasparśanahetostvāmupayānti samīpataḥ ||14||

15etābhiḥ saha saṃmantrya pravakṣyāmyanupūrvaśaḥ
|
prabhavanyo'nahaṃvādī sa vai puruṣa ucyate ||15||

16strī ca bhūteśa satataṃ striyamevānudhāvati
|
mayā saṃmānitāścaiva bhaviṣyanti saridvarāḥ ||16||

17eṣā sarasvatī puṇyā nadīnāmuttamā nadī
|
prathamā sarvasaritāṃ nadī sāgaragāminī ||17||

18vipāśā ca vitastā ca candra‌bhāgā irāvatī
|
śatadrurdevikā sindhuḥ kauśikī gautamī tathā ||18||

19tathā devanadī ceyaṃ sarvatīrthābhisaṃbhṛtā
|
gaganādgāṃ gatā devī gaṅgā sarvasaridvarā ||19||

20ityuktvā devadevasya patnī dharmabhṛtāṃ varā
|
smitapūrvamathābhāṣya sarvāstāḥ saritastathā ||20||

21apṛcchaddevamahiṣī strīdharmaṃ dharmavatsalā
|
strīdharmakuśalāstā vai gaṅgādyāḥ saritāṃ varāḥ ||21||

22umovāca
|
ayaṃ bhagavatā proktaḥ praśnaḥ strīdharmasaṃśritaḥ
|
taṃ tu saṃmantrya yuṣmābhirvaktumicchāmi śaṅkaram ||22||

23na caikasādhyaṃ paśyāmi vijñānaṃ bhuvi kasyacit
|
divi vā sāgaragamāstena vo mānayāmyaham ||23||

24evaṃ sarvāḥ saricchreṣṭhāḥ pṛṣṭāḥ puṇyatamāḥ śivāḥ
|
tato devanadī gaṅgā niyuktā pratipūjya ca ||24||

25bahvībhirbuddhibhiḥ sphītā strīdharmajñā śucismitā
|
śailarājasutāṃ devīṃ puṇyā pāpabhayāpahā ||25||

26buddhyā vinayasaṃpannā sarvadharmaviśāradā
|
sasmitaṃ bahubuddhyāḍhyā gaṅgā vacanamabravīt ||26||

27gaṅgovāca
|
dhanyā'smyanugṛhītā'smi devi dharmaparāyaṇe
|
yā tvaṃ sarvajaganmānyā nadīṃ mānayase'naghe ||27||

28prabhavan pṛcchate yo hi saṃmānayati vā punaḥ
|
nūnaṃ janamaduṣṭātmā paṇḍitākhyāṃ sa gacchati ||28||

29jñānavijñānasaṃpannānūhāpohaviśāradān
|
pravaktṝn pṛcchate yo'nyān sa vai nāpadamṛcchati ||29||

30anyathā bahubuddhyāḍhyo vākyaṃ vadati saṃsadi
|
anyathaiva hyahaṃvādī durbalaṃ vadate vacaḥ ||30||

31divyajñāne divi śreṣṭhe divyapuṇyaiḥ sahotthite
|
tvamevārhasi no devi strīdharmānanubhāṣitum ||31||

32tataḥ sārādhitā devī gaṅgayā bahubhirguṇaiḥ
|
prāha sarvamaśeṣeṇa strīdharmaṃ surasundarī ||32||

33umovāca
|
strīdharmo māṃ prati yathā prati bhāti yathāvidhi
|
tamahaṃ kīrtayiṣyāmi tathaiva prathitā bhava ||33||

34strīdharmaḥ pūrva evāyaṃ vivāhe bandhubhiḥ kṛtaḥ
|
sahadharmacarī bharturbhavatyagnisamīpataḥ ||34||

35susvabhāvā suvacanā suvṛttā sukhadarśanā
|
ananyacittā sumukhī bhartuḥ sā dharmacāriṇī ||35||

36sā bhaveddharmaparamā sā bhaveddharmabhāginī
|
devavatsatataṃ sādhvī yā bhartāraṃ prapaśyati ||36||

37śuśrūṣāṃ paricāraṃ ca devavadyā karoti ca
|
nānyabhāvā hyavimanāḥ suvratā sukhadarśanā ||37||

38putravaktramivābhīkṣṇaṃ bharturvadanamīkṣate
|
yā sādhvī niyatāhārā sā bhaveddharmacāriṇī ||38||

39śrutvā dampatidharmaṃ vai sahadharmaṃ kṛtaṃ śubham
|
yā bhaveddharmaparamā nārī bhartṛsamavratā ||39||

40devavatsatataṃ sādhvī bhartāramanupaśyati
|
dampatyoreṣa vai dharmaḥ sahadharmakṛtaḥ śubhaḥ ||40||

41śuśrūṣāṃ paricāraṃ ca devatulyaṃ prakurvatī
|
vaśyā bhāvena sumanāḥ suvratā sukhadarśanā ||41||

42ananyacittā sumukhī bhartuḥ sā dharmacāriṇī
|
paruṣāṇyapi coktā yā dṛṣṭā duṣṭena cakṣuṣā
|
suprasannamukhī bharturyā nārī sā pativratā ||42||

43na candrasūryau na taruṃ puṃnāmnā yā nirīkṣate
|
bhartṛvarjaṃ varārohā sā bhaveddharmacāriṇī ||43||

44daridraṃ vyādhitaṃ dīnamadhvanā parikarśitam
|
patiṃ putramivopāste sā nārī dharmabhāginī ||44||

45yā nārī prayatā dakṣā yā nārī putriṇī bhavet
|
patipriyā patiprāṇā sā nārī dharmabhāginī ||45||

46śuśrūṣāṃ paricaryāṃ ca karotyavimanāḥ sadā
|
supratītā vinītā ca sā nārī dharmabhāginī ||46||

47na kāmeṣu na bhogeṣu naiśvarye na sukhe tathā
|
spṛhā yasyā yathā patyau sā nārī dharmabhāginī ||47||

48kalyotthānaratirnityaṃ gṛhaśuśrūṣaṇe ratā
|
susaṃmṛṣṭakṣayā caiva gośakṛtkṛtalepanā ||48||

49agnikāryaparā nityaṃ sadā puṣpabalipradā
|
devatātithibhṛtyānāṃ nirvāpya patinā saha ||49||

50śeṣānnamupabhuñjānā yathānyāyaṃ yathāvidhi
|
tuṣṭapuṣṭajanā nityaṃ nārī dharmeṇa yujyate ||50||

51śvaśrūśvaśurayoḥ pādau joṣayantī guṇānvitā
|
mātāpitṛparā nityaṃ yā nārī sā tapodhanā ||51||

52brāhmaṇān durbalānāthāndīnāndhakṛpaṇāṃstathā
|
bibhartyannena yā nārī sā pativratabhāginī ||52||

53vrataṃ carati yā nityaṃ duścaraṃ laghusattvayā
|
paticittā patihitā sā pativratabhāginī ||53||

54puṇyametattapaścaitatsvargaścaiṣa sanātanaḥ
|
yā nārī bhartṛparamā bhavedbhartṛvratā satī ||54||

55patirhi devo nārīṇāṃ patirbandhuḥ patirgatiḥ
|
patyā samā gatirnāsti daivataṃ vā yathā patiḥ ||55||

56patiprasādaḥ svargo vā tulyo nāryā na vā bhavet
|
ahaṃ svargaṃ na hīccheyaṃ tvayyaprīte maheśvare ||56||

57yadyakāryamadharmaṃ vā yadi vā prāṇanāśanam
|
patirbrūyāddaridro vā vyādhito vā kathañcana ||57||

58āpanno ripusaṃstho vā brahmaśāpārditopi vā
|
āpaddharmānanuprekṣya tatkāryamaviśaṃkayā ||58||

59eṣa deva mayā proktaḥ strīdharmo vacanāttava
|
yā tvevabhāvinī nārī sā pativratabhāginī ||59||

60bhīṣma uvāca|
ityuktaḥ sa tu deveśaḥ pratipūjya gireḥ sutām
|
lokān visarjayāmāsa sarvairanucarairvṛtān ||60||

61tato yayurbhūtagaṇāḥ saritaśca yathāgatam
|
gandharvāpsarasaścaiva praṇamya śirasā bhavam ||61||
