Mahābhārata 18.1 (Svargārohaṇaparvan)

1janamejaya uvāca|
svargaṃ triviṣṭapaṃ prāpya mama pūrvapitāmahāḥ
|
pāṇḍavā dhārtarāṣṭrāśca kāni sthānāni bhejire ||1||

2etadicchāmyahaṃ śrotuṃ sarvaviccāsi me mataḥ
|
maharṣiṇā'bhyanujñāto vyāsenādbhutakarmaṇā ||2||

3vaiśampāyana uvāca|
svargaṃ triviṣṭapaṃ prāpya tava pūrvapitāmahāḥ
|
yudhiṣṭhiraprabhṛtayo yadakurvata tacchṛṇu ||3||

4svargaṃ triviṣṭapaṃ prāpya dharmarājo yudhiṣṭhiraḥ
|
duryodhanaṃ śriyā juṣṭaṃ dadarśāsīnamāsane ||4||

5bhrājamānamivādityaṃ vīralakṣmyā'bhisaṃvṛtam
|
devairbhrājiṣṇubhiḥ sādhyaiḥ sahitaṃ puṇyakarmabhiḥ ||5||

6tato yudhiṣṭhiro rājā duryodhanamamarṣitaḥ
|
sahasā sannivṛtto'bhūcchriyaṃ dṛṣṭvā suyodhane ||6||

7bruvannuccairvacastānvai nāhaṃ duryodhanena vai
|
sahitaḥ kāmaye lokā~llabdhenādīrghadarśinā ||7||

8yatkṛte pṛthivī sarvā suhṛdo bāndhavāstathā
|
hatāsmābhiḥ prasahyājau kilṣṭaiḥ pūrvaṃ mahāvane ||8||

9draupadī ca sabhāmadhye pāñcālī dharmacāriṇī
|
paryākṛṣṭā'navadyāṅgī patnī no gurusannidhau ||9||

10asti devā na me kāmaḥ suyodhanamudīkṣitum
|
tatrāhaṃ gantumicchāmi yatra te bhrātaro mama ||10||

11naivamityabravīttaṃ tu nāradaḥ prahasanniva
|
svarge nivāse rājendra viruddhaṃ cāpi naśyati ||11||

12yudhiṣṭhira mahābāho maivaṃ vocaḥ kathañcana
|
duryodhanaṃ prati nṛpaṃ śṛṇu cedaṃ vaco mama ||12||

13eṣa duryodhano rājā pūjyate tridaśaiḥ saha
|
sadbhiśca rājapravaraite ime svargavāsinaḥ ||13||

14vīralokagatiṃ prāptā yuddhe hutvā''tmanastanum
|
yūyaṃ sarve surasamā yena yuddhena bādhitāḥ ||14||

15sa eṣa kṣatradharmeṇa sthānametadavāptavān
|
bhaye mahati yo'bhīto babhūva pṛthivīpatiḥ ||15||

16na tanmanasi kartavyaṃ putra yaddyūtakāritam
|
draupadyāśca parikleśaṃ na cintayitumarhasi ||16||

17ye cānye'pi parikleśā yuṣmākaṃ jñātikāritāḥ
|
saṃgrāmeṣvathavā'nyatra na tānsaṃsmartumarhasi ||17||

18samāgaccha yathānyāyaṃ rājñā duryodhanena vai
|
svargo'yaṃ neha vairāṇi bhanti manujādhipa ||18||

19nāradenaivamuktastu kururājo yudhiṣṭhiraḥ
|
bhrātṝnpapraccha medhāvī vākyametaduvāca ha ||19||

20yadi duryodhanasyaite vīralokāḥ sanātanāḥ
|
adharmajñasya pāpasya pṛthivīsuhṛdadruhaḥ ||20||

21yatkṛte pṛthivī naṣṭā sahasā sanaradvipā
|
vayaṃ ca manyunā dagdhā vairaṃ praticikīrṣavaḥ ||21||

22ye te vīrī mahātmāno bhrātaro me mahāvratāḥ
|
satyapratijñā lokasya śūrā vai satyavādinaḥ ||22||

23teṣāmidānīṃ ke lokā draṣṭumicchāmi tānaham
|
karṇaṃ caiva mahātmānaṃ kaunteyaṃ satyasaṅgaram
|
||23||

24dhṛṣṭadyumnaṃ sātyakiṃ ca dhṛṣṭadyumnasya cātmajān
|
ye ca śastrairvadhaṃ prāptāḥ kṣatradharmeṇa pārthivāḥ ||24||

25ka nu ta pārthivānbrahmannaitānpaśyāmi nārada
|
virāṭadrupadau caiva dhṛṣṭaketumukhāṃśca tān ||25||

26śikhaṇḍinaṃ ca pāñcālyaṃ draupadeyāṃśca sarvaśaḥ
|
abhimanyuṃ ca durdharṣaṃ draṣṭumicchāmi nārada ||26||
