Mahābhārata 12.251 (Śāntiparvan)

1vyāsa uvāca |
gandhān rasānnānurundhyātsukhaṃ vā nālaṃkārāṃścāpnuyāttasya tasya
|
mānaṃ ca kīrtiṃ ca yaśaśca necchetsa vai pracāraḥ paśyato brāhmaṇasya ||1||

2sarvānvedānadhīyīta śuśrūṣurbrahmacaryavān
|
ṛco yajūṃṣi sāmāni yo veda na sa vai dvijaḥ ||2||

3jñātivatsarvabhūtānāṃ sarvavitsarvavedavit
|
nākāmo mriyate jātu na tena na ca vai dvijaḥ ||3||

4iṣṭīśca vividhāḥ prāpya ṛtūṃścaivāptadakṣiṇān
|
prāpnoti naiva brāhmaṇyamavidhānātkathaṃcana ||4||

5yadā cāyaṃ na bibheti yadā cāsmānna vibhyati
|
yadā necchati na dveṣṭi brahma saṃpadyate tadā ||5||

6yadā na kurute bhāvaṃ sarvabhūteṣu pāpakam
|
karmaṇā manasā vācā brahma sampadyate tadā ||6||

7kāmabandhanamevaikaṃ nānyadastīha bandhanam
|
kāmabandhanamukto hi brahmabhūyāya kalpate ||7||

8kāmato mucyamānastu dhūmrābhrādiva candramāḥ
|
virajāḥ kālamākāṅkṣan dhīro dhairyeṇa vartate ||8||

9āpūryamāṇamacalapratiṣṭhaṃ samudramāpaḥ praviśanti yadvat
|
tadvatkāmā yaṃ praviśanti sarve sa śāntimāpnoti na kāmakāmaḥ ||9||

10sa kāmakānto na tu kāmakāmaḥ sa vai kāmātsvargamupaiti dehī ||10||

11vedasyopaniṣatsatyaṃ satyasyopaniṣaddamaḥ
|
damasyopaniṣaddānaṃ dānasyopaniṣattapaḥ ||11||

12tapasopaniṣattyāgastyāgasyopaniṣatsukham
|
sukhasyopaniṣatsvargaḥ svargasyopaniṣacchamaḥ ||12||

13kledanaṃ śokamanaso saṃtāpaṃ tṛṣṇayā saha
|
sattvamicchasi saṃtoṣācchāntilakṣaṇamuttamam ||13||

14viśoko nirmamaḥ śāntaḥ prasannātmā vimatsaraḥ
|
ṣaḍbhirlakṣaṇavānetaiḥ samagraḥ punareṣyati ||14||

15ṣaḍbhiḥ sattvaguṇopetaiḥ prājñairadhigataṃ tribhiḥ
|
ye viduḥ pretya cātmānamihasthaṃ taṃ guṇaṃ viduḥ ||15||

16akṛtrimamasaṃhāryaṃ prākṛtaṃ nirupaskṛtam
|
adhyātmaṃ sukṛtaṃ prāptaḥ sukhamavyayamaśnute ||16||

17niṣpracāraṃ manaḥ kṛtvā pratiṣṭhāpya ca sarvaśaḥ
|
yāmayaṃ labhate tuṣṭiṃ sā na śakyā''tmano'nyathā ||17||

18yena tṛpyatyabhuñjāno yena tṛpyatyavittavān
|
yenāsneho balaṃ dhatte yastaṃ veda sa vedavit ||18||

19saṃguptānyātmano dvārāṇyapidhāya vicintayan
|
yo hyāste brāhmaṇaḥ śiṣṭaḥ sa ātmaratirucyate ||19||

20samāhitaṃ pare tattve kṣīṇakāmamavasthitam
|
sarvataḥ sukhamanveti vapuścāndramasaṃ yathā ||20||

21aviśeṣāṇi bhūtāni guṇāṃśca jahato muneḥ
|
sukhenāpohyate duḥkhaṃ bhāskareṇa tamo yathā ||21||

22tamatikrāntakarmāṇamatikrāntaguṇakṣayam
|
brāhmaṇaṃ viṣayāśliṣṭaṃ jarāmṛtyū na vindataḥ ||22||

23sa yadā sarvato muktaḥ samaḥ paryavatiṣṭhate
|
indriyāṇīndriyārthāṃśca śarīrastho'tivartate ||23||

24kāraṇaṃ paramaṃ prāpya atikrāntasya kāryatām
|
punarāvartanaṃ nāsti samprāptasya paraṃ padam ||24||
