Mahābhārata 12.235 (Śāntiparvan)

1vyāsa uvāca |
trayīṃ vidyāmavekṣeta vedeṣūktāmathāṅgataḥ
|
ṛksāmavarṇākṣarato yajuṣo'tharvaṇastathā ||1||

2tiṣṭhatyeteṣu bhagavān ṣaṭsu karmasu saṃsthitaḥ
|
vedavādeṣu kuśalā hyadhyātmakuśalāśca ye ||2||

3sattvavanto mahābhāgāḥ paśyanti prabhavāpyayau
|
evaṃ dharmeṇa varteta kriyāṃ śiṣṭavadācaret ||3||

4asaṃrodhena bhūtānāṃ vṛttiṃ lipseta vai dvijaḥ
|
sadbhya āgatavijñānaḥ śiṣṭaḥ śāstravicakṣaṇaḥ ||4||

5svadharmeṇa kriyāloke karma sattvasthasaṃcaraḥ
|
tiṣṭhate teṣu gṛhavān ṣaṭsu karmasu sa dvijaḥ ||5||

6pañcabhiḥ satataṃ yajñaiḥ śraddadhāno yajeta ca
|
dhṛtimānapramattaśca dānto dharmavidātmavān ||6||

7vītaharṣamadakrodho brāhmaṇo nāvasīdati
|
dānamadhyayanaṃ yajñastapo hrīrārjavaṃ damaḥ ||7||

8etairvardhayate tejaḥ pāpmānaṃ cāpakarṣati
|
dhūtapāpmā ca medhāvī laghvāhāro jitendriyaḥ ||8||

9kāmakrodhau vaśe kṛtvā ninīṣedbrahmaṇaḥ padam
|
agnīṃśca brāhmaṇāṃścārceddevatāḥ praṇameta ca ||9||

10varjayeduśatīṃ vācaṃ hiṃsāṃ cādharmasaṃhitām
|
eṣā pūrvagatā vṛttirbrāhmaṇasya vidhīyate ||10||

11jñānāgamena karmāṇi kurvankarmasu sidhyati
|
pañcendriyajalāṃ ghorāṃ lobhakūlāṃ sudustarām ||11||

12manyupaṅkāmanādhṛṣyāṃ nadīṃ tarati buddhimān
|
kālamabhyudyataṃ paśyennityamatyantamohanam ||12||

13mahatā vidhidṛṣṭena balenāpratighātinā
|
svabhāvasrotasā vṛttamuhyate satataṃ jagat ||13||

14kālodake mahatā varṣāvartena saṃtatam
|
māsormiṇartuvagena pakṣolapatṛṇena ca ||14||

15nimeṣonmeṣaphenena ahorātrajalena ca
|
kāmagrāheṇa ghoreṇa vedayajñaplavena ca ||15||

16dharmadvīpena bhūtānāṃ cārthakāmajalena ca
|
ṛtavāṅmokṣatīreṇa vihiṃsātaruvāhinā ||16||

17yugahradaughamadhyena brahmaprāyabhavena ca
|
dhātrā sṛṣṭāni bhūtāni kṛṣyante yamasādanam ||17||

18etatprajñāmayairdhīrā nistaranti manīṣiṇaḥ
|
plavairaplavavanto hi kiṃ kariṣyantyacetasaḥ ||18||

19upapannaṃ hi yatprājño nistarennetaro janaḥ
|
dūrato guṇadoṣau hi prājñaḥ sarvatra paśyati ||19||

20saṃśayaṃ sa tu kāmātmā calacitto'lpacetanaḥ
|
aprājño na taratyenaṃ yo hyāste na sa gacchati ||20||

21aplavo hi mahādoṣaṃ muhyamāno niyacchati
|
kāmagrāhagṛhītasya jñānamapyasya na plavaḥ ||21||

22tasmādunmajjanasyārthe prayateta vicakṣaṇaḥ
|
etadunmajjanaṃ tasya yadayaṃ brāhmaṇo bhavet ||22||

23avadāteṣu saṃjātastrisandehastrikarmakṛt
|
tasmādunpajjane tiṣṭhetprajñayā nistaredyathā ||23||

24saṃskṛtasya hi dāntasya niyatasya yatātmanaḥ
|
prājñasyānantarā siddhirihaloke paratra ca ||24||

25varteta teṣu gṛhavānakruddhyananasūyakaḥ
|
pajabhiḥ satataṃ yajñairvighasāśī yajeta ca ||25||

26satāṃ dharmeṇa varteta kriyāṃ śiṣṭavadācaret
|
asaṃrodhena lokasya vṛttiṃ lipsedagarhitām ||26||

27śrutivijñānatattvajñaḥ śiṣṭācāro vicakṣaṇaḥ
|
svadharmeṇa kriyāvāṃśca karmaṇā so'pyasaṃkaraḥ ||27||

28kriyāvān śraddadhāno hi dāntaḥ prājño'nasūyakaḥ
|
dharmādharmaviśeṣajñaḥ sarvaṃ taranti dustaram ||28||

29dhṛtimānapramattaśca dānto dharmavidātmavān
|
vītaharṣamadakrodho brāhmaṇo nāvasīdati ||29||

30eṣā purātanī vṛttirbrāhmaṇasya vidhīyate
|
jñānavattvena karmāṇi kurvansarvatra siddhyati ||30||

31adharmaṃ dharmakāmo hi karoti hyavicakṣaṇaḥ
|
dharmaṃ vā dharmasaṃkāśaṃ śocanniva karoti saḥ ||31||

32dharmaṃ karomīti karotyadharmamadharmakāmaśca karoti dharmam
|
ubhe'bālaḥ karmaṇī na prajānan sa jāyate mriyate cāpi dehī ||32||
