Mahābhārata 3.20 (Āraṇyakaparvan)

1vāsudeva uvāca|
ānartanagaraṃ muktaṃ tato'hamagamaṃ tadā
|
mahākratau rājasūye nivṛtte nṛpate tava||1||

2apaśyaṃ dvārakāṃ cāhaṃ mahārāja hatatviṣam
|
niḥsvādhyāyavaṣaṭkārāṃ nirbhūṣaṇavarastriyam||2||

3anabhijñeyarūpāṇi dvārakopavanāni ca
|
dṛṣṭvā śaṅkopapanno'hamapṛcchaṃ hṛdikātmajam||3||

4asvasthanaranārīkamidaṃ vṛṣṇikulaṃ bhṛśam
|
kimidaṃ naraśārdūla śrotumicchāmi tattvataḥ||4||

5evamuktaḥ sa tu mayā vistareṇedamabravīt
|
rodhaṃ mokṣaṃ ca śālvena hārdikyo rājasattama||5||

6tato'haṃ bharataśreṣṭha śrutvā sarvamaśeṣataḥ
|
vināśe śālvarājasya tadaivākaravaṃ matim||6||

7tato'haṃ bharataśreṣṭha samāśvāsya pure janam
|
rājānamāhukaṃ caiva tathaivānakadundubhim||7||

8sarvanvṛṣṇipravīrāṃśca harṣayannabruvaṃ tadā
|
apramādaḥ sadā kāryo nagare yādavarṣabhāḥ||8||

9śālvarājavināśāya prayātaṃ māṃ nibodhata
|
nāhatvā taṃ nivartiṣye purīṃ dvāravatīṃ prati||9||

10sa śālvaṃ saubhanagaraṃ hatvā draṣṭāsmi vaḥ punaḥ
|
triḥsāmā hanyatāmeṣā dundubhiḥ śatrubhīṣaṇā||10||

11te mayāśvāsitā vīrā yathāvadbharatarṣabha
|
sarve māmabruvanhṛṣṭāḥ prayāhi jahi śātravān||11||

12taiḥ prahṛṣṭātmabhirvīrairāśīrbhirabhinanditaḥ
|
vācayitvā dvijaśreṣṭhānpraṇamya śirasābhavam||12||

13śaibyasugrīvayuktena rathenānādayandiśaḥ
|
pradhmāya śaṅkhapravaraṃ pāñcajanyamahaṃ nṛpa||13||

14prayāto'smi naravyāghra balena mahatā vṛtaḥ
|
kḷptena caturaṅgeṇa yattena jitakāśinā||14||

15samatītya bahūndeśāngirīṃśca bahupādapān
|
sarāṃsi saritaścaiva mārttikāvatamāsadam||15||

16tatrāśrauṣaṃ naravyāghra śālvaṃ sāgaramantikāt
|
prayāntaṃ saubhamāsthāya tamahaṃ pṛṣṭhato'nvayām||16||

17tataḥ sāgaramāsādya kukṣau tasya mahormiṇaḥ
|
samudranābhyāṃ śālvo'bhūtsaubhamāsthāya śatruhan||17||

18sa samālokya dūrānmāṃ smayanniva yudhiṣṭhira
|
āhvayāmāsa duṣṭātmā yuddhāyaiva muhurmuhuḥ||18||

19tasya śārṅgavinirmuktairbahubhirmarmabhedibhiḥ
|
puraṃ nāsādyata śaraistato māṃ roṣa āviśat||19||

20sa cāpi pāpaprakṛtirdaiteyāpasado nṛpa
|
mayyavarṣata durdharṣaḥ śaradhārāḥ sahasraśaḥ||20||

21sainikānmama sūtaṃ ca hayāṃśca sa samākirat
|
acintayantastu śarānvayaṃ yudhyāma bhārata||21||

22tataḥ śatasahasrāṇi śarāṇāṃ nataparvaṇām
|
cikṣipuḥ samare vīrā mayi śālvapadānugāḥ||22||

23te hayāṃśca rathaṃ caiva tadā dārukameva ca
|
chādayāmāsurasurāstairbāṇairmarmabhedibhiḥ||23||

24na hayā na ratho vīra na yantā mama dārukaḥ
|
adṛśyanta śaraiśchannāstathāhaṃ sainikāśca me||24||

25tato'hamapi kaunteya śarāṇāmayutānbahūn
|
āmantritānāṃ dhanuṣā divyena vidhinā'kṣipam||25||

26na tatra viṣamastvāsīnmama sainyasya bhārata
|
khe viṣaktaṃ hi tatsaubhaṃ krośamātra ivābhavat||26||

27tataste prekṣakāḥ sarve raṅgavāṭa iva sthitāḥ
|
harṣayāmāsuruccairmāṃ siṃhanādatalasvanaiḥ||27||

28matkarāgravinirmuktā dānavānāṃ śarāstathā
|
aṅgeṣu rucirāpāṅgā viviśuḥ śalabhā iva||28||

29tato halahalāśabdaḥ saubhamadhye vyavardhata
|
vadhyatāṃ viśikhaistīkṣṇaiḥ patatāṃ ca mahārṇave||29||

30te nikṛttabhujaskandhāḥ kabandhākṛtidarśanāḥ
|
nadanto bhairavānnādānnipatanti sma dānavāḥ||30||

31patitāste'pi bhakṣante samudrāmbhonivāsibhiḥ
|
tato gokṣīrakundendumṛṇālarajataprabham||31||

32jalajaṃ pāñcajanyaṃ vai prāṇenāhamapūrayam
|
tāndṛṣṭvā patitāṃstatra śālvaḥ saubhapatistataḥ||32||

33māyāyuddhena mahatā yodhayāmāsa māṃ yudhi
|
tato gadā halāḥ prāsāḥ śūlaśaktiparaśvadhāḥ||33||

34asayaḥ śaktikuliśapāśarṣṭikanapāḥ śarāḥ
|
paṭṭiśāśca bhuśuṇḍyaśca prapatantyaniśaṃ mayi||34||

35tāmahaṃ māyayaivāśu pratigṛhya vyanāśayam
|
tasyāṃ hatāyāṃ māyāyāṃ giriśṛṅgairayodhayat||35||

36tato'bhavattama iva prakāśa iva cābhavat
|
durdinaṃ sudinaṃ caiva śītamuṣṇaṃ ca bhārata||36||

37aṅgārapāṃśuvarṣaṃ ca śastravarṣaṃ ca bhārata
|
evaṃ māyāṃ prakurvāṇo yodhayāmāsa māṃ ripuḥ||37||

38vijñāya tadahaṃ sarvaṃ māyayaiva vyanāśayam
|
yathākālaṃ tu yuddhena vyadhamaṃ sarvataḥ śaraiḥ||38||

39tato vyoma mahārāja śatasūryamivābhavat
|
śatacandraṃ ca kaunteya sahasrāyutatārakam||39||

40tato nājñāyata tadā divārātraṃ tathā diśaḥ
|
tato'haṃ mohamāpannaḥ prajñāstraṃ samayojayam||40||

41tatastadastraṃ kaunteya dhūtaṃ tūlamivānalaiḥ
|
tathā tadabhavadyuddhaṃ tumulaṃ lomaharṣaṇam
|
labdhā lokastu rājendra punaḥ śatrumayodhayam||41||
