Mahābhārata 7.21 (Droṇaparvan)

1sañjaya uvāca|
tato yudhiṣṭhiro droṇaṃ dṛṣṭvā'ntikamupāgatam
|
mahatā śaravarṣeṇa pratyagṛhṇādabhītavat||1||

2tato halahalāśabda āsīdyaudhiṣṭhire bale
|
jighṛkṣati mahāsiṃhe gajānāmiva yūthapam||2||

3dṛṣṭvā droṇaṃ tataḥ śūraḥ satyajit satyavikramaḥ
|
yudhiṣṭhiramabhiprepsurācāryaṃ samupādravat||3||

4tata ācāryapāñcālyau yuyudhāte mahābalau
|
vikṣobhayantau tat sainyamindravairocanāviva||4||

5tato droṇaṃ maheṣvāsaḥ satyajitsatyavikramaḥ
|
avidhyanniśitāgreṇa paramāstraṃ vidarśayan||5||

6tathāsya sāratheḥ pañca śarān sarpaviṣopamān
|
amuñcadantakaprakhyān saṃmumohāsya sārathiḥ||6||

7athāsya sahasā'vidhyaddhayān daśabhirāśugaiḥ
|
daśabhirdaśabhiḥ kuddha ubhau ca pārṣṇisārathī||7||

8maṇḍalaṃ tu samāvṛtya vicaran pṛtanāmukhe
|
dhvajaṃ ciccheda ca kruddho droṇasyāmitrakarṣaṇaḥ||8||

9droṇastu tatsamālokya caritaṃ tasya saṃyuge
|
manasā cintayāmāsa prāptakālamarindamaḥ||9||

10tataḥ satyajitaṃ tīkṣṇairdaśabhirmarmabhedibhiḥ
|
avidhyacchīghramācāryaśchittvāsya saśaraṃ dhanuḥ||10||

11sa śīghrataramādāya dhanuranyat pratāpavān
|
droṇamabhyahanadrājaṃstriṃśatā kaṅkapatribhiḥ||11||

12dṛṣṭvā satyajitā droṇaṃ grasyamānamivāhave
|
vṛkaḥ śaraśataistīkṣṇaiḥ pāñcālyo droṇamārdayat||12||

13saṃchādyamānaṃ samare droṇaṃ dṛṣṭvā mahāratham
|
cukruśuḥ pāṇḍavā rājan vastrāṇi dudhuvuśca ha||13||

14vṛkastu paramakruddho droṇaṃ ṣaṣṭyā stanāntare
|
vivyādha balavān rājaṃstadadbhutamivābhavat||14||

15droṇastu śaravarṣeṇa cchādyamāno mahārathaḥ
|
vegaṃ cakre mahāvegaḥ krodhāduddhṛtya cakṣuṣī||15||

16tataḥ satyajitaścāpaṃ chittvā droṇo vṛkasya ca
|
paḍbhiḥ sasūtaṃ sahayaṃ śarairdroṇo'vadhīhṛkam||16||

17athānyaddhanurādāya satyajidvegavattaram
|
sāśvaṃ sasūtaṃ viśikhairdroṇaṃ vivyādha sadhvajam||17||

18sa tanna mamṛṣe droṇaḥ pāñcālyenārdito mṛdhe
|
tatastasya vināśāya satvaraṃ vyasṛjaccharān||18||

19hayān dhvajaṃ dhanurmuṣṭimubhau ca pārṣṇisārathī
|
avākirat tato droṇaḥ śaravarṣai sahasraśaḥ||19||

20tathā saṃchidyamāneṣu kārmukeṣu punaḥ punaḥ
|
pāñcālyaḥ paramāstrajñaḥ śoṇāśvaṃ samayodhayat||20||

21sa satyajitamālokya tathodīrṇaṃ mahāhave
|
ardhacandreṇa ciccheda śirastasya mahātmanaḥ||21||

22tasmin hate mahāmātre pañcālānāṃ mahārathe
|
apāyājjavanairaśvairdoṇāt trasto yudhiṣṭhiraḥ||22||

23pañcālāḥ kekayā matsyāścedikārūṣakosalāḥ
|
yudhiṣṭhiramabhīpsanto dṛṣṭvā droṇamupādravan||23||

24tato yudhiṣṭhiraṃ prepsurācāryaḥ śatrupūgahā
|
vyadhamattānyanīkāni tūlarāśimivānalaḥ||24||

25nirdahantamanīkāni tāni tāni punaḥ punaḥ
|
droṇaṃ matsyādavarajaḥ śatānīko'bhyavartata||25||

26sūryaraśmipratīkāśaiḥ karmāraparimārjitaiḥ
|
ṣaḍbhiḥ sasūtaṃ sahayaṃ droṇaṃ viddhvā'nadadṛśam ||26||

27krūrāya karmaṇe yuktaścikīrṣuḥ karma duṣkaram
|
avākiraccharaśatairbhāradvājaṃ mahāratham||27||

28tasya nānadato droṇaḥ śiraḥ kāyāt sakuṇḍalam
|
kṣureṇāpāharat tūrṇaṃ tato matsyāḥ pradudruvuḥ||28||

29matsyāñjitvā'jayaccedīn karūṣān kekayānapi
|
pañcālān sṛñjayān pāṇḍūn bhāradvāja punaḥ punaḥ||29||

30taṃ dahantamanīkāni kruddhamagniṃ yathā vanam
|
dṛṣṭvā rukmarathaṃ vīraṃ samakaṃpanta sṛñjayāḥ||30||

31uttamaṃ hyādadānasya dhanurasvāśukāriṇaḥ
|
jyāghoṣo nighnato'mitrān dikṣu sarvāsu śuśruve||31||

32nāgānaśvān padātīśca rathino gajasādinaḥ
|
raudrā hastavatāmuktāḥ pramathnanti sma sāyakāḥ||32||

33nānadyamānaḥ parjanyo miśravāto himātyaye
|
aśmavarṣabhivāvarṣat pareṣāṃ bhayamādadhat||33||

34sarvā diśaḥ samacarat sainyaṃ vikṣobhayanniva
|
balī śūro maheṣvāso mitrāṇāmabhayaṃkaraḥ||34||

35tasya vidyudivābhreṣu cāpaṃ hemapariṣkṛtam
|
dikṣu sarvāsu paśyāmo droṇasyāmitatejasaḥ||35||

36śobhamānāṃ dhvaje cāsya vedīmadrākṣma bhārata
|
himavacchikharākārāṃ carataḥ saṃyuge bhṛśam||36||

37droṇastu pāṇḍavānīke cakāra kadanaṃ mahat
|
yathā daityagaṇe viṣṇuḥ surāsuranamaskṛtaḥ||37||

38sa śūraḥ satyavāk prājño balavān satyavikramaḥ
|
mahānubhāvaḥ kalpānte raudrāṃ bhīruvibhīṣaṇām||38||

39kavacormidhvajāvartāṃ martyakūlāpahāriṇīm
|
gajavājimahāgrāhāmasimīnāṃ durāsadām||39||

40vīrāsthiśarkarāṃ raudrāṃ bherīmurajakacchapām
|
carmavarmaplavāṃ ghorāṃ keśaśaivalaśādvalām||40||

41śaraudhiṇīṃ dhanuḥsrotāṃ bāhupannagasaṃkulām
|
raṇabhūmivahāṃ tīvrāṃ kurusṛñjayavāhinīn||41||

42manuṣyaśīrṣapāṣāṇāṃ śaktimīnāṃ gadāḍupām
|
uṣṇīṣaphenavasanāṃ vikīrṇāntrasarīsṛpān||42||

43vīrāpahāriṇīmugrāṃ māṃsaśoṇitakardamām
|
hastigrāhāṃ ketuvṛkṣāṃ kṣatriyāṇāṃ nimajjanīm||43||

44krūrāṃ śarīrasaṃghaṭṭāṃ sādinakrāṃ duratyayāt
|
droṇaḥ prāvartayattatra nadīmantakagāminīm||44||

45kravyādagaṇasaṃjuṣṭāṃ śvaśṛgālagaṇāyutām
|
niṣevitāṃ mahāraudraiḥ piśitāśaiḥ samaṃtataḥ||45||

46taṃ dahantamanīkāni rathodāraṃ kṛtāntavat
|
sarvato'bhyadravan droṇaṃ kuntīputrapurogamāḥ||46||

47te droṇaṃ sahitāḥ śūrāḥ sarvataḥ pratyavārayan
|
gabhastibhirivādityaṃ tapantaṃ bhuvanaṃ yathā||47||

48taṃ tu śūraṃ maheṣvāsaṃ tāvakābhyudyatāyudhāḥ
|
rājāno rājaputrāśca samantāt paryavārayan||48||

49śikhaṇḍī tu tato droṇaṃ pañcabhirnataparvabhiḥ
|
kṣatravarmā ca viṃśatyā vasudānaśca pañcabhiḥ||49||

50uttamaujāstribhirbāṇaiḥ kṣatradevaśca saptabhiḥ
|
sātyakiśca śatenājau yudhāmanyustathā'ṣṭabhiḥ||50||

51yudhiṣṭhiro dvādaśabhirdoṇaṃ vivyādha sāyakaiḥ
|
dhṛṣṭadyumnaśca daśabhiścekitānastribhiḥ śaraiḥ||51||

52tato droṇaḥ satyasandhaḥ prabhinna iva kuñjaraḥ
|
abhyatītya rathānīkaṃ dṛḍhasenamapātayat||52||

53tato rājānamāsādya praharantamabhītavat
|
avidhyannavabhiḥ kṣemaṃ sa hataḥ prāpatadrathāt||53||

54sa madhyaṃ prāpya sainyānāṃ sarvāḥ pravicaran diśaḥ
|
trātā hyabhavadanyeṣāṃ na trātavyaḥ kathañcana||54||

55śikhaṇḍinaṃ dvādaśabhirviṃśatyā cottamaujasam
|
vasudānaṃ ca bhallena praiṣayadyamasādanam||55||

56aśītyā kṣatravarmāṇaṃ ṣaḍviṃśatyā sudakṣiṇam
|
kṣatradevaṃ tu bhallena rathanīḍādapātayat||56||

57yudhāmanyuṃ catuḥṣaṣṭyā triṃśatā caiva sātyakim
|
viddhvā rukmarathastūrṇaṃ yudhiṣṭhiramupādravat||57||

58tato yudhiṣṭhiraḥ kṣipraṃ guruto rājasattamaḥ
|
apāyājjavanairaśvaiḥ pāñcālyo droṇamabhyayāt||58||

59taṃ droṇaḥ sadhanuṣkaṃ tu sāśvayantāramākṣiṇot
|
sa hataḥ prāpatadbhūmau rathājjyotirivāmbarāt||59||

60tasmin hate rājaputre pañcālānāṃ yaśaskare
|
hatadroṇaṃ hatadroṇamityāsīnniḥsvano mahān||60||

61tāṃstathā bhṛśasaṃrabdhān pañcālān matsyakekayān
|
sṛñjayān pāṇḍavāṃścaiva droṇo vyakṣobhayadbalī||61||

62sātyakiṃ cekitānaṃ ca dhṛṣṭadyumnaśikhaṇḍinau
|
vārdhakṣemiṃ caitraseniṃ senābinduṃ suvarcasam||62||

63etāṃścānyāṃśca subahūnnānājanapadeśvarān
|
sarvān droṇo'jayadyuddhe kurubhiḥ parivāritaḥ||63||

64tāvakāñca mahārāja jayaṃ labdhvā mahāhave
|
pāṇḍaveyān raṇe jaghnurdravamāṇān samantataḥ||64||

65te dānavā ivendreṇa vadhyamānā mahātmanā
|
pañcālāḥ kekayā matsyāḥ samakampanta bhārata||65||
