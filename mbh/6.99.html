Mahābhārata 6.99 (Bhīṣmaparvan)

1sañjaya uvāca|
tataḥ śāntanavo bhīṣmo niryayau saha senayā
|
vyūhaṃ cāvyūhata mahat sarvatobhadramātmanaḥ ||1||

2kṛpaśca kṛtavarmā ca śaibyaścaiva mahārathaḥ
|
śakuniḥ saindhavaścaiva kāmbojaśca sudakṣiṇaḥ ||2||

3bhīṣmeṇa sahitāḥ sarve putraiśca tava bhārata
|
agrataḥ sarvasainyānāṃ vyūhasya pramukhe sthitāḥ ||3||

4droṇo bhūriśravāḥ śalyo bhagadattaśca māriṣa
|
dakṣiṇaṃ pakṣamāśritya sthitā vyūhasya daṃśitāḥ ||4||

5aśvatthāmā somadattaścāvantyau ca mahārathau
|
mahatyā senayā yuktā vāmaṃ pakṣamapālayan ||5||

6duryodhano mahārāja trigartaiḥ sarvato vṛtaḥ
|
vyūhamadhye sthito rājan pāṇḍavān prati bhārata ||6||

7alambuṣo rathaśreṣṭhaḥ śrutāyuśca mahārathaḥ
|
pṛṣṭhataḥ sarvasainyānāṃ sthitau vyūhasya daṃśitau ||7||

8evaṃ ca taṃ tadā vyūhaṃ kṛtvā bhārata tāvakāḥ
|
sannaddhāḥ samadṛśyanta pratapanta ivāgnayaḥ ||8||

9tato yudhiṣṭhiro rājā bhīmasenaśca pāṇḍavaḥ
|
nakulaḥ sahadevaśca mādrīputrāvubhāvapi ||9||

10agrataḥ sarvasainyānāṃ sthitā vyūhasya daṃśitāḥ
|
dhṛṣṭadyumno virāṭaśca sātyakiśca mahārathaḥ ||10||

11sthitāḥ sainyena mahatā parānīkavināśanāḥ
|
śikhaṇḍī vijayaścaiva rākṣasaśca ghaṭotkacaḥ ||11||

12cekitāno mahābāhuḥ kuntibhojaśca vīryavān
|
sthitā raṇe mahārāja mahatyā senayā vṛtāḥ ||12||

13abhimanyurmaheṣvāso drupadaśca mahābalaḥ
|
yuyudhāno maheṣvāso yudhāmanyuśca vīryavān ||13||

14kekayā bhrātaraścaiva sthitā yuddhāya daṃśitāḥ
|
evaṃ te'pi mahāvyūhaṃ prativyūhya sudurjayam ||14||

15pāṇḍavāḥ samare śūrāḥ sthitā yuddhāya daṃśitāḥ
|
tāvakāstu raṇe yattāḥ sahasenā narādhipāḥ ||15||

16abhyudyayū raṇe pārthān bhīṣmaṃ kṛtvā'grato nṛpa
|
tathaiva pāṇḍavā rājan bhīmasenapurogamāḥ ||16||

17bhīṣmaṃ yoddhumabhīpsantaḥ saṃgrāme vijayaiṣiṇaḥ
|
kṣveḍāḥ kilakilāḥ śaṅkhān krakacān goviṣāṇikāḥ ||17||

18bherī-mṛdaṅga-paṇavānnādayantaśca puṣkarān
|
pāṇḍavā abhyavartanta nadanto bhairavān ravān ||18||

19bherī-mṛdaṅga-śaṅkhānāṃ dundubhīnāṃ ca niḥsvanaḥ
|
utkṛṣṭasiṃhanādaiśca valgitaiśca pṛthagvidhaiḥ ||19||

20vayaṃ pratinadantastānagacchāma tvarānvitāḥ
|
sahasaivābhisaṃkruddhāstadāsīt tumulaṃ mahat ||20||

21tato'nyonyaṃ pradhāvantaḥ saṃprahāraṃ pracakrire
|
tataḥ śabdena mahatā pracakampe vasundharā ||21||

22pakṣiṇaśca mahāghoraṃ vyāharanto vibabhramuḥ
|
saprabhaścoditaḥ sūryo niṣprabhaḥ samapadyata ||22||

23vavuśca vātāstumulāḥ śaṃsantaḥ sumahadbhayam
|
ghorāśca ghoranirhrādāḥ śivāstatra vavāśire ||23||

24vedayantyo mahārāja mahadvaiśasamāgatam
|
diśaḥ prajvalitā rājan pāṃsuvarṣaṃ papāta ca ||24||

25rudhireṇa samunmiśramasthivarṣaṃ tathaiva ca
|
rudatāṃ vāhanānāṃ ca netrebhyaḥ prāpatajjalam ||25||

26susruvuśca śakṛnmūtraṃ pradhyāyanto viśāṃpate
|
antarhitā mahānādāḥ śrūyante bharatarṣabha ||26||

27rakṣasāṃ puruṣādānāṃ nadatāṃ bhairavān ravān
|
saṃpatantaśca dṛśyante gomāyubalavāyasāḥ ||27||

28śvānaśca vividhairnādairvāśantastatra māriṣa
|
jvalitāśca maholkā vai samāhatya divākaram
|
nipetuḥ sahasā bhūmau vedayantyo mahadbhayam ||28||

29mahāntyanīkāni mahāsamucchraye tatastayoḥ pāṇḍavadhārtarāṣṭrayoḥ
|
cakampire śaṅkhamṛdaṅganiḥsvanaiḥ prakampitānīva vanāni vāyunā ||29||

30narendranāgāśvasamākulānāmabhyāyatīnāmaśive muhūrte
|
babhūva ghoṣastumulaścamūnāṃ vātoddhutānāmiva sāgarāṇām ||30||
