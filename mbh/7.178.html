Mahābhārata 7.178 (Droṇaparvan)

1sañjaya uvāca|
saṃdṛśya samare bhīmaṃ rakṣasā grastamantikāt
|
vāsudevo'bravīdrājan ghaṭotkacamidaṃ vacaḥ||1||

2paśya bhīmaṃ mahābāho rakṣasā grastamāhave
|
paśyatāṃ sarvasainyānāṃ tava caiva mahādyute||2||

3sa karṇaṃ tvaṃ samutsṛjya rākṣasendramalāyudham
|
jahi kṣipraṃ mahābāho paścātkarṇaṃ vadhiṣyasi||3||

4sa vārṣṇeyavacaḥ śrutvā karṇamutsṛjya vīryavān
|
yuyudhe rākṣasendreṇa bakabhrātrā ghaṭotkacaḥ||4||

5tayoḥ sutumulaṃ yuddhaṃ babhūva niśi rakṣasoḥ
|
alāyudhasya caivograṃ haiḍimbeścāpi bhārata||5||

6alāyudhasya yodhāṃśca rākṣasān bhīmadarśanān
|
vegenāpatataḥ śūrānpragṛhītaśarāsanān||6||

7āttāyudhaḥ susaṃkruddho yuyudhāno mahārathaḥ
|
nakulaḥ sahadevaśca cicchidurniśitaiḥ śaraiḥ||7||

8sarvāṃśca samare rājankirīṭī kṣatriyarṣabhān
|
paricikṣepa bībhatsuḥ sarvataḥ prakirañcharān||8||

9karṇaśca samare rājan vyadrāvayata pārthivān
|
dhṛṣṭadyumnaśikhaṃḍyādīnpañcālānāṃ mahārathān||9||

10tānvadhyamānāndṛṣṭvā'tha bhīmo bhīmaparākramaḥ
|
abhyayāttvaritaḥ karṇaṃ viśikhānprakiran raṇe||10||

11tataste'pyāyayurhatvā rākṣasānyatra sūtajaḥ
|
nakulaḥ sahadevaśca sātyakiśca mahārathaḥ||11||

12te karṇaṃ yodhayāmāsuḥ pañcālā droṇameva tu
|
alāyudhastu saṃkruddho ghaṭotkacamariṃdamam
|
parigheṇātikāyena tāḍayāmāsa mūrdhani||12||

13sa tu tena prahāreṇa bhaimasenirmahābalaḥ
|
īṣanmūrchitamātmānamastaṃbhayata vīryavān||13||

14tato dīptāgnisaṃkāśāṃ śataghaṇṭāmalaṃkṛtām
|
cikṣepa tasmai samare gadāṃ kāñcanabhūṣitām||14||

15sā hayāṃśca rathaṃ cāsya sārathiṃ ca mahāsvanā
|
cūrṇayāmāsa vegena visṛṣṭā bhīmakarmaṇā||15||

16sa bhagnahayacakrākṣādviśīrṇadhvajakūbarāt
|
utpapāta rathāttūrṇaṃ māyāmāsthāya rākṣasīm||16||

17sa samāsthāya māyāṃ tu vavarṣa rudhiraṃ bahu
|
vidyudvibhrājitaṃ cāsīttumulābhrākulaṃ nabhaḥ||17||

18tato vajranipātāśca sāśanistanayitnavaḥ
|
mahāṃścaṭacaṭāśabdastatrāsīcca mahāhave||18||

19tāṃ prekṣya mahatīṃ māyāṃ rākṣaso rākṣasasya ca
|
ūrdhvamutpatya haiḍimbistāṃ māyāṃ māyayā'vadhīt||19||

20so'bhivīkṣya hatāṃ māyāṃ māyāvī māyayaiva hi
|
aśmavarṣaṃ sutumulaṃ visasarja ghaṭotkace||20||

21aśmavarṣaṃ sa taṃ ghoraṃ śaravarṣeṇa vīryavān
|
dikṣu vidhvaṃsayāmāsa tada‌dbhutamivābhavat||21||

22tato nānāpraharaṇairanyonyamabhivarṣatām
|
āyasaiḥ paridhaiḥ śūlairgadāmusalamudgaraiḥ||22||

23pinākaiḥ karavālaiśca tomaraprāsakampanaiḥ
|
nārācairniśitairbhalaiḥ śaraiścakaiḥ paraśvadhaiḥ
|
ayoguḍairbhindipālairgośīrṣolūkhalairapi||23||

24utpāṭitairmahāśākhairvividhairjagatīruhaiḥ
|
śamīpīlukadaṃbaiśca campakaiścaiva bhārata||24||

25iṅgudairbadarībhiśca kovidāraiśca puṣpitaiḥ
|
palāśaiścārimedaiśca plakṣanyagrodhapippalaiḥ||25||

26mahadbhiḥ samare tasminnanyonyamabhijaghnatuḥ
|
vipulaiḥ parvatāgraiśca nānādhātubhirācitaiḥ||26||

27teṣāṃ śabdo mahānāsīdvajrāṇāṃ bhidyatāmiva
|
yuddhaṃ samabhavaddhoraṃ bhaimyalāyudhayornṛpa||27||

28harīndrayoryathārājanvālisugrīvayoḥ purā
|
tau yuddhvā vividhairghorairāyudhairviśikhaistathā
|
pragṛhya ca śitau khaḍgāvanyonyamabhipetatuḥ||28||

29tāvanyonyamabhidrutya keśeṣu sumahābalau
|
bhujābhyāṃ paryagṛhṇītāṃ mahākāyau mahābalau||29||

30tau svinnagātrau prasvedaṃ susruvāte janādhipa
|
rudhiraṃ ca mahākāyāvativṛṣṭāvivāṃbudau||30||

31athābhipatya vegena samudbhrāmya ca rākṣasam
|
balenākṣipya haiḍimbiścakartāsya śiro mahat||31||

32so'pahṛtya śirastasya kuṇḍalābhyāṃ vibhūṣitam
|
tadā sutumulaṃ nādaṃ nanāda sumahābalaḥ||32||

33hataṃ dṛṣṭvā mahākāyaṃ bakajñātimarindamam
|
pañcālāḥ pāṇḍavāścaiva siṃhanādānvinedire||33||

34tato bherīsahasrāṇi śaṃkhānāmayutāni ca
|
avādayanpāṇḍaveyā rākṣase nihate yudhi||34||

35atīva sā niśā teṣāṃ babhūva vijayāvahā
|
vidyotamānā vibabhau samantāddīpamālinī||35||

36alāyudhasya tu śiro bhaimasenirmahābalaḥ
|
duryodhanasya pramukhe cikṣepa gatacetasaḥ||36||

37atha duryodhano rājā dṛṣṭvā hatamalāyudham
|
babhūva paramodvignaḥ saha sainyena bhārata||37||

38tena hyasya pratijñātaṃ bhīmasenamahaṃ yudhi
|
hanteti svayamāgamya smaratā vairamuttamam||38||

39dhruvaṃ sa tena hantavya ityamanyata pārthivaḥ
|
jīvitaṃ cirakālaṃ hi bhrātṝṇāṃ cāpyamanyata||39||

40sa taṃ dṛṣṭvā vinihataṃ bhīmasenātmajena vai
|
pratijñāṃ bhīmasenasya pūrṇāmevābhyamanyata||40||
