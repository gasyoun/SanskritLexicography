Mahābhārata 12.158 (Śāntiparvan)

1yudhiṣṭhira uvāca|
pāpasya yadadhiṣṭhānaṃ yataḥ pāpaṃ pravartate
|
etadicchāmyahaṃ śrotuṃ tattvena bharatarṣabha ||1||

2bhīṣma uvāca|
pāpasya yadadhiṣṭhānaṃ tacchṛṇuṣva narādhipa
|
eko lobho mahāgrāho lobhātpāpaṃ pravartate ||2||

3ataḥ pāpamadharmaśca tathā duḥkhamanuttamam
|
nikṛtyā mūlametaddhi yena pāpakṛto janāḥ ||3||

4lobhātkrodhaḥ prabhavati lobhātkāmaḥ pravartate
|
lobhānmohaśca māyā ca mānastambhaḥ parāsutā ||4||

5akṣamā hrīparityāgaḥ śrīnāśo dharmasaṃkṣayaḥ
|
abhidhyā'prakhyatā caiva sarvaṃ lobhātpravartate ||5||

6atyāgaścātitarṣaśca vikarmasu ca yāḥ kriyāḥ
|
kulavidyāmadaścaiva rūpaiśvaryamadastathā ||6||

7sarvabhūteṣvabhidrohaḥ sarvabhūteṣvasatkṛtiḥ
|
sarvabhūteṣvaviśvāsaḥ sarvabhūteṣvanārjavam ||7||

8haraṇaṃ paravittānāṃ paradārābhimarśanam
|
vāgvego manaso vego nindāvegastathaiva ca ||8||

9upasthodarayorvego mṛtyuvegaśca dāruṇaḥ
|
īrṣyāvegaśca balavān mithyāvegaśca durjayaḥ ||9||

10rasavegaśca durvāryaḥ śrotravegaśca duḥsahaḥ
|
kutsā vikatthā mātsaryaṃ pāpaṃ duṣkarakāritā ||10||

11sāhasānāṃ ca sarveṣāmakāryāṇāṃ kriyāstathā
|
jātau bālye ca kaumāre yauvane cāpi mānavāḥ ||11||

12na saṃtyajantyātmakarma yo na jīryati jīryataḥ
|
yo na pūrayituṃ śakyo lobhaḥ prāptyā kurūdvaha ||12||

13nityaṃ gambhīratoyābhirāpagābhirivodadhiḥ
|
na prahṛṣyati yo lobhaiḥ kāmairyaśca na tṛpyati ||13||

14yoṃ na devairna gandharvairnāsurairna mahoragaiḥ
|
jñāyate nṛpa tattvena sarvairbhūtagaṇaistathā ||14||

15sa lobhaḥ saha mohena vijetavyo jitātmanā
|
dambho drohaśca nindā ca paiśunyaṃ matsarastathā ||15||

16bhavantyetāni kauravya lubdhānāmakṛtātmanām
|
sumahāntyapi śāstrāṇi dhārayanti bahuśrutāḥ ||16||

17chettāraḥ saṃśayānāṃ ca kliśyantīhālpabuddhayaḥ
|
dveṣakrodhaprasaktāśca śiṣṭācārabahiṣkṛtāḥ ||17||

18antaḥkrūrā vāṅmadhurāḥ kūpāśchannāstṛṇairiva
|
dharmavaitaṃsikāḥ kṣudrā muṣṇanti dhvajino jagat ||18||

19kurvate ca bahūnmārgāṃstān hetubalamāśritāḥ
|
satāṃ mārgān vilumpanti lobhājñāneṣvavasthitāḥ ||19||

20dharmasya hriyamāṇasya lobhagrastairdurātmabhiḥ
|
yā yā vikriyate saṃsthā tataḥ sā'pi prapadyate ||20||

21darpaḥ krodho madaḥ svapno harṣaḥ śoko'timānitā
|
eva eva hi kauravya dṛśyante lubdhabuddhiṣu ||21||

22etānaśiṣṭānbudhyasva nityaṃ lobhasamanvitān
|
śiṣṭāṃstu paripṛcchethā yānvakṣyāmi śucivratān ||22||

23yeṣvāvṛttibhayaṃ nāsti paralokabhayaṃ na ca
|
nāmiṣeṣu prasaṃgo'sti na priyeṣvapriyeṣu ca ||23||

24śiṣṭācāraḥ priyo yeṣu damo yeṣu pratiṣṭhitaḥ
|
sukhaṃ duḥkhaṃ samaṃ yeṣāṃ satyaṃ yeṣāṃ parāyaṇam ||24||

25dātāro na grahītāro dayāvantastathaiva ca
|
pitṛdevātitheyāśca nityodyuktāstathaiva ca ||25||

26sarvopakāriṇo vīrāḥ sarvadharmānupālakāḥ
|
sarvabhūtahitāścaiva sarvadeyāśca bhārata ||26||

27na te cālayituṃ śakyā dharmavyāpārakāriṇaḥ
|
na teṣāṃ bhidyate vṛttaṃ yatpurā sādhubhiḥ kṛtam ||27||

28na trāsino na capalā na raudrāḥ satpathe sthitāḥ
|
te sevyāḥ sādhubhirnityaṃ yeṣvahiṃsā pratiṣṭhitā ||28||

29kāmakrodhavyapetā ye nirmamā nirahaṃkṛtāḥ
|
suvratāḥ sthiramaryādāstānupāsva ca pṛccha ca ||29||

30na dhanārthaṃ yaśo'rthaṃ vā dharmasteṣāṃ yudhiṣṭhira
|
avaśyaṃ kārya ityeva śarīrasya kriyāstathā ||30||

31na bhayaṃ krodhacāpalye na śokasteṣu vidyate
|
na dharmadhvajinaścaiva na guhyaṃ kañcidāsthitāḥ ||31||

32yeṣvalobhastathā moho ye ca satyārjave sthitāḥ
|
teṣu kaunteya rajyethā yeṣāṃ na bhraśyate punaḥ ||32||

33ye na hṛṣyanti lābheṣu nālābheṣu vyathanti ca
|
nirmamā nirahaṃkārāḥ sattvasthāḥ samadarśinaḥ ||33||

34lābhālābhau sukhaduḥkhe ca tāta priyāpriye maraṇaṃ jīvitaṃ ca
|
samāni yeṣāṃ sthiravikramāṇāṃ bubhutsatāṃ sattvapathe sthitānām ||34||

35dharmapriyāṃstānsumahānubhāvān dānto'pramattaśca samarcayethāḥ
|
daivānsarve guṇavanto bhavanti śubhāśubhe vākpralāpāstathānye ||35||
