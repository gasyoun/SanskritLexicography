Mahābhārata 9.2 (Śalyaparvan)

1vaiśampāyana uvāca|
visṛṣṭāsvatha nārīṣu dhṛtarāṣṭro'mbikāsutaḥ
|
vilalāpa mahārāja duḥkhād duḥkhāntaraṃ gataḥ||1||

2sadhūmamiva niḥśvasya karau dhunvanpunaḥ punaḥ
|
vicintya ca mahārāja vacanaṃ cedamabravīt||2||

3dhṛtarāṣṭra uvāca|
aho bata mahadduḥkhaṃ yadahaṃ pāṇḍavān raṇe
|
kṣemiṇaścāvyayāṃścaiva tvattaḥ sūta śṛṇomi vai||3||

4vajrasāramayaṃ nūnaṃ hṛdayaṃ sudṛḍhaṃ mama
|
yacchrutvā nihatānputrān dīryate na sahasradhā||4||

5cintayitvā vayasteṣāṃ bālakrīḍāṃ ca sañjaya
|
hatānputrānaśeṣeṇa dīryate me bhṛśaṃ manaḥ||5||

6anetratvādyadeteṣāṃ na me rūpanidarśanam
|
putrasnehakṛtā prītirnityameteṣu dhāritā||6||

7bālabhāvamatikramya yauvanasthāṃśca tānaham
|
madhyaprāptāṃstathā śrutvā hṛṣṭa āsaṃ tadānagha||7||

8tānadya nihatāñśrutvā hataiśvaryān hataujasaḥ
|
na labheyaṃ kvacicchāntiṃ putrādhibhirabhiplutaḥ||8||

9ehyehi putra rājendra mamānāthasya sāmpratam
|
tvayā hīno mahābāho kāṃ nu yāsyāmyahaṃ gatim||9||

10kathaṃ tvaṃ pṛthivīpālāṃstyaktvā tāta samāgatān
|
śeṣe vinihato bhūmau prākṛtaḥ kunṛpo yathā||10||

11gatirbhūtvā mahārāja jñātīnāṃ suhṛdāṃ tathā
|
andhaṃ vṛddhaṃ ca māṃ vīra vihāya kva nu yāsyasi||11||

12sā kṛpā sā ca te prītiḥ sā rājansumānitā
|
kathaṃ vinihataḥ pārthaiḥ saṃyugeṣvaparājitaḥ||12||

13ko nu māmutthitaṃ vīra tāta tāteti vakṣyati
|
mahārājeti satataṃ lokanātheti tāsakṛt||13||

14pariṣvajya ca māṃ kaṇṭhe snehena kinnalocanaḥ
|
anuśādhīti kauravya tatsādhu vada me vacaḥ||14||

15nanu nāmāhamaśrauṣaṃ vacanaṃ tava putraka
|
bhūyasī mama pṛthvīyaṃ yathā pārthasya no tathā||15||

16bhagadattaḥ kṛpaḥ śalya āvantyo'tha jayadrathaḥ
|
bhūriśravāḥ somadatto mahārājaśca bāhnikaḥ||16||

17aśvatthāmā ca bhojaśca māgadhaśca mahābalaḥ
|
bṛhadvalaśca kāśīśaḥ śakuniścāpi saubalaḥ||17||

18mlecchaśca śatasāhasrāḥ śakāścaḥ yavanaiḥ saha
|
sudakṣiṇaśca kāmbojastrigartādhipatistathā||18||

19bhīṣmaḥ pitāmahaścaiva bhāradvājo'tha gautamaḥ
|
śrutāyuścāyutāyuśca śatāyuścāpi vīryavān||19||

20jalasandho'thārṣyaśṛṅgī rākṣasaścāpyalāyudhaḥ
|
alambuṣo mahābāhuḥ subāhuśca mahārathaḥ||20||

21ete cānye ca bahavo rājāno rājasattama
|
madarthamudyatāḥ sarve prāṇāṃstyaktvā dhanāni ca||21||

22teṣāṃ madhye sthito yuddhe bhrātṛbhiḥ parivāritaḥ
|
yodhayiṣyāmyahaṃ pārthānpañcālāṃścaiva sarvaśaḥ||22||

23cedīṃśca nṛpaśārdūla draupadeyāṃśca saṃyuge
|
sātyakiṃ kuntibhojaṃ ca rākṣasaṃ ca ghaṭotkacam||23||

24eko'pyeṣāṃ mahārāja samarthaḥ sannivāraṇe
|
samare pāṇḍaveyānāṃ saṅkruddho hyabhidhāvatām||24||

25kiṃ punaḥ sahitā vīrāḥ kṛtavairāśca pāṇḍavaiḥ
|
athavā sarva evaite pāṇḍavasyānuyāyibhiḥ||25||

26yotsyante saha rājendra haniṣyanti ca tān mṛghe
|
karṇa eko mayā sārdhaṃ nihaniṣyati pāṇḍavān||26||

27tato nṛpatayo vīrāḥ sthāsyanti mama śāsane
|
yaśca teṣāṃ praṇetā vai vāsudevo mahābalaḥ||27||

28na sa sannahyate rājanniti māmabravīdvacaḥ
|
tasyātha vadataḥ sūta bahuśo mama sannidhau||28||

29śaktito hyanupaśyāmi nihatānpāṇḍavān raṇe
|
teṣāṃ madhye sthitā yatra hanyante mama putrakāḥ||29||

30vyāyacchamānāḥ samare kimanyadbhāgadheyataḥ
|
bhīṣmaśca nihato yatra lokanāthaḥ pratāpavān||30||

31śikhaṇḍinaṃ samāsādya mṛgendra iva jambukam
|
droṇaśca brāhmaṇo yatra sarvaśastrāstrapāragaḥ||31||

32nihataḥ pāṇḍavaiḥ saṅkhye kimanyadbhāgadheyataḥ
|
karṇaśca nihataṃ saṅkhye divyāstrajño mahābalaḥ||32||

33bhūriśravā hato yatra somadattaśca saṃyuge
|
bāhlikaśca mahārāja kimanyadbhāgadheyataḥ||33||

34bhagadatto hato yatra gajayuddhaviśāradaḥ
|
jayadrathaśca nihataḥ kimanyadbhādadheyataḥ||34||

35sudakṣiṇo hato yatra jalasandhaśca pauravaḥ
|
śrutāyuścāyutāyuśca kimanyadbhāgadheyataḥ||35||

36mahābalastathā pāṇḍayaḥ sarvaśastrabhṛtāṃ varaḥ
|
nihataḥ pāṇḍavaiḥ saṅkhye kimanyadbhāgadheyataḥ||36||

37bṛhadbalo hato yatra māgadhaśca mahābalaḥ
|
ugrāyudhaśca vikrāntaḥ pratimānaṃ dhanuṣmatām||37||

38āvantyo nihato yatra traigartaśca janādhipaḥ
|
saṃśaptakāśca nihatāḥ kimanyadbhāgadheyataḥ||38||

39alambuṣastathā rājan rākṣasaścāpyalāyudhaḥ
|
ārṣyaśṛṅgiśca nihataḥ kimanyadbhāgadheyataḥ||39||

40nārāyaṇā hatā yatra gopālā yuddhadurmadāḥ
|
mlecchāśca bahusāhasrāḥ kimanyadbhāgadheyataḥ||40||

41śakuniḥ saubalo yatra kaitavyaśca mahābalaḥ
|
nihataḥ sabalo vīraḥ kimanyadbhāgadheyataḥ||41||

42ete cānye ca bahavaḥ kṛtāstrā yuddhadurmadāḥ
|
rājāno rājaputrāśca śūrāḥ parighabāhavaḥ||42||

43nihatā bahavo yatra kimanyadbhāgadheyataḥ
|
yatra śūrā maheṣvāsāḥ kṛtāstrā yuddhadurmadāḥ||43||

44bahavo nihatāḥ sūta mahendrasamavikramāḥ
|
nānādeśasamāvṛttāḥ kṣatriyā yatra sañjaya||44||

45nihatāḥ samare sarve kimanyadbhāgadheyataḥ
|
putrāśca me vinihatāḥ pautrāścaiva mahābalāḥ||45||

46vayasyā bhrātaraścaiva kimanyadbhāgadheyataḥ
|
bhāgadheyasamāyukto dhruvamutpadyate naraḥ||46||

47yastu bhāgyasamāyuktaḥ sa śubhaṃ prāpnuyānnaraḥ
|
ahaṃ viyuktastairbhāgyaiḥ putraiścaiveha sañjaya||47||

48kathamadya bhaviṣyāmi vṛddhaḥ śatruvaśaṃ gataḥ
|
nānyadatra paraṃ manye vanavāsādṛte prabho||48||

49so'haṃ vanaṃ gamiṣyāmi nirbandhurjñātisaṃkṣaye
|
na hi me'nyadbhavecchreyo vanābhyupagamādṛte||49||

50imāmavasthāṃ prāptasya lūnapakṣasya sañjaya
|
duryodhano hato yatra śalyaśca nihato yudhi||50||

51duḥśāsano viviṃśaśca vikarṇaśca mahābalaḥ
|
kathaṃ hi bhīmasenasya śroṣye'haṃ śabdamuttamam||51||

52ekena samare yena hataṃ putraśataṃ mama
|
asakṛdvadatastasya duryodhanavadhena ca||52||

53duḥkhaśokābhisantapto na śroṣye paruṣā giraḥ
|
vaiśampāyana uvāca|
evaṃ vṛddhaśca santaptaḥ pārthivo hatabāndhavaḥ||53||

54muhurmuhurmuhyamānaḥ putrādibhirabhiplutaḥ
|
vilapya suciraṃ kālaṃ dhṛtarāṣṭro'mbikāsutaḥ||54||

55dīrghamuṣṇaṃ sa niḥśvasya cintayitvā parābhavam
|
duḥkhena mahatā rājansantapto bharatarṣabhaḥ||55||

56punargāvalgaṇiṃ sūtaṃ paryapṛcchadyathātatham
|
dhṛtarāṣṭra uvāca|
bhīṣmadrauṇau hatau śrutvā sūtaputraṃ ca ghātitam||56||

57senāpatiṃ praṇetāraṃ kimakurvata māmakāḥ
|
yaṃ yaṃ senāpraṇetāraṃ yudhi kurvanti māmakāḥ||57||

58acireṇaiva kālena taṃ taṃ nighnanti pāṇḍavāḥ
|
raṇamūrdhni hato bhīṣmaḥ paśyatāṃ vaḥ kirīṭinā||58||

59evameva hato droṇaḥ sarveṣāmeva paśyatām
|
evameva hataḥ karṇaḥ sūtaputraḥ pratāpavān||59||

60sa rājakānāṃ sarveṣāṃ paśyatāṃ vaḥ kirīṭinā
|
pūrvamevāhamukto vai vidureṇa mahātmanā||60||

61duryodhanāparādhena prajeyaṃ vinaśiṣyati
|
kecinna samyakpaśyanti mūḍhāḥ samyagavekṣya ca
|
tadidaṃ mama mūḍhasya tathābhūtaṃ vacaḥ sma tat||61||

62yadabravīt sa dharmātmā viduro dīrghadarśivān
|
tattathā samanuprāptaṃ vacanaṃ satyavādinaḥ||62||

63daivopahatacittena yanmayā na kṛtaṃ purā
|
anayasya phalaṃ tasya brūhi gāvalgaṇe punaḥ||63||

64ko vā mukhamanīkānāmāsītkarṇe nipātite
|
arjunaṃ vāsudevaṃ ca ko vā pratyudyayau rathī||64||

65ke'rakṣan dakṣiṇaṃ cakraṃ madrarājasya saṃyuge
|
vāmaṃ ca yoddhukāmasya ke vā vīrasya pṛṣṭhataḥ||65||

66kathaṃ ca vaḥ sametānāṃ madrarājo mahārathaḥ
|
nihataḥ pāṇḍavaiḥ saṅkhye putro vā mama sañjaya||66||

67brūhi sarvaṃ yathātattvaṃ bharatānāṃ mahākṣayam
|
yathā ca nihataḥ saṅkhye putro duryodhano mama||67||

68pañcālānāṃ yathā sarve nihatāḥ sapadānugāḥ
|
dhṛṣṭadyumnaḥ śikhaṇḍī ca draupadyāḥ pañca cātmajāḥ||68||

69pāṇḍavāśca yathā muktāstathobhau mādhavau yudhi
|
kṛpaśca kṛtavarmā ca bhāradvājasya cātmajaḥ||69||

70yadyathā yādṛśaṃ caiva yuddhaṃ vṛttaṃ ca sāmpratam
|
akhilaṃ śrotumicchāmi kuśalo hyasi sañjaya||70||
