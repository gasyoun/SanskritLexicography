Mahābhārata 1.61 (Ādiparvan)

1vaiśaṃpāyana uvāca|
gurave prāṅnamaskṛtya manobuddhisamādhibhiḥ
|
saṃpūjya ca dvijānsarvāṃstathānyānviduṣo janān||1||

2maharṣerviśrutasyeha sarvalokeṣu dhīmataḥ
|
pravakṣyāmi mataṃ kṛtsnaṃ vyāsasyāsya mahātmanaḥ||2||

3śrotuṃ pātraṃ ca rājaṃstvaṃ prāpyemāṃ bhāratīṃ kathām
|
gurorvaktraparispando manaḥ protsāhatīva me||3||

4śṛṇu rājanyathā bhedaḥ kurupāṇḍavayorabhūt
|
rājyārthe dyūtasaṃbhūto vanavāsastathaiva ca||4||

5yathā ca yuddhamabhavatpṛthivīkṣayakārakam
|
tatte'haṃ kathayiṣyāmi pṛcchate bharatarṣabha||5||

6mṛte pitari te vīrā vanādetya svamandiram
|
na cirādeva vidvāṃso vede dhanuṣi cābhavan||6||

7tāṃstathā satvavīryaujaḥ saṃpannānpaurasaṃmatān
|
nāmṛṣyankuravo dṛṣṭā pāṇḍavāñśrīyaśobhṛtaḥ||7||

8tato duryodhanaḥ krūraḥ karṇaśca sahasaubalaḥ
|
teṣāṃ nigrahanirvāsānvividhāṃste samārabhan||8||

9tato duryodhanaḥ śūraḥ kuliṅgasya mate sthitaḥ
|
pāṇḍavānvividhopāyai rājyahetorapīḍayat||9||

10dadāvatha viṣaṃ pāpo bhīmāya dhṛtarāṣṭrajaḥ
|
jarayāmāsa tadvīraḥ sahānnena vṛkodaraḥ||10||

11pramāṇakoṭyāṃ saṃsuptaṃ punarbaddhvā vṛkodaram
|
toyeṣu bhīmaṃ gaṅgāyāḥ prakṣipya puramāvrajat||11||

12yadā vibuddhaḥ kaunteyastadā saṃcchidya bandhanam
|
udatiṣṭhanmahābāhurbhīmaseno gatavyathaḥ||12||

13āśīviṣaiḥ kṛṣṇasarpaiḥ suptaṃ cainamadaṃśayat
|
sarveṣvevāṅgadeśeṣu na mamāra sa śatruhā||13||

14teṣāṃ tu viprakāreṣu teṣu teṣu mahāmatiḥ
|
mokṣaṇe pratikāre ca viduro'vahito'bhavat||14||

15svargastho jīvalokasya yathā śakraḥ sukhāvahaḥ
|
pāṇḍavānāṃ tathā nityaṃ viduro'pi sukhāvahaḥ||15||

16yadā tu vividhopāyaiḥ saṃvṛtairvivṛtairapi
|
nāśakadvinihantuṃ tāndaivabhāvyartharakṣitān||16||

17tataḥ saṃmantrya sacivairvṛṣaduḥśāsanādibhiḥ
|
dhṛtarāṣṭramanujñāpya jātuṣaṃ gṛhamādiśat||17||

18sutapriyaiṣī tānrājā pāṇḍavānambikāsutaḥ
|
tato vivāsayāmāsa rājyabhogabubhukṣayā||18||

19te prātiṣṭhanta sahitā nagarānnāgasāhvayāt
|
prasthāne cābhavanmantrī kṣattā teṣāṃ mahātmanām||19||

20tena muktā jatugṛhānniśīthe prādravanvanam
|
tataḥ saṃprāpya kaunteyā nagaraṃ vāraṇāvatam||20||

21nyavasanta mahātmāno mātrā saha parantapāḥ
|
dhṛtarāṣṭreṇa cājñaptā uṣitā jātuṣe gṛhe||21||

22purocanādrakṣamāṇāḥ saṃvatsaramatandritāḥ
|
suruṅgāṃ kārayitvā tu vidureṇa pracoditāḥ||22||

23ādīpya jātuṣaṃ veśma dagdhvā caiva purocanam
|
prādravanbhayasaṃvignā mātrā saha pantapāḥ||23||

24dadṛśurdāruṇaṃ rakṣo hiḍimbaṃ vananirjhare
|
hatvā ca taṃ rākṣasendraṃ bhītāḥ samavabodhanāt||24||

25niśi saṃprādravanpārthā dhārtarāṣṭrabhayārditāḥ
|
prāptā hiḍimbā bhīmena yatra jāto ghaṭotkacaḥ||25||

26ekacakrāṃ tato hatvā pāṇḍavāḥ saṃśitavratāḥ
|
vedādhyayanasaṃpannāste'bhavanbrahmacāriṇaḥ||26||

27te tatra niyatāḥ kālaṃ kaṃcidūṣurnararṣabhāḥ
|
mātrā sahaikacakrāyāṃ brāhmaṇasya niveśane||27||

28tatrāsasāda kṣudhitaṃ puruṣādaṃ vṛkodaraḥ
|
bhīmaseno mahābāhurbakaṃ nāma mahābalam||28||

29taṃ cāpi puruṣavyāghro bāhuvīryeṇa pāṇḍavaḥ
|
nihatya tarasā vīro nāgarānparyasāntvayat||29||

30tataste śuśruvuḥ kṛṣṇāṃ pañcāleṣu svayaṃvarām
|
śrutvā caivābhyagacchta gatvā caivālabhanta tām||30||

31te tatra draupadīṃ labdhvā parisaṃvatsaroṣitāḥ
|
viditā hāstinapuraṃ pratyājagmurarindamāḥ||31||

32te uktā dhṛtarāṣṭreṇa rājñā śāntanavena ca
|
bhrātṛbhirvigrahastāta kathaṃ vo na bhavediti||32||

33asmābhiḥ khāṇḍavaprasthe yuṣmadvāso'nucintitaḥ
|
tasmājjanapadopetaṃ suvibhaktamahāpatham||33||

34vāsāya khāṇḍavaprasthaṃ vrajadhvaṃ gatamatsarāḥ
|
tayoste vacanājjagmuḥ saha sarvaiḥ suhṛjjanaiḥ||34||

35nagaraṃ khāṇḍavaprasthaṃ ratnānyādāya sarvaśaḥ
|
tatra te nyavasanpārthāḥ saṃvatsaragaṇāṃnbahūn||35||

36vaśe śastrapratāpena kurvanto'nyānmahībhṛtaḥ
|
evaṃ dharmapradhānāste satyavrataparāyaṇāḥ||36||

37apramattotthitāḥ kṣāntāḥ pratapanto'hitānbahūn
|
ajayadbhīmasenastu diśaṃ prācīṃ mahāyaśāḥ||37||

38udīcīmarjuno vīraḥ pratīcīṃ nakulastathā
|
dakṣiṇāṃ sahadevastu vijigye paravīrahā||38||

39evaṃ cakrurimāṃ sarve vaśe kṛtsnāṃ vasundharām
|
pañcabhiḥ sūryasaṅkāśaiḥ sūryeṇa ca virājatā||39||

40ṣaṭsūryevābhavatpṛthvī pāṇḍavaiḥ satyavikramaiḥ
|
tato nimitte kasmiṃściddharmarājo yudhiṣṭhiraḥ||40||

41vanaṃ prasthāpayāmāsa tejasvī satyavikramaḥ
|
prāṇebhyo'pi priyataraṃ bhrātaraṃ savyasācinam||41||

42arjunaṃ puruṣavyāghraṃ sthirātmānaṃ guṇairyutam
|
sa vai saṃvatsaraṃ pūrṇaṃ māsaṃ caikaṃ vane vasan||42||

43tato'gacchaddhṛṣīkeśaṃ dvāravatyāṃ kadācana
|
labdhavāṃstatra bībhatsurbhāryāṃ rājīvalocanām||43||

44anujāṃ vāsudevasya subhadrāṃ bhadrabhāṣiṇīm
|
sā śacīva mahendreṇa śrīḥ kṛṣṇeneva saṅgatā||44||

45subhadrā yuyuje prītyā pāṇḍavenārjunena ha
|
atarpayacca kaunteyaḥ khāṇḍave havyavāhanam||45||

46bībhatsurvāsudevena sahito nṛpasattama
|
nātibhāro hi pārthasya keśavena sahābhavat||46||

47vyavasāyasahāyasya viṣṇoḥ śatruvadheṣviva
|
pārthāyāgnirdadau cāpi gāṇḍīvaṃ dhanuruttamam||47||

48iṣudhī cākṣayairbāṇai rathaṃ ca kapilakṣaṇam
|
mokṣayāmāsa bībhatsurmayaṃ yatra mahāsuram||48||

49sa cakāra sabhāṃ divyāṃ sarvaratnasamācitām
|
tasyāṃ duryodhano mando lobhaṃ cakre sudurmatiḥ||49||

50tato'kṣairvañcayitvā ca saubalena yudhiṣṭhiram
|
vanaṃ prasthāpayāmāsa saptavarṣāṇi pañca ca||50||

51ajñātamekaṃ rāṣṭre ca tato varṣaṃ trayodaśam
|
tataścaturdaśe varṣe yācamānāḥ svakaṃ vasu||51||

52nālabhanta mahārāja tato yuddhamavartata
|
tataste kṣatramutsādya hatvā duryodhanaṃ nṛpam||52||

53rājyaṃ vihatabhūyiṣṭhaṃ pratyapadyanta pāṇḍavāḥ
|
evametatpurāvṛttaṃ teṣāmakliṣṭakarmaṇām
|
bhedo rājyavināśaśca jayaśca jayatāṃvara||53||
