Mahābhārata 2.76 (Sabhāparvan)

1vaiśampāyana uvāca|
tato vyadhvagataṃ pārthaṃ prātikāmī yudhiṣṭhiram
|
uvāca vacanādrājño dhṛtarāṣṭrasya dhīmataḥ||1||

2upāstīrṇā sabhā rājannakṣānuptvā yudhiṣṭhira
|
ehi pāṇḍava dīvyeti pitā tvāheti bhārata||2||

3yudhiṣṭhira uvāca|
dhāturniyogādbhūtāni prāpnuvanti śubhāśubham
|
na nivṛttistayorasti devitavyaṃ punaryadi||3||

4akṣadyūte samāhvānaṃ niyogātsthavirasya ca
|
jānannapi kṣayakaraṃ nātikramitumutsahe||4||

5vaiśampāyana uvāca|
asambhave hemamayasya jantostathāpi rāmo lulubhe mṛgāya
|
prāyaḥ samāsannaparābhavāṇāṃ dhiyo viparyastatarā bhavanti||5||

6iti bruvannivavṛte bhrātṛbhiḥ saha pāṇḍavaḥ
|
jānanśca śakunermāyāṃ pārtho dyūtamiyātpunaḥ||6||

7viviśuste sabhāṃ tāṃ tu punareva mahārathāḥ
|
vyathayanti sma cetāṃsi suhṛdāṃ bharatarṣabhāḥ||7||

8yathopajoṣamāsīnāḥ punardyūtapravṛttaye
|
sarvalokavināśāya daivenopanipīḍitāḥ||8||

9śakuniruvāca| amuñcatsthaviro yadvo dhanaṃ pūjitameva tat
|
mahādhanaṃ glahaṃ tvekaṃ śṛṇu bho bharatarṣabha||9||

10vayaṃ vā dvādaśābdāni yuṣmābhirdyūtanirjitāḥ
|
praviśema mahāraṇyaṃ rauravājinavāsasaḥ||10||

11trayodaśaṃ ca sajane ajñātāḥ parivatsaram
|
jñātāśca punaranyāni vane varṣāṇi dvādaśa||11||

12asmābhirnirjitā yūyaṃ vane dvādaśa vatsarān
|
vasadhvaṃ kṛṣṇayā sārdhamajinaiḥ prativāsitāḥ||12||

13trayodaśaṃ ca sajane ajñātāḥ parivatsaram
|
jñātāśca punaranyāni vane varṣāṇi dvādaśa||13||

14trayodaśe ca nirvṛtte punareva yathocitam
|
svarājyaṃ pratipattavyamitarairathavetaraiḥ||14||

15anena vyavasāyena sahāsmābhiryudhiṣṭhira
|
akṣānuptvā punardyūtamehi dīvyasva bhārata||15||

16atha sabhyāḥ sabhāmadhye samucchritakarāstadā
|
ūcurudvignamanasaḥ saṃvegātsarva eva hi||16||

17sabhyā ūcuḥ| aho dhigbāndhavā nainaṃ bodhayanti mahadbhayam
|
buddhyā buddhyenna vā buddhyedayaṃ vai bharatarṣabha||17||

18vaiśampāyana uvāca|
janapravādānsubahūñśṛṇvannapi narādhipaḥ
|
hriyā ca dharmasaṃyogātpārtho dyūtamiyātpunaḥ||18||

19jānannāpi mahābuddhiḥ punardyūtamavartayat
|
apyāsanno vināśaḥ syātkurūṇāmiticintayan||19||

20yudhiṣṭhira uvāca|
kathaṃ vai madvidho rājā svadharmamanupālayan
|
āhūto vinivarteta dīvyāmi śakune tvayā||20||

21śakuniruvāca| gavāśvaṃ bahudhenukamaparyantamajāvikam
|
gajāḥ kośo hiraṇyaṃ ca dāsīdāsāśca sarvaśaḥ||21||

22eṣa no glaha evaiko vanavāsāya pāṇḍavāḥ
|
yūyaṃ vayaṃ vā vijitā vasema vanamāśritāḥ||22||

23trayodaśaṃ ca vai varṣamajñātāḥ sajane tathā
|
anena vyavasāyena dīvyāma puruṣarṣabhāḥ||23||

24samutkṣepeṇa caikena vanavāsāya bhārata
|
pratijagrāha taṃ pārtho glahaṃ jagrāha saubalaḥ
|
jitamityeva śakuniryudhiṣṭhiramabhāṣata||24||
