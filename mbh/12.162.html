Mahābhārata 12.162 (Śāntiparvan)

1yudhiṣṭhira uvāca|
satyaṃ dharmaṃ praśaṃsanti viprarṣipitṛdevatāḥ
|
satyamicchāmyahaṃ śrotuṃ tanme brūhi pitāmaha ||1||

2satyaṃ kiṃlakṣaṇaṃ rājan kathaṃ vā tadavāpyate
|
satyaṃ prāpya bhavetkiṃ ca kathaṃ caiva taducyatām ||2||

3bhīṣma uvāca|
cāturvarṇyasya dharmāṇāṃ saṃkaro na praśasyate
|
avikāritamaṃ satyaṃ sarvavarṇeṣu bhārata ||3||

4satyaṃ satsu sadā dharmaḥ satyaṃ dharmaḥ sanātanaḥ
|
satyameva namasyeta satyaṃ hi paramā gatiḥ ||4||

5satyaṃ dharmastapo yogaḥ satyaṃ brahma sanātanam
|
satyaṃ yajñaḥ paraḥ proktaḥ sarvaṃ satye pratiṣṭhitam ||5||

6ācārāniha satyasya yathāvadanupūrvaśaḥ
|
lakṣaṇaṃ ca pravakṣyāmi satyasyeha yathākramam ||6||

7prāpyate ca yathā satyaṃ tacca śrotumihārhasi
|
satyaṃ trayodaśavidhaṃ sarvalokeṣu bhārata ||7||

8satyaṃ ca samatā caiva damaścaiva na saṃśayaḥ
|
amātsaryaṃ kṣamā caiva hrīstitikṣā'nasūyatā ||8||

9tyāgo dhyānamathāryatvaṃ dhṛtiśca satataṃ sthirā
|
ahiṃsā caiva rājendra satyākārāstrayodaśa ||9||

10satyaṃ nāmāvyayaṃ nityamavikāri tathaiva ca
|
sarvadharmāviruddhena yogenaitadavāpyate ||10||

11ātmanīṣṭe tathā'niṣṭe ripau ca samatā tathā
|
icchādveṣaṃ kṣayaṃ prāpya kāmakrodhakṣayaṃ tathā ||11||

12damo nānyaspṛhā nityaṃ gāmbhīryaṃ dhairyameva ca
|
abhayaṃ rogaśamanaṃ jñānenaitadavāpyate ||12||

13amātsaryaṃ budhāḥ prāhurdāne dharme ca saṃyamaḥ
|
avasthitena nityaṃ ca satyenāmatsarī bhavet ||13||

14akṣamāyāḥ kṣamāyāśca priyāṇīhāpriyāṇi ca
|
kṣamate sammataḥ sādhuḥ sādhvāpnoti ca satyavāk ||14||

15kalyāṇaṃ kurute bāḍhaṃ dhīmānna glāyate kvacit
|
praśāntavāṅmanā nityaṃ hrīstu dharmādavāpyate ||15||

16dharmārthahetoḥ kṣamate titikṣā kṣāntirucyate
|
lokasaṃgrahaṇārthaṃ vai sā tu dhairyeṇa labhyate ||16||

17tyāgaḥ snehasya yattyāgo viṣayāṇāṃ tathaiva ca
|
rāgadveṣaprahīṇasya tyāgo bhavati nānyathā ||17||

18āryatā nāma bhūtānāṃ yaḥ karoti prayatnataḥ
|
śubhaṃ karma nirākāro vītarāgastathaiva ca ||18||

19dhṛtirnāma sukhe duḥkhe yathā nāpnoti vikriyām
|
tāṃ bhajeta sadā prājño ya icchedbhūtimātmanaḥ ||19||

20sarvathā kṣamiṇā bhāvyaṃ tathā satyapareṇa ca
|
vītaharṣabhayakrodho dhṛtimāpnoti paṇḍitaḥ ||20||

21adrohaḥ sarvabhūteṣu karmaṇā, manasā, girā
|
anugrahaśca dānaṃ ca satāṃ dharmaḥ sanātanaḥ ||21||

22ete trayodaśākārāḥ pṛthak satyaikalakṣaṇāḥ
|
bhajante satyameveha bṛṃhayante ca bhārata ||22||

23nāntaḥ śakyo guṇānāṃ ca vaktuṃ satyasya pārthiva
|
ataḥ satyaṃ praśaṃsanti viprāḥ sapitṛdevatāḥ ||23||

24nāsti satyātparo dharmo nānṛtātpātakaṃ param
|
śrutirhi satyaṃ dharmasya tasmātsatyaṃ na lopayet ||24||

25upaiti satyāddānaṃ hi tathā yajñāḥ sadakṣiṇāḥ
|
tretāgnihotraṃ vedāśca ye cānye dharmaniścayāḥ ||25||

26aśvamedhasahasraṃ ca satyaṃ ca tulayā dhṛtam
|
aśvamedhasahasrāddhi satyameva viśiṣyate ||26||
