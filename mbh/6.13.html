Mahābhārata 6.13 (Bhīṣmaparvan)

1vaiśampāyana uvāca|
atha gāvalgaṇirvidvānsaṃyugādetya bhārata
|
pratyakṣadarśī sarvasya bhūtabhavyabhaviṣyavit ||1||

2dhyāyate dhṛtarāṣṭrāya sahasotpatya duḥkhitaḥ
|
ācaṣṭa nihataṃ bhīṣmaṃ bharatānāṃ pitāmaham ||2||

3sañjaya uvāca|
sañjayo'haṃ mahārāja namaste bharatarṣabha
|
hato bhīṣmaḥ śāntanavo bharatānāṃ pitāmahaḥ ||3||

4kakudaṃ sarvayodhānāṃ dhāma sarvadhanuṣmatām
|
śaratalpagataḥ so'dya śete kurupitāmahaḥ ||4||

5yasya vīryaṃ samāśritya dyūtaṃ putrastavākarot
|
sa śete nihato rājansaṅkhye bhīṣmaḥ śikhaṇḍinā ||5||

6yaḥ sarvānpṛthivīpālānsamavetānmahāmṛdhe
|
jigāyaikarathenaiva kāśipuryāṃ mahārathaḥ ||6||

7jāmadagnyaṃ raṇe rāmaṃ yo'yudhyadapasambhramaḥ
|
na hato jāmadagnyena sa hato'dya śikhaṇḍinā ||7||

8mahendrasadṛśaḥ śaurye sthairye ca himavāniva
|
samudra iva gāmbhīryo sahiṣṇutve dharāsamaḥ ||8||

9śaradaṃṣṭro dhanurvakraḥ khaṅgajihvo durāsadaḥ
|
narasiṃhaḥ pitā te'dya pāñcālyena nipātitaḥ ||9||

10pāṇḍavānāṃ mahāsainyaṃ yaṃ dṛṣṭvodyatamāhave
|
prāvepata bhayodvignaṃ siṃha dṛṣṭveva gogaṇaḥ ||10||

11parirakṣya sa senāṃ te daśarātramanīkahā
|
jagāmāstamivādityaḥ kṛtvā karma suduṣkaram ||11||

12yaḥ sa śakra ivākṣobhyo varṣanbāṇānsahasraśaḥ
|
jaghāna yudhi yodhānāmarbudaṃ daśabhirdinaiḥ ||12||

13sa śete nihato bhūmau vātabhagna iva drumaḥ
|
tava durmantrite rājanyathā nārhaḥ sa bhārata ||13||
