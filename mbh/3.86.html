Mahābhārata 3.86 (Āraṇyakaparvan)

1vaiśaṃpāyana uvāca| bhrātṝṇāṃ matamājñāya nāradasya ca dhīmataḥ
|
pitāmahasamaṃ dhaumyaṃ prāha rājā yudhiṣṭhiraḥ||1||

2mayā sa puruṣavyāghro jiṣṇuḥ satyaparākramaḥ
|
astrahetormahābāhuramitātmā vivāsitaḥ||2||

3sa hi vīronuraktaśca samarthaśca tapodhanaḥ
|
kṛtī ca bhṛśamapyastre vāsudeva iva prabhuḥ||3||

4ahaṃ hyetāvubhau brahmankṛṣṇāvarivighātinau
|
abhijānāmi vikrāntau tathā vyāsaḥ pratāpavān||4||

5triyugau puṇḍarīkākṣau vāsudevadhanaṃjayau
|
nārado'pi tathā veda yo'pyaśaṃsatsadā mama||5||

6tathāhamapi jānāmi naranārāyaṇāvṛṣī
|
śakto'yamityato matvā mayā sa preṣitorjunaḥ||6||

7indrādanavaraḥ śakraṃ surasūnuḥ surādhipam
|
draṣṭumastrāṇi cādātumindrāditi vivāsitaḥ||7||

8bhīṣmadroṇāvatirathau kṛpo drauṇiśca durjayaḥ
|
dhṛtarāṣṭrasya putreṇa vṛtā yudhi mahārathāḥ||8||

9sarve vedavidaḥ śūrāḥ sarvāstraviduṣastathā
|
yoddhukāmāśca pārthena satataṃ ye mahābalāḥ
|
sa ca divyāstravitkarṇaḥ sūtaputro mahārathaḥ||9||

10yostravegānilabalaḥ śarārcistalaniḥsvanaḥ
|
rajodhūmostrasaṃpāto dhārtarāṣṭrāniloddhataḥ||10||

11nisṛṣṭa iva kālena yugānte jvalano mahān
|
mama sainyamayaṃ kakṣaṃ pradhakṣyati na saṃśayaḥ||11||

12taṃ sa kṛṣṇāniloddhūto divyāstrajvalano mahān
|
śvetavājibalākābhṛdgāṇḍīvendrāyudholbaṇaḥ||12||

13saṃrabdhaḥ śaradhārābhiḥ sudīptaṃ karṇapāvakam
|
arjunodīrito meghaḥ śamayiṣyati saṃyuge||13||

14sa sākṣādeva sarvāṇi śakrātparapuraṃjayaḥ
|
divyānyastrāṇi bībhatsustataśca pratipatsyate||14||

15alaṃ sa teṣāṃ sarveṣāmiti me dhīyate matiḥ
|
nāsti tvanikṛtārthānāṃ raṇe'rīṇāṃ pratikriyā||15||

16te vayaṃ pāṇḍavaṃ sarve gṛhītāstramariṃdamam
|
draṣṭāro na hi bībhatsurbhāramudyamya sīdati||16||

17vayaṃ tu tamṛte vīraṃ vane'smindvipadāṃ vara
|
avadhānaṃ na gacchāmaḥ kāmyake saha kṛṣṇayā||17||

18bhavānanyadvanaṃ sādhu bahvannaṃ phalavacchuci
|
ākhyātu ramaṇīyaṃ ca sevitaṃ puṇyakarmabhiḥ||18||

19yatra kaṃcidvayaṃ kālaṃ vasantaḥ satyavikramam
|
pratīkṣāmo'rjunaṃ vīraṃ vṛṣṭikāmā ivāmbudam||19||

20vividhānāśramānkāṃściddvijātibhyaḥ pratiśrutān
|
sarāṃsi saritaścaiva ramaṇīyāṃśca parvatān||20||

21ācakṣva na hi me brahmanrocate tamṛte'rjunam
|
vane'sminkāmyake vāso gacchāmo'nyāṃ diśaṃ prati||21||
