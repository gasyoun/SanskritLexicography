Mahābhārata 12.29 (Śāntiparvan)

1vaiśampāyana uvāca|
avyāharati rājendre dharmaputre yudhiṣṭhire
|
guḍākeśo hṛṣīkeśamabhyabhāṣata pāṇḍavaḥ ||1||

2arjuna uvāca |
jñātiśokābhisantapto dharmaputraḥ parantapaḥ
|
eṣa śokārṇave magnastamāśvāsaya mādhava ||2||

3sarve sma te saṃśayitāḥ punareva janārdana
|
asya śokaṃ mahābāho praṇāśayitumarhasi ||3||

4vaiśampāyana uvāca|
evamuktastu govindo vijayena mahātmanā
|
paryavartata rājānaṃ puṇḍarīkekṣaṇo'cyutaḥ ||4||

5anatikramaṇīyo hi dharmarājasya keśavaḥ
|
bālyātprabhṛti govindaḥ prītyā cābhyadhiko'rjunāt ||5||

6sampragṛhya mahābāhurbhujaṃ candanabhūṣitam
|
śailastambhopamaṃ śauriruvācābhivinodayan ||6||

7śuśubhe vadanaṃ tasya sudaṃṣṭraṃ cārulocanam
|
vyākośamiva vispaṣṭaṃ padmaṃ sūrya ivodite ||7||

8vāsudeva uvāca |
mā kṛthāḥ puruṣavyāghra śokaṃ tvaṃ gātraśoṣaṇam
|
na hi te sulabhā bhūyo ye hatā'smin raṇājire ||8||

9svapnalabdhā yathā lābhā vitathāḥ pratibodhane
|
evaṃ te kṣatriyā rājan ye vyatītā mahāraṇe ||9||

10sarvepyabhimukhāḥ śūrā vijitā raṇaśobhina:
|
naiṣāṃ kaścitpṛṣṭhato vā palāyanvāpi pātitaḥ ||10||

11sarve tyaktvā''tmanaḥ prāṇānyuddhvā vīrā mahāmṛdhe
|
śastrapūtā divaṃ prāptā na tāñchocitumarhasi ||11||

12kṣatradharmaratāḥ śūrāḥ vedavedāṅgapāragāḥ
|
prāptā vīragatiṃ puṇyāṃ tānna śocitumarhasi ||12||

13mṛtānmahānubhāvāṃstvaṃ śrutvaiva pṛthivīpatīn
|
atraivodāharantīmamitihāsaṃ purātanam ||13||

14sañjayaṃ putraśokārtaṃ yathāyaṃ nārado'bravīt
|
sukhaduḥkhairahaṃ tvaṃ ca prajāḥ sarvāśca sañjaya ||14||

15avimuktā mariṣyāmastatra kā paridevanā
|
mahābhāgyaṃ purā rājñāṃ kīrtyamānaṃ mayā śṛṇu ||15||

16gacchāvadhānaṃ nṛpate tato duḥkhaṃ prahāsyasi
|
mṛtānmahānubhāvāṃstvaṃ śrutvaiva pṛthivīpatīn ||16||

17śamamānaya santāpaṃ śṛṇu vistaraśaśca me
|
krūragrahābhiśamanamāyurvardhanamuttamam ||17||

18āvikṣitaṃ maruttaṃ ca mṛtaṃ sañjaya śuśruma ||18||

19yasya sendrāḥ savaruṇā bṛhaspatipurogamāḥ
|
devā viśvasṛjo rājño yajñamīyurmahātmanaḥ ||19||

20yaḥ spardhayā'yajacchakraṃ devarājaṃ purandaram
|
śakrapriyaiṣī yaṃ vidvānpratyācaṣṭa bṛhaspatiḥ ||20||

21saṃvarto yājayāmāsa yavīyānsa bṛhaspateḥ
|
yasminpraśāsati mahīṃ nṛpatau rājasattama
|
akṛṣṭapacyā pṛthivī vibabhau caityamālinī ||21||

22āvikṣitasya vai satre viśvedevāḥ sabhāsadaḥ
|
marutaḥ pariveṣṭāraḥ sādhyāścāsanmahātmanaḥ ||22||

23marudgaṇā maruttasya yatsomamapibaṃstataḥ
|
devānmanuṣyāngandharvānatyaricyanta dakṣiṇāḥ ||23||

24sa cenmamāra sañjaya caturbhadratarastvayā
|
putrātpuṇyataraścaiva mā putramanutapyathāḥ ||24||

25suhotraṃ caivātithinaṃ mṛtaṃ sañjaya śuśruma
|
yasminhiraṇyaṃ vavṛṣe maghavā parivatsaram ||25||

26satyanāmā vasumatī yaṃ prāpyāsījjanādhipam
|
hiraṇyamavahannadyastasmiñjanapadeśvare ||26||

27kūrmānkarkaṭakānnakrānmakarāṃñchiśukānapi
|
nadīṣvapātayadrājanmaghavā lokapūjitaḥ ||27||

28hiraṇyānpātitāndṛṣṭvā matsyānmakarakacchapān
|
sahasraśo'tha śataśastato'smayadatho'tithiḥ ||28||

29taddhiraṇyamaparyantamāvṛtaṃ kurujāṅgale
|
ījāno vitate yajñe brāhmaṇebhyaḥ samārpayat ||29||

30sa cenmamāra sañjaya caturbhadratarastvayā
|
putrātpuṇyataraścaiva mā putramanutapyathāḥ ||30||

31aṅgaṃ bṛhadrathaṃ caiva mṛtaṃ sañjaya śuśruma ||31||

32yaḥ sahasraṃ sahasrāṇāṃ śvetānaśvānavāsṛjat
|
sahasraṃ ca sahasrāṇāṃ kanyā hemapariṣkṛtāḥ ||32||

33ījāno vitate yajñe dakṣiṇāmatyakālayat
|
yaḥ sahasraṃ sahasrāṇāṃ gajānāmatipadminām ||33||

34ījāno vitate yajñe dakṣiṇāmatyakālayat
|
śataṃ śatasahasrāṇi vṛṣāṇāṃ hemamālinām ||34||

35gavāṃ sahasrānucaraṃ dakṣiṇāmatyakālayat
|
aṅgasya yajamānasya tadā viṣṇupade girau ||35||

36amādyadindraḥ somena dakṣiṇābhirdvijātayaḥ
|
yasya yajñeṣu rājendra śatasaṃkhyeṣu vai purā ||36||

37devānmanuṣyān gandharvānatyaricyanta dakṣiṇāḥ
|
na jāto janitā nānyaḥ pumānyaḥ sampradāsyati ||37||

38yadaṅgaḥ pradadau vittaṃ somasaṃsthāsu saptasu
|
sa cenmamāra sañjaya caturbhadratarastvayā ||38||

39śibimauśīnaraṃ caiva mṛtaṃ sañjaya śuśruma ||39||

40ya imāṃ pṛthivīṃ sarvāṃ carmavatsamaveṣṭayat
|
mahatā rathaghoṣeṇa pṛthivīmanunādayan ||40||

41ekacchatrāṃ mahīṃ cakre jaitreṇaikarathena yaḥ
|
yāvadasya gavāśvaṃ syādāraṇyaiḥ paśubhiḥ saha ||41||

42tāvatīḥ pradadau gāḥ sa śibirauśīnaro'dhvare
|
na voḍhāraṃ dhuraṃ tasya kañcinmene prajāpatiḥ ||42||

43na bhūtaṃ na bhaviṣyaṃ ca sarvarājasu sañjaya
|
anyatrauśīnarācchaibyādrājarṣerindravikramāt ||43||

44adakṣiṇamayajvānaṃ mā putramanutapyathāḥ
|
sa cenmamāra sañjaya caturbhadratarastvayā
|
putrātpuṇyataraścaiva mā putramanutapyathāḥ ||44||

45bharataṃ caiva dauṣyantiṃ mṛtaṃ sañjaya śuśruma
|
śākuntalaṃ mahātmānaṃ bhūridraviṇasañcayam ||45||

46yo baddhvā triśataṃ cāśvāndevebhyo yamunāmanu
|
sarasvatīṃ viṃśatiṃ ca gaṅgāmanu caturdaśa ||46||

47aśvamedhasahasreṇa rājasūyaśatena ca
|
iṣṭavānsa mahātejā dauṣyantirbharataḥ purā ||47||

48bharatasya mahatkarma sarvarājasu pārthivāḥ
|
khaṃ martyā iva bāhubhyāṃ nānugantumaśaknuvan ||48||

49paraṃ sahasrādyo baddhān hayānvedīrvitatya ca
|
sahasraṃ yatra padyānāṃ kaṇvāya bharato dadau ||49||

50sa cenmamāra sañjaya caturbhadratarastvayā
|
putrātpuṇyataraścaiva mā putramanutapyathāḥ ||50||

51rāmaṃ dāśarathiṃ caiva mṛtaṃ sañjaya śuśruma
|
yo'nvakampata vai nityaṃ prajāḥ putrānivaurasān ||51||

52vidhavā yasya viṣaye nānāthāḥ kāśca nābhavan
|
sadaivāsītpitṛsamo rāmo rājyaṃ yadanvaśāt ||52||

53kālavarṣī ca parjanyaḥ sasyāni samapādayat
|
nityaṃ subhikṣamevāsīdrāme rājyaṃ praśāsati ||53||

54prāṇino nāpsu majjanti nānyathā pāvako'dahat
|
rujā bhayaṃ na tatrāsīdrāme rājyaṃ praśāsati ||54||

55āsanvarṣasahastriṇyastathā varṣasahasrakāḥ
|
arogā: sarvasiddhārthā rāme rājyaṃ praśāsati ||55||

56nānyo'nyena vivādo'bhūt strīṇāmapi kuto nṛṇām
|
dharmanityāḥ prajāścāsan rāme rājyaṃ praśāsati ||56||

57santuṣṭāḥ sarvasiddhārthā nirbhayāḥ svairacāriṇaḥ
|
narāḥ satyavratāścāsan rāme rājyaṃ praśāsati ||57||

58nityapuṣpaphalāścaiva pādapā nirupadravāḥ
|
sarvā droṇadughā gāvo rāme rājyaṃ praśāsati ||58||

59sa caturdaśavarṣāṇi vane proṣya mahātapāḥ
|
daśāśvamedhān jārūthyānājahāra nirargalān ||59||

60yuvā śyāmo lohitākṣo mātaṅga iva yūthapaḥ
|
ājānubāhuḥ sumukhaḥ siṃhaskandho mahābhujaḥ ||60||

61daśavarṣasahasrāṇi daśavarṣaśatāni ca
|
ayodhyādhipatirbhūtvā rāmo rājyamakārayat ||61||

62sa cenmamāra sañjaya caturbhadratarastvayā
|
putrātpuṇyataraścaiva mā putramanutapyathāḥ ||62||

63bhagīrathaṃ ca rājānaṃ mṛtaṃ sañjaya śuśruma
|
yasyendro vitate yajñe somaṃ pītvā madotkaṭaḥ ||63||

64asurāṇāṃ sahasrāṇi bahūni surasattamaḥ
|
ajayadbāhuvīryeṇa bhagavānpākaśāsanaḥ ||64||

65yaḥ sahasraṃ sahasrāṇāṃ kanyā hemavibhūṣitāḥ
|
ījāno vitate yajñe dakṣiṇāmatyakālayat ||65||

66sarvā rathagatāḥ kanyā rathāḥ sarve caturyujaḥ
|
śataṃ śataṃ rathe nāgāḥ padmino hemamālinaḥ ||66||

67sahasramaśvā ekaikaṃ hastinaṃ pṛṣṭhato'nvayuḥ
|
gavāṃ sahasramaśve'śve sahasraṃ gavyajāvikam ||67||

68upahvare nivasato yasyāṅke niṣasāda ha
|
gaṅgā bhāgīrathī tasmādurvaśī cābhavatpurā ||68||

69bhūridakṣiṇamikṣvākuṃ yajamānaṃ bhagīratham
|
trilokapathagā gaṅgā duhitṛtvamupeyuṣī ||69||

70sa cenmamāra sañjaya caturbhadratarastvayā
|
putrātpuṇyataraścaiva mā putramanutapyathāḥ ||70||

71dilīpaṃ ca mahātmānaṃ mṛtaṃ sañjaya śuśruma
|
yasya karmāṇi bhūrīṇi kathayanti dvijātayaḥ ||71||

72ya imāṃ vasusampūrṇāṃ vasudhāṃ vasudhādhipaḥ
|
dadau tasminmahāyajñe brāhmaṇebhyaḥ samāhitaḥ ||72||

73yasyeha yajamānasya yajñe yajñe purohitaḥ
|
sahasraṃ vāraṇānhaimāndakṣiṇāmatyakālayat ||73||

74yasya yajñe mahānāsīdyūpaḥ śrīmānhiraṇmayaḥ
|
taṃ devāḥ karma kurvāṇāḥ śakrajyeṣṭhā upāśrayan ||74||

75caṣāle yasya sauvarṇe tasmin yūpe hiraṇmaye
|
nanṛturdevagandharvāḥ ṣaṭ sahasrāṇi saptadhā ||75||

76avādayattatra vīṇāṃ madhye viśvāvasuḥ svayam
|
sarvabhūtānyamanyanta mama vādayatītyayam ||76||

77etadrājño dilīpasya rājāno nānucakrire
|
yasyebhā hemasaṃchannāḥ pathi mattāḥ sma śerate ||77||

78rājānaṃ śatadhanvānaṃ dilīpaṃ satyavādinam
|
ye'paśyansumahātmānaṃ te'pi svargajito narāḥ ||78||

79trayaḥ śabdā na jīryante dilīpasya niveśane
|
svādhyāyaghoṣo jyāghoṣo dīyatāmiti vai trayaḥ ||79||

80sa cenmamāra sañjaya caturbhadratarastvayā
|
putrātpuṇyataraścaiva mā putramanutapyathāḥ ||80||

81māndhātāraṃ yauvanāśvaṃ mṛtaṃ sañjaya śuśruma
|
yaṃ devā maruto garbhaṃ pituḥ pārśvādapāharan ||81||

82samṛddho yuvanāśvasya jaṭhare yo mahātmanaḥ
|
pṛṣadājyodbhavaḥ śrīmāṃstrilokavijayī nṛpaḥ ||82||

83yaṃ dṛṣṭvā piturutsaṅge śayānaṃ devarūpiṇam
|
anyonyamabruvandevāḥ kamayaṃ dhāsyatīti vai ||83||

84māmeva dhāsyatītyevamindro'thābhyupapadyata
|
māndhāteti tatastasya nāma tasya nāma cakre śatakratuḥ ||84||

85tatastu payaso dhārāṃ puṣṭihetormahātmanaḥ
|
tasyāsye yauvanāśvasya pāṇirindrasya cāsravat ||85||

86taṃ pibanpāṇimindrasya śatamahnā vyavardhata
|
sa āsīddvādaśasamo dvādaśāhena pārthivaḥ ||86||

87tamimaṃ pṛthivī sarvā ekāhnā samapadyata
|
dharmātmānaṃ mahātmānaṃ śūramindrasamaṃ yudhi ||87||

88yaścāṅgāraṃ tu nṛpatiṃ maruttamasitaṃ gayam
|
aṅgaṃ bṛhadrathaṃ caiva māndhātā samare'jayat ||88||

89yauvanāśvo yadāṅgāraṃ samare pratyayudhyata
|
visphārairdhanuṣo devā dyaurabhedīti menire ||89||

90yatra sūrya udeti sma yatra ca pratitiṣṭhati
|
sarvaṃ tadyauvanāśvasya māndhātuḥ kṣetramucyate ||90||

91aśvamedhaśateneṣṭvā rājasūyaśatena ca
|
adadādrohitānmatsyānbrāhmaṇebhyo viśāmpate ||91||

92hairaṇyān yojanotsedhānāyatāndaśayojanam
|
atiriktāndvijātibhyo vyabhajaṃstvitare janāḥ ||92||

93sa cenmamāra sañjaya caturbhadratarastvayā
|
putrātpuṇyataraścaiva mā putramanutapyathāḥ ||93||

94yayātiṃ nāhuṣaṃ caiva mṛtaṃ sañjaya śuśruma
|
ya imāṃ pṛthivīṃ kṛtsnāṃ vijitya sahasāgarām ||94||

95śamyāpātenābhyatīyādvedībhiścitrayanmahīm
|
ījānaḥ kratubhirmukhyaiḥ paryagacchadvasundharām ||95||

96dṛṣṭvā kratusahasreṇa vājapeyaśatena ca
|
tarpayāmāsa viprendrāṃstribhiḥ kāñcanaparvataiḥ ||96||

97vyūḍhenāsurayuddhena hatvā daiteyadānavān
|
vyabhajatpṛthivīṃ kṛtsnāṃ yayātirnahuṣātmajaḥ ||97||

98antyeṣu putrānnikṣipya yadudruhyupurogamān
|
pūruṃ rājye'bhiṣicyātha sadāraḥ prāviśadvanam ||98||

99sa cenmamāra sañjaya caturbhadratarastvayā
|
putrātpuṇyataraścaiva mā putramanutapyathāḥ ||99||

100ambarīṣaṃ ca nābhāgaṃ mṛtaṃ sañjaya śuśruma
|
yaṃ prajā vavrire puṇyaṃ goptāraṃ nṛpasattamam ||100||

101yaḥ sahasraṃ sahasrāṇāṃ rājñāmayutayājinām
|
ījāno vitate yajñe brāhmaṇebhyaḥ susaṃhitaḥ ||101||

102naitatpūrve janāścakrurna kariṣyanti cāpare
|
ityambarīṣaṃ nābhāgimanvamodanta dakṣiṇāḥ ||102||

103śataṃ rājasahasrāṇi śataṃ rājaśatāni ca
|
sarve'śvamedhairījānāste'nvayurdakṣiṇāyanam ||103||

104sa cenmamāra sañjaya caturbhadratarastvayā
|
putrātpuṇyataraścaiva mā putramanutapyathāḥ ||104||

105śaśabinduṃ caitrarathaṃ mṛtaṃ śuśruma sṛñjaya
|
yasya bhāryāsahasrāṇāṃ śatamāsīnmahātmanaḥ ||105||

106sahasraṃ tu sahasrāṇāṃ yasyāsan śāśabindavāḥ
|
hiraṇyakavacāḥ sarve sarve cottamadhanvinaḥ ||106||

107śataṃ kanyā rājaputramekaikaṃ pṛthaganvayuḥ
|
kanyāṃ kanyāṃ śataṃ nāgā nāgaṃ nāgaṃ śataṃ rathāḥ ||107||

108rathe rathe śataṃ cāśvā deśajā hemamālinaḥ
|
aśve aśve śataṃ gāvo gavāṃ tadvadajāvikam ||108||

109etaddhanamaparyantamaśvamedhe mahāmakhe
|
śaśabindurmahārāja brāhmaṇebhyaḥ samārpayat ||109||

110sa cenmāra sañjaya caturbhadratarastvayā
|
putrātpuṇyataraścaiva mā putramanutapyathāḥ ||110||

111gayaṃ cāmūrtarayasaṃ mṛtaṃ śuśruma sañjaya
|
yaḥ sa varṣaśataṃ rājā hutaśiṣṭāśano'bhavat ||111||

112yasmai vahnirvaraṃ prādāttato vavre varāngayaḥ
|
dadato yo'kṣayaṃ vittaṃ dharme śraddhā ca vardhatām ||112||

113mano me ramatāṃ satye tvatprasādādbhutāśana
|
lebhe ca kāmāṃstānsarvānpāvakāditi naḥ śrutam ||113||

114darśe ca pūrṇamāse ca cāturmāsyaṃ punaḥ punaḥ
|
ayajaddhayamedhena sahasraṃ parivatsarān ||114||

115śataṃ gavāṃ sahasrāṇi śatamaśvatarāṇi ca
|
utthāyotthāya vai prādātsahasraṃ parivatsarān ||115||

116tarpayāmāsa somena devānvittairdvijānapi
|
pitṝnsvadhābhiḥ kāmaiśca striyaḥ sa puruṣarṣabha ||116||

117sauvarṇīṃ pṛthivīṃ kṛtvā daśavyāmāṃ dvirāyatām
|
dakṣiṇāmadadadrājā vājimedhe mahākratau ||117||

118yāvatyaḥ sikatā rājangaṅgāyāṃ puruṣarṣabha
|
tāvatīreva gāḥ prādādāmūrtarayaso gayaḥ ||118||

119sa cenmamāra sañjaya caturbhadratarastvayā
|
putrātpuṇyataścaiva mā putramanutapyathāḥ ||119||

120rantidevaṃ ca sāṃkṛtyaṃ mṛtaṃ sañjaya śuśruma
|
samyagārādhya yaḥ śakrādvaraṃ lebhe mahātapāḥ ||120||

121annaṃ ca no bahu bhavedatithīṃśca labhemahi
|
śraddhā ca no mā vyagamanmā ca yāciṣma kañcana ||121||

122upātiṣṭhanta paśavaḥ svayaṃ taṃ saṃśitavratam
|
grāmyāraṇyā mahātmānaṃ rantidevaṃ yaśasvinam ||122||

123mahānadī carmarāśerutkledātsasṛje yataḥ
|
tataścarmaṇvatītyevaṃ vikhyātā sā mahānadī ||123||

124brāhmaṇebhyo dadau niṣkānsadasi pratate nṛpaḥ
|
tubhyaṃ niṣkaṃ tubhyaṃ niṣkamiti krośanti vai dvijāḥ ||124||

125sahasraṃ tubhyamityuktvā brāhmaṇānsamprapadyate
|
anvāhāryopakaraṇaṃ dravyopakaraṇaṃ ca yat ||125||

126ghaṭāḥ pātryaḥ kaṭāhāni sthālyaśca piṭharāṇi ca
|
nāsītkiñcidasauvarṇaṃ rantidevasya dhīmataḥ ||126||

127sāṃkṛte rantidevasya yāṃ rātrimavasan gṛhe
|
ālabhyanta śataṃ gāvaḥ sahasrāṇi ca viṃśatiḥ ||127||

128tatra sma sūdāḥ krośanti sumṛṣṭamaṇikuṇḍalāḥ
|
sūpaṃ bhūyiṣṭhamaśnīdhvaṃ nādya māṃsaṃ yathā purā ||128||

129sa cenmāra sañjaya caturbhadratarastvayā
|
putrātpuṇyataraścaiva mā putramanutapyathāḥ ||129||

130sagaraṃ ca mahātmānaṃ mṛtaṃ śuśruma sañjaya
|
aikṣvākaṃ puruṣavyāghramatimānuṣavikramam ||130||

131ṣaṣṭiḥ putrasahasrāṇi yaṃ yāntamanujagmire
|
nakṣatrarājaṃ varṣānte vyabhre jyotirgaṇā iva ||131||

132ekacchatrā mahī yasya pratāpādabhavatpurā
|
yo'śvamedhasahasreṇa tarpayāmāsa devatāḥ ||132||

133yaḥ prādātkanakastambhaṃ prāsādaṃ sarvakāñcanam
|
pūrṇaṃ padmadalākṣīṇāṃ strīṇāṃ śayanasaṃkulam ||133||

134dvijātibhyo'nurūpebhyaḥ kāmāṃśca vividhānbahūn
|
yasyādeśena tadvittaṃ vyabhajanta dvijātayaḥ ||134||

135khānayāmāsa yaḥ kopātpṛthivīṃ sāgarāṅkitām
|
yasya nāmnā samudraśca sāgaratvamupāgataḥ ||135||

136sa cenmamāra sañjaya caturbhadratarastvayā
|
putrātpuṇyataraścaiva mā putramanutapyathāḥ ||136||

137rājānaṃ ca pṛthuṃ vainyaṃ mṛtaṃ śuśruma sañjaya
|
yamabhyaṣiñcansambhūya mahāraṇye maharṣayaḥ ||137||

138prathayiṣyati vai lokānpṛthurityeva śabditaḥ
|
kṣatādyo vai trāyatīti sa tasmātkṣatriyaḥ smṛtaḥ ||138||

139pṛthuṃ vainyaṃ prajā dṛṣṭvā raktāḥ smeti yadabruvan
|
tato rājeti nāmāsya anurāgādajāyata ||139||

140akṛṣṭapacyā pṛthivī puṭake puṭake madhu
|
sarvā droṇadudhā gāvo vainyasyāsanpraśāsataḥ ||140||

141arogāḥ sarvasiddhārthā manuṣyā akutobhayāḥ
|
yathābhikāmamavasan kṣetreṣu ca gṛheṣu ca ||141||

142āpastastambhire cāsya samudramabhiyāsyataḥ
|
saritaścānudīryanta dhvajabhaṅgaśca nābhavat ||142||

143hairaṇyāṃstrinalotsedhānparvatānekaviṃśatim
|
brāhmaṇebhyo dadau rājā yo'śvamedhe mahāmakhe ||143||

144sa cenmamāra sañjaya caturbhadratarastvayā
|
putrātpuṇyataraścaiva mā putramanutapyathāḥ ||144||

145kiṃ vā tūṣṇīṃ dhyāyase sañjaya tvaṃ na me rājanvācamimāṃ śṛṇoṣi
|
na cenmoghaṃ vipralaptaṃ mamedaṃ pathyaṃ mumūrṣoriva suprayuktam ||145||

146sañjaya uvāca |
śṛṇomi te nārada vācamenāṃ vicitrārthāṃ srajamiva puṇyagandhām
|
rājarṣīṇāṃ puṇyakṛtāṃ mahātmanāṃ kīrtyā yuktānāṃ śokanirnāśanārthām ||146||

147na te moghaṃ vipralaptaṃ maharṣe dṛṣṭvaivāhaṃ nārada tvāṃ viśokaḥ
|
śuśrūṣe te vacanaṃ brahmavādin na te tṛpyāmyamṛtasyeva pānāt ||147||

148amoghadarśinmama cetprasādaṃ santāpadagdhasya vibho prakuryāḥ
|
sutasya sañjīvanamadya me syāttavaprasādātsutasaṅgamaśca ||148||

149nārada uvāca|
yaste putro gamito'yaṃ vijātaḥ svarṇaṣṭhīvī yamadātparvataste
|
punastu te putramahaṃ dadāmi hiraṇyanābhaṃ varṣasahastriṇaṃ ca ||149||
