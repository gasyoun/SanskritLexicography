Mahābhārata 6.50 (Bhīṣmaparvan)

1sañjaya uvāca|kṛte'vahāre sainyānāṃ prathame bharatarṣabha
|
bhīṣme ca yuddhasaṃrabdhe hṛṣṭe duryodhane tathā ||1||

2dharmarājastatastūrṇamabhigamya janārdanam
|
bhrātṛbhiḥ sahitaḥ sarvaiḥ sarvaiścaiva janeśvaraiḥ ||2||

3śucā paramayā yuktaścintayānaḥ parājayam
|
vārṣṇeyamabravīdrājan dṛṣṭvā bhīṣmasya vikramam ||3||

4kṛṣṇa paśya maheṣvāsaṃ bhīṣmaṃ bhīmaparākramam
|
śarairdahantaṃ sainyaṃ me grīṣme kakṣamivānalam ||4||

5kathamenaṃ mahātmānaṃ śakṣyāmaḥ prativīkṣitum
|
lelihyamānaṃ sainyaṃ me haviṣmantamivānalam ||5||

6etaṃ hi puruṣavyāghraṃ dhanuṣmataṃ mahābalam
|
dṛṣṭvā vipradrutaṃ sainyaṃ samare mārgaṇāhatam ||6||

7śakyo jetuṃ yamaḥ kruddho vajrapāṇiśca saṃyuge
|
varuṇaḥ pāśabhṛdvāpi kubero vā gadādharaḥ ||7||

8na tu bhīṣmo mahātejāḥ śakyo jetuṃ mahābalaḥ
|
so'hamevaṃgate magno bhīṣmāgādhajale'plave ||8||

9ātmano buddhidaurbalyādbhīṣmamāsādya keśava
|
vanaṃ yāsyāmi vārṣṇeya śreyo me tatra jīvitum ||9||

10na tvetān pṛthivīpālān dātuṃ bhīṣmāya mṛtyave
|
kṣapayiṣyati senāṃ me kṛṣṇa bhīṣmo mahāstravit ||10||

11yathā'nalaṃ prajvalitaṃ pataṅgāḥ samabhidrutāḥ
|
vināśāyopagacchanti tathā me sainiko janaḥ ||11||

12kṣayaṃ nīto'smi vārṣṇeya rājyahetoḥ parākramī
|
bhrātaraścaiva me vīrāḥ karśitāḥ śarapīḍitāḥ ||12||

13matkṛte bhrātṛhārdena rājyādbhraṣṭāstathā sukhāt
|
jīvitaṃ bahu manye'haṃ jīvitaṃ hyadya durlabham ||13||

14jīvitasya ca śeṣeṇa tapastapsyāmi duścaram
|
na ghātayiṣyāmi raṇe mitrāṇīmāni keśava ||14||

15rathān me bahusāhastrān divyairastrairmahābalaḥ
|
ghātayatyaniśaṃ bhīṣmaḥ pravarāṇāṃ prahāriṇām ||15||

16kiṃ nu kṛtvā hitaṃ me syādbrūhi mādhava māciram
|
madhyasthamiva paśyāmi samare savyasācinam ||16||

17eko bhīmaḥ paraṃ śaktyā yudhyatyeva mahābhujaḥ
|
kevalaṃ bāhuvīryeṇa kṣatradharmamanusmaran ||17||

18gadayā vīraghātinyā yathotsāhaṃ mahāmanāḥ
|
karotyasukaraṃ karma rathāśvanaradantiṣu ||18||

19nālameṣa kṣayaṃ kartuṃ parasainyasya māriṣa
|
ārjavenaiva yuddhena vīra varṣaśatairapi ||19||

20eko'stravitsakhā te'yaṃ so'pyasmānsamupekṣate
|
nirdahyamānān bhīṣmeṇa droṇena ca mahātmanā ||20||

21divyānyastrāṇi bhīṣmasya droṇasya ca mahātmanaḥ
|
dhakṣyanti kṣatriyān sarvān prayuktāni punaḥ punaḥ ||21||

22kṛṣṇa bhīṣmaḥ susaṃrabdhaḥ sahitaḥ sarvapārthivaḥ
|
kṣapayiṣyati no nūnaṃ yādṛśo'sya parākramaḥ ||22||

23sa tvaṃ paśya mahābhāga yogeśvara mahāratham
|
bhīṣmaṃ yaḥ śamayetsaṅkhye dāvāgniṃ jalado yathā ||23||

24tava prasādādgovinda pāṇḍavā nihatadviṣaḥ
|
svarājyamanusaṃprāptā modiṣyante sabāndhavāḥ ||24||

25evamuktvā tataḥ pārtho dhyāyannāste mahāmanāḥ
|
ciramantarmanā bhūtvā śokopahatacetanaḥ
|
śokārtaṃ tamatho jñātvā duḥkhopahatacetasam ||25||

26abravīt tatra govindo harṣayan sarvapāṇḍavān
|
mā śuco bharataśreṣṭha na tvaṃ śocitumarhasi ||26||

27yasya te bhrātaraḥ śūrāḥ sarvalokeṣu dhanvinaḥ
|
ahaṃ ca priyakṛdrājan sātyakiśca mahāyaśāḥ||27||

28virāṭadrupadau cemau dhṛṣṭadyumnaśca pārṣataḥ
|
tathaiva sabalāśceme rājāno rājasattama ||28||

29tvatprasādaṃ pratīkṣante tvadbhaktāśca viśāṃpate
|
eṣa te pārṣato nityaṃ hitakāmaḥ priye rataḥ ||29||

30saināpatyamanuprāpto dhṛṣṭadyumno mahābalaḥ
|
śikhaṇḍī ca mahābāho bhīṣmasya nidhanaṃ kila ||30||

31etacchrutvā tato dharmo dhṛṣṭadyumnaṃ mahāratham
|
abravīt samitau tasyāṃ vāsudevasya śṛṇvataḥ ||31||

32dhṛṣṭadyumna nibodhedaṃ yattvāṃ vakṣyāmi māriṣa
|
nātikramyaṃ bhavet tacca vacanaṃ mama bhāṣitam ||32||

33bhavān senāpatirmahyaṃ vāsudevena saṃmitaḥ
|
kārtikeyo yathā nityaṃ devānāmabhavat purā ||33||

34tathā tvamapi pāṇḍūnāṃ senānīḥ puruṣarṣabha
|
sa tvaṃ puruṣaśārdūla vikramya jahi kauravān ||34||

35ahaṃ ca te'nuyāsyāmi bhīmaḥ kṛṣṇaśca māriṣa
|
mādrīputrau ca sahitau draupadeyāśca daṃśitāḥ ||35||

36ye cānye pṛthivīpālāḥ pradhānāḥ puruṣarṣabha
|
tata uddharṣayan sarvān dhṛṣṭadyumno'bhyabhāṣata ||36||

37ahaṃ droṇāntakaḥ pārtha vihitaḥ śaṃbhunā purā
|
raṇe bhīṣmaṃ kṛpaṃ droṇaṃ tathā śalyaṃ jayadratham ||37||

38sarvānadya raṇe dṛptān pratiyotsyāmi pārthiva
|
athotkṛṣṭaṃ maheṣvāsaiḥ pāṇḍavairyuddhadurmadaiḥ ||38||

39samudyate pārthivendre pārṣate śatrusūdane
|
tamabravīt tataḥ pārthaḥ pārṣataṃ pṛtanāpatim ||39||

40vyūhaḥ krauñcāruṇo nāma sarvaśatrunibarhaṇaḥ
|
yaṃ bṛhaspatirindrāya tadā devāsure'bravīt ||40||

41taṃ yathāvat prativyūhaṃ parānīkavināśanam
|
adṛṣṭapūrvaṃ rājānaḥ paśyantu kurubhiḥ saha ||41||

42yathoktaḥ sa nṛdevena viṣṇurvajrabhṛtā yathā
|
prabhāte sarvasainyānāmagre cakre dhanañjayam ||42||

43ādityapathagaḥ ketustasyādbhutamanoramaḥ
|
śāsanāt puruhūtasya nirmito viśvakarmaṇā ||43||

44indrāyudhasavarṇābhiḥ patākābhiralaṅkṛtaḥ
|
ākāśaga ivākāśe gandharvanagaropamaḥ ||44||

45nṛtyamāna ivābhāti rathacaryāsu māriṣa
|
tena ratnavatā pārthaḥ sa ca gāṇḍīvadhanvanā ||45||

46babhūva paramopetaḥ sumaruriva bhānunā
|
śiro'bhūddrupado rājā mahatyā senayā vṛtaḥ ||46||

47kuntibhojaśca caidyaśca cakṣurbhyāṃ tau janeśvarau
|
dāśārṇakāḥ prabhadrāśca dāśerakagaṇaiḥ saha ||47||

48anūpakāḥ kirātāśca grīvāyāṃ bharatarṣabha
|
paṭaccaraiśca pauṇḍreśca rājan pauravakaistathā ||48||

49niṣādaiḥ sahitaścāpi pṛṣṭhamāsīdyudhiṣṭhiraḥ
|
pakṣau tu bhīmasenaśca dhṛṣṭadyumnaśca pārṣataḥ ||49||

50draupadeyābhimanyuśca sātyakiśca mahārathaḥ
|
piśācā dāradāścaiva puṇḍrāḥ kuṇḍīviṣaiḥ saha ||50||

51mārutā dhenukāścaiva taṅgaṇāḥ parataṅgaṇāḥ
|
vāhlikāstittirāścaiva colāḥ paṇḍyāśca bhārata ||51||

52ete janapadā rājan dakṣiṇaṃ pakṣamāśritāḥ
|
agniveśāstu huṇḍāśca mālavā dānabhārayaḥ ||52||

53śabarā udbhasāścaiva vatsāśca saha nākulaiḥ
|
nakulaḥ sahadevaśca vāmaṃ pakṣaṃ samāśritāḥ ||53||

54rathānāmayutaṃ pakṣau śirastu niyutaṃ tathā
|
pṛṣṭhamarbudamevāsīt sahasrāṇi ca viṃśatiḥ ||54||

55grīvāyāṃ niyutaṃ cāpi sahasrāṇi ca saptatiḥ
|
pakṣakoṭiprapakṣeṣu pakṣānteṣu ca vāraṇāḥ ||55||

56jagmuḥ parivṛtā rājaṃścalanta iva parvatāḥ
|
jaghanaṃ pālayāmāsa virāṭaḥ saha kekayaiḥ ||56||

57kāśirājaśca śaibyaśca rathānāmayutaistribhiḥ
|
evamenaṃ mahāvyūhaṃ vyūhya bhārata pāṇḍavāḥ ||57||

58sūryodayaṃ ta icchantaḥ sthitā yuddhāya daṃśitāḥ
|
teṣāmādityavarṇāni vimalāni mahānti ca
|
śvetacchatrāṇyaśobhanta vāraṇeṣu ratheṣu ca ||58||
