Mahābhārata 7.51 (Droṇaparvan)

1sañjaya uvāca|
hate tasmin mahāvīrye saubhadre rathayūthape
|
vimuktarathasannāhāḥ sarve nikṣiptakārmukāḥ ||1||

2upopaviṣṭā rājānaṃ parivārya yudhiṣṭhiram
|
tadeva yuddhaṃ dhyāyantaḥ saubhadragatamānasāḥ ||2||

3tato yudhiṣṭhiro rājā vilalāpa suduḥkhitaḥ
|
abhimanyau hate vīre bhrātuḥ putre mahārathe ||3||

4droṇānīkamasambādhaṃ mama priyacikīrṣayā
|
bhittvā vyūhaṃ praviṣṭo'sau gomadhyamiva kesarī ||4||

5yasya śūrā maheṣvāsāḥ pratyanīkagatā raṇe
|
prabhagnā vinivartante kṛtāstrā yuddhadurmadāḥ ||5||

6atyantaśatrurasmākaṃ yena duḥśāsanaḥ śaraiḥ
|
kṣipraṃ hyabhimukhaḥ saṅkhye visaṃjño vimukhīkṛtaḥ ||6||

7sa tīrtvā dustaraṃ vīro droṇānīkamahārṇavam
|
prāpya dauḥśāsaniṃ kārṣṇiḥ prāpto vaivasvatakṣayam ||7||

8kathaṃ drakṣyāmi kaunteyaṃ saubhadre nihate'rjunam
|
subhadrāṃ vā mahābhāgāṃ priyaṃ putramapaśyatīm ||8||

9kiṃsvidvayamapetārthamakliṣṭamasamañjasam
|
tāvubhau prativakṣyāmo hṛṣīkeśadhanañjayau ||9||

10ahameva subhadrāyāḥ keśavārjunayorapi
|
priyakāmo jayākāṃkṣī kṛtavāni‌damapriyam ||10||

11na lubdho budhyate doṣā~llobhānmohāt pravartate
|
madhulipsurhi nāpaśyaṃ prapātamahamīdṛśam ||11||

12yo hi bhojye puraskāryo yāneṣu śayaneṣu ca
|
bhūṣaṇeṣu ca so'smābhirbālo yudhi puraskṛtaḥ ||12||

13kathaṃ hi bālastaruṇo yuddhānāmaviśāradaḥ
|
sadaśva iva sambādhe viṣame kṣemamarhati ||13||

14no ceddhi vayamapyenaṃ mahīmanu śayīmahi
|
bībhatsoḥ kopadīptasya dagdhāḥ kṛpaṇacakṣuṣā ||14||

15alubdho gatimān hrīmān kṣamāvān rūpavān balī
|
vapuṣmān mānakṛdvīraḥ priyaḥ satyaparākramaḥ ||15||

16yasya ślāghanti vibudhāḥ karmāṇyūrjitakarmaṇaḥ
|
nivātakavacān jaghne kālakeyāṃśca vīryavān ||16||

17mahendraśatravo yena hiraṇyapuravāsinaḥ
|
akṣṇornimeṣamātreṇa paulomāḥ sagaṇā hatāḥ||17||

18parebhyo'pyabhayārthibhyo yo dadātyabhayaṃ vibhuḥ
|
tasyāsmābhirna śakitastrātumapyātmajo balī ||18||

19bhayaṃ tu sumahat prāptaṃ dhārtarāṣṭrānmahābalān
|
pārthaḥ putravadhāt kruddhaḥ kauravān śoṣayiṣyati ||19||

20kṣudraḥ kṣudrasahāyaśca svapakṣakṣayakārakaḥ
|
vyaktaṃ duryodhano dṛṣṭvā śocan hāsyati jīvitam ||20||

21na me jayaḥ prītikaro na rājyaṃ na cāmaratvaṃ na suraiḥ salokatā
|
imaṃ samīkṣyāprativīryapauruṣaṃ nipātitaṃ devavarātmajātmajam ||21||
