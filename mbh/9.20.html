Mahābhārata 9.20 (Śalyaparvan)

1sañjaya uvāca|
sannivṛtte janaughe tu śālvo mlecchagaṇādhipaḥ
|
abhyavartata saṅkruddhaḥ pāṇḍavānāṃ mahadbalam||1||

2āsthāya sumahānāgaṃ prabhinnaṃ parvatopamam
|
dṛptamairāvataprakhyamamitragaṇamardanam||2||

3yo'sau mahānbhadrakulaprasūtaḥ sumpūjito dhārtarāṣṭreṇa nityam
|
sukalpitaḥ śāstraviniścayajñaiḥ sadopavāhyaḥ samareṣu rājan||3||

4tamāsthito rājavaro babhūva yathodayasthaḥ savitā kṣapānte
|
sa tena nāgapravareṇa rājannabhyudyayau pāṇḍusutānsametān||4||

5sitaiḥ pṛṣatkairvidadāra vegairmahendravajrapratimaiḥ sughoraiḥ
|
tataḥ śarānvai sṛjato mahāraṇe yodhāṃśca rājannayato yamālayam||5||

6nāsyāntaraṃ dadṛśuḥ sve pare vā yathā purā vajradharasya daityāḥ
|
airāvaṇasthasya camūvirde daityāḥ purā vāsavasyeva rājan||6||

7te pāṇḍavāḥ somakāḥ sṛñjayāśca tamekanāgaṃ dadṛśuḥ samantāt
|
sahasraśo vai vicarantamekaṃ yathā mahendrasya gajaṃ samīpe||7||

8sandrāvyamāṇaṃ tu balaṃ pareṣāṃ parītakalpaṃ vibabhau samantataḥ
|
naivāvatasthe samare bhṛśaṃ bhayādvimṛdyamānaṃ tu parasparaṃ tadā||8||

9tataḥ prabhagnā sahasā mahācamūḥ sā pāṇḍavī tena narādhipena
|
diśaścatasraḥ sahasā vidhāvitā gajendravegaṃ tamapārayantī||9||

10dṛṣṭvā ca tāṃ vegavatīṃ prabhagnāṃ sarve tvadīyā yudhi yodhamukhyāḥ
|
apūjayaṃste tu narādhipaṃ taṃ dadhmuśca śaṅkhān śaśisannikāśān||10||

11śrutvā ninādaṃ tvatha kauravāṇāṃ harṣādvimuktaṃ saha śaṅkhaśabdaiḥ
|
senāpatiḥ pāṇḍavasṛñjayānāṃ pāñcālaputro mamṛṣe na kopāt||11||

12tatastu taṃ vai dviradaṃ mahātmā pratyudyayau tvaramāṇo jayāya
|
jambho yathā śakrasamāgame vai nāgendramairāvaṇamindravāhyam||12||

13tamāpatantaṃ sahasā tu dṛṣṭvā pāñcālaputraṃ yudhi rājasiṃhaḥ
|
taṃ vai dvipaṃ preṣayāmāsa tūrṇaṃ vadhāya rājan drupadātmajasya||13||

14sa taṃ dvipendraṃ sahasā patantamavidhyadagnipratimaiḥ pṛṣatkaiḥ
|
karmāradhautairniśitairjvaladbhirnārācamukhyaistribhirupavegaiḥ||14||

15tato parānpañcaśatānmahātmā nārācamukhyānvisasarja kumbhe
|
sa taistu viddhaḥ paramadvipo raṇe tadā parāvṛtya bhṛśaṃ pradudruve||15||

16taṃ nāgarājaṃ sahasā praṇunnaṃ vidrāvyamāṇaṃ vinivartya śālvaḥ
|
totrāṅkuśaiḥ preṣayāmāsa tūrṇaṃ pāñcālarājasya rathaṃ pradiśya||16||

17dṛṣṭvā patantaṃ sahasā tu nāgaṃ dhṛṣṭadyumnaḥ svarathācchrīghrameva
|
gadāṃ pragṛhyograjavena vīro bhūmiṃ prapanno bhayavihvalāṅgaḥ||17||

18sa taṃ rathaṃ hemavibhūṣitāṅgaṃ sāśvaṃ sasūtaṃ sahasā vimṛdyā
|
utkṣipya hastena nadan mahādvipo vipothayāmāsa vasundharātale||18||

19pāñcālarājasya sutaṃ ca dṛṣṭvā tadārditaṃ nāgavareṇa tena
|
tamabhyadhāvatsahasā javena bhīmaḥ śikhaṇḍī ca śineśca naptā||19||

20śaraiśca vegaṃ sahasā nigṛhya tasyābhito vyāpatato gajasya
|
sa saṅgṛhīto rathibhirgajo vai cacāla tairvāryamāṇāśca saṅkhye||20||

21tataḥ pṛṣatkān pravavarṣa rājā sūryo yathā raśmijālaṃ samantāt
|
tairāśugairvadhyamānā rathaudhāḥ pradudruvuḥ sahitāstatra tatra||21||

22tat karma śālvasya samīkṣya sarve pāñcālaputrā nṛpa sṛñjayāśca
|
hāhākārairnādayanti sma yuddhe dvipaṃ samantādrurudhurnarāgryāḥ||22||

23pāñcālaputrastvaritastu śūro gadāṃ pragṛhyācalaśṛṅgakalpām
|
sasambhramaṃ bhārataśatrughātī javena vīro'nusasāra nāgam||23||

24tatastu nāgaṃ dharaṇīdharābhaṃ madaṃ sravantaṃ jaladaprakāśam
|
gadāṃ samāviddhya bhṛśaṃ jaghāna pāñcālarājasya sutastarasvī||24||

25sa bhinnakumbhaḥ sahasā vinadya mukhātprabhūtaṃ kṣatajaṃ vimuñcan
|
papāta nāgo dharaṇīdharābhaḥ kṣitiprakampāccalito yathā'dbhiḥ||25||

26nipātyamāne tu tadā gajendre hāhākṛte tava putrasya sainye
|
sa śālvarājasya śinipravīro jahāra bhallena śiraḥ śitena||26||

27hṛtottamāṅgo yudhi sātvatena papāta bhūmau saha nāgarājñā
|
yathādriśṛṅgaṃ sumahatpraṇunnaṃ vajreṇa devādhipacoditena||27||
