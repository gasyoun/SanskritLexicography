Mahābhārata 5.38 (Udyogaparvan)

1vidura uvāca|
ūrdhvaṃ prāṇā hyutkrāmanti yūnaḥ sthavira āyati
|
pratyutthānābhivādābhyāṃ punastān pratipadyate ||1||

2pīṭhaṃ dattvā sādhave'bhyāgatāya ānīyāpaḥ parinirṇijya pādau
|
sukhaṃ pṛṣṭvā prativedyātmasaṃsthāṃ tato dadyādannamavekṣya dhīraḥ ||2||

3yasyodakaṃ madhuparkaṃ ca gāṃ ca na mantravit pratigṛhṇāti gehe
|
lobhādbhayādatha kārpaṇyato vā tasyānarthaṃ jīvitamāhurāryāḥ ||3||

4cikitsakaḥ śalyakartā'vakīrṇī stenaḥ krūro madyapo bhrūṇahā ca
|
senājīvī śrutivikrāyakaśca bhṛśaṃ priyo'pyatithirnodakārhaḥ ||4||

5avikreyaṃ lavaṇaṃ pakvamannaṃ dadhi kṣīraṃ madhu tailaṃ ghṛtaṃ ca
|
tilā māṃsaṃ phalamūlāni śākaṃ raktaṃ vāsaḥ sarvagandhā guḍāśca||5||

6aroṣaṇo yaḥ samaloṣṭhāśmakāñcanaḥ prahīṇaśoko gatasandhivigrahaḥ
|
nindāpraśaṃsoparataḥ priyāpriye tyajannudāsīnavadeṣa bhikṣukaḥ ||6||

7nīvāramūleṅgudaśākavṛttiḥ susaṃyatātmā'gnikāryeṣu codyaḥ
|
vane vasannatithiṣvapramatto dhurandharaḥ puṇyakṛdeṣa tāpasaḥ ||7||

8apakṛtya buddhimato dūrastho'smīti nāśvaset
|
dīrghau buddhimato bāhū yābhyāṃ hiṃsati hiṃsitaḥ ||8||

9na viśvasedaviśvaste viśvaste nātiviśvaset
|
viśvāsādbhayamutpannaṃ mūlānyapi nikṛntati ||9||

10anīrṣurguptadāraśca saṃvibhāgī priyaṃvadaḥ
|
ślakṣṇo madhuravāk strīṇāṃ na cāsāṃ vaśago bhavet ||10||

11pūjanīyā mahābhāgāḥ puṇyāśca gṛhadīptayaḥ
|
striyaḥ śriyo gṛhasyoktāstasmādrakṣyā viśeṣataḥ ||11||

12piturantaḥpuraṃ dadyānmāturdadyānmahānasam
|
goṣu cātmasamaṃ dadyāt svayameva kṛṣiṃ vrajet||12||

13bhṛtyairvāṇijyacāraṃ ca putraiḥ seveta ca dvijān
|
adbhyo'gnirbrahmataḥ kṣatramaśmano lohamutthitam||13||

14teṣāṃ sarvatragaṃ tejaḥ svāsu yoniṣu śāmyati
|
nityaṃ santaḥ kule jātāḥ pāvakopamatejasaḥ||14||

15kṣamāvanto nirākārāḥ kāṣṭhe'gniriva śerate
|
yasya mantraṃ na jānanti bāhyāścābhyantarāśca ye||15||

16sa rājā sarvataścakṣuściramaiśvaryamaśnute
|
kariṣyanna prabhāṣeta kṛtānyeva tu darśayet||16||

17dharmakāmārthakāryāṇi tathā mantro na bhidyate
|
giripṛṣṭhamupāruhya prāsādaṃ vā rahogataḥ||17||

18araṇye niḥśalāke vā tatra mantro'bhidhīyate
|
nāsuhṛtparamaṃ mantraṃ bhāratārhati veditum||18||

19apaṇḍito vāpi suhṛtpaṇḍito vā'pyanātmavān
|
nāparīkṣya mahīpālaḥ kuryāt sacivamātmanaḥ||19||

20amātye hyarthalipsā ca mantrarakṣaṇameva ca
|
kṛtāni sarvakāryāṇi yasya pāriṣadā viduḥ||20||

21dharme cārthe ca kāme ca sa rājā rājasattamaḥ
|
gūḍhamantrasya nṛpatestasya siddhirasaṃśayam ||21||

22apraśastāni kāryāṇi yo mohādanutiṣṭhati
|
sa teṣāṃ viparibhraṃśādbhraśyate jīvitādapi ||22||

23karmaṇāṃ tu praśastānāmanuṣṭhānaṃ sukhāvaham
|
teṣāmevānanuṣṭhānaṃ paścāttāpakaraṃ matam||23||

24anadhītya yathā vedānna vipraḥ śrāddhamarhati
|
evamaśrutaṣāḍguṇyo na mantraṃ śrotumarhati ||24||

25sthānavṛddhikṣayajñasya ṣāṅguṇyaviditātmanaḥ
|
anavajñātaśīlasya svādhīnā pṛthivī nṛpa ||25||

26amoghakrodhaharṣasya svayaṃ kṛtvā'nvavekṣiṇaḥ
|
ātmapratyayakośasya vasudaiva vasundharā ||26||

27nāmamātreṇa tuṣyeta chatreṇa ca mahīpatiḥ
|
bhṛtyebhyo visṛjedarthānnaikaḥ sarvaharo bhavet ||27||

28brāhmaṇaṃ brāhmaṇo veda bhartā veda striyaṃ tathā
|
amātyaṃ nṛpatirveda rājā rājānameva ca ||28||

29na śatrurvaśamāpanno moktavyo vadhyatāṃ gataḥ
|
nyagbhūtvā paryupāsīta vadhvaṃ hanyādbale sati
|
ahatāddhi bhayaṃ tasmājjāyate na cirādiva ||29||

30daivateṣu prayatnena rājasu brāhmaṇeṣu ca
|
niyantavyaḥ sadā krodho vṛddhabālātureṣu ca||30||

31nirarthaṃ kalahaṃ prājño varjayenmūḍhasevitam
|
kīrtiṃ ca labhate loke na cānarthena yujyate ||31||

32prasādo niṣphalo yasya krodhaścāpi nirarthakaḥ
|
na taṃ bhartāramicchanti ṣaṇḍhaṃ patimiva striyaḥ ||32||

33na buddhirdhanalābhāya na jāḍyamasamṛddhaye
|
lokaparyāyavṛttāntaṃ prājño jānāti netaraḥ ||33||

34vidyāśīlavayovṛddhān buddhivṛddhāṃśca bhārata
|
dhanābhijātavṛddhāṃśca nityaṃ mūḍho'vamanyate||34||

35anāryavṛttamaprājñamasūyakamadhārmikam
|
anarthāḥ kṣipramāyānti vāgduṣṭaṃ krodhanaṃ tathā ||35||

36avisaṃvādanaṃ dānaṃ samayasyāvyatikramaḥ
|
āvartayanti bhūtāni samyakpraṇihitā ca vāk||36||

37avisaṃvādako dakṣaḥ kṛtajño matimānṛjuḥ
|
api saṃkṣīṇakośo'pi labhate parivāraṇam ||37||

38dhṛtiḥ śamo damaḥ śaucaṃ kāruṇyaṃ vāganiṣṭhurā
|
mitrāṇāṃ cānabhidrohaḥ saptaitāḥ samidhaḥ śriyaḥ ||38||

39asaṃvibhāgī duṣṭātmā kṛtaghno nirapatrapaḥ
|
tādṛṅnārādhipo loke varjanīyo narādhipa ||39||

40na ca rātrau sukhaṃ śete sasarpa iva veśmani
|
yaḥ kopayati nirdoṣaṃ sadoṣo'bhyantaraṃ janam ||40||

41yeṣu duṣṭeṣu doṣaḥ syādyogakṣemasya bhārata
|
sadā prasādanaṃ teṣāṃ devatānāmivācaret ||41||

42ye'rthāḥ strīṣu samāyuktāḥ pramattapatiteṣu ca
|
ye cānārye samāsaktāḥ sarve te saṃśayaṃ gatāḥ ||42||

43yatra strī yatra kitavo bālo yatrānuśāsitā
|
majjanti te'vaśā rājannadyāmaśmaplavā iva ||43||

44prayojaneṣu ye saktā na viśeṣeṣu bhārata
|
tānahaṃ paṇḍitānmanye viśeṣā hi prasaṅginaḥ ||44||

45yaṃ praśaṃsanti kitavā yaṃ praśaṃsanti cāraṇāḥ
|
yaṃ praśaṃsanti bandhakyo na sa jīvati mānavaḥ ||45||

46hitvā tān parameṣvāsān pāṇḍavānamitaujasaḥ
|
āhitaṃ bhārataiśvaryaṃ tvayā duryodhane mahat ||46||

47taṃ drakṣyasi paribhraṣṭaṃ tasmāttvamacirādiva
|
aiśvaryamadasaṃmūḍhaṃ baliṃ lokatrayādiva ||47||
