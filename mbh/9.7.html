Mahābhārata 9.7 (Śalyaparvan)

1sañjaya uvāca|
etacchrutvā vaco rājño madrarājaḥ pratāpavān
|
duryodhanaṃ tadā rājan vākyametaduvāca ha||1||

2duryodhana mahābāho śṛṇu vākyavidāṃ vara
|
yāvatau manyase kṛṣṇau rathasthau rathināṃ varau||2||

3na me tulyāvubhāvetau bāhuvīrye kathañcana
|
udyatāṃ pṛthivīṃ sarvāṃ sasurāsuramānavām||3||

4yodhayeyaṃ raṇamukhe saṅkruddhaḥ kimu pāṇḍavān
|
vijeṣyāmi raṇe pārthan somakāṃśca samāgatān||4||

5ahaṃ senāpraṇetā te bhaviṣyāmi na saṃśayaḥ
|
taṃ ca vyūhaṃ vidhāsyāmi na tariṣyanti yaṃ pare||5||

6iti satyaṃ bravīmyeṣa duryodhana na saṃśayaḥ
|
evamuktastato rājā madrādhipatimañjasā||6||

7abhyaṣiñcata senāyā madhye bharatasattama
|
vidhinā śāstradṛṣṭena kliṣṭarūpo viśāmpate||7||

8abhiṣikte tatastasminsiṃhanādo mahānabhūt
|
tava sainye'bhyavādyanta vāditrāṇi ca bhārata||8||

9kuṣṭāścāsaṃstathā yodhā madrakāśca mahārathāḥ
|
tuṣṭuvuścaiva rājānaṃ śalyamāhavaśobhinam||9||

10jaya rājaṃścirañjīva jahi śatrūnsamāgatān
|
tava bāhubalaṃ prāpya dhārtarāṣṭrā mahābalāḥ||10||

11nikhilāḥ pṛthivīṃ sarvā praśāsantu hatadviṣaḥ
|
tvaṃ hi śakto raṇe jetuṃ sasurāsuramānavān||11||

12martyadharmāṇa iha tu kimu sṛñjayasomakān
|
evaṃ sampūjyamānastu madrāṇāmadhipo balī||12||

13harṣaṃ prāpa tadā vīro durāpamakṛtātmabhiḥ
|
śalya uvāca|
adya cāhaṃ raṇe sarvān pañcālānsaha pāṇḍavaiḥ||13||

14nihaniṣyāmi vā rājan svargaṃ yāsyāmi vā hataḥ
|
adya paśyantu māṃ lokā vicarantamabhītavat||14||

15adya pāṇḍusutāḥ sarve vāsudevaḥ sasātyakiḥ
|
pañcālāścedayaścaiva draupadeyāśca sarvaśaḥ||15||

16dhṛṣṭadyumnaḥ śikhaṇḍī ca sarve cāpi prabhadrakāḥ
|
vikramaṃ mama paśyantu dhanuṣaśca mahadbalam||16||

17lāghavaṃ cāstravīryaṃ ca bhujayośca balaṃ yudhi
|
adya paśyantu me pārthāḥ siddhāśca saha cāraṇaiḥ||17||

18yādṛśaṃ me balaṃ bāhvoḥ sampadastreṣu yā ca me
|
adya me vikramaṃ dṛṣṭvā pāṇḍavānāṃ mahārathāḥ||18||

19pratīkāraparā bhūtvā ceṣṭantāṃ vividhāḥ kriyāḥ
|
adya sainyāni pāṇḍūnāṃ drāvayiṣye samantataḥ||19||

20droṇabhīṣmāvati vibho sūtaputraṃ ca saṃyuge
|
vicariṣye raṇe yudhyanpriyārthaṃ tava kaurava||20||

21sañjaya uvāca|
abhiṣikte tathā śalye tava sainyeṣu mānada
|
na karṇaṃ vyasanaṃ kiñcinmenire tatra bhārata||21||

22hṛṣṭāḥ sumanasaścaiva babhūvustatra sainikāḥ
|
menire nihatānpārthānmadrarājavaśaṃ gatān||22||

23praharṣaṃ prāpya senā tu tāvakī bharatarṣabha
|
tāṃ rātrimuṣitā suptā harṣacittā ca sābhavat||23||

24sainyasya tava taṃ śabdaṃ śrutvā rājā yudhiṣṭhiraḥ
|
vārṣṇeyamabravīdvākyaṃ sarvakṣatrasya paśyataḥ||24||

25madrarājaḥ kṛtaḥ śalyo dhārtarāṣṭreṇa mādhava
|
senāpatirmaheṣvāsaḥ sarvasainyeṣu pūjitaḥ||25||

26etajjñātvā yathābhūtaṃ kuru mādhava yatkṣamam
|
bhavānnetā ca goptā ca vidhatsva yadanantaram||26||

27tamabravīnmahārāja vāsudevo janādhipam
|
ārtāyanimahaṃ jāne yathā tattvena bhārata||27||

28vīryavāṃśca mahātejā mahātmā ca viśeṣataḥ
|
kṛtī ca citrayodhī ca saṃyukto lāghavena ca||28||

29yādṛgbhīṣmastathā droṇo yādṛkkarṇaśca saṃyuge
|
tādṛśastadviśiṣṭo vā madrarājo mato mama||29||

30yuddhyamānasya tasyāhaṃ cintayānaśca bhārata
|
yoddhāraṃ nādhigacchāmi tulyarūpaṃ janādhipa||30||

31śikhaṇḍyarjunabhīmānāṃ sātvatasya ca bhārata
|
dhṛṣṭadyumnasya ca tathā balenābhyadhiko raṇe||31||

32madrarājo mahārājaḥ siṃhadviradavikramaḥ
|
vicariṣyatyabhīḥ kāle kālaḥ kruddhaḥ prajāsviva||32||

33tasyādya na prapaśyāmi pratiyoddhāramāhave
|
tvāmṛte puruṣavyāghra śārdūlasamavikramam||33||

34sa devaloke kṛtsne'sminnānyastvattaḥ pumān bhavet
|
madrarājaṃ raṇe kruddhaṃ yo hanyāt kurunandana||34||

35ahanyahani yudhyantaṃ kṣobhayantaṃ balaṃ tava
|
tasmājjahi raṇe śalyaṃ maghavāniva śambaram||35||

36ajeyaścāpyasau vīro dhārtarāṣṭreṇa satkṛtaḥ
|
tavaiva hi jayo nūnaṃ hate madreśvare yudhi||36||

37tasminhate hataṃ sarvaṃ dhārtarāṣṭrabalaṃ mahat
|
etacchrutvā mahārāja vacanaṃ mama sāmpratam||37||

38pratyudyāhi raṇe pārtha madrarājaṃ mahāratham
|
jahi cainaṃ mahābāho vāsavo namuciṃ yathā||38||

39na caivātra dayā kāryā mātulo'yaṃ mameti vai
|
kṣatradharmaṃ puraskṛtya jahi madrajaneśvaram||39||

40droṇabhīṣmārṇavaṃ tīrtvā karṇapātālasambhavam
|
mā nimajjasva sagaṇaḥ śalyamāsādya goṣpadam||40||

41yacca te tapaso vīryaṃ yacca kṣātraṃ balaṃ tava
|
taddarśaya raṇe sarvaṃ jahi cainaṃ mahāratham||41||

42etāvaduktvā vacanaṃ keśavaḥ paravīrahā
|
jagāma śibiraṃ sāyaṃ pūjyamāno'tha pāṇḍavaiḥ||42||

43keśave tu tadā yāte dharmaputro yudhiṣṭhiraḥ
|
visṛjya sarvān bhrātṝṃśca pañcālānatha somakān||43||

44suṣvāpa rajanīṃ tāṃ tu viśalya iva kuñjaraḥ
|
te ca sarve maheṣvāsāḥ pañcālāḥ pāṇḍavāstathā||44||

45karṇasya nidhane hṛṣṭāḥ suṣupustāṃ niśāṃ tadā
|
gatajvaraṃ maheṣvāsaṃ tīrṇapāraṃ mahāratham||45||

46babhūva pāṇḍaveyānāṃ sainyaṃ ca muditaṃ nṛpa
|
sūtaputrasya nidhane jayaṃ labdhvā ca māriṣa||46||
