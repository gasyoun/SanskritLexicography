Mahābhārata 1.66 (Ādiparvan)

1vaiśaṃpāyana uvāca|
brahmaṇo mānasāḥ putrā viditāḥ ṣaṇmaharṣayaḥ
|
ekādaśa sutāḥ sthāṇoḥ khyātāḥ paramatejasaḥ||1||

2mṛgavyādhaśca sarpaśca nirṛtiśca mahāyaśāḥ
|
ajaikapādahirbidhnyaḥ pinākī ca parantapaḥ||2||

3dahano'theśvaraścaiva kapālī ca mahādyutiḥ
|
sthāṇurbhagaśca bhagavān rudrā ekādaśa smṛtāḥ||3||

4marīciraṅgirā atriḥ pulastyaḥ pulahaḥ kratuḥ
|
ṣaḍete brahmaṇaḥ putrā vīryavanto maharṣayaḥ||4||

5trayastvaṅgirasaḥ putrā loke sarvatra viśrutāḥ
|
bṛhaspatirutathyaśca saṃvartaśca dhṛtavratāḥ||5||

6atrestu bahavaḥ putrāḥ śrūyante manujādhipa
|
sarve vedavidaḥ siddhāḥ śāntātmāno maharṣayaḥ||6||

7rākṣasāśca pulastyasya vānarāḥ kinnarāstathā
|
yakṣāśca manujavyāghra putrāstasya ca dhīmataḥ||7||

8pulahasya sutā rājañchalabhāśca prakīrtitāḥ
|
siṃhāḥ kipuruṣā vyāghrā yakṣā īhāmṛgāstathā||8||

9kratoḥ kratusamāḥ putrāḥ pataṅgasahacāriṇaḥ
|
viśrutāstriṣu lokeṣu satyavrataparāyaṇāḥ||9||

10dakṣastvajāyatāṅguṣṭhāddakṣiṇādbhagavānṛṣiḥ
|
brahmaṇaḥ pṛthivīpāla śāntātmā sumahātapāḥ||10||

11vāmādajāyatāṅguṣṭhādbhāryā tasya mahātmanaḥ
|
tasyāṃ pañcāśataṃ kanyāḥ sa evājanayanmuniḥ||11||

12tāḥ sarvāstvanavadyāṅgyaḥ kanyāḥ kamalalocanāḥ
|
putrikāḥ sthāpayāmāsa naṣṭaputraḥ prajāpatiḥ||12||

13dadau sa daśa dharmāya saptaviṃśatimindave
|
divyena vidhinā rājankaśyapāya trayodaśa||13||

14nāmato dharmapatnyastāḥ kīrtyamānā nibodha me
|
kīrtirlakṣmīrdhṛtirmedhā puṣṭiḥ śraddhā kriyā tathā||14||

15buddhirlajjā matiścaiva patnyo dharmasya tā daśa
|
dvārāṇyetāni dharmasya vihitāni svayaṃbhuvā||15||

16saptaviṃśatiḥ somasya patnyo lokasya viśrutāḥ
|
kālasya nayane yuktāḥ somapatnyāḥ śucivratāḥ||16||

17sarvā nakṣatrayoginyo lokayātrā vidhānataḥ
|
paitāmaho munirdevastasya putraḥ prajāpatiḥ
|
tasyā'ṣṭau vasavaḥ putrāsteṣāṃ vakṣyāmi vistaram||17||

18dharo dhruvaśca somaśca ahaścaivānilo'nalaḥ
|
pratyūṣaśca prabhāsaśca vasavo'ṣṭau prakīrtitāḥ||18||

19dhūmrāyāstu dharaḥ putro brahmavidyo dhruvastathā
|
candramāstu manasvinyāḥ śvāsāyāḥ śvasanastathā||19||

20ratāyāścāpyahaḥ putraḥ śāṇḍilyāśca hutāśanaḥ
|
pratyūṣaśca prabhāsaśca prabhātāyāḥ sutau smṛtau||20||

21dharasya putro draviṇo hutahavyavahastathā
|
dhruvasya putro bhagavānkālo lokaprakālanaḥ||21||

22somasya tu suto varcā varcasvī yena jāyate
|
manoharāyāḥ śiśiraḥ prāṇo'tha ramaṇastathā||22||

23ahnaḥ sutastathā jyotiḥ śamaḥ śāntastathā muniḥ
|
agneḥ putraḥ kumārastu śrīmāñcharavaṇālayaḥ||23||

24tasya śākho viśākhaśca naigameyaśca pṛṣṭhajaḥ
|
kṛttikābhyupapatteśca kārtikeya iti smṛtaḥ||24||

25anilasya śivā bhāryā tasyāḥ putro manojavaḥ
|
avijñātagatiścaiva dvau putrāvanilasya tu||25||

26pratyūṣasya viduḥ putramṛṣiṃ nāmnā'tha devalam
|
dvau putrau devalasyāpi kṣamāvantau manīṣiṇau
|
bṛhaspatestu bhaginī varastrī brahmavādinī||26||

27yogasaktā jagatkṛtsnamasaktā vicacāra ha
|
prabhāsasya tu bhāryā sā vasūnāmaṣṭamasya ha||27||

28viśvakarmā mahābhāgo jajñe śilpaprajāpatiḥ
|
kartā śilpasahasrāṇāṃ tridaśānāṃ ca vardhakiḥ||28||

29bhūṣaṇānāṃ ca sarveṣāṃ kartā śilpavatāṃ varaḥ
|
yo divyāni vimānāni tridaśānāṃ cakāra ha||29||

30manuṣyāścopajīvanti yasya śilpaṃ mahātmanaḥ
|
pūjayanti ca yaṃ nityaṃ viśvakarmāṇamavyayam||30||

31stanaṃ tu dakṣiṇaṃ bhittvā brahmaṇo naravigrahaḥ
|
niḥsṛto bhagavāndharmaḥ sarvalokasukhāvahaḥ||31||

32trayastasya varāḥ putrāḥ sarvabhūtamanoharāḥ
|
śamaḥ kāmaśca harṣaśca tejasā lokadhāriṇaḥ||32||

33kāmasya tu ratirbhāryā śamasya prāptiraṅganā
|
nandā tu bhāryā harṣasya yāsu lokāḥ pratiṣṭhitāḥ||33||

34marīceḥ kaśyapaḥ putraḥ kaśyapasya surāsurāḥ
|
jajñire nṛpaśārdūla lokānāṃ prabhavastu saḥ||34||

35tvāṣṭrī tu saviturbhāryā vaḍavārūpadhāriṇī
|
asūyata mahābhāgā sāntarikṣe'svināvubhau||35||

36dvādaśaivāditeḥ putrāḥ śakramukhyā narādhipa
|
teṣāmavarajo viṣṇuryatra lokāḥ pratiṣṭhitāḥ||36||

37trayastriṃśata ityete devāsteṣāmahaṃ tava
|
anvayaṃ saṃpravakṣyāmi pakṣaiśca kulato gaṇān||37||

38rudrāṇāmaparaḥ pakṣaḥ sādhyānāṃ marutāṃ tathā
|
vasūnāṃ bhārgavaṃ vidyādviśvedevāṃstathaiva ca||38||

39vainateyastu garuḍo balavānaruṇastathā
|
bṛhaspatiśca bhagavānādityeṣveva gaṇyate||39||

40aśvinau guhyakānviddhi sarvauṣadhyastathā paśūn
|
ete devagaṇā rājankīrtitāste'nupūrvaśaḥ||40||

41yānkīrtayitvā manujaḥ sarvapāpaiḥ pramucyate
|
brahmaṇo hṛdayaṃ bhittvā niḥsṛto bhagavānbhṛguḥ||41||

42bhṛgoḥ putraḥ kavirvidvāñchukraḥ kavisuto grahaḥ
|
trailokyaprāṇayātrārthaṃ varṣāvarṣe bhayābhaye
|
svayaṃbhuvā niyuktaḥ sanbhuvanaṃ paridhāvati||42||

43yogācāryo mahābuddhirdaityānāmabhavadguruḥ
|
surāṇāṃ cāpi medhāvī brahmacārī yatavrataḥ||43||

44tasminniyukte vidhinā yogakṣemāya bhārgave
|
anyamutpādayāmāsa putraṃ bhṛguraninditam||44||

45cyavanaṃ dīptatapasaṃ dharmātmānaṃ yaśasvinam
|
yaḥ sa roṣāccyuto garbhānmāturmokṣāya bhārata||45||

46āruṣī tu manoḥ kanyā tasya patnī manīṣiṇaḥ
|
aurvastasyāṃ samabhavadūruṃ bhittvā mahāyaśāḥ||46||

47mahātejā mahāvīryo bāla eva guṇairyutaḥ
|
ṛcīkastasya putrastu jamadagnistato'bhavat||47||

48jamadagnestu catvāra āsanputrā mahātmanaḥ
|
rāmasteṣāṃ jaghanyo'bhūdajaghanyairguṇairyutaḥ
|
sarvaśastreṣu kuśalaḥ kṣatriyāntakaro vaśī||48||

49aurvasyāsītputraśataṃ jamadagnipurogamam
|
teṣāṃ putrasahasrāṇi babhūvurbhuvi vistaraḥ||49||

50dvau putro brahmaṇastvanyau yayostiṣṭhati lakṣaṇam
|
loke dhātā vidhātā ca yau sthitau manunā saha||50||

51tayoreva svasā devī lakṣmīḥ padmagṛhā śubhā
|
tasyāstu mānasāḥ putrāsturagā vyomacāriṇaḥ||51||

52varuṇasya bhāryā yā jyeṣṭhā śukrāddevī vyajāyata
|
tasyāḥ putraṃ balaṃ viddhi surāṃ ca suranandinīm||52||

53prajānāmannakāmānāmanyonyaparibhakṣaṇāt
|
adharmastatra saṃjātaḥ sarvabhūtavināśakaḥ||53||

54tasyāpi nirṛtirbhāryā nairṛtā yena rākṣasāḥ
|
ghorāstasyāstrayaḥ putrāḥ pāpakarmaratāḥ sadā ||54||

55bhayo mahābhayaścaiva mṛtyurbhūtāntakastathā
|
na tasya bhāryā putro vā kaścidastyantako hi saḥ||55||

56kākīṃ śyenīṃ tathā bhāsīṃ dhṛtarāṣṭrīṃ tathā śukīm
|
tāmrā tu suṣuve devī pañcaitā lokaviśrutāḥ||56||

57ulūkānsuṣuve kākī śyenī śyenānvyajāyata
|
bhāsī bhāsānajanayadgṛdhrāṃścaiva janādhipa||57||

58dhṛtarāṣṭrī tu haṃsāṃśca kalahaṃsāṃśca sarvaśaḥ
|
cakravākāṃśca bhadrā tu janayāmāsa saiva tu||58||

59śukī ca janayāmāsa śukāneva yaśasvinī
|
kalyāṇaguṇasaṃpannā sarvalakṣaṇapūjitā||59||

60nava krodhavaśā nārīḥ prajajñe krodhasaṃbhavāḥ
|
mṛgī ca mṛgamandā ca harī bhadramanā api||60||

61mātaṅgī tvatha śārdūlī śvetā surabhireva ca
|
sarvalakṣaṇasaṃpannā surasā caiva bhāminī||61||

62apatyaṃ tu mṛgāḥ sarve mṛgyā naravarottama
|
ṛkṣāśca mṛgamandāyāḥ sṛmarāśca paraṃtapa||62||

63tatastvairāvataṃ nāgaṃ jajñe bhadramanāḥ sutam
|
airāvataḥ sutastasyā devanāgo mahāgajaḥ||63||

64haryāśca harayo'patyaṃ vānarāśca tarasvinaḥ
|
golāṅgūlāṃśca bhadraṃ te haryāḥ putrānpracakṣate||64||

65prajajñe tvatha śārdūlī siṃhānvyāghrānanekaśaḥ
|
dvīpinaśca mahāsatvānsarvāneva na saśaṃyaḥ||65||

66mātaṅgyapi ca mātaṅgānapatyāni narādhipa
|
diśāṃ gajaṃ tu śvetākhyaṃ śvetā'janayadāśugam||66||

67tathā duhitarau rājansurabhirvai vyajāyata
|
rohiṇī caiva bhadraṃ te gandharvī tu yaśasvinī||67||

68vimalāmapi bhadraṃ te analāmapi bhārata
|
rohiṇyāṃ jajñire gāvo gandharvyāṃ vājinaḥ sutāḥ
|
saptapiṇḍaphalānvṛkṣānanalāpi vyajāyata||68||

69analāyāḥ śukī putrī kaṃkastu surasāsutaḥ
|
aruṇasya bhāryā śyenī tu vīryavantau mahābalau||69||

70saṃpātiṃ janayāmāsa vīryavantaṃ jaṭāyuṣam
|
surasā'janayannāgānkadrūḥ putrāṃstu pannagān||70||

71dvau putrau vinatāyāstu vikhyātau garuḍāruṇau
|
ityeṣa sarvabhūtānāṃ mahatāṃ manujādhipa
|
prabhavaḥ kīrtitaḥ samyaṅmayā matimatāṃvara||71||

72yaṃ śrutvā puruṣaḥ samyaṅmukto bhavati pāpmanaḥ
|
sarvajñatāṃ ca labhate ratimagryāṃ ca vindati||72||
