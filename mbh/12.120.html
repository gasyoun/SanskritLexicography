Mahābhārata 12.120 (Śāntiparvan)

1yudhiṣṭhira uvāca|
rājavṛttānyanekāni tvayā proktāni bhārata
|
pūrvaiḥ pūrvaniyuktāni rājadharmārthavedibhiḥ||1||

2tadeva vistareṇoktaṃ pūrvadṛṣṭaṃ satāṃ matam
|
praṇeyaṃ rājadharmāṇāṃ prabrūhi bharatarṣabha||2||

3bhīṣma uvāca|
rakṣaṇaṃ sarvabhūtānāmiti kṣātraṃ paraṃ matam
|
tadyathā rakṣaṇaṃ kuryāttathā śṛṇu mahīpate||3||

4yathā barhāṇi citrāṇi bibharti bhujagāśanaḥ
|
tathā bahuvidhaṃ rājā rūpaṃ kurvīta dharmavit||4||

5taikṣṇyaṃ jihmatvamādālbhyaṃ satyamārjavameva ca
|
madhyasthaḥ sattvamātiṣṭhaṃstathā vai sukhamṛcchati||5||

6yasminnarthe hitaṃ yatsyāttadvarṇaṃ rūpamādiśet
|
bahurūpasya rājño hi sūkṣmo'pyartho na sīdati||6||

7nityaṃ rakṣitamantraḥ syādyathā mūkaḥ śaracchikhī
|
ślakṣṇākṣaratanuḥ śrīmānbhavecchāstraviśāradaḥ||7||

8āpaddvāreṣu yuktaḥ syājjalaprasravaṇeṣviva
|
śailavarṣodakānīva dvijānsiddhānsamāśrayet
|
arthakāmaḥ śikhāṃ rājā kuryāddharmadhvajopamām||8||

9nityamudyatadaṇḍaḥ syādācaredapramādataḥ
|
loke cāyavyayau dṛṣṭvā bṛhadvṛkṣamivāsravat||9||

10mṛjāvān syāt svayūthyeṣu bhaumāni caraṇaiḥ kṣipet
|
jātapakṣaḥ parispandet prekṣedvaikalyamātmanaḥ||10||

11doṣānvivṛṇuyācchatroḥ parapakṣānvidhūnayet
|
kānaneṣviva puṣpāṇi bahirarthātsamācaran||11||

12ucchritānnāśayetsphītānnarendrānacalopamān
|
śrayecchāyāmavijñātāṃ guptaṃ raṇamupāśrayet||12||

13prāvṛṣīvāsitagrīvo majjeta niśi nirjane
|
māyūreṇa guṇenaiva strībhiścāvekṣitaścaret||13||

14na jahyācca tanutrāṇaṃ rakṣedātmānamātmanā
|
cārabhūmiṣvabhigatānpāśāṃśca parivarjayet||14||

15praṇayedvāpi tāṃ bhūmiṃ praṇaśyedgahane punaḥ
|
hanyātkruddhānativiṣāṃstān jihmagatayo'hitān||15||

16nāśayedbalabarhāṇi sannivāsānnivāsayet
|
sadā barhinibhaḥ kāmaṃ praśastaṃ kṛtamācaret
|
sarvataścādadetprajñā pataṅgaṃ gahaneṣviva||16||

17evaṃ mayūravadrājā svarājyaṃ paripālayet
|
ātmavṛddhikarīṃ nītiṃ vidadhīta vicakṣaṇaḥ||17||

18ātmasaṃyamanaṃ buddhyā parabuddhyāvadhāraṇam
|
buddhyā cātmaguṇaprāptiretacchāstranidarśanam||18||

19paraṃ viśvāsayetsāmnā svaśaktiṃ copalakṣayet
|
ātmanaḥ parimarśena buddhiṃ buddhyā vicārayet||19||

20sāntvayogamatiḥ prājñaḥ kāryākāryaprayojakaḥ
|
nigūḍhabuddherdhīrasya vaktavye vā kṛtaṃ tathā||20||

21sa nikṛṣṭāṃ kathāṃ prājño yadi buddhyā bṛhaspatiḥ
|
svabhāvameṣyate taptaṃ kṛṣṇāyasamivodake||21||

22anuyuñjīta kṛtyāni sarvāṇyeva mahīpatiḥ
|
āgamairupadiṣṭāni svasya caiva parasya ca||22||

23mṛdu śīlaṃ tathā prājñaṃ śūraṃ cārthavidhānavit
|
svakarmaṇi niyuñjīta ye cānye ca balādhikāḥ||23||

24atha dṛṣṭvā niyuktāni svānurūpeṣu karmasu
|
sarvāṃstānanuvarteta svarāṃstantrīrivāyatā||24||

25dharmāṇāmavirodhena sarveṣāṃ priyamācaret
|
mamāyamiti rājā yaḥ saparvata ivācalaḥ||25||

26vyavasāyaṃ samādhāya sūryo raśmīnivāyatān
|
dharmamevābhirakṣeta kṛtvā tulye priyāpriye||26||

27kulaprakṛtideśānāṃ dharmajñānmṛdubhāṣiṇaḥ
|
madhye vayasi nirdoṣān hite yuktānaviklavān||27||

28alubdhān śikṣitāndāntān dharmeṣu pariniṣṭhitān
|
sthāpayetsarvakāryeṣu rājā dharmārtharakṣiṇaḥ||28||

29etena ca prakāreṇa kṛtyānāmāgatiṃ gatim
|
yuktaḥ samanutiṣṭheta tuṣṭaścārairupaskṛtaḥ||29||

30amoghakrodhaharṣasya svayaṃ kṛtyānvavekṣituḥ
|
ātmapratyayakośasya vasudhaiva vasundharā||30||

31vyaktaścānugraho yasya yathārthaścāpi nigrahaḥ
|
guptātmā guptarāṣṭraśca sa rājā rājadharmavit||31||

32nityaṃ rāṣṭramavekṣeta gobhiḥ sūrya ivoditaḥ
|
carānsvanucarānvidyāttathā buddhyā svayaṃ caret||32||

33kālaṃ prāptamupādadyānnārthaṃ rājā prasūcayet
|
ahanyahani sanduhyānmahīṃ gāmiva buddhimān||33||

34yathā krameṇa puṣpebhyaścinoti madhu ṣaṭpadaḥ
|
tathā dravyamupādāya rājā kurvīta saṃcayam||34||

35yaddhi guptāvaśiṣṭaṃ syāttadvittaṃ dharmakāmayoḥ
|
sañcayānna visargī syādrājā śāstravidātmavān||35||

36nārthamalpaṃ paribhavennāvamanyeta śātravān
|
buddhyā tu buddhyedātmānaṃ na cābuddhiṣu viśvaset||36||

37dhṛtirdākṣyaṃ saṃyamo buddhirātmā dhairyaṃ śauryaṃ deśakālāpramādaḥ
|
alpasya vā bahuno vā vivṛddhau dhanasyaitānyaṣṭa samindhanāni||37||

38agniḥ stoko vardhate'pyājyasikto bījaṃ caikaṃ rohasahasrameti
|
āyavyayau vipulau sanniśāmya tasmādalpaṃ nāvamanyeta vittam||38||

39bālo'pyabālaḥ sthaviro ripuryaḥ sadā pramattaṃ puruṣaṃ nihanyāt
|
kālenānyastasya mūlaṃ hareta kālajñātā pārthivānāṃ variṣṭhaḥ||39||

40haretkīrtiṃ dharmamasyoparundhyādarthe dīrghaṃ vīryamasyopahanyāt
|
ripurdveṣṭā durbalo vā balī vā tasmācchatrīrnaiva hīyedyatātmā||40||

41kṣayaṃ, vṛddhiṃ, pālanaṃ, saṃcayaṃ vā buddhvā'pyubhau saṃhatau sarvakāmau
|
tataścānyanmatimānsaṃdadhīta tasmādrājā buddhimattāṃ śrayeta||41||

42buddhirdīptā balavantaṃ hinasti balaṃ buddhyā pālyate vardhamānam
|
śatrurbuddhyā sīdate vardhamāno buddheḥ paścātkarma yattatpraśastam||42||

43sarvānkāmānkāmayāno hi dhīraḥ sattvenālpenāpnute hīnadoṣaḥ
|
yaścātmānaṃ prārthayate'rthyamānaiḥ śreyaḥ pātraṃ pūrayate ca nālyam||43||

44tasmādrājā pragṛhītaḥ prajāsu mūlaṃ lakṣmyāḥ sarvaśo hyādadīta
|
dīrghaṃ kālaṃ hyapi saṃpīḍyamāno vidyutsampātamapi vā norjitaḥ syāt||44||

45vidyā tapo vā vipulaṃ dhanaṃ vā sarvaṃ hyetadvyavasāyena śakyam
|
buddhyāyattaṃ tannivaseddehavatsu tasmādvidyādvyavasāyaṃ prabhūtam||45||

46yatrāsate matimanto manasvinaḥ śakro viṣṇuryatra sarasvatī ca
|
vasanti bhūtāni ca yatra nityaṃ tasmādvidvānnāvamanyeta deham||46||

47lubdhaṃ hanyātsampradānena nityaṃ lubdhastṛptiṃ paravittasya naiti
|
sarvo lubdhaḥ karmaguṇopabhoge yo'rthairhīno dharmakāmau jahāti||47||

48dhanaṃ bhogaṃ putradāraṃ samṛddhiṃ sarvaṃ lubdhaḥ prārthayate pareṣām
|
lubdhe doṣāḥ sambhavantīha sarve tasmādrājā na pragṛhṇīta lubdham||48||

49saṃdarśanena puruṣaṃ jaghanyamapi codayet
|
ārambhāndviṣatāṃ prājñaḥ sarvārthāśca prasūdayet||49||

50dharmānviteṣu vijñātā mantrī guptaśca pāṇḍava
|
āpto rājā kulīnaśca paryāpto rājasaṃgrahe||50||

51vidhiprayuktānnaradevadharmānuktānsamāsena nibodha buddhyā
|
imānvidadhyādvyatisṛtya yo vai rājā mahīṃ pālayituṃ sa śaktaḥ||51||

52anītijaṃ yasya vidhānajaṃ sukhaṃ haṭhapraṇītaṃ vidhivatpradṛśyate
|
na vidyate tasya gatirmahīpartena vidyate rājyasukhaṃ hyanuttamam||52||

53dhanairviśiṣṭānmatiśīlapūjitānguṇopapannānyudhi dṛṣṭavikramān
|
guṇeṣu dṛṣṭvā na cirādivātmavān yato'bhisandhāya nihanti śātravān||53||

54paśyedupāyānvividhaiḥ kriyāpathairna cānupāyena matiṃ niveśayet
|
śriyaṃ viśiṣṭāṃ, vipulaṃ yaśo, dhanaṃ na doṣadarśī puruṣaḥ samaśnute||54||

55prītipravṛttau vinivartitau yathā suhṛtsu vijñāya nivṛtya cobhayoḥ
|
yadeva mitraṃ gurubhāramāvahettadeva susnigdhamudāharedbudhaḥ||55||

56etānmayoktāṃścara rājadharmānnṝṇāṃ ca guptau matimādadhatsva
|
avāpsyase puṇyaphalaṃ sukhena sarvo hi loko nṛpa dharmamūlaḥ||56||
