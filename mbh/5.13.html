Mahābhārata 5.13 (Udyogaparvan)

1śalya uvāca|
atha tāmabravīddṛṣṭvā nahuṣo devarāṭ tadā
|
trayāṇāmapi lokānāmahamindraḥ śucismite ||1||

2bhajasva māṃ varārohe patitve varavarṇini
|
evamuktā tu sā devī nahuṣeṇa pativratā ||2||

3prāvepata bhayodvignā pravāte kadalī yathā
|
praṇamya sā hi brahmāṇaṃ śirasā tu kṛtāñjaliḥ||3||

4devarājamathovāca nahuṣaṃ ghoradarśanam
|
kālamicchāmyahaṃ labdhuṃ tvattaḥ kaṃcitsureśvara ||4||

5na hi vijñāyate śakraḥ kiṃvā prāptaḥ kva vā gataḥ
|
tattvametattu vijñāya yadi na jñāyate prabho ||5||

6tato'haṃ tvāmupasthāsye satyametadbravīmi te
|
evamuktaḥ sa indrāṇyā nahuṣaḥ prītimānabhūt ||6||

7nahuṣa uvāca|
evaṃ bhavatu suśroṇi yathā māmiha bhāṣase
|
jñātvā cāgamanaṃ kāryaṃ satyametadanusmareḥ ||7||

8nahuṣeṇa visṛṣṭā ca niścakrāma tataḥ śubhā
|
bṛhaspatiniketaṃ ca sā jagāma yaśasvinī ||8||

9tasyāḥ saṃśrutya ca vaco devāścāgnipurogamāḥ
|
cintayāmāsurekāgrāḥ śakrārthaṃ rājasattama||9||

10devadevena saṅgamya viṣṇunā prabhaviṣṇunā
|
ūcuścainaṃ samudvignā vākyaṃ vākyaviśāradāḥ ||10||

11brahmavadhyābhibhūto vai śakraḥ suragaṇeśvaraḥ
|
gatiśca nastvaṃ deveśa pūrvajo jagataḥ prabhuḥ ||11||

12rakṣārthaṃ sarvabhūtānāṃ viṣṇutvamupajagmivān
|
tvadvīryanihate vṛtre vāsavo brahmahatyayā ||12||

13vṛtaḥ suragaṇaśreṣṭha mokṣaṃ tasya vinirdiśa
|
teṣāṃ tadvacanaṃ śrutvā devānāṃ viṣṇurabravīt ||13||

14māmeva yajatāṃ śakraḥ pāvayiṣyāmi vajriṇam
|
puṇyena hayamedhena māmiṣṭvā pākaśāsanaḥ ||14||

15punareṣyati devānāmindratvamakutobhayaḥ
|
svakarmabhiśca nahuṣo nāśaṃ yāsyati durmatiḥ ||15||

16kiṃcitkālamidaṃ devā marṣayadhvamatandritāḥ
|
śrutvā viṣṇoḥ śubhāṃ satyāṃ vāṇīṃ tāmamṛtopamām||16||

17tataḥ sarve suragaṇāḥ sopādhyāyāḥ saharṣibhiḥ
|
yatra śakro bhayodvignastaṃ deśamupacakramuḥ ||17||

18tatrāśvamedhaḥ sumahānmahendrasya mahātmanaḥ
|
vavṛte pāvanārthaṃ vai brahmahatyāpaho nṛpa ||18||

19vibhajya brahmahatyāṃ tu vṛkṣeṣu ca nadīṣu ca
|
parvateṣu pṛthivyāṃ ca strīṣu caiva yudhiṣṭhira ||19||

20saṃvibhajya ca bhūteṣu visṛjya ca sureśvaraḥ
|
vijvaro dhūtapāpmā ca vāsavo'bhavadātmavān ||20||

21akampannahuṣaṃ sthānāddṛṣṭvā balaniṣūdanaḥ
|
tejoghnaṃ sarvabhūtānāṃ varadānācca duḥsaham||21||

22tataḥ śacīpatirdevaḥ punareva vyanaśyata
|
adṛśyaḥ sarvabhūtānāṃ kālākāṅkṣī cacāra ha ||22||

23pranaṣṭe tu tataḥ śakre śacī śokasamanvitā
|
hā śakreti tadā devī vilalāpa suduḥkhitā ||23||

24yadi dattaṃ yadi hutaṃ guravastoṣitā yadi
|
ekabhartṛtvamevāstu satyaṃ yadyasti vā mayi ||24||

25puṇyāṃ cemāmahaṃ divyāṃ pravṛttāmuttarāyaṇe
|
devīṃ rātriṃ namasyāmi sidhyatāṃ me manorathaḥ ||25||

26prayatā ca niśāṃ devīmupātiṣṭhata tatra sā
|
pativratātvāt satyena sopaśrutimathākarot ||26||

27yatrāste devarājo'sau taṃ deśaṃ darśayasva me
|
ityāhopaśrutiṃ devīṃ satyaṃ satyena dṛśyatām ||27||
