Mahābhārata 9.13 (Śalyaparvan)

1sañjaya uvāca|
pīḍite dharmarāje tu madrarājena māriṣa
|
sātyakirbhīmasenaśca mādrīputrau ca pāṇḍavau||1||

2parivārya rathaiḥ śalyaṃ pīḍayāmāsurāhave
|
tamekaṃ bahubhirdṛṣṭvā pīḍyamānaṃ mahārathaiḥ||2||

3sādhuvādo mahāñjajñe siddhāścāsanpraharṣitāḥ
|
āścaryamityabhāṣanta munayaścāpi saṅgatāḥ||3||

4bhīmaseno raṇe śalyaṃ śalyabhūtaṃ parākrame
|
ekena viddhvā bāṇena punarvivyādha saptabhiḥ||4||

5sātyakiśca śatenainaṃ dharmaputraparīpsayā
|
madreśvaramavākīrya siṃhanādamathānadat||5||

6nakulaḥ pañcabhiścainaṃ sahadevaśca pañcabhiḥ
|
viddhvā taṃ tu punastūrṇaṃ tato vivyādha saptabhiḥ||6||

7sa tu śūro raṇe yattaḥ pīḍitastairmahārathaiḥ
|
vikṛtya kārmukaṃ ghoraṃ veganaṃ bhārasādhanam||7||

8sātyakiṃ pañcaviṃśatyā śalyo vivyādha māriṣa
|
bhīmasenaṃ tu saptatyā nukalaṃ saptabhistathā||8||

9tataḥ saviśikhaṃ cāpaṃ sahadevasya dhanvinaḥ
|
chittvā bhallena samare vivyādhainaṃ trisaptabhiḥ||9||

10sahadevastu samare mātulaṃ bhūrivarcasam
|
sajyamanyaddhanuḥ kṛtvā pañcabhiḥ samatāḍayat||10||

11śarairāśīviṣākārairdhvalajvalanasannibhaiḥ
|
sārathiṃ cāsya samare śareṇānataparvaṇā||11||

12vivyādha bhṛśasaṅkruddhastaṃ vai bhūyastribhiḥ śaraiḥ
|
bhīmasenastu saptatyā sātyakirnavabhiḥ śaraiḥ||12||

13dharmarājastathā ṣaṣṭyā gātre śalyaṃ samārpayat
|
tataḥ śalyo mahārāja nirviddhastairmahārathaiḥ||13||

14susrāva rudhiraṃ gātrairgairikaṃ parvato yathā
|
tāṃśca sarvānmaheṣvāsānpañcabhiḥ pañcabhiḥ śaraiḥ||14||

15vivyādha tarasā rājaṃstadadbhutamivābhavat
|
tato'pareṇa bhallena dharmaputrasya māriṣa||15||

16dhanuściccheda samare sajyaṃ sa sumahārathaḥ
|
athānyaddhanurādāya dharmaputro yudhiṣṭhiraḥ||16||

17sāśvasūtadhvajarathaṃ śalyaṃ prācchādayaccharaiḥ
|
sa cchādyamānaḥ samare dharmaputrasya sāyakaiḥ||17||

18yudhiṣṭhiramathāvidhyaddaśabhirniśitaiḥ śaraiḥ
|
sātyakistu tataḥ kruddho darmaputre śarārdite||18||

19madrāṇāmadhipaṃ śūraṃ śarairvivyādha pañcabhiḥ
|
sa sātyakeḥ praciccheda kṣurapreṇa mahaddhanuḥ||19||

20bhīmasenamukhāṃstāṃśca tribhistribhiratāḍayat
|
tasya kruddho mahārāja sātyakiḥ satyavikramaḥ||20||

21tomaraṃ preṣayāmāsa svarṇadaṇḍaṃ mahādhanam
|
bhīmaseno'tha nārācaṃ jvalantamiva pannagam||21||

22nakulaḥ samare śaktiṃ sahadevo gadāṃ śubhām
|
dharmarājaḥ śataghnīṃ ca jighāṃsuḥ śalyamāhave||22||

23tānāpatata evāśu pañcānāṃ vai bhujacyutān
|
vārayāmāsa samare śastrasaṅghaiḥ sa madrarāṭ||23||

24sātyakiprahitaṃ śalyo bhallaiściccheda tomaram
|
prahitaṃ bhīmasenena śaraṃ kanakabhūṣaṇam||24||

25dvidhā ciccheda samare kṛtahastaḥ pratāpavān
|
nakulapreṣitāṃ śaktiṃ hemadaṇḍāṃ bhayāvahām||25||

26gadāṃ ca sahadevena śaraughaiḥ samavārayat
|
śarābhyāṃ ca śataghnīṃ tāṃ rājñaściccheda bhārata||26||

27paśyatāṃ pāṇḍuputrāṇāṃ siṃhanādaṃ nanāda ca
|
nāmṛṣyattatra śaineyaḥ śatrorvijayamāhave||27||

28athānyaddhanurādāya sātyakiḥ krodhamūrcchitaḥ
|
dvābhyāṃ madreśvaraṃ viddhvā sārathiṃ ca tribhiḥ śaraiḥ||28||

29tataḥ śalyo raṇe rājansarvāstāndaśabhiḥ śaraiḥ
|
vivyādha bhṛśasaṅkruddhastotrairiva mahādvipān||29||

30te vāryamāṇāḥ samare madrarājñā mahārathāḥ
|
na śekuḥ sammukhe sthātuṃ tasya śatruniṣūdanāḥ||30||

31tato duryodhano rājā dṛṣṭvā śalyasya vikramam
|
nihatānpāṇḍavān mene pañcālānatha sṛñjayān||31||

32tato rājanmahābāhurbhīmasenaḥ pratāpavān
|
santyajya manasā prāṇān madrādhipamayodhayat||32||

33nakulaḥ sahadevaśca sātyakiśca mahārathaḥ
|
parivārya tadā śalyaṃ samantādavyakirañśaraiḥ||33||

34sa caturbhirmaheṣvāsaiḥ pāṇḍavānāṃ mahārathaiḥ
|
vṛtastānyodhayāmāsa madrārājaḥ pratāpavān||34||

35tasya dharmasuto rājan kṣurapreṇa mahāhave
|
cakrarakṣaṃ jaghānāśu madrarājasya pārthivaḥ||35||

36tasmiṃstu nihate śūre cakrarakṣe mahārathe
|
madrarājo'pi balavānsainikānāvṛṇoccharaiḥ||36||

37samāvṛtāṃstatastāṃstu rājanvīkṣya svasainikān
|
cintayāmāsa samare dharmaputro yudhiṣṭhiraḥ||37||

38kathaṃ nu samare śakyaṃ tanmādhavavaco mahat
|
na hi kruddho raṇe rājan kṣapayeta balaṃ mama||38||

39tataḥ sarathanāgāśvāḥ pāṇḍavāḥ pāṇḍupūrvaja
|
madrarājaṃ samāseduḥ pīḍayantaḥ samantataḥ||39||

40nānāśastraughabahulāṃ śastrāvṛṣṭiṃ samudyatām
|
vyadhamatsamare rājā mahābhrāṇīva mārutaḥ||40||

41tataḥ kanakapuṅkhāṃ tāṃ śalyakṣiptāṃ viyadgatām
|
śaravṛṣṭimapaśyāma śalabhānāmivāyatim||41||

42te śarā madrarājena preṣitā raṇamūrdhani
|
sampatantaḥ sma dṛśyante śalabhānāṃ vrajā iva||42||

43madrarājadhanurmuktaiḥ śaraiḥ kanakabhūṣaṇaiḥ
|
nirantaramivākāśaṃ sambabhūva janādhipa||43||

44na pāṇḍavānāṃ nāsmākaṃ tatra kiñcidvyadṛśyata
|
bāṇāndhakāre mahati kṛte tatra mahāhave||44||

45madrarājena balinā lāghavāccharavṛṣṭibhiḥ
|
cālyamānaṃ tu taṃ dṛṣṭvā pāṇḍavānāṃ balārṇavam||45||

46vismayaṃ paramaṃ jagmurdevagandharvadānavāḥ
|
sa tu tānsarvato yattāñśaraiḥ sañchādya māriṣa||46||

47dharmarājamavacchādya siṃhavadvyanadanmuhuḥ
|
te cchannāḥ samare tena pāṇḍavānāṃ mahārathāḥ||47||

48nāśaknuvaṃstadā yuddhe pratyudyātuṃ mahāratham
|
dharmarājapurogāstu bhīmasenamukhā rathāḥ
|
na jahuḥ samare śūraṃ śalyamāhavaśobhinam||48||
