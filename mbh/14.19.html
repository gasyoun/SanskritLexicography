Mahābhārata 14.19 (Āśvamedhikaparvan)

1brāhmaṇa uvāca|
yaḥ syādekāyane līnastūṣṇīṃ kiñcidacintayan
|
pūrvaṃ pūrvaṃ parityajya sa tīrṇo bandhanād bhavet||1||

2sarvamitraḥ sarvasahaḥ śame rakto jitendriyaḥ
|
vyapetabhayamanyuśca ātmavān mucyate naraḥ||2||

3ātmavatsarvabhūteṣu yaścareniyataḥ śuciḥ
|
amānī nirabhīmānaḥ sarvato mukta eva saḥ||3||

4jīvitaṃ maraṇaṃ cobhe sukhaduḥkhe tathaiva ca
|
lābhālābhe priyadveṣye yaḥ samaḥ sa ca mucyate||4||

5na kasyacit spṛhayate nāvajānāti kiñcana
|
nirdvandvo vītarāgātmā sarvathā mukta eva saḥ||5||

6anamitraśca nirbandhuranapatyaśca yaḥ kvacit
|
tyaktadharmārthakāmaśca nirākāṃkṣī ca mucyate||6||

7naiva dharmī na cādharmī pūrvopacitahāyakaḥ
|
dhātukṣayapraśāntātmā nirdvandvaḥ sa vimucyate||7||

8akarmavān vikāṃkṣaśca paśyejjagadaśāśvatam
|
aśvatthasadṛśaṃ nityaṃ janmamṛtyujarāyutam||8||

9vairāgyabuddhiḥ satatamātmadoṣavyapekṣakaḥ
|
ātmabandhavinirmokṣaṃ sa karotyacirādiva||9||

10agandhamarasasparśamaśabdamaparigraham
|
arūpamanabhijñeyaṃ dṛṣṭvā''tmānaṃ vimucyate||10||

11pañcabhūtaguṇairhīnamamūrtimadahetukam
|
aguṇaṃ guṇabhoktāraṃ yaḥ paśyati sa mucyate||11||

12vihāya sarvasaṅkalpān buddhyā śārīramānasān
|
śanairnirvāṇamāpnoti nirindhana ivānalaḥ||12||

13sarvasaṃskāranirmukto nirdvandvo niṣparigrahaḥ
|
tapasā indriyagrāmaṃ yaścarenmukta eva saḥ||13||

14vimuktaḥ sarvasaṃskāraistato brahma sanātanam
|
paramāpnoti saṃśāntamacalaṃ nityamakṣaram||14||

15ataḥ paraṃ pravakṣyāmi yogaśāstramanuttamam
|
yuñjantaḥ siddhamātmānaṃ yathā paśyanti yoginaḥ||15||

16tasyopadeśaṃ vakṣyāmi yathāvat tannivodha me
|
yairdvāraiścārayannityaṃ paśyatyātmānamātmani||16||

17indriyāṇi tu saṃhṛtya mana ātmani dhārayet
|
tīvraṃ taptvā tapaḥ pūrvaṃ mokṣayogaṃ samācaret||17||

18tapasvī satataṃ yukto yogaśāsramathācaret
|
manīṣī manasā vipraḥ paśyannātmānamātmani||18||

19ya cecchaknotyayaṃ sādhuryoktumātmānamātmani
|
tata ekāntaśīlaḥ sa paśyatyātmānamātmani||19||

20saṃyataḥ satataṃ yukta ātmavānvijitendriyaḥ
|
tathā ya ātmanā''tmānaṃ samprayuktaḥ prapaśyati||20||

21yathā puruṣaḥ svapne dṛṣṭvā paśyatyasāviti
|
tathā rūpamivātmānaṃ sādhuyuktaḥ prapaśyati||21||

22iṣīkāṃ ca yathā muñjātkaścinniṣkṛṣya darśayet
|
yogī niṣkṛṣya cātmānaṃ tathā paśyati dehataḥ||22||

23muñjaṃ śarīramityāhuriṣīkāmātmani śritām
|
etannidarśanaṃ proktaṃ yogavidbhiranuttamam||23||

24yadā hi yuktamātmānaṃ samyak paśyati dehabhṛt
|
na tasyeheśvaraḥ kaścittrailokyasyāpi yaḥ prabhuḥ||24||

25anyānyāścaiva tanavo yatheṣṭaṃ pratipadyate
|
vinivṛtya jarāṃ mṛtyuṃ na śocati na hṛṣyati||25||

26devānāmapi devatvaṃ yuktaḥ kārayate vaśī
|
brahma cāvyayamāpnoti hitvā dehamaśāśvatam||26||

27vinaśyatsu ca bhūteṣu na bhayaṃ tasya jāyate
|
kliśyamāneṣu bhūteṣu na sa kliśyati kenacit||27||

28duḥkhaśokamayairghoraiḥ saṅgasnehasamudbhavaiḥ
|
na vicālyati yuktātmā niḥspṛhaḥ śāntamānasaḥ||28||

29nainaṃ śastrāṇi vidhyante na mṛtyuścāsya vidyate
|
nātaḥ sukhataraṃ kiñcilloke kvacana dṛśyate||29||

30samyagyuktvā sa ātmānamātmanyeva pratiṣṭhate
|
vinivṛttajarāduḥkhaḥ sukhaṃ svapiti cāpi saḥ||30||

31dehānyatheṣṭamabhyeti hitvemāṃ mānuṣīṃ tanum
|
nirvedastu na kartavyo bhuñjānena kathañcana||31||

32samyagyukto yadā''tmānamātmanyeva prapaśyati
|
tadaiva na spṛhayate sākṣādapi śatakratoḥ||32||

33yogamekāntaśīlastu yathā vindati tacchṛṇu
|
dṛṣṭapūrvāṃ diśaṃ cintya yasminsannivasetpure||33||

34purasyābhyantare tasya manaḥ sthāpyaṃ na bāhyataḥ
|
purasyābhyantare tiṣṭhan yasminnāvasathe vaset
|
tasminnāvasathe dhāryaṃ sabāhyābhyantaraṃ manaḥ||34||

35pracintyāvasathe kṛtsnaṃ yasminkāle sa paśyati
|
tasminkāle manaścāsya na ca kiñcana bāhyataḥ||35||

36sanniyamyendriyagrāmaṃ nirghoṣaṃ nirjane vane
|
kāyamabhyantaraṃ kṛtsnamekāgraḥ paricintayet||36||

37dantāṃstālu ca jihvāṃ ca galaṃ grīvāṃ tathaiva ca
|
hṛdayaṃ cintayeccāpi tathā hṛdayabandhanam||37||

38ityuktaḥ sa mayā śiṣyo medhāvī madhusūdana
|
papraccha punarevemaṃ mokṣadharmaṃ sudurvacam||38||

39bhuktaṃ bhuktamidaṃ koṣṭhe kathamannaṃ vipacyate
|
kathaṃ rasatvaṃ vrajati śoṇitatvaṃ kathaṃ punaḥ||39||

40tathā māṃsaṃ ca medaśca snāyvasthīni ca yoṣiti
|
kathametāni sarvāṇi śarīrāṇi śarīriṇām||40||

41vardhante vardhamānasya vardhate ca kathaṃ balam
|
nirodhānāṃ nirgamanaṃ malānāṃ ca pṛthak pṛthak||41||

42kuto vāyaṃ praśvasiti ucchvasityapi vā punaḥ
|
kaṃ ca deśamadhiṣṭhāya tiṣṭhetyātmāyamātmani||42||

43jīvaḥ kathaṃ vahati ca ceṣṭamānaḥ kalevaram
|
kiṃ varṇaṃ kīdṛśaṃ caiva niveśayati vai punaḥ||43||

44yāthātathyena bhagavan vaktumarhasi me'nagha
|
iti samparipṛṣṭo'haṃ tena vipreṇa mādhava||44||

45pratyabruvaṃ mahābāho yathāśrutamarindama
|
yathā svakoṣṭhe prakṣipya bhāṇḍaṃ bhāṇḍamanā bhavet||45||

46tathā svakāye prakṣipya mano dvārairaniścalaiḥ
|
ātmānaṃ tatra mārgeta pramādaṃ parivarjayet||46||

47evaṃ satatamudyuktaḥ prītātmā na cirādiva
|
āsādayati tadbrahma yaddṛṣṭvā syātpradhānavit||47||

48na tvasau cakṣuṣā grāhyo na ca sarvairapīndriyaiḥ
|
manasaiva pradīpena mahānātmā pradṛśyate||48||

49sarvataḥ pāṇipādāntaḥ sarvato'kṣiśiromukhaḥ
|
sarvataḥ śrutimālloke sarvamāvṛtya tiṣṭhati||49||

50jīvo niṣkrāntamātmānaṃ śarīrātsamprapaśyati
|
sa tamutsṛjya dehe svaṃ dhārayan brahma kevalam||50||

51ātmānamālokayati manasā prahasanniva
|
tadevamāśrayaṃ kṛtvā mokṣa yāti tato mayi||51||

52idaṃ sarvarahasyaṃ te mayā proktaṃ dvijottama
|
āpṛcche sādhayiṣyāmi gaccha vipra yathāsukham||52||

53ityuktaḥ sa tadā kṛṣṇa mayā śiṣyo mahātapāḥ
|
agacchata yathākāmaṃ brāhmaṇaḥ saṃśitavrataḥ||53||

54vāsudeva uvāca|
ityuktvā sa tadā vākyaṃ māṃ pārtha dvijasattamaḥ
|
mokṣadharmāśritaḥ samyak tatraivāntaradhīyata||54||

55kaccidetattvayā pārtha śrutamekāgracetasā
|
tadāpi hi rathasthastvaṃ śrutavānetadeva hi||55||

56naitatpārtha suvijñeyaṃ vyāmiśreṇeti me matiḥ
|
nareṇākṛtasaṃjñena viśuddhenāntarātmanā||56||

57surahasyamidaṃ proktaṃ devānāṃ bharatarṣabha
|
kaccinnedaṃ śrutaṃ pārtha manuṣyeṇeha karhicit||57||

58na hyetacchrotumarho'nyo manuṣyastvāmṛte'nagha
|
naitadadya suvijñeyaṃ vyāmiśreṇāntarātmanā||58||

59kriyāvadbhirhi kaunteya devalokaḥ samāvṛtaḥ
|
na caitadiṣṭaṃ devānāṃ martyarūpanivartanam||59||

60parā hi sā gatiḥ pārtha yattadbrahma sanātanam
|
yatrāmṛtatvaṃ prāpnoti tyaktvā dehaṃ sadā sukhī||60||

61imaṃ dharmaṃ samāsthāya ye'pi syuḥ pāpayonayaḥ
|
striyo vaiśayāstathā śūdrāste'pi yānti parāṃ gatim||61||

62kiṃ punarbrāhmaṇāḥ pārtha kṣatriyā vā bahuśrutāḥ
|
svadharmaratayo nityaṃ brahmalokaparāyaṇāḥ||62||

63hetumaccaitaduddiṣṭamupāyāścāsya sādhane
|
siddhi phalaṃ ca mokṣaśca duḥkhasya ca vinirṇayaḥ||63||

64nātaḥ paraṃ sukhaṃ tvanyat kiñcitsyādbharatarṣabha
|
buddhimāñśraddadhānaśca parākrāntaśca pāṇḍava||64||

65yaḥ parityajyate martyo lokasāramasāravat
|
etairupāyaiḥ sa kṣipraṃ parāṃ gatimavāpnute||65||

66etāvadeva vaktavyaṃ nāto bhūyo'sti kiñcana
|
ṣaṇmāsānnityayuktasya yogaḥ pārtha pravartate||66||
