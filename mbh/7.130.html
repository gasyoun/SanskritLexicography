Mahābhārata 7.130 (Droṇaparvan)

1sañjaya uvāca|
tasminvilulite sainye saindhavāyārjune gate
|
sātvate bhīmasene ca putraste droṇamabhyayāt ||1||

2tvarannekarathenaiva bahukṛtyaṃ vicintayan
|
sa rathastava putrasya tvarayā parayā yutaḥ ||2||

3tūrṇamabhyadravaddroṇaṃ manomārutavegavān
|
uvāca cainaṃ putraste saṃraṃbhādraktalocanaḥ ||3||

4sasaṃbhramamidaṃ vākyamabravītkurunandanaḥ
|
arjuno bhīmasenaśca sātyakiścāparājitaḥ ||4||

5vijitya sarvasainyāni sumahānti mahārathāḥ
|
saṃprāptāḥ sindhurājasya samīpamanivāritāḥ ||5||

6vyāyacchanti ca tatrāpi sarva evāparājitāḥ
|
yadi tāvadraṇe pārtho vyatikrānto mahārathaḥ ||6||

7kathaṃ sātyakibhīmābhyāṃ vyatikrānto'si mānada
|
āścaryabhūtaṃ loke'smin samudrasyeva śoṣaṇam ||7||

8nirjayastava viprāgrya sātvatenārjunena ca
|
tathaiva bhīmasenena lokaḥ saṃvadate bhṛśam ||8||

9kathaṃ droṇo jitaḥ saṃkhye dhanurvedasya pāragaḥ
|
ityevaṃ bruvate yodhā aśraddheyamidaṃ tava ||9||

10nāśa eva tu me nūnaṃ mandabhāgyasya saṃyuge
|
yatra tvāṃ puruṣavyāghraṃ vyatikrāntāstrayo rathāḥ ||10||

11evaṃgate tu kṛtye'smin brūhi yatte vivakṣitam
|
yadgataṃ gatamevedaṃ śeṣaṃ cintaya mānada ||11||

12yatkṛtyaṃ sindhurājasya prāptakālamanantaram
|
tatsaṃvidhīyatāṃ kṣipraṃ sādhu sañcintya no dvija ||12||

13droṇa uvāca|
cintyaṃ bahuvidhaṃ tāta yatkṛtyaṃ tacchṛṇuṣva me
|
trayo hi samatikrāntāḥ pāṇḍavānāṃ mahārathāḥ ||13||

14yāvatteṣāṃ bhayaṃ paścāttāvadeṣāṃ puraḥsaram
|
tadgarīyastaraṃ manye yatra kṛṣṇadhanañjayau ||14||

15sā purastācca paścācca gṛhītā bhāratī camūḥ
|
tatra kṛtyamahaṃ manye saindhavasyābhirakṣaṇam ||15||

16sa no rakṣyatamastāta kruddhādbhīto dhanañjayāt
|
gatau ca saindhavaṃ bhīmau yuyudhānavṛkodarau ||16||

17saṃprāptaṃ tadidaṃ dyūtaṃ yattacchakunibuddhijam
|
na sabhāyāṃ jayo vṛtto nāpi tatra parājayaḥ||17||

18iha no glahamānānāmadya tāvañjayājayau
|
yānsma tānglahate ghorāñchakuniḥ kurusaṃsadi ||18||

19akṣān sa manyamānaḥ prāk śarāste hi durāsadāḥ
|
yatra te bahavastāta kauraveyā vyavasthitāḥ ||19||

20senāṃ durodaraṃ viddhi śarānakṣānviśāṃpate
|
glahaṃ ca saindhavaṃ rājaṃstatra dyūtasya niścayaḥ ||20||

21saindhave tu mahat dyūtaṃ samāsaktaṃ paraiḥ saha
|
atra sarve mahārāja tyaktyā jīvitamātmanaḥ ||21||

22saindhavasya raṇe rakṣāṃ vidhivatkartumarhatha
|
tatra no glahamānānāṃ dhuvau jayaparājayau ||22||

23yatra te parameṣvāsā yattā rakṣanti saindhavam
|
tatra gaccha svayaṃ śīghraṃ tāṃśca rakṣasva rakṣiṇaḥ ||23||

24ihaiva tvahamāsiṣye preṣayiṣyāmi cāparān
|
nirotsyāmi ca pañcālān sahitān pāṇḍusṛñjayaiḥ ||24||

25tato duryodhano'gacchattūrṇamācāryaśāsanāt
|
udyamyātmānamugrāya karmaṇe sapadānugaḥ ||25||

26cakrarakṣau tu pāñcālyau yudhāmanyūttamaujasau
|
bāhyena senāmabhyetya jagmatuḥ savyasācinam ||26||

27yau tu pūrvaṃ mahārāja vāritau kṛtavarmaṇā
|
praviṣṭe tvarjune rājaṃstava sainyaṃ yuyutsayā ||27||

28pārśve bhittvā camūṃ vīrau praviṣṭau tava vāhinīm
|
pārśvena sainyamāyāntau kururājo dadarśa ha ||28||

29tābhyāṃ duryodhanaḥ sārdhamakarotsaṃkhyamuttamam
|
tvaritastvaramāṇābhyāṃ bhrātṛbhyāṃ bhārato balī ||29||

30tāvenamabhyadravatāmubhāvudyatakārmukau
|
mahārathasamākhyātau kṣatriyapravarau yudhi ||30||

31tamavidhyadyadhāmanyustriṃśatā kaṅkapatribhiḥ
|
viṃśatyā sārathiṃ cāsya caturbhiścaturo hayān ||31||

32duryodhano yudhāmanyordhvajamekeṣuṇā'cchinat
|
ekena kārmukaṃ cāsya cakarta tanayastava ||32||

33sārathiṃ cāsya bhallena rathanīḍādapāharat
|
tato'vidhyaccharaistīkṣṇaiścaturbhiścaturo hayān ||33||

34yudhāmanyuśca saṃkruddhaḥ śarāṃstriṃśatamāhave
|
vyasṛjattava putrasya tvaramāṇaḥ stanāntare ||34||

35tathottamaujāḥ saṃkruddhaḥ śarairhemāvabhūṣitaiḥ
|
avidhyatsārathiṃ cāsya prāhiṇodyamasādanam ||35||

36duryodhano'pi rājendra pāñcālyasyottamaujasaḥ
|
jaghāna caturo'syāśvānubhau tau pārṣṇisārathī ||36||

37uttamaujā hatāśvastu hatasūtaśca saṃyuge
|
āruroha rathaṃ bhrāturyudhāmanyorabhitvaran ||37||

38sarathaṃ prāpya taṃ bhrāturduryodhanahayāñśaraiḥ
|
bahubhistāḍayāmāsa te hatāḥ prāpatanbhuvi ||38||

39hayeṣu patiteṣvasya ciccheda parameṣuṇā
|
yudhāmanyordhanuḥ śīghraṃ śarāvāpaṃ ca saṃyuge ||39||

40hatāśvasūtātsa rathādavatīrya narādhipaḥ
|
gadāmādāya te putraḥ pāñcālyāvabhyadhāvata ||40||

41tamāpatantaṃ saṃprekṣya kruddhaṃ kurupatiṃ tadā
|
avaplutau rathopasthādyudhāmanyūttamaujasau ||41||

42tataḥ sa hemacitraṃ taṃ gadayā syandanaṃ gadī
|
saṃkruddhaḥ pothayāmāsa sāśvasūtadhvajaṃ nṛpa ||42||

43bhaṃktvā rathaṃ sa putraste hatāśvo hatasārathiḥ
|
madrarājarathaṃ tūrṇamāruroha paraṃtapaḥ ||43||

44pañcālānāṃ tato mukhyau rājaputrau mahārathau
|
rathāvanyau samāruhya bībhatsumabhijagmatuḥ ||44||
