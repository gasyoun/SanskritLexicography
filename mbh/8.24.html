Mahābhārata 8.24 (Karṇaparvan)

1sañjaya uvāca|
nakulaṃ rabhasaṃ yuddhe drāvayantaṃ varūthinīm
|
karṇo vaikartano rājanvārayāmāsa vai ruṣā ||1||

2nakulastu tataḥ karṇe prahasannida‌mabravīt
|
cirasya bata dṛṣṭo'haṃ daivataiḥ saumyacakṣuṣā ||2||

3paśya māṃ tvaṃ raṇe pāpa cakṣurviṣayamāgatam
|
tvaṃ hi mūlamanarthānāṃ vairasya kalahasya ca ||3||

4tvaddoṣāt kuravaḥ kṣīṇāḥ samāsādya parasparam
|
tvāmadya samare hatvā kṛtakṛtyo'smi vijvaraḥ ||4||

5evamuktaḥ pratyuvāca nakulaṃ sūtanandanaḥ
|
sadṛśaṃ rājaputrasya dhanvinaśca viśeṣataḥ ||5||

6praharasva ca me vīra paśyāmastava pauruṣam
|
karma kṛtvā raṇe śūra tataḥ katthitumarhasi ||6||

7anuktvā samare tāta śūrā yudhyanti śaktitaḥ
|
prayudhyasva mayā śaktyā haniṣye darpameva te ||7||

8ityuktvā prāharattūrṇaṃ pāṇḍuputrāya sūtajaḥ
|
vivyādha cainaṃ samare trisaptatyā śilīmukhaiḥ ||8||

9nakulastu tato viddhaḥ sūtaputreṇa bhārata
|
aśītyāśīviṣaprakhyaiḥ sūtaputramavidhyata ||9||

10tasya karṇo dhanuśchittvā svarṇapuṅkhaiḥ śilāśitaiḥ
|
triṃśatā parameṣvāsaḥ śaraiḥ pāṇḍavamardayat ||10||

11te tasya kavacaṃ bhittvā papuḥ śoṇitamāhave
|
āśīviṣā yathā nāgā bhittvā gāṃ salilaṃ papuḥ ||11||

12athānyaddhanurādāya hemapṛṣṭhaṃ durāsadam
|
karṇaṃ vivyādha saptatyā sārathiṃ ca tribhiḥ śaraiḥ ||12||

13tataḥ kruddho mahārāja nakulaḥ paravīrahā
|
kṣurapreṇa sutīkṣṇena karṇasya dhanurācchinat ||13||

14athainaṃ chinnadhanvānaṃ sāyakānāṃ śataistribhiḥ
|
ājaghne prahasanvīraḥ sarvalokamahāratham ||14||

15karṇamabhyarditaṃ dṛṣṭvā pāṇḍuputreṇa māriṣa
|
vismayaṃ paramaṃ jagmū rathinaḥ saha daivataiḥ ||15||

16athānyaddhanurādāya karṇo vaikartanastadā
|
nakulaṃ pañcabhirbāṇairjatrudeśe samārpayat ||16||

17tatrasthairatha tairbāṇairmādrīputro vyarocayat
|
svaraśmibhirivādityo bhuvane visṛjanprabhām ||17||

18nakulastu tataḥ karṇaṃ viddhvā saptabhirāśugaiḥ
|
athāsya dhanuṣaḥ koṭiṃ punaściccheda māriṣa ||18||

19so'nyatkārmukamādāya samare vegavattaram
|
nakulasya tato bāṇaiḥ samantācchādayaddiśaḥ ||19||

20saṃchādyamānaḥ sahasā karṇacāpacyutaiḥ śaraiḥ
|
ciccheda sa śarāṃstūrṇaṃ śaraireva mahārathaḥ ||20||

21tato bāṇamayaṃ jālaṃ vitataṃ vyomni dṛśyate
|
khadyotānāmiva vrātaiḥ saṃpatadbhiryathā nabhaḥ ||21||

22tairvimuktaiḥ śaraśataiśchāditaṃ gaganaṃ tadā
|
śalabhānāṃ yathā vrātaistadvadāsīdviśāṃpate ||22||

23te śarā hemavikṛtāḥ sampatanto muhurmuhuḥ
|
śreṇīkṛtā vyakāśanta krauñcāḥ śreṇīkṛtā iva ||23||

24bāṇajālāvṛte vyomni chādite ca divākare
|
na sma saṃpatate bhūmyāṃ kiñcidapyantarikṣagam ||24||

25niruddhe tatra mārge ca śarasaṃghaiḥ samantataḥ
|
vyarocetāṃ mahātmānau kālasūryāvivoditau ||25||

26karṇacāpacyutairbāṇairvadhyamānāstu somakāḥ
|
avālīyanta rājendra vedanārtā bhṛśārditāḥ ||26||

27nakulasya tathā bāṇairhanyamānā camūstava
|
vyaśīryata diśo rājanvātanunnā ivāmbudāḥ||27||

28te sene hanyamāne tu tābhyāṃ divyairmahāśaraiḥ
|
śarapātamapākramya tasthatuḥ prekṣike tadā ||28||

29protsāritajane tasmin karṇapāṇḍavayoḥ śaraiḥ
|
avidhyetāṃ mahātmānāvanyonyaṃ śaravṛṣṭibhiḥ ||29||

30vidarśayantau divyāni śastrāṇi raṇamūrdhani
|
chādayantau ca sahasā parasparavadhaiṣiṇau ||30||

31nakulena śarā muktāḥ kaṅkabarhiṇavāsasaḥ
|
sūtaputramavacchādya vyatiṣṭhanta yathā'mbare ||31||

32tathaiva sūtaputreṇa preṣitāḥ paramāhave
|
pāṇḍuputramavacchādya vyatiṣṭhantāmbare śarāḥ ||32||

33śaraveśmapraviṣṭau tau dadṛśāte na kaiścana
|
sūryācandramasau rājañchādyamānau ghanairiva ||33||

34tataḥ kruddho raṇe karṇaḥ kṛtvā ghorataraṃ vapuḥ
|
pāṇḍavaṃ chādayāmāsa samantāccharavṛṣṭibhiḥ ||34||

35so'ticchanno mahārāja sūtaputreṇa pāṇḍavaḥ
|
na cakāra vyathāṃ rājan bhāskaro jaladairyathā ||35||

36tataḥ prahasyādhirathiḥ śarajālāni māriṣa
|
preṣayāmāsa samare śataśo'tha sahasraśaḥ ||36||

37ekacchāyamabhūtsarvaṃ tasya bāṇairmahātmanaḥ
|
abhracchāyeva sañjajñe saṃpatadbhiḥ śarottamaiḥ ||37||

38tataḥ karṇo mahārāja dhanuśchittvā mahātmanaḥ
|
sārathiṃ pātayāmāsa rathanīḍāddhasanniva ||38||

39tato'śvāṃścaturaścāsya caturbhirniśitaiḥ śaraiḥ
|
yamasya bhavanaṃ tūrṇaṃ preṣayāmāsa bhārata ||39||

40athāsya taṃ rathaṃ divyaṃ tilaśo vyadhamaccharaiḥ
|
patākāṃ cakrarakṣāṃśca gadāṃ khaḍgaṃ ca māriṣa ||40||

41śatacandraṃ ca taccarma sarvopakaraṇāni ca
|
hatāśvo virathaścaiva vivarmā ca viśāṃpate ||41||

42avatīrya rathāttūrṇaṃ parighaṃ gṛhya dhiṣṭhitaḥ
|
tamudyataṃ mahāghoraṃ parighaṃ tasya sūtajaḥ ||42||

43vyahanatsāyakai rājansutīkṣṇairbhārasādhanaiḥ
|
vyāyudhaṃ cainamālakṣya śaraiḥ sannataparvabhiḥ ||43||

44ārpayadbahubhiḥ karṇo na cainaṃ samapīḍayat
|
sa hanyamānaḥ samare kṛtāstreṇa balīyasā ||44||

45prādravatsahasā rājannakulo vyākulendriyaḥ
|
tamabhidrutya rādheyaḥ prahasanvai punaḥ punaḥ ||45||

46sajyamasya dhanuḥ kaṇṭhe vyavāsṛjata bhārata
|
tataḥ sa śuśubhe rājankaṇṭhāsaktamahādhanuḥ ||46||

47pariveṣamanuprāpto yathā syādvyomni candramāḥ
|
yathaiva cāsito meghaḥ śakracāpena śobhitaḥ ||47||

48tamabravīttataḥ karṇo vyarthaṃ vyāhṛtavānasi
|
vadedānīṃ punarhṛṣṭo vadhyamānaḥ punaḥ punaḥ ||48||

49mā yotsīḥ kurubhiḥ sārdhaṃ balavadbhiśca pāṇḍava
|
sadṛśaistāta yudhyasva vrīḍāṃ mā kuru pāṇḍava ||49||

50gṛhaṃ vā gaccha mādreya yatra vā kṛṣṇaphālgunau
|
evamuktvā mahārāja vyasarjayata taṃ tadā ||50||

51vadhaprāptaṃ tu taṃ śūro nāhanaddharmavittadā
|
smṛtvā kuntyā vaco rājaṃstata enaṃ vyasarjayat ||51||

52visṛṣṭaḥ pāṇḍavo rājan sūtaputreṇa dhanvinā
|
vrīḍanniva jagāmātha yudhiṣṭhirarathaṃ prati ||52||

53āruroha rathaṃ cāpi sūtaputrapratāpitaḥ
|
niḥśvasan duḥkhasantaptaḥ kuṃbhastha iva pannagaḥ ||53||

54taṃ vijityātha karṇo'pi pañcālāṃstvarito yayau
|
rathenātipatākena candravarṇahayena ca ||54||

55tatrākrando mahānāsītpāṇḍavānāṃ viśāṃpate
|
dṛṣṭvā senāpatiṃ yāntaṃ pañcālānāṃ rathavrajān ||55||

56tatrākaronmahārāja kadanaṃ sutanandanaḥ
|
madhyaṃ prāpte dinakare cakravadvicaranprabhuḥ ||56||

57bhagnacakrai rathaiḥ kaiścicchinnadhvajapatākibhiḥ
|
hatāśvairhatasutaiśca bhagnākṣaiścaiva māriṣa ||57||

58hriyamāṇānapaśyāma pañcālānāṃ rathavrajān
|
tatra tatra ca saṃbhrāntā viceruratha kuñjarāḥ ||58||

59dāvāgniparidagdhāṅgā yathaiva syurmahāvane
|
bhinnakumbhārdrarudhirāśchinnahastāśca vāraṇāḥ ||59||

60chinnagātrāvarāścaiva cchinnavāladhayo'pare
|
chinnābhrāṇīva saṃpeturhanyamānā mahātmanā ||60||

61apare trāsitā nāgā nārācaśaratomaraiḥ
|
tamevābhimukhaṃ jagmuḥ śalabhā iva pāvakam ||61||

62apare niṣṭanantaśca vyadṛśyanta mahādvipāḥ
|
kṣarantaḥ śoṇitaṃ gātrairnagā iva jalastravāḥ ||62||

63uraśchadairviyuktāṃśca vālabandhaiśca vājinaḥ
|
rājataiśca tathā kāṃsyaiḥ sauvarṇaiścaiva bhūṣaṇaiḥ ||63||

64hīnāṃścābharaṇaiścaiva khalīnaiśca vivarjitān
|
cāmaraiśca kuthābhiśca tūṇīraiḥ patitairapi ||64||

65nihataiḥ sādibhiścaiva śūrairāhavaśobhitaiḥ
|
apaśyāma raṇe tatra bhrāmyamāṇān hayottamān ||65||

66prāsaiḥ khaḍgaiśca rahitānṛṣṭibhiścāpi bhārata
|
hayasādīnapaśyāma kañcukoṣṇīṣadhāriṇaḥ ||66||

67nihatānvadhyamānāṃśca vepamānāṃśca bhārata
|
nāgāṅgāvayavairhīnāṃstatra tatraiva bhārata ||67||

68rathān hemapariṣkārānsaṃyuktāñjavanairhayaiḥ
|
bhrāmyamāṇānapaśyāma hateṣu rathiṣu drutam ||68||

69bhagnākṣakūbarānkāṃścidbhagnacakrāṃśca bhārata
|
vipatākadhvajāṃścānyāñchinneṣādaṇḍabandhurān ||69||

70vihatān rathinastatra dhāvamānāṃstatastataḥ
|
sūtaputraśaraistīkṣṇairhanyamānānviśāṃpate ||70||

71viśastrāṃśca tathaivānyān saśastrāṃśca hatānbahūn
|
tārakājālasaṃchannānvaraghaṇṭāviśobhitān ||71||

72nānāvarṇavicitrābhiḥ patākābhiralaṃkṛtān
|
vāraṇānanupaśyāma dhāvamānānsamantataḥ ||72||

73śirāṃsi bāhūnūrūṃśca cchinnānanyāṃstathaiva ca
|
karṇacāpacyutairbāṇairapaśyāma samantataḥ ||73||

74mahānvyatikaro raudro yodhānāmanvapadyata
|
karṇasāyakanunnānāṃ yudhyatāṃ ca śitaiḥ śaraiḥ ||74||

75te vadhyamānāḥ samare sūtaputreṇa sṛñjayāḥ
|
tamevābhimukhaṃ yānti pataṅgā iva pāvakam ||75||

76taṃ dahantamanīkāni tatra tatra mahāratham
|
kṣatriyā varjayāmāsuryugāntāgnimivolbaṇam ||76||

77hataśeṣāstu ye vīrāḥ pañcālānāṃ mahārathāḥ
|
tānprabhagnān drutānvīraḥ pṛṣṭhato vikirañcharaiḥ ||77||

78abhyadhāvata tejasvī viśīrṇakavacadhvajān
|
tāpayāmāsa tānbāṇaiḥ sūtaputro mahābalaḥ
|
madhyaṃdinamanuprāpto bhūtānīva tamonudaḥ ||78||
