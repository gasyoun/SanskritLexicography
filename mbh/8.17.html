Mahābhārata 8.17 (Karṇaparvan)

1sañjaya uvāca|
tataḥ samabhavadyuddhaṃ śukrāṅgirasavarcasoḥ
|
nakṣatramabhito vyomni śukrāṅgirasayoriva ||1||

2santāpayantāvanyonyaṃ dīptaiḥ śaragabhastibhiḥ
|
lokatrāsakarāvāstāṃ vimārgasthau grahāviva ||2||

3tato'vidhyadbhruvormadhye nārācenārjuno bhṛśam
|
sa tena vibabhau drauṇirūrdhvaraśmiryathā raviḥ ||3||

4atha kṛṣṇau śaraśatairaśvatthāmnā'rditau bhṛśam
|
svaraśmijālavikacau yugāntārkāvivāsatuḥ ||4||

5tato'rjunaḥ sarvatodhāramastramavāsṛjadvāsudeve'bhibhūte
|
drauṇāyaniṃ cābhyahanat pṛṣatkairvajrāgnivaivasvatadaṇḍakalpaiḥ ||5||

6sa keśavaṃ cārjunaṃ cātitejā vivyādha marmasvatiraudrakarmā
|
bāṇaiḥ suyuktairatitīvravegairyairāhato mṛtyurapi vyatheta ||6||

7drauṇeriṣūnarjunaḥ sannivārya vyāyacchatastaddviguṇaiḥ supuṅkhaiḥ
|
taṃ sāśvasūtadhvajamekavīramāvṛtya saṃśaptakasainyamārcchat ||7||

8dhanūṃṣi bāṇāniṣudhīrdhanurjyāḥ pāṇīn bhujān pāṇigataṃ ca śastram
|
chatrāṇi ketūṃsturagān ratheṣāṃ vastrāṇi mālyānyatha bhūṣaṇāni ||8||

9carmāṇi varmāṇi manoramāṇi priyāṇi sarvāṇi śirāṃsi caiva
|
ciccheda pārtho dviṣatāṃ suyuktairbāṇaiḥ sthitānāmaparāṅmukhānām ||9||

10sukalpitāḥ syandanavājināgāḥ samāsthitā yatnakṛtairnṛvīraiḥ
|
pārtheritairbāṇaśatairnirastāstaireva sārdhaṃ nṛvarā nipetuḥ ||10||

11padmārkapūrṇendunibhānanāni kirīṭamālyābharaṇojjvalāni
|
bhallārdhacandrakṣurakartitāni prapetururvyāṃ nṛśirāṃsyajasram ||11||

12atha dvipairdevaripudvipābhairdevāridarpāpahamatyugram
|
kaliṅgavaṅgāṅganiṣādavīrā jighāṃsavaḥ pāṇḍavamabhyadhāvan ||12||

13teṣāṃ dvipānāṃ nicakarta pārtho varmāṇi carmāṇi karānniyantṝn
|
dhvajānpatākāśca tataḥ prapeturvajrāhatānīva gireḥ śirāṃsi ||13||

14teṣu prabhagneṣu gurostanūjaṃ bāṇaiḥ kirīṭī navasūryavarṇaiḥ
|
pracchādayāmāsa mahābhrajālairvāyuḥ samudyantamivāṃśumantam ||14||

15tato'rjuneṣūniṣubhirnirasya drauṇiḥ śitairarjunavāsudevau
|
pracchādayitvā divi candrasūryau nanāda sombhoda ivātapānte ||15||

16tamarjunastāṃśca punastvadīyānabhyarditastairabhisṛtya śastraiḥ
|
bāṇāndhakāraṃ sahasaiva kṛtvā vivyādha sarvāniṣubhiḥ supuṃkhaiḥ ||16||

17nāpyādadatsaṃdadhannaiva muñcan bāṇānrathe'dṛśyata savyasācī
|
rathāṃśca nāgāṃsturagānpadātīn saṃsyūtadehāndadṛśurhatāṃśca ||17||

18saṃdhāya nārācavarāndaśāśu drauṇistvarannekamivotsasarja
|
teṣāṃ ca pañcārjunamabhyavidhyan pañcācyutaṃ nirbibhiduḥ supuṃkhāḥ ||18||

19tairāhatau sarvamanuṣyamukhyāvasṛk sravantau dhanadendrakalpau
|
samāptavidyena tathābhibhūtau hatau raṇe tāviti menire'nye ||19||

20athārjunaṃ prāha daśārhanāthaḥ pramādyase kiṃ jahi yodhametam
|
kuryāddhi doṣaṃ samupekṣito'yaṃ kaṣṭo bhavedvyādhirivākriyāvān ||20||

21tatheti coktvā'cyutamapramādī drauṇiṃ prayatnādiṣubhistatakṣa
|
bhujau varau candanasāradigdhau vakṣaḥ śiro'thāpratimau tathorū ||21||

22gāṇḍīvamuktaiḥ kupito'vikarṇaidrauṇiṃ śaraiḥ saṃyati nirbibheda
|
chittvā tu raśmīṃsturagānavidhyatte taṃ raṇādūhuratīva dūram ||22||

23sa tairhṛto vātajavaisturaṅgairdrauṇirdṛḍhaṃ pārthaśarābhibhūtaḥ
|
iyeṣa nāvṛtya punastu yoddhuṃ pārthena sārdhaṃ matimānvimṛśya
|
jānañjayaṃ nitataṃ vṛṣṇivīre dhanañjaye cāṅgirasāṃ variṣṭhaḥ ||23||

24niyamya sa hayān drauṇiḥ samāśvāsya ca māriṣa
|
rathāśvanarasaṃbādhaṃ karṇasya prāviśadbalam ||24||

25pratīpakāriṇi raṇādaśvatthāmni hate hayaiḥ
|
mantrauṣadhikriyāyogairvyādhau dehādivāhṛte ||25||

26saṃśaptakānabhimukhau prayātau keśavārjunau
|
vātoddhūtapatākena syandanenaughanādinā ||26||
