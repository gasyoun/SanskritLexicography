Mahābhārata 5.101 (Udyogaparvan)

1nārada uvāca|
ayaṃ lokaḥ suparṇānāṃ pakṣiṇāṃ pannagāśinām
|
vikrame gamane bhāre naiṣāmasti pariśramaḥ ||1||

2vaitaneyasutaiḥ sūta ṣaḍbhistatamidaṃ kulam
|
sumukhena sunāmnā ca sunetreṇa suvarcasā ||2||

3surucā pakṣirājena subalena ca mātale
|
vardhitāni prasṛtyā vai vinatākulakartṛbhiḥ ||3||

4pakṣirājābhijātyānāṃ sahasrāṇi śatāni ca
|
kaśyapasya tato vaṃśe jātairbhūtivivardhanaiḥ ||4||

5sarve hyete śriyā yuktāḥ sarve śrīvatsalakṣaṇāḥ
|
sarve śriyamabhīpsanto dhārayanti balānyuta ||5||

6karmaṇā kṣatriyāścaite nirghṛṇā bhogibhojinaḥ
|
jñātisaṃkṣayakartṛtvādbrāhmaṇyaṃ na labhanti vai ||6||

7nāmāni caiṣā vakṣyāmi yathā prādhānyataḥ śṛṇu
|
mātale ślāghyametaddhi kulaṃ viṣṇuparigraham ||7||

8daivataṃ viṣṇureteṣāṃ viṣṇureva parāyaṇam
|
hṛdi caiṣāṃ sadā viṣṇurviṣṇureva sadā gatiḥ ||8||

9suvarṇacūḍo nāgāśī dāruṇaścaṇḍatuṇḍakaḥ
|
anilaścānalaścaiva viśālākṣo'tha kuṇḍalī ||9||

10paṅkajidvajraviṣkambho vainateyo'tha vāmanaḥ
|
vātavego diśācakṣurnimeṣo'nimiṣastathā ||10||

11trirāvaḥ saptarāvaśca vālmīkirdvīpakastathā
|
daityadvīpaḥ sariddvīpaḥ sārasaḥ padmaketanaḥ ||11||

12sumukhaścitraketuśca citrabarhastathā'naghaḥ
|
meṣahṛt kumudo dakṣaḥ sarpāntaḥ sahabhojanaḥ ||12||

13gurubhāraḥ kapotaśca sūryanetraścirāntakaḥ
|
viṣṇudharmā kumāraśca paribarho haristathā ||13||

14susvaro madhuparkaśca hemavarṇastathaiva ca
|
mālayo mātariśvā ca niśākaradivākarau ||14||

15ete pradeśamātreṇa mayoktā garuḍātmajāḥ
|
prādhānyataste yaśasā kīrtitāḥ prāṇinaśca ye ||15||

16yadyatra na ruciḥ kācidehi gacchāva mātale
|
taṃ nayiṣyāmi deśaṃ tvāṃ varaṃ yatropalapsyase ||16||
