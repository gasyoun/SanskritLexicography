Mahābhārata 8.28 (Karṇaparvan)

1sañjaya uvāca|
yudhiṣṭhiraṃ mahārāja visṛjantaṃ śarān bahūn
|
svayaṃ duryodhano rājā pratyagṛhṇā‌dabhītavat ||1||

2tamāpatantaṃ sahasā tava putraṃ mahāratham
|
dharmarājo drutaṃ viddhvā tiṣṭhatiṣṭheti cābravīt ||2||

3sa tu taṃ prativivyādha navabhirniśitaiḥ śaraiḥ
|
sārathiṃ cāsya bhallena bhṛśaṃ kruddho'bhyatāḍayat ||3||

4tato yudhiṣṭhiro rājan svarṇapuṅkhāñśilīmukhān
|
duryodhanāya cikṣepa trayodaśa śilāśitān ||4||

5caturbhiścaturo vāhāṃstasya hatvā mahārathaḥ
|
pañcamena śiraḥ kāyatsāratheśca samākṣipat ||5||

6ṣaṣṭhena tu dhvajaṃ rājñaḥ saptamena tu kārmukam
|
aṣṭamena tathā khaḍgaṃ pātayāmāsa bhūtale ||6||

7pañcabhirnṛpatiṃ cāpi dharmarājordayadbhṛśam
|
hatāśvāttu rathāttasmādavaplutya sutastava ||7||

8uttamaṃ vyasanaṃ prāpto bhūmāvevāvatiṣṭhata
|
taṃ tu kṛcchragataṃ dṛṣṭvā karṇadrauṇikṛpādayaḥ ||8||

9abhyavartanta sahasā parīpsanto narādhipam
|
atha pāṇḍusutāḥ sarve parivārya yudhiṣṭhiram ||9||

10anvayuḥ samare rājaṃstato yuddhamavartata
|
tatastūryasahasrāṇi prāvādyanta mahāmṛdhe ||10||

11tataḥ kilakilāśabdāḥ prādurāsanmahīpate
|
yatrābhyagacchan samare pañcālāḥ kauravaiḥ saha ||11||

12narā naraiḥ samājagmurvāraṇā varavāraṇaiḥ
|
rathāśca rathibhiḥ sārdhaṃ hayāśca hayasādibhiḥ ||12||

13dvaṃdvānyāsanmahārāja prekṣaṇīyāni saṃyuge
|
vividhānyapyacintyāni śastravantyuttamāni ca ||13||

14te śūrāḥ samare sarve citraṃ laghu ca suṣṭhu ca
|
ayudhyanta mahāvegāḥ parasparavadhaiṣiṇaḥ ||14||

15anyonyaṃ samare jaghnuryodhavratamanuṣṭhitāḥ
|
na hi te samaraṃ cakruḥ pṛṣṭhato vai kathañcana ||15||

16muhūrtameva tadyuddhamāsīnmadhuradarśanam
|
tata unmattavadrājannirmaryādamavartata ||16||

17rathī nāgaṃ samāsādya dārayanniśitaiḥ śaraiḥ
|
preṣayāmāsa kālāya śaraiḥ sannataparvabhiḥ ||17||

18nāgā hayānsamāsādya vikṣipanto bahūn raṇe
|
dārayāmāsuratyugraṃ tatra tatra tadā tadā ||18||

19hayārohāśca bahavaḥ parivārya hayottamān
|
talaśabdaravāṃścakruḥ saṃpatantastatastataḥ ||19||

20dhāvamānāṃstatastāṃstu dravamāṇānmahāgajān
|
pārśvataḥ pṛṣṭhataścaiva nijaghnurhayasādinaḥ ||20||

21vidrāvya ca bahūnaśvānnāgā rājanmadotkaṭāḥ
|
viṣāṇaiścāpare jaghnurmamṛduścāpare bhṛśam ||21||

22sāśvārohāṃśca turagān viṣāṇairvivyadhū ruṣā
|
apare cikṣipurvegāt pragṛhyātibalāstadā ||22||

23pādātairāhatā nāgā vivareṣu samantataḥ
|
cakrurārtasvaraṃ ghoraṃ dudruvuśca diśo daśa ||23||

24padātīnāṃ tu sahasā pradrutānāṃ mahāhave
|
utsṛjyābharaṇaṃ tūrṇamavavabrū raṇājire ||24||

25nimittaṃ manyamānāstu pariṇāmya mahāgajāḥ
|
jagṛhurbibhiduścaiva citrāṇyābharaṇāni ca ||25||

26tāṃstu tatra prasaktānvai parivārya padātayaḥ
|
hastyārohānnijaghnuste mahāvegā balotkaṭāḥ ||26||

27apare hastibhirhastaiḥ khaṃ vikṣiptā mahāhave
|
nipatanto viṣāṇāgrairbhṛśaṃ viddhāḥ suśikṣitaiḥ ||27||

28apare sahasā gṛhya viṣāṇaireva sūditāḥ
|
senāntaraṃ samāsādya kecittatra mahāgajaiḥ ||28||

29kṣuṇṇagātrā mahārāja vikṣipya ca punaḥ punaḥ
|
apare vyajanānīva vibhrāmya nihatā mṛdhe ||29||

30puraḥsarāśca nāgānāmapareṣāṃ viśāṃpate
|
śarīrāṇyatividdhāni tatra tatra raṇājire ||30||

31pratimāneṣu kumbheṣu dantaveṣṭeṣu cāpare
|
nigṛhītā bhṛśaṃ nāgāḥ prāsatomaraśaktibhiḥ ||31||

32nigṛhya ca gajāḥ kecit pārśvasthairbhṛśadāruṇaiḥ
|
rathāśvasādibhistatra saṃbhinnā nyapatanbhuvi ||32||

33sahayāḥ sādinastatra tomareṇa mahāmṛdhe
|
bhūmāvamṛdnanvegena sacarmāṇaṃ padātinam ||33||

34tathā sāvaraṇānkāṃścittatra tatra viśāṃpate
|
rathānnāgāḥ samāsādya parigṛhya ca māriṣa ||34||

35vyākṣipansahasā tatra ghorarūpe bhayānake
|
nārācairnihatāścāpi gajāḥ peturmahābalāḥ ||35||

36parvatasyeva śikharaṃ vajrarugṇaṃ mahītale
|
yodhā yodhānsamāsādya muṣṭibhirvyahananyudhi ||36||

37keśeṣvanyonyamākṣipya cikṣipurvibhiduśca ha
|
udyamya ca bhujānanye nikṣipya ca mahītale ||37||

38padā coraḥ samākramya sphurato'pāharacchiraḥ
|
patataścāparo rājanvijahārāsinā śiraḥ ||38||

39jīvataśca tathaivā'nyaḥ śastraṃ kāye nyamajjayat
|
muṣṭiyuddhaṃ mahaccāsīdyodhānāṃ tatra bhārata ||39||

40tathā keśagrahaścogro bāhuyuddhaṃ ca bhairavam
|
samāsaktasya cānyena avijñātastathā'paraḥ ||40||

41jahāra samare prāṇānnānāśastrairanekadhā
|
saṃsakteṣu ca yodheṣu vartamāne ca saṃkule ||41||

42kabandhānyutthitāni syuḥ śataśo'tha sahasraśaḥ
|
śoṇitaiḥ sicyamānāni śastrāṇi kavacāni ca ||42||

43mahārāgānuraktāni vastrāṇīva cakāśire
|
evametanmahadyuddhaṃ dāruṇaṃ śastrasaṃkulam ||43||

44unmattagaṅgāpratimaṃ śabdenāpūrayajjagat
|
naiva sve na pare rājan vijñāyante śarāturāḥ ||44||

45yoddhavyamiti yudhyante rājāno jayagṛddhinaḥ
|
svān sve jaghnurmahārāja parāṃścaiva samāgatān ||45||

46ubhayoḥ senayorvīrairvyākulaṃ samapadyata
|
rathairbhagnairmahārāja vāraṇaiśca nipātitaiḥ ||46||

47hayaiśca patitaistatra naraiśca vinipātitaiḥ
|
agamyarūpā pṛthivī kṣaṇena samapadyata ||47||

48kṣaṇenāsīnmahīpāla kṣatajaughapravartinī
|
pañcālānahanatkarṇastrigartāṃśca dhanañjayaḥ ||48||

49bhīmasenaḥ kurūn rājan hastyanīkaṃ ca sarvaśaḥ
|
evameṣa kṣayo vṛttaḥ kurupāṇḍavasenayoḥ
|
aparāhṇe gate sūrye kāṅkṣatāṃ vipulaṃ yaśaḥ ||49||
