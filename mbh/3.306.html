Mahābhārata 3.306 (Āraṇyakaparvan)

1vaiśampāyana uvāca|
gate tasmin dvijaśreṣṭhe kasmiṃścitkāraṇāntare
|
cintayāmāsa sā kanyā mantragrāmabalābalam||1||

2ayaṃ vai kīdṛśastena mama datto mahātmanā
|
mantragrāmo balaṃ tasya jñāsye nāticirāditi||2||

3evaṃ sañcintayantī sā dadarśartuṃ yadṛcchayā
|
vrīḍitā sā'bhavadbālā kanyābhāve rajasvalā||3||

4tato harmyatalasthā sā mahārhaśayanocitā
|
prācyāṃ diśi samudyantaṃ dadarśādityamaṇḍalam||4||

5tatra baddhamanodṛṣṭirabhavat sā sumadhyamā
|
na cātapyata rūpeṇa bhānoḥ sandhyāgatasya sā||5||

6tasyā dṛṣṭirabhūddivyā sā'paśyaddivyadarśanam
|
āmuktakavacaṃ devaṃ kuṇḍalābhyāṃ vibhūṣitam||6||

7tasyāḥ kautūhalaṃ tvāsīnmantraṃ prati narādhipa
|
āhvānamakarot sā'tha tasya devasya bhāvinī||7||

8prāṇānupaspṛśya tadā hyājuhāva divākaram
|
ājagāma tato rājaṃstvaramāṇo divākaraḥ||8||

9madhupiṅgo mahābāhuḥ kambugrīvo hasanniva
|
aṅgadī baddhamukuṭo diśaḥ prajvālayanniva||9||

10yogāt kṛtvā dvidhā''tmānamājagāma tatāpa ca
|
ābabhāṣe tataḥ kuntīṃ sāmnā paramavalgunā||10||

11āgato'smi vaśaṃ bhadre tava mantrabalātkṛtaḥ
|
kiṃ karomi vaśo rājñi brūhi kartā tadasmi te||11||

12kuntyuvāca|
gamyatāṃ bhagavaṃstatra yata evāgato hyasi
|
kautūhalāt samāhūtaḥ prasīda bhagavanniti||12||

13sūrya uvāca|
gamiṣye'haṃ yathā māṃ tvaṃ bravīṣi tanumadhyame
|
na tu devaṃ samāhūya nyāyyaṃ preṣayituṃ vṛthā||13||

14tavābhisandhiḥ subhage sūryāt putro bhavediti
|
vīryeṇāpratimo loke kavacī kuṇḍalīti ca||14||

15sā tvamātmapradānaṃ vai kuruṣva gajagāmini
|
utpatsyati hi putraste yathāsaṅkalpamaṅgane||15||

16atha gacchāmyahaṃ bhadre tvayā saṅgamya susmite
|
yadi tvaṃ vacanaṃ nādya kariṣyasi mama priyam||16||

17śapiṣye tvāmahaṃ kruddho brāhmaṇaṃ pitaraṃ ca te
|
tvatkṛte tān pradhakṣyāmi sarvānapi na saṃśayaḥ||17||

18pitaraṃ caitra te mūḍhaṃ yo na vetti tavānayam
|
tasya ca brāhmaṇasyādya yo'sau mantramadāttava||18||

19śīlavṛttamavijñāya dhāsyāmi vinayaṃ param
|
ete hi vibudhāḥ sarve purandaramukhā divi||19||

20tvayā pralabdhaṃ paśyanti smayanta iva bhāvini
|
paśya cainān suragaṇān divyaṃ cakṣuridaṃ hi te
|
pūrvameva mayā dattaṃ dṛṣṭavatyasi yena mām||20||

21vaiśampāyana uvāca|
tato'paśyat tridaśān rājaputrī sarvāneva sveṣu dhiṣṇyeṣu svasthān
|
prabhāvantaṃ bhānumantaṃ mahāntaṃ yathā''dityaṃ rocamānāṃstathaiva ca||21||

22sā tān dṛṣṭvā vrīḍamāneva bālā sūryaṃ devī vacanaṃ prāha bhītā
|
gaccha tvaṃ vai gopate svaṃ vimānaṃ kanyābhāvādduḥkha evāpacāraḥ||22||

23pitā mātā guravaścaiva ye'nye dehasyāsya prabhavanti pradāne
|
nāhaṃ dharmaṃ lopayiṣyāmi loke strīṇāṃ vṛttaṃ pūjyate deharakṣā||23||

24mayā mantrabalaṃ jñātumāhūtastvaṃ vibhāvaso
|
bālyādvāleti tatkṛtvā kṣantumarhasi me vibho||24||

25sūrya uvāca|
bāleti kṛtvā'nunayaṃ tavāhaṃ dadāni nānyānunayaṃ labheta
|
ātmapradānaṃ kuru kunti kanye śāṃtistavaivaṃ hi bhavecca bhīru||25||

26na cāpi gantuṃ yuktaṃ hi mayā mithyākṛtena vai
|
asametya tvayā bhīru mantrāhūtena bhāvini||26||

27gamiṣyāmyanavadyāṅgi loke samavahārayatām
|
sarveṣāṃ vibudhānāṃ ca vaktavyaḥ syāṃ tathā śubhe||27||

28sā tvaṃ mayā samāgaccha putraṃ lapsyasi mādṛśam
|
viśiṣṭā sarvalokeṣu bhaviṣyasi na saṃśayaḥ||28||
