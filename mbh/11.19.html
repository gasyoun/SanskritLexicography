Mahābhārata 11.19 (Strīparvan)

1gāndhāryuvācaeṣa mādhava putro me vikarṇaḥ pājñasammataḥ
|
bhūmau vinihataḥ śete bhīmena śatadhā kṛtaḥ||1||

2gajamadhye hataḥ śete vikarṇo madhusūdana
|
nīlameghaparikṣiptaḥ śaradīva niśākaraḥ||2||

3asya cāpagraheṇaiva pāṇiḥ kṛtakiṇo mahān
|
kathañcicchidyate gṛdhrairattukāmaistalatravān||3||

4asya bhāryā''miṣaprepsūngṛdhrakākāṃstapasvinī
|
vārayatyaniśaṃ bālā na ca śaknoti mādhava||4||

5yuvā vṛndārakaḥ śūro vikarṇaḥ puruṣarṣabha
|
sukhoṣitaḥ sukhārhaśca śete pāṃsuṣu mādhava||5||

6karṇinālīkanārācairbhinnamarmāṇamāhave
|
adyāpi na jahātyenaṃ lakṣmīrbharatasattamam||6||

7eṣa saṅgrāmaśūraṇe pratijñāṃ pālayiṣyatā
|
durmukho'bhimukhaḥ śete hato'rigaṇahā raṇe||7||

8tasyaitadvadanaṃ kṛṣṇa śvāpadairardhabhakṣitam
|
vibhātyabhyadhikaṃ tāta saptamyāmiva candramāḥ||8||

9śūrasya hi raṇe kṛṣṇa paśyānanamathedṛśam
|
sa kathaṃ nihato'mitraiḥ pāṃsūngrasati me sutaḥ||9||

10yasyāhavamukhe saumya sthātā naivopapadyate
|
sa kathaṃ durmukho'mitrairhato vibudhalokajit||10||

11citrasenaṃ hataṃ bhūmau śayānaṃ madhusūdana
|
dhārtarāṣṭramimaṃ paśya pratimānaṃ dhanuṣmatām||11||

12taṃ citramālyābharaṇaṃ yuvatyaḥ śokakarśitāḥ
|
kravyādasaṅghaiḥ sahitā rudantyaḥ paryupāsate||12||

13strīṇāṃ ruditanirghoṣaḥ śvāpadānāṃ ca garjitam
|
citrarūpamidaṃ kṛṣṇa vicitraṃ pratibhāti me||13||

14yuvā vṛndārako nityaṃ pravarastrīniṣevitaḥ
|
viviṃśatirasau śete dhvastaḥ pāṃsuṣu mādhava||14||

15śarasaṅkṛttavarmāṇaṃ vīraṃ viśasane hatam
|
parivāryāsate gṛdhrāḥ paśya kṛṣṇa viviṃśatim||15||

16praviśya samare śūraḥ pāṇḍavānāmanīkinīm
|
sa vīraśayane śete paraḥ satpuruṣocite||16||

17smitopapannaṃ sunasaṃ subhru tārādhipopamam
|
atīva śubhraṃ vadanaṃ kṛṣṇa paśya viviṃśateḥ||17||

18enaṃ hi paryupāsante bahudhā varayoṣitaḥ
|
krīḍantamiva gandharvaṃ devakanyāḥ sahasraśaḥ||18||

19hantāraṃ parasainyānāṃ śūraṃ samitiśobhanam
|
nibarhaṇamamitrāṇāṃ duḥsahaṃ viṣaheta kaḥ||19||

20duḥsahasyaitadābhāti śarīraṃ saṃvṛtaṃ śaraiḥ
|
girirātmaruhaiḥ phullaiḥ karṇikārairivācitaḥ||20||

21śātakaumbhyā srajā bhāti kavacena ca bhāsvatā
|
agnineva giriḥ śveto gatāsurapi duḥsahaḥ||21||
