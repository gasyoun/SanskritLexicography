Mahābhārata 1.214 (Ādiparvan)

1vaiśaṃpāyana uvāca|
taṃ prayāntaṃ mahābāhuṃ kauravāṇāṃ yaśaskaram
|
anujagmurmahātmāno brāhmaṇā vedapāragāḥ||1||

2vedavedāṅgavidvāsastathaivādhyātmacintakāḥ
|
bhaikṣāśca bhagavadbhaktāḥ sūtāḥ paurāṇikāśca ye||2||

3kathakāścāpare rājañśramaṇāśca vanaukasaḥ
|
divyākhyānāni ye cāpi paṭhanti madhuraṃ dvijāḥ||3||

4etaiścānyaiśca bahubhiḥ sahāyaiḥ pāṇḍunandanaḥ
|
vṛtaḥ ślakṣṇakathaiḥ prāyānmarudbhiriva vāsavaḥ||4||

5ramaṇīyāni citrāṇi vanāni ca sarāṃsi ca
|
saritaḥ sāgarāṃścaiva deśānapi ca bhārata||5||

6puṇyānyapi ca tīrthāni dadarśa bharatarṣabhaḥ
|
sa gaṅgādvāramāśritya niveśamakarotprabhuḥ||6||

7tatra tasyādbhutaṃ karma śṛṇu tvaṃ janamejaya
|
kṛtavānyadviśuddhātmā pāṇḍūnāṃ pravaro hi saḥ||7||

8niviṣṭe tatra kaunteye brāhmaṇeṣu ca bhārata
|
agnihotrāṇi viprāste prāduścakruranekaśaḥ||8||

9teṣu prabodhyamāneṣu jvaliteṣu huteṣu ca
|
kṛtapuṣpopahāreṣu tīrāntaragateṣu ca||9||

10kṛtābhiṣekairvidvadbhirniyataiḥ satpathi sthitaiḥ
|
śuśubhe'tīva tadrājangaṅgādvāraṃ mahātmabhiḥ||10||

11tathā paryākule tasminniveśe pāṇḍavarṣabhaḥ
|
abhiṣekāya kaunteyo gaṅgāmavatatāra ha||11||

12tatrābhiṣekaṃ kṛtvā sa tarpayitvā pitāmahān
|
uttitīrṣurjalādrājannagnikāryacikīrṣayā||12||

13apakṛṣṭo mahābāhurnāgarājasya kanyayā
|
antarjale mahārāja ulūpyā kāmayānayā||13||

14dadarśa pāṇḍavastatra pāvakaṃ susamāhitaḥ
|
kauravyasyātha nāgasya bhavane paramārcite||14||

15tatrāgnikāryaṃ kṛtavānkuntīputro dhanañjayaḥ
|
aśaṅkamānena hutastenātuṣyaddhutāśanaḥ||15||

16agnikāryaṃ sa kṛtvā tu nāgarājasutāṃ tadā
|
prahasanniva kaunteya idaṃ vacanamabravīt||16||

17kimidaṃ sāhasaṃ bhīru kṛtavatyasi bhāmini
|
kaścāyaṃ subhage deśaḥ kā ca tvaṃ kasya vātmajā||17||

18ulūpyuvāca
|
airāvatakule jātaḥ kauravyo nāma pannagaḥ
|
tasyāsmi duhitā rājannulūpī nāma pannagī||18||

19sā'haṃ tvāmabhiṣekārthamavatīrṇaṃ samudragām
|
dṛṣṭvaiva puruṣavyāghra kandarpeṇābhimūrcchitā||19||

20tāṃ māmanaṅgaglapitāṃ tvatkṛte kurunandana
|
ananyāṃ nandayasvādya pradānenātmano'nagha||20||

21arjuna uvāca|
brahmacaryamidaṃ bhadre mama dvādaśamāsikam
|
dharmarājena cādiṣṭaṃ nāhamasmi svayaṃ vaśaḥ||21||

22tava cāpi priyaṃ kartumicchāmi jalacāriṇi
|
anṛtaṃ noktapūrvaṃ ca mayā kiṃcana karhicit||22||

23kathaṃ ca nānṛtaṃ me syāttava cāpi priyaṃ bhavet
|
na ca pīḍyeta me dharmastathā kuryā bhujaṅgame||23||

24ulūpyuvāca
|
jānāmyahaṃ pāṇḍaveya yathā carasi medinīm
|
yathā ca te brahmacaryamidamādiṣṭavānguruḥ||24||

25parasparaṃ vartamānāṃ drupadasyātmajāṃ prati
|
yo no'nupraviśenmohātsa vai dvādaśavārṣikam||25||

26vane caredbrahmacaryamiti vaḥ samayaḥ kṛtaḥ
|
tadidaṃ draupadīhetoranyo'nyasya pravāsanam||26||

27kṛtavāṃstatra dharmārthamatra dharmo na duṣyati
|
paritrāṇaṃ ca kartavyamārtānāṃ pṛthulocana||27||

28kṛtvā mama paritrāṇaṃ tava dharmo na lupyate
|
yadi vāpyasya dharmasya sūkṣmo'pi syādvyatikramaḥ||28||

29sa ca te dharma eva syāddatvā prāṇānmamārjuna
|
bhaktāṃ ca bhaja māṃ pārtha satāmetanmataṃ prabho||29||

30na kariṣyasi cedevaṃ mṛtāṃ māmupadhāraya
|
prāṇadānānmahābāho cara dharmamanuttamam||30||

31śaraṇaṃ ca prapannāsmi tvāmadya puruṣottama
|
dīnānanāthānkaunteya parirakṣasi nityaśaḥ||31||

32sā'haṃ śaṟaṇamabhyemi roravīmi ca duḥkhitā
|
yāce tvāṃ cābhikāmāhaṃ tasmātkuru mama priyam
|
sa tvamātmapradānena sakāmāṃ kartumarhasi||32||

33vaiśaṃpāyana uvāca|
evamuktastu kaunteyaḥ pannageśvarakanyayā
|
kṛtavāṃstattathā sarvaṃ dharmamuddiśya kāraṇam||33||

34sa nāgabhavane rātriṃ tāmuṣitvā pratāpavān
|
udite'bhyutthitaḥ sūrye kauravyasya niveśanāt||34||

35āgatastu punastatra gaṅgādvāraṃ tayā saha
|
parityajya gatā sādhvī ulūpī nijamandiram||35||

36dattvā varamajeyatvaṃ jale sarvatra bhārata
|
sādhyā jalacarāḥ sarve bhaviṣyanti na saṃśayaḥ||36||
