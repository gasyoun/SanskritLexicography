Mahābhārata 13.111 (Anuśāsanaparvan)

1yudhiṣṭhira uvāca|
pitāmaha mahāprājña sarvaśāstraviśārada
|
śrotumicchāmi martyānāṃ saṃsāravidhimuttamam ||1||

2kena vṛttena rājendra vartamānā narā bhuvi
|
prāpnuvantyuttamaṃ svargaṃ kathaṃ ca narakaṃ nṛpa ||2||

3mṛtaṃ śarīramutsṛjya kāṣṭhaloṣṭasamaṃ janāḥ
|
prayāntyamuṃ lokamitaḥ ko vai tānanugacchati ||3||

4bhīṣma uvāca|
ayamāyāti bhagavān bṛhaspatirudāradhīḥ
|
pṛcchainaṃ sumahābhāgametadguhyaṃ sanātanam ||4||

5naitadanyena śakyaṃ hi vaktuṃ kenacidadya vai
|
vaktā bṛhaspatisamo na hyanyo vidyate kvacit ||5||

6vaiśampāyana uvāca|
tayoḥ saṃvadatorevaṃ pārthagāṅgeyayostadā
|
ājagāma viśuddhātmā nākapṛṣṭhādbṛhaspatiḥ ||6||

7tato rājā samutthāya dhṛtarāṣṭrapurogamaḥ
|
pūjāmanupamāṃ cakre sarve te ca sabhāsadaḥ ||7||

8tato dharmasuto rājā bhagavantaṃ bṛhaspatim
|
upagamya yathānyāyaṃ praśnaṃ papraccha tattvataḥ ||8||

9yudhiṣṭhira uvāca|
bhagavansarvadharmajña sarvaśāstraviśārada
|
martyasya kaḥ sahāyo vai pitā mātā suto guruḥ ||9||

10jñātisambandhivargaśca mitravargastathaiva ca
|
mṛtaṃ śarīrasutsṛjya kāṣṭhaloṣṭasamaṃ janāḥ ||10||

11gacchantyamutra lokaṃ vai ka enamanugacchati
|
bṛhaspatiruvāca|
ekaḥ prasūyate rājanneka eva vinaśyati ||11||

12ekastarati durgāṇi gacchatyekastu durgatim
|
asahāyaḥ pitā mātā tathā bhrātā suto guruḥ ||12||

13jñātisambandhivargaśca mitravargastathaiva ca
|
mṛtaṃ śarīramutsṛjya kāṣṭhaloṣṭasamaṃ janāḥ ||13||

14muhūrtamiva roditvā tato yānti parāṅmukhāḥ
|
taistaccharīramutsṛṣṭaṃ dharma eko'nugacchati ||14||

15tasmāddharmaḥ sahāyaśca sevitavyaḥ sadā nṛbhiḥ
|
prāṇī dharmasamāyukto gacchetsvargagatiṃ parām ||15||

16tathaivādharmasaṃyukto narakaṃ copapadyate
|
tasmānnyāyāgatairarthairdharmaṃ seveta paṇḍitaḥ ||16||

17dharma eko manuṣyāṇāṃ sahāyaḥ pāralaukikaḥ
|
lobhānmohāda‌nukrośādbhayādvāpyabahuśrutaḥ ||17||

18naraḥ karotyakāryāṇi parārthe lobhamohitaḥ
|
dharmaścārthaśca kāmaśca tritayaṃ jīvite phalam ||18||

19etattrayamavāptavyamadharmaparivarjitam
|
yudhiṣṭhira uvāca|
śrutaṃ bhagavato vākyaṃ dharmayuktaṃ paraṃ hitam ||19||

20śarīranicayaṃ jñātuṃ buddhistu mama jāyate
|
mṛtaṃ śarīraṃ hi nṛṇāṃ sūkṣmamavyaktatāṃ gatam ||20||

21acakṣurviṣayaṃ prāptaṃ kathaṃ dharmo'nugacchati
|
bṛhaspatiruvāca|
pṛthivī vāyurākāśamāpo jyotirmanontagaḥ ||21||

22buddhirātmā ca sahitā dharmaṃ paśyanti nityadā
|
prāṇināmiha sarveṣāṃ sākṣibhūtā niśāniśam ||22||

23etaiśca saha dharmo'pi taṃ jīvamanugacchati
|
tvagasthimāṃsaṃ śukraṃ ca śoṇitaṃ ca mahāmate ||23||

24śarīraṃ varjayantyete jīvitena vivarjitam
|
tato dharmasamāyuktaḥ prāpnute jīva eva hi ||24||

25tato'sya karma paśyanti śubhaṃ vā yadi vā'śubham
|
devatāḥ pañcabhūtasthāḥ kiṃ bhūyaḥ śrotumicchasi||25||

26tato dharmasamāyuktaḥ sa jīvaḥ sukhamedhate
|
ihaloke pare caiva kiṃ bhūyaḥ kathayāmi te ||26||

27yudhiṣṭhira uvāca|
taddarśitaṃ bhagavatā yathā dharmo'nugacchati
|
etattu jñātumicchāmi kathaṃ retaḥ pravartate ||27||

28bṛhaspatiruvāca|
annamaśnanti yaddevāḥ śarīrasthā nareśvara
|
pṛthivī vāyurākāśamāpo jyotirmanastathā ||28||

29tatastṛpteṣu rājendra teṣu bhūteṣu pañcasu
|
manaḥṣaṣṭheṣu śuddhātman retaḥ sampadyate mahat ||29||

30tato garbhaḥ sambhavati śleṣātstrīpuṃsayornṛpa
|
etatte sarvamākhyātaṃ bhūyaḥ kiṃ śrotumicchasi ||30||

31yudhiṣṭhira uvāca|
ākhyātaṃ me bhagavatā garbhaḥ sañjāyate tathā
|
yathā jātastu puruṣaḥ prapadyati taducyatām ||31||

32bṛhaspatiruvāca|
āsannamātraḥ puruṣastairbhūtairabhibhūyate
|
viprayuktaśca tairbhūtaiḥ punaryātyaparāṃ gatim ||32||

33sarvabhūtasamāyuktaḥ prāpnute jīva eva hi
|
tato'sya karma paśyanti śubhaṃ vā yadi vā'śubham
|
devatāḥ pañcabhūtasthāḥ kiṃ bhūyaḥ śrotumicchasi ||33||

34yudhiṣṭhira uvāca|
tvagasthimāṃsamutsṛjya taiśca bhūtairvivarjitaḥ
|
jīvaḥ sa bhagavankvasthaḥ sukhaduḥkhe samaśnute ||34||

35bṛhaspatiruvāca|
jīvaḥ karmasamāyuktaḥ śīghraṃ retastvamāgataḥ
|
strīṇāṃ puṣpaṃ samāsādya sūte kālena bhārata ||35||

36yamasya puruṣaiḥ kleśaṃ yamasya puruṣairvadham
|
duḥkhaṃ saṃsāracakraṃ ca naraḥ kleśaṃ sa vindati ||36||

37iha loke sa ca prāṇī janmaprabhṛti pārthiva
|
sukṛtaṃ karma vai bhuṅkte dharmasya phalamāśritaḥ ||37||

38yadi dharmaṃ yathāśakti janmaprabhṛti sevate
|
tataḥ sa puruṣo bhūtvā sevate nityadā sukham ||38||

39athāntarā tu dharmasyāpyadharmamupasevate
|
sukhasyānantaraṃ duḥkhaṃ sa jīvopyadhigacchati ||39||

40adharmeṇa samāyukto yamasya viṣayaṃ gataḥ
|
mahadduḥkhaṃ samāsādya tiryagyonau prajāyate ||40||

41karmaṇā yena yeneha yasyāṃ yonau prajāyate
|
jīvo mohasamāyuktastanme nigadataḥ śṛṇu ||41||

42yadetaducyate śāstre setihāse ca cchandasi
|
yamasya viṣayaṃ ghoraṃ martyo lokaḥ prapadyate ||42||

43iha sthānāni puṇyāni devatulyāni bhūpate
|
tiryagyonyatiriktāni gatimanti ca sarvaśaḥ ||43||

44yamasya bhavane divye brahmalokasame guṇaiḥ
|
karmabhirniyatairbaddho janturduḥkhānyupāśnute ||44||

45yena yena tu bhāvena karmaṇā puruṣo gatim
|
prayāti paruṣāṃ ghorāṃ tatte vakṣāmyataḥ param ||45||

46adhītya caturo vedān dvijo mohasamanvitaḥ
|
patitātpratigṛhyātha kharayonau prajāyate ||46||

47kharo jīvati varṣāṇi daśa pañca ca bhārata
|
kharo mṛto balīvardaḥ sapta varṣāṇi jīvati ||47||

48balīvardo mṛtaścāpi jāyate brahmarākṣasaḥ
|
brahmarakṣaśca māsāṃstrīṃstato jāyati brāhmaṇaḥ ||48||

49patitaṃ yājayitvā tu kṛmiyonau prajāyate
|
tatra jīvati varṣāṇi daśa pañca ca bhārata ||49||

50kṛmibhāvādvimuktastu tato jāyati gardabhaḥ
|
gardarbhaḥ pañcavarṣāṇi pañcavarṣāṇi sūkaraḥ ||50||

51kukkuṭaḥ pañca varṣāṇi pañca varṣāṇi jambukaḥ
|
śvā varṣamekaṃ bhavati tato jāyati mānavaḥ ||51||

52upādhyāyasya yaḥ pāpaṃ śiṣyaḥ kuryādabuddhimān
|
sa jīva iha saṃsārāṃstrīnāpnoti na saṃśayaḥ ||52||

53prāk śvā bhavati rājendra tataḥ kravyāttataḥ kharaḥ
|
tataḥ pretaḥ parikliṣṭaḥ paścājjāyati brāhmaṇaḥ ||53||

54manasāpi gurorbhāryāṃ yaḥ śiṣyo yāti pāpakṛt
|
sa ugrānpraiti saṃsārānadharmeṇeha cetasā ||54||

55śvayonau tu sa sambhūtastrīṇi varṣāṇi jīvati
|
tatrāpi nidhanaṃ prāptaḥ kṛmiyonau prajāyate ||55||

56kṛmibhāvamanuprāpto varṣamekaṃ tu jīvati
|
tatastu nidhanaṃ prāpto brahmayonau prajāyate ||56||

57yadi putrasamaṃ śiṣyaṃ gururhanyāda‌kāraṇe
|
ātmanaḥ kāmakāreṇa so'pi hiṃsraḥ prajāyate ||57||

58pitaraṃ mātaraṃ caiva yastu putro'vamanyate
|
so'pi rājanmṛto jantuḥ pūrvaṃ jāyeta gardabhaḥ ||58||

59gardabhatvaṃ tu samprāpya daśavarṣāṇi jīvati
|
saṃvatsaraṃ tu kumbhīrastato jāyeta mānavaḥ ||59||

60putrasya mātāpitarau yasya ruṣṭāvubhāvapi
|
gurvapadhyānataḥ so'pi mṛto jāyati gardabhaḥ ||60||

61kharo jīvati māsāṃstu daśa śvā ca caturdaśa
|
biḍālaḥ saptamāsāṃstu tato jāyati mānavaḥ ||61||

62mātāpitarāvākruśya sārikaḥ samprajāyate
|
tāḍayitvā tu tāveva jāyate kacchapo nṛpa ||62||

63kacchapo daśavarṣāṇi trīṇi varṣāṇi śalyakaḥ
|
vyālo bhūtvā ca ṣaṇmāsāṃstato jāyati mānuṣaḥ ||63||

64bhartṛpiṇḍamupāśnanyo rājadviṣṭāni sevate
|
so'pi mohasamāpanno mṛto jāyati vānaraḥ ||64||

65vānaro daśavarṣāṇi pañcavarṣāṇi mūṣikaḥ
|
śvā'tha bhūtvā tu ṣaṇmāsāṃstato jāyati mānuṣaḥ ||65||

66nyāsāpahartā tu naro yamasya viṣayaṃ gataḥ
|
saṃsārāṇāṃ śataṃ gatvā kṛmiyonau prajāyate ||66||

67tatra jīvati varṣāṇi daśa pañca ca bhārata
|
duṣkṛtasya kṣayaṃ kṛtvā tato jāyati mānuṣaḥ ||67||

68asūyako naraścāpi mṛto jāyati śārṅgakaḥ
|
viśvāsahartā tu naro mīno jāyati durmatiḥ ||68||

69bhūtvā mīno'ṣṭavarṣāṇi mṛto jāyati bhārata
|
mṛgastu caturo māsāṃstataśchāgaḥ prajāyate ||69||

70chāgastu nidhanaṃ prāpya pūrṇe saṃvatsare tataḥ
|
kīṭaḥ sañjāyate jantustato jāyati mānuṣaḥ ||70||

71dhānyān yavāṃstilān māṣān kulatthān sarṣapāṃścaṇān
|
kalāpānatha mudgāṃśca godhūmānatasīstathā ||71||

72sasyasyānyasya hartā ca mohājjanturacetanaḥ
|
sa jāyate mahārāja mūṣiko nirapatrapaḥ ||72||

73tataḥ pretya mahārāja mṛto jāyati sūkaraḥ
|
sukaro jātamātrastu rogeṇa mriyate nṛpa ||73||

74śvā tato jāyate mūḍhaḥ karmaṇā tena pārthiva
|
bhūtvā śvā pañca varṣāṇi tato jāyati mānavaḥ ||74||

75paradārābhimarśaṃ tu kṛtvā jāyati vai vṛkaḥ
|
śvā śṛgālastato gṛdhro vyālaḥ kaṅko bakastathā ||75||

76bhrāturbhāryāṃ tu pāpātmā yo dharṣayati mohitaḥ
|
puṃskokilatvamāpnoti so'pi saṃvatsaraṃ nṛpa ||76||

77sakhibhāryāṃ gurorbhāryāṃ rājabhāryāṃ tathaiva ca
|
pradharṣayitvā kāmāya mṛto jāyati sūkaraḥ ||77||

78sūkaraḥ pañcavarṣāṇi daśavarṣāṇi śvāvidhaḥ
|
biḍālaḥ pañcavarṣāṇi daśavarṣāṇi kukkuṭaḥ ||78||

79pipīlikastu māsāṃstrīn kīṭaḥ syānmāsameva tu
|
etānāsādya saṃsārān kṛmiyonau prajāyate ||79||

80tatra jīvati māsāṃstu kṛmiyonau caturdaśa
|
tato'dharmakṣayaṃ kṛtvā punarjāyati mānavaḥ ||80||

81upasthite vivāhe tu yajñe dāne'pi vā vibho
|
mohātkaroti yo vighnaṃ sa mṛto jāyate kṛmiḥ ||81||

82kṛmirjīvati varṣāṇi daśa pañca ca bhārata
|
adharmasya kṣayaṃ kṛtvā tato jāyati mānavaḥ ||82||

83pūrvaṃ datvā tu yaḥ kanyāṃ dvitīye dātumicchati
|
sopi rājanmṛto jantuḥ kṛmiyonau prajāyate ||83||

84tatra jīvati varṣāṇi trayodaśa yudhiṣṭhira
|
adharmasaṅkṣaye yuktastato jāyati mānavaḥ ||84||

85devakāryamakṛtvā tu pitṛkāryamathāpi vā
|
anirvāpya samaśnanvai mṛto jāyati vāyasaḥ ||85||

86vāyasaḥ śatavarṣāṇi tato jāyati kukkuṭaḥ
|
jāyate vyālakaścāpi māsaṃ tasmāttu mānuṣaḥ ||86||

87jyeṣṭhaṃ pitṛsamaṃ cāpi bhrātaraṃ yo'vamanyate
|
so'pi mṛtyumupāgamya krauñcayonau prajāyate ||87||

88krauñco jīvati varṣaṃ tu tato jāyati cīrakaḥ
|
tato nidhanamāpanno mānuṣatvamupāśnute ||88||

89vṛṣalo brāhmaṇīṃ gatvā kṛmiyonau prajāyate
|
tataḥ samprāpya nidhanaṃ jāyate sūkaraḥ punaḥ ||89||

90sūkaro jātamātrastu rogeṇa mriyate nṛpa
|
śvā tato jāyate mūḍhaḥ karmaṇā tena pārthiva ||90||

91śvā bhūtvā kṛtakarmā'sau jāyate mānuṣastataḥ
|
tatrāpatyaṃ samutpādya mṛto jāyati mūṣikaḥ ||91||

92kṛtaghnastu mṛto rājan yamasya viṣayaṃ gataḥ
|
yamasya puruṣaiḥ kruddhairvadhaṃ prāpnoti dāruṇam ||92||

93daṇḍaṃ samudgaraṃ śūlamagnikumbhaṃ ca dāruṇam
|
asipatravanaṃ ghoravālukaṃ kūṭaśālmalīm ||93||

94etāścānyāśca bahvīśva yamasya viṣayaṃ gataḥ
|
yātanāḥ prāpya tatrogrāstato vadhyati bhārata ||94||

95tato hataḥ kṛtaghnaḥ sa tatrograirbharatarṣabha
|
saṃsāracakramāsādya kṛmiyonau prajāyate ||95||

96kṛmirbhavati varṣāṇi daśa pañca ca bhārata
|
tato garbhaṃ samāsādya tatraiva mriyate śiśuḥ ||96||

97tato garbhaśatairjanturbahubhiḥ samprapadyate
|
saṃsārāṃśca bahūngatvā tatastiryakṣu jāyate ||97||

98tato duḥkhamanuprāpya bahu varṣagaṇāniha
|
apunarbhavasaṃyuktastataḥ kūrmaḥ prajāyate ||98||

99dadhi hṛtvā bakaścāpi plavo matsyānasaṃskṛtān
|
corayitvā tu durbuddhirmadhudaṃśaḥ prajāyate ||99||

100phalaṃ vā mūlakaṃ hṛtvā apūpaṃ vā pipīlikāḥ
|
corayitvā ca niṣpāvaṃ jāyate halagolakaḥ ||100||

101pāyasaṃ corayitvā tu tittiritvamavāpnute
|
hṛtvā piṣṭamayaṃ pūpaṃ kumbholūkaḥ prajāyate ||101||

102ayo hṛttvā tu durbuddhirvāyaso jāyate naraḥ
|
kāṃsyaṃ hṛtvā tu durbuddhirhārito jāyate naraḥ ||102||

103rājataṃ bhājanaṃ hṛtvā kapotaḥ samprajāyate
|
hṛtvā tu kāñcanaṃ bhāṇḍaṃ kṛmiyonau prajāyate ||103||

104patrorṇaṃ corayitvā tu kṛkalatvaṃ nigacchati
|
kauśikaṃ tu tato hṛtvā naro jāyati vartakaḥ ||104||

105aṃśukaṃ corayitvā tu śuko jāyati mānavaḥ
|
corayitvā dukūlaṃ tu mṛto haṃsaḥ prajāyate ||105||

106krauñcaḥ kārpāsikaṃ hṛtvā mṛto jāyati mānavaḥ
|
corayitvā naraḥ paṭṭaṃ tvāvikaṃ caiva bhārata ||106||

107kṣaumaṃ ca vastramādāya śaśo jantuḥ prajāyate
|
varṇān hṛtvā tu puruṣo mṛto jāyati barhiṇaḥ ||107||

108hṛtvā raktāni vastrāṇi jāyate jīvajīvakaḥ
|
varṇakādīṃstathā gandhāṃścorayitveha mānavaḥ ||108||

109chucchundaritvamāpnoti rājan lobhaparāyaṇaḥ
|
tatra jīvati varṣāṇi tato daśa ca pañca ca ||109||

110adharmasya kṣayaṃ gatvā tato jāyati mānuṣaḥ
|
corayitvā payaścāpi balākā samprajāyate ||110||

111yastu corayate tailaṃ naro mohasamanvitaḥ
|
so'pi rājanmṛto jantustailapāyī prajāyate ||111||

112aśastraṃ puruṣaṃ hatvā saśastraḥ puruṣādhamaḥ
|
arthārthī yadi vā vairī sa mṛto jāyate kharaḥ ||112||

113kharo jīvati varṣe dve tataḥ śastreṇa vadhyate
|
sa mṛto mṛgayonau tu nityodvigno'bhijāyate ||113||

114mṛgo vadhyati śastreṇa gate saṃvatsare tu saḥ
|
hato mṛgastato mīnaḥ sopi jālena badhyate ||114||

115māse caturthe samprāpte śvāpadaḥ samprajāyate
|
śvāpado daśavarṣāṇi dvīpī varṣāṇi pañca ca ||115||

116tatastu nidhanaṃ prāptaḥ kālaparyāyacoditaḥ
|
adharmasya kṣayaṃ kṛtvā tato jāyati mānuṣaḥ ||116||

117striyaṃ hatvā tu durbuddhiryamasya viṣayaṃ gataḥ
|
bahūnkleśānsamāsādya saṃsārāṃścaiva viṃśatim ||117||

118tataḥ paścānmahārāja kṛmiyonau prajāyate
|
kṛmirviṃśativarṣāṇi bhūtvā jāyati mānuṣaḥ ||118||

119bhojanaṃ corayitvā tu makṣikā jāyate naraḥ
|
makṣikāsaṅghavaśago bahūnmāsānbhavatyuta ||119||

120tataḥ pāpakṣayaṃ kṛtvā mānuṣatvamavāpnute
|
dhānyaṃ hṛtvā tu puruṣo lomaśaḥ samprajāyate||120||

121tathā piṇyākasammiśramaśanaṃ corayennaraḥ
|
sa jāyate babhrusamo dāruṇo mūṣiko naraḥ ||121||

122daśan vai mānuṣānnityaṃ pāpātmā sa viśāmpate
|
ghṛtaṃ hṛtvā tu durbuddhiḥ kākamadguḥ prajāyate ||122||

123matsyamāṃsamatho hṛtvā kāko jāyati durmatiḥ
|
lavaṇaṃ corayitvā tu cirikākaḥ prajāyate ||123||

124viśvāsena tu nikṣiptaṃ yo vinihnoti mānavaḥ
|
sa gatāyurnarastāta matsyayonau prajāyate ||124||

125matsyayonimanuprāpya mṛto jāyati mānuṣaḥ
|
mānuṣatvamanuprāpya kṣīṇāyurupapadyate ||125||

126pāpāni tu narāḥ kṛtvā tiryagjāyanti bhārata
|
na cātmanaḥ pramāṇaṃ te dharmaṃ jānanti kiñcana ||126||
