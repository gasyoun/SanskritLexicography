Mahābhārata 3.194 (Āraṇyakaparvan)

1vaiśampāyana uvāca|
tataḥ pāṇḍavāḥ punarmārkaṇḍeyamūcuḥ||1||

2kathitaṃ brāhmaṇamahābhāgyaṃ rājanyamahābhāgyamidānīṃ śuśrūṣāmaha iti tānuvāca mārkaṇḍeyo maharṣiḥ śrūyatāmiti idānīṃ rājanyānāṃ mahābhāgyamiti
|
kurūṇāmanyatamaḥ suhotro nāma rājā maharṣīnabhigamya nivṛtya rathasthameva rājānamauśīnaraṃ śibiṃ dadarśābhimukhaṃ tau sametya paraspareṇa yathāvayaḥ pūjāṃ prayujya guṇasāmyena paraspareṇa tulyātmānau viditvā'nyonyasya panthānaṃ na dadatustatra nāradaḥ prādurāsīt kimidaṃ bhavantau parasparasya panthānamāvṛtya tiṣṭhata iti||2||

3tāvūcaturnāradaṃ naitadbhagavan pūrvakarmakartrādibhirviśiṣṭasya panthā upadiśyate samarthāya vā āvāṃ ca sakhyaṃ paraspareṇopagatau taccāvadhānato'tyutkṛṣṭamadharottaraṃ paribhraṣṭaṃ nāradastvevamuktaḥ ślokatrayamapaṭhat||3||

4krūraḥ kauravya mṛdave mṛduḥ krūre ca kaurava
|
sādhuścāsādhave sādhuḥ sādhave nāpnuyāt katham||4||

5kṛtaṃ śataguṇaṃ kuryānnāsti deveṣu nirṇayaḥ
|
auśīnaraḥ sādhuśīlo bhavato vai mahīpatiḥ||5||

6jayet kadaryaṃ dānena satyenānṛtavādinam
|
kṣamayā krūrakarmāṇamasādhu sādhunā jayet||6||

7tadubhāveva bhavantāvudārau ya idānīṃ bhavadbhyāmanyatamaḥ sopasarpatu etadvai nidarśanamityuktvā tūṣṇīṃ nārado babhūva
|
etacchrutvā tu kauravyaḥ śibiṃ pradakṣiṇaṃ kṛtvā panthānaṃ datvā bahukarmabhiḥ praśasya prayayau||7||

8tadetadrājño mahābhāgyamapyuktavānnāradaḥ||8||
