Mahābhārata 3.300 (Āraṇyakaparvan)

1janamejaya uvāca|
yat tattadā mahadbrahman lomaśo vākyamabravīt
|
indrasya vacanādeva pāṇḍuputraṃ yudhiṣṭhiram||1||

2yaccāpi te bhayaṃ tīvraṃ na ca kīrtayase kvacit
|
taccāpyapahariṣyāmi dhanañjaya ito gate||2||

3kiṃ nu tajjapatāṃ śreṣṭha karṇaṃ prati mahadbhayam
|
āsīnna ca sa dharmātmā kathayāmāsa kasyacit||3||

4vaiśampāyana uvāca|
ayaṃ te rājaśārdūla kathayāmi kathāmimām
|
pṛcchato bharataśreṣṭha śuśrūṣarava giraṃ mama||4||

5dvādaśe samatikrānte varṣe prāpte trayodaśe
|
pāṇḍūnāṃ hitakṛcchakraḥ karṇaṃ bhikṣitṛmudyataḥ||5||

6abhiprāyamatho jñātvā mahendrasya vibhāvasuḥ
|
kuṇḍalārthe mahārāja sūryaḥ karṇamupāgataḥ||6||

7mahārhe śayane vīraṃ sparddhyāstaraṇasaṃvṛte
|
śayānamatimiśvastaṃ brahmaṇyaṃ satyavādinam||7||

8svapnānte niśi rājendra darśayāmāsa raśmivān
|
kṛpayā parayā''viṣṭaḥ putrasnehācca bhārata||8||

9brāhmaṇo vedavidbhūtvā sūryo yogardhirūpavān
|
hitārthamanbravīt karṇaṃ sāntvapūrvamidaṃ vacaḥ||9||

10karṇa madvacanaṃ tāta śṛṇu satyabhṛtāṃ vara
|
bruvato'dya mahābāho sauhṛdāt paramaṃ hitam||10||

11upāyāsyati śakrastvāṃ pāṇḍavānāṃ hitepsayā
|
brāhmaṇacchadmanā karṇa kuṇḍalopajihīrṣayā||11||

12viditaṃ tena śīlaṃ te sarvasya jagatastathā
|
yathā tvaṃ bhikṣitaḥ sadbhirdadāsyeva na yācase||12||

13tvaṃ hi tāta dadāsyeva brāhmaṇebhyaḥ prayācitam
|
vittaṃ yaccānyadapyāhurna pratyākhyāsi kasyacit||13||

14tvāṃ tu caivaṃvidhaṃ jñātvā svayaṃ vai pākaśāsanaḥ
|
āgantā kuṇḍalārthāya kavacaṃ caiva bhikṣitum||14||

15tasmai prayācamānāya na deye kuṇḍale tvayā
|
anuneyaḥ paraṃ śaktyā śreya etaddhi te param||15||

16kuṇḍalārthe bruvaṃstāta kāraṇairbahubhistvayā
|
anyairbahuvidhairvittaiḥ sannivāryaḥ punaḥ punaḥ||16||

17ratnaiḥ strībhistathā gobhirdhanairbahuvidhairapi
|
nidarśanaiśca bahubhiḥ kuṇḍalepsuḥ purandaraḥ||17||

18yadi dāsyasi karṇa tvaṃ sahaje kuṇḍale śubhe
|
āyuṣaḥ prakṣayaṃ gatvā mṛtyorvaśamupaiṣyasi||18||

19kavacena samāyuktaḥ kuṇḍalābhyāṃ ca mānada
|
avadhyastvaṃ raṇe'rīṇāmiti viddhi vaco mama||19||

20amṛtādutthitaṃ hyetadubhayaṃ ratnasambhavam
|
tasmādrakṣyaṃ tvayā karṇa jīvitaṃ cetpriyaṃ tava||20||

21karṇa uvāca|
ko māmevaṃ bhavān prāha darśayan sauhṛdaṃ param
|
kāmayā bhagavan brūhi ko bhavān dvijaveṣadhṛk||21||

22brāhmaṇa uvāca|
ahaṃ tāta sahastrāṃśuḥ sauhṛdāttvāṃ nidarśaye
|
kuruṣvaitadvaco me tvametacchreyaḥ paraṃ hi te||22||

23karṇa uvāca|
śreya eva mamātyantaṃ yasya me gopatiḥ prabhuḥ
|
pravaktā'dya hitānveṣī śṛṇu cedaṃ vaco mama||23||

24prasādaye tvāṃ varadaṃ praṇayācca bravīmyaham
|
na nivāryo vratādasmādahaṃ yadyasmi te priyaḥ||24||

25vrataṃ vai mama loko'yaṃ vetti kṛtsraṃ vibhāvaso
|
yathā'haṃ dvijamukhyebhyo dadyāṃ prāṇānapi dhruvam||25||

26yadyāgacchati māṃ śakro brāhmaṇacchadmanā vṛtaḥ
|
hitārthaṃ pāṇḍuputrāṇāṃ khecarottama bhikṣitum||26||

27dāsyāmi vibudhaśreṣṭha kuṇḍale varma cottamam
|
na me kīrtiḥ praṇaśyeta triṣu lokeṣu viśrutā||27||

28madvidhasya yaśasyaṃ hi na yuktaṃ prāṇarakṣaṇam
|
yuktaṃ hi yaśasā yuktaṃ maraṇaṃ lokasammatam||28||

29so'hamindrāya dāsyāmi kuṇḍale saha varmaṇā
|
yadi māṃ balavṛtraghno bhikṣārthamupayāsyati||29||

30hitārthaṃ pāṇḍuputrāṇāṃ kuṇḍale meṃ prayācitum
|
tanme kīrtikaraṃ loke tasyākīrtirbhaviṣyati||30||

31vṛṇomi kīrtiṃ lāke hi jīvitenāpi bhānuman
|
kīrtimānaśrute svargaṃ hīnakīrtistu naśyati||31||

32kīrtirhi puruṣaṃ loke sañjīvayati mātṛvat
|
akīrtirjīvitaṃ hanti javito'pi śarīriṇaḥ||32||

33ayaṃ purāṇaḥ śloko hi svayaṃ gīto vibhāvaso
|
dhātrā lokeśvara yathā kīrtirāyurnarasya ha||33||

34puruṣasya pare loke kīrtireva parāyaṇam
|
iha loke viśuddhā ca kīrtirāyurvivarddhanī||34||

35so'haṃ śarīraje dattvā kīrtiṃ prāpsyāmi śāśvatīm
|
datvā ca vidhivaddānaṃ brāhmaṇebhyo yathāvidhi||35||

36hutvā śarīraṃ saṅgrāme kṛtvā kama suduṣkaram
|
vijitya ca parānājau yaśaḥ prāpsyāmi kevalam||36||

37bhītānāmabhayaṃ datvā saṅgrāme jīvitārthinām
|
vṛddhān bālāndvijātīṃśca mokṣayitvā mahābhayāt||37||

38prāpsyāmi paramaṃ loke yaśaḥ svargyamanuttamam
|
jīvitenāpi me rakṣyā kīrtistadviddhi me vratam||38||

39so'haṃ datvā maghavate bhikṣāmetāmanuttamām
|
brāhmaṇacchadmine deva loke gantā parāṃ gatim||39||
