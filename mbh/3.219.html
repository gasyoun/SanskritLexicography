Mahābhārata 3.219 (Āraṇyakaparvan)

1mārkaṇḍeya uvāca|
bṛhaspateścāndramasī bhāryā''sīdyā yaśasvinī
|
agnīn sā'janayat puṇyānṣaḍekāṃ cāpi putrikām||1||

2āhutiṣveva yasyāgnerhaviṣādyaṃ vidhīyate
|
so'gnirbṛhaspateḥ putraḥ śaṃyurnāma mahāvrataḥ||2||

3cāturmāsyeṣu yasyeṣṭyamaśvamedhe'grajaḥ paśuḥ
|
dīpto jyāleranekābhiragnireko'tha vīryavān||3||

4śaṃyorapratimā bhāryā satyā satyā'tha dharmajā
|
agnistasya suto dīptastisraḥ kanyāśca suvratāḥ||4||

5prathamenājyabhāgena pūjyate yo'gniradhvare
|
agnistasya bharadvājaḥ prathamaḥ putra ucyate||5||

6paurṇamāseṣu sarveṣu haviṣājyaṃ sruvodyatam
|
bharato nāmataḥ so'gnirdvitīyaḥ śaṃyutaḥ sutaḥ||6||

7tisraḥ kanyā bhavantyanyā yāsāṃ sa bharataḥ patiḥ
|
bharatastu sutastasya bharatyekā ca putrikā||7||

8bharato bharatasyāgneḥ pāvakastu prajāpateḥ
|
mahānatyarthamahitastathā bharatasattama||8||

9bharadvājasya bhāryā tu vīrā vīrasya piṇḍadā
|
prāhurājyena tasyejyāṃ somasyeva dvijāḥ śanaiḥ||9||

10haviṣā yo dvitīyena somena saha yujyate
|
rathaprabhū rayādhvānakumbharetāḥ sa ucyate||10||

11śarayvāṃ janayat siddhiṃ bhānuṃ bhābhiḥ samāvṛṇot
|
āgneyamānayannityamāhvāne hyeṣa sūyate||11||

12yastu na cyavate nityaṃ yaśasā varcasā śriyā
|
agnirniścyavano nāma pṛthivīṃ stauti kevalam||12||

13vipāpmā kaluṣairmukto viśuddhaścārciṣā jvalan
|
vipāpo'gniḥ sutastasya satyaḥ samayadharmakṛt||13||

14ākrośatāṃ hi bhūtānāṃ yaḥ karoti hi niṣkṛtim
|
agniḥ sa niṣkṛtirnāma śobhayatyabhisevite||14||

15anukūjanti yeneha vedanārtāḥ svayaṃ janāḥ
|
tasya putraḥ svano nāma pāvakaḥ sa rujaskaraḥ||15||

16yastu viśvasya jagato buddhimākramya tiṣṭhati
|
taṃ prāhuradhyātmavido viśvajinnāma pāvakam||16||

17antarāgniḥ smṛto yastu bhuktaṃ pacati dehinām
|
sa yajñe viśvabhuṅnāma sarvalokeṣu bhārata||17||

18brahmacārī yatātmā ca satataṃ vipulavrataḥ
|
brāhmaṇāḥ pūjayantyenaṃ pākayajñeṣu pāvakam||18||

19pavitrā gomatī nāma nadī yasyābhavat priyā
|
tasmin karmāṇi sarvāṇi kriyante dharmakartṛbhiḥ||19||

20vaḍavāgniḥ pibatyambho yo'sau paramadāruṇaḥ
|
ūrdhvabhāgūrdhvabhāṅnāma kaviḥ prāṇāśritastu yaḥ||20||

21udagdvāraṃ haviryasya gṛhe nityaṃ pradīyate
|
tataḥ sviṣṭaṃ bhavedājyaṃ sviṣṭakṛt paramaḥ smṛtaḥ||21||

22yaḥ praśānteṣu bhūteṣu manyurbhavati pāvakaḥ
|
kuddhasya tu raso jajñe manyetāṃ cātha putrikām||22||

23svāheti dāruṇā krūrā sarvabhūteṣu tiṣṭhati
|
tridive yasya sadṛśo nāsti rūpeṇa kaścana
|
atulatvāt kṛto devairnāmnā kāmastu pāvakaḥ||23||

24saṃharṣāddhārayan krodhaṃ dhanvī sragvī rathe sthitaḥ
|
samare nāśayecchatrūnamogho nāma pāvakaḥ||24||

25uktho nāma mahābhāga tribhirukthairabhiṣṭutaḥ
|
mahāvācaṃ tvajanayat samāśvāsaṃ hi yaṃ viduḥ||25||
