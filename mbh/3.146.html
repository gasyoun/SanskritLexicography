Mahābhārata 3.146 (Āraṇyakaparvan)

1vaiśaṃpāyana uvāca|
tatra te puruṣavyāghrāḥ paramaṃ śaucamāsthitāḥ
|
ṣaḍrātramavasanvīrā dhanaṃjayadidṛkṣavaḥ||1||

2tataḥ pūrvottare vāyuḥ plavamāno yadṛcchayā
|
sahastrapatramarkābhaṃ divyaṃ padmamupāharat||2||

3tadavaikṣata pāñcālī divyagandhaṃ manoramam
|
anilenāhṛtaṃ bhūmau patitaṃ jalajaṃ śuci||3||

4tacchubhā śubhamāsādya saugandhikamanuttamam
|
atīva muditā rājanbhīmasenamathābravīt||4||

5paśya divyaṃ suruciraṃ bhīma puṣpamanuttamam
|
gandhasaṃsthānasaṃpannaṃ manaso mama nandanam||5||

6idaṃ ca dharmarājāya pradāsyāmi paraṃtapa
|
haredaṃ mama kāmāya kāmyake punarāśrame||6||

7yadi te'haṃ priyā pārtha bahūnīmānyupāhara
|
tānyahaṃ netumicchāmi kāmyakaṃ punarāśramam||7||

8evamuktvā śubhāpāṅgī bhīmasenamaninditā
|
jagāma puṣpamādāya dharmarājāya tattadā ||8||

9abhiprāyaṃ tu vijñāya mahiṣyāḥ puruṣarṣabhaḥ
|
priyāyāḥ priyakāmaḥ sa prāyādbhīmo mahābalaḥ||9||

10vātaṃ tamevābhimukho yatastatpuṣpamāgatam
|
ājihīrṣurjagāmāśu sa puṣpāṇyaparāṇyapi||10||

11rukmapṛṣṭhaṃ dhanurguhya śarāṃścāśīviṣopamān
|
mṛgarāḍiva saṃkruddhaḥ prabhinna iva kuñjaraḥ||11||

12dadṛśuḥ sarvabhūtāni mahābāṇadhanurdharam
|
na glānirna ca vaiklavyaṃ na bhayaṃ na ca saṃbhramaḥ
|
kadācijjuṣate pārthamātmajaṃ mātariśvanaḥ||12||

13draupadyāḥ priyamanvicchansa bāhubalamāśritaḥ||13||

14vyapetabhayasaṃmohaḥ śailamabhyapatadbalī
|
sa taṃ drumalatāgulmacchannaṃ nīlaśilātalam||14||

15giriṃ cacārāriharaḥ kinnarācaritaṃ śubham
|
nānāvarṇadharaiścitraṃ dhātudrumamṛgāṇḍajaiḥ||15||

16sarvabhūṣaṇasaṃpūrṇaṃ bhūmerbhujamivocchritam
|
sarvatra ramaṇīyeṣu gandhamādanasānuṣu||16||

17saktacakṣurabhiprāyānhṛdayenānucintayan
|
puṃskokilaninādeṣu ṣaṭpadācariteṣu ca||17||

18baddhaśrotramanaścakṣurjagāmāmitavikramaḥ
|
ājighransa mahātejāḥ sarvartukusumodbhavam||18||

19gandhamuddhatamuddāmo vane matta iva dvipaḥ
|
vījyamānaḥ supuṇyena nānākusumagandhinā||19||

20pituḥ saṃsparśaśītena gandhamādanavāyunā
|
kriyamāṇaśramaḥ pitrā saṃprahṛṣṭatanūruhaḥ||20||

21sa yakṣagandharvasurabrahmarṣigaṇasevitam
|
vilokayāmāsa tadā puṣpahetorariṃdamaḥ||21||

22viṣamacchadairacitairanulipta ivāṅgulaiḥ
|
valibhirdhātuvicchedaiḥ kāñcanāñjanarājataiḥ
|
sapakṣamiva nṛtyantaṃ pārśvalagnaiḥ payodharaiḥ||22||

23muktāhārairiva citaṃ cyutaiḥ prastravaṇodakaiḥ
|
abhirāmadīkuñjanirjharodakakandaram||23||

24apsaro nūpuraravaiḥ pranṛttavarabarhiṇam
|
digvāraṇaviṣāṇāgrairghṛṣṭopalaśilātalam||24||

25srastāṃśukamivākṣobhyairnimnagāniḥsṛtairjalaiḥ
|
saśaṣpakavalaiḥ svasthairadūraparivartibhiḥ||25||

26bhayānabhijñairhariṇaiḥ kautūhalanirīkṣitaḥ
|
cālayānaḥ svavegena latājālānyanekaśaḥ||26||

27ākrīḍamāno hṛṣṭātmā śrīmānvāyusuto yayau
|
priyāmanorathaṃ kartumudyataścārulocanaḥ||27||

28prāṃśuḥ kanakavarṇābhaḥ siṃhasaṃhanano yuvā
|
mattavāraṇavikrānto mattavāraṇavegavān||28||

29mattavāraṇatāmrākṣo mattavāraṇavāraṇaḥ
|
priyapāśvarthopaviṣṭābhirvyāvṛttābhirviceṣṭitaiḥ||29||

30yakṣagandharvayoṣābhiradṛśyābhirnirīkṣitaḥ
|
navāvatāraṃ rūpasya vikrīḍanniva pāṇḍavaḥ||30||

31cacāra ramaṇīyeṣu gandhamādanasānuṣu
|
saṃsmaranvividhānkleśānduryodhanakṛtānbahūn||31||

32draupadyā vanavāsinyāḥ priyaṃ kartuṃ samudyataḥ
|
so'cintayadgate svargamarjune mayi cāgate||32||

33puṣpahetoḥ kathaṃ tvāryaḥ kariṣyati yudhiṣṭhiraḥ
|
snehānnaravaro nūnamaviśvāsādbalasya ca||33||

34nakulaṃ sahadevaṃ ca na mokṣyati yudhiṣṭhiraḥ
|
kathaṃ tu kusumāvāptiḥ syācchīghramiti cintayan||34||

35pratasthe naraśārdūlaḥ pakṣirāḍiva vegitaḥ
|
sajjamānamanodṛṣṭiḥ phulleṣu girisānuṣu||35||

36draupadīvākyapātheyo bhīmaḥ śīghrataraṃ yayau
|
kampayanmedinīṃ padbhyāṃ nirghāta iva parvasu||36||

37trāsayangajayūthāni vātaraṃhā vṛkodaraḥ
|
siṃhavyāghramṛgāṃścaiva mardayāno mahābalaḥ||37||

38unmūlayanmahāvṛkṣānpothayaṃstarasā balī
|
latāvallīśca vegena vikarṣanpāṇḍunandanaḥ
|
uparyupari śailāgramārurukṣuriva dvipaḥ||38||

39vinardamāno'tibhṛśaṃ savidyudiva toyadaḥ
|
tena śabdena mahatā bhīmasya pratibodhitāḥ||39||

40guhāṃ santatyajurvyāghrā nililyurvanavāsinaḥ
|
samutpetuḥ khagāstrastā mṛgayūthāni dudruvuḥ||40||

41ṛkṣāścotsasṛjurvṛkṣāṃstatyajurharayo guhām
|
vyajṛmbhanta mahāsiṃhā mahiṣāścāvalokayan||41||

42tena vitrāsitā nāgāḥ kareṇuparivāritāḥ
|
tadvanaṃ saṃparityajya jagmuranyanmahāvanam||42||

43varāhamṛgasaṃghāśca mahiṣāśca vanecarāḥ
|
vyāghragomāyusaṃghāśca praṇedurgavayaiḥ saha||43||

44rathāṅgasāhvadātyūhā haṃsakāraṇḍavaplavāḥ
|
śukāḥ puṃskokilāḥ krauñcā visaṃjñā bhejire diśaḥ||44||

45tathānye darpitā nāgāḥ kareṇuśarapīḍitāḥ
|
siṃhavyāghrāśca saṃkruddhā bhīmasenamathādravan||45||

46śakṛnmūtraṃ ca muñcānā bhayavibhrāntamānasāḥ
|
vyāditāsyā mahāraudrā vyanadanbhīṣaṇānravān||46||

47tato vāyusutaḥ krodhātsvabāhubalamāśritaḥ
|
gajenānyāngajāñchrīmānsiṃhaṃ siṃhena vā vibhuḥ||47||

48talaprahārairanyāṃśca vyahanatpāṇḍavo balī
|
te vadhyamānā bhīmena siṃhavyāghratarakṣavaḥ||48||

49bhayādvisasṛjurbhīmaṃ śakṛnmūtraṃ ca susruvuḥ
|
praviveśa tataḥ kṣipraṃ tānapāsya mahābalaḥ||49||

50vanaṃ pāṇḍusutaḥ śrīmāñchabdenāpūrayandiśaḥ
|
athāpaśyanmahābāhurgandhamādanasānuṣu||50||

51suramyaṃ kadalīṣaṇḍaṃ bahuyojanavistṛtam
|
tamabhyagacchadvegena kṣobhayiṣyanmahāvalaḥ||51||

52mahāgaja ivāsrāvī prabhañjanvividhāndrumān
|
utpāṭya kadalīstambhānbahutālasamucchrayān||52||

53cikṣepa tarasā bhīmaḥ samantādbalināṃ varaḥ
|
vinadansumahātejā nṛsiṃha iva darpitaḥ||53||

54tataḥ sattvānyupākrāmadbahūni sumahānti ca
|
ruruvānarasiṃhāṃśca mahiṣāṃśca jalāśayān||54||

55tena śabdena caivātha bhīmasenaraveṇa ca
|
vanāntaragatāścāpi vitresurmṛgapakṣiṇaḥ||55||

56taṃ śabdaṃ sahasā śrutvā mṛgapakṣisamīritam
|
jalārdrapakṣā vihagāḥ samutpetuḥ sahasraśaḥ||56||

57tānaudakānpakṣigaṇānnirīkṣya bharatarṣabhaḥ
|
tānevānusaranramyaṃ dadarśa sumahatsaraḥ||57||

58kāñcanaiḥ kadalīṣaṇḍairmandamārutakampitaiḥ
|
vījyamānamivākṣobhyaṃ tīrāttīravisarpibhiḥ||58||

59tatsaro'thāvatīryāśu prabhūtanalinotpalam
|
mahāgaja ivoddāmaścikrīḍa balavadbalī||59||

60vikrīḍya tasminruciramuttatārāmitadyutiḥ
|
tato'dhyagantuṃ vegena tadvanaṃ bahupādapam||60||

61dadhmau ca śaṅkhaṃ svanavatsarvaprāṇena pāṇḍavaḥ
|
āsphoṭayacca balavānbhīmaḥ saṃnādayandiśaḥ||61||

62tasya śaṅkhasya śabdena bhīmasenaraveṇa ca
|
bāhuśabdena cogreṇa nadantīva girerguhāḥ||62||

63taṃ vajraniṣpeṣasamamāsphoṭitamahāravam
|
śrutvā śailaguhāsuptaiḥ siṃhairmukto mahāsvanaḥ||63||

64siṃhanādabhayatrastaiḥ kuñjarairapi bhārata
|
mukto virāvaḥ sumahānparvato yena pūritaḥ||64||

65taṃ tu nādaṃ tataḥ śrutvā muktaṃ vāraṇapuṅgavaiḥ
|
bhrātaraṃ bhīmasenaṃ tu vijñāya hanumānkapiḥ||65||

66divaṃgamaṃ rurodhātha mārgaṃ bhīmasya kāraṇāt
|
anena hi pathā mā vai gacchediti vicārya saḥ||66||

67āsta ekāyane mārge kadalīṣaṇḍamaṇḍite
|
bhrāturbhīmasya rakṣārthaṃ taṃ mārgamavarudhya vai||67||

68mā'tra prāpsyati śāpaṃ vā gharṣaṇāṃ veti pāṇḍavaḥ
|
kadalīṣaṇḍamadhyastho hyevaṃ saṃcintya vānaraḥ||68||

69prājṛmbhata mahākāyo hanūmānnāma vānaraḥ
|
kadalīṣaṇḍamadhyastho nidrāvaśagatastadā||69||

70jṛmbhamāṇaḥ suvipulaṃ śakradhvajamivocchritam
|
āsphoṭayacca lāṅgūlamindrāśanisamasvanam||70||

71tasya lāṅgūlaninadaṃ parvataḥ suguhāmukhaiḥ
|
udgāramiva gaurnardannutsasarja samantataḥ||71||

72lāṅgūlāsphoṭaśabdācca calitaḥ sa mahāgiriḥ
|
vighūrṇamānaśikharaḥ samantātparyaśīryata||72||

73sa lāṅgūlaravastasya mattavāraṇaniḥsvanam
|
antardhāya vicitreṣu cacāra girisānuṣu||73||

74sa bhīmasenastacchrutvā saṃprahṛṣṭatanūruhaḥ
|
śabdaprabhavamanvicchaṃścacāra kadalīvanam||74||

75kadalīvanamadhyasthamatha pīne śilātale
|
dadarśa sumahābāhurvānarādhipatiṃ tadā||75||

76vidyutsaṃpātaduṣprekṣaṃ vidyutsaṃpātapiñcalam
|
vidyutsaṃpātaninadaṃ vidyutsaṃpātacañcalam||76||

77bāhusvastikavinyastapīnahrasvaśirodharam
|
skandhabhūyiṣṭhakāyatvāttanumadhyakaṭītaṭam||77||

78kiṃciccābhugnaśīrṣeṇa dīrgharomāñcitena ca
|
lāṅgūlenordhvagatinā dhvajeneva virājitam||78||

79hrasvauṣṭhaṃ tāmrajihvāsyaṃ raktavarṇaṃ caladbhruvam
|
vivṛttadaṃṣṭrādaśanaṃ śuklatīkṣṇāgraśobhitam||79||

80apaśyadvadanaṃ tasya raśmivantamivoḍupam
|
vadanābhyantaragataiḥ śuklairdantairalaṃkṛtam||80||

81kesarotkarasaṃmiśramaśokānāmivotkaram
|
hiraṇmayīnāṃ madhyasthaṃ kadalīnāṃ mahādyutim||81||

82dīpyamānena vapuṣā svarciṣmantamivānalam
|
nirīkṣantamamitraghnaṃ locanairmadhupiṅgalaiḥ||82||

83taṃ vānaravaraṃ dhīmānatikāyaṃ mahābalam
|
svargapanthānamāvṛtya himavantamiva sthitam||83||

84dṛṣṭvā cainaṃ mahābāhurekaṃ tasminmahāvane
|
athopasṛtya tarasā vibhīrbhīmastato balī||84||

85siṃhanādaṃ cakārograṃ vajrāśanisamaṃ balī
|
tena śabdena bhīmasya vitresurmṛgapakṣiṇaḥ||85||

86hanūmāṃśca mahāsattva īṣadunmīlya locane
|
dṛṣṭvā tamatha sāvajñaṃ locanairmadhupiṅgalaiḥ
|
smitena cainamāsādya hanūmānidamabravīt||86||

87hanumānuvāca|
kimarthaṃ sarujaste'haṃ sukhasuptaḥ prabodhitaḥ
|
nanu nāma tvayā kāryā dayā bhūteṣu jānatā||87||

88vayaṃ dharmaṃ na jānīmastiryagyonimupāśritāḥ
|
narāstu buddhisaṃpannā dayāṃ kurvanti jantuṣu||88||

89krūreṣu karmasu kathaṃ dehavākcittadūṣiṣu
|
dharmaghātiṣu sajjante buddhimanto bhavadvidhāḥ||89||

90na tvaṃ dharmaṃ vijānāsi budhā nopāsitāstvayā
|
alpabuddhitayā bālyādutsādayasi yanmṛgān||90||

91brūhi kastvaṃ kimarthaṃ vā kimidaṃ vanamāgataḥ
|
varjitaṃ mānuṣairbhāvaistathaiva puruṣairapi||91||

92kva ca tvayādya gantavyaṃ prabrūhi puruṣarṣabha
|
ataḥ paramagamyo'yaṃ parvataḥ sudurāruhaḥ||92||

93vinā siddhagatiṃ vīra gatiratra na vidyate
|
devalokasya mārgo'yamagamyo mānuṣaiḥ sadā||93||

94kāruṇyāttvāmahaṃ vīra vārayāmi nibodha me
|
nātaḥparaṃ tvayā śakyaṃ gantumāśvasihi prabho||94||

95svāgataṃ sarvathaiveha tavādya manujarṣabha
|
imānyamṛtakalpāni mūlāni ca phalāni||95||

96bhakṣayitvā nivartasva mā vṛthā prāpsyase vadham
|
grāhyaṃ yadi vaco mahyaṃ hitaṃ manujapuṅgava||96||
