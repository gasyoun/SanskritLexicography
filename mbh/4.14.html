Mahābhārata 4.14 (Virāṭaparvan)

1vaiśampāyana uvāca|vasamāneṣu pārtheṣu matsyasya nagare tadā
|
mahāratheṣu channeṣu māsā daśa samāyayuḥ ||1||

2yājñasenī sudeṣṇāṃ tu śuśrūṣantī viśāmpate
|
āvasat paricārārhā suduḥkhaṃ janamejaya ||2||

3tathā carantī pāñcālī sudeṣṇāyā niveśane
|
tāṃ devīṃ toṣayāmāsa tathā cāntaḥpurastriyaḥ ||3||

4tasmin varṣe gataprāye kīcakastu mahābalaḥ
|
senā patirvirāṭasya dadarśa drupadātmajām ||4||

5tāṃ dṛṣṭvā devagarbhābhāṃ carantīṃ devatāmiva
|
kīcakaḥ kāmayāmāsa kāmabāṇaprapīḍitaḥ ||5||

6sa tu kāmāgnisaṃtaptaḥ sudeṣṇāmabhigamya vai
|
prahasanniva senānīridaṃ vacanamabravīt ||6||

7neyaṃ mayā jātu pureha dṛṣṭā rājño virāṭasya niveśane śubhā
|
rūpeṇa conmādayatīva māṃ bhṛśaṃ gandhena jātā madireva bhāminī ||7||

8kā devarūpā hṛdayaṅgamā śubhe hyācakṣva me kasya kuto'tra śobhane
|
cittaṃ hi nirmathya karoti māṃ vaśe na cānyadatrauṣadhamasti me matam ||8||

9aho taveyaṃ paricārikā śubhā pratyagrarūpā pratibhāti māmiyam
|
ayuktarūpaṃ hi karoti karma te praśāstu māṃ yacca mamāsti kiñcana ||9||

10prabhūtanāgāśvarathaṃ mahājanaṃ samṛddhiyuktaṃ bahupānabhojanam
|
manoharaṃ kāñcanacitrabhūṣaṇaṃ gṛhaṃ mahacchobhayatāmiyaṃ mama ||10||

11tataḥ sudeṣṇāmanumantrya kīcakastataḥ samabhyetya narādhipātmajām
|
uvāca kṛṣṇāmabhisāntvayaṃstadā mṛgendrakanyāmiva jambuko vane ||11||

12kā tvaṃ kasyāsi kalyāṇi kuto vā tvaṃ varānane
|
prāptā virāṭanagaraṃ tattvamācakṣva śobhane ||12||

13rūpamagryaṃ tathā kāntiḥ saukumāryamanuttamam
|
kāntyā vibhāti vaktraṃ te śaśāṅka iva nirmalam ||13||

14netre suvipule subhru padmapatranibhe śubhe
|
vākyaṃ te cārusarvāṅgi parapuṣṭarutopamam ||14||

15evaṃrūpā mayā nārī kācidanyā mahītale
|
na dṛṣṭapūrvā suśroṇi yādṛśī tvamanindite ||15||

16lakṣmīḥ padmālayā kā tvamatha bhūtiḥ sumadhyame
|
hrīḥ śrīḥ kīrtiratho kāntirāsāṃ kā tvaṃ varānane ||16||

17atīva rūpiṇī kiṃ tvamanaṅgāṅgavihāriṇī
|
atīva bhrājase subhru prabhevendoranuttamā ||17||

18api cekṣaṇapakṣmāṇāṃ smitajyotsnopamaṃ śubham
|
divyāṃśuraśmibhirvṛttaṃ divyakāntimanoramam ||18||

19nirīkṣya vaktracandraṃ te lakṣmyā'nupamayā yutam
|
kṛtsne jagati ko neha kāmasya vaśago bhavet ||19||

20hārālaṅkārayogyau tu stanau cobhau suśobhanau
|
sujātau sahitau lakṣmyā pīnau vṛttau nirantarau ||20||

21kuḍmalāmburuhākārau tava subhru payodharau
|
kāmapratodāviva māṃ tudataścāruhāsini ||21||

22valīvibhaṅgacaturaṃ stanabhāravināmitam
|
karāgrasaṃmitaṃ madhyaṃ tavedaṃ tanumadhyame ||22||

23dṛṣṭvaiva cāru jaghanaṃ saritpulinasaṃnibham
|
kāmavyādhirasādhyo māmapyākrāmati bhāmini ||23||

24jajvāla cāgnimadano dāvāgniriva nirdayaḥ
|
tvatsaṅgamābhisaṅkalpavivṛddho māṃ dahatyayam ||24||

25ātmapradānavarṣeṇa saṃgamāmbhodhareṇa ca
|
śamayasva varārohe jvalantaṃ manmathānalam ||25||

26maccittonmādanakarā manmathasya śarotkarāḥ
|
tvatsaṃgamāśāniśitāstīvrāḥ śaśinibhānane
|
mahyaṃ vidārya hṛdayamidaṃ nirdayavegitāḥ ||26||

27praviṣṭā hyasitāpāṅgi pracaṇḍāścaṇḍadāruṇāḥ
|
atyunmādasamārambhāḥ prītyunmādakarā mama
|
ātmapradānasambhogairmāmuddhartumihārhasi ||27||

28citramālyāmbaradharā sarvābharaṇabhūṣitā
|
kāmaṃ prakāmaṃ seva tvaṃ mayā saha vilāsini ||28||

29nārhasīhāsukhaṃ vastuṃ sukhārhā sukhavarjitā
|
prāpnuhyanuttamaṃ saukhyaṃ mattastvaṃ mattagāmini ||29||

30svādūnyamṛtakalpāni peyāni vividhāni ca
|
pibamānā manojñāni ramamāṇā yathāsukham ||30||

31bhogopacārān ‌vividhān saubhāgyaṃ cāpyanuttamam
|
pānaṃ piba mahābhāge bhogaiścānuttamaiḥ śubhaiḥ ||31||

32idaṃ hi rūpaṃ prathamaṃ tavānaghe nirarthakaṃ kevalamadya bhāmini
|
adhāryamāṇā sragivottamā śubhā na śobhase sundari śobhanā satī ||32||

33tyajāmi dārān mama ye purātanā bhavantu dāsyastava cāruhāsini
|
ahaṃ ca te sundari dāsavat sthitaḥ sadā bhaviṣye vaśago varānane ||33||

34draupadyuvāca
|
aprārthanīyāmiha māṃ sūtaputrābhimanyase
|
nihīnavarṇāṃ sairandhrīṃ bībhatsāṃ keśakāriṇīm ||34||

35paradārā'smi bhadraṃ te na yuktaṃ tava sāmpratam
|
dayitāḥ prāṇināṃ dārā dharmaṃ samanucintaya ||35||

36paradāre na te buddhirjātu kāryā kathaṃcana
|
vivarjanaṃ hyakāryāṇāmetat supuruṣavratam ||36||

37mithyābhigṛdhno hi naraḥ pāpātmā mohamāsthitaḥ
|
ayaśaḥ prāpnuyādghoraṃ mahadvā prāpnuyādbhayam ||37||

38vaiśampāyana uvāca|evamuktastu sairandhryā kīcakaḥ kāmamohitaḥ
|
jānannapi sudurbuddhiḥ paradārābhimarśane ||38||

39doṣān bahūn prāṇaharān sarvalokavigarhitān
|
provācedaṃ sudurbuddhirdraupadīmajitendriyaḥ ||39||

40nārhasyevaṃ varārohe pratyākhyātuṃ varānane
|
māṃ manmathasamāviṣṭaṃ tvatkṛte cāruhāsini ||40||

41pratyākhyāya ca māṃ bhīru vaśagaṃ priyavādinam
|
nūnaṃ tvamasitāpāṅgi paścāttāpaṃ kariṣyasi ||41||

42ahaṃ hi subhrū rājyasya kṛtsnasyāsya sumadhyame
|
prabhurvāsayitā caiva vīrye cāpratimaḥ kṣitau ||42||

43pṛthivyāṃ matsamo nāsti kaścidanyaḥ pumāniha
|
rūpayauvanasaubhāgyairbhogaiścānuttamaiḥ śubhaiḥ ||43||

44sarvakāmasamṛddheṣu bhogeṣvanupameṣviha
|
bhoktavyeṣu ca kalyāṇi kasmāddāsye ratā hyasi ||44||

45mayā dattamidaṃ rājyaṃ svāminyasi śubhānane
|
bhajasva māṃ varārohe bhuṅkṣva bhogānanuttamān ||45||

46evamuktā tu sā sādhvī kīcakenāśubhaṃ vacaḥ
|
kīcakaṃ pratyuvācedaṃ garhayantyasya tadvacaḥ ||46||

47sairandhyuvāca
|
mā sūtaputra muhyasva mā'dya tyakṣyasva jīvitam
|
jānīhi pañcabhirghorairnityaṃ māmabhirakṣitām ||47||

48na cāpyahaṃ tvayā labhyā gandharvāḥ patayo mama
|
te tvāṃ nihanyuḥ kupitāḥ sādhvalaṃ mā vyanīnaśaḥ ||48||

49aśakyarūpaṃ puruṣairadhvānaṃ gantumicchasi
|
yathā niścetano vālaḥ kūlasthaḥ kūlamuttaram
|
tartumicchati mandātmā tathā tvaṃ kartumicchasi ||49||

50antarmahīṃ vā yadi vordhvamutpateḥ samudrapāraṃ yadi vā pradhāvasi
|
tathā'pi teṣāṃ na vimokṣamarhasi pramāthino devasutā hi khecarāḥ ||50||

51tvaṃ kālarātrīmiva kaścidāturaḥ kiṃ māṃ dṛḍhaṃ prārthayase'dya kīcaka
|
kiṃ māturaṅke śayito yathā śiśuścandraṃ jighṛkṣuriva manyase hi mām ||51||

52teṣāṃ priyāṃ prārthayato na te bhuvi gatvā divaṃ vā śaraṇaṃ bhaviṣyati
|
na vartate kīcaka te dṛśā śubhaṃ yā tena saṃjīvanamarthayeta sā ||52||
