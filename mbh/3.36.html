Mahābhārata 3.36 (Āraṇyakaparvan)

1vaiśaṃpāyana uvāca|
bhīmasenavacaḥ śrutvā kuntīputro yudhiṣṭhiraḥ
|
niḥśvasya puruṣavyāghraḥ saṃpradadhyau parantapaḥ||1||

2śrutā me rājadharmāśca varṇānāṃ ca viniścayāḥ
|
āyatyāṃ ca tadātve ca yaḥ paśyati sa paśyati||2||

3dharmasya jānamāno'haṃ gatimagryāṃ sudurvidām
|
kathaṃ balātkariṣyāmi meroriva vimardanam||3||

4sa muhūrtamiva dhyātvā viniścityetikṛtyatām
|
bhīmasenamidaṃ vākyamapadāntaramabravīt||4||

5yudhiṣṭhira uvāca|
evametanmahābāho yathā vadasi bhārata
|
idamanyatsamādatsva vācyaṃ me vācyakovida||5||

6mahāpāpāni karmāṇi yāni kevalasāhasāt
|
ārabhyante bhīmasena vyathante tāni bhārata||6||

7sumantrite suvikrānte sukṛte suvicārite
|
sidhyantyarthā mahābāho daivaṃ cātra pradakṣiṇam||7||

8yattu kevalacāpalyādbaladarpo'tthitaḥ svayam
|
ārabdhavyamidaṃ kāryaṃ manyase śṛṇu tatra me||8||

9bhūriśravāḥ śalaścaiva jalasandhaśca vīryavān
|
bhīṣmo droṇaśca karṇaśca droṇaputraśca vīryavān||9||

10dhārtarāṣṭrā durādharṣā duryodhanapurogamāḥ
|
sarva eva kṛtāstrāśca satataṃ cātatāyinaḥ||10||

11rājānaḥ pārthivāścaiva ye'smābhirupatāpitāḥ
|
saṃśritāḥ kauravaṃ pakṣaṃ jātasnehāśca taṃ prati||11||

12duryodhanahite yuktā na tathāsmāsu bhārata
|
pūrṇakośā balopetāḥ prayatiṣyanti saṅgare||12||

13sarve kauravasainyasya saputrāmātyasainikāḥ
|
saṃvibhaktā hi mātrābhirbhogairapi ca sarvaśaḥ||13||

14duryodhanena te vīrā mānitāśca viśeṣataḥ
|
prāṇāṃstyakṣyanti saṅgrāme iti me niścitā matiḥ||14||

15samā yadyapi bhīṣmasya vṛttirasmāsu teṣu ca
|
droṇasya ca mahābāho kṛpasya ca mahātmanaḥ||15||

16avaśyaṃ rājapiṇḍastairnirveśya iti me matiḥ
|
tasmāttyakṣyanti saṅgrāme prāṇānapi sudustyajān||16||

17sarve divyāstravidvāṃsaḥ sarve dharmaparāyaṇāḥ
|
ajeyāśceti me buddhirapi devaiḥ savāsavaiḥ||17||

18amarṣī nityasaṃrabdhastatra karṇo mahārathaḥ
|
sarvāstravidanādhṛṣyo hyamedyakavacāvṛtaḥ||18||

19anirjitya raṇe sarvānetānpuruṣasattamān
|
aśakyo hyasahāyena hantuṃ duryodhanastvayā||19||

20na nidrāmadhigacchāmi cintayāno vṛkodara
|
atisarvāndhanurgāhānsūtaputrasya lāghavam||20||

21vaiśaṃpāyana uvāca|
etadvacanamājñāya bhīmasenotyamarṣaṇaḥ
|
babhūva vimanāstrasto na caivovāca kiñcana||21||

22tayoḥ saṃvadatorevaṃ tadā pāṇḍavayordvayoḥ
|
ājagāma mahāyogī vyāsaḥ satyavatīsutaḥ||22||

23so'bhigamya yathānyāyaṃ pāṇḍavaiḥ pratipūjitaḥ
|
yudhiṣṭhiramidaṃ vākyamuvāca vadatāṃ varaḥ||23||

24vyāsa uvāca|
yudhiṣṭhira mahābāho vedmi te hṛdayasthitam
|
manīṣayā tataḥ kṣipramāgatorima nararṣabha||24||

25bhīṣmāddroṇātkṛpātkarṇādroṇaputrācca bhārata
|
duryodhanānnṛpasutāttathā duḥśāsanādapi||25||

26yatte bhayamamitraghnna hṛdi samparivartate
|
tatte'haṃ nāśayiṣyāmi vidhidṛṣṭena karmaṇā||26||

27tacchrutvā dhṛtimāsthāya karmaṇā pratipādaya
|
pratipādya tu rājendra tataḥ kṣipraṃ jvaraṃ jahi||27||

28tata ekāntamunnīya pārāśaryo yudhiṣṭhiram
|
abravīdupapannārthamidaṃ vākyaviśāradaḥ||28||

29śreyasaste paraḥ kālaḥ prāpto bharatasattama
|
yenābhibhavitā śatrūnraṇe pārtho dhanurdharaḥ||29||

30gṛhāṇemāṃ mayā proktāṃ siddhiṃ mūrtimatīmiva
|
vidyāṃ pratismṛtiṃ nāma prapannāya bravīmi te||30||

31yāmavāpya mahābāhurarjunaḥ sādhayiṣyati
|
astrahetormahendraṃ ca rudraṃ caivābhigacchatu||31||

32varuṇaṃ ca kuberaṃ ca dharmarājaṃ ca pāṇḍava
|
śakto hyeṣa surāndraṣṭuṃ tapasā vikrameṇa ca||32||

33ṛṣireṣa mahātejā nārāyaṇasahāyavān
|
purāṇaḥ śāśvato devastvajeyo jiṣṇuracyutaḥ||33||

34astrāṇīndrācca rudrācca lokapālebhya eva ca
|
samādāya mahābāhurmahatkarma kariṣyati||34||

35vanādasmācca kaunteya vanamanyadvicintyatām
|
nivāsārthāya yadyuktaṃ bhavedvaḥ pṛthivīpate||35||

36ekatra ciravāso hi na prītijanano bhavet
|
tāpasānāṃ ca sarveṣāṃ bhavedudvegakārakaḥ||36||

37mṛgāṇāmupayogaśca vīrudauṣadhisaṃkṣayaḥ
|
bibharṣi ca bahūnviprānvedavedāṅgapāragān||37||

38vaiśaṃpāyana uvāca|
evamuktvā prapannāya śucaye bhagavānprabhuḥ
|
provāca lokatattvajño yogī vidyāmanuttamām||38||

39dharmarājāya dhīmānsa vyāsaḥ satyavatīsutaḥ
|
anujñāya ca kaunteyaṃ tatraivāntaradhīyata||39||

40yudhiṣṭhirastu dharmātmā tadbrahma manasā yataḥ
|
dhārayāmāsa medhāvī kāle kāle sadā'bhyasan||40||

41sa vyāsavākyanudito vanāddvaitavanāttataḥ
|
yayau sarasvatīkūle kāmyakaṃ nāma kānanam||41||

42tamanvayurmahārāja śikṣākṣaraviśāradāḥ
|
brāhmaṇāstapasā yuktā devendramṛṣayo yathā||42||

43tataḥ kāmyakamāsādya punaste bharatarṣabha
|
nyaviśanta mahātmānaḥ sāmātyāḥ saparicchadāḥ||43||

44tatra te nyavasanrājankiñcitkālaṃ manasvinaḥ
|
dhanurvedaparā vīrāḥ śṛṇvanto vedamuttamam||44||

45caranto mṛgayāṃ nityaṃ śuddhairbāṇairmṛgārthinaḥ
|
pitṛdaivataviprebhyo nirvapanto yathāvidhi||45||
