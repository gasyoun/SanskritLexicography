Mahābhārata 5.162 (Udyogaparvan)

1sañjaya uvāca|
ulūkastvarjunaṃ bhūyo yathoktaṃ vākyamabravīt
|
āśīviṣamiva kruddhaṃ tudan vākyaśalākayā ||1||

2tasya tadvacanaṃ śrutvā ruṣitāḥ pāṇḍavā bhṛśam
|
prāgeva bhṛśasaṃkruddhāḥ kaitavyenāpi dharṣitāḥ ||2||

3āsaneṣūdatiṣṭhanta bāhuṃścaiva pracikṣipuḥ
|
āśīviṣā iva kruddhā vīkṣāṃcakuḥ parasparam ||3||

4avākśirā bhīmasenaḥ samudaikṣata keśavam
|
netrābhyāṃ lohitāntābhyāmāśīviṣa iva śvasan ||4||

5ārtaṃ vātātmajaṃ dṛṣṭvā krodhenābhihataṃ bhṛśam
|
utsmayanniva dāśārhaḥ kaitavyaṃ pratyabhāṣata ||5||

6prayāhi śīghraṃ kaitavyaṃ brūyāścaiva suyodhanam
|
śrutaṃ vākyaṃ gṛhīto'rthoṃ mataṃ yatte tathā'stu tat||6||

7evamuktvā mahābāhuḥ keśavo rājasattama
|
punareva mahāprājñaṃ yudhiṣṭhiramudaikṣata ||7||

8sṛñjayānāṃ ca sarveṣāṃ kṛṣṇasya ca yaśasvinaḥ
|
drupadasya saputrasya virāṭasya ca sannidhau ||8||

9bhūmipānāṃ ca sarveṣāṃ madhye vākyaṃ jagāda ha
|
ulūko'pyarjunaṃ bhūyo yathoktaṃ vākyamabravīt ||9||

10āśīviṣamiva kruddhaṃ tudan vākyaśalākayā
|
kṛṣṇādīṃścaiva tān sarvān yathoktaṃ vākyamabravīt||10||

11ulūkasya tu tadvākyaṃ pāpaṃ dāruṇamīritam
|
śrutvā vicukṣubhe pārtho lalāṭaṃ cāpyamārjayat ||11||

12tadavasthaṃ tadā dṛṣṭvā pārthaṃ sā samitirnṛpa
|
nāmṛṣyanta mahārāja pāṇḍavānāṃ mahārathāḥ ||12||

13adhikṣepeṇa kṛṣṇasya pārthasya ca mahātmanaḥ
|
śrutvā te puruṣavyāghrāḥ krodhājjajvaluracyuta ||13||

14dhṛṣṭadyumnaḥ śikhaṇḍī ca sātyakiśca mahārathaḥ
|
kekayā bhrātaraḥ pañca rākṣasaśca ghaṭotkacaḥ ||14||

15draupadeyābhimanyuśca dhṛṣṭaketuśca pārthivaḥ
|
bhīmasenaśca vikrānto yamajau ca mahārathau ||15||

16utpeturāsanāt sarve krodhasaṃraktalocanāḥ
|
bāhūn pragṛhya rucirān raktacandanarūṣitān
|
aṅgadaiḥ pārihāryaiśca keyūraiśca vibhūṣitān ||16||

17dantān danteṣu niṣpiṣya sṛkkiṇī parilelihan
|
teṣāmākārabhāvajñaḥ kuntīputro vṛkodaraḥ ||17||

18udatiṣṭhat sa vegena krodhena prajvalanniva
|
uddhṛtya sahasā netre dantān kaṭakaṭāyya ca ||18||

19hastaṃ hastena niṣpiṣya ulūkaṃ vākyamabravīt
|
aśaktānāmivāsmākaṃ protsāhananimittakam ||19||

20śrutaṃ te vacanaṃ mūrkha yattvāṃ duryodhano'bravīt
|
tanme kathayato manda śṛṇu vākyaṃ durāsadam ||20||

21sarvakṣatrasya madhyaṃ taṃ yadvakṣyasi suyodhanam
|
śṛṇvataḥ sūtaputrasya pituśca tvaṃ durātmanaḥ ||21||

22asmābhiḥ prītikāmaistu bhrāturjyeṣṭhasya nityaśaḥ
|
marṣitaṃ te durācāra tattvaṃ na bahu manyase ||22||

23preṣitaśca hṛṣīkeśaḥ śamākāṅkṣī kurūn prati
|
kulasya hitakāmena dharmarājena dhīmatā ||23||

24tvaṃ kālacodito nūnaṃ gantukāmo yamakṣayam
|
gacchasvāhavamasmābhistacca śvo bhavitā dhruvam ||24||

25mayā'pi ca pratijñāto vadhaḥ sabhrātṛkasya te
|
sa tathā bhavitā pāpa nātra kāryā vicāraṇā ||25||

26velāmatikramet sadyaḥ sāgaro varuṇālayaḥ
|
parvatāśca viśīryeyurmayoktaṃ na mṛṣā bhavet ||26||

27sahāyaste yadi yamaḥ kubero rudra eva vā
|
yathāpratijñaṃ durbuddhe prakariṣyanti pāṇḍavāḥ
|
duḥśāsanasya rudhiraṃ pātā cāsmi yathepsitam ||27||

28yaśceha pratisaṃrabdhaḥ kṣatriyo mā'bhiyāsyati
|
api bhīṣmaṃ puraskṛtya taṃ neṣyāmi yamakṣayam ||28||

29yaccaitaduktaṃ vacanaṃ mayā kṣatrasya saṃsadi
|
yathaitadbhavitā satyaṃ tathaivātmānamālabhe ||29||

30bhīmasenavacaḥ śrutvā sahadevo'pyamarṣaṇaḥ
|
krodhasaṃraktanayanastato vākyamuvāca ha ||30||

31śauṭīraśūrasadṛśamanīkajanasaṃsadi
|
śṛṇu pāpa vaco mahyaṃ yadvācyo hi pitā tvayā ||31||

32nāsmākaṃ bhavitā bhedaḥ kadācit kurubhiḥ saha
|
dhṛtarāṣṭrasya sambandho yadi na syāttvayā saha ||32||

33tvaṃ tu lokavināśāya dhṛtarāṣṭrakulasya ca
|
utpanno vairapuruṣaḥ svakulaghnaśca pāpakṛt ||33||

34janmaprabhṛti cāsmākaṃ pitā te pāpapūruṣaḥ
|
ahitāni nṛśaṃsāni nityaśaḥ kartumicchati ||34||

35tasya vairānuṣaṅgasya gantā'smyantaṃ sudurgamam
|
ahamādau nihatya tvāṃ śakuneḥ saṃprapaśyataḥ ||35||

36tato'smi śakuniṃ hantāmiṣatāṃ sarvadhanvinām
|
bhīmasya vacanaṃ śrutvā sahadevasya cobhayoḥ ||36||

37uvāca phālguno vākyaṃ bhīmasenaṃ smayānniva
|
bhīmasena na te santi yeṣāṃ vairaṃ tvayā saha ||37||

38mandā gṛheṣu sukhino mṛtyupāśavaśaṃ gatāḥ
|
ulūkaśca na te vācyaḥ paruṣaṃ puruṣottama ||38||

39dūtāḥ kimaparādhyante yathoktasyānubhāṣiṇaḥ
|
evamuktvā mahābāhurbhīmaṃ bhīmaparākramam ||39||

40dhṛṣṭadyumnamukhān vīrān suhṛdaḥ samabhāṣata
|
śrutaṃ vastasya pāpasya dhārtarāṣṭrasya bhāṣitam ||40||

41kutsanaṃ vāsudevasya mama caiva viśeṣataḥ
|
śrutvā bhavantaḥ saṃrabdhā asmākaṃ hitakāmyayā ||41||

42prabhāvādvāsudevasya bhavatāṃ ca prayatnataḥ
|
samagraṃ pārthivaṃ kṣatraṃ sarvaṃ na gaṇayāmyaham ||42||

43bhavadbhiḥ samanujñāto vākyamasya yaduttaram
|
ulūke prāpayiṣyāmi yadvakṣyati suyodhanam ||43||

44śvobhūte katthitasyāsya prativākyaṃ camūmukhe
|
gāṇḍīvenābhidhāsyāmi klībā hi vacanottarāḥ ||44||

45tataste pārthivāḥ sarve praśaśaṃsurdhanañjayam
|
tena vākyopacāreṇa vismitā rājasattamāḥ ||45||

46anunīya ca tān sarvān yathāmānyaṃ yathāvayaḥ
|
dharmarājaṃ tadā vākyaṃ tatprāpyaṃ pratyabhāṣata ||46||

47ātmānamavamanvāno na hi syāt pārthivottamaḥ
|
tatrottaraṃ pravakṣyāmi tava śuśrūṣaṇe rataḥ ||47||

48ulūkaṃ bharataśreṣṭha sāmapūrvamathorjitam
|
duryodhanasya tadvākyaṃ niśamya bharatarṣabhaḥ ||48||

49atilohitanetrābhyāmāśīviṣa iva śvasan
|
smayamāna iva krodhāt sṛkkiṇī parisaṃlihan ||49||

50janārdanamabhiprekṣya bhrātṝṃścaivedamabravīt
|
abhyabhāṣata kaitavyaṃ pragṛhya vipulaṃ bhujam ||50||

51ulūka gaccha kaitavya brūhi tāta suyodhanam
|
kṛtaghnaṃ vairapuruṣaṃ durmatiṃ kulapāṃsanam ||51||

52pāṇḍaveṣu sadā pāpa nityaṃ jihmaṃ pravartate
|
svavīryādyaḥ parākramya pāpa āhvayate parān
|
abhītaḥ pūrayan vākyameṣa vai kṣatriyaḥ pumān ||52||

53sa pāpaḥ kṣatriyo bhūtvā asmānāhūya saṃyuge
|
mānyāmānyān puraskṛtya yuddhaṃ mā gāḥ kulādhama ||53||

54ātmavīryaṃ samāśritya bhṛtyavīryaṃ ca kaurava
|
āhvayasva raṇe pārthān sarvathā kṣatriyo bhava ||54||

55paravīryaṃ samāśritya yaḥ samāhvayate parān
|
aśaktaḥ svayamādātumetadeva napuṃsakam ||55||

56sa tvaṃ pareṣāṃ vīryeṇa ātmānaṃ bahu manyase
|
kathamevamaśaktastvamasmān samabhigarjasi ||56||

57kṛṣṇa uvāca|
madvacaścāpi bhūyaste vaktavyaḥ sa suyodhanaḥ
|
śva idānīṃ prapadyethāḥ puruṣo bhava durmate ||57||

58manyase yacca mūḍha tvaṃ na yotsyati janārdanaḥ
|
sārathyena vṛtaḥ pārthairiti tvaṃ na bibheṣi ca ||58||

59jaghanyakālamapyetanna bhavet sarvapārthivān
|
nirdaheyamahaṃ krodhāt tṛṇānīva hutāśanaḥ ||59||

60yudhiṣṭhiraniyogānnu phālgunasya mahātmanaḥ
|
kariṣye yudhyamānasya sārathyaṃ vijitātmanaḥ ||60||

61yadyutpatasi lokāṃstrīn yadyāviśasi bhūtalam
|
tatra tatrārjunarathaṃ prabhāte drakṣyase punaḥ ||61||

62yaccāpi bhīmasenasya manyase moghabhāṣitam
|
duḥśāsanasya rudhiraṃ pītamadyāvadhāraya ||62||

63na tvāṃ samīkṣate pārtho nāpi rājā yudhiṣṭhiraḥ
|
na bhīmaseno na yamau pratikūlaprabhāṣiṇam ||63||
