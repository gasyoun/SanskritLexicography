Mahābhārata 6.43 (Bhīṣmaparvan)

1vaiśampāyana uvāca|
gītā sugītā kartavyā kimanyaiḥ śāstrasaṅgrahaiḥ
|
yā svayaṃ padmanābhasya mukhapadmādviniḥsṛtā ||1||

2sarvaśāstramayī gītā sarvadevamayo hariḥ
|
sarvatīrthamayī gaṅgā sarvavedamayo manuḥ ||2||

3gītā gaṅgā ca gāyatrī govindeti hṛdi sthite
|
caturgakārasaṃyukte punarjanma na vidyate ||3||

4ṣaṭśatāni saviṃśāni ślokānāṃ prāha keśavaḥ
|
arjunaḥ saptapañcāśatsaptaṣaṣṭiṃ tu sañjayaḥ ||4||

5dhṛtarāṣṭraḥ ślokamekaṃ gītāyā mānamucyate
|
bhāratāmṛtasarvasvagītāyā mathitasya ca
|
sāramuddhṛtya kṛṣṇena arjunasya mukhe hutam ||5||

6sañjaya uvāca|
tato dhanañjayaṃ dṛṣṭvā bāṇagāṇḍīvadhāriṇam
|
punareva mahānādaṃ vyasṛjanta mahārathāḥ ||6||

7pāṇḍavāḥ somakāścaiva ye caiṣāmanuyāyinaḥ
|
dadhmuśca muditāḥ śaṅkhān vīrāḥ sāgarasambhavān ||7||

8tato bheryaśca peśyaśca krakacā goviṣāṇikāḥ
|
sahasaivābhyahanyanta tataḥ śabdo mahānabhūt ||8||

9tathā devāḥ sagandharvāḥ pitaraśca janādhipa
|
siddhacāraṇasa‌ṅghāśca samīyuste didṛkṣayā ||9||

10ṛṣayaśca mahābhāgāḥ puraskṛtya śatakratum
|
samīyustatra sahitā draṣṭuṃ tadvaiśasaṃ mahat ||10||

11tato yudhiṣṭhiro dṛṣṭvā yuddhāya samavasthite
|
te sene sāgaraprakhye muhuḥ pracalite nṛpa ||11||

12vimucya kavacaṃ vīro nikṣipya ca varāyudham
|
avaruhya rathātkṣipraṃ padbhyāmeva kṛtāñjaliḥ ||12||

13pitāmahamabhiprekṣya dharmarājo yudhiṣṭhiraḥ
|
vāgyataḥ prayayau yena prāṅmukho ripuvāhinīm ||13||

14taṃ prayāntamabhiprekṣya kuntīputro dhanañjayaḥ
|
avatīrya rathāttūrṇaṃ bhrātṛbhiḥ sahito'nvayāt ||14||

15vāsudevaśca bhagavān pṛṣṭhato'nujagāma tam
|
tathā mukhyāśca rājānastaccittā jagmurutsukāḥ ||15||

16arjuna uvāca|
kiṃ te vyavasitaṃ rājan yadasmānapahāya vai
|
padbhyāmeva prayāto'si prāṅmukho ripuvāhinīm ||16||

17bhīmasena uvāca|
kva gamiṣyasi rājendra nikṣiptakavacāyudhaḥ
|
daṃśiteṣvarisainyeṣu bhrātṝnutsṛjya pārthiva ||17||

18nakula uvāca|
evaṃ gate tvayi jyeṣṭhe mama bhrātari bhārata
|
bhīrme dunoti hṛdayaṃ brūhi gantā bhavān kva nu ||18||

19sahadeva uvāca|
asmin raṇasamūhe vai vartamāne mahābhaye
|
utsṛjya kva nu gantā'si śatrūnabhimukho nṛpa ||19||

20sañjaya uvāca|
evamābhāṣyamāṇo'pi bhrātṛbhiḥ kurunandanaḥ
|
novāca vāgyataḥ kiñcidgacchatyeva yudhiṣṭhiraḥ ||20||

21tānuvāca mahāprājño vāsudevo mahāmanāḥ
|
abhiprāyo'sya vijñāto mayeti prahasanniva ||21||

22eṣa bhīṣmaṃ tathā droṇaṃ gautamaṃ śalyameva ca
|
anumānya gurūn sarvān yotsyate pārthivo'ribhiḥ ||22||

23śrūyate hi purākalpe gurūnananumānya yaḥ
|
yudhyate sa bhavedvyaktamapadhyāto mahattaraiḥ ||23||

24anumānya yathāśāstraṃ yastu yudhyenmahattaraiḥ
|
dhruvastasya jayo yuddhe bhavediti matirmama ||24||

25evaṃ bruvati kṛṣṇe'tra dhārtarāṣṭracamūṃ prati
|
hāhākāro mahānāsīnniḥśabdāstvapare'bhavan ||25||

26dṛṣṭvā yudhiṣṭhiraṃ dūrāddhārtarāṣṭrasya sainikāḥ
|
mithaḥ saṅkathayāñcakrureṣo hi kulapāṃsanaḥ ||26||

27vyaktaṃ bhīta ivābhyeti rājā'sau bhīṣmamantikam
|
yudhiṣṭhiraḥ sasodaryaḥ śaraṇārthaṃ prayācakaḥ ||27||

28dhanañjaye kathaṃ nāthe pāṇḍave ca vṛkodare
|
nakule sahadeve ca bhītirabhyeti pāṇḍavam ||28||

29na nūnaṃ kṣatriyakule jātaḥ saṃprathite bhuvi
|
yathā'sya hṛdayaṃ bhītamalpasattvasya saṃyuge ||29||

30tataste sainikāḥ sarve praśaṃsanti sma kauravān
|
hṛṣṭāḥ sumanaso bhūtvā cailāni dudhuvuśca ha ||30||

31vyanidaṃśca tathā sarve yodhāstava viśāṃpate
|
yudhiṣṭhiraṃ sasodaryaṃ sahitaṃ keśavena hi ||31||

32tatastatkauravaṃ sainyaṃ dhikkṛtvā tu yudhiṣṭhiram
|
niḥśabdamabhavattūrṇaṃ punareva viśāṃpate ||32||

33kiṃ nu vakṣyati rājā'sau kiṃ bhīṣmaḥ prativakṣyati
|
kiṃ bhīmaḥ samaraślāghī kiṃ nu kṛṣṇārjunāviti ||33||

34vivakṣitaṃ kimasyeti saṃśayaḥ sumahānabhūt
|
ubhayoḥ senayo rājan yudhiṣṭhirakṛte tadā ||34||

35so'vagāhya camūṃ śatroḥ śaraśaktisamākulām
|
bhīṣmamevābhyayāttūrṇaṃ bhrātṛbhiḥ parivāritaḥ ||35||

36tamuvāca tataḥ pādau karābhyāṃ pīḍya pāṇḍavaḥ
|
bhīṣmaṃ śāntanavaṃ rājā yuddhāya samupasthitam ||36||

37yudhiṣṭhira uvāca|
āmantraye tvāṃ durdharṣa tvayā yotsyāmahe saha
|
anujānīhi māṃ tāta āśiṣaśca prayojaya ||37||

38bhīṣma uvāca|yadyevaṃ nābhigacchethā yudhi māṃ pṛthivīpate
|
śapeyaṃ tvāṃ mahārāja parābhāvāya bhārata ||38||

39prīto'haṃ putra yudhyasva jayamāpnuhi pāṇḍava
|
yatte'bhilaṣitaṃ cānyattadavāpnuhi saṃyuge ||39||

40vriyatāṃ ca varaḥ pārtha kimasmatto'bhikāṃkṣasi
|
evaṃgate mahārāja na tavāsti parājayaḥ ||40||

41arthasya puruṣo dāso dāsastvartho na kasyacit
|
iti satyaṃ mahārāja baddhosmyarthena kauravaiḥ ||41||

42atastvāṃ klībavadvākyaṃ bravīmi kurunandana
|
bhṛto'smyarthena kauravya yuddhādanyatkimicchasi ||42||

43yudhiṣṭhira uvāca|mantrayasva mahābāho hitaiṣī mama nityaśaḥ
|
yudhyasva kauravasyārthe mamaiṣa satataṃ varaḥ ||43||

44bhīṣma uvāca|rājankimatra sāhyaṃ te karomi kurunandana
|
kāmaṃ yotsye parasyārthe brūhi yatte vivakṣitam ||44||

45yudhiṣṭhira uvāca|
kathaṃ jayeyaṃ saṅgrāme bhavantamaparājitam
|
etanme mantraya hitaṃ yadi śreyaḥ prapaśyasi ||45||

46bhīṣma uvāca|
nainaṃ paśyāmi kaunteya yo māṃ yudhyantamāhave
|
vijayeta pumān kaścitsākṣādapi śatakratuḥ ||46||

47yudhiṣṭhira uvāca|hanta pṛcchāmi tasmāttvāṃ pitāmaha namo'stu te
|
vadhopāyaṃ bravīhi tvamātmanaḥ samare paraiḥ ||47||

48bhīṣma uvāca|na sma taṃ tāta paśyāmi samare yo jayeta mām
|
na tāvanmṛtyukālo'pi punarāgamanaṃ kuru ||48||

49sañjaya uvāca|tato yudhiṣṭhiro vākyaṃ bhīṣmasya kurunandana
|
śirasā pratijagrāha bhūyastamabhivādya ca ||49||

50prāyāt punarmahābāhurācāryasya rathaṃ prati
|
paśyatāṃ sarvasainyānāṃ madhyena bhrātṛbhiḥ saha ||50||

51sa droṇamabhivādyātha kṛtvā cābhipradakṣiṇam
|
uvāca rājā durdharṣamātmaniḥśreyasaṃ vacaḥ ||51||

52āmantraye tvāṃ bhagavan yotsye vigatakalmaṣaḥ
|
kathaṃ jaye ripūn sarvānanujñātastvayā dvija ||52||

53droṇa uvāca|yadi māṃ nābhigacchethā yuddhāya kṛtaniścayaḥ
|
śapeyaṃ tvāṃ mahārāja parābhāvāya sarvaśaḥ ||53||

54tadyudhiṣṭhira tuṣṭo'smi pūjitaśca tvayā'nagha
|
anujānāmi yudhyasva vijayaṃ samavāpnuhi ||54||

55karavāṇi ca te kāmaṃ brūhi tvamabhikāṅkṣitam
|
evaṃgate mahārāja yuddhādanyatkimicchasi ||55||

56arthasya puruṣo dāso dāsastvartho na kasyacit
|
iti satyaṃ mahārāja baddho'smyarthena kauravaiḥ ||56||

57bravīmyetatklībavattvāṃ yuddhādanyatkimicchasi
|
yotsye'haṃ kauravasyārthe tavāśāsyo jayo mayā ||57||

58yudhiṣṭhira uvāca|jayamāśāsva me brahman mantrayasva ca maddhitam
|
yuddhyasva kauravasyārthe vara eṣa vṛto mayā ||58||

59droṇa uvāca|dhruvaste vijayo rājan yasya mantrī haristava
|
ahaṃ tvāmabhijānāmi raṇe śatrūn vimokṣyase ||59||

60yato dharmastataḥ kṛṣṇo yataḥ kṛṣṇastasto jayaḥ
|
yuddhyasva gaccha kaunteya pṛccha māṃ kiṃ bravīmi te ||60||

61yudhiṣṭhira uvāca|pṛcchāmi tvāṃ dvijaśreṣṭha śṛṇu yanme'bhikāṅkṣitam
|
kathaṃ jayeyaṃ saṅgrāme bhavantamaparājitam ||61||

62droṇa uvāca|na te'sti vijayastāvadyāvadyuddhyāmyahaṃ raṇe
|
mamāśu nidhane rājan yatasva saha sodaraiḥ ||62||

63yudhiṣṭhira uvāca|hanta tasmānmahābāho vadhopāyaṃ vadātmanaḥ
|
ācārya praṇipatyaiṣa pṛcchāmi tvāṃ namo'stu te ||63||

64droṇa uvāca|na śatruṃ tāta paśyāmi yo māṃ hanyādrathe sthitam
|
yudhyamānaṃ susaṃrabdhaṃ śaravarṣaughavarṣiṇam ||64||

65ṛte prāyagataṃ rājannyastaśastramacetanam
|
hanyānmāṃ yudhi yodhānāṃ satyametadbravīmi te ||65||

66śastraṃ cāhaṃ raṇe jahyāṃ śrutvā tu mahadapriyam
|
śraddheyavākyātpuruṣādetatsatyaṃ bravīmi te ||66||

67sañjaya uvāca|etacchrutvā mahārāja bhāradvājasya dhīmataḥ
|
anumānya tamācāryaṃ prāyācchāradvataṃ prati ||67||

68so'bhivādya kṛpaṃ rājā kṛtvā cāpi pradakṣiṇam
|
uvāca durdharṣatamaṃ vākyaṃ vākyavidāṃ varaḥ ||68||

69anumānaye tvāṃ yotsye'haṃ guro vigatakalmaṣaḥ
|
jayeyaṃ ca ripūn sarvānanujñātastvayā'nagha ||69||

70kṛpa uvāca|yadi māṃ nābhigacchethā yuddhāya kṛtaniścayaḥ
|
śapeyaṃ tvāṃ mahārāja parābhāvāya sarvaśaḥ ||70||

71arthasya puruṣo dāso dāsastvartho na kasyacit
|
iti satyaṃ mahārāja baddho'smyarthena kauravaiḥ ||71||

72teṣāmarthe mahārāja yoddhavyamiti me matiḥ
|
atastvāṃ klībavadbrūyāṃ yuddhādanyatkimicchasi ||72||

73yudhiṣṭhira uvāca|hanta pṛcchāmi te tasmādācārya śṛṇu me vacaḥ
|
ityuktvā vyathito rājā novāca gatacetanaḥ ||73||

74sañjaya uvāca|taṃ gautamaḥ pratyuvāca vijñāyāsya vivakṣitam
|
avadhyo'haṃ mahīpāla yuddhyasva jayamāpnuhi ||74||

75prītaste'bhigamenāhaṃ jayaṃ tava narādhipa
|
āśāsiṣye sotthāya satyametadbravīmi te ||75||

76etacchrutvā mahārāja gautamasya viśāṃpate
|
anumānya kṛpaṃ rājā prayayau yena madrarāṭ ||76||

77sa śalyamabhivādyātha kṛtvā cābhipradakṣiṇam
|
uvāca rājā durdharṣamātmaniḥśreyasaṃ vacaḥ ||77||

78anumānaye tvāṃ durdharṣa yotsye vigatakalmaṣaḥ
|
jayeyaṃ nu parān rājannanujñātastvayā ripūn ||78||

79śalya uvāca|yadi māṃ nādhigacchethā yuddhāya kṛtaniścayaḥ
|
śapeyaṃ tvāṃ mahārāja parābhāvāya vai raṇe ||79||

80tuṣṭo'smi pūjitaścāsmi yatkāṃkṣasi tadastu te
|
anujānāmi caiva tvāṃ yudhyasva jayamāpnuhi ||80||

81brūhi caiva paraṃ vīra kenārthaḥ kiṃ dadāmi te
|
evaṃgate mahārāja yuddhādanyatkimicchasi ||81||

82arthasya puruṣo dāso dāsastvartho na kasyacit
|
iti satyaṃ mahārāja baddho'smyarthena kauravaiḥ ||82||

83kariṣyāmi hi te kāmaṃ bhāgineya yathepsitam
|
bravīmyataḥ klībavattvāṃ yuddhādanyatkimicchasi ||83||

84yudhiṣṭhira uvāca|
mantrayasva mahārāja nityaṃ maddhitamuttamam
|
kāmaṃ yuddhya parasyārthe varametaṃ vṛṇomyaham ||84||

85śalya uvāca|
kimatra brūhi sāhyaṃ te karomi nṛpasattama
|
kāmaṃ yotsye parasyārthe baddho'smyarthena kauravaiḥ ||85||

86yudhiṣṭhira uvāca|sa eva me varaḥ śalya udyoge yastvayā kṛtaḥ
|
sūtaputrasya saṃgrāme kāryastejovadhastvayā ||86||

87śalya uvāca|
sampatsyatyeṣa te kāmaḥ kuntīputra yathepsitam
|
gaccha yudhyasva viśrabdhaḥ pratijāne vacastava ||87||

88sañjaya uvāca|anumānyātha kaunteyo mātulaṃ madrakeśvaram
|
nirjagāma mahāsainyādbhrātṛbhiḥ parivāritaḥ ||88||

89vāsudevastu rādheyamāhave'bhijagāma vai
|
tata enamuvācedaṃ pāṇḍavārthe gadāgrajaḥ ||89||

90śrutaṃ me karṇa bhīṣmasya dveṣātkila na yotsyase
|
asmānvaraya rādheya yāvadbhīṣmo na hanyate ||90||

91hate tu bhīṣme rādheya punareṣyasi saṃyugam
|
dhārtarāṣṭrasya sāhāyyaṃ yadi paśyasi cetsamam ||91||

92karṇa uvāca|
na vipriyaṃ kariṣyāmi dhārtarāṣṭrasya keśava
|
tyaktaprāṇaṃ hi māṃ viddhi duryodhanahitaiṣiṇam ||92||

93sañjaya uvāca|
tacchrutvā vacanaṃ kṛṣṇaḥ saṃnyavartata bhārata
|
yudhiṣṭhirapurogaiśca pāṇḍavaiḥ saha saṅgataḥ ||93||

94atha sainyasya madhye tu prākrośatpāṇḍavāgrajaḥ
|
yo'smānvṛṇoti tamahaṃ varaye sāhyakāraṇāt ||94||

95atha tān samabhiprekṣya yuyutsuridamabravīt
|
prītātmā dharmarājānaṃ kuntīputraṃ yudhiṣṭhiram ||95||

96ahaṃ yotsyāmi bhavataḥ saṃyuge dhṛtarāṣṭrajān
|
yuṣmadarthaṃ mahārāja yadi māṃ vṛṇuṣe'nagha ||96||

97yudhiṣṭhira uvāca|
ehyehi sarve yotsyāmastava bhrātṝnapaṇḍitān
|
yuyutso vāsudevaśca vayaṃ ca brūma sarvaśaḥ ||97||

98vṛṇomi tvāṃ mahābāho yuddhyasva mama kāraṇāt
|
tvayi piṇḍaśca tantuśca dhṛtarāṣṭrasya dṛśyate ||98||

99bhajasvāsmān rājaputra bhajamānānmahādyute
|
na bhaviṣyati durbuddhirdhārtarāṣṭro'tyamarṣaṇaḥ ||99||

100sañjaya uvāca|
tato yuyutsuḥ kauravyān parityajya sutāṃstava
|
jagāma pāṇḍuputrāṇāṃ senāṃ viśrāvya dundubhim ||100||

101tato yudhiṣṭhiro rājā saṃprahṛṣṭaḥ sahānujaḥ
|
jagrāha kavacaṃ bhūyo dīptimatkanakojjvam ||101||

102pratyapadyanta te sarve svarathān puruṣarṣabhāḥ
|
tato vyūhaṃ yathāpūrvaṃ pratyavyūhanta te punaḥ ||102||

103avādayan dundubhīṃśca śataśaścaiva puṣkarān
|
siṃhanādāṃśca vividhānvineduḥ puruṣarṣabhāḥ ||103||

104rathasthān puruṣavyāghrān pāṇḍavān prekṣya pārthivāḥ
|
dhṛṣṭadyumnādayaḥ sarve punarjahṛṣire tadā ||104||

105gauravaṃ pāṇḍuputrāṇāṃ mānyānmānayatāṃ ca tān
|
dṛṣṭvā mahīkṣitastatra pūjayāñcakrire bhṛśam ||105||

106sauhṛdaṃ ca kṛpāṃ caiva prāptakālaṃ mahātmanām
|
dayāṃ ca jñātiṣu parāṃ kathayāñcakrire nṛpāḥ ||106||

107sādhu sādhviti sarvatra niśceruḥ stutisaṃhitāḥ
|
vācaḥ puṇyāḥ kīrtimatāṃ manohṛdayaharṣaṇāḥ ||107||

108mlecchāścāryāśca ye tatra dadṛśuḥ śuśruvustathā
|
vṛttaṃ tatpāṇḍuputrāṇāṃ ruruduste sagadgadāḥ ||108||

109tato jaghnurmahābherīḥ śataśaśca sahasraśaḥ
|
śaṅkhāṃśca gokṣīranibhān dadhmurhṛṣṭā manasvinaḥ ||109||
