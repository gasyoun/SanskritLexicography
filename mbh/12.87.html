Mahābhārata 12.87 (Śāntiparvan)

1yudhiṣṭhira uvāca|
rāṣṭraguptiṃ ca me rājan rāṣṭrasyaiva tu saṃgraham
|
samyagjijñāsamānāya prabrūhi bharatarṣabha||1||

2bhīṣma uvāca|
rāṣṭraguptiṃ ca te samyagrāṣṭrasyaiva tu saṃgraham
|
hanta sarvaṃ pravakṣyāmi tattvamekamanāḥ śṛṇu||2||

3grāmasyādhipatiḥ kāryo daśagrāmyāstathā paraḥ
|
dviguṇāyāḥ śatasyaivaṃ sahasrasya ca kārayet||3||

4grāme yān grāmadoṣāṃśca grāmikaḥ pratibhāvayet
|
tān brūyāddaśapāyāsau sa tu viṃśatipāya vai||4||

5so'pi viṃśatyadhipatirvṛttaṃ jānapade jane
|
grāmāṇāṃ śatapālāya sarvameva nivedayet||5||

6yāni grāmyāṇi bhojyāni grāmikastānyupāśriyāt
|
daśapastena bhartavyastenāpi dviguṇādhipaḥ||6||

7grāmaṃ grāmaśatādhyakṣo bhoktumarhati satkṛtaḥ
|
mahāntaṃ bharataśreṣṭha susphītaṃ janasaṃkulam||7||

8tatra hyanekapāyattaṃ rājño bhavati bhārata
|
śākhānagaramarhastu sahasrapatiruttamaḥ||8||

9dhānyahairaṇyabhogena bhoktuṃ rāṣṭrīyasaṅgataḥ
|
teṣāṃ saṃgrāmakṛtyaṃ syādgrāmakṛtyaṃ ca teṣu yat||9||

10dharmajñaḥ sacivaḥ kaścittattatpaśyedatandritaḥ
|
nagare nagare vā syādekaḥ sarvārthacintakaḥ||10||

11uccaiḥ sthāne ghorarūpo nakṣatrāṇāmiva grahaḥ
|
bhavetsa tānparikrāmetsarvāneva sabhāsadaḥ||11||

12teṣāṃ vṛttiṃ pariṇayetkaścidrāṣṭreṣu taccaraḥ
|
jighāṃsavaḥ pāpakāmāḥ parasvādāyinaḥ śaṭhāḥ||12||

13rakṣābhyadhikṛtā nāma tebhyo rakṣedimāḥ prajāḥ
|
vikrayaṃ krayamadhvānaṃ bhaktaṃ ca saparicchadam||13||

14yogakṣemaṃ ca samprekṣya vaṇijāṃ kārayetkarān
|
utpattiṃ dānavṛttiṃ ca śilpaṃ samprekṣya cāsakṛt||14||

15śilpaṃ prati karānevaṃ śilpinaḥ prati kārayet
|
uccāvacakarā dāpyā mahārājñā yudhiṣṭhira||15||

16yathā yathā na sīderaṃstathā kuryānmahīpatiḥ
|
phalaṃ karma ca samprekṣya tataḥ sarvaṃ prakalpayet||16||

17phalaṃ karma ca nirhetu na kaścit sampravartate
|
yathā rājā ca kartā ca syātāṃ karmaṇi bhāginau||17||

18saṃvekṣya tu tathā rājñā praṇeyāḥ satataṃ karāḥ
|
nocchindyādātmāno mūlaṃ pareṣāṃ cāpi tṛṣṇayā||18||

19īhādvārāṇi saṃrudhya rājā samprītadarśanaḥ
|
pradviṣanti parikhyātaṃ rājānamatikhādinam||19||

20pradviṣṭasya kutaḥ śreyo nāpriyo labhate phalam
|
vatsaupamyena dogdhavyaṃ rāṣṭramakṣīṇabuddhinā||20||

21bhṛto vatso jātabalaḥ pīḍāṃ sahati bhārata
|
na karma kurute vatso bhṛśaṃ dugdho yudhiṣṭhira||21||

22rāṣṭramapyatidugdhaṃ hi na karma kurute mahat
|
yo rāṣṭramanugṛhṇāti parirakṣansvayaṃ nṛpaḥ||22||

23saṃjātamupajīvansa labhate sumahatphalam
|
āpadarthaṃ ca niryātaṃ dhanaṃ tviha vivardhayet||23||

24rāṣṭraṃ ca kośabhūtaṃ syātkośo veśmagatastathā
|
paurajānapadānsarvānsaṃśritopāśritāṃstathā
|
yathāśaktyanukampeta sarvānsvalpadhanānapi||24||

25bāhyaṃ janaṃ bhedayitvā bhoktavyo madhyamaḥ sukham
|
evaṃ nāsya prakupyanti janāḥ sukhitaduḥkhitāḥ||25||

26prāgeva tu dhanādānamanubhāṣya tataḥ punaḥ
|
saṃnipatya svaviṣaye bhayaṃ rāṣṭra pradarśayet||26||

27iyamāpatsamutpannā paracakrabhayaṃ mahat
|
api cāntāya kalpante veṇoriva phalāgamāḥ||27||

28arayo me samutthāya bahubhirdasyubhiḥ saha
|
idamātmavadhāyaiva rāṣṭramicchanti bādhitum||28||

29asyāmāpadi ghorāyāṃ samprāpte dāruṇe bhaye
|
paritrāṇāya bhavataḥ prārthayiṣye dhanāni vaḥ||29||

30pratidāsye ca bhavatāṃ sarvaṃ cāhaṃ bhayakṣaye
|
nārayaḥ pratidāsyanti yaddhareyurbalāditaḥ||30||

31kalatramāditaḥ kṛtvā sarvaṃ vo vinaśediti
|
api cetputradārārthamarthasaṃcaya iṣyate||31||

32nandāmi vaḥ prabhāveṇa putrāṇāmiva codaye
|
yathāśaktyupagṛhṇāmi rāṣṭrasyāpīḍayā ca vaḥ||32||

33āpatsveva ca voḍhavyaṃ bhavadbhiḥ puṅgavairiva
|
na ca priyataraṃ kāryaṃ dhanaṃ kasyāñcidāpadi||33||

34iti vācā madhurayā ślakṣṇayā sopacārayā
|
svaraśmīnabhyavasṛjedyogamādhāya kālavit||34||

35prākāraṃ bhṛtyabharaṇaṃ vyayaṃ saṃgrāmatobhayam
|
yogakṣemaṃ ca samprekṣya gominaḥ kārayetkaram||35||

36upekṣitā hi naśyeyurgomino'raṇyavāsinaḥ
|
tasmātteṣu viśeṣeṇa mṛdupūrvaṃ samācaret||36||

37sāntvanaṃ rakṣaṇaṃ dānamavasthā cāpyabhīkṣṇaśaḥ
|
gomināṃ pārtha kartavyaḥ saṃvibhāgaḥ priyāṇi ca||37||

38ajasramupayoktavyaṃ phalaṃ gomiṣu bhārata
|
prabhāvayanti rāṣṭraṃ ca vyavahāraṃ kṛṣiṃ tathā||38||

39tasmādgomiṣu yatnena prītiṃ kuryādvicakṣaṇaḥ
|
dayāvānapramattaśca karānsampraṇayanmṛdūn||39||

40sarvatra kṣemacaraṇaṃ sulabhaṃ nāma gomiṣu
|
na hyataḥ sadṛśaṃ kiṃcidvaramasti yudhiṣṭhira||40||
