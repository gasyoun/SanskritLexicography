Mahābhārata 5.36 (Udyogaparvan)

1vidura uvāca|
atraivodāharantīmamitihāsaṃ purātanam
|
ātreyasya ca saṃvādaṃ sādhyānāṃ ceti naḥ śrutam ||1||

2carantaṃ haṃsarūpeṇa maharṣiṃ saṃśitavratam
|
sādhyā devā mahāprājñaṃ paryapṛcchanta vai purā ||2||

3sādhyā ūcuḥ| sādhyā devā vayamete maharṣe dṛṣṭvā bhavantaṃ na śaknumo'numātum
|
śrutena dhīro buddhimāṃstvaṃ mato naḥ kāvyāṃ vācaṃ vaktumarhasyudārām ||3||

4haṃsa uvāca|
etatkāryamamarāḥ saṃśrutaṃ me dhṛtiḥ śamaḥ satyadharmānuvṛttiḥ
|
granthiṃ vinīya hṛdayasya sarvaṃ priyāpriye cātmasamaṃ nayīta ||4||

5ākruśyamāno nākrośenmanyureva titikṣataḥ
|
ākroṣṭāraṃ nirdahati sukṛtaṃ cāsya vindati ||5||

6nākrośī syānnāvamānī parasya mitradrohī nota nīcopasevī
|
na cābhimānī na ca hīnavṛtto rūkṣāṃ vācaṃ ruṣatīṃ varjayīta||6||

7marmāṇyasthīni hṛdayaṃ tathāsūn rūkṣā vāco nirdahantīha puṃsām
|
tasmādvācamuṣatīṃ rūkṣarūpāṃ dharmārāmo nityaśo varjayīta ||7||

8aruntudaṃ paruṣaṃ rūkṣavācaṃ vākkaṇṭakairvitudantaṃ manuṣyān
|
vidyādalakṣmīkatamaṃ jananāṃ mukhe nibaddhāṃ nirṛtiṃ vai vahantam||8||

9paraścedenamabhividdhyeta bāṇairbhṛśaṃ sutīkṣṇairanalārkadīptaiḥ
|
sa viddhyamāno'pyatidahyamāno vidyāt kaviḥ sukṛtaṃ me dadhāti ||9||

10yadi santaṃ sevati yadyasantaṃ tapasvinaṃ yadi vā stenameva
|
vāso yathā raṅgavaśaṃ prayāti tathā sa teṣāṃ vaśamabhyupaiti||10||

11ativādaṃ na pravadenna vādayedyo'nāhataḥ pratihanyānna ghātayet
|
hantuṃ ca yo necchati pāpakaṃ vai tasmai devāḥ spṛhayantyāgatāya ||11||

12avyāhṛtaṃ vyāhṛtācchreya āhuḥ satyaṃ vadedvyāhṛtaṃ taddvitīyam
|
priyaṃ vadedvyāhṛtaṃ tattṛtīyaṃ dharmyaṃ vadedvyāhṛtaṃ taccaturtham ||12||

13yādṛśaiḥ sanniviśate yādṛśāṃścopasevate
|
yādṛgicchecca bhavituṃ tādṛgbhavati pūruṣaḥ ||13||

14yato yato nivartate tatastato vimucyate
|
nivartanāddhi sarvato na vetti duḥkhamaṇvapi ||14||

15na jīyate cānujigīṣate'nyānna vairakṛccāpratighātakaśca
|
nindāpraśaṃsāsu samasvabhāvo na śocate hṛṣyati naiva cāyam ||15||

16bhāvamicchati sarvasya nābhāve kurute manaḥ
|
satyavādī mṛdurdānto yaḥ sa uttamapūruṣaḥ ||16||

17nānarthakaṃ sāntvayati pratijñāya dadāti ca
|
randhraṃ parasya jānāti yaḥ sa madhyamapūruṣaḥ ||17||

18duḥśāsanastūpahato'bhiśasto nāvartate manyuvaśāt kṛtaghnaḥ
|
na kasyacinmitramatho durātmā kalāścaitā adhamasyeha puṃsaḥ||18||

19na śraddadhāti kalyāṇaṃ parebhyo'pyātmaśaṅkitaḥ
|
nirākaroti mitrāṇi yo vai so'dhamapūruṣaḥ ||19||

20uttamāneva seveta prāptakāle tu madhyamān
|
adhamāṃstu na seveta ya icchedbhūtimātmanaḥ ||20||

21prāgnoti vai vittamasadbalena nityotthānāt prajñayā pauruṣeṇa
|
na tveva samyaglabhate praśaṃsāṃ na vṛttamāpnoti mahākulānām ||21||

22dhṛtarāṣṭra uvāca|
mahākulebhyaḥ spṛhayanti devā dharmārthanityāśca bahuśrutāśca
|
pṛcchāmi tvāṃ viduraṃ praśnametaṃ bhavanti vai kāni mahākulāni ||22||

23vidura uvāca|
tapo damo brahmavittvaṃ vitānāḥ puṇyā vivāhāḥ satatānnadānam
|
yeṣvevaite sapta guṇā vasanti samyagvṛttāstāni mahākulāni ||23||

24yeṣāṃ na vṛttaṃ vyathate na yoniścittaprasādena caranti dharmam
|
ye kīrtimicchanti kule viśiṣṭāṃ tyaktānṛtāstāni mahākulāni ||24||

25anijyayā kuvivāhairvedasyotsādanena ca
|
kulānyakulatāṃ yānti dharmasyātikrameṇa ca ||25||

26devadravyanināśena brahmasvaharaṇena ca
|
kulānyakulatāṃ yānti brāhmaṇātikrameṇa ca ||26||

27brāhmaṇānāṃ paribhavāt parīvādācca bhārata
|
kulānyakulatāṃ yānti nyāsāpaharaṇena ca||27||

28kulāni samupetānani gobhiḥ puruṣato'rthataḥ
|
kulasaṃkhyāṃ na gacchanti yāni hīnāni vṛttataḥ ||28||

29vṛttatastvavihīnāni kulānyalpadhanānyapi
|
kulasaṅkhyāṃ ca gacchanti karṣanti ca mahadyaśaḥ ||29||

30vṛttaṃ yatnena saṃrakṣedvittameti ca yāti ca
|
akṣīṇo vittataḥ kṣīṇo vṛttatastu hato hataḥ ||30||

31gobhiḥ paśubhiraśvaiśca kṛṣyā ca susamṛddhayā
|
kulāni na prarohanti yāni hīnāni vṛttataḥ ||31||

32mā naḥ kule vairakṛtkaścidastu rājā'mātyo mā parasvāpahārī
|
mitradrohī naikṛtiko'nṛtī vā pūrvāśī vā pitṛdevātithibhyaḥ ||32||

33yaśca no brāhmaṇān hanyādyaśca no brāhmaṇān dviṣet
|
na naḥ sa samitiṃ gacchedyaśca no nirvapet kṛṣim ||33||

34tṛṇāni bhūmirudakaṃ vāk caturthī ca sūnṛtā
|
satāmetāni geheṣu nocchidyante kadācana ||34||

35śraddhayā parayā rājannupanītāni satkṛtim
|
pravṛttāni mahāprājña dharmiṇāṃ puṇyakarmiṇām ||35||

36sūkṣmo'pi bhāraṃ nṛpate syandano vai śakto voḍhuṃ na tathā'nye mahījāḥ
|
evaṃ yuktā bhārasahā bhavanti mahākulīnā na tathānye manuṣyāḥ ||36||

37na tanmitraṃ yasya kopādbibheti yadvā mitraṃ śaṅkitenopacaryam
|
yasminmitre pitarīvāśvasīta tadvai mitraṃ saṅgatānītarāṇi ||37||

38yaḥ kaścidapyasaṃbaddho mitrabhāvena vartate
|
sa eva bandhustanmitraṃ sā gatistatparāyaṇam ||38||

39calacittasya vai puṃso vṛddhānanupasevataḥ
|
pāriplavamaternityamadhruvo mitrasaṅgrahaḥ ||39||

40calacittamanātmānamindriyāṇāṃ vaśānugam
|
arthāḥ samabhivartante haṃsāḥ śuṣkaṃ saro yathā ||40||

41akasmādeva kupyanti prasīdantyanimittataḥ
|
śīlametadasādhūnāmabhraṃ pāriplavaṃ yathā ||41||

42satkṛtāśca kṛtārthāśca mitrāṇāṃ na bhavanti ye
|
tān mṛtānapi kravyādāḥ kṛtaghnānnopabhuñjate||42||

43arcayedeva mitrāṇi sati vā'sati vā dhane
|
nānarthayan prajānāti mitrāṇāṃ sāraphalgutām ||43||

44santāpādbhraśyate rūpaṃ santāpādbhraśyate balam
|
santāpādbhraśyate jñānaṃ santāpādvyādhimṛcchati ||44||

45anavāpyaṃ ca śokena śarīraṃ copatapyate
|
amitrāśca prahṛṣyanti mā sma śoke manaḥ kṛthāḥ ||45||

46punarnaro mriyate jāyate ca punarnaro hīyate vardhate ca
|
punarnaro yācati yācyate ca punarnaraḥ śocati śocyate ca ||46||

47sukhaṃ ca duḥkhaṃ ca bhavābhavau ca lābhālābhau maraṇaṃ jīvitaṃ ca
|
paryāyaśaḥ sarvamete spṛśanti tasmāddhīro na ca hṛṣyenna śocet ||47||

48calāni hīmāni ṣaḍindriyāṇi teṣāṃ yadyadvardhate yatra yatra
|
tatastataḥ sravate buddhirasya chidrodakumbhādiva nityamambhaḥ ||48||

49dhṛtarāṣṭra uvāca|
tanuruddhaḥ śikhī rājā mithyopacarito mayā
|
mandānāṃ mama putrāṇāṃ yuddhenāntaṃ kariṣyati ||49||

50nityodvignamidaṃ sarvaṃ nityodvignamidaṃ manaḥ
|
yattatpadamanudvignaṃ tanme vada mahāmate ||50||

51vidura uvāca|
nānyatra vidyātapasornānyatrendriyanigrahāt
|
nānyatra lobhasantyāgācchāntiṃ paśyāmi te'nagha ||51||

52buddhyā bhayaṃ praṇudati tapasā vindate mahat
|
guruśuśrūṣayā jñānaṃ śāntiṃ bhogena vindati ||52||

53anāśritā dānapuṇyaṃ vedapuṇyamanāśritāḥ
|
rāgadveṣavinirmuktā vicarantīha mokṣiṇaḥ ||53||

54svadhītasya suyuddhasya sukṛtasya ca karmaṇaḥ
|
tapasaśca sutaptasya tasyānte sukhamedhate ||54||

55svāstīrṇāni śayanāni prapannā na vai bhinnā jātu nidrāṃ labhante
|
na strīṣu rājan ratimāpnuvanti na māgadhaiḥ stūyamānā na sūtaiḥ ||55||

56na vai bhinnā jātu caranti dharmaṃ na vai sukhaṃ prāpnuvantīha bhinnāḥ
|
na vai bhinnā gauravaṃ prāpnuvanti na vai bhinnāḥ praśamaṃ rocayanti ||56||

57na vai teṣāṃ svadate pathyamuktaṃ yogakṣemaṃ kalpate naiva teṣām
|
bhinnānāṃ vai manujendra parāyaṇaṃ na vidyate kiñcidanyadvināśāt ||57||

58saṃpannaṃ goṣu saṃbhāvyaṃ saṃbhāvyaṃ brāhmaṇe tapaḥ
|
saṃbhāvyaṃ cāpalaṃ strīṣu saṃbhāvyaṃ jñātito bhayaṃ ||58||

59tantavo'pyāyatā nityaṃ tanavo bahulāḥ samāḥ
|
bahūn bahutvādāyāsān sahantītsupamā satām ||59||

60dhūmāyante vyapetāni jvalanti sahitāni ca
|
dhṛtarāṣṭrolmukānīva jñātayo bharatarṣabha ||60||

61brāhmaṇeṣu ca ye śūrāḥ strīṣu jñātiṣu goṣu ca
|
vṛntādiva phalaṃ pakvaṃ dhṛtarāṣṭra patanti te||61||

62mahānapyekajo vṛkṣo balavā supratiṣṭhitaḥ
|
prasahya eva vātena saskandho mardituṃ kṣaṇāt ||62||

63atha ye sahitā vṛkṣāḥ saṅghaśaḥ supratiṣṭhitāḥ
|
te hi śīghratamān vātān sahante'nyonyasaṃśrayāt ||63||

64evaṃ manuṣyamapyekaṃ guṇairapi samanvitam
|
śakyaṃ dviṣanto manyante vāyurdrumamivaikajam ||64||

65anyonyasamupaṣṭambhādanyonyāpāśrayeṇa ca
|
jñātayaḥ saṃpravardhante sarasīvotpalānyuta ||65||

66avadhyā brāhmaṇā gāvo jñātayaḥ śiśivaḥ striyaḥ
|
yeṣāṃ cānnāni bhuñjīta ye ca syuḥ śaraṇāgatāḥ||66||

67na manuṣye guṇaḥ kaścidrājan sadhanatāmṛte
|
anāturatvādbhadraṃ te mṛtakalpā hi rogiṇaḥ ||67||

68avyādhijaṃ kaṭukaṃ śīrṣarogi pāpānubandhaṃ paruṣaṃ tīkṣṇamuṣṇam
|
satāṃ peyaṃ yanna pibantyasanto manyuṃ mahārāja piba praśāmya ||68||

69rogārditā na phalānyādriyante na vai labhante viṣayeṣu tattvam
|
duḥkhopetā rogiṇo nityameva na budhyante dhanabhogānna saukhyam||69||

70purā hyuktaṃ nākarostvaṃ vaco me dyūte jitāṃ draupadīṃ prekṣya rājan
|
duryodhanaṃ vārayetyakṣavatyāṃ kitavatvaṃ paṇḍitā varjayanti||70||

71na tadbalaṃ yanmṛdunā virudhyate sūkṣmo dharmastarasā sevitavyaḥ
|
pradhvaṃsinī krūrasamāhitā śrīrmṛduprauḍhā gacchati putrapautrān ||71||

72dhārtarāṣṭrāḥ pāṇḍavān pālayantu pāṇḍoḥ sutāstava putrāṃśca pāntu
|
ekārimitrāḥ kuravo hyekakāryā jīvantu rājan sukhinaḥ samṛddhāḥ ||72||

73meḍhībhūtaḥ kauravāṇāṃ tvamadya tvayyādhīnaṃ kurukulamājamīḍha
|
pārthān balān vanavāsaprataptān gopāyasva svaṃ yaśastāta rakṣan ||73||

74sandhatsva tvaṃ kaurava pāṇḍuputrairmā te'ntaraṃ ripavaḥ prārthayantu
|
satye sthitāste naradeva sarve duryodhanaṃ sthāpaya tvaṃ narendra ||74||
