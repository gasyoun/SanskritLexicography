Mahābhārata 7.144 (Droṇaparvan)

1dhṛtarāṣṭra uvāca|
ajito droṇarādheyavikarṇakṛtavarmabhiḥ
|
tīrṇaḥ sainyārṇavaṃ vīraḥ pratiśrutya yudhiṣṭhire||1||

2sa kathaṃ kauraveyeṇa samareṣvanivāritaḥ
|
nigṛhya bhūriśravasā balādbhuvi nipātitaḥ||2||

3sañjaya uvāca|
śṛṇu rājannihotpattiṃ śaineyasya yathā purā
|
yathā ca bhūriśravaso yatra te saṃśayo nṛpa||3||

4atreḥ putro'bhavatsomaḥ somasya tu budhaḥ smṛtaḥ
|
budhasyaiko mahendrābhaḥ putra āsītpurūravāḥ||4||

5purūravasa āyustu āyuṣo nahuṣaḥ sutaḥ
|
nahuṣasya yayātistu rājā devarṣisaṃmataḥ||5||

6yayāterdevayānyāṃ tu yadurjyeṣṭho'bhavatsutaḥ
|
yadorabhūdanvavāye devamīḍha iti smṛtaḥ||6||

7yādavastasya tu sutaḥ śūrastrailokyasaṃmataḥ
|
śūrasya śaurirnṛvaro vasudevo mahāyaśāḥ||7||

8dhanuṣyanavaraḥ śūraḥ kārtavīryasamo yudhi
|
tadvīryaścāpi tatraiva kule śinirabhūnnṛpa||8||

9etasminneva kāle tu devakasya mahātmanaḥ
|
duhituḥ svayaṃvare rājansarvakṣatrasamāgame||9||

10tatra vai devakīṃ devīṃ vasudevārthamāśu vai
|
nirjitya pārthivānsarvān rathamāropayacchiniḥ||10||

11tāṃ dṛṣṭvā devakīṃ śūro rathasthāṃ puruṣarṣabha
|
nāmṛṣyata mahātejāḥ somadattaḥ śinernṛpa||11||

12tayoryuddhamabhūdrājandinārdhaṃ citramadbhutam
|
bāhuyuddhaṃ subalinoḥ prasaktaṃ puruṣarṣabhaḥ||12||

13śininā somadattastu prasahya bhuvi pātitaḥ
|
asimudyamya keśeṣu pragṛhya ca padā hataḥ||13||

14madhye rājasahasrāṇāṃ prekṣakāṇāṃ samantataḥ
|
kṛpayā ca punastena sa jīveti visarjitaḥ||14||

15tadavasthaḥ kṛtastena somadatto'tha māriṣa
|
prāsādayanmahādevamamarṣavaśamāsthitaḥ||15||

16tasya tuṣṭo mahādevo varāṇāṃ varadaḥ prabhuḥ
|
vareṇa cchandayāmāsa sa tu vavre varaṃ nṛpaḥ||16||

17putramicchāmi bhagavanyo nipātya śineḥ sutam
|
madhye rājasahasrāṇāṃ padā hanyācca saṃyuge||17||

18tasya tadvacanaṃ śrutvā somadattasya pārthiva
|
evamastviti tatroktvā sa devo'ntaradhīyata||18||

19sa tena varadānena labdhavānbhūridakṣiṇam
|
apātayacca samare saumadattiḥ śineḥ sutam||19||

20paśyatāṃ sarvasainyānāṃ padā cainamatāḍayat
|
etatte kathitaṃ rājanyanmāṃ tvaṃ paripṛcchasi||20||

21na hi śakyo raṇe jetuṃ sātvato manujarṣabhaiḥ
|
labdhalakṣyāśca saṃgrāme bahuśaścitrayodhinaḥ||21||

22devadānavagandharvānvijetāro hyavismitāḥ
|
svavīryavijaye yuktā naite paraparigrahāḥ||22||

23na tulyaṃ vṛṣṇibhiriha dṛśyate kiñcana prabho
|
bhūtaṃ bhavyaṃ bhaviṣyacca balena bharatarṣabha||23||

24na jñātimavamanyante vṛddhānāṃ śāsane ratāḥ
|
na devāsuragandharvā na yakṣoragarākṣasāḥ||24||

25jetāro vṛṣṇivīrāṇāṃ kiṃ punarmānavā raṇe
|
brahmadravye gurudravye jñātisve cāpyahiṃsakāḥ||25||

26eteṣāṃ rakṣitāraśca ye syuḥ kasyāṃcidāpadi
|
arthavanto na cotsiktā brahmaṇyāḥ satyavādinaḥ||26||

27samarthānnāvamanyante dīnānabhyuddharanti ca
|
nityaṃ devaparā dāntāstrātāraścāvikatthanāḥ||27||

28tena vṛṣṇipravīrāṇāṃ cakraṃ na pratihanyate
|
api meruṃ vahetkaścittaredvā makarālayam||28||

29na tu vṛṣṇipravīrāṇāṃ sametyāntaṃ vrajennṛpa
|
etatte sarvamākhyātaṃ yatra te saṃśayaḥ prabho
|
kururāja naraśreṣṭha tava vyapanayo mahān||29||
