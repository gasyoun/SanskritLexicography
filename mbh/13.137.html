Mahābhārata 13.137 (Anuśāsanaparvan)

1yudhiṣṭhira uvāca|
dānena vartatetyāha tapasā caiva bhārata
|
tadetanme mano duḥkhaṃ vyapoha tvaṃ pitāmaha
|
kiṃsvitpṛthivyāṃ hyetanme bhavāñchaṃsitumarhati ||1||

2bhīṣma uvāca|
śṛṇu yairdharmanirataistapasā bhāvitātmabhiḥ
|
lokā hyasaṃśayaṃ prāptā dānapuṇyaratairnṛpaiḥ ||2||

3satkṛtaśca tathā''treyaḥ śiṣyebhyo brahma nirguṇam
|
upadiśya tadā rājan gato lokānanuttamān ||3||

4śibirauśīnaraḥ prāṇān priyasya tanayasya ca
|
brāhmaṇārthamupākṛtya nākapṛṣṭhamito gataḥ ||4||

5pratardanaḥ kāśipatiḥ pradāya tanayaṃ svakam
|
brāhmaṇāyātulāṃ kīrtimiha cāmutra cāśnute ||5||

6rantidevaśca sāṃkṛtyo vasiṣṭhāya mahātmane
|
arghyaṃ pradāya vidhivallebhe lokānanuttamān ||6||

7divyaṃ śataśalākaṃ ca yakṣārthaṃ kāñcanaṃ śubham
|
chatraṃ devāvṛdho datvā brāhmaṇāyāsthito divam ||7||

8bhagavānambarīṣaśca brāhmaṇāyāmitaujase
|
pradāya sakalaṃ rāṣṭraṃ suralokamavāptavān ||8||

9sāvitraḥ kuṇḍalaṃ divyaṃ yānaṃ ca janamejayaḥ
|
brāhmaṇāya ca gāṃ dattvā gato lokānanuttamān ||9||

10vṛṣādarbhiśca rājarṣī ratnāni vividhāni ca
|
ramyāṃścāvasathāndattvā dvijebhyo divamāgataḥ ||10||

11nimīrāṣṭraṃ ca vaida‌rbhiḥ kanyāṃ dattvā mahātmane
|
agastyāya gataḥ svargaṃ saputrapaśubāndhavaḥ ||11||

12jāmadagnyaśca viprāya bhūmiṃ datvā mahāyaśāḥ
|
rāmo'kṣayāṃstathā lokān jagāma manaso'dhikān ||12||

13avarṣati ca parjanye sarvabhūtāni devarāṭ
|
vasiṣṭho jīvayāmāsa yena yāto'kṣayāṃ gatim ||13||

14rāmo dāśarathiścaiva hutvā yajñeṣu vai vasu
|
sa gato hyakṣayān lokānyasya loke mahadyaśaḥ ||14||

15kakṣasenaśca rājarṣirvasiṣṭhāya mahātmane
|
nyāsaṃ yathāvatsaṃnyasya jagāma sumahāyaśāḥ ||15||

16karaṃdhamasya pautrastu marutto'vikṣitaḥ sutaḥ
|
kanyāmāṅgirase datvā divamāśu jagāma saḥ ||16||

17brahmadattaśca pāñcālyo rājā dharmabhṛtāṃ varaḥ
|
nidhiṃ śaṅkhamanujñāpya jagāma paramāṃ gatim ||17||

18rājā mitrasahaścaiva vasiṣṭhāya mahātmane
|
madyantīṃ priyāṃ bhāryāṃ dattvā ca tridivaṃ gataḥ ||18||

19manoḥ putraśca sudyumno likhitāya mahātmane
|
daṇḍamudhṛtya dharmeṇa gato lokānanuttamān ||19||

20sahasracityo rājarṣiḥ prāṇāniṣṭānmahāyaśāḥ
|
brāhmaṇārthe parityajya gato lokānanuttamān ||20||

21sarvakāmaiśca saṃpūrṇaṃ dattvā veśma hiraṇmayam
|
maudgalyāya gataḥ svargaṃ śatadyumno mahīpatiḥ ||21||

22bhakṣyabhojyasya ca kṛtān rāśayaḥ parvatopamān
|
śāṇḍilyāya purā datvā sumanyurdivamāsthitaḥ ||22||

23nāmnā ca dyutimānnāma śālvarājo mahādyutiḥ
|
dattvā rājyamṛcīkāya gato lokānanuttamān ||23||

24madirāśvaśca rājarṣirdatvā kanyāṃ sumadhyamām
|
hiraṇyahastāya gato lokāndevairadhiṣṭhitān ||24||

25lomapādaśca rājarṣiḥ śāntāṃ datvā sutāṃ prabhuḥ
|
ṛṣyaśṛṅgāya vipulaiḥ sarvaiḥ kāmairayujyata ||25||

26kautsāya datvā kanyāṃ tu haṃsīṃ nāma yaśasvinīm
|
gato'kṣayānato lokān rājarṣiśca bhagīrathaḥ ||26||

27datvā śatasahasraṃ tu gavāṃ rājā bhagīrathaḥ
|
savatsānāṃ kohalāya gato lokānanuttamān ||27||

28ete cānye ca bahavo dānena tapasā ca ha
|
yudhiṣṭhira gatāḥ svargaṃ nivartante punaḥ punaḥ ||28||

29teṣāṃ pratiṣṭhitā kīrtiryāvatsthāsyati medinī
|
gṛhasthairdānatapasā yairlokā vai vinirjitāḥ ||29||

30śiṣṭānāṃ caritaṃ hyetatkīrtitaṃ me yudhiṣṭhira
|
dānayajñaprajāsargairete hi divamāsthitāḥ ||30||

31datvā tu satataṃ te'stu kauravāṇāṃ dhurandhara
|
dānayajñakriyāyuktā buddhirdharmopacāyinī ||31||

32yatra te nṛpaśārdūla sandeho cai bhaviṣyati
|
śvaḥ prabhāte hi vakṣyāmi saṃdhyā hi samupasthitā ||32||
