Mahābhārata 6.67 (Bhīṣmaparvan)

1duryodhana uvāca|
vāsudevo mahadbhūtaṃ sarvalokeṣu kathyate
|
tasyāgamaṃ pratiṣṭhāṃ ca jñātumicche pitāmaha ||1||

2bhīṣma uvāca|
vāsudevo mahadbhūtaṃ sarvadaivatadaivatam
|
na paraṃ puṇḍarīkākṣāddṛśyate bharatarṣabha ||2||

3mārkaṇḍeyaśca govinde kathayatyadbhutaṃ mahat
|
sarvabhūtāni bhūtātmā mahātmā puruṣottamaḥ ||3||

4āpo vāyuśca tejaśca trayametadakalpayat
|
sa sṛṣṭvā pṛthivīṃ devīṃ sarvalokeśvaraḥ prabhuḥ ||4||

5apsu vai śayanaṃ cakre mahātmā puruṣottamaḥ
|
sarvatejomayo devo yogātsuṣvāpa tatra ha ||5||

6mukhataḥ so'gnimasṛjat prāṇādvāyumathāpi ca
|
sarasvatīṃ ca vedāṃśca manasaḥ sasṛje'cyutaḥ ||6||

7eṣa lokān sasarjādau devāṃśca ṛṣibhiḥ saha
|
nidhanaṃ caiva mṛtyuṃ ca prajānāṃ prabhavāpyayau ||7||

8eṣa dharmaśca dharmajño varadaḥ sarvakāmadaḥ
|
eṣa kartā ca kāryaṃ ca pūrvadevaḥ svayaṃprabhuḥ ||8||

9bhūtaṃ bhavyaṃ bhaviṣyacca pūrvametadakalpayat
|
ubhe sandhye diśaḥ khaṃ ca niyamāṃśca janārdanaḥ ||9||

10ṛṣīṃścaiva hi govindastapaścaivābhyakalpayat
|
sraṣṭāraṃ jagataścāpi mahātmā prabhuravyayaḥ ||10||

11agrajaṃ sarvabhūtānāṃ saṅkarṣaṇamakalpayat
|
tasmānnārāyaṇo jajñe devadevaḥ sanātanaḥ ||11||

12nābhau padmaṃ babhūvāsya sarvalokasya saṃbhavāt
|
tasmātpitāmaho jātastasmājjātāstvimāḥ prajāḥ ||12||

13śeṣaṃ cākalpayaddevamanantaṃ viśvarūpiṇam
|
yo dhārayati bhūtāni dharāṃ cemāṃ saparvatām ||13||

14dhyānayogena viprāśca taṃ vidanti mahaujasam
|
karṇastrotobhavaṃ cāpi madhuṃ nāma mahāsuram ||14||

15tamugramugrakarmāṇamugrāṃ buddhiṃ samāsthitam
|
brahmaṇo'pacitiṃ yātuṃ jaghāna puruṣottamaḥ ||15||

16tasya tāta vadhādeva devadānavamānavāḥ
|
madhusūda‌namityāhurṛṣayaśca janārdanam ||16||

17varāhaścaiva siṃhaśca trivikramagatiḥ prabhuḥ
|
eṣa mātā pitā caiva sarveṣāṃ prāṇināṃ hariḥ ||17||

18paraṃ hi puṇḍarīkākṣānna bhūtaṃ na bhaviṣyati
|
mukhataḥ so'sṛjadviprān bāhubhyāṃ kṣatriyāṃstathā ||18||

19vaiśyāṃścāpyūruto rājan śūdrānvai pādatastathā
|
tapasā niyato devo nidhānaṃ sarvadehinām ||19||

20brahmabhūtamamāvāsyāṃ paurṇamāsyāṃ tathaiva ca
|
yogabhūtaṃ paricaran keśavaṃ mahadāpnuyāt ||20||

21keśavaḥ paramaṃ tejaḥ sarvalokapitāmahaḥ
|
evamāhurhṛṣīkeśaṃ munayo vai narādhipa ||21||

22evamenaṃ vijānīhi ācāryaṃ pitaraṃ gurum
|
kṛṣṇo yasya prasīdeta lokāstenākṣayā jitāḥ ||22||

23yaścaivainaṃ bhayasthāne keśavaṃ śaraṇaṃ vrajet
|
sadā naraḥ paṭhaṃścedaṃ svastimān sa sukhī bhavet ||23||

24ye ca kṛṣṇaṃ prapadyante te na muhyanti mānavāḥ
|
bhaye mahati magnāṃśca pāti nityaṃ janārdanaḥ ||24||

25sa taṃ yudhiṣṭhiro jñātvā yāthātathyena bhārata
|
sarvātmanā mahātmānaṃ keśavaṃ jagadīśvaram
|
prapannaḥ śaraṇaṃ rājan yogānāṃ prabhumīśvaram ||25||
