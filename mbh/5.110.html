Mahābhārata 5.110 (Udyogaparvan)

1suparṇa uvāca|
iyaṃ digdayitā rājño varuṇasya tu gopateḥ
|
sadā salilarājasya pratiṣṭhā cādireva ca ||1||

2atra paścādahaḥ sūryo visarjayati gāḥ svayam
|
paścimetyabhivikhyātā digiyaṃ dvijasattama ||2||

3yādasāmatra rājyena salilasya ca guptaye
|
kaśyapo bhagavān devo varuṇaṃ smābhyaṣecayat ||3||

4atra pītvā samastān vai varuṇasya rasāṃstu ṣaṭ
|
jāyate taruṇaḥ somaḥ śuklasyādau tamisrahā ||4||

5atra paścātkṛtā daityā vāyunā saṃyatāstadā
|
niḥśvasanto mahāvātairarditāḥ suṣupurdvija ||5||

6atra sūryaṃ praṇayinaṃ pratigṛhṇāti parvataḥ
|
asto nāma yataḥ sandhyā paścimā pratisarpati||6||

7ato rātriśca nidrā ca nirgatā divasakṣaye
|
jāyate jīvalokasya hartumardhamivāyuṣaḥ ||7||

8atra devīṃ ditiṃ suptāmātmaprasavadhāriṇīm
|
vigarbhāmakarocchakro yatra jāto marudgaṇaḥ ||8||

9atra mūlaṃ himavato mandaraṃ yāti śāśvatam
|
api varṣasahastreṇa na cāsyānto'dhigamyate ||9||

10atra kāñcanaśailasya kāñcanāṃburuhasya ca
|
udadhestīramāsādya surabhiḥ kṣarate payaḥ ||10||

11atra madhye samudrasya kabandhaḥ pratidṛśyate
|
svarbhānoḥ sūryakalpasya somasūryau jighāṃsataḥ ||11||

12suvarṇaśiraso'pyatra hariromṇaḥ pragāyataḥ
|
adṛśyasyāprameyasya śrūyate vipulī dhvaniḥ ||12||

13atra dhvajavatī nāma kumārī harimedhasaḥ
|
ākāśe tiṣṭha tiṣṭheti tasthau sūryasya śāsanāt ||13||

14atra vāyustathā vahnirāpaḥ khaṃ cāpi gālava
|
āhnikaṃ caiva naiśaṃ ca duḥkhaṃ sparśaṃ vimuñcati ||14||

15ataḥprabhṛti sūryasya tiryagāvartate gatiḥ
|
atra jyotīṃṣi sarvāṇi viśantyādityamaṇḍalam ||15||

16aṣṭāviṃśatirātraṃ ca caṅkramya saha bhānunā
|
niṣpatanti punaḥ sūryāt somasaṃyogayogataḥ ||16||

17atra nityaṃ sravantīnāṃ prabhavaḥ sāgarodayaḥ
|
atra lokatrayasyāpastiṣṭhanti varuṇālaye ||17||

18atra pannagarājasyāpyanantasya niveśanam
|
anādinidhanasyātra viṣṇoḥ sthānamanuttamam ||18||

19atrānalasakhasyāpi pavanasya niveśanam
|
maharṣeḥ kaśyapasyātra mārīcasya niveśanam ||19||

20eṣa te paścimo mārgo digdvāreṇa prakīrtitaḥ
|
brūhi gālava gacchāvo buddhiḥ kā dvijasattama ||20||
