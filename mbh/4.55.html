Mahābhārata 4.55 (Virāṭaparvan)

1vaiśampāyana uvāca|apayāte tu rādheye duryodhanapurogamāḥ
|
anīkena yathāsvena śanairārchanta pāṇḍavam ||1||

2bahudhā tasya sainyasya vyūḍhasyāpatataḥ śaraiḥ
|
adhārayata vegaṃ sa veleva tu mahodadheḥ ||2||

3tataḥ prahasya bībhatsuḥ kaunteyaḥ śvetavāhanaḥ
|
divyamastraṃ prakurvāṇaḥ pratyāyādrathasattamaḥ ||3||

4yathā raśmibhirādityaḥ pracchādayati medinīm
|
tathā gāṇḍīvanirmuktaiḥ śaraiḥ pārtho diśo daśa ||4||

5na rathānāṃ na cāśvānāṃ na gajānāṃ na varmaṇām
|
anividdhaṃ śitairbāṇairāsīt dvyaṅgulamantaram ||5||

6divyayogācca pārthasya hayānāmuttarasya ca
|
śikṣāśilpopapannatvādastrāṇāṃ ca parikramāt
|
vīryavattvaṃ drutaṃ cāgryaṃ dṛṣṭvā jiṣṇorapūjayan ||6||

7kālāgnimiva bībhatsuṃ nirdahantamiva prajāḥ
|
nārayaḥ prekṣituṃ śekurjvalantamiva pāvakam ||7||

8tāni grastānyanīkāni rejurarjunamārgaṇaiḥ
|
śailaṃ prati balābhrāṇi vyāptānīvārkaraśmibhiḥ ||8||

9aśokānāṃ vanānīva channāni bahuśaḥ śubhaiḥ
|
rejuḥ pārthaśaraistatra tadā sainyāni bhārata ||9||

10srajo'rjunaśaraiḥ śīrṇaṃ śuṣyatpuṣpaṃ hiraṇmayam
|
chatrāṇi ca patākāśca khe dadhāra sadāgatiḥ ||10||

11svabalatrāsanāttrastāḥ paripeturdiśo daśa
|
rathāṅgadeśānādāya pārthacchinnayugā hayāḥ ||11||

12karṇakakṣaviṣāṇeṣu antaroṣṭheṣu caiva ha
|
marmasvaṅgeṣu cāhatya pātayat samare gajān ||12||

13kauravāgragajānāṃ tu śarīrairgatacetasām
|
kṣaṇena saṃvṛtā bhūmirmeghairiva nabhastalam ||13||

14yugāntasamaye sarvaṃ yathā sthāvarajaṅgamam
|
kālakṣayamaśeṣeṇa dahatyagraśikhaḥ śikhī
|
tadvat pārtho mahārāja dadāha samare ripūn ||14||

15tataḥ sarvāstratejobhirdhanuṣo niḥsvanena ca
|
śabdenāmānuṣāṇāṃ ca bhūtānāṃ dhvajavāsinām
|
bhairavaṃ śabdamatyarthaṃ vānarasya ca kurvataḥ ||15||

16daivāripācca bībhatsustasmin dauryodhane bale
|
bhayamutpādayāmāsa balavānarimardanaḥ ||16||

17rathaśaktimamitrāṇāṃ prāgeva nipatadbhuvi
|
sopayāt sahasā paścāt sāhasāccābhyupeyivān ||17||

18śaravrātaiḥ sutīkṣṇāgraiḥ samādiṣṭaiḥ khagairiva
|
arjunastu khamāvavre lohitaprāśanaiḥ khagaiḥ ||18||

19atramadhye yathārkasya raśmayastigmatejasaḥ
|
diśāsu ca tathā rājannasaṃkhyātāḥ śarāstadā ||19||

20sakṛdevānataṃ śekū rathamabhyasituṃ pare
|
alabhyaḥ punaraśvaistu rathātso'tiprapādayet ||20||

21te śarā dviṭśarīreṣu yathaiva na sasajjire
|
dviḍanīkeṣu bībhatsorna sasajje rathastadā ||21||

22sa tadvikṣobhayāmāsa hyarātibalamañjasā
|
anantabhogo bhujagaḥ krīḍanniva mahārṇave ||22||

23asyato nityamatyarthaṃ sarvamevātigastathā
|
aśrutaḥ śrūyate bhūtairdharghoṣaḥ kirīṭinaḥ ||23||

24saṃtatāstatra mātaṅgā bāṇairalpāntarāntare
|
saṃvṛtāstena dṛśyante meghā iva gabhastibhiḥ ||24||

25diśo'nubhramataḥ sarvāḥ savyadakṣiṇamasyataḥ
|
satataṃ dṛśyate yuddhe sāyakāsanamaṇḍalam ||25||

26patantyarūpeṣu yathā cakṣūṃṣi na kadācana
|
nālakṣyeṣu śarāḥ petustathā gāṇḍīvadhanvanaḥ ||26||

27mārgo gajasahasrasya yugapadgacchato vane
|
yathā bhavettathā jajñe rathamārgaḥ kirīṭinaḥ ||27||

28nūnaṃ pārthajayaiṣitvācchakraḥ sarvāmaraiḥ saha
|
hantyasmānityamanyanta pārthene nihatāḥ pare ||28||

29ghnantamatyarthamahitān vijayaṃ tatra menire
|
kālamarjunarūpeṇa saṃharantamiva prajāḥ ||29||

30kurusenāśarīrāṇi pārthenaivāhatānyapi
|
seduḥ pārthahatānīva pārthakarmānuśāsanāt ||30||

31oṣadhīnāṃ śirāṃsīva dviṣacchīrṣāṇi so'nvayāt
|
avaneśuḥ kurūṇāṃ hi vīryāṇyarjunajādbhayāt ||31||

32arjunānilabhinnāni vanānyarjunavidviṣām
|
cakrurlohitadhārābhirdharaṇīṃ lohitāntarām ||32||

33lohitena samāyuktaiḥ pāṃsubhiḥ pavanoddhṛtaiḥ
|
babhūvurlohitāstatra bhṛśamādityaraśmayaḥ ||33||

34sārkaṃ khaṃ tatkṣaṇenāsīt sandhyāyāmiva lohitam
|
apyastaṃ prāpya sūryo'pi nivarteta na pāṇḍavaḥ ||34||

35tānsarvān samare śūraḥ pauruṣe samavasthitān
|
divyairastrairacintyātmā sarvānārchaddhanurdharān ||35||

36sa tu droṇaṃ trisaptatyā kṣuraprāṇāṃ samārpayat
|
duḥsahaṃ daśabhirbāṇaidrauṇimaṣṭābhireva ca ||36||

37duḥśāsanaṃ dvādaśabhiḥ kṛpaṃ śāradvataṃ tribhiḥ
|
bhīṣmaṃ śāntanavaṃ ṣaṣṭhyā rājānaṃ ca śatena ha
|
karṇaṃ ca karṇinā karṇe vivyādha paravīrahā ||37||

38tasminviddhe maheṣvāse karṇe sarvāstrakovide
|
hatāśvasūte virathe tato'nīkamabhajyata ||38||

39tat prabhagnaṃ balaṃ dṛṣṭvā pārthamājisthitaṃ punaḥ
|
abhiprāyaṃ samājñāya vairāṭirida‌mabravīt ||39||

40āsthāya ruciraṃ jiṣṇo rathaṃ sārathinā mayā
|
katamaṃ yāsyase'nīkamukto yāsyāmyahaṃ tvayā ||40||

41arjuna uvāca|lohitāśvamariṣṭaṃ yaṃ vaiyāghramanupaśyasi
|
nīlāṃ patākāmāśritya rathe tiṣṭhantamuttara ||41||

42kṛpasyaitadanīkāgryaṃ prāpayasvaitadeva mām
|
etasya darśayiṣyāmi śīghrāstraṃ dṛḍhadhanvinaḥ ||42||

43dhvaje kamaṇḍaluryasya śātakaumbhamayaḥ śubhaḥ
|
ācārya eṣa hi droṇaḥ sarvaśastrabhṛtāṃ varaḥ ||43||

44sadā mamaiṣa mānyastu sarvaśastrabhṛtāmapi
|
suprasannaṃ mahāvīraṃ kuruṣvainaṃ pradakṣiṇam ||44||

45atraiva vā'varohainameṣa dharmaḥ sanātanaḥ
|
yadi me prathamaṃ droṇaḥ śarīre prahariṣyati
|
tato'sya prahariṣyāmi nāsya kopo bhavediti||45||

46asyāvidūre hi dhanurdhvajāgre yasya dṛśyate
|
ācāryasyaiṣa putro vai aśvatthāmā mahārathaḥ ||46||

47sadā mamaiṣa mānyastu sarvaśastrabhṛtāmapi
|
etasya tvaṃ rathaṃ prāpya nivartethāḥ punaḥ punaḥ ||47||

48ya eṣa tu rathānīke suvarṇakavacāvṛtaḥ
|
senāgryeṇa tṛtīyena vyāvahāryeṇa tiṣṭhati ||48||

49yasya nāgo dhvajāgre'sau hemaketanasaṃvṛtaḥ
|
dhṛtarāṣṭrātmajaḥ śrīmāneṣa rājā suyodhanaḥ ||49||

50etasyābhimukhaṃ vīra rathaṃ pararathārujam
|
prāpayasvaiṣa rājā hi pramāthī yuddhadurmadaḥ ||50||

51eṣa droṇasya śiṣyāṇāṃ śīghrāstre prathamo mataḥ
|
etasya darśayiṣyāmi śīghrāstraṃ vipulaṃ raṇe ||51||

52nāgakakṣā tu rucirā dhvajāgre yasya tiṣṭhati
|
eṣa vaikartanaḥ karṇo viditaḥ pūrvameva te ||52||

53etasya rathamāsthāya rādheyasya durātmanaḥ
|
yatto bhavethāḥ saṅgrāme spardhate hi sadā mayā ||53||

54yastu nīlānusāreṇa pañcatāreṇa ketunā
|
hastāvāpī bṛhaddhanvā rathe tiṣṭhati vīryavān ||54||

55yasya tārārkacitro'sau dhvajo rathavare sthitaḥ
|
yasyaitat pāṇḍuraṃ chatraṃ vimalaṃ mūrdhni tiṣṭhati ||55||

56mahato rathavaṃśasya nānādhvajapatākinaḥ
|
balāhakāgre sūryo vā ya eṣa pramukhe sthitaḥ ||56||

57hemaṃ candrārkasaṃkāśaṃ kavacaṃ yasya dṛśyate
|
jātarūpaśirastrāṇaṃ manastāpayatīva me ||57||

58eṣa śāṃtanavo bhīṣmaḥ sarveṣāṃ naḥ pitāmahaḥ
|
rājaśriyābhivṛddhaśca suyodhanavaśānugaḥ ||58||

59paścādeṣa prayātavyo na me vighnakaro bhavet
|
etena yudhyamānasya yattaḥ saṃyaccha me hayān ||59||

60tato'bhyavahadavyagro vairāṭiḥ savyasācinam
|
yatrātiṣṭhatkṛpo rājanyotsyamāno dhanaṃjayam ||60||
