Mahābhārata 3.242 (Āraṇyakaparvan)

1vaiśampāyana uvāca|
gandharvaistu mahārāja bhagne karṇe mahārathe
|
samprādravaccamūḥ sarvā dhārtarāṣṭrasya paśyataḥ||1||

2tān dṛṣṭvā dravataḥ sarvān dhārtarāṣṭrān parāṅmukhān
|
duryodhano mahārājo nāsīt tatra parāṅmukhaḥ||2||

3tāmāpatantīṃ samprekṣya gandharvāṇāṃ mahācamūm
|
mahatā śaravarṣeṇa so'bhyavarṣadrindamaḥ||3||

4acintya śaravarṣaṃ tu gandharvāstasya taṃ ratham
|
duryodhanaṃ jighāṃsantaḥ samantāt paryavārayan||4||

5yugamīṣāṃ varūthaṃ ca tathaiva dhvajasārathī
|
aśvāṃstriveṇuṃ talpaṃ ca tilaśo vyadhamañcharaiḥ||5||

6duryodhanaṃ citraseno virathaṃ patitaṃ bhuvi
|
abhidrutya mahābāhurjīvagrāhamathāgrahīt||6||

7tasmin gṛhīte rājendra sthitaṃ duḥśāsanaṃ rathe
|
paryagṛhṇanta gandharvāḥ parivārya samantataḥ||7||

8viviṃśati citrasenāvādāyānye vidudruvuḥ
|
vindānuvindāvapare rājadārāṃśca sarvaśaḥ||8||

9sainyaṃ taddhārtarāṣṭrasya gandharvaiḥ samabhidrutam
|
pūrvaṃ prabhagnāḥ sahitāḥ pāṇḍavānabhyayustadā||9||

10śakaṭā paṇaveśāśca yānayugyaṃ ca sarvaśaḥ
|
śaraṇaṃ pāṇḍavān jagmurhriyamāṇe mahīpatau||10||

11sainikā ūcuḥ| priyadarśī mahābāho dhārtarāṣṭro mahābalaḥ
|
gandharvairhriyate rājā pārthāstamanudhāvata||11||

12duḥśāsano durviṣaho durmukho durjayastathā
|
vadhvā hriyante gandharvai rājadārāśca sarvaśaḥ||12||

13iti duryodhanāmātyāḥ krośanto rājagṛddhinaḥ
|
ārtā dīnāstataḥ sarve yudhiṣṭhiramupāgaman||13||

14tāṃstathā vyathitān dīnānbhikṣamāṇānyudhiṣṭhiram
|
vṛddhān duryodhanāmātyān bhīmaseno'bhyabhāṣata||14||

15mahatā hi prayatnena sannahya gajavājibhiḥ
|
asmābhiryadanuṣṭheyaṃ gandharvaistadanuṣṭhitam||15||

16anyathā vartamānānāmaryo jāto'yamanyathā
|
durmantritamidaṃ tāvadrājño durdyūtadedhinaḥ||16||

17dveṣṭāramanye klībasya pātayantīti naḥ śrutam
|
idaṃ kṛtaṃ naḥ pratyakṣaṃ gandharvairatimānuṣam||17||

18diṣṭyā loke pumānasti kaścidasmatpriye sthitaḥ
|
yenāsmākaṃ hṛto bhāra āsīnānāṃ sukhāvahaḥ||18||

19śītavātātapasahāṃstapasā caiva karśitān
|
samastho viṣamasthān hi draṣṭumicchati durmatiḥ||19||

20adharmacāriṇastasya kauravyasya durātmanaḥ
|
ye śīlamanuvartanti te paśyanti parābhavam||20||

21adharmo hi kṛtastena yenaitadupaśikṣitam
|
anṛśaṃsāstu kaunteyāstatpratyakṣaṃ bravīmi vaḥ||21||

22evaṃ bruvāṇaṃ konteyaṃ bhīmasenamapasvaram
|
na kālaḥ paruṣasyāyamiti rājā'bhyabhāṣata||22||
