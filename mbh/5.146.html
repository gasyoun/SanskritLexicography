Mahābhārata 5.146 (Udyogaparvan)

1vaiśampāyana uvāca|
tataḥ sūryānniścaritāṃ karṇaḥ śuśrāva bhāratīm
|
duratyayāṃ praṇayinīṃ pitṛvadbhāskareritām ||1||

2satyamāha pṛthā vākyaṃ karṇa mātṛvacaḥ kuru
|
śreyaste syānnaravyāghra sarvamācaratastathā ||2||

3vaiśampāyana uvāca|
evamuktasya mātrā ca svayaṃ pitrā ca bhānunā
|
cacāla naiva karṇasya matiḥ satyadhṛtestadā ||3||

4karṇa uvāca|
na caitacchraddadhe vākyaṃ kṣatriye bhāṣitaṃ tvayā
|
dharmadvāraṃ mamaitatsyānniyogakaraṇaṃ tava ||4||

5akaronmayi yat pāpaṃ bhavatī sumahātyayam
|
apākīrṇo'smi yanmātastadyaśaḥkīrtināśanam ||5||

6ahaṃ cetkṣatriyo jāto na prāptaḥ kṣatrasatkriyām
|
tvatkṛte kiṃ nu pāpīyaḥ śatruḥ kuryānmamāhitam ||6||

7kriyākāle tvanukrośamakṛtvā tvamimaṃ mama
|
hīnasaṃskārasamayamadya māṃ samacūcudaḥ ||7||

8na vai mama hitaṃ pūrvaṃ mātṛvacceṣṭitaṃ tvayā
|
sā māṃ sambodhayasyadya kevalātmahitaiṣiṇī ||8||

9kṛṣṇena sahitāt ko vai na vyatheta dhanañjayāt
|
ko'dya bhītaṃ na māṃ vidyāt pārthānāṃ samitiṃ gatam||9||

10abhrātā viditaḥ pūrvaṃ yuddhakāle prakāśitaḥ
|
pāṇḍavān yadi gacchāmi kiṃ māṃ kṣatraṃ vadiṣyati ||10||

11sarvakāmaiḥ saṃvibhaktaḥ pūjitaśca yathāsukham
|
ahaṃ vai dhārtarāṣṭrāṇāṃ kuryāṃ tadaphalaṃ katham ||11||

12upanahya parairvairaṃ ye māṃ nityamupāsate
|
namaskurvanti ca sadā vasavo vāsavaṃ yathā ||12||

13mama prāṇena ye śatrūn śaktāḥ pratisamāsitum
|
manyante te kathaṃ teṣāmahaṃ chindyāṃ manoratham ||13||

14mayā plavena saṅgrāmaṃ titīrṣanti duratyayam
|
apāre pārakāmā ye tyajeyaṃ tānahaṃ katham ||14||

15ayaṃ hi kālaḥ samprāpto dhārtarāṣṭropajīvinām
|
nirveṣṭavyaṃ mayā tatra prāṇānaparirakṣatā ||15||

16kṛtārthāḥ subhṛtā ye hi kṛtyakāle hyupasthite
|
anavekṣya kṛtaṃ pāpā vikurvantyanavasthitāḥ ||16||

17rājakilbiṣiṇāṃ teṣāṃ bhartṛpiṇḍāpahāriṇām
|
naivāyaṃ na paro loko vidyate pāpakarmaṇām ||17||

18dhṛtarāṣṭrasya putrāṇāmarthe yotsyāmi te sutaiḥ
|
balaṃ ca śaktiṃ cāsthāya na vai tvayyanṛtaṃ vade ||18||

19ānṛśaṃsyamatho vṛttaṃ rakṣan satpuruṣocitam
|
ato'rthakaramapyetanna karomyadya te vacaḥ ||19||

20na ca te'yaṃ samārambho mayi mogho bhaviṣyati
|
vadhyān viṣahyān saṅgrāme na haniṣyāmi te sutān ||20||

21yudhiṣṭhiraṃ ca bhīmaṃ ca yamau caivārjunādṛte
|
arjunena samaṃ yuddhamapi yaudhiṣṭhire bale ||21||

22arjunaṃ hi nihatyājau samprāptaṃ syāt phalaṃ mayā
|
yaśasā cāpi yujyeyaṃ nihataḥ savyasācinā ||22||

23na te jātu na śiṣyanti putrāḥ pañca yaśasvini
|
nirarjunāḥ sakarṇā vā sārjunā vā hate mayi ||23||

24iti karṇavacaḥ śrutvā kuntī duḥkhāt pravepatī
|
uvāca putramāśliṣya karṇaṃ dhairyādakampanam ||24||

25evaṃ vai bhāvyametena kṣayaṃ yāsyanti kauravāḥ
|
yathā tvaṃ bhāṣase karṇa daivaṃ tu balavattaram ||25||

26tvayā caturṇāṃ bhrātṝṇāmabhayaṃ śatrukarśana
|
dattaṃ tat pratijānīhi saṅgarapratimocanam ||26||

27anāmayaṃ svasti ceti pṛthā'tho karṇamabravīt
|
tāṃ karṇo'tha tathetyuktvā tatastau jagmatuḥ pṛthak ||27||
