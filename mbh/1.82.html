Mahābhārata 1.82 (Ādiparvan)

1vaiśaṃpāyana uvāca|
yayātiḥ svapuraṃ prāpya mahendrapurasaṃnibham
|
praviśyāntaḥpuraṃ tatra devayānīṃ nyaveśayat||1||

2devayānyāścānumate sutāṃ tāṃ vṛṣaparvaṇaḥ
|
aśokavanikābhyāśe gṛhaṃ kṛtvā nyaveśayat||2||

3vṛtāṃ dāsīsahasreṇa śarmiṣṭhāṃ vārṣaparvaṇīm
|
vāsobhirannapānaiśca saṃvibhajya susatkṛtām||3||

4devayānyā tu sahitaḥ sa nṛpo nahuṣātmajaḥ
|
vijahāra bahūnabdāndevavanmuditaḥ sukhī||4||

5ṛtukāle tu saṃprāpte devayānī varāṅganā
|
lebhe garbhaṃ prathamataḥ kumāraṃ ca vyajāyata||5||

6gate varṣasahasre tu śarmiṣṭhā vārṣaparvaṇī
|
dadarśa yauvanaṃ prāptā ṛtuṃ sā cānvacintayat||6||

7ṛtukālasya saṃprāpto na ca me'sti patirvṛtaḥ
|
kiṃ prāptaṃ kiṃ nu kartavyaṃ kiṃ vā kṛtvā sukhaṃ bhavet||7||

8devayānī prajātā'sau vṛthā'haṃ prāptayauvanā
|
yathā tayā vṛto bhartā tathaivāhaṃ vṛṇomi tam||8||

9rājñā putraphalaṃ deyamiti me niścitā matiḥ
|
apīdānīṃ sa dharmātmā iyānme darśanaṃ rahaḥ||9||

10atha niṣkramya rājā'sau tasminkāle yadṛcchayā
|
aśokavanikābhyāśe śarmiṣṭhāṃ prekṣya dhīṣṭhitaḥ||10||

11tamekaṃ rahite dṛṣṭvā śarmiṣṭhā cāruhāsinī
|
pratyudgamyāñjaliṃ kṛtvā rājānaṃ vākyamabravīt||11||

12śarmiṣṭhovāca|
somasyendrasya viṣṇorvā yamasya varuṇasya vā
|
tava vā nāhuṣagṛhe kaḥ striyaṃ draṣṭumarhati||12||

13rūpābhijanaśīlairhi tvaṃ rājanvettha māṃ sadā
|
sā tvāṃ yāce prasādyāhamṛtuṃ dehi narādhipa||13||

14yayātiruvāca|
vedmi tvāṃ śīlasaṃpannāṃ daityakanyāmaninditām
|
rūpaṃ ca te na paśyāmi sūcyagramapi ninditam||14||

15abravīduśanā kāvyo devayānīṃ yadā'vaham
|
neyamāhvayitavyā te śayane vārṣaparvaṇī||15||

16śarmiṣṭhovāca|
na narmayuktaṃ vacanaṃ hinasti na strīṣu rājanna vivāhakāle
|
prāṇātyaye sarvadhanāpahāre pañcānṛtānyāhurapātakāni||16||

17pṛṣṭaṃ tu sākṣye pravadantamanyathā vadanti mithyā patitaṃ narendra
|
ekārthatāyāṃ tu samāhitāyāṃ mithyā vadantaṃ tvanṛtaṃ hinasti||17||

18yayātiruvāca|
rājā pramāṇaṃ bhūtānāṃ sa naśyeta mṛṣā vadan
|
arthakṛcchramapi prāpya na mithyā kartumutsahe||18||

19śarmiṣṭhovāca|
samāvetau mato rājanpatiḥ sakhyāśca yaḥ patiḥ
|
samaṃ vivāhamityāhuḥ sakhyā me'si vṛtaḥ patiḥ||19||

20yayātiruvāca|
dātavyaṃ yācamānebhya iti me vratamāhitam
|
tvaṃ ca yācasi māṃ kāmaṃ brūhi kiṃ karavāṇi te||20||

21śarmiṣṭhovāca|
adharmātpāhi māṃ rājandharmaṃ ca pratipādaya
|
tvatto'patyavatī loke careyaṃ dharmamuttamam||21||

22traya evādhanā rājanbhāryā dāsastathā sutaḥ
|
yatte samadhigacchanti yasyaite tasya taddhanam||22||

23devayānyā bhujiṣyā'smi vaśyā ca tava bhārgavī
|
sā cāhaṃ ca tvayā rājanbhajanīye bhajasva mām||23||

24vaiśaṃpāyana uvāca|
evamuktastu rājā sa tathyamityabhijajñivān
|
pūjayāmāsa śarmiṣṭhāṃ dharmaṃ ca pratyapādayat||24||

25sa samāgamya śarmiṣṭhāṃ yathākāmamavāpya ca
|
anyonyaṃ cābhisaṃpūjya jagmatustau yathāgatam||25||

26tasminsamāgame subhrūḥ śarmiṣṭhā cāruhāsinī
|
lebhe garbhaṃ prathamatastasmānnṛpatisattamāt||26||

27prayajñe ca tataḥ kāle rājanrājīvalocanā
|
kumāraṃ devagarbhābha rājīvanibhalocanam||27||
