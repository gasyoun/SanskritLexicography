Mahābhārata 13.54 (Anuśāsanaparvan)

1bhīṣma uvāca|
tataḥ sa rājā rātryante pratibuddho mahāmanāḥ
|
kṛtapūrvāhṇikaḥ prāyātsabhāryastadvanaṃ prati ||1||

2tato dadarśa nṛpatiḥ prāsādaṃ sarvakāñcanam
|
maṇistambhasahasrāḍhyaṃ gandharvanagaropamam
|
tatra divyānabhiprāyāndadarśa kuśikastadā ||2||

3parvatān rūpyasānūṃśca nalinīśca sa paṅkajāḥ
|
citraśālāśca vividhāstoraṇāni ca bhārata
|
śādvalopacitāṃ bhūmiṃ tathā kāñcanakuṭṭimām ||3||

4sahakārānpraphullāṃśca ketakoddālakānvarān
|
aśokānsahakundāṃśca phullāṃścaivātimuktakān ||4||

5campakāṃstilakān bhavyānpanasānvaṃjulānapi
|
puṣpitānkarṇikārāṃśca tatra tantra dadarśa ha ||5||

6śyāmānvāraṇapuṣpāṃśca tathā'ṣṭapadikā latāḥ
|
tatra tatra pariklṛptā dadarśa sa mahīpatiḥ ||6||

7ramyānpadmotpaladharānsarvartukusumāṃstathā
|
vimānapratimāṃścāpi prāsādānśailasannibhān ||7||

8śītalāni ca toyāni kvaciduṣṇāni bhārata
|
āsanāni vicitrāṇi śayanapravarāṇi ca ||8||

9paryaṅkān ratnasauvarṇānparārdhyāstaraṇāvṛtān
|
bhakṣyaṃ bhojyamanantaṃ ca tatra tatropakalpitam ||9||

10vāṇīvādāñchukāṃścaiva sārikānbhṛṅgarājakān
|
kokilāñchatapatrāṃśca sakoyaṣṭikakukkubhān ||10||

11mayūrānkukkuṭāṃścāpi dātyūhān jīvajīvakān
|
cakorānvānarānhaṃsānsārasāṃścakrasāhvayān ||11||

12samantataḥ pramuditāndadarśa sumanoharān
|
kvacidapsarasāṃ saṃghān gandharvāṇāṃ ca pārthiva ||12||

13kāntābhiraparāṃstatra pariṣvaktāndadarśa ha
|
na dadarśa ca tānbhūyo dadarśa ca punarnupaḥ ||13||

14gītadhvaniṃ sumadhuraṃ tathaivādhyāpanadhvanim
|
haṃsānsumadhurāṃścāpi tatra śuśrāva pārthivaḥ ||14||

15taṃ dṛṣṭvā'tyadbhutaṃ rājā manasā'cintayattadā
|
svapno'yaṃ cittavibhraṃśa utāho satyameva tu ||15||

16aho saha śarīreṇa prāpto'smi paramāṃ gatim
|
uttarānvā kurūnpuṇyānathavā'pyamarāvatīm ||16||

17kiñcedaṃ mahadāścaryaṃ saṃpaśyāmītyacintayat
|
evaṃ sañcintayanneva dadarśa munipuṅgavam ||17||

18tasminvimāne sauvarṇe maṇistambhasamākule
|
mahārhe śayane divye śayānaṃ bhṛgunandanam ||18||

19tamabhyayātpraharṣeṇa narendraḥ saha bhāryayā
|
antarhitastato bhūyaścyavanaḥ śayanaṃ ca tat ||19||

20tato'nyasminvanoddeśe punareva dadarśa tam
|
kauśyāṃ vṛsyāṃ samāsīnaṃ japamānaṃ mahāvratam ||20||

21evaṃ yogabalādvipro mohayāmāsa pārthivam
|
kṣaṇena taddhanaṃ caiva te caivāpsarasāṃ gaṇāḥ ||21||

22gandharvāḥ pādapāścaiva sarvamantaradhīyata
|
niḥśabdamabhavaccāpi gaṅgākūlaṃ punanṛpa ||22||

23kuśavalmīkabhūyiṣṭhaṃ babhūva ca yathā purā
|
tataḥ sa rājā kuśikaḥ sabhāryastena karmaṇā ||23||

24vismayaṃ paramaṃ prāptastaddṛṣṭvā mahada‌dbhutam
|
tataḥ provāca kuśiko bhāryāṃ harṣasamanvitaḥ ||24||

25paśya bhadre yathā bhāvāścitrā dṛṣṭāḥ sudurlabhāḥ
|
prasādādbhṛgumukhyasya kimanyatra tapobalāt ||25||

26tapasā tadavāpyaṃ hi yattu śakyaṃ manorathaiḥ
|
trailokyarājyādapi hi tapa eva viśiṣyate ||26||

27tapasā hi sutaptena śakyo mokṣastapobalāt
|
aho prabhāvo brahmarṣeścyavanasya mahātmanaḥ ||27||

28icchayaiṣa tapovīryādanyāঁllokānsṛjedapi
|
brāhmaṇā eva jāyeranpuṇyavāgbuddhikarmaṇaḥ ||28||

29utsahediha kṛtvaiva ko'nyo vai cyavanādṛte
|
brāhmaṇyaṃ durlabhaṃ loke rājyaṃ hi sulabhaṃ naraiḥ ||29||

30brāhmaṇyasya prabhāvāddhi rathe yuktau svadhuryavat
|
ityevaṃ cintayānaḥ sa viditaścyavanasya vai ||30||

31saṃprekṣyovāca nṛpatiṃ kṣipramāgamyatāmiti
|
ityuktaḥ sahabhāryastu so'bhyagacchanmahāmunim ||31||

32śirasā vandanīyaṃ tamavandata ca pārthivaḥ
|
tasyāśiṣaḥ prayujyātha sa munistaṃ narādhipam
|
niṣīdetyabravīddhīmānsāntvayanpuruṣarṣabhaḥ ||32||

33tataḥ prakṛtimāpanno bhārgavo nṛpate nṛpam
|
uvāca ślakṣṇayā vācā tarpayanniva bhārata ||33||

34rājansamyagjitānīha pañca pañca svayaṃ tvayā
|
manaḥṣaṣṭhānīndriyāṇi kṛchrānmukto'si tena vai ||34||

35samyagārādhitaḥ putra tvayā pravadatāṃ vara
|
na hi te vṛjinaṃ kiñcitsusūkṣmamapi vidyate ||35||

36anujānīhi māṃ rājangamiṣyāmi yathāgatam
|
prīto'smi tava rājendra varaśca pratigṛhyatām ||36||

37kuśika uvāca|
agnimadhye gateneva bhagavansannidhau mayā
|
vartitaṃ bhṛguśārdūla yanna dagdho'smi tadbahu ||37||

38eṣa eva varo mukhyaḥ prāpto me bhṛgunandana
|
yatprīto'si mayā brahman kulaṃ trātaṃ ca me'nagha ||38||

39eṣa me'nugraho vipra jīvite ca prayojanam
|
etadrājyaphalaṃ caiva tapasaśca phalaṃ mama ||39||

40yadi tvaṃ prītimānvipra mayi vai bhṛgunandana
|
asti me saṃśayaḥ kaścittanme vyākhyātumarhasi ||40||
