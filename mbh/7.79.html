Mahābhārata 7.79 (Droṇaparvan)

1sañjaya uvāca|
tato'rjunasya bhavanaṃ praviśyāpratimaṃ vibhuḥ
|
spṛṣṭvāṃ'bhaḥ puṇḍarīkākṣaḥ sthaṇḍile śubhalakṣaṇe ||1||

2saṃtastāra śumāṃ śayyāṃ darbhairvaidūryasannibhaḥ
|
tato mālyena vidhivallājairgandhaiḥ sumaṅgalaiḥ ||2||

3alaṃcakāra tāṃ śayyāṃ parivāryāyudhottamaiḥ
|
tataḥ spṛṣṭodake pārthe vinītāḥ paricārakāḥ ||3||

4darśayanto'ntike cakrurnaiśaṃ traiyambakaṃ balim
|
tataḥ prītamanāḥ pārtho gandhamālyaiśca mādhavam ||4||

5alaṃkṛtyopahāraṃ taṃ naiśaṃ tasmai nyavedayat
|
smayamānastu govindaḥ phālgunaṃ pratyabhāṣata ||5||

6supyatāṃ pārtha bhandraṃ te kalyāṇāya vrajāmyaham
|
sthāpayitvā tato dvāḥsthān goptṛṃścāttāyudhānnarān ||6||

7dārukānugataḥ śrīmān viveśa śibiraṃ svakam
|
śiśye ca śayane śubhre bahukṛtyaṃ vicintayan ||7||

8pārthāya sarvaṃ bhagavān śokaduḥkhāpahaṃ vidhim
|
vyada‌dyāt puṇḍarīkākṣastejodyutivivardhanam ||8||

9yogamāsthāya yuktātmā sarveṣāmīśvareśvaraḥ
|
śreyaskāmaḥ pṛthuyaśā viṣṇurjiṣṇupriyaṃkaraḥ ||9||

10na pāṇḍavānāṃ śibire kaścit suṣvāpa tāṃ niśām
|
prajāgaraḥ sarvajanaṃ hyāviveśa viśāṃpate ||10||

11putraśokābhitaptena pratijñāto mahātmanā
|
sahasā sindhurājasya vadho gāṇḍīvadhanvanā ||11||

12tat kathaṃ nu mahābāhurvāsaviḥ paravīrahā
|
pratijñāṃ saphalāṃ kuryāditi te samacintayan ||12||

13kaṣṭaṃ hīdaṃ vyavasitaṃ pāṇḍavena mahātmanā
|
sa ca rājā mahāvīryaḥ pārayatvarjunaḥ satām ||13||

14putraśokābhitaptena pratijñā mahatī kṛtā
|
bhrātaraścāpi vikrāntā bahulāni balāni ca ||14||

15dhṛtarāṣṭrasya putreṇa sarvaṃ tasmai niveditam
|
sa hatvā saindhavaṃ saṃkhye punaretu dhanañjayaḥ ||15||

16jitvā ripugaṇāṃścaiva pārayannarjuno vratam
|
śvo'hatvā sindhurājaṃ vai dhūmaketuṃ pravekṣyati ||16||

17na hyāsāvanṛtaṃ kartumalaṃ pārtho dhanañjayaḥ
|
dharmaputraḥ kathaṃ rājā bhaviṣyati mṛte'rjune ||17||

18tasmin hi vijayaḥ kṛtsnaḥ pāṇḍavena samāhitaḥ
|
yadi no'sti kṛtaṃ kiñcidyadi dattaṃ hutaṃ yadi ||18||

19phalena tasya sarvasya savyasācī jayatvarīn
|
evaṃ kathayatāṃ teṣāṃ jayamāśaṃsatāṃ pramo ||19||

20kṛcchreṇa mahatā rājan rajanī vyatyavartata
|
tasyāṃ rajanyāṃ madhye tu pratibuddho janārdanaḥ ||20||

21smṛtvā pratijñāṃ pārthasya dārukaṃ pratyabhāṣata
|
arjunena pratijñātamārtena hatabandhunā ||21||

22jayadrathaṃ vadhiṣyāmi śvobhūta iti dāruka
|
tattu duryodhanaḥ śrutvā mantribhirmantrayiṣyati ||22||

23yathā jayadrathaṃ pārtho na hanyāditi saṃyuge
|
akṣauhiṇyo hi tāḥ sarvā rakṣiṣyanti jayadratham ||23||

24droṇaśca saha putreṇa sarvāstravidhipāragaḥ
|
eko vīraḥ sahasrākṣo daityadānavada‌rpahā ||24||

25so'pi taṃ notsahetājau hantuṃ droṇena rakṣitam
|
so'haṃ śvastat kariṣyāmi yathā kuntīsuto'rjunaḥ ||25||

26aprāpte'staṃ dinakare haniṣyati jayadratham
|
na hi dārānamitrāṇi jñātayo na ca bāndhavāḥ ||26||

27kaścidanyaḥ priyataraḥ kuntīputrānmamārjunāt
|
anarjunamimaṃ lokaṃ muhūrtamapi dāruka ||27||

28uddīkṣituṃ na śakno'haṃ bhavitā na ca tat tathā
|
ahaṃ vijitya tān sarvān sahasā sahayadvipān ||28||

29arjunārthe haniṣyāmi sakarṇān sasuyodhanān
|
śvo nirīkṣantu me vīryaṃ trayo lokā mahāhave ||29||

30dhanañjayārthe samare parākrāntasya dāruka
|
śvo narendrasahasrāṇi rājaputraśatāni ca ||30||

31sāśvadviparathānyājau vidraviṣyāmi dāruka
|
śvastāṃ cakrapramathitāṃ drakṣyase nṛpavāhinīm ||31||

32mayā kruddhena samare pāṇḍavārthe nipātitām
|
śvaḥ sadevāḥ sagandharvāḥ piśācoragarākṣasāḥ ||32||

33jñāsyanti lokāḥ sarve māṃ suhṛdaṃ savyasācinaḥ
|
yastaṃ dveṣṭi sa māṃ dveṣṭi yastaṃ cātu sa māmanu ||33||

34iti saṃkalpyatāṃ buddhyā śarīrārdhaṃ mamārjunaḥ
|
yathā tvaṃ me prabhātāyāmasyāṃ niśi rathottamam ||34||

35kalpayitvā yathāśāsramādāya vraja saṃyataḥ
|
gadāṃ kaumodakīṃ divyāṃ śaktiṃ cakraṃ dhanuḥ śarān ||35||

36āropya vai rathe sūta sarvopakaraṇāni ca
|
sthānaṃ ca kalpayitvā'tha rathopasthe dhvajasya me ||36||

37vainateyasya vīrasya samare rathaśobhinaḥ
|
chatraṃ jāmbūnadairjālairarkajvalanasaprabhaiḥ ||37||

38viśvakarmakṛtairdivyairaśvānapi vibhūṣitān
|
balāhakaṃ meghapuṣpaṃ śaibyaṃ sugrīvameva ca ||38||

39yuktān ‌vājivarān yattaḥ kavacī tiṣṭha dāruka
|
pāñcajanyasya nirghoṣamārṣabheṇaiva pūritam ||39||

40śrutvā ca bhairavaṃ nādamupeyāstvaṃ javena mām
|
ekāhnā'hamamarṣaṃ ca sarvaduḥkhāni caiva ha ||40||

41bhrātuḥ paitṛṣvaseyasya vyapaneṣyāmi dāruka
|
sarvopāyairyatiṣyāmi yathā bībhatsurāhave ||41||

42paśyatāṃ dhārtarāṣṭrāṇāṃ haniṣyati jayadratham
|
yasya yasya ca bībhatsurvadhe yatnaṃ kariṣyati
|
āśaṃse sārathe tatra bhavitā'sya dhruvo jayaḥ ||42||

43dāruka uvāca| jaya eva dhruvastasya kuta eva parājayaḥ
|
yasya tvaṃ puruṣavyāghra sārathyamupajagmivān ||43||

44evaṃ caitat kariṣyāmi yathā māmanuśāsasi
|
suprabhātāmimāṃ rātriṃ jayāya vijayasya hi ||44||
