Mahābhārata 12.273 (Śāntiparvan)

1yudhiṣṭhira uvāca|
kathaṃ bhavati pāpātmā kathaṃ dharmaṃ karoti vā
|
kena nirvedamādatte mokṣaṃ vā kena gacchati ||1||

2bhīṣma uvāca|
viditāḥ sarvadharmāste sthityarthaṃ tvaṃ tu pṛcchasi
|
śṛṇu mokṣaṃ sanirvedaṃ pāpaṃ dharmaṃ ca mūlataḥ ||2||

3vijñānārthaṃ hi pañcānāmicchā pūrvaṃ pravartate
|
prāpyaikaṃ jāyate kāmo dveṣo vā bharatarṣabha ||3||

4tatastadarthaṃ yatate karma cārabhate mahat
|
iṣṭānāṃ rūpagandhānāmabhyāsaṃ sa cikīrṣati ||4||

5tato rāgaḥ prabhavati dveṣaśca tadanantaram
|
tato lobhaḥ prabhavati mohaśca tadanantaram ||5||

6lobhamohābhibhūtasya rāgadveṣānvitasya ca
|
na dharme jāyate buddhirvyājāddharmaṃ karoti ca||6||

7vyājena carate dharmamartha vyājena rocate
|
vyājena siddhyamāneṣu dhaneṣu kurunandana ||7||

8tatraiva kurute buddhiṃ tataḥ pāpaṃ cikīrṣati
|
suhṛdbhirvāryamāṇo'pi paṇḍitaiścāpi bhārata ||8||

9uttaraṃ nyāsasambaddhaṃ bravīti vidhicoditam
|
adharmastrividhastasya vardhate rāgamohajaḥ ||9||

10pāpaṃ cintayate caiva prabravīti karoti ca
|
tasyādharmapravṛttasya doṣānpaśyanti sādhavaḥ ||10||

11ekaśīlāśca mitratvaṃ bhajante pāpakarmiṇaḥ
|
sa neha sukhamāpnoti kuta eva paratra vai ||11||

12evaṃ bhavati pāpātmā dharmātmānaṃ tu me śṛṇu
|
yathā kuśaladharmā sa kuśalaṃ pratipadyate ||12||

13kuśalenaiva dharmeṇa gatimiṣṭāṃ prapadyate
|
ya etānprajñayā doṣānpūvamevānupaśyati ||13||

14kuśalaḥ sukhaduḥkhānāṃ sādhūṃścāpyatha sevate
|
tasya sādhusamācārādabhyāsāccaiva vardhate ||14||

15prajñā dharme ca ramate dharmaṃ caivopajīvati
|
so'tha dharmādavāpteṣu dhaneṣu kurute manaḥ ||15||

16tasyaiva siñcate mūlaṃ guṇānpaśyati tatra vai
|
dharmātmā bhavati hyevaṃ mitraṃ ca labhate śubham ||16||

17sa mitradhanalābhāttu pretya ceha ca nandati
|
śabde, sparśe, rase, rūpe, tathā gandhe ca bhārata ||17||

18prabhutvaṃ labhate janturdharmasyaitatphalaṃ viduḥ
|
sa tu dharmaphalaṃ labdhvā na hṛṣyati yudhiṣṭhira ||18||

19atṛpyamāṇo nirvedamādatte jñānacakṣuṣā
|
prajñācakṣuryadā kāme, rase, gandhe, na rajyate ||19||

20śabde, sparśe, tathā rūpe na ca bhāvayate manaḥ
|
vimucyate tadā kāmānna ca dharmaṃ vimuñcati ||20||

21dharvatyāge ca yatate dṛṣṭvā lokaṃ kṣayātmakam
|
tato mokṣāya yatate nānupāyādupāyataḥ ||21||

22śanairnirvedamādatte pāpaṃ karma jahāti ca
|
dharmātmā caiva bhavati mokṣaṃ ca labhate param ||22||

23etat te kathitaṃ tāta yanmāṃ tvaṃ paripṛcchasi
|
pāpaṃ dharmastathā mokṣo nirvedaścaiva bhārata ||23||

24tasmāddharme pravartethāḥ sarvāvasthaṃ yudhiṣṭhira
|
dharme sthitānāṃ kaunteya siddhirbhavati śāśvatī ||24||
