Mahābhārata 5.92 (Udyogaparvan)

1vaiśampāyana uvāca|
taṃ bhuktavantamāśvastaṃ niśāyāṃ viduro'bravīt
|
nedaṃ samyagvyavasitaṃ keśavāgamanaṃ tava ||1||

2arthadharmātigo mandaḥ saṃraṃbhī ca janārdana
|
mānaghno mānakāmaśca vṛddhānāṃ śāsanātigaḥ ||2||

3dharmaśāstrātigo mūḍho durātmā pragrahaṃ gataḥ
|
aneyaḥ śreyasāṃ mando dhārtarāṣṭro janārdana ||3||

4kāmātmā prājñamānī ca mitradhruksarveśaṅkitaḥ
|
akartā cākṛtajñaśca tyaktadharmā priyānṛtaḥ||4||

5mūḍhaścākṛtabuddhiśca indriyāṇāmanīśvaraḥ
|
kāmānusārī kṛtyeṣu sarveṣvakṛtaniścayaḥ ||5||

6etaiścānyaiśca bahubhirdoṣaireva samanvitaḥ
|
tvayocyamānaḥ śreyo'pi saṃraṃbhānna grahīṣyati ||6||

7bhīṣme droṇe kṛpe karṇe droṇaputre jayadrathe
|
bhūyasīṃ vartate vṛttiṃ na śame kurute manaḥ ||7||

8niścitaṃ dhārtarāṣṭrāṇāṃ sakarṇānāṃ janārdana
|
bhīṣmadroṇamukhān pārthā na śaktāḥ prativīkṣitum ||8||

9senāsamudayaṃ kṛtvā pārthivaṃ madhusūdana
|
kṛtārthaṃ manyate bāla ātmānamavicakṣaṇaḥ ||9||

10ekaḥ karṇaḥ parān jetuṃ samartha iti niścitam
|
dhārtarāṣṭrasya durbuddheḥ sa śamaṃ nopayāsyati ||10||

11saṃvicca dhārtarāṣṭrāṇāṃ sarveṣāmeva keśava
|
śame prayatamānasya tava saubhrātrakāṅkṣiṇaḥ ||11||

12na pāṇḍavānāmasmābhiḥ pratideyaṃ yathocitam
|
iti vyavasitāsteṣu vacanaṃ syānnirarthakam ||12||

13yatra sūktaṃ duruktaṃ ca samaṃ syānmadhusūdana
|
na tatra pralapet prājño badhireṣviva gāyanaḥ ||13||

14avijānatsu mūḍheṣu nirmaryādeṣu mādhava
|
na tvaṃ vākyaṃ bruvanyuktaścāṇḍāleṣu dvijo yathā ||14||

15so'yaṃ balastho mūḍhaśca na kariṣyati te vacaḥ
|
tasminnirarthakaṃ vākyamuktaṃ sampatsyate tava ||15||

16teṣāṃ samupaviṣṭānāṃ sarveṣāṃ pāpacetasām
|
tava madhyāvataraṇaṃ mama kṛṣṇa na rocate ||16||

17durbuddhīnāmaśiṣṭānāṃ bahūnāṃ duṣṭacetasām
|
pratīpaṃ vacanaṃ madhye tava kṛṣṇa na rocate ||17||

18anupāsitavṛddhatvācchriyo darpācca mohitaḥ
|
vayodarpādamarṣācca na te śreyo grahīṣyati ||18||

19balaṃ balavadapyasya yadi vakṣyasi mādhava
|
tvayyasya mahatī śaṅkā na kariṣyati te vacaḥ ||19||

20nedamadya yudhā śakyamindreṇāpi sahāmaraiḥ
|
iti vyavasitāḥ sarve dhārtarāṣṭrā janārdana ||20||

21teṣvevamupapanneṣu kāmakrodhānuvartiṣu
|
samarthamapi te vākyamasamarthaṃ bhaviṣyati ||21||

22madhye tiṣṭhanhastyanīkasya mando rathāśvayuktasya balasya mūḍhaḥ
|
duryodhano manyate vītabhītiḥ kṛtsnā mayeyaṃ pṛthivī jiteti ||22||

23āśaṃsate vai dhṛtarāṣṭrasya putro mahārājyamasapatnaṃ pṛthivyām
|
tasmin śamaḥ kevalo nopalabhyo baddhaṃ santaṃ manyate labdhamartham ||23||

24paryasteyaṃ pṛthivī kālapakvā duryodhanārthe pāṇḍavān yoddhukāmāḥ
|
samāgatāḥ sarvayodhāḥ pṛthivyāṃ rājānaśca kṣitipālaiḥ sametāḥ ||24||

25sarve caite kṛtavairāḥ purastāt tvayā rājāno hṛtasārāśca kṛṣṇa
|
tavodvegātsaṃśritā dhārtarāṣṭrān susaṃhatāḥ saha karṇena vīrāḥ ||25||

26tyaktātmānaḥ saha duryodhanena hṛṣṭā yoddhuṃ pāṇḍavān sarvayodhāḥ
|
teṣāṃ madhye praviśethā yadi tvaṃ na tanmataṃ mama dāśārha vīra ||26||

27teṣāṃ samupaviṣṭānāṃ bahūnāṃ duṣṭacetasām
|
kathaṃ madhyaṃ prapadyethāḥ śatrūṇāṃ śatrukarśana ||27||

28sarvathā tvaṃ mahābāho devairapi durutsahaḥ
|
prabhāvaṃ pauruṣaṃ buddhiṃ jānāmi tava śatruhan ||28||

29yā me prītiḥ pāṇḍaveṣu bhūyaḥ sā tvayi mādhava
|
premṇā ca bahumānācca sauhṛdācca bravīmyaham ||29||

30yā me prītiḥ puṣkarākṣa tvaddarśanasamudbhavā
|
sā kimākhyāyate tubhyamantarātmā'si dehinām ||30||
