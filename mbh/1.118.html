Mahābhārata 1.118 (Ādiparvan)

1janamejaya uvāca|
kathito dhārtarāṣṭrāṇāmārṣaḥ sambhava uttamaḥ
|
amanuṣyo manuṣyāṇāṃ bhavatā brahmavādinā||1||

2nāmadheyāni cāpyeṣāṃ kathyamānāni bhāgaśaḥ
|
tvattaḥ śrutāni me brahmanprāṇḍavānāṃ ca kīrtaya||2||

3te hi sarve mahātmāno devarājaparākramāḥ
|
tvayaivāṃśāvataraṇe devabhāgāḥ prakīrtitāḥ||3||

4etadicchāmyahaṃ śrotumatimānuṣakarmaṇām
|
teṣāmājananaṃ sarvaṃ vaiśaṃpāyana kīrtaya||4||

5vaiśaṃpāyana uvāca|
rājā pāṇḍurmahāraṇye mṛgavyālaniṣevite
|
caranmaithunadharmasthaṃ dadarśa mṛgayūthapam||5||

6tatastaṃ ca mṛgīṃ caiva rukmapuṅkhaiḥ supatribhiḥ
|
nirbibheda śaraistīkṣṇaiḥ pāṇḍuḥ pañcabhirāśugaiḥ||6||

7sa ca rājanmahātejā ṛṣiputrastapodhanaḥ
|
bhāryayā saha tejasvī mṛgarūpeṇa saṅgataḥ||7||

8saṃyuktaśca tayā mṛgyā mānuṣīmīrayangiram
|
kṣaṇena patito bhūmau vilalāpākulendriyaḥ||8||

9mṛga uvāca|
kāmamanyuparītā hi buddhyā virahitā api
|
varjayanti nṛśaṃsāni pāpeṣvapi ratā narāḥ||9||

10na vidhiṃ grasate prajñā prajñāṃ tu grasate vidhiḥ
|
vidhiparyāgatānarthānprājño na pratipadyate||10||

11śasvaddharmātmanāṃ mukhye kule jātasya bhārata
|
kāmalobhābhibhūtasya kathaṃ te calitā matiḥ||11||

12pāṇḍuruvāca|
śatrūṇāṃ yā vadhe vṛttiḥ sā mṛgāṇāṃ vadhe smṛtā
|
rājñāṃ mṛga na māṃ mohāttvaṃ garhayitumarhasi||12||

13acchadmanā māyayā ca mṛgāṇāṃ vadha iṣyate
|
sa eva dharmo rājñāṃ tu taddhitvaṃ kiṃ nu garhase||13||

14agastyaḥ satramāsīnaścakāra mṛgayāmṛṣiḥ
|
āraṇyānsarvadaivatyānmṛgānprokṣya mahāvane||14||

15pramāṇadṛṣṭadharmeṇa kathamasmānvigarhase
|
agastyasyābhicāreṇa yuṣmākaṃ ca vapā hutā||15||

16mṛga uvāca|
na ripūnvai samuddiśya vimuñcanti narāḥ śarān
|
randhra eṣāṃ viśeṣeṇa vadhakālaḥ praśasyate||16||

17pāṇḍuruvāca|
pramattamapramattaṃ vā vivṛtaṃ ghnanti caujasā
|
upāyairvividhaistīkṣṇaiḥ kasmānmṛga vigarhase||17||

18mṛga uvāca|
nāhaṃ ghnantaṃ mṛganrājanvigarhe cātmakāraṇāt
|
maithunaṃ tu pratīkṣyaṃ me tvayehādyānṛśaṃsyataḥ||18||

19sarvabhūtahite kāle sarvabhūtepsite tathā
|
ko hi vidvānmṛgaṃ hanyāccarantaṃ maithunaṃ vane||19||

20asyāṃ mṛgyāṃ ca rājendra harṣānmaithunamācaram
|
puruṣārthaphalaṃ kartuṃ tattvayā viphalīkṛtam||20||

21pauravāṇāṃ mahārāja teṣāmakliṣṭakarmaṇām
|
vaṃśe jātasya kauravya nānurūpamidaṃ tava||21||

22nṛśaṃsaṃ karma sumahatsarvalokavigarhitam
|
asvargyamayaśasyaṃ cāpyadharmiṣṭhaṃ ca bhārata||22||

23strībhogānāṃ viśeṣajñaḥ śāstradharmārthatattvavit
|
nārhastvaṃ surasaṅkāśa kartumasvargyamīdṛśam||23||

24tvayā nṛśaṃsakartāraḥ pāpācārāśca mānavāḥ
|
nigrāhyāḥ pārthivaśreṣṭha trivargaparivarjitāḥ||24||

25kiṃ kṛtaṃ te naraśreṣṭha māmihānāgasaṃ ghnatā
|
muniṃ mūlaphalāhāraṃ mṛgaveṣadharaṃ nṛpa||25||

26vasamānamaraṇyeṣu nityaṃ śamaparāyaṇam
|
tvayā'haṃ hiṃsito yasmāttasmāttvāmapyahaṃ śape||26||

27dvayornṛśaṃsakartāramavaśaṃ kāmamohitam
|
jīvitāntakaro bhāva evamevāgamiṣyati||27||

28ahaṃ hi kiṃdamo nāma tapasā bhāvito muniḥ
|
vyapatrapanmanuṣyāṇāṃ mṛgyāṃ maithunamācaram||28||

29mṛgo bhūtvā mṛgaiḥ sārdhaṃ carāmi gahane vane
|
na tu te brahmahatyeyaṃ bhaviṣyatyavijānataḥ||29||

30mṛgarūpadharaṃ hatvā māmevaṃ kāmamohitam
|
asya tu tvaṃ phalaṃ mūḍha prāpsyasīdṛśameva hi||30||

31priyayā saha saṃvāsaṃ prāpya kāmavimohitaḥ
|
tvamapyasyāmavasthāyāṃ pretalokaṃ gamiṣyasi||31||

32antakāle hi saṃvāsaṃ yayā gantāsi kāntayā
|
pretarājapuraṃ prāptaṃ sarvabhūtaduratyayam
|
bhaktyā matimatāṃ śreṣṭha saiva tvā'nugamiṣyati||32||

33vartamānaḥ sukhe duḥkhaṃ yathāhaṃ prāpitastvayā
|
tathā tvāṃ ca sukhaṃ prāptaṃ duḥkhamabhyāgamiṣyati||33||

34vaiśaṃpāyana uvāca|
evamuktvā suduḥkhārto jīvitātsa vyamucyata
|
mṛgaḥ pāṇḍuśca duḥkhārtaḥ kṣaṇena samapadyata||34||
