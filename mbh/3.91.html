Mahābhārata 3.91 (Āraṇyakaparvan)

1vaiśaṃpāyana uvāca| evaṃ saṃbhāṣamāṇe tu dhaumye kauravanandana
|
lomaśaḥ sa mahātejā ṛṣistatrājagāma ha||1||

2taṃ pāṇḍavāgrajo rājā sagaṇo brāhmaṇāśca te
|
upātiṣṭhanmahābhāgaṃ divi śakramivāmarāḥ||2||

3samabhyarcya yathānyāyaṃ dharmaputro yudhiṣṭhiraḥ
|
papracchāgamane hetumaṭane ca prayojanam||3||

4sa pṛṣṭaḥ pāṇḍuputreṇa prīyamāṇo mahāmanāḥ
|
uvāca ślakṣṇayā vācā harṣayanniva pāṇḍavān||4||

5saṃcarannasmi kaunteya sarvāṃllokānyadṛcchayā
|
gataḥ śakrasya bhavanaṃ tatrāpaśyaṃ sureśvaram||5||

6tava ca bhrātaraṃ vīramapaśyaṃ savyasācinam
|
śakrasyārdhāsanagataṃ tatra me vismayo mahān||6||

7āsītpuruṣaśārdūla dṛṣṭvā pārthaṃ tathāgatam
|
āha māṃ tatra deveśo gaccha pāṇḍusutānprati||7||

8sohamabhyāgataḥ kṣipraṃ didṛkṣustvāṃ sahānujam
|
vacanātpuruhūtasya pārthas ca mahātmanaḥ||8||

9ākhyāsye te priyaṃ tāta mahatpāṇḍavanandana
|
ṛṣibhiḥ sahito rājankṛṣṇayā caiva tacchṛṇu||9||

10yattvayokto mahābāhurastrārthaṃ bharatarṣabha
|
tadastramāptaṃ pārthena rudrādapratimaṃ vibho||10||

11yattadbrahmaśiro nāma tapasā rudramāgamat
|
amṛtādutthitaṃ raudraṃ tallabdhaṃ savyasācinā||11||

12tatsamantraṃ sasaṃhāraṃ saprāyaścittamaṅgalam
|
vajramastrāṇi cānyāni daṇḍādīni yudhiṣṭhira||12||

13yamātkuberādvaruṇādindrācca kurunandana
|
astrāṇyadhītavānpārtho divyānyamitavikramaḥ||13||

14viśvāvaso'stu tanayādgītaṃ nṛtyaṃ ca sāma ca
|
vāditraṃ ca yathānyāyaṃ pratyavindadyathāvidhi||14||

15evaṃ kṛtāstraḥ kaunteyo gāndharvaṃ vedamāptavān
|
sukhaṃ vasati bībhatsuranujasyānujastava||15||

16yadarthaṃ māṃ suraśreṣṭha idaṃ vacanamabravīt
|
tacca te kathayiṣyāmi yudhiṣṭhira nibodha me||16||

17bhavānmanuṣyaloke'pi gamiṣyati na saṃśayaḥ
|
brūyādyudhiṣṭhiraṃ tatra vacanānme dvijottama||17||

18āgamiṣyati te bhrātā kṛtāstraḥ kṣipramarjunaḥ
|
surakāryaṃ mahatkṛtvā yadaśakyaṃ divaukasaiḥ||18||

19tapasāpi tvamātmānaṃ yojaya bhrātṛbhiḥ saha
|
tapaso hi paraṃ nāsti tapasā vindate mahat||19||

20ahaṃ ca karṇaṃ jānāmi yathāvadbharatarṣabha
|
satyasandhaṃ mahotsāhaṃ mahāvīryaṃ mahābalam||20||

21mahāhaveṣvapratimaṃ mahāyuddhaviśāradam
|
mahādhanurdharaṃ vīraṃ mahāstraṃ varavarṇinam||21||

22maheśvarasutaprakhyamādityatanayaṃ prabhum
|
tathārjunamatiskandhaṃ sahajolvaṇapauruṣam||22||

23na sa pārthasya saṃgrāme kalāmarhati ṣoḍaśīm
|
yaccāpi te bhayaṃ karṇānmanasisthamariṃdama||23||

24taccāpyapahariṣyāmi savyasācinyupāgate
|
yacca te mānasaṃ vīra tīrthayātrāmimāṃ prati
|
sa maharṣirlomaśaste kathayiṣyatyasaṃśayam||24||

25yacca kiṃcittapoyuktaṃ phalaṃ tīrtheṣu bhārata
|
brahmarṣireṣa brūyātte na tacchraddheyamanyathā||25||
