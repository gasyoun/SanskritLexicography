Mahābhārata 12.103 (Śāntiparvan)

1yudhiṣṭhira uvāca|
kathaṃ mṛdau kathaṃ tīkṣṇe mahāpakṣe ca pārthiva
|
ādau varteta nṛpatistanme brūhi pitāmaha||1||

2bhīṣma uvāca|
atrāpyudāharantīmamitihāsaṃ purātanam
|
bṛhaspateśca saṃvādamindrasya ca yudhiṣṭhira||2||

3bṛhaspatiṃ devapatirabhivādya kṛtāñjaliḥ
|
upasaṃgamya papraccha vāsavaḥ paravīrahā||3||

4indra uvāca| ahiteṣu kathaṃ brahmanpravarteyamatandritaḥ
|
asamucchidya caivaitānniyaccheyamupāyataḥ||4||

5senayorvyatiṣaṅgeṇa jayaḥ sādhāraṇo bhavet
|
kiṃkurvāṇaṃ na māṃ jahyājjvalitā śrīḥ pratāpinī||5||

6tato dharmārthakāmānāṃ kuśalaḥ pratibhānavān
|
rājadharmavidhānajñaḥ pratyuvāca puraṃdaram||6||

7bṛhaspatiruvāca| na jātu kalahenecchenniyantumapakāriṇaḥ
|
bālairāsevitaṃ hyetadyadamarṣo yadakṣamā||7||

8na śatrurvivṛtaḥ kāryo vadhamasyābhikāṅkṣatā
|
krodhaṃ bhayaṃ ca harṣaṃ ca niyamya svayamātmani||8||

9amitramupaseveta viśvastavadaviśvasan
|
priyameva vadennityaṃ nāpriyaṃ kiṃcidācaret||9||

10viramecchuṣkavairebhyaḥ kaṇṭhāyāsāṃśca varjayet
|
yathā vaitaṃsiko yukto dvijānāṃ sadṛśasvanaḥ||10||

11tān dvijānkurute vaśyāṃstathā yukto mahīpatiḥ
|
vaśaṃ copanayecchatrūnnihanyācca puraṃdara||11||

12na nityaṃ paribhūyārīnsukhaṃ svapiti vāsava
|
jāgartyeva hi duṣṭātmā saṃkare'gnirivotthitaḥ||12||

13na sannipātaḥ kartavyaḥ sāmānye vijaye sati
|
viśvāsyaivopasannārtho vaśe kṛtvā ripuḥ prabho||13||

14sampradhārya sahāmātyairmantravidbhirmahātmabhiḥ
|
upekṣyamāṇo'vajñāto hṛdayenāparājitaḥ||14||

15athāsya praharetkāle kiṃcidvicalite pade
|
daṇḍaṃ ca dūṣayedasya puruṣairāptakāribhiḥ||15||

16ādimadhyāvasānajñaḥ pracchannaṃ ca vidhārayet
|
balāni dūṣayedasya jānanneva pramāṇataḥ||16||

17bhedenopapradānena saṃsṛjedauṣadhaistathā
|
na tvevaṃ khalu saṃsargaṃ rocayedaribhiḥ saha||17||

18dīrghakālamapīkṣeta nihanyādeva śātravān
|
kālākāṅkṣīhi kṣapayedyathā viśrambhamāpnuyuḥ||18||

19na sadyo'rīnvihanyācca draṣṭavyo vijayo dhruvaḥ
|
na śalyaṃ vā ghaṭayati na vācā kurute vraṇam||19||

20prāpte na praharetkāle na ca saṃvartate punaḥ
|
hantukāmasya devendra puruṣasya ripūnprati||20||

21yo hi kālo vyatikrāmetpuruṣaṃ kālakākṣiṇam
|
durlabhaḥ sa punastena kālaḥ karmacikīrṣuṇā||21||

22ojaśca janayedeva saṃgṛhṇansādhusammatam
|
akāle sādhayenmitraṃ na ca prāpte prapīḍayet||22||

23vihāya kāmaṃ krodhaṃ ca tathā'haṃkārameva ca
|
yukto vivaramanvicchedahitānāṃ punaḥ punaḥ||23||

24mārdavaṃ daṇḍa ālasyaṃ pramādaśca surottama
|
māyāḥ suvihitāḥ śakra sādayantyavicakṣaṇam||24||

25nihatyaitāni catvāri māyāṃ prati vidhāya ca
|
tataḥ śaknoti śatrūṇāṃ prahartumavicārayan||25||

26yadaivaikena śakyeta guhyaṃ kartuṃ tadācaret
|
yacchanti sacivā guhyaṃ mitho viśrāvayantyapi||26||

27aśakyamiti kṛtvā vā tato'nyaiḥ saṃvidaṃ caret
|
brahmadaṇḍamadṛṣṭeṣu dṛṣṭeṣu caturaṅgiṇīm||27||

28bhedaṃ ca prathamaṃ yuṃjyāttūṣṇīmapi tathaiva ca
|
kāle prayojayedrājā tasmiṃstasmiṃstadā tadā||28||

29praṇipātaṃ ca gaccheta kāle śatrorbalīyasaḥ
|
yukto'sya vadhamanvicchedapramattaḥ pramādyataḥ||29||

30praṇipātena, dānena, vācā madhurayā brūvan
|
amitramapi seveta na ca jātu viśaṅkayet||30||

31sthānāni śaṅkitānāṃ ca nityameva vivarjayet
|
na ca teṣvāśvasedrājā jāgratīha nirākṛtāḥ||31||

32na hyato duṣkaraṃ karma kiṃcidasti surottamā
|
yathā vividhavṛttānāmaiśvaryamamarādhipa||32||

33tathā vividhavṛttānāmapi sambhava ucyate
|
yatate yogamāsthāya mitrāmitraṃ vicārayet||33||

34mṛdumapyavamanyante tīkṣṇādudvijate janaḥ
|
mā tīkṣṇo mā mṛdurbhūstvaṃ tīkṣṇo bhava mṛdurbhava||34||

35yathā vapre vegavati sarvataḥ samplutodake
|
nityaṃ vivaraṇādbādhastathā rājyaṃ pramādyataḥ||35||

36na bahūnabhiyuñjīta yaugapadyena śātravān
|
sāmnā, dānena, bhedena, daṇḍena ca puraṃdara||36||

37ekaikameṣāṃ niṣpiṣya śiṣṭeṣu nipuṇaṃ caret
|
na tu śakto'pi medhāvī sarvānevārabhennṛpa||37||

38yadā syānmahatī senā haya-nāga-rathākulā
|
padātiyantrabahulā anuraktā ṣaḍaṅginī||38||

39yadā bahuvidhāṃ vṛddhiṃ manyeta pratilomataḥ
|
tadā vivṛtya prahareddasyūnāmavicārayan||39||

40na sāmadaṇḍopaniṣatpraśasyate na mārdavaṃ śatruṣu yātrikaṃ sadā
|
na sasyaghāto na ca saṃkarakriyā na cāpi bhūyaḥ prakṛtervicāraṇā||40||

41māyāvibhedānupasarjanāni tathaiva pāpaṃ na yaśaḥprayogāt
|
āptairmanuṣyairupacārayeta pureṣu rāṣṭreṣu ca samprayuktān||41||

42purāpi caiṣāmanusṛtya bhūmipāḥ pureṣu bhogānakhilān jayanti
|
pureṣu nītiṃ vihitāṃ yathāvidhi prayojayanto balavṛtrasūdana||42||

43pradāya gūḍhāni vasūnī rājan pracchidya bhogānavadhāya ca svān
|
duṣṭānsvadoṣairiti kīrtayitvā pureṣu rāṣṭreṣu ca yojayanti||43||

44tathaiva cānyairapi śāstravedibhiḥ svalaṃkṛtaiḥ śāstravidhānadṛṣṭibhiḥ
|
suśikṣitairbhāṣyakathāviśāradaiḥ pareṣu kṛtyāmupadhārayecca||44||

45indra uvāca| kāni liṅgāni duṣṭasya bhavanti dvijasattama
|
kathaṃ duṣṭaṃ vijānīyāmetatpṛṣṭo bravīmi me||45||

46bṛhaspatiruvāca| parokṣamaguṇānāha sadguṇānabhyasūyate
|
parairvā kīrtyamāneṣu tūṣṇīmāste parāṅmukhaḥ||46||

47tūṣṇīmbhāve'pi vijñeyaṃ na cedbhavati kāraṇam
|
niśvāsaṃ coṣṭhasaṃdaṃśaṃ śirasaśca prakampanam||47||

48karotyabhīkṣṇaṃ saṃsṛṣṭamasaṃsṛṣṭaśca bhāṣate
|
adṛṣṭito na kurute dṛṣṭo naivābhibhāṣate||48||

49pṛthagetya samaśnāti nedamadya yathāvidhi
|
āsane, śayane, yāne bhāvā lakṣyā viśeṣataḥ||49||

50ārtirārte priye prītiretāvanmitralakṣaṇam
|
viparītaṃ tu boddhavyamarilakṣaṇameva tat||50||

51etānyeva yathoktāni budhyethāstridaśādhipa
|
puruṣāṇāṃ praduṣṭānāṃ svabhāvo balavattaraḥ||51||

52iti duṣṭasya vijñānamuktaṃ te surasattama
|
niśamya śāstratattvārthaṃ yathāvadamareśvara||52||

53bhīṣma uvāca|
sa tadvacaḥ śatrunibarhaṇe ratastathā cakārāvitathaṃ bṛhaspateḥ
|
cacāra kāle vijayāya cārihā vaśaṃ ca śatrūnanayatpuraṃdaraḥ||53||
