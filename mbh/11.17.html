Mahābhārata 11.17 (Strīparvan)

1vaiśampāyana uvāca|duryodhanaṃ hataṃ dṛṣṭvā gāndhārī śokakarśitā
|
sahasā nyapatadbhūmau chinneva kadalī vane||1||

2sā tu labdhvā punaḥ saṃjñāṃ vikruśya ca vilapya ca
|
duryodhanamabhiprekṣya śayānaṃ rudhirokṣitam||2||

3pariṣvajya ca gāndhārī kṛpaṇaṃ paryadevayat
|
hāhā putreti śokārtā vilalāpākulendriyā||3||

4sugūḍhajatruvipulaṃ hāraniṣkavibhūṣitam
|
vāriṇā netrajenoraḥ siñcantī śokatāpitā||4||

5samīpasthaṃ hṛṣīkeśamidaṃ vacanamabravīt
|
upasthite'sminsaṅgrāme jñātīnāṃ saṃkṣaye vibho||5||

6māmayaṃ prāha vārṣṇeya prāñjalirnṛpasattamaḥ
|
asmiñjñātisamuddharṣe jayamambā bravītu me||6||

7ityukte jānatī sarvamahaṃ svavyasanāgamam
|
abruvaṃ puruṣavyāghra yato dharmastato jayaḥ||7||

8tathā ca yudhyamānastvaṃ na vai muhyati putraka
|
dhruvaṃ śastrajitā~llokānprāpsyasyamaravatprabho||8||

9ityevamabruvaṃ pūrvaṃ naivaṃ śocāmi vai prabho
|
dhṛtarāṣṭraṃ tu śocāmi kṛpaṇaṃ hatabāndhavam||9||

10amarṣaṇaṃ yudhāṃ śreṣṭhaṃ kṛtāstraṃ yuddhadurmadam
|
śayānaṃ vīraśayane paśya mādhava me sutam||10||

11yo'yaṃ mūrdhāvasiktānāmagre yāti parantapaḥ
|
so'yaṃ pāṃsuṣu śete'dya paśya kālasya paryayam||11||

12dhruvaṃ duryodhano vīro gatiṃ na sulabhāṃ gataḥ
|
tathā hyabhimukhaḥ śete śayane vīrasevite||12||

13yaṃ purā paryupāsīnā ramayanti varastriyaḥ
|
saṃ vīraśayane suptaṃ ramayantyaśivāḥ śivāḥ||13||

14yaṃ purā paryupāsīnā ramayanti mahīkṣitaḥ
|
mahītalasthaṃ nihataṃ gṛdhrāstaṃ paryupāsate||14||

15yaṃ purā vyajanai ramyairupavījanti yoṣitaḥ
|
tamadya pakṣavyajanairupavījanti pakṣiṇaḥ||15||

16eṣa śete mahābāhurbalavānsatyavikramaḥ
|
siṃheneva dvipaḥ saṅkhye bhīmasenena pātitaḥ||16||

17paśya duryodhanaṃ kṛṣṇa śayānaṃ rudhirokṣitam
|
nihataṃ bhīmasenena gadāṃ sammṛjya bhārata||17||

18akṣauhiṇīrmahābāhurdaśa caikāṃ ca keśava
|
ānayadyaḥ purā saṅkhye so'nayānnidhanaṃ gataḥ||18||

19eṣa duryodhanaḥ śete maheṣvāso mahābalaḥ
|
śārdūla iva siṃhena bhīmasenena pātitaḥ||19||

20viduraṃ hyavamatyaiṣa pitaraṃ caiva mandabhāk
|
bālo vṛddhāvamānena mando mṛtyuvaśaṃ gataḥ||20||

21niḥsapatnā mahī yasya trayodaśa samāḥ sthitā
|
sa śete nihato bhūmau putro me pṛthivīpatiḥ||21||

22apaśyaṃ kṛṣṇa pṛthivīṃ dhārtarāṣṭrānuśāsitām
|
pūrṇāṃ hastigavāśvaiśca vārṣṇeya na tu tacciram||22||

23tāmevādya mahābāho paśyāmyanyānuśāsitām
|
hīnāṃ hastigavāśvena kinnu jīvāmi mādhava||23||

24idaṃ kaṣṭataraṃ paśya putrasyāpi vadhānmama
|
yadimāḥ paryupāsante hatāñśūrānraṇe striyaḥ||24||

25prakīrṇakeśāṃ suśroṇīṃ duryodhanaśubhaṅkagām
|
rukmavedinibhāṃ paśya kṛṣṇa lakṣmaṇamātaram||25||

26nūnameṣā purā bālā jīvamāne mahābhuje
|
bhujāvāśritya ramate subhujasya manasvinī||26||

27kathaṃ tu śatadhā nedaṃ hṛdayaṃ mama dīryate
|
paśyantyā nihataṃ putraṃ putreṇa sahitaṃ raṇe||27||

28putraṃ rudhirasaṃsiktamupajighratyaninditā
|
duryodhanaṃ tu vāmorūḥ pāṇinā parimārjatī||28||

29kinnu śocati bhartāraṃ putraṃ caiṣā manasvinī
|
tathā hyavasthitā bhāti putraṃ cāpyabhivīkṣya sā||29||

30svaśiraḥ pañcaśākhābhyāmabhihatyāyatekṣaṇā
|
patatyurasi vīrasya kururājasya mādhava||30||

31puṇḍarīkanibhā bhāti puṇḍarīkāntaraprabhā
|
mukhaṃ vimṛjya putrasya bhartuścaiva tapasvinī||31||

32yadi satyāgamāḥ santi yadi vai śrutayastathā
|
dhruvaṃ lokānavāpto'yaṃ nṛpo bāhubalārjitān||32||
