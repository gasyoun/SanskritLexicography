Mahābhārata 1.209 (Ādiparvan)

1nārada uvāca|
śrṛṇu me vistareṇemamitihāsaṃ purātanam
|
bhrātṛbhiḥ sahitaḥ pārtha yathā vṛttaṃ yudhiṣṭhira||1||

2mahāsurasyānvavāye hiraṇyakaśipoḥ purā
|
nikumbho nāma daityendrastejasvī balavānabhūt||2||

3tasya putrau mahāvīryau jātau bhīmaparākramau
|
sundopasundau daityendrau dāruṇau krūramānasau||3||

4tāvekaniścayau daityāvekakāryārthasaṃmatau
|
nirantaramavartetāṃ samaduḥkhasukhāvubhau||4||

5vinā'nyonyaṃ na bhuñjāte vinā'nyonyaṃ na jalpataḥ
|
anyo'nyasya priyakarāvanyo'nyasya priyaṃvadau||5||

6ekaśīlasamācārau dvidhaivaiko'bhavatkṛtaḥ
|
tau vivṛddhau mahāvīryau kāryeṣvapyekaniścayau||6||

7trailokyavijayārthāya samādhāyaikaniścayam
|
dīkṣāṃ kṛtvā gatau vindhyaṃ tāvugraṃ tepatustapaḥ||7||

8tau tu dīrgheṇa kālena tapoyuktau babhūvatuḥ
|
kṣutpipāsāpariśrāntau jaṭāvalkaladhāriṇau||8||

9malopacitasarvāṅgau vāyubhakṣau babhūvatuḥ
|
ātmamāṃsāni juhvantau pādāṅguṣṭhāgradhiṣṭhitau
|
ūrdhvabāhū cānimiṣau dīrghakālaṃ dhṛtavratau||9||

10tayostapaḥprabhāveṇa dīrghakālaṃ pratāpitaḥ
|
dhūmaṃ pramumuce vindhyastadbhutamivābhavat||10||

11tato devā bhayaṃ jagmurugraṃ dṛṣṭvā tayostapaḥ
|
tapovighātārthamatho devā vighnāni cakrire||11||

12ratnaiḥ pralobhayāmāsuḥ strībhiścobhau punaḥ punaḥ
|
na ca tau cakraturbhaṅgaṃ vratasya sumahāvratau||12||

13atha māyāṃ punardevāstayoścakrurmahātmanoḥ
|
bhaginyo mātaro bhāryāstayoścātmajanastathā||13||

14prapātyamānā visrastāḥ śūlahastena rakṣasā
|
bhraṣṭābharaṇakeśāntā bhraṣṭābharaṇavāsasaḥ||14||

15abhibhāṣya tataḥ sarvāstau trāhīti vicukruśuḥ
|
na ca tau cakraturbhaṅgaṃ vratasya sumahāvratau||15||

16yadā kṣobhaṃ nopayāti nārtimanyatarastayoḥ
|
tataḥ striyastā bhūtaṃ ca sarvamantaradhīyata||16||

17tataḥ pitāmahaḥ sākṣādabhigamya mahāsurau
|
vareṇa chandayāmāsa kvalokahitaḥ prabhuḥ||17||

18tataḥ sundopasundau tau bhrātarau dṛḍhavikramau
|
dṛṣṭvā pitāmahaṃ devaṃ tasthatuḥ prāñjalī tadā||18||

19ūcatuśca prabhuṃ devaṃ tatastau sahitau tadā
|
āvayostapasā'nena yadi prītaḥ pitāmahaḥ||19||

20māyāvidāvastravidau balinau kāmarūpiṇau
|
ubhāvapyamarau syāvaḥ prasanno yadi nau prabhuḥ||20||

21brahmovāca|
ṛte'maratvaṃ yuvayoḥ sarvamuktaṃ bhaviṣyati
|
anyadvṛṇītaṃ mṛtyośca vidhānamamaraiḥ samam||21||

22prabhaviṣyāva iti yanmahadabhyudyataṃ tapaḥ
|
yuvayorhetunānena nāmaratvaṃ vidhīyate||22||

23trailokyavijayārthāya bhavadbhyāmāsthitaṃ tapaḥ
|
hetunā'nena daityendrau na vā kāmaṃ karomyaham||23||

24sundopasundāvūcatuḥ
|
triṣu lokeṣu yadbhūtaṃ kiṃcitsthāvarajaṅgamam
|
sarvasmānnau bhayaṃ na syādṛte'nyo'nyaṃ pitāmaha||24||

25pitāmaha uvāca|
yatprārthitaṃ yathoktaṃ ca kāmametaddadāni vām
|
mṛtyorvidhānametacca yathāvadvā bhaviṣyati||25||

26nārada uvāca|
tataḥ pitāmaho dattvā varametattadā tayoḥ
|
nivartya tapasastau ca brahmalokaṃ jagāma ha||26||

27labdhvā varāṇi daityendrāvatha tau bhrātarāvubhau
|
avadhyau sarvalokasya svameva bhavanaṃ gatau||27||

28tau tu labdhavarau dṛṣṭvā kṛtakāmau manasvinau
|
sarvaḥ suhṛñjanastābhyāṃ praharṣamupajagmivān||28||

29tatastau tu jaṭā bhittvā maulinau saṃbabhūvatuḥ
|
mahārhābharaṇopetau virajombaradhāriṇau||29||

30akālakaumudīṃ caiva cakratuḥ sārvakālikīm
|
nityaḥ pramuditaḥ sarvastayoścaiva suhṛñjanaḥ||30||

31bhakṣyatāṃ bhujyatāṃ nityaṃ dīyatāṃ ramyatāmiti
|
gīyatāṃ pīyatāṃ ceti śabhdaścāsīdgṛhe gṛhe||31||

32tatra tatra mahānādairutkṛṣṭatalanāditaiḥ
|
hṛṣṭaṃ pramuditaṃ sarvaṃ daityānāmabhavatpuram||32||

33taistairvihārairbahubhirdaityānāṃ kāmarūpiṇām
|
samāḥ saṃkrīḍatāṃ teṣāmaharekamivābhavat||33||
