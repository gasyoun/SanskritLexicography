Mahābhārata 13.35 (Anuśāsanaparvan)

1bhīṣma uvāca|
janmanaiva mahābhāgo brāhmaṇo nāma jāyate
|
namasyaḥ sarvabhūtānāmatithiḥ prasṛtāgrabhuk ||1||

2sarvārthāḥ suhṛdastāta brāhāṇāḥ sumanomukhāḥ
|
gīrbhirmaṃgalayuktābhiranudhyāyanti pūjitāḥ ||2||

3sarvānno dviṣatastāta brāhmaṇā jātamanyavaḥ
|
gīrbhirdāruṇayuktābhirabhidhyāsurapūjitāḥ ||3||

4atra gāthāḥ purāgītāḥ kīrtayanti purāvidaḥ
|
sṛṣṭvā dvijātīn dhātā hi yathāpūrvaṃ samādadhat ||4||

5na cānyadiha kartavyaṃ kiñcidūrdhvaṃ yathāvidhi
|
gupto gopāyate brahmā śreyo vastena śobhanam ||5||

6svameva kurvatāṃ karma śrīrvo brāhmī bhaviṣyati
|
pramāṇaṃ sarvabhūtānāṃ pragrahāśca bhaviṣyatha ||6||

7na śaudraṃ karma kartavyaṃ brāhmaṇena vipaścitā
|
śaudraṃ hi kurvataḥ karma dharmaḥ samuparudhyate ||7||

8śrīśca buddhiśca tejaśca vibhūtiśca pratāpinī
|
svādhyāye caiva māhātmyaṃ vipulaṃ pratipatsyate ||8||

9hutvā cāhavanīyasthaṃ mahābhāgye pratiṣṭhitāḥ
|
agrabhojyāḥ prasūtīnāṃ śriyā brāhmayānukalpitāḥ ||9||

10śraddhayā parayā yuktā hyanabhidrohalabdhayā
|
damasvādhyāyaniratāḥ sarvānkāmānavāpsyatha ||10||

11yaccaiva mānuṣe loke yacca deveṣu kiñcana
|
sarvaṃ tu tapasā sādhyaṃ jñānena niyamena ca ||11||

12ityevaṃ brahmagītāste samākhyātā mayā'nagha
|
viprāṇāmanukampārthaṃ tena proktaṃ hi dhīmatā ||12||

13bhūyasteṣāṃ balaṃ manye yathā rājñastapasvinaḥ
|
durāsadāśca caṇḍāśca rabhasāḥ kṣiprakāriṇaḥ ||13||

14santyeṣāṃ siṃhasattvāśca vyāghrasattvāstathāpare
|
varāhamṛgasattvāñca jalasattvāstathāpare ||14||

15sarpasparśasamāḥ kecittathānye makaraspṛśaḥ
|
vibhāṣyaghātinaḥ kecittathā cakṣurhaṇo pare ||15||

16santi cāśīviṣasamāḥ santi mandāstathāpare
|
vividhānīha vṛttāni brāhmaṇānāṃ yudhiṣṭhira ||16||

17mekalā drāviḍā lāṭāḥ pauṃḍrāḥ kānvaśirāstathā
|
śauṃḍikā daradā dārvāścaurāḥ śabarabarnarāḥ ||17||

18kirātā yavanāścaiva tāstāḥ kṣatriyajātayaḥ
|
vṛṣalatvamanuprāptā brāhmaṇānāmamarṣaṇāt ||18||

19brāhmaṇānāṃ paribhavādasurāḥ salileśayāḥ
|
brāhmaṇānāṃ prasādācca devāḥ svarganivāsinaḥ ||19||

20aśakyaṃ spraṣṭumākāśamacālyo himavān giriḥ
|
adhāryā setunā gaṅgā durjayā brāhāṇā bhuvi ||20||

21na brāhmaṇavirodhena śakyā śāstuṃ basundharā
|
brāhmaṇā hi mahātmāno devānāmapi devatāḥ ||21||

22tānpūjayasva satataṃ dānena paricaryayā
|
yadīcchasi mahīṃ bhoktumimāṃ sāgaramekhalām ||22||

23pratigraheṇa tejo hi viprāṇāṃ śāmyate'nagha
|
pratigrahaṃ ye neccheyustebhyo rakṣyaṃ tvayā nṛpa ||23||
