Mahābhārata 5.6 (Udyogaparvan)

1drupada uvāca|
bhūtānāṃ prāṇinaḥ śreṣṭhāḥ prāṇināṃ buddhijīvinaḥ
|
buddhimatsu narāḥ śreṣṭhā nareṣvapi dvijātayaḥ ||1||

2dvijeṣu vaidyāḥ śreyāṃso vaidyeṣu kṛtabuddhayaḥ
|
kṛtabuddhiṣu kartāraḥ kartṛṣu brahmavādinaḥ ||2||

3sa bhavān kṛbuddhīnāṃ pradhāna iti me matiḥ
|
kulena ca viśiṣṭo'si vayasā ca śrutena ca||3||

4prajñayā sadṛśaścāsi śukreṇāṅgirasena ca
|
viditaṃ cāpi te sarvaṃ yathāvṛttaḥ sa kauravaḥ ||4||

5pāṇḍavaśca yathāvṛttaḥ kuntīputro yudhiṣṭhiraḥ
|
dhṛtarāṣṭrasya vidite vañcitāḥ pāṇḍavāḥ paraiḥ ||5||

6vidureṇānunīto'pi putramevānuvartate
|
śakunirbuddhipūrvaṃ hi kuntīputraṃ samāhvayat ||6||

7anakṣajñaṃ matākṣaḥ san kṣatravṛtte sthitaṃ śucim
|
te tathā vañcayitvā tu dharmarājaṃ yudhiṣṭhiram ||7||

8na kasyāñcidavasthāyāṃ rājyaṃ dāsyanti vai svayam
|
bhavāṃstu dharmasaṃyuktaṃ dhṛtarāṣṭraṃ bruvanvacaḥ ||8||

9manāṃsi tasya yodhānāṃ dhruvamāvartayiṣyati
|
viduraścāpi tadvākyaṃ sādhayiṣyati tāvakam||9||

10bhīṣmadroṇakṛpādīnāṃ bhedaṃ saṃjanayiṣyati
|
amātyeṣu ca bhinneṣu yodheṣu vimukheṣu ca||10||

11punarekatrakaraṇaṃ teṣāṃ karma bhaviṣyati
|
etasminnantare pārthāḥ sukhamekāgrabuddhayaḥ||11||

12senākarma kariṣyanti dravyāṇāṃ caiva sañcayam
|
bhidyamāneṣu ca sveṣu lambamāne tathā tvayi||12||

13na tathā te kariṣyanti senākarma na saṃśayaḥ
|
etatprayojanaṃ cātra prādhānyenopalabhyate||13||

14saṃgatyā dhṛtarāṣṭraśca kuryāddharmyaṃ vacastava
|
sa bhavān dharmayuktaśca dharmyaṃ teṣu samācaran ||14||

15kṛpāluṣu parikleśān pāṇḍavīyānprakīrtayan
|
vṛddheṣu kuladharmaṃ ca bruvan pūrvairanuṣṭhitam ||15||

16vibhetsyati manāṃsyeṣāmiti me nātra saṃśayaḥ
|
na ca tebhyo bhayaṃ te'sti brāhmaṇo hyasi vedavit ||16||

17dūtakarmaṇi yuktaśca sthaviraśca viśeṣataḥ
|
sa bhavān puṣyayogena muhūrtena jayena ca||17||

18kauraveyān prayātvāśu kaunteyasyārthasiddhaye || vaiśampāyana uvāca|
tathā'nuśiṣṭaḥ prayayau drupadena mahātmanā
|
purodhā vṛttasampanno nagaraṃ nāgasāhvayam ||18||

19śiṣyaiḥ parivṛto vidvān nītiśāstrārthakovidaḥ
|
pāṇḍavānāṃ hitārthāya kauravān prati jagmivān ||19||
