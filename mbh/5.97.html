Mahābhārata 5.97 (Udyogaparvan)

1vaiśampāyana uvāca|
jāmadagnyavacaḥ śrutvā kaṇvo'pi bhagavānṛṣiḥ
|
duryodhanamidaṃ vākyamabravīt kurusaṃsadi ||1||

2kaṇva uvāca|
akṣayaścāvyayaścaiva brahmā lokapitāmahaḥ
|
tathaiva bhagavantau tau naranārāyaṇāvṛṣī ||2||

3ādityānāṃ hi sarveṣāṃ viṣṇurekaḥ sanātanaḥ
|
akṣayyaścāvyayaścaiva śāśvataḥ prabhurīśvaraḥ ||3||

4nimittamaraṇāścānye candrasūryau mahī jalam
|
vāyuragnistathā''kāśaṃ grahāstārāgaṇāstathā ||4||

5te ca kṣayānte jagato hitvā lokatrayaṃ sadā
|
kṣayaṃ gacchanti vai sarve sṛjyante ca punaḥ punaḥ ||5||

6muhūrtamaraṇāstvanye mānuṣā mṛgapakṣiṇaḥ
|
tairyagyonyaśca ye cānye jīvalokacarāṃstathā ||6||

7bhūyiṣṭhena tu rājānaḥ śriyaṃ bhuktvā''yuṣaḥ kṣaye
|
taruṇāḥ pratipadyante bhoktuṃ sukṛtaduṣkṛte ||7||

8sa bhavān dharmaputreṇa śamaṃ kartumihārhati
|
pāṇḍavāḥ kuravaścaiva pālayantu vasundharām ||8||

9balavānahamityeva na mantavyaṃ suyodhana
|
balavanto balibhyo hi dṛśyante puruṣarṣabha ||9||

10na balaṃ balināṃ madhye balaṃ bhavati kaurava
|
balavanto hi te sarve pāṇḍavā devavikramāḥ ||10||

11atrāpyudāharantīmamitihāsaṃ purātanam
|
mātalerdātukāmasya kanyāṃ mṛgayato varam ||11||

12matastrilokarājasya mātalirnāma sārathiḥ
|
tasyaikaiva kule kanyā rūpato lokaviśrutā ||12||

13guṇakeśīti vikhyātā nāmnā sā devarūpiṇī
|
śriyā ca vapuṣā caiva striyo'nyāḥ sā'tiricyate ||13||

14tasyāḥ pradānasamayaṃ mātaliḥ saha bhāryayā
|
jñātvā vimamṛśe rājaṃstatparaḥ paricintayan ||14||

15dhikkhalvalaghuśīlānāmucchritānāṃ yaśasvinām
|
narāṇāṃ mṛdusatvānāṃ kule kanyāprarohaṇam ||15||

16mātuḥ kulaṃ pitṛkulaṃ yatra caiva pradīyate
|
kulatrayaṃ saṃśayitaṃ kurute kanyakā satām ||16||

17devamānuṣalokau dvau mānuṣeṇaiva cakṣuṣā
|
avagāhyaiva vicitau na ca me rocate varaḥ ||17||

18kaṇva uvāca|
na devānnaiva ditijānna gandharvānna mānuṣān
|
arocayadvarakṛte tathaiva bahulānṛṣīn ||18||

19bhāryayā'nu sa saṃmantrya saha rātrau sudharmayā
|
mātalirnāgalokāya cakāra gamane matim ||19||

20na me devamanuṣyeṣu guṇakeśyāḥ samo varaḥ
|
rūpato dṛśyate kaścinnāgeṣu bhavitā dhruvam ||20||

21ityāmantrya sudharmāṃ sa kṛtvā cābhipradakṣiṇam
|
kanyāṃ śirasyupāghrāya praviveśa mahītalam ||21||
