Mahābhārata 3.3 (Āraṇyakaparvan)

1vaiśaṃpāyana uvāca|
śaunakenaivamuktastu kuntīputro yudhiṣṭhiraḥ
|
purohitamupāgamya bhrātṛmadhye'bravīdidam||1||

2prasthitaṃ mānuyāntīme brāhmaṇā vedapāragāḥ
|
na cāsmi poṣaṇe śakto bahuduḥkhasamanvitaḥ||2||

3parityaktuṃ na śakto'smi dānaśaktiśca nāsti me
|
kathamatra mayā kāryaṃ tadbrūhi bhagavanmama||3||

4vaiśaṃpāyana uvāca|
muhūrtamiva sa dhyātvā dharmeṇānviṣya tāṃ gatim
|
yudhiṣṭhiramuvācedaṃ dhaumyo dharmabhṛtāṃ varaḥ||4||

5dhaumya uvāca|
purā sṛṣṭāni bhūtāni pīḍyante kṣudhayā bhṛśam
|
tato'nukampayā teṣāṃ savitā svapitā yathā||5||

6gatvottarāyaṇaṃ tejo rasānuddhṛtya raśmibhiḥ
|
dakṣiṇāyanamāvṛtto mahīṃ niviśate raviḥ||6||

7kṣetrabhūte tatastasminnoṣadhīroṣadhīpatiḥ
|
divastejaḥ samuddhṛtya janayāmāsa vāriṇā||7||

8niṣiktaścandratejobhiḥ svayonau nirgate raviḥ
|
oṣadhyaḥ ṣaḍrasā medhyāstadannaṃ prāṇināṃ bhuvi||8||

9evaṃ bhānumayaṃ hyannaṃ bhūtānāṃ prāṇadhāraṇam
|
pitaiṣa sarvabhūtānāṃ tasmāttaṃ śaraṇaṃ vraja||9||

10rājāno hi mahātmāno yonikarmaviśodhitāḥ
|
uddharanti prajāḥ sarvāstapa āsthāya puṣkalam||10||

11bhīmena kārtavīryeṇa vainyena nahuṣeṇa ca
|
tapoyogasamādhisthairuddhṛtā hyāpadaḥ prajāḥ||11||

12tathā tvamapi dharmātmankarmaṇā ca viśodhitaḥ
|
tapa āsthāya dharmeṇa dvijātīnbhara bhārata||12||

13janamejaya uvāca|
kathaṃ kurūṇāmṛṣabhaḥ sa tu rājā yudhiṣṭhiraḥ
|
viprārthamārādhitavānsūryamadbhutadarśanam||13||

14vaiśampāyana uvāca|
śṛṇuṣvāvahito rājanśucirbhūtvā samāhitaḥ
|
samaṃ ca kuru rājendra sampravakṣyāmyaśeṣataḥ||14||

15dhaumyena tu yathāpūrvaṃ pārthāya sumahātmane
|
nāmāṣṭatamākhyātaṃ tacchṛṇuṣva mahāmate||15||

16dhaumya uvāca|
sūryoryamā bhagastvaṣṭā pūṣārkaḥ savitā raviḥ
|
gabhastimānajaḥ kālo mṛtyurdhātā prabhākaraḥ||16||

17pṛthivyāpaśca tejaśca khaṃ vāyuśca parāyaṇam
|
somo bṛhaspatiḥ śukro budho'ṅgāraka eva ca||17||

18indro vivasvāndīptāṃśuḥ śuciḥ śauriḥ śanaiścaraḥ
|
brahmā viṣṇuśca rudraśca skando vai varuṇo yamaḥ||18||

19vaidyuto jāṭharaścāgniraindhanastejasāṃ patiḥ
|
dharmadhvajo vedakartā vedāṅgo vedavāhanaḥ||19||

20kṛtaṃ tretā dvāparaśca kaliḥ sarvamalāśrayaḥ
|
kalā kāṣṭhā muhūrtāśca kṣapā yāmastathā kṣaṇaḥ||20||

21saṃvatsarakaro'śvatthaḥ kālacakro vibhāvasuḥ
|
puruṣaḥ śāśvato yogī vyaktāvyaktaḥ sanātanaḥ||21||

22kālādhyakṣaḥ prajādhyakṣo viśvakarmā tamonudaḥ
|
varuṇaḥ sāgaroṃśaśca jīmūto jīvanorihā||22||

23bhūtāśrayo bhūtapatiḥ sarvalokanamaskṛtaḥ
|
sraṣṭā saṃvartako vahniḥ sarvasyādiralolupaḥ||23||

24anantaḥ kapilo bhānuḥ kāmadaḥ sarvatomukhaḥ
|
jayo viśālo varadaḥ sarvadhātuniṣecitā||24||

25manaḥ suparṇo bhūtādiḥ śīghragaḥ prāṇadhārakaḥ
|
dhanvantarirdhūmaketurādidevo'diteḥ sutaḥ||25||

26dvādaśātmāravindākṣaḥ pitā mātā pitāmahaḥ
|
svargadvāraṃ prajādvāraṃ mokṣadvāraṃ triviṣṭapam||26||

27dehakartā praśāntātmā viśvātmā viśvatomukhaḥ
|
carācarātmā sūkṣmātmā maitreyaḥ karuṇānvitaḥ||27||

28etadvai kīrtanīyasya sūryasyāmitatejasaḥ
|
nāmāṣṭaśatakaṃ cedaṃ proktametatsvayaṃbhuvā||28||

29suragaṇapitṛyakṣasevitaṃ hyasuraniśācarasiddhavanditam
|
varakanakahutāśanaprabhaṃ praṇipatito'smi hitāya bhāskaram||29||

30sūryodaye yaḥ susamāhitaḥ paṭhetsaputradārāndhanaratnasañcayān
|
labheta jātismaratāṃ naraḥ sadā dhṛtiṃ ca medhāṃ ca sa vindate pumān||30||

31imaṃ stavaṃ devavarasya yo naraḥ prakīrtayecchuci sumanāḥ samāhitaḥ
|
vimucyate śokadavāgnisāgarāllabheta kāmānmanasā yathepsitān||31||

32vaiśampāyana uvāca|
evamuktastu dhaumyena tatkālasadṛśaṃ vacaḥ
|
vipratyāgasamādhisthaḥ saṃyatātmā dṛḍhavrataḥ||32||

33dharmarājo viśuddhātmā tapa ātiṣṭhaduttamam
|
puṣpopahārairbalibhirarcayitvā divākaram||33||

34so'vagāhya jalaṃ rājā devasyābhimukho'bhavat
|
yogamāsthāya dharmātmā vāyubhakṣo jitendriyaḥ||34||

35gāṅgeyaṃ vāryupaspṛśya prāṇāyāmena tasthivān
|
śuciḥ prayatavāgbhūtvā stotramārabdhavāṃstataḥ||35||

36yudhiṣṭhira uvāca|
tvaṃ bhāno jagataścakṣustvamātmā sarvadehinām
|
tvaṃ yoniḥ sarvabhūtānāṃ tvamācāraḥ kriyāvatām||36||

37tvaṃ gatiḥ sarvasāṅkhyānāṃ yogināṃ tvaṃ parāyaṇam
|
anāvṛtārgaladvāraṃ tvaṃ gatistvaṃ mumukṣatām||37||

38tvayā saṃvāryate lokastvayā lokaḥ prakāśyate
|
tvayā pavitrīkriyate nirvyājaṃ pālyate tvayā||38||

39tvāmupasthāya kāle tu brāhmaṇā vedapāragāḥ
|
svaśākhāvihitairmantrairarcantyṛṣigaṇārcitam||39||

40tava divyaṃ rathaṃ yāntamanuyānti varārthinaḥ
|
siddhacāraṇagandharvā yakṣaguhyakapannagāḥ||40||

41trayastriṃśacca vai devāstathā vaimānikā gaṇāḥ
|
sopendrāḥ samahendrāśca tvāmiṣṭvā siddhimāgatāḥ||41||

42upayāntyarcayitvā tu tvāṃ vai prāptamanorathāḥ
|
divyamandāramālābhistūrṇaṃ vidyādharottamāḥ||42||

43guhyāḥ pitṛgaṇāḥ sapta ye divyā ye ca mānuṣāḥ
|
te pūjayitvā tvāmeva gacchantyāśu pradhānatām||43||

44vasavo maruto rudrā ye ca sādhyā marīcipāḥ
|
vālakhilyādayaḥ siddhāḥ śreṣṭhatvaṃ prāṇināṃ gatāḥ||44||

45sabrahmakeṣu lokeṣu saptasvapyakhileṣu ca
|
na tadbhūtamahaṃ manye yadarkādatiricyate||45||

46santi cānyāni sattvāni vīryavanti mahānti ca
|
na tu teṣāṃ tathā dīptiḥ prabhāvo vā yathā tava||46||

47jyotīṃṣi tvayi sarvāṇi tvaṃ sarvajyotiṣāṃ patiḥ
|
tvayi satyaṃ ca sattvaṃ ca sarve bhāvāśca sāttvikāḥ||47||

48tvattejasā kṛtaṃ cakraṃ sunābhaṃ viśvakarmaṇā
|
devārīṇāṃ mado yena nāśitaḥ śārṅgadhanvanā||48||

49tvamādāyāṃśubhistejo nidāghe sarvadehinām
|
sarvauṣadhirasānāṃ ca punarvarṣāsu muñcasi||49||

50tapantyanye dahantyanye garjantyanye tathā ghanāḥ
|
vidyotante pravarṣanti tava prāvṛṣi raśmayaḥ||50||

51na tathā sukhayatyagnirna prāvārā na kambalāḥ
|
śītavātārditaṃ loka yathā tava marīcayaḥ||51||

52trayodaśadvīpavatīṃ gobhirbhāsayase mahīm
|
trayāṇāmapi lokānāṃ hitāyaikaḥ pravartase||52||

53tava yadyudayo na syādandhaṃ jagadidaṃ bhavet
|
na ca dharmārthakāmeṣu pravarteranmanīṣiṇaḥ||53||

54ādhānapaśubandheṣṭimantrayajñatapaḥkriyāḥ
|
tvatprasādādavāpyante brahmakṣatraviśāṃ gaṇaiḥ||54||

55yadaharbrahmaṇaḥ proktaṃ sahasrayugasaṃmitam
|
tasya tvamādirantaśca kālajñaiḥ parikīrtitaḥ||55||

56manūnāṃ manuputrāṇāṃ jagato'mānavasya ca
|
manvantarāṇāṃ sarveṣāmīśvarāṇāṃ tvamīśvaraḥ||56||

57saṃhārakāle saṃprāpte tava krodhaviniḥsṛtaḥ
|
saṃvartakāgnistrailokyaṃ bhasmīkṛtyāvatiṣṭhate||57||

58tvaddīdhitisamutpannā nānāvarṇā mahāghanāḥ
|
sairāvatāḥ sāśanayaḥ kurvantyābhūtasaṃplavam||58||

59kṛtvā dvādaśadhātmānaṃ dvādaśādityatāṃ gataḥ
|
saṃhṛtyaikārṇavaṃ sarvaṃ tvaṃ śoṣayasi raśmibhiḥ||59||

60tvāmindramāhustvaṃ rudrastvaṃ viṣṇustvaṃ prajāpatiḥ
|
tvamagnistvaṃ manaḥ sūkṣmaṃ prabhustvaṃ brahma śāśvatam||60||

61tvaṃ haṃsaḥ savitā bhānuraṃśumālī vṛṣākapiḥ
|
vivasvānmihiraḥ pūṣā mitro dharmastathaiva ca||61||

62sahasraraśmirādityastapanastvaṃ gavāṃ patiḥ
|
mārtaṇḍo'rko raviḥ sūryaḥ śaraṇyo dinakṛttathā||62||

63divākaraḥ saptasaptirdhāmakeśī virocanaḥ
|
āśugāmī tamoghnaśca haritāśvaśca kīrtyase||63||

64saptamyāmathavā ṣaṣṭhyāṃ bhaktyā pūjāṃ karoti yaḥ
|
anirviṇṇo'nahaṃkārī taṃ lakṣmīrbhajate naram||64||

65na teṣāmāpadaḥ santi nādhayo vyādhayastathā
|
ye tavānanyamanasaḥ kurvantyarcanavandanam||65||

66sarvarogairvirahitāḥ sarvapāpavivarjitāḥ
|
tvadbhāvabhaktāḥ sukhino bhavanti cirajīvinaḥ||66||

67tvaṃ mamāpannakāmasya sarvātithyaṃ cikīrṣataḥ
|
annamannapate dātumabhitaḥ śraddhayārhasi||67||

68ye ca te'nucarāḥ sarve pādopāntaṃ samāśritāḥ
|
māṭharāruṇadaṇḍādyāstāṃstānvande'śanikṣubhān||68||

69kṣubhayā sahitā maitrī yāścānyā bhūtamātaraḥ
|
tāśca sarvā namasyāmi pāntu māṃ śaraṇāgatam||69||

70vaiśaṃpāyana uvāca|
evaṃ stuto mahārāja bhāskaro lokabhāvanaḥ
|
tato divākaraḥ prīto darśayāmāsa pāṇḍavam
|
dīpyamānaḥ svavapuṣā jvalanniva hutāśanaḥ||70||

71vivasvānuvāca
|
yatte'bhilaṣitaṃ kiṃcittattvaṃ sarvamavāpsyasi
|
ahamannaṃ pradāsyāmi sapta pañca ca te samāḥ||71||

72gṛhṇīṣva piṭharaṃ tāmraṃ mayā dattaṃ narādhipa
|
yāvadvartsyati pāñcālī pātreṇānena suvrata||72||

73phalamūlāmiṣaṃ śākaṃ saṃskṛtaṃ yanmahānase
|
caturvidhaṃ tadannādyamakṣayyaṃ te bhaviṣyati||73||

74itaścaturdaśe varṣe bhūyo rājyamavāpsyasi
|
vaiśaṃpāyana uvāca|
evamuktvā tu bhagavāṃstatraivāntaradhīyata||74||

75imaṃ stavaṃ prayatamanāḥ samādhinā paṭhedihānyo'pi varaṃ samarthayan
|
tattasya dadyācca ravirmanīṣataṃ tadāpnuyādyadyapi tatsudurlabham||75||

76yaścedaṃ dhārayennityaṃ śṛṇuyādvāpyabhīkṣṇaśaḥ
|
putrārthī labhate putraṃ dhanārthī labhate dhanam
|
vidyārthī labhate vidyāṃ puruṣo'pyathavā striyaḥ||76||

77ubhe sandhye paṭhennityaṃ nārī vā puruṣo yadi
|
āpadaṃ prāpya mucyeta baddho mucyeta bandhanāt||77||

78etadbrahmā dadau pūrvaṃ śakrāya sumahātmane
|
śakrācca nāradaḥ prāpto dhaumyastu tadanantaram||78||

79saṅgrāme ca jayennityaṃ vipulaṃ cāpnuyādvasu
|
mucyate sarvapāpebhyaḥ sūryalokaṃ sa gacchati||79||

80vaiśaṃpāyana uvāca|
labdhvā varaṃ tu kaunteyo jalāduttīrya dharmavit
|
jagrāha pādau dhaumyasya bhrātṝṃśca pariṣasvaje||80||

81draupadyā saha saṃgamya vandyamānastayā prabhuḥ
|
mahānase tadānīṃ tu sādhayāmāsa pāṇḍavaḥ||81||

82saṃskṛtaṃ prasavaṃ yāti svalpamannaṃ caturvidham
|
akṣayyaṃ vardhate cānnaṃ tena bhojayate dvijān||82||

83bhuktavatsu ca vipreṣu bhojayitvānujānapi
|
śeṣaṃ vighasasaṃjñaṃ tu paścādbhuṅkte yudhiṣṭhiraḥ||83||

84yudhiṣṭhiraṃ bhojayitvā śeṣamaśnāti pārṣatī
|
draupadyāṃ bhujyamānāyāṃ tadannaṃ kṣayameti ca
|
evaṃ divākarātprāpya divākarasamaprabhaḥ||84||

85kāmānmanobhilaṣitānbrāhmaṇebhyo'dadātprabhuḥ
|
purohitapurogāśca tithinakṣatraparvasu
|
yajñiyārthāḥ pravartante vidhimantrapramāṇataḥ||85||

86tataḥ kṛtasvastyayanā dhaumyena saha pāṇḍavāḥ
|
dvijasaṅghaiḥ parivṛtāḥ prayayuḥ kāmyakaṃ vanam||86||
