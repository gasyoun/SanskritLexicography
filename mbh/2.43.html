Mahābhārata 2.43 (Sabhāparvan)

1bhīṣma uvāca|
cedirājakule jātastryakṣa eṣa caturbhujaḥ
|
rāsabhārāvasadṛśaṃ rarāsa ca nanāda ca||1||

2tenāsya mātāpitarau tresatustau sabāndhavau
|
vaikṛtaṃ tasya tau dṛṣṭvā tyāgāya kurutāṃ matim||2||

3tataḥ sabhāryaṃ nṛpatiṃ sāmātyaṃ sapurohitam
|
cintāsaṃmūḍhahṛdayaṃ vāguvācāśarīreṇī||3||

4eṣa te nṛpate putraḥ śrīmāñjāto balādhikaḥ
|
tasmādasmānna bhetavyamavyagraḥ pāhi vai śiśum||4||

5na ca vai tasya mṛtyurvai na kālaḥ pratyupasthitaḥ
|
mṛtyurhantāsya śastreṇa sa cotpanno narādhipa||5||

6saṃśrutyodāhṛtaṃ vākyaṃ bhūtamantarhitaṃ tataḥ
|
putrasnehābhisantaptā jananī vākyamabravīt||6||

7yenedamīritaṃ vākyaṃ mamaitaṃ tanayaṃ prati
|
prāñjalistaṃ namasyāmi bravītu sa punarvacaḥ||7||

8yāthātathyena bhagavāndevo vā yadi vetaraḥ
|
śrotumicchāmi putrasya ko'sya mṛtyurbhaviṣyati||8||

9antarbhūtaṃ tato bhūtamuvācedaṃ punarvacaḥ
|
yasyotsaṅge gṛhītasya bhujāvabhyadhikāvubhau||9||

10patiṣyataḥ kṣititale pañcaśīrṣāvivoragau
|
tṛtīyametadbālasya lalāṭasthaṃ tu locanam||10||

11nimajjiṣyati yaṃ dṛṣṭvā so'sya mṛtyurbhaviṣyati
|
tryakṣaṃ caturbhujaṃ śrutvā tathā ca samudāhṛtam||11||

12pṛthivyāṃ pārthivāḥ sarve abhyāgacchandidṛkṣavaḥ
|
tānpūjayitvā samprāptānyathārhaṃ sa mahīpatiḥ||12||

13ekaikasya nṛpasyāṅke putramāropayattadā
|
evaṃ rājasahasrāṇāṃ pṛthaktvena yathākramam||13||

14śiśuraṅkasamārūḍho na tatprāpa nidarśanam
|
etadeva tu saṃśrutya dvāravatyāṃ mahābalau||14||

15tataścedipuraṃ prāptau saṅkarṣaṇajanārdanau
|
yādavau yādavīṃ druṣṭuṃ svasāraṃ tau pitustadā||15||

16abhivādya yathānyāyaṃ yathāśreṣṭhaṃ nṛpaṃ ca tām
|
kuśalānāmayaṃ pṛṣṭvā niṣaṇṇau rāmakeśavau||16||

17sā'bhyarcya tau tadā vīrau prītyā cābhyadhikaṃ tataḥ
|
putraṃ dāmodarotsaṅge devī saṃnyadadhātsvayam||17||

18nyastamātrasya tasyāṅke bhujāvabhyadhikāvubhau
|
petatustacca nayanaṃ nyamajjata lalāṭajam||18||

19taddṛṣṭvā vyathitā trastā varaṃ kṛṣṇamayācata
|
dadasva me varaṃ kṛṣṇa bhayārtāyā mahābhuja||19||

20tvaṃ hyārtānāṃ samāśvāso bhītānāmabhayapradaḥ
|
evamuktastataḥ kṛṣṇaḥ so'bravīdyadunandanaḥ||20||

21mā bhaistvaṃ devi dharmajñe na matto'sti bhayaṃ tava
|
dadāmi kaṃ varaṃ kiṃ ca karavāṇi pitṛṣvasaḥ||21||

22śakyaṃ vā yadi vā'śakyaṃ kariṣyāṇi vacastava
|
evamuktā tataḥ kṛṣṇamabravīdyadunandanam||22||

23śiśupālasyāparādhānkṣamethāstvaṃ mahābala
|
matkṛte yaduśārdūla viddhyenaṃ me varaṃ prabho||23||

24kṛṣṇa uvāca|
aparādhaśataṃ kṣāmyaṃ mayā hyasya pitṛṣvasaḥ
|
putrasya te vadhārhasya mā tvaṃ śoke manaḥ kṛthāḥ||24||

25bhīṣma uvāca|
evameṣa nṛpaḥ pāpaḥ śiśupāḥ sumandadhīḥ
|
tvāṃ samāhvayate vīra govindavaradarpitaḥ||25||
