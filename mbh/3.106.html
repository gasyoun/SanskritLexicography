Mahābhārata 3.106 (Āraṇyakaparvan)

1lomaśa uvāca|
tānuvāca sametāṃstu brahmā lokapitāmahaḥ
|
gacchadhvaṃ vibudhāḥ sarve yathākāmaṃ yathepsitam||1||

2mahatā kālayogena prakṛtiṃ yāsyate'rṇavaḥ
|
jñātīṃśca kāraṇaṃ kṛtvā mahārājño bhagīrathāt||2||

3pitāmahavacaḥ śrutvā sarve vibudhasattamāḥ
|
kālayogaṃ pratīkṣanto jagmuścāpi yathāgatam||3||

4yudhiṣṭhira uvāca|
kathaṃ vai jñātayo brahmankāraṇaṃ cātra kiṃ mune
|
kathaṃ samudraḥ pūrṇaśca bhagīrathapratiśrayāt||4||

5etadicchāmyahaṃ śrotuṃ vistareṇa tapodhana
|
kathyamānaṃ tvayā vipra rājñāṃ caritamuttamam||5||

6vaiśaṃpāyana uvāca|
evamuktastu viprendro dharmarājñā mahātmanā
|
kathayāmāsa māhātmyaṃ sagarasya mahātmanaḥ||6||

7lomaśa uvāca|
ikṣvākūṇāṃ kule jātaḥ sagaro nāma pārthivaḥ
|
rūpasattvabalopetaḥ sa cāputraḥ pratāpavān||7||

8sa haihayānsamutsādya tālajaṅghāṃśca bhārata
|
vaśe ca kṛtvā rājanyānsvarājyamanvaśāsata||8||

9tasya bhārye tvabhavatāṃ rūpayauvanadarpite
|
vaidarbhī bharataśreṣṭha śaibyā ca bharatarṣabha||9||

10sa putrakāmo nṛpatistapyate sma mahattapaḥ
|
patnībhyāṃ saha rājendra kailāsaṃ girimāśritaḥ||10||

11sa tapyamānaḥ sumahattapo yogasamanvitaḥ
|
āsasāda mahātmānaṃ tryakṣaṃ tripuramardanam||11||

12śaṅkaraṃ bhavamīśānaṃ pinākiṃ śūlapāṇinam
|
tryambakaṃ śivamugreśaṃ bahurūpamumāpatim||12||

13sa taṃ dṛṣṭvai varadaṃ patnībhyāṃ sahito nṛpaḥ
|
praṇipatya mahābāhuḥ putrārthe samayācata||13||

14taṃ prītimānharaḥ prāha sabhāryaṃ nṛpasattamam
|
yasminvṛto muhūrte'haṃ tvayeha nṛpate varam||14||

15ṣaṣṭiḥ putrasahasrāṇi śūrāḥ paramadarpitāḥ
|
ekasyāṃ saṃbhaviṣyanti patnyāṃ naravarottama||15||

16te caiva sarve sahitāḥ kṣayaṃ yāsyanti pārthiva
|
eko vaṃśadharaḥ śūra ekasyāṃ saṃbhaviṣyati||16||

17evamuktvā tu taṃ rudrastatraivāntaradhīyata
|
sa cāpi sagaro rājā jagāma svaṃ niveśanam||17||

18patnībhyāṃ sahitastatra so'tihṛṣṭamanāstadā
|
tasya te manujaśreṣṭha bhārye kamalalocane||18||

19vaidarbhī caiva śaibyā ca garbhiṇyau saṃbabhūvatuḥ
|
tataḥ kālena vaidarbhī garmālābuṃ vyajāyata||19||

20śaibyā ca suṣuve putraṃ kumāraṃ devarūpiṇam
|
tadālābuṃ samutsraṣṭuṃ manaścakre sa pārthivaḥ||20||

21athāntarikṣācchuśrāva vācaṃ gambhīraniḥsvanām
|
rājanmā sāhasaṃ kārṣīḥ putrānna tyaktumarhasi||21||

22alābumadhyānniṣkṛṣya bījaṃ yatnena gopyatām
|
sopasvedeṣu pātreṣu ghṛtapūrṇeṣu bhāgaśaḥ||22||

23tataḥ putrasahastrāṇi ṣaṣṭiṃ prāpsyasi bhārata
|
mahādevena diṣṭaṃ te putrajanma narādhipa
|
anena kramayogena mā te buddhirato'nyathā||23||
