Mahābhārata 5.40 (Udyogaparvan)

1vidura uvāca|
yo'bhyarcitaḥ sadbhirasajjamānaḥ karotyarthaṃ śaktimahāpayitvā
|
kṣipraṃ yaśastaṃ samupaiti santamalaṃ prasannā hi sukhāya santaḥ ||1||

2mahāntamapyarthamadharmayuktaṃ yaḥ santyajatyanapākṛṣṭa eva
|
sukhaṃ suduḥkhānyavamucya śete jīrṇāṃ tvacaṃ sarpa ivāvamucya ||2||

3anṛte ca samutkarṣo rājagāmi ca paiśunam
|
guroścālīkanirbandhaḥ samāni brahmahatyayā ||3||

4asūyaikapadaṃ mṛtyurativādaḥ śriyo vadhaḥ
|
aśuśrūṣā tvarā ślāghā vidyāyāḥ śatravastrayaḥ ||4||

5ālasyaṃ madamohau ca cāpalaṃ goṣṭhireva ca
|
stabdhatā cābhimānitvaṃ tathā'tyāgitvameva ca ||5||

6ete vai sapta doṣāḥ syuḥ sadā vidyārthināṃ matāḥ
|
sukhārthinaḥ kuto vidyā nāsti vidyārthinaḥ sukham||6||

7sukhārthī vā tyajedvidyāṃ vidyārthī vā tyajetsukham
|
nāgnistṛpyati kāṣṭhānāṃ nāpagānāṃ mahodadhiḥ
|
nāntakaḥ sarvabhūtānāṃ na puṃsāṃ vāmalocanā ||7||

8āśā dhṛtiṃ hanti samṛddhimantakaḥ krodhaḥ śriyaṃ hanti yaśaḥ kadaryatā
|
apālanaṃ hanti paśūṃśca rājannekaḥ kruddho brāhmaṇo hanti rāṣṭram ||8||

9ajāśca kāṃsyaṃ rajataṃ ca nityaṃ madhvākarṣaḥ śakuniḥ śrotriyaśca
|
vṛddho jñātiravasannaḥ kulīna etāni te santu gṛhe sadaiva ||9||

10ajokṣā candanaṃ vīṇā ādarśo madhusarpiṣī
|
viṣamaudumbaraṃ śaṅkhaḥ svarṇanābho'tha rocanā ||10||

11gṛhe sthāpayitavyāni dhanyāni manurabravīt
|
devabrāhmaṇapūjārthamatithīnāṃ ca bhārata ||11||

12idaṃ ca tvāṃ sarvaparaṃ bravīmi puṇyaṃ padaṃ tāta mahāviśiṣṭam
|
na jātu kāmānna bhayānna lobhāddharmaṃ jahyājjīvitasyāpi hetoḥ ||12||

13nityo dharmaḥ sukhaduḥkhe tvanitye jīvo nityo heturasya tvanityaḥ
|
tyaktvā'nityaṃ pratitiṣṭhasva nitye saṃtuṣya tvaṃ toṣaparo hi lābhaḥ ||13||

14mahābalān paśya mahānubhāvān praśāsya bhūmiṃ dhanadhānyapūrṇām
|
rājyāni hitvā vipulāṃśca bhogān gatānnarendrān vaśamantakasya ||14||

15mṛtaṃ putraṃ duḥkhapuṣṭaṃ manuṣyā utkṣipya rājan svagṛhānnirharanti
|
taṃ muktakeśāḥ karuṇaṃ rudanti citāmadhye kāṣṭhamiva kṣipanti||15||

16anyo dhanaṃ pretagatasya bhuṅkte vayāṃsi cāgniśca śarīradhātūn
|
dvābhyāmayaṃ saha gacchatyamutra puṇyena pāpena ca veṣṭyamānaḥ ||16||

17usṛjya vinivartante jñātayaḥ suhṛdaḥ sutāḥ
|
apuṣpānaphalān vṛkṣān yathā tāta patatriṇaḥ ||17||

18agnau prāstaṃ tu puruṣaṃ karmānveti svayaṃ kṛtam
|
tasmāttu puruṣo yatnāddharmaṃ sañcinuyācchanaiḥ ||18||

19asmāllokādūrdhvamamuṣya cādho mahattamastiṣṭhati hyandhakāram
|
tadvai mahāmohanamindriyāṇāṃ buddhyasva mā tvāṃ pralabheta rājan ||19||

20idaṃ vacaḥ śakṣyasi cedyathāvanniśamya sarvaṃ pratipattumeva
|
yaśaḥ paraṃ prāpsyasi jīvaloke bhayaṃ na cāmutra na ceha te'sti ||20||

21ātmā nadī bhārata puṇyatīrthā satyodayā dhṛtikūlā dayormiḥ
|
tasyāṃ snātaḥ pūyate puṇyakarmā puṇyo hyātmā nityamalobha eva ||21||

22kāmakrodhagrāhavatīṃ pañcendriyajalāṃ nadīm
|
nāvaṃ dhṛtimayīṃ kṛtvā janmadurgāṇi saṃtara ||22||

23prajñāvṛddhaṃ dharmavṛddhaṃ svabandhuṃ vidyāvṛddhaṃ vayasā cāpi vṛddham
|
kāryākārye pūjayitvā prasādya yaḥ saṃpṛcchenna sa muhyet kadācit ||23||

24dhṛtyā śiśnodaraṃ rakṣet pāṇipādaṃ ca cakṣuṣā
|
cakṣuḥśrotre ca manasā mano vācaṃ ca karmaṇā ||24||

25nityodakī nityayajñopavītī nityasvādhyāyī patitānnavarjī
|
satyaṃ bruvan gurave karma kurvanna brāhmaṇaścyavate brahmalokāt ||25||

26adhītya vedān parisaṃstīrya cāgnīniṣṭvā yajñaiḥ pālayitvā prajāśca
|
gobrahmaṇārthaṃ śastrapūtāntarātmā hataḥ saṅgrāme kṣatriyaḥ svargameti ||26||

27vaiśyo'dhītya brāhmaṇān kṣatriyāṃśca dhanaiḥ kāle saṃvibhajyāśritāṃśca
|
tretāpūtaṃ dhūmamāghrāya puṇyaṃ pretya svarge divyasukhāni bhuṅkte ||27||

28brahma kṣatraṃ vaiśyavarṇaṃ ca śūdraḥ krameṇaitānnyāyataḥ pūjayānaḥ
|
tuṣṭeṣveteṣvavyatho dagdhapāpastyaktvā dehaṃ svargasukhāni bhuṅkte ||28||

29cāturvarṇyasyaiṣa dharmastavokto hetuṃ cānubruvato me nibodha
|
kṣātrāddharmāddhīyate pāṇḍuputrastaṃ tvaṃ rājan rājadharme niyuṅkṣva ||29||

30dhṛtarāṣṭra uvāca|
evametadyathā tvaṃ māmanuśāsasi nityadā
|
mamāpi ca matiḥ saumya bhavatyevaṃ yathā''ttha mām ||30||

31sā tu buddhiḥ kṛtā'pyevaṃ pāṇḍavān prati me sadā
|
duryodhanaṃ samāsādya punarviparivartate ||31||

32na diṣṭamabhyatikrāntuṃ śakyaṃ bhūtena kenacit
|
diṣṭameva dhruvaṃ manye pauruṣaṃ tu nirarthakam ||32||
