Mahābhārata 12.32 (Śāntiparvan)

1vaiśampāyana uvāca|
tūṣṇībhūtaṃ tu rājānaṃ śocamānaṃ yudhiṣṭhiram
|
tapasvī dharmatattvajñaḥ kṛṣṇadvaipāyano'bravīt ||1||

2vyāsa uvāca |
prajānāṃ pālanaṃ dharmo rājñāṃ rājīvalocana
|
dharmaḥ pramāṇaṃ lokasya nityaṃ dharmānuvartinaḥ ||2||

3anutiṣṭhasva tadrājanpitṛpaitāmahaṃ padam
|
brāhmaṇeṣu tapo dharmaḥ sa nityo vedaniścitaḥ ||3||

4tatpramāṇaṃ brāhmaṇānāṃ śāśvataṃ bharatarṣabha
|
tasya dharmasya kṛtsnasya kṣatriyaḥ parirakṣitā ||4||

5yaḥ svayaṃ pratihanti sma śāsanaṃ viṣaye rataḥ
|
sa bāhubhyāṃ vinigrāhyo lokayātrāvighātakaḥ ||5||

6pramāṇamapramāṇaṃ yaḥ kuryānmohavaśaṃ gataḥ
|
bhṛtyo vā yadi vā putrastapasvī vā'tha kaścana ||6||

7pāpānsarvairupāyaistān niyacchecchātayīta vā
|
ato'nyathā vartamāno rājā prāpnoti kilbiṣam ||7||

8dharmaṃ vinaśyamānaṃ hi yo na rakṣetsa dharmahā
|
te tvayā dharmahantāro nihatāḥ sapadānugāḥ ||8||

9svadharme vartamānastvaṃ kiṃ nu śocasi pāṇḍava
|
rājā hi hanyāddadyācca prajā rakṣecca dharmataḥ ||9||

10yudhiṣṭhira uvāca|
na te'bhiśaṃke vacanaṃ yadbravīṣi tapodhana
|
aparokṣo hi te dharmaḥ sarvadharmavidāṃ vara ||10||

11mayā tvavadhyā bahavo ghātitā rājyakāraṇāt
|
tāni karmāṇi me brahmandahanti ca pacanti ca ||11||

12vyāsa uvāca |
īśvaro vā bhavetkartā puruṣo vā'pi bhārata
|
haṭho vā vartate loke karmajaṃ vā phalaṃ smṛtam ||12||

13īśvareṇa niyukto hi sādhvasādhu ca bhārata
|
kurute puruṣaḥ karma phalamīśvaragāmi tat ||13||

14yathā hi puruṣaśchidyādvṛkṣaṃ paraśunā vane
|
chettureva bhavetpāpaṃ paraśorna kathañcana ||14||

15athavā tadupādānātprāpnuyātkarmaṇaḥ phalam
|
daṇḍaśastrakṛtaṃ pāpaṃ puruṣe tanna vidyate ||15||

16na caitadiṣṭaṃ kaunteya yadanyena kṛtaṃ phalam
|
prāpnuyāditi yasmācca īśvare tanniveśaya ||16||

17athāpi puruṣaḥ kartā karmaṇoḥ śubhapāpayoḥ
|
na paro vidyate tasmādevametacchubhaṃ kṛtam ||17||

18na hi kaścitkvacidrājandiṣṭaṃ pratinivartate
|
daṇḍaśastrakṛtaṃ pāpaṃ puruṣe tanna vidyate ||18||

19yadi vā manyase rājan hatamekaṃ pratiṣṭhitam
|
evamapyaśubhaṃ karma na bhūtaṃ na bhaviṣyati ||19||

20athābhipattirlokasya kartavyā puṇyapāpayoḥ
|
abhipannamidaṃ loke rājñāmudyatadaṇḍanam ||20||

21tathāpi loke karmāṇi samāvartanti bhārata
|
śubhāśubhaphalaṃ caite prāpnuvantīti me matiḥ ||21||

22evamapyaśubhaṃ karma karmaṇastatphalātmakam
|
tyaja tvaṃ rājaśārdūla maivaṃ śoke manaḥ kṛthā ||22||

23svadharme vartamānasya sāpavāde'pi bhārata
|
evamātmaparityāgastava rājanna śobhanaḥ ||23||

24vihitāni hi kaunteya prāyaścittāni karmaṇām
|
śarīravāṃstāni kuryādaśarīraḥ parābhavet ||24||

25tadrājan jīvanamānastvaṃ prāyaścittaṃ kariṣyasi
|
prāyaścittamakṛtvā tu pretya taptāsi bhārata ||25||
