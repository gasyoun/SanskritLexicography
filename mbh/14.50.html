Mahābhārata 14.50 (Āśvamedhikaparvan)

1brahmovāca|
hanta vaḥ sampravakṣyāmi yanmāṃ pṛcchatha sattamāḥ
|
guruṇā śiṣyamāsādya yaduktaṃ tannibodhata||1||

2samastamiha tacchrutvā samyagevāvadhāryatām
|
ahiṃsā sarvabhūtānāmetatkṛtyatamaṃ matam||2||

3etatpadamanudvignaṃ variṣṭhaṃ dharmalakṣaṇam
|
jñānaṃ niḥśreya ityāhurvṛddhā niścitadarśinaḥ||3||

4tasmājjñānena śuddhena mucyate sarvakilbiṣaiḥ
|
hiṃsāparāśca ye kecidye ca nāstikavṛttayaḥ
|
lobhamohasamāyuktāste vai nirayagāminaḥ||4||

5āśīryuktāni karmāṇi kurvate ye tvatandritāḥ
|
te'smi~lloke pramodante jāyamānāḥ punaḥ punaḥ||5||

6kurvate ye tu karmāṇi śraddadhānā vipaścitaḥ
|
anāśīryogasaṃyuktāste dhīrāḥ sādhudarśinaḥ||6||

7ataḥ paraṃ pravakṣyāmi sattvakṣetrajñayoryathā
|
saṃyogo viprayogaśca tannibodhata sattamāḥ||7||

8viṣayo viṣayitvaṃ ca sambandho'yamihocyate
|
viṣayī puruṣo nityaṃ sattvaṃ ca viṣayaḥ smṛtaḥ||8||

9vyākhyātaṃ pūrvakalpena maśakodumbaraṃ yathā
|
bhujyamānaṃ na jānīte nityaṃ sattvamacetanam
|
yastvevaṃ taṃ vijānīte yo bhuṅkte yaśca bhujyate||9||

10nityaṃ dvandvasamāyuktaṃ sattvamāhurmanīṣiṇaḥ
|
nirdvandvo niṣkalo nityaḥ kṣetrajño nirguṇātmakaḥ||10||

11samaṃ saṃjñānugaścaiva sa sarvatra vyavasthitaḥ
|
upabhuṅkte sadā sattvamapa: puṣkaraparṇavat||11||

12sarvairapi guṇairvidvān vyatiṣakto na lipyate
|
jalabinduryathā lolaḥ padminīpatrasaṃsthitaḥ||12||

13evamevāpyasaṃyuktaḥ puruṣaḥ syānna saṃśayaḥ
|
dravyamātramabhūtsattvaṃ puruṣasyeti niścayaḥ||13||

14yathā dravyaṃ ca kartā ca saṃyogo'pyanayostathā
|
yathā pradīpamādāya kaścittamasi gacchati
|
tathā sattvapradīpena gacchanti paramaiṣiṇaḥ||14||

15yāvaddravyaṃ guṇastāvatpradīpaḥ samprakāśate
|
kṣīṇe dravye guṇe jyotirantardhānāya gacchati||15||

16vyaktaḥ sattvaguṇastvevaṃ puruṣo'vyakta iṣyate
|
etadviprā vijānīta hanta bhūyo bravīmi vaḥ||16||

17sahasreṇāpi durmedhā na buddhimadhigacchati
|
caturthenāpyathāṃśena buddhimān sukhamedhate||17||

18evaṃ dharmasya vijñeyaṃ saṃsādhanamupāyataḥ
|
upāyajño hi medhāvī sukhamatyantamaśnute||18||

19yathādhvānamapātheyaḥ prapanno manujaḥ kvacit
|
kleśena yāti mahatā vinaśyedantarāpi ca||19||

20tathā karmasu vijñeyaṃ phalaṃ bhavati vā na vā
|
puruṣasyātmaniḥśreyaḥ śubhāśubhanidarśanam||20||

21yathā ca dīrghamadhvānaṃ padbhyāmeva prapadyate
|
adṛṣṭapūrvaṃ sahasā tattvadarśanavarjitaḥ||21||

22tameva ca yathādhvānaṃ rathenehāśugāminā
|
gacchatyaśvaprayuktena tathā buddhimatāṃ gatiḥ||22||

23ūrdhvaṃ parvatamāruhya nānvavekṣeta bhūtalam
|
rathena rathinaṃ paśya kliśyamānamacetanam||23||

24yāvadrathapathastāvadrathena sa tu gacchati
|
kṣīṇe rathapade vidvān rathamutsṛjya gacchati||24||

25evaṃ gacchati medhāvī tattvayogavidhānavit
|
parijñāya guṇajñaśca uttarāduttarottaram||25||

26yathārṇavaṃ mahāghoramaplavaḥ sampragāhate
|
bāhubhyāmeva sammohādvadhaṃ vāñchatyasaṃśayam||26||

27nāvā cāpi yathā prājño vibhāgajñaḥ svaritrayā
|
aśrāntaḥ salile gacchecchīghraṃ santarate hradam||27||

28tīrṇo gacchetparaṃ pāraṃ nāvamutsṛjya nirmamaḥ
|
vyākhyātaṃ pūrvakalpena yathā rathapadātinoḥ||28||

29snehātsammohamāpanno nāvi dāśo yathā tathā
|
mamatvenābhibhūtaḥ saṃstatraiva parivartate||29||

30nāvaṃ na śakyamāruhya sthale viparivartitum
|
tathaiva rathamāruhya nāpsu caryā vidhīyate||30||

31evaṃ karmakṛtaṃ citraṃ viṣayasthaṃ pṛthak pṛthak
|
yathā karmakṛtaṃ loke tathaitānupadyate||31||

32yanaiva gandhino rasyaṃ na rūpasparśaśabdavat
|
manyante munayo buddhyā tatpradhānaṃ pracakṣate||32||

33tatra pradhānamavyaktamavyaktasya guṇo mahān
|
mahatpradhānabhūtasya guṇo'haṅkāra eva ca||33||

34ahaṅkārāttu sambhūto mahābhūtakṛto guṇaḥ
|
pṛthaktvena hi bhūtānāṃ viṣayā vai guṇāḥ smṛtāḥ||34||

35bījadharmaṃ tathā vyaktaṃ prasavātmakameva ca
|
bījadharmo mahānātmā prasavaśceti naḥ śrutam||35||

36bījadharmastvahaṅkāraḥ prasavaśca punaḥ punaḥ
|
bījaprasavadharmāṇi mahābhūtāni pañca vai||36||

37bījadharmiṇa ityāhuḥ prasavaṃ ca prakurvate
|
viśeṣāḥ pañcabhūtānāṃ teṣāṃ cittaṃ viśeṣaṇam||37||

38tatraikaguṇamākāśaṃ dviguṇo vāyurucyate
|
triguṇaṃ jyotirityāhurāmaścapi caturguṇāḥ||38||

39pṛthvī pañcaguṇā jñeyā carasthāvarasaṅkulā
|
sarvabhūtakarī devī śubhāśubhanidarśinī||39||

40śabdaḥ sparśastathā rūpaṃ raso gandhaśca pañcamaḥ
|
ete pañca guṇā bhūmervijñeyā dvijasattamāḥ||40||

41pārthivaśca sadā gandho gandhaśca bahudhā smṛtaḥ
|
tasya gandhasya vakṣyāmi vistareṇa bahūn guṇān||41||

42iṣṭaścāniṣṭagandhaśca madhuro'mlaḥ kaṭustathā
|
nirhārī saṃhataḥ snigdho rūkṣo viśada eva ca||42||

43evaṃ daśavidho jñeyaḥ pārthivo gandha ityuta
|
śabdaḥ sparśastathā rūpaṃ dravaścāpāṃ guṇāḥ smṛtāḥ||43||

44rasajñānaṃ tu vakṣyāmi rasastu bahudhā smṛtaḥ
|
madhuro'mlaḥ kaṭustiktaḥ kaṣāyo lavaṇastathā||44||

45evaṃ ṣaḍvidhavistāro raso vārimayaḥ smṛtaḥ
|
śabdaḥ sparśastathā rūpaṃ triguṇaṃ jyotirucyate||45||

46jyotiṣaśca guṇo rūpaṃ rūpaṃ ca bahudhā smṛtam
|
śuklaṃ kṛṣṇaṃ tathā raktaṃ nīlaṃ pītāruṇaṃ tathā||46||

47hrasvaṃ dīrghaṃ kṛśaṃ sthūlaṃ caturasraṃ tu vṛttavat
|
evaṃ dvādaśavistāraṃ tejaso rūpamucyate||47||

48vijñeyaṃ brāhmaṇaivṛddhardhamejñaiḥ satyavādibhiḥ
|
śabdasparśau ca vijñeyau dviguṇo vāyurucyate||48||

49vāyoścāpi guṇaḥ sparśaḥ sparśaśca bahudhā smṛtaḥ
|
rūkṣaḥ śītastathaivoṣṇaḥ snigdho viśada eva ca||49||

50kaṭhinaścikkaṇaḥ ślakṣṇaḥ picchilo dāruṇo mṛduḥ
|
evaṃ dvādaśavistāro vāyavyo guṇa ucyate||50||

51vidhivadbrāhmaṇaiḥ siddhairdharmastittvadarśibhiḥ||51||

52tatraikaguṇamākāśaṃ śabda ityeva ca smṛtaḥ
|
tasya śabdasya vakṣyāmi vistareṇa bahūn guṇān||52||

53ṣaḍjarṣabhaḥ sa gāndhāro madhyamaḥ pañcamastathā
|
ataḥ paraṃ tu vijñeyo niṣādo dhaivatastathā
|
aṣṭaścāniṣṭaśabdaśca saṃhataḥ pravibhāgavān||53||

54evaṃ daśavidho jñeyaḥ śabda ākāśasambhavaḥ
|
ākāśamuttamaṃ bhūtamahaṅkārastataḥ paraḥ||54||

55ahaṅkārātparā buddhirbuddherātmā tataḥ paraḥ
|
tasmāttu paramavyaktamavyaktātpuruṣaḥ paraḥ||55||

56parāparajño bhūtānāṃ vidhijñaḥ sarvakarmaṇām
|
sarvabhūtātmabhūtātmā gacchatyātmānamavyayam||56||
