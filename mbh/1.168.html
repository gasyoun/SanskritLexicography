Mahābhārata 1.168 (Ādiparvan)

1vaiśaṃpāyana uvāca|
etacchrutvā tu kaunteyāḥ śalyaviddhā ivābhavan
|
sarve cāsvasthamanaso babhūvuste mahābalāḥ||1||

2tataḥ kuntīsutāndṛṣṭvā sarvāṃstadgatacetasaḥ
|
tataḥ kuntī sutāndṛṣṭvā sarvāṃstadgatacetasaḥ
|
yudhiṣṭhiramuvācedaṃ vacanaṃ satyavādinī||2||

3kuntyuvāca|
cirarātroṣitāḥ smeha brāhmaṇasya niveśane
|
ramamāṇāḥ pure ramye labdhabhaikṣā mahātmanaḥ||3||

4yānīha ramaṇīyāni vanānyupavanāni ca
|
sarvāṇi tāni dṛṣṭāni punaḥpunararindama||4||

5punardraṣṭuṃ hi tānīha prīṇayanti na nastathā
|
bhaikṣaṃ ca na tathā vīra labhyate kurunandana||5||

6te vayaṃ sādhu pañcālāngacchāma yadi manyase
|
apūrvadarśanaṃ vīra ramaṇīyaṃ bhaviṣyati||6||

7subhikṣāścaiva pañcālāḥ śrūyante śatrukarśana
|
yajñasenaśca rājāsau brahmaṇya iti suśruma||7||

8ekatra ciravāsaśca kṣamo na ca mato mama
|
te tatra sādhu gacchāmo yadi tvaṃ putra manyase||8||

9yudhiṣṭhira uvāca|
bhavatyā yanmataṃ kāryaṃ tadasmākaṃ paraṃ hitam
|
anujāṃstu na jānāmi gaccheyurneti vā punaḥ||9||

10vaiśaṃpāyana uvāca|
tataḥ kuntī bhīmasenamarjunaṃ yamajau tathā
|
uvāca gamanaṃ te ca tathetyevābruvaṃstadā||10||

11tata āmantrya taṃ vipraṃ kuntī rājasutaiḥ saha
|
pratasthe nagarīṃ ramyāṃ drupadasya mahātmanaḥ||11||
