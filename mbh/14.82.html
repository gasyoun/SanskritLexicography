Mahābhārata 14.82 (Āśvamedhikaparvan)

1vaiśampāyana uvāca|
sa tu vājī samudrāntāṃ paryetya vasudhāmimām
|
nivṛtto'bhimukho rājan yena vāraṇasāhvayam||1||

2anugacchaṃśca turagaṃ nivṛtto'tha kirīṭabhṛt
|
yadṛcchayā samāpede puraṃ rājagṛhaṃ tadā||2||

3tamabhyāśagataṃ dṛṣṭvā sahadevātmajaḥ prabho
|
kṣatradharme sthito vīraḥ samarāyājuhāva ha||3||

4tataḥ purātsa niṣkramya rathī dhanvī śarī talī
|
meghasandhiḥ padāti taṃ dhanañjayamupādravat||4||

5āsādya ca mahātejā meghasandhirdhanañjayam
|
bālabhāvānmahārāja provācedaṃ na kauśalāt||5||

6kimayaṃ cāryate vājī strīmadhya iva bhārata
|
hayamenaṃ hariṣyāmi prayatasva vimokṣaṇe||6||

7adattānunayo yuddhe yadi tvaṃ pitṛbhirmama
|
kariṣyāmi tavātithyaṃ prahara praharāmi ca||7||

8ityuktaḥ pratyuvācainaṃ prahasanniva pāṇḍavaḥ
|
vighnakartā mayā vārya iti me vratamāhitam||8||

9bhrātrā jyeṣṭhena nṛpate tavāpi viditaṃ dhruvam
|
praharasva yathāśakti na manyurvidyate mama||9||

10ityuktaḥ prāharatpūrvaṃ pāṇḍavaṃ magadheśvaraḥ
|
kiran śarasahasrāṇi varṣāṇīva sahasradṛk||10||

11tato gāṇḍīvabhṛcchūro gāṇḍīvaprahitaiḥ śaraiḥ
|
cakāra moghāṃstānbāṇānsayalānbharatarṣabha||11||

12sa moghaṃ tasya bāṇaughaṃ kṛtvā vānaraketanaḥ
|
śarānmumoca jvalitāndīptāsyāniva pannagān||12||

13dhvaje patākādaṇḍeṣu rathe yantre hayeṣu ca
|
anyeṣu ca rathāṅgeṣu na śarīre na sārathau||13||

14saṃrakṣyamāṇaḥ pārthena śarīre savyasācinā
|
manyamānaḥ svavīryaṃ tanmāgadhaḥ prāhiṇoccharān||14||

15tato gāṇḍīvadhanvā tu māgadhena bhṛśāhataḥ
|
babhau vasantasamaye palāśaḥ puṣpito yathā||15||

16avadhyamānaḥ so'bhyaghnanmāgadhaḥ pāṇḍavarṣabham
|
tena tasthau ya kauravya lokavīrasya darśane||16||

17savyasācī tu saṅkruddho vikṛṣya balavaddhanuḥ
|
hayāṃścakāra nirjīvānsāratheśca śiro'harat||17||

18dhanuścāsya mahaccitraṃ kṣureṇa pracakarta ha
|
hastāvāpaṃ patākāṃ ca dhvajaṃ cāsya nyapātayat||18||

19sa rājā vyathito vyaśvo vidhanurhatasārathiḥ
|
gadāmādāya kaunteyamabhidudrāva vegavān||19||

20tasyāpatata evāśu gadāṃ hemapariṣkṛtām
|
śaraiścakarta bahudhā bahubhirgṛdhravājitaiḥ||20||

21sā gadā śakalībhūtā viśīrṇamaṇibandhanā
|
vyālī vimucyamāneva papāta dharaṇītale||21||

22virathaṃ vidhanuṣkaṃ ca gadayā parivarjitam
|
sāntvapūrvamidaṃ vākyamabravītkapiketanaḥ||22||

23paryāptaḥ kṣatradharmo'yaṃ darśitaḥ putra gamyatām
|
bahvetatsamare karma tava bālasya pārthiva||23||

24yudhiṣṭhirasya sandeśo na hantavyā nṛpā iti
|
tena jīvasi rājaṃstvamaparāddho'pi me raṇe||24||

25iti matvā tadātmānaṃ pratyādiṣṭaṃ sma māgadhaḥ
|
tathyamityabhigamyainaṃ prāñjaliḥ pratyapūjayat||25||

26parājito'smi bhadraṃ te nāhaṃ yodhumihātsahe
|
yadyatkṛtyaṃ mayā te'dya tadbrūhi kṛtameva tu||26||

27tamarjunaḥ samāśvāsya punarevedamabravīt
|
āgantavyaṃ parāṃ caitrīmaśvamedhe nṛpasya naḥ||27||

28ityuktaḥ sa tathetyuktvā pūjayāmāsa taṃ hayam
|
phālgunaṃ ca yudhi śreṣṭhaṃ vidhivatsahadevajaḥ||28||

29tato yatheṣṭamagamatpunareva sa kesarī
|
tataḥ samudratīreṇa vaṅgānpuṇḍrānsakosalān||29||

30tatra tatra ca bhūrīṇi mlecchasainyānyanekaśaḥ
|
vijigye dhanuṣā rājangāṇḍīvena dhanañjayaḥ||30||
