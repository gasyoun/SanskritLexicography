Mahābhārata 5.93 (Udyogaparvan)

1śrībhagavānuvāca
|
yathā brūyānmahāprājño yathā brūyādvicakṣaṇaḥ
|
yathā vācyastvadvidhena bhavatā madvidhaḥ sṛhṛt ||1||

2dharmārthayuktaṃ tathyaṃ ca yathā tvayyupapadyate
|
tathā vacanamukto'smi tvayaitatpitṛmātṛvat ||2||

3satyaṃ prāptaṃ ca yuktaṃ vā'pyevameva yathā''ttha mām
|
śṛṇuṣvāgamane hetuṃ vidurāvahito bhava ||3||

4daurātmyaṃ dhārtarāṣṭrasya kṣatriyāṇāṃ ca vairatām
|
sarvametadahaṃ jānan kṣattaḥ prāpto'dya kauravān ||4||

5paryastāṃ pṛthivīṃ sarvāṃ sāśvāṃ sarathakuñjarām
|
yo mocayenmṛtyupāśāt prāpnuyāddharmamuttamam ||5||

6dharmakāryaṃ yatan śaktyā no cetprāpnoti mānavaḥ
|
prāpto bhavati tatpuṇyamatra me nāsti saṃśayaḥ ||6||

7manasā cintayan pāpaṃ karmaṇā nātirocayan
|
na prāpnoti phalaṃ tasyetyevaṃ dharmavido viduḥ ||7||

8so'haṃ yatiṣye praśamaṃ kṣattaḥ kartumamāyayā
|
kurūṇāṃ sṛñjayānāṃ ca saṅgrāme vinaśiṣyatām ||8||

9seyamāpanmahāghorā kuruṣveva samutthitā
|
karṇaduryodhanakṛtā sarve hyete tadanvayāḥ ||9||

10vyasane kliśyamānaṃ hi yo mitraṃ nābhipadyate
|
anarthāya yathāśakti tannṛśaṃsaṃ vidurbudhāḥ ||10||

11ākeśagrahaṇānmitramakāryāt saṃnivartayan
|
avācyaḥ kasyacidbhavati kṛtayatno yathābalam ||11||

12tatsamarthaṃ śubhaṃ vākyaṃ dharmārthasahitaṃ hitam
|
dhārtarāṣṭraḥ sahāmātyo grahītuṃ vidurārhati ||12||

13hitaṃ hi dhārtarāṣṭrāṇāṃ pāṇḍavānāṃ tathaiva ca
|
pṛthivyāṃ kṣatriyāṇāṃ ca yatiṣye'hamamāyayā ||13||

14hite prayatamānaṃ māṃ śaṅkedduryodhano yadi
|
hṛdayasya ca me prītirānṛṇyaṃ ca bhaviṣyati ||14||

15jñātīnāṃ hi mitho bhede yanmitraṃ nābhipadyate
|
sarvayatnena mādhyasthaṃ na tanmitraṃ vidurbudhāḥ ||15||

16na māṃ būyuradharmiṣṭhā mūḍhā hyasuhṛdastathā
|
śakto nāvārayat kṛṣṇaḥ saṃrabdhān kurupāṇḍavān ||16||

17ubhayoḥ sādhayannarthamahamāgata ityuta
|
tatra yatnamahaṃ kṛtvā gaccheyaṃ nṛṣvavācyatām ||17||

18mama dharmārthayuktaṃ hi śrutvā vākyamanāmayam
|
na cedādāsyate bālo diṣṭasya vaśameṣyati ||18||

19ahāpayan pāṇḍavārthaṃ yathāvacchamaṃ kurūṇāṃ yadi cācareyam
|
puṇyaṃ ca me syāccaritaṃ mahātman mucyeraṃśca kuravo mṛtyupāśāt ||19||

20api vācaṃ bhāṣamāṇasya kāvyāṃ dharmārāmāmarthavatīmahiṃsrām
|
avekṣeran dhārtarāṣṭrāḥ śamārthāṃ māṃ ca prāptaṃ kuravaḥ pūjayeyuḥ ||20||

21na cāpi mama paryāptāḥ sahitāḥ sarvapārthivāḥ
|
kruddhasya pramukhe sthātuṃ siṃhasyevetare mṛgāḥ ||21||

22vaiśampāyana uvāca|
ityevamuktvā vacanaṃ vṛṣṇīnāmṛṣabhastadā
|
śayane sukhasaṃsparśe śiśye yadusukhāvahaḥ ||22||
