Mahābhārata 12.307 (Śāntiparvan)

1vasiṣṭha uvāca |
sāṃkhyadarśanametāvaduktaṃ te nṛpasattama
|
vidyāvidye tvidānīṃ me tvaṃ nibodhānupūrvaśaḥ||1||

2avidyāmāhuravyaktaṃ sargapralayadharmi vai
|
sargapralayanirmuktāṃ vidyāṃ vai pañcaviṃśakaḥ||2||

3parasparasya vidyāṃ vai tvaṃ nabodhānupūrvaśaḥ
|
yathoktamṛṣibhistāta sāṃkhyasyābhinidarśanam||3||

4karmendriyāṇāṃ sarveṣāṃ vidyā buddhīndriyaṃ smṛtam
|
buddhīndriyāṇāṃ ca tathā viśeṣā iti naḥ śrutam||4||

5viśeṣāṇāṃ manasteṣāṃ vidyāmāhurmanīṣiṇaḥ
|
manasaḥ pañcabhūtāni vidyā ityabhicakṣate||5||

6ahaṅkārastu bhūtānāṃ pañcānāṃ nātra saṃśayaḥ
|
ahaṅkārasya ca tathā buddhirvidyā nareśvara||6||

7vidyā prakṛtiravyaktaṃ tattvānāṃ parameśvarī
|
vidyā jñeyā naraśreṣṭha vidhiśca paramaḥ smṛtaḥ||7||

8avyaktasya paraṃ prāhurvidyāṃ vai pañcaviṃśakam
|
sarvasya sarvamityuktaṃ jñeyaṃ jñānasya pārthiva||8||

9jñānamavyaktamityuktaṃ jñeyo vai pañcaviṃśakaḥ
|
tathaiva jñānamavyaktaṃ vijñātā pañcaviṃśakaḥ||9||

10vidyā vidyārthatattvena mayoktā te viśeṣataḥ
|
akṣaraṃ ca kṣaraṃ caiva yaduktaṃ tannibodha me||10||

11ubhāvevākṣarāvuktāvubhāvetāvanakṣarau
|
kāraṇaṃ tu pravakṣyāmi yāthātathyaṃ tu jñānataḥ||11||

12anādinidhanāvetāvubhāveveśvarau matau
|
tattvasaṃjñāvubhāvetau procyeta jñānacintakaiḥ||12||

13sargapralayadharmatvādavyaktaṃ prāhurakṣaram
|
tadetadguṇasargāya vikurvāṇaṃ punaḥ punaḥ||13||

14guṇānāṃ mahadādīnāmutpattiśca parasparam
|
adhiṣṭhānātkṣetramāhuretattatpañcaviṃśakam||14||

15yadā tu guṇajālaṃ tadavyaktātmani saṃkṣipet
|
tadā saha guṇaistaistu pañcaviṃśo vilīyate||15||

16guṇā guṇeṣu līyante tadaikā prakṛtirbhavet
|
kṣetrajño'pi yadā tāta tatkṣetre saṃpralīyate||16||

17tadā kṣaratvaṃ prakṛtirgacchate guṇasaṃśritā
|
nirguṇatvaṃ ca vaideha guṇeṣvaprativartanāt||17||

18evameva ca kṣetrajñaḥ kṣetrajñānaparikṣaye
|
prakṛtyā nirguṇastveṣa ityevamanuśuśruma||18||

19kṣaro bhavatyeṣa yadā tadā guṇavatīmatha
|
prakṛtiṃ tvabhijānāti nirguṇatvaṃ tathā''tmanaḥ||19||

20tadā viśuddho bhavati prakṛteḥ parivarjanāt
|
anyo'hamanyeyamiti yadā budhyati buddhimān||20||

21tadaiṣa tattvatāmeti na cāpi miśratāṃ vrajet
|
prakṛtyā caiva rājendra miśro hyanyaśca dṛśyate||21||

22yadā tu guṇajālaṃ tatprākṛtaṃ vai jugupsate
|
paśyate ca paraṃ paśyan tadā paśyanna saṃtyajet||22||

23kiṃ mayā kṛtametāvadyo'haṃ kālamimaṃ janam
|
matsyo jālaṃ hyavijñānādanuvartitavāniha||23||

24ahameva hi saṃmohādanyamanyaṃ janājjanam
|
matsyo yathodakajñānādanuvartitavānaham||24||

25matsyo'nyatvaṃ yathā'jñānādudakānnābhimanyate
|
ātmānaṃ tadvadajñānādanyatvaṃ naiva vedmyaham||25||

26mamāstu dhigabuddhasya yo'haṃ magnamimaṃ punaḥ
|
anuvartitavānmohādanyamanyaṃ janājjanam||26||

27ayamatra bhavedbandhuranena saha me kṣamam
|
sāmyamekatvamāyāto yādṛśastādṛśastvaham||27||

28tulyatāmiha paśyāmi sadṛśo'hamanena vai
|
ayaṃ hi vimalo vyaktamahamīdṛśakastathā||28||

29yo'hamajñānasaṃmohādajñayā saṃpravṛttavān
|
sasaṅgayā'haṃ ni:saṅgaḥ sthitaḥ kālamimaṃ tvaham||29||

30anayā'haṃ vaśībhūtaḥ kālametaṃ na buddhavān
|
uccamadhyamanīcānāṃ tāmahaṃ kathamāvase||30||

31samānayānayā ceha saha vāsamahaṃ katham
|
gacchāmyabuddhabhāvatvādeṣedānīṃ sthiro bhave||31||

32sahavāsaṃ na yāsyāmi kālametaddhi vañcanāt
|
vañcito'smyanayā yaddhi nirvikāro vikārayā||32||

33na cāyamaparādho'syā hyaparādho hyayaṃ mama
|
yo'hamatrābhavaṃ saktaḥ parāṅmukhamupasthitaḥ||33||

34tato'smi bahurūpāsu sthito mūrtiṣvamūrtimān
|
amūrtaścāpi mūrtātmā mamatvena pradharṣitaḥ||34||

35prākkṛtena mamatvena tāsu tāsviha yoniṣu
|
nirmamasya mamatvena kiṃ kṛtaṃ tāsu tāsu ca||35||

36yonīṣu vartamānena naṣṭasaṃjñena cetasā
|
na mamātrānayā kāryamahaṅkārakṛtātmayā||36||

37ātmānaṃ bahudhā kṛtvā yeyaṃ bhūyo yunakti mām
|
idānīmeṣa buddho'smi nirmamo nirahaṃkṛtaḥ||37||

38mamatvamanayā nityamahaṅkārakṛtātmakam
|
apetyāhamimāṃ hitvā saṃśrayiṣye nirāmayam||38||

39anena sāmyaṃ yāsyāmi nānayā'hamacetayā
|
kṣemaṃ mama sahānena naikatvamanayā saha||39||

40evaṃ paramasaṃbodhātpañcaviṃśo'nubuddhavān
|
akṣaratvaṃ niyaccheta tyaktvā'kṣaramanāmayam||40||

41avyaktaṃ vyaktadharmāṇaṃ saguṇaṃ nirguṇaṃ tathā
|
nirguṇaṃ prathamaṃ dṛṣṭvā tādṛgbhavati maithila||41||

42akṣarakṣarayoretaduktaṃ tava nidarśanam
|
mayeha jñānasaṃpannaṃ yathāśruti nidarśanāt||42||

43niḥsaṃdigdhaṃ ca sūkṣmaṃ ca vibuddha vimalaṃ yathā
|
pravakṣyāmi tu te bhūyastannibodha yathāśrutam||43||

44sāṃkhyayogau mayā proktau śāstradvayanidarśanāt
|
yadeva śāstraṃ sāṃkhyoktaṃ yogadarśanameva tat||44||

45prabodhanakaraṃ jñānaṃ sāṃkhyānāmavanīpate
|
vispaṣṭaṃ procyate tatra śiṣyāṇāṃ hitakāmyayā||45||

46bṛhaccaivamidaṃ śāstramityāhurviduṣo janāḥ
|
asmiṃśca śāstre yogānāṃ punarvede puraḥsaraḥ||46||

47pañcaviṃśātparaṃ tattvaṃ paṭhyate na narādhipa
|
sāṃkhyānāṃ tu paraṃ tattvaṃ yathāvadanuvarṇitam||47||

48buddhamapratibuddhatvādbudhyamānaṃ ca tattvataḥ
|
budhyamānaṃ ca buddhaṃ ca prāhuryoganidarśanam||48||
