Mahābhārata 4.38 (Virāṭaparvan)

1vaiśampāyana uvāca| sa rājadhānyā niryāya vairāṭirakutobhayaḥ
|
prayāhītyabravīt sūtaṃ yatra te kuravo gatāḥ ||1||

2samavetān kurūn sarvān jigīṣūnavajitya vai
|
gāsteṣāṃ kṣipramādāya punareṣyāmyahaṃ puram ||2||

3tatastāṃścodayāmāsa sadaśvān pāṇḍunandanaḥ
|
te hayā narasiṃhena noditā vātaraṃhasaḥ
|
ālikhanta ivākāśamūhuḥ kāñcanamālinaḥ ||3||

4nātidūramatho gatvā matsyaputradhanañjayau
|
avekṣetāmamitraghnau kurūṇāṃ balināṃ balam ||4||

5śmaśānamabhito gatvā āsasāda kurūnatha
|
tāṃ śarmāmanvavīkṣetāṃ vyūḍhānīkāṃśca sarvaśaḥ ||5||

6tadanīkaṃ mahatteṣāṃ vibabhau sāgaropamam
|
sarpamāṇamivākāśe vanaṃ bahulapādapam ||6||

7dadṛśe pārthivo reṇurjanitastena sarpatā
|
dṛṣṭipraṇāśo bhūtānāṃ divaspṛk kurusattama ||7||

8tadanīkaṃ mahaddṛṣṭvā gajāśvarathasaṅkulam
|
karṇaduryodhanakṛpairguptaṃ śāntanavena ca ||8||

9droṇena ca saputreṇa maheṣvāsena dhīmatā
|
hṛṣṭaromā bhayodvignaḥ pārthaṃ vairāṭirabravīt ||9||

10uttara uvāca|notsahe kurubhiryoddhuṃ romaharṣaṃ hi paśya me
|
bahupravīramatyugraṃ devairapi durāsadam ||10||

11pratiyoddhuṃ na śakṣyāmi kurusainyamanantakam
|
nāśaṃse bhāratīṃ senāṃ praveṣṭuṃ bhīmakārmukām ||11||

12rathanāgāśvakalilāṃ pattidhvajasamākulām
|
dṛṣṭvaiva hi parānājau manaḥ pravyathatīva me ||12||

13yatra droṇaśca bhīṣmaśca kṛpaḥ karṇo viviṃśatiḥ
|
aśvatthāmā vikarṇaśca somadattaśca bālhikaḥ ||13||

14duryodhanastathā vīro rājā ca rathināṃ varaḥ
|
dyutimanto maheṣvāsāḥ sarve yuddhaviśāradāḥ ||14||

15dṛṣṭvaiva hi kurūnetān vyūḍhānīkān prahāriṇaḥ
|
hṛṣitāni ca romāṇi kaśmalaṃ cāgataṃ mama ||15||

16vaiśampāyana uvāca|avijāto vijātasya maurkhyāt dhūrtasya paśyataḥ
|
paridevayate mandaḥ sakāśe savyasācinaḥ ||16||

17trigartānme pitā yātaḥ śūnye saṃpraṇidhāya mām
|
sarvāṃ senāmupādāya na me santīha sainikāḥ ||17||

18so'hameko bahūn bālaḥ kṛtāstrānakṛtaśramaḥ
|
pratiyoddhuṃ na śakṣyāmi nivartasva bṛhannale ||18||

19bṛhannalovāca
|
bhayena dīnarūpo'si dviṣatāṃ harṣavarddhanaḥ
|
na ca tāvatkṛtaṃ karma paraiḥ kiñcidraṇājire ||19||

20svayameva ca māmāttha vaha māṃ kauravān prati
|
so'haṃ tvāṃ tatra neṣyāmi yatraite bahulā dhvajāḥ ||20||

21madhyamāmiṣagṛdhrāṇāṃ kurūṇāmātatāyinām
|
neṣyāmi tvāṃ mahābāho pṛthivyāmapi yudhyatām ||21||

22tathā strīṣu pratiśrutya pauruṣaṃ puruṣeṣu ca
|
katthamāno'bhiniryāya kimarthaṃ na yuyutsase ||22||

23na cedvijitya gāstāstvaṃ gṛhān vai pratiyāsyasi
|
prahasiṣyanti vīrāstvāṃ narā nāryaśca saṃgatāḥ ||23||

24ahamapyatra sairandhryā khyātā sārathyakarmaṇi
|
na ca śakṣyāmyanirjitya gāḥ prayātuṃ puraṃ prati ||24||

25stotreṇa caiva sairandhryāstava vākyena tena ca
|
kathaṃ na yudhyeyamahaṃ kurūn sarvān sthiro bhava ||25||

26uttara uvāca|kāmaṃ harantu matsyānāṃ bhūyāṃsaḥ kuravo dhanam
|
prahasantu ca māṃ nāryo narā vā'pi bṛhannale ||26||

27saṅgrāme na ca kāryaṃ me gāvo gacchantu cāpi me
|
śūnyaṃ me nagaraṃ cāpi pituścaiva bibhemyaham ||27||

28vaiśampāyana uvāca|ityuktvā prādravadbhīto rathāt praskandya kuṇḍalī
|
tyaktvā mānaṃ ca darpaṃ ca visṛjya saśaraṃ dhanuḥ ||28||

29bṛhannalovāca
|
naiṣa śūraiḥ smṛto dharmaḥ kṣatriyasya palāyanam
|
śreyastu maraṇaṃ yuddhe na bhītasya palāyanam ||29||

30vaiśampāyana uvāca|evamuktvā tu kaunteyaḥ so'vaplutya rathottamāt
|
tamanvadhāvaddhāvantaṃ rājaputraṃ dhanañjayaḥ ||30||

31dīrghāṃ veṇīṃ vidhunvānaḥ sādhu rakte ca vāsasī
|
vidhūya veṇīṃ dhāvantamajānanto'rjunaṃ tadā ||31||

32sainikāḥ prāhasan kecittathārūpamavekṣya tam
|
taṃ śīghramabhidhāvantaṃ samprekṣya kuravo'bruvan ||32||

33ka eṣa veṣasaṃcchanno bhasmanyeva hutāśanaḥ
|
kiṃcidasya yathā puṃsaḥ kiṃcidasya yathā striyaḥ ||33||

34sārūpyamarjunasyeva klībarūpaṃ bibharti ca
|
tadevaitacchiro grīvaṃ tau bāhū parighopamau
|
tadvadevāsya vikrāntaṃ nāyamanyo dhanañjayāt ||34||

35amareṣviva devendro mānuṣeṣu dhanañjayaḥ
|
ekaḥ ko'smānupāyāyādanyo loke dhanañjayāt ||35||

36ekaḥ putro virāṭasya śūnye sannihitaḥ pure
|
sa eṣa kila niryāto bālabhāvānna pauruṣāt ||36||

37satreṇa nūnaṃ channaṃ hi carantaṃ pārthamarjunam
|
uttaraḥ sārathiṃ kṛtvā niryāto nagarādbahiḥ ||37||

38sa no manyāmahe dṛṣṭvā bhīta eṣa palāyate
|
taṃ nūnameṣa dhāvantaṃ jighṛkṣati dhanañjayaḥ ||38||

39vaiśampāyana uvāca|iti sma kuravaḥ sarve vimṛśantaḥ pṛthak pṛthak
|
na ca vyavasituṃ kiṃciduttaraṃ śaknuvanti te ||39||

40channaṃ tathā taṃ satreṇa pāṇḍavaṃ prekṣya bhārata
|
uttaraṃ tu pradhāvantamabhidrutya dhanañjayaḥ
|
gatvā padaśataṃ tūrṇaṃ keśapakṣe parāmṛśat ||40||

41so'rjunena parāmṛṣṭaḥ paryadevayadārtavat
|
bahulaṃ kṛpaṇaṃ caiva virāṭasya sutastadā ||41||

42uttara uvāca|śṛṇuyāstvaṃ hi kalyāṇi bṛhannale sumadhyame
|
nivartaya rathaṃ kṣipraṃ jīvan bhadrāṇi paśyati ||42||

43śātakumbhasya śuddhasya śataṃ niṣkān dadāmi te
|
maṇīnaṣṭau ca vaidūryān hemabaddhān mahāprabhān ||43||

44hemadaṇḍapraticchannaṃ rathaṃ yuktaṃ ca suvrataiḥ
|
mattāṃśca daśamātaṅgān muñca māṃ tvaṃ bṛhannale ||44||

45vaiśampāyana uvāca|evamādīni vākyāni vilapantamacetasam
|
prahasya puruṣavyāghro rathasyāntikamānayat ||45||

46athainamabravīt pārtho bhayārtaṃ naṣṭacetasam
|
yadi notsahase yoddhuṃ śatrubhiḥ śatrukarṣaṇa
|
ehi me tvaṃ hayān yaccha yudhyamānasya śatrubhiḥ ||46||

47prayāhyetadrathānīkaṃ madbāhubalarakṣitaḥ
|
apradhṛṣyatamaṃ ghoraṃ guptaṃ vīrairmahārathaiḥ ||47||

48mā bhaistvaṃ rājaputrāgrya kṣatriyo'si parantapa
|
kathaṃ puruṣaśārdūla śatrumadhye viṣīdasi ||48||

49ahaṃ vai kurubhiryotsye vijeṣyāmi ca te paśūn
|
praviśyaitadrathānīkamapradhṛṣyaṃ durāsadam ||49||

50yantā bhava naraśreṣṭha yotsye'haṃ kurubhiḥ saha
|
evaṃ bruvāṇo bībhatsurvairāṭimaparājitaḥ
|
samāśvāsya muhūrtaṃ tamuttaraṃ bharatarṣabha ||50||

51tata enaṃ viceṣṭantamakāmaṃ bhayapīḍitam
|
rathamāropayāmāsa pārthaḥ praharatāṃ varaḥ ||51||
