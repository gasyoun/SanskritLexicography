Mahābhārata 12.212 (Śāntiparvan)

1bhīṣma uvāca|
pravṛttilakṣaṇo dharmo yathā samupalabhyate
|
teṣāṃ vijñānaniṣṭhānāmanyattatvaṃ na rocate ||1||

2durlabhā vedavidvāṃso vedokteṣu vyavasthitāḥ
|
prayojanaṃ mahattvāttu mārgamicchanti saṃstutam ||2||

3sadbhirācaritatvāttu vṛttametadagarhitam
|
iyaṃ sā buddhirabhyetya yayā yāti parāṃ gatim ||3||

4śarīravānupādatte mohātsarvānparigrahān
|
krodhalobhādibhirbhāvairyukto rājasatāmasaiḥ ||4||

5nāśuddhamācarettasmādabhīpsandehayāpanam
|
karmaṇā vivaraṃ kurvanna lokānāpnuyācchubhān ||5||

6lohayuktaṃ yathā hema vipakvaṃ na virājate
|
tathā'pakvakaṣāyākhyaṃ vijñānaṃ na prakāśate ||6||

7yaścādharmaṃ carellobhātkāmakrodhāvanuplavan
|
dharmyaṃ panthānamākramya sānubandho vinaśyati ||7||

8śabdādīnviṣayāṃstasmānna saṃrāgādayaṃ vrajet
|
krodho harṣo viṣādaśca jāyanteha parasparāt ||8||

9pañcabhūtātmake dehe sattve rājasatāmase
|
kamabhiṣṭuvate cāyaṃ kaṃ vā''krośati kiṃ vadan ||9||

10sparśa-rūpa-rasādyeṣu saṅgaṃ gacchanti bāliśāḥ
|
nāvagacchanti vijñānādātmānaṃ pārthivaṃ guṇam ||10||

11mṛnmayaṃ śaraṇaṃ yadvanmṛdaiva parilipyate
|
pārthivo'yaṃ tathā deho mṛdvikārānna naśyati ||11||

12madhu, tailaṃ, payaḥ, sarpirmāṃsāni, lavaṇaṃ, guḍaḥ
|
dhānyāni, phalamūlāni mṛdvikārāḥ sahāmbhasā ||12||

13yadvatkāntāramātiṣṭhannautsukyaṃ samanuvrajet
|
grāmyamāhāramādadyādasvādvapi hi yāpanam ||13||

14tadvatsasārakāntāramātiṣṭhan śramatatparaḥ
|
yātrārthamadyādāhāraṃ vyādhito bheṣajaṃ yathā ||14||

15satyaśaucārjavatyāgairvarcasā vikrameṇa ca
|
kṣāntyā, dhṛtyā ca buddhyā ca manasā tapasaiva ca ||15||

16bhāvānsarvānupāvṛttān samīkṣya viṣayātmakān
|
śāntimicchannadīnātmā saṃyacchedindriyāṇi ca ||16||

17sattvena rajasā caiva tamasā caiva mohitāḥ
|
cakravatparivartante hyajñānājjantavo bhṛśam ||17||

18tasmātsamyak parīkṣeta doṣānajñānasambhavān
|
ajñānaprabhavaṃ duḥkhamahaṃkāraṃ parityajet ||18||

19mahābhūtānīndriyāṇi guṇāḥ sattvaṃ rajastamaḥ
|
trelokyaṃ seśvaraṃ sarvamahaṃkāre pratiṣṭhitam ||19||

20yatheha niyataḥ kālo darśayatyārtavān guṇān
|
tadvadbhūteṣvahaṃkāraṃ vidyātkarmapravartakam ||20||

21sammohakaṃ tamo vidyātkṛṣṇamajñānasambhavam
|
prītiduḥkhanibaddhāṃśca samastāṃstrīnatho guṇān ||21||

22sattvasya rajasaścaiva tamasaśca nibodha tān
|
prasādo harṣajā prītirasaṃdaho dhṛtiḥ smṛtiḥ
|
etān sattvaguṇān vidyādimān rājasatāmasān ||22||

23kāmakrodhau pramādaśca lobhamohau bhayaṃ klamaḥ
|
viṣādaśokāvaratirmānadarpāvanāryatā ||23||

24doṣāṇāmevamādīnāṃ parokṣya gurulāghavam
|
vimṛśedātmasaṃsthānamekaikamanusantatam ||24||

25yudhiṣṭhira uvāca|
ke doṣā manasā tyaktāḥ ke buddhyā śithilīkṛtāḥ
|
ke punaḥ punarāyānti ke mohādaphalā iva ||25||

26keṣāṃ balābalaṃ buddhyā hetubhirvimaśedbudhaḥ
|
eṣa me saṃśayastāta tanme brūhi pitāmaha ||26||

27bhīṣma uvāca|
doṣairmūlādavacchinnairviśuddhātmā vimucyate
|
vināśayati sambhūtamayasmayamayo yathā
|
tathā kṛtātmā sahajairdoṣairnaśyati tāmasaiḥ ||27||

28rājasaṃ tāmasaṃ caiva śuddhātmakamakalmaṣam
|
tatsarvaṃ dehināṃ bījaṃ sattvamātmavataḥ samam ||28||

29tasmādātmavatā varjyaṃ rajaśca tama eva ca
|
rajastamobhyāṃ nirmuktaṃ sattvaṃ nirmalatāmiyāt ||29||

30athavā mantravadbrūyurātmādānāya duṣkṛtam
|
sa vai heturanādāne śuddhadharmānupālane ||30||

31rajasā'dharmayuktāni kāryāṇyapi samāpnute
|
arthayuktāni cātyarthaṃ kāmān sarvāṃśca sevate ||31||

32tamasā lobhayuktāni krodhajāni ca sevate
|
hiṃsāvihārābhiratastandrīnidrāsamanvitaḥ ||32||

33sattvasthaḥ sāttvikān bhāvān śuddhānpaśyati saṃśritaḥ
|
sa dehī vimalaḥ śrīmān śraddhāvidyāsamanvitaḥ ||33||
