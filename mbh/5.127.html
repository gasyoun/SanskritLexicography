Mahābhārata 5.127 (Udyogaparvan)

1vaiśampāyana uvāca|
śrutvā duryodhano vākyamapriyaṃ kurusaṃsadi
|
pratyuvāca mahābāhuṃ vāsudevaṃ yaśasvinam ||1||

2prasamīkṣya bhavānetadvaktumarhati keśava
|
māmeva hi viśeṣeṇa vibhāṣya parigarhase ||2||

3bhaktivādena pārthānāmakasmānmadhusūdana
|
bhavān garhayate nityaṃ kiṃ samīkṣya balābalam ||3||

4bhavānkṣattā ca rājā vā'pyācāryo vā pitāmahaḥ
|
māmeva parigarhante nānyaṃ kañcana pārthivam ||4||

5na cāhaṃ lakṣaye kañcidvyabhicāramihātmanaḥ
|
atha sarve bhavanto māṃ vidviṣanti sarājakāḥ ||5||

6na cāhaṃ kañcidatyarthamaparādhamarindama
|
vicintayan prapaśyāmi susūkṣmamapi keśava ||6||

7priyābhyupagate dyūte pāṇḍavā madhusūdana
|
jitāḥ śakuninā rājyaṃ tatra kiṃ mama duṣkṛtam ||7||

8yatpunardraviṇaṃ kiñcittatrājīyanta pāṇḍavāḥ
|
tebhya evābhyanujñātaṃ tattadā madhusūdana ||8||

9aparādho na cāsmākaṃ yatte hyakṣaiḥ parājitāḥ
|
ajeyā jayatāṃ śreṣṭha pārthāḥ pravrājitā vanam ||9||

10kena vā'pyapavādena viruddhyantyaribhiḥ saha
|
aśaktāḥ pāṇḍavāḥ kṛṣṇa prahṛṣṭāḥ pratyamitravat ||10||

11kimasmābhiḥ kṛtaṃ teṣāṃ kasmin vā punarāgasi
|
dhārtarāṣṭrān jighāṃsanti pāṇḍavāḥ sṛñjayaiḥ saha ||11||

12na cāpi vayamugreṇa karmaṇā vacanena vā
|
prabhraṣṭāḥ praṇamāmeha bhayādapi śatakratum ||12||

13na ca taṃ kṛṣṇa paśyāmi kṣatradharmamanuṣṭhitam
|
utsaheta yudhā jetuṃ yo naḥ śatrunibarhaṇa ||13||

14na hi bhīṣmakṛpadroṇāḥ sakarṇā madhusūdana
|
devairapi yudhā jetuṃ śakyāḥ kimuta pāṇḍavaiḥ ||14||

15svadharmamanupaśyanto yadi mādhava saṃyuge
|
astreṇa nidhanaṃ kāle prāpsyāmaḥ svargyameva tat ||15||

16mukhyaścaivaiṣa no dharmaḥ kṣatriyāṇāṃ janārdana
|
yacchayīmahi saṃgrāme śaratalpagatā vayam ||16||

17te vayaṃ vīraśayanaṃ prāpsyāmo yadi saṃyuge
|
apraṇamyaiva śatrūṇāṃ na nastapsyanti mādhava ||17||

18kaśca jātu kule jātaḥ kṣatradharmeṇa vartayan
|
bhayādvṛttiṃ samīkṣyaivaṃ praṇamediha karhicit ||18||

19udyacchedeva na namedudyamo hyeva pauruṣam
|
apyaparvaṇi bhajyeta na namediha karhicit ||19||

20iti mātaṅgavacanaṃ parīpsanti hitepsavaḥ
|
dharmāya caiva praṇamedbrāhmaṇebhyaśca madvidhaḥ ||20||

21acintayan kañcidanyaṃ yāvajjīvaṃ tathā''caret
|
eṣa dharmaḥ kṣatriyāṇāṃ matametacca me sadā ||21||

22rājyāṃśaścābhyanujñāto yo me pitrā purā'bhavat
|
na sa labhyaḥ punarjātu mayi jīvati keśava ||22||

23yāvacca rājā dhriyate dhṛtarāṣṭro janārdana
|
nyarataśastrā vayaṃ te vā'pyupajīvāma mādhava
|
apradeyaṃ purā dattaṃ rājyaṃ paravato mama ||23||

24ajñānādvā bhayādvā'pi mayi bāle janārdana
|
na tadadya punarlabhyaṃ pāṇḍavairvṛṣṇinandana ||24||

25ghriyamāṇe mahābāhau mayi samprati keśava
|
yāvaddhi tīkṣṇayā sūcyā vidhyedagreṇa keśava|| tāvadapyaparityājyaṃ bhūmernaḥ pāṇḍavān prati ||25||
