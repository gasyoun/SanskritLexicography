Mahābhārata 8.64 (Karṇaparvan)

1sañjaya uvāca|
drauṇistu rathavaṃśena mahatā parivāritaḥ
|
apatatsahasā rājan yatra pārtho vyavasthitaḥ ||1||

2tamāpatantaṃ sahasā śūraḥ śaurisahāyavān
|
dadhāra sahasā pārtho veleva makarālayam ||2||

3tataḥ kruddho mahārāja droṇaputraḥ pratāpavān
|
arjunaṃ vāsudevaṃ ca chādayāmāsa sāyakaiḥ ||3||

4avacchannau tataḥ kṛṣṇau dṛṣṭvā tatra mahārathāḥ
|
vismayaṃ paramaṃ gatvā praikṣanta kuravastadā ||4||

5arjunastu tato divyamastraṃ cakre hasanniva
|
tadastraṃ vārayāmāsa brāhmaṇo yudhi bhārata ||5||

6yadyaddhi vyākṣipadyuddhe pāṇḍavo'strajighāṃsayā
|
tattadastraṃ maheṣvāso droṇaputro vyaśātayat ||6||

7astrayuddhe tato rājan vartamāne mahābhaye
|
apaśyāma raṇe drauṇiṃ vyāttānanamivāntakam ||7||

8sa diśaḥ pradiśaścaiva cchādayitvā hyajihmagaiḥ
|
vāsudevaṃ tribhirbāṇairavidhyaddakṣiṇe bhuje ||8||

9tato'rjuno hayānhatvā sarvāṃstasya mahātmanaḥ
|
cakāra samare bhūmiṃ śoṇitaughataraṅgiṇīm ||9||

10sarvalokavahāṃ raudrāṃ paralokavahāṃ nadīm
|
sarathānrathinaḥ sarvānpārthacāpacyutaiḥ śaraiḥ ||10||

11drauṇerapahatānsaṃkhye dadṛśuḥ sa ca tāṃ tathā
|
prāvartayanmahāghorāṃ nadīṃ paravahāṃ tadā ||11||

12tayostu vyākule yuddhe drauṇeḥ pārthasya dāruṇe
|
amaryādaṃ yodhayantaḥ paryadhāvanta pṛṣṭhataḥ ||12||

13rathairhatāśvasūtaiśca hatārohaiśca vājibhiḥ
|
dviradaiśca hatārohairmahāmātrairhatadvipaiḥ ||13||

14pārthena samare rājan kṛto ghoro janakṣayaḥ
|
nihatā rathinaḥ petuḥ pārthacāpacyutaiḥ śaraiḥ ||14||

15hayāśca paryadhāvanta muktayoktrāstatastataḥ
|
taddṛṣṭvā karma pārthasya drauṇirāhavaśobhinaḥ ||15||

16arjunaṃ jayatāṃ śreṣṭhaṃ tvarito'bhyetya vīryavān
|
vidhunvāno mahaccāpaṃ kārtasvaravibhūṣitam ||16||

17avākirattato drauṇiḥ samantānniśitaiḥ śaraiḥ
|
bhūyo'rjunaṃ mahārāja drauṇirāyamya patriṇā ||17||

18vakṣodeśe bhṛśaṃ pārthaṃ tāḍayāmāsa nirdayam
|
so'tividdho raṇe tena droṇaputreṇa bhārata ||18||

19gāṇḍīvadhanvā prasabhaṃ śaravarṣairudāradhīḥ
|
saṃchādya samare drauṇiṃ cicchedāsya ca kārmukam ||19||

20sa chinnadhanvā parighaṃ vajrasparśasamaṃ yudhi
|
ādāya cikṣepa tadā droṇaputraḥ kirīṭine ||20||

21tamāpatantaṃ parighaṃ jāmbūnada‌pariṣkṛtam
|
ciccheda sahasā rājanprahasanniva pāṇḍavaḥ ||21||

22sa papāta tadā bhūmau nikṛttaḥ pārthasāyakaiḥ
|
vikīrṇaḥ parvato rājan yathā vajreṇa tāḍitaḥ||22||

23tataḥ kruddho mahārāja droṇaputro mahārathaḥ
|
aindreṇa cāstravegena bībhatsuṃ samavākirat ||23||

24tasyendrajālāvatataṃ samīkṣya pārtho rājan gāṇḍivamādade saḥ
|
aindraṃ jālaṃ pratyaharattarasvī varāstramādāya mahendrasṛṣṭam ||24||

25vidārya tajjālamathendramuktaṃ pārthastato drauṇirathaṃ kṣaṇena
|
pracchādayāmāsa tato'bhyupetya drauṇistadā pārthaśarābhibhūtaḥ ||25||

26vigāhya tāṃ pāṇḍavabāṇavṛṣṭiṃ śaraiḥ paraṃ nāma tataḥ prakāśya
|
śatena kṛṣṇaṃ sahasā'bhyaviddhyastribhiḥ śatairarjunaṃ kṣudrakāṇām ||26||

27tato'rjunaḥ sāyakānāṃ śatena guroḥ sutaṃ marmasu nirbibheda
|
aśvāṃśca sūtaṃ ca tathā dhanurjyāmavākirat paśyatāṃ tāvakānām ||27||

28sa viddhvā marmasu drauṇiṃ pāṇḍavaḥ paravīrahā
|
sārathiṃ cāsya bhallena rathanīḍāda‌pātayat ||28||

29sa saṃgṛhya svayaṃ vāhān kṛṣṇau prācchādayaccharaiḥ
|
tatrādbhutamapaśyāma drauṇerāśu parākramam ||29||

30prāyacchatturagānyacca phālgunaṃ cāpyayodhayat
|
tadasya samare rājan sarve yodhā apūjayan ||30||

31tataḥ prahasya bībhatsurdroṇaputrasya saṃyuge
|
kṣipraṃ raśmīnathāśvānāṃ kṣurapraiścicchide jayaḥ ||31||

32prādravaṃsturagāste tu śaravegaprapīḍitāḥ
|
tato'bhūnninado ghorastava sainyasya bhārata ||32||

33pāṇḍavāstu jayaṃ labdhvā tava sainyaṃ samādravan
|
samantānniśitān bāṇān vimuñcanto jayaiṣiṇaḥ ||33||

34pāṇḍavaistu mahārāja dhārtarāṣṭrī mahācamūḥ
|
punaḥ punaratho vīraiḥ saṃyuge jitakāśibhiḥ ||34||

35paśyatāṃ te mahārāja putrāṇāṃ citrayodhinām
|
śakuneḥ saubaleyasya karṇasya ca viśāṃpate ||35||

36vāryamāṇā mahāsenā putraistava janeśvara
|
na cātiṣṭhata saṃgrāme pīḍyamānā samantataḥ ||36||

37tato yodhairmahārāja palāyadbhiḥ samantataḥ
|
abhavadvyākulaṃ bhītaṃ putrāṇāṃ te mahadbalam ||37||

38tiṣṭha tiṣṭheti ca tataḥ sūtaputrasya jalpataḥ
|
nāvatiṣṭhati sā senā vadhyamānā mahātmabhiḥ ||38||

39athotkruṣṭaṃ mahārāja pāṇḍavairjitakāśibhiḥ
|
dhārtarāṣṭrabalaṃ dṛṣṭvā vidrutaṃ vai samantataḥ ||39||

40tato duryodhanaḥ karṇamabravītpraṇayādiva
|
paśya karṇa mahāsenā pañcālairarditā bhṛśam ||40||

41tvayi tiṣṭhati saṃtrāsātpalāyanaparāyaṇā
|
etajjñātvā mahābāho kuru prāptamarindama ||41||

42sahasrāṇi ca yodhānāṃ tvāmeva puruṣottama
|
krośanti samare vīra drāvyamāṇāni pāṇḍavaiḥ ||42||

43etacchrutvā'pi rādheyo duryodhanavaco mahān
|
madrarājamidaṃ vākyamabravītprahasanniva ||43||

44paśya me bhujayorvīryamastrāṇāṃ ca janeśvara
|
adya hanmi raṇe sarvānpañcālānpāṇḍubhiḥ saha ||44||

45vāhayāśvānnaravyāghra bhadreṇaiva na saṃśayaḥ
|
evamuktvā mahārāja sūtaputraḥ pratāpavān ||45||

46pragṛhya vijayaṃ vīro dhanuḥ śreṣṭhaṃ purātanam
|
sajyaṃ kṛtvā mahārāja saṃgṛhya ca punaḥ punaḥ ||46||

47sannivārya ca yodhānsa satyena śapathena ca
|
prāyojayada‌meyātmā bhārgavāstraṃ mahābalaḥ ||47||

48tato rājansahasrāṇi prayutānyarbudāni ca
|
koṭiśaśca śarāstīkṣṇā niragacchanmahāmṛdhe ||48||

49jvalitaistaiḥ śarairghoraiḥ kaṅkabarhiṇavājitaiḥ
|
saṃchannā pāṇḍavī senā na prājñāyata kiñcana ||49||

50hāhākāro mahānāsītpañcālānāṃ viśāṃpate
|
pīḍitānāṃ balavatā bhārgavāstreṇa saṃyuge ||50||

51nipatadbhirgajai rājannaśvaiścāpi sahasraśaḥ
|
rathaiścāpi naravyāghra naraiścaiva samantataḥ ||51||

52prākampata mahī rājannihataistaiḥ samantataḥ
|
vyākulaṃ sarvamabhavatpāṇḍavānāṃ mahadbalam ||52||

53karṇastveko yudhāṃ śreṣṭho vidhūma iva pāvakaḥ
|
dahan śatrūnnaravyāghra śuśubhe sa paraṃtapaḥ ||53||

54te vadhyamānāḥ karṇena pañcālāścedibhiḥ saha
|
tatra tatra vyamuhyanta vanadāhe yathā dvipāḥ ||54||

55cukruśuśca naravyāghra yathā vyāghrā narottamāḥ
|
teṣāṃ tu krośatāmāsīdbhītānāṃ raṇamūrdhani ||55||

56dhāvatāṃ ca tato rājaṃstrastānāṃ ca samantataḥ
|
ārtanādo mahāṃstatra bhūtānāmiva saṃplave ||56||

57vadhyamānāṃstu tān dṛṣṭvā sūtaputreṇa māriṣa
|
vitresuḥ sarvabhūtāni tiryagyonigatānyapi ||57||

58te vadhyamānāḥ samare sūtaputreṇa sṛñjayāḥ
|
arjunaṃ vāsudevaṃ ca krośanti ca muhurmuhuḥ ||58||

59pretarājapure yadvat pretarājaṃ vicetasaḥ
|
śrutvā tu ninadaṃ teṣāṃ vadhyatāṃ karṇasāyakaiḥ ||59||

60athābravīdvāsudevaṃ kuntīputro dhanañjayaḥ
|
bhārgavāstraṃ mahāghoraṃ dṛṣṭvā tatra samīritam ||60||

61paśya kṛṣṇa mahābāho bhārgavāstrasya vikramam
|
naitadastraṃ hi samare śakyaṃ hantuṃ kathañcana ||61||

62sūtaputraṃ ca saṃrabdhaṃ paśya kṛṣṇa mahāraṇe
|
antakapratimaṃ vīrye kurvāṇaṃ karma dāruṇam ||62||

63abhīkṣṇaṃ codayannaśvānprekṣate māṃ muhurmuhuḥ
|
na ca paśyāmi samare karṇaṃ prati palāyitum ||63||

64jīvanprāpnoti puruṣaḥ saṃkhye jayaparājayau
|
mṛtasya tu hṛṣīkeśa bhaṅga eva kuto jayaḥ ||64||

65evamuktastu pārthena kṛṣṇo matimatāṃ varam
|
dhanañjayamuvācedaṃ prāptakālamarindamam ||65||

66karṇena hi dṛḍhaṃ rājā kuntīputraḥ parikṣitaḥ
|
taṃ dṛṣṭvāśvāsya ca punaḥ karṇaṃ pārtha vadhiṣyasi ||66||

67evamuktvā punaḥ prāyāddraṣṭumicchan yudhiṣṭhiram
|
śrameṇa grāhayiṣyaṃśca yuddhe karṇaṃ viśāṃpate ||67||

68tato dhanañjayo draṣṭuṃ rājānaṃ bāṇapīḍitam
|
rathena prayayau kṣipraṃ saṃgrāmātkeśavājñayā ||68||

69gacchanneva tu kaunteyo dharmarājadidṛkṣayā
|
sainyamālokayāmāsa nāpaśyattatra cāgrajam ||69||

70yuddhaṃ kṛtvā tu kaunteyo droṇaputreṇa bhārata
|
duḥsahaṃ vajriṇā saṃkhye parājitya guroḥ sutam ||70||
