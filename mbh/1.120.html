Mahābhārata 1.120 (Ādiparvan)

1vaiśaṃpāyana uvāca|
tatrāpi tapasi śreṣṭhe vartamānaḥ sa vīryavān
|
siddhacāraṇasaṅghānāṃ babhūva priyadarśanaḥ||1||

2suśrūṣuranahaṃvādī saṃyatātmā jitendriyaḥ
|
svargaṃ gantuṃ parākrāntaḥ svena vīryeṇa bhārata||2||

3keṣāṃcidabhavadbhrātā keṣāṃcidabhavatsakhā
|
ṛṣayastvapare cainaṃ putravatparyapālayan||3||

4sa tu kālena mahatā prāpya niṣkalmaṣaṃ tapaḥ
|
brahmarṣisadṛśaḥ pāṇḍurbabhūva bharatarṣabha||4||

5amāvāsyāṃ tu sahitā ṛṣayaḥ saṃśitavratāḥ
|
brahmāṇaṃ draṣṭukāmāste saṃpratasthurmaharṣayaḥ||5||

6saṃprayātānṛṣīndṛṣṭvā pāṇḍurvacanamabravīt
|
bhavantaḥ kva gamiṣyanti brūte me vadatāṃ varāḥ||6||

7ṛṣaya ūcuḥ|
samāvāyo mahānadya brahmaloke mahātmanām
|
devānāṃ ca ṛṣīṇāṃ ca pitṝṇāṃ ca mahātmanām
|
vayaṃ tatra gamiṣyāmo draṣṭukāmāḥ svayaṃbhuvam||7||

8vaiśaṃpāyana uvāca|
pāṇḍurutthāya sahasā gantukāmo maharṣibhiḥ
|
svargapāraṃ titīrṣuḥ sa śataśṛṅgādudaṅmukhaḥ||8||

9pratasthe saha patnībhyāmabruvaṃstaṃ ca tāpasāḥ
|
uparyupari gacchantaḥ śailarājamudaṅmukhāḥ||9||

10dṛṣṭavanto girau ramye durgāndeśānbahūnvayam
|
vimānaśatasaṃbādhāṃ gītasvaranināditām|| 10||

11ākrīḍabhūmiṃ devānāṃ gandharvāpsarasāṃ tathā
|
udyānāni kuberasya samāni viṣamāṇi ca||11||

12mahānadīnitambāṃśca gahanāngirigahvarān
|
santi nityahimā deśā nirvṛkṣamṛgapakṣiṇaḥ||12||

13santi kvacinmahādaryo durgāḥ kāściddurāsadāḥ
|
nātikrāmeta pakṣī yānkuta evetare mṛgāḥ||13||

14vāyureko hi yātyatra siddhāśca paramarṣayaḥ
|
gacchantyau śailarāje'sminrājaputryau kathaṃ tvime||14||

15na sīdetāmaduḥkhārhe māgamo bharatarṣabha
|
pāṇḍuruvāca|
aprajasya mahābhāgā na dvāraṃ paricakṣate||15||

16svarge tenābhitapto'hamaprajastu bravīmi vaḥ
|
pitryādṛṇādanirmuktastena tapye tapodhanāḥ||16||

17dehanaśe dhruvo nāśaḥ pitṝṇāmeṣa niścayaḥ
|
ṛṇaiścaturbhiḥ saṃyuktā jayante mānavā bhuvi||17||

18pitṛdevarṣimanujairdeyaṃ tebhyaśca dharmataḥ
|
etāni tu yathākālaṃ yo na buddhyati mānavaḥ||18||

19na tasya lokāḥ santīti dharmavidbhiḥ pratiṣṭhitam
|
yajñaistu devānprīṇāti svādhyāyatapasā munīn||19||

20putraiḥ śrāddhaiḥ pitṝṃścāpi anuśaṃsyena mānavan
|
ṛṣidevamanuṣyāṇāṃ parimukto'smi dharmataḥ||20||

21trayāṇāmitareṣāṃ tu nāśa ātmani naśyati
|
pitryādṛṇādanirmukta idānīmasmi tāpasāḥ||21||

22iha tasmātprajāhetoḥ prajāyante narottamāḥ
|
yathaivāhaṃ pituḥ kṣetre jātastena maharṣiṇā||22||

23tathaivāsminmama kṣetre kathaṃ vai sambhavetprajā
|
ṛṣaya ūcuḥ|
asti vai tava dharmātmanvidma devopamaṃ śubham||23||

24apatyamanaghaṃ rājanvayaṃ divyena cakṣuṣā
|
daivoddiṣṭaṃ naravyāghra karmaṇehopapādayā||24||

25akliṣṭaṃ phalamavyagro vindate buddhimānnaraḥ
|
tasmindṛṣṭe phale rājanprayatnaṃ kartumarhasi||25||

26apatyaṃ guṇasaṃpannaṃ labdhā prītikaraṃ hyasi
|
vaiśaṃpāyana uvāca|
tacchrutvā tāpasavacaḥ pāṇḍuścintā'paro'bhavat||26||

27ātmano mṛgaśāpena jānannupahatāṃ kriyām
|
sobravīdvijane kuntīṃ dharmapatnīṃ yaśasvinīm
|
apatyotpādane yatnamāpadi tvaṃ samarthaya||27||

28apatyaṃ nāma lokeṣu pratiṣṭhā dharmasaṃhitā
|
iti kunti vidurdhīrāḥ śāśvataṃ dharmavādinaḥ||28||

29iṣṭaṃ dattaṃ tapastaptaṃ niyamaśca svanuṣṭhitaḥ
|
sarvamevānapatyasya na pāvanamihocyate||29||

30sohamevaṃ viditvaitatprapaśyāmi śucismite
|
anapatyaḥ śubhāṃllokānna prāpsyāmīti cintayan||30||

31mṛgābhiśāpānnaṣṭaṃ me jananaṃ hyakṛtātmanaḥ
|
nṛśaṃsakāriṇo bhīru yathaivopahataṃ purā||31||

32ime vai bandhudāyādāḥ ṣaṭputrā dharmadarśane
|
ṣaḍevābandhudāyādāḥ putrāstañchṛṇu me pṛthe||32||

33svayaṃjātaḥ praṇītaśca parikrītaśca yaḥ sutaḥ
|
paunarbhavaśca kānīnaḥ svairiṇyāṃ yaśca jāyate||33||

34dattaḥ krītaḥ kṛtrimaśca upagacchetsvayaṃ ca yaḥ
|
sahoḍho jñātiretāśca hīnayonidhṛtaśca yaḥ||34||

35pūrvapūrvatamābhāvaṃ matvā lipseta vai sutam
|
uttamāddevarātpuṃsaḥ kāṃkṣante putramāpadi||35||

36apatyaṃ dharmaphaladaṃ śreṣṭhāṃ vindanti mānavāḥ
|
ātmaśukrādapi pṛthe manuḥ svayaṃbhuvo'bravīt||36||

37tasmātpraheṣyāmyadya tvāṃ hīnaḥ prajanātsvayam
|
sadṛśācchreyaso vā tvaṃ viddhyapatyaṃ yaśasvini||37||

38śruṇu kunti kathāmetāṃ śāradaṇḍāyinīṃ prati
|
sā vīrapatnī guruṇā niyuktā putrajanmani||38||

39puṣpeṇa prayatā snātā niśi kunti catuṣpathe
|
varayitvā dvijaṃ siddhaṃ hutvā puṇsavane'nalam||39||

40karmaṇyavasite tasminsā tenaiva sahavāsat
|
tatra trīñjanayāmāsa durjayādīnmahārathān||40||

41tathā tvamapi kalyāṇi brahmaṇāttapasādhikāt
|
manniyogādyata kṣipramapatyotpādanaṃ prati||41||
