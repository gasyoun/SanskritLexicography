Mahābhārata 12.129 (Śāntiparvan)

1yudhiṣṭhira uvāca|
nāmṛtasyeva paryāptirmamāsti bruvati tvayi
|
yathā hi svātmavṛttisthastathā tṛpto'smi bhārata ||1||

2tasmātkathaya bhūyastvaṃ dharmameva pitāmaha
|
na hi tṛptimahaṃ yāmi pibandharmāmṛtaṃ hi te ||2||

3bhīṣma uvāca|
atrāpyudāharantīmamitihāsaṃ purātanam
|
gautamasya ca saṃvādaṃ yamasya ca mahātmanaḥ ||3||

4pāriyātraṃ giriṃ prāpya gautamasyāśramo mahān
|
uvāsa gautamo yaṃ ca kālaṃ tamapi me śṛṇu ||4||

5ṣaṣṭiṃ varṣasahasrāṇi so'tapyadgautamastapaḥ
|
tamugratapasā yuktaṃ bhāvitaṃ sumahāmunim ||5||

6upayāto naravyāghra lokapālo yamastadā
|
tamapaśyatsutapasamṛṣiṃ vai gautamaṃ tadā ||6||

7sa taṃ viditvā brahmarṣiryamamāgatamojasā
|
prāñjaliḥ prayato bhūtvā upaviṣṭastapodhanaḥ ||7||

8taṃ dharmarājo dṛṣṭvaiva satkṛtyaiva dvijarṣabham
|
nyamantrayata dharmeṇa kriyatāṃ kimiti bruvan ||8||

9gautama uvāca |
mātāpitṛbhyāmānṛṇyaṃ kiṃ kṛtvā samavāpnuyāt
|
kathaṃ ca lokānāpnoti puruṣo durlabhāñśucīn ||9||

10yama uvāca |
tapaḥśaucavatā nityaṃ satyadharmaratena ca
|
mātāpitroraharahaḥ pūjanaṃ kāryamañjasā ||10||

11aśvamedhaiśca yaṣṭavyaṃ bahubhiḥ svāptadakṣiṇaiḥ
|
tena lokānavāpnoti puruṣo'dbhutadarśanān ||11||
