Mahābhārata 6.80 (Bhīṣmaparvan)

1sañjaya uvāca|
atha śūrā mahārāja parasparakṛtāgasaḥ
|
jagmuḥ svaśibirāṇyeva rudhireṇa samukṣitāḥ ||1||

2viśramya ca yathānyāyaṃ pūjayitvā parasparam
|
sannaddhāḥ samadṛśyanta bhūyo yuddhacikīrṣayā ||2||

3tatastava suto rājaṃścintayā'bhipariplutaḥ
|
visravacchoṇitāktāṅgaḥ papracchedaṃ pitāmaham ||3||

4sainyāni raudrāṇi bhayānakāni vyūḍhāni samyagbahuladhvajāni
|
vidārya hatvā ca nipīḍya śūrāste pāṇḍavānāṃ tvaritā mahārathāḥ ||4||

5saṃmohya sarvān yudhi kīrtimanto vyūhaṃ ca taṃ makaraṃ vajrakalpam
|
praviśya bhīmena raṇe hato'smi ghoraiḥ śarairmṛtyudaṇḍaprakāśaiḥ ||5||

6kuddhaṃ tamudvīkṣya bhayena rājan saṃmūrcchito na labhe śāntimadya
|
icche prasādāttava satyasandha prāptuṃ jayaṃ pāṇḍaveyāṃśca hantum ||6||

7tenaivamuktaḥ prahasanmahātmā duryodhanaṃ manyugataṃ viditvā
|
taṃ pratyuvācāvimanā manasvī gaṅgāsutaḥ śastrabhṛtāṃ variṣṭhaḥ ||7||

8pareṇa yatnena vigāhya senāṃ sarvātmanā'haṃ tava rājaputra
|
icchāmi dātuṃ vijayaṃ sukhaṃ ca na cātmānaṃ chādaye'haṃ tvadarthe ||8||

9ete tu raudrā bahavo mahārathā yaśasvinaḥ śūratamāḥ kṛtāstrāḥ
|
ye pāṇḍavānāṃ samare sahāyā jitaklamā roṣaviṣaṃ vamanti ||9||

10te naiva śakyāḥ sahasā vijetuṃ vīryoddhatāḥ kṛtavairāstvayā ca
|
ahaṃ senāṃ pratiyotsyāmi rājan sarvātmanā jīvitaṃ tyajya vīra ||10||

11raṇe tavārthāya mahānubhāva na jīvitaṃ rakṣyatamaṃ mamādya
|
sarvāṃstavārthāya sadevadaityān ghorān daheyaṃ kimu śatrusenām ||11||

12tān pāṇḍavān yodhayiṣyāmi rājan priyaṃ ca te sarvamahaṃ kariṣye
|
śrutvaiva caitadvacanaṃ tadānīṃ duryodhanaḥ prītamanā babhūva ||12||

13sarvāṇi sainyāni tataḥ prahṛṣṭo nirgacchatetyāha nṛpāṃśca sarvān
|
tadājñayā tāni viniryayurdrutaṃ gajāśvapādātarathāyutāni ||13||

14praharṣayuktāni tu tāni rājan mahānti nānāvidhaśastravanti
|
sthitāni nāgāśvapadātimanti virejurājau tava rājan balāni ||14||

15śastrāstravidbhirnaravīrayodhairadhiṣṭhitāḥ sainyagaṇāstvadīyāḥ
|
rathaughapādātagajāśvasaṅghaiḥ prayādbhirājau vidhivatpraṇunnaiḥ ||15||

16samuddhataṃ vai taruṇārkavarṇaṃ rajo babhau cchādayatsūryaraśmīn
|
rejuḥ patākā rathada‌ntisaṃsthā vāteritā bhrāmyamāṇāḥ samantāt ||16||

17nānāraṅgāḥ samare tatra rājan medhairyutā vidyutaḥ khe yathaiva
|
vṛndaiḥ sthitāścāpi susaṃprayuktāścakāśire dantigaṇāḥ samantāt ||17||

18dhanūṃṣi visphārayatāṃ nṛpāṇāṃ babhūva śabdastumulo'tighoraḥ
|
vimathyato devamahāsuraugharyathā'rṇavasyādiyuge tadānīm ||18||

19tadugranāgaṃ bahurūpavarṇaṃ tavātmajānāṃ samudīrṇamevam
|
babhūva sainyaṃ ripusainyahantṛ yugāntameghaughanibhaṃ tadānīm ||19||
