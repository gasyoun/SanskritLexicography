Mahābhārata 7.64 (Droṇaparvan)

1nārada uvāca| nābhāgamambarīṣaṃ ca mṛtaṃ sṛñjaya śuśruma
|
yaḥ sahasraṃ sahasrāṇāṃ rājñāṃ caikastvayodhayat ||1||

2jigīṣamāṇāḥ saṅgrāme samantādvairiṇo'bhyayuḥ
|
astrayuddhavido ghorāḥ sṛjantaścāśivā giraḥ ||2||

3balalāghavaśikṣābhisteṣāṃ so'strabalena ca
|
chatrāyudhadhvajarathāṃśchittvā prāsān gatavyathaḥ ||3||

4ta enaṃ muktasannāhāḥ prārthayan jīvitaiṣiṇaḥ
|
śaraṇyamīyuḥ śaraṇaṃ tavāsma iti vādinaḥ ||4||

5sa tu tān vaśagān kṛtvā jitvā cemāṃ vasundharām
|
īje yajñaśatairiṣṭairyathā śāstraṃ tathā'nagha ||5||

6bubhujuḥ sarvasaṃpannamannamanye janāḥ sadā
|
tasmin yajñe tu viprendrāḥ santṛptāḥ paramārcitāḥ ||6||

7modakān pūrikāpūpān svādupūrṇāśca śaṣkulīḥ
|
karambhān pṛthumṛdvīkā annāni sukṛtāni ca ||7||

8sūpān maireyakāpūpān rāgakhāṇḍavapānakān
|
mṛṣṭānnāni suyuktāni mṛdūni surabhīṇi ca ||8||

9ghṛtaṃ madhu payastoyaṃ dadhīni rasavanti ca
|
phalaṃ mūlaṃ ca susvādu dvijāstatropabhuñjate ||9||

10mādanīyāni pāpāni viditvā cātmanaḥ sukham
|
apibanta yathākāmaṃ pānapā gītavāditaiḥ ||10||

11tatra sma gāthāṃ gāyanti kṣībā hṛṣṭāḥ paṭhanti ca
|
nābhāgastutisaṃyuktā nanṛtuśca sahasraśaḥ ||11||

12teṣu yajñeṣvambarīṣo dakṣiṇāmatyakālayat
|
rājñāṃ śatasahasrāṇi daśa prayutayājinām ||12||

13hiraṇyakavacān sarvān śvetacchatraprakīrṇakān
|
hiraṇyasyandanārūḍhān sānuyātraparicchadān ||13||

14ījāno vitate yajñe dakṣiṇāmatyakālayat
|
mūrdhābhiṣiktāṃśca nṛpān rājaputraśatāni ca ||14||

15sadaṇḍakośanicayān brāhmaṇebhyo hyamanyata
|
naivaṃ pūrve janāścakrurna kariṣyanti cāpare ||15||

16yadambarīṣo nṛpatiḥ karotyamitadakṣiṇaḥ
|
ityevamanumodante prītā yasya maharṣayaḥ ||16||

17sa cenmamāra sṛñjaya caturbhadratarastvayā
|
putrāt puṇyatarastubhyaṃ mā putramanutapyathāḥ
|
ayajvānamadākṣiṇyamabhiśvaityeti vyāharan ||17||
