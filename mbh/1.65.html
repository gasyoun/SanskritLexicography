Mahābhārata 1.65 (Ādiparvan)

1vaiśaṃpāyana uvāca|
atha nārāyaṇenendraścakāra saha saṃvidam
|
avatartuṃ mahīṃ svargādaṃśataḥ mahitaḥ suraiḥ||1||

2ādiśya ca svayaṃ śakraḥ sarvāneva divaukasaḥ
|
nirjagāma punastasmātkṣayānnārāyaṇasya ha||2||

3te'marārivināśāya sarvalokahitāya ca
|
avateruḥ krameṇaiva mahīṃ svargāddivaukasaḥ||3||

4tato brahmarṣivaṃśeṣu pārthivarṣikuleṣu ca
|
jajñire rājaśārdūla yathākāmaṃ divaukasaḥ||4||

5dānavānrākṣasāṃścaiva gandharvānpannagāṃstathā
|
puruṣādāni cānyāni jaghnuḥ satvānyanekaśaḥ||5||

6|
dānavā rākṣasāścaiva gandharvāḥ pannagāstathā
|
na tānbalasthānbālye'pi jaghnurbharatasattama||6||

7janamejaya uvāca|
devadānavasaṅghānāṃ gandharvāpsarasāṃ tathā
|
mānavānāṃ ca sarveṣāṃ tathā vai yakṣarakṣasām||7||

8śrotumicchāmi tattvena saṃbhavaṃ kṛtsnamāditaḥ
|
prāṇināṃ caiva sarveṣāṃ saṃbhavaṃ vaktumarhasi||8||

9vaiśampāyana uvāca|
hanta te kathayiṣyāmi namaskṛtya svayaṃbhuve
|
surādīnāmahaṃ samyaglokānāṃ prabhavāpyayam||9||

10brahmaṇo mānasāḥ putrā viditāḥ ṣaṇmaharṣayaḥ
|
marīciratryaṅgirasau pulastyaḥ pulahaḥ kratuḥ||10||

11marīceḥ kaśyapaḥ putraḥ kaśyapāttu imāḥ prajāḥ
|
prajajñire mahābhāgā dakṣakanyāstrayodaśa||11||

12aditirditirdanuḥ kālā danāyuḥ siṃhikā tathā
|
krodhā prādhā ca viśvā ca vinatā kapilā muniḥ||12||

13kadrūśca manujavyāghra dakṣakanyaiva bhārata
|
etāsāṃ vīryasaṃpannaṃ putrapautramanantakam||13||

14adityāṃ dvādaśādityāḥ saṃbhūtā bhuvaneśvarāḥ
|
ye rājannāmatastāṃste kīrtayiṣyāmi bhārata||14||

15dhātā mitro'ryamā śakro varuṇastvaṃśa eva ca
|
bhago vivasvānpūṣā ca savitā daśamastathā||15||

16ekādaśastathā tvaṣṭā dvādaśo viṣṇurucyate
|
jaghanyajastu sarveṣāmādityānāṃ guṇādhikaḥ||16||

17eka eva diteḥ putro hiraṇyakaśipuḥ smṛtaḥ
|
nāmnā khyātāstu tasyeme pañca putrā mahātmanaḥ||17||

18prahlādaḥ pūrvajasteṣāṃ saṃhlādastadanantaram
|
anuhlādastṛtīyo'bhūttasmācca śibibāṣkalau||18||

19prahlādasya trayaḥ putrāḥ khyātāḥ sarvatra bhārata
|
virocanaśca kumbhaśca nikumbhaśceti bhārata||19||

20virocanasya putro'bhūdbalirekaḥ pratāpavān
|
baleśca prathitaḥ putro bāṇo nāma mahāsuraḥ||20||

21rudrasyānucaraḥ śrīmānmahākāleti yaṃ viduḥ
|
catvāriṃśaddanoḥ putrāḥ khyātāḥ sarvatra bhārata||21||

22teṣāṃ prathamajo rājā vipracittirmahāyaśāḥ
|
śambaro namuciścaiva pulomā ceti viśrutaḥ||22||

23asilomā ca keśī ca durjayaścaiva dānavaḥ
|
ayaḥśirā aśvaśirā aśvaśaṅkuśca vīryavān||23||

24tathā gaganamūrdhā ca vegavānketumāṃśca saḥ
|
svarbhānuraśvo'śvapatirvṛṣaparvājakastathā||24||

25aśvagrīvaśca sūkṣmaśca tuhuṇḍaśca mahābalaḥ
|
iṣupādekacakraśca virūpākṣaharāharau||25||

26nicandraśca nikumbhaśca kupaṭaḥ kapaṭastathā
|
śarabhaḥ śalabhaścaiva sūryācandramasau tathā
|
ete khyātā danorvaṃśe dānavāḥ parikīrtitāḥ||26||

27anyau tu khalu devānāṃ sūryācandramasau smṛtau
|
anyau dānavamukhyānāṃ sūryācandramasau tathā||27||

28ime ca vaṃśāḥ prathitāḥ satvavanto mahābalāḥ
|
danuputrā mahārāja daśa dānavavaṃśajāḥ||28||

29ekākṣo mṛtapo vīraḥ pralambanarakāvapi
|
vātāpī śatrutapanaḥ śaṭhaścaiva mahāsuraḥ||29||

30gaviṣṭhaśca vanāyuśca dīrghajihvaśca dānavaḥ
|
asaṅkhyeyāḥ smṛtāsteṣāṃ putrāḥ pautrāśca bhārata||30||

31siṃhikā suṣuve putraṃ rāhuṃ candrārkamardanam
|
sucandraṃ candrahartāraṃ tathā candrapramardanam||31||

32krūrasvabhāvaṃ krūrāyāḥ putrapautramanantakam
|
gaṇaḥ krodhavaśo nāma krūrakarmā'rimardanaḥ||32||

33danāyuṣaḥ punaḥ putrāścatvāro'surapuṅgavāḥ
|
vikṣaro balavīrau ca vṛtraścaiva mahāsuraḥ||33||

34kālāyāḥ prathitāḥ putrāḥ kālakalpāḥ prahāriṇaḥ
|
pravikhyātā mahāvīryā dānaveṣu parantapāḥ||34||

35vināśanaśca krodhaśca krodhahantā tathaiva ca
|
krodhaśatrustathaivānye kālakeyā iti śrutāḥ||35||

36asurāṇāmupādhyāyaḥ śakrastvaṣisuto'bhavat
|
khyātāścośanasaḥ putrāścatvāro'surayājakāḥ||36||

37tvaṣṭādharastathātriśca dvāvanyau raudrakarmiṇau
|
tejasā sūryasaṃkāśā brahmalokaparāyaṇāḥ||37||

38ityeṣa vaṃśaprabhavaḥ kathitaste tarasvinām
|
asurāṇāṃ surāṇāṃ ca purāṇe saṃśruto mayā||38||

39eteṣāṃ yadapatyaṃ tu na śakyaṃ tadaśeṣataḥ
|
prasaṃkhyātuṃ mahīpāla guṇabhūtamanantakam||39||

40tārkṣyaścāriṣṭanemiśca tathaiva garuḍāruṇau
|
āruṇirvāruṇiścaiva vainateyāḥ prakīrtitāḥ||40||

41śeṣo'nanto vāsukiśca takṣakaśca bhujaṅgamaḥ
|
kūrmaśca kulikaścaiva kādraveyāḥ prakīrtitāḥ||41||

42bhīmasenograsenau ca suparṇo varuṇastathā
|
gopatirdhṛtarāṣṭraśca sūryavarcāśca saptamaḥ||42||

43satyavāgarkaparṇaśca prayutaścāpi viśrutaḥ
|
bhīmaścitrarathaścaiva vikhyātaḥ sarvavidvaśī||43||

44tathā śāliśirā rājanparjanyaśca caturdaśaḥ
|
kaliḥ pañcadaśasteṣāṃ nāradaścaiva ṣoḍaśaḥ||
ityete devagandharvā mauneyāḥ parikīrtitāḥ||44||

45atha prabhūtānyanyāni kīrtayiṣyāmi bhārata
|
anavadyāṃ manuṃ vaṃśāmasurāṃ mārgaṇapriyām||45||

46arūpāṃ subhagāṃ bhāsīmiti prādhā vyajāyata
|
siddhaḥ pūrṇaśca barhiśca pūrṇāyuśca mahāyaśāḥ||46||

47brahmacārī ratiguṇaḥ suparṇaścaiva saptamaḥ
|
viśvāvasuśca bhānuśca sucandro daśamastathā||47||

48ityete devagandharvāḥ prādheyāḥ parikīrtitāḥ
|
imaṃ tvapsarasāṃ vaṃśaṃ viditaṃ puṇyalakṣaṇam||48||

49prādhā'sūta mahābhāgā devī devarṣitaḥ purā
|
alambuṣā miśrakeśī vidyutparṇā tilottamā||49||

50aruṇā rakṣitā caiva rambhā tadvanmanoramā
|
keśinī ca subāhuśca suratā surajā tathā||50||

51supriyā cātibāhuśca vikhyātau ca hāhā hūhūḥ
|
tumburuśceti catvāraḥ smṛtā gandharvasattamāḥ||51||

52amṛtaṃ brāhmaṇā gāvo gandharvāpsarasastathā
|
apatyaṃ kapilāyāstu purāṇe parikīrtitam||52||

53iti te sarvabhūtānāṃ saṃbhavaḥ kathito mayā
|
yathāvatsaṃparikhyāto gandharvāpsarasāṃ tathā||53||

54bhujaṃgānāṃ suparṇānāṃ rudrāṇāṃ marutāṃ tathā
|
gavāṃ ca brāhmaṇānāṃ ca śrīmatāṃ puṇyakarmaṇām||54||

55āyuṣyaścaiva puṇyaśca dhanyaḥ śrutisukhāvahaḥ
|
śrotavyaścaiva satataṃ śrāvyaścaivānasūyatā||55||

56imaṃ tu vaṃśaṃ niyamena yaḥ paṭhet
mahātmanāṃ brāhmaṇadevasannidhau
|
apatyalābhaṃ labhate sa puṣkalaṃ
śriyaṃ yaśaḥ pretya ca śobhanāṃ gatim||56||
