Mahābhārata 12.351 (Śāntiparvan)

1brahmovāca|
śṛṇu putra yathā hyeṣa puruṣaḥ śāśvato'vyayaḥ
|
akṣayaścāprameyaśca sarvagaśca nirucyate||1||

2na sa śakyastvayā draṣṭuṃ mayā'nyairvāpi sattama
|
saguṇairnirguṇairviśvo jñānadṛśyo hyasau smṛtaḥ||2||

3aśarīraḥ śarīreṣu sarveṣu nivasatyasau
|
vasannapi śarīreṣu na sa lipyati karmabhiḥ||3||

4mamāntarātmā tava ca ye cānye dehisaṃjñitāḥ
|
sarveṣāṃ sākṣibhūto'sau na grāhyaḥ kenacitkvacit||4||

5viśvamūrdhā viśvabhujo viśvapādākṣināsikaḥ
|
ekaścarati kṣetreṣu svairacārī yathāsukham||5||

6kṣetrāṇi hi śarīrāṇi bījaṃ cāpi śubhāśubham
|
tāni vetti sa yogātmā tataḥ kṣetrajña ucyate||6||

7nāgatirna gatistasya jñeyā bhūteṣu kenacit
|
sāṃkhyena vidhinā caiva yogena ca yathākramam||7||

8cintayāmi gatiṃ cāsya na gatiṃ vedmi cottarām
|
yathājñānaṃ tu vakṣyāmi puruṣaṃ tu sanātanam||8||

9tasyaikatvaṃ mahattvaṃ ca sa caikaḥ puruṣaḥ smṛtaḥ
|
mahāpuruṣaśabdaṃ sa bibhartyekaḥ sanātanaḥ||9||

10eko hutāśo bahudhā samidhyate ekaḥ sūryastapaso yonirekā
|
eko vāyurbahudhā vāti loke mahodadhiścāṃbhasāṃ yonirekaḥ
|
puruṣaścaiko nirguṇo viśvarūpastaṃ nirguṇaṃ puruṣaṃ cāviśanti||10||

11hitvā guṇamayaṃ sarvaṃ karma hitvā śubhāśubham
|
ubhe satyānṛte tyaktvā evaṃ bhavati nirguṇaḥ||11||

12acintyaṃ cāpi taṃ jñātvā bhāvasūkṣmaṃ catuṣṭayam
|
vicaredyo'samunnaddhaḥ sa gacchetpuruṣaṃ śubham||12||

13evaṃ hi paramātmānaṃ kecidicchanti paṇḍitāḥ
|
ekātmānaṃ tathā''tmānamapare jñānacintakāḥ||13||

14tatra ya: paramātmā hi sa nityaṃ nirguṇaḥ smṛtaḥ
|
sa hi nārāyaṇo jñeyaḥ sarvātmā puruṣo hi saḥ||14||

15na lipyate phalaiścāpi padmapatramivāṃbhasā
|
karmātmā tvaparo yo'sau mokṣabandhaiḥ sa yujyate||15||

16sa saptadaśakenāpi rāśinā yujyate ca saḥ
|
evaṃ bahuvidhaḥ proktaḥ puruṣaste yathākramam||16||

17yattatkṛtsnaṃ lokataṃtrasya dhāma vedyaṃ paraṃ bodhanīyaṃ sa boddhā
|
mantā mantavyaṃ prāśitā prāśanīyaṃ ghrātā ghreyaṃ sparśitā sparśanīyam||17||

18draṣṭā draṣṭavyaṃ śrāvitā śrāvaṇīyaṃ jñātā jñeyaṃ saguṇaṃ nirguṇaṃ ca
|
yadvai proktaṃ tāta samyakpradhānaṃ nityaṃ caitacchāśvataṃ cāvyayaṃ ca||18||

19yadvai sūte dhāturādyaṃ vidhānaṃ tadvai viprāḥ pravadante'niruddham
|
yadvai loke vaidikaṃ karma sādhu āśīryuktaṃ taddhi tasyaiva bhāvyam||19||

20devāḥ sarve munayaḥ sādhu śāntāstaṃ prāgvaṃśe yajñabhāgaṃ bhajante
|
ahaṃ brahmā ādya īśaḥ prajānāṃ tasmājjātastvaṃ ca mattaḥ prasūtaḥ||20||

21matto jagajjaṅgamaṃ sthāvaraṃ ca sarve vedāḥ sarahasyā hi putra||21||

22caturvibhaktaḥ puruṣaḥ sa krīḍati yathecchati
|
evaṃ sa bhagavānsvena jñānena pratibodhitaḥ||22||

23etatte kathitaṃ putra yathāvadanupṛcchataḥ
|
sāṃkhyajñāne tathā yoge yathāvadanuvarṇitam||23||
