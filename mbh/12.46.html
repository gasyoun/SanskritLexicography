Mahābhārata 12.46 (Śāntiparvan)

1yudhiṣṭhira uvāca|
kimidaṃ paramāścaryaṃ dhyāyasyamitavikrama
|
kaccillokatrayasyāsya svasti lokaparāyaṇa ||1||

2caturthaṃ dhyānamārgaṃ tvamālambya puruṣarṣabha
|
apakrānto yato devastena me vismitaṃ manaḥ ||2||

3nigṛhīto hi vāyuste pañcakarmā śarīragaḥ
|
indriyāṇi prasannāni manasi sthāpitāni te ||3||

4vākca sattvaṃ ca govinda buddhau saṃveśitāni te
|
sarve caiva guṇā devāḥ kṣetrajñe te niveśitāḥ ||4||

5neṅganti tava romāṇi sthirā buddhistathā manaḥ
|
kāṣṭhakuḍyaśilābhūto nirīhaścāsi mādhava ||5||

6yathā dīpo nivātastho niriṅgo jvalate punaḥ
|
tathā'si bhagavandeva pāṣāṇa iva niścalaḥ ||6||

7yadi śrotumihārhāmi na rahasyaṃ ca te yadi
|
chindhi me saṃśayaṃ deva prapannāyābhiyācate ||7||

8tvaṃ hi kartā vikartā ca kṣaraṃ caivākṣaraṃ ca hi
|
anādinidhanaścādyastvameva puruṣottama ||8||

9tvatprapannāya bhaktāya śirasā praṇatāya ca
|
dhyānasyāsya yathā tattvaṃ brūhi dharmabhṛtāṃ vara ||9||

10tataḥ sve gocare nyasya manobuddhīndriyāṇi saḥ
|
smitapūrvamuvācedaṃ bhagavānvāsavānujaḥ ||10||

11vāsudeva uvāca |
śaratalpagato bhīṣmaḥ śāmyanniva hutāśanaḥ
|
māṃ dhyāti puruṣavyāghrastato me tadgataṃ manaḥ ||11||

12yasya jyātalanirghoṣaṃ visphūrjitamivāśaneḥ
|
na sehe devarājo'pi tamasmi manasā gataḥ ||12||

13yenābhijitya tarasā samastaṃ rājamaṇḍalam
|
ūḍhāstistrastutāḥ kanyāstamasmi manasā gataḥ ||13||

14trayoviṃśatirātraṃ yo yodhayāmāsa bhārgavam
|
na ca rāmeṇa nistīrṇastamasmi manasā gataḥ ||14||

15ekīkṛtyendriyagrāmaṃ manaḥ saṃyamya medhayā
|
śaraṇaṃ māmupāgacchattato me tadgataṃ manaḥ ||15||

16yaṃ gaṅgā garbhavidhinā dhārayāmāsa pārthiva
|
vasiṣṭhaśikṣitaṃ tāta tamasmi manasā gataḥ ||16||

17divyāstrāṇi mahātejā yo dhārayati buddhimān
|
sāṅgāṃśca caturo vedāṃstamasmi manasā gataḥ ||17||

18rāmasya dayitaṃ śiṣyaṃ jāmadagnyasya pāṇḍava
|
ādhāraṃ sarvavidyānāṃ tamasmi manasā gataḥ ||18||

19sa hi bhūtaṃ bhaviṣyacca bhavacca bharatarṣabha
|
vetti dharmavidāṃ śreṣṭhaṃ tamasmi manasā gataḥ ||19||

20tasminhi puruṣavyāghne karmabhiḥ svairdivaṃ gate
|
bhaviṣyati mahī pārtha naṣṭacandreva śarvarī ||20||

21tadyudhiṣṭhira gāṅgeyaṃ bhīṣmaṃ bhīmaparākramam
|
abhigamyopasaṅgṛhya pṛccha yatte manogatam ||21||

22cāturvidyaṃ cāturhotraṃ cāturāśramyameva ca
|
rājadharmāṃśca nikhilān pṛcchainaṃ pṛthivīpate ||22||

23tasminnastamite bhīṣme kauravāṇāṃ dhurandhare
|
jñānānyastaṃ gamiṣyanti tasmāttvāṃ codayāmyaham ||23||

24tacchrutvā vāsudevasya tathyaṃ vacanamuttamam
|
sāśrukaṇṭhaḥ sa dharmajño janārdanamuvāca ha ||24||

25yadbhavānāha bhīṣmasya prabhāvaṃ prati mādhava
|
tathā tannātra sandeho vidyate mama mādhava ||25||

26mahābhāgyaṃ ca bhīṣmasya prabhāvaśca mahādyute
|
śrutaṃ mayā kathayatāṃ brāhmaṇānāṃ mahātmanām ||26||

27bhavāṃśca kartā lokānāṃ yadbravītyarisūdana
|
tathā tadanabhidhyeyaṃ vākyaṃ yādavanandana ||27||

28yadi tvanugrahavatī buddhiste mayi mādhava
|
tvāmagrataḥ puraskṛtya bhīṣmaṃ yāsyāmahe vayam ||28||

29āvṛtte bhagavatyarke sa hi lokān gamiṣyati
|
tvaddarśanaṃ mahābāho tasmādarhati kauravaḥ ||29||

30tava cādyasya devasya kṣarasyaivākṣarasya ca
|
darśanaṃ tvasya lābhaḥ syāttvaṃ hi brahmamayo nidhiḥ ||30||

31vaiśampāyana uvāca|
śrutvaivaṃ dharmarājasya vacanaṃ madhusūdanaḥ
|
pārśvasthaṃ sātyakiṃ prāha ratho me yujyatāmiti ||31||

32sātyakistvāśu niṣkramya keśavasya samīpataḥ
|
dārukaṃ prāha kṛṣṇasya yujyatāṃ ratha ityuta ||32||

33sa sātyakerāśu vaco niśamya rathottamaṃ kāñcanabhūṣitāṅgam
|
masāragalvarkamayairvibhaṅgairvibhūṣitaṃ hemanibaddhacakram ||33||

34divākarāṃśuprabhamāśugāminaṃ vicitranānāmaṇibhūṣitāntaram
|
navoditaṃ sūryamiva pratāpinaṃ vicitratārkṣyadhvajinaṃ patākinam ||34||

35sugrīvaśaibyapramukhairvarāśvairmanojavaiḥ kāñcanabhūṣitāṅgaiḥ
|
saṃyuktamāvedayadacyutāya kṛtāñjalirdāruko rājasiṃha ||35||
