Mahābhārata 7.78 (Droṇaparvan)

1sañjaya uvāca|
etacchrutvā vacastasya keśavasya mahātmanaḥ
|
subhadrā putraśokārtā vilalāpa suduḥkhitā ||1||

2hā putra mama mandāyāḥ kathametyāsi saṃyugam
|
nidhanaṃ prāptavāṃstāta pitustulyaparākramaḥ ||2||

3kathamindīvaraśyāmaṃ sudaṃṣṭraṃ cārulocanam
|
mukhaṃ te dṛśyate vatsa guṇṭhitaṃ raṇareṇunā ||3||

4nūnaṃ śūraṃ nipatitaṃ tvāṃ paśyantyanivartinam
|
suśirogrīvabāhvaṃsaṃ vyūḍhoraskaṃ natodaram ||4||

5cārūpacitasarvāṃgaṃ svakṣaṃ śastrakṣatācitam
|
bhūtāni tvāṃ nirīkṣante nūnaṃ candramivoditam ||5||

6śayanīyaṃ purā yasya spardhyāstaraṇasaṃvṛtam
|
bhūmāvadya kathaṃ śeṣe vipraviddhaḥ sukhocitaḥ ||6||

7yo'nvāsyata purā vīro varasrībhirmahābhujaḥ
|
kathamanvāsyate so'dya śivābhiḥ patito mṛdhe ||7||

8yo'stūyata purā hṛṣṭaiḥ sūta-māgadha-bandibhiḥ
|
so'dya kravyādgaṇairghorairvinada‌dbhirupāsyate ||8||

9pāṇḍaveṣu ca nātheṣu vṛṣṇivīreṣu vā vibho
|
pañcāleṣu ca vīreṣu hataḥ kenāsyanāthavat ||9||

10atṛptadarśanā putra darśanasya tavānagha
|
mandabhāgyā gamiṣyāmi vyaktamadya yamakṣayam ||10||

11viśālākṣaṃ sukeśāntaṃ cāruvākyaṃ sugandhi ca
|
tava putra kadā bhūyo mukhaṃ drakṣyāmi nirvraṇam ||11||

12dhigbalaṃ bhīmasenasya dhikpārthasya dhanuṣmatām
|
dhigvīryaṃ vṛṣṇivīrāṇāṃ pañcālānāṃ ca dhigbalam ||12||

13dhikkekayāṃstathā cedīn matsyāṃścaivātha sṛñjayān
|
ye tvāṃ raṇagataṃ vīraṃ na śekurabhirakṣitum ||13||

14adya paśyāmi pṛthivīṃ śūnyāmiva hatatviṣam
|
abhimanyumapaśyantī śokavyākulalocanā ||14||

15svasrīyaṃ vāsudevasya putraṃ gāṇḍīvadhanvanaḥ
|
kathaṃ tvā'tirathaṃ vīraṃ drakṣyāmyadya nipātitam ||15||

16ehyehi tṛṣito vatsa stanau pūrṇau pibāśu me
|
aṅkamāruhya mandāyā hyatṛptāyāśca darśane ||16||

17hā vīra dṛṣṭo naṣṭaśca dhanaṃ svapna ivāsi me
|
aho hyanityaṃ mānuṣyaṃ jalabudbudacañcalam ||17||

18imāṃ te taruṇīṃ bhāryāṃ tavādhibhirabhiplutām
|
kathaṃ saṃdhārayiṣyāmi vivatsāmiva dhenukām ||18||

19aho hyakāle prasthānaṃ kṛtavānasi putraka
|
vihāya phalakāle māṃ sugṛddhāṃ tava darśane ||19||

20nūnaṃ gatiḥ kṛtāntasya prājñairapi sudurvidā
|
yatra tvaṃ keśave nāthe saṃgrāme'nāthavaddhataḥ ||20||

21yajvanāṃ dānaśīlānāṃ brāhmaṇānāṃ kṛtātmanām
|
caritabrahmacaryāṇāṃ puṇyatīrthāvagāhinām ||21||

22kṛtajñānāṃ vadānyānāṃ guruśuśrūṣiṇāmapi
|
sahasradakṣiṇānāṃ ca yā gatistāmavāpnuhi ||22||

23yā gatiryudhyamānānāṃ śūrāṇāmanivartinām
|
hatvā'rīnnihatānāṃ ca saṃgrāme tāṃ gatiṃ vraja ||23||

24gosahasrapradātṝṇāṃ kratudānāṃ ca yā gatiḥ
|
naiveśikaṃ cābhimataṃ dadatāṃ yā gatiḥ śubhā ||24||

25brāhmaṇebhyaḥ śaraṇyebhyo nidhiṃ nidadhatāṃ ca yā
|
yā cāpi nyastadaṇḍānāṃ tāṃ gatiṃ vraja putraka ||25||

26brahmacaryeṇa yāṃ yānti munayaḥ saṃśitavratāḥ
|
ekapatnyaśca yāṃ yānti tāṃ gatiṃ vraja putraka ||26||

27rājñāṃ sucaritairyā ca gatirbhavati śāśvatī
|
caturāśramiṇāṃ puṇyaiḥ pāvitānāṃ surakṣitaiḥ ||27||

28dīnānukampināṃ yā ca satataṃ saṃvibhāginām
|
paiśunyācca nivṛttānāṃ tāṃ gatiṃ vraja putraka ||28||

29vratināṃ dharmaśīlānāṃ guruśuśrūṣiṇāmapi
|
amoghātithināṃ yā ca tāṃ gatiṃ vraja putraka ||29||

30kṛcchreṣu yā dhārayatāmātmānaṃ vyasaneṣu ca
|
gatiḥ śokāgnidagdhānāṃ tāṃ gatiṃ vraja putraka ||30||

31mātāpitrośca śuśrūṣāṃ kalpayantīha ye sadā
|
svadāraniratānāṃ ca yā gatistāmavāpnuhi ||31||

32ṛtukāle svakāṃ bhāryāṃ gacchatāṃ yā manīṣiṇām
|
parastrībhyo nivṛttānāṃ tāṃ gatiṃ vraja putraka ||32||

33sāmnā ye sarvabhūtāni paśyanti gatamatsarāḥ
|
nāruṃtudānāṃ kṣamiṇāṃ yā gatistāmavāpnuhi ||33||

34madhumāṃsanivṛttānāṃ madāddaṃbhāttathā'nṛtāt
|
paropatāpatyaktānāṃ tāṃ gatiṃ vraja putraka ||34||

35hrīmantaḥ sarvaśāstrajñā jñānatṛptā jitendriyāḥ
|
yāṃ gatiṃ sādhavo yānti gatiṃ vraja putraka ||35||

36evaṃ vilapatīṃ dīnāṃ subhadrāṃ śokakarśitām
|
anvapadyata pāñcālī vairādisahitāṃ tadā ||36||

37tāḥ prakāmaṃ ruditvā ca vilapya ca suduḥkhitāḥ
|
unmattavattadā rājan visaṃjñā nyapatan kṣitau ||37||

38sopacārastu kṛṣṇaśca duḥkhitāṃ bhṛśaduḥkhitaḥ
|
siktvāṃbhasā samāśvāsya tattaduktvā hitaṃ vacaḥ ||38||

39visaṃjñakalpāṃ rudatīṃ marmaviddhāṃ pravepatīm
|
bhaginīṃ puṇḍarīkākṣa idaṃ vacanamabravīt ||39||

40subhadre mā śucaḥ putraṃ pāñcālyāśvāsayottarām
|
gato'bhimanyuḥ prathitāṃ gatiṃ kṣatriyapuṅgavaḥ ||40||

41ye cānye'pi kule santi puruṣā no varānane
|
sarve te tāṃ gātiṃ yāntu hyabhimanyoryaśasvinaḥ ||41||

42kuryāma tadvayaṃ karma kriyāsu suhṛdaśca naḥ
|
kṛtavān yādṛgadyaikastava putro mahārathaḥ ||42||

43evamāśvāsya bhaginīṃ draupadīmapi cottarām
|
pārthasyaiva mahābāhuḥ pārśvabhāgādarindamaḥ ||43||

44tato'bhyanujñāya nṛpān kṛṣṇo bandhūṃstathārjunam
|
viveśāntaḥpure rājaṃste ca jagmuryathālayam ||44||
