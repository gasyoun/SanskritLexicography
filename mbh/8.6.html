Mahābhārata 8.6 (Karṇaparvan)

1dhṛtarāṣṭra uvāca|
ākhyātā māmakāstāta nihatā yudhi pāṇḍavaiḥ
|
hatāṃśca pāṇḍaveyānāṃ māmakairbrūhi sañjaya ||1||

2sañjaya uvāca|
kuntayo yudhi vikrāntā mahāsattvā mahābalāḥ
|
sānubandhāḥ sahāmātyā gāṅgeyena nipātitāḥ ||2||

3nārāyaṇā balabhadrāḥ śūrāśca śataśo'pare
|
anuraktāśca vīreṇa bhīṣmeṇa yudhi pātitāḥ ||3||

4samaḥ kirīṭinā saṃkhye vīryeṇa ca balena ca
|
satyajitsatyasandhena droṇena nihato yudhi ||4||

5pañcālānāṃ maheṣvāsāḥ sarve yuddhaviśāradāḥ
|
droṇena saha saṅgamya gatā vaivasvatakṣayam ||5||

6tathā virāṭadrupadau vṛddhau sahasutau nṛpau
|
parākramantau mitrārthe droṇena nihatau raṇe ||6||

7yo bāla eva samare saṃmitaḥ savyasācinā
|
keśavena ca durdharṣo baladevena vā vibho ||7||

8pareṣāṃ kadanaṃ kṛtvā mahārathaviśāradaḥ
|
parivārya mahāmātraiḥ ṣaḍbhiḥ paramakai rathaiḥ ||8||

9aśaknuvadbhirbībhatsumabhimanyurnipātitaḥ
|
kṛtaṃ taṃ virathaṃ vīraṃ kṣatradharme vyavasthitam ||9||

10dauḥśāsanirmahārāja saubhadraṃ hatavānraṇe
|
sapatnānāṃ nihantā ca mahatyā senayā vṛtaḥ ||10||

11ambaṣṭhasya sutaḥ śrīmānmitrahetoḥ parākraman
|
āsādya lakṣmaṇaṃ vīraṃ duryodhanasutaṃ raṇe ||11||

12sumahatkadanaṃ kṛtvā gato vaivasvatakṣayam
|
bṛhantaḥ sumaheṣvāsaḥ kṛtāstro yuddhadurmadaḥ ||12||

13duḥśāsanena vikramya gamito yamasādanam
|
maṇimān daṇḍadhāraśca rājānau yuddhadurmadau ||13||

14parākramantau mitrārthe droṇena yudhi pātitau
|
aṃśumān bhojarājastu sahasainyo mahārathaḥ ||14||

15bhāradvājena vikramya gamito yamasādanam
|
sāmudraścitrasenaśca saha putreṇa bhārata ||15||

16samudrasenena valādgamito yamasādanam
|
anūpavāsī nīlaśca vyāghradattaśca vīryavān ||16||

17aśvatthāmnā vikarṇena gamito yamasādanam
|
citrāyudhaścitrayodhī kṛtvā ca kadanaṃ mahat ||17||

18citramārgeṇa vikramya vikarṇena hato mṛdhe
|
vṛkodarasamo yuddhe vṛtaḥ kaikeyayodhibhiḥ ||18||

19kaikeyena ca vikramya bhrātā bhrātrā nipātitaḥ
|
janamejayo gadāyodhī pārvatīyaḥ pratāpavān ||19||

20durmukhena mahārāja tava putreṇa pātitaḥ
|
rocamānau naravyāghrau rocamānau grahāviva ||20||

21droṇena yugapadrājan divaṃ saṃprāpitau śaraiḥ
|
nṛpāśca pratiyudhyantaḥ parākrāntā viśāṃpate ||21||

22kṛtvā nasukaraṃ karma gatā vaivasvatakṣayam
|
purujit kuntibhojaśca mātulau savyasācinaḥ ||22||

23saṃgrāmanirjitā~llokān gamitau droṇasāyakaiḥ
|
abhibhūḥ kāśirājaśca kāśikairbahubhirvṛtaḥ ||23||

24vasudānasya putreṇa nyāsito dehamāhave
|
amitaujā yudhāmanyuruttamaujāśca vīryavān ||24||

25nihatya śataśaḥ śūrānasmadīyairnipātitāḥ
|
mitravarmāca pāñcālyaḥ kṣatradharmā ca bhārata ||25||

26droṇena parameṣvāsau gamitau yamasādanam
|
śikhaṇḍitanayo yuddhe kṣatradevo yudhāṃ patiḥ ||26||

27lakṣmaṇena hato rājaṃstava pautreṇa bhārata
|
sucitraścitravarmā ca pitāputrau mahārathau ||27||

28pracarantau mahāvīrau droṇena nihatau raṇe
|
vārdhakṣemirmahārāja samudra iva parvaṇi ||28||

29āyudhakṣayamāsādya praśāntiṃ paramāṃ gataḥ
|
senābindusutaḥ śreṣṭhaḥ śastravān pravaro yudhi ||29||

30bāhlikena mahārāja kauravendreṇa pātitaḥ
|
dhṛṣṭaketurmahārāja cedīnāṃ pravaro rathaḥ ||30||

31kṛtvā nasukaraṃ karma gato vaivasvatakṣayam
|
tathā satyadhṛtirvīraḥ kṛtvā kadanamāhave ||31||

32pāṇḍavārthe parākrānto gamito yamasādanam
|
senābinduḥ kuruśreṣṭhaḥ kṛtvā kadanamāhave ||32||

33putrastu śiśupālasya suketuḥ pṛthivīpatiḥ
|
nihatya śātravānsaṃkhye droṇena nihato yudhi ||33||

34tathā satyadhṛtirvīro madirāśvaśca vīryavān
|
sūryadattaśca vikrānto nihato droṇasāyakaiḥ ||34||

35śreṇimāṃśca mahārāja yudhyamānaḥ parākramī
|
kṛtvā nasukaraṃ karma gato vaivasvatakṣayam ||35||

36tathaiva yudhi vikrānto māgadhaḥ paramāstravit
|
bhīṣmeṇa nihato rājañśete'dya paravīrahā ||36||

37virāṭaputraḥ śaṅkhastu uttaraśca mahārathaḥ
|
kurvantau sumahatkarma gatau vaivasvatakṣayam ||37||

38vasudānaśca kadanaṃ kurvāṇo'tīva saṃyuge
|
bhāradvājena vikramya gamito yamasādanam ||38||

39ete cānye ca bahavaḥ pāṇḍavānāṃ mahārathāḥ
|
hatā droṇena vikramya yanmāṃ tvaṃ paripṛcchasi ||39||
