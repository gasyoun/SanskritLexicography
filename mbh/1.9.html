Mahābhārata 1.9 (Ādiparvan)

1sautiruvāca|
teṣu tatropaviṣṭeṣu brāhmaṇeṣu mahātmasu
|
ruruścukrośa gahanaṃ vanaṃ gatvā'tiduḥkhitaḥ||1||

2śokenābhihataḥ so'tha vilapankaruṇaṃ bahu
|
abravīdvacanaṃ śocanpriyāṃ smṛtvā pramadvarām||2||

3śete sā bhuvi tanvaṅgī mama śokavivardhinī
|
bāndhavānāṃ ca sarveṣāṃ kiṃ nu duḥkhamataḥparam||3||

4yadi dattaṃ tapastaptaṃ guravo vā mayā yadi
|
samyagārādhitāstena saṃjīvatu mama priyā||4||

5yathā ca janmaprabhṛti yatātmā'haṃ dhṛtavrataḥ
|
pramadvarā tathā hyeṣā samuttiṣṭhatu bhāminī||5||

6evaṃ lālapyatastasya bhāryārthe duḥkhitasya ca
|
devadūtastadā'bhyetya vākyamāha ruruṃ vane||6||

7devadūta uvāca|
abhidhatse ha yadvācā ruro duḥkhena tanmṛṣā
|
yato martyasya dharmātmannāyurasti gatāyuṣaḥ||7||

8gatāyureṣā kṛpaṇā gandharvāpsarasoḥ sutā
|
tasmācchoke manastāta mā kṛthāstvaṃ kathaṃcana||8||

9upāyaścātra vihitaḥ pūrvaṃ devairmahātmabhiḥ
|
taṃ yadīcchasi kartuṃ tvaṃ prāpsyasīha pramadvarām||9||

10rururuvāca|
ka upāyaḥ kṛto devairbūhi tattvena khecara
|
kariṣye'haṃ tathā śrutvā trātumarhati māṃ bhavān||10||

11devedūta uvāca|
āyuṣo'rdhaṃ prayaccha tvaṃ kanyāyai bhṛgunandana
|
evamutthāsyati ruro tava bhāryā prayadvarā||11||

12rururuvāca|
āyuṣo'rdhaṃ prayacchāmi kanyāyai khecarottama
|
śṛṅgārarūpābharaṇā samuttiṣṭhatu me priyā||12||

13sautiruvāca|
tato gandharvarājaśca devadūtaśca sattamau
|
dharmarājamupetyedaṃ vacanaṃ pratyabhāṣatām||13||

14dharmarājāyuṣo'rdhena rurorbhāryā pramadvarā
|
samuttiṣṭhatu kalyāṇī mṛtaivaṃ yadi manyase||14||

15dharmarāja uvāca|
pramadvarāṃ rurorbhāryāṃ devadūta yadīcchasi
|
uttiṣṭhatvāyuṣo'rdhena ruroreva samanvitā||15||

16sautiruvāca|
evamukte tataḥ kanyā sodatiṣṭhatpramadvarā
|
rurostasyāyuṣo'rdhena supteva varavarṇinī||16||

17etaddṛṣṭaṃ bhaviṣye hi ruroruttamatejasaḥ
|
āyuṣo'tipravṛddhasya bhāryārthe'rdhamalupyata||17||

18tata iṣṭe'hani tayoḥ pitarau cakraturmudā
|
vivāhaṃ tau ca remāte parasparahitaiṣiṇau||18||

19sa labdhvā durlabhāṃ bhāryāṃ padmakiñjalkasuprabhām
|
vrataṃ cakre vināśāya jihmagānāṃ dhṛtavrataḥ||19||

20sa dṛṣṭvā jihmagānsarvāṃstīvrakopasamanvitaḥ
|
abhihanti yathā sattvaṃ gṛhya praharaṇaṃ sadā||20||

21sa kadācidvanaṃ vipro rururabhyāgamanmahat
|
śayānaṃ tatra cāpaśyat ḍuṇḍubhaṃ vayasānvitam||21||

22tata udyamya daṇḍaṃ sa kāladaṇḍopamaṃ tadā
|
jighāṃsuḥ kupito viprastamuvācā'tha ḍuṇḍubhaḥ||22||

23nāparādhyāmi te kiṃcidahamadya tapodhana
|
saṃrambhācca kimarthaṃ māmabhihaṃsi ruṣānvitaḥ||23||
