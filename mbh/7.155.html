Mahābhārata 7.155 (Droṇaparvan)

1dhṛtarāṣṭra uvāca|
tasminpraviṣṭe durdharṣe sṛñjayānamitaujasi
|
amṛṣyamāṇe saṃrabdhe kā vo'bhūdvai matistadā||1||

2duryodhanaṃ tathā putramuktvā śāstrātigaṃ mama
|
yatprāviśadameyātmā kiṃ pārthaḥ pratyapadyata||2||

3nihate saindhave vīre bhūriśravasi caiva ha
|
yadābhyagānmahātejāḥ pañcālānaparājitaḥ||3||

4kimamanyata durdharṣe praviṣṭe śatrutāpane
|
duryodhanastu kiṃ kṛtyaṃ prāptakālamamanyata||4||

5ke ca taṃ varadaṃ vīramanvayurdvijasattamam
|
ke cāsya pṛṣṭhato'gacchan vīrāḥ śūrasya yudhyataḥ||5||

6ke purastādvartanta nighnantaḥ śātravān raṇe
|
manye'haṃ pāṇḍavān sarvān bhāradvājaśarārditān||6||

7śiśire kaṃpamānā vai kṛśā gāva iva prabho
|
praviśya sa maheṣvāsaḥ pañcālānarimardanaḥ
|
kathaṃ tu puruṣavyāghraḥ pañcatvamupajagmivān||7||

8sarveṣu yodheṣu ca saṅgateṣu rātrau sameteṣu mahāratheṣu
|
saṃloḍyamāneṣu pṛthagbaleṣu ke vastadānīṃ matimanta āsan||8||

9hatāṃścaiva viṣaktāṃśca parābhūtāṃśca śaṃsasi
|
rathino virathāṃścaiva kṛtānyuddheṣu māmakān||9||

10teṣāṃ saṃloḍyamānānāṃ pāṇḍavairhatacetasām
|
andhe tamasi magnānāmabhavatkā matistadā||10||

11prahṛṣṭāṃścāpyudagrāṃśca saṃtuṣṭāṃścaiva pāṇḍavān
|
śaṃsasīhāprahṛṣṭāṃśca vibhraṣṭāṃścaiva māmakān||11||

12kathameṣāṃ tadā tatra pārthānāmapalāyinām
|
prakāśamabhavadrātrau kathaṃ kuruṣu sañjaya||12||

13sañjaya uvāca|
rātriyuddhe tadā rājanvartamāne sudāruṇe
|
droṇamabhyadravansarve pāṇḍavāḥ saha somakaiḥ||13||

14tato droṇaḥ kekayāṃśca dhṛṣṭadyumnasya cātmajān
|
saṃpraiṣayatpretalokaṃ sarvāniṣubhirāśugaiḥ||14||

15tasya pramukhato rājan ye'vartanta mahārathāḥ
|
tānsarvānpreṣayāmāsa pitṛlokaṃ sa bhārata||15||

16pramathnantaṃ tadā vīrānbhāradvājaṃ mahāratham
|
abhyavartata saṃkruddhaḥ śibī rājā pratāpavān||16||

17tamāpatantaṃ saṃprekṣya pāṇḍavānāṃ mahāratham
|
vivyādha daśabhirbāṇaiḥ sarvapāraśavaiḥ śitaiḥ||17||

18taṃ śibiḥ prativivyādha triṃśatā niśitaiḥ śaraiḥ
|
sārathiṃ cāsya bhallena smayamāno nyapātayat||18||

19tasya droṇo hayānhatvā sārathiṃ ca mahātmanaḥ
|
athāsya saśirastrāṇaṃ śiraḥ kāyādapāharat||19||

20tato'sya sārathiṃ kṣipramanyaṃ duryodhano'diśat
|
sa tena saṃgṛhītāśvaḥ punarabhyadravadripūn||20||

21kaliṅgānāmanīkena kāliṅgasya suto raṇe
|
pūrvaṃ pitṛvadhātkruddho bhīmasenasupādravat||21||

22sa bhīmaṃ pañcabhirviddhvā punarvivyādha saptabhiḥ
|
viśokaṃ tribhirānarcchat dhvajamekena pattriṇā||22||

23kaliṅgānāṃ tu taṃ śūraṃ kruddhaṃ kruddho vṛkodaraḥ
|
rathādrathamabhidrutya muṣṭinā'bhijaghāna ha||23||

24tasya muṣṭihatasyājau pāṇḍavena balīyasā
|
sarvāṇyasthīni sahasā prāpatanvai pṛthak pṛthak||24||

25taṃ karṇo bhrātaraścāsya nāmṛṣyanta paraṃtapa
|
te bhīmasenaṃ nārācairjaghnurāśīviṣopamaiḥ||25||

26tataḥ śatrurathaṃ tyaktā bhīmo dhruvarathaṃ gataḥ
|
dhruvaṃ cāsyantamaniśaṃ muṣṭinā samapothayat||26||

27sa tathā pāṇḍuputreṇa balinā'bhihato'patat
|
taṃ nihatya mahārāja bhīmaseno mahābalaḥ||27||

28jayarātarathaṃ prāpya muhuḥ siṃha ivānadat
|
jayarātamathākṣipya nadansavyena pāṇinā||28||

29talena nāśayāmāsa karṇasyaivāgrataḥ sthitaḥ
|
karṇastu pāṇḍave śaktiṃ kāñcanīṃ samavāsṛjat||29||

30tatastāmeva jagrāha prahasanpāṇḍunandanaḥ
|
karṇāyaiva ca durdharṣaścikṣepājau vṛkodaraḥ||30||

31tāmāpatantīṃ ciccheda śakunistailapāyinā
|
etatkṛtvā mahatkarma raṇe'dbhutaparākramaḥ||31||

32punaḥ svarathamāsthāya dudrāva tava vāhinīm
|
tamāyāntaṃ jighāṃsantaṃ bhīmaṃ kruddhamivāntakam||32||

33nyavārayanmahābāhuṃ tava putrā viśāṃpate
|
mahatā śaravarṣeṇa cchādayanto mahārathāḥ||33||

34durmadastu tato bhīmaḥ prahasanniva saṃyuge
|
sārathiṃ ca hyayāṃścaiva śarairninye yamakṣayam||34||

35durmadastu tato yānaṃ duṣkarṇasyāvacakrame
|
tāvekarathamārūḍhau bhrātarau paratāpanau||35||

36saṃgrāmaśiraso madhye bhīmaṃ dvāvapyadhāvatām
|
yathāmbupatimitrau hi tārakaṃ daityasattamam||36||

37tatastu durmadaścaiva duṣkarṇaśca tavātmajau
|
rathamekaṃ samāruhya bhīmaṃ bāṇairavidhyatām||37||

38tataḥ karṇasya miṣato drauṇerduryodhanasya ca
|
kṛpasya somadattasya bālhīkasya ca pāṇḍavaḥ||38||

39durmadasya ca vīrasya duṣkarṇasya ca taṃ ratham
|
pādaprahāreṇa dharāṃ prāveśayadarindimaḥ||39||

40tataḥ sutau te balinau śūrau duṣkarṇadurmadau
|
muṣṭinā''hatya saṃkruddho mamarda ca nanarda ca||40||

41tato hāhākṛte sainye dṛṣṭvā bhīmaṃ nṛpā'bruvan
|
rudro'yaṃ bhīmarūpeṇa dhārtarāṣṭreṣu yudhyati||41||

42evamuktvā palāyante sarve bhārata pārthivāḥ
|
visaṃjñā vāhayanvāhānna ca dvau saha dhāvataḥ||42||

43tato bale bhṛśalulite niśāmukhe supūjito nṛpavṛṣabhairvṛkodaraḥ
|
mahābalaḥ kamalavibuddhalocano yudhiṣṭhiraṃ nṛpatimapūjayadbalī||43||

44tato yamau drupada-virāṭa-kekayā yudhiṣṭhiraścāpi parāṃ mudaṃ yayuḥ
|
vṛkodaraṃ bhṛśamanupūjayaṃśca te yathā'ndhake pratinihate haraṃ surāḥ||44||

45tataḥ sutāste varuṇātmajopamā ruṣānvitāḥ saha guruṇā mahātmanā
|
vṛkodaraṃ sarathapadātikuñjarā yuyutsavo bhṛśamabhiparyavārayan||45||

46tato'bhavattimiraghanairivāvṛte mahābhaye bhayadamatīva dāruṇam
|
niśāmukhe vṛkabalagṛdhramodanaṃ mahātmanāṃ nṛpavarayuddhamadbhutam||46||
