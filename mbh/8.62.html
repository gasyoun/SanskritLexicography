Mahābhārata 8.62 (Karṇaparvan)

1sañjaya uvāca|
tataḥ śvetāśvasaṃyukte nārāyaṇasamāhite
|
tiṣṭhanrathavare śrīmānarjunaḥ samapadyata ||1||

2tadbalaṃ nṛpatiśreṣṭha tāvakaṃ vijayo raṇe
|
vyakṣobhayadudīrṇāśvaṃ mahodadhimivānilaḥ ||2||

3duryodhanastava sutaḥ pramatte śvetavāhane
|
abhyetya sahasā kruddhaḥ sainyārdhenābhisaṃvṛtaḥ ||3||

4paryavārayadāyāntaṃ yudhiṣṭhiramamarṣaṇam
|
kṣuraprāṇāṃ trisaptatyā tato'vidhyata pāṇḍavam ||4||

5akrudhyata bhṛśaṃ tatra kuntīputro yudhiṣṭhiraḥ
|
sa bhallāṃstriśatastūrṇaṃ tava putre nyaveśayat ||5||

6tato'dhāvanta kauravyā jighṛkṣanto yudhiṣṭhiram
|
duṣṭabhāvānparāñjñātvā samavetā mahārathāḥ ||6||

7ājagmustaṃ parīpsantaḥ kuntīputraṃ yudhiṣṭhiram
|
nakulaḥ sahadevaśca dhṛṣṭadyumnaśca pārṣataḥ ||7||

8akṣauhiṇyā parivṛtāste'bhyadhāvan yudhiṣṭhiram
|
bhīmasenaśca samare mṛdnaṃstava mahārathān ||8||

9abhyadhāvadabhiprepsū rājānaṃ śatrubhirvṛtam
|
tāṃstu sarvānmaheṣvāsānkarṇo vaikartano nṛpa ||9||

10śaravarṣeṇa mahatā pratyavārayadāgatān
|
śaraughānvisṛjantaste prerayantaśca tomarān ||10||

11na śekuryatnavanto'pi rādheyaṃ prativīkṣitum
|
tāṃśca sarvānmaheṣvāsānsarvaśastrāstrapāragaḥ ||11||

12mahatā śaravarṣeṇa rādheyaḥ pratyavārayat
|
duryodhanaṃ ca viṃśatyā śīghramastramudīrayan ||12||

13avidhyattūrṇamabhyetya sahadevaḥ pratāpavān
|
sa viddhaḥ sahadevena rarājācalasannibhaḥ ||13||

14prabhinna iva mātaṅgo rudhireṇa pariplutaḥ
|
dṛṣṭvā tava sutaṃ tatra gāḍhaviddhaṃ sutejanaiḥ ||14||

15abhyadhāvaddṛḍhaṃ kruddho rādheyo rathināṃ varaḥ
|
duryodhanaṃ tathā dṛṣṭvā śīghramastramudairayat ||15||

16tena yaudhiṣṭhiraṃ sainyamavadhītpārṣataṃ tathā
|
tato yaudhiṣṭhiraṃ sainyaṃ vadhyamānaṃ mahātmanā ||16||

17sahasā prādravadrājan sutaputraśarārditam
|
vividhā viśikhāstatra saṃpatantaḥ parasparam ||17||

18phalaiḥ puṃkhānsamājagmuḥ sūtaputradhanuścyutāḥ
|
antarikṣe śaraughāṇāṃ patatāṃ ca parasparam ||18||

19saṃgharṣeṇa mahārāja pāvakaḥ samajāyata
|
tato daśa diśaḥ karṇaḥ śalabhairiva yāyibhiḥ ||19||

20abhyaghnaṃstarasā rājañśaraiḥ paraśarīragaiḥ
|
raktacandanasandigdhau maṇihemavibhūṣitau ||20||

21bāhū vyatyakṣipatkarṇaḥ paramāstraṃ vidarśayan
|
tataḥ sarvā diśo rājansāyakairvipramohayan ||21||

22apīḍayadbhṛśaṃ karṇo dharmarājaṃ yudhiṣṭhiram
|
tataḥ kruddho mahārāja dharmaputro yudhiṣṭhiraḥ ||22||

23niśitairiṣubhiḥ karṇaṃ pañcāśadbhiḥ samārpayat
|
bāṇāndhakāramabhavattadyuddhaṃ ghoradarśanam ||23||

24hāhākāro mahānāsīttāvakānāṃ viśāṃpate
|
vadhyamāne tadā sainye dharmaputreṇa māriṣa ||24||

25sāyakairvividhaistīkṣṇaiḥ kaṅkapatraiḥ śilāśitaiḥ
|
bhallairanekairvividhaiḥ śaktyṛṣṭimusalairapi ||25||

26yatra yatra sa dharmātmā duṣṭāṃ dṛṣṭiṃ vyasarjayat
|
tatra tatra vyaśīryanta tāvakā bharatarṣabha ||26||

27karṇo'pi bhṛśasaṃkruddho dharmarājaṃ yudhiṣṭhiram
|
nārācairardhacandraiśca vatsadantaiśca saṃyuge ||27||

28amarṣī krodhanaścaiva roṣaprasphuritānanaḥ
|
sāyakairaprameyātmā yudhiṣṭhiramabhidravat ||28||

29yudhiṣṭhiraścāpi sa taṃ svarṇapuṅkhaiḥ śitaiḥ śaraiḥ
|
prahasanniva taṃ karṇaḥ kaṅkapatraiḥ śilāśitaiḥ ||29||

30urasyavidhyadrājānaṃ tribhirbhallaiśca pāṇḍavam
|
sa pīḍito bhṛśaṃ tena dharmarājo yudhiṣṭhiraḥ ||30||

31upaviśya rathopasthe sūtaṃ yāhītyacodayat
|
akrośanta tataḥ sarve dhārtarāṣṭrāḥ sarājakāḥ ||31||

32gṛhṇīdhvamiti rājānamabhyadhāvanta sarvaśaḥ
|
tataḥ śatāḥ saptadaśa kekayānāṃ prahāriṇām ||32||

33pañcālaiḥ sahitā rājan dhārtarāṣṭrānnyavārayan
|
tasmin sutumule yuddhe vartamāne janakṣaye ||33||

34duryodhanaśca bhīmaśca sameyātāṃ mahābalau ||34||
