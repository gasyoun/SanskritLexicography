Mahābhārata 12.197 (Śāntiparvan)

1yudhiṣṭhira uvāca|
gatīnāmuttamā prāptiḥ kathitā jāpakeṣviha
|
ekaivaiṣā gatisteṣāmuta yāntyaparāmapi ||1||

2bhīṣma uvāca|
śṛṇuṣvāvahito rājan jāpakānāṃ gatiṃ vibho
|
yathā gacchanti nirayānanekānpuruṣarṣabha ||2||

3yathoktapūrvaṃ pūrvaṃ yo nānutiṣṭhati jāpakaḥ
|
ekadeśakriyaścātra nirayaṃ sa ca gacchati ||3||

4avamānena kurute na prīyati na hṛṣyati
|
īdṛśo jāpako yāti nirayaṃ nātra saṃśayaḥ ||4||

5ahaṅkārakṛtaścaiva sarve nirayagāminaḥ
|
parāvamānī puruṣo bhavitā nirayopagaḥ ||5||

6abhidhyāpūrvakaṃ japyaṃ kurute yaśca mohitaḥ
|
yatrāsya rāgaḥ patati tatra tatropapadyate||6||

7athaiśvaryapravṛtteṣu jāpakastatra rajyate
|
sa eva nirayastasya nāsau tasmātpramucyate ||7||

8rāgeṇa jāpako japyaṃ kurute tatra mohitaḥ
|
yatrāsya rāgaḥ patati tatra tatropapadyate ||8||

9durbuddhirakṛtaprajñaścale manasi tiṣṭhati
|
calāmeva gatiṃ yāti nirayaṃ vā niyacchati ||9||

10akṛtaprajñako bālo mohaṃ gacchati jāpakaḥ
|
sa mohānnirayaṃ yāti tatra gatvā'nuśocati ||10||

11dṛḍhagrāhī karomīti jāpyaṃ japati jāpakaḥ
|
na sampūrṇo na saṃyukto nirayaṃ so'nugacchati ||11||

12yudhiṣṭhira uvāca|
anivṛttaṃ paraṃ yattadavyaktaṃ brahmaṇi sthitam
|
tadbhūto jāpakaḥ kasmātsa śarīramihāviśet ||12||

13bhīṣma uvāca|
duṣprajñānena nirayā bahavaḥ samudāhṛtāḥ
|
praśastaṃ jāpakatvaṃ ca doṣāścaite tadātmakāḥ ||13||
