Mahābhārata 12.271 (Śāntiparvan)

1yudhiṣṭhira uvāca|
dharmamarthaṃ ca kāmaṃ ca vedāḥ śaṃsanti bhārata
|
kasya lābho viśiṣṭo'tra tanme brūhi pitāmaha ||1||

2bhīṣma uvāca|
atra te vartayiṣyāmi itihāsaṃ purātanam
|
kuṇḍadhāreṇa yatprītyā bhaktāyopakṛtaṃ purā ||2||

3adhano brāhmaṇaḥ kaścitkāmāddharmamavaikṣata
|
yajñārthaṃ satato'rthārthī tapo'tapyata dāruṇam ||3||

4sa niścayamatho kṛtvā pūjayāmāsa devatāḥ
|
bhaktyā na caivādhyagacchaddhanaṃ sampūjya devatāḥ ||4||

5tataścintāmanuprāptaḥ katamaddaivataṃ tu tat
|
yanme drutaṃ prasīdeta mānuṣairajaḍīkṛtam ||5||

6so'tha saumyena manasā devānucaramantike
|
pratyapaśyajjaladharaṃ kuṇḍadhāramavasthitam ||6||

7dṛṣṭvaiva taṃ mahābāhuṃ tasya bhaktirajāyata
|
ayaṃ me dhāsyati śreyo vapuretaddhi tādṛśam ||7||

8saṃnikṛṣṭaśca devasya na cānyairmānuṣairvṛtaḥ
|
eṣa me dāsyati dhanaṃ prabhūtaṃ śīghrameva ca ||8||

9tato dhūpaiśca gandhaiśca mālyairuccāvacairapi
|
balibhirvividhābhiśca pūjayāmāsa taṃ dvijaḥ ||9||

10tatastvalpena kālena tuṣṭo jaladharastadā
|
tasyopakāraniyatāmimāṃ vācamuvāca ha ||10||

11brahmaghne ca surāpe ca caure bhagnavrate tathā
|
niṣkṛtirvihitā sadbhiḥ kṛtaghne nāsti niṣkṛtiḥ ||11||

12āśāyāstanayo'dharmaḥ krodho'sūyāsutaḥ smṛtaḥ
|
lobhaḥ putro nikṛtyāstu kṛtaghno nārhati prajām ||12||

13tataḥ sa brāhmaṇaḥ svapne kuṇḍadhārasya tejasā
|
apaśyatsarvabhūtāni kuśeṣu śayitastadā ||13||

14śamena tapasā caiva bhaktyā ca nirupaskṛtaḥ
|
śuddhātmā brāhmaṇo rātrau nidarśanamapaśyata ||14||

15maṇibhadraṃ sa tatrasthaṃ devatānāṃ mahādyutim
|
apaśyata mahātmānaṃ vyādiśantaṃ yudhiṣṭhira ||15||

16tatra devāḥ prayacchanti rājyāni ca dhanāni ca
|
śubhaiḥ karmabhirārabdhāḥ pracchindantyaśubheṣu ca ||16||

17paśyatāmatha yakṣāṇāṃ kuṇḍadhāro mahādyutiḥ
|
nipatya patito bhūmau devānāṃ bharatarṣabha ||17||

18tatasya devavacanānmaṇibhadro mahāmanāḥ
|
uvāca patitaṃ bhūmau kuṇḍadhāra kimiṣyate ||18||

19kuṇḍadhāra uvāca |
yadi prasannā devā me bhakto'yaṃ brāhmaṇo mama
|
asyānugrahamicchāmi kṛtaṃ kiṃcitsukhodayam ||19||

20tatastaṃ maṇibhadrastu punarvacanamabravīt
|
devānāmeva vacanātkuṇḍadhāraṃ mahādyutim ||20||

21maṇibhadra uvāca |
uttiṣṭhottiṣṭha bhadraṃ te kṛtakṛtyaḥ sukhī bhava
|
dhanārthī yadi vipro'yaṃ dhanamasmai pradīyatām ||21||

22yāvaddhanaṃ prārthayate brāhmaṇo'yaṃ sakhā tava
|
devānāṃ śāsanāttāvadasaṃkhyeyaṃ dadāmyaham ||22||

23vicārya kuṇḍadhārastu mānuṣyaṃ calamadhruvam
|
tapase matimādhatta brāhmaṇasya yudhiṣṭhira ||23||

24kuṇḍadhāra uvāca |
nāhaṃ dhanāni yācāmi brāhmaṇāya dhanapradā
|
anyamevāhamicchāmi bhaktāyānugrahaṃ kṛtam ||24||

25pṛthivīṃ ratnapūrṇāṃ vā mahadvā ratnasaṃcayama
|
bhaktāya nāhamicchāmi bhavedeṣa tu dhārmikaḥ ||25||

26dharme'sya ramatāṃ buddhirdharmaṃ caivopajīvatu
|
dharmapradhāno bhavatu mamaiṣo'nugraho mataḥ ||26||

27maṇibhadra uvāca |
sadā dharmaphalaṃ rājyaṃ sukhāni vividhāni ca
|
phalānyevāyamaśnātu kāyakleśavivarjitaḥ ||27||

28bhīṣma uvāca|
tatastadeva bahuśaḥ kuṇḍadhāro mahāyaśāḥ
|
abhyāsamakaroddharme tatastuṣṭāstu devatāḥ ||28||

29maṇibhadra uvāca |
prītāste devatāḥ sarvā dvijasyāsya tathaiva ca
|
bhaviṣyatyeṣa dharmātmā dharme cādhāsyate matiḥ ||29||

30tataḥ prīto jaladharaḥ kṛtakārṇe yudhiṣṭhira
|
īpsitaṃ manaso labdhvā varamanyaiḥ sudurlabham ||30||

31tato'payata cīrāṇi sūkṣmāṇi dvijasattamaḥ
|
pārśvato'bhyāśato nyastānyatha nirvedamāgataḥ ||31||

32brāhmaṇa uvāca|
ayaṃ na sukṛtaṃ vetti ko nvanyo vetsyate kṛtam
|
gacchāmi vanamevāhaṃ varaṃ dharmeṇa jīvitum ||32||

33bhīṣma uvāca|
nirvedāddevatānāṃ ca prasādātsa dvijottamaḥ
|
vanaṃ praviśya sumahattapa ārabdhavāṃstadā ||33||

34devatātithiśeṣeṇa phalamūlāśano dvijaḥ
|
dharme cāsya mahārāja dṛḍhā buddhirajāyata ||34||

35tyaktvā mūlaphalaṃ sarvaṃ parṇāhāro'bhavaddvijaḥ
|
parṇaṃ tyaktvā jalāhāraḥ punarāsīddvijastadā ||35||

36vāyubhakṣastataḥ paścādbahūnvarṣagaṇānabhūt
|
na cāsya kṣīyate prāṇastadadbhutamivābhavat ||36||

37dharme ca śraddadhānasya tapasyugre ca vartataḥ
|
kālena mahatā tasya divyā dṛṣṭirajāyata ||37||

38tasya buddhiḥ prādurāsīdyadi dadyāmahaṃ dhanam
|
tuṣṭaḥ kasyacideveha mithyāvāṅ na bhavenmama ||38||

39tataḥ prahṛṣṭavadano bhūya ārabdhavāṃstapaḥ
|
bhūyaścacintayatsiddho yatparaṃ so'bhimanyate ||39||

40yadi dadyāmahaṃ rājyaṃ tuṣṭo vai yasya kasyacit
|
sa bhavedacirādrājā na mithyā vāgbhavenmama ||40||

41tasya sākṣātkuṇḍadhāro darśayāmāsa bhārata
|
brāhmaṇasya tapoyogātsauhṛdenābhicoditaḥ ||41||

42samāgamya sa tenātha pūjāṃ cakre yathāvidhi
|
brāhmaṇaḥ kuṇḍadhārasya vismitaścābhavannṛpa ||42||

43tato'bravītkuṇḍadhāro divyaṃ te cakṣuruttamam
|
paśya rājñāṃ gatiṃ vipra lokāṃścaiva tu cakṣuṣā ||43||

44tato rājasahasrāṇi magnāni niraye tadā
|
dūrādapaśyadvipraḥ sa divyayuktena cakṣuṣā ||44||

45kuṇḍadhāra uvāca |
māṃ pūjayitvā bhāvena yadi tvaṃ duḥkhamāpnuyāḥ
|
kṛtaṃ mayā bhavetkiṃ te kaśca te'nugraho bhevat ||45||

46paśya paśya ca bhūyastvaṃ kāmānicchetkathaṃ naraḥ
|
svargadvāraṃ hi saṃruddhaṃ mānuṣeṣu viśeṣataḥ ||46||

47bhīṣma uvāca|
tato'paśyatsa kāmaṃ ca krodhaṃ lobhaṃ bhayaṃ madam
|
nidrāṃ tandrīṃ tathā''lasyamāvṛtya puruṣān sthitān ||47||

48kuṇḍadhāra uvāca |
etairlokāḥ saṃsuruddhā devānāṃ mānuṣādbhayam
|
tathaiva devavacanādvighnaṃ kurvanti sarvaśaḥ ||48||

49na devairananujñātaḥ kaścidbhavati dhārmikaḥ
|
eṣa śakto'si tapasā dātuṃ rājyaṃ dhanāni ca ||49||

50bhīṣma uvāca|
tataḥ papāta śirasā brāhmaṇastoyadhāriṇe
|
uvāca cainaṃ dharmātmā mahānme'nugrahaḥ kṛtaḥ ||50||

51kāmalobhānubandhena purā te yadasūyitam
|
mayā snehamavijñāya tatra me kṣantumarhasi ||51||

52kṣāntameva mayetyuktvā kuṇḍadhāro dvijarṣabham
|
sampariṣvajya bāhubhyāṃ tatraivāntaradhīyata ||52||

53tataḥ sarvāṃstadā lokān brāhmaṇo'nucacāra ha
|
kuṇḍadhāraprasādena tapasā siddhimāgataḥ ||53||

54vihāya sā ca gamanaṃ tathā saṃkalpitārthatā
|
dharmācchaktyā tathā yogādyā caiva paramā gatiḥ ||54||

55devatāḥ brāhmaṇāḥ santo yakṣā mānuṣacāraṇāḥ
|
dhārmikānpūjayantīha na dhanāḍhyānna kāminaḥ ||55||

56suprasannā hi te devā yatte dharme ratā matiḥ
|
dhane sukhakalā kāciddharme tu paramaṃ sukham ||56||
