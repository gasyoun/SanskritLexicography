Mahābhārata 13.11 (Anuśāsanaparvan)

1yudhiṣṭhira uvāca|
kīdṛśe puruṣe tāta strīṣu vā bharatarṣabha
|
śrīḥ padmā vasate nityaṃ tanme brūhi pitāmaha ||1||

2bhīṣma uvāca|
atra te varṇayiṣyāmi yathāvṛttaṃ yathāśrutam
|
rukmiṇī devakīputrasannidhau paryapṛcchata ||2||

3nārāyaṇasyāṅkagatāṃ jvalantīṃ dṛṣṭvā śriyaṃ padmasamānavarṇām
|
kautūhalādvismitacārunetrā papraccha mātā makaradhvajasya ||3||

4kānīha bhūtānyupasevase tvaṃ santiṣṭhase kāniva sevase tvam
|
tāni trilokeśvarabhūtakānte tattvena me brūhi maharṣikalpe ||4||

5evaṃ tadā śrīrabhibhāṣyamāṇā devyā samakṣaṃ garuḍadhvajasya
|
uvāca vākyaṃ madhurābhidhānaṃ manoharaṃ candramukhī prasannā ||5||

6śrīruvāca|
vasāmi nityaṃ subhage pragalbhe dakṣe nare karmaṇi vartamāne
|
akrodhane devapare kṛtajñe jitendriye nityamudīrṇasattve ||6||

7nākarmaśīle puruṣe vasāmi na nāstike sāṅkarike kṛtaghne
|
na bhinnavṛtte na nṛśaṃsavarṇe na cāpi caure na guruṣvasūye ||7||

8ye cālpatejobalasattvamānāḥ kliśyanti kupyanti ca yatra tatra
|
na caiva tiṣṭhāmi tathāvidheṣu nareṣu saṃguptamanoratheṣu ||8||

9yaścātmani prārthayate na kiñcidyaśca svabhāvopahatāntarātmā
|
teṣvalpasantoṣapareṣu nityaṃ nareṣu nāhaṃ nivasāmi samyak ||9||

10svadharmaśīleṣu ca dharmavitsu vṛddhopasevānirate ca dānte
|
kṛtātmani kṣāntipare samarthe kṣāntāsu dāntāsu tathā'balāsu ||10||

11satyasvabhāvārjavasaṃyutāsu vasāmi devadvijapūjikāsu
|
prakīrṇabhāṇḍāmanapekṣyakāriṇīṃ sadā ca bhartuḥ pratikūlavādinīm ||11||

12parasya veśmābhiratāmalajjāmevaṃvidhāṃ tāṃ parivarjayāmi
|
pāpāmacokṣāmavalehinīṃ ca vyapetadhairyāṃ kalahapriyāṃ ca ||12||

13nidrābhibhūtāṃ satataṃ śayānāṃ evaṃvidhāṃ tāṃ parivarjayāmi
|
satyāsu nityaṃ priyadarśanāsu saubhāgyayuktāsu guṇānvitāsu ||13||

14vasāmi nārīṣu pativratāsu kalyāṇaśīlāsu vibhūṣitāsu
|
yāneṣu kanyāsu vibhūṣaṇeṣu yajñeṣu megheṣu ca vṛṣṭimatsu ||14||

15vasāmi phullāsu ca padminīṣu nakṣatravīthīṣu ca śāradīṣu
|
gajeṣu goṣṭheṣu tathā''saneṣu saraḥsu phullotpalapaṅkajeṣu ||15||

16nadīṣu haṃsasvananāditāsu krauñcāvaghuṣṭasvaraśobhitāsu
|
vikīrṇakūladrumarājitāsu tapasvisiddhadvijasevitāsu ||16||

17vasāmi nityaṃ subahūda‌kālu siṃhairgajaiścākulitodakāsu
|
matte gaje govṛṣabhe narendre siṃhāsane satpuruṣeṣu nityam ||17||

18yasmin jano havyabhujaṃ juhoti gobrāhmaṇaṃ cārcati devatāśca
|
kāle ca puṣpairbalayaḥ kriyante tasmin gṛhe nityamupaimi vāsam ||18||

19svādhyāyanityeṣu sadā dvijeṣu kṣatre ca dharmābhirate sadaiva
|
vaiśye ca kṛṣyābhirate vasāmi śūdre ca śuśrūṣaṇanityayukte ||19||

20nārāyaṇe tvekamanā vasāmi sarveṇa bhāvena śarīrabhūtā
|
tasmin hi dharmaḥ sumahānniviṣṭo brahmaṇyatā cātra tathā priyatvam ||20||

21nāhaṃ śarīreṇa vasāmi devi naivaṃ mayā śakyamihābhidhātum
|
bhāvena yasminnivasāmi puṃsi sa vardhate dharmayaśorthakāmaiḥ ||21||
