Mahābhārata 7.184 (Droṇaparvan)

1sañjaya uvāca|
vyāsenaivamathoktastu dharmarājo yudhiṣṭhiraḥ
|
svayaṃ karṇavadhādvīro nivṛtto bharatarṣabha||1||

2ghaṭotkace tu nihate sūtaputreṇa tāṃ niśām
|
duḥkhāmarṣavaśaṃ prāpto dharmarājo yudhiṣṭhiraḥ||2||

3dṛṣṭvā bhīmena mahatīṃ vāryamāṇāṃ camūṃ tava
|
dhṛṣṭadyumnamuvācedaṃ kuṃbhayoniṃ nivāraya||3||

4tvaṃ hi droṇavināśāya samutpanno hutāśanāt
|
saśaraḥ kavacī khaḍgī dhanvī ca paratāpanaḥ||4||

5abhidrava raṇe hṛṣṭo mā ca te bhīḥ kathaṃcana
|
janamejayaḥ śikhaṇḍī ca daurmukhiśca yaśodharaḥ||5||

6abhidravantu saṃhṛṣṭāḥ kuṃbhayoniṃ samaṃtataḥ
|
nakulaḥ sahadevaśca draupadeyāḥ prabhadrakāḥ||6||

7drupadaśca virāṭaśca putrabhrātṛsamanvitau
|
sātyakiḥ kekayāścaiva pāṇḍavaśca dhanañjayaḥ||7||

8abhidravantu vegena kuṃbhayonivadhepsayā
|
tathaiva rathinaḥ sarve hastyaśvaṃ yacca kiñcana||8||

9padātāśca raṇe droṇaṃ pātayantu mahāratham
|
tathā''jñaptāstu te sarve pāṇḍavena mahātmanā||9||

10abhyadravanta vegena kuṃbhayonivadhepsayā
|
āgacchatastānsahasā sarvodyogena pāṃḍavān||10||

11pratijagrāha samare droṇaḥ śastrabhṛtāṃ varaḥ
|
tato duryodhano rājā sarvodyogena pāṇḍavān||11||

12abhyadravatsusaṃkruddha icchandroṇasya jīvitam
|
tataḥ pravavṛte yuddhaṃ śrāntavāhanasainikam||12||

13pāṇḍavānāṃ kurūṇāṃ ca garjatāmitaretaram
|
nidrāndhāste mahārāja pariśrāntāśca saṃyuge||13||

14nābhyapadyanta samare kāñcicceṣṭāṃ mahārathāḥ
|
triyāmā rajanī caiṣā ghorarūpā bhayānakā||14||

15sahastrayāmapratimā babhūva prāṇahāriṇī
|
vadhyatāṃ ca tathā teṣāṃ kṣatānāṃ ca viśeṣataḥ||15||

16ardharātriḥ samājajñe nidrāndhānāṃ viśeṣataḥ
|
sarve hyāsannirutsāhāḥ kṣatriyā dīnacetasaḥ||16||

17tava caiva pareṣāṃ ca gatāstrā vigateṣavaḥ
|
te tadā pārayantaśca hrīmantaśca viśeṣataḥ||17||

18svadharmamanupaśyanto na jahuḥ svāmanīkinīm
|
astrāṇyanye samutsṛjya nidrāṃdhāḥ śerate janāḥ||18||

19ratheṣvanye gajeṣvanye hayeṣvanye ca bhārata
|
nidrāndhā no bubudhire kāñcicceṣṭāṃ narādhipa||19||

20tānanye samare yodhāḥ preṣayanto yamakṣayam
|
svapnāyamānāṃstvapare parānativicetasaḥ||20||

21ātmānaṃ samare jaghnuḥ svāneva ca parānapi
|
nānāvāco vimuñcanto nidrāṃdhāste mahāraṇe||21||

22asmākaṃ ca mahārāja parebhyo bahavo janāḥ
|
yoddhavyamiti tiṣṭhanto nidrāsaṃraktalocanāḥ||22||

23saṃsarpanto raṇe kecinnidrāndhāste tathā parān
|
jaghnuḥ śūrā raṇe śūrāṃstasmiṃstamasi dāruṇe||23||

24hanyamānamathātmānaṃ parebhyo bahavo janāḥ
|
nābhyajānanta samare nidrayā mohitā bhṛśam||24||

25teṣāmetādṛśīṃ ceṣṭāṃ vijñāya puruṣarṣabhaḥ
|
uvāca vākyaṃ bībhatsuruccaiḥ sannādayan diśaḥ||25||

26śrāntā bhavanto nidrāṃdhāḥ sarva eva savāhanāḥ
|
tamasā ca vṛte sainye rajasā bahulena ca||26||

27te yūyaṃ yadi manyadhvamupāramata sainikāḥ
|
nimīlayata cātraiva raṇabhūmau muhūrtakam||27||

28tato vinidrā viśrāntāścandramasyudite punaḥ
|
saṃsādhayiṣyathānyonyaṃ saṃgrāmaṃ kurupāṇḍavāḥ||28||

29tadvacaḥ sarvadharmajñā dhārmikasya viśāṃpate
|
arocayanta sainyāni tathā cānyonyamabruvan||29||

30cukruśuḥ karṇa karṇeti tathā duryodhaneti ca
|
upāramata pāṇḍūnāṃ viratā hi varūthinī||30||

31tathā vikrośamānasya phālgunasya tatastataḥ
|
upāramata pāṇḍūnāṃ senā tava ca bhārata||31||

32tāmasya vācaṃ devāśca ṛṣayaśca mahātmanaḥ
|
sarvasainyāni cākṣudrāṃ prahṛṣṭāḥ pratyapūjayan||32||

33tatsaṃpūjya vaco'krūraṃ sarvasainyāni bhārata
|
muhūrtamasvapan rājan śrāntāni bharatarṣabha||33||

34sā tu saṃprāpya viśrāmaṃ dhvajinī tava bhārata
|
sukhamāptavatī vīramarjunaṃ pratyapūjayat||34||

35tvayi vedāstathā'strāṇi tvayi buddhiparākramau
|
dharmastvayi mahābāho dayā bhūteṣu cānagha||35||

36yaccāśvastāstavecchāmaḥ śarma pārtha tadastu te
|
manasaśca priyānarthān vīra‌kṣipramavāpnuhi||36||

37iti te taṃ naravyāghraṃ praśaṃsanto mahārathāḥ
|
nidrayā samavākṣiptāstūṣṇīmāsanviśāṃpate||37||

38aśvapṛṣṭheṣu cāpyanye rathanīḍeṣu cāpare
|
gajaskandhagatāścānye śerate cāpare kṣitau||38||

39sāyudhāḥ sagadāścaiva sakhaḍgāḥ saparaśvadhāḥ
|
saprāsakavacāścānye narāḥ suptāḥ pṛthak pṛthak||39||

40gajāste pannagābhogairhastairbhūreṇaguṇṭhitaiḥ
|
nidrāndhā vasudhāṃ cakrurghāṇaniḥśvāsaśītalām||40||

41suptāḥ śuśubhire tatra niḥśvasanto mahītale
|
vikīrṇā girayo yadvanniḥśvasadbhirmahoragaiḥ||41||

42sa māṃ ca viṣamāṃ cakruḥ khurāgrairvikṛtāṃ mahīm
|
hayāḥ kāñcanayoktrāste kesarālambibhiryugaiḥ||42||

43suṣupustatra rājendra yuktā vāheṣu sarvaśaḥ
|
evaṃ hayāśca nāgāśca yodhāśca bharatarṣabha
|
yuddhādviramya suṣupuḥ śrameṇa mahatā'nvitāḥ||43||

44tattathā nidrayā bhagnamabodhaṃ prāsvapadbhṛśam
|
kuśalaiḥ śilpibhirvyastaṃ paṭe citramivādbhutam||44||

45te kṣatriyāḥ kuṇḍalino yuvānaḥ parasparaṃ sāyakavikṣatāṅgāḥ
|
kumbheṣu līnāḥ suṣupurgajānāṃ kuceṣu lagnā iva kāminīnām||45||

46tataḥ kumuda‌nāthena kāminīgaṇḍapāṇḍunā
|
netrānandena candreṇa māhendrī digalaṅkṛtā||46||

47daśaśatākṣakakubdariniḥsṛtaḥ kiraṇakesarabhāsurapiñjaraḥ
|
timiravāraṇayūthavidāraṇaḥ samudiyādu‌dayācalakesarī||47||

48haravṛṣottamagātrasamadyutiḥ smaraśarāsanapūrṇasamaprabhaḥ
|
navavadhūsmitacārumanoharaḥ pravisṛtaḥ kumudākarabāndhavaḥ||48||

49tato muhūrtādbhagavānpurastācchaśalakṣaṇaḥ
|
aruṇaṃ darśayāmāsa grasan jyotiḥprabhāṃ prabhuḥ||49||

50aruṇasya tu tasyānu jātarūpasamaprabham
|
raśmijālaṃ mahaccandro mandaṃ mandamavāsṛjat||50||

51utsārayantaḥ prabhayā tamaste candraraśmayaḥ
|
paryagacchañchanaiḥ sarvā diśaḥ khaṃ ca kṣitiṃ tathā||51||

52tato muhūrtādbhuvanaṃ jyotirbhūtamivābhavat
|
aprakhyamaprakāśaṃ ca jagāmāśu tamastathā||52||

53pratiprakāśite loke divābhūte niśākare
|
vicerurna viceruśca rājannaktañcarāstataḥ||53||

54bodhyamānaṃ tu tatsainyaṃ rājaṃścandrasya raśmibhiḥ
|
bubudhe śatapatrāṇāṃ vanaṃ sūryāṃśubhiryathā||54||

55yathā candrodayoddhūtaḥ kṣubhitaḥ sāgaro'bhavat
|
tathā candrodayoddhūtaḥ sa babhūva balārṇavaḥ||55||

56tataḥ pravavṛte yuddhaṃ punareva viśāṃpate
|
loke lokavināśāya paraṃ lokamabhīpsatām||56||
