Mahābhārata 12.320 (Śāntiparvan)

1yudhiṣṭhira uvāca|
aparityajya gārhasthyaṃ kururājarṣisattama
|
kaḥ prāpto vinayaṃ buddhyā mokṣatattvaṃ vadasva me||1||

2saṃnyasyate yathā''tmāyaṃ vyaktasyātmā yathā ca yat
|
paraṃ mokṣasya yaccāpi tanme brūhi pitāmaha||2||

3bhīṣma uvāca|
atrāpyudāharantīmamitihāsaṃ purātanam
|
janakasya ca saṃvādaṃ sulabhāyāśca bhārata||3||

4saṃnyāsaphalikaḥ kaścidbabhūva nṛpatiḥ purā
|
maithilo janako nāma dharmadhvaja iti śrutaḥ||4||

5sa vede mokṣaśāstre ca sve ca śāstre kṛtaśramaḥ
|
indriyāṇi samādhāya śaśāsa vasudhāmimām||5||

6tasya vedavidaḥ prājñāḥ śrutvā tāṃ sādhuvṛttatām
|
lokeṣu spṛhayantyanye puruṣāḥ puruṣeśvara||6||

7atha dharmayuge tasminyogadharmamanuṣṭhitā
|
mahīmanucacāraikā sulabhā nāma bhikṣukī||7||

8tayā jagadidaṃ kṛtsnamaṭantyā mithileśvaraḥ
|
tatra tatra śruto mokṣe kathyamānastridaṇḍibhiḥ||8||

9sā'tisūkṣmāṃ kathāṃ śrutvā tathyaṃ neti sasaṃśayā
|
darśane jātasaṃkalpā janakasya babhūva ha||9||

10tatra sā viprahāyātha pūrvarūpaṃ hi yogataḥ
|
abibhradanavadyāṅgī rūpamanyadanuttamam||10||

11cakṣurnimeṣamātreṇa laghvastragatigāminī
|
videhānāṃ purīṃ subhrūrjagāma kamalekṣaṇā||11||

12sā prāpya mithilāṃ ramyāṃ prabhūtajanasaṃkulām
|
bhaikṣyacaryāpadeśena dadarśa mithileśvaram||12||

13rājā tasyāḥ paraṃ dṛṣṭvā saukumāryaṃ vapustadā
|
keyaṃ kasya kuto veti babhūvāgatavismayaḥ||13||

14tato'syāḥ svāgataṃ kṛtvā vyādiśya ca varāsanam
|
pūjitāṃ pādaśaucena varānnenāpyatarpayat||14||

15atha bhuktavatī prītā rājānaṃ mantribhirvṛtam
|
sarvabhāṣyavidāṃ madhye codayāmāsa bhikṣukī||15||

16sulabhā tvasya dharmeṣu mutto neti sasaṃśayā
|
sattvaṃ sattvena yogajñā praviveśa mahīpateḥ||16||

17netrābhyāṃ netrayorasya raśmīnsaṃyamya raśmibhiḥ
|
sā sma taṃ codayiṣyantī yogabandhairbabandha ha||17||

18janako'pyutsmayan rājā bhāvamasyā viśeṣayan
|
pratijagrāha bhāvena bhāvamasyā nṛpottama||18||

19tadekasminnadhiṣṭhāne saṃvādaḥ śrūyatāmayam
|
chatrādiṣu vimuktasya muktāyāśca tridaṇḍake||19||

20janaka uvāca |
bhagavatyāḥ kva caryeyaṃ kṛtā kva ca gamiṣyasi
|
kasya ca tvaṃ kuto veti papracchaināṃ mahīpatiḥ||20||

21śrute vayasi jātau ca sadbhāvo nādhigamyate
|
eṣvartheṣūttaraṃ tasmātpravedyaṃ matsamāgame||21||

22chatrādiṣu viśeṣeṣu muktaṃ māṃ viddhi tattvataḥ
|
sa tvāṃ saṃmantumicchāmi mānārhā hi matāsi me||22||

23yasmāccaitanmayā prāptaṃ jñānaṃ vaiśeṣikaṃ purā
|
yasya nānyaḥ pravaktā'sti mokṣaṃ tamapi me śṛṇu||23||

24parāśarasagotrasya vṛddhasya sumahātmanaḥ
|
bhikṣoḥ pañcaśikhasyāhaṃ śiṣyaḥ paramasaṃmataḥ||24||

25sāṃkhyajñāne ca yoge ca mahīpālavidhau tathā
|
trividhe mokṣadharme'smingatādhvā chinnasaṃśayaḥ||25||

26sa yathāśāstradṛṣṭena mārgeṇeha paribhraman
|
vārṣikāṃścaturo māsānpurā mayi sukhoṣitaḥ||26||

27tenāhaṃ sāṃkhyamukhyena sudṛṣṭārthena tattvataḥ
|
śrāvitastrividhaṃ mokṣaṃ na ca rājyāddhi cālitaḥ||27||

28so'haṃ tāmakhilāṃ vṛttiṃ trividhāṃ mokṣasaṃhitām
|
muktarāgaścarāmyekaḥ pade paramake sthitaḥ||28||

29vairāgyaṃ punaretasya mokṣasya paramo vidhiḥ
|
jñānādeva ca vairāgyaṃ jāyate yena mucyate||29||

30jñānena kurute yatnaṃ yatnena prāpyate mahat
|
mahaddvandvapramokṣāya sā siddhiryā vayotigā||30||

31seyaṃ paramikā buddheḥ prāptā nirdvandvatā mayā
|
ihaiva gatamohena caratā muktasaṅginā||31||

32yathā kṣetraṃ mṛdūbhūtamadbhirāplāvitaṃ tathā
|
janayatyaṅkuraṃ karma nṛṇāṃ tadvatpunarbhavam||32||

33yathā cottāpitaṃ bījaṃ kapāle yatra tatra vā
|
prāpyāpyaṃkurahetutvamabījatvānna jāyate||33||

34tadvadbhagavatā'nena śikhā proktena bhikṣuṇā
|
jñānaṃ kṛtamabījaṃ me viṣayeṣu na jāyate||34||

35nābhirajyati kasmiṃścinnānarthe na parigrahe
|
nābhirajyati caiteṣu vyarthatvādrāgaroṣayoḥ||35||

36yaśca me dakṣiṇaṃ bāhuṃ candanena samukṣayet
|
savyaṃ vāsyāpi yastakṣetsamāvetāvubhau mama||36||

37sukhī so'hamavāptārthaḥ samaloṣṭāśmakāñcanaḥ
|
muktasaṅgaḥ sthito rājye viśiṣṭo'nyaistridaṇḍibhiḥ||37||

38mokṣe hi trividhā niṣṭhā dṛṣṭā'nyairmokṣavittamaiḥ
|
jñānaṃ lokottaraṃ yacca sarvatyāgaśca karmaṇām||38||

39jñānaniṣṭhāṃ vadantyeke mokṣaśāstravido janāḥ
|
karmaniṣṭhāṃ tathaivānye yatayaḥ sūkṣmadarśinaḥ||39||

40prahāyobhayamapyeva jñānaṃ karma ca kevalam
|
tṛtīyeyaṃ samākhyātā niṣṭhā tena mahātmanā||40||

41yame ca niyame caiva kāme dveṣe parigrahe
|
māne daṃbhe tathā snehe sadṛśāste kuṭumbibhiḥ||41||

42tridaṇḍādiṣu yadyasti mokṣo jñānena kasyacit
|
chatrādiṣu kathaṃ na syāttulyahetau parigrahe||42||

43yena yena hi yasyārthaḥ kāraṇeneha karmaṇi
|
tattadālambate sarvadravye svārthaparigrahe||43||

44doṣadarśī tu gārhasthye yo vrajatyāśramāntare
|
utsṛjanparigṛhṇaṃśca so'pi saṅgānna mucyate||44||

45ādhipatye tathā tulye nigrahānugrahātmake
|
rājabhirbhikṣukāstulyā mucyante kena hetunā||45||

46atha satyādhipatye'pi jñānenaiveha kevalam
|
mucyante sarvapāpebhyo dehe paramake sthitāḥ||46||

47kāṣāyadhāraṇaṃ mauṇḍyaṃ triviṣṭabdhaṃ kamaṇḍalum
|
liṅgānyutpathabhūtāni na mokṣāyeti me matiḥ||47||

48yadi satyapi liṅge'smin jñānamevātra kāraṇam
|
nirmokṣāyeha duḥkhasya liṅgamātraṃ nirarthakam||48||

49athavā duḥkhaśaithilyaṃ vīkṣya liṅge kṛtā matiḥ
|
kiṃ tadevārthasāmānyaṃ chatrādiṣu na lakṣyate||49||

50ākiñcanye na mokṣo'sti kiñcanye nāsti bandhanam
|
kiñcanye cetare caiva janturjñānena mucyate||50||

51tasmāddharmārthakāmeṣu tathā rājyaparigrahe
|
bandhanāyataneṣveṣu viddhyabandhe pade sthitam||51||

52rājyaiśvaryamayaḥ pāśaḥ snehāyatanabandhanaḥ
|
mokṣāśmaniśiteneha cchinnastyāgāsinā mayā||52||

53so'hamevaṃgato mukto jātāsthastvayi bhikṣuki
|
ayathārthaṃ hi te varṇaṃ vakṣyāmi śṛṇu tanmama||53||

54saukumāryaṃ tathā rūpaṃ vapuragryaṃ tathā vayaḥ
|
tavaitāni samastāni niyamaśceti saṃśayaḥ||54||

55yaccāpyananurūpaṃ te liṅgasyāsya viceṣṭitam
|
mukto'yaṃ syānnaveti syāddharṣito matparigrahaḥ||55||

56na ca kāmasamāyukte yukte'pyasti tridaṇḍake
|
na rakṣyate tvayā cedaṃ na muktasyāsti gopanā||56||

57matpakṣasaṃśrayāccāyaṃ śṛṇu yaste vyatikramaḥ
|
āśrayantyāḥ svabhāvena mama pūrvaparigraham||57||

58praveśaste kṛtaḥ kena mama rāṣṭre pure'pi vā
|
kasya vā sannikarṣāttvaṃ praviṣṭā hṛdayaṃ mama||58||

59varṇapravaramukhyāsi brāhmaṇī kṣatriyastvaham
|
nāvayorekayogo'smi mā kṛthā varṇasaṃkaram||59||

60vartase mokṣadharmeṇa tvaṃ gārhasthye'hamāśrame
|
ayaṃ cāpi suṣṭaste dvitīyo''śramasaṃkaraḥ||60||

61sagotrāṃ vā'sagotrāṃ vā na veda tvāṃ na vettha mām
|
sagotramāviśantyāste tṛtīyo gotrasaṃkaraḥ||61||

62atha jīvati te bhartā proṣito'pyathavā kvacit
|
agamyā parabhāryeti caturtho dharmasaṃkaraḥ||62||

63sā tvametānyakāryāṇi kāryāpekṣā vyavasyasi
|
avijñānena vā yuktā mithyājñānena vā punaḥ||63||

64athavāpi svataṃtrā'si svadoṣeṇeha karhicit
|
yadi kiṃcicchrutaṃ te'sti sarvaṃ kṛtamanarthakam||64||

65idamanyattṛtīyaṃ te bhāvasparśavighātakam
|
duṣṭāyā lakṣyate liṅgaṃ vivṛṇvatyā prakāśitam||65||

66na mayyevābhisaṃdhiste jayaiṣiṇyā jaye kṛtaḥ
|
yeyaṃ matpariṣatkṛtsnā jetumicchasi tāmapi||66||

67tathā'rhatastataśca tvaṃ dṛṣṭiṃ svāṃ prati muñcasi
|
matpakṣapratighātāya svapakṣodbhāvanāya ca||67||

68sā svenāmarṣajena tvamṛddhimohena mohitā
|
bhūyaḥ sṛjasi yogāṃstvaṃ viṣāmṛtamivaikatām||68||

69icchatoratra yo lābhaḥ strīpuṃsoramṛtopamaḥ
|
alābhaścāpi raktasya so'pi doṣo viṣopamaḥ||69||

70mā tyākṣīḥ sādhu jānīṣva svaśāstramanupālaya
|
kṛteyaṃ hi vijijñāsā mukto neti tvayā mama
|
etatsarvaṃ praticchannaṃ mayi nārhasi gūhitam||70||

71sā yadi tvaṃ svakāryeṇa yadyanyasya mahīpateḥ
|
tattvaṃ satrapraticchannā mayi nārhasi gūhitam||71||

72na rājānaṃ mṛṣā gacchenna dvijātiṃ kañcana
|
na striyaṃ strīguṇopetāṃ hanyurhyete mṛṣā gatāḥ||72||

73rājñāṃ hi balamaiśvaryaṃ brahma brahmavidāṃ balam
|
rūpayauvanasaubhāgyaṃ strīṇāṃ balamanuttamam||73||

74ata etairbalaireva balinaḥ svārthamicchatā
|
ārjavenābhigantavyā vināśāya hyanārjavam||74||

75sā tvaṃ jātiṃ śrutaṃ vṛttaṃ bhāvaṃ prakṛtimātmanaḥ
|
kṛtyamāgamane caiva vaktumarhasi tattvataḥ||75||

76bhīṣma uvāca|
ityetairasukhairvākyairayuktairasamañjasaiḥ
|
pratyādiṣṭā narendreṇa sulabhā na vyakampata||76||

77uktavākye tu nṛpatau sulabhā cārudarśanā
|
tataścārutaraṃ vākyaṃ pracakrāmātha bhāṣitum||77||

78sulabhovāca |
navabhirnavabhiścaiva doṣairvāgbuddhidūṣaṇaiḥ
|
apetamupapannārthamaṣṭādaśaguṇānvitam||78||

79saukṣamyaṃ sāṃkhyakramau cobhau nirṇayaḥ saprayojanaḥ
|
pañcaitānyarthajātāni vākyamityucyate nṛpa||79||

80eṣāmekaikeśo'rthānāṃ saukṣmyādīnāṃ svalakṣaṇam
|
śṛṇu saṃsāryamāṇānāṃ padārthapadavākyataḥ||80||

81jñānaṃ jñeyeṣu bhinneṣu yadā bhedena vartate
|
yatrādhivāsinī buddhistatsaukṣmyamiti vartate||81||

82doṣāṇāṃ ca guṇānāṃ ca pramāṇaṃ pravibhāgataḥ
|
kañcidarthamabhipretya sā saṃkhyetyupadhāryatām||82||

83idaṃ pūrvamidaṃ paścādvaktavyaṃ yadvivakṣitam
|
kramayogaṃ tamapyāhurvākyaṃ vākyavido janāḥ||83||

84dharmakāmārthamokṣeṣu pratijñāya viśeṣataḥ
|
idaṃ taditi vākyānte procyate sa vinirṇayaḥ||84||

85icchādveṣābhavairduḥkhaiḥ prakarṣo yatra jāyate
|
tatra yā nṛpate vṛttistatprayojanamiṣyate||85||

86tānyetāni yathoktāni saukṣmyādīni janādhipa
|
ekārthasamavetāni vākyaṃ mama niśāmaya||86||

87upetārthamabhinnārthaṃ nyāyavṛttaṃ na cādhikam
|
nāślakṣṇaṃ na ca saṃdigdhaṃ vakṣyāmi paramaṃ tataḥ||87||

88na gurvakṣarasaṃyuktaṃ parāṅmukhasukhaṃ na ca
|
nānṛtaṃ na trivargeṇa viruddhaṃ nāpyasaṃskṛtam||88||

89na nyūnaṃ kaṣṭaśabdaṃ vā vikramābhihitaṃ na ca
|
na śeṣamanukalpena niṣkāraṇamahetukam||89||

90kāmātkrodhādbhayāllobhāddainyāccānāryakāttathā
|
hrīto'nukrośato mānānna vakṣyāmi kathañcana||90||

91vaktā śrotā ca vākyaṃ ca yadā tvavikalaṃ nṛpa
|
samameti vivakṣāyāṃ tadā so'rthaḥ prakāśate||91||

92vaktavye tu yadā vaktā śrotāramavamanya vai
|
svārthamāha parārthaṃ tattadā vākyaṃ na rohati||92||

93atha yaḥ svārthamutsṛjya parārthaṃ prāha mānavaḥ
|
viśaṅkā jāyate tasminvākyaṃ tadapi doṣavat||93||

94yastu vaktā dvayorarthamaviruddhaṃ prabhāṣate
|
śrotuścaivātmanaścaiva sa vaktā netaro nṛpa||94||

95tadarthavadidaṃ vākyaṃ rājannekamanāḥ śruṇu
|
yathā jatu ca kāṣṭhaṃ ca pāṃsavaścodabindavaḥ||95||

96saṃśliṣṭāni tathā rājanprāṇināmiha saṃbhavaḥ
|
śabdaḥ sparśo raso rūpaṃ gandhaḥ pañcendriyāṇi ca||96||

97pṛthagātmāna ātmānaṃ saṃśliṣṭā jatukāṣṭhavat
|
na caiṣāṃ codanā kācidastītyeṣa viniścayaḥ||97||

98ekaikasyeha vijñānaṃ nāstyātmani tathā pare
|
na veda cakṣuścakṣuṣṭvaṃ śrotraṃ nātmani vartate||98||

99tathaiva vyabhicāreṇa na vartante parasparam
|
praśliṣṭaṃ ca na jānanti yathā''pa iva pāṃsavaḥ ||99||

100bāhyānanyānapekṣante guṇāṃstānapi me śṛṇu
|
rūpaṃ cakṣuḥ prakāśaśca darśane hetavastrayaḥ ||100||

101yathaivātra tathānyeṣu jñānajñeyeṣu hetavaḥ
|
jñānajñeyāntare tasminmano nāmāparo guṇaḥ ||101||

102vicārayati yenāyaṃ niścaye sādhvasādhuni
|
dvādaśastvaparastatra buddhirnāma guṇaḥ smṛtaḥ
|
yena saṃśayapūrveṣu boddhavyeṣu vyavasyati||102||

103atha dvādaśake tasminsattvaṃ nāmāparo guṇaḥ
|
mahāsattvo'lpasattvo vā janturyenānumīyate||103||

104ahaṃ karteti cāpyanyo guṇastatra caturdaśaḥ
|
mamāyamiti yenāyaṃ manyate na mameti ca||104||

105atha pañcadaśo rājanguṇastatrāparaḥ smṛtaḥ
|
pṛthakkalāsamūhasya sāmagryaṃ tadihocyate||105||

106guṇastvevāparastatra saṃghāta iva ṣoḍaśaḥ
|
prakṛtirvyaktirityetau guṇau yasminsamāśritau||106||

107sukhāsukhe jarāmṛtyū lābhālābhau priyāpriye
|
iti caikonaviṃśo'yaṃ dvandvayoga iti smṛtaḥ||107||

108ūrdhvaṃ caikonaviṃśatyā kālo nāmāparo guṇaḥ
|
itīmaṃ viddhi viṃśatyā bhūtānāṃ prabhavāpyayam||108||

109viṃśaścaiṣa saṃghāto mahābhūtāni pañca ca
|
sadasadbhāvayogau tu guṇāvanyau prakāśakau||109||

110ityevaṃ viṃśakaścaiva guṇāḥ sapta ca ye smṛtāḥ
|
vidhiḥ śukraṃ balaṃ ceti traya ete guṇāḥ pare||110||

111viṃśatirdaśa caivaṃ hi guṇāḥ saṃkhyānataḥ smṛtāḥ
|
samagrā yatra vartante taccharīramiti smṛtam||111||

112avyaktaṃ prakṛtiṃ tvāsāṃ kalānāṃ kaścidicchati
|
vyaktaṃ cāsāṃ tathā cānyaḥ sthūladarśī prapaśyati||112||

113avyaktaṃ yadi vā vyaktaṃ dvayīmatha catuṣṭayīm
|
prakṛtiṃ sarvabhūtānāṃ paśyantyadhyātmacintakāḥ||113||

114yeyaṃ prakṛtiravyaktā kalābhirvyaktatāṃ gatā
|
ahaṃ ca tvaṃ ca rājendra ye cāpyanye śarīriṇaḥ||114||

115bindunyāsādayo'vasthāḥ śukraśoṇitasambhavāḥ
|
yāsāmeva nipātena kalalaṃ nāma jāyate||115||

116kalalādbudbudotpattiḥ peśī ca budbudāt smṛtā
|
peśyāstvaṅgābhinirvṛttirnakharomāṇi cāṅgataḥ||116||

117saṃpūrṇe navame māsi jantorjātasya maithila
|
jāyate nāmarūpatvaṃ strī pumānveti liṅgataḥ||117||

118jātamātraṃ tu tadrūpaṃ dṛṣṭvā tāmranakhāṅguli
|
kaumāraṃ rūpamāpannaṃ rūpato nopalabhyate||118||

119kaumārādyauvanaṃ cāpi sthāviryaṃ cāpi yauvanāt
|
anena kramayogena pūrvaṃ pūrvaṃ na labhyate||119||

120kalānāṃ pṛthagarthānāṃ pratibhedaḥ kṣaṇe kṣaṇe
|
vartate sarvabhūteṣu saukṣmyāttu na vibhāvyate||120||

121na caiṣāmatyayo rājan lakṣyate prabhavo na ca
|
avasthāyāmavasthāyāṃ dīpasyevārciṣo gatiḥ||121||

122tasyāpyevaṃprabhāvasya sadaśvasyeva dhāvataḥ
|
ajasraṃ sarvalokasya kaḥ kuto vā na vā kutaḥ||122||

123kasyedaṃ kasya vā nedaṃ kuto vedaṃ na vā kutaḥ
|
sambandhaḥ ko'sti bhūtānāṃ svairapyavayavairiha||123||

124yathā''dityānmaṇeścāpi vīrudbhyaścaiva pāvakaḥ
|
jāyantyevaṃ samudayātkalānāmiva jantavaḥ||124||

125ātmanyevātmanā''tmānaṃ yathā tvamanupaśyasi
|
evamevātmanā''tmānamanyasminkiṃ na paśyasi||125||

126yadyātmani parasmiṃśca samatāmadhyavasyasi
|
atha māṃ kā'si kasyeti kimarthamanupṛcchasi||126||

127idaṃ me syādidaṃ neti dvandvairmuktasya maithila
|
kā'si kasya kuto veti vacanaiḥ kiṃ prayojanam||127||

128ripau mitre'tha madhyasthe vijaye sandhivigrahe
|
kṛtavān yo mahīpālaḥ kiṃ tasminmuktalakṣaṇam||128||

129trivargaṃ saptadhā vyaktaṃ yo na vedeha karmasu
|
saṅgavān yastrivargeṇa kiṃ tasminmuktalakṣaṇam||129||

130priye vā'pyapriye vāpi durbale balavatyapi
|
yasya nāsti samaṃ cakṣuḥ kiṃ tasminmuktalakṣaṇam||130||

131tadayuktasya te mokṣe yo'bhimāno bhavennṛpa
|
suhṛddhiḥ sannivāryaste'viraktasyeva bheṣajam||131||

132tāni tāni tu sañcintya saṅgasthānānyarindama
|
ātmanā''tmani sampaśyetkimanyanmuktalakṣaṇam||132||

133imānyanyāni sūkṣmāṇi mokṣamāśritya kānicit
|
caturaṅgapravṛttāni saṅgasthānāni me śṛṇu||133||

134ya imāṃ pṛthivīṃ kṛtsnāmekacchatrāṃ praśāsti ha
|
eka eva sa vai rājā puramadhyāvasatyuta||134||

135tatpure caikamevāsya gṛhaṃ yadadhitiṣṭhati
|
gṛhe śayanamapyekaṃ niśāyāṃ yatra līyate||135||

136śayyārdhaṃ tasya cāpyatra strīpūrvamadhitiṣṭhati
|
tadanena prasaṅgena phalenaiveha yujyate||136||

137evamevopabhogeṣu bhojanācchādaneṣu ca
|
guṇeṣu parimeyeṣu nigrahānugrahaṃ prati||137||

138paratantraḥ sadā rājā svalpeṣvapi prasajjate
|
sandhivigrahayoge ca kuto rājñaḥ svatantratā||138||

139strīṣu krīḍāvihāreṣu nityamasyāsvatantratā
|
mantre cāmātyasamitau kutastasya svatantratā||139||

140yadā hyājñāpayatyanyāṃstatrāsyoktā svatantratā
|
avaśaḥ kārya te tatra tasmiṃstasminkṣaṇe sthitaḥ||140||

141svapnakāmo na labhate svaptuṃ kāryārthibhirjanaiḥ
|
śayane cāpyanujñātaḥ supta utthāpyate'vaśaḥ||141||

142snāhyālabha piba prāśa juhudhyagnīnyajetyapi
|
bravīhi śṛṇu cāpīti vivaśa: kāryate paraiḥ||142||

143abhigamyābhigamyaivaṃ yācante satataṃ narāḥ
|
na cāpyutsahate dātuṃ vittarakṣī mahājanān||143||

144dāne koṣakṣayo'pyasya vairaṃ cāsyāprayacchataḥ
|
kṣaṇenāsyopavartante doṣā vairāgyakārakāḥ||144||

145prājñān śūrāṃstathaivāḍhyānekasthānapi śaṅkate
|
bhayamapyabhaye rājño yaiśca nityamupāsyate||145||

146tathā caite praduṣyanti rājan ye kīrtitā mayā
|
tathaivāsya bhayaṃ tebhyo jāyate paśya yādṛśam||146||

147sarvaḥ sve sve gṛhe rājā sarvaḥ sve sve gṛhe gṛhī
|
nigrahānugrahānkurvaṃstulyo janaka rājabhiḥ||147||

148putrā dārāstathaivātmā kośo mitrāṇi sañcayāḥ
|
paraiḥ sādhāraṇā hyete taistairevāsya hetubhiḥ||148||

149hato deśaḥ puraṃ dagdhaṃ pradhānaḥ kuñjaro mṛtaḥ
|
lokasādhāraṇeṣveṣu mithyājñānena tapyate||149||

150amukto mānasairduḥkhairicchādveṣabhayodbhavaiḥ
|
śirorogādibhī rogaistathaivābhiniyantṛbhiḥ||150||

151dvandvaistaistaistvapahataḥ sarvataḥ pariśaṅkitaḥ
|
bahupratyarthikaṃ rājyamupāste gaṇayanniśāḥ||151||

152tadalpasukhamatyarthaṃ bahuduḥkhamasāravat
|
tṛṇāgnijvalanaprakhyaṃ phenabudbudasaṃnibham||152||

153ko rājyamabhipadyeta prāpya copaśamaṃ labhet
|
mamedamiti yaccedaṃ puraṃ rāṣṭraṃ ca manyase||153||

154balaṃ kośamamātyāṃśca kasyaitāni na vā nṛpa
|
mitrāmātyapuraṃ rāṣṭraṃ daṇḍaḥ kośo mahīpatiḥ||154||

155saptāṅgasyāsya rājyasya tridaṇḍyasyeva tiṣṭhataḥ
|
anyonyaguṇayuktasya kaḥ kena guṇato'dhikaḥ||155||

156teṣu teṣu hi kāleṣu tattadaṅgaṃ viśiṣyate
|
yena yatsidhyate kāryaṃ tatprādhānyāya kalpate||156||

157saptāṅgaścaiva saṃghātastrayaścānye nṛpottama
|
saṃbhūya daśavargo'yaṃ bhuṅkte rājyaṃ hi rājavat
|
yaśca rājā mahotsāhaḥ kṣatradharme rato bhavet||157||

158sa tuṣyeddaśabhāgena tatastvanyo daśāvaraiḥ
|
nāstyasādhāraṇo rājā nāsti rājyamarājakam||158||

159rājye'sati kuto dharmo dharme'sati kutaḥ param
|
yo'pyatra paramo dharmaḥ pavitraṃ rājarājyayoḥ||159||

160pṛthivī dakṣiṇā yasya so'śvamedho na yujyate
|
sā'hametāni karmāṇi rājaduḥkhāni maithila||160||

161samarthā śataśo vaktumathavāpi sahasraśaḥ
|
svadehenābhiṣaṅgo me kutaḥ paraparigrahe||161||

162na māmevaṃvidhāṃ yuktāmīdṛśaṃ vaktumarhasi
|
nanu nāma tvayā mokṣaḥ kṛtsnaḥ pañcaśikhācchrutaḥ||162||

163sopāya: sopaniṣadaḥ sopāsaṅgaḥ saniścayaḥ
|
tasya te muktasaṅgasya pāśānākramya tiṣṭhataḥ||163||

164chatrādiṣu viśeṣeṣu punaḥ saṅgaḥ kathaṃ nṛpa
|
śrutaṃ te na śrutaṃ manye mṛṣā vāpi śrutaṃ śrutam||164||

165athavā śrutasaṃkāśaṃ śrutamanyacchrutaṃ tvayā
|
athāpīmāsu saṃjñāsu laukikīṣu pratiṣṭhase||165||

166abhiṣaṅgāvarodhābhyāṃ baddhastvaṃ prākṛto yathā
|
sattvenānupraveśo hi yo'yaṃ tvayi kṛto mayā||166||

167kiṃ tavāpakṛtaṃ yatra yadi mukto'si sarvaśaḥ
|
niyamo hyeṣu varṇeṣu yatīnāṃ śūnyavāsitā||167||

168śūnyamāveśayantyā ca mayā kiṃ kasya dūṣitam
|
na pāṇibhyāṃ na bāhubhyāṃ pādorubhyāṃ ca cānagha||168||

169na gātrāvayavairanyaiḥ spṛśāmi tvāṃ narādhipa
|
kule mahati jātena hrīmatā dīrghadarśinā||169||

170naitatsadasi vaktavyaṃ sadvā'sadvā mithaḥ kṛtam
|
brāhmaṇā guravaśceme tathā mānyā gurūttamāḥ
|
tvaṃ cātha gururapyeṣāmeva manyonyagauravam ||170||

171tadevamanusaṃdṛśya vācyāvācyaṃ parīkṣatā
|
strīpuṃsoḥ samavāyo'yaṃ tvayā vācyo na saṃsadi||171||

172yathā puṣkaraparṇasthaṃ jalaṃ tatparṇamaspṛśat
|
tiṣṭhatyaspṛśatī tadvattvayi vatsyāmi maithila||172||

173yadi cādya spṛśantyā me sparśaṃ jānāsi kañcana
|
jñānaṃ kṛtamabījaṃ te kathaṃ teneha bhikṣuṇā||173||

174sa gārhasthyāccyutaśca tvaṃ mokṣaṃ cānāpya durvidam
|
ubhayorantarāle vai vartase mokṣavārtikaḥ||174||

175na hi muktasya muktena jñasyaikatvapṛthaktvayoḥ
|
bhāvābhāvasamāyoge jāyate varṇasaṃkaraḥ||175||

176varṇāśramāḥ pṛthaktvena dṛṣṭārthasyāpṛthaktvinaḥ
|
nānyadanyaditi jñātvā nānyadanyatra vartate||176||

177pāṇau kuṇḍaṃ tathā kuṇḍe payaḥ payasi makṣikā
|
āśritāśrayayogena pṛthaktvenāśritāḥ punaḥ||177||

178na tu kuṇḍe payobhāvaḥ payaścāpi na makṣikā
|
svayamevāpnuvantyete bhāvā na tu parāśrayam||178||

179pṛthaktvādāśramāṇāṃ ca varṇānyatve tathaiva ca
|
parasparapṛthaktvācca kathaṃ te varṇasaṃkaraḥ||179||

180nāsmi varṇottamā jātyā na vaiśyā nāvarā tathā
|
tava rājansavarṇāsmi śuddhayoniraviplutā||180||

181pradhāno nāma rājarṣirvyaktaṃ te śrotramāgataḥ
|
kule tasya samutpannāṃ sulabhā nāma viddhi mām||181||

182droṇaśca śataśṛṅgaśca cakradvāraśca parvataḥ
|
mama satreṣu pūrveṣāṃ citā maghavatā saha||182||

183sāhaṃ tasminkule jātā bhartaryasati madvidhe
|
vinītā mokṣadharmeṣu carāmyekā munivratam||183||

184nāsmi satrapraticchannā na parasvāpahāriṇī
|
na dharmasaṃkarakarī svadharme'si dhṛtavratā||184||

185nāsthirā svapratijñāyāṃ nāsamīkṣya pravādinī
|
nāsamīkṣyāgatā ceha tvatsakāśaṃ janādhipa||185||

186mokṣe te bhāvitāṃ buddhiṃ śrutvā'haṃ kuśalaiṣiṇī
|
tava mokṣasya cāpyasya jijñāsārthamihāgatā||186||

187na vargasthā bravīmyetatsvapakṣaparapakṣayoḥ
|
mukto vyāyacchate yaśca śāntau yaśca na śāmyati||187||

188yathā śūnye purāgāre bhikṣurekāṃ niśāṃ vaset
|
tathāhaṃ tvaccharīre'sminnimāṃ vatsyāmi śarvarīm||188||

189sā'haṃ mānapradānena vāgātithyena cārcitā
|
suptā suśaraṇaṃ prītā śvo gamiṣyāmi maithila||189||

190bhīṣma uvāca|
ityetāni sa vākyāni hetumantyarthavanti ca
|
śrutvā nādhijagau rājā kiñcidanyadataḥ param||190||
