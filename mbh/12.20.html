Mahābhārata 12.20 (Śāntiparvan)

1vaiśampāyana uvāca|
asminvākyāntare vaktā devasthāno mahātapāḥ
|
abhinītataraṃ vākyamityuvāca yudhiṣṭhiram ||1||

2devasthāna uvāca |
yadvacaḥ phālgunenoktaṃ na jyāyo'sti dhanāditi
|
atra te vartayiṣyāmi tadekāntamanāḥ śṛṇu ||2||

3ajātaśatro dharmeṇa kṛtsnā te vasudhā jitā
|
tāṃ jitvā ca vṛthā rājanna parityaktumarhasi ||3||

4catuṣpadī hi niḥśreṇī brahmaṇyeva pratiṣṭhitā
|
tāṃ krameṇa mahābāho yathāvajjaya pārthiva ||4||

5tasmātpārtha mahāyajñairyajasva bahudakṣiṇaiḥ
|
svādhyāyayajñā ṛṣayo jñānayajñāstathā'pare ||5||

6karmaniṣṭhāṃśca buddhyethāstaponiṣṭhāṃśca pārthiva
|
vaikhānasānāṃ kaunteya vacanaṃ śrūyate yathā ||6||

7īheta dhanahetoryastasyānīhā garīyasī
|
bhūyāndoṣo hi vardheta yastaṃ dharmamupāśrayet ||7||

8kṛtsnaṃ ca dhanasaṃhāraṃ kurvanti vidhikāraṇāt
|
ātmānaṃ dūṣito buddhyā bhrūṇahatyāṃ na buddhyate ||8||

9anarhate yaddadāti na dadāti yadarhate
|
arhānarhāparijñānāddāna dharmo'pi duṣkaraḥ ||9||

10yajñāya sṛṣṭāni dhanāni dhātrā yaśoddiṣṭaḥ puruṣo rakṣitā ca
|
tasmātsarvaṃ yajña evopayojyaṃ dhanaṃ tato'nantara eva kāmaḥ ||10||

11yajñairindro vividhai ratnavadbhirdevānsarvānabhyayādbhūritejāḥ
|
tenendratvaṃ prāpya vibhrājate'sau tasmādyajñe sarvamevopayojyam ||11||

12mahādevaḥ sarvayajñe mahātmā hutvā''tmānaṃ devadevo babhūva
|
viśvā~llokān vyāpya viṣṭabhya kīrtyā virājate dyutimānkṛttivāsāḥ ||12||

13āvikṣitaḥ pārthivo'sau marutto buddhyā śakraṃ yo'jayaddevarājam
|
yajñe yasya śrīḥ svayaṃ sanniviṣṭā yasminbhāṇḍaṃ kāñcanaṃ sarvamāsīt ||13||

14hariścandraḥ pārthivendraḥ śrutaste yajñairiṣṭvā puṇyabhāgvītaśokaḥ
|
ṛddhyā śakaṃ yo'jayanmānuṣaḥ saṃstasmādyajñe sarvamevopayojyam ||14||
