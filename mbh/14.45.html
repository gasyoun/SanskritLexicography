Mahābhārata 14.45 (Āśvamedhikaparvan)

1brahmovāca|
buddhisāraṃ manastambhamindriyagrāmabandhanam
|
mahābhūtapariskandhaṃ niveśapariveśanam||1||

2jarāśokasamāviṣṭaṃ vyādhivyasanasambhavam
|
deśakālavicārīdaṃ śramavyāyāmaniḥsvanam||2||

3ahorātraparikṣepaṃ śītoṣṇaparimaṇḍalam
|
sukhaduḥkhāntasaṃśleṣaṃ kṣutpipāsāvakīlakam||3||

4chāyātapavilekhaṃ ca nimeṣonmeṣavihvalam
|
ghoramohajalākīrṇaṃ vartamānamacetanam||4||

5māsārdhamāsagaṇitaṃ viṣamaṃ lokasañcaram
|
tamoniyamapaṅkaṃ ca rajovegapravartakam||5||

6mahāhaṅkāradīptaṃ ca guṇasañjātavartanam
|
aratigrahaṇānīkaṃ śokasaṃhāravartanam||6||

7kriyākāraṇasaṃyuktaṃ rāgavistāramāyatam
|
lobhepsāparivikṣobhaṃ vicitrājñānasambhavam||7||

8bhayamohaparīvāraṃ bhūtasammohakārakam
|
ānandaprīticāraṃ ca kāmakrodhaparigraham||8||

9mahadādiviśeṣāntamasaktaṃ prabhavāvyayam
|
manojavaṃ manaḥkāntaṃ kālacakraṃ pravartate||9||

10etaddvandvasamāyuktaṃ kālacakramacetanam
|
visṛjetsaṃkṣipeccāpi bodhayetsāmaraṃ jagat||10||

11kālacakrapravattiṃ ca nivṛttiṃ caiva tattvataḥ
|
yastu veda naro nityaṃ na sa bhūteṣu muhyati||11||

12vimuktaḥ sarvasaṃskāraiḥ sarvadvandvavivarjitaḥ
|
vimuktaḥ sarvapāpebhyaḥ prāpnoti paramāṃ gatim||12||

13gṛhastho brahmacārī ca vānaprastho'tha bhikṣukaḥ
|
catvāra āśramāḥ proktāḥ sarve gārhasthyamūlakāḥ||13||

14yaḥ kaścidiha loke'sminnāgamaḥ parikīrtitaḥ
|
tasyāntagamanaṃ śreyaḥ kīrtireṣā sanātanī||14||

15saṃskāraiḥ saṃskṛtaḥ pūrvaṃ yathāvaccaritavrataḥ
|
jātau guṇaviśiṣṭāyāṃ samāvarteta tattvavit||15||

16svadāranirato nityaṃ śiṣṭācāro jitendriyaḥ
|
pañcabhiśca mahāyajñaiḥ śraddadhāno yajediha||16||

17devatātithiśiṣṭāśī nirato vedakarmasu
|
ijyāpradānayuktaśca yathāśakti yathāsukham||17||

18na pāṇipādacapalo na netracapalo muniḥ
|
na ca vāgaṅgacapala iti śiṣṭasya gocaraḥ||18||

19nityaṃ yajñopavītī syācchuklavāsāḥ śucivrataḥ
|
niyato yamadānābhyāṃ sadā śiṣṭaiśca saṃviśet||19||

20jitaśiśnodaro maitraḥ śiṣṭācārasamanvitaḥ
|
vaiṇavīṃ dhārayedyaṣṭiṃ sodakaṃ ca kamaṇḍalum||20||

21adhītyādhyāpanaṃ kuryāttathā yajanayājane
|
dānaṃ pratigrahaṃ vāpi ṣaḍguṇāṃ vṛttimācaret||21||

22trīṇi karmāṇi jānīta brāhmaṇānāṃ tu jīvikā
|
yājanādhyāpane cobhe śuddhāccāpi pratigrahaḥ||22||

23atha śeṣāṇi cānyāni trīṇi karmāṇi yāni tu
|
dānamadhyayanaṃ yajño dharmayuktāni tāni tu||23||

24teṣvapramādaṃ kurvīta triṣu karmasu dharmavit
|
dānto maitraḥ kṣamāyuktaḥ sarvabhūtasamo muniḥ||24||

25sarvametadyathāśakti vipro nirvartayan śuciḥ
|
evaṃ yukto jayetsvargaṃ gṛhasthaḥ saṃśitavrataḥ||25||
