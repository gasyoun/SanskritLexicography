Mahābhārata 5.10 (Udyogaparvan)

1indra uvāca|
sarvaṃ vyāptamidaṃ devā vṛtreṇa jagadavyayam
|
na hyasya sadṛśaṃ kiñcit pratighātāya yadbhavet ||1||

2samartho hyabhavaṃ pūrvamasamartho'smi sāṃpratam
|
kathaṃ nu kāryaṃ bhadraṃ vo durdharṣaḥ sa hi me mataḥ ||2||

3tejasvī ca mahātmā ca yuddhe cāmitavikramaḥ
|
grasettribhuvanaṃ sarvaṃ sadevāsuramānuṣam ||3||

4tasmādviniścayamimaṃ śṛṇudhvaṃ tridivaukasaḥ
|
viṣṇoḥ kṣayamupāgamya sametya ca mahātmanā ||4||

5tena saṃmantrya vetsyāmo vadhopāyaṃ durātmanaḥ| śalya uvāca|
evamukte maghavatā devāḥ sarṣigaṇāstadā
|
śaraṇyaṃ śaraṇaṃ devaṃ jagmurviṣṇuṃ mahābalam ||5||

6ūcuśca sarve deveśaṃ viṣṇuṃ vṛtrabhayārditāḥ
|
trayo lokāstvayā krāntāstribhirvikramaṇaiḥ purā ||6||

7amṛtaṃ cāhṛtaṃ viṣṇo daityāśca nihatā raṇe
|
baliṃ baddhvā mahādaityaṃ śakro devādhipaḥ kṛtaḥ ||7||

8tvaṃ prabhuḥ sarvadevānāṃ tvayā sarvamidaṃ tatam
|
tvaṃ hi devo mahādeva sarvalokanamaskṛtaḥ ||8||

9gatirbhava tvaṃ devānāṃ sendrāṇāmamarottama
|
jagadvyāptamidaṃ sarvaṃ vṛtreṇāsurasūdana ||9||

10viṣṇuruvāca| avaśyaṃ karaṇīyaṃ me bhavatāṃ hitamuttamam
|
tasmādupāyaṃ vakṣyāmi yathā'sau nabhaviṣyati ||10||

11gacchadhvaṃ sarṣigandharvā yatrāsau viśvarūpadhṛk
|
sāma tasya prayuñjadhvaṃ tata enaṃ vijeṣyatha ||11||

12bhaviṣyati jayo devāḥ śakrasya mama tejasā
|
adṛśyaśca pravekṣyāmi vajre hyasyāyudhottame ||12||

13gacchadhvamṛṣibhiḥ sārdhaṃ gandharvaiśca surottamāḥ
|
vṛtrasya saha śakreṇa sandhiṃ kuruta māciram ||13||

14śalya uvāca|
evamukte tu devena ṛṣayastridaśāstathā
|
yayuḥ sametya sahitāḥ śakraṃ kṛtvā puraḥsaram ||14||

15samīpametya ca yadā sarva eva mahaujasaḥ
|
taṃ tejasā prajvalitaṃ pratapantaṃ diśo daśa ||15||

16grasantamiva lokāṃstrīn sūryācandramasau yathā
|
dadṛśuste tato vṛtraṃ śakreṇa sahadevatāḥ ||16||

17ṛṣayo'tha tato'bhyetya vṛtramūcuḥ priyaṃ vacaḥ
|
vyāptaṃ jagadidaṃ sarvaṃ tejasā tava durjaya ||17||

18na ca śaknoṣi nirjeṣuṃ vāsavaṃ balināṃ vara
|
yudhyatoścāpi vāṃ kālo vyatītaḥ sumahāniha ||18||

19pīḍyante ca prajāḥ sarvāḥ sadevāsuramānuṣāḥ
|
sakhyaṃ bhavatu te vṛtra śakreṇa saha nityadā ||19||

20avāpsyasi sukhaṃ tvaṃ ca śakralokāṃśca śāśvatān
|
ṛṣivākyaṃ niśamyātha vṛtraḥsa tu mahābalaḥ ||20||

21uvāca tānṛṣīn sarvān praṇamya śirasā'suraḥ
|
sarve yūyaṃ mahābhāgā gandharvāścaiva sarvaśaḥ ||21||

22yadbrūtha tacchrutaṃ sarvaṃ mamāpi śrṛṇutānaghāḥ
|
sandhiḥ kathaṃ vai bhavitā mama śakrasya cobhayoḥ
|
tejasorhi dvayordevāḥ sakhyaṃ vai bhavitā katham ||22||

23ṛṣaya ūcuḥ| sakṛt satāṃ saṅgataṃ lipsitavyaṃ tataḥ paraṃ bhavitā bhavyameva
|
nātikrāmet satpuruṣeṇa saṅgataṃ tasmāt satāṃ saṅgataṃ lipsitavyam ||23||

24dṛḍhaṃ satāṃ saṅgataṃ cāpi nityaṃ brūyāccārthaṃ hyarthakṛcchreṣu dhīraḥ
|
mahārthavatsatpuruṣeṇa saṅgataṃ tasmātsantaṃ na jighāṃseta dhīraḥ ||24||

25indraḥ satāṃ saṃmataśca nivāsaśca mahātmanām
|
satyavādī hyanindyaśca dharmavit sūkṣmaniścayaḥ ||25||

26tena te saha śakreṇa sandhirbhavatu nityadā
|
evaṃ viśvāsamāgaccha mā te'bhūdbuddhiranyathā ||26||

27śalya uvāca|
maharṣivacanaṃ śrutvā tānuvāca mahādyutiḥ
|
avaśyaṃ bhagavanto me mānanīyāstapasvinaḥ||27||

28bravīmi yadahaṃ devāstatsarvaṃ kriyate yadi
|
tataḥ sarvaṃ kariṣyāmi yadūcurmāṃ dijarṣabhāḥ ||28||

29na śuṣkeṇa na cārdreṇa nāśmanā na ca dāruṇā
|
na śastreṇa na cāstreṇa na divā na tathā niśi ||29||

30vadhyo bhaveyaṃ viprendrāḥ śakrasya saha daivataiḥ
|
evaṃ me rocate sandhiḥ śakreṇa saha nityadā ||30||

31bāḍhamityeva ṛṣayastamūcurbharatarṣabha
|
evaṃ vṛtte tu sandhāne vṛtraḥ pramudito'bhavat ||31||

32yuktaḥ sadā'bhavaccāpi śakro harṣasamanvitaḥ
|
vṛtrasya vadhasaṃyuktānupāyānanvacintayat ||32||

33chidrānveṣī samudvignaḥ sadā vasati devarāṭ
|
sa kadācit samudrānte samapaśyanmahāsuram ||33||

34sandhyākāla upāvṛtte muhūrte cātidāruṇe
|
tataḥ sañcintya bhagavān varadānaṃ mahātmanaḥ ||34||

35sandhyeyaṃ vartate raudrā na rātrirdivasaṃ na ca
|
vṛtraścāvaśyavadhyo'yaṃ mama sarvaharo ripuḥ ||35||

36yadi vṛtraṃ na hanmyadya vañcayitvā mahāsuram
|
mahābalaṃ mahākāyaṃ na me śreyo bhaviṣyati||36||

37evaṃ sañcintayanneva śakro viṣṇumanusmaran
|
atha phenaṃ tadā'paśyat samudre parvatopamap ||37||

38nāyaṃ śuṣko na cārdro'yaṃ na ca śastramidaṃ tathā
|
enaṃ kṣepsyāmi vṛtrasya kṣaṇādeva naśiṣyati||38||

39savajramatha phenaṃ taṃ kṣipraṃ vṛtre nisṛṣṭavān
|
praviśya phenaṃ taṃ viṣṇuratha vṛtraṃ vyanāśayat ||39||

40nihate tu tato vṛtre diśo vitimirā'bhavan
|
pravavau ca śivo vāyuḥ prajāśca jahṛṣustathā ||40||

41tato devāḥ sagandharvā yakṣarakṣomahoragāḥ
|
ṛṣayaśca mahendraṃ tamastuvan vividhaiḥ stavaiḥ ||41||

42namaskṛtaḥ sarvabhūtaiḥ sarvabhūtānyasāntvayat
|
hatvā śatruṃ prahṛṣṭātmā vāsavaḥ saha daivataiḥ ||42||

43viṣṇuṃ tribhuvanaśreṣṭhaṃ pūjayāmāsa dharmavit
|
tato hate mahāvīrye vṛtre devabhayaṅkare ||43||

44anṛtenābhibhūto'bhūcchakraḥ paramadurmanāḥ
|
traiśīrṣayā'bhibhūtaśca sa pūrvaṃ brahmahatyayā||44||

45so'ntamāśritya lokānāṃ naṣṭasaṃjño vicetanaḥ
|
na prājñāyata devendrastvabhibhūtaḥ svakalmaṣaiḥ ||45||

46praticchanno'vasaccāpsu ceṣṭamāna ivoragaḥ
|
tataḥ pranaṣṭe devendre brahmahatyābhayārdite ||46||

47bhūmiḥ pradhvastasaṅkāśā nirvṛkṣā śuṣkakānanā
|
vicchinnasrotaso nadyaḥ sarāṃsyanudakāni ca||47||

48saṃkṣobhaścāpi sattvānāmanāvṛṣṭikṛto'bhavat
|
devāścāpi bhṛśaṃ trastāstathā sarve maharṣayaḥ ||48||

49arājakaṃ jagatsarvamabhibhūtamupadravaiḥ
|
tato bhītā'bhavan devāḥ ko no rājā bhavediti ||49||

50divi devarṣayaścāpi devarājavinākṛtāḥ
|
na sma kaścana devānāṃ rājye vai kurute matim ||50||
