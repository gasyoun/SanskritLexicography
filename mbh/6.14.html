Mahābhārata 6.14 (Bhīṣmaparvan)

1dhṛtarāṣṭra uvāca|
kathaṃ kurūṇāmṛṣabho hato bhīṣmaḥ śikhaṇḍinā
|
kathaṃ rathāt sa nyapatatpitā me vāsavopamaḥ ||1||

2kathamācakṣva me yodhā hīnā bhīṣmeṇa sañjaya
|
balinā devakalpena gurvarthe brahmacāriṇā ||2||

3tasminhate mahāprājñe maheṣvāse mahābale
|
mahāsatve naravyāghre kimu āsīnmanastava ||3||

4ārtiṃ parāmāviśati manaḥ śaṃsasi me hatam
|
kurūṇāmṛṣabhaṃ vīramakampaṃ puruṣarṣabham ||4||

5ke taṃ yāntamanuprāptāḥ ke vāsyasanpurogamāḥ
|
ke'tiṣṭhanke nyavartanta ke'nvavartanta sañjaya ||5||

6ke śūrā rathaśārdūlamadbhutaṃ kṣatriyarṣabham
|
tathā'nīkaṃ gāhamānaṃ sahasā pṛṣṭhato'nvayuḥ ||6||

7yastamo'rka ivāpohanparasainyamamitrahā
|
sahasraraśmipratimaḥ pareṣāṃ bhayamādadhat ||7||

8akarodduṣkaraṃ karma raṇe pāṇḍusuteṣu yaḥ
|
grasamānamanīkāni ya enaṃ paryavārayan ||8||

9kṛtinaṃ taṃ durādharṣaṃ sañjayāsya tvamantike
|
kathaṃ śāntanavaṃ yuddhe pāṇḍavāḥ pratyavārayan ||9||

10nikṛntantamanīkāni śaradaṃṣṭraṃ manasvinam
|
cāpavyāttānanaṃ ghoramasijihvaṃ durāsadam ||10||

11anarhaṃ puruṣavyāghraṃ hrīmantamaparājitam
|
pātayāmāsa kaunteyaḥ kathaṃ tamajitaṃ yudhi ||11||

12ugradhanvānamugreṣuṃ vartamānaṃ rathottame
|
pareṣāmuttamāṅgāni pracinvantamatheṣubhiḥ ||12||

13pāṇḍavānāṃ mahatsainyaṃ yaṃ dṛṣṭvodyatamāhave
|
kālāgnimiva durdharṣaṃ samaceṣṭata nityaśaḥ ||13||

14parikṛṣya sa senāṃ tu daśarātramanīkahā
|
jagāmāstamivādityaḥ kṛtvā karma suduṣkaram ||14||

15yaḥ sa śakra ivākṣayyaṃ varṣaṃ śaramayaṃ kṣipan
|
jaghāna yudhi yodhānāmarbudaṃ daśabhirdinaiḥ ||15||

16sa śete nihato bhūmau vātabhugna iva drumaḥ
|
mama durmantritenājau yathā nārhati bhārataḥ ||16||

17kathaṃ śāntanavaṃ dṛṣṭvā pāṇḍavānāmanīkinī
|
prahartumaśakattatra bhīṣmaṃ bhīmaparākramam ||17||

18kathaṃ bhīṣmeṇa saṅgrāmaṃ prākurvanpāṇḍunandanāḥ
|
kathaṃ ca nājayadbhīṣmo droṇe jīvati sañjaya ||18||

19kṛpe sannihite tatra bharadvājātmaje tathā
|
bhīṣmaḥ praharatāṃ śreṣṭhaḥ kathaṃ sa nidhanaṃ gataḥ ||19||

20kathaṃ cātirathastena pāñcālyena śikhaṇḍinā
|
bhīṣmo vinihato yuddhe devairapi durāsadaḥ ||20||

21yaḥ spardhate raṇe nityaṃ jāmadagnyaṃ mahābalam
|
ajitaṃ jāmadagnyena śakratulyaparākramam ||21||

22taṃ hataṃ samare bhīṣmaṃ mahārathakuloditam
|
sañjayācakṣva me vīraṃ yena śarma na vidmahe ||22||

23māmakāḥ ke maheṣvāsā nājahuḥ sañjayācyutam
|
duryodhanasamādiṣṭāḥ ke vīrāḥ paryavārayan ||23||

24yacchikhaṇḍimukhāḥ sarve pāṇḍavā bhīṣmamabhyayuḥ
|
kaccitte kuravaḥ sarve nājahuḥ sañjayācyutam ||24||

25aśmasāramayaṃ nūnaṃ hṛdayaṃ mudṛḍhaṃ mama
|
yacchrutvā puruṣavyāghraṃ hataṃ bhīṣmaṃ na dīryate ||25||

26yasminsatyaṃ ca medhā ca nītiśca bharatarṣabhe
|
aprameyāṇi durdharṣe kathaṃ sa nihato yudhi ||26||

27maurvīghoṣastanayutnuḥ pṛṣatkapṛṣato mahān
|
dhanurhrādamahāśabdo mahāmegha ivonnataḥ ||27||

28yo'bhyavarṣata kaunteyānsapāñcālānsasṛñjayān
|
nighnanparathānvīro dānavāniva vajrabhṛt ||28||

29iṣvastrasāgaraṃ ghoraṃ bāṇagrahaṃ durāsadam
|
kārmukormiṇamakṣayyamadvīpaṃ calamaplavam ||29||

30gadāsimakarāvāsaṃ hayāvarttaṃ gajākulam
|
padātimatsyakalilaṃ śaṅkhadundubhiniḥsvanam ||30||

31hayāngajapadātīṃśca rathāṃśca tarasā bahūn
|
nimajjayantaṃ samare paravīrāpahāriṇam ||31||

32vidahyamānaṃ kopena tejasā ca parantapam
|
veleva makarāvāsaṃ ke vīrāḥ paryavārayan ||32||

33bhīṣmo yadakarotkarma samare sañjayārihā
|
duryodhanahitārthāya ke tasyāsya puro'bhavan ||33||

34ke rakṣandakṣiṇaṃ cakraṃ bhīṣmasyāmitatejasaḥ
|
pṛṣṭhataḥ ke parānvīrānapāsedhanyatavratāḥ ||34||

35ke purastādavartanta rakṣanto bhīṣmamantike
|
ke rakṣannuttaraṃ cakraṃ vīrā vīrasya yudhyataḥ ||35||

36vāme cakre vartamānāḥ ke'ghnansañjaya sṛñjayān
|
agrato'gryamanīkeṣu ke'bhyarakṣandurāsadam ||36||

37pārśvataḥ ke'bhyarakṣanta gacchanto durgamāṃ gatim
|
samūhe ke parānvīrānpratyayudhyanta sañjaya ||37||

38rakṣyamāṇaḥ kathaṃ vīrairgopyamānāśca tena te
|
durjayānāmanīkāni nājayaṃstarasā yudhi ||38||

39sarvalokeśvarasyeva parameṣṭhī prajāpateḥ
|
kathaṃ prahartumapi te śekuḥ sañjaya pāṇḍavāḥ ||39||

40yasmindvīpe samāśvasya yudhyante kuravaḥ paraiḥ
|
taṃ nimagnaṃ naravyāghraṃ bhīṣmaṃ śaṃsasi sañjaya ||40||

41yasya vīryaṃ samāśritya mama putro bṛhadbalaḥ
|
na pāṇḍavānagaṇayatkathaṃ sa nihataḥ paraiḥ ||41||

42yaḥ purā vibudhaiḥ sarvaiḥ sahāye yuddhadurmadaḥ
|
kāṅkṣito dānavāndhnadbhiḥ pitā mama mahāvrataḥ ||42||

43yasminjāte mahāvīrye śāntanurlokaviśrutaḥ
|
śokaṃ dainyaṃ ca duḥkhaṃ ca prājahāt putralakṣmaṇi ||43||

44proktaṃ parāyaṇaṃ prājñaṃ svadharmanirataṃ śucim
|
vedavedāṅgatatvajñaṃ kathaṃ śaṃsasi me hatam ||44||

45sarvāstravinayopetaṃ śāntaṃ dāntaṃ manasvinam
|
hataṃ śāntanavaṃ śrutvā manye śeṣaṃ hataṃ balam ||45||

46dharmādadharmo balavānsamprāpta iti me matiḥ
|
yatra vṛddhaṃ guruṃ hatvā rājyamicchanti pāṇḍavāḥ ||46||

47jāmadagnyaḥ purā rāmaḥ sarvāstravidanuttamaḥ
|
ambārthamudyataḥ saṅkhye bhīṣmeṇa yudhi nirjitaḥ ||47||

48tamindrasamakarmāṇaṃ kakudaṃ sarvadhanvinām
|
hataṃ śaṃsasi me bhīṣmaṃ kiṃ nu duḥkhamataḥ param ||48||

49asakṛtkṣatriyavrātāḥ saṅkhye yena vinirjitāḥ
|
jāmadagnyena vīreṇa paravīranighātinā ||49||

50na hato yo mahābuddhiḥ sa hato'dya śikhaṇḍinā
|
tasmānnūnaṃ mahāvīryādbhārgavādyuddhadurmadāt ||50||

51tejovīryabalairyūyān śikhaṇḍī drupadātmajaḥ
|
yaḥ śūraṃ kṛtinaṃ yuddhe sarvaśastraviśāradam ||51||

52paramāstravidaṃ vīraṃ jaghāna bhagtarṣabham
|
ke vīrāstamamitraghnamanvayuḥ śastrasaṃsadi ||52||

53śaṃsa me tadyathā cāsīdyudbhaṃ bhīṣmasya pāṇḍavaiḥ
|
yoṣeva hatavīrā me senā putrasya sañjaya ||53||

54agopamiva codbhrāntaṃ gokulaṃ tadbalaṃ mama
|
pauruṣaṃ sarvalokasya paraṃ yasminmahāhave ||54||

55parāsakte ca vastasminkathamāsīnmanastadā
|
jīvite'pyadya sāmarthyaṃ kimivāsmāsu sañjaya ||55||

56ghātayitvā mahāvīryaṃ pitaraṃ lokadhārmikam
|
agādhe salile magnāṃ nāvaṃ dṛṣṭveva pāragāḥ ||56||

57bhīṣme hate bhṛśaṃ duḥkhānmanye śocanti putrakāḥ
|
adrisāramayaṃ nūnaṃ hṛdayaṃ mama sañjaya ||57||

58yacchrutvā puruṣavyāghraṃ hataṃ bhīṣmaṃ na dīryate
|
yasminnastrāṇi medhā ca nītiśca puruṣarṣabhe ||58||

59aprameyāṇi durdharṣe kathaṃ sa nihato yudhi
|
na cāstreṇa na śauryeṇa tapasā medhayā na ca ||59||

60na dhṛtyā na punastyāgānmṛtyoḥ kaścidvimucyate
|
kālo nūnaṃ mahāvīryaḥ sarvalokaduratyayaḥ ||60||

61yatra śāntanavaṃ bhīṣmaṃ hataṃ śaṃsasi sañjaya
|
putraśokābhisantapto mahadduḥkhamacintayan ||61||

62āśaṃse'haṃ paraṃ trāṇaṃ bhīṣmācchāntanunandanāt
|
yadādityamivāpaśyatpatitaṃ bhuvi sañjaya ||62||

63duryodhanaḥ śāntanavaṃ kiṃ tadā pratyapadmata
|
nāhaṃ sveṣāṃ pareṣāṃ vā buddhyā sañjaya cintayan ||63||

64śeṣaṃ kiñcitprapaśyāmi pratyanīke mahīkṣitām
|
dāruṇaḥ kṣatradharmo'yamṛṣibhiḥ sampradarśitaḥ ||64||

65yatra śāntanavaṃ hatvā rājyamicchanti pāṇḍavāḥ
|
vayaṃ vā rājyamicchāmo ghātayitvā mahāvratam ||65||

66kṣatradharme sthitāḥ pārthā nāparādhyanti putrakāḥ
|
etadāryeṇa kartavyaṃ kṛcchrāsvāpatsu sañjaya ||66||

67parākramaḥ parāśaktistattu tasminpratiṣṭhitam
|
anīkāni vinighnantaṃ hrīmantamaparājitam ||67||

68kathaṃ śāntanavaṃ tātaṃ pāṇḍuputrā nyavārayan || yathāyuktānyanīkāni kathaṃ yuddhaṃ mahātmabhiḥ ||68||

69kathaṃ vā nihato bhīṣmaḥ pitā sañjaya me paraiḥ
|
duryodhanaśca karṇaśca śakuniścāpi saubalaḥ ||69||

70duḥśāsanaśca kitavo hate bhīṣme kimabruvan
|
yaccharīrairupāstīrṇāṃ naravāraṇavājinām ||70||

71śaraśaktimahākhaṅgatomarākṣāṃ mahābhayām
|
prāviśankitavā mandāḥ sabhāṃ yuddhadurāsadām ||71||

72prāṇadyūte pratibhaye ke'dīvyanta nararṣabhāḥ
|
ke jīyante jitāstatra kṛtalakṣyā nipātitāḥ ||72||

73anye bhīṣmācchāntanavāttanmamācakṣva sañjaya
|
na hi me śāntirastīha śrutvā devavrataṃ hatam ||73||

74pitaraṃ bhīmakarmāṇaṃ bhīṣmamāhavaśobhinam || ārtiṃ me hṛdaye rūḍhāṃ mahatīṃ putrahānijām ||74||

75tvaṃ hi me sarpiṣevāgnimuddīpayasi sañjaya
|
mahāntaṃ bhāramudyamya viśrutaṃ sārvalaukikam ||75||

76dṛṣṭvā vinihataṃ bhīṣmaṃ manye śocanti putrakāḥ
|
śroṣyāmi tāni duḥkhāni duryodhanakṛtānyaham ||76||

77tasmānme sarvamācakṣva yadvṛttaṃ tatra sañjaya
|
yadvṛttaṃ tatra saṅgrāme mandasyābuddhisambhavam ||77||

78apanītaṃ sunītaṃ yattanmamācakṣva sañjaya
|
yatkṛtaṃ tatra saṅgrāme bhīṣmeṇa jayamicchatā ||78||

79tejoyuktaṃ kṛtāstreṇa śaṃsa taccāpyaśeṣataḥ
|
yathā tadabhavadyuddhaṃ kurupāṇḍavasenayoḥ ||79||

80krameṇa yena yasmiṃśca kāle yacca yathā'bhavat ||80||
