Mahābhārata 14.81 (Āśvamedhikaparvan)

1arjuna uvāca|
kimāgamanakṛtyaṃ te kauravyakulanandini
|
maṇipūrapatermātastathaiva ca raṇājire||1||

2kaccitkuśalakāmāsi rājño'sya bhujagātmaje
|
mama vā capalāpāṅgi kaccittvaṃ śubhamicchasi||2||

3kaccitte pṛthulaśroṇi nāpriyaṃ priyadarśane
|
akārṣamahamajñānādayaṃ vā babhruvāhanaḥ||3||

4kaccinnu rājaputrī te sapatnī caitravāhanī
|
citrāṅgadā varārohā nāparādhyati kiñcana||4||

5tamuvācoragapaterduhitā prahasanniva
|
na me tvamaparāddho'si na hi me babhruvāhanaḥ||5||

6na janitrī tathāsyeyaṃ mama yā preṣyavat sthitā
|
śrūyatāṃ yadyathā cedaṃ mayā sarvaṃ viceṣṭitam||6||

7na me kopastvayā kāryaḥ śirasā tvāṃ prasādaye
|
tvatpriyārthaṃ hi kauravya kṛtametanmayā vibho||7||

8yattacchṛṇu mahābāho nikhilena dhanañjaya
|
mahābhāratayuddhe yattvayā śāntanavo nṛpaḥ||8||

9adharmeṇa hataḥ pārtha tasyaiṣā niṣkṛtiḥ kṛtā
|
na hi bhīṣmastvayā vīra yuddhyamāno hi pātitaḥ||9||

10śikhaṇḍinā tu saṃyuktastamāśritya hatastvayā
|
tasya śāntimakṛtvā tvaṃ tyajethā yadi jīvitam||10||

11karmaṇā tena pāpena patethā niraye dhruvam
|
eṣā tu vihitā śāntiḥ putrādyāṃ prāptavānasi
|
vasubhirvasudhāpāla gaṅgayā ca mahāmate||11||

12purā hi śrutametatte vasubhiḥ kathitaṃ mayā
|
gaṅgāyāstīramāśritya hate śāntanave nṛpa||12||

13āplutya devā vasavaḥ sametya ca mahānadīm
|
idamūcurvaco ghoraṃ bhāgīrathyā mate tadā||13||

14eṣa śāntanavo bhīṣmo nihataḥ savyasācinā
|
ayudhyamānaḥ saṅgrāme saṃsakto'nyena bhāvini
|
tadanenānuṣaṅgeṇa vayamadya dhanañjayam||14||

15śāpena yojayāmeti tathāstviti ca sābravīt
|
tadahaṃ piturāvedya praviśya vyathitendriyā||15||

16abhavaṃ sa ca tacchrutvā viṣādamagamatparam
|
pitā tu me vasūn gatvā tvadarthe samayācata||16||

17punaḥ punaḥ prasādyaitāṃsta enamidamabruvan
|
putrastasya mahābhāga maṇipūreśvaro yuvā||17||

18sa enaṃ raṇamadhyasthaḥ śaraiḥ pātayitā bhuvi
|
evaṃ kṛte sa nāgendra muktaśāpo bhaviṣyati||18||

19gaccheti vasubhiścokto mama cedaṃ śaśaṃsa saḥ
|
tacchrutvātvaṃ mayā tasmācchāpādasi vimokṣitaḥ||19||

20na hi tvāṃ devarājo'pi samareṣu parājayet
|
ātmā putraḥ smṛtastasmāttenehāsi parājitaḥ||20||

21na hi doṣo mama mataḥ kathaṃ vā manyase vibho
|
ityevamukto vijayaḥ prasannātmābravīdidam||21||

22sarvaṃ me supriyaṃ devi yadetatkṛtavatyasi
|
ityuktvā so'bravītputraṃ maṇipūrapatiṃ jayaḥ||22||

23citrāṅgadāyāḥ śṛṇvatyāḥ kauravyaduhitustadā
|
yudhiṣṭhirasyāśvamedhaḥ paricaitrī bhaviṣyati||23||

24tatrāgaccheḥ sahāmātyo mātṛbhyāṃ sahito nṛpa||24||

25ityevamuktaḥ pārthena sa rājā babhruvāhanaḥ
|
uvāca pitaraṃ dhīmānidamasrāvilekṣaṇaḥ||25||

26upayāsyāmi dharmajña bhavataḥ śāsanādaham
|
aśvamedhe mahāyajñe dvijātipariveṣakaḥ||26||

27mama tvanugrahārthāya praviśasva puraṃ svakam
|
bhāryābhyāṃ saha dharmajña mā bhūtte'tra vicāraṇā||27||

28uṣitveha niśāmekāṃ sukhaṃ svabhavane prabho
|
punaraśvānugamanaṃ kartāsi jayatāṃ vara||28||

29ityuktaḥ sa tu putreṇa tadā vānaraketanaḥ
|
smayanprovāca kaunteyastadā citrāṅgadāsutam||29||

30viditaṃ te mahābāho yathā dīkṣāṃ carāmyaham
|
na sa tāvatpravekṣyāmi puraṃ te pṛthulocana||30||

31yathākāmaṃ vrajatyeṣa yajñiyāśvo nararṣabha
|
svasti te'stu gamiṣyāmi na sthānaṃ vidyate mama||31||

32sa tatra vidhivattena pūjita: pākaśāsaniḥ
|
bhāryābhyāmabhyanujñātaḥ prāyādbharatasattamaḥ||32||
