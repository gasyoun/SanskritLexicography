Mahābhārata 14.91 (Āśvamedhikaparvan)

1janamejaya uvāca|
yajñe saktā nṛpatayastapaḥsaktā maharṣayaḥ
|
śāntivyavasthitā viprāḥ śame dama iti prabho||1||

2tasmādyajñaphalaistulyaṃ na kiñcidiha dṛśyate
|
iti me vartate buddhistathā caitadasaṃśayam||2||

3yajñairiṣṭvā tu bahavo rājāno dvijasattamāḥ
|
iha kīrtiṃ parāṃ prāpya pretya svargamavāpnuyuḥ||3||

4devarājaḥ sahasrākṣaḥ kratubhirbhūridakṣiṇaiḥ
|
devarājyaṃ mahātejāḥ prāptavānakhilaṃ vibhuḥ||4||

5yadā yudhiṣṭhiro rājā bhīmārjuna puraḥsaraḥ
|
sadṛśo devarājena samṛddhyā vikrameṇa ca||5||

6atha kasmātsa nakulo garhayāmāsa taṃ kratum
|
aśvamedhaṃ mahāyajñaṃ rājñastasya mahātmanaḥ||6||

7vaiśampāyana uvāca|
yajñasya vidhimagryaṃ vai phalaṃ cāpi narādhipa
|
gadataḥ śṛṇu me rājanyathāvadiha bhārata||7||

8purā śakrasya yajataḥ sarva ūcurmaharṣayaḥ
|
ṛtvikṣu karmavyagreṣu vitate yajñakarmaṇi||8||

9hūyamāne tathā vahnau hotre guṇasamanvite
|
deveṣvāhūyamāneṣu sthiteṣu paramarṣiṣu||9||

10supratītaistathā vipraiḥ svāgamaiḥ susvarairnṛpa
|
aśrāntaiścāpi laghubhiradhvaryuvṛṣabhaistathā||10||

11ālambhasamaye tasmin gṛhīteṣu paśuṣvatha
|
maharṣayo mahārāja babhūvuḥ kṛpayānvitāḥ||11||

12tato dīnānpaśūndṛṣṭvā ṛṣayaste tapodhanāḥ
|
ūcuḥ śakraṃ samāgamya nāyaṃ yajñavidhiḥ śubhaḥ||12||

13aparijñānametatte mahāntaṃ dharmamicchataḥ
|
na hi yajñe paśugaṇa vidhidṛṣṭāḥ purandara||13||

14dharmopaghātakastveṣa samārambhastava prabho
|
nāyaṃ dharmakṛto yajño na hiṃsā dharma ucyate||14||

15āgamenaiva te yajñaṃ kurvantu yadi cecchasi||15||

16vidhidṛṣṭena yajñena dharmaste sumahānbhavet
|
yaja bījaiḥ sahasrākṣa trivarṣaparamoṣitaiḥ||16||

17eṣa dharmo mahān śakra mahāguṇaphalodayaḥ
|
śatakratustu tadvākyamṛṣibhistattvadarśibhiḥ||17||

18uktaṃ na pratijagrāha mānānmohavaśaṃ gataḥ
|
teṣāṃ vivādaḥ sumahān śakrayajñe tapasvinām||18||

19jaṅgamaiḥ sthāvarairvāpi yaṣṭavyamiti bhārata
|
te tu khinnā vivādena ṛṣayastattvadarśinaḥ||19||

20tadā sandhāya śakreṇa papraccharnṛpatiṃ vasum
|
dharmasaṃśayamāpannānsatyaṃ brūhi mahāmate||20||

21mahābhāga kathaṃ yajñeṣvāgamo nṛpasattama
|
yaṣṭavyaṃ paśubhirmukhyairatho bījai rasairiti||21||

22tacchrutvā tu vasusteṣāmavicārya balābalam
|
yathopanītairyaṣṭavyamiti provāca pārthivaḥ||22||

23evamuktvā sa nṛpatiḥ praviveśa rasātalam
|
uktvātha vitathaṃ praśnaṃ cedīnāmīśvaraḥ prabhuḥ||23||

24tasmānna vācyaṃ hyekena bahujñenāpi saṃśaye
|
prajāpatimapāhāya svayambhuvamṛte prabhum||24||

25tena dattāni dānāni pāpenāśuddhabuddhinā
|
tāni sarvānādṛtya naśyanti vipulānyapi||25||

26tasyādharmapravṛttasya hiṃsakasya durātmanaḥ
|
dānena kīrtirbhavati na pretyeha ca durmateḥ||26||

27anyāyopagataṃ dravyamabhīkṣṇaṃ yo hapaṇḍitaḥ
|
dharmābhiśaṅkī yajate na sa dharmaphalaṃ labhet||27||

28dharmavaitaṃsiko yastu pāpātmā puruṣādhamaḥ
|
dadāti dānaṃ viprebhyo lokaviśvāsakāraṇam||28||

29pāpena karmaṇā vipro dhanaṃ prāpya niraṅkuśaḥ
|
rāgamohānvitaḥ so'nte kaluṣāṃ gatimaśnute||29||

30api sañcayabuddhirhi lobhamohavaśaṅgataḥ
|
udvejayati bhūtāni pāpenāśuddhabuddhinā||30||

31evaṃ labdhvā dhanaṃ mohādyo hi dadyādyajeta vā
|
na tasya sa phalaṃ pretya bhuṅkte pāpadhanāgamāt||31||

32uñchaṃ mūlaṃ phalaṃ śākamudapātraṃ tapodhanāḥ
|
dānaṃ vibhavato datvā narāḥ svaryānti dhārmikāḥ||32||

33eṣa dharmo mahāyogo dānaṃ bhūtadayā tathā
|
brahmacaryaṃ tathā satyamanukrośo dhṛtiḥ kṣamā||33||

34sanātanasya dharmasya mūlametatsanātanam
|
śrūyante hi purā vṛttā viśvāmitrādayo nṛpāḥ||34||

35viśvāmitro'sitaścaiva janakaśca mahīpati
|
kakṣasenārṣṭisenau ca sindhudvīpaśca pārthivaḥ||35||

36ete cānye ca bahavaḥ siddhiṃ paramikāṃ gatāḥ
|
nṛpāḥ satyaiśca dānaiśca nyāyalabdhaistapodhanāḥ||36||

37brāhmaṇāḥ kṣatriyā vaiśyāḥ śūdrā ye cāśritāstapaḥ
|
dānadharmāgninā śuddhāste svargaṃ yānti bhārata||37||
