Mahābhārata 12.301 (Śāntiparvan)

1yudhiṣṭhira uvāca|
samyak tvayā'yaṃ nṛpate varṇitaḥ śiṣṭasammataḥ
|
yogamārgo yathānyāyaṃ śiṣyāyeha hitaiṣiṇā||1||

2sāṃkhye tvidānīṃ kārtsnyena vidhiṃ prabrūhi pṛcchate
|
triṣu lokeṣu yajjñānaṃ sarvaṃ tadviditaṃ hi te||2||

3bhīṣma uvāca|
śṛṇu me tvamidaṃ sūkṣmaṃ sāṃkhyānāṃ viditātmanām
|
vihitaṃ yatibhiḥ sarvaiḥ kapilādibhirīśvaraiḥ||3||

4yasminna vibhramāḥ kecid dṛśyante manujarṣabha
|
guṇāśca yasminbahavo doṣahāniśca kevalā||4||

5jñānena parisaṃkhyāya sadoṣānviṣayānnṛpa
|
mānuṣāndurjayānkṛtsnānpaiśācānviṣayāṃstathā||5||

6rākṣasānviṣayānjñātvā yakṣāṇāṃ viṣayāṃstathā
|
viṣayānauragānjñātvā gāndharvaviṣayāṃstathā||6||

7pitṝṇāṃ viṣayānjñātvā tiryakṣu caratāṃ nṛpa
|
suparṇaviṣayānjñātvā marutāṃ viṣayāṃstathā||7||

8rājarṣiviṣayānjñātvā brahmarṣiviṣayāṃstathā
|
āsurānviṣayānjñātvā vaiśvadevāṃstathaiva ca||8||

9devarṣiviṣayānjñātvā yogānāmapi ceśvarān
|
prajāpatīnāṃ viṣayānbrahmaṇo viṣayāṃstathā||9||

10āyuṣaśca paraṃ kālaṃ loke vijñāya tattvataḥ
|
sukhasya ca paraṃ tattvaṃ vijñāya vadatāṃ vara||10||

11prāpte kāle ca yadduḥkhaṃ satataṃ viṣayaiṣiṇām
|
tiryakṣu patatāṃ duḥkhaṃ patatāṃ narake ca yat||11||

12svargasya ca guṇānkṛtsnāndoṣānsarvāṃśca bhārata
|
vedavāde'pi ye doṣā guṇā ye cāpi vaidikāḥ||12||

13jñānayoge ca ye doṣā guṇā yoge ca ye nṛpa
|
sāṃkhyajñāne ca ye doṣāstathaiva ca guṇā nṛpa||13||

14sattvaṃ daśaguṇaṃ jñātvā rajo navaguṇaṃ tathā
|
tamaścāṣṭaguṇaṃ jñātvā buddhiṃ saptaguṇāṃ tathā||14||

15ṣaḍguṇaṃ ca mano jñātvā nabhaḥ pañcaguṇaṃ tathā
|
buddhiṃ caturguṇāṃ jñātvā tamaśca triguṇaṃ tathā||15||

16dviguṇaṃ ca rajo jñātvā sattvamekaguṇaṃ punaḥ
|
mārgaṃ vijñāya tattvena pralaye prekṣaṇe tathā||16||

17jñānavijñānasaṃpannāḥ kāraṇairbhāvitāḥ śubhāḥ
|
prāpnuvanti śubhaṃ mokṣaṃ sūkṣmā iva nabhaḥ param||17||

18rūpeṇa dṛṣṭiṃ saṃyuktāṃ ghrāṇaṃ gandhaguṇena ca
|
śabde saktaṃ tathā śrotraṃ jihvā rasaguṇeṣu ca||18||

19tanuṃ sparśe tathā saktāṃ vāyuṃ nabhasi cāśritam
|
mohaṃ tamasi saṃyuktaṃ lobhamartheṣu saṃśritam||19||

20viṣṇuṃ krānte bale śakraṃ koṣṭhe saktaṃ tathānalam
|
apsu devīṃ samāsaktāmapastejasi saṃśritāḥ||20||

21tejo vāyau tu saṃsaktaṃ vāyuṃ nabhasi cāśritam
|
nabho mahati saṃyuktaṃ mahadbuddhau ca saṃśritam||21||

22buddhiṃ tamasi saṃsaktāṃ tamo rajasi saṃśritam
|
rajaḥ sattve tathā saktaṃ sattvaṃ saktaṃ tathā''tmani||22||

23saktamātmānamīśe ca deve nārāyaṇe tathā
|
devaṃ mokṣe ca saṃsaktaṃ mokṣaṃ saktaṃ tu na kvacit||23||

24jñātvā sattvaguṇaṃ dehaṃ vṛtaṃ ṣoḍaśabhirguṇaiḥ
|
svabhāvaṃ cetanāṃ caiva jñātvā dehasamāśrite||24||

25madhyasthamekamātmānaṃ pāpaṃ yasminna vidyate
|
dvitīyaṃ karma vijñāya nṛpate viṣayaiṣiṇām||25||

26indriyāṇindriyārthāṃśca sarvānātmani saṃśritān
|
durlabhatvaṃ ca mokṣasya vijñāya śrutipūrvakam||26||

27prāṇāpānau samānaṃ ca vyānodānau ca tattvataḥ
|
adhaścaivānilaṃ jñātvā pravahaṃ cānilaṃ punaḥ||27||

28sapta vātāṃstathā jñātvā saptadhā pihitānpunaḥ
|
prajāpatīnṛṣīṃścaiva mārgāṃścaiva bahūnvarān||28||

29saptarṣīṃśca bahūnjñātvā rājarṣīṃśca paraṃtapa
|
surarṣīnmahataścānyānbrahmarṣīnsūryasannibhān||29||

30aiśvaryāccyāvitāndṛṣṭvā kālena mahatā nṛpa
|
mahatāṃ bhūtasaṃghānāṃ śrutvā nāśaṃ ca pārthiva||30||

31gatiṃ cāpyaśubhāṃ jñātvā nṛpate pāpakarmiṇām
|
vaitaraṇyāṃ ca yadduḥkhaṃ patitānāṃ yamakṣaye||31||

32yonīṣu ca vicitrāsu saṃsārānaśubhāṃstathā
|
jaṭhare cāśubhe vāsaṃ śoṇitodakabhājane||32||

33śleṣmamūtrapurīṣe ca tīvragandhasamanvite
|
śukraśoṇitasaṃghāte majjāsnāyuparigrahe||33||

34śirāśatasamākīrṇe navadvāre pure'śucau
|
vijñāya hitamātmānaṃ yogāṃśca vividhānnṛpa||34||

35tāmasānāṃ ca jantūnāṃ ramaṇīyāvṛtātmanām
|
sāttvikānāṃ ca jantūnāṃ kutsitaṃ bharatarṣabha||35||

36garhitaṃ mahatāmarthe sāṃkhyānāṃ viditātmanām
|
upaplavāṃstathā ghorān śaśinastejasastathā||36||

37tārāṇāṃ patanaṃ dṛṣṭvā nakṣatrāṇāṃ ca paryayam
|
dvandvānāṃ viprayogaṃ ca vijñāya kṛpaṇaṃ nṛpa||37||

38anyonyabhakṣaṇaṃ dṛṣṭvā bhūtānāmapi cāśubham
|
bālye mohaṃ ca vijñāya kṣayaṃ dehasya cāśubham||38||

39rāge mohe ca samprāpte kvacitsattvaṃ samāśritam
|
sahasreṣu naraḥ kaścinmokṣabuddhiṃ samāśritaḥ||39||

40durlabhatvaṃ ca mokṣasya vijñāya śrutipūrvakam
|
bahumānamalabdheṣu labdhe madhyasthatāṃ punaḥ||40||

41viṣayāṇāṃ ca daurātmyaṃ vijñāya nṛpate punaḥ
|
gatāsūnāṃ ca kaunteya dehāndṛṣṭvā tathā'śubhān||41||

42vāsaṃ kuleṣu jantūnāṃ duḥkhaṃ vijñāya bhārata
|
brahmaghnānāṃ gatiṃ jñātvā patitānāṃ sudāruṇām||42||

43surāpāne ca saktānāṃ brāhmaṇānāṃ durātmanām
|
gurudāraprasaktānāṃ gatiṃ vijñāya cāśubhām||43||

44jananīṣu ca vartante ye na samyagyudhiṣṭhira
|
sadevakeṣu lokeṣu yena vartanti mānavāḥ||44||

45tena jñānena vijñāya gatiṃ cāśubhakarmaṇām
|
tiryagyonigatānāṃ ca vijñāya gatayaḥ pṛthak||45||

46vedavādāṃstathā citrānṛtūnāṃ paryayāṃstathā
|
kṣayaṃ saṃvatsarāṇāṃ ca māsānāṃ ca kṣayaṃ tathā||46||

47pakṣakṣayaṃ tathā dṛṣṭvā divasānāṃ ca saṃkṣayam
|
kṣayaṃ vṛddhiṃ ca candrasya dṛṣṭvā pratyakṣatastathā||47||

48vṛddhiṃ dṛṣṭvā samudrāṇāṃ kṣayaṃ teṣāṃ tathā punaḥ
|
kṣayaṃ dhanānāṃ dṛṣṭvā ca punarvṛddhiṃ tathaiva ca||48||

49saṃyogānāṃ kṣayaṃ dṛṣṭvā yugānāṃ ca viśeṣataḥ
|
kṣayaṃ ca dṛṣṭvā śailānāṃ kṣayaṃ ca saritāṃ tathā||49||

50varṇānāṃ ca kṣayaṃ dṛṣṭvā kṣayāntaṃ ca punaḥ punaḥ
|
jarāmṛtyustathā janma dṛṣṭvā duḥkhāni caiva ha||50||

51dehadoṣāṃstathā jñātvā teṣāṃ duḥkhaṃ ca tattvataḥ
|
dehaviklavatāṃ caiva samyagvijñāya tattvataḥ||51||

52ātmadoṣāṃśca vijñāya sarvānātmani saṃśritān
|
svadehādutthitān gandhāṃstathā vijñāya cāśubhān||52||

53yudhiṣṭhira uvāca|
kānsvagātrodbhavāndoṣānpaśyasyamitavikram
|
etanme saṃśayaṃ kṛtsnaṃ vaktumarhasi tattvataḥ||53||

54bhīṣma uvāca|
pañca doṣānprabho dehe pravadanti manīṣiṇaḥ
|
mārgajñāḥ kāpilāḥ sāṃkhyāḥ śṛṇu tānarisūdana||54||

55kāmakrodhau bhayaṃ nidrā pañcamaḥ śvāsa ucyate||55||

56ete doṣāḥ śarīreṣu dṛśyante sarvadehinām
|
chindanti kṣamayā krodhaṃ kāmaṃ saṃkalpavarjanāt||56||

57sattvasaṃsevanānnidrāmapramādādbhayaṃ tathā
|
chindanti pañcamaṃ śvāsamalpāhāratayā nṛpa||57||

58guṇānguṇaśatairjñātvā doṣāndoṣaśatairapi
|
hetūnhetuśataiścitraiścitrānvijñāya tattvataḥ||58||

59apāṃ phenopamaṃ lokaṃ viṣṇormāyāśatairvṛtam
|
citrabhittipratīkāśaṃ nalasāramanarthakam||59||

60tamaḥ śvabhranibhaṃ dṛṣṭvā varṣabudbudasaṃnibham
|
nāśaprāyaṃ sukhāddhīnaṃ nāśottaramihāvaśam||60||

61rajastamasi sammagnaṃ paṅke dvipamivāvaśam
|
sāṃkhyā rājanmahāprājñāstyaktvā snehaṃ prajākṛtam||61||

62jñānayogena sāṃkhyena vyāpinā mahatā nṛpa
|
rājasānaśubhān gandhāṃstāmasāṃśca tathāvidhān||62||

63puṇyāṃśca sāttvikān gandhān sarśajāndehasaṃśritān
|
chittvā'śu jñānaśastreṇa tapodaṇḍena bhārata||63||

64tato duḥkhodakaṃ ghoraṃ cintāśokamahāhradam
|
vyādhimṛtyumahāgrāhaṃ mahābhayamahoragam||64||

65tamaḥkūrmaṃ rajomīnaṃ prajñayā santarantyuta
|
snehapaṅkaṃ jarādurgaṃ jñānadīpamarindama||65||

66karmāgādhaṃ satyatīraṃ sthitavratamarindama
|
hiṃsāśīghramahāvegaṃ nānārasasamākaram||66||

67nānāprītimahāratnaṃ duḥkhajvarasamīraṇam
|
śokatṛṣṇāmahāvartaṃ tīkṣṇavyādhimahāgajam||67||

68asthisaṃghātasaṃghaṭṭāṃ śleṣmaphenamarindama
|
dānamuktākaraṃ ghoraṃ śoṇitahradavidrumam||68||

69hasitotkruṣṭanirghoṣaṃ nānājñānasudustaram
|
rodanāśrumalakṣāraṃ saṃgatyāgaparāyaṇam||69||

70putradārajalaukaughaṃ mitrabāndhavapattanam
|
ahiṃsāsatyamaryādaṃ prāṇatyāgamahormiṇam||70||

71vedāntagamanadvīpaṃ sarvabhūtadayodadhim
|
mokṣadurlābhaviṣayaṃ vaḍavāmukhasāgaram||71||

72taranti yatayaḥ siddhā jñānayānena bhārata
|
tīrtvā'tidustaraṃ janma viśanti vimalaṃ nabhaḥ||72||

73tatra tānsukṛtīnsāṃkhyān sūryo vahati raśmibhiḥ
|
padmatantuvadāviśya pravahanviṣayānnṛpa||73||

74tatra tānpravaho vāyuḥ pratigṛhṇāti bhārata
|
vītarāgānyatīn siddhān vīryayuktāṃstapodhanān||74||

75sūkṣmaḥ śītaḥ sugandhī ca sukhasparśaśca bhārata
|
saptānāṃ marutāṃ śreṣṭho lokāngacchati yaḥ śubhān
|
sa tānvahati kaunteya nabhasaḥ paramāṃ gatim||75||

76nabho vahati lokeśa rajasaḥ paramāṃ gatim
|
rajo vahati rājendra sattvasya paramāṃ gatim||76||

77sattvaṃ vahati śuddhātmanparaṃ nārāyaṇaṃ prabhum
|
prabhurvahati śuddhātmā paramātmānamātmanā||77||

78paramātmānamāsādya tadbhūtāyatanā'malāḥ
|
amṛtattvāya kalpante na nivartanti vā vibho||78||

79paramā sā gatiḥ pārtha nirdvandvānāṃ mahātmanām
|
satyārjavaratānāṃ vai sarvabhūtadayāvatām||79||

80yudhiṣṭhira uvāca|
sthānamuttamamāsādya bhagavantaṃ sthiravratāḥ
|
ājanmamaraṇaṃ vā te smarantyuta na vā'nagha||80||

81yadatra tathyaṃ tanme tvaṃ yathāvadvaktumarhasi
|
tvadṛte puruṣaṃ nānyaṃ praṣṭumarhāmi kaurava||81||

82mokṣe doṣo mahāneṣa prāpya siddhigatānṛṣīn
|
yadi tatraiva vijñāne vartante yatayaḥ pare||82||

83pravṛttilakṣaṇaṃ dharmaṃ paśyāmi paramaṃ nṛpa
|
magnasya hi paraṃ jñānaṃ kiṃ na duḥkhataraṃ bhavet||83||

84bhīṣma uvāca|
yathānyāyaṃ tvayā tāta praśnaḥ pṛṣṭaḥ susaṃkaṭaḥ
|
budhānāmapi sammohaḥ praśne'sminbharatarṣabha||84||

85atrāpi tattvaṃ paramaṃ śṛṇu samyaṅmayeritam
|
buddhiśca paramā yatra kāpilānāṃ mahātmanām||85||

86indriyāṇyeva budhyante svadehe dehināṃ nṛpa
|
kāraṇānyātmanastāni sūkṣmaḥ paśyati taistu saḥ||86||

87ātmanā viprahīṇāni kāṣṭhakuḍyasamāni tu
|
vinaśyanti na sandehaḥ phenā iva mahārṇave||87||

88indriyaiḥ saha suptasya dehinaḥ śatrutāpana
|
sūkṣmaścarati sarvatra nabhasīva samīraṇaḥ||88||

89sa paśyati yathānyāyaṃ sparśānspṛśati vā vibho
|
budhyamāno yathāpūrvamakhileneha bhārata||89||

90indriyāṇīha sarvāṇi sve sve sthāne yathāvidhi
|
anīśatvātpralīyante sarpā hataviṣā iva||90||

91indriyāṇāṃ tu sarveṣāṃ svasthāneṣveva sarvaśaḥ
|
ākramya gatayaḥ sūkṣmāścaratyātmā na saṃśayaḥ||91||

92sattvasya ca guṇānkṛtsnānrajasaśca guṇānpunaḥ
|
guṇāṃśca tamasaḥ sarvānguṇānbuddheśca bhārata||92||

93guṇāṃśca manasaścāpi nabhasaśca guṇāṃśca saḥ
|
guṇānvāyośca dharmātmansnehajāṃśca guṇānpunaḥ||93||

94apāṃ guṇāṃstathā pārtha pārthivāṃśca guṇānapi
|
sarvāṇyeva guṇairvyāpya kṣetrajñeṣu yudhiṣṭhira||94||

95ātmā ca vyāti kṣetrajñaṃ karmaṇī ca śubhāśubhe
|
śiṣyā iva mahātmānamindriyāṇi ca taṃ prabho||95||

96prakṛtiṃ cāpyatikramya gacchatyātmānamavyayam
|
paraṃ nārāyaṇātmānaṃ nirdvandvaṃ prakṛteḥ param||96||

97vimuktaḥ puṇyapāpebhyaḥ praviṣṭastamanāmayam
|
paramātmānamaguṇaṃ na nivartati bhārata||97||

98śiṣṭaṃ tatra manastāta indriyāṇi ca bhārata
|
āgacchanti yathākālaṃ guroḥ saṃdeśakāriṇaḥ||98||

99śakyaṃ cālpena kālena śāntiṃ prāptuṃ guṇārthinā
|
evamuktena kaunteya yuktajñānena mokṣiṇā||99||

100sāṃkhyā rājanmahāprājñā gacchanti paramāṃ gatim
|
jñānenānena kaunteya tulyaṃ jñānaṃ na vidyate||100||

101atra te saṃśayo mābhūjjñānaṃ sāṃkhyaṃ paraṃ matam
|
akṣaraṃ dhruvamevoktaṃ pūrṇaṃ brahma sanātanam||101||

102anādimadhyanidhanaṃ nirdvandvaṃ kartṛ śāśvatam
|
kūṭasthaṃ caiva nityaṃ ca yadvadanti manīṣiṇaḥ||102||

103yataḥ sarvāḥ pravartante sargapralayavikriyāḥ
|
yacca śaṃsanti śāstreṣu vadanti paramarṣayaḥ||103||

104sarve viprāśca devāśca tathā śamavido janāḥ
|
brahmaṇyaṃ paramaṃ devamanantaṃ paramacyutam||104||

105prārthayantaśca taṃ viprā vadanti guṇabuddhayaḥ
|
samyagyuktāstathā yogāḥ sāṃkhyāścāmitadarśanāḥ||105||

106amūrtestasya kaunteya sāṃkhyaṃ mūrtiriti śrutiḥ
|
abhijñānāni tasyāhurmataṃ hi bharatarṣabha||106||

107dvividhānīha bhūtāni pṛthivyāṃ pṛthivīpate
|
jaṅgamāgamasaṃjñāni jaṅgamaṃ tu viśiṣyate||107||

108jñānaṃ mahadyaddhi mahatsu rājanvedeṣu sāṃkhyeṣu tathaiva yoge
|
yaccāpi dṛṣṭaṃ vividhaṃ purāṇe sāṃkhyāgataṃ tannikhilaṃ narendra||108||

109yaccetihāseṣu mahatsudṛṣṭaṃ yaccārthaśāstre nṛpaśiṣṭajuṣṭe
|
jñānaṃ ca loke yadihāsti kiṃcit sāṃkhyāgataṃ tacca mahanmahātman||109||

110śamaśca dṛṣṭaḥ paramaṃ balaṃ ca jñānaṃ ca sūkṣmaṃ ca yathāvaduktam
|
tapāṃsi sūkṣmāṇi sukhāni caiva sāṃkhye yathāvadvihitāni rājan||110||

111viparyaye tasya hi pārtha devān gacchanti sāṃkhyāḥ satataṃ sukhena
|
tāṃścānusañcārya tataḥ kṛtārthāḥ patanti vipreṣu yateṣu bhūyaḥ||111||

112hitvā ca dehaṃ praviśanti devaṃ divaukasau dyāmiva pārtha sāṃkhyā:
|
ato'dhikaṃ te'bhiratā mahārhe sāṃkhye dvijāḥ pārthivaśiṣṭajuṣṭe||112||

113teṣāṃ na tiryaggamanaṃ hi dṛṣṭaṃ nārvāggatiḥ pāpakṛtādhivāsaḥ
|
na vā pradhānā api te dvijātayo ye jñānametannṛpate'nuraktāḥ||113||

114sāṃkhyaṃ viśālaṃ paramaṃ purāṇaṃ mahārṇavaṃ vimalamudārakāntam
|
kṛtsnaṃ ca sāṃkhyaṃ nṛpate mahātmā nārāyaṇo dhārayate'prameyam||114||

115etanmayoktaṃ naradeva tattvaṃ nārāyaṇo viśvamidaṃ purāṇam
|
sa sargakāle ca karoti sargaṃ saṃhārakāle ca tadatti bhūyaḥ||115||

116saṃhṛtya sarvaṃ nijadehasaṃsthaṃ kṛtvāpsu śete jagadantarātmā||116||
