Mahābhārata 7.28 (Droṇaparvan)

1sañjaya uvāca|
yiyāsatastataḥ kṛṣṇaḥ pārthasyāśvānmanojavān
|
saṃpraiṣīddhemasaṃchannān droṇānīkāya saṃtvaran||1||

2taṃ prayāntaṃ kuruśreṣṭhaṃ svān bhrātṝn droṇatāpitān
|
suśarmā bhrātṛbhiḥ sārdhaṃ yuddhārthī pṛṣṭhato'nvayāt||2||

3tataḥ śvetahayaḥ kṛṣṇamabravīdajitaṃ jayaḥ
|
eṣa māṃ bhrātṛbhiḥ sārdhaṃ suśarmā''hvayate'cyuta||3||

4dīryate cottareṇaiva tat sainyaṃ madhusūdana
|
dvaidhībhūtaṃ mano me'dya kṛtaṃ saṃśaptakairidam||4||

5kiṃ nu saṃśaptakān hanmi svān rakṣāmyahitārditān
|
iti me tvaṃ mataṃ vetsi tatra kiṃ sukṛtaṃ bhavet||5||

6evamuktastu dāśārhaḥ syandanaṃ pratyavartayat
|
yena trigartādhipatiḥ pāṇḍavaṃ samupāhvayava||6||

7tato'rjunaḥ suśarmāṇaṃ viddhvā saptabhirāśugaiḥ
|
dhvajaṃ dhanuścāsya tathā kṣurābhyāṃ samakṛntata||7||

8trigartādhipateścāpi bhrātaraṃ ṣaḍbhirāśugaiḥ
|
sāśvaṃ sasūtaṃ tvaritaḥ pārthaḥ praiṣīdyamakṣayam||8||

9tato bhujagasaṃkāśāṃ suśarmā śaktimāyasīm
|
cikṣepārjunamādiśya vāsudevāya tomaram||9||

10śaktiṃ tribhiḥ śaraiśchittvā tomaraṃ tribhirarjunaḥ
|
suśarmāṇaṃ śaravrātairmohayitvā nyavartayat||10||

11taṃ vāsavamivāyāntaṃ bhūrivarṣaṃ śaraudhiṇam
|
rājaṃstāvakasainyānāṃ nograṃ kaścidavārayat||11||

12tato dhanañjayo bāṇaiḥ sarvāneva mahārathān
|
āyādvinighnan kauravyān dahankakṣamivānalaḥ||12||

13tasya vegamasahyaṃ taṃ kuntīputrasya dhīmataḥ
|
nāśaknuvaṃste saṃsoḍhuṃ sparśamagneriva prajāḥ||13||

14saṃveṣṭayannanīkāni śaravarṣeṇa pāṇḍavaḥ
|
suparṇapātavadrājannāyāt prāgjyotiṣaṃ prati||14||

15yattadānāmayajjiṣṇurbharatānāmapāpinām
|
dhanuḥ kṣemakaraṃ saṃkhye dviṣatāmaśruvardhanam||15||

16tadeva tava putrasya rājan durdyūtadevinaḥ
|
kṛte kṣatravināśāya dhanurāyacchadarjunaḥ||16||

17tathā vikṣobhyamāṇā sā pārthena tava vāhinī
|
vyaśīryata mahārāja naurivāsādya parvatam||17||

18tato daśasahasrāṇi nyavartanta dhanuṣmatām
|
matiṃ kṛtvā raṇe krūrāṃ vīrā jayaparājaye||18||

19vyapetahṛdayatrāsā āvabrustaṃ mahārathāḥ
|
ārcchat pārtho guruṃ bhāraṃ sarvabhārasaho yudhi||19||

20yathā nalavanaṃ kruddhaḥ prabhinnaḥ ṣaṣṭihāyanaḥ
|
mṛdnīyāttadvadāyastaḥ pārtho'mṛ‌dnāccamūṃ tava||20||

21tasmin pramathite sainye bhagadatto narādhipaḥ
|
tena nāgena sahasā dhanañjayamupādravat||21||

22taṃ rathena naravyāghraḥ pratyagṛhṇāddhanañjayaḥ
|
sa sannipātastumulo babhūva rathanāgayoḥ||22||

23kalpitābhyāṃ yathāśāstraṃ rathena ca gajena ca
|
saṅgrāme ceraturvīrau bhagada‌ttadhanañjayau||23||

24tato jīmūtasaṅkāśānnāgādindra iva prabhuḥ
|
abhyavarṣaccharaugheṇa bhagada‌tto dhanañjayam||24||

25sa cāpi śaravarṣaṃ taṃ śaravarṣeṇa vāsaviḥ
|
aprāptameva ciccheda bhagadattasya vīryavān||25||

26tataḥ prāgjyotiṣo rājā śaravarṣaṃ nivārayat
|
śarairjaghne mahābāhuṃ pārthaṃ kṛṣṇaṃ ca māriṣa||26||

27tatastu śarajālena mahatā'bhyavakīrya tau
|
codayāmāsa taṃ nāgaṃ vadhāyācyutapārthayoḥ||27||

28tamāpatantaṃ dviradaṃ dṛṣṭvā kruddhamivāntakam
|
cakre'pasavyaṃ tvaritaḥ syandanena janārdanaḥ||28||

29saṃprāptamapi neyeṣa parāvṛttaṃ mahādvipam
|
sārohaṃ mṛtyusāt kartuṃ smaran dharmaṃ dhanañjayaḥ||29||

30sa tu nāgo dviparathān hayāṃścāmṛdya māriṣa
|
prāhiṇonmṛtyulokāya tataḥ kuddho dhanañjayaḥ||30||
