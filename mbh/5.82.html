Mahābhārata 5.82 (Udyogaparvan)

1vaiśampāyana uvāca|
rājñastu vacanaṃ śrutvā dharmārthasahitaṃ hitam
|
kṛṣṇā dāśārhamāsīnamabravīcchokakarśitā ||1||

2sutā drupadarājasya svasitāyatamūrdhajā
|
saṃpūjya sahadevaṃ ca sātyakiṃ ca mahāratham ||2||

3bhīmasenaṃ ca saṃśāntaṃ dṛṣṭvā paramadurmanāḥ
|
aśrupūrṇekṣaṇā vākyamuvācedaṃ manasvinī ||3||

4viditaṃ te mahābāho dharmajña madhusūdana
|
yathā nikṛtimāsthāya bhraṃśitāḥ pāṇḍavāḥ sukhāt ||4||

5dhṛtarāṣṭrasya putreṇa sāmātyena janārdana
|
yathā ca sañjayo rājñā mantraṃ rahasi śrāvitaḥ ||5||

6yudhiṣṭhirasya dāśārha taccāpi viditaṃ tava
|
yathoktaḥ sañjayaścaiva tacca sarvaṃ śrutaṃ tvayā ||6||

7pañca nastāta dīyantāṃ grāmā iti mahādyute
|
avisthalaṃ vṛkasthalaṃ mākandīṃ vāraṇāvatam ||7||

8avasānaṃ mahābāho kañcidekaṃ ca pañcamam
|
iti duryodhano vācyaḥ suhṛdaścāsya keśava ||8||

9na cāpi hyakarodvākyaṃ śrutvā kṛṣṇa suyodhanaḥ
|
yudhiṣṭhirasya dāśārha śrīmataḥ sandhimicchataḥ ||9||

10apradānena rājyasya yadi kṛṣṇa suyodhanaḥ
|
sandhimicchenna kartavyaṃ tatra gatvā kathañcana ||10||

11śakṣyanti hi mahābāho pāṇḍavāḥ sṛñjayaiḥ saha
|
dhārtarāṣṭrabalaṃ ghoraṃ kuddhaṃ pratisamāsitum ||11||

12na hi sāmnā na dānena śakyo'rthasteṣu kaścana
|
tasmātteṣu na kartavyā kṛpā te madhusūdana ||12||

13sāmnā dānena vā kṛṣṇa ye na śāmyanti śatravaḥ
|
yoktavyasteṣu daṇḍaḥ syājjīvitaṃ parirakṣatā ||13||

14tasmātteṣu mahādaṇḍaḥ kṣeptavyaḥ kṣipramacyuta
|
tvayā caiva mahābāho pāṇḍavaiḥ saha sṛñjayaiḥ ||14||

15etatsamarthaṃ pārthānāṃ tava caiva yaśaskaram
|
kriyamāṇaṃ bhavet kṛṣṇa kṣatrasya ca sukhāvaham ||15||

16kṣatriyeṇa hi hantavyaḥ kṣatriyo lobhamāsthitaḥ
|
akṣatriyo vā dāśārha svadharmamanutiṣṭhatā ||16||

17anyatra brāhmaṇāttāta sarvapāpeṣvavasthitāt
|
gururhi sarvavarṇānāṃ brāhmaṇaḥ prasṛtāgrabhuk ||17||

18yathā'vadhye vadhyamāne bhaveddoṣo janārdana
|
sa vadhyasyāvadhe dṛṣṭa iti dharmavido viduḥ ||18||

19yathā tvāṃ na spṛśedeṣa doṣaḥ kṛṣṇa tathā kuru
|
pāṇḍavaiḥ saha dāśārhaiḥ sṛñjayaiśca sasainikaiḥ ||19||

20punaruktaṃ ca vakṣyāmi viśrambheṇa janārdana
|
kā tu sīmantinī mādṛk pṛthivyāmasti keśava ||20||

21sutā drupadrarājasya vedimadhyāt samutthitā
|
dhṛṣṭadyumnasya bhaginī tava kṛṣṇa priyā sakhī ||21||

22ājamīḍhakulaṃ prāptā snuṣā pāṇḍormahātmanaḥ
|
mahiṣī pāṇḍuputrāṇāṃ pañcendrasamavarcasām ||22||

23sutā me pañcabhirvīraiḥ pañca jātā mahārathāḥ
|
abhimanyuryathā kṛṣṇa tathā te tava dharmataḥ ||23||

24sā'haṃ keśagrahaṃ prāptā parikliṣṭā sabhāṃ gatā
|
paśyatāṃ pāṇḍuputrāṇāṃ tvayi jīvati keśava ||24||

25jīvatsu pāṇḍuputreṣu pañcāleṣvatha vṛṣṇiṣu
|
dāsībhūtā'smi pāpānāṃ sabhāmadhye vyavasthitā ||25||

26niramarṣeṣvaceṣṭeṣu prekṣamāṇeṣu pāṇḍuṣu
|
pāhi māmiti govinda manasā cintito'si me ||26||

27yatra māṃ bhagavānrājā śvaśuro vākyamabravīt
|
varaṃ vṛṇīṣva pāñcāli varārhā'si matā mama ||27||

28adāsāḥ pāṇḍavāḥ santu sarathāḥ sāyudhā iti
|
mayokte yatra nirmuktā vanavāsāya keśava ||28||

29evaṃvidhānāṃ duḥkhānāmabhijño'si janārdana
|
trāyasva puṇḍarīkākṣa sabhartṛjñātibāndhavān ||29||

30nanvahaṃ kṛṣṇa bhīṣmasya dhṛtarāṣṭrasya cobhayoḥ
|
snuṣā bhavāmi dharmeṇa sā'haṃ dāsīkṛtā balāt ||30||

31dhik pārthasya dhanuṣmattāṃ bhīmasenasya dhigbalam
|
yatra duryodhanaḥ kṛṣṇa muhūrtamapi jīvati ||31||

32yadi te'hamanugrāhyā yadi te'sti kṛpā mayi
|
dhārtarāṣṭreṣu vai kopaḥ sarvaḥ kṛṣṇa vidhīyatām ||32||

33vaiśampāyana uvāca|
ityuktvā mṛdusaṃhāraṃ vṛjināgraṃ sudarśanam
|
sunīlamasitāpāṅgī sarvagandhādhivāsitam ||33||

34sarvalakṣaṇasampannaṃ mahābhujagavarcasam
|
keśapakṣaṃ varārohā gṛhya vāmena pāṇinā ||34||

35padmākṣī puṇḍarīkākṣamupetya gajagāminī
|
aśrupūrṇekṣaṇā kṛṣṇā kṛṣṇaṃ vacanamabravīt ||35||

36ayaṃ te puṇḍarīkākṣa duḥśāsanakaroddhṛtaḥ
|
smartavyaḥ sarvakāryeṣu pareṣāṃ sandhimicchatā ||36||

37yadi bhīmārjunau kṛṣṇa kṛpaṇau sandhikāmukau
|
pitā me yotsyate vṛddhaḥ saha putrairmahārathaiḥ ||37||

38pañca caiva mahāvīryāḥ putrā me madhusūdana
|
abhimanyuṃ puraskṛtya yotsyante kurubhiḥ sahaḥ ||38||

39duḥśāsanabhujaṃ śyāmaṃ saṃchinnaṃ pāṃsuguṇṭhitam
|
yadyahaṃ tu na paśyāmi kā śāntirhṛdayasya me ||39||

40trayodaśa hi varṣāṇi pratīkṣantyā gatāni me
|
vidhāya hṛdaye manyuṃ pradīptamiva pāvakam ||40||

41vidīryate me hṛdayaṃ bhīmavākśalyapīḍitam
|
yo'yamadya mahābāhurdharmamevānupaśyati ||41||

42ityuktvā bāṣparuddhena kaṇṭhenāyatalocanā
|
ruroda kṛṣṇā sotkampaṃ sasvaraṃ bāṣpagadgadam ||42||

43stanau pīnāyataśroṇī sahitāvabhivarṣatī
|
dravībhūtamivātyuṣṇaṃ muñcatī vāri netrajam ||43||

44tāmuvāca mahābāhuḥ keśavaḥ parisāntvayan
|
acirāddrakṣyase kṛṣṇe rudatīrbharatastriyaḥ ||44||

45evaṃ tā bhīru rotsyanti nihatajñātibāndhavāḥ
|
hatamitrā hatabalā yeṣāṃ kruddhā'si bhāminī ||45||

46ahaṃ ca tatkariṣyāmi bhīmārjunayamaiḥ saha
|
yudhiṣṭhiraniyogena daivācca vidhinirmitāt ||46||

47dhārtarāṣṭrāḥ kālapakvā na cecchṛṇvanti me vacaḥ
|
śeṣyante nihatā bhūmau śvasṛgālādanīkṛtāḥ ||47||

48caleddhi himavān śailo medinī śatadhā phalet
|
dyauḥ patecca sanakṣatrā na me moghaṃ vaco bhavet ||48||

49satyaṃ te pratijānāmi kṛṣṇe bāṣpo nigṛhyatām
|
hatāmitrān śriyā yuktānacirāddrakṣyase patīn ||49||
