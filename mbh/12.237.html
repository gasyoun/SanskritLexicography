Mahābhārata 12.237 (Śāntiparvan)

1vyāsa uvāca |
atha jñānaplavaṃ dhīro gṛhītvā śāntimātmanaḥ
|
unmajjaṃśca nimajjaṃśca jñānamevābhisaṃśrayet ||1||

2śuka uvāca |
kiṃ tajjñānamayo vidyā yathā nistarate dvayam
|
pravṛttilakṣaṇo dharmo nivṛttiriti vā vada ||2||

3vyāsa uvāca |
yastu paśyansvabhāvena vinābhāvamacetanaḥ
|
puṣyate ca punaḥ sarvānprajñayā muktahetukān ||3||

4yeṣāṃ caikāntabhāvena svabhāvātkāraṇaṃ matam
|
pūtvā tṛṇamiṣīkāṃ vā te labhante na kiṃcana ||4||

5ye cainaṃ pakṣamāśritya nivartantyalpamedhasaḥ
|
svabhāvaṃ kāraṇaṃ jñātvā na śreyaḥ prāpnuvanti te ||5||

6svabhāvo hi vināśāya mohakarma manobhavaḥ
|
nirutametayoretatsvabhāvaparibhāvayoḥ ||6||

7kṛṣyādīnīha karmāṇi sasyasaṃharaṇāni ca
|
prajñāvadbhiḥ praklṛptāni yānāsanagṛhāṇi ca ||7||

8ākrīḍānāṃ gṛhāṇāṃ ca gadānāmagadasya ca
|
prajñāvantaḥ prayoktāro jñānavadbhiranuṣṭhitāḥ ||8||

9prajñā saṃyojayatyarthaiḥ prajñā śreyo'dhigacchati
|
rājāno bhuñjate rājyaṃ prajñayā tulyalakṣaṇāḥ ||9||

10parāvaraṃ tu bhūtānāṃ jñānenaivopalabhyate
|
vidyayā tāta sṛṣṭānāṃ vidyaiveha parā gatiḥ ||10||

11bhūtānāṃ janma sarveṣāṃ vividhānāṃ caturvidham
|
jarāyujāṇḍajodbhijjasvedajaṃ copalakṣayet ||11||

12sthāvarebhyo viśiṣṭāni jaṅgamānyupadhārayet
|
upapannaṃ hi yacceṣṭā viśiṣyeta viśeṣyayā ||12||

13āhurvai bahupādāni jaṅgamāni dvayāni tu
|
bahupādbhyo viśiṣṭāni dvipadāni bahūnyapi ||13||

14dvipadāni dvayānyāhuḥ pārthivānītarāṇi ca
|
pārthivāni viśiṣṭāni tāni hyannāni bhuñjate ||14||

15pārthivāni dvayānyāhurmadhyamānyadhamāni tu
|
madhyamāni viśiṣṭāni jātidharmopadhāraṇāt ||15||

16madhyamāni dvayānyāhurdharmajñānītarāṇi ca
|
dharmajñāni viśiṣṭāni kāryākāryopadhāraṇāt ||16||

17dharmajñāni dvayānyāhurvedajñānītarāṇi ca
|
vedajñāni viśiṣṭāni vedo hyeṣu pratiṣṭhitaḥ ||17||

18vedajñāni dvayānyāhuḥ pravaktṝṇītarāṇi ca
|
pravaktṝṇi viśiṣṭāni sarvadharmopadhāraṇāt ||18||

19vijñāyante hi yairvedāḥ sadharmāḥ sakriyāphalāḥ
|
sadharmā nikhilā vedāḥ pravaktṛbhyo viniḥsṛtāḥ ||19||

20pravaktṝṇi dvayānyāhurātmajñānītarāṇi ca
|
ātmajñāni viśiṣṭāni janmājanmopadhāraṇāt ||20||

21dharmadvayaṃ hi yo veda sa sarvajñaḥ sa sarvavit
|
sa tyāgī satyasaṃkalpaḥ satyaḥ śuciratheśvaraḥ ||21||

22brahmajñānapratiṣṭhaṃ hi taṃ devā brāhmaṇaṃ viduḥ
|
śabdabrahmaṇi niṣṇātaṃ pare ca kṛtaniścayam ||22||

23antasthaṃ ca bahiṣṭhaṃ ca sādhiyajñādhidaivatam
|
jñānānvitā hi paśyanti te devāstāta te dvijāḥ ||23||

24teṣu viśvamidaṃ bhūtaṃ sarvaṃ ca jagadāhitam
|
teṣāṃ māhātmyabhāvasya sadṛśaṃ nāsti kiṃcana ||24||

25ādyante nidhanaṃ caiva karma cātītya sarvaśaḥ
|
caturvidhasya bhūtasya sarvasyeśāḥ svayambhuvaḥ ||25||
