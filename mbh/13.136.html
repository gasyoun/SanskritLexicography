Mahābhārata 13.136 (Anuśāsanaparvan)

1yudhiṣṭhira uvāca|
uktāstu bhavatā bhojyāstathābhojyāśca sarvaśaḥ
|
atra me praśnasandehastanme vada pitāmaha ||1||

2brāhmaṇānāṃ viśeṣeṇa havyakavyapratigrahe
|
nānāvidheṣu bhojyeṣu prāyaścittāni śaṃsa me ||2||

3bhīṣma uvāca|
hanta vakṣyāmi te rājan brāhmaṇānāṃ mahātmanām
|
pratigraheṣu bhojye ca mucyate yena pāpmanaḥ ||3||

4ghṛtapratigrahe caiva sāvitrī samidāhutiḥ
|
tilapratigrahe caiva samametayudhiṣṭhira ||4||

5māṃsapratigrahe caiva madhuno lavaṇasya ca
|
ādityodayanaṃ sthitvā pūto bhavati brāhmaṇaḥ ||5||

6kāñcanaṃ pratigṛhyātha japamāno guruśrutim
|
kṛṣṇāyasaṃ ca vivṛtaṃ dhārayan mucyate dvijaḥ ||6||

7evaṃ pratigṛhīte'tha dhane vastre tathā striyām
|
evameva naraśreṣṭha suvarṇasya pratigrahe ||7||

8annapratigrahe caiva pāyasekṣurase tathā
|
ikṣutailapavitrāṇāṃ trisandhye'psu nimajjanam ||8||

9vrīhau puṣpe phale caiva jale piṣṭamaye tathā
|
yāvake dadhidugdhe ca sāvitrīṃ śataśonvitām ||9||

10upānahau ca cchatraṃ ca pratigṛhyaurdhvadehike
|
japecchataṃ samāyuktastena mucyeta pāpmanā ||10||

11kṣetrapratigrahe caiva grahasūtakayostathā
|
trīṇi rātrāṇyupoṣitvā tena pāpādvimucyate ||11||

12kṛṣṇapakṣe tu yaḥ śrāddhaṃ pitṝṇāmaśnute dvijaḥ
|
annametadahorātrātpūto bhavati brāhmaṇaḥ ||12||

13na ca sandhyāmupāsīta na ca jāpyaṃ pravartayet
|
na saṅkirettadannaṃ ca tataḥ pūyeta brāhmaṇaḥ ||13||

14ityarthamaparāhṇe tu pitṝṇāṃ śrāddhamucyate
|
yathoktānāṃ yadaśnīyurbrāhmaṇāḥ pūrvaketitāḥ ||14||

15mṛtakasya tṛtīyāhe brāhmaṇo yo'nnamaśnute
|
sa trivelaṃ samunmajya dvādaśāhena śudhyati ||15||

16dvādaśāhe vyatīte tu kṛtaśauco viśeṣataḥ
|
brāhmaṇebhyo havirdatvā mucyate tena pāpmanā ||16||

17mṛtasya daśarātreṇa prāyaścittāni dāpayet
|
sāvitrīṃ raivatīmiṣṭiṃ kūśmāṇḍamaghamarṣaṇam ||17||

18mṛtakasya trirātre yaḥ samuddiṣṭe samaśnute
|
sapta triṣavaṇaṃ snātvā pūto bhavati brāhmaṇaḥ ||18||

19siddhimāpnoti vipulāmāpadaṃ caiva nāpnuyāt ||19||

20yastu śūdraiḥ samaśnīyādbrāhmaṇopyekabhojane
|
aśaucaṃ vidhivattasya śaucamatra vidhīyate ||20||

21yastu vaiśyaiḥ sahāśnīyādbrāhmaṇo'pyekabhojane
|
sa vai trirātraṃ dīkṣitvā mucyate tena karmaṇā ||21||

22kṣatriyaiḥ saha yo'śnīyādbrāhmaṇo'pyekabhojane
|
āplutaḥ saha vāsobhistena mucyeta pāpmanā ||22||

23śūdrasya tu kulaṃ hanti vaiśyasya paśubāndhavān
|
kṣatriyastha śriyaṃ hanti brāhmaṇasya suvarcasam ||23||

24prāyaścittaṃ ca śāntiṃ ca juhuyāttena mucyate
|
sāvitrīṃ raivatīmiṣṭiṃ kūśmāṇḍamaghamarṣaṇam ||24||

25tathocchiṣṭamathānyonyaṃ saṃprāśennātra saṃśayaḥ
|
rocanā virajā rātrirmaṅgalālaṃbhanāni ca ||25||
