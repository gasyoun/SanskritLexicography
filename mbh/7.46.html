Mahābhārata 7.46 (Droṇaparvan)

1dhṛtarāṣṭra uvāca|
yathā vadasi me sūta ekasya bahubhiḥ saha
|
saṃgrāmaṃ tumalaṃ ghoraṃ jayaṃ caiva mahātmanaḥ||1||

2aśraddheyamivāścaryaṃ saubhadrasyātha vikramam
|
kiṃ tu nātyadbhutaṃ teṣāṃ yeṣāṃ dharmo vyapāśrayaḥ||2||

3duryodhane ca vimukhe rājaputraśate hate
|
saubhadre pratipattiṃ kāṃ pratyapadyanta māmakāḥ||3||

4sañjaya uvāca|
saṃśuṣkāsyāścalannetrāḥ prasvinnā lomaharṣaṇāḥ
|
palāyanakṛtotsāhā nirutsāhā dviṣajjaye||4||

5hatān bhrātṝn pitṝn putrān suhṛtsaṃbandhibāndhavān
|
utsṛjyotsṛjya saṃjagmustvarayanto hayadvipān||5||

6tān prabhagnāṃstathā dṛṣṭvā droṇo drauṇirbṛhadbalaḥ
|
kṛpo duryodhanaḥ karṇaḥ kṛtavarmā'tha saubalaḥ||6||

7abhyadhāvansusaṃkruddhāḥ saubhadramaparājitam
|
te tu pautreṇa te rājan prāyaśo vimukhīkṛtāḥ||7||

8ekastu sukhasaṃvṛddho bālyāddarpācca nirbhayaḥ
|
iṣvastravinmahātejā lakṣmaṇo'rjunimabhyayāt||8||

9tamanvagevāsya pitā putragṛddhī nyavartata
|
anuduryodhanaṃ cānye nyavartanta mahārathāḥ||9||

10taṃ te'bhiṣiṣicurbāṇairmeghā girimivāṃbubhiḥ
|
sa tu tān pramamāthaiko viṣvagvāto yathāmbudān||10||

11pautraṃ tava ca durdharṣaṃ lakṣmaṇaṃ priyadarśanam
|
pituḥ samīpe tiṣṭhantaṃ śūramudyatakārmukam||11||

12atyantasukhasaṃvṛddhaṃ dhaneśvarasutopamam
|
āsasāda raṇe kāṣṇirmatto mattamiva dvipam||12||

13lakṣmaṇena tu saṃgamya saubhadraḥ paravīrahā
|
śaraiḥ suniśitaistīkṣṇairbāhvorurasi cārpayat ||13||

14saṃkruddho vai mahārāja daṇḍāhata ivoragaḥ
|
pautrastava mahārāja tava pautramabhāṣata||14||

15sudṛṣṭaḥ kriyatāṃ loko hyamuṃ lokaṃ gamiṣyasi
|
paśyatāṃ bāndhavānāṃ tvāṃ nayāmi yamasādanam||15||

16evamuktvā tato bhallaṃ saubhadraḥ paravīrahā
|
udbabarha mahābāhurnirbhuktoragasannibham||16||

17sa tasya bhujanirmukto lakṣmaṇasya sudarśanam
|
sunasaṃ subhrukeśāntaṃ śiro'hārṣīta sakuṇḍalam||17||

18lakṣmaṇaṃ nihataṃ dṛṣṭvā hāhetyuccukruśurjanāḥ
|
tato duryodhanaḥ kuddhaḥ priye putre nipātite||18||

19hatainamiti cukrośa kṣatriyān kṣatriyarṣabhaḥ
|
tato droṇaḥ kṛpaḥ karṇo droṇaputro bṛhadbalaḥ||19||

20kṛtavarmā ca hārdikyaḥ ṣaḍrathāḥ paryavārayan
|
tāṃstu viddhvā śitairbāṇairvimukhīkṛtya cārjuniḥ||20||

21vegenābhyapatatkruddhaḥ saindhavasya mahadbalam
|
āvavrustasya panthānaṃ gajānīkena daṃśitāḥ||21||

22kaliṃgāśca niṣādāśca krāthaputraśca vīryavān
|
tatprasaktamivātyarthaṃ yuddhamāsīdviśāṃ pate||22||

23tatastatkuñjarānīkaṃ vyadhamaddhṛṣṭamārjuniḥ
|
yathā vāyurnityagatirjaladān śataśo'mbare||23||

24tataḥ krāthaḥ śaravrātairārjuniṃ samavākirat
|
athetare sannivṛttāḥ punardroṇamukhā rathāḥ||24||

25paramāstrāṇi dhunvānāḥ saubhadramabhidudruvuḥ
|
tānnivāryārjunirbāṇaiḥ krāthaputramathārdayat||25||

26śaraugheṇāprameyeṇa tvaramāṇo jighāṃsayā
|
sadhanurbāṇakeyūrau bāhū samukuṭaṃ śiraḥ||26||

27sacchatradhvajayantāraṃ rathaṃ cāśvānnyāpātayat
|
kulaśīlaśrutibalaiḥ kīrtyā cāstrabalena ca
|
yukte tasminhate vīrāḥ prāyaśo vimukhā'bhavan||27||
