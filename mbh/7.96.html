Mahābhārata 7.96 (Droṇaparvan)

1sañjaya uvāca|
rājan saṅgrāmamāścaryaṃ śṛṇu kīrtayato mama
|
kurūṇāṃ pāṇḍavānāṃ ca yathā yuddhamavartata ||1||

2bhāradvājaṃ samāsādya vyūhasya pramukhe sthitam
|
ayodhayan raṇe pārthā droṇānīkaṃ bibhitsavaḥ||2||

3rakṣamāṇaḥ svakaṃ vyūhaṃ droṇo'pi saha sainikaiḥ
|
ayodhayadraṇe pārthān prārthamāno mahadyaśaḥ ||3||

4vindānuvindāvāvantyau virāṭaṃ daśabhiḥ śaraiḥ
|
ājaghnatuḥ susaṅkruddhau tava putrahitaiṣiṇau ||4||

5virāṭaśca mahārāja tāvubhau samare sthitau
|
parākrāntau parākramya yodhayāmāsa sānugau ||5||

6teṣāṃ yuddhaṃ samabhavaddāruṇaṃ śoṇitodakam
|
siṃhasya dvipamukhyābhyāṃ prabhinnābhyāṃ yathā vane ||6||

7bālhīkaṃ rabhasaṃ yuddhe yājñasenirmahābalaḥ
|
ājaghne viśikhaistīkṣṇairghorairmarmāsthibhedibhiḥ ||7||

8bālhīko yājñaseniṃ tu hemapuṅkhaiḥ śilāśitaiḥ
|
ājaghāna bhṛśaṃ kruddho navabhirnataparvabhiḥ ||8||

9tadyuddhamabhavaddhoraṃ śaraśaktisamākulam
|
bhīrūṇāṃ trāsajananaṃ śūrāṇāṃ harṣavardhanam ||9||

10tābhyāṃ tatra śarairmuktairantarikṣaṃ diśastathā
|
abhavat saṃvṛtaṃ sarvaṃ na prājñāyata kiñcana ||10||

11śaivyo govāsano yuddhe kāśyaputraṃ mahāratham
|
sasainyo yodhayāmāsa gajaḥ pratigajaṃ yathā ||11||

12bālhīkarājaḥ saṅkruddho draupadeyānmahārathān
|
manaḥ pañcendriyāṇīva śuśubhe yodhayan raṇe ||12||

13ayodhayaṃste subhṛśaṃ taṃ śaraughaiḥ samantataḥ
|
indriyārthā yathā dehaṃ śaśvaddehavatāṃ vara ||13||

14vārṣṇeyaṃ sātyakiṃ yuddhe putro duḥśāsanastava
|
ājaghne sāyakaistīkṣṇairnavabhirnataparvabhiḥ ||14||

15so'tividdho balavatā maheṣvāsena dhanvinā
|
īṣanmūrcchāṃ jagāmāśu sātyakiḥ satyavikramaḥ ||15||

16samāśvastastu vārṣṇeyastava putraṃ mahāratham
|
vivyādha daśabhistūrṇaṃ sāyakaiḥ kaṅkapatribhiḥ
|
||16||

17tāvanyonyaṃ dṛḍhaṃ viddhāvanyonyaśarapīḍitau
|
rejatuḥ samare rājan puṣpitāviva kiṃśukau ||17||

18alambuṣastu saṅkruddhaḥ kuntibhojaśarārditaḥ
|
aśobhata bhṛśaṃ lakṣmyā puṣpāḍhya iva kiṃśukaḥ ||18||

19kuntibhojaṃ tato rakṣau viddhvā bahubhirāyasaiḥ
|
anadadbhairavaṃ nādaṃ vāhinyāḥ pramukhe tava ||19||

20tatastau samare śūrau yodhayantau parasparam
|
dadṛśuḥ sarvasainyāni śakrajambhau yathā purā ||20||

21śakuniṃ rabhasaṃ yuddhe kṛtavairaṃ ca bhārata
|
mādrīputrau ca saṃrabdhau śaraiścārdayatāṃ bhṛśam ||21||

22tumulaḥ sa mahān rājan prāvartata janakṣayaḥ
|
tvayā sañjanito'tyarthaṃ karṇena ca vivardhitaḥ ||22||

23rakṣitastava putraiśca krodhamūlo hutāśanaḥ
|
ya imāṃ pṛthivīṃ rājan dagdhuṃ sarvāṃ samudyataḥ ||23||

24śakuniḥ pāṇḍuputrābhyāṃ kṛtaḥ sa vimukhaḥ śaraiḥ
|
na sma jānāti kartavyaṃ yuddhe kiñcitparākramam ||24||

25vimukhaṃ cainamālokya mādrīputrau mahārathau
|
vavarṣatuḥ punarbāṇairyathā meghau mahāgirim ||25||

26sa vadhyamāno bahubhiḥ śaraiḥ sannataparvabhiḥ
|
samprāyājjavanairaśvairdroṇānīkāya saubalaḥ ||26||

27ghaṭotkacastathā śūraṃ rākṣasaṃ tamalāyudham
|
abhyayādrabhasaṃ yuddhe vegamāsthāya madhyamam ||27||

28tayoryuddhaṃ mahārāja citrarūpamivābhavat
|
yādṛśaṃ hi purā vṛttaṃ rāmarāvaṇayormṛdhe ||28||

29tato yudhiṣṭhiro rājā madrarājānamāhave
|
viddhvā pañcāśatā bāṇaiḥ punarvivyādha saptabhiḥ ||29||

30tataḥ pravavṛte yuddhaṃ tayoratyadbhutaṃ nṛpa
|
yathā pūrvaṃ mahadyuddhaṃ śambarāmararājayoḥ ||30||

31viviṃśatiścitraseno vikarṇaśca tavātmajaḥ
|
ayodhayan bhīmasenaṃ mahatyā senayā vṛtāḥ ||31||
