Mahābhārata 7.7 (Droṇaparvan)

1droṇa uvāca|
vedaṃ ṣaḍaṅgaṃ vedāhamarthavidyāṃ ca mānavīm
|
traiyyambakamatheṣvastraṃ śastrāṇi vividhāni ca||1||

2ye cāpyuktā mayi guṇā bhavadbhirjayakāṃkṣibhiḥ
|
cikīrṣustānahaṃ sarvān yodhayiṣyāmi pāṇḍavān||2||

3pārṣataṃ tu raṇe rājanna haniṣye kathaṃcana
|
sa hi sṛṣṭo vadhārthāya mamaiva puruṣarṣabhaḥ||3||

4yodhayiṣyāmi sainyāni nāśayan sarvasomakān
|
na ca māṃ pāṇḍavā yuddhe yodhayiṣyanti harṣitāḥ||4||

5sañjaya uvāca|
sa evamabhyanujñātaścakre senāpatiṃ tataḥ
|
droṇaṃ tava suto rājan vidhidṛṣṭena karmaṇā||5||

6athābhiṣiṣicurdroṇaṃ duryodhanamukhā nṛpāḥ
|
saināpatye yathā skandaṃ purā śakramukhāḥ surāḥ||6||

7tato vāditraghoṣeṇa śaṃkhānāṃ ca mahāsvanaiḥ
|
prādurāsīt kṛte droṇe harṣaḥ senāpatau tadā||7||

8tataḥ puṇyāhaghoṣeṇa svastivādasvanena ca
|
saṃstavairgītaśabdaiśca sūtamāgadhabandinām||8||

9jayaśabdairdvijāgryāṇāṃ subhagānartitaistathā
|
satkṛtya vidhinā droṇaṃ menire pāṇḍavāñjitān||9||

10sañjaya uvāca|
saināpatyaṃ tu saṃprāpya bhāradvājo mahārathaḥ
|
yuyutsurvyūhya sainyāni prāyāttava sutaiḥ saha||10||

11saindhavaśca kaliṅgaśca vikarṇaśca tavātmajāḥ
|
dakṣiṇaṃ pārśvamāsthāya samatiṣṭhanta daṃśitāḥ||11||

12prapakṣaḥ śakunisteṣāṃ pravarairhayasādibhiḥ
|
yayau gāndhārakaiḥ sārdhaṃ vimalaprāsayodhibhiḥ||12||

13kṛpaśca kṛtavarmā ca citraseno viviṃśatiḥ
|
duḥśāsanamukhā yattāḥ savyaṃ pakṣamapālayan||13||

14teṣāṃ prapakṣāḥ kāṃbojāḥ sudakṣiṇapuraḥsarāḥ
|
yayuraśvairmahāvegaiḥ śakāśca yavanaiḥ saha||14||

15madrāstrigartāḥ sāṃbaṣṭhāḥ pratīcyodīcyamālavāḥ
|
śibayaḥ śūrasenāśca śūdrāśca maladaiḥ saha||15||

16sauvīrāḥ kitavāḥ prācyā dākṣiṇātyāśca sarvaśaḥ
|
tavātmajaṃ puraskṛtya sūtaputrasya pṛṣṭhataḥ||16||

17harṣayantaḥ svasainyāni yayustava sutaiḥ saha
|
pravaraḥ sarvayodhānāṃ baleṣu balamādadhat||17||

18yayau vaikartanaḥ karṇaḥ pramukhe sarvadhanvinām
|
tasya dīpto mahākāyaḥ svānyanīkāni harṣayan||18||

19hastikakṣyo mahāketurbabhau sūryasamadyutiḥ
|
na bhīṣmavyasanaṃ kaściddṛṣṭvā karṇamamanyata||19||

20viśokāścābhavan sarve rājānaḥ kurubhiḥ saha
|
hṛṣṭāśca bahavo yodhāstatrājalpanta vegataḥ||20||

21na hi karṇaṃ raṇe dṛṣṭvā yudhi sthāsyanti pāṇḍavāḥ
|
karṇo hi samare śakto jetuṃ devān savāsavān||21||

22kimu pāṇḍusutānyuddhe hīnavīryaparākramān
|
bhīṣmeṇa tu raṇe pārthāḥ pālitā bāhuśālinā||22||

23tāṃstu karṇaḥ śaraistīkṣṇairnāśayiṣyati saṃyuge
|
evaṃ bruvantaste'nyonyaṃ hṛṣṭarūpā viśāṃpate||23||

24rādheyaṃ pūjayantaśca praśaṃsantaśca niryayuḥ
|
asmākaṃ śakaṭavyūho droṇena vihito'bhavat||24||

25pareṣāṃ krauñca evāsīdvyūho rājanmahātmanām
|
prīyamāṇena vihito dharmarājena bhārata||25||

26vyūhapramukhatasteṣāṃ tasthatuḥ puruṣarṣabhau
|
vānaradhvajamucchritya viṣvaksenadhanañjayau||26||

27kakudaṃ sarvasainyānāṃ dhāma sarvadhanuṣmatām
|
ādityapathagaḥ ketuḥ pārthasyāmitatejasaḥ||27||

28dīpayāmāsa tat sainyaṃ pāṇḍavasya mahātmanaḥ
|
yathā prajvalitaḥ sūryo yugānte vai vasundharām||28||

29dīṣyan dṛśyeta hi tathā ketuḥ savatra dhīmataḥ
|
yodhānāmarjunaḥ śreṣṭho gāṇḍīvaṃ dhanuṣāṃ varam||29||

30vāsudevaśca bhūtānāṃ cakrāṇāṃ ca sudarśanam
|
catvāryetāni tejāṃsi vahan śvetahayo rathaḥ||30||

31pareṣāmagratastasthau kālacakramivodyatam
|
evaṃ tau sumahātmānau balasenāgragāvubhau||31||

32tāvakānāṃ mukhe karṇaḥ pareṣāṃ ca dhanañjayaḥ
|
tato jayābhisaṃrabdhau parasparavadhaiṣiṇau||32||

33avekṣetāṃ tadā'nyonyaṃ samare karṇapāṇḍavau
|
tataḥ prayāte sahasā bhāradvāje mahārathe||33||

34ārtanādena ghoreṇa vasudhā samakampata
|
tatastumulamākāśamāvṛṇot sadivākaram||34||

35vātoddhūtaṃ rajastīvraṃ kauśeyanikaropamam
|
vavarṣa dyauranabhrāpi māṃsāsthirudhirāṇyuta||35||

36gṛdhrāḥ śyenā bakāḥ kaṅkā vāyasāśca sahasraśaḥ
|
uparyupari senāṃ te tadā paryapatannṛpa||36||

37gomāyavaśca prākrośan bhayadāndāruṇān ravān
|
akārṣurapasavyaṃ ca bahuśaḥ pṛtanāṃ tava||37||

38cikhādiṣaṃto māṃsāni pipāsaṃtaśca śoṇitam
|
apataddīpyamānā ca sanirghātā sakampanā||38||

39ulkā jvalantī saṃgrāme pucchenāvṛttya sarvaśaḥ
|
pariveṣo mahāṃścāpi savidyutstanayitnumān||39||

40bhāskarasyābhavadrājan prayāte vāhinīpatau
|
ete cānye ca bahavaḥ prādurāsan sudāruṇāḥ||40||

41utpātā yudhi vīrāṇāṃ jīvitakṣayakāriṇaḥ
|
tataḥ pravavṛte yuddhaṃ parasparavadhaiṣiṇām||41||

42kurupāṇḍavasainyānāṃ śabdenāpūrayajjagat
|
te tvanyonyaṃ susaṃrabdhāḥ pāṇḍavāḥ kauravaiḥ saha||42||

43abhyaghnanniśitaiḥ śastrairjayagṛddhāḥ prahāriṇaḥ
|
sa pāṇḍavānāṃ mahatīṃ maheṣvāso mahādyutiḥ||43||

44vegenābhyadravatsenāṃ kiran śaraśataiḥ śitaḥ
|
droṇamabhyudyataṃ dṛṣṭvā pāṇḍavāḥ saha sṛñjayaiḥ||44||

45pratyagṛhṇaṃstadā rājañcharavarṣeḥ pṛthak pṛthak
|
vikṣobhyamāṇā droṇena bhidyamānā mahācamūḥ||45||

46vyaśīryata sapāñcālā vāteneva balāhakāḥ
|
bahūnīha vikurvāṇo divyānyastrāṇi saṃyuge||46||

47apīḍayat kṣaṇenaiva droṇaḥ pāṇḍavasṛñjayān
|
te vadhyamānā droṇena vāsaveneva dānavāḥ||47||

48pañcālāḥ samakampanta dhṛṣṭadyumnapurogamāḥ
|
tato divyāstravicchūro yājñasenirmahārathaḥ||48||

49abhinaccharavarṣeṇa droṇānīkamanekadhā
|
droṇasya śaravarṣāṇi śaravarṣeṇa pārṣataḥ||49||

50sannivārya tataḥ sarvān kurūnapyavadhīdbalī
|
saṃyamya tu tato droṇaḥ samavasthāpya cāhave||50||

51svamanīkaṃ maheṣvāsaḥ pārṣataṃ samupādravat
|
sa bāṇavarṣaṃ sumahadasṛjat pārṣataṃ prati||51||

52maghavān samabhikruddhaḥ sahasā dānavāniva
|
te kaṃpyamānā droṇena bāṇaiḥ pāṇḍavasṛñjayāḥ||52||

53punaḥ punarabhajyanta siṃhenevetare mṛgāḥ
|
tathā paryacaraddroṇaḥ pāṇḍavānāṃ bale balī
|
alātacakravadrājaṃstadadbhutamivābhavat||53||

54khacaranagarakalpaṃ kalpitaṃ śāstradṛṣṭhyā
|
caladanilapatākaṃ lhādanaṃ valgitāśvam
|
sphaṭikavimalaketuṃ trāsanaṃ śātravāṇām
|
rathavaramadhirūḍhaḥ saṃjahārārisenām||54||
