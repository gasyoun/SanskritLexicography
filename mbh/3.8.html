Mahābhārata 3.8 (Āraṇyakaparvan)

1vyāsa uvāca|
dhṛtarāṣṭra mahāprājña nibodha vacanaṃ mama
|
vakṣyāmi tvāṃ kauravāṇāṃ sarveṣāṃ hitamuttamam||1||

2na me priyaṃ mahābāho yadgatāḥ pāṇḍavā vanam
|
nikṛtyā nikṛtāścaiva duryodhanapurogamaiḥ||2||

3te smarantaḥ parikleśānvarṣe pūrṇe trayodaśe
|
vimokṣyanti viṣaṃ kruddhāḥ kauraveyeṣu bhārata||3||

4tadayaṃ kiṃ nu pāpātmā tava putraḥ sumandadhīḥ
|
pāṇḍavānnityasaṅkruddho rājyahetorjighāṃsati||4||

5vāryatāṃ sādhvayaṃ mūḍhaḥ śamaṃ gacchatu te sutaḥ
|
vanasthāṃstānayaṃ hantumicchanprāṇānvimokṣyati||5||

6yathā hi viduraḥ prājño yathā bhīṣmo yathā vayam
|
yathā kṛpaśca droṇaśca tathā sādhurbhavānapi||6||

7vigraho hi mahāprājña svajanena vigarhitaḥ
|
adharmyamayaśasyaṃ ca mā rājanpratipadyatām||7||

8samīkṣā yādṛśī hyasya pāṇḍavānprati bhārata
|
upekṣyamāṇā sā rājanmahāntamanayaṃ spṛśet||8||

9athavāyaṃ sumandātmā vanaṃ gacchatu te sutaḥ
|
pāṇḍavaiḥ sahito rājanneka evāsahāyavān||9||

10tataḥ saṃsargajaḥ snehaḥ putrasya tava pāṇḍavaiḥ
|
yadi syātkṛtakāryo'dya bhavestvaṃ manujeśvara||10||

11athavā jāyamānasya yacchīlamanujāyate
|
śrūyate tanmahārāja nāmṛtasyāpasarpati||11||

12kathaṃ vā manyate bhīṣmo droṇo'tha viduro'pi vā
|
bhavānvātra kṣamaṃ kāryaṃ purā vortho'bhivardhate||12||
