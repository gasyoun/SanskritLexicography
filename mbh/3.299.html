Mahābhārata 3.299 (Āraṇyakaparvan)

1mārkaṇḍeya uvāca|
tasyāṃ rātryāṃ vyatītāyāmudite sūryamaṇḍale
|
kṛtapaurvāhṇikāḥ sarve sameyuste tapodhanāḥ||1||

2tadeva sarvaṃ sāvitryā mahābhāgyaṃ maharṣayaḥ
|
dyumatsenāya nātṛpyan kathayantaḥ punaḥ punaḥ||2||

3tataḥ prakṛtayaḥ sarvāḥ śālvebhyo'bhyāgatā nṛpa
|
ācakhyurnihataṃ caiva svenāmātyena taṃ dviṣam||3||

4taṃ mantriṇā hataṃ śrutvā sasahāyaṃ sabāndhavam
|
nyavedayan yathāvṛttaṃ vidrutaṃ ca dviṣadbalam||4||

5aikamatyaṃ ca sarvasya janasyātha nṛpaṃ prati
|
sacakṣurvā'pyacakṣurvā sa no rājā bhavatviti||5||

6anena niścayeneha vayaṃ prasthāpitā nṛpa
|
prāptānīmāni yānāni caturaṅgaṃ ca te balam||6||

7prayāhi rājan bhadraṃ te ghuṣṭaste nagare jayaḥ
|
adhyāsva cirarātrāya pitṛpaitāmahaṃ padam||7||

8cakṣuṣmantaṃ ca taṃ dṛṣṭā rājānaṃ vapuṣā'nvitam
|
mūrdhnā nipatitāḥ sarve vismayotphullalocanāḥ||8||

9tato'bhivādya tān vṛddhān dvijānāśramavāsinaḥ
|
taiścābhipūjitaḥ sarvaiḥ prayayau nagaraṃ prati||9||

10śaibyā ca saha sāvitryā svāstīrṇena suvarcasā
|
narayuktena yānena prayayau senayā vṛtā||10||

11tato'bhiṣiṣicuḥ prītyā dyumatsenaṃ purohitāḥ
|
putraṃ cāsya mahātmānaṃ yauvarājye'bhyaṣecayan||11||

12tataḥ kālena mahatā sāvitryāḥ kīrtivardhanam
|
tadvai putraśataṃ jajñe śūrāṇāmanivartinām||12||

13bhrātṝṇāṃ sodarāṇāṃ ca tathaivāsyābhavacchatam
|
madrādhipasyāśvapatermālavyāṃ sumahadbalam||13||

14evamātmā pitā mātā śvaśrūḥ śvaśura eva ca
|
bhartuḥ kulaṃ ca sāvitryā sarvaṃ kṛcchrāt samuddhṛtam||14||

15tathaivaiṣā hi kalyāṇī draupadī śīlasammatā
|
tārayiṣyati vaḥ sarvān sāvitrīva kulāṅganā||15||

16vaiśampāyana uvāca|
evaṃ sa pāṇḍavastena anunīto mahātmanā
|
viśoko vijvaro rājan kāmyake nyavasat tadā||16||

17yaścedaṃ śṛṇuyādbhaktyā sāvitryākhyānamuttamam
|
sa sukhī sarvasiddhārtho na duḥkhaṃ prāpnuyānnaraḥ||17||
