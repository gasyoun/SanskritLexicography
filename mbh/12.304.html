Mahābhārata 12.304 (Śāntiparvan)

1vasiṣṭha uvāca |
evamapratibuddhatvādabuddhajanasevanāt
|
sargakoṭisahasrāṇi patanāntāni gacchati||1||

2dhāmnā dhāmasahasrāṇi maraṇāntāni gacchati
|
tiryagyonimanuṣyatve devaloke tathaiva ca||2||

3candramā iva bhūtānāṃ punastatra sahasraśaḥ
|
līyate'pratibuddhatvādevameṣa hyabuddhimān||3||

4kalā pañcadaśī yonistaddhāma pratibudhyate
|
nityametadvijānīhi somaṃ vai ṣoḍaśīṃ kalām||4||

5kalāyāṃ jāyate'jasraṃ punaḥ punarabuddhimān
|
dhāma tasyopayuñjanti bhūya evopajāyate||5||

6ṣoḍaśī tu kalā sūkṣmā sa soma upadhāryatām
|
na tūpayujyate devairdevānupayunakti sā||6||

7etāmakṣapayitvā hi jāyate nṛpasattama
|
sā hyasya prakṛtirdṛṣṭā tatkṣayānmokṣa ucyate||7||

8tadeva ṣoḍaśakalaṃ dehamavyaktasaṃjñakam
|
mamāyamiti manvānastatraiva parivartate||8||

9pañcaviṃśo mahānātmā tasyaivāpratibodhanāt
|
vimalasya viśuddhasya śuddhāśuddhaniṣevaṇāt||9||

10aśuddha eva śuddhātmā tādṛgbhavati pārthiva
|
abuddhasevanāccāpi buddho'pyabuddhatāṃ vrajet||10||

11tathaivāpratibuddho'pi vijñeyo nṛpasattama
|
prakṛtestriguṇāyāstu sevanāttriguṇo bhavet||11||
