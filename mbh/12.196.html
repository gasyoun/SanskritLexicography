Mahābhārata 12.196 (Śāntiparvan)

1yudhiṣṭhira uvāca|
cāturāśramyamuktaṃ te rājadharmāstathaiva ca
|
nānāśrayāśca bahava itihāsāḥ pṛthagvidhāḥ ||1||

2śrutāstvattaḥ kathāścaiva dharmayuktā mahāmate
|
sandeho'sti tu kaścinme tadbhavānvaktumarhati ||2||

3jāpakānāṃ phalāvāptiṃ śrotumicchāmi bhārata
|
kaṃ phalaṃ japatāmuktaṃ kva vā tiṣṭhanti jāpakāḥ ||3||

4japyasya ca vidhiṃ kṛtsnaṃ vaktumarhasi me'nagha
|
jāpakā iti kiñcaitatsāṃkhyayogakriyāvidhiḥ ||4||

5kiṃ yajñavidhirevaiṣa kimetajjapyamucyate
|
etanme sarvamācakṣva sarvajño hyasi me mataḥ ||5||

6bhīṣma uvāca|
atrāpyudāharantīmamitihāsaṃ purātanam
|
yamasya yatpurāvṛttaṃ kālasya brāhmaṇasya ca ||6||

7sāṃkhyayogau tu yāvuktau munibhirmokṣadarśibhiḥ
|
saṃnyāsa eva vedānte vartate japanaṃ prati ||7||

8vedavādāśca nirvṛttāḥ śāntā brahmaṇyavasthitāḥ
|
sāṃkhyayogau tu yāvuktau munibhiḥ samadarśibhiḥ ||8||

9mārgau tāvapyubhāvetau saṃśritau na ca saṃśritau
|
yathā saṃśrūyate rājan kāraṇaṃ cātra vakṣyate
|
manaḥsamādhiratrāpi tathendriyajayaḥ smṛtaḥ ||9||

10satyamagniparīcāro viviktānāṃ ca sevanam
|
dhyānaṃ tapo damaḥ kṣāntiranasūyā mitāśanam ||10||

11viṣayapratisaṃhāro mitajalpastathā śamaḥ
|
eṣa pravartako yajño nivartakamatho śṛṇu ||11||

12yathā nivartate karma japato brahmacāriṇaḥ
|
etatsarvamaśeṣeṇa yathoktaṃ parivartayet ||12||

13nivṛttaṃ mārgamāsādya vyaktāvyaktamanāśrayam ||13||

14kuśoccayaniṣaṇṇaḥ san kuśahastaḥ kuśaiḥ śikhī
|
kuśaiḥ parivṛtastasmin madhye channaḥ kuśaistathā ||14||

15viṣayebhyo namaskuryādviṣayānna ca bhāvayet
|
sāmyamutpādya manasā manasyeva mano dadhat ||15||

16taddhiyā dhyāyati brahma japanvai saṃhitāṃ hitām
|
saṃnyasyatyathavā tāṃ vai samādhau paryavasthitaḥ ||16||

17dhyānamutpādayatyatra saṃhitābalasaṃśrayāt
|
śuddhātmā tapasā dānto nivṛttadveṣakāmavān ||17||

18arāgamoho nirdvandvo na śocati na sajjate
|
na kartā kāraṇānāṃ ca na kāryāṇāmiti sthitiḥ ||18||

19na cāhaṅkārayogena manaḥ prasthāpayetkvacit
|
na cārthagrahaṇe yukto nāvamānī na cākriyaḥ ||19||

20dhyānakriyāparo yukto dhyānavān dhyānaniścayaḥ
|
dhyāne samādhimutpādya tadapi tyajati kramāt ||20||

21sa vai tasmāvasthāyāṃ sarvatyāgakṛtaḥ sukham
|
niricchastyajati prāṇān brāhmīṃ saṃviśate tanum ||21||

22athavā necchate tatra brahmakāyaniṣevaṇam
|
utkrāmati ca mārgastho naiva kvacana jāyate ||22||

23ātmabuddhyā samāsthāya śāntībhūto nirāmayaḥ
|
amṛtaṃ virajaḥ śuddhamātmānaṃ pratipadyate ||23||
