Mahābhārata 7.9 (Droṇaparvan)

1dhṛtarāṣṭra uvāca|
kiṃ kurvāṇaṃ raṇe droṇaṃ jaghnuḥ pāṇḍavasṛñjayāḥ
|
tathā nipuṇamastreṣu sarvaśastrabhṛtāmapi||1||

2rathabhaṅgo babhūvāsya dhanurvāśīryatāsyataḥ
|
pramatto vā'bhavaddroṇastato mṛtyumupeyivān||2||

3kathaṃ tu pārṣatastāta śatrubhirduṣpradharṣaṇam
|
kirantabhiṣusaṅghātān rukmapuṃkhānanekaśaḥ||3||

4kṣiprahastaṃ dvijaśreṣṭhaṃ kṛtinaṃ citrayodhinam
|
dūreṣupātinaṃ dāntamastrayuddheṣu pāragam||4||

5pāñcālaputro nyavadhīddivyāstradharamacyutam
|
kurvāṇaṃ dāruṇaṃ karma raṇe yattaṃ mahāratham||5||

6vyaktaṃ hi daivaṃ balavatpauruṣāditi me matiḥ
|
yaddroṇo nihataḥ śūraḥ pārṣatena mahātmanā||6||

7astraṃ caturvidhaṃ vīre yasminnāsīt pratiṣṭhitam
|
tamiṣvastradharācāryaṃ droṇaṃ śaṃsasi me hatam||7||

8śrutvā hataṃ rukmarathaṃ vaiyāghraparivāritam
|
jātarūpapariṣkāraṃ nādya śokasupādade||8||

9na nūnaṃ paraduḥkhena mriyate ko'pi sañjaya
|
yatra droṇamahaṃ śrutvā hataṃ jīvāmi mandadhīḥ||9||

10daivameva paraṃ manye nanvanarthaṃ hi pauruṣam
|
aśmasāramayaṃ nūnaṃ hṛdayaṃ sudṛḍhaṃ mama||10||

11yacchrutvā nihataṃ droṇaṃ śatadhā na vidīryate
|
brāhme daive tatheṣvastre yamupāsan guṇārthinaḥ||11||

12brāhmaṇā rājaputrāśca sa kathaṃ mṛtyunā hṛtaḥ
|
śoṣaṇaṃ sāgarasyeva meroriva visarpaṇam||12||

13patanaṃ bhāskarasyeva na mṛṣye droṇapātanam
|
duṣṭānāṃ pratiṣeddhāsīddhārmikāṇāṃ ca rakṣitā||13||

14yo'hāsīt kṛpaṇarsyotha prāṇānapi paraṃtapaḥ
|
mandānāṃ mama putrāṇāṃ jayāśā yasya vikrame||14||

15bṛhaspatyuśanastulyo buddhyā sa nihataḥ katham
|
te ca śoṇā bṛhanto'śvāśchannā jālairhiraṇmayaiḥ||15||

16rathe vātajavāyuktāḥ sarvaśastrātigā raṇe
|
balino hreṣiṇo dāntāḥ saindhavāḥ sādhuvāhinaḥ||16||

17dṛḍhāḥ saṃgrāmamadhyeṣu kaccidāsanna vihvalāḥ
|
kariṇāṃ bṛṃhatāṃ yuddhe śaṃkhadundubhiniḥsvanaiḥ||17||

18jyākṣepaśaravarṣāṇāṃ śastrāṇāṃ ca sahiṣṇavaḥ
|
āśaṃsantaḥ parāñjetuṃ jitaśvāsā jitavyathāḥ||18||

19hayāḥ parājitāḥ śīghrā bhāradvājarathodvahāḥ
|
te sma rukmarathe yuktā naravīrasamāhatāḥ||19||

20kathaṃ nābhyataraṃstāta pāṇḍavānāmanīkinīm
|
jātarūpapariṣkāramāsthāya rathamuttamam||20||

21bhāradvājaḥ kimakarodyudhi satyaparākramaḥ
|
vidyāṃ yasyopajīvanti sarvalokadhanurdharāḥ||21||

22sa satyasandho balavān droṇaḥ kimakarodyudhi
|
divi śakramiva śreṣṭhaṃ mahāmātraṃ dhanurbhṛtām||22||

23ke nu taṃ raudrakarmāṇaṃ yuddhe pratyudyayū rathāḥ
|
nanu rukmarathaṃ dṛṣṭvā prādravanti sma pāṇḍavāḥ||23||

24divyamastraṃ vikurvāṇaṃ raṇe tasminmahābalam
|
utāho sarvasainyena dharmarājaḥ sahānujaḥ||24||

25pāñcālyapragraho droṇaṃ sarvataḥ samavārayat
|
nūnamāvārayatpārtho rathino'nyānajihmagaiḥ||25||

26tato droṇaṃ samārohat pārṣataḥ pāpakarmakṛt
|
na hyahaṃ paripaśyāmi vadhe kañcana śuṣmiṇaḥ||26||

27dhṛṣṭadyumnādṛte raudrāt pālyamānāt kirīṭinā
|
tairvṛtaḥ sarvataḥ śūraḥ pāñcālyāpasadastataḥ||27||

28kekayaiścedikārūpairmatsyairanyaiśca bhūmipaiḥ
|
vyākulīkṛtamācāryaṃ pipīlairuragaṃ yathā||28||

29karmaṇyasukare saktaṃ jaghāneti matirmama
|
yo'dhītya caturo vedān sāṅgānākhyānapañcamān||29||

30brāhmaṇānāṃ pratiṣṭhāsīt srotasāmiva sāgaraḥ
|
kṣatraṃ ca brahma caiveha yo'bhyatiṣṭhat paraṃtapaḥ||30||

31sa kathaṃ brāhmaṇo vṛddhaḥ śastreṇa vadhamāptavān
|
amarṣiṇā marpitavān kliśyamānānsadā mayā||31||

32anarhamāṇān kaunteyān karmaṇastasya tat phalam
|
yasya karmānujīvanti loke sarvadhanurbhṛtaḥ||32||

33sa satyasandhaḥ sukṛtī śrīkāmaurnihataḥ katham
|
divi śakra iva śreṣṭho mahāsattvo mahābalaḥ||33||

34sa kathaṃ nihataḥ pārthaiḥ kṣudramatsyairyathā timiḥ
|
kṣiprahastaśca balavān dṛḍhadhanvā'rimardanaḥ||34||

35na yasya vijayākāṃkṣī viṣayaṃ prāpya jīvati
|
yaṃ dvau na jahataḥ śabdau jīvamānaṃ kadācana||35||

36brāhmaśca vedakāmānāṃ jyāghoṣaśca dhanuṣmatām
|
adīnaṃ puruṣavyāghraṃ hrīmantamaparājitam||36||

37nāhaṃ mṛṣye hataṃ droṇaṃ siṃhadviradavikramam
|
kathaṃ sañjaya durdharṣamanādhṛṣyayaśobalam||37||

38paśyatāṃ puruṣendrāṇāṃ samare pārṣato'vadhīt
|
ke purastādayudhyanta rakṣanto droṇamantikāt||38||

39ke tu paścādavartanta gacchato durgamāṃ gatim
|
ke'rakṣan dakṣiṇaṃ cakraṃ savyaṃ ke ca mahātmanaḥ||39||

40purastāt ke ca vīrasya yudhyamānasya saṃyuge
|
ke ca tasmiṃstanūṃstyaktvā pratīpaṃ mṛtyumāvrajan||40||

41droṇasya samare vīrāḥ ke'kurvanta parāṃ dhṛtim
|
kaccinnainaṃ bhayānmandāḥ kṣatriyā vyajahan raṇe||41||

42rakṣitārastataḥ śūnye kaścittairna hataḥ paraiḥ
|
na sa pṛṣṭhamarestrāsādraṇe śauryāt pradarśayet||42||

43parāmapyāpadaṃ prāpya sa kathaṃ nihataḥ paraiḥ
|
etadāryeṇa kartavyaṃ kṛcchrāsvāpatsu sañjaya||43||

44parākramedyathāśaktyā tacca tasmin pratiṣṭhitam
|
muhyate me manastāta kathā tāvannivāryatām||44||

45bhūyastu labdhasaṃjñastvāṃ paripṛcchāmi sañjaya||45||
