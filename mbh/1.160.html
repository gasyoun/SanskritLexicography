Mahābhārata 1.160 (Ādiparvan)

1kuntyuvāca|
kuto mūlamidaṃ duḥkhaṃ jñātumicchāmi tattvataḥ
|
viditvāpyapakarṣeyaṃ śakyaṃ cedapakarṣitum||1||

2brāhmaṇa uvāca|
upapannaṃ satāmetadyadbravīṣi tapodhane
|
na tu duḥkhamidaṃ śakyaṃ mānuṣeṇa vyapohitum||2||

3samīpe nagarasyāsya vako vasati rākṣasaḥ
|
īśo janapadasyāsya purasya ca mahābalaḥ||3||

4puṣṭo mānuṣamāṃsena durbuddhiḥ puruṣādakaḥ
|
rakṣatyasurarāṇnityamimaṃ janapadaṃ balī||4||

5nagaraṃ caiva deśaṃ ca rakṣobalasamanvitaḥ
|
tatkṛte paracakrācca bhūtebhyaśca na no bhayam||5||

6vetanaṃ tasya vihitaṃ śālivāhasya bhojanam
|
mahiṣau puruṣaścaiko yastadādāya gacchati||6||

7ekaikaścāpi puruṣastatprayacchati bhojanam
|
sa vāro bahubhirvarṣairbhavatyasukaro naraiḥ||7||

8tadvimokṣāya ye kecidyatante puruṣāḥ kvacit
|
sa putradārāṃstānhatvā tadrakṣo bhakṣayatyuta||8||

9vetrakīyagṛhe rājā nāyaṃ nayamihāsthitaḥ
|
upāyaṃ taṃ na kurute yatnādapi sa mandadhīḥ
|
anāmayaṃ janasyāsya yena syādadya śāśvatam||9||

10etadarhā vacaṃ nūnaṃ vasāmo durbalasya ye
|
viṣaye nityavāstavyāḥ kurājānamupāśritāḥ||10||

11brāhmaṇāḥ kasya vaktavyāḥ kasya vā chandacāriṇaḥ
|
guṇairete hi vatsyanti kāmagāḥ pakṣiṇo yathā||11||

12rājānaṃ prathamaṃ vindettato bhāryāṃ tato dhanam
|
trayasya saṃcayenāsya jñātīnputrāṃśca tārayet||12||

13viparītaṃ mayā cedaṃ trayaṃ sarvamupārjitam
|
tadimāmāpadaṃ prāpya bhṛśaṃ tapyāmahe vayam||13||

14soyamasmānanuprāpto vāraḥ kulavināśanaḥ
|
bhojanaṃ puruṣaścaikaḥ pradeyaṃ vetanaṃ mayā||14||

15na ca me vidyate vittaṃ saṃkretuṃ puruṣaṃ kvacit
|
suhṛjjanaṃ pradātuṃ ca na śakṣyāmi kadācana||15||

16gatiṃ caiva na paśyāmi tasmānmokṣāya rakṣasaḥ
|
so'haṃ duḥkhārṇave magno mahatyasukare bhṛśam||16||

17sahaivaitairgamiṣyāmi bāndhavairadya rākṣasam
|
tato naḥ sahitānkṣudraḥ sarvānevopabhokṣyati||17||
