Mahābhārata 8.92 (Karṇaparvan)

1sañjaya uvāca|
śalyastu karṇārjunayorvimarde balāni dṛṣṭvā mṛditāni bāṇaiḥ
|
yayau hate cādhirathau padānuge rathena saṃchinnaparicchadena ||1||

2nipātitasyanandanavājināgaṃ balaṃ ca dṛṣṭvā hatasūtaputram
|
duryodhano'śrupratipūrṇanetro dīno muhurniḥśvasaṃścārtarūpaḥ ||2||

3karṇaṃ tu śūraṃ patitaṃ pṛthivyāṃ śarācitaṃ śoṇitadigdhagātram
|
yadṛcchayā sūryamivāvanisthaṃ didṛkṣavaḥ saṃparivārya tasthuḥ ||3||

4prahṛṣṭavitrastaviṣaṇṇavismitāstathā pare śokahatā ivābhavan
|
pare tvadīyāśca paraspareṇa yathāyathaiṣāṃ prakṛtistathā'bhavan ||4||

5praviddhavarmābharaṇāmbarāyudhaṃ dhanañjayenābhihataṃ mahaujasam
|
niśāmya karṇaṃ kuravaḥ pradudruvurhatarṣabhā gāva ivājane vane ||5||

6bhīmaśca bhīmena tadā svanena nādaṃ kṛtvā rodasī kampayānaḥ
|
āsphoṭayanvalgate nṛtyate ca hate karṇe trāsayan dhārtarāṣṭrān ||6||

7tathaiva rājan somakāḥ sṛñjayāśca śaṅkhān dadhmuḥ sasvajuścāpi sarve
|
parasparaṃ kṣatriyā hṛṣṭarūpāḥ sūtātmaje vai nihate tadānīm ||7||

8kṛtvā vimardaṃ mahadarjunena karṇo hataḥ kesariṇeva nāgaḥ
|
tīrṇā pratijñā puruṣarṣabheṇa vairasyāntaṃ gatavāṃścāpi pārthaḥ ||8||

9madrādhipaścāpi vimūḍhacetāstūrṇaṃ rathenāpakṛtadhvajena
|
duryodhanasyāntikametya rājan sabāṣpaduḥkhādvacanaṃ babhāṣe ||9||

10viśīrṇanāgāśvarathapravīraṃ balaṃ tvadīyaṃ yamarāṣṭrakalpam
|
anyonyamāsādya hataṃ mahadbhirnarāśvanāgairgirikūṭakalpaiḥ ||10||

11naitādṛśaṃ bhārata yuddhamāsīdyathā tu karṇārjunayorbabhūva
|
grastau hi karṇena sametya kṛṣṇāvanye ca sarve tava śatravo ye ||11||

12daivaṃ dhruvaṃ pārthavaśātpravṛttaṃ yatpāṇḍavānpāti hinasti cāsmān
|
tavārthasiddhyarthakarāstu sarve prasahya vīrā nihatā dviṣadbhiḥ ||12||

13kuberavaivasvatavāsavānāṃ tulyaprabhāvā nṛpate suvīrāḥ
|
vīryeṇa śauryeṇa balena tejasā taistaistu yuktā vividhairguṇaughaiḥ ||13||

14avadhyakalpā nihatā narendrāstavārthakāmā yudhi pāṇḍaveyaiḥ
|
tānmā śuco bhārata diṣṭametat paryāśvasa tvaṃ na sadā'sti siddhiḥ ||14||

15etadvaco madrapaterniśamya svaṃ cāpyanītaṃ manasā nirīkṣya
|
duryodhano dīnamanā visaṃjñaḥ punaḥ punarnyaśvasadārtarūpaḥ ||15||
