Mahābhārata 14.72 (Āśvamedhikaparvan)

1vaiśampāyana uvāca|
evamuktastu kṛṣṇena dharmaputro yudhiṣṭhiraḥ
|
vyāsamāmantrya medhāvī tato vacanamabravīt||1||

2yadā kālaṃ bhavānvetti hayamedhasya tattvataḥ
|
dīkṣayasva tadā māṃ tvaṃ tvayyāyatto hi me kratuḥ||2||

3vyāsa uvāca|
ahaṃ pailo'tha kaunteya yājñavalkyastathaiva ca
|
vidhānaṃ yadyathākālaṃ tatkartāro na saṃśayaḥ||3||

4caitryāṃ hi paurṇamāsyāṃ tu tava dīkṣā bhaviṣyati
|
sambhārāḥ sambhiyantāṃ ca yajñārthaṃ puruṣarṣabha||4||

5aśvavidyāvidaścaiva sūtā viprāśca tadvidaḥ
|
medhyamaśvaṃ parīkṣantāṃ tava yajñārthasiddhaye||5||

6tamutsṛja yathāśāstraṃ pṛthivīṃ sāgarāmbarām
|
sa paryetu yaśo dīptaṃ tava pārthiva darśayan||6||

7vaiśampāyana uvāca|
ityuktaḥ sa tathetyuktvā pāṇḍavaḥ pṛthivīpatiḥ
|
cakāra sarve rājendra yathoktaṃ brahmavādinā||7||

8sambhāraścaiva rājendra sarve saṅkalpitā'bhavan
|
sa sambhārānsamāhṛtya nṛpo dharmasutastadā||8||

9nyavedayadameyātmā kṛṣṇadvaipāyanāya vai
|
tato'bravīnmahātejā vyāso dharmātmajaṃ nṛpam||9||

10yathākālaṃ yathāyogaṃ sajjāḥ sma tava dīkṣaṇe
|
sphyaśca kūrcaśca sauvarṇo yaccānyadapi kaurava||10||

11tatra yogyaṃ bhavetkiñcidraukmaṃ tatkriyatāmiti
|
aśvaścotsṛjyatāmadya pṛthvyāmatha yathākramam
|
suguptaṃ caratāṃ cāpi yathāśāstraṃ yathāvidhi||11||

12yudhiṣṭhira uvāca|
ayamaśvo yathā brahmannustṛṣṭaḥ pṛthivīmimām
|
cariṣyati yathākāmaṃ tatra vai saṃvidhīyatām||12||

13pṛthivīṃ paryaṭantaṃ hi turagaṃ kāmacāriṇam
|
kaḥ pālayediti mune tadbhavānvaktumarhati||13||

14vaiśampāyana uvāca|
ityuktaḥ sa tu rājendra kṛṣṇadvaipāyano'bravīt
|
bhīmasenādavarajaḥ śreṣṭhaḥ sarvadhanuṣmatām||14||

15jiṣṇuḥ sahiṣṇurdhṛṣṇuśca sa enaṃ pālayiṣyati
|
śaktaḥ sa hi mahīṃ jetuṃ nivātakavacāntakaḥ||15||

16tasmin vastrāṇi divyāni divyaṃ saṃhananaṃ tathā
|
divyaṃ dhanuśceṣudhī ca sa enamanuyāsyati||16||

17sa hi dharmārthakuśalaḥ sarvavidyāviśāradaḥ
|
yathāśāstraṃ nṛpaśreṣṭha cārayiṣyati te hayam||17||

18rājaputro mahābāhuḥ śyāmo rājīvalocanaḥ
|
abhimanyoḥ pitā vīraḥ sa enaṃ pālayiṣyati||18||

19bhīmaseno'pi tejasvī kaunteyo'mitavikramaḥ
|
samartho rakṣituṃ rāṣṭraṃ nakulaśca viśāmpate||19||

20sahadevastu kauravya samādhāsyati buddhimān
|
kuṭumbatantraṃ vidhivatsarvameva mahāyaśāḥ||20||

21tattu sarve yathānyāyamuktaḥ kurukulodvahaḥ
|
cakāra phālgunaṃ cāpi sandideśa hayaṃ prati||21||

22yudhiṣṭhira uvāca|
ehyarjuna tvayā vīra hayo'yaṃ paripālyatām
|
tvamarho rakṣituṃ hyenaṃ nānyaḥ kaścana mānavaḥ||22||

23ye cāpi tvāṃ mahābāho pratyudyānti narādhipāḥ
|
tairvigraho yathā na syāttathā kāryaṃ tvayānagha||23||

24ākhyātavyaśca bhavatā yajño'yaṃ mama sarvaśaḥ
|
pārthivebhyo mahābāho samaye gamyatāmiti||24||

25vaiśampāyana uvāca|
evamuktvā ca dharmātmā bhrātaraṃ savyasācinam
|
bhīmaṃ ca nakulaṃ caiva puraguptau samādadhat||25||

26kuṭumbatantre tadā sahadevaṃ yudhāṃ patim
|
anumānya mahīpālaṃ dhṛtarāṣṭraṃ yudhiṣṭhiraḥ||26||
