Mahābhārata 14.26 (Āśvamedhikaparvan)

1brāhmaṇa uvāca|
ekaḥ śāstā na dvitīyo'sti śāstā yo hṛcchayastamahamanubravīmi
|
tenaiva yuktaḥ pravaṇādivodakaṃ yathāniyukto'smi tathā vahāmi||1||

2eko gururnāsti tato dvitīyo yo hṛcchayastamahamanubravīmi
|
tenānuśiṣṭā guruṇā sadaiva loke dviṣṭāḥ pannagāḥ sarva eva||2||

3eko bandhurnāsti tato dvitīyo yo hṛcchayastamahamanubravīmi
|
tenānuśiṣṭā bāndhavā bandhumantaḥ saptarṣayaḥ pārtha divi prabhānti||3||

4eka: śrotā nāsti tato dvitīyo yo hṛcchayastamahamanubravīmi
|
tasmingurau guruvāsaṃ niruṣya śakro gataḥ sarvalokāmaratvam||4||

5eko dveṣṭā nāsti tato dvitīyo yo hṛcchayastamahamanubravīmi
|
tenānuśiṣṭā guruṇā sadaiva loke dviṣṭāḥ pannagāḥ sarva eva||5||

6atrāpyudāharantīmamitihāsaṃ purātanam
|
prajāpatau pannagānāṃ devarṣīṇāṃ ca saṃvidam||6||

7devarṣayaśca nāgāścāpyasurāśca prajāpatim
|
paryapṛcchannupāsīnāḥ śreyo naḥ procyatāmiti||7||

8teṣāṃ provāca bhagavāśreyaḥ samanupṛcchatām
|
omityekākṣaraṃ brahma te śrutvā prādravan diśaḥ||8||

9teṣāṃ pradravamāṇānāmupadeśārthamātmanaḥ
|
saryāṇāṃ daṃśane bhāvaḥ pravṛttaḥ pūrvameva tu||9||

10asurāṇāṃ pravṛttastu dambhabhāvaḥ svabhāvajaḥ
|
dānaṃ devā vyavasitā damameva maharṣayaḥ||10||

11ekaṃ śāstāramāsādya śabdenaikena saṃskṛtāḥ
|
nānā vyavasitāḥ sarve sarpadevarpidānavāḥ||11||

12śṛṇotyayaṃ procyamānaṃ gṛhṇāti ca yathātatham
|
pṛcchatastadato bhūyo gururanyo na vidyate||12||

13tasya cānumate karma tataḥ paścāt pravartate
|
gururboddhā ca śrotā ca dveṣṭā ca hṛdi niḥsṛtaḥ||13||

14pāpena vicarallauke pāpacārī bhavatyayam
|
śubhena vicarallauke śubhacārī bhavatyuta||14||

15kāmacārī tu kāmena ya indriyasukhe rataḥ
|
brahmacārī sadaivaiṣa ya indriyajaye rataḥ||15||

16apetavratakarmā tu kevalaṃ brahmaṇi sthitaḥ
|
brahmabhūtaścara~lloke brahmacārī bhavatyayam||16||

17brahmaiva samidhastasya brahmāgnirbrahmasambhavaḥ
|
āpo brahma gururbahma sa brahmaṇi samāhitaḥ||17||

18etadevedṛśaṃ sūkṣmaṃ brahmacaryaṃ vidurbudhāḥ
|
viditvā cānvapadyanta kṣetrajñenānudarśitāḥ||18||
