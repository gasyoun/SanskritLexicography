Mahābhārata 12.265 (Śāntiparvan)

1bhīṣma uvāca|
atrāpyudāharantīmamitihāsaṃ purātanam
|
prajāranāmanukampārthaṃ gītaṃ rājñā vicakhnunā ||1||

2chinnasthūṇaṃ vṛṣaṃ dṛṣṭvā vilāpaṃ ca gavāṃ bhṛśam
|
gograhe yajñavāṭasya prekṣamāṇaḥ sa pārthivaḥ ||2||

3svasti gobhyo'stu lokeṣu tato nirvacanaṃ kṛtam
|
hiṃsāyāṃ hi pravṛttāyāmāśīreṣā tu kalpitā ||3||

4avyavasthitamaryādairvimūḍhairnāstikainaraiḥ
|
saṃśayātmabhiravyaktairhiṃsā samanuvarṇitā ||4||

5sarvakarmasvahiṃsā hi dharmātmā manurabravīt
|
kāmakārādvihiṃsanti bahirvedyāṃ paśūnnarāḥ ||5||

6tasmātpramāṇataḥ kāryo dharmaḥ sūkṣmo vijānatā
|
ahiṃsā sarvabhūtebhyo dharmebhyo jyāyasī matā ||6||

7upoṣya saṃśito bhūtvā hitvā vedakṛtāḥ śrutīḥ
|
ācāra ityanācāraḥ kṛpaṇāḥ phalahetavaḥ ||7||

8yadi yajñāṃśca vṛkṣāṃśca yūpāṃścoddiśya mānavāḥ
|
vṛthā māṃsaṃ na khādanti naiṣa dharmaḥ praśasyate ||8||

9surā matsyā madhu māsaṃmāsavaṃ kṛsaraudanam
|
dhūrtaiḥ pravartitaṃ hyetannaitadvedeṣu kalpitam ||9||

10mānānmohācca lobhācca laulyametatprakalpitam
|
viṣṇumevābhijānanti sarvayajñeṣu brāhmaṇāḥ ||10||

11pāyasaiḥ sumanobhiśca tasyāpi yajanaṃ smṛtam
|
yajñiyāścaiva ye vṛkṣā vedeṣu parikalpitāḥ ||11||

12yaccāpi kiṃcitkartavyamanyaccokṣaiḥ susaṃskṛtam
|
mahatsattvaiḥ śuddhabhāvaiḥ sarvaṃ devārhameva tat ||12||

13yudhiṣṭhira uvāca|
śarīramāpadaścāpi vivadantyavihiṃsataḥ
|
kathaṃ yātrā śarīrasya nirārambhasya setsyate ||13||

14bhīṣma uvāca|
yathā śarīraṃ na glāyenneyānmṛtyuvaśaṃ yathā
|
tathā karmasu varteta samartho dharmamācaret ||14||
