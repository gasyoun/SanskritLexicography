Mahābhārata 1.135 (Ādiparvan)

1vaiśaṃpāyana uvāca|
kururāje hi raṅgasthe bhīme ca balināṃ vare
|
pakṣapātakṛtasnehaḥ sa dvidhevābhavajjanaḥ||1||

2hī vīra kururājeti jaya he bhīma iti jalpatām
|
puruṣāṇāṃ suvipulāḥ praṇādāḥ sahasotthitāḥ||2||

3tataḥ kṣubdhārṇavanibhaṃ raṅgamālokya buddhimān
|
bhāradvājaḥ priyaṃ putramaśvatthāmānamabravīt||3||

4droṇa uvāca|
vārayaitau mahāvīryau kṛtayogyāvubhāvapi
|
mā bhūdraṅgaprakopo'yaṃ bhīmaduryodhanodbhavaḥ||4||

5vaiśaṃpāyana uvāca|
tatastāvudyatagadau guruputreṇa vāritau
|
yugāntānilasaṃkṣubdhau mahāvelāvivārṇavau||5||

6tato raṅgāṅgaṇagato droṇo vacanamabravīt
|
nivārya vāditragaṇaṃ mahāmeghanibhasvanam||6||

7yo me putrātpriyataraḥ sarvaśastraviśāradaḥ
|
aindririndrānujasamaḥ sa pārtho dṛśyatāmiti||7||

8ācāryavacane nātha kṛtasvastyayano yuvā
|
baddhagodhāṅgulitrāṇaḥ pūrṇatūṇaḥ sakārmukaḥ||8||

9kāñcanaṃ kavacaṃ bibhratpratyadṛśya phālgunaḥ
|
sārkaḥ sendrāyudhataḍitsasandhya iva toyadaḥ||9||

10tataḥ sarvasya raṅgasya samutpiñjalako'bhavat
|
prāvādyanta ca vādyāni śaṅkhāni samantataḥ||10||

11eṣa kuntīsutaḥ śrīmāneṣa madhyamapāṇḍavaḥ
|
eṣa putro mahendrasya kurūṇāmeṣa rakṣitā||11||

12eṣo'straviduṣāṃ śreṣṭha eṣa dharmabhṛtāṃ varaḥ
|
eṣa śīlavatāṃ cāpi śīlajñānanidhiḥ paraḥ||12||

13ityevaṃ tumulā vācaḥ śuśruvuḥ prekṣakeritāḥ
|
kuntyāḥ prasravasaṃyuktairasraiḥ klinnamuro'bhavat||13||

14tena śabdena mahatā pūrṇaśrutirathābravīt
|
dhṛtarāṣṭro naraśreṣṭho viduraṃ hṛṣṭamānasaḥ||14||

15kṣattaḥ kṣubdhārṇavanibhaḥ kimeṣa sumahāsvanaḥ
|
sahasaivotthito raṅge bhindanniva nabhastalam||15||

16vidura uvāca|
eṣa pārtho mahārāja phālgunaḥ pāṇḍunandanaḥ
|
avatīrṇaḥ sakavacastatraiva sumihāsvanaḥ||16||

17dhṛtarāṣṭra uvāca|
dhanyo'smyanugṛhīto'smi rakṣito'smi mahāmate
|
pṛthāraṇisamudbhūtaistribhiḥ pāṇḍavavahnibhiḥ||17||

18vaiśaṃpāyana uvāca|
tasminpramudite raṅge kathaṃcitpratyupasthite
|
darśayāmāsa bībhatsurācāryāyāstralāghavam||18||

19āgneyenāsṛjadvahniṃ vāruṇenāsṛjatpayaḥ
|
vāyavyenāsṛjadvayuṃ pārjanyenāsṛjaddhanān||19||

20bhaumena prāsṛjadbhūmiṃ pārvatenāsṛjadgirīn
|
antardhānena cāstreṇa punarantarhito'bhavat||20||

21kṣaṇātprāṃśuḥ kṣaṇāddhrasvaḥ kṣaṇācca rathadhūrgataḥ
|
kṣaṇena rathamadhyasthaḥ kṣaṇenāvataranmahīm||21||

22sukumāraṃ ca sūkṣmaṃ ca guru cāpi gurupriyaḥ
|
sauṣṭhavenābhisaṃkṣiptaḥ so'vidhyadvividhaiḥ śaraiḥ||22||

23bhramataśca varāhasya lohasya pramukhe samam
|
pañca bāṇānasaṃktānsaṃmumocaikabāṇavat||23||

24gavye viṣāṇakośe ca cale rajjvavalambini
|
nicakhāna mahāvīryaḥ sāyakānekaviṃśatim||24||

25ityevamādi sumahatkhaḍge dhanuṣi cānagha
|
gadāyāṃ śastrakuśalo maṇḍalāni hyadarśayat||25||

26tataḥ samāptabhūyiṣṭhe tasminkarmaṇi bhārata
|
mandībhūte samāje ca vāditrasya ca niḥsvane||26||

27dvāradeśātsamudbhūto māhātmyabalasūcakaḥ
|
vajraniṣpeṣasadṛśaḥ śuśruve bhujaniḥsvanaḥ||27||

28dīryante kiṃ nu girayaḥ kiṃsvidbhūmirvidīryate
|
kiṃsvidāpūryate vyoma jaladhārāghanairghanaiḥ||28||

29raṅgasyaivaṃ matirabhūtkṣaṇena vasudhādhipa
|
dvāraṃ cābhimukhāḥ sarve babhūvuḥ prekṣakāstadā||29||

30pañcabhirbhrātṛbhiḥ pārthairdroṇaḥ parivṛto babhau
|
pañcatāreṇa saṃyuktaḥ sāvitreṇaiva candramāḥ||30||

31aśvatthāmnā ca sahitaṃ bhrātṝṇāṃ śatamūrjitam
|
duryodhanamamitraghnamutthitaṃ paryavārayat||31||

32sa taistadā bhrātṛbhirudyatāyudhairgadāgrapāṇiḥ samavasthitairvṛtaḥ
|
babhau yathā dānavasaṃkṣaye purā punandaro devagaṇaiḥ samāvṛtaḥ||32||
