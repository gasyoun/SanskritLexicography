Mahābhārata 12.35 (Śāntiparvan)

1vyāsa uvāca |
tapasā karmaṇā caiva pradānena ca bhārata
|
punāti pāpaṃ puruṣaḥ punaścenna pravartate ||1||

2ekakālaṃ tu bhuñjīta caranbhaikṣyaṃ svakarmakṛt
|
kapālapāṇiḥ khaṭvāṅgī brahmacārī sadotthitaḥ ||2||

3anasūyuradhaḥśāyī karma loke prakāśayan
|
pūrṇairdvādaśābhirvaṣairbrahmahā vipramucyate ||3||

4lakṣyaḥ śastrabhṛtāṃ vā syādviduṣāmicchayā''tmanaḥ
|
prāsyedātmānamagnau vā samiddhe triravākchirāḥ ||4||

5japanvā'nyatamaṃ vedaṃ yojanānāṃ śataṃ vrajet
|
sarvasvaṃ vā vedavide brāhmaṇāyopapādayet ||5||

6dhanaṃ vā jīvanāyālaṃ gṛhaṃ vā saparicchadam
|
mucyate brahmahatyāyā goptā gobrāhmaṇasya ca ||6||

7ṣaḍbhirvarṣeḥ kṛcchrabhojī brahmahā pūyate naraḥ
|
māse māse samaśnaṃstu tribhirvarṣeḥ pramucyate ||7||

8saṃvatsareṇa māsāśī pūyate nātra saṃśayaḥ
|
tathaivopavasan rājan svalpenāpi prapūyate ||8||

9kratunā cāśvamedhena pūyate nātra saṃśayaḥ
|
ye cāpyavabhṛthasnātāḥ kecidevaṃvidhā narāḥ ||9||

10te sarve dhūtapāpmāno bhavantīti parā śrutiḥ
|
brāhmaṇārthe hato yuddhe mucyate brahmahatyayā ||10||

11gavāṃ śatasahasraṃ tu pātrebhyaḥ pratipādayet
|
brahmahā vipramucyeta sarvapāpebhya eva ca ||11||

12kapilānāṃ sahasrāṇi yo dadyātpañcaviṃśatim
|
dogdhrīṇāṃ sa ca pāpebhyaḥ sarvebhyo vipramucyate ||12||

13gosahasraṃ savatsānāṃ dogdhrīṇāṃ prāṇasaṃśaye
|
sādhubhyo vai daridrebhyo dattvā mucyeta kilbiṣāt ||13||

14śataṃ vai yastu kāmbojānbrāhmaṇebhyaḥ prayacchati
|
niyatebhyo mahīpāla sa ca pāpātpramucyate ||14||

15manorathaṃ tu yo dadyādekasmā api bhārata
|
na kīrtayeta dattvā yaḥ sa ca pāpātpramucyate ||15||

16surāpānaṃ sakṛtkṛtvā yo'gnivarṇāṃ surāṃ pibet
|
sa pāvayatyathātmānamiha loke paratra ca ||16||

17maruprapātaṃ prapatan jvalanaṃ vā samāviśan
|
mahāprasthānamātiṣṭhanmucyate sarvakilviṣaiḥ ||17||

18bṛhaspatisaveneṣṭvā surāpo brāhmaṇaḥ punaḥ
|
samitiṃ brāhmaṇo gacchediti vai brahmaṇaḥ śrutiḥ ||18||

19bhūmipradānaṃ kuryādyaḥ surāṃ pītvā vimatsaraḥ
|
punarna ca pibedrājansaṃskṛtaḥ sa ca śuddhyati ||19||

20gurutalpī śilāṃ taptāmāyasīmabhisaṃviśet
|
avakṛtyātmanaḥ śephaṃ pravrajedūrdhvadarśanaḥ ||20||

21śarīrasya vimokṣeṇa mucyate karmaṇo'śubhāt
|
karmabhyo vipramucyante yattāḥ saṃvatsaraṃ striyaḥ ||21||

22mahāvrataṃ caredyastu dadyātsarvasvameva tu
|
gurvarthe vā hato yuddhe sa mucyetkarmaṇo'śubhāt ||22||

23anṛtenāpavartī cetpratiroddhā gurostathā
|
upāhṛtya priyaṃ tasmai tasmātpāpātpramucyate ||23||

24avakīrṇinimittaṃ tu brahmahatyāvrataṃ caret
|
gocarmavāsāḥ ṣaṇmāsāṃstathā mucyeta kilbiṣāt ||24||

25paradārāpahārī tu parasyāpaharanvasu
|
saṃvatsaraṃ vratī bhūtvā tathā mucyeta kilbiṣāt ||25||

26dhanaṃ tu yasyāpaharettasmai dadyātsamaṃ vasu
|
vividhenābhyupāyena tadā mucyeta kilbiṣāt ||26||

27kṛcchrāddvādaśarātreṇa saṃyatātmā vrate sthitaḥ
|
parivettā bhavetpūtaḥ parivittistathaiva ca ||27||

28niveśyaṃ tu punastena sadā tārayatā pitṝn
|
na tu striyā bhaveddoṣo na tu sā tena lipyate ||28||

29bhojanaṃ hyantarāśuddhaṃ cāturmāsye vidhīyate
|
striyastena praśudhyanti iti dharmavido viduḥ ||29||

30striyastvāśaṅkitāḥ pāpā nopagamyā vijānatā
|
rajasā tā viśudhyante bhasmanā bhājanaṃ yathā ||30||

31pādajocchiṣṭakāṃsyaṃ yadgavā ghrātamathāpi vā
|
gaṇḍūṣocchiṣṭamapi vā viśudhyeddaśabhistu tat ||31||

32catuṣpātsakalo dharmo brāhmaṇasya vidhīyate
|
pādāvakṛṣṭo rājanye tathā dharmo vidhīyate ||32||

33tathā vaiśye ca śūdre ca pādaḥ pādo vidhīyate
|
vidyādevaṃvidhenaiṣāṃ gurulāghavaniścayam ||33||

34tiryagyonivadhaṃ kṛtvā drumāṃśchittvetarānbahūn
|
trirātraṃ vāyubhakṣaḥ syātkarma ca prathayannaraḥ ||34||

35agamyāgamane rājanprāyaścittaṃ vidhīyate
|
ārdravastreṇa ṣaṇmāsānvihāryaṃ bhasmaśāyinā ||35||

36eṣa eva tu sarveṣāmakāryāṇāṃ vidhirbhavet
|
brāhmaṇoktena vidhinā dṛṣṭāntāgamahetubhiḥ ||36||

37sāvitrīmapyadhīyīta śucau deśe mitāśanaḥ
|
ahiṃso mandako'jalpo mucyate sarvakilbiṣaiḥ ||37||

38ahaḥsu satataṃ tiṣṭhedabhyākāśaṃ niśāṃ svapan
|
trirahni trirniśāyāṃ ca savāsā jalamāviśet ||38||

39strīśūdraṃ patitaṃ cāpi nābhibhāṣedvratānvitaḥ
|
pāpānyajñānataḥ kṛtvā mucyedevaṃvrato dvijaḥ ||39||

40śubhāśubhaphalaṃ pretya labhate bhūtasākṣikam
|
atiricyeta yo yatra tatkartā labhate phalam ||40||

41tasmāddānena tapasā karmaṇā ca phalaṃ śubham
|
vardhayedaśubhaṃ kṛtvā yathā syādatirekavān ||41||

42kuryācchubhāni karmāṇi nivartetpāpakarmaṇaḥ
|
dadyānnityaṃ ca vittāni tathā mucyeta kilbiṣāt ||42||

43anurūpaṃ hi pāpasya prāyaścittamudāhṛtam
|
mahāpātakavarjaṃ tu prāyaścittaṃ vidhīyate ||43||

44bhakṣyābhakṣyeṣu cānyeṣu vācyāvācye tathaiva ca
|
ajñānajñānayo rājan vihitānyanujānataḥ ||44||

45jānatā tu kṛtaṃ pāpaṃ guru sarvaṃ bhavatyuta
|
ajñānātsvalpako doṣaḥ prāyaścittaṃ vidhīyate ||45||

46śakyate vidhinā pāpaṃ yathoktena vyapohitum
|
āstike śraddadhāne ca vidhireṣa vidhīyate ||46||

47nāstikāśraddadhāneṣu puruṣeṣu kadācana
|
dambhadveṣapradhāneṣu vidhireṣa na dṛśyate ||47||

48śiṣṭācāraśca śiṣṭaśca dharmo dharmabhṛtāṃ vara
|
sevitavyo naravyāghra pretyeha ca sukhepsunā ||48||

49sa rājanmokṣyase pāpāttena pūrṇena hetunā
|
prāṇārthaṃ vā dhanenaiṣāmathavā nṛpakarmaṇā ||49||

50athavā te ghṛṇā kācitprāyaścittaṃ cariṣyasi
|
mā tvevānāryajuṣṭena manyunā nidhanaṃ gamaḥ ||50||

51vaiśampāyana uvāca|
evamukto bhagavatā dharmarājo yudhiṣṭhiraḥ
|
cintayitvā muhūrtena pratyuvāca tapodhanam ||51||
