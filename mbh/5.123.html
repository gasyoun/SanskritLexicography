Mahābhārata 5.123 (Udyogaparvan)

1nārada uvāca|
sadbhirāropitaḥ svargaṃ pārthivairbhūridakṣiṇaiḥ
|
abhyanujñāya dauhitrān yayātirdivamāsthitaḥ ||1||

2abhivṛṣṭaśca varṣeṇa nānāpuṣpasugandhinā
|
pariṣvaktaśca puṇyena vāyunā puṇyagandhinā ||2||

3acalaṃ sthānamāsādya dauhitraphalanirjitam
|
karmabhiḥ svairupacito jajvāla parayā śriyā ||3||

4upagītopanṛttaśca gandharvāpsarasāṃ gaṇaiḥ
|
prītyā pratigṛhītaśca svarge dundubhiniḥsvanaiḥ ||4||

5abhiṣṭutaśca vividhairdevarājarṣicāraṇaiḥ
|
arcitaścottamārdheṇa daivatairabhinanditaḥ ||5||

6prāptaḥ svargaphalaṃ caiva tamuvāca pitāmahaḥ
|
nirvṛtaṃ śāntamanasaṃ vacobhistarpayanniva ||6||

7catuṣpādastvayā dharmaścito lokyena karmaṇā
|
akṣayastava loko'yaṃ kīrtiścaivākṣayā divi ||7||

8punastvayaiva rājarṣe sukṛtena vighātitam
|
āvṛtaṃ tamasā cetaḥ sarveṣāṃ svargavāsinām ||8||

9yena tvāṃ nābhijānanti tato'jñāto'si pātitaḥ
|
prītyaiva cāsi dauhitraistāritastvamihāgataḥ ||9||

10sthānaṃ ca pratipanno'si karmaṇā svena nirjitam
|
acalaṃ śāśvataṃ puṇyamuttamaṃ dhruvamavyayam ||10||

11yayātiruvāca| bhagavan saṃśayo me'sti kaścittaṃ chettumarhasi
|
na hyanyamahamarhāmi praṣṭuṃ lokapitāmaha ||11||

12bahuvarṣasahasrāntaṃ prajāpālanavardhitam
|
anekakratudānaughairarjitaṃ me mahatphalam ||12||

13kathaṃ tadalpakālena kṣīṇaṃ yenāsmi pātitaḥ
|
bhagavan vettha lokāṃśca śāśvatān mama nirmitān
|
kathaṃ nu mama tatsarvaṃ vipranaṣṭaṃ mahādyute ||13||

14pitāmaha uvāca|
bahuvarṣasahasrāntaṃ prajāpālanavardhitam
|
anekakratudānaughairyattvayopārjitaṃ phalam ||14||

15tadanenaiva doṣeṇa kṣīṇaṃ yenāsi pātitaḥ
|
abhimānena rājendra dhikkṛtaḥ svargavālimiḥ ||15||

16nāyaṃ mānena rājarṣe na balena na hiṃsayā
|
na śāṭhyena na māyābhirloko bhavati śāśvataḥ ||16||

17nāvamānyāstvayā rājannadhamotkṛṣṭamadhyamāḥ
|
na hi mānapradagdhānāṃ kaścidasti śamaḥ kvacit ||17||

18patanārohaṇāmidaṃ kathayiṣyanti ye narāḥ
|
viṣamāṇyapi te prāptāstariṣyanti na saṃśayaḥ ||18||

19nārada uvāca|
eṣa doṣo'bhimānena purā prāpto yayātinā
|
nirbadhnatātimātraṃ ca gālavena mahīpate ||19||

20śrotavyaṃ hitakāmānāṃ suhṛdāṃ hitamicchatām
|
na kartavyo hi nirbandho nirbandho hi kṣayodayaḥ ||20||

21tasmāttvamapi gāndhāre mānaṃ krodhaṃ ca varjaya
|
sandhatsva pāṇḍavairvīra saṃrambhaṃ tyaja pārthiva ||21||

22dadāti yatpārthiva yatkaroti yadvā tapastapyati yajjuhoti
|
na tasya nāśo'sti na cāpakarṣo nānyastadaśnāti sa eva kartā ||22||

23idaṃ mahākhyānamanuttamaṃ hitaṃ bahuśrutānāṃ gataroṣarāgiṇām
|
samīkṣya loke bahudhā pradhāritaṃ trivargadṛṣṭiḥ pṛthivīmupāśnute ||23||
