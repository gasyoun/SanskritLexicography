Mahābhārata 13.98 (Anuśāsanaparvan)

1yudhiṣṭhira uvāca|
ālokadānaṃ nāmaitatkīdṛśaṃ bharatarṣabha
|
kathametatsamutpannaṃ phalaṃ vā tadbravīhi me ||1||

2bhīṣma uvāca|
atrāpyudāharantīmamitihāsaṃ purātanam
|
manoḥ prajāpatervādaṃ suvarṇasya ca bhārata ||2||

3tapasvī kaścidabhavatsuvarṇo nāma bhārata
|
varṇato hemavarṇaḥ sa suvarṇa iti paprathe ||3||

4kulaśīlaguṇopetaḥ svādhyāye ca paraṃgataḥ
|
bahūnsuvaṃśaprabhavānsamatītaḥ svakairguṇaiḥ ||4||

5sa kadācinmanuṃ vipro dadarśāpasasarpa ca
|
kuśalapraśnamanyonyaṃ tau cobhau tatra cakratuḥ ||5||

6tatastau satyasaṅkalpau merau kāñcanaparvate
|
ramaṇīye śilāpṛṣṭhe sahitau saṃnyaṣīdatām ||6||

7tatra tau kathayantau stāṃ kathā nānāvidhāśrayāḥ
|
brahmarṣidevadaityānāṃ purāṇānāṃ mahātmanām ||7||

8suvarṇastvabravīdvākyaṃ manuṃ svāyaṃbhuvaṃ prati
|
hitārthaṃ sarvabhūtānāṃ praśnaṃ me vaktumarhasi ||8||

9sumanobhiryadijyante daivatāni prajeśvara
|
kimetatkathamutpannaṃ phalaṃ yogaṃ ca śaṃsa me ||9||

10manuruvāca|
atrāpyudāharantīmamitihāsaṃ purātanam
|
śukrasya ca baleścaiva saṃvādaṃ vai mahātmanoḥ ||10||

11balervairocanasyeha trailokyamanuśāsataḥ
|
samīpamājagāmāśu śukro bhṛgukulodvahaḥ ||11||

12tamarghyādibhirabhyarcya bhārgavaṃ so'surādhipaḥ
|
niṣasādāsane paścādvidhivadbhūridakṣiṇaḥ ||12||

13katheyamabhavattatra tvayā yā parikīrtitā
|
sumanodhūpadīpānāṃ saṃpradāne phalaṃ prati ||13||

14tataḥ papraccha daityendraḥ kavīndraṃ praśnamuttamam ||14||

15baliruvāca|
sumanodhūpadīpānāṃ kiṃ phalaṃ brahmavittama
|
pradānasya dvijaśreṣṭha tadbhavānvaktumarhati ||15||

16śukra uvāca|
tapaḥ pūrvaṃ samutpannaṃ dharmastasmādanantaram
|
etasminnantare caiva vīrudoṣadhya eva ca ||16||

17somasyātmā ca bahudhā saṃbhūtaḥ pṛthivītale
|
amṛtaṃ ca viṣaṃ caiva ye cānye tṛṇajātayaḥ ||17||

18amṛtaṃ manasaḥ prītiṃ sadyastṛptiṃ dadāti ca
|
mano glapayate tīvraṃ viṣaṃ gandhena sarvaśaḥ ||18||

19amṛtaṃ maṅgalaṃ viddhi mahadviṣamamaṅgalam
|
oṣadhyo hyamṛtaṃ sarvā viṣaṃ tejognisambhavam ||19||

20mano hlādayate yasmācchriyaṃ cāpi dadhāti ca
|
tasmātsumanasaḥ proktā naraiḥ sukṛtakarmabhiḥ ||20||

21devatābhyaḥ sumanaso yo dadāti naraḥ śuciḥ
|
tasya tuṣyanti vai devāstuṣṭāḥ puṣṭiṃ dadatyapi ||21||

22yaṃ yamuddiśya dīyerandevaṃ sumanasaḥ prabho
|
maṅgalārthaṃ sa tenāsya prīto bhavati daityapa ||22||

23jñeyāstūgrāśca saumyāśca tejasvinyaśca tāḥ pṛthak
|
oṣadhyo bahuvīryā hibahurūpāstathaiva ca ||23||

24yajñiyānāṃ ca vṛkṣāṇāmayajñīyānnibodha me
|
āsurāṇi ca mālyāni daivatebhyo hitāni ca ||24||

25rakṣasāmuragāṇāṃ ca yakṣāṇāṃ ca tathā priyāḥ
|
manuṣyāṇāṃ pitṝṇāṃ ca kāntāyāstvanupūrvaśaḥ ||25||

26vanyā grāmyāśceha tathā kṛṣṭoptāḥ parvatāśrayāḥ
|
akaṇṭakāḥ kaṇṭakino gandharūparasānvitāḥ ||26||

27dvividho hi smṛto gandha iṣṭo'niṣṭaśca puṣpajaḥ
|
iṣṭagandhāni devānāṃ puṣpāṇīti vibhāvaya ||27||

28akaṇṭakānāṃ vṛkṣāṇāṃ śvetaprāyāśca varṇataḥ
|
teṣāṃ puṣpāṇi devānāmiṣṭāni satataṃ prabho ||28||

29jalajāni ca mālyāni padmādīni ca yāni vai
|
gandharvanāgayakṣebhyastāni dadyādvicakṣaṇaḥ ||29||

30oṣadhyo raktapuṣpāśca kaṭukāḥ kaṇṭakānvitāḥ
|
śatrūṇāmabhicārārthamātharveṣu nidarśitāḥ ||30||

31tīkṣṇavīryāstu bhūtānāṃ durālambhāḥ sakaṃṭakāḥ
|
raktabhūyiṣṭhavarṇāśca kṛṣṇāścaivopahārayet ||31||

32manohṛdayanaṃdinyo viśeṣamadhurāśca yāḥ
|
cārurūpāḥ sumanaso mānuṣāṇāṃ smṛtā vibho ||32||

33na tu śmaśānasambhūtā devatāyatanodbhavāḥ
|
sannayetpuṣṭiyukteṣu vivāheṣu rahaḥsu ca ||33||

34girisānuruhāḥ saumyā devānāmupapādayet
|
prokṣitā'bhyukṣitāḥ saumyā yathāyogyaṃ yathāsmṛti ||34||

35gandhena devāstuṣyanti darśanādyakṣarākṣasāḥ
|
nāgāḥ samupabhogena tribhiretaistu mānuṣāḥ ||35||

36sadyaḥ prīṇāti devānvai te prītā bhāvayantyuta
|
saṅkalpasiddhā martyānāmīpsitaiśca manoramaiḥ ||36||

37prītāḥ prīṇanti satataṃ mānitā mānayanti ca
|
avajñātāvadhūtāśca nirdahantyadhamānnarān ||37||

38ata ūrdhvaṃ pravakṣyāmi dhūpadānavidheḥ phalam
|
dhūpāṃśca vividhānsādhūnasādhūṃśca nibodha me ||38||

39niryāsāḥ sāriṇaścaiva kṛtrimāścaiva te trayaḥ
|
iṣṭo'niṣṭo bhavedgandhastanme vistaraśaḥ śṛṇu ||39||

40niryāsāḥ sallakīvarjyā devānāṃ dayitā'stute
|
guggulupravarasteṣāṃ sarveṣāmiti niścayaḥ ||40||

41aguruḥ sāriṇāṃ śreṣṭho yakṣarākṣasabhoginām
|
daityānāṃ sallakīyaśca kāṃkṣito yaśca tadvidhaḥ ||41||

42atha sarjarasādīnāṃ gandhaiḥ pārthivadāravaiḥ
|
phāṇitāsavasaṃyuktairmanuṣyāṇāṃ vidhīyate ||42||

43devadānavabhūtānāṃ sadyastuṣṭikaraḥ smṛtaḥ
|
ye'nye vaihārikāstatra mānuṣāṇāmiti smṛtāḥ ||43||

44ya evoktāḥ sumanasāṃ pradāne guṇahetavaḥ
|
dhūpeṣvapi parijñeyāsta eva prītivardhanāḥ ||44||

45dīpadāne pravakṣyāmi phalayogamanuttamam
|
yathā yena yadā caiva pradeyā yādṛśāśca te ||45||

46jyotistejaḥ prakāśaṃ vā'pyūrdhvagaṃ cāpi varṇyate
|
pradānaṃ tejasāṃ tasmāttejo vardhayate nṛṇām ||46||

47andhantamastamisraṃ ca dakṣiṇāyanameva ca
|
uttarāyaṇametasmājjyotirdānaṃ praśasyate ||47||

48yasmādūrdhvagametattu tamasaścaiva bheṣajam
|
tasmādūrdhvagaterdātā bhavedatreti niścayaḥ ||48||

49devāstejasvino hyasmātprabhāvantaḥ prakāśakāḥ
|
tāmasā rākṣasāścaiva tasmāddīpaḥ pradīyate ||49||

50ālokadānāccakṣuṣmānprabhāyukto bhavennaraḥ
|
tāndatvā nopahiṃseta na harennopanāśayet ||50||

51dīpahartā bhavedandhastamogatirasuprabhaḥ
|
dīpapradaḥ svargaloke dīpamāleva rājate ||51||

52haviṣā prathamaḥ kalpo dvitīyaścauṣadhīrasaiḥ
|
vasāmedosthiniryāsairna kāryaḥ puṣṭimicchatā ||52||

53giriprapāte gahane caityasthāne catuṣpathe
|
dīpadānaṃ bhavennityaṃ ya icchedbhūtimātmanaḥ ||53||

54kulodyoto viśuddhātmā prakāśatvaṃ ca gacchati
|
jyotiṣāṃ caiva sālokyaṃ dīpadātā naraḥ sadā ||54||

55balikarmasu vakṣyāmi guṇānkarmaphalodayān
|
devayakṣoraganṛṇāṃ bhūtānāmatha rakṣasām ||55||

56yeṣāṃ nāgrabhujo viprā devatātithibālakāḥ
|
rākṣasāneva sānviddhi nirviśaṃkānamaṅgalān ||56||

57tasmādagraṃ prayaccheta devebhyaḥ pratipūjitam
|
śirasā prayataścāpi haredbalimatandritaḥ ||57||

58gṛhṇanti devatā nityamāśaṃsanti sadā gṛhān
|
bāhyāścāgantavo ye'nye yakṣarākṣasapannagāḥ ||58||

59ito dattena jīvanti devatāḥ pitarastathā
|
te prītāḥ prīṇayantyenamāyuṣā yaśasā dhanaiḥ ||59||

60balayaḥ saha puṣpaistu devānāmupahārayet
|
dadhidugdhamayāḥ puṇyāḥ sugandhāḥ priyadarśanāḥ ||60||

61kāryā rudhiramāṃsāḍhyā balayo yakṣarakṣasām
|
surāsavapuraskārā lājollāpikabhūṣitāḥ ||61||

62nāgānāṃ dayitā nityaṃ padmotpalavimiśritāḥ
|
tilān guḍasusaṃpannānbhūtānāmupahārayet ||62||

63agradātāgrabhogī syādbalavīryasamanvitaḥ
|
tasmādayaṃ prayaccheta devebhyaḥ pratipūjitam ||63||

64jvalantyaharaho veśma yāścāsya gṛhadevatāḥ
|
tāḥ pūjyā bhūtikāmena prasṛtāgrapradāyinā ||64||

65ityetadasurendrāya kāvyaḥ provāca bhārgavaḥ
|
suvarṇāya manuḥ prāha suvarṇo nāradāya ca ||65||

66nārado'pi mayi prāha guṇānetānmahādyute
|
tvamapyetadviditveha sarvamācara putraka ||66||
