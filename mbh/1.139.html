Mahābhārata 1.139 (Ādiparvan)

1vaiśaṃpāyana uvāca|
tataḥ saṃvatsarasyānte yauvarājyāya pārthiva
|
sthāpito dhṛtarāṣṭreṇa pāṇḍuputro yudhiṣṭhiraḥ||1||

2dhṛtisthairyasahiṣṇutvādānṛśaṃsyāttathārjavāt
|
bhṛtyānāmanukampārthaṃ tathaiva sthirasauhṛdāt||2||

3tato'dīrgheṇa kālena kuntīputro yudhiṣṭhiraḥ
|
piturantardadhe kīrtiṃ śīlavṛttasamādhibhiḥ||3||

4asiyuddhe gadāyuddhe rathayuddhe ca pāṇḍavaḥ
|
saṃkarṣaṇādaśikṣadvai śaśvacchikṣāṃ vṛkodaraḥ||4||

5samāptaśikṣo bhīmastu dyumatsenasamo bale
|
parākrameṇa saṃpanno bhrātṝṇāmacaradvaśe||5||

6pragāḍhadṛḍhamuṣṭitve lāghave vedhane tathā
|
kṣuranārācabhallānāṃ vipāṭhānāṃ ca tattvavit||6||

7ṛjuvakraviśālānāṃ prayoktā phālguno'bhavat
|
lāghave sauṣṭhave caiva nānyaḥ kaścana vidyate||7||

8bībhatsusadṛśo loke iti droṇo vyavasthitaḥ
|
tato'bravīdguḍākeśaṃ droṇaḥ kauravasaṃsadi||8||

9agastyasya dhanurvede śiṣyo mama guruḥ purā
|
agniveśya iti khyātastasya śiṣyo'smi bhārata||9||

10tīrthāttīrthaṃ gamayitumahametatsamudyataḥ
|
tapasā yanmayā prāptamamoghamaśaniprabham||10||

11astraṃ brahmaśiro nāma yaddahetpṛthivīmapi
|
dadatā guruṇā coktaṃ na manuṣyeṣvidaṃ tvayā||11||

12bhāradvāja vimoktavyamalpavīryeṣvapi prabho
|
tvayā prāptamidaṃ vīra divyaṃ nānyo'rhati tvidam12||12||

13samayastu tvayā rakṣyo munisṛṣṭo viśāṃpate
|
ācāryadakṣiṇāṃ dehi jñātigrāmasya paśyataḥ||13||

14dadānīti pratijñāte phālgunenābravīdguruḥ
|
yuddhe'haṃ pratiyoddhavyo yudhyamānastvayā'nagha||14||

15tatheti ca pratijñāya droṇāya kurupuṅgavaḥ
|
upasaṃgṛhya caraṇau sa prātyāduttarāṃ diśam||15||

16svabhāvādagamacchabdo mahīṃ sāgaramekhalām
|
arjunasya samo loke nāsti kaściddhanurdharaḥ||16||

17gadāyuddhe'siyuddhe ca rathayuddhe ca pāṇḍavaḥ
|
pāragaśca dhanuryuddhe babhūvātha dhanañjayaḥ||17||

18nītimānsakalāṃ nītiṃ vibudhādhipatestadā
|
avāpya sahadevo'pi bhrātṝṇāṃ vavṛte vaśe||18||

19droṇenaivaṃ vinītaśca bhrātṝṇāṃ nakulaḥ priyaḥ
|
citrayodhī samākhyāto babhūvātirathoditaḥ||19||

20trivarṣakṛtayajñastu gandharvāṇāmupaplave
|
arjunapramukhaiḥ pārthaiḥ sauvīraḥ samare hataḥ||20||

21na śaśāka vaśe kartuṃ yaṃ pāṇḍurapi vīryavān
|
so'rjunena vaśaṃ nīto rājāsīdyavanādhipaḥ||21||

22atīva balasaṃpannaḥ sadā māni kurūnprati
|
vipulo nāma sauvīraḥ śastaḥ pārthena dhīmatā||22||

23dattāmitra iti khyātaṃ saṅgrāme kṛtaniścayam
|
sumitraṃ nāma sauvīramarjuno'damayaccharaiḥ||23||

24bhīmasenasahāyaśca rathānāmayutaṃ ca saḥ
|
arjunaḥ samare prācyānsarvānekaratho'jayat||24||

25tathaivaikaratho gatvā dakṣiṇāmajayaddiśam
|
dhanaughaṃ prāpayāmāsa kururāṣṭraṃ dhanañjayaḥ||25||

26evaṃ sarve mahātmānaḥ pāṇḍavā manujottamāḥ
|
pararāṣṭrāṇi nirjitya svarāṣṭraṃ vavṛdhuḥ purā||26||

27tato balamatikhyātaṃ vijñāya dṛḍhadhanvinām
|
dūṣitaḥ sahasā bhāvo dhṛtarāṣṭrasya pāṇḍuṣu
|
sa cintāparamo rājā na nidrāmalabhanniśi||27||
