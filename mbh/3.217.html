Mahābhārata 3.217 (Āraṇyakaparvan)

1vaiśampāyana uvāca|
śrutvemāṃ dharmasaṃyuktāṃ dharmarājaḥ kathāṃ śubhām
|
punaḥ papraccha tamṛṣiṃ mārkaṇḍeyamidaṃ tadā||1||

2yudhiṣṭhira uvāca|
kathamāgnirvanaṃ yātaḥ kathaṃ cāpyaṅgirāḥ purā
|
naṣṭe'gnau havyamavahadagnirbhūtvā mahādyutiḥ||2||

3agniryadā tveka eva bahutvaṃ cāsya karmasu
|
dṛśyate bhagavan sarvametadicchāmi veditum||3||

4kumāraśca yathotpanno yathā cāgneḥ suto'bhavat
|
yathā rudrācca sambhūto gaṅgāyāṃ kṛttikāsu ca||4||

5etadicchāmyahaṃ tvattaḥ śrotuṃ bhārgavasattama
|
kautūhalasamāviṣṭo yāthātathyaṃ mahāmune||5||

6mārkaṇḍeya uvāca|
atrāpyudāharantīmamitihāsaṃ purātanam
|
yathā kruddho hutavahastapastaptuṃ vanaṃ gataḥ||6||

7yathā ca bhagavānagniḥ svayamevāṅgirā'bhavat
|
santāpayaṃśca prabhayā nāśayaṃstimirāṇi ca||7||

8purā'ṅgirā mahābāho cacāra tapa uttamam
|
āśramastho mahābhāgo havyavāhaṃ viśeṣayan
|
tathā sa bhūtvā tu tadā jagat sarvaṃ vyakāśayat||8||

9tapaścaraṃstu hutabhuk santaptastasya tejasā
|
bhṛśaṃ glānaśca tejasvī na ca kiñcit prajajñivān||9||

10atha sañcintayāmāsa bhagavān havyavāhanaḥ
|
anyo'gniriha lokānāṃ brahmaṇā samprakalpitaḥ||10||

11agnitvaṃ vipranaṣṭaṃ hi tapyamānasya me tapaḥ
|
kathamagniḥ punarahaṃ bhaveyamiti cintya saḥ||11||

12apaśyadagnivallokāṃstāpayantaṃ mahāmunim
|
sopāsarpacchanairbhītastamuvāca tadā'ṅgirāḥ||12||

13śīghrameva bhavasvāgnistvaṃ punarlokabhāvanaḥ
|
vijñātaścāsi lokeṣu triṣu saṃsthānacāriṣu||13||

14tvamagniḥ prathamaṃ sṛṣṭo brahmaṇā timirāpahaḥ
|
svasthānaṃ pratipadyasva śīghrameva tamonuda||14||

15agniruvāca|
naṣṭakīrtirahaṃ loke bhavān jāto hutāśanaḥ
|
bhavantameva jñāsyanti pāvakaṃ na tu māṃ janāḥ||15||

16nikṣipāmyahamagnitvaṃ tvamagniḥ prathamo bhava
|
bhaviṣyāmi dvitīyo'haṃ prājāpatyaka eva ca||16||

17aṅgirā uvāca|
kuru puṇyaṃ prajāsvargyaṃ bhavāgnistimirāpahaḥ
|
māṃ ca deva kuruṣvāgne prathamaṃ putramañjasā||17||

18mārkaṇḍeya uvāca|
tacchrutvā'ṅgiraso vākyaṃ jātavedāstadā'karot
|
rājan bṛhaspatirnāma tasyāpyaṅgirasaḥ sutaḥ||18||

19jñātvā prathamajaṃ taṃ tu vahnerāṅgirasaṃ sutam
|
upetya devāḥ papracchuḥ kāraṇaṃ tatra bhārata||19||

20sa tu pṛṣṭastadā devaistataḥ kāraṇamabravīt
|
pratyagṛhṇaṃstu devāśca tadvaco'ṅgirasastadā||20||

21tatra nānāvidhānagnīn pravakṣyāmi mahāprabhān
|
karmabhirbahubhiḥ khyātānnānārthān brāhmaṇeṣviha||21||
