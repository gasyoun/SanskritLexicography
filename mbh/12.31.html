Mahābhārata 12.31 (Śāntiparvan)

1vaiśampāyana uvāca|
tato rājā pāṇḍusuto nāradaṃ pratyabhāṣata
|
bhagavañchrotumicchāmi suvarṇaṣṭhīvisambhavam ||1||

2evamuktastu sa munirdharmarājena nāradaḥ
|
ācacakṣe yathāvṛttaṃ suvarṇaṣṭhīvinaṃ prati ||2||

3nārada uvāca|
evametanmahābāho yathā'yaṃ keśavo'bravīt
|
kāryasyāsya tu yaccheṣaṃ tatte vakṣyāmi pṛcchataḥ ||3||

4ahaṃ ca parvataścaiva svastrīyo me mahāmuniḥ
|
vastu kāmāvabhigatau sañjayaṃ jayatāṃ varam ||4||

5tatrāvāṃ pūjitau tena vidhidṛṣṭena karmaṇā
|
sarvakāmaiḥ suvihitau nivasāvo'sya veśmani ||5||

6vyatikrāntāsu varṣāsu samaye gamanasya ca
|
parvato māmuvācedaṃ kāle vacanamarthavat ||6||

7āvāmasya narendrasya gṛhe paramapūjitau
|
uṣitau samaye brahmaṃstadvicintaya sāmpratam ||7||

8tato'hamabruvaṃ rājanparvataṃ śubhadarśanam
|
sarvametattvayi vibho bhāgineyopapadyate ||8||

9vareṇa cchandyatāṃ rājā labhatāṃ yadyadicchati
|
āvayostapasā siddhiṃ prāpnotu yadi manyase ||9||

10tata āhūya rājānaṃ sañjayaṃ jayatāṃ varam
|
parvato'numato vākyamuvāca kurupuṅgava ||10||

11prītau svo nṛpa satkārairbhavadārjavasaṃbhṛtaiḥ
|
āvābhyāmabhyanujñāto varaṃ nṛvara cintaya ||11||

12devānāmavihiṃsāyāṃ na bhavenmānuṣakṣayam
|
tadgṛhāṇa mahārāja pūjārho nau mato bhavān ||12||

13sañjaya uvāca |
prītau bhavantau yadi me kṛtametāvatā mama
|
eṣa eva paro lābho nirvṛtto me mahāphalaḥ ||13||

14tamevaṃvādinaṃ bhūyaḥ parvataḥ pratyabhāṣata
|
vṛṇīṣva rājansaṃkalpaṃ yatte hṛdi ciraṃ sthitam ||14||

15sañjaya uvāca |
abhīpsāmi sutaṃ vīraṃ vīryavantaṃ dṛḍhavratam
|
āyuṣmantaṃ mahābhāgaṃ devarājasamadyutim ||15||

16parvata uvāca |
bhaviṣyatyeṣa te kāmo na tvāyuṣmānbhaviṣyati
|
devarājābhibhūtyarthaṃ saṃkalpo hyeṣa te hṛdi ||16||

17khyātaḥ suvarṇaṣṭhīvīti putrastava bhaviṣyati
|
rakṣyaśca devarājātsa devarājasamadyutiḥ ||17||

18tacchutvā sañjayo vākyaṃ parvatasya mahātmanaḥ
|
prasādayāmāsa tadā naitadevaṃ bhavediti ||18||

19āyuṣmānme bhavetputro bhavatastapasā mune
|
na ca taṃ parvata: kiñciduvācendravyapekṣayā ||19||

20tamahaṃ nṛpatiṃ dīnamabruvaṃ punareva ca
|
smartavyo'smi mahārāja darśayiṣyāmi te sutam ||20||

21ahaṃ te dayitaṃ putraṃ pretarājavaśaṃ gatam
|
punardāsyāmi tadrūpaṃ mā śucaḥ pṛthivīpate ||21||

22evamuktvā tu nṛpatiṃ prayātau svo yathepsitam
|
sañjayaśca yathākāmaṃ praviveśa svamandiram ||22||

23sañjayasyātha rājarṣeḥ kasmiṃścitkālaparyaye
|
jajñe putro mahāvīryastejasā prajvalanniva ||23||

24vavṛdhe sa yathākālaṃ sarasīva mahotpalam
|
babhūva kāñcanaṣṭhīvī yathārthaṃ nāma tasya tat ||24||

25tadadbhutatamaṃ loke paprathe kurusattama
|
bubudhe tacca devendro varadānaṃ maharṣitaḥ ||25||

26tataḥ svābhibhavādbhīto bṛhaspatimate sthitaḥ
|
kumārasyāntaraprekṣī babhūva balavṛtrahā ||26||

27codayāmāsa tadvajraṃ divyāstraṃ mūrtimatsthitam
|
vyāghro bhūtvā jahīmaṃ tvaṃ rājaputramiti prabho ||27||

28pravṛddhaḥ kila vīryeṇa māmeṣo'bhibhaviṣyati
|
sañjayasya suto vajra yathainaṃ parvato'bravīt ||28||

29evamuktastu śakreṇa vajraḥ parapurañjayaḥ
|
kumāramantaraprekṣī nityamevānvapadyata ||29||

30sañjayo'pi sutaṃ prāpya devarājasamadyutim
|
hṛṣṭaḥ sāntaḥpuro rājā vananityo babhūva ha ||30||

31tato bhāgīrathītīre kadācinnirjane vane
|
dhātrīdvitīyo bālaḥ sa krīḍārthaṃ paryadhāvata ||31||

32pañcavarṣakadeśīyo bālo nāgendravikramaḥ
|
sahasotpatitaṃ vyāghramāsasāda mahābalam ||32||

33sa bālastena niṣpiṣṭo vepamāno nṛpātmajaḥ
|
vyasuḥ papāta medinyāṃ tato dhātrī vicukruśe ||33||

34hatvā tu rājaputraṃ sa tatraivāntaradhīyata
|
śārdūlo devarājasya māyayā'ntarhitastadā ||34||

35dhātryāstu ninadaṃ śrutvā rudatyāḥ paramārtavat
|
abhyadhāvata taṃ deśaṃ svayameva mahīpatiḥ ||35||

36sa dadarśa śayānaṃ taṃ gatāsu pītaśoṇitam
|
kumāraṃ vigatānandaṃ niśākaramiva cyutam ||36||

37sa tamutsaṅgamāropya paripīḍitamānasaḥ
|
putraṃ rudhirasaṃsiktaṃ paryadevayadāturaḥ ||37||

38tatastā mātarastasya rudatyaḥ śokakarśitāḥ
|
abhyadhāvanta taṃ deśaṃ yatra rājā sa sañjayaḥ ||38||

39tataḥ sa rājā sasmāra māmeva gatamānasaḥ
|
tadāhaṃ cintanaṃ jñātvā gatavāṃstasya darśanam ||39||

40mayaitāni ca vākyāni śrāvitaḥ śokalālasaḥ
|
yāni te yaduvīreṇa kathitāni mahīpate ||40||

41sañjīvitaścāpi punarvāsavānumate tadā
|
bhavitavyaṃ tathā tacca na tacchakyamato'nyathā ||41||

42tata ūrdhvaṃ kumārastu svarṇaṣṭhīvī mahāyaśāḥ
|
cittaṃ prasādayāmāsa piturmātuśca vīryavān ||42||

43kārayāmāsa rājyaṃ ca pitari svargate nṛpa
|
varṣāṇāṃ śatamekaṃ ca sahasraṃ bhīmavikramaḥ ||43||

44tata īje mahāyajñairbahubhirbhūridakṣiṇaiḥ
|
tarpayāmāsa devāṃśca pitṝścaiva mahādyutiḥ ||44||

45utpādya ca bahūnputrānkulasantānakāriṇaḥ
|
kālena mahatā rājankāladharmamupeyivān ||45||

46sa tvaṃ rājendra sañjātaṃ śokamenaṃ nivartaya
|
yathā tvāṃ keśavaḥ prāha vyāsaśca sumahātapāḥ ||46||

47pitṛpaitāmahaṃ rājyamāsthāya dhuramudvaha
|
iṣṭvā puṇyairmahāyajñairiṣṭaṃ lokamavāpsyasi ||47||
