Mahābhārata 6.108 (Bhīṣmaparvan)

1dhṛtarāṣṭra uvāca|
kathaṃ śikhaṇḍī gāṅgeyamabhyavartata saṃyuge
|
pāṇḍavāṃśca kathaṃ bhīṣmastanmamācakṣva sañjaya ||1||

2sañjaya uvāca|tataste pāṇḍavāḥ sarve sūryasyodayanaṃ prati
|
tāḍyamānāsu bherīṣu mṛdaṅgeṣvānakeṣu ca ||2||

3dhmāyatsu dadhivarṇeṣu jalajeṣu samantataḥ
|
śikhaṇḍinaṃ puraskṛtya niryātāḥ pāṇḍavā yudhi ||3||

4kṛtvā vyūhaṃ mahārāja sarvaśatrunibarhaṇam
|
śikhaṇḍī sarvasainyānāmagra āsīdviśāṃ pate ||4||

5cakrarakṣau tatastasya bhīmasenadhanañjayau
|
pṛṣṭhato draupadeyāśca saubhadraścaiva vīryavān ||5||

6sātyakiścekitānaśca teṣāṃ goptā mahārathaḥ
|
dhṛṣṭadyumnastataḥ paścātpañcālairabhirakṣitaḥ ||6||

7tato yudhiṣṭhiro rājā yamābhyāṃ sahitaḥ prabhuḥ
|
prayayau siṃhanādena nādayanbharatarṣabha ||7||

8virāṭastu tataḥ paścātsvena sainyena saṃvṛtaḥ
|
drupadaśca mahābāho tataḥ paścādupādravat ||8||

9kekayā bhrātaraḥ pañca dhṛṣṭaketuśca vīryavān
|
jaghanaṃ pālayāmāsuḥ pāṇḍusainyasya bhārata ||9||

10evaṃ vyūhya mahāsainyaṃ pāṇḍavāstava vāhinīm
|
abhyadravanta saṅgrāme tyaktvā jīvitamātmanaḥ ||10||

11tathaiva kuravo rājan bhīṣmaṃ kṛtvā mahāratham
|
agrataḥ sarvasainyānāṃ prayayuḥ pāṇḍavān prati ||11||

12putraistava durādharṣo rakṣitaḥ sumahābalaiḥ
|
tato droṇo maheṣvāsaḥ putraścāsya mahābalaḥ ||12||

13bhagadattastataḥ paścādgajānīkena saṃvṛtaḥ
|
kṛpaśca kṛtavarmā ca bhagada‌ttamanuvratau ||13||

14kāmbojarājo balavāṃstataḥ paścātsudakṣiṇaḥ
|
māgadhaśca jayatsenaḥ saubalaśca bṛhadbalaḥ ||14||

15tathaivānye maheṣvāsāḥ suśarmapramukhā nṛpāḥ
|
jaghanaṃ pālayāmāsustava sainyasya bhārata ||15||

16divase divase prāpte bhīṣmaḥ śāntanavo yudhi
|
āsurānakarodvyūhānpaiśācānatha rākṣasān ||16||

17tataḥ pravavṛte yuddhaṃ tava teṣāṃ ca bhārata
|
anyonyaṃ nighnatāṃ rājan yamarāṣṭravivardhanam ||17||

18arjunapramukhāḥ pārthāḥ puraskṛtya śikhaṇḍinam
|
bhīṣmaṃ yuddhe'bhyavartanta kiranto vividhān śarān ||18||

19tatra bhārata bhīmena tāḍitāstāvakāḥ śaraiḥ
|
rudhiraudhapariklinnāḥ paralokaṃ yayustadā ||19||

20nakulaḥ sahadevaśca sātyakiśca mahārathaḥ
|
tava sainyaṃ samāsādya pīḍayāmāsurojasā ||20||

21te vadhyamānāḥ samare tāvakā bharatarṣabha
|
nāśaknuvan vārayituṃ pāṇḍavānāṃ mahadbalam ||21||

22tatastu tāvakaṃ sainyaṃ vadhyamānaṃ samantataḥ
|
susaṃprāptaṃ daśa diśaḥ kālyamānaṃ mahārathaiḥ ||22||

23trātāraṃ nādhyagacchanta tāvakā bharatarṣabha
|
vadhyamānāḥ śitairbāṇaiḥ pāṇḍavaiḥ saha sṛñjayaiḥ ||23||

24dhṛtarāṣṭra uvāca|pīḍyamānaṃ balaṃ dṛṣṭvā pārthairbhīṣmaḥ parākramī
|
yadakārṣīdraṇe kruddhastanmamācakṣva sañjaya ||24||

25kathaṃ vā pāṇḍavānyuddhe pratyudyātaḥ paraṃtapaḥ
|
vinighnan somakān vīrastadācakṣva mamānagha ||25||

26sañjaya uvāca|ācakṣe te mahārāja yadakārṣīt pitā tava
|
pīḍite tava putrasya sainye pāṇḍavasṛñjayaiḥ ||26||

27prahṛṣṭamanasaḥ śūrāḥ pāṇḍavāḥ pāṇḍupūrvaja
|
abhyavartanta nighnantastava putrasya vāhinīm ||27||

28taṃ vināśaṃ manuṣyendra naravāraṇavājinām
|
nāmṛṣyata tadā bhīṣmaḥ sainyaghātaṃ raṇe paraiḥ ||28||

29sa pāṇḍavānmaheṣvāsaḥ pañcālāṃścaiva sṛñjayān
|
nārācairvatsadantaiśca śitairañjalikaistathā ||29||

30abhyavarṣata durdharṣastyaktvā jīvitamātmanaḥ
|
sa pāṇḍavānāṃ pravarān pañca rājan mahārathān ||30||

31āttaśastro raṇe yatnādvārayāmāsa sāyakaiḥ
|
nānāśastrāstravarṣaistān vīryāmarṣapraveritaiḥ ||31||

32nijaghne samare kruddho hastyaśvaṃ cāmitaṃ bahu
|
rathino'pātayadrājan rathebhyaḥ puruṣarṣabha ||32||

33sādinaścāśvapṛṣṭhebhyaḥ pādātāṃśca samāgatān ||33||

34gajārohān gajebhyaśca pareṣāṃ jayakāriṇaḥ
|
tamekaṃ samare bhīṣmaṃ tvaramāṇaṃ mahāratham||34||

35pāṇḍavāḥ samavartanta vajrahastamivāsurāḥ
|
śakrāśanisamasparśānvimuñcanniśitāñcharān‌||35||

36dikṣvadṛśyata sarvāsu ghoraṃ sandhārayan vapuḥ
|
maṇḍalībhūtamevāsya nityaṃ dhanuradṛśyata ||36||

37saṅgrāme yudhyamānasya śakracāpopamaṃ mahat
|
taddṛṣṭvā samare karma putrāstava viśāṃpate||37||

38vismayaṃ paramaṃ gatvā pitāmahamapūjayan
|
pārthā vimanaso bhūtvā praikṣanta pitaraṃ tava ||38||

39yudhyamānaṃ raṇe śūraṃ viracittimivāmarāḥ
|
na cainaṃ vārayāmāsurvyāttānanamivāntakam ||39||

40daśame'hani saṃprāpte rathānīkaṃ śikhaṇḍinaḥ
|
adahanniśitairbāṇaiḥ kṛṣṇavartmeva kānanam ||40||

41taṃ śikhaṇḍī tribhirbāṇairabhyavidhyatstanāntare
|
āśīviṣamiva kruddhaṃ kālasṛṣṭamivāntakam ||41||

42sa tenātibhṛśaṃ viddhaḥ prekṣya bhīṣmaḥ śikhaṇḍinam
|
anicchanniva saṅkruddhaḥ prahasannida‌mabravīt ||42||

43kāmamabhyasa vā mā vā na tvāṃ yotsye kathañcana
|
yaiva hi tvaṃ kṛtā dhātrā saiva hi tvaṃ śikhaṇḍinī ||43||

44tasya tadvacanaṃ śrutvā śikhaṇḍī krodhamūrcchitaḥ
|
uvācainaṃ tathā bhīṣmaṃ sṛkkiṇī parisaṃlihan ||44||

45jānāmi tvāṃ mahābāho kṣatriyāṇāṃ kṣayaṃ kara
|
mayā śrutaṃ ca te yuddhaṃ jāmadagnyena vai saha ||45||

46divyaśca te prabhāvo'yaṃ mayā ca bahuśaḥ śrutaḥ
|
jānannapi prabhāvaṃ te yotsye'dyāhaṃ tvayā saha ||46||

47pāṇḍavānāṃ priyaṃ kurvannātmanaśca narottama
|
adya tvāṃ yodhayiṣyāmi raṇe puruṣasattama ||47||

48dhruvaṃ ca tvāṃ haniṣyāmi śape satyena te'grataḥ
|
etacchrutvā ca madvākyaṃ yatkṛtyaṃ tat samācara ||48||

49kāmamabhyasa vā mā vā na ma jīvan pramokṣyase
|
sudṛṣṭaḥ kriyatāṃ bhīṣma loko'yaṃ samitiñjaya ||49||

50sañjaya uvāca|evamuktvā tato bhīṣmaṃ pañcabhirnataparvabhiḥ
|
avidhyata raṇe bhīṣmaṃ praṇunnaṃ vākyasāyakaiḥ ||50||

51tasya tadvacanaṃ śrutvā savyasācī mahārathaḥ
|
kālo'yamiti sañcintya śikhaṇḍinamacodayat ||51||

52ahaṃ tvāmanuyāsyāmi parānvidrāvayan śaraiḥ
|
abhidrava susaṃrabdho bhīṣmaṃ bhīmaparākramam ||52||

53na hi te saṃyuge pīḍāṃ śaktaḥ kartuṃ mahābalaḥ
|
tasmādadya mahābāho yatnādbhīṣmamabhidrava ||53||

54ahatvā samare bhīṣmaṃ yadi yāsyīsa māriṣa
|
avahāsyo'sya lokasya bhaviṣyasi mayā saha ||54||

55nāvahāsyā yathā vīra bhavema paramāhava
|
tathā kuru raṇe yatnaṃ sādhayasva pitāmaham ||55||

56ahaṃ te rakṣaṇaṃ yuddhe kariṣyāmi mahābala
|
vārayan rathinaḥ sarvānsādhayasva pitāmaham ||56||

57droṇaṃ ca droṇaputraṃ ca kṛpaṃ cātha suyodhanam
|
citrasenaṃ vikarṇaṃ ca saindhavaṃ ca jayadratham ||57||

58vindānuvindāvāvantyau kāmbojaṃ ca sudakṣiṇam
|
bhagadattaṃ tathā śūraṃ māgadhaṃ ca mahābalam ||58||

59saumadattiṃ tathā śūramārṣyaśṛṅgiṃ ca rākṣasam
|
trigartarājaṃ ca raṇa saha sarvairmahārathaiḥ ||59||

60ahamāvārayiṣyāmi veleva makarālayam
|
kurūṃśca sahitānsarvān yudhyamānānmahābalān
|
nivārayiṣyāmi raṇe sādhayasva pitāmaham ||60||
