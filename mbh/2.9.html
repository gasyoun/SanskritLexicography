Mahābhārata 2.9 (Sabhāparvan)

1nārada uvāca|
yudhiṣṭhira sabhā divyā varuṇasyāmitaprabhā
|
pramāṇena yathā yāmyā śubhaprākāratoraṇā||1||

2antaḥ salilamāsthāya vihitā viśvakarmaṇā
|
divyai ratnamayairvṛkṣaiḥ phalapuṣpapradairyutāḥ||2||

3nīlapītaiḥ sitāḥ śyāmaiḥ sitairlohitakairapi
|
avatānaistathā gulmairmañjarījāladhāribhiḥ||3||

4tathā śakunayastasyāṃ vicitrā madhurasvarāḥ
|
anirdeśyā vapuṣmantaḥ śataśo'tha sahasraśaḥ||4||

5sā sabhā sukhasaṃsparśā na śītā na ca dharmadā
|
veśmāsanavatī ramyā sitā varuṇapālitā||5||

6yasyāmāste sa varuṇo vāruṇyā ca samanvitaḥ
|
divyaratnāmbaradharo divyābharaṇabhūṣitaḥ||6||

7sragviṇo divyagandhāśca divyagandhānulepanāḥ
|
ādityāstatra varuṇaṃ jaleśvaramupāsate||7||

8vāsukistakṣakaścaiva nāgaścairāvatastathā
|
kṛṣṇaśca lohitaścaiva padmaścitraśca vīryavān||8||

9kambalāśvatarau nāgau dhṛtarāṣṭrabalāhakau
|
maṇimānkuṇḍadhāraśca karkoṭakadhanañjayau||9||

10pāṇimānkuṇḍadhāraśca balavānpṛthivīpate
|
prahrādo muṣikādaśca tathaiva janamejayaḥ||10||

11patākino maṇḍalinaḥ phaṇavantaśca sarvaśaḥ
|
ete cānye ca bahavaḥ sarpāstasyāṃ yudhiṣṭhira
|
upāsate mahātmānaṃ varuṇaṃ vigataklamāḥ||11||

12balirvairocano rājā narakaḥ pṛthivīñjayaḥ
|
saṃhrādo vipracittiśca kālakhañjāśca dānavāḥ||12||

13suhanurdurmukhaḥ śaṅkhaḥ sumanāḥ sumatistataḥ
|
ghaṭodaro mahāpārśvaḥ krathanaḥ piṭharastathā||13||

14viśvarūpaḥ svarūpaśca virūpo'tha mahāśirāḥ
|
daśagrīvaśca vālī ca meghavāsā daśāvaraḥ||14||

15ṭiṭṭibho viṭabhūtaśca saṃhrādaścendratāpanaḥ
|
daityadānavasaṅghāśca sarve rucirakuṇḍalāḥ||15||

16sragviṇo maulinaścaiva tathā divyaparicchadāḥ
|
sarve labdhavarāḥ śūrāḥ sarve vigatamṛtyavaḥ||16||

17te tasyāṃ varuṇaṃ devaṃ dharmapāśadharaṃ sadā
|
upāsate mahātmānaṃ sarve sucaritavratāḥ||17||

18tathā samudrāścatvāro nadī bhāgīrathī ca sā
|
kālindī vidiśā veṇā narmadā vegavāhinī||18||

19vipāśā ca śatadruśca candrabhāgā sarasvatī
|
irāvatī vitastā ca sindhurdevanadī tathā||19||

20godāvarī kṛṣṇaveṇī kāverī ca saridvarā
|
kiṃpunā ca viśalyā ca tathā vaitaraṇī nadī||20||

21tṛtīyā jyeṣṭhilā caiva śoṇaścāpi mahānadaḥ
|
carmaṇvatī tathā caiva parṇāśā ca mahānadī||21||

22sarayūrvāravatyā'tha lāṅgalī ca saridvarā
|
karatoyā tathātreyī lauhityaśca mahānadaḥ||22||

23laṅghatī gomatī caiva sandhyā triḥsrotasī tathā
|
etāścanyāśca rājendra sutīrthā lokaviśrutāḥ||23||

24saritaḥ sarvataścānyāḥ tīrthāni ca sarāṃsi ca
|
kūpāśca saprasravaṇā dehavanto yudhiṣṭhira||24||

25palvalāni taḍāgāni dehavantyatha bhārata
|
diśastathā mahī caiva tathā sarve mahīdharāḥ||25||

26upāsate mahātmānaṃ sarve jalacarāstathā
|
gītavāditravantaśca gandharvāpsarasāṃ gaṇāḥ||26||

27stuvanto varuṇaṃ tasyāṃ sarva eva samāsate
|
mahīdharā ratnavanto rasā ye ca pratiṣṭhitāḥ||27||

28kathayantaḥ sumadhurāḥ kathāstatra samāsate
|
vāruṇaśca tathā mantrī sunābhaḥ paryupāsate||28||

29putrapautraiḥ parivṛto gonāmnā puṣkareṇa ca
|
sarve vigrahavantaste tamīśvaramupāsate||29||

30eṣā mayā saṃpatatā vāruṇī bharatarṣabha
|
dṛṣṭapūrvā sabhā ramyā kuberasya sabhāṃ śṛṇu||30||
