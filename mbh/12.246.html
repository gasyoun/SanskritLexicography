Mahābhārata 12.246 (Śāntiparvan)

1vyāsa uvāca |
prakṛtyāstu vikārā ye kṣetrajñastairadhiṣṭhitaḥ
|
na cainaṃ te prajānanti sa ta jānāti tānapi ||1||

2taiścaivaṃ kurute kāryaṃ manaḥṣaṣṭhairihendriyaiḥ
|
sudāntairiva saṃyantā dṛḍhaiḥ paramavājibhiḥ ||2||

3indriyebhyaḥ pare hyā arthebhyaḥ paramaṃ manaḥ
|
manasastu parā buddhirbuddherātmā mahānparaḥ ||3||

4mahataḥ paramavyaktamavyaktātparato'mṛtam
|
amṛtānna paraṃ kiṃcitsā kāṣṭhā sā parā gatiḥ ||4||

5evaṃ sarveṣu bhūteṣu gūḍho''tmā na prakāśate
|
dṛśyate tvagryayā buddhyā sūkṣmayā sūkṣmadarśibhiḥ ||5||

6antarātmani saṃlīya manaḥṣaṣṭhāni medhayā
|
indriyāṇīndriyārthāṃśca bahucintyamacintayan ||6||

7dhyānenoparamaṃ kṛtvā vidyāsampāditaṃ manaḥ
|
anīśvaraḥ praśāntātmā tatorcchatyamṛtaṃ padam ||7||

8indriyāṇāṃ tu sarveṣāṃ vaśyātmā calitasmṛtiḥ
|
ātmanaḥ sampradānena martyo mṛtyumupāśnute ||8||

9āhatya sarvasaṃkalpān sattve cittaṃ niveśayet
|
sattve cittaṃ samāveśya tataḥ kālaṃjaro bhavet ||9||

10cittaprasādena yatirjahātīha śubhāśubham
|
prasannātmā''tmani sthitvā sukhamatyantamaśnute ||10||

11lakṣaṇaṃ tu prasādasya yathā svapne sukhaṃ svapet
|
nivāte vā yathā dīpo dīpyamāno na kampate ||11||

12evaṃ pūrvāpare kāle yuñjannātmānamātmani
|
laghvāhāro viśuddhātmā paśyatyātmānamātmani ||12||

13rahasyaṃ sarvavedānāmanaitihyamanāgamam
|
ātmapratyayikaṃ śāstramidaṃ putrānuśāsanam ||13||

14dharmākhyāneṣu sarveṣu satyākhyāne ca yadvasu
|
daśedamṛksahasrāṇi nirmathyāmṛtamuddhṛtam ||14||

15navanītaṃ yathā dadhnaḥ kāṣṭhādagniryathaiva ca
|
tathaiva viduṣāṃ jñānaṃ putrahetoḥ samuddhṛtam ||15||

16snātakānāmidaṃ śāstraṃ vācyaṃ putrānuśāsanam
|
tadidaṃ nāpraśāntāya nādāntāyātapasvine ||16||

17nāvedaviduṣe vācyaṃ tathā nānugatāya ca
|
nāsūyakājānṛjave na cānirdiṣṭakāriṇe ||17||

18na tarkaśāstradagdhāya tathaiva piśunāya ca
|
ślāghine ślāghanīyāya praśāntāya tapasvine ||18||

19idaṃ priyāya putrāya śiṣyāyānugatāya ca
|
rahasyadharmaṃ vaktavyaṃ nānyasmai tu kathañcna ||19||

20yadyapyasya mahīṃ dadyādratnapūrṇāmimāṃ naraḥ
|
idameva tataḥ śreya iti manyeta tattvavit ||20||

21ato guhyatarārthaṃ tadadhyātmamatimānuṣam
|
yattanmaharṣibhirdṛṣṭaṃ vedānteṣu ca gīyate ||21||

22tatte'haṃ sampravakṣyāmi yanmāṃ tvaṃ paripṛcchasi ||22||

23yacca te manasi vartate paraṃ yatra cāsita tava saṃśayaḥ kvacit
|
śrūyatāmayamahaṃ tavāgrataḥ putra kiṃ hi kathayāmi te punaḥ ||23||
