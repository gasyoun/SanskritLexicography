Mahābhārata 3.75 (Āraṇyakaparvan)

1bṛhadaśva uvāca| damayantī tu tacchrutvā bhṛśaṃ śokaparāyaṇā
|
śaṅkamānā nalaṃ taṃ vai keśinīmidamabravīt||1||

2gaccha keśini bhūyastvaṃ parīkṣāṃ kuru bāhuke
|
abruvāṇā samīpasthā caritānyasya lakṣaya||2||

3yadā ca kiñcitkuryātsa kāraṇaṃ tatra bhāmini
|
tatra sañceṣṭamānasya lakṣayantī viceṣṭitam||3||

4na cāsya pratibandhena deyo'gnirapi keśini
|
yācate na jalaṃ deyaṃ sarvathā tvaramāṇayā||4||

5etatsarvaṃ samīkṣya tvaṃ caritaṃ me nivedaya
|
nimittaṃ yattvayā dṛṣṭaṃ bāhuke daivamānuṣam||5||

6yaccānyadapi paśyethāstaccākhyeyaṃ tvayā mama
|
damayantyaivamuktā sā jagāmātha ca keśinī||6||

7niśamyātha hayajñasya liṅgāni punarāgamat
|
sā tatsarvaṃ yathāvṛttaṃ damayantyai nyavedayat
|
nimittaṃ yattayā dṛṣṭaṃ bāhuke divyamānuṣam||7||

8keśinyuvāca
|
dṛḍhaṃ śucyupacārosau na mayā mānuṣaḥ kvacit
|
dṛṣṭapūrvaḥ śruto vāpi damayanti tathāvidhaḥ||8||

9hrasvamāsādya sañcāraṃ nāsau vinamate kvacit
|
taṃ tu dṛṣṭvā yathā saṅgamutsarpati yathāsukham||9||

10saṅkaṭe'pyasya sumahānvivaro jāyate'dhikaḥ
|
ṛtuparṇasya cārthāya bhojanīyamanekaśaḥ||10||

11preṣitaṃ tatra rājñā tu māṃsaṃ bahu ca pāśavam
|
tasya prakṣālanārthāya kumbhāstatropakalpitāḥ||11||

12te tenāvekṣitāḥ kumbhāḥ pūrṇā evābhavaṃstataḥ
|
tataḥ prakṣālanaṃ kṛtvā samadhiśritya bāhukaḥ||12||

13tṛṇamuṣṭiṃ samādāya savitustaṃ samādadhat
|
atha prajvalitastatra sahasā havyavāhanaḥ||13||

14tadadbhutatamaṃ dṛṣṭvā vismitāhamihāgatā
|
anyacca tasminsumahadāścaryaṃ lakṣitaṃ mayā||14||

15yadagnimapi saṃspṛśya naivāsau dahyate śubhe
|
chandena codakaṃ tasya vahatyāvarjitaṃ drutam||15||

16atīva cānyatsumahadāścaryaṃ dṛṣṭavatyaham
|
yatsa puṣpāṇyupādāya hastābhyāṃ mamṛde śanaiḥ||16||

17mṛdyamānāni pāṇibhyāṃ tena puṣpāṇi nānyathā
|
bhūya eva sugandhīni hṛṣitāni bhavanti hi
|
etānyadbhutaliṅgāni dṛṣṭvā'haṃ drutamāgatā||17||

18bṛhadaśva uvāca| damayantī tu tacchrutvā puṇyaślokasya ceṣṭitam
|
amanyata nalaṃ prāptaṃ karmaceṣṭābhisūcitam||18||

19sā śaṅkamānā bhartāraṃ bāhukaṃ punariṅgitaiḥ
|
keśinīṃ ślakṣṇayā vācā rudatī punarabravīt||19||

20punargaccha pramattasya bāhukasyopasaṃskṛtam
|
mahānasācchritaṃ māṃsamānayasveha bhāvini||20||

21sā gatvā bāhukasyāgre tanmāṃsamapakṛṣya ca
|
atyuṣṇameva tvaritā tatkṣaṇātpriyakāriṇī||21||

22damayantyai tataḥ prādātkeśinī kurunandana
|
sā citānalasiddhasya māṃsasya bahuśaḥ purā||22||

23prāśya matvā nalaṃ sūtaṃ prākrośadbhṛśaduḥkhitā
|
vaiklavyaṃ paramaṃ gatvā prakṣālya ca mukhaṃ tataḥ||23||

24mithunaṃ preṣayāmāsa keśinyā saha bhārata
|
indrasenāṃ saha bhrātrā samabhijñāya bāhukaḥ||24||

25abhidrutya tato rājā pariṣvajyāṅkamānayat
|
bāhukastu samāsādya sutau surasutopamau||25||

26bhṛśaṃ duḥkhaparītātmā susvaraṃ praruroda ha
|
naiṣadho darśayitvā tu vikāramasakṛttadā
|
utsṛjya sahasā putrau keśinīmidamabravīt||26||

27idaṃ ca sadṛśaṃ bhadre mithunaṃ mama putrayoḥ
|
ato dṛṣṭvaiva sahasā bāṣpamutsṛṣṭavānaham||27||

28bahuśaḥ sampatantīṃ tvāṃ janaḥ saṅketadoṣataḥ
|
vayaṃ ca deśātithayo gaccha bhadre yathāsukham||28||
