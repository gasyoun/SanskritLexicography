Mahābhārata 7.123 (Droṇaparvan)

1sañjaya uvāca|
tato duḥśāsano rājan śaineyaṃ samupādravat
|
kirañśatasahasrāṇi parjanya iva vṛṣṭimān ||1||

2sa viddhvā sātyakiṃ ṣaṣṭyā tathā ṣoḍaśabhiḥ śaraiḥ
|
nākampayatsthitaṃ yuddhe mainākamiva parvatam ||2||

3taṃ tu duḥśāsanaḥ śūraḥ sāyakairāvṛṇodbhṛśam
|
rathavrātena mahatā nānādeśodbhavena ca ||3||

4sarvato bharataśreṣṭha visṛjansāyakānbahūn
|
parjanya iva ghoṣeṇa nādayanvai diśo daśa ||4||

5tamāpatantamālokya sātyakiḥ kauravaṃ raṇe
|
abhidrutya mahābāhuśchādayāmāsa sāyakaiḥ ||5||

6te chādyamānā bāṇaighairduḥśāsanapurogamāḥ
|
prādravansamare bhītāstava sainyasya paśyataḥ||6||

7teṣu dravatsu rājendra putro duḥśāsanastava
|
tasthau vyapetabhī rājansātyakiṃ cārdayaccharaiḥ ||7||

8caturbhirvājinastasya sārathiṃ ca tribhiḥ śaraiḥ
|
sātyakiṃ ca śatenājau viddhvā nādaṃ mumoca saḥ ||8||

9tataḥ kruddho mahārāja mādhavastasya saṃyuge
|
rathaṃ sūtaṃ dhvajaṃ taṃ ca cakre'dṛśyamajihmagaiḥ ||9||

10sa tu duḥśāsanaṃ śūraṃ sāyakairāvṛṇodbhṛśam
|
saśaṅkaṃ samanuprāptamūrṇanābhirivorṇayā ||10||

11tvaransamāvṛṇodbāṇairduḥśāsanamamitrajit
|
dṛṣṭvā duḥśāsanaṃ rājā tathā śaraśatācitam ||11||

12trigartāṃścodayāmāsa yuyudhānarathaṃ prati
|
te'gacchan yuyudhānasya samīpaṃ krūrakarmaṇaḥ ||12||

13trigartānāṃ trisāhasrā rathā yuddhaviśāradāḥ
|
te tu taṃ rathavaṃśena mahatā paryavārayan ||13||

14sthirāṃ kṛtvā matiṃ yuddhe bhūtvā saṃśaptakā mithaḥ
|
teṣāṃ prapatatāṃ yuddhe śaravarṣāṇi muñcatām ||14||

15yodhānpañcaśatānmukhyānagryānīke vyapothayat
|
te'patannihatāstūrṇaṃ śinipravarasāyakaiḥ ||15||

16mahāmārutavegena bhagnā iva nagāddrumāḥ
|
nāgaiśca bahudhā cchinairdhvajaiścaiva viśāṃpate ||16||

17hayaiśca kanakāpīḍaiḥ patitaistatra medinī
|
śaineyaśarasaṃkṛttaiḥ śoṇitaughapariplutaiḥ ||17||

18aśobhata mahārāja kiṃśukairiva puṣpitaiḥ
|
te vadhyamānāḥ samare yuyudhānena tāvakāḥ||18||

19trātāraṃ nādhyagacchanta paṅkamagnā iva dvipāḥ
|
tataste paryavartanta sarve droṇarathaṃ prati ||19||

20bhayātpatagarājasya gartānīva mahoragāḥ
|
hatvā pañcaśatānyodhāñcharairāśīviṣopamaiḥ ||20||

21prāyātsa śanakairvīro dhanañjayarathaṃ prati
|
taṃ prayāntaṃ naraśreṣṭhaṃ putro duḥśāsanastava ||21||

22vivyādha navabhistūrṇaṃ śaraiḥ sannataparvabhiḥ
|
sa tu taṃ prativivyādha pañcabhirniśitaiḥ śaraiḥ ||22||

23rukmapuṅkhairmaheṣvāso gārdhrapatrairajihmagaiḥ
|
sātyakiṃ tu mahārāja prahasanniva bhārata ||23||

24duḥśāsanakhibhirviddhvā punarvivyādha pañcabhiḥ
|
śaineyastava putraṃ tu hatvā pañcabhirāśugaiḥ ||24||

25dhanuścāsya raṇe chittvā vismayannarjunaṃ yayau
|
tato duḥśāsanaḥ kruddho vṛṣṇivīrāya gacchate ||25||

26sarvapāraśīṃ śaktiṃ visasarja jighāṃsayā
|
tāṃ tu śaktiṃ tadā ghorāṃ tava putrasya sātyakiḥ ||26||

27ciccheda śatadhā rājanniśitaiḥ kaṅkapatribhiḥ
|
athānyaddhanurādāya putrastava janeśvara ||27||

28sātyakiṃ ca śarairviddhyā siṃhanādaṃ nanarda ha
|
sātyakistu raṇe kruddho mohayitvā sutaṃ tava ||28||

29śarairagniśikhākārairājadhāna stanāntare
|
tribhireva mahābhāgaḥ śaraiḥ sannataparvabhiḥ ||29||

30sarvāyasaistīkṣṇavaktraiḥ punarvivyādha cāṣṭabhiḥ
|
duḥśāsanastu viṃśatyā sātyakiṃ pratyavidhyata ||30||

31sātvato'pi mahārāja taṃ vivyādha stanāntare
|
tribhireva mahābhāgaḥ śaraiḥ sannataparvabhiḥ ||31||

32tato'sya vāhānniśitaiḥ śarairjaghne mahārathaḥ
|
sārathiṃ ca susaṃkruddhaḥ śaraiḥ sannataparvabhiḥ ||32||

33dhanurekena bhallena hastāvāpaṃ ca pañcabhiḥ
|
dhvajaṃ ca rathaśaktiṃ ca bhalābhyāṃ paramāstravit ||33||

34ciccheda viśikhaistīkṣṇaistathobhau pārṣṇisārathī
|
sa cchinnadhanvā viratho hatāśvo hatasārathiḥ ||34||

35trigartasenāpatinā svarathenāpavāhitaḥ
|
tamabhidrutya śaineyo muhūrtamiva bhārata ||35||

36na jaghāna mahābāhurbhīmasenavacaḥ smaran
|
bhīmasenena tu vadhaḥ sutānāṃ tatha bhārata ||36||

37pratijñātaḥ sabhāmadhye sarveṣāmeva saṃyuge
|
tato duḥśāsanaṃ jitvā sātyakiḥ saṃyuge prabho
|
jagāma tvarito rājan yena yāto dhanañjayaḥ||37||
