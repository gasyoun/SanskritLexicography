Mahābhārata 13.5 (Anuśāsanaparvan)

1yudhiṣṭhira uvāca|
ānṛśaṃsyasya dharmajña guṇān bhaktajanasya ca
|
śrotumicchāmi dharmajña tanme brūhi pitāmaha ||1||

2bhīṣma uvāca|
atrāpyudāharantīmamitihāsaṃ purātanam
|
vāsavasya ca saṃvādaṃ śukasya ca mahātmanaḥ ||2||

3viṣaye kāśirājasya grāmānniṣkramya lubdhakaḥ
|
saviṣaṃ kāṇḍamādāya mṛgayāmāsa vai mṛgam ||3||

4tatra cāmiṣalubdhena lubdhakena mahāvane
|
avidūre mṛgān dṛṣṭvā bāṇaḥ pratisamāhitaḥ ||4||

5tena durvāritāstreṇa nimittacapaleṣuṇā
|
mahānvanatarustatra viddho mṛgajighāṃsayā ||5||

6sa tīkṣṇaviṣadigdhena śareṇātibalātkṣataḥ
|
utsṛjya phalapatrāṇi pādapaḥ śoṣamāgataḥ ||6||

7tasminvṛkṣe tathābhūte koṭareṣu ciroṣitaḥ
|
na jahāti śuko vāsaṃ tasya bhaktyā vanaspateḥ ||7||

8niṣpracāro nirāhāro glānaḥ śithilavāgapi
|
kṛtajñaḥ saha vṛkṣeṇa dharmātmā so'pyaśuṣyata ||8||

9tamudāraṃ mahāsattvamatimānuṣaceṣṭitam
|
samaduḥkhasukhaṃ dṛṣṭvā vismitaḥ pākaśāsanaḥ ||9||

10tataścintāmupagataḥ śakraḥ kathamayaṃ dvijaḥ
|
tiryagyonāvasambhāvyamānṛśaṃsyamavasthitaḥ ||10||

11athavā nātra cintyaṃ hi abhavadvāsavasya tu
|
prāṇināmapi sarveṣāṃ sarvaṃ sarvatra dṛśyate ||11||

12tato brāhmaṇaveṣeṇa mānuṣaṃ rūpamāsthitaḥ
|
avatīrya mahīṃ śakrastaṃ pakṣiṇamuvāca ha ||12||

13śuka bho pakṣiṇāṃ śreṣṭha dākṣeyī suprajā tvayā
|
pṛcche tvāṃ śukamenaṃ tvaṃ kasmānna tyajasi drumam ||13||

14atha pṛṣṭaḥ śukaḥ prāha mūrdhnā samabhivādya tam
|
svāgataṃ devarāja tvaṃ vijñātastapasā mayā ||14||

15tato daśaśatākṣeṇa sādhu sādhviti bhāṣitam
|
aho vijñānamityevaṃ manasā pūjitastataḥ ||15||

16tamevaṃ śubhakarmāṇaṃ śukaṃ paramadhārmikam
|
vijānannapi tāṃ prītiṃ papraccha balasūdanaḥ ||16||

17niṣpatramaphalaṃ śuṣkamaśaraṇyaṃ patatriṇām
|
kimarthaṃ sevase vṛkṣaṃ yadā mahadidaṃ vanam ||17||

18anye'pi bahavo vṛkṣāḥ patrasañcchannakoṭarāḥ
|
śubhāḥ paryāptasañcārā vidyante'sminmahāvane ||18||

19gatāyuṣamasāmarthyaṃ kṣīṇasāraṃ hataśriyam
|
vimṛśya prajñayā dhīra jahīmaṃ sthaviraṃ drumam ||19||

20bhīṣma uvāca|
tadupaśrutya dharmātmā śukaḥ śakreṇa bhāṣitam
|
sudīrghamatiniḥśvasya dīno vākyamuvāca ha ||20||

21anatikramaṇīyāni daivatāni śacīpate
|
yatrābhavattava praśnastannibodha surādhipa ||21||

22asminnahaṃ drume jātaḥ sādhubhiśca guṇairyutaḥ
|
bālabhāvena saṅguptaḥ śatrubhiśca na dharṣitaḥ ||22||

23kimanukrośya vaiphalyamutpādayasi me'nagha
|
ānuśaṃsyābhiyuktasya bhaktasyānanyagasya ca ||23||

24anukrośo hi sādhūnāṃ mahaddharmasya lakṣaṇam
|
anukrośaśca sādhūnā sadā prītiṃ prayacchati ||24||

25tvameva daivataiḥ sarvaiḥ pṛcchyase dharmasaṃśayāt
|
atastvaṃ devadevānāmādhipatye pratiṣṭhitaḥ ||25||

26nārhase māṃ sahasrākṣa drumaṃ tyājayituṃ cirāt
|
samarthamupajīvyemaṃ tyajeyaṃ kathamadya vai ||26||

27tasya vākyena saumyena harṣitaḥ pākaśāsanaḥ
|
śukaṃ provāca dharmātmā ānṛśaṃsyena toṣitaḥ ||27||

28varaṃ vṛṇīṣveti tadā sa ca vavre varaṃ śukaḥ
|
ānṛśaṃsyaparo nityaṃ tasya vṛkṣasya sambhavam ||28||

29viditvā ca dṛḍhāṃ bhaktiṃ tāṃ śuke śīlasampadam
|
prītaḥ kṣipramatho vṛkṣamamṛtenāvasiktavān ||29||

30tataḥ phalāni patrāṇi śākhāścāpi manoharāḥ
|
śukasya dṛḍhabhaktitvācchrīmatāṃ prāpa sa drumaḥ ||30||

31śukaśca karmaṇā tena ānṛśaṃsyakṛtena vai
|
āyuṣo'nte mahārāja prāpa śakrasalokatām ||31||

32evameva manuṣyendra bhaktimantaṃ samāśritaḥ
|
sarvārthasiddhiṃ labhate śukaṃ prāpya yathā drumaḥ ||32||
