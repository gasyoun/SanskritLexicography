Mahābhārata 3.290 (Āraṇyakaparvan)

1mārkaṇḍeya uvāca|
tataḥ kruddho daśagrīvaḥ priye putre nipātite
|
niryayau rathamāsthāya hemaratnavibhūṣitam||1||

2sa vṛto rākṣasairghorairvividhāyudhapāṇibhiḥ
|
abhidudrāva rāmaṃ sa yodhayan hariyūthapān||2||

3tamādravantaṃ saṅkruddhaṃ maindanīlanalāṅgadāḥ
|
hanūmān jāmbavāṃścaiva sasainyāḥ paryavārayan||3||

4te daśagrīvasainyaṃ tadṛkṣavānarapuṅgavāḥ
|
drumairvidhvaṃsayāṃcakrurdaśagrīvasya paśyataḥ||4||

5tataḥ sa sainyamālokya vadhyamānamarātibhiḥ
|
māyāvī cāsṛjanmāyāṃ rāvaṇo rākṣasādhipaḥ||5||

6tasya dehaviniṣkrāntāḥ śataśo'tha sahasraśaḥ
|
rākṣasāḥ pratyadṛśyanta śaraśaktyṛṣṭipāṇayaḥ||6||

7tān rāmo jaghnivānsarvāndivyenāstreṇa rākṣasān
|
atha bhūyo'pi māyāṃ sa vyadadhādrākṣasādhipaḥ||7||

8kṛtvā rāmasya rūpāṇi lakṣmaṇasya ca bhārata
|
abhidudrāva rāmaṃ ca lakṣmaṇaṃ ca daśānanaḥ||8||

9tataste rāmamarcchanto lakṣmaṇaṃ ca kṣapācarāḥ
|
abhipetustadā rāmaṃ pragṛhītaśarāsanāḥ||9||

10tāṃ dṛṣṭvā rākṣasendrasya māyāmikṣvākunandanaḥ
|
uvāca rāmaṃ saumitrirasaṃbhrānto bṛhadvacaḥ||10||

11jahīmān rākṣasān pāpānātmanaḥ pratirūpakān
|
jaghāna rāmastāṃścānyānātmanaḥ pratirūpakān||11||

12tato haryaśvayuktena rathenādityavarcasā
|
upatasthe raṇe rāmaṃ mātaliḥ śakrasārathiḥ||12||

13mātaliruvāca|
ayaṃ haryaśvayuk jaitro maghonaḥ syandanottamaḥ
|
anena śakraḥ kākutstha samare daityadānavān||13||

14śataśaḥ puruṣavyāghra rathodāreṇa jaghnivān
|
tadanena naravyāghra mayā yattena saṃyuge||14||

15syandanena jahi kṣipraṃ rāvaṇaṃ mā ciraṃ kṛthāḥ
|
ityukto rāghavastathyaṃ vaco'śaṅkata mātaleḥ||15||

16māyaiṣā rākṣasasyeti tamuvāca vibhīṣaṇaḥ
|
neyaṃ māyā naravyāghra rāvaṇasya durātmanaḥ||16||

17tadātiṣṭha rathaṃ śīghramimamaindraṃ mahādyute
|
tataḥ prahṛṣṭaḥ kākutsthastathetyuktvā vibhīṣaṇam||17||

18rathenābhipapātātha daśagrīvaṃ ruṣānvitaḥ
|
hāhākṛtāni bhūtāni rāvaṇe samabhidrute||18||

19siṃhanādāḥ sapaṭahā divi divyāstathā'nadan
|
daśakandhararājasūnvostathā yuddhamabhūnmahat||19||

20alabdhopamamanyatra tayoreva tathā'bhavat
|
sa rāmāya mahāghoraṃ visasarja niśācaraḥ||20||

21śūlamindrāśaniprakhyaṃ brahmadaṇḍamivodyatam
|
tacchūlaṃ satvaraṃ rāmaściccheda niśitaiḥ śaraiḥ||21||

22tadṛṣṭvā duṣkaraṃ karma rāvaṇaṃ bhayamāviśat
|
tataḥ kruddhaḥ sasarjāśu daśagrīvaḥ śitāñcharān||22||

23sahasrāyutaśo rāme śastrāṇi vividhāni ca
|
tato bhuśuṇḍīḥ śūlāni musalāni paraśvadhān||23||

24śaktīśca vividhākārāḥ śataghnīśca śitān kṣurān
|
tāṃ māyāṃ vikṛtāṃ dṛṣṭvā daśagrīvasya rakṣasaḥ||24||

25bhayāt pradudruvuḥ sarve vānarāḥ sarvato diśam
|
tataḥ supatraṃ sumukhaṃ hemapuṅkhaṃ śarottamam||25||

26tūṇādādāya kākutstho brahmāstreṇa yuyoja ha
|
taṃ bāṇavaryaṃ rāmeṇa brahmāstreṇānumantritam||26||

27jahṛṣurdevagandharvā dṛṣṭvā śakrapurogamāḥ
|
alpāvaśeṣamāyuśca tato'manyanta rakṣasaḥ||27||

28brahmāstrodīraṇācchatrordevadānavakinnarāḥ
|
tataḥ sasarja taṃ rāmaḥ śaramapratimaujasam||28||

29rāvaṇāntakaraṃ ghoraṃ brahmadaṇḍamivodyatam
|
muktamātreṇa rāmeṇa dūrākṛṣṭena bhārata||29||

30sa tena rākṣasaśreṣṭhaḥ sarathaḥ sāśvasārathiḥ
|
prajajvāla mahājvālenāgninā'bhipariplutaḥ||30||

31tataḥ prahṛṣṭāstridaśāḥ sahagandharvacāraṇāḥ
|
nihataṃ rāvaṇaṃ dṛṣṭvā rāmeṇākliṣṭakarmaṇā||31||

32tatyajustaṃ mahābhāgaṃ pañca bhūtāni rāvaṇam
|
bhraṃśitaḥ sarvalokeṣu sa hi brahmāstratejasā||32||

33śarīraghātavo hāsya māṃsaṃ rudhirameva ca
|
neśurbrahmāstranirdagdhā na ca bhasmāpyadṛśyata||33||
