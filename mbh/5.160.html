Mahābhārata 5.160 (Udyogaparvan)

1sañjaya uvāca|
hiraṇvatyāṃ niviṣṭeṣu pāṇḍaveṣu mahātmasu
|
nyaviśanta mahārāja kauraveyā yathāvidhi ||1||

2tatra duryodhano rājā niveśya balamojasā
|
saṃmānayitvā nṛpatīnnyasya gulmāṃstathaiva ca ||2||

3ārakṣaraya vidhiṃ kṛtvā yodhānāṃ tatra bhārata
|
karṇaṃ duḥśāsanaṃ caiva śakuniṃ cāpi saubalam ||3||

4ānāyya nṛpatistatra mantrayāmāsa bhārata
|
tatra duryodhano rājā karṇena saha bhārata ||4||

5saṃbhāṣitvā ca karṇena bhrātrā duḥśāsanena ca
|
saubalena ca rājendra mantrayitvā nararṣabha ||5||

6āhūyopahvare rājannulūkamidamabravīt
|
ulūka gaccha kaitavya pāṇḍavān sahasomakān ||6||

7gatvā mama vaco brūhi vāsudevasya śṛṇvataḥ
|
idaṃ tat samanuprāptaṃ varṣapūgābhicintitam ||7||

8pāṇḍavānāṃ kurūṇāṃ ca yuddhaṃ lokabhayaṅkaram
|
yadetat katthanāvākyaṃ sañjayo mahadabravīt ||8||

9vāsudevasahāyasya garjataḥ sānujasya te
|
madhye kurūṇāṃ kaunteya tasya kālo'yamāgataḥ ||9||

10yathā vaḥ saṃpratijñātaṃ tat sarvaṃ kriyatāmiti
|
jyeṣṭhaṃ tathaiva kaunteyaṃ brūyāstvaṃ vacanānmama ||10||

11bhrātṛ‌bhiḥ sahitaḥ sarvaiḥ somakaiśca sakekayaiḥ
|
kathaṃ vā dhārmiko bhūtvā tvamadharme manaḥ kṛthāḥ ||11||

12ya icchasi jagat sarvaṃ naśyamānaṃ nṛśaṃsavat
|
abhayaṃ sarvabhūtebhyo dātā tvamiti me matiḥ ||12||

13śrūyate hi purā gītaḥ śloko'yaṃ bharatarṣabha
|
prahrādenātha bhadraṃ te hṛte rājye tu daivataiḥ ||13||

14yasya dharmadhvajo nityaṃ surādhvaja ivocchritaḥ
|
pracchannāni ca pāpāni beḍālaṃ nāma tadvratam ||14||

15atra te vartayiṣyāmi ākhyānamidamuttamam
|
kathitaṃ nāradeneha piturmama narādhipa ||15||

16mārjāraḥ kila duṣṭātmā niśceṣṭaḥ sarvakarmasu
|
ūrdhvabāhuḥ sthito rājan gaṅgātīre kadācana ||16||

17sa vai kṛtvā manaḥśuddhiṃ pratyayārthaṃ śarīriṇām
|
karomi dharmamityāha sarvāneva śarīriṇaḥ ||17||

18tasya kālena mahatā viśraṃbhaṃ jagmuraṇḍajāḥ
|
sametya ca praśaṃsanti mārjāraṃ taṃ viśāmpate ||18||

19pūjyamānastu taiḥ sarvaiḥ pakṣibhiḥ pakṣibhojanaḥ
|
ātmakāryaṃ kṛtaṃ mene caryāyāśca kṛtaṃ phalam ||19||

20atha dīrghasya kālasya taṃ deśaṃ mūṣikā yayuḥ
|
dadṛśustaṃ ca te tatra dhārmikaṃ vratacāriṇam ||20||

21kāryeṇa mahatā yuktaṃ dambhayuktena bhārata
|
teṣāṃ matiriyaṃ rājannāsīttatra viniścaye ||21||

22bahumitrā vayaṃ sarve teṣāṃ no mātulo hyayam
|
rakṣāṃ karotu satataṃ vṛddhabālasya sarvaśaḥ ||22||

23upagamya tu te sarve biḍālamidamabruvan
|
bhavatprasādādicchāmaścartuṃ caiva yathāsukham ||23||

24bhavānno gatiravyagrā bhavānnaḥ paramaḥ suhṛt
|
te vayaṃ sahitāḥ sarve bhavantaṃ śaraṇaṃ gatāḥ ||24||

25bhavān dharmaparo nityaṃ bhavān dharme vyavasthitaḥ
|
sa no rakṣa mahāprajña tridaśāniva vajrabhṛt ||25||

26evamuktastu taiḥ sarvairmūṣikaiḥ sa viśāmpate
|
pratyuvāca tataḥ sarvān mūṣikān mūṣikāntakṛt ||26||

27dvayoryogaṃ na paśyāmi tapaso rakṣaṇasya ca
|
avaśyaṃ tu mayā kāryaṃ vacanaṃ bhavatāṃ hitam ||27||

28yuṣmābhirapi kartavyaṃ vacanaṃ mama nityaśaḥ
|
tapasā'smi pariśrānto dṛḍhaṃ niyamamāsthitaḥ ||28||

29na cāpi gamane śaktiṃ kāñcitpaśyāmi cintayan
|
so'smi neyaḥ sadā tātā nadīkūlamitaḥ param ||29||

30tatheti taṃ pratijñāya mūṣikā bharatarṣabha
|
vṛddhabālamatho sarvaṃ mārjārāya nyavedayan ||30||

31tataḥ sa pāpo duṣṭātmā mūṣikānatha bhakṣayan
|
pīvaraśca suvarṇaśca dṛḍhabandhaśca jāyate ||31||

32mūṣikāṇāṃ gaṇaścātra bhṛśaṃ saṃkṣīyate'tha saḥ
|
mārjāro vardhate cāpi tejobalasamanvitaḥ ||32||

33tataste mūṣikāḥ sarve sametyānyo'nyamabruvan
|
mātulo vardhate nityaṃ vayaṃ kṣīyāmahe bhṛśam ||33||

34tataḥ prājñatamaḥ kaściḍḍiṇḍiko nāma mūṣikaḥ
|
abravīdvacanaṃ rājan mūṣikāṇāṃ mahāgaṇam ||34||

35gacchatāṃ vo nadītīraṃ sahitānāṃ viśeṣataḥ
|
pṛṣṭhato'haṃ gamiṣyāmi sahaiva mātulena tu ||35||

36sādhu sādhviti te sarve pūjayāñcakrire tadā
|
cakruścaiva yathānyāyaṃ ḍiṇḍikasya vaco'rthavat ||36||

37avijñānāttataḥ so'tha ḍiṇḍikaṃ hyupabhuktavān
|
tataste sahitāḥ sarve mantrayāmāsurañjasā ||37||

38tatra vṛddhatamaḥ kaścit koliko nāma mūṣikaḥ
|
abravīdvacanaṃ rājan jñātimadhye yathātatham ||38||

39na mātulo dharmakāmaśchadmamātraṃ kṛtā śikhā
|
na mūlaphalabhakṣasya viṣṭhā bhavati lomaśā ||39||

40asya gātrāṇi vardhante gaṇaśca parihīyate
|
adya saptāṣṭadivasān ḍiṇḍiko'pi na dṛśyate ||40||

41etacchrutvā vacaḥ sarve mūṣikā vipradudruvuḥ
|
biḍālo'pi sa duṣṭātmā jagāmaiva yathāgatam ||41||

42tathā tvamapi duṣṭātman baiḍālaṃ vratamāsthitaḥ
|
carasi jñātiṣu sadā biḍālo mūṣikeṣviva ||42||

43anyathā kila te vākyamanyathā karma dṛśyate
|
dambhanārthāya lokasya vedāścopaśamaśca te ||43||

44tyaktvā chadma tvidaṃ rājan kṣatradharmaṃ samāśritaḥ
|
kuru kāryāṇi sarvāṇi dharmiṣṭho'si nararṣabha ||44||

45bāhuvīryeṇa pṛthivīṃ labdhvā bharatasattama
|
dehi dānaṃ dvijātibhyaḥ pitṛbhyaśca yathocitam ||45||

46kliṣṭāyā varṣapūgāṃśca māturmātṛhite sthitaḥ
|
pramārjāśru raṇe jitvā sammānaṃ paramāvaha ||46||

47pañca grāmā vṛtā yatnānnāsmābhirapavarjitāḥ
|
yudhyāmahe kathaṃ saṅkhye kopayema ca pāṇḍavān ||47||

48tvatkṛte duṣṭabhāvasya santyāgo vidurasya ca
|
jātuṣe ca gṛhe dāhaṃ smara taṃ puruṣo bhava ||48||

49yacca kṛṣṇamavocastvamāyāntaṃ kurusaṃsadi
|
ayamasmi sthito rājan śamāya samarāya ca ||49||

50tasyāyamāgataḥ kālaḥ samarasya narādhipa
|
etadarthaṃ mayā sarvaṃ kṛtametadyudhiṣṭhira ||50||

51kiṃ nu yuddhāt paraṃ lābhaṃ kṣatriyo bahu manyate
|
kiṃ ca tvaṃ kṣatriyakule jātaḥ saṃprathito bhuvi ||51||

52droṇādastrāṇi saṃprāpya kṛpācca bharatarṣabha
|
tulyayonau samabale vāsudevaṃ samāśritaḥ ||52||

53brūyāstvaṃ vāsudevaṃ ca pāṇḍavānāṃ samīpataḥ
|
ātmārthaṃ pāṇḍavārthaṃ ca yatto māṃ prati yodhaya ||53||

54sabhāmadhye ca yadrūpaṃ māyayā kṛtavānasi
|
tattathaiva punaḥ kṛtvā sārjuno māmabhidrava ||54||

55indrajālaṃ ca māyāṃ vai kuhakā vā'pi bhīṣaṇā
|
āttaśastrasya saṅgrāme vahanti pratigarjanāḥ ||55||

56vayamapyutsahema dyāṃ khaṃ ca gacchema māyayā
|
rasātalaṃ viśāmo'pi aindraṃ vā purameva tu ||56||

57darśayema ca rūpāṇi svaśarīre bahūnyapi
|
na tu paryāyataḥ siddhirbuddhimāpnoti mānuṣīm||57||

58manasaiva hi bhūtāni dhātaiva kurute vaśe
|
yadbravīṣi ca vārṣṇeya dhārtarāṣṭrānahaṃ raṇe ||58||

59ghātayitvā pradāsyāmi pārthebhyo rājyamuttamam
|
ācacakṣe ca me sarvaṃ sañjayastava bhāṣitam ||59||

60maddvitīyena pārthena vairaṃ vaḥ savyasācinā
|
sa satyasaṅgaro bhūtvā pāṇḍavārthe parākramī ||60||

61yuddhyasvādya raṇe yattaḥ paśyāmaḥ puruṣo bhava
|
yastu śatrumabhijñāya śuddhaṃ pauruṣamāsthitaḥ ||61||

62karoti dviṣatāṃ śokaṃ sa jīvati sujīvitam
|
akasmāccaiva te kṛṣṇa khyātaṃ loke mahadyaśaḥ ||62||

63adyedānīṃ vijānīmaḥ santi ṣaṇḍhāḥ saśrṛṅgakāḥ
|
madvidho nāpi nṛpatistvayi yuktaḥ kathañcana ||63||

64sannāhaṃ saṃyuge kartuṃ kaṃsabhṛtye viśeṣataḥ
|
taṃ ca tūbarakaṃ bālaṃ bahvāśinamavidyakam ||64||

65ulūka madvaco brūhi asakṛdbhīmasenakam
|
virāṭanagare pārtha yastvaṃ sūdo hyabhūḥ purā ||65||

66ballavo nāma vikhyātastanmamaiva hi pauruṣam
|
pratijñātaṃ sabhāmadhye na tanmithyā tvayā purā ||66||

67duḥśāsanasya rudhiraṃ pīyatāṃ yadi śakyate
|
yadbravīṣi ca kaunteya dhārtarāṣṭrānahaṃ raṇe ||67||

68nihaniṣyāmi tarasā tasya kālo'yamāgataḥ
|
tvaṃ hi bhojye puraskāryo bhakṣye peye ca bhārata ||68||

69kva yuddhaṃ kva ca bhoktavyaṃ yudhyasva puruṣo bhava
|
śayiṣyase hato bhūmau gadāmāliṅgya bhārata ||69||

70tadvṛthā ca sabhāmadhye valgitaṃ te vṛkodara
|
ulūka nakulaṃ brūhi vacanānmama bhārata ||70||

71yuddhyasvādya sthiro bhūtvā paśyāmastava pauruṣam
|
yudhiṣṭhirānurāgaṃ ca dveṣaṃ ca mayi bhārata
|
kṛṣṇāyāśca parikleśaṃ smaredānīṃ yathātatham ||71||

72brūyāstvaṃ sahadevaṃ ca rājamadhye vaco mama
|
yuddhyedānīṃ raṇe yattaḥ kleśān smara ca pāṇḍava ||72||

73virāṭadrupadau cobhau brūyāstvaṃ vacanānmama
|
na dṛṣṭapūrvā bhartāro bhṛtyairapi mahāguṇaiḥ ||73||

74tathā'rthapatibhirbhṛtyā yataḥ sṛṣṭāḥ prajāstataḥ
|
aślāghyo'yaṃ narapatiryuvayoriti cāgatam ||74||

75te yūyaṃ saṃhatā bhūtvā tadvadhārthaṃ mamāpi ca
|
ātmārthaṃ pāṇḍavārthaṃ ca prayuddhyadhvaṃ mayā saha ||75||

76dhṛṣṭadyumnaṃ ca pāñcālyaṃ brūyāstvaṃ vacanānmama
|
eṣa te samayaḥ prāpto labdhavyaśca tvayā'pi saḥ ||76||

77droṇamāsādya samare jñāsyase hitamuttamam
|
yudhyasva sasuhṛtpāpaṃ kuru karma suduṣkaram ||77||

78śikhaṇḍinamatho brūhi ulūkavacanānmama
|
strīti matvā mahābāhurna haniṣyati kauravaḥ ||78||

79gāṅgeyo dhanvināṃ śreṣṭho yuddhyedānīṃ sunirbhaya
|
kuru karma raṇe yattaḥ paśyāmaḥ pauruṣaṃ tava ||79||

80evamuktvā tato rājā prahasyolūkamabravīt
|
dhanañjayaṃ punarbrūhi vāsudevasya śṛṇvataḥ ||80||

81asmān vā tvaṃ parājitya praśādhi pṛthivīmimām
|
athavā nirjito'smābhī raṇe vīra śayiṣyasi ||81||

82rāṣṭrānnirvāsanakleśaṃ vanavāsaṃ ca pāṇḍava
|
kṛṣṇāyāśca parikleśaṃ saṃsmaran puruṣo bhava ||82||

83yadarthaṃ kṣatriyā sūte sarvaṃ tadidamāgatam
|
balaṃ vīryaṃ ca śauryaṃ ca paraṃ cāpyastralāghavam ||83||

84pauruṣaṃ darśayan yuddhe kopasya kuru niṣkṛtim
|
parikliṣṭasya dīnasya dīrghakāloṣitasya ca
|
hṛdayaṃ kasya na sphoṭedaiśvaryādbhraṃśitasya ca ||84||

85kule jātasya śūrasya paravitteṣvagṛdhyataḥ
|
āsthitaṃ rājyamākramya kopaṃ kasya na dīpayet ||85||

86yattaduktaṃ mahadvākyaṃ karmaṇā tadvibhāvyatām
|
akarmaṇā katthitena santaḥ kupuruṣaṃ viduḥ ||86||

87amitrāṇāṃ vaśe sthānaṃ rājyaṃ ca punaruddhara
|
dvāvarthau yuddhakāmasya tasmāttat kuru pauruṣam ||87||

88parājito'si dyūtena kṛṣṇā cānāyitā sabhām
|
śakyo'marṣo manuṣyeṇa kartuṃ puruṣamāninā ||88||

89dvādaśaiva tu varṣāṇi vane dhiṣṇyādvivāsitaḥ
|
saṃvatsaraṃ virāṭasya dāsyamāsthāya coṣitaḥ ||89||

90rāṣṭrānnirvāsanakleśaṃ vanavāsaṃ ca pāṇḍava
|
kṛṣṇāyāśca parikleśaṃ saṃsmaran puruṣo bhava ||90||

91apriyāṇāṃ ca vacanaṃ prabruvatsu punaḥ punaḥ
|
amarṣaṃ darśayasva tvamamarṣo hyeva pauruṣam ||91||

92krodho balaṃ tathā vīryaṃ jñānayogo'stralāghavam
|
iha te dṛśyatāṃ pārtha yuddhyasva puruṣo bhava ||92||

93lohābhisāro nirvṛttaḥ kurukṣetramakardamam
|
puṣṭāste'śvā bhṛtā yodhāḥ śvo yuddhyasva sakeśavaḥ ||93||

94asamāgamya bhīṣmeṇa saṃyuge kiṃ vikatthase
|
ārurukṣuryathā mandaḥ parvataṃ gandhamādanam ||94||

95evaṃ katthasi kaunteya akatthan puruṣo bhava
|
sūtaputraṃ sudurdharṣaṃ śalyaṃ ca balināṃ varam ||95||

96droṇaṃ ca balināṃ śreṣṭhaṃ śacīpatisamaṃ yudhi
|
ajitvā saṃyuge pārtha rājyaṃ kathamihecchasi ||96||

97brāhme dhanuṣi cācāryaṃ vedayorantagaṃ dvayoḥ
|
yudhi dhuryamavikṣobhyamanīkacaramacyutam ||97||

98droṇaṃ mahādyutiṃ pārtha jetumicchasi tanmṛṣā
|
na hi śuśruma vātena merumunmathitaṃ girim ||98||

99anilo vā vahenmeruṃ dyorvā'pi nipatenmahīm
|
yugaṃ vā parivarteta yadyevaṃ syādyathā''ttha mām ||99||

100ko hyasti jīvitākāṅkṣī prāpyemamarimardanam
|
pārtho vā itaro vā'pi ko'nyaḥ svasti gṛhānvrajet ||100||

101kathamābhyāmabhidhyātaḥ saṃspṛṣṭo dāruṇena vā
|
raṇe jīvan pramucyeta padā bhūmimupaspṛśan ||101||

102kiṃ darduraḥ kūpaśayo yathemāṃ na budhyase rājacamūṃ sametām
|
durādharṣāṃ devacamūprakāśāṃ guptāṃ narendaistridaśairiva dyām ||102||

103prācyeḥ pratīcyeratha dākṣiṇātyairudīcyakāmbojaśakaiḥ khaśaiśca
|
śālvaiḥ samatsyaiḥ kurumadhyadeśyairmlecchaiḥ pulindairdraviḍāndhrakāñcyaiḥ ||103||

104nānājanaughaṃ yudhi saṃpravṛddhaṃ gāṅgaṃ yathā vegamapāraṇīyam
|
māṃ ca sthitaṃ nāgabalasya madhye yuyutsase manda kimalpabuddhe ||104||

105akṣayyāviṣudhī caiva agnidattaṃ ca te ratham
|
jānīmo hi raṇe pārtha ketuṃ divyaṃ ca bhārata ||105||

106akatthamāno yuddhyasva katthase'rjuna kiṃ bahu
|
paryāyātsiddhiretasya naitatsiddhyati katthanāt ||106||

107yadīdaṃ katthanālloke sidhyet karma dhanañjaya
|
sarve bhaveyuḥ siddhārthāḥ katthane ko hi durgataḥ ||107||

108jānāmi te vāsudevaṃ sahāyaṃ jānāmi te gāṇḍivaṃ tālamātram
|
jānāmyahaṃ tvādṛśo nāsti yoddhā jānānaste rājyametaddharāmi ||108||

109na tu paryāyadharmeṇa siddhiṃ prāpnoti mānavaḥ
|
manasaivānukūlāni dhātaiva kurute vaśe ||109||

110trayodaśa samā bhuktaṃ rājyaṃ vilapatastava
|
bhūyaścaiva praśāsiṣye tvāṃ nihatya sabāndhavam ||110||

111kka tadā gāṇḍivaṃ te'bhūdyattvaṃ dāsa paṇairjitaḥ
|
kka tadā bhīmasenasya balamāsīcca phālguna ||111||

112sagadādbhīmasenādvā phālgunādvā sagāṇḍivāt
|
na vai mokṣastadā yo'bhūdvinā kṛṣṇāmaninditām ||112||

113sā vo dāsye samāpannān mocayāmāsa pārṣatī
|
amānuṣyaṃ samāpannān dāsakarmaṇyavasthitān ||113||

114avocaṃ yat ṣaṇḍhatilānahaṃ vastathyameva tat
|
dhṛtā hi veṇī pārthena virāṭanagare tadā ||114||

115sūdakarmaṇi viśrāntaṃ virāṭasya mahānase
|
bhīmasenena kaunteya yattu tanmama pauruṣam ||115||

116evameva sadā daṇḍaṃ kṣatriyāḥ kṣatriye dadhuḥ
|
veṇīṃ kṛtvā ṣaṇḍhaveṣaḥ kanyāṃ nartitavānasi ||116||

117na bhayādvāsudevasya na cāpi tava phālguna
|
rājyaṃ prati pradāsyāmi yuddhyasva sahakeśavaḥ ||117||

118na māyā'hīndrajālaṃ vā kuhakā vā'pi bhīṣaṇā
|
āttaśastrasya saṅgrāme vahanti pratigarjanāḥ ||118||

119vāsudevasahasraṃ vā phālgunānāṃ śatāni vā
|
āsādya māmamogheṣuṃ draviṣyanti diśo daśa ||119||

120saṃyugaṃ gaccha bhīṣmeṇa bhindhi vā śirasā girim
|
tarasva vā mahāgādhaṃ bāhubhyāṃ puruṣodadhim ||120||

121śāradvatamahāmīnaṃ viviṃśatimahoragam
|
bṛhadbalamahodvelaṃ saumadattitimiṅgilam ||121||

122bhīṣmavegamaparyantaṃ droṇagrāhadurāsadam
|
karṇa-śalyajhaṣāvartaṃ kāmbojavaḍavāmukham ||122||

123duḥśāsanaughaṃ śala-śalyamatsyaṃ suṣeṇa-citrāyudhanāga-nakram
|
jayadra‌thādriṃ purumitra-gādhaṃ durmarṣaṇodaṃ śakuniprapātam ||123||

124śastraughamakṣayyamabhipravṛddhaṃ yadā'vagāhya śramanaṣṭacetāḥ
|
bhaviṣyasi tvaṃ hatasarvabāndhavastadā manaste paritāpameṣyati ||124||
