Mahābhārata 3.131 (Āraṇyakaparvan)

1śyena uvāca|
dharmātmānaṃ tvāhurekaṃ sarve rājanmahīkṣitaḥ
|
sarvadharmaviruddhaṃ tvaṃ kasmātkarma cikīrṣasi||1||

2vihitaṃ bhakṣaṇaṃ rājanpīḍyamānasya me kṣudhā
|
ā rakṣīrdharmalobhena dharmamutsṛṣṭavānasi||2||

3rājovāca|
saṃtrastarūpastrāṇārthī tvatto bhīto mahādvija
|
matsakāśamanuprāptaḥ prāṇagṛdhurayaṃ dvijaḥ||3||

4evamabhyāgatasyeha kapotasyābhayārthinaḥ
|
apradāne paraṃ dharmaṃ kathaṃ śyena na paśyasi||4||

5praspandamānaḥ saṃbhrāntaḥ kapotaḥ śyena lakṣyate
|
matsakāśaṃ jīvitārthī tasya tyāgo vigarhitaḥ||5||

6yo hi kaściddvijānhanyādgāṃ vā lokasya mātaram
|
śaraṇāgataṃ ca tyajate tulyaṃ teṣāṃ hi pātakam||6||

7śyena uvāca|
āhārātsarvabhūtāni saṃbhavanti mahīpate
|
āhāreṇa vivardhante tena jīvanti jantavaḥ||7||

8śakyate dustyaje'pyarthe cirarātrāya jīvitum
|
na tu bhojanamutsṛjya śakyaṃ vartayituṃ ciram||8||

9bhakṣyādviyojitasyādya mama prāṇā viśāṃpate
|
visṛjya kāyameṣyanti panthānamakutobhayam ||9||

10pramṛte mayi dharmātmanputradārādi naṅkṣyati
|
rakṣamāṇaḥ kapotaṃ tvaṃ bahūnprāṇānna rakṣasi||10||

11dharmaṃ yo bādhate dharmo na sa dharmaḥ kuvartma tat
|
avirodhāttu yo dharmaḥ sa dharmaḥ satyavikrama||11||

12virodhiṣu mahīpāla niścitya gurulāghavam
|
na bādhā vidyate yatra taṃ dharmaṃ samupācaret||12||

13gurulāghavamādāya dharmādharmaviniścaye
|
yato bhūyāṃstato rājankuruṣva dharmaniścayam||13||

14rājovāca|
bahukalyāṇasaṃyuktaṃ bhāṣase vihagottama
|
suparṇaḥ pakṣirāṭ kiṃ tvaṃ dharmajñaścāsya saṃśayam||14||

15tathā hi dharmasaṃyuktaṃ bahu citraṃ ca bhāṣase
|
na te'styaviditaṃ kiṃciditi tvāṃ lakṣayāmyaham||15||

16śaraṇaiṣiparityāgaṃ kathaṃ sādhviti manyase
|
āhārārthaṃ samārambhastava cāyaṃ vihaṃgama||16||

17śakyaścāpyanyathā kartumāhāro'pyadhikastvayā
|
govṛṣo vā varāho vā mṛgo vā mahiṣo'pi vā
|
tvadarthamadya kriyatāṃ yaccānyadiha kāṅkṣasi||17||

18śyena uvāca|
na varāhaṃ na cokṣāṇaṃ na mṛgānvividhāṃstathā
|
bhakṣayāmi mahārāja kiṃ mamānyena kenacit||18||

19yastu me devavihito bhakṣaḥ kṣatriyapuṅgava
|
tamutsṛja mahīpāla kapotamimameva me||19||

20śyenaḥ kapotānattīti sthitireṣā sanātanī
|
mā rājansāramajñātvā kada‌līskandhamāsaja||20||

21rājovāca|
rāṣṭraṃ śibīnāmṛddhaṃ vai dadāni tava khecara
|
yaṃ vā kāmayase kāmaṃ śyena sarvaṃ dadāni te||21||

22vinemaṃ pakṣiṇaṃ śyena śaraṇārthinamāgatam
|
yenemaṃ varjayethāstvaṃ karmaṇā pakṣisattama
|
tadācakṣva kariṣyāmi na hi dāsye kapotakam||22||

23śyena uvāca|
uśīnara kapote te yadi sneho narādhipa
|
ātmano māṃsamutkṛtya kapotatulayā dhṛtam||23||

24yadā samaṃ kapotena tava māṃsaṃ nṛpottama
|
tadā deyaṃ tu tanmahyaṃ sā me tuṣṭirbhaviṣyati||24||

25rājovāca
|
anugrahamimaṃ manye śyena yanmābhiyācase
|
tasmātte'dya pradāsyāmi svamāṃsaṃ tulayā dhṛtam||25||

26lomaśa uvāca|
utkṛtya sa svayaṃ māṃsaṃ rājā paramadharmavit
|
tulayāmāsa kaunteya kapotena samaṃ vibho||26||

27dhriyamāṇaḥ kapotastu māṃsenātyatiricyate
|
punaścotkṛtya māṃsāni rājā prādāduśīnaraḥ||27||

28na vidyate yadā māṃsaṃ kapotena samaṃ dhṛtam
|
tata utkṛttamāṃso'sāvāruroha svayaṃ tulām||28||

29śyena uvāca|
indro'hamasmi dharmajña kapoto havyavāḍayam
|
jijñāsamānau dharmaṃ tvāṃ yajñavāṭamupāgatau||29||

30yatte māṃsāni gātrebhya utkṛttāni viśāṃpate
|
eṣā te bhāsvatī kīrtirlokānabhibhaviṣyati||30||

31yāvalloke manuṣyāstvāṃ kathayiṣyanti pārthiva
|
tāvatkīrtiśca lokāśca sthāsyanti tava śāśvatāḥ||31||

32ityevamuktvā rājānamāruroha divaṃ punaḥ
|
uśīnaro'pi dharmātmā dharmeṇāvṛtya rodasī||32||

33vibhrājamāno vapuṣāpyāruroha triviṣṭapam
|
tadetatsadanaṃ rājanrājñastasya mahātmanaḥ||33||

34paśyasvaitanmayā sārdhaṃ puṇyaṃ pāpapramocanam
|
tatra vai satataṃ devā munayaśca sanātanāḥ
|
dṛśyante brāhmaṇai rājanpuṇyavadbhirmahātmabhiḥ||34||
