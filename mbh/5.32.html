Mahābhārata 5.32 (Udyogaparvan)

1vaiśampāyana uvāca|
anujñātaḥ pāṇḍavena prayayau sañjayastadā
|
śāsanaṃ dhṛtarāṣṭrasya sarvaṃ kṛtvā mahātmanaḥ ||1||

2samprāpya hāstinapuraṃ śīghraṃmeva praviśya ca
|
antaḥpuraṃ samāsthāya dvāḥsthaṃ vacanamabravīt ||2||

3ācakṣva dhṛtarāṣṭrāya dvāḥstha māṃ samupāgatam
|
sakāśāt pāṇḍuputrāṇāṃ sañjayaṃ mā ciraṃ kṛthāḥ||3||

4jāgarti cedabhivadestvaṃ hi dvāḥstha praviśeyaṃ vidito bhūmipasya
|
nivedyamatrātyayikaṃ hi me'sti dvāḥstho'tha śrutvā nṛpatiṃ jagāda||4||

5dvāḥstha uvāca|
sañjayo'yaṃ bhūmipate namaste didṛkṣayā dvāramupāgataste
|
prāpto dūtaḥ pāṇḍavānāṃ sakāśāt praśādhi rājan kimayaṃ karotu ||5||

6dhṛtarāṣṭra uvāca|
ācakṣva māṃ kuśālinaṃ kalpamasmai praveśyatāṃ svāgataṃ sañjayāya
|
na cāhametasya bhavāmyakalpaḥ sa me kasmāddvāri tiṣṭhecca saktaḥ ||6||

7vaiśampāyana uvāca|
tataḥ praviśyānumate nṛpasya mahadveśma prājñaśūrāryaguptam
|
siṃhāsanasthaṃ pārthivamāsasāda vaicitravīryaṃ prāñjaliḥ sūtaputraḥ ||7||

8sañjaya uvāca|
sañjayo'haṃ bhūmipate namaste prāpto'smi gatvā naradeva pāṇḍavān
|
abhivādya tvāṃ pāṇḍuputro manasvī yudhiṣṭhiraḥ kuśalaṃ cānvapṛcchat ||8||

9sa te putrān pṛcchati prīyamāṇaḥ kaccitputraiḥ prīyase naptṛbhiśca
|
tathā suhṛdbhiḥ sacivaiśca rājan ye cāpi tvāmupajīvanti taiśca ||9||

10dhṛtarāṣṭra uvāca|
abhinandya tvāṃ tāta vadāmi sañjaya ajātaśatruṃ ca sukhena pārtham
|
kaccit sa rājā kuśalī saputraḥ sahāmātyaḥ sānujaḥ kauravāṇām ||10||

11sañjaya uvāca|
sahāmātyaḥ kuśalī pāṇḍuputro bubhūṣate yacca te'gre''mano'bhūt
|
nirṇiktadharmārthakaro manasvī bahuśruto dṛṣṭimān śīlavāṃśca ||11||

12paro dharmāt pāṇḍavasyānṛśaṃsyaṃ dharmaḥ paro vittacayānmato'sya
|
sukhapriye dharmahīne'napārthe'nurudhyate bhārata tasya buddhiḥ ||12||

13paraprayuktaḥ puruṣo viceṣṭate sūtraprotā dārumayīva yoṣā
|
imaṃ dṛṣṭvā niyamaṃ pāṇḍavasya manye paraṃ karma daivaṃ manuṣyāt ||13||

14imaṃ ca dṛṣṭvā tava karmadoṣaṃ pāpodarkaṃ ghoramavarṇarūpam
|
yāvatparaḥ kāmayate'tivelaṃ tāvannaro'yaṃ labhate praśaṃsām ||14||

15ajātaśatrustu vihāya pāpaṃ jīrṇāṃ tvacaṃ sarpa ivāsamarthām
|
virocate'hāryavṛttena vīro yudhiṣṭhirastvayi pāpaṃ visṛjya ||15||

16hantātmanaḥ karma nibodha rājan dharmārthayuktādāryavṛttādapetam
|
upakrośaṃ ceha gato'si rājan bhūyaśca pāpaṃ prasajedamutra ||16||

17sa tvamarthaṃ saṃśayitaṃ vinā tairāśaṃsase putravaśānugo'sya
|
adharmaśabdaśca mahān pṛthivyāṃ nedaṃ karma tvatsamaṃ bhāratāgrya ||17||

18hīnaprajño dauṣkuleyo nṛśaṃso dīrghaṃ vairī kṣatravidyāsvadhīraḥ
|
evaṃdharmānāpadaḥ saṃśrayeyurhīnavīryo yaśca bhavedaśiṣṭaḥ ||18||

19kule jāto balavān yo yaśasvī bahuśrutaḥ sukhajīvī yatātmā
|
dharmādharmau grathitau yo bibharti sa hyasya diṣṭasya vaśādupaiti ||19||

20kathaṃ hi mantrāgryadharo manīṣī dharmārthayorāpadi sampraṇetā
|
evamuktaḥ sarvamantrairahīno naro nṛśaṃsaṃ karma kuryādamūḍhaḥ ||20||

21tava hyamī mantravidaḥ sametya samāsate karmasu nityayuktāḥ
|
teṣāmayaṃ balavānniścayaśca kurukṣaye niyamenodapādi ||21||

22akālikaṃ kuravo nābhaviṣyan pāpena cetpāpamajātaśatruḥ
|
icchejjātu tvayi pāpaṃ visṛjya nindā ceyaṃ tava loke'bhaviṣyat ||22||

23kimanyatra viṣayādīśvarāṇāṃ yatra pārthaḥ paralokaṃ sma draṣṭum
|
atyakrāmat sa tathā saṃmataḥ syānna saṃśayo nāsti manuṣyakāraḥ ||23||

24etān guṇān karmakṛtānavekṣya bhāvābhāvau vartamānāvanityau
|
balirhi rājā pāramavindamāno nānyatkālāt kāraṇaṃ tatra mene ||24||

25cakṣuḥśrotre nāsikā tvak ca jihvā jñānasyaitānyāyatanāni jantoḥ
|
tāni prītānyeva tṛṣṇākṣayānte tānyavyatho duḥkhahīnaḥ praṇudyāt ||25||

26na tveva manye puruṣasya karma saṃvartate suprayuktaṃ yathāvat
|
mātuḥ pituḥ karmaṇā'bhiprasūtaḥ saṃvardhate vidhivadbhojanena ||26||

27priyāpriye sukhaduḥkhe ca rājannindāpraśaṃse ca bhajanta eva
|
parastvenaṃ garhayate'parādhe praśaṃsate sādhuvṛttaṃ tameva ||27||

28sa tvāṃ garhe bhāratānāṃ virodhādanto nūnaṃ bhavitā'yaṃ prajānām
|
no cedidaṃ tava karmāparādhāt kurūn dahet kṛṣṇavartmeva kakṣam ||28||

29tvamevaiko jātu putrasya rājan vaśaṃ gatvā sarvaloke narendra
|
kāmātmanaḥ ślāghano dyūtakāle nāgāḥ śamaṃ paśya vipākamasya ||29||

30anāptānāṃ saṃgrahāttvaṃ narendra tathā''ptānāṃ nigrahāccaiva rājan
|
bhūmiṃ sphītāṃ durbalatvādanantāmaśaktastvaṃ rakṣituṃ kauraveya ||30||

31anujñāto rathavegāvadhūtaḥ śrānto'bhipadye śayanaṃ nṛsiṃha
|
prātaḥ śrotāraḥ kuravaḥ sabhāyāmajātaśatrorvacanaṃ sametāḥ ||31||

32dhṛtarāṣṭra uvāca|
anujñāto'syāvasathaṃ parehi prapadyasva śayanaṃ sūtaputra
|
prātaḥ śrotāraḥ kuravaḥ sabhāyāmajātaśatrorvacanaṃ tvayoktam||32||
