Mahābhārata 8.80 (Karṇaparvan)

1sañjaya uvāca|
rājan kurūṇāṃ pravarairbalairbhīmamabhidrutam
|
majjantamiva kaunteyamujjihīrṣardhanañjayaḥ ||1||

2vimṛdya sūtaputrasya senāṃ bhārata sāyakaiḥ
|
prāhiṇonmṛtyulokāya paravīrān dhanañjayaḥ ||2||

3tato'syāmbaramāśritya śarajālāni bhāgaśaḥ
|
adṛśyanta tathā'nye ca nijaghnustava vāhinīm ||3||

4sa pakṣisaṃghācaritamākāśaṃ pūrayañśaraiḥ
|
dhanañjayo mahābāhuḥ kurūṇāmantako'bhavat ||4||

5tato bhallaiḥ kṣurapraiśca nārācairvimalairapi
|
gātrāṇi prācchinatpārthaḥ śirāṃsi ca cakarta ha ||5||

6chinnagātrairvikavacairviśiraskaiḥ samantataḥ
|
pātitaiśca patadbhiśca yodhairāsītsamāvṛtā ||6||

7dhanañjayaśarābhyastaiḥ syandanāśvarathādvipaiḥ
|
saṃchinnabhinnāvidhvastairvyaṅgāṅgāvayavaiḥ stṛtā ||7||

8sudurgamā suviṣamā ghorā'tyarthaṃ sudurdṛśā
|
raṇabhūmirabhūdrājanmahāvaitaraṇī yathā ||8||

9īṣācakrākṣabhallaiśca vyaśvaiḥ sāśvaiśca yudhyatām
|
sasūtairhatasūtaiśca rathaistīrṇā'bhavanmahī ||9||

10suvarṇavarṇasannāhairyodhaiḥ kanakabhūṣaṇaiḥ
|
āsthitāḥ klṛptavarmāṇo bhadrā nityamadā dvipāḥ ||10||

11kruddhāḥ krūrairmahāmātraiḥ pārṣṇyaṅguṣṭhapracoditāḥ
|
catuḥśatāḥ śaravarairhatāḥ petuḥ kirīṭinā ||11||

12paryastānīva śṛṅgāṇi samṛddhāni mahāgireḥ
|
dhanañjayaśarābhyastaiḥ stīrṇā bhūrvaravāraṇaiḥ ||12||

13samantājjaladaprakhyānvāraṇānmadavarṣiṇaḥ
|
abhipede'rjunaratho ghanān bhindannivāṃśumān ||13||

14hatairgajamanuṣyāśvairbhinnaiśca bahudhā rathaiḥ
|
viśastrayantrakavacairyuddhaśauṇḍairgatāsubhiḥ ||14||

15apaviddhāyudhairmārgaḥ stīrṇo'bhūtphālgunena vai
|
vyasphārayadvai gāṇḍīvaṃ sumahadbhairavāravam ||15||

16ghoravajraviniṣpeṣaṃ stanayitnurivāmbare
|
tataḥ prādīryata camūrdhanañjayaśarāhatā ||16||

17mahāvātasamāviddhā mahānauriva sāgara
|
nānārūpāḥ prāṇaharāḥ śarā gāṇḍīvacoditāḥ ||17||

18alātolkāśaniprakhyāstava sainyaṃ vinirdahan
|
mahāgirau veṇuvanaṃ niśi prajvalitaṃ yathā ||18||

19tathā tava mahāsainyaṃ prāsphuraccharapīḍitam
|
saṃpiṣṭadagdhavidhvastaṃ tava sainyaṃ kirīṭinā ||19||

20kṛtaṃ pravihataṃ bāṇaiḥ sarvataḥ pradrutaṃ diśaḥ
|
mahāvane mṛgagaṇā dāvāgnitrāsatā yathā ||20||

21kuravaḥ paryavartanta nirdagdhāḥ savyasācinā
|
utsṛjya ca mahābāhuṃ bhīmasenaṃ tathā raṇe ||21||

22balaṃ kurūṇāmudvignaṃ sarvamāsītparāṅmukham
|
tataḥ kuruṣu bhagneṣu bībhatsuraparājitaḥ ||22||

23bhīmasenaṃ samāsādya muhūrtaṃ so'bhyavartata
|
samāgamya ca bhīmena mantrayitvā ca phālgunaḥ ||23||

24viśalyamarujaṃ cāsmai kathayitvā yudhiṣṭhiram
|
bhīmasenābhyanujñātastataḥ prāyāddhanañjayaḥ ||24||

25nādayan rathaghoṣeṇa pṛthivīṃ dyāṃ ca bhārata
|
tataḥ parivṛto vīrairdaśabhiryodhapuṅgavaiḥ ||25||

26duḥśāsanādavarajaistava putrairdhanañjayaḥ
|
te tamabhyardayanbāṇairulkābhiriva kuñjaram ||26||

27ātateṣvasanāḥ śūrā nṛtyanta iva bhārata
|
apasavyāṃstu tāṃścakre rathena madhusūdanaḥ ||27||

28niyuktānhi sa tānmene yamāyāśu kirīṭinā
|
tataste prādravañśūrāḥ parāṅmukharathe'rjune ||28||

29teṣāmāpatatāṃ ketūnaśvāṃścāpāni sāyakān
|
nārācairardhacandraiśca kṣipraṃ pārtho nyapātayat ||29||

30athānyairdaśabhirbhallaiḥ śirāṃsyeṣāmapātayat
|
roṣasaṃraktanetrāṇi saṃda‌ṣṭauṣṭhāni bhūtale ||30||

31tāni vaktrāṇi vibabhuḥ kamalānīva bhūriśaḥ
|
tāṃstu bhallairmahāvegairdaśabhirdaśa kauravān ||31||

32rukmāṅgadān rukmapuṅkhairhatvā prāyāda‌mitrahā ||32||
