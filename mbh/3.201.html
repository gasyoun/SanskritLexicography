Mahābhārata 3.201 (Āraṇyakaparvan)

1vaiśampāyana uvāca|
śrutvā tu rājā rājarṣerindradyumnasya tat tathā
|
mārkaṇḍeyānmahābhāgāt svargasya pratipādanam||1||

2yudhiṣṭhiro mahārāja papraccha bharatarṣabha
|
mārkaṇḍeyaṃ tapovṛddhaṃ dīrghāyuṣamakalmaṣam||2||

3viditāstava dharmajña devadānavarākṣasāḥ
|
rājavaṃśāśca vividhā ṛṣivaṃśāśca śāśvatāḥ||3||

4na te'styaviditaṃ kiñcidasmi~lloke dvijottama
|
kathāṃ vetsi mune divyāṃ manuṣyoragarakṣasām||4||

5devagandharvayakṣāṇāṃ kinnarāpsarasāṃ tathā
|
idamicchāmyahaṃ śrotuṃ tattvena dvijasattama||5||

6kuvalāśva iti khyāta ikṣvākuraparājitaḥ
|
kathaṃ nāma viparyāsāddhundhumāratvamāgataḥ||6||

7etadicchāmi tattvena jñātuṃ bhārgavasattama
|
viparyastaṃ yathā nāma kuvalāśvasya dhīmataḥ||7||

8vaiśampāyana uvāca|
yudhiṣṭhireṇaivamukto mārkaṇḍeyo mahāmuniḥ
|
dhaundhumāramupākhyānaṃ kathayāmāsa bhārata||8||

9mārkaṇḍeya uyāca
|
hanta te kathayiṣyāmi śrṛṇu rājan yudhiṣṭhira
|
dharmiṣṭhamidamākhyānaṃ dhundhumārasya tacchṛṇu||9||

10yathā sa rājā ikṣvākuḥ kuvalāśvo mahīpatiḥ
|
dhundhumāratvamagamat tatcchṛṇuṣva mahīpate||10||

11maharṣirviśrṛtastāta uttaṅka iti bhārata
|
marudhanvasu ramyeṣu āśramastasya kaurava||11||

12uttaṅkastu mahārāja tapo'tapyat suduścaram
|
ārirādhayiṣurviṣṇuṃ bahūn varṣagaṇān vibhuḥ||12||

13tasya prītaḥ sa bhagavān sākṣāddarśanameyivān
|
dṛṣṭvaiva cārṣiḥ prahvastaṃ tuṣṭāva vividhaiḥ stavaiḥ||13||

14uttaṅka uvāca|
tvayā deva prajāḥ sarvāḥ sasurāsuramānavāḥ
|
sthāvarāṇi ca bhūtāni jaṅgamāni tathaiva ca||14||

15brahma vedāśca vedyaṃ ca tvayā sṛṣṭaṃ mahādyute
|
śiraste gaganaṃ deva netre śaśidivākarau||15||

16niḥśvāsaḥ pavanaścāpi tejo'gniśca tavācyuta
|
bāhavaste diśaḥ sarvāḥ kukṣiścāpi mahārṇavaḥ||16||

17ūrū te parvatā deva khaṃ jaṅghe madhusūdana
|
pādau te pṛthivī devī romāṇyoṣadhayastathā||17||

18indrasomāgnivaruṇā devāsuramahoragāḥ
|
prahvāstvāmupatiṣṭhanti stuvanto vividhaistavaiḥ||18||

19tvayā vyāptāni sarvāṇi bhūtāni bhuvaneśvara
|
yoginaḥ sumahāvīryāḥ stuvanti tvāṃ maharṣayaḥ||19||

20tvayi tuṣṭe jagatsvāsthyaṃ tvayi kruddhe mahadbhayam
|
bhayānāmapanetā'si tvamekaḥ puruṣottama||20||

21devānāṃ mānuṣāṇāṃ ca sarvabhūtasukhāvahaḥ
|
tribhirvikramaṇairdeva trayo lokāstvayā hṛtāḥ||21||

22asurāṇāṃ samṛddhānāṃ vināśaśca tvayā kṛtaḥ
|
tava vikramaṇardevā nirvāṇamagaman param||22||

23parābhūtāśca daityendrāstvayi kruddhe mahādyute
|
tvaṃ hi kartā vikartā ca bhūtānāmiha sarvaśaḥ||23||

24ārādhayitvā tvāṃ devāḥ sukhamedhanti sarvaśaḥ
|
evaṃ stuto hṛṣīkeśa uttaṅkena mahātmanā||24||

25uttaṅkamabravīdviṣṇuḥ prītaste'haṃ varaṃ vṛṇu
|
uttaṅka uvāca|
paryāpto me varo hyeṣa yadahaṃ dṛṣṭavān harim||25||

26puruṣaṃ śāśvataṃ divyaṃ sraṣṭāraṃ jagataḥ prabhum
|
viṣṇuruvāca| prītaste'hamalaulyena bhaktyā tava ca sattama||26||

27avaśyaṃ hi tvayā brahān matto grāhyo varo dvija
|
evaṃ sa chandyamānastu vareṇa hariṇā tadā||27||

28uttaṅkaḥ prāñjalirvavre varaṃ bharatasattama
|
yadi me bhagavan prītaḥ puṇḍarīkanibhekṣaṇa||28||

29dharme satye dame caiva buddhirbhavatu me sadā
|
abhyāsaśca bhavedbhaktyā tvayi nityaṃ mameśvara||29||

30bhagavānuvāca|
sarvametaddhi bhavitā matprasādāt tava dvija
|
pratibhāsyati yogaśca yena yukto divaukasām||30||

31trayāṇāmapi lokānāṃ mahat kāryaṃ kariṣyasi
|
utsādanārthaṃ lokānāṃ dhundhurnāma mahāsuraḥ||31||

32tapasyati tapo ghoraṃ śrṛṇu yastaṃ haniṣyati
|
rājā hi vīryavāṃstāta ikṣvākuraparājitaḥ||32||

33bṛhadaśva iti khyāto bhaviṣyati mahīpatiḥ
|
tasya putraḥ śucirdāntaḥ kuvalāśva iti śrutaḥ||33||

34sa yogabalamāsthāya māmakaṃ pārthivottamaḥ
|
śāsanāt tava viprarṣe dhundhumāro bhaviṣyati
|
evamuktvā tu taṃ vipraṃ viṣṇurantaradhīyata||34||
