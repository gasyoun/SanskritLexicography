Mahābhārata 3.239 (Āraṇyakaparvan)

1vaiśampāyana uvāca|
dhṛtarāṣṭraṃ tataḥ sarve dadṛśurjanamejaya
|
pṛṣṭvā sukhamatho rājñaḥ pṛṣṭā rājñā ca bhārata||1||

2tatastairvihitaḥ pūrvaṃ samaṅgo nāma ballavaḥ
|
samīpasthāstadā gāvo dhṛtarāṣṭre nyavedayat||2||

3anantaraṃ ca rādheyaḥ śakuniśca viśāmpate
|
āhatuḥ pārthivaśreṣṭhaṃ dhṛtarāṣṭraṃ janādhipam||3||

4ramaṇīyeṣu deśeṣu ghoṣāḥ samprati kaurava
|
smāraṇe samayaḥ prāpto vatsānāmapi cāṅkanam||4||

5mṛgayā cocitā rājannasmin kāle sutasya te
|
duryodhanasya gamanaṃ samanujñātumarhasi||5||

6dhṛtarāṣṭra uvāca|
mṛgayā śobhanā tāta gavāṃ hi samavekṣaṇam
|
viśrambhastu na gantavyo vallavānāmiti smare||6||

7te tu tatra naravyāghrāḥ samīpa iti naḥ śrutam
|
ato nābhyanujānāmi gamanaṃ tatra vaḥ svayam||7||

8chadmanā nirjitāste tu kārśitāśca mahāvane
|
taponityāśca rādheya samarthāśca mahārathāḥ||8||

9dharmarājo na saṅkruddhyedbhīmasenastvamarpaṇaḥ
|
yajñasenasya duhitā teja eva tu kevalam||9||

10yūyaṃ cāpyaparādhyeyurdarpamohasamanvitāḥ
|
tato vinirdaheyuste tapasā hi samanvitāḥ||10||

11athavā sāyudhā vīrā manyunā'bhipariplutāḥ
|
sahitā baddhanistriṃśā daheyuḥ śastratejasā||11||

12atha yūyaṃ bahutvāttānabhiyāta kathañcana
|
anāryaṃ paramaṃ tat syādaśakyaṃ tacca vai matam||12||

13uṣito hi mahābāhurindraloke dhanañjayaḥ
|
divyānyastrāṇyavāpyātha tataḥ pratyāgato vanam||13||

14akṛtāstreṇa pṛthivī jitā bībhatsunā purā
|
kiṃ punaḥ sa kṛtāstro'dya na hanyādvo mahārathaḥ||14||

15athavā madvacaḥ śrutvā tatra yattā bhaviṣyatha
|
udvignavāso viśrambhādduḥkhaṃ tatra bhaviṣyati||15||

16athavā sainikāḥ kecidapakuryuryudhiṣṭhiram
|
tadabuddhikṛtaṃ karma doṣamutpādayecca vaḥ||16||

17tasmādgacchantu puruṣāḥ smāraṇāyāptakāriṇaḥ
|
na svayaṃ tatra gamanaṃ rocaye tava bhārata||17||

18śakuniruvāca| dharmajñaḥ pāṇḍavo jyeṣṭhaḥ pratijñātaṃ ca saṃsadi
|
tena dvādaśa varṣāṇi vastavyānīti bhārata||18||

19anuvṛttāśca te sarve pāṇḍavā dharmacāriṇaḥ
|
yudhiṃṣṭhirastu kaunteyo na naḥ kopaṃ kariṣyati||19||

20mṛgayāṃ caiva no gantumicchā saṃvartate bhṛśam
|
smāraṇaṃ tu cikīrṣāmo na tu pāṇḍavadarśanam||20||

21na cānāryasamācāraḥ kaścit tatra bhaviṣyati
|
na ca tatra gamiṣyāmo yatra teṣāṃ pratiśrayaḥ||21||

22vaiśampāyana uvāca|
evamuktaḥ śakuninā dhṛtarāṣṭro janeśvaraḥ
|
duryodhanaṃ sahāmātyamanujajñe na kāmataḥ||22||

23anujñātastu gāndhāriḥ karṇena sahitastadā
|
niryayau bharataśreṣṭho balena mahatā vṛtaḥ||23||

24duḥśāsanena ca tathā saubalena ca dhīmatā
|
saṃvṛto bhrātṛbhiścānyaiḥ strībhiścāpi sahasraśaḥ||24||

25taṃ niryāntaṃ mahābāhuṃ draṣṭuṃ dvaitavanaṃ saraḥ
|
paurāścānuyayuḥ sarve sahadārā vanaṃ ca tat||25||

26aṣṭau rathasahastrāṇi trīṇi nāgāyutāni ca
|
pattayo bahusāhasrā hayāśca navatiḥ śatāḥ||26||

27śakaṭāpaṇaveśāśca vaṇijo bandinastathā
|
narāśca mṛgayāśīlāḥ śataśo'tha sahasraśaḥ||27||

28tataḥ prayāṇe nṛpateḥ sumahānabhavat svanaḥ
|
prāvṛṣīva mahāvāyoruddhatasya viśāmpate||28||

29gavyūtimātre nyavasadrājā duryodhanastadā
|
prayāto vāhanaiḥ sarvaistato dvaitavanaṃ saraḥ||29||
