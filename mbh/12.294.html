Mahābhārata 12.294 (Śāntiparvan)

1parāśara uvāca |
pratigrahāgatā vipre kṣatriye yudhi nirjitāḥ
|
vaiśya nyāyārjitāścaiva śūdre śuśrūṣayārjitāḥ ||1||

2svalpāpyarthāḥ praśasyante dharmasyārthe mahāphalāḥ
|
nityaṃ trayāṇāṃ varṇānāṃ śuśrūṣuḥ śūdra ucyate ||2||

3kṣatradharmā vaiśyadharmā nāvṛttiḥ patate dvijaḥ
|
śūdradharmā yadā tu syāttadā patati vai dvijaḥ ||3||

4vāṇijyaṃ pāśupālyaṃ ca tathā śilpopajīvanam
|
śūdrasyāpi vidhīyante yadā vṛttirna jāyate ||4||

5raṅgāvataraṇaṃ caiva tathā rūpopajīvanam
|
madyamāṃsopajīvyaṃ ca vikrayaṃ lohacarmaṇoḥ ||5||

6apūrviṇā na kartavyaṃ karma loke vigarhitam
|
kṛtapūrvaṃ tu tyajato mahāndharma iti śrutiḥ ||6||

7saṃsiddhaḥ puruṣo loke yadācarati pāpakam
|
madenābhiplutamanāstacca na grāhyamucyate ||7||

8śrūyante hi purāṇeṣu prajā dhigdaṇḍaśāsanāḥ
|
dāntā dharmapradhānāśca nyāyadharmānuvṛttikāḥ ||8||

9dharma eva sadā nṝṇāmiha rājanpraśasyate
|
dharmavṛddhā guṇāneva sevante hi narā bhuvi ||9||

10taṃ dharmamasurāstāta nāmṛṣyanta janādhipa
|
vivardhamānāḥ kramaśastatra te'nvāviśanprajāḥ ||10||

11tāsāṃ darpaḥ samabhavatprajānāṃ dharmanāśanaḥ
|
darpātmanāṃ tataḥ paścāt krodhastāsāmajāyata ||11||

12tataḥ krodhābhibhūtānāṃ vṛttaṃ lajjāsamanvitam
|
hrīścaivāpyanaśadrājaṃstato moho vyajāyata ||12||

13tato mohaparītāstā nāpaśyanta yathā purā
|
parasparāvamardena vardhayantyo yathāsukham ||13||

14tāḥ prāpya tu sa dhigdaṇḍo na kāraṇamato'bhavat
|
tato'bhyagacchandevāṃśca brāhmaṇāṃścāvamanya ha ||14||

15etasminneva kāle tu devā devavaraṃ śivam
|
agacchanśaraṇaṃ dhīraṃ bahurūpaṃ guṇādhikam ||15||

16tena sma te gaganagāḥ sapurāḥ pātitāḥ kṣitau
|
tridhāpyekena bāṇena devāpyāyitatejasā ||16||

17teṣāmadhipatistvāsīdbhīmo bhīmaparākramaḥ
|
devatānāṃ bhayakaraḥ sa hataḥ śūlapāṇinā ||17||

18tasminhate'tha svaṃ bhāvaṃ pratyapadyanta mānavāḥ
|
prāpadyanta ca vedānvai śāstrāṇi ca yathā purā ||18||

19tato'bhiṣicya rājyena devānāṃ divi vāsavam
|
saptarṣayaścānvayuñjannarāṇāṃ daṇḍadhāraṇe ||19||

20saptarṣīṇāmathordhvaṃ ca vipṛthurnāma pārthivaḥ
|
rājānaḥ kṣatriyāścaiva maṇḍaleṣu pṛthakpṛthak ||20||

21mahākuleṣu ye jātā vṛddhāḥ pūrvatarāśca ye
|
teṣāmapyāsuro bhāvo hṛdayānnāpasarpati ||21||

22tasmāttenaiva bhāvena sānuṣaṅgeṇa pārthivāḥ
|
āsurāṇyeva karmāṇi nyasevanbhīmavikramāḥ ||22||

23pratyatiṣṭhaṃśca teṣveva tānyeva sthāpayantyapi
|
bhajante tāni cādyāpi ye bāliśatarā narāḥ ||23||

24tasmādahaṃ bravīmi tvāṃ rājansaṃcintya śāstrataḥ
|
saṃsiddhādhigamaṃ kuryātkarma hiṃsātmakaṃ tyajet ||24||

25na saṃkareṇa draviṇaṃ pracinvīyādvicakṣaṇaḥ
|
dharmārthaṃ nyāyamutsṛjya na tatkalyāṇamucyate ||25||

26sa tvamevaṃvidho dāntaḥ kṣatriyaḥ priyabāndhavaḥ
|
prajā bhṛtyāṃśca putrāṃśca svadharmeṇānupālaya ||26||

27iṣṭāniṣṭasamāyoge vairaṃ sauhārdameva ca
|
atha jātisahasrāṇi bahūni parivartate ||27||

28tasmādguṇeṣu rajyethā mā doṣeṣu kathaṃcana
|
nirguṇo'pi hi durbuddhirātmanaḥ so'tirajyate ||28||

29mānuṣeṣu mahārāja dharmādharmau pravartataḥ
|
na tathā'nyeṣu bhūteṣu manuṣyarahiteṣviha ||29||

30dharmaśīlo naro vidvānīhako'nīhako'pi vā
|
ātmabhūtaḥ sadā loke caredbhūtānyahiṃsayā ||30||

31yadā vyapetahṛllekhaṃ mano bhavati tasya vai
|
nānṛtaṃ caiva bhavati tadā kalyāṇamṛcchati ||31||
