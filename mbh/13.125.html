Mahābhārata 13.125 (Anuśāsanaparvan)

1yudhiṣṭhira uvāca|
janma mānuṣyakaṃ prāpya karmakṣetraṃ sudurlabham
|
śreyorthinā daridreṇa kiṃ kartavyaṃ pitāmaha ||1||

2dānānāmuttamaṃ yacca deyaṃ yacca yathā yathā
|
mānyānpūjyāṃśca gāṅgeya rahasyaṃ vaktumarhasi ||2||

3vaiśampāyana uvāca|
evaṃ pṛṣṭo narendreṇa pāṇḍavena yaśasvinā
|
dharmāṇāṃ paramaṃ guhyaṃ bhīṣmaḥ provāca pārthivam ||3||

4bhīṣma uvāca|
śṛṇuṣvāvahito rājandharmaguhyāni bhārata
|
yathā hi bhagavānvyāsaḥ purā kathitavānmayi||4||

5devaguhyamidaṃ rājanyamenākliṣṭakarmaṇā
|
niyamasthena yuktena tapaso mahataḥ phalam ||5||

6yena yaḥ prīyate devaḥ prīyante pitarastathā
|
ṛṣayaḥ pramathāḥ śrīśca citragupto diśāṃ gajāḥ||6||

7ṛṣidharmaḥ smṛto yatra sarahasyo mahāphalaḥ
|
mahādānaphalaṃ caiva sarvayajñaphalaṃ tathā ||7||

8yaścaitadevaṃ jānīyājjñātvā vā kurute'nagha
|
sa doṣo doṣavāṃśceha tairguṇaiḥ saha yujyate ||8||

9daśasūnāsamaṃ cakraṃ daśacakrasamo dhvajaḥ
|
daśadhvajasamā veśyā daśaveśyāsamo nṛpaḥ||9||

10ardhenaitāni sarvāṇi nṛpatiḥ kathyate'dhikaḥ
|
trivargasahitaṃ śāsraṃ pavitraṃ puṇyalakṣaṇam ||10||

11dharmavyākaraṇaṃ puṇyaṃ rahasyaśravaṇaṃ mahat
|
śrotavyaṃ dharmasaṃyuktaṃ vihitaṃ tridaśaiḥ svayam ||11||

12pitṝṇāṃ yatra guhyāni procyante śrāddhakarmaṇi
|
devatānāṃ ca sarveṣāṃ rahasyaṃ kathyate'khilam||12||

13ṛṣidharmaḥ smṛto yatra sarahasyo mahāphalaḥ
|
mahāyajñaphalaṃ caiva sarvadānaphalaṃ tathā ||13||

14ye paṭhanti sadā martyā yeṣāṃ caivopatiṣṭhati
|
śrutvā ca phalamācaṣṭe svayaṃ nārāyaṇaḥ prabhuḥ ||14||

15gavāṃ phalaṃ tīrthaphalaṃ yajñānāṃ caiva yatphalam
|
etatphalamavāpnoti yo naro'tithipūjakaḥ ||15||

16śrotāraḥ śraddadhānāśca yeṣāṃ śuddhaṃ ca mānasam
|
teṣāṃ vyaktaṃ jitā lokāḥ śraddadhānena sādhunā ||16||

17mucyate kilviṣāścaiva na sa pāpena lipyate
|
dharmaṃ ca labhate nityaṃ pretya lokagato naraḥ ||17||

18kasyacittvatha kālasya devadūto yadṛcchayā
|
sthito hyantarhito bhūtvā paryabhāṣata vāsavam||18||

19yau tau kāmaguṇopetāvaśvinau bhiṣajāṃ varau
|
ājñayā'haṃ tayoḥ prāptaḥ sanarān pitṛdaivatān||19||

20kasmāddhi maithunaṃ śrāddhe dāturbhoktuśca varjitam
|
kimarthaṃ ca trayaḥ piṃḍāḥ pravibhaktāḥ pṛthak pṛthak||20||

21prathamaḥ kasya dātavyo madhyamaḥ kva ca gacchati
|
uttaraśca smṛtaḥ kasya etadicchāmi veditum||21||

22śraddadhānena dūtena bhāṣitaṃ dharmasaṃhitam
|
pūrvasthāstridaśāḥ sarve pitaraḥ pūjya khecaram||22||

23pitara ūcuḥ|
svāgataṃ te'stu bhadraṃ te śrūyatāṃ khecarottama
|
gūḍhārthaḥ paramaḥ praśno bhavatā samudīritaḥ ||23||

24śrāddhaṃ dattvā ca bhuktvā ca puruṣo yaḥ striyaṃ vrajet
|
pitarastasya taṃ māsaṃ tasmin retasi śerate||24||

25pravibhāgaṃ tu piṃḍānāṃ pravakṣyāmyanupūrvaśaḥ
|
piṃḍo hyadhastādgacchaṃstu apa āviśya bhāvayet||25||

26piṇḍaṃ tu madhyamaṃ tatra patnī tvekā samaśnute
|
piṇḍastṛtīyo yasteṣāṃ taṃ dadyājjātavedasi ||26||

27eṣa śrāddhavidhiḥ prokto yathā dharmo na lupyate
|
pitarastasya tuṣyanti prahṛṣṭamanasaḥ sadā ||27||

28prajā vivardhate cāsya akṣayaṃ copatiṣṭhati
|
devadūta uvāca|
ānupūrvyeṇa piṃḍānāṃ pravibhāgaḥ pṛthak pṛthak||28||

29pitṝṇāṃ triṣu sarveṣāṃ niruktaṃ kathitaṃ tvayā
|
ekaḥ samuddhṛtaḥ piṃḍo hyadhastātkasya gacchati ||29||

30kaṃ vā prīṇayate devaṃ kathaṃ tārayate pitṝn
|
madhyamaṃ tu tadā patnī bhuṃkte'nujñātameva hi ||30||

31kimarthaṃ pitarastasya kavyameva ca bhuñjate
|
atra yastvantimaḥ piṇḍo gacchate jātavedasam||31||

32bhavate kā gatistasya kaṃ vā samanugacchati
|
etadicchāmyahaṃ śrotuṃ piṇḍeṣu triṣu yā gatiḥ||32||

33phalaṃ vṛttiṃ ca mārgaṃ ca yaścainaṃ pratipadyate
|
pitara ūcuḥ |
sumahāneṣa praśno vai yastvayā samudīritaḥ ||33||

34rahasyamadbhutaṃ cāpi pṛṣṭāḥ sma gaganecara
|
etadeva praśaṃsanti devāśca munayastathā ||34||

35te'pyevaṃ nābhijānanti pitṛkāryaviniścayam
|
varjayitvā mahātmānaṃ cirajīvinamuttamam ||35||

36pitṛbhaktastu yo vipro varalabdho mahāyaśāḥ
|
trayāṇāmapi piṇḍānāṃ śrutvā bhagavato gatim ||36||

37devadūtena yaḥ pṛṣṭaḥ śrāddhasya vidhiniścayaḥ
|
gatiṃ trayāṇāṃ piṇḍānāṃ śṛṇuṣvāvahito mama||37||

38apo gacchati yo hyatra śaśinaṃ hyeṣa prīṇayet
|
śaśī prīṇayate devān pitṝṃścaiva mahāmate ||38||

39bhuṃkte tu patnī yaṃ caiṣāmanujñātā tu madhyamam
|
putrakāmāya putraṃ tu prayacchanti pitāmahāḥ ||39||

40havyavāhe tu yaḥ piṇḍo dīyate tannibodha me
|
pitarastena tṛpyanti prītāḥ kāmān diśaṃti ca||40||

41etatte kathitaṃ sarvaṃ triṣu piṇḍeṣu yā gatiḥ
|
ṛtvigbhyo yajamānasya pitṛtvamanugacchati ||41||

42tasminnahani manyante parihāryaṃ hi maithunam
|
śucinā tu sadā śrāddhaṃ bhoktavyaṃ khecarottama||42||

43ye mayā kathitā doṣāste tathā syurna cānyathā
|
tasmātsnātaḥ śuciḥ kṣāntaḥ śrāddhaṃ bhuñjīta vai dvijaḥ ||43||

44prajā vivardhate cāsya yaścaivaṃ saṃprayacchati
|
tato vidyutprabho nāma ṛṣirāha mahātapāḥ ||44||

45ādityatejasā tasya tulyaṃ rūpaṃ prakāśate
|
sa ca dharmarahasyāni śrutvā śakramathābravīt ||45||

46tiryagyonigatān sattvān martyā hiṃsanti mohitāḥ
|
kīṭān pipīlikān sarpān meṣān samṛgapakṣiṇaḥ ||46||

47kilviṣaṃ subahu prāptāḥ kiṃsvideṣāṃ pratikriyā
|
tato devagaṇāḥ sarve ṛṣayaśca tapodhanāḥ ||47||

48pitaraśca mahābhāgāḥ pūjayanti sma taṃ munim
|
śakra uvāca|
kurukṣetraṃ gayāṃ gaṅgāṃ prabhāsaṃ puṣkarāṇi ca||48||

49etāni manasā dhyātvā avagāhettato jalam
|
tathā mucyati pāpena rāhuṇā candramā yathā ||49||

50tryahaṃ snātaḥ sa bhavati nirāhāraśca vartate
|
spṛśate yo gavāṃ pṛṣṭhaṃ vāladhiṃ ca namasyati ||50||

51tato vidyutprabho vākyamabhyabhāṣata vāsavam
|
ayaṃ sūkṣmataro dharmastaṃ nibodha śatakrato ||51||

52ghṛṣṭo vaṭakaṣāyeṇa anuliptaḥ priyaṃguṇā
|
kṣīreṇa ṣaṣṭikān bhuktvā sarvapāpaiḥ pramucyate||52||

53śrūyatāṃ cāparaṃ guhyaṃ rahasyamṛṣicintitam
|
śrutaṃ me bhāṣamāṇasya sthāṇoḥ sthāne bṛhaspateḥ ||53||

54rudreṇa saha deveśa tannibodha śacīpate
|
parvatārohaṇaṃ kṛtvā ekapādo vibhāvasum||54||

55nirīkṣeta nirāhāra ūrdhvabāhuḥ kṛtāñjaliḥ
|
tapasā mahatā yukta upavāsaphalaṃ labhet ||55||

56raśmibhistāpito'rkasya sarvapāpamapohati
|
grīṣmakāle'tha vā śīte evaṃ pāpamapohati ||56||

57tataḥ pāpātpramuktasya dyutirbhavati śāśvatī
|
tejasā sūryavaddīpto bhrājate somavatpunaḥ ||57||

58madhye tridaśavargasya devarājaḥ śatakratuḥ
|
uvāca madhuraṃ vākyaṃ bṛhaspatimanuttamam ||58||

59dharmaguhyaṃ tu bhagavanmānuṣāṇāṃ sukhāvaham
|
sarahasyāśca ye doṣāstān yathāvadudīraya ||59||

60bṛhaspatiruvāca|
pratimehanti ye sūryamanilaṃ dviṣate ca ye
|
havyavāhe pradīpte ca samidhaṃ ye na juhvati ||60||

61bālavatsāṃ ca ye dhenuṃ duhanti kṣīrakāraṇāt
|
teṣāṃ doṣānpravakṣyāmi tānnibodha śacīpate ||61||

62bhānumānanilaścaiva havyavāhaśca vāsava
|
lokānāṃ mātaraścaiva gāvaḥ sṛṣṭāḥ svayaṃbhuvā ||62||

63lokāṃstārayituṃ śaktā martyeṣveteṣu devatāḥ
|
sarve bhavantaḥ śṛṇvantu ekaikaṃ dharmaniścayam||63||

64varṣāṇi ṣaḍaśītiṃ tu durvṛttāḥ kulapāṃsanāḥ
|
striyaḥ sarvāśca durvṛttāḥ pratimehanti yā ravim ||64||

65aniladveṣiṇaḥ śakra garbhasthā cyavate prajā
|
havyavāhasya dīptasya samidhaṃ ye na juhvati||65||

66agnikāryeṣu vai teṣāṃ havyaṃ nāśnāti pāvakaḥ
|
kṣīraṃ tu bālavatsānāṃ ye pibantīha mānavāḥ ||66||

67na teṣāṃ kṣīrapāḥ kecijjāyante kulavardhanāḥ
|
prajākṣayeṇa yujyante kulavaṃśakṣayeṇa ca ||67||

68evametatpurā dṛṣṭaṃ kulavṛddhairdvijātibhiḥ
|
tasmādvarjyāni varjyāni kāryaṃ kāryaṃ ca nityaśaḥ||68||

69bhūtikāmena martyena satyametadbravīmi te
|
tataḥ sarvā mahābhāga devatāḥ samarudgaṇāḥ ||69||

70ṛṣayaśca mahābhāgāḥ pṛcchanti sma pitṝṃstataḥ
|
pitaraḥ kena tuṣyanti martyānāmalpacetasām ||70||

71akṣayaṃ ca kathaṃ dānaṃ bhaveccaivordhvadehikam
|
ānṛṇyaṃ vā kathaṃ martyā gaccheyuḥ kena karmaṇā ||71||

72etadicchāmahe śrotuṃ paraṃ kautūhalaṃ hi naḥ
|
pitara ūcuḥ |
nyāyato vai mahābhāgāḥ saṃśayaḥ samudāhṛtaḥ||72||

73śrūyatāṃ yena tuṣyāmo martyānāṃ sādhukarmaṇām
|
nīlaṣaṇḍapramokṣeṇa amāvāsyāṃ tilodakaiḥ||73||

74varṣāsu dīpakaiścaiva pitṝṇāmanṛṇo bhavet
|
akṣayaṃ nirvyalīkaṃ ca dānametanmahāphalam ||74||

75asmākaṃ paritoṣaśca akṣayaḥ parikīrtyate
|
śraddadhānāśca ye martyā āhariṣyanti santatim||76||

76durgātte tārayiṣyanti narakātprapitāmahān
|
pitṝṇāṃ bhāṣitaṃ śrutvā hṛṣṭaromā tapodhanaḥ ||76||

77vṛddhagārgyo mahātejāstānevaṃ vākyamabravīt
|
ke guṇā nīlaṣaṇḍasya pramuktasya tapodhanāḥ ||77||

78varṣāsu dīpadānena tathaiva ca tilodakaiḥ
|
pitara ūcuḥ |
nīlaṣaṇḍasya lāṅgūlaṃ toyamabhyuddharedyadi||78||

79ṣaṣṭiṃ varṣasahastrāṇi pitarastena tarpitāḥ
|
yastu śṛṅgagataṃ paṅkaṃ kūlāduddhṛtya tiṣṭhati ||79||

80pitarastena gacchanti somalokamasaṃśayam
|
varṣāsu dīpadānena śaśīvacchobhate naraḥ ||80||

81tamorūpaṃ na tasyāsti dīpakaṃ yaḥ prayacchati
|
amāvāsyāṃ tu ye martyāḥ prayacchanti tilodakam||81||

82pātramaudumbaraṃ gṛhya madhumiśraṃ tapodhana
|
kṛtaṃ bhavati taiḥ śrāddhaṃ sarahasyaṃ yathārthavat ||82||

83hṛṣṭapuṣṭamanāsteṣāṃ prajā bhavati nityadā
|
kulavaṃśasya vṛddhistu piṇḍadasya phalaṃ bhavet||83||

84śraddadhānastu yaḥ kuryātpitṝṇāmanṛṇo bhavet
|
evameṣa samuddiṣṭaḥ śrāddhakālakramastathā
|
vidhiḥ pātraṃ phalaṃ caiva yathāvadanukīrtitam||84||
