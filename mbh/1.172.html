Mahābhārata 1.172 (Ādiparvan)

1gandharva uvāca|
atha tasyāmadṛśyāyāṃ nṛpatiḥ kāmamohitaḥ
|
pātanaḥ śatrusaṅghānāṃ papāta dharaṇītale||1||

2tasminnipatite bhūmāvatha sā cāruhāsinī
|
punaḥ pīnāyataśroṇī darśayāmāsa taṃ nṛpam||2||

3athābabhāṣe kalyāṇī vācā madhurayā nṛpam
|
taṃ kurūṇāṃ kulakaraṃ kāmābhihatacetasam||3||

4uvāca madhuraṃ vākyaṃ tapatī hasatīva sā
|
uttiṣṭhottiṣṭha bhadraṃ te na tvamarhasyarindama||4||

5mohaṃ nṛpatiśārdūla gantumāviṣkṛtaḥ kṣitau
|
evamukto'tha nṛpatirvācā madhurayā tadā||5||

6dadarśa vipulaśroṇīṃ tāmevābhimukhe sthitām
|
atha tāmasitāpāṅgīmābabhāṣe sa pārthivaḥ||6||

7manmathāgniparītātmā sandigdhākṣarayā girā
|
sādhu tvamasitāpāṅgi kāmārtaṃ mattakāśini||7||

8bhajasva bhajamānaṃ māṃ prāṇā hi prajahanti mām
|
tvadarthaṃ hi viśālākṣi māmayaṃ niśitaiḥ śaraiḥ||8||

9kāmaḥ kamalagarbhābhe pratividhyanna śāmyati
|
daṣṭamevamanākrande bhadre kāmamahāhinā||9||

10sā tvaṃ pīnāyataśroṇī māmāpnuhi varānane
|
tvadadhīnā hi me prāṇāḥ kinnarodgītabhāṣiṇi||10||

11cārusarvānavadyāṅgi padmendupratimānane
|
na hyahaṃ tvadṛte bhīru śakṣyāmi khalu jīvitum||11||

12kāmaḥ kamalapatrākṣi pratividhyati māmayam
|
tasmātkuru viśālākṣi mayyanukrośamaṅgane||12||

13bhaktaṃ māmasitāpāṅgi na parityaktumarhasi
|
tvaṃ hi māṃ prītiyogena trātumarhasi bhāmini||13||

14tvaddarśanakṛtasnehaṃ manaścalati me bhṛśam
|
na tvāṃ dṛṣṭvā punaścānyāṃ draṣṭuṃ kalyāṇi rocate||14||

15prasīda vaśago'haṃ te bhaktaṃ māṃ bhaja bhāvini
|
dṛṣṭvaiva tvāṃ varārohe manmatho bhṛśamaṅgane||15||

16antargataṃ viśālākṣi vidhyati sma patattribhiḥ
|
manmathāgnisamudbhūtaṃ dāhaṃ kamalalocane||16||

17prītisaṃyogayuktābhiradbhiḥ prahlādayasva me
|
puṣpāyudhaṃ durādharṣaṃ pracaṇḍaśarakārmukam||17||

18tvaddarśanasamudbhūtaṃ vidhyantaṃ duḥsahaiḥ śaraiḥ
|
upaśāmaya kalyāṇi ātmadānena bhāmini||18||

19gāndharveṇa vivāhena māmupaihi varāṅgane
|
vivāhānāṃ hi rambhoru gāndharvaḥ śreṣṭha ucyate||19||

20tapatyuvāca
|
nāhamīśātmano rājankanyā pitṛmatī hyaham
|
mayi cedasti te prītiryācasva pitaraṃ mama||20||

21yathā hi te mayā prāṇāḥ saṃgṛhītā nareśvara
|
darśanādeva bhūyastvaṃ tathā prāṇānmamāharaḥ||21||

22na cāhamīśā dehasya tasmānnṛpatisattama
|
samīpaṃ nopagacchāmi na svatantrā hi yoṣitaḥ||22||

23kā hi sarveṣu lokeṣu viśrutābhijanaṃ nṛpam
|
kanyā nābhilaṣennāthaṃ bhārtāraṃ bhaktavatsalam||23||

24tasmādevaṃ gate kāle yācasva pitaraṃ mama
|
ādityaṃ praṇipātena tapasā niyamena ca||24||

25sa cetkāmayate dātuṃ tava māmarisūdana
|
bhaviṣyāmyadya te rājansatataṃ vaśavartinī||25||

26ahaṃ hi tapatī nāma sāvitryavarajā sutā
|
asya lokapradīpasya savituḥ kṣatriyarṣabha||26||
