Mahābhārata 12.338 (Śāntiparvan)

1bhīṣma uvāca|
prāpya śvetaṃ mahādvīpaṃ nārado bhagavānṛṣiḥ
|
dadarśa tāneva narānśvetāṃścandrasamaprabhān||1||

2pūjayāmāsa śirasā manasā taiśca pūjitaḥ
|
didṛkṣurjapyaparamaḥ sarvakṛcchragataḥ sthitaḥ||2||

3bhūtvaikāgramanā vipra urdhvabāhuḥ samāhitaḥ
|
stotraṃ jagau sa viśvāya nirguṇāya guṇātmane||3||
