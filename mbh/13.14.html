Mahābhārata 13.14 (Anuśāsanaparvan)

1yudhiṣṭhira uvāca|
tvayā''pageya nāmāni śrutānīha jagatpateḥ
|
pitāmaheśāya vibho nāmānyācakṣva śaṃbhave ||1||

2brabhrave viśvarūpāya mahābhāgyaṃ ca tattvataḥ
|
surāsuragurau deve śaṃkare'vyaktayonaye ||2||

3bhīṣma uvāca|
aśakto'haṃ guṇānvaktuṃ mahādevasya dhīmataḥ
|
yo hi sarvagato devo na ca sarvatra dṛśyate ||3||

4brahmaviṣṇusureśānāṃ sraṣṭā ca prabhureva ca
|
brahmādayaḥ piśācāntā yaṃ hi devā upāsate ||4||

5prakṛtīnāṃ paratvena puruṣasya ca yaḥ paraḥ
|
cintyate yo yogavidbhirṛṣibhistattvadārśabhiḥ
|
akṣaraṃ paramaṃ brahma asacca sadasacca yaḥ ||5||

6prakṛtiṃ puruṣaṃ caiva kṣobhayitvā svatejasā
|
brahmāṇamasṛjattasmāddevadevaḥ prajāpatiḥ ||6||

7ko hi śakto guṇānvaktuṃ devadevasya dhīmataḥ
|
garbhajanmajarāyukto martyo mṛtyusamanvitaḥ ||7||

8ko hi śakto bhavaṃ jñātuṃ madvidhaḥ parameśvaram
|
ṛte nārāyaṇātputra śaṃkhacakragadādharāt ||8||

9eṣa vidvān guṇaśreṣṭho viṣṇuḥ paramadurjayaḥ
|
divyacakṣurmahātejā vīkṣyate yogacakṣuṣā ||9||

10rudrabhaktyā tu kṛṣṇena jagadvyāptaṃ mahātmanā
|
taṃ prasādya tadā devaṃ badaryāṃ kila bhārata ||10||

11arthātpriyataratvaṃ ca sarvalokeṣu vai tadā
|
prāptavāneva rājendra suvarṇākṣānmaheśvarāt ||11||

12pūrṇaṃ varṣasahasraṃ tu taptavāneṣa mādhavaḥ
|
prasādya varadaṃ devaṃ carācaraguruṃ śivam ||12||

13yuge yuge tu kṛṣṇena toṣito vai maheśvaraḥ
|
bhaktyā paramayā caiva prītaścaiva mahātmanaḥ||13||

14aiśvaryaṃ yādṛśaṃ tasya jagadyonermahātmanaḥ
|
tadayaṃ dṛṣṭavān sākṣātputrārthe hariracyutaḥ ||14||

15yasmātparataraṃ caiva nānyaṃ paśyāmi bhārata
|
vyākhyātuṃ devadevasya śakto nāmānyaśeṣataḥ ||15||

16eṣa śakto mahābāhurvaktuṃ bhagavato guṇān
|
vibhūtiṃ caiva kārtsnyena satyāṃ māheśvarīṃ nṛpa ||16||

17vaiśampāyana uvāca|
evamuktvā tadā bhīṣmo vāsudevaṃ mahāyaśaḥ
|
bhavamāhātmyasaṃyuktamidamāha pitāmahaḥ ||17||

18bhīṣma uvāca|
surāsuraguro deva viṣṇo tvaṃ vaktumarhasi
|
śivāya viṣṇurūpāya yanmāṃ pṛcchadyudhiṣṭhiraḥ ||18||

19nāmnāṃ sahasraṃ devasya taṃḍinā brahmayoninā
|
niveditaṃ brahmaloke brahmaṇo yatpurā'bhavat ||19||

20dvaipāyanaprabhṛtayastathā ceme tapodhanāḥ
|
ṛṣayaḥ suvratā dāntāḥ śṛṇvantu gadatastava ||20||

21dhruvāya naṃdine hotre goptre viśvasṛje'gnaye
|
mahābhāgyaṃ vibhorbrūhi muṃḍine'tha kapardine ||21||

22vāsudeva uvāca|
na gatiḥ karmaṇāṃ śakyā vettumīśasya tattvataḥ
|
hiraṇyagarbhapramukhā devāḥ sendrā maharṣayaḥ ||22||

23na viduryasya bhavanamādityāḥ sūkṣmadarśinaḥ
|
sa kathaṃ naramātreṇa śakyo jñātuṃ satāṃ gatiḥ ||23||

24tasyāhamasuraghnasya kāṃścidbhagavato guṇān
|
bhavatāṃ kīrtayiṣyāmi vrateśāya yathātatham ||24||

25vaiśampāyana uvāca|
evamuktvā tu bhagavān guṇāṃstasya mahātmanaḥ
|
upaspṛśya śucirbhūtvā kathayāmāsa dhīmataḥ ||25||

26vāsudeva uvāca|
śuśrūṣadhvaṃ brāhmaṇendrāstvaṃ ca tāta yudhiṣṭhira
|
tvaṃ cāpageya nāmāni śṛṇuṣveha kapardine ||26||

27yadavāptaṃ ca me pūrvaṃ sāṃbahetoḥ suduṣkaram
|
yathāvadbhagavān dṛṣṭo mayā pūrvaṃ samādhinā ||27||

28śaṃbare nihate pūrvaṃ raukmiṇeyena dhīmatā
|
atīte dvādaśe varṣe jāṃbavatyabravīddhi mām ||28||

29pradyumnacārudeṣṇādīnrukmiṇyā vīkṣya putrakān
|
putrārthinī māmupetya vākyamāha yudhiṣṭhiraḥ ||29||

30śūraṃ balavatāṃ śreṣṭhaṃ kāntarūpamakalmaṣam
|
ātmatulyaṃ mama sutaṃ prayacchācyuta māciram ||30||

31na hi te'prāpyamastīha triṣu lokeṣu kiñcana
|
lokān sṛjestvamaparānicchanyadukulodvaha ||31||

32tvayā dvādaśavarṣāṇi vratībhūtena śuṣyatā
|
ārādhya paśubhartāraṃ rukmiṇyāṃ janitāḥ sutāḥ ||32||

33cārudeṣṇaḥ sucāruśca cāruveśo yaśodharaḥ
|
cāruśravāścāruyaśāḥ pradyumnaḥ śaṃbhureva ca ||33||

34yathā te janitāḥ putrā rukmiṇyāṃ cāruvikramāḥ
|
tathā mamāpi tanayaṃ prayaccha madhusūdana ||34||

35ityevaṃ codito devyā tāmavocaṃ sumadhyamām
|
anujānīhi māṃ rājñi kariṣye vacanaṃ tava ||35||

36sā ca māmabravīdgaccha śivāya vijayāya ca
|
brahmā śivaḥ kāśyapaśca nadyo devā manonugāḥ ||36||

37kṣetrauṣadhyo yajñavāhāśchaṃdāṃsyṛṣigaṇādhvarāḥ
|
samudrā dakṣiṇāstobhā ṛkṣāṇi pitaro grahāḥ ||37||

38devapatnyo devakanyā devamātara eva ca
|
manvantarāṇi gāvaśca caṃdramāḥ savitā hariḥ ||38||

39sāvitrī brahmavidyā ca ṛtavo vatsarāstathā
|
kṣaṇā lavā muhūrtāśca nimeṣā yugaparyayāḥ ||39||

40rakṣantu sarvatra gataṃ tvāṃ yādava sukhāya ca
|
ariṣṭaṃ gaccha paṃthānamapramatto bhavānagha ||40||

41evaṃ kṛtasvastyayanastayā'haṃ tato'bhyanujñāya narendraputrīm
|
pituḥ samīpaṃ narasattamasya mātuśca rājñaśca tathā''hukasya ||41||

42gatvā samāvedya yadadbravīnmāṃ vidyādharendrasya sutā bhṛśārtā
|
tānabhyanujñāya tadātiduḥkhādgadaṃ tathaivātibalaṃ ca rāmam
|
athocatuḥ prītiyutau tadānīṃ tapaḥsamṛddhirbhavatostvavighnam ||42||

43prāpyānujñāṃ gurujanādahaṃ tārkṣyamacintayam
|
so'vahaddhimavantaṃ māṃ prāpya cainaṃ vyasarjayam ||43||

44tatrāhamadbhutān bhāvānapaśyaṃ girisattame
|
kṣetraṃ ca tapasāṃ śreṣṭhaṃ paśyāmyadbhutamuttamam ||44||

45divyaṃ vaiyāghrapadyasya upamanyormahātmanaḥ
|
pūjitaṃ devagandharvairbrahmyā lakṣmyā samāvṛtam ||45||

46dhava-kakubha-kadaṃba-nārikelaiḥ kuravaka-ketaka-jaṃbu-pāṭalābhiḥ
|
vaṭa-varuṇaka-vatsanābha-bilvaiḥ sarala-kapittha-priyāla-sāla-tālaiḥ ||46||

47badarī-kuṃda-punnāgairaśokāmrātimuktakaiḥ
|
madhūkaiḥ kovidāraiśca caṃpakaiḥ panasaistathā ||47||

48vanyairbahuvidhairvṛkṣaiḥ phalapuṣpapradairyutam
|
puṣpagulmalatākīrṇaṃ kadalīṣaṃḍaśo'bhitam ||48||

49nānāśakunisaṃbhojyaiḥ phalairvṛkṣairalaṃkṛtam
|
yathāsthānavinikṣiptairbhūṣitaṃ bhasmarāśibhiḥ ||49||

50ruru-vānara-śārdūla-siṃha-dvīpi-samākulam
|
kuraṅga-barhiṇākīrṇaṃ mārjāra-bhujagāvṛtam
|
pūgaiśca mṛgajātīnāṃ mahiṣarkṣaniṣevitam ||50||

51sakṛtprabhinnaiśca gajairvibhūṣitaṃ prahṛṣṭanānāvidhapakṣisevitam
|
supuṣpitairambudharaprakāśairmahīruhāṇāṃ ca vanairvicitraiḥ ||51||

52nānāpuṣparajomiśro gajadānādhivāsitaḥ
|
divyastrīgītabahulo māruto'bhimukho vavau ||52||

53dhārāninādairvihagapraṇādaiḥ śubhaistathā bṛṃhitaiḥ kuñjarāṇām
|
gītaistathā kinnarāṇāmudāraiḥ śubhaiḥ svanaiḥ sāmagānāṃ ca vīra ||53||

54acintyaṃ manasā'pyanyaiḥ sarobhiḥ samalaṃkṛtam
|
viśālaiścāgniśaraṇairbhūṣitaṃ kusumāvṛtaiḥ ||54||

55vibhūṣitaṃ puṇyapavitratoyayā sadā ca juṣṭaṃ nṛpa jahnukanyayā
|
vibhūṣitaṃ dharmabhṛtāṃ variṣṭhairmahātmabhirvahnisamānakalpaiḥ ||55||

56vāyvāhārairaṃbupairjapyanityaiḥ saṃprakṣālairyogibhirdhyānanityaiḥ
|
dhūmaprāśairūṣmapaiḥ kṣīrapaiśca saṃjuṣṭaṃ ca brāhmaṇendraiḥ samantāt ||56||

57gocāriṇo'thāśmakuṭṭā dantolūkhalikāstathā
|
marīcipāḥ phenapāśca tathaiva mṛgacāriṇaḥ ||57||

58aśvatthaphalabhakṣāśca tathā hyuda‌kaśāyinaḥ
|
cīracarmāmbaradharāstathā valkaladhāriṇaḥ ||58||

59suduḥkhānniyamāṃstāṃstānvahataḥ sutapodhanān
|
paśyan munīnbahuvidhān praveṣṭumupacakrame ||59||

60supūjitaṃ devagaṇairmahātmabhiḥ śivādibhirbhārata puṇyakarmabhiḥ
|
rarāja taccāśramamaṇḍalaṃ sadā divīva rājan śaśimaṇḍalaṃ yathā ||60||

61krīḍanti sarpairnakulā mṛgairvyāghrāśca mitravat
|
prabhāvāddīptatapasāṃ sannikarṣānmahātmanām ||61||

62tatrāśramapade śreṣṭhe sarvabhūtamanorame
|
sevite dvijaśārdūlairvedavedāṅgapāragaiḥ ||62||

63nānāniyamavikhyātairṛṣibhiḥ sumahātmabhiḥ
|
praviśanneva cāpaśyaṃ jaṭācīradharaṃ prabhum ||63||

64tejasā tapasā caiva dīpyamānaṃ yathā'nalam
|
śiṣyairanugataṃ śāntaṃ yuvānaṃ brāhmaṇarṣabham ||64||

65śirasā vanda‌mānaṃ māmupamanyurabhāṣata ||65||

66svāgataṃ puṃḍarīkākṣa saphalāni tapāṃsi naḥ
|
yaḥ pūjyaḥ pūjayasi māṃ draṣṭavyo draṣṭumicchasi ||66||

67tamahaṃ prāñjalirbhūtvā mṛgapakṣiṣvathāgniṣu
|
dharme ca śiṣyavarge ca samapṛcchamanāmayam ||67||

68tato māṃ bhagavānāha sāmnā paramavalgunā
|
lapsyase tanayaṃ kṛṣṇa ātmatulyamasaṃśayam ||68||

69tapaḥ sumahadāsthāya toṣayeśānamīśvaram
|
iha devaḥ sapatnīkaḥ samākrīḍatyadhokṣaja ||69||

70ihainaṃ daivataśreṣṭhaṃ devāḥ sarṣigaṇāḥ purā
|
tapasā brahmacaryeṇa satyena ca damena ca ||70||

71toṣayitvā śubhānkāmān prāptavanto janārdana
|
tejasāṃ tapasāṃ caiva nidhiḥ sa bhagavāniha ||71||

72śubhāśubhānvitān bhāvānvisṛjan saṃkṣipannapi
|
āste devyā sadācintyo yaṃ prārthayasi śatruhan ||72||

73hiraṇyakaśipuryo'bhūddānavo marukampanaḥ
|
tena sarvāmaraiśvaryaṃ śarvātprāptaṃ samārbudam ||73||

74tasyaiva putrapravaro maṃdāro nāma viśrutaḥ
|
mahādevavarācchakraṃ varṣārbudamayodhayat ||74||

75viṣṇoścakraṃ ca taddhoraṃ vajramākhaṇḍalasya ca
|
śīrṇaṃ purā'bhavattāta grahasyāṅgeṣu keśava ||75||

76yattadbhagavatā pūrvaṃ dattaṃ cakraṃ tavānagha
|
jalāntaracaraṃ hatvā daityaṃ ca balagarvitam ||76||

77utpāditaṃ vṛṣāṅkena dīptajvalanasannibham
|
dattaṃ bhagavatā tubhyaṃ durdharṣaṃ tejasā'dbhutam ||77||

78na śakyaṃ draṣṭumanyena varjayitvā pinākinam
|
sudarśanaṃ bhavatyevaṃ bhavenoktaṃ tadā tu tat ||78||

79sudarśanaṃ tadā tasya loke nāma pratiṣṭhitam
|
tajjīrṇamabhavattāta grahasyāṅgeṣu keśava ||79||

80grahasyātibalasyāṅge varadattasya dhīmataḥ
|
na śastrāṇi vahantyaṅge cakravajraśatānyapi ||80||

81ardhamānāśca vibudhā graheṇa subalīyasā
|
śivadattavarān jaghnurasurendrān surā bhṛśam ||81||

82tuṣṭo vidyutprabhasyāpi trilokeśvaratāṃ dadau
|
śataṃ varṣasahasrāṇāṃ sarvalokeśvaro'bhavat ||82||

83mamaivānucaro nityaṃ bhavitāsīti cābravīt
|
tathā putrasahasrāṇāmayutaṃ ca dadau prabhuḥ ||83||

84kuśadvīpaṃ ca sa dadau rājyena bhagavānajaḥ
|
tathā śatamukho nāma dhātrā sṛṣṭo mahāsuraḥ ||84||

85yena varṣaśataṃ sāgramātmamāṃsairhuto'nalaḥ
|
taṃ prāha bhagavāṃstuṣṭaḥ kiṃ karomīti śaṅkaraḥ ||85||

86taṃ vai śatamukhaḥ prāha yogo bhavatu me'dbhutaḥ
|
balaṃ ca daivataśreṣṭha śāśvataṃ saṃprayaccha me ||86||

87tatheti bhagavānāha tasya tadvacanaṃ prabhuḥ
|
svāyaṃbhuvaḥ kratuścāpi putrārthamabhavatpurā ||87||

88āviśya yogenātmānaṃ trīṇi varṣaśatānyapi
|
tasya copadadau putrānsahasraṃ kratusaṃmitān ||88||

89yogeśvaraṃ devagatiṃ vettha kṛṣṇa na saṃśayaḥ
|
yājñavalkya iti khyāta ṛṣiḥ paramadhārmikaḥ ||89||

90ārādhya sa mahādevaṃ prāptavānatulaṃ yaśaḥ
|
vedavyāsaśca yogātmā parāśarasuto muniḥ ||90||

91so'pi śaṅkaramārādhya prāptavānatulaṃ yaśaḥ
|
vālakhilyā maghavatā hyavajñātāḥ purā kila ||91||

92taiḥ kruddhairbhagavān rudrastapasā toṣito hyabhūt
|
tāṃścāpi daivataśreṣṭhaḥ prāha prīto jagatpatiḥ ||92||

93suparṇaṃ somahartāraṃ tapasotpādayiṣyatha
|
mahādevasya roṣācca āpo naṣṭāḥ purā'bhavan ||93||

94tāśca saptakapālena devairanyāḥ pravartitāḥ
|
tataḥ pānīyamabhavatprasanne tryaṃbake bhuvi ||94||

95atrerbhāryā'pi bhartāraṃ santyajya brahmavādinī
|
nāhaṃ tasya munerbhūyo vaśagā syāṃ kathañcana ||95||

96ityuktvā sā mahādevamagacchaccharaṇaṃ kila
|
nirāhārā bhayādatrestrīṇi varṣaśatānyapi ||96||

97aśeta musaleṣveva prasādārthaṃ bhavasya sā
|
tāmabravīddhasandevo bhavitā vai sutastava ||97||

98vinā bhartrā ca rudreṇa bhaviṣyati na saṃśayaḥ
|
vaṃśe tavaiva nāmnā tu khyātiṃ yāsyati cepsitām ||98||

99vikarṇaśca mahādevaṃ tathā bhaktasukhāvaham
|
prasādya bhagavān siddhiṃ prāptavānmadhusūdana ||99||

100śākalyaḥ saṃśitātmā vai navavarṣaśatānyapi
|
ārādhayāmāsa bhavaṃ manoyajñena keśava ||100||

101taṃ cāha bhagavāṃstuṣṭo granthakāro bhaviṣyasi
|
vatsākṣayā ca te kīrtistrailokye vai bhaviṣyati ||101||

102akṣayaṃ ca kulaṃ te'stu maharṣibhiralaṅkṛtam
|
bhaviṣyati dvijaśreṣṭhaḥ sūtrakartā sutastava ||102||

103sāvarṇiścāpi vikhyāta ṛṣirāsītkṛte yuge
|
iha tena tapastaptaṃ ṣaṣṭivarṣaśatānyatha ||103||

104tamāha bhagavān rudraḥ sākṣāttuṣṭo'smi te'nagha
|
granthakṛllokavikhyāto bhavitā'syajarāmaraḥ ||104||

105śakreṇa tu purā devo vārāṇasyāṃ janārdana
|
ārādhito'bhūdbhaktena digvāsā bhasmaguṇṭhitaḥ ||105||

106ārādhya sa mahādevaṃ devarājamavāptavān
|
nāradena tu bhaktyā'sau bhava ārādhitaḥ purā ||106||

107tasya tuṣṭo mahādevo jagau devagururguruḥ
|
tejasā tapasā kīrtyā tvatsamo na bhaviṣyati ||107||

108gītena vāditavyena nityaṃ māmanuyāsyasi
|
mayā'pi ca yathā dṛṣṭo devadevaḥ purā vibho ||108||

109sākṣātpaśupatistāta taccāpi śṛṇu mādhava
|
yadarthaṃ ca mayā devaḥ prayatena tathā vibho ||109||

110prabodhito mahātejāstaṃ cāpi śṛṇu vistaram
|
yadavāptaṃ ca me pūrvaṃ devadevānmaheśvarāt ||110||

111tatsarvaṃ nikhilenādya kathayiṣyāmi te'nagha
|
purā kṛtayuge tāta ṛṣirāsīnmahāyaśāḥ ||111||

112vyāghrapāda iti khyāto vedavedāṅgapāragaḥ
|
tasyāhamabhavaṃ putro dhaumyaścāpi mamānujaḥ ||112||

113kasyacittvatha kālasya dhaumyena saha mādhava
|
āgacchamāśramaṃ krīḍanmunīnāṃ bhāvitātmanām ||113||

114tatrāpi ca mayā dṛṣṭā duhyamānā payasvinī
|
lakṣitaṃ ca mayā kṣīraṃ svāduto hyamṛtopamam ||114||

115tato'hamabruvaṃ bālyājjananīmātmanastathā
|
kṣīrodanasamāyuktaṃ bhojanaṃ hi prayaccha me ||115||

116abhāvāccaiva dugdhasya duḥkhitā jananī tadā
|
tataḥ piṣṭaṃ samāloḍya toyena saha mādhava ||116||

117āvayoḥ kṣīramityeva pānārthaṃ samupānayat
|
atha gavyaṃ payastāta kadācitprāśitaṃ mayā ||117||

118pitrā'haṃ yajñakāle hi nīto jñātikulaṃ mahat
|
tatra sā kṣarate devī divyā gauḥ suranandinī ||118||

119tasyāhaṃ tatpayaḥ pītvā rasena hyamṛtopamam
|
jñātvā kṣīraguṇāṃścaiva upalabhya hi saṃbhavam ||119||

120sa ca piṣṭarasastāta na me prītimupāvahat
|
tato'hamabruvaṃ bālyājjananīmātmanastadā ||120||

121nedaṃ kṣīrodanaṃ mātaryattvaṃ me dattavatyasi
|
tato māmabravīnmātā duḥkhaśokasamanvitā ||121||

122putrasnehātpariṣvajya mūrdhni cāghrāya mādhava
|
kutaḥ kṣīrodanaṃ vatsa munīnāṃ bhāvitātmanām ||122||

123vane nivasatāṃ nityaṃ kandamūlaphalāśinām
|
āsthitānāṃ nadīṃ divyāṃ vālakhilyairniṣevitām ||123||

124kutaḥ kṣīraṃ vanasthānāṃ munīnāṃ girivāsinām
|
pāvanānāṃ vanāśānāṃ vanāśramanivāsinām ||124||

dine'ṣṭame tu vipreṇa dīkṣito'haṃ yathāvidhi ||379||

380daṇḍī muṇḍī kuśī cīrī ghṛtākto mekhalī kṛtaḥ
|
māsamekaṃ phalāhāro dvitīyaṃ salilāśanaḥ ||380||

381tṛtīyaṃ ca caturthaṃ ca pañcamaṃ cānilāśanaḥ
|
ekapādena tiṣṭhaṃśca ūrdhvabāhuratandritaḥ ||381||

382tejaḥ sūryasahasrasya apaśyaṃ divi bhārata
|
tasya madhyagataṃ cāpi tejasaḥ pāṇḍunandana ||382||

383indrāyudhapinaddhāṅgaṃ vidyunmālāgavākṣakam
|
nīlaśailacayaprakhyaṃ balākābhūṣitāmbaram ||383||

384tatra sthitaśca bhagavān devyā saha mahādyutiḥ
|
tapasā tejasā kāntyā dīptayā saha bhāryayā ||384||

385rarāja bhagavāṃstatra devyā saha maheśvaraḥ
|
somena sahitaḥ sūryo yathā meghasthitastathā ||385||

386saṃhṛṣṭaromā kaunteya vismayotphullalocanaḥ
|
apaśyaṃ devasaṃghānāṃ gatimārtiharaṃ haram ||386||

387kirīṭinaṃ gadinaṃ śūlapāṇiṃ vyāghrājinaṃ jaṭilaṃ daṇḍapāṇim
|
pinākinaṃ vajriṇaṃ tīkṣṇadaṃṣṭraṃ śubhāṅgadaṃ vyālayajñopavītam ||387||

388divyāṃ mālāsurasā'nekavarṇāṃ samudvahantaṃ gulphadeśāvalambām
|
candraṃ yathā pariviṣṭaṃ sasandhyaṃ varṣātyaye tadvadapaśyamenam ||388||

389pramathānāṃ gaṇaiścaiva samantātparivāritam
|
śaradīva suduṣprekṣyaṃ pariviṣṭaṃ divākaram ||389||

390ekādaśaśatānyevaṃ rudrāṇāṃ vṛṣavāhanam
|
astuvaṃ niyatātmānaṃ karmabhiḥ śubhakarmiṇam ||390||

391ādityā vasavaḥ sādhyā viśvedevāstathā'śvinau
|
viśvābhistutibhirdevaṃ viśvadevaṃ samastuvam ||391||

392śatakratuśca bhagavān viṣṇuścāditinandanau
|
brahmā rathantaraṃ sāma īrayanti bhavāntike ||392||

393yogīśvarāḥ subahavo yogadaṃ pitaraṃ gurum
|
brahmarṣayaśca sasutāstathā devarṣayaśca vai ||393||

394pṛthivī cāntarikṣaṃ ca nakṣatrāṇi grahāstathā
|
māsārdhamāsā ṛtavo rātriḥ saṃvatsarāḥ kṣaṇāḥ ||394||

395muhūrtāśca nimeṣāśca tathaiva yugaparyayāḥ
|
divyā rājannamasyanti vidyāḥ sattvavidastathā ||395||

396sanatkumāro devāśca itihāsāstathaiva ca
|
marīciraṅgirā atriḥ pulastyaḥ pulahaḥ kratuḥ ||396||

397manavaḥ sapta somaśca atharvā sabṛhaspatiḥ
|
bhṛgurdakṣaḥ kaśyapaśca vasiṣṭhaḥ kāśya eva ca ||397||

398chandāṃsi dīkṣā yajñāśca dakṣiṇāḥ pāvako haviḥ
|
yajñopagāni dravyāṇi mūrtimanti yudhiṣṭhira ||398||

399prajānāṃ pālakāḥ sarve saritaḥ pannagā nagāḥ
|
devānāṃ mātaraḥ sarvā devapatnyaḥ sakanyakāḥ ||399||

400sahasrāṇi munīnāṃ ca ayutānyarbudāni ca
|
namasyanti prabhuṃ śāntaṃ parvatāḥ sāgarā diśaḥ ||400||

401gandharvāpsarasaścaiva gītavāditrakovidāḥ
|
divyatāleṣu gāyantaḥ stuvanti bhavamadbhutam ||401||

402vidyādharā dānavāśca guhyakā rākṣasāstathā
|
sarvāṇi caiva bhūtāni sthāvarāṇi carāṇi ca
|
namasyanti mahārāja vāṅmanaḥkarmabhirvibhum ||402||

403purastāddhiṣṭhitaḥ śarvo mamāsīttridaśeśvaraḥ
|
purastāddhiṣṭhitaṃ dṛṣṭvā mameśānaṃ ca bhārata ||403||

404saprajāpatiśakrāntaṃ jaganmāmabhyudaikṣata
|
īkṣituṃ ca mahādevaṃ na me śaktirabhūttadā ||404||

405tato māmabravīddevaḥ paśya kṛṣṇa vadasva ca
|
tvayā hyārādhitaścāhaṃ śataśo'tha sahasraśaḥ ||405||

406tvatsamo nāsti me kaścittriṣu lokeṣu vai priyaḥ
|
śirasā vandite deve devī prītā hyumā tadā
|
tato'hamabruvaṃ sthāṇuṃ stutaṃ brahmādibhiḥ suraiḥ ||406||

407kṛṣṇa uvāca|
namo'stu te śāśvata sarvayone brahmādhipaṃ tvāmṛṣayo vadanti
|
tapaśca sattvaṃ ca rajastamaśca tvāmeva satyaṃ ca vadanti santaḥ ||407||

408tvaṃ vai brahmā ca rudraśca varuṇo'gnirmanurbhavaḥ
|
dhātā tvaṣṭā vidhātā ca tvaṃ prabhuḥ sarvatomukhaḥ ||408||

409tvatto jātāni bhūtāni sthāvarāṇi carāṇi ca
|
tvayā sṛṣṭamidaṃ kṛtsnaṃ trailokyaṃ sacarācaram ||409||

410yānīndriyāṇīha manaśca kṛtsnaṃ ye vāyavaḥ sapta tathaiva cāgnayaḥ
|
ya devasaṃsthāstavadevatāśca tasmātparaṃ tvāmṛṣayo vadanti ||410||

411vedāśca yajñāḥ somaśca dakṣiṇā pāvako haviḥ
|
yajñopagaṃ ca yatkiñcidbhagavāṃstadasaṃśayam ||411||

412iṣṭaṃ dattamadhītaṃ ca vratāni niyamāśca ye
|
hrīḥ kīrtiḥ śrīrdyutistuṣṭiḥ siddhiścaiva tadarpaṇī ||412||

413kāmaḥ krodho bhayaṃ lobho madaḥ stambho'tha matsaraḥ
|
ādhayo vyādhayaścaiva bhagavaṃstanavastava ||413||

414kṛtirvikāraḥ praṇayaḥ pradhānaṃ bījamavyayam
|
manasaḥ paramā yoniḥ prabhāvaścāpi śāśvataḥ ||414||

415avyaktaḥ pāvano'cintyaḥ sahastrāṃśurhiraṇmayaḥ
|
ādirgaṇānāṃ sarveṣāṃ bhavānvai jīvitāśrayaḥ ||415||

416mahānātmā matirbrahmā viśvaḥ śambhuḥ svayaṃbhuvaḥ
|
buddhiḥ prajñopalabdhiśca saṃvitkhyātirdhṛtiḥ smṛtiḥ ||416||

417paryāyavācakaiḥ śabdairmahānātmā vibhāvyate
|
tvāṃ buddhvā brāhmaṇo vedātpramohaṃ viniyacchati ||417||

418hṛdayaṃ sarvabhūtānāṃ kṣetrajñastvamṛṣistutaḥ
|
sarvataḥ pāṇipādastvaṃ sarvatokṣiśiromukhaḥ ||418||

419sarvataḥ śrutimā~lloke sarvamāvṛtya tiṣṭhasi
|
phalaṃ tvamasi tigmāṃśornimeṣādiṣu karmasu ||419||

420tvaṃ vai prabhārciḥ puruṣaḥ sarvasya hṛdi saṃśritaḥ
|
aṇimā mahimā prāptirīśāno jyotiravyayaḥ ||420||

421tvayi buddhirmatirlokāḥ prapannāḥ saṃśritāśca ye
|
dhyānino nityayogāśca satyasattvā jitendriyāḥ ||421||

422yastvāṃ dhruvaṃ vedayate guhāśayaṃ prabhuṃ purāṇaṃ puruṣaṃ ca vigraham
|
hiraṇmayaṃ buddhimatāṃ parāṃ gatiṃ sa buddhimān buddhimatītya tiṣṭhati ||422||

423viditvā sapta sūkṣmāṇi ṣaḍaṅgaṃ tvāṃ ca mūrtitaḥ
|
pradhānavidhiyogasthastvāmeva viśate budhaḥ ||423||

424evamukte mayā pārtha bhave cārtivināśane
|
carācaraṃ jagatsarvaṃ siṃhanādaṃ tadā'karot ||424||

425taṃ viprasaṃghāśca surāsurāśca nāgāḥ piśācāḥ pitaro vayāṃsi
|
rakṣogaṇā bhūtagaṇāśca sarve maharṣayaścaiva tadā praṇemuḥ ||425||

426mama mūrdhni ca divyānāṃ kusumānāṃ sugandhinām
|
rāśayo nipatanti sma vāyuśca susukho vavau ||426||

427nirīkṣya bhagavān devīṃ hyumāṃ māṃ ca jagaddhitaḥ
|
śatakratuṃ cābhivīkṣya svayaṃ māmāha śaṅkaraḥ ||427||

428viduḥ kṛṣṇa parāṃ bhaktimasmāsu tava śatruhan
|
kriyatāmātmanaḥ śreyaḥ prītirhi tvāya me parā ||428||

429vṛṇīṣvāṣṭau varān kṛṣṇa dātā'smi tava sattama
|
brūhi yādavaśārdūla yānicchasi sudurlabhān ||429||
