Mahābhārata 3.140 (Āraṇyakaparvan)

1yudhiṣṭhira uvāca|
antarhitāni bhūtāni balavanti mahānti ca
|
agninā tapasā caiva śakyaṃ gantuṃ vṛkodara||1||

2saṃnivartaya kaunteya śrutpipāse balāśrayāt
|
tato balaṃ ca dākṣyaṃ ca saṃśrayasva vṛkodara||2||

3ṛṣestvayā śrutaṃ vākyaṃ kailāsaṃ parvataṃ prati
|
buddhyā prapaśya kaunteya kathaṃ kṛṣṇā gamiṣyati||3||

4athavā sahadevena dhaumyena ca samaṃ vibho
|
sūtaiḥ paurogavaiścaiva sarvaiśca paricārakaiḥ||4||

5rathairaśvaiśca ye cānye viprāḥ kleśāsahāḥ pathi
|
sarvaistvaṃ sahito bhīma nivartasvāyatekṣaṇa||5||

6trayo vayaṃ gamiṣyāmo laghvāhārā yatavratāḥ
|
ahaṃ ca nakulaścaiva lomaśaśca mahātapāḥ||6||

7mamāgamanamākāṅkṣangaṅgādvāre samāhitaḥ
|
vaseha draupadīṃ rakṣanyāvadāgamanaṃ mama||7||

8bhīma uvāca|
rājaputrī śrameṇārtā duḥkhārtā caiva bhārata
|
vrajatyeva hi kalyāṇī śvetavāhadidṛkṣayā||8||

9tava cāpyaratistīvrā vartate tamapaśyataḥ
|
guḍākeśaṃ mahātmānaṃ saṃgrāmeṣvapalāyinam||9||

10kiṃ punaḥ sahadevaṃ ca māṃ ca kṛṣṇāṃ ca bhārata
|
dvijāḥ kāmaṃ nivartantāṃ sarve ca paricārakāḥ||10||

11sūtāḥ paurogavāścaiva yaṃ ca manyeta no bhavān
|
na hyahaṃ hātumicchāmi bhavantamiha karhicit||11||

12śaile'sminrākṣasākīrṇe durgeṣu viṣameṣu ca
|
iyaṃ cāpi mahābhāgā rājaputrī pativratā||12||

13tvāmṛte puruṣavyāghra notsahedvinivartitum
|
tathaiva sahadevo'yaṃ satataṃ tvāmanuvrataḥ||13||

14na jātu vinivarteta manojño hyahamasya vai
|
api cātra mahārāja savyasācididṛkṣayā||14||

15sarve lālasabhūtāḥ sma tasmādyāsyāmahe saha
|
yadyaśakyo rathairgantuṃ śailo'yaṃ bahukaṃdaraḥ||15||

16padbhireva gamiṣyāmo mā rājanvimanā bhava
|
ahaṃ vahiṣye pāñcālīṃ yatra yatra na śakṣyati||16||

17iti me vartate buddhirmā rājānvimanā bhava
|
sukumārau tathā vīrau mādrīnandikarāvubhau
|
durge saṃtārayiṣyāmi yatrāśaktau bhaviṣyataḥ||17||

18yudhiṣṭhira uvāca|
evaṃ te bhāṣamāṇasya balaṃ bhīmābhivardhatām
|
yattvamutsahase voḍhuṃ pāñcālīṃ ca yaśasvinīm||18||

19yamajau cāpi bhadraṃ te naitadanyatra vidyate
|
balaṃ tava yaśaścaiva dharmaḥ kīrtiśca vardhatām||19||

20yattvamutsahase netuṃ bhrātarau saha kṛṣṇayā
|
mā te glānirmahābāho mā ca te'stu parābhavaḥ||20||

21vaiśaṃpāyana uvāca|
tataḥ kṛṣṇābravīdvākyaṃ prahasantī manoramā
|
gamiṣyāmi na saṃtāpaḥ kāryo māṃ prati bhārata||21||

22lomaśa uvāca|
tapasā śakyate gantuṃ parvataṃ gandhamādanam
|
tapasā caiva kaunteya sarve yokṣyāmahe vayam||22||

23nakulaḥ sahadevaśca bhīmasenaśca pārthiva
|
ahaṃ ca tvaṃ ca kaunteya drakṣyāmaḥ śvetavāhanam||23||

24vaiśaṃpāyana uvāca|
evaṃ saṃbhāṣamāṇāste subāhuviṣayaṃ mahat
|
dadṛśurmuditā rājanprabhūtagajavājimat||24||

25kirātataṅgaṇākīrṇaṃ pulindaśatasaṃkulam
|
himavatyamarairjuṣṭaṃ bahvāścaryasamākulam
|
subāhuścāpi tāndṛṣṭvā pūjayā pratyagṛhṇata||25||

26viṣayānte kulindānāmīśvaraḥ prītipūrvakam
|
tataste pūjitāstena sarva eva sukhoṣitāḥ||26||

27pratasthurvimale sūrye himavantaṃ giriṃ prati
|
indrasenamukhāṃścāpi bhṛtyānpaurogavāṃstathā||27||

28sūdāṃśra pāribarhāṃśca draupadyāḥ sarvaśo nṛpa
|
rājñaḥ kulindādhipateḥ paridāya mahārathāḥ||28||

29padbhireva mahāvīryā yayuḥ kauravanandanāḥ
|
te śanaiḥ prādravansarve kṛṣṇayā saha pāṇḍavāḥ
|
tasmāddeśātsusaṃhṛṣṭā draṣṭukāmā dhanaṃjayam||29||
