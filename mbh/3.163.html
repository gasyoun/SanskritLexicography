Mahābhārata 3.163 (Āraṇyakaparvan)

1vaiśampāyana uvāca|
tataḥ sūryodaye dhaumyaḥ kṛtvā''hnikamarindama
|
ārṣṭiṣeṇena sahitaḥ pāṇḍavānabhyavartata||1||

2te'bhivādyārṣṭiṣeṇasya pādau dhaumyasya caiva ha
|
tataḥ prāñjalayaḥ sarve brāhmaṇāṃstānapūjayan||2||

3tato yudhiṣṭhiraṃ dhaumyo gṛhītvā dakṣiṇe kare
|
prācīṃ diśamabhiprekṣya maharṣiridamabravīt||3||

4asau sāgaraparyantāṃ bhūmimāvṛtya tiṣṭhati
|
śailarājo mahārāja mandaro'tivirājate||4||

5indravaiśravaṇāvetāṃ diśaṃ pāṇḍava rakṣataḥ
|
parvataiśca vanāntaiśca kānanaiścaiva śobhitām||5||

6etadāhurmahendrasya rājño vaiśravaṇasya ca
|
ṛṣayaḥ sarvadharmajñāḥ sadma tāta manīṣiṇaḥ||6||

7ataścodyantamādityamupatiṣṭhanti vai prajāḥ
|
ṛṣayaścāpi dharmajñāḥ siddhāḥ sādhyāśca devatāḥ||7||

8yamastu rājā dharmajñaḥ sarvaprāṇabhṛtāṃ prabhuḥ
|
pretasattvagatiṃ hyenāṃ dakṣiṇāmāśrito diśam||8||

9etat saṃyamanaṃ puṇyamatīvādbhutadarśanam
|
pretarājasya bhavanamṛddhyā paramayā yutam||9||

10taṃ prāpya savitā rājan satyena pratitiṣṭhati
|
astaṃ parvatarājānametamāhurmanīṣiṇaḥ||10||

11etaṃ parvatarājānaṃ samudraṃ ca mahodadhim
|
āvasan varuṇo rājā bhūtāni parirakṣati||11||

12udīcīṃ dīpayanneṣa diśaṃ tiṣṭhati vīryavān
|
mahāmerurmahābhāga śivo brahmavidāṃ gatiḥ||12||

13yasmin brahmasadaścaiva bhūtātmā cāvatiṣṭhate
|
prajāpatiḥ sṛjan sarvaṃ yatkiñcijjaṅgamāgamam||13||

14yānāhurbrahmaṇaḥ putrān mānasān dakṣasaptamān
|
teṣāmapi mahāmeruḥ śivaṃ sthānamanāmayam||14||

15atraiva pratitiṣṭhanti punarevodayanti ca
|
sapta devarṣayastāta vasiṣṭhapramukhāstadā||15||

16deśaṃ virajasaṃ paśya meroḥ śikharamuttamam
|
yatrātmatṛptairadhyāste devaiḥ saha pitāmahaḥ||16||

17yamāhuḥ sarvabhūtānāṃ prakṛteḥ prakṛtiṃ dhruvam
|
anādinidhanaṃ devaṃ prabhuṃ nārāyaṇaṃ param||17||

18brahmaṇaḥ sadanāttasya paraṃ sthānaṃ prakāśate
|
devā'pi yaṃ na paśyanti sarvatejomayaṃ śubham||18||

19atyarkānaladīptaṃ tat sthānaṃ viṣṇormahātmanaḥ
|
svayaiva prabhayā rājan duṣprekṣyaṃ devadānavaiḥ||19||

20prācyāṃ nārāyaṇasthānaṃ merāvativirājate
|
yatra bhūteśvarastāta sarvaprakṛtirātmabhūḥ||20||

21bhāsayan sarvabhūtāni suśriyā'bhivirājate
|
nātra brahmarṣayastāta kuta eva maharṣayaḥ||21||

22prāpnuvanti gatiṃ hyetāṃ yatīnāṃ kurusattama
|
na taṃ jyotīṃṣi sarvāṇi prāpya bhāsanti pāṇḍava||22||

23svayaṃ prabhuracintyātmā tatra hyativirājate
|
yatayastatra gacchanti bhaktyā nārāyaṇaṃ harim||23||

24pareṇa tapasā yuktā bhāvitāḥ karmabhiḥ śubhaiḥ
|
yogasiddhā mahātmānastamomohavivarjitāḥ||24||

25tatra gatvā punarnemaṃ lokamāyānti bhārata
|
svayambhuvaṃ mahātmānaṃ devadevaṃ sanātanam||25||

26sthānametanmahābhāga dhruvamakṣayamavyayam
|
īśvarasya sadā hyetat praṇamātra yudhiṣṭhira||26||

27enaṃ tvaharaharmeruṃ sūryācandramasau dhruvam
|
pradakṣiṇamupāvṛtya kurutaḥ kurunandana||27||

28jyotīṃṣi cāpyaśeṣeṇa savāṇyanagha sarvataḥ
|
pariyānti mahārāja girirājaṃ pradakṣiṇam||28||

29etaṃ jyotīṃṣi sarvāṇi prakarṣan bhagavānapi
|
kurute vitamaskarmā ādityo'bhipradakṣiṇam||29||

30astaṃ prāpya tataḥ sandhyāmatikramya divākaraḥ
|
udīcīṃ bhajate kāṣṭhāṃ diśameṣa vibhāvasuḥ||30||

31sa merumanuvṛttaḥ sa punargacchati pāṇḍava
|
prāṅmukhaḥ savitā devaḥ sarvabhūtahite rataḥ||31||

32sa māsān vibhajan kāle bahudhā parvasandhiṣu
|
tathaiva bhagavān somo nakṣatraiḥ saha gacchati||32||

33evametaṃ tvatikramya mahāmerumatandritaḥ
|
bhāvayan sarvabhūtāni punargacchati mandaram||33||

34tathā tamisrahā devo mayūkhairbhāvayañjagat
|
mārgametadasambādhamādityaḥ parivartate||34||

35sisṛkṣuḥ śiśirāṇyeva dakṣiṇāṃ bhajate diśam
|
tataḥ sarvāṇi bhūtāni kālo'bhyarcchati śaiśiraḥ||35||

36sthāvarāṇāṃ ca bhūtānāṃ jaṅgamānāṃ ca tejasā
|
tejāṃsi samupādatte nivṛttaḥ sa vibhāvasuḥ||36||

37tataḥ svedaklamau tandrī glāniśca bhajate narān
|
prāṇibhiḥ satataṃ svapno hyabhīkṣṇaṃ ca niṣevyate||37||

38evametadanirdeśyaṃ mārgamāvṛtya bhānumān
|
punaḥ sṛjati varṣāṇi bhagavān bhāvayan prajāḥ||38||

39vṛṣṭimārutasantāpaiḥ sukhaiḥ sthāvarajaṅgamān
|
vardhayan sumahātejāḥ punaḥ pratinivartate||39||

40evameṣa caran pārtha kālacakramatandritaḥ
|
prakarṣan sarvabhūtāni savitā parivartate||40||

41santatā gatiretasya naiṣa tiṣṭhati pāṇḍava
|
ādāyaiva tu bhūtānāṃ tejo visṛjate punaḥ||41||

42vibhajan sarvabhūtānāmāyuḥ karma ca bhārata
|
ahorātraṃ kalāḥ kāṣṭhāḥ sṛjatyeṣa sadā vibhuḥ||42||
