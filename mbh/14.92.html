Mahābhārata 14.92 (Āśvamedhikaparvan)

1janamejaya uvāca|
dharmāgatena tyāgena bhagavansvargamasti cet
|
etanme sarvamācakṣva kuśalo hyasi bhāṣitum||1||

2tasyoñchavṛtteryadvṛttaṃ saktudāne phalaṃ mahat
|
kathitaṃ tu mama brahmaṃstathyametadasaṃśayam||2||

3kathaṃ hi sarvayajñeṣu niścayaḥ paramo'bhavat
|
etadarhasi me vaktuṃ nikhilena dvijarṣabhā||3||

4vaiśampāyana uvāca|
atrāpyudāharantīmamitihāsaṃ purātanam
|
agastyasya mahāyajñe purāvṛttamarindama||4||

5purāgastyo mahātejā dīkṣāṃ dvādaśavārṣikīm
|
praviveśa mahārāja sarvabhūtahite rataḥ||5||

6tatrāgnikalpā hotāra āsansatre mahātmanaḥ
|
mūlāhārā: phalāhārāḥ sāśmakuṭṭā marīcipāḥ||6||

7paripṛṣṭikā vaighasikāḥ prasaṅkhyānāstathaiva ca
|
yatayo bhikṣavaścātra babhūvuḥ paryavasthitāḥ||7||

8sarve pratyakṣadharmāṇo jitakrodhā jitendriyāḥ
|
dame sthitāśca sarve te hiṃsādambhavivarjitāḥ||8||

9vṛtte śuddhe sthitā nityamindriyaiścāpyabādhitāḥ
|
upātiṣṭhanta taṃ yajñaṃ yajantaste maharṣayaḥ||9||

10yathāśaktyā bhagavatā tadannaṃ samupārjitam
|
tasminsatre tu yadvṛttaṃ yadyogyaṃ ca tadābhavat||10||

11tathā hyenaikairmunibhirmahāntaḥ kratavaḥ kṛtāḥ
|
evaṃvidhe tvagarastyasya vartamāne tathādhvare
|
na vavarṣa sahasrākṣastadā bharatasattama||11||

12tataḥ karmāntare rājannagastyasya mahātmanaḥ
|
katheyamabhinivṛttā munīnāṃ bhāvitātmanām||12||

13agastyo yajamāno'sau dadātyannaṃ vimatsaraḥ
|
na ca varṣati parjanyaḥ kathamannaṃ bhaviṣyati||13||

14satraṃ cedaṃ mahadviprā munerdvādaśavārṣikam
|
na varṣiṣyati devaśca varṣāṇyetāni dvādaśa||14||

15etadbhavantaḥ sañcintya maharṣerasya dhīmataḥ
|
agastyasyātitapasaḥ kartumarhantyanugraham||15||

16ityevamukte vacane tato'gastyaḥ pratāpavān||16||

17provāca vākyaṃ sa tadā prasādya śirasā munīn
|
yadi dvādaśavarṣāṇi na varṣiṣyati vāsavaḥ||17||

18cintāyajñaṃ kariṣyāmi vidhireṣa sanātanaḥ
|
yadi dvādaśavarṣāṇi na varṣiṣyati vāsavaḥ||18||

19sparśayajñaṃ kariṣyāmi vidhireṣa sanātanaḥ
|
yadi dvādaśavarṣāṇi na varṣiṣyati vāsavaḥ||19||

20dhyeyātmanā hariṣyāmi yajñānetānyatavrataḥ
|
bījayajño mayāyaṃ vai bahuvarṣasamācitaḥ||20||

21bījairhitaṃ kariṣyāmi nātra vighno bhaviṣyati
|
nedaṃ śakyaṃ vṛthā kartuṃ mama satraṃ kathañcana||21||

22varṣiṣyatīha vā devo na vā varṣaṃ bhaviṣyati
|
athāvābhyarthanāmindro na kariṣyati kāmataḥ||22||

23svayamindro bhaviṣyāmi jīvayiṣyāmi ca prajāḥ
|
yo yadāhārajātaśca sa tathaiva bhaviṣyati||23||

24viśeṣaṃ caiva kartāsmi punaḥ punaratīva hi
|
adyeha svarṇamabhyetu yaccānyadvasu kiñcana||24||

25triṣu lokeṣu yaccāsti tadihāgamyatāṃ svayam
|
divyāścāpsarasāṃ saṅghā gandharvāśca sakinnarāḥ||25||

26viśvāvasuśca ye cānye te'pyupāsantu me makham
|
uttarebhyaḥ kurubhyaśca yatkiñcidvasu vidyate||26||

27sarvaṃ tadiha yajñeṣu svayamevopatiṣṭhatu
|
svargaḥ svargasadaścaiva dharmaśca svayameva tu||27||

28ityukte sarvamevaitadabhavattapasā muneḥ
|
tasya dīptāgnimaha sastvagastyasyātitejasaḥ||28||

29tataste munayo hṛṣṭā dadṛśustapaso balam
|
vismitā vacanaṃ prāhuridaṃ sarve mahārthavat||29||

30ṛṣaya ūcuḥ|
prītāḥ sma tava vākyena na tvicchāmastapovyayam
|
taireva yajñaistuṣṭāḥ sma nyāyenecchāmahe vayam||30||

31yajñaṃ dīkṣāṃ tathā homānyaccānyanmṛgayāmahe
|
nyāyenopārjitāhārāḥ svakarmābhiratā vayam||31||

32vedāṃśca brahmacaryeṇa nyāyataḥ prārthayāmahe
|
nyāyenottarakālaṃ ca gṛhebhyo niḥsṛtā vayam||32||

33dharmadṛṣṭairvidhidvāraistapastapsyāmahe vayam
|
bhavataḥ samyagiṣṭā tu buddhihiṃsāvivarjitā||33||

34etāmahiṃsāṃ yajñeṣu brūyāstvaṃ satataṃ prabho
|
prītāstato bhaviṣyāmo vayaṃ tu dvijasattama||34||

35visarjitāḥ samāptau ca satrādasmādvrajāmahe
|
tathā kathayatāṃ teṣāṃ devarājaḥ purandaraḥ||35||

36vavarṣa sumahātejā dṛṣṭvā tasya tapobalam
|
āsamāpteśca yajñasya tasyāmitaparākramaḥ||36||

37nikāmavarṣī parjanyo babhūva janamejaya
|
prasādayāmāsa ca tamagastyaṃ tridaśeśvaraḥ
|
svayamabhyetya rājarṣe puraskṛtya bṛhaspatim||37||

38tato yajñasamāptau tānvisasarja mahāmunīn
|
agastyaḥ paramaprītaḥ pūjayitvā yathāvidhi||38||

39janamejaya uvāca|
ko'sau nakularūpeṇa śirasā kāñcanena vai
|
prāha mānuṣavadvācametatpṛṣṭo vadasva me||39||

40vaiśampāyana uvāca|
etatpūrvaṃ na pṛṣṭo'haṃ na cāsmābhiḥ prabhāṣitam
|
śrūyatāṃ nakulo yo'sau yathā vāktasya mānuṣī||40||

41śrāddhaṃ saṅkalpayāmāsa jamadagniḥ purā kila
|
homadhenustamāgācca svayameva dudoha tām||41||

42tatpaya: sthāpayāmāsa nave bhāṇḍe dṛḍhe śucau
|
tacca krodhasvarūpeṇa piṭharaṃ dharma āviśat||42||

43jijñāsustamṛṣiśreṣṭhaṃ kiṃ kuryādvipriye kṛte
|
iti sañcintya dharmaḥ sa dharmayāmāsa tatpayaḥ||43||

44tamājñāya muniḥ krodhaṃ naivāsya sa cukopa ha
|
sa tu krodhastato rājanbrāhmaṇī mūrtimāsthitaḥ
|
jite tasmin bhṛguśreṣṭhamabhyabhāṣadamarṣaṇaḥ||44||

45jito'smīti bhṛguśreṣṭha bhṛgavo hyatiroṣaṇāḥ
|
loke mithyā pravādo'yaṃ yattvayāsmi vinirjitaḥ||45||

46vaśe sthito'haṃ tvayyadya kṣamāvati mahātmani
|
bibhemi tapasaḥ sādho prasādaṃ kuru me prabho||46||

47jamadagniruvāca|
sākṣāddṛṣṭo'si me krodha gaccha tvaṃ vigatajvaraḥ
|
na tvayāpakṛtaṃ me'dya na ca me panyurasti vai||47||

48yānsamuddiśya saṅkalpaḥ payaso'sya kṛto mayā
|
pitaraste mahābhāgāstebhyo buddhyasva gamyatām||48||

49ityukto jātasantrāsastatraivāntaradhīyata
|
pitṝṇāmabhiṣaṅgācca nakulatvamupāgataḥ||49||

50sa tānprasādayāmāsa śāpasyānto bhavediti
|
taiścāpyuktaḥ kṣipandharmaṃ śāpasyāntamavāpsyasi||50||

51taiścokto yajñiyāndeśāndharmāraṇyaṃ tathaiva ca
|
jugupsamāno dhāvansa taṃ yajñaṃ samupāsadat||51||

52dharmaputramathākṣipya saktuprasthena tena saḥ
|
muktaḥ śāpāttataḥ krodho dharmo hyāsīdyudhiṣṭhiraḥ||52||

53evametattadā vṛtte yajñe tasya mahātmanaḥ
|
paśyatāṃ cāpi nastatra nakulo'ntarhitastadā||53||
