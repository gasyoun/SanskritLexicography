Mahābhārata 2.74 (Sabhāparvan)

1janamejaya uvāca|
anujñātāṃstānviditvā saratnadhanasañcayān
|
pāṇḍavāndhārtarāṣṭrāṇāṃ kathamāsīnmanastadā||1||

2vaiśampāyana uvāca|
anujñātāṃstānviditvā dhṛtarāṣṭreṇa dhīmatā
|
rājanduḥśāsanaḥ kṣipraṃ jagāma bhrātaraṃ prati||2||

3duryodhanaṃ samāsādya sāmātyaṃ bharatarṣabha
|
duḥkhārto bharataśreṣṭha idaṃ vacanamabravīt||3||

4duḥśāsana uvāca|
duḥkhenaitatsamānītaṃ sthaviro nāśayatyasau
|
śatrusādgamayaddravyaṃ tadbudhyadhvaṃ mahārathāḥ||4||

5atha duryodhanaḥ karṇaḥ śakuniścāpi saubalaḥ
|
mithaḥ saṅgamya sahitāḥ pāṇḍavānpratimāninaḥ||5||

6vaicitravīryaṃ rājānaṃ dhṛtarāṣṭraṃ manīṣiṇam
|
abhigamya tvarāyuktāḥ ślakṣṇaṃ vacanamabruvan||6||

7duryodhana uvāca|
na tvayedaṃ śrutaṃ rājanyajjagāda bṛhaspatiḥ
|
śakrasya nītiṃ pravadanvidvāndevapurohitaḥ||7||

8sarvopāyairnihantavyāḥ śatravaḥ śatrusūdana
|
purā yuddhādbalādvāpi prakurvanti tavāhitam||8||

9te vayaṃ pāṇḍavadhanaiḥ sarvānsampūjya pārthivān
|
yadi tānyodhayiṣyāmaḥ kiṃ vai niḥ parihāsyati||9||

10ahīnāśīviṣānkruddhānnāśāya samupasthitān
|
kṛtvā kaṇṭhe ca pṛṣṭhe ca kaḥ samutsraṣṭumarhati||10||

11āttaśastrā rathagatāḥ kupitāstāta pāṇḍavāḥ
|
niḥśeṣaṃ vaḥ kariṣyanti kruddhā hyāśīviṣā iva||11||

12sannaddho hyarjuno yāti vidhṛtya parameṣudhī
|
gāṇḍīvaṃ muhurādatte niḥśvasaṃśca nirīkṣate||12||

13gadāṃ gurvī samudyamya tvaritaśca vṛkodaraḥ
|
svarathaṃ yojayitvā'śu niryāta iti naḥ śrutam||13||

14nakulaḥ khahgamādāya carma cāpyardhacandravat
|
sahadevaśca rājā ca cakrurākāramiṅgitaiḥ||14||

15te tvāsthāya rathānsarve bahuśastraparicchadān
|
abhighnānto rathavrātānsenāyogāya niryayuḥ||15||

16na kṣaṃsyante tathā'smābhirjātu viprakṛtā hi te
|
draupadyāśca parikleśaṃ kasteṣāṃ kṣantumarhati||16||

17punardivyāma bhadraṃ te vanavāsāya pāṇḍavaiḥ
|
evametānvaśe kartuṃ śakṣyāmaḥ puruṣarṣabha||17||

18te vā dvādaśa varṣāṇi vayaṃ vā dyūtanirjitāḥ
|
praviśema mahāraṇyamajinaiḥ prativāsitāḥ||18||

19trayodaśaṃ sajanena ajñātāḥ prativatsaram
|
jñātāśca punaranyāni vane varṣāṇi dvādaśa||19||

20nivasema vayaṃ te vā tathā dyūtaṃ pravartatām
|
akṣānuptvā aunardyūtamidaṃ kurvantu pāṇḍavāḥ||20||

21etatkṛtyatamaṃ rājannasmākaṃ bharatarṣabha
|
ayaṃ hi śakunirveda savidyāmakṣasaṃpadam||21||

22dṛḍhamūlāvayaṃ rājye mitrāṇi parigṛhya ca
|
sāravadvipulaṃ sainyaṃ satkṛtya ca durāsadam||22||

23te ca trayodaśaṃ varṣaṃ pārayiṣyanti cedvratam
|
jyeṣyāmastānvayaṃ rājanrocatāṃ te parantapa||23||

24dhṛtarāṣṭra uvāca|
tūrṇaṃ pratyānayasvaitānkāmaṃ vyadhvagatānapi
|
āgacchantu punardyūtamidaṃ kurvantu pāṇḍavāḥ||24||

25vaiśampāyana uvāca|
tato droṇaḥ somadatto bāhlīkaścaiva gautamaḥ
|
viduro droṇaputraśca vaiśyāputraśca vīryavān||25||

26bhūriśravāḥ śāntanavo vikarṇaśca mahārathaḥ
|
mā dyūtamityabhāṣanta śamostviti ca sarvaśaḥ||26||

27akāmānāṃ ca sarveṣāṃ suhṛdāmarthadarśinām
|
akarotpāṇḍavāhvānaṃ dhṛtarāṣṭraḥ sutapriyaḥ||27||
