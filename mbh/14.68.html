Mahābhārata 14.68 (Āśvamedhikaparvan)

1vaiśampāyana uvāca|
evamuktastu rājendra keśihā duḥkhamūrchitaḥ
|
tatheti vyājahāroccairhlādayanniva taṃ janam||1||

2vākyenaitena hi tadā taṃ janaṃ puruṣarṣabhaḥ
|
hlādayāmāsa sa vibhurdharmārtaṃ salilairavi||2||

3tataḥ sa prāviśattūrṇaṃ janmaveśma pitustava
|
arcitaṃ puruṣavyāghra sitairmālyairyathāvidhi||3||

4apāṃ kumbhaiḥ supūrṇaiśca vinyastaiḥ sarvatodiśam
|
ghṛtena tindukālātaiḥ sarṣapaiśca mahābhuja||4||

5astraiśca vimalairvyastaiḥ pāvakaiśca samantataḥ
|
vṛddhābhiścāpi rāmābhiḥ paricārārthamāvṛtam||5||

6dakṣaiśca parito dhīra bhiṣagbhiḥ kuśalaistathā
|
dadarśa ca sa tejasvī rakṣonānyapi sarvaśaḥ||6||

7dravyāṇi sthāpitāni sma vidhivatkuśalairjanaiḥ
|
tathāyuktaṃ ca taddṛṣṭvā janmaveśma pitustava||7||

8hṛṣṭo'bhavaddhṛṣīkeśaḥ sādhu sādhviti cābravīt
|
tathā bruvati vārṣṇeye prahṛṣṭavadane tadā||8||

9draupadī tvaritā gatvā vairāṭīṃ vākyamabravīt
|
ayamāyāti te bhadre śvaśuro madhusūdanaḥ||9||

10purāṇarṣiracintyātmā samīpamaparājitaḥ
|
sāpi bāṣpakalāṃ vācaṃ nigṛhāśrūṇi caiva ha||10||

11susaṃvītābhavaddevī devavatkṛṣṇamīyuṣī
|
sā tathā dūyamānena hṛdayena tapasvinī||11||

12dṛṣṭvā govindamāyāntaṃ kṛpaṇaṃ paryadevayat
|
puṇḍarīkākṣa paśyāvāṃ bālena hi vinākṛtau
|
abhimanyuṃ ca māṃ caiva hatau tulyaṃ janārdana||12||

13vārṣṇeya madhuhanvīra śirasā tvāṃ prasādaye
|
droṇaputrāstranirdagdhaṃ jīvayainaṃ mamātmajam||13||

14yadi sma dharmarājñā vā bhīmasenena vā punaḥ
|
tvayā vā puṇḍarīkākṣa vākyamuktamidaṃ bhavet||14||

15ajānatīmiṣīkeyaṃ janitrīṃ hantviti prabho
|
ahameva vinaṣṭā syāṃ naitadevaṅgate bhavet||15||

16garbhasthasyāsya bālasya brahmāstreṇa nipātanam
|
kṛtvā nṛśaṃsaṃ durbuddhirdrauṇiḥ kiṃ phalamaśnute||16||

17sā tvāṃ prasādya śirasā yāce śatrunibarhaṇam
|
prāṇāṃstyakṣyāmi govinda nāyaṃ sañjīvate yadi||17||

18asminhi bahavaḥ sādho ye mamāsanmanorathāḥ
|
te droṇaputreṇa hatāḥ kiṃ nu jīvāmi keśava||18||

19āsīnmama matiḥ kṛṣṇa putrotsaṅgā janārdana
|
abhivādayiṣye hṛṣṭeti tadidaṃ vitathīkṛtam||19||

20capalākṣasya dāyāde mṛte'sminpuruṣarṣabha
|
viphalā me kṛtāḥ kṛṣṇa hṛdi sarve manorathāḥ||20||

21capalākṣaḥ kilātīva priyaste madhusūdana
|
sutaṃ paśya tvamasyainaṃ brahmāstreṇa nipātitam||21||

22kṛtaghno'yaṃ nṛśaṃso'yaṃ yathāsya janakastathā
|
yaḥ pāṇḍavīṃ śriyaṃ tyaktvā gato'dya yamasādanam||22||

23mayā caitatpratijñātaṃ raṇamūrdhani keśava
|
abhimanyau hate vīra tvāmeṣyāmyacirāditi||23||

24tacca nākaravaṃ kṛṣṇa nṛśaṃsāṃ jīvitapriyā
|
idānīṃ māṃ gatāṃ tatra kiṃ nu vakṣyati phālguniḥ||24||
