Mahābhārata 7.93 (Droṇaparvan)

1sañjaya uvāca|
hate sudakṣiṇe rājan vīre caiva śrutāyudhe
|
javenābhyadravan pārthaṃ kupitāḥ sainikāstava ||1||

2abhīṣāhāḥ śūrasenāḥ śibayo'tha vasātayaḥ
|
abhyavarṣaṃstato rājan śaravarṣairdhanañjayam ||2||

3teṣāṃ ṣaṣṭiśatānanyān prāmathnāt pāṇḍavaḥ śaraiḥ
|
te sma bhītāḥ palāyante vyāghrāt kṣudramṛgā iva ||3||

4te nivṛttāḥ punaḥ pārthaṃ sarvataḥ paryavārayan
|
raṇe sapatnānnighnantaṃ jigīṣantaṃ parān yudhi ||4||

5teṣāmāpatatāṃ tūrṇaṃ gāṇḍīvapreṣitaiḥ śaraiḥ
|
śirāṃsi pātayāmāsa bāhūṃścāpi dhanañjayaḥ ||5||

6śirobhiḥ pātitaistatra bhūmirāsīnnirantarā
|
abhracchāyeva caivāsīt dhvāṃkṣagṛdhrabalairyudhi ||6||

7teṣu tūtsādyamāneṣu krodhāmarṣasamanvitau
|
śrutāyuścācyutāyuśca dhanañjayamayudhyatām ||7||

8balinau spardhinau vīrau kulajau bāhuśālinau
|
tāvenaṃ śaravarṣāṇi savyadakṣiṇamasyatām ||8||

9tvarāyuktau mahārāja prārthayānau mahadyaśaḥ
|
arjunasya vadhaprepsū putrārthe tava dhanvinau ||9||

10tāvarjunaṃ sahasreṇa patriṇāṃ nataparvaṇām
|
pūrayāmāsatuḥ kruddhau taṭāgaṃ jaladau yathā ||10||

11śrutāyuśca tataḥ kruddhastomareṇa dhanañjayam
|
ājaghāna rathaśreṣṭhaḥ pītena niśitena ca ||11||

12so'tividdho balavatā śatruṇā śatrukarśanaḥ
|
jagāma paramaṃ mohaṃ mohayan keśavaṃ raṇe ||12||

13etasminneva kāle tu so'cyutāyurmahārathaḥ
|
śūlena bhṛśatīkṣṇena tāḍayāmāsa pāṇḍavam ||13||

14kṣate kṣāraṃ sa hi dadau pāṇḍavasya mahātmanaḥ
|
pārtho'pi bhṛśasaṃviddho dhvajayaṣṭiṃ samāśritaḥ ||14||

15tataḥ sarvasya sainyasya tāvakasya viśāmpate
|
siṃhanādo mahānāsīddhataṃ matvā dhanañjayam ||15||

16kṛṣṇaśca bhṛśasantapto dṛṣṭā pārthaṃ vicetanam
|
āśvāsayatsu hṛdyābhirvāgbhistatra dhanañjayam ||16||

17tatastau rathināṃ śreṣṭhau labdhalakṣau dhanañjayam
|
vāsudevaṃ ca vārṣṇeyaṃ śaravarṣaiḥ samantataḥ ||17||

18sa cakrakūbararathaṃ sāśvadhvajapatākinam
|
adṛśyaṃ cakraturyuddhe tada‌dbhutamivābhavat ||18||

19pratyāśvastastu bībhatsuḥ śanakairiva bhārata
|
pretarājapuraṃ prāpya punaḥ pratyāgato yathā ||19||

20sañchannaṃ śarajālena rathaṃ dṛṣṭvā sakeśavam
|
śatrū cābhimukhau dṛṣṭvā dīpyamānāvivānalau ||20||

21prāduścakre tataḥ pārthaḥ śākramastraṃ mahārathaḥ
|
tasmādāsan sahasrāṇi śarāṇāṃ nataparvaṇām ||21||

22te jaghnustau maheṣvāsau tābhyāṃ muktāṃśca sāyakān
|
vicerurākāśagatāḥ pārthabāṇavidāritāḥ ||22||

23pratihatya śarāṃstūrṇaṃ śaravegena pāṇḍavaḥ
|
pratasthe tatra tatraiva yodhayan vai mahārathān ||23||

24tau ca phālgunabāṇaughairvibāhuśirasau kṛtau
|
vasudhāmanvapadyetāṃ vātanunnāviva drumau ||24||

25śrutāyuṣaśca nidhanaṃ vadhaścaivācyutāyuṣaḥ
|
lokavismāpanamabhūtsamudrasyeva śoṣaṇam ||25||

26tayoḥ padānugān hatvā punaḥ pañcāśataṃ rathān
|
pratyagādbhāratīṃ senāṃ nighnan pārtho varān varān ||26||

27śrutāyuṣaṃ ca nihataṃ prekṣya caivācyutāyuṣam
|
niyatāyuśca saṅkruddho dīrghāyuścaiva bhārata ||27||

28putrau tayornaraśreṣṭhau kaunteyaṃ pratijagmatuḥ
|
kirantau vividhānbāṇānpitṛvyasanakarśitau ||28||

29tāvarjuno muhūrtena śaraiḥ sannataparvabhiḥ
|
praiṣayat paramakruddho yamasya sadanaṃ prati ||29||

30loḍayantamanīkāni dvipaṃ padmasaro yathā
|
nāśaknuvanvārayituṃ pārthaṃ kṣatriyapuṅgavāḥ ||30||

31aṅgāstu gajavāreṇa pāṇḍavaṃ paryavārayan
|
kruddhāḥ sahasraśo rājan śikṣitā hastisādinaḥ ||31||

32duryodhanasamādiṣṭāḥ kuñjaraiḥ parvatopamaiḥ
|
prācyāśca dākṣiṇātyāśca kaliṅgapramukhā nṛpāḥ ||32||

33teṣāmāpatatāṃ śīghraṃ gāṇḍīvapreṣitaiḥ śaraiḥ
|
nicakarta śirāṃsyugro bāhūnapi subhūṣaṇān ||33||

34taiḥ śirobhirmahī kīrṇāṃ bāhubhiśca sahāṅgadaiḥ
|
babhau kanakapāṣāṇā bhujagairiva saṃvṛtā ||34||

35bāhavo viśikhaiśchinnāḥ śirāṃsyunmathitāni ca
|
patamānānyadṛśyanta drumebhya iva pakṣiṇaḥ ||35||

36śaraiḥ sahasraśo viddhā dvipāḥ prasṛtaśoṇitāḥ
|
adṛśyantādrayaḥ kāle gairikāmbusravā iva ||36||

37nihatāḥ śerate smānye bībhatsorniśitaiḥ śaraiḥ
|
gajapṛṣṭhagatā mlecchā nānāvikṛtadarśanāḥ ||37||

38nānāveṣadharā rājan nānāśastraughasaṃvṛtāḥ
|
rudhireṇānuliptāṅgā bhānti citraiḥ śarairhatāḥ ||38||

39śoṇitaṃ nirvamanti sma dvipāḥ pārthaśarāhatāḥ
|
sahasraśaśchinnagātrāḥ sārohāḥ sapadānugāḥ ||39||

40cukruśuśca nipetuśca babhramuścāpare diśaḥ
|
bhṛśaṃ trastāśca bahavaḥ svāneva mamṛdurgajāḥ ||40||

41sāntarāyudhinaścaiva dvipāṃstīkṣṇaviṣopamāḥ
|
vidantyasuramāyāṃ ye sughorā ghoracakṣuṣaḥ ||41||

42yavanāḥ pāradāścaiva śakāśca saha bāhlikaiḥ
|
kākavarṇā durācārāḥ strīlolāḥ kalahapriyāḥ ||42||

43drāviḍāstatra yudhyante mattamātaṅgavikramāḥ
|
goyoniprabhavā mlecchāḥ kālakalpāḥ prahāriṇaḥ ||43||

44dārvātisārā daradāḥ puṇḍrāścaiva sahasraśaḥ
|
te na śakyāḥ sma saṅkhyātuṃ vrātāḥ śatasahasraśaḥ ||44||

45abhyavarṣanta te sarve pāṇḍavaṃ niśitaiḥ śaraiḥ
|
avākiraṃśca te mlecchā nānāyuddhaviśāradāḥ ||45||

46teṣāmapi sasarjāśu śaravṛṣṭiṃ dhanañjayaḥ
|
sṛṣṭistathāvidhāhyāsīcchalabhānāmivāyatiḥ ||46||

47abhracchāyāmiva śaraiḥ sainye kṛtvā dhanañjayaḥ
|
muṇḍārdhamuṇḍāñjaṭilānaśucīñjaṭilānanān ||47||

48mlecchānaśātayatsarvān sametānastratejasā
|
śaraiśca śataśo viddhāste saṅghā giricāriṇaḥ
|
prādravanta raṇe bhītā girigahvaravāsinaḥ ||48||

49gajāśvasādimlecchānāṃ patitānāṃ śitai śaraiḥ
|
bakāḥ kaṅkā vṛkā bhūmāvapiban rudhiraṃ mudā
|
pattyaśvarathanāgaiśca pracchannakṛtasaṅkramām ||49||

50śaravarṣaplavāṃ ghorāṃ keśaśaivalaśādvalām
|
prāvartayannadīmugrāṃ śoṇitaughataraṅgiṇīm ||50||

51chinnāṅgulīkṣudramatsyāṃ yugānte kālasannibhām
|
prākarodgajasambādhāṃ nadīmuttaraśoṇitām ||51||

52dehebhyo rājaputrāṇāṃ nāgāśvarathasādinām
|
yathāsthalaṃ ca nimnaṃ ca na syādvarṣati vāsave ||52||

53tathāsīt pṛthivī sarvāṃ śoṇitena pariplutā
|
ṣaṭsahastrāhnayānvīrān punardaśaśatān varān ||53||

54prāhiṇonmṛtyulokāya kṣatriyān kṣatriyarṣabhaḥ
|
śaraiḥ sahasraśo viddhā vidhivatkalpitā dvipāḥ ||54||

55śerate bhūmimāsādya śailā vajrahatā iva
|
savājirathamātaṅgānnighnan vyacaradarjunaḥ ||55||

56prabhinna iva mātaṅgo mṛdnannalavanaṃ yathā
|
bhūridrumalatāgulmaṃ śuṣkendhanatṛṇolapam ||56||

57nirdahedanalo'raṇyaṃ yathā vāyusamīritaḥ
|
senāraṇyaṃ tava tathā kṛṣṇānilasamīritaḥ ||57||

58śarārciradahat kruddhaḥ pāṇḍavāgnirdhanañjayaḥ
|
śūnyān kurvan rathopasthān mānavaiḥ saṃstaran mahīm ||58||

59prānṛtyadiva sambādhe cāpahasto dhanañjayaḥ
|
vajrakalpaiḥ śarairbhūmiṃ kurvannuttaraśoṇitām ||59||

60prāviśadbhāratīṃ senāṃ saṅkruddho vai dhanañjayaḥ
|
taṃ śrutāyustathāmbaṣṭho vrajamānaṃ nyavārayat ||60||

61tasyārjunaḥ śaraistīkṣṇaiḥ kaṅkapatraparicchadaiḥ
|
nyapātayaddhayān śīghraṃ yatamānasya māriṣa ||61||

62dhanuścāsyāparaiśchittvā śaraiḥ pārtho vicakrame
|
ambaṣṭhastu gadāṃ gṛhya kopaparyākulekṣaṇaḥ ||62||

63āsasāda raṇe pārthaṃ keśavaṃ ca mahāratham
|
tataḥ sampraharan vīro gadāmudyamya bhārata||63||

64rathamāvārya gadayā keśavaṃ samatāḍayat
|
gadayā tāḍitaṃ dṛṣṭvā keśavaṃ paravīrahā ||64||

65arjuno'tha bhṛśaṃ kruddhaḥ sombaṣṭhaṃ prati bhārata
|
tataḥ śarairhemapuṅkhaiḥ sagadaṃ rathināṃ varam ||65||

66chā‌dayāmāsa samare meghaḥ sūryamivoditam
|
athāparaiḥ śaraiścāpi gadāṃ tasya mahātmanaḥ ||66||

67acūrṇayat tadā pārthastada‌dbhutamivābhavat
|
atha tāṃ patitāṃ dṛṣṭvā gṛhyānyāṃ ca mahāgadām ||67||

68arjunaṃ vāsudevaṃ ca punaḥ punaratāḍayat
|
tasyārjunaḥ kṣuraprābhyāṃ sagadāvudyatau bhujau ||68||

69cicchedendradhvajākārau śiraścānyena patriṇā
|
sa papāta hato rājan vasudhāmanunādayan ||69||

70indradhvaja ivotsṛṣṭo yantranirmuktabandhanaḥ
|
rathānīkāvagāḍhaśca vāraṇāśvaśatairvṛtaḥ
|
adṛśyata tadā pārtho ghanaiḥ sūrya ivāvṛtaḥ ||70||
