Mahābhārata 7.117 (Droṇaparvan)

1sañjaya uvāca|
kālyamāneṣu sainyeṣu śaineyena tatastataḥ
|
bhāradvājaḥ śaravrātairmahadbhiḥ samavākirat||1||

2sa saṃprahārastumulo droṇasātvatayorabhūt
|
paśyatāṃ sarvasainyānāṃ balivāsavayoriva||2||

3tato droṇaḥ śineḥ pautraṃ citraiḥ sarvāyasaiḥ śaraiḥ
|
tribhirāśīviṣākārairlalāṭe samavidhyata ||3||

4tairlalāṭārpitairbāṇairyuyudhānastvajihmagaiḥ
|
vyarocata mahārāja triśṛṅga iva parvataḥ ||4||

5tato'sya bāṇānaparānindrāśanisamasvanān
|
bhāradvājo'ntaraprekṣī preṣayāmāsa saṃyuge ||5||

6tāndroṇacāpanirmuktān dāśārhaḥ patataḥ śarān
|
dvābhyāṃ dvābhyāṃ supuṃkhābhyāṃ ciccheda paramāstravit||6||

7tāmasya laghutāṃ droṇaḥ samavekṣya viśāṃpate
|
prahasya sahasā'vidhyattriṃśatā śinipuṃgavam ||7||

8punaḥ pañcāśateṣūṇāṃ śitena ca samārpayat
|
laghutāṃ yuyudhānasya lāghavena viśeṣayan ||8||

9samutpatanti valmīkādyathā kruddhā mahoragāḥ
|
tathā droṇarathādrājannāpatanti tanucchidaḥ ||9||

10tathaiva yuyudhānena sṛṣṭāḥ śatasahasraśaḥ
|
avākiran droṇarathaṃ śarā rudhirabhojanāḥ||10||

11lāghavāddvijamukhyasya sātvatasya ca māriṣa
|
viśeṣaṃ nādhyagacchāma samāvāstāṃ nararṣabhau||11||

12sātyakistu tato droṇaṃ navabhirnataparvabhiḥ
|
ājaghāna bhṛśaṃ kruddho dhvajaṃ ca niśitaiḥ śaraiḥ||12||

13sārathiṃ ca śatenaiva bhāradvājasya paśyataḥ
|
lāghavaṃ yuyudhānasya dṛṣṭvā droṇo mahārathaḥ||13||

14saptatyā sārathiṃ viddhvā turaṅgāṃśca tribhistribhiḥ
|
dhvajamekena ciccheda mādhavasya rathe sthitam||14||

15athāpareṇa bhallena hemapuṅgena patriṇā
|
dhanuściccheda samare mādhavasya mahātmanaḥ||15||

16sātyakistu tataḥ kruddho dhanustyaktvā mahārathaḥ
|
gadāṃ jagrāha mahatīṃ bhāradvājāya cākṣipat ||16||

17tāmāpatantīṃ saha‌sā paṭṭabaddhāmayasmayīm
|
nyavārayaccharairdroṇo bahubhirbahurūpibhiḥ||17||

18athānyaddhanurādāya sātyakiḥ satyavikramaḥ
|
vivyādha bahubhirvīraṃ bhāradvājaṃ śilāśitaiḥ||18||

19sa viddhvā samare droṇaṃ siṃhanādamamuñcata
|
taṃ vai na mamṛṣe droṇaḥ sarvaśastrabhṛtāṃ varaḥ ||19||

20tataḥ śaktiṃ gṛhītvā tu rukmadaṇḍāmayasmayīm
|
tarasā preṣayāmāsa mādhavasya rathaṃ prati ||20||

21anāsādya tu śaineyaṃ sā śaktiḥ kālasannibhā
|
bhittvā rathaṃ jagāmogrā dharaṇīṃ dāruṇasvanā ||21||

22tato droṇaṃ śineḥ pautro rājanvivyādha patriṇā
|
dakṣiṇaṃ bhujamāsādya pīḍayan bharatarṣabha ||22||

23droṇo'pi samare rājan mādhavasya mahaddhanuḥ
|
ardhacandreṇa ciccheda rathaśaktyā ca sārathim||23||

24mumoha sārathistasya rathaśaktyā samāhataḥ
|
sa rathopasthamāsādya muhūrtaṃ saṃnyaṣīdata ||24||

25cakāra sātyakī rājan sūtakarmātimānuṣam
|
ayodhayacca yaddroṇaṃ raśmīñjagrāha ca svayam ||25||

26tataḥ śaraśatenaiva yuyudhāno mahārathaḥ
|
avidhyadbrāhmaṇaṃ saṃkhye hṛṣṭarūpo viśāṃpate ||26||

27tasya droṇaḥ śarān pañca preṣayāmāsa bhārata
|
te ghorāḥ kavacaṃ bhittvā papuḥ śoṇitamāhave ||27||

28nirviddhastu śarairghorairakruddhyat sātyakirbhṛśam
|
sāyakān vyasṛjaccāpi vīro rukmarathaṃ prati||28||

29tato droṇasya yantāraṃ nipātyaikeṣuṇā bhuvi
|
aśvān vyadrāvayadbāṇairhatasūtāṃstatastataḥ||29||

30sa rathaḥ pradrutaḥ saṃkhye maṇḍalāni sahasraśaḥ
|
cakāra rājato rājan bhrājamāna ivāṃśumān||30||

31abhidravata gṛhṇīta hayān droṇasya dhāvata
|
iti sma cukruśuḥ sarve rājaputrāḥ sarājakāḥ ||31||

32te sātyakimapāsyāśu rājan yudhi mahārathāḥ
|
yato droṇastataḥ sarve sahasā samupādravan ||32||

33tān‌ dṛṣṭvā pradrutānsaṃkhye sātvatena śarārditān
|
prabhagnaṃ punarevāsīttava sainyaṃ samākulam ||33||

34vyūhasyaiva punardvāraṃ gatvā droṇo vyavasthitaḥ
|
vātāyamānaistairaśvairnīto vṛṣṇiśarārditaḥ ||34||

35pāṇḍupāñcālasaṃbhinnaṃ vyūhamālokya vīryavān
|
śaineye nākarodyatnaṃ vyūhamevābhyarakṣata ||35||

36nivārya pāṇḍupañcālān droṇāgniḥ pradahanniva
|
tasthau krodhedhmasaṃdīptaḥ kālasūrya ivodyataḥ ||36||
