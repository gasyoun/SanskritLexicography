Mahābhārata 13.135 (Anuśāsanaparvan)

1yudhiṣṭhira uvāca|
ke bhojyā brāhmaṇasyeha ke bhojyāḥ kṣatriyasya ha
|
tathā vaiśyasya ke bhojyāḥ ke śūdrasya ca bhārata ||1||

2bhīṣma uvāca|
brāhmaṇā brāhmaṇasyeha bhojyā ye caiva kṣatriyāḥ
|
vaiśyāścāpi tathā bhojyāḥ śūdrāśca parivarjitāḥ ||2||

3brāhmaṇāḥ kṣatriyā vaiśyā bhojyā vai kṣatriyasya ha
|
varjanīyāstu vai śūdrāḥ sarvabhakṣā vikarmiṇaḥ ||3||

4vaiśyāstu bhojyā viprāṇāṃ kṣatriyāṇāṃ tathaiva ca
|
nityāgnayo viviktāśca cāturmāsyaratāśca ye ||4||

5śūdrāṇāmatha yo bhuṃkte sa bhuṃkte pṛthivīmalam
|
malaṃ nṛṇāṃ sa pibati malaṃ bhuṃkte janasya ca ||5||

6śūdrāṇāṃ yastathā bhuṃkte sa bhuṃkte pṛthivīmalam
|
pṛthivīmalamaśnanti ye dvijāḥ śūdrabhojinaḥ ||6||

7śūdrasya karmaniṣṭhāyāṃ vikarmastho'pi pacyate
|
brāhmaṇaḥ kṣatriyo vaiśyo vikarmasthaśca pacyate ||7||

8svādhyāyaniratā viprāstathā svastyayane nṛṇām
|
rakṣaṇe kṣatriyaṃ prāhurvaiśyaṃ puṣṭyarthameva ca ||8||

9karoti karma yadvaiśyastadgatvā hyapajīvati
|
kṛṣigorakṣyavāṇijyamakutsā vaiśyakarmaṇi ||9||

10śūdrakarma tu yaḥ kuryādavahāya svakarma ca
|
sa viśeyo yathā śūdro na ca bhojyaḥ kadācana ||10||

11cikitsakaḥ kāṇḍapṛṣṭhaḥ purādhyakṣaḥ purohitaḥ
|
sāṃvatsaro vṛthādhyāyī sarve te śūdrasaṃmitāḥ ||11||

12śūdrakarmasvathaiteṣu yo bhuṃkte nirapatrapaḥ
|
abhojyabhojanaṃ bhuktvā bhayaṃ prāpnoti dāruṇam ||12||

13kulaṃ vīryaṃ ca tejaśca tiryagyonitvameva ca
|
sa prayāti yathā śvā vai niṣkriyo dharmavarjitaḥ ||13||

14bhuṃkte cikitsakasyānnaṃ tadannaṃ ca purīṣavat
|
puṃścalyannaṃ ca mūtraṃ syātkārukānnaṃ ca śoṇitam ||14||

15vidyopajīvino'nnaṃ ca yo bhuṃkte sādhusaṃmataḥ
|
tadapyannaṃ yathā śaudraṃ tatsādhuḥ parivarjayet ||15||

16vacanīyasya yo bhuṃkte tamāhuḥ śoṇitaṃ hradam
|
piśunaṃ bhojanaṃ bhuṃkte brahmahatyāsamaṃ viduḥ ||16||

17asatkṛtamavajñātaṃ na bhoktavyaṃ kadācana ||17||

18vyādhiṃ kulakṣayaṃ caiva kṣipraṃ prāpnoti brāhmaṇaḥ
|
nagarīrakṣiṇo bhuṃkte śvapacapravaṇo bhavet ||18||

19goghne ca brāhmaṇaghne ca surāpe gurutalpage
|
bhuktvānnaṃ jāyate vipro rakṣasāṃ kulavardhanaḥ ||19||

20nyāsāpahāriṇo bhuktvā kṛtaghne klībavartini
|
jāyate śabarāvāse madhyadeśabahiṣkṛte ||20||

21abhojyāścaiva bhojyāśca mayā proktā yathāvidhi
|
kimanyadadya kaunteya mattastvaṃ śrotumicchasi ||21||
