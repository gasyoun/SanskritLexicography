Mahābhārata 7.44 (Droṇaparvan)

1sañjaya uvāca|
saindhavena niruddheṣu jayagṛddhiṣu pāṇḍuṣu
|
sughoramabhavadyuddhaṃ tvadīyānāṃ paraiḥ saha||1||

2praviśyāthārjuniḥ senāṃ satyasandho durāsadaḥ
|
vyakṣobhayata tejasvī makaraḥ sāgaraṃ yathā||2||

3ta tathā śaravarṣeṇa kṣobhayantamarindamam
|
yathā pradhānāḥ saubhadramabhyayū rathasattamāḥ||3||

4teṣāṃ tasya ca saṃmardo dāruṇaḥ samapadyata
|
sṛjatāṃ śaravarṣāṇi prasaktamamitaujasām||4||

5rathavrajena saṃruddhastairamitraistathārjuniḥ
|
vṛṣasenasya yantāraṃ hatvā ciccheda kārmukam||5||

6tasya vivyādha balavān śarairaśvānajihmagaiḥ
|
vātāyamānairatha tairaśvairapahṛto raṇāt||6||

7tenāntareṇābhimanyoryantāpāsārayadratham
|
rathavrajāstato hṛṣṭāḥ sādhu sādhviti cukruśuḥ||7||

8taṃ siṃhamiva saṃkruddhaṃ pramathnantaṃ śarairarīn
|
ārādāyāntamabhyetya vasātīyo'bhyayāddrutam||8||

9so'bhimanyuṃ śaraiḥ ṣaṣṭyā rukmapuṃkhairavākirat
|
abravīcca na me jīvan jīvato yudhi mokṣyase||9||

10tamayasmayavarmāṇamiṣuṇā dūrapātinā
|
vivyādha hṛdi saubhadraḥ sa papāta vyasuḥ kṣitau||10||

11vasātīyaṃ hataṃ dṛṣṭvā kruddhāḥ kṣatriyapuṅgavāḥ
|
parivavrustadā rājaṃstava pautraṃ jighāṃsavaḥ||11||

12visphārayantaścāpāni nānārūpāṇyanekaśaḥ
|
tadyuddhamabhavadraudraṃ saubhadrasyāribhiḥ saha||12||

13teṣāṃ śarān seṣvasanān śarīrāṇi śirāṃsi ca
|
sakuṇḍalāni sragvīṇi kuddhaściccheda phālguniḥ||13||

14sakhaḍgāḥ sāṅgulitrāṇāḥ sapaṭṭiśaparaśvadhāḥ
|
apaśyanta bhujāśchinnā hemābharaṇabhūṣitāḥ||14||

15stragbhirābharaṇairvastraiḥ pātitaiśca mahābhujaiḥ
|
varmabhiścarmabhirhārairmukuṭaiśchatracāmaraiḥ||15||

16upaskarairadhiṣṭhānairīṣādaṇḍakabandhuraiḥ
|
akṣairvimathitaiścakrairbhagnaiśca bahudhā yugaiḥ||16||

17anukarṣaiḥ patākābhistathā sārathivājibhiḥ
|
rathaiśca bhagnairnāgaiśca hataiḥ kīrṇā'bhavanmahī||17||

18nihataiḥ kṣatriyaiḥ śūrairnānājanapadeśvaraiḥ
|
jayagṛddhairvṛtā bhūmirdāruṇā samapadyata||18||

19diśo vicaratastasya sarvāśca pradiśastathā
|
raṇe'bhimanyoḥ kruddhasya rūpamantaradhīyata||19||

20kāñcanaṃ yadyadasyāsīddharma cābharaṇāni ca
|
dhanuṣaśca śarāṇāṃ ca tadapaśyāma kevalam||20||

21taṃ tadā nāśakat kaściccakṣurbhyāmabhivīkṣitum
|
ādadānaṃ śarairyodhān madhye sūryamiva sthitam||21||
