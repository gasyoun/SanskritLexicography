Mahābhārata 4.70 (Virāṭaparvan)

1vaiśampāyana uvāca| tatastṛtīye divase bhrātaraḥ pañca pāṇḍavāḥ
|
snātāḥ śuklāmbaradharāḥ samaye caritavratāḥ ||1||

2yudhiṣṭhiraṃ puraskṛtya sarvābharaṇabhūṣitāḥ
|
dvāri mattā yathā nāgā bhrājamānā mahārathāḥ ||2||

3virāṭasya sabhāṃ gatvā bhūmipālāsaneṣvatha
|
niṣeduḥ pāvakaprakhyāḥ sarve dhiṣṇyeṣvivāgnayaḥ ||3||

4teṣu tatropaviṣṭeṣu birāṭaḥ pṛthivīpatiḥ
|
ājagāma sabhāṃ kartuṃ rājakāryāṇi sarvaśaḥ ||4||

5śrīmataḥ pāṇḍavān dṛṣṭvā jvalataḥ pāvakāniva
|
muhūrtamiva ca dhyātvā saroṣaḥ pṛthivīpatiḥ ||5||

6atha matsyo'bravīt kaṅkaṃ devarūpamiva sthitam
|
marudgaṇairupāsīnaṃ tridaśānāmiveśvaram ||6||

7sa kilākṣātivāpastvaṃ sabhāstāro mayā vṛtaḥ
|
atha rājāsane kasmādupaviṣṭastvalaṅkṛtaḥ ||7||

8vaiśampāyana uvāca|parihāsepsayā vākyaṃ virāṭasya niśamya tat
|
smayamāno'rjuno rājannidaṃ vacanamabravīt ||8||

9arjuna uvāca|indrasyārdhāsanaṃ rājannayamāroḍhumarhati
|
brahmaṇyaḥ śrutavāṃstyāgī yajñaśīlo dṛḍhavrataḥ ||9||

10eṣa vigrahavān dharma eṣa vīryavatāṃ varaḥ
|
eṣa buddhyā'dhiko loke tapasāṃ ca parāyaṇam ||10||

11eṣo'straṃ vividhaṃ veātta trailokye sacarācare
|
na caivānyaḥ pumān vetti na vetsyati kadācana ||11||

12na devā nāsurāḥ kecinna manuṣyā na rākṣasāḥ
|
gandharvayakṣapravarāḥ sakiṃnaramahoragāḥ ||12||

13dīrghadarśī mahātejāḥ paurajānapada‌priyaḥ
|
pāṇḍavānāmatiratho yajñadharmaparo vaśī ||13||

14maharṣikalpo rājarṣiḥ sarvalokeṣu viśrutaḥ
|
balavān dhṛtimān dakṣaḥ satyavādī jitendriyaḥ
|
dhanaiśca sañcayaiścaiva śakravaiśravaṇopamaḥ ||14||

15yathā manurmahātejā lokānāṃ parirakṣitā
|
evameṣa mahātejāḥ prajānugrahakārakaḥ ||15||

16ayaṃ kurūṇāmṛṣabho dharmarājo yudhiṣṭhiraḥ
|
asya kīrtiḥ sthitā loke sūryasyevodyataḥ prabhā ||16||

17saṃsaranti diśaḥ sarvā yaśaso'sya ivāṃśavaḥ
|
uditasyeva sūryasya tejaso'nu gabhastayaḥ ||17||

18enaṃ daśasahasrāṇi kuñjarāṇāṃ tarasvinām
|
anvayuḥ pṛṣṭhato rājan yāvadadhyāvasat kurūn ||18||

19triṃśadevaṃ sahastrāṇi rathāḥ kāñcanamālinaḥ
|
sadaśvairupasampannāḥ pṛṣṭhato'nuyayustadā ||19||

20enamaṣṭaśatāḥ sūtāḥ sumṛṣṭamaṇikuṇḍalāḥ
|
abruvan māgadhaiḥ sārdhaṃ purā śakramivarṣayaḥ ||20||

21enaṃ nityamupāsanta kuravaḥ kiṅkarā yathā
|
sarve ca rājan rājāno dhaneśvaramivāmarāḥ ||21||

22eṣa sarvān mahīpālān karadān samakārayat
|
vaiśyāniva mahābhāgo vivaśān svavaśānapi ||22||

23aṣṭāśītisahasrāṇi snātakānāṃ mahātmanām
|
upajīvanti rājānamenaṃ sucaritavratam ||23||

24eṣa vṛddhānanāthāṃśca paṅgūnandhāṃśca mānavān
|
putravat pālayāmāsa prajā dharmeṇa vai vibhuḥ ||24||

25eṣa dharme dame caiva krodhe cāpi jitavrataḥ
|
mahāprasādo brahmaṇyaḥ satyavādī ca pārthivaḥ ||25||

26śīghraṃ tāpena caitasya tapyate sa suyodhanaḥ
|
sagaṇaḥ saha karṇena saubalenāpi vā vibhuḥ ||26||

27na śakyante hyasya guṇāḥ prasaṅkhyātuṃ nareśvara
|
eṣa dharmaparo nityamānṛśaṃsyaśca pāṇḍavaḥ ||27||

28evaṃ yukto mahārājaḥ pāṇḍavaḥ pārthivarṣabhaḥ
|
kathaṃ nārhati rājārhamāsanaṃ pṛthivīpate ||28||
