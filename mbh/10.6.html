Mahābhārata 10.6 (Sauptikaparvan)

1dhṛtarāṣṭra uvāca|
dvāradeśe tato drauṇimavasthitamavekṣya tau
|
akurvatāṃ bhojakṛpau kiṃ sañjaya vadasva me||1||

2sañjaya uvāca|
kṛtavarmāṇamāmantrya kṛpaṃ ca sa mahārathaḥ
|
drauṇirmanyuparītātmā śibiradvāramāgamat||2||

3tatra bhūtaṃ mahākāyaṃ candrārkasadṛśadyutim
|
so'paśyaddvāramāśritya tiṣṭhantaṃ lomaharṣaṇam||3||

4vasānaṃ carma vaiyāghraṃ mahārudhiravisravam
|
kṛṣṇājinottarāsaṅgaṃ nāgayajñopavītinam||4||

5bāhubhiḥ svāyateḥ pīnairnānāpraharaṇodyataiḥ
|
baddhāṅgadamahāsarpaṃ jvālāmālākulānanam||5||

6daṃṣṭrākarālavadanaṃ vyāditāsyaṃ bhayānakam
|
nayanānāṃ sahasraiśca vicitrairabhibhūṣitam||6||

7naiva tasya vapuḥ śakyaṃ pravaktuṃ veṣa eva ca
|
sarvathā tu tadālakṣya sphuṭeyurapi parvatāḥ||7||

8tasyāsya nāsikābhyāṃ ca śravaṇābhyāṃ ca sarvaśaḥ
|
tebhyaścākṣisahasrebhyaḥ prādurāsanmahārciṣaḥ||8||

9tathā tejomarīcibhyaḥ śaṅkhacakragadādharāḥ
|
prādurāsan hṛṣīkeśāḥ śataśo'tha sahasraśaḥ||9||

10tadatyadbhutamālokya bhūtaṃ lokabhayaṅkaram
|
drauṇiravyathito divyairasravarṣairavākirat||10||

11drauṇimuktāñcharāṃstāṃstu tadbhūtaṃ mahadagrasat
|
udadheriva vāryodhānpāvako vaḍavāmukhaḥ||11||

12agrasattāṃstathābhūtaṃ drauṇinā prahitān śarān
|
aśvatthāmā tu samprekṣya śaraughāṃstānnirarthakān||12||

13rathaśaktiṃ mumocāsau dīptāmagniśikhāmiva
|
sā tamāhatya dīptāgrā rathaśaktiradīryata||13||

14yugmānte sūryamāhatya maholkeva divaścyutā
|
atha hematsaruṃ divyaṃ khaḍgamākāśavarcasam||14||

15kośātsamudbabarhāśu bilāddīptamivoragam
|
tataḥ khaḍgavaraṃ dhīmān bhūtāya prāhiṇottadā||15||

16sa tadāsādya bhūtaṃ vai bilaṃ nakulavadyayau
|
tataḥ sa kupito drauṇirindraketunibhāṅgadām||16||

17jvalantīṃ prāhiṇottasmai bhūtaṃ tāmapi cāgrasat
|
tataḥ sarvāyudhābhāve vīkṣamāṇastatastataḥ||17||

18apaśyatkṛtamākāśamanākāśaṃ janārdanaiḥ
|
tadadbhutatamaṃ dṛṣṭvā droṇaputro nirāyudhaḥ||18||

19abravīdatisantaptaḥ kṛpavākyamanusmaran
|
bruvatāmapriyaṃ pathyaṃ suhṛdāṃ na śṛṇoti yaḥ||19||

20sa śocatyāpadaṃ prāpya yathā'hamativartyatau
|
śāstradṛṣṭānavidvānyaḥ samatītya jighāṃsati||20||

21sa pathaḥ pracyuto dharmātkupathe pratihanyate
|
gobrāhmaṇanṛpastrīṣu sakhyurmāturgurostathā||21||

22hīnaprāṇajaḍāndheṣu suptabhītotthiteṣu ca
|
mattonmattapramatteṣu na śastrāṇi ca pātayet||22||

23ityevaṃ gurubhiḥ pūrvamupadiṣṭaṃ nṛṇāṃ sadā
|
so'hamutkramya panthānaṃ śāstradṛṣṭaṃ sanātanam||23||

24amārgeṇaivamārabhya ghorāmāpadamāgataḥ
|
tāṃ cāpadaṃ ghoratarāṃ pravadanti manīṣiṇaḥ||24||

25yadudyamya mahatkṛtyaṃ bhayādapi nivartate
|
aśaktaścaiva tatkartuṃ karma śaktibalādiha||25||

26na hi daivādgarīyo vai mānuṣaṃ karma kathyate
|
mānuṣyaṃ kurvataḥ karma yadi daivānna sidhyati||26||

27sapathaḥ pracyuto dharmādvipadaṃ pratipadyate
|
pratijñānaṃ hyavijñānaṃ pravadanti manīṣiṇaḥ||27||

28yadārabhya kriyāṃ kāñcidbhayādiha nivartate
|
tadidaṃ duṣpraṇītena bhayaṃ māṃ samupasthitam||28||

29na hi droṇasutaḥ saṅkhye nivarteta kathañcana
|
idaṃ ca sumahadbhūtaṃ daivadaṇḍamivodyatam||29||

30na caitadabhijānāmi cintayannapi sarvathā
|
dhruvaṃ yeyamadharmaṃ me pravṛttā kaluṣāmatiḥ||30||

31tasyāḥ phalamidaṃ ghoraṃ pratighātāya kalpate
|
tadidaṃ daivavihitaṃ mama saṅkhye nivartanam||31||

32nānyatra daivādudyantamiha śakyaṃ kathañcana
|
so'hamadya mahādevaṃ prapadye śaraṇaṃ vibhum||32||

33daivadaṇḍamimaṃ ghoraṃ sa hi me nāśayiṣyati
|
kapardinaṃ devadevamumāpatimanāmayam||33||

34kapālamālinaṃ rudraṃ bhaganetraharaṃ haram
|
sa hi devotyagāddevāṃstapasā vikrameṇa ca
|
tasmāccharaṇamabhyemi giriśaṃ śūlapāṇinam||34||
