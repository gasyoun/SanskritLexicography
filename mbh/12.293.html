Mahābhārata 12.293 (Śāntiparvan)

1parāśara uvāca |
vṛttiḥ sakāśādvarṇebhyastribhyo hīnasya śobhanā
|
prītyopanītā nirdiṣṭā dharmiṣṭhānkurute sadā ||1||

2vṛttiścennāsti śūdrasya pitṛpaitāmahī dhruvā
|
na vṛttiṃ parato mārgecchuśrūṣāṃ tu prayojayet ||2||

3sadbhistu saha saṃsargaḥ śobhate dharmadarśibhiḥ
|
nityaṃ sarvāsvavasthāsu nāsadbhiriti me matiḥ ||3||

4yathodayagirau dravyaṃ sannikarṣeṇa dīpyate
|
tathā satsannikarṣeṇa hīnavarṇo'pi dīpyate ||4||

5yādṛśena hi varṇena bhāvyate śuklamambaram
|
tādṛśaṃ kurute rupametadevamavehi me ||5||

6tasmādguṇeṣu rajyethā mā doṣeṣu kadācana
|
anityamiha mānāṃ jīvitaṃ hi calācalam ||6||

7sukhe vā yadi vā duḥkhe vartamāno vicakṣaṇaḥ
|
yaścinoti śubhānyeva sa tantrāṇīha paśyati ||7||

8dharmādapetaṃ yatkarma yadyapi syānmahāphalam
|
na tatseveta medhāvī na taddhitamihocyate ||8||

9yo hṛtvā gosahasrāṇi nṛpo dadyādarakṣitā
|
sa śabdamātraphalabhāk rājā bhavati taskaraḥ ||9||

10svayambhūrasṛjaccāgre dhātāraṃ lokasatkṛtam
|
dhātā'sṛjatputramekaṃ lokānāṃ dhāraṇe ratam ||10||

11tamarcayitvā vaiśyastu kuryādatyarthamṛddhimat
|
rakṣitavyaṃ tu rājanyairupayojyaṃ dvijātibhiḥ ||11||

12ajihmairaśaṭhakrodhairhavyakavyaprayoktṛbhiḥ
|
śūdrairnirmārjanaṃ kāryamevaṃ dharmo na naśyati ||12||

13apraṇaṣṭe tato dharme bhavanti sukhitāḥ prajāḥ
|
sukhena tāsāṃ rājendra modante divi devatāḥ ||13||

14tasmādyo rakṣati nṛpaḥ sa dharmeṇeti pūjyate
|
adhīte cāpi yo vipro vaiśyo yaścārjane rataḥ ||14||

15yaśca śuśrūṣate śūdraḥ satataṃ niyatendriyaḥ
|
ato'nyathā manuṣyendra svadharmātparihīyate ||15||

16prāṇasantāpanirdiṣṭāḥ kākiṇyo'pi mahāphalāḥ
|
nyāyenopārjitā dattāḥ kimutānyāḥ sahasraśaḥ ||16||

17satkṛtya hi dvijātibhyo yo dadāti narādhipaḥ
|
yādṛśaṃ tādṛśaṃ nityamaśnāti phalamūrjitam ||17||

18abhigamya ca tattuṣṭyā dattamāhurabhiṣṭutam
|
yācitena tu taddattaṃ tadāhurmadhyamaṃ budhāḥ ||18||

19avajñayā dīyate yattathaivāśraddhayā'pi vā
|
tamāhuradhamaṃ dānaṃ munayaḥ satyavādinaḥ ||19||

20atikrāmenmajjamāno vividhena naraḥ sadā
|
tathā prayatnaṃ kurvīta yathā mucyeta saṃśrayāt ||20||

21damena śobhate vipraḥ kṣatriyo vijayena tu
|
dhanena vaiśyaḥ śūdrastu nityaṃ dākṣyeṇa śobhate ||21||
