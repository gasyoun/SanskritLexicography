Mahābhārata 1.119 (Ādiparvan)

1vaiśaṃpāyana uvāca|
taṃ vyatītamupakramya rājā svamiva bāndhavam
|
sabhāryaḥ śokaduḥkhārtaḥ paryadevayadāturaḥ||1||

2pāṇḍuruvāca|
satāmapi kule jātāḥ karmaṇā bata durgatim
|
prāpnuvantyakṛtātmānaḥ kāmajālavimohitāḥ||2||

3śaśvaddharmātmanā jāto bāla eva pitā mama
|
jīvitāntamanuprāptaḥ kāmātmaiveti naḥ śrutam||3||

4tasya kāmātmanaḥ kṣetre rājñaḥ saṃyatavāgṛṣiḥ
|
kṛṣṇadvaipāyanaḥ sākṣādbhagavānmāmajījanat||4||

5tasyādya vyasane buddhiḥ saṃjāteyaṃ mamādhamā
|
tyaktasya devairanayānmṛgayāṃ paridhāvataḥ||5||

6mokṣameva vyavasyāmi bandho hi vyasanaṃ mahat
|
suvṛttimanuvartiṣye tāmahaṃ pituravyayām||6||

7atīva tapasātmānaṃ yojayiṣyāmyasaṃśayam
|
tasmādekāhamekākī ekaikasminvanaspatau||7||

8caranbhaikṣyaṃ munirmuṇḍaścariṣyāmyāśramānimān
|
pāṃsunā samavacchannaḥ śūnyāgārakṛtālayaḥ||8||

9vṛkṣamūlaniketo vā tyaktasarvapriyāpriyaḥ
|
na śocanna prahṛṣyaṃśca tulyanindātmasaṃstutiḥ||9||

10nirāśīrnirnamaskāro nirdvandvo niṣparigrahaḥ
|
na cāpyavahasankaṃcinna kurvanbhrukuṭīṃ kvacit||10||

11prasannavadano nityaṃ sarvabhūtahite rataḥ
|
jaṅgamājaṅgamaṃ sarvamavihiṃsaṃścaturvidham||11||

12svāsu prajāsviva sadā samaḥ prāṇabhṛtaḥ prati
|
ekakālaṃ caranbhaikṣaṃ kulāni daśa pañca ca||12||

13asaṃbhave vā bhaikṣyasya carannanaśanānyapi
|
alpamalpaṃ ca bhuñjānaḥ pūrvālābhe na jātucit||13||

14anyānyapi cara~llobhādalābhe sapta pūrayan
|
alābhe yadi vā lābhe samadarśī mahātapāḥ||14||

15vāsyaikaṃ takṣato bāhuṃ candanenaikamukṣataḥ
|
nākalyāṇaṃ na kalyāṇaṃ cintayannubhayostayoḥ||15||

16na jijīviṣuvatkiṃcinna mumūrṣuvadācaran
|
jīvitaṃ maraṇaṃ caiva nābhinandanna ca dviṣan||16||

17yāḥ kāścijjīvatā śakyāḥ kartumabhyudayakriyāḥ
|
tāḥ sarvāḥ samatikramya nimeṣādivyavasthitaḥ||17||

18tāsu cāpyanavasthāsu tyaktasarvendriyakriyaḥ
|
saṃparityaktadharmārthaḥ sunirṇiktātmakalmaṣaḥ||18||

19nirmuktaḥ sarvapāpebhyo vyatītaḥ sarvavāgurāḥ
|
na vaśe kasyacittiṣṭhansadharmā mātariśvanaḥ||19||

20etayā satataṃ dhṛttyā carannevaṃ prakārayā
|
dehaṃ saṃsthāpayiṣyāmi nirbhayaṃ mārgamāsthitaḥ||20||

21nāhaṃ sukṛpaṇe mārge svavīryakṣayaśocite
|
svadharmātsatatāpete careyaṃ vīryavarjitaḥ||21||

22satkṛto'satkṛto vā'pi yo'nyāṃ kṛpaṇacakṣuṣā
|
upaiti vṛttiṃ kāmātmā sa śunāṃ vartate pathi||22||

23vaiśaṃpāyana uvāca|
evamuktvā suduḥkhārto niḥśvāsaparamo nṛpaḥ
|
avekṣamāṇaḥ kuntīṃ ca māndrīṃ ca samabhāṣata||23||

24kausalyā viduraḥ kṣattā rājā ca saha bandhubhiḥ
|
āryā satyavatī bhīṣmaste ca rājapurohitāḥ||24||

25brāhmaṇāśca mahātmānaḥ somapāḥ saṃśitavratāḥ
|
pauravṛddhāśca ye tatra nivasantyasmadāśrayāḥ
|
prasādya sarve vaktavyāḥ pāṇḍuḥ pravrājito vane||25||

26niśamya vacanaṃ bharturvanavāse ghṛtātmanaḥ
|
tatsamaṃ vacanaṃ kuntī mādrī ca samabhāṣatām||26||

27anye'pi hyāśramāḥ santi ye śakyā bharatarṣabha
|
āvābhyāṃ dharmapatnībhyāṃ saha taptuṃ tapo mahat||27||

28śarīrasyāpi mokṣāya dharmaṃ prāpya mahāphalam
|
tvameva bhavitā bhartā svargasyāpi na saṃśayaḥ||28||

29praṇidhāyendriyagrāmaṃ bhartṛlokaparāyaṇe
|
tyaktvā kāmasukhe hyāvāṃ tapsyavo vipulaṃ tapaḥ||29||

30yadi cāvāṃ mahāprājña tyakṣyasi tvaṃ viśāṃpate
|
adyaivāvāṃ prahāsyāvo jīvitaṃ nātra saṃśayaḥ||30||

31pāṇḍuruvāca|
yadi vyavasitaṃ hyetadyuvayordharmasaṃhitam
|
svavṛttimanuvartiṣye tāmahaṃ pituravyayām||31||

32tyaktvā grāmyasukhāhāraṃ tapyamāno mahattapaḥ
|
valkalī phalamūlāśī cariṣyāmi mahāvane||32||

33agnau juhvannubhau kālāvubhau kālāvupaspṛśan
|
kṛśaḥ parimitāhāraścīracarmajaṭādharaḥ||33||

34śītavātātapasahaḥ kṣutpipāsānavekṣakaḥ
|
tapasā duścareṇedaṃ śarīramupaśoṣayan||34||

35ekāntaśīlī vimṛśanpakvāpakvena vartayan
|
pitṝndevāṃśca vanyena vāgbhiradbhiśca tarpayan||35||

36vānaprasthajanasyāpi darśanaṃ kulavāsinām
|
nāpriyāṇyācariṣyāmi kiṃ punargrāmavāsinām||36||

37evamāraṇyaśāstrāṇāmugramugrataraṃ vidhim
|
kāṅkṣamāṇo'hamāsthāsye dehasyāsyāsamāpanāt||37||

38vaiśaṃpāyana uvāca|
ityevamuktvā bhārye te rājā kauravanandanaḥ
|
tataścūḍāmaṇiṃ niṣkamaṅgade kuṇḍalāni ca||38||

39vāsāṃsi ca mahārhāṇi strīṇāmābharaṇāni ca
|
pradāya sarvaṃ viprebhyaḥ pāṇḍuḥ punarabhāṣata||39||

40gatvā nāgapuraṃ vācyaṃ pāṇḍuḥ pravrājito vanam
|
arthaṃ kāmaṃ sukhaṃ caiva ratiṃ ca paramātmikām||40||

41pratasthe sarvamutsṛjya sabhāryaḥ kurunandanaḥ
|
tatastasyānuyātāraste caiva paricārakāḥ|| 41||

42śrutvā bharatasiṃhasya vidhidhāḥ karuṇā giraḥ
|
bhīmamārtasvaraṃ kṛtvā hāheti paricukruśuḥ||42||

43uṣṇamaśru vimuñcantastaṃ vihāya mahīpatim
|
yayurnāgapuraṃ tūrṇaṃ sarvamādāya taddhanam||43||

44te gatvā nagaraṃ rājño yathāvṛttaṃ mahātmanaḥ
|
kathayāṃcakrire rājñastaddhanaṃ vividhaṃ daduḥ||44||

45śrutvā tebhyastataḥ sarvaṃ yathāvṛttaṃ mahāvane
|
dhṛtarāṣṭro naraśreṣṭhaḥ pāṇḍumevānvaśocata||45||

46na śayyāsanabhogeṣu ratiṃ vindati karhicit
|
bhrātṛśokasamāviṣṭastamevārthaṃ vicintayan||46||

47rājaputrastu kauravya pāṇḍurmūlaphalāśanaḥ
|
jagāma sahapatnībhyāṃ tato nāgaśataṃ girim||47||

48sa caitrarathamāsādya kālakūṭamatītya ca
|
himavantamatikramya prayayau gandhamādanam||48||

49rakṣyamāṇo mahābhūtaiḥ siddhaiśca paramarṣibhiḥ
|
uvāsa sa mahārāja sameṣu viṣameṣu ca||49||

50indradyumnasaraḥ prāpya haṃsakūṭamatītya ca
|
śataśṛṅge mahārāja tāpasaḥ samatapyata||50||
