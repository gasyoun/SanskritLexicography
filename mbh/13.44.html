Mahābhārata 13.44 (Anuśāsanaparvan)

1yudhiṣṭhira uvāca|
yanmūlaṃ sarvadharmāṇāṃ svajanasya gṛhasya ca
|
pitṛdevātithīnāṃ ca tanme brūhi pitāmaha ||1||

2ayaṃ hi sarvadharmāṇāṃ dharmaścintyatamo mataḥ
|
kīdṛśasya pradeyā syātkanyeti vasudhādhipa||2||

3bhīṣma uvāca|
śīlavṛtte samājñāya vidyāṃ yoniṃ ca karma ca
|
sadbhirevaṃ pradātavyā kanyā guṇayute vare ||3||

4brāhmaṇānāṃ satāmeṣa brāhmo dharmo yudhiṣṭhira
|
āvāhyamāvahedevaṃ yo dadyādanukūlataḥ ||4||

5śiṣṭānāṃ kṣatriyāṇāṃ ca dharma eṣa sanātanaḥ
|
ātmābhipretamutsṛjya kanyābhipreta eva yaḥ ||5||

6abhipretā ca yā yasya tasmai deyā yudhiṣṭhira
|
gāndharvamiti taṃ dharmaṃ prāhurvedavido janāḥ ||6||

7dhanena bahudhā krītvā saṃpralobhya ca bāndhavān
|
asurāṇāṃ nṛpaitaṃ vai dharmamāhurmanīṣiṇaḥ ||7||

8hatvā chittvā ca śīrṣāṇi rudatāṃ rudatīṃ gṛhāt
|
prasahya haraṇaṃ tāta rākṣaso vidhirucyate ||8||

9pañcānāṃ tu trayo dharmyā dvāvadharmyau yudhiṣṭhira
|
paiśācaścāsuraścaiva na kartavyau kathañcana ||9||

10brāhmaḥ kṣātro'tha gāndharva ete dharmyā nararṣabha
|
pṛthagvā yadi vā miśrāḥ kartavyā nātra saṃśayaḥ||10||

11tisro bhāryā brāhmaṇasya dve bhārye kṣatriyasya tu
|
vaiśyaḥ svajātyāṃ vindeta tāsvapatyaṃ samaṃ bhavet||11||

12brāhmaṇī tu bhavejjyeṣṭhā kṣatriyā kṣatriyasya tu
|
ratyarthamapi śūdrā syānnetyāhurapare janāḥ ||12||

13apatyajanma śūdrāyāṃ na praśaṃsanti sādhavaḥ
|
śūdrāyāṃ janayanvipraḥ prāyaścittī vidhīyate ||13||

14triṃśadvarṣo daśavarṣīṃ bhāryāṃ vindeta nagnikām
|
ekaviṃśativarṣo vā saptavarṣāmavāpnuyāt ||14||

15yasyāstu na bhavedbhrātā pitā vā bharatarṣabha
|
nopayaccheta tāṃ jātu putrikādharmiṇī hi sā||15||

16trīṇi varṣāṇyudīkṣeta kanyā ṛtumatī satī
|
caturthe tvatha saṃprāpta svayaṃ bhartāramarjayet ||16||

17prajā na hīyate tasyā ratiśca bharatarṣabha
|
ato'nyathā vartamānā bhavedvācyā prajāpateḥ||17||

18asapiṇḍā ca yā māturasagotrā ca yā pituḥ
|
ityetāmanugaccheta taṃ dharmaṃ manurabravīt ||18||

19yudhiṣṭhira uvāca|
śulkamanyena dattaṃ syāddadānītyāha cāparaḥ
|
balādanyaḥ prabhāṣeta dhanamanyaḥ pradarśayet||19||

20pāṇigrahītā cānyaḥsyātkasya bhāryā pitāmaha
|
tattvaṃ jijñāsamānānāṃ cakṣurbhavatu no bhavān||20||

21bhīṣma uvāca|
yatkiñcitkarma mānuṣyaṃ saṃsthānāya pradṛśyate
|
mantravanmantritaṃ tasya mṛṣāvādaratu pātakaḥ||21||

22bhāryāṃ patyartvigācāryāḥ śiṣyopādhyāya eva ca
|
mṛṣokte daṇḍamarhanti netyāhurapare janāḥ ||22||

23na hyakāmena saṃvāsaṃ manurevaṃ praśaṃsati
|
ayaśasyamadharmyaṃ ca yanmṛṣā dharmakopanam ||23||

24naikānto doṣa ekasmiṃstadā kenopapadyate
|
dharmato yāṃ prayacchanti yāṃ ca krīṇanti bhārata||24||

25bandhubhiḥ samanujñāte mantrahomau prayojayet
|
tathā siddhyanti te mantrā nādattāyāḥ kathañcana||25||

26yastvatra mantrasamayo bhāryāpattyormithaḥ kṛtaḥ
|
tamevāhurgarīyāṃsaṃ yaścāsau jñātibhiḥ kṛtaḥ||26||

27devada‌tāṃ patirbhāryāṃ vetti dharmasya śāsanāt
|
sa daivīṃ mānuṣīṃ vācamanṛtāṃ paryudasyati ||27||

28yudhiṣṭhira uvāca|
kanyāyāṃ prāptaśulkāyāṃ jyāyāṃścedāvrajedvaraḥ
|
dharmakāmārthasaṃpanno vācyamatrānṛtaṃ na vā ||28||

29tasminnubhayato doṣe kurvañchreyaḥ samācaret
|
ayaṃ naḥ sarvadharmāṇāṃ dharmaścintyatamo mataḥ||29||

30tattvaṃ jijñāsamānānāṃ cakṣurbhavatu no bhavān
|
tadetatsarvamācakṣva na hi tṛpyāmi kathyatām ||30||

31bhīṣma uvāca|
naiva niṣṭhākaraṃ śulkaṃ jñātvā''sīttena nāhṛtam
|
na hi śulkaparāḥ santaḥ kanyāṃ dadati karhicit||31||

32anyairguṇairupetaṃ tu śulkaṃ yācanti bāndhavāḥ
|
alaṃkṛtvā vahasveti yo dadyādanukūlataḥ ||32||

33yacca tāṃ ca dadatyevaṃ na śulkaṃ vikrayo na saḥ
|
pratigṛhya bhaveddeyameṣa dharmaḥ sanātanaḥ ||33||

34dāsyāmi bhavate kanyāmiti pūrvaṃ na bhāṣitam
|
ye cāhurye ca nāhurye ye cāvaśyaṃ vadantyuta ||34||

35tasmādāgrahaṇātpāṇeryācayanti parasparam
|
kanyāvaraḥ purā datto marudbhiriti naḥ śrutam||35||

36nāniṣṭāya pradātavyā kanyā ityṛṣicoditam
|
tanmūlaṃ kāmamūlasya prajanasyeti me matiḥ||36||

37samīkṣya ca bahūndoṣānsaṃvāsādviddhi pāṇayoḥ
|
yathā niṣṭhākaraṃ śulkaṃ na jātvāsīttathā śṛṇu||37||

38ahaṃ vicitrarvāryasya dve kanye samudāvaham
|
jitvā ca māgadhānsarvān kārśānatha ca kosalān ||38||

39gṛhītapāṇirekā''sītprāptaśulkā parā'bhavat
|
kanyā gṛhītā tatraiva visarjyā iti me pitā||39||

40abravīditarāṃ kanyāmāvaheti sa kauravaḥ
|
apyanyānanupapraccha śaṃkamānaḥ piturvacaḥ ||40||

41atīva hyasya dharmecchā piturme'bhyadhikābhavat
|
tato'hamabruvaṃ rājannācārepsuridaṃ vacaḥ
|
ācāraṃ tattvato vettumicchāmi ca punaḥ punaḥ||41||

42tato mayaivamukte tu vākye dharmabhṛtāṃ varaḥ
|
pitā mama mahārāja bāhlīko vākyamabravīt||42||

43yadi vaḥ śulkato niṣṭhā na pāṇigrahaṇāttathā
|
lājāntaramupāsīta prāptaśulka iti smṛtiḥ||43||

44na hi dharmavidaḥ prāhuḥ pramāṇaṃ vākyataḥ smṛtam
|
yeṣāṃ vai śulkato niṣṭhā na pāṇigrahaṇāttathā||44||

45prasiddhaṃ bhāṣitaṃ dāne naiṣāṃ pratyāyakaṃ punaḥ
|
ye manyante krayaṃ śulkaṃ na te dharmavido narāḥ||45||

46na caitebhyaḥ pradātavyā na voḍhavyā tathāvidhā
|
na hyeva bhāryā kretavyā na vikrayyā kathañcana||46||

47ye ca krīṇanti dāsīṃ ca vikrīṇanti tathaiva ca
|
bhavetteṣāṃ tathā niṣṭhā lubdhānāṃ pāpacetasām||47||

48asminnarthe satyavantaṃ paryapṛcchanta vai janāḥ
|
kanyāyāḥ prāptaśulkāyāḥ śulkadaḥ praśamaṃ gataḥ||48||

49pāṇigrahītā vā'nyaḥ syādatra no dharmasaṃśayaḥ
|
tannaśchindhi mahāprājña tvaṃ hi vai prājñasaṃmataḥ||49||

50tattvaṃ jijñāsamānānāṃ cakṣurbhavatu no bhavān
|
tānevaṃ bruvataḥ sarvānsatyavānvākyamabravīt||50||

51yatreṣṭaṃ tatra deyā syānnātra kāryā vicāraṇā
|
kurvate jīvato'pyevaṃ mṛte naivāsti saṃśayaḥ||51||

52devaraṃ praviśetkanyā tapyedvā'pi tapaḥ punaḥ
|
tamevānugatā bhūtvā pāṇigrāhasya kāmyayā ||52||

53likhantyeva tu keṣāṃcidapareṣāṃ śanairapi
|
iti ye saṃvadantyatra ta etaṃ niścayaṃ viduḥ||53||

54tatpāṇigrahaṇātpūrvamantaraṃ yatra vartate
|
sarvamaṅgalamantraṃ vai mṛṣā vādastu pātakaḥ ||54||

55pāṇigrahaṇamantrāṇāṃ niṣṭhā syātsaptame pade
|
pāṇigrahasya bhāryā syādyasya cādbhiḥ pradīyate||55||

56iti deyaṃ vadantyatra ta enaṃ niścayaṃ viduḥ
|
anukūlāmanuvaṃśāṃ bhrātrā dattāmupāgnikām
|
parikramya yathānyāyaṃ bhāryāṃ vindeddvijottamaḥ||56||
