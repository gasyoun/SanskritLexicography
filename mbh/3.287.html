Mahābhārata 3.287 (Āraṇyakaparvan)

1mārkaṇḍeya uvāca|
tato niryāya svapurāt kumbhakarṇaḥ sahānugaḥ
|
apaśyat kapisainyaṃ tajjitakāśyagrataḥ sthitam||1||

2sa vīkṣamāṇastat sainyaṃ rāmadarśanakāṅkṣayā
|
apaśyaccāpi saumitriṃ dhanuṣpāṇiṃ vyavasthitam||2||

3tamabhyetyāśu harayaḥ parivavruḥ samantataḥ
|
abhyaghnaṃśca mahākāyairbahubhirjagatīruhaiḥ||3||

4karajairatudaṃścānye vihāya bhayamuttamam
|
bahudhā yudhyamānāste yuddhamārgaiḥ plavaṅgamāḥ||4||

5nānāpraharaṇairbhīmai rākṣasendramatāḍayan
|
sa tāḍyamānaḥ prahasan bhakṣayāmāsa vānarān||5||

6balaṃ caṇḍabalākhyaṃ ca vajrabāhuṃ ca vānaram
|
taddṛṣṭvā vyathanaṃ karma kumbhakarṇasya rakṣasaḥ||6||

7udakrośan paritrastāstāraprabhṛtayastadā
|
tānuccaiḥ krośataḥ sainyān śrutvā sa hariyūthapān||7||

8abhidudrāva sugrīvaḥ kumbhakarṇamapetabhīḥ
|
tato nipatya vegena kumbhakarṇaṃ mahāmanāḥ||8||

9śālena jaghnivān mūrdhni balena kapikuñjaraḥ
|
sa mahātmā mahāvegaḥ kumbhakarṇasya mūrdhani||9||

10bibheda śālaṃ sugrīvo na caivāvyathayat kapiḥ
|
tato vinadya sahasā śālasparśavibodhitaḥ||10||

11dorbhyāmādāya sugrīvaṃ kumbhakarṇo'haradbalāt
|
hriyamāṇaṃ tu sugrīvaṃ kumbhakarṇena rakṣasā||11||

12avekṣyābhyadravadvīraḥ saumitrirmitranandanaḥ
|
so'bhipatya mahāvegaṃ rukmapuṅkhaṃ mahāśaram||12||

13prāhiṇot kumbhakarṇāya lakṣmaṇaḥ paravīrahā
|
sa tasya dehāvaraṇaṃ bhitvā dehaṃ ca sāyakaḥ||13||

14jagāma dārayan bhūmiṃ rudhireṇa samukṣitaḥ
|
tathā sa bhinnahṛdayaḥ samutsṛjya kapīśvaram||14||

15kumbhakarṇo maheṣvāsaḥ pragṛhītaśilāyudhaḥ
|
abhidudrāva saumitrimudyamya mahatīṃ śilām||15||

16tasyābhipatatastūrṇaṃ kṣurābhyāmucchritau karau
|
ciccheda niśitāgrābhyāṃ sa babhūva caturbhujaḥ||16||

17tānapyasya bhujān sarvān pragṛhītaśilāyudhān
|
kṣuraiściccheda ladhvastraṃ saumitriḥ pratidarśayan||17||

18sa babhūvātikāyaśca bahupādaśirobhujaḥ
|
taṃ brahmāstreṇa saumitrirdadārādricayopamam||18||

19sa papāta mahāvīryo divyāstrābhihato raṇe
|
mahāśanivinirdagdhaḥ pādapo'ṅkuravāniva||19||

20taṃ dṛṣṭvā vṛtrasaṅkāśaṃ kumbhakarṇaṃ tarasvinam
|
gatāsuṃ patitaṃ bhūmau rākṣasāḥ prādravan bhayāt||20||

21tathā tān dravato yodhān dṛṣṭvā tau dūṣaṇānujau
|
avasthāpyātha saumitriṃ saṅkruddhāvabhyadhāvatām||21||

22tāvādravantau saṅkruddhau vajravegapramāthinau
|
abhijagrāha saumitrirvinadyobhau patantribhiḥ||22||

23tataḥ sutumulaṃ yuddhamabhavallomaharṣaṇam
|
dūṣaṇānujayoḥ pārtha lakṣmaṇasya ca dhīmataḥ||23||

24mahatā śaravarṣeṇa rākṣasau so'bhyavarṣata
|
tau cāpi vīrau saṅkruddhāvubhau tau samavarṣatām||24||

25muhūrtamevamabhavadvajravegapramāthinoḥ
|
saumitreśca mahābāhoḥ samprahāraḥ sudāruṇaḥ||25||

26athādriśṛṅgamādāya hanumān mārutātmajaḥ
|
abhidrutyādade prāṇān vajravegasya rakṣasaḥ||26||

27nīlaśca mahatā grāvṇā dūṣaṇāvarajaṃ hariḥ
|
pramāthinamabhidrutya pramamātha mahābalaḥ||27||

28tataḥ prāvartata punaḥ saṅgrāmaḥ kaṭukodayaḥ
|
rāmarāvaṇasainyānāmanyonyamabhidhāvatām||28||

29śataśo nairṛtān vanyā jaghnurvanyāṃśca nairṛtāḥ
|
nairṛtāstatra vadhyante prāyeṇa na tu vānarāḥ||29||
