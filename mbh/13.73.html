Mahābhārata 13.73 (Anuśāsanaparvan)

1pitāmaha uvāca|
yo'yaṃ praśnastvayā pṛṣṭo gopradānādikāritaḥ
|
nāsti praṣṭā'sti loke'smiṃstvatto'nyo hi śatakrato ||1||

2santi nānāvidhā lokā yāṃstvaṃ śakra na paśyasi
|
paśyāmi yānahaṃ lokānekapatnyaśca yāḥ striyaḥ ||2||

3karmabhiścāpi suśubhaiḥ suvratā ṛṣayastathā
|
saśarīrā hi tānyānti brāhmaṇāḥ śubhabuddhayaḥ ||3||

4śarīranyāsamokṣeṇa manasā nirmalena ca
|
svapnabhūtāṃśca tā~llokānpaśyantīhāpi suvratāḥ ||4||

5te tu lokāḥ sahastrākṣa śṛṇu yādṛgguṇānvitāḥ
|
na tatra kramate kālo na jarā na ca pāvakaḥ ||5||

6tathā nāstyaśubhaṃ kiñcinna vyādhistatra na klamaḥ
|
yadyacca gāvo manasā tasminvāṃcchanti vāsava ||6||

7tatsarvaṃ prāpnuvanti sma mama pratyakṣadarśanāt
|
kāmagāḥ kāmacāriṇyaḥ kāmātkāmāṃśca bhuñjate ||7||

8vāpyaḥ sarāṃsi sarito vividhāni vanāni ca
|
gṛhāṇi parvatāścaiva yāvaddravyaṃ ca kiñcana ||8||

9manojñaṃ sarvabhūtebhyaḥ sarvatantraṃ pradṛśyata
|
īdṛśādvipulāllokānnāsti lokastathāvidhaḥ ||9||

10tatra sarvasahāḥ kṣāntā vatsalā guruvartinaḥ
|
ahaṅkārairvirahitā yānti śakra narottamāḥ ||10||

11yaḥ sarvamāṃsāni na bhakṣayīta pumānsadā bhāvitā dharmayuktaḥ
|
mātāpitrorarcitā satyayuktaḥ śuśrūṣitā brāhmaṇānāmanindyaḥ ||11||

12akrodhano goṣu tathā dvijeṣu dharme rato guruśuśrūṣakaśca
|
yāvajjīvaṃ satyavṛtte rataśca dāne rato yaḥ kṣamī cāparādhe ||12||

13mṛdurdānto devaparāyaṇaśca sarvātithiścāpi tathā dayāvān
|
īdṛgguṇo mānavastaṃ prayāti lokaṃ gavāṃ śāśvataṃ cāvyayaṃ ca ||13||

14na pāradārī paśyati lokametaṃ na vai gurughno na mṛṣā saṃpralāpī
|
sadā pravādī brāhmaṇeṣvāttavairo doṣairetairyaśca yukto durātmā ||14||

15na mitradhruṅnaikṛtikaḥ kṛtaghnaḥ śaṭho'nṛjurdharmavidveṣakaśca
|
na brahmahā manasāpi prapaśyedgavāṃ lokaṃ puṇyakṛtāṃ nivāsam ||15||

16etatte sarvamākhyātaṃ nipuṇena sureśvara
|
gopradānaratānāṃ tu phalaṃ śṛṇu śatakrato ||16||

17dāyādyalabdhairarthairyo gāḥ krītvā saṃprayacchati
|
dharmārjitāndhanaiḥ krītānsa lokānāpnune'kṣayān ||17||

18yo vai dyūte dhanaṃ jitvā gāḥ krītvā saṃprayacchati
|
sa divyamayutaṃ śakraṃ varṣāṇāṃ phalamaśnute ||18||

19dāyādyādyāḥ sma vai gāvo nyāyapūrvairupārjitāḥ
|
pradadyāttāḥ pradātṝṇāṃ sambhavantyapi ca dhruvāḥ ||19||

20pratigṛhya tu yo dadyādgāḥ saṃśuddhena cetasā
|
tasyāpīhākṣayānlokān dhruvān viddhi śacīpate ||20||

21janmaprabhṛti satyaṃ ca yo brūyānniyatendriyaḥ
|
gurudvijasahaḥ kṣāntastasya gobhiḥ samā gatiḥ ||21||

22na jātu brāhmaṇo vācyo yadavācyaṃ śacīpate
|
manasā goṣu na druhyedgovṛttirgo'nukalpakaḥ ||22||

23satye dharme ca niratastasya śakra phalaṃ śṛṇu
|
gosahasreṇa samitā tasya ghenurbhavatyuta ||23||

24kṣatriyasya guṇairetairapi tulyaphalaṃ śṛṇu
|
tasyāpi dvijatulyā gaurbhavatīti viniścayaḥ ||24||

25vaiśasyaite yadi guṇāstasya pañcaśataṃ bhavet
|
śūdrasyāpi vinītasya caturbhāgaphalaṃ smṛtam ||25||

26etaccainaṃ yo'nutiṣṭheta yuktaḥ satye rato guruśuśrūṣayā ca
|
dakṣaḥ kṣānto devatārthī praśāntaḥ śucirbuddho dharmaśīlo'nahaṃvāk ||26||

27mahatphalaṃ prāpyate sa dvijāya datvā dogdhrīṃ vidhinā'nena dhenum
|
nityaṃ dadyādekabhaktaḥ sadā ca satye sthito guruśuśrūṣitā ca ||27||

28vedādhyāyī goṣu yo bhaktimāṃśca nityaṃ datvā yo'bhinandeta gāśca
|
ājātito yaśca gavāṃ nameta idaṃ phalaṃ śakra nibodha tasya ||28||

29yatsyādiṣṭvā rājasūye phalaṃ tu yatsyādiṣṭvā bahunā kāñcanena
|
etattulyaṃ phalamapyāhuratagryaṃ sarve santastvṛṣayo ye ca siddhāḥ ||29||

30yo'graṃ bhaktaṃ kiñcidaprāśya dadyādgobhyo nityaṃ govratī satyavādī
|
śānto'lubdho gosahastrasya puṇyaṃ saṃvatsareṇāpnuyātsatyaśīlaḥ ||30||

31yadekabhaktamaśnīyāddadyādekaṃ gavāṃ ca yat
|
daśavarṣāṇyanantāni govratī go'nukampakaḥ ||31||

32ekenaiva ca bhaktena yaḥ krītvā gāṃ prayacchati
|
yāvanti tasyā romāṇi saṃbhavanti śatakrato ||32||

33tāvatpradānātsa gavāṃ phalamāpnoti śāśvatam
|
brāhmaṇasya phalaṃ hīdaṃ kṣatriyasya tu vai śṛṇu ||33||

34pañcavārṣikamevaṃ tu kṣatriyasya phalaṃ smṛtam
|
tato'rdhena tu vaiśyasya śūdro vaiśyārdhataḥ smṛtaḥ ||34||

35yaścātmavikrayaṃ kṛtvā gāḥ krītvā saṃprayacchati
|
yāvatsandarśayedgāṃ vai sa tāvatphalamaśnute ||35||

36romṇi romṇi mahābhāga lokāścāsyā'kṣayāḥ smṛtāḥ
|
saṃgrāmeṣvarjayitvā tu yo vai gāḥ samprayacchati
|
ātmavikrayatulyāstāḥ śāśvatā viddhi kauśika ||36||

37abhāve yo gavāṃ dadyāttiladhenuṃ yatavrataḥ
|
durgātsa tārito dhenvā kṣīranadyāṃ pramodate ||37||

38na tvevāsāṃ dānamātraṃ praśastaṃ pātraṃ kālo goviśeṣo vidhiśca
|
kālajñānaṃ vipra gavāntaraṃ hi duḥkhaṃ jñātuṃ pāvakādityabhūtam ||38||

39svādhyāyāḍhyaṃ śuddhayoniṃ praśāntaṃ vaitānasthaṃ pāpabhīruṃ bahujñam
|
goṣu kṣāntaṃ nātitīkṣṇaṃ śaraṇyaṃ vṛttiglānaṃ tādṛśaṃ pātramāhuḥ ||39||

40vṛttiglāne sīdati cātimātraṃ kṛṣyarthe vā homyahetoḥ prasūteḥ
|
gurvarthaṃ vā bālasaṃvṛddhaye vā dhenuṃ dadyāddeśakāle viśiṣṭe ||40||

41antarjñātāḥ sakrayajñānalabdhāḥ prāṇaiḥ krītāstejasā yautakāśca
|
kṛcchrotsṛṣṭāḥ poṣaṇābhyāgatāśca dvārairetairgoviśeṣāḥ praśastāḥ ||41||

42balānvitāḥ śīlavayopapannāḥ sarvāḥ praśaṃsante sugandhavatyaḥ
|
yathā hi gaṅgā saritāṃ variṣṭhā tathārjunīnāṃ kapilā variṣṭhā ||42||

43tisro rātrīstvadbhirupoṣya bhūmau tṛptā gāvastarpitebhyaḥ pradeyāḥ
|
vatsaiḥ puṣṭaiḥ kṣīrapaiḥ supracārāstryahaṃ dattvā gorasairvartitavyam ||43||

44dattvā dhenuṃ suvratāṃ sādhudohāṃ kalyāṇavatsāmapalāyunī ca
|
yāvanti romāṇi bhavanti tasyā stāvanti varṣāṇi bhavantyamutra ||44||

45tathā'naḍvāhaṃ brāhmaṇāya pradāya dhuryaṃ yuvānaṃ balinaṃ vinītam
|
halasya voḍhāramanantavīryaṃ prāpnoti lokān daśadhenudasya ||45||

46kāṃtāre brāhmaṇāngāśca yaḥ paritrāti kauśika
|
kṣaṇena vipramucyeta tasya puṇyaphalaṃ śṛṇu ||46||

47aśvamedhakratostulyaṃ phalaṃ bhavati śāśvatam
|
mṛtyukāle sahastrākṣa yāṃ vṛttimanukāṅkṣate ||47||

48lokānbahuvidhāndivyānyaccāsya hṛdi vartate
|
tatsarvaṃ samavāpnoti karmaṇaitena mānavaḥ ||48||

49gobhiśca samanujñātaḥ sarvatra ca mahīyate
|
yastvetenaiva kalpena gāṃ vaneṣvanugacchati ||49||

50tṛṇagomayaparṇāśī nispṛho niyataḥ śuciḥ
|
akāmaṃ tena vastavyaṃ muditena śatakrato ||50||

51mama loke suraiḥ sārdhaṃ loke yatrāpi cecchati||51||
