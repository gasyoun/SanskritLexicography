Mahābhārata 8.48 (Karṇaparvan)

1dhṛtarāṣṭra uvāca|
yattatpraviśya pārthānāṃ sainyaṃ kurvañjanakṣayam
|
karṇo rājānamabhyetya tanmamācakṣva sañjaya ||1||

2ke ca pravīrāḥ pārthānāṃ yudhi karṇamavārayan
|
kāṃśca pramathyādhirathiryudhiṣṭhiramapīḍayat ||2||

3sañjaya uvāca|
dhṛṣṭadyumnamukhān pārthān dṛṣṭvā karṇo vyavasthitān
|
samabhyadhāvattvaritaḥ pañcālāñchatrukarṣiṇaḥ ||3||

4taṃ tūrṇamabhidhāvantaṃ pañcālā jitakāśinaḥ
|
pratyudyayurmahātmānaṃ haṃsā iva mahārṇavam ||4||

5tataḥ śaṅkhasahasrāṇāṃ niḥsvano hṛdayaṅgamaḥ
|
prādurāsīdubhayato bherīśabdaśca dāruṇaḥ ||5||

6nānābāṇanipātāśca dvipāśva-ratha-niḥsvanaḥ
|
siṃhanādaśca vīrāṇāmabhavaddāruṇastadā ||6||

7sādridrumārṇavā bhūmiḥ savātāmbudamambaram
|
sārkendugrahanakṣatrā dyauśca vyaktaṃ vighūrṇitā ||7||

8iti bhūtāni taṃ śabdaṃ menire te ca vivyathuḥ
|
yāni cāpyalpasattvāni prāyastāni mṛtāni ca ||8||

9atha karṇo bhṛśaṃ kruddhaḥ śīghramastramudīrayan
|
jaghāna pāṇḍavīṃ senāmāsurīṃ maghavāniva ||9||

10sa pāṇḍavabalaṃ karṇaḥ praviśya visṛjañcharān
|
prabhadrakāṇāṃ pravarānahanatsaptasaptatim ||10||

11tataḥ supuṅkhairniśitai rathaśreṣṭho ratheṣubhiḥ
|
avadhītpañcaviṃśatyā pañcālān pañcaviṃśatim ||11||

12suvarṇapuṅkhairnārācaiḥ parakāyavidāraṇaiḥ
|
cedikānavadhīdvīraḥ śataśo'tha sahasraśaḥ ||12||

13taṃ tathā samare karma kurvāṇamatimānuṣam
|
parivavrurmahārāja pañcālānāṃ rathavrajāḥ ||13||

14tataḥ sandhāya viśikhānpañca bhārata duḥsahān
|
pañcālānavadhītpañca karṇo vaikartano vṛṣaḥ ||14||

15bhānudevaṃ citrasenaṃ senābinduṃ ca bhārata
|
tapanaṃ śūrasenaṃ ca pañcālānahanadraṇe ||15||

16pañcāleṣu ca śūreṣu vadhyamāneṣu sāyakaiḥ
|
hāhākāro mahānāsītpañcālānāṃ mahāhave ||16||

17parivavrurmahārāja pañcālānāṃ rathā daśa
|
punareva ca tān karṇo jaghānāśu patatribhiḥ ||17||

18cakrarakṣau tu karṇasya putrau māriṣa durjayau
|
suṣeṇaḥ satyasenaśca tyaktvā prāṇānayudhyatām ||18||

19pṛṣṭhagoptā tu karṇasya jyeṣṭhaḥ putro mahārathaḥ
|
vṛṣasenaḥ svayaṃ karṇaṃ pṛṣṭhataḥ paryapālayat ||19||

20dhṛṣṭadyumnaḥ sātyakiśca draupadeyā vṛkodaraḥ
|
janamejayaḥ śikhaṇḍī ca pravīrāśca prabhadrakāḥ ||20||

21cedikekayapañcālā yamau matsyāśca daṃśitāḥ
|
samabhyadhāvan rādheyaṃ jighāṃsantaḥ prahāriṇam ||21||

22ta enaṃ vividhaiḥ śastraiḥ śaradhārābhireva ca
|
abhyavarṣanvimardantaṃ prāvṛṣīvāmbudā girim ||22||

23pitaraṃ tu parīpsantaḥ karṇaputrāḥ prahāriṇaḥ
|
tvadīyāścāpare rājanvīrā vīrānavārayan ||23||

24suṣeṇo bhīmasenasya cchittvā bhallena kārmukam
|
nārācaiḥ saptabhirviddhvā hṛdi bhīmaṃ nanāda ha ||24||

25athānyaddhanurādāya sudṛḍhaṃ bhīmavikramaḥ
|
sajyaṃ vṛkodaraḥ kṛtvā suṣeṇasyācchinaddhanuḥ ||25||

26vivyādha cainaṃ daśabhiḥ kruddho nṛtyanniveṣubhiḥ
|
karṇaṃ ca tūrṇaṃ vivyādha trisaptatyā śitaiḥ śaraiḥ ||26||

27bhānusenaṃ ca daśabhiḥ sāśvasūtāyudhadhvajam
|
paśyatāṃ suhṛdāṃ madhye karṇaputramapātayat ||27||

28kṣurapraṇunnaṃ tattasya śiraścandranibhānanam
|
śubhadarśanamevāsīnnālabhraṣṭamivāmbujam ||28||

29hatvā karṇasutaṃ bhīmastāvankāpunarārdayat
|
kṛpahārdikyayośchittvā cāpau tāvapyathārdayat ||29||

30duḥśāsanaṃ tribhirviddhvā śakuniṃ ṣaḍbhirāyasaiḥ
|
ulūkaṃ ca patatriṃ ca cakāra virathāvubhau ||30||

31suṣeṇaṃ ca hato'sīti bruvannādatta sāyakam
|
tamasya karṇaściccheda tribhiścainamatāḍayat ||31||

32athānyaṃ parijagrāha suparvāṇaṃ sutejanam
|
suṣeṇāyāsṛjadbhīmastamapyasyācchinadvṛṣaḥ ||32||

33punaḥ karṇastrisaptatyā bhīmasenamatheṣubhiḥ
|
putraṃ parīpsanvivyādha krūraṃ krūrairjighāṃsayā ||33||

34suṣeṇastu dhanurguhya bhārasādhanamuttamam
|
nakulaṃ pañcabhirbāṇairbāhvorurasi cārpayat ||34||

35nakulastaṃ tu viṃśatyā viddhvā bhārasahairdṛḍhaiḥ
|
nanāda balavannādaṃ karṇasya bhayamādadhat ||35||

36taṃ suṣeṇo mahārāja viddhvā daśabhirāśugaiḥ
|
ciccheda ca dhanuḥ śīghraṃ kṣurapreṇa mahārathaḥ ||36||

37athānyaddhanurādāya nakulaḥ krodhamūrcchitaḥ
|
suṣeṇaṃ navabhirbāṇairvārayāmāsa saṃyuge ||37||

38sa tu bāṇairdiśo rājannācchādya paravīrahā
|
ājaghne sārathiṃ cāsya suṣeṇaṃ ca tatastribhiḥ ||38||

39ciccheda cāsya sudṛḍhaṃ dhanurbhallaistribhistridhā
|
athānyaddhanurādāya suṣeṇaḥ krodhamūrcchitaḥ ||39||

40āvidhyakunnalaṃ paṣṭyā sahadevaṃ ca saptabhiḥ
|
tadyuddhaṃ sumahaddhoramāsīddevāsuropamam ||40||

41nighnatāṃ sāyakaistūrṇamanyonyasya vadhaṃ prati
|
sātyakirvṛṣasenasya sūtaṃ hatvā tribhiḥ śaraiḥ ||41||

42dhanuściccheda bhallena jaghānāśvāṃśca saptabhiḥ
|
dhvajamekeṣuṇonmadhya tribhistaṃ hṛdyatāḍayat ||42||

43athāvasannaḥ svarathe muhūrtātpunarutthitaḥ
|
sa raṇe yuyudhānena visūtāśvarathadhvajaḥ ||43||

44kṛto jighāṃsuḥ śaineyaṃ khaḍgacarmadhṛgabhyayāt
|
tasya cāpatataḥ śīghraṃ vṛṣasenasya sātyakiḥ||44||

45vārāhakarṇairdaśabhiravidhyadasicarmaṇī
|
duḥśāsanastu taṃ dṛṣṭvā virathaṃ vyāyudhaṃ kṛtam ||45||

46āropya svarathaṃ tūrṇamapovāha raṇāturam
|
athānyaṃ rathamāsthāya vṛṣaseno mahārathaḥ ||46||

47draupadeyāṃstrisaptatyā yuyudhānaṃ ca pañcabhiḥ
|
bhīmasenaṃ catuḥṣaṣṭyā sahadevaṃ ca pañcabhiḥ||47||

48nakulaṃ triṃśatā bāṇaiḥ śatānīkaṃ ca saptabhiḥ
|
śikhaṇḍinaṃ ca daśabhirdharmarājaṃ śatena ca ||48||

49etāṃścānyāṃśca rājendra pravīrāñjayagṛddhinaḥ
|
abhyardayanmaheṣvāsaḥ karṇaputro viśāṃpate ||49||

50karṇasya yudhi durdharṣastataḥ pṛṣṭhamapālayat
|
duḥśāsanaṃ ca śaineyo navairnavabhirāyasaiḥ ||50||

51visūtāśvarathaṃ kṛtvā lalāṭe tribhirārpayat
|
sa tvanyaṃ rathamāsthāya vidhivatkalpitaṃ punaḥ ||51||

52yuyudhe pāṇḍubhiḥ sārdhaṃ karṇasyāpyāyayanbalam
|
dhṛṣṭadyumnastataḥ karṇamavidhyaddaśabhiḥ śaraiḥ ||52||

53draupadeyāstrisaptatyā yuyudhānastu saptabhiḥ
|
bhīmasenaścatuḥṣaṣṭyā sahadevaśca saptabhiḥ ||53||

54nakulastriṃśatā bāṇaiḥ śatānīkastu saptabhiḥ
|
śikhaṇḍī daśabhirvīro dharmarājaḥ śatena tu ||54||

55ete cānye ca rājendra pravīrā jayagṛddhinaḥ
|
abhyardayanmaheṣvāsaṃ sūtaputraṃ mahāmṛdhe ||55||

56tānsūtaputro viśikhairdaśabhirdaśabhiḥ śaraiḥ
|
rathenānucaranvīraḥ pratyavidhyadarindamaḥ ||56||

57tatrāstravīryaṃ karṇasya lāghavaṃ ca mahātmanaḥ
|
apaśyāma mahābhāga tada‌dbhutamivābhavat ||57||

58na hyādadānaṃ dadṛśuḥ sandadhānaṃ ca sāyakān
|
vimuñcantaṃ ca saṃraṃbhādapaśyanta hatānarīn ||58||

59dyaurviyadbhūrdiśaścaiva prapūrṇāni śitaiḥ śaraiḥ
|
aruṇābhrāvṛtākāraṃ tasmindeśe babhau viyat ||59||

60nṛtyanniva hi rādheyaścāpahastaḥ pratāpavān
|
yairviddhaḥ pratyaviddhyattānekaikaṃ triguṇaiḥ śaraiḥ ||60||

61śataiśca daśabhiścaitān punarviddhvā nanāda ca
|
sāśvasūtarathāśchannāstataste vivaraṃ daduḥ ||61||

62tānpramathya maheṣvāsān rādheyaḥ śaravṛṣṭibhiḥ
|
gajānīkamasambādhaṃ prāviśacchatrukarśanaḥ ||62||

63sa rathāṃstriśataṃ hatvā cedīnāmanivartinām
|
rādheyo niśitairbāṇaistato'bhyārchadyudhiṣṭhiram ||63||

64tataste pāṇḍavā rājacchikhaṃḍī ca sasātyakiḥ
|
rādheyātparirakṣanto rājānaṃ paryavārayan‌ ||64||

65tathaiva tāvakāḥ sarve karṇaṃ durvāraṇaṃ raṇe
|
yattāḥ śūrā maheṣvāsāḥ paryarakṣanta sarvaśaḥ||65||

66nānāvāditraghoṣāśca prādurāsanviśāṃpate
|
siṃhanādaśca sañjajñe śūrāṇāmabhigarjatām ||66||

67tataḥ punaḥ samājagmurabhītāḥ kurupāṇḍavāḥ
|
yudhiṣṭhiramukhāḥ pārthāḥ sūtaputramukhā vayam ||67||
