Mahābhārata 7.23 (Droṇaparvan)

1dhṛtarāṣṭra uvāca|
sarveṣāmeva me brūhi rathacihnāni sañjaya
|
ye droṇamabhyavartanta kruddhā bhīmapurogamāḥ||1||

2sañjaya uvāca|
ṛkṣavarṇairhayairdṛṣṭvā vyāyacchantaṃ vṛkodaram
|
rajatāśvastataḥ śūraḥ śaineyaḥ saṃnyavartata||2||

3sāraṅgāśvo yudhāmanyuḥ svayaṃ pratvarayan hayān
|
paryavartata durdharṣaḥ kruddho droṇarathaṃ prati||3||

4pārāvatasavarṇaistu hemabhāṇḍairmahājavaiḥ
|
pāñcālarājasya suto dhṛṣṭadyumno nyavartata||4||

5pitaraṃ tu pariprepsuḥ kṣatradharmā yatavrataḥ
|
siddhiṃ cāsya parāṃ kāṃkṣan śoṇāśvaḥ saṃnyavartata||5||

6padmapatranibhāṃścāśvān mallikākṣān svalaṃkṛtān
|
śaikhaṇḍiḥ kṣatradevastu svayaṃ pratvarayan yayau||6||

7darśanīyāstu kāmbojāḥ śukapatraparicchadāḥ
|
vahanto nakulaṃ śītraṃ tāvakānabhidudruvuḥ||7||

8kṛṣṇāstu meghasaṅkāśā avaha‌duttamaujasam
|
durdharṣāyābhisandhāya kruddhaṃ yuddhāya bhārata||8||

9tathā tittirikalmāṣā hayā vātasamā jave
|
avahaṃstumule yuddhe sahadevamudāyudham||9||

10dantavarṇāstu rājānaṃ kālavālā yudhiṣṭhiram
|
bhīmavegā naravyāghramavahan vātaraṃhasaḥ||10||

11hemottamapraticchannairhayairvātasamairjave
|
abhyavartanta sainyāni sarvāṇyeva yudhiṣṭhiram||11||

12rājñastvanantaro rājā pāñcālyo drupado'bhavat
|
jātarūpamayacchatraḥ sarvaistairabhirakṣitaḥ||12||

13lalāmairharibhiryuktaḥ sarvaśabdakṣamairyudhi
|
rājñāṃ madhye maheṣvāsaḥ śāntabhīrabhyavartata||13||

14taṃ virāṭo'nvayācchīghraṃ saha sarvairmahārathaiḥ
|
kekayāśca śikhaṇḍī ca dhṛṣṭaketustathaiva ca||14||

15svaiḥsvaiḥ sainyaiḥ parivṛtā matsyarājānamanvayuḥ
|
taṃ tu pāṭalipuṣpāṇāṃ samavarṇā hayottamāḥ||15||

16vahamānā dhyarājanta matsyasyāmitraghātinaḥ
|
hīradrāsamavarṇāstu javanā hemamālinaḥ||16||

17putraṃ virāṭarājasya satvaraṃ samudāvahan
|
indragopakavarṇaiśca bhrātaraḥ pañca kekayāḥ||17||

18jātarūpasamābhāsāḥ sarve lohitakadhvajāḥ
|
te hemamālinaḥ śūrāḥ sarve yuddhaviśāradāḥ||18||

19varṣanta iva jīmūtāḥ pratyadṛśyanta daṃśitāḥ
|
āmapātranikāśāstu pāñcālyamamitaujasam||19||

20dattāstumburuṇā divyāḥ śikhaṇḍinamudāvahan
|
tathā dvādaśa sāhasrāḥ pañcālānāṃ mahārathāḥ||20||

21teṣāṃ tu ṣaṭ sahasrāṇi ye śikhaṇḍinam vayuḥ
|
putraṃ tu śiśupālasya narasiṃhasya māriṣa||21||

22ākrīḍanto vahanti sma sāraṅgaśabalā hayāḥ
|
dhṛṣṭaketustu cedīnāmṛṣabho'tibaloditaḥ||22||

23kāmbojaiḥ śabalairaśvairabhyavartata durjayaḥ
|
bṛhatkṣatraṃ tu kaikeyaṃ sukumāraṃ hayottamāḥ||23||

24palāladhmasaṃkāśāḥ saindhavāḥ śīghramāvahan
|
mallikākṣāḥ padmavarṇā bālhijātāḥ svalaṃkṛtāḥ||24||

25śūraṃ śikhaṇḍinaḥ putramṛkṣadevamudāvahan
|
rukmabhāṇḍapraticchannāḥ kauśeyasadṛśā hayāḥ||25||

26kṣamāvanto'vahan saṃkhye senābindumarindamam
|
yuvānamavahan yuddhe krauñcavarṇā hayottamāḥ||26||

27kāśyasyābhibhuvaḥ putraṃ sukumāraṃ mahāratham
|
śvetāstu prativindhyaṃ taṃ kṛṣṇīgrīvā manojavāḥ
|
yantuḥ preṣyakarā rājan rājaputramudāvahan||27||

28sutasomaṃ tu yaḥ saumyaṃ pārthaḥ putramajījanat
|
māṣapuṣpasavarṇāstamavahan vājino raṇe||28||

29sahasrasomapratimo babhūva pure kurūṇāmudayendunāmni
|
tasmiñjātaḥ somasakrandamadhye yasmāttasmātsutasomo'bhavatsaḥ||29||

30nākuliṃ tu śatānīkaṃ śālapuṣpanibhā hayāḥ
|
ādityataruṇaprakhyāḥ ślāghanīyamudāvahan||30||

31kāñcanāpihitairyoktrairmayūragrīvasannibhāḥ
|
draupadeyaṃ naravyāghraṃ śrutakarmāṇamāhave||31||

32śrutakīrtiṃ śrutanidhiṃ draupadeyaṃ hayottamāḥ
|
ūhuḥ pārthasamaṃ yuddhe cāṣapatranibhā hayāḥ||32||

33yamāhuradhyardhaguṇaṃ kṛṣṇāt pārthācca saṃyuge
|
abhimanyuṃ piśaṃgāstaṃ kumāramavahan raṇe||33||

34ekastu dhārtarāṣṭrebhyaḥ pāṇḍavānyaḥ samāśritaḥ
|
taṃ bṛhanto mahākāyā yuyutsumavahan raṇe||34||

35palālakāṇḍavarṇāstu vārdhakṣemiṃ tarasvinam
|
ūhuḥ sutamule yuddhe hayāḥ kṛṣṇāḥ svalaṃkṛtāḥ||35||

36kumāraṃ śitipādāstu rukmacitrairuracchadaiḥ
|
saucittimavahan yuddhe yantuḥ preṣyakarā hayāḥ||36||

37rukmapīṭhāvakīrṇāstu kauśeyasadṛśā hayāḥ
|
suvarṇamālinaḥ kṣāntāḥ śreṇimantamudāvahan||37||

38rukmamālādharāḥ śūrā hemapṛṣṭhāḥ svalaṅkṛtāḥ
|
kāśirājaṃ naraśreṣṭhaṃ ślāghanīyamudāvahan||38||

39astrāṇāṃ ca dhanurvede brāhme vede ca pāragam
|
taṃ sa yadhṛtimāyāntamaruṇāḥ samudāvahan||39||

40yaḥ sa pāṃcālasenānīrdroṇamaṃśamakalpayat
|
pārāvatasavarṇāstaṃ dhṛṣṭadyumnamudāvahan||40||

41tamanvayāt satyadhṛtiḥ saucittiryuddhadurmadaḥ
|
śreṇimān vasudānaśca putraḥ kāśyasya cābhibhūḥ||41||

42yuktaiḥ paramakāmbojairjavanairhemamālibhiḥ
|
bhīṣayanto dviṣatsainyaṃ yamavaiśravaṇopamāḥ||42||

43prabhadrakāstu kāmbojāḥ ṣaṭsahasrāṇyudāyudhāḥ
|
nānāvarṇairhayaiḥ śreṣṭhairhemavarṇarathadhvajāḥ||43||

44śaravrātairvidhunvantaḥ śatrūn vitatakākāḥ
|
samānamutyavo bhūtvā dhṛṣṭadyumnaṃ samanvayuḥ||44||

45babhrukauśeyavarṇāstu suvarṇavaramālinaḥ
|
ūhuramlānamanasaścekitānaṃ hayottamāḥ||45||

46indrāyudhasavarṇastu kuntibhojo hayottamaiḥ
|
āyāt sadaśvaiḥ purujinmātulaḥ savyasācinaḥ||46||

47antarikṣasavarṇāstu tārakā citritā iva
|
rājānaṃ rocamānaṃ te hayāḥ saṃkhye samāvahan||47||

48karburāḥ śitipādāstu svarṇajālaparicchadāḥ
|
jārāsandhiṃ hayāḥ śreṣṭhāḥ sahadevamudāvahan||48||

49ye tu puṣkaranālasya samavarṇā hayottamāḥ
|
jave śyenasamāścitrāḥ sudāmānamudāvahan||49||

50śaśalohitavarṇāstu pāṇḍurodatarājayaḥ
|
pāñcālyaṃ gopateḥ putraṃ siṃhasenamudāvahan||50||

51pañcālānāṃ naravyāghro yaḥ khyāto janamejayaḥ
|
tasya sarṣapapuṣpāṇāṃ tulyavarṇā hayottamāḥ||51||

52māṣavarṇāśca javanā bṛhanto hemamālinaḥ
|
dadhipṛṣṭhāścitramukhāḥ pāñcālyamavahan drutam||52||

53śūrāśca bhadrakāścaiva śarakāṇḍanibhā hayāḥ
|
padmakiñjalkavarṇābhā daṇḍadhāramudāvahan||53||

54rāsabhāruṇavarṇābhāḥ pṛṣṭato mūṣikaprabhāḥ
|
valganta iva saṃyattā vyāghradattamudāvahan||54||

55harayaḥ kālakāścitrāścitramālyāvabhūṣitāḥ
|
sudhanvānaṃ naravyāghraṃ pāñcālyaṃ samudāvahan||55||

56indrāśanisamasparśā indragopakasannibhāḥ
|
kāye citrāntarāścitrāścitrāyudhamudāvahan||56||

57bibhrato hemamālāstu cakravākodarā hayāḥ
|
kosalādhipateḥ putra sukṣatraṃ vājino'vahan||57||

58śabalāstu bṛhanto'śvā dāntā jāṃbūtadasrajaḥ
|
yuddhe satyadhṛtiṃ kṣemimavahan prāṃśavaḥ śubhāḥ||58||

59ekavarṇena sarveṇa dhvajena kavacena ca
|
aśvaiśca dhanuṣā caiva śuklaiḥ śuklo nyavartataḥ||59||

60samudrasenaputraṃ tu sāmudrā rudratejasam
|
aśvāḥ śaśāṅkasadṛśāścandrasenamudāvahan||60||

61nīlotpalasavarṇāstu tapanīyavibhūṣitāḥ
|
śaibyaṃ citrarathaṃ saṃkhye citramālyā'vahan hayāḥ||61||

62kalāyapuṣpavarṇāstu śvetalohetarājayaḥ
|
rathasenaṃ hayaśreṣṭhāḥ samūhuryuddhadurmadam||62||

63yaṃ tu sarvamanuṣyebhyaḥ prāhuḥ śūrataraṃ nṛpam
|
taṃ paṭaccarahantāraṃ śukavarṇā'vahan hayāḥ||63||

64citrāyudhaṃ citramālyaṃ citravarmāyudhadhvajam
|
ūhuḥ kiṃśukapuṣpāṇāṃ samavarṇā hayottamāḥ||64||

65ekavarṇena sarveṇa dhvajena kavacena ca
|
dhanuṣā rathavāhaiśca nīlairnīlo'bhyavartata||65||

66nānārūpai ratnacihnairvarūtharathakārmukaiḥ
|
vājidhvajapatākābhiścitraiścitro'bhyavartata||66||

67ye tu puṣkaravarṇasya tulyavarṇā hayottamāḥ
|
te rocamānasya sutaṃ hemavarṇamudāvahan||67||

68yodhāśca bhadrakārāśca śaradaṇḍānudaṇḍayaḥ
|
śvetāṇḍāḥ kuṭāṇḍābhādaṇḍaketuṃ hayā'vahan||68||

69keśavena hate saṃkhye pitaryatha narādhipe
|
bhinne kapāṭe pāṇḍyānāṃ vidruteṣu ca bandhuṣu||69||

70bhīṣmādavāpya cāstrāṇi droṇādrāmāt kupāt tathā
|
astraiḥ samatvaṃ saṃprāpya rukmikarṇārjunācyutaiḥ||70||

71iyeṣa dvārakāṃ hantuṃ kṛtsnāṃ jetuṃ ca medinīm
|
nivāritastataḥ prājñaiḥ sahṛdbhirhitakāmyayā||71||

72vairānubandhamutsṛjya svarājyamanuśāsti yaḥ
|
sasāgaradhvajaḥ pāṇḍyaścandraraśminibhairhayaiḥ||72||

73vaiḍūryajālasaṃchannairvīryadraviṇamāśritaḥ
|
divyaṃ visphārayaṃścāpaṃ droṇamabhyadravadbalī||73||

74āṭarūṣakavarṇābhā hayāḥ pāṇḍyānuyāyinām
|
avahan rathamukhyānāmayutāni caturda‌śa||74||

75nānāvarṇena rūpeṇa nānākṛtimukhā hayāḥ
|
rathacakradhvajaṃ vīraṃ ghaṭotkacamudāvahan||75||

76bhāratānāṃ sametānāmutsṛjyaiko matāni yaḥ
|
gato yudhiṣṭhiraṃ bhaktyā tyaktvā sarvamabhīpsitam||76||

77lohitākṣaṃ mahābāhuṃ bṛhantaṃ tamaraṭṭajāḥ
|
mahāsatvā mahākāyāḥ sauvarṇasyandane sthitam||77||

78suvarṇavarṇā dharmajñamanīkasthaṃ yudhiṣṭhiram
|
rājaśreṣṭhaṃ hayaśreṣṭhāḥ sarvataḥ pṛṣṭhato'nvayuḥ||78||

79varṇairuccāvacairanyaiḥ sadaśvānāṃ prabhadrakāḥ
|
saṃnyavartanta yuddhāya bahavo devarūpiṇaḥ||79||

80te yattā bhīmasenena sahitāḥ kāñcanadhvajāḥ
|
pratyadṛśyanta rājendra sendrā iva divaukasaḥ||80||

81atyarocata tān sarvān dhṛṣṭadyumnaḥ samāgatān
|
sarvāṇyati ca sainyāni bhāradvājo vyarocataḥ||81||

82atīva śuśubhe tasya dhvajaḥ kuṣṇājinottaraḥ
|
kamaṇḍalurmahārāja jātarūpamayaḥ śubhaḥ||82||

83dhvajaṃ tu bhīmasenasya vaiḍhūryamaṇilocanam
|
bhrājamānaṃ mahāsiṃhaṃ rājantaṃ dṛṣṭavānaham||83||

84dhvajaṃ tu kururājasya pāṇḍavasya mahaujasaḥ
|
dṛṣṭavānasmi sauvarṇaṃ somaṃ grahagaṇānvatam||84||

85mṛdaṅgau cātra vipulau divyau nandopanandakau
|
yantreṇāhanyamānau ca susvanau harṣavardhanau||85||

86śarabhaṃ pṛṣṭhasauvarṇaṃ nakulasya mahādhvajam
|
apaśyāma rathe'tyugraṃ bhīṣayāṇamavasthitam||86||

87haṃsastu rājataḥ śrīmān dhvaje ghaṇṭāpatākavān
|
sahadevasya durdharṣo dviṣatāṃ śokavardhanaḥ||87||

88pañcānāṃ draupadeyānāṃ pratimādhvajabhūṣaṇam
|
dharmamārutaśakrāṇāmaśvinośca mahātmanoḥ||88||

89abhimanyoḥ kumārasya śārṅgapakṣī hiraṇmayaḥ
|
rathe dhvajavaro rājaṃstaptacāmīkarojjvalaḥ||89||

90ghaṭotkacasya rājendra dhvaje gṛdhro vyarocata
|
aśvāśca kāmagāstasya rāvaṇasya purā yathā||90||

91māhendraṃ ca dhanurdivyaṃ dharmarāje yudhiṣṭhire
|
vāyavyaṃ bhīmasenasya dhanurdivyamabhūnnṛpa||91||

92trailokyarakṣaṇārthāya brahmaṇā sṛṣṭamāyudham
|
taddivyamajaraṃ caiva phālgunārthāya vai dhanuḥ||92||

93vaiṣṇavaṃ nakulāyātha sahadevāya cāśvijam
|
ghaṭotkacasya paulastyaṃ dhanurdivyaṃ bhayānakam||93||

94raudramāgneyakauberaṃ yāmyaṃ giriśameva ca
|
pañcānāṃ draupadeyānāṃ dhanūratnāni bhārata||94||

95raudraṃ dhanurvaraṃ śreṣṭhaṃ lebhe yadrohiṇīsutaḥ
|
tat tuṣṭaḥ pradadau rāmaḥ saubhadrāya mahātmane||95||

96ete cānye ca bahavo dhvajā hemavibhūṣitāḥ
|
tatrādṛśyanta śūrāṇāṃ dviṣatāṃ śokavardhanāḥ||96||

97tadabhūddhvajasaṃbādhamakāpuruṣasevitam
|
droṇānīkaṃ mahārāja paṭe citramivārpitam||97||

98śuśruvurnāmagotrāṇi vīrāṇāṃ saṃyuge tadā
|
droṇamādravatāṃ rājan svayaṃvara ivāhave||98||
