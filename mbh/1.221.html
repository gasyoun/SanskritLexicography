Mahābhārata 1.221 (Ādiparvan)

1vaiśaṃpāyana uvāca|
uktavanto yathā vīryamasakṛtsarvavṛṣṇayaḥ
|
tato'bravīdvāsudevo vākyaṃ dharmārthasaṃyutam||1||

2nāvamānaṃ kulasyāsya guḍākeśaḥ prayuktavān
|
saṃmāno'bhyadhikastena prayukto'yaṃ na saṃśayaḥ||2||

3arthalubdhānna vaḥ pārtho manyate sātvatānsadā
|
svayaṃvaramanādhṛṣyaṃ manyate cāpi pāṇḍavaḥ||3||

4pradānamapi kanyāyāḥ paśuvatko nu manyate
|
vikrayaṃ cāpyapatyasya kaḥ kuryātpuruṣo bhuvi||4||

5etāndoṣāṃstu kaunteyo dṛṣṭavāniti me matiḥ
|
ataḥ prasahya hṛtavānkanyāṃ dharmeṇa pāṇḍavaḥ||5||

6ucitaścaiva saṃbandhaḥ subhadrāṃ ca śayasvinīm
|
eṣa cāpīdṛśaḥ pārthaḥ prasahya hṛtavānataḥ||6||

7bharatasyānvaye jātaṃ śāntanośca yaśasvinaḥ
|
kuntibhojātmājāputraṃ kā bubhūṣeta nārjunam||7||

8na taṃ paśyāmi yaḥ pārthaṃ vijayeta raṇe balāt
|
varjayitvā virūpākṣaṃ bhaganetraharaṃ haram||8||

9api sarveṣu lokeṣu sendrarudreṣu māriṣa
|
sa ca nāma rathastādṛṅmadīyāste ca vājinaḥ||9||

10yoddhā pārthaśca śīghrāstraḥ ko nu tena samo bhavet
|
tamabhidrutya sāntvena parameṇa dhanañjayam||10||

11nyavartayata saṃhṛṣṭā mamaiṣā paramā matiḥ
|
yadi nirjitya vaḥ pārtho balādgacchetsvakaṃ puraṃ||11||

12praṇaśyedvo yaśaḥ sadyo na tu sāntve parājayaḥ
|
tacchrutvā vāsudevasya tathā cakrurjanādhipa||12||

13nivṛttaścārjustatra vivāhaṃ kṛtavānprabhuḥ
|
uṣitvā tatra kaunteyaḥ saṃvatsaraparāḥ kṣapāḥ||13||

14vihṛtya ca yathākāmaṃ pūjito vuṣṇinandanaiḥ
|
puṣkare tu tataḥ śoṣaṃ kālaṃ vartitavānprabhuḥ||14||

15pūrṇe tu dvādaśe varṣe khāṇḍavaprasthamāgataḥ
|
abhigamya ca rājānaṃ niyamena samāhitaḥ||15||

16abhyarcya brāhmaṇānpārtho draupadīmabhijagmivān‌
|
taṃ draupadī pratyuvāca praṇayātkurunandanam‌||16||

17tatraiva gaccha kaunteya yatra sā sātvatātmajā
|
subaddhasyāpi bhārasya pūrvabandhaḥ ślathāyate || 17 ||

18tathā bahuvidhaṃ kṛṣṇāṃ vilapantīṃ dhanaṃjayaḥ
|
sāntvayāmāsa bhūyaśca kṣamayāmāsa cāsakṛt‌|| 18 ||

19subhadrāṃ tvaramāṇaśca raktakauśeyavāsinīm‌
|
pārthaḥ prasthāpayāmāsa kṛtvā gopālikāvapuḥ ||19||

20sā'dhikaṃ tena rūpeṇa śobhasānā yaśāsvinī
|
bhavanaṃ śreṣṭhamāsādya vīrapatnī varāṅganā ||20||

21vavande pṛthutāmrākṣī pṛthāṃ bhadrā yaśasvinī
|
tāṃ kuntī cārusarvāṅgīmupājighrata mūrdhani || 21||

22prītyā paramayā yuktā āśīrbhiryuñjatātulām
|
tato'bhigamya tvaritā pūṇendusadṛśānanā || 22 ||

23vavande draupadīṃ bhadrā preṣyā'hāmiti cābravīt‌
|
pratyutthāya tadā kṛṣṇā svasāraṃ māghavasya ca||23||

24pariṣvajyāvadatprītyā niḥsepatno'stu te patiḥ
|
tathaiva muditā bhadrā tāsuvācaivamastviti || 24||

25tataste hṛṣṭamanasaḥ pāṇḍaveyā mahārathāḥ
|
kuntī ca paramaprītā babhūva janamejaya||25||

26śrutvā tu puṇḍarīkākṣaḥ saṃprāptaṃ svaṃ purottamam‌
|
arjunaḥ pāṇḍavaśreṣṭhamindraprasthagataṃ tadā ||26||

27ājagāma viśuddhātmā saha rāmeṇa keśavaḥ
|
vṛṣṇyandhakamahāmātraiḥ saha vīrairmahārathaiḥ ||27||

28bhrātṛbhiśva kumāraiśca yodhaiśca bahubhirvṛtaḥ
|
sainyena mahatā śaurirabhiguptaḥ paraṃtapaḥ ||28||

29tatra dānapatirdhīmānājagāma mahāyaśāḥ
|
akrūro vṛṣṇivīrāṇāṃ senāpatirāriṃdamaḥ||29||

30anādhṛṣṭirmahātejā uddhavaśca mahāyaśāḥ
|
sākṣādbṛhaspateḥ śiṣyo mahābuddhirmahāmanāḥ||30||

31satyakaḥ sātyakiścaiva kṛtavarmā ca sātvatuḥ
|
pradyumnaścaiva sāmbaśca niśaṭhaḥ śaṅkureva ca ||31||

32cārudeṣṇaśca vikrānto jhillī vipṛthureva ca
|
sāraṇaśca mahābāhurgadaśva viduṣāṃ varaḥ ||32||

33ete cānye ca bahavo vṛṣṇibhojāndhakāstathā
|
ājagmuḥ khāṇḍavaprasthamādāya haraṇaṃ bahu ||33||

34tato yudhiṣṭhiro rājā śrutvā mādhavamāgatam
|
pratigrahārthaṃ kṛṣṇasya yamau prāsthāpayattadā ||34||

35tāmyāṃ pratigṛhītaṃ tu vṛṣṇicakraṃ maharddhimat
|
viveśa khāṇḍavaprasthaṃ patākādhvajaśobhitam ||35||

36saṃmṛṣṭasiktapanthānaṃ puṣpaprakaraśobhitam
|
candanasya rasaiḥ śītaiḥ puṇyagandhairniṣevitam || 36||

37dahyatā'guruṇā caiva deśe deśe sugandhinā
|
hṛṣṭapuṣṭajanākīrṇaṃ vaṇigbhirupaśobhitam ||37||

38pratipede mahābāhuḥ saha rāmeṇa keśavaḥ
|
vṛṣṇyandhakaistathā bhojaiḥ sametaḥ puruṣottamaḥ ||38||

39saṃpūjyamānaḥ pauraiśca brāhmaṇaiśca sahasraśaḥ
|
viveśa bhavanaṃ rājñaḥ purandaragṛhopamam ||39||

40yudhiṣṭhirastu rāmeṇa samāgacchadyathāvidhi
|
mūrdhni keśavamāghrāya bāhubhyāṃ pariṣastraje ||40||

41taṃ prīyamāṇo govindo vinayenābhipūjayan
|
bhīmaṃ ca puruṣavyāghraṃ vidhivatpratyapūjayat ||41||

42tāṃśca vṛṣṇyandhakaśreṣṭhānkuntīputro yudhiṣṭhiraḥ
|
pratijagrāha satkārairyathāvidhi yathāgatam ||42||

43guruvatpūjayāmāsa kāṃścitkāṃścidvayasyavat
|
kāṃścidabhyavadatpremṇā kaiścidapyabhivāditaḥ ||43||

44teṣāṃ dadau hṛṣīkeśo janyārthe dhanamuttamam
|
haraṇaṃ vai subhadrāyā jñātideyaṃ mahāyaśāḥ|| 44||

45rathānāṃ kāñcanāṅgānāṃ kiṅkiṇījālamālinām
|
caturyujāmupetānāṃ sūtaiḥ kuśalaśikṣitaiḥ|| 45||

46sahasraṃ pradadau kṛṣṇo gavāmayutameva ca
|
śrīmānmāthuradeśyānāṃ dogdhrīṇāṃ puṇyavarcasām|| 46||

47vaḍavānāṃ ca śuddhānāṃ candrāṃśusamavarcasām
|
dadau janārdanaḥ prītyā sahasraṃ hemabhūṣitam || 47||

48tathaivāśvavarīṇāṃ ca dāntānāṃ vātaraṃhasām
|
śatānyañjanakeśīnāṃ śvetānāṃ pañca pañca ca || 48||

49snānapānotsave caiva prayuktaṃ vayasānvitam
|
strīṇāṃ sahasraṃ gaurīṇāṃ suveṣāṇāṃ suvarcasām|| 49||

50suvarṇaśatakaṇṭhīnāmaromāṇāṃ svalaṃkṛtām
|
paricaryāsu dakṣāṇāṃ pradadau puṣkarekṣaṇaḥ ||50||

51pṛṣṭhyānāmapi cāśvānāṃ bālhikānāṃ janārdanaḥ
|
dadau śatasahastrākhyaṃ kanyādhanamanuttamam || 51||

52kṛtākṛtasya mukhyasya kanakasyāgnivarcasaḥ
|
manuṣyabhārāndāśārho dadau daśa janārdanaḥ || 52||

53gajānāṃ tu prabhinnānāṃ tridhā prasnavatāṃ madam
|
girikūṭanikāśānāṃ samareṣvanivartinām || 53||

54guptānāṃ paṭughaṇṭānāṃ cārūṇāṃ hemamālinām
|
hastyārohairupetānāṃ sahasraṃ sāhasapriyaḥ ||54||

55rāmaḥ pāṇigrahaṇikaṃ dadau pārthāya lāṅgalī
|
prīyamāṇo haladharaḥ saṃbandhaṃ pratimānayan||55||

56sa mahādhanaratnaugho vastrakambalaphenavān
|
mahāgajamahāgrahaḥ patākāśaivalākulaḥ||56||

57pāṇḍusāgaramāviddhaḥ praviveśa mahādhanaḥ
|
pūrṇamāpūrayaṃsteṣāṃ dviṣacchokāvaho'bhavat ||57||

58pratijagrāha tatsarvaṃ dharmarājā yudhiṣṭhiraḥ
|
pūjayāmāsa tāṃścaiva vṛṣṇyandhakamahārathān||58||

59te sametā mahātmānaḥ kuruvṛṣṇyandhakottamāḥ
|
vijahruramarāvāse narāḥ sukṛtino yathā ||59||

60tatra tatra mahānādairutkṛṣṭatalanāditaiḥ
|
yathāyogaṃ yathāprīti vijahruḥ kuruvṛṣṇayaḥ||60||

61evamuttamavīryāste vihṛtya divasānbahūn
|
pūjitāḥ kurubhirjagmuḥ punardvāravatīṃ prati ||61||

62rāmaṃ puraskṛtya yayurvṛṣṇyandhakamahārathāḥ
|
ratnānyādāya śubhrāṇi dattāni kurusattamaiḥ||62||

63vāsudevastu pārthena tatraiva saha bhārata
|
uvāsa nagare ramye śakraprasthe mahātmanā ||63||

64vyacaradyamunātīre mṛgayāṃ sa mahāyaśāḥ
|
mṛgānvidhyanvarāhāṃśca reme sārdhaṃ kirīṭinā||64||

65tataḥ subhadrā saubhadraṃ keśavasya priyā svasā
|
jayantamiva paulomī khyātimantamajījanat||65||

66dīrghabāhuṃ mahoraskaṃ vṛṣabhākṣamariṃdamam
|
subhadrā suṣuve vīramabhimanyuṃ nararṣabham||66||

67abhiśca manyumāṃścaiva tatastamarimardanam
|
abhimanyumiti prāhurārjuniṃ puruṣarṣamam||67||

68sa sātvatyāmatirathaḥ saṃbabhūva ghanaṃjayāt
|
makhe nirmathaneneva śamīgarbhāddhutāśanaḥ||68||

69yasmiñjāte mahātejāḥ kuntīputro yudhiṣṭhiraḥ
|
ayutaṃ gā dvijātibhyaḥ pādānniṣkāṃśca bhārata|| 69||

70dayito vāsudevasya bālyātprabhṛti cābhavat
|
pitṝṇāmiva sarveṣāṃ prajānāmiva candramāḥ || 70||

71janmaprabhṛti kṛṣṇaśca cakre tasya kriyāḥ śubhāḥ
|
sa cāpi vavṛdhe bālaḥ śuklapakṣe yathā śaśī || 71||

72catuṣpādaṃ daśavidhaṃ dhanurvedamariṃdamaḥ
|
arjunādvedavedaśaḥ sakalaṃ divyamānuṣam ||72||

73vijñāneṣvapi cāstrāṇāṃ sauṣṭhave ca mahābalaḥ
|
kriyāsvapi ca sarvāsu viśeṣānabhyāśikṣayat||73||

74āgame ca prayoge ca cakre tulyāmivātmanā
|
tutoṣa putraṃ saubhadraṃ prekṣamāṇo dhanaṃjayaḥ ||74||

75sarvasaṃhananopetaṃ sarvalakṣaṇalakṣitam
|
durdharṣamṛṣabhaskandhaṃ vyāttānanāmavoragam ||75||

76siṃhadarpaṃ maheṣvāsaṃ mattamātaṅgavikramam
|
meghadundubhinirghoṣaṃ pūrṇacandranibhānanam ||76||

77kṛṣṇasya sadṛśaṃ śaurye vīrye rūpe tathā kṛtau
|
dadarśa putraṃ bībhatsurmaghavāniva taṃ yathā ||77||

78pāñcālyapi tu pañcabhyaḥ patibhyaḥ śubhalakṣaṇā
|
lebhe pañca sutānvīrān śreṣṭhānpañcācalāniva||78||

79yudhiṣṭhirātmativindhyaṃ sutasomaṃ vṛkodāt
|
arjunācchrutakarmāṇaṃ śatānīkaṃ ca nākulim ||79||

80sahadevācchrutasenametānpañca mahārathān
|
pāñcālī suṣuve vīrānādityānaditiryathā||80||

81śāstrataḥ prativindhyaṃ tamūcurviprā yudhiṣṭhiram
|
parapraharaṇajñāne prativindhyo bhavatvayam ||81||

82sute somasahastre tu somārkasamatejasam
|
sutasomaṃ maheṣvāsaṃ suṣuve bhīmasenataḥ ||82||

83śrutaṃ karma mahatkṛtvā nivṛttena kirīṭinā
|
jātaḥ putrastathetyevaṃ śrutakarmā tato'bhavat || 83||

84śatānīkasya rājarṣeḥ kauravyasya mahātmanaḥ
|
cakre putraṃ sa nāmānaṃ nakulaḥ kīrtivardhanam || 84||

85tatastvajījanatkṛṣṇā nakṣatre vahnidaivate
|
sahadevātsutaṃ tasmācchataseneti yaṃ viduḥ || 85||

86ekavarṣāntarāstvete draupadeyā yaśasvinaḥ
|
anvajāyanta rājendra parasparahitaiṣiṇaḥ ||86||

87jātakarmāṇyānupūrvyāccūḍopanayanāni ca
|
cakāra vidhivaddhaumyasteṣāṃ bharatasattama ||87||

88kṛtvā ca vedādhyayanaṃ tataḥ sucaritavratāḥ
|
jagṛhuḥ sarvabhiṣvastramarjunāddivyamānuṣam ||88||

89divyagarbhopamaiḥ putrairvyūḍhoraskairmahārathaiḥ
|
anvitā rājaśārdūla pāṇḍavā mudamāpnuvan ||89||
