Mahābhārata 12.236 (Śāntiparvan)

1atha cedrocayedetaduhyeta srotasā yathā
|
unmajjaṃśca nimajjaṃśca jñānavān plavavānbhavet ||1||

2prajñayā niścitā dhīrāstārayantyabudhānplavaiḥ
|
nābudhāstārayantyanyānātmānaṃ vā kathaṃcana ||2||

3chinnadoṣo muniryogānmukto yuñjīta dvādaśa
|
deśakarmānurāgārthānupāyāpāyaniścayaḥ ||3||

4cakṣurāhārasaṃhārairmanasā darśanena ca
|
yacchedvāṅmānasī buddhyā ya icchejjñānamuttamam ||4||

5jñānena yacchedātmānaṃ ya icchecchāntitātmanaḥ
|
eteṣāṃ cedanudraṣṭā puruṣo'pi sudāruṇaḥ ||5||

6yadi vā sarvavedajño yadi vāpyanṛco dvijaḥ
|
yadi vā dhārmiko yajvā yadi vā pāpakṛttamaḥ ||6||

7yadi vā puruṣavyāghro yadi vā kleśadhāritaḥ
|
taratyevaṃ mahādurgaṃ jarāmaraṇasāgaram ||7||

8evaṃ hyetena yogena yuñjāno hyevamantataḥ
|
api jijñāsamāno'pi śabdabrahmātivartate ||8||

9dharmopastho hrīvarūtha upāyāpāyakūbaraḥ
|
apānākṣaḥ prāṇayugaḥ prajñāyurjīvabandhanaḥ ||9||

10cetanābandhuraścāruścāragrahaneminān
|
darśanasparśanavaho ghrāṇaśravaṇavāhanaḥ ||10||

11prajñānābhiḥ sarvatantrapradoto jñānasārathiḥ
|
kṣetrajñādhiṣṭhito dhīraḥ śraddhādamapuraḥsaraḥ ||11||

12tyāgasūkṣmānugaḥ kṣemyaḥ śaucago dhyānagocaraḥ
|
jīvayukto ratho divyo brahmaloke virājate ||12||

13atha santvaramāṇasya rathamevaṃ yuyukṣataḥ
|
akṣaraṃ gantumanaso vidhiṃ vakṣyāmi śīghragam ||13||

14sapta yā dhāraṇāḥ kṛtsnā vāgyataḥ pratipadyate
|
pṛṣṭhataḥ pārvataścānyāstāvatyastāḥ pradhāraṇāḥ ||14||

15kramaśaḥ pārthivaṃ yacca vāyavyaṃ khaṃ tathā payaḥ
|
jyotiṣo yattadaiśvaryamahaṅkārasya buddhitaḥ
|
avyaktasya tathaiśvaryaṃ kramaśaḥ pratipadyate ||15||

16vikramāścāpi yasyaite tathā yukteṣu yogataḥ
|
tathā yogasya yuktasya siddhimātmani paśyataḥ ||16||

17nirmucyamānaḥ sūkṣmatvādrūpāṇīmāni paśyataḥ
|
śaiśirastu yathā dhūmaḥ sūkṣmaḥ saṃśrayate nabhaḥ ||17||

18tathā dehādvimuktasya pūrvaṃ rūpaṃ bhavatyuta
|
atha dhūmasya virame dvitīyaṃ rūpadarśanam ||18||

19jalarūpamivākāśe tathaivātmani paśyati
|
apāṃ vyatikrame cāsya vahnirūpaṃ prakāśate ||19||

20tasminnuparate'jo'sya pītaśastraḥ prakāśate
|
urṇārūpasavarṇasya tasya rūpaṃ prakāśate ||20||

21atha śvetāṃ gatiṃ gatvā vāyavyaṃ sūkṣmamapyuta
|
aśuklaṃ cetasaḥ saukṣyamapyuktaṃ brāhmaṇasya vai ||21||

22eteṣvapi hi jāteṣu phalajātāni me śṛṇu
|
jātasya pārthivaiśvaryaiḥ sṛṣṭiratra vidhīyate ||22||

23prajāpatirivākṣobhyaḥ śarīrātsṛjate prajāḥ
|
aṅgulyaṅguṣṭhamātreṇa hastapādena vā tathā ||23||

24pṛthivīṃ kampayatyeko guṇo vāyoriti śrutiḥ
|
ākāśabhūtaścākāśe savarṇatvātprakāśate ||24||

25varṇato guhyate cāpi kāmātpibati cāśayān
|
na cāsya tejasā rūpaṃ dṛśyate śāmyate tathā
|
ahaṅkāre'sya vijite pañcaite syurvaśānugāḥ ||25||

26ṣaṇṇāmātmani buddhau ca jitāyāṃ prabhavatyatha
|
nirdoṣapratibhā hyenaṃ kṛtsnā samabhivartate ||26||

27tathaiva vyaktamātmānamavyaktaṃ pratipadyate
|
yato niḥsarate loko bhavati vyaktasaṃjñakaḥ ||27||

28tatrāvyaktamayīṃ vidyāṃ śṛṇu tvaṃ vistareṇa me
|
tathā vyaktamayaṃ caiva sāṃkhye pūrvaṃ nibodha me ||28||

29pañcaviṃśatitattvāni tulyānyubhayataḥ samam
|
yoge sāṃkhye'pi ca tathā viśeṣaṃ tatra me śṛṇu ||29||

30proktaṃ tadvyaktamityeva jāyate vardhate ca yat
|
jīryate mriyate caiva caturbhirlakṣaṇairyutam ||30||

31viparītamato yattu tadavyaktamudāhṛtam
|
dvāvātmānau ca vedeṣu siddhānteṣvapyudāhṛtau ||31||

32caturlakṣaṇa tvādyaṃ caturvargaṃ pracakṣate
|
vyaktamavyaktajaṃ caiva tathā buddhamacetanam
|
sattvaṃ kṣetrajña ityetaddvayamapyanudarśitam ||32||

33dvāvātmānau ca vedeṣu viṣayeṣvanurajyataḥ
|
viṣayātpratisaṃhāraḥ sāṃkhyānāṃ viddhi lakṣaṇam ||33||

34nirmamaścānahaṅkāro nirdvandvaśchinnasaṃśayaḥ
|
naiva kruddhyati na dveṣṭi nānṛtā bhāṣate giraḥ ||34||

35ākruṣṭastāḍitaścaiva maitreṇa dhyāti nāśubham
|
vāgdaṇḍakarmamanasāṃ trayāṇāṃ ca nivartakaḥ ||35||

36samaḥ sarveṣu bhūteṣu brahmāṇamabhivartate
|
naivecchati na cāniccho yātrāmātravyavasthitaḥ ||36||

37alolupo'vyatho dānto na kṛtī na nirākṛtiḥ
|
nāsyendriyamanekāgraṃ nāvikṣiptamanorathaḥ ||37||

38sarvabhūtasadṛḍmaitraḥ samaloṣṭāśmakāñcanaḥ
|
tulyapriyāpriyo dhīrastulyanindātmasaṃstutiḥ ||38||

39aspṛhaḥ sarvakāmebhyo brahmacaryadṛḍhavrataḥ
|
ahiṃsraḥ sarvabhūtānāmīdṛk sāṃkhyo vimucyate ||39||

40yathā yogadvimucyante kāraṇairyairnibodha tat
|
yogaiśvaryamatikrānto yo niṣkrāmati mucyate ||40||

41ityeṣā bhāvajā buddhiḥ kathitā te na saṃśayaḥ
|
evaṃ bhavati nirdvandvo brahmāṇaṃ cādhigacchati ||41||
