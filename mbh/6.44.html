Mahābhārata 6.44 (Bhīṣmaparvan)

1dhṛtarāṣṭra uvāca|evaṃ vyūḍheṣvanīkeṣu māmakeṣvitareṣu ca
|
ke pūrvaṃ prāharaṃstatra kuravaḥ pāṇḍavā nu kim ||1||

2sañjaya uvāca|bhrātṛbhiḥ sahito rājan putro duḥśāsanastava
|
bhīṣmaṃ pramukhataḥ kṛtvā prayayau saha senayā ||2||

3tathaiva pāṇḍavāḥ sarve bhīmasenapurogamāḥ
|
bhīṣmeṇa yuddhamicchantaḥ prayayurhṛṣṭamānasāḥ ||3||

4kṣveḍāḥ kilakilāśabdāḥ krakacā goviṣāṇikāḥ
|
bherīmṛdaṅgamurajā hayakuñjaraniḥsvanāḥ ||4||

5ubhayoḥ senayorhyāsaṃstataste'smān samādravan
|
vayaṃ tān pratinardaṃtastadāsīttumulaṃ mahat ||5||

6mahāntyanīkāni mahāsamucchraye samāgame pāṇḍavadhārtarāṣṭrayoḥ
|
cakalpire śaṅkhamṛdaṅgāniḥsvanaiḥ prakampitānīva vanāni vāyunā ||6||

7narendranāgāśvarathākulānāmabhyāgatānāmaśive muhūrte
|
babhūva ghoṣaratumulaścamūnāṃ vātoddhutānāmiva sāgarāṇām ||7||

8tasmin samutthite śabde tumule lomaharṣaṇe
|
bhīmaseno mahābāhuḥ prāṇadadgovṛṣo yathā ||8||

9śaṅkhadundubhinirghoṣaṃ vāraṇānāṃ ca vṛhitam
|
siṃhanādaṃ ca sainyānāṃ bhīmasenaravo'bhyabhūt ||9||

10hayānāṃ heṣamāṇānāmanīkeṣu sahasraśaḥ
|
sarvānabhyabhavacchabdān bhīmasya nadataḥ svanaḥ ||10||

11taṃ śrutvā ninadaṃ tasya sainyāstava vitatrasuḥ
|
jīmūtasyeva nadataḥ śakrāśanisamasvanam ||11||

12vāhanāni ca sarvāṇi śakṛnmūtraṃ prasusruvuḥ
|
śabdena tasya vīrasya siṃhasyevetare mṛgāḥ ||12||

13darśayan ghoramātmānaṃ mahābhramiva nādayan
|
vibhīṣayaṃstava sutān bhīmasenaḥ samabhyayāt ||13||

14tamāyāntaṃ maheṣvāsaṃ sodaryāḥ paryavārayan
|
chādayantaḥ śaravrātairmeghā iva divākaram ||14||

15duryodhanaśca putraste durmukho duḥśalaḥ śalaḥ
|
duḥśāsanaścātirathastathā durmarṣaṇo nṛpaḥ ||15||

16viviṃśatiścitraseno vikarṇaśca mahārathaḥ
|
purumitro jayo bhojaḥ saumadattiśca vīryavān ||16||

17mahācāpāni dhunvanto meghā iva savidyutaḥ
|
ādadānāśca nārācānnirmuktāśīviṣopamān ||17||

18atha te draupadīputrāḥ saubhadraśca mahārathaḥ
|
nakulaḥ sahadevaśca dhṛṣṭadyumnaśca pārṣataḥ ||18||

19dhārtarāṣṭrān pratiyayurardayantaḥ śitaiḥ śaraiḥ
|
vajrairiva mahāvegaiḥ śikharāṇi dharābhṛtām ||19||

20tasmin prathamasaṅgrāme bhīmajyātalaniḥsvane
|
tāvakānāṃ pareṣāṃ ca nāsītkaścitparāṅmukhaḥ ||20||

21lāghavaṃ droṇaśiṣyāṇāmapaśyaṃ bharatarṣabha
|
nimittavedhināṃ caiva śarānutsṛjatāṃ bhṛśam ||21||

22nopaśāmyati nirghoṣo dhanuṣāṃ kūjatāṃ tathā
|
viniśceruḥ śarā dīptā jyotīṃṣīva nabhastalāt ||22||

23sarve tvanye mahīpālāḥ prekṣakā iva bhārata
|
dadṛśurdarśanīyaṃ taṃ bhīmaṃ jñātisamāgamam ||23||

24tataste jātasaṃrambhāḥ parasparakṛtāgasaḥ
|
anyonyaspardhayā rājan vyāyacchanta mahārathāḥ ||24||

25kurupāṇḍavasene te hastyaśvarathasaṃkule
|
śuśubhāte raṇe'tīva paṭe citrārpite iva ||25||

26tataste pārthivāḥ sarve pragṛhītaśarāsanāḥ
|
sahasainyāḥ samāpetuḥ putrasya tava śāsanāt ||26||

27yudhiṣṭhireṇa cādiṣṭāḥ pārthivāste sahasraśaḥ
|
vinadantaḥ samāpetuḥ putrasya tava vāhinīm ||27||

28ubhayoḥ senayostīvraḥ sainyānāṃ sa samāgamaḥ
|
antardhīyata cādityaḥ sainyena rajasā''vṛtaḥ ||28||

29prayuddhānāṃ prabhagnānāṃ punarāvartināmapi
|
nātra sveṣāṃ pareṣāṃ vā viśeṣaḥ samadṛśyata ||29||

30tasmiṃstu tumule yuddhe vartamāne mahābhaye
|
atisarvāṇyanīkāni pitā te'bhivyarocata ||30||
