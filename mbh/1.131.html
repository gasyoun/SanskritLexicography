Mahābhārata 1.131 (Ādiparvan)

1vaiśaṃpāyana uvāca|
tato drupadamāsādya bhāradvājaḥ pratāpavān
|
abravītpārthivaṃ rājansakhāyaṃ viddhi māmiha||1||

2ityevamuktaḥ sakhyā sa prītirpūrvaṃ janeśvaraḥ
|
bhāradvājena pāñcālyo nāmṛṣyata vaco'sya tat||2||

3sa krodhāmarṣajihmabhrūḥ kaṣāyīkṛtalocanaḥ
|
aiśvaryamadasaṃpanno droṇaṃ rājā'bravīdidam||3||

4drupada uvāca|
akṛteyaṃ tava prajñā brahmannātisamañjasā
|
yanmāṃ vravīṣi prasabhaṃ sakhā te'hamiti dvija||4||

5na hi rājñāmudīrṇānāmevaṃbhūtairnaraiḥ kvacit
|
sakhyaṃ bhavati mandātmañśriyā hīnairdhanacyutaiḥ||5||

6sauhṛdānyapi jīryante kālena parijīryataḥ
|
sauhṛdaṃ me tvayā hyāsītpūrvaṃ sāmarthyabandhanam||6||

7na sakhyamajaraṃ loke hṛdi tiṣṭhati kasyacit
|
kālo hyenaṃ viharati krodho vainaṃ rahatyuta||7||

8maivaṃ jīrṇamupāssva tvaṃ sakhyaṃ bhavatvapākṛdhi
|
āsītsakhyaṃ dvijaśreṣṭha tvayā me'rthanibandhanam||8||

9na daridro vasumato nāvidvānviduṣaḥ sakhā
|
na śūrasya sakhā klībaḥ sakhipūrvaṃ kimiṣyate||9||

10yayoreva samaṃ vittaṃ yayoreva samaṃ śrutam
|
tayorvivāhaḥ sakhyaṃ ca na tu puṣṭavipuṣṭayoḥ||10||

11nāśrotriyaḥ śrotriyasya nārathī rathinaḥ sakhā
|
nārājā pārthivasyāpi sakhipūrvaṃ kimiṣyate||11||

12vaiśaṃpāyana uvāca|
drupadenaivamuktastu bhāradvājaḥ pratāpavān
|
muhūrtaṃ cintayitvā tu manyunābhipariplutaḥ||12||

13sa viniścitya manasā pāñcālyaṃ prati buddhimān
|
jagāma kurumukhyānāṃ nāgaraṃ nāgasāhvayam||13||

14sa nāgapuramāgamya gautamasya niveśane
|
bhāradvājo'vasattatra pracchannaṃ dvijasattamaḥ||14||

15tato'sya tanujaḥ pārthānkṛpasyānantaraṃ prabhuḥ
|
astrāṇi śikṣayāmāsa nābudhyanta ca taṃ janāḥ||15||

16evaṃ sa tatra gūḍhātmā kaṃcitkālamuvāsa ha
|
kumārāstvatha niṣkramya sametā gajasāhvayāt||16||

17krīḍanto vīṭayā tatra vīrāḥ paryacaranmudā
|
papāta kūpe sa vīṭā teṣāṃ vai krīḍatāṃ tadā||17||

18tataste yatnamātiṣṭhanvīṭāmuddhartumādṛtāḥ
|
na ca te pratyayadyanta karma vīṭopalabdhaye||18||

19tato'nyonyamavaikṣanta vrīḍayāvanatānanāḥ
|
tasyā yogamavidanto bhṛśaṃ cotkaṇṭhitābhavan||19||

20te'paśyanbrāhmaṇaṃ śyāmamāpannaṃ palitaṃ kṛśam
|
kṛtyavantamadūrasthamagnihotrapuraskṛtam||20||

21te taṃ dṛṣṭvā mahātamānamupagamya kumārakāḥ
|
bhagnotsāhakriyātmāno brāhmaṇaṃ paryavārayan||21||

22atha droṇaḥ kumārāṃstāndṛṣṭvā kṛtyavaśastadā
|
prahasya mandaṃ paiśalyādabhyabhāṣata vīryavān||22||

23aho vo dhigbalaṃ kṣātraṃ dhigetāṃ vaḥ kṛtāstratām
|
bharatasyānvaye jātā ye vīṭāṃ nādhigacchata||23||

24vīṭāṃ ca mudrikāṃ caiva hyahametadapi dvayam
|
uddhareyamiṣīkābhirbhojanaṃ me pradīyatām||24||

25evamuktvā kumārāṃstāndroṇaḥ svāṅguliveṣṭanam
|
kūpe nirudake tasminnapātayadaridamaḥ||25||

26tato'bravīttadā droṇaṃ kuntīputro yudhiṣṭhiraḥ
|
yudhiṣṭhira uvāca|
kṛpasyānumate brahmanbhikṣāmāpnuhi śāśvatīm||26||

27evamuktaḥ pratyuvāca prahasya bharatānidam
|
droṇa uvāca|
eṣā muṣṭiriṣīkāṇāṃ mayā'streṇābhimantritā||27||

28asyā vīryaṃ nirīkṣadhvaṃ yadanyeṣu na vidyate
|
bhetsyāmīṣikayā vīṭāṃ tāmiṣīkāṃ tathā'nyayā||28||

29tāmanyayā samāyoge vīṭāyā grahaṇaṃ mama
|
vaiśaṃpāyana uvāca|
tato yathoktaṃ droṇena tatsarvaṃ kṛtamañjasā||29||

30tadavekṣya kumārāste vismayotphullalocanāḥ
|
āścaryamidamatyantamiti matvā vaco'bruvan||30||

31kumārā ūcuḥ|
mudrikāmaṇi viprarṣe śīdhrametāṃ samuddhara
|
vaiśaṃpāyana uvāca|
tataḥ śaraṃ samādāya dhanurdroṇo mahāyaśāḥ||31||

32śareṇa viddhvā mudrāṃ tāmūrdhvamāvāhayatprabhuḥ
|
sa śaraṃ samupādāya kūpādaṅguliveṣṭanam||32||

33dadau tataḥ kumārāṇāṃ vismitānāmavismitaḥ
|
mudrikāmuddhṛtāṃ dṛṣṭvā tamāhuste kumārakāḥ||33||

34kumārā ūcuḥ|
abhivādāyāmahe brahmannaitadanyeṣu vidyate
|
ko'si kasyāsi jānīmo vayaṃ kiṃ karavāmahe||34||

35vaiśaṃpāyana uvāca|
evamuktastato droṇaḥ pratyuvāca kumārakān
|
droṇa uvāca|
ācakṣadhvaṃ ca bhīṣmāya rūpeṇa ca guṇaiśca mām||35||

36sa eva sumahātejāḥ sāṃprataṃ pratipatsyate
|
vaiśaṃpāyana uvāca|
tathetyuktvā ca gatvā ca bhīṣmamūcuḥ kumārakāḥ||36||

37brāhmaṇasya vacastathyaṃ tacca karma tathāvidham
|
bhīṣmaḥ śrutvā kumārāṇāṃ droṇaṃ taṃ pratyajānata||37||

38yuktarūpaḥ sa hi gururityevamanucintya ca
|
athainamānīya tadā svayameva susatkṛtam||38||

39paripapraccha nipuṇaṃ bhīṣmaḥ śastrabhṛtāṃ varaḥ
|
hetumāgamane tacca droṇaḥ sarvaṃ nyavedayat||39||

40droṇa uvāca|
maharṣeragniveśyasya sakāśamahamacyuta
|
astrārthamagamaṃ pūrvaṃ dhanurvedacikīrṣayā||40||

41brahmacārī vinītātmā jaṭilo bahulāḥ samāḥ
|
avasaṃ suciraṃ tatra guruśuśrūṣaṇe rataḥ||41||

42pāñcālyo rājaputraśca yajñaseno mahābalaḥ
|
iṣvastrahetornyavasattasminneva gurau prabhuḥ||42||

43sa me tatra sakhā cāsīdupakārī priyaśca me
|
tenāhaṃ saha saṃgamya vartayansuciraṃ prabho||43||

44bālyātprabhṛti kauravya sahādhyayanameva ca
|
sa me sakhā sadā tatra priyavādī priyaṃkaraḥ||44||

45abravīditi māṃ bhīṣma vacanaṃ prītivardhanam
|
ahaṃ priyatamaḥ putraḥ piturdroṇa mahātmanaḥ||45||

46abhiṣekṣyati māṃ rājye sa pāñcālyo yadā tadā
|
tadbhojyaṃ bhavitā tāta sakhe satyena te śape||46||

47mama bhogāśca vittaṃ ca tvadadhīnaṃ sukhāni ca
|
evamuktvā'tha vavrāja kṛtāstraḥ pūjito mayā||47||

48tacca vākyamahaṃ nityaṃ manasā dhārayaṃstadā
|
so'haṃ pitṛniyogena putralobhādyaśasvinīm||48||

49nātikeśīṃ mahāprajñāmupayeme mahāvratām
|
agnihotre ca satre ca dame ca satataṃ ratām||49||

50alabhadgautamī putramaśvatthāmānamaurasam
|
bhīmavikramakarmāṇamādityasamatejasam||50||

51putreṇa tena prīto'haṃ bharadvājo mayā yathā
|
gokṣīraṃ pibato dṛṣṭvā dhaninastatra putrakān
|
aśvatthāmārudadbālastanme saṃdehayaddiśaḥ||51||

52na snātako'vasīdeta vartamānaḥ svakarmasu
|
iti saṃcintya manasā taṃ deśaṃ bahuśo bhraman||52||

53viśuddhaṇicchangāṅgeya dharmopetaṃ pratigraham
|
antādantaṃ parikramya nādhyagacchaṃ payasvinīm||53||

54atha piṣṭodakenainaṃ lobhayanti kumārakāḥ
|
pītvā piṣṭarasaṃ bālaḥ kṣīraṃ pītaṃ mayā'pi ca||54||

55nanartotthāya kauravya hṛṣṭo bālyādvimohitaḥ
|
taṃ dṛṣṭvā nṛtyamānaṃ tu bālaiḥ parivṛtaṃ sutam||55||

56hāsyatāmupasaṃprāptaṃ kaśmalaṃ tatra me'bhavat
|
droṇaṃ dhigastvadhaninaṃ yo dhanaṃ nādhigacchati||56||

57piṣṭodakaṃ suto yasya pītvā kṣīrasya tṛṣṇayā
|
nṛtyati sma mudāviṣṭaḥ kṣīraṃ pītaṃ mayā'pyuta||57||

58iti saṃbhāṣatāṃ vācaṃ śrutvā me buddhiracyavat
|
ātmānaṃ cātmanā garhanmanasedaṃ vyacintayam||58||

59api cāhaṃ purā viprairvarjito garhito vase
|
paropasevāṃ pāpiṣṭhāṃ na ca kuryāṃ dhanepsayā||59||

60iti matvā priyaṃ putraṃ bhīṣmādāya tato hyaham
|
pūrvasnehānurāgitvātsadāraḥ saumakiṃ gataḥ||60||

61abhiṣiktaṃ tu śrutvaiva kṛtārtho'smīti cintayan
|
priyaṃ sakhāyaṃ suprīto rājyasthaṃ samupāgamam||61||

62saṃsmaransaṅgamaṃ caiva vacanaṃ caiva tasya tat
|
tato drupadamāgamya sakhaipūrvamahaṃ prabho||62||

63abruvaṃ puruṣavyāghra sakhāyaṃ viddhi māmiti
|
upasthitastu drupadaṃ sakhivaccāsmi saṅgataḥ||63||

64sa māṃ nirākāramiva prahasannidamabravīt
|
akṛteyaṃ tava prajñā brahmannātisamañjasā||64||

65yadāttha māṃ tvaṃ prasabhaṃ sakhā te'hamiti dvija
|
saṃgatanīha jīryanti kālena parijīryataḥ||65||

66sauhṛdaṃ me tvayā hyāsītpūrvaṃ sāmarthyabandhanam
|
nāśrotriyaḥ śrotriyasya nārathī rathinaḥ sakhā||66||

67sāmyāddhi sakhyaṃ bhavati vaiṣamyānnopapadyate
|
na sakhyamajaraṃ loke vidyate jātu kasyacit||67||

68kālo vainaṃ viharati krodho vainaṃ rahatyuta
|
maivaṃ jīrṇamupāsva tvaṃ satyaṃ bhavatvapākṛdhi||68||

69āsītsakhyaṃ dvijaśreṣṭha tvayā me'rthanibandhanam
|
na hyanāḍhyaḥ sakhāḍhyasya nāvidvānviduṣaḥ sakhā||69||

70na śūrasya sakhā klībaḥ sakhipūrvaṃ kimiṣyate
|
na hi rājñāmudīrṇānāmevaṃ bhūtairnaraiḥ kvacit||70||

71sakhyaṃ bhavati mandātmañchriyāhīnairdhanacyutaiḥ
|
nāśrotriyaḥ śrotriyasya nārathī rathinaḥ sakhā||71||

72nārājā pārthivasyāpi sakhipūrvaṃ kimiṣyate
|
ahaṃ tvayā na jānāmi rājyārthe saṃvidaṃ kṛtām||72||

73ekarātraṃ tu te brahmankāmaṃ dāsyāmi bhojanam
|
evamuktastvahaṃ tena sadāraḥ prasthitastadā||73||

74tāṃ pratijñāṃ pratijñāya yāṃ kartāsmyacirādiva
|
drupadenaivamukto'haṃ manyunā'bhipariplutaḥ||74||

75abhyāgacchaṃ kurūnbhīṣma śiṣyairarthī guṇānvitaiḥ
|
tato'haṃ bhavataḥ kāmaṃ saṃvardhayitumāgataḥ||75||

76idaṃ nāgapuraṃ ramyaṃ brūhi kiṃ karavāṇi te
|
vaiśaṃpāyana uvāca|
evamuktastadā bhīṣmo bhāradvājamabhāṣata||76||

77bhīṣma uvāca|
apajyaṃ kriyatāṃ cāpaṃ sādhvastraṃ pratipādaya
|
bhuṅkṣva bhogānbhṛśaṃ prītaḥ pūjyamānaḥ kurukṣaye||77||

78kurūṇāmasti yadvittaṃ rājyaṃ cedaṃ sarāṣṭrakam
|
tvameva paramo rājā sarve ca kuravastava||78||

79yacca te prārthitaṃ brahmankṛtaṃ taditi cintyatām
|
diṣṭyā prāpto'si viprarṣe mahānme'nugrahaḥ kṛtaḥ||79||
