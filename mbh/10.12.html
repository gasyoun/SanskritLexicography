Mahābhārata 10.12 (Sauptikaparvan)

1vaiśampāyana uvāca|
tasminprayāte durdharṣe yadūnāmṛṣabhastataḥ
|
abravītpuṇḍarīkākṣaḥ kuntīputraṃ yudhiṣṭhiram||1||

2eṣa pāṇḍava te bhrātā putraśokaparāyaṇaḥ
|
jighāṃsurdroṇimākrande eka evābhidhāvati||2||

3bhīmaḥ priyaste sarvebhyo bhrātṛbhyo bharatarṣabha
|
taṃ kṛcchragatamadya tvaṃ kasmānnābhyupapadyase||3||

4yattadācaṣṭa putrāya droṇaḥ parapurañjayaḥ
|
astraṃ brahmaśiro nāma daheta pṛthivīmapi||4||

5tanmahātmā mahābhāgaḥ ketuḥ sarvadhanuṣmatām
|
pratyapādayadācāryaḥ prīyamāṇo dhanañjayam||5||

6taṃ putro'pyeka evainamanvayācadamarṣaṇaḥ
|
tataḥ provāca putrāya nātihṛṣṭamanā iva||6||

7viditaṃ cāpalaṃ hyāsīdātmajasya durātmanaḥ
|
sarvadharmavidācāryaḥ so'nvaśātsvasutaṃ tataḥ||7||

8paramāpadgatenāpi na sma tāta tvayā raṇe
|
idamastraṃ prayoktavyaṃ mānuṣeṣu viśeṣataḥ||8||

9ityuktavānguruḥ putraṃ droṇaḥ paścādathoktavān
|
na tvaṃ jātu satāṃ mārge sthāteti puruṣarṣabha||9||

10sa tadājñāya duṣṭātmā piturvacanamapriyam
|
nirāśaḥ sarvakalyāṇaiḥ śokātparyacaranmahīm||10||

11tatastadā kuruśreṣṭha vanasthe tvayi bhārata
|
avasaddvārakāmetya vṛṣṇibhiḥ paramārcitaḥ||11||

12sa kadācitsamudrānte vasan dvāravatīmanu
|
eka ekaṃ samāgamya māmuvāca hasanniva||12||

13yattadugraṃ tapaḥ kṛṣṇa caransatyaparākramaḥ
|
agastyādbhāratācāryaḥ pratyapadyata me pitā||13||

14asraṃ brahmaśiro nāma devagandharvapūjitam
|
tadadya mayi dāśārha yathā pitari me tathā||14||

15asmattastadupādāya divyamastraṃ yadūttama
|
mamāpyastraṃ prayaccha tvaṃ cakraṃ ripuhaṇaṃ raṇe||15||

16sa rājanprīyamāṇena mayā'pyuktaḥ kṛtāñjaliḥ
|
yācamānaḥ prayatnena matto'straṃ bharatarṣabha||16||

17devadānavagandharvamanuṣyapatagoragāḥ
|
na samā mama vīryasya śatāṃśenāpi piṇḍitāḥ||17||

18idaṃ dhanuriyaṃ śaktiridaṃ cakramiyaṃ gadā
|
yadyadicchasi cedastraṃ mattastattaddadāmi te||18||

19yacchaknoṣi samudyantuṃ prayoktumapi vā raṇe
|
tadgṛhāṇa vinā'streṇa yanme dātumabhīpsasi||19||

20sa sunābhaṃ sahasrāraṃ vajranābhamayasmayam
|
vavre cakraṃ mahābhāgo mattaḥ spardhanmayā saha||20||

21gṛhāṇa cakramityukto mayā tu tadanantaram
|
jagrāhotpatya sahasā cakraṃ savyena pāṇinā||21||

22na cainamaśakatsthānātsañcālayitumapyuta
|
athainaṃ dakṣiṇenāpi gṛhītumupacakrame||22||

23sarvayatnabalenāpi gṛhṇannevamidaṃ tataḥ
|
tataḥ sarvabalenāpi yadainaṃ na śaśāka ha||23||

24udyantuṃ vā cālayituṃ drauṇiḥ paramadurmanāḥ
|
kṛtvā yatnaṃ pariśrāntaḥ sa nyavartata bhārata||24||

25nivṛttamanasaṃ tasmādabhiprāyādvicetasam
|
ahamāmantrya saṃvigramaśvatthāmānamabruvam||25||

26yaḥ sadaiva manuṣyeṣu pramāṇaṃ paramaṃ gataḥ
|
gāṇḍīvadhanvā śvetāśvaḥ kapipravaraketanaḥ||26||

27yaḥ sākṣāddevadeveśaṃ śitikaṇṭhamumāpatim
|
dvandvayuddhe parājiṣṇustoṣayāmāsa śaṅkaram||27||

28yasmātpriyataro nāsti mamānyaḥ puruṣo bhuvi
|
nādeyaṃ yasya me kiñcidapi dārāḥ sutāstathā||28||

29tenāpi suhṛdā brahmanpārthenākliṣṭakarmaṇā
|
noktapūrvamidaṃ vākyaṃ yastvaṃ māmabhibhāṣase||29||

30brahmacaryaṃ mahadghoraṃ tīrtvā dvādaśavārṣikam
|
himavatpārśvamāsthāya yo mayā tapasā''rjitaḥ||30||

31samānavratacāriṇyāṃ rukmiṇyāṃ yo'nvajāyata
|
sanatkumārastejasvī pradyumno nāma me sutaḥ||31||

32tenāpyetanmahaddivyaṃ cakramapratimaṃ raṇe
|
na prārthitamabhūnmūḍha yadidaṃ prārthitaṃ tvayā||32||

33rāmeṇātibalenaitannoktapūrvaṃ kadācana
|
na gadena na sāmbena yadidaṃ prārthitaṃ tvayā||33||

34dvārakāvāsibhiścānyairvṛṣṇyandhakamahārathaiḥ
|
noktapūrvamidaṃ jātu yadidaṃ prārthitaṃ tvayā||34||

35bhāratācāryaputrastvaṃ mānitaḥ sarvayādavaiḥ
|
cakreṇa rathināṃ śreṣṭha kaṃ nu tāta yuyutsase||35||

36evamukto mayā drauṇirmāmidaṃ pratyuvāca ha
|
prayujya bhavate pūjāṃ yotsye kṛṣṇa tvayā saha||36||

37prārthitaṃ te mayā cakraṃ devadānavapūjitam
|
ajeyaḥ syāmiti vibho satyametadbravīmi te||37||

38tvatto'haṃ durlabhaṃ kāmamanavāpyaiva keśava
|
pratiyāsyāmi govinda śivenābhivadasva mām||38||

39etatsubhīmaṃ bhīmānāmṛṣabheṇa tvayā dhṛtam
|
cakramapraticakreṇa bhūvi nānyo'bhipadyate||39||

40etāvaduktvā drauṇirmāṃ yugyānaśvān dhanāni ca
|
ādāyopayayau kāle ratnāni vividhāni ca||40||

41sa saṃrambhī durātmā ca capalaḥ krūra eva ca
|
veda cāstraṃ brahmaśirastasmādrakṣyo vṛkodaraḥ||41||
