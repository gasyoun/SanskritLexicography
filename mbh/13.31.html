Mahābhārata 13.31 (Anuśāsanaparvan)

1yudhiṣṭhira uvāca|
ke pūjyā vai triloke'sminmānavā bharatarṣabha
|
vistareṇa tadācakṣva na hi tṛṣyāmi kathyataḥ ||1||

2bhīṣma uvāca|
atrāpyudāharantīmamitihāsaṃ purātanam
|
nāradasya ca saṃvādaṃ vāsudevasya cobhayoḥ ||2||

3nāradaṃ prāñjaliṃ dṛṣṭvā pūjayānaṃ dvijarṣabhān
|
keśavaḥ paripapraccha bhagavan kānnamasyasi ||3||

4bahumānaparasteṣu bhagavanyānnamasyasi
|
śakyaṃ cecchrotumasmābhirbrūhyetaddharmavittama ||4||

5nārada uvāca|
śṛṇu govinda yānetān pūjayāmyarimardana
|
tvatto'nyaḥ kaḥ pumā~lloke śrotumetadihārhati ||5||

6varuṇaṃ vāyumādityaṃ parjanyaṃ jātavedasam
|
sthāṇuṃ skandaṃ tathā lakṣmīṃ viṣṇuṃ brahmāṇameva ca ||6||

7vācaspatiṃ candramasamapaḥ pṛthvīṃ sarasvatīm
|
satataṃ ye namasyanti tānnamasyāmyahaṃ vibho ||7||

8tapodhanānvedavido nityaṃ vedaparāyaṇān
|
mahārhānvṛṣṇiśārdūla sadā sampūjayāmyaham ||8||

9abhuktvā devakāryāṇi kurvate ye'vikatthanāḥ
|
santuṣṭāśca kṣamāyuktāstānnamasyāmyahaṃ vibho ||9||

10samyagyajanti ye ceṣṭīḥ kṣāṃtā dāntā jitendriyāḥ
|
satyaṃ dharmaṃ kṣitiṃ gāśca tānnamasyāmi yādava ||10||

11ye vai tapasi vartante vane mūlaphalāśanāḥ
|
asañcayāḥ kriyāvantastānnamasyāmi yādava ||11||

12ye bhṛtyabharaṇe śaktāḥ satataṃ cātithivratāḥ
|
bhuñjate devaśeṣāṇi tānnamasyāmi yādava ||12||

13ye vedaṃ prāpya durdharṣā vāgmino brahmacāriṇaḥ
|
yājanādhyāpane yuktā nityaṃ tānpūjayāmyaham ||13||

14prasannahṛdayāścaiva sarvasattveṣu nityaśaḥ
|
āpṛṣṭhatāpātsvādhyāye yuktāstānpūjayāmyaham ||14||

15guruprasāde svādhyāye yatanto ye sthiravratāḥ
|
śuśrūṣavo'nasūyantastānnamasyāmi yādava ||15||

16suvratā munayo ye ca brāhmaṇāḥ satyasaṃgarāḥ
|
voḍhāro havyakavyānāṃ tānnamasyāmi yādava ||16||

17maikṣyacaryāsu niratāḥ kuśā gurukulāśrayāḥ
|
niḥsukhā nirdhanā ye tu tānnamasyāmi yādava ||17||

18nirmamā niṣpratidvandvā nihrīkā niṣprayojanāḥ
|
ye vedaṃ prāpya dudhairṣā vāgmino brahmavādinaḥ ||18||

19ahiṃsāniratā ye ca ye ca satyavratā narāḥ
|
dāṃtāḥ śamaparāścaiva tānnamasyāmi keśava ||19||

20devatātithi pūjāyāṃ yuktā ye gṛhamedhinaḥ
|
kapotavṛttayo nityaṃ tānnamasyāmi yādava ||20||

21yeṣāṃ trivargaḥ kṛtyeṣu vartate nopahīyate
|
śiṣṭācārapravṛttāśca tānnamasyāmyahaṃ sadā ||21||

22brāhmaṇāḥ śrutasampannā ye trivargamanuṣṭhitāḥ
|
alolupāḥ puṇyaśīlāstānnamasyāmi keśava ||22||

23abbhakṣā vāyubhakṣāśca sudhābhakṣāśca ye sadā
|
vrataiśca vividhairyuktāstānnamasyāmi mādhava ||23||

24ayonīnagniyonīṃśca brahmayonīṃstathaiva ca
|
sarvabhūtātmayonīṃśca tānnamasyāmyahaṃ sadā ||24||

25nityametānnamasyāmi kṛṣṇa lokakarānṛṣīn
|
lokajyeṣṭhānkulajyeṣṭhāṃstamoghnā~llokabhāskarān ||25||

26tasmāttvamapi vārṣṇeya dvijān pūjaya nityadā
|
pūjitāḥ pūjanārhā hi sukhaṃ dāsyanti te'nagha ||26||

27asmin loke sadā hyete paratra ca sukhapradāḥ
|
carante mānyamānā vai pradāsyanti sukhaṃ tava ||27||

28ye sarvātithayo nityaṃ goṣu ca brāhmaṇeṣu ca
|
nityaṃ satye cābhiratā durgāṇyatitaranti te ||28||

29nityaṃ śamaparā ye ca tathā ye cānasūyakāḥ
|
nityasvādhyāyino ye ca durgāṇyatitaranti te ||29||

30sarvāndevānnamasyānti ye caikaṃ vedamāśritāḥ
|
śraddadhānāśca dāntāśca durgāṇyatitaranti te ||30||

31tathaiva viprapravarānnamaskṛtya yatavratāḥ
|
bhavanti ye dānaratā durgāṇyatitaranti te ||31||

32tapasvinaśca ye nityaṃ kaumārabrahmacāriṇaḥ
|
tapasā bhāvitātmāno durgāṇyatitaranti te ||32||

33devatātithibhṛtyānāṃ pitṝṇāṃ cārcane ratāḥ
|
śiṣṭānnabhojino ye ca durgāṇyatitaranti te ||33||

34agnimādhāya vidhivatpraṇatā dhārayanti ye
|
prāptāḥ somāhutiṃ caiva durgāṇyatitaranti te ||34||

35mātāpitrorguruṣu ca samyagvartanti ye sadā
|
yathā tvaṃ vṛṣṇiśārdūletyuktvaivaṃ virarāma saḥ ||35||

36tasmāttvamapi kaunteya pitṛdevadvijātithīn
|
samyakpūjayase nityaṃ gatimiṣṭāmavāpsyasi ||36||
