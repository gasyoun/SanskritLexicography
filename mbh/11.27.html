Mahābhārata 11.27 (Strīparvan)

1vaiśampāyana uvāca|te samāsādya tu gaṅgāṃ tu śivāṃ puṇyajalānvitām
|
hlādinīṃ ca prasannāṃ ca mahārūpāṃ mahābalām||1||

2bhūṣaṇānyuttarīyāṇi veṣṭanānyavamucya ca
|
tataḥ pitṝṇāṃ bhrātṝṇāṃ pautrāṇāṃ svajanasya ca||2||

3putrāṇāmāryakāṇāṃ ca patīnāṃ ca kurustriyaḥ
|
udakaṃ cakrire sarvā rudantyo bhṛśaduḥkhitāḥ||3||

4suhṛdāṃ cāpi dharmajñāḥ pracakruḥ salilakriyāḥ
|
udake kriyamāṇe tu vīrāṇāṃ vīrapatnibhiḥ||4||

5sūpatīrthā'bhavadgaṅgā bhūyo viprasasāra ca
|
tanmahodadhisaṅkāśaṃ nirānandamanutsavam||5||

6vīrapatnībhirākīrṇaṃ gaṅgātīramaśobhata
|
tataḥ kuntī mahārāja sahasā śokakarśitā||6||

7rudatī mandayā vācā putrānvacanamabravīt
|
yaḥ sa vīro maheṣvāso rathayūthapayūthapaḥ||7||

8arjunena jitaḥ saṅkhye vīralakṣaṇalakṣitaḥ
|
yaṃ sūtaputraṃ manyadhvaṃ rādheyamiti pāṇḍavāḥ||8||

9yo vyarājaccamūmadhye divākara iva prabhuḥ
|
pratyayudhyata vaḥ sarvānpurā yaḥ sapadānugān||9||

10duryodhanabalaṃ sarvaṃ yaḥ prakarṣanvyarocata
|
yasya nāsti samo vīrye pṛthivyāmapi pārthivaḥ||10||

11yo'vṛṇīta yaśaḥ śūraḥ prāṇairapi sadā bhuvi
|
karṇasya satyasandhasya saṅgrāmeṣvapalāyinaḥ||11||

12kurudhvamudakaṃ tasya bhrāturakliṣṭakarmaṇaḥ
|
sa hi vaḥ pūrvajo bhrātā bhāskarānmayyajāyata||12||

13kuṇḍalī kavacī śūro divākarasamaprabhaḥ
|
śrutvā tu pāṇḍavāḥ sarve māturvacanamapriyam||13||

14karṇamevānuśocanto bhūyaḥ klāntatarā'bhavan
|
tataḥ sa puruṣavyāghraḥ kuntīputro yudhiṣṭhiraḥ||14||

15uvāca mātaraṃ vīro niḥśvasanniva pannagaḥ
|
yaḥ śarormirdhvajāvarto mahābhujamahāgrahaḥ||15||

16talaśabdānunadito mahārathamahāhladaḥ
|
yasyeṣupātamāsādya nānyastiṣṭheddhanañjayāt||16||

17kathaṃ putro bhavatyāḥ sa devagarbhaḥ purā'bhavat
|
yasya bāhupratāpena tāpitāḥ sarvato vayam||17||

18tamagnimiva vastreṇa kathaṃ chāditavatyasi
|
yasya bāhubalaṃ nityaṃ dhārtarāṣṭrairupāsitam||18||

19upāsitaṃ yathā'smābhirbalaṃ gāṇḍīvadhanvanaḥ
|
bhūmipānāṃ ca sarveṣāṃ balaṃ balavatāṃ varaḥ||19||

20nānyaṃ kuntīsutātkarṇādagṛhṇādrathināṃ rathī
|
sa naḥ prathamajo bhrātā sarvaśastrabhṛtāṃ varaḥ||20||

21asūta taṃ bhavatyagre kathamadbhutavikramam
|
aho bhavatyā mantrasya gūhanena vayaṃ hatāḥ||21||

22nidhanena hi karṇasya pīḍitāstu sabāndhavāḥ
|
abhimanyorvināśena draupadeyavadhena ca||22||

23pāñcālānāṃ vināśena kurūṇāṃ patanena ca
|
tataḥ śataguṇaṃ duḥkhamidaṃ māmaspṛśadbhṛśam||23||

24karṇamevānuśocāpi dahyāmyagnāvivāhitaḥ
|
neha sma kiñcidaprāpya bhavedapi divi sthitam||24||

25na cedaṃ vaiśasaṃ ghoraṃ kauravāntakaraṃ bhavet
|
evaṃ vilapya bahulaṃ dharmarājo yudhiṣṭhiraḥ||25||

26vyarudacchanakai rājaṃścakārāsyodakaṃ prabhuḥ
|
tato vineduḥ sahasā striyastāḥ khalu sarvaśaḥ||26||

27abhito yāḥ sthitāstatra tasminnudakakarmaṇi
|
tata ānāyayāmāsa karṇasya saparicchadāḥ||27||

28striyaḥ kurupatirdhīmānbhrātuḥ premṇā yudhiṣṭhiraḥ
|
sa tābhiḥ saha dharmātmā pretakṛtyamanantaram||28||

29cakāra vidhivaddhīmāndharmarājo yudhiṣṭhiraḥ
|
pāpenāsau mayā śreṣṭho bhrātā jñātirnipātitaḥ||29||

30ato manasi yadguhyaṃ strīṇāṃ tanna bhaviṣyati
|
ityuktvā sa tu gaṅgāyā uttatārākulendriyaḥ
|
bhrātṛbhiḥ sahitaḥ sarvairgaṅgātīramupeyivān||30||
