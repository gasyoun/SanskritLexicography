Mahābhārata 2.48 (Sabhāparvan)

1śakuniruvāca| duryodhana na te'marṣaḥ kāryaḥ prati yudhiṣṭhiram
|
bhāgadheyāni hi svāni pāṇḍavā bhuñjate sadā||1||

2vidhānaṃ vividhākāraṃ paraṃ teṣāṃ vidhānataḥ
|
anekairabhyupāyaiśca tvayā na śakitāḥ purā||2||

3ārabdhāpi mahārāja punaḥ punararindama
|
vimuktāśca naravyāghrā bhāgadheyapuraskṛtāḥ||3||

4tairlabdhā draupadī bhāryā drupadaśca sutaiḥ saha
|
sahāyaḥ pṛthivīpāle vāsudevaśca vīryavān||4||

5labdhaścānabhibhūtārthaiḥ pitryoṃśaḥ pṛthivīpate
|
vivṛddhastejasā teṣāṃ tatra kā paridevanā||5||

6dhanañjayena gāṇḍīvamakṣayyau ca maheṣudhī
|
labdhānyastrāṇi divyāni toṣayitvā hutāśanam||6||

7tena kārmukamukhyena bāhuvīryeṇa cātmanaḥ
|
kṛtā vaśe mahīpālāstatra kā paridevanā||7||

8agnidāhānmayaṃ cāpi mokṣayitvā sa dānavam
|
sabhāṃ tāṃ kārayāmāsa savyasācī parantapaḥ||8||

9tena caiva mayenoktāḥ kiṅkarā nāma rākṣasāḥ
|
vahanti tāṃ sabhāṃ bhīmāstatra kā paridevanā||9||

10yaccāsahāyatāṃ rājannuktavānasi bhārata
|
tanmithyā bhrātaro hīme tava sarve vaśānugāḥ||10||

11droṇastava maheṣvāsaḥ saha putreṇa vīryavān
|
sūtaputraśca rādheyo gautamaśca mahārathaḥ||11||

12ahaṃ ca saha sodaryaiḥ saumadattiśca pārthivaḥ
|
etaistvaṃ sahitaḥ sarvairjaya kṛtsnaṃ vasundharām||12||

13duryodhana uvāca|
tvayā ca sahito rājannetaiścānyairmahārathaiḥ
|
etāneva vijeṣyāmi yadi tvamanumanyase||13||

14eteṣu vijiteṣvadya bhaviṣyati mahī mama
|
sarve ca pṛthivīpālāḥ sabhā sā ca mahādhanā||14||

15śakuniruvāca| dhanañjayo vāsudevo bhīmaseno yudhiṣṭhiraḥ
|
nakulaḥ sahadevaśca drupadaśca sahātmajaiḥ||15||

16naite yudhi parājetuṃ śakyā devagaṇairapi
|
mahārathā maheṣvāsāḥ kṛtāstrā yuddhadurmadāḥ||16||

17ahaṃ tu tadvijānāmi vijetuṃ yena śakyate
|
yudhiṣṭhiraṃ svayaṃ rājaṃstannibodha juṣasva ca||17||

18duryodhana uvāca|
apramādena suhṛdāmanyeṣāṃ ca mahātmanām
|
yadi śakyā vijetuṃ te tanmamācakṣva mātula||18||

19śakuniruvāca| dyūtapriyaśca kaunteyo na sa jānāti devitum
|
samāhūtaśca rājendro na śakṣyati nivartitum||19||

20devane kuśalaścāhaṃ na me'sti sadṛśo bhuvi
|
triṣu lokeṣu kauravya taṃ tvaṃ dyūte samāhvaya||20||

21tasyākṣakuśalo rājannāmasye'hamasaṃśayam
|
rājyaṃ śriyaṃ ca tāṃ dīptāṃ tvadarthaṃ puruṣarṣabha||21||

22idaṃ tu sarvaṃ tvaṃ rājñe duryodhana nivedaya
|
anujñātastu te pitrā vijeṣye tānna saṃśayaḥ||22||

23duryodhana uvāca|
tvameva karumukhyāya dhṛtarāṣṭrāya saubala
|
nivedaya yathānyāyaṃ nāhaṃ śakṣye niveditum||23||
