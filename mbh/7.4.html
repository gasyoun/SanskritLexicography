Mahābhārata 7.4 (Droṇaparvan)

1sañjaya uvāca|
tasya lālapyamānasya kuruvṛddhaḥ pitāmahaḥ
|
deśakālocitaṃ vākyamabravīt prītamānasaḥ||1||

2samudra iva siṃdhūnāṃ jyotiṣāmiva bhāskaraḥ
|
satyasya ca yathā santo bījānāmiva corvarā||2||

3parjanya iva bhūtānāṃ pratiṣṭhā suhṛdāṃ bhava
|
bāndhavāstvānujīvantu sahasrākṣamivāmarāḥ||3||

4mānahā bhava śatrūṇāṃ mitrāṇāṃ nandivardhanaḥ
|
kauravāṇāṃ bhava gatiryathā viṣṇurdivaukasām||4||

5svabāhubalavīryeṇa dhārtarāṣṭrajayaiṣiṇā
|
karṇa rājapuraṃ gatvā kāmbojā nirjitāstvayā||5||

6girivrajagatāścāpi nagnajitpramukhā nṛpāḥ
|
ambaṣṭhāśca videhāśca gāndhārāśca jitāstvayā||6||

7himavaddurganilayāḥ kirātā raṇakarkaśāḥ
|
duryodhanasya vaśagāstvayā karṇa purā kṛtāḥ||7||

8utkalā mekalāḥ pauṇḍrāḥ kaliṅgāndhrāśca saṃyuge
|
niṣādāśca trigartāśca bālhīkāśca jitāstvayā||8||

9tatra tatra ca saṃgrāme duryodhanahitaiṣiṇāḥ
|
bahavaśca jitāḥ karṇa tvayā vīrā mahaujasā||9||

10yathā duryodhanastāta sajñātikulabāndhavaḥ
|
tathā tvamapi sarveṣāṃ kauravāṇāṃ gatirbhava||10||

11śivenābhivadāmi tvāṃ gaccha yuddhyasva śatrubhiḥ
|
anuśādhi kurūn saṅkhye dhatsva duryodhane jayam||11||

12bhavān pautrasamo'smākaṃ yathā duryodhanastathā
|
tavāpi dharmataḥ sarve yathā tasya vayaṃ tathā||12||

13yaunāt saṃbandhakālloke viśiṣṭaṃ saṃgataṃ satām
|
sadbhiḥ saha naraśreṣṭha pravadanti manīṣiṇaḥ||13||

14sa satyasaṅgato bhūtvā mamedamiti niścitaḥ
|
kurūṇāṃ pālaya balaṃ yathā duryodhanastathā||14||

15niśamya vacanaṃ tasya caraṇāvabhivādya ca
|
yayau vaikartanaḥ karṇaḥ samīpaṃ sarvadhanvinām||15||

16so'bhivīkṣya naraughāṇāṃ sthānamapratimaṃ mahat
|
vyūḍhapraharaṇoraskaṃ sainyaṃ tat samabṛṃhayat||16||

17hṛṣitāḥ kuravaḥ sarve duryodhanapurogamāḥ
|
upāgataṃ mahābāhuṃ sarvānīkapuraḥsaram||17||

18karṇaṃ dṛṣṭvā mahātmānaṃ yuddhāya samupasthitam
|
kṣveḍitāsphoṭitaravaiḥ siṃhanādavairapi
|
dhanuḥśabdaiśca vividhaiḥ kuravaḥ samapūjayan||18||
