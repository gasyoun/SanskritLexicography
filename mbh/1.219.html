Mahābhārata 1.219 (Ādiparvan)

1vaiśaṃpāyana uvāca|
tataḥ katipayāhasya tasminraivatake girau
|
vṛṣṇyandhakānāmabhavadutsavo nṛpasattama||1||

2tatra dānaṃ dadurvīrā brāhmaṇebhyaḥ sahasraśaḥ
|
bhojavṛṣṇyandhakāścaiva mahe tasya girestadā||2||

3prasādai ratnacitraiśca girestasya samantataḥ
|
sa deśaḥ śobhito rājankalpavṛkṣaiśca sarvaśaḥ||3||

4vāditrāṇi ca tatrānye vādakāḥ samavādayan
|
nanṛturnartakāścaiva jagurgeyāni gāyanāḥ||4||

5alaṅkṛtāḥ kumārāśca vṛṣṇīnāṃ sumahaujasām
|
yānairhāṭakacitraiśca cañcūryante sma sarvaśaḥ||5||

6paurāśca pādacāreṇa yānairuccāvacaistathā
|
sadārāḥ sānuyātrāśca śataśo'tha sahasraśaḥ||6||

7tato haladharaḥ kṣībo revatīsahitaḥ prabhuḥ
|
anugamyamāno gandharvairacaratratra bhārata||7||

8tathaiva rājā vṛṣṇīnāmugrasenaḥ pratāpavān
|
anugīyamāno gandharvaiḥ strīsahasrasahāyavān||8||

9raukmiṇeyaśca sāmbaśca kṣībau samaradurmadau
|
divyamālyāmbaradharau vijahrāte'marāviva||9||

10akrūraḥ sāraṇaścaiva gado babhrurvidūrathaḥ
|
niśaṭhaścārudeṣṇaśca pṛthurvipṛthureva ca||10||

11satyakaḥ sātyakiścaiva bhaṅgakāramahāravau
|
hārdikya uddhavaścaiva ye cānye nānukīrtitāḥ||11||

12ete parivṛtāḥ strībhirgandharvaiśca pṛthakpṛthak
|
tamutsavaṃ raivatake śobhayāñcakrire tadā||12||

13citrakautūhale tasminvartamāne mahādbhute
|
vāsudevaśca pārthaśca sahitau parijagmatuḥ||13||

14tatra caṅkramamāṇau tau vasudevasutāṃ śubhām
|
alaṅkṛtāṃ sakhīmadhye bhadrāṃ dadṛśatustadā||14||

15dṛṣṭvaiva tāmarjunasya kandarpaḥ samajāyata
|
taṃ tadaikāgramanasaṃ kṛṣṇaḥ pārthamalakṣayat||15||

16abravītpuruṣavyāghraḥ prahasanniva bhārata
|
vanecarasya kimidaṃ kāmenāloḍyate manaḥ||16||

17mamaiṣā bhaginī pārtha sāraṇasya sahodarī
|
subhadrā nāma bhadraṃ te piturme dayitā sutā
|
yadi te vartate buddhirvakṣyāmi pitaraṃ svayam||17||

18arjuna uvāca|
duhitā vasudevasya vāsudevasya ca svasā
|
rūpeṇa caiṣā saṃpannā kamivaiṣā na mohayet||18||

19kṛtameva tu kalyāṇaṃ sarvaṃ mama bhaveddhruvam
|
yadi syānmama vārṣṇeyī mahiṣīyaṃ svasā tava||19||

20prāptau tu ka upāyaḥ syāttaṃ vravīhi janārdana
|
āsthāsyāmi tadā sarvaṃ yadi śakyaṃ nareṇa tat||20||

21vāsudeva uvāca|
svayaṃvaraḥ kṣatriyāṇāṃ vivāhaḥ puruṣarṣabha
|
sa ca saṃśayitaḥ pārtha svabhāvasyānimittataḥ||21||

22prasahya haraṇaṃ cāpi kṣatriyāṇāṃ praśasyate
|
vivāhahetuḥ śūrāṇāmiti dharmavido viduḥ||22||

23sa tvamarjuna kalyāṇīṃ prasahya bhaginīṃ mama
|
hara svayaṃvare hyasyāḥ ko vai veda cikīrṣitam||23||

24tato'rjunaśca kṛṣṇaśca viniścityetikṛtyatām
|
śīghragānpuruṣānanpreṣayāmāsatustadā||24||

25dharmarājāya tatsarvamindraprasthagatāya vai
|
śrutvaiva ca mahābāhuranujajñe sa pāṇḍavaḥ||25||
