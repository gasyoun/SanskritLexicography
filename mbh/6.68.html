Mahābhārata 6.68 (Bhīṣmaparvan)

1bhīṣma uvāca|
śṛṇu cedaṃ mahārāja brahmabhūtaṃ stavaṃ mama
|
brahmarṣibhiśca devaiśca yaḥ purā kathito bhuvi ||1||

2sādhyānāmapi devānāṃ devadeveśvaraḥ prabhuḥ
|
lokabhāvanabhāvajña iti tvāṃ nārado'bravīt ||2||

3bhūtaṃ bhavyaṃ bhaviṣyaṃ ca mārkaṇḍeyo'bhyuvāca ha
|
yajñaṃ tvāṃ caiva yajñānāṃ tapaśca tapasāmapi ||3||

4devānāmapi devaṃ ca tvāmāha bhagavān bhṛguḥ
|
purāṇaṃ caiva paramaṃ viṣṇo rūpaṃ taveti ca ||4||

5vāsudevo vasūnāṃ tvaṃ śakraṃ sthāpayitā tathā
|
deva devo'si devānāmiti dvaipāyano'bravīt ||5||

6pūrve prajānisarge ca dakṣamāhuḥ prajāpatim
|
sraṣṭāraṃ sarvalokānāmaṅgirāstvāṃ tathā'bravīt ||6||

7avyaktaṃ te śarīrotthaṃ vyaktaṃ te manasi sthitam
|
devāstvatsaṃbhavāścaiva devalastvasito'bravīt ||7||

8śirasā te divaṃ vyāptaṃ bāhubhyāṃ pṛthivī tathā
|
jaṭharaṃ te trayo lokāḥ puruṣo'si sanātanaḥ ||8||

9evaṃ tvāmabhijānanti tapasā bhāvitā narāḥ
|
ātmadarśanatṛptānāmṛṣīṇāṃ cāsi sattamaḥ ||9||

10rājarṣīṇāmudārāṇāmāhaveṣvanivartinām
|
sarvadharmapradhānānāṃ tvaṃ gatirmadhusūdana ||10||

11iti nityaṃ yogavidbhirbhagavān puruṣottamaḥ
|
sanatkumārapramukhaiḥ stūyate'bhyarcyate hariḥ ||11||

12eṣa te vistarastāta saṅkṣepaśca prakīrtitaḥ
|
keśavasya yathātattvaṃ suprīto bhaja keśavam ||12||

13sañjaya uvāca|
puṇyaṃ śrutvaitadākhyānaṃ mahārāja sutastava
|
keśavaṃ bahu mene sa pāṇḍavāṃśca mahārathān ||13||

14tamabravīnmahārāja bhīṣmaḥ śāntanavaḥ punaḥ
|
māhātmyaṃ te śrutaṃ rājan keśavasya mahātmanaḥ ||14||

15narasya ca yathātattvaṃ yanmāṃ tvaṃ pṛcchase nṛpa
|
yadarthaṃ nṛṣu saṃbhūtau naranārāyaṇāvṛṣī ||15||

16avadhyau ca yathā vīrau saṃyugeṣvaparājitau
|
yathā ca pāṇḍavā rājannavadhyā yudhi kasyacit ||16||

17prītimān hi dṛḍhaṃ kṛṣṇaḥ pāṇḍaveṣu yaśasviṣu
|
tasmādbravīmi rājendra śamo bhavatu pāṇḍavaiḥ ||17||

18pṛthivīṃ bhuṅkṣva sahito bhrātṛbhirbalibhirvaśī
|
naranārāyaṇau devāvavajñāya na śiṣyasi ||18||

19evamuktvā tava pitā tūṣṇīmāsīdviśāṃpate
|
vyasarjayacca rājānaṃ śayanaṃ ca viveśa ha ||19||

20rājā ca śibiraṃ prāyāt praṇipatya mahātmane
|
śiśye ca śayane śubhre rātriṃ tāṃ bharatarṣabha ||20||
