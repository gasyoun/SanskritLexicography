Mahābhārata 5.167 (Udyogaparvan)

1bhīṣma uvāca|
śakunirmātulaste'sau ratha eko narādhipa
|
prayujya pāṇḍavairvairaṃ yotsyate nātra saṃśayaḥ ||1||

2etasya senā durdharṣā samare pratiyāyinaḥ
|
vikṛtāyudhabhūyiṣṭhā vāyuvegasamā jave ||2||

3droṇaputro maheṣvāsaḥ sarvanivātidhanvinaḥ
|
samare citrayodhī ca dṛḍhāstraśca mahārathaḥ ||3||

4etasya hi mahārāja yathā gāṇḍīvadhanvanaḥ
|
śarāsanavinirmuktāḥ saṃsaktā yānti sāyakāḥ ||4||

5naiṣa śakyo mayā vīraḥ saṃkhyātuṃ rathasattamaḥ
|
nirdahedapi lokāṃstrīnicchanneṣa mahārathaḥ ||5||

6krodhastejaśca tapasā saṃbhṛto''śramavāsinām
|
droṇenānugṛhītaśca divyairastrairudāradhīḥ ||6||

7doṣastvasya mahāneko yenaiva bharatarṣabha
|
na me ratho nātiratho mataḥ pārthivasattamaḥ ||7||

8jīvitaṃ priyamatyarthamāyuṣkāmaḥ sadā dvijaḥ
|
na hyasya sadṛśaḥ kaścidubhayoḥ senayorapi ||8||

9hanyādekarathenaiva devānāmapi vāhinīm
|
vapuṣmāṃstalaghoṣeṇa sphoṭayedapi parvatān ||9||

10asaṃkhyeyaguṇo vīraḥ prahantā dāruṇadyutiḥ
|
daṇḍapāṇirivāsahyaḥ kālavat pracariṣyati ||10||

11yugāntāgnisamaḥ krodhāt siṃhagrīvo mahādyutiḥ
|
eṣa bhāratayuddhasya pṛṣṭhaṃ saṃśamayiṣyati ||11||

12pitā tvasya mahātejā vṛddho'pi yuvabhirvaraḥ
|
raṇe karma mahat kartā atra me nāsti saṃśayaḥ ||12||

13astravegāniloddhūtaḥ senākakṣendhanotthitaḥ
|
pāṇḍuputrasya sainyāni pradhakṣyati raṇe dhṛtaḥ ||13||

14rathayūthapayūthānāṃ yūthapo'yaṃ nararṣabhaḥ
|
bhāradvājātmajaḥ kartā karma tīvraṃ hitaṃ tava ||14||

15sarvamūrdhābhiṣiktānāmācāryaḥ sthaviro guruḥ
|
gacchedantaṃ sṛñjayānāṃ priyastvasya dhanañjayaḥ ||15||

16naiṣa jātu maheṣvāsaḥ pārthamakliṣṭakāriṇam
|
hanyādācāryakaṃ dīptaṃ saṃsmṛtya guṇanirjitam ||16||

17ślāghate'yaṃ sadā vīra pārthasya guṇavistaraiḥ
|
putrādabhyadhikaṃ cainaṃ bhāradvājo'nupaśyatti ||17||

18hanyādekarathenaiva devagandharvamānuṣān
|
ekībhūtānapi raṇe divyairastraiḥ pratāpavān ||18||

19pauravo rājaśārdūlastava rājan mahārathaḥ
|
mato mama rathodāraḥ paravīrarathārujaḥ ||19||

20svena sainyena mahatā pratapan śatruvāhinīm
|
pradhakṣyati sa pañcālān kakṣamagnigatiryathā ||20||

21satyaśravā rathastveko rājaputro bṛhadvalaḥ
|
tava rājan ripubale kālavat pracariṣyati ||21||

22etasya yodhā rājendra vicitrakavacāyudhāḥ
|
vicariṣyanti saṅgrāme nighnantaḥ śātravāṃstava ||22||

23vṛṣaseno rathaste'gryaḥ karṇaputro mahārathaḥ
|
pradhakṣyati ripūṇāṃ te balaṃ tu balināṃ varaḥ ||23||

24jalasandho mahātejā rājan rathavarastava
|
tyakṣyate samare prāṇān mādhavaḥ paravīrahā ||24||

25eṣa yotsyati saṅgrāme gajaskandhaviśāradaḥ
|
rathena vā mahābāhuḥ kṣapayan śatruvāhinīm ||25||

26ratha eṣa mahārāja mato me rājasattama
|
tvadarthe tyakṣyate prāṇān sahasainyo mahāraṇe ||26||

27eṣa vikrāntayodhī ca citrayodhī ca saṅgare
|
vītabhīścāpi te rājan śatrubhiḥ saha yotsyate ||27||

28bāhlīko'tirathaścaiva samare cānivartanaḥ
|
mama rājanmato yuddhe śūro vaivasvatopamaḥ ||28||

29na hyeṣa samaraṃ prāpya nivarteta kathañcana
|
yathā satatago rājan sa hi hanyāt parān raṇe ||29||

30senāpatirmahārāja satyavāṃste mahārathaḥ
|
raṇeṣvadbhutakarmā ca rathī pararathārujaḥ ||30||

31etasya samaraṃ dṛṣṭvā na vyathā'sti kathañcana
|
utsmayannutpatatyeṣa parān rathapatha sthitān ||31||

32eṣa cāriṣu vikrāntaḥ karma satpuruṣocitam
|
kartā vimarde sumahattvadarthe puruṣottamaḥ ||32||

33alambuṣo rākṣasendraḥ krūrakarmā mahārathaḥ
|
haniṣyati parān rājan pūrvavairamanusmaran ||33||

34eṣa rākṣasasainyānāṃ sarveṣāṃ rathasattamaḥ
|
māyāvī dṛḍhavairaśca samare vicariṣyatti ||34||

35prāgjyotiṣādhipo vīro bhagadattaḥ pratāpavān
|
gajāṅkuśadharaśreṣṭho rathe caiva viśāradaḥ ||35||

36etena yuddhamabhavat purā gāṇḍīvadhanvanaḥ
|
divasān subahun rājannubhayorjayagṛddhinoḥ||36||

37tataḥ sakhāyaṃ gāndhāre mānayan pākaśāsanam
|
akarot saṃvidaṃ tena pāṇḍavena mahātmanā ||37||

38eṣa yotsyati saṃgrāme gajaskandhaviśāradaḥ
|
airāvatagato rājā devānāmiva vāsavaḥ ||38||
