Mahābhārata 1.33 (Ādiparvan)

1sautiruvāca|
jāmbūnadamayo bhūtvā marīcinikarojjvalaḥ
|
praviveśa balātpakṣī vārivega ivārṇavam||1||

2sa cakraṃ kṣuraparyantamapaśyadamṛtāntike
|
paribhramantamaniśaṃ tīkṣṇadhāramayasmayam||2||

3jvalanārkaprabhaṃ ghoraṃ chedanaṃ somahāriṇām
|
ghorarūpaṃ tadatyarthaṃ yantraṃ devaiḥ sunirmitam||3||

4tasyāntaraṃ sa dṛṣṭvaiva paryavartata khecaraḥ
|
arāntareṇābhyapatatsaṃkṣipyāṅgaṃ kṣaṇena ha||4||

5adhaścakrasya caivātra dīptānalasamadyutī
|
vidyujjihvau mahāvīryau dīptāsyau dīptalocanau||5||

6cakṣurviṣau mahāghorau nityaṃ kruddhau tarasvinau
|
rakṣārthamevāmṛtasyaiva dadarśa bhujagottamau||6||

7sadā saṃrabdhanayanau sadā cānimiṣekṣaṇau
|
tayoreko'pi yaṃ paśyetsa tūrṇaṃ bhasmasādbhavet||7||

8tayoścakṣūṃṣi rajasā suparṇaḥ sahasā''vṛṇot
|
tābhyāmadṛṣṭarūpo'sau sarvataḥ samatāḍayat||8||

9tayoraṅge samākramya vainateyo'ntarikṣagaḥ
|
ācchinattarasā madhye somamabhyadravattataḥ||9||

10samutpāṭyāmṛtaṃ tatra vainateyastato balī
|
utpapāta javenaiva yantramunmathya vīryavān||10||

11apītvaivā'mṛtaṃ pakṣī parigṛhyāśuniḥsṛtaḥ
|
āgacchadapariśrānta āvāryārkaprabhāṃ tataḥ||11||

12viṣṇunā ca tadākāśe vainateyaḥ sameyivān
|
tasya nārāyaṇastuṣṭastenālaulyena karmaṇā||12||

13tamuvācā'vyayo devo varado'smīti khecaram
|
sa vavre tava tiṣṭheyamuparītyantarikṣagaḥ||13||

14uvāca cainaṃ bhūyo'pi nārāyaṇamidaṃ vacaḥ
|
ajaraścāmaraśca syāmamṛtena vinā'pyaham||14||

15evamastviti taṃ viṣṇuruvāca vinatāsutam
|
pratigṛhya vanau tau ca garuḍo viṣṇumabravīt||15||

16bhavate'pi varaṃ dadyāṃ vṛṇotu bhagavānapi
|
taṃ vavre vāhanaṃ viṣṇurnarutmantaṃ mahābalam||16||

17dhvajaṃ ca cakre bhagavānupari sthāsyasīti tam
|
evamastviti taṃ devamuktvā nārāyaṇaṃ khagaḥ||17||

18vavrāja tarasā vegādvāyuṃ spardhanmahājavaḥ
|
taṃ vrajantaṃ khagaśreṣṭhaṃ vajreṇendro'bhyatāḍayat||18||

19harantamamṛtaṃ roṣādgaruḍaṃ pakṣiṇāṃ varam
|
tamuvācendramākrande garuḍaḥ patatāṃ varaḥ||19||

20prahasañślakṣṇayā vācā tathā vajrasamāhataḥ
|
ṛṣermānaṃ kariṣyāmi vajraṃ yasyāsthisaṃbhavam||20||

21vajrasya ca kariṣyāmi tavaiva ca śatakrato
|
etatpatraṃ tyajāmyekaṃ yasyāntaṃ nopalapsyase||21||

22na ca vajranipātena rujā me'stīha kācana
|
evamuktvā tataḥ putramutsasarja sa pakṣirāṭ||22||

23tadutsṛṣṭamabhiprekṣya tasya parṇamanuttamam
|
hṛṣṭāni sarvabhūtāni nāma cakrurgarutmataḥ||23||

24surūpaṃ patramālakṣya suparṇo'yaṃ bhavatviti
|
taddṛṣṭvā mahadāścaryaṃ sahasrākṣaḥ puraṃdaraḥ
|
khago mahadidaṃ bhūtamiti matvā'bhyabhāṣata||24||

25śakra uvāca| balaṃ vijñātumicchāmi yatte paramanuttamam
|
sakhyaṃ cānantamicchāmi tvayā saha khagottama||25||
