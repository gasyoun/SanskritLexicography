Mahābhārata 7.162 (Droṇaparvan)

1sañjaya uvāca|
somadattaṃ tu saṃprekṣya vidhunvānaṃ mahaddhanuḥ
|
sātyakiḥ prāha yantāraṃ somadattāya māṃ vaha||1||

2na hyahatvā raṇe śatraṃ somadattaṃ mahābalam
|
nivartiṣye raṇātsūta satyametadvaco mama||2||

3tataḥ saṃpraiṣayadyantā saindhavāṃstānmanojavān
|
turaṅgamāñchaṅkhavarṇānsarvaśabdātigān raṇe||3||

4te'vahan yuyudhānaṃ tu manomārutaraṃhasaḥ
|
yathendraṃ harayo rājanpurā daityavadhodyatam||4||

5tamāpatantaṃ saṃprekṣya sātvataṃ rabhasaṃ raṇe
|
somadatto mahābāhurasaṃbhrānto nyavartata||5||

6vimuñcañcharavarṣāṇi parjanya iva vṛṣṭimān
|
chādayāmāsa śaineyaṃ jalado bhāskaraṃ yathā||6||

7asaṃbhrāntaśca samare sātyakiḥ kurupuṅgavam
|
chā‌yāmāsa bāṇaughaiḥ samantādbharatarṣabha||7||

8somada‌ttastu taṃ ṣaṣṭhyā vivyādhorasi mādhavam
|
sātyakiścāpi taṃ rājannavidhyatsāyakaiḥ śitaiḥ||8||

9tāvanyonyaṃ śaraiḥ kṛttau vyarājetāṃ nararṣabhau
|
supuṣpau puṣpasamaye puṣpitāviva kiṃśukau||9||

10rudhirokṣitasarvāṃgau kuruvṛṣṇiyaśaskarau
|
parasparamavekṣetāṃ dahantāviva locanaiḥ||10||

11rathamaṇḍalamārgeṣu carantāvarimardanau
|
ghorarūpau hitāvāstāṃ vṛṣṭimantāvivāṃbudau||11||

12śarasaṃbhinnagātrau tu sarvataḥ śakalīkṛtau
|
śvāvidhāviva rājendra dṛśyetāṃ śaravikṣatau||12||

13suvarṇapuṃkhairiṣubhirācitau tau vyarājatām
|
khadyotairāvṛtau rājanprāvṛṣīva vanaspatī||13||

14saṃpradīpitasarvāṅgau sāyakaistairmahārathau
|
adṛśyetāṃ raṇe kruddhāvulkābhiriva kuñjarau||14||

15tato yudhi mahārāja somadatto mahārathaḥ
|
ardhacandreṇa ciccheda mādhavasya mahaddhanuḥ||15||

16athainaṃ pañcaviṃśatyā sāyakānāṃ samārpayat
|
tvaramāṇastvarākāle punaśca daśabhiḥ śaraiḥ||16||

17athānyaddhanurādāya sātyakirvegavattaram
|
pañcabhiḥ sāyakaistūrṇaṃ somadattamavidhyata||17||

18tato'pareṇa bhallena dhvajaṃ ciccheda kāñcanam
|
bāhlīkasya raṇe rājansātyakiḥ prahasanniva||18||

19somadattastvasaṃbhrānto dṛṣṭvā ketuṃ nipātitam
|
śaineyaṃ pañcaviṃśatyā sāyakānāṃ samācinot||19||

20sātvato'pi raṇe kruddhaḥ somadattasya dhanvinaḥ
|
dhanuściccheda bhallena kṣurapreṇa śitena ha||20||

21athainaṃ rukmapuṅkhānāṃ śatena nataparvaṇām
|
ācinodbahudhā rājanbhagnadaṃṣṭramiva dvipam||21||

22athānyaddhanurādāya somadatto mahārathaḥ
|
sātyakiṃ chā‌dayāmāsa śaravṛṣṭyā mahābalaḥ||22||

23somadattaṃ tu saṃkruddho raṇe vivyādha sātyakiḥ
|
sātyakiṃ śarajālena somada‌tto'pyapīḍayat||23||

24daśabhiḥ sātvatasyārthe bhīmo'hanbāhlikātmajam
|
somadatto'pyasaṃbhrānto bhīmamārcchacchitaiḥ śaraiḥ||24||

25tatastu sātvatasyārthe bhīmaseno navaṃ dṛḍham
|
mumoca parighaṃ ghoraṃ somadattasya vakṣasi||25||

26tamāpatantaṃ vegena parighaṃ ghoradarśanam
|
dvidhā ciccheda samare prahasanniva kauravaḥ||26||

27sa papāta dvidhā cchinna āyasaḥ parigho mahān
|
mahīdharasyeva mahacchikharaṃ vajradāritam||27||

28tatastu sātyakī rājansomadattasya saṃyuge
|
dhanuściccheda bhallena hastāvāpaṃ ca pañcabhiḥ||28||

29tataścaturbhiśca śaraistūrṇaṃ tāṃsturagottamān
|
samīpaṃ preṣayāmāsa pretarājasya bhārata||29||

30sāratheśca śiraḥ kāyādbhallena nataparvaṇā
|
jahāra naraśārdūlaḥ prahasañchinipuṅgavaḥ||30||

31tataḥ śaraṃ mahāghoraṃ jvalantamiva pāvakam
|
mumoca sātvato rājansvarṇapuṅkhaṃ śilāśitam||31||

32sa vimukto balavatā śaineyena śarottamaḥ
|
ghorastasyorasi vibho nipapātāśu bhārata||32||

33so'tividdho mahārāja sātvatena mahārathaḥ
|
somada‌tto mahābāhurnipapāta mamāra ca||33||

34taṃ dṛṣṭvā nihataṃ tatra somadattaṃ mahārathāḥ
|
mahatā śaravarṣeṇa yuyudhānamupādravan||34||

35chādyamānaṃ śarairdṛṣṭvā yuyudhānaṃ yudhiṣṭhiraḥ
|
pāṇḍavāśca mahārāja saha sarvaiḥ prabhadrakaiḥ
|
mahatyā senayā sārdhaṃ droṇānīkamupādravan||35||

36tato yudhiṣṭhiraḥ kuddhastāvakānāṃ mahābalam
|
śarairvidrāvayāmāsa bhāradvājasya paśyataḥ||36||

37sainyāni drāvayantaṃ tu droṇo dṛṣṭvā yudhiṣṭhiram
|
abhidudrāva vegena krodhasaṃraktalocanaḥ||37||

38tataḥ suniśitairbāṇaiḥ pārthaṃ vivyādha saptabhiḥ
|
yudhiṣṭhiro'pi saṃkruddhaḥ prativivyādha pañcabhiḥ||38||

39so'tividdho mahābāhuḥ sṛkkiṇī parisaṃlihan
|
yudhiṣṭhirasya ciccheda dhvajaṃ kārmukameva ca||39||

40sa cchinnadhanvā tvaritastvarākāle nṛpottamaḥ
|
anyadādatta vegena kārmukaṃ samare dṛḍham||40||

41tataḥ śarasahastreṇa droṇaṃ vivyādha pārthivaḥ
|
sāśvasūtadhvajarathaṃ tadadbhutamivābhavat||41||

42tato muhūrtaṃ vyathitaḥ śarapātaprapīḍitaḥ
|
niṣasāda rathopasthe droṇo bharatasattama||42||

43pratilabhya tataḥ saṃjñāṃ muhūrtāddvijasattamaḥ
|
krodhena mahatā''viṣṭo vāyavyāstramavāsṛjat||43||

44asaṃbhrāntastataḥ pārtho dhanurākṛṣya vīryavān
|
tatastadastramastreṇa staṃbhayāmāsa bhārata||44||

45ciccheda ca dhanurdīrghaṃ brāhmaṇasya ca pāṇḍavaḥ
|
tato'nyaddhanurādatta droṇaḥ kṣatriyamardanaḥ||45||

46tadapyasya śitairbhallaiściccheda kurupuṅgavaḥ
|
tato'bravīdvāsudevaḥ kuntīputraṃ yudhiṣṭhiram||46||

47yudhiṣṭhira mahābāho yattvāṃ vakṣyāmi tacchṛṇu
|
upāramasva yuddhe tvaṃ droṇādbharatasattama||47||

48yatate hi sadā droṇo grahaṇe tava saṃyuge
|
nānurūpamahaṃ manye yuddhamasya tvayā saha||48||

49yo'sya sṛṣṭo vināśāya sa evainaṃ haniṣyati
|
parivarjya guruṃ yāhi yatra rājā suyodhanaḥ||49||

50rājā rājñā hi yoddhavyo nārājñā yuddhamiṣyate
|
tatra tvaṃ gaccha kaunteya hastyaśvarathasaṃvṛtaḥ||50||

51yāvanmātreṇa ca mayā sahāyena dhanañjayaḥ
|
bhīmaśca rathaśārdūlo yudhyate kauravaiḥ saha||51||

52vāsudevavacaḥ śrutvā dharmarājo yudhiṣṭhiraḥ
|
muhūrtaṃ cintayitvā tu tato dāruṇamāhavam||52||

53prāyāddrutamamitraghno yatra bhīmo vyavasthitaḥ
|
vinighnaṃstāvakānyodhānvyāditāsya ivāntakaḥ||53||

54rathaghoṣeṇa mahatā nādayanvasudhātalam
|
parjanya iva gharmānte nādayanvai diśo daśa||54||

55bhīmasya nighnataḥ śatrūn pārṣṇiṃ jagrāha pāṇḍavaḥ
|
droṇo'pi pāṇḍupañcālān vyadhamadrajanīmukhe||55||
