Mahābhārata 12.312 (Śāntiparvan)

1yājñavalkya uvāca |
tattvānāṃ sarvasaṃkhyā ca kālasaṃkhyā tathaiva ca
|
mayā proktā''nupūrvyeṇa saṃhāramapi me śṛṇu||1||

2yathā saṃharate jantūnsasarja ca punaḥ punaḥ
|
anādinidhano brahmā nityaścākṣara eva ca||2||

3ahaḥkṣayamatho buddhvā niśi svapnamanāstathā
|
codayāmāsa bhagavānavyakto'haṃkṛtaṃ naram||3||

4tataḥ śatasahasrāṃśuravyaktenābhicoditaḥ
|
kṛtvā dvādaśadhā''tmānamādityo jvaladagnivat||4||

5caturvidhaṃ mahīpāla nirdahatyāśu tejasā
|
jarāyujāṇḍajasvedajodbhijjaṃ ca narādhipa||5||

6etadunmeṣamātreṇa vinaṣṭaṃ sthāṇu jaṅgamam
|
kūrmapṛṣṭhasamā bhūmirbhavatyatha samantataḥ||6||

7jagaddagdhvā'mitabalaḥ kevalāṃ jagatīṃ tataḥ
|
aṃbhasā balinā kṣipramāpūrayati sarvaśaḥ||7||

8tataḥ kālāgnimāsādya tadambho yāti saṃkṣayam
|
vinaṣṭe'mbhasi rājendra jājvalatyanalo mahān||8||

9tamaprameyo'tibalaṃ jvalamānaṃ vibhāvasum
|
ūṣmāṇaṃ sarvabhūtānāṃ saptārciṣamathāñjasā||9||

10bhakṣayāmāsa bhagavān vāyuraṣṭātmako balī
|
vicarannamitaprāṇastiryagūrdhvamadhastathā||10||

11tamapratibalaṃ bhīmamākāśaṃ grasate''tmanā
|
ākāśamapyabhinadanmano grasati cādhikam||11||

12mano grasati bhūtātmā so'haṃkāraḥ prajāpatiḥ
|
ahaṃkāro mahānātmā bhūtabhavyabhaviṣyavit||12||

13tamapyanupamātmānaṃ viśvaṃ śaṃbhuḥ prajāpatiḥ
|
aṇimā laghimā prāptirīśāno jyotiravyayaḥ||13||

14sarvata:pāṇipādāntaḥ sarvatokṣiśiromukhaḥ
|
sarvataḥśrutimā~lloke sarvamāvṛtya tiṣṭhati||14||

15hṛdayaṃ sarvabhūtānāṃ parvaṇāṅguṣṭhamātrakaḥ
|
atha grasatyananto hi mahātmā viśvamīśvaraḥ||15||

16tataḥ samabhavatsarvamakṣayāvyayamavraṇam
|
bhūtabhavyabhaviṣyāṇāṃ sraṣṭāramanaghaṃ tathā||16||

17eṣo'pyayaste rājendra yathāvatsamudāhṛtaḥ
|
adhyātmamadhibhūtaṃ ca adhidaivaṃ ca śrūyatām||17||
