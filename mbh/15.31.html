Mahābhārata 15.31 (Āśramavāsikaparvan)

1vyāsa uvāca|
bhadre drakṣyasi gāndhāri putrān bhrātṝn sakhīṃstathā
|
vadhūśca patibhiḥ sārdhaṃ niśi suptotthitā iva||1||

2karṇaṃ drakṣyati kuntī ca saubhadraṃ cāpi yādavī
|
draupadī pañca putrāṃśca pitṝnbhrātṝṃstathaiva ca||2||

3pūrvamevaiṣa hṛdaye vyavasāyo'bhavanmama
|
yadāsmi codito rājñā bhavatyā pṛthayaiva ca||3||

4na te śocyā mahātmānaḥ sarva eva nararṣabhāḥ
|
kṣatradharmaparāḥ santastathā hi nidhanaṃ gatāḥ||4||

5bhavitavyamavaśyaṃ tatsurakāryamanindite
|
avaterustataḥ sarve devabhāgā mahītalam||5||

6gandharvāpsarasaścaiva piśācā guhmarākṣasāḥ
|
tathā puṇyajanāścaiva siddhā devarṣayo'pi ca||6||

7devāśca dānavāścaiva tathā devarṣayo'malāḥ
|
ta ete nidhanaṃ prāptāḥ kurukṣetre raṇājire||7||

8gandharvarājo yo dhīmān dhṛtarāṣṭra iti śrutaḥ
|
sa eva mānuṣe loke dhṛtarāṣṭraḥ patistava||8||

9pāṇḍuṃ marudgaṇādviddhi viśiṣṭatamamacyutam
|
dharmasyāṃśo'bhavatkṣattā rājā caiva yudhiṣṭhiraḥ||9||

10kaliṃ duryodhanaṃ viddhi śakuniṃ dvāparaṃ tathā
|
duḥśāsanādīnviddhi tvaṃ rākṣasāśubhadarśane||10||

11marudgaṇādbhīmasenaṃ balavantamarindamam
|
viddhi tvaṃ tu naramṛṣimimaṃ pārthaṃ dhanañjayam||11||

12nārāyaṇaṃ hṛṣīkeśamaśvinau yamajau tathā
|
yaḥ sa vai pārthamudbhūtaḥ saharṣajananastathā||12||

13yaśca pāṇḍavadāyādo hataḥ ṣaḍbhirmahārathaiḥ
|
sa soma iha sobhadro yogādevābhavaddvidhā||13||

14dvidhā kṛtvā''tmano dehamādityaṃ tapatāṃ varam
|
lokāṃśca tāpayānaṃ vai viddhi karṇaṃ ca śobhane||14||

15draupadyā saha sambhūtaṃ dhṛṣṭadyumnaṃ ca pāvakāt
|
agnerbhāgaṃ śubhaṃ viddhi rākṣasaṃ tu śikhaṇḍinam||15||

16droṇaṃ bṛhaspaterbhāgaṃ viddhi drauṇiṃ ca rudrajam
|
bhīṣmaṃ ca viddhi gāṅgeyaṃ vasuṃ mānuṣatāṃ gatam||16||

17evamete mahāprajñe devā mānuṣyametya hi
|
tataḥ punargatāḥ svargaṃ kṛte karmaṇi śobhane||17||

18yacca vai hṛdi sarveṣāṃ duḥkhametacciraṃ sthitam
|
tadadya vyapaneṣyāmi paralokakṛtādbhayāt||18||

19sarve bhavanto gacchantu nadīṃ bhāgīrathīṃ prati
|
tatra drakṣyatha tānsarvān ye hatāsmin raṇājire||19||

20vaiśampāyana uvāca|
iti vyāsasya vacanaṃ śrutvā sarvo janastadā
|
mahatā siṃhanādena gaṅgāmabhimukho yayau||20||

21dhṛtarāṣṭrāśca sāmātyaḥ prayayau saha pāṇḍavaiḥ
|
sahito muniśārdūlairgandharvaiśca samāgataiḥ||21||

22tato gaṅgāṃ samāsādya krameṇa sa janārṇavaḥ
|
nivāsamakarotsarvo yathāprīti yathāsukham||22||

23rājā ca pāṇḍavaiḥ sārdhamiṣṭe deśe sahānugaḥ
|
nivāsamakaroddhīmān sastrīvṛddhapuraḥsaraḥ||23||

24jagāma tadahaścāpi teṣāṃ varṣaśataṃ yathā
|
niśāṃ pratīkṣamāṇānāṃ didṛkṣūṇāṃ mṛtānnṛpān||24||

25atha puṇyaṃ girivaramastamabhyagamadraviḥ
|
tataḥ kṛtābhiṣekāste naiśaṃ karma samācaran||25||
