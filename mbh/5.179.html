Mahābhārata 5.179 (Udyogaparvan)

1bhīṣma uvāca|
tamahaṃ smayanniva raṇe pratyabhāṣaṃ vyavasthitam
|
bhūmiṣṭhaṃ notsahe yoddhuṃ bhavantaṃ rathamāsthitaḥ ||1||

2āroha syandanaṃ vīra kavacaṃ ca mahābhuja
|
badhāna samare rāma yadi yoddhuṃ mayecchasi ||2||

3tato māmabravīdrāmaḥ smayamāno raṇājire
|
ratho me medinī bhīṣma vāhā vedāḥ sadaśvavat ||3||

4sutaśca mātariśvā vai kavacaṃ vedamātaraḥ
|
susaṃvīto raṇe tābhiryotsye'haṃ kurunandana ||4||

5evaṃ bruvāṇo gāndhāre rāmo māṃ satyavikramaḥ
|
śaravrātena mahatā sarvataḥ pratyavārayat ||5||

6tato'paśyaṃ jāmadagnyaṃ rathamadhye vyavasthitam
|
sarvāyudhavare śrīmatyadbhutopamadarśane ||6||

7manasā vihite puṇye vistīrṇe nagaropame
|
divyāśvayuji sannaddhe kāñcanena vibhūṣite ||7||

8kavacena mahābāho somārkakṛtalakṣmaṇā
|
dhanurdharo baddhatūṇo baddhagodhāṅgulitravān ||8||

9sārathyaṃ kṛtavāṃstatra yuyutsorakṛtavraṇaḥ
|
sakhā vedavidatyantaṃ dayito bhārgavasya ha ||9||

10āhvayānaḥ sa māṃ yuddhe mano harṣayatīva me
|
punaḥ punarabhikrośannabhiyāhīti bhārgavaḥ ||10||

11tamādityamivodyantamanādhṛṣyaṃ mahābalam
|
kṣatriyāntakaraṃ rāmamekamekaḥ samāsadam ||11||

12tato'haṃ bāṇapāteṣu triṣu vāhānnigṛhya vai
|
avatīrya dhanurnyasya padātirṛṣisattamam ||12||

13abhyāgacchaṃ tadā rāmamarciṣyan dvijasattamam
|
abhivādya cainaṃ vidhivada‌bruvaṃ vākyamuttamam ||13||

14yotsye tvayā raṇe rāma sadṛśenādhikena vā
|
guruṇā dharmaśīlena jayamāśāsva me vibho ||14||

15rāma uvāca|
evametat kuruśreṣṭha kartavyaṃ bhūtimicchatā
|
dharmo hyeṣa mahābāho viśiṣṭaiḥ saha yudhyatām ||15||

16śapeyaṃ tvāṃ na cedevamāgaccheyā viśāṃpate
|
yudhyasva tvaṃ raṇe yatto dhairyamālambya kaurava ||16||

17na tu te jayamāśāse tvāṃ vijetumahaṃ sthitaḥ
|
gaccha yudhyasva dharmeṇa prīto'smi caritena te ||17||

18tato'haṃ taṃ namaskṛtya rathamāruhya satvaraḥ
|
prādhmāpayaṃ raṇe śaṅkhaṃ punarhemapariṣkṛtam ||18||

19tato yuddhaṃ samabhavanmama tasya ca bhārata
|
divasān subahūn rājan parasparajigīṣayā ||19||

20sa me tasmin raṇe pūrvaṃ prāharat kaṅkapatribhiḥ
|
ṣaṣṭyā śataiśca navabhiḥ śarāṇāṃ nataparvaṇām ||20||

21catvārastena me vāhāḥ sutaścaiva viśāmpate
|
pratiruddhāstathaivāhaṃ samare daṃśitaḥ sthitaḥ ||21||

22namaskṛtya ca devebhyo brāhmaṇebhyo viśeṣataḥ
|
tamahaṃ smayanniva raṇe pratyabhāṣaṃ vyavasthitam ||22||

23ācāryatā mānitā me nirmaryāde hyapi tvayi
|
bhūyaśca śṛṇu me brahman sampadaṃ dharmasaṃgrahe ||23||

24ye te vedāḥ śarīrasthā brāhmaṇyaṃ yacca te mahat
|
tapaśca te mahattaptaṃ na tebhyaḥ praharāmyaham ||24||

25prahāre kṣatradharmasya yaṃ rāma tvaṃ samāśritaḥ
|
brāhmaṇaḥ kṣatriyatvaṃ hi yāti śastrasamudyamāt ||25||

26paśya me dhanuṣo vīryaṃ paśya bāhvorbalaṃ mama
|
eṣa te kārmukaṃ vīra chinadmi niśiteṣuṇā ||26||

27tasyāhaṃ niśitaṃ bhallaṃ cikṣepa bharatarṣabha
|
tenāsya dhanuṣaḥ koṭiṃ chitvā bhūmāvapātayam ||27||

28tathaiva ca pṛṣatkānāṃ śatāni nataparvaṇām
|
cikṣepa kaṅkapatrāṇāṃ jāmadagnyarathaṃ prati ||28||

29kāye viṣaktāstu tadā vāyunā samudīritāḥ
|
celuḥ kṣaranto rudhiraṃ nāgā iva ca te śarāḥ ||29||

30kṣatajokṣitasarvāṅgaḥ kṣaran sa rudhiraṃ raṇe
|
babhau rāmastadā rājan merurdhātumivotsṛjan ||30||

31hemantānte'śoka iva raktastabakamaṇḍitaḥ
|
babhau rāmastathā rājan praphulla iva kiṃśukaḥ ||31||

32tato'nyaddhanurādāya rāmaḥ krodhasamanvitaḥ
|
hemapuṅkhān suniśitān śarāṃstān hi vavarṣa saḥ ||32||

33te samāsādya māṃ raudrā bahudhā marmabhedinaḥ
|
akampayanmahāvegāḥ sarpānalaviṣopamāḥ ||33||

34tamahaṃ samavaṣṭabhya punarātmānamāhave
|
śatasaṃkhyaiḥ śaraiḥ kruddhastadā rāmamavākiram ||34||

35sa tairagnyarkasaṅkāśaiḥ śarairāśīviṣopamaiḥ
|
śitairabhyardito rāmo mandacetā ivābhavat ||35||

36tato'haṃ kṛpayā''viṣṭo viṣṭabhyātmānamātmanā
|
dhigdhigityabuvaṃ yuddhaṃ kṣatradharmaṃ ca bhārata ||36||

37asakṛccābruvaṃ rājan śokavegapariplutaḥ
|
aho bata kṛtaṃ pāpaṃ mayedaṃ kṣatradharmaṇā ||37||

38gururdvijātirdharmātmā yadevaṃ pīḍitaḥ śaraiḥ
|
tato na prāharaṃ bhūyo jāmadagnyāya bhārata ||38||

39athāvatāpya pṛthivīṃ pūṣā divasasaṃkṣaye
|
jagāmāstaṃ sahastrāṃśustato yuddhamupāramat ||39||
