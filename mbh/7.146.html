Mahābhārata 7.146 (Droṇaparvan)

1śakrāśanisphoṭasamaṃ sughoraṃ vikṛṣyamāṇasya dhanañjayena ||1||

2trāsodvignaṃ tathodbhrāntaṃ tvadīyaṃ tadbalaṃ nṛpa
|
yugāntavātasaṃkṣubdhaṃ caladvīcitaraṅgitam ||2||

3pralīnamīnamakaraṃ sāgarāṃbha ivābhavat
|
sa raṇe vyacaratpārthaḥ prekṣamāṇo dhanañjayaḥ ||3||

4yugapaddikṣu sarvāsu sarvāṇyastrāṇi darśayan
|
ādadānaṃ mahārāja sandadhānaṃ ca pāṇḍavam ||4||

5utkarṣantaṃ sṛjantaṃ ca na sma paśyāma lāghavāt
|
tataḥ kruddho mahābāhuraindramastraṃ durāsadam ||5||

6prāduścakre mahārāja trāsayansarvabhāratān
|
tataḥ śarāḥ prādurāsandivyāstrapratimantritāḥ||6||

7pradīptāśca śikhimukhāḥ śataśo'tha sahasraśaḥ
|
ākarṇapūrṇanirmuktairagnyarkāṃśunibhaiḥ śaraiḥ ||7||

8nabho'bhavattadduṣprekṣyamulkābhiriva saṃvṛtam
|
tataḥ śastrāndhakāraṃ tatkauravaiḥ samudīritam ||8||

9aśakyaṃ manasā'pyanyaiḥ pāṇḍavaḥ saṃbhramanniva
|
nāśayāmāsa vikramya śarairdivyāstramantritaiḥ ||9||

10naiśaṃ tamoṃ'śubhiḥ kṣipraṃ dinādāviva bhāskaraḥ
|
tatastu tāvakaṃ sainyaṃ dīptaiḥ śaragabhastibhiḥ ||10||

11ākṣipatpalvalāṃbūni nidāghārka iva prabhuḥ
|
tato divyāstraviduṣā prahitāḥ sāyakāṃśavaḥ ||11||

12samāplavan dviṣatsainyaṃ lokaṃ bhānorivāṃśavaḥ
|
athāpare samutsṛṣṭā viśikhāstigmatejasaḥ ||12||

13hṛdayānyāśu vīrāṇāṃ viviśuḥ priyabandhuvat
|
ya enamīyuḥ samare tvadyodhāḥ śūramāninaḥ ||13||

14śalabhā iva te dīptamagniṃ prāpya yayuḥ kṣayam
|
evaṃ sa mṛdnan śatrūṇāṃ jīvitāni yaśāṃsi ca ||14||

15pārthaścacāra saṃgrāme mṛtyurvigrahavāniva
|
sakirīṭāni vaktrāṇi sāṃgadān vipulān bhujān ||15||

16sakuṇḍalayugānkarṇānkeṣāṃcidaharaccharaiḥ
|
satomarān gajasthānāṃ saprāsānhayasādinām ||16||

17sacarmaṇaḥ padātīnāṃ rathīnāṃ ca sadhanvanaḥ
|
sapratodānniyantṝṇāṃ bāhūṃściccheda pāṇḍavaḥ ||17||

18pradīptograśarārciṣmānbabhau tatra dhanañjayaḥ
|
savisphuliṅgāgraśikho jvalanniva hutāśanaḥ ||18||

19taṃ devarājapratimaṃ sarvaśastrabhṛtāṃ varam
|
yugapaddikṣu sarvāsu rathasthaṃ puruṣarṣabham ||19||

20nikṣipantaṃ mahāstrāṇi prekṣaṇīyaṃ dhanañjayam
|
nṛtyantaṃ rathamārgeṣu dhanurjyātalanādinam ||20||

21nirīkṣituṃ na śekuste yatnavanto'pi pārthivāḥ
|
madhyandinagataṃ sūryaṃ pratapantamivāmbare ||21||

22dīptograsaṃbhṛtaśaraḥ kirīṭī virarāja ha
|
varṣāsvivodīrṇajalaḥ sendradhanvāṃbudo mahān ||22||

23mahāstrasaṃplave tasmiñjiṣṇunā saṃpravartite
|
sudustare mahāghore mamajjuryodhapuṅgavāḥ ||23||

24utkṛttavadanairdehaiḥ śarīraiḥ kṛttabāhubhiḥ
|
bhujaiśca pāṇinirmukaiḥ pāṇibhirvyaṃgulīkṛtaiḥ ||24||

25kṛttāgrahastaiḥ karibhiḥ kṛttadantairmadotkaṭaiḥ
|
hayaiśca vidhuragrīvai rathaiśca śakalīkṛtaiḥ ||25||

26nikṛttāntraiḥ kṛttapādaistathānyaiḥ kṛttasandhibhiḥ
|
niśceṣṭairvisphuradbhiśca śataśo'tha sahasraśaḥ ||26||

27mṛtyorāghātalalitaṃ tatpārthāyodhanaṃ mahat
|
apaśyāma mahīpāla bhīrūṇāṃ bhayavardhanam ||27||

28ākrīḍamiva rudrasya purā'bhyardayataḥ paśūn
|
gajānāṃ kṣuranirmuktaiḥ karaiḥ sabhujageva bhūḥ ||28||

29kvacidbabhau sragviṇīva vaktrapadmaiḥ samācitā
|
vicitroṣṇīṣamukuṭaiḥ keyūrāṅgadakuṇḍalaiḥ ||29||

30svarṇacitratanutraiśca bhāṇḍaiśca gajavājinām
|
kirīṭaśatasaṃkīrṇā tatra tatra samācitā ||30||

31virarāja bhṛśaṃ citrā mahī navavadhūriva
|
majjāmedaḥ kardaminīṃ śoṇitaughataraṅgiṇīm ||31||

32marmāsthibhiragādhāṃ ca keśaśaivalaśādvalām
|
śirobāhūpalataṭāṃ rugṇakroḍāsthisaṃkaṭām ||32||

33citradhvajapatākāḍhyāṃ chatracāpormimālinīm
|
vigatāsumahākāyāṃ gajadehābhisaṃkulām ||33||

34rathoḍupaśatākīrṇāṃ hyasaṃghātarodhasam
|
rathacakrayugeṣākṣakūbarairatidurgamām ||34||

35prāsāsiśaktiparaśuviśikhāhidurāsadām
|
balakaṅkamahānakrāṃ gomāyumakarotkaṭām ||35||

36gṛdhrodagramahāgrāhāṃ śivāvirutabhairavām
|
nṛtyatpretapiśācādyairbhūtākīrṇāṃ sahasraśaḥ ||36||

37gatāsuyodhaniśceṣṭaśarīraśatavāhinīm
|
mahāpratibhayāṃ raudrāṃ ghorāṃ vaitaraṇīmiva ||37||

38nadīṃ pravartayāmāsa bhīrūṇāṃ bhayavardhinīm
|
taṃ dṛṣṭvā tasya vikrāntamantakasyeva rūpiṇaḥ ||38||

39abhūtapūrvaṃ kuruṣu bhayamāgādraṇājire
|
tata ādāya vīrāṇāmastrairastrāṇi pāṇḍavaḥ ||39||

40ātmānaṃ raudramācaṣṭa raudrakarmaṇyadhiṣṭhitaḥ
|
tato rathavarān rājannatyatikrāmadarjunaḥ ||40||

41madhyaṃdinagataṃ sūryaṃ pratapantamivāmbare
|
na śekuḥ sarvabhūtāni pāṇḍavaṃ prativīkṣitum ||41||

42prasṛtāṃstasya gāṇḍīvāccharavrātānmahātmanaḥ
|
saṃgrāme saṃprapaśyāmo haṃsapaṃktimivāmbare ||42||

43vinivārya sa vīrāṇāmastrairastrāṇi sarvataḥ
|
darśayan raudramātmānamugre karmaṇi dhiṣṭhitaḥ ||43||

44sa tān rathavarān rājannatyākrāmattadārjunaḥ
|
mohayanniva nārācairjayadrathavadhepsayā
|
visṛjandikṣu sarvāsu śarānasitasārathiḥ ||44||

45saratho vyacarattūrṇaṃ prekṣaṇīyo dhanañjayaḥ
|
bhramanta iva śūrasya śaravrātā mahātmanaḥ ||45||

46adṛśyantāntarikṣasthāḥ śataśo'tha sahasraśaḥ
|
ādadānaṃ maheṣvāsaṃ sandadhānaṃ ca sāyakam ||46||

47visṛjantaṃ ca kaunteyaṃ nānupaśyāma vai tadā
|
tathā sarvā diśo rājansarvāṃśca rathino raṇe ||47||

48kadaṃbīkṛtya kaunteyo jayadrathamupādravat
|
vivyādha ca catuḥṣaṣṭyā śarāṇāṃ nataparvaṇām ||48||

49saindhavābhimukhaṃ yāntaṃ yodhāḥ saṃprekṣya pāṃḍavam
|
nyavartanta raṇādvīrā nirāśāstasya jīvite ||49||

50yo yo'bhyadhāvadākrande tāvakaḥ pāṇḍavaṃ raṇe
|
tasya tasyāntagā bāṇāḥ śarīre nyapatanprabho ||50||

51kabandhasaṃkulaṃ cakre tava sainyaṃ mahārathaḥ
|
arjuno jayatāṃ śreṣṭhaḥ śarairagnyaṃśusannibhaiḥ ||51||

52evaṃ tattava rājendra caturaṅgabalaṃ tadā
|
vyākulīkṛtya kaunteyo jayadrathamupādravat ||52||

53drauṇiṃ pañcāśatā'vidhyadvṛṣasenaṃ tribhiḥ śaraiḥ
|
kṛpāyamāṇaḥ kaunteyaḥ kṛpaṃ navabhirārdayat ||53||

54śalyaṃ ṣoḍaśabhirbāṇaiḥ karṇaṃ dvātriṃśatā śaraiḥ
|
saindhavaṃ tu catuḥṣaṣṭyā viddhvā siṃha ivānadat ||54||

55saindhavastu tathā viddhaḥ śarairgāṇḍīvadhanvanā
|
na ca kṣame susaṃkruddhastotrārdita iva dvipaḥ||55||

56sa varāhadhvajastūrṇaṃ gārdhrapatrānajihmagān
|
kruddhāśīviṣasaṃkāśānkarmāraparimārjitān ||56||

57ākarṇapūrṇāñcikṣepa phālgunasya rathaṃ prati
|
tribhistu viddhvā govindaṃ nārācaiḥ ṣaḍbhirarjunam ||57||

58aṣṭabhirvājino'vidhyat dhvajaṃ caikena patriṇā
|
sa vikṣipyārjunastūrṇaṃ saindhavaprahitāñśarān ||58||

59yugapattasya ciccheda śarābhyāṃ saindhavasya ha
|
sāratheśca śiraḥ kāyāt dhvajaṃ ca samalaṃkṛtam ||59||

60sa chinnayaṣṭiḥ sumahān dhanañjayaśarāhataḥ
|
varāhaḥ sindhurājasya papātāgniśikhopamaḥ ||60||

61etasminneva kāle tu drutaṃ gacchati bhāskare
|
abravītpāṇḍavaṃ rājaṃstvaramāṇo janārdanaḥ ||61||

62eṣa madhye kṛtaḥ ṣaḍbhiḥ pārthavīrairmahārathaiḥ
|
jīvitepsurmahābāho bhītastiṣṭhati saindhavaḥ||62||

63etānanirjitya raṇe ṣaḍrathānpuruṣarṣabha
|
na śakyaḥ saindhavo hantuṃ yato nirvyājamarjuna ||63||

64yogamatra vidhāsyāmi sūryasyāvaraṇaṃ prati
|
astaṃgata iti vyaktaṃ drakṣyatyekaḥ sa sindhurāṭra ||64||

65harṣeṇa jīvitākāṃkṣī vināśārthaṃ tava prabho
|
na gopsyati durācāraḥ sa ātmānaṃ kathaṃcana ||65||

66tatra chidre prahartavyaṃ tvayā'sya kurusattama
|
vyapekṣā naiva kartavyā gato'stamiti bhāskaraḥ ||66||

67evamastviti bībhatsuḥ keśavaṃ pratyabhāṣatā
|
tato'sṛjattamaḥ kṛṣṇaḥ sūryasyāvaraṇaṃ prati ||67||

68yogī yogena saṃyukto yogināmīśvaro hariḥ
|
sṛṣṭe tamasi kṛṣṇena gato'stamiti bhāskaraḥ ||68||

69tvadīyā jahṛṣuryodhāḥ pārthanāśānnarādhipa
|
te prahṛṣṭā raṇe rājannāpaśyansainikā ravim ||69||

70unnāmya vakrāṇi tadā sa ca rājā jayadrathaḥ
|
vīkṣyamāṇe tatastasminsindhurāje divākaram ||70||

71punarevābravītkṛṣṇo dhanañjayamidaṃ vacaḥ
|
paśya sindhupatiṃ vīraṃ prekṣamāṇaṃ divākaram ||71||

72bhayaṃ hi vipramucyaitattvatto bharatasattama
|
ayaṃ kālo mahābāho vadhāyāsya durātmanaḥ ||72||

73chindhi mūrdhānamasyāśu kuru sāphalyamātmanaḥ
|
ityevaṃ keśavenoktaḥ pāṇḍuputraḥ pratāpavān ||73||

74nyavadhīttāvakaṃ sainyaṃ śarairarkāgnisannibhaiḥ
|
kṛpaṃ vivyādha viṃśatyā karṇaṃ pañcāśatā śaraiḥ ||74||

75śalyaṃ duryodhanaṃ caiva ṣaḍbhiḥ ṣaḍbhiratāḍayat
|
vṛṣasenaṃ tathā'ṣṭābhiḥ ṣaṣṭyā saindhavameva ca ||75||

76tathaiva ca mahābāhustvadīyānpāṇḍunandanaḥ
|
gāḍhaṃ viddhvā śarai rājan jayadrathamupādravat ||76||

77taṃ samīpasthitaṃ dṛṣṭvā lelihānamivānalam
|
jayadrathasya goptāraḥ saṃśayaṃ paramaṃ gatāḥ ||77||

78tataḥ sarve mahārāja tava yodhā jayaiṣiṇaḥ
|
siṣicuḥ śaradhārābhiḥ pākaśāsanimāhave ||78||

79saṃchādyamānaḥ kaunteyaḥ śarajālairanekaśaḥ
|
akrudhyatsa mahābāhurajitaḥ kurunandanaḥ ||79||

80tataḥ śaramayaṃ jālaṃ tumulaṃ pākaśāsaniḥ
|
vyasṛjatpuruṣavyāghrastava sainyajighāṃsayā ||80||

81te hanyamānā vīreṇa yodhā rājan raṇe tava
|
prajahuḥ saindhavaṃ bhītā dvau samaṃ nāpyadhāvatām ||81||

82tatrādbhutamapaśyāma kuntīputrasya vikramam
|
tādṛṅna bhāvī bhūto vā yaccakāra mahāyaśāḥ ||82||

83dvipāndvipagatāṃścaiva hayānhayagatānapi
|
tathā sarathinaścaiva nyahan rudraḥ paśūniva ||83||

84na tatra samare kaścinmayā dṛṣṭo narādhipa
|
gajo vājī naro vāpi yo na pārthaśarāhataḥ ||84||

85rajasā tamasā caiva yodhāḥ saṃcchannacakṣuṣaḥ
|
kaśmalaṃ prāviśanghoraṃ nānvajānanparasparam ||85||

86te śarairbhinnamarmāṇaḥ sainikāḥ pārthacoditaiḥ
|
babhramuścaskhaluḥ petuḥ sedurmamluśca bhārata ||86||

87tasminmahābhīṣaṇake prajānāmiva saṃkṣaye
|
raṇe mahati duṣpāre vartamāne sudāruṇe ||87||

88śoṇitasya prasekena śīghratvādanilasya ca
|
aśāmyattadrajo bhaumamasṛk sikte dharātale ||88||

89ānābhi niramajjaṃśca rathacakrāṇi śoṇite
|
mattā vegavatā rājaṃstāvakānāṃ raṇāṅgane||89||

90hastinaśca hatārohā dāritāṅgāḥ sahasraśaḥ
|
svānyanīkāni mṛdnanta ārtanādāḥ pradudruvuḥ||90||

91hayāśca patitārohāḥ pattayaśca narādhipa
|
pradudruvurbhayādrājandhanañjayaśarāhatāḥ||91||

92muktakeśā vikavacāḥ kṣarantaḥ kṣatajaṃ kṣataiḥ
|
prāpalāyanta saṃtrastāstyaktvā raṇaśiro janāḥ||92||

93ūrugrāhagṛhītāśca kecittatrābhavan bhuvi
|
hatānāṃ cāpare madhye dviradānāṃ nililyire||93||

94evaṃ tava balaṃ rājandrāvayitvā dhanañjayaḥ
|
nyavadhītsāyakairghoraiḥ sindhurājasya rakṣiṇaḥ||94||

95drauṇiṃ kṛpaṃ karṇaśalyau vṛṣasenaṃ suyodhanam
|
chādayāmāsa tīvreṇa śarajālena pāṇḍavaḥ||95||

96na gṛhṇannakṣipan rājanmuñcannāpi ca sandadhat
|
adṛśyatārjunaḥ saṃkhye śīghrāstratvātkathañcana||96||

97dhanurmaṇḍalamevāsya dṛśyate smāsyataḥ sadā
|
sāyakāśca vyadṛśyanta niścarantaḥ samantataḥ||97||

98karṇasya tu dhanuśchittvā vṛṣasenasya caiva ha
|
śalyasya sūtaṃ bhallena rathanīḍādapātayat ||98||

99gāḍhaviddhāvubhau kṛtvā śaraiḥ svastrīyamātulau
|
arjuno jayatāṃ śreṣṭho drauṇiśāradvatau raṇe ||99||

100evaṃ tānvyākulīkṛtya tvayānāṃ mahārathān
|
ujjahāra śaraṃ ghoraṃ pāṇḍavo'nalasannibham ||100||

101indrāśanisamaprakhyaṃ divyamastrābhimantritam
|
sarvabhārasahaṃ śaśvadgandhamālyārcitaṃ mahat ||101||

102vajreṇāstreṇa saṃyojya vidhivatkurunandanaḥ
|
samādadhanmahābāhurgāṇḍīve kṣipramarjunaḥ||102||

103tasminsandhīyamāne tu śare jvalanatejasi
|
antarikṣe mahānādo bhūtānāmabhavannṛpa ||103||

104abravīcca punastatra tvaramāṇo janārdanaḥ
|
dhanañjaya śiraśchindhi saindhavasya durātmanaḥ||104||

105astaṃ mahīdharaśreṣṭhaṃ yiyāsati divākaraḥ
|
śṛṇuṣvaitacca vākyaṃ me jayadrathavadhaṃ prati||105||

106vṛddhakṣatraḥ saindhavasya pitā jagati viśrutaḥ
|
sa kāleneha mahatā saindhavaṃ prāptavānsutam ||106||

107jayadrathamamitraghnaṃ vāguvācāśarīriṇī
|
nṛpamantarhitā vāṇī meghadundubhiniḥsvanā||107||

108tavātmajo manuṣyendra kulaśīladamādibhiḥ
|
guṇairbhaviṣyati vibho sadṛśo vaṃśayordvayoḥ||108||

109kṣatriyapravaro loke nityaṃ śūrābhisatkṛtaḥ
|
kiṃ tvasya yudhyamānasya saṃgrāme kṣatriyarṣabhaḥ||109||

110śiraśchetsyati saṃkruddhaḥ śatruścālakṣito bhuvi
|
etacchrutvā sindhurājo dhyātvā ciramarindamaḥ||110||

111jñātīnsarvānuvācedaṃ putrasnehābhicoditaḥ
|
saṃgrāme yudhyamānasya vahato mahatīṃ dhuram ||111||

112dharaṇyāṃ mama putrasya pātayiṣyati yaḥ śiraḥ
|
tasyāpi śatadhā mūrdhā phaliṣyati na saṃśayaḥ||112||

113evamuktvā tato rājye sthāpayitvā jayadratham
|
vṛddhakṣatro vanaṃ yātastapaścograṃ samāsthitaḥ ||113||

114so'yaṃ tapyati tejasvī tapo ghoraṃ durāsadam
|
samantapañcakādasmādbahirvānaraketana ||114||

115tasmājjayadrathasya tvaṃ śiraśchittvā mahāmṛdhe
|
divyenāstreṇa ripughoreṇādbhutakarmaṇā||115||

116sakuṇḍalaṃ sindhupateḥ prabhañjanasutānuja
|
utsaṅge pātayasvāsya vṛddhakṣatrasya bhārata ||116||

117atha tvamasya mūrdhānaṃ pātayiṣyasi bhūtale
|
tavāpi śatadhā mūrdhā phaliṣyati na saṃśayaḥ||117||

118yathā cedaṃ na jānīyātsa rājā tapasi sthitaḥ
|
tathā kuru kuruśreṣṭha divyamastramupāśritaḥ||118||

119na hyasādhyamakāryaṃ vā vidyate tava kiṃcana
|
samasteṣvapi lokeṣu triṣu vāsavanandana ||119||

120etacchrutvā tu vacanaṃ sṛkkiṇī parisaṃlihan
|
indrāśanisamasparśaṃ divyamantrābhimantritam ||120||

121sarvabhārasahaṃ śaśvadgandhamālyārcitaṃ śaram
|
visasarjārjunastūrṇaṃ saindhavasya vadhe ghṛtam ||121||

122sa tu gāṇḍīvanirmuktaḥ śaraḥ śyena ivāśugaḥ
|
chittvā śiraḥ sindhupaterutpapāta vihāyasam||122||

123tacchiraḥ sindhurājasya śarairūrdhvamavāhayat
|
durhṛdāmapraharṣāya suhṛdāṃ harṣaṇāya ca ||123||

124śaraiḥ kadambakīkṛtya kāle tasmiṃśca pāṇḍavaḥ
|
yodhayāmāsa tāṃścaiva pāṇḍavaḥ ṣaṇmahārathān||124||

125tataḥ sumahadāścaryaṃ tatrāpaśyāma bhārata
|
samantapañcakādbāhyaṃ śiro yadvyaharattataḥ||125||

126etasminneva kāle tu vṛddhakṣatro mahīpatiḥ
|
sandhyāmupāste tejasvī sambandhī tava māriṣa||126||

127upāsīnasya tasyātha kṛṣṇakeśaṃ sakuṇḍalam
|
sindhurājasya mūrdhānamutsaṅge samapātayat ||127||

128tasyotsaṅge nipatitaṃ śirastaścārukuṇḍalam
|
vṛddhakṣatrasya nṛpateralakṣitamarindama||128||

129kṛtajapyasya tasyātha vṛddhakṣatrasya bhārata
|
prottiṣṭhatastatsahasā śiro'gacchaddharātalam||129||

130tatastasya narendrasya putramūrdhani bhūtale
|
gate tasyāpi śatadhā mūrdhā'gacchadarindama ||130||

131tataḥ sarvāṇi sainyāni vismayaṃ jagmuruttamam
|
vāsudevaṃ ca bībhatsuṃ praśaśaṃsurmahāratham ||131||

132tato vinihate rājan sindhurāje kirīṭinā
|
tamastadvāsudevena saṃhṛtaṃ bharatarṣabha ||132||

133paścājjñātaṃ mahīpāla tava putraiḥ sahānugaiḥ
|
vāsudevaprayukteyaṃ māyeti nṛpasattama ||133||

134evaṃ sa nihato rājanpārthenāmitatejasā
|
akṣauhiṇīraṣṭa hatvā jāmātā tava saindhavaḥ||134||

135hataṃ jayadrathaṃ dṛṣṭvā tava putrā narādhipa
|
duḥkhādaśrūṇi mumucurnirāśāścābhavañjaye||135||

136tato jayadrathe rājanhate pārthena keśavaḥ
|
dadhmau śaṃkhaṃ mahābāhurarjunaśca parantapaḥ||136||

137bhīmaśca vṛṣṇisiṃhaśca yudhāmanyuśca bhārata
|
uttamaujāśca vikrāntaḥ śaṃkhāndadhmuḥ pṛthakpṛthak||137||

138śrutvā mahāntaṃ taṃ śabdaṃ dharmarājo yudhiṣṭhiraḥ
|
saindhavaṃ nihataṃ mene phālgunena mahātmanā||138||

139tato vāditraghoṣeṇa svānyodhānparyaharṣayat
|
abhyavartata saṃgrāme bhāradvājaṃ yuyutsayā||139||

140tataḥ pravavṛte rājannastaṃ gacchati bhāskare
|
droṇasya somakaiḥ sārdhaṃ saṃgrāmo lomaharṣaṇaḥ||140||

141te tu sarve prayatnena bhāradvājaṃ jighāṃsavaḥ
|
saindhave nihate rājannayudhyanta mahārathāḥ||141||

142pāṇḍavāstu jayaṃ labdhvā saindhavaṃ vinihatya ca
|
ayodhayaṃstu te droṇaṃ jayonmattāstatastataḥ||142||

143arjuno'pi tato yodhāṃstāvakān rathasattamān
|
ayodhayanmahābāhurhatvā saindhavakaṃ nṛpam ||143||

144sa devaśatrūniva devarājaḥ kirīṭamālī vyadhamatsamantāt
|
yathā tamāṃsyabhyuditastamoghnaḥ pūrvapratijñāṃ samavāpya vīraḥ||144||
