Mahābhārata 7.192 (Droṇaparvan)

1sañjaya uvāca|
sātvatasya tu tatkarma dṛṣṭvā duryodhanādayaḥ
|
śaineyaṃ sarvataḥ kruddhā vārayāmāsurañjasā||1||

2kṛpakarṇau ca samare putrāśca tava māriṣa
|
śaineyaṃ tvarayā'bhyetya vinighnanniśitaiḥ śaraiḥ||2||

3yudhiṣṭhirastato rājā mādrīputrau ca pāṇḍavau
|
bhīmasenaśca balavānsātyakiṃ paryavārayan||3||

4karṇaśca śaravarṣeṇa gautamaśca mahārathaḥ
|
duryodhanādayaste ca śaineyaṃ paryavārayan||4||

5tāṃ vṛṣṭiṃ sahasā rājannutthitāṃ ghorarūpiṇīm
|
vārayāmāsa śaineyo yodhayaṃstānmahārathān||5||

6teṣāmastrāṇi divyāni saṃhitāni mahātmanām
|
vārayāmāsa vidhivaddivyairastrairmahāmṛdhe||6||

7krūramāyodhanaṃ jajñe tasmin rājasamāgame
|
rudrasyeva hi kruddhasya nighnatastānpaśūnpurā||7||

8hastānāmuttamāṅgānāṃ kārmukāṇāṃ ca bhārata
|
chatrāṇāṃ cāpaviddhānāṃ cāmarāṇāṃ ca sañcayaiḥ||8||

9rāśayaḥ sma vyadṛśyanta tatra tatra raṇājire
|
bhagnacakrai rathaiścāpi pātitaiśca mahādhvajaiḥ||9||

10sādibhiśca hataiḥ śūraiḥ saṃkīrṇā vasudhā'bhavat
|
bāṇapātanikṛttāstu yodhāste kurusattama||10||

11ceṣṭaṃto vividhāśceṣṭā vyadṛśyanta mahāhave
|
vartamāne tathā yuddhe ghore devāsuropame||11||

12abravītkṣatriyāṃstatra dharmarājo yudhiṣṭhiraḥ
|
abhidravata saṃyattāḥ kuṃbhayoniṃ mahārathāḥ||12||

13eṣo hi pārṣato vīro bhāradvājena saṅgataḥ
|
ghaṭate ca yathāśakti bhāradvājasya nāśane||13||

14yādṛśāni hi rūpāṇi dṛśyante'sya mahāraṇe
|
adya droṇaṃ raṇe kruddho ghātayiṣyati pārṣataḥ||14||

15te yūyaṃ sahitā bhūtvā yudhyadhvaṃ kuṃbhasaṃbhavam
|
yudhiṣṭhirasamājñaptāḥ sṛñjayānāṃ mahārathāḥ||15||

16abhyadravanta saṃyattā bhāradvājajighāṃsavaḥ
|
tānsamāpatataḥ sarvān bhāradvājo mahārathaḥ||16||

17abhyavartata vegena martavyamiti niścitaḥ
|
prayāte satyasandhe tu samakampata medinī||17||

18vavurvātāḥ sanirghātāstrāsayānā varūthinīm
|
papāta mahatī colkā ādityānniścarantyuta||18||

19dīpayantī ubhe sene śaṃsantīva mahadbhayam
|
jajvaluścaiva śastrāṇi bhāradvājasya māriṣa||19||

20rathāḥ svananti cātyarthaṃ hayāścāśrūṇyavāsṛjan
|
hataujā iva cāpyāsīdbhāradvājo mahārathaḥ||20||

21prāsphurannayanaṃ cāsya vāmabāhustathaiva ca
|
vimanāścābhavadyuddhe dṛṣṭvā pārpatamagrataḥ||21||

22ṛṣīṇāṃ brahmavādānāṃ svargasya gamanaṃ prati
|
suyuddhena tataḥ prāṇānutstraṣṭumupacakrame||22||

23tataścaturdiśaṃ sainyairdrupadasyābhisaṃvṛtaḥ
|
nirdahan kṣatriyavrātāndroṇaḥ paryacaradraṇe||23||

24hatvā viṃśatisāhastrān kṣatriyānarimardanaḥ
|
daśāyutāni kariṇāmavadhīdviśikhaiḥ śitaiḥ||24||

25so'tiṣṭhadāhave yatto vidhūmo'gniriva jvalan
|
kṣatriyāṇāmabhāvāya brāhmamastraṃ samāsthitaḥ||25||

26pāñcālyaṃ virathaṃ bhīmo hatasarvāyudhaṃ balī
|
suviṣaṇṇaṃ mahātmānaṃ tvaramāṇaḥ samabhyayāt||26||

27tataḥ svarathamāropya pāñcālyamarimardanaḥ
|
abravīdabhisaṃprekṣya droṇamasyantamantikāt||27||

28na tvadanya ihācāryaṃ yoddhumutsahate pumān
|
tvarasva prāgvadhāyaiva tvayi bhāraḥ samāhitaḥ||28||

29sa tathokto mahābāhuḥ sarvabhārasahaṃ dhanuḥ
|
abhipatyādade kṣipramāyudhapravaraṃ dṛḍham||29||

30saṃrabdhaśca śarānasyandroṇaṃ durvāraṇaṃ raṇe
|
vivārayiṣurācāryaṃ śaravarṣairavākirat||30||

31tau nyavārayatāṃ śreṣṭhau saṃrabdhau raṇaśobhinau
|
udīrayetāṃ brāhmāṇi divyānyastrāṇyanekaśaḥ||31||

32sa mahāstrairmahārāja droṇamācchādayadraṇe
|
nihatya sarvāṇyastrāṇi bhāradvājasya pārṣataḥ||32||

33savasātīñśibīṃścaiva bālhīkānkauravānapi
|
rakṣiṣyamāṇānsaṃgrāme droṇaṃ vyadhamadacyutaḥ||33||

34dhṛṣṭadyumnastathā rājan gabhastibhirivāṃśumān
|
babhau pracchādayannāśāḥ śarajālaiḥ samantataḥ||34||

35tasya droṇo dhanuśchittvā viddhvā cainaṃ śilīmukhaiḥ
|
marmāṇyabhyahanadbhūyaḥ sa vyathāṃ paramāmagāt||35||

36tato bhīmo dṛḍhakrodho droṇasyāśliṣya taṃ ratham
|
śanakairiva rājendra droṇaṃ vacanamabravīt||36||

37yadi nāma na yudhyerañśikṣitā brahmabandhavaḥ
|
svakarmabhirasantuṣṭā na sma kṣatraṃ kṣayaṃ vrajet||37||

38ahiṃsāṃ sarvabhūteṣu dharmaṃ jyāyastaraṃ viduḥ
|
tasya ca brāhmaṇo mūlaṃ bhavāṃśca brahmavittamaḥ||38||

39śvapākavanmlecchagaṇān hatvā cānyānpṛthagvidhān
|
ajñānānmūḍhavadbrahmanputra-dāra-dhanepsayā||39||

40ekasyārthe bahūnhatvā putrasyādharmavidyayā
|
svakarmasthānvikarmastho na vyapatrapase katham||40||

41yasyārthe śastramādāya yamapekṣya ca jīvasi
|
sa cādya patitaḥ śete pṛṣṭhenāveditastava||41||

42dharmarājasya tadvākyaṃ nābhiśaṅkitumarhasi
|
evamuktastato droṇo bhīmenotsṛjya taddhanuḥ||42||

43sarvāṇyastrāṇi dharmātmā hātukāmo'bhyabhāṣata
|
karṇa karṇa maheṣvāsa kṛpa duryodhaneti ca||43||

44saṃgrāme kriyatāṃ yatno bravīmyeṣa punaḥ punaḥ
|
pāṇḍavebhyaḥ śivaṃ vo'stu śastramabhyutsṛjāmyaham||44||

45iti tatra mahārāja prākrośaddrauṇimeva ca
|
utsṛjya ca raṇe śastraṃ rathopasthe niviśya ca||45||

46abhayaṃ sarvabhūtānāṃ pradadau yogamīyivān
|
tasya tacchidramājñāya dhṛṣṭadyumnaḥ pratāpavān||46||

47saśaraṃ taddhanurghoraṃ saṃnyasyātha rathe tataḥ
|
khaḍgī rathādavaplutya sahasā droṇamabhyayāt||47||

48hāhākṛtāni bhūtāni mānuṣāṇītarāṇi ca
|
droṇaṃ tathāgataṃ dṛṣṭvā dhṛṣṭadyumnavaśaṃ gatam||48||

49hāhākāraṃ bhṛśaṃ cakruraho dhigiti cābruvan
|
droṇo'pi śastrāṇyutsṛjya paramaṃ sāṅkhyamāsthitaḥ||49||

50tathoktvā yogamāsthāya jyotirbhūto mahātapāḥ
|
purāṇaṃ puruṣaṃ viṣṇuṃ jagāma manasā param||50||

51mukhaṃ kiñcitsamunnāmya viṣṭabhya uramagrataḥ
|
nimīlitākṣaḥ sattvastho nikṣipya hṛdi dhāraṇām ||51||

52omityekākṣaraṃ brahma jyotirbhūto mahātapāḥ
|
smaritvā devadeveśamakṣaraṃ paramaṃ prabhum||52||

53divamākrāmadācāryaḥ sākṣātsadbhirdurākramām
|
dvau sūryāviti no buddhirāsīttasmiṃstathāgate||53||

54ekāgramiva cāsīcca jyotirbhiḥ pūritaṃ nabhaḥ
|
samapadyata cārkābhe bhāradvājadivākare||54||

55nimeṣamātreṇa ca tat jyotirantaradhīyata
|
āsītkilakilāśabdaḥ prahṛṣṭānāṃ divaukasām||55||

56brahmalokagate droṇe dhṛṣṭadyumne ca mohite
|
vayameva tadā'drākṣma pañca mānuṣayonayaḥ||56||

57yogayuktaṃ mahātmānaṃ gacchantaṃ paramāṃ gatim
|
ahaṃ dhanañjayaḥ pārtho bhāradvājasya cātmajaḥ||57||

58vāsudevaśca vārṣṇeyo dharmaputraśca pāṇḍavaḥ
|
anye tu sarve nāpaśyanbhāradvājasya dhīmataḥ||58||

59mahimānaṃ mahārāja yogayuktasya gacchataḥ
|
brahmalokaṃ mahaddivyaṃ devaguhyaṃ hi tatparam||59||

60gatiṃ paramikāṃ prāptamajānanto nṛyonayaḥ
|
nāpaśyangacchamānaṃ hi taṃ sārdhamṛṣipuṅgavaiḥ||60||

61ācāryaṃ yogamāsthāya brahmalokamarindamam
|
vitunnāṅgaṃ śaravrātairnyastāyudhamasṛkkṣaram||61||

62dhikkṛtaḥ pārṣatastaṃ tu sarvabhūtaiḥ parāmṛśat
|
tasya mūrdhānamālambya gatasattvasya dehinaḥ||62||

63kiñcidabruvataḥ kāyādvicakartāsinā śiraḥ
|
harṣeṇa mahatā yukto bhāradvāje nipātite||63||

64siṃhanādaravaṃ cakre bhrāmayankhaḍgamāhave
|
ākarṇapalitaśyāmo ‘vayasā'śītipañcakaḥ’||64||

65tvatkṛte vyacaratsaṅkhye sa tu ṣoḍaśavarṣavat
|
uktavāṃśca mahābāhuḥ kuntīputro dhanañjayaḥ||65||

66jīvantamānayācāryaṃ mā vadhīrdrupadātmaja
|
na hantavyo na hantavya iti te sainikāśca ha||66||

67utkrośannarjunaścaiva sānukrośastamāvrajat
|
krośamāne'rjune caiva pārthiveṣu ca sarvaśaḥ||67||

68dhṛṣṭadyumno'vadhīddroṇaṃ rathatalpe nararṣabham
|
śoṇitena pariklinno rathādbhūmimathāpatat||68||

69lohitāṅga ivādityo durdharṣaḥ samapadyata
|
evaṃ taṃ nihataṃ saṅkhye dadṛśe sainiko janaḥ||69||

70dhṛṣṭadyumnastu tadrājanbhāradvājaśiro'harat
|
tāvakānāṃ maheṣvāsaḥ pramukhe tatsamākṣipat||70||

71te tu dṛṣṭvā śiro rājanbhāradvājasya tāvakāḥ
|
palāyanakṛtotsāhā dudruvuḥ sarvatodiśam||71||

72droṇastu divamāsthāya nakṣatrapathamāviśat
|
ahameva tadā'drākṣaṃ droṇasya nidhanaṃ nṛpa||72||

73ṛṣeḥ prasādātkṛṣṇasya satyavatyāḥ sutasya ca
|
vidhūmāmiha saṃyāntīmulkāṃ prajvalitāmiva||73||

74apaśyāma divaṃ stabdhvā gacchantaṃ taṃ mahādyutim
|
hate droṇe nirutsāhāḥ kurupāṇḍavasṛñjayāḥ||74||

75abhyadravanmahāvegāstataḥ sainyaṃ vyadīryata
|
nihatā hatabhūyiṣṭhāḥ saṃgrāme niśitaiḥ śaraiḥ||75||

76tāvakā nihate droṇe gatāsava ivābhavan
|
parājayamathāvāpya paratra ca mahadbhayam||76||

77ubhayenaiva te hīnā nāvindan dhṛtimātmanaḥ
|
anvicchantaḥ śarīraṃ tu bhāradvājasya pārthivāḥ||77||

78nānvagacchanmahārāja kabandhāyutasaṃkule
|
pāṇḍavāstu jayaṃ labdhvā paratra ca mahadyaśaḥ||78||

79bāṇaśaṃkharavāṃścakruḥ siṃhanādāṃśca puṣkalān
|
bhīmasenastato rājan dhṛṣṭadyumnaśca pārṣataḥ||79||

80varūthinyāmanṛtyetāṃ pariṣvajya parasparam
|
abravīcca tadā bhīmaḥ pārṣataṃ śatrutāpanam||80||

81bhūyo'haṃ tvāṃ vijayinaṃ pariṣvajyāmi pārṣata
|
sūtaputre hate pāpe dhārtarāṣṭre ca saṃyuge||81||

82etāvaduktvā bhīmastu harṣeṇa mahatā yutaḥ
|
bāhuśabdena pṛthivīṃ kampayāmāsa pāṇḍavaḥ||82||

83tasya śabdena vitrastāḥ prādravaṃstāvakā yudhi
|
kṣatradharmaṃ samutsṛjya palāyanaparāyaṇāḥ||83||

84pāṇḍavāstu jayaṃ labdhvā hṛṣṭā hyāsanviśāṃpate
|
arikṣayaṃ ca saṃgrāme tena te sukhamāpnuvan||84||
