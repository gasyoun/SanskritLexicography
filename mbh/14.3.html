Mahābhārata 14.3 (Āśvamedhikaparvan)

1vyāsa uvāca|
yudhiṣṭhira tava prajñā na samyagiti me matiḥ
|
na hi kaścitsvayaṃ martyaḥ svavaśaḥ kurute kriyām||1||

2īśvareṇa ca yukto'yaṃ sādhvasādhu ca mānavaḥ
|
karoti puruṣaḥ karma tatra kā paridevanā||2||

3ātmānaṃ manyase cātha pāpakarmāṇamantataḥ
|
śṛṇu tatra yathāpāpamapakṛṣyeta bhārata||3||

4tapobhiḥ kratubhiścaiva dānena ca yudhiṣṭhira
|
taranti nityaṃ puruṣā ye sma pāpāni kurvate||4||

5yajñena tapasā caiva dānena ca narādhipa
|
pūyante naraśārdūla narā duṣkṛtakāriṇaḥ||5||

6asurāśca surāścaiva puṇyahetormakhakriyām
|
prayatante mahātmānastasmād yajñāḥ parāyaṇam||6||

7yajñaireva mahātmāno babhūvuradhikāḥ surāḥ
|
tato devāḥ kriyāvanto dānavanabhyadharṣayan||7||

8rājasūyāśvamedhau ca sarvamedhaṃ ca bhārata
|
naramedhaṃ ca nṛpate tvamāhara yudhiṣṭhira||8||

9yajasva vājimedhena vidhivad dakṣiṇāvatā
|
bahukāmānnavittena rāmo dāśarathiryathā||9||

10yathā ca bharato rājā dauṣyantiḥ pṛthivīpatiḥ
|
śākuntalo mahāvīryastava pūrvapitāmahaḥ||10||

11yudhiṣṭhira uvāca|
asaṃśayaṃ vājimedhaḥ pāvayet pṛthivīmapi
|
abhiprāyastu me kaścittaṃ tvaṃ śrotumihārhasi||11||

12imaṃ jñātivadhaṃ kṛtvā sumahāntaṃ dvijottama
|
dānamalpaṃ na śaknomi dātuṃ vittaṃ ca nāsti me||12||

13na tu bālānimān dīnānutsahe vasu yācitum
|
tathaivārdravraṇān kṛcchre vartamānān nṛpātmajān||13||

14svayaṃ vināśya pṛthivīṃ yajñārthaṃ dvijasattama
|
karamāhārayiṣyāmi kathaṃ śokaparāyaṇaḥ||14||

15duryodhanāparādhena vasudhā vasudhādhipāḥ
|
pranaṣṭā yojayitvāsmānakīrtyā munisattama||15||

16duryodhanena pṛthivī kṣayitā vittakāraṇāt
|
kośaścāpi viśīrṇo'sau dhārtarāṣṭrasya durmateḥ||16||

17pṛthivī dakṣiṇā cātra vidhiḥ prathamakalpitaḥ
|
vidvadbhiḥ paridṛṣṭo'yaṃ śiṣṭo vidhiviparyayaḥ||17||

18na ca pratinidhiṃ kartuṃ cikīrṣāmi tapodhana
|
atra me bhagavan samyak sācivyaṃ kartumarhasi||18||

19evamuktastu pārthena kṛṣṇadvaipāyanastadā
|
muhūrtamanusañcintya dharmarājānamabravīt||19||

20kośaścāpi viśīrṇo'yaṃ paripūrṇo bhaviṣyati
|
vidyate draviṇaṃ pārtha girau himavati sthitam||20||

21utsṛṣṭaṃ brāhmaṇairyajñe maruttasya mahātmanaḥ
|
tadānayasva kaunteya paryāptaṃ tad bhaviṣyati||21||

22yudhiṣṭhira uvāca|
kathaṃ yajñe maruttasya draviṇaṃ tat samācitam
|
kasmiṃśca kāle sa nṛpo babhūva vadatāṃ vara||22||

23vyāsa uvāca|
yadi śuśrūṣase pārtha śṛṇu kārandhamaṃ nṛpam
|
yasmin kāle mahāvīryaḥ sa rājāsīnmahādhanaḥ||23||
