Mahābhārata 6.78 (Bhīṣmaparvan)

1sañjaya uvāca|
tato duryodhano rājā mohāt pratyāgatastadā
|
śaravarṣaiḥ punarbhīmaṃ pratyavārayadacyutam ||1||

2ekībhūtāstataścaiva tava putrā mahārathāḥ
|
sametya samare bhīmaṃ yodhayāmāsurudyatāḥ ||2||

3bhīmaseno'pi samare saṃprāpya svarathaṃ punaḥ
|
samāruhya mahābāhuryayau yena tavātmajaḥ ||3||

4pragṛhya ca mahāvegaṃ parāsukaraṇaṃ dṛḍham
|
sajjaṃ śarāsanaṃ saṃkhye śarairvivyādha te sutam ||4||

5tato duryodhano rājā bhīmasenaṃ mahābalam
|
nārācena sutīkṣṇena bhṛśaṃ marmaṇyatāḍayat ||5||

6so'tividdho maheṣvāsastava putreṇa dhanvinā
|
krodhasaṃraktanayano vegenākṣipya kārmukam ||6||

7duryodhanaṃ tribhirbāṇairbāhvorurasi cārpayat
|
sa tatra śuśubhe rājā śikharairgirirāḍiva ||7||

8tau dṛṣṭvā samare kruddhau vinighnantau parasparam
|
duryodhanānujāḥ sarve śūrāḥ saṃtyaktajīvitāḥ ||8||

9saṃsmṛtya mantritaṃ pūrvaṃ nigrahe bhīmakarmaṇaḥ
|
niścayaṃ paramaṃ kṛtvā nigrahītuṃ pracakramuḥ ||9||

10tānāpatata evājau bhīmaseno mahābalaḥ
|
pratyudyayau mahārāja gajaḥ pratigajāniva ||10||

11bhṛśaṃ kruddhaśca tejasvī nārācena samarpayat
|
citrasenaṃ mahārāja tava putraṃ mahāyaśāḥ ||11||

12tathetarāṃstava sutāṃstāḍayāmāsa bhārata
|
śarairbahuvidhaiḥ saṃkhye rukmapuṅkhaiḥ sutejanaiḥ ||12||

13tataḥ saṃsthāpya samare tānyanīkāni sarvaśaḥ
|
abhimanyuprabhṛtayaste dvādaśa mahārathāḥ ||13||

14preṣitā dharmarājena bhīmasenapadānugāḥ
|
pratijagmurmahārāja tava putrān mahābalān ||14||

15dṛṣṭvā rathasthāṃstān śūrān sūryāgnisamatejasaḥ
|
sarvāneva maheṣvāsān bhrājamānān śriyā vṛtān ||15||

16mahāhave dīpyamānān suvarṇamukuṭojjvalān
|
tatyajuḥ samare bhīmaṃ tava putrā mahābalāḥ ||16||

17tānnāmṛṣyata kaunteyo jīvamānā gatā iti
|
anvīya ca punaḥ sarvāṃstava putrānapīḍayat ||17||

18athābhimanyuṃ samare bhīmasenena saṅgatam
|
pārṣatena ca saṃprekṣya tava sainye mahārathāḥ ||18||

19duryodhanaprabhṛtayaḥ pragṛhītaśarāsanāḥ
|
bhṛśamaśvaiḥ prajavitaiḥ prayayuryatra te rathāḥ ||19||

20aparāhṇe mahārāja prāvartata mahāraṇaḥ
|
tāvakānāṃ ca balināṃ pareṣāṃ caiva bhārata ||20||

21abhimanyurvikarṇasya hayān hatvā mahāhave
|
athainaṃ pañcaviṃśatyā kṣudrakāṇāṃ samarpayat ||21||

22hatāśvaṃ rathamutsṛjya vikarṇastu mahārathaḥ
|
āruroha rathaṃ rājaṃścitrasenasya bhārata ||22||

23sthitāvekarathe tau tu bhrātarau kulavardhanau
|
ārjuniḥ śarajālena cchā‌dayāmāsa bhārata ||23||

24citraseno vikarṇaśca kārṣṇiṃ pañcabhirāyasaiḥ
|
vivyādha tena cākampat kārṣṇirmeruriva sthitaḥ ||24||

25duḥśāsanastu samare kekayān pañca māriṣa
|
yodhayāmāsa rājendra tada‌dbhutamivābhavat ||25||

26draupadeyā raṇe kruddhā duryodhanamavārayan
|
śarairāśīviṣākāraiḥ putraṃ tava viśāṃpate ||26||

27putro'pi tava durdharṣo draupadyāstanayān raṇe
|
sāyakairniśitai rājannājaghāna pṛthak pṛthak ||27||

28taiścāpi viddhaḥ śuśubhe rudhireṇa samukṣitaḥ
|
giriḥ prastravaṇairyadvadgairikādivimiśritaiḥ ||28||

29bhīṣmo'pi samare rājan pāṇḍavānāmanīkinīm
|
kālayāmāsa balavān pālaḥ paśugaṇāniva ||29||

30tato gāṇḍīvanirghoṣaḥ prādurāsīdviśāṃpate
|
dakṣiṇena varūthinyāḥ pārthasyārīn vinighnataḥ ||30||

31uttasthuḥ samare tatra kabandhāni samantataḥ
|
kurūṇāṃ caiva sainyeṣu pāṇḍavānāṃ ca bhārata ||31||

32śoṇitodaṃ śarāvartaṃ gajadvīpaṃ hayormiṇam
|
rathanaubhirnaravyāghrāḥ prateruḥ sainyasāgaram ||32||

33chinnahastā vikavacā videhāśca narottamāḥ
|
dṛśyante patitāstatra śataśo'tha sahasraśaḥ ||33||

34nihatairmattamātaṅgaiḥ śoṇitaughapariplutaiḥ
|
bhūrbhāti bharataśreṣṭha parvatairācitā yathā ||34||

35tatrādbhutamapaśyāma tava teṣāṃ ca bhārata
|
na tatrāsīt pumān kaścidyo yuddhaṃ nābhikāṅkṣati ||35||

36evaṃ yuyudhire vīrāḥ prārthayānā mahadyaśaḥ
|
tāvakāḥ pāṇḍavaiḥ sārdhamākāṅkṣanto jayaṃ yudhi ||36||
