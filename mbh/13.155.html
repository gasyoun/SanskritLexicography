Mahābhārata 13.155 (Anuśāsanaparvan)

1bhīṣma uvāca|
ityuktaḥ sa nṛpastūṣṇīmabhūdvāyustato'bravīt
|
śṛṇu rājannagastyasya māhātmyaṃ brāhmaṇasya ha ||1||

2asurairnirjitā devā nirutsāhāśca te kṛtāḥ
|
yajñāścaiṣāṃ hṛtāḥ sarve pitṝṇāṃ ca svadhāstathā||2||

3karmejyā mānavānāṃ ca dānavairhaihayarṣabha
|
bhraṣṭaiśvaryāstato devāśceruḥ pṛthvīmiti śrutiḥ ||3||

4tataḥ kadācitte rājandīptamādityavarcasam
|
dadṛśustejasā yuktamagastyaṃ vipulavratam ||4||

5abhivādya tu taṃ devāḥ pṛṣṭvā kuśalameva ca
|
idamūcurmahātmānaṃ vākyaṃ kāle janādhipa ||5||

6dānavairyudhi bhagnāḥ sma tathaiśvaryācca bhraṃśitāḥ
|
tadasmānno bhayāttīvrāttrāhi tvaṃ munipuṅgava ||6||

7ityuktaḥ sa tadā devairagastyaḥ kupito'bhavat
|
prajajvāla ca tejasvī kālāgniriva saṃkṣaye ||7||

8tena dīptāṃśujālena nirdagdhā dānavāstadā
|
antarikṣānmahārāja nipetuste sahasraśaḥ ||8||

9dahyamānāstu te daityāstasyāgastyasya tejasā
|
ubhau lokau parityajya gatāḥ kāṣṭhāṃ tu dakṣiṇām ||9||

10balistu yajate yajñamaśvamedhaṃ mahīṃ gataḥ
|
ye'nye'dhasthā mahīsthāśca tena dagdhā mahāsurāḥ ||10||

11tato lokāḥ punaḥ prāptāḥ suraiḥ śāntabhayairnṛpa
|
athainamabruvan devā bhūmiṣṭhānasurān jahi ||11||

12ityuktaḥ prāha devānsa na śaktosmi mahīgatān
|
dagdhuṃ tapo hi kṣīyenme na śakyāmīti pārthiva ||12||

13evaṃ dagdhā bhagavatā dānavāḥ svena tejasā
|
agastyena tadā rājaṃstapasā bhāvitātmanā ||13||

14īdṛśaścāpyagastyo hi kathitaste mayā'nagha
|
bravīmyahaṃ brūhi vā tvamagastyātkṣatriyaṃ varam ||14||

15bhīṣma uvāca|
ityuktaḥ sa tadā tūṣṇīmabhūdvāyustato'bravīt
|
śṛṇu rājan vasiṣṭhasya mukhyaṃ karma yaśasvinaḥ ||15||

16ādityāḥ satramāsanta saro vai mānasaṃ prati
|
vasiṣṭhaṃ manasā gatvā jñātvā tattasya gauravam ||16||

17yajamānāṃstu tān dṛṣṭvā sarvāndīkṣānukarśitān
|
hantumaicchanta śailābhāḥ khalino nāma dānavāḥ||17||

18adūrāttu tatasteṣāṃ brahmadattavaraṃ saraḥ
|
hatā hatā vai tatraite jīvantyāplutya dānavāḥ||18||

19te pragṛhya mahāghorān parvatān parighān drumān
|
vikṣobhayantaḥ salilamutthitaṃ śatayojanam ||19||

20abhyadravanta devāṃste sahasrāṇi daśaiva hi
|
tatastairarditā devāḥ śaraṇaṃ vāsavaṃ yayuḥ ||20||

21sa ca tairvyathitaḥ śakro vasiṣṭhaṃ śaraṇaṃ yayau
|
tato'bhayaṃ dadau tebhyo vasiṣṭho bhagavānṛṣiḥ||21||

22tadā tānduḥkhitānjñātvā ānṛśaṃsyaparo muniḥ
|
ayatnenādahatsarvānkhalinaḥ svena tejasā||22||

23kailāsaṃ prasthitāṃ caiva nadīṃ gaṅgāṃ mahātapāḥ
|
ānayattatsaro divyaṃ tayā bhinnaṃ ca tatsaraḥ||23||

24saro bhinnaṃ tayā nadyā sarayūḥ sā tato'bhavat
|
hatāśca khalino yatra sa deśaḥ khalino'bhavat ||24||

25evaṃ sendrā vasiṣṭhena rakṣitāstridivaukasaḥ
|
brahmadattavarāścaiva hatā daityā mahātmanā ||25||

26etatkarma vasiṣṭhasya kathitaṃ hi mayā'nagha
|
bravīmyahaṃ brūhi vā tvaṃ vasiṣṭhātkṣatriyaṃ varam ||26||
