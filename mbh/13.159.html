Mahābhārata 13.159 (Anuśāsanaparvan)

1yudhiṣṭhira uvāca|
brūhi brāhmaṇapūjāyāṃ vyuṣṭiṃ tvaṃ madhusūdana
|
vettā tvamasya cārthasya veda tvāṃ hi pitāmahaḥ ||1||

2vāsudeva uvāca|
śṛṇuṣvāvāhito rājandvijānāṃ bharatarṣabha
|
yathā tattvena vadato guṇānvai kurusattama ||2||

3dvāravatyāṃ samāsīnaṃ purā māṃ kurunandana
|
pradyumnaḥ paripapraccha brāhmaṇaiḥ parikopitaḥ ||3||

4kiṃ phalaṃ brāhmaṇeṣvasti pūjāyāṃ madhusūdana
|
īśvaratvaṃ kutasteṣāmihaiva ca paratra ca ||4||

5sadā dvijātīnsaṃpūjya kiṃ phalaṃ tatra mānada
|
etadbrūhi sphuṭaṃ sarvaṃ sumahānsaṃśayo'tra me ||5||

6ityukte vacane tasminpradyumnena tathā tvaham
|
pratyabruvaṃ mahārāja yattacchṛṇu samāhitaḥ||6||

7vyuṣṭiṃ brāhmaṇapūjāyāṃ raukmiṇeya nibodha me
|
ete hi somarājāna īśvarāḥ sukhaduḥkhayoḥ||7||

8asminloke raukmiṇeya tathā'muṣmiṃśca putraka
|
brāhmaṇapramukhaṃ saumyaṃ na me'trāsti vicāraṇā ||8||

9brāhmaṇapratipūjāyāmāyuḥ kīrtiryaśo balam
|
lokā lokeśvarāścaiva sarve brāhmaṇapūjakāḥ ||9||

10trivarge cāpavarge ca yaśaḥ śrīrogaśāntiṣu
|
devatāpitṛpūjāsu santoṣyāścaiva no dvijāḥ ||10||

11tatkathaṃ vai nādriyeyamīśvaro'smīti putraka
|
mā te manyurmahābāho bhavatvatra dvijān prati ||11||

12brāhmaṇā hi mahadbhūtamasmin loke paratra ca
|
bhasma kuryurjagadidaṃ kruddhāḥ pratyakṣadarśinaḥ ||12||

13anyānāpi sṛjeyuśca lokānlokeśvarāṃstathā
|
kathaṃ teṣu na varteran samyagjñānātsutejasaḥ ||13||

14avasanmadgṛhe tāta brāhmaṇo haripiṅgalaḥ
|
cīravāsā bilvadaṇḍī dīrghaśmaśruḥ kṛśo mahān ||14||

15dīrghebhyaśca manuṣyebhyaḥ pramāṇādadhiko bhuvi
|
sa svairaṃ carate lokānye divyā ye ca mānuṣāḥ ||15||

16imāṃ gāthāṃ gāyamānaścatvareṣu sabhāsu ca
|
durvāsasaṃ vāsayetko brāhmaṇaṃ satkṛtaṃ gṛhe||16||

17roṣaṇaḥ sarvabhūtānāṃ sūkṣmeṣvapakṛte kṛte
|
paribhāṣāṃ ca me śrutvā ko nu dadyātpratiśrayam||17||

18yo māṃ kaścidvāsayīta na sa māṃ kopayediti
|
yasmānnādriyate kaścitto'haṃ samavāsayam ||18||

19sa saṃbhuṅkte sahasrāṇāṃ bahūnāmannamekadā
|
ekadā so'lpakaṃ bhuṅkte na caivaiti punargṛhān ||19||

20akasmācca prahasati tathā'kasmātpraroditi
|
na cāsya vayasā tulyaḥ pṛthivyāmabhavattadā ||20||

21atha svāvasathaṃ gatvā sa śayyāstaraṇāni ca
|
kanyāścālaṅkṛtā dagdhvā tato vyapagataḥ punaḥ ||21||

22atha māmabravīdbhūyaḥ sa muniḥ saṃśitavrataḥ
|
kṛṣṇa pāyasamicchāmi bhoktumityeva satvaraḥ ||22||

23tadaiva tu mayā tasya cittajñena gṛhe janaḥ
|
sarvāṇyannāni pānāni bhakṣyāścoccāvacāstathā ||23||

24bhavantu satkṛtānīha pūrvameva pracoditaḥ
|
tato'haṃ jvalamānaṃ vai pāyasaṃ pratyavedayam ||24||

25taṃ bhuktvaiva sa tu kṣipraṃ tato vacanamabravīt
|
kṣipramaṅgāni liṃpasva pāyaseneti sa sma ha ||25||

26avimṛśyaiva ca tataḥ kṛtavānasmi tattathā
|
tenocchiṣṭena gātrāṇi śiraścaivābhyamṛkṣayam ||26||

27sa dadarśa tadābhyāśe mātaraṃ te śubhānanām
|
tāmapi smayamānāṃ sa pāyasenābhyalepayam ||27||

28muniḥ pāyasadigdhāṅgīṃ rathe tūrṇamayojayat
|
tamāruhya rathaṃ caiva niryayau sa gṛhānmama ||28||

29agnivarṇo jvalandhīmānsa dvijo rathadhuryavat
|
pratodenātudadbālāṃ rukmiṇīṃ mama paśyataḥ ||29||

30na ca me stokamapyāsīdduḥkhamīrṣyākṛtaṃ tadā
|
tathā sa rājamārgeṇa mahatā niryayau bahiḥ ||30||

31taddṛṣṭvā mahadāścaryaṃ dāśārhā jātamanyavaḥ
|
tatrājalpanmithaḥ kecitsamābhāṣya parasparam ||31||

32brāhmaṇā eva jāyerannānyo varṇaḥ kathañcana
|
ko hyenaṃ rathamāsthāya jīvedanyaḥ pumāniha ||32||

33āśīviṣaviṣaṃ tīkṣṇaṃ tatastīkṣṇataro dvijaḥ
|
brahmāśīviṣadagdhasya nāsti kaściccikitsakaḥ ||33||

34tasminvrajati durdharṣe prāskhaladrukmiṇī pathi
|
tannāmarṣayata śrīmāṃstatastūrṇamacodayat ||34||

35tataḥ paramasaṃkruddho rathātpraskandya sa dvijaḥ
|
padātirutpathenaiva prādravaddakṣiṇāmukhaḥ ||35||

36tamutpathena dhāvantamanvadhāvaṃ dvijottamam
|
tathaiva pāyasādigghaḥ prasīda bhagavanniti ||36||

37tato vilokya tejasvī brāhmaṇo māmuvāca ha
|
jitaḥ krodhastvayā kṛṣṇa prakṛtyaiva mahābhuja ||37||

38na te'parādhamiha vai dṛṣṭavānasmi suvrata
|
prīto'smi tava govinda vṛṇu kāmān yathepsitān ||38||

39prasannasya ca me tāta paśya vyuṣṭiṃ yathāvidhi
|
yāvadeva manuṣyāṇāmanne bhāvo bhaviṣyati ||39||

40yathaivānne tathā teṣāṃ tvayi bhāvo bhaviṣyati
|
yāvacca puṇyā lokeṣu tvayi kīrtirbhaviṣyati ||40||

41triṣu lokeṣu tāvacca vaiśiṣṭyaṃ pratipatsyase
|
supriyaḥ sarvalokasya bhaviṣyasi janārdana ||41||

42yatte bhinnaṃ ca dagdhaṃ ca yacca kiñcidvināśitam
|
sarvaṃ tathaiva draṣṭā'si viśiṣṭaṃ vā janārdana ||42||

43yāvadetatpraliptaṃ te gātreṣu madhusūdana
|
ato mṛtyubhayaṃ nāsti yāvadicchasi cācyuta ||43||

44na tu pādatale lipte kasmātte putrakādya vai
|
naitanme priyamityevaṃ sa māṃ prīto'bravīttadā ||44||

45ityukto'haṃ śarīraṃ svaṃ dadarśa śrīsamāyutam
|
rukmiṇīṃ cābravītprītaḥ sarvastrīṇāṃ varaṃ yaśaḥ ||45||

46kīrtiṃ cānuttamāṃ loke samavāpsyasi śobhane
|
na tvāṃ jarā vā rogo vā vaivarṇyaṃ cāpi bhāvinī ||46||

47sprakṣyanti puṇyagandhā ca kṛṣṇamārādhayiṣyasi
|
ṣoḍaśānāṃ sahasrāṇāṃ vadhūnāṃ keśavasya ha ||47||

48variṣṭhā ca salokyā ca keśavasya bhaviṣyasi
|
tava mātaramityuktvā tato māṃ punarabravīt ||48||

49prasthitaḥ sumahātejā durvāsāgniriva jvalan
|
eṣaiva te buddhirastu brāhmaṇānprati keśava ||49||

50ityuktvā sa tadā putra tatraivāntaradhīyata
|
tasminnantarhite cāhamupāṃśu vratamācaram ||50||

51yatkiñcidbrāhmaṇo brūyātsarvaṃ kuryāmiti prabho
|
etadvratamahaṃ kṛtvā mātrā te saha putraka ||51||

52tataḥ paramahṛṣṭātmā prāviśaṃ gṛhameva ca
|
praviṣṭamātraśca gṛhe sarvaṃ paśyāmi tannavam ||52||

53yadbhinnaṃ yacca vai dagdhaṃ tena vipreṇa putraka
|
tato'haṃ vismayaṃ prāptaḥ sarvaṃ dṛṣṭvā navaṃ dṛḍham ||53||

54apūjayaṃ ca manasā raukmiṇeya sadā dvijān
|
ityahaṃ raukmiṇeyasya pṛcchato bharatarṣabha ||54||

55māhātmyaṃ dvijamukhyasya sarvamākhyātavāṃstadā
|
tathā tvamapi kaunteya brāhmaṇānsatataṃ prabho ||55||

56pūjayasva mahābhāgānvāgbhirdānaiśca nityadā
|
evaṃ vyuṣṭimahaṃ prāpto brāhmaṇasya prasādajām
|
yacca māmāha bhīṣmo'yaṃ tatsatyaṃ bharatarṣabha ||56||
