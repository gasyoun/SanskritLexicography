Mahābhārata 6.64 (Bhīṣmaparvan)

1sañjaya uvāca|
tato bhūriśravā rājansātyakiṃ navabhiḥ śaraiḥ
|
prāvidhyadbhṛśasaṃkruddhastotrairiva mahādvipam ||1||

2kauravaṃ sātyakiścaiva śaraiḥ sannataparvabhiḥ
|
avārayada‌meyātmā sarvalokasya paśyataḥ ||2||

3tato duryodhano rājā soda‌ryaiḥ parivāritaḥ
|
saumadattiṃ raṇe yattaḥ samantāt paryavārayat ||3||

4taṃ caiva pāṇḍavāḥ sarve sātyakiṃ rabhasaṃ raṇe
|
parivārya sthitāḥ saṃkhye samantātsumahaujasaḥ ||4||

5bhīmasenastu saṃkruddho gadāmudyamya bhārata
|
duryodhanamukhān sarvān putrāṃste paryavārayat ||5||

6rathairanekasāhastraiḥ krodhāmarṣasamanvitaḥ
|
nandakastava putrastu bhīmasenaṃ mahābalam ||6||

7vivyādha viśikhaiḥ ṣaḍbhiḥ kaṅkapatraiḥ śilāśitaiḥ
|
duryodhanaśca samare bhīmasenaṃ mahāratham ||7||

8ājaghānorasi kruddho mārgaṇairnavabhiḥ śitaiḥ
|
tato bhīmo mahābāhuḥ svarathaṃ sumahābalaḥ ||8||

9āruroha rathaśreṣṭhaṃ viśokaṃ cedamabravīt
|
ete mahārathāḥ śūrā dhārtarāṣṭrāḥ samāgatāḥ ||9||

10māmeva bhṛśasaṃkruddhā hantumabhyudyatā yudhi
|
manorathadrumo'smākaṃ cintito bahuvārṣikaḥ ||10||

11saphalaḥ sūta cādyeha yo'haṃ paśyāmi sodarān
|
yatrāśokasamutkṣiptā reṇavo rathanemibhiḥ ||11||

12prayāsyantyantarikṣaṃ hi śaravṛndairdigantare
|
tatra tiṣṭhati sannaddhaḥ svayaṃ rājā suyodhanaḥ ||12||

13bhrātaraścāsya sannaddhāḥ kulaputrā madotkaṭāḥ
|
etānadya haniṣyāmi paśyataste na saṃśayaḥ ||13||

14tasmānmamāśvān saṃgrāme yattaḥ saṃyaccha sārathe
|
evamuktvā tataḥ pārthastava putraṃ viśāṃpate ||14||

15vivyādha niśitaistīkṣṇaiḥ śaraiḥ kanakabhūṣaṇaiḥ
|
nandakaṃ ca tribhirbāṇairabhyavidhyatstanāntare ||15||

16taṃ tu duryodhanaḥ ṣaṣṭyā viddhvā bhīmaṃ mahābalam
|
tribhiranyaiḥ suniśitairviśokaṃ pratyavidhyata ||16||

17bhīmasya ca raṇe rājan dhanuściccheda bhāsuram
|
muṣṭideśe bhṛśaṃ tīkṣṇaistribhirbhallairhasanniva ||17||

18samare prekṣya yantāraṃ viśokaṃ tu vṛkodaraḥ
|
pīḍitaṃ viśikhaistīkṣṇaistava putreṇa dhanvinā ||18||

19amṛṣyamāṇaḥ saṃrabdho dhanurdivyaṃ parāmṛśat
|
putrasya te mahārāja vadhārthaṃ bharatarṣabha ||19||

20samādadhatsusaṃkruddhaḥ kṣuragraṃ lomavāhinam
|
tena ciccheda nṛpaterbhīmaḥ kārmukamuttamam ||20||

21sopaviddhya dhanuśchinnaṃ putraste krodhamūrcchitaḥ
|
anyatkārmukamādatta satvaraṃ vegavattaram ||21||

22sandadhe viśikhaṃ ghoraṃ kālamṛtyusamaprabham
|
tenājaghāna saṃkruddho bhīmasenaṃ stanāntare ||22||

23sa gāḍhaviddho vyathitaḥ syandanopastha āviśat
|
sa niṣaṇṇo rathopasthe mūrchāmabhijagāma ha ||23||

24taṃ dṛṣṭvā vyathitaṃ bhīmamabhimanyupurogamāḥ
|
nāmṛṣyanta maheṣvāsāḥ pāṇḍavānāṃ mahārathāḥ ||24||

25tatastu tumulāṃ vṛṣṭiṃ śastrāṇāṃ tigmatejasām
|
pātayāmāsuravyagrāḥ putrasya tava mūrdhani ||25||

26pratilabhya tataḥ saṃjñāṃ bhīmaseno mahābalaḥ
|
duryodhanaṃ tribhirviddhvā punarvivyādha pañcabhiḥ ||26||

27śalyaṃ ca pañcaviṃśatyā śarairvivyādha pāṇḍavaḥ
|
rukmapuṅkhairmaheṣvāsaḥ sa viddho vyapayādraṇāt ||27||

28pratyudyayustato bhīmaṃ tava putrāścaturdaśa
|
senāpatiḥ suṣeṇaśca jalasandhaḥ sulocanaḥ ||28||

29ugro bhīmaratho bhīmo vīrabāhuralolupaḥ
|
durmukho duṣpradharṣaśca vivitsurvikaṭaḥ samaḥ ||29||

30visṛjanto bahun bāṇān krodhasaṃraktalocanāḥ
|
bhīmasenamabhidrutya vivyadhuḥ sahitā bhṛśam ||30||

31putrāṃstu tava saṃprekṣya bhīmaseno mahābalaḥ
|
sṛkkiṇī vilihanvīraḥ paśumadhye yathā vṛkaḥ ||31||

32abhipatya mahābāhurgarutmāniva vegitaḥ
|
senāpateḥ kṣurapreṇa śiraściccheda pāṇḍavaḥ ||32||

33saṃprahasya ca hṛṣṭātmā tribhirbāṇairmahābhujaḥ
|
jalasandhaṃ vinirbhidya so'nayadyamasādanam ||33||

34suṣeṇaṃ ca tato hatvā preṣayāmāsa mṛtyave
|
ugrasya saśirastrāṇaṃ śiraścandropamaṃ bhuvi ||34||

35pātayāmāsa bhallena kuṇḍalābhyāṃ vibhūṣitam
|
vīrabāhuṃ ca saptatyā sāśvaketuṃ sasārathim ||35||

36nināya samare vīraḥ paralokāya pāṇḍavaḥ
|
bhīmabhīmarathau cobhau bhīmaseno hasanniva ||36||

37putrau te durmadau rājannanayadyamasādanam
|
tataḥ sulocanaṃ bhīmaḥ kṣurapreṇa mahāmṛdhe ||37||

38miṣatāṃ sarvasainyānāmanayadyamasādanam
|
putrāstu tava taṃ dṛṣṭvā bhīmasenaparākramam ||38||

39śeṣā ye'nye'bhavaṃstatra te bhīmasya bhayārditāḥ
|
vipradrutā diśo rājan vadhyamānā mahātmanā ||39||

40tato'bravīcchāntanavaḥ sarvāneva mahārathān
|
eṣa bhīmo raṇe kruddho dhārtarāṣṭrānmahārathān ||40||

41yathā prāgryān yathā jyeṣṭhān yathā śūrāṃśca saṅgatān
|
nipātayatyugradhanvā taṃ pragṛhṇīta māciram ||41||

42evamuktāstataḥ sarve dhārtarāṣṭrasya sainikāḥ
|
abhyadravanta saṃkruddhā bhīmasenaṃ mahābalam ||42||

43bhagadattaḥ prabhinnena kuñjareṇa viśāṃpate
|
abhyayātsahasā tatra yatra bhīmo vyavasthitaḥ ||43||

44āpatanneva ca raṇe bhīmasenaṃ śilīmukhaiḥ
|
adṛśyaṃ samare cakre jīmūta iva bhāskaram ||44||

45abhimanyumukhāstattu nāmṛṣyanta mahārathāḥ
|
bhīmasyācchādanaṃ saṃkhye svabāhubalamāśritāḥ ||45||

46ta enaṃ śaravarṣeṇa samantāt paryavārayan
|
gajaṃ ca śaravṛṣṭyā tu bibhiduste samantataḥ ||46||

47sa śastravṛṣṭyā'bhihataḥ samastaistairmahārathaiḥ
|
prāgjyotiṣagajo rājannānāliṅgaiḥ sutejanaiḥ ||47||

48sañjātarudhirotpīḍaḥ prekṣaṇīyo'bhavadraṇe
|
gabhastibhirivārkasya saṃsyūto jalado mahān ||48||

49sañcodito madasrāvī bhagadattena vāraṇaḥ
|
abhyadhāvata tān sarvān kālotsṛṣṭa ivāntakaḥ ||49||

50dviguṇaṃ javamāsthāya kampayaṃścaraṇairmahīm
|
tasya tatsumahadrūpaṃ dṛṣṭvā sarve mahārathāḥ ||50||

51asahyaṃ manyamānāśca nātipramanaso'bhavan
|
tatastu nṛpatiḥ kruddho bhīmasenaṃ stanāntare ||51||

52ājaghāna mahārāja śareṇānataparvaṇā
|
so'tividdho maheṣvāsastena rājñā mahārathaḥ ||52||

53mūrcchayā'bhiparītātmā dhvajayaṣṭiṃ samāśrayat
|
tāṃstu bhītān samālakṣya bhīmasenaṃ ca mūrcchitam ||53||

54nanāda balavannādaṃ bhagadattaḥ pratāpavān
|
tato ghaṭotkaco rājan prekṣya bhīmaṃ tathāgatam ||54||

55saṃkruddho rākṣaso ghorastatraivāntaradhīyata
|
sa kṛtvā dāruṇāṃ māyāṃ bhīrūṇāṃ bhayavardhinīm ||55||

56adṛśyata nimeṣārdhāddhorarūpaṃ samāsthitaḥ
|
airāvaṇaṃ samārūḍhaḥ sa vai māyākṛtaṃ svayam ||56||

57tasya cānye'pi diṅnāgā babhūvuranuyāyinaḥ
|
añjano vāmanaścaiva mahāpadmaśca suprabhaḥ ||57||

58traya ete mahānāgā rākṣasaiḥ samadhiṣṭhitāḥ
|
mahākāyāstridhā rājan prasravanto madaṃ bahu ||58||

59tejovīryabalopetā mahābalaparākramāḥ
|
ghaṭotkacastu svaṃ nāgaṃ codayāmāsa taṃ tadā ||59||

60sagajaṃ bhagadattaṃ tu hantukāmaḥ paraṃtapaḥ
|
te cānye coditā nāgā rākṣasaistairmahābalaiḥ ||60||

61paripetuḥ susaṃrabdhāścaturdaṃṣṭrāścaturdiśam
|
bhagadattasya taṃ nāgaṃ viṣāṇairabhyapīḍayan ||61||

62sa pīḍyamānastairnāgairvedanārtaḥ śarāhataḥ
|
anadatsumahānāda‌mindrāśanisamasvanam ||62||

63tasya taṃ nadato nādaṃ sughoraṃ bhīmaniḥsvanam
|
śrutvā bhīṣmo'bravīddroṇaṃ rājānaṃ ca suyodhanam ||63||

64eṣa yudhyati saṃgrāme haiḍimbena durātmanā
|
bhagadatto maheṣvāsaḥ kṛcchre ca parivartate ||64||

65rākṣasaśca mahākāyaḥ sa ca rājā'tikopanaḥ
|
etau sametau samare kālamṛtyusamāvubhau ||65||

66śrūyate caiva hṛṣṭānāṃ pāṇḍavānāṃ mahāsvanaḥ
|
hastinaścaiva sumahān bhītasya ruditadhvaniḥ ||66||

67tatra gacchāma bhadraṃ vo rājānaṃ parirakṣitum
|
arakṣyamāṇaḥ samare kṣipraṃ prāṇān vimokṣyati ||67||

68te tvaradhvaṃ mahāvīryāḥ kiṃ cireṇa prayāmahe
|
mahān hi vartate raudraḥ saṃgrāmo lomaharṣaṇaḥ ||68||

69bhaktaśca kulaputraśca śūraśca pṛtanāpatiḥ
|
yuktaṃ tasya paritrāṇaṃ kartumasmābhiracyuta ||69||

70bhīṣmasya tadvacaḥ śrutvā sarva eva mahārathāḥ
|
droṇabhīṣmau puraskṛtya bhagadattaparīpsayā ||70||

71uttamaṃ javamāsthāya prayayuryatra so'bhavat
|
tān‌ prayātān samālokya yudhiṣṭhirapurogamāḥ ||71||

72pañcālāḥ pāṇḍavaiḥ sārdhaṃ pṛṣṭato'nuyayuḥ parān
|
tānyanīkānyathālokya rākṣasendraḥ pratāpavān ||72||

73nanāda sumahānādaṃ visphoṭamaśaneriva
|
tasya taṃ ninadaṃ śrutvā dṛṣṭvā nāgāṃśca yudhyataḥ ||73||

74bhīṣmaḥ śāntanavo bhūyo bhāradvājamabhāṣata
|
na rocate me saṅgrāmo haiḍimbena durātmanā ||74||

75balavīryasamāviṣṭaḥ sasahāyaśca sāṃpratam
|
naiṣa śakyo yudhā jetumapi vajrabhṛtā svayam ||75||

76labdhalakṣaḥ prahārī ca vayaṃ ca śrāntavāhanāḥ
|
pañcālaiḥ pāṇḍaveyaiśca divasaṃ kṣatavikṣatāḥ ||76||

77tanna me rocate yuddhaṃ pāṇḍavairjitakāśibhiḥ
|
ghuṣyatāmavahāro'dya śvo yotsyāmaḥ paraiḥ saha ||77||

78pitāmahavacaḥ śrutvā tathā cakruḥ sma kauravāḥ
|
upāyenāpayānaṃ te ghaṭotkacabhayārditāḥ ||78||

79kauraveṣu nivṛtteṣu pāṇḍavā jitakāśinaḥ
|
siṃhanādān bhṛśaṃ cakruḥ śaṅkhān dadhmuśca bhārata ||79||

80evaṃ tadabhavadyuddhaṃ divasaṃ bharatarṣabha
|
pāṇḍavānāṃ kurūṇāṃ ca puraskṛtya ghaṭotkacam ||80||

81kauravāstu tato rājan prayayuḥ śibiraṃ svakam
|
vrīḍamānā niśākāle pāṇḍaveyaiḥ parājitāḥ ||81||

82śaravikṣatagātrāstu pāṇḍuputrā mahārathāḥ
|
yuddhe sumanaso bhūtvā jagmuḥ svaśibiraṃ prati ||82||

83puraskṛtya mahārāja bhīmasenaghaṭotkacau
|
pūjayantastadā'nyonyaṃ mudā paramayā yutāḥ ||83||

84nadanto vividhānnādāṃstūryasvanavimiśritān
|
siṃhanādāṃśca kurvanto vimiśrān śaṅkhaniḥsvanaiḥ ||84||

85vinadanto mahātmānaḥ kampayantaśca medinīm
|
ghaṭṭayantaśca marmāṇi tava putrasya māriṣa ||85||

86prayātāḥ śibirāyaiva niśākāle paraṃtapa
|
duryodhanastu nṛpatirdīno bhrātṛvadhena ca ||86||

87muhūrtaṃ cintayāmāsa bāṣpaśokasamākulaḥ
|
tataḥ kṛtvā vidhiṃ sarvaṃ śibirasya yathāvidhi
|
pradadhyau śokasaṃtapto bhrātṛvyasanakarśitaḥ ||87||
