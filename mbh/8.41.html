Mahābhārata 8.41 (Karṇaparvan)

1sañjaya uvāca|
māriṣādhiratheḥ śrutvā vāco yuddhābhinandinaḥ
|
śalyo'bravītpunaḥ karṇaṃ nidarśanamidaṃ vacaḥ ||1||

2jāto'haṃ yajvānāṃ vaṃśe saṅgrāmeṣvanivartinām
|
rājñāṃ mūrdhābhiṣiktānāṃ svayaṃ dharmaparāyaṇaḥ ||2||

3yathaiva matto madyena tvaṃ tathā lakṣyase vṛṣa
|
tathā'dya tvāṃ pramādyantaṃ cikitseyaṃ suhṛttayā ||3||

4imāṃ kākopamāṃ karṇa procyamānāṃ nibodha me
|
śrutvā yatheṣṭaṃ kuryāstvaṃ nihīna kulapāṃsana ||4||

5nāhamātmani kiñcidvai kilbiṣaṃ karṇa saṃsmare
|
yena māṃ tvaṃ mahābāho hantumicchasyanāgasam ||5||

6avaśyaṃ tu mayā vācyaṃ buddhyatā tvaddhitāhitam
|
viśeṣato rathasthena rājñaścaiva hitaiṣiṇā ||6||

7samaṃ ca viṣamaṃ caiva rathinaśca balābalam
|
śramaḥ khedaśca satataṃ hayānāṃ rathinā saha ||7||

8āyudhasya parijñānaṃ rutaṃ ca mṛgapakṣiṇām
|
bhāraścāpyatibhāraśca śalyānāṃ ca pratikriyā ||8||

9astrayogaśca yuddhaṃ ca nimittāni tathaiva ca
|
sarvametanmayā jñeyaṃ rathasyāsya kuṭumbinā ||9||

10atastvāṃ kathaye karṇa nidarśanamidaṃ punaḥ
|
vaiśyaḥ kila samudrānte prabhūtadhanadhānyavān ||10||

11yajvā dānapatiḥ kṣāntaḥ svakarmastho'bhavacchuciḥ
|
bahuputraḥ priyāpatyaḥ sarvabhūtānukampakaḥ ||11||

12rājño dharmapradhānasya rāṣṭre vasati nirbhayaḥ
|
putrāṇāṃ tasya bālānāṃ kumārāṇāṃ yaśasvinām ||12||

13kāko bahūnāmabhavaducchiṣṭakṛtabhojanaḥ
|
tasmai sadā prayacchanti vaiśyaputrāḥ kumārakāḥ ||13||

14māṃsodanaṃ, dadhi, kṣīraṃ, pāyasaṃ, madhusarpiṣī
|
sa cocchiṣṭabhṛtaḥ kāko vaiśyaputraiḥ kumārakaiḥ ||14||

15sadṛśānpakṣiṇo dṛptaḥ śreyasaścādhicikṣipe
|
atha haṃsāḥ samudrānte kadācidatipātinaḥ ||15||

16garuḍasya gatau tulyāścakrāṅgā hṛṣṭacetasaḥ
|
kumārakāstadā haṃsān dṛṣṭvā kākamathābruvan ||16||

17bhavāneva viśiṣṭo hi patatribhyo vihaṅgama
|
pratāryamāṇastaiḥ sarvairalpabuddhibhiraṇḍajaḥ ||17||

18tadvacaḥ satyamityeva maurkhyāddarpācca manyate
|
tānso'bhipatya jijñāsuḥ ka eṣāṃ śreṣṭhabhāgiti ||18||

19ucchiṣṭadarpitaḥ kāko bahūnāṃ dūrapātinām
|
teṣāṃ yaṃ pravaraṃ mene haṃsānāṃ dūrapātinām ||19||

20tamāhvayata durbuddhiḥ patāva iti pakṣiṇam
|
tacchrutvā prāhasanhaṃsā ye tatrāsansamāgatāḥ ||20||

21bhāṣato bahu kākasya balinaḥ patatāṃ varāḥ
|
idamūcuḥ sma cakrāṃgā vacaḥ kākaṃ vihaṅgamāḥ ||21||

22haṃsā ūcuḥ | vayaṃ haṃsāścarāmemāṃ pṛthivīṃ mānasaukasaḥ
|
pakṣiṇāṃ ca vayaṃ nityaṃ dūrapātena pūjitāḥ ||22||

23kathaṃ haṃsaṃ nu balinaṃ cakrāṅgaṃ dūrapātinam
|
kāko bhūtvā nipatane samāhvayasi durmate ||23||

24kathaṃ tvaṃ patitā kāka sahāsmābhirbravīhi tat
|
atha haṃsavaco mūḍhaḥ kutsayitvā punaḥ punaḥ
|
prajagādottaraṃ kākaḥ katthano jātilāghavāt ||24||

25kāka uvāca|
śatamekaṃ ca pātānāṃ patitā'smi na saṃśayaḥ
|
śatayojanamekaikaṃ vicitraṃ vividhaṃ tathā ||25||

26uḍḍīnamavaḍīnaṃ ca praḍīnaṃ ḍīnameva ca
|
niṃḍīnamatha saṃḍīnaṃ tiryakḍīnagatāni ca ||26||

27viḍīnaṃ pariḍīnaṃ ca parāḍīnaṃ suḍīnakam
|
abhiḍīnaṃ mahāḍīnaṃ nirḍīnamatiḍīnakam ||27||

28avaḍīnaṃ praḍīnaṃ ca saṃḍīnaṃ ḍīnaḍīnakam
|
saṃḍīnoḍḍīnaḍīnaṃ ca punarḍīnaviḍīnakam ||28||

29saṃpātaṃ samudīpaṃ ca tato'nyadvyatiriktakam
|
gatāgatapratigataṃ bahvīśca nikulīnakāḥ ||29||

30kartā'smi miṣatāṃ vo'dya tato drakṣyatha me balam
|
teṣāmanyatamenāhaṃ patiṣyāmi vihāyasam ||30||

31pradiśadhvaṃ yathānyāyaṃ kena haṃsāḥ patāmyaham
|
te vai dhruvaṃ viniścitya patadhvaṃ na mayā saha ||31||

32pātairebhiḥ khalu khagāḥ patituṃ khe nirāśraye
|
evamukte tu kākena prahasyaiko vihaṅgamaḥ ||32||

33uvāca kākaṃ rādheya vacanaṃ tannibodha me
|
haṃsa uvāca|
śatamekaṃ ca pātānāṃ tvaṃ kāka patitā dhruvam ||33||

34ekameva tu yaṃ pātaṃ viduḥ sarve vihaṅgamāḥ
|
tamahaṃ patitā kāka nānyaṃ jānāmi kañcana ||34||

35pata tvamapi tāmrākṣa yena pātena manyase
|
atha kākāḥ prajahasurye tatrāsansamāgatāḥ ||35||

36kathamekena pātena haṃsaḥ pātaśataṃ jayet
|
ekenaiva śatasyaiṣa pātenābhipatiṣyati ||36||

37haṃsasya patitaṃ kāko balavānāśu vikramaḥ
|
prapetatuḥ spardhayā ca tatastau haṃsavāyasau ||37||

38ekapātī ca cakrāṅgaḥ kākaḥ pātaśatena ca
|
patitā vā'tha cakrāṅgaḥ patitā vā'tha vāyasaḥ ||38||

39visismāpayiṣuḥ pātairācakṣāṇo''tmanaḥ kriyāḥ
|
atha kākasya citrāṇi patitāni muhurmuhuḥ ||39||

40dṛṣṭvā pramuditāḥ kākā vineduradhikaiḥ svaraiḥ
|
haṃsāṃścāvahasanti sma prāvadannapriyāṇi ca ||40||

41utpatyotpatya ca muhurmuhūrtamiti ceti ca
|
vṛkṣāgrebhyaḥ sthalebhyaśca nipatantyutpatanti ca ||41||

42kurvāṇā vividhān rāvānāśaṃsanto jayaṃ tathā
|
haṃsastu mṛdunaikena vikrāntumupacakrame ||42||

43pratyahīyata kākācca muhūrtamiva māriṣa
|
avamanya ca haṃsāṃstānidaṃ vacanamabruvan ||43||

44yo'sāvutpatito haṃsaḥ so'sāvevaṃ prahīyate
|
atha haṃsaḥ sa tacchrutvā prāpatatpaścimāṃ diśam ||44||

45uparyupari vegena sāgaraṃ makarālayam
|
tato bhīḥ prāviśatkākaṃ tadā tatra vicetasam ||45||

46dvīpadrumānapaśyantaṃ nipātārthe śramānvitam
|
nipateyaṃ kva nu śrānta iti tasmiñjalārṇave ||46||

47aviṣahyaḥ samudro hi bahusattvagaṇālayaḥ
|
mahāsattvaśatodbhāsī nabhaso'pi viśiṣyate ||47||

48gāṃbhīryāddhi samudrasya na viśeṣaṃ hi sūtaja
|
digambarāṃbhasaḥ karṇa samudrasthā vidurjanāḥ ||48||

49vidūrapātāttoyasya kiṃ punaḥ karṇa vāyasaḥ
|
atha haṃso'pyatikramya muhūrtamiti ceti ca ||49||

50avekṣamāṇastaṃ kākaṃ nāśakadvyapasarpitum
|
atikramya ca cakrāṅgaḥ kākaṃ taṃ samudaikṣata ||50||

51yāvadgatvā patatyeṣa kāko māmiti cintayan
|
tataḥ kāko bhṛśaṃ śrānto haṃsamabhyāgamattadā ||51||

52taṃ tathā hīyamānaṃ tu haṃso dṛṣṭvā'bravīdidam
|
ujjihīrṣurnimajjantaṃ smaransatpuruṣavratam ||52||

53haṃsa uvāca|
bahūni patitāni tvamācakṣāṇo muhurmuhuḥ
|
pātasya vyāharaṃścedaṃ na no guhyaṃ prabhāṣase ||53||

54kiṃ nāma patitaṃ kāka yattvaṃ patasi sāṃpratam
|
jalaṃ spṛśasi pakṣābhyāṃ tuṇḍena ca punaḥ punaḥ ||54||

55prabrūhi katame tatra pāte vartasi vāyasa
|
ehyehi kāka śīghraṃ tvameṣa tvāṃ pratipālaye ||55||

56śalya uvāca|
sa pakṣābhyāṃ spṛśannārtastuṇḍena ca jalaṃ tadā
|
dṛṣṭo haṃsena duṣṭātmannidaṃ haṃsaṃ tato'bravīt ||56||

57apaśyannaṃbhasaḥ pāraṃ nipataṃśca śramānvitaḥ
|
pātavegapramathito haṃsaṃ kāko'bravīdidam ||57||

58vayaṃ kākāḥ kuto nāma carāmaḥ kākavāśikāḥ
|
haṃsa prāṇaiḥ prapadye tvāmudakāntaṃ nayasva mām ||58||

59sa pakṣābhyāṃ spṛśannārtastuṇḍena ca mahārṇave
|
kāko dṛḍhapariśrāntaḥ sahasā nipapāta ha ||59||

60sāgarāṃbhasi taṃ dṛṣṭvā patitaṃ dīnacetasam
|
mriyamāṇamidaṃ kākaṃ haṃso vākyamuvāca ha ||60||

61śatamekaṃ ca pātānāṃ patāmyahamanusmara
|
ślāghamānastvamātmānaṃ kāka bhāṣitavānasi ||61||

62sa tvamekaśataṃ pātaṃ patannabhyadhiko mayā
|
kathamevaṃ pariśrāntaḥ patito'si mahārṇave ||62||

63pratyuvāca tataḥ kākaḥ sīda‌māna idaṃ vacaḥ
|
upariṣṭhaṃ tadā haṃsamabhivīkṣya prasādayan ||63||

64kāka uvāca|
ucchiṣṭadarpito haṃsa manyetmānaṃ suparṇavat
|
avamanya bahūṃścāhaṃ kākānanyāṃśca pakṣiṇaḥ ||64||

65prāṇairhaṃsa prapadye tvāṃ dvīpāntaṃ prāpayasva mām
|
yadyahaṃ svastimān haṃsa sva deśaṃ prāpnuyāṃ vibho ||65||

66na kañcidavamanye'hamāpado māṃ samuddhara
|
tamevaṃvādinaṃ dīnaṃ vilapantamacetanam ||66||

67kāka kāketi vāśantaṃ nimajjantaṃ mahārṇave
|
kṛpayā''dāya haṃsastaṃ jalaklinaṃ sudurdaśam ||67||

68padbhyāmutkṣipya vegena pṛṣṭhamāropayacchanaiḥ
|
āropya pṛṣṭhaṃ haṃsastaṃ kākaṃ tūrṇaṃ vicetanam ||68||

69ājagāma punardvīpaṃ spardhayā petaturyataḥ
|
saṃsthāpya taṃ cāpi punaḥ samāśvāsya ca khecaram ||69||

70gato yathepsitaṃ deśaṃ haṃso mana ivāśugaḥ
|
evamucchiṣṭapuṣṭaḥ sa kāko haṃsaparājitaḥ ||70||

71balaṃ vīryaṃ mahatkarṇaṃ tyaktvā kṣāntimupāgataḥ
|
ucchiṣṭabhojanaḥ kāko yathā vaiśyakule purā ||71||

72evaṃ tvamucchiṣṭabhṛto dhārtarāṣṭrairna saṃśayaḥ
|
sadṛśān śreyasaścāpi sarvānkarṇāvamanyase ||72||

73droṇadrauṇikṛpairgupto bhīṣmeṇānyaiśca kauravaiḥ
|
virāṭanagare pārthamekaṃ kiṃ nāvadhīstadā ||73||

74yatra vyastāḥ samastāśca nirjitāḥ sya kirīṭinā
|
śṛgālā iva siṃhena kva ta vīryamabhūttadā ||74||

75bhrātaraṃ nihataṃ dṛṣṭvā samare savyasācinā
|
paśyatāṃ kuruvīrāṇāṃ prathamaṃ tvaṃ palāyitaḥ ||75||

76tathā dvaitavane karṇa gandharvaiḥ samabhidrutaḥ
|
kurūn samagrānutsṛjya prathamaṃ tvaṃ palāyitaḥ ||76||

77hatvā jitvā ca gandharvāṃścitrasenamukhānraṇe
|
karṇa duryodhanaṃ pārthaḥ sabhāryaṃ samamokṣayat ||77||

78punaḥ prabhāvaḥ pārthasya paurāṇaḥ keśavasya ca
|
kathitaḥ karṇa rāmeṇa sabhāyāṃ rājasaṃsadi ||78||

79satataṃ ca tvamaśrauṣīrvacanaṃ droṇabhīṣmayoḥ
|
avadhyau vadataḥ kṛṣṇau sannidhau ca mahīkṣitām ||79||

80kiyattattatpravakṣyāmi yena yena dhanañjayaḥ
|
tvatto'tiriktaḥ sarvebhyo bhūtebhyo brāhmaṇo yathā ||80||

81idānīmeva draṣṭāsi pradhāne syandane sthitau
|
putraṃ ca vasudevasya kuntīputraṃ ca pāṇḍavam ||81||

82yathāśrayata cakrāṅgaṃ vāyaso buddhimāsthitaḥ
|
tathāśrayasva vārṣṇeyaṃ pāṇḍavaṃ ca dhanañjayam ||82||

83yadā tvaṃ yudhi vikrāntau vāsudevadhanañjayau
|
draṣṭāsyekarathe karṇa tadā naivaṃ vadiṣyasi ||83||

84yadā śaraśataiḥ pārtho darpaṃ tava vadhiṣyati
|
tadā tvamantaraṃ draṣṭā ātmanaścārjunasya ca ||84||

85devāsuramanuṣyeṣu prakhyātau yau narottamau
|
tau māvamaṃsthā maurkhyāttvaṃ khadyota iva rocatau ||85||

86sūryācandramasau yadvattadvadarjunakeśavau
|
prākāśyenābhivikhyātau tvaṃ tu khadyotavannṛṣu ||86||

87evaṃ vidvan māvamaṃsthāḥ sūtaputrācyutārjunau
|
nṛsiṃhau tau mahātmānau joṣamāssva vikatthane ||87||
