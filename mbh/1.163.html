Mahābhārata 1.163 (Ādiparvan)

1yudhiṣṭhira uvāca|
upapannamidaṃ mātastvayā yadbuddhipūrvakam
|
ārtasya brāhmaṇasyaitadanukrośādidaṃ kṛtam||1||

2dhruvameṣyati bhīmo'yaṃ nihatya puruṣādakam
|
sarvathā brāhmaṇasyārthe yadanukrośavatyasi||2||

3yathā tvidaṃ na vindeyurnarā nagaravāsinaḥ
|
tathā'yaṃ brāhmaṇo vācyaḥ parigrāhyaśca yatnataḥ||3||

4vaiśaṃpāyana uvāca|
tato rātryāṃ vyatītāyāmannamādāya pāṇḍavaḥ
|
bhīmaseno yayau tatra yatrāsau puruṣādakaḥ||4||

5āsādya tu vanaṃ tasya rakṣasaḥ pāṇḍavo balī
|
ājuhāva tato nāmnā tadannamupapādayan||5||

6tataḥ sa rākṣasaḥ kruddho bhīmasya vacanāttadā
|
ājagāma susaṃkruddho yatra bhīmo vyavasthitaḥ||6||

7mahākāyo mahāvego dārayanniva medinīm
|
lohitākṣaḥ karālaśca lohitaśmaśrumūrdhajaḥ||7||

8ākarṇādbhinnavaktraśca śaṅkukarṇo vibhīṣaṇaḥ
|
triśikhāṃ bhrukuṭiṃ kṛtvā saṃdaśya daśanacchadam||8||

9bhuñjānamannaṃ taṃ dṛṣṭvā bhīmasenaṃ sa rākṣasaḥ
|
vivṛtya nayane kruddha idaṃ vacanamabravīt||9||

10ko'yamannamidaṃ bhuṅkte madarthamupakalpitam
|
paśyato mama durbuddhiryiyāsuryamasādanam||10||

11bhīmasenastataḥ śrutvā prahasanniva bhārata
|
rākṣasaṃ tamanādṛtya bhuṅkta eva parāṅmukhaḥ||11||

12ravaṃ sa bhairavaṃ kṛtvā samudyamya karāvubhau
|
abhyadravadbhīmasenaṃ jighāṃsuḥ puruṣādakaḥ||12||

13tathāpi paribhūyainaṃ prekṣamāṇo vṛkodaraḥ
|
rākṣasaṃ bhuṅkta evānnaṃ pāṇḍavaḥ paravīrahā||13||

14amarṣeṇa tu saṃpūrṇaḥ kuntīputraṃ vṛkodaram
|
jaghāna pṛṣṭhe pāṇibhyāmubhābhyāṃ pṛṣṭhataḥ sthitaḥ||14||

15tathā balavatā bhīmaḥ pāṇibhyāṃ bhṛśamāhataḥ
|
naivāvalokayāmāsa rākṣasaṃ bhuṅkta eva saḥ||15||

16tataḥ sa bhūyaḥ saṃkruddho vṛkṣamādāya rākṣasaḥ
|
tāḍayiṣyaṃstadā bhīmaṃ punarabhyadravadbalī||16||

17tato bhīmaḥ śanairbhuktvā tadannaṃ puruṣarṣabhaḥ
|
vāyurpaspṛśya saṃhṛṣṭastasthau yudhi mahābalaḥ||17||

18kṣiptaṃ kruddhena taṃ vṛkṣaṃ pratijagrāha vīryavān
|
savyena pāṇinā bhīmaḥ prahasanniva bhārata||18||

19tataḥ sa punarudyamya vṛkṣānbahuvidhānbalī
|
prāhiṇodbhīmasenāya tasmai bhīmaśca pāṇḍavaḥ||19||

20tadvṛkṣayuddhamabhavanmahīruhavināśanam
|
ghorarūpaṃ mahārāja nararākṣasarājayoḥ||20||

21nāma viśrāvya tu bakaḥ samabhidrutya pāṇḍavam
|
bhujābhyāṃ parijagrāha bhīmasenaṃ mahābalam||21||

22bhīmaseno'pi tadrakṣaḥ parirabhya mahābhujaḥ
|
visphurantaṃ mahābāhuṃ vicakarṣa balādbalī||22||

23sa kṛṣyamāṇo bhīmena karṣamāṇaśca pāṇḍavam
|
samayujyata tīvreṇa klamena puruṣādaḥ||23||

24tayorvegena mahatā pṛthivī samakampata
|
pādapāṃśca mahākāyāṃścūrṇayāmāsatustadā||24||

25hīyamānaṃ tu tadrakṣaḥ samīkṣya puruṣādakam
|
niṣpiṣya bhūmau jānubhyāṃ samājaghne vṛkodaraḥ||25||

26tato'sya jānunā pṛṣṭhamavapīḍya balādiva
|
bāhunā parijagrāha dakṣiṇena śirodharāma||26||

27savyena ca kaṭīdeśe gṛhya vāsasi pāṇḍavaḥ
|
tadrakṣo dviguṇaṃ cakre ravantaṃ bhairavaṃ ravam||27||

28tato'sya rudhiraṃ vaktrātprādurāsīdviśāṃpate
|
bhajyamānasya bhīmena tasya ghorasya rakṣasaḥ||28||
