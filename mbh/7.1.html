Mahābhārata 7.1 (Droṇaparvan)

1janamejaya uvāca|
tamapratimasattvaujobalavīryasamanvitam
|
hataṃ devavrataṃ śrutvā pāñcālyena śikhaṇḍinā||1||

2dhṛtarāṣṭrastato rājā śokavyākulalocanaḥ
|
kimaceṣṭata viprarṣe hate pitari vīryavān||2||

3tasya putro hi bhagavān bhīṣmadroṇamukhai rathaiḥ
|
parājitya maheṣvāsān pāṇḍavān rājyamicchati||3||

4tasmin hate tu bhagavan ketau sarvadhanuṣmatām
|
yadaceṣṭata kauravyastanme brūhi tapodhana||4||

5vaiśampāyana uvāca|
nihataṃ pitaraṃ śrutvā dhṛtarāṣṭro janādhipaḥ
|
lebhe na śāntiṃ kauravyaścintāśokaparāyaṇaḥ||5||

6tasya cintayato duḥkhamaniśaṃ pārthivasya tat
|
ājagāma viśuddhātmā punargāvalgaṇistadā||6||

7śibirāt sañjayaṃ prāptaṃ niśi nāgāhvayaṃ puram
|
āmbikeyo mahārāja dhṛtarāṣṭro'nvapṛcchata||7||

8śrutvā bhīṣmasya nidhanamaprahṛṣṭamanā bhṛśam
|
putrāṇāṃ jayamākāṅkṣan vilalāpāturo yathā||8||

9dhṛtarāṣṭra uvāca|
saṃśocya tu mahātmānaṃ bhīṣmaṃ bhīmaparākramam
|
kimakārṣuḥ paraṃ tāta kuravaḥ kālacoditāḥ||9||

10tasmin vinihate śūre durādharṣe mahātmani
|
kiṃ nu svitkuravo'kārṣurnimagnāḥ śokasāgare||10||

11tadudīrṇaṃ mahat sainyaṃ trailokyasyāpi sañjaya
|
bhayamutpādayet tīvraṃ pāṇḍavānāṃ mahātmanām||11||

12ko hi dauryodhane sainye pumānāsīnmahārathaḥ
|
yaṃ prāpya samare vīrā na trasyanti mahābhaye ||12||

13devavrate tu nihate kurūṇāmṛṣabhe tadā
|
kimakārṣurnṛpatayastanmamācakṣva sañjaya||13||

14sañjaya uvāca|
śṛṇu rājannekamanā vacanaṃ bruvato mama
|
yat te putrāstadā'kārṣurhate devavrate sṛdhe||14||

15nihate tu tadā bhīṣme rājan satyaparākrame
|
tāvakāḥ pāṇḍaveyāśca prādhyāyanta pṛthak pṛthak||15||

16vismitāśca prahṛṣṭāśca kṣatradharmaṃ niśamya te
|
svadharmaṃ nindamānāste praṇipatya mahātmane||16||

17śayanaṃ kalpayāmāsurbhīṣmāyāmitakarmaṇe
|
sopadhānaṃ naravyāghra śaraiḥ sannataparvabhiḥ||17||

18vidhāya rakṣāṃ bhīṣmāya samābhāṣya parasparam
|
anumānya ca gāṅgeyaṃ kṛtvā cāpi pradakṣiṇam||18||

19krodhasaṃraktanayanāḥ samavetya parasparam
|
punaryuddhāya nirjagmuḥ kṣatriyāḥ kālacoditāḥ||19||

20tatastūryaninādaiśca bherīṇāṃ ninadena ca
|
tāvakānāmanīkāni pareṣāṃ ca viniryayuḥ||20||

21vyāvṛtte'ryamṇi rājendra patite jāhnavīsute
|
amarṣavaśamāpannāḥ kālopahatacetasaḥ||21||

22anādṛtya vacaḥ pathyaṃ gāṅgeyasya mahātmanaḥ
|
niryayurbharataśreṣṭhāḥ śastrāṇyādāya satvarāḥ||22||

23mohāt tava saputrasya vadhācchāntanavasya ca
|
kauravyā mṛtyusādbhūtāḥ sahitāḥ sarvarājabhiḥ||23||

24ajāvaya ivāgopā vane śvāpadasaṅkule
|
bhṛśamudvignamanaso hīnā devavratena te||24||

25patite bharataśreṣṭhe babhūva kuruvāhinī
|
dyaurivāpetanakṣatrā hīnaṃ khamiva vāyunā||25||

26vipannasasyeva mahī vākcaivāsaṃskṛtā tathā
|
āsurīva yathā senā nigṛhīte nṛpe balau||26||

27vidhaveva varārohā śuṣkatoyeva nimnagā
|
vṛkairiva vane ruddhā pṛṣatī hatayūthapā||27||

28śarabhāhatasiṃheva mahatī girikandarā
|
bhāratī bharataśreṣṭhe patite jānhavīsute||28||

29viṣvagvātāhatā rugṇā naurivāsīnmahārṇave
|
balibhiḥ pāṇḍavairvīrairlabdhalakṣairbhṛśārditā||29||

30sā tadāsīdbhṛśaṃ senā vyākulāśvarathadvipā
|
vipannabhūyiṣṭhanarā kṛpaṇā dhvastamānasā||30||

31tasyāṃ trastā nṛpatayaḥ sainikāśca pṛthagvidhāḥ
|
pātāla iva majjanto hīnā devavratena te||31||

32karṇaṃ hi kuravo'smārṣuḥ sa hi devavratopamaḥ
|
sarvaśastrabhṛtāṃ śreṣṭhaṃ rocamānamivātithim||32||

33bandhumāpadgatasyeva tamevopāgamanmanaḥ
|
cukruśuḥ karṇa karṇeti tatra bhārata pārthivāḥ||33||

34rādheyaṃ hitamasmākaṃ sūtaputraṃ tanutyajam
|
sa hi nāyudhyata tadā daśāhāni mahāyaśāḥ||34||

35sāmātyabandhuḥ karṇo vai tamānayata mā ciram
|
bhīṣmeṇa hi mahābāhuḥ sarvakṣatrasya paśyataḥ||35||

36ratheṣu gaṇyamāneṣu balavikramaśāliṣu
|
saṃkhyāto'rdharathaḥ karṇo dviguṇaḥ sannararṣabhaḥ||36||

37rathātirathasaṃkhyāyāṃ yo'graṇīḥ śūrasaṃmataḥ
|
sāsurānapi deveśān raṇe yo yoddhumutsahet||37||

38sa tu tenaiva kopena rājan gāṅgeyamuktavān
|
tvayi jīvati kauravya nāhaṃ yotsye kadācana||38||

39tvayā tu pāṇḍaveyeṣu nihateṣu mahāmṛdhe
|
duryodhanamanujñāpya vanaṃ yāsyāmi kaurava||39||

40pāṇḍavairvā hate bhīṣme tvayi svargamupeyuṣi
|
hantāsmyekarathenaiva kṛtsnān yān manyase rathān||40||

41evamuktvā mahābāhurdaśāhāni mahāyaśāḥ
|
nāyudhyata tataḥ karṇaḥ putrasya tava saṃmate||41||

42bhīṣmaḥ samaravikrāntaḥ pāṇḍaveyasya bhārata
|
jaghāna samare yodhānasaṃkhyeyaparākramaḥ||42||

43tasmiṃstu nihate śūre satyasandhe mahaujasi
|
tvatsutāḥ karṇamasmārṣustartukāmā iva plavam||43||

44tāvakāstava putrāśca sahitāḥ sarvarājabhiḥ
|
hā karṇa iti cākrandan kālo'yamiti cābruvan||44||

45evaṃ te sma hi rādheyaṃ sūtaputraṃ tanutyajam
|
cukruśuḥ sahitā yodhāstatra tatra mahābalāḥ||45||

46jāmadagnyābhyanujñātamastre durvārapauruṣam
|
agamanno manaḥ karṇaṃ bandhumātyayikeṣviva||46||

47sa hi sakto raṇe rājaṃstrātumasmān mahābhayāt
|
tridaśāniva govindaḥ satataṃ sumahābhayāt||47||

48vaiśampāyana uvāca|
tathā tu sañjayaṃ karṇaṃ kīrtayantaṃ punaḥ punaḥ
|
āśīviṣavaducchvasya dhṛtarāṣṭro'bravīdidam||48||

49dhṛtarāṣṭra uvāca|
yat tadvaikartanaṃ karṇamagamadvo manastadā
|
apyapaśyata rādheyaṃ sūtaputraṃ tanutyajam||49||

50api tanna mṛṣākārṣīt kañcit satyaparākramaḥ
|
saṃbhrāntānāṃ tadārtānāṃ trastānāṃ trāṇamicchatām||50||

51api tat pūrayāṃcakre dhanurdharavaro yudhi
|
yattadvinihate bhīṣme kauravāṇāmapākṛtam||51||

52tat khaṇḍaṃ pūrayan karṇaḥ pareṣāmādadhadbhayam
|
sa hi vai puruṣavyāghro loke sañjaya kathyate||52||

53ārtānāṃ bāndhavānāṃ ca krandatāṃ ca viśeṣataḥ
|
parityajya raṇe prāṇāṃstatrāṇārthe ca śarma ca
|
kṛtavān mama putrāṇāṃ jayāśāṃ saphalāmapi||53||
