Mahābhārata 5.8 (Udyogaparvan)

1vaiśampāyana uvāca|
śalyaḥ śrutvā tu dūtānāṃ sainyena mahatā vṛtaḥ
|
abhyayāt pāṇḍavān rājan saha putrairmahārathaiḥ ||1||

2tasya senāniveśo'bhūdadhyardhamiva yojanam
|
tathā hi vipulāṃ senāṃ bibharti sa nararṣabhaḥ ||2||

3akṣauhiṇīpatī rājan mahāvīryaparākramaḥ
|
vicitrakavacāḥ śūrā vicitradhvajakārmukāḥ ||3||

4vicitrābharaṇāḥ sarve vicitrarathavāhanāḥ
|
vicitrasragdharāḥ sarve vivitrāmbarabhūṣaṇāḥ ||4||

5svadeśaveṣābharaṇā vīrāḥ śatasahasraśaḥ
|
tasya senāpraṇetāro babhūvuḥ kṣatriyarṣabhāḥ ||5||

6vyathayanniva bhūtāni kampayanniva medinīm
|
śanairviśrāmayan senāṃ sa yayau yena pāṇḍavaḥ ||6||

7tato duryodhanaḥ śrutvā mahātmānaṃ mahāratham
|
upāyāntamabhidrutya svayamānarca bharata ||7||

8kārayāmāsa pūjārthaṃ tasya duryodhanaḥ sabhāḥ
|
ramaṇīyeṣu deśeṣu ratnacitrāḥ svalaṅkṛtāḥ ||8||

9śilpibhirvividhaiścaiva krīḍāstatra prayojitāḥ
|
tatra mālyāni māṃsāni bhakṣyaṃ peyaṃ ca satkṛtam ||9||

10kūpāśca vividhākārā manoharṣavivardhanāḥ
|
vāpyaśca vividhākārā audakāni gṛhāṇi ca ||10||

11sa tāḥ sabhāḥ samāsādya pūjyamāno yathā'maraḥ
|
duryodhanasya sacivairdeśe deśe samantataḥ ||11||

12ājagāma sabhāmanyāṃ devāvasathavarcasam
|
sa tatra viṣayairyuktaḥ kalyāṇairatimānuṣaiḥ ||12||

13mene'bhyadhikamātmānamavamene purandaram
|
papraccha sa tataḥ preṣyān prahṛṣṭaḥ kṣatriyarṣabhaḥ ||13||

14yudhiṣṭhirasya puruṣāḥ ke'tra cakruḥ sabhā imāḥ
|
ānīyantāṃ sabhākārāḥ pradeyārhā hi me matāḥ ||14||

15prasādameṣāṃ dāsyāmi kuntīputro'numanyatām
|
duryodhanāya tat sarvaṃ kathayanti sma vismitāḥ ||15||

16saṃprahṛṣṭo yadā śalyo diditsurapi jīvitam
|
gūḍho duryodhanastatra darśayāmāsa mātulam ||16||

17taṃ dṛṣṭvā madrarājaśca jñātvā yatnaṃ ca tasya tam
|
pariṣvajyābravīt prīta iṣṭo'rtho gṛhyatāmiti ||17||

18duryodhana uvāca|
satyavāgbhava kalyāṇa varo vai mama dīyatām
|
sarvasenāpraṇetā vai bhavān bhavitumarhati ||18||

19vaiśampāyana uvāca|
kṛtamityabravīcchalyaḥ kimanyatkriyatāmiti
|
kṛtamityeva gāndhāriḥ pratyuvāca punaḥ punaḥ ||19||

20śalya uvāca|
gaccha duryodhana puraṃ svakameva nararṣabha
|
ahaṃ gamiṣye draṣṭuṃ vai yudhiṣṭhiramarindamam ||20||

21dṛṣṭvā yudhiṣṭhiraṃ rājan kṣiprameṣye narādhipa
|
avaśyaṃ cāpi draṣṭavyaḥ pāṇḍavaḥ puruṣarṣabhaḥ ||21||

22duryodhana uvāca|
kṣipramāgamyatāṃ rājan pāṇḍavaṃ vīkṣya pārthiva
|
tvayyadhīnāḥ sma rājendra varadānaṃ smarasva naḥ ||22||

23śalya uvāca|
kṣiprameṣyāmi bhadraṃ te gacchasva svapuraṃ nṛpa
|
pariṣvajya tathā'nyonyaṃ śalyaduryodhanāvubhau ||23||

24sa tathā śalyamāmantrya punarāyāt svakaṃ puram
|
śalyo jagāma kaunteyānākhyātuṃ karma tasya tat ||24||

25upaplavyaṃ sa gatvā tu skandhāvāraṃ praviśya ca
|
pāṇḍavānatha tān sarvān śalyastatra dadarśa ha ||25||

26sametya ca mahābāhuḥ śalyaḥ pāṇḍusutaistadā
|
pādyamarghyaṃ ca gāṃ caiva pratigṛhṇādyathavidhi ||26||

27tataḥ kuśalapūrvaṃ hi madrarājo'risūdanaḥ
|
prītyā paramayā yuktaḥ samāśliṣyadyudhiṣṭhiram||27||

28tathā bhīmārjunau kṛṣṇau sasrīyau ca yamāvubhau
|
āsane copaviṣṭastu śalyaḥ pārthamuvāca ha||28||

29kuśalaṃ rājaśārdūla kaccitte kurunandana
|
araṇyavāsāddiṣṭyā'si vimukto jayatāṃ vara ||29||

30suduṣkaraṃ kṛtaṃ rājannirjane vasatā tvayā
|
bhrātṛbhiḥ saha rājendra kṛṣṇāyā cānayā saha ||30||

31ajñātavāsaṃ ghoraṃ ca vasatā duṣkaraṃ kṛtam
|
duḥkhameva kutaḥ saukhyaṃ bhraṣṭarāṣṭrasya bhārata ||31||

32duḥkhasyaitasya mahato dhārtarāṣṭrakṛtasya vai
|
avāpsyasi sukhaṃ rājan hatvā śatrūn parantapa||32||

33viditaṃ te mahārāja lokatantraṃ narādhipa
|
tasmāllobhakṛtaṃ kiñcittava tāta na vidyate||33||

34rājarṣiṇāṃ purāṇānāṃ mārgamanviccha bhārata
|
dāne tapasi satye ca bhava tāta yudhiṣṭhira ||34||

35kṣamā damaśca satyaṃ ca ahiṃsā ca yudhiṣṭhira
|
adbhutaśca punarlokastvayi rājan pratiṣṭhitaḥ||35||

36mṛdurvadānyo brahmaṇyo dānto dharmaparāyaṇaḥ
|
dharmāste viditā rājan bahavo lokasākṣikāḥ ||36||

37sarvaṃ jagadidaṃ tāta viditaṃ te parantapa
|
diṣṭyā kṛcchramidaṃ rājan pāritaṃ bharatarṣabha ||37||

38diṣṭyā paśyāmi rājendra dharmātmānaṃ sahānugam
|
nistīrṇaṃ duṣkaraṃ rāja~stvāṃ dharmanicayaṃ prabho ||38||

39vaiśampāyana uvāca|
tato'syākathayadrājā duryodhanasamāgamam
|
tacca śuśrūṣitaṃ sarvaṃ varadānaṃ ca bhārata ||39||

40yudhiṣṭhira uvāca|
sukṛtaṃ te kṛtaṃ rājan prahṛṣṭenāntarātmanā
|
duryodhanasya yadvīra tvayā vācā pratiśrutam ||40||

41ekaṃ tvicchāmi bhadraṃ te kriyamāṇaṃ mahīpate
|
rājannakartavyamapi kartumarhasi sattama ||41||

42mama tvavekṣayā vīra śṛṇu vijñāpayāmi te
|
bhavāniha mahārāja vāsudevasamo yudhi ||42||

43karṇārjunābhyāṃ saṃprāpte dvairathe rājasattama
|
karṇasya bhavatā kāryaṃ sārathyaṃ nātra saṃśayaḥ ||43||

44tatra pālyo'rjuno rājan yadi matpriyamicchasi
|
tejovadhaśca te kāryaḥ sauterasmajjayāvahaḥ ||44||

45akartavyamapi hyetat kartumarhasi mātula| śalya uvāca|
śrṛṇu pāṇḍava te bhadraṃ yadbravīṣi mahātmanaḥ
|
tejovadhanimittaṃ māṃ sūtaputrasya saṅgame ||45||

46ahaṃ tasya bhaviṣyāmi saṅgrāme sārathirdhruvam
|
vāsudevena hi samaṃ nityaṃ māṃ sa hi manyate ||46||

47tasyāhaṃ kuruśārdūla pratīpamahitaṃ vacaḥ
|
dhruvaṃ saṃkathayiṣyāmi yoddhukāmasya saṃyuge ||47||

48yathā sa hṛtadarpaśca hṛtatejāśca pāṇḍava
|
bhaviṣyati sukhaṃ hantuṃ satyametadbravīmi te ||48||

49evametat kariṣyāmi yathā tāta tvamāttha mām
|
yaccānyadapi śakṣyāmi tatkariṣyāmi te priyam ||49||

50yacca duḥkhaṃ tvayā prāptaṃ dyūte vai kṛṣṇayā saha
|
paruṣāṇi ca vākyāni sūtaputrakṛtāni vai ||50||

51jaṭāsurāt parikleśaḥ kīcakācca mahādyute
|
draupadyā'dhigataṃ sarvaṃ damayantyā yathā'śubham ||51||

52sarvaṃ duḥkhamidaṃ vīra sukhodarkaṃ bhaviṣyati
|
nātra manyustvayā kāryo vidhirhi balavattaraḥ ||52||

53duḥkhāni hi mahātmānaḥ prāpnuvanti yudhiṣṭhira
|
devairapi hi duḥkhāni prāptāni jagatīpate ||53||

54indreṇa śrūyate rājan sabhāryeṇa mahātmanā
|
anubhūtaṃ mahadduḥkhaṃ devarājena bhārata ||54||
