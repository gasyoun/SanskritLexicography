Mahābhārata 8.37 (Karṇaparvan)

1sañjaya uvāca|
dṛṣṭvā karṇaṃ maheṣvāsaṃ yuyutsuṃ samavasthitam
|
cukruśuḥ kuravaḥ sarve hṛṣṭarūpāḥ samantataḥ ||1||

2tato dundubhinirghoṣairbherīṇāṃ ninadena ca
|
bāṇaśabdaiśca vividhairgarjitaiśca tarasvinām ||2||

3niryayustāvakā yuddhe mṛtyuṃ kṛtvā nivartanam
|
prayāte tu tataḥ karṇe yodheṣu muditeṣu ca ||3||

4cacāla pṛthivī rājan vavāśa ca suvistaram
|
niḥsaranto vyadṛśyanta sūryātsapta mahāgrahāḥ ||4||

5ulkāpātāśca saṃjajñurdiśāṃ dāhāstathaiva ca
|
śuṣkāśanyaśca saṃpeturvavurvātāśca bhairavāḥ||5||

6mṛgapakṣigaṇāścaiva pṛtanāṃ bahuśastava
|
apasavyaṃ tadā cakrurvedayanto mahābhayam ||6||

7prathitasya ca karṇasya nipetusturagā bhuvi
|
asthivarṣaṃ ca patitamantarikṣādbhayānakam ||7||

8jajvaluścaiva śastrāṇi dhvajāścaiva cakampire
|
aśrūṇi ca vyamuñcanta vāhanāni viśāṃpate ||8||

9ete cānye ca bahava utpātāstatra dāruṇāḥ
|
samutpeturvināśāya kauravāṇāṃ sudāruṇāḥ ||9||

10na ca tān gaṇayāmāsuḥ sarve daivena mohitāḥ
|
prasthitaṃ sūtaputraṃ ca jayetyūcurnarādhipāḥ
|
nirjitānpāṇḍavāṃścaiva menire tatra kauravāḥ ||10||

11tato rathasthaḥ paravīrahantā bhīṣma-droṇāvativīryau samīkṣya
|
samujjvalan bhāskarapāvakābho vaikartano'sau rathakuñjaro nṛpa ||11||

12sa śalyamābhāṣya jagāda vākyaṃ pārthasya karmātiśayaṃ vicintya
|
mānena darpaṇa vidahyamānaḥ krodhena dīpyanniva niḥśvasaṃśca ||12||

13nāhaṃ mahendrādapi vajrapāṇeḥ kruddhādbibhemyāyudhavānrathasthaḥ
|
dṛṣṭvā hi bhīṣmapramukhān śayānānatīva māṃ hyasthiratā jahāti ||13||

14mahendra-viṣṇupratimāvaninditau rathāśva-nāgapravarapramāthinau
|
avadhyakalpau nihatau yadā paraistato na me'pyasti raṇe'dya sādhvasam ||14||

15samīkṣya saṃkhye'tibalānnarādhipān sasūta-mātaṅga-rathānparairhatān
|
kathaṃ na sarvānahitānraṇe'vadhīnmahāstravidbrāhmaṇapuṅgavo guruḥ ||15||

16sa saṃsmaran droṇamahaṃ mahāhave bravīmi satyaṃ kuravo nibodhata
|
na vā madanyaḥ prasahedraṇe'rjunaṃ samāgataṃ mṛtyumivograrūpiṇam ||16||

17śikṣā prasādaśca balaṃ dhṛtiśca droṇe mahāstrāṇi ca saṃnatiśca
|
sa cedagānmṛtyuvaśaṃ mahātmā sarvānanyānāturānadya manye ||17||

18neha dhruvaṃ kiñcidapi pracintayan vidyāṃ loke karmaṇo daivayogāt
|
sūryodaye ko hi vimuktasaṃśayo bhāvaṃ kurvītādya gurau nipātite ||18||

19na nūnamastrāṇi balaṃ parākramaḥ kriyāḥ sunītaṃ paramāyudhāni vā
|
alaṃ manuṣyasya sukhāya vartituṃ tathā hi yuddhe nihataḥ parairguruḥ ||19||

20hutāśanādityasamānatejasaṃ parākrame viṣṇu-purandaropamam
|
naye bṛhaspatyuśanoḥ sadā samaṃ na cainamastraṃ tadupāsta duḥsaham ||20||

21saṃprākruṣṭe ruditastrī-kumāre parābhūte pauruṣe dhārtarāṣṭre
|
mayā kṛtyamiti jānāmi śalya prayāhi tasmāddviṣatāmanīkam ||21||

22yatra rājā pāṇḍavaḥ satyasandho vyavasthito bhīmasenārjunau ca
|
vāsudevaḥ sātyakiḥ sṛñjayāśca yamau ca kastān viṣahenmadanyaḥ ||22||

23tasmātkṣipraṃ madrapate prayāhi raṇe pañcālānpāḍavānsṛñjayāṃśca
|
tānvā haniṣyāmi sametya saṃkhye yāsyāmi vā droṇapathā yamāya ||23||

24nanvevāhaṃ na gamiṣyāmi madhye teṣāṃ śūrāṇāmiti māṃ śalya viddhi
|
mitradroho marṣaṇīyo na me'yaṃ tyaktvā prāṇānanuyāsyāmi droṇam ||24||

25prājñasya mūḍhasya ca jīvitānte nāsti pramokṣo'ntakasatkṛtasya
|
ato vidvannabhiyāsyāmi pārthān diṣṭaṃ na śakyaṃ vyativartituṃ vai ||25||

26kalyāṇavṛttaḥ satataṃ hi rājā vaicitravīryasya suto mamāsīt
|
tasyārthasiddhyarthamahaṃ tyajāmi priyān bhogān dustyajaṃ jīvitaṃ ca ||26||

27vaiyāghracarmāṇamakūjanākṣaṃ haimaṃ trikośaṃ rajatatriveṇum
|
rathaprabarhaṃ turagaprabarhairyuktaṃ prādānmahyamimaṃ hi rāmaḥ ||27||

28dhanūṃṣi citrāṇi nirīkṣya śalya dhvajān gadāḥ sāyakāṃścograrūpān
|
asiṃ ca dīptaṃ paramāyudhaṃ ca śaṅkhaṃ ca śubhraṃ svanavantamugram ||28||

29patākinaṃ vajranipātaniḥsvanaṃ sitāśvayuktaṃ śubhatūṇaśobhitam
|
imaṃ samāsthāya rathaṃ ratharṣabhaṃ raṇe haniṣyāmyahamarjunaṃ balāt ||29||

30taṃ cenmṛtyuḥ sarvaharo'bhirakṣet sadā'pramattaḥ samare pāṇḍuputram
|
taṃ vā haniṣyāmi raṇe sametya yāsyāmi vā bhīṣmamukho yamāya ||30||

31yama-varuṇa-kubera-vāsavā vā yadi yugapatsagaṇā mahāhave
|
jugupiṣava ihādya pāṇḍavaṃ kimu bahunā saha tairjayāmi tam ||31||

32sañjaya uvāca|
iti raṇarabhasasya katthatastaduta niśamya vacaḥ saḥ madrarāṭ
|
avahasadavamanya vīryavān pratiṣiṣidhe ca jagāda cottaram ||32||

33śalya uvāca|
virama virama karṇa katthanādatirabhaso'pyativācamuktavān
|
kva ca hi naravaro dhanañjayaḥ kva punaraho puruṣādhamo bhavān ||33||

34yadusadanamupendrapālitaṃ tridaśamivāmararājarakṣitam
|
prasabhamativiloḍya ko haret puruṣavarāvarajāmṛte'rjunāt ||34||

35tribhuvanavibhumīśvareśvaraṃ ka iha pumān bhavamāhvayedyudhi
|
mṛgavadhakalahe ṛte'rjunāt surapativīryasamaprabhāvataḥ ||35||

36asura-sura-mahoragānnarān garuḍa-piśāca-sayakṣarākṣasān
|
iṣubhirajayadagnigauravāt svabhilaṣitaṃ ca havirdadau jayaḥ ||36||

37smarasi nanu yadā parairhṛtaḥ sa ca dhṛtarāṣṭrasuto'pi mokṣitaḥ
|
dinakarasadṛśaiḥ śarottamairyudhā kuruṣu bahūnvinihatya tānarīn ||37||

38prathamamapi palāyite tvayi priyakalahā dhṛtarāṣṭrasūnavaḥ
|
smarasi nanu yadā pramocitāḥ khacaragaṇānavajitya pāṇḍavaiḥ ||38||

39samuditabalavāhanāḥ punaḥ puruṣavareṇa jitāḥ stha gograhe
|
sagurugurusutāḥ sabhīṣmakāḥ kimu na jitaḥ sa tadā tvayā'rjunaḥ ||39||

40idamaparamupasthitaṃ punastava nidhanāya suyuddhamadya vai
|
yadi na ripubhayātpalāyase samaragato'dya hato'si sūtaja ||40||

41sañjaya uvāca|
iti bahu paruṣaṃ prabhāṣati pramanasi madrapatau ripustavam
|
bhṛśamabhiruṣitaḥ parantapaḥ kurupṛtanāpatirāha madrapam ||41||

42karṇa uvāca|
bhavatu bhavatu kiṃ vikatthase nanu mama tasya hi yuddhamudyatam
|
yadi sa jayati māmihāhave tata idamastu sukatthitaṃ tava ||42||

43sañjaya uvāca|
evamastviti madreśa uktvā nottaramuktavān
|
yāhi śalyeti cāpyenaṃ karṇaḥ prāha yuyutsayā ||43||

44sa rathaḥ prayayau śatrūn śvetāśvaḥ śalyasārathiḥ
|
nighnannamitrānsamare tamo ghnansavitā yathā ||44||

45tataḥ prāyātprītimānvai rathena vaiyāghreṇa śvetayujā'tha karṇaḥ
|
sa cālokya dhvajinīṃ pāṇḍavānāṃ dhanañjayaṃ tvarayā paryapṛcchat ||45||
