Mahābhārata 12.108 (Śāntiparvan)

1yudhiṣṭhira uvāca|
mahānayaṃ dharmapatho bahuśākhaśca bhārata
|
kiṃsvideveha dharmāṇāmanuṣṭheyatamaṃ matam||1||

2kiṃ kāryaṃ sarvadharmāṇāṃ garīyo bhavato matam
|
yathāhaṃ paramaṃ dharmamiha ca pretya cāpnuyām||2||

3bhīṣma uvāca|
mātāpitrorgurūṇāṃ ca pūjā bahumatā mama
|
iha yukto naro lokānyaśaśca mahadaśnute||3||

4yacca te'bhyanujānīyuḥ karma tāta supūjitāḥ
|
dharmādharmaviruddhaṃ vā tatkartavyaṃ yudhiṣṭhira||4||

5na ca tairabhyanujñāto dharmamanyaṃ samācaret
|
yaṃ ca te'bhyanujānīyuḥ sa dharma iti niścayaḥ||5||

6eta eva trayo lokā eta evāśramāstrayaḥ
|
eta eva trayo vedā eta eva trayo'gnayaḥ||6||

7pitā vai gārhapatyo'gnirmātā'gnirdakṣiṇaḥ smṛtaḥ
|
gururāhavanīyastu sāgnitretā garīyasī||7||

8triṣvapramādyanneteṣu trīllo~kāṃśca vijeṣyasi
|
pitṛvṛttyā tvimaṃ lokaṃ mātṛvṛttyā tathā param||8||

9brahmalokaṃ gurorvṛttyā niyamena tariṣyasi
|
samyageteṣu vartasva triṣu lokeṣu bhārata||9||

10yaśaḥ prāpsyasi bhadraṃ te dharmaṃ ca sumahatphalam
|
naitānatiśayejjātu nātyaśnīyānna dūṣayet||10||

11nityaṃ paricareccaiva tadvai sukṛtamuttamam
|
kīrtiṃ puṇyaṃ yaśo lokānprāpsyase rājasattama||11||

12sarve tasyādṛtā lokā yasyaite traya ādṛtāḥ
|
anādṛtāstu yasyaite sarvāstasyāphalāḥ kriyāḥ||12||

13na cāyaṃ na paro lokastasya caiva paraṃtapa
|
amānitā nityameva yasyaite guravastrayaḥ||13||

14na cāsminnapare loke yaśastasya prakāśate
|
na cānyadapi kalyāṇaṃ paratra samudāhṛtam||14||

15tebhya eva hi yatsarvaṃ kṛtvā ca visṛjāmyaham
|
tadā'sīnme śataguṇaṃ sahasraguṇameva ca||15||

16tasmānme samprakāśante trayo lokā yudhiṣṭhira
|
daśaiva tu sadā''cāryaḥ śrotriyānatiricyate||16||

17daśācāryānupādhyāya upādhyāyānpitā daśa
|
pitṝndaśa tu mātaikā sarvāṃ vā pṛthivīmapi||17||

18gurutvenābhibhavati nāsti mātṛsamo guruḥ
|
gururgarīyānpitṛto mātṛtaśceti me matiḥ||18||

19ubhau hi mātāpitarau janmanyevopayujyataḥ
|
śarīrameva sṛjataḥ pitā mātā ca bhārata||19||

20ācāryaśiṣṭā yā jātiḥ sā divyā sā'jarāmarā
|
avadhyā hi sadā mātā pitā cāpyapakāriṇau||20||

21na saṃduṣyati tatkṛtvā na ca te dūṣayanti tam
|
dharmāya yatamānānāṃ vidurdevā maharṣibhiḥ||21||

22yaścāvṛṇotyavitathena karmaṇā ṛtaṃ bruvannanṛtaṃ samprayacchan
|
taṃ vai manyeta pitaraṃ mātaraṃ ca tasmai na druhyetkṛtamasya jānan||22||

23vidyāṃ śrutvā ye guruṃ nādriyante pratyāsannā manasā karmaṇā vā
|
teṣāṃ pāpaṃ bhrūṇahatyāviśiṣṭaṃ nānyastebhyaḥ pāpakṛdasti loke
|
yathaiva te gurubhirbhāvanīyāstathā teṣāṃ guravo'bhyarcanīyāḥ||23||

24tasmātpūjayativyāśca saṃvibhajyāśca yatnataḥ
|
guravo'rcayitavyāśca purāṇaṃ dharmabhicchatā||24||

25yena prīṇāti pitaraṃ tena prītaḥ prajāpatiḥ
|
prīṇāti mātaraṃ yena pṛthivī tena pūjitā||25||

26yena prīṇātyupādhyāyaṃ tena syādbrahma pūjitam
|
mātṛtaḥ pitṛtaścaiva tasmātpūjyatamo guruḥ||26||

27ṛṣayaśca hi devāśca prīyante pitṛbhiḥ saha
|
pūjyamāneṣu guruṣu tasmātpūjyatamo guruḥ||27||

28kenacinna ca vṛttena hyavajñeyo gururbhavet
|
na ca mātā na ca pitā manyate yādṛśo guruḥ||28||

29na te'vamānamarhanti na teṣāṃ dūṣayet kṛtam
|
gurūṇāmeva satkāraṃ vidurdevā maharṣibhiḥ||29||

30upādhyāyaṃ pitaraṃ mātaraṃ ca ye'bhidruhyante manasā karmaṇā vā
|
teṣāṃ pāpaṃ bhrūṇahatyāviśiṣṭaṃ tasmānnānyaḥ pāpakṛdasti loke||30||

31bhṛto vṛddho yo na bibharti putraḥ svayonijaḥ pitaraṃ mātaraṃ ca
|
tadvai pāpaṃ bhrūṇahatyāviśiṣṭaṃ tasmānnānya pāpakṛdasti loke||31||

32mitradruhaḥ kṛtaghnasya strīghnasya gurughātinaḥ
|
caturṇāṃ vayameteṣāṃ niṣkṛtiṃ nānuśuśruma||32||

33etatsarvamanirdeśenaivamuktaṃ yatkartavyaṃ puruṣeṇeha loke
|
etacchreyo nānyadasmādviśiṣṭaṃ sarvān dharmānanusṛtyaitaduktam||33||
