Mahābhārata 1.111 (Ādiparvan)

1vaiśaṃpāyana uvāca|
śūro nāma yaduśreṣṭho vasudevapitā'bhavat
|
tasya kanyā pṛthā nāma rūpeṇāpratimā bhuvi||1||

2pitṛṣvasrīyāya sa tāmanapatyāya bhārata
|
agryamagre pratijñāya svasyāpatyaṃ sa satyavāk||2||

3agrajāmatha tāṃ kanyāṃ śūro'nugrahakāṅkṣiṇe
|
pradadau kuntibhojāya sakhā sakhye mahātmane||3||

4niyuktā sā piturgehe brāhmaṇātithipūjane
|
ugraṃ paryacarattatra brāhmaṇaṃ saṃśitavratam||4||

5nigūḍhaniścayaṃ dharme yaṃ taṃ durvāsasaṃ viduḥ
|
tamugraṃ saṃśitātmānaṃ sarvayatnairatoṣayat||5||

6tasyai sa pradadau mantramāpaddharmānvavekṣayā
|
abhicārābhisaṃyuktamabravīccaiva tāṃ muniḥ||6||

7yaṃ yaṃ devaṃ tvametena mantreṇāvāhayiṣyasi
|
tasya tasya prabhāveṇa tava putro bhaviṣyati||7||

8tathoktā sā tu vipreṇa kuntī kautūhalānvitā
|
kanyā satī devamarkamājuhāva yaśasvinī||8||

9sā dadarśa tamāyāntaṃ bhāskaraṃ lokabhāvanam
|
vismitā cānavadyāṅgī dṛṣṭvā tanmahadadbhutam||9||

10tāṃ samāsādya devastu vivasvānidamabravīt
|
ayamasmyasitāpāṅgī brūhi kiṃ karavāṇi te||10||

11kuntyuvāca|
kaścinme brāhmaṇaḥ prādādvaraṃ vidyāṃ ca śatruhan
|
tadvijijñāsayāhvānaṃ kṛtavatyasmi te vibho||11||

12etasminnaparādhe tvāṃ śirasā'haṃ prasādaye
|
yoṣito hi sadā rakṣyā: svāparādhe'pi nityaśaḥ||12||

13sūrya uvāca|
vedāhaṃ sarvamevaitadyaddurvāsā varaṃ dadau
|
saṃtyajya bhayameveha kriyatāṃ saṅgamo mama||13||

14amoghaṃ darśanaṃ mahyamāhūtaścāsmi te śubhe
|
vṛthāhvāne'pi te bhīru doṣaḥ syānnātra saṃśayaḥ||14||

15vaiśaṃpāyana uvāca|
evamuktā bahuvidhaṃ sāntvapūrvaṃ vivasvatā
|
sā tu naicchadvarārohā kanyāhamiti bhārata||15||

16bandhupakṣabhayādbhītā lajjayā ca yaśasvinī
|
tāmarkaḥ punarevedabravīdbharatarṣabha||16||

17matprasādānna te rājñi bhavitā doṣa ityuta
|
evamuktvā sa bhaganānkuntirājasutāṃ tadā||17||

18prakāśakarmā tapanaḥ saṃbabhūva tayā saha
|
tatra vīraḥ samabhavatsarvaśastrabhṛtāṃ varaḥ
|
āmuktakavacaḥ śrīmāndevagarbhaḥ śriyānvitaḥ||18||

19sahajaṃ kavacaṃ bibhratkuṇḍalo dyotitānanaḥ
|
ajāyata sutaḥ karṇaḥ sarvalokeṣu viśrutaḥ||19||

20prādācca tasyai kanyātvaṃ punaḥ sa paramadyutiḥ
|
dattvā ca tapatāṃ śreṣṭho divamācakrame tataḥ||20||

21dṛṣṭvā kumāraṃ jātaṃ sā vārṣṇeyī dīnamānasā
|
ekāgraṃ cintayāmāsa kiṃ kṛtvā sukṛtaṃ bhavet||21||

22gūhamānāpacāraṃ sā bandhupakṣabhayāttadā
|
utsasarja kumāraṃ taṃ jale kuntī mahābalam||22||

23tamutsṛṣṭaṃ jale garbhaṃ rādhābhartā mahāyaśāḥ
|
putratve kalpayāmāsa sabhāryaḥ sūtanandanaḥ||23||

24nāmadheyaṃ ca cakrāte tasya bālasya tāvubhau
|
vasunā saha jāto'yaṃ vasuṣeṇo bhavatviti||24||

25sa vardhamāno balavānsarvāstreṣūdyato'bhavat
|
āpṛṣṭhatāpādādityamupātiṣṭhata vīryavān||25||

26tasminkāle tu japatastasya vīrasya dhīmataḥ
|
nādeyaṃ brāhmaṇeṣvāsītkiṃcidvasu mahītale||26||

27tamindro brāhmaṇo bhūtvā bhikṣārthī samupāgamat
|
kavacaṃ prārthayāmāsa phālgunasya hite rataḥ||27||

28svaśarīrātsamutkṛtya kavacaṃ svaṃ nisargajam
|
viprarūpāya śakrāya dadau karṇaḥ kṛtāñjaliḥ||28||

29pratigṛhya tu deveśastuṣṭastenāsya karmaṇā
|
dadau śaktiṃ surapatirvākyaṃ cedamuvāca ha||29||

30devāsuramanuṣyāṇāṃ gandharvoragarakṣasām
|
yamekaṃ jetumicchethāḥ so'nayā na bhaviṣyati||30||

31prāṅnāma tasya kathitaṃ vasuṣeṇa iti kṣitau
|
karṇo vaikartanaścaiva karmaṇā tena sobhavat||31||
