Mahābhārata 3.245 (Āraṇyakaparvan)

1vaiśampāyana uvāca|
tato divyāstrasampannā gandharvā hemamālinaḥ
|
visṛjantaḥ śarān dīptān samantāt paryavārayan||1||

2catvāraḥ pāṇḍavā vīrā gandharvāśca sahasraśaḥ
|
raṇe sannyapatan rājaṃstadadbhutamivābhavat||2||

3yathā karṇasya ca ratho dhārtarāṣṭrasya cobhayoḥ
|
gandharvaiḥ śataśacchinnau tathā teṣāṃ pracakrire||3||

4tān samāpatato rājan gandharvāñchataśo raṇe
|
pratyagṛhṇan naravyāghrāḥ śaravarṣairanekaśaḥ||4||

5te kīryamāṇāḥ svagamāḥ śaravarṣaiḥ samantataḥ
|
na śekuḥ pāṇḍuputrāṇāṃ samīpe parivartitum||5||

6abhikruddhānabhikruddho gandharvānarjunastadā
|
lakṣayitvā'tha divyāni mahāstrāṇyupacakrame||6||

7sahasrāṇāṃ sahasrāṇi prāhiṇodyamasādanam
|
āgneyenārjunaḥ saṅkhye gandharvāṇāṃ balotkaṭaḥ||7||

8tathā bhīmo maheṣvāsaḥ saṃyuge balināṃ varaḥ
|
gandharvān śataśo rājan jaghāna niśitaiḥ śaraiḥ||8||

9mādrīputrāvapi tathā yudhyamānau balotkaṭau
|
parigṛhyāgrato rājan jaghnatuḥ śataśaḥ parān||9||

10te vadhyamānā gandharvā divyairastrairmahārathaiḥ
|
utpetuḥ svamupādāya dhṛtarāṣṭrasutāṃstataḥ||10||

11sa tānutpatitān dṛṣṭvā kuntīputro dhanañjayaḥ
|
mahatā śarajālena samantāt paryavārayat||11||

12te baddhāḥ śarajālena śakuntā iva pañjare
|
vavarṣurarjunaṃ krodhādgadāśaktyuṣṭivṛṣṭibhiḥ||12||

13gadāśaktyṛṣṭivṛṣṭīstā nihatya paramāstravit
|
gātrāṇi cāhanadbhallairgandharvāṇāṃ dhanañjayaḥ||13||

14śirobhiḥ prapatadbhiśca caraṇairbāhubhistathā
|
aśmavṛṣṭirivābhāti pareṣāmabhavadbhayam||14||

15te vadhyamānā gandharvāḥ pāṇḍavena mahātmanā
|
bhūmiṣṭhamantarikṣasthāḥ śaravarṣairavākiran||15||

16teṣāṃ tu śaravarṣāṇi savyasācī parantapaḥ
|
astraiḥ saṃvārya tejasvī gandharvān pratyavidhyata||16||

17sthūṇākarṇendrajālaṃ ca sauraṃ cāpi tathā'rjunaḥ
|
āgneyaṃ cāpi saumyaṃ ca sasarja kurunandanaḥ||17||

18te dahyamānā gandharvāḥ kuntīputrasya sāyakaiḥ
|
daiteyā iva śakreṇa viṣādamagaman param||18||

19ūrdhvamākramamāṇāśca śarajālena vāritāḥ
|
visarpamāṇā bhallaiśca vāryante savyasācinā||19||

20gandharvāṃstrāsitān dṛṣṭvā kuntīputreṇa bhārata
|
citraseno gadāṃ gṛhya savyasācinamādravat||20||

21tasyābhipatatastūrṇaṃ gadāhastasya saṃyuge
|
gadāṃ sarvāyasīṃ pārthaḥ śaraiściccheda saptadhā||21||

22sa gadāṃ bahudhā dṛṣṭvā kṛttāṃ bāṇaistarasvinā
|
saṃvṛtya vidyayā''tmānaṃ yodhayāmāsa pāṇḍavam||22||

23astrāṇi tasya divyāni samprayuktāni sarvaśaḥ
|
divyairastraistadā vīraḥ paryavārayadarjunaḥ||23||

24sa vāryamāṇastairastrairarjunena mahātmanā
|
gandharvarājo balavān māyayā'ntarhitastadā||24||

25antarhitaṃ tamālakṣya praharantamathārjunaḥ
|
tāḍayāmāsa khacarairdivyāstrapratimantritaiḥ||25||

26antardhānavadhaṃ cāsya cakre kruddho'rjunastadā
|
śabdavedhaṃ samāśritya bahurūpo dhanañjayaḥ||26||

27sa vadhyamānastairastrairarjunena mahātmanā
|
tato'sya darśayāmāsa tadā''tmānaṃ priyaḥ sakhā||27||

28citrasenastathovāca sakhāyaṃ yudhi viddhi mām
|
citrasenamathālakṣya sakhāyaṃ yudhi durbalam||28||

29sañjahārāstramatha tat prasṛṣṭaṃ pāṇḍavarṣabhaḥ
|
dṛṣṭvā tu pāṇḍavāḥ sarve saṃhṛtāstraṃ dhanañjayam||29||

30sañjahruḥ pradrutānaśvān śaravegān dhanūṃṣi ca
|
citrasenaśca bhīmaśca savyasācī yamāvapi
|
pṛṣṭvā kauśalamanyonyaṃ ratheṣvevāvatasthire||30||
