Mahābhārata 9.33 (Śalyaparvan)

1sañjaya uvāca|
evaṃ duryodhane rājan garjamāne muhurmuhuḥ
|
yudhiṣṭhirasya saṅkruddho vāsudevo'bravīdidam||1||

2yadi nāma hyayaṃ yuddhe varayettvāṃ yudhiṣṭhira
|
arjunaṃ nakulaṃ caiva sahadevamathāpi vā||2||

3kimidaṃ sāhasaṃ rājaṃstvayā vyāhṛtamīdṛśam
|
ekameva nihatyājau bhava rājā kuruṣviti||3||

4na samarthānahaṃ manye gadāhastasya saṃyuge
|
etena hi kṛtā yogyā varṣāṇīha trayodaśa||4||

5āyase puruṣe rājan bhīmasenajighāṃsayā
|
kathaṃ nāma bhavetkāryamasyābhirbharatarṣabha||5||

6sāhasaṃ kṛtavāṃstvaṃ tu hyanukrośānnṛpottama
|
nānyamasyānupaśyāmi pratiyoddhāramāhave||6||

7ṛte vṛkodarātpārthātsa ca nātikṛtaśramaḥ
|
tadidaṃ dyūtamārabdhaṃ punareva yathā purā||7||

8viṣamaṃ śakuneścaiva tava caiva viśāmpate
|
balī bhīmaḥ samarthaśca kṛtī rājā suyodhanaḥ||8||

9balavānvā kṛtī veti kṛtī rājanviśiṣyate
|
so'yaṃ rājaṃstvayā śatruḥ same pathi niveśitaḥ||9||

10nyastaścātmā suviṣame kṛcchramāpāditā vayam
|
ko nu sarvānvinirjitya śatrūnekena vairiṇā||10||

11kṛcchraprāptena ca tathā hārayedrājyamāgatam
|
paṇitvā caikapāṇena rocayedevamāhavam||11||

12na hi paśyāmi taṃ loke yo'dya duryodhanaṃ raṇe
|
gadāhastaṃ vijetuṃ vai śaktaḥ syādamaro'pi hi||12||

13na tvaṃ bhīmo na nakulaḥ sahadevo'tha phālgunaḥ
|
jetuṃ nyāyena śaktoḥ vai kṛtī rājā suyodhanaḥ||13||

14sa kathaṃ vadase śatruṃ yudhyasva gadayeti hi
|
ekaṃ ca no nihatyājau bhava rājeti bhārata||14||

15vṛkodaraṃ samāsādya saṃśayo vai jaye hi naḥ
|
nyāyato yudhyamānānāṃ kṛtī hyeṣa mahābalaḥ||15||

16ekaṃ vāsmānnihatya tvaṃ bhava rājeti vai punaḥ
|
nūnaṃ na rājyabhāgeṣā pāṇḍoḥ kuntyāśca santatiḥ||16||

17atyantavanavāsāya sṛṣṭā bhaikṣyāya vā punaḥ
|
bhīmasena uvāca|
madhusūdana mā kārṣīrviṣādaṃ yadunandana||17||

18adya pāraṃ gamiṣyāmi vairasya bhṛśadurgamam
|
ahaṃ suyodhanaṃ saṅkhye haniṣyāmi na saṃśayaḥ||18||

19vijayo vai dhruvaḥ kṛṣṇa dharmarājasya dṛśyate
|
adhyardhena guṇeneyaṃ gadā gurutarī mama||19||

20na tathā dhārtarāṣṭrasya mā kārṣīrmādhava vyathām
|
ahamenaṃ hi gadayā saṃyuge yoddhamutsahe||20||

21bhavantaḥ prekṣakāḥ sarve mama santu janārdana
|
sāmarānapi lokāṃstrīnnānāśastradharān yudhi||21||

22yodhayeyaṃ raṇe kṛṣṇa kimutādya suyodhanam
|
sañjaya uvāca|
tathā sambhāṣamāṇaṃ tu vāsudevo vṛkodaram||22||

23hṛṣṭaḥ sampūjayāmāsa vacanaṃ cedamabravīt
|
tvāmāśritya mahābāho dharmarājo yudhiṣṭhiraḥ||23||

24nihatāriḥ svakāṃ dīptāṃ śriyaṃ prāpto na saṃśayaḥ
|
tvayā vinihatāḥ sarve dhṛtarāṣṭrasutā raṇe||24||

25rājāno rājaputrāśca nāgāśca vinipātitāḥ
|
kaliṅgā māgadhāḥ prācyā gāndhārāḥ kuravastathā||25||

26tvāmāsādya mahāyuddhe nihatāḥ pāṇḍunandana
|
hatvā duryodhana cāpi prayacchorvīṃ sasāgarām||26||

27dharmarājāya kaunteya yathā viṣṇuḥ śacīpateḥ
|
tvāṃ ca prāpya raṇe pāpo dhārtarāṣṭro vinaṃkṣyati||27||

28tvamasya sakthinī bhaṅktvā pratijñāṃ pālayiṣyasi
|
yatnena tu sadā pārtha yoddhavyo dhṛtarāṣṭrajaḥ||28||

29kṛtī ca balavāṃścaiva yuddhaśauṇḍaśca nityadā
|
tatastu sātyakī rājanpūjayāmāsa pāṇḍavam||29||

30pañcālāḥ pāṇḍaveyāśca dharmarājapurogamāḥ
|
tadvaco bhīmasenasya sarva evābhyapūjayan||30||

31tato bhīmabalo bhīmo yudhiṣṭhiramathābravīt
|
sṛñjayaiḥ saha tiṣṭhantaṃ tapantamiva bhāskaram||31||

32ahametena saṅgamya saṃyuge yoddhamutsahe
|
na hi śakto raṇe jetuṃ māmeṣa puruṣādhamaḥ||32||

33adya krodhaṃ vimokṣyāmi nihitaṃ hṛdaye bhṛśam
|
suyodhane dhārtarāṣṭre khāṇḍave'gnimivārjunaḥ||33||

34śalyamadyoddhariṣyāmi tava pāṇḍavahṛcchayam
|
nihatya gadayā pāpamadya rājan sukhī bhava||34||

35adya kīrtimayīṃ mālāṃ pratimokṣye tavānagha
|
prāṇāñśriyaṃ ca rājyaṃ ca mokṣyate'dya suyodhanaḥ||35||

36rājā ca dhṛtarāṣṭro'dya śrutvā putraṃ mayā hatam
|
smariṣyatyaśubhaṃ karma yattacchakunibuddhijam||36||

37ityuktvā bharataśreṣṭho gadāmudyamya vīryavān
|
udatiṣṭhata yuddhāya śakro vṛtramivāhvayan||37||

38tadāhvānamamṛṣyan vai tava putro'tivīryavān
|
pratyupasthita evāśu matto mattamiva dvipam||38||

39gadāhasta tava sutaṃ yuddhāya samupasthitam
|
dadṛśuḥ pāṇḍavāḥ sarve kailāsamiva śṛṅgiṇam||39||

40tamekākinamāsādya dhārtarāṣṭraṃ mahābalam
|
viyūthamiva mātaṅgaṃ samahaṣyanta pāṇḍavāḥ||40||

41na sambhramo na ca bhayaṃ na ca glānirna ca vyathā
|
āsīdduryodhanasyāpi sthitaḥ siṃha ivāhave||41||

42samudyatagadaṃ dṛṣṭvā kailāsamiva śṛṅgiṇam
|
bhīmasenastadā rājan duryodhanamathābravīt||42||

43rājñāpi dhṛtarāṣṭreṇa tvayā cāsmāsu yatkṛtam
|
smara taddṛṣkṛtaṃ karma yadbhūtaṃ vāraṇāvate||43||

44draupadī ca parikliṣṭā sabhāmadhye rajasvalā
|
dyūte yadvijito rājā śakunerbuddhiniścayāt||44||

45yāni cānyāni duṣṭātmanpāpāni kṛtavānasi
|
anāgaḥsu ca pārtheṣu tasya paśya mahatphalam||45||

46tvakṛte nihataḥ śete śaratalpe mahāyaśāḥ
|
gāṅgeyo bharataśreṣṭhaḥ sarveṣāṃ naḥ pitāmahaḥ||46||

47hato droṇaśca karṇaśca hataḥ śalyaḥ pratāpavān
|
vairasta cādikartā'sau śakunirnihato raṇe||47||

48bhrātaraste hatāḥ śūrāḥ putrāśca sahasainikāḥ
|
rājānaśca hatāḥ śūrāḥ samareṣvanivartinaḥ||48||

49ete cānye ca nihatā bahavaḥ kṣatriyarṣabhāḥ
|
prātikāmī tathā pāpo draupadyāḥ keśakṛddhataḥ||49||

50avaśiṣṭastvamevaikaḥ kulaghno'dhamapūruṣaḥ
|
tvāmapyadya haniṣyāmi gadayā nātra saṃśayaḥ||50||

51adya te'haṃ raṇe darpaṃ sarvaṃ nāśayitā nṛpa
|
rājyāśāṃ vipulāṃ rājan pāṇḍaveṣu ca duṣkṛtam||51||

52duryodhana uvāca|
kiṃ katthitena bahunā yuddhyasvādya mayā saha
|
adya te'haṃ vineṣyāmi yuddhaśraddhāṃ vṛkodara||52||

53kiṃ na paśyami māṃ pāpa gadāyuddhe vyavasthitam
|
himavacchikharākārāṃ pragṛhya mahatīṃ gadām||53||

54gadinaṃ ko'dha māṃ pāpa hantumutsahate ripuḥ
|
nyāyato yuddhyamānaśca deveṣvapi purandaraḥ||54||

55mā vṛthā garja kaunteya śāradābhramivājalam
|
darśayasva balaṃ yuddhe yāvattatte'dya vidyate||55||

56tasya tadvacanaṃ śrutvā pāṇḍavāḥ sahasṛñjayāḥ
|
sarve sampūjayāmāsustadvaco vijigīṣavaḥ||56||

57unmattamiva mātaṅgaṃ talaśabdena mānavāḥ
|
bhūyaḥ saṃharṣayāmāsū rājan duryodhanaṃ nṛpam||57||

58bṛṃhanti kuñjarāstatra hayā dveṣanti cāsakṛt
|
śastrāṇi sampradīpyante pāṇḍavānāṃ jayaiṣiṇām||58||
