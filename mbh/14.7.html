Mahābhārata 14.7 (Āśvamedhikaparvan)

1saṃvarta uvāca|
kathamasmi tvayā jñātaḥ kena vā kathito'smi te
|
etadācakṣva me tattvamicchase cenmama priyam||1||

2satyaṃ te bruvataḥ sarve sampatsyante manorathāḥ
|
mithyā ca bruvato mūrdhā śatadhā te sphuṭiṣyati||2||

3marutta uvāca|
nāradena bhavān mahyamākhyāto hyaṭatā pathi
|
guruputrau mameti tvaṃ tato me prītiruttamā||3||

4saṃvarta uvāca|
satyametad bhavānāha sa māṃ jānāti satriṇam
|
kathayasva tadetanme kva na samprati nāradaḥ||4||

5marutta uvāca|
bhavantaṃ kathayitvā tu mama devarṣisattamaḥ
|
tato māmabhyanujñāya praviṣṭo havyavāhanam||5||

6vyāsa uvāca|
śrutvā tu pārthivasyaitat saṃvartaḥ pramudaṃ gataḥ
|
etāvadahamapyevaṃ śaknuyāmiti so'bravīt||6||

7tato maruttamunmatto vācā nirbhartsayanniva
|
rūkṣayā brāhmaṇo rājan punaḥ punarathābravīt||7||

8vātapradhānena mayā svacittavaśavartinā
|
evaṃ vikṛtarūpeṇa kathaṃ yājitumicchasi||8||

9bhrātā mama samarthaśca vāsavena ca saṅgataḥ
|
vartate yājane caiva tena karmāṇi kāraya||9||

10gārhasthyaṃ caiva yājyāśca sarvā gṛhyāśca devatāḥ
|
pūrvajena mamākṣiptaṃ śarīraṃ varjitaṃ tvidam||10||

11nāhaṃ tenānanujñātastvāmāvikṣita karhicit
|
yājayeyaṃ kathañcidvai sa hi pūjyatamo mama||11||

12sa tvaṃ bṛhaspatiṃ gaccha tamanujñāpya cāvraja
|
tato'haṃ yājayiṣye tvāṃ yadi yaṣṭumihecchasi||12||

13marutta uvāca|
bṛhaspatiṃ gataḥ pūrvamahaṃ saṃvarta tacchṛṇu
|
na māṃ kāmayate yājyamasau vāsavakāmyayā||13||

14amaraṃ yājyamāsādya yājayiṣye na mānuṣam
|
śakreṇa pratiṣiddho'haṃ maruttaṃ mā sma yājayaḥ||14||

15spardhate hi mayā vipra sadā hi sa tu pārthivaḥ
|
evamastviti cāpyukto bhrātrā te balasūdanaḥ||15||

16sa māmadhigataṃ premṇā yājyatvena bubhūṣati
|
devarājaṃ samāśritya tad viddhi munipuṅgava||16||

17so'hamicchāmi bhavatā sarvasvenāpi yājitum
|
kāmaye samatikrāntuṃ vāsavaṃ tvatkṛtairguṇaiḥ||17||

18na hi me vartate buddhirgantuṃ brahman bṛhaspatim
|
pratyākhyāto hi tenāsmi tathā'napakṛte sati||18||

19saṃvarta uvāca|
cikīrṣasi yathākāmaṃ sarvametat tvayi dhruvam
|
yadi sarvānabhiprāyān kartā'si mama pārthiva||19||

20yājyamānaṃ mayā hi tvāṃ bṛhaspatipurandarau
|
dviṣetāṃ samabhikruddhāvetadekaṃ samarthayeḥ||20||

21sthairyamatra kathaṃ me syāt sattvaṃ niḥsaṃśayaṃ kuru
|
kupitastvāṃ na hīdānīṃ bhasma kuryāṃ sabāndhavam||21||

22marutta uvāca|
yāvattapetsahasrāṃśustiṣṭheraṃścāpi parvatāḥ
|
tāvallokānna labheyaṃ tyajeyaṃ saṅgataṃ yadi||22||

23mā cāpi śubhabuddhitvaṃ labheyamiha karhicit
|
viuṣayaiḥ saṅgataṃ cāstu tyajeyaṃ saṅgataṃ yadi||23||

24saṃvarta uvāca|
āvikṣita śubhā buddhirvartatāṃ tava karmasu
|
yājanaṃ hi mamāpyeva vartate hṛdi pārthiva||24||

25abhidhāsye ca te rājannakṣayaṃ dravyamuttamam
|
yena devān sagandharvānśakraṃ cābhibhaviṣyasi||25||

26na tu me vartate buddhirdhane yājyeṣu vā punaḥ
|
vipriyaṃ tu kariṣyāmi bhrātuścendrasya cobhayoḥ||26||

27gamayiṣyāmi śakreṇa samatāmapi te dhruvam
|
priyaṃ ca te kariṣyāmi satyametad bravīmi te||27||
