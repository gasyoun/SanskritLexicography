Mahābhārata 3.19 (Āraṇyakaparvan)

1vāsudeva uvāca|
evamuktastu kaunteya sūtaputrastato'bravīt
|
pradyumnaṃ balināṃ śreṣṭaṃ madhuraṃ ślakṣṇamañjasā||1||

2na me bhayaṃ raukmiṇeya saṅgrāme yacchato hayān
|
yuddhajño'smi ca vṛṣṇīnāṃ nātra kiñcidato'nyathā||2||

3āyuṣmannupadeśastu sārathye vartatāṃ smṛtaḥ
|
sarvārtheṣu rathī rakṣyastvaṃ cāpi bhṛśapīḍitaḥ||3||

4tvaṃ hi śālvaprayuktena śareṇābhihato bhṛśam
|
kaśmalābhihato vīra tato'hamapayātavān||4||

5sa tvaṃ sātvatamukhyādya labdhasañjño yadṛcchayā
|
paśya me hayasaṃyāne śikṣāṃ keśavanandana||5||

6dārukeṇāhamutpanno yathāvaccaiva śikṣitaḥ
|
vītabhīḥ praviśāmyetāṃ śālvasya prathitāṃ camūm||6||

7vāsudeva uvāca|
evamuktvā tato vīra hayānsañcodya saṅgare
|
raśmibhistu samudyamya javenābhyapatattadā||7||

8maṇḍalāni vicitrāṇi yamakānītarāṇi ca
|
savyāni ca vicitrāṇi dakṣiṇāni ca sarvaśaḥ||8||

9pratodenāhatā rājanraśmibhiśca samudyatāḥ
|
utpatanta ivākāśe vyacaraṃste hayottamāḥ||9||

10te hastalāghavopetaṃ vijñāya nṛpadārukim
|
dahyamānā iva tadā nāpaspṛśuñścaraṇairmahīm||10||

11so'pasavyāṃ camūṃ tasya śālvasya bharatarṣabha
|
cakāra nātiyatnena tadadbhutamivābhavat||11||

12amṛṣyamāṇo'pasavyaṃ pradyumnena sa saubharāṭ
|
yantāramasya sahasā tribhirbāṇaiḥ samārdayat||12||

13dārukasya sutastatra bāṇavegamacintayan
|
bhūya eva mahābāho prayayāvapasavyataḥ||13||

14tato bāṇānbahuvidhānpunareva sa saubharāṭ
|
mumoca tanaye vīre mama rukmiṇinandane||14||

15tānaprāptāñcchitairbāṇaiściccheda paravīrahā
|
raukmiṇeyaḥ smitaṃ kṛtvā darśayanhastalāghavam||15||

16chinnāndṛṣṭvā tu tānbāṇānpradyumnena sa saubharāṭ
|
āsurīṃ dāruṇīṃ māyāmāsthāya vyasṛjaccharān||16||

17prayujyamānamājñāya daiteyāstraṃ mahābalam
|
brahmāstreṇāntarā cchittvā mumocānyānpatatriṇaḥ||17||

18te tadastraṃ vidhūyāśu vivyadhū rudhirāśanāḥ
|
śirasyurasi vaktre ca sa mumoha papāta ca||18||

19tasminnipatite kṣudre śālve bāṇaprapīḍite
|
raukmiṇeyo paraṃ bāṇaṃ saṃdadhe śatrunāśanam||19||

20tamarcitaṃ sarvadāśārhapūgairāśīviṣāgnijvalanaprakāśam
|
dṛṣṭvā śaraṃ jyāmabhinīyamānaṃ babhūva hāhākṛtamantarikṣam||20||

21tato devagaṇāḥ sarve sendrāḥ saha dhaneśvarāḥ
|
nāradaṃ preṣayāmāsuḥ śvasanaṃ ca manojavam||21||

22tau raukmiṇeyamāgamya vaco'brūtāṃ divaukasām
|
naiṣa vadhyastvayā vīra śālvarājaḥ kathañcana||22||

23saṃharasva punarbāṇamavadhyo'yaṃ tvayā raṇe
|
etasya ca śarasyājau nāvadhyo'sti pumānkvacit||23||

24mṛtyurasya mahābāho raṇe devakinandanaḥ
|
kṛṣṇaḥ saṅkalpito dhātrā tanmithyā na bhavediti||24||

25tataḥ paramasaṃhṛṣṭaḥ pradyumnaḥ śaramuttamam
|
sañjahāra dhanuḥśreṣṭhāttūṇe caiva nyaveśayat||25||

26tata utthāya rājendra śālvaḥ paramadurmanāḥ
|
vyapāyātsabalastūrṇaṃ pradyumnaśarapīḍitaḥ||26||

27sa dvārakāṃ parityajya krūro vṛṣṇibhirarditaḥ
|
saubhamāsthāya rājendra divamācakrame tadā||27||
