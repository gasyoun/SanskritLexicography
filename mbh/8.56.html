Mahābhārata 8.56 (Karṇaparvan)

1sañjaya uvāca|
bhīmasenaṃ ca pāñcālyaṃ cedikekayasaṃvṛtam
|
vaikartanaḥ svayaṃ ruddhvā vārayāmāsa sāyakaiḥ ||1||

2tatastu cedikārūṣānsṛñjayāṃśca mahārathān
|
karṇo jaghāna samare bhīmasenasya paśyataḥ ||2||

3bhīmasenastataḥ karṇaṃ vihāya rathasattamam
|
prayayau kauravaṃ sainyaṃ kakṣamagniriva jvalan ||3||

4sūtaputro'pi samare pañcālānkekayāṃstathā
|
sṛñjayāṃśca maheṣvāsānnijaghāna sahasraśaḥ ||4||

5saṃśaptakeṣu pārthaśca kauraveṣu vṛkodaraḥ
|
pañcāleṣu tathā karṇaḥ kṣayaṃ cakrurmahārathāḥ ||5||

6te kṣatriyā dahyamānāstribhistaiḥ pāvakopamaiḥ
|
jagmurvināśaṃ samare rājan durmantrite tava ||6||

7tato duryodhanaḥ kruddho nakulaṃ navabhiḥ śaraiḥ
|
vivyādha bharataśreṣṭha caturaścāsya vājinaḥ ||7||

8tataḥ punarameyātmā tava putro janādhipa
|
kṣureṇa sahadevasya dhvajaṃ ciccheda kāñcanam ||8||

9nakulastu tataḥ kruddhastava putraṃ ca saptabhiḥ
|
jaghāna samare rājan sahadevaśca pañcabhiḥ ||9||

10tāvubhau bharataśreṣṭhau jyeṣṭhau sarvadhanuṣmatām
|
vivyādhorasi saṃkruddhaḥ pañcabhiḥ pañcabhiḥ śaraiḥ ||10||

11tato parābhyāṃ bhallābhyāṃ dhanuṣī samakṛntata
|
yamayoḥ sahasā rājanvivyādha ca trisaptabhiḥ ||11||

12tāvanye dhanuṣī śreṣṭhe śakracāpanibhe śubhe
|
pragṛhya rejatuḥ śūrau devaputrasamau yudhi ||12||

13tatastau rabhasau yuddhe bhrātarau bhrātaraṃ yudhi
|
śarairvavṛṣaturghorairmahāmeghau yathā'calam ||13||

14tataḥ kruddho mahārāja tava putro mahārathaḥ
|
pāṇḍuputrau maheṣvāsau vārayāmāsa patribhiḥ ||14||

15dhanurmaṇḍalamevāsya dṛśyate yudhi bhārata
|
sāyakāścaiva dṛśyante niścarantaḥ samantataḥ ||15||

16ācchādayandiśaḥ sarvāḥ sūryasyevāṃśavo yathā
|
bāṇabhūte tatastasminsaṃchanne ca nabhastale ||16||

17yamābhyāṃ dadṛśe rūpaṃ kālāntakayamopamam
|
parākramaṃ tu taṃ dṛṣṭvā tava sūnormahārathāḥ ||17||

18mṛtyorupāntikaṃ prāptau mādrīputrau sma menire
|
tataḥ senāpatī rājanpāṇḍavasya mahārathaḥ ||18||

19pārṣataḥ prayayau tatra yatra rājā suyodhanaḥ
|
mādrīputrau tataḥ śūrau vyatikramya mahārathau ||19||

20dhṛṣṭadyumnastava sutaṃ vārayāmāsa sāyakaiḥ
|
tamavidhyadameyātmā tava putro hyamarṣaṇaḥ ||20||

21pāñcālyaṃ pañcaviṃśatyā prahasan puruṣarṣabhaḥ
|
tataḥ punarameyātmā tava putro hyamarṣaṇaḥ ||21||

22viddhvā nanāda pāñcālyaṃ ṣaṣṭyā pañcabhireva ca
|
tathā'sya saśaraṃ cāpaṃ hastāvāpaṃ ca māriṣa ||22||

23kṣurapreṇa sutīkṣṇena rājā ciccheda saṃyuge
|
tadapāsya dhanuśchinnaṃ pāñcālyaḥ śatrukarśanaḥ ||23||

24anyadādatta vegena dhanurbhārasahaṃ navam
|
prajvalanniva vegena saṃraṃbhādrudhirekṣaṇaḥ ||24||

25aśobhata maheṣvāso dhṛṣṭadyumnaḥ kṛtavraṇaḥ
|
sa pañcadaśa nārācān śvasataḥ pannagāniva ||25||

26jighāṃsurbharataśreṣṭhaṃ dhṛṣṭadyumno vyapāsṛjat
|
te varma hemavikṛtaṃ bhittvā rājñaḥ śilāśitāḥ ||26||

27viviśurvasudhāṃ vegātkaṅkabarhiṇavāsasaḥ
|
so'tividdho mahārāja putraste'tivyarājata ||27||

28vasantakāle sumahān praphulla iva kiṃśukaḥ
|
sa chinnavarmā nārācaprahārairjarjarīkṛtaḥ ||28||

29dhṛṣṭadyumnasya bhallena kruddhaściccheda kārmukam
|
athainaṃ chinnadhanvānaṃ tvaramāṇo mahīpatiḥ ||29||

30sāyakairdaśabhī rājan bhruvormadhye samārpayat
|
tasya te'śobhayanvaktraṃ karmāraparimārjitāḥ ||30||

31praphullaṃ paṅkajaṃ yadvadbhramarā madhulipsavaḥ
|
ta‌dapāsya dhanuśchinnaṃ dhṛṣṭadyumno mahāmanāḥ ||31||

32anyadādatta vegena dhanurbhallāṃśca ṣoḍaśa
|
tato duryodhanasyāśvān hatvā sūtaṃ ca pañcabhiḥ ||32||

33dhanuściccheda bhallena jātarūpapariṣkṛtam
|
rathaṃ sopaskaraṃ chatraṃ śaktiṃ khaḍgaṃ gadāṃ dhvajam ||33||

34bhallaiściccheda daśabhiḥ putrasya tava pārṣataḥ
|
tapanīyāṅgadaṃ citraṃ nāgaṃ maṇimayaṃ śubham ||34||

35dhvajaṃ kurupateśchinnaṃ dadṛśuḥ sarvapārthivāḥ
|
duryodhanaṃ tu virathaṃ chinnavarmāyudhaṃ raṇe ||35||

36bhrātaraḥ paryarakṣanta sodarā bharatarṣabha
|
tamāropya rathe rājandaṇḍadhāro janādhipaḥ ||36||

37apāharadasaṃbhrānto dhṛṣṭadyumnasya paśyataḥ
|
karṇastu sātyakiṃ jitvā rājagṛddhī mahābalaḥ ||37||

38droṇahantāramugreṣuṃ sasārābhimukho raṇe
|
taṃ pṛṣṭhato'bhyayāttūrṇaṃ śaineyo vitudañcharaiḥ ||38||

39vāraṇaṃ jaghanopānte viṣāṇābhyāmiva dvipaḥ
|
sa bhārata mahānāsīdyodhānāṃ sumahātmanām ||39||

40karṇapārṣatayormadhye tvadīyānāṃ mahāraṇaḥ
|
na pāṇḍavānāṃ nāsmākaṃ yodhaḥ kaścitparāṅmukhaḥ ||40||

41pratyadṛśyattataḥ karṇaḥ pañcālāṃstvarito yayau
|
tasmin kṣaṇe naraśreṣṭha gaja-vāji-janakṣayaḥ ||41||

42prādurāsīdubhayato rājanmadhyagate'hani
|
pañcālāstu mahārāja tvaritā vijigīṣavaḥ ||42||

43te sarve'bhyadravankarṇaṃ patatriṇa iva drumam
|
tāṃstathādhirathiḥ kruddho yatamānānmanasvinaḥ ||43||

44vicinvanniva bāṇaughaiḥ samāsādayadagragān
|
vyāghraketuṃ suśarmāṇaṃ citraṃ cogrāyudhaṃ jayam ||44||

45śuklaṃ ca rocamānaṃ ca siṃhasenaṃ ca durjayam
|
te vīrā rathamārgeṇa parivavrurnarottamam ||45||

46sṛjantaṃ sāyakān kruddhaṃ karṇamāhavaśobhinam
|
yudhyamānāṃstu tāndūrānmanujendra pratāpavān ||46||

47aṣṭābhiraṣṭau rādheyo'bhyardayanniśitaiḥ śaraiḥ
|
athāparānmahārāja sūtaputraḥ pratāpavān ||47||

48jaghāna bahusāhasrān yodhānyuddhaviśāradān
|
jiṣṇuṃ ca jiṣṇukarmāṇaṃ devāpiṃ bhadrameva ca ||48||

49daṇḍaṃ ca rājansamare citraṃ citrāyudhaṃ harim
|
siṃhaketuṃ rocamānaṃ śalabhaṃ ca mahāratham ||49||

50nijaghāna susaṃkruddhaścedīnāṃ ca mahārathān
|
teṣāmādadataḥ prāṇānāsīdādhirathervapuḥ ||50||

51śoṇitābhyukṣitāṅgasya rudrasyevorjitaṃ mahat
|
tatra bhārata karṇena mātaṅgāstāḍitāḥ śaraiḥ ||51||

52sarvato'bhyadravan bhītāḥ kurvanto mahadākulam
|
nipetururvyāṃ samare karṇasāyakatāḍitāḥ ||52||

53kurvanto vividhānnādānvajranunnā ivācalāḥ
|
gaja-vāji-manuṣyaiśca nipatadbhiḥ samantataḥ ||53||

54rathaiścādhirathermārge samāstīryata medinī
|
naivaṃ bhīṣmo na ca droṇo nānye yudhi ca tāvakāḥ ||54||

55cakraḥ sma tādṛśaṃ karma yādṛśaṃ vai kṛtaṃ raṇe
|
sūtaputreṇa nāgeṣu hayeṣu ca ratheṣu ca ||55||

56nareṣu ca mahārāja kṛtaṃ sma kadanaṃ mahat
|
mṛgamadhye yathā siṃho dṛśyate nirbhayaścaran ||56||

57pañcālānāṃ tathā madhye karṇo'caradabhītavat
|
yathā mṛgagaṇāṃstrastānsiṃho drāvayate diśaḥ ||57||

58pañcālānāṃ rathavrātān karṇo vyadrāvayattathā
|
sihāsyaṃ ca yathā prāpya na jīvanti mṛgāḥ kvacit ||58||

59tathā karṇamanuprāpya na jijīvurmahārathāḥ
|
vaiśvānaraṃ yathā prāpya pratidahyanti vai janāḥ ||59||

60karṇāgninā vane tadvaddagdhā bhārata suñjayāḥ
|
karṇena cedikaikeyapāñcāleṣu ca bhārata ||60||

61viśrāvya nāma nihatā bahavaḥ śūrasaṃmatāḥ
|
mama cāsīnmatī rājandṛṣṭvā karṇasya vikramam ||61||

62naiko'pyādhiratherjīvanpāñcālyo mokṣyate yudhi
|
pañcālānvyadhamatsaṃkhye sūtaputraḥ punaḥ punaḥ ||62||

63pañcālānatha nighnantaṃ karṇaṃ dṛṣṭvā mahāraṇe
|
abhyadhāvatsusaṃkruddho dharmarājo yudhiṣṭhiraḥ ||63||

64dhṛṣṭadyumnaśca rādheyaṃ draupadeyāśca māriṣa
|
parivavrurabhitraghnaṃ śataśaścāpare janāḥ ||64||

65śikhaṇḍī sahadevaśca nakulo nākulistathā
|
janamejayaḥ śinernaptā bahavaśca prabhadrakāḥ ||65||

66ete purogamā bhūtvā dhṛṣṭadyumnaśca saṃyuge
|
karṇamasyantamiṣvastrairviceruramitaujasaḥ ||66||

67tāṃstatrādhirathiḥ saṃkhye cedipāñcālapāṇḍavān
|
eko bahūnabhyapatadgarutmānpannagāniva ||67||

68taiḥ karṇasyābhavadyuddhaṃ ghorarūpaṃ viśāṃpate
|
tādṛgyādṛkpurā vṛttaṃ devānāṃ dānavaiḥ saha ||68||

69tān sametānmaheṣvāsān śaravarṣaughavarṣiṇaḥ
|
eko vyadhamadavyagrastamāṃsīva divākaraḥ ||69||

70bhīmasenastu saṃsakte rādheye pāṇḍavaiḥ saha
|
sarvato'bhyahanatkruddho yamadaṇḍanibhaiḥ śaraiḥ
|
vāhīkānkekayānmatsyān vāsātyānmadrasaindhavān ||70||

71ekaḥ saṃkhye maheṣvāso yodhayanbahvaśobhata
|
tatra marmasu bhīmena nārācaistāḍitā gajāḥ ||71||

72prapatanto hatārohāḥ kampayanti sma medinīm
|
vājinaśca hatārohāḥ pattayaśca gatāsavaḥ ||72||

73śerate yudhi nirbhinnā vamanto rudhiraṃ bahu
|
sahasraśaśca rathinaḥ pātitāḥ pātitāyudhāḥ ||73||

74te kṣatāḥ samadṛśyanta bhīmabhītā gatāsavaḥ
|
rathibhiḥ sādibhiḥ sūtaiḥ pādātairvājibhirgajaiḥ ||74||

75bhīmasenaśaraiśchinnairācchannā vasudhā'bhavat
|
tatstambhitamivātiṣṭhadbhīmasenabhayārditam ||75||

76duryodhanabalaṃ sarvaṃ nirutsāhaṃ kṛtavraṇam
|
niśceṣṭaṃ tumulaṃ dīnaṃ babhau tasminmahāraṇe ||76||

77prasannasalile kāle yathā syātsāgaro nṛpa
|
tadvattava balaṃ tadvai niścalaṃ samavasthitam ||77||

78manyuvīryabalopetaṃ darpātpratyavaropitam
|
abhavattava putrasya tatsainyaṃ niṣprabhaṃ tadā ||78||

79tadbalaṃ bharataśreṣṭha vadhyamānaṃ parasparam
|
rudhiraughapariklinnaṃ rudhirārdraṃ babhūva ha ||79||

80jagāma bharataśreṣṭha vadhyamānaṃ parasparam
|
sūtaputro raṇe kruddhaḥ pāṇḍavānāmanīkinīm ||80||

81bhīmasenaḥ kurūṃścāpi drāvayantau virejatuḥ
|
vartamāne tathā raudre saṃgrāme'dbhutadarśane ||81||

82nihatya pṛtanāmadhye saṃśaptakagaṇānbahūn
|
arjuno jayatāṃ śreṣṭho vāsudevamathābravīt ||82||

83prabhagnaṃ balametaddhi yotsyamānaṃ janārdana
|
ete dravanti sagaṇāḥ saṃśaptakamahārathāḥ ||83||

84apārayanto madbāṇānsiṃhaśabdaṃ mṛgā iva
|
dīryate ca mahatsainyaṃ sṛñjayānāṃ mahāraṇe ||84||

85hastikakṣo hyasau kṛṣṇa ketuḥ karṇasya dhīmataḥ
|
dṛśyate rājasainyasya madhye vicarato mudā ||85||

86na ca karṇaṃ raṇe śaktā jetumanye mahārathāḥ
|
jānīte hi bhavān karṇaṃ vīryavantaṃ parākrame ||86||

87tatra yāhi yataḥ karṇo drāvayatyeṣa no balam
|
varjayitvā raṇe yāhi sūtaputraṃ mahāratham ||87||

88etanme rocate kṛṣṇa yathā vā tava rocate
|
etacchrutvā vacastasya govindaḥ prahasanniva ||88||

89abravīda‌rjunaṃ tūrṇaṃ kauravāñjahi pāṇḍava
|
tatastava mahāsainyaṃ govindapreritā hayāḥ ||89||

90haṃsavarṇāḥ praviviśurvahantaḥ kṛṣṇapāṇḍavau
|
keśavapreritairaśvaiḥ śvetaiḥ kāñcanabhūṣaṇaiḥ ||90||

91praviśadbhistava balaṃ caturdiśamabhidyata
|
meghastanitanirhrādaḥ sa ratho vānaradhvajaḥ ||91||

92calatpatākastāṃ senāṃ vimānaṃ dyāmivāviśat
|
tau vidārya mahāsenāṃ praviṣṭau keśavārjunau ||92||

93kruddhau saṃrambharaktākṣau vibhrājetāṃ mahādyutī
|
yuddhaśauṇḍau samāhūtāvāgatau tau raṇādhvaram ||93||

94yajvabhirvidhināhūtau makhe devāvivāśvinau
|
kruddhau tau tu naravyāghrau yogavantau babhūvatuḥ ||94||

95talaśabdena ruṣitau yathā nāgau mahāvane
|
vigāhya tu rathānīkamaśvasaṅghāṃśca phālgunaḥ ||95||

96vyacaratpṛtanāmadhye pāśahasta ivāntakaḥ
|
taṃ dṛṣṭvā yudhi vikrāntaṃ senāyāṃ tava bhārata ||96||

97saṃśaptakagaṇānbhūyaḥ putraste samacūcudat
|
tato rathasahastreṇa dviradānāṃ tribhiḥ śataiḥ ||97||

98caturdaśasahasraistu turagāṇāṃ mahāhave
|
dvābhyāṃ śatasahasrābhyāṃ padātīnāṃ ca dhanvinām ||98||

99śūrāṇāṃ labdhalakṣāṇāṃ viditānāṃ samantataḥ
|
abhyavartanta kaunteyaṃ chādayanto mahārathāḥ ||99||

100śaravarṣairmahārāja sarvataḥ pāṇḍunandanam
|
sa cchādyamānaḥ samare śaraiḥ parabalārdanaḥ ||100||

101darśayan raudramātmānaṃ pāśahasta ivāntakaḥ
|
nighnansaṃśaptakānpārthaḥ prekṣaṇīyataro'bhavat ||101||

102tato vidyutprabhairbāṇaiḥ kārtasvaravibhūṣitaiḥ
|
nirantaramivākāśamāsīcchannaṃ kirīṭinā ||102||

103kirīṭibhujanirmuktaiḥ saṃpatadbhirmahāśaraiḥ
|
samācchannaṃ babhau sarvaṃ kādraveyairiva prabho ||103||

104rukmapuṅkhānprasannāgrāñcharānsannataparvaṇaḥ
|
avāsṛjada‌meyātmā dikṣu sarvāsu pāṇḍavaḥ ||104||

105mahī viyaddiśaḥ sarvāḥ samudrā girayo'pi vā
|
sphuṭantīti janā jajñuḥ pārthasya talaniḥsvanāt ||105||

106hatvā daśasahasrāṇi pārthivānāṃ mahārathaḥ
|
saṃśaptakānāṃ kaunteyaḥ pratyakṣaṃ tvarito'bhyayāt ||106||

107pratyakṣaṃ ca samāsādya pārthaḥ kāmbojarakṣitam
|
pramamātha balaṃ bāṇairdānavāniva vāsavaḥ ||107||

108pracicchedāśu bhallena dviṣatāmātatāyinām
|
śastraṃ pāṇiṃ tathā bāhuṃ tathāpi ca śirāṃsyuta ||108||

109aṅgāṅgāvayavaiśchinnairvyāyudhāste'patan bhuvi
|
viṣvagvātābhisaṃbhagnā bahuśākhā iva drumāḥ ||109||

110hastyaśvarathapattīnāṃ vrātānnighnantamarjunam
|
sudakṣiṇādavarajaḥ śaravṛṣṭyā'bhyavīvṛṣat ||110||

111tasyāsyatordhacandrābhyāṃ bāhū parighasannibhau
|
pūrṇacandrābhavaktraṃ ca kṣureṇābhyaharacchiraḥ ||111||

112sa papāta tato vāhātsulohitaparisravaḥ
|
manaḥśilāgireḥ śṛṅgaṃ vajreṇevāvadāritam ||112||

113sudakṣiṇādavarajaṃ kāmbojaṃ dadṛśurhatam
|
prāṃśuṃ kamalapatrākṣamatyarthaṃ priyadarśanam ||113||

114kāñcanastambhasadṛśaṃ bhinnaṃ hemagiriṃ yathā
|
tato'bhavatpunaryuddhaṃ ghoramatyarthamadbhutam ||114||

115nānāvasthāśca yodhānāṃ babhūvustatra yuddhyatām
|
ekeṣunihatairaśvaiḥ kāmbojairyavanaiḥ śakaiḥ ||115||

116śoṇitāktaistadā raktaṃ sarvamāsīdviśāṃpate
|
rathairhatāśvasūtaiśca hatārohaiśca vājibhiḥ ||116||

117dviradaiśca hatārohairmahāmātrairhatadvipaiḥ
|
anyonyena mahārāja kṛto ghoro janakṣayaḥ ||117||

118tasminprapakṣe pakṣe ca nihate savyasācinā
|
arjunaṃ jayatāṃ śreṣṭhaṃ tvarito drauṇirabhyayāt ||118||

119vidhunvāno mahaccāpaṃ kārtasvaravibhūṣitam
|
ādadānaḥ śarān ghorān svaraśmīniva bhāskaraḥ ||119||

120krodhāmarṣavivṛttāsyo lohitākṣo babhau balī
|
antakāle yathā kruddho mṛtyuḥ kiṃ karadaṇḍabhṛt ||120||

121tataḥ prāsṛjadugrāṇi śaravarṣāṇi saṅghaśaḥ
|
tairvisṛṣṭairmahārāja vyadravatpāṇḍavī camūḥ ||121||

122sa dṛṣṭvaiva tu dāśārhaṃ syandanasthaṃ viśāṃpate
|
punaḥ prāsṛjadugrāṇi śaravarṣāṇi māriṣa ||122||

123taiḥ patadbhirmahārāja drauṇimuktaiḥ samantataḥ
|
sañchāditau rathasthau tāvubhau kṛṣṇadhanañjayau ||123||

124tataḥ śaraśataistīkṣṇairaśvatthāmā pratāpavān
|
niśceṣṭau tāvubhau yuddhe kṛtvā mādhavapāṇḍavau ||124||

125hāhākṛtamabhūtsarvaṃ sthāvaraṃ jaṅgamaṃ tathā
|
carācarasya goptārau dṛṣṭvā saṃchāditau śaraiḥ ||125||

126siddhacāraṇasaṃghāśca saṃpetuste samantataḥ
|
cintayanto bhavedadya lokānāṃ svastyapīti ca ||126||

127na mayā tādṛśo rājan dṛṣṭapūrvaḥ parākramaḥ
|
saṃgrāme yādṛśo drauṇeḥ kṛṣṇau saṃchāda‌yiṣyataḥ ||127||

128drauṇestu dhanuṣaḥ śabda‌mahitatrāsanaṃ raṇe
|
aśrauṣaṃ bahuśo rājan siṃhasya ninado yathā ||128||

129jyā cāsya carato yuddhe savyada‌kṣiṇamasyataḥ
|
vidyudambudamadhyasthā bhrājamāneva sā'bhavat ||129||

130sa tathā kṣiprakārī ca dṛḍhahastaśca pāṇḍavaḥ
|
pramohaṃ paramaṃ gatvā prekṣantaṃ droṇajaṃ tataḥ ||130||

131vikramaṃ vihataṃ mene ātmanaḥ sa mahāyaśāḥ
|
tasyāsya samare rājanvapurāsītsudurdṛśam ||131||

132drauṇipāṇḍavayorevaṃ vartamāne mahāraṇe
|
vardhamāne ca rājendra droṇaputre mahābale ||132||

133hīyamāne ca kaunteye kṛṣṇe roṣaḥ samāviśat
|
sa roṣānniḥśvasan rājannirdahanniva cakṣuṣā ||133||

134drauṇiṃ hyapaśyatsaṃgrāme phālgunaṃ ca muhurmuhuḥ
|
tataḥ kruddho'bravītkṛṣṇaḥ pārthaṃ sapraṇayaṃ tadā ||134||

135atyadbhutamidaṃ pārtha tava paśyāmi saṃyuge
|
atiśete hi yatra tvāṃ droṇaputro'dya bhārata ||135||

136kaccidvīryaṃ yathāpūrvaṃ bhūjayorvā balaṃ tava
|
kaccitte gāṇḍivaṃ haste rathe tiṣṭhasi cārjuna ||136||

137kaccitkuśalino bāhū muṣṭirvā na vyaśīryata
|
udīryamāṇaṃ hi raṇe paśyāmi drauṇimāhave ||137||

138guruputra iti hyenaṃ mānayan bharatarṣabha
|
upekṣāṃ kuru mā pārtha nāyaṃ kāla upekṣitum ||138||

139evamuktastu kṛṣṇena gṛhya bhallāṃścaturdaśa
|
tvaramāṇastvarākāle drauṇerdhanurathācchinat ||139||

140dhvajaṃ chatraṃ patākāśca rathaṃ śaktiṃ gadāṃ tathā
|
jatrudeśe ca subhṛśaṃ vatsadantairatāḍayat ||140||

141sa mūrchāṃ paramāṃ gatvā dhvajayaṣṭiṃ samāśritaḥ
|
taṃ visaṃjñaṃ mahārāja śatruṇā bhṛśapīḍitam ||141||

142apovāha raṇātsūto rakṣamāṇo dhanañjayaḥ
|
etasminneva kāle ca vijayaḥ śatrutāpanaḥ ||142||

143vyahanattāvakaṃ sainyaṃ śataśo'tha sahasraśaḥ
|
paśyatastasya vīrasya tava putrasya bhārata ||143||

144evameṣa kṣayo vṛttastāvakānāṃ paraiḥ saha
|
krūro viśasano ghoro rājan durmantrite tava ||144||

145saṃśaptakāṃśca kaunteyaḥ kurūṃścāpi vṛkodaraḥ
|
vasuṣeṇaśca pañcālān kṣaṇena vyadhamadraṇe ||145||

146vartamāne tathā raudre rājanvīravarakṣaye
|
utthitānyagaṇeyāni kabandhāni samantataḥ ||146||

147yudhiṣṭhiro'pi saṃgrāme prahārairgāḍhavedanaḥ
|
krośamātramapakramya tasthau bharatasattamaḥ ||147||
