Mahābhārata 2.1 (Sabhāparvan)

1nārāyaṇaṃ namaskṛtya naraṃ caiva narottamam
|
devīṃ sarasvatīṃ caiva tato jayamudīrayet||1||

2vaiśampāyana uvāca|
tato'bravīnmayaḥ pārthaṃ vāsudevasya sannidhau
|
prāñjaliḥ ślakṣṇayā vācā pūjayitvā punaḥ punaḥ||2||

3maya uvāca|
asmātkṛṣṇātsusaṃrabdhātpāvakācca didhakṣataḥ
|
tvayā trāto'smi kaunteya brūhi kiṃ karavāṇi te||3||

4arjuna uvāca|
kṛtameva tvayā sarvaṃ svasti gaccha mahāsura
|
prītimānbhava me nityaṃ prītimanto vayaṃ ca te||4||

5maya uvāca|
yuktametattvayi vibho yathāttha puruṣarṣabha
|
prītipūrvamahaṃ kiñcitkartumicchāmi bhārata||5||

6ahaṃ hi viśvakarmā vai dānavānāṃ mahākaviḥ
|
sohaṃ vai tvatkṛte kartuṃ kiñcidicchāmi pāṇḍava||6||

7arjuna uvāca|
prāṇakṛcchrādvinirmuktam tvamātmānaṃ manyase mayā
|
evaṃ gate na śakṣyāmi kiñcitkārayituṃ tvayā||7||

8na cāpi tava saṅkalpaṃ moghamicchāmi dānava
|
kṛṣṇasya kriyatāṃ kiñcittathā pratikṛtaṃ mayi||8||

9codito vāsudevastu mayena prati bharatarṣabha
|
muhūrtamiva sandadhyau kimayaṃ codyatāmiti||9||

10tato vicintya manasā lokanāthaḥ prajāpitaḥ
|
codayāmāsa taṃ kṛṣṇaḥ sabhā vai kriyatāmiti||10||

11yadi tvaṃ kartukāmo'si priyaṃ śilpavatāṃ vara
|
dharmarājasya daiteya yādṛśīmiha manyase||11||

12yāṃ kriyāṃ nānukuryuste mānavāḥ prekṣya dhiṣṭhitāḥ
|
manuṣyaloke sakale tādṛśīṃ kuru vai sabhām||12||

13yatra dvivyānabhiprāyānpaśyema hi kṛtāṃstvayā
|
āsurānmānuṣāṃścaiva sabhāṃ tāṃ kuru vai maya||13||

14vaiśampāyana uvāca|
pratigṛhya tu tadvākyaṃ samprahṛṣṭo mayastadā
|
vimānapratimāṃ cakre pāṇḍavasya śubhāṃ sabhām||14||

15tataḥ kṛṣṇaśca pārthaśca dharmarāje yudhiṣṭhire
|
sarvametatsamāvedya darśayāmāsaturmayam||15||

16tasmai yudhiṣṭhiraḥ pūjāṃ yathārhamakarottadā
|
sa tu tāṃ pratijagrāha mayaḥ satkṛtya bhārata||16||

17sa pūrvadevacaritaṃ tadā tatra viśāmpate
|
kathayāmāsa daiteyaḥ pāṇḍuputreṣu bhārata||17||

18sa kālaṃ kañcidāśvasya viśvakarmā vicintya tu
|
sabhāṃ pracakrame kartuṃ pāṇḍavānāṃ mahātmanām||18||

19abhiprāyeṇa pārthānāṃ kṛṣṇasya ca mahātmanaḥ
|
puṇye'hani mahātejāḥ kṛtakautukamaṅgalaḥ||19||

20tarpayitvā dvijaśreṣṭhānpāyasena sahasraśaḥ
|
dhanaṃ bahuvidhaṃ dattvā tebhya eva ca vīryavān||20||

21sarvartuguṇasampannāṃ divyarūpāṃ manoramām
|
daśakiṣkusahasrāṃ tāṃ māpayāmāsa sarvataḥ||21||
