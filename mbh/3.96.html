Mahābhārata 3.96 (Āraṇyakaparvan)

1vaiśaṃpāyana uvāca| tataḥ saṃprasthito rājā kaunteyo bhūridakṣiṇaḥ
|
agastyāśramamāsādya durjayāyāmuvāsa ha||1||

2tatraiva lomaśaṃ rājā papraccha vadatāṃ varaḥ
|
agastyeneha vātāpiḥ kimarthamupaśāmitaḥ||2||

3āsīdvā kiṃprabhāvaśca sa daityo mānavāntakaḥ
|
kimarthaṃ codito manyuragastyasya mahātmanaḥ||3||

4lomaśa uvāca| ilvalo nāma daiteya āsītkauravanandanā
|
maṇimatyāṃ puri purā vātāpistasya cānujaḥ||4||

5sa brāhmaṇaṃ tapoyuktamuvāca ditinandanaḥ
|
putraṃ me bhagavānekamindratulyaṃ prayacchatu||5||

6tasmai sa brāhmaṇo nādātputraṃ vāsavasaṃmitam
|
cukrodha sosurastasya brāhmaṇasya tato bhṛśam||6||

7tadāprabhṛti rājendra ilvalo brahmahā'suraḥ
|
manyumānbhrātaraṃ chāgaṃ māyāvī hyakarottataḥ||7||

8meṣarūpī ca vātāpiḥ kāmarūpyabhavatkṣaṇāt
|
saṃskṛtya ca bhojayati tato vipraṃ jidhāṃsati||8||

9sa cāhvayati yaṃ vācā gataṃ vaivasvatakṣayam
|
sa punardehamāsthāya jīvansma pratyadṛśyata||9||

10tato vātāpimasuraṃ chāgaṃ kṛtvā susaṃskṛtam
|
taṃ brāhmaṇaṃ bhojayitvā punareva samāhvayat||10||

11tāmilvalena mahatā svareṇa vācamīritām
|
śrutvātimāyo bālavānkṣipraṃ brāhmaṇakaṇṭakaḥ||11||

12tasya pārśvaṃ vinirbhidya brāhmaṇasya mahāsuraḥ
|
vātāpiḥ prahasanrājanniścakrāma viśāṃpate||12||

13evaṃ sa brāhmaṇānrājanbhojayitvā punaḥpunaḥ
|
hiṃsayāmāyasa daiteya ilvalo duṣṭacetanaḥ||13||

14agastyaścāpi bhagavānetasminkāla eva tu
|
pitṝndadarśa garte vai lambamānānadhomukhān||14||

15sopṛcchallambamānāṃstānbhagavantaśca kampitāḥ
|
saṃtānahetoriti te pratyūcurbrahmavādinaḥ||15||

16te tasmai kathayāmāsurvayaṃ te pitaraḥ svakāḥ
|
gartametamanuprāptā lambāmaḥ prasavārthinaḥ||16||

17yadi no janayethāstvamagastyāpatyamuttamam
|
syānno'smānnirayānmokṣastvaṃ ca putrāpnuyā gatim||17||

18sa tānuvāca tejasvī satyadharmaparāyaṇaḥ
|
kariṣye pitaraḥ kāmaṃ vyetu vo mānaso jvaraḥ||18||

19tataḥ prasavasantānaṃ cintayanbhagavānṛṣiḥ
|
ātmanaḥ prasavasyārthe nāpaśyatsadṛśīṃ striyam||19||

20sa tasya tasya sattvasya tattadaṅgamanuttamam
|
saṃgṛhya tatsamairaṅgairnirmame striyamuttamām||20||

21sa tāṃ vidarbharājāya putrārthaṃ tapyatastapaḥ
|
nirmitāmātmanorthāya muniḥ prādānmahātapāḥ||21||

22sā tatra jajñe subhagā vidyutsaudāmanī yathā
|
vibhrājamānā vapuṣā vyavardhata śubhānanā||22||

23jātamātrāṃ ca tāṃ dṛṣṭvā vaidarbhaḥ pṛthivīpatiḥ
|
praharṣeṇa dvijātibhyo nyavedayata bhārata||23||

24abhyanandanta tāṃ sarve brāhmaṇā vasudhādhipa
|
lopāmudreti tasyāśca cakrire nāma te dvijāḥ||24||

25vavṛdhe sā mahārāja bibhratī rūpamuttamam
|
apsvivotpalinī śīghramagneriva śikhā śubhā||25||

26tāṃ yauvanasthāṃ rājendra śataṃ kanyāḥ svalaṃkṛtāḥ
|
dāsyaḥ śataṃ ca kalyāṇīmupātasthurvaśānugāḥ||26||

27sā sma dāsīśatavṛtā madhye kanyāśatasya ca
|
āste tejasvinī kanyā rohiṇīva divi prabhā||27||

28yauvanasthāmapi ca tāṃ śīlācārasamanvitām
|
na vavre puruṣaḥ kaścidbhayāttasya mahātmanaḥ||28||

29sā tu satyavatī kanyā rūpeṇāpsarasopyati
|
toṣayāmāsa pitaraṃ śīlena svajanaṃ tathā||29||

30vaidarbhīṃ tu tathāyuktāṃ yuvatīṃ prekṣya vai pitā
|
manasā cintayāmāsa kasmai dadyāmimāṃ sutām||30||
