Mahābhārata 12.53 (Śāntiparvan)

1vaiśampāyana uvāca|
tataḥ śayanamāviśya prasupto madhusūdanaḥ
|
yāmamātrārdhaśeṣāyāṃ yāminyāṃ pratyabuddhyata||1||

2sa dhyānapathamāviśya sarvajñānāni mādhavaḥ
|
avalokya tataḥ paścāddadhyau brahma sanātanam||2||

3tataḥ stutipurāṇajñā raktakaṇṭhāḥ suśikṣitāḥ
|
astuvanviśvakarmāṇaṃ vāsudevaṃ prajāpatim||3||

4paṭhanti pāṇisvanikāstathā gāyanti gāyanāḥ
|
śaṅkhānatha mṛdaṅgāṃśca pravādyanti sahasraśaḥ||4||

5vīṇāpaṇavaveṇūnāṃ svanaścātimanoramaḥ
|
sahāsa iva vistīrṇaḥ śuśruve tasya veśmanaḥ||5||

6tato yudhiṣṭhirasyāpi rājño maṅgalasaṃhitāḥ
|
uccerurmadhurā vāco gītavāditrani:svanāḥ||6||

7tata utthāya dāśārhaḥ snātaḥ prāñjaliracyutaḥ
|
japtvā guhyaṃ mahābāhuragnīnāśritya tasthivān||7||

8tataḥ sahasraṃ viprāṇāṃ caturvedavidāṃ tathā
|
gavāṃ sahasreṇaikaikaṃ vācayāmāsa mādhavaḥ||8||

9maṅgalālambhanaṃ kṛtvā ātmānamavalokya ca
|
ādarśa vimale kṛṣṇastataḥ sātyakimabravīt||9||

10gaccha śaineya jānīhi gatvā rājaniveśanam
|
api sajjo mahātejā bhīṣmaṃ draṣṭuṃ yudhiṣṭhiraḥ||10||

11tataḥ kṛṣṇasya vacanātsātyakistvarito yayau
|
upagamya ca rājānaṃ yudhiṣṭhiramabhāṣata||11||

12yukto rathavaro rājanvāsudevasya dhīmataḥ
|
samīpamāpageyasya prayāsyati janārdanaḥ||12||

13bhavatpratīkṣaḥ kṛṣṇo'sau dharmarāja mahādyute
|
yadatrānantaraṃ kṛtyaṃ tadbhavānkartumarhati||13||

14evamuktaḥ pratyuvāca dharmaputro yudhiṣṭhiraḥ
|
yudhiṣṭhira uvāca|
yujyatāṃ me rathavaraḥ phālgunāpratimadyute||14||

15na sainikaiśca yātavyaṃ yāsyāmo vayameva hi
|
na ca pīḍayitavyo me bhīṣmo dharmabhṛtāṃ varaḥ||15||

16ataḥ puraḥsarāścāpi nivartantu dhanaṃjaya
|
adyaprabhṛti gāṅgeyaḥ paraṃ guhyaṃ pravakṣyati||16||

17ato necchāmi kaunteya pṛthagjanasamāgamam
|
vaiśampāyana uvāca|
sa tadvākyamathājñāya kuntīputro dhanaṃjayaḥ||17||

18yuktaṃ rathavaraṃ tasmā ācacakṣe nararṣabhaḥ
|
tato yudhiṣṭhiro rājā yamau bhīmārjunāvapi||18||

19bhūtānīva samastāni yayuḥ kṛṣṇaniveśanam
|
āgacchatsvatha kṛṣṇo'pi pāṇḍaveṣu mahātmasu||19||

20śaineyasahito dhīmān rathamevānvapadyata
|
rathasthāḥ saṃvidaṃ kṛtvā sukhāṃ pṛṣṭvā ca śarvarīm||20||

21dārukaścodayāmāsa vāsudevasya vājinaḥ||21||

22te hayā vāsudevasya dārukeṇa pracoditāḥ
|
gāṃ khurāgaistathā rājan likhantaḥ prayayustada||22||

23te grasanta ivākāśaṃ vegavanto mahābalāḥ
|
kṣetraṃ dharmasya kṛtsnasya kurukṣetramavātaran||23||

24tato yayuryatra bhīṣmaḥ śaratalpagataḥ prabhuḥ
|
āste maharṣibhiḥ sārdhaṃ brahmā devagaṇairyathā||24||

25tato'vatīrya govindo rathātsa ca yudhiṣṭhiraḥ
|
bhīmo gāṇḍīvadhanvā ca yamau sātyakireva ca||25||

26ṛṣīnabhyarcayāmāsuḥ karānudyamya dakṣiṇān
|
sa taiḥ parivṛto rājā nakṣatrairiva candramāḥ
|
abhyājagāma gāṅgeyaṃ brahmāṇamiva vāsavaḥ||26||
