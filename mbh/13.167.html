Mahābhārata 13.167 (Anuśāsanaparvan)

1vaiśampāyana uvāca|
tataḥ kuntīsuto rājā paurajānapadaṃ janam
|
pūjayitvā yathānyāyamanujajñe gṛhānprati ||1||

2sāntvayāmāsa nārīśca hatavīrā hateśvarāḥ
|
vipulairarthadānaiḥ sa tadā pāṇḍusuto nṛpaḥ ||2||

3sobhiṣikto mahāprājñaḥ prāpya rājyaṃ yudhiṣṭhiraḥ
|
avasthāpya naraśreṣṭhaḥ sarvāḥ svaprakṛtīstathā ||3||

4dvijebhyo guṇamukhyebhyo naigamebhyaśca sarvaśaḥ
|
pratigṛhyāśiṣo mukhyāstathā dharmabhṛtāṃ varaḥ ||4||

5uṣitvā śarvarīḥ śrīmānpañcāśannagarottame
|
samayaṃ kauravāgryasya sasmāra puruṣarṣabhaḥ ||5||

6sa niryayau gajapurādyājakaiḥ parivāritaḥ
|
dṛṣṭvā nivṛttamādityaṃ pravṛttaṃ cottarāyaṇam ||6||

7ghṛtaṃ mālyaṃ ca gandhāṃśca kṣaumāṇi ca yudhiṣṭhiraḥ
|
candanāgurumukhyāni tathā kālīyakānyapi ||7||

8prasthāpya pūrvaṃ kaunteyo bhīṣmasaṃskaraṇāya vai
|
mālyāni ca varārhāṇi ratnāni vividhāni ca ||8||

9dhṛtarāṣṭraṃ puraskṛtya gāndhārīṃ ca yaśasvinīm
|
mātaraṃ ca pṛthāṃ dhīmānbhrātṝṃśca puruṣarṣabhān ||9||

10janārdanenānugato vidureṇa ca dhīmatā
|
yuyutsunā ca kauravyo yuyudhānena vā vibho ||10||

11mahatā rājabhogena pāribarheṇa saṃvṛtaḥ
|
stūyamāno mahātejā bhīṣmasyāgnīnanuvrajan ||11||

12niścakrāma purāttasmādyathā devapatistathā
|
āsasāda kurukṣetre tataḥ śāntanavaṃ nṛpaḥ ||12||

13upāsyamānaṃ vyāsena pārāśaryeṇa dhīmatā
|
nāradena ca rājarṣe devalenāsitena ca ||13||

14hataśiṣṭairnṛpaiścānyairnānādeśasamāgataiḥ
|
rakṣibhiśca mahātmānaṃ rakṣyamāṇaṃ samantataḥ ||14||

15śayānaṃ vīraśayane dadarśa nṛpatistataḥ
|
tato rathā‌davatīrya bhrātṛbhiḥ saha dharmarāṭ ||15||

16abhivādyātha kaunteyaḥ pitāmahamarindama
|
dvaipāyanādīnviprāṃśca taiśca pratyabhinanditaḥ ||16||

17ṛtvigbhirbrahmakalpaiśca bhrātṛbhiḥ saha dharmajaḥ
|
āsādya śaratalpasthamṛṣibhiḥ parivāritam ||17||

18abravīdbharataśreṣṭhaṃ dharmarājo yudhiṣṭhiraḥ
|
bhrātṛbhiḥ saha kauravyaḥ śayānaṃ nimnagāsutam ||18||

19yudhiṣṭhiro'haṃ nṛpate namaste jāhnavīsuta
|
śṛṇoṣi cenmahābāho brūhi kiṃ karavāṇi te ||19||

20prāpto'smi samaye rājannagnīnādāya te vibho
|
ācāryānbrāhmaṇāṃścaiva ṛtvijo bhrātaraśca me ||20||

21putraśca te mahātejā dhṛtarāṣṭro janeśvaraḥ
|
upasthitaḥ sahāmātyo vāsudevaśca vīryavān ||21||

22hataśiṣṭāśca rājānaḥ sarve ca kurujāṃgalāḥ
|
tānpaśya naraśārdūla samunmīlaya locane ||22||

23yacceha kiñcitkartavyaṃ tatsarvaṃ prāpitaṃ mayā
|
yathoktaṃ bhavatā kāle sarvameva ca tatkṛtam ||23||

24vaiśampāyana uvāca|
evamuktastu gāṅgeyaḥ kuntīputreṇa dhīmatā
|
dadarśa bhāratānsarvānsthitānsaṃparivārya ha ||24||

25tataśca taṃ balī bhīṣmaḥ pragṛhya vipulaṃ bhujam
|
udyanmeghasvaro vāgmī kāle vacanamabravīt ||25||

26diṣṭyā prāpto'si kaunteya sahāmātyo yudhiṣṭhira
|
parivṛtto hi bhagavān sahastrāṃśurdivākaraḥ ||26||

27aṣṭapañcāśataṃ rātryaḥ śayānasyādya me gatāḥ
|
śareṣu niśitāgreṣu yathā varṣaśataṃ tathā ||27||

28māgho'yaṃ samanuprāpto māsaḥ saumyo yudhiṣṭhira
|
tribhāgaśeṣaḥ pakṣo'yaṃ śuklo bhavitumarhati ||28||

29evamuktvā tu gāṅgeyo dharmaputraṃ yudhiṣṭhiram
|
dhṛtarāṣṭramathāmantrya kāle vacanamabravīt ||29||

30bhīṣma uvāca|
rājanviditadharmo'si sunirṇītārthasaṃśayaḥ
|
bahuśrutā hi te viprā bahavaḥ paryupāsitāḥ ||30||

31vedaśāstrāṇi sarvāṇi dharmāśca manujeśvara
|
vedāṃśca caturaḥ sarvānnikhilenānubuddhyase ||31||

32na śocitavyaṃ kauravya bhavitavyaṃ hi tattathā
|
śrutaṃ devarahasyaṃ te kṛṣṇadvaipāyanādapi ||32||

33yathā pāṇḍoḥ sutā rājaṃstathaiva tava dharmataḥ
|
tānpālaya sthito dharme guruśuśrūṣaṇe ratān ||33||

34dharmarājo hi śuddhātmā nideśe sthāsyate tava
|
ānṛśaṃsyaparaṃ hyenaṃ jānāmi guruvatsalam ||34||

35tava putrā durātmānaḥ krodhalobhaparāyaṇāḥ
|
īrṣyābhibhūtā durvṛttāstānna śocitumarhasi ||35||

36vaiśampāyana uvāca|
etāvaduktvā vacanaṃ dhṛtarāṣṭraṃ manīṣiṇam
|
vāsudevaṃ mahābāhumabhyabhāṣata kauravaḥ ||36||

37bhīṣma uvāca|
bhagavandevadeveśa surāsuranamaskṛta
|
trivikrama namastubhyaṃ śaṅkhacakragadādhara ||37||

38vāsudevo hiraṇyātmā puruṣaḥ savitā virāṭ
|
jīvabhūto'nurūpastvaṃ paramātmā sanātanaḥ ||38||

39trāyasva puṇḍarīkākṣa puruṣottama nityaśaḥ
|
anujānīhi māṃ kṛṣṇa vaikuṇṭha puruṣottama ||39||

40rakṣyāśca te pāṇḍaveyā bhavānyeṣāṃ parāyaṇam
|
uktavānasmi durbuddhiṃ mandaṃ duryodhanaṃ tadā ||40||

41yataḥ kṛṣṇastato dharmo yato dharmastato jayaḥ
|
vāsudevena tīrthena putra saṃśāmya pāṇḍavaiḥ ||41||

42sandhānasya paraḥ kālastaveti ca punaḥ punaḥ
|
na ca me tadvaco mūḍhaḥ kṛtavānsa sumandadhīḥ
|
ghātayitveha pṛthivīṃ tataḥ sa nidhanaṃ gataḥ ||42||

43tvāṃ tu jānāmyahaṃ devaṃ purāṇamṛṣisattamam
|
nareṇa sahitaṃ deva badaryāṃ suciroṣitam ||43||

44tathā me nāradaḥ prāha vyāsaśca sumahātapāḥ
|
naranārāyaṇāvetau saṃbhūtau manujeṣviti ||44||

45sa māṃ tvamanujānīhi kṛṣṇa mokṣye kalevaram
|
tvayā'haṃ samanujñāto gaccheyaṃ paramāṃ gatim ||45||

46vāsudeva uvāca|
anujānāmi bhīṣma tvāṃ vasūnprāpnuhi pārthiva
|
na te'sti vṛjinaṃ kiñcidihaloke mahādyute ||46||

47pitṛbhakto'si rājarṣe mārkaṇḍeya ivāparaḥ
|
tena mṛtyustava vaśe sthito bhṛtya ivānataḥ ||47||

48vaiśampāyana uvāca|
evamuktastu gāṅgeyaḥ pāṇḍavānida‌mabravīt
|
dhṛtarāṣṭrasukhāṃścāpi sarvāṃśca suhṛdastathā ||48||

49prāṇānutsraṣṭumicchāmi tatrānujñātumarhatha
|
satyeṣu yatitavyaṃ vaḥ satyaṃ hi paramaṃ balam ||49||

50ānṛśaṃsyaparairbhāvyaṃ sadaiva niyatātmabhiḥ
|
brahmaṇyairdharmaśīlaiśca taponityaiśca bhāratāḥ ||50||

51ityuktvā suhṛdaḥ sarvānsaṃpariṣvajya caiva ha
|
punarevābravīddhīmānyudhiṣṭhiramidaṃ vacaḥ ||51||

52brāhmaṇāścaiva te nityaṃ prājñāścaiva viśeṣataḥ
|
ācāryā ṛtvijaścaiva pūjanīyā janādhipa ||52||
