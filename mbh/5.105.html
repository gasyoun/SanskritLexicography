Mahābhārata 5.105 (Udyogaparvan)

1kaṇva uvāca|
garuḍastatra śuśrāva yathāvṛttaṃ mahābalaḥ
|
āyuḥpradānaṃ śakreṇa kṛtaṃ nāgasya bhārata ||1||

2pakṣavātena mahatā ruddhvā tribhuvanaṃ khagaḥ
|
suparṇaḥ paramakruddho vāsavaṃ samupādravat ||2||

3garuḍa uvāca|
bhagavan kimavajñānādvṛttiḥ pratihatā mama
|
kāmakāravaraṃ dattvā punaścalitavānasi ||3||

4nisargāt sarvabhūtānāṃ sarvabhūteśvareṇa me
|
āhāro vihito dhātrā kimarthaṃ vāryate tvayā ||4||

5vṛtaścaiṣa mahānāgaḥ sthāpitaḥ samayaśca me
|
anena ca mayā deva bhartavyaḥ prasavo mahān ||5||

6etasmiṃstu tathābhūte nānyaṃ hiṃsitamutsahe
|
krīḍase kāmakāreṇa devarāja yathecchakam ||6||

7so'haṃ prāṇān vimokṣyāmi tathā parijano mama
|
ye ca bhṛtyā mama gṛhe prītimān bhava vāsava ||7||

8etaccaivāhamarhāmi bhūyaśca balavṛtrahan
|
trailokasyeśvaro yo'haṃ parabhṛtyatvamāgataḥ ||8||

9tvayi tiṣṭhati deveśa na viṣṇuḥ kāraṇaṃ mama
|
trailokyarāja rājyaṃ hi tvayi vāsava śāśvatam||9||

10mamāpi dakṣasya sutā jananī kaśyapaḥ pitā
|
ahamapyutsahe lokān samantādvoḍhumañjasā ||10||

11asahyaṃ sarvabhūtānāṃ mamāpi vipulaṃ balam
|
mayā'pi sumahatkarma kṛtaṃ daiteyavigrahe ||11||

12śrutaśrīḥ śrutasenaśca vivasvānrocanāmukhaḥ
|
prasṛtaḥ kālakākṣaśca mayā'pi ditijā hatāḥ ||12||

13yattu dhvajasthānagato yatnāt paricarāmyaham
|
vahāmi caivānujaṃ te tena māmavamanyase ||13||

14ko'nyo bhārasaho hyasti ko'nyosti balavattaraḥ
|
mayā yo'haṃ viśiṣṭaḥ san vahāmīmaṃ sabāndhavam ||14||

15avajñāya tu yatte'haṃ bhojanādvyaparopitaḥ
|
tena me gauravaṃ naṣṭaṃ tvattaścāsmācca vāsava ||15||

16adityāṃ ya ime jātā balavikramaśālinaḥ
|
tvameṣāṃ kila sarveṣāṃ balena balavattaraḥ ||16||

17so'haṃ pakṣaikadeśena vahāmi tvāṃ gataklamaḥ
|
vimṛśa tvaṃ śanaistāta ko nvatra balavāniti ||17||

18kaṇva uvāca|
sa tasya vacanaṃ śrutvā khagasyodarkadāruṇam
|
akṣobhyaṃ kṣobhayaṃstārkṣyamuvāca rathacakrabhṛt ||18||

19garutman manyase'tmānaṃ balavantaṃ sudurbalam
|
alamasmatsamakṣaṃ te stotumātmānamaṇḍaja ||19||

20trailokyamapi me kṛtsnamaśaktaṃ dehadhāraṇe
|
ahamevātmanā''tmānaṃ vahāmi tvāṃ ca dhāraye ||20||

21imaṃ tāvanmamaikaṃ tvaṃ bāhuṃ savyetaraṃ vaha
|
yadyenaṃ dhārayasyekaṃ saphalaṃ te vikatthitam ||21||

22tataḥ sa bhagavāṃstasya skandhe bāhuṃ samāsajat
|
nipapāta sa bhārārto vihvalo naṣṭacetanaḥ ||22||

23yāvān hi bhāraḥ kṛtsnāyāḥ pṛthivyāḥ parvataiḥ saha
|
ekasyā dehaśākhāyāstāvadbhāramamanyata ||23||

24na tvenaṃ pīḍayāmāsa balena balavattaraḥ
|
tato hi jīvitaṃ tasya na vyanīnaśadacyutaḥ ||24||

25vyāttāsyaḥ srastakāyaśca vicetā vihvalaḥ khagaḥ
|
mumoca patrāṇi tadā gurubhāraprapīḍitaḥ ||25||

26sa viṣṇuṃ śirasā pakṣī praṇamya vinatāsutaḥ
|
vicetā vihvalo dīnaḥ kiñcidvacanamabravīt ||26||

27bhagavan lokasārasya sadṛśena vapuṣmatā
|
bhujena svairamuktena niṣpiṣṭo'smi mahītale ||27||

28kṣantumarhasi me deva vihvalasyālpacetasaḥ
|
baladāhavidagdhasya pakṣiṇo dhvajavāsinaḥ ||28||

29na hi jñātaṃ balaṃ deva mayā te paramaṃ vibho
|
tena manyāmyahaṃ vīryamātmano na samaṃ paraiḥ ||29||

30tataścakre sa bhagavān prasādaṃ vai garutmataḥ
|
maivaṃ bhūyaḥ iti snehāttadā cainamuvāca ha ||30||

31pādāṅguṣṭhena cikṣepa sumukhaṃ garuḍorasi
|
tataḥprabhṛti rājendra saha sarpeṇa vartate ||31||

32evaṃ viṣṇubalākrānto garvanāśamupāgataḥ
|
garuḍo balavānrājanvainateyo mahāyaśāḥ ||32||

33kaṇva uvāca|
tathā tvamapi gāndhāre yāvatpāṇḍusutānraṇe
|
nāsādayasi tān vīrāṃstāvajjīvasi putraka ||33||

34bhīmaḥ praharatāṃ śreṣṭho vāyuputro mahābalaḥ
|
dhanañjayaścendrasuto na hanyātāṃ tu kaṃ raṇe ||34||

35viṣṇurvāyuśca śakraśca dharmastau cāśvināvubhau
|
ete devāstvayā kena hetunā vīkṣituṃ kṣamāḥ ||35||

36tadalaṃ te virodhena śamaṃ gaccha nṛpātmaja
|
vāsudevena tīrthena kulaṃ rakṣitumarhasi ||36||

37pratyakṣadarśī sarvasya nārado'yaṃ mahātapāḥ
|
māhātmyasya tadā viṣṇoḥ so'yaṃ cakragadādharaḥ ||37||

38vaiśampāyana uvāca|
duryodhanastu tacchrutvā niḥśvasan bhṛkuṭīmukhaḥ
|
rādheyamabhisaṃprekṣya jahāsa svanavattadā ||38||

39kadarthīkṛtya tadvākyamṛṣeḥ kaṇvasya durmatiḥ
|
ūruṃ gajakarākāraṃ tāḍayannida‌mabravīt ||39||

40yathaiveśvarasṛṣṭo'smi yadbhāvi yā ca me gatiḥ
|
tathā maharṣe vartāmi kiṃ pralāpaḥ kariṣyati ||40||
