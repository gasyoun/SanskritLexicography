Mahābhārata 6.95 (Bhīṣmaparvan)

1sañjaya uvāca|
tasmin mahati saṃkrande rājā duryodhanastadā
|
gāṅgeyamupasaṅgamya vinayenābhivādya ca ||1||

2tasya sarvaṃ yathāvṛttamākhyātumupacakrame
|
ghaṭotkacasya vijayamātmanaśca parājayam ||2||

3kathayāmāsa durdharṣo viniḥśvasya punaḥ punaḥ
|
abravīcca tadā rājan bhīṣmaṃ kurupitāmaham ||3||

4bhavantaṃ samupāśritya vāsudevaṃ yathā paraiḥ
|
pāṇḍavairvigraho ghoraḥ samārabdho mayā prabho ||4||

5ekādaśa samākhyātā akṣauhiṇyaśca yā mama
|
nideśe tava tiṣṭhanti mayā sārdhaṃ paraṃtapa ||5||

6so'haṃ bharataśārdūla bhīmasenapurogamaiḥ
|
ghaṭotkacaṃ samāśritya pāṇḍurvairyudhi nirjitaḥ ||6||

7tanme dahati gātrāṇi śuṣkavṛkṣamivānalaḥ
|
tadicchāmi mahābhāga tvatprasādāt paraṃtapa ||7||

8rākṣasāpasadaṃ hantuṃ svayameva pitāmaha
|
tvāṃ samāśritya durdharṣaṃ tanme kartuṃ tvamarhasi ||8||

9etacchrutvā tu vacanaṃ rājño bharatasattama
|
duryodhanamidaṃ vākyaṃ bhīṣmaḥ śāṃtanavo'bravīt ||9||

10śṛṇu rājan mama vaco yattvāṃ vakṣyāmi kaurava
|
yathā tvayā mahārāja vartitavyaṃ paraṃtapa ||10||

11ātmā rakṣyo raṇe tāta sarvāvasthāsvārindama
|
dharmarājena saṃgrāmastvayā kāryaḥ sadā'nagha ||11||

12arjunena yamābhyāṃ vā bhīmasenena vā punaḥ
|
rājadharmaṃ puraskṛtya rājā rājānamārchati ||12||

13ahaṃ droṇaḥ kṛpo drauṇiḥ kṛtavarmā ca sāttvataḥ
|
śalyaśca saumada‌ttiśca vikarṇaśca mahārathāḥ ||13||

14tava ca bhrātaraḥ śreṣṭhā duḥśāsanapurogamāḥ
|
tvadarthe pratiyotsyāmo rākṣasaṃ taṃ mahābalam ||14||

15raudre tasmin rākṣasendre yadi te'nuśayo mahān
|
ayaṃ vā gacchatu raṇe tasya yuddhāya durmateḥ ||15||

16bhagadatto mahīpālaḥ purandarasamo yudhi
|
etāvaduktvā rājānaṃ bhagadattamathābravīt ||16||

17samakṣaṃ pārthivendrasya vākyaṃ vākyaviśāradaḥ
|
gaccha śīghraṃ mahārāja haiḍimbaṃ yuddhadurmadam ||17||

18vārayasva raṇe yatto miṣatāṃ sarvadhanvinām
|
rākṣasaṃ krūrakarmāṇaṃ yathendrastārakaṃ purā ||18||

19tava divyāni cāstrāṇi vikramaśca paraṃtapa
|
samāgamaśca bahubhiḥ purābhūdamaraiḥ saha ||19||

20tvaṃ tasya nṛpaśārdūla pratiyoddhā mahāhave
|
svabalenocchrito rājan jahi rākṣasapuṅgavam ||20||

21etacchrutvā tu vacanaṃ bhīṣmasya pṛtanāpateḥ
|
prayayau siṃhanādena parānabhimukho drutam ||21||

22tamādravantaṃ saṃprekṣya garjantamiva toyadam
|
abhyavartanta saṃkruddhāḥ pāṇḍavānāṃ mahārathāḥ ||22||

23bhīmaseno'bhimanyuśca rākṣasaśca ghaṭotkacaḥ
|
draupadeyāḥ satyadhṛtiḥ kṣatradevaśca bhārata ||23||

24cedipo vasudānaśca daśārṇādhipatistathā
|
supratīkena tāṃścāpi bhagadatto'pyupādravat ||24||

25tataḥ samabhavadyuddhaṃ ghorarūpaṃ bhayānakam
|
pāṇḍūnāṃ bhagadattena yamarāṣṭravivardhanam ||25||

26prayuktā rathibhirbāṇā bhīmavegāḥ sutejanāḥ
|
te nipeturmahārāja nāgeṣu ca ratheṣu ca ||26||

27prabhinnāśca mahānāgā vinītā hastisādibhiḥ
|
parasparaṃ samāsādya saṃnipeturabhītavat ||27||

28madāndhā roṣasaṃrabdhā viṣāṇāgrairmahāhave
|
bibhidurdantamusalaiḥ samāsādya parasparam ||28||

29hayāśca cāmarāpīḍāḥ prāsapāṇibhirāsthitāḥ
|
coditāḥ sādibhiḥ kṣipraṃ nipeturitaretaram ||29||

30pādātāśca padātyodhaistāḍitāḥ śaktitomaraiḥ
|
nyapatanta tadā bhūmau śataśo'tha sahasraśaḥ ||30||

31rathinaśca rathai rājan karṇinālīkasāyakaiḥ
|
nihatya samare vīrān siṃhanādānvinedire ||31||

32tasmiṃstathā vartamāne saṃgrāme lomaharṣaṇe
|
bhagadatto maheṣvāso bhīmasenamathādravat ||32||

33kuñjareṇa prabhinnena saptadhā sravatā madam
|
parvatena yathā toyaṃ sravamāṇena sarvaśaḥ ||33||

34kirañcharasahasrāṇi supratīkaśirogataḥ
|
airāvatastho maghavān vāridhārā ivānagha ||34||

35sa bhīmaṃ śaradhārābhistāḍayāmāsa pārthivaḥ
|
parvataṃ vāridhārābhistapānte jalado yathā ||35||

36bhīmasenastu saṃkruddhaḥ pādarakṣān paraḥśatān
|
nijaghāna maheṣvāsaḥ saṃrabdhaḥ śaravṛṣṭibhiḥ ||36||

37tān dṛṣṭvā nihatān kruddho bhagadattaḥ pratāpavān
|
codayāmāsa nāgendraṃ bhīmasenarathaṃ prati ||37||

38sa nāgaḥ preṣitastena bāṇo jyācodito yathā
|
abhyadhāvata vegena bhīmasenamarindamam ||38||

39tamāpatantaṃ saṃprekṣya pāṇḍavānāṃ mahārathāḥ
|
abhyavartanta vegena bhīmasenapurogamāḥ ||39||

40kekayāścābhimanyuśca draupadeyāśca sarvaśaḥ
|
daśārṇādhipatiḥ śūraḥ kṣatradevaśca māriṣa ||40||

41cedipaścitraketuśca saṃrabdhāḥ sarva eva te
|
uttamāstrāṇi divyāni darśayanto mahābalāḥ ||41||

42tamekaṃ kuñjaraṃ kruddhāḥ samantāt paryavārayan
|
sa viddho bahubhirbāṇairvyarocata mahādvipaḥ ||42||

43saṃjātarudhirotpīḍo dhātucitra ivādrirāṭ
|
daśārṇādhipatiścāpi gajaṃ bhūmidharopamam ||43||

44samāsthito'bhidudrāva bhagadattasya vāraṇam
|
tamāpatantaṃ samare gajaṃ gajapatiḥ sa ca ||44||

45dadhāra supratīko'pi veleva makarālayam
|
vāritaṃ prekṣya nāgendraṃ daśārṇasya mahātmanaḥ ||45||

46sādhu sādhviti sainyāni pāṇḍaveyānyapūjayan
|
tataḥ prāgjyotiṣaḥ kruddhastomarānvai caturdaśa ||46||

47prāhiṇottasya nāgasya pramukhe nṛpasattama
|
varma mukhyaṃ tanutrāṇaṃ śātakumbhapariṣkṛtam ||47||

48vidārya prāviśan kṣipraṃ valmīkabhiva pannagāḥ
|
sa gāḍhaviddho vyathito nāgo bharatasattama ||48||

49upāvṛttamadaḥ kṣipramabhyavartata vegitaḥ
|
sa pradudrāva vegena praṇadanbhairavaṃ ravam ||49||

50saṃmardayānaḥ svabalaṃ vāyurvṛkṣānivaujasā
|
tasminparājite nāge pāṇḍavānāṃ mahārathāḥ ||50||

51siṃhanādaṃ vinadyoccairyuddhāyaivāvatasthire
|
tato bhīmaṃ puraskṛtya bhagada‌ttamupādravan ||51||

52kiranto vividhān bāṇān śastrāṇi vividhāni ca
|
teṣāmāpatatāṃ rājan saṃkruddhānāmamarṣiṇām ||52||

53śrutvā sa ninadaṃ ghoramamarṣādgatasādhvasaḥ
|
bhagadatto maheṣvāsaḥ svanāgaṃ pratyacodayat ||53||

54aṅkuśāṅguṣṭhanuditaḥ sa gajapravaro yudhi
|
tasmin kṣaṇe samabhavatsāṃvartaka ivānalaḥ ||54||

55rathasaṅghāṃstathā nāgān hayāṃśca hayasādibhiḥ
|
pādātāṃśca susaṃkraddhaḥ śataśo'tha sahasraśaḥ ||55||

56amṛdūnātsamare nāgaḥ saṃpradhāvaṃstatastataḥ
|
tena saṃloḍyamānaṃ tu pāṇḍavānāṃ balaṃ mahat ||56||

57saṃcukoca mahārāja carmevāgnau samāhitam
|
bhagnaṃ tu svabalaṃ dṛṣṭvā bhagadattena dhīmatā ||57||

58ghaṭotkaco'tha saṃkruddho bhagadattamupādravat
|
vikaṭaḥ paruṣo rājan dīptāsyo dīptalocanaḥ ||58||

59rūpaṃ vibhīṣaṇaṃ kṛtvā roṣeṇa prajvalanniva
|
jagrāha vimalaṃ śūlaṃ girīṇāmapi dāraṇam ||59||

60nāgaṃ jighāṃsuḥ sahasā cikṣepa ca mahābalaḥ
|
sa visphuliṅgamālābhiḥ samaṃtātpariveṣṭitaḥ ||60||

61tamāpatantaṃ sahasā dṛṣṭvā prāgjyotiṣo nṛpaḥ
|
cikṣepa ruciraṃ tīkṣṇamardhacandraṃ sudāruṇam ||61||

62ciccheda tanmahacchūlaṃ tena bāṇena vegavān
|
utpapāta dvidhā cchinnaṃ śūlaṃ hemapariṣkṛtam ||62||

63mahāśaniryathā bhraṣṭā śakramuktā nabhogatā
|
śūlaṃ nipatitaṃ dṛṣṭvā dvidhā kṛttaṃ ca pārthivaḥ ||63||

64rukmadaṇḍāṃ mahāśaktiṃ jagrāhāgniśikhopamām
|
cikṣepa tāṃ rākṣasasya tiṣṭha tiṣṭheti cābravīt ||64||

65tāmāpatantīṃ saṃprekṣya viyatsthāmaśanīmiva
|
utpatya rākṣasastūrṇaṃ jagrāha ca nanāda ca ||65||

66babhañja caināṃ tvarito jānunyāropya bhārata
|
paśyataḥ pārthivendrasya tada‌dbhutamivābhavat ||66||

67tadavekṣya kṛtaṃ karma rākṣasena balīyasā
|
divi devāḥ sagandharvā munayaścāpi vismitāḥ ||67||

68pāṇḍavāśca mahārāja bhīmasenapurogamāḥ
|
sādhu sādhviti nādena pṛthivīmanvanādayan ||68||

69taṃ tu śrutvā mahānādaṃ prahṛṣṭānāṃ mahātmanām
|
nāmṛṣyata maheṣvāso bhagadattaḥ pratāpavān ||69||

70sa visphārya mahaccāpamindrāśanisamaprabham
|
tarjayāmāsa vegena pāṇḍavānāṃ mahārathān ||70||

71visṛjan vimalāṃstīkṣṇān nārācān jvalanaprabhān
|
bhīmamekena vivyādha rākṣasaṃ navabhiḥ śaraiḥ ||71||

72abhimanyuṃ tribhiścaiva kekayānpañcabhistathā
|
pūrṇāyatavisṛṣṭena śareṇānataparvaṇā ||72||

73bibheda dakṣiṇaṃ bāhuṃ kṣatradevasya cāhave
|
papāta sahasā tasya saśaraṃ dhanuruttamam ||73||

74draupadeyāṃstataḥ pañca pañcabhiḥ samatāḍayat
|
bhīmasenasya ca krodhānnijaghāna turaṅgamān ||74||

75dhvajaṃ kesariṇaṃ cāsya ciccheda viśikhaistribhiḥ
|
nirbibheda tribhiścānyaiḥ sārathiṃ cāsya patribhiḥ ||75||

76sa gāḍhaviddho vyathito rathopastha upāviśat
|
viśoko bharataśreṣṭha bhagadattena saṃyuge ||76||

77tato bhīmo mahābāhurviratho rathināṃ varaḥ
|
gadāṃ pragṛhya vegena pracaskanda rathottamāt ||77||

78tamudyatagadaṃ dṛṣṭvā saśṛṅgamiva parvatam
|
tāvakānāṃ bhayaṃ ghoraṃ samapadyata bhārata ||78||

79etasminneva kāle tu pāṇḍavaḥ kṛṣṇasārathiḥ
|
ājagāma mahārāja nighnan śatrūn samantataḥ ||79||

80yatra tau puruṣavyāghrau pitāputrau mahābalau
|
prāgjyotiṣeṇa saṃyuktau bhīmasenaghaṭotkacau ||80||

81dṛṣṭvā ca pāṇḍavo bhrātṝnyudhyamānānmahārathān
|
tvarito bharataśreṣṭha tatrāyudhyatkirañcharān ||81||

82tato duryodhano rājā tvaramāṇo mahārathaḥ
|
senāmacodayat kṣipraṃ rathanāgāśvasaṃkulām ||82||

83tāmāpatantīṃ sahasā kauravāṇāṃ mahācamūm
|
abhidudrāva vegena pāṇḍavaḥ śvetavāhanaḥ ||83||

84bhagada‌ttaśca samare tena nāgena bhārata
|
vimṛdnanpāṇḍavabalaṃ yudhiṣṭhiramupādravat ||84||

85tadā''sītsumahadyuddhaṃ bhagadattasya māriṣa
|
pañcālaiḥ pāṇḍaveyaiśca kekayaiścodyatāyudhaiḥ ||85||

86bhīmaseno'pi samare tāvubhau keśavārjunau
|
aśrāvayadyathāvṛttabhirāvadvadhamuttamam ||86||
