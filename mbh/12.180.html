Mahābhārata 12.180 (Śāntiparvan)

1yudhiṣṭhira uvāca|
bāndhavāḥ karma vittaṃ vā prajñā veha pitāmaha
|
narasya kā pratiṣṭhā syādetatpṛṣṭo vadasva me ||1||

2bhīṣma uvāca|
prajñā pratiṣṭhā bhūtānāṃ prajñā lābhaḥ paro mataḥ
|
prajñā niḥśreyasī loke prajñā svargo mataḥ satām ||2||

3prajñayā prāpitārtho hi baliraiśvaryasaṃkṣaye
|
prahlādo namucirmaṅkistasyāḥ kiṃ vidyate param ||3||

4atrāpyudāharantīmamitihāsaṃ purātanam
|
indrakāśyapasaṃvādaṃ tannibodha yudhiṣṭhira ||4||

5vaiśyaḥ kaścidṛṣisutaṃ kāśyapaṃ saṃśitavratam
|
rathena pātayāmāsa śrīmān dṛptastapasvinam ||5||

6ārtaḥ sa patitaḥ kruddhastyaktvā''tmānamathābravīt
|
mariṣyāmyadhanasyeha jīvitārtho na vidyate ||6||

7tathā mumūrṣumāsīnamakūjantamacetasam
|
indraḥ śṛgālarūpeṇa babhāṣe lubdhamānasam ||7||

8manuṣyayonimicchanti sarvabhūtāni sarvaśaḥ
|
manuṣyatve ca vipratvaṃ sarva evābhinandati ||8||

9manuṣyo brāhmaṇaścāsi śrotriyaścāsi kāśyapa
|
sudurlabhamavāpyaitanna doṣānmartumarhasi ||9||

10sarve lābhāḥ sābhimānā iti satyavatī śrutiḥ
|
santoṣaṇīyarūpo'si lobhādyadabhimanyase ||10||

11aho siddhārthatā teṣāṃ yeṣāṃ santīha pāṇayaḥ
|
atīva spṛhaye teṣāṃ yeṣāṃ santīha pāṇayaḥ ||11||

12pāṇimadbhyaḥ spṛhā'smākaṃ yathā tava dhanasya vai
|
na pāṇilābhādadhiko lābhaḥ kaścana vidyate ||12||

13apāṇitvādvayaṃ brahman kaṇṭakaṃ noddharāmahe
|
jantūnuccāvacānaṅge daśato na kaṣāma vā ||13||

14atha yeṣāṃ punaḥ pāṇī devadattau daśāṅgulī
|
uddharanti kṛmīnaṅgāddaśato nikaṣanti ca ||14||

15varṣāhimātapānāṃ ca paritrāṇāni kurvate
|
cailamannaṃ sukhaṃ śayyāṃ nivātaṃ copabhuñjate ||15||

16adhiṣṭhāya ca gāṃ loke bhuñjate vāhayanti ca
|
upāyairbahubhiścaiva vaśyānātmani kurvate ||16||

17ye khalvajihvāḥ kṛpaṇā alpaprāṇā apāṇayaḥ
|
sahante tāni duḥkhāni diṣṭyā tvaṃ na tathā mune ||17||

18diṣṭyā tvaṃ na śṛgālo vai na kṛmirna ca mūṣakaḥ
|
na sarpo na ca maṇḍūko na cānyaḥ pāpayonijaḥ ||18||

19etāvatāpi lābhena toṣṭumarhasi kāśyapa
|
kiṃ punaryo'si sattvānāṃ sarveṣāṃ brāhmaṇottamaḥ ||19||

20ime māṃ kṛmayo'danti yeṣāmuddharaṇāya vai
|
nāsti śaktirapāṇitvātpaśyāvasthāmimāṃ mama ||20||

21akāryamiti caivemaṃ nātmānaṃ santyajāmyaham
|
nātaḥ pāpīyasīṃ yoniṃ pateyamaparāmiti ||21||

22madhye vai pāpayonīnāṃ śārgālīṃ yāmahaṃ gataḥ
|
pāpīyasyo bahutarā ito'nyāḥ pāpayonayaḥ ||22||

23jātyaivaike sukhitarāḥ santyanye bhṛśaduḥkhitāḥ
|
naikāntaṃ sukhameveha kvacitpaśyāmi kasyacit ||23||

24manuṣyā hyāḍhyatāṃ prāpya rājyamicchantyanantaram
|
rājyāddevatvamicchanti devatvādindratāmapi ||24||

25bhavestvaṃ yadyapi tvāḍhyo na rājā na ca daivatam
|
devatvaṃ prāpya cendratvaṃ naiva tuṣyestathā sati ||25||

26na tṛptiḥ priyalābhe'sti tṛṣṇā nādbhiḥ praśāmyati
|
samprajvalati sā bhūyaḥ samidbhiriva pāvakaḥ ||26||

27astyeva tvayi śoko'pi harṣaścāpi tathā tvayi
|
sukhaduḥkhe tathā cobhe tatra kā parivedanā ||27||

28paricchidyaiva kāmānāṃ sarveṣāṃ caiva karmaṇām
|
mūlaṃ buddhīndriyagrāmaṃ śakuntāniva pañjare ||28||

29na dvitīyasya śirasaśchedanaṃ vidyate kvacit
|
na ca pāṇestṛtīyasya yannāsti na tato bhayam ||29||

30na khalvapyarasajñasya kāmaḥ kvacana jāyate
|
saṃsparśāddarśanādvāpi śravaṇādvāpi jāyate ||30||

31na tvaṃ smarasi vāruṇyā laṭvākānāṃ ca pakṣiṇām
|
tābhyāṃ cābhyadhiko bhakṣyo na kaścidvidyate kvacit ||31||

32yāni cānyāni bhūteṣu bhakṣyajātāni kasyacit
|
yeṣāmabhuktapūrvāṇi teṣāmasmṛtireva te ||32||

33aprāśanamasaṃsparśamasandarśanameva ca
|
puruṣasyaiṣa niyamo manye śreyo na saṃśayaḥ ||33||

34pāṇimanto balavanto dhanavanto na saṃśayaḥ
|
manuṣyā mānuṣaireva dāsatvamupapāditāḥ ||34||

35vadhabandhaparikleśaiḥ kliśyante ca punaḥ punaḥ
|
te khalvapi ramante ca modante ca hasanti ca ||35||

36apare bāhubalinaḥ kṛtavidyā manasvinaḥ
|
jugupsitāṃ ca kṛpaṇāṃ pāpavṛttimupāsate ||36||

37utsahante ca te vṛttimanyāmapyupasevitum
|
svakarmaṇā tu niyataṃ bhavitavyaṃ tu tattathā ||37||

38na pulkaso na cāṇḍāla ātmānaṃ tyaktumicchati
|
tayā tuṣṭaḥ svayā yonyā māyāṃ paśyasva yādṛśīm ||38||

39dṛṣṭvā kuṇīnpakṣahatān manuṣyānāmayāvinaḥ
|
susampūrṇaḥ svayā yonyā labdhalābho'si kāśyapa ||39||

40yadi brāhmaṇa dehaste nirātaṅko nirāmayaḥ
|
aṅgāni ca samagrāṇi na ca lokeṣu dhikkṛtaḥ ||40||

41na kenacitpravādena satyenaivāpahāriṇā
|
dharmāyottiṣṭha viprarṣe nātmānaṃ tyaktumarhasi ||41||

42yadi brahman śṛṇoṣyetacchraddadhāsi ca me vacaḥ
|
vedoktasyaiva dharmasya phalaṃ mukhyamavāpsyasi ||42||

43svādhyāyamagnisaṃskāramapramatto'nupālaya
|
satyaṃ damaṃ ca dānaṃ ca spardhiṣṭhā mā ca kenacit ||43||

44ye kecana svadhyayanāḥ prāptā yajanayājam
|
kathaṃ te cānuśoceyuārdhyāyurvā'pyaśobhanam
|
icchantaste vihārāya sukhaṃ mahadavāpnuyuḥ ||44||

45uta jātāḥ sunakṣatre sutithau sumuhūrtajāḥ
|
yajñadānaprajehāyāṃ yatante śaktipūrvakam ||45||

46nakṣatreṣvāsureṣvanye dustithau durmuhūrtajāḥ
|
sampatantyāsurī yoniṃ yajñaprasavavarjitāḥ ||46||

47ahamāsaṃ paṇḍitako haituko vedanindakaḥ
|
ānvīkṣikīṃ tarkavidyāmanurakto nirarthikām ||47||

48hetuvādānpravaditā vaktā saṃsatsu hetumat
|
ākroṣṭā cābhivaktā ca brahmavākyeṣu ca dvijān ||48||

49nāstikaḥ sarvaśaṅkī ca mūrkhaḥ paṇḍitamānikaḥ
|
tasyeyaṃ phalanirvṛttiḥ sṛgālatvaṃ mama dvija ||49||

50api jātu tathā tasmādahorātraśatairapi
|
yadahaṃ mānuṣīṃ yoniṃ sṛgālaḥ prāpnuyāṃ punaḥ ||50||

51santuṣṭaścāpramattaśca yajñadānataporatiḥ
|
jñeyajñātā bhaveyaṃ vai varjyavarjayitā tathā ||51||

52tataḥ sa munirutthāya kāśyapastamuvāca ha
|
aho batāsi kuśalo buddhimāṃśceti vismitaḥ ||52||

53samavaikṣata taṃ vipro jñānadīrgheṇa cakṣuṣā
|
dadarśa cainaṃ devānāṃ devamindraṃ śacīpatim ||53||

54tataḥ sampūjayāmāsa kāśyapo harivāhanam
|
anujñātastu tenātha praviveśa svamālayam ||54||
