Mahābhārata 3.181 (Āraṇyakaparvan)

1yudhiṣṭhira uvāca|
bhavānetādṛśo loke vedavedāṅgapāragaḥ
|
brūhi kiṃ kurvataḥ karma bhavedgatiranuttamā||1||

2sarpa uvāca|
pātre datvā priyāṇyuktvā satyamuktvā ca bhārata
|
ahiṃsānirataḥ svargaṃ gacchediti matirmama||2||

3yudhiṣṭhira uvāca|
dānādvā sarpa satyādvā kimato guru dṛśyate
|
ahiṃsāpriyayoścaiva gurulāghavamucyatām||3||

4sarpa uvāca|
dānaṃ ca satyaṃ tattvaṃ vā ahiṃsā priyameva ca
|
eṣāṃ kāryagarīyastvāddṛśyate gurulāghavam||4||

5kasmācciddānayogāddhi satyameva viśiṣyate
|
satyavākyācca rājendra kiṃciddānaṃ viśiṣyate||5||

6evameva maheṣvāsa priyavākyānmahīpate
|
ahiṃsā dṛśyate gurvī tataśca priyamiṣyate||6||

7evametadbhavedrājan kāryāpekṣamanantaram
|
yadabhipretamanyatte brūhi yāvadbravīmyaham||7||

8yudhiṣṭhira uvāca|
kathaṃ svarge gatiḥ sarpa karmaṇāṃ ca phalaṃ dhruvam
|
aśarīrasya dṛśyeta prabrūhi viṣayāṃśca me||8||

9sarpa uvāca|
tisro vai gatayo rājan paridṛṣṭāḥ svakarmabhiḥ
|
mānuṣaṃ svargavāsaśca tiryagyoniśca tattridhā||9||

10tatra vai mānuṣāllokāddānādibhiratandritaḥ
|
ahiṃsārthasamāyuktaiḥ kāraṇaiḥ svargamaśnute||10||

11viparītaiśca rājendra kāraṇairmānuṣo bhavet
|
tiryagyonistathā tāta viśeṣaścātra vakṣyate||11||

12kāmakrodhasamāyukto hiṃsālobhasamanvitaḥ
|
manuṣyatvāt paribhraṣṭastiryagyonau prasūyate||12||

13tiryagyonyāḥ pṛthagbhāvo manuṣyārthe vidhīyate
|
gavādibhyastathā'śvebhyo devatvamapi dṛśyate||13||

14so'yametā gatīstāta jantuścarati kāryavān
|
nitye mahati cātmānamavasthāpayate dvijaḥ||14||

15jāto jātaśca balavān bhuṅkte cātmā sa dehavān
|
phalārthastāta niṣpṛktaḥ prajālakṣaṇabhāvanaḥ||15||

16yudhiṣṭhira uvāca|
śabde sparśe ca rūpe ca tathaiva rasagandhayoḥ
|
tasyādhiṣṭhānamavyagro brūhi sarpa yathātatham||16||

17kiṃ na gṛhṇāsi viṣayān yugapat tvaṃ mahāmate
|
etāvaducyatāṃ coktaṃ sarvaṃ pannagasattama||17||

18sarpa uvāca|
yadātmadravyamāyuṣman dehasaṃśrayaṇānvitam
|
karaṇādhiṣṭhitaṃ bhogānupabhuṅkte yathāvidhi||18||

19jñānaṃ caivātra buddhiśca manaśca bharatarṣabha
|
tasya bhogādhikaraṇe karaṇāni nibodha me||19||

20manasā tāta paryeti kramaśo viṣayānimān
|
viṣayāyatanasthena bhūtātmā kṣetraniḥsṛtaḥ||20||

21tatra cāpi naravyāghra mano jantorvidhīyate
|
tasmādyugapadatrāsya grahaṇaṃ nopapadyate||21||

22sa ātmā puruṣavyāghra bhruvorantaramāśritaḥ
|
buddhiṃ dravyeṣu sṛjati vividheṣu parāvarām||22||

23buddheruttarakālā ca vedanā dṛśyate budhaiḥ
|
eṣa vai rājaśārdūla vidhiḥ kṣetrajñabhāvanaḥ||23||

24yudhiṣṭhira uvāca|
manasaścāpi buddheśca brūhi me lakṣaṇaṃ param
|
etadadhyātmaviduṣāṃ paraṃ kāryaṃ vidhīyate||24||

25sarpa uvāca|
buddhirātmānugātīva utpātena vidhīyate
|
tadāśritā hi sā jñeyā buddhistasyaiṣiṇī bhavet||25||

26buddhirutpadyate kāryānmanastūtpannameva hi
|
buddherguṇavidhānena manastadguṇavadbhavet||26||

27etadviśeṣaṇaṃ tāta manobuddhyoryadantaram
|
tvamapyatrābhisambuddhaḥ kathaṃ vā manyate bhavān||27||

28yudhiṣṭhira uvāca|
aho buddhimatāṃ śreṣṭha śubhā buddhiriyaṃ tava
|
viditaṃ veditavyaṃ te kasmān samanupṛcchasi||28||

29sarvajñaṃ tvāṃ kathaṃ moha āviśat svargavāsinam
|
evamadbhutakarmāṇamiti me saṃśayo mahān||29||

30sarpa uvāca|
suprajñamapi cecchūramṛddhirmohayate naram
|
vartamānaḥ sukhe sarvo muhyatīti matirmama||30||

31so'hamaiśvaryamohena madāviṣṭo yudhiṣṭhira
|
patitaḥ pratisambuddhastvāṃ tu sambodhayāmyaham||31||

32kṛtaṃ kāryaṃ mahārāja tvayā mama parantapa
|
kṣīṇaḥ śāpaḥ sukṛcchro me tvayā sambhāṣya sādhunā||32||

33ahaṃ hi divi divyena vimānena caran purā
|
abhimānena mattaḥ san kañcinnānyamacintayam||33||

34brahmarṣi-deva-gandharva-yakṣa-rākṣasa-pannagāḥ
|
karān mama prayacchanti sarve trailokyavāsinaḥ||34||

35cakṣuṣā yaṃ prapadyāmi prāṇinaṃ pṛthivīpate
|
tasya tejo harāmyāśu taddhi dṛṣṭerbalaṃ mama||35||

36brahmarṣīṇāṃ sahasraṃ hi uvāha śibikāṃ mama
|
sa māmapanayo rājan bhraṃśayāmāsa vai śriyaḥ||36||

37tatra hyagastyaḥ pādena vahan spṛṣṭo mayā muniḥ
|
agastyena tato'smyukto dhvaṃsa sarpeti vai ruṣā||37||

38tatastasmādvimānāgryāt pracyutaścyutalakṣaṇaḥ
|
prapatan bubudhe''tmānaṃ vyālībhūtamadhomukham
|
āyācantamahaṃ vipraṃ śāpasyānto bhavediti||38||

39sarpa uvāca|
pramādāt sampramūḍhasya bhagavan kṣantumarhasi
|
tataḥ sa māmuvācedaṃ prapatantaṃ kṛpānvitaḥ||39||

40yudhiṣṭhiro dharmarājaḥ śāpāttvāṃ mokṣayiṣyati
|
abhimānasya ghorasya pāpasya ca narādhipa||40||

41phale kṣīṇe mahārāja phalaṃ puṇyamavāpsyasi
|
tato me vismayo jātastadṛṣṭvā tapaso balam||41||

42brahma ca brāhmaṇatvaṃ ca yena tvāhamacūcudam
|
satyaṃ damastapo dānamahiṃsā dharmanityatā||42||

43sādhakāni sadā puṃsāṃ na jātirna kulaṃ nṛpa
|
ariṣṭa eṣa te bhrātā bhīmaseno mahābalaḥ
|
svasti te'stu mahārāja gamiṣyāmi divaṃ punaḥ||43||

44vaiśampāyana uvāca|
ityuktvā''jagaraṃ dehaṃ muktvā sa nahuṣo nṛpaḥ
|
divyaṃ vapuḥ samāsthāya gatastridivameva ha||44||

45yudhiṣṭhiro'pi dharmātmā bhrātrā bhīmena saṅgataḥ
|
dhaumyena sahitaḥ śrīmānāśramaṃ punarāgamat||45||

46tato dvijebhyaḥ sarvebhyaḥ sametebhyo yathātatham
|
kathayāmāsa tat sarvaṃ dharmarājo yudhiṣṭhiraḥ||46||

47tacchrutvā te dvijāḥ sarve bhrātaraścāsya te trayaḥ
|
āsan suvrīḍitā rājan draupadī ca yaśasvinī||47||

48te tu sarve dvijaśreṣṭhāḥ pāṇḍavānāṃ hitepsayā
|
maivamityabruvan bhīmaṃ garhayanto'sya sāhasam||48||

49pāṇḍavāstu bhayānmuktaṃ prekṣya rbhāmaṃ mahābalam
|
harṣamāhārayāñcakrurvijahruśca mudā yutāḥ||49||
