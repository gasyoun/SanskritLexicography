Mahābhārata 1.164 (Ādiparvan)

1vaiśaṃpāyana uvāca|
tataḥ sa bhagnapārśvāṅgo naditvā bhairavaṃ ravam
|
śailarājapratīkāśo gatāsurabhavadbakaḥ||1||

2tena śabdena vitrasto janastasyātha rakṣasaḥ
|
niṣpapāta gṛhādrājansahaiva paricāribhiḥ||2||

3tānbhītānvigatajñānānbhīmaḥ praharatāṃ varaḥ
|
sāntvayāmāsa balavānsamaye ca nyaveśayat||3||

4na hiṃsyā mānuṣā bhūyo yuṣmābhiriti karhicit
|
hiṃsatāṃ hi vadhaḥ śīghramevameva bhavediti||4||

5tasya tadvacanaṃ śrutvā tāni rakṣāṃsi bhārata
|
evamastviti taṃ prāhurjagṛhuḥ samayaṃ ca tam||5||

6tataḥ prabhṛti rakṣāṃsi tatra saumyāni bhārata
|
nagare pratyadṛśyanta narairnagaravāsibhiḥ||6||

7tato bhīmastamādāya gatāsuṃ puruṣādakam
|
dvāradeśe vinikṣipya puramāgāmānupalakṣitaḥ||7||

8dṛṣṭvā bhīmabalodbhūtaṃ bakaṃ vinihataṃ tadā
|
jñātayo'sya bhayodvignāḥ pratijagmustatastataḥ||8||

9tataḥ sa bhīmastaṃ hatvā gatvā brāhmaṇaveśma tat
|
ācacakṣe yathāvṛttaṃ rājñaḥ sarvamaśeṣataḥ||9||

10tato narā viniṣkrāntā nagarātkalyameva tu
|
dadṛśurnihataṃ bhūmau rākṣasaṃ rudhirokṣitam||10||

11tamadrikūṭasadṛśaṃ vinikīrṇaṃ bhayānakam
|
dṛṣṭvā saṃhṛṣṭaromāṇo babhūvustatra nāgarāḥ||11||

12ekacakrāṃ tato gatvā pravṛttiṃ pradaduḥ pure
|
tataḥ sahasraśo rājannarā nagaravāsinaḥ||12||

13tatrājagmurbakaṃ draṣṭuṃ sastrīvṛddhakumārakāḥ
|
tataste vismitāḥ sarve karma dṛṣṭvā'timānuṣam
|
daivatānyarcayāñcakruḥ sarva eva viśāṃpate||13||

14tataḥ pragaṇayāmāsuḥ kasya vāro'dya bhojane
|
jñātvā cāgamya taṃ vipraṃ papracchuḥ sarva eva te||14||

15evaṃ pṛṣṭaḥ sa bahuśo rakṣamāṇaśca pāṇḍavān
|
uvāca nāgarānsarvānidaṃ viprarṣabhastadā||15||

16ājñāpitaṃ māmaśane rudantaṃ saha bandhubhiḥ
|
dadarśa brāhmaṇaḥ kaścinmantrasiddho mahāmanāḥ||16||

17paripṛcchya sa māṃ pūrvaṃ parikleśaṃ purasya ca
|
abravīdbrāhmaṇaśreṣṭho viśvāsya prahasanniva||17||

18prāpayiṣyāmyahaṃ tasmā annametaddurātmane
|
mannimittaṃ bhayaṃ cāpi na kāryamiti cābravīt||18||

19sa tadannamupādāya gato bakavanaṃ prati
|
tena nūnaṃ bhavedetatkarma lokahitaṃ kṛtam||19||

20tataste brāhmaṇāḥ sarve kṣatriyāśca suvismitāḥ
|
vaiśyāḥ śūdrāśca muditāścakrurbrahmamahaṃ tadā||20||

21tato jānapadāḥ sarve ājagmurnagaraṃ prati
|
tadadbhutatamaṃ dṛṣṭvā pārthāstatraiva cāvasan||21||
