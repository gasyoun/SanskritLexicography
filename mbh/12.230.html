Mahābhārata 12.230 (Śāntiparvan)

1yudhiṣṭhira uvāca|
priyaḥ sarvasya lokasya sarvasattvābhinanditā
|
guṇaiḥ sarvairupetaśca ko nvasti bhuvi mānavaḥ ||1||

2bhīṣma uvāca|
atra te vartayiṣyāmi pṛcchato bharatarṣabha
|
ugrasenasya saṃvādaṃ nārade keśavasya ca ||2||

3ugrasena uvāca |
yasya saṃkalpate loko nāradasya prakīrtane
|
manye sa guṇasampanno brūhi tanmama pṛcchataḥ ||3||

4vāsudeva uvāca |
kukurādhipa yānmanye śṛṇu tānme vivakṣataḥ
|
nāradasya guṇān sādhūn saṃkṣepeṇa narādhipa ||4||

5na cāritranimitto'syāhaṃkāro dehatāpanaḥ
|
abhinnaśrutacāritrastasmātsarvatra pūjitaḥ ||5||

6aratikrodhacāpalye bhayaṃ naitāni nārade
|
adīrghasūtraḥ śūraśca tasmātsarvatra pūjitaḥ ||6||

7upāsyo nārado bāḍhaṃ vāci nāsya vyatikramaḥ
|
kāmato yadi vā lobhāttasmātsarvatra pūjitaḥ ||7||

8adhyātmavidhitattvajñaḥ kṣāntaḥ śakto jitendriyaḥ
|
ṛjuśca satyavādī ca tasmātsarvatra pūjitaḥ ||8||

9tejasā yaśasā buddhyā jñānena vinayena ca
|
janmanā tapasā vṛddhastasmātsarvatra pūjitaḥ ||9||

10suśīlaḥ sukhasaṃveśaḥ subhojaḥ svādaraḥ śuciḥ
|
suvākyaścāpyanīrṣyaśca tasmātsarvatra pūjitaḥ ||10||

11kalyāṇaṃ kurute bāḍhaṃ pāpamasminna vidyate
|
na prīyate parānarthaistasmātsarvatra pūjitaḥ ||11||

12vedaśrutibhirākhyānairarthānabhijigīṣati
|
titikṣuranavajñātastasmātsarvatra pūjitaḥ ||12||

13samatvācca priyo nāsti nāpriyaśca kathaṃcana
|
mano'nukūlavādī ca tasmātsarvatra pūjitaḥ ||13||

14bahuśrutaścitrakathaḥ paṇḍito'lālaso'śaṭhaḥ
|
adīno'krodhano'lubdhastasmātsarvatra pūjitaḥ ||14||

15nārthe dhane vā kāme vā bhūtapūrvo'sya vigrahaḥ
|
doṣāścāsya samucchinnāstasmātsarvatra pūjitaḥ ||15||

16dṛḍhabhaktiranindyātmā śrutavānanṛśaṃsavān
|
vītasammohadoṣaśca tasmātsarvatra pūjitaḥ ||16||

17asaktaḥ sarvasaṅgeṣu saktātmeva ca lakṣyate
|
adīrghasaṃśayo vāgmī tasmātsarvatra pūjitaḥ ||17||

18samādhirnāsya kāmārthe nātmānaṃ stauti karhicit
|
anīrṣurmṛdusaṃvādastasmātsarvatra pūjitaḥ ||18||

19lokasya vividhaṃ cittaṃ prekṣate cāpyakutsayan
|
saṃsargavidyākuśalastasmātsarvatra pūjitaḥ ||19||

20nāsūyatyāgamaṃ kaṃcitsvanayenopajīvati
|
avandhyakālo vaśyātmā tasmātsarvatra pūjitaḥ ||20||

21kṛtaśramaḥ kṛtaprajño na ca tṛptaḥ samādhitaḥ
|
nityayukto'pramattaśca tasmātsarvatra pūjitaḥ ||21||

22nāpatrapaśca yuktaśca niyuktaḥ śreyase paraiḥ
|
abhettā paraguhyānāṃ tasmātsarvatra pūjitaḥ ||22||

23na hṛṣyatyarthalābheṣu nālābhe tu vyathatyapi
|
sthirabuddhirasaktātmā tasmātsarvatra pūjitaḥ ||23||

24taṃ sarvaguṇasampannaṃ dakṣaṃ śucimanāmayam
|
kālajñaṃ ca priyajñaṃ ca kaḥ priyaṃ na kariṣyati ||24||
