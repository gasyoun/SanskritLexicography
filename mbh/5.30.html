Mahābhārata 5.30 (Udyogaparvan)

1sañjaya uvāca|
āmantraye tvāṃ naradevadeva gacchāmyahaṃ pāṇḍava svasti te'stu
|
kaccinna vācā vṛjinaṃ hi kiñciduccāritaṃ me manaso'bhiṣaṅgāt ||1||

2janārdanaṃ bhīmasenārjunau ca mādrīsutau sātyakiṃ cekitānam
|
āmantrya gacchāmi śivaṃ sukhaṃ vaḥ saumyena māṃ paśyata cakṣuṣā nṛpāḥ ||2||

3yudhiṣṭhira uvāca|
anujñātaḥ sañjaya svasti gaccha na naḥ smarasyapriyaṃ jātu vidvan
|
vidmaśca tvāṃ te ca vayaṃ ca sarve śuddhātmānaṃ madhyagataṃ sabhāstham ||3||

4āpto dūtaḥ sañjaya supriyo'si kalyāṇavāk śīlavāṃstṛptimāṃśca
|
na muhyestvaṃ sañjaya jātu matyā na ca kruddhyerucyamāno duruktaiḥ ||4||

5na marmagāṃ jātu vaktā'si rūkṣāṃ nopaśrutiṃ kaṭukāṃ nota muktām
|
dharmārāmāmarthavatīmahiṃsrāmetāṃ vācaṃ tava jānīma sūta ||5||

6tvameva naḥ priyatamo'si dūta ihāgacchedviduro vā dvitīyaḥ
|
abhīkṣṇadṛṣṭo'si purā hi nastvaṃ dhanañjayasyātmasamaḥ sakhā'si ||6||

7ito gatvā sañjaya kṣiprameva upātiṣṭhethā brāhmaṇān ye tadarhāḥ
|
viśuddhavīryāścaraṇopapannāḥ kule jātāḥ sarvadharmāpapannāḥ ||7||

8svādhyāyino brāhmaṇā bhikṣavaśca tapasvino ye ca nityā vaneṣu
|
abhivādya vai madvacanena vṛddhāstathetareṣāṃ kuśalaṃ vadethāḥ ||8||

9purohitaṃ dhṛtarāṣṭrasya rājñastathā''cāryānṛtvijo ye ca tasya
|
taiśca tvaṃ tāta sahitairyathārhaṃ saṅgacchethāḥ kuśalenaiva sūta ||9||

10aśrotriyā ye ca vasanti vṛddhā manasvinaḥ śīlabalopapannāḥ
|
āśaṃsanto'smākamanusmaranto yathāśakti dharmamātrāṃ carantaḥ ||10||

11ślāghasva māṃ kuśalinaṃ sma tebhyo hyanāmayaṃ tāta pṛccherjaghanyam
|
ye jīvanti vyavahāreṇa rāṣṭre ye pālayanto nivasanti rāṣṭre||11||

12ācārya iṣṭo nayago vidheyo vedānabhīpsan brahmacaryaṃ cacāra
|
yo'straṃ catuṣpātpunareva cakre droṇaḥ prasanno'bhivādyastvayā'sau ||12||

13adhītavidyaścaraṇopapanno yo'straṃ catuṣpātpunareva cakre
|
gandharvaputrapratimaṃ tarasvinaṃ tamaśvatthāmānaṃ kuśalaṃ sma pṛccheḥ ||13||

14śāradvatasyāvasathaṃ sma gatvā mahārathasyātmavidāṃ varasya
|
tvaṃ māmabhīkṣṇaṃ parikīrtayan vai kṛpasya pādau sañjaya pāṇinā spṛśeḥ ||14||

15yasmin śauryamānṛśaṃsyaṃ tapaśca prajñā śīlaṃ śrutisattve dhṛtiśca
|
pādau gṛhītvā kurusattamasya bhīṣmasya māṃ tatra nivedayethāḥ ||15||

16prajñācakṣuryaḥ praṇetā kurūṇāṃ bahuśruto vṛddhasevī manīṣī
|
tasmai rājñe sthavirāyābhivādya ācakṣīthāḥ sañjaya māmarogam ||16||

17jyeṣṭhaḥ putro dhṛtarāṣṭrasya mando mūrkhaḥ śaṭhaḥ sañjaya pāpaśīlaḥ
|
praśastā vai pṛthivī yena sarvā suyodhanaṃ kuśalaṃ tāta pṛccheḥ ||17||

18bhrātā kanīyānapi tasya mandastathāśīlaḥ sañjaya so'pi śaśvat
|
maheṣvāsaḥ śūratamaḥ kurūṇāṃ duḥśāsanaḥ kuśalaṃ tāta vācyaḥ ||18||

19yasya kāmo vartate nityameva nānyacchamādbhāratānāmiti sma
|
sa bāhlikānāmṛṣabho manīṣī tvayā'bhivādyaḥ sañjaya sādhuśīlaḥ ||19||

20guṇairanekaiḥ pravaraiśca yukto vijñānavānnaiva ca niṣṭhuro yaḥ
|
snehādamarṣaṃ sahate sadaiva sa somadattaḥ pūjanīyo mato me||20||

21arhattamaḥ kuruṣu saumadattiḥ sa no bhrātā sañjaya matsakhā ca
|
maheṣvāso rathināmuttamo'rhaḥ sahāmātyaḥ kuśalaṃ tasya pṛccheḥ||21||

22ye caivānye kurumukhyā yuvānaḥ putrāḥ pautrā bhrātaraścaiva ye naḥ
|
yaṃ yameṣāṃ manyase yena yogyaṃ tattatprocyānāmayaṃ sūta vācyāḥ ||22||

23ye rājānaḥ pāṇḍavāyodhanāya samānītā dhārtarāṣṭreṇa kecit
|
vaśātayaḥ śālvakāḥ kekayāśca tathā'vaṣṭhā ye trigartāśca mukhyāḥ ||23||

24prācyodīcyā dākṣiṇātyāśca śūrāstathā pratīcyāḥ pārvatīyāśca sarve
|
anṛśaṃsāḥ śīlavṛttopapannāsteṣāṃ sarveṣāṃ kuśalaṃ sūta pṛccheḥ ||24||

25hastyārohā rathinaḥ sādinaśca padātayaścāryasaṅghā mahāntaḥ
|
ākhyāya māṃ kuśalinaṃ sma nityamanāmayaṃ paripṛccheḥ samagrān ||25||

26tathā rājño hyarthayuktānamātyān dauvārikān ye ca senāṃ nayanti
|
āyavyayaṃ ye gamayanti nityamarthāṃśca ye mahataścintayanti ||26||

27vṛndārakaṃ kurumadhyeṣvamūḍhaṃ mahāprajñaṃ sarvadharmopapannam
|
na tasya yuddhaṃ rocate vai kadācidvaiśyāputraṃ kuśalaṃ tāta pṛccheḥ ||27||

28nikartane devane yo'dvitīyaśchannopadhaḥ sādhudevī matākṣaḥ
|
yo durjayo devarathena saṅkhye sa citrasenaḥ kuśalaṃ tāta vācyaḥ ||28||

29gāndhārarājaḥ śakuniḥ pārvatīyo nikartanīyo'dvitīyo'kṣadevī
|
mānaṃ kurvan dhārtarāṣṭrasya sūta mithyābuddheḥ kuśalaṃ tāta pṛccheḥ ||29||

30yaḥ pāṇḍavānekarathena vīraḥ samutsahatyapradhṛṣyan vijetum
|
yo muhyatāṃ mohayitā'dvitīyo vaikartanaḥ kuśalaṃ tasya pṛccheḥ ||30||

31sa eva bhaktaḥ sa guruḥ sa bhartā sa vai pitā sa ca mātā suhṛcca
|
agādhabuddhirviduro dīrghadarśī sa no mantrī kuśalaṃ taṃ sma pṛccheḥ ||31||

32vṛddhāḥ striyo yāśca guṇopapannā jñāyante naḥ sañjaya mātarastāḥ
|
tābhiḥ sarvābhiḥ sahitābhiḥ sametya strībhiḥ savṛddhābhirabhivādaṃ vadethāḥ ||32||

33kaccit putrā jīvaputrāḥ susamyagvartante vo vṛttimanṛśaṃsarūpāḥ
|
iti smoktvā sañjaya brūhi paścādajātaśatruḥ kuśalī saputraḥ ||33||

34yā no bhāryāḥ sañjaya vettha tatra tāsāṃ sarvāsāṃ kuśalaṃ tāta pṛccheḥ
|
susaṃguptāḥ surabhayo'navadyāḥ kaccidgṛhānāvasathāpramattāḥ ||34||

35kaccidvṛttiṃ śvaśureṣu bhadrāḥ kalyāṇīṃ vartadhvamanṛśaṃsarūpām
|
yathā ca vaḥ syuḥ patayo'nukūlāstathā vṛttimātmanaḥ sthāpayadhvam ||35||

36yā naḥ snuṣāḥ sañjaya vettha tatra prāptāḥ kulebhyaśca guṇopapannāḥ
|
prajāvatyo brūhi sametya tāśca yudhiṣṭhiro vo'bhyavadat prasannaḥ ||36||

37kanyāḥ svajethāḥ sadaneṣu sañjaya anāmayaṃ madvacanena pṛṣṭvā
|
kalyāṇā vaḥ santu patayo'nukūlā yūyaṃ patīnāṃ bhavatānukūlāḥ ||37||

38alaṅkṛtā vastravatyaḥ sugandhā abībhatsāḥ sukhitā bhogavatyaḥ
|
laghu yāsāṃ darśanaṃ vāk ca laghvī veśastriyaḥ kuśalaṃ tāta pṛccheḥ ||38||

39dāsyaḥ syuryā ye ca dāsāḥ kurūṇāṃ tadāśrayā bahavaḥ kubjakhañjāḥ
|
ākhyāya māṃ kuśalinaṃ sma tebhyo'pyanāmayaṃ paripṛccherjaghanyam ||39||

40kaccidvṛttiṃ vartate vai purāṇīṃ kaccidbhogān dhārtarāṣṭro dadāti
|
aṅgahīnān kṛpaṇān vāmanān vā yānānṛśaṃsyo dhṛrtarāṣṭro bibharti ||40||

41andhāṃśca sarvān sthavirāṃstathaiva hastyājīvā bahavo ye'tra santi
|
ākhyāya māṃ kuśalinaṃ sma tebhyo'pyanāmayaṃ paripṛccherjaghanyam ||41||

42mā bhaiṣṭa duḥkhena kujīvitena nūnaṃ kṛtaṃ paralokeṣu pāpam
|
nigṛhya śatrūn suhṛdo'nugṛhya vāsobhirannena ca vo bhariṣye ||42||

43santyeva me brāhmaṇebhyaḥ kṛtāni bhāvīnyatho no bata vartayanti
|
tān paśyāmi yuktarūpāṃstathaiva tāmeva siddhiṃ śrāvayethā nṛpaṃ tam ||43||

44ye cānāthā durbalāḥ sarvakālamātmanyeva prayatante'tha mūḍhāḥ
|
tāṃścāpi tvaṃ kṛpaṇān sarvathaivāsmadvākyāt kuśalaṃ tāta pṛccheḥ ||44||

45ye cāpyanye saṃśritā dhārtarāṣṭrānnānādigbhyo'bhyāgatāḥ sūtaputra
|
dṛṣṭvā tāṃścaivārhataścāpi sarvān saṃpṛcchethāḥ kuśalaṃ cāvyayaṃ ca ||45||

46evaṃ sarvānāgatābhyāgatāṃśca rājño dūtān sarvadigbhyo'bhyupetān
|
pṛṣṭvā sarvān kuśalaṃ tāṃśca sūta paścādahaṃ kuśalī teṣu vācyaḥ ||46||

47na hīdṛśāḥ santyapare pṛthivyāṃ ye yodhakā dhārtarāṣṭreṇa labdhāḥ
|
dharmastu nityo mama dharma eva mahābalaḥ śatrunibarhaṇāya ||47||

48idaṃ punarvacanaṃ dhārtarāṣṭraṃ suyodhanaṃ sañjaya śrāvayethāḥ
|
yaste śarīre hṛdayaṃ dunoti kāmaḥ kurūnasapatno'nuśiṣyām ||48||

49na vidyate yuktiretasya kācinnaivaṃvidhāḥ syāma yathā priyaṃ te
|
dadasva vā śakrapurīṃ mamaiva yuddhyasva vā bhāratamukhyavīra ||49||
