Mahābhārata 12.171 (Śāntiparvan)

1bhīṣma uvāca|
tataḥ sa vidito rājñaḥ praviśya gṛhamuttamam
|
pūjito rākṣasendreṇa niṣasādāsanottame ||1||

2pṛṣṭaśca gotracaraṇaṃ svādhyāyaṃ brahmacārikam
|
na tatra vyājahārānyadgotramātrādṛte dvijaḥ ||2||

3brahmavarcasahīnasya svādhyāyoparatasya ca
|
gotramātravido rājā nivāsaṃ samapṛcchata ||3||

4rākṣasa uvāca |
kvate nivāsaḥ kalyāṇa kiṃgotrā brāhmaṇī ca te
|
tattvaṃ brūhi na bhīḥ kāryā viśvasasva yathāsukham ||4||

5gautama uvāca |
madhyadeśaprasūto'haṃ vāso me śabarālaye
|
śūdrā punarbhūrbhāryā me satyametadbravīmi te ||5||

6bhīṣma uvāca|
tato rājā vimamṛśe kathaṃ kāryamidaṃ bhavet
|
kathaṃ vā sukṛtaṃ me syāditi buddhyā'nvacintayat ||6||

7ayaṃ vai janmanā vipraḥ suhṛttasya mahātmanaḥ
|
sampreṣitaśca tenāyaṃ kāśyapena mamāntikam ||7||

8tasya priyaṃ kariṣyāmi sa hi māmāśritaḥ sadā
|
bhrātā me bāndhavaścāsau sakhā ca hṛdayaṅgamaḥ ||8||

9kārtikyāmadya bhoktāraḥ sahasraṃ me dvijottamāḥ
|
tatrāyamapi bhoktā ca deyamasmai ca me dhanam ||9||

10sa cādya divasaḥ puṇyo hyatithiścāyamāgataḥ
|
saṃkalpitaṃ caiva dhanaṃ kiṃ vicāryamataḥ param ||10||

11tataḥ sahasraṃ viprāṇāṃ viduṣāṃ samalaṃkṛtam
|
snātānāmanusamprāptaṃ sumahatkṣaumavāsasām ||11||

12tānāgatān dvijaśreṣṭhān virūpākṣo viśāmpate
|
yathārha pratijagrāha vidhidṛṣṭena karmaṇā ||12||

13bṛsyasteṣāṃ tu saṃnyastā rākṣasendrasya śāsanāt
|
bhūmau varakuśāḥ stīrṇāḥ preṣyairbharatasattama||13||

14tāsu te pūjitā rājñā niṣaṇṇā dvijasattamāḥ
|
tiladarbhodakenātha arcitā vidhivaddvijāḥ ||14||

15viśvedevāḥ sapitaraḥ sāgnayaścopakalpitāḥ
|
viliptāḥ puṣpavantaśca supracārāḥ supūjitāḥ ||15||

16vyarājanta mahārāja nakṣatrapatayo yathā
|
tato jāmbūnadīḥ pātrīrvajrāṅkā vimalāḥ śubhāḥ ||16||

17varānnapūrṇā viprebhyaḥ prādānmadhughṛtaplutāḥ
|
tasya nityaṃ sadā''ṣāḍhyāṃ mādhyāṃ ca bahavo dvijāḥ ||17||

18īpsitaṃ bhojanavaraṃ labhante satkṛtaṃ sadā
|
viśeṣatastu kārtikyāṃ dvijebhyaḥ samprayacchati ||18||

19śaradvyapāye ratnāni paurṇamāsyāmiti śrutiḥ
|
suvarṇaṃ rajataṃ caiva maṇīnatha ca mauktikān ||19||

20vajrānmahādhanāṃścaiva vaidūryājinarāṅkavān
|
ratnarāśīnvinikṣipya dakṣiṇārthe sa bhārata ||20||

21tataḥ prāha dvijaśreṣṭhān virūpākṣo mahābalaḥ
|
gṛhṇīta ratnānyetāni yathotsāhaṃ yatheṣṭataḥ ||21||

22yeṣu yeṣu ca bhāṇḍeṣu bhuktaṃ vo dvijasattamāḥ
|
tānyevādāya gacchadhvaṃ svaveśmānīti bhārata ||22||

23ityuktavacane tasmin rākṣasendre mahātmani
|
yatheṣṭaṃ tāni ratnāni jagṛhurbrāhmaṇarṣabhāḥ ||23||

24tato mahārhāste sarve ratnairabhyarcitāḥ śubhaiḥ
|
brāhmaṇā mṛṣṭavasanāḥ suprītāḥ sma tato'bhavan ||24||

25tatastān rākṣasendraśca dvijānāha punarvacaḥ
|
nānādeśagatān rājan rākṣasān pratiṣidhya vai ||25||

26adyaikaṃ divasaṃ viprā na vo'stīha bhayaṃ kvacit
|
rākṣasebhyaḥ pramodadhvamiṣṭato yāta māciram ||26||

27tataḥ pradudruvuḥ sarve viprasaṃghāḥ samantataḥ
|
gautamo'pi suvarṇasya bhāramādāya satvaraḥ ||27||

28kṛcchrātsamuddharan bhāraṃ nyagrodhaṃ samupāgamat
|
nyaṣīdacca pariśrāntaḥ klāntaśca kṣudhitaśca saḥ ||28||

29tatastamabhyagādrājan rājadharmā khagottamaḥ
|
svāgatenābhinandaṃśca gautamaṃ mitravatsalaḥ ||29||

30tasya pakṣāgravikṣepaiḥ klamaṃ vyapanayatkhagaḥ
|
pūjāṃ cāpyakaroddhīmān bhojanaṃ cāpyakalpayat ||30||

31sa bhuktavānsuviśrānto gautamo'cintayattadā
|
hāṭakasyābhirūpasya bhāro'yaṃ sumahānmayā ||31||

32gṛhīto lobhamohābhyāṃ dūraṃ ca gamanaṃ mama
|
na cāsti pathi bhoktavyaṃ prāṇasandhāraṇaṃ mama ||32||

33kiṃ kṛtvā dhārayeyaṃ vai prāṇānityabhyacintayat
|
tataḥ sa pathi bhoktavyaṃ prekṣamāṇo na kiñcana ||33||

34kṛtaghnaḥ puruṣavyāghra manasedamacintayat
|
ayaṃ bakapatiḥ pārśve māṃsarāśiḥ sthito mahān
|
imaṃ hatvā gṛhītvā ca yāsye'haṃ samabhidrutam ||34||
