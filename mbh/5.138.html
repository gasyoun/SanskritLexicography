Mahābhārata 5.138 (Udyogaparvan)

1vaiśampāyana uvāca|
kuntyāstu vacanaṃ śrutvā bhīṣmadroṇau mahārathau
|
duryodhanamidaṃ vākyamūcatuḥ śāsanātigam ||1||

2śrutaṃ te puruṣavyāghra kuntyāḥ kṛṣṇasya sannidhau
|
vākyamarthavadatyugramuktaṃ dharmyamanuttamam ||2||

3tat kariṣyanti kaunteyā vāsudevasya saṃmatam
|
na hi te jātu śāmyerannṛte rājyena kaurava ||3||

4kleśitā hi tvayā pārthā dharmapāśasitāstadā
|
sabhāyāṃ draupadī caiva taiśca tanmarṣitaṃ tava ||4||

5kṛtāstraṃ hyarjunaṃ prāpya bhīmaṃ ca kṛtaniścayam
|
gāṇḍīvaṃ ceṣudhī caiva rathaṃ ca dhvajameva ca ||5||

6nakulaṃ sahadevaṃ ca balavīryasamanvitau
|
sahāyaṃ vāsudevaṃ ca na kṣaṃsyati yudhiṣṭhiraḥ ||6||

7pratyakṣaṃ te mahābāho yathā pārthena dhīmatā
|
virāṭanagare pūrvaṃ sarve sma yudhi nirjitāḥ ||7||

8dānavā ghorakarmāṇo nivātakavacā yudhi
|
raudramastraṃ samādāya dagdhā vānaraketunā ||8||

9karṇaprabhṛtayaśceme tvaṃ cāpi kavacī rathī
|
mokṣito ghoṣayātrāyāṃ paryāptaṃ tannidarśanam
|
praśāmya bharataśreṣṭha bhrātṛbhiḥ saha pāṇḍavaiḥ ||9||

10rakṣemāṃ pṛthivīṃ sarvāṃ mṛtyordaṣṭrāntaraṃ gatām
|
jyeṣṭho bhrātā dharmaśīlo vatsalaḥ ślakṣṇavākkaviḥ ||10||

11taṃ gaccha puruṣavyāghraṃ vyapanīyeha kilbiṣam
|
dṛṣṭaśca tvaṃ pāṇḍavena vyapanītaśarāsanaḥ ||11||

12praśāntabhrukuṭiḥ śrīmān kṛtā śāntiḥ kulasya naḥ
|
tamabhyetya sahāmātyaḥ pariṣvajya nṛpātmajam ||12||

13abhivādaya rājānaṃ yathāpūrvamarindama
|
abhivādayamānaṃ tvāṃ pāṇibhyāṃ bhīmapūrvajaḥ ||13||

14pratigṛhṇātu sauhārdāt kuntīputro yudhiṣṭhiraḥ
|
siṃhaskandhorubāhustvāṃ vṛttāyatamahābhujaḥ ||14||

15pariṣvajatu bāhubhyāṃ bhīmaḥ praharatāṃ varaḥ
|
kambugrīvo guḍākeśastatastvāṃ puṣkarekṣaṇaḥ ||15||

16abhivādayatāṃ pārthaḥ kuntīputro dhanañjayaḥ ||16||

17āśvineyau naravyāghrau rupeṇāpratimau bhuvi
|
tau ca tvāṃ guruvatpremṇā pūjayā pratyudīyatām ||17||

18muñcantyānandajāśrūṇi dāśārhapramukhā nṛpāḥ
|
saṃgaccha bhrātṛbhiḥ sārdhaṃ mānaṃ saṃtyajya pārthiva ||18||

19praśādhi pṛthivīṃ kṛtsnāṃ tatastvaṃ bhrātṛbhiḥ saha
|
samāliṅgya ca harṣeṇa nṛpā yāntu parasparam ||19||

20alaṃ yuddhena rājendra sahṛdāṃ śṛṇu vāraṇam
|
dhruvaṃ vināśo yuddhe hi kṣatriyāṇāṃ pradṛdayate ||20||

21jyotīṃṣi pratikūlāni dāruṇā mṛgapakṣiṇaḥ
|
utpātā vividhā vīra dṛśyante kṣatranāśanāḥ ||21||

22viśeṣata ihāsmākaṃ nimittāni niveśane
|
ulkābhirhi pradīptābhirbādhyate pṛtanā tava ||22||

23vāhanānyaprahṛṣṭāni rudantīva viśāmpate
|
gṛdhrāste paryupāsante sainyāni ca samantataḥ ||23||

24nagaraṃ na yathāpūrvaṃ tathā rājaniveśanam
|
śivāścāśivanirghoṣā dīptāṃ sevanti vai diśam ||24||

25kuru vākyaṃ piturmāturasmākaṃ ca hitaiṣiṇām
|
tvayyāyatto mahābāho śamo vyāyāma eva ca ||25||

26na cetkariṣyasi vacaḥ suhṛdāmarikarśana
|
tapsyase vāhinīṃ dṛṣṭvā pārthabāṇaprapīḍitām ||26||

27bhīmasya ca mahānādaṃ nadataḥ śuṣmiṇo raṇe
|
śrutvā smartāsi me vākyaṃ gāṇḍīvasya ca niḥsvanam
|
yadyetadapasavyaṃ te vaco mama bhaviṣyati ||27||
