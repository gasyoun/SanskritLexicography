Mahābhārata 12.298 (Śāntiparvan)

1bhīṣma uvāca|
punareva tu papraccha janako mithilādhipaḥ
|
parāśaraṃ mahātmānaṃ dharme paramaniścayam ||1||

2janaka uvāca |
kiṃ śreyaḥ kā gatirbrahmankiṃ kṛtaṃ na vinaśyati
|
kva gato na nivarteta tanme brūhi mahāmate ||2||

3parāśara uvāca |
asaṅga śreyaso mūlaṃ jñānaṃ jñānagatiḥ parā
|
cīrṇaṃ tapo na praṇaśyedvāpaḥ kṣetre na naśyati ||3||

4chittvā'dharmamayaṃ pāśaṃ yadā dharme'bhirajyate
|
dattvā'bhayakṛtaṃ dānaṃ tadā siddhimavāpnute ||4||

5yo dadāti sahasrāṇi gavāmaśvaśatāni ca
|
abhayaṃ sarvabhūtebhyaḥ sadā tamabhivartate ||5||

6vasanviṣayamadhye'pi na vasatyeva buddhimān
|
saṃvasatyeva durbuddhirasatsu viṣayeṣvapi ||6||

7nādharmaḥ śliṣyate prājñaṃ payaḥ puṣkaraparṇavat
|
aprājñamadhikaṃ pāpaṃ śliṣyate jatukāṣṭhavat ||7||

8nādharmaḥ kāraṇāpekṣī kartāramabhimuñcati
|
kartā khalu yathākālaṃ tataḥ samabhipadyate ||8||

9na bhidyante kṛtātmāna ātmapratyayadarśinaḥ
|
buddhikarmendriyāṇāṃ hi pramatto yo na buddhyate
|
śubhāśubhe prasaktātmā prāpnoti sumahadbhayam ||9||

10vītarāgo jitakrodhaḥ samyagbhavati yaḥ sadā
|
viṣaye vartamāno'pi na sa pāpena yujyate ||10||

11maryādāyāṃ dharmaseturnibaddho naiva sīdati
|
puṣṭasrota ivāsaktaḥ sphīto bhavati saṃcayaḥ ||11||

12yathā bhānugataṃ tejo maṇiḥ śuddhaḥ samādhinā
|
ādatte rājaśārdūla tathā yogaḥ pravartate ||12||

13yathā tilānāmiha puṣpasaṃśrayātpṛthakpṛthagyāti guṇo'tisaumyatām
|
tathā narāṇāṃ tu vibhāvitātmanāṃ yathā''śrayaṃ sattvaguṇaḥ pravartate ||13||

14jahāti dārāṃśca jahāti sampadaḥ padaṃ ca yānaṃ vividhāśca satkriyāḥ
|
triviṣṭape jātamatiryadā narastadā'sya buddhirviṣayeṣu bhidyate ||14||

15prasaktabuddhirviṣayeṣu yo naro na budhyate hyātmahitaṃ kathaṃcana
|
sa sarvabhāvānugatena cetasā nṛpāmiṣeṇeva jhaṣo vikṛṣyate ||15||

16saṃghātavanmartyalokaḥ parasparamapāśritaḥ
|
kadalīgarbhaniḥsāro naurivāpsu nimajjati ||16||

17na dharmakālaḥ puruṣasya niścito na cāpi mṛtyuḥ puruṣaṃ pratīkṣate
|
sadā hi dharmasya kriyaiva śobhanā yadā naro mṛtyumukhe'bhivartate ||17||

18yathāndhaḥ svagṛhe yukto hyabhyāsādeva gacchati
|
tathā yuktena manasā prājño gacchati tāṃ gatim ||18||

19maraṇaṃ janmani proktaṃ janma vai maraṇāśritam
|
avidvānmokṣadharmeṣu baddho bhramati cakravat ||19||

20buddhimārgaprayātasya sukhaṃ tviha paratra ca
|
vistarāḥ kleśasaṃyuktāḥ saṃkṣepāstu sukhāvahāḥ
|
parārthaṃ vistarāḥ sarve tyāgamātmahitaṃ viduḥ ||20||

21yathā mṛṇālānugatamāśu muñcati kardamam
|
tathā''tmā puruṣasyeha manasā parimucyate ||21||

22manaḥ praṇayate''tmānaṃ sa enamabhiyuñjati
|
yukto yadā sa bhavati tadā taṃ paśyate param ||22||

23parārthe vartamānastu svaṃ kāryaṃ yo'bhimanyate
|
indriyārtheṣu saṃyuktaḥ svakāryātparimucyate ||23||

24adhastiryaggatiṃ caiva svarge caiva parāṃ gatim
|
prāpnoti svakṛtairātmā prājñasyehetarasya ca ||24||

25mṛṇmaye bhājane pakve yathā vai naśyati dravaḥ
|
tathā śarīraṃ tapasā taptaṃ viṣayamaśnute ||25||

26viṣayānuśnute yastu na sa bhokṣyatyasaṃśayam
|
yastu bhogāṃstyajedātmā sa vai bhoktuṃ vyavasyati ||26||

27nīhāreṇa hi saṃvītaḥ śiśnodaraparāyaṇaḥ
|
jātyandha iva panthānamāvṛtātmā na buddhyate ||27||

28vaṇigyathā samudrādvai yathārthaṃ labhate dhanam
|
tathā martyārṇave jantoḥ karmavijñānato gatiḥ ||28||

29ahorātramaye loke jarārūpeṇa saṃsaran
|
mṛtyurgrasati bhūtāni pavanaṃ pannago yathā ||29||

30svayaṃ kṛtāni karmāṇi jāto jantuḥ prapadyate
|
nākṛtvā labhate kaścitkiṃcidatra priyāpriyam ||30||

31śayānaṃ yāntamāsīnaṃ pravṛttaṃ viṣayeṣu ca
|
śubhāśubhāni karmāṇi prapadyante naraṃ sadā ||31||

32na hyanyattīramāsādya punastartuṃ vyavasyati
|
durlabho dṛśyate hyasya vinipāto mahārṇave ||32||

33yathā bhāvāvasannā hi naurmahāmbhasi tantunā
|
tathā manobhiyogādvai śarīraṃ pracikīrṣati ||33||

34yathā samudramabhitaḥ saṃśritāḥ sarito parāḥ
|
tathā'dyā prakṛtiryogādabhisaṃśriyate sadā ||34||

35snehapāśairbahuvidhairāsaktamanaso narāḥ
|
prakṛtisthā viṣīdanti jale saikataveśmavat ||35||

36śarīragṛhasaṃjñasya śaucatīrthasya dehinaḥ
|
buddhimārgaprayātasya sukhaṃ tviha paratra ca ||36||

37vistarāḥ kleśasaṃyuktāḥ saṃkṣepāstu sukhāvahāḥ
|
parārthaṃ vistarāḥ sarve tyāgamātmahitaṃ viduḥ ||37||

38saṃkalpajo mitravargo jñātayaḥ kāraṇātmakāḥ
|
bhāryā putraśca dāsaśca svamarthamupabhuñjate ||38||

39na mātā na pitā kiṃcitkasyacitpratipadyate
|
dānapathyaudano jantuḥ svakarmaphalamaśnute ||39||

40mātā putraḥ pitā bhrātā bhāryā mitrajanastathā
|
aṣṭāpadapadasthāne dakṣamudreva lakṣyate ||40||

41sarvāṇi karmāṇi purā kṛtāni śubhāśubhānyātmano yānti jantoḥ
|
upasthitaṃ karmaphalaṃ viditvā buddhiṃ tathā codayate'ntarātmā ||41||

42vyavasāyaṃ samāśritya sahāyānyo'dhigacchati
|
na tasya kaścidārambhaḥ kadācidavasīdati ||42||

43advaidhamanasaṃ yuktaṃ śūraṃ dhīraṃ vipaścitam
|
na śrīḥ santyajate nityamādityamiva raśmayaḥ ||43||

44āstikyavyavasāyābhyāmupāyādvismayāddhiyā
|
samārabhedanindyātmā na so'rthaḥ pariṣīdati ||44||

45sarvaḥ svāni śubhāśubhāni niyataṃ karmāṇi jantuḥ svayaṃ garbhātsampratipadyate tadubhayaṃ yattena pūrvaṃ kṛtam
|
mṛtyuścāparihāravānsamagatiḥ kālena vicchedinā dāroścūrṇamivāśmasāravihitaṃ karmāntikaṃ prāpayet ||45||

46svarūpatāmātmakṛtaṃ ca vistaraṃ kulānvayaṃ dravyasamṛddhisaṃcayam
|
naro hi sarvo labhate yathākṛtaṃ śubhāśubhenātmakṛtena karmaṇā ||46||

47bhīṣma uvāca|
ityukto janako rājan yāthātathyaṃ manīṣiṇā
|
śrutvā dharmavidāṃ śreṣṭhaḥ parāṃ mudamavāpa ha ||47||
