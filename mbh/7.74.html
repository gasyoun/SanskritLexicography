Mahābhārata 7.74 (Droṇaparvan)

1sañjaya uvāca|
śrutvā tu taṃ mahāśabdaṃ pāṇḍūnāṃ jayagṛddhinām
|
cāraiḥ pravedite tatra samutthāya jayadrathaḥ ||1||

2śokasaṃmūḍhahṛdayo duḥkhenābhipariplutaḥ
|
mañjamāna ivāgādhe vipule śokasāgare ||2||

3jagāma sabhitiṃ rājñāṃ saindhavo vimṛśan bahu
|
sa teṣāṃ naradevānāṃ sakāśe parvadevayan ||3||

4abhimanyoḥ piturbhītaḥ savrīḍo vākyamabravīt
|
yo'sau pāṇḍoḥ kila kṣetre jātaḥ śakreṇa kāminā ||4||

5sa ninīṣati durbuddhirmāṃ kilaikaṃ yamakṣayam
|
tat svasti vo'stu yāsyāmi svagṛhaṃ jīvitepsayā ||5||

6athavāstrapratibalāstrāta māṃ kṣatriyarṣabhāḥ
|
parthena prārthitaṃ vīrāste saṃdatta mamābhayam ||6||

7droṇa-duryodhana-kṛpāḥ karṇa-madreśa-bālhikāḥ
|
duḥśāsanādayaḥ śaktāstrātuṃ māmantakārditam ||7||

8kimaṅga punarekena phālgunena jighāṃsatā
|
na trāyeyurbhavanto māṃ samastāḥ patayaḥ kṣiteḥ ||8||

9praharṣaṃ pāṇḍaveyānāṃ śrutvā mama mahadbhayam
|
sīdanti mama gātrāṇi mumūrṣoriva pārthivāḥ ||9||

10vadho nūnaṃ pratijñāto mama gāṇḍīvadhanvanā
|
tathā hi hṛṣṭāḥ krośanti śokakāle sma pāṇḍavāḥ ||10||

11tanna devā na gandharvā nāsuroragarākṣasāḥ
|
utsahante'nyathā kartuṃ kuta eva narādhipāḥ ||11||

12tasmānmāmanujānīta bhadraṃ vo'stu nararṣabhāḥ
|
adarśanaṃ gamiṣyāmi na māṃ drakṣyanti pāṇḍavāḥ ||12||

13evaṃ vilapamānaṃ taṃ bhayādvyākulacetasam
|
ātmakāryagarīyastvādrājā duryodhano'bravīt ||13||

14na bhetavyaṃ naravyāghra ko hi tvāṃ puruṣarṣabha
|
madhye kṣatriyavīrāṇāṃ tiṣṭhantaṃ prārthayedyudhi ||14||

15ahaṃ vaikartanaḥ karṇaścitraseno viviṃśatiḥ
|
bhūriśravāḥ śalaḥ śalyo vṛṣaseno durāsadaḥ ||15||

16purumitro jayo bhojaḥ kāmbojaśca sudakṣiṇaḥ
|
satyavrato mahābāhurvikarṇo durmukhaśca ha ||16||

17duḥśāsanaḥ subāhuśca kāliṅgaścāpyudāyudhaḥ
|
vindānuvindāvāvantyau droṇo drauṇiśca saubalaḥ ||17||

18ete cānye ca bahavo nānājanapadeśvarāḥ
|
sasainyāstvābhiyāsyanti vyetu te mānaso jvaraḥ ||18||

19tvaṃ cāpi rathināṃ śreṣṭhaḥ svayaṃ śūro'mitadyute
|
sa kathaṃ pāṇḍaveyebhyo bhayaṃ paśyasi saindhava ||19||

20akṣauhiṇyo daśaikā ca madīyāstava rakṣaṇe
|
yattā yotsyanti mā bhaistvaṃ saindhava vyetu te bhayam ||20||

21sañjaya uvāca|
evamāśvāsito rājan putreṇa tava saindhavaḥ
|
duryodhanena sahito droṇaṃ rātrāvupāgamat ||21||

22upasaṃgrahaṇaṃ kṛtvā droṇāya sa viśāṃpate
|
upopaviśya praṇataḥ paryapṛcchadivaṃ tadā ||22||

23nimitte dūrapātitve laghutve dṛḍhavedhane
|
mama bravītu bhagavān viśeṣaṃ phālgunasya ca ||23||

24vidyāviśeṣamicchāmi jñātumācārya tattvataḥ
|
arjunasyātmanaścaiva yāthātathyaṃ pracakṣva me ||24||

25droṇa uvāca| sa mamācāryakaṃ tāta tava caivārjunasya ca
|
yogādduḥkhoṣitatvācca tasmāttvatto'dhiko'rjunaḥ ||25||

26na tu te yudhi saṃtrāsaḥ kāryaḥ pārthāt kathañcana
|
ahaṃ hi rakṣitā tāta bhayāttvāṃ nātra saṃśayaḥ ||26||

27na hi madbāhuguptasya prabhavantyamarā api
|
vyūhayiṣyāmi taṃ vyūhaṃ yaṃ pārtho na tariṣyati ||27||

28tasmādyuddhyasva mā bhaistvaṃ svadharmamanupālaya
|
pitṛpaitāmahaṃ mārgamanuyāhi mahāratha ||28||

29adhītya vidhivadvedānagnayaḥ suhutāstvayā
|
iṣṭaṃ bahubhiryajñairna te mṛtyurbhayaṅkaraḥ ||29||

30durlabhaṃ mānuṣairmandairmahābhāgyamavāpyatu
|
bhujavīryārjitān lokān divyān prāpsyasyanuttamān ||30||

31kuravaḥ pāṇḍavāścaiva vṛṣṇayo'nye ca mānavāḥ
|
ahaṃ ca saha putreṇa adhruvā iti cintyatām ||31||

32paryāyeṇa vayaṃ sarve kālena balinā hatāḥ
|
paralokaṃ gamiṣyāmaḥ svaiḥ svaiḥ karmābhiranvitāḥ ||32||

33tapastāptvā tu yān lokān prāpnuvanti tapasvinaḥ
|
kṣatradharmāśritā vīrāḥ kṣatriyāḥ prāpnuvanti tān ||33||

34evamāśvāsito rājā bhāradvājena saindhavaḥ
|
apānudadbhayaṃ pārthādyuddhāya ca mano dadhe ||34||

35tataḥ praharṣa sainyānāṃ tavāpyāsīdviśāṃpate
|
vāditrāṇāṃ dhvaniścograḥ siṃhanādaravaiḥ saha ||35||
