Mahābhārata 5.171 (Udyogaparvan)

1bhīṣma uvāca|
pañcālarājasya suto rājan parapurañjayaḥ
|
śikhaṇḍī rathamukhyo me mataḥ pārthasya bhārata ||1||

2eṣa yotsyati saṃgrāme nāśayan pūrvasaṃsthitam
|
paraṃ yaśo viprathayaṃstava senāsu bhārata ||2||

3etasya bahulāḥ senāḥ pañcālāśca prabhadrakāḥ
|
tenāsau rathavaṃśena mahat karma kariṣyati ||3||

4dhṛṣṭadyumnaśca senānīḥ sarvasenāsu bhārata
|
mato me'tiratho rājan droṇaśiṣyo mahārathaḥ ||4||

5eṣa yotsyati saṃgrāme sūdayan vai parān raṇe
|
bhagavāniva saṃkruddhaḥ pinākī yugasaṃkṣaye ||5||

6etasya tadrathānīkaṃ kathayanti raṇapriyāḥ
|
bahutvāt sāgaraprakhyaṃ devānāmiva saṃyuge ||6||

7kṣatradharmā tu rājendra mato me'rdharatho nṛpa
|
dhṛṣṭadyumnasya tanayo bālyānnātikṛtaśramaḥ ||7||

8śiśupālasuto vīraścedigajo mahārathaḥ
|
dhṛṣṭaketurmaheṣvāsaḥ sambandhī pāṇḍavasya ha ||8||

9eṣa cedipatiḥ śūraḥ saha putreṇa bhārata
|
mahārathānāṃ sukaraṃ mahat karma kariṣyati ||9||

10kṣatradharmarato mahyaṃ mataḥ parapurañjayaḥ
|
kṣatradevastu rājendra pāṇḍaveṣu rathottamaḥ ||10||

11jayantaścāmitaujāśca satyajicca mahārathaḥ
|
mahārathā mahātmānaḥ sarve pāñcālasattamāḥ ||11||

12yotsyante samare tāta saṃrabdhā iva kuñjarāḥ
|
ajo bhojaśca vikrāntau pāṇḍavārthe mahārathau ||12||

13yotsyete balinau śūrau paraṃ śaktyā kṣayiṣyataḥ
|
śīghrāstrāścitrayoddhāraḥ kṛtino dṛḍhavikramāḥ ||13||

14kekayāḥ pañca rājendra bhrātaro dṛḍhavikramāḥ
|
sarve caiva rathodārāḥ sarve lohitakadhvajāḥ ||14||

15kāśikaḥ sukumāraśca nīlo yaścāparo nṛpa
|
sūryadattaśca śaṅkhaśca madirāśvaśca nāmataḥ ||15||

16sarva eva rathodārāḥ sarve cāhavalakṣaṇāḥ
|
sarvāstraviduṣaḥ sarve mahātmāno matā mama ||16||

17vārdhakṣemirmahārāja mato mama mahārathaḥ
|
citrāyudhaśca nṛpatirmato me rathasattamaḥ ||17||

18sa hi saṃgrāmaśomī ca bhaktaścāpi kirīṭinaḥ
|
cekitānaḥ satyadhṛtiḥ pāṇḍavānāṃ mahārathau
|
dvāvimau puruṣavyāghrau rathodārau matau mama ||18||

19vyāghradattaśca rājendra candrasenaśca bhārata
|
matau mama rathodārau pāṇḍavānāṃ na saṃśayaḥ ||19||

20senābinduśca rājendra krodhahantā ca nāmataḥ
|
yaḥ samo vāsudevena bhīmasenena vā vibho ||20||

21sa yotsyati hi vikramya samare tava sainikaiḥ
|
māṃ ca droṇaṃ kṛpaṃ caiva yathā saṃmanyate bhavān ||21||

22tathā sa samaraślāghī mantavyo rathasattamaḥ
|
kāśyaḥ paramaśīghrāstraḥ ślāghanīyo narottamaḥ ||22||

23ratha ekaguṇo mahyaṃ jñeyaḥ parapurañjayaḥ
|
ayaṃ ca yudhi vikrānto mantavyo'ṣṭaguṇo rathaḥ ||23||

24satyajit samaraślāghī drupadasyātmajo yuvā
|
gataḥ so'tirathatvaṃ hi dhṛṣṭadyumnena saṃmitaḥ ||24||

25pāṇḍavānāṃ yaśaskāmaḥ paraṃ karma kariṣyati
|
anuraktaśca śūraśca ratho'yamaparo mahān ||25||

26pāṇḍyarājo mahāvīryaḥ pāṇḍavānāṃ dhuraṃdharaḥ
|
dṛḍhadhanvā maheṣvāsaḥ pāṇḍavānāṃ mahārathaḥ ||26||

27śreṇimān kauravaśreṣṭha vasudānaśca pārthivaḥ
|
ubhāvetāvatirathau matau parapurañjayau ||27||
