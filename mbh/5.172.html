Mahābhārata 5.172 (Udyogaparvan)

1bhīṣma uvāca|
rocamāno mahārāja pāṇḍavānāṃ mahārathaḥ
|
yotsyate'maravatsaṃkhye parasainyeṣu bhārata ||1||

2purujit kuntibhojaśca maheṣvāso mahābalaḥ
|
mātulo bhīmasenasya sa ca me'tiratho mataḥ ||2||

3eṣa vīro maheṣvāsaḥ kṛtī ca nipuṇaśca ha
|
citrayodhī ca śaktaśca mato me rathapuṅgavaḥ ||3||

4sa yotsyati hi vikramya maghavāniva dānavaiḥ
|
yodhā ye cāsya vikhyātāḥ sarve yuddhaviśāradāḥ||4||

5bhāgineyakṛte vīraḥ sa kariṣyati saṅgare
|
sumahat karma pāṇḍūnāṃ sthitaḥ priyāhate rataḥ ||5||

6bhaimasenirmahārāja haiḍimbo rākṣaseśvaraḥ
|
mato me bahumāyāvī rathayūthapayūthapaḥ ||6||

7yotsyate samare tāta māyāvī samarapriyaḥ
|
ye cāsya rākṣasā vīrāḥ sacivā vaśavartinaḥ ||7||

8ete cānye ca bahavo nānājanapadeśvarāḥ
|
sametāḥ pāṇḍavasyārthe vāsudevapurogamāḥ ||8||

9ete prādhānyato rājan pāṇḍavasya mahātmanaḥ
|
rathāścātirathāścaiva ye cānye'rdharathā nṛpa ||9||

10neṣyanti samare senāṃ bhīmāṃ yodhiṣṭhirīṃ nṛpa
|
mahendreṇeva vīreṇa pālyamānāṃ kirīṭinā ||10||

11tairahaṃ samare vīra māyāvidbhijayaiṣibhiḥ
|
yotsyāmi jayamākāṅkṣannathavā nidhanaṃ raṇe ||11||

12vāsudevaṃ ca pārthaṃ ca cakragāṇḍīvadhāriṇau
|
sandhyāgatāvivārkendū sameṣyete rathottamau ||12||

13ye caiva te rathodārāḥ pāṇḍuputrasya sainikāḥ
|
sahasainyānahaṃ tāṃśca pratīyāṃ raṇamūrdhani ||13||

14ete rathāścātirathāśca tubhyaṃ yathāpradhānaṃ nṛpa kīrtitā mayā
|
tathā'pare ye'rdharathāśca kecittathaiva teṣāmapi kauravendra ||14||

15arjunaṃ vāsudevaṃ ca ye cānye tatra pārthivāḥ
|
sarvāṃstān vārayiṣyāmi yāvaddrakṣyāmi bhārata ||15||

16pāñcālyaṃ tu mahābāho nāhaṃ hanyāṃ śikhaṇḍinam
|
udyateṣumatho dṛṣṭvā pratiyudhyantamāhave ||16||

17lokastaṃ veda yadahaṃ pituḥ priyacikīrṣayā
|
prāptaṃ rājyaṃ parityajya brahmacaryavrate sthitaḥ ||17||

18citrāṅgadaṃ kauravāṇāmādhipatye'bhyaṣecayam
|
vicitravīryaṃ ca śiśuṃ yauvarājye'bhyaṣecayam ||18||

19devavratatvaṃ vijñāpya pṛthivīṃ sarvarājasu
|
naiva hanyāṃ striyaṃ jātu na strīpūrvaṃ kadācana ||19||

20sa hi strīpūrvako rājan śikhaṇḍī yadi te śrutaḥ
|
kanyā bhūtvā pumān jāto na yotsye tena bhārata ||20||

21sarvāṃstvanyān haniṣyāmi pārthivān bharatarṣabha
|
yān sameṣyāmi samare na tu kuntīsutānnṛpa ||21||
