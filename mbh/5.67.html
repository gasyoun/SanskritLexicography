Mahābhārata 5.67 (Udyogaparvan)

1vaiśampāyana uvāca|
duryodhane dhārtarāṣṭre tadvaco nābhinandati
|
tūṣṇīṃbhūteṣu sarveṣu samuttasthurnararṣabhāḥ ||1||

2utthiteṣu mahārāja pṛthivyāṃ sarvarājasu
|
rahite sañjayaṃ rājā paripraṣṭuṃ pracakrame ||2||

3āśaṃsamāno vijayaṃ teṣāṃ putravaśānugaḥ
|
ātmanaśca pareṣāṃ ca pāṇḍavānāṃ ca niścayam||3||

4dhṛtarāṣṭra uvāca|
gāvalgaṇe brūhi naḥ sāraphalgu svasenāyāṃ yāvadihāsti kiñcit
|
tvaṃ pāṇḍavānāṃ nipuṇaṃ vettha sarvaṃ kimeṣāṃ jyāyaḥ kimu teṣāṃ kanīyaḥ||4||

5tvametayoḥ sāravit sarvadarśī dharmārthayornipuṇo niścayajñaḥ
|
sa me pṛṣṭaḥ sañjaya brūhi sarvaṃ yudhyamānāḥ katare'sminna santi ||5||

6sañjaya uvāca|
na tvāṃ brūyāṃ rahite jātu kiñcidasūyā hi tvāṃ praviśeta rājan
|
ānayasva pitaraṃ mahāvrataṃ gāndhārīṃ ca mahirṣāmājamīḍha ||6||

7tau te'sūyāṃ vinayetāṃ narendra dharmajñau tau nipuṇau niścayajñau
|
tayostu tvāṃ sannidhau tadvadeyaṃ kṛtsnaṃ mataṃ keśavapārthayoryat ||7||

8vaiśampāyana uvāca|
ityuktena ca gāndhārī vyāsaścātrājagāma ha
|
ānītau vidureṇeha sabhāṃ śīghraṃ praveśitau ||8||

9tatastanmatamājñāya sañjayasyātmajasya ca
|
abhyupetya mahāprājñaḥ kṛṣṇadvaipāyano'bravīt ||9||

10vyāsa uvāca|
saṃpṛcchate dhṛtarāṣṭrāya sañjaya ācakṣva sarvaṃ yāvadeṣo'nuyuṅkte
|
sarvaṃ yāvadvettha tasmin yathāvadyāthātathyaṃ vāsudeve'rjune ca ||10||
