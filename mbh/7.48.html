Mahābhārata 7.48 (Droṇaparvan)

1sañjaya uvāca|
sa karṇaṃ karṇīnā karṇe punarvivyādha phālguniḥ
|
śaraiḥ pañcāśatā cainamavidhyat kopayan bhṛśam||1||

2prativivyādha rādheyastāvadbhiratha taṃ punaḥ
|
śarairācitasarvāṅgo bahuśobhata bhārata||2||

3karṇaṃ cāpyakarotkruddho rudhirotpīḍavāhinam
|
karṇo'pi vibabhau śūraḥ śaraiśchinno'sṛgāplutaḥ||3||

4tāvubhau śaracitrāṅgau rudhireṇa samukṣitau
|
babhūvaturmahātmānau puṣpitāviva kiṃśukau||4||

5atha karṇasya sacivān ṣaṭ śūrāṃścitrayodhinaḥ
|
sāśvasūtadhvajarathān saubhadro nijaghāna ha||5||

6tathetarān maheṣvāsān daśabhirdaśabhiḥ śaraiḥ
|
pratyavidhyadasaṃbhrāntastada‌dbhutamivābhavat||6||

7māgadhasya tathā putraṃ hatvā ṣaḍbhirajihmagaiḥ
|
sāśvaṃ sasūtaṃ taruṇamaśvaketumapātayat||7||

8mārtikāvatakaṃ bhojaṃ tataḥ kuñjaraketanam
|
kṣurapreṇa samunmathya nanāda visṛjan śarān||8||

9tasya dauḥśāsanirviddhvā caturbhiścaturo hayān
|
sūtamekena vidhyādha daśabhiścārjunātmajam||9||

10tato dauḥśāsaniṃ kārṣṇirvidhvā saptabhirāśugaiḥ
|
saṃrambhādraktanayano vākyamuccairathābravīt||10||

11pitā tavāhavaṃ tyaktvā gataḥ kāpuruṣo yathā
|
diṣṭyā tvamapi jānīṣe yoddhuṃ na tvadya mokṣyase||11||

12etāvaduktvā vacanaṃ karmāraparimārjitam
|
nārācaṃ visasarjāsmai taṃ drauṇistribhirācchinat||12||

13tasyā‌rjunirdhvajaṃ chittvā śalyaṃ tribhiratāḍayat
|
taṃ śalyo navabhirbāṇairgārdhrapatrairatāḍayat||13||

14hṛdyasaṃbhrāntavadrājaṃstada‌dbhutamivābhavat
|
tasyārjunirdhvajaṃ chittvā hatvobhau pārṣṇisārathī||14||

15taṃ vivyādhāyasaiḥ ṣaḍbhiḥ sopākrāmadrathāntaram
|
śatruñjayaṃ candraketuṃ meghavegaṃ suvarcasam||15||

16sūryabhāsaṃ ca pañcaitān hatvā vivyādha saubalam
|
taṃ saubalastribhirviddhvā duryodhanamathābravīt||16||

17sarva enaṃ vimathnīmaḥ puraikaikaṃ hinasti naḥ
|
athābravīt punardroṇaṃ karṇo vaikartano raṇe||17||

18purā sarvān pramathnāti brahyasya vadhamāśu naḥ
|
tato droṇo maheṣvāsaḥ sarvāṃstān pratyabhāṣata||18||

19asti vā'syāntaraṃ kiñcit kumārasyātha paśyata
|
aṇvapyasyāntaraṃ hyadya carataḥ sarvatodiśam||19||

20śīghratāṃ narasiṃhasya pāṇḍaveyasya paśyata
|
dhanurmaṇḍalamevāsya rathamārgeṣu dṛśyate||20||

21saṃdadhānasya viśikhān śīghraṃ caiva vimuñcataḥ
|
ārujannapi me prāṇān mohayannapi sāyakaiḥ||21||

22praharṣayati māṃ bhūyaḥ saubhadraḥ paravīrahā
|
ati māṃ nandayatyeṣa saubhadro vicaran raṇe||22||

23antaraṃ yasya saṃrabdhā na paśyanti mahārathāḥ
|
asyato laghuhastasya diśaḥ sarvā maheṣubhiḥ||23||

24na viśeṣaṃ prapaśyāmi raṇe gāṇḍīvadhanvanaḥ
|
atha karṇaḥ punardroṇamāhārjuniśarāhataḥ||24||

25sthātavyamiti tiṣṭhāmi pīḍyamāno'bhimanyunā
|
tejasvinaḥ kumārasya śarāḥ paramadāruṇāḥ||25||

26kṣiṇvanti hṛdayaṃ me'dya ghorāḥ pāvakatejasaḥ
|
tamācāryo'bravīt karṇaṃ śanakaiḥ prahasanniva||26||

27abhedyamasya kavacaṃ yuvā cāśuparākramaḥ
|
upadiṣṭā mayā cāsya pituḥ kavacadhāraṇā||27||

28tāmeṣa nikhilāṃ vetti dhruvaṃ parapurañjayaḥ
|
śakyaṃ tvasya dhanuśchettuṃ jyāṃ ca bāṇaiḥ samāhitaiḥ||28||

29abhīṣūṃśca hayāṃścaiva tathobhau pārṣṇisārathī
|
etatkuru maheṣvāsa rādheya yadi śakyate||29||

30athainaṃ vimukhīkṛtya paścāt praharaṇaṃ kuru
|
sadhanuṣko na śakyo'yamapi jetuṃ surāsuraiḥ||30||

31virathaṃ vidhanuṣkaṃ ca kuruṣvainaṃ yadīcchasi
|
tadācāryavacaḥ śrutvā karṇo vaikartanastvaran||31||

32asyato laghuhastasya pṛṣatkairdhanurācchinat
|
aśvānasyāvadhīdbhojo gautamaḥ pārṣṇisārathī||32||

33śeṣāstu cchinnadhanvānaṃ śaravarṣairavākiran
|
tvaramāṇāstvarākāle virathaṃ ṣaṇmahārathāḥ||33||

34śaravarṣairakaruṇā bālamekamavākiran
|
sacchinnadhanvā virathaḥ svadharmamanupālayan||34||

35khaḍgacarmadharaḥ śrīmānutpapāta vihāyasā
|
mārgaiḥ sakauśikādyaiśca lāghavena balena ca||35||

36ārjunirvyacaradvyomni bhṛśaṃ vai pakṣirāḍiva
|
mayyeva nipatatyeṣa sāsirityūrdhvadṛṣṭayaḥ||36||

37vivyadhustaṃ maheṣvāsaṃ samare chidradarśinaḥ
|
tasya droṇo'cchinanmuṣṭau khaḍgaṃ maṇimayatsarum||37||

38kṣurapreṇa mahātejāstvaramāṇaḥ sapatnajit
|
rādheyo niśitairbāṇairvyadhamaccarma cottamam||38||

39vyasicarmeṣupūrṇāṅgaḥ so'ntarikṣāt punaḥ kṣitim
|
āsthitaścakramudyamya droṇaṃ kruddho'bhyadhāvata||39||

40sa cakrareṇūjjvalaśobhitāṅgobabhāvatīvojjvalacakrapāṇiḥ
|
raṇe'bhimanyuḥ kṣaṇamāsa raudraḥ sa vāsudevānukṛtiṃ prakurvan||40||

41srutarudhirakṛtaikarāgavastro bhrukuṭipuṭākulito'tisiṃhanādaḥ
|
prabhuramitabalo raṇe'bhimanyurnṛpavaramadhyagato bhṛśaṃ vyarājat||41||
