Mahābhārata 5.155 (Udyogaparvan)

1vaiśampāyana uvāca|
vyuṣṭāyāṃ vai rajanyāṃ hi rājā duryodhanastataḥ
|
vyabhajattānyanīkāni daśa caikaṃ ca bhārata ||1||

2nara-hasti-rathāśvānāṃ sāraṃ madhyaṃ ca phalgu ca
|
sarveṣveteṣvanīkeṣu sandideśa narādhipaḥ ||2||

3sānukarṣāḥ satūṇīrāḥ savarūthāḥ satomarāḥ
|
sopāsaṅgāḥ saśaktīkāḥ saniṣaṅgāḥ saharṣṭayaḥ ||3||

4sadhvajāḥ sapatākāśca saśarāsanatomarāḥ
|
rajjubhiśca vicitrābhiḥ sapāśāḥ saparicchadāḥ ||4||

5sakacagraha‌vikṣepāḥ satailaguḍavālukāḥ
|
sāśīviṣaghaṭāḥ sarve sasarjarasapāṃsavaḥ ||5||

6saghaṇṭaphalakāḥ sarve sāyoguḍajalopalāḥ
|
saśālabhindipālāśca samadhūcchiṣṭamudgarāḥ ||6||

7sakāṇḍadaṇḍakāḥ sarve sasīraviṣatomarāḥ
|
saśūrpapiṭakāḥ sarve sadātrāṅ‌kuśatomarāḥ ||7||

8sakīlakavacāḥ sarve vāsīvṛkṣādanānvitāḥ
|
vyāghracarmaparīvārā dvīpicarmāvṛtāśca te ||8||

9saharṣṭayaḥ saśṛṅgāśca saprāsavividhāyudhāḥ
|
sakuṭhārāḥ sakuddālāḥ satailakṣaumasarpiṣaḥ ||9||

10rukmajālapraticchannā nānāmaṇivibhūṣitāḥ
|
citrānīkāḥ suvapuṣo jvalitā iva pāvakāḥ ||10||

11tathā kavacinaḥ śūrāḥ śastreṣu kṛtaniścayāḥ
|
kulīnā hayayonijñāḥ sārathye viniveśitāḥ ||11||

12baddhāriṣṭā baddhakakṣā baddhadhvajapatākinaḥ
|
baddhābharaṇaniryūhā baddhacarmāsipaṭṭiśāḥ ||12||

13caturyujo rathāḥ sarve sarve cottamavājinaḥ
|
saprāsaṛṣṭikāḥ sarve sarve śataśarāsanāḥ ||13||

14dhuryayorhayayorekastathā'nyau pārṣṇisārathī
|
tau cāpi rathināṃ śreṣṭhau rathī ca hayavittathā ||14||

15nagarāṇīva guptāni durādharṣāṇi śatrubhiḥ
|
āsan rathasahastrāṇi hemamālīni sarvaśaḥ ||15||

16yathā rathāstathā nāgā baddhakakṣāḥ svalaṅkṛtāḥ
|
babhūvuḥ saptapuruṣā ratnavanta ivādrayaḥ ||16||

17dvāvaṅkuśadharau tatra dvāvuttamadhanurdharau
|
dvau varāsidharau rājannekaḥ śaktipinākadhṛk ||17||

18gajairmattaiḥ samākīrṇaṃ sarvamāyudhakośakaiḥ
|
tadbabhūva balaṃ rājan kauravyasya mahātmanaḥ ||18||

19āmuktakavacairyuktaiḥ sapatākaiḥ svalaṅkṛtaiḥ
|
sādibhiścopapannāstu tathā cāyutaśo hayāḥ ||19||

20asaṅgrāhāḥ susampannā hemabhāṇḍaparicchadāḥ
|
anekaśatasāhasrāḥ sarve sādivaśe sthitāḥ ||20||

21nānārūpavikārāśca nānākavacaśastriṇaḥ
|
padātino narāstatra babhūvurhemamālinaḥ ||21||

22rathasyāsan daśa gajā gajasya daśa vājinaḥ
|
narā daśa hayasyāsan pādarakṣāḥ samantataḥ ||22||

23rathasya nāgāḥ pañcāśannāgasyāsan śataṃ hayāḥ
|
hayasya puruṣāḥ sapta bhinnasandhānakāriṇaḥ ||23||

24senā pañcaśataṃ nāgā rathāstāvanta eva ca
|
daśa senā ca pṛtanā pṛtanā daśavāhinī ||24||

25senā ca vāhinī caiva pṛtanā dhvajinī camūḥ
|
akṣauhiṇīti paryāyairniruktā ca varūthinī ||25||

26evaṃ vyūḍhānyanīkāni kauraveyeṇa dhīmatā
|
akṣauhiṇyo daśaikā ca saṅkhyātāḥ sapta caiva ha ||26||

27akṣauhiṇyastu saptaiva pāṇḍavānāmabhūdbalam
|
akṣauhiṇyo daśaikā ca kauravāṇāmabhūdbalam ||27||

28narāṇāṃ pañcapañcāśadeṣā pattirvidhīyate
|
senāmukhaṃ ca tisrastā gulma ityabhiśabditam ||28||

29trayo gulmā gaṇastvāsīdgaṇāstvayutaśo'bhavan
|
duryodhanasya senāsu yotsyamānāḥ prahāriṇaḥ ||29||

30tatra duryodhano rājā śūrān buddhimato narān
|
prasamīkṣya mahābāhuścakre senāpatīṃstadā ||30||

31pṛthagakṣauhiṇīnāṃ ca praṇetṝnnarasattamān
|
vidhivat pūrvamānīya pārthivānabhyabhāṣata ||31||

32kṛpaṃ droṇaṃ ca śalyaṃ ca saindhavaṃ ca jayadratham
|
sudakṣiṇaṃ ca kāmbojaṃ kṛtavarmāṇameva ca ||32||

33droṇaputraṃ ca karṇaṃ ca bhūriśravasameva ca
|
śakuniṃ saubalaṃ caiva bāhlīkaṃ ca mahābalam||33||

34divase divase teṣāṃ prativelaṃ ca bhārata
|
cakre sa vividhāḥ pūjāḥ pratyakṣaṃ ca punaḥ punaḥ ||34||

35tathā viniyatāḥ sarve ye ca teṣāṃ padānugāḥ
|
babhūvuḥ sainikā rājñāṃ priyaṃ rājñaścikīrṣavaḥ ||35||
