Mahābhārata 13.126 (Anuśāsanaparvan)

1bhīṣma uvāca|
kena te ca bhavetprītiḥ kathaṃ tuṣṭiṃ tu gacchasi
|
iti pṛṣṭaḥ surendreṇa provāca harirīśvaraḥ||1||

2viṣṇuruvāca|
brāhmaṇānāṃ parīvādo mama vidveṣaṇaṃ mahat
|
brāhmaṇaiḥ pūjitairnityaṃ pūjito'haṃ na saṃśayaḥ ||2||

3nityābhivādyā viprendrā bhuktvā pādau tathātmanaḥ
|
teṣāṃ tuṣyāmi martyānāṃ yaścakre ca baliṃ haret||3||

4vāmanaṃ brāhmaṇaṃ dṛṣṭvā varāhaṃ ca jalotthitam
|
uddhṛtāṃ dharaṇīṃ caiva mūrdhnā dhārayate tu yaḥ||4||

5na teṣāmaśubhaṃ kiñcitkalmaṣaṃ copapadyate
|
aśvatthaṃ rocanāṃ gāṃ ca pūjayedyo naraḥ sadā ||5||

6pūjitaṃ ca jagattena sadevāsuramānuṣam
|
tena rūpeṇa teṣāṃ ca pūjāṃ gṛhṇāmi tattvataḥ ||6||

7pūjā mamaiṣā nāstyanyā yāvallokāḥ pratiṣṭhitāḥ
|
anyathā hi vṛthā martyāḥ pūjayantyalpabuddhayaḥ||7||

8nāhaṃ tatpratigṛhṇāmi na sā tuṣṭikarī mama ||8||

9indra uvāca|
cakraṃ pādau varāhaṃ ca brāhmaṇaṃ cāpi vāmanam
|
uddhṛtāṃ dharaṇīṃ caiva kimarthaṃ tvaṃ praśaṃsasi ||9||

10bhavānsṛjati bhūtāni bhavānsaṃharati prajāḥ
|
prakṛtiḥ sarvabhūtānāṃ samartyānāṃ sanātanī ||10||

11bhīṣma uvāca|
saṃprahasya tato viṣṇuridaṃ vacanamabravīt
|
cakreṇa nihatā daityāḥ padbhyāṃ krāntā vasuṃdharā||11||

12vārāhaṃ rūpamāsthāya hiraṇyākṣo nipātitaḥ
|
vāmanaṃ rūpamāsthāya jito rājā mayā baliḥ||12||

13parituṣṭo bhavāmyevaṃ mānuṣāṇāṃ mahātmanām
|
tanmāṃ ye pūjayiṣyanti nāsti teṣāṃ parābhavaḥ||13||

14api vā brāhmaṇaṃ dṛṣṭvā brahmacāriṇamāgatam
|
brāhmaṇāgryāhutiṃ datvā amṛtaṃ tasya bhojanam||14||

15aindrīṃ sandhyāmupāsitvā ādityābhimukhaḥ sthitaḥ
|
sarvatīrtheṣu sa snāto mucyate sarvakilbiṣaiḥ ||15||

16etadvaḥ kathitaṃ guhyamakhilena tapodhanāḥ
|
saṃśayaṃ pṛcchamānānāṃ kiṃ bhūyaḥ kathayāmyaham||16||

17baladeva uvāca|
śrūyatāṃ paramaṃ guhyaṃ mānuṣāṇāṃ sukhāvaham
|
ajānanto yadabudhāḥ kliśyante bhūtapīḍitāḥ||17||

18kalya utthāya yo martyaḥ spṛśedgāṃ vai ghṛtaṃ dadhi
|
sarṣapaṃ ca priyaṃguṃ ca kalmaṣāt pratimucyate ||18||

19bhūtāni caiva sarvāṇi agrataḥ pṛṣṭhato'pi vā
|
ucchiṣṭaṃ vā'pi cchidreṣu varjayanti tapodhanāḥ ||19||

20devā ūcuḥ |
pragṛhyauduṃbaraṃ pātraṃ toyapūrṇa udaṅmukhaḥ
|
upavāsaṃ tu gṛhṇīyādyadvā saṃkalpayedvratam ||20||

21devatāstasya tuṣyanti kāmikaṃ cāpi sidhyati
|
anyathā hi vṛthā martyāḥ kurvate svalpabuddhayaḥ||21||

22upavāse balau cāpi tāmrapātraṃ viśiṣyate
|
balirbhikṣā tathā'rghyaṃ ca pitṝṇāṃ ca tilodakam||22||

23tāmrapātreṇa dātavyamanyathā'lpaphalaṃ bhavet
|
guhyametatsamuddiṣṭaṃ yathā tuṣyanti devatāḥ ||23||

24dharma uvāca|
rājapauruṣike vipre ghāṇṭike paricārike
|
gorakṣake vāṇijake tathā kārukuśīlave||24||

25mitradruhyanadhīyāne yaśca syādvṛṣalīpatiḥ
|
eteṣu daivaṃ pitryaṃ vā na deyaṃ syātkathañcana ||25||

26piṇḍadāstasya hīyante na ca prīṇāti vai pitṝn
|
atithiryasya bhagnāśo gṛhātpratinivartate ||26||

27pitarastasya devāśca agnayaśca tathaiva hi
|
nirāśāḥ pratigacchanti atitherapratigrahāt ||27||

28strīghnaurgoghnaiḥ kṛtaghnaiśca brahmaghnairgurutalpagaiḥ
|
tulyadoṣo bhavatyebhiryasyātithiranarcitaḥ||28||

29agniruvāca|
pāda‌mudyamya yo martyaḥ spṛśedgāśca sudurmatiḥ
|
brāhmaṇaṃ vā mahābhāgaṃ dīpyamānaṃ tathā'nalam||29||

30tasya doṣānpravakṣyāmi tacchṛṇudhvaṃ samāhitāḥ
|
divaṃ spṛśatyaśabdo'sya trasyanti pitaraśca vai||30||

31vaimanasyaṃ ca devānāṃ kṛtaṃ bhavati puṣkalam
|
pāvakaśca mahātejā havyaṃ na pratigṛhṇati ||31||

32ājanmanāṃ śataṃ caiva narake pacyate tu saḥ
|
niṣkṛtiṃ ca na tasyāpi anumanyanti karhicit||32||

33tasmādgāvo na pādena spraṣṭavyā vai kadācana
|
brāhmaṇaśca mahātejā dīpyamānastathā'nalaḥ ||33||

34śraddadhānena martyena ātmano hitamicchatā
|
ete doṣā mayā proktāstriṣu yaḥ pādamutsṛjet||34||

35viśvāmitra uvāca|
śrūyatāṃ paramaṃ guhyaṃ rahasyaṃ dharmasaṃhitam
|
paramānnena yo dadyātpitṛṇāmaupahārikam ||35||

36gajacchāyāyāṃ pūrvasyāṃ kutape dakṣiṇāmukhaḥ
|
yadā bhādrapade māsi bhavate bahule maghā ||36||

37śrūyatāṃ tasya dānasya yādṛśo guṇavistaraḥ
|
kṛtaṃ tena mahacchrāddhaṃ varṣāṇīha trayodaśa||37||

38gāva ūcuḥ |
bahule samaṃge hyakutobhaye ca kṣeme ca saṃkhyeva hi bhūyasī ca
|
yathā purā brahmapure savatsā śatakratorvajradharasya yajñe||38||

39bhūyaśca yā viṣṇupade sthitā yā vibhāvasoścāpi pathe sthitā yā
|
devāśca sarve saha nāradena prakurvate sarvasaheti nāma ||39||

40maṃtreṇaitenābhivandeta yo vai vimucyate pāpakṛtena karmaṇā
|
lokānavāpnoti puraṃdarasya gavāṃ phalaṃ candramaso dyutiṃ ca ||40||

41etaṃ hi mantraṃ tridaśābhijuṣṭaṃ paṭheta yaḥ parvasu goṣṭhamadhye
|
na tasya pāpaṃ na bhayaṃ na śokaḥ sahasranetrasya ca yāti lokam ||41||

42bhīṣma uvāca|
atha sapta mahābhāgā ṛṣayo lokaviśrutāḥ
|
vasiṣṭhapramukhāḥ sarve brahmāṇaṃ padmasaṃbhavam ||42||

43pradakṣiṇamabhikramya sarve prāñjalayaḥ sthitāḥ
|
uvāca vacanaṃ teṣāṃ vasiṣṭho brahmavittamaḥ ||43||

44sarvaprāṇihitaṃ praśnaṃ brahmakṣatre viśeṣataḥ
|
dravyahīnāḥ kathaṃ martyā daridrāḥ sādhuvartinaḥ||44||

45prāpnuvantīha yajñasya phalaṃ kena ca karmaṇā
|
etacchrutvā vacasteṣāṃ brahmā vacanamabravīt||45||

46brahmovāca|
aho praśno mahābhāgā gūḍhārthaḥ paramaḥ śubhaḥ
|
sūkṣmaḥ śreyāṃśca martyānāṃ bhavadbhiḥ samudāhṛtaḥ ||46||

47śrūyatāṃ sarvamākhyāsye nikhilena tapodhanāḥ
|
yathā yajñaphalaṃ martyo labhate nātra saṃśayaḥ ||47||

48pauṣamāsasya śukle vai yadā yujyeta rohiṇī
|
tena nakṣatrayogena ākāśaśayano bhavet ||48||

49ekavastraḥ śuciḥ snātaḥ śraddadhānaḥ samāhitaḥ
|
somasya raśmayaḥ pītvā mahāyajñaphalaṃ labhet||49||

50etadvaḥ paramaṃ guhyaṃ kathitaṃ dvijasattamāḥ
|
yanmāṃ bhavantaḥ pṛcchanti sūkṣmatattvārthadarśinaḥ||50||
