Mahābhārata 5.136 (Udyogaparvan)

1mātovāca
|
naiva rājñā daraḥ kāryo jātu kasyāñcidāpadi
|
atha cedapi dīrṇaḥ syānnaiva varteta dīrṇavat ||1||

2dīrṇaṃ hi dṛṣṭvā rājānaṃ sarvamevānudīryate
|
rāṣṭraṃ balamamātyāśca pṛthakkurvanti te matīḥ ||2||

3śatrūneke prapadyante prajahatyapare punaḥ
|
anye tu prājihīrṣanti ye purastādvimānitāḥ ||3||

4ya evātyantasuhṛdasta enaṃ paryupāsate
|
aśaktayaḥ svastikāmā baddhavatsā ilā iva ||4||

5śocantamanuśocanti patitāniva bāndhavān
|
api te pūjitāḥ pūrvamapi te suhṛdo matāḥ ||5||

6ye rāṣṭramabhimanyante rājño vyasanamīyuṣaḥ
|
mā dīdarastvaṃ suhṛdo mā tvāṃ dīrṇaṃ prahāsiṣuḥ ||6||

7prabhāvaṃ pauruṣaṃ buddhiṃ jijñāsantyā mayā tava
|
vidadhatyā samāśvāsamuktaṃ tejovivṛddhaye ||7||

8yadetatsaṃvijānāsi yadi samyagbravīmyaham
|
kṛtvā'saumyamivātmānaṃ jayāyottiṣṭha sañjaya ||8||

9asti naḥ kośanicayo mahān hyaviditastava
|
tamahaṃ veda nānyastamupasaṃpādayāmi te ||9||

10santi naikatamā bhūyaḥ suhṛdastava sañjaya
|
sukhaduḥkhasahā vīra saṅgrāmādanivartinaḥ ||10||

11tādṛśā hi sahāyā vai puruṣasya bubhūṣataḥ
|
iṣṭaṃ jihīrṣataḥ kiñcitsacivāḥ śatrukarśana ||11||

12yasyāstvīdṛśakaṃ vākyaṃ śrutvā'pi svalpacetasaḥ
|
tamastvapāgamattasya sucitrārthapadākṣaram ||12||

13putra uvāca|
udake bhūriyaṃ dhāryā martavyaṃ pravaṇe mayā
|
yasya me bhavatī netrī bhaviṣyadbhūtidarśinī ||13||

14ahaṃ hi vacanaṃ tvattaḥ śuśrūṣuraparāparam
|
kiñcit kiñcit prativadaṃstūṣṇīmāsaṃ muhurmuhuḥ ||14||

15atṛpyannamṛtasyeva kṛcchrāllabdhasya bāndhavāt
|
udyacchāmyeṣa śatrūṇāṃ niyamārthaṃ jayāya ca ||15||

16kuntyuvāca
|
sadaśva iva sa kṣiptaḥ praṇunno vākyasāyakaiḥ
|
taccakāra tathā sarvaṃ yathāvadanuśāsanam ||16||

17idamuddharṣaṇaṃ bhīmaṃ tejovardhanamuttamam
|
rājānaṃ śrāvayenmantrī sīdantaṃ śatrupīḍitam ||17||

18jayo nāmetihāso'yaṃ śrotavyo vijigīṣuṇā
|
mahīṃ vijayate kṣipraṃ śrutvā śatrūṃśca mardati ||18||

19idaṃ puṃsavanaṃ caiva vīrājananameva ca
|
abhīkṣṇaṃ garbhiṇī śrutvā dhruvaṃ vīraṃ prajāyate ||19||

20vidyāśūraṃ tapaḥśūraṃ dānaśūraṃ tapasvinam
|
brāhmyā śriyā dīpyamānaṃ sādhuvāde ca saṃmatam ||20||

21arciṣmantaṃ balopetaṃ mahābhāgaṃ mahāratham
|
dhṛtimantamanādhṛṣyaṃ jetāramaparājitam ||21||

22niyantāramasādhūnāṃ goptāraṃ dharmacāriṇām
|
īdṛśaṃ kṣatriyā sūte vīraṃ satyaparākramam ||22||
