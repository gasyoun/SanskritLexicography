Mahābhārata 7.31 (Droṇaparvan)

1dhṛtarāṣṭra uvāca|
teṣvanīkeṣu bhagneṣu pāṇḍuputreṇa sañjaya
|
calitānāṃ drutānāṃ ca kathamāsīnmano hi vaḥ||1||

2anīkānāṃ prabhagnānāmavasthānamapaśyatām
|
duṣkaraṃ pratisandhānaṃ tanmamācakṣva sañjaya||2||

3sañjaya uvāca|
tathāpi tava putrasya priyakāmā viśāṃpate
|
yaśaḥ pravīrā lokeṣu rakṣanto droṇamanvayuḥ||3||

4samudyateṣu cāstreṣu saṃprāpte ca sudhiṣṭhire
|
akurvannāryakarmāṇi bhairave satyabhītavat||4||

5antaraṃ bhīmasenasya prāpatannamitaujasaḥ
|
sātyakeścaiva vīrasya dhṛṣṭadyumnasya vā vibho||5||

6droṇaṃ droṇamiti krūrāḥ pañcālāḥ samacodayan
|
mā droṇamiti putrāste kurūn sarvānacodayan||6||

7droṇaṃ droṇamiti hyeke mā droṇamiti cāpare
|
kurūṇāṃ pāṇḍavānāṃ ca droṇadyūtamavartata||7||

8yaṃ yaṃ pramathate droṇaḥ pañcālānāṃ rathavrajam
|
tatra tatra tu pāñcālyo dhṛṣṭadyumno'bhyavartata||8||

9tathā bhāgaviparyāsaiḥ saṃgrāme bhairave sati
|
vīrāḥ samāsadan vīrān kurvanto bhairavaṃ ravam||9||

10akampanīyāḥ śatrūṇāṃ babhūvustatra pāṇḍavāḥ
|
akampayannanīkāni smarantaḥ kleśamātmanaḥ||10||

11te tvamarṣavaśaṃ prāptā hrīmantaḥ sattvacoditāḥ
|
tyaktvā prāṇānnyavartanta ghnanto droṇaṃ mahāhave||11||

12ayasāmiva saṃpātaḥ śilānāmiva cābhavat
|
dīvyatāṃ tumule yuddhe prāṇairamitatejasām||12||

13na tu smaranti saṃgrāmamapi vṛddhāstathāvidham
|
dṛṣṭapūrvaṃ mahārāja śrutapūrvamathāpi vā||13||

14prākampateva pṛthivī tasmin vīrāvasādane
|
nivartatā balaughena mahatā bhārapīḍitā||14||

15ghūrṇato'pi balaughasya divaṃ stabdhveva niḥsvanaḥ
|
ajātaśatrostatsainyamāviveśa subhairavaḥ||15||

16samāsādya tu pāṇḍūnāmanīkāni sahasraśaḥ
|
droṇena caratā saṃkhye prabhagnāni śitaiḥ śaraiḥ||16||

17teṣu pramathyamāneṣu droṇenādbhutakarmaṇā
|
paryavārayadāsādya droṇaṃ senāpatiḥ svayam||17||

18tadadbhutamabhūdyuddhaṃ droṇapāñcālayostathā
|
naiva tasyopamā kāciditi me niścitā matiḥ||18||

19tato nīlo'nalaprakhyo dadāha kuruvāhinīm
|
śarasphuliṅgaścāpārcirdahan kakṣamivānalaḥ||19||

20taṃ dahantamanīkāni droṇaputraḥ pratāpavān
|
pūrvābhibhāṣī suślakṣṇaṃ smayamāno'bhyabhāṣata||20||

21nīla kiṃ bahubhirdagdhaistava yodhaiḥ śarārciṣā
|
mayaikena hi yudhyasva kruddhaḥ prahara cāśu mām||21||

22taṃ padmanikarākāraṃ padmapatranibhekṣaṇam
|
vyākośapadmābhamukho nīlo vivyādha sāyakaiḥ||22||

23tenāpi viddhaḥ sahasā drauṇirbhallaiḥ śitaistribhiḥ
|
dhanurdhvajaṃ ca chatraṃ ca dviṣataḥ sa nyakṛntata||23||

24sa plutaḥ syandanāttasmānnīlaścarmavarāsibhṛt
|
drauṇāyaneḥ śiraḥ kāyāddhartumaicchat patatrivat||24||

25tasyonnatāṃsaṃ sunasaṃ śiraḥ kāyāt sakuṇḍalam
|
bhallenāpāharaddrauṇiḥ smayamāna ivānagha||25||

26saṃpūrṇacandrābhamukhaḥ padmapatranibhekṣaṇaḥ
|
prāṃśurutpalapatrābho nihato nyapatadbhuvi||26||

27tataḥ pravivyathe senā pāṇḍavī bhṛśamākulā
|
ācāryaputreṇa hate nīle jvalitatejasi||27||

28acintayaṃśca te sarve pāṇḍavānāṃ mahārathāḥ
|
kathaṃ no vāsavistrāyācchatrubhya iti māriṣa||28||

29dakṣiṇena tu senāyāḥ kurute kadanaṃ balī
|
saṃśaptakāvaśeṣasya nārāyaṇabalasya ca||29||
