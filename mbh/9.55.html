Mahābhārata 9.55 (Śalyaparvan)

1vaiśampāyana uvāca|
evaṃ tadabhavadyuddhaṃ tumulaṃ janamejaya
|
yatra duḥkhānvito rājā dhṛtarāṣṭro'bravīdidam||1||

2dhṛtarāṣṭra uvāca|
rāmaṃ sannihitaṃ dṛṣṭvā gadāyuddha upasthite
|
mama putraḥ kathaṃ bhīmaṃ pratyayudhyata sañjaya||2||

3sañjaya uvāca|
rāmasānnidhyamāsādya putro duryodhanastava
|
yuddhakāmo mahābāhuḥ samahṛṣyata vīryavān||3||

4dṛṣṭvā lāgalinaṃ rājā pratyutthāya ca bhārata
|
prītyā paramayā yuktaḥ samabhyarcya yathāvidhi||4||

5āsanaṃ ca dadau tasmai paryapṛcchadanāmayam
|
tato yudhiṣṭharaṃ rāmo vākyametaduvāca ha||5||

6madhuraṃ dharmasaṃyuktaṃ śūrāṇāṃ hitameva ca
|
mayā śrutaṃ kathayatāmṛṣīṇāṃ rājasattama||6||

7kurukṣetraṃ paraṃ puṇyaṃ pāvanaṃ svargameva ca
|
daivatairṛṣibhirjuṣṭaṃ brāhmaṇaiśca mahātmabhiḥ||7||

8tatra vai yotsyamānā ye dehaṃ tyakṣyanti mānavāḥ
|
teṣāṃ svarge dhruvo vāsaḥ śakreṇa saha māriṣa||8||

9tasmātsamantapañcakamito yāma drutaṃ nṛpa
|
prathitottaravedī sā devaloke prajāpateḥ||9||

10tasminmahāpuṇyatame trailokyasya sanātane
|
saṅgrāme nidhanaṃ prāpya dhruvaṃ svarge bhaviṣyati||10||

11tathetyuktvā mahārāja kuntīputro yudhiṣṭhiraḥ
|
samantapañcakaṃ vīraḥ prāyādabhimukhaḥ prabhuḥ||11||

12tato duryodhano rājā pragṛhya mahatīṃ gadām
|
padbhyāmaparṣī dyutimānagacchatpāṇḍavaiḥ saha||12||

13tathā''yāntaṃ gadāhastaṃ varmaṇā cāpi daṃśitam
|
antarikṣacarā devāḥ sādhu sādhvityapūjayan||13||

14vātikāścāraṇā ye tu dṛṣṭvā te harṣamāgatāḥ
|
sa pāṇḍavaiḥ parivṛtaḥ kururājastavātmajaḥ||14||

15mattasyeva gajendrasya gatimāsthāya so'vrajat
|
tataḥ śaṅkhaninādena bherīṇāṃ ca mahāsvanaiḥ||15||

16siṃhanādaiśca śūrāṇāṃ diśaḥ sarvāḥ prapūritāḥ
|
tataste tu kurukṣetraṃ prāptā naravarottamāḥ||16||

17pratīcyabhimukhaṃ deśaṃ yathoddiṣṭaṃ sutena te
|
dakṣiṇena sarasvatyāḥ svayanaṃ tīrthamuttamam||17||

18tasmindeśe tvaniriṇe te tu yuddhamarocayan
|
tato bhīmo mahākoṭiṃ gadāṃ gṛhyātha varmabhṛt||18||

19bibhradrūpaṃ mahārāja sadṛśaṃ hi garutmataḥ
|
avabaddhaśirastrāṇaḥ saṅkhye kāñcanavarmabhṛt||19||

20rarāja rājanputraste kāñcanaḥ śailarāḍiva
|
varmabhyāṃ saṃyatau vīrau bhīmaduryodhanāvubhau||20||

21saṃyuge ca prakāśete saṃrabdhāviva kuñjarau
|
raṇamaṇḍalamadhyasthau bhrātarau tau nararṣabhau||21||

22aśobhetāṃ mahārāja candrasūryāvivoditau
|
tāvanyonyaṃ nirīkṣetāṃ kruddhāviva mahādvipau||22||

23dahantau locanau rājanparasparavadhaiṣiṇau
|
samprahṛṣṭamanā rājan gadāmādāya kauravaḥ||23||

24sṛkkiṇī saṃlihanrājankrodharaktekṣaṇaḥ śvasan
|
tato duryodhano rājangadāmādāya vīryavān||24||

25bhīmasenamabhiprekṣya gajo gajamivāhvayat
|
adrisāramayīṃ bhīmastathaivādāya vīryavān||25||

26āhvayāmāsa nṛpatiṃ siṃhaṃ siṃho yathā vane
|
tāvudyatagadāpāṇī duryodhanavṛkodarau||26||

27saṃyuge ca prakāśetāṃ girī saśikharāviva
|
tāvubhau samatikruddhāvubhau bhīmaparākramau||27||

28ubhau śiṣyau gadāyuddhe rauhiṇeyasya dhīmataḥ
|
ubhau sadṛśakarmāṇau yamavāsavayoriva||28||

29tathā sadṛśakarmāṇau varuṇasya mahābalau
|
vāsudevasya rāmasya tathā vaiśravaṇasya ca||29||

30sadṛśau tau mahārāja madhukaiṭabhayoryudhi
|
ubhau sadṛśakarmāṇau tathā sundopasundayoḥ||30||

31rāmarāvaṇayoścaiva vālisugrīvayostathā
|
tathaiva kālasya samau mṛtyoścaiva parantapau||31||

32anyonyamabhidhāvantau mattāviva mahādvipau
|
vāsitāsaṅgame dṛptau śaradīva madotkaṭau||32||

33ubhau krodhaviṣaṃ dīptaṃ vamantāvuragāviva
|
anyonyamabhisaṃrabdhau prekṣamāṇāvarindamau||33||

34ubhau bharataśārdūlau vikrameṇa samanvitau
|
siṃhāviva durādharṣau gadāyuddhaviśāradau||34||

35nakhadaṃṣṭrāyudhau vīrau vyāghrāviva durutsahau
|
prajāsaṃharaṇe kṣubdhau samudrāviva dustarau||35||

36lohitāṅgāviva kruddhau pratapantau mahārathau
|
pūrvapaścimajau medhau prekṣamāṇāvarindamau||36||

37garjamānau suviṣamaṃ kṣarantau prādṛṣīva hi
|
raśmiyuktau mahātmānau dīptimantau mahābalau||37||

38dadṛśāte kuruśreṣṭhau kālasūryāvivoditau
|
vyāghrāviva susaṃrabdhau garjantāviva toyadau||38||

39jahṛṣāte mahābāhū siṃhakesariṇāviva
|
jagāviva susaṃrabdhau jvalitāviva pāvakau||39||

40dadṛśāte mahātmānau saśṛṅgāviva parvatau
|
roṣātprasphuramāṇoṣṭhau nirīkṣantau parasparam||40||

41tau sametau mahātmānau gadāhastau narottamau
|
ubhau paramasaṃhṛṣṭāvubhau paramasammatau||41||

42sadaśvāviva heṣantau bṛhantāviva kuñjarau
|
vṛṣabhāviva garjantau duryodhanavṛkodarau||42||

43daityāviva balonmattau rejatustau narottamau
|
tato duryodhano rājannidamāha yudhiṣṭhiram||43||

44bhrātṛbhiḥ sahitaṃ caiva kṛṣṇena ca mahātmanā
|
rāmeṇāmitavīryeṇa vākyaṃ śauṭīryasammatam||44||

45kekayaiḥ sṛñjayairdṛptaṃ pañcālaiśca mahātmabhiḥ
|
idaṃ vyavasitaṃ yuddhaṃ mama bhīmasya cobhayoḥ||45||

46upopaviṣṭāḥ paśyadhvaṃ sahitairnupapuṅgavaiḥ
|
śrutvā duryodhanavacaḥ pratyapadyanta tattathā||46||

47tataḥ samupaviṣṭaṃ tatsumahadrājamaṇḍalam
|
virājamānaṃ dadṛśe divīvādityamaṇḍalam||47||

48teṣāṃ madhye mahābāhuḥ śrīmān keśavapūrvajaḥ
|
upaviṣṭo mahārāja pūjyamānaḥ samantataḥ||48||

49śuśubhe rājamadhyastho nīlavāsāḥ sitaprabhaḥ
|
nakṣatrairiva sampūrṇo vṛto niśi niśākaraḥ||49||

50tau tathā tu mahārāja gadāhastau suduḥsahau
|
anyonyaṃ vāgbhirugrābhistakṣamāṇau vyavasthitau||50||

51apriyāṇi tato'nyonyamuktvā tau kurusattamau
|
udīkṣantau sthitau tatra vṛtraśakrau yathā''have||51||
