Mahābhārata 1.42 (Ādiparvan)

1śṛṅgyuvāca|
yadyetatsāhasaṃ tāta yadi vā duṣkṛtaṃ kṛtam
|
priyaṃ vā'pyapriyaṃ vā te vāguktā na mṛṣā bhavet||1||

2naivānyathedaṃ bhavitā pitareṣa bravīmi te
|
nāhaṃ mṛṣā bravīmyevaṃ svaireṣvapi kutaḥ śapan||2||

3śamīka uvāca|
jānāmyugraprabhāvaṃ tvāṃ tāta satyagiraṃ tathā
|
nānṛtaṃ coktapūrvaṃ te naitanmithyā bhaviṣyati||3||

4pitrā putro vayastho'pi satataṃ vācya eva tu
|
yathā syādguṇasaṃyuktaḥ prāpnuyācca mahadyaśaḥ||4||

5kiṃ punarbāla eva tvaṃ tapasā bhāvitaḥ sadā
|
vardhate ca prabhavatāṃ kopo'tīva mahātmanām||5||

6so'haṃ paśyāmi vaktavyaṃ tvayi dharmabhṛtāṃ vara
|
putratvaṃ bālatāṃ caiva tavāvekṣya ca sāhasam||6||

7sa tvaṃ śamaparo bhūtvā vanyamāhāramācaran
|
cara krodhamimaṃ hitvā naivaṃ dharmaṃ prahāsyasi||7||

8krodho hi dharmaṃ harati yatīnāṃ duḥkhasaṃcitam
|
tato dharmavihīnānāṃ gatiriṣṭā na vidyate||8||

9śama eva yatīnāṃ hi kṣamiṇāṃ siddhikārakaḥ
|
kṣamāvatāmayaṃ lokaḥ paraścaiva kṣamāvatām||9||

10tasmāccarethāḥ satataṃ kṣamāśīlo jitendriyaḥ
|
kṣamayā prāpsyase lokānbrahmaṇaḥ samanantarān||10||

11mayā tu śamamāsthāya yacchakyaṃ kartumadya vai
|
tatkariṣyāmyahaṃ tāta prepayiṣye nṛpāya vai||11||

12mama putreṇa śapto'si bālena kṛśabuddhinā
|
mamemāṃ dharṣaṇāṃ tvattaḥ prekṣya rājannamarṣiṇā||12||

13sautiruvāca|
evamādiśya śiṣyaṃ sa preṣayāmāsa suvrataḥ
|
parikṣite nṛpataye dayāpanno mahātapāḥ||13||

14saṃdiśya kuśalapraśnaṃ kāryavṛttāntameva ca
|
śiṣyaṃ gauramukhaṃ nāma śīlavantaṃ samāhitam||14||

15so'bhigamya tataḥ śīghraṃ narendraṃ kuruvardhanam
|
viveśa bhavanaṃ rājñaḥ pūrvaṃ dvāsthairniveditaḥ||15||

16pūjitastu narendreṇa dvijo gauramukhastadā
|
ācakhyau ca pariśrānto rājñaḥ sarvamaśeṣataḥ||16||

17śamīkavacanaṃ ghoraṃ yathoktaṃ mantrisannidhau
|
gauramukha uvāca|
śamīko nāma rājendra vartate viṣaye tava||17||

18ṛṣiḥ paramadharmātmā dāntaḥ śānto mahātapāḥ
|
tasya tvayā naravyāghra sarpaḥ prāṇairviyojitaḥ||18||

19avasakto dhanuṣkoṭyā skandhe maunānvitasya ca
|
kṣāntavāṃstava tatkarma putrastasya na cakṣame||19||

20tena śapto'si rājendra piturajñātamadya vai
|
takṣakaḥ saptarātreṇa mṛtyustava bhaviṣyati||20||

21tatra rakṣāṃ kuruṣveti punaḥ punarathā'bravīt
|
tadanyathā na śakyaṃ ca kartuṃ kenacidapyuta||21||

22na hi śaknoti saṃyantuṃ putraṃ kopasamanvitam
|
tato'haṃ preṣitastena tava rājanhitārthinā||22||

23sātiruvāca|
iti śrutvā vaco ghoraṃ sa rājā kurunandanaḥ
|
paryatapyata tatpāpaṃ kṛtvā rājā mahātapāḥ||23||

24taṃ ca maunavrataṃ śrutvā vane munivaraṃ tadā
|
bhūya evābhavadrājā śokasaṃtaptamānasaḥ||24||

25anukrośātmatāṃ tasya śamīkasyāvadhārya ca
|
paryatapyata bhūyo'pi kṛtvā tatkilbiṣaṃ muneḥ||25||

26na hi mṛtyuṃ tathā rājā śrutvā vai so'nvatapyata
|
aśocadamaraprakhyo yathā kṛtveha karma tat||26||

27tatastaṃ preṣayāmāsa rājā gauramukhaṃ tadā
|
bhūyaḥ prasādaṃ bhagavānkarotviha mameti vai||27||

28tasmiṃśca gatamātre'tha rājā gauramukhe tadā
|
mantribhirmantrayāmāsa saha saṃvignamānasaḥ||28||

29saṃmantrya mantribhiścaiva sa tathā mantratattvavit
|
prāsādaṃ kārayāmāsa ekastambhaṃ surakṣitam||29||

30rakṣāṃ ca vidadhe tatra bhiṣajaścauṣadhāni ca
|
brāhmaṇānmantrasiddhāṃśca sarvato vai nyayojayat||30||

31rājakāryāṇi tatrasthaḥ sarvāṇyevākarocca saḥ
|
mantribhiḥ saha dharmajñaḥ samantātparirakṣitaḥ||31||

32na cainaṃ kaścidārūḍhaṃ labhate rājasattamam
|
vāto'pi niścaraṃstatra praveśe vinivāryate||32||

33prāpte ca divase tasminsaptame dvijasattamaḥ
|
kāśyapo'bhyāgamadvidvāṃstaṃ rājānaṃ cikitsitum||33||

34śrutaṃ hi tena tadabhūdyathā taṃ rājasattamam
|
takṣakaḥ pannagaśreṣṭho neṣyate yamasādanam||34||

35taṃ daṣṭaṃ pannagendreṇa kariṣye'hamapajvaram
|
tatra me'rthaśca dharmaśca bhaviteti vicintayan||35||

36taṃ dadarśa sa nāgendrastakṣakaḥ kāśyapaṃ pathi
|
gacchantamekamanasaṃ dvijo bhūtvā vayo'tigaḥ||36||

37tamabravītpannagendraḥ kāśyapaṃ munipuṃgavam
|
kva bhavāṃstvarito yāti kiṃca kāryaṃ cikīrṣati||37||

38kāśyapa uvāca|
nṛpaṃ kurukulotpannaṃ parikṣitamariṃdamam
|
takṣakaḥ pannagaśreṣṭhastejasā'dya pradhakṣyati||38||

39taṃ daṣṭaṃ pannagendreṇa tenāgnisamatejasam
|
pāṇḍavānāṃ kulakaraṃ rājānamamitaujasam
|
gacchāmi tvaritaṃ saumya sadyaḥ kartumapajvaram||39||

40takṣaka uvāca|
ahaṃ sa takṣako brahmaṃstaṃ dhakṣyāmi mahīpatim
|
nivartasva na śaktastvaṃ mayā daṣṭaṃ cikitsitum||40||

41kāśyapa uvāca|
ahaṃ taṃ nṛpatiṃ gatvā tvayā daṣṭamapajvaram
|
kariṣyāmīti me buddhirvidyābalasamanvitā||41||
