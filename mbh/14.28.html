Mahābhārata 14.28 (Āśvamedhikaparvan)

1brāhmaṇa uvāca|
gandhānna jighrāmi rasānna vedhmi rūpaṃ na paśyāmi na ca spṛśāmi
|
na cāpi śabdānvividhān śṛṇomi na cāpi saṅkalpamupaimi kañcit||1||

2arthāniṣṭānkāmayate svabhāva: sarvāndveṣyānpradviṣate svabhāvaḥ
|
kāmadveṣāvudbhavataḥ svabhāvāt prāṇāpānau jantudehānniveśya||2||

3tebhyaścānyāṃsteṣu nityāṃśca bhāvān bhūtātmānaṃ lakṣayeraśarīre
|
tasmistiṣṭhannāsmi saktaḥ kathañcit kāmakrodhābhyāṃ jarayā mṛtyunā ca||3||

4akāmayānasya ca sarvakāmānavidviṣāṇasya ca sarvadoṣān
|
na me svabhāveṣu bhavanti lopāstoyasya bindoriva puṣkareṣu||4||

5nityasya caitasya bhavanti nityā nirīkṣyamāṇasya bahusvabhāvān
|
na sajjate karmasu bhogajālaṃ divīva sūryasya mayūkhajālam||5||

6atrāpyudāharantīmamitihāsaṃ purātanam
|
adhvaryuyatisaṃvādaṃ taṃ nibodha yaśasvini||6||

7prokṣyamāṇaṃ paśuṃ dṛṣṭvā yajñakarmaṇyathābravīt
|
yatiradhvaryumāsīno hiṃseyamiti kutsayan||7||

8tamadhvaryuḥ pratyuvāca nāyaṃ chāgo vinaśyati
|
śreyasā yokṣyate janturyadi śrutiriyaṃ tathā||8||

9yo hyasya pārthivo bhāgaḥ pṛthivīṃ sa gamiṣyati
|
yadasya vārija kiñcidapastatsampravekṣyati||9||

10sūrye cakṣurdiśaḥ śrotraṃ prāṇo'sya divameva ca
|
āgame vartamānasya na me doṣā'sti kaścana||10||

11yatiruvāca|
prāṇairviyoge cchāgasya yadi śreyaḥ prapaśyasi
|
chāgārthe vartate yajño bhavataḥ kiṃ prayojanam||11||

12atra tvāṃ manyatāṃ bhrātā pitā mātā sakheti ca
|
mantrayasvainamunnīya paravantaṃ viśeṣataḥ||12||

13evamevānumanyerastān bhavān draṣṭumarhati
|
teṣāmanumataṃ śrutvā śakyā kartuṃ vicāraṇā||13||

14prāṇā apyasya chāgasya prāpitāste svayonipu
|
śarīraṃ kevalaṃ śiṣṭaṃ niśceṣṭamiti me matiḥ||14||

15indhanasya tu tulyena śarīreṇa vicetasā
|
hiṃsānirveṣṭukāmānāmindhanaṃ paśusaṃjñitam||15||

16ahiṃsā sarvadharmāṇāmiti vṛddhānuśāsanam
|
yadahiṃsraṃ bhavet karma tatkāryamiti vidmahe||16||

17ahiṃseti pratijñeyaṃ yadi vakṣyāmyataḥ param
|
śakyaṃ bahuvidhaṃ kartuṃ bhavatā kāryadūṣaṇam||17||

18ahiṃsā sarvabhūtānāṃ nityamasmāsu rocate
|
pratyakṣataḥ sādhayāmo na parokṣamupāsmahe||18||

19adhvaryuruvāca|
bhūmergandhaguṇān bhuṃkṣe pibasyāpomayān rasān
|
jyotiṣāṃ paśyase rūpaṃ spṛśasyanilajānguṇān||19||

20śṛṇoṣyākāśajān śabdān manasā manyase matim
|
sarvāṇyetāni bhūtāni prāṇā iti ca manyase||20||

21prāṇādāne nivṛtto'si hiṃsāyāṃ vartate bhavān
|
nāsti ceṣṭā vinā hiṃsāṃ kiṃ vā tvaṃ manyase dvija||21||

22yatiruvāca|
akṣaraṃ ca kṣaraṃ caiva dvaidhībhāvo'yamātmanaḥ
|
akṣaraṃ tatra sadbhāvaḥ svabhāvaḥ kṣara ucyate||22||

23prāṇo jihvā manaḥ sattvaṃ sadbhāvo rajasā saha
|
bhāvairetairvimuktasya nirdvandvasya nirāśiṣaḥ||23||

24samasya sarvabhūteṣu nirmamasya jitātmanaḥ
|
samantāt parimuktasya na bhayaṃ vidyate kvacit||24||

25adhvaryuruvāca|
sadbhireveha saṃvāsaḥ kāryo matimatāṃ vara
|
bhavato hi mataṃ śrutvā pratibhāti matirmama||25||

26bhagavan bhagavadbuddhyā pratipanno bravīmyaham
|
vrataṃ mantrakṛtaṃ karturnāparādho'sti me dvija||26||

27brāhmaṇa uvāca|
upapattyā yatistūṣṇīṃ vartamānastataḥ param
|
adhvaryurapi nirmohaḥ pracacāra mahāmakhe||27||

28evametādṛśaṃ mokṣaṃ susūkṣmaṃ brāhmaṇā viduḥ
|
viditvā cānutiṣṭhanti kṣetrajñenārthadarśinā||28||
