Mahābhārata 15.6 (Āśramavāsikaparvan)

1dhṛtarāṣṭra uvāca|
maṇḍalāni ca budhyethāḥ pareṣāmātmanastathā
|
udāsīnagaṇānāṃ ca madhyasthānāṃ ca bhārata||1||

2caturṇāṃ śatrujātānāṃ sarveṣāmātatāyinām
|
mitraṃ cāmitramitraṃ ca boddhavyaṃ te'rikarśana||2||

3tathāmātyā janapadā durgāṇi vividhāni ca
|
balāni ca kuruśreṣṭha bhavatyeṣāṃ yathecchakam||3||

4te ca dvādaśa kaunteya rājñāṃ vai viṣayātmakāḥ
|
mantripradhānāśca guṇāḥ ṣaṣṭirdvādaśa ca prabho||4||

5etanmaṇḍalamityāhurācāryā nītikovidāḥ
|
atra ṣāḍguṇyamāyattaṃ yudhiṣṭhira nibodha tat||5||

6vṛddhikṣayau ca vijñeyau sthānaṃ ca kurusattama
|
dvisaptatyāṃ mahābāho tataḥ ṣāḍguṇyajā guṇāḥ||6||

7yadā svapakṣo balavān parapakṣastathā'balaḥ
|
vigṛhya śatrūnkaunteya jeyaḥ kṣitipatistadā||7||

8yadā pare ca balinaḥ svapakṣaścaiva durbalaḥ
|
sārdhaṃ vidvāṃstadā kṣīṇaḥ paraiḥ sandhi samāśrayet||8||

9dravyāṇāṃ sañcayaścaiva kartavyaḥ subhahāṃstathā
|
yadā samartho yānāya na cireṇaiva bhārata||9||

10tadā sarvaṃ vidheyaṃ syātsthānena sa vicārayet
|
bhūmiralpaphalā deyā viparītasya bhārata||10||

11hiraṇyaṃ kuṣyabhūyiṣṭhaṃ mitraṃ kṣīṇamatho balam
|
viparītānnigṛhṇīyātsvaṃ hi sandhiviśāradaḥ||11||

12sandhyarthaṃ rājaputraṃ vā lipsethā bharatarṣabha
|
viparītaṃ na tacchreyaḥ putra kasyāñcidāpadi||12||

13tasyāḥ pramokṣe yatnaṃ ca kuryāḥ sopāyamantravit
|
prakṛtīnāṃ ca rājendra rājā dīnānvibhāvayet||13||

14krameṇa yugapatsarvaṃ vyavasāyaṃ mahābalaḥ
|
pīḍanaṃ stambhanaṃ caiva kośabhaṅgastathaiva ca||14||

15kāryaṃ yatnena śatrūṇāṃ svarājyaṃ rakṣatā svayam
|
na ca hiṃsyo'bhyupagataḥ sāmanto vṛddhimicchatā||15||

16kaunteya taṃ na hiṃsetsa yo mahīṃ vijigīṣate
|
gaṇānāṃ bhedane yogamīpsethāḥ saha mantribhiḥ||16||

17sādhusaṅgrahaṇāccaiva pāpanigrahaṇāttathā
|
durbalāścaiva satataṃ nānveṣṭavyā balīyasā||17||

18tiṣṭhethā rājaśārdūla vaitasīṃ vṛttimāsthitaḥ
|
yadyenamabhiyāyācca balavān durbalaṃ nṛpaḥ||18||

19sāmādibhirupāyaistaṃ krameṇa vinivartayeḥ
|
aśaknuvaṃśca yuddhāya niṣpatetsaha mantribhiḥ||19||

20kośena paurairdaṇḍena ye cāsya priyakāriṇaḥ
|
asambhave tu sarvasya yathā mukhyena niṣpatet
|
krameṇānena muktiḥ syāccharīramiti kevalam||20||
