Mahābhārata 7.131 (Droṇaparvan)

1sañjaya uvāca|
vartamāne mahārāja saṃgrāme lomaharṣaṇe
|
vyākuleṣu ca sarveṣu pīḍyamāneṣu sarvaśaḥ ||1||

2rādheyo bhīmamānarcchadyuddhāya bharatarṣabha
|
yathā nāgo vane nāgaṃ matto mattamabhidravan ||2||

3dhṛtarāṣṭra uvāca|
yau tau karṇaśca bhīmaśca saṃprayuddhau mahābalau
|
arjunasya rathopānte kīdṛśaḥ so'bhavadraṇaḥ||3||

4pūrvaṃ hi nirjitaḥ karṇo bhīmasenena saṃyuge
|
kathaṃ bhūyaḥ sa rādheyo bhīmamāgānmahārathaḥ||4||

5bhīmo vā sūtatanayaṃ pratyudyātaḥ kathaṃ raṇe
|
mahārathaṃ samākhyātaṃ pṛthivyāṃ pravaraṃ ratham ||5||

6bhīṣmadroṇāvatikramya dharmarājo yudhiṣṭhiraḥ
|
nānyato bhayamādatta vinā karṇānmahārathāt||6||

7bhayādyasya mahābāho na śete bahulāḥ samāḥ
|
cintayannityaśo vīryaṃ rādheyasya mahātmanaḥ
|
taṃ kathaṃ sūtaputraṃ tu bhīmo'yodhayatāhave ||7||

8brahmaṇyaṃ vīryasaṃpannaṃ samareṣvanivartinam
|
kathaṃ karṇaṃ yudhāṃ śreṣṭhaṃ yodhayāmāsa pāṇḍavaḥ||8||

9yau tau samīyaturvīrau vaikartanavṛkodarau
|
kathaṃ tāvatra yuddhyetāṃ mahābalaparākramau ||9||

10bhrātṛtvaṃ darśitaṃ pūrvaṃ ghṛṇī cāpi sa sūtajaḥ
|
kathaṃ bhīmena yuyudhe kuntyā vākyamanusmaran||10||

11bhīmo vā sūtaputreṇa smaran vairaṃ purā kṛtam
|
ayudhyata kathaṃ śūraḥ karṇena saha saṃyuge ||11||

12āśāste ca sadā sūtaputro duryodhano mama
|
karṇo jeṣyati saṃgrāme samastānpāṇḍavāniti||12||

13jayāśā yatra putrasya mama mandasya saṃyuge
|
sa kathaṃ bhīmakarmāṇaṃ bhīmasenamayodhayat ||13||

14yaṃ samāsādya putrairme kṛtaṃ vairaṃ mahārathaiḥ
|
taṃ sūtatanayaṃ tāta kathaṃ bhīmo hyayodhayat||14||

15anekān viprakārāṃśca sūtaputrasamudbhavān
|
smaramāṇaḥ kathaṃ bhīmo yuyudhe sūtasūnunā||15||

16yo'jayatpṛthivīṃ sarvāṃ rathenaikena vīryavān
|
taṃ sūtatanayaṃ yuddhe kathaṃ bhīmo hyayodhayat||16||

17yo jātaḥ kuṇḍalābhyāṃ ca kavacena sahaiva ca
|
taṃ sūtaputraṃ samare bhīmaḥ kathamayodhayat ||17||

18yathā tayoryuddhamabhūdyaścāsīdvijayī tayoḥ
|
tanmamācakṣva tattvena kuśalo hyasi sañjaya||18||

19sañjaya uvāca|
bhīmasenastu rādheyamutsṛjya rathināṃ varam
|
iyeṣa gantuṃ yatrāstāṃ vīrau kṛṣṇadhanañjayau||19||

20taṃ prayāntamabhidrutya rādheyaḥ kaṅkapatribhiḥ
|
abhyavarṣanmahārāja megho vṛṣṭhyeva parvatam ||20||

21phullatā paṅkajeneva vaktreṇa vihasan balī
|
ājuhāva raṇe yāntaṃ bhīmamādhirathistadā||21||

22karṇa uvāca|
bhīmāhitaistava raṇaḥ svapne'pi na vibhāvitaḥ
|
taddarśayasi kasmānme pṛṣṭhaṃ pārthadidṛkṣayā ||22||

23kuntyāḥ putrasya sadṛśaṃ nedaṃ pāṇḍavanandana
|
tena māmabhitaḥ sthitvā śaravarṣairavākira||23||

24bhīmasenastadāhvānaṃ karṇānnāmarṣayadyudhi
|
ardhamaṇḍalamāvṛtya sūtaputramayodhayat ||24||

25avakragāmibhirbāṇaurabhyavarṣanmahāyaśāḥ
|
daṃśitaṃ dvairathe yattaṃ sarvaśastraviśāradam ||25||

26vidhitsuḥ kalahasyāntaṃ jighāṃsuḥ karṇamakṣiṇot
|
hatvā tasyānugāṃstaṃ ca hantukāmo mahābalaḥ ||26||

27tasmai vyasṛjadugrāṇi vividhāni parantapaḥ
|
amarṣātpāṇḍavaḥ kruddhaḥ śaravarṣāṇi māriṣa||27||

28tasya tānīṣuvarṣāṇi mattadvirada‌gāminaḥ
|
sūtaputro'stramāyābhiragrasatparamāstravit ||28||

29sa yathāvanmahābāhurvidyayā vai supūjitaḥ
|
ācāryavanmaheṣvāsaḥ karṇaḥ paryacaradbalī||29||

30yudhyamānaṃ tu saṃrambhādbhīmasenaṃ hasanniva
|
abhyapadyata kaunteyaṃ karṇo rājan vṛkodaram||30||

31tannākṛṣyata kaunteyaḥ karṇasya smitamāhave
|
yudhyamāneṣu vīreṣu paśyatsu ca samantataḥ||31||

32taṃ bhīmasenaḥ saṃprāptaṃ vatsadantaiḥ stanāntare
|
vivyādha balavān kruddhastotrairiva mahādvipam||32||

33punaśca sūtaputraṃ tu svarṇapuṅkhaiḥ śilāśitaiḥ
|
sumuktaiścitravarmāṇaṃ nirbibheda trisaptabhiḥ||33||

34karṇo jāmbūnadairjālaiḥ saṃchannānvātaraṃhasaḥ
|
hayān vivyādha bhīmasya pañcabhiḥ pañcabhiḥ śaraiḥ||34||

35tato bāṇamayaṃ jālaṃ bhīmasenarathaṃ prati
|
karṇena vihitaṃ rājannimeṣārdhādadṛśyata||35||

36sarathaḥ sadhvajastatra sasūtaḥ pāṇḍavastadā
|
prācchādyata mahārāja karṇacāpacyutaiḥ śaraiḥ||36||

37tasya karṇaścatuḥṣaṣṭyā vyadhamatkavacaṃ dṛḍham
|
kruddhaścāpyahanatpārthaṃ nārācairmarmabhedibhiḥ||37||

38tatociṃtya mahābāhuḥ karṇakārmukaniḥsṛtān
|
samāśliṣyadasaṃbhrāntaḥ sūtaputraṃ vṛkodaraḥ||38||

39sa karṇacāpaprabhavānniṣūnāśīviṣopamān
|
bibhradbhīmo mahārāja na jagāma vyathāṃ raṇe ||39||

40tato dvātriṃśatā bhallairniśitaistigmatejanaiḥ
|
vivyādha samare karṇaṃ bhīmasenaḥ pratāpavān||40||

41ayatnenaiva taṃ karṇaḥ śarairbhṛśamavākirat
|
bhīmasenaṃ mahābāhuṃ saindhavasya vadhaiṣiṇam||41||

42mṛdupūrvaṃ tu rādheyo bhīmamājāvayodhayat
|
krodhapūrvaṃ tathā bhīmaḥ pūrvaṃ vairamanusmaran||42||

43taṃ bhīmaseno nāmṛṣyadavamānamamarṣaṇaḥ
|
sa tasmai vyasṛjattūrṇaṃ śaravarṣamamitrahā||43||

44te śarāḥ preṣitāstena bhīmasenena saṃyuge
|
nipetuḥ sarvato vīre kūjanta iva pakṣiṇaḥ||44||

45hemapuṅkhāḥ prasannāgrā bhīmasenadhanuścyutāḥ
|
prācchādayaṃste rādheyaṃ śalabhā iva pāvakam||45||

46karṇastu rathināṃ śreṣṭhaśchādyamānaḥ samantataḥ
|
rājanvyasṛjadugrāṇi śaravarṣāṇi bhārata||46||

47tasya tānaśaniprakhyāniṣūnsamaraśobhinaḥ
|
ciccheda bahubhirbhallairasaṃprāptān vṛkodaraḥ||47||

48punaśca śaravarṣeṇa cchādayāmāsa bhārata
|
karṇo vaikartano yuddhe bhīmasenamarindamaḥ||48||

49tatra bhārata bhīmaṃ tu dṛṣṭavantaḥ sma sāyakaiḥ
|
samācitatanuṃ saṃkhye śvāvidhaṃ śalalairiva||49||

50hemapuṅkhāñchilādhautān karṇacāpacyutāñcharān
|
dadhāra samare vīraḥ svaraśmīniva raśmivān ||50||

51rudhirokṣitasarvāṅgo bhīmaseno vyarājata
|
samṛddhakusumāpīḍo vasante'śokavṛkṣavat||51||

52tattu bhīmo mahābāhoḥ karṇasya caritaṃ raṇe
|
nāmṛṣyata mahābāhuḥ krodhāduddhṛttalocanaḥ||52||

53sa karṇaṃ pañcaviṃśatyā nārācānāṃ samārpayat
|
mahīdharamiva śvetaṃ gūḍhapādairviṣolbaṇaiḥ ||53||

54punareva ca vivyādha ṣaḍbhiraṣṭābhireva ca
|
marmasvamaravikrāntaḥ sūtaputraṃ tanutyajam ||54||

55punaranyena bāṇena bhīmasenaḥ pratāpavān
|
ciccheda kārmukaṃ tūrṇaṃ karṇasya prahasanniva||55||

56jaghāna caturaścāśvān sūtaṃ ca tvaritaḥ śaraiḥ
|
nārācairarkaraśmyābhaiḥ karṇaṃ vivyādha corasi||56||

57te jagmurdharaṇīmāśu karṇaṃ nirbhidya patriṇaḥ
|
yathā jaladharaṃ bhittvā divākaramarīcayaḥ||57||

58sa vaiklavyaṃ mahatprāpya chinnadhanvā śarāhataḥ
|
tathā puruṣamānī sa pratyapāyā‌drathāntaram||58||
