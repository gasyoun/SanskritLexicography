Mahābhārata 1.216 (Ādiparvan)

1vaiśaṃpāyana uvāca|
tataḥ samudre tīrthāni dakṣime bharatarṣabha
|
abhyagacchatsupuṇyāni śobhitāni tapasvibhiḥ||1||

2varjayanti sma tīrtāni tatra pañca sama tāpasāḥ
|
avakīrṇāni yānyāsanpurastāttu tapasvibhiḥ||2||

3agastyatīrthaṃ saubhadraṃ paulomaṃ ca supāvanam
|
kārandhamaṃ prasannaṃ ca mahamedhaphalaṃ ca tat||3||

4bhāradvājasya tīrthaṃ tu pāpapraśamanaṃ mahat
|
etāni pañca tīrthāni dadarśa kurusattamaḥ||4||

5viviktānyupalakṣyātha tāni tīrthāni pāṇḍavaḥ
|
dṛṣṭvā ca varjyamānāni munibhirdharmabuddhibhiḥ||5||

6tapasvinastato'pṛcchatprāñjaliḥ kurunandanaḥ
|
tīrthānīmāni varjyante kimarthaṃ brahmavādibhiḥ||6||

7tāpasā ūcuḥ|
grāhāḥ pañca vasantyeṣu haranti ca tapodhanān
|
tata etāni varjyante tīrthāni kurunandana||7||

8vaiśaṃpāyana uvāca|
teṣāṃ śrutvā mahābāhurvāryamāṇastapodhanaiḥ
|
jagāma tāni tīrthāni draṣṭuṃ puruṣasattamaḥ||8||

9tataḥ saubhadramāsādya maharṣestīrthamuttamam
|
vigāhya sahasā śūraḥ snānaṃ cakre parantapaḥ||9||

10atha taṃ puruṣavyāghramantarjalacaro mahān
|
jagrāha caraṇe grāhaḥ kuntīputraṃ dhanañjayam||10||

11sa tamādāya kaunteyo visphurantaṃ jalecaram
|
udatiṣṭhanmahābāhurbalena balināṃ varaḥ||11||

12utkṛṣṭa eva grāhastu so'rjunena yaśasvinā
|
babhūva nārī kalyāṇī sarvābharaṇabhūṣaitā||12||

13dīpyamānā śriyā rājandivyarūpā manoramā
|
tadadbhutaṃ mahaddṛṣṭvā kuntīputro dhanañjayaḥ||13||

14tāṃ striyaṃ paramaprīta idaṃ vacanamabravīt
|
kā vai tvamasi kalyāṇikuto vā'si jalecarī||14||

15kimarthaṃ ca mahatpāpamidaṃ kṛtavatī purā
|
vargovāca
|
apsarā'smi mahābāho devāraṇyavihāriṇī||15||

16iṣṭā dhanapaternityaṃ vargā nāma mahābala
|
mama sakhyaścatasro'nyāḥ sarvāḥ kāmagamāḥ śubhāḥ||16||

17tābhiḥ sārdhaṃ prayātā'smi lokapālaniveśanam
|
tataḥ paśyāmahe sarvā brāhmaṇaṃ saṃśitavratam||17||

18rūpavantamadhīyānamekamekāntacāriṇam
|
tasyaiva tapasā rājaṃstadvayaṃ tejasā vṛtam||18||

19āditya iva taṃ deśaṃ itvaṃ sarvaṃ vyakāśayat
|
tasya dṛṣṭvā tapastādṛgrūpaṃ cādbhutamuttamam||19||

20avatīrṇāḥ sma taṃ deśaṃ tapovighnacikīrṣayā
|
ahaṃ ca saurabheyī ca samīcī budbudā latā||20||

21yaugapadyena taṃ vipramabhyagacchāma bhārata
|
gāyantyo'tha hasantyaśca lobhayitvā ca taṃ dvijam||21||

22sa ca nāsmāsu kṛtavānmano vīra kathaṃcana
|
nākampata mahātejāḥ sthitastapasi nirmale||22||

23sośapatkupitosmāṃstu brāhmaṇaḥ kṣatriyarṣabha
|
grāhabhūtā jale yūyaṃ cariṣyatha śataṃ samāḥ||23||
