Mahābhārata 7.25 (Droṇaparvan)

1sañjaya uvāca|
mahadbhairavamāsīnnaḥ sannivṛtteṣu pāṇḍuṣu
|
dṛṣṭvā droṇaṃ chādyamānaṃ tairbhāskaramivāṃbudaiḥ||1||

2taiścodbhūtaṃ rajastīvramavacakre camūṃ tava
|
tato hatamamaṃsyāma droṇaṃ dṛṣṭipathe hate||2||

3tāṃstu śūrān maheṣvāsānkrūraṃ karma cikīrṣataḥ
|
dṛṣṭvā duryodhanastūrṇaṃ svasainyaṃ samacūvudat||3||

4yathāśakti yathotsāhaṃ yathāsattvaṃ narādhipāḥ
|
vārayadhvaṃ yathāyogaṃ pāṇḍavānāmanīkinīm||4||

5tato durmarṣaṇo bhīmamabhyagacchat sutastava
|
ārāddṛṣṭvākiran bāṇairjighūkṣustasya jīvitam||5||

6taṃ bāṇairavatastāra kruddho mṛtyurivāhave
|
taṃ sa bhīmo'tudadbāṇaistadā''sīttumulaṃ mahat||6||

7ta īśvarasamādiṣṭāḥ prājñāḥ śūrāḥ prahāriṇaḥ
|
rājyaṃ mṛtyubhayaṃ tyaktvā pratyatiṣṭhan parānyudhi||7||

8kṛtavarmā śineḥ pautraṃ droṇaṃ prepsuṃ viśāṃpate
|
paryavārayadāyāntaṃ śūraṃ samaraśobhinam||8||

9taṃ śaineyaḥ śaravrātaiḥ kruddhaḥ kruddhamavārayat
|
kṛtavarmā ca śaineyaṃ mattā mattāmiva dvipam||9||

10saindhavaḥ kṣatravarmāṇamāyāntaṃ niśitaiḥ śaraiḥ
|
ugradhanvā maheṣvāsaṃ yatto droṇādavārayat||10||

11kṣatravarmā sindhupateñchittvā ketanakārmuke
|
nārācairdaśabhiḥ kruddhaḥ sarvamarmasvatā ḍayat||11||

12athānyaddhanurādāya saindhavaḥ kṛtahastavat
|
vivyādha kṣatravarmāṇaṃ raṇe sarvāyasaiḥ śaraiḥ||12||

13yuyutsuṃ pāṇḍavārthāya yatamānaṃ mahāratham
|
subāhurbhārataṃ śūraṃ yatto droṇādavārayat||13||

14subāhoḥ sudhanurbāṇāvasyataḥ parighopamau
|
yuyutsuḥ śitapītābhyāṃ kṣurābhyāmacchinadbhujau||14||

15rājānaṃ pāṇḍavaśreṣṭhaṃ dharmātmānaṃ yudhiṣṭhiram
|
veleva sāgaraṃ kṣubdhaṃ madrarāṭ samavārayat||15||

16taṃ dharmarājo bahubhirmarmabhidbhiravākirat
|
madreśastaṃ catuḥṣaṣṭyā śarairviddhvā'nadadbhṛśam||16||

17tasya nānadanaḥ ketumuccakarta ca kārmukam
|
kṣurābhyāṃ pāṇḍavo jyeṣṭhastata uccukuśurjanāḥ||17||

18tathaiva rājā bālhīko rājānaṃ drupadaṃ śaraiḥ
|
ādravantaṃ sahānīkaḥ sahānīkaṃ nyavārayat||18||

19tadyuddhamabhavaddhoraṃ vṛddhayoḥ saha senayoḥ
|
yathā mahāyūthapayorddhipayoḥ saṃpabhinnayoḥ||19||

20vindānuvindāvāvantyau virāṭaṃ matsyamārcchatām
|
sahasainyau sahānīkaṃ yathendrāgnī purā balim||20||

21tadutpiñjalakaṃ yuddhamāsīddevāsuropamam
|
matsyānāṃ kekayaiḥ sārdhamabhītāśvarathādvipam||21||

22nākuāliṃ tu śatānīkaṃ bhūnakarmā sabhāpatiḥ
|
asyantamiṣujālāni yāntaṃ droṇādarayat||22||

23tato nakuladāyādastribhirbhaṭṭaiḥ susaṃśitaiḥ
|
cakre vibāhuśirase bhūtakarmāṇamāhave||23||

24sutasomaṃ tu vikrāntamāyāntaṃ taṃ śaraughiṇam
|
droṇāyābhimukhaṃ vīraṃ viviṃśatiravārayat||24||

25sutasomastu saṃkruddhaḥ svapitṛvyamājihmagaiḥ
|
viviṃśatiṃ śarairbhitvā nābhyavartata daṃśitaḥ||25||

26atha bhīmarathaḥ śālvamāśugairāyasaiḥ śitaiḥ
|
ṣaṅiḥ sāśvaniyantāramanayadyamasādanam||26||

27śrutakarmāṇamāyāntaṃ mayūrasadṛśaiheyaiḥ
|
caitrasenirmahārāja tava pautraṃ nyavārayat||27||

28tau pautrau tava rdharṣau parasparavadhaiṣiṇau
|
pitṝṇāmarthasiddhyarthaṃ cakraturyuddhamuttamam||28||

29tiṣṭhantamagre taṃ dṛṣṭvā prativindhyaṃ mahāhave
|
drauṇirmāniṃ pituḥ kurvan mārgaṇaiḥ samavārayat||29||

30taṃ kruddhaṃ prativivyādha prativindhyaḥ śitaiḥ śaraiḥ
|
siṃhalāṃgūlalakṣmāṇaṃ piturarthe vyavasthitam||30||

31pravapanniva bījāni bījakāle nararṣabhaḥ
|
drauṇāyaniṃ draupadeyāḥ śaravarṣairavākiran||31||

32ājuāna śrutakīrtiṃ tu draupadeyaṃ mahāratham
|
droṇāyābhimukhaṃ yāntaṃ dauḥśāsaniravārayat||32||

33tasya kṛṣṇasamaḥ kārṣṇistribhirbhallaiḥ susāśitaiḥ
|
dhanurdhvajaṃ ca sūtaṃ ca chittvā droṇāntikaṃ yayau||33||

34yastu śūratamo rājannubhayoḥ senayormataḥ
|
taṃ paṭaccarahantāraṃ lakṣmaṇaḥ samavārayat||34||

35sa lakṣmaṇasyeṣvasanaṃ chittvā lakṣma ca bhārata
|
lakṣmaṇe śarajālāni visṛjan bahvaśobhata||35||

36vikarṇastu mahāprājño yājñaseniṃ śikhaṇḍinam
|
paryavārayadāyāntaṃ yuvānaṃ samare yuvā||36||

37tatastābhiṣujālena yājñaseniḥ samāvṛṇot
|
vidhūya tadbāṇajālaṃ babhau tava sutobalī||37||

38aṅgado'bhimukhaṃ vīramuttamaujasamāhave
|
droṇāyābhimukhaṃ yāntaṃ śaraugheṇa nyavārayat||38||

39sa saṃprahārastumulastayoḥ puruṣasiṃhayoḥ
|
sainikānāṃ ca sarveṣāṃ tayośca prītivardhanaḥ||39||

40durmukhastu maheṣvāso vīraṃ purujitaṃ balī
|
droṇāyābhimukhaṃ yāntaṃ vatsadantairavārayat||40||

41sa durmukhaṃ bhruvormadhye nārācenābhyatāḍayat
|
tasya tadvibabhau vaktraṃ sanālamiva paṅkajam||41||

42karṇastu kekayān bhrātṝn pañca lohitakadhjān
|
droṇāyābhimukhaṃ yātān śaravarṣairavārayat||42||

43te cainaṃ bhṛśasaṃtaptāḥ śaravarṣairavākiran
|
sa ca tāṃśchādayāmāsa śarajālaiḥ punaḥ punaḥ||43||

44naiva karṇo na te pañca dadṛśurbāṇasaṃvṛtāḥ
|
sāśvasūtadhvajarathāḥ parasparaśarācitāḥ||44||

45putrāste durjayaścaiva jayaśca vijayaśca ha
|
nīlakāśyajayatsenāṃstrayastrīn pratyavārayan||45||

46tadyuddhamabhavadghoramīkṣitṛprītivardhanam
|
siṃhavyāghratarakṣūṇāṃ yatharkṣamahiṣarṣabhaiḥ||46||

47kṣemadhūrtibṛhantau tu bhrātarau sātvataṃ yudhi
|
droṇāyābhimukhaṃ yāntaṃ śaraistīkṣṇaistatakṣatuḥ||47||

48tayostasya ca tadyuddhamatyadbhutamivābhavat
|
siṃhasya dvipamukhyābhyāṃ prabhinnābhyāṃ yathā vane||48||

49rājānaṃ tu tathā'mbaṣṭhamekaṃ yuddhābhinandinam
|
cedirājaḥ śarānasyan kruddho droṇādavārayat||49||

50tatombaṣṭho'sthimedinyā nirabhidyacchalākayā
|
sa tyaktvā saśaraṃ cāpaṃ rathādbhūmimupāgamat||50||

51vārdhakṣomaṃ tu vārṣṇeyaṃ kṛpaḥ śāradvataḥ śaraiḥ
|
akṣudraḥ kṣudrakairbāṇaiḥ kruddharūpamavārayat||51||

52yudhyantau kṛpavārṣṇeyau ye'paśyāṃścitrayodhinau
|
te yuddhāsaktamanaso nānyāṃ bubudhire kriyām||52||

53saumadattistu rājānaṃ maṇimantamatandritam
|
paryavārayadāyāntaṃ yaśo droṇasya vardhayan||53||

54sa saumada‌ttestvaritaścitreṣvasanaketane
|
punaḥ patākāṃ sūtaṃ ca chatraṃ cāpātayadrathāt||54||

55athāplutya rathāt tūrṇaṃ yūpaketuramitrahā
|
sāśvasūtadhvajarathaṃ taṃ cakarta varāsinā||55||

56rathaṃ ca svaṃ samāsthāya dhanurādāya cāparam
|
svayaṃ yacchan hayān rājan vyadhamat pāṇḍavīṃ camūm||56||

57pāṇḍyāmindramivāyāntamasurān prati durjayam
|
samarthaḥ sāyakaughena vṛṣaseno nyavārayat||57||

58gadāparighanistriṃśapaṭṭiśāyo ghanopalaiḥ
|
kaḍaṅgarairbhuśuṇḍībhiḥ prāsaistomarasāyakaiḥ||58||

59musalairmudgaraiścakrairbhindipālaparaśvaghaiḥ
|
pāṃsuvātāgnisalilairbhasmaloṣṭhatṛṇadrumaiḥ||59||

60ātudan prarujan bhañjannighnan vidrāvayan kṣipan
|
senāṃ vibhīṣayannāyāddroṇaprepsurghaṭotkacaḥ||60||

61taṃ tu nānāpraharaṇairnānāyuddhaviśeṣaṇaiḥ
|
rākṣasaṃ rākṣasaḥ kruddhaḥ samājaghne hyalaṃvuṣaḥ||61||

62tayostadabhavadyuddhaṃ rakṣogrāmaṇimukhyayoḥ
|
tādṛgyādṛk purāvṛttaṃ śambarāmararājayoḥ||62||

63evaṃ dvandvaśatānyāsan rathavāraṇavājinām
|
padātīnāṃ ca bhadraṃ te tava teṣāṃ ca saṃkule||63||

64nainādṛśo dṛṣṭapūrvaḥ saṃgrāmo naiva ca śrutaḥ
|
droṇasyābhāvabhāve tu prasaktānāṃ yathā'bhavat||64||

65idaṃ ghoramidaṃ citramidaṃ raudramiti prabho
|
tatra yuddhānyadṛśyanta pratatāni bahūni ca||65||
