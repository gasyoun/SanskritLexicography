Mahābhārata 4.47 (Virāṭaparvan)

1vaiśampāyana uvāca|atha duryodhano rājā samare bhīṣmamabravīt
|
droṇaṃ ca rathaśārdūlaṃ kṛpaṃ ca sumahāratham ||1||

2ukto'yamartha ācāryau mayā karṇena cāsakṛt
|
punareva pravakṣyāmi na hi tṛpyāmi taṃ bruvan ||2||

3parābhūtairhi vastavyaṃ taiśca dvādaśa vatsarān
|
vane janapade jñātaireṣa eva paṇo hi naḥ ||3||

4teṣāṃ na tāvannirvṛttaṃ vartate tu trayodaśam
|
ajñātavāso bībhatsurathāsmābhiḥ samāgataḥ ||4||

5anivṛtte tu nirvāse yadi bībhatsurāgataḥ
|
punardvādaśavarṣāṇi vane vatsyanti pāṇḍavāḥ ||5||

6lobhādvā te na jānīyurasmān vā moha āviśat
|
hīnātiriktameteṣāṃ bhīṣmo veditumarhati ||6||

7arthānāṃ ca punardvaidhe nityaṃ bhavati saṃśayaḥ
|
anyathā cintito hyarthaḥ punarbhavati so'nyathā ||7||

8uttaraṃ mārgamāṇānāṃ matsyānāṃ ca yuyutsatām
|
yadi bībhatsurāyātastadā kasyāparādhnumaḥ ||8||

9trigartānāṃ vayaṃ hetormatsyānyoddhumihāgatāḥ
|
matsyānāṃ viprakārāṃste bahūnasmānakīrtayan ||9||

10teṣāṃ bhayābhibhūtānāṃ tadasmābhiḥ pratiśrutam
|
prathamaṃ tairgrahītavyaṃ matsyānāṃ godhanaṃ mahat
|
saptamyāmaparāhṇe vai tathā taistu samāhitam ||10||

11aṣṭamyāṃ punarasmābhirādityasyodayaṃ prati
|
imā gāvo grahītavyā gate matsye gavāṃ padam ||11||

12te vā gāścānayiṣyanti yadi vā syuḥ parājitāḥ
|
asmān ‌vā hyupasaṃdhāya kuryurmatsyena saṃgatam ||12||

13athavā tānapāhāya matsyo janapadaiḥ saha
|
sarvayā senayā sārdhaṃ saṃvṛto bhīmarūpayā
|
āyātaḥ kevalaṃ rātrimasmānyoddhumihāgataḥ ||13||

14teṣāmeva mahāvīryaḥ kaścideṣa puraḥsaraḥ
|
asmān jetumihāyāto matsyo vā'pi svayaṃ bhavet ||14||

15yadyeṣa rājā matsyānāṃ yadi bībhatsurāgataḥ
|
sarvairyoddhavyamasmābhiriti naḥ samayaḥ kṛtaḥ ||15||

16atha kasmāt sthitā hyete ratheṣu rathasattamāḥ
|
bhīṣmo droṇaḥ kṛpaścaiva vikarṇo drauṇireva ca ||16||

17saṃbhrāntamanasaḥ sarve kāle hyasmin mahārathāḥ
|
nānyatra yuddhācchreyo'sti tathā'tmā praṇidhīyatām ||17||

18ācchinne godhane'smākamapi devena vajriṇā
|
yamena vā'pi saṅgrāme ko hāstinapuraṃ vrajet ||18||

19śarairebhiḥ praṇunnānāṃ bhagnānāṃ gahane vane
|
ko hi jīvet padātīnāṃ bhaveda‌śveṣu saṃśayaḥ ||19||

20duryodhanavacaḥ śrutvā rādheyastvabravīdvacaḥ
|
ācāryaṃ pṛṣṭhataḥ kṛtvā tathā nītirvidhīyatām ||20||

21jānāti hi mataṃ teṣāmatastrāsayatīha naḥ
|
arjune cāsya samprītimadhikāmupalakṣaye ||21||

22tathā hi dṛṣṭvā bībhatsumupāyāntaṃ praśaṃsati
|
yathā senā na bhajyeta tathā nītirvidhīyatām ||22||

23hreṣitaṃ hyupaśṛṇvāne droṇe sarvaṃ vighaṭṭitam
|
adeśikā mahāraṇye grīṣme śatruvaṃśaṃ gatāḥ
|
yathā na vibhramet senā tathā nītirvidhīyatām ||23||

24iṣṭā hi pāṇḍavā nityamācāryasya viśeṣataḥ
|
āsayannaparārthāśca kathyate sma svayaṃ tathā ||24||

25aśvānāṃ hreṣitaṃ śrutvā kaḥ praśaṃsāparo bhavet
|
sthāne vā'pi vrajanto vā sadā hreṣanti vājinaḥ ||25||

26sadā ca vāyavo vānti nityaṃ varṣati vāsavaḥ
|
stanayitnośca nirghoṣaḥ śrūyate bahuśastathā ||26||

27kimatra kāryaṃ pārthasya kathaṃ vā sa praśasyate
|
anyatra kāmāddveṣādvā roṣādasmāsu kevalāt ||27||

28ācāryā vai kāruṇikāḥ prājñāścāpāpadarśinaḥ
|
naite mahābhaye prāpte saṃpraṣṭavyāḥ kathañcana ||28||

29prāsādeṣu vicitreṣu goṣṭhīṣūpavaneṣu ca
|
kathā vicitrāḥ kurvāṇāḥ paṇḍitāstatra śobhanāḥ ||29||

30bahūnyāścaryarūpāṇi kurvāṇā janasaṃsadi
|
ijyāstre copasandhāne paṇḍitāstatra śobhanāḥ ||30||

31pareṣāṃ vivarajñāne manuṣyacariteṣu ca
|
hastyaśvarathacaryāsu kharoṣṭrājāvikarmaṇi ||31||

32godhaneṣu pratolīṣu varadvāramukheṣu ca
|
annasaṃskāradoṣeṣu paṇḍitāstatra śobhanāḥ ||32||

33paṇḍitān pṛṣṭhataḥ kṛtvā pareṣāṃ guṇavādinaḥ
|
vidhīyatāṃ tathā nītiryathā vadhyo bhavet paraḥ ||33||

34gā vaśca saṃpratiṣṭhāpya senāṃ vyūhya samantataḥ
|
ārakṣāśca vidhīyantāṃ yatra yotsyāmahe parān ||34||
