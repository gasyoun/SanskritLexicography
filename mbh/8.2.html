Mahābhārata 8.2 (Karṇaparvan)

1vaiśampāyana uvāca|
hate karṇe mahārāja niśi gāvalgaṇistadā
|
dīno yayau nāgapuramaśvairvātasamairjave ||1||

2sa hāstinapuraṃ gatvā bhṛśamudvignacetanaḥ
|
jagāma dhṛtarāṣṭrasya kṣayaṃ prakṣīṇabāndhavam ||2||

3sa tamudvīkṣya rājānaṃ kaśmalābhihataujasam
|
vavande prāñjalirbhūtvā mūrdhnā pādau nṛpasya ha ||3||

4saṃpūjya ca yathānyāyaṃ dhṛtarāṣṭraṃ mahīpatim
|
hā kaṣṭamiti coktvā sa tato vacanamādade ||4||

5sañjayo'haṃ kṣitipate kaccidāste sukhaṃ bhavān
|
svadoṣairāpadaṃ prāpya kaccinnādya vimuhyati ||5||

6hitānyuktāni vidura-droṇa-gāṅgeya-keśavaiḥ
|
agṛhītānyanusmṛtya kaccinna kuruṣe vyathām ||6||

7rāma-nārada-kaṇvādyairhitamuktaṃ sabhātale
|
na gṛhītamanusmṛtya kaccinna kuruṣe vyathām ||7||

8suhṛdastvaddhite yuktān ‌bhīṣmadroṇamukhān paraiḥ
|
nihatānyudhi saṃsmṛtya kaccinna kuruṣe vyathām ||8||

9tamevaṃvādinaṃ rājā sūtaputraṃ kṛtāñjalim
|
sudīrdhamaya niḥśvasya duḥkhārta idamabravīt ||9||

10dhṛtarāṣṭra uvāca|
āpageye hate śūre divyāstravati sañjaya
|
droṇe ca parameṣvāse bhṛśaṃ me vyathitaṃ manaḥ ||10||

11yo rathānāṃ sahasrāṇi daṃśitānāṃ daśaiva tu
|
ahanyahani tejasvī nijaghne vasusaṃbhavaḥ ||11||

12taṃ hataṃ yajñasenasya putreṇeha śikhaṇḍinā
|
pāṇḍaveyābhiguptena śrutvā me vyathitaṃ manaḥ ||12||

13bhārgavaḥ pradadau yasmai paramāstraṃ mahāhave
|
sākṣādrāmeṇa yo bālye dhanurveda upākṛtaḥ ||13||

14yasya prasādātkaunteyā rājaputrā mahārathāḥ
|
mahārathatvaṃ saṃprāptāstathānye vasudhādhipāḥ ||14||

15taṃ droṇaṃ nihataṃ śrutvā dhṛṣṭadyumnena saṃyuge
|
satyasandhaṃ maheṣvāsaṃ bhṛśaṃ me vyathitaṃ manaḥ ||15||

16yayorloke pumānastre na samo'sti caturvidhe
|
tau droṇabhīṣmau śrutvā tu hatau me vyathitaṃ manaḥ ||16||

17trailokye yasya cāstreṣu na pumānvidyate samaḥ
|
taṃ droṇaṃ nihataṃ śrutvā kimakurvata māmakāḥ ||17||

18saṃśaptakānāṃ ca bale pāṇḍavena mahātmanā
|
dhanañjayena vikramya gamite yamasādanam ||18||

19nārāyaṇāstre ca hate droṇaputrasya dhīmataḥ
|
vipradruteṣvanīkeṣu kimakurvata māmakāḥ ||19||

20vipradrutānahaṃ manye nimagnāñśokasāgare
|
plavamānān hate droṇe sannanaukānivārṇave ||20||

21duryodhanasya karṇasya bhojasya kṛtavarmaṇaḥ
|
madrarājasya śalyasya drauṇeścaiva kṛpasya ca ||21||

22matputrasya ca śeṣasya tathā'nyeṣāṃ ca sañjaya
|
vipradruteṣvanīkeṣu mukhavarṇo'bhavatkatham ||22||

23etatsarvaṃ yathāvṛttaṃ tathā gāvalgaṇe mama
|
ācakṣva pāṇḍaveyānāṃ māmakānāṃ ca vikramam ||23||

24sañjaya uvāca|
tavāparādhādyadvṛttaṃ kauraveyeṣu māriṣa
|
tacchrutvā mā vyathāṃ kārṣīrdiṣṭe na vyathate budhaḥ ||24||

25yasmādabhāvī bhāvī vā bhavedartho naraṃ prati
|
aprāptau tasya vā prāptau na kaścidvyathate budhaḥ ||25||

26dhṛtarāṣṭra uvāca|
na vyathā'bhyadhikā kācidvidyate mama sañjaya
|
diṣṭametatpurā manye kathayasva yathecchakam ||26||
