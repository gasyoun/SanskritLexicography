Mahābhārata 3.277 (Āraṇyakaparvan)

1yudhiṣṭhira uvāca|
uktaṃ bhagavatā janma rāmādīnāṃ pṛthak pṛthak
|
prasthānakāraṇaṃ brahman śrotumicchāmi kathyatām||1||

2kathaṃ dāśarathī vīrau bhrātarau rāmalakṣmaṇau
|
samprasthitau vane brahman maithilī ca yaśasvinī||2||

3mārkaṇḍeya uvāca|
jātaputro daśarathaḥ prītimānabhavannṛpa
|
kriyāratirdharmarataḥ satataṃ vṛddhasevitā||3||

4krameṇa cāsya te putrā vyavardhanta mahaujasaḥ
|
vedeṣu sarahasyeṣu dhanurvedeṣu pāragāḥ||4||

5caritabrahmacaryāste kṛtadārāśca pārthiva
|
yadā tadā daśarathaḥ prītimānabhavat sukhī||5||

6jyeṣṭho rāmo'bhavat teṣāṃ ramayāmāsa hi prajāḥ
|
manoharatayā dhīmān piturhṛdayanandanaḥ||6||

7tataḥ sa rājā matimān matvā''tmānaṃ vayodhikam
|
mantrayāmāsa sacivairdharmajñaiśca purohitaiḥ||7||

8abhiṣekāya rāmasya yauvarājyena bhārata
|
prāptakālaṃ ca te sarve menire mantrisattamāḥ||8||

9lohitākṣaṃ mahābāhuṃ mattamātaṅgagāminam
|
dīrghabāhuṃ mahoraskaṃ nīlakuñcitamūrdhajam||9||

10dīpyamānaṃ śriyā vīraṃ śakrādanavaraṃ raṇe
|
pāragaṃ sarvadharmāṇāṃ bṛhaspatisamaṃ matau||10||

11sarvānuraktaprakṛtiṃ sarvavidyāviśāradam
|
jitendriyamamitrāṇāmapi dṛṣṭimanoharam||11||

12niyantāramasādhūnāṃ goptāraṃ dharmacāriṇām
|
dhṛtimantamanādhṛṣyaṃ jetāramaparājitam||12||

13putraṃ rājā daśarathaḥ kausalyānandavardhanam
|
sandṛśya paramāṃ prītimagacchat kurunandana||13||

14cintayaṃśca mahātejā guṇān rāmasya vīryavān
|
abhyabhāṣata bhadraṃ te prīyamāṇaḥ purohitam||14||

15adya puṣyo niśi brahman puṇyaṃ yogamupaiṣyati
|
sambhārāḥ sambhriyantāṃ me rāmaścopanimantryatām||15||

16iti tadrājavacanaṃ pratiśrutyātha mantharā
|
kaikeyīmabhigamyedaṃ kāle vacanamabravīt||16||

17adya kaikeyi daurbhāgyaṃ rājñā te khyāpitaṃ mahat
|
āśīviṣastvāṃ saṅkruddhaścaṇḍo daśatu durbhage||17||

18subhagā khalu kausalyā yasyāḥ putro'bhiṣekṣyate
|
kuto hi tava saubhāgyaṃ yasyāḥ putro na rājyabhāk||18||

19sā tadvacanamājñāya sarvābharaṇabhūṣitā
|
vedīvilagnamadhyeva bibhratī rūpamuttamam||19||

20vivikte patimāsādya hasantīva śucismitā
|
praṇayaṃ vyañjayantīva madhuraṃ vākyamabravīt||20||

21satyapratijña yanme tvaṃ kāmamekaṃ nisṛṣṭavān
|
upākuruṣva tadrājaṃstasmānmucyasva saṅkaṭāt||21||

22rājovāca|
varaṃ dadāni te hanta tadgṛhāṇa yadicchasi
|
avadhyo vadhyatāṃ ko'dya vadhyaḥ ko'dya vimucyatām||22||

23dhanaṃ dadāni kasyādya hriyatāṃ kasya vā punaḥ
|
brāhmaṇasvādihānyatra yatkiñcidvittamasti me||23||

24pṛthivyāṃ rājarājo'smi cāturvarṇyasya rakṣitā
|
yaste'bhilaṣitaḥ kāmo brūhi kalyāṇi mā ciram||24||

25sā tadvacanamājñāya parigṛhya narādhipam
|
ātmano balamājñāya tata enamuvāca ha||25||

26ābhiṣecanikaṃ yat te rāmārthamupakalpitam
|
bharatastadavāpnotu vanaṃ gacchatu rāghavaḥ||26||

27sa tadrājā vacaḥ śrutvā vipriyaṃ dāruṇodayam
|
duḥkhārto bharataśreṣṭha na kiñcidvyājahāra ha||27||

28tatastathoktaṃ pitaraṃ rāmo vijñāya vīryavān
|
vanaṃ pratasthe dharmātmā rājā satyo bhavatviti||28||

29tamanvagacchallakṣmīvān dhanuṣmā~llakṣmaṇastadā
|
sītā ca bhāryā bhadraṃ te vaidehī janakātmajā||29||

30tato vanaṃ gate rāme rājā daśarathastadā
|
samayujyata dehasya kālaparyāyadharmaṇā||30||

31rāmaṃ tu gatamājñāya rājānaṃ ca tathāgatam
|
ānāyya bharataṃ devī kaikeyī vākyamabravīt||31||

32gato daśarathaḥ svargaṃ vanasthau rāmalakṣmaṇau
|
gṛhāṇa rājyaṃ vipulaṃ kṣemaṃ nihatakaṇṭakam||32||

33tāmuvāca sa dharmātmā nṛśaṃsaṃ bata te kṛtam
|
patiṃ hatvā kulaṃ cedamutsādya dhanalubdhayā||33||

34ayaśaḥ pātayitvā me mūrdhni tvaṃ kulapāṃsane
|
sakāmā bhava me mātarityuktvā praruroda ha||34||

35sa cāritraṃ viśodhyātha sarvaprakṛtisannidhau
|
anvayādbhrātaraṃ rāmaṃ vinivartanalālasaḥ||35||

36kausalyāṃ ca sumitrāṃ ca kaikeyīṃ ca suduḥkhitaḥ
|
agre prasthāpya yānaiḥ sa śatrughnasahito yayau||36||

37vasiṣṭhavāmadevābhyāṃ vipraiścānyaiḥ sahasraśaḥ
|
paurajānapadaiḥ sārdhaṃ rāmānayanakāṅkṣayā||37||

38dadarśa citrakūṭasthaṃ sa rāmaṃ sahalakṣmaṇam
|
tāpasānāmalaṅkāraṃ dhārayantaṃ dhanurdharam||38||

39visarjitaḥ sa rāmeṇa piturvacanakāriṇā
|
nandigrāme'karodrājyaṃ puraskṛtyāsya pāduke||39||

40rāmastu punarāśaṅkya paurajānapadāgamam
|
praviveśa mahāraṇyaṃ śarabhaṅgāśramaṃ prati||40||

41satkṛtya śarabhaṅgaṃ sa daṇḍakāraṇyamāśritaḥ
|
nadīṃ godāvarīṃ ramyāmāśritya nyavasat tadā||41||

42vasatastasya rāmasya tataḥ śūrpaṇakhākṛtam
|
khareṇāsīnmahadvairaṃ janasthānanivāsinā||42||

43rakṣārthaṃ tāpasānāṃ tu rāghavo dharmavatsalaḥ
|
caturdaśasahasrāṇi jaghāna bhuvi rākṣasān||43||

44dūṣaṇaṃ ca kharaṃ caiva nihatya sumahābalau
|
cakre kṣemaṃ punardhīmān dharmāraṇyaṃ sa rāghavaḥ||44||

45hateṣu teṣu rakṣaḥsu tataḥ śūrpaṇakhā punaḥ
|
yayau nikṛttanāsoṣṭhī laṅkāṃ bhrāturniveśanam||45||

46tato rāvaṇamabhyetya rākṣasī duḥkhamūrchitā
|
papāta pādayorbhrātuḥ saṃśuṣkarudhirānanā||46||

47tāṃ tathā vikṛtāṃ dṛṣṭvā rāvaṇaḥ krodhamūrcchitaḥ
|
utpapātāsanāt kruddho dantairdantānupaspṛśan||47||

48svānamātyān visṛjyātha vivikte tāmuvāca saḥ
|
kenāsyevaṃ kṛtā bhadre māmacintyāvamanya ca||48||

49kaḥ śūlaṃ tīkṣṇamāsādya sarvagātrairniṣevate
|
kaḥ śirasyagnimādhāya viśvastaḥ svapate sukham||49||

50āśīviṣaṃ ghorataraṃ pādena spṛśatīha kaḥ
|
siṃhaṃ kesariṇaṃ kaśca daṃṣṭrāyāṃ spṛśya tiṣṭhati||50||

51ityevaṃ bruvatastasya srotobhyastejaso'rciṣaḥ
|
niścerurdahyato rātrau vṛkṣasyeva svarandhrataḥ||51||

52tasya tat sarvamācakhyau bhaginī rāmavikramam
|
kharadūṣaṇasaṃyuktaṃ rākṣasānāṃ parābhavam||52||

53sa niścitya tataḥ kṛtyaṃ svasāramupasāntvya ca
|
ūrdhvamācakrame rājā vidhāya nagare vidhim||53||

54trikūṭaṃ samatikramya kālaparvatameva ca
|
dadarśa makarāvāsaṃ gambhīrodaṃ mahodadhim||54||

55tamatītyātha gokarṇamabhyagacchaddaśānanaḥ
|
dayitaṃ sthānamavyagraṃ śūlapāṇermahātmanaḥ||55||

56tatrābhyagacchanmārīcaṃ pūrvāmātyaṃ daśānanaḥ
|
purā rāmabhayādeva tāpasyaṃ samupāśritam||56||
