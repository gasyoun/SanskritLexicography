Mahābhārata 3.310 (Āraṇyakaparvan)

1vaiśampāyana uvāca|
devarājamanuprāptaṃ brāhmaṇacchādmanā vṛtam
|
dṛṣṭvā svāgatamityāha na bubodhāsya mānasam||1||

2hiraṇyakaṇṭhīḥ pramadā grāmān vā bahugokulān
|
kiṃ dadānīti taṃ vipramuvācādhirathistataḥ||2||

3brāhmaṇa uvāca|
hiraṇyakaṇṭhyaḥ pramadā yaccānyat prītivardhanam
|
nāhaṃ dattamihecchāmi tadarthibhyaḥ pradīyatām||3||

4yadetatsahajaṃ varma kuṇḍale ca tavānagha
|
etadutkṛtya me dehi yadi satyavrato bhavān||4||

5etadicchāmyahaṃ kṣipraṃ tvayā dattaṃ parantapa
|
eṣa me sarvalābhānāṃ lābhaḥ paramako mataḥ||5||

6karṇa uvāca|
avaniṃ pramadā gāśca nivāpaṃ bahuvārṣikam
|
tapte vipra pradāsyāmi na tu varma sakuṇḍalam||6||

7vaiśampāyana uvāca|
evaṃ bahuvidhairvākyairyācyamānaḥ sa tu dvijaḥ
|
karṇena bharataśreṣṭha nānyaṃ varamayācata||7||

8sāntvitaśca yathāśakti pūjitaśca yathāvidhi
|
na cānyaṃ sa dvijaśreṣṭhaḥ kāmayāmāsa vai varam||8||

9yadā nānyaṃ pravṛṇute varaṃ vai dvijasattamaḥ
|
tadainamabravīdbhūyo rādheyaḥ prahasanniva||9||

10sahajaṃ varma me vipra kuṇḍale cāmṛtodbhave
|
tenāvadhyo'smi lokeṣu tato naitajjahāmyaham||10||

11viśālaṃ pṛthivīrājyaṃ kṣemaṃ nihatakaṇṭakam
|
pratigṛhṇīṣva mattastvaṃ sādhu brāhmaṇapuṅgava||11||

12kuṇḍalābhyāṃ vimukto'haṃ varmaṇā sahajena ca
|
gamanīyo bhaviṣyāmi śatrūṇāṃ dvijasattama||12||

13vaiśampāyana uvāca|
yadanyaṃ na varaṃ vavre bhagavān pākaśāsanaḥ
|
tataḥ prahasya karṇastaṃ punarityabravīdvacaḥ||13||

14vidito devadeveśa prāgevāsi mama prabho
|
na tu nyāyyaṃ mayā dātuṃ tava śakyaṃ vṛthā varam||14||

15tvaṃ hi deveśvaraḥ sākṣāttvayā deyo varo mama
|
anyeṣāṃ caiva bhūtānāmīśvaro hyasi bhūtakṛt||15||

16yadi dāsyāmi te deva kuṇḍale kavacaṃ tathā
|
vadhyatāmupayāsyāmi tvaṃ ca śakrāvahāsyatām||16||

17ttasmādvinimayaṃ kṛtvā kuṇḍale varma cottamam
|
harasva śakra kāmaṃ me na dadyāmahamanyathā||17||

18śakra uvāca|
vidito'haṃ raveḥ pūrvamāyāneva tavāntikam
|
tena te sarvamākhyātamevametanna saṃśayaḥ||18||

19kāmamastu tathā tāta tava karṇa yathecchasi
|
varjayitvā tu me vajraṃ pravṛṇīṣva yathecchasi||19||

20vaiśampāyana uvāca|
tataḥ karṇaḥ prahṛṣṭastu upasaṅgamya vāsavam
|
amoghāṃ śaktimabhyetya vavre sampūrṇamānasaḥ||20||

21karṇa uvāca|
varmaṇā kuṇḍalābhyāṃ ca śaktiṃ me dehi vāsava
|
amoghāṃ śatrusaṅghānāṃ ghātinīṃ pṛtanāmukhe||21||

22tataḥ sañcintya manasā muhūrtamiva vāsavaḥ
|
śaktyarthaṃ pṛthivīpāla karṇaṃ vākyamathābravīt||22||

23kuṇḍale me prayacchasva varma caiva śarīrajam
|
gṛhāṇa karṇa śaktiṃ tvamanena samayena ca||23||

24amoghā hanti śataśaḥ śatrūn mama karacyutā
|
punaśca pāṇimabhyeti mama daityān vinighnataḥ||24||

25seyaṃ tava karaprāptā hatvaikaṃ ripumūrjitam
|
garjantaṃ pratapantaṃ ca māmevaiṣyati sūtaja||25||

26karṇa uvāca|
ekamevāhamicchāmi ripuṃ hantuṃ mahāhave
|
garjantaṃ pratapantaṃ ca yato mama bhayaṃ bhavet||26||

27indra uvāca|
ekaṃ haniṣyasi ripuṃ garjantaṃ balinaṃ raṇe
|
tvaṃ tu yaṃ prārthayasyekaṃ rakṣyate sa mahātmanā||27||

28yamāhurvedavidvāṃso varārhamaparājitam
|
nārāyaṇamacintyaṃ ca tena kṛṣṇena rakṣyate||28||

29karṇa uvāca|
evamapyastu bhagavannekavīravadhe mama
|
amoghāṃ dehi me śaktiṃ yathā hanyāṃ pratāpinam||29||

30utkṛtya tu pradāsyāmi kuṇḍale kavacaṃ ca te
|
nikṛtteṣu tu gātreṣu na me bībhatsatā bhavet||30||

31indra uvāca|
na te bībhatsatā karṇa bhaviṣyati kathañcana
|
vraṇaścaiva na gātreṣu yastvaṃ nānṛtamicchasi||31||

32yādṛśaste piturvarṇastejaśca vadatāṃ vara
|
tādṛśenaiva varṇena tvaṃ karṇa bhavitā punaḥ||32||

33vidyamāneṣu śastreṣu yadyamoghāmasaṃśaye
|
pramatto mokṣyase cāpi tvayyevaiṣā patiṣyati||33||

34karṇa uvāca|
saṃśayaṃ paramaṃ prāpya vimokṣye vāsavīmimām
|
yathā māmāttha śakra tvaṃ satyametadbravīmi te||34||

35vaiśampāyana uvāca|
tataḥ śaktiṃ prajvalitāṃ pratigṛhya viśāmpate
|
śasraṃ gṛhītvā niśitaṃ sarvagātrāṇyakṛntata||35||

36tato devā mānavā dānavāśca nikṛntantaṃ karṇamātmānamevam
|
dṛṣṭvā sarve siṃhanādān praṇedurna hyasyāsīnmukhajo vai vikāraḥ||36||

37tato divyā dundubhayaḥ praṇeduḥ papātoccaiḥ puṣpavarṣaṃ ca divyas
|
dṛṣṭvā karṇaṃ śastrasaṅkṛttagātraṃ muhuścāpi smayamānaṃ nṛvīram||37||

38tataśchitvā kavacaṃ divyamaṅgāstathaivārdraṃ pradadau vāsavāya
|
tathotkṛtya pradadau kuṇḍale te karṇāttasmāt karmaṇā tena karṇaḥ||38||

39tataḥ śakraḥ prahasan vañcayitvā karṇaṃ loke yaśasā yojayitvā
|
kṛtaṃ kāryaṃ pāṇḍavānāṃ hi mene tataḥ paścāddivamevotpapāta||39||

40śrutvā karṇaṃ muṣitaṃ dhārtarāṣṭrā dīnāḥ sarve bhagnadarpā ivāsan
|
tāṃ cāvasthāṃ gamitaṃ sūtaputraṃ śrutvā pārthā jahṛṣuḥ kānanasthāḥ||40||

41janamejaya uvāca|
kvasthā vīrāḥ pāṇḍavāste babhūvuḥ kutaścaite śrutavantaḥ priyaṃ tat
|
kiṃ vā'kārṣurdvādaśe'bde vyatīte tanme sarve bhagavān vyākarotu||41||

42vaiśampāyana uvāca|
labdhvā kṛṣṇāṃ saindhavaṃ drāvayitvā vipraiḥ sārdhaṃ kāmyakāśuśramātte
|
mārkaṇḍeyācchrutavantaḥ purāṇaṃ devarṣīṇāṃ caritaṃ vistareṇa||42||
