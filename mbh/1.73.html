Mahābhārata 1.73 (Ādiparvan)

1duṣyanta uvāca|
suvyaktaṃ rājaputrī tvaṃ yathā kalyāṇi bhāṣase
|
bhāryā me bhava suśroṇi brūhi kiṃ karavāṇi te||1||

2suvarṇamālāṃ vāsāṃsi kuṇḍale parihāṭake
|
nānāpattanaje śubhre maṇiratne ca śobhane||2||

3āharāmi tavādyāhaṃ niṣkādīnyajināni ca
|
sarvaṃ rājyaṃ tavādyāstu bhāryā me bhava śobhane||3||

4gāndharveṇa ca māṃ bhīru vivāhenaihi sundari
|
vivāhānāṃ hi rambhoru gāndharvaḥ śreṣṭha: ucyate||4||

5śakuntalovāca
|
phalāhāro gato rājanpitā me ita āśramāt
|
muhūrtaṃ saṃpratīkṣasva sa māṃ tubhyaṃ pradāsyati||5||

6duṣyanta uvāca|
icchāmi tvāṃ varārohe bhajamānāmanindite
|
tvadarthaṃ māṃ sthitaṃ viddhi tvadgataṃ hi mano mama||6||

7ātmano bandhurātmaiva gatirātmaiva cātmanaḥ
|
ātmanaivavātmano dānaṃ kartumarhasi dharmataḥ||7||

8aṣṭāveva samāsena vivāhā dharmataḥ smṛtāḥ
|
brāhmo daivastathaivārṣaḥ prājāpatyastathāsuraḥ||8||

9gāndharvo rākṣasaścaiva paiśācaścāṣṭamaḥ smṛtaḥ
|
teṣāṃ dharmānyathāpūrvaṃ manuḥ svāyaṃbhuvo'bravīt||9||

10praśastāṃścaturaḥ pūrvānbrāhmaṇasyopadhāraya
|
ṣaḍānupūrvyā kṣatrasya viddhi dharmānanindite||10||

11rājñāṃ tu rākṣaropyukto viṭśūdreṣvāsuraḥ smṛtaḥ
|
pañcānāṃ tu trayo dharmyā adharmyo dvau smṛtāviha||11||

12paiśāca āsuraścaiva na kartavyau kadācana
|
anena vidhinā kāryau dharmasyaiṣā gatiḥ smṛtā||12||

13gāndharvarākṣasau kṣatre dharmyau tau mā viśaṅkithāḥ
|
pṛthagvā yadi vā miśrau kartavyau nātra saṃśayaḥ||13||

14sā tvaṃ mama sakāmasya sakāmā varavarṇinī
|
gāndharveṇa vivāhena bhāryā bhavitumarhasi||14||

15śakuntalovāca
|
yadi dharmapathastveṣa yadi cātmā prabhurmama
|
pradāne pauravaśreṣṭha śṛṇu me samayaṃ prabho||15||

16satyaṃ me pratijānīhi yathā vakṣyāmyahaṃ rahaḥ
|
mayi jāyeta yaḥ putraḥ sa bhavettvadanantaraḥ||16||

17yuvarājo mahārāja satyametadbravīmi te
|
yadyetadevaṃ duṣyanta astu me saṅgamastvayā||17||

18vaiśaṃpāyana uvāca|
evamastviti tāṃ rājā pratyuvācā'vicārayan
|
api ca tvāṃ hi neṣyāmi nagaraṃ svaṃ śucismite||18||

19yathā tvamarhā suśroṇi manyase tadbravīmi te
|
evamuktvā sa rājarṣistāmaninditagāminīm||19||

20jagrāha vidhivatpāṇāvuvāsa ca tayā saha
|
viśvāsya caināṃ sa prāyādabravīcca punaḥ punaḥ||20||

21preṣayiṣye tavārthāya vāhinīṃ caturaṅgiṇīm
|
tayā tvānāyayuṣyāmi nivāsaṃ svaṃ śucismite||21||

22vaiśaṃpāyana uvāca|
iti tasyāḥ pratiśrutya sa nṛpo janamejaya
|
manasā cintayanprāyātkāśyapaṃ prati pārthivaḥ||22||

23bhagavāṃstapasā yuktaḥ śrutvā kiṃ nu kariṣyati
|
evaṃ sa cintayanneva praviveśa svakaṃ puram||23||

24muhūrtayāte tasmiṃstu kaṇvo'pyāśramamāgamat
|
śakuntalā ca pitaraṃ hriyā nopajagāma tam||24||

25vijñāyātha ca tāṃ kaṇvo divyajñāno mahātapāḥ
|
uvāca bhagavānprītaḥ paśyandivyena cakṣuṣā||25||

26tvayā'dya bhadre rahasi māmanādṛtya yaḥ kṛtaḥ
|
puṃsā saha samāyogo na sa dharmopaghātakaḥ||26||

27kṣatriyasya hi gāndharvo vivāhaḥ śreṣṭha ucyate
|
sakāmāyāḥ sakāmena nimantro rahasi smṛtaḥ||27||

28dharmātmā ca mahātmā ca duṣyantaḥ puruṣottamaḥ
|
abhyāgaccha: patiṃ yatvaṃ bhajamānaṃ śakuntale||28||

29mahātmā janitā loke putrastava mahābalaḥ
|
ya imāṃ sāgarāpāṅgīṃ kṛtsnāṃ bhokṣyati medinīm||29||

30paraṃ cābhiprayātasya cakraṃ tasya mahātmanaḥ
|
bhaviṣyatyapratihataṃ satataṃ cakravartinaḥ||30||

31tataḥ prakṣālya pādau sā viśrāntaṃ munimabravīt
|
vinidhāya tato bhāraṃ sannidhāya phalāni ca ||31||

32śakuntalovāca
|
mayā patirvṛto rājā duṣyantaḥ puruṣottamaḥ
|
tasmai sasacivāya tvaṃ prasādaṃ kartumarhasi||32||

33kaṇva uvāca|
prasanna eva tasyāhaṃ tvatkṛte varavarṇini
|
gṛhāṇa ca varaṃ mattastvaṃ śubhe yadabhīpsitam||33||

34vaiśaṃpāyana uvāca|
tato dharmiṣṭhatāṃ vavre rājyāccaskhalanaṃ tathā
|
śakuntalā pauravāṇāṃ duṣyantahitakāmyayā||34||
