Mahābhārata 12.62 (Śāntiparvan)

1yudhiṣṭhira uvāca|
śivān sukhān mahodarkānahiṃsrāllokasammatān
|
brūhi dharmānsukhopāyānmadvidhānāṃ sukhāvahān||1||

2bhīṣma uvāca|
brāhmaṇasya tu catvārastvāśramā vihitāḥ prabho
|
varṇāstān nānuvartante trayo bhāratasattama||2||

3uktāni karmāṇi bahūni rājan svargyāṇi rājanyaparāyaṇāni
|
nemāni dṛṣṭāntavidhau smṛtāni kṣātre hi sarvaṃ vihitaṃ yathāvat||3||

4kṣātrāṇi vaiśyāni ca sevamānaḥ śaudrāṇi karmāṇi ca brāhmaṇaḥ san
|
asmi~lloke nindito mandacetāḥ pare ca loke nirayaṃ prayāti||4||

5yā saṃjñā vihitā loke dāse śuni vṛke paśau
|
vikarmaṇi sthite vipre saiva saṃjñā ca pāṇḍava||5||

6ṣaṭkarmasampravṛttasya āśrameṣu caturṣvapi
|
sarvadharmopapannasya saṃvṛtasya kṛtātmanaḥ||6||

7brāhmaṇasya viśuddhasya tapasyabhiratasya ca
|
nirāśiṣo vadānyasya lokā hyakṣarasammitāḥ||7||

8yo yasminkurute karma yādṛśaṃ yena yatra ca
|
tādṛśaṃ tādṛśenaiva saguṇaṃ pratipadyate||8||

9vṛddhyā kṛṣivaṇiktvena jīvasaṃjīvanena ca
|
vettumarhasi rājendra svādhyāyagaṇitaṃ mahat||9||

10kālasaṃcodito lokaḥ kālaparyāyaniścitaḥ
|
uttamādhamamadhyāni karmāṇi kurute'vaśaḥ||10||

11antavanti pradhānāni purā śreyaskarāṇi ca
|
svakarmanirato loke hyakṣaraḥ sarvatomukhaḥ||11||
