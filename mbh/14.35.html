Mahābhārata 14.35 (Āśvamedhikaparvan)

1arjuna uvāca|
brahma yatparamaṃ jñeyaṃ tanme vyākhyātumarhasi
|
bhavato hi prasādena sūkṣme me ramate matiḥ||1||

2vāsudeva uvāca|
atrāpyudāharantīmamitihāsaṃ purātanam
|
saṃvādaṃ mokṣasaṃyuktaṃ śiṣyasya guruṇā saha||2||

3kaścidbrāhmaṇamāsīnamācāryaṃ saṃśitavratam
|
śiṣyaḥ papraccha medhāvī kiṃsvicchreyaḥ parantapa||3||

4bhagavantaṃ prapanno'haṃ niḥśreyasaparāyaṇaḥ
|
yāce tvāṃ śirasā vipra yadbrūyāṃ brūhi tanmama||4||

5tamevaṃvādinaṃ pārtha śiṣyaṃ gururuvāca ha
|
sarve tu te pravakṣyāmi yatra vai saṃśayo dvija||5||

6ityuktaḥ sa kuruśreṣṭha guruṇā guruvatsalaḥ
|
prāñjaliḥ paripapraccha yattacchṛṇu mahāmate||6||

7śiṣya uvāca|
kutaścāhaṃ kutaśca tvaṃ tatsatyaṃ brūhi yatparam
|
kuto jātāni bhūtāni sthāvarāṇi carāṇi ca||7||

8kena jīvanti bhūtāni teṣāmāyuśca kiṃ param
|
kiṃ satyaṃ kiṃ tapo vipra ke guṇāḥ sadbhirīritāḥ||8||

9ke panthānaḥ śivāśca syuḥ kiṃ sukhaṃ kiñca duṣkṛtam
|
etānme bhagavanpraścān yāthātathyena suvrata||9||

10vaktumarhasi viprarṣe yathāvadiha tattvataḥ
|
tvadanyaḥ kaścana praścānetānvaktumihārhati||10||

11brūhi dharmavidāṃ śreṣṭha paraṃ kautūhalaṃ mama
|
mokṣadharmārthakuśalo bhavā~llokeṣu gīyate||11||

12sarvasaṃśayasañcchettā tvadanyo na ca vidyate
|
saṃsārabhīravaścaiva mokṣakāmāstathā vayam||12||

13vāsudeva uvāca|
tasmai sampratipannāya yathāvatparipṛcchate
|
śiṣyāya guṇayuktāya śāntāya priyavartine||13||

14chāyābhūtāya dāntāya yatate brahmacāriṇe
|
tānpraśnānabravītpārtha medhāvī sa dhṛtavrataḥ
|
guruḥ kurukulaśreṣṭha samyaksarvānarindama||14||

15gururuvāca|
brahmaṇoktamidaṃ sarvamṛṣipravarasevitam
|
vedavidyāṃ samāśritya tattvabhūtārthabhāvanam||15||

16jñānaṃ tveva paraṃ vidyaḥ saṃnyāsaṃ tapa uttamam
|
yastu veda nirābādhaṃ jñānatattvaṃ viniścayāt
|
sarvabhūtasthamātmānaṃ sa sarvagatiriṣyate||16||

17yo vidvānsahasaṃvāsaṃ vivāsaṃ caiva paśyati
|
tathaivaikatvanānātve sa duḥkhātparimucyate||17||

18yo na kāmayate kiñcinna kiñcidabhimanyate
|
ihalokastha evaiṣa brahmabhūyāya kalpate||18||

19pradhānaguṇatattvajñaḥ sarvabhūtavidhānavit
|
nirmamo nirahaṅkāro mucyate nātra saṃśayaḥ||19||

20avyaktabījaprabhavo buddhiskandhamayo mahān
|
mahāhaṅkāraviṭapa indriyāṅkurakoṭaraḥ||20||

21mahābhūtaviśeṣaśca viśeṣapratiśākhavān
|
sadāparṇaḥ sadāpuṣpaḥ sadā śubhaphalodayaḥ||21||

22ajīvaḥ sarvabhūtānāṃ brahmabījaḥ sanātanaḥ
|
etajjñātvā ca tattvāni jñānena paramāsinā||22||

23chittvā cāmaratāṃ prāpya jahāti mṛtyujanmanī
|
bhūtabhavyabhaviṣyādi dharmakāmārthaniścayam
|
siddhasaṅghaparijñātaṃ purākalpaṃ sanātanam||23||

24pravakṣye'haṃ mahāprājña padamuttamamadya te
|
buddhvā yadiha saṃsiddhā bhavantīha manīṣiṇaḥ||24||

25upagamyarṣayaḥ pūrvaṃ jijñāsantaḥ parasparam
|
prajāpatibharadvājau gautamo bhārgavastathā||25||

26vasiṣṭhaḥ kaśyapaścaiva viśvāmitro'trireva ca
|
mārgānsarvānparikramya pariśrāntāḥ svakarmabhiḥ||26||

27ṛṣimāṅgirasaṃ vṛddhaṃ puraskṛtya tu te dvijāḥ
|
dadṛśurbrahmabhavane brahmāṇaṃ vītakalmaṣam||27||

28taṃ praṇamya mahātmānaṃ sukhāsīnaṃ maharṣayaḥ
|
papracchurvinayopetā naiḥśreyasamidaṃ param||28||

29kathaṃ karma kriyātsādhu kathaṃ mucyeta kilbiṣāt
|
ke no mārgāḥ śivāśca syuḥ kiṃ satyaṃ kiṃ ca dupkṛtam||29||

30ko cobhau karmaṇāṃ mārgau prāpnuyurdakṣiṇottarau
|
pralayaṃ cāpavargaṃ ca bhūtānāṃ prabhavāpyayau||30||

31ityuktaḥ sa muniśreṣṭhairyadāha prapitāmahaḥ
|
tatte'haṃ sampravakṣyāmi śṛṇu śiṣya yathāgamam||31||

32brāhmaṇa uvāca
satyādbhūtāni jātāni sthāvarāṇi carāṇi ca
|
tapasā tāni jīvanti iti tadvitta suvratāḥ
|
svāṃ yoniṃ samatikramya vartante stena karmaṇā||32||

33satyaṃ hi guṇasaṃyuktaṃ niyataṃ pañcalakṣaṇam||33||

34brahma satyaṃ tapaḥ satyaṃ satyaṃ caiva prajāpatiḥ
|
satyādbhūtāni jātāni satyaṃ bhūtamayaṃ jagat||34||

35tasmātsatyamayā viprā nityaṃ yogaparāyaṇāḥ
|
atītakrodhasantāpā niyatā dharmasevina:||35||

36anyonyaniyatānvaidyāndharmasetupravartakān
|
tānahaṃ sampravakṣyāmi śāśvatāllokabhāvanān||36||

37cāturvidyaṃ tathā varṇāṃścāturāśramikān pṛthak
|
dharmamekaṃ catuṣpādaṃ nityamāhurmanīṣiṇaḥ||37||

38panthānaṃ vaḥ pravakṣyāmi śivaṃ kṣemakaraṃ dvijāḥ
|
niyataṃ brahmabhāvāya gataṃ pūrvaṃ manīṣibhiḥ||38||

39gadantastaṃ mayādyeha panthānaṃ durvidaṃ param
|
nibodhata mahābhāgā nikhilena paraṃ padam||39||

40brahmacārikamevāhurāśramaṃ prathamaṃ padam
|
gārhasthyaṃ tu dvitīyaṃ syād vānaprasthamataḥ param
|
tataḥ paraṃ tu vijñeyamadhyātmaṃ paramaṃ padam||40||

41jyotirākāśamādityo vāyurindraḥ prajāpatiḥ
|
nopaiti yāvadadhyātma tāvadetān na paśyati||41||

42tasyopāyaṃ pravakṣyāmi purastāt taṃ nibodhata
|
phalamūlānilabhujāṃ munīnāṃ vasatāṃ vane||42||

43vānaprasthaṃ dvijātīnāṃ trayāṇāmupadiśyate
|
sarveṣāmeva varṇānāṃ gārhasthyaṃ tad vidhīyate||43||

44śraddhālakṣaṇamityevaṃ dharmaṃ dhīrāḥ pracakṣate
|
ityevaṃ devayānā vaḥ panthānaḥ parikīrtitāḥ
|
sadbhiradhyāsitā dhīraiḥ karmabhirdharmasetavaḥ||44||

45eteṣāṃ pṛthagadhyāste yo dharmaṃ saṃśitavrataḥ
|
kālātpaśyati bhūtānāṃ sadaiva prabhavāpyayau||45||

46atastattvāni vakṣyāmi yāthātathyena hetunā
|
viṣayasthāni sarvāṇi vartamānāni bhāgaśaḥ||46||

47mahānātmā tathāvyaktamahaṅkārastathaiva ca
|
indriyāṇi daśaikaṃ ca mahābhūtāni pañca ca||47||

48viśeṣāḥ pañcabhūtānāmiti sargaḥ sanātanaḥ
|
caturviṃśatirekā ca tattvasaṅkhyā prakīrtitā||48||

49tattvānāmatha yo veda sarveṣāṃ prabhavāpyayau
|
sa dhīraḥ sarvabhūteṣu na mohamadhigacchati||49||

50tattvāni yo vedayate yathātathaṃ guṇāṃśca sarvānakhilāṃśca devatāḥ
|
vidhūtapāpmā pratimucya bandhanaṃ sa sarvalokānamalān samaśnute||50||
