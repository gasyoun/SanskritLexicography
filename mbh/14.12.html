Mahābhārata 14.12 (Āśvamedhikaparvan)

1vāsudeva uvāca|
dvividho jāyate vyādhiḥ śārīro mānasastathā
|
parasparaṃ tayorjanma nirdvandvaṃ nopapadyate||1||

2śarīre jāyate vyādhiḥ śārīraḥ sa nigadyate
|
mānase jāyate vyādhirmānasastu nigadyate||2||

3śītoṣṇe caiva vāyuśca guṇā rājan śarīrajāḥ
|
teṣāṃ guṇānāṃ sāmyaṃ cet tadāhuḥ svasthalakṣaṇam||3||

4uṣṇena bādhyate śītaṃ śītenoṣṇaṃ ca bādhyate
|
sattvaṃ rajastamaśceti traya ātmaguṇāḥ smṛtāḥ||4||

5teṣāṃ guṇānāṃ sāmyaṃ cet tadāhuḥ svasthalakṣaṇam
|
teṣāmanyatamotseke vidhānamupadiśyate||5||

6harṣeṇa bādhyate śoko harṣaḥ śokena bādhyate
|
kaścidduḥkhe vartamānaḥ sukhasya smartumicchati
|
kaścitsukhe vartamāno duḥkhasya smartumicchati||6||

7sa tvaṃ na duḥkhī duḥkhasya na sukhī susukhasya ca
|
smartumicchasi kaunteya kimanyadduḥkhavibhramāt||7||

8athavā te svabhāvo'yaṃ yena pārthāvakṛṣyase
|
dṛṣṭvā sabhāgatāṃ kṛṣṇāmekavastrāṃ rajasvalām
|
miṣatāṃ pāṇḍaveyānāṃ na tasya smartumicchasi||8||

9pravrājanaṃ ca nagarādajinaiśca vivāsanam
|
mahāraṇyanivāsaśca na tasya smartumicchasi||9||

10jaṭāsurātparikleśaścitrasenena cāhavaḥ
|
saindhavācca parikleśo na tasya smartumicchasi||10||

11punarajñātacaryāyāṃ kīcakena padā vadhaḥ
|
yājñasenyāstathā pārtha na tasya smartumicchasi||11||

12yacca te droṇabhīṣmābhyāṃ yuddhamāsīdarindama
|
manasaikena yoddhavyaṃ tatte yuddhamupasthitam||12||

13tasmādabhyupagantavyaṃ yuddhāya bharatarṣabha
|
paramavyaktarūpasya pāraṃ yuktyā svakarmabhiḥ||13||

14yatra naiva śaraiḥ kāryaṃ na bhṛtyairna ca bandhubhiḥ
|
ātmanaikena yoddhavyaṃ tat te yuddhamupasthitam||14||

15tasminnanirjite yuddhe kāmavasthāṃ gamiṣyasi
|
etajjñātvā tu kaunteya kṛtakṛtyo bhaviṣyasi||15||

16etāṃ buddhiṃ viniścitya bhūtānāmāgatiṃ gatim
|
pitṛpaitāmahe vṛtte śādhi rājyaṃ yathocitam||16||
