Mahābhārata 14.60 (Āśvamedhikaparvan)

1vāsudeva uvāca|
śrutavānasmi vārṣṇeya saṅgrāmaṃ paramādbhutam
|
narāṇāṃ vadatāṃ tatra kathaṃ vā teṣu nityaśaḥ||1||

2tvaṃ tu pratyakṣadarśī ca rūpajñaśca mahābhujaḥ
|
tasmātprabrūhi saṅgrāmaṃ yāthātathyena me'nagha||2||

3yathā tadabhavadyuddhaṃ pāṇḍavānāṃ mahātmanām
|
bhīṣmakarṇakṛpadroṇaśalyādibhiranuttamam||3||

4anyeṣāṃ kṣatriyāṇāṃ ca kṛtāstrāṇāmanekaśaḥ
|
nānāveṣākṛtimatāṃ nānādeśanivāsinām||4||

5vaiśampāyana uvāca|
ityuktaḥ puṇḍarīkākṣaḥ pitrā mātustadantike
|
śaśaṃsa kuruvīrāṇāṃ saṅgrāme nidhanaṃ yathā||5||

6vāsudeva uvāca|
atyuddhatāni karmāṇi kṣatriyāṇāṃ mahātmanām
|
bahulatvānna saṅkhyātuṃ śakyānyabdaśatairapi||6||

7prādhānyatastu gadataḥ samāsenaiva me śṛṇu
|
karmāṇi pṛthivīśānāṃ yathāvadamaradhute||7||

8bhīṣmaḥ senāpatirabhūdekādaśacabhūpatiḥ
|
kauravyaḥ kauravendrāṇāṃ devānāmiva vāsavaḥ||8||

9śikhaṇḍī pāṇḍuputrāṇāṃ netā saptacamūpatiḥ
|
babhūva rakṣito dhīmānśrīmatā savyasācinā||9||

10teṣāṃ tadabhavadyuddhaṃ daśāhāni mahātmanām
|
kurūṇāṃ pāṇḍavānāṃ ca sumahallomaharṣaṇam||10||

11tata: śikhaṇḍī gāṅgeyaṃ yudhyamānaṃ mahāhave
|
jaghāna bahubhirbāṇaiḥ saha gāṇḍīvadhanvanā||11||

12akarotsa tataḥ kālaṃ śaratalpagato muniḥ
|
ayanaṃ dakṣiṇaṃ hitvā samprāpte cottarāyaṇe||12||

13tataḥ senāpatirabhūddroṇo'straviduṣāṃ varaḥ
|
pravīraḥ kauravendrasya kāvyo daityapateriva||13||

14akṣauhiṇībhiḥ śiṣṭābhirnavabhirdvijasattamaḥ
|
saṃvṛtaḥ samaraślāghī guptaḥ kṛpavṛṣādibhiḥ||14||

15dhṛṣṭadyumnastvabhūnnetā pāṇḍavānāṃ mahāstravit
|
gupto bhīmena medhāvī mitreṇa varuṇo yathā||15||

16sa ca senāparivṛto droṇaprepsurmahāmanāḥ
|
piturnikārānsaṃsmṛtya raṇe karmākaronmahat||16||

17tasmiṃste pṛthivīpālā droṇapārṣatasaṅgare
|
nānādigāgatā vīrāḥ prāyaśo nidhanaṃ gatāḥ||17||

18dināni pañca tadyuddhamabhūtparamadāruṇam
|
tato droṇaḥ pariśrānto dhṛṣṭadyumnavaśaṃ gataḥ||18||

19tataḥ senāpatirabhūtkarṇo dauryodhane bale
|
akṣauhiṇībhiḥ śiṣṭābhirvṛtaḥ pañcabhirāhave||19||

20tisrastu pāṇḍuputrāṇāṃ camvo bībhatsupālitāḥ
|
hatapravīrabhūyiṣṭhā babhūvuḥ samavasthitāḥ||20||

21tataḥ pārthaṃ samāsādya pataṅga iva pāvakam
|
pañcatvamagamatsautirdvitīye'hani dāruṇaḥ||21||

22hate karṇe tu kauravyā nirutsāhā hataujasaḥ
|
akṣauhiṇībhistisṛbhirmadreśaṃ paryavārayan||22||

23hatavāhanabhūyiṣṭhāḥ pāṇḍavā'pi yudhiṣṭhiram
|
akṣauhiṇyā nirutsāhāḥ śiṣṭayā paryavārayan||23||

24avadhīnmadrarājānaṃ kururājo yudhiṣṭhiraḥ
|
tasmiṃstadārdhadivase kṛtvā karma suduṣkaram||24||

25hate śalye tu śakuniṃ sahadevo mahātmanāḥ
|
āhartāraṃ kalestasya jaghānāmitavikramaḥ||25||

26nihate śakunau rājā dhārtarāṣṭraḥ sudurmanāḥ
|
apākrāmadgadāpāṇirhatabhūyiṣṭhasainikaḥ||26||

27tamanvadhāvatsaṅkruddho bhīmasenaḥ pratāpavān
|
hṛde dvaipāyane cāpi salilasthaṃ dadarśa tam||27||

28hataśiṣṭena sainyena samantātparyavārya tam
|
athopaviviśurhṛṣṭā hṛdasthaṃ pañca pāṇḍavāḥ||28||

29vigāhya salilaṃ tvāśu vāgbāṇairbhṛśavikṣataḥ
|
utthāya sa gadāpāṇiryuddhāya samupasthitaḥ||29||

30tataḥ sa nihato rājā dhārtarāṣṭro mahāraṇe
|
bhīmasenena vikramya paśyatāṃ pṛthivīkṣitām||30||

31tatastatpāṇḍavaṃ sainyaṃ prasuptaṃ śibire niśi
|
nihataṃ droṇaputreṇa piturvadhamamṛṣyatā||31||

32hataputrā hatabalā hatamitrā mayā saha
|
yuyudhānasahāyena pañca śiṣṭāstu pāṇḍavāḥ||32||

33sahaiva kṛpabhojābhyāṃ drauṇiryuddhādamucyata
|
yuyutsuścāpi kauravyo muktaḥ pāṇḍavasaṃśrayāt||33||

34nihate kauravendre tu sānubandhe suyodhane
|
viduraḥ sañjayaścaiva dharmarājamupasthitau||34||

35evaṃ tadabhavadyuddhamahānyaṣṭādaśa prabho
|
yatra te pṛthivīpālā nihatāḥ svargamāvasan||35||

36vaiśampāyana uvāca|
śṛṇvatāṃ tu mahārāja kathāṃ tāṃ lomaharṣaṇām
|
duḥkhaśokaparikleśā vṛṣṇīnāmabhavaṃstadā||36||
