Mahābhārata 2.41 (Sabhāparvan)

1śiśupāla uvāca|
bibhīṣikābhirbahvībhirbhīṣayansarvapārthivān
|
na vyapatrapase kasmādvṛddhaḥ sankulapāṃsanaḥ||1||

2yuktametattṛtīyāyāṃ prakṛtau vartatā tvayā
|
vaktuṃ dharmādapetārthaṃ tvaṃ hi sarvakurūttamaḥ||2||

3nāvi nauriva sambaddhā yathāndho vāndhamanviyāt
|
tathābhūtā hi kauravyā yeṣāṃ bhīṣma tvamagraṇīḥ||3||

4pūtanāghātapūrvāṇi karmāṇyasya viśeṣataḥ
|
tvayā kīrtayatā'smākaṃ bhūyaḥ pravyathitaṃ manaḥ||4||

5avaliptasya mūrkhasya keśavaṃ stotumicchataḥ
|
kathaṃ bhīṣma na te jihvā śatadheyaṃ vidīryate||5||

6yatra kutsā prayoktavyā bhīṣma bālatarairnaraiḥ
|
tamimaṃ jñānavṛddhaḥ sangopaṃ saṃstotumicchasi||6||

7yadyanena hatā bālye śakuniścitramatra kim
|
tau vā'śvavṛṣabhau bhīṣṇa yau na yuddhaviśāradau||7||

8cetanārahitaṃ kāṣṭhaṃ yadyanena nipātitam
|
pādena śakaṭaṃ bhīṣṇa tatra kiṃ kṛtamadbhutam||8||

9valmīkamātraḥ saptāhaṃ yadyanena dhṛto'calaḥ
|
tadā govardhano bhīṣma na taccitraṃ mataṃ mama||9||

10bhuktametena bahvannaṃ krīḍatā nagamūrdhani
|
iti te bhīṣma śṛṇvānāḥ pare vismayamāgatāḥ||10||

11yasya cānena dharmajña bhuktamannaṃ balīyasaḥ
|
sa cānena hataḥ kaṃsa ityetanna mahādbhutam||11||

12na te śrutamidaṃ bhīṣma nūnaṃ kathayatāṃ satām
|
yadvakṣye tvāmadharmajñaṃ vākyaṃ kurukulādhama||12||

13strīṣu goṣu na śastrāṇi pātayedbrāhmaṇeṣu ca
|
yasya cānnāni bhuñjīta yatra ca syātpratiśrayaḥ||13||

14iti santo'nuśāsanti sajjanaṃ dharmiṇaḥ sadā
|
bhīṣma loke hi tatsarvaṃ vitathaṃ tvayi dṛśyate||14||

15jñānavṛddhaṃ ca vṛddhaṃ ca bhūyāṃsaṃ keśavaṃ mama
|
ajānata ivākhyāsi saṃstuvankauravādhama||15||

16goghraḥ strīghnaśca sanbhīṣma tvadvākyādyadi pūjyate
|
evambhūtaśca yo bhīṣma kathaṃ saṃstavamarhati||16||

17asau matimatāṃ śreṣṭho ya eṣa jagataḥ prabhuḥ
|
sambhāvayati cāpyevaṃ tvadvākyācca janārdanaḥ
|
evametatsarvamiti tatsarvaṃ vitathaṃ dhruvam||17||

18na gāthā gāthinaṃ śāsti bahu cedapi gāyati
|
prakṛtiṃ yānti bhūtāni kuliṅgaśakuniryathā||18||

19nūnaṃ prakṛtireṣā te jaghanyā nātra saṃśayaḥ
|
ataḥ pāpīyasī caiṣā pāṇḍavānāmapīṣyate||19||

20yeṣāmarcyatamaḥ kṛṣṇastvaṃ ca yeṣāṃ pradarśakaḥ
|
dharmavāṃstvamadharmajñaḥ satāṃ mārgādavaplutaḥ||20||

21ko hi dharmiṇamātmānaṃ jānanjñānavidāṃ varaḥ
|
kuryādyathā tvayā bhīṣma kṛtaṃ dharmamavekṣatā||21||

22cettvaṃ dharmaṃ vijānāsi yadi prājñā matistava
|
anyakāmā hi dharmajñā kanyakā prājñamāninā
|
ambā nāmeti bhadraṃ te kathaṃ sā'pahṛtā tvayā||22||

23tāṃ tvayā'pi hṛtāṃ bhīṣma kanyāṃ naiṣitavānyataḥ
|
bhrātā vicitravīryaste satāṃ mārgamanuṣṭhitaḥ||23||

24dārayoryasya cānyena miṣataḥ prājñamāninaḥ
|
tava jātānyapatyāni sajjanācarite pathi||24||

25ko hi dharmo'sti te bhīṣama brahmacaryamidaṃ vṛthā
|
yaddhārayasi mohādvā klībatvādvā na saṃśayaḥ||25||

26na tvaṃ tava dharmajña paśyāmyupacaraṃ kvacit
|
na hi te sevitā vṛddhā ya evaṃ dharmamabravīḥ||26||

27iṣṭaṃ dattamadhītaṃ ca yajñāśca bahudakṣiṇāḥ
|
sarvametadapatyasya kalāṃ nārhanti ṣoḍaśīm||27||

28vratopavāsairbahubhiḥ kṛtaṃ bhavati bhīṣma yat
|
sarvaṃ tadanapatyasya moghaṃ bhavati niścayāt||28||

29so'napatyaśca vṛddhaśca mithyādharmānusārakaḥ
|
haṃsavattvamapīdānīṃ jñātibhyaḥ prāpnuyā vadham||29||

30evaṃ hi kathayantyanye narā jñānavidaḥ purā
|
bhīṣma yattadahaṃ samyagvakṣyāmi tava śṛṇvataḥ||30||

31vṛddhaḥ kila samudrānte kaściddhaṃso'bhavatpurā
|
dharmavāganyathāvṛttaḥ pakṣiṇaḥ so'nuśāsti ca||31||

32dharmaṃ carata mā'dharmamiti tasya vacaḥ kila
|
pakṣiṇaḥ śuśruvurbhīṣma satataṃ dharmavādinaḥ||32||

33athāsya bhakṣyamājahruḥ samudrajalacāriṇaḥ
|
aṇḍajā bhīṣma tasyānye dharmārthamiti śuśruma||33||

34te ca tasya samabhyāśe nikṣipyāṇḍāni sarvaśaḥ
|
samudrāmbhasyamajjanta caranto bhīṣma pakṣiṇaḥ
|
teṣāmaṇḍāni sarveṣāṃ bhakṣayāmāsa pāpakṛt||34||

35sa haṃsaḥ sampramattānāmapramattaḥ svakarmaṇi
|
tataḥ prakṣīyamāṇeṣu teṣu teṣvaṇḍajo'paraḥ
|
aśaṅkata mahāprājñaḥ sa kadāciddadarśa ha||35||

36tataḥ sa kathayāmāsa dṛṣṭvā haṃsasya kilbiṣam
|
teṣāṃ paramaduḥkhārtaḥ sa pakṣī sarvapakṣiṇām||36||

37tataḥ pratyakṣato dṛṣṭvā pakṣiṇaste samīpagāḥ
|
nijaghnastaṃ tadā haṃsaṃ mithyāvṛttaṃ kurūdvaha||37||

38te tvāṃ haṃsasadharmāṇamapīme vasudhādhipāḥ
|
nihanyurbhīṣma saṅkruddhāḥ pakṣiṇastaṃ yathāṇḍajam||38||

39gāthāmapyatra gāyanti ye purāṇavido janāḥ
|
bhīṣma yāṃ tāṃ ca te samyakvathayiṣyāmi bhārata||39||

40antarātmanyabhihate rauṣi patrarathāśuci
|
aṇḍabhakṣaṇakarmaitattava vācamatīyate||40||
