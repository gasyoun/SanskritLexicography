Mahābhārata 7.194 (Droṇaparvan)

1dhṛtarāṣṭra uvāca|
adharmeṇa hataṃ śrutvā dhṛṣṭadyumnena sañjaya
|
brahmaṇaṃ pitaraṃ vṛddhamaśvatthāmā kimabravīt||1||

2mānavaṃ vāruṇāgneyaṃ brahmamastraṃ ca vīryavān
|
aindraṃ nārāyaṇaṃ caiva yasminnityaṃ pratiṣṭhitam||2||

3tamadharmeṇa dharmiṣṭaṃ dhṛṣṭadyumnena saṃyuge
|
śrutvā nihatamācāryaṃ so'śvatthāmā kimabravīt||3||

4yena rāmādavāpyeha dhanurvedaṃ mahātmanā
|
proktānyastrāṇi divyāni putrāya guṇakāṅkṣiṇā||4||

5ekameva hi loke'sminnātmano guṇavattaram
|
icchanti puruṣāḥ putraṃ loke nānyaṃ kathañcana||5||

6ācāryāṇāṃ bhavantyeva rahasyāni mahātmanām
|
tāni putrāya vā dadyuḥ śiṣyāyānugatāya vā||6||

7sa śiṣyaḥ prāpya tatsarvaṃ saviśeṣaṃ ca sañjaya
|
śūraḥ śāradvatīputraḥ saṅkhye droṇādanantaraḥ||7||

8rāmasya tu samaḥ śastre puraṃdarasamo yudhi
|
kārtavīryasamo vīrye bṛhaspatisamo matau||8||

9mahīdharasamaḥ sthairye tejasā'gnisamo yuvā
|
samudra iva gāmbhīrye krodhe cāśīviṣopamaḥ||9||

10sa rathī prathamo loke dṛḍhadhanvā jitaklamaḥ
|
śīghro'nila ivākrande caran kruddha ivāntakaḥ||10||

11asyatā yena saṃgrāme dharaṇyabhinipīḍitā
|
yo na vyathati saṃgrāme vīraḥ satyaparākramaḥ||11||

12vedasnāto vratasnāto dhanurvede ca pāragaḥ
|
mahodadhirivākṣobhyo rāmo dāśarathiryathā||12||

13tamadharmeṇa dharmiṣṭhaṃ dhṛṣṭadyumnena saṃyuge
|
śrutvā nihatamācāryamaśvatthāmā kimabravīt||13||

14dhṛṣṭadyumnasya yo mṛtyuḥ sṛṣṭastena mahātmanā
|
yathā droṇasya pāñcālyo yajñasenasuto'bhavat||14||

15taṃ nṛśaṃsena pāpena krūreṇādīrghadarśinā
|
śrutvā nihatamācāryamaśvatthāmā kimabravīt||15||
