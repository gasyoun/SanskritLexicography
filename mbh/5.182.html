Mahābhārata 5.182 (Udyogaparvan)

1bhīṣma uvāca|
tataḥ prabhāte rājendra sūrye vimalatāṃ gate
|
bhārgavasya mayā sārdhaṃ punaryuddhamavartata ||1||

2tato'bhrānte rathe tiṣṭhan rāmaḥ praharatāṃ varaḥ
|
vavarṣa śarajālāni mayi megha ivācale ||2||

3tataḥ sūto mama suhṛccharavarṣeṇa tāḍitaḥ
|
apayāto rathopasthānmano mama viṣādayan ||3||

4tataḥ sūto mamātyarthaṃ kaśmalaṃ prāviśanmahat
|
pṛthivyāṃ ca śarādhātānnipapāta mumoha ca ||4||

5tataḥ suto'jahāt prāṇān rāmabāṇaprapīḍitaḥ
|
muhūrtādiva rājendra māṃ ca bhīrāviśattadā ||5||

6tataḥ sūte hate tasmin kṣipatastasya me śarān
|
pramattamanaso rāmaḥ prāhiṇonmṛtyusaṃmitam ||6||

7tataḥ sūtavyasaninaṃ viplutaṃ māṃ sa bhārgavaḥ
|
śareṇābhyahanadgāḍhaṃ vikṛṣya balavaddhanuḥ ||7||

8sa me bhujāntare rājannipatya rudhirāśanaḥ
|
mayaiva saha rājendra jagāma vasudhātalam ||8||

9matvā tu nihataṃ rāmastato māṃ bharatarṣabha
|
meghavadvinanādoccairjahṛṣe ca punaḥ punaḥ ||9||

10tathā tu patite rājan mayi rāmo mudā yutaḥ
|
u‌krośanmahānādaṃ saha tairanuyāyibhiḥ ||10||

11mama tatrābhavan ye tu kuravaḥ pārśvataḥ sthitāḥ
|
āgatā api yuddhaṃ tajjanāstatra didṛkṣavaḥ
|
ārtiṃ paramikāṃ jagmuste tadā patite mayi ||11||

12tato'paśyaṃ patito rājasiṃha dvijānaṣṭau sūryahutāśanābhān
|
te māṃ samantāt parivārya tasthuḥ svabāhubhiḥ paridhāryājimadhye ||12||

13rakṣyamāṇaśca tairviprairnāhaṃ bhūmimupāspṛśam
|
antarikṣe dhṛto hyasmi tairviprairbāndhavairiva ||13||

14śvasannivāntarikṣe ca jalabindubhirukṣitaḥ
|
tataste brāhmaṇā rājannabruvan parigṛhya mām ||14||

15mā bhairiti samaṃ sarve svasti te'stviti cāsakṛt
|
tatasteṣāṃmahaṃ vāgbhistarpitaḥ sahasotthitaḥ
|
mātaraṃ saritāṃ śreṣṭhāmapaśyaṃ rathamāsthitām ||15||

16hayāśca me saṅgṛhītāstayā''san mahānadyā saṃyati kauravendra
|
pādau jananyāḥ pratigṛhya cāhaṃ tathā pitṝṇāṃ rathamabhyaroham ||16||

17rarakṣa sā māṃ sarathaṃ hayāṃścopaskarāṇi ca
|
tāmahaṃ prāñjalirbhūtvā punareva vyasarjayam ||17||

18tato'haṃ svayamudyamya hayāṃstān vātaraṃhasaḥ
|
ayudhyaṃ jāmadagnyena nivṛtte'hani bhārata ||18||

19tato'haṃ bharataśreṣṭha vegavantaṃ mahābalam
|
amuñcaṃ samare bāṇaṃ rāmāya hṛdayacchidam ||19||

20tato jagāma vasudhāṃ mama bāṇaprapīḍitaḥ
|
jānubhyāṃ dhanurutsṛjya rāmo mohavaśaṃ gataḥ ||20||

21tatastasminnipatite rāme bhūrisahasrade
|
āvavrurjaladā vyoma kṣaranto rudhiraṃ bahu ||21||

22ulkāśca śataśaḥ petuḥ sanirghātāḥ sakampanāḥ
|
arkaṃ ca sahasā dīptaṃ svarbhānurabhisaṃvṛṇot ||22||

23vavuśca vātāḥ paruṣāścalitā ca vasundharā
|
gṛdhrā balāśca kaṅkāśca paripeturmudā yutāḥ ||23||

24dīptāyāṃ diśi gomāyurdāruṇaṃ muhurunnadat
|
anāhatā dundubhayo vinedurbhṛśaniḥsvanāḥ ||24||

25etadautpātikaṃ sarvaṃ ghoramāsīdbhayaṅkaram
|
visaṃjñakalpe dharaṇīṃ gate rāme mahātmani ||25||

26tato vai sahasotthāya rāmo māmabhyavartata
|
punaryuddhāya kauravya vihvalaḥ krodhamūrchitaḥ ||26||

27ādadāno mahābāhuḥ kārmukaṃ balasannibham
|
tato mayyādadānaṃ taṃ rāmameva nyavārayan ||27||

28maharṣayaḥ kṛpāyuktāḥ krodhāviṣṭo'tha bhārgavaḥ
|
sa me'harada‌meyātmā śaraṃ kālānalopamam ||28||

29tato ravirmaṃtramarīcimaṇḍalo jagāmāstaṃ pāṃsupuñjavagūḍhaḥ
|
niśā vyagāhat sukhaśītamārutā tato yuddhaṃ pratyavahārayāvaḥ ||29||

30evaṃ rājannavahāro babhūva tataḥ punarvimale'bhūt sughoram
|
kalyaṃ kalyaṃ viṃśatiṃ vai dināni tathaiva cānyāni dināni trīṇi ||30||
