Mahābhārata 13.66 (Anuśāsanaparvan)

1yudhiṣṭhira uvāca|
dahyamānāya viprāya yaḥ prayacchatyupānahau
|
yatphalaṃ tasya bhavati tanme brūhi pitāmaha ||1||

2bhīṣma uvāca|
upānahau prayacchedyo brāhmaṇebhyaḥ samāhitaḥ
|
mardate kaṇṭakānsarvānviṣamānnistaratyapi ||2||

3sa śatrūṇāmupari ca santiṣṭati yudhiṣṭhira
|
yānaṃ cāśvatarīyuktaṃ tasya śubhraṃ viśāṃpate ||3||

4upatiṣṭhati kaunteya raupyakāṃcanabhūṣitam
|
śakaṭaṃ damyasaṃyuktaṃ dattaṃ bhavati caiva hi ||4||

5yudhiṣṭhira uvāca|
yatphalaṃ tiladāne ca bhūmidāne ca kīrtitam
|
godāne cānnadāne ca bhūyastadbrūhi kaurava ||5||

6bhīṣma uvāca|
śṛṇuṣva mama kaunteya tiladānasya yatphalam
|
niśamya ca yathānyāyaṃ prayaccha kurusattama ||6||

7pitṝṇāṃ paramaṃ bhojyaṃ tilāḥ sṛṣṭāḥ svayaṃbhuvā
|
tiladānena vai tasmātpitṛpakṣaḥ pramodate ||7||

8māghamāse tilānyastu brāhmaṇebhyaḥ prayacchati
|
sarvasattvasamākīrṇaṃ narakaṃ sa na paśyati ||8||

9sarvasatraiśca yajate yastilairyajate pitṝn
|
na cākāmena dātavyaṃ tilaśrāddhaṃ kadācana ||9||

10maharṣeḥ kaśyapasyaite gātrebhyaḥ prasṛtāstilāḥ
|
tato divyaṃ gatā bhāvaṃ pradāneṣu tilāḥ prabho ||10||

11pauṣṭikā rūpadāścaiva tathā pāpavināśanāḥ
|
tasmātsarvapradānebhyastiladānaṃ viśiṣyate ||11||

12āpastaṃbaśca medhāvī śaṃkhaśca likhitastathā
|
maharṣigautamaścāpi tiladānairdivaṃ gatāḥ ||12||

13tilahomaratā viprāḥ sarve saṃyatamaithunāḥ
|
samā gavyena haviṣā pravṛttiṣu ca saṃsthitāḥ ||13||

14sarveṣāmiti dānānāṃ tiladānaṃ viśiṣyate
|
akṣayaṃ sarvadānānāṃ tiladānamihocyate ||14||

15ucchinne tu purā havye kuśikarṣiḥ paraṃtapaḥ
|
tilairagnitrayaṃ hutvā prāptavān gatimuttamām ||15||

16iti proktaṃ kuruśreṣṭha tiladānamanuttamam
|
vidhānaṃ yena vidhinā tilānāmiha śasyate ||16||

17ata ūrdhvaṃ nibodhedaṃ devānāṃ yaṣṭumicchatām
|
samāgame mahārāja brahmaṇā vai svayaṃbhuvā ||17||

18devāḥ sametya brahmāṇaṃ bhūmibhāge yiyakṣavaḥ
|
śubhaṃ deśamayācanta yajema iti pārthiva ||18||

19devā ūcuḥ |
bhagavaṃstvaṃ prabhurbhūmeḥ sarvasya tridivasya ca
|
yajemahi mahābhāga yajñaṃ bhavadanujñayā ||19||

20nānanujñātabhūmirhi yajñasya phalamaśrute
|
tvaṃ hi sarvasya jagataḥ sthāvarasya carasya ca ||20||

21prabhurbhavasi tasmāttvaṃ samanujñātumarhasi
|
brahmovāca|
dadāni medinībhāgaṃ bhavadbhyo'haṃ surarṣabhāḥ||21||

22yasmindeśe kariṣyadhvaṃ yajñānkāśyapanandanāḥ
|
devā ūcuḥ |
bhagavankṛtakāryāḥ sma yakṣmahe svāptada‌kṣiṇaiḥ ||22||

23imaṃ tu deśaṃ munayaḥ paryupāsanti nityadā
|
tato'gastyaśca kaṇvaśca bhṛguratrirvṛṣākapiḥ||23||

24asito devalaścaiva devayajñamupāgaman
|
tato devā mahātmāna ījire yajñamacyutam ||24||

25tathā samāpayāmāsuryathākālaṃ surarṣabhāḥ
|
ta iṣṭayajñāstridaśā himavatyacalottame ||25||

26ṣaṣṭhamaṃśaṃ kratostasya bhūmidānaṃ pracakrire
|
prādeśamātraṃ bhūmestu yo dadyādanupaskṛtam ||26||

27na sīdati sa kṛcchreṣu na ca durgāṇyavāpnute
|
śītavātātapasahāṃ gṛhabhūmiṃ susaṃskṛtām ||27||

28pradāya suralokasthaḥ puṇyānte'pi na cālyate
|
mudito vasati prājñaḥ śakreṇa saha pārthiva ||28||

29pratiśrayapradānācca so'pi svarge mahīyate
|
adhyāpakakule jātaḥ śrotriyo niyatendriyaḥ ||29||

30gṛhe yasya vasettuṣṭaḥ pradhānaṃ lokamaśnute
|
tathā gavārthe śaraṇaṃ śītavarṣasahaṃ dṛḍham ||30||

31āsaptamaṃ tārayati kulaṃ bharatasattama
|
kṣetrabhūmiṃ dadalloke śubhāṃ śriyamavāpnuyāt ||31||

32ratnabhūmiṃ pradadyāttu kulavaṃśaṃ pravardhayet
|
na coṣarāṃ na nirdagdhāṃ mahīṃ dadyātkathañcana ||32||

33na śmaśānaparītāṃ ca na ca pāpaniṣevitām
|
pārakye bhūmideśe tu pitṝṇāṃ nirvapettu yaḥ||33||

34tadbhūmiṃ vāpi pitṛbhiḥ śrāddhakarma vihanyate
|
tasmātkrītvāmahīṃdadyātsvalpāmapi vicakṣaṇaḥ ||34||

35piṃḍaḥ pitṛbhyo datto vai tasyāṃ bhavati śāśvataḥ
|
aṭavīparvatāścaiva nadyastīrthāni yāni ca ||35||

36sarvāṇyasvāmikānyāhurnahi tatra parigrahaḥ
|
ityetadbhūmidānasya phalamuktaṃ viśāṃpate ||36||

37ataḥ paraṃ tu godānaṃ kīrtayiṣyāmi te'nagha
|
gāvo'dhikāstapasvibhyo yasmātsarvebhya eva ca ||37||

38tasmānmaheśvaro devastapastābhiḥ sahāsthitaḥ
|
brāhme loke vasantyetāḥ somena saha bhārata ||38||

39yāṃ tāṃ brahmarṣayaḥ siddhāḥ prārthayanti parāṃ gatim
|
payasā haviṣā dadhnā śakṛtā cātha carmaṇā ||39||

40asthibhiścopakurvanti śṛṅgairvālaiśca bhārata
|
nāsāṃ śītātapau syātāṃ sadaitāḥ karma kurvate ||40||

41na varṣaviṣayaṃ vāpi duvuḥkhamāsāṃ bhavatyuta
|
brāhmaṇaiḥ sahitā yānti tasmātpāramakaṃ padam ||41||

42ekaṃ gobrāhmaṇaṃ tasmātpravadanti manīṣiṇaḥ
|
rantidevasya yajñe tāḥ paśutvenopakalpitāḥ ||42||

43ataścarmaṇvatī rājan gocarmabhyaḥ pravartitā
|
paśutvācca vinirmuktāḥ pradānāyopakalpitāḥ ||43||

44tā imā vipramukhyebhyo yo dadāti mahīpate
|
nistaredāpadaṃ kṛcchrāṃ viṣamastho'pi pārthiva ||44||

45gavāṃ sahasradaḥ pretya narakaṃ na prapadyate
|
sarvatra vijayaṃ cāpi labhate manujādhipa ||45||

46amṛtaṃ vai gavāṃ kṣīramityāha tridaśādhipaḥ
|
tasmādaddāti yo dhenumamṛtaṃ sa prayacchati ||46||

47agnīnāmavyayaṃ hyetaddhaumyaṃ vedavido viduḥ
|
tasmāddadāti yo dhenuṃ sa haumyaṃ saṃprayacchati ||47||

48svargo vai mūrtimāneṣa vṛṣabhaṃ yo gavāṃ patim
|
vipre guṇayute dadyātsa vai svarge mahīyate ||48||

49prāṇā vai prāṇināmete procyante bharatarṣabha
|
tasmāddadāti yo dhenuṃ prāṇāneṣa prayacchati ||49||

50gāvaḥ śaraṇyā bhūtānāmiti vedavido viduḥ
|
tasmāddadāti yo dhenuṃ śaraṇaṃ saṃprayacchati ||50||

51na vadhārthaṃ pradātavyā na kīnāśe na nāstike
|
gojīvine na dātavyā tathā gaurbharatarṣabha ||51||

52dadatsa tādṛśānāṃ vai naro gāṃ pāpakarmaṇām
|
akṣayaṃ narakaṃ yātītyevamāhurmaharṣayaḥ ||52||

53na kṛśāṃ nāpavatsāṃ vā vaṃdhyāṃ rogānvitāṃ tathā
|
na vyaṅgāṃ na pariśrāntāṃ dadyāṅgāṃ brāhmaṇāya vai ||53||

54daśagosahasrado hi śakreṇa saha modate
|
akṣayā~llabhate lokānnaraḥ śatasahasraśaḥ ||54||

55ityetadgopradānaṃ ca tiladānaṃ ca kīrtitam
|
tathā bhūmipradānaṃ ca śṛṇuṣvānne ca bhārata ||55||

56annadānaṃ pradhānaṃ hi kaunteya paricakṣate
|
annasya hi pradānena rantidevo divaṃ gataḥ ||56||

57śrāntāya kṣudhitāyānnaṃ yaḥ prayacchati bhūmipaḥ
|
svāyaṃbhuvaṃ mahatsthānaṃ sa gacchati narādhipa ||57||

58na hiraṇyairna vāsobhirnānyadānena bhārata
|
prāpnuvanti narāḥ śreyo yathā hyannapradāḥ prabho ||58||

59annaṃ vai prathamaṃ dravyamannaṃ śrīśca parā matā
|
annātprāṇaḥ prabhavati tejo vīryaṃ balaṃ tathā ||59||

60sadyo dadāti yaścānnaṃ sadaikāgramanā naraḥ
|
na sa durgāṇyavāpnotītyevamāha parāśaraḥ ||60||

61arcayitvā yathānyāyaṃ devebhyo'nnaṃ nivedayeva
|
yadannā hi narā rājaṃstadannāstasya devatāḥ ||61||

62kaumude śuklapakṣe tu yo'nnadānaṃ karotyuta
|
sa santarati durgāṇi pretya cānantyamaśrute ||62||

63abhuktvā'tithaye cānnaṃ prayacchedyaḥ samāhitaḥ
|
sa vai brahmavidāṃ lokānprāpnuyādbharatarṣabha ||63||

64sukṛcchrāmāpadaṃ prāptaścānnadaḥ puruṣastaret
|
pāpaṃ tarati caiveha duṣkṛtaṃ cāpakarṣati ||64||

65ityetadannadānasya tiladānasya caiva ha
|
bhūmidānasya ca phalaṃ godānasya ca kīrtitam ||65||
