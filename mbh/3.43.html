Mahābhārata 3.43 (Āraṇyakaparvan)

1vaiśaṃpāyana uvāca|
dadarśa sa purīṃ ramyāṃ siddhacāraṇasevitām
|
sarvartukusumaiḥ puṇyaiḥ pāda‌pairupaśobhitām||1||

2tatra saugandhikānāṃ ca puṣpāṇāṃ puṇyagandhinām
|
udvījyamāno miśreṇa vāyunā puṇyagandhinā||2||

3nandanaṃ ca vanaṃ divyamapsarogaṇasevitam
|
dadarśa divyakusumairāhvayadbhiriva drumaiḥ||3||

4nātaptatapasā śakyo draṣṭuṃ nānāhitāgninā
|
sa lokaḥ puṇyakartṝṇāṃ nāpi yuddhe parāṅmukhaiḥ||4||

5nāyajvabhirnāvratikairna vedaśrutivarjitaiḥ
|
nānāplutāṅgaistīrtheṣu yajñadānabahiṣkṛtaiḥ||5||

6nāpi yajñahanaiḥ kṣudrairdraṣṭuṃ śakyaḥ kathañcana
|
pānapairgurutalpaiśca māṃsādairvā durātmabhiḥ||6||

7sa taddivyaṃ vanaṃ paśyandivyagītanināditam
|
praviśeśa mahābāhuḥ śakrasya dayitāṃ purīm||7||

8tatra devavimānāni kāmagāni sahastraśaḥ
|
saṃsthitānyabhiyātāni dadarśāyutaśastadā||8||

9saṃstūyamāno gandharvairapsarobhiśca pāṇḍavaḥ
|
puṣpagandhavahaiḥ puṇyairvāyubhiścānuvījitaḥ||9||

10tato devāḥ sagandharvāḥ siddhāśca paramarṣayaḥ
|
hṛṣṭāḥ sampūjayāmāsuḥ pārthamakliṣṭakāriṇam||10||

11āśīrvādaiḥstūyamāno divyavāditraniḥsvanaiḥ
|
pratipede mahābāhuḥ śaṅkhadundubhināditam||11||

12nakṣatramārgaṃ vipulaṃ suravīthīti viśrutam
|
indrājñayā yayau pārthaḥ stūyamānaḥ samantataḥ||12||

13tatra sādhyāstathā viśve maruto'thāśvinau tathā
|
ādityā vasavo rudrāstathā brahmarṣayo'malāḥ||13||

14rājarṣayaśca bahavo dilīpapramukhā nṛpāḥ
|
tumbururnāradaścaiva gandharvau ca hāhāhuhūḥ||14||

15tānsa sarvānsamāgamya vidhivatkurunandanaḥ
|
tato'paśyaddevarājaṃ śatakratumarindamaḥ||15||

16tataḥ pārtho mahābāhuravatīrya rathottamāt
|
dadarśa sākṣāddeveśaṃ pitaraṃ pākaśāsanam||16||

17pāṇḍureṇātapatreṇa hemadaṇḍena cāruṇā
|
divyagandhādhivāsena vyajanena vidhūyatā||17||

18viśvāvasuprabhṛtibhirgandharvaistutivandabhiḥ
|
stūyamānaṃ dvijāgryaiśca ṛgyajuḥsāmasambhavaiḥ||18||

19tato'bhigamya kaunteyaḥ śirasābhyagamadbalī
|
sa cainaṃ vṛttapīnābhyāṃ bāhubhyāṃ pratyagṛhṇata||19||

20tataḥ śakrāsane puṇye devarṣigaṇasevite
|
śakraḥ pāṇau gṛhītvainamupāveśayadantike||20||

21mūrdhni cainamupāghrāya devendraḥ paravīrahā
|
aṅkamāropayāmāsa praśrayāvanataṃ tadā||21||

22sahasrākṣaniyogātsa pārthaḥ śakrāsanaṃ gataḥ
|
adhyakrāmadameyātmā dvitīya iva vāsavaḥ||22||

23tataḥ premṇā vṛtraśatrurarjunasya śubhaṃ mukham
|
pasparśa puṇyagandhena kareṇa parisāntvayan||23||

24pramārjamānaḥ śanakairbāhū cāsyāyatau śubhau
|
jyāśarakṣepakaṭhinau stambhāviva hiraṇmayau||24||

25vajragrahaṇacihnena kareṇa parisāntvayan
|
muhurmuhurvajradharo bāhū cāsphoṭayañcchanaiḥ||25||

26smayanniva guḍākeśaṃ prekṣamāṇaḥ sahastradṛk
|
harṣeṇotphullanayano na cātṛpyata vṛtrahā||26||

27ekāsano'paviṣṭau tau śobhayāñcakratuḥ sabhām
|
sūryācandramasau vyoma caturdaśyāmivoditau||27||

28tatra sma gāthā gāyanti sāmnā paramavalgunā
|
gandharvāstumburuśreṣṭhāḥ kuśalā gītasāmasu||28||

29ghṛtācī menakā rambhā pūrvacittiḥ svayamprabhā
|
urvaśī miśrakeśī ca daṇḍagaurī varūthinī||29||

30gopālī sahajanyā ca kumbhayoniḥ prajāgarā
|
citrasenā citralekhā sahā ca madhurasvarā||30||

31etāścānyāśca nanṛtustatra tatra sahasraśaḥ
|
cittaprasādane yuktāḥ siddhānāṃ padmalocanāḥ||31||

32mahākaṭitaṭaśroṇyaḥ kampamānaiḥ payodharaiḥ
|
kaṭākṣahāvamādhuryaiścetobuddhimanoharaiḥ||32||
