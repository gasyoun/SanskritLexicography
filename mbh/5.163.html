Mahābhārata 5.163 (Udyogaparvan)

1sañjaya uvāca|
duryodhanasya tadvākyaṃ niśamya bharatarṣabha
|
netrābhyāmatitāmrābhyāṃ kaitavyaṃ samudaikṣata ||1||

2sa keśavamabhiprekṣya guḍākeśo mahāyaśāḥ
|
abhyabhāṣata kaitavyaṃ pragṛhya vipulaṃ bhujam ||2||

3svavīryaṃ yaḥ samāśritya samāhvayati vai parān
|
abhīto yudhyate śatrūn sa vai puruṣa ucyate ||3||

4paravīryaṃ samāśritya yaḥ samāhvayate parān
|
kṣatrabandhuraśaktatvālloke sa puruṣādhamaḥ ||4||

5sa tvaṃ pareṣāṃ vīryeṇa manyase vīryamātmanaḥ
|
svayaṃ kāpuruṣo mūḍha parāṃśca kṣeptumicchasi ||5||

6yastvaṃ vṛddhaṃ sarvarājñāṃ hitabuddhiṃ jitendriyam
|
maraṇāya mahāprajñaṃ dīkṣayitvā vikatthase ||6||

7bhāvaste vidito'smābhirdurbuddhe kulapāṃsana
|
na haniṣyanti gāṅgeyaṃ pāṇḍavā ghṛṇayeti hi ||7||

8yasya vīryaṃ samāśritya dhārtarāṣṭra vikatthase
|
hantā'smi prathamaṃ bhīṣmaṃ miṣatāṃ sarvadhanvinām ||8||

9kaitavya gatvā bharatān sametya suyodhanaṃ dhārtarāṣṭraṃ vadasva
|
tathetyuvācārjunaḥ savyasācī niśāvyapāye bhavitā vimardaḥ ||9||

10yadvā'bravīdvākyamadīnasattvāṃ madhye kurūn harṣayan satyasandhaḥ
|
ahaṃ hantā sṛñjayānāmanīkaṃ śālveyakāṃśceti mamaiṣa bhāraḥ
|
kaitavya gatvā bharatān sametya suyodhanaṃ dhārtarāṣṭraṃ vadasva ||10||

11hanyāmahaṃ droṇamṛte'pi lokaṃ na te bhayaṃ vidyate pāṇḍavebhyaḥ
|
tato hi te labdhatamaṃ ca rājyamāpadgatāḥ pāṇḍavāśceti bhāvaḥ ||11||

12sa darpapūrṇo na samīkṣase tvamanarthamātmanyapi vartamānam
|
tasmādahaṃ te prathamaṃ samūhe hantā samakṣaṃ kuruvṛddhameva ||12||

13sūryodaye yuktasenaḥ pratīkṣya dhvajī rathī rakṣata satyasandham
|
ahaṃ hi vaḥ paśyatāṃ dvīpamenaṃ bhīṣmaṃ rathāt pātayiṣyāmi bāṇaiḥ ||13||

14śvobhūte katthanāvākyaṃ vijñāsyati yodhanaḥ
|
ācitaṃ śarajālena mayā dṛṣṭvā pitāmaham ||14||

15yaduktaśca sabhāmadhye puruṣo hrasvadarśanaḥ
|
kruddhena bhīmasenena bhrātā duḥśāsanastava ||15||

16adharmajño nityavairī pāpabuddhirnṛśaṃsavat
|
satyāṃ pratijñāmacirāddrakṣyase tāṃ suyodhana ||16||

17abhimānasya darpasya krodhapāruṣyayostathā
|
naiṣṭhuryasyāvalepasya ātmasaṃbhāvanasya ca ||17||

18nṛśaṃsatāyāstaikṣṇyasya dharmavidveṣaṇasya ca
|
adharmasyātivādasya vṛddhātikramaṇasya ca ||18||

19darśanasya ca cakrasya kṛtsnasyāpanayasya ca
|
drakṣyasi tvaṃ phalaṃ tīvramacireṇa suyodhana ||19||

20vāsudevadvitīye hi mayi kuddhe narādhama
|
āśā te jīvite mūḍha rājye vā kena hetunā ||20||

21śānte bhīṣme tathā droṇe sūtaputre ca pātite
|
nirāśo jīvite rājye putreṣu ca bhaviṣyasi ||21||

22bhrātṝṇāṃ nidhanaṃ śrutvā putrāṇāṃ ca suyodhana
|
bhīmasenena nihato duṣkṛtāni smariṣyasi ||22||

23na dvitīyāṃ pratijñāṃ hi pratijānāmi kaitava
|
satyaṃ bravīmyahaṃ hyetat sarvaṃ satyaṃ bhaviṣyati ||23||

24yudhiṣṭhiro'pi kaitavyamulūkamidamabravīt
|
ulūka madvaco brūhi gatvā tāta suyodhanam ||24||

25svena vṛttena me vṛttaṃ nādhigantuṃ tvamarhasi
|
ubhayorantaraṃ veda sūnṛtānṛtayorapi ||25||

26na cāhaṃ kāmaye pāpamapi kīṭapipīlayoḥ
|
kiṃ punarjñātiṣu vadhaṃ kāmayeyaṃ kathañcana ||26||

27etadarthaṃ mayā tāta pañca grāmā vṛtāḥ purā
|
kathaṃ tava sudurbuddhe na prekṣye vyasanaṃ mahat ||27||

28sa tvaṃ kāmaparītātmā mūḍhabhāvācca katthase
|
tathaiva vāsudevasya na gṛhṇāsi hitaṃ vacaḥ ||28||

29kiñcedānīṃ bahūktena yudhyasva saha bāndhavaiḥ
|
mama vipriyakartāraṃ kaitavya brūhi kauravam ||29||

30śrutaṃ vākyaṃ gṛhīto'rtho mataṃ yatte tathā'stu tat
|
bhīmasenastato vākyaṃ bhūya āha nṛpātmajam ||30||

31ulūka madvaco brūhi durmatiṃ pāpapūruṣam
|
śaṭhaṃ naikṛtikaṃ pāpaṃ durācāraṃ suyodhanam ||31||

32gṛdhrodare vā vastavyaṃ pure vā nāgasāhvaye
|
pratijñātaṃ mayā tacca sabhāmadhye narādhama ||32||

33kartā'haṃ tadvacaḥ satyaṃ satyenaiva śapāmi te
|
duḥśāsanasya rudhiraṃ hatvā pāsyāmyahaṃ mṛdhe ||33||

34sakthinī tava bhaṅktvaiva hatvā hi tava sodarān
|
sarveṣāṃ dhārtarāṣṭrāṇāmahaṃ mṛtyuḥ suyodhana ||34||

35sarveṣāṃ rājaputrāṇāmabhimanyurasaṃśayam
|
karmaṇā toṣayiṣyāmi bhūyaścaiva vacaḥ śṛṇu ||35||

36hatvā suyodhana tvāṃ vai sahitaṃ sarvasodaraiḥ
|
ākramiṣye padā mūrdhni dharmarājasya paśyataḥ ||36||

37nakulastu tato vākyamidamāha mahīpate
|
ulūka brūhi kauravyaṃ dhārtarāṣṭraṃ suyodhanam ||37||

38śrutaṃ te gadato vākyaṃ sarvameva yathātatham
|
tathā kartā'smi kauravya yathā tvamanuśāsi mām||38||

39sahadevo'pi nṛpate idamāha vaco'rthavat
|
suyodhana matiryā te vṛthaiṣā te bhaviṣyati ||39||

40śociṣyase mahārāja saputrajñātibāndhavaḥ
|
imaṃ ca kleśamasmākaṃ hṛṣṭo yattvaṃ vikatthase ||40||

41virāṭadrupadau vṛddhāvulūkamidamūcatuḥ
|
dāsabhāvaṃ niyaccheva sādhoriti matiḥ sadā
|
tau ca dāsāvadāsau vā pauruṣaṃ yasya yādṛśam ||41||

42śikhaṇḍī tu tato vākyamulūkamidamabravīt
|
vaktavyo bhavatā rājā pāpeṣvabhirataḥ sadā ||42||

43paśya tvaṃ māṃ raṇe rājan kurvāṇaṃ karma dāruṇam
|
yasya vīryaṃ samāsādya manyase vijayaṃ yudhi ||43||

44tamahaṃ pātayiṣyāmi rathāttava pitāmaham
|
ahaṃ bhīṣmavadhāt sṛṣṭo nūnaṃ dhātrā mahātmanā ||44||

45so'haṃ bhīṣmaṃ haniṣyāmi miṣatāṃ sarvadhanvinām
|
dhṛṣṭadyumno'pi kaitavyamulūkamidamabravīt ||45||

46suyodhano mama vaco vaktavyo nṛpateḥ sutaḥ
|
ahaṃ droṇaṃ haniṣyāmi sagaṇaṃ sahabāndhavam ||46||

47avaśyaṃ ca mayā kāryaṃ pūrveṣāṃ caritaṃ mahat
|
kartā cāhaṃ tathā karma yathā nānyaḥ kariṣyati ||47||

48tamabravīddharmarājaḥ kāruṇyārthaṃ vaco mahat
|
nāhaṃ jñātivadhaṃ rājan kāmayeyaṃ kathañcana ||48||

49tavaiva doṣāddurbuddhe sarvametattvanāvṛtam
|
sa gaccha mā ciraṃ tāta ulūka yadi manyase ||49||

50iha vā tiṣṭha bhadraṃ te vayaṃ hi tava bāndhavāḥ
|
ulūkastu tato rājan dharmaputraṃ yudhiṣṭhiram ||50||

51āmantrya prayayau tatra yatra rājā suyodhanaḥ
|
ulūkastata āgamya duryodhanamamarṣaṇam ||51||

52arjunasya samādeśaṃ yathoktaṃ sarvamabravīt
|
vāsudevasya bhīmasya dharmarājasya pauruṣam ||52||

53nakulasya virāṭasya drupadasya ca bhārata
|
sahadevasya ca vaco dhṛṣṭadyumnaśikhaṇḍinoḥ
|
keśavārjunayorvākyaṃ yathoktaṃ sarvamabravīt ||53||

54kaitavyasya tu tadvākyaṃ niśamya bharatarṣabhaḥ
|
duḥśāsanaṃ ca karṇaṃ ca śakuniṃ cāpi bhārata ||54||

55ājñāpayata rājñaśca balaṃ mitrabalaṃ tathā
|
yathā prāgudayāt sarve yuktāstiṣṭhantyanīkinaḥ ||55||

56tataḥ karṇasamādiṣṭā dūtāḥ saṃtvaritā rathaiḥ
|
uṣṭravāmībhirapyanye sadaśvaiśca mahājavaiḥ ||56||

57tūrṇaṃ pariyayuḥ senāṃ kṛtsnāṃ karṇasya śāsanāt
|
ājñāpayanto rājñaśca yogaḥ prāgudayāditi ||57||
