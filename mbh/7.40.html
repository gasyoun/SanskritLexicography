Mahābhārata 7.40 (Droṇaparvan)

1sañjaya uvāca|
śaravikṣatagātrastu pratyamitramavasthitam
|
abhimanyuḥ smayan śrīmān duḥśāsanamathābravīt||1||

2diṣṭyā paśyāmi saṃgrāme māninaṃ śūramāgatam
|
niṣṭhuraṃ tyaktadharmāṇamākrośanaparāyaṇam||2||

3yatsabhāyāṃ tvayā rājño dhṛtarāṣṭrasya śṛṇvataḥ
|
kopitaḥ paruṣairvākyairdharmarājo yudhiṣṭhiraḥ||3||

4jayonmattena bhīmaśca bahvabaddhaṃ prabhāṣitaḥ
|
akṣakūṭaṃ samāśritya saubalasyātmano balam||4||

5tattvaveda‌manuprāptaṃ tasya kopānmahātmanaḥ
|
paravittāpahārasya krodhasyāpraśamasya ca||5||

6lobhasya jñānanāśasya drohasyātyāhitasya ca
|
pitṝṇāṃ mama rājyasya haraṇasyogradhanvinām||6||

7tattvayeda‌manuprāptaṃ prakopādvai mahātmanām
|
sa tasyogramadharmasya phalaṃ prāpnuhi durmate||7||

8śāsitāsmyadya te bāṇaiḥ sarvasainyasya paśyataḥ
|
adyāhamanṛṇastasya kopasya bhavitā raṇe||8||

9amarṣitāyāḥ kṛṣṇāyāḥ kāṃkṣitasya ca me pituḥ
|
adya kauravya bhīmasya bhavitā'smyanṛṇo yudhi||9||

10na hi me mokṣyase jīvanyadi notsṛjase raṇam
|
evamuktvā mahābāhurbāṇaṃ duḥśāsanāntakam||10||

11saṃda‌dhe paravīraghnaḥ kālāgnyanilavarcasam
|
tasyorastūrṇamāsādya jatrudeśe vibhidya tam||11||

12jagāma saha puṅkhena valmīkamiva pannagaḥ
|
athainaṃ pañcaviṃśatyā punareva samārpayat||12||

13śarairagnisamasparśairākarṇasamacoditaiḥ
|
sa gāḍhaviddho vyathito rathopasya upāviśat||13||

14duḥśāsano mahārāja kaśmalaṃ cāviśanmahat
|
sārathistvaramāṇastu duḥśāsanamacetanam||14||

15raṇamadhyādapovāha saubhadraśarapīḍitam
|
pāṇḍavā draupadeyāśca virāṭaśca samīkṣya tam||15||

16pañcālāḥ kekayāścaiva siṃhanādamathānadan
|
vāditrāṇi ca sarvāṇi nānāliṅgāni sarvaśaḥ||16||

17prāvādayanta saṃhṛṣṭāḥ pāṇḍūnāṃ tatra sainikāḥ
|
apaśyan smayamānāśca saubhadrasya viceṣṭitam||17||

18atyantavairiṇaṃ dṛptaṃ dṛṣṭvā śatruṃ parājitam
|
dharmamārutaśakrāṇāmaśvinoḥ pratimāstathā||18||

19dhārayanto dhvajāgreṣu draupadeyā mahārathāḥ
|
sātyakiścekitānaśca dhṛṣṭadyumnaśikhaṇḍinau||19||

20kekayā dhṛṣṭaketuśca matsyāḥ pañcālasṛñjayāḥ
|
pāṇḍavāśca mudā yuktā yudhiṣṭhirapurogamāḥ||20||

21abhyadravanta tvaritā droṇānīkaṃ bibhitsavaḥ
|
tato'bhavanmahāyuddhaṃ tvadīyānāṃ paraiḥ saha||21||

22jayamākāṃkṣamāṇānāṃ śūrāṇāmanivartinām
|
tathā tu vartamāne vai saṃgrāme'tibhayaṅkare||22||

23duryodhano mahārāja rādheyamidamabravīt
|
paśya duḥśāsanaṃ vīramabhimanyuvaśaṃ gatam||23||

24pratapantamivādityaṃ nighnantaṃ śātravān raṇe
|
atha caite susaṃrabdhāḥ siṃhā iva balotkaṭāḥ||24||

25saubhadramudyatāstrātumabhyadhāvanta pāṇḍavāḥ
|
tataḥ karṇaḥ śaraistīkṣṇairabhimanyuṃ durāsadam||25||

26abhyavarṣata saṃkruddhaḥ putrasya hitakṛt tava
|
tasya cānucarāṃstīkṣṇairvivyādha parameṣubhiḥ||26||

27avajñāpūrvakaṃ śūraḥ saubhadrasya raṇājire
|
abhimanyustu rādheyaṃ trisaptatyā śilīmukhaiḥ||27||

28avidhyattvarito rājan droṇaṃ prepsurmahāmanāḥ
|
taṃ tathā nāśakat kaściddroṇādvārayituṃ rathī||28||

29ārujantaṃ rathavrātān vajrahastātmajātmajam
|
tataḥ karṇo jayaprepsurmānī sarvadhanuṣmatām||29||

30saubhadraṃ śataśo'vidhyaduttamāstrāṇi darśayan
|
so'strairastravidāṃ śreṣṭho rāmaśiṣyaḥ pratāpavān||30||

31samare śatrudurdharṣamabhimanyumapīḍayat
|
sa tathā pīḍyamānastu rādheyenāstravṛṣṭibhiḥ||31||

32samare'marasaṃkāśaḥ saubhadro na vyaśīryata
|
tataḥ śilāśitaistīkṣṇairbhallairānataparvabhiḥ||32||

33chittvā dhanūṃṣi śūrāṇāmārjuniḥ karṇamārdayat
|
dhanurmaṇḍalanirmuktaiḥ śarairāśīviṣopamaiḥ||33||

34sacchatradhvajayantāraṃ sāśvamāśu smayanniva
|
karṇo'pi cāsya cikṣepa bāṇān sannataparvaṇaḥ||34||

35asaṃbhrāntaśca tānsarvānagṛhṇāt phālgunātmajaḥ
|
tato muhūrtāt karṇasya bāṇenaikena vīryavān||35||

36sadhvajaṃ kārmukaṃ vīraśchittvā bhūmāvapātayat
|
tataḥ kṛchragataṃ karṇaṃ dṛṣṭvā karṇādanantaraḥ||36||

37saubhadramabhyayāt turṇaṃ dṛḍhamudyamya kārmukam
|
tata uccukruśuḥ pārthasteṣāṃ cānucarā janāḥ
|
vāditrāṇi ca saṃjaghnuḥ saubhadraṃ cāpi tuṣṭuvuḥ||37||
