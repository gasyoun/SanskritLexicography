Mahābhārata 5.186 (Udyogaparvan)

1rāma uvāca|
pratyakṣametallokānāṃ sarveṣāmeva bhāvini
|
yathāśaktyā mayā yuddhaṃ kṛtaṃ vai pauruṣaṃ param ||1||

2na caivamapi śaknomi bhīṣmaṃ śasrabhṛtāṃ varam
|
viśeṣayitumatyarthamuttamāsrāṇi darśayan ||2||

3eṣā me paramā śaktiretanme paramaṃ balam
|
yatheṣṭaṃ gamyatāṃ bhadre kimanyadvā karomi te ||3||

4bhīṣmameva prapadyasva na te'nyā vidyate gatiḥ
|
nirjito hyasmi bhīṣmeṇa mahāstrāṇi pramuñcatā ||4||

5evamuktvā tato rāmo viniḥśvasya mahāmanāḥ
|
tūṣṇīmāsīttataḥ kanyā provāca bhṛgunandanam ||5||

6bhagavannevamevaitadyathā''haṃ bhagavāṃstathā
|
ajeyo yudhi bhīṣmo'yamapi devairudāradhīḥ ||6||

7yathāśakti yathotsāhaṃ mama kāryaṃ kṛtaṃ tvayā
|
anivāryaṃ raṇe vīryamastrāṇi vividhāni ca ||7||

8na caiva śakyate yuddhe viśeṣayitumantataḥ
|
na cāhamenaṃ yāsyāmi punarbhīṣmaṃ kathañcana ||8||

9gamiṣyāmi tu tatrāhaṃ yatra bhīṣmaṃ tapodhana
|
samare pātayiṣyāmi svayameva bhṛgūdvaha ||9||

10evamuktvā yayau kanyā roṣavyākulalocanā
|
tāpasye dhṛtasaṃkalpā sā me cintayatī vadham ||10||

11tato mahendraṃ sahitairmunibhirbhṛgusattamaḥ
|
yathāgataṃ tathā so'gānmāmupāmantrya bhārata ||11||

12tato rathaṃ samāruhya stūyamāno dvijātibhiḥ
|
praviśya nagaraṃ mātre satyavatyai nyavedayam ||12||

13yathāvṛttaṃ mahārāja sā ca māṃ pratyanandata
|
puruṣāṃścādiśaṃ prākṣān kanyāvṛttāntakarmaṇi ||13||

14divase divase hyasyā gatijalpitaceṣṭitam
|
pratyāharaṃśca me yuktāḥ sthitāḥ priyahite sadā ||14||

15yadaiva hi vanaṃ prāyāt sā kanyā tapase dhṛtā
|
tadaiva vyathito dīno gatacetā ivābhavam ||15||

16na hi māṃ kṣatriyaḥ kaścidvīryeṇa vyajayadyudhi
|
ṛte brahmavidastāta tapasā saṃśitavratāt ||16||

17api caitanmayā rājannārade'pi niveditam
|
vyāse caiva tathā kāryaṃ tau cobhau māmavocatām ||17||

18na viṣādastvayā kāryo bhīṣma kāśisutāṃ prati
|
daivaṃ puruṣakāreṇa ko nivartitumutsahet ||18||

19sā kanyā tu mahārāja praviśyāśramamaṇḍalam
|
yamunātīramāśritya tapastepe'timānuṣam ||19||

20nirāhārā kṛśā rukṣā jaṭilā malapaṅkinī
|
ṣaṇmāsān vāyubhakṣā ca sthāṇubhūtā tapodhanā ||20||

21yamunājalamāśritya saṃvatsaramathāparam
|
udavāsaṃ nirāhārā pārayāmāsa bhāvinī ||21||

22śīrṇaparṇena caikena pārayāmāsa sā param
|
saṃvatsaraṃ tīvrakopā pādāṅguṣṭhāgradhiṣṭhitā ||22||

23evaṃ dvādaśa varṣāṇi tāpayāmāsa rodasī
|
nivartyamānā'pi ca sā jñātibhirnaiva śakyate ||23||

24tatto'gamadvatsabhūmiṃ siddhacāraṇasevitām
|
āśramaṃ puṇyaśīlānāṃ tāpasānāṃ mahātmanām ||24||

25tatra puṇyeṣu tīrtheṣu sā''plutāṅgī divāniśam
|
vyacarat kāśikanyā sā yathākāmavicāriṇī ||25||

26nandāśrame mahārāja tatholūkāśrame śubhe
|
cyavanasyāśrame caiva brahmaṇaḥ sthāna eva ca ||26||

27prayāge devayajane devāraṇyeṣu caiva ha
|
bhogavatyāṃ mahārāja kauśikasyāśrame tathā||27||

28māṇḍavyasyāśrame rājan dilīpasyāśrame tathā
|
rāmahrade ca kauravya pailagargasya cāśrame ||28||

29eteṣu tīrtheṣu tadā kāśikanyā viśāmpate
|
āplāvayata gātrāṇi vratamāsthāya duṣkaram ||29||

30tāmabravīcca kauravya mama mātā jale sthitā
|
kimarthaṃ kliśyase bhadre tathyameva vadasva me ||30||

31saināmathābravīdrājan kṛtāñjaliraninditā
|
bhīṣmeṇa samare rāmo nirjitaścārulocane ||31||

32ko'nyastamutsahejjetumudyateṣu mahīpatiḥ
|
sā'haṃ bhīṣmavināśāya tapastapsye sudāruṇam ||32||

33vicarāmi mahīṃ devi yathā hanyāmahaṃ nṛpam
|
etadvrataphalaṃ devi paramasminyathā hi me ||33||

34tato'bravītsāgaragā jihmaṃ carasi bhāvini
|
naiṣa kāmo'navadyāṅgi śakyaḥ prāptuṃ tvayā'bale ||34||

35yadi bhīṣmavināśāya kāśye carasi vai vratam
|
vratasthā ca śarīraṃ tvaṃ yadi nāma vimokṣyasi ||35||

36nadī bhaviṣyasi śubhe kuṭilā vārṣikodakā
|
dustīrthā na tu vijñeyā vārṣikī nāṣṭamāsikī ||36||

37bhīmagrāhavatī ghorā sarvabhūtabhayaṅkarī
|
evamuktvā tato rājan kāśikanyāṃ nyavartata ||37||

38mātā mama mahābhāgā smayamāneva bhāvinī
|
kadācidaṣṭame māsi kadāciddaśame tathā
|
na prāśnītodakamapi punaḥ sā varavarṇinī ||38||

39sā vatsabhūmiṃ kauravya tīrthalobhāttatastataḥ
|
patitā paridhāvantī punaḥ kāśipateḥ sutā ||39||

40sā nadī vatsabhūmyāṃ tu prathitāmbeti bhārata
|
vārṣikī grāhabahulā dustīrthā kuṭilā tathā ||40||

41sā kanyā tapasā tena dehārdhena vyajāyata
|
nadī ca rājan vatseṣu kanyā caivābhavattadā ||41||
