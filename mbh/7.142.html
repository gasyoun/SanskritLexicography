Mahābhārata 7.142 (Droṇaparvan)

1sañjaya uvāca|
tamāpatantaṃ saṃprekṣya sātvataṃ yuddhadurmadam
|
krodhādbhūriśravā rājan sahasā samupādravat||1||

2tamabravīnmahārāja kauravyaḥ śinipuṅgavam
|
adya prāpto'si diṣṭyā me cakṣurviṣayamityuta||2||

3cirābhilaṣitaṃ kāmamahaṃ prāpsyāmi saṃyuge
|
na hi me mokṣyase jīvan yadi notsṛjase raṇam||3||

4adya tvāṃ samare hatvā nityaṃ śūrābhimāninam
|
naṃdayiṣyāmi dāśārha kururājaṃ suyodhanam||4||

5adya madbāṇanirdagdhaṃ patitaṃ dharaṇītale
|
drakṣyatastvāṃ raṇe vīrau sahitau keśavārjunau||5||

6adya dharmasuto rājā śrutvā tvāṃ nihataṃ mayā
|
savrīḍo bhavitā sadyo yenāsīha praveśitaḥ||6||

7adya me vikramaṃ pārtho viśāsyati dhanañjayaḥ
|
tvathi bhūmau vinihate śayāne rudhirokṣite||7||

8cirābhilaṣito hyeṣa tvayā saha samāgamaḥ
|
purā devāsure yuddhe śakrasya balinā yathā ||8||

9adya yuddhaṃ mahāghoraṃ tava dāsyāmi sātvata
|
tato jñāsyasi tattvena madvīryabalapauruṣam||9||

10adya saṃyamanīṃ yātā mayā tvaṃ nihato raṇe
|
yathā rāmānujenājau rāvaṇirlakṣmaṇena ha||10||

11adya kṛṣṇaśca pārthaśca dharmarājaśca mādhava
|
hate tvayi nirutsāhā raṇaṃ tyakṣyantyasaṃśayam||11||

12adya te'pacitiṃ kṛtvā śitairmādhava sāyakaiḥ
|
tatstriyo nandayiṣyāmi ye tvayā nihatā raṇe||12||

13maccakṣurviṣayaṃ prāpto na tvaṃ mādhava mokṣyase
|
siṃhasya viṣayaṃ prāpto yathā kṣudramṛgastathā||13||

14yuyudhānastu taṃ rājanpratyuvāca hasanniva
|
kauraveya na saṃtrāso vidyate mama saṃyuge||14||

15nāhaṃ bhīṣayituṃ śakyo vāṅmātreṇa tu kevalam
|
sa māṃ nihanyātsaṃgrāme yo māṃ kuryānnirāyudham||15||

16samāstu śāśvatīrhanyādyo māṃ hanyāddhi saṃyuge
|
kiṃ vṛthoktena bahunā karmaṇā tatsamācara ||16||

17śāradasyeva meghasya garjitaṃ niṣphalaṃ hi te
|
śrutvā tvadgarjitaṃ vīra hāsyaṃ hi mama jāyate||17||

18cirakālepsitaṃ loke yuddhamadyāstu kaurava
|
tvarate me matistāta tava yuddhābhikāṃkṣiṇī||18||

19nāhatvā'haṃ nivartiṣye tvāmadya puruṣādhama
|
anyonyaṃ tau tathā vāgbhistakṣantau narapuṅgavau||19||

20jighāṃsū paramakruddhāvabhijaghnaturāhave
|
sametau tau maheṣvāsau śuṣmiṇau spardhinau raṇe||20||

21dviradāviva saṃkruddhau vāsitārthe madotkaṭau
|
bhūriśravāḥ sātyakiśca vavarṣaturarindamau||21||

22śaravarṣāṇi ghorāṇi meghāviva parasparam
|
saumadattistu śaineyaṃ pracchādyeṣubhirāśugaiḥ||22||

23jighāṃsurbharataśreṣṭha vivyādha niśitaiḥ śaraiḥ
|
daśabhiḥ sātyakiṃ viddhvā saumadattirathāparān||23||

24mumoca niśitānbāṇān jighāṃsuḥ śinipuṅgavam
|
tānasya viśikhāṃstīkṣṇānantarikṣe viśāṃpate||24||

25aprāptānastramāyābhiragrasat sātyakiḥ prabho
|
tau pṛthak śastravarṣābhyāmavarṣetāṃ parasparam||25||

26uttamābhijanau vīrau kuruvṛṣṇiyaśaskarau
|
tau nakhairiva śārdūlau dantairiva mahādvipau||26||

27rathaśaktibhiranyonyaṃ viśikhaiścāpyakṛntatām
|
nirbhindaṃtau hi gātrāṇi vikṣarantau ca śoṇitam||27||

28vyaṣṭambhayetāmanyonyaṃ prāṇadyūtābhidevinau
|
evamuttamakarmāṇau kuruvṛṣṇiyaśaskarau||28||

29parasparamayudhyetāṃ vāraṇāviva yūthapau
|
tāvadīrgheṇa kālena brahmalokapuraskṛtau||29||

30yiyāsantau paraṃ sthānamanyonyaṃ sañjagarjatuḥ
|
sātyakiḥ saumadattiśca śaravṛṣṭyā parasparam||30||

31hṛṣṭavaddhārtarāṣṭrāṇāṃ paśyatāmabhyavarṣatām
|
saṃpraikṣanta janāstau tu yudhyamānau yudhāṃpatī||31||

32yūthapau vāsitāhetoḥ prayuddhāviva kuñjarau
|
anyonyasya hayānhatvā dhanuṣī vinikṛtya ca||32||

33virathāvasiyuddhāya sameyātāṃ mahāraṇe
|
ārṣabhe carmaṇī citre pragṛhya vipule śubhe ||33||

34vikośau cāpyasī kṛtvā samare tau viceratuḥ
|
carantau vividhānmārgān maṇḍalāni ca bhāgaśaḥ||34||

35muhurājaghnatuḥ kruddhāvanyonyamarimardanau
|
sakhaḍgau citravarmāṇau saniṣkāṅgadabhūṣaṇau||35||

36bhrāntamudbhrāntamāviddhamāplutaṃ viplutaṃ sṛtam
|
saṃpātaṃ samudīrṇaṃ ca darśayantau yaśasvinau||36||

37asibhyāṃ saṃprajahrāte parasparamarindamau
|
ubhau chidraiṣiṇau vīrāvubhau citraṃ vavalgatuḥ||37||

38darśayantāvubhau śikṣāṃ lāghavaṃ sauṣṭhavaṃ tathā
|
raṇe raṇakṛtāṃ śreṣṭhāvanyonyaṃ paryakarṣatām||38||

39muhūrtamiva rājendra samāhatya parasparam
|
paśyatāṃ sarvasainyānāṃ vīrāvāśvasatāṃ punaḥ||39||

40asibhyāṃ carmaṇī citre śatacandre narādhipa
|
nikṛtya puruṣavyāghrau bāhuyuddhaṃ pracakratuḥ||40||

41vyūḍhoraskau dīrghabhujau niyuddhakuśalāvubhau
|
bāhubhiḥ samasajjetāmāyasaiḥ parighairiva||41||

42tayo rājan bhujāghātanigrahapragrahāstathā
|
śikṣābalasamudbhūtāḥ sarvayodhapraharṣaṇāḥ||42||

43tayornṛvarayo rājansamare yudhyamānayoḥ
|
bhīmo'bhavanmahāśabdo vajraparvatayoriva||43||

44dvipāviva viṣāṇāgraiḥ śṛṅgairiva maharṣabhau
|
bhujayoktrāvabandhaiśca śirobhyāṃ cāvaghātanaiḥ||44||

45pādāvakarṣasandhānaistomarāṃkuśalāsanaiḥ
|
pādodaravibandhaiśca bhūmāvudbhramaṇaistathā||45||

46gatapratyāgatākṣepaiḥ pātanotthānasaṃplutaiḥ
|
yuyudhāte mahātmānau kurusātvatapuṅgavau||46||

47dvātriṃśatkaraṇāni syuryāni yuddhāni bhārata
|
tānyadarśayatāṃ tatra yudhyamānau mahābalau||47||

48kṣīṇāyudhe sātvate yudhyamāne tato'bravīdarjunaṃ vāsudevaḥ
|
paśyasvainaṃ virathaṃ yudhyamānaṃ raṇe varaṃ sarvadhanurdharāṇām||48||

49praviṣṭo bhāratīṃ bhitvā tava pāṇḍava pṛṣṭhataḥ
|
yodhitaśca mahāvīryaiḥ sarvairbhārata bhārataiḥ||49||

50pariśrāntaṃ yudhāṃ śreṣṭhaṃ saṃprāpto bhūridakṣiṇaḥ
|
yuddhākāṃkṣī samāyāntaṃ naitatsamamivārjuna||50||

51tato bhūriśravāḥ kruddhaḥ sātyakiṃ yuddhadurmadaḥ
|
udyamyābhyahanadrājanmatto mattamiva dvipam||51||

52rathasthayordvayoryuddhe kruddhayoryodhamukhyayoḥ
|
keśavārjunayo rājansamare prekṣamāṇayoḥ||52||

53atha kṛṣṇo mahābāhurarjunaṃ pratyabhāṣata
|
paśya vṛṣṇyandhakavyāghraṃ saumadattivaśaṃ gatam||53||

54paribhrāntaṃ gataṃ bhūmau kṛtvā karma suduṣkaram
|
tavāntevāsinaṃ vīraṃ pālayārjuna sātyakim||54||

55na vaśaṃ yajñaśīlasya gacchedeṣa varo'rjuna
|
tvatkṛte puruṣavyāghra tadāśu kriyatāṃ vibho||55||

56athābravīddhṛṣṭamanā vāsudevaṃ dhanañjayaḥ
|
paśya vṛṣṇipravīreṇa krīḍantaṃ kurupuṅgavam||56||

57mahādvipeneva vane mattena hariyūthapam
|
sañjaya uvāca|
ityevaṃ bhāṣamāṇe tu pāṇḍave vai dhanañjaye ||57||

58hāhākāro mahānāsītsainyānāṃ bharatarṣabha
|
tadudyamya mahābāhuḥ sātyakiṃ nyahanadbhuvi||58||

59sa siṃha iva mātaṅgaṃ vikarṣan bhūridakṣiṇaḥ
|
vyarocata kuruśreṣṭhaḥ sātvatapravaraṃ yudhi||59||

60atha kośādviniṣkṛṣya khaḍgaṃ bhūriśravā raṇe
|
mūrdhajeṣu nijagrāha padā corasyatāḍayat||60||

61tato'sya chettumārabdhaḥ śiraḥ kāyātsakuṇḍalam
|
tāvatkṣaṇātsātvato'pi śiraḥ saṃbhramayaṃstvaran||61||

62yathā cakraṃ tu kaulālo daṇḍaviddhaṃ tu bhārata
|
sahaiva bhūriśravaso bāhunā keśadhāriṇā||62||

63taṃ tathā parikṛṣyantaṃ dṛṣṭvā sātvatamāhave
|
vāsudevastato rājanbhūyo'rjunamabhāṣata||63||

64paśya vṛṣṇyandhakavyāghraṃ saumadattivaśaṃ gatam
|
tava śiṣyaṃ mahābāho dhanuṣyanavaraṃ tvayā||64||

65asatyo vikramaḥ pārtha yatra bhūriśravā raṇe
|
viśeṣayati vārṣṇeyaṃ sātyakiṃ satyavikramam||65||

66evamukto mahābāhurvāsudevena pāṇḍavaḥ
|
manasā pūjayāmāsa bhūriśravasamāhave||66||

67vikarṣansātvataśreṣṭhaṃ krīḍamāna ivāhave
|
saṃharṣayati māṃ bhūyaḥ kurūṇāṃ kīrtivardhanaḥ||67||

68pravaraṃ vṛṣṇivīrāṇāṃ yanna hanyāddhi sātyakim
|
mahādvipamivāraṇye mṛgendra iva karṣati||68||

69evaṃ tu manasā rājanpārthaḥ saṃpūjya kauravam
|
vāsudevaṃ mahābāhurarjunaḥ pratyabhāṣata||69||

70saindhave saktadṛṣṭitvānnainaṃ paśyāmi mādhavam
|
etattvasukaraṃ karma yādavārthe karomyaham ||70||

71ityuktvā vacanaṃ kurvanvāsudevasya pāṇḍavaḥ
|
tataḥ kṣurapraṃ niśitaṃ gāṇḍīve samayojayat||71||

72pārthabāhuvisṛṣṭaḥ sa maholkeva nabhaścyutā
|
sakhaḍgaṃ yajñaśīlasya sāṅgadaṃ bāhumacchinat||72||
