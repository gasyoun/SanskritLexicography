Mahābhārata 1.178 (Ādiparvan)

1gandharva uvāca|
āśramasthā tataḥ putramadṛśyantī vyajāyata
|
śakteḥ kulakaraṃ rājandvitīyamiva śaktinam||1||

2jātakarmādikāstasya kriyāḥ sa munisattamaḥ
|
pautrasya bharataśreṣṭha cakāra bhagavānsvayam||2||

3parāsuḥ sa yatastena vasiṣṭhaḥ sthāpito muniḥ
|
garbhasthena tato loke parāśara iti smṛtaḥ||3||

4amanyata sa dharmātmā vasiṣṭhaṃ pitaraṃ muniḥ
|
janmaprabhṛti tasmiṃstu pitarīvānvavartata||4||

5sa tāta iti viprarṣiṃ vasiṣṭhaṃ pratyabhāṣata
|
mātuḥ samakṣaṃ kaunteya adṛśyantyāḥ parantapa||5||

6tāteti paripūrṇārthaṃ tasya tanmadhuraṃ vacaḥ
|
adṛśyantyaśrupūrṇākṣī śṛṇvatī tamuvāca ha||6||

7mā tāta tāta tāteti brūhyenaṃ pitaraṃ pituḥ
|
rakṣasā bhakṣitastāta tava tāto vanāntare||7||

8manyase yaṃ tu tāteti naiṣa tātastavānagha
|
ārya eṣa pitā tasya pitustava yaśasvinaḥ||8||

9sa evamukto duḥkhārtaḥ satyavāgṛṣisattamaḥ
|
sarvalokavināśāya matiṃ cakre mahāmanāḥ||9||

10taṃ tathā niścitātmānaṃ sa mahātmā mahātapāḥ
|
ṛṣirbrahmavidāṃ śraṣṭho maitrāvaruṇirantyadhīḥ||10||

11vasiṣṭho vārayāmāsa hetunā yena tacchṛṇu
|
vasiṣṭha uvāca|
kṛtavīrya iti khyāto babhūva pṛthivīpatiḥ||11||

12yājyo vedavidāṃ loke bhṛgūṇāṃ pārthivarṣabhaḥ
|
sa tānagrabhujastāta dhānyena ca dhanena ca||12||

13somānte tarpayāmāsa vipulena viśāṃpatiḥ
|
tasminnṛpatiśārdūle svaryāte'tha kathaṃcana||13||

14babhūva tatkuleyānāṃ dravyakāryamupasthitam
|
bhṛgūṇāṃ tu dhanaṃ jñātvā rājānaḥ sarva eva te||14||

15yāciṣṇavo'bhijagmustāṃstato bhārgavasattamān
|
bhūmau tu nidadhuḥ kecidbhṛgavo dhanamakṣayam||15||

16daduḥ keciddvijātibhyo jñātvā kṣatriyato bhayam
|
bhṛhavastu daduḥ kecitteṣāṃ vittaṃ yathepsitam||16||

17kṣatriyāṇāṃ tadā tāta kāraṇāntaradarśanāt
|
tato mahītalaṃ tāta kṣatriyeṇa yadṛcchayā||17||

18khanatādhigataṃ vittaṃ kenacciddhṛguveśmani
|
tadvittaṃ dadṛśuḥ sarve sametāḥ kṣatriyarṣabhāḥ||18||

19avamanya tataḥ krodhādbhṛgūṃstāñcharaṇagatān
|
nijaghnuḥ parameṣvāsāḥ sarvāṃstānniśitaiḥ śaraiḥ||19||

20āgarbhādavakṛntantaśceruḥ sarvāṃ vasundharām
|
tata ucchidyamāneṣu bhṛguṣvevaṃ bhayāttadā||20||

21bhṛgupatnyo giriṃ durgaṃ himavantaṃ prapedire
|
tāsāmanyatamā garbhaṃ bhayāddadhre mahaujasam||21||

22ūruṇaikena vābhorūrbhartuḥ kulavivṛddhaye
|
tadgarbhamupalabhyāśu brāhmaṇī yā bhayārditā||22||

23gatvaikā kathayāmāsa kṣatriyāṇāmupahvare
|
tataste kṣatriyā jagmustaṃ garbhaṃ hantumudyatāḥ||23||

24dadṛśurbrāhmaṇīṃ te'tha dīpyamānāṃ svatejasā
|
atha garbhaḥ sa bhittvoruṃ brāhmaṇyā nirjagāma ha||24||

25muṣṇandṛṣṭīḥ kṣatriyāṇāṃ madhyāhna iva bhāskaraḥ
|
tataścakṣurvihīnāste giridurgeṣu babhramuḥ||25||

26tataste mohamāpannā rājāno naṣṭadṛṣṭayaḥ
|
brāhmaṇīṃ śaraṇaṃ jagmurdṛṣṭyarthaṃ tāmaninditām||26||

27ūcuścaināṃ mahābhāgāṃ kṣatriyāste vicetasaḥ
|
jyotiḥprahīṇā duḥkhārtāḥ śāntārciṣa ivāgnayaḥ||27||

28bhagavatyāḥ prasādena gacchetkṣatraṃ sacakṣuṣam
|
upāramya ca gacchema sahitāḥ pāpakarmiṇaḥ||28||

29saputrā tvaṃ prasādaṃ naḥ kartumarhasi śobhane
|
punardṛṣṭipradānena rājñaḥ saṃtrātumarhasi||29||
