Mahābhārata 9.4 (Śalyaparvan)

1sañjaya uvāca|
patitānrathanīḍāṃśca rathāṃścāpi mahātmanām
|
raṇe ca nihatānnāgān dṛṣṭvā pattīṃśca māriṣa||1||

2āyodhanaṃ cātighoraṃ rudrasyākrīḍasannibham
|
aprakhyātiṃ gatānāṃ tu rājñāṃ śatasahasraśaḥ||2||

3vimukhe tava putre tu śokopahatacetasi
|
bhṛśodvigneṣu sainyeṣu dṛṣṭvā pārthasya vikramam||3||

4dhyāyamāneṣu sainyeṣu duḥkhaṃ prāpteṣu bhārata
|
balānāṃ mathyamānānāṃ śrutvā ninadamuttamam||4||

5abhijñānaṃ narendrāṇāṃ vikṣataṃ prekṣya saṃyuge
|
kṛpāviṣṭaḥ kṛpo rājanvayaḥśīlasamanvitaḥ||5||

6abravīttatra tejasvī so'bhisṛtya janādhipam
|
duryodhanaṃ manyuvaśādvākyaṃ vākyaviśāradaḥ||6||

7duryodhana nibodhedaṃ yattvāṃ vakṣyāmi kaurava
|
śrutvā kuru mahārāja yadi te rocate'nagha||7||

8na yuddhadharmācchreyān vai panthā rājendra vidyate
|
yaṃ samāśritya yuddhyante kṣatriyāḥ kṣatriyarṣabha||8||

9putro bhrātā pitā caiva svasrīyo mātulastathā
|
sambandhibāndhavāścaiva yoddhyā vai kṣatrajīvinā||9||

10vadhe caiva paro dharmastathādharmaḥ palāyane
|
te sma ghorāṃ samāpannā jīvikā jīvitārthinaḥ||10||

11tadatra prativakṣyāmi kiñcideva hitaṃ vacaḥ
|
hate bhīṣme ca droṇe ca karṇe caiva mahārathe||11||

12jayadrathe ca nihate tava bhrātṛṣu canāgha
|
lakṣmaṇe tava putre ca kiṃ śeṣaṃ paryupāsate||12||

13yeṣu bhāraṃ samāsādya rājye matimakurmahi
|
te santyajya tanūryātāḥ śūrā brahmavidāṃ gatim||13||

14vayaṃ tviha vinā bhūtā guṇavadbhirmahārathaiḥ
|
kṛpaṇaṃ vartayiṣyāma pātayitvā nṛpānbahūn||14||

15sarvairatha ca jīvadbhirbībhatsurāparājitaḥ
|
kṛṣṇanetro mahābāhurdevairapi durāsadaḥ||15||

16indrakārmukatulyābhamindraketumivocchritam
|
vānaraṃ ketumāsādya sañcacāla mahācamūḥ||16||

17siṃhanādācca bhīmasya pāñcajanyasvenana ca
|
gāṇḍīvasya ca nirghoṣātsaṃhṛṣyanti manāṃsi naḥ||17||

18carantīva mahāvidyunmuṣṇantī nayanaprabhām
|
alātamiva cāviddhaṃ gāṇḍīvaṃ samadṛśyata||18||

19jāmbūnadavicitraṃ ca dhūyamānaṃ mahaddhanum
|
dṛśyate dikṣu sarvāsu vidyudabhraghaneṣviva||19||

20śvetāśca vegasampannā śaśikāśasamaprabhāḥ
|
pibanta iva cākāśaṃ rathe yuktāstu vājinaḥ||20||

21uhyamānāṃśca kṛṣṇena vāyuneva balāhakāḥ
|
jāmbūnadavicitrāṅgā vahante cārjunaṃ raṇe||21||

22tāvakaṃ tadbalaṃ rājannarjuno'straviśāradaḥ
|
gahanaṃ śiśirāpāye dadāhāgnirivolbaṇaḥ||22||

23gāhamānamanīkāni mahendrasadṛśaprabham
|
dhanañjayamapaśyāma caturdaṣṭramiva dvipam||23||

24vikṣobhayantaṃ senāṃ te trāsayantaṃ ca pārthivān
|
dhanañjayamapaśyāma nalinīmiva kuñjaram||24||

25trāsayantaṃ tathā yodhāndhanurghoṣeṇa pāṇḍavam
|
bhūya enamapaśyāma siṃhaṃ mṛgagaṇāniva||25||

26sarvalokamaheṣvāsau vṛṣabhau sarvadhanvinām
|
āmuktakavacau kṛṣṇau lokamadhye viceratuḥ||26||

27adya saptadaśāhāni vartamānasya bhārata
|
saṅgrāmasyātighorasya vadhyatāṃ cābhito yudhi||27||

28vāyuneva vidhūtāni tava sainyāni sarvataḥ
|
śaradambhodajālāni vyaśīryanta samantataḥ||28||

29tāṃ nāvamiva paryastāṃ vātadhūtāṃ mahārṇave
|
tava senāṃ mahārāja savyasācī vyakampayat||29||

30kva nu te sūtaputro'bhūtkva nu droṇaḥ sahānugaḥ
|
ahaṃ kva ca kva cātmā te hārdikyaśca tathā kva nu||30||

31duḥśāsanaśca te bhrātā bhrātṛbhiḥ sahitaḥ kva nu
|
bāṇagocarasamprāptaṃ prekṣya caiva jayadratham||31||

32sambandhinaste bhrātṝṃśca sahāyānmātulāṃstathā
|
sarvānvikramya miṣato lokamākrasya mūrdhani||32||

33jayadratho hato rājankiṃ nu śeṣamupāsmahe
|
ko hīha sa pumānasti yo vijeṣyati pāṇḍavam||33||

34tasya cāstrāṇi divyāni vividhāni mahātmanaḥ
|
gāṇḍīvasya na nirghoṣo dhairyāṇi harate hi naḥ||34||

35naṣṭacandrā yathā rātriḥ seneyaṃ hatanāyakā
|
nāgabhagnadrumā śuṣkā nadīvākulatāṃ gatā||35||

36dhvajinyāṃ hatanetrāyāṃ yatheṣṭaṃ śvetavāhanaḥ
|
cariṣyati mahābāhuḥ kakṣeṣvagniriva jvalan||36||

37sātyakeścaiva yo vego bhīmasenasya cobhayoḥ
|
dārayecca girīnsarvān śoṣayeccaiva sāgarān||37||

38uvāca vākyaṃ yadbhīmaḥ sabhāmadhye viśāmpate
|
kṛtaṃ tatsaphalaṃ tena bhūyaścaiva kariṣyati||38||

39pramukhasthe tadā karṇe balaṃ pāṇḍavarakṣitam
|
durāsadaṃ tadā guptaṃ vyūḍhaṃ gāṇḍīvadhanvanā||39||

40yuṣmābhistāni cīrṇāni yānyasādhūni sādhuṣu
|
akāraṇakṛtānyeva teṣāṃ vaḥ phalamāgatam||40||

41ātmano'rthe tvayā loko yatnataḥ sarva āhṛtaḥ
|
sa te saṃśāyitastāta ātmā vai bharatarṣabha||41||

42rakṣa duryodhanātmānamātmā sarvasya bhājanam
|
bhinne hi bhājane tāta diśo gacchati tadgatam||42||

43hīyamānena vai sandhiḥ paryeṣṭavyaḥ samena vā
|
vigraho vardhamānena matireṣā bṛhaspateḥ||43||

44te vayaṃ pāṇḍuputrebhyo hīnā sma balaśaktitaḥ
|
tadatra pāṇḍavaiḥ sārdhaṃ sandhiṃ manye kṣamaṃ prabho||44||

45na jānīte hi yaḥ śreyaḥ śreyasaścāvamanyate
|
sa kṣipraṃ bhraśyate rājyānna ca śreyo'nuvindate||45||

46praṇipatya hi rājānaṃ rājyaṃ yadi labhemahi
|
śreyaḥ syānna tu mauḍhyena rājan gantuḥ parābhavam||46||

47vaicitravīryavacanātkṛpāśīlo yudhiṣṭhiraḥ
|
viniyuñjīta rājye tvāṃ govindavacanena ca||47||

48yad brūyāddhi hṛṣīkeśo rājānamaparājitam
|
arjunaṃ bhīmasenaṃ ca sarve kuryurasaṃśayam||48||

49nātikramiṣyate kṛṣṇo vacanaṃ kauravasya tu
|
dhṛtarāṣṭrasya manye'haṃ nāpi kṛṣṇasya pāṇḍavaḥ||49||

50etatkṣemamahaṃ manye na ca pārthaiśca vigraham
|
na tvāṃ bravīmi kārpaṇyānna prāṇaparirakṣaṇāt||50||

51pathyaṃ rājan bravīmi tvāṃ tatparāsuḥ smariṣyasi
|
iti vṛddho vilapyaitatkṛpaḥ śāradvato vacaḥ
|
dīrghamuṣṇaṃ ca niḥśvasya śuśoca ca mumoha ca||51||
