Mahābhārata 12.213 (Śāntiparvan)

1bhīṣma uvāca|
rajasā sādhyate mohastamasā bharatarṣabha
|
krodhalobhau, bhayaṃ, darpa eteṣāṃ sādanācchuciḥ ||1||

2paramaṃ paramātmānaṃ devamakṣayamavyayam
|
viṣṇumavyaktasaṃsthānaṃ vidustaṃ devasattamam ||2||

3tasya māyāpinaddhāṅgā naṣṭajñānā vicetasaḥ
|
mānavā jñānasammohāttataḥ krodhaṃ prayānti vai ||3||

4krodhātkāmamavāpyātha lobhamohau ca mānavāḥ
|
mānadarpāvahaṅkāramahaṅkārāttataḥ kriyāḥ ||4||

5kriyābhiḥ snehasambandhātsnehācchokamanantaram
|
sukhaduḥkhakriyārambhājjanmājanmakṛtakṣaṇāḥ ||5||

6janmato garbhavāsaṃ tu śukraśoṇitasambhavam
|
purīṣamūtravikledaṃ śoṇitaprabhavāvilam ||6||

7tṛṣṇābhibhūtastairbaddhastānevābhipariplavan
|
saṃsāratantravāhinyastatra buddhyeta yoṣitaḥ ||7||

8prakṛtyā kṣetrabhūtāstā narāḥ kṣetrajñalakṣaṇāḥ
|
tasmādevāviśeṣeṇa naro'tīyādviśeṣataḥ ||8||

9kṛtyā hyetā ghorarūpā mohayantyavicakṣaṇān
|
rajasyantarhitā mūrtirindriyāṇāṃ sanātanī ||9||

10tasmāttadātmakādrāgādbījājjāyanti jantavaḥ
|
svadehajānasvasaṃjñān yadvadaṅgātkṛmīṃstyajet
|
svasaṃjñānasvakāṃstadvatsutasaṃjñān kṛmīṃstyajet ||10||

11śukrato rasataścaiva dehājjāyanti jantavaḥ
|
svabhāvātkarmayogādvā tānupekṣeta buddhimān ||11||

12rajastamasi paryastaṃ sattvaṃ ca rajasi sthitam
|
jñānādhiṣṭhānamavyaktaṃ buddhyahaṅkāralakṣaṇam ||12||

13tadbījaṃ dehināmāhustadbījaṃ jīvasaṃjñitam
|
karmaṇā kālayuktena saṃsāraparivartanam ||13||

14ramatyayaṃ yathā svapne manasā dehavāniva
|
karmagarbhairguṇairdehī garbhe tadupalabhyate ||14||

15karmaṇā bījabhūtena codyate yadyadindriyam
|
jāyate tadahaṅkārādrāgayuktena cetasā ||15||

16śabdarāgācchrotramasya jāyate bhāvitātmanaḥ
|
rūparāgāttathā cakṣurghrāṇaṃ gandhacikīrṣayā ||16||

17sparśane tvaktathā vāyuḥ prāṇāpānavyapāśrayaḥ
|
vyānodānau samānaśca pañcadhā dehayāpanam ||17||

18saṃjātairjāyate gātraiḥ karmajairvarṣmaṇā vṛtaḥ
|
duḥkhādyantairduḥkhamadhyairnaraḥ śārīramānasaiḥ ||18||

19duḥkhaṃ vidyādupādānādabhimānācca vardhate
|
tyāgāttebhyo nirodhaḥ syānnirodhajño vimucyate ||19||

20indriyāṇāṃ rajasyeva pralayaprabhavāvubhau
|
parīkṣya saṃcaredvidvānyathāvacchāstracakṣuṣā ||20||

21jñānendriyāṇīndriyārthānnopasarpantyatarṣulam
|
hīnaiśca karaṇairdehī na dehaṃ punararhati ||21||
