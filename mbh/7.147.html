Mahābhārata 7.147 (Droṇaparvan)

1dhṛtarāṣṭra uvāca|
tasminvinihate vīre saindhave savyasācinā
|
māmakā yadakurvanta tanmamācakṣva sañjaya||1||

2sañjaya uvāca|
saindhavaṃ nihataṃ dṛṣṭvā raṇe pārthena bhārata
|
amarṣavaśamāpannaḥ kṛpaḥ śāradvatastataḥ||2||

3mahatā śaravarṣeṇa pāṇḍavaṃ samavākirat
|
drauṇiścābhyadravadrājanrathamāsthāya phālgunam||3||

4tāvetau rathināṃ śreṣṭhau rathābhyāṃ rathasattamau
|
ubhāvubhayatastīkṣṇairviśikhairabhyavarṣatām||4||

5sa tathā śaravarṣābhyāṃ sumahadbhyāṃ mahābhujaḥ
|
pīḍyamānaḥ parāmārtimagamadrathināṃ varaḥ||5||

6so'jighāṃsurguruṃ saṃkhye gurostanayameva ca
|
cakārācāryakaṃ tatra kuntīputro dhanañjayaḥ||6||

7astrairastrāṇi saṃvārya drauṇeḥ śāradvatasya ca
|
mandavegāniṣūṃstābhyāmajighāṃsuravāsṛjat||7||

8te cāpi bhṛśamabhyaghnanviśikhāḥ pārthacoditāḥ
|
bahutvāttu parāmārtiṃ śarāṇāṃ tāvagacchatām||8||

9atha śāradvato rājankaunteyaśarapīḍitaḥ
|
avāsīdadrathopasthe mūrcchāmabhijagāma ha||9||

10vihvalaṃ tamabhijñāya bhartāraṃ śarapīḍitam
|
hato'yamiti ca jñātvā sārathistamapāvahat||10||

11tasmin bhagne mahārāja kṛpe śāradvate yudhi
|
aśvatthāmāpyapāyāsītpāṇḍaveyādrathāntaram||11||

12dṛṣṭvā śāradvataṃ pārtho mūcchitaṃ śarapīḍitam
|
ratha eva maheṣvāsaḥ sakṛpaṃ paryadevayat||12||

13aśrupūrṇamukho dīno vacanaṃ cedamabravīt
|
paśyannidaṃ mahāprājñaḥ kṣattā rājānamuktavān||13||

14kulāntakaraṇe pāpe jātamātre suyodhane
|
nīyatāṃ paralokāya sādhvayaṃ kulapāṃsanaḥ||14||

15asmāddhi kurumukhyānāṃ mahadutpatsyate bhayam
|
tadidaṃ samanuprāptaṃ vacanaṃ satyavādinaḥ||15||

16tatkṛte hyadya paśyāmi śaratalpagataṃ gurum
|
dhigastu kṣātramācāraṃ dhigastu balapauruṣam||16||

17ko hi brāhmaṇamācāryamabhidruhyeta mādṛśaḥ
|
ṛṣiputro mamācāryo droṇasya paramaḥ sakhā||17||

18eṣa śete rathopastha kṛpo madbāṇapīḍitaḥ
|
akāmayānena mayā viśikhairardito bhṛśam||18||

19avasīdan rathopasthe prāṇānpīḍayatīva me
|
putraśokābhitaptena śarairabhyarditena ca||19||

20abhyasto bahubhirbāṇairdaśadharmagatena vai
|
śocayatyeṣa niyataṃ bhūyaḥ putravadhāddhi mām||20||

21kṛpaṇaṃ svarathe sannaṃ paśya kṛṣṇa yathāgatam
|
upākṛtya tu vai vidyāmācāryebhyo nararṣabhāḥ||21||

22prayacchantīha ye kāmāndevatvamupayānti te
|
ye ca vidyāmupādāya gurubhyaḥ puruṣādhamāḥ||22||

23ghnanti tāneva durvṛttāste vai nirayagāminaḥ
|
tadidaṃ narakāyādya kṛtaṃ karma mayā dhruvam||23||

24ācāryaṃ śaravarṣeṇa rathe sādayatā kṛpam
|
yattatpūrvamupākurvannastraṃ māmabravītkṛpaḥ||24||

25na kathañcana kauravya prahartavyaṃ gurāviti
|
tadidaṃ vacanaṃ sādhorācāryasya mahātmanaḥ||25||

26nānuṣṭhitaṃ tamevājī viśikhairabhivarṣatā
|
namastasmai supūjyāya gautamāyāpalāyine||26||

27dhigastu mama vārṣṇeya yadasmai praharāmyaham
|
tathā vilapamāne tu savyasācini taṃ prati||27||

28saindhavaṃ nihataṃ dṛṣṭvā rādheyaḥ samupādravat
|
tamāpatantaṃ rādheyamarjunasya rathaṃ prati||28||

29pāñcālyau sātyakiścaiva sahasā samupādravan
|
upāyāntaṃ tu rādheyaṃ dṛṣṭvā pārtho mahārathaḥ||29||

30prahasandevakīputramidaṃ vacanamabravīt
|
eṣa prayātyādhirathiḥ sātyakeḥ syandanaṃ prati||30||

31na mṛṣyati hataṃ nūnaṃ bhūriśravasamāhave
|
yatra yātyeṣa tatra tvaṃ codayāśvān janārdana||31||

32na saumadattipadavīṃ gamayetsātyakiṃ vṛṣaḥ
|
evamukto mahābāhuḥ keśavaḥ savyasācinā||32||

33pratyuvāca mahātejāḥ kālayuktamidaṃ vacaḥ
|
alameṣa mahābāhuḥ karṇāyaiko'pi pāṇḍava||33||

34kiṃ punardraupadeyābhyāṃ sahitaḥ sātvatarṣabhaḥ
|
na ca tāvatkṣamaḥ pārtha tava karṇena saṅgaraḥ||34||

35prajvalantī maholkeva tiṣṭhatyasya hi vāsavī
|
tvadarthaṃ pūjyamānaiṣā rakṣyate paravīrahan||35||

36ataḥ karṇaḥ prayātvatra sātvatasya yathātathā
|
ahaṃ jñāsyāmi kaunteya kālamasya durātmanaḥ
|
yatrainaṃ viśikhaistīkṣṇaiḥ pātayiṣyasi bhūtale||36||

37dhṛtarāṣṭra uvāca|
yo'sau karṇena vīrasya vārṣṇeyasya samāgamaḥ
|
hate tu bhūriśravasi saindhave ca nipātite||37||

38sātyakiścāpi virathaḥ kaṃsamārūḍhavān ratham
|
cakrarakṣau ca pāñcālyau tanmamācakṣva sañjaya||38||

39sañjaya uvāca|
hanta te vartayiṣyāmi yathā vṛttaṃ mahāraṇe
|
śuśrūṣasva sthiro bhūtvā durācaritamātmanaḥ||39||

40pūrvameva hi kṛṣṇasya manogatamidaṃ prabho
|
vijetavyo yathā vīraḥ sātyakiḥ saumadattinā||40||

41atītānāgate rājansa hi vetti janārdanaḥ
|
tataḥ sūtaṃ samāhūya dārukaṃ saṃdideśa ha||41||

42ratho me yujyatāṃ kalyamiti rājanmahābalaḥ
|
na hi devā na gandharvā na yakṣoragarākṣasāḥ||42||

43mānavā vā'pi jetāraḥ kṛṣṇayoḥ santi kecana
|
pitāmahapurogāśca devāḥ siddhāśca taṃ viduḥ||43||

44tayoḥ prabhāvamatulaṃ śṛṇu yuddhaṃ tu tattathā
|
sātyakiṃ virathaṃ dṛṣṭvā karṇaṃ cābhyudyataṃ raṇe||44||

45dadhmau śaṅkhaṃ mahānādamārṣabheṇātha mādhavaḥ
|
dāruko'vetya saṃdeśaṃ śrutvā śaṅkhasya ca svanam||45||

46rathamanvānayattasmai suparṇocchritaketanam
|
sa keśavasyānumate rathaṃ dārukasaṃyutam||46||

47āruroha śineḥ pautro jvalanādityasannibham
|
kāmagaiḥ śaibyasugrīvameghapuṣpabalāhakaiḥ||47||

48hayodagrairmahāvegairhemabhāṇḍavibhūṣitaiḥ
|
yuktaṃ samāruhya ca taṃ vimānapratimaṃ ratham||48||

49abhyadravata rādheyaṃ pravapansāyakānbahūn
|
cakrarakṣāvapi tadā yudhāmanyūttamaujasau||49||

50dhanañjayarathaṃ hitvā rādheyaṃ pratyudīyatuḥ
|
rādheyo'pi mahārāja śaravarṣaṃ samutsṛjan||50||

51abhyadravatsusaṃkruddho raṇe śaineyamacyutam
|
naiva daivaṃ na gāndharvaṃ nāsuraṃ na ca rākṣasam||51||

52tādṛśaṃ bhuvi no yuddhaṃ divi vā śrutamityuta
|
upāramata tatsainyaṃ sarathāśva-nara-dvipam||52||

53tayordṛṣṭvā mahārāja karma saṃmūḍhacetasaḥ
|
sarve ca samapaśyanta tadyuddhamatimānuṣam||53||

54tayornṛvarayo rājansārathyaṃ dārukasya ca
|
gatapratyāgatāvṛttairmaṇḍalaiḥ sannivartanaiḥ ||54||

55sārathestu rathasthasya kāśyapeyasya vismitāḥ
|
nabhastalagatāścaiva deva-gandharva-dānavāḥ||55||

56atīvāvahitā draṣṭuṃ karṇaśaineyayo raṇam
|
mitrārthe tau parākrāntau śuṣmiṇau spardhinau raṇe||56||

57karṇaścāmarasaṃkāśo yuyudhānaśca sātyakiḥ
|
anyonyaṃ tau mahārāja śaravarṣairavarṣatām||57||

58pramamātha śineḥ pautraṃ karṇaḥ sāyakavṛṣṭibhiḥ
|
amṛṣyamāṇo nidhanaṃ kauravyajalasandhayoḥ||58||

59karṇaḥ śokasamāviṣṭo mahoraga iva śvasan
|
sa śaineyaṃ raṇe kruddhaḥ pradahanniva cakṣuṣā||59||

60abhyadhāvata vegena punaḥ punararindama
|
taṃ tu sakrodhamālokya sātyakiḥ pratyayuddhyata||60||

61mahatā śaravarṣeṇa gajaṃ prati gajo yathā
|
tau sametau naravyāghrau vyāghrāviva tarasvinau||61||

62anyonyaṃ santatakṣāte raṇe'nupamavikramau
|
tataḥ karṇaṃ śineḥ pautraḥ sarvapārasavaiḥ śaraiḥ||62||

63bibheda sarvagātreṣu punaḥ punararindama
|
sārathiṃ cāsya bhallena rathanīḍādapātayat||63||

64aśvāṃśca caturaḥ śvetānnijaghāna śitaiḥ śaraiḥ
|
chittvā dhvajaṃ rathaṃ caiva śatadhā puruṣarṣabha||64||

65cakāra virathaṃ karṇaṃ tava putrasya paśyataḥ
|
tato vimanaso rājaṃstāvakāste mahārathāḥ||65||

66vṛṣasenaḥ karṇasutaḥ śalyo madrādhipastathā
|
droṇaputrañca śaineyaṃ sarvataḥ paryavārayan||66||

67tataḥ paryākulaṃ sarvaṃ na prājñāyata kiñcana
|
tathā sātyakinā vīre virathe sūtaje kṛte||67||

68hāhākārastato rājansarvasainyeṣvabhūnmahān
|
karṇo'pi viratho rājansātvatena kṛtaḥ śaraiḥ||68||

69duryodhanarathaṃ tūrṇamāruroha viniḥśvasan
|
mānayaṃstava putrasya bālyātprabhṛti sauhṛdam||69||

70kṛtāṃ rājyapradānena pratijñāṃ paripālayan
|
tathā tu virathaṃ karṇaṃ putrāṃśca tava pārthiva||70||

71duḥśāsanamukhānvīrānnāvadhītsātyakirvaśī
|
rakṣanpratijñāṃ bhīmena pārthena ca purākṛtām||71||

72virathānvihvalāṃścakre na tu prāṇairvyayojayat
|
bhīmasenena tu vadhaḥ putrāṇāṃ te pratiśrutaḥ||72||

73anudyūte ca pārthena vadhaḥ karṇasya saṃśrutaḥ
|
vadhe tvakurvanyatnaṃ te tasya karṇamukhāstadā||73||

74nāśaknuvaṃstato hantuṃ sātyakiṃ pravarā rathāḥ
|
drauṇiśca kṛtavarmā ca tathaivānye mahārathāḥ||74||

75nirjitā dhanuṣaikena śataśaḥ kṣatriyarṣabhāḥ
|
kāṃkṣatā paralokaṃ ca dharmarājasya ca priyam||75||

76kṛṣṇayoḥ sadṛśo vīrye sātyakiḥ śatrutāpanaḥ
|
jitavānsarvasainyāni tāvakāni hasanniva||76||

77kṛṣṇo vāpi bhavelloke pārtho vāpi dhanurdharaḥ
|
śaineyo vā naravyāghra caturthastu na vidyate||77||

78dhṛtarāṣṭra uvāca|
ajayyaṃ vāsudevasya rathamāsthāya sātyakiḥ
|
virathaṃ kṛtavānkarṇaṃ vāsudevasamo yudhi||78||

79dārukeṇa samāyuktaḥ svabāhubaladarpitaḥ
|
kaccidanyaṃ samārūḍhaḥ sātyakiḥ śatrutāpanaḥ||79||

80etadicchāmyahaṃ śrotuṃ kuśalo hyasi bhāṣitum
|
asahyaṃ tamahaṃ manye tanmamācakṣva sañjaya||80||

81sañjaya uvāca|
śṛṇu rājanyathāvṛttaṃ rathamanyaṃ mahāmatiḥ
|
dārukasyānujastūrṇaṃ kalpanāvidhikalpitam||81||

82āyasaiḥ kāñcanaiścāpi paṭṭaiḥ sannaddhakūbaram
|
tārāsahasrakhacitaṃ siṃhadhvajapatākinam||82||

83aśvaitajavairyuktaṃ hemabhāṇḍaparicchadaiḥ
|
saindhavairindusaṃkāśaiḥ sarvaśabdātigairdṛḍhaiḥ||83||

84citrakāścanasannāhairvājimukhyairviśāṃpate
|
ghaṇṭājālākularavaṃ śakti-tomaravidyutam||84||

85yuktaṃ sāṃgrāmikairdravyairbahuśastraparicchadaiḥ
|
rathaṃ saṃpādayāmāsa meghagaṃbhīraniḥsvanam||85||

86taṃ samāruhya śaineyastava sainyamupādravat
|
dāruko'pi yathākāmaṃ prayayau keśavāntikam||86||

87karṇasyāpi rathaṃ rājañśaṃkha-gokṣīrapāṇḍuraiḥ
|
citrakāñcanasannāhaiḥ sadaśvairyegavattaraiḥ||87||

88hemakakṣyādhvajopetaṃ klaptayantrapatākinam
|
agryaṃ rathaṃ suyaṃtāraṃ bahuśastraparicchadam||88||

89upājahrustamāsthāya karṇo'pyabhyadravadripūn
|
etatte sarvamākhyātaṃ yanmāṃ tvaṃ paripṛcchasi||89||

90bhūyaścāpi nibodhemaṃ tavāpanayajaṃ kṣayam
|
ekatriṃśattava sutā bhīmasenena pātitāḥ||90||

91durmukhaṃ pramukha kṛtvā satataṃ citrayodhinam
|
śataśo nihatāḥ śūrāḥ sātvatenārjunena ca||91||

92bhīṣmaṃ pramukhataḥ kṛtvā bhagadattaṃ ca bhārata
|
evameṣa kṣayo vṛtto rājandurmantrite tava||92||
