Mahābhārata 7.59 (Droṇaparvan)

1nārada uvāca| rāmaṃ dāśarathiṃ caiva mṛtaṃ sṛñjaya śuśruma
|
yaṃ prajā anvamodanta pitā putrānivaurasān ||1||

2asaṅkhyeyā guṇā yasminnāsannamitatejasi
|
yaścaturdaśa varṣāṇi nideśāt pituracyutaḥ ||2||

3vane vanitayā sārdhamavasallakṣmaṇāgrajaḥ
|
jaghāna ca janasthāne rākṣasān manujarṣabhaḥ ||3||

4tapasvināṃ rakṣaṇārthaṃ sahasrāṇi caturdaśa
|
tatraiva vasatastasya rāvaṇo nāma rākṣasaḥ ||4||

5jahāra bhāryāṃ vaidehīṃ saṃmohyainaṃ sahānujam
|
tamāgaskāriṇaṃ rāmaḥ paulastyamajitaṃ paraiḥ ||5||

6jaghāna samare kruddhaḥ pureva tryambako'ndhakam
|
surāsurairavadhyaṃ taṃ devabrāhmaṇakaṇṭakam ||6||

7jaghāna sa mahābāhuḥ paulastyaṃ sagaṇaṃ raṇe
|
sa prajānugrahaṃ kṛtvā tridaśairabhipūjitaḥ ||7||

8vyāpya kṛtsnaṃ jagatkīrtyā surarṣigaṇasevitaḥ
|
sa prāpya vividhaṃ rājyaṃ sarvabhūtānukampakaḥ ||8||

9ājahāra mahāyajñaṃ prajā dharmeṇa pālayan
|
nirargalaṃ sajārūthyamaśvamedhaṃ ca taṃ vibhuḥ ||9||

10ājahāra sureśasya haviṣā muda‌māharat
|
anyaiśca vividhairyajñairīje bahuguṇairnṛpaḥ ||10||

11kṣutpipāse'jayadrāmaḥ sarvarogāṃśca dehinām
|
satataṃ guṇasampanno dīpyamānaḥ svatejasā ||11||

12ati sarvāṇi bhūtāni rāmo dāśarathirbabhau
|
ṛṣīṇāṃ devatānāṃ ca mānuṣāṇāṃ ca sarvaśaḥ ||12||

13pṛthivyāṃ sahavāso'bhūdrāme rājyaṃ praśāsati
|
nāhīyata tadā prāṇaḥ prāṇināṃ na tadanyathā ||13||

14prāṇo'pānaḥ samānaśca rāme rājyaṃ praśāsati
|
paryadīpyanta tejāṃsi tadanarthāśca nābhavan ||14||

15dīrghāyuṣaḥ prajāḥ sarvā yuvā na mriyate tadā
|
vedaiścaturbhiḥ suprītāḥ prāpnuvanti divaukasaḥ ||15||

16havyaṃ kavyaṃ ca vividhaṃ niṣpūrtaṃ hutameva ca
|
adaṃśamaśakā deśā naṣṭavyālasarīsṛpāḥ ||16||

17nāpsu prāṇabhṛtāṃ mṛtyurnākāle jvalano'dahat
|
adharmarucayo lubdhā mūrkhā vā nābhavaṃstadā ||17||

18śiṣṭeṣṭaprājñakarmāṇaḥ sarve varṇāstadā'bhavan
|
svadhāṃ pūjāṃ ca rakṣobhirjanasthāne praṇāśitām ||18||

19prādānnihatya rakṣāṃsi pitṛdevebhya īśvaraḥ
|
sahasraputrāḥ puruṣā daśavarṣaśatāyuṣaḥ ||19||

20na ca jyeṣṭhāḥ kaniṣṭhebhyastadā śrāddhānyakārayan
|
śyāmo yuvā lohitākṣo mattamātaṅgavikramaḥ ||20||

21ājānubāhuḥ subhujaḥ siṃhaskandho mahābalaḥ
|
daśavarṣasahasrāṇi daśavarṣaśatāni ca ||21||

22sarvabhūtamanaḥkānto rāmo rājyamakārayat
|
rāmo rāmo rāma iti prajānāmabhavat kathā ||22||

23rāmādrāmaṃ jagadabhūdrāme rājyaṃ praśāsati
|
caturvidhāḥ prajā rāmaḥ svargaṃ nītvā divaṃ gataḥ ||23||

24ātmānaṃ sampratiṣṭhāpya rājavaṃśamihāṣṭadhā
|
sa cenmamāra sṛñjaya caturbhadratarastvayā ||24||

25putrāt puṇyatarastubhyaṃ mā putramanutapyathāḥ
|
ayajvānamadākṣiṇyamabhiśvaityeti vyāharan ||25||
