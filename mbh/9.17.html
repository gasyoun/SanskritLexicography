Mahābhārata 9.17 (Śalyaparvan)

1sañjaya uvāca|
athānyaddhanurādāya balavānvegavattaram
|
yudhiṣṭhiraṃ madrapatirbhitvā siṃha ivānadat||1||

2tataḥ sa śaravarṣeṇa parjanya iva vṛṣṭimān
|
abhyavarṣadameyātmā kṣatriyaṃ kṣatriyarṣabhaḥ||2||

3sātyakiṃ daśabhirviddhvā bhīmasenaṃ tribhiḥ śaraiḥ
|
sahadevaṃ tribhirviddhvā yudhiṣṭhiramapīḍayat||3||

4tāṃstānanyānmaheṣvāsān sāśvānsarathakūbarān
|
ardayāmāsa viśikhairulkābhiriva kuñjarān||4||

5kuñjarānkuñjarārohānaśvānaśvaprayāyinaḥ
|
rathāṃśca rathinaḥ sārdhaṃ jaghāna rathināṃ varaḥ||5||

6bāhūṃściccheda tarasā sāyudhānketanāni ca
|
cakāra ca mahīṃ yodhaistīrṇāṃ vedīṃ kuśairiva||6||

7tathā tamarisainyāni ghnantaṃ mṛtyumivāntakam
|
parivavrurbhṛśaṃ kruddhāḥ pāṇḍupāñcālasomakāḥ||7||

8taṃ bhīmasenaśca śineśca naptā mādryāśca putrau puruṣapravīrau
|
samāgataṃ bhīmabalena rājñā paryāptamanyonyamathāhvayanta||8||

9tatastu śūrāḥ samare narendra nareśvaraṃ prāpya yudhāṃ variṣṭham
|
āvārya cainaṃ samare nṛvīrā jaghnuḥ śaraiḥ patribhirugravegaiḥ||9||

10saṃrakṣito bhīmasenena rājā mādrīsutābhyāmatha mādhavena
|
madrādhipaṃ patribhirugravegaiḥ stanāntare dharmasuto nijaghne||10||

11tato raṇe tāvakānāṃ rathaughāḥ samīkṣya madrādhipatiṃ śarārtam
|
pāryavavruḥ pravarāste susajjā duryodhanasyānumate purastāt||11||

12tato drutaṃ madrajanādhipo raṇe yudhiṣṭhiraṃ saptabhirabhyaviddhyat
|
taṃ cāpi pārtho navabhiḥ pṛṣatkairvivyādha rājaṃstumule mahātmā||12||

13ākarṇapūrṇāyatasamprayuktaiḥ śaraistadā saṃyati tailadhautaiḥ
|
anyonyamācchādayatāṃ mahārathau madrādhipaścāpi yudhiṣṭhiraśca||13||

14tatastu tūrṇaṃ samare mahārathau parasparasyāntaramīkṣamāṇau
|
śarairbhṛśaṃ vivyadhaturnṛpottamau mahābalau śatrubhirapradhṛṣyau||14||

15tayordhanurjyātalaniḥsvano mahān mahendravajrāśanitulyaniḥsvanaḥ
|
parasparaṃ bāṇagaṇairmahātmanoḥ pravarṣatormadrapapāṇḍuvīrayoḥ||15||

16tau ceraturvyāghraśiśuprakāśau mahāvaneṣvāmiṣagṛddhināviva
|
viṣāṇinau nāgavarāvivobhau tatakṣatuḥ saṃyati jātadarpau||16||

17tatastu madrādhipatirmahātmā yudhiṣṭhiraṃ bhīmabalaṃ prasahya
|
vivyādha vīraṃ hṛdaye'tivegaṃ śareṇa sūryāgnisamaprabheṇa||17||

18tato'tividdho'tha yudhiṣṭhiro'pi susamprayuktena śareṇa rājan
|
jaghāna madrādhipatiṃ mahātmā mudaṃ ca lebhe ṛṣabhaḥ kurūṇām||18||

19tato muhūrtādiva pārthivendro labdhvā saṃjñāṃ krodhasaṃraktanetraḥ
|
śatena pārthaṃ tvarito jaghāna sahasranetrapratimaprabhāvaḥ||19||

20tvaraṃstato dharmasuto mahātmā śalyasya kopānnavabhiḥ pṛṣatkaiḥ
|
bhitvā jhurastapanīyaṃ ca varma jaghāna ṣaḍbhistvaparaiḥ pṛṣatkaiḥ||20||

21tatastu madrādhipatiḥ prakṛṣṭaṃ dhanurvikṛṣya vyasṛjat pṛṣatkān
|
dvābhyāṃ śarābhyāṃ ca tathaiva rājñaściccheda cāpaṃ kurupuṅgavasya||21||

22navaṃ tato'nyatsamare pragṛhya rājā dhanurghorataraṃ mahātmā
|
śalyaṃ vivyādha śaraiḥ samantādyathā mahendro namuciṃ śitāgraiḥ||22||

23tatastu śalyo navabhiḥ pṛṣatkairbhīmasya rājñaśca yudhiṣṭhirasya
|
nikṛtya raukme paṭuvarmaṇī tayorvidārayāmāsa bhujau mahātmā||23||

24tato'pareṇa jvalanārkatejasā kṣureṇa rājño dhanurunmamātha
|
kṛpaśca tasyaiva jaghāna sūtaṃ ṣaḍbhiḥ śaraiḥ so'bhimukhaḥ papāta||24||

25madrādhipaścāpi yudhiṣṭhirasya śaraiścaturbhirnijaghāna vāhān
|
vāhāṃśca hatvā vyakaronmahātmā yodhakṣayaṃ dharmasutasya rājñaḥ||25||

26tathā kṛte rājani bhīmaseno madrādhipasyātha tato mahātmā
|
chittvā dhanurvegavatā śareṇa dvābhyāmavidhyatsubhṛśaṃ narendram||26||

27tathāpareṇāsya jahāra yantuḥ kāyācchiraḥ saṃhananīyamadhyāt
|
jadhāna cāśvāṃścaturaḥ suśīghraṃ tathā bhṛśaṃ kupito bhīmasenaḥ||27||

28tamagraṇīḥ sarvadhanurdharāṇāmekaṃ carantaṃ samare'tivegam
|
bhīmaḥ śatena vyakiraccharāṇāṃ mādrīputraḥ sahadevastathaiva||28||

29taiḥ sāyakairmohitaṃ vīkṣya śalyaṃ bhīmaḥ śarairasya cakarta varma
|
sa bhīmasenena nikṛttavarmā madrādhipaścarmasahasratāram||29||

30pragṛhya khaḍgaṃ ca rathānmahātmā praskandha kuntīsutamabhyadhāvat
|
chittvā ratheṣāṃ nakulasya so'tha yudhiṣṭhiraṃ bhīmabalo'bhyadhāvat||30||

31taṃ cāpi rājānamathotpatantaṃ kruddhaṃ yathaivāntakamāpatantam
|
dhṛṣṭadyumno draupadeyāḥ śikhaṇḍī śineśca naptā sahasā parīyuḥ||31||

32athāsya carmāpratimaṃ nyakṛntadbhīmo mahātmā navabhiḥ pṛṣatkaiḥ
|
khaḍgaṃ ca bhallairnicakarta muṣṭau nadanprahṛṣṭastava sainyamadhye||32||

33tatkarma bhīmasya samīkṣya hṛṣṭāste pāṇḍavānāṃ pravarā rathaughāḥ
|
nādaṃ ca cakrurbhṛśamutsmayantaḥ śaṅkhāṃśca dadhmuḥ śasisannikāśān||33||

34tenātha śabdena vibhīṣaṇena tathābhitaptaṃ balamapradhṛṣyam
|
kāndigbhūtaṃ rudhireṇokṣitāṅgaṃ visaṃjñakalpaṃ ca tadā viṣaṇṇam||34||

35sa madrarājaḥ sahasā vikīrṇo bhīmāgragaiḥ pāṇḍavayodhamukhyaiḥ
|
yudhiṣṭhirasyābhimukhaṃ javena siṃho yathā mṛgahetoḥ prayātaḥ||35||

36sa dharmarājo nihatāśvasūtaḥ krodhena dīpto jvalanaprakāśaḥ
|
dṛṣṭvā ca madrādhipatiṃ sma tūrṇaṃ samabhyadhāvattamariṃ balena||36||

37govindavākyaṃ tvaritaṃ vicintya dadhre matiṃ śalyavināśanāya
|
sa dharmarājo nihatāśvasūto rathe tiṣṭhan śaktimevābhyakākṣat||37||

38taccāpi śalyasya niśamya karma mahātmano bhāgamathāvaśiṣṭam
|
kṛtvā manaḥ śalyavadhe mahātmā yathoktamindrāvarajasya cakre||38||

39sa dharmarājo maṇihemadaṇḍāṃ jagrāha śaktiṃ kanakaprakāśām
|
netre ca dīpte sahasā vivṛtya madrādhipaṃ kruddhamanā niraikṣat||39||

40nirīkṣito'sau naradeva rājñā pūtātmanā nirhṛtakalmaṣeṇa
|
āsīnna yadbhasmasānmadrarājastatadbhutaṃ me pratibhāti rājan||40||

41tatastu śaktiṃ rucirogradaṇḍāṃ maṇipravekojjvalitāṃ pradīptām
|
cikṣepa vegātsubhṛśaṃ mahātmā madrādhipāya pravaraḥ kurūṇām||41||

42dīptāmathaināṃ prahitāṃ balena savisphuliṅgāṃ sahasā patantīm
|
praikṣanta sarve kuravaḥ sametā divo yugānte mahatīmivolkām||42||

43tāṃ kālarātrīmiva pāśahastāṃ yamasya dhātrīmiva cograrūpām
|
sa brahmadaṇḍapratimāmamoghāṃ sasarja yatto yudhi dharmarājaḥ||43||

44gandhasragagryāsanapānabhojanairabhyarcitāṃ pāṇḍusutaiḥ prayatnāt
|
sāṃvartakagnipratimāṃ jvalantīṃ kṛtyāmatharvāṅgirasīmivogrām||44||

45īśānahetoḥ pratinirmitāṃ tāṃ tvaṣṭrā ripūṇāmasudehabhakṣyām
|
bhūmyantarikṣādijalāśayāni prasahya bhūtāni nihantumīśām||45||

46ghaṇṭāpatākāṃ maṇivajranālāṃ vaidūryacitrāṃ tapanīyadaṇḍām
|
tvaṣṭrā prayatnānniyamena klṛptāṃ brahmadviṣāmantakarīmamoghām||46||

47balaprayatnādadhirūḍhavegāṃ mantraiśca ghorairabhimantrya yatnāt
|
sasarja mārgeṇa ca tāṃ pareṇa vadhāya madrādhipatestadānīm||47||

48hato'si pāpetyabhigarjamāno rudro'ndhakāyāntakaraṃ yatheṣum
|
prasārya bāhuṃ sudṛḍhaṃ supāṇiṃ krodhena nṛtyanniva dharmarājaḥ||48||

49tāṃ sarvaśaktyā prahitāṃ suśaktiṃ yudhiṣṭhireṇāprativāryavīryam
|
pratigrahāyābhinanarda śalyaḥ samyagdhutāmagnirivājyadhārām||49||

50sā tasya marmāṇi vidārya śubhramuro viśālaṃ ca tathaiva bhittvā
|
viveśa gāṃ toyamivāprasaktā yaśo viśālaṃ nṛpaterdahantī||50||

51nāsākṣikarṇāsyaviniḥsṛtena prasyandatā ca vraṇasambhavena
|
saṃsiktagātro rudhireṇa so'bhūtkrauñco yathā skandahato mahādriḥ||51||

52prasārya bāhū ca rathādgato gāṃ sañchinnavarmā kurunandanena
|
mahendravāhapratimo mahātmā vajrāhataṃ śṛṅgamivācalasya||52||

53bāhū prasāryābhimukho dharmarājasya madrarāṭ
|
tato nipatito bhūmāvindradhvaja ivocchritaḥ||53||

54sa tathā bhinnasarvāṅgo rudhireṇa samukṣitaḥ
|
pratyudgata iva premṇā bhūmyā sa narapuṅgavaḥ||54||

55priyayā kāntayā kāntaḥ patamāna ivorasi
|
ciraṃ bhuktvā vasumatīṃ priyāṃ kāntāmiva prabhuḥ||55||

56sarvairaṅgaiḥ samāśliṣya prasupta iva cābhavat
|
dharmye dharmātmanā yuddhe nihato dharmasūnunā||56||

57samyagghuta iva sviṣṭaḥ praśānto'gnirivādhvare
|
śaktyā vibhinnahṛdayaṃ vipraviddhāyudhadhvajam||57||

58saṃśāntamapi madreśaṃ lakṣmīrnaiva vimuñcati
|
tato yudhiṣṭhiraścāpamādāyendradhanuṣprabham||58||

59vyadhamaddviṣataḥ saṅkhye khagarāḍiva pannagān
|
dehānsuniśitairbhallairipūṇāṃ nāśayan kṣaṇāt||59||

60tataḥ pārthasya bāṇaughairāvṛtāḥ sainikāstava
|
nimīlitākṣāḥ kṣiṇvanto bhṛśamanyonyamarditāḥ||60||

61kṣaranto rudhiraṃ dehairvipannāyudhajīvitāḥ
|
tataḥ śalye nipatite madrarājānujo yuvā||61||

62bhrātustulyo gaṇaiḥ sarvai rathī pāṇḍavamabhyayāt
|
vivyādha ca naraśreṣṭho nārācairbahubhistvaran||62||

63hatasyāpacitiṃ bhrātuścikīrṣuryuddhadurmadaḥ
|
taṃ vivyādhāśugaiḥ ṣaḍbhirdharmarājastvaranniva||63||

64kārmukaṃ cāsya ciccheda kṣurābhyāṃ dhvajameva ca
|
tato'sya dīpyamānena sudṛḍhena śitena ca||64||

65pramukhe vartamānasya bhallenāpāharacchiraḥ
|
sa kuṇḍalaṃ taddadṛśe patamānaṃ śiro rathāt||65||

66puṇyakṣayamanuprāpya patan svargādiva cyutaḥ
|
tasyāpakṛttaśīrṣaṃ tu śarīraṃ patitaṃ rathāt||66||

67rudhireṇāvasiktāṅgaṃ dṛṣṭvā sainyamabhajyata
|
vicitrakavace tasminhate madranṛpānuje||67||

68hāhākāraṃ prakurvāṇāḥ kuravo'bhipradudruvaḥ
|
śalyānujaṃ hataṃ dṛṣṭvā tāvakāstyaktajīvitāḥ||68||

69vitresuḥ pāṇḍavabhayādrajodhvastāstadā bhṛśam
|
tāṃstathā bhajyamānāṃstu kauravān bharatarṣabha||69||

70śinernaptā kiranbāṇairabhyavartata sātyakiḥ
|
tamāyāntaṃ maheṣvāsaṃ duṣprasahyaṃ durāsadam||70||

71hārdikyastvarito rājanpratyagṛhṇādabhītavat
|
tau sametau mahātmānau vārṣṇeyau varavājinau||71||

72hārdikyaḥ sātyakiścaiva siṃhāviva balotkaṭau
|
iṣubhirvimalābhāsaiśchādayantau parasparam||72||

73arcibhiriva sūryasya divākarasamaprabhau
|
cāpamārgabaloddhūtānmārgaṇān vṛṣṇisiṃhayoḥ||73||

74ākāśagānapaśyāma pataṅgāniva śīghragān
|
sātyakiṃ daśabhirviddhvā hayāṃścāsya tribhiḥ śaraiḥ||74||

75cāpamekena ciccheda hārdikyo nataparvaṇā
|
tannikṛttaṃ dhanuḥ śreṣṭhamapāsya śinipuṅgavaḥ||75||

76anyadādatta vegena vegavattaramāyudham
|
tadādāya dhanuḥ śreṣṭhaṃ variṣṭhaḥ sarvadhanvinām||76||

77hārdikyaṃ daśabhirbāṇaiḥ pratyavidhyatstanāntare
|
tato rathaṃ yugeṣāṃ ca cchittvā bhallaiḥ susaṃyataiḥ||77||

78aśvāṃstasyāvadhīttūrṇamubhau ca pārṣṇisārathī
|
tatastaṃ virathaṃ dṛṣṭvā kṛpaḥ śāradvataḥ prabho||78||

79apovāha tataḥ kṣipraṃ rathamāropya vīryavān
|
madrarāje hate rājanvirathe kṛtavarmaṇi||79||

80duryodhanabalaṃ sarvaṃ punarāsītparāṅmukham
|
tatpare nānvabudhyanta sainyena rajasā vṛte||80||

81balaṃ tu hatabhūyiṣṭhaṃ tattadāsītparāṅmukham
|
tato muhūrtātte'paśyan rajo bhīmaṃ samutthitam||81||

82vividhaiḥ śoṇitasrāvaiḥ praśāntaṃ puruṣarṣabha
|
tato duryodhano dṛṣṭvā bhagnaṃ svabalamantikāt||82||

83javenāpatataḥ pārthānekaḥ sarvānavārayat
|
pāṇḍavānsarathāndṛṣṭvā dhṛṣṭadyumnaṃ ca pārṣatam||83||

84ānartaṃ ca durādharṣaṃ śitairbāṇairavārayat
|
taṃ pare nābhyavartanta mā mṛtyumivāgatam||84||

85athānyaṃ rathamāsthāya hārdikyo'pi nyavartata
|
tato yudhiṣṭhiro rājā tvaramāṇo mahārathaḥ||85||

86caturbhirnijaghānāśvānpatribhiḥ kṛtavarmaṇaḥ
|
vivyādha gautamaṃ cāpi ṣaḍbhirbhallaiḥ sutejanaiḥ||86||

87aśvatthāmā tato rājñā hatāśvaṃ virathīkṛtam
|
tamapovāha hārdikyaṃ svarathena yudhiṣṭhirāt||87||

88tataḥ śāradvataḥ ṣaḍbhiḥ pratyaviddhyadyudhiṣṭhiram
|
vivyādha cāśvānniśitaistasyāṣṭābhiḥ śilīmukhaiḥ||88||

89evametanmahārāja yuddhaśeṣamavartata
|
tava durmantrite rājansaha putrasya bhārata||89||

90tasminmaheṣvāsadhare viśaste saṅgrāmamadhye kurupuṅgavena
|
pārthāḥ sametāḥ paramaprahṛṣṭāḥ śaṅkhānpradadhmurhatamīkṣya śalyam||90||

91yudhiṣṭhiraṃ ca praśaśaṃsurājau purā kṛte vṛtravadhe yathendram
|
cakruśca nānāvidhavādyaśabdānninādayanto vasudhāṃ sametāḥ||91||
