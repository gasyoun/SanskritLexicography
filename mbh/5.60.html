Mahābhārata 5.60 (Udyogaparvan)

1vaiśampāyana uvāca|
sañjayasya vacaḥ śrutvā prajñācakṣurjaneśvaraḥ
|
tataḥ saṅkhyātumārebhe tadvaco guṇadoṣataḥ ||1||

2prasaṅkhyāya ca saukṣmyeṇa guṇadoṣān vicakṣaṇaḥ
|
yathāvanmatitattvena jayakāmaḥ sutān prati ||2||

3balābalaṃ viniścitya yāthātathyena buddhimān
|
śaktiṃ saṅkhyātumārebhe tadā vai manujādhipaḥ ||3||

4devamānuṣayoḥ śaktyā tejasā caiva pāṇḍavān
|
kurūn śaktyā'lpatarayā duryodhanamathābravīt ||4||

5duryodhaneyaṃ cintā me śaśvanna vyupaśāmyati
|
satyaṃ hyetadahaṃ manye pratyakṣaṃ nānumānataḥ ||5||

6ātmajeṣu paraṃ snehaṃ sarvabhūtāni kurvate
|
priyāṇi caiṣāṃ kurvanti yathāśakti hitāni ca ||6||

7evamevopakartṝṇāṃ prāyaśo lakṣayāmahe
|
icchanti bahulaṃ santaḥ pratikartuṃ mahat priyam ||7||

8agniḥ sācivyakartā syāt khāṇḍave tatkṛtaṃ smaran
|
arjunasyāpi bhīme'smin kurupāṇḍusamāgame ||8||

9jātigṛddhyābhipannāśca pāṇḍavānāmanekaśaḥ
|
dharmādayaḥ sameṣyanti samāhūtā divaukasaḥ ||9||

10bhīṣma-droṇa-kṛpādīnāṃ bhayādaśanisannibham
|
rirakṣipantaḥ saṃrambhaṃ gamiṣyantīti me matiḥ ||10||

11te devaiḥ sahitāḥ pārthā na śakyāḥ prativīkṣitum
|
mānuṣeṇa naravyāghrā vīryavanto'strapāragāḥ ||11||

12durāsadaṃ yasya divyaṃ gāṇḍīvaṃ dhanuruttamam
|
dāruṇau cākṣayau divyau śarapūrṇau maheṣudhī ||12||

13vānaraśca dhvaje divyo niḥsaṅgo dhūmavadgatiḥ
|
rathaśca caturantāyāṃ yasya nāsti samaḥ kṣitau ||13||

14mahāmeghanibhaścāpi nirghoṣaḥ śrūyate janaiḥ
|
mahāśanisamaḥ śabdaḥ śātravāṇāṃ bhayaṅkaraḥ ||14||

15yaṃ cātimānuṣaṃ vīrye kṛtsno loko vyavasyati
|
devānāmapi jetāraṃ yaṃ viduḥ pārthivā raṇe ||15||

16śatāni pañca caiveṣūn yo gṛhṇannaiva dṛśyate
|
nimeṣāntaramātreṇa muñcan dūraṃ ca pātayan ||16||

17yamāha bhīṣmo droṇaśca kṛpo drauṇistathaiva ca
|
madrarājastathā śalyo madhyasthā ye ca mānavāḥ ||17||

18yuddhāyāvasthitaṃ pārthaṃ pārthivairatimānuṣaiḥ
|
aśakyaṃ naraśārdūlaṃ parājetumarindamam ||18||

19kṣipatyekena vegena pañca bāṇaśatāni yaḥ
|
sadṛśaṃ bāhuvīryeṇa kārtavīryasya pāṇḍavam ||19||

20tamarjunaṃ maheṣvāsaṃ mahendropendravikramam
|
nighnantamiva paśyāmi vimarde'smin mahāhave ||20||

21ityevaṃ cintayan kṛtsnamahorātrāṇi bhārata
|
anidro niḥsukhaścāsmi kurūṇāṃ śamacintayā ||21||

22kṣayodayo'yaṃ sumahān kurūṇāṃ pratyupasthitaḥ
|
asya cetkalahasyāntaḥ śamādanyo na vidyate ||22||

23śamo me rocate nityaṃ pārthaistāta na vigrahaḥ
|
kurubhyo hi sadā manye pāṇḍavān śaktimattarān ||23||
