Mahābhārata 13.60 (Anuśāsanaparvan)

1yudhiṣṭhira uvāca|
yau ca syātāṃ caraṇenopapannau yau vidyayā sadṛśau janmanā ca
|
tābhyāṃ dānaṃ katamasmai viśiṣṭamayācamānāya ca yācate ca ||1||

2bhīṣma uvāca|
śreyo vai yācataḥ pārtha dānamāhurayācate
|
arhattamo vai dhṛtimānkṛpaṇādadhṛtātmanaḥ ||2||

3kṣatriyo rakṣaṇadhṛtirbrāhmaṇo'narthanādhṛtiḥ
|
brāhmaṇo dhṛtimānvidvāndevānprīṇāti tuṣṭimān ||3||

4yācyamāhuranīśasya abhihāraṃ ca bhārata
|
udvejayanti yācanti sadā bhūtāni dasyuvat ||4||

5mriyate yācamāno vai na jātu mriyate dadat
|
dadatsañjīvayatyenamātmānaṃ ca yudhiṣṭhira ||5||

6ānṛśaṃsyaṃ paro dharmo yācate yatpradīyate
|
ayācataḥ sīdamānānsarvopāyairnimantrayet ||6||

7yadi vai tādṛśā rāṣṭrānvaseyuste dvijottamāḥ
|
bhasmacchannānivāgnīṃstānbudhyethāstvaṃ prayatnataḥ ||7||

8tapasā dīpyamānāste daheyuḥ pṛthivīmapi
|
apūjyamānāḥ kauravya pūjārhāstu tathāvidhāḥ ||8||

9pūjyā hi jñānavijñānatapoyogasamanvitāḥ
|
tebhyaḥ pūjāṃ prayuñjīthā brāhmaṇebhyaḥ parantapa ||9||

10dadadbahuvidhāndāyānupāgacchannayācatām
|
yadagnihotre suhute sāyaṃprātarbhavetphalam ||10||

11vidyāvedavratavati taddānaphalamucyate
|
vidyāvedavratasnātānavyapāśrayajīvinaḥ ||11||

12gūḍhāsvadhyāyatapaso brāhmaṇānsaṃśitavratān
|
kṛtairāvasathairhṛdyaiḥ sapreṣyaiḥ saparicchadaiḥ ||12||

13nimantrayethāḥ kauravya kāmaiścānyairdvijottamān
|
api te pratigṛhṇīyuḥ śraddhopetaṃ yudhiṣṭhira ||13||

14kāryamityeva manvānā dharmajñāḥ sūkṣmadarśinaḥ
|
api te brāhmaṇā bhuktvā gatāḥ soddharaṇān gṛhān ||14||

15yeṣāṃ dārāḥ pratīkṣante parjanyamiva karṣakāḥ
|
annāni prātaḥsavane niyatā brahmacāriṇaḥ ||15||

16brāhmaṇāstāta bhuñjānāstretāgniṃ prīṇayantyuta
|
mādhyandinaṃ te savanaṃ dadatastāta vartatām ||16||

17gohiraṇyāni vāsāṃsi tenendraḥ prīyatāṃ tava
|
tṛtīyaṃ savanaṃ te vai vaiśvadevaṃ yudhiṣṭhira ||17||

18yaddevebhyaḥ pitṛbhyaśca viprebhyaśca prayacchasi
|
ahiṃsā sarvabhūtebhyaḥ saṃvibhāgaśca bhāgaśaḥ ||18||

19damastyāgo dhṛtiḥ satyaṃ bhavatyavabhṛthāya te
|
eṣa te vitato yajñaḥ śraddhāpūtaḥ sadakṣiṇaḥ ||19||

20viśiṣṭaḥ sarvayajñānāṃ nityaṃ tāta pravartatām ||20||
