Mahābhārata 9.15 (Śalyaparvan)

1sañjaya uvāca|
duryodhano mahārāja dhṛṣṭadyumnaśca pārṣataḥ
|
cakratuḥ sumahadyuddhaṃ śaraśaktisamākulam||1||

2tayorāsanmahārāja śaradhārāḥ sahasraśaḥ
|
ambudānāṃ yathā kāle jaladhārāḥ samantataḥ||2||

3rājā ca pārṣataṃ viddhvā śaraiḥ pañcabhirāśugaiḥ
|
droṇahantāramugreṣu punarvivyādha saptabhiḥ||3||

4dhṛṣṭadyumnastu samare balavān dṛḍhavikramaḥ
|
saptatyā viśikhānāṃ vai duryodhanamapīḍayat||4||

5pīḍitaṃ vīkṣya rājānaṃ sodaryā bharatarṣabha
|
mahatyā senayā sārdhaṃ parivavruḥ sma pārṣatam||5||

6sa taiḥ parivṛtaḥ śūraḥ sarvato'tirathairbhṛśam
|
vyacaratsamare rājan darśayannastralāghavam||6||

7śikhaṇḍī kṛtavarmāṇaṃ gautamaṃ ca mahāratham
|
prabhadrakaiḥ samāyukto yodhayāmāsa dhanvinau||7||

8tatrāpi sumahadyuddhaṃ ghorarūpaṃ viśāmpate
|
prāṇānsantyajatāṃ yuddhe prāṇadyūtābhidevane||8||

9śalyaḥ sāyakavarṣāṇi vimuñcansarvatodiśam
|
pāṇḍavānpīḍayāmāsa sasātyakivṛkodarān||9||

10tathā tau tu yamau yuddhe yamatulyaparākramau
|
yodhayāmāsa rājendra vīryeṇāstrabalena ca||10||

11śalyasāyakanunnānāṃ pāṇḍavānāṃ mahāmṛdhe
|
trātāraṃ nābhyagacchanta kecittatra mahārathaḥ||11||

12tatastu nakulaḥ śūro dharmarāje prapīḍate
|
abhidudrāva vegena mātulaṃ mātṛnandanaḥ||12||

13sañchādya samare vīraṃ nakulaḥ paravīrahā
|
vivyādha cainaṃ daśabhiḥ smayamānaḥ stanāntare||13||

14sarvapārasavairbāṇaiḥ karmāraparimārjitaiḥ
|
svarṇapuṅkhaiḥ śilādhautairdhanuryantrapracoditaiḥ||14||

15śalyastu pīḍitastena svasrīyeṇa mahātmanā
|
nakulaṃ pīḍayāmāsa patribhirnataparvabhiḥ||15||

16tato yudhiṣṭhiro rājā bhīmaseno'tha sātyakiḥ
|
sahadevaśca mādreyo madrarājamupādravan||16||

17tānāpatata evāśu pūrayāṇān rathasvanaiḥ
|
diśaśca vidiśaścaiva kampayānāṃśca medinīm||17||

18pratijagrāha samare senāpatiramitrajit
|
yudhiṣṭhiraṃ tribhirviddhvā bhīmasenaṃ ca pañcabhiḥ||18||

19sātyakiṃ ca śatenājau sahadevaṃ tribhiḥ śaraiḥ
|
tatastu śasaraṃ cāpaṃ nukalasya mahātmanaḥ||19||

20madreśvaraḥ kṣurapreṇa tadā māriṣa cicchide
|
tadaśīryata vicchinnaṃ dhanuḥ śalyasya sāyakaiḥ||20||

21athānyaddhanurādāya mādrīputro mahārathaḥ
|
madrarājarathaṃ tūrṇaṃ pūrayāmāsa patribhiḥ||21||

22yudhiṣṭhirastu madreśaṃ sahadevaśca māriṣa
|
daśabhirdaśabhirbāṇairurasyenamavidhyatām||22||

23bhīmesenastu taṃ ṣaṣṭyā sātyakirdaśabhiḥ śaraiḥ
|
madrarājamabhidrutya jaghnatuḥ kaṅkapatribhiḥ||23||

24madrarājastataḥ kruddhaḥ sātyakiṃ navabhiḥ śaraiḥ
|
vivyādha bhūyaḥ saptatyā śarāṇāṃ nataparvaṇām||24||

25athāsya saśaraṃ cāpaṃ muṣṭau ciccheda māriṣa
|
hayāṃśca caturaḥ saṅkhye preṣayāmāsa mṛtyave||25||

26virathaṃ sātyakiṃ kṛtvā madrarājo mahārathaḥ
|
viśikhānāṃ śatenainamājaghāna samantataḥ||26||

27mādrīputrau ca saṃrabdhau bhīmasenaṃ ca pāṇḍava
|
yudhiṣṭhiraṃ ca kauravya vivyādha daśabhiḥ śaraiḥ||27||

28tatrādbhutamapaśyāma madrarājasya pauruṣam
|
yadenaṃ sahitāḥ pārthā nābhyavartanta saṃyuge||28||

29athānyaṃ rathamāsthāya sātyakiḥ satyavikramaḥ
|
pīḍitān pāṇḍavān dṛṣṭvā madrarājavaśaṅgatān||29||

30abhidudrāva vegena madrāṇāmadhipaṃ balāt
|
āpatantaṃ rathaṃ tasya śalyaḥ samitiśobhanaḥ||30||

31pratyudyayau rathenaiva matto mattamiva dvipam
|
sa sannipātastumulo babhūvādbhutadarśanaḥ||31||

32sātyakeścaiva śūrasya madrāṇāmadhipasya ca
|
yādṛśo vai purā vṛttaḥ śambarāmararājayoḥ||32||

33sātyakiḥ prekṣya samare madrarājamavasthitam
|
vivyādha daśabhirbāṇaistiṣṭha tiṣṭheti cābravīt||33||

34madrarājastu subhṛśaṃ viddhastena mahātmanā
|
sātyakiṃ prativivyādha citrapuṅkhaiḥ śitaiḥ śaraiḥ||34||

35tataḥ pārthā maheṣvāsāḥ sātvatābhisṛtaṃ nṛpam
|
abhyavartan rathaistūrṇaṃ mātulaṃ vadhakāṃkṣayā||35||

36tata āsītparāmardastumulaḥ śoṇitodakaḥ
|
śūrāṇāṃ yudhyamānānāṃ siṃhānāmiva nardatām||36||

37teṣāmāsīnmahārāja vyatikṣepaḥ parasparam
|
siṃhānāmāmiṣepsūnāṃ kūjatāmiva saṃyuge||37||

38teṣāṃ bāṇasahasraughairākīrṇā vasudhābhavat
|
antarikṣaṃ ca sahasā bāṇabhūtamabhūttadā||38||

39śarāndhakāraṃ sahasā kṛtaṃ tatra samantataḥ
|
abhracchāyeva sañjajñe śarairmuktairmahātmabhiḥ||39||

40tatra rājan śarairmuktairnirmuktairiva pannagaiḥ
|
svarṇapuṅkhaiḥ prakāśadbhirvyarocanta diśastadā||40||

41tatrādbhutaṃ paraṃ cakre śalyaḥ śatrunibarhaṇaḥ
|
yadekaḥ samare śūro yodhayāmāsa vai bahūn||41||

42madrarājabhujotsaṣṭaiḥ kaṅkabarhiṇavājitaiḥ
|
sampatadbhiḥ śarairghorairavākīryata medinī||42||

43tatra śalyarathaṃ rājanvicarantaṃ mahāhave
|
apaśyāma yathāpūrvaṃ śakrasyāsurasaṃkṣaye||43||
