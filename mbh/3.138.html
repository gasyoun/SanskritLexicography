Mahābhārata 3.138 (Āraṇyakaparvan)

1lomaśa uvāca|
etasminneva kāle tu bṛhaddyumno mahīpatiḥ
|
satraṃ tene mahābhāgo raibhyayājyaḥ pratāpavān||1||

2tena raibhyasya vai putrāvarvāvasuparāvasū
|
vṛtau sahāyau satrārthaṃ bṛhaddyumnena dhīmatā||2||

3tatra tau samanujñātau pitrā kaunteya jagmatuḥ
|
āśrame tvabhavadraibhyo bhāryā caiva parāvasoḥ||3||

4athāvalokako'gacchadgṛhānekaḥ parāvasuḥ
|
kṛpṇājinena saṃvītaṃ dadarśa pitaraṃ vane||4||

5jaghanyarātre nidrāndhaḥ sāvaśeṣe tamasyapi
|
carantaṃ gahane'raṇye mene sa pitaraṃ mṛgam||5||

6mṛgaṃ tu manyamānena pitā vai tena hiṃsitaḥ
|
akāmayānena tadā śarīratrāṇamicchatā||6||

7tasya sa pretakāryāṇi kṛtvā sarvāṇi bhārata
|
punarāgamya tatsatramabravīdbhrātaraṃ vacaḥ||7||

8idaṃ karma na śaktastvaṃ voḍhumekaḥ kathaṃcana
|
mayā ca hiṃsitastāto manyamānena taṃ mṛgam||8||

9so'smarthe vrataṃ tāta cara tvaṃ brahmahiṃsanam
|
samartho'pyahamekākī karma kartumidaṃ mune||9||

10arvāvasuruvāca|
karotu vai bhavānsatraṃ bṛhaddyumnasya dhīmataḥ
|
brahmavadhyāṃ cariṣye'haṃ tvadarthaṃ niyatendriyaḥ||10||

11lomaśa uvāca|
sa tasya brahmavadhyāyāḥ pāraṃ gatvā yudhiṣṭhira
|
arvāvasustadāsatramājagāma punarmuniḥ||11||

12tataḥ parāvasurdṛṣṭvā bhrātaraṃ samupasthitam
|
vṛhaddyumnamuvācedaṃ vacanaṃ harṣagadgadam||12||

13eṣa te brahmahā yajñaṃ mā draṣṭuṃ praviśediti
|
brahmahā prekṣitenāpi pīḍayettvāmasaṃśayam||13||

14lomaśa uvāca|
tacchrutvaiva tadā rājā preṣyānāha sa viṭpate
|
preṣyairutsāryamāṇastu rājannarvāvasustadā||14||

15na mayā brahmahatyeyaṃ kṛtetyāha punaḥ punaḥ
|
ucyamāno'sakṛtpreṣyairbrahmahanniti bhārata||15||

16naiva sma pratijānāti brahmavadhyāṃ svayaṃkṛtām
|
mama bhrātrā kṛtamidaṃ mayā sa parimokṣitaḥ||16||

17sa tathā pravadankrodhāttaiśca preṣyaiḥ prabhāṣitaḥ
|
tūṣṇīṃ jagāma brahmarṣirvanameva mahātapāḥ||17||

18ugraṃ tapaḥ samāsthāya divākaramathāśritaḥ
|
rahasyavedaṃ kṛtavānsūryasya dvijasattamaḥ||18||

19mūrtimāṃstaṃ dadarśātha svayamagrabhugavyayaḥ
|
lomaśa uvāca|
prītāstasyābhavandevāḥ karmaṇārvāvasornṛpa||19||

20taṃ te pravarayāmāsurnirāsuśca parāvasum
|
tato devā varaṃ tasmai daduragnipurogamāḥ||20||

21sa cāpi varayāmāsa piturutthānamātmanaḥ
|
anāgastvaṃ tato bhrātuḥ pituścāsmaraṇaṃ vadhe||21||

22bharadvājasya cotthānaṃ yavakrītasya cobhayoḥ
|
pratiṣṭhāṃ cāpi vedasya saurasya dvijasattamaḥ
|
evamastviti taṃ devāḥ procuścāpi varāndaduḥ||22||

23tataḥ prādurbabhūvuste sarva eva yudhiṣṭhira
|
athābravīdyavakrīto devānagnipurogamān||23||

24samadhītaṃ mayā brahma vratāni caritāni ca
|
kathaṃ ca raibhyaḥ śakto māmadhīyānaṃ tapasvinam||24||

25tathāyuktena vidhinā nihantumamarottamāḥ
|
devā ūcuḥ| maivaṃ kṛthā yavakrīta tathā vadasi vai mune
|
ṛte gurumadhītā hi sukhaṃ vedāstvayā purā||25||

26anena tu gurūnduḥkhāttoṣayitvātmakarmaṇā
|
kālena mahatā kleśādbrahmādhigatamuttamam||26||

27lomaśa uvāca|
yavakrītamathoktvaivaṃ devāḥ sendrapurogamāḥ
|
saṃjīvayitvā tānsarvānpunarjagmustriviṣṭapam||27||

28āśramastasya puṇyo'yaṃ sadā puṣpaphaladrumaḥ
|
atroṣya rājaśārdūla sarvaṃ pāpaṃ pramokṣyasi||28||
