Mahābhārata 12.118 (Śāntiparvan)

1bhīṣma uvāca|
sa śvā prakṛtimāpannaḥ paraṃ dainyamupāgataḥ
|
ṛṣiṇā huṃkṛtaḥ pāpastapovanabahiṣkṛtaḥ||1||

2evaṃ rājñā matimatā viditvā satyaśaucatām
|
ārjavaṃ, prakṛtiṃ, satyaṃ, śrutaṃ, vṛttaṃ, kulaṃ, damam||2||

3anukrośaṃ, balaṃ, vīryaṃ prabhāvaṃ praśrayaṃ, kṣamām
|
bhṛtyā ye yatra yogyāḥ syustatra sthāpyāḥ surakṣitāḥ||3||

4nāparīkṣya mahīpālaḥ sacivaṃ kartumarhati
|
akulīnanarākīrṇo na rājā sukhamedhate||4||

5kulajaḥ prākṛto rājñā svakulīnatayā sadā
|
na pāpe kurute buddhiṃ bhidyamāno'pyanāgasi||5||

6akulīnastu puruṣaḥ prākṛtaḥ sādhusaṃśrayāt
|
durlabhaiśvaryatāṃ prāpto ninditaḥ śatrutāṃ vrajet||6||

7kulīnaṃ śikṣitaṃ prājñaṃ jñānavijñānapāragam
|
sarvaśāstrārthatattvajñaṃ sahiṣṇuṃ deśajaṃ tathā||7||

8kṛtajñaṃ balavantaṃ ca kṣāntaṃ dāntaṃ jitendriyam
|
alubdhaṃ labdhasaṃtuṣṭaṃ svāmimitrabubhūṣakam||8||

9sacivaṃ deśakālajñaṃ sattvasaṃgrahaṇe ratam
|
satataṃ yuktamanasaṃ hitaiṣiṇamatandritam||9||

10yuktacāraṃ svaviṣaye sandhivigrahakovidam
|
rājñastrivargavettāraṃ paurajānapadapriyam||10||

11khātakavyūhatattvajñaṃ balaharṣaṇakovidam
|
iṅgitākāratattvajñaṃ yātrājñānaviśāradam||11||

12hastiśikṣāsu tattvajñamahaṃkāravivarjitam
|
pragalbhaṃ dakṣiṇaṃ dāntaṃ balinaṃ yuktakāriṇam||12||

13caukṣaṃ caukṣajanākīrṇaṃ sumukhaṃ sukhadarśanam
|
nāyakaṃ nītikuśalaṃ guṇaceṣṭāsamanvitam||13||

14astabdhaṃ prasṛtaṃ ślakṣṇaṃ mṛduvādinameva ca
|
dhīraṃ śūraṃ mahardhiṃ ca deśakālopapādakam||14||

15sacivaṃ yaḥ prakurute na cainamavamanyate
|
tasya vistīryate rājyaṃ jyotsnā grahapateriva||15||

16etaireva guṇairyukto rājā śāstraviśāradaḥ
|
eṣṭavyo dharmaparamaḥ prajāpālanatatparaḥ||16||

17dhīro mārṣī śucistīkṣṇaḥ kāle puruṣakāravit
|
śuśrūṣuḥ śrutavān śrotā ūhāpohaviśāradaḥ||17||

18medhāvī dhāraṇāyukto yathānyāyopapādakaḥ
|
dāntaḥ sadā priyābhāṣī kṣamāvāṃśca viparyaye||18||

19dānācchede svayaṃkārī śraddhāluḥ sukhadarśanaḥ
|
ārtahastaprado nityamamātyo hi hite rataḥ||19||

20nāhaṃvādī na nirdvandvo na yatkiṃcanakārakaḥ
|
kṛte karmaṇyamātyānāṃ kartā bhaktajanapriyaḥ||20||

21saṃgṛhītajano stabdhaḥ prasannavadanaḥ sadā
|
sadā bhṛtyajanāpekṣī na krodhī sumahāmanāḥ||21||

22yuktadaṇḍo na nirdaṇḍo dharmakāryānuśāsanaḥ
|
cāranetraḥ prajāvekṣī dharmārthakuśalaḥ sadā||22||

23rājā guṇaśatākīrṇa eṣṭavyastādṛśo bhavet
|
yodhāścaiva manuṣyendra sarve guṇagaṇairvṛtāḥ||23||

24anveṣṭavyāḥ supuruṣāḥ sahāyā rājyadhāraṇe
|
na vimānayitavyāste rājñā vṛddhimabhīpsatā||24||

25yodhāḥ samaraśauṭīrāḥ kṛtajñāḥ śastrakovidāḥ
|
dharmaśāstrasamāyuktāḥ padātijanasaṃvṛtāḥ||25||

26abhayā gajapṛṣṭhasthā rathacaryāviśāradāḥ
|
iṣvastrakuśalā yasya tasyeyaṃ nṛpatermahī||26||

27sarvasaṃgrahaṇe yukto nṛpo bhavati yaḥ sadā
|
utthānaśīlo mitrāḍhyaḥ sa rājā rājasattamaḥ||27||

28śakyā cāśvasahasreṇa vīrāroheṇa bhārata
|
saṃgṛhītamanuṣyeṇa kṛtsnā jetuṃ vasundharā||28||
