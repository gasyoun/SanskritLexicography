Mahābhārata 3.211 (Āraṇyakaparvan)

1mārkaṇḍeya uvāca|
evamuktaḥ sa viprastu dharmavyādhena bhārata
|
kathāmakathayadbhūyo manasaḥ prītivardhanīm||1||

2brāhmaṇa uvāca|
mahābhūtāni yānyāhuḥ pañca dharmabhṛtāṃ vara
|
ekaikasya guṇān samyak pañcānāmapi me vada||2||

3vyādha uvāca|
bhūmirāpastathā jyotirvāyurākāśameva ca
|
guṇottarāṇi sarvāṇi teṣāṃ vakṣyāmi te guṇān||3||

4bhūmiḥ pañcaguṇā brahmannudakaṃ ca caturguṇam
|
guṇāstrayastejasi ca trayaścākāśavātayoḥ||4||

5śabdaḥ sparśaśca rūpaṃ ca raso gandhaśca pañcamaḥ
|
ete guṇāḥ pañca bhūmeḥ sarvebhyo guṇavattarāḥ||5||

6śabdaḥ sparśaśca rūpaṃ ca rasaścāpi dvijottama
|
apāmete guṇā brahman kīrtitāstava suvrata||6||

7śabdaḥ sparśaśca rūpaṃ ca tejaso'tha guṇāstrayaḥ
|
śabdaḥ sparśaśca vāyau tu śabdaścākāśa eva tu||7||

8ete pañcadaśa brahman guṇā bhūteṣu pañcasu
|
vartante sarvabhūteṣu yeṣu lokāḥ pratiṣṭhitāḥ||8||

9anyonyaṃ nātivartante samyakca bhavati dvija
|
yadā tu viṣayībhāvamācaranti carācarāḥ||9||

10tadā dehī dehamanyaṃ vyatirohati kālataḥ
|
ānupūrvyā vinaśyanti jāyante cānupūrvaśaḥ||10||

11tatra tatra hi dṛśyante dhātavaḥ pāñcabhautikāḥ
|
yairāvṛtamidaṃ sarvaṃ jagat sthāvarajaṅgamam||11||

12indriyaiḥ sṛjyate yadyat tat tadvyaktamiti smṛtam
|
tadavyaktamiti jñeyaṃ liṅgagrāhyamatīndriyam||12||

13yathāsvaṃ grāhakānyeṣāṃ śabdādīnāmimāni tu
|
indriyāṇi yadā dehī dhārayanniva tapyate||13||

14loke vitatamātmānaṃ lokaṃ cātmani paśyati
|
parāparajñaḥ saktaḥ san sa tu bhūtāni paśyati||14||

15paśyataḥ sarvabhūtāni sarvāvasthāsu sarvadā
|
brahmabhūtasya saṃyogo nāśubhenopapadyate||15||

16jñānamūlātmakaṃ kleśamativṛttasya pauruṣam
|
lokavṛttiprakāśena jñānamārgeṇa gamyate||16||

17anādinidhanaṃ jantumātmayoniṃ sadāvyayam
|
anaupamyamamūrtaṃ ca bhagavānāha buddhimān||17||

18tapomūlamidaṃ sarvaṃ yanmāṃ viprānupṛcchasi
|
indriyāṇyeva saṃyamya tapo bhavati nānyathā||18||

19indriyāṇyeva tat sarvaṃ yat svarganarakāvubhau
|
nigṛhītavisṛṣṭāni svargāya narakāya ca||19||

20eṣa yogavidhiḥ kṛtsno yāvadindriyadhāraṇam
|
etanmūlaṃ hi tapasaḥ kṛtsnasya narakasya ca||20||

21indriyāṇāṃ prasaṅgena doṣamārcchantyasaṃśayam
|
sanniyamya tu tānyeva tataḥ siddhiṃ samāpnuyāt||21||

22ṣaṇṇāmātmani nityānāmaiśvaryaṃ yo'dhigacchati
|
na sa pāpaiḥ kuto'narthairyujyate vijitendriyaḥ||22||

23rathaḥ śarīraṃ puruṣasya dṛṣṭamātmā niyantendriyāṇyāhuraśvān
|
tairapramattaḥ kuśalī sadaśvairdāntaiḥ sukhaṃ yāti rathīva dhīraḥ||23||

24ṣaṇṇāmātmaniyuktānāmindriyāṇāṃ pramāthitam
|
yo dhīro dhārayedraśmīn sa syāt paramasārathiḥ||24||

25indriyāṇāṃ prasṛṣṭānāṃ hayānāmiva vartmasu
|
dhṛtiṃ kurvīta sārathye dhṛtyā tāni jayet dhruvam||25||

26indriyāṇāṃ vicaratāṃ yanmano'nuvidhīyate
|
tadasya harate buddhiṃ nāvaṃ vāyurivāmbhasi||26||

27yeṣu vipratipadyante ṣaṭsu mohāt phalāgamam
|
teṣvadhyavasitādhyāyī vindate dhyānajaṃ phalam||27||
