Mahābhārata 13.122 (Anuśāsanaparvan)

1bhīṣma uvāca|
evamuktaḥ sa bhagavān maitreyaṃ pratyabhāṣata
|
diṣṭyaivaṃ tvaṃ vijānāsi diṣṭyā te buddhirīdṛśī||1||

2loko hyāryaguṇāneva bhūyiṣṭhaṃ tu praśaṃsati
|
rūpamānavayomānaśrīmānāścāpyasaṃśayam ||2||

3diṣṭyā nābhibhavanti tvāṃ daivaste'yamanugrahaḥ
|
yatte bhṛśataraṃ dānādvartayiṣyāmi tacchṛṇu ||3||

4yānīhāgamaśāstrāṇi yāśca kāścitpravṛttayaḥ
|
tāni vedaṃ puraskṛtya pravṛttāni yathākramam ||4||

5ahaṃ dānaṃ praśaṃsāmi bhavānapi tapaḥśrute
|
tapaḥ pavitraṃ vedasya tapaḥ svargasya sādhanam ||5||

6tapasā mahadāpnoti vidyayā ceti naḥ śrutam
|
tapasaiva cāpanudedyaccānyadapi duṣkṛtam ||6||

7yadyaddhi kiṃcitsaṃdhāya puruṣastapyate tapaḥ
|
sarvametada‌vāpnoti vidyayā ceti naḥ śrutam ||7||

8duranvayaṃ duṣpradharṣaṃ durāpaṃ duratikramam
|
sarvaṃ vai tapasā'bhyeti tapo hi balavattaram ||8||

9surāpo'saṃmatādāyī bhrūṇahā gurutalpagaḥ
|
tapasā tarate sarvamenasaśca pramucyate ||9||

10sarvavidyastu cakṣuṣmānapi yādṛśatādṛśam
|
tapasvinaṃ tathaivāhustābhyāṃ kāryaṃ sadā namaḥ||10||

11sarve pūjyāḥ śrutadhanāstathaiva ca tapasvinaḥ
|
dānapradāḥ sukhaṃ pretya prāpnuvantīha ca śriyam ||11||

12imaṃ ca brahmalokaṃ ca lokaṃ ca balavattaram
|
annadānaiḥ sukṛtinaḥ pratipadyanti laukikāḥ||12||

13pūjitāḥ pūjayantyete mānitā mānayanti ca
|
sa dātā yatra yatraiti sarvataḥ saṃpraṇūyate ||13||

14akartā caiva kartā ca labhate yasya yādṛśam
|
yadi cordhvaṃ yadyadho vā svān lokānabhiyāsyati||14||

15prāpsyasi tvannapānāni yāni vāṃcchasi kānicit
|
medhāvyasi kule jātaḥ śrutavānanṛśaṃsavān||15||

16kaumāracārī vratavānmaitreya nirato bhava
|
etadgṛhāṇa prathamaṃ praśastaṃ gṛhamedhinām ||16||

17yo bhartā vāsitā tuṣṭo bhartustuṣṭā ca vāsitā
|
yasminnevaṃ kule sarvaṃ kalyāṇaṃ tatra vartate ||17||

18adbhirgātrānmalamiva tamo'gniprabhayā yathā
|
dānena tapasā caiva sarvapāpamapohati ||18||

19svasti prāpnuhi maitreya gṛhān sādhu vrajāmyaham
|
etanmanasi kartavyaṃ śreya evaṃ praviṣyati ||19||

20taṃ praṇamyātha maitreyaḥ kṛtvā cāpi pradakṣiṇam
|
svasti prāpnotu bhagavānityuvāca kṛtāñjaliḥ||20||
