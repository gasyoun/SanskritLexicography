Mahābhārata 4.6 (Virāṭaparvan)

1vaiśampāyana uvāca|virāṭanagaraṃ ramyaṃ gacchamāno yudhiṣṭhiraḥ
|
astuvanmanasā devīṃ durgāṃ tribhuvaneśvarīm ||1||

2yaśodāgarbhasaṃbhūtāṃ nārāyaṇavarapriyām
|
nanda‌gopakule jātāṃ maṅgalyāṃ kulavardhinīm ||2||

3kaṃsavidrāvaṇakarīmasurāṇāṃ kṣayaṃkarīm
|
śilātaṭavinikṣiptāmākāśaṃ prati gāminīm ||3||

4vāsudevasya bhaginīṃ divyamālyavibhūṣitām
|
divyāmbaradharāṃ devīṃ khaḍgakheṭakadhāriṇīm ||4||

5bhārāvataraṇe puṇye ye smaranti sadāśivām
|
tān vai tārayate pāpāt paṅke gāmiva durbalām ||5||

6stotuṃ pracakrame bhūyo vividhaiḥ stotrasaṃbhavaiḥ
|
āmantrya darśanākāṅkṣī rājā devīṃ sahānujaḥ ||6||

7namo'stu varade kṛṣṇe kumāri brahmacāriṇi
|
bālārkasadṛśākāre pūrṇacandranibhānane ||7||

8caturbhuje caturvaktre pīnaśroṇipayodhare
|
mayūrapicchavalaye keyūrāṅgadadhāriṇi
|
bhāsi devi yathā padmā nārāyaṇaparigrahaḥ ||8||

9svarūpaṃ brahmacaryaṃ ca viśadaṃ tatra khecari
|
kṛṣṇacchavisamā kṛṣṇā saṃkarṣaṇasamānanā ||9||

10bibhratī vipulau bāhū śakradhvajasamucchrayau
|
pātrī ca paṅkajī ghaṇṭī strīviśuddhā ca yā bhuvi ||10||

11pāśaṃ dhanurmahācakraṃ vividhānyāyudhāni ca
|
kuṇḍalābhyāṃ supūrṇābhyāṃ karṇābhyāṃ ca vibhūṣitā ||11||

12candravisparddhinā devi mukhena tvaṃ virājase
|
mukuṭena vicitreṇa keśabandhena śobhinā ||12||

13bhujaṅgābhogavāsena śroṇisūtreṇa rājatā
|
vibhrājase cābaddhena bhogeneveha mandaraḥ ||13||

14dhvajena śikhipicchānāmucchritena virājase
|
kaumāraṃ vratamāsthāya tridivaṃ pāvitaṃ tvayā||14||

15tena tvaṃ stūyase devi tridaśaiḥ pūjyase'pi ca
|
trailokyarakṣaṇārthāya mahiṣāsuranāśini
|
prasannā me suraśreṣṭhe dayāṃ kuru śivā bhava||15||

16jayā tvaṃ vijayā caiva saṅgrāme ca jayapradā
|
mamāpi vijayaṃ dehi varadā tvaṃ ca sāmpratam ||16||

17vindhye caiva nagaśreṣṭhe tava sthānaṃ hi śāśvatam
|
kāli kāli mahākāli sīdhumāṃsapaśupriye ||17||

18kṛtānuyātrā bhūtaistvaṃ varadā kāmacāriṇi
|
bhārāvatāre ye ca tvāṃ saṃsmariṣyanti mānavāḥ ||18||

19praṇamanti ca ye tvāṃ hi prabhāte tu narā bhuvi
|
na teṣāṃ durlabhaṃ kiñcit putrato dhanato'pi vā ||19||

20durgāt tārayase durge tat tvaṃ durgā smṛtā janaiḥ
|
kāntāreṣvavasannānāṃ magnānāṃ ca mahārṇave ||20||

21dasyubhirvā niruddhānāṃ tvaṃ gatiḥ paramā nṛṇām
|
jalaprataraṇe caiva kāntāreṣvaṭavīṣu ca ||21||

22ye smaranti mahādevi na ca sīdanti te narāḥ
|
tvaṃ kīrtiḥ śrīrdhṛtiḥ siddhirhrīrvidyā santatirmatiḥ ||22||

23sandhyā rātriḥ prabhā nidrā jyotsnā kāntiḥ kṣamā dayā
|
nṛṇāṃ ca bandhanaṃ mohaṃ putranāśaṃ dhanañjayam ||23||

24vyādhiṃ mṛtyuṃ bhayaṃ caiva pūjitā nāśayiṣyasi
|
so'haṃ rājyāt paribhraṣṭaḥ śaraṇaṃ tvāṃ prapannavān||24||

25praṇataśca yathā mūrdhnā tava devi sureśvari
|
trāhi māṃ padmapatrākṣi satye satyā bhavasva naḥ ||25||

26śaraṇaṃ bhava me durge śaraṇye bhaktavatsale
|
evaṃ stutā hi sā devī darśayāmāsa pāṇḍavam ||26||

27upagamya tu rājānamidaṃ vacanamabravīt
|
devyuvāca
|
śṛṇu rājan mahābāho madīyaṃ vacanaṃ prabho ||27||

28bhaviṣyatyacirādeva saṃgrāme vijayastava
|
mama prasādānnirjitya hatvā kauravavāhinīm ||28||

29rājyaṃ niṣkaṇṭakaṃ kṛtvā bhokṣyase medinīṃ punaḥ
|
bhrātṛbhiḥ sahito rājan prītiṃ prāpsyasi puṣkalām ||29||

30matprasādācca te saukhyamārogyaṃ ca bhaviṣyati
|
ye ca saṃkīrtayiṣyanti loke vigatakalmaṣāḥ ||30||

31teṣāṃ tuṣṭā pradāsyāmi rājyamāyurvapuḥ sutam
|
pravāse nagare cāpi saṅgrāme śatrusaṅkaṭe ||31||

32aṭavyāṃ durgakāntāre sāgare gahane girau
|
ye smariṣyanti māṃ rājan yathā'haṃ bhavatā smṛtā ||32||

33na teṣāṃ durlabhaṃ kiñcidasmi~lloke bhaviṣyati
|
idaṃ stotravaraṃ bhaktyā śṛṇuyādvā paṭheta vā ||33||

34tasya sarvāṇi kāryāṇi siddhiṃ yāsyanti pāṇḍavāḥ
|
matprasādācca vaḥ sarvān virāṭanagare sthitān ||34||

35na prajñāsyanti kuravo narā vā tannivāsinaḥ
|
ityuktvā varadā devī yudhiṣṭhiramarindamam
|
rakṣāṃ kṛtvā ca pāṇḍūnāṃ tatraivāntaradhīyata ||35||
