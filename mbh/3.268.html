Mahābhārata 3.268 (Āraṇyakaparvan)

1vaiśampāyana uvāca|
saroṣarāgopahatena valgunā sarāganetreṇa natonnatabhruvā
|
mukhena visphūrya suvīrarāṣṭrapaṃ tato'bravīt taṃ drupadātmajā punaḥ||1||

2yaśasvinastīkṣṇaviṣān mahārathānabhibruvan mūḍha na lajjase katham
|
mahendrakalpān niratān svakarmasu sthitān samūheṣvapi yakṣarakṣasām||2||

3na kiñcidīḍyaṃ pravadanti pāpaṃ vanecaraṃ vā gṛhamedhinaṃ vā
|
tapasvinaṃ samparipūrṇavidyaṃ bhaṣanti haivaṃ śvanarāḥ suvīra||3||

4ahaṃ tu manye tava nāsti kaścidetādṛśe kṣatriyasaṃniveśe
|
yastvadya pātālamukhe patantaṃ pāṇau gṛhītvā pratisaṃhareta||4||

5nāgaṃ prabhinnaṃ girikūṭakalpamupatyakāṃ haimavatīṃ carantam
|
daṇḍīva yūthādapasedhasi tvaṃ yo jetumāśaṃsāsa dharmarājam||5||

6bālyāt prasuptasya mahābalasya siṃhasya pakṣmāṇi mukhāllanāsi
|
padā samāhatya palāyamānaḥ kruddhaṃ yadā drakṣyasi bhīmasenam||6||

7mahābalaṃ ghorataraṃ pravṛddhaṃ jātaṃ hariṃ parvatakandareṣu
|
prasuptamugraṃ prapadena haṃsi yaḥ kruddhamāyotsyasi jiṣṇumugram||7||

8kṛṣṇoragau tīkṣṇamukhau dvijihvau mattaḥ padā''krāmasi pucchadeśe
|
yaḥ pāṇḍavābhyāṃ puruṣottamābhyāṃ jaghanyajābhyāṃ prayuyutsase tvam||8||

9yathā ca veṇuḥ kadalī nalo vā phalantyabhāvāya na bhūtaye''tmanaḥ
|
tathaiva māṃ taiḥ parirakṣyamāṇāmādāsyase karkaṭakīva garbham||9||

10jayadratha uvāca|
jānāmi kṛṣṇe viditaṃ mamaitadyathāvidhāste naradevaputrāḥ
|
na tvevametena vibhīṣaṇena śakyā vayaṃ trāsayituṃ tvayā'dya||10||

11vayaṃ punaḥ saptadaśeṣu kṛṣṇe kuleṣu sarve'navameṣu jātāḥ
|
ṣaḍbhyo guṇebhyo'bhyadhikā vihīnān manyāmahe draupadi pāṇḍuputrān||11||

12sā kṣipramātiṣṭha gajaṃ rathaṃ vā na vākyamātreṇa vayaṃ hi śakyāḥ
|
āśaṃsaṃ vā tvaṃ kṛpaṇaṃ vadantī sauvīrarājasya punaḥ prasādam||12||

13draupadyuvāca|
mahābalā kiṃ tviha durbaleva sauvīrarājasya matā'hamasmi
|
nāhaṃ pramāthādiha sampratītā sauvīrarājaṃ kṛpaṇaṃ vadeyam||13||

14yasyā hi kṛṣṇau padavīṃ caretāṃ samāsthitāvekarathe sametau
|
indro'pi tāṃ nāpaharet kathañcinmanuṣyamātraḥ kṛpaṇaḥ kuto'nyaḥ||14||

15yathā kirīṭī paravīraghātī nighnan rathastho dviṣatāṃ manāṃsi
|
madantare tvaddhvajinīṃ praveṣṭā kakṣaṃ dahannagnirivoṣṇageṣu||15||

16janārdanaḥ sāndhakavṛṣṇivīro maheṣvāsāḥ kekayāścāpi sarve
|
ete hi sarve mama rājaputrāḥ prahṛṣṭarūpāḥ padavīṃ careyuḥ||16||

17maurvīvisṛṣṭāḥ stanayitnughoṣā gāṇḍīvamuktāstvativegavantaḥ
|
hastaṃ samāhatya dhanañjayasya bhīmāḥ śabdaṃ ghorataraṃ nadanti||17||

18gāṇḍīvamuktāṃśca mahāśaraughān pataṅgasaṅgāniva śīghravegān
|
yadā draṣṭā'syarjunaṃ vīryaśālinaṃ tadā svabuddhiṃ pratininditā'si||18||

19saśaṅkhaghoṣaḥ satalatraghoṣo gāṇḍīvadhanvā muhurudvahaṃśca
|
yadā śarānarpayitā tavorasi tadā manaste kimivābhaviṣyat||19||

20gadāhastaṃ bhīmamabhidravantaṃ mādrīputrau sampatantau diśaśca
|
amarṣajaṃ krodhaviṣaṃ vamantau dṛṣṭvā ciraṃ tāpamupaiṣyase'dhama||20||

21yathā vā'haṃ nāticare kathañcit patīn mahārhān manasā'pi jātu
|
tenādya satyena vaśīkṛtaṃ tvāṃ draṣṭā'smi pārthaiḥ parikṛṣyamāṇam||21||

22na sambhramaṃ gantumahaṃ hi śakṣye tvayā nṛśaṃsena vikṛṣyamāṇā
|
samāgatā'haṃ hi kurupravīraiḥ punarvanaṃ kāmyakamāgatā'smi||22||

23vaiśampāyana uvāca|
sā tānanuprekṣya viśālanetrā jighṛkṣamāṇānavabhartsayantī
|
provāca mā mā spṛśateti bhītā dhaumyaṃ pracukrośa purohitaṃ sā||23||

24jagrāha tāmuttaravastradeśe jayadrathastaṃ samavākṣipat sā
|
tayā samākṣiptatanuḥ sa pāpaḥ papāta śākhīva nikṛttamūlaḥ||24||

25pragṛhyamāṇā tu mahājavena muhurviniḥśvasya ca rājaputrī
|
sā kṛṣyamāṇā rathamāruroha dhaumyasya pādāvabhivādya kṛṣṇā||25||

26dhaumya uvāca|
neyaṃ śakyā tvayā netumavijitya mahārathān
|
dharmaṃ kṣatrasya paurāṇamavekṣasva jayadratha||26||

27kṣudraṃ kṛtvā phalaṃ pāpaṃ tvaṃ prāpsyasi na saṃśayaḥ
|
āsādya pāṇḍavān vīrān dharmarājapurogamān||27||

28vaiśampāyana uvāca|
ityuktvā hriyamāṇāṃ tāṃ rājaputrīṃ yaśasvinīm
|
anvagacchat tadā dhaumyaḥ padātigaṇamadhyagaḥ||28||
