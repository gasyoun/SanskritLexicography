Mahābhārata 6.2 (Bhīṣmaparvan)

1vaiśampāyana uvāca|
tataḥ pūrvāpare sainye samīkṣya bhagavānṛṣiḥ
|
sarvavedavidāṃ śreṣṭho vyāsaḥ satyavatīsutaḥ ||1||

2bhaviṣyati raṇe ghore bharatānāṃ pitāmahaḥ
|
pratyakṣadarśī bhagavānbhūtabhavyabhaviṣyavit ||2||

3vaicitravīryaṃ rājānaṃ sarahasyaṃ bravīdidam
|
śocantamārtaṃ dhyāyantaṃ putrāṇāmanayaṃ tadā ||3||

4vyāsa uvāca|
rājanparikālāste putrāścānye ca pārthivāḥ
|
te hiṃsantīva saṅgrāme samāsādyetaretaram ||4||

5teṣu kālaparīteṣu vinaśyatsveva bhārata
|
kālaparyāyamājñāya mā sma śoke manaḥ kṛthāḥ ||5||

6yadi cecchasi saṅgrāme druṣṭumetān viśāmpate
|
cakṣurdadāni te putra yuddhaṃ tatra niśāmaya ||6||

7dhṛtarāṣṭra uvāca|
na rocaye jñātivadhaṃ draṣṭuṃ brahmarṣisattama
|
yuddhametattvaśeṣeṇa śrṛṇuyāṃ tava tejasā ||7||

8vaiśampāyana uvāca|
etasminnecchati draṣṭuṃ saṅgrāmaṃ śrotumicchati
|
carāṇāmīśvaro vyāsaḥ sañjayāya varaṃ dadau ||8||

9eṣa te sañjayo rājanyuddhametadvadiṣyati
|
etasya sarvasaṅgrāme na parokṣaṃ bhaviṣyati ||9||

10cakṣuṣā sañjayo rājandivyenaiva samanvitaḥ
|
kathayiṣyati te yuddhaṃ sarvajñaśca bhaviṣyati ||10||

11prakāśaṃ vā'prakāśaṃ vā divā vā yadi vā niśi
|
manasā cintitamapi sarvaṃ vetsyati sañjayaḥ ||11||

12nainaṃ śastrāṇi chetsyanti nainaṃ bādhiṣyate śramaḥ
|
gāvalgaṇirayaṃ jīvanyuddhādasmādvimokṣyate ||12||

13ahaṃ tu kīrtimeteṣāṃ karūṇāṃ bharatarṣabha
|
pāṇḍavānāṃ ca sarveṣāṃ prathayiṣyāmi mā śucaḥ ||13||

14diṣṭametannaravyāghra nābhiśocitumarhasi
|
na caiva śakyaṃ saṃyantuṃ yato dharmastato jayaḥ ||14||

15vaiśampāyana uvāca|
evamuktvā sa bhagavānkurūṇāṃ pratitāmahaḥ
|
punareva mahābhāgo dhṛtarāṣṭramuvāca ha ||15||

16iha yuddhe mahārāja bhaviṣyati mahānkṣayaḥ
|
tatheha ca nimittāni bhayadānyupalakṣaye ||16||

17śyenā gṛdhrāśca kākāśca kāṅkāśca sahitā bakaiḥ
|
sampatanti nagāgreṣu samavāyāṃśca kurvate ||17||

18abhyagraṃ ca prapaśyanti yuddhamānandino dvijāḥ
|
kravyādā bhakṣayiṣyanti māṃsāni gajavājinām ||18||

19nirdayaṃ cābhivāśanto bhairavā bhayavedinaḥ
|
kaṅkāḥ prayānti madhyena dakṣiṇāmabhito diśam ||19||

20ubhe pūrvāpare sandhye nityaṃ paśyāmi bhārata
|
udayāstamane sūryaṃ kabandhaiḥ parivāritam ||20||

21śvetalohitaparyantāḥ kṛṣṇāgrīvāḥ savidyutaḥ
|
trivarṇāḥ parighāḥ sandhau bhānumantamavārayan ||21||

22jvalitārkendunakṣatraṃ nirviśeṣadinakṣayam
|
candro'bhūdagnivarṇaśca padmavarṇanabhastale ||22||

23ālakṣyaḥ prabhayā hīnāṃ paurṇamāsīṃ ca kīrtikīm
|
candro'bhūdagnivarṇaśca padmavarṇe nabhastale ||23||

24svapsyanti nihatā vīrā bhūmimāvṛtya pārthivāḥ
|
rājāno rājaputrāśca śūrāḥ parighabāhavaḥ ||24||

25antarikṣe varāhasya pṛṣadaṃśakasya cobhayoḥ
|
praṇādaṃ yuddhyato rātrau raudraṃ nityaṃ pralakṣaye ||25||

26devatāpratimāścaiva kampanti ca hasanti ca
|
vamanti rudhiraṃ cāsyaiḥ khidyanti prapatanti ca ||26||

27anāhatā dundubhayaḥ praṇadanti viśāmpate
|
ayuktāśca pravartante kṣatriyāṇāṃ mahārathāḥ ||27||

28kokilāḥ śatapatrāśca cāṣā bhāsāḥ śukāstathā
|
sārasāśca mayūrāśca vāco muñcanti dāruṇāḥ ||28||

29gṛhītaśastrāḥ krośanti carmiṇo vājipṛṣṭhagāḥ
|
aruṇodaye pradṛśyante śataśaḥ śalabhavrajāḥ ||29||

30ubhe sandhye prakāśante diśo dāhasamanvite
|
parjanyaḥ pāṃsuvarṣī ca māṃsavarṣī ca bhārata ||30||

31yā caiṣā viśrutā rājaṃstrailokye sādhusammatā
|
arundhatī tayā'pyeṣa vasiṣṭhaḥ pṛṣṭhataḥ kṛtaḥ ||31||

32rohiṇīṃ pīḍayanneṣa sthito rājan śanaiścaraḥ
|
vyāvṛttaṃ lakṣma somasya bhaviṣyati mahadbhayam ||32||

33anabhre ca mahāghorastanitaṃ śrūyate svanaḥ
|
vāhanānāṃ ca rudatāṃ nipatantyaśrubindavaḥ ||33||
