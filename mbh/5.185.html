Mahābhārata 5.185 (Udyogaparvan)

1bhīṣma uvāca|
tato halahalāśabdo divi rājan mahānabhūt
|
prasvāpaṃ bhīṣma mā srakṣīriti kauravanandana ||1||

2ayuñjameva caivāhaṃ tadastraṃ bhṛgunandane
|
prasvāpaṃ māṃ prayuñjānaṃ nārado vākyamabravīt ||2||

3ete viyati kauravya divi devagaṇāḥ sthitāḥ
|
te tvāṃ nivārayantyadya prasvāpaṃ mā prayojaya ||3||

4rāmastapasvī brahmaṇyo brāhmaṇaśca guruśca te
|
tasyāvamānaṃ kauravya mā sma kārṣīḥ kathañcana ||4||

5tato'paśyaṃ diviṣṭhān vai tānaṣṭau brahmavādinaḥ
|
te māṃ smayanto rājendra śanakairidamabruvan ||5||

6yathā''ha bharataśreṣṭha nāradastattathā kuru
|
etaddhi paramaṃ śreyo lokānāṃ bharatarṣabha ||6||

7tataśca pratisaṃhṛtya tadasraṃ svāpanaṃ mahat
|
brahmāstraṃ dīpayāṃcakre taṃsmin yudhi yathāvidhi ||7||

8tato rāmo hṛṣito rājasiṃha dṛṣṭvā tadastraṃ vinivartitaṃ vai
|
jito'smi bhīṣmeṇa sumandabuddhirityeva vākyaṃ sahasā vyamuñcat ||8||

9tato'paśyatpitaraṃ jāmadagnyaḥ pitustathā pitaraṃ cāsya mānyam
|
te tatra cainaṃ parivārya tasthurūcuścainaṃ sāntvapūrvaṃ tadānīm ||9||

10pitara ūcuḥ| mā smaivaṃ sāhasaṃ tāta punaḥ kārṣīḥ kathañcana
|
bhīṣmeṇa saṃyugaṃ gantuṃ kṣatriyeṇa viśeṣataḥ ||10||

11kṣatriyasya tu dharmo'yaṃ yadyuddhaṃ bhṛgunandana
|
svādhyāyo vratacaryā'tha brāhmaṇānāṃ paraṃ dhanam||11||

12idaṃ nimitte kasmiṃścidasmābhiḥ prāgudāhṛtam
|
śastradhāraṇamatyugraṃ taccākāryaṃ kṛtaṃ tvayā ||12||

13vatsa paryāptametāvadbhīṣmeṇa saha saṃyuge
|
vimardaste mahābāho vyapayāhi raṇāditaḥ ||13||

14paryāptametadbhadraṃ te tava kārmukadhāraṇam
|
visarjayaitaddurdharṣa tapastapyasva bhārgava ||14||

15eṣa bhīṣmaḥ śāntanavo devaiḥ sarvairnivāritaḥ
|
nivartasva raṇādasmāditi caiva prasāditaḥ ||15||

16rāmeṇa saha mā yotsīrguruṇeti punaḥ punaḥ
|
na hi rāmo raṇe jetuṃ tvayā nyāyyaḥ kurudvaha ||16||

17mānaṃ kuruṣva gāṅgeya brāhmaṇasya raṇājire
|
vayaṃ tu guravastubhyaṃ tasmāttvāṃ vārayāmahe ||17||

18bhīṣmo vasunāmanyatamo diṣṭyā jīvasi putraka
|
gāṅgeyaḥ śāntanoḥ putro vasureṣa mahāyaśāḥ ||18||

19kathaṃ śakyastvayā jetuṃ nivartasveha bhārgava
|
arjunaḥ pāṇḍavaśreṣṭhaḥ purandarasuto balī ||19||

20naraḥ prajāpatirvīraḥ pūrvadevaḥ sanātanaḥ
|
savyasācīti vikhyātasriṣu lokeṣu vīryavān
|
bhīṣmamṛtyuryathākālaṃ vihito vai svayaṃbhuvā ||20||

21bhīṣma uvāca|
evamuktaḥ sa pitṛbhiḥ pitṝn rāmo'bravīdidam
|
nāhaṃ yudhi nivarteyamiti me vratamāhitam ||21||

22na nivartitapūrvaśca-kadācidraṇamūrdhani
|
nivartyatāmāpageyaḥ kāmaṃ yuddhāt pitāmahāḥ ||22||

23na tvahaṃ vinivartiṣye yuddhādasmāt kathañcana
|
tataste munayo rājannṛcīkapramukhāstadā ||23||

24nāradenaiva sahitāḥ samāgamyedamabruvan
|
nivartasva raṇāttāta mānayasva dvijottamam ||24||

25ityavocamahaṃ tāṃśca kṣatradharmavyapekṣayā
|
mama vratamidaṃ loke nāhaṃ yuddhāt kadācana ||25||

26vimukho vinivarteyaṃ pṛṣṭhato'bhyāhataḥ śaraiḥ
|
nāhaṃ lobhānna kārpaṇyācca bhayānnārthakāraṇāt ||26||

27tyajeyaṃ śāśvataṃ dharmamiti me niścitā matiḥ
|
tataste munayaḥ sarve nāradapramukhā nṛpa ||27||

28bhāgīrathī ca me mātā raṇamadhyaṃ prapedire
|
tathaivāttaśaro dhanvī tathaiva dṛḍhaniścayaḥ
|
sthiro'hamāhave yoddhuṃ tataste rāmamabruvan ||28||

29sametya sahitā bhūyaḥ samare bhṛgunandanam
|
nāvanītaṃ hi hṛdayaṃ viprāṇāṃ śāmya bhārgava ||29||

30rāma rāma nivartasva yuddhādasmāddvijottama
|
avadhyo vai tvayā bhīṣmastvaṃ ca bhīṣmasya bhārgava ||30||

31evaṃ bruvantaste sarve pratirudhya raṇājiram
|
nyāsayāṃcākrire śastraṃ pitaro bhṛgunandanam ||31||

32tato'haṃ punarevātha tānaṣṭau brahmavādinaḥ
|
adrākṣaṃ dīpyamānān vai grahānaṣṭāvivoditān ||32||

33te māṃ sapraṇayaṃ vākyamabruvan samare sthitam
|
praihi rāmaṃ mahābāho guruṃ lokahitaṃ kuru ||33||

34dṛṣṭvā nivartitaṃ rāmaṃ suhṛdvākyena tena vai
|
lokānāṃ ca hitaṃ kurvannahamapyādade vacaḥ ||34||

35tato'haṃ rāmamāsādya vavande bhṛśavikṣataḥ
|
rāmaścābhyutsmayan premṇā māmuvāca mahātapāḥ ||35||

36tvatsamo nāsti loke'smin kṣatriyaḥ pṛthivīcaraḥ
|
gamyatāṃ bhīṣma yuddhe'smiṃstoṣito'haṃ bhṛśaṃ tvayā ||36||

37mama caiva samakṣaṃ tāṃ kanyāmāhūya bhārgavaḥ
|
uktavān dīnayā vācā madhye teṣāṃ mahātmanām ||37||
