Mahābhārata 9.5 (Śalyaparvan)

1sañjaya uvāca|
evamuktastato rājā gautamena tapasvinā
|
niḥśvasya dīrghamuṣṇaṃ ca tūṣṇīmāsīdviśāmpate||1||

2tato muhūrtaṃ sa dhyātvā dhārtarāṣṭro mahāmanāḥ
|
kṛpaṃ śāradvataṃ vākyamityuvāca parantapaḥ||2||

3yatkiñcitsuhṛdā vācyaṃ tatsarvaṃ śrāvito hyaham
|
kṛtaṃ ca bhavatā sarvaṃ prāṇānsantyajya yudhyatā||3||

4gāhamānamanīkāni yudhyamānaṃ mahārathaiḥ
|
pāṇḍavairatitejobhirlokastvāmanudṛṣṭavān||4||

5suhṛdā yadidaṃ vākyaṃ bhavatā śrāvito hyaham
|
na māṃ prīṇāti tatsarvaṃ mumūrṣoriva bheṣajam||5||

6hetukāraṇasaṃyuktaṃ hitaṃ vacanamuttamam
|
ucyamānaṃ mahābāho na me viprāya rocate||6||

7rājyādvinikṛto'smābhiḥ kathaṃ so'smāsu viśvaset
|
akṣadyūte ca nṛpatirjito'smābhirmahādhanaḥ||7||

8sa kathaṃ mama vākyāni śraddadhyādbhūya eva tu
|
tathā dautyena samprāptaḥ kṛṣṇaḥ pārthahite rataḥ||8||

9pralabdhaśca hṛṣīkeśastacca karmāvicāritam
|
sa ca me vacanaṃ brahmankathamevābhimanyate||9||

10vilalāpa ca yatkṛṣṇā sabhāmadhye sameyuṣī
|
na tanmarṣayate kṛṣṇo na rājyaharaṇaṃ tathā||10||

11ekaprāṇāvubhau kṛṣṇāvanyonyamabhisaṃśritau
|
purā yacchrutamevāsīdadya paśyāmi tatprabho||11||

12svasrīyaṃ nihataṃ śrutvā duḥkhaṃ svapiti keśavaḥ
|
kṛtāgaso vayaṃ tasya sa madarthaṃ kathaṃ kṣamet||12||

13ābhimanyorvināśena na śarma labhate'rjunaḥ
|
sa kathaṃ maddhite yatnaṃ prakariṣyati yācitaḥ||13||

14madhyamaḥ pāṇḍavastīkṣṇo bhīmaseno mahābalaḥ
|
pratijñātaṃ ca tenograṃ bhajyetāpi na sannamet||14||

15ubhau tau baddhanistriṃśāvubhau cābaddhakaṅkaṭau
|
kṛtavairāvubhau vīrau yamāvapi yamopamau||15||

16dhṛṣṭadyumnaḥ śikhaṇḍī ca kṛtavairau mayā saha
|
tau kathaṃ maddhite yatnaṃ kuryātāṃ dvijasattama||16||

17duḥśāsanena yatkṛṣṇā ekavastrā rajasvalā
|
parikliṣṭā sabhāmadhye sarvalokasya paśyataḥ||17||

18tathā vivasanāṃ dīnāṃ smaratvadyāpi pāṇḍavāḥ
|
na nivārayituṃ śakyāḥ saṅgrāmātte parantapāḥ||18||

19yadā ca draupadī kliṣṭā madvināśāya duḥkhitā
|
sthaṇḍile nityadā śete yāvadvairasya yātanam||19||

20ugraṃ tepe tapaḥ kṛṣṇā bhartṝṇāmarthasiddhaye
|
nikṣipya mānaṃ darpaṃ ca vāsudevasahodarā||20||

21kṛṣṇāyāḥ preṣyavadbhūtvā śuśrūṣāṃ kurute sadā
|
iti sarvaṃ samunnaddhaṃ na nirvāti kathañcana||21||

22abhimanyorvināśena sa sandheyaḥ kathaṃ mayā
|
kathaṃ ca rājā bhuktvemāṃ pṛthivīṃ sāgarāmbarām||22||

23pāṇḍavānāṃ prasādena bhokṣye rājyamahaṃ katham
|
uparyupari rājñāṃ vai jvalitvā bhāskaro yathā||23||

24yudhiṣṭhiraṃ kathaṃ paścādanuyāsyāmi dāsavat
|
kathaṃ bhuktvā svayaṃ bhogān dattvā dāyāṃśca yuṣkalān||24||

25kṛpaṇaṃ vartayiṣyāmi kṛpaṇaiḥ saha jīvikām
|
nābhyasūyāmi te vākyamuktaṃ snigdhaṃ hitaṃ tvayā||25||

26na tu sandhimahaṃ manye prāptakālaṃ kathañcana
|
sunītamanupaśyāmi suyuddhena parantapa||26||

27nāyaṃ kībayituṃ kālaḥ saṃyoddhaṃ kāla eva naḥ
|
iṣṭaṃ me buhabhiryajñairdattā vipreṣu dakṣiṇāḥ||27||

28prāptāḥ kāmāḥ śrutā vedāḥ śatrūṇāṃ mūrdhni ca sthitam
|
bhṛtyā me subhṛtāstāta dīnaścābhyuddhṛto janaḥ||28||

29notsahe'dya dvijaśreṣṭha pāṇḍavānvaktumīdṛśam
|
jitāni pararāṣṭrāni svarāṣṭramanupālitam||29||

30bhuktāśca vividhā bhogāstrivargaḥ sevito mayā
|
pitṝṇāṃ gatamānṛṇyaṃ kṣatradharmasya cobhayoḥ||30||

31na dhruvaṃ sukhamastīti kuto rāṣṭraṃ kuto yaśaḥ
|
iha kīrtirvidhātavyā sā ca yuddhena nānyathā||31||

32gṛhe yatkṣatriyasyāpi nidhanaṃ tadvigarhitam
|
adharmaḥ sumahāneṣa yacchayyāmaraṇaṃ gṛhe||32||

33araṇye yo vimucyeta saṅgrāme vā tanuṃ naraḥ
|
ṛtūnādṛtya mahato mahimānaṃ sa gacchati||33||

34kṛpaṇaṃ vilapannārto jarayābhipariplutaḥ
|
mriyate rudratāṃ madhye jñātīnāṃ na sa pūruṣaḥ||34||

35tyaktvā tu vividhān bhogān prāptānāṃ paramāṃ gatim
|
apīdānīṃ suyuddhena gaccheyaṃ yatsalokatām||35||

36śūrāṇāmāryavṛttānāṃ saṅgrāmeṣvanivartinām
|
dhīmatāṃ satyasanghānāṃ sarveṣāṃ kratuyājinām||36||

37śastrāvabhṛthapūtānāṃ dhruvaṃ vāsastriviṣṭape
|
mudā nūnaṃ prapaśyanti yuddhe hyapsarasāṃ gaṇāḥ||37||

38paśyanti nūnaṃ pitaraḥ pūjitānsurasaṃsadi
|
apsarobhiḥ parivṛtānmodamānāṃstriviṣṭape||38||

39panthānamamarairyāntaṃ śūraiścaivānivartibhiḥ
|
api tatsaṅgataṃ mārgaṃ vayamadhyāruhemahi||39||

40pitāmahena vṛddhena tathā''cāryeṇa dhīmatā
|
jayadrathena karṇena tathā duḥśāsanena ca||40||

41ghaṭamānā madarthe'smin hatāḥ śūrā janādhipāḥ
|
śerate lohitāktāṅgā saṅgrāme śaravikṣatāḥ||41||

42uttamāstravidaḥ śūrā yathoktakratuyājinaḥ
|
tyaktvā prāṇān yathānyāyamindrasadmasu dhiṣṭhitāḥ||42||

43taiḥ svayaṃ racito mārgo durgamo hi punarbhavet
|
sampatadbhirmahāvegairyāsyadbhiriha sadgatim||43||

44ye madarthe hatā śūrāsteṣāṃ kṛtamanusmaran
|
ṛṇaṃ tatpratiyuñjāno na rājye mana ādadhe||44||

45ghātayitvā vayasyāṃśca bhrātṝnatha pitāmahān
|
jīvitaṃ yadi rakṣeyaṃ loko māṃ garhayeddhruvam||45||

46kīdṛśaṃ ca bhavedrājyaṃ mama hīnasya bandhubhiḥ
|
sakhibhiśca viśeṣeṇa praṇipatya ca pāṇḍavam||46||

47so'hametādṛśaṃ kṛtvā jagato'sya parābhavam
|
suyuddhena tataḥ svargaṃ prāpsyāmi na tadanyathā||47||

48evaṃ duryodhanenoktaṃ sarve sampūjya tadvacaḥ
|
sādhu sādhviti rājānaṃ kṣatriyāḥ sambabhāṣire||48||

49parājayamaśocantaḥ kṛtacittāśca vikrame
|
sarve suniścitā yoddhamudagramanaso'bhavan||49||

50tato vāhānsamāśvasya sarve yuddhābhinandinaḥ
|
ūne dviyojane gatvā pratyatiṣṭhanta kauravāḥ||50||

51ākāśe vidrume puṇye prasthe himavataḥ śubhe
|
aruṇāṃ sarasvatīṃ prāpya papuḥ sasnuśca te jalam||51||

52tava putrakṛtotsāhāḥ paryavartanta te tataḥ
|
paryavasthāpya cātmānamanyonyena punastadā
|
sarve rājannyavartanta kṣatriyāḥ kālacoditāḥ||52||
