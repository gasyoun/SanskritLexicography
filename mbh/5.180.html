Mahābhārata 5.180 (Udyogaparvan)

1bhīṣma uvāca|
ātmanastu tataḥ sūto hayānāṃ ca viśāmpate
|
mama cāpanayāmāsa śalyān kuśalasammataḥ ||1||

2snātāpavṛttaisturagairlabdhatoyairavihvalaiḥ
|
prabhāte codite sūrye tato yuddhamavartata ||2||

3dṛṣṭvā māṃ tūrṇamāyāntaṃ daṃśitaṃ syandane sthitam
|
akarodrathamatyarthaṃ rāmaḥ sajjaṃ pratāpavān ||3||

4tato'haṃ rāmamāyāntaṃ dṛṣṭvā samarakāṅkṣiṇam
|
dhanuḥśreṣṭhaṃ samutsṛjya sahasā'vataraṃ rathāt ||4||

5abhivādya tathaivāhaṃ rathamāruhya bhārata
|
yuyutsurjāmadagnyasya pramukhe vītabhīḥ sthitaḥ ||5||

6tato'haṃ śaravarṣeṇa mahatā samavākiram
|
sa ca māṃ śaravarṣeṇa varṣantaṃ samavākirat ||6||

7saṃkruddho jāmadagnyastu punareva sutejitān
|
sampraiṣīnme śarān ghorān dīptāsyānuragāniva ||7||

8tato'haṃ niśitairbhallaiḥ śataśo'tha sahasraśaḥ
|
acchidaṃ sahasā rājannantarikṣe punaḥ punaḥ ||8||

9tatastvastrāṇi divyāni jāmadagnyaḥ pratāpavān
|
mayi prayojayāmāsa tānyahaṃ pratyaṣedhayam ||9||

10astraireva mahābāho cikīrṣannadhikāṃ kriyām
|
tato divi mahānnādaḥ prādurāsīt samantataḥ ||10||

11tato'hamastraṃ vāyavyaṃ jāmadagnye prayuktavān
|
pratyājaghne ca tadrāmo guhyakāstreṇa bhārata ||11||

12tato'hamastramāgneyamanumantrya prayuktavān
|
vāruṇenaiva tadrāmo vārayāmāsa me vibhuḥ ||12||

13evamastrāṇi divyāni rāmasyāhamavārayam
|
rāmaśca mama tejasvī divyāstravidarindamaḥ ||13||

14tato māṃ savyato rājan rāmaḥ kurvan dvijottamaḥ
|
urasyavidhyat saṃkruddho jāmadagnyaḥ pratāpavān ||14||

15tato'haṃ bharataśreṣṭha saṃnyaṣīdaṃ rathottame
|
tato māṃ kaśmalāviṣṭaṃ sūtastūrṇamudāvahat||15||

16glāyantaṃ bharataśreṣṭha rāmabāṇaprapīḍitam
|
tato māmapayātaṃ vai bhṛśaṃ viddhamacetasam ||16||

17rāmasyānucarā hṛṣṭāḥ sarve dṛṣṭvā vicukruśuḥ
|
akṛtavraṇaprabhṛtayaḥ kāśikanyā ca bhārata ||17||

18tatastu labdhasaṃjño'haṃ jñātvā sūtamathābruvam
|
yāhi sūta yato rāmaḥ sajjo'haṃ gatavedanaḥ ||18||

19tato māmavahat sūto hayaiḥ paramaśobhitaiḥ
|
nṛtyadbhiriva kauravya mārutapratimairgatau ||19||

20tato'haṃ rāmamāsādya bāṇavarṣaiśca kaurava
|
avākiraṃ susaṃrabdhaḥ saṃrabdhaṃ ca jigīṣayā ||20||

21tānāpatata evāsau rāmo bāṇānajihmagān
|
bāṇairevācchinattūrṇamekaikaṃ tribhirāhave ||21||

22tataste sūditāḥ sarve mama bāṇāḥ susaṃśitāḥ
|
rāmabāṇairdvidhā chinnāḥ śataśo'tha sahasraśaḥ ||22||

23tataḥ punaḥ śaraṃ dīptaṃ suprabhaṃ kālasammitam
|
asṛjaṃ jāmadagnyāya rāmāyāhaṃ jighāṃsayā ||23||

24tena tvabhihato gāḍhaṃ bāṇavegavaśaṃ gataḥ
|
mumoha samare rāmo bhūmau ca nipapāta ha ||24||

25tato hāhākṛtaṃ sarvaṃ rāme bhūtalamāśrite
|
jagadbhārata saṃvignaṃ yathārkapatane bhavet ||25||

26tata enaṃ samudvignāḥ sarva evābhidudruvuḥ
|
tapodhanāste sahasā kāśyā ca kurunandana ||26||

27tata enaṃ pariṣvajya śanairāśvāsayaṃstadā
|
pāṇibhirjalaśītaiśca jayāśīrbhiśca kaurava ||27||

28tataḥ sa vihvalaṃ vākyaṃ rāma utthāya cābravīt
|
tiṣṭha bhīṣma hato'sīti bāṇaṃ sandhāya kārmuke||28||

29sa mukto nyapatattūrṇaṃ savye pārśve mahāhave
|
yenāhaṃ bhṛśamudvigno vyāghūrṇita iva drumaḥ ||29||

30hatvā hayāṃstato rāmaḥ śīghrāstreṇa mahāhave
|
avākiranmāṃ visrabdho bāṇaistairlomavāhibhiḥ ||30||

31tato'hamapi śīghrāstraṃ samaraprativāraṇam
|
avāsṛjaṃ mahābāho te'ntarādhiṣṭhitāḥ śarāḥ ||31||

32rāmasya mama caivāśu vyomāvṛtya samantataḥ
|
na sma sūryaḥ pratapati śarajālasamāvṛtaḥ ||32||

33mātariśvā tatastasmin megharuddha ivābhavat
|
tato vāyoḥ prakampācca sūryasya ca gabhastibhiḥ ||33||

34abhighātaprabhāvācca pāvakaḥ samajāyata
|
te śarāḥ svasamutthena pradīptāścitrabhānunā ||34||

35bhūmau sarve tadā rājan bhasmabhūtāḥ prapedire
|
tadā śatasahastrāṇi prayutānyarbudāni ca ||35||

36ayutānyatha kharvāṇi nikharvāṇi ca kaurava
|
rāmaḥ śarāṇāṃ saṃkruddho mayi tūrṇaṃ nyapātayat ||36||

37tato'haṃ tānapi raṇe śarairāśīviṣopamaiḥ
|
saṃchidya bhūmau nṛpate pātayeyaṃ nagāniva ||37||

38evaṃ tadabhavadyuddhaṃ tadā bharatasattama
|
sandhyākāle vyatīte tu vyapāyāt sa ca me guruḥ ||38||
