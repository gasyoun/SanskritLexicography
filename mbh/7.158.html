Mahābhārata 7.158 (Droṇaparvan)

1sañjaya uvāca|
udīryamāṇaṃ tadṛṣṭvā pāṇḍavānāṃ mahadbalam
|
aviṣahyaṃ ca manvānaḥ karṇaṃ duryodhano'bravīt||1||

2ayaṃ sa kālaḥ saṃprāpto mitrāṇāṃ mitravatsala
|
trāyasva samare karṇa sarvānyodhānmahārathān||2||

3pañcālairmatsyakaikeyaiḥ pāṇḍavaiśca mahārathaiḥ
|
vṛtānsamantātsaṃkruddhairniḥśvasadbhirivoragaiḥ||3||

4ete nadanti saṃhṛṣṭāḥ pāṇḍavā jitakāśinaḥ
|
śakropamāśca bahavaḥ pañcālānāṃ rathavrajāḥ||4||

5karṇa uvāca|
paritrātumiha prāpto yadi pārthaṃ purandaraḥ
|
tamapyāśu parājitya tato hantāsmi pāṇḍavam||5||

6satyaṃ te pratijānāmi samāśvasihi bhārata
|
hantāsmi pāṇḍutanayān pañcālāṃśca samāgatān||6||

7jayaṃ te pratidāsyāmi vāsavasyeva pāvakiḥ
|
priyaṃ tava mayā kāryamiti jīvāmi pārthiva||7||

8sarveṣāmeva pārthānāṃ phālguno balavattaraḥ
|
tasyāmoghāṃ vimokṣyāmi śaktiṃ śakravinirmitām||8||

9tasminhate maheṣvāse bhrātarastasya mānada
|
tava vaśyā bhaviṣyanti vanaṃ yāsyanti vā punaḥ||9||

10mayi jīvati kauravya viṣādaṃ mā kṛthāḥ kvacit
|
ahaṃ jeṣyāmi samare sahitānsarvapāṇḍavān||10||

11paṃcālānkekayāṃścaiva vṛṣṇīṃścāpi samāgatān
|
bāṇaughaiḥ śakalīkṛtya tava dāsyāmi medinīm||11||

12sañjaya uvāca|
evaṃ bruvāṇaṃ karṇaṃ tu kṛpaḥ śāradvato'bravīt
|
smayānniva mahābāhuḥ sūtaputramidaṃ vacaḥ||12||

13śobhanaṃ śobhanaṃ karṇa sanāthaḥ kurupuṅgavaḥ
|
tvayā nāthena rādheya vacasā yadi sidhyati||13||

14bahuśaḥ katthase karṇa kauravasya samīpataḥ
|
na tu te vikramaḥ kaściddṛśyate phalameva vā||14||

15samāgamaḥ pāṇḍusutairdṛṣṭaste bahuśo yudhi
|
sarvatra nirjitaścāsi pāṇḍavaiḥ sūtanandana||15||

16hriyamāṇe tadā karṇa gandharvaidhṛtarāṣṭraje
|
tadā'yudhyanta sainyāni tvameko'gre'palāyithāḥ||16||

17virāṭanagare cāpi sametāḥ sarvakauravāḥ
|
virāṭanagare cāpi sametāḥ sarvakauravāḥ||17||

18pārthena nirjitā yuddhe tvaṃ ca karṇa sahānujaḥ
|
ekasyāpyasamarthastvaṃ phālgunasya raṇājire||18||

19kathamutsahase jetuṃ sakṛṣṇānsarvapāṇḍavān
|
abruvankarṇa yudhyasva katthase bahusūtaja||19||

20anuktvā vikramedyastu tadvai satpuruṣavratam
|
garjitvā sūtaputra tvaṃ śāradābhramivāphalam||20||

21niṣphalo dṛśyase karṇa tacca rājā na budhyate
|
tāvadgarjasva rādheya yāvatpārthaṃ na paśyasi
|
ārātpārthaṃ hi te dṛṣṭvā durlabhaṃ garjitaṃ punaḥ||21||

22tvamanāsādya tānbāṇān phālgunasya vigarjasi
|
pārthasāyakaviddhasya durlabhaṃ garjitaṃ tava||22||

23bāhubhiḥ kṣatriyāḥ śūrā vāgbhiḥ śūrā dvijātayaḥ
|
dhanuṣā phālgunaḥ śūraḥ karṇaḥ śūro manorathaiḥ||23||

24toṣito yena rudro'pi kaḥ pārthaṃ pratighātayet
|
evaṃ saṃruṣitastena tadā śāradvatena ha||24||

25karṇaḥ praharatāṃ śreṣṭhaḥ kṛpaṃ vākyamathābravīt
|
śūrā garjanti satataṃ prāvṛṣīva balāhakāḥ||25||

26phalaṃ cāśu prayacchanti bajimuptamṛtāviva
|
doṣamatra na paśyāmi śūrāṇāṃ raṇamūrdhani||26||

27tattadvikatthamānānāṃ bhāraṃ codvahatāṃ mṛdhe
|
yaṃ bhāraṃ puruṣo voḍhuṃ manasā hi vyavasyati||27||

28daivamasya dhruvaṃ tatra sāhāyyāyopapadyate
|
vyavasāyadvitīyo'haṃ manasā bhāramudvahan||28||

29hatvā pāṇḍusutānājau sakṛṣṇān sahasātvatān
|
garjāmi yadyahaṃ vipra tava kiṃ tatra naśyati||29||

30vṛthā śūrā na garjanti śāradā iva toyadāḥ
|
sāmarthyamātmano jñātvā tato garjanti paṃḍitāḥ||30||

31so'hamadya raṇe yattau sahitau kṛṣṇapāṇḍavau
|
utsahe manasā jetuṃ tato garjāmi gautama||31||

32paśya tvaṃ garjitasyāsya phalaṃ me vipra sānugān
|
hatvā pāṇḍusutānājau sahakṛṣṇān sasātvatān||32||

33duryodhanāya dāsyāmi pṛthivīṃ hatakaṇṭakām
|
kṛpa uvāca|
manorathapralāpā me na grāhyāstava sutaja||33||

34sadā kṣipasi vai kṛṣṇau dharmarājaṃ ca pāṃḍavam
|
dhruvastatra jayaḥ karṇa yatra yuddhaviśāradau||34||

35devagandharvayakṣāṇāṃ manuṣyoragarakṣasām
|
daṃśitānāmapi raṇe ajesau kṛṣṇapāṇḍavau||35||

36brahmaṇyaḥ satyavāgdānto gurudaivatapūjakaḥ
|
nityaṃ dharmarataścaiva kṛtāstraśca viśeṣataḥ||36||

37dhṛtimāṃśca kṛtajñaśca dharmaputro yudhiṣṭhiraḥ
|
bhrātaraścāsya balinaḥ sarvāstreṣu kṛtaśramāḥ||37||

38guruvṛttiratāḥ prājñā dharmanityā yaśasvinaḥ
|
saṃbandhinaścendravīryāḥ svanuraktāḥ prahāriṇaḥ||38||

39dhṛṣṭadyumnaḥ śikhaṇḍī ca daurmukhirjanamejayaḥ
|
caṃdraseno rudrasenaḥ kīrtidharmā dhruvo dharaḥ||39||

40vasucandro dāmacandraḥ siṃhacandraḥ sutejanaḥ
|
drupadasya tathā putrā drupadaśca mahāstravit||40||

41yeṣāmarthāya saṃyatto matsyarājaḥ sahānujaḥ
|
śatānīkaḥ sūryadattaḥ śrutānīkaḥ śrutadhvajaḥ||41||

42balānīko jayānīko jayāśvo rathavāhanaḥ
|
candrodayaḥ samaratho virāṭabhrātaraḥ śubhāḥ||42||

43yamau ca draupadeyāśca rākṣasaśca ghaṭotkacaḥ
|
yeṣāmarthāya yudhyante na teṣāṃ vidyate kṣayaḥ||43||

44ete cānye ca bahavo gaṇāḥ pāṇḍusutasya vai
|
kāmaṃ khalu jagatsarvaṃ sadevāsuramānuṣam||44||

45sayakṣarākṣasagaṇaṃ sabhūtabhujagadvipam
|
niḥśeṣamastravīryeṇa kurvāte bhīmaphālgunau||45||

46yudhiṣṭhiraśca pṛthivīṃ nirdaheddhoracakṣuṣā
|
aprameyabalaḥ śauriryeṣāmarthe ca daṃśitaḥ||46||

47kathaṃ tānsaṃyuge karṇa jetumutsahase parān
|
mahānapanayastveṣa nityaṃ hi tava sūtaja||47||

48yastvamutsahase yoddhuṃ samare śauriṇā saha
|
sañjaya uvāca|
evamuktastu rādheyaḥ prahasan bharatarṣabha||48||

49abravīcca tadā karṇo guruṃ śāradvataṃ kṛpam
|
satyamuktaṃ tvayā brahmanpāṇḍavānprati yadvacaḥ||49||

50ete cānye ca bahavo guṇāḥ pāṇḍusuteṣu vai
|
ajayyāśca raṇe pārthā devairapi savāsavaiḥ||50||

51sadaityayakṣagandharvaiḥ piśācoragarākṣasaiḥ
|
tathāpi pārthāñjeṣyāmi śaktyā vāsavadattayā||51||

52mama hyamoghā datteyaṃ śaktiḥ śakreṇa vai dvija
|
etayā nihaniṣyāmi savyasācinamāhave||52||

53hate tu pāṇḍave kṛṣṇe bhrātaraścāsya sodarāḥ
|
anarjunā na śakṣyanti mahīṃ bhoktuṃ kathañcana||53||

54teṣu naṣṭeṣu sarveṣu pṛthivīyaṃ sasāgarā
|
ayatnātkauravendrasya vaśe sthāsyati gautama||54||

55sunītairiha sarvārthāḥ sidhyante nātra saṃśayaḥ
|
etamarthamahaṃ jñātvā tato garjāmi gautama||55||

56tvaṃ tu vipraśca vṛddhaśca aśaktaścāpi saṃyuge
|
kṛtasnehaśca pārtheṣu mohānmāmavamanyase||56||

57yadyevaṃ vakṣyase bhūyo mamāpriyamiha dvija
|
tataste khaḍgamudyamya jihvāṃ chetsyāmi durmate||57||

58yaccāpi pāṇḍavānvipra stotumicchasi saṃyuge
|
bhīṣayansarvasainyāni kauraveyāṇi durmate||58||

59atrāpi śṛṇu me vākyaṃ yathāvadbruvato dvija
|
duryodhanaśca droṇaśca śakunirdurmukho jayaḥ||59||

60duḥśāsano vṛṣaseno madrarājastvameva ca
|
somadattaśca bhūriśca tathā drauṇirviviṃśatiḥ||60||

61tiṣṭheyurdaṃśitā yatra sarve yuddhaviśāradāḥ
|
jayedetānnaraḥ ko nu śakratulyabalo'pyariḥ||61||

62śūrāśca hi kṛtāstrāśca balinaḥ svargalipsavaḥ
|
dharmajñā yuddhakuśalā hanyuryuddhe surānapi||62||

63ete sthāsyanti saṃgrāme pāṇḍavānāṃ vadhārthinaḥ
|
jayamākāṃkṣamāṇā hi kauraveyasya daṃśitāḥ||63||

64daivāyattamahaṃ manye jayaṃ subalināmapi
|
yatra bhīṣmo mahābāhuḥ śete śaraśatācitaḥ||64||

65vikarṇaścitrasenaśca bālhīko'tha jayadrathaḥ
|
bhūriśravā jayaścaiva jalasandhaḥ sudakṣiṇaḥ||65||

66śalaśca rathināṃ śreṣṭho bhagadattaśca vīryavān
|
ete cānye ca rājāno devairapi sudurjayāḥ||66||

67nihatāḥ samare śūrāḥ pāṇḍavairbalavattarāḥ
|
kimanyaddaivasaṃyogānmanyase puruṣādhama||67||

68yāṃśca tāṃstauṣi satataṃ duryodhanaripūndvija
|
teṣāmapi hatāḥ śūrāḥ śataśo'tha sahasraśaḥ||68||

69kṣīyante sarvasainyāni kurūṇāṃ pāṇḍavaiḥ saha
|
prabhāvaṃ nātra paśyāmi pāṇḍavānāṃ kathañcana||69||

70yastānbalavato nityaṃ manyase tvaṃ dvijādhama
|
yatiṣye'haṃ yathāśakti yoddhuṃ taiḥ saha saṃyugaḥ||70||

71duryodhanahitārthāya 'jayo daive pratiṣṭhitaḥ'||71||
