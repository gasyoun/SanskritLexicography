Mahābhārata 7.124 (Droṇaparvan)

1dhṛtarāṣṭra uvāca|
kiṃ tasyāṃ mama senāyāṃ nāsankecinmahārathāḥ
|
ye tathā sātyakiṃ yāntaṃ naivāghnannāpyavārayan ||1||

2eko hi samare karma kṛtavān satyavikramaḥ
|
śakratulyabalo yuddhe mahendro dānaveṣviva ||2||

3athavā śūnyamāsīttadyena yātaḥ sa sātyakiḥ
|
hatabhūyiṣṭhamathavā yena yātaḥ sa sātyakiḥ ||3||

4yatkṛtaṃ vṛṣṇivīreṇa karma śaṃsasi me raṇe
|
naitadutsahate kartuṃ karma śakro'pi saṃjaya ||4||

5aśraddheyamacintyaṃ ca karma tasya mahātmanaḥ
|
vṛṣṇyandhakapravīrasya śrutvā me vyathitaṃ manaḥ ||5||

6na santi tasmāt putrā me yathā sañjaya bhāṣase
|
eko vai bahulāḥ senāḥ prāmṛdnāta satyavikramaḥ ||6||

7kathaṃ ca yudhyamānānāmapakrānto mahātmanām
|
eko bahūnāṃ śaineyastanmamācakṣva sañjaya ||7||

8sañjaya uvāca|
rājansenāsamudyogo rathanāgāśvapattimān
|
tumulastava sainyānāṃ yugāntasadṛśo'bhavat ||8||

9āhūteṣu samūheṣu tava sainyasya mānada
|
nābhūlloke samaḥ kaścitsamūha iti me matiḥ ||9||

10tatra devāstvabhāṣanta cāraṇāśca samāgatāḥ
|
etadantāḥ samūhā vai bhaviṣyanti mahītale ||10||

11na ca vai tādṛśo vyūha āsītkaścidviśāṃpate
|
yādṛgjayadrathavadhe droṇena vihito'bhavat ||11||

12caṇḍavātavibhinnānāṃ samudrāṇāmiva svanaḥ
|
raṇe'bhavadbalaughānāmanyonyamabhidhāvatām ||12||

13pārthivānāṃ sametānāṃ bahūnyāsannarottama
|
tvadbale pāṇḍavānāṃ ca saha‌srāṇi śatāni ca ||13||

14saṃrabdhānāṃ pravīrāṇāṃ samare dṛḍhakarmaṇām
|
tatrāsītsumahāśabdastumulo lomaharṣaṇaḥ ||14||

15athākrandadbhīmaseno dhṛṣṭadyumnaśca māriṣa
|
nakulaḥ sahadevaśca dharmarājaśca pāṇḍavaḥ ||15||

16āgacchata praharata drutaṃ viparidhāvata
|
praviṣṭāvarisenāṃ hi vīrau mādhavapāṇḍavau ||16||

17yathā sukhena gacchetāṃ jayadrathavadhaṃ prati
|
tathā prakuruta kṣipramiti sainyānyacodayan ||17||

18tayorabhāve kuravaḥ kṛtārthāḥ syurvayaṃ jitāḥ
|
te yūyaṃ sahitā bhūtvā tūrṇameva balārṇavam ||18||

19kṣobhayadhvaṃ mahāvegāḥ pavanaḥ sāgaraṃ yathā
|
bhīmasenena te rājanpāñcālyena ca noditāḥ ||19||

20ājaghnuḥ kauravānsaṅkhye tyaktvā'sūnātmanaḥ priyān
|
icchanto nidhanaṃ yuddhe śastrairuttamatejasaḥ ||20||

21svargepsavo mitrakārye nābhyanandanta jīvitam
|
tathaiva tāvakā rājanprārthayanto mahadyaśaḥ ||21||

22āryāṃ yuddhe matiṃ kṛtvā yuddhāyaivāvatasthire
|
tasminsutumule yuddhe vartamāne bhayāvahe ||22||

23jitvā sarvāṇi sainyāni prāyātsātyakirarjunam
|
kavacānāṃ prabhāstatra sūryaraśmivirājitāḥ ||23||

24dṛṣṭīḥ saṅkhye sainikānāṃ pratijaghnuḥ samantataḥ
|
tathā prayatamānānāṃ pāṇḍavānāṃ mahātmanām ||24||

25duryodhano mahārāja vyagāhata mahadbalanam
|
sa sannipātastumulasteṣāṃ tasya ca bhārata ||25||

26abhavatsarvabhūtānāmabhāvakaraṇo mahān
|
dhṛtarāṣṭra uvāca|
tathā yāteṣu sainyeṣu tathā kṛcchragataḥ svayam ||26||

27kaccidduryodhanaḥ sūta nākārṣītpṛṣṭhato raṇam
|
ekasya ca bahūnāṃ ca sannipāto mahāhave ||27||

28viśeṣato narapaterviṣamaḥ pratibhāti me
|
sotyantasukhasaṃvṛddho lakṣmyā lokasya ceśvaraḥ ||28||

29eko bahūnsamāsādya kaccinnāsītparāṅmukhaḥ
|
sañjaya uvāca|
rājansaṃgrāmamāścaryaṃ tava putrasya bhārata ||29||

30ekasya bahubhiḥ sārdhaṃ śṛṇuṣva gadato mama
|
duryodhanena samare pṛtanā pāṇḍavī raṇe ||30||

31nalinī dviradeneva samantātpratiloḍitā
|
tatastāṃ prahitāṃ senāṃ dṛṣṭvā putreṇa te nṛpa ||31||

32bhīmasenapurogāstaṃ pañcālāḥ samupādravan
|
sa bhīmasenaṃ daśabhiḥ śarairvivyādha pāṇḍavam ||32||

33tribhistribhiryamau vīrau dharmarājaṃ ca saptabhiḥ
|
virāṭadrupadau ṣaḍbhiḥ śatena ca śikhaṇḍinamv ||33||

34dhṛṣṭadyumnaṃ ca viṃśatyā draupadeyāṃstribhistribhiḥ
|
śataśaścāparānyodhānsadvipāṃśca rathān raṇe ||34||

35śarairavacakartograiḥ kruddho'ntaka iva prajāḥ
|
na sandadhanvimuñcanvā maṇḍalīkṛtakārmukaḥ ||35||

36adṛśyata ripūnnighnañchikṣayā'strabalena ca
|
tasya tānnighnataḥ śatrūnhemapṛṣṭhaṃ mahaddhanuḥ ||36||

37ajasraṃ maṇḍalībhūtaṃ dadṛśuḥ samare janāḥ
|
tato yudhiṣṭhiro rājā bhallābhyāmacchinaddhanuḥ ||37||

38tava putrasya kauravya yatamānasya saṃyuge
|
vivyādha cainaṃ daśabhiḥ samyagastaiḥ śarottamaiḥ ||38||

39varma cāśu samāsādya te bhittvā kṣitimāviśan
|
tataḥ pramuditāḥ pārthāḥ parivavruryudhiṣṭhiram ||39||

40yathā vṛtravadhe devāḥ purā śakraṃ maharṣayaḥ
|
tato'nyaddhanurādāya tava putraḥ pratāpavān ||40||

41tiṣṭha tiṣṭheti rājānaṃ bruvanpāṇḍavamabhyayāt
|
tamāyāntamabhiprekṣya tava putraṃ mahāmṛdhe ||41||

42pratyudyayuḥ samuditāḥ pañcālā jayagṛddhinaḥ
|
tāndroṇaḥ pratijagrāha parīpsan yudhi pāṇḍavam ||42||

43caṇḍavātoddhutānmeghān girirambumuco yathā
|
tatra rājanmahānāsītsaṃgrāmo lomaharṣaṇaḥ ||43||

44pāṇḍavānāṃ mahābāho tāvakānāṃ ca saṃyuge
|
rudrasyākrīḍasadṛśaḥ saṃhāraḥ sarvadehinām ||44||

45tataḥ śabdo mahānāsītpunaryena dhanañjayaḥ
|
atīva sarvaśabdebhyo lomaharṣakaraḥ prabho ||45||

46arjunasya mahābāho tāvakānāṃ ca dhanvinām
|
madhye bhāratasainyasya mādhavasya mahāraṇe ||46||

47droṇasyāpi paraiḥ sārdhaṃ vyūhadvāre mahāraṇe
|
evameṣa kṣayo vṛttaḥ pṛthivyāṃ pṛthivīpate
|
kruddhe'rjune tathā droṇe sātvate ca mahārathe ||47||
