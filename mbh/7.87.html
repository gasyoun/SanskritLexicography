Mahābhārata 7.87 (Droṇaparvan)

1sañjaya uvāca| tasyāṃ niśāyāṃ vyuṣṭāyāṃ droṇaḥ śāstrabhṛtāṃ varaḥ
|
svānyanīkāni sarvāṇi prākrāmadvyūhituṃ tataḥ ||1||

2śūrāṇāṃ garjatāṃ rājan saṃkruddhānāmamarṣiṇām
|
śrūyante sma giraścitrāḥ parasparavadhaiṣiṇām ||2||

3visphārya ca dhanūṃṣyanye jyāḥ pare parimṛjya ca
|
viniḥśvasantaḥ prākośan kvedānīṃ sa dhanañjayaḥ ||3||

4vikośān sutsarūnanye kṛtadhārān samāhitān
|
pītānākāśasaṃkāśānasīn kecicca cikṣipuḥ ||4||

5carantastvasimārgāṃśca dhanurmārgāṃśca śikṣayā
|
saṃgrāmamanasaḥ śūrā dṛśyante sma sahasraśaḥ ||5||

6saghaṇṭāścandanādigdhāḥ svarṇavajravibhūṣitāḥ
|
samutkṣipya gadāścānye paryapṛcchanta pāṇḍavam ||6||

7anye balamadonmattāḥ parighairbāhuśālinaḥ
|
cakruḥ sambādhamākāśamucchritendradhvajopamaiḥ ||7||

8nānāpraharaṇaiścānye vicitrasragalaṅkṛtāḥ
|
saṃgrāmamanasaḥ śūrāstatra tatra vyavasthitāḥ ||8||

9kvārjunaḥ kva sa govindaḥ kva ca mānī vṛkodaraḥ
|
kva ca te suhṛdasteṣāmāhvayante raṇe tadā ||9||

10tataḥ śaṃkhamupādhmāya tvarayan vājinaḥ svayam
|
itastatastān racayan droṇaścarati vegitaḥ ||10||

11teṣvanīkeṣu sarveṣu sthiteṣvāhavanandiṣu
|
bhāradvājo mahārāja jayadrathamathābravīt ||11||

12tvaṃ caiva saumadattiśca karṇaścaiva mahārathaḥ
|
aśvatthāmā ca śalyaśca vṛṣasenaḥ kṛpastathā ||12||

13śataṃ cāśvasahasrāṇāṃ rathānāmayutāni ṣaṭ
|
dviradānāṃ prabhinnānāṃ sahasrāṇi caturdaśa ||13||

14padātīnāṃ sahasrāṇi daṃśitānyekaviṃśatiḥ
|
gavyūtiṣu trimātrāsu māmanāsādya tiṣṭhata ||14||

15tatrasthaṃ tvāṃ na saṃsoḍhuṃ śaktā devāḥ savāsavāḥ
|
kiṃ punaḥ pāṇḍavāḥ sarve samāśvasihi saindhava ||15||

16evamuktaḥ samāśvastaḥ sindhurājo jayadrathaḥ
|
saṃprāyāt saha gāndhārairvṛtastaiśca mahārathaiḥ ||16||

17varmibhiḥ sādibhiryattaiḥ prāsapāṇibhirāsthitaiḥ
|
cāmarāpīḍinaḥ sarve jāmbūnadavibhūṣitāḥ ||17||

18jayadrathasya rājendra hayāḥ sādhupravāhinaḥ
|
te caikasapta sāhasrāstrisāhasrāśca saindhavāḥ ||18||

19mattānāṃ suviruḍhānāṃ hastyārohairviśāradaiḥ
|
nāgānāṃ bhīmarūpāṇāṃ varmiṇāṃ raudrakarmiṇām ||19||

20adhyardhena sahasreṇa putro durmarṣaṇastava
|
agrataḥ sarvasainyānāṃ yudhyamāno vyavasthitaḥ ||20||

21tato duḥśāsanaścaiva vikarṇaśca tavātmajau
|
sindhurājārthasiddhyarthamagrānīke vyavasthitau ||21||

22dīrgho dvādaśa gavyūtiḥ paścārdhe pañca vistṛtaḥ
|
vyūhastu cakraśakaṭo bhāradvājena nirmitaḥ ||22||

23nānānṛpatibhirvīraistatra tatra vyavasthitaiḥ
|
rathāśvagajapattyoghairdroṇena vihitaḥ svayam ||23||

24paścārdhe tasya padmastu garbhavyūhaḥ sudurbhidaḥ
|
sūcīpadmasya garbhastho gūḍho vyūhaḥ kṛtaḥ punaḥ ||24||

25evametaṃ mahāvyūhaṃ vyūhya droṇo vyavasthitaḥ
|
sūcīmukhe maheṣvāsaḥ kṛtavarmā vyavasthitaḥ ||25||

26anantaraṃ ca kāṃbojo jalasandhaśca māriṣa
|
duryodhanaśca karṇaśca tadanantarameva ca ||26||

27tataḥ śatasahasrāṇi yodhānāmanivartinām
|
vyavasthitāni sarvāṇi śakaṭe mukharakṣiṇām ||27||

28teṣāṃ ca pṛṣṭhato rājā balena mahatā vṛtaḥ
|
jayadrathastato rājā sūcīpārśve vyavasthitaḥ ||28||

29śakaṭasya tu rājendra bhāradvājo mukhe sthitaḥ
|
anu tasyābhavadbhojo jugopainaṃ tataḥ svayam ||29||

30śvetavarmāmbaroṣṇīṣo vyūḍhorasko mahābhujaḥ
|
dhanurvisphārayan droṇastasthau kruddha ivāntakaḥ ||30||

31patākinaṃ śoṇahayaṃ vedikṛṣṇājinadhvajam
|
droṇasya rathamālokya prahṛṣṭāḥ kuravo'bhavan ||31||

32siddhacāraṇasaṃghānāṃ vismayaḥ sumahānabhūt
|
droṇena vihitaṃ dṛṣṭvā vyūhaṃ kṣubdhārṇavopamam ||32||

33saśailasāgaravanāṃ nānājanapadākulām
|
grasedvyūhaḥ kṣitiṃ sarvāmiti bhūtāni menire ||33||

34bahurathamanujāśvapattināgaṃ pratibhayaniḥsvanamadbhutānurūpam
|
ahitahṛdayabhedanaṃ maha‌dvai śakaṭamavekṣya kṛtaṃ nananda rājā ||34||
