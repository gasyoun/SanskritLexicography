Mahābhārata 5.53 (Udyogaparvan)

1dhṛtarāṣṭra uvāca|
yathaiva pāṇḍavāḥ sarve parākrāntā jigīṣavaḥ
|
tathaivābhisarāsteṣāṃ tyaktātmāno jaye dhṛtāḥ ||1||

2tvameva hi parākrāntānācakṣīthāḥ parānmama
|
pañcālānkekayānmatsyānmāgadhānvatsabhūmipān ||2||

3yaśca sendrānimā~llokānicchan kuryādvaśe balī
|
sa sraṣṭā jagataḥ kṛṣṇaḥ pāṇḍavānāṃ jaye dhṛtaḥ ||3||

4samastāmarjunādvidyāṃ sātyakiḥ kṣipramāptavān
|
śaineyaḥ samare sthātā bījavatpravapan śarān ||4||

5dhṛṣṭadyumnaśca pāñcālyaḥ krūrakarmā mahārathaḥ
|
māmakeṣu raṇaṃ kartā baleṣu paramāstravit ||5||

6yudhiṣṭhirasya ca krodhādarjunasya ca vikramāt
|
yamābhyāṃ bhīmasenācca bhayaṃ me tāta jāyate ||6||

7amānuṣaṃ manuṣyendrairjālaṃ vitatamantarā
|
na me sainyāstariṣyanti tataḥ krośāmi sañjaya ||7||

8darśanīyo manasvī ca lakṣmīvān brahmavarcasī
|
medhāvī sukṛtaprajño dharmātmā pāṇḍunandanaḥ ||8||

9mitrāmātyaiḥ susaṃpannaḥ saṃpanno yuddhayojakaiḥ
|
bhrātṛbhiḥ śvaśurairvīrairupapanno mahārathaiḥ ||9||

10dhṛtyā ca puruṣavyāghro naibhṛtyena ca pāṇḍavaḥ
|
anṛśaṃso vadānyaśca hrīmān satyaparākramaḥ ||10||

11bahuśrutaḥ kṛtātmā ca vṛddhasevī jitendriyaḥ
|
taṃ sarvaguṇasampannaṃ samiddhamiva pāvakam ||11||

12tapantamabhi ko mandaḥ patiṣyati pataṅgavat
|
pāṇḍavāgnimanāvāryaṃ mumūrṣurnaṣṭacetanaḥ ||12||

13tanuruddhaḥ śikhī rājā mithyopacarito mayā
|
mandānāṃ sama putrāṇāṃ yuddhenāntaṃ kariṣyati ||13||

14tairayuddhaṃ sādhu manye kuravastannibodhata
|
yuddhe vināśaḥ kṛtsnasya kulasya bhavitā dhruvam ||14||

15eṣā me paramā buddhiryayā śāmyati me manaḥ
|
yadi tvayuddhamiṣṭaṃ vo vayaṃ śāntyai yatāmahe ||15||

16na tu naḥ kliśmamānānāmupekṣeta yudhiṣṭhiraḥ
|
jugupsati hyadharmeṇa māmevoddiśya kāraṇam ||16||
