Mahābhārata 4.1 (Virāṭaparvan)

2janamejaya uvāca| kathaṃ virāṭanagare mama pūrvapitāmahāḥ
|
ajñātavāsamuṣitā duryodhanabhayārditāḥ ||2||

3pativratā mahābhāgā satataṃ brahmavādinī
|
draupadī ca kathaṃ brahmannajñātā duḥkhitā'vasat ||3||

4vaiśampāyana uvāca|yathā virāṭanagare tava pūrvapitāmahāḥ
|
ajñātavāsamuṣitāstacchṛṇuṣva narādhipa ||4||

5tathā sa tu varā~llabdhvā dharmo dharmabhṛtāṃ varaḥ
|
gatvā''śramaṃ brāhmaṇebhya ācakhyau sarvameva tat ||5||

6kathayitvā tu tat sarvaṃ brāhmaṇebhyo yudhiṣṭhiraḥ
|
araṇīsahitaṃ tasmai brāhmaṇāya nyavedayat ||6||

7tato yudhiṣṭhiro rājā dharmaputro mahāmanāḥ
|
saṃnivartyānujān sarvāniti hovāca bhārata ||7||

8dvādaśemāni varṣāṇi rājyaviproṣitā vayam
|
trayodaśo'yaṃ samprāptaḥ kṛcchrāt paramadurvasaḥ ||8||

9sa sādhu kaunteya ito vāsamarjuna rocaya
|
saṃvatsaramimaṃ yatra vasemāviditāḥ paraiḥ ||9||

10arjuna uvāca|tasyaiva varadānena dharmasya manujādhipa
|
ajñātā vicariṣyāmo narāṇāṃ nātra saṃśayaḥ ||10||

11tatra vāsāya rāṣṭrāṇi kīrtayiṣyāmi kānicit
|
ramaṇīyāni guptāni teṣāṃ kiñcit sma rocaya ||11||

12santi ramyā janapadā bahvannāḥ paritaḥ kurūn
|
pāñcālāścedimatsyāśca śūrasenāḥ paṭaccarāḥ ||12||

13daśārṇā navarāṣṭrāśca mallāḥ śālvā yugandharāḥ
|
kuntirāṣṭraṃ ca vipulaṃ surāṣṭrāvantayastathā ||13||

14eteṣāṃ katamo rājan nivāsastava rocate
|
yatra vatsyāmahe rājan saṃvatsaramimaṃ vayam ||14||

15yudhiṣṭhira uvāca|śrutametanmahābāho yathā sa bhagavān prabhuḥ
|
abravīt sarvabhūteśastat tathā na tadanyathā ||15||

16avaśyaṃ tveva vāsārthaṃ ramaṇīyaṃ śivaṃ sukham
|
sammantrya sahitaiḥ sarvairvastavyamakutobhayaiḥ ||16||

17matsyo virāṭo balavānabhirakto'tha pāṇḍavān
|
dharmaśīlo vadānyaśca vṛddhaśca satataṃ priyaḥ ||17||

18virāṭanagare tāta saṃvatsaramimaṃ vayam
|
kurvantastasya karmāṇi vihariṣyāma bhārata ||18||

19yāni yāni ca karmāṇi tasya vakṣyāmahe vayam
|
āsādya matsyaṃ tat karmā brūta kurunandanāḥ ||19||

20arjuna uvāca|naradeva kathaṃ tasya rāṣṭre karma kariṣyasi
|
virāṭanagare sādho raṃsyase kena karmaṇā ||20||

21mṛdurvadānyo hrīmāṃśca dhārmikaḥ satyavikramaḥ
|
rājaṃstvamāpadā''kṛṣṭaḥ kiṃ kariṣyasi pāṇḍava ||21||

22na duḥkhamucitaṃ kiñcidrājā veda yathā janaḥ
|
sa imāmāpadaṃ prāpya kathaṃ ghorāṃ tariṣyasi ||22||

23yudhiṣṭhira uvāca|śṛṇudhvaṃ yat kariṣyāmi karma vai kurunandanāḥ
|
virāṭamanusamprāpya rājānaṃ puruṣarṣabhāḥ ||23||

24sabhāstāro bhaviṣyāmi tasya rājñāṃ mahātmanaḥ
|
kaṅko nāma dvijo bhūtvā matākṣaḥ priyadevanaḥ ||24||

25vaidūryān kāñcanān dāntān phalairjyotīrasaiḥ saha
|
kṛṣṇākṣā~llohitākṣāṃśca nirvartsyāmi manoramān ||25||

26virāṭarājaṃ ramayan sāmātyaṃ sahabāndhavam
|
na ca māṃ vetsyate kaścit toṣayiṣye ca taṃ nṛpam ||26||

27āsaṃ yudhiṣṭhirasyāhaṃ purā prāṇasamaḥ sakhā
|
iti vakṣyāmi rājānaṃ yadi māṃ so'nuyokṣyate ||27||

28ityetadvo mayā''khyātaṃ vihariṣyāmyahaṃ yathā
|
vṛkodara virāṭe tvaṃ raṃsyase kena hetunā ||28||
