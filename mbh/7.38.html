Mahābhārata 7.38 (Droṇaparvan)

1dhṛtarāṣṭra uvāca|
tathā pramathamānaṃ taṃ maheṣvāsānajihmagaiḥ
|
ārjuniṃ māmakāḥ saṃkhye ke tvena samavārayan||1||

2sañjaya uvāca|
śruṇu rājan kumārasya raṇe vikrīḍitaṃ mahat
|
bibhitsato rathānīkaṃ bhāradvājena rakṣitam||2||

3mandreśaṃ sāditaṃ dṛṣṭvā saubhadreṇāśugai raṇe
|
śalyādavarajaḥ kruddhaḥ kiran bāṇān samabhyayāt||3||

4sa viddhvā daśabhirbāṇaiḥ sāśvayantāramārjunim
|
uda‌kośanmahāśabdaṃ tiṣṭha tiṣṭheti cābravīt||4||

5tasyārjuniḥ śirogrīvaṃ pāṇipādaṃ dhanurhayān
|
chatraṃ dhvajaṃ niyantāraṃ triveṇuṃ talpameva ca||5||

6cakraṃ yugaṃ ca tūṇīraṃ hyanukarṣaṃ ca sāyakaiḥ
|
patākāṃ cakragoptārau sarvopakaraṇāni ca||6||

7laghuhastaḥ praciccheda dadṛśe taṃ na kaścana
|
sa papāta kṣitau kṣīṇaḥ praviddhābharaṇāmbaraḥ||7||

8vāyuneva mahāśailaḥ saṃbhagno'mitatejasā
|
anugāstasya vitrastāḥ prādravan sarvato diśaḥ||8||

9ārjuneḥ karma taddṛṣṭvā saṃpraṇeduḥ samantataḥ
|
nodana sarvabhūtāni sādhu sādhviti bhārata||9||

10śalyabhrātaryathārugṇe bahuśastasya sainikāḥ
|
kulādhivāsanāmāni śrāvayanto'rjunātmajam||10||

11abhyadhāvanta saṃkruddhā vividhāyudhapāṇayaḥ
|
rathairaśvairgajaiścānye padbhiścānye balotkaṭāḥ||11||

12bāṇaśabdena mahatā rathanemisvanena ca
|
huṃkāraiḥ kṣveḍitotkruṣṭaiḥ siṃhanādaiḥ sagarjitaiḥ||12||

13jyātalatrasvanairanye garjanto'rjunanandanam
|
buvantaśca na no jīvanmokṣyase jīvitāditi||13||

14tāṃstathā bruvato dṛṣṭvā saubhadraḥ prahasanniva
|
yo yo'smai prāharat pūrvaṃ taṃ taṃ vivyādha patribhiḥ||14||

15saṃdarśayiṣyannastrāṇi vicitrāṇi laghūni ca
|
ārjuniḥ samare śūro mṛdupūrvamayudhyata||15||

16vāsudevādupāttaṃ yadastraṃ yacca dhanañjayāt
|
adarśayata tatkārṣṇiḥ kṛṣṇābhyāmaviśeṣavat||16||

17dūramasya guruṃ bhāraṃ sādhvasaṃ ca punaḥ punaḥ
|
saṃdadhadvisṛjaṃśceṣūnnirviśeṣamadṛśyata||17||

18cāpamaṇḍalamevāsya visphuraddikṣvadṛśyata
|
sudīptasya śaratkāle saviturmaṇḍalaṃ yathā||18||

19jyāśabdaḥ śuśruve tasya talaśabdaśca dāruṇaḥ
|
mahāśanimucaḥ kāle payodasyeva niḥsvanaḥ||19||

20hrīmānamarṣī saubhadro mānakṛtpriyadarśanaḥ
|
saṃmimānayiṣurvīrāniṣvastraiścāpyayudhyata||20||

21mṛdurbhūtvā mahārāja dāruṇaḥ samapadyata
|
varṣābhyatīto bhagavāñchaśaradīva divākaraḥ||21||

22śarān vicitrān subahūn rukmapuṅkhāñchilāśitān
|
mumoca śataśaḥ kruddho gabhastīniva bhāskaraḥ||22||

23kṣuraprairvatsadantaiśca vipāṭhaiśca mahāyaśāḥ
|
nārācairardhacandrābhairbhallairañjalikairapi||23||

24avākiradrathānīkaṃ bhāradvājasya paśyataḥ
|
tatastatsainyamabhavadvimukhaṃ śarapīḍitam||24||
