Mahābhārata 1.46 (Ādiparvan)

1sautiruvāca|
etacchrutvā jaratkārurbhṛśaṃ śokaparāyaṇaḥ
|
uvāca tānpitṝnduḥkhādbāṣpasaṃdigdhayā girā||1||

2jaratkāruruvāca|
mama pūrve bhavanto vai pitaraḥ sapitāmahāḥ
|
tadbrūta yanmayā kāryaṃ bhavatāṃ priyakāmyayā||2||

3ahameva jaratkāruḥ kilbiṣī bhavatāṃ sutaḥ
|
te daṇḍaṃ dhārayata me duṣkṛterakṛtātmanaḥ||3||

4pitara ūcuḥ|
putra diṣṭyā'si saṃprāpta imaṃ deśaṃ yadṛcchayā
|
kimarthaṃ ca tvayā brahmannakṛto dārasaṅgrahaḥ||4||

5jaratkāruruvāca|
mamāyaṃ pitaro nityaṃ hṛdyarthaḥ parivartate
|
ūrdhvaretāḥ śarīraṃ vai prāpayeyamamutra vai||5||

6na dārānvai kariṣye'hamiti me bhāvitaṃ manaḥ
|
evaṃ dṛṣṭvā tu bhavataḥ śakuntāniva lambataḥ||6||

7mayā nivartitā buddhirbrahmacaryātpitāmahāḥ
|
kariṣye vaḥ priyaṃ kāmaṃ nivekṣye'hamasaṃśayam||7||

8sanāmnīṃ yadyahaṃ kanyāmupalapsye kadācana
|
bhaviṣyati ca yā kācidbhaikṣyavatsvayamudyatā||8||

9pratigrahītā tāmasmi na bhareyaṃ ca yāmaham
|
evaṃ vidhamahaṃ kuryāṃ niveśaṃ prāpnuyāṃ yadi
|
anyathā na kariṣye'haṃ satyametatpitāmahāḥ||9||

10tatra cotpatsyate janturbhavatāṃ tāraṇāya vai
|
śāśvatāścāvyayāścaiva tiṣṭhantu pitaro mama||10||

11sautiruvāca|
evamuktvā tu sa pitṝṃścacāra pṛthivī muniḥ
|
na ca sma labhate bhāryāṃ vṛddho'yamiti śānaka||11||

12yadā nirvedamāpannaḥ pitṛbhiścodistathā
|
tadā'raṇyaṃ sa gatvoccaiścukrośa bhṛśaduḥkhitaḥ||12||

13satvaraṇyagataḥ prājñaḥ pitṝṇāṃ hitakāmyayā
|
uvāca kanyāṃ yācāmi tisro vācaḥ śanairimāḥ||13||

14yāni bhūtāni santīha sthāvarāṇi carāṇi ca
|
antarhitāni vā yāni tāni śṛṇvantu me vacaḥ||14||

15ugre tapasi vartantaṃ pitaraścodayanti mām
|
niviśasveti duḥkhārtāḥ santānasya cikīrṣayā||15||

16niveśāyākhilāṃ bhūmiṃ kanyābhaikṣyaṃ carāmi bhoḥ
|
daridro duḥkhaśīlaśca pitṛbhiḥ sanniyojitaḥ||16||

17yasya kanyā'sti bhūtasya ye mayeha prakīrtitāḥ
|
te me kanyāṃ prayacchantu carataḥ sarvatodiśam||17||

18mama kanyā sanāmnīyā bhaikṣyavaccoditā bhavet
|
bhareyaṃ caiva yāṃ nāhaṃ tāṃ me kanyāṃ prayacchata||18||

19tataste pannagā ye vai jaratkārau samāhitāḥ
|
tāmādāya pravṛttiṃ te vāsukeḥ pratyavedayan||19||

20teṣāṃ śrutvā sa nāgendrastāṃ kanyāṃ samalaṅkṛtām
|
pragṛhyāraṇyamagamatsamīpaṃ tasya pannagaḥ||20||

21tatra tāṃ bhaikṣyavatkanyāṃ prādāttasmai mahātmane
|
nāgendro vāsukirbrahmanna sa tāṃ pratyagṛhṇata||21||

22asanāmeti vai matvā bharaṇe cāvicārite
|
mokṣabhāve sthitaścāpi mandībhūtaḥ parigrahe||22||

23tato nāma sa kanyāyāḥ papraccha bhṛgunandana
|
vāsukiṃ bharaṇaṃ cāsyā na kuryāmityuvāca ha||23||
