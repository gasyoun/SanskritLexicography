Mahābhārata 12.40 (Śāntiparvan)

1vaiśampāyana uvāca|
tata: kuntīsuto rājā gatamanyurgatajvaraḥ
|
kāñcane prāṅmukho hṛṣṭo nyaṣīdatparamāsane ||1||

2tamevābhimukhau pīṭhe pradīpte kāñcane śubhe
|
sātyakirvāsudevaśca niṣīdaturarindamau ||2||

3madhye kṛtvā tu rājānaṃ bhīmasenārjunāvubhau
|
niṣīdaturmahātmānau ślakṣṇayormaṇipīṭhayoḥ ||3||

4dānte siṃhāsane śubhre jāmbūnadavibhūṣite
|
pṛthā'pi sahadevena sahāste nakulena ca ||4||

5sudharmā viduro dhaumyo dhṛtarāṣṭraśca kauravaḥ
|
niṣedurjvalanākāreṣvāsaneṣu pṛthak pṛthak ||5||

6yuyutsuḥ sañjayaścaiva gāndhārī ca yaśasvinī
|
dhṛtarāṣṭro yato rājā tataḥ sarve samāviśan ||6||

7tatropaviṣṭo dharmātmā śvetāḥ sumanaso'spṛśat
|
svastikānakṣatānbhūmi suvarṇaṃ rajataṃ maṇim ||7||

8tataḥ prakṛtayaḥ sarvāḥ puraskṛtya purohitam
|
dadṛśurdharmarājānamādāya bahumaṅgalam ||8||

9pṛthivīṃ ca suvarṇaṃ ca ratnāni vividhāni ca
|
ābhiṣecanikaṃ bhāṇḍaṃ sarvasambhārasambhṛtam ||9||

10kāñcanaudumbarāstatra rājatāḥ pṛthivīmayāḥ
|
pūrṇakumbhāḥ sumanaso lājā barhīṣi gorasam ||10||

11śamīpippalapālāśasamidho madhusarpiṣī
|
sruva audumbaraḥ śaṅkhastathā hemavibhūṣitaḥ ||11||

12dāśārheṇābhyanujñātastatra dhaumyaḥ purohitaḥ
|
prāgudakpravaṇe vedīṃ lakṣaṇenopalikhya ca ||12||

13vyāghracarmottare śukle sarvatobhadra āsane
|
dṛḍhapādapratiṣṭhāne hutāśanasamatviṣi ||13||

14upaveśya mahātmānaṃ kṛṣṇāṃ ca drupadātmajām
|
juhāva pāvakaṃ dhīmānvidhimantrapuraskṛtam ||14||

15tata utthāya dāśārhaḥ śaṅkhamādāya pūjitam
|
abhyaṣiñcatpatiṃ pṛthvyāḥ kuntīputraṃ yudhiṣṭhiram ||15||

16dhṛtarāṣṭraśca rājarṣiḥ sarvāḥ prakṛtayastathā
|
anujñāto'tha kṛṣṇena bhrātṛbhiḥ saha pāṇḍavaḥ ||16||

17pāñcajanyābhiṣiktaśca rājā'mṛtamukho'bhavat
|
tato'nuvādayāmāsuḥ paṇavānakadundubhīn ||17||

18dharmarājo'pi tatsarvaṃ prati jagrāha dharmataḥ
|
pūjayāmāsa tāṃścāpi vidhivadbhūridakṣiṇaḥ ||18||

19tato niṣkasahasreṇa brāhmaṇānsvasti vācayan
|
vedādhyayanasampannāndhṛtiśīlasamanvitān ||19||

20te prītā brāhmaṇā rājansvastyūcurjayameva ca
|
haṃsā iva ca nardantaḥ praśaśaṃsuryudhiṣṭhiram ||20||

21yudhiṣṭhira mahābāho diṣṭyā jayasi pāṇḍava
|
diṣṭyā svadharmaṃ prāpto'si vikrameṇa mahādyute ||21||

22diṣṭyā gāṇḍīvadhanvā ca bhīmasenaśca pāṇḍavaḥ
|
tvaṃ cāpi kuśalī rājanmādrīputrau ca pāṇḍavau ||22||

23muktā vīrakṣayāttasmātsaṅgrāmādvijitadviṣaḥ
|
kṣipramuttarakāryāṇi kuru sarvāṇi bhārata ||23||

24tataḥ pratyarcitaḥ sadbhirdharmarājo yudhiṣṭhiraḥ
|
pratipede mahadrājyaṃ suhṛdbhiḥ saha bhārata ||24||
