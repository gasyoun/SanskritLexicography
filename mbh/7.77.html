Mahābhārata 7.77 (Droṇaparvan)

1sañjaya uvāca| tāṃ niśāṃ duḥkhaśokārtau niḥśvasantāvivoragau
|
nidrāṃ naivopalebhāte vāsudevadhanañjayau ||1||

2naranārāyaṇau kruddhau jñātvā devāḥ savāsavāḥ
|
vyathitāścintayāmāsuḥ kiṃsvidetadbhaviṣyati ||2||

3vavucca dāruṇā vātā rūkṣā ghorābhiśaṃsinaḥ
|
sakabandhastathā'ditye parighaḥ samadṛśyata ||3||

4śuṣkāśanyaśca niṣpetuḥ sanirghātāḥ savidyutaḥ
|
cacāla cāpi pṛthivī saśailavanakānanā ||4||

5cukṣubhuśca mahārāja sāgarā makarālayāḥ
|
pratissrotaḥ pravṛttāśca tathā gantuṃ samudragāḥ ||5||

6rathāśvanaranāgānāṃ pravṛttamadharottaram
|
kravyādānāṃ pramodārthaṃ yamarāṣṭravivṛddhaye ||6||

7vāhanāni śakṛnmūtre mumucū ruruduśca ha
|
tān dṛṣṭvā dāruṇān sarvānutpātā~llomaharṣaṇān ||7||

8sarve te vyathitāḥ sainyāstvadīyā bharatarṣabha
|
śrutvā mahābalasyogrāṃ pratijñāṃ savyasācinaḥ ||8||

9atha kṛṣṇaṃ mahābāhurabravīt pākaśāsaniḥ
|
āśvāsaya subhadrāṃ tvaṃ bhaginīṃ snuṣayā saha ||9||

10snuṣāṃ cāsyā vayasyāśca viśokāḥ kuru mādhava
|
sāmnā satyena yuktena vacasā'śvāsaya prabho ||10||

11tato'rjunagṛhaṃ gatvā vāsudevaḥ sudurmanāḥ
|
bhaginīṃ putraśokārtāmāśvāsayata duḥkhitām ||11||

12vāsudeva uvāca| mā śokaṃ kuru vārṣṇeyi kumāraṃ prati sasnuṣā
|
sarveṣāṃ prāṇināṃ bhīru niṣṭhaiṣā kālanirmitā ||12||

13kule jātasya dhīrasya kṣatriyasya viśeṣataḥ
|
sadṛśaṃ maraṇaṃ hyetat tava putrasya mā śucaḥ ||13||

14diṣṭyā mahāratho dhīraḥ pitustulyaparākramaḥ
|
kṣātreṇa vidhinā prāpto vīrābhilaṣitāṃ gatim ||14||

15jitvā subahuśaḥ śatrūn preṣayitvā ca mṛtyave
|
gataḥ puṇyakṛtāṃ lokān sarvakāmaduho'kṣayān ||15||

16tapasā brahmacaryeṇa śrutena prajñayā'pi ca
|
santo yāṃ gatimicchanti tāṃ prāptastava putrakaḥ ||16||

17vīrasūrvīrapatnī tvaṃ vīrajā vīrabāndhavā
|
mā śucastanayaṃ bhadre gataḥ sa paramāṃ gatim ||17||

18prāpsyate cāpyasau pāpaḥ saindhavo bālaghātakaḥ
|
asyāvalepasya phala sasuhṛdgaṇabāndhavaḥ ||18||

19vyuṣṭāyāṃ tu varārohe rajanyāṃ pāpakarmakṛt
|
na hi mokṣyati pārthāt sa praviṣṭo'pyamarāvatīm ||19||

20śvaḥ śiraḥ śroṣyase tasya saindhavasya raṇe hṛtam
|
samantapañcakādbāhyaṃ viśokā bhava mā rudaḥ ||20||

21kṣatradharmaṃ puraskṛtya gataḥ śūraḥ satāṃ gatim
|
yāṃ gatiṃ prāpnuyāmeha ye cānye śastrajīvinaḥ ||21||

22vyūḍhorasko mahābāhuranivartī rathapraṇut
|
gatastava varārohe putraḥ svargaṃ jvaraṃ jahi ||22||

23anuyātaśca pitaraṃ mātṛpakṣaṃ ca vīryavān
|
sahasraśo ripūn hatvā hataḥ śūro mahārathaḥ ||23||

24āśvāsayansnuṣāṃ rājñi mā śucaḥ kṣatriye bhūśam
|
śvaḥ priyaṃ sumahacchrutvā viśokā bhava nandini ||24||

25yat pārthena pratijñātaṃ tat tathā na tadanyathā
|
cikīrṣitaṃ hi te bharturna bhavejjātu niṣphalam ||25||

26yadi ca manujapannāgāḥ piśācā rajanicarāḥ patagāḥ surāsurāśca
|
raṇagatamabhiyānti sindhurājaṃ na sa bhavitā saha tairapi prabhāte ||26||
