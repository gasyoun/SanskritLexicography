Mahābhārata 1.213 (Ādiparvan)

1vaiśaṃpāyana uvāca|
evaṃ te samayaṃ kṛtvā nyavasaṃstatra pāṇḍavāḥ
|
vaśe śastrapratāpena kurvanto'nyānmahīkṣitaḥ||1||

2teṣāṃ manujasiṃhānāṃ pañcānāmamitaujasām
|
babhūva kṛṣṇā sarveṣāṃ pārthānāṃ vaśavartinī||2||

3te tayā taiśca sā vīraiḥ patibhiḥ saha pañcabhiḥ
|
babhūva paramaprītā nāgairiva sarasvatī||3||

4vartamāneṣu dharmeṇa pāṇḍaveṣu mahātmasu
|
vyavardhankuravaḥ sarve hīnadoṣāḥ sukhānvitāḥ||4||

5atha dīrgheṇa kālena brāhmaṇasya viśāṃpate
|
kasyacittaskarā jahruḥ kecidgā nṛpasattama||5||

6hriyamāṇe dhane tasminbrāhmaṇaḥ krodhamūrcchitaḥ
|
āgamya khāṇḍavaprasthamudakrośatsa pāṇḍavān||6||

7hriyate godhanaṃ kṣudrairnṛśaṃsairakṛtātmabhiḥ
|
prasahya cāsmadviṣayādabhyadhāvata pāṇḍavāḥ||7||

8brāhmaṇasya praśāntasya havirdhvāṅkṣaiḥ pralupyate
|
śārdūlasya guhāṃ śūnyāṃ nīcaḥ kroṣṭābhimardati||8||

9arakṣitāraṃ rājānaṃ baliṣaḍbhāgahāriṇam
|
tamāhuḥ sarvalokasya samagraṃ pāpacāriṇam||9||

10brāhmaṇasve hṛte corairdharmārthe ca vilopite
|
rorūyamāṇe ca mayi kriyatāṃ hastadhāraṇā||10||

11vaiśaṃpāyana uvāca|
rorūyamāṇasyābhyāśe bhṛśaṃ viprasya pāṇḍavaḥ
|
tāni vākyāni śuśrāva kuntīputro dhanañjayaḥ||11||

12śrutvaiva ca mahābāhurmā bhairityāha taṃ dvijam
|
āyudhāni ca yatrāsanpāṇḍavānāṃ mahātmanām||12||

13kṛṣṇayā saha tatrāste dharmarājo yudhiṣṭhiraḥ
|
saṃpraveśāya cāśakto gamanāya ca pāṇḍavaḥ||13||

14tasya cārtasya tairvākyaiścodyamānaḥ punaḥpunaḥ
|
ākrande tatra kaunteyaścintayāmāsa duḥkhitaḥ||14||

15hriyamāṇe dhane tasminbrāhmaṇasya tapasvinaḥ
|
aśrupramārjanaṃ tasya kartavyamiti niścayaḥ||15||

16upakṣepaṇajo'dharmaḥ sumahānsyānmahīpateḥ
|
yadyasya rudato dvāri na karomyadya rakṣaṇam||16||

17anāstikyaṃ ca sarveṣāmasmākamapi rakṣaṇe
|
pratitiṣṭheta loke'sminnadharmaścaiva no bhavet||17||

18anādṛtya tu rājānaṃ gate mayi na saṃśayaḥ
|
ajātaśatrornṛpatermayi caivānṛtaṃ bhavet||18||

19anupraveśe rājñastu vanavāso bhavenmama
|
sarvamanyatparihṛtaṃ dharṣaṇāttu mahīpateḥ||19||

20adharmo vai mahānastu vane vā maraṇaṃ mama
|
śarīrasya vināśena dharma eva viśiṣyate||20||

21evaṃ viniścitya tataḥ kuntīputro dhanañjayaḥ
|
anupraviśya rājānamāpṛcchya ca viśāṃpate||21||

22dhanurādāya saṃhṛṣṭo brāhmaṇaṃ pratyabhāṣata
|
brāhmaṇāgamyatāṃ śīghraṃ yāvatparadhanairṣiṇaḥ||22||

23na dūre te gatāḥ kṣudrāstāvadgacchāvahe saha
|
yāvannivartayāmyadya caurahastāddhanaṃ tava||23||

24so'nusṛtya mahābāhurdhanvī varmī rathī dhvajī
|
śarairvidhvasya tāṃścorānavajitya ca taddhanam||24||

25brāhmaṇaṃ samupākṛtya yaśaḥ prāpya ca pāṇḍavaḥ
|
tatastadgedhanaṃ pārtho dattvā tasmai dvijātaye||25||

26ājagāma puraṃ vīraḥ savyasācī dhanañjayaḥ
|
so'bhivādya gurūnsarvānsarvaiścāpyabhinanditaḥ||26||

27dharmarājamuvācedaṃ vratamādiśa me prabho
|
samayaḥ samatikrānto bhavatsaṃdarśane mayā||27||

28vanavāsaṃ gamiṣyāmi samayo hyeṣa naḥ kṛtaḥ
|
ityukto dharmarājastu sahasā vākyamapriyam||28||

29kathamityabravīdvācā śokārtaḥ sajjamānayā
|
yudhiṣṭhiro guḍākeśaṃ bhrātā bhrātaramacyutam||29||

30uvāca dīno rājā ca dhanañjayamidaṃ vacaḥ
|
pramāṇamasmi yadi te mattaḥ śṛṇu vaco'nagha||30||

31anupraveśe yadvīra kṛtavāṃstvaṃ mamāpriyam
|
sarvaṃ tadanujānāmi vyalīkaṃ na ca me hṛdi||31||

32guroranupraveśo hi nopaghāto yavīyasaḥ
|
yavīyaso'nupraveśo jyeṣṭhasya vidhilopakaḥ||32||

33nivartasva mahābāho kuruṣva vacanaṃ mama
|
na hi te dharmalopo'sti na ca te dharpaṇā kṛtā||33||

34arjuna uvāca|
na vyājena careddharmamiti me bhavataḥ śrutam
|
na satyādvicaliṣyāmi satyenāyudhamālabhe||34||

35vaiśaṃpāyana uvāca|
so'bhyanujñāya rājānaṃ vanacaryāya dīkṣitaḥ
|
vane dvādaśa varṣāṇi vāsāyānujagāma ha||35||
