Mahābhārata 12.58 (Śāntiparvan)

1bhīṣma uvāca|
etat te rājadharmāṇāṃ navanītaṃ yudhiṣṭhira
|
bṛhaspatirhi bhagavānnyāyyaṃ dharmaṃ praśaṃsati||1||

2viśālākṣaśca bhagavānkāvyaścaiva mahātapāḥ
|
sahasrākṣo mahendraśca tathā prācetaso manuḥ||2||

3bharadvājaśca bhagavāṃstathā gauraśirā muniḥ
|
rājaśāstrapraṇetāro brahmaṇyā brahmavādinaḥ||3||

4rakṣāmeva praśaṃsanti dharmaṃ dharmabhṛtāṃ vara
|
rājñāṃ rājīvatāmrākṣa sādhanaṃ cātra me śṛṇu||4||

5cāraśca praṇidhiścaiva kāle dānamamatsarāt
|
yuktyā dānaṃ na cādānamayogena yudhiṣṭhira||5||

6satāṃ saṃgrahaṇaṃ śauryaṃ dākṣyaṃ satyaṃ prajāhitam
|
anārjavairārjavaiśca śatrupakṣasya bhedanam||6||

7ketanānāṃ ca jīrṇānāmavekṣā caiva sīdatām
|
dvividhasya ca daṇḍasya prayogaḥ kālacoditaḥ||7||

8sādhūnāmaparityāgaḥ kulīnānāṃ ca dhāraṇam
|
nicayaśca niceyānāṃ sevā buddhimatāmapi||8||

9balānāṃ harṣaṇaṃ nityaṃ prajānāmanvavekṣaṇam
|
kāryeṣvakhedaḥ kośasya tathaiva ca vivardhanam||9||

10puraguptiraviśvāsaḥ paurasaṃghātabhedanam
|
arimadhyasthamitrāṇāṃ yathāvaccānvavekṣaṇam||10||

11upajāpaśca bhṛtyānāmātmanaḥ puradarśanam
|
aviśvāsaḥ svayaṃ caiva parasyāśvāsanaṃ tathā||11||

12nītidharmānusaraṇaṃ nityamutthānameva ca
|
ripūṇāmanavajñānaṃ nityaṃ cānāryavarjanam||12||

13utthānaṃ hi narendrāṇāṃ bṛhaspatirabhāṣata
|
rājadharmasya tanmūlaṃ ślokāṃścātra nibodha me||13||

14utthānenāmṛtaṃ labdhamutthānenāsurā hatāḥ
|
utthānena mahandreṇa śraiṣṭhyaṃ prāptaṃ divīha ca||14||

15utthānavīraḥ puruṣo vāgvīrānadhitiṣṭhati
|
utthānavīrānvāgvīrā ramayanta upāsate||15||

16utthānahīno rājā hi buddhimānapi nityaśaḥ
|
pradharṣaṇīyaḥ śatrūṇāṃ bhujaṅga iva nirviṣaḥ||16||

17na ca śatruravajñeyo durbalo'pi balīyasā
|
alpo'pi hi dahatyagnirviṣamalpaṃ hinasti ca||17||

18ekāṅgenāpi sambhūtaḥ śatrurdurgamupāśritaḥ
|
sarvaṃ tāpayate deśamapi rājñaḥ samṛddhinaḥ||18||

19rājño rahasyaṃ tadvākyaṃ yathārthaṃ lokasaṃgrahaḥ
|
hṛdi yaccāsya jihyaṃ syātkāraṇena ca yadbhavet||19||

20yaccāsya kāryaṃ vṛjinamārjavenaiva nirjayet
|
dambhanārthaṃ ca lokasya dharmiṣṭhāmācaretkriyām||20||

21rājyaṃ hi sumahattantraṃ dhāryate nākṛtātmabhiḥ
|
na śakyaṃ mṛdunā voḍhumāyāsasthānamuttamam||21||

22rājyaṃ sarvāmiṣaṃ nityamārjaveneha dhāryate
|
tasmānmiśreṇa satataṃ vartitavyaṃ yudhiṣṭhira||22||

23yadyapyasya vipattiḥ syādrakṣamāṇasya vai prajāḥ
|
so'pyasya vipulo dharma evaṃvṛttā hi bhūmipāḥ||23||

24eṣa te rājadharmāṇāṃ leśaḥ samanuvarṇitaḥ
|
bhūyaste yatra saṃdehastadbrūhi kurusattama||24||

25vaiśampāyana uvāca|
tato vyāsaśca bhagavāndevasthāno'śma eva ca
|
vāsudevaḥ kṛpaścaiva sātyakiḥ saṃjayastathā||25||

26sādhu sādhviti saṃhṛṣṭāḥ puṣṣyamāṇairivānanaiḥ
|
astuvaṃśca naravyāghraṃ bhīṣmaṃ dharmabhṛtāṃ varam||26||

27tato dīnamanā bhīṣmamuvāca kurusattamaḥ
|
netrābhyāmaśrupūrṇābhyāṃ pādau tasya śanaiḥ spṛśan||27||

28śva idānīṃ svasandehaṃ prakṣyāmi tvāṃ pitāmaha
|
upaiti savitā hyastaṃ rasamāpīya pārthivam||28||

29tato dvijātīnabhivādya keśavaḥ kṛpaśca te caiva yudhiṣṭhirādayaḥ
|
pradakṣiṇīkṛtya mahānadīsutaṃ tato rathānāruruhurmudānvitāḥ||29||

30dṛṣadvatīṃ cāpyavagāhya suvratāḥ kṛtodakārthāḥ kṛtajaṣyamaṅgalāḥ
|
upāsya saṃdhyāṃ vidhivatparaṃtapāstataḥ puraṃ te viviśurgajāhvayam||30||
