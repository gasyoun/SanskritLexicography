Mahābhārata 7.127 (Droṇaparvan)

1bhīmasena uvāca|
brahmeśānendravaruṇānavahadyaḥ purā rathaḥ
|
tamāsthāya gatau kṛṣṇau na tayorvidyate bhayam ||1||

2āśāṃ tu śirasā bibhradeṣa gacchāmi mā śucaḥ
|
sametya tānnaravyāghrāṃstava dāsyāmi saṃvidam ||2||

3sañjaya uvāca|
etāvaduktvā prayayau paridāya yudhiṣṭhiram
|
dhṛṣṭadyumnāya balavān sṛhṛdbhyaśca punaḥ punaḥ ||3||

4dhṛṣṭadyumnaṃ cedamāha bhīmaseno mahābalaḥ
|
viditaṃ te mahābāho yathā droṇo mahārathaḥ ||4||

5grahaṇe dharmarājasya sarvopāyena vartate
|
na ca me gamane kṛtyaṃ tādṛk pārṣata vidyate ||5||

6yādṛśaṃ rakṣaṇe rājñaḥ kāryamātyayikaṃ hi naḥ
|
evamukto'smi pārthena prativaktuṃ na cotsahe ||6||

7prayāsye tatra yatrāsau mumūrṣuḥ saindhavaḥ sthitaḥ
|
dharmarājasya vacane sthātavyamaviśaṃkayā ||7||

8yāsyāmi padavīṃ bhrātuḥ sātvatasya ca dhīmataḥ
|
so'dya yatto raṇe pārthaṃ parirakṣa yudhiṣṭhiram ||8||

9etaddhi sarvakāryāṇāṃ paramaṃ kṛtyamāhave
|
tamabravīnmahārāja dhṛṣṭadyumno vṛkodaram ||9||

10īpsitaṃ te kariṣyāmi gaccha pārthovicārayan
|
nāhatvā samare droṇo dhṛṣṭadyumnaṃ kathañcana ||10||

11nigrahaṃ dharmarājasya prakariṣyati saṃyuge
|
tato nikṣipya rājānaṃ dhṛṣṭadyumne ca pāṃḍavam ||11||

12abhivādya guruṃ jyeṣṭhaṃ prayayau yena phālgunaḥ
|
pariṣvaktaśca kaunteyo dharmarājena bhārata ||12||

13āghrātaśca tathā mūrdhni śrāvitaścāśiṣaḥ śubhāḥ
|
kṛtvā pradakṣiṇānviprānarcitāṃstuṣṭamānasān ||13||

14ālabhya maṃgalānyaṣṭau pītvā kairātakaṃ madhu
|
dviguṇadraviṇo vīro madaraktāntalocanaḥ ||14||

15vipraiḥ kṛtasvastyayano vijayotpādasūcitaḥ
|
paśyannevātmano buddhiṃ vijayānanda‌kāriṇīm ||15||

16anulomānilaiścāśu pradarśitajayodayaḥ
|
bhīmaseno mahābāhuḥ kavacī śubhakuṇḍalī ||16||

17sāṅgadī satalatrāṇaḥ sarathī rathināṃ varaḥ
|
tasya kārṣṇāyasaṃ varma hemacitraṃ mahardhimat ||17||

18vibabhau sarvataḥ śliṣṭaṃ savidyudiva toyadaḥ
|
pītaraktāsitasitairvāsobhiśca suvoṣṭitaḥ ||18||

19kaṇṭhatrāṇena ca babhau sendrāyudha ivāṃbudaḥ
|
prayāte bhīmasene tu tava sainyaṃ yuyutsayā ||19||

20pāñcajanyaravo ghoraḥ punarāsīdviśāṃpate
|
taṃ śrutvā ninadaṃ ghoraṃ trailokyatrāsanaṃ mahat ||20||

21punarbhīmaṃ mahābāhuṃ dharmaputro'bhyabhāṣata
|
eṣa vṛṣṇipravīreṇa dhmātaḥ salilajo bhṛśam ||21||

22pṛthivīṃ cāntarikṣaṃ ca vinādayati śaṃkharāṭ
|
nūnaṃ vyasanamāpanne sumahatsavyasācini ||22||

23kurubhiryudhyate sārdhaṃ sarvaiścakragadādharaḥ
|
āha kuntī nūnamāryā pāpamadya nidarśanam ||23||

24draupadī ca subhadrā ca paśyantyau saha bandhubhiḥ
|
sa bhīma tvarayā yukto yāhi yatra dhanañjayaḥ ||24||

25muhyantīva hi me sarvā dhanañjayadidṛkṣayā
|
diśaśca pradiśaḥ pārtha sātvatasya ca kāraṇāt ||25||

26gaccha gaccheti guruṇā so'nujñāto vṛkodaraḥ
|
tataḥ pāṇḍusuto rājan bhīmasenaḥ pratāpavān ||26||

27baddhagodhāṃgulitrāṇaḥ pragṛhītaśarāsanaḥ
|
jyeṣṭhena prahito bhrātrā bhrātā bhrātuḥ priyaṃkaraḥ ||27||

28āhatya dundubhiṃ bhīmaḥ śaṃkhaṃ pradhmāpya cāsakṛt
|
vinadya siṃhanādena jyāṃ vikarṣan punaḥ punaḥ ||28||

29tena śabdena vīrāṇāṃ pātayitvā manāṃsyuta
|
darśayan ghoramātmānamamitrān saha‌sā'bhyayāt ||29||

30tamūhurjavanā dāntā viruvanto hayottamāḥ
|
viśokenābhisaṃpannā manomārutaraṃhasaḥ ||30||

31ārujanvirujan pārtho jyāṃ vikarṣaṃśca pāṇinā
|
saṃprakarṣan vikarṣaṃśca senāgraṃ samaloḍayat ||31||

32taṃ prayāntaṃ mahābāhuṃ pañcālāḥ sahasomakāḥ
|
pṛṣṭhato'nuyayuḥ śūrā maghavantamivāmarāḥ ||32||

33taṃ sametya mahārāja tāvakāḥ paryavārayan
|
duḥśalaścitrasenaśca kuṇḍabhedī viviṃśatiḥ ||33||

34durmukho duḥsahaścaiva vikarṇaśca śalastathā
|
vindānuvindau sumukhau dīrghabāhuḥ sudarśanaḥ ||34||

35vṛndārakaḥ suhastaśca suṣeṇo dīrghalocanaḥ
|
abhayo raudrakarmā ca suvarmā durvimocanaḥ ||35||

36śobhanto rathināṃ śreṣṭhāḥ sahasainyapadānugāḥ
|
saṃyattāḥ samare vīrā bhīmasenamupādravan ||36||

37taiḥ samantādvṛtaḥ śūraiḥ samareṣu mahārathaḥ
|
tān samīkṣya tu kaunteyo bhīmasenaḥ parākramī
|
abhyavartata vegena siṃhaḥ kṣudramṛgāniva ||37||

38te mahāstrāṇi divyāni tatra vīrā adarśayan
|
chādayantaḥ śarairbhīmaṃ meghāḥ sūryamivoditam ||38||

39sa tānatītya vegena droṇānīkamupādravat
|
agrataśca gajānīkaṃ śaravarṣairavākirat ||39||

40so'cireṇaiva kālena tadgajānīkamāśugaiḥ
|
diśaḥ sarvāḥ samabhyasya vyadhamat pavanātmajaḥ ||40||

41trāsitāḥ śarabhasyeva garjitena vane mṛgāḥ
|
prādravan dviradāḥ sarve nadanto bhairavān ravān ||41||

42punaścātīva vegena droṇānīkamupādravat
|
tamavārayadācāryo velodvṛttamivārṇavam ||42||

43lalāṭe'tāḍayaccainaṃ nārācena smayanniva
|
ūrdhvaraśmirivādityo vibabhau tena pāṇḍavaḥ ||43||

44sa manyamānastvācāryo mamāyaṃ phālguno yathā
|
bhīmaḥ kariṣyate pūjāmityuvāca vṛkodaram ||44||

45bhīmasena na te śakyā praveṣṭumarivāhinī
|
māmanirjitya samare śatrumaya mahābala ||45||

46yadi te so'nujaḥ kṛṣṇaḥ praviṣṭo'numate mama
|
anīkaṃ na tu śakyaṃ me praveṣṭumiha vai tvayā ||46||

47atha bhīmastu tacchrutvā gurorvākyamapetabhīḥ
|
kruddhaḥ provāca vai droṇaṃ raktatāmrekṣaṇastvaran ||47||

48tavārjuno nānumate brahmabandho raṇājiram
|
praviṣṭaḥ sa hi durdharṣaḥ śakrasyāpi viśedbalam ||48||

49tena vai paramāṃ pūjāṃ kurvatā mānito hyasi
|
nārjuno'haṃ ghṛṇī droṇa bhīmaseno'smi te ripuḥ ||49||

50pitā nastvaṃ gururbandhustathā putrāstu te vayam
|
iti manyāmahe sarve bhavantaṃ praṇatāḥ sthitāḥ ||50||

51adya tadviparītaṃ te vadato'smāsu dṛśyate
|
yadi tvaṃ śatrumātmānaṃ manyase tattathāstviha ||51||

52eṣa te sadṛśaṃ śatroḥ karma bhīmaḥ karomyaham
|
athodbhrāmya gadāṃ bhīmaḥ kāladaṃḍamivāṃtakaḥ ||52||

53droṇāya vyasṛjadrājan sa rathādavapupluve
|
sāśvasūtadhvajaṃ yānaṃ droṇasyāpothayattadā ||53||

54prāmudūnācca bahūnyodhān vāyurvṛkṣānivaujasā
|
taṃ punaḥ parivavruste tava putrā rathottamam ||54||

55anyaṃ tu rathamāsthāya droṇaḥ praharatāṃ varaḥ
|
vyūhadvāraṃ samāsādya yuddhāya samupasthitaḥ ||55||

56tataḥ kruddho mahārāja bhīmasenaḥ parākramī
|
agrataḥ syandanānīkaṃ śaravarṣairavākirat ||56||

57te vadhyamānāḥ samare tava putrā mahārathāḥ
|
bhīmaṃ bhīmabalā yuddhe yodhayanti jayaiṣiṇaḥ ||57||

58tato duḥśāsanaḥ kruddho rathaśaktiṃ samākṣipat
|
sarvapārasavīṃ tīkṣṇāṃ jighāṃsuḥ pāṇḍunandanam ||58||

59āpatantīṃ mahāśaktiṃ tava putrapraṇoditām
|
dvidhā ciccheda tāṃ bhīmastada‌dbhutamivābhavat ||59||

60athānyairviśikhaistīkṣṇaiḥ saṃkruddhaḥ kuṇḍabhedinam
|
suṣeṇaṃ dīrdhanetraṃ ca tribhistrīnabadhīdbalī ||60||

61tato vṛndārakaṃ vīraṃ kurūṇāṃ kīrtivardhanam
|
putrāṇāṃ tava vīrāṇāṃ yudhyatāmavadhīt punaḥ ||61||

62abhayaṃ raudrakarmāṇaṃ durvimocanameva ca
|
tribhistrīnavadhīdbhīmaḥ punareva sutāṃstava ||62||

63vadhyamānā mahārāja putrāstava balīyasā
|
bhīmaṃ praharatāṃ śreṣṭhaṃ samantāt paryavārayan ||63||

64te śarairbhīmakarmāṇaṃ vavarṣuḥ pāṇḍavaṃ yudhi
|
meghā ivātapāpāye dhārābhirdharaṇīdharam ||64||

65sa tadbāṇamayaṃ varṣamaśmavarṣamivācalaḥ
|
pratīcchan pāṇḍudāyādo na prāvyathata śatruhā ||65||

66vindānuvindau sahitau suvarmāṇaṃ ca te sutam
|
prahasanneva kaunteyaḥ śarairninye yamakṣayam ||66||

67tataḥ sudarśanaṃ vīraṃ putraṃ te bharatarṣabha
|
vivyādha samare tūrṇaṃ sa papāta mamāra ca ||67||

68so'cireṇaiva kālena tadrathānīkamāśugaiḥ
|
diśaḥ sarvāḥ samālokya vyadhamat pāṇḍunaṃdanaḥ ||68||

69tato vai rathaghoṣeṇa garjitena mṛgā iva
|
bhajyamānāśca samare tava putrā viśāṃpate ||69||

70prādravan sahasā sarve bhīmasenabhayārditāḥ
|
anuyāyācca kaunteyaḥ putrāṇāṃ te mahadbalam ||70||

71vivyādha samare rājan kauraveyān samantataḥ
|
vadhyamānā mahārāja bhīmasenena tāvakāḥ ||71||

72tyaktvā bhīmaṃ raṇājjagmuścodayanto hayottamān
|
tāṃstu nirjitya samare bhīmaseno mahābalaḥ ||72||

73siṃhanādaravaṃ cakre bāhuśabdaṃ ca pāṇḍavaḥ
|
talaśabdaṃ ca sumahat kṛtvā bhīmo mahābalaḥ ||73||

74bhīṣayitvā rathānīkaṃ hatvā yodhān varān varān
|
vyatītya rathinaścāpi droṇānīkamupādravat ||74||
