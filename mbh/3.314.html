Mahābhārata 3.314 (Āraṇyakaparvan)

1vaiśampāyana uvāca|
tataste yakṣavacanādudatiṣṭhanta pāṇḍavāḥ
|
kṣutpipāse ca sarveṣāṃ kṣaṇena vyapagacchatām||1||

2yudhiṣṭhira uvāca|
sarasyekena pādena tiṣṭhantamaparājitam
|
pṛcchāmi ko bhavān devo na me yakṣo mato bhavān||2||

3vasūnāṃ vā bhavāneko rudrāṇāmathavā bhavān
|
athavā marutāṃ śreṣṭho vajrī vā tridaśeśvaraḥ||3||

4mama hi bhrātara ime sahasraśatayodhinaḥ
|
taṃ yodhaṃ na prapaśyāmi yena sarve nipātitāḥ||4||

5sukhaṃ pratiprabuddhānāmindriyāṇyupalakṣaye
|
sa bhavān suhṛdo'smākamathavā naḥ pitā bhavān||5||

6yakṣa uvāca|
ahaṃ te janakastāta dharmo mṛduparākrama
|
tvāṃ didṛkṣuranuprāpto viddhi māṃ bharatarṣabha||6||

7yaśaḥ satyaṃ damaḥ śaucamārjavaṃ hrīracāpalam
|
dānaṃ tapo brahmacaryamityetāstanavo mama||7||

8ahiṃsā samatā śāntistapaḥ śaucamamatsaraḥ
|
dvārāṇyetāni me viddhi priyo hyasi sadā mama||8||

9diṣṭyā pañcasu rakto'si viṣṭyā te ṣaṭpadī jitāḥ
|
dve pūrve madhyame dve ca dve cānte sāmparāyike||9||

10dharmo'hamiti bhadraṃ te jijñāsustvāmihāgataḥ
|
ānṛśaṃsyena tuṣṭo'smi varaṃ dāsyāmi te'nagha||10||

11varaṃ vṛrṇīṣva rājendra dātā hyasmi tavānagha
|
ye hi me puruṣā bhaktā na teṣāmāsti durgatiḥ||11||

12yudhiṣṭhira uvāca|
araṇīsahitaṃ yasya mṛgo hyādāya gacchati
|
tasyāgnayo na lupyeran prathamo'stu varo mama||12||

13yakṣa uvāca|
araṇīsahitaṃ hyasya brāhmaṇasya hṛtaṃ mayā
|
mṛgaveṣeṇa kaunteya jijñāsārthaṃ tava prabho||13||

14vaiśampāyana uvāca|
dadānītyeva bhagavānuttaraṃ pratyapadyata
|
anyaṃ varaya bhadraṃ te varaṃ tvamamaropama||14||

15yudhiṣṭhira uvāca|
varṣāṇi dvādaśāraṇye trayodaśamupasthitam
|
tatra no nābhijānīyurvasato manujāḥ kvacit||15||

16vaiśampāyana uvāca|
dadānītyeva bhagavānuttaraṃ pratyapadyata
|
bhūyaścāśvāsayāmāsa kaunteyaṃ satyavikramam||16||

17yadyapi svena rūpeṇa cariṣyatha mahīmimām
|
na vo vijñāsyate kaścittriṣu lokeṣu bhārata||17||

18varṣaṃ trayodaśamidaṃ matprasādāt kurūdvahāḥ
|
virāṭanagare gūḍhā avijñātāścariṣyatha||18||

19yadvaḥ saṅkalpitaṃ rūpaṃ manasā yasya yādṛśam
|
tādṛśaṃ tādṛśaṃ sarve chandato dhārayiṣyatha||19||

20araṇīsahitaṃ cedaṃ brāhmaṇāya prayacchata
|
jijñāsārthaṃ mayā hyetadāhṛtaṃ mṛgarūpiṇā||20||

21pravṛṇīṣvāparaṃ saumya varamiṣṭaṃ dadāni te
|
na tṛpyāmi naraśreṣṭha prayacchan vai varāṃstathā||21||

22tṛtīyaṃ gṛhyatāṃ putra caramapratimaṃ mahat
|
tvaṃ hi matprabhavo rājan viduraśca mamāṃśajaḥ||22||

23yudhiṣṭhira uvāca|
devadevo mayā dṛṣṭo bhavān sākṣāt sanātanaḥ
|
yaṃ dadāsi varaṃ tuṣṭastaṃ grahīṣyāmyahaṃ pitaḥ||23||

24jayeyaṃ lobhamohau ca krodhaṃ cāhaṃ sadā vibho
|
dāne tapasi satye ca mano me satataṃ bhavet||24||

25dharma uvāca|
upapanno guṇairetaiḥ svabhāvenāsi pāṇḍava
|
bhavān dharmaḥ punaścaiva yathoktaṃ te bhaviṣyati||25||

26vaiśampāyana uvāca|
ityuktvā'ntardadhe dharmo bhagavā~llokabhāvanaḥ
|
sametāḥ pāṇḍavāścaiva sukhasuptā manasvinaḥ||26||

27upetya cāśramaṃ vīrāḥ sarva eva gataklamāḥ
|
āraṇeyaṃ dadustasmai brāhmaṇāya tapasvine||27||

28idaṃ samutthānasamāgataṃ mahat pituśca putrasya ca kīrtivardhanam
|
paṭhannaraḥ syādvijitendriyo vaśī sa putrapautraḥ śatavarṣabhāgbhavet||28||

29na cāpyadharme na suhṛdvibhedane parasvahāre paradāramarśane
|
kadaryabhāve na ramenmanaḥ sadā nṛṇāṃ sadākhyānamidaṃ vijānatām||29||
