Mahābhārata 8.3 (Karṇaparvan)

1sañjaya uvāca|
hate droṇe maheṣvāse tava putrā mahārathāḥ
|
babhūvurasvasthamukhā viṣaṇṇā gatacetasaḥ ||1||

2avāṅmukhāḥ śastrabhṛtaḥ sarva eva viśāṃpate
|
avekṣamāṇāḥ śokārtā nābhyabhāṣan parasparam ||2||

3tān dṛṣṭvā vyathitākārān sainyāni tava bhārata
|
ūrdhvameva niraikṣanta duḥkhatrastānyanekaśaḥ ||3||

4śastrāṇyeṣāṃ tu rājendra śoṇitāktāni sarvaśaḥ
|
prābhraśyanta karāgrebhyo dṛṣṭvā droṇaṃ hataṃ yudhi ||4||

5tāni baddhānyariṣṭāni lambamānāni bhārata
|
adṛśyanta mahārāja nakṣatrāṇi yathā divi ||5||

6tathā tu stimitaṃ dṛṣṭvā gatasattvamavasthitam
|
balaṃ tava mahārāja rājā duryodhano'bravīt ||6||

7bhavatāṃ bāhuvīryaṃ hi samāśritya mayā yudhi
|
pāṇḍaveyāḥ samāhūtā yuddhaṃ cedaṃ pravartitam ||7||

8tadidaṃ nihate droṇe viṣaṇṇamiva lakṣyate
|
yudhyamānāśca samare yodhā vadhyanti sarvaśaḥ ||8||

9jayo vāpi vadho vāpi yudhyamānasya saṃyuge
|
bhavetkimatra citraṃ vai yudhyadhvaṃ sarvatomukhāḥ ||9||

10paśyadhvaṃ ca mahātmānaṃ karṇaṃ vaikartanaṃ yudhi
|
pracarantaṃ maheṣvāsaṃ divyairastrairmahābalam ||10||

11yasya vai yudhi saṃtrāsāt kuntīputro dhanañjayaḥ
|
nivartate sadā mandaḥ siṃhāt kṣudramṛgo yathā ||11||

12yena nāgāyutaprāṇo bhīmaseno mahābalaḥ
|
mānuṣeṇaiva yuddhena tāmavasthāṃ praveśitaḥ ||12||

13yena divyāstravicchūro māyāvī sa ghaṭotkacaḥ
|
amoghayā raṇe śaktyā nihato bhairavaṃ nadan ||13||

14tasya durvāravīryasya satyasandhasya dhīmataḥ
|
bāhvordraviṇamakṣayyamadya drakṣyatha saṃyuge ||14||

15droṇaputrasya vikrāntaṃ rādheyasyaiva cobhayoḥ
|
paśyantu pāṇḍuputrāste viṣṇuvāsavayoriva ||15||

16sarva eva bhavantaśca śaktāḥ pratyekaśo'pi vā
|
pāṇḍuputrānraṇe hantuṃ sasainyān kimu saṃhatāḥ
|
vīryavantaḥ kṛtāstrāśca drakṣyathādya parasparam ||16||

17sañjaya uvāca|
evamuktvā tataḥ karṇaṃ cakre senāpatiṃ tadā
|
tava putro mahāvīryo bhrātṛbhiḥ sahito'nagha ||17||

18saināpatyamathāvāpya karṇo rājanmahārathaḥ
|
siṃhanādaṃ vinadyoccaiḥ prāyudhyata raṇotkaṭaḥ ||18||

19sa sṛñjayānāṃ sarveṣāṃ pañcālānāṃ ca māriṣa
|
kekayānāṃ videhānāṃ cakāra kadanaṃ mahat ||19||

20tasyeṣudhārāḥ śataśaḥ prādurāsañcharāsanāt
|
agre puṃkheṣu saṃsaktā yathā bhramarapaṅktayaḥ ||20||

21sa pīḍayitvā pañcālān pāṇḍavāṃśca tarasvinaḥ
|
hatvā sahasraśo yodhān arjunena nipātitaḥ ||21||
