Mahābhārata 7.195 (Droṇaparvan)

1sañjaya uvāca|
chadmanā nihataṃ śrutvā pitaraṃ pāpakarmaṇā
|
bāṣpeṇāpūryata drauṇī roṣeṇa ca nararṣabha||1||

2tasya kruddhasya rājendra vapurdīptamadṛśyata
|
antakasyeva bhūtāni jihīrṣoḥ kālaparyaye||2||

3aśrupūrṇe tato netre vyapamṛjya punaḥ punaḥ
|
uvāca kopānniḥśvasya duryodhanamidaṃ vacaḥ||3||

4pitā mama yathā kṣudrairnyastaśastro niyātitaḥ
|
dharmadhvajavatā pāpaṃ kṛtaṃ tadviditaṃ mama||4||

5anāryaṃ sunṛśaṃsaṃ ca dharmaputrasya me śrutam
|
yuddheṣvapi pravṛttānāṃ dhruvaṃ jayaparājayau||5||

6dvayametadbhavedrājan vadhastatra praśasyate
|
nyāyavṛtto vadho yastu saṃgrāme yudhyato bhavet||6||

7na sa duḥkhāya bhavati tathā dṛṣṭo hi sa dvijaiḥ
|
gataḥ sa vīralokāya pitā mama na saṃśayaḥ||7||

8na śocyaḥ puruṣavyāghra yastadā nidhanaṃ gataḥ
|
yattu dharmapravṛttaḥ sankeśagrahaṇamāptavān||8||

9paśyatāṃ sarvasainyānāṃ tanme marmāṇi kṛntati
|
mayi jīvati yattātaḥ keśagrahamavāptavān||9||

10kathamanye kariṣyanti putrebhyaḥ putriṇaḥ spṛhām
|
kāmātkrodhādavijñānāddharṣādbālyena vā punaḥ||10||

11vidharmakāṇi kurvanti tathā paribhavanti ca
|
tadidaṃ pārṣateneha mahadādharmikaṃ kṛtam||11||

12avajñāya ca māṃ nūnaṃ nṛśaṃsena durātmanā
|
tasyānubandhaṃ draṣṭā'sau dhṛṣṭadyumnaḥ sudāruṇam||12||

13akāryaṃ paramaṃ kṛtvā mithyāvādī ca pāṇḍavaḥ
|
yo hyasau chadmanā''cāryaṃ śastraṃ saṃnyāsayattadā||13||

14tasyādya dharmarājasya bhūmiḥ pāsyati śoṇitam
|
śape satyena kauravya iṣṭāpūrtena caiva ha||14||

15ahatvā sarvapañcālān jīveyaṃ na kathañcana
|
sarvopāyairyatiṣyāmi pañcālānāmahaṃ vadhe||15||

16dhṛṣṭadyumnaṃ ca samare hantā'haṃ pāpakāriṇam
|
karmaṇā yena teneha mṛdunā dāruṇena ca||16||

17pañcālānāṃ vadhaṃ kṛtvā śāntiṃ labdhāsmi kaurava
|
yadarthaṃ puruṣavyāghra putrānicchanti mānavāḥ||17||

18pretya ceha ca saṃprāptāstrāyante mahato bhayāt
|
pitrā tu mama sā'vasthā prāptā nirbandhunā yathā||18||

19mayi śailapratīkāśe putre śiṣye ca jīvati
|
dhiṅmamāstrāṇi divyāni dhigbāhū dhik parākramam||19||

20yaṃ sma droṇaḥ sutaṃ prāpya keśagrahamavāptavān
|
sa tathā'haṃ kariṣyāmi yathā bharatasattama||20||

21paralokagatasyāpi bhaviṣyāmyanṛṇaḥ pituḥ
|
āryeṇa hi na vaktavyā kadācitstutirātmanaḥ||21||

22piturvadhamamṛṣyaṃstu vakṣyāmyadyeha pauruṣam
|
adya paśyantu me vīryaṃ pāṇḍavāḥ sajanārdanāḥ||22||

23mṛdnataḥ sarvasainyāni yugāntamiva kurvataḥ
|
na hi devā na gandharvā nāsurā na ca rākṣasāḥ||23||

24adya śaktā raṇe jetuṃ rathasthaṃ māṃ nararṣabhāḥ
|
madanyo nāsti loke'sminnarjunādvā'stravitkvacit||24||

25ahaṃ hi jvalatāṃ madhye mayūkhānāmivāṃśumān
|
prayoktā devasṛṣṭānāmastrāṇāṃ pṛtanāgataḥ||25||

26bhṛśamiṣvasanādadya matprayuktā mahāhave
|
darśayantaḥ śarā vīryaṃ pramathiṣyanti pāṇḍavān||26||

27adya sarvā diśo rājandhārābhiriva saṃkulāḥ
|
āvṛtāḥ patribhistīkṣṇairdraṣṭāro māmakairiha||27||

28vikirañcharajālāni sarvato bhairavasvanān
|
śatrūnnipātayiṣyāmi mahāvāta iva drumān||28||

29na hi jānāti bībhatsustadastraṃ na janārdanaḥ
|
na bhīmaseno na yamau na ca rājā yudhiṣṭhiraḥ||29||

30na pārṣato durātmā'sau na śikhaṇḍī na sātyakiḥ
|
yadidaṃ mayi kauravyaṃ sakalyaṃ sanivartanam||30||

31nārāyaṇāya me pitrā praṇamya vidhipūrvakam
|
upahāraḥ purā datto brahmarūpa upasthitaḥ||31||

32taṃ svayaṃ pratigṛhyātha bhagavānsa varaṃ dadau
|
vavre pitā me paramamastraṃ nārāyaṇaṃ tataḥ||32||

33athainamabravīdrājan bhagavāndevasattamaḥ
|
bhavitā tvatsamo nānyaḥ kaścidyudhi naraḥ kvacit||33||

34na tvidaṃ sahasā brahmanprayoktavyaṃ kathañcana
|
na hyetadastramanyatra vadhācchatrornivartate||34||

35na caitacchakyate jñātuṃ kena vadhyediti prabho
|
avadhyamapi hanyāddhi tasmānnaitatprayojayet||35||

36atha saṃkhye rathasyaiva śastrāṇāṃ ca visarjanam
|
prayācatāṃ ca śatrūṇāṃ gamanaṃ śaraṇasya ca||36||

37ete praśamane yogā mahāstrasya paraṃtapa
|
sarvathā pīḍito hiṃsyādavadhyānpīḍayan raṇe||37||

38tajjagrāha pitā mahyamabravīccaiva sa prabhuḥ
|
tvaṃ vadhiṣyasi sarvāṇi śastravarṣāṇyanekaśaḥ||38||

39anenāstreṇa saṃgrāme tejasā ca jvaliṣyasi
|
evamuktvā sa bhagavān divamācakrame prabhuḥ||39||

40etannārāyaṇādastraṃ tatprāptaṃ pitṛbandhunā
|
tenāhaṃ pāṇḍavāṃścaiva pañcālānmatsyakekayān||40||

41vidrāvayiṣyāmi raṇe śacīpatirivāsurān
|
yathāyathā'hamiccheyaṃ tathā bhūtvā śarā mama||41||

42nipateyuḥ sapatneṣu vikramatsvapi bhārata
|
yatheṣṭamaśmavarṣeṇa pravarṣiṣye raṇe sthitaḥ||42||

43ayomukhaiśca vihagairdrāvayiṣye mahārathān
|
paraśvadhāṃśca niśitānutsrakṣye'hamasaṃśayam||43||

44so'haṃ nārāyaṇāstreṇa mahatā śatrutāpanaḥ
|
śatrūnvidhvaṃsayiṣyāmi kadarthīkṛtya pāṇḍavān||44||

45mitra-brahma-gurudrohī jālmakaḥ suvigarhitaḥ
|
pāñcālāpasa‌ścādya na me jīvanvimokṣyate||45||

46tacchrutvā droṇaputrasya paryavartata vāhinī
|
tataḥ sarve mahāśaṅkhāndadhmuḥ puruṣasattamāḥ||46||

47bherīścābhyahananhṛṣṭā ḍiṇḍibhāṃśca sahasraśaḥ
|
tathā nanāda vasudhā khuranemiprapīḍitā||47||

48sa śabdastumulaḥ khaṃ dyāṃ pṛthivīṃ ca vyanādayat
|
taṃ śabdaṃ pāṇḍavāḥ śrutvā parjanyaninadopamam||48||

49sametya rathināṃ śreṣṭhāḥ sahitāścāpyamantrayan
|
tathoktvā droṇaputrastu vāryupaspṛśya bhārata||49||

50prāduścakāra taddivyamastraṃ nārāyaṇaṃ tadā||50||
