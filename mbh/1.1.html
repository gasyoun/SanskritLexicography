Mahābhārata 1.1 (Ādiparvan)

1lomaharṣaṇaputra ugraśravāḥ sautiḥ paurāṇiko
|
naimiṣāraṇye śaunakasya kulapaterdvādaśavārṣike satre||1||

2sukhāsīnānabhyagacchadbrahmarṣīnsaṃśitavratān
|
vinayāvanato bhūtvā kadācitsūtanandanaḥ||2||

3tamāśramamanuprāptaṃ naimiṣāraṇyavāsinām
|
citrāḥ śrotuṃ kathāstatra parivavrustapasvinaḥ||3||

4abhivādya munīṃstāṃstu sarvāneva kṛtāñjaliḥ
|
apṛcchansa tapovṛddhiṃ sadbhiścaivābhipūjitaḥ||4||

5atha teṣūpaviṣṭeṣu sarveṣveva tapasviṣu
|
nirdiṣṭamāsanaṃ bheje vinayāllaumaharṣaṇiḥ||5||

6sukhāsīnaṃ tatastaṃ tu viśrāntamupalakṣya ca
|
athāpṛcchadṛṣistatra kaścitprastāvayankathāḥ||6||

7kuta āgamyate saute kva cāyaṃ vihṛtastvayā
|
kālaḥ kamalapatrākṣa śaṃsetatpṛcchato mama||7||

8evaṃ pṛṣṭo'bravītsamyagyathāvallaumaharṣaṇiḥ
|
vākyaṃ vacanasaṃpannasteṣāṃ ca caritāśrayam||8||

9tasminsadasi vistīrṇe munīnāṃ bhāvitātmanām
|
sautiruvāca|
janamejayasya rājarṣeḥ sarpasatre mahātmanaḥ||9||

10samīpe pārthivendrasya samyakpārikṣitasya ca
|
kṛṣṇadvaipāyanaproktāḥ supuṇyā vividhāḥ kathāḥ||10||

11kathitāścāpi vidhivadyā vaiśaṃpāyanena vai
|
śrutvā'haṃ tā vicitrārthā mahābhāratasaṃśritāḥ||11||

12bahūni saṃparikramya tīrthānyāyatanāni ca
|
samantapañcakaṃ nāma puṇyaṃ dvijaniṣevitam||12||

13gatavānasmi taṃ deśaṃ yuddhaṃ yatrābhavatpurā
|
kurūṇāṃ pāṇḍavānāṃ ca sarveṣāṃ ca mahīkṣitām||13||

14didṛkṣurāgatastasmātsamīpaṃ bhavatāmiha
|
āyuṣmantaḥ sarva eva brahmabhūtā hi me matāḥ
|
asminyajñe mahābhāgāḥ sūryapāvakavarcasaḥ||14||

15kṛtābhiṣekāḥ śucayaḥ kṛtajapyāhutāgnayaḥ
|
bhavanta āsane svasthā bravīmi kimahaṃ dvijāḥ||15||

16purāṇasaṃhitāḥ puṇyāḥ kathā dharmārthasaṃśritāḥ
|
itivṛttaṃ narendrāṇāmṛṣīṇāṃ ca mahātmanām||16||

17ṛṣaya ūcuḥ|
dvaipāyanena yatproktaṃ purāṇaṃ paramarṣiṇā
|
surairbrahmarṣibhiścaiva śrutvā yadabhipūjitam||17||

18tasyākhyānavariṣṭhasya vicitrapadaparvaṇaḥ
|
sūkṣmārthanyāyayuktasya vedārthairbhūṣitasya ca||18||

19bhāratasyetihāsasya puṇyāṃ granthārthasaṃyutām
|
saṃskāropagatāṃ brāhmīṃ nānāśāstropabṛṃhitām||19||

20janamejayasya yāṃ rājño vaiśaṃpāyana uktavān
|
yathāvatsa ṛṣiḥ tuṣṭyā satre dvaipāyanājñayā||20||

21vedaiścaturbhiḥ saṃyuktāṃ vyāsasyādbhutakarmaṇaḥ
|
saṃhitāṃ śrotumicchāmaḥ puṇyāṃ pāpabhayāpahām||21||

22sautiruvāca|
ādyaṃ puruṣamīśānaṃ puruhūtaṃ puruṣṭutam
|
ṛtamekākṣaraṃ brahma vyaktāvyaktaṃ sanātanam||22||

23asacca sadasaccaiva ca yadviśvaṃ sadasatparam
|
parāvarāṇāṃ sraṣṭāraṃ purāṇaṃ paramavyayam||23||

24maṅgalyaṃ maṅgalaṃ viṣṇuṃ vareṇyamanaghaṃ śucim
|
namaskṛtya hṛṣīkeśaṃ carācaraguruṃ harim||24||

25maharṣeḥ pūjitasyeha sarvalokairmahātmanaḥ
|
pravakṣyāmi mataṃ puṇyaṃ vyāsasyādbhutakarmaṇaḥ||25||

26ācakhyuḥ kavayaḥ kecitsaṃpratyācakṣate pare
|
ākhyāsyanti tathaivānye itihāsamimaṃ bhuvi||26||

27idaṃ tu triṣu lokeṣu mahajjñānaṃ pratiṣṭhitam
|
vistaraiśca samāsaiśca dhāryate yaddvijātibhiḥ||27||

28alaṅkṛtaṃ śubhaiḥ śabdaiḥ samayairdivyamānuṣaiḥ
|
chandovṛttaiśca vividhairanvitaṃ viduṣāṃ priyam||28||

29niṣprabhe'sminnirāloke sarvatastamasā vṛte
|
bṛhadaṇḍamabhūdekaṃ prajānāṃ bījamavyayam||29||

30yugasyādau nimittaṃ tanmahaddivyaṃ pracakṣate
|
yasminsaṃśrūyate satyaṃ jyotirbrahma sanātanam||30||

31adbhutaṃ cāpyacintyaṃ ca sarvatra samatāṃ matam
|
avyaktaṃ kāraṇaṃ sūkṣmaṃ yattatsadasadātmakam||31||

32yasmātpitāmaho jajñe prabhurekaḥ prajāpatiḥ
|
brahmā suraguruḥ sthāṇurmanuḥ kaḥ parameṣṭhyatha||32||

33prācetasastathā dakṣo dakṣaputrāśca sapta vai
|
tataḥ prajānāṃ patayaḥ prābhavannekaviṃśatiḥ||33||

34puruṣaścāprameyātmā yaṃ sarvaṛṣayo viduḥ
|
viśvedevāstathā''dityā vasavothāśvināvapi||34||

35yakṣāḥ sādhyāḥ piśācāśca guhyakāḥ pitarastathā
|
tataḥ prasūtā vidvāṃsaḥ śiṣṭā brahmarṣisattamāḥ||35||

36rājarṣayaśca bahavaḥ sarvaiḥ samuditā guṇaiḥ
|
āpo dyauḥ pṛthivī vāyurantarikṣaṃ diśastathā||36||

37saṃvatsarartavo māsāḥ pakṣāhorātrayaḥ kramāt
|
yaccānyadapi tatsarvaṃ saṃbhūtaṃ lokasākṣikam||37||

38yadidaṃ dṛśyate kiṃcidbhūtaṃ sthāvarajaṅgamam
|
punaḥ saṃkṣipyate sarvaṃ jagatprāpte yugakṣaye||38||

39yathartāvṛtuliṅgāni nānārūpāṇi paryaye
|
dṛśyante tāni tānyeva tathā bhāvā yugādiṣu||39||

40evametadanādyantaṃ bhūtasaṃhārakārakam
|
anādinidhanaṃ loke cakraṃ saṃparivartate||40||

41trayastriṃśatsahasrāṇi trayastriṃśacchatāni ca
|
trayastriṃśacca devanāṃ sṛṣṭiḥ saṃkṣepalakṣaṇā||41||

42divaḥ putro bṛhadbhānuścakṣurātmā vibhāvasuḥ
|
savitā sa ṛcīko'rko bhānurāśāvaho raviḥ||42||

43putrā vivasvataḥ sarve mahyasteṣāṃ tathā'varaḥ
|
devabhrāṭ tanayastasya subhrāḍiti tataḥ smṛtaḥ||43||

44subhrājastu trayaḥ putrāḥ prajāvanto bahuśrutāḥ
|
daśajyotiḥ śatajyotiḥ sahasrajyotireva ca||44||

45daśaputrasahasrāṇi daśajyotermahātmanaḥ
|
tato daśaguṇāścānye śatajyoterihātmajāḥ||45||

46bhūyastato daśaguṇāḥ sahasrajyotiṣaḥ sutāḥ
|
tebhyo'yaṃ kuruvaṃśaśca yadūnāṃ bharatasya ca||46||

47yayātīkṣvākuvaṃśaśca rājarṣīṇāṃ ca sarvaśaḥ
|
saṃbhūtā bahavo vaṃśā bhūtasargāḥ suvistarāḥ||47||

48bhūtasthānāni sarvāṇi rahasyaṃ trividhaṃ ca yat
|
vedā yogaḥ savijñāno dharmo'rthaḥ kāma eva ca||48||

49dharmakāmārthayuktāni śāstrāṇi vividhāni ca
|
lokayātrāvidhānaṃ ca sarvaṃ taddṛṣṭavānṛṣiḥ||49||

50itihāsāḥ savaiyākhyā vividhāḥ śrutayo'pi ca
|
iha sarvamanukrāntamuktaṃ granthasya lakṣaṇam||50||

51vistīryaitanmahajjñānamṛṣiḥ saṃkṣipya cābravīt
|
iṣṭaṃ hi viduṣāṃ loke samāsavyāsadhāraṇam||51||

52manvādi bhārataṃ kecidāstīkādi tathā'pare
|
tathoparicarādyanye viprāḥ samyagadhīyate||52||

53vividhaṃ saṃhitājñānaṃ dīpayanti manīṣiṇaḥ
|
vyākhyātuṃ kuśalāḥ kecidgranthāndhārayituṃ pare||53||

54tapasā brahmacaryeṇa vyasya vedaṃ sanātanam
|
itihāsamimaṃ cakre puṇyaṃ satyavatīsutaḥ||54||

55parāśarātmajo vidvānbrahmarṣiḥ saṃśitavrataḥ
|
tadākhyānavariṣṭhaṃ sa kṛtvā dvaipāyanaḥ prabhuḥ||55||

56kathamadhyāpayānīha śiṣyānityanvacintayat
|
tasya taccintitaṃ jñātvā ṛṣerdvaipāyanasya ca||56||

57tatrājagāma bhagavānbrahmā lokaguruḥ svayam
|
prītyarthaṃ tasya caivarṣerlokānāṃ hitakāmyayā||57||

58taṃ dṛṣṭvā vismito bhūtvā prāñjaliḥ praṇataḥ sthitaḥ
|
āsanaṃ kalpayāmāsa sarvairmunigaṇairvṛtaḥ||58||

59hiraṇyagarbhamāsīnaṃ tasmiṃstu paramāsane
|
parivṛtyāsanābhyāśe vāsaveyaḥ sthito'bhavat||59||

60anujñāto'tha kṛṣṇastu brahmaṇā parameṣṭhinā
|
niṣasādāsanābhyāśe prīyamāṇaḥ śucismitaḥ||60||

61uvāca sa mahātejā brahmāṇaṃ parameṣṭhinam
|
kṛtaṃ mayedaṃ bhagavānkāvyaṃ paramapūjitam||61||

62brahmanvedarahasyaṃ ca yaccānyatsthāpitaṃ mayā
|
sāṅgopaniṣadāṃ caiva vedānāṃ vistarakriyā||62||

63itihāsapurāṇānāmunmeṣaṃ nirmitaṃ ca yat
|
bhūtaṃ bhavyaṃ bhaviṣyacca trividhaṃ kālasaṃjñitam||63||

64jarāmṛtyubhayavyādhibhāvābhāvaviniścayaḥ
|
vividhasya ca dharmasya hyāśramāṇāṃ ca lakṣaṇam||64||

65cāturvarṇyavidhānaṃ ca purāṇānāṃ ca kṛtsnaśaḥ
|
tapaso brahmacaryasya pṛthivyāścandrasūryayoḥ||65||

66grahanakṣatratārāṇāṃ pramāṇaṃ ca yugaiḥ saha
|
ṛco yajūṃṣi sāmāni vedādhyātmaṃ tathaiva ca||66||

67nyāyaśikṣā cikitsā ca dānaṃ pāśupataṃ tathā
|
hetunaiva samaṃ janma divyamānuṣasaṃjñitam||67||

68tīrthānāṃ caiva puṇyānāṃ deśānāṃ caiva kīrtanam
|
nadīnāṃ parvatānāṃ ca vanānāṃ sāgarasya ca||68||

69purāṇāṃ caiva divyānāṃ kalpānāṃ yuddhakauśalam
|
vākyajātiviśeṣāśca lokayātrākramaśca yaḥ||69||

70yaccāpi sarvagaṃ vastu taccaiva pratipāditam
|
paraṃ na lekhakaḥ kaścidetasya bhuvi vidyate||70||

71brahmovāca|
tapoviśiṣṭadapi vai viśiṣṭānmunisaṃcayāt
|
manye śreṣṭhataraṃ tvāṃ vai rahasyajñānavedanāt||71||

72janmaprabhṛti satyāṃ te vedmi gāṃ brahmavādinīm
|
tvayā ca kāvyamityuktaṃ tasmātkāvyaṃ bhaviṣyati||72||

73asya kāvyasya kavayo na samarthā viśeṣaṇe
|
viśeṣaṇe gṛhasthasya śeṣāstraya ivāśramāḥ||73||

74kāvyasya lekhanārthāya gaṇeśaḥ smaryatāṃ mune
|
sautiruvāca|
evamābhāṣya taṃ brahmā jagāma svaṃ niveśanam||74||

75tataḥ sasmāra herambaṃ vyāsaḥ satyavatīsutaḥ
|
smṛtamātro gaṇeśāno bhaktacintitapūrakaḥ||75||

76tatrājagāma vighneśo vedavyāso yataḥ sthitaḥ
|
pūjitaścopaviṣṭaśca vyāsenoktastadā'nagha||76||

77lekhako bhāratasyāsya bhava tvaṃ gaṇanāyaka
|
mayaiva procyamānasya manasā kalpitasya ca||77||

78śrutvaitatprāha vighneśo yadi me lekhanī kṣaṇam
|
likhato nāvatiṣṭheta tadā syāṃ lekhako hyaham ||78||

79vyāso'pyuvāca taṃ devamabuddhvā mā likha kvacit
|
omityuktvā gaṇeśo'pi babhūva kila lekhakaḥ||79||

80granthagranthiṃ tadā cakre munirgūḍhaṃ kutūhalāt
|
yasminpratijñayā prāha munirdvaipāyanastvidam||80||

81aṣṭau ślokasahasrāṇi aṣṭau ślokaśatāni ca
|
ahaṃ vedmi śuko vetti saṃjayo vetti vā na vā||81||

82tacchlokakūṭamadyāpi grathitaṃ sudṛḍhaṃ mune
|
bhettuṃ na śakyate'rthasya gūḍhatvātpraśritasya ca||82||

83sarvajñopi gaṇeśo yatkṣaṇamāste vicārayan
|
tāvaccakāra vyāsopi ślokānanyānbahūnapi||83||

84ajñānatimirāndhasya lokasya tu viceṣṭataḥ
|
jñānāñjanaśālākābhiḥ netro'nmīlanakārakam||84||

85dharmārthakāmamokṣārthaiḥ samāsavyāsakīrtanaiḥ
|
tathā bhāratasūryeṇa nṛṇāṃ vinihataṃ tamaḥ||85||

86purāṇapūrṇacandreṇa śrutijyotsnāḥ prakāśitāḥ
|
nṛbuddhikauravāṇāṃ ca kṛtametatprakāśanam||86||

87itihāsapradīpena mohāvaraṇaghātinā
|
lokagarbhagṛhaṃ kṛtsnaṃ yathāvatsamprakāśitam||87||

88saṅgrahādhyāyabījo vai paulomāstīkamūlavān
|
saṃbhavaskandhavistāraḥ sabhāraṇyaviṭaṅkavān||88||

89araṇīparvarupāḍhyo virāṭodyogasāravān
|
bhīṣmaparvamahāśākho droṇaparvapalāśavān||89||

90karṇaparvasitaiḥ puṣpaiḥ śalyaparvasugandhibhiḥ
|
strīparvaivīkaviśrāmaḥ śāntiparvamahāphalaḥ||90||

91aśvamedhāmṛtarasasvāśramasthānasaṃśayaḥ
|
mausalaḥ śrutisaṃkṣepaḥ śiṣṭadvijaniṣevitaḥ||91||

92sarveṣāṃ kavimukhyānāmupajīvyo bhaviṣyati
|
parjanya iva bhūtānāmakṣayo bhāratadrumaḥ||92||

93sautiruvāca|
tasya vṛkṣasya vakṣyāmi śaśvatpuṣpaphalodayam
|
svādumedhyarasopetamacchedyamamarairapi||93||

94mātarniyogāddharmātmā gāṅgeyasya ca dhīmataḥ
|
kṣetre vicitravīryasya kṛṣṇadvaipāyanaḥ purā||94||

95trīnagnīniva kauravyāñjanayāmāsa vīryavān
|
utpādya dhṛtarāṣṭraṃ ca pāṇḍuṃ vidurameva ca||95||

96jagāma tapase dhīmān punarevāśramaṃ prati
|
teṣu jāteṣu vṛddheṣu gateṣu paramāṃ gatim||96||

97abravīdbhārataṃ loke mānuṣe'sminmahānṛṣiḥ
|
janamejayena pṛṣṭaḥ sanbrāhmaṇaiśca sahasraśaḥ||97||

98śaśāsa śiṣyamāsīnaṃ vaiśaṃpāyanamantike
|
sa sadasyaiḥ sahāsīnaḥ śrāvayāmāsa bhāratam||98||

99karmāntareṣu yajñasya codyamānaḥ punaḥ punaḥ
|
vistaraṃ kuruvaṃśasya gāndhāryā dharmaśīlatām||99||

100kṣattuḥ prājñāṃ dhṛtiṃ kuntyāḥ samyagdvaipāyano'bravīt
|
vāsudevasya māhātmyaṃ pāṇḍavānāṃ ca satyatām||100||

101durvṛttaṃ dhārtarāṣṭrāṇāmuktavān bhagavānṛṣiḥ
|
idaṃ śatasahasraṃ tu lokānāṃ puṇyakarmaṇām||101||

102upākhyānaiḥ saha jñeyamādyaṃ bhāratamuttamam
|
caturviṃśatisāhasrīṃ cakre bhāratasaṃhitām||102||

103upākhyānairvinā tāvat bhārataṃ procyate budhaiḥ
|
tatodhyardhaśataṃ bhūyaḥ saṃkṣepaṃ kṛtavānṛṣiḥ||103||

104anukramaṇikādhyāyaṃ vṛttāntānāṃ saparvaṇām
|
idaṃ dvaipāyanaḥ pūrvaṃ putramadhyāpayacchukam||104||

105tato'nyebhyo'nurūpebhyaḥ śiṣyebhyaḥ pradadau vibhuḥ
|
ṣaṣṭiṃ śatasahasrāṇi cakārānyāṃ sa saṃhitām||105||

106triṃśacchatasahasraṃ ca devaloke pratiṣṭhitam
|
pitrye pañcadaśa proktaṃ gandharveṣu caturdaśa||106||

107ekaṃ śatasahasraṃ tu mānuṣeṣu pratiṣṭhitam
|
nārado śrāvayaddevānasito devalaḥ pitṝn||107||

108gandharvayakṣarakṣāṃsi śrāvayāmāsa vai śukaḥ
|
asmiṃstu māneṣe loke vaiśaṃpāyana uktavān||108||

109śiṣyo vyāsasya dharmātmā sarvavedavidāṃ varaḥ
|
ekaṃ śatasahasraṃ tu mayoktaṃ vai nibodhata||109||

110duryodhano manyumayo mahādrumaḥ karṇaḥ skandhaḥ śakunistasya śākhāḥ
|
duśśāsanaḥ phalapuṣpe samṛddhe mūlaṃ rājā dhṛtarāṣṭro'manīṣi||110||

111yudhiṣṭhiro dharmamayo mahādrumaḥ skandho'rjuno bhīmaseno'sya śākhāḥ
|
mādrīsutau puṣpaphale samṛddhe mūlaṃ kṛṣṇo brahma ca brāhmaṇāśca||111||

112pāṇḍurjitvā bahūndeśānbuddhyā vikramaṇena ca
|
araṇye mṛgayāśīlo nyavasanmunibhiḥ saha||112||

113mṛgavyavāyanidhanātkṛcchrāṃ prāpa sa āpadam
|
janmaprabhṛti pārthānāṃ tatrācāravidhikramaḥ||113||

114mātrorabhyupapattiśca dharmopaniṣadaṃ prati
|
dharmasya vāyoḥ śakrasya devayośca tathāśvinoḥ||114||

115tāpasaiḥ saha saṃvṛddhā mātṛbhyāṃ parirakṣitāḥ
|
medhyāraṇyeṣu puṇyeṣu mahatāmāśrameṣu ca||115||

116ṛṣibhiryattadānītā dhārtarāṣṭrānprati svayam
|
śiśavaścābhirūpāśca jaṭilā brahmacāriṇaḥ||116||

117putrāśca bhrātaraśceme śiṣyāśca suhṛdaśca vaḥ
|
pāṇḍavā eta ityuktvā munayo'ntarhitāstataḥ||117||

118tāṃstairniveditāndṛṣṭvā pāṇḍavānkauravāstadā
|
śiṣṭāśca varṇāḥ paurā ye te harṣāccukruśurbhṛśam||118||

119āhuḥ kecinna tasyaite tasyaita iti cāpare
|
yadā ciramṛtaḥ pāṇḍuḥ kathaṃ tasyeti cāpare||119||

120svāgataṃ sarvathā diṣṭyā pāṇḍoḥ paśyāma santatim
|
ucyatāṃ svāgatamiti vāco'śrūyanta sarvaśaḥ||120||

121tasminnuparate śabde diśaḥ sarvā ninādayan
|
antarhitānāṃ bhūtānāṃ niḥsvanastumulo'bhavat||121||

122puṣpavṛṣṭiḥ śubhā gandhāḥ śaṅkhadundubhiniḥsvanāḥ
|
āsanpradeśe pārthānāṃ tadadbhutamivābhavat||122||

123tatprītyā caiva sarveṣāṃ paurāṇāṃ harṣasaṃbhavaḥ
|
śabda āsīnmahāṃstatra divaspṛkkīrtivardhanaḥ||123||

124te'dhītya nikhilānvedāñcchāstrāṇi vividhāni ca
|
nyavasanpāṇḍavāstatra pūjitā akutobhayāḥ||124||

125yudhiṣṭhirasya śaucena prītāḥ prakṛtayo'bhavan
|
dhṛtyā ca bhīmasenasya vikrameṇārjunasya ca||125||

126guruśuśrūṣayā kṣāntyā yamayorvinayena ca
|
tutoṣa lokaḥ sakalasteṣāṃ śauryaguṇena ca||126||

127samavāye tato rājñāṃ kanyāṃ bhartṛsvayaṃvarām
|
prāptavānarjunaḥ kṛṣṇāṃ kṛtvā karma suduṣkaram||127||

128tataḥ prabhṛti loke'sminpūjyaḥ sarvadhanuṣmatām
|
āditya iva duṣprekṣyaḥ samareṣvapi cābhavat||128||

129sa sarvānpārthivāñjitvā sarvāṃśca mahatogaṇān
|
ājahārārjuno rājño rājasūyaṃ mahākratum||129||

130annavāndakṣiṇāvāṃśca sarvaiḥ samudito guṇaiḥ
|
yudhiṣṭhireṇa saṃprāpto rājasūyo mahākratuḥ||130||

131sunayādvāsudevasya bhīmārjunabalena ca
|
ghātayitvā jarāsandhaṃ caidyaṃ ca balagarvitam||131||

132duryodhanaṃ samāgacchannarhaṇāni tatastataḥ
|
maṇikāñcanaratnāni gohastyaśvadhanāni ca||132||

133vicitrāṇi ca vāsāṃsi prāvārāvaraṇāni ca
|
kambalājinaratnāni rāṅkavāstaraṇāni ca||133||

134samṛddhāṃ tāṃ tathā dṛṣṭvā pāṇḍavānāṃ tadā śriyam
|
īrṣyāsamutthaḥ sumahāṃstasya manyurajāyata||134||

135vimānapratimāṃ tatra mayena sukṛtāṃ sabhām
|
pāṇḍavānāmupahṛtāṃ sa dṛṣṭvā paryatapyata||135||

136tatrāvahasitaścāsītpraskandanniva saṃbhramāt
|
pratyakṣaṃ vāsudevasya bhīmenānabhijātavat||136||

137sa bhogānvividhānbhuñjanratnāni vividhāni ca
|
kathito dhṛtarāṣṭrasya vivarṇo hariṇaḥ kṛśaḥ||137||

138anvajānāttato dyūtaṃ dhṛtarāṣṭraḥ sutapriyaḥ
|
tacchrutvā vāsudevasya kopaḥ samabhavanmahān||138||

139nātiprītamanāścāsīdvivādāṃścānvamodata
|
dyūtādīnanayānghorānvividhāṃścāpyupaikṣata||139||

140nirasya viduraṃ bhīṣmaṃ droṇaṃ śāradvataṃ kṛpam
|
vigrahe tumule tasmindahankṣatraṃ parasparam||140||

141jayatsu pāṇḍuputreṣu śrutvā sumahadapriyam
|
duryodhanamataṃ jñātvā karṇasya śakunestathā||141||

142dhṛtarāṣṭraściraṃ dhyātvā saṃjayaṃ vākyamabravīt
|
śṛṇu saṃjaya sarvaṃ me na cāsūyitumarhasi||142||

143śrutavānasi medhāvī buddhimānprājñasaṃmataḥ
|
na vigrahe mama matirna ca prīye kulakṣaye||143||

144na me viśeṣaḥ putreṣu sveṣu pāṇḍusuteṣu vā
|
vṛddhaṃ māmabhyasūyanti putrā manyuparāyaṇāḥ||144||

145ahaṃ tvacakṣuḥ kārpaṇyātputraprītyā sahāmi tat
|
muhyantaṃ cānumuhyāmi duryodhanamacetanam||145||

146rājasūye śriyaṃ dṛṣṭvā pāṇḍavasya mahaujasaḥ
|
taccāvahasanaṃ prāpya sabhārohaṇadarśane||146||

147amarṣaṇaḥ svayaṃ jetumaśaktaḥ pāṇḍavānraṇe
|
nirutsāhaśca saṃprāptuṃ suśriyaṃ kṣatriyo'pi san||147||

148gāndhārarājasahitaśchadmadyūtamamantrayat
|
tatra yadyadyathā jñātaṃ mayā saṃjaya tacchṛṇu||148||

149śrutvā tu mama vākyāni buddhiyuktāni tattvataḥ
|
tato jñāsyasi māṃ saute prajñāṭacakṣuṣamityuta||149||

150yadā'śrauṣaṃ dhanurāyamya citraṃ viddhaṃ lakṣyaṃ pātitaṃ vai pṛthivyām
|
kṛṣṇāṃ hṛtāṃ prekṣatāṃ sarvarājñāṃ tadā nāśaṃse vijayāya saṃjaya||150||

151yadā'śrauṣaṃ dvārakāyāṃ subhadrāṃ prasahyoḍhāṃ mādhavīmarjunena
|
indraprasthaṃ vṛṣṇivīrau ca yātau tadā nāśaṃse vijayāya saṃjaya||151||

152yadā'śrauṣaṃ devarājaṃ pravṛṣṭaṃ śarairdivyairvāritaṃ cārjunena
|
agniṃ tathā tarpitaṃ khāṇḍave ca tadā nāśaṃse vijayāya saṃjaya||152||

153yadā'śrauṣaṃ jātuṣādveśmanastānmuktānpārthānpañca kuntyā sametān
|
yuktaṃ caiṣāṃ viduraṃ svārthasiddhau tadā nāśaṃse vijayāya saṃjaya||153||

154yadā'śrauṣaṃ draupadīṃ raṅgamadhye lakṣyaṃ bhittvā nirjitāmarjunena
|
śūrānpañcālānpāṇḍaveyāṃśca yuktāṃstadā nāśaṃse vijayāya saṃjaya||154||

155yadā'śrauṣaṃ māgadhānāṃ variṣṭhaṃ jarāsandhaṃ kṣatramadhye jvalantam
|
dorbhyāṃ hataṃ bhīmasenena gatvā tadā nāśaṃse vijayāya saṃjaya||155||

156yadā'śrauṣaṃ digjaye pāṇḍuputrairvaśīkṛtānbhūmipālānprasahya
|
mahākratuṃ rājasūyaṃ kṛtaṃ ca tadā nāśaṃse vijayāya saṃjaya||156||

157yadā'śrauṣaṃ draupadīmaśrukaṇṭhīṃ sabhāṃ nītāṃ duḥkhitāmekavastrām
|
rajasvalāṃ nāthavatīmanāthavattadā nāśaṃse vijayāya saṃjaya||157||

158yadā'śrauṣaṃ vāsasāṃ tatra rāśiṃ samākṣipatkitavo mandabuddhiḥ
|
duḥśāsano gatavānnaiva cāntaṃ tadā nāśaṃse vijayāya saṃjaya||158||

159yadā'śrauṣaṃ hṛtarājyaṃ yudhiṣṭhiraṃ parājitaṃ saubalenākṣavatyām
|
anvāgataṃ bhrātṛbhiraprameyaistadā nāśaṃse vijayāya saṃjaya||159||

160yadāśrauṣaṃ vividhāstatra ceṣṭā dharmātmanāṃ prasthitānāṃ vanāya
|
jyeṣṭhaprītyā kliśyatāṃ pāṇḍavānāṃ tadā nāśaṃse vijayāya saṃjaya||160||

161yadā'śrauṣaṃ snātakānāṃ sahasrairanvāgataṃ dharmarājaṃ vanastham
|
bhikṣābhujāṃ brāhmaṇānāṃ mahātmanāṃ tadā nāśaṃse vijayāya saṃjaya||161||

162yadā'śrauṣamarjunaṃ devadevaṃ kirātarūpaṃ tryambakaṃ toṣya yuddhe
|
avāptavantaṃ pāśupataṃ mahāstraṃ tadā nāśaṃse vijayāya saṃjaya||162||

163yadāśrauṣaṃ tridivasthaṃ dhanaṃjayaṃ śakrātsākṣāddivyamastraṃ yathāvat
|
adhīyānaṃ śaṃsitaṃ satyasandhaṃ tadā nāśaṃse vijayāya saṃjaya||163||

164yadā'śroṣaṃ kālakeyāstataste paulomāno varadānācca dṛptāḥ
|
devairajeyā nirjitāścārjunena tadā nāśaṃse vijayāya saṃjaya||164||

165yadā'śrauṣamasurāṇāṃ vadhārthe kirīṭinaṃ yāntamamitrakarśanam
|
kṛtārthaṃ cāpyāgataṃ śakralokāttadā nāśaṃse vijayāya saṃjaya||165||

166yadā'śrauṣaṃ vaiśravaṇena sārdhaṃ samāgataṃ bhīmamanyāṃśca pārthān
|
tasmindeśe mānuṣāṇāmagamye tadā nāśaṃsi vijayāya saṃjayā||166||

167yadā'śrauṣaṃ ghoṣayātrāgatānāṃ bandhaṃ gandharvairmokṣaṇaṃ cārjunena
|
sveṣāṃ sutānāṃ karṇabuddhau ratānāṃ tadā nāśaṃse vijayāya saṃjaya||167||

168yadā'śrauṣaṃ yakṣarūpeṇa dharmaṃ samāgataṃ dharmarājena sūta
|
praśnānkāṃścidvibruvāṇaṃ ca samyak tadā nāśaṃse vijayāya saṃjaya||168||

169yadā'śrauṣaṃ na vidurmāmakāstān pracchannarūpānvasataḥ pāṇḍaveyān
|
virāṭarāṣṭre saha kṛṣṇayā ca tadā nāśaṃse vijayāya saṃjaya||169||

170yadā'śrauṣaṃ māmakānāṃ variṣṭhāndhanaṃjayenaikarathena bhagnān
|
virāṭarāṣṭre vasatā mahātmanā tadā nāśaṃse vijayāya saṃjaya||170||

171yadā'śrauṣaṃ satkṛtaṃ matsyarājñā sutāṃ dattāmuttarāmarjunāya
|
tāṃ cārjunaḥ pratyagṛhṇātsutārthe tadā nāśaṃse vijayāya saṃjaya||171||

172yadā'śrauṣaṃ nirjitasyādhanasya pravrājitasya svajanātpracyutasya
|
akṣauhiṇīḥ sapta yudhiṣṭhirasya tadā nāśaṃse vijayāya saṃjaya||172||

173yadā'śrauṣaṃ mādhavaṃ vāsudevaṃ sarvātmanā pāṇḍavārthe niviṣṭam
|
yasyemāṃ gāṃ vikramamekamāhustadā nāśaṃse vijayāya saṃjaya||173||

174yadā'śrauṣaṃ naranārāyaṇau tau kṛṣṇārjunau vadato nāradasya
|
ahaṃ draṣṭā brahmaloke ca samyak tadā nāśaṃse vijayāya saṃjaya||174||

175yadā'śrauṣaṃ lokahitāya kṛṣṇaṃ śamārthinamupayātaṃ kurūṇām
|
śamaṃ kurvāṇamakṛtārthaṃ ca yātaṃ tadā nāśaṃse vijayāya saṃjaya||175||

176yadāśrauṣaṃ karṇaduryodhanābhyāṃ buddhiṃ kṛtāṃ nigrahe keśavasya
|
taṃ cātmānaṃ bahudhā darśayānaṃ tadā nāśaṃse vijayāya saṃjaya||176||

177yadā'śrauṣaṃ vāsudeve prayāte rathasyaikāmagratastiṣṭhamānām
|
ārtāṃ pṛthāṃ sāntvitāṃ keśavena tadā nāśaṃse vijayāya saṃjaya||177||

178yadā'śrauṣaṃ mantriṇaṃ vāsudevaṃ tathā bhīṣmaṃ śāntanavaṃ ca teṣām
|
bhāradvājaṃ cāśiṣo'nubruvāṇaṃ tadā nāśaṃse vijayāya saṃjaya||178||

179yadāśrauṣaṃ karṇa uvāca vākyaṃ nāhaṃ yotsye yudhyamāne tvayīti
|
hitvā senāmapacakrāma cāpi tadā nāśaṃse vijayāya saṃjaya||179||

180yadā'śrauṣaṃ vāsudevārjunau tau tathā dhanurgāṇḍivamaprameyam
|
trīṇyugravīryāṇi samāgatāni tadā nāśaṃse vijayāya saṃjaya||180||

181yadā'śrauṣaṃ kaśmalenābhipanne rathopasthe sīdamāne'rjune vai
|
kṛṣṇaṃ lokāndarśayānaṃ śarīre tadā nāśaṃse vijayāya saṃjaya||181||

182yadā'śrauṣaṃ bhīṣmamitrakarśanaṃ nighnantamājāvayutaṃ rathānām
|
naiṣāṃ kaścidvadhyate khyātarūpastadā nāśaṃse vijayāya saṃjaya||182||

183yadā'śrauṣaṃ cāpageyena saṅkhye svayaṃ mṛtyuṃ vihitaṃ dhārmikeṇa
|
taccākārṣuḥ pāṇḍaveyāḥ prahṛṣṭāstadā nāśaṃse vijayāya saṃjaya||183||

184yadā'śrauṣaṃ bhīṣmamatyantaśūraṃ hataṃ pārthenāhaveṣvapradhṛṣyam
|
śikhaṇḍinaṃ purataḥ sthāpayitvā tadā nāśaṃse vijayāya saṃjaya||184||

185yadā'śrauṣaṃ śaratalpe śayānaṃ vṛddhaṃ vīraṃ sāditaṃ citrapuṅkhaiḥ
|
bhīṣmaṃ kṛtvā somakānalpaśeṣāṃstadā nāśaṃse vijayāya saṃjaya||185||

186yadā'śrauṣaṃ śāntanave śayāne pānīyārthe coditenārjunena
|
bhūmiṃ bhittvā tarpitaṃ tatra bhīṣmaṃ tadā nāśaṃse vijayāya saṃjaya||186||

187yadā vāyuścandrasūryau ca yuktau kaunteyānāmanulomā jayāya
|
nityaṃ cāsmāñśvāpadā bhīṣayanti tadā nāśaṃse vijayāya saṃjaya||187||

188yadā droṇo vividhānastramārgānnidarśayansamare citrayodhī
|
na pāṇḍavāñśreṣṭhatarānnihanti tadā nāśaṃse vijayāyaṃ saṃjaya||188||

189yadā'śrauṣaṃ cāsmadīyānmahārathānvyavasthitānarjunasyāntakāya
|
saṃśaptakānnihatānarjunena tadā nāśaṃse vijayāya saṃjaya||189||

190yadā'śrauṣaṃ vyūhamabhedyamanyairbhāradvājenāttaśastreṇa guptam
|
bhittvā saubhadraṃ vīramekaṃ praviṣṭaṃ tadā nāśaṃse vijayāya saṃjaya||190||

191yadā'bhimanyuṃ parivārya bālaṃ sarve hattvā hṛṣṭarūpā babhūvuḥ
|
mahārathāḥ pārthamaśaknuvantastadā nāśaṃse vijayāya saṃjaya||191||

192yadā'śrauṣamabhimanyuṃ nihatya harṣānmūḍhānkrośato dhārtarāṣṭrān
|
krodhāduktaṃ saindhave cārjunena tadā nāśaṃse vijayāya saṃjaya||192||

193yadā'śrauṣaṃ saindhavārthe pratijñāṃ pratijñātāṃ tadvadhāyārjunena
|
satyāṃ tīrṇāṃ śatrumadhye ca tena tadā nāśaṃse vijayāya saṃjaya||193||

194yadā'śrauṣaṃ śrāntahaye dhanaṃjaye muktvā hayānpāyayitvopavṛttān
|
punaryuktvā vāsudevaṃ prayātaṃ tadā nāśaṃse vijayāya saṃjaya||194||

195yadā'śrauṣaṃ vāhaneṣvakṣameṣu rathopasthe tiṣṭhatā pāṇḍavena
|
sarvānyodhānvāritānarjunena tadā nāśaṃse vijayāya saṃjaya||195||

196yadā'śrauṣaṃ nāgabalaiḥ suduḥsahaṃ droṇānīkaṃ yuyudhānaṃ pramathya
|
yātaṃ vārṣṇeyaṃ yatra tau kṛṣṇapārthau tadā nāśaṃse vijayāya saṃjaya||196||

197yadā'śrauṣaṃ karṇamāsādya muktaṃ vadhādbhīmaṃ kutsayitvā vacobhiḥ
|
dhanuṣkoṭyā tudya karṇena vīraṃ tadā nāśaṃse vijayāya saṃjaya||197||

198yadā droṇaḥ kṛtavarmā kṛpaśca karṇo drauṇirmadrarājaśca śūraḥ
|
amarṣayansaindhavaṃ vadhyamānaṃ tadā nāśaṃse vijayāya saṃjaya||198||

199yadā'śrauṣaṃ devarājena dattāṃ divyāṃ śaktiṃ vyaṃsitāṃ mādhavena
|
ghaṭotkace rākṣase ghorarūpe tadā nāśaṃse vijayāya saṃjaya||199||

200yadā'śrauṣaṃ karṇaghaṭotkacābhyāṃ yuddhe muktāṃ sūtaputreṇa śaktim
|
yayā vadhyaḥ samare savyasācī tadā nāśaṃse vijayāya saṃjaya||200||

201yadā'śrauṣaṃ droṇamācāryamekaṃ dhṛṣṭadyumnenābhyatikramya dharmam
|
rathopasthe prāyagataṃ viśastaṃ tadā nāśaṃse vijayāya saṃjaya||201||

202yadā'śrauṣaṃ drauṇinā dvairathasthaṃ mādrīsutaṃ nakulaṃ lokamadhye
|
samaṃ yuddhe maṇḍalebhyaścarantaṃ tadā nāśaṃse vijayāya saṃjaya||202||

203yadā droṇe nihate droṇaputro nārāyaṇaṃ divyamastraṃ vikurvan
|
naiṣāmantaṃ gatavānpāṇḍavānāṃ tadā nāśaṃse vijayāya saṃjaya||203||

204yadā'śrauṣaṃ bhīmasenena pītaṃ raktaṃ bhrāturyudhi duḥśāsanasya
|
nivāritaṃ nānyatamena bhīmaṃ tadā nāśaṃse vijayāya saṃjaya||204||

205yadā'śrauṣaṃ karṇamatyantaśūraṃ hataṃ pārthenāhaveṣvapradhṛṣyam
|
tasminbhrātṛṇāṃ vigrahe devaguhye tadā nāśaṃse vijayāya saṃjaya||205||

206yadā'śrauṣaṃ droṇaputraṃ ca śūraṃ duḥśāsanaṃ kṛtavarmāṇamugram
|
yudhiṣṭiraṃ dharmarājaṃ jayantaṃ tadā nāśaṃse vijayāya saṃjaya||206||

207yadā'śrauṣaṃ nihataṃ madrarājaṃ raṇe śūraṃ dharmarājena sūta
|
sadā saṅgrāme spardhate yastu kṛṣṇaṃ tadā nāśaṃse vijayāya saṃjaya||207||

208yadā'śrauṣaṃ kalahadyūtamūlaṃ māyābalaṃ saubalaṃ pāṇḍavena
|
hataṃ saṅgrāme sahadevena pāpaṃ tadā nāśaṃse vijayāya saṃjaya||208||

209yadā'śrauṣaṃ śrāntamekaṃ śayānaṃ hradaṃ gatvā stambhayitvā tadambhaḥ
|
duryodhanaṃ virathaṃ bhagnaśaktiṃ tadā nāśaṃse vijayāya saṃjaya||209||

210yadā'śrauṣaṃ pāṇḍavāṃstiṣṭhamānān gatvā hrade vāsudevena sārdham
|
amarṣaṇaṃ dharṣayataḥ sutaṃ me tadā nāśaṃse vijayāya saṃjaya||210||

211yadā'śrauṣaṃ vividhāṃścitramārgān gadāyuddhe maṇḍalaśaścarantam
|
mithyā hataṃ vāsudevasya buddhyā tadā nāśaṃse vijayāya saṃjaya||211||

212yadā'śrauṣaṃ droṇaputrādibhistairhatānpañcālāndraupadeyāṃśca suptān
|
kṛtaṃ bībhatsamayaśasyaṃ ca karma tadā nāśaṃse vijayāya saṃjaya||212||

213yadā'śrauṣaṃ bhīmasenānuyāte nāśvatthāmnā paramāstraṃ prayuktam
|
kruddhenaiṣīkamavadhīdyena garbhaṃ tadā nāśaṃse vijayāya saṃjaya||213||

214yadā'śrauṣaṃ brahmaśiro'rjunena svastītyuktvā'stramastreṇa śāntam
|
aśvatthāmnā maṇiratnaṃ ca dattaṃ tadā nāśaṃse vijayāya saṃjaya||214||

215yadā'śrauṣaṃ droṇaputreṇa garbhe vairāṭyā vai pātyamāne mahāstraiḥ
|
dvaipāyanaḥ keśavo droṇaputraṃ parasperaṇābhiśāpaiḥ śaśāpa||215||

216śocyā gāndhārī putrapautrairvihīnā tathā bandhubhiḥ pitṛbhirbhrātṛbhiśca
|
kṛtaṃ kāryaṃ duṣkaraṃ pāṇḍaveyaḥ prāptaṃ rājyamasapatnaṃ punastaiḥ||216||

217kaṣṭaṃ yuddhe daśa śeṣāḥ śrutā me trayo'smākaṃ pāṇḍavānāṃ ca sapta
|
dvyūnā viṃśatirāhatā'kṣauhiṇīnāṃ tasminsaṅgrāme bhairave kṣatriyāṇām||217||

218tamastvatīva vistīrṇaṃ moha āviśatīva mām
|
saṃjñāṃ nopalabhe sūta mano vihvalatīva me||218||

219sautiruvāca|
ityuktvā dhṛtarāṣṭro'tha vilapya bahu duḥkhitaḥ
|
mūrcchitaḥ punarāśvastaḥ saṃjayaṃ vākyamabravīt||219||

220dhṛtarāṣṭra uvāca|
saṃjayaivaṃ gate prāṇāṃstyaktumicchāmi mā ciram
|
stokaṃ hyapi na paśyāmi phalaṃ jīvitadhāraṇe||220||

221sautiruvāca|
taṃ tathā vādinaṃ dīnaṃ vilapantaṃ mahīpatim
|
niḥśvasantaṃ yathā nāgaṃ muhyamānaṃ punaḥ punaḥ||221||

222gāvalgaṇiridaṃ dhīmānmahārthaṃ vākyamabravīt
|
saṃjaya uvāca|
śrutavānasi vai rājanmahotsāhānmahābalān||222||

223dvaipāyanasya vadato nāradasya ca dhīmataḥ
|
mahatsu rājavaṃśeṣu guṇaiḥ samuditeṣu ca||223||

224jātāndivyāstraviduṣaḥ śakrapratimatejasaḥ
|
dharmeṇa pṛthivīṃ jitvā yajñairiṣṭvāptadakṣiṇaiḥ||224||

225asmi~lloke yaśaḥ prāpya tataḥ kālavaśaṃ gatān
|
śaibyaṃ mahārathaṃ vīraṃ sṛṃjayaṃ jayatāṃ varam||225||

226suhotraṃ rantidevaṃ ca kākṣīvantamathauśijam
|
bāhlīkaṃ damanaṃ caidyaṃ śaryātimajitaṃ nalam||226||

227viśvāmitramamitraghnamambarīṣaṃ mahābalam
|
maruttaṃ manumikṣvākuṃ gayaṃ bharatameva ca||227||

228rāmaṃ dāśarathiṃ caiva śaśabinduṃ bhagīratham
|
kṛtavīryaṃ mahābhāgaṃ tathaiva janamejayam||228||

229yayātiṃ śubhakarmāṇaṃ devairyo yājitaḥ svayam
|
caityayūpāṅkitā bhūmiryasyeyaṃ savanākarā||229||

230iti rājñāṃ caturviṃśannāradena surarṣiṇā
|
putraśokābhitaptāya purā śvaityāya kīrtitam||230||

231tebhyaścānye gatāḥ pūrvaṃ rājāno balavattarāḥ
|
mahārathā mahātmānaḥ sarvaiḥ samuditā guṇaiḥ||231||

232pūruḥ kururyaduḥ śūro viṣvagaśvo mahādyutiḥ
|
aṇuho yuvanāśvaśca kakutstho vikramī raghuḥ||232||

233vijayo vītihotro'ṅgo bhavaḥ śveto bṛhadguruḥ
|
uśīnaraḥ śatarathaḥ kaṅko duliduho drumaḥ||233||

234dambhodbhavaḥ paro venaḥ sagaraḥ saṃkṛtirnimiḥ
|
ajeyaḥ paraśuḥ puṇḍraḥ śaṃbhurdevāvṛdho'naghaḥ||234||

235devāhvayaḥ supratimaḥ supratīko bṛhadrathaḥ
|
mahotsāho vinītātmā sukraturnaiṣadho nalaḥ||235||

236satyavrataḥ śāntabhayaḥ sumitraḥ subalaḥ prabhuḥ
|
jānujaṅgho'naraṇyo'rkaḥ priyabhṛtyaḥ śucivrataḥ||236||

237balabandhurnirāmardaḥ ketuśṛṅgo bṛhadbalaḥ
|
dhṛṣṭaketurbṛhatketurdīptaketurnirāmayaḥ||237||

238avikṣiccapalo dhūrtaḥ kṛtabandhurdṛḍheṣudhiḥ
|
mahāpurāṇasaṃbhāvyaḥ pratyaṅgaḥ parahā śrutiḥ||238||

239ete cānye ca rājānaḥ śataśo'tha sahasraśaḥ
|
śrūyante śataśaścānye saṅkhyātāścaiva padmaśaḥ||239||

240hitvā suvipulānbhogānbuddhimanto mahābalāḥ
|
rājāno nidhanaṃ prāptāstava putrā iva prabho||240||

241yeṣāṃ divyāni karmāṇi vikramastyāga eva ca
|
māhātmyamapi cāstikyaṃ satyaṃ śaucaṃ dayā'rjavam||241||

242vidvadbhiḥ kathyate loke purāṇe kavisattamaiḥ
|
sarvārddhiguṇasaṃpannāste cāpi nidhanaṃ gatāḥ||242||

243tava putrā durātmānaḥ prataptāścaiva manyunā
|
lubdhā durvṛttabhūyiṣṭhā na tāñchocitumarhasi||243||

244śrutavānasi medhāvī buddhimānprājñasaṃmataḥ
|
yeṣāṃ śāstrānugā buddhirna te muhyanti bhārata||244||

245nigrahānugrahau cāpi viditau te narādhipa
|
nātyantamevānuvṛttiḥ kāryā te putrarakṣaṇe||245||

246bhavitavyaṃ tathā tacca nānuśocitumarhasi
|
daivaṃ prajñāviśeṣeṇa ko nivartitumarhati||246||

247vidhātṛvihitaṃ mārgaṃ na kaścidativartate
|
kālamūlamidaṃ sarvaṃ bhāvābhāvau sukhāsukhe||247||

248kālaḥ sṛjati bhūtāni kālaḥ saṃharate prajāḥ
|
saṃharantaṃ prajāḥ kālaṃ kālaḥ śamayate punaḥ||248||

249kālo hi kurute bhāvānsarvalloke śubhāśubhān
|
kālaḥ saṃkṣipate sarvāḥ prajā visṛjate punaḥ||249||

250kālaḥ supteṣu jāgarti kālo hi duratikramaḥ
|
kālaḥ sarveṣu bhūteṣu caratyavidhṛtaḥ samaḥ||250||

251atītānāgatā bhāvā ye ca vartanti sāṃpratam
|
tānkālanirmitānbuddhvā na saṃjñāṃ hātumarhasi||251||

252sautiruvāca|
ityevaṃ putraśokārtaṃ dhṛtarāṣṭraṃ janeśvaram
|
āśvāsya svasthamakarotsūto gāvalgaṇistadā||252||

253atropaniṣadaṃ puṇyāṃ kṛṣṇadvaipāyano'bravīt
|
vidvadbhiḥ kathyate loke purāṇe kavisattamaiḥ||253||

254bhāratādhyayanaṃ puṇyamapi pādamadhīyataḥ
|
śraddadhānasya pūyante sarvapāpānyaśeṣataḥ||254||

255devā devarṣayo hyatra tathā brahmarṣayo'malāḥ
|
kīrtyante śumakarmāṇastathā yakṣā mahoragāḥ||255||

256bhagavānvāsudevaśca kīrtyate'tra sanātanaḥ
|
sa hi satyamṛtaṃ caiva pavitraṃ puṇyameva ca||256||

257śāśvataṃ brahmaparamaṃ dhruvaṃ jyotiḥ sanātanam
|
yasya divyāni karmāṇi kathayanti manīṣiṇaḥ||257||

258asacca sadasaccaiva yasmādviśvaṃ pravartate
|
santatiśca pravṛttiśca janmamṛtyupunarbhavāḥ||258||

259adhyātmaṃ śrūyate yacca pañcabhūtaguṇātmakam
|
avyaktādi paraṃ yacca sa eva parigīyate||259||

260yattadyativarā muktā dhyānayogabalānvitāḥ
|
pratibimbamivādarśe paśyantyātmanyavasthitam||260||

261śraddadhānaḥ sadā yuktaḥ sadā dharmaparāyaṇaḥ
|
āsevannimamadhyāyaṃ naraḥ pāpātpramucyate||261||

262anukramaṇikādhyāyaṃ bhāratasyemamāditaḥ
|
āstikaḥ satataṃ śṛṇvanna kṛcchreṣvavasīdati||262||

263ubhe sandhye japankiṃcitsadyo mucyeta kilbiṣāt
|
anukramaṇyā yāvatsyādahnā rātryā ca saṃcitam||263||

264bhāratasya vapurhyetatsatyaṃ cāmṛtameva ca
|
navanītaṃ yathā dadhno dvipadāṃ brāhmaṇo yathā||264||

265āraṇyakaṃ ca vedebhya oṣadhibhyo'mṛtaṃ yathā
|
hradānāmudadhiḥ śreṣṭho gaurvariṣṭhā catuṣpadām||265||

266yathaitānītihāsānāṃ tathā bhāratamucyate
|
yaścainaṃ śrāvayecchrāddhe brāhmaṇānpādamantataḥ||266||

267akṣayyamannapānaṃ vai pitṛṃstasyopatiṣṭhate
|
itihāsapurāṇābhyāṃ vedaṃ samupabṛṃhayet||267||

268bibhetyalpaśrutādvedo māmayaṃ prahariṣyati
|
kārṣṇaṃ vedamimaṃ vidvāñśrāvayitvārthamaśnute||268||

269bhrūṇahatyādikaṃ cāpi pāpaṃ jahyādasaṃśayam
|
ya imaṃ śuciradhyāyaṃ paṭhetparvaṇi parvaṇi||269||

270adhītaṃ bhārataṃ tena kṛtsnaṃ syāditi me matiḥ
|
yaścainaṃ śṛṇuyānnityamārṣaṃ śraddhāsamanvitaḥ||270||

271sa dīrghamāyuḥ kīrtiṃ ca svargatiṃ cāpnuyānnaraḥ
|
ekataścaturo vedā bhārataṃ caitadekataḥ||271||

272purā kila suraiḥ sarvaiḥ sametya tulayā dhṛtam
|
caturbhyaḥ sarahasyebhyo vedebhyo hyadhikaṃ yadā||272||

273tadāprabhṛti loke'sminmahābhāratamucyate
|
mahattve ca gurutve ca dhriyamāṇaṃ yato'dhikam ||273||

274mahattvādbhāravattvācca mahābhāratamucyate
|
niruktamasya yo veda sarvapāpaiḥ pramucyate||274||

275tapo na kalko'dhyayanaṃ na kalkaḥ svābhāviko vedavidhirna kalkaḥ
|
prasahya vittāharaṇaṃ na kalkastānyeva bhāvopahatāni kalkaḥ||275||
