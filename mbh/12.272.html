Mahābhārata 12.272 (Śāntiparvan)

1yudhiṣṭhira uvāca|
bahūnāṃ yajñatapasāmekārthānāṃ pitāmaha
|
dharmārthaṃ na sukhārthārthaṃ kathaṃ yajñaḥ samāhitaḥ ||1||

2bhīṣma uvāca|
atra te vartayiṣyāmi nāradenānukīrtitam
|
ucchavṛtteḥ purāvṛttaṃ yajñārthe brāhmaṇasya ca ||2||

3nārada uvāca|
rāṣṭre dharmottare śreṣṭhe vidarbheṣvabhavaddvijaḥ
|
ucchavṛttirṛṣiḥ kaścidyajñaṃ yaṣṭuṃ samādadhe ||3||

4śyāmākamaśanaṃ tatra sūryaparṇī suvarcalā
|
tiktaṃ ca virasaṃ śākaṃ tapasā svādutāṃ gatam ||4||

5upagamya vane siddhiṃ sarvabhūtāvihiṃsayā
|
api mūlaphalairiṣṭo yajñaḥ svargyaḥ paraṃtapa ||5||

6tasya bhāryā vratakṛśā śuciḥ puṣkaradhāriṇī
|
yajñapatnī samānītā satyenānuvidhīyate ||6||

7sā tu śāpaparitrastā tatsvabhāvānuvartinī
|
māyūrajīrṇaparṇānāṃ vastraṃ tasyāśca varṇitam ||7||

8akāmayā kṛtastatra yajño hotranuśāsanāt
|
śukrasya punarājātiḥ parṇādo nāma dharmavit ||8||

9tasminvane samīpastho mṛgo'bhūtsahavāsikaḥ
|
vacobhirabravītsatyaṃ tvayedaṃ duṣkṛtaṃ kṛtam ||9||

10yadi mantrāgahīno'yaṃ yajño bhavati vai kṛtaḥ
|
māṃ bhoḥ prakṣipa hotre tvaṃ gaccha svargamaninditaḥ ||10||

11tatastu yajñe sāvitrī sākṣāttaṃ saṃnyamantrayat
|
nimantrayantī pratyuktā na hanyāṃ sahavāsinam ||11||

12evamuktā nivṛttā sā praviṣṭā yajñapāvakam
|
kiṃ nu duścaritaṃ yajñe didṛkṣuḥ sā rasātalam ||12||

13sa tu baddhāñjaliṃ satyamayācaddhariṇaḥ punaḥ
|
satyena sa pariṣvajya saṃdiṣṭo gamyatāmiti ||13||

14tataḥ sa hariṇo gatvā padānyaṣṭau nyavartata
|
sādhu hiṃsaya māṃ satya hato yāsyāmi sadgatim ||14||

15paśya hyapsaraso divyā mayā dattena cakṣuṣā
|
vimānāni vicitrāṇi gandharvāṇāṃ mahātmanām ||15||

16tataḥ sa suciraṃ dṛṣṭvā spṛhālagnena cakṣuṣā
|
mṛgamālokya hiṃsāyāṃ svargavāsaṃ samarthayat ||16||

17sa tu dharmo mṛgo bhūtvā bahuvarṣoṣito vane
|
tasya niṣkṛtimādhatta na tvasau yajñasaṃvidhiḥ ||17||

18tasya tenānubhāvena mṛgahiṃsā''tmanastadā
|
tapo mahatsamucchinnaṃ tasmāddhiṃsā na yajñiyā ||18||

19tatastaṃ bhagavāndharmo yajñaṃ yājayataḥ svayam
|
samādhānaṃ ca bhāryāyā lebhe sa tapasā param ||19||

20ahiṃsā sakalo dharmo hiṃsādharmastathāhitaḥ
|
satyaṃ te'haṃ pravakṣyāmi no dharmaḥ satyavādinām ||20||
