Mahābhārata 13.26 (Anuśāsanaparvan)

1vaiśampāyana uvāca|
bṛhaspatisamaṃ buddhyā kṣamayā brahmaṇaḥ samam
|
parākrame śakrasamamādityasamatejasam ||1||

2gāṅgeyamarjunenājau nihataṃ bhūritejasam
|
bhrātṛbhiḥ sahito'nyaiśca paryapṛcchadyudhiṣṭhiraḥ ||2||

3śayānaṃ vīraśayane kālākāṅkṣiṇamacyutam
|
ājagmurbharataśreṣṭhaṃ draṣṭukāmā maharṣayaḥ ||3||

4atrirvasiṣṭho'tha bhṛguḥ pulastyaḥ pulahaḥ kratuḥ
|
aṅgirā gautamo'gastyaḥ sumatiḥ suyatātmavān ||4||

5viśvāmitraḥ sthūlaśirāḥ saṃvartaḥ pramatirdamaḥ
|
bṛhaspatyuśanovyāsāścyavanaḥ kāśyapo dhruvaḥ ||5||

6durvāsā jamadagniśca mārkaṇḍeyo'tha gālavaḥ
|
bharadvājo'tha raibhyaśca yavakrītastritastathā ||6||

7sthūlākṣaḥ śabalākṣaśca kaṇvo medhātithiḥ kṛśaḥ
|
nāradaḥ parvataścaiva sudhanvā'thaikato dvijaḥ ||7||

8nitambhūrbhuvano dhaumyaḥ śatānando'kṛtavraṇaḥ
|
jāmadagnyastathā rāmaḥ kacaścetyevamādayaḥ ||8||

9samāgatā mahātmāno bhīṣmaṃ draṣṭuṃ maharṣayaḥ
|
teṣāṃ mahātmanāṃ pūjāmāgātānāṃ yudhiṣṭhiraḥ ||9||

10bhrātṛbhiḥ sahitaścakre yathāvadanupūrvaśaḥ
|
te pūjitāḥ sukhāsīnāḥ kathāścakrurmaharṣayaḥ ||10||

11bhīṣmāśritāḥ sumadhurāḥ sarvendriyamanoharāḥ
|
bhīṣmasteṣāṃ kathāḥ śrutvā ṛṣīṇāṃ bhāvitātmanām ||11||

12mene diviṣṭhamātmānaṃ tuṣṭyā paramayā yutaḥ
|
tataste bhīṣmamāmantrya pāṇḍavāṃśca maharṣayaḥ ||12||

13antardhānaṃ gatāḥ sarve sarveṣāmeva paśyatām
|
tānṛṣīnsumahābhāgānantardhānagatānapi ||13||

14pāṇḍavāstuṣṭuvuḥ sarve praṇemuśca muhurmuhuḥ
|
prasannamanasaḥ sarve gāṅgeyaṃ kurusattamam ||14||

15upasthuryathodyantamādityaṃ mantrakovidāḥ
|
prabhāvāttapasasteṣāmṛṣīṇāṃ vīkṣya pāṃḍavāḥ ||15||

16prakāśanto diśaḥ sarvā vismayaṃ paramaṃ yayuḥ
|
mahābhāgyaṃ paraṃ teṣāmṛṣīṇāmanucintya te
|
pāṃḍavāḥ saha bhīṣmeṇa kathāścakrustadāśrayāḥ ||16||

17vaiśampāyana uvāca|
kathānte śirasā pādau spṛṣṭvā bhīṣmasya pāṃḍavaḥ
|
dharmyaṃ dharmasutaḥ praśnaṃ paryapṛcchadyudhiṣṭhiraḥ ||17||

18yudhiṣṭhira uvāca|
ke deśāḥ ke janapadā āśramāḥ ke ca parvatāḥ
|
prakṛṣṭāḥ puṇyataḥ kāśca jñeyā nadyaḥ pitāmaha ||18||

19bhīṣma uvāca|
atrāpyudāharantīmamitihāsaṃ purātanam
|
śiloṃcchavṛtteḥ saṃvādaṃ siddhasya ca yudhiṣṭhira ||19||

20imāṃ kaścitparikramya pṛthivīṃ śailabhūṣaṇām
|
asakṛddvipadāṃ śreṣṭhaḥ śreṣṭhasya gṛhamedhinaḥ ||20||

21śilavṛttergṛhaṃ prāptaḥ sa tena vidhinā'rcitaḥ
|
uvāsa rajanīṃ tatra sumukhaḥ sukhabhāgṛṣiḥ ||21||

22śilavṛttistu yat kṛtyaṃ prātastatkṛtavāñchuciḥ
|
kṛtakṛtyamupātiṣṭhat siddhaṃ tamatithiṃ tadā ||22||

23tau sametya mahātmānau sukhāsīnau kathāḥ śubhāḥ
|
cakraturvedasaṃbaddhāstaccheṣakṛtalakṣaṇāḥ ||23||

24śilavṛttiḥ kathānte tu siddhamāmantrya yatnataḥ
|
praśnaṃ papraccha medhāvī yanmāṃ tvaṃ paripṛcchasi ||24||

25śilavṛttiruvāca|
ke deśāḥ ke janapadāḥ ke śramāḥ ke ca parvatāḥ
|
prakṛṣṭā puṇyataḥ kāśca jñeyā nadyastaducyatām ||25||

26siddha uvāca|
te deśāste janapadāste'śramāste ca parvatāḥ
|
yeṣāṃ bhāgīrathī gaṅgā madhyenaiti saridvarā ||26||

27tapasā brahmacaryeṇa yajñaistyāgena vā punaḥ
|
gatiṃ tāṃ na labhejjanturgaṅgāṃ saṃsevya yāṃ labhet ||27||

28spṛṣṭāni yeṣāṃ gāṅgeyaistoyairgātrāṇi dehinām
|
nyastāni na punasteṣāṃ tyāgaḥ svargādvidhīyate ||28||

29sarvāṇi yeṣāṃ gāṅgeyaistoyaiḥ kāryāṇi dehinām
|
gāṃ tyaktvā mānavā vipra divi tiṣṭhanti te janāḥ ||29||

30pūrve vayasi karmāṇi kṛtvā pāpāni ye narāḥ
|
paścādgaṅgāṃ niṣevante te'pi yāntyuttamāṃ gatim ||30||

31snātānāṃ śucibhistoyairgāṅgeyaiḥ prayatātmanām
|
vyuṣṭirbhavati yā puṃsāṃ na sā kratuśatairapi ||31||

32yāvadasthi manuṣyasya gaṅgātoyeṣu tiṣṭhati
|
tāvadvarṣasahasrāṇi svargaloke mahīyate ||32||

33apahatya tamastīvraṃ yathā bhātyudaye raviḥ
|
tathā'pahatya pāpmānaṃ bhāti gaṅgājalokṣitaḥ ||33||

34visomā iva śarvaryo vipuṣpāstaravo yathā
|
|
tadvaddeśā diśaścaiva hīnā gaṅgājalaiḥ śivaiḥ ||34||

35varṇāśramā yathā sarve dharmajñānavivarjitāḥ
|
kratavaśca yathā'somāstathā gaṅgāṃ vinā jagat ||35||

36yathā hīnaṃ nabho'rkeṇa bhūḥ śailaiḥ khaṃ ca vāyunā
|
tathā deśā diśaścaiva gaṃgāhīnā na saṃśayaḥ ||36||

37triṣu lokeṣu ye kecit prāṇinaḥ sarva eva te
|
tarpyamāṇāḥ parāṃ tṛptiṃ yānti gaṃgājalaiḥ śubhaiḥ ||37||

38yastu sūryeṇa niṣṭaptaṃ gāṃgeyaṃ pibate jalam
|
gavāṃ nirhāranirmuktādyāvakāttadviśiṣyate ||38||

39induvratasahasraṃ tu yaścaretkāyaśodhanam
|
pibedyaścāpi gaṅgāmbhaḥ samau syātāṃ na vā samau ||39||

40tiṣṭhedyugasahasraṃ tu padenaikena yaḥ pumān
|
māsamekaṃ tu gaṃgāyāṃ samau syātāṃ na vā samau ||40||

41lambate'vākśirā yastu yugānāmayutaṃ pumān
|
tiṣṭhedyatheṣṭaṃ yaścāpi gaṃgāyāṃ sa viśiṣyate ||41||

42agnau prāstaṃ pradhūyeta yathā tūlaṃ dvijottama
|
tathā gaṃgāvagāḍhasya sarvapāpaṃ pradhūyate ||42||

43bhūtānāmiha sarveṣāṃ duḥkhopahatacetasām
|
gatimanveṣamāṇānāṃ na gaṃgāsadṛśī gatiḥ ||43||

44bhavanti nirviṣāḥ sarpā yathā tārkṣyasya darśanāt
|
gaṃgāyā darśanāttadvatsarvapāpaiḥ pramucyate ||44||

45apratiṣṭhāśca ye kecidadharmaśaraṇāśca ye
|
teṣāṃ pratiṣṭhā gaṃgeha śaraṇaṃ śarma varma ca ||45||

46prakṛṣṭairaśubhairgrastanane-- puruṣādhamān
|
patato narake gaṃgā saṃśritānpretya tārayet ||46||

47te saṃvibhaktā munibhirnūnaṃ devaiḥ savāsavaiḥ
|
ye'bhigacchanti satataṃ gaṅgāṃ matimatāṃ vara ||47||

48vinayācārahīnāśca aśivāśca narādhamāḥ
|
te bhavanti śivā vipra ye vai gaṅgāmupāśritāḥ ||48||

49yathā surāṇāmamṛtaṃ pitṝṇāṃ ca yathā svadhā
|
sudhā yathā ca nāgānāṃ tathā gaṅgājalaṃ nṛṇām ||49||

50upāsate yathā bālā mātaraṃ kṣudhayā'rditāḥ
|
śreyaskāmāstathā gaṅgāmupāsantīha dehinaḥ ||50||

51svayaṃbhuvaṃ yathā sthānaṃ sarveṣāṃ śreṣṭhamucyate
|
snātānāṃ saritāṃ śreṣṭhā gaṅgā tadvadihocyate ||51||

52yathopajīvināṃ dhenurdevādīnāṃ dharā smṛtā
|
|
tathopajīvināṃ gaṃgā sarvaprāṇabhṛtāmiha ||52||

53devāḥ somārkasaṃsthāni yathā satrādibhirmakhaiḥ
|
amṛtānyupajīvanti tathā gaṃgājalaṃ narāḥ ||53||

54jāhnavīpulinotthābhiḥ sikatābhiḥ samukṣitam
|
ātmānaṃ manyate loko diviṣṭhamiva śobhitam ||54||

55jāhnavītīrasaṃbhūtāṃ mṛdaṃ mūrdhnā bibharti yaḥ
|
bibharti rūpaṃ so'rkasya tamonāśāya nirmalam ||55||

56gaṃgormibhiratho digdhaḥ puruṣaṃ pavano yadā
|
spṛśate so'sya pāpmānaṃ sadya evāpakarṣati ||56||

57vyasanairabhitaptasya narasya vinaśiṣyataḥ
|
gaṃgādarśanajā prītirvyasanānyapakarṣati ||57||

58haṃsārāvaiḥ kokaravai ravairanyaiśca pakṣiṇām
|
paspardha gaṅgā gandharvān pulinaiśca śiloccayān ||58||

59haṃsādibhiḥ subahubhirvividhaiḥ pakṣibhirvṛtām
|
gaṃgāṃ gokulasambādhāṃ dṛṣṭvā svargo'pi vismṛtaḥ ||59||

60na sā prītirdiviṣṭhasya sarvakāmānupāśnataḥ
|
saṃbhavedyā parā prītirgaṅgāyāḥ puline nṛṇām ||60||

61vāṅmanaḥkarmajairgrastaḥ pāpairapi pumāniha
|
vīkṣya gaṃgāṃ bhavetpūto atra me nāsti saṃśayaḥ ||61||

62saptāvarān sapta parān pitṝṃstebhyaśca ye pare
|
pumāṃstārayate gaṃgāṃ vīkṣya spṛṣṭvā'vagāhya ca ||62||

63śrutābhilaṣitā pītā spṛṣṭā dṛṣṭāvagāhitā
|
gaṃgā tārayate nṛṇāṃmubhau vaṃśau viśeṣataḥ ||63||

64darśanātsparśanātpānāttathā gaṃgeti kīrtanāt
|
punātyapuṇyānpuruṣāñchataśo'tha sahasraśaḥ ||64||

65ya icchetsaphalaṃ janma jīvitaṃ śrutameva ca
|
sa pitṝṃstarpayedgaṃgāmabhigamya surāṃstathā ||65||

66na sutairna ca vittena karmaṇā na ca tatphalam
|
prāpnuyātpuruṣo'tyantaṃ gaṃgāṃ prāpya yadāpnuyāt ||66||

67jātyandhairiha tulyāste mṛtaiḥ paṃgubhireva ca
|
samarthā ye na paśyanti gaṃgāṃ puṇyajalāṃ śivām ||67||

68bhūtabhavyabhaviṣyajñairmaharṣibhirupasthitām
|
devaiḥ sendraiśca ko gaṃgāṃ nopaseveta mānavaḥ ||68||

69vānaprasthairgṛhasthaiśca yatibhirbrahmacāribhiḥ
|
vidyāvadbhiḥ śritāṃ gaṃgāṃ pumānko nāma nāśrayet ||69||

70utkrāmadbhiśca yaḥ prāṇaiḥ prayata: śiṣṭasaṃmataḥ
|
cintayenmanasā gaṃgāṃ sa gatiṃ paramāṃ labhet ||70||

71na bhayebhyo bhayaṃ tasya na pāpebhyo na rājataḥ
|
ā dehapatanādgaṃgāmupāste yaḥ pumāniha ||71||

72mahāpuṇyāṃ ca gaganātpataṃtīṃ vai maheśvaraḥ
|
dadhāra śirasā gaṃgāṃ tāmeva divi sevate ||72||

73alaṃkṛtāstrayo lokāḥ pathibhirvimalaistribhiḥ
|
yastu tasyā jalaṃ sevetkṛtakṛtyaḥ pumān bhavet ||73||

74divi jyotiryathā''dityaḥ pitṝṇāṃ caiva candramāḥ
|
deveśaśca tathā nṛṇāṃ gaṃgā ca saritāṃ tathā ||74||

75mātrā pitrā sutairdārairvimuktasya dhanena vā
|
na bhaveddhi tathā duḥkhaṃ yathā gaṃgāviyogajam ||75||

76nāraṇyairneṣṭuviṣayairna sutairna dhanāgamaiḥ
|
tathā prasādo bhavati gaṃgāṃ vīkṣya yathā bhavet ||76||

77pūrṇamiṃduṃ yathā dṛṣṭvā nṛṇāṃ dṛṣṭiḥ prasīdati
|
tathā tripathagāṃ dṛṣṭvā nṛṇāṃ dṛṣṭiḥ prasīdati ||77||

78tadbhāvastadgatamanāstanniṣṭhastatparāyaṇaḥ
|
gaṃgāṃ yo'nugato bhaktyā sa tasyāḥ priyatāṃ vrajet ||78||

79bhūsthaiḥ svasthairdiviṣṭhaiśca bhūtairuccāvacairapi
|
gaṃgā vigāhyā satatametatkāryatamaṃ satām ||79||

80viśvalokeṣu puṇyatvādgaṅgāyāḥ prathitaṃ yaśaḥ
|
yatputrānsagarasyeto bhasmākhyānanayaddivam ||80||

81vāyvīrīritābhiḥ sumanoharābhirdrutābhiratyarthasamutthitābhiḥ
|
gaṃgormibhirbhānumatībhiriddhāḥ sahasraraśmipratimā bhavanti ||81||

82payasvinīṃ ghṛtinīmatyudārāṃ samṛddhinīṃ veginīṃ durvigāhyām
|
gaṃgāṃ gatvā yaiḥ śarīraṃ visṛṣṭaṃ gatā dhīrāste vibudhaiḥ samatvam ||82||

83andhān jaḍāndravyahīnāṃśca gaṃgā yaśasvinī bṛhatī viśvarūpā
|
devaiḥ sendrairmunibhirmānavaiśca niṣevitā sarvakāmairyunakti ||83||

84ūrjāvatīṃ mahāpuṇyāṃ madhumatīṃ trivartmagām
|
trilokagoptrīṃ ye gaṃgāṃ saṃśritāste divaṃ gatāḥ ||84||

85yo vatsyati drakṣyati vāpi martyastasmai prayacchanti sukhāni devāḥ
|
tadbhāvitāḥ sparśanadarśanena iṣṭāṃ gatiṃ tasya surā diśanti ||85||

86dakṣāṃ pṛśniṃ bṛhatīṃ viprakṛṣṭāṃ śivāmṛddhāṃ bhāginīṃ suprasannām
|
vibhāvarīṃ sarvabhūtapratiṣṭhāṃ gaṃgāṃ gatā ye tridivaṃ gatāste ||86||

87khyātiryasyāḥ khaṃ divaṃ gāṃ ca nityaṃ purā diśo vidiśaścāvatasthe
|
tasyā jalaṃ sevya saridvarāyā martyāḥ sarve kṛtakṛtyā bhavanti ||87||

88iyaṃ gaṃgeti niyataṃ pratiṣṭhā guhasya rukmasya ca garbhayoṣā
|
prātastrivargā ghṛtavahā vipāpmā gaṃgāyatīrṇā viyato viśvatoyā ||88||

89sutāvatīdhrasya harasya bhāryā divo bhuvaścāpi kṛtānurūpā
|
bhavyā pṛthivyāṃ bhāginī cāpi rājan gaṃgā lokānāṃ puṇyadā vai trayāṇām ||89||

90madhusravā ghṛtadhārā ghṛtārcirmahormibhiḥ śobhitā brāhmaṇaiśca
|
divaścyutā śirasā''ptā śivena gaṃgā'vanīdhrāttridivasya mātā ||90||

91yonirvariṣṭhā virajā vitanvī śayyā cirā vārivahā yaśodā
|
viśvāvatī cākṛtiriṣṭasiddhā gaṃgokṣitānāṃ bhuvanasya panthāḥ ||91||

92kṣāntyā mahyā gopane dhāraṇe ca dīptyā kṛśānostapanasya caiva
|
tulyā gaṃgā saṃmatā brāhmaṇānāṃ guhasya brahmaṇyatayā ca nityaṃ ||92||

93ṛṣiṣṭutāṃ viṣṇupadīṃ purāṇāṃ supuṇyatoyāṃ manasā'pi loke
|
sarvātmanā jāhnavīṃ ye prapannāste brahmaṇaḥ sadanaṃ saṃprayātāḥ ||93||

94lokānavekṣya jananīva putrān sarvātmanā sarvaguṇopapannān
|
tattasthānakaṃ brāhmamabhīpsamānairgaṃgā sadaivātmavaśairupāsyā ||94||

95usrāṃ puṣṭāṃ miṣatīṃ viśvabhojyāmirāvatīṃ dhāriṇīṃ bhūdharāṇām
|
śiṣṭāśrayāmamṛtāṃ brahmakāntāṃ gaṅgāṃ śrayedātmavān siddhikāmaḥ ||95||

96prasādya devān savibhūnsamastān bhagīrathastapasogreṇa gaṅgām
|
gāmānayattāmabhigamya śaśvat puṃsāṃ bhayaṃ neha cāmutra vidyāt ||96||

97udāhṛtaḥ sarvathā te guṇānāṃ mayaikadeśaḥ prasamīkṣya buddhyā
|
śaktirna me kācidihāsti vaktuṃ guṇānsarvānparimārtuṃ tathaiva ||97||

98meroḥ samudrasya ca sarvayatnaiḥ saṃkhyopalānāmudakasya vāpi
|
śakyaṃ vaktuṃ neha gaṃgājalānāṃ guṇākhyānaṃ parimātuṃ tathaiva ||98||

99tasmādetānparayā śraddhayoktān guṇānsarvān jāhnavīyān sadaiva
|
bhavedvācā manasā karmaṇā ca bhaktyā yuktaḥ śraddhayā śraddadhānaḥ ||99||

100lokānimāṃstrīnyaśasā vitatya siddhiṃ prāpya mahatīṃ tāṃ durāpām
|
gaṅgākṛtānacireṇaiva lokān yatheṣṭamiṣṭān vihariṣyasi tvam ||100||

101tava mama ca guṇairmahānubhāvā juṣatu matiṃ satataṃ svadharmayuktaiḥ
|
abhimatajanavatsalā hi gaṃgā jagati punāti sukhaiśca bhaktimantam ||101||

102bhīṣma uvāca|
iti paramamatirguṇānaśeṣān śilarataye tripathānuyogarūpān
|
bahuvidhamanuśāsya tathyarūpān gaganatalaṃ dyutimān viveśa siddhaḥ ||102||

103śilavṛttistu siddhasya vākyaiḥ saṃbodhitastadā
|
gaṃgāmupāsya vidhivatsiddhiṃ prāpa sudurlabhām ||103||

104tathā tvamapi kaunteya bhaktyā paramayā yutaḥ
|
gaṅgāmabhyehi satataṃ prāpsyase siddhimuttamām ||104||

105vaiśampāyana uvāca|
śrutvetihāsaṃ bhīṣmoktaṃ gaṃgāyāḥ stavasaṃyutam
|
yudhiṣṭhiraḥ parāṃ prītimagacchadbhrātṛbhiḥ saha ||105||

106itihāsamimaṃ puṇyaṃ śṛṇuyādyaḥ paṭheta vā
|
gaṃgāyāḥ stavasaṃyuktaṃ sa mucyetasarvakilbiṣaiḥ ||106||
