Mahābhārata 6.10 (Bhīṣmaparvan)

1dhṛtarāṣṭra uvāca|
bhāratasyāsya varṣasya tathā haimavatasya ca
|
pramāṇamāyuṣaḥ sūta balaṃ cāpi śubhāśubham ||1||

2anāgatamatikrāntaṃ vartamānaṃ ca sañjaya
|
ācakṣva me vistareṇa harivarṣaṃ tathaiva ca ||2||

3sañjaya uvāca|
catvāri bhārate varṣe yugāni bharatarṣabha
|
kṛtaṃ tretā dvāparaṃ ca tiṣyaṃ ca kuruvardhana ||3||

4pūrvaṃ kṛtayugaṃ nāma tatasretāyugaṃ prabho
|
saṅkṣepāddvāparasyātha tatastiṣyaṃ pravartate ||4||

5catvāri tu sahasrāṇi varṣāṇāṃ kurusattama
|
āyuḥsaṅkhyā kṛtayuge saṅkhyātā rājasattama ||5||

6tathā trīṇi sahasrāṇi tretāyāṃ manujādhipa
|
dve sahasre dvāpare tu bhuvi tiṣṭhanti sāmpratam ||6||

7na pramāṇasthitirhyasti tiṣye'sminbharatarṣabha
|
garbhasthāśca mriyante'tra tathā jātā mriyanti ca ||7||

8mahābalā mahāsatvāḥ prajñāguṇasamanvitāḥ
|
prajāyante ca jātāśca śataśo'tha sahasraśaḥ ||8||

9jātāḥ kṛtayuge rājandhaninaḥ prayadarśanāḥ
|
prajāyante ca jātāśca munayo vai tapodhanāḥ ||9||

10mahotsāhā mahātmāno dhārmikāḥ satyavādinaḥ
|
priyadarśanā vapuṣmanto mahāvīryā dhanurdharāḥ ||10||

11varārhā yudhi jāyante kṣatriyāḥ śūrasattamāḥ
|
tretāyāṃ kṣatriyā rājansarve vai cakravartinaḥ ||11||

12sarvavarṇāśca jāyante sadā caiva ca dvāpare
|
mahotsāhā vīryavantaḥ parasparajayaiṣiṇaḥ ||12||

13tejasālpena saṃyuktāḥ krodhanāḥ puruṣā nṛpa
|
lubdhā anṛtakāścaiva tiṣye jāyanti bhārata ||13||

14īrṣyā mānastathā krodho māyā'sūyā tathaiva ca
|
tiṣye bhavati bhūtānāṃ rāgo lobhaśca bhārata ||14||

15saṅkṣepo vartate rājandvāpare'sminnarādhipa
|
guṇottaraṃ haimavataṃ harivarṣaṃ tataḥ param ||15||
