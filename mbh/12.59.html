Mahābhārata 12.59 (Śāntiparvan)

1vaiśampāyana uvāca|
tataḥ kalyaṃ samutthāya kṛtapūrvāhṇikakriyāḥ
|
yayuste nagarākārai rathaiḥ pāṇḍavayādavāḥ||1||

2pratipadya kurukṣetraṃ bhīṣmamāsādya cānagha
|
sukhāṃ na rajanīṃ pṛṣṭvā gāṅgeyaṃ rathināṃ varam||2||

3vyāsadīnabhivādyarṣīnsarvaistaiścābhinanditāḥ
|
niṣedurabhito bhīṣmaṃ parivāryaṃ samantataḥ||3||

4tato rājā mahātejā dharmarājo yudhiṣṭhiraḥ
|
abravītprāñjalirbhīṣmaṃ pratipūjya yathāvidhi||4||

5yudhiṣṭhira uvāca|
ya eṣa rājan rājeti śabdaścarati bhārata
|
kathameṣa samutpannastanme brūhi paraṃtapa||5||

6tulyapāṇibhujagrīvastulyabuddhīndriyātmakaḥ
|
tulyaduḥkhasukhātmā ca tulyapṛṣṭhamukhodaraḥ||6||

7tulyaśukrāsthimajjā ca tulyamāṃsāsṛgeva ca
|
niḥśvāsocchvāsatulyaśca tulyaprāṇaśarīravān||7||

8samānajanmamaraṇaḥ samaḥ sarvairguṇairnṛṇām
|
viśiṣṭabuddhīn śūrāṃśca kathameko'dhitiṣṭhati||8||

9kathameko mahīṃ kṛtsnāṃ śūravīrāryasaṃkulām
|
rakṣatyapi ca lokasya prasādamabhivāñchati||9||

10ekasya tu prasādena kṛtsno lokaḥ prasīdati
|
vyākule cākulaḥ sarvo bhavatīti viniścayaḥ||10||

11etadicchāmyahaṃ śrotuṃ tattvena bharatarṣabha
|
kṛtsnaṃ tanme yathātattvaṃ prabrūhi vadatāṃ vara||11||

12naitatkāraṇamalpaṃ hi bhaviṣyati viśāmpate
|
yadekasmin jagatsarvaṃ devavadyāti saṃnatim||12||

13bhīṣma uvāca|
niyatastvaṃ naravyāghra śṛṇu sarvamaśeṣataḥ
|
yathā rājyaṃ samutpannamādau kṛtayuge'bhavat||13||

14na vai rājyaṃ na rājā''sīnna ca daṇḍo na dāṇḍikaḥ
|
dharmeṇaiva prajāḥ sarvā rakṣanti sma parasparam||14||

15pālyamānāstathānyo'nyaṃ narā dharmeṇa bhārata
|
khedaṃ paramupājagmustatastānmoha āviśat||15||

16te mohavaśamāpannā manujā manujarṣabha
|
pratipattivimohācca dharmasteṣāmanīnaśat||16||

17naṣṭāyāṃ pratipattau ca mohavaśyā narāstadā
|
lobhasya vaśamāpannāḥ sarve bharatasattama||17||

18aprāptasyābhimarśaṃ tu kurvanto manujāstataḥ
|
kāmo nāmāparastatra pratyapadyata vai prabho||18||

19tāṃstu kāmavaśaṃ prāptān rāgo nāmabhisaṃspṛśat
|
raktāśca nābhyajānanta kāryākārye yudhiṣṭhira||19||

20agamyāgamanaṃ caiva vācyāvācyaṃ tathaiva ca
|
bhakṣyābhakṣyaṃ ca rājendra doṣādoṣaṃ ca nātyajan||20||

21viplute naraloke vai brahma caiva nanāśa ha
|
nāśācca brahmaṇo rājandharmo nāśamathāgamat||21||

22naṣṭe brahmaṇi dharme ca devāṃstrāsaḥ samāviśat
|
te trastā naraśārdūla brahmāṇaṃ śaraṇaṃ yayuḥ||22||

23prasādya bhagavantaṃ te devaṃ lokapitāmaham
|
ūcuḥ prāñjalayaḥ sarve duḥkhavegasamāhatāḥ||23||

24bhagavannaralokasthaṃ grastaṃ brahma sanātanam
|
lobhamohādibhirbhāvaistato no bhayamāviśat||24||

25brahmaṇaśca praṇāśena dharmo vyanaśadīśvara
|
tataḥ sma samatāṃ yātā martyaistribhuvaneśvara||25||

26adho hi varṣamasmākaṃ narāstūrdhvapravarṣiṇaḥ
|
kriyāvyuparamātteṣāṃ tato gacchāma saṃśayam||26||

27atra niḥśreyasaṃ yannastaddhyāyasva pitāmaha
|
tvatprabhāvasamuttho'sau svabhāvo no vinaśyati||27||

28tānuvāca surānsarvān svayambhūrbhagavāṃstataḥ
|
śreyo'haṃ cintayiṣyāmi vyetu vo bhīḥ surarṣabhāḥ||28||

29tato'dhyāyasahasrāṇāṃ śataṃ cakre svabuddhijam
|
yatra dharmastathaivārthaḥ kāmaścaivābhivarṇitaḥ||29||

30trivarga iti vikhyāto gaṇa eṣa svayambhuvā
|
caturtho mokṣa ityeva pṛthagarthaḥ pṛthagguṇaḥ||30||

31mokṣasyāsti trivargo'nyaḥ proktaḥ sattvaṃ rajastamaḥ
|
sthānaṃ vṛddhiḥ kṣayaścaiva trivargaścaiva daṇḍajaḥ||31||

32ātmā deśaśca kālaścāpyupāyāḥ kṛtyameva ca
|
sahāyāḥ kāraṇaṃ caiva ṣaḍvargo nītijaḥ smṛtaḥ||32||

33trayī cānvīkṣikī caiva vārtā ca bharatarṣabha
|
daṇḍanītiśca vipulā vidyāstatra nidarśitāḥ||33||

34amātyarakṣā praṇidhī rājaputrasya lakṣaṇam
|
cāraśca vividhopāyaḥ praṇidheyaḥ pṛthagvidhaḥ||34||

35sāmabhedaḥ pradānaṃ ca tato daṇḍaśca pārthiva
|
upekṣā pañcamī cātra kātsnyena samudāhṛtā||35||

36mantraśca varṇitaḥ kṛtsnastathā bhedārtha eva ca
|
vibhramaścaiva mantrasya siddhyasiddhyośca yat phalam||36||

37sandhiśca trividhābhikhyo hīno madhyastathottamaḥ
|
bhayasatkāravittākhyaṃ kārtsnyena parivarṇitam||37||

38yātrā kālāśca catvārastrivargasya ca vistaraḥ
|
vijayo dharmayuktaśca tathārthavijayaśca ha||38||

39āsuraścaiva vijayastathā kārtsnyena varṇitaḥ
|
lakṣaṇaṃ pañcavargasya trividhaṃ cātra varṇitam||39||

40prakāśaścāprakāśaśca daṇḍo'tha pariśabditaḥ
|
prakāśo'vidhastatra guhyaśca bahuvistaraḥ||40||

41rathā nāgā hayāścaiva pādātāścaiva pāṇḍava
|
viṣṭirnāvaścarāścaiva deśikā iti cāṣṭamam||41||

42aṅgānyetāni kauravya prakāśāni balasya tu
|
jaṅgamājaṅgamāścoktāścūrṇayogā viṣādayaḥ||42||

43sparśe cābhyavahārye cāpyupāṃśurvividhaḥ smṛtaḥ
|
arirmitra udāsīna ityete'pyanuvarṇitāḥ||43||

44kṛtsnā mārgaguṇāścaiva tathā bhūmiguṇāśca hā
|
ātmarakṣaṇamāśvāsaḥ sargāṇāṃ cānvavekṣaṇam||44||

45kalpanā vividhāścāpi nṛ-nāga-ratha-vājinām
|
vyūhāśca vividhābhikhyā vicitraṃ yuddhakauśalam||45||

46utpātāśca nipātāśca suyuddhaṃ supalāyitam
|
śastrāṇāṃ pālanaṃ jñānaṃ tathaiva bharatarṣabha||46||

47balavyasanamuktaṃ ca tathaiva balaharṣaṇam
|
pīḍā cāpadakālaśca pattijñānaṃ ca pāṇḍava||47||

48tathākhyātavidhānaṃ ca yogaḥ saṃcāra eva ca
|
corairāṭavikaiścograiḥ pararāṣṭrasya pīḍanam||48||

49agnidairgaradaiścaiva pratirūpakakārakaiḥ
|
śreṇimukhyopajāpena vīrudhaśchedanena ca||49||

50dūṣaṇena ca nāgānāmātaṅkajananena ca
|
ārādhanena bhaktasya pratyayopārjanena ca||50||

51saptāṅgasya ca rājyasya hrāsavṛddhisamañjasam
|
dūtasāmarthyasaṃyogāt sarāṣṭrasya vivardhanam||51||

52arimadhyasthamitrāṇāṃ samyak coktaṃ prapañcanam
|
avamardaḥ pratīghātastathaiva ca balīyasām||52||

53vyavahāraḥ susūkṣmaśca tathā kaṇṭakaśodhanam
|
śramo vyāyāmayogaśca tyāgo dravyasya saṃgrahaḥ||53||

54abhṛtānāṃ ca bharaṇaṃ bhṛtānāṃ cānvavekṣaṇam
|
arthasya kāle dānaṃ ca vyasane cāprasaṅgitā||54||

55tathā rājaguṇāścaiva senāpatiguṇāśca ha
|
kāraṇaṃ ca trivargasya guṇadoṣāstathaiva ca||55||

56duśceṣṭitaṃ ca vividhaṃ vṛttiścaivānuvartinām
|
śaṅkitatvaṃ ca sarvasya pramādasya ca varjanam||56||

57alabdhalābho labdhasya tathaiva ca vivardhanam
|
pradānaṃ ca vivṛddhasya pātrebhyo vidhivattataḥ||57||

58visargo'rthasya dharmārthaṃ kāmahaitukamucyate
|
caturthaṃ vyasanāghāte tathaivātrānuvarṇitam||58||

59krodhajāni tathogrāṇi kāmajāni tathaiva ca
|
daśoktāni kuruśreṣṭha vyasanānyatra caiva ha||59||

60mṛgayā'kṣāstathā pānaṃ striyaśca bharatarṣabha
|
kāmajānyāhurācāryāḥ proktānīha svayambhuvā||60||

61vākpāruṣyaṃ tathogratvaṃ daṇḍapāruṣyameva ca
|
ātmano nigrahastyāgo hyarthadūṣaṇameva ca||61||

62yantrāṇi vividhānyeva kriyāsteṣāṃ ca varṇitāḥ
|
avamardaḥ pratīghātaḥ ketanānāṃ ca bhañjanam||62||

63caityadrumāvamardaśca rodhaḥ karmānuśāsanam
|
apaskaro'tha vasanaṃ tathopāyāśca varṇitāḥ||63||

64paṇavānakaśaṅkhānāṃ bherīṇāṃ ca yudhiṣṭhira
|
upārjanaṃ ca dravyāṇāṃ parimardaśca tāni ṣaṭ||64||

65labdhasya ca praśamanaṃ satāṃ caivābhipūjanam
|
vidvadbhirekībhāvaśca dānahomavidhijñatā||65||

66maṅgalālambhanaṃ caiva śarīrasya pratikriyā
|
āhārayojanaṃ caiva nityamāstikyameva ca||66||

67ekena ca yathottheyaṃ satyatvaṃ madhurā giraḥ
|
utsavānāṃ samājānāṃ kriyāḥ ketanajāstathā||67||

68pratyakṣāśca parokṣāśca sarvādhikaraṇeṣvathā
|
vṛtterbharataśārdūla nityaṃ caivānvavekṣaṇam||68||

69adaṇḍyatvaṃ ca viprāṇāṃ yuktyā daṇḍanipātanam
|
anujīvī svajātibhyo guṇebhyaśca samudbhavaḥ||69||

70rakṣaṇaṃ caiva paurāṇāṃ rāṣṭrasya ca vivardhanam
|
maṇḍalasthā ca yā cintā rājandvādaśarājikā||70||

71dvāsaptatividhā caiva śarīrasya pratikriyā
|
deśajātikulānāṃ ca dharmāḥ samanuvarṇitāḥ||71||

72dharmaścārthaśca kāmaśca mokṣaścātrānuvarṇitāḥ
|
upāyāścārthalipsā ca vividhā bhūridakṣiṇa||72||

73mūlakarmakriyā cātra māyāyogaśca varṇitaḥ
|
dūṣaṇaṃ strotasāṃ caiva varṇitaṃ cāsthirāmbhasām||73||

74yairyairupāyairlokastu na caledāryavartmanaḥ
|
tat sarvaṃ rājaśārdūla nītiśāstre'bhivarṇitam||74||

75etatkṛtvā śubhaṃ śāstraṃ tataḥ sa bhagavānprabhuḥ
|
devānuvāca saṃhṛṣṭaḥ sarvāñchakrapurogamān||75||

76upakārāya lokasya trivargasthāpanāya ca
|
navanītaṃ sarasvatyā buddhireṣā prabhāvitā||76||

77daṇḍena sahitā hyeṣā lokarakṣaṇakārikā
|
nigrahānugraharatā lokānanucariṣyati||77||

78daṇḍena nīyate cedaṃ daṇḍaṃ nayati vā punaḥ
|
daṇḍanītiriti khyātā trīllokānabhivartate||78||

79ṣāḍguṇyaguṇasāraiṣā sthāsyatyagre mahātmasu
|
dharmārthakāmamokṣāśca sakalā hyatra śabditāḥ||79||

80tatastāṃ bhagavānnītiṃ pūrvaṃ jagrāha śaṅkaraḥ
|
bahurūpo viśālākṣaḥ śivaḥ sthāṇurumāpatiḥ||80||

81prajānāmāyuṣo hrāsaṃ vijñāya bhagavāñśivaḥ
|
sañcikṣepa tataḥ śāstraṃ mahāstraṃ brahmaṇā kṛtam||81||

82vaiśālākṣamiti proktaṃ tadindraḥ pratyapadyapta
|
daśādhyāyasahasrāṇi subrahmaṇyo mahātapāḥ||82||

83bhagavānapi tacchāstraṃ saṃcikṣepa puraṃdaraḥ
|
sahasraiḥ pañcabhistāta yaduktaṃ bāhudantakam||83||

84adhyāyānāṃ sahasraistu tribhireva bṛhaspatiḥ
|
saṃcikṣepeśvaro buddhyā bārhaspatyaṃ taducyate||84||

85adhyāyānāṃ sahasreṇa kāvyaḥ saṃkṣepamabravīt
|
tacchāstramamitaprajño yogācāryo mahāyaśāḥ||85||

86evaṃ lokānurodhena śāstrametanmaharṣibhiḥ
|
saṃkṣiptamāyurvijñāya martyānāṃ hrāsameva ca||86||

87atha devāḥ samāgamya viṣṇumūcuḥ prajāpatim
|
eko yo'rhati martyebhyaḥ śreṣṭhyaṃ vai taṃ samādiśa||87||

88tataḥ saṃcintya bhagavāndevo nārāyaṇaḥ prabhuḥ
|
taijasaṃ vai virajasaṃ so'sṛjanmānasaṃ sutam||88||

89virajāstu mahābhāgaḥ prabhutvaṃ bhuvi naicchata
|
nyāsāyaivābhavadbuddhiḥ praṇītā tasya pāṇḍava||89||

90kīrtimāṃstasya putro'bhūt so'pi pañcātigo'bhavat
|
kardamastasya tu sutaḥ so'pyatapyanmahattapaḥ||90||

91prajāpateḥ kardamasya tvanaṅgo nāma vai sutaḥ
|
prajā rakṣayitā sādhurdaṇḍanītiviśāradaḥ||91||

92anaṅgaputro'tibalo nītimānabhigamya vai
|
pratipede mahārājyamathendriyavaśo'bhavat||92||

93mṛtyostu duhitā rājansunīthā nāma mānasī
|
prakhyātā triṣu lokeṣu yā'sau venamajījanat||93||

94taṃ prajāsu vidharmāṇaṃ rāgadveṣavaśānugam
|
mantrapūtaiḥ kuśairjaghnurṛṣayo brahmavādinaḥ||94||

95mamanthurdakṣiṇaṃ corumṛṣayastasya mantrataḥ
|
tato'sya vikṛto jajñe hrasvāṅgaḥ puruṣo bhuvi||95||

96dagdhasthūṇāpratīkāśo raktākṣaḥ kṛṣṇamūrdhajaḥ
|
niṣīdetyevamūcustamṛṣayo brahmavādinaḥ||96||

97tasmānniṣādāḥ sambhūtāḥ krūrāḥ śailavanāśrayāḥ
|
ye cānye vindhyanilayā mlecchāḥ śatasahasraśaḥ||97||

98bhūyo'sya dakṣiṇaṃ pāṇiṃ mamanthuste maharṣayaḥ
|
tataḥ puruṣa utpanno rūpeṇendra ivāparaḥ||98||

99kavacī baddhanistriṃśaḥ saśaraḥ saśarāsanaḥ
|
vedavedāṅgaviccaiva dhanurvede ca pāragaḥ||99||

100taṃ daṇḍanītiḥ sakalā śritā rājan narottamam
|
tatastu prāñjalirvainyo maharṣīṃstānuvāca ha||100||

101susūkṣmā me samutpannā buddhirdharmārthadarśinī
|
anayā kiṃ mayā kāryaṃ tanme tattvena śaṃsata||101||

102yanmāṃ bhavanto vakṣyanti kāryamarthasamanvitam
|
tadahaṃ vai kariṣyāmi nātra kāryā vicāraṇā||102||

103tamūcustatra devāste te caiva paramarṣayaḥ
|
niyato yatra dharmo vai tamaśaṅkaḥ samācara||103||

104priyāpriye parityajya samaḥ sarveṣu jantuṣu
|
kāmaṃ krodhaṃ ca lobhaṃ ca mānaṃ cotsṛjya dūrataḥ||104||

105yaśca dharmātpravicalelloke kaścana mānavaḥ
|
nigrāhyaste svabāhubhyāṃ śaśvaddharmamavekṣatā||105||

106pratijñāṃ cādhirohasva manasā, karmaṇā girā
|
pālayiṣyāmyahaṃ bhaumaṃ brahma ityeva cāsakṛta||106||

107yaścātra dharmo nityokto daṇḍanītivyapāśrayaḥ
|
tamaśaṅkaḥ kariṣyāmi svavaśo na kadācana||107||

108adaṇḍyā me dvijāśceti pratijānīhi he vibho
|
lokaṃ ca saṃkarātkṛtsnaṃ trāsā'smīti paraṃtapa||108||

109vainyastatastānuvāca devānṛṣipurogamān
|
brāhmaṇā me mahābhāgā namasyāḥ puruṣarṣabhaḥ||109||

110evamastviti vainyastu tairukto brahmavādibhiḥ
|
purodhāścābhavattasya śukro brahmamayo nidhiḥ||110||

111mantriṇo vālakhilyāśca sārasvatyo gaṇastathā
|
maharṣirbhagavāngargastasya sāṃvatsaro'bhavat||111||

112ātmanā'ṣṭama ityeva śrutireṣā parā nṛṣu
|
utpannau bandinau cāsya tatpūrvau sūtamāgadhau||112||

113tayoḥ prīto dadau rājā pṛthurvainyaḥ pratāpavān
|
anūpadeśaṃ sūtāya magadhaṃ māgadhāya ca||113||

114samatāṃ vasudhāyāśca sa samyagudapādayat
|
vaiṣamyaṃ hi paraṃ bhūmerāsīditi ca naḥ śrutam||114||

115manvantareṣu sarveṣu viṣamā jāyate mahī
|
ujjahāra tato vainyaḥ śilājālānsamantataḥ||115||

116dhanuṣkoṭyā mahārāja tena śailā vivardhitāḥ
|
sa viṣṇunā ca devena śakreṇa vibudhaiḥ saha||116||

117ṛṣibhiśca prajāpālairbrāhmaṇaiścābhiṣecitaḥ
|
taṃ sākṣātpṛthivī bheje ratnānyādāya pāṇḍava||117||

118sāgaraḥ saritāṃ bhartā himavāṃścācalottamaḥ
|
śakraśca dhanamakṣayyaṃ prādāttasmai yudhiṣṭhira||118||

119rukmaṃ cāpi mahāmeruḥ svayaṃ kanakaparvataḥ
|
yakṣarākṣasabhartā ca bhagavānnaravāhanaḥ||119||

120dharme, cārthe, ca kāme, ca samarthaṃ pradadau dhanam
|
hayā rathāśca nāgāśca koṭiśaḥ puruṣāstathā||120||

121prādurbabhūvurvainyasya cintanādeva pāṇḍava
|
na jarā na ca durbhikṣaṃ nādhayo vyādhayastathā||121||

122sarīsṛpebhyaḥ stenebhyo na cānyonyātkadācana
|
bhayamutpadyate tatra tasya rājño'bhirakṣaṇāt||122||

123āpastastambhire cāsya samudramabhiyāsyataḥ
|
parvatāśca dadurmārgaṃ dhvajabhaṅgaśca nābhavat||123||

124teneyaṃ pṛthivī dugdhā sasyāni daśa sapta ca
|
yakṣarākṣasanāgaiścāpīpsitaṃ yasya yasya yat||124||

125tena dharmottasthāyaṃ kṛto loko mahātmanā
|
raṃjitāśca prajāḥ sarvāstena rājeti śabdyate||125||

126brāhmaṇānāṃ kṣatatrāṇāttataḥ kṣatriya ucyate
|
prathitā dharmataśceyaṃ pṛthivī bahubhiḥ smṛtā||126||

127sthāpanaṃ cākarodviṣṇuḥ svayameva sanātanaḥ
|
nātivartiṣyate kaścidrājaṃstvāmiti bhārata||127||

128tapasā bhagavānviṣṇurāviveśa ca bhūmipam
|
devavannaradevānāṃ namate yaṃ jagannṛpam||128||

129daṇḍanītyā ca satataṃ rakṣitavyaṃ nareśvara
|
nādharṣayettathā kaściccāraniṣpandadarśanāt||129||

130śubhaṃ hi karma rājendra śubhatvāyopakalpate
|
ātmanā kāraṇaiścaiva samasyeha mahīkṣitaḥ||130||

131ko heturyadvaśe tiṣṭhelloko daivādṛte guṇāt
|
viṣṇorlalāṭātkamalaṃ sauvarṇamabhavattadā||131||

132śrīḥ sambhūtā yato devī patnī dharmasya dhīmataḥ
|
śriyaḥ sakāśādarthaśca jāto dharmeṇa pāṇḍava||132||

133atha dharmastathaivārthaḥ śrīśca rājye pratiṣṭhitā
|
sukṛtasya kṣayāccaiva svarlokādetya medinīm||133||

134pārthivo jāyate tāta daṇḍanītiviśāradaḥ
|
mahattvena ca saṃyukto vaiṣṇavena naro bhuvi||134||

135buddhyā bhavati saṃyukto māhātmyaṃ cādhigacchati
|
sthāpitaṃ ca tato devairna kaścidativartate
|
tiṣṭhatyekasya ca vaśe taṃ cedaṃ na vidhīyate||135||

136śubhaṃ hi karma rājendra śubhatvāyopakalpate
|
tulyasyaikasya yasyāyaṃ loko vacasi tiṣṭhate||136||

137yo'sya vai mukhamadrākṣīt saumyaṃ so'sya vaśānugaḥ
|
subhagaṃ cārthavantaṃ ca rūpavantaṃ ca paśyati||137||

138mahattvāttasya daṇḍasya nītirvispaṣṭalakṣaṇā
|
nayacāraśca vipulo yena sarvamidaṃ tatam||138||

139āgamaśca purāṇānāṃ maharṣīṇāṃ ca sambhavaḥ
|
tīrthavaṃśaśca vaṃśaśca nakṣatrāṇāṃ yudhiṣṭhira||139||

140sakalaṃ cāturāśramyaṃ cāturhotraṃ tathaiva ca
|
cāturvarṇyaṃ tathaivātra cāturvidyaṃ ca kīrtitam||140||

141itihāsāśca vedāśca nyāyaḥ kṛtsnaśca varṇitaḥ
|
tapo jñānamahiṃsā ca satyāsatyena yaḥ paraḥ||141||

142vṛddhopasevā dānaṃ ca śaucamutthānameva ca
|
sarvabhūtānukampā ca sarvamatropavarṇitam||142||

143bhuvi cādhogataṃ yacca tacca sarvaṃ samarpitam
|
tasminpaitāmahe śāstre pāṇḍavaitanna saṃśayaḥ||143||

144tato jagati rājendra satataṃ śabditaṃ budhaiḥ
|
devāśca naradevāśca tulyā iti viśāmpate||144||

145etatte sarvamākhyātaṃ mahattvaṃ prati rājasu
|
kārtsnyena bharataśreṣṭha kimanyadiha vartate||145||
