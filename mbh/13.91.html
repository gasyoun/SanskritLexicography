Mahābhārata 13.91 (Anuśāsanaparvan)

1yudhiṣṭhira uvāca|
kena saṅkalpitaṃ śrāddhaṃ kasminkāle kimātmakam
|
bhṛgvaṅgirasike kāle muninā katareṇa vā ||1||

2kāni śrāddhāni varjyāni kāni mūlaphalāni ca
|
dhānyajātyaśca kā varjyāstanme brūhi pitāmaha ||2||

3bhīṣma uvāca|
yathā śrāddhaṃ saṃpravṛttaṃ yasminkāle sadātmakam
|
yena saṅkalpitaṃ caiva tanme śṛṇu janādhipa ||3||

4svāyaṃbhuvo'triḥ kauravya paramarṣiḥ pratāpavān
|
tasya vaṃśe mahārāja dattātreya iti smṛtaḥ ||4||

5dattātreyasya putro'bhūnnimirnāma tapodhanaḥ
|
nimeścāpyabhavatputraḥ śrīmānnāma śriyā vṛtaḥ ||5||

6pūrṇe varṣasahasrānte sa kṛtvā duṣkaraṃ tapaḥ
|
kāladharmaparītātmā nidhanaṃ samupāgataḥ ||6||

7nimistu kṛtvā śaucāni vidhidṛṣṭena karmaṇā
|
saṃtāpamagamattīvraṃ putraśokaparāyaṇaḥ ||7||

8atha kṛtvopahāryāṇi caturdaśyāṃ mahāmatiḥ
|
tameva gaṇayanśokaṃ virātre pratyabudhyata ||8||

9tasyāsītpratibuddhasya śokena vyathitātmanaḥ
|
manaḥ saṃvṛtya viṣaye buddhirvistāragāminī ||9||

10tataḥ sañcintayāmāsa śrāddhakalpaṃ samāhitaḥ
|
yāni tasyaiva bhojyāni mūlāni ca phalāni ca ||10||

11uktāni yāni cānnāni yāni ceṣṭāni tasya ha
|
tāni sarvāṇi manasā viniścitya tapodhanaḥ ||11||

12amāvāsyāṃ mahāprājño viprānānāyya pūjitān
|
dakṣiṇāvartikāḥ sarvā vṛsīḥ svayamathākarot||12||

13saptaviprāṃstato bhojye yugapatsamupānayat
|
ṛte ca lavaṇaṃ bhojyaṃ śyāmākānnaṃ dadau prabhuḥ ||13||

14dakṣiṇāgrāstato darbhā viṣṭareṣu niveśitāḥ
|
pāda‌yoścaiva viprāṇāṃ ye tvannamupabhuñjate ||14||

15kṛtvā ca dakṣiṇāgrānvai darbhān sa prayataḥ śuciḥ
|
prada‌dau śrīmataḥ piṇḍānnāmagotramudāharan ||15||

16tatkṛtvā sa muniśreṣṭho dharmasaṅkaramātmanaḥ
|
paścāttāpena mahatā tapyamānobhyacintayat ||16||

17akṛtaṃ munibhiḥ pūrvaṃ kiṃ mayeda‌manuṣṭhitam
|
kathaṃ nu śāpena na māṃ daheyurbāhmaṇā iti ||17||

18tataḥ sañcintayāmāsa vaṃśakartāramātmanaḥ
|
dhyātamātrastathā cātrirājagāma tapodhanaḥ ||18||

19athātristaṃ tathā dṛṣṭvā putraśokena karṣitam
|
bhṛśamāśvāsayāmāsa vāgbhiriṣṭābhiravyayaḥ ||19||

20nime saṃkalpitaste'yaṃ pitṛyajñastapodhana
|
mā tebhūdbhīḥ pūrvadṛṣṭo dharmoyaṃ brahmaṇā svayam ||20||

21soyaṃ svayaṃbhuvihito dharmaḥ saṃkalpitastvayā
|
ṛte svayaṃbhuvaḥ konyaḥ śrāddheyaṃ vidhimāharet ||21||

22athākhyāsyāmi te putra śrāddheyaṃ vidhimuttamam
|
svayaṃbhuvihitaṃ putra tatkuruṣva nibodha me ||22||

23kṛtvā'gnaukaraṇaṃ pūrvaṃ mantrapūrvaṃ tapodhana
|
tato'gnaye'tha somāya varuṇāya ca nityaśaḥ ||23||

24viśvedevāśca ye nityaṃ pitṛbhiḥ saha gocarāḥ
|
tebhyaḥ saṃkalpitā bhāgāḥ svayameva svayaṃbhuvā ||24||

25stotavyā ceha pṛthivī nivāpasyeha dhāriṇī
|
vaiṣṇavī kāśyapī ceti tathaivehākṣayeti ca ||25||

26uda‌kānayane caiva stotavyo varuṇo vibhuḥ
|
tato'gniścaiva somaśca āpyāyyāviha te'nagha ||26||

27devāstu pitaro nāma nirmitā ye svayaṃbhuvā
|
uṣṇapā ye mahābhāgāsteṣāṃ bhāgaḥ prakalpitaḥ ||27||

28te śrāddhenārcyamānāvai vimucyante ha kilbiṣāt
|
saptakaḥ pitṛvaṃśastu pūrvadṛṣṭaḥ svayaṃbhuvā ||28||

29viśve cāgnimukhā devāḥ saṃkhyātāḥ pūrvameva te
|
teṣāṃ nāmāni vakṣyāmi bhāgārhāṇāṃ mahātmanām ||29||

30balaṃ dhṛtirvipāpmā ca puṇyakṛtpāvanastathā
|
pārṣṇikṣemā samūhaśca divyasānustathaiva ca ||30||

31vivasvānvīryavān hrīmānkīrtimānkṛta eva ca
|
jitātmā munivīryaśca dīptaromā bhayaṅkaraḥ ||31||

32anukarmā pratītaśca pradātā'pyaṃśumāṃstathā
|
śailābhaḥ paramakrodhī dhīroṣṇī bhūpatistathā ||32||

33srajo vajrī varī caiva viśvedevāḥ sanātanāḥ
|
vidyudvarcāḥ somavarcāḥ sūryaśrīśceti nāmataḥ ||33||

34somapaḥ sūryasāvitro dattātmā puṇḍarīyakaḥ
|
uṣṇīnāmo nabhodaśca viśvāyurdīptireva ca ||34||

35camūharaḥ sureśaśca vyomāriḥ śaṃkaro bhavaḥ
|
īśaḥ kartā kṛtirdakṣo bhuvano divyakarmakṛt||35||

36gaṇitaḥ pañcavīryaśca ādityo raśmivāṃstathā
|
saptakṛtsomavarcāśca viśvakṛtkavireva ca ||36||

37anugoptā sugoptā ca naptā ceśvara eva ca
|
kīrtitāste mahābhāgāḥ kālasya gatigocarāḥ ||37||

38aśrāddheyāni dhānyāni kodravāḥ pulakāstathā
|
hiṃgudravyeṣu śākeṣu palāṇḍuṃ laśunaṃ tathā ||38||

39saubhāñjanaḥ kovidārastathā gṛñjanakādayaḥ
|
kūṣmāṇḍajātyalābuṃ ca kṛṣṇaṃ lavaṇameva ca ||39||

40grāmyavārāhamāṃsaṃ ca yaccaivāprokṣitaṃ bhavet
|
kṛṣṇājājī viḍaścaiva śītapākī tathaiva ca
|
aṅkurādyāstathā varjyā iha śrṛṃgāṭakāni ca ||40||

41varjayellavaṇaṃ sarvaṃ tathā jambūphalāni ca
|
avakṣutāvaruditaṃ tathā śrāddhe ca varjayet ||41||

42nivāpe havyakavye yā garhitaṃ ca sudarśanam
|
pitaraśca hi devāśca nābhinandanti taddhaviḥ ||42||

43cāṇḍālaśvapacau varjyau nivāpe samupasthite
|
kāṣāyavāsāḥ kuṣṭhī vā patito brahmahā'pi vā ||43||

44saṃkīrṇayonirvipraśca sambandhī patitaśca yaḥ
|
varjanīyā budhairete nivāpe sasupasthite ||44||

45ityevamuktvā bhagavānsvavaṃśyaṃ tamṛṣiṃ purā
|
pitāmahasabhāṃ divyāṃ jagāmātristapodhanaḥ ||45||
