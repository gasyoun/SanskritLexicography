Mahābhārata 7.95 (Droṇaparvan)

1sañjaya uvāca|
praviṣṭayormahārāja pārthavārṣṇeyayo raṇe
|
duryodhane prayāte ca pṛṣṭhataḥ puruṣarṣabhe ||1||

2javenābhyadravaddroṇaṃ mahatā niḥsvanena ca
|
pāṇḍavāḥ somakaiḥ sārdhaṃ tato yuddhamavartata ||2||

3tadyuddhamabhavat tīvraṃ tumulaṃ lomaharṣaṇam
|
kurūṇāṃ pāṇḍavānāṃ ca vyūhasya purato'dbhutam ||3||

4rājan kadācinnāsmābhirdṛṣṭaṃ tādṛṅna ca śrutam
|
yādṛṅmadhyagate sūrye yuddhamāsīdviśāmpate ||4||

5dhṛṣṭadyumnamukhāḥ pārthā vyūḍhānīkāḥ prahāriṇaḥ
|
droṇasya sainyaṃ te sarve śaravarṣairavākiran ||5||

6vayaṃ droṇaṃ puraskṛtya sarvaśastrabhṛtāṃ varam
|
pārṣatapramukhān pārthānabhyavarṣāma sāyakaiḥ ||6||

7mahāmeghāvivodīrṇau miśravātau himātyaye
|
senāgre pracakāśete rucire rathabhūṣite ||7||

8sametya tu mahāsene cakraturvegamuttamam
|
jāhnavīyamune nadyau prāvṛṣīvolbaṇodake ||8||

9nānāśastrapurovāto dvipāśvarathasaṃvṛtaḥ
|
gadāvidyunmahāraudraḥ saṅgrāmajalado mahān ||9||

10bhāradvājāniloddhūtaḥ śaradhārāsahasravān
|
abhyavarṣanmahāsainyaḥ pāṇḍusenāgnimuddhatam ||10||

11samudramiva gharmānte viśan ghoro mahānilaḥ
|
vyakṣobhayadanīkāni pāṇḍavānāṃ dvijottamaḥ ||11||

12te'pi sarvaprayatnena droṇameva samādravan
|
bibhitsanto mahāsetuṃ vāryoghāḥ prabalā iva ||12||

13vārayāmāsa tān droṇo jalaughamacalo yathā
|
pāṇḍavān samare kruddhān pañcālāṃśca sakekayān ||13||

14athāpare ca rājānaḥ parivṛtya samantataḥ
|
mahābalā raṇe śūrāḥ pañcālānanvavārayan ||14||

15tato raṇe naravyāghra pārṣataḥ pāṇḍavaiḥ saha
|
sañjaghānāsakṛddroṇaṃ bibhitsurarivāhinīm ||15||

16yathaiva śaravarṣāṇi droṇo varṣati pārṣate
|
tathaiva śaravarṣāṇi dhṛṣṭadyumno'pyavarṣata ||16||

17sanistriṃśapurovātaḥ śaktiprāsarṣṭisaṃvṛtaḥ
|
jyāvidyuccāpasaṃhrādo dhṛṣṭadyumnabalāhakaḥ||17||

18śaradhārāśmavarṣāṇi vyasṛjat sarvato diśam
|
nighnan rathavarāśvaughān plāvayāmāsa vāhinīm ||18||

19yaṃ yamārcchaccharairdroṇaḥ pāṇḍavānāṃ rathavrajam
|
tatastataḥ śarairdroṇamapākarṣata pārṣataḥ ||19||

20tathā tu yatamānasya droṇasya yudhi bhārata
|
dhṛṣṭadyumnaṃ samāsādya tridhā sainyamabhidyata ||20||

21bhojameke'bhyavartanta jalasandhaṃ tathā'pare
|
pāṇḍavairhanyamānāśca droṇamevāpare yayuḥ ||21||

22saṅghaṭṭayati sainyāni droṇastu rathināṃ varaḥ
|
vyadhamaccāpi tānyasya dhṛṣṭadyumno mahārathaḥ ||22||

23dhārtarāṣṭrāstathā bhūtā vadhyante pāṇḍusṛñjayaiḥ
|
agopāḥ paśavo'raṇye bahubhiḥ śvāpadairiva ||23||

24kālaḥ sma grasate yodhān dhṛṣṭadyumnena me hitān
|
saṅgrāme tumule tasminniti saṃmenire janāḥ ||24||

25kunṛpasya yathā rāṣṭraṃ durbhikṣavyādhitaskaraiḥ
|
drāvyate tadvadāpannā pāṇḍavaistava vāhinī ||25||

26arkaraśmivimiśreṣu śastreṣu kavaceṣu ca
|
cakṣūṃṣi pratyahanyanta sainyena rajasā tathā ||26||

27tridhābhūteṣu sainyeṣu vadhyamāneṣu pāṇḍavaiḥ
|
amarṣitastato droṇaḥ pañcālān vyadhamaccharaiḥ ||27||

28mṛdnatastānyanīkāni nighnataścāpi sāyakaiḥ
|
babhūva rūpaṃ droṇasya kālāgneriva dīpyataḥ ||28||

29rathaṃ nāgaṃ hayaṃ cāpi pattinaśca viśāmpate
|
ekaikeneṣuṇā saṅkhye nirbibheda mahārathaḥ ||29||

30pāṇḍavānāṃ tu sainyeṣu nāsti kaścit sa bhārata
|
dadhāra yo raṇe bāṇān droṇacāpacyutān prabhā ||30||

31tat pacyamānamarkeṇa droṇasāyakatāpitam
|
babhrāma pārṣataṃ sainyaṃ tatra tatraiva bhārata ||31||

32tathaiva pārṣatenāpi kālyamānaṃ balaṃ tava
|
abhavat sarvato dīptaṃ śuṣkaṃ vanamivāgninā ||32||

33bādhyamāneṣu sainyeṣu droṇapārṣatasāyakaiḥ
|
tyaktvā prāṇān paraṃ śaktyā yudhyante sarvatomukhāḥ ||33||

34tāvakānāṃ pareṣāṃ ca yudhyatāṃ bharatarṣabha
|
nāsīt kaścinmahārāja yo'tyākṣīt saṃyugaṃ bhayāt ||34||

35bhīmasenaṃ tu kaunteyaṃ sodaryāḥ paryavārayan
|
viviṃśatiścitraseno vikarṇaśca mahārathaḥ ||35||

36vindānuvindāvāvantyau kṣemadhūrtiśca vīryavān
|
trayāṇāṃ tava putrāṇāṃ traya evānuyāyinaḥ ||36||

37bālhīkarājastejasvī kulaputro mahārathaḥ
|
sahasenaḥ sahāmātyo draupadeyānavārayat||37||

38śaibyo govāsano rājā yodhairdaśaśatāvaraiḥ
|
kāśyasyābhibhuvaḥ putraṃ parākrāntamavārayat ||38||

39ajātaśatruṃ kaunteyaṃ jvalantamiva pāvakam
|
madrāṇāmīśvaraḥ śalyo rājā rājānamāvṛṇot ||39||

40duḥśāsanastvavasthāpya svamanīkamamarṣaṇaḥ
|
sātyakiṃ prayayau kruddhaḥ śūro rathavaraṃ yudhi ||40||

41svakenāhamanīkena sannaddhaḥ kavacāvṛtaḥ
|
catuḥśatairmaheṣvāsaiścekitānamavārayam ||41||

42śakunistu sahānīko mādrīputramavārayat
|
gāndhārakaiḥ saptaśataiścāpaśaktyasipāṇibhiḥ ||42||

43vindānuvindāvāvantyau virāṭaṃ matsyamārcchatām
|
prāṇāṃstyaktvā maheṣvāsau mitrārthe'bhyudyatāyudhau ||43||

44śikhaṇḍinaṃ yājñaseniṃ rundhānamaparājitam
|
bālhīkaḥ pratisaṃyattaḥ parākrāntamavārayat ||44||

45dhṛṣṭadyumnaṃ tu pāñcālyaṃ krūraiḥ sārdhaṃ prabha drakaiḥ
|
āvantyaḥ saha sauvīraiḥ kruddharūpamavārayat ||45||

46ghaṭotkacaṃ tathā śūraṃ rākṣasaṃ krūrakarmiṇam
|
alāyudho'dravattūrṇaṃ kruddhamāyāntamāhave ||46||

47alambuṣaṃ rākṣasendraṃ kuntibhojo mahārathaḥ
|
sainyena mahatā yuktaḥ kruddharūpamavārayat ||47||

48saindhavaḥ pṛṣṭhatastvāsīt sarvasainyasya bhārata
|
rakṣitaḥ parameṣvāsaiḥ kṛpaprabhṛtibhī rathaiḥ ||48||

49tasyāstāṃ cakrarakṣau dvau saindhavasya bṛhattamau
|
drauṇirdakṣiṇato rājan sūtaputraśca vāmataḥ ||49||

50pṛṣṭhagopāstu tasyāsan saumada‌ttipurogamāḥ
|
kṛpaśca vṛṣasenaśca śalaḥ śalyaśca durjayaḥ ||50||

51nītimanto maheṣvāsāḥ sarve yuddhaviśāradāḥ
|
saindhavasya vidhāyaivaṃ rakṣāṃ yuyudhire tataḥ||51||
