Mahābhārata 11.2 (Strīparvan)

1vaiśampāyana uvāca|tato'mṛtarasairvākyairhlādayanpuruṣarṣabham
|
vaicitravīryaṃ viduro yaduvāca nibodha tat||1||

2vidura uvāca|uttiṣṭha rājankiṃ śeṣe dhārayātmānamātmanā
|
eṣā vai sarvasatvānāṃ lokeśvara parā gatiḥ||2||

3sarve kṣayāntā nicayāḥ patanāntāḥ samucchrayāḥ
|
saṃyogā viprayogāntā maraṇāntaṃ ca jīvitam||3||

4yadā śūraṃ ca bhīruṃ ca yamaḥ karṣati bhārata
|
tatkiṃ na yotsyanti hi te kṣatriyāḥ kṣatriyarṣabha||4||

5ayudhyamāno mriyate yudhyamānaśca jīvati
|
kālaṃ prāpya mahārāja na kaścidativartate||5||

6abhāvādīni bhūtāni bhāvamadhyāni bhārata
|
abhāvanidhanānyeva tatra kā paridevanā||6||

7na śocanmṛtamanveti na śocanmriyate naraḥ
|
evaṃ sāṃsiddhike loke kimarthamanuśocasi||7||

8kālaḥ karṣati bhūtāni sarvāṇi vividhānyuta
|
na kālasya priyaḥ kaścinna dveṣyaḥ kurusattama||8||

9yathā vāyustṛṇāgrāṇi saṃvartayati sarvaśaḥ
|
tathā kālavaśaṃ yānti bhūtāni bharatarṣabha||9||

10ekasārthaprayātānāṃ sarveṣāṃ tatra gāminām
|
yasya kālaḥ prayātyagre tatra kā paridevanā||10||

11na cāpyetānhatānyuddhe rājañśocitumarhasi
|
pramāṇaṃ yadi śāstrāṇi gatāste paramāṃ gatim||11||

12sarve svādhyāyavanto hi sarve ca caritavratāḥ
|
sarve cābhimukhāḥ kṣīṇāstatra kā paridevanā||12||

13adarśanādāpatitāḥ punaścādarśanaṃ gatāḥ
|
naite tava na teṣāṃ tvaṃ tatra kā paridevanā||13||

14hato hi labhate svargaṃ jitvā ca labhate yaśaḥ
|
ubhayaṃ no bahuguṇaṃ nāsti niṣphalatā raṇe||14||

15teṣāṃ kāmadughā~llokānindraḥ saṅkalpayiṣyati
|
indrasyātithayo hyete bhavanti bharatarṣabha||15||

16na yajñairdakṣiṇāvadbhirna tapobhirna vidyayā
|
svargaṃ yānti tathā martyā yathā śūrā raṇe hatāḥ||16||

17śarīrāgniṣu śūrāṇāṃ juhuvuste śarāhutīḥ
|
hūyamānāñśarāṃścaiva sehustejasvino mithaḥ||17||

18evaṃ rājaṃstavācakṣe svargyaṃ panthānamuttamam
|
na yuddhādadhikaṃ kiñcitkṣatriyasyeha vidyate||18||

19kṣatriyāste mahātmānaḥ śūrāḥ samitiśobhanāḥ
|
āśiṣaḥ paramāḥ prāptā na śocyāḥ sarva eva hi||19||

20ātmānamātmanā''śvāsya mā śucaḥ puruṣarṣabha
|
nādya śokābhibhūtastvaṃ kāyamutsraṣṭumarhasi||20||

21mātāpitṛsahasrāṇi putradāraśatāni ca
|
saṃsāreṣvanubhūtāni kasya te kasya vā vayam||21||

22śokasthānasahasrāṇi bhayasthānaśatāni ca
|
divase divase mūḍhamāviśanti na paṇḍitam||22||

23na kālasya priyaḥ kaścinna dveṣyaḥ kurusattama
|
na madhyasthaḥ kvacitkālaḥ sarvaṃ kālaḥ prakarṣati||23||

24kālaḥ pacati bhūtāni kālaḥ saṃharate prajāḥ
|
kālaḥ supteṣu jāgarti kālo hi duratikramaḥ||24||

25anityaṃ yauvanaṃ rūpaṃ jīvitaṃ dravyasañcayaḥ
|
ārogyaṃ priyasaṃvāso gṛddhyedeṣu na paṇḍitaḥ||25||

26na jānapadikaṃ duḥkhamekaḥ śocitumarhasi
|
apyabhāvena yujyeta taccāsya na nivartate||26||

27aśocanpratikurvīta yadi paśyetparākramam
|
bhaiṣajyametadduḥkhasya yadetannānucintayet||27||

28cintyamānaṃ hi na vyeti bhūyaścāpi pravardhate
|
aniṣṭasamprayogācca viprayogātpriyasya ca||28||

29mānuṣā mānasairduḥkhairdahyante cā'lpabuddhayaḥ
|
nārtho na dharmo na sukhaṃ yadetadanuśocasi||29||

30na ca nāpnauti kāryārthantrivargāccaiva hīyate
|
anyāmanyāṃ dhanāvasthāṃ prāpya vaiṣayikīṃ narāḥ||30||

31asantuṣṭāḥ pramuhyanti santoṣaṃ yānti paṇḍitāḥ
|
prajñayā mānasaṃ duḥkhaṃ hanyācchārīramauṣadhaiḥ
|
etadvijñānasāmarthyaṃ na bālaiḥ samatāmiyāt||31||

32śayānaṃ cānuśete hi tiṣṭhantaṃ cānutiṣṭhati
|
anudhāvati dhāvantaṃ karma pūrvakṛtaṃ naram||32||

33yasyāṃ yasyāmavasthāyāṃ yatkaroti śubhāśubham
|
tasyāṃ tasyāmavasthāyāṃ tattatphalamavāpnute||33||

34yena yena śarīreṇa yadyatkarma karoti yaḥ
|
tena tena śarīreṇa tatphalaṃ samupāśnute||34||

35ātmaiva hyātmano bandhurātmaiva ripurātmanaḥ
|
ātmaiva hyātmanaḥ sākṣī kṛtasyāpakṛtasya ca||35||

36śubhena karmaṇā saukhyaṃ duḥkhaṃ pāpena karmaṇā
|
kṛtaṃ bhavati sarvatra nākṛtaṃ vidyate kvacit||36||

37na hi jñānaviruddheṣu bahvapāyeṣu karmasu
|
mūlaghātiṣu sajjante buddhimanto bhavadvighāḥ||37||
