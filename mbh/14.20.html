Mahābhārata 14.20 (Āśvamedhikaparvan)

1vāsudeva uvāca|
atrāpyudāharantīmamitihāsaṃ purātanam
|
dampatyoḥ pārtha saṃvādo yo'bhavadbharatarṣabha||1||

2brāhmaṇī brāhmaṇaṃ kañcijjñānavijñānapāragam
|
dṛṣṭvā vivikta āsīnaṃ bhāryā bhartāramabravīt||2||

3kaṃ nu lokaṃ gamiṣyāmi tvāmahaṃ patimāśritā
|
nyastakarmāṇamāsīnaṃ kīnāśamavicakṣaṇam||3||

4bhāryāḥ patikṛtā~llokānāpnuvantīti naḥ śrutam
|
tvāmahaṃ patimāsādya kāṃ gamiṣyāmi vai gatim||4||

5evamuktaḥ sa śāntātmā tāmuvāca hasanniva
|
subhage nābhyasūyāmi vākyasyāsya tavātaghe||5||

6grāhyaṃ dṛśyaṃ ca satyaṃ vā yadidaṃ karma vidyate
|
etadeva vyavasyanti karma karmeti karmiṇaḥ||6||

7mohameva niyacchanti karmaṇā jñānavarjitāḥ
|
naiṣkarmyaṃ na ca loke'smin muhūrtamapi labhyate||7||

8karmaṇā manasā vācā śubhaṃ vā yadi vāśubham
|
janmādimūrtibhedāntaṃ karma bhūteṣu vartate||8||

9rakṣobhirvadhyamāneṣu dṛśyadravyeṣu vartmasu
|
ātmasthamātmanā tebhyo dṛṣṭamāyatanaṃ mayā||9||

10yatra tadbrahma nirdvandvaṃ yatra somaḥ sahāgninā
|
vyavāyaṃ kurute nityaṃ dhīro bhūtāni dhārayan||10||

11yatra brahmādayo yuktāstadakṣaramupāsate
|
vidvāṃsaḥ suvratā yatra śāntātmāno jitendriyāḥ||11||

12ghrāṇena na tadādhreyaṃ nāsvādyaṃ caiva jihvayā
|
sparśanena tadaspṛśyaṃ manasā tvavagamyate||12||

13cakṣuṣāmaviṣahyaṃ ca yatkiñcicchravaṇāt param
|
agandhamarasasparśamarūpāśabdalakṣaṇam||13||

14yataḥ pravartate tantraṃ yatra ca pratitiṣṭhati
|
prāṇo'pāna: samānaśca vyānaścodāra eva ca||14||

15tata eva pravartante tadeva praviśanti ca
|
samānavyānayormadhye prāṇāpānau viceratuḥ||15||

16tasmillīne pralīyeta samāno vyāna eva ca
|
apānapraṇayormadhye udāno vyāpya tiṣṭhati
|
tasmācchayānaṃ puruṣaṃ prāṇāpānau na muñcataḥ||16||

17prāṇānāmāyatatvena tamudānaṃ pracakṣate
|
tasmāt tapo vyavasyanti madgataṃ brahmavādinaḥ||17||

18teṣāmanyonyabhakṣāṇāṃ sarveṣāṃ dehacāriṇām
|
agnirvaiśvānaro madhye saptadhā dīvyate'ntarā||18||

19ghrāṇaṃ jihā ca cakṣuścatvak ca śrotraṃ ca pañcamam
|
mano buddhiśca saptaitā jihvā vaiśvānarārciṣaḥ||19||

20ghreyaṃ dṛśyaṃ ca peyaṃ ca spṛśyaṃ śravyaṃ tathaiva ca
|
mantavyamatha boddhavyaṃ tāḥ sapta samidho mama||20||

21ghrātā bhakṣayitā draṣṭā spraṣṭā śrotā ca pañcamaḥ
|
mantā boddhā ca saptaite bhavanti paramartvijaḥ||21||

22ghreye peye ca dṛśye ca spṛśye śravye tathaiva ca
|
mantavye'pyatha boddhavye subhage paśya sarvadā||22||

23havīṃṣyagniṣu hotāra: saptadhā sapta saptasu
|
samyak prakṣipya vidvāṃso janayanti svayoniṣu||23||

24pṛthivī vāyurākāśamāpo jyotiśca pañcamam
|
mano buddhiśca saptaitā yonirityeva śabditāḥ||24||

25havirbhūtā guṇāḥ sarve praviśantyagnijaṃ guṇam
|
antarvāsamuṣitvā ca jāyante svāsu yoniṣu||25||

26tatraiva ca nirudhyante pralaye bhūtabhāvane
|
tataḥ sañjāyate gandhastataḥ sañjāyate rasaḥ||26||

27tataḥ sañjāyate rūpaṃ tataḥ sparśo'bhijāyate
|
tataḥ sañjāyate śabdaḥ saṃśayastatra jāyate
|
tataḥ sañjāyate niṣṭhā janmaitatsaptadhā viduḥ||27||

28anenaiva prakāreṇa pragṛhītaṃ purātanaiḥ
|
pūrṇāhutibhirāpūrṇāstribhiḥ pūryanti tejasā||28||
