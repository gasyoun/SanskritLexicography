Mahābhārata 12.231 (Śāntiparvan)

1yudhiṣṭhira uvāca|
ādyaṃ taṃ sarvabhūtānāṃ jñātumicchāmi kaurava
|
dhyānaṃ karma ca kālaṃ ca tathaivāyuryuge yuge ||1||

2lokatattvaṃ ca kārtsnyena bhūtānāmāgatiṃ gatim
|
sargaśca nidhanaṃ caiva kuta etatpravartate ||2||

3yadi te'nugrahe buddhirasmāsviha satāṃ vara
|
etadbhavantaṃ pṛcchāmi tadbhavānprabravītu me ||3||

4pūrvaṃ hi kathitaṃ śrutvā bhṛgubhāṣitamuttamam
|
bharadvājasya viprarṣestato me buddhiruttamā ||4||

5jātā paramadharmiṣṭhā divyasaṃsthānasaṃsthitā
|
tato bhūyastu pṛcchāmi tadbhavānvaktumarhati ||5||

6bhīṣma uvāca|
atra te vartayiṣye'hamitihāsaṃ purātanam
|
jagau yadbhagavānvyāsaḥ putrāya paripṛcchate ||6||

7adhītya vedānakhilān sāṅgopaniṣadastathā
|
anvicchannaiṣṭhikaṃ karma dharmanaipuṇadarśanāt ||7||

8kṛṣṇadvaipāyanaṃ vyāsaṃ putro vaiyāsakiḥ śukaḥ
|
papraccha sandehamimaṃ chinnadharmārthasaṃśayam ||8||

9śrīśuka uvāca |
bhūtagrāmasya kartāraṃ kālajñānena niṣṭhitam
|
brāhmaṇasya ca yatkṛtyaṃ tadbhavānvaktumarhati ||9||

10bhīṣma uvāca|
tasmai provāca tatsarvaṃ pitā putrāya pṛcchate
|
atītānāgate vidvānsarvajñaḥ sarvadharmavit ||10||

11vyāsa uvāca |
anādyantamajaṃ divyamajaraṃ dhruvamavyayam
|
apratarkyamavijñeyaṃ brahmāgre sampravartate ||11||

12kāṣṭhā nimeṣā daśa pañca caiva triṃśattu kāṣṭhā gaṇayetkalāṃ tām
|
triṃśatkalaścāpi bhavenmuhūrto bhāgaḥ kalāyā daśamaśca yaḥ syāt ||12||

13triṃśanmuhūrtaṃ tu bhavedahaśca rātriśca saṃkhyā munibhiḥ praṇītā
|
māsaḥ smṛto rātryahanī ca triṃśatsaṃvatsaro dvādaśamāsa uktaḥ ||13||

14saṃvatsaraṃ dve tvayane vadanti saṃkhyāvido dakṣiṇamuttaraṃ ca ||14||

15ahorātre vibhajate sūryo mānuṣalaukike
|
rātriḥ svapnāya bhūtānāṃ ceṣṭāyai karmaṇāmahaḥ ||15||

16pitrye rātryahanī māsaḥ pravibhāgastayoḥ punaḥ
|
śuklo'haṃ karmaceṣṭāyāṃ kṛṣṇaḥ svapnāya śarvarī ||16||

17daive rātryahanī varṣaṃ pravibhāgastayoḥ punaḥ
|
ahastatrodagayanaṃ rātriḥ syāddakṣiṇāyanam ||17||

18ye te rātryahanī pūrvaṃ kīrtite jīvalaukike
|
tayoḥ saṃkhyāya varṣāgraṃ brāhme vakṣyāmyahaḥkṣape ||18||

19pṛthak saṃvatsarāgrāṇi pravakṣyāmyanupūrvaśaḥ
|
kṛte tretāyuge caiva dvāpare ca kalau tathā ||19||

20catvāryāhuḥ sahasrāṇi varṣāṇāṃ tatkṛtaṃ yugam
|
tasya tāvacchatī sandhyā sandhyāṃśaśca tathāvidhaḥ ||20||

21itareṣu sasaṃdhyeṣu saṃdhyāṃśeṣu tatastriṣu
|
ekapādena hīyante sahasrāṇi śatāni ca ||21||

22etāni śāśvatāllokān dhārayanti sanātanān
|
etadbrahmavidāṃ tāta viditaṃ brahma śāśvatam ||22||

23catuṣpātsakalo dharmaḥ satyaṃ caiva kṛte yuge
|
nādharmeṇāgamaḥ kaścitparastasya pravartate ||23||

24itareṣvāgamāddharmaḥ pādaśastvavaropyate
|
cauryakānṛtamāyābhiradharmaścopacīyate ||24||

25arogāḥ sarvasiddhārthāścaturvarṣaśatāyuṣaḥ
|
kṛte tretāyuge tveṣāṃ pādaśo hrasate vayaḥ ||25||

26vedavādāścānuyugaṃ hrasantītīha naḥ śrutam
|
āyūṃṣi cāśiṣaścaiva vedasyaiva ca yatphalam ||26||

27anye kṛtayuge dharmāstretāyāṃ dvāpare'pare
|
anye kaliyuge nṝṇāṃ yugahrāsānurūpataḥ ||27||

28tapaḥ paraṃ kṛtayuge tretāyāṃ jñānamuttamam
|
dvāpare yajñamevāhurdānamekaṃ kalau yuge ||28||

29etāṃ dvādaśasāhasrīṃ yugākhyāṃ kavayo viduḥ
|
sahasraparivartaṃ tadbrāhmaṃ divasamucyate ||29||

30rātrimetāvatīṃ caiva tadādau viśvamīśvaraḥ
|
pralaye dhyānamāviśya suptvā so'nte vibuddhyate ||30||

31sahasrayugaparyantamaharyadbrahmaṇo viduḥ
|
rātriṃ yugasahasrāntāṃ te'horātravido janāḥ ||31||

32pratibuddho vikurute brahmā'kṣayyaṃ kṣapākṣaye
|
sṛjate ca mahadbhūtaṃ tasmādvyaktātmakaṃ manaḥ ||32||
