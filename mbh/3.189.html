Mahābhārata 3.189 (Āraṇyakaparvan)

1deva uvāca|
kāmaṃ devā'pi māṃ vipra na hi jānanti tattvataḥ
|
tvatprītyā tu pravakṣyāmi yathedaṃ visṛjāmyaham||1||

2pitṛbhakto'si viprarṣe māṃ caiva śaraṇaṃ gataḥ
|
tato dṛṣṭo'smi te sākṣādbrahmacaryaṃ ca te mahat||2||

3apāṃ nārā iti purā saṃjñākarma kṛtaṃ mayā
|
tena nārāyaṇo'pyukto mama tat tvayanaṃ sadā||3||

4ahaṃ nārāyaṇo nāma prabhavaḥ śāśvato'vyayaḥ
|
vidhātā sarvabhūtānāṃ saṃhartā ca dvijottama||4||

5ahaṃ viṣṇurahaṃ brahmā śakraścāhaṃ surādhipaḥ
|
ahaṃ vaiśravaṇo rājā yamaḥ pretādhipastathā||5||

6ahaṃ śivaśca somaśca kaśyapo'tha prajāpatiḥ
|
ahaṃ dhātā vidhātā ca yajñaścāhaṃ dvijottama||6||

7agnirāsyaṃ kṣitiḥ pādau candrādityau ca locane
|
dyaurmūrdhā khaṃ diśaḥ śrotre tathā''paḥ svedasambhavāḥ||7||

8sadiśaṃ ca nabhaḥ kāyo vāyurmanasi me sthitaḥ
|
mayā kratuśatairiṣṭaṃ bahubhiḥ svāptadakṣiṇaiḥ||8||

9yajante veda‌viduṣo māṃ devayajane sthitam
|
pṛthivyāṃ kṣatriyendrāśca pārthivāḥ svargakāṅkṣiṇaḥ||9||

10yajante māṃ tathā vaiśyāḥ svargalokajigīṣayā
|
catuḥsamudraparyantāṃ merumandarabhūṣaṇām||10||

11śeṣo bhūtvā'hamevaitāṃ dhārayāmi vasundharām
|
vārāhaṃ rūpamāsthāya mayeyaṃ jagatī purā||11||

12majjamānā jale vipra vīryeṇāsīt samuddhṛtā
|
agniśca vaḍavāvaktro bhūtvā'haṃ dvijasattama||12||

13pibāmyapaḥ sadā vidvaṃstāścaivaṃ visṛjāmyaham
|
brahma vaktraṃ bhujau kṣatramūrū me saṃsthitā viśaḥ||13||

14pādau śūdrā bhavantīme vikrameṇa krameṇa ca
|
ṛgvedaḥ sāmavedaśca yajurvedo'pyatharvaṇaḥ||14||

15mattaḥ prādurbhavantyete māmeva praviśanti ca
|
yatayaḥ śāntiparamā yatātmāno bubhutsavaḥ||15||

16kāma-krodha-dveṣamuktā niḥsaṃjñā vītakalmaṣāḥ
|
sattvasyā nirahaṅkārā nityamadhyātmakovidāḥ||16||

17māmeva satataṃ viprāścintayanta upāsate
|
ahaṃ saṃvartako vahnirahaṃ saṃvartako'nalaḥ||17||

18ahaṃ saṃvartakaḥ sūryastvahaṃ saṃvartako'nilaḥ
|
tārārūpāṇi dṛśyante yānyetāni nabhastale||18||

19mama vai romakūpāṇi viddhi tvaṃ dvijasattama
|
ratnākarāḥ samudrāśca sarva eva caturdiśam||19||

20vasanaṃ śayanaṃ caiva vilayaṃ caiva viddhi me
|
mayaiva suvibhaktāste devakāryārthasiddhaye||20||

21kāmaṃ krodhaṃ ca harṣaṃ ca bhayaṃ mohaṃ tathaiva ca
|
mamaiva viddhi romāṇi sarvāṇyetāni sattama||21||

22prāpnuvanti narā vipra yat kṛtvā karma śobhanam
|
satyaṃ dānaṃ tapaścogramahiṃsā caiva jantuṣu||22||

23madvidhānena vihitā mama dehavihāriṇaḥ
|
mayā'bhibhūtavijñānā viceṣṭante na kāmataḥ||23||

24samyagvedamadhīyānā yajante vividhairmakhaiḥ
|
śāntātmāno jitakrodhāḥ prāpnuvanti dvijātayaḥ||24||

25prāptuṃ na śakyo yo vidvannarairduṣkṛtakarmabhiḥ
|
lobhābhibhūtaiḥ kṛpaṇairanāryairakṛtātmabhiḥ||25||

26tasmānmahāphalaṃ viddhi narāṇāṃ bhāvitātmanām
|
suduṣprāpaṃ vimūḍhānāṃ mārgaṃ yogairniṣevitam||26||

27yadā yadā ca dharmasya glānirbhavati sattama
|
abhyutthānamadharmasya tadā''tmānaṃ sṛjāmyaham||27||

28daityā hiṃsānuraktāśca avadhyāḥ surasattamaiḥ
|
rākṣasāścāpi loke'sminyadotpatsyanti dāruṇāḥ||28||

29tadā'haṃ samprasūyāmi gṛheṣu śubhakarmaṇām
|
praviṣṭo mānuṣaṃ dehaṃ sarvaṃ praśamayāmyaham||29||

30sṛṣṭvā devamanuṣyāṃstu gandharvoragarākṣasān
|
sthāvarāṇi ca bhūtāni saṃharāmyātmamāyayā||30||

31karmakāle punardehamavicintyaṃ sṛjāmyaham
|
āviśya mānuṣaṃ dehaṃ maryādābandhakāraṇāt||31||

32śvetaḥ kṛtayuge varṇaḥ pītastretāyuge mama
|
rakto dvāparamāsādya kṛṣṇaḥ kaliyuge tathā||32||

33trayo bhāgā hyadharmasya tasmin kāle bhavanti ca
|
antakāle ca samprāpte kālo bhūtvā'tidāruṇaḥ||33||

34trailokyaṃ nāśayāmyekaḥ kṛtsnaṃ sthāvarajaṅgamam
|
ahaṃ trivartmā viśvātmā sarvalokasukhāvahaḥ||34||

35āvirbhūḥ sarvago'nanto hṛṣīkeśa urukramaḥ
|
kālacakraṃ nayāmyeko brahmannahamarūpakam||35||

36śamanaṃ sarvabhūtānāṃ sarvalokakṛtodyamam
|
evaṃ praṇihitaḥ samyaṅmamātmā munisattama
|
sarvabhūteṣu viprendra na ca māṃ vetti kaścana||36||

37sarvaloke ca māṃ bhaktāḥ pūjayanti ca sarvaśaḥ
|
yacca kiñcit tvayā prāptaṃ mayi kleśātmakaṃ dvija||37||

38sukhodayāya tat sarvaṃ śreyase ca tavānagha
|
yacca kiñcit tvayā loke dṛṣṭaṃ sthāvarajaṅgamam||38||

39vihitaḥ sarvathavāsau mamātmā bhūtabhāvanaḥ
|
ardhaṃ mama śarīrasya sarvalokapitāmahaḥ||39||

40ahaṃ nārāyaṇo nāma śaṅkhacakragadādharaḥ
|
yāvadyugānāṃ viprarṣe sahastraparivartanāt||40||

41tāvat svapimi viśvātmā sarvabhūtāni mohayan
|
evaṃ sarvamahaṃ kālabhihāse munisattama||41||

42āśiśuḥ śiśurūpeṇa yāvadbrahmā na budhyate
|
mayā ca datto viprāgrya varaste brahmarūpiṇā||42||

43asakṛt parituṣṭena viprarṣigaṇapūjita
|
sarvamekārṇavaṃ dṛṣṭvā naṣṭaṃ sthāvarajaṅgamam||43||

44viklavo'si mayā jñātastataste darśitaṃ jagat
|
abhyantaraṃ śarīrasya praviṣṭo'si yadā mama||44||

45dṛṣṭvā lokaṃ samastaṃ ca vismito nāvabudhyase
|
tato'si vaktrādviprarṣe drutaṃ niḥsārito mayā||45||

46ākhyātaste mayā cātmā durjñeyo hi surāsuraiḥ||46||

47yāvat sa bhagavān brahmā na budhyeta mahātapāḥ
|
tāvat tvamiha viprarṣe viśrabdhaścara vai sukham||47||

48tato vibuddhe tasmiṃstu sarvalokapitāmahe
|
ekībhūto hi srakṣyāmi śarīrāṇi dvijottama||48||

49ākāśaṃ pṛthivīṃ jyotirvāyuṃ salilameva ca
|
loke yacca bhaveccheṣamiha sthāvarajaṅgamam||49||

50mārkaṇḍeya uvāca|
ityuktvā'ntarhitastāta sa devaḥ paramādbhutaḥ
|
prajāścemāḥ prapaśyāmi vicitrā vividhāḥ kṛtāḥ||50||

51evaṃ dṛṣṭaṃ mayā rājaṃstasmin prāpte yugakṣaye
|
āścaryaṃ bharataśreṣṭha sarvadharmabhṛtāṃ vara||51||

52yaḥ sa devo mayā dṛṣṭaḥ purā padmāyatekṣaṇaḥ
|
sa eṣa puruṣavyāghra sambandhī te janārdanaḥ||52||

53asyaiva varadānāddhi smṛtirna prajahāti mām
|
dīrghamāyuśca kaunteya svacchandamaraṇaṃ mama||53||

54sa eṣa kṛṣṇo vārṣṇeyaḥ purāṇapuruṣo vibhuḥ
|
āste hariracintyātmā krīḍanniva mahābhujaḥ||54||

55eṣa dhātā vidhātā ca saṃhartā caiva śāśvataḥ
|
śrīvatsavakṣā govindaḥ prajāpatipatiḥ prabhuḥ||55||

56dṛṣṭvemaṃ vṛṣṇipravaraṃ smṛtirmāmiyamāgatā
|
ādidevamayaṃ jiṣṇuṃ puruṣaṃ pītavāsasam||56||

57sarveṣāmeva bhūtānāṃ pitā mātā ca mādhavaḥ
|
gacchadhvamenaṃ śaraṇaṃ śaraṇyaṃ kauravarṣabhāḥ||57||

58vaiśampāyana uvāca|
evamuktāśca te pārthā yamau ca puruṣarṣabhau
|
draupadyā sahitāḥ sarve namaścakrurjanārdanam||58||

59sa caitān puruṣavyāghra sāmnā paramavalgunā
|
sāntvayāmāsa mānārho manyamāno yathāvidhi||59||
