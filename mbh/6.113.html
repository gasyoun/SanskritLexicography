Mahābhārata 6.113 (Bhīṣmaparvan)

1sañjaya uvāca|
bhagadattaḥ kṛpaḥ śalyaḥ kṛtavarmā tathaiva ca
|
vindānu‌vindāvāvantyau saindhavaśca jayadrathaḥ ||1||

2citraseno vikarṇaśca tathā durmarṣaṇādayaḥ
|
daśaite tāvakā yodhā bhīmasenamayodhayan ||2||

3mahatyā senayā yuktā nānādeśasamutthayā
|
bhīṣmasya samare rājan prārthayānā mahadyaśaḥ ||3||

4śalyastu navabhirbāṇairbhīmasenamatāḍayat
|
kṛtavarmā tribhirbāṇaiḥ kṛpaśca navabhiḥ śaraiḥ ||4||

5citraseno vikarṇaśca bhagadattaśca māriṣa
|
daśabhirdaśabhirbāṇairbhīmasenamatāḍayan ||5||

6saindhavaśca tribhirbāṇairbhīmasenamatāḍayat
|
vindānuvindāvāvantyau pañcabhiḥ pañcabhiḥ śaraiḥ ||6||

7durmarṣaṇastu viṃśatyā pāṇḍavaṃ niśitaiḥ śaraiḥ
|
sa tān sarvān mahārāja rājamānān pṛthak pṛthak ||7||

8pravīrān sarvalokasya dhārtarāṣṭrān mahārathān
|
jaghāna samare vīraḥ pāṇḍavaḥ paravīrahā ||8||

9saptabhiḥ śalyamāvidhyat kṛtavarmāṇamaṣṭabhiḥ
|
kṛpasya saśaraṃ cāpaṃ madhye ciccheda bhārata ||9||

10athainaṃ chinnadhanvānaṃ punarvivyādha saptabhiḥ
|
vindānuvindau ca tathā tribhistribhiratāḍayat ||10||

11durmarṣaṇaṃ ca viṃśatyā citrasenaṃ ca pañcabhiḥ
|
vikarṇaṃ daśabhirbāṇaiḥ pañcabhiśca jayadratham ||11||

12viddhvā bhīmo'nadaddhṛṣṭaḥ saindhavaṃ ca punastribhiḥ
|
athānyaddhanurādāya gautamo rathināṃ varaḥ ||12||

13bhīmaṃ vivyādha saṃrabdho daśabhirniśitaiḥ śaraiḥ
|
sa viddho daśabhirbāṇaistotrairiva mahādvipaḥ ||13||

14tataḥ kruddho mahārāja bhīmasenaḥ pratāpavān
|
gautamaṃ tāḍayāmāsa śarairbahubhirāhave ||14||

15saindhavasya tathāśvāṃśca sārathiṃ ca tribhiḥ śaraiḥ
|
prāhiṇonmṛtyulokāya kālāntakasamadyutiḥ ||15||

16hatāśvāt tu rathāt turṇamavaplutya mahārathaḥ
|
śarāṃścikṣepa niśitān bhīmasenasya saṃyuge ||16||

17tasya bhīmo dhanurmadhye dvābhyāṃ ciccheda māriṣa
|
bhallābhyāṃ bharataśreṣṭha saindhavasya mahātmanaḥ ||17||

18sa chinnadhanvā viratho hatāśvo hatasārathiḥ
|
citrasenarathaṃ rājannāruroha tvarānvitaḥ ||18||

19atyadbhutaṃ raṇe karma kṛtavāṃstatra pāṇḍavaḥ
|
mahārathān śarairviddhvā vārayitvā ca māriṣa ||19||

20virathaṃ saindhavaṃ cakre sarvalokasya paśyataḥ
|
tadā na mamṛṣe śalyo bhīmasenasya vikramam ||20||

21sa sandhāya śarāṃstīkṣṇān karmāraparimārjitān
|
bhīmaṃ vivyādha samare tiṣṭha tiṣṭhati cābravīt ||21||

22kṛpaśca kṛtavarmā ca bhagadattaśca vīryavān
|
vindānuvindāvāvantyau citrasenaśca saṃyuge ||22||

23durmarṣaṇo vikarṇaśca sindhurājaśca vīryavān
|
bhīmaṃ te vivyadhustūrṇaṃ śalyahetorarindamāḥ ||23||

24sa ca tān prativivyādha pañcabhiḥ pañcabhiḥ śaraiḥ
|
śalyaṃ vivyādha saptatyā punaśca daśabhiḥ śaraiḥ ||24||

25taṃ śalyo navabhirbhittvā punarvivyādha pañcabhiḥ
|
sārathiṃ cāsya bhallena gāḍhaṃ vivyādha marmaṇi ||25||

26viśokaṃ prekṣya nirbhinnaṃ bhīmasenaḥ pratāpavān
|
madrarājaṃ tribhirbāṇairbāhvorurasi cārpayat ||26||

27tathetarān maheṣvāsāṃstribhistribhirajihmagaiḥ
|
tāḍayāmāsa samare siṃhavadvinanāda ca ||27||

28te hi yattā maheṣvāsāḥ pāṇḍavaṃ yuddhakovidam
|
tribhistribhirakuṇṭhāgrairbhṛśaṃ marmasvatāḍayan ||28||

29so'tividdho maheṣvāso bhīmaseno na vivyathe
|
parvato vāridhārābhirvarṣamāṇairivāṃbudaiḥ ||29||

30sa tu krodhasamāviṣṭaḥ pāṇḍavānāṃ mahārathaḥ
|
madreśvaraṃ tribhirbāṇairbhṛśaṃ viddhvā mahāyaśāḥ ||30||

31kṛpaṃ ca navabhirbāṇairbhṛśaṃ viddhvā samaṃtataḥ
|
prāgjyotiṣaṃ śatairājau rājan vivyādha sāyakaiḥ ||31||

32tatastu saśaraṃ cāpaṃ sātvatasya mahātmanaḥ
|
kṣurapreṇa sutīkṣṇena ciccheda kṛtahastavat ||32||

33tathā'nyaddhanurādāya kṛtavarmā vṛkodram
|
ājaghāna bhruvormadhye nārācena paraṃtapaḥ ||33||

34bhīmastu samare viddhvā śalyaṃ navabhirāyasaiḥ
|
bhagadattaṃ tribhiścaiva kṛtavarmāṇamaṣṭabhiḥ ||34||

35dvābhyāṃ dvābhyāṃ tu vivyādha gautamaprabhṛtīnrathān
|
te'pi taṃ samare rājan vivyadhurniśitaiḥ śaraiḥ ||35||

36sa tathā pīḍyamāno'pi sarvaśastrairmahārathaiḥ
|
matvā tṛṇena tāṃstulyān vicacāra gatavyathaḥ ||36||

37te cāpi rathināṃ śreṣṭhā bhīmāya niśitān śarān
|
preṣayāmāsuravyagrāḥ śataśo'tha sahasraśaḥ ||37||

38tasya śaktiṃ mahāvegāṃ bhagadatto mahārathaḥ
|
cikṣepa samare vīraḥ svarṇadaṇḍāṃ mahāmate ||38||

39tomaraṃ saindhavo rājā paṭṭiśaṃ ca mahābhujaḥ
|
śataghnīṃ ca kṛpo rājañcharaṃ śalyaśca saṃyuge ||39||

40athetare maheṣvāsāḥ pañca pañca śilīmukhān
|
bhīmasenaṃ samuddiśya preṣayāmāsurojasā ||40||

41tomaraṃ ca dvidhā cakre kṣurapreṇānilātmajaḥ
|
paṭṭiśaṃ ca tribhirbāṇaiściccheda tilakāṇḍavat ||41||

42sa bibheda śataghnīṃ ca navabhiḥ kaṅkapatribhiḥ
|
madrarājaprayuktaṃ ca śaraṃ chittvā mahārathaḥ ||42||

43śaktiṃ ciccheda sahasā bhagada‌tteritāṃ raṇe
|
tathetarān śarān ghorān śaraiḥ sannataparvabhiḥ ||43||

44bhīmaseno raṇaślāghī tridhaikaikaṃ samācchinat
|
tāṃśca sarvān maheṣvāsāṃstribhistribhiratāḍayat ||44||

45tato dhanañjayastatra vartamāne mahāraṇe
|
ājagāma rathenājau bhīmaṃ dṛṣṭvā mahāratham ||45||

46nighnantaṃ samare śatrūnyodhayānaṃ ca sāyakaiḥ
|
tau tu tatra mahātmānau sametau vīkṣya pāṇḍavau ||46||

47na śaśaṃsurjayaṃ tatra tāvakāḥ puruṣarṣabhāḥ
|
athārjuno raṇe bhīmaṃ yodhayantaṃ mahārathān ||47||

48bhīṣmasya nidhanākāṅkṣī puraskṛtya śikhaṇḍinam
|
āsasāda raṇe vīrāṃstāvakān daśa bhārata ||48||

49ye sma bhīmaṃ raṇe rājan yodhayanto vyavasthitāḥ
|
bībhatsustānathāvidhyadbhīmasya priyakāmyayā ||49||

50tato duryodhano rājā suśarmāṇamacodayat
|
arjunasya vadhārthāya bhīmasenasya cobhayoḥ ||50||

51suśarman gaccha śīghraṃ tvaṃ balaughaiḥ parivāritaḥ
|
jahi pāṇḍusutāvetau dhanañjayavṛkodarau ||51||

52tacchrutvā vacanaṃ tasya traigartaḥ prasthalādhipaḥ
|
abhidrutya raṇe bhīmamarjunaṃ caiva dhanvinau ||52||

53rathairanekasāhasraiḥ samaṃtāt paryavārayat
|
tataḥ pravavṛte yuddhamarjunasya paraiḥ saha ||53||
