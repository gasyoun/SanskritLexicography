Mahābhārata 13.21 (Anuśāsanaparvan)

1yudhiṣṭhira uvāca|
na bibheti kathaṃ sā strī śāpācca paramadyuteḥ
|
kathaṃ nivṛtto bhagavāṃstadbhavān prabravītu me ||1||

2bhīṣma uvāca|
aṣṭāvakro'nvapṛcchattāṃ rūpaṃ vikuruṣe katham
|
na cānṛtaṃ te vaktavyaṃ brūhi brāhmaṇakāmyayā ||2||

3stryuvāca
|
dyāvāpṛthivyoryatraiṣā kāmyā brāhmaṇasattama
|
śṛṇuṣvāvahitaḥ sarvaṃ yadidaṃ satyavikrama ||3||

4jijñāseyaṃ prayuktā me sthirīkartuṃ tavānagha
|
avyutthānena te lokā jitāḥ satyaparākrama ||4||

5uttarāṃ māṃ diśaṃ viddhi dṛṣṭaṃ strīcāpalaṃ ca te
|
sthavirāṇāmapi strīṇāṃ bādhate maithunajvaraḥ ||5||

6tuṣṭaḥ pitāmahaste'dya tathā devāḥ savāsavāḥ
|
sa tvaṃ yena ca kāryeṇa samprāpto bhagavāniha ||6||

7preṣitastena vipreṇa kanyāpitrā dvijarṣabha
|
tavopadeśaṃ kartuṃ vai tacca sarvaṃ kṛtaṃ mayā ||7||

8kṣemairgamiṣyasi gṛhaṃ śramaśca na bhaviṣyati
|
kanyāṃ prāpsyasi tāṃ vipra putriṇī ca bhaviṣyati ||8||

9kāmyayā pṛṣṭavāṃstvaṃ māṃ tato vyāhṛtamuttamam
|
anatikramaṇīyā sā kṛtsnairlokaistribhiḥ sadā ||9||

10gacchasva sukṛtaṃ kṛtvā kiṃ cānyacchrotumicchasi
|
yāvadbravīmi viprarṣe aṣṭāvakra yathātatham ||10||

11ṛṣiṇā prasāditā cāsmi tava hetordvijarṣabha
|
tasya saṃmānanārthaṃ me tvayi vākyaṃ prabhāṣitam ||11||

12bhīṣma uvāca|
śrutvā tu vacanaṃ tasyāḥ sa vipraḥ prāñjaliḥ sthitaḥ
|
anujñātastayā cāpi svagṛhaṃ punarāvrajat ||12||

13gṛhamāgatya viśrāntaḥ svajanaṃ paripṛcchya ca
|
abhyagacchacca taṃ vipraṃ nyāyataḥ kurunandana ||13||

14pṛṣṭaśca tena vipreṇa dṛṣṭaṃ tvetannidarśanam
|
prāha vipraṃ tadā vipraḥ suprītenāntarātmanā ||14||

15bhavatā samanujñātaḥ prasthito gandhamādanam
|
tasya cottarato deśe dṛṣṭaṃ me daivataṃ mahat ||15||

16tayā cāhamanujñāto bhavāṃścāpi prakīrtitaḥ
|
śrāvitaścāpi tadvākyaṃ gṛhaṃ cābhyāgataḥ prabho ||16||

17tamuvāca tadā vipraḥ sutāṃ prati gṛhāṇa me
|
nakṣatravidhiyogena pātraṃ hi paramaṃ bhavān ||17||

18bhīṣma uvāca|
aṣṭāvakrastathetyuktvā pratigṛhya ca tāṃ prabho
|
kanyāṃ paramadharmātmā prītimāṃścābhavattadā ||18||

19kanyāṃ tāṃ pratigṛhyaiva bhāryāṃ paramaśobhanām
|
uvāsa muditastatra svāśrame vigatajvaraḥ ||19||
