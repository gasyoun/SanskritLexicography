Mahābhārata 12.22 (Śāntiparvan)

1vaiśampāyana uvāca|
asminnevāntare vākyaṃ punarevārjuno'bravīt
|
nirviṇṇamanasaṃ jyeṣṭhamidaṃ bhrātaramacyutam ||1||

2kṣatradharmeṇa dharmajña prāpya rājyaṃ sudurlabham
|
jitvā cārīnnaraśreṣṭha tapyate kiṃ bhṛśaṃ bhavān ||2||

3kṣatriyāṇāṃ mahārāja saṅgrāme nidhanaṃ matam
|
viśiṣṭaṃ bahubhiryajñaiḥ kṣatradharmamanusmara ||3||

4brāhmaṇānāṃ tapastyāgaḥ pretya dharmavidhiḥ smṛtaḥ
|
kṣatriyāṇāṃ ca nidhanaṃ saṅgrāme vihitaṃ prabho ||4||

5kṣātradharmoṃ mahāraudraḥ śastranitya iti smṛtaḥ
|
vadhaśca bharataśreṣṭha kāle śastreṇa saṃyuge ||5||

6brāhmaṇasyāpi cedrājan kṣatradharmeṇa vartataḥ
|
praśastaṃ jīvitaṃ loke kṣatraṃ hi brahmasaṃbhavam ||6||

7na tyāgo na punaryajño na tapo manujeśvara
|
kṣatriyasya vidhīyante na parasvopajīvanam ||7||

8sa bhavān sarvadharmajño dharmātmā bharatarṣabha
|
rājā manīṣī nipuṇo loka dṛṣṭaparāvaraḥ ||8||

9tyaktvā santāpajaṃ śokaṃ daṃśito bhava karmaṇi
|
kṣatriyasya viśeṣeṇa hṛdayaṃ vajrasannibham ||9||

10jitvā'rīn kṣatradharmeṇa prāpya rājyamakaṇṭakam
|
vijitātmā manuṣyendra yajñadānaparo bhava ||10||

11indro vai brahmaṇaḥ putraḥ kṣatriyaḥ karmaṇā'bhavat
|
jñātīnāṃ pāpavṛttīnāṃ jaghāna navatīrnava ||11||

12taccāsya karma pūjyaṃ ca praśasyaṃ ca viśāṃpate
|
tenendratvaṃ samāpede devānāmiti naḥ śrutam ||12||

13sa tvaṃ yajñairmahārāja yajasva bahuda‌kṣiṇaiḥ
|
yathaivendro manuṣyendra cirāya vigatajvaraḥ ||13||

14mā tvamevaṃ gate kiñcicchocethāḥ kṣatriyarṣabha
|
gatāste kṣatradharmeṇa śastrapūtāḥ parāṃ gatim ||14||

15bhavitavyaṃ tathā tacca yadvṛttaṃ bharatarṣabha
|
diṣṭaṃ hi rājaśārdūla na śakyamativartitum ||15||
