Mahābhārata 3.13 (Āraṇyakaparvan)

1vāsudeva uvāca|
naitatkṛcchramanuprāpto bhavānsyādvasudhādhipa
|
yadyahaṃ dvārakāyāṃ syāṃ rājansaṃnihitaḥ purā||1||

2āgaccheyamahaṃ dyūtamanāhūto'pi kauravaiḥ
|
āmbikeyena durdharṣa rājñā duryodhanena ca
|
vārayeyamahaṃ dyūtaṃ bahūndoṣānpradarśayan||2||

3bhīṣmadroṇau samānāyya kṛpaṃ bāhlīkameva ca
|
vaicitravīryaṃ rājānamalaṃ dyūtena kaurava||3||

4putrāṇāṃ tava rājendra tvannimittamiti prabho
|
tatrācakṣamahaṃ doṣānyairbhavānvyatiropitaḥ||4||

5vīrasenasuto yaistu rājyātprabhraṃśitaḥ purā
|
atarkitavināśaśca devanena viśāṃpate||5||

6sātatyaṃ ca prasaṅgasya varṇayeyaṃ yathātatham||6||

7striyo'kṣā mṛgayā pānametatkāmasamutthitam
|
duḥkhaṃ catuṣṭayaṃ proktaṃ yairnaro bhraśyate śriyaḥ||7||

8tatra sarvatra vaktavyaṃ manyante śāstrakovidāḥ
|
viśeṣataśca vaktavyaṃ dyūte paśyanti tadvidaḥ||8||

9ekāhāddravyanāśo'tra dhruvaṃ vyasanameva ca
|
abhuktanāśaścārthānāṃ vākpāruṣyaṃ ca kevalam||9||

10etaccānyacca kauravya prasaṅgikaṭukodayam
|
dyūte brūyāṃ mahābāho samāsādyāmbikāsutam||10||

11evamukto yadi mayā gṛhṇīyādvacanaṃ mama
|
anāmayaṃ syāddharmaśca kurūṇāṃ kuruvardhana||11||

12na cetsa mama rājendra gṛhṇīyānmadhuraṃ vacaḥ
|
pathyaṃ ca bharataśreṣṭha nigṛhṇīyāṃ balena tam||12||

13athainamapanītena suhṛdo nāma durhṛdaḥ
|
sabhāsadonuvarteraṃstāṃśca hanyāṃ durodarān||13||

14asānnidhyaṃ tu kauravya mamānarteṣvabhūttadā
|
yenedaṃ vyasanaṃ prāptā bhavanto dyūtakāritam||14||

15so'hametya kuruśreṣṭha dvārakāṃ pāṇḍunandana
|
aśrauṣaṃ tvāṃ vyasaninaṃ yuyudhānādyathātatham||15||

16śrutvaiva cāhaṃ rājendra paramodvignamānasaḥ
|
tūrṇamabhyāgato'smi tvāṃ draṣṭukāmo viśāṃpate||16||

17aho kṛcchramanuprāptāḥ sarve sma bharatarṣabha
|
so'haṃ tvāṃ vyasane magnaṃ paśyāmi saha sodaraiḥ||17||
