Mahābhārata 13.118 (Anuśāsanaparvan)

1vyāsa uvāca|
śubhena karmaṇā yadvai tiryagyonau na muhyase
|
mamaiva kīṭa tatkarma yena tvaṃ na pramuhyase ||1||

2ahaṃ tvāṃ darśanādeva tārayāmi tapobalāt
|
tapobalāddhi balavadbalamanyanna vidyate ||2||

3jānāmi pāpaiḥ svakṛtairgataṃ tvāṃ kīṭa kīṭatām
|
avāpsyasi punardharmaṃ dharmaṃ tu yadi manyase ||3||

4karma bhūmikṛtaṃ devā bhuñjate tiryagāśca ye
|
dharmo'pi hi manuṣyeṣu kāmārthaśca tathā guṇāḥ||4||

5vāgbuddhipāṇipādaiśca vyapetasya vipaścitaḥ
|
kiṃ hāsyati manuṣyasya mandasyāpi hi jīvataḥ||5||

6jīvan ‌hi kurute pūjāṃ viprāgryaḥ śaśisūryayoḥ
|
bruvannapi kathāṃ puṇyāṃ tatra kīṭa tvameṣyasi ||6||

7guṇabhūtāni bhūtāni tatra tvamupabhokṣyase
|
tatra te'haṃ vineṣyāmi brahma tvaṃ yatra vaiṣyasi ||7||

8sa tatheti pratiśrutya kīṭo vartmanyatiṣṭhata
|
śakaṭo vrajaṃśca sumahānāgataśca yadṛcchayā ||8||

9cakrākrameṇa bhinnaśca kīṭaḥ prāṇānmumoca ha
|
sambhūtaḥ kṣatriyakule prasādāda‌mitaujasaḥ ||9||

10tamṛrṣiṃ draṣṭumagamatsarvāsvanyāsu yoniṣu
|
śvāvidgodhāvarāhāṇāṃ tathaiva mṛgapakṣiṇām ||10||

11śvapākaśūdravaiśyānāṃ kṣatriyāṇāṃ ca yoniṣu
|
sa kīṭa evamābhāṣya ṛṣiṇā satyavādinā
|
pratismṛtyātha jagrāha pādau mūrdhni kṛtāñjaliḥ||11||

12kīṭa uvāca|
idaṃ tadatulaṃ sthānamīpsitaṃ daśabhirguṇaiḥ
|
yadahaṃ prāpya kīṭatvamāgato rājaputratām ||12||

13vahanti māmatibalāḥ kuñjarā hemamālinaḥ
|
syandaneṣu ca kāṃbojā yuktāḥ paramavājinaḥ ||13||

14uṣṭrāśvatarayuktāni yānāni ca vahanti mām
|
sabāndhavaḥ sahāmātyaścāśnāmi piśitaudanam ||14||

15gṛheṣu svanivāseṣu sukheṣu śayaneṣu ca
|
varāheṣu mahābhāga svapāmi ca supūjitaḥ ||15||

16sarveṣvapararātreṣu sūtamāgadhabandinaḥ
|
stuvanti māṃ yathā devā mahendraṃ priyavādinaḥ ||16||

17prasādātsatyasandhasya bhavato'mitatejasaḥ
|
yadahaṃ kīṭatāṃ prāpya saṃprāpto rājaputratām ||17||

18namaste'stu mahāprājña kiṃ karomi praśādhi mām
|
tvattapobalanirdiṣṭamidaṃ hyadhigataṃ mayā ||18||

19vyāsa uvāca|
arcito'haṃ tvayā rājan vāgbhiradya yadṛcchayā
|
adya te kīṭatāṃ prāpya smṛtirjātā jugupsitā||19||

20na tu nāśo'sti pāpasya yastvayopacitaḥ purā
|
śudreṇārthapradhānena nṛśaṃsenātatāyinā ||20||

21mama te darśanaṃ prāptaṃ tacca vai sukṛtaṃ tvayā
|
tiryagyonau sma jātena mama cābhyarcanāttathā||21||

22itastvaṃ rājaputratvādbrāhmaṇyaṃ samavāpsyasi
|
gobrāhmaṇakṛte prāṇān hutvā''tmānaṃ raṇājire||22||

23rājaputra sukhaṃ prāpya kratūṃścaivāptadakṣiṇān
|
atha modiṣyase svarge brahmabhūto'vyayaḥ sukhī ||23||

24tiryagyonyāḥ śūdratāmabhyupaiti śūdro vaiśyaṃ kṣatriyatvaṃ ca vaiśyaḥ
|
vṛttaślāghī kṣatriyo brāhmaṇatvaṃ svargaṃ puṇyaṃ brāhmaṇaḥ sādhuvṛttaḥ ||24||
