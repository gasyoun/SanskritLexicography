Mahābhārata 14.87 (Āśvamedhikaparvan)

1yudhiṣṭhira uvāca|
śrutaṃ priyamidaṃ kṛṣṇa yattvamarhasi bhāṣitum
|
tanye'mṛtarasaṃ puṇyaṃ mano hlādayati prabho||1||

2bahūni kila yuddhāni vijayasya narādhipaiḥ
|
punarāsan hṛṣīkeśa tatra tatra ca me śrutam||2||

3kiṃ nimittaṃ sa nityaṃ hi pārthaḥ sukhavivarjitaḥ
|
atīva vijayo dhīmanniti me dūyate manaḥ||3||

4sañcintayāmi kaunteyaṃ raho jiṣṇuṃ janārdana
|
atīva duḥkhabhāgī sa satataṃ pāṇḍunandanaḥ||4||

5kiṃ nu nasya śarīre'sti sarvalakṣaṇapūjite
|
aniṣṭaṃ lakṣaṇaṃ kṛṣṇa yena duḥkhānyupāśnute||5||

6atīvāsukhabhogī sa satataṃ kuntinandanaḥ
|
na hi paśyāmi bībhatsornindyaṃ gātreṣu kiñcana
|
śrotavyaṃ cenmayai tadvai tanme vyākhyātumarhasi||6||

7ityuktaḥ sa hṛṣīkeśo dhyātvā sumahaduttaram
|
rājānaṃ bhojarājanyavardhano viṣṇurabravīt||7||

8na hyasya nṛpate kiñcitsaṃśliṣṭamupalakṣaye
|
ṛte puruṣasiṃhasya piṇḍike'syādhike yataḥ||8||

9sa tābhyāṃ puruṣavyāghro nityamadhvasu vartate
|
na cānyadanupaśyāmi yenāsau duḥkhabhājanam||9||

10ityuktaḥ puruṣaśreṣṭhastadā kṛṣṇena dhīmatā
|
provāca vṛṣṇiśārdūlamevametaditi prabho||10||

11kṛṣṇā tu draupadī kṛṣṇaṃ tiryak sāsūyamaikṣata
|
pratijagrāha tasyāstaṃ praṇayaṃ cāpi keśihā||11||

12sakhyuḥ sakhā hṛṣīkeśaḥ sākṣādiva dhanañjayaḥ
|
tatra bhīmādayaste tu kuravo yājakāśca ye||12||

13remuḥ śrutvā vicitrāṃ tāṃ dhanañjayakathaṃ śubhām
|
teṣāṃ kathayatāmeva puruṣo'rjunasaṅkathāḥ||13||

14upāyādvacanāddūto vijayasya mahātmanaḥ
|
so'bhigamya kuruśreṣṭhaṃ namaskṛtya ca buddhimān||14||

15upāyātaṃ naravyāghraṃ phālgunaṃ pratyavedayat
|
tacchutvā nṛpatistasya harṣabādhyākulekṣaṇaḥ||15||

16priyākhyānanimittaṃ vai dadau bahudhanaṃ tadā
|
tato dvitīye divase mahān śabdo vyavardhata||16||

17āgacchati naravyāghre kauravāṇāṃ dhurandhare
|
tato reṇuḥ samudbhūto vibabhau tasya vājinaḥ||17||

18abhito vartamānasya yathoccaiḥśravasastathā
|
tatra harṣakarī vāco narāṇāṃ śuśruve'rjunaḥ||18||

19diṣṭyāsi pārtha kuśalī dhanyo rājā yudhiṣṭhiraḥ
|
ko'nyo hi pṛthivīṃ kṛtsnāṃ jitvā hi yudhi pārthivān
|
cārayitvā hayaśreṣṭhamupāgacchedṛte'rjunāt
|
ye vyatītā mahātmāno rājānaḥ sagarādayaḥ||19||

20teṣāmapīdṛśaṃ karma na kadācana śuśruma
|
naitadanye kariṣyanti bhaviṣyā vasudhādhipāḥ||20||

21yattvaṃ kurukulaśreṣṭha duṣkaraṃ kṛtavānasi
|
ityevaṃ vadatāṃ teṣāṃ puṃsāṃ karṇasukhā giraḥ||21||

22śṛṇvanviveśa dharmātmā phālguno yajñasaṃstaram
|
tato rājā sahāmātyaḥ kṛṣṇaśca yadunandanaḥ||22||

23dhṛtarāṣṭra puraskṛtya taṃ pratyudyayatustadā
|
so'bhivādya pituḥ pādau dharmarājasya dhīmataḥ||23||

24bhīmādīṃścāpi sampūjya paryadhvajata keśavam
|
taiḥ sametyārcitastāṃśca pratyātha yathā vidhi||24||

25viśaśrāma mahābāhustīraṃ labdhveva pāragaḥ
|
etasminneva kāle tu sa rājā babhruvāhanaḥ||25||

26mātṛbhyāṃ sahito dhīmānkurūneva jagāma ha
|
tatra vṛddhānyathā vatsa kurūnanyāṃśca pārthivān||26||

27abhivādya mahābāhustaiścāpi pratinanditaḥ
|
praviveśa pitāmahyāḥ kuntyā bhavanamuttamam||27||
