Mahābhārata 6.49 (Bhīṣmaparvan)

1dhṛtarāṣṭra uvāca|śvete senāpatau tāta saṃgrāme nihate paraiḥ
|
kimakurvanmaheṣvāsāḥ pañcālāḥ pāṇḍavaiḥ saha ||1||

2senāpatiṃ samākarṇya śvetaṃ yudhi nipātitam
|
tadarthaṃ yatatāṃ cāpi pareṣāṃ prapalāyinām ||2||

3manaḥ prīṇāti me vākyaṃ jayaṃ saṃjaya śṛṇvataḥ
|
pratyupāyaṃ cintayantaḥ sajjanāḥ prasravanti me ||3||

4sa hi vīro'nuraktaśca vṛddhaḥ kurupatistadā
|
kṛtaṃ vairaṃ sadā tena pituḥ putreṇa dhīmatā ||4||

5tasyodvegabhayāccāpi saṃśritaḥ pāṇḍavān purā
|
sarvaṃ balaṃ parityajya durgaṃ saṃśritya tiṣṭhati ||5||

6pāṇḍavānāṃ pratāpena durgaṃ deśaṃ niveśya ca
|
sapatnān satataṃ bādhannāryavṛttimanuṣṭhitaḥ ||6||

7āścaryaṃ vai sadā teṣāṃ purā rājñāṃ sudurmatiḥ
|
tato yudhiṣṭhire bhaktaḥ kathaṃ sañjaya sūditaḥ ||7||

8prakṣiptaḥ saṃmataḥ kṣudraḥ putro me puruṣādhamaḥ
|
na yuddhaṃ rocayedbhīṣmo na cācāryaḥ kathañcana ||8||

9na kṛpo na ca gāndhārī nāhaṃ sañjaya rocaye
|
na vāsudevo vārṣṇeyo dharmarājaśca pāṇḍavaḥ ||9||

10na bhīmo nārjunaścaiva na yamau puruṣarṣabhau
|
vāryamāṇo mayā nityaṃ gāndhāryā vidureṇa ca ||10||

11jāmadagnyena rāmeṇa vyāsena ca mahātmanā
|
duryodhano yudhyamāno nityameva hi sañjaya ||11||

12karṇasya matamāsthāya saubalasya ca pāpakṛt
|
duḥśāsanasya ca tathā pāṇḍavānanvacintayat ||12||

13tasyāhaṃ vyasanaṃ ghoraṃ manye prāptaṃ tu sañjaya
|
śvetasya ca vināśena bhīṣmasya vijayena ca ||13||

14saṃkruddhaḥ kṛṣṇasahitaḥ pārthaḥ kimakarodyudhi
|
arjunāddhi bhayaṃ bhūyastanme tāta na śāmyati ||14||

15sa hi śūraśca kaunteyaḥ kṣiprakārī dhanañjayaḥ
|
manye śaraiḥ śarīrāṇi śatrūṇāṃ pramathiṣyati ||15||

16aindrimindrānujasamaṃ mahendrasadṛśaṃ bale
|
amoghakrodhasaṃkalpaṃ dṛṣṭvā vaḥ kimabhūnmanaḥ ||16||

17tathaiva veda‌vicchūro jvalanārkasamadyutiḥ
|
indrāstravida‌meyātmā prapatan samitiñjayaḥ ||17||

18vajrasaṃsparśarūpāṇāmastrāṇāṃ ca prayojakaḥ
|
sa khaḍgākṣepahastastu ghoṣaṃ cakre mahārathaḥ ||18||

19sa sañjaya mahāprājño drupadasyātmajo balī
|
ghṛṣṭadyumnaḥ kimakarocchvete yudhi nipātite ||19||

20purā caivāparādhena vadhena ca camūpateḥ
|
manye manaḥ prajajvāla pāṇḍavānāṃ mahātmanām ||20||

21teṣāṃ krodhaṃ cintayaṃstu ahaḥsu ca niśāsu ca
|
na śāntimadhigacchāmi duryodhanakṛtena hi
|
kathaṃ cābhūnmahāyuddhaṃ sarvamācakṣva sañjaya ||21||

22sañjaya uvāca|
śṛṇu rājan sthiro bhūtvā tavāpanayano mahān
|
na ca duryodhane doṣamimamādhātumarhasi ||22||

23gatodake setubandho yādṛktādṛṅmatistava
|
saṃdīpte bhavane yadvatkūpasya khananaṃ tathā ||23||

24gatapūrvāhṇabhūyiṣṭhe tasminnahani dāruṇe
|
tāvakānāṃ pareṣāṃ ca punaryuddhamavartata ||24||

25śvetaṃ tu nihataṃ dṛṣṭvā virāṭasya camūpatim
|
kṛtavarmaṇā ca sahitaṃ dṛṣṭvā śalyamavasthitam ||25||

26śaṅkhaḥ krodhāt prajajvāla haviṣā havyavāḍiva
|
sa visphārya mahaccāpaṃ śakracāpopamaṃ balī ||26||

27abhyadhāvajjighāṃsan vai śalyaṃ madrādhipaṃ yudhi
|
mahatā rathasaṅghena samantāt parirakṣitaḥ ||27||

28sṛjan bāṇamayaṃ varṣaṃ prāyācchalyarathaṃ prati
|
tamāpatantaṃ saṃprekṣya mattavāraṇavikramam ||28||

29tāvakānāṃ rathāḥ sapta samantāt paryavārayan
|
madrarājaṃ parīpsanto mṛtyordaṃṣṭrāntaraṃ gatam ||29||

30bṛhadbalaśca kausalyo jayatsenaśca māgadhaḥ
|
tathā rukmaratho rājan putraḥ śalyasya mānitaḥ ||30||

31vindānuvindāvāvantyau kāṃbojaśca sudakṣiṇaḥ
|
bṛhatkṣatrasya dāyādaḥ saindhavaśca jayadrathaḥ ||31||

32nānādhātuvicitrāṇi kārmukāṇi mahātmanām
|
visphāritānyadṛśyanta toyadeṣviva vidyutaḥ ||32||

33te tu bāṇamayaṃ varṣaṃ śaṃkhamūrdhni nyapātayan
|
nidāghānte'niloddhūtā meghā iva nage jalam ||33||

34tataḥ kruddho maheṣvāsaḥ saptabhallaiḥ sutejanaiḥ
|
dhanūṃṣi teṣāmācchidya nanarda pṛtanāpatiḥ ||34||

35tato bhīṣmo mahābāhurvinadya jalado yathā
|
tālamātraṃ dhanurgṛhya śaṅkhamabhyadravadraṇe ||35||

36tamudyantamudīkṣyātha maheṣvāsaṃ mahābalam
|
saṃtrastā pāṇḍavī senā vātavegahateva nauḥ ||36||

37tato'rjunaḥ saṃtvaritaḥ śaṅkhasyāsīt puraḥsaraḥ
|
bhaṣmiādrakṣyo'yamadyeti tato yuddhamavartata ||37||

38hāhākāro mahānāsīdyodhānāṃ yudhi yudhyatām
|
tejastejasi saṃpṛktamityevaṃ vismayaṃ yayuḥ ||38||

39atha śalyo gadāpāṇiravatīrya mahārathāt
|
śaṅkhasya caturo vāhānahanadbharatarṣabha ||39||

40sa hatāśvādrathāt tūrṇaṃ khaḍgamādāya vidrutaḥ
|
bībhatsośca rathaṃ prāpya punaḥ śāntimavindata ||40||

41tato bhīṣmarathāt tūrṇamutpatanti patattriṇaḥ
|
yairantarikṣaṃ bhūmiśca sarvataḥ samavastṛtā ||41||

42pañcālānatha matsyāṃśca kekayāṃśca prabhadrakān
|
bhīṣmaḥ praharatāṃ śreṣṭhaḥ pātayāmāsa patribhiḥ ||42||

43utsṛjya samare rājan pāṇḍavaṃ savyasācinam
|
abhyadravata pāñcālyaṃ drupadaṃ senayā vṛtam ||43||

44priyaṃ saṃbandhinaṃ rājan śarānavakiran bahūn
|
agnineva pradagdhāni vanāni śiśirātyaye ||44||

45śaradagdhānyadṛśyanta sainyāni drupadasya ha
|
atyatiṣṭhadraṇe bhīṣmo vidhūma iva pāvakaḥ ||45||

46madhyaṃdine yathā''dityaṃ tapantamiva tejasā
|
na śekuḥ pāṇḍaveyasya yodhā bhīṣmaṃ nirīkṣitum ||46||

47vīkṣāñcakruḥ samantāt te pāṇḍavā bhayapīḍitāḥ
|
trātāraṃ nādhyagacchanta gāvaḥ śītārditā iva ||47||

48sā tu yaudhiṣṭhirī senā gāṅgeyaśarapīḍitā
|
siṃheneva vinirbhinnā śuklā gauriva gopateḥ ||48||

49hate vipradrute sainye nirutsāhe vimardite
|
hāhākāro mahānāsīt pāṇḍusainyeṣu bhārata ||49||

50tato bhīṣmaḥ śāntanavo nityaṃ maṇḍalakārmukaḥ
|
mumoca bāṇān dīptāgrānahīnāśīviṣāniva ||50||

51śarairekāyanīkurvan diśaḥ sarvā yatavrataḥ
|
jaghāna pāṇḍavarathānādiśyādiśya bhārata ||51||

52tataḥ sainyeṣu bhagneṣu mathiteṣu ca sarvaśaḥ
|
prāpte cāstaṃ dinakare na prājñāyata kiñcana ||52||

53bhīṣmaṃ ca samudīryantaṃ dṛṣṭvā pārthā mahāhave
|
avahāramakurvanta sainyānāṃ bharatarṣabha ||53||
