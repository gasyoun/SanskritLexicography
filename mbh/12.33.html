Mahābhārata 12.33 (Śāntiparvan)

1yudhiṣṭhira uvāca|
hatāḥ putrāśca pautrāśca bhrātaraḥ pitarastathā
|
śvaśurā guravaścaiva mātulāśca pitāmahāḥ ||1||

2kṣatriyāśca mahātmānaḥ sambandhisuhṛdastathā
|
vayasyā bhāgineyāśca jñātayaśca pitāmaha ||2||

3bahavaśca manuṣyendrā nānādeśasamāgatāḥ
|
ghātitā rājyalubdhena mayaikena pitāmaha ||3||

4tāṃstādṛśānahaṃ hatvā dharmanityānmahīkṣitaḥ
|
asakṛtsomapānvīrānkiṃ prāpsyāmi tapodhana ||4||

5dahyāmyaniśamadyāpi cintayānaḥ punaḥ punaḥ
|
hīnāṃ pārthivasiṃhaistaiḥ śrīmadbhiḥ pṛthivīmimām ||5||

6dṛṣṭvā jñātivadhaṃ ghoraṃ hantāṃśca śataśaḥ parān
|
koṭiśaśca narānanyānparitapye pitāmaha ||6||

7kā nu tāsāṃ varastrīṇāmavasthā'dya bhaviṣyati
|
vihīnānāṃ tu tanayaiḥ patibhirbhrātṛbhistathā ||7||

8asmānantakarānghorān pāṇḍavānvṛṣṇisaṃhatān
|
ākrośantyaḥ kṛśā dīnāḥ prapatiṣyanti bhūtale ||8||

9apaśyantyaḥ pitṝn bhrātṝn patīnputrāṃśca yoṣitaḥ
|
tyaktvā prāṇān striyaḥ sarvā gamiṣyanti yamakṣayam ||9||

10vatsalatvāddvijaśreṣṭha tatra me nāsti saṃśayaḥ
|
vyaktaṃ saukṣyācca dharmasya prāpsyāmaḥ strīvadhaṃ vayam ||10||

11yadvayaṃ suhṛdo hatvā kṛtvā pāpamanantakam
|
narake nipatiṣyāmo hyadhaḥśirasa eva ha ||11||

12śarīrāṇi vimokṣyāmastapasogreṇa sattama
|
āśramāṇāṃ viśeṣaṃ tvamathācakṣva pitāmaha ||12||

13vaiśampāyana uvāca|
yudhiṣṭhirasya tadvākyaṃ śrutvā dvaipāyanastadā
|
nirīkṣya nipuṇaṃ buddhyā ṛṣiḥ provāca pāṇḍavam ||13||

14vyāsa uvāca |
mā viṣādaṃ kṛthā rājankṣatradharmamanusmaran
|
svadharmeṇa hatā hyete kṣatriyāḥ kṣatriyarṣabha ||14||

15kāṃkṣamāṇāḥ śriyaṃ kṛtsnāṃ pṛthivyāṃ ca mahadyaśaḥ
|
kṛtāntavidhisaṃyuktāḥ kālena nidhanaṃ gatāḥ ||15||

16na tvaṃ hantā na bhīmo'yaṃ nārjuno na yamāvapi
|
kālaḥ paryāyadharmeṇa prāṇānādatta dehinām ||16||

17na tasya mātāpitarau nānugrāhyo hi kaścana
|
karmasākṣī prajānāṃ yastena kālena saṃhṛtāḥ ||17||

18hetumātramidaṃ tasya vihitaṃ bharatarṣabha
|
yaddhanti bhūtairbhūtāni tadasmai rūpamaiśvaram ||18||

19karmasūtrātmakaṃ viddhi sākṣiṇaṃ śubhapāpayoḥ
|
sukhaduḥkhaguṇodarkaṃ kālaṃ kālaphalapradam ||19||

20teṣāmapi mahābāho karmāṇi paricintaya
|
vināśahetukāni tvaṃ yaiste kālavaśaṃ gatāḥ ||20||

21ātmanaśca vijānīhi niyatavrataśāsanam
|
yadā tvamīdṛśaṃ karma vidhinā''kramya kāritaḥ ||21||

22tvaṣṭreva vihitaṃ yantraṃ yathā ceṣṭayiturvaśe
|
karmaṇā kālayuktena tathedaṃ ceṣṭate jagat ||22||

23puruṣasya hi dṛṣṭvemāmutpattimanimittataḥ
|
yadṛcchayā vināśaṃ ca śokaharṣāvanarthakau ||23||

24vyalīkamapi yattvatra cittavaitaṃsikaṃ tava
|
tadarthamiṣyate rājanprāyaścittaṃ tadācara ||24||

25idaṃ tu śrūyate pārtha yuddhe devāsure purā
|
asurā bhrātaro jyeṣṭhā devāścāpi yavīyasaḥ ||25||

26teṣāmapi śrīnimittaṃ mahānāsītsamucchrayaḥ
|
yuddhaṃ varṣasahasrāṇi dvātriṃśadabhavatkila ||26||

27ekārṇavāṃ mahīṃ kṛtvā rudhireṇa pariplutām
|
jaghnurdaityāṃstathā devāstridivaṃ cābhilebhire ||27||

28tathaiva pṛthivīṃ labdhvā brāhmaṇā vedapāragāḥ
|
saṃśritā dānavānāṃ vai sāhyārthaṃ darpamohitāḥ ||28||

29śālāvṛkā iti khyātāstriṣu lokeṣu bhārata
|
aṣṭāśītisahasrāṇi te cāpi vibudhairhatāḥ ||29||

30dharmavyucchittimicchanto ye'dharmasya pravartakāḥ
|
hantavyāste durātmāno devairdaityā ivolbaṇāḥ ||30||

31ekaṃ hatvā yadi kule śiṣṭānāṃ syādanāmayam
|
kulaṃ hatvā ca rāṣṭre ca na tadvṛttopaghātakam ||31||

32adharmarūpo dharmo hi kaścidasti narādhipa
|
dharmaścādharmarūpo'sti tacca jñeyaṃ vipaścitā ||32||

33tasmātsaṃstambhayātmānaṃ śrutavānasi pāṇḍava
|
devaiḥ pūrvagataṃ mārgamanuyāto'si bhārata ||33||

34na hīdṛśā gamiṣyanti narakaṃ pāṇḍavarṣabha
|
bhrātṝnāśvāsayaitāṃstvaṃ suhṛdaśca parantapa ||34||

35yo hi pāpasamārambhe kārye tadbhāvabhāvitaḥ
|
kurvannapi tathaiva syātkṛtvā ca nirapatrapaḥ ||35||

36tasmiṃstatkaluṣaṃ sarvaṃ samāptamiti śabditam
|
prāyaścittaṃ na tasyāsti hrāso vā pāpakarmaṇaḥ ||36||

37tvaṃ tu śuklābhijātīyaḥ paradoṣeṇa kāritaḥ
|
anicchamānaḥ karmedaṃ kṛtvā ca paritapyase ||37||

38aśvamedho mahāyajñaḥ prāyaścittamudāhṛtam
|
tamāhara mahārāja vipāpmaivaṃ bhaviṣyasi ||38||

39marudbhiḥ saha jitvā'rīnbhagavānpākaśāsanaḥ
|
ekaikaṃ kratumāhṛtya śatakṛtva: śatakratuḥ ||39||

40dhūtapāpmā jitasvargo lokānprāpya sukhodayān
|
marudgaṇairvṛtaḥ śakraḥ śuśubhe bhāsayandiśaḥ ||40||

41svarge loke mahīyantamapsarobhiḥ śacīpatim
|
ṛṣayaḥ paryupāsante devāśca vibudheśvaram ||41||

42seyaṃ tvāmanusamprāptā vikrameṇa vasundharā
|
nirjitāśca mahīpālā vikrameṇa tvayā'nadha ||42||

43teṣāṃ purāṇi rāṣṭrāṇi gatvā rājansuhṛdvṛtaḥ
|
bhrātṝnputrāṃśca pautrāṃśca sve sve rājye'bhiṣecaya ||43||

44bālānapi ca garbhasthānsāntvena samudācaran
|
rañjayanprakṛtīḥ sarvāḥ paripāhi vasundharām ||44||

45kumāro nāsti yeṣāṃ ca kanyāstatrābhiṣecaya
|
kāmāśayo hi strīvargaḥ śokamevaṃ prahāsyasi ||45||

46evamāśvāsanaṃ kṛtvā sarvarāṣṭreṣu bhārata
|
yajasva vājimedhena yathendro vijayī purā ||46||

47aśocyāste mahātmānaḥ kṣatriyāḥ kṣatriyarṣabha
|
svakarmabhirgatā nāśaṃ kṛtāntabalamohitāḥ ||47||

48avāptaḥ kṣatradharmaste rājyaṃ prāptamakaṇṭakam
|
rakṣasva dharmaṃ kaunteya śreyānyaḥ pretya bhārata ||48||
