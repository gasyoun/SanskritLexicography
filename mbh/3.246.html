Mahābhārata 3.246 (Āraṇyakaparvan)

1vaiśampāyana uvāca|
tato'rjunaścitrasenaṃ prahasannidamabravīt
|
madhye gandharvasainyānāṃ maheṣvāso mahādyutiḥ||1||

2kiṃ te vyavasitaṃ vīra kauravāṇāṃ vinigrahe
|
kimarthaṃ ca sadāro'yaṃ nigṛhītaḥ suyodhanaḥ||2||

3citrasena uvāca|
vidito'yamabhiprāyastatrasthena durātmanaḥ
|
duryodhanasya pāpasya karṇasya ca dhanañjaya||3||

4vanasthān bhavato jñātvā kliśyamānānanāthavat
|
samastho viṣamasthāṃstān drakṣyāmītyanavasthitān||4||

5ime'vahasituṃ prāptā draupadīṃ ca yaśasvinīm
|
jñātvā cikīrṣitaṃ caiṣāṃ māmuvāca sureśvaraḥ||5||

6gaccha duryodhanaṃ baddhvā sahāmātyamihānaya
|
dhanañjayaśca te rakṣyaḥ saha bhrātṛbhirāhave||6||

7sa ca priyaḥ sakhā tubhyaṃ śiṣyaśca tava pāṇḍava
|
vacanāddevarājasya tato'smīhāgato drutam||7||

8ayaṃ durātmā baddhaśca gamiṣyāmi surālayam
|
neṣyāmyenaṃ durātmānaṃ pākaśāsanaśāsanāt||8||

9arjuna uvāca|
utsṛjyatāṃ citrasena bhrātā'smākaṃ suyodhanaḥ
|
dharmarājasya sandeśānmama cedicchasi priyam||9||

10citrasena uvāca|
pāpo'yaṃ nityasantuṣṭo na vimokṣaṇamarhati
|
pralabdhā dharmarājasya kṛṣṇāyāśca dhanañjaya||10||

11nedaṃ cikīrṣitaṃ tasya kuntīputro yudhiṣṭhiraḥ
|
jānāti dharmarājo hi śrutvā kuru yathecchasi||11||

12vaiśampāyana uvāca|
te sarva eva rājānamabhijagmuryudhiṣṭhiram
|
abhigamya ca tat sarvaṃ śaśaṃsustasya ceṣṭitam||12||

13ajātaśatrustacchrutvā gandharvasya vacastadā
|
mokṣayāmāsa tān sarvān gandharvān praśaśaṃsa ca||13||

14diṣṭyā bhavadbhirbalibhiḥ śaktaiḥ sarvairna hiṃsitaḥ
|
durvṛtto dhārtarāṣṭro'yaṃ sāmātyajñātibāndhavaḥ||14||

15upakāro mahāṃstāta kṛto'yaṃ mama khecaraiḥ
|
kulaṃ na paribhūtaṃ me mokṣaṇe'sya durātmanaḥ||15||

16ājñāpayadhvamiṣṭāni prīyāmo darśanena vaḥ
|
prāpya sarvānabhiprāyāṃstato vrajata mā ciram||16||

17anujñātāstu gandharvāḥ pāṇḍuputreṇa dhīmatā
|
sahāpsarobhiḥ saṃhṛṣṭāścitrasenamukhā yayuḥ||17||

18devarāḍapi gandharvān mṛtāṃstān samajīvayat
|
divyenāmṛtavarṣeṇa ye hatāḥ kauravairyudhi||18||

19jñātīṃstānavamucyātha rājadārāṃśca sarvaśaḥ
|
kṛtvā ca duṣkaraṃ karma prītiyuktāśca pāṇḍavāḥ||19||

20sastrīkumāraiḥ kurubhiḥ pūjyamānā mahārathāḥ
|
babhrājire mahātmānaḥ kratumadhye yathā'gnayaḥ||20||

21tato duryodhanaṃ muktaṃ bhrātṛbhiḥ sahitastadā
|
yudhiṣṭhirastu praṇayādidaṃ vacanamabravīt||21||

22mā sma tāta punaḥ kārṣīrīdṛśaṃ sāhasaṃ kvacit
|
na hi sāhasakartāraḥ sukhamedhanti bhārata||22||

23svastimān sahitaḥ sarvairbhātṛbhiḥ kurunandana
|
gṛhān vraja mathākāmaṃ vaimanasyaṃ ca mā kṛthāḥ||23||

24vaiśampāyana uvāca|
pāṇḍavenābhyanujñāto rājā duryodhanastadā
|
abhivādya dharmaputraṃ gatendriya ivāturaḥ||24||

25vidīryamāṇo vrīḍāvān jagāma nagaraṃ prati
|
tasmin gate kauraveye kuntīputro yudhiṣṭhiraḥ||25||

26bhrātṛbhiḥ sahito vīraḥ pūjyamāno dvijātibhiḥ
|
tapodhanaiśca taiḥ sarvairvṛtaḥ śakra ivāmaraiḥ||26||

27tathā dvaitavane tasmin vijahāra mudā yutaḥ||27||
