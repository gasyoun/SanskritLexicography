Mahābhārata 9.31 (Śalyaparvan)

1sañjaya uvāca|
tatasteṣvapayāteṣu ratheṣu triṣu pāṇḍavāḥ
|
te hradaṃ pratyapadyanta yatra duryodhano'bhavat||1||

2āsādya ca kuruśreṣṭha tadā dvaipāyanaṃ hradam
|
stambhitaṃ dhārtarāṣṭreṇa dṛṣṭvā taṃ salilāśayam||2||

3vāsudevamidaṃ vākyamabravītkurunandanaḥ
|
paśyemāṃ dhārtarāṣṭreṇa māyāmapsu prayojitām||3||

4viṣṭabhya salilaṃ śete nāsya mānuṣato bhayam
|
daivīṃ māyāmimāṃ kṛtvā salilāntargato hyayam||4||

5nikṛtyā nikṛtiprajño na me jīvanvimokṣyate
|
yadyasya samare sāhyaṃ kurute vajrabhṛtsvayam||5||

6tathāpyenaṃ hayaṃ yuddhe lokā drakṣyanti mādhava
|
vāsudeva uvāca|
māyāvina imāṃ māyāṃ māyayā jahi bhārata||6||

7māyāvī māyayā vadhyaḥ satyametadyudhiṣṭhira
|
kriyābhyupāyairbahubhirmāyāmapsu prayojya ca||7||

8jahi tvaṃ bharataśreṣṭha māyātmānaṃ suyodhanam
|
kriyābhyupāyairindreṇa nihatā daityadānavāḥ||8||

9kriyābhyupāyairbahubhirbalirbaddho mahātmanā
|
kriyābhyupāyairbahubhirhiraṇyākṣo mahāsuraḥ||9||

10hiraṇyakaśipuścaiva kriyayaiva niṣūditau
|
vṛtraśca nihato rājan kriyayaiva na saṃśayaḥ||10||

11tathā paulastyatanayo rāvaṇo nāma rākṣasaḥ
|
rāmeṇa nihato rājan sānubandhaḥ sahānugaḥ||11||

12kriyayā yogamāsthāya tathā tvamapi vikrama
|
kriyābhyupāyairnihatau mayā rājan purātanau||12||

13tārakaśca mahādaityo vipracittiśca vīryavān
|
vātāpirilvalaścaiva triśirāśca tathā vibho||13||

14sundopasundāvasurau kriyayaiva niṣūditau
|
kriyābhyupāyairindreṇa tridivaṃ bhujyate vibho||14||

15kriyā balavatī rājan nānyatkiñcidyudhiṣṭhira
|
daityāśca dānavāścaiva rākṣasāḥ pārthivāstathā||15||

16kriyābhyupāyairnihatāḥ kriyāṃ tasmātsamācara
|
sañjaya uvāca|
ityukto vāsudevena pāṇḍavaḥ saṃśitavrataḥ||16||

17jalasthaṃ taṃ mahārāja tava putraṃ mahābalam
|
abhyabhāṣata kaunteyaḥ prahasanniva bhārata||17||

18suyodhana kimartho'yamārambho'psu kṛtastvayā
|
sarvaṃ kṣatraṃ ghātayitvā svakulaṃ ca viśāmpate||18||

19jalāśayaṃ praviṣṭo'dya vāñchañjīvitamātmanaḥ
|
uttiṣṭha rājanyudhyasva sahāsmābhiḥ suyodhana||19||

20sa te darpo naraśreṣṭha sa ca mānaḥ kva te gataḥ
|
yastvaṃ saṃstabhya salilaṃ bhīto rājan vyavasthitaḥ||20||

21sarve tvāṃ śūra ityevaṃ janā jalpanti saṃsadi
|
vyarthaṃ tadbhavato manye śauryaṃ salilaśāyinaḥ||21||

22uttiṣṭha rājan yudhyasva kṣatriyo'si kulodbhavaḥ
|
kauraveyo viśeṣeṇa kulaṃ janmaṃ ca saṃsmara||22||

23sa kathaṃ kaurave vaṃśe praśaṃsañjanma cātmanaḥ
|
yuddhādbhītastatastoyaṃ praviśya pratitiṣṭhasi||23||

24ayuddhamavyasthānaṃ naiṣa dharmaḥ sanātanaḥ
|
anāryajuṣṭamasvargyaṃ raṇe rājanpalāyanam||24||

25kathaṃ pāramagatvā hi yuddhe tvaṃ vai jijīvīṣuḥ
|
imānnipatitān dṛṣṭvā putrān bhrātṝn pitṝṃstathā||25||

26sambandhino vayasyāṃśca mātulān bāndhavāṃstathā
|
ghātayitvā kathaṃ tāta hrade tiṣṭhasi sāmpratam||26||

27śūramānī na śūrastvaṃ mṛṣā vadasi bhārata
|
śūro'hamiti durbuddhe sarvalokasya śṛṇvataḥ||27||

28na hi śūrāḥ palāyante śatrūn dṛṣṭvā kathañcana
|
brūhi vā tvaṃ yayā vṛttyā śūra tyajasi saṅgaram||28||

29sa tvamuttiṣṭha yudhyasva vinīya bhayamātmanaḥ
|
ghātayitvā sarvasainyaṃ bhrātṝṃścaiva suyodhana||29||

30nedānīṃ jīvite buddhiḥ kāryā dharmacikīrṣayā
|
kṣatradharmamupāśritya tvadvidhena suyodhana||30||

31yattu karṇamupāśritya śakuniṃ cāpi saubalam
|
amartya iva sammohāttvamātmānaṃ na buddhavān||31||

32tatpāpaṃ sumahatkṛtvā pratiyuddhyasva bhārata
|
kathaṃ hi tvadvidho mohādrocayeta palāyanam||32||

33kva te tat pauruṣaṃ yātaṃ kva ca mānaḥ suyodhana
|
kva ca vikrāntatā yātā kva ca visphūrjitaṃ mahat||33||

34kva te kṛtāstratā yātā kiñca śeṣe jalāśaye
|
sa tvamuttiṣṭha yudhyasva kṣatradharmeṇa bhārata||34||

35asmāṃstu vā parājitya praśādhi pṛthivīmimām
|
athavā nihato'smābhirbhūmau svapsyasi bhārata||35||

36eṣa te paramo dharmaḥ sṛṣṭo dhātrā mahātmanā
|
taṃ kuruṣva yathātathyaṃ rājā bhava mahāratha||36||

37sañjaya uvāca|
evamukto mahārāja dharmaputreṇa dhīmatā
|
salilasthastava suta idaṃ vacanamabravīt||37||

38duryodhana uvāca|
naitaccitraṃ mahārāja yadbhīḥ prāṇinamāviśet
|
na ca prāṇabhayādbhīto vyapayāto'smi bhārata||38||

39arathaścāniṣaṅgī ca nihataḥ pārṇisārathiḥ
|
ekaścāpyagaṇaḥ saṅkhye pratyāśvāsamarocayam||39||

40na prāṇahetorna bhayānna viṣādādviśāmpate
|
idamambhaḥ praviṣṭo'smi śramāttvidamanuṣṭhitam||40||

41tvaṃ cāśvasihi kaunteya ye cāpyanugatāstava
|
ahamutthāya vaḥ sarvānpratiyotsyāmi saṃyuge||41||

42yudhiṣṭhira uvāca|
āśvastā eva sarve sma ciraṃ tvāṃ mṛgayāmahe
|
tadidānī samuttiṣṭha yudhyasveha suyodhana||42||

43hatvā vā samare pārthān sphītaṃ rājyamavāpnuhi
|
nihato vā raṇe'smābhirvīralokamavāpsyasi||43||

44duryodhana uvāca|
yadarthaṃ rājyamicchāmi kurūṇāṃ kurunandana
|
ta ime nihatāḥ sarve bhrātaro me janeśvara||44||

45kṣīṇaratnāṃ ca pṛthivīṃ hatakṣatriyapuṅgavām
|
na hyutsahāmyahaṃ bhoktuṃ vidhavāmiva yoṣitam||45||

46adyāpi tvahamāśaṃse tvahamāśaṃse tvāṃ vijetuṃ yudhiṣṭhira
|
bhaṅktvā pāñcālapāṇḍūnāmutsāhaṃ bharatarṣabha||46||

47na tvidānīmāhaṃ manye kāryaṃ yuddhena karhicit
|
droṇe karṇe ca saṃśānte nihate ca pitāmahe||47||

48astvidānīmiyaṃ rājan kevalā pṛthivī tava
|
asahāyo hi ko rājā rājyamicchetatpraśāsitum||48||

49suhṛdastādṛśān hitvā putrān bhrātṝn pitṝnapi
|
bhavadbhiśca hṛte rājye ko nu jīveta mādṛśaḥ||49||

50ahaṃ vanaṃ gamiṣyāmi hyajinaiḥ prativāsitaḥ
|
ratirhi nāsti me rājye hatapakṣasya bhārata||50||

51hatabāndhavabhūyiṣṭhā hatāśvā hatakuñjarā
|
eṣā te pṛthivī rājan bhuṃkṣvaināṃ vigatajvaraḥ||51||

52vanameva gamiṣyāmi vasāno mṛgacarmaṇī
|
na hi me nirjanasyāsti jīvite'dya spṛhā vibho||52||

53gaccha tvaṃ bhukṣva rājendra pṛthivīṃ nihateśvarām
|
hatayodhāṃ naṣṭaratnāṃ kṣīṇavṛttiryathāsukham||53||

54sañjaya uvāca|
duryodhanaṃ tava sutaṃ salilasthaṃ mahāyaśāḥ
|
śrutvā tu karuṇaṃ vākyamabhāṣata yudhiṣṭhiraḥ||54||

55yudhiṣṭhira uvāca|
ārtapralāpānmā tāta salilasthaḥ prabhāṣithāḥ
|
naitanmanasi me rājan vāśitaṃ śakuneriva||55||

56yadi vāpi samarthaḥ syāstvaṃ dānāya suyodhana
|
nāhamiccheyamavaniṃ tvayā dattāṃ praśāsitum||56||

57adharmeṇa na gṛhṇīyāṃ tvayā dattāṃ mahīmimām
|
na hi dharmaḥ smṛto rājan kṣatriyasya pratigrahaḥ||57||

58tvayā dattāṃ na ceccheyaṃ pṛthivīmakhilāmaham
|
tvāṃ tu yuddhe vinirjitya bhoktāsmi vasudhāmimām||58||

59anīśvaraśca pṛthivīṃ kathaṃ tvaṃ dātumicchasi
|
tvayeyaṃ pṛthivī rājan kinna dattā tadaiva hi||59||

60dharmato yācamānānāṃ praśamārthaṃ kulasya naḥ
|
vārṣṇeyaṃ prathamaṃ rājan pratyākhyāya mahābalam||60||

61kimidānīṃ dadāsi tvaṃ ko hi ke cittavibhramaḥ
|
abhiyuktastu ko rājā dātumiccheddhi medinīm||61||

62na tvamadya mahīṃ dātumīśaḥ kauravanandana
|
ācchettuṃ vā balādrājan sa kathaṃ dātumicchasi||62||

63māṃ tu nirjitya saṅgrāme pālayemāṃ vasundharām
|
sūcyagreṇāpi yadbhūmerapi bhidyeta bhārata||63||

64tanmātramapi tanmahyaṃ na dadāti purā bhavān
|
sa kathaṃ pṛthivīmetāṃ pradadāsi viśāmpate||64||

65sūcyagraṃ nātyajaḥ pūrvaṃ sa kathaṃ tyajasi kṣitim
|
evamaiśvaryamāsādya praśāsya pṛthivīmimām||65||

66ko hi mūḍho vyavasyeta śatrordātuṃ vasundharām
|
tvaṃ tu kevalamaurkhyeṇa vimūḍho nāvabuddhyase||66||

67pṛthivīṃ dātukāmo'pi jīvitena vimokṣyase
|
asmānvā tvaṃ parājitya praśādhi pṛthivīmimām||67||

68athavā nihato'smābhirvraja lokānanuttamān
|
āvayorjīvato rājan mayi ca tvayi ca dhruvam||68||

69saṃśayaḥ sarvabhūtānāṃ vijaye nau bhaviṣyati
|
jīvitaṃ tava duṣprajñaṃ mayi samprati vartate||69||

70jīvayeyamahaṃ kāmaṃ na tu tvaṃ jīvituṃ kṣamaḥ
|
dahane hi kṛto yatnastvayāsmāsu viśeṣataḥ||70||

71āśīviṣairviṣaiścāpi jale cāpi praveśanaiḥ
|
tvayā vinikṛtā rājan rājyasya haraṇena ca||71||

72apriyāṇāṃ ca vacanairdraupadyāḥ karṣaṇena ca
|
etasmātkāraṇātpāpa jīvitaṃ te na vidyate||72||

73uttiṣṭhottiṣṭha yudhyasva yuddhe śreyo bhaviṣyati
|
evaṃ tu vividhā vāco jayayuktāḥ punaḥ punaḥ
|
kīrtayanti sma te vīrāstatra tatra janādhipa||73||
