Mahābhārata 3.209 (Āraṇyakaparvan)

1mārkaṇḍeya uvāca|
dharmavyādhastu nipuṇaṃ punareva yudhiṣṭhira
|
viprarṣabhamuvācedaṃ sarvadharmabhṛtāṃ varaḥ||1||

2vyādha uvāca|
śrutipramāṇo dharmo'yamiti vṛddhānuśāsanam
|
sūkṣmā gatirhi dharmasya bahuśākhā hyanantikā||2||

3prāṇāntike vivāhe ca vaktavyamanṛtaṃ bhavet
|
anṛtena bhavet satyaṃ satyenaivānṛtaṃ bhavet||3||

4yadbhūtahitamatyantaṃ tatsatyamiti dhāraṇā
|
viparyayakṛto'dharmaḥ paśya dharmasya sūkṣmatām||4||

5yat karotyaśubhaṃ karma śubhaṃ vā yadi sattama
|
avaśyaṃ tat samāpnoti puruṣo nātra saṃśayaḥ||5||

6viṣamāṃ ca daśāṃ prāpto devān garhati vai bhṛśam
|
ātmanaḥ karmadoṣāṇi na vijānātyapaṇḍitaḥ||6||

7mūḍho naikṛtikaścāpi capalaśca dvijottama
|
sukhaduḥkhaviparyāsān sadā samupapadyate||7||

8nainaṃ prajñā sunītaṃ vā trāyate naiva pauruṣam
|
yo'yamicchedyathā kāmaṃ taṃ taṃ kāmaṃ sa āpnuyāt||8||

9yadi syādaparādhīnaṃ pauruṣasya kriyāphalam
|
saṃyatāścāpi dakṣāśca matimantaśca mānavāḥ||9||

10dṛśyante niṣphalāḥ santaḥ prahīṇāḥ sarvakarmabhiḥ
|
bhūtānāmaparaḥ kaściddhiṃsāyāṃ satatotthitaḥ||10||

11vañcanāyāṃ ca lokasya sa sukhī jīvate sadā
|
aceṣṭamapi cāsīnaṃ śrīḥ kañcidupatiṣṭhati||11||

12kaścit karmāṇi kurvan hi na prāpyamadhigacchati
|
devāniṣṭvā tapastaptvā kṛpaṇaiḥ putragṛddhibhiḥ||12||

13daśamāsadhṛtā garbhe jāyante kulapāṃsanāḥ
|
apare dhanadhānyaiśca bhogaiśca pitṛsañcitaiḥ||13||

14vipulairabhijāyante labdhāstaireva maṅgalaiḥ
|
karmajā hi manuṣyāṇāṃ rogā nāstyatra saṃśayaḥ||14||

15vyādhayo vinivāryante vyādhaiḥ kṣudramṛgā iva
|
te cāpi kuśalairvaidyairnipuṇaiḥ saṃbhṛtauṣadhaiḥ||15||

16vyādhayo vinivāryante mṛgā vyādhairiva dvija
|
yeṣāmasti ca bhoktavyaṃ grahaṇīdoṣapīḍitāḥ||16||

17na śaknuvanti te bhoktuṃ paśya dharmabhṛtāṃ vara
|
apare bāhubalinaḥ kliśyanti bahavo janāḥ||17||

18duḥkhena cādhigacchanti bhojanaṃ dvijasattama
|
iti lokamanākrandaṃ mohaśokapariplutam||18||

19srotasā'sakṛdākṣiptaṃ hriyamāṇaṃ balīyasā
|
na mriyeyurna jīryeyuḥ sarve syuḥ sarvakāmikāḥ||19||

20nāpriyaṃ pratipaśyeyurvaśitvaṃ yadi vai bhavet
|
uparyupari lokasya savoṃ gantuṃ samīhate
|
yatate ca yathāśakti na ca tadvartate tathā||20||

21bahavaḥ sampradṛśyante tulyanakṣatramaṅgalāḥ
|
mahat tu phalavaiṣamyaṃ dṛśyate karmasandhiṣu||21||

22na kecidīśate brahman svayaṃgrāhyasya sattama
|
karmaṇā prākṛtānāṃ vai iha siddhiḥ pradṛśyate||22||

23yayāśrutiriyaṃ brahman jīvaḥ kila sanātanaḥ
|
śarīramadhruvaṃ loke sarveṣāṃ prāṇināmiha||23||

24vadhyamāne śarīre tu dehanāśo bhavatyuta
|
jīvaḥ saṅkramate'nyatra karmabandhanibandhanaḥ||24||

25brāhmaṇa uvāca|
kathaṃ karmavidāṃ śreṣṭha jīvo bhavati śāśvataḥ
|
etadicchāmyahaṃ jñātuṃ tattvena vadatāṃ vara||25||

26vyādha uvāca|
na jīvanāśo'sti hi dehabhede mithyaitadāhurmriyatīti mūḍhāḥ
|
jīvastu dehāntaritaḥ prayāti daśārdhataivāsya śarīrabhedaḥ||26||

27anyo hi nāśnāti kṛtaṃ hi karma manuṣyaloke manujasya kaścit
|
yat tena kiñciddhi kṛtaṃ hi karma tadaśnute nāsti kṛtasya nāśaḥ||27||

28supuṇyaśīlā hi bhavanti puṇyā narādhamāḥ pāpakṛto bhavanti
|
naro'nuyātāstviha karmabhiḥ svaistataḥ samutpadyati bhāvitastaiḥ||28||

29brāhmaṇa uvāca|
kathaṃ sambhavate yonau kathaṃ vā puṇyapāpayoḥ
|
jātīḥ puṇyāstvapuṇyāśca kathaṃ gacchati sattama||29||

30vyādha uvāca|
garbhādhānasamāyuktaṃ karmedaṃ sampradṛśyate
|
samāsena tu te kṣipraṃ pravakṣyāmi dvijottama||30||

31yathā sambhṛtasaṃbhāraḥ punareva prajāyate
|
śubhakṛcchubhayonīṣu pāpakṛt pāpayoniṣu||31||

32śubhaiḥ prayogairdevatvaṃ vyāmiśrairmānuṣo bhavet
|
mohanīyairviyonīṣu tvadhogāmī ca kilviṣī||32||

33jātimṛtyujarāduḥkhaiḥ satataṃ samabhidrutaḥ
|
sañcare pacyamānaśca doṣairātmakṛtairnaraḥ||33||

34tiryagyonisahasrāṇi gatvā narakameva ca
|
jīvāḥ samparivartante karmabandhanibandhanāḥ||34||

35jantustu karmabhistaistaiḥ svakṛtaiḥ pretya duḥkhitaḥ
|
tadduḥkhapratighātārthamapuṇyāṃ yonimāpnute||35||

36tataḥ karma samādatte punaranyaṃ navaṃ bahu
|
pacyate tu punastena bhuktvā'pathyamivāturaḥ||36||

37ajastrameva duḥkhārto'duḥkhitaḥ sukhasaṃjñakaḥ
|
tato nivṛttabandhatvāt karmaṇāmudayādapi||37||

38parikrāmati saṃsāre cakravadbahuvedanaḥ
|
sa cennivṛttabandhastu viśuddhaścāpi karmabhiḥ||38||

39tapoyogasamārambhaṃ kurute dvijasattama
|
karmabhirbahubhiścāpi lokānaśnāti mānavaḥ||39||

40sa cennivṛttabandhastu viśuddhaścāpi karmabhiḥ
|
prāpnoti sukṛtā~llokān yatra gatvā na śocati||40||

41pāpaṃ kurvan pāpavṛttaḥ pāpasyāntaṃ na gacchati
|
tasmāt puṇyaṃ yatet kartuṃ varjayīta ca pāpakam||41||

42anasūyuḥ kṛtajñaśca kalyāṇāni ca sevate
|
sukhāni dharmamarthaṃ ca svargaṃ ca labhate naraḥ||42||

43saṃskṛtasya ca dāntasya niyatasya yatātmanaḥ
|
prājñasyānantarā vṛttiriha loke paratra ca||43||

44satāṃ dharmeṇa varteta kriyāṃ śiṣṭavadācaret
|
asaṃkleśena lokasya vṛttiṃ lipseta vai dvijaḥ||44||

45svadharmeṇa kriyā loke karmaṇaḥ so'pyasaṅkaraḥ
|
santi hyāgamavijñānāḥ śiṣṭāḥ śāstre vicakṣaṇāḥ||45||

46prājño dharmeṇa ramate dharmaṃ caivopajīvati
|
tasmāddharmādavāptena dhanena dvijasattama||46||

47tasyaiva siñcate mūlaṃ guṇān paśyati yatra vai
|
dharmātmā bhavati hyevaṃ cittaṃ cāsya prasīdati||47||

48sa mitrajanasantuṣṭa iha pretya ca nandati
|
śabdaṃ sparśaṃ tathā rūpaṃ gandhāniṣṭāṃśca sattama||48||

49prabhutvaṃ labhate cāpi dharmasyaitat phalaṃ viduḥ
|
dharmasya ca phalaṃ labdhvā na tuṣyati mahādvija||49||

50atṛpyamāṇo nirvedamāpede jñānacakṣuṣā
|
prajñācakṣurnara iha doṣaṃ naivānurudhyate||50||

51virajyate yathākāmaṃ na ca dharmaṃ vimuñcati
|
sarvatyāge ca yatate dṛṣṭvā lokaṃ kṣayātmakam||51||

52tato mokṣe prayatate nānupāyādupāyataḥ
|
evaṃ nirvedamādatte pāpaṃ karma jahāti ca||52||

53dhārmikaścāpi bhavati mokṣaṃ ca labhate param
|
tapo niḥśreyasaṃ jantostasya mūlaṃ śamo damaḥ||53||

54tena sarvānavāpnoti kāmān yān manasecchati
|
indriyāṇāṃ nirodhena satyena ca damena ca
|
brahmaṇaḥ padamāpnoti yatparaṃ dvijasattama||54||

55brāhmaṇa uvāca|
indriyāṇi tu yān yāhuḥ kāni tāni yatavrata
|
nigrahaśca kathaṃ kāryo nigrahasya ca kiṃ phalam||55||

56kathaṃ ca phalamāpnoti teṣāṃ dharmabhṛtāṃ vara
|
etadicchāmi tattvena dharmaṃ jñātuṃ nibodha me||56||
