Mahābhārata 12.176 (Śāntiparvan)

1yudhiṣṭhira uvāca|
dhaninaścādhanā ye ca vartayante svatantriṇaḥ
|
sukhaduḥkhāgamasteṣāṃ kaḥ kathaṃ vā pitāmaha ||1||

2bhīṣma uvāca|
atrāpyudāharantīmamitihāsaṃ purātanam
|
śampākeneha muktena gītaṃ śāntigatena ca ||2||

3abravīnmāṃ purā kaścidbrāhmaṇastyāgamāśritaḥ
|
kliśyamānaḥ kudāreṇa kucailena bubhukṣayā ||3||

4utpannamiha loke vai janmaprabhṛti mānavam
|
vividhāmanyupavartante duḥkhāni ca sukhāni ca ||4||

5tayorekatare mārge yadenamabhisannayet
|
na sukhaṃ prāpya saṃhṛṣyennāsukhaṃ prāpya saṃjvaret ||5||

6na vai carasi yacchreya ātmano vā yadīśiṣe
|
akāmātmāpi hi sadā dhuramudyamya caiva ha ||6||

7akiṃcanaḥ paripatan sukhamāsvādayiṣyasi
|
akiṃcanaḥ sukhaṃ śete samuttiṣṭhati caiva ha ||7||

8ākiṃcanyaṃ sukhaṃ loke pathyaṃ śivamanāmayam
|
anamitrapatho hyeṣa durlabhaḥ sulabho mataḥ ||8||

9akiṃcanasya śuddhasya upapannasya sarvataḥ
|
avekṣamāṇastrī~llokānna tulyamiha lakṣaye ||9||

10ākiñcanyaṃ ca rājyaṃ ca tulayā samatolayam
|
atyaricyata dāridryaṃ rājyādapi guṇādhikam ||10||

11ākiṃcanye ca rājye ca viśeṣaḥ sumahānayam
|
nityodvigno hi dhanavān mṛtyorāsyagato yathā ||11||

12naivāsyāgnirna cāriṣṭo na mṛtyurna ca dasyavaḥ
|
prabhavanti dhanatyāgādvimuktasya nirāśiṣaḥ ||12||

13taṃ vai sadā kāmacaramanupastīrṇaśāyinam
|
bāhūpadhānaṃ śāmyantaṃ praśaṃsanti divaukasaḥ ||13||

14dhanavān krodhalobhābhyāmāviṣṭo naṣṭacetanaḥ
|
tiryagīkṣaḥ śuṣkamukhaḥ pāpako bhrukuṭīmukhaḥ ||14||

15nirdaśannadharoṣṭhaṃ ca kruddho dāruṇabhāṣitā
|
kastamicchetparidraṣṭuṃ dātumicchati cenmahīm ||15||

16śriyā hyabhīkṣṇaṃ saṃvāso mohayatyavicakṣaṇam
|
sā tasya cittaṃ harati śāradābhramivānilaḥ ||16||

17athainaṃ rūpamānaśca dhanamānaśca vindati
|
abhijāto'smi siddho'smi nāsmi kevalamānuṣaḥ ||17||

18ityebhiḥ kāraṇaistasya tribhiścittaṃ pramādyati
|
samprasaktamanā bhogānvisṛjya pitṛsaṃcitān
|
parikṣīṇaḥ parasvānāmādānaṃ sādhu manyate ||18||

19tamatikrāntamaryādamādadānaṃ tatastataḥ
|
pratiṣedhanti rājāno lubdhā mṛgāmiveṣubhiḥ ||19||

20evametāni duḥkhāni tāni tānīha mānavam
|
vividhānyupapadyante gātrasaṃsparśajānyapi ||20||

21teṣāṃ paramaduḥkhānāṃ buddhyā bhaiṣajyamācaret
|
lokadharmamavajñāya dhruvāṇāmadhruvaiḥ saha ||21||

22nātyaktvā sukhamāpnoti nātyaktvā vindate param
|
nātyaktvā cābhayaḥ śete tyaktvā sarvaṃ sukhī bhava ||22||

23ityeddhāstinapure brāhmaṇenopavarṇitam
|
śampākena purā mahyaṃ tasmāttyāgaḥ paro mataḥ ||23||
