Mahābhārata 16.8 (Mausalaparvan)

1vaiśampāyana uvāca|
praviśannarjuno rājannāśramaṃ satyavādinaḥ
|
dadarśāsīnamekānte muniṃ satyavatīsutam||1||

2sa tamāsādya dharmajñamupatasthe mahāvratam
|
arjuno'smīti nāmāsmai nivedyābhyavadattataḥ||2||

3svāgataṃ te'stviti prāha muniḥ satyavatīsutaḥ
|
āsyatāmiti hovāca prasannātmā mahāmuniḥ||3||

4tamapratītamanasaṃ niḥśvasantaṃ punaḥ punaḥ
|
nirviṇṇamanasaṃ dṛṣṭvā pārthaṃ vyāso'bravīdidam||4||

5nakhakeśadaśākumbhavāriṇā kiṃ samukṣitaḥ
|
āvīrajānugamanaṃ brāhmaṇo vā hatastvayā||5||

6yuddhe parājito vā'si gataśrīriva lakṣyase
|
na tvāṃ prabhinnaṃ jānāmi kimidaṃ bharatarṣabha||6||

7śrotavyaṃ cenmayā pārtha kṣipramākhyātumarhasi
|
arjuna uvāca|
yaḥ sa meghavapuḥ śrīmān bṛhatpaṅkajalocanaḥ||7||

8sa kṛṣṇaḥ saha rāmeṇa tyaktvā dehaṃ divaṃ gataḥ
|
mausale vṛṣṇivīrāṇāṃ vināśo brahmaśāpajaḥ||8||

9babhūva vīrāntakaraḥ prabhāse lomaharṣaṇaḥ
|
ete śūrā mahātmānaḥ siṃhadarpā mahābalāḥ||9||

10bhojavṛṣṇyandhakā brahmannanyonyaṃ tairhataṃ yudhi
|
gadāparighaśaktīnāṃ sahāḥ parighabāhavaḥ||10||

11ta erakābhirnihitāḥ paśya kālasya paryayam
|
hataṃ pañcaśataṃ teṣāṃ sahasraṃ bāhuśālinām||11||

12nidhanaṃ samanuprāptaṃ samāsādyetaretaram
|
punaḥ punarna mṛṣyāmi vināśamamitaujasām||12||

13cintayāno yadūnāṃ ca kṛṣṇasya ca yaśasvinaḥ
|
śoṣaṇaṃ sāgarasyeva parvatasyeva cālanam||13||

14nabhasaḥ patanaṃ caiva śaityamagnestathaiva ca
|
aśraddheyamahaṃ manye vināśaṃ śārṅgadhanvanaḥ||14||

15na ceha sthātumicchāmi loke kṛṣṇavinākṛtaḥ
|
itaḥ kaṣṭataraṃ cānyacchṛṇu tadvai tapodhana||15||

16mano me dīryate yena cintayānasya vai muhuḥ
|
paśyato vṛṣṇidārāśca mama brahman sahasraśaḥ||16||

17ābhīrairanusṛtyājau hṛtāḥ pañcanadālayaiḥ
|
dhanurādāya tatrāhaṃ nāśakaṃ tasya pūraṇe||17||

18yathā purā ca me vīryaṃ bhujayorna tathā'bhavat
|
astrāṇi me pranaṣṭāni trividhāni mahāmune||18||

19śarāśca kṣayamāpannāḥ kṣaṇenaiva samantataḥ
|
puruṣaścāprameyātmā śaṅkhacakragadādharaḥ||19||

20caturbhujaḥ pītavāsāḥ śyāmaḥ padmadalekṣaṇaḥ
|
yaśca yāti purastānme rathasya sumahādyutiḥ||20||

21pradahan ripusainyāni na paśyāmyahamacyutam
|
yena pūrvaṃ pradagdhāni śatrusainyāni tejasā||21||

22śarairgāṇḍīvanirmuktairahaṃ paścācca nāśayam
|
tamapaśyanviṣīdāmi ghūrṇāmīva ca sattama||22||

23parinirviṇṇacetāśca śānti nopalabhe'pi ca
|
vinā janārdanaṃ vīraṃ nāhaṃ jīvitumutsahe||23||

24śrutvaiva hi gataṃ viṣṇuṃ mamāpi mumuhurdiśaḥ
|
pranaṣṭajñātivīryasya śūnyasya paridhāvataḥ||24||

25upadeṣṭuṃ mama śreyo bhavānarhati sattama
|
vyāsa uvāca|
brahmaśāpavinirdagdhā vṛṣṇyandhakamahārathāḥ||25||

26vinaṣṭāḥ kuruśārdūla na tān śocitumarhasi
|
bhavitavyaṃ tathā tacca diṣṭametanmahātmanām||26||

27upekṣitaṃ ca kṛṣṇena śaktenāpi vyapohitum
|
trailokyamapi goviṃdaḥ kṛtsnaṃ sthāvarajaṅgamam||27||

28prasahedanyathākartuṃ kutaḥ śāpaṃ mahātmanām
|
rathasya purato yāti yaḥ sacakragadādharaḥ||28||

29tava snehātpurāṇarṣirvāsudevaścaturbhujaḥ
|
kṛtvā bhārāvataraṇaṃ pṛthivyāḥ pṛthulocanaḥ||29||

30mokṣayitvā tanuṃ prāptaḥ kṛṣṇaḥ svasthānamuttamam
|
tvayā'pīha mahatkarma devānāṃ puruṣarṣabha||30||

31kṛtaṃ bhīmasahāyena yamābhyāṃ ca mahābhuja
|
kṛtakṛtyāṃśca vo manye saṃsiddhānkurupuṅgava||31||

32gamanaṃ prāptakālaṃ va idaṃ śreyaskaraṃ vibho
|
evaṃ buddhiśca tejaśca pratipattiśca bhārata||32||

33bhavanti bhavakāleṣu vipadyante viparyaye
|
kālamūlamidaṃ sarvaṃ jagadbījaṃ dhanañjaya||33||

34kāla eva samādatte punareva yadṛcchayā
|
sa eva balavānbhūtvā punarbhavati durbalaḥ||34||

35sa eveśaśca bhūtveha parairājñāpyate punaḥ
|
kṛtakṛtyāni cāstrāṇi gatānyadya yathāgatam||35||

36punareṣyanti te haste yadā kālo bhaviṣyati
|
kālo gantuṃ gatiṃ mukhyāṃ bhavatāmapi bhārata||36||

37etacchreyo hi vo manye paramaṃ bharatarṣabha
|
vaiśampāyana uvāca|
etadvacanamājñāya vyāsasyāmitatejasaḥ||37||

38anujñāto yayau pārtho nagaraṃ nāgasāhvayam
|
praviśya ca purī vīrā samāsādya yudhiṣṭhiram
|
ācaṣṭa tadyathāvṛttaṃ vṛṣṇyandhakakulaṃ prati||38||
