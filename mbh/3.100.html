Mahābhārata 3.100 (Āraṇyakaparvan)

1yudhiṣṭhira uvāca| bhūya evāhamicchāmi maharṣestasya dhīmataḥ
|
karmaṇāṃ vistaraṃ śrotumagastyasya dvijottama||1||

2lomaśa uvāca| śṛṇu rājankathāṃ divyāmadbhutāmatimānuṣīm
|
agastyasya mahārāja prabhāvamamitaujasaḥ||2||

3āsankṛtayuge ghorā dānavā yuddhadurmadāḥ
|
kālakeyā iti khyātā gaṇāḥ paramadāruṇāḥ||3||

4te tu vṛtraṃ samāśritya nānāpraharaṇodyatāḥ
|
samantātparyadhāvanta mahendrapramukhānsurān||4||

5tato vṛtravadhe yatnamakurvaṃstridaśāḥ purā
|
puraṃdaraṃ puraskṛtya brahmāṇamupatasthire||5||

6kṛtāñjalīṃstu tānsarvānparameṣṭhītyuvāca ha
|
viditaṃ me surāḥ sarvaṃ yadvaḥ kāryaṃ cikīrṣitam||6||

7tamupāyaṃ pravakṣyāmi yathā vṛtraṃ vadhiṣyatha
|
dadhīca iti vikhyāto mahānṛṣirudāradhīḥ||7||

8taṃ gatvā sahitāḥ sarve varaṃ vai saṃprayācata
|
sa vo dāsyati dharmātmā suprītenāntarātmanā||8||

9sa vācyaḥ sahitaiḥ sarvairbhavadbhirjayakāṅkṣibhiḥ
|
svānyasthīni prayaccheti trailokyasya hitāya vai||9||

10sa śarīraṃ samutsṛjya svānyasthīni pradāsyati
|
tasyāsthibhirmahāghoraṃ vajraṃ saṃskriyatāṃ dṛḍham||10||

11mahacchatruhaṇaṃ ghoraṃ ṣaḍasri bhīmaniḥsvanam
|
tena vajreṇa vai vṛtraṃ vadhiṣyati śatakratuḥ||11||

12etadvaḥ sarvamākhyātaṃ tasmācchīghraṃ vidhīyatām
|
evamuktāstato devā anujñāpya pitāmaham||12||

13nārāyaṇaṃ puraskṛtya dadhīcasyāśramaṃ yayuḥ
|
sarasvatyāḥ pare pāre nānādrumalatāvṛtam||13||

14ṣaṭpadodgītaninadairvighuṣṭaṃ sāmagairiva
|
puṃskokilaravonmiśraṃ jīvaṃ jīvakanāditam||14||

15mahiṣaiśca varāhaiśca sṛmaraiścamarairapi
|
tatra tatrānucaritaṃ śārdūlabhayavarjitaiḥ||15||

16kareṇubhirvāraṇaiśca prabhinnakaraṭāmukhaiḥ
|
sarovagāḍhaiḥ krīḍadbhiḥ samantādanunāditam||16||

17siṃhavyāghrairmahānādānnadadbhiranunāditam
|
aparaiścāpi saṃlīnairguhākandaraśāyibhiḥ||17||

18teṣu teṣvavakāśeṣu śobhitaṃ sumanoramam
|
triviṣṭapasamaprakhyaṃ dadhīcāśramamāgaman||18||

19tatrāpaśyandadhīcaṃ te divākarasamadyutim
|
jājvalyamānaṃ vapuṣā yathā lakṣmyā pitāmaham||19||

20tasya pādau surā rājannabhivādya praṇamya ca
|
ayācanta varaṃ sarve yathoktaṃ parameṣṭhinā||20||

21tato dadhīcaḥ paramapratītaḥ surottamāṃstānidamabhyuvāca
|
karomi yadvo hitamadya devāḥ svaṃ cāpi dehaṃ svayamutsṛjāmi||21||

22sa evamuktvā dvipadāṃ variṣṭhaḥ prāṇānvaśī svānsahasotsasarja
|
tataḥ surāste jagṛhuḥ parāso- rasthīni tasyātha yathopadeśam||22||

23prahṛṣṭarūpāśca jayāya devāstvaṣṭāramāgamya tamarthamūcuḥ
|
tvaṣṭā tu teṣāṃ vacanaṃ niśamya prahṛṣṭarūpaḥ prayataḥ prayatnāt||23||

24cakāra vajraṃ bhṛśamugrarūpaṃ kṛtvā ca śakraṃ sa uvāca hṛṣṭaḥ
|
anena vajrapravareṇa deva bhasmīkuruṣvādya surārimugram||24||

25tato hatāriḥ sagaṇaḥ sukhaṃ vai praśādhi kṛtsnaṃ tridivaṃ diviṣṭhaḥ
|
tvaṣṭrā tathoktastu puraṃdarastadvajraṃ prahṛṣṭaḥ prayato hyagṛhṇāt||25||
