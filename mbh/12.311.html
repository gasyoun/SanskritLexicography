Mahābhārata 12.311 (Śāntiparvan)

1yājñavalkya uvāca |
avyaktasya naraśreṣṭha kālasaṃkhyāṃ nibodha me
|
pañcakalpasahasrāṇi dviguṇānyaharucyate||1||

2rātriretāvatī cāsya pratibuddho narādhipa
|
sṛjatyoṣadhimevāgre jīvanaṃ sarvadehinām||2||

3tato brahmāṇamasṛjaddhiraṇyāṇḍasamudbhavam
|
sā mūrtiḥ sarvabhūtānāmityevamanuśuśruma||3||

4saṃvatsaramuṣitvāṇḍe niṣkramya ca mahāmuniḥ
|
saṃdadhe sa mahīṃ kṛtsnāṃ divamūrdhvaṃ prajāpatiḥ||4||

5dyāvāpṛthivyorityeṣa rājanvedeṣu paṭhyate
|
tayoḥ śakalayormadhyamākāśamakarotprabhuḥ||5||

6etasyāpi ca saṃkhyānaṃ vedavedāṅgapāragaiḥ
|
daśakalpasahasrāṇi pādonānyaharucyate||6||

7rātrimetāvatīṃ cāsya prāhuradhyātmacintakāḥ
|
sṛjatyahaṅkāramṛrṣirbhūtaṃ divyātmakaṃ tathā||7||

8caturaścāparān putrāndehātpūrvaṃ mahānṛṣiḥ
|
te vai pitṝṇāṃ pitaraḥ śrūyante rājasattama||8||

9devāḥ pitṝṇāṃ ca sutā devairlokāḥ samāvṛtāḥ
|
carācarā naraśreṣṭha ityevamanuśuśruma||9||

10parameṣṭhī tvahaṅkāraḥ sṛjan bhūtāni pañcadhā
|
pṛthivī vāyurākāśamāpo jyotiśca pañcamam||10||

11etasyāpi niśāmāhustṛtīyamiha kurvataḥ
|
pañcakalpasahasrāṇi tāvadevāharucyate||11||

12śabdaḥ sparśaśca rūpaṃ ca raso gandhastathaiva ca
|
ete viśeṣā rājendra mahābhūteṣu pañcasu||12||

13yairāviṣṭāni bhūtāni ahanyahani pārthiva
|
anyonyaṃ spṛhayantyete anyonyasya hite ratāḥ||13||

14anyonyamativartante anyonyaspardhinastathā
|
te vadhyamānā hyanyonyaṃ guṇairhāribhiravyayaiḥ||14||

15ihaiva parivartante tiryagyonipraveśinaḥ
|
trīṇi kalpasahasrāṇi eteṣāmaharucyate||15||

16rātriretāvatī caiva manasaśca narādhipa
|
manaścarati rājendra vāritaṃ sarvamindriyaiḥ||16||

17na cendriyāṇi paśyanti mana evānupaśyati
|
cakṣuḥ paśyati rūpāṇi manasā tu na cakṣuṣā||17||

18manasi vyākule cakṣuḥ paśyannapi na paśyati
|
tathendriyāṇi sarvāṇi paśyantītyabhicakṣate||18||

19na cendriyāṇi paśyanti mana evātra paśyati
|
manasyuparate rājannindriyoparamo bhavet||19||

20tadindriyeṣūparame manasyuparamo bhavet
|
evaṃ manaḥpradhānāni indriyāṇi prabhāvayet||20||

21indriyāṇāṃ tu sarveṣāmīśvaraṃ mana ucyate
|
etadviśanti bhūtāni sarvāṇīha mahāyaśaḥ||21||
