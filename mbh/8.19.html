Mahābhārata 8.19 (Karṇaparvan)

1sañjaya uvāca|
pratyāgatya punarjiṣṇurjaghne saṃśaptakānbahūn
|
cakrātivakragamanādaṅgāraka iva grahaḥ ||1||

2pārthabāṇahatā rājannarāśvarathakuñjarāḥ
|
vicelurbabhramurneśuḥ peturmamluśca bhārata ||2||

3dhuryān dhuryagatānsūtān dhvajāṃścāpāni sāyakān
|
pāṇīnpāṇigataṃ śastraṃ bāhūnapi śirāṃsi ca ||3||

4bhallaiḥ kṣurairardhacandrairvatsadantaiśca pāṇḍavaḥ
|
cicchedāmitravīrāṇāṃ samare pratiyudhyatām ||4||

5vāsitārthe yuyutsanto vṛṣabhā vṛṣabhaṃ yathā
|
nipatantyarjunaṃ śūrāḥ śataśo'tha sahasraśaḥ ||5||

6teṣāṃ tasya ca tadyuddhamabhavallomaharṣaṇam
|
trailokyavijaye yādṛgdaityānāṃ saha vajriṇā ||6||

7tamavidhyastribhirbāṇairdandaśūkairivāhibhiḥ
|
ugrāyudhasutastasya śiraḥ kāyāda‌pāharat ||7||

8te'rjunaṃ sarvataḥ kruddhā nānāśastrairavīvṛṣan
|
marudbhiḥ preritā meghā himavantamivoṣṇage ||8||

9astrairasrāṇi saṃvārya dviṣatāṃ sarvato'rjunaḥ
|
samyagastaiḥ śaraiḥ sarvānahitānahanadbahūn ||9||

10chinnatriveṇusaṅghātān hatāśvān pārṣṇisārathīn
|
visrastahastatūṇīrān vicakrarathaketanān ||10||

11saṃchinnaraśmiyoktrākṣān vyanukarṣayugānrathān
|
viddhvastasarvasannāhān bāṇaiścakre'rjunastadā ||11||

12te rathāstatra viddhvastāḥ parārdhyā bhāntyanekaśaḥ
|
dhanināmiva veśmāni hatānyagnyanilāmbubhiḥ ||12||

13dvipāḥ saṃbhinnavarmāṇo vajrāśanisamaiḥ śaraiḥ
|
peturgiryagraveśmāni vajrapātāgnibhiryathā ||13||

14sārohāsturagāḥ peturbahavo'rjunatāḍitāḥ
|
nirjihvāntrāḥ kṣitau kṣīṇā rudhirārdrāḥ sudurdṛśaḥ ||14||

15narāśvanāgā nārācaiḥ saṃsyūtāḥ savyasācinā
|
babhramuścaskhaluḥ peturnedurmamluśca māriṣa ||15||

16anekaiśca śilādhautairvajrāśaniviṣopamaiḥ
|
śarairnijaghnivānpārtho mahendra iva dānavān ||16||

17mahārhavarmābharaṇā nānārūpāmbarāyudhāḥ
|
sarathāḥ sadhvajā vīrā hatāḥ pārthena śerate ||17||

18vijitāḥ puṇyakarmāṇo viśiṣṭābhijanaśrutāḥ
|
gatāḥ śarīrairvasudhāmūrjitaiḥ karmabhirdivam ||18||

19athārjunaṃ rathavaraṃ tvadīyāḥ samabhidravan
|
nānājanapadādhyakṣāḥ sagaṇā jātamanyavaḥ ||19||

20uhyamānā rathāśvebhaiḥ pattayaśca jighāṃsavaḥ
|
samabhyadhāvannasyanto vividhaṃ kṣipramāyudham ||20||

21tadāyudhamahāvarṣaṃ muktaṃ yodhamahāmbudaiḥ
|
vyadhamanniśitairbāṇaiḥ kṣipramarjunamārutaḥ ||21||

22sāśvapattidviparathaṃ mahāśastraughasaṃplavam
|
sahasā santitīrṣantaṃ pārthaṃ śastrāstrasetunā ||22||

23athābravīdvāsudevaḥ pārtha kiṃ krīḍase'nagha
|
saṃśaptakān pramathyaināṃstataḥ karṇavadhe tvara ||23||

24tathetyuktvā'rjunaḥ kṛṣṇaṃ śiṣṭānsaṃśaptakāṃstadā
|
ākṣipya śastreṇa balāddaityānindra iyāvadhīt ||24||

25ādadansaṃdadhanneṣūn dṛṣṭaḥ kaiścidraṇe'rjunaḥ
|
vimuñcanvā śarāñśīghraṃ dṛśyate'vahitairapi ||25||

26āścaryamiti govindaḥ samamanyata bhārata
|
haṃsāṃśugaurāste senāṃ haṃsāḥ sara ivāviśan ||26||

27tataḥ saṃgrāmabhūmiṃ ca vartamāne janakṣaye
|
avekṣamāṇo goviṃdaḥ savyasācinamabravīt ||27||

28eṣa pārtha mahāraudro vartate bharatakṣayaḥ
|
pṛthivyāṃ pārthivānāṃ vai duryodhanakṛte mahān ||28||

29paśya bhārata cāpāni rukmapṛṣṭhāni dhanvinām
|
mahatāṃ cāpaviddhāni kalāpāniṣudhīṃstathā ||29||

30jātarūpamayaiḥ puṅkhaiḥ śarāṃśca nataparvaṇaḥ
|
tailadhautāṃśca nārācān vimuktāniva pannagān ||30||

31ākīrṇāṃstomarāṃścāpi vicitrān hemabhūṣitān
|
carmāṇi cāpaviddhāni rukmapṛṣṭhāni bhārata ||31||

32suvarṇavikṛtānprāsān śaktīḥ kanakabhūṣitāḥ
|
jāmbūnada‌mayaiḥ paṭṭairbaddhāśca vipulā gadāḥ ||32||

33jātarūpamayīścarṣṭīḥ paṭṭiśān hemabhūṣitān
|
daṇḍaiḥ kanakacitraiśca vipraviddhānparaśvadhān ||33||

34parighān bhindipālāṃśca bhuśuṇḍīḥ kuṇapānapi
|
ayaskuntāṃśca patitān musalāni gurūṇi ca ||34||

35nānāvidhāni śastrāṇi pragṛhya jayagṛddhinaḥ
|
jīvanta iva dṛśyante gatasattvāstarasvinaḥ ||35||

36gadāvimathitairgātrairmusalairbhinnamastakān
|
gajavājirathaiḥ kṣuṇṇānpaśya yodhānsahasraśaḥ ||36||

37manuṣya-gaja-vājīnāṃ śaraśaktyṛṣṭitomaraiḥ
|
nistriṃśaiḥ paṭṭiśaiḥ prāsairnakharairlaguḍairapi ||37||

38śarīrairbahudhā chinnaiḥ śoṇitaughapariplutaiḥ
|
gatāsubhiramitraghnasaṃvṛtā raṇabhūmayaḥ ||38||

39bāhubhiścandanādigdhaiḥ sāṅgadaiḥ śubhabhūṣaṇaiḥ
|
satalatraiḥ sakeyūrairbhāti bhārata medinī ||39||

40sāṅgalitrairbhujāgraiśca vipraviddhairalaṅkṛtaiḥ
|
hastihastopamaiśchinnairūrubhiśca tarasvinām ||40||

41baddhacūḍāmaṇivaraiḥ śirobhiśca sakuṇḍalaiḥ
|
rathāṃśca bahudhā bhagnān hemakiṅkiṇinaḥ śubhān ||41||

42aśvāṃśca bahudhā paśya śoṇitena pariplutān
|
anukarṣānupāsaṅgān patākā vividhān dhvajān ||42||

43yodhānāṃ ca mahāśaṅkhān pāṇḍurāṃśca prakīrṇakān
|
nirastajihvānmātaṅgān śayānānparvatopamān ||43||

44vaijayantīrvicitrāśca hatāṃśca gajayodhinaḥ
|
vāraṇānāṃ paristomān saṃyuktānekakambalān ||44||

45vipāṭitavicitrāśca rūpacitrāḥ kuthāstathā
|
bhinnāśca bahudhā ghaṇṭāḥ patadbhiścūrṇitā gajaiḥ ||45||

46vaidūryamaṇidaṇḍāṃśca patitāṃścāṅkuśān bhuvi
|
aśvānāṃ ca yugāpīḍān ratnacitrānuraśchadān ||46||

47viddhāḥ sādidhvajāgreṣu suvarṇavikṛtāḥ kuthāḥ
|
vicitrānmaṇicitrāṃśca jātarūpapariṣkṛtān ||47||

48aśvāstarapārastomān rāṅkavānpatitānbhuvi
|
cūḍāmaṇīnnaredrāṇāṃ vicitrāḥ kāñcanasrajaḥ ||48||

49chatrāṇi cāpaviddhāni cāmaravyajanāni ca
|
candranakṣatrabhāsaiśca vadanaiścārukuṇḍalaiḥ ||49||

50klṛptaśmaśrubhirākīrṇāṃ pūrṇacandranibhairmahīm
|
kumudotpalapadmānāṃ khaṇḍaiḥ phullaṃ yathā saraḥ ||50||

51tathā mahībhṛtāṃ vaktraiḥ kumudotpalasannibhaiḥ
|
tārāgaṇavicitrasya nirmalendudyutitviṣaḥ ||51||

52paśyemāṃ nabhasastulyāṃ śarannakṣatramālinīm
|
etattavaivānurūpaṃ karmārjuna mahāhave ||52||

53divi vā devarājasya tvayā yatkṛtamāhave
|
evaṃ tāṃ darśayan kṛṣṇo yuddhabhūmiṃ kirīṭine ||53||

54gacchannevāśrṛṇocchabdaṃ duryodhanabale mahat
|
śaṅkhadundubhinirghoṣaṃ bherīpaṇavaniḥsvanam ||54||

55rathāśvagajanādāṃśca śastraśabdāṃśca dāruṇān
|
praviśya tadbalaṃ kṛṣṇasturagairvātavegitaiḥ ||55||

56pāṇḍyenābhyarditaṃ sainyaṃ tvadīyaṃ vīkṣya vismitaḥ
|
sa hi nānāvidhairbāṇairiṣvastrapravaro yudhi ||56||

57nyahanaddviṣatāṃ pūgān gatāsūnantako yathā
|
gajavājimanuṣyāṇāṃ śarīrāṇi śitaiḥ śaraiḥ ||57||

58bhittvā praharatāṃ śreṣṭho videhāsūnapātayat
|
śatrupravīrairastrāṇi nānāśastrāṇi sāyakaiḥ
|
chittvā tānavadhīcchatrūn pāṇḍyaḥ śakra ivāsurān ||58||
