Mahābhārata 3.248 (Āraṇyakaparvan)

1duryodhana uvāca|
ajānataste rādheya nābhyasūyāmyahaṃ vacaḥ
|
jānāsi tvaṃ jitān śatrūn gandharvāṃstejasā mayā||1||

2āyodhitāstu gandharvāḥ suciraṃ sodarairmama
|
mayā saha mahābāho kṛtaścobhayataḥ kṣayaḥ||2||

3māyādhikāstvayudhyanta yadā śūrā viyadgatāḥ
|
tadā no na samaṃ yuddhamabhavat khecaraiḥ saha||3||

4parājayaṃ ca prāptāḥ smo raṇe bandhanameva ca
|
sabhṛtyāmātyaputrāśca sadārabalavāhanāḥ||4||

5uccairākāśamārgeṇa hriyāmastaiḥ suduḥkhitāḥ
|
atha naḥ sainikāḥ kecidamātyāśca mahārathāḥ||5||

6upagamyābruvan dīnāḥ pāṇḍavāṃśca raṇapradān
|
eṣa duryodhano rājā dhārtarāṣṭraḥ sahānujaḥ||6||

7sāmātyadāro hriyate gandharvaidivamāśritaiḥ
|
taṃ mokṣayata bhadraṃ vaḥ sahadāraṃ narādhipam||7||

8parāmarśo mā bhaviṣyat kurudāreṣu sarvaśaḥ
|
evamukte tu dharmātmā jyeṣṭhaḥ pāṇḍusutastadā||8||

9prasādya pāṇḍavān sarvānājñāpayata mokṣaṇe
|
athāgamya tamuddeśaṃ pāṇḍavāḥ puruṣarṣabhāḥ||9||

10sāntvapūrvamayācanta śaktāḥ santo mahārathāḥ
|
yadā cāsmān na mumucurgandharvāḥ sāntvitā api||10||

11tato'rjunaśca bhīmaśca yamajau ca balotkaṭau
|
mumucuḥ śaravarṣāṇi gandharvān pratyanekaśaḥ||11||

12atha sarve raṇaṃ muktvā prayātāḥ khecarā divam
|
asmānevābhikarṣanto dīnān muditamānasāḥ||12||

13tataḥ samantāt paśyāmaḥ śarajālena veṣṭitam
|
amānuṣāṇi cāstrāṇi pramuñcantaṃ dhanañjayam||13||

14samāvṛtā diśo dṛṣṭvā pāṇḍavena śitaiḥ śaraiḥ
|
dhanañjayasakhā''tmānaṃ darśayāmāsa vai tadā||14||

15citrasenaḥ pāṇḍavena samāśliṣya parasparam
|
kuśalaṃ paripapraccha taiḥ pṛṣṭaścāpyanāmayam||15||

16te sametya tathā'nyonyaṃ sannāhān vipramucya ca
|
ekībhūtāstato vīrā gandharvāḥ saha pāṇḍavaiḥ
|
apūjayetāmanyonyaṃ citrasenadhanañjayau||16||
