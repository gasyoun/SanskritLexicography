Mahābhārata 12.153 (Śāntiparvan)

1yudhiṣṭhira uvāca|
kaccitpitāmahenāsīcchrutaṃ vā dṛṣṭameva ca
|
kaccinmartyo mṛto rājan punarujjīvito'bhavat ||1||

2bhīṣma uvāca|
śṛṇu pārtha yathāvṛttamitihāsaṃ purātanam
|
gṛdhrajambukasaṃvādaṃ yo vṛtto naimiṣe purā ||2||

3kasyacidbrāhmaṇasyāsīdduḥkhalabdhaḥ suto mṛtaḥ
|
bāla eva viśālākṣo bālagrahanipīḍitaḥ ||3||

4duḥkhitāḥ kecidādāya bālamaprāptayauvanam
|
kulasarvasvabhūtaṃ vai rudantaḥ śokavihvalāḥ ||4||

5bālaṃ mṛtaṃ gṛhītvā'tha śmaśānābhimukhāḥ sthitāḥ
|
aṅkenaiva ca saṃkramya rurudurbhṛśaduḥkhitāḥ ||5||

6śocantastasya pūrvoktān bhāṣitāṃścāsakṛtpunaḥ
|
taṃ bālaṃ bhūtale kṣipya pratigantuṃ na śaknuyuḥ ||6||

7teṣāṃ ruditaśabdena gṛdhro'bhyetya vaco'bravīt
|
ekātmajamimaṃ loke tyaktvā gacchata mā ciram ||7||

8iha puṃsāṃ sahasrāṇi strīsahasrāṇi caiva ha
|
samānītāni kālena hitvā vai yānti bāndhavāḥ ||8||

9sampaśyata jagatsarvaṃ sukhaduḥkhairadhiṣṭhitam
|
saṃyogo viprayogaśca paryāyeṇopalabhyate ||9||

10gṛhītvā ye ca gacchanti ye na yānti ca tān mṛtān
|
te'pyāyuṣaḥ pramāṇena svena gacchanti jantavaḥ ||10||

11alaṃ sthitvā śmaśāne'smin gṛdhragomāyusaṃkule
|
kaṅkālabahule raudre sarvaprāṇibhayaṅkare ||11||

12na punarjīvitaḥ kaścitkāladharmamupāgataḥ
|
priyo vā yadi vā dveṣyaḥ prāṇināṃ gatirīdṛśī ||12||

13sarveṇa khalu martavyaṃ martyaloke prasūyatā
|
kṛtāntavihite mārge mṛtaṃ ko jīvayiṣyati ||13||

14karmāntavirate loke astaṃ gacchati bhāskare
|
gamyatāṃ svamadhiṣṭhānaṃ sutasnehaṃ visṛjya vai ||14||

15tato gṛdhravacaḥ śrutvā prākrośantastadā nṛpa
|
bāndhavāste'bhyagacchanta putramutsṛjya bhūtale ||15||

16viniścityātha ca tadā vikrośantastatastataḥ
|
mṛtamityeva gacchanto nirāśāstasya darśane ||16||

17niścitārthāśca te sarve saṃtyajantaḥ svamātmajam
|
nirāśā jīvite tasya mārgamāvṛtya dhiṣṭhitāḥ ||17||

18dhvāṃkṣapakṣasavarṇastu vilānnisṛtya jambukaḥ
|
gacchamānān sma tānāha nighṛṇāḥ khalu mānuṣāḥ ||18||

19ādityo'yaṃ sthito mūḍhāḥ snehaṃ kuruta mā bhayam
|
bahurūpo muhūrtaśca jīvedapi kadācana ||19||

20yūyaṃ bhūmau vinikṣipya putrasnehavinākṛtāḥ
|
śmaśāne sutamutsṛjya kasmādgacchata nirghṛṇāḥ ||20||

21na vo'styasminsute sneho bāle madhurabhāṣiṇi
|
yasya bhāṣitamātreṇa prasādamadhigacchata ||21||

22te paśyata sutasneho yādṛśaḥ paśupakṣiṇām
|
na teṣāṃ dhārayitvā tān kaścidasti phalāgamaḥ ||22||

23catuṣpātpakṣikīṭānāṃ prāṇināṃ snehasaṅginām
|
paralokagatisthānāṃ muniyajñakriyāmiva ||23||

24teṣāṃ putrābhirāmāṇāmihaloke paratra ca
|
na guṇo dṛśyate kaścitprajāḥ saṃdhārayanti ca ||24||

25apaśyatāṃ priyānputrān teṣāṃ śoko na tiṣṭhati
|
na ca puṣṇanti saṃvṛddhāste mātāpitarau kvacit ||25||

26mānuṣāṇāṃ kutaḥ sneho yeṣāṃ śoko bhaviṣyati
|
imaṃ kulakaraṃ putraṃ tyaktvā kva na gamiṣyatha ||26||

27ciraṃ mañcata bāṣpaṃ ca ciraṃ snehena paśyata
|
evaṃvidhāni hīṣṭāni dustyajāni viśeṣataḥ ||27||

28kṣīṇasyābhiyuktasya śmaśānābhimukhasya ca
|
bāndhavā yatra tiṣṭhanti tatrānyo nādhitiṣṭhati ||28||

29sarvasya dayitāḥ prāṇāḥ sarvaḥ snehaṃ ca vindati
|
tiryagyoniṣvapi satāṃ snehaṃ paśyata yādṛśam ||29||

30tyaktvā kathaṃ gacchathemaṃ padmalolāyatākṣikam
|
yathā navodvāhakṛtaṃ snānamālyavibhūṣitam ||30||

31jambukasya vacaḥ śrutvā kṛpaṇaṃ paridevataḥ
|
nyavartanta tadā sarve śavārthaṃ te sma mānuṣāḥ ||31||

32gṛdhra uvāca |
aho vata nṛśaṃsena jambukenālpamedhasā
|
kṣudreṇoktā hīnasattvā mānuṣāḥ kiṃ nivartatha ||32||

33pañcendriyaparityaktaṃ śuṣkaṃ kāṣṭhatvamāgatam
|
kasmācchovatha tiṣṭhantamātmānaṃ kiṃ na śocatha ||33||

34tapaḥ kuruta vai tīvraṃ mucyadhvaṃ yena kilbiṣāt
|
tapasā labhyate sarvaṃ vilāpaḥ kiṃ kariṣyati ||34||

35aniṣṭāni ca bhāgyāni jātāni saha mūrtinā
|
yena gacchati bālo'yaṃ dattvā śokamanantakam ||35||

36dhanaṃ, gāvaḥ, suvarṇaṃ ca maṇiratnamathāpi ca
|
apatyaṃ ca tapomūlaṃ tapoyogācca labhyate ||36||

37yathākṛtā ca bhūteṣu prāpyate sukhaduḥkhitā
|
gṛhītvā jāyate janturduḥkhāni ca sukhāni ca ||37||

38na karmaṇā pituḥ putraḥ pitā vā putrakarmaṇā
|
mārgeṇānyena gacchanti baddhāḥ sukṛtaduṣkṛtaiḥ ||38||

39dharmaṃ carata yatnena na cādharme manaḥ kṛthāḥ
|
vartadhvaṃ ca yathākālaṃ daivateṣu dvijeṣu ca ||39||

40śokaṃ tyajata dainyaṃ ca sutasnehānnivartata
|
tyajyatāmayamākāśe tataḥ śīghraṃ nivartata ||40||

41yatkaroti śubhaṃ karma tathā karma sudāruṇam
|
tatkartaiva samaśnāti bāndhavānāṃ kimatra ha ||41||

42iha tyaktvā na tiṣṭhanti bāndhavā bāndhavaṃ priyam
|
snehamutsṛjya gacchanti bāṣpapūrṇāvilekṣaṇāḥ ||42||

43prājño vā yadi vā mūrkhaḥ sadhano nirdhano'pi vā
|
sarvaḥ kālavaśaṃ yāti śubhāśubhasamanvitaḥ ||43||

44kiṃ kariṣyatha śocitvā mṛtaṃ kimanuśocatha
|
sarvasya hi prabhuḥ kālo dharmataḥ samadarśanaḥ ||44||

45yauvanasthāṃśca bālāṃśca vṛddhān garbhagatānapi
|
sarvānāviśate mṛtyurevaṃbhūtamidaṃ jagat ||45||

46jambuka uvāca |
aho mandīkṛtaḥ sneho gṛdhreṇehālpabuddhinā
|
putrasnehābhibhūtānāṃ yuṣmākaṃ śocatāṃ bhṛśam ||46||

47samaiḥ samyakprayuktaiśca vacanaiḥ pratyayottaraiḥ
|
yadgacchati janaścāyaṃ snehamutsṛjya dustyajam ||47||

48aho putraviyogena mṛtaśūnyopasevanāt
|
krośatāṃ subhṛśaṃ duḥkhaṃ vivatsānāṃ gavāmiva ||48||

49adya śokaṃ vijānāmi mānuṣāṇāṃ mahītale
|
snehaṃ hi kāraṇaṃ kṛtvā mamāpyaśrūṇyathāpatan ||49||

50yatno hi satataṃ kāryastato daivena siddhyati
|
daivaṃ puruṣakāraśca kṛtāntenopapadyate ||50||

51anirvedaḥ sadā kāryo nirvedāddhi kutaḥ sukham
|
prayatnātprāpyate hyarthaḥ kasmādgacchatha nirdayam ||51||

52ātmamāṃsopavṛttaṃ ca śarīrārdhamayīṃ tanum
|
pitṝṇāṃ vaṃśakartāraṃ vane tyaktvā kva yāsyatha ||52||

53athavā'staṃgate sūrye saṃdhyākāla upasthite
|
tato neṣyatha vā putramihasthā vā bhaviṣyatha ||53||

54gṛdhra uvāca |
adya varṣasahasraṃ me sāgraṃ jātasya mānuṣāḥ
|
na ca paśyami jīvantaṃ mṛtaṃ strīpuṃnapuṃsakam ||54||

55mṛtā garbheṣu jāyante jātamātrā mriyanti ca
|
caṅkramanto mriyante ca yauvanasthāstathā pare ||55||

56anityānīha bhāgyāni catuṣpātpakṣiṇāmapi
|
jaṅgamānāṃ nagānāṃ vāpyāyuragre'vatiṣṭhate ||56||

57iṣṭadāraviyuktāśca putraśokānvitāstathā
|
dahyamānāḥ sma śokena gṛhaṃ gacchanti nityaśaḥ ||57||

58aniṣṭānāṃ sahasrāṇi tatheṣṭānāṃ śatāni ca
|
utsṛjyeha prayātā vai bāndhavā bhṛśaduḥkhitāḥ ||58||

59tyajyatāmeṣa nistejāḥ śūnyaḥ kāṣṭhatvamāgataḥ
|
anyadehaviṣaktaṃ hi śāvaṃ kāṣṭhatvamāgatam ||59||

60tyaktajīvasya caivāsya kasmāddhitvā na gacchata
|
nirarthako hyayaṃ sneho niṣphalaśca pariśramaḥ ||60||

61cakṣurbhyāṃ na ca karṇābhyāṃ saṃśṛṇoti samīkṣate
|
kasmādenaṃ samutsṛjya na gṛhāngacchatāśu vai ||61||

62mokṣadharmāśritairvākyairhetumadbhiḥ suniṣṭhuraiḥ
|
mayoktā gacchata kṣipraṃ svaṃ svameva niveśanam ||62||

63prajñāvijñānayuktena buddhisaṃjñāpradāyinā
|
vacanaṃ śrāvitā nūnaṃ mānuṣāḥ saṃnyavartata
|
śoko dviguṇatāṃ yāti dṛṣṭvā smṛtvā ca ceṣṭitam ||63||

64ityetadvacanaṃ śrutvā sannivṛttāstu mānuṣāḥ
|
apaśyattaṃ tadā suptaṃ drutamāgatya jambukaḥ ||64||

65jambuka uvāca |
imaṃ kanakavarṇābhaṃ bhūṣaṇaiḥ samalaṃkṛtam
|
gṛdhravākyāt kathaṃ putraṃ tyajadhvaṃ pitṛpiṇḍadam ||65||

66na snehasya ca vicchedo vilāparuditasya ca
|
mṛtasyāsya parityāgāt tāpo vai bhavitā dhruvam ||66||

67śrūyate śambuke śūdre hate brāhmaṇadārakaḥ
|
jīvito dharmamāsādya rāmātsatyaparākramāt ||67||

68tathā śvetasya rājarṣerbālo dṛṣṭāntamāgataḥ
|
śvetena dharmaniṣṭhena mṛtaḥ saṃjīvitaḥ punaḥ ||68||

69tathā kaścillabhetsiddho munirvā devatāpi vā
|
kṛpaṇānāmanukrośaṃ kuryādvo rudatāmiha ||69||

70ityuktāste nyavartanta śokārtāḥ putravatsalāḥ
|
aṅke śiraḥ samādhāya rurudurbahuvistaram
|
teṣāṃ ruditaśabdena gṛdhro'bhyetya vaco'bravīt ||70||

71gṛdhra uvāca |
aśrupātapariklinnaḥ pāṇisparśaprapīḍitaḥ
|
dharmarājaprayogācca dīrghanidrāṃ praveśitaḥ ||71||

72tapasāpi hi saṃyuktā dhanavanto mahādhiyaḥ
|
sarve mṛtyuvaśaṃ yānti tadidaṃ pretapattanam ||72||

73bālavṛddhasahasrāṇi sadā santyajya bāndhavāḥ
|
dināni caiva rātrīśca duḥkhaṃ tiṣṭhanti bhūtale ||73||

74alaṃ nirbandhamāgatya śokasya paridhāraṇe
|
apratyayaṃ kuto hyasya punaradyeha jīvitam ||74||

75mṛtasyotsṛṣṭadehasya punardeho na vidyate
|
naiva mūrtipradānena jambukasya śatairapi ||75||

76śakyaṃ jīvayituṃ hyeṣa bālo varṣaśatairapi
|
atha rudraḥ kumāro vā brahmā vā viṣṇureva ca ||76||

77varamasmai prayaccheyustato jīvedayaṃ śiśuḥ
|
naiva bāṣpavimokṣeṇa na vā śvāsakṛte na ca ||77||

78na dīrgharuditenāyaṃ punarjīvaṃ gamiṣyati
|
ahaṃ ca kroṣṭukaścaiva yūyaṃ ye cāsya bāndhavāḥ ||78||

79dharmādharmau gṛhītveha sarve vartāmahe'dhvani
|
apriyaṃ paruṣaṃ cāpi paradrohaṃ parastriyam ||79||

80adharmamanṛtaṃ caiva dūrātprājño vivarjayet
|
dharmaṃ satyaṃ śrutaṃ nyāyyaṃ mahatīṃ prāṇināṃ dayām ||80||

81ajihmatvamaśāṭhyaṃ ca yatnataḥ parimārgata
|
mātaraṃ pitaraṃ vāpi bāndhavān suhṛdastathā ||81||

82jīvato ye na paśyanti teṣāṃ dharmaviparyayaḥ
|
yo na paśyati cakṣurbhyāṃ neṅgate ca kathañcana ||82||

83tasya niṣṭhāvasānānte rudantaḥ kiṃ kariṣyatha
|
ityuktāste sutaṃ tyaktvā bhūmau śokapariplutāḥ
|
dahyamānāḥ sutasnehātprayayurbāndhavā gṛham ||83||

84jambuka uvāca |
dāruṇo martyaloko'yaṃ sarvaprāṇivināśanaḥ
|
iṣṭabandhuviyogaśca tathehālpaṃ ca jīvitam ||84||

85bahvalīkamasatyaṃ cāpyativādāpriyaṃvadam
|
imaṃ prekṣya punarbhāvaṃ duḥkhaśokavivardhanam ||85||

86na me mānuṣaloko'yaṃ muhūrtamapi rocate
|
aho dhiggṛdhravākyena yathaivābuddhayastathā ||86||

87kathaṃ gacchata niḥsnehāḥ sutasnehaṃ visṛjya ca
|
pradīptāḥ putraśokena sannivartata mānuṣāḥ ||87||

88śrutvā gṛdhrasya vacanaṃ pāpasyehākṛtātmanaḥ
|
sukhasyānantaraṃ duḥkhaṃ duḥkhasyānantaraṃ sukham ||88||

89sukhaduḥkhāvṛte loke nehāstyekamanantaram
|
imaṃ kṣititale tyaktvā bālaṃ rūpasamanvitam ||89||

90kulaśobhākaraṃ mūḍhāḥ putraṃ tyaktvā kva yāsyatha
|
rūpa-yauvanasampannaṃ dyotamānamiva śriyā ||90||

91jīvantameva paśyāmi manasā nātra saṃśayaḥ
|
vināśo nāsya na hi vai sukhaṃ prāpsyatha mānuṣāḥ ||91||

92putraśokābhitaptānāṃ mṛtānāmadya vaḥ kṣamam
|
sukhasambhāvanaṃ kṛtvā dhārayitvā sukhaṃ svayam
|
tyaktvā gamiṣyatha kvādya samutsṛjyālpabuddhivat ||92||

93bhīṣma uvāca|
tathā dharmavirodhena priyamithyābhidhāyinā
|
śmaśānavāsinā nityaṃ rātriṃ mṛgayatā nṛpa ||93||

94tato madhyasthatāṃ nītā vacanairamṛtopamaiḥ
|
jambukena svakāryārthaṃ bāndhavāstasya dhiṣṭhitāḥ ||94||

95gṛdhra uvāca |
ayaṃ pretasamākīrṇo yakṣarākṣasasevitaḥ
|
dāruṇaḥ kānanoddeśaḥ kauśikairabhināditaḥ ||95||

96bhīmaḥ sughoraśca tathā nīlameghasamaprabhaḥ
|
asmiñchavaṃ parityajya pretakāryāṇyupāsata ||96||

97bhānuvatprayātyastaṃ yāvacca vimalā diśaḥ
|
tāvadenaṃ parityajya pretakāryāṇyupāsata ||97||

98nadanti paruṣaṃ śyenāḥ śivāḥ krośanti dāruṇam
|
mṛgendrāḥ pratinandanti ravirastaṃ ca gacchati ||98||

99citādhūmena nīlena saṃrajyante ca pādapāḥ
|
śmaśāne ca nirāhārāḥ pratinardanti devatāḥ ||99||

100sarve vikṛtadehāścāpyasmindeśe sudāruṇe
|
yuṣmānpradharṣayiṣyanti vikṛtā māṃsabhojinaḥ ||100||

101krūraścāyaṃ vanoddeśo bhayamadya bhaviṣyati
|
tyajyatāṃ kāṣṭhabhūto'yaṃ mṛṣyatāṃ jāmbukaṃ vacaḥ ||101||

102yadi jambukavākyāni niṣphalānyanṛtāni ca
|
śroṣyatha bhraṣṭavijñānāstataḥ sarve vinaṃkṣyatha ||102||

103jambuka uvāca |
sthīyatāṃ neha bhetavyaṃ yāvattapati bhāskaraḥ
|
tāvadasmin sute snehādanirvedena vartata ||103||

104svairaṃ rudanto viśrabdhāściraṃ snehena paśyata
|
sthīyatāṃ yāvadādityaḥ kiṃ ca kravyādabhāṣitaiḥ ||104||

105yadi gṛdhrasya vākyāni tīvrāṇi rabhasāni ca
|
gṛhṇīta mohitātmānaḥ suto vo na bhaviṣyati ||105||

106bhīṣma uvāca|
gṛdhro'stamityāha gato gato neti ca jambukaḥ
|
mṛtasya taṃ parijanamūcatustau kṣudhānvitau ||106||

107svakāryabaddhakakṣau tau rājan gṛdhro'tha jambukaḥ
|
kṣutpipāsāpariśrāntau śāstramālambya jalpataḥ ||107||

108tayorvijñānaviduṣordvayormugapatatriṇoḥ
|
vākyairamṛtakalpaistaiḥ pratiṣṭhanti vrajanti ca ||108||

109śokadainyasamāviṣṭā rudantastasthire tadā
|
svakāryakuśalābhyāṃ te sambhrāmyante ha naipuṇāt ||109||

110tathā tayorvivadatorvijñānaviduṣordvayoḥ
|
bāndhavānāṃ sthitānāṃ cāpyupātiṣṭhata śaṅkaraḥ ||110||

111devyā praṇodito devaḥ kāruṇyārdrīkṛtekṣaṇaḥ
|
tatastānāha manujān varado'smīti śaṅkaraḥ ||111||

112te pratyūcuridaṃ vākyaṃ duḥkhitāḥ praṇatāḥ sthitāḥ
|
ekaputravihīnānāṃ sarveṣāṃ jīvitārthinām ||112||

113putrasya no jīvadānājjīvitaṃ dātumarhasi
|
evamuktaḥ sa bhagavān vāripūrṇena cakṣuṣā ||113||

114jīvitaṃ sma kumārāya prādādvarṣaśatāni vai
|
tathā gomāyugṛdhrābhyāṃ prādadatkṣudvināśanam ||114||

115varaṃ pinākī bhagavān sarvabhūtahite rataḥ
|
tataḥ praṇamya te devaṃ prāyo harṣasamanvitāḥ ||115||

116kṛtakṛtyāḥ sukhaṃ hṛṣṭāḥ prātiṣṭhanta tadā vibho
|
anivedena dīrgheṇa niścayena dhruveṇa ca ||116||

117devadevaprasādācca kṣipraṃ phalamavāpyate
|
paśya daivasya saṃyogaṃ bāndhavānāṃ ca niścayam ||117||

118kṛpaṇānāṃ tu rudatāṃ kṛtamaśrupramārjanam
|
paśya cālpena kālena niścayānveṣaṇena ca ||118||

119prasādaṃ śaṅkarātprāpya duḥkhitāḥ sukhamāpnuvan
|
te vismitāḥ prahṛṣṭāśca putrasaṃjīvanāt punaḥ ||119||

120babhūvurbharataśreṣṭha prasādācchaṅkarasya vai
|
tataste tvaritā rājaṃstyaktvā śokaṃ śiśūdbhavam ||120||

121viviśuḥ putramādāya nagaraṃ hṛṣṭamānasāḥ
|
eṣā buddhiḥ samastānāṃ cāturvarṇyena darśitā ||121||

122dharmārthamokṣasaṃyuktamitihāsamimaṃ śubham
