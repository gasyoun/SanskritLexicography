Mahābhārata 13.23 (Anuśāsanaparvan)

1yudhiṣṭhira uvāca|
śrāddhakāle ca daive ca pitrye'pi ca pitāmaha
|
icchāmīha tvayā'khyātaṃ vihitaṃ yatsurarṣibhiḥ ||1||

2bhīṣma uvāca|
daivaṃ paurvāhṇikaṃ kuryādaparāhṇe tu paitṛkam
|
maṅgalācārasampannaḥ kṛtaśaucaḥ prayatnavān ||2||

3manuṣyāṇāṃ tu madhyāhne pradadyādupapattibhiḥ
|
kālahīnaṃ tu yaddānaṃ taṃ bhāgaṃ rakṣasāṃ viduḥ ||3||

4laṅghitaṃ cāvalīḍhaṃ ca kalipūrvaṃ ca yatkṛtam
|
rajasvalābhidṛṣṭaṃ ca taṃ bhāgaṃ rakṣasāṃ viduḥ ||4||

5avaghuṣṭaṃ ca yadbhuktamavratena ca bhārata
|
parāmṛṣṭaṃ śunā caiva taṃ bhāgaṃ rakṣasāṃ viduḥ ||5||

6keśakīṭāvapatitaṃ kṣutaṃ śvabhiravekṣitam
|
ruditaṃ cāvadhūtaṃ ca taṃ bhāgaṃ rakṣasāṃ viduḥ ||6||

7niroṅkāreṇa yadbhuktaṃ saśastrena ca bhārata
|
durātmanā ca yadbhuktaṃ taṃ bhāgaṃ rakṣasāṃ viduḥ ||7||

8parocchiṣṭaṃ ca yadbhuktaṃ paribhuktaṃ ca yadbhavet
|
daive pitrye ca satataṃ taṃ bhāgaṃ rakṣasāṃ viduḥ ||8||

9mantrahīnaṃ kriyāhīnaṃ yacchrāddhaṃ pariviṣyate
|
tribhirvarṇairnaraśreṣṭha taṃ bhāgaṃ rakṣasāṃ viduḥ ||9||

10ājyāhutiṃ vinā caiva yatkiñcitpariviṣyate
|
durācāraiśca yadbhuktaṃ taṃ bhāgaṃ rakṣasāṃ viduḥ ||10||

11ye bhāgā rakṣasāṃ prāptāsta uktā bharatarṣabha
|
ata ūrdhvaṃ visargasya parīkṣāṃ brāhmaṇe śṛṇu ||11||

12yāvantaḥ patitā viprā jaḍonmattāstathaiva ca
|
daive vā'pyatha pitrye vā rājannārhanti ketanam ||12||

13śvitrī klībaśca kuṣṭhī ca tathā yakṣmahataśca yaḥ
|
apasmārī ca yaścāndho rājannārhanti ketanam ||13||

14cikitsakā devalakā vṛthā niyamadhāriṇaḥ
|
somavikrayiṇaścaiva rājannārhanti ketanam ||14||

15gāyanā nartakāścaiva plavakā vādakāstathā
|
kathakā yodhakāścaiva rājannārhanti ketanam ||15||

16hotāro vṛṣalānāṃ ca vṛṣalādhyāpakāstathā
|
tathā vṛṣalaśiṣyāśca rājannārhanti ketanam ||16||

17anuyoktā ca yo vipro anuyuktaśca bhārata
|
nārhatastāvapi śrāddhaṃ brahmavikrayiṇau hi tau ||17||

18agraṇīryaḥ kṛtaḥ pūrvaṃ varṇāvaraparigrahaḥ
|
brāhmaṇaḥ sarvavidyo'pi rājannārhanti ketanam ||18||

19anagnayaśca ye viprā mṛtaniryātakāśca ye
|
stenāśca patitāścaiva rājannārhanti ketanam ||19||

20aparijñātapūrvāśca gaṇapūrvāśca bhārata
|
putrikāpūrvaputrāśca śrāddhe nārhanti ketanam ||20||

21ṛṇakartā ca yo rājanyaśca vārdhuṣiko naraḥ
|
prāṇivikrayavṛttiśca rājannārhanti ketanam ||21||

22strīpūrvāḥ kāṇḍapṛṣṭhāśca yāvanto bharatarṣabha
|
ajapā brāhmaṇāścaiva śrāddhe nārhanti ketanam ||22||

23śrāddhe daive ca nirdiṣṭo brāhmaṇo bharatarṣabha
|
dātuḥ pratigrahītuśca śṛṇuṣvānugrahaṃ punaḥ ||23||

24cīrṇavratā guṇairyuktā bhaveyurye'pi karṣakāḥ
|
sāvitrījñāḥ kriyāvantaste rājanketanakṣamāḥ ||24||

25kṣātradharmiṇamapyājau ketayetkulajaṃ dvijam
|
na tveva vaṇijaṃ tāta śrāddhe ca parikalpayet ||25||

26agnihotrī ca yo vipro grāmavāsī ca yo bhavet
|
astenaścātithijñaśca sa rājan ketanakṣamaḥ ||26||

27sāvitrīṃ japate yastu trikālaṃ bharatarṣabha
|
bhikṣāvṛttiḥ kriyāvāṃśca sa rājanketanakṣamaḥ ||27||

28uditāstamito yaśca tathaivāstamitoditaḥ
|
ahiṃsraścālpadoṣaśca sa rājanketanakṣamaḥ ||28||

29akalkako hyatarkaśca brāhmaṇo bharatarṣabha
|
saṃsarge bhaikṣyavṛttiśca sa rājanketanakṣamaḥ ||29||

30avratī kitavaḥ stenaḥ prāṇivikrayiko vaṇik
|
paścācca pītavānsomaṃ sa rājanketanakṣamaḥ ||30||

31arjayitvā dhanaṃ pūrvaṃ dāruṇairapi karmabhiḥ
|
bhavetsarvātithiḥ paścātsa rājanketanakṣamaḥ ||31||

32brahmavikrayanirdiṣṭaṃ striyā yaccārjitaṃ dhanam
|
adeyaṃ pitṛviprebhyo yacca klaibyādupārjitam ||32||

33kriyamāṇe'pavarge ca yo dvijo bharatarṣabha
|
na vyāharati yadyuktaṃ tasyādharmo gavānṛtam ||33||

34śrāddhasya brāhmaṇaḥ kālaḥ prāptaṃ dadhi ghṛtaṃ tathā
|
somakṣayaśca māṃsaṃ ca yadaraṇyaṃ yudhiṣṭhira ||34||

35śrāddhāpavarge viprasya svadhā vai muditā bhavet
|
kṣatriyasyāpi yo brūyātprīyantāṃ pitarastviti ||35||

36apavarge tu vaiśyasya śrāddhakarmāṇi bhārata
|
akṣayyamabhidhātavyaṃ svasti śūdrasya bhārata ||36||

37puṇyāhavācanaṃ daivaṃ brāhmaṇasya vidhīyate
|
etadeva niroṅkāraṃ kṣatriyasya vidhīyate ||37||

38vaiśyasya daive vaktavyaṃ prīyantāṃ devatā iti
|
karmaṇāmānupūrvyeṇa vidhipūrvaṃ kṛtaṃ śṛṇu ||38||

39jātakarmādikāḥ sarvāstriṣu varṇeṣu bhārata
|
brahmakṣatre hi mantroktā vaiśyasya ca yudhiṣṭhira ||39||

40viprasya raśanā mauñjī maurvī rājanyagāminī
|
bālvajī hyeva vaiśyasya dharma eṣa yudhiṣṭhira ||40||

41dātuḥ pratigrahītuśca dharmādharmāvimau śṛṇu
|
brāhmaṇasyānṛte'dharmaḥ proktaḥ pātakasaṃjñitaḥ
|
caturguṇaḥ kṣatriyasya vaiśyasyāṣṭaguṇaḥ smṛtaḥ ||41||

42nānyatra brāhmaṇo'śnīyātpūrvaṃ vipreṇa ketitaḥ
|
yavīyānpaśuhiṃsāyāṃ tulyadharmo bhavetsa hi ||42||

43tathā rājanyavaiśyābhyāṃ yadyaśnīyāttu ketitaḥ
|
yavīyān paśuhiṃsāyāṃ bhāgārdhaṃ samavāpnuyāt ||43||

44daivaṃ vā'pyathavā pitryaṃ yo'śnīyādbrāhmaṇādiṣu
|
asnāto brāhmaṇo rājaṃstasyādharmo gavānṛtam ||44||

45āśauco brāhmaṇo rājanyo'śnīyādbrāhmaṇādiṣu
|
jñānapūrvamatho lobhāttasyādharmo gavānṛtam ||45||

46arthenānyena yo lipsetkarmārthaṃ caiva bhārata
|
āmantrayati rājendra tasyādharmo'nṛtaṃ smṛtam ||46||

47avedavratacāritrāstribhirvarṇairyudhiṣṭhira
|
mantravatpariviṣyante tasyādharmo gavānṛtam ||47||

48yudhiṣṭhira uvāca|
pitryaṃ vā'pyathavā daivaṃ dīyate yatpitāmaha
|
etadicchāmyahaṃ jñātuṃ dattaṃ keṣu mahāphalam ||48||

49bhīṣma uvāca|
yeṣāṃ dārāḥ pratīkṣante suvṛṣṭimiva karṣakāḥ
|
uccheṣapariśeṣaṃ hi tānbhojaya yudhiṣṭhira ||49||

50cāritraniratā rājanye kṛśāḥ kṛśavṛttayaḥ
|
arthinaścopagacchanti teṣu dattaṃ mahāphalam ||50||

51tadbhaktāstadgṛhā rājaṃstadbalāstadapāśrayāḥ
|
arthinaśca bhavantyarthe teṣu dattaṃ mahāphalam ||51||

52taskarebhyaḥ parebhyo vā ye bhayārtā yudhiṣṭhira
|
arthino bhoktumicchanti teṣu dattaṃ mahāphalam ||52||

53akalkakasya viprasya raukṣyātkarkaśatātmanaḥ
|
vaṭavo yasya bhikṣanti tebhyo dattaṃ mahāphalam ||53||

54hṛtasvā hṛtadārāśca ye viprā deśasaṃplave
|
arthārthamabhigacchanti tebhyo dattaṃ mahāphalam ||54||

55vratino niyamasthāśca ye viprāḥ śrutasaṃmatāḥ
|
tatsamāptyarthamicchanti tebhyo dattaṃ mahāphalam ||55||

56atyutkrāntāśca dharmeṣu pāṣaṇḍasamayeṣu ca
|
kṛśaprāṇāḥ kṛśadhanāstebhyo dattaṃ mahāphalam ||56||

57kṛtasarvasvaharaṇā nirdoṣāḥ praviṣṇubhiḥ
|
spṛhayanti ca bhuktvā'nnaṃ teṣu dattaṃ mahāphalam ||57||

58tapasvinastaponiṣṭhāsteṣāṃ bhaikṣacarāśca ye
|
arthinaḥ kiñcidicchanti teṣu dattaṃ mahāphalam ||58||

59mahāphalavidhirdāne śrutaste bharatarṣabha
|
nirayaṃ yena gacchanti svargaṃ caiva hi tacchṛṇu ||59||

60gurvarthamabhayārthaṃ vā varjayitvā yudhiṣṭhira
|
ye'nṛtaṃ kathayanti sma te vai nirayagāminaḥ ||60||

61paradārābhihartāraḥ paradārābhimarśinaḥ
|
paradāraprayoktāraste vai nirayagāminaḥ ||61||

62ye parasvāpahartāraḥ parasvānāṃ ca nāśakāḥ
|
sūcakāśca pareṣāṃ ye te vai nirayagāminaḥ ||62||

63prapāṇāṃ ca sabhānāṃ ca saṅkramāṇāṃ ca bhārata
|
agārāṇāṃ ca bhettāro narā nirayagāminaḥ ||63||

64anāthaṃ pramadāṃ bālāṃ vṛddhāṃ bhītāṃ tapasvinīm
|
vañcayanti narā ye ca te vai nirayagāminaḥ ||64||

65vṛtticchedaṃ gṛhacchedaṃ dāracchedaṃ ca bhārata
|
mitracchedaṃ tathā''śāyāste vai nirayagāminaḥ ||65||

66sūcakāḥ setubhettāraḥ paravṛttyupajīvakāḥ
|
akṛtajñāśca mitrāṇāṃ te vai nirayagāminaḥ ||66||

67pāṣaṇḍā dūṣakāścaiva samayānāṃ ca dūṣakāḥ
|
ye pratyavasitāścaiva te vai nirayagāminaḥ ||67||

68viṣamavyavahārāśca viṣamāścaiva vṛddhiṣu
|
lābheṣu viṣamāścaiva te vai nirayagāminaḥ ||68||

69dūtasaṃvyavahārāśca niṣparīkṣāśca mānavāḥ
|
prāṇihiṃsāpravṛttāśca te vai nirayagāminaḥ ||69||

70kṛtāśaṃ kṛtanirdeśaṃ kṛtabhaktaṃ kṛtaśramam
|
bhedairye vyapakarṣanti te vai nirayagāminaḥ ||70||

71paryaśnanti ca ye dārānagnibhṛtyātithīṃstathā
|
utsannapitṛdevejyāste vai nirayagāminaḥ ||71||

72vedavikrayiṇaścaiva vedānāṃ caiva dūṣakāḥ
|
vedānāṃ lekhakāścaiva te vai nirayagāminaḥ ||72||

73cāturāśramyabāhyāśca śrutibāhyāśca ye narāḥ
|
vikarmabhiśca jīvanti te vai nirayagāminaḥ ||73||

74keśavikrayikā rājan viṣavikrayikāśca ye
|
kṣīravikrayikāścaiva te vai nirayagāminaḥ ||74||

75brāhmaṇānāṃ gavāṃ caiva kanyānāṃ ca yudhiṣṭhira
|
ye'ntaraṃ yānti kāryeṣu te vai nirayagāminaḥ ||75||

76śastravikrayikāścaiva kartāraśca yudhiṣṭhira
|
śalyānāṃ dhanuṣāṃ caiva te vai nirayagāminaḥ ||76||

77śilābhiḥ śaṃkubhirvāpi śvabhrairvā bharatarṣabha
|
ye mārgamanurundhanti te vai nirayagāminaḥ ||77||

78upādhyāyāṃśca bhṛtyāṃśca bhaktāṃśca bharatarṣabha
|
ye tyajantyavikārāṃstrīṃste vai nirayagāminaḥ ||78||

79aprāptadamakāścaiva nāsānāṃ vedhakāśca ye
|
bandhakāśca paśūnāṃ ye te vai nirayagāminaḥ ||79||

80agoptāraśca rājāno baliṣaḍbhāgataskarāḥ
|
samarthāścāpyadātāraste vai nirayagāminaḥ ||80||

81kṣāntān dāntāṃstathā prājñān dīrghakālaṃ sahoṣitān
|
|
tyajanti kṛtakṛtyā ye te vai nirayagāminaḥ ||81||

82bālānāmatha vṛddhānāṃ dāsānāṃ caiva ye narāḥ
|
adatvā bhakṣayantyagre te vai nirayagāminaḥ ||82||

83ete pūrvaṃ vinirdiṣṭāḥ proktā nirayagāminaḥ
|
bhāginaḥ svargalokasya vakṣyāmi bharatarṣabha ||83||

84sarveṣveva tu kāryeṣu daivapūrveṣu bhārata
|
hanti putrān paśūnkṛtsnānbrāhmaṇātikramaḥ kṛtaḥ ||84||

85dānena tapasā caiva satyena ca yudhiṣṭhira
|
ye dharmamanuvartante te narāḥ svargagāminaḥ ||85||

86śuśrūṣābhistapobhiśca vidyāmādāya bhārata
|
ye pratigrahāniḥsnehāste narāḥ svargagāminaḥ ||86||

87bhayātpāpāttathā bādhāddāridryādvyādhidharṣaṇāt
|
yatkṛte pratimucyante te narāḥ svargagāminaḥ ||87||

88kṣamāvantaśca dhīrāśca dharmakāryeṣu cotthitāḥ
|
maṅgalācārasampannāḥ puruṣāḥ svargagāminaḥ ||88||

89nivṛttā madhumāṃsebhyaḥ paradārebhya eva ca
|
nivṛttāścaiva madyebhyaste narāḥ svargagāminaḥ ||89||

90āśramāṇāṃ ca kartāraḥ kulānāṃ caiva bhārata
|
deśānāṃ nagarāṇāṃ ca te narāḥ svargagāminaḥ ||90||

91vastrābharaṇadātāro bhaktapānānnadāstathā
|
kuṭumbānāṃ ca dātāraḥ puruṣāḥ svargagāminaḥ ||91||

92sarvahiṃsānivṛttāśca narāḥ sarvasahāśca ye
|
sarvasyāśrayabhūtāśca te narāḥ svargagāminaḥ ||92||

93mātaraṃ pitaraṃ caiva śuśrūṣanti jitendriyāḥ
|
bhrātṝṇāṃ caiva sasnehāste narāḥ svargagāminaḥ ||93||

94āḍhyāśca balavantaśca yauvanasthāśca bhārata
|
ye vai jitendriyā dhīrāste narāḥ svargagāminaḥ ||94||

95aparādhiṣu sasnehā mṛdavo mṛduvatsalāḥ
|
ārādhanasukhāścāpi puruṣāḥ svargagāminaḥ ||95||

96sahasrapariveṣṭārastathaiva ca sahasradāḥ
|
trātāraśca sahasrāṇāṃ te narāḥ svargagāminaḥ ||96||

97suvarṇasya ca dātāro gavāṃ ca bharatarṣabha
|
yānānāṃ vāhanānāṃ ca te narāḥ svargagāminaḥ ||97||

98vaivāhikānāṃ dravyāṇāṃ preṣyāṇāṃ ca yudhiṣṭhira
|
dātāro vāsasāṃ caiva te narāḥ svargagāminaḥ ||98||

99vihārāvasathodyānakūpārāmasabhāprapāḥ
|
vaprāṇāṃ caiva kartāraste narāḥ svargagāminaḥ ||99||

100niveśanānāṃ kṣetrāṇāṃ vasatīnāṃ ca bhārata
|
dātāraḥ prārthitānāṃ ca te narāḥ svargagāminaḥ ||100||

101rasānāṃ cātha bījānāṃ dhānyānāṃ ca yudhiṣṭhira
|
svayamutpādya dātāraḥ puruṣāḥ svargagāminaḥ ||101||

102yasmiṃstasmin kule jātā bahuputrāḥ śatāyuṣaḥ
|
sānukrośā jitakrodhāḥ puruṣāḥ svargagāminaḥ ||102||

103etaduktamamutrārtha daivaṃ pitryaṃ ca bhārata
|
dānadharmaṃ ca dānasya yatpūrvamṛṣibhiḥ kṛtam ||103||
