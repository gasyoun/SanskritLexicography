Mahābhārata 12.83 (Śāntiparvan)

1yudhiṣṭhira uvāca|
sabhāsadaḥ sahāyāśca suhṛdaśca viśāmpate
|
paricchadāstathā'mātyāḥ kīdṛśāḥ syuḥ pitāmaha||1||

2bhīṣma uvāca|
hrīniṣevāstathā dāntāḥ satyārjavasamanvitāḥ
|
śaktāḥ kathayituṃ samyakte tava syuḥ sabhāsadaḥ||2||

3amātyāṃścātiśūrāṃśca brāhmaṇāṃśca pariśrutān
|
susaṃtuṣṭāṃśca kaunteya mahotsāhāṃśca karmasu||3||

4etānsahāyā~llipsethāḥ sarvāsvāpatsu bhārata
|
kulīnaḥ pūjito nityaṃ na hi śaktiṃ nigṛhati||4||

5prasannamaprasannaṃ vā pīḍitaṃ hatameva vā
|
āvartayati bhūyiṣṭhaṃ tadeva hyanupālitam||5||

6kulīnā deśajāḥ prājñā rūpavanto bahuśrutāḥ
|
pragalbhāścānuraktāśca te tava syuḥ paricchadāḥ||6||

7dauṣkuleyāśca lubdhāśca nṛśaṃsā nirapatrapāḥ
|
te tvāṃ tāta niṣeveyuryāvadārdrakapāṇayaḥ||7||

8kulīnān śīlasampannāniṅgitajñānaniṣṭhurān
|
deśakālavidhānajñān bhartṛkāryahitaiṣiṇaḥ||8||

9nityamartheṣu sarveṣu rājā kurvīta mantriṇaḥ
|
arthamānārghasatkārairbhogairuccāvacaiḥ priyaiḥ||9||

10yānarthabhājo manyethāste te syuḥ sukhabhāginaḥ
|
abhinnavṛttā vidvāṃsaḥ sadvṛttāścaritavratāḥ
|
na tvāṃ nityārthino jahyurakṣudrāḥ satyavādinaḥ||10||

11anāryā ye na jānanti samayaṃ mandacetasaḥ
|
tebhyaḥ parijugupsethā ye cāpi samayacyutāḥ||11||

12naikamicchedgaṇaṃ hitvā syāccedanyataragrahaḥ
|
yastveko bahubhiḥ śreyānkāmaṃ tena gaṇaṃ tyajet||12||

13śreyaso lakṣaṇaṃ caitadvikramo yasya dṛśyate
|
kīrtipradhāno yaśca syātsamaye yaśca tiṣṭhati||13||

14samarthānpūjayedyaśca nāspardhyaiḥ spardhate ca yaḥ
|
na ca kāmādbhayātkrodhāllobhādvā dharmamutsṛjet||14||

15amānī satyavānkṣānto jitātmā mānasaṃyutaḥ
|
sa mantrasahāyaḥ syāt sarvāvasthāparīkṣitaḥ||15||

16kulīnaḥ kulasampannastitikṣurdakṣa ātmavān
|
śūraḥ kṛtajñaḥ satyaśca śreyasaḥ pārtha lakṣaṇam||16||

17tasyaivaṃ vartamānasya puruṣasya vijānataḥ
|
amitrāḥ samprasīdanti tathā mitrībhavantyapi||17||

18ata ūrdhvamamātyānāṃ parīkṣeta guṇāguṇam
|
saṃyatātmā kṛtaprajño bhūtikāmaśca bhūmipaḥ||18||

19sambandhipuruṣairāptairabhijātaiḥ svadeśajaiḥ
|
ahāryairavyabhīcāraiḥ sarvaśaḥ suparīkṣitaiḥ||19||

20yaunāḥ śrautāstathā maulāstathaivāpyanahaṃkṛtāḥ
|
kartavyā bhūtikāmena puruṣeṇa bubhūṣatā||20||

21eṣāṃ vainayikī buddhiḥ prakṛtiścaiva śobhanā
|
tejo, dhairyaṃ, kṣamā, śaucamanurāgaḥ sthitirdhṛtiḥ||21||

22parīkṣya ca guṇānnityaṃ prauḍhabhāvāndhuraṃdharān
|
pañcopadhāvyatītāṃśca kuryādrājā'rthakāriṇaḥ||22||

23paryāptavacanānvīrānpratipattiviśāradān
|
kulīnānsattvasampannāniṅgitajñānaniṣṭhurān||23||

24deśakālavidhānajñānbhartṛkāryahitaiṣiṇaḥ
|
nityamartheṣu sarveṣu rājankurvīta mantriṇaḥ||24||

25hīnatejo'bhisaṃsṛṣṭo naiva jātu vyavasyati
|
avaśyaṃ janayatyeva sarvakarmasu saṃśayam||25||

26evamalpaśruto mantrī kalyāṇābhijano'pyuta
|
dharmārthakāmasaṃyukto nālaṃ mantraṃ parīkṣitum||26||

27tathaivānabhijāto'pi kāmamastu bahuśrutaḥ
|
anāyaka ivācakṣurmuhyatyaṇuṣu karmasu||27||

28yo vāpyasthirasaṃkalpo buddhimānāgatāgamaḥ
|
upāyajño'pi nālaṃ sa karma prāpayituṃ ciram||28||

29kevalātpunarādānātkarmaṇo nopapadyate
|
parāmarśo viśeṣāṇāmaśrutasyeha durmateḥ||29||

30mantriṇyananurakte tu viśvāso nopapadyate
|
tasmādananuraktāya naiva mantraṃ prakāśayet||30||

31vyathayeddhi sa rājānaṃ mantribhiḥ sahito'nṛjuḥ
|
mārutopahitacchidraiḥ praviśyāgniriva dumam||31||

32saṃkruddhaścaikadā svāmī sthānāccaivāpakarṣati
|
vācā kṣipati saṃrabdhaḥ punaḥ paścātprasīdati||32||

33tāni tānyanuraktena śakyāni hi titikṣitum
|
mantriṇāṃ ca bhavetkrodho visphūrjitamivāśaneḥ||33||

34yastu saṃsahate tāni bhartuḥ priyacikīrṣayā
|
samānasukhaduḥkhaṃ taṃ pṛcchedartheṣu mānavam||34||

35anṛjustvanurakto'pi sampannaścetarairguṇaiḥ
|
rājñaḥ prajñānayukto'pi na mantraṃ śrotumarhati||35||

36yo'mitraiḥ saha sambaddho na paurānbahu manyate
|
asuhṛttādṛśo jñeyo na mantraṃ śrotumarhati||36||

37avidvānaśuciḥ stabdhaḥ śatrusevī vikatthanaḥ
|
asuhṛtkrodhano lubdho na mantraṃ śrotumarhati||37||

38āgantuścānurakto'pi kāmamastu bahuśrutaḥ
|
satkṛtaḥ saṃvibhakto vā na mantraṃ śrotumarhati||38||

39vidharmato viprakṛtaḥ pitā yasyābhavatpurā
|
satkṛtaḥ sthāpitaḥ so'pi na mantraṃ śrotumarhati||39||

40yaḥ svalpenāpi kāryeṇa suhṛdākṣārito bhavet
|
punaranyairguṇairyukto na mantraṃ śrotumarhati||40||

41kṛtaprajñaśca medhāvī budho jānapadaḥ śuciḥ
|
sarvakarmasu yaḥ śuddhaḥ sa mantraṃ śrotumarhati||41||

42jñānavijñānasampannaḥ prakṛtijñaḥ parātmanoḥ
|
suhṛdātmasamo rājñaḥ sa mantraṃ śrotumarhati||42||

43satyavāk śīlasampanno gambhīraḥ satrapo mṛduḥ
|
pitṛpaitāmaho yaḥ syātsa mantraṃ śrotumarhati||43||

44saṃtuṣṭaḥ sammataḥ satyaḥ śauṭīro dveṣyapāpakaḥ
|
mantravitkālavicchūraḥ sa mantraṃ śrotumarhati||44||

45sarvalokamimaṃ śaktaḥ sāntvena kurute vaśe
|
tasmai mantraḥ prayoktavyo daṇḍamādhitsatā nṛpa||45||

46paurajānapadā yasminviśvāsaṃ dharmato gatāḥ
|
yoddhā nayavipaścicca sa mantraṃ śrotumarhati||46||

47tasmātsarvairguṇairetairupapannāḥ supūjitāḥ
|
mantriṇaḥ prakṛtijñāḥ syustryavarā mahadīpsavaḥ||47||

48svāsu prakṛtiṣu cchidraṃ lakṣayeranparasya ca
|
mantriṇāṃ mantramūlaṃ hi rājño rāṣṭraṃ vivardhate||48||

49nāsya cchidraṃ paraḥ paśyecchidreṣu paramanviyāt
|
gṛhetkūrma ivāṅgāni rakṣedvivaramātmanaḥ||49||

50mantragūḍhā hi rājyasya mantriṇo ye manīṣiṇaḥ
|
mantrasaṃhanano rājā mantrāṅgānītare janāḥ||50||

51rājyaṃ praṇidhimūlaṃ hi mantrasāraṃ pracakṣate
|
svāminastvanuvartante vṛttyarthamiha mantriṇaḥ||51||

52saṃvinīya madakrodhau mānamarṣyāṃ ca nivṛtāḥ
|
nityaṃ pañcopadhātītairmantrayetsaha mantribhiḥ||52||

53teṣāṃ trayāṇāṃ vividhaṃ vimarśaṃ vibuddhya cittaṃ viniveśya tatra
|
svaniścayaṃ tatpratiniścayajñaṃ nivedayeduttaramantrakāle||53||

54dharmārthakāmajñamupetya pṛcchedyukto guruṃ brāhmaṇamuttarārtham
|
niṣṭhā kṛtā tena yadā sahaḥ syāttaṃ mantramārgaṃ praṇayedasaktaḥ||54||

55evaṃ sadā mantrayitavyamāhurye mantratattvārthaviniścayajñāḥ
|
tasmāttamevaṃ praṇayetsadaiva mantraṃ prajāsaṃgrahaṇe samartham||55||

56na vāmanāḥ kubjakṛśā na khañjā nāndho jaḍaḥ strī ca napuṃsakaṃ ca
|
na cātra tiryakca puro na paścānordhvaṃ na cādhaḥ pracaretkathaṃcit||56||

57āruhya vā veśma tathaiva śūnyaṃ sthalaṃ prakāśaṃ kuśakāśahīnam
|
vāgaṅgadoṣānparihṛtya sarvān sammantrayetkāryamahīnakālam||57||
