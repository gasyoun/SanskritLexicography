Mahābhārata 7.182 (Droṇaparvan)

1dhṛtarāṣṭra uvāca|
ekavīravadhe'moghā śaktiḥ sutātmaje yadā
|
kasmātsarvānsamutsṛjya sa tāṃ pārthe na muktavām||1||

2tasminhate hatā hi syuḥ sarve pāṇḍavasṛñjayāḥ
|
ekavīravadhe kasmādyuddhena jayamādadhe||2||

3āhūto na nivarteyamiti tasya mahāvratam
|
svayaṃ mārgayitavyaḥ sa sūtaputreṇa phālgunaḥ||3||

4tato dvairathamānīya phālgunaṃ śakradattayā
|
jaghāna na vṛṣaḥ kasmāttanmamācakṣva sañjaya||4||

5nūnaṃ buddhivihīnaścāpyasahāyaśca me sutaḥ
|
śatrubhirvyaṃsitaḥ pāpaḥ kathaṃ nu sa jayedarīn||5||

6yā hyasya paramā śaktirjayasya ca parāyaṇam
|
sā śaktirvāsudevena vyaṃsitā ca ghaṭotkace||6||

7kuṇeryathā hastagataṃ hriyetphalaṃ balīyasā
|
tathā śaktiramoghā sā moghībhūtā ghaṭotkace||7||

8yathā varāhasya śunaśca yudhyatostayorabhāve śvapacasya lābhaḥ
|
manye vidvanvāsudevasya tadvadyuddhe lābhaḥ karṇahaiḍimbayorvai||8||

9ghaṭotkaco yadi hanyāddhi karṇaṃ paro lābhaḥ sa bhavetpāṇḍavānām
|
vaikartano vā yadi taṃ nihanyāttathāpi kṛtyaṃ śaktināśātkṛtaṃ syāt||9||

10iti prājñaḥ prajñayaitadvicintya ghaṭotkacaṃ sūtaputreṇa yuddhe
|
aghātayadvāsudevo nṛsiṃhaḥ priyaṃ kurvanpāṇḍavānāṃ hitaṃ ca||10||

11sañjaya uvāca|
etaccikīrṣitaṃ jñātvā karṇasya madhusūdanaḥ
|
niyojayāmāsa tadā dvairathe rākṣaseśvaram||11||

12ghaṭotkacaṃ mahāvīryaṃ mahābuddhirjanārdanaḥ
|
amoghāyā vighātārthaṃ rājandurmantrite tava||12||

13tadaiva kṛtakāryā hi vayaṃ syāma kurūdvaha
|
na rakṣedyadi kṛṣṇastaṃ pārthaṃ karṇānmahārathāt||13||

14sāśvadhvajarathaḥ saṃkhye dhṛtarāṣṭra patedbhuvi
|
vinā janārdanaṃ pārtho yogānāmīśvaraṃ prabhum||14||

15taistairupāyairbahubhī rakṣyamāṇaḥ sa pārthiva
|
jayatyabhimukhaḥ śatrūnpārthaḥ kṛṣṇena pālitaḥ||15||

16sa viśeṣāttvamoghāyāḥ kṛṣṇo'rakṣata pāṇḍavam
|
hanyātkṣipraṃ hi kaunteyaṃ śaktirvṛkṣamivāśaniḥ||16||

17dhṛtarāṣṭra uvāca|
virodhī ca kumantrī ca prājñamānī mamātmajaḥ
|
yasyaiṣa samatikrānto vadhopāyo jayaṃ prati||17||

18sa vā karṇo mahābuddhiḥ sarvaśastrabhṛtāṃ varaḥ
|
na muktavānkathaṃ sūta tāmamoghāṃ dhanañjaye||18||

19tavāpi samatikrāntametadgāvalgaṇe katham
|
etamarthaṃ mahābuddhe yattvayā nāvabodhitaḥ||19||

20sañjaya uvāca|
duryodhanasya śakunermama duḥśāsanasya ca
|
rātrau rātrau bhavatyeṣā nityameva samarthanā||20||

21śvaḥ sarvasainyānutsṛjya jahi karṇa dhanañjayam
|
preṣyavatpāṇḍupañcālānupabhokṣyāmahe tataḥ||21||

22athavā nihate pārthe pāṇḍavānyatamaṃ tataḥ
|
sthāpayedyadi vārṣṇeyastasmātkṛṣṇo hi hanyatām||22||

23kṛṣṇo hi mūlaṃ pāṇḍūnāṃ pārthaḥ skandha ivodgataḥ
|
śākhā ivetare pārthāḥ pañcālāḥ patrasaṃjñitāḥ||23||

24kṛṣṇāśrayāḥ kṛṣṇabalāḥ kṛṣṇanāthāśca pāṃḍavāḥ
|
kṛṣṇaḥ parāyaṇaṃ caiṣāṃ jyotiṣāmiva candramāḥ||24||

25tasmātparṇāni śākhāśca skandhaṃ cotsṛjya sūtaja
|
kṛṣṇaṃ hi viddhi pāṇḍūnāṃ mūlaṃ sarvatra sarvadā||25||

26hanyādyadi hi dāśārhaṃ karṇo yādavanandanam
|
kṛtsnā vasumatī rājanvaśe tasya na saṃśayaḥ||26||

27yadi hi sa nihataḥ śayīta bhūmau yadukulapāṇḍavanandano mahātmā
|
nanu tava vasudhā narendra sarvā girisamudravanā vaśaṃ vrajeta||27||

28sā tu buddhiḥ kṛtā'pyevaṃ jāgrati tridaśeśvare
|
aprameye hṛṣīkeśa yuddhakāle'pyamuhyata||28||

29arjunaṃ cāpi rādheyātsadā rakṣati keśavaḥ
|
na hyenamaicchatpramukhe sauteḥ sthāpayituṃ raṇe||29||

30anyāṃścāsmai rathodārānupāsthāpayadacyutaḥ
|
amoghāṃ tāṃ kathaṃ śaktiṃ moghāṃ kuryāmiti prabho||30||

31yaścaivaṃ rakṣate pārthaṃ karṇātkṛṣṇo mahāmanāḥ
|
ātmānaṃ sa kathaṃ rājanna rakṣetpuruṣottamaḥ||31||

32paricintya tu paśyāmi cakrāyudhamarindamam
|
na so'sti triṣu lokeṣu yo jayeta janārdanam||32||

33sañjaya uvāca|
tataḥ kṛṣṇaṃ mahābāhuṃ sātyakiḥ satyavikramaḥ
|
papraccha rathaśārdūlaḥ karṇaṃ prati mahārathaḥ||33||

34ayaṃ ca pratyayaḥ karṇe śaktiścāmitavikramā
|
kimarthaṃ sūtaputreṇa na muktā phālgune tu sā||34||

35śrīvāsudeva uvāca|
duḥśāsanaśca karṇaśca śakuniśca sasaindhavaḥ
|
satataṃ mantrayanti sma duryodhanapurogamāḥ||35||

36karṇa karṇa maheṣvāsa raṇe'mitaparākrama
|
nānyasya śaktireṣā te moktavyā jayatāṃ vara||36||

37ṛte mahārathātkarṇa kuntīputrāddhanañjayāt
|
sa hi teṣāmatiyaśā devānāmiva vāsavaḥ||37||

38tasminvinihate pārthe pāṇḍavāḥ sṛñjayaiḥ saha
|
bhaviṣyanti gatātmānaḥ surā iva niragnayaḥ||38||

39tatheti ca pratijñātaṃ karṇena śinipuṅgava
|
hṛdi nityaṃ ca karṇasya vadho gāṃḍīvadhanvanaḥ||39||

40ahameva tu rādheyaṃ mohayāmi yudhāṃ vara
|
tato nāvāsṛjacchaktiṃ pāṇḍave śvetavāhane||40||

41phālgunasya hi sā mṛtyuritācatayato'niśam
|
na nidrā na ca me harṣo manaso'sti yudhāṃ vara||41||

42ghaṭotkace vyaṃsitāṃ tu dṛṣṭvā tāṃ śinipuṅgava
|
mṛtyorāsyāntarānmuktaṃ paśyāmyadya dhanañjayam||42||

43na pitā na ca ma mātā na yūyaṃ bhrātarastathā
|
na ca prāṇāstathā rakṣyā yathā bībhatsurāhave||43||

44trailokyarājyādyatkiñcidbhavedanyatsudurlabham
|
neccheyaṃ sātvatāhaṃ tadvinā pārthaṃ dhanañjayam||44||

45ataḥ praharṣaḥ sumahānyuyudhānādya me'bhavat
|
mṛtaṃ pratyāgatamiva dṛṣṭvā pārthaṃ dhanañjayam||45||

46ataśca prahito yuddhe mayā karṇāya rākṣasaḥ
|
na hyanyaḥ samare rātrau śaktaḥ karṇaṃ prabādhitum||46||

47sañjaya uvāca|
iti sātyakaye prāha tadā devakinandanaḥ
|
dhanañjayahite yuktastatpriye satataṃ rataḥ||47||
