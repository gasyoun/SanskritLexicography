Mahābhārata 3.90 (Āraṇyakaparvan)

1dhaumya uvāca| udīcyāṃ rājaśārdūla diśi puṇyāni yāni vai
|
tāni te kīrtayiṣyāmi puṇyānyāyatanāni ca||1||

2śṛṇuṣvāvahito bhūtvā mama mantrayataḥ prabho
|
kathāpratigraho vīra śraddhāṃ janayate śubhām||2||

3sarasvatī mahāpuṇyā hradinī tīrthamālinī
|
samudragā mahāvegā yamunā yatra pāṇḍava||3||

4yatra puṇyataraṃ tīrthaṃ plakṣāvataraṇaṃ śubham
|
yatra sārasvatairiṣṭvā gacchantyavabhṛthairdvijāḥ||4||

5puṇyaṃ cākhyāyate divyaṃ śivamagniśiro'nagha
|
sahadevo'yajadyatra śamyākṣepeṇa bhārata||5||

6etasminneva cārthe'sau indragītā yudhiṣṭhira
|
gāthā carati loke'smingīyamānā dvijātibhiḥ||6||

7agnayaḥ sahadevena ye citā yamunāmanu
|
te tasya kuruśārdūla sahasraśatadakṣiṇāḥ||7||

8tatraiva bharato rājā cakravartī mahāyaśāḥ
|
viṃśatiḥ sapta cāṣṭau ca hayamedhānupāharat||8||

9kāmakṛdyo dvijātīnāṃ śrutastāta yathā purā
|
atyantamāśramaḥ puṇyaḥ śarabhaṅgasya viśrutaḥ||9||

10sarasvatī nadī sadbhiḥ satataṃ pārtha pūjitā
|
vālakhilyairmahārāja yatreṣṭamṛṣibhiḥ purā||10||

11dṛṣadvatī mahāpuṇyā yatra khyātā yudhiṣṭhira
|
nyagrodhākhyastu pāñcālyaḥ pāñcālyo dvipadāṃ vara||11||

12dālbhyaghoṣaśca dālbhyāśca dharaṇīstho mahātmanaḥ
|
kaunteyānantayaśasaḥ suvratasyāmitaujasaḥ||12||

13āśramaḥ khyāyate puṇyastriṣu lokeṣu viśrutaḥ
|
etāvarṇāvavarṇau ca viśrutau manujādhipa||13||

14vedajñau vedavidvāṃsau vedavidyāvidāvubhau
|
ījāte kratubhirmukhyaiḥ puṇyairbharatasattama||14||

15sametya bahuśo devāḥ sendrāḥ savaruṇāḥ purā
|
viśākhayūpe'tapyan tena puṇyatamaśca saḥ||15||

16ṛṣirmahānmahābhāgo jamadagnirmahāyaśāḥ
|
palāśakeṣu puṇyeṣu ramyeṣvayajata prabhuḥ||16||

17yatra sarvāḥ saricchreṣṭāḥ sākṣāttamṛṣisattamam
|
svaṃ svaṃ toyamupādāya parivāryopatasthire||17||

18api cātra mahārāja svayaṃ viśvāvasurjagau
|
imaṃ ślokaṃ tadā vīra prekṣya dīkṣāṃ mahātmanaḥ||18||

19yajamānasya vai devāñjamadagnermahātmanaḥ
|
āgamya sarito viprānmadhunā samatarpayan||19||

20gandharvayakṣarakṣobhirapsarobhiśca sevitam
|
kirātakinnarāvāsaṃ śailaṃ śikhariṇāṃ varam||20||

21bibheda tarasā gaṅgā gaṅgādvāraṃ yudhiṣṭhira
|
puṇyaṃ tatkhyāyate rājanbrahmarṣigaṇasevitam||21||

22sanatkumāraḥ kauravya puṇyaṃ kanakhalaṃ tathā
|
parvataśca pururnāma yatra yātaḥ purūravāḥ||22||

23bhṛguryatra tapastepe maharṣigaṇasevite
|
rājansa āśramaḥ khyāto bhṛgutuṅgo mahāgiriḥ||23||

24yaḥ sa bhūtaṃ bhaviṣyacca bhavacca bharatarṣabha
|
nārāyaṇaḥ prabhurviṣṇuḥ śāśvataḥ puruṣottamaḥ||24||

25tasyātiyaśasaḥ puṇyāṃ viśālāṃ badarīmanu
|
āśramaḥ khyāyate puṇyastriṣu lokeṣu viśrutaḥ||25||

26uṣṇatoyavahā gaṅgā śītatoyavahā purā
|
suvarṇasikatā rājanviśālāṃ badarīmanu||26||

27ṛṣayo yatra devāśca mahābhāgā mahaujasaḥ
|
prāpya nityaṃ namasyanti devaṃ nārāyaṇaṃ prabhum||27||

28yatra nārājaṇo devaḥ paramātmā sanātanaḥ
|
tatra kṛtsnaṃ jagatsarvaṃ tīrthānyāyatanāni ca||28||

29tatpuṇyaṃ paramaṃ brahma tattīrthaṃ tattapovanam
|
tatparaṃ paramaṃ devaṃ bhūtānāṃ parameśvaram||29||

30śāśvataṃ paramaṃ caiva dhātāraṃ paramaṃ padam
|
yaṃ viditvā na śocanti vidvāṃsaḥ śāstradṛṣṭayaḥ||30||

31tatra devarṣayaḥ siddhāḥ sarve caiva tapodhanāḥ
|
ādidevo mahāyogī yatrāste madhusūdanaḥ||31||

32puṇyānāmapi tatpuṇyamatra te saṃśayo'stu mā
|
etāni rājanpuṇyāni pṛthivyāṃ pṛthivīpate||32||

33kīrtitāni naraśreṣṭha tīrthānyāyatanāni ca
|
etāni vasubhiḥ sādhyairādityairmarudaśvibhiḥ||33||

34ṛṣibhirdevakalpaiśca sevitāni mahātmabhiḥ
|
carannetāni kaunteya sahito brāhmaṇarṣabhaiḥ
|
bhrātṛbhiśca mahābhāgairutkaṇṭhāṃ vijayiṣyasi||34||
