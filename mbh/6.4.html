Mahābhārata 6.4 (Bhīṣmaparvan)

1vaiśampāyana uvāca|
evamuktvā yayau vyāso dhṛtarāṣṭrāya dhīmate
|
dhṛtarāṣṭro'pi tacchrutvā dhyānamevānvapadyata ||1||

2sa muhūrtamiva dhyātvā viniḥśvasya muhurmuhuḥ
|
sañjayaṃ saṃśitātmānamapṛcchadbharatarṣabha ||2||

3sañjayeme mahīpālāḥ śūrā yuddhābhinandinaḥ
|
anyonyamabhinighnanti śastrairuccāvacairiha ||3||

4pārthivāḥ pṛthivīhetoḥ samabhityajya jīvitam
|
na vā śāmyanti nighnanto vardhayanti yamakṣayam ||4||

5bhaumamaiśvaryamicchanto na mṛṣyante parasparam
|
manye bahuguṇā bhūmistanmamācakṣva sañjaya ||5||

6bahūni ca sahasrāṇi prayutānyarbudāni ca
|
koṭyaśca lokavīrāṇāṃ sammetāḥ kurujāṅgale ||6||

7deśānāṃ ca parīmāṇaṃ nagarāṇāṃ ca sañjaya
|
śrotumicchāmi tattvena yata ete samāgatāḥ ||7||

8divyabuddhipradīpena yuktastvaṃ jñānacakṣuṣā
|
prabhāvāttasya viprarṣervyāsasyāmitatejasaḥ ||8||

9sañjaya uvāca|
yathāprajñaṃ mahāprājña bhaumānvakṣyāmi te guṇān
|
śāstracakṣuravekṣasva namaste bharatarṣabha ||9||

10dvividhānīha bhūtāni carāṇi sthāvarāṇi ca
|
trasānāṃ trividhā yoniraṇḍasvedajarāyujāḥ ||10||

11trasānāṃ khalu sarveṣāṃ śreṣṭhā rājañjarāyujāḥ
|
jarāyujānāṃ pravarā mānavāḥ paśavaśca ye ||11||

12nānārūpadharā rājaṃsteṣāṃ bhedāścaturdaśa
|
vedoktāḥ pṛthivīpāla yeṣu yajñāḥ pratiṣṭhitāḥ ||12||

13grāmyāṇāṃ puruṣāḥ śreṣṭhāḥ siṃhāścāraṇyavāsinām
|
sarveṣāmeva bhūtānāmanyonyenopajīvanam ||13||

14udbhijjāḥ sthāvarāḥ proktāsteṣāṃ pañcaiva jātayaḥ
|
vṛkṣagulmalatāvallyastvaksārāstṛṇajātayaḥ ||14||

15teṣāṃ viṃśatirekonā mahābhūteṣu pañcasu
|
caturviśatiruddiṣṭā gāyatrī lokasammatā ||15||

16ya etāṃ veda gāyatrīṃ puṇyāṃ sarvaguṇānvitām
|
tattvena bharataśreṣṭha sa loke na praṇaśyati ||16||

17araṇyavāsinaḥ sapta saptaiṣāṃ grāmavāsinaḥ
|
siṃhā vyāghrā varāhāśca mahiṣā vāraṇāstathā ||17||

18ṛkṣāśca vānarāścaiva saptāraṇyāḥ smṛtā nṛpa
|
gaurajāvimanuṣyāśca aśvāśvataragardabhāḥ ||18||

19ete grāmyāḥ samākhyātāḥ paśavaḥ sapta sādhubhiḥ
|
ete vai paśavo rājangrāmyāraṇyāścaturdaśa ||19||

20bhūmau ca jāyate sarvaṃ bhūmau sarvaṃ vinaśyati
|
bhūmiḥ pratiṣṭhā bhūtānāṃ bhūmireva sanātanam ||20||

21yasya bhūmistasya sarvaṃ jagatsthāvarajaṅgamam
|
tatrātigṛddhā rājāno vinighnantītaretaram ||21||
