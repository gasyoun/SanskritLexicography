Mahābhārata 17.1 (Mahāprasthānikaparvan)

1janamejaya uvāca|
evaṃ vṛṣṇyandhakakule śrutvā mausalamāhavam
|
pāṇḍavāḥ kimakurvaṃta tathā kṛṣṇe divaṃ gate||1||

2vaiśampāyana uvāca|
śrutvaivaṃ kauravo rājā vṛṣṇīnāṃ kadanaṃ mahat
|
prasthāne matimādhāya vākyamarjunamabravīt||2||

3kālaḥ pacati bhūtāni sarvāṇyeva mahāmate
|
kālapāśamahaṃ manye tvamapi draṣṭumarhasi||3||

4ityuktaḥ sa tu kaunteyaḥ kālaḥ kālaḥ iti bruvan
|
anvapadyata tadvākyaṃ bhrāturjyeṣṭhasya dhīmataḥ||4||

5ajunasya mataṃ jñātvā bhīmaseno yamau tathā
|
anvapadyanta tadvākyaṃ yaduktaṃ savyasācinā||5||

6tato yuyutsumānāyya pravrajan dharmakāmyayā
|
rājyaṃ paridadau sarvaṃ vaiśyāputraṃ yudhiṣṭhiraḥ||6||

7abhiṣicya svarājye ca rājānaṃ ca parikṣitam
|
duḥkhārtaścābravīdrājā subhadrāṃ pāṇḍavāgrajaḥ||7||

8eṣa putrasya putraste kururājo bhaviṣyati
|
yadūnāṃ pariśeṣaśca vajro rājā kṛtaśca ha||8||

9parikṣiddhāstinapure śakraprasthe ca yādavaḥ
|
vajro rājā tvayā rakṣyo mā cādharme manaḥ kṛthāḥ||9||

10ityuktvā dharmarājaḥ sa vāsudevasya dhīmataḥ
|
mātulasya ca vṛddhasya rāmādīnāṃ tathaiva ca||10||

11bhrātṛbhiḥ saha dharmātmā kṛtvodakamatandritaḥ
|
śrāddhānyuddiśya sarveṣāṃ cakāra vidhivattadā||11||

12dvaipāyanaṃ nāradaṃ ca mārkaṃḍeyaṃ tapodhanam
|
bhāradvājaṃ yājñavalkyaṃ harimuddiśya yatnavān||12||

13abhojayatsvādubhojyaṃ kīrtayitvā ca śārṅgiṇam
|
dadau ratnāni vāsāṃsi grāmānaśvān rathāṃstathā||13||

14striyaśca dvijamukhyebhyastadā śatasahasraśaḥ
|
kṛpamabhyarcya ca gurumatha paurapuraskṛtam||14||

15śiṣyaṃ parikṣitaṃ tasmai dadau bharatasattamaḥ
|
tatastu prakṛtīḥ sarvāḥ samānāyya yudhiṣṭhiraḥ||15||

16sarvamācaṣṭa rājarṣiścikīrṣitamathātmanaḥ
|
te śrutvaiva vacastasya paurajānapadā janāḥ||16||

17bhṛśamudvignamanaso nābhyanandanta tadvacaḥ
|
naivaṃ kartavyamiti te tadocustaṃ janādhipam||17||

18na ca rājā tathā'kārṣotkālaparyāyadharmavit
|
tato'numānya dharmātmā paurajānapadaṃ janam||18||

19gamanāya matiṃ cakre bhrātaraścāsya te tadā
|
tataḥ sa rājā kauravyo dharmaputro yudhiṣṭhiraḥ||19||

20utsṛjyābharaṇānyaṅgājjagṛhe valkalānyuta
|
bhīmārjunayamāścaiva dropadī ca yaśasvinī||20||

21tathaiva jagṛhuḥ sarve valkalāni narādhipa
|
vidhivatkārayitveṣṭiṃ naiṣṭhikīṃ bharatarṣabha||21||

22samutsṛjyāpsu sarve'gnīn pratasthurnarapuṃgavāḥ
|
tataḥ praruruduḥ sarvāḥ striyo dṛṣṭvā narottamān||22||

23prasthitān draupadī ṣaṣṭhān purā dyūtajitān yathā
|
hṛrṣo'bhavacca sarveṣāṃ bhrātṝṇāṃ gamanaṃ prati||23||

24yudhiṣṭhiramataṃ jñātvā vṛṣṇikṣayamavekṣya ca
|
bhrātaraḥ pañca kṛṣṇā ca ṣaṣṭhī śvā caiva saptamaḥ||24||

25ātmanā saptamo rājā niryayau gajasāhvayāt
|
paurairanugato dūraṃ sarvairantaḥ puraistathā||25||

26na cainamaśakatkaścinnivartasveti bhāṣitum
|
nyavartaṃta tataḥ sarve narā nagaravāsinaḥ||26||

27kṛpaprabhṛtayaścaiva yuyutsuṃ paryavārayan
|
viveśa gaṅgāṃ kauravya ulūpī bhujagātmajā||27||

28citrāṅgadā yayau cāpi maṇipūrapuraṃ prati
|
śiṣṭāḥ parikṣitaṃ tvanyā mātaraḥ paryavārayan||28||

29pāṇḍavāśca mahātmāno draupadī ca yaśasvinī
|
kṛtopavāsāḥ kauravya prayayuḥ prāṅmukhāstataḥ||29||

30yogayuktā mahātmānastyāgadharmamupeyuṣaḥ
|
abhijagmurbahūndeśānsaritaḥ sāgarāṃstathā||30||

31yudhiṣṭhiro yayāvagre bhīmastu tadanantaram
|
arjunastasya cānveva yayau cāpi yathākramam||31||

32pṛṣṭhatastu varārohā śyāmā padmadalekṣaṇā
|
draupadī yoṣitāṃ śreṣṭhā yayau bharatasattama||32||

33śvā caivānuyayāvekaḥ prasthitānpāṇḍavānvanam
|
krameṇa te yayurvīrā lauhityaṃ salilārṇavam||33||

34gāṇḍīvaṃ tu dhanurdivyaṃ na mumoca dhanaṃjayaḥ
|
ratnalobhānmahārāja te cākṣayye maheṣudhī||34||

35agniṃ te dadṛśustatra sthitaṃ śailamivāgrataḥ
|
mārgamāvṛtya tiṣṭhantaṃ sākṣātpuruṣavigraham||35||

36tato devaḥ sasaptārciḥ pāṇḍavānidamabravīt
|
bho bho pāṇḍusutā vīrāḥ pāvakaṃ māṃ nibodhata||36||

37yudhiṣṭhira mahābāho bhīmasena parantapa
|
arjunāśvisutau vīrau nibodhata vaco mama||37||

38ahamagniḥ kuruśreṣṭhā mayā dagdhaṃ ca khāṇḍavam
|
arjunasya prabhāveṇa tathā nārāyaṇasya ca||38||

39ayaṃ vaḥ phālguno bhrātā gāṇḍīvaṃ paramāyudham
|
parityajya vane yātu nānenārtho'sti kaścana||39||

40cakraratnaṃ tu yatkṛṣṇe sthitamāsīnmahātmani
|
gataṃ tacca punarhaste kālenaiṣyati tasya ha||40||

41varuṇādāhṛtaṃ pūrvaṃ mayaitatpārthakāraṇāt
|
gāṇḍīvaṃ dhanuṣāṃ śreṣṭhaṃ varuṇāyaiva dīyatām||41||

42tataste bhrātaraḥ sarve dhanañjayamacodayan
|
sajale prākṣipaccaitattathā'kṣayye maheṣudhī||42||

43tato'gnirbharataśreṣṭha tatraivāntaradhīyata
|
yayuśca pāṇḍavā vīrāstataste dakṣiṇāmukhāḥ||43||

44tataste tūttareṇaiva tīreṇa lavaṇāmbhasaḥ
|
jagmurbharataśārdūla diśaṃ dakṣiṇapaścimām||44||

45tataḥ punaḥ samāvṛttāḥ paścimāṃ diśameva te
|
dadṛśurdvārakāṃ cāpi sāgareṇa pariplutām||45||

46udīcīṃ punarāvṛttya yayurbharatasattamāḥ
|
prādakṣiṇyaṃ cikīrṣantaḥ pṛthivyā yogadharmiṇaḥ||46||
