Mahābhārata 13.29 (Anuśāsanaparvan)

1bhīṣma uvāca|
evamukto mataṅgastu saṃśitātmā yatavrataḥ
|
sahasramekapādena tato dhyāne vyatiṣṭhata ||1||

2taṃ sahasrāvare kāle śakro draṣṭumupāgamat
|
tadeva ca punarvākyamuvāca balavṛtrahā ||2||

3mataṅga uvāca|
idaṃ varṣasahasraṃ vai brahmacārī samāhitaḥ
|
atiṣṭhamekapādena brāhmaṇyaṃ nāpnuyāṃ katham ||3||

4śakra uvāca|
caṇḍālayonau jātena nāvāpyaṃ vai kathañcana
|
anyaṃ kāmaṃ vṛṇīṣva tvaṃ mā vṛthā te'stvayaṃ śramaḥ ||4||

5evamukto mataṅgastu bhṛśaṃ śokaparāyaṇaḥ
|
adhyatiṣṭhadgayāṃ gatvā sauṅguṣṭhena śataṃ samāḥ ||5||

6sudurvahaṃ vahanyogaṃ kṛśo dhamanisantataḥ
|
tvagasthibhūto dharmātmā sa papāteti naḥ śrutam ||6||

7ta patantamabhidrutya parijagrāha vāsavaḥ
|
varāṇāmīśvaro dātā sarvabhūtahite rataḥ ||7||

8śakra uvāca|
mataṅga brāhmaṇatvaṃ te viruddhamiha dṛśyate
|
brāhmaṇyaṃ durlabhataraṃ saṃvṛtaṃ paripanthibhiḥ ||8||

9pūjayansukhamāpnoti duḥkhamāpnotyapūjayan
|
brāhmaṇaḥ sarvabhūtānāṃ yogakṣemasamarpitā ||9||

10brāhmaṇebhyo'nutṛpyante pitaro devatāstathā
|
brāhmaṇaḥ sarvabhūtānāṃ mataṅga para ucyate ||10||

11brāhmaṇaḥ kurute taddhi yathā yadyacca vāñchati
|
bahvīstu saṃviśantyonīrjāyamānaḥ punaḥpunaḥ ||11||

12paryāye tāta kasmiṃścidbrāhmaṇyamiha vindati
|
tadutsṛjyeha duṣprāpaṃ brāhmaṇyamakṛtātmabhiḥ ||12||

13anyaṃ varaṃ vṛṇīṣva tvaṃ durlabho'yaṃ hi te varaḥ
|
mataṅga uvāca|
kiṃ māṃ tudasi duḥkhārtaṃ mṛtaṃ mārayase ca māṃ ||13||

14tvāṃ tu śocāmi yo labdhvā brāhmaṇyaṃ na bubhūṣase
|
brāhmaṇyaṃ yadi duṣprāpaṃ tribhirvarṇaiḥ śatakrato ||14||

15sudurlabhaṃ sadā'vāpya nānutiṣṭhanti mānavāḥ
|
yaḥ pāpebhyaḥ pāpatamasteṣāmadhama eva saḥ ||15||

16brāhmaṇyaṃ yo na jānīte dhanaṃ labdhveva durlabham
|
duṣprāpaṃ khalu vipratvaṃ prāptaṃ duranupālanam ||16||

17durāvāpamavāpyaitannānutiṣṭhanti mānavāḥ
|
ekārāmo hyahaṃ śakra nirdvandvo niṣparigrahaḥ ||17||

18ahiṃsādamamāsthāya kathaṃ nārhāmi vipratām
|
daivaṃ tu kathametadvai yadahaṃ mātṛdoṣataḥ ||18||

19etāmavasthāṃ saṃprāpto dharmajñaḥ sanpurandara
|
nūnaṃ daivaṃ na śakyaṃ hi pauruṣeṇātivartitum ||19||

20yadarthaṃ yatnavāneva na labhe vipratāṃ vibho
|
evaṃgate tu dharmajña dātumarhasi me varam ||20||

21yadi te'hamanugrāhyaḥ kiñcidvā sukṛtaṃ mama
|
vaiśampāyana uvāca|
vṛṇīṣveti tadā prāha tatastaṃ balavṛtrahā ||21||

22coditastu mahendreṇa mataṅgaḥ prābravīdidam
|
yathā kāmavihārī syāṃ kāmarūpī vihaṅgamaḥ ||22||

23brahmakṣatrāvirodhena pūjāṃ ca prāpnuyāmaham
|
yathā mamākṣayā kīrtirbhaveccāpi purandara ||23||

24kartumahasi tadeva śirasā tvāṃ prasādaye
|
śakra uvāca|
chandodeva iti khyātaḥ strīṇāṃ pūjyo bhaviṣyasi
|
kīrtiśca te'tulā vatsa triṣu lokeṣu yāsyati ||24||

25evaṃ tasmai varaṃ datvā vāsavo'ntaradhīyata ||25||

26prāṇāṃstyaktvā mataṅgo'pi saṃprāptaḥsthānamuttamam
|
evametatparaṃ sthānaṃ brāhmaṇyaṃ nāma bhārata
|
tacca duṣprāpamiha vai mahendravacanaṃ yathā ||26||
