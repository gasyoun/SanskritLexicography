Mahābhārata 9.63 (Śalyaparvan)

1janamejaya uvāca|
kimarthaṃ dvijaśārdūlaṃ dharmarājo yudhiṣṭhiraḥ
|
gāndhāryāḥ preṣayāmāsa vāsudevaṃ parantapam||1||

2yadā pūrvaṃ gataḥ kṛṣṇaḥ śamārthaṃ kauravānprati
|
na ca taṃ labdhavānkāmaṃ tato yuddhamabhūdidam||2||

3nihateṣu tu yodheṣu hate duryodhane tadā
|
pṛthivyāṃ pāṇḍaveyasya niḥsapatne kṛte yudhi||3||

4vidrute śibire śūnye prāpte yaśasi cottame
|
kiṃ na tatkāraṇaṃ brahman yena kṛṣṇo gataḥ punaḥ||4||

5na caitatkārapāṃ brahmannalpaṃ vipratibhāti me
|
yatrāgamadameyātmā svayameva janārdanaḥ||5||

6tattvato vai samācakṣva sarvamadhvaryusattama
|
yaccātra kāraṇaṃ brahman kāryasyāsya viniścaye||6||

7vaiśampāyana uvāca|
tvadyukto'yamanupraśno yanmāṃ pṛcchasi pārthiva
|
tatte'haṃ sampravakṣyāmi yathāvadbharatarṣabha||7||

8hataṃ duryodhanaṃ dṛṣṭvā bhīmasenena saṃyuge
|
vyutkramya samayaṃ rājan dhārtarāṣṭraṃ mahābalam||8||

9anyāyena hataṃ dṛṣṭvā gadāyuddhena bhārata
|
yudhiṣṭhiraṃ mahārāja mahadbhayamathāviśat||9||

10cintayāno mahābhāgaṃ gāndhārīṃ tapasānvitām
|
ghoreṇa tapasā yuktāṃ trailokyamapi sā dahet||10||

11tasya cintayamānasya buddhiḥ samabhavattadā
|
gāndhāryāḥ krodhadīptāyāḥ pūrvaṃ praśamanaṃ bhavet||11||

12sā hi putravadhaṃ śrutvā kṛtamasmābhirīdṛśam
|
mānasenāgninā kruddhā bhasmasānnaḥ kariṣyati||12||

13kathaṃ duḥkhamidaṃ tīvraṃ gāndhārī sampraśakṣyati
|
śrutvā vinihataṃ putraṃ chalenājihmayodhinam||13||

14evaṃ vicintya bahudhā bhayaśokasamanvitaḥ
|
vāsudevamidaṃ vākyaṃ dharmarājo'bhyabhāṣata||14||

15tava prasādādgovinda rājyaṃ nihatakaṇṭakam
|
aprāpyaṃ manasāpīdaṃ prāptamasmābhiracyuta||15||

16pratyakṣaṃ me mahābāho saṅgrāme lomaharṣaṇe
|
vimardaḥ sumahānprāptastvayā yādavanandana||16||

17tvayā devāsure yuddhe vadhārthamamaradviṣām
|
yathā sāhyaṃ purā dattaṃ hatāśca vibudhadviṣaḥ||17||

18sāhyaṃ tathā mahābāho dattamasmākamacyuta
|
sārathyena ca vārṣṇeya bhavatā hi dhṛtā vayam||18||

19yadi na tvaṃ bhavennāthaḥ phālgunasya mahāraṇe
|
kathaṃ śakyo raṇe jetuṃ bhavedeṣa balārṇavaḥ||19||

20gadāprahārā vipulāḥ parighaiścāpi tāḍanam
|
śaktibhirbhindipālaiśca tomaraiḥ saparaśvadhaiḥ||20||

21asmatkṛte tvayā kṛṣṇa vācaḥ suparuṣāḥ śrutāḥ
|
śastrāṇāṃ ca nipātā vai vajrasparśopamā raṇe||21||

22te ca te saphalā jātā hate duryodhane'cyuta
|
tatsarvaṃ na yathā naśyetpunaḥ krodhaṃ kṛṣṇa tathā kuru||22||

23sandehadolāṃ prāptaṃ naścetaḥ kṛṣṇa jaye sati
|
gāndhāryā hi mahābāho krodhaṃ buddhyasva mādhava||23||

24sā hi nityaṃ mahābhāga tapasogreṇa karśitā
|
putrapautravadhaṃ śrutvā dhruvaṃ naḥ sampradhakṣyati||24||

25tasyāḥ prasādanaṃ vīra prāptakālaṃ mataṃ mama
|
kaśca tāṃ krodhatāmrākṣīṃ putravyasanakarśitām||25||

26vīkṣituṃ puruṣaḥ śaktastvāmṛte puruṣottama
|
tatra me gamanaṃ prāptaṃ rocate tava mādhava||26||

27gāndhāryāḥ krodhadīptāyāḥ praśamārthamarindama
|
tvaṃ hi kartā vikartā ca lokānāṃ prabhavāvyayaḥ||27||

28hetukāraṇasaṃyuktairvākyaiḥ kālasamīritaiḥ
|
kṣiprameva mahābāho gāndhārīṃ śamayiṣyasi||28||

29pitāmahaśca bhagavān kṛṣṇastatra bhaviṣyati
|
sarvathā te mahābāho gāndhāryāḥ krodhanāśanam||29||

30kartavyaṃ sātvatāṃ śreṣṭha pāṇḍavānāṃ hitārthinā
|
dharmarājasya vacanaṃ śrutvā yadukulodvahaḥ||30||

31āmantrya dārukaṃ prāha rathaḥ sajjo vidhīyatām
|
keśavasya vacaḥ śrutvā tvaramāṇo'tha dārukaḥ||31||

32nyavedayadrathaṃ sajje keśavāya mahātmane
|
taṃ rathaṃ yādavaśreṣṭhaḥ samāruhya parantapaḥ||32||

33jagāma hāstinapuraṃ tvaritaḥ keśavo vibhuḥ
|
tataḥ prāyānmahārāja mādhavo bhagavān rathī||33||

34nāgasāhvayamāsādya praviveśa na vīryavān
|
praviśya nagaraṃ vīro rathaghoṣeṇa nādayan||34||

35viditaṃ dhṛtarāṣṭrasya so'vatīrya rathottamāt
|
abhyagacchadadīnātmā dhṛtarāṣṭraniveśanam||35||

36pūrvaṃ cābhigataṃ tatra so'paśyadṛṣisattamam
|
pādau prapīḍya kṛṣṇasya rājñaścāpi janārdanaḥ||36||

37abhyavādayadavyagro gāndhārīṃ cāpi keśavaḥ
|
tatastu yādavaśreṣṭho dhṛtarāṣṭramadhokṣajaḥ||37||

38pāṇimālambya rājendra susvaraṃ praruroda ha
|
sa muhūrtādivotsṛjya bāṣpaṃ śokasamudbhavam||38||

39prakṣālya vāriṇā netre hyācamya ca yathāvidhi
|
uvāca prastutaṃ vākyaṃ dhṛtarāṣṭramarindamaḥ||39||

40na te'styaviditaṃ kiñcidvṛddhasya tava bhārata
|
kālasya ca yathāvṛttaṃ tatte suviditaṃ prabho||40||

41yadidaṃ pāṇḍavaiḥ sarvaistava cittānurodhibhiḥ
|
kathaṃ kulakṣayo na syāttathā kṣatrasya bhārata||41||

42bhrātṛbhiḥ samayaṃ kṛtvā kṣāntavān dharmavatsalaḥ
|
dyūtacchalajitaiḥ śuddhairvanavāso hyupāgataḥ||42||

43ajñātavāsacaryā ca nānāveṣasamāvṛtaiḥ
|
anye ca bahavaḥ kleśātsvaśaktairiva sarvadā||43||

44mayā ca svayamāgamya yuddhakāla upasthite
|
sarvalokasya sānnidhye grāmāṃstvaṃ pañca yācitaḥ||44||

45tvayā kālopasṛṣṭena lobhato nāpavarjitāḥ
|
tavāparādhānnṛpate sarvaṃ kṣatraṃ kṣayaṃ gatam||45||

46bhīṣmeṇa somadattena bāhlīkena kṛpeṇa ca
|
droṇena ca saputreṇa vidureṇa ca dhīmatā||46||

47yācitastvaṃ śamaṃ nityaṃ na ca tatkṛtavānasi
|
kālopahatacittā hi sarve muhyanti bhārata||47||

48yathā mūḍho bhavānpūrvamasminnarthe samudyate
|
kimanyatkālayogāddhi diṣṭameva parāyaṇam||48||

49mā ca doṣānmahāprājña pāṇḍaveṣu niveśaya
|
alpo'pyatikramo nāsti pāṇḍavānāṃ mahātmanām||49||

50dharmato nyāyataścaiva snehataśca parantapa
|
etatsarvaṃ tu vijñāya hyātmadoṣakṛtaṃ phalam||50||

51asūyāṃ pāṇḍuputreṣu na bhavānkartumarhati
|
kulaṃ vaṃśaśca piṇḍāśca yacca putrakṛtaṃ phalam||51||

52gāndhāryāstava vai nātha pāṇḍaveṣu pratiṣṭhitam
|
tvaṃ caiva kuruśārdūla gāndhārī ca yaśasvinī||52||

53mā śuco naraśārdūla pāṇḍavānprati kilbiṣam
|
etatsarvamanudhyāya ātmanaśca vyatikramam||53||

54śivena pāṇḍavānpāhi namaste bharatarṣabha
|
jānāsi ca mahābāho dharmarājasya yā tvayi||54||

55bhaktirbharataśārdūla snehaścāpi svabhāvataḥ
|
etacca kadanaṃ kṛtvā śatrūṇāmapakāriṇām||55||

56dahyate sa divā rātrau na ca śarmādhigacchati
|
tvāṃ caiva naraśārdūla gāndhārīṃ ca yaśasvinīm||56||

57sa śocannaraśārdūlaḥ śāntiṃ naivādhigacchati
|
hriyā ca parayā''viṣṭo bhavantaṃ nādhigacchati||57||

58putraśokābhisantaptaṃ buddhivyākulatendriyam
|
evamuktvā mahārāja dhṛtarāṣṭraṃ yadūttamaḥ||58||

59uvāca paramaṃ vākyaṃ gāndhārīṃ śokakarśitām
|
saubaleyi nibodha tvaṃ yattvāṃ vakṣyāmi tacchṛṇu||59||

60tvatsamānāsti loke'sminnadya sīmantinī śubhe
|
jānāsi ca yathā rājñi sabhāyāṃ mama sannidhau||60||

61dharmārthasahitaṃ vākyamubhayoḥ pakṣayorhitam
|
uktavatyasi kalyāṇi na ca te tanayaiḥ kṛtam||61||

62duryodhanastvayā cokto jayārthī paruṣaṃ vacaḥ
|
śṛṇu mūḍha vaco mahyaṃ yato dharmastato jayaḥ||62||

63tadidaṃ samanuprāptaṃ tava vākyaṃ nṛpātmaje
|
evaṃ viditvā kalyāṇi mā sma śoke manaḥ kṛthāḥ||63||

64pāṇḍavānāṃ vināśāya mā te buddhiḥ kadācana
|
śaktā cāsi mahābhāge pṛthivīṃ sacarācarām||64||

65cakṣuṣā krodhadīptena nirdagdhuṃ tapaso balāt
|
vāsudevavacaḥ śrutvā gāndhārī vākyamabravīt||65||

66evametanmahābāho yathā vadasi keśava
|
ādhibhirdahyamānāyā matiḥ sañcalitā mama||66||

67sā me vyavasthitā śrutvā tava vākyaṃ janārdana
|
rājñastvandhasya vṛddhasya hataputrasya keśava||67||

68tvaṃ gatiḥ sahitairvīraiḥ pāṇḍavairdvipadāṃ varaḥ
|
etāvaduktvā vacanaṃ mukhaṃ pracchādya vāsasā||68||

69putraśokābhisantaptā gāndhārī praruroda ha
|
tata enāṃ mahābāhuḥ keśavaḥ śokakarśitām||69||

70hetukāraṇasaṃyuktairvākyairāśvāsayatprabhuḥ
|
samāśvāsya ca gāndhārī dhṛtarāṣṭraṃ ca mādhavaḥ||70||

71drauṇisaṅkalpitaṃ bhāvamavabuddhyata keśavaḥ
|
tatastvarita utthāya pādau mūrghnā praṇamya ca||71||

72dvaipāyanasya rājendra tataḥ kauravamabravīt
|
āpṛcche tvāṃ kuruśreṣṭha mā ca śoke manaḥ kṛthāḥ||72||

73drauṇeḥ pāpo'styabhiprāyastenāsmi sahasotthitaḥ
|
pāṇḍavānāṃ vadhe rātrau buddhistena pradarśitā||73||

74etacchrutvā tu vacanaṃ gāndhāryā sahito'bravīt
|
dhṛtarāṣṭro mahābāhuḥ keśavaṃ keśisūdanam||74||

75śīghraṃ gaccha mahābāho pāṇḍavānparipālaya
|
bhūyastvayā sameṣyāmi kṣiprameva janārdana||75||

76prāyāttatastu tvarito dārukeṇa sahācyutaḥ
|
vāsudeve gate rājan dhṛtarāṣṭraṃ janeśvaram||76||

77āśvāsayadameyātmā vyāso lokanamaskṛtaḥ
|
vāsudevo'pi dharmātmā kṛtakṛtyo jagāma ha||77||

78śibiraṃ hāstinapurāddidṛkṣuḥ pāṇḍavānnṛpa
|
āgamya śibiraṃ so'bhyagacchata pāṇḍavān
|
tacca tebhyaḥ samākhyāya sahitastaiḥ samāhitaḥ||78||
