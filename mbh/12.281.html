Mahābhārata 12.281 (Śāntiparvan)

1yudhiṣṭhira uvāca|
aho dharmiṣṭhatā tāta vṛtrasyāmitatejasaḥ
|
yasya vijñānamatulaṃ viṣṇorbhaktiśca tādṛśī ||1||

2durvijñeyaṃ padaṃ tāta viṣṇoramitatejasaḥ
|
kathaṃ vā rājaśārdūla padaṃ tu jñātavānasau ||2||

3bhavatā kathitaṃ hyetacchraddadhe cāhamacyutam
|
bhūyastu me samutpannā buddhiravyaktadarśanāt ||3||

4kathaṃ vinihato vṛtraḥ śakreṇa puruṣarṣabha
|
dhārmiko viṣṇubhaktaśca tattvajñaśca padānvaye ||4||

5etanme saṃśayaṃ brūhi pṛcchate bharatarṣabha
|
vṛtrastu rājaśārdūla yathā śakreṇa nirjitaḥ ||5||

6yathā caivābhavadyuddhaṃ taccācakṣva pitāmaha
|
vistareṇa mahābāho paraṃ kautūhalaṃ hi me ||6||

7bhīṣma uvāca|
rathenendraḥ prayāto vai sārdhaṃ devagaṇaiḥ purā
|
dadarśāthāgrato vṛtraṃ dhiṣṭhitaṃ parvatopamam ||7||

8yojanānāṃ śatānyūrdhvaṃ pañcocchritamariṃdam
|
śatāni vistareṇātha trīṇyevābhyadhikāni vai ||8||

9tatprekṣya tādṛśaṃ rūpaṃ trailokyenāpi durjayam
|
vṛtrasya devāḥ saṃtrastā na śāntimupalebhire ||9||

10śakrasya tu tadā rājannūrustambho vyajāyata
|
bhayādvṛtrasya sahasā dṛṣṭvā tadrūpamuttamam ||10||

11tato nādaḥ samabhavadvāditrāṇāṃ ca niḥsvanaḥ
|
devāsurāṇāṃ sarveṣāṃ tasminyuddhe hyupasthite ||11||

12atha vṛtrasya kauravya dṛṣṭvā śakramavasthitam
|
na sambhramo na bhīḥ kācidāsthā vā samajāyata ||12||

13tataḥ samabhavadyuddhaṃ trailokyasya bhayaṃkaram
|
śakrasya ca surendrasya vṛtrasya ca mahātmanaḥ ||13||

14asibhiḥ paṭṭiśaiḥ śūlaiḥ śakti-tomara-mudgaraiḥ
|
śilābhirvividhābhiśca kārmukaiśca mahāsvanaiḥ ||14||

15śastraiśca vividhairdivyaiḥ pāvakolkābhireva ca
|
devāsuraistataḥ sainyaiḥ sarvamāsītsamākulam ||15||

16pitāmahapurogāśca sarve devagaṇāstathā
|
ṛṣayaśca mahābhāgāstadyuddhaṃ draṣṭumāgaman ||16||

17vimānāgryairmahārāja siddhāśca bharatarṣabha
|
gandharvāśca vimānāgryairapsarobhiḥ samāgaman ||17||

18tato'ntarikṣamāvṛtya vṛtro dharmabhṛtāṃ varaḥ
|
aśvamavarṣeṇa devendraṃ samākiradatidrutam ||18||

19tato devagaṇāḥ kruddhāḥ sarvataḥ śaravṛṣṭibhiḥ
|
aśmavarṣamapohanta vṛtrapreritamāhave ||19||

20vṛtrastu kuruśārdūla mahāmāyo mahābalaḥ
|
mohayāmāsa devendraṃ māyāyuddhena sarvaśaḥ ||20||

21tasya vṛtrārditasyātha moha āsīcchatakratoḥ
|
rathantareṇa taṃ tatra vasiṣṭhaḥ samabodhayat ||21||

22vasiṣṭha uvāca |
devaśreṣṭho'si devendra daityāsarunibarhaṇa
|
trailokyabalasaṃyuktaḥ kasmācchakra viṣīdasi ||22||

23eṣa brahmā ca viṣṇuśca śivaścaiva jagatpatiḥ
|
somaśca bhagavāndevaḥ sarve ca paramarṣayaḥ ||23||

24mākārṣīḥ kaśmalaṃ śakra kaścidevetaro yathā
|
āryāṃ yuddhe matiṃ kṛtvā jahi śatrūnsurādhipa ||24||

25eṣa lokagurustryakṣaḥ sarvalokanamaskṛtaḥ
|
nirīkṣate tvāṃ bhagavāṃstyaja mohaṃ surādhipa ||25||

26ete brahmarṣayaścaiva bṛhaspatipurogamāḥ
|
stavena śakra divyena stuvanti tvāṃ jayāya vai ||26||

27bhīṣma uvāca|
evaṃ sambodhyamānasya vasiṣṭhena mahātmanā
|
atīva vāsavasyāsīdbalamuttamatejasaḥ ||27||

28tato buddhimupāgamya bhagavānpākaśāsanaḥ
|
yogena mahatā yuktastāṃ māyāṃ vyapakarṣata ||28||

29tato'ṅgiraḥsutaḥ śrīmāṃste caiva sumaharṣayaḥ
|
dṛṣṭvā vṛtrasya vikrāntamupāgamya maheśvaram ||29||

30ūcurvṛtravināśārthaṃ lokānāṃ hitakāmyayā
|
tato bhagavatastejo jvaro bhūtvā jagatpateḥ ||30||

31samāviśattadā raudro vṛtraṃ lokapatiṃ tadā
|
viṣṇuśca bhagavāndevaḥ sarvalokābhipūjitaḥ ||31||

32aindraṃ samāviśadvajraṃ lokasaṃrakṣaṇe rataḥ
|
tato bṛhaspatirdhīmānupāgamya śatakratum
|
vasiṣṭhaśca mahātejāḥ sarve ca paramarṣayaḥ ||32||

33te samāsādya varadaṃ vāsavaṃ lokapūjitam
|
ūcurekāgramanaso jahi vṛtramiti prabho ||33||

34maheśvara uvāca |
eṣa vṛtro mahāśakra balena mahatā vṛtaḥ
|
viśvātmā sarvagaścaiva bahumāyaśca viśrutaḥ ||34||

35tadenamasuraśreṣṭhaṃ trailokyenāpi durjayam
|
jahi tvaṃ yogamāsthāya mā'vamaṃsthāḥ sureśvara ||35||

36anena hi tapastaptaṃ balārthamamarādhipa
|
ṣaṣṭiṃ varṣasahasrāṇi brahmā cāsmai varaṃ dadau ||36||

37mahattvaṃ yogināṃ caiva mahāmāyatvameva ca
|
mahābalattvaṃ ca tathā tejaścāgryaṃ sureśvara ||37||

38etattvāṃ māmakaṃ tejaḥ samāviśati vāsava
|
vyagramenaṃ tvamapyenaṃ vajreṇa jahi dānavam ||38||

39śakra uvāca |
bhagavaṃstvatprasādena ditijaṃ sudurāsadam
|
vajreṇa nihaniṣyāmi paśyataste surarṣabha ||39||

40bhīṣma uvāca|
āviśyamāne daitye tu jvareṇātha mahāsure
|
devatānāmṛṣīṇāṃ ca harṣānnādo mahānabhūt ||40||

41tato dundubhayaścaiva śaṅkhāśca sumahāsvanāḥ
|
murajā ḍiṇḍimāścaiva prāvādyanta sahasraśaḥ ||41||

42asurāṇāṃ tu sarveṣāṃ smṛtilopo mahānabhūt
|
māyānāśaśca balavānkṣaṇena samapadyata ||42||

43tathāviṣṭamatho jñātvā ṛṣayo devatāstathā
|
stuvantaḥ śakramīśānaṃ tathā prācodayannapi ||43||

44rathasthasya hi śakrasya yuddhakāle mahātmanaḥ
|
ṛṣibhiḥ stūyamānasya rūpamāsītsudurdṛśam ||44||
