Mahābhārata 7.43 (Droṇaparvan)

1sañjaya uvāca|
yanmāṃ pṛcchasi rājendra sindhurājasya vikramam
|
śṛṇu tat sarvamākhyāsye yathā pāṇḍūnayodhayat||1||

2tamūhurvājino vaśyā saindhavāḥ sādhuvāhinaḥ
|
vikurvāṇā bṛhanto'śvāḥ śvasanopamaraṃhasaḥ||2||

3gandharvanagarākāraṃ vidhivatkalpitaṃ ratham
|
tasyābhyaśobhayatketurvārāho rājato mahān||3||

4śvetacchatrapatākābhiścāmaravyajanena ca
|
sa babhau rājaliṅgaistaistārāpatirivāmbare||4||

5muktāvajramaṇisvarṇairbhūṣitaṃ tamayasmayam
|
varūthaṃ vibabhau tasya jyotirbhiḥ svamivāvṛtam||5||

6sa visphārya mahaccāpaṃ kiranniṣugaṇān bahūn
|
tat khaṇḍaṃ pūrayāmāsa yadvyadārayadārjuniḥ||6||

7sa sātyakiṃ tribhirbāṇairaṣṭabhiśca vṛkodaram
|
dhṛṣṭadyumnaṃ tathā ṣaṣṭyā virāṭaṃ daśabhiḥ śaraiḥ||7||

8drupadaṃ pañcabhistīkṣṇaiḥ saptabhiśca śikhaṇḍinam
|
kekayān pañcaviṃśatyā draupadeyāṃstribhistribhiḥ||8||

9yudhiṣṭhiraṃ tu saptatyā tataḥ śeṣānapānudat
|
iṣujālena mahatā tada‌dbhutamivābhavat||9||

10athāsya śitapītena bhallenādiśya kārmukam
|
ciccheda prahasan rājā dharmaputraḥ pratāpavān||10||

11akṣṇornimeṣamātreṇa so'nyadādāya kārmukam
|
vivyādha daśabhiḥ pārthaṃ tāṃścaivānyāṃstribhistribhiḥ||11||

12tat tasya lāghavaṃ jñātvā bhīmo bhallaustribhistribhiḥ
|
dhanurdhvajaṃ ca cchatraṃ ca kṣitau kṣipramapātayat||12||

13so'nyadādāya balavān sajjaṃ kṛtvā ca kārmukam
|
bhīmasyāpātayat ketuṃ dhanuraśvāṃśca māriṣa||13||

14sa hatāśvādavaplutya cchinnadhanvā rathottamāt
|
sātyakerāpluto yānaṃ giryagramiva kesarī||14||

15tatastvadīyāḥ saṃhṛṣṭāḥ sādhu sādhviti vādinaḥ
|
sindhurājasya tat karma prekṣyāśraddheyamadbhutam||15||

16saṃkruddhān pāṇḍavāneko yadda‌dhārāstratejasā
|
tattasya karma bhūtāni sarvāṇyevābhyapūjayan||16||

17saubhadreṇa hataiḥ pūrvaṃ sottarāyodhibhirdvipaiḥ
|
pāṇḍūnāṃ darśitaḥ panthāḥ saindhavena nivāritaḥ||17||

18yatamānāstu te vīrā mātsyapañcālakekayāḥ
|
pāṇḍavāścānvapadyanta pratiśekurna sandhavam||18||

19yo yo hi yatate bhettuṃ droṇānīkaṃ tavāhitaḥ
|
taṃ tameva varaṃ prāpya saindhavaḥ pratyavārayat||19||
