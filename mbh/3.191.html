Mahābhārata 3.191 (Āraṇyakaparvan)

1mārkaṇḍeya uvāca|
tataścorakṣayaṃ kṛtvā dvijebhyaḥ pṛthivīmimām
|
vājimedhe mahāyajñe vidhivat kalpayiṣyati||1||

2sthāpayitvā ca maryādāḥ svayambhuvihitāḥ śubhāḥ
|
vanaṃ puṇyayaśaḥkarmā ramaṇīyaṃ pravekṣyati||2||

3tacchīlamanuvartsyanti manuṣyā lokavāsinaḥ
|
vipraiścorakṣayaścaiva kṛte kṣemaṃ bhaviṣyati||3||

4kṛṣṇājināni śaktīśca triśūlānyāyudhāni ca
|
sthāpayan dvijaśārdulo deśeṣu vijiteṣu ca||4||

5saṃstūyamāno viprendrairmānayāno dvijottamān
|
kalkī cariṣyati mahīṃ sadā dasyuvadhe rataḥ||5||

6hā mātastāta putreti tāstā vācaḥ sudāruṇāḥ
|
vikrośamānān subhṛśaṃ dasyūnneṣyati saṅkṣayam||6||

7tato'dharmavināśo vai dharmavṛddhiśca bhārata
|
bhaviṣyati kṛte prāpte kriyāvāṃśca janastathā||7||

8ārāmāścaiva caityāśca taṭākāvasathāstathā
|
puṣkariṇyaśca vividhā devatāyatanāni ca||8||

9yajñakriyāśca vividhā bhaviṣyanti kṛte yuge
|
brāhmaṇāḥ sādhavaścaiva munayaśca tapasvinaḥ||9||

10āśramāḥ sahapāṣaṇḍāḥ sthitāḥ satyajanāḥ prajāḥ
|
prayanti sarvabījāni roṣyamāṇāni caiva ha||10||

11sarveṣvṛtuṣu rājendra sarvaṃ sasyaṃ bhaviṣyati
|
narā dāneṣu niratā vrateṣu niyameṣu ca||11||

12japyayajñaparā viprā dharmakāmā mudā yutāḥ
|
pālayiṣyanti rājāno dharmeṇemāṃ vasundharām||12||

13vyavahāraratā vaiśyā bhaviṣyanti kṛte yuge
|
ṣaṭkarmaniratā viprāḥ kṣatriyā vikrame ratāḥ||13||

14śuśrūṣāyāṃ ratāḥ śūdrāstathā varṇatrayasya ca
|
eṣa dharmaḥ kṛtayuge tretāyāṃ dvāpare tathā||14||

15paścime yugakāle ca yaḥ sa te samprakīrtitaḥ
|
sarvalokasya viditā yugasaṅkhyā ca pāṇḍava||15||

16etat te sarvamākhyātamatītānāgataṃ tathā
|
vāyuproktamanusmṛtya purāṇamṛṣisaṃstutam||16||

17evaṃ saṃsāramārgā me bahuśaścirajīvinā
|
dṛṣṭāścaivānubhūtāśca tāṃste kathitavānaham||17||

18idaṃ caivāparaṃ bhūyaḥ saha bhrātṛbhiracyuta
|
dharmasaṃśayamokṣārthaṃ nibodha vacanaṃ mama||18||

19dharme tvayā''tmā saṃyojyo nityaṃ dharmabhṛtāṃ vara
|
dharmātmā hi sukhaṃ rājan pretya ceha ca nandati||19||

20nibodha ca śubhāṃ vāṇīṃ yāṃ pravakṣyāmi te'nagha
|
na brāhmaṇe paribhavaḥ kartavyaste kadācana||20||

21brāhmaṇaḥ kupito hanyādapi lokān pratijñayā
|
vaiśampāyana uvāca|
mārkaṇḍeyavacaḥ śrutvā kurūṇāṃ pravaro nṛpaḥ||21||

22uvāca vacanaṃ dhīmān paramaṃ paramadyutiḥ
|
kasmin dharme mayā stheyaṃ prajāḥ saṃrakṣatā mune||22||

23kathaṃ ca vartamāno vai na cyaveyaṃ svadharmataḥ
|
mārkaṇḍeya uvāca|
dayāvān sarvabhūteṣu hito rakto'nasūyakaḥ||23||

24satyavādī mṛdurdāntaḥ prajānāṃ rakṣaṇe rataḥ
|
cara dharmaṃ tyajādharmaṃ pitṝn devāṃśca pūjaya||24||

25pramādādyat kṛtaṃ te'bhūt samyagdānena tajjaya
|
alaṃ te mānamāśritya satataṃ paravān bhava||25||

26vijitya pṛthivīṃ sarvāṃ modamānaḥ sukhī bhava
|
eṣa bhūto bhaviṣyaśca dharmaste samudīritaḥ||26||

27na te'styaviditaṃ kiñcidatītānāgataṃ yudhi
|
tasmādimaṃ parikleśaṃ tvaṃ tāta hṛdi mā kṛthāḥ||27||

28prājñāstāta na muhyanti kālenāpi prapīḍitāḥ
|
eṣa kālo mahābāho api sarvadivaukasām||28||

29muhyanti hi prajāstāta kālenāpi pracoditāḥ
|
mā ca tatra viśaṅkā'bhūdyanmayoktaṃ tavānagha||29||

30aśaṅkyaṃ madvaco hyetaddharmalopo bhavet tava
|
jāto'si prathite vaṃśe kurūṇāṃ bharatarṣabha||30||

31karmaṇā manasā vācā sarvametat samācara
|
yudhiṣṭhira uvāca|
yat tvayoktaṃ dvijaśreṣṭha vākyaṃ śrutimanoharam||31||

32tathā kariṣye yatnena bhavataḥ śāsanaṃ vibho
|
na me lobho'sti viprendra na bhayaṃ na ca matsaraḥ||32||

33kariṣyāmi hi tat sarvamuktaṃ yat te mayi prabho
|
vaiśampāyana uvāca|
śrutvā tu vacanaṃ tasya mārkaṇḍeyasya dhīmataḥ||33||

34saṃhṛṣṭāḥ pāṇḍavā rājan sahitāḥ śārṅgadhanvanā
|
viprarṣabhāśca te sarve ye tatrāsan samāgatāḥ||34||

35tathā kathāṃ śubhāṃ śrutvā mārkaṇḍeyasya dhīmataḥ
|
vismitāḥ samapadyanta purāṇasya nivedanāt||35||
