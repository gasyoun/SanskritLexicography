Mahābhārata 3.81 (Āraṇyakaparvan)

1vaiśaṃpāyana uvāca|
dhanaṃjayotsukānāṃ tu bhrātṝṇāṃ kṛṣṇayā saha
|
śrutvā vākyāni vimanā dharmarājopyajāyata||1||

2athāpaśyanmahātmānaṃ devarṣiṃ tatra nāradam
|
dīpyamānaṃ śriyā brāhmyā hutārciṣamivānalam||2||

3tamāgatamabhiprekṣya bhrātṛbhiḥ saha dharmarāṭ
|
pratyutthāya yathānyāyaṃ pūjāṃ cakre mahātmane||3||

4sa taiḥ parivṛtaḥ śrīmānbhrātṛbhiḥ kurusattamaḥ
|
vibabhāvatidīptaujā devairiva śatakratuḥ||4||

5yathā ca vedānsāvitrī yājñasenī tathā patīn
|
na jahau dharmataḥ pārthānmerumarkaprabhā yathā||5||

6pratigṛhya ca tāṃ pūjāṃ nārado bhagavānṛṣiḥ
|
āśvāsayaddharmasutaṃ yuktarūpamivānagha||6||

7uvāca ca mahātmānaṃ dharmarājaṃ yudhiṣṭhiram
|
brūhi dharmabhṛtāṃ śreṣṭha kenārthaḥ kiṃ dadāni te||7||

8atha dharmasuto rājā praṇamya bhrātṛbhiḥ saha
|
uvāca prāñjalirbhūtvā nāradaṃ devasaṃmitam||8||

9tvayi tuṣṭe mahābhāga sarvalokābhipūjite
|
kṛtamityeva manyehaṃ prasādāttava suvrata||9||

10yadi tvahamanugrāhyo bhrātṛbhiḥ sahito'nagha
|
saṃdehaṃ me muniśreṣṭha tattvataśchettumarhasi||10||

11pradakṣiṇāṃ yaḥ kurute pṛthivīṃ tīrthatatparaḥ
|
kiṃ phalaṃ tasya kārtsnyena tadbhavānvaktumarhati||11||

12nārada uvāca|
śṛṇu rājannavahito yathā bhīṣmeṇa dhīmatā
|
pulastyasya sakāśādvai sarvametadupaśrutam||12||

13purā bhāgīrathītīre bhīṣmo dharmabhṛtāṃ varaḥ
|
pitryaṃ vrataṃ samāsthāya nyavasanmunibhiḥ saha||13||

14śubhe deśe tathā rājanpuṇye devarṣisevite
|
gaṅgādvāre mahābhāga devagandharvasevite||14||

15sa pitṝṃstarpayāmāsa devāṃśca paramadyutiḥ
|
ṛṣīṃśca tarpayāmāsa vidhidṛṣṭena karmaṇā||15||

16kasyacittvatha kālasya japanneva mahāyaśāḥ
|
dadarśādbhutasaṃkāśaṃ pulastyamṛṣisattamam||16||

17sa taṃ dṛṣṭvogratapasaṃ dīpyamānamiva śriyā
|
praharṣamatulaṃ lebhe vismayaṃ paramaṃ yayau||17||

18upasthitaṃ mahābhāgaṃ pūjayāmāsa bhārata
|
bhīṣmo dharmabhṛtāṃ śreṣṭho vidhidṛṣṭena karmaṇā||18||

19śirasā cārghamādāya śuciḥ prayatamānasaḥ
|
nāma saṃkīrtayāmāsa tasminbrahmarṣisattame||19||

20bhīṣmohamasmi bhadraṃ te dāso'smi tava suvrata
|
tava saṃdarśanādeva mukto'haṃ sarvakilvaṣaiḥ||20||

21evamuktvā mahārāja bhīṣmo dharmabhṛtāṃ varaḥ
|
vāgyataḥ prāñjalirbhūtvā tūṣṇīmāsīdyudhiṣṭhiraḥ||21||

22taṃ dṛṣṭvā niyamenātha svādhyāyāmnāyakarśitam
|
bhīṣmaṃ kurukulaśreṣṭhaṃ muniḥ prītamanābhavat||22||
