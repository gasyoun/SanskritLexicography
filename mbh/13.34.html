Mahābhārata 13.34 (Anuśāsanaparvan)

1bhīṣma uvāca|
brāhmaṇāneva satataṃ bhṛśaṃ samparipūjayet
|
ete hi somarājāna īśvarāḥ sukhaduḥkhayoḥ ||1||

2ete bhaugairalaṅkārairanyaiścaiva kimicchakaiḥ
|
sadā pūjyā namaskārai rakṣyāśca pitṛvannṛpaiḥ ||2||

3tato rāṣṭrasya śāntirhi bhūtānāmiva vāsavāt
|
jāyatāṃ brahmavarcasvī rāṣṭra vai brāhmaṇaḥ śuciḥ ||3||

4mahārathaśca rājanya eṣṭavyaḥ śatrutāpanaḥ
|
brāhmaṇaṃ jātisampannaṃ dharmajñaṃ saṃśitavratam ||4||

5vāsayeta gṛhe rājanna tasmātparamasti vai
|
brāhmaṇebhyo havirdattaṃ pratigṛṇhanti devatāḥ ||5||

6pitaraḥ sarvabhūtānāṃ naitebhyo vidyate param
|
ādityaścandramā vāyurāpo bhūrambaraṃ diśaḥ ||6||

7sarve brāhmaṇamāviśya sadā'nnamupabhuñjate
|
na tasyāśnanti pitaro yasya viprā na bhuñjate ||7||

8devāścāpyasya nāśnanti pāpasya brāhmaṇadviṣaḥ
|
brāhmaṇeṣu tu tuṣṭeṣu prīyante pitaraḥ sadā ||8||

9tathaiva devatā rājannātra kāryā vicāraṇā
|
tathaiva te'pi prīyante yeṣāṃ bhavati taddhaviḥ ||9||

10na ca pretya vinaśyanti gacchanti ca parāṃ gatim
|
yena yenaiva haviṣā brāhmaṇāṃstarpayennaraḥ ||10||

11tena tenaiva prīyante pitaro devatāstathā
|
brāhmaṇādeva tadbhūtaṃ prabhavanti yataḥ prajāḥ ||11||

12yataścāyaṃ prabhavati pretya yatra ca gacchati
|
vedaiṣa mārgaṃ svargasya tathaiva narakasya ca ||12||

13agatānāgate cobhe brāhmaṇo dvipadāṃ varaḥ
|
brāhmaṇo bharataśreṣṭha svadharmaṃ caiva veda yaḥ ||13||

14ye cainamanuvartante te na yānti parābhavam
|
na te pretya vinaśyanti gacchanti na parābhavam ||14||

15yadbrāhmaṇamukhātprāptaṃ pratigṛhṇanti vai vacaḥ
|
bhūtātmāno mahātmānaste na yānti parābhavam ||15||

16kṣatriyāṇāṃ pratapatāṃ tejasā ca balena ca
|
brāhmaṇeṣveva śāmyanti tejāṃsi ca balāni ca ||16||

17bhṛgavastālajaṃghāṃśca nīpānāṅgiraso'jayan
|
bharadvājo vaihatavyānailāṃśca bharatarṣabha ||17||

18citrāyudhāṃścāpyajayannete kṛṣṇājinadhvajāḥ
|
prakṣipyātha ca kuṃbhānvai pāragāminamārabhet ||18||

19yatkiṃcitkathyate loke śrūyate paṭhyate'pi vā
|
sarvaṃ tadbrāhāṇeṣveva gūḍho'gniriva dāruṣu ||19||

20atrāpyudāharantīmamitihāsaṃ purātanam
|
saṃvādaṃ vāsudevasya pṛthvyāśca bharatarṣabha ||20||

21vāsudeva uvāca|
mātaraṃ sarvabhūtānāṃ pṛcche tvāṃ saṃśayaṃ śubhe
|
kenasvitkarmaṇā pāpaṃ vyapohati naro gṛhī ||21||

22pṛthivyuvāca
|
brāhmaṇāneva seveta pavitraṃ hyetaduttamam
|
brāhmaṇānsevamānasya rajaḥ sarvaṃ praṇaśyati
|
ato bhūtirataḥ kīrtirato buddhiḥ prajāyate ||22||

23mahārathaśca rājanya eṣṭavyaḥ śatrutāpanaḥ
|
iti māṃ nāradaḥ prāha satataṃ sarvabhūtaye ||23||

24brāhmaṇaṃ jātisampannaṃ dharmajñaṃ saṃśitaṃ śucim
|
apareṣāṃ pareṣāṃ ca parebhyaścaiva ye'pare ||24||

25brāhmaṇā yaṃ praśaṃsanti sa manuṣyaḥ pravardhate
|
atha yo brāhmaṇān kruṣṭaḥ parābhavati so'cirāt ||25||

26yathā mahārṇave kṣiptā sītā neṣṭurvinaśyati
|
tathā duścaritaṃ sarvaṃ parābhāvāya kalpate ||26||

27paśya candre kṛtaṃ lakṣma samudro lavaṇodakaḥ
|
tathā bhagasahasreṇa mahendraḥ paricihnitaḥ ||27||

28teṣāmeva prabhāvena sahasranayano hyasau
|
śatakratuḥ samabhavatpaśya mādhava yādṛśam ||28||

29icchan kīrtiṃ ca bhūtiṃ ca lokāṃśca madhusūdana
|
brāhmaṇānumate tiṣṭhetpuruṣaḥ śucirātmavān ||29||

30bhīṣma uvāca|
ityetadvacanaṃ śrutvā medinyā madhusūdanaḥ
|
sādhu sādhviti kauravya medinī pratyapūjayat ||30||

31etāṃ śrutvopamāṃ pārtha prayato brāhmaṇarṣabhān
|
satataṃ pūjayethāstvaṃ tataḥ śreyo'bhipatsyase ||31||
