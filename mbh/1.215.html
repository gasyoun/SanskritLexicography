Mahābhārata 1.215 (Ādiparvan)

1vaiśaṃpāyana uvāca|
kathayitvā ca tatsarvaṃ brāhmaṇebhyaḥ sa bhārata
|
prayayau himavatpārśvaṃ tato vajradharātmajaḥ||1||

2agastyavaṭamāsādya vasiṣṭhasya ca parvatam
|
bhṛgutuṅge ca kaunteyaḥ kṛtavāñśaucamātmanaḥ||2||

3pradadau gosahasrāṇi subahūni ca bhārata
|
niveśāṃśca dvijātibhyaḥ so'dadatkurusattamaḥ||3||

4hiraṇyabindostīrthe ca snātvā puruṣasattamaḥ
|
dṛṣṭavānpāṇḍavaśreṣṭhaḥ puṇyānyāyatanāni ca||4||

5avatīrya naraśreṣṭho brāhmaṇaiḥ saha bhārata
|
prācīṃ diśamabhiprepsurjagāma bharatarṣabhaḥ||5||

6ānupūrvyeṇa tīrthāni dṛṣṭavānkurusattamaḥ
|
nadīṃ cotpalinīṃ ramyāmaraṇyaṃ naimiṣaṃ prati||6||

7nandāmaparanandāṃ ca kauśikīṃ ca yaśasvinīm
|
mahānadīṃ gayāṃ caiva gaṅgāmapi ca bhārata||7||

8evaṃ tīrthāni sarvāṇi paśyamānastathāśramān
|
ātmanaḥ pāvanaṃ kurvanbrāhmaṇebhyo dadau ca gāḥ||8||

9aṅgavaṅgakaliṅgeṣu yāni tīrthāni kānicit
|
jagāma tāni sarvāṇi puṇyānyāyatanāni ca||9||

10dṛṣṭvā ca vidhivattāni dhanaṃ cāpi dadau tataḥ
|
kaliṅgarāṣṭradvāreṣu brāhmaṇāḥ pāṇḍavānugāḥ
|
abhyanujñāya kaunteyamupāvartanta bhārata||10||

11sa tu tairabhyanujñātaḥ kuntīputro dhanañjayaḥ
|
sahāyairalpakaiḥ śūraḥ prayayau yatra sāgaraḥ||11||

12sa kaliṅgānatikramya deśānāyatanāni ca
|
harmyāṇi ramaṇīyāni prekṣaṇāṇo yayau prabhuḥ||12||

13mahendraparvataṃ dṛṣṭvā tāpasairupaśobhitam
|
samudratīreṇa śanairmaṇipūraṃ jagāma ha||13||

14tatra sarvāṇi tīrthāni puṇyānyāyatanāni ca
|
abhigamya mahābāhurabhyagacchanmahīpatim||14||

15maṇapūreśvaraṃ rājandharmajñaṃ citravāhanam
|
tasya citrāṅgadā nāma duhitā cārudarśanā||15||

16tāṃ dadarśa pure tasminvicarantīṃ yadṛcchayā
|
dṛṣṭvā ca tāṃ varārohāṃ cakame caitravāhanīm||16||

17abhigamya ca rājānamavadatsvaṃ prayojanam
|
dehi me khalvimāṃ rājankṣatriyāya mahātmane||17||

18tacchrutvā tvabravīdrājā kasya putro'si nāma kim
|
uvāca taṃ pāṇḍavohaṃ kuntīputro dhanañjayaḥ||18||

19tamuvācātha rājā sa sāntvapūrvamidaṃ vacaḥ
|
rājā prabhañjano nāma kule'sminsaṃbabhūva ha||19||

20aputraḥ prasavenārthī tapastepe sa uttamam
|
ugreṇa tapasā tena devadevaḥ pinākadhṛk||20||

21īśvarastoṣitaḥ pārtha devadevaḥ umāpatiḥ
|
sa tasmai bhaghavānprādādekaikaṃ prasavaṃ kule||21||

22ekaikaḥ prasavastasmādbhavatyasminkule sadā
|
teṣāṃ kumārāḥ sarveṣāṃ pūrveṣāṃ mama jajñire||22||

23ekā ca mama kanyeyaṃ kulasyotpādinī bhṛśam
|
putro mamāyamiti me bhāvanā puruṣarṣabha||23||

24putrikā hetuvidhinā saṃjñitā bharatarṣabha
|
tasmādekaḥ suto yo'syāṃ jāyate bhārata tvayā||24||

25etacchulkaṃ bhavatvasyāḥ kulakṛjjāyatāmiha
|
etena samayenemāṃ pratigṛhṇīṣva pāṇḍava||25||

26sa tatheti pratijñāya tāṃ kanyāṃ pratigṛhya ca
|
uvāsa nagare tasmiṃstisraḥ kuntīsutaḥ samāḥ||26||

27tasyāṃ sute samutpanne pariṣvajya varāṅganām
|
āmantrya nṛpatiṃ taṃ tu jagāma parivartitum||27||
