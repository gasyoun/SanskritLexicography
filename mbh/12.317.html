Mahābhārata 12.317 (Śāntiparvan)

1yājñavalkya uvāca |
tathaivotkramamāṇaṃ tu śṛṇuṣvāvahito nṛpa
|
padbhyāmutkramamāṇasya vaiṣṇavaṃ sthānamucyate||1||

2jaṅghābhyāṃ tu vasūndevānāpnuyāditi na śrutam
|
jānubhyāṃ ca mahābhāgān sādhyāndevānavāpnuyāt||2||

3pāyunotkramamāṇastu maitraṃ sthānamavāpnuyāt
|
pṛthivīṃ jadhanenātha urubhyāṃ ca prajāpatim||3||

4pārśvābhyāṃ maruto devānnābhyāmindratvameva ca
|
bāhubhyāmindramevāhururasā rudrameva ca||4||

5grīvayā tu muniśreṣṭhaṃ naramāpnotyanuttamam
|
viśvedevānmukhenātha diśaḥ śrotreṇa cāpnuyāt||5||

6ghrāṇena gandhavahanaṃ netrābhyāmagnimeva ca
|
bhūbhyāṃ caivāśvinau devau lalāṭena pitṝnatha||6||

7brahmāṇamāpnoti vibhuṃ mūrdhnā devāgrajaṃ tathā
|
etānyutkramaṇasthānānyuktāni mithileśvara||7||

8ariṣṭāni pravakṣyāmi vihitāni manīṣibhiḥ
|
saṃvatsaraviyogasya saṃbhavanti śarīriṇaḥ||8||

9yo'rundhatīṃ na paśyeta dṛṣṭapūrvāṃ kadācana
|
tathaiva dhruvamityāhuḥ pūrṇenduṃ dīpameva ca||9||

10khaṇḍābhāsaṃ dakṣiṇataste'pi saṃvatsarāyuṣaḥ
|
paracakṣuṣi cātmānaṃ ye na paśyanti pārthiva||10||

11ātmacchāyākṛtībhūtaṃ te'pi saṃvatsarāyuṣaḥ
|
atidyutiratiprajñā aprajñā cādyutistathā||11||

12prakṛtervikriyāpattiḥ ṣaṇmāsānmṛtyulakṣaṇam
|
daivatānyavajānāti brāhmaṇaiśca viruddhyate||12||

13kṛṣṇaśyāvacchavicchāyaḥ ṣaṇmāsānmṛtyulakṣaṇam
|
ūrṇanābheryathā cakraṃ chidraṃ somaṃ prapaśyati||13||

14tathaiva ca sahasrāṃśuṃ saptarātreṇa mṛtyubhāk
|
śavagandhamupāghrāti surabhiṃ prāpya yo naraḥ||14||

15devatāyatanasthastu saptarātreṇa mṛtyubhāk
|
karṇanāsāvanamanaṃ dantadṛṣṭivirāgitā||15||

16saṃjñālopo nirūṣmatvaṃ sadyomṛtyunidarśanam
|
akasmācca sravedyasya vāmamakṣi narādhipa||16||

17mūrdhataścotpateddhūmaḥ sadyo mṛtyunidarśanam
|
etāvanti tvariṣṭāni viditvā mānavo''tmavān||17||

18niśi cāhani cātmānaṃ yojayetparamātmani
|
pratīkṣamāṇastatkālaṃ yatkālaṃ pretatā bhavet||18||

19athāsya neṣṭaṃ maraṇaṃ sthātumicchedimāṃ kriyām
|
sarvagandhān rasāṃścaiva dhārayīta narādhipa||19||

20sasāṃkhyadhāraṇaṃ caiva viditātmā nararṣabha
|
jayecca mṛtyuṃ yogena tatpareṇāntarātmanā||20||

21gacchetprāpyākṣayaṃ kṛtsnamajanma śivamavyayam
|
śāśvataṃ sthānamacalaṃ duṣprāpamakṛtātmabhiḥ||21||
