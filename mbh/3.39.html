Mahābhārata 3.39 (Āraṇyakaparvan)

1vaiśaṃpāyana uvāca|
gateṣu teṣu sarveṣu tapasviṣu mahātmasu
|
pinākapāṇirbhagavānsarvapāpaharo haraḥ||1||

2kairātaṃ veṣamāsthāya kāñcanadrumasannibham
|
vimrājamāno vipulo girirmarurivāparaḥ||2||

3śrīmaddhanurupādāya śarāṃścāśīviṣopamān
|
niṣpapāta mahāvego dahano dehavāniva||3||

4devyā sahomayā śrīmānsamānavrataveṣayā
|
nānāveṣadharairhṛṣṭairbhūteranugatastadā||4||

5kirātaveṣasañcchannaḥ strībhiścāpi sahasraśaḥ
|
aśobhata tadā rājansa deśo'tīva bhārata||5||

6kṣaṇena tadvanaṃ sarvaṃ niḥśabdamabhavattadā
|
nādaḥ prastravaṇānāṃ ca pakṣiṇāṃ cāpyupāramat||6||

7sa sannikarṣamāgamya pārthasyākliṣṭakarmaṇaḥ
|
mūkaṃ nāma danoḥ putraṃ dadarśādbhutadarśanam||7||

8vārāhaṃ rūpamāsthāya tarkayantamivārjunam
|
hantuṃ paraṃ dīpyamānaṃ tamuvācātha phālgunaḥ||8||

9gāṇḍīvaṃ dhanurādāya śarāṃścāśīviṣopamān
|
sajyaṃ dhanurvaraṃ kṛtvā jyāghoṣeṇa ninādayan||9||

10yanmāṃ prārthayase hantumanāgasamihāgatam
|
tasmāttvāṃ pūrvamevāhaṃ netā'dya yamasādanam||10||

11dṛṣṭvā taṃ prahariṣyantaṃ phālgunaṃ hṛḍhadhanvinam
|
kirātarūpī sahasā vārayāmāsa śaṅkaraḥ||11||

12mayaiṣa prārthitaḥ pūrvamindrakīlasamaprabhaḥ
|
anādṛtya ca tadvākyaṃ prajahārātha phālgunaḥ||12||

13kirātaśca samaṃ tasminnekalakṣye mahādyutiḥ
|
pramumocāśaniprakhyaṃ śaramagniśikhopamam||13||

14tau muktau sāyakau tābhyāṃ samaṃ tatra nipetatuḥ
|
mūkasya gātre vistīrṇe śailasaṃhanane tadā||14||

15yathāśanervinirghoṣo vajrasyeva ca parvate
|
tathā tayoḥ sannipātaḥ śarayorabhavattadā||15||

16sa viddho bahubhirbāṇairdīptāsyaiḥ pannagairiva
|
mamāra rākṣasaṃ rūpaṃ bhūyaḥ kṛtvā bibhīṣaṇam||16||

17sa dadarśa tato jiṣṇuḥ puruṣaṃ kāñcanaprabham
|
kirātaveśasañcchannaṃ strīsahāyamamitrahā||17||

18tamabravītprītamanāḥ kaunteyaḥ prahasanniva
|
ko bhavānaṭate śūnye vane strīgaṇasaṃvṛtaḥ||18||

19na tvamasminvane ghore bibheṣi kanakaprabha
|
kimarthaṃ ca tvayā viddho varāho matparigrahaḥ||19||

20mayābhipannaḥ pūrvaṃ hi rākṣaso'yamihāgataḥ
|
kāmātparibhavādvāpi na me jīvanvimokṣyase||20||

21na hyeṣa mṛgayādharmo yastvayādya kṛto mayi
|
tena tvāṃ bhraṃśayiṣyāmi jīvitātparvatāśrayam||21||

22ityuktaḥ pāṇḍaveyena kirātaḥ prahasanniva
|
uvāca ślakṣṇayā vācā pāṇḍavaṃ savyasācinam||22||

23na matkṛte tvayā vīra bhīḥ kāryā vanamantikāt
|
iyaṃ bhūmiḥ sadāsmākamucitā vasatāṃ vane||23||

24tvayā tu duṣkaraḥ kasmādiha vāsaḥ prarocitaḥ
|
vayaṃ tu bahusattve'sminnivasāmastapodhana||24||

25bhavāṃstu kṛṣṇavartmābhaḥ sukumāraḥ sukhocitaḥ
|
kathaṃ śūnyamimaṃ deśamekākī vicariṣyati||25||

26arjuna uvāca|
gāṇḍīvamāśrayaṃ kṛtvā nārācāṃścāgnisannibhān
|
nivasāmi mahāraṇye dvitīya iva pāvakiḥ||26||

27eṣa cāpi mayā janturmṛgarūpaṃ samāśritaḥ
|
rākṣaso nihato ghoro hantuṃ māmiha cāgataḥ||27||

28kirāta uvāca|
mayaiṣa dhanvanirmuktaistāḍitaḥ pūrvameva hi
|
bāṇairabhihataḥ śete nītaśca yamasādanam||28||

29mamaiṣa lakṣyabhūto hi mama pūrvaparigrahaḥ
|
mamaiva ca prahāreṇa jīvitādvyaparopitaḥ||29||

30doṣānsvānnārhase'nyasmai vaktuṃ svabaladarpitaḥ
|
avalipto'si mandātmanna me jīvanvimokṣase||30||

31sthiro bhavasva mokṣyāmi sāyakānaśīniva
|
ghaṭasva parayā śaktyā muñca tvamapi sāyakān||31||

32tasya tadvacanaṃ śrutvā kirātasyārjunastadā
|
roṣamāhārayāmāsa tāḍayāmāsa ceṣubhiḥ||32||

33tato hṛṣṭena manasā pratijagrāha sāyakān
|
bhūyo bhūya iti prāha mandamandetyuvāca ha||33||

34praharasva śarānetānnārācānmarmabhedinaḥ
|
ityukto bāṇavarṣaṃ sa mumoca sahasārjunaḥ||34||

35tatastau tatra saṃrabdhau rājamānau muhurmuhuḥ
|
śarairāśīviṣākāraistatakṣāte parasparam||35||

36tato'rjunaḥ śaravarṣaṃ kirāte samavāsṛjat
|
tatprasannena manasā pratijagrāha śaṅkaraḥ||36||

37muhūrtaṃ śaravarṣaṃ tatpratigṛhya pinākadhṛk
|
akṣatena śarīreṇa tasthau giririvācalaḥ||37||

38sa dṛṣṭvā bāṇavarṣaṃ tu moghībhūtaṃ dhanañjayaḥ
|
paramaṃ vismayaṃ cakre sādhu sādhviti cābravīt||38||

39aho'yaṃ sukumārāṅgo himavacchikharāśrayaḥ
|
gāṇḍīvamuktānnārācānpratigṛhṇātyavihvalaḥ||39||

40ko'yaṃ devo bhavetsākṣādrudro yakṣaḥ suro'suraḥ
|
vidyate hi giriśreṣṭhe tridaśānāṃ samāgamaḥ||40||

41na hi madbāṇajālānāmutsṛṣṭānāṃ sahasraśaḥ
|
śakto'nyaḥ sahituṃ vegamṛte devaṃ pinākinam||41||

42devo vā yadi vā yakṣo rudrādanyo vyavasthitaḥ
|
ahamenaṃ śaraistīkṣṇairnayāmi yamasādanam||42||

43tato hṛṣṭamanā jiṣṇurnārācānmarmabhedinaḥ
|
vyasṛjacchatadhā rājanmayūkhāniva bhāskaraḥ||43||

44tānprasannena manasā bhagavā~llokabhāvanaḥ
|
śūlapāṇiḥ pratyagṛhṇācchilāvarṣamivācalaḥ||44||

45kṣaṇena kṣīṇabāṇo'tha saṃvṛttaḥ phālgunastadā
|
bhīścainabhāviśattīvrā taṃ dṛṣṭvā śarasaṃkṣayam||45||

46cintayāmāsa jiṣṇustu bhagavantaṃ hutāśanam
|
purastādakṣayau dattau tūṇau yenāsya khāṇḍave||46||

47kiṃ nu mokṣyāmi dhanuṣā yanme bāṇāḥ kṣayaṃ gatāḥ
|
ayaṃ ca puruṣaḥ ko'pi bāṇāngrasati sarvaśaḥ||47||

48hatvā cainaṃ dhanuṣkoṭyā śūlāgreṇeva kuñjaram
|
nayāmi daṇḍadhārasya yamasya sadanaṃ prati||48||

49pragṛhyātha dhanuṣkoṭyā jyāpāśenāvakṛṣya ca
|
muṣṭibhiścāpi hatavānvajrakalpairmahādyutiḥ||49||

50samprayuddho dhanuṣkoṭyā kaunteyaḥ paravīrahā
|
tadapyasya dhanurdivyaṃ jagrāha girigocaraḥ||50||

51tato'rjuno grastadhanuḥ khaḍgapāṇiratiṣṭhata
|
yuddhasyāntamabhīpsanvai vegenābhijagāma tam||51||

52tasya mūrdhni śitaṃ khaḍgamasaktaṃ parvateṣvapi
|
mumoca bhujavīryeṇa vikramya kurunandanaḥ||52||

53tasya mūrdhānamāsādya paphālāsivaro hi saḥ
|
tato vṛkṣaiḥ śilābhiśca yodhayāmāsa phālgunaḥ||53||

54tadā vṛkṣānmahākāyaḥ pratyagṛhṇā‌datho śilāḥ
|
kirātarūpī bhagavāṃstataḥ pārthoṃ mahābalaḥ||54||

55muṣṭibhirvajrasaṅkāśairdhūmamutpādayanmukhe
|
prajahāra durādharṣe kirātasamarūpiṇi||55||

56tataḥ śakrāśanisamairmuṣṭibhirbhṛśadāruṇaiḥ
|
kirātarūpī bhagavānardayāmāsa phālgunam||56||

57tataścaṭacaṭāśabdaḥ sughoraḥ samapadyata
|
pāṇḍavasya ca muṣṭīnāṃ kirātasya ca yudhyataḥ||57||

58sumuhūrtaṃ tu tadyuddhamabhavallomaharṣaṇam
|
bhujaprahārasaṃyuktaṃ vṛtravāsavayoriva||58||

59jaghānātha tato jiṣṇuḥ kirātamurasā balī
|
pāṇḍavaṃ ca viceṣṭaṃ taṃ kirāto'pyahanadbalī||59||

60tayorbhujaviniṣpeṣātsaṅgharṣeṇorasostathā
|
samajāyata gātreṣu pāvako'ṅgāradhūmavān||60||

61tata enaṃ mahādevaḥ pīḍya gātraiḥ supīḍitam
|
tejasā vyakramadroṣāccetastasya vimohayan||61||

62tato'bhipīḍitairgātraiḥ piṇḍīkṛta ivābabhau
|
phālguno gātrasaṃruddho devadevena bhārata||62||

63nirucchvāso'bhavaccaiva sanniruddho mahātmanā
|
papāta bhūmyāṃ niśceṣṭo gatasattva ivābhavata||63||

64sa muhūrtaṃ tathā bhūtvā sacetāḥ punarutthitaḥ
|
rudhireṇāśrutāṅgastu pāṇḍavo bhṛśaduḥkhitaḥ||64||

65śaraṇyaṃ śaraṇaṃ gatvā bhagavantaṃ pinākinam
|
mṛnmayaṃ sthāṇḍilaṃ kṛtvā mālyonāpūjayadbhavam||65||

66tañca mālyaṃ tadā pārthaḥ kirātaśirasi sthitam
|
apaśyatpāṇḍavaśreṣṭho harṣeṇa prakṛtiṃ gataḥ||66||

67papāta pādayostasya tataḥ prītobhavadbhavaḥ
|
uvāca cainaṃ vacasā meghagambhīragīrharaḥ
|
jātavismayamālokya tataḥ kṣīṇāṅgasaṃhitam||67||

68bhava uvāca|
bho bho phālguna tuṣṭo'smi karmaṇā'pratimena te
|
śauryeṇānena dhṛtyā ca kṣatriyo nāsti te samaḥ||68||

69samaṃ tejaśca vīryaṃ ca mamādya tava cānagha
|
prītaste'haṃ mahābāho paśya māṃ bharatarṣabha||69||

70dadāmi te viśālākṣa cakṣuḥ pūrvaṛṣirbhavān
|
vijeṣyasi raṇe śatrūnapi sarvāndivaukasaḥ||70||

71prītyā ca te'haṃ dāsyāmi yadastramanivāritam
|
tvaṃ hi śakto madīyaṃ tadastraṃ dhārayituṃ kṣaṇāt||71||

72vaiśaṃpāyana uvāca|
tato devaṃ mahādevaṃ giriśaṃ śūlapāṇinam
|
dadarśa phālgunastatra saha devyā mahādyutim||72||

73sa jānubhyāṃ mahīṃ gatvā śirasā praṇipatya ca
|
prasādayāmāsa haraṃ pārthaḥ parapurañjayaḥ||73||

74arjuna uvāca|
kapardinsarvadeveśa bhaganetranipātana
|
devadeva mahādeva nīlagrīva jaṭādhara||74||

75kāraṇānāṃ ca paramaṃ jāne tvāṃ tryambakaṃ vibhum
|
devānāṃ ca gatiṃ devaṃ tvatprasūtamidaṃ jagat||75||

76ajeyastvaṃ tribhirlokaiḥ sadevāsuramānuṣaiḥ
|
śivāya viṣṇurūpāya viṣṇave śivarūpiṇe||76||

77dakṣayajñavināśāya harirudrāya vai namaḥ
|
lalāṭākṣāya śarvāya mīḍhuṣe śūlapāṇaye||77||

78pinākagoptre sūryāya mārjālīyāya vedhase
|
prasādaye tvāṃ bhagavansarvabhūtamaheśvara||78||

79gaṇeśaṃ jagataḥ śambhuṃ lokakāraṇakāraṇam
|
pradhānapuruṣātītaṃ paraṃ sūkṣmataraṃ haram||79||

80vyatikramaṃ me bhagavankṣantumarhasi śaṅkara
|
bhagavandarśanākāṅkṣī prāpto'smīmaṃ mahāgirim||80||

81dayitaṃ tava deveśa tāpasālayamuttamam
|
prasādaye tvāṃ bhagavansarvalokanamaskṛtam||81||

82na me syādaparādho'yaṃ mahādevātisāhasāt
|
kṛto mayāyamajñānādvimardoṃ yastvayā saha
|
śaraṇaṃ pratipannāya tatkṣamasvādya śaṅkara||82||

83vaiśaṃpāyana uvāca|
tamuvāca mahātejāḥ prahasya vṛṣabhadhvajaḥ
|
pragṛhya ruciraṃ bāhuṃ kṣāntamityeva phālgunam||83||

84pariṣvajya ca bāhubhyāṃ prītātmā bhagavānharaḥ
|
punaḥ pārthaṃ sāntvapūrvamuvāca vṛṣabhadhvajaḥ||84||
