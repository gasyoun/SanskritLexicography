Mahābhārata 14.40 (Āśvamedhikaparvan)

1brahmovāca|
avyaktātpūrvamutpanno mahānātmā mahāmatiḥ
|
ādirguṇānāṃ sarveṣāṃ prathamaḥ sarga ucyate||1||

2mahānātmā matirviṣṇurjiṣṇuḥ śambhuśca vīryavān
|
buddhiḥ prajñopalabdhiśca tathā khyātidhṛtiḥ smṛtiḥ||2||

3paryāyavācakaiḥ śabdairmahānātmā vibhāvyate
|
taṃ jānanbrāhmaṇo vidvānpramohaṃ nādhigacchati||3||

4sarvataḥpāṇipādaśca sarvato'kṣiśiromukhaḥ
|
sarvataḥśrutimā~lloke sarvaṃ vyāpya sa tiṣṭhati||4||

5mahāprabhāvaḥ puruṣaḥ sarvasya hṛdi niścitaḥ
|
aṇimā laghimā prāptirīśāno jyotiravyayaḥ||5||

6tatra buddhivido lokāḥ sadbhāvaniratāśca ye
|
dhyānino nityayogāśca satvasandhā jitendriyāḥ||6||

7jñānavantaśca ye kecidalubdhā jitamanyavaḥ
|
prasannamanaso dhīrā nirmamā nirahaṅkṛtāḥ||7||

8vimuktāḥ sarva evaite mahattvamupayāntyuta
|
ātmano mahato veda yaḥ puṇyāṃ gatimuttamām||8||

9ahaṅkārāt prasūtāni mahābhūtāni pañca vai
|
pṛthivī vāyurākāśamāpo jyotiśca pañcamam||9||

10teṣu bhūtāni yujyante mahābhūteṣu pañcasu
|
te śabdasparśarūpeṣu rasagandhakriyāsu ca||10||

11mahābhūtavināśānte pralaye pratyupasthite
|
sarvaprāṇabhṛtāṃ dhīrā mahadutpadyate bhayam||11||

12sa dhīraḥ sarvalokeṣu na mohamadhigacchati
|
viṣṇurevādisargeṣu svayambhūrbhavati prabhuḥ||12||

13evaṃ hi yo veda guhāśayaṃ prabhuṃ paraṃ purāṇaṃ puruṣaṃ viśvarūpam
|
hiraṇmayaṃ buddhimatāṃ parāṃ gatiṃ sa buddhimān buddhimatītya tiṣṭhati||13||
