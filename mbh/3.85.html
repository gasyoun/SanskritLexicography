Mahābhārata 3.85 (Āraṇyakaparvan)

1pulastya uvāca|
atha sandhyāṃ samāsādya saṃvedyaṃ tīrthamuttamam
|
upaspṛśya naro vidyāṃ labhate nātra saṃśayaḥ||1||

2rāmasya ca prasādena tīrthaṃ rājankṛtaṃ purā
|
tallauhityaṃ samāsādya vindyādbahu suvarṇakam||2||

3karatoyāṃ samāsādya trirātropoṣito naraḥ
|
aśvamedhamavāpnoti prajāpatikṛto vidhiḥ||3||

4gaṅgāyāstatra rājendra sāgarasya ca saṅgame
|
aśvamedhaṃ daśaguṇaṃ pravadanti manīṣiṇaḥ||4||

5gaṅgāyāstvaparaṃ pāraṃ prāpya yaḥ snāti mānavaḥ
|
trirātramuṣito rājansarvāpāpaiḥ pramucyate||5||

6tato vaitaraṇīṃ gacchetsarvapāpapramocanīm
|
virajaṃ tīrthamāsādya virājati yathā śaśī||6||

7pratarecca kulaṃ puṇyaṃ sarvapāpaṃ vyapohati
|
gosahasraphalaṃ labdhvā punāti svakulaṃ naraḥ||7||

8śoṇasya jyotirathyāyāḥ saṃgame niyataḥ śuciḥ
|
tarpayitvā pitṝndevānagniṣṭomaphalaṃ labhet||8||

9śoṇasya narmadāyāśca prabhede kurunandana
|
vaṃśagulma upaspṛśya vājimedhaphalaṃ labhet||9||

10ṛṣabhaṃ tīrthamāsādya kośalāyāṃ narādhipa
|
vājapeyamavāpnoti trirātropoṣito naraḥ||10||

11gosahasraphalaṃ vindyātkulaṃ caiva samuddharet
|
kośalāṃ tu samāsādya kālatīrthamupaspṛśet||11||

12vṛṣabhaikādaśaphalaṃ labhate nātra saṃśayaḥ
|
puṣpavatyāmupaspṛśya trirātropoṣito naraḥ||12||

13gosahasraphalaṃ labdhvā punāti svakulaṃ nṛpa
|
tato badarikātīrthaṃ snātvā bharatasattama||13||

14dīrghamāyuravāpnoti svargalokaṃ ca gacchati
|
tathā campāṃ samāsādya bhāgīrathyāṃ kṛtodakaḥ||14||

15daṇḍākhyamabhigamyaiva gosahasraphalaṃ labhet
|
lapeṭikāṃ tato gacchetpuṇyāṃ puṇyopaśobhitām||15||

16vājapeyamavāpnoti devaiḥ sarvaiśca pūjyate
|
tato mahendramāsādya jāmadagnyaniṣevitam||16||

17rāmatīrthe naraḥ snātvā aśvamedhaphalaṃ labhet
|
mataṅgasya tu kedārastatraiva kurunandana||17||

18tatra snātvā kuruśreṣṭha gosahasraphalaṃ labhet
|
śrīparvataṃ samāsādya nadītīramupaspṛśet||18||

19aśvamedhamavāpnoti pūjayitvā vṛṣadhvajam
|
śrīparvate mahādevo devyā saha mahādyutiḥ||19||

20nyavasatparamaprīto brahmā ca tridaśaiḥ saha
|
tatra devahrade snātvā śuciḥ prayatamānasaḥ||20||

21aśvamedhamavāpnoti parāṃ siddhiṃ ca gacchati
|
ṛṣabhaṃ parvataṃ gatvā pāṇḍye daivatapūjitam
|
vājapeyamavāpnoti nākapṛṣṭhe ca modate||21||

22tato gaccheta kāverīṃ vṛtāmapsarasāṃ gaṇaiḥ
|
tatra snātvā naro rājangosahasraphalaṃ labhet||22||

23tatastīre samudrasya kanyātīrthamupaspṛśet
|
tattoyaṃ spṛśya rājendra sarvapāpaiḥ pramucyate||23||

24atha gokarṇamāsādya triṣu lokeṣu viśrutam
|
samudramadhye rājendra sarvalokanamaskṛtam||24||

25yatra brahmādayo devā ṛṣayaśca tapodhanāḥ
|
bhūtayakṣapiśācāśca kinnarāḥ samahoragāḥ||25||

26siddhacāraṇagandharvamānuṣāḥ pannagāstathā
|
saritaḥ sāgarāḥ śailā upāsanta umāpatim||26||

27tatreśānaṃ samabhyarcya trirātropoṣito naraḥ
|
aśvamedhamavāpnoti gāṇapatyaṃ ca vindati||27||

28uṣya dvādaśarātraṃ tu pūtātmā ca bhavennaraḥ
|
tata eva ca gāyatryāḥ sthānaṃ trailokyapūjitam||28||

29trirātramuṣitastatra gosahasraphalaṃ labhet
|
nidarśanaṃ ca pratyakṣaṃ brāhmaṇānāṃ narādhipa||29||

30gāyatrīṃ paṭhate yastu yonisaṃkarajastathā
|
gāthā ca gāthikā cāpi tasya saṃpadyate nṛpa||30||

31abrāhmaṇasya sāvitrīṃ paṭhatastu praṇaśyati
|
saṃvartasya tu viprarṣervāpīmāsādya durlabhām||31||

32rūpasya bhāgī bhavati subhagaśca prajāpate
|
tato veṇāṃ samāsādya trirātropoṣito naraḥ||32||

33mayūrahaṃsasaṃyuktaṃ vimānaṃ labhate naraḥ
|
tato godāvarīṃ prāpya nityaṃ siddhaniṣevitām||33||

34gavāṃ medhamavāpnoti vāsukerlokamuttamam
|
veṇāyāḥ saṃgame snātvā vājimedhaphalaṃ labhet||34||

35varadāsaṃgame snātvā gosahasraphalaṃ labhet
|
brahmasthānaṃ samāsādya trirātropoṣito naraḥ||35||

36gosahasraphalaṃ vindyātsvargalokaṃ ca gacchati
|
kuśaplavanamāsādya brahmacārī samāhitaḥ||36||

37trirātramuṣitaḥ snātvā aśvamedhaphalaṃ labhet
|
tato devahrade'raṇye kṛṣṇaveṇājalodbhave||37||

38jātismarahrade snātvā bhavejjātismaro naraḥ
|
yatra kratuśatairiṣṭvā devarājo divaṃ gataḥ||38||

39agniṣṭomaphalaṃ vindyādgamanādeva bhārata
|
tataḥ sarvahrade snātvā gosahasraphalaṃ labhet||39||

40tato vāpīṃ mahāpuṇyāṃ payoṣṇīṃ saritāṃ varām
|
pitṛdevārcanarato gosahasraphalaṃ labhet||40||

41daṇḍakāraṇyamāsādya puṇyaṃ rājannupaspṛśet
|
gosahasraphalaṃ tasya snātamātrasya bhārata||41||

42śarabhaṅgāśramaṃ gatvā śukasya ca mahātmanaḥ
|
na durgatimavāpnoti punāti ca kulaṃ naraḥ||42||

43tataḥ śūrpārakaṃ gacchejjāmadagnyaniṣevitam
|
rāmatīrthe naraḥ snātvā vindyādbahusuvarṇakam||43||

44saptagodāvare snātvā niyato niyatāśanaḥ
|
mahatpuṇyamavāpnoti devalokaṃ ca gacchati||44||

45tato devapathaṃ gatvā niyato niyatāśanaḥ
|
devasatrasya yatpuṇyaṃ tadevāpnoti mānavaḥ||45||

46tuṅgakāraṇyamāsādya brahmacārī jitendriyaḥ
|
vedānadhyāpayattatra ṛṣiḥ sārasvataḥ purā||46||

47tatra vedeṣu naṣṭeṣu muneraṅgirasaḥ sutaḥ
|
ṛṣīṇāmuttarīyeṣu sūpaviṣṭo yathāsukham||47||

48oṃkāreṇa yathānyāyaṃ samyaguccāritena ha
|
yena yatpūrvamabhyastaṃ tatsarvaṃ samupasthitam||48||

49ṛṣayastatra devāśca varuṇo'gniḥ prajāpatiḥ
|
harirnārāyaṇastatra mahādevastathaiva ca||49||

50pitāmahaśca bhagavāndevaiḥ saha mahādyutiḥ
|
bhṛguṃ niyojayāmāsa yājanārthe mahādyutim||50||

51tataḥ sa cakre bhagavānṛṣīṇāṃ vidhivattadā
|
sarveṣāṃ punarādhānaṃ vidhidṛṣṭena karmaṇā||51||

52ājyabhāgena tatrāgniṃ tarpayitvā yathāvidhi
|
devāḥ svabhavanaṃ yātā ṛṣayaśca yathākramam||52||

53tadaraṇyaṃ praviṣṭasya tuṅgakaṃ rājasattama
|
pāpaṃ praṇaśyatyakhilaṃ striyo vā puruṣasya vā||53||

54tatra māsaṃ vaseddhīro niyato niyatāśanaḥ
|
brahmalokaṃ vrajedrājankulaṃ caiva samuddharet||54||

55medhāvikaṃ samāsādya pitṝndevāṃśca tarpayet
|
agniṣṭomamavāpnoti smṛtiṃ medhāṃ ca vindati||55||

56atra kālañjaraṃ gatvā parvataṃ lokaviśrutam
|
tatra devahrade snātvā gosahasraphalaṃ labhet||56||

57yo snātaḥ snāpayettatra girau kālañjare nṛpa
|
svargaloke mahīyeta naro nāstyatra saṃśayaḥ||57||

58tato girivaraśreṣṭhe citrakūṭe viśāṃpate
|
mandākinīṃ samāsādya sarvapāpapraṇāśinīm||58||

59tatrābhiṣekaṃ kurvāṇaḥ pitṛdevārcane rataḥ
|
aśvamedhamavāpnoti gatiṃ ca paramāṃ vrajet||59||

60tato gaccheta dharmajña bhartṛsthānamanuttamam
|
yatra nityaṃ mahāseno guhaḥ sannihito nṛpa||60||

61tatra gatvā nṛpaśreṣṭha gamanādeva siddhyati
|
koṭitīrthe naraḥ snātvā gosahasraphalaṃ labhet||61||

62pradakṣiṇamupāvṛtya jyeṣṭhasthānaṃ vrajennaraḥ
|
abhigamya mahādevaṃ virājati yathā śaśī||62||

63tatra kūpe mahārāja viśrutā bharatarṣabha
|
samudrāstatra catvāro nivasanti yudhiṣṭhira||63||

64tatropaspṛśya rājendra pitṛdevārcane rataḥ
|
niyatātmā naraḥ pūto gaccheta paramāṃ gatim||64||

65tato gaccheta rājendra śṛṅgaverapuraṃ mahat
|
yatra tīrṇo mahārāja rāmo dāśarathiḥ purā||65||

66tasmiṃstīrthe mahābāho snātvā pāpaiḥ pramucyate
|
gaṅgāyāṃ tu naraḥ snātvā brahmacārī samāhitaḥ||66||

67vidhūtapāpmā bhavati vājapeyaṃ ca vindati
|
tato muñjavaṭaṃ gacchetsthānaṃ devasya dhīmataḥ||67||

68abhigamya mahādevamabhivādya ca bhārata
|
pradakṣiṇamupāvṛtya gāṇapatyamavāpnuyāt||68||

69tasmiṃstīrthe tu jāhnavyāṃ snātvā pāpaiḥ pramucyate
|
tato gaccheta rājendra prayāgamṛṣisaṃstutam||69||

70yatra brahmādayo devā diśaśca sadigīśvarāḥ
|
lokapālāśca sādhyāśca pitaro lokasaṃmatāḥ||70||

71sanatkumārapramukhāstathaiva paramarṣayaḥ
|
aṅgiraḥpramukhāścaiva tathā brahmarṣayomalāḥ||71||

72tathā nāgāḥ suparṇaśca siddhāścakracarāstathā
|
saritaḥ sāgarāścaiva gandharvāpsaraso'pi ca||72||

73hariśca bhagavānāste prajāpatipuraskṛtaḥ
|
tatra trīṇyagnikuṇḍāni yeṣāṃ madhyena jāhnavī||73||

74vegena samatikrāntā sarvatīrthapuraskṛtā
|
tapanasya sutā devī triṣu lokeṣu viśrutā||74||

75yamunā gaṅgayā sārdhaṃ saṃgatā lokapāvanī
|
gaṅgāyamunayormadhyaṃ pṛthivyā jaghanaṃ smṛtam||75||

76prayāgaṃ jaghanasthānamupasthamṛṣayo viduḥ
|
prayāgaṃ sapratiṣṭhānaṃ kambalāśvatarau tathā||76||

77tīrthaṃ bhogavatī caiva vedireṣā prajāpateḥ
|
tatra vedāśca yajñāśca mūrtimanto yudhiṣṭhira||77||

78prajāpatimupāsante ṛṣayaśca tapodhanāḥ
|
yajante kratubhirdevāstathā cakradharā nṛpāḥ||78||

79tataḥ puṇyatamaṃ nāma triṣu lokeṣu bhārata
|
prayāgaṃ sarvatīrthebhyaḥ pravadantyadhikaṃ vibho||79||

80gamanāttasya tīrthasya nāmasaṃkīrtanādapi
|
mṛtyukālabhayāccāpi naraḥ pāpātpramucyate||80||

81tatrābhiṣekaṃ yaḥ kuryātsaṃgame lokaviśrute
|
puṇyaṃ sa phalamāpnoti rājasūyāśvamedhayoḥ||81||

82eṣā yajanabhūmirhi devānāmabhisaṃskṛtā
|
tatra dattaṃ sūkṣmamapi mahadbhavati bhārata||82||

83na vedavacanāttāta na lokavacanādapi
|
matirutkramaṇīyā te prayāgamaraṇaṃ prati||83||

84daśa tīrtasahasrāṇi ṣaṣṭiḥ koṭyastathāparāḥ
|
yeṣāṃ sānnidhyamatraiva kīrtitaṃ kurundana||84||

85caturvidye ca yatpuṇyaṃ satyavādiṣu caiva yat
|
snāta eva tadāpnoti gaṅgāyamunasaṃgame||85||

86tatra bhogavatī nāma vāsukestīrthamuttamam
|
tatrābhiṣekaṃ yaḥ kuryātsośvamedhaphalaṃ labhet||86||

87tatra haṃsaprapatanaṃ tīrthaṃ trailokyaviśrutam
|
daśāśvamedhikaṃ caiva gaṅgāyāṃ kurunandana||87||

88kurukṣetrasamā gaṅgā yatra tatrāvagāhitā
|
viśeṣo vai kanakhale prayāge paramaṃ mahat||88||

89yadyakāryaśataṃ kṛtvā kṛtaṃ gaṅgāvasecanam
|
sarvaṃ tattasya gaṅgāpo dahatyagnirivendhanam||89||

90sarvaṃ kṛtayuge puṇyaṃ tretāyāṃ puṣkaraṃ smṛtam
|
dvāparepi kurukṣetraṃ gaṅgā kaliyuge smṛtā||90||

91puṣkare tu tapastapyeddānaṃ dadyānmahālaye
|
malaye tvagnimārohedbhṛgutuṅge tvanāśanam||91||

92puṣkare tu kurukṣetre ragaṅgāyāṃ magadheṣu ca
|
snātvā tārayate jantuḥ saptasaptāvarāṃstathā||92||

93punāti kīrtitā pāpaṃ dṛṣṭā bhadraṃ prayacchati
|
avagāḍhā ca pītā ca punātyāsaptamaṃ kulam||93||

94yāvadasthi manuṣyasya gaṅgāyāḥ spṛśate jalam
|
tāvatsa puruṣo rājansvargaloke mahīpate||94||

95yathā puṇyāni tīrthāni puṇyānyāyatanāni ca
|
upāsya puṇyaṃ labdhvā ca bhavatyamaralokabhāk||95||

96na gaṅgāsadṛśaṃ tīrthaṃ na devaḥ keśavātparaḥ
|
brāhmaṇebhyaḥ paraṃ nāsti evamāha pitāmahaḥ||96||

97yatra gaṅgā mahārāja sa deśastattapokavanam
|
siddhikṣetraṃ ca tajjñeyaṃ gaṅgātīrasamāśritam||97||

98idaṃ satyaṃ dvijātīnāṃ sādhūnāmātmajasya ca
|
suhṛdāṃ ca japetkarṇe śiṣyasyānugatasya ca||98||

99idaṃ dhanyamidaṃ medhyamidaṃ svargyamanuttamam
|
idaṃ puṇyamidaṃ ramyaṃ pāvanaṃ dharmyamuttamam||99||

100maharṣīṇāmidaṃ guhyaṃ sarvapāpapramocanam
|
adhītya dvijamadhye ca nirmalaḥ svargamāpnuyāt||100||

101śrīmatsvargyaṃ tathā puṇyaṃ sapatnaśamanaṃ śivam
|
medhājananamagryaṃ vai tīrthavaṃśānukīrtanam||101||

102aputro labhate putramadhano dhanamāpnuyāt
|
mahīṃ vijayate rājā vaiśyo dhanamavāpnuyāt||102||

103śūdro yathepsitānkāmānbrāhmaṇaḥ pāragaḥ paṭhan
|
yaścedaṃ śṛṇuyānnityaṃ tīrthapuṇyaṃ naraḥ śuciḥ||103||

104jātīḥ sa smarate bahvīrnākapṛṣṭhe ca modate
|
gamyānyapi ca tīrthāni kīrtitānyagamāni ca||104||

105manasā tāni gaccheta sarvatīrthasamīkṣayā
|
etāni vasubhiḥ sādhyairādityairmarudaśvibhiḥ||105||

106ṛṣibhirdevakalpaiśca snātāni sukṛtaiṣibhiḥ
|
evaṃ tvamapi kauravya vidhinānena suvrata||106||

107vrata tīrthāni niyataḥ puṇyaṃ puṇyena vardhayan
|
bhāvitaiḥ karaṇaiḥ pūrvamāstikyācchrutidarśanāt||107||

108prāpyante tāni tīrthāni sadbhiḥ śāstrānudarśibhiḥ
|
nāvratī nākṛtātmā ca nāśucirna ca taskaraḥ||108||

109snāti tīrtheṣu kauravya na ca vakramatirnaraḥ
|
tvayā tu samyagvṛttena nityaṃ dharmārthadarśinā||109||

110pitā pitāmahaścaiva sarve ca prapitāmahāḥ
|
pitāmahapurogāśca devāḥ sarṣigaṇā nṛpa||110||

111tava dharmeṇa dharmajña nityamevābhitoṣitāḥ
|
avāpsyasi tvaṃ lokānvai vasūnāṃ vāsavopama
|
kīrtiṃ ca mahātīṃ bhīṣma prāpsyase bhuvi śāśvatīm||111||

112nārada uvāca| evamuktvābhyanujñāya pulastyo bhagavānṛṣiḥ
|
prītaḥ prītena manasā tatraivāntaradhīyata||112||

113bhīṣmaśca kuruśārdūla śāstratattvārthadarśivān
|
pulastyavacanāccaiva pṛthivīṃ paricakrame||113||

114evameṣā mahābhāgā pratiṣṭhāne pratiṣṭhitā
|
tīrthayātrā mahāpuṇyā sarvapāpapramocanī||114||

115anena vidhinā yastu pṛthivīṃ saṃcariṣyati
|
aśvamedhaśataṃ sāgraṃ phalaṃ pretya sa bhokṣyati||115||

116tataścāṣṭaguṇaṃ pārtha prāpsyase dharmamuttamam
|
bhīṣmaḥ kurūṇāṃ pravaro yathāpūrvamavāptavān||116||

117netā ca tvamṛṣīnyasmāttena teṣṭaguṇaṃ phalam
|
rakṣogaṇavikīrṇāni tīrthānyetāni bhārata
|
na gatāni manuṣyendraistvāmṛte kurunandana||117||

118idaṃ devarṣicaritaṃ sarvatīrthābhisaṃvṛtam
|
yaḥ paṭhetkulyamutthāya sarvapāpaiḥ pramucyate||118||

119ṛṣimukhyāḥ sadā yatra vālmīkistvatha kaśyapaḥ
|
ātreyaḥ kuṇḍajaṭharo viśvāmitrotha gautamaḥ||119||

120asito devalaścaiva mārkaṇḍeyo'tha gālavaḥ
|
bharadvājo vasiṣṭhaśca muniruddālakastathā||120||

121śaunakaḥ saha putreṇa vyāsaśca tapatāṃ varaḥ
|
durvāsāśca muniśreṣṭho jābāliśca mahātapāḥ||121||

122ete ṛṣivarāḥ sarve tvatpratīkṣāstapodhanāḥ
|
ebhiḥ saha mahārāja tīrthānyetānyanuvraja||122||

123eṣa te lomaśo nāma maharṣiramitadyutiḥ
|
sameṣyati mahārāja tena sārdhamanuvraja||123||

124mayāpi saha dharmajña tīrthānyetānyanukramāt
|
prāpsyase mahatīṃ kīrtiṃ yathā rājā mahābhiṣaḥ||124||

125yathā yayātirdharmātmā yathā rājā purūravāḥ
|
tathā tvaṃ rājaśārdūla svena dharmeṇa śobhase||125||

126yathā bhagīratho rājā yathā rāmaśca viśrutaḥ
|
tathā tvaṃ sarvarājabhyo bhrājase raśmivāniva||126||

127yathā manuryathekṣvākuryathā pūrurmahāyaśāḥ
|
yathā vainyo mahārāja tathā tvamapi viśrutaḥ||127||

128yathā ca vṛtrahā sarvānsapatnānnirdahanpurā
|
trailokyaṃ pālayāmāsa devarāḍvigatajvaraḥ||128||

129tathā śatrukṣayaṃ kṛtvā tvaṃ prajāḥ pālayiṣyasi
|
svadharmavijitāmurvīṃ prāpya rājīvalocana||129||

130khyātiṃ yāsyasi dharmeṇa kārtavīryārjuno yathā||130||

131vaiśaṃpāyana uvāca| evamāśvāsya rājānaṃ nārado bhagavānṛṣiḥ
