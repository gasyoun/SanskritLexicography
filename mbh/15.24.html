Mahābhārata 15.24 (Āśramavāsikaparvan)

1vaiśampāyana uvāca|
tataste pāṇḍavā dūrādavatīrya padātayaḥ
|
abhijagmurnarapaterāśramaṃ vinayānatāḥ||1||

2sa ca yodhajanaḥ sarvo ye ca rāṣṭranivāsinaḥ
|
striyaśca kurumukhyānāṃ padbhirevānvayustadā||2||

3āśramaṃ te tato jagmurdhṛtarāṣṭrasya pāṇḍavāḥ
|
śūnyaṃ mṛgagaṇākīrṇaṃ kadalīvanaśobhitam||3||

4tatastatra samājagmustāpasā niyatavratāḥ
|
pāṇḍavānāgatān draṣṭuṃ kautūhalasamanvitāḥ||4||

5tānapṛcchattato rājā kvāsau kauravavaṃśabhṛt
|
pitā jyeṣṭho gato'smākamiti bāṣpapariplutaḥ||5||

6te tamūcustato vākyaṃ yamunāmavagāhitum
|
yuṣyāṇāmudakumbhasya cārthe gata iti prabho||6||

7tairākhyātena mārgeṇa tataste jagmurañjasā
|
dadṛśuścāvidūre tānsarvānatha padātayaḥ||7||

8tataste satvarā jagmuḥ piturdarśanakākṣiṇaḥ
|
sahadevastu vegena prādhāvadyatra sā pṛthā||8||

9susvaraṃ rurude dhīmānmātuḥ pādāvupaspṛśan
|
sā ca bāṣpākulamukhī dadarśa dayitaṃ sutam||9||

10bāhubhyāṃ sampariṣvajya samunnāmya ca putrakam
|
gāndhāryāḥ kathayāmāsa sahadevamupasthitam||10||

11anantaraṃ ca rājānaṃ bhīmasenamathārjunam
|
nakulaṃ ca pṛthā dṛṣṭvā tvaramāṇopacakrame||11||

12sā hyagre gacchati tayordampatyorhataputrayoḥ
|
karṣantī tau tataste tāṃ dṛṣṭvā saṃnyapatanbhuvi||12||

13rājā tānsvarayogena sparśena ca mahāmanāḥ
|
pratyabhijñāya medhāvī samāśvāsayata prabhuḥ||13||

14tataste bāṣpamutsṛjya gāndhārīsahitaṃ nṛpam
|
upatasthurmahātmāno mātaraṃ ca yathāvidhi||14||

15sarveṣāṃ toyakalaśājagṛhuste svayaṃ tadā
|
pāṇḍavā labdhasaṃjñāste mātrā cāśvāsitāḥ punaḥ||15||

16tathā nāryo nṛsiṃhānāṃ so'varodhajanastadā
|
paurajānapadāścaiva dadṛśustaṃ janādhipam||16||

17nivedayāmāsa tadā janaṃ tannāmagotrataḥ
|
yudhiṣṭhiro narapatiḥ sa cainaṃ pratyapūjayat||17||

18sa taiḥ parivṛto mene harṣabāṣpāvilekṣaṇaḥ
|
rājā''tmānaṃ gṛhagataṃ pureva gajasāhvaye||18||

19abhivādito vadhūbhiśca kṛṣṇādyābhiḥ sa pārthivaḥ
|
gāndhāryā sahito dhīmānkuntyā ca pratyanandata||19||

20tataścāśramamāgacchatsidvayāraṇasevitam
|
didṛkṣubhiḥ samākīrṇaṃ namastārāgaṇairiva||20||
