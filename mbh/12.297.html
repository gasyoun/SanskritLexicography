Mahābhārata 12.297 (Śāntiparvan)

1parāśara uvāca |
pitā sakhāyo guravastriyaśca na nirguṇānāṃ hi bhavanti loke
|
ananyabhaktāḥ priyavādinaśca hitāśca vaśyāśca bhavanti rājan ||1||

2pitā paraṃ daivataṃ mānavānāṃ māturviśiṣṭaṃ pitaraṃ vadanti
|
jñānasya lābhaṃ paramaṃ vadanti jitendriyārthāḥ paramāpnuvanti ||2||

3raṇājire yatra śarāgnisaṃstare nṛpātmajo ghātamavāpya dahyate
|
prayāti lokānamaraiḥ sudurlabhānniṣevate svargaphalaṃ yathāsukham ||3||

4śrāntaṃ bhītaṃ bhraṣṭaśastraṃ rudantaṃ parāṅmukhaṃ pāribarhaiśca hīnam
|
anudyantaṃ rogiṇaṃ yācamānaṃ na vai hiṃsyādbālavṛddhau ca rājan ||4||

5pāribarhaiḥ susaṃyuktamudyataṃ tulyatāṃ gatam
|
atikramettaṃ nṛpatiḥ saṃgrāme kṣatriyātmajam ||5||

6tulyādiha vadhaḥ śreyānviśiṣṭācceti niścayaḥ
|
nihīnātkātarāccaiva kṛpaṇādgarhito vadhaḥ ||6||

7pāpātpāpasamācārannihīnācca narādhipa
|
pāpa eva vadhaḥ prokto narakāyeti niścayaḥ ||7||

8na kaścittrāti vai rājandiṣṭāntavaśamāgatam
|
sāvaśeṣāyuṣaṃ cāpi kaścinnaivāpakarṣati ||8||

9snigdhaiśca kriyamāṇāni karmāṇīha nivartayet
|
hiṃsātmakāni sarvāṇi nāyuricchetparāyuṣā ||9||

10gṛhasthānāṃ tu sarveṣāṃ vināśamabhikāṅkṣatām
|
nidhanaṃ śobhanaṃ tāta pulineṣu kriyāvatām ||10||

11āyuṣi kṣayamāpanne pañcatvamupagacchati
|
tathā hyakāraṇādbhavati kāraṇairupapāditam ||11||

12tathā śarīraṃ bhavati dehādyenopapāditam
|
adhvānaṃ gatakaśāyaṃ prāptaścāyaṃ gṛhādgṛham ||12||

13dvitīyaṃ kāraṇaṃ tatra nānyatkiṃcana vidyate
|
taddehaṃ dehināṃ yuktaṃ pañcabhūteṣu vartate ||13||

14śirāsnāyvasthisaṃghātaṃ bībhatsāmedhyasaṃkulam
|
bhūtānāmindriyāṇāṃ ca guṇānāṃ ca samāgamam ||14||

15tvagantaṃ dehamityāhurvidvāṃso'dhyātmacintakāḥ
|
guṇairapi parikṣīṇaṃ śarīraṃ martyatāṃ gatam ||15||

16śarīriṇā parityaktaṃ niśceṣṭaṃ gatacetanam
|
bhūtaiḥ prakṛtimāpannastato bhūmau nimajjati ||16||

17bhāvitaṃ karmayogena jāyate tatra tatra ha
|
idaṃ śarīraṃ vaideha mriyate yatra yatra ha
|
tatsvabhāvo paro dṛṣṭo visargaḥ karmaṇastathā ||17||

18na jāyate tu nṛpate kaṃcitkālamayaṃ punaḥ
|
paribhramati bhūtātmā dyāmivāmbudharo mahān ||18||

19sa punarjāyate rājanprāpyehāyatanaṃ nṛpa
|
manasaḥ paramo hyātmā indriyebhyaḥ paraṃ manaḥ ||19||

20vividhānāṃ ca bhūtānāṃ jaṅgamāḥ paramā nṛpā
|
jaṅgamānāmapi tathā dvipadāḥ paramā matāḥ ||20||

21dvipadānāmapi tathā dvijā vai paramāḥ samṛtāḥ
|
dvijānāmapi rājendra prajñāvantaḥ parā matāḥ
|
prājñānāmātmasambuddhāḥ sambuddhānāmamāninaḥ ||21||

22jātamanveti maraṇaṃ nṛṇāmiti viniścayaḥ
|
antavanti hi karmāṇi sevante guṇataḥ prajāḥ ||22||

23āpanne tūttarāṃ kāṣṭhāṃsūrye yo nidhanaṃ vrajet
|
nakṣatre ca muhūrte ca puṇye rājansa puṇyakṛt ||23||

24ayojayitvā kleśena janaṃ plāvya ca duṣkṛtam
|
mṛtyunā''tmakṛte neha karma kṛtvā''tmaśaktibhiḥ ||24||

25viṣamudbabandhanaṃ dāho dasyuhastāttathā vadhaḥ
|
daṃṣṭribhyaśca paśubhyaśca prākṛto vadha ucyate ||25||

26na caibhiḥ puṇyakarmāṇo yujyante cābhisandhijaiḥ
|
evaṃvidhaiśca bahubhiraparaiḥ prākṛtairapi ||26||

27ūrdhvaṃ bhittvā pratiṣṭhante prāṇāḥ puṇyavatāṃ nṛpa
|
madhyato madhyapuṇyānāmadho duṣkṛtakarmaṇām ||27||

28ekaḥ śatrurna dvitīyo'sti śatrurajñānatulyaḥ puruṣasya rājan
|
yenāvṛtaḥ kurute samprayukto ghorāṇi karmāṇi sudāruṇāni ||28||

29prabādhanārthaṃ śrutidharmayuktaṃ vṛddhānupāsya prabhaveta yasya
|
prayatnasādhyo hi sa rājaputra prajñāśareṇonmathitaḥ paraiti ||29||

30adhītya vedaṃ tapasā brahmacārī yajñān śaktyā sannigṛhyeha pañca
|
vanaṃ gacchetpuruṣo dharmakāmaḥ śreyaḥsthitvā sthāpayitvā svavaṃśam ||30||

31upabhogairapi tyaktaṃ nātmānaṃ sādayennaraḥ
|
caṇḍālatve'pi mānuṣyaṃ sarvathā tāta śobhanam ||31||

32iyaṃ hi yoniḥ prathamā yāṃ prāpya jagatīpate
|
ātmā vai śakyate trātuṃ karmabhiḥ śubhalakṣaṇaiḥ ||32||

33kathaṃ na vipraṇaśyema yonito'syā iti prabho
|
kurvanti dharmaṃ manujāḥ śrutiprāmāṇyadarśanāt ||33||

34yo durlabhataraṃ prāpya mānuṣyaṃ dviṣate naraḥ
|
dharmāvamantā kāmātmā bhavetsa khalu vañcyate ||34||

35yastu prītipurogena cakṣuṣā tāta paśyati
|
dīpopamāni bhūtāni yāvadarthānna paśyati ||35||

36sāntvenānnapradānena priyavādena cāpyuta
|
samaduḥkhasukho bhūtvā sa paratra mahīyate ||36||

37dānaṃ tyāgaḥ śobhanā mūrtiradbhyo bhūtaplāvyaṃ tapasā vai śarīram
|
sarasvatī naimiṣapuṣkareṣu ye cāpyanye puṇyadeśāḥ pṛthivyām ||37||

38gṛheṣu yeṣāmasavaḥ patanti teṣāmathānirharaṇaṃ praśastam
|
yānena vai prāpaṇaṃ ca śmaśāne śaucena nūnaṃ vidhinā caiva dāhaḥ ||38||

39iṣṭiḥ puṣṭiryajanaṃ yājanaṃ ca dānaṃ puṇyānāṃ karmaṇāṃ ca prayogaḥ
|
śaktyā pitryaṃ yacca kiṃcitpraśastaṃ sarvāṇyātmārthe mānavo'yaṃ karoti ||39||

40dharmaśāstrāṇi vedāśca ṣaḍaṅgāni narādhipa
|
śreyaso'rthe vidhīyante narasyākliṣṭakarmaṇaḥ ||40||

41bhīṣma uvāca|
etadvai sarvamākhyātaṃ muninā sumahātmanā
|
videharājāya purā śreyaso'rthe narādhipa ||41||
