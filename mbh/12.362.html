Mahābhārata 12.362 (Śāntiparvan)

1brāhmaṇa uvāca|
vivasvato gacchati paryayeṇa voḍhuṃ bhavāṃstaṃ rathamekacakram
|
āścaryabhūtaṃ yadi tatra kiñciddṛṣṭaṃ tvayā śaṃsitumarhasi tvam||1||

2nāga uvāca|
āścaryāṇāmanekānāṃ pratiṣṭhā bhagavānraviḥ
|
yato bhūtāḥ pravartante sarve trailokyasaṃmatāḥ||2||

3yasya raśmisahasreṣu śākhāsviva vihaṅgamāḥ
|
vasantyāśritya munayaḥ saṃsiddhā daivataiḥ saha||3||

4yato vāyurviniḥsṛtya sūryaraśmyāśrito mahān
|
vijṛṃbhatyambare tatra kimāścaryamataḥ param||4||

5vibhajya taṃ tu viprarṣe prajānāṃ hitakāmyayā
|
toyaṃ sṛjati varṣāsu kimāścaryamata: param||5||

6yasya maṇḍalamadhyastho mahātmā paramatviṣā
|
dīptaḥ samīkṣate lokān kimāścaryamataḥ param||6||

7śukro nāmāsitaḥ pādo yaśca vāridharo'mbare
|
toyaṃ sṛjati varṣāsu kimāścaryamataḥ param||7||

8yo'ṣṭamāsāṃstu śucinā kiraṇenokṣitaṃ payaḥ
|
pratyādatte punaḥ kāle kimāścaryamataḥ param||8||

9yasya tejoviśeṣeṣu svayamātmā pratiṣṭhitaḥ
|
yato bījaṃ mahī ceyaṃ dhāryate sacarācarā||9||

10yatra devo mahābāhuḥ śāśvataḥ puruṣottamaḥ
|
anādinidhano vipra kimāścaryamataḥ param||10||

11āścaryāṇāmivāścaryamidamekaṃ tu me śṛṇu
|
vimale yanmayā dṛṣṭamambare sūryasaṃśrayāt||11||

12purā madhyāhnasamaye lokāṃstapati bhāskare
|
pratyādityapratīkāśaḥ sarvataḥ samadṛśyat||12||

13sa lokāṃstejasā sarvān svabhāsā nirvibhāsayan
|
ādityābhimukho'bhyeti gaganaṃ pāṭayanniva||13||

14hutāhutiriva jyotirvyāpya tejomarīcibhiḥ
|
anirdeśyena rūpeṇa dvitīya iva bhāskaraḥ||14||

15tasyābhigamanaprāptau hastau dattau vivasvatā
|
tenāpi dakṣiṇo hasto dattaḥ pratyarcitārthinā||15||

16tato bhittvaiva gaganaṃ praviṣṭo raśmimaṇḍalam
|
ekībhūtaṃ ca tattejaḥ kṣaṇenādityatāṃ gatam||16||

17tatra naḥ saṃśayo jātastayostejaḥ samāgame
|
anayoḥ ko bhavetsūryo rathastho yo'yamāgataḥ||17||

18te vayaṃ jātasaṃdehāḥ paryapṛcchāmahe ravim
|
ka eṣa divamākramya gataḥ sūrya ivāparaḥ||18||
