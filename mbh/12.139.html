Mahābhārata 12.139 (Śāntiparvan)

1yudhiṣṭhira uvāca|
ukto mantro mahābāho viśvāso nāsti śatruṣu
|
kathaṃ hi rājā varteta yadi sarvatra nāśvaset ||1||

2viśvāsāddhi paraṃ rājan rājñāmutpadyate bhayam
|
kathaṃ hi nāśvasan rājā śatrūn jayati pārthivaḥ ||2||

3etanme saṃśayaṃ chindhi matirme sampramuhyati
|
aviśvāsakathāmetāmupaśrutya pitāmaha ||3||

4bhīṣma uvāca|
śṛṇuṣva rājan yadvṛttaṃ brahmadattaniveśane
|
pūjanyā saha saṃvādaṃ brahmadattasya bhūpateḥ ||4||

5kāmpilye brahmadattasya tvantaḥpuranivāsinī
|
pūjanī nāma śakunirdīrghakālaṃ sahoṣitā ||5||

6rutajñā sarvabhūtānāṃ yathā vai jīvajīvakaḥ
|
sarvajñā sarvatattvajñā tiryagyoniṃ gatā'pi sā ||6||

7abhiprajātā sā tatra putramekaṃ suvarcasam
|
samakālaṃ ca rājño'pi devyāṃ putro vyajāyata ||7||

8tayorarthe kṛtajñā sā khecarī pūjanī sadā
|
samudratīraṃ sā gatvā ājahāra phaladvayam ||8||

9puṣṭyarthaṃ ca svaputrasya rājaputrasya caiva ha
|
phalamekaṃ sutāyādādrājaputrāya cāparam ||9||

10amṛtāsvādasadṛśaṃ balatejo'bhivardhanam
|
ādāyādāya saivāśu tayoḥ prādātpunaḥ punaḥ ||10||

11tato'gacchatparāṃ vṛddhiṃ rājaputraḥ phalāśanāt
|
tataḥ sa dhācyā kakṣeṇa uhyamāno nṛpātmajaḥ ||11||

12dadarśa taṃ pakṣisutaṃ bālyādāgatya bālakaḥ
|
tato bālyācca yatnena tenākrīḍata pakṣiṇā ||12||

13śūnye ca tamupādāya pakṣiṇaṃ samajātakam
|
hatvā tataḥ sa rājendra dhātryā hastamupāgataḥ ||13||

14atha sā pūjanī rājannāgamatphalahāriṇī
|
apaśyannihataṃ putraṃ tena bālena bhūtale ||14||

15vāṣpapūrṇamukhī dīnā dṛṣṭvā taṃ rudatī sutam
|
pūjanī duḥkhasaṃtaptā rudatī vākyamabravīt ||15||

16kṣatriye sagataṃ nāsti na prītirna ca sauhṛdam
|
kāraṇātsāntvayantyete kṛtārthāḥ saṃtyajanti ca ||16||

17kṣatriyeṣu na viśvāsaḥ kāryaḥ sarvāpakāriṣu
|
apakṛtyāpi satataṃ sāntvayanti nirarthakam ||17||

18ahamasya karomyadya sadṛśīṃ vairayātanām
|
kṛtaghnasya nṛśaṃsasya bhṛśaṃ viśvāsaghātinaḥ ||18||

19sahasaṃjātavṛddhasya tathaiva sahabhojinaḥ
|
śaraṇāgatasya ca vadhastrividhaṃ hyeva pātakam ||19||

20ityuktvā caraṇābhyāṃ netre nṛpasutasya sā
|
bhittvā svasthā tata idaṃ pūjanī vākyamabravīt ||20||

21icchayeha kṛtaṃ pāpaṃ sadyastaṃ copasarpati
|
kṛtaṃ pratikṛtaṃ yeṣāṃ na naśyati śubhāśubham ||21||

22pāpaṃ karma kṛtaṃ kiṃcidyadi tasminna dṛśyate
|
nṛpate tasya putreṣu pautreṣvapi ca naptṛṣu ||22||

23tu brahmadattaḥ sutaṃ dṛṣṭvā pūjanyāhṛtalocanam
|
kṛte pratikṛtaṃ matvā pūjanīmidamabravīt ||23||

24brahmadatta uvāca |
asti vai kṛtamasmābhirasti pratikṛtaṃ tvayā
|
ubhayaṃ tatsamībhūtaṃ vasa pūjani mā gamaḥ ||24||

25pūjanyuvāca |
sakṛtkṛtāparādhasya tatraiva parilambataḥ
|
na tadbudhāḥ praśaṃsanti śreyastatrāpasarpaṇam ||25||

26sāntve prayukte satataṃ kṛtavaire na viśvaset
|
kṣipraṃ sa badhyate mūḍho na hi vairaṃ praśāmyati ||26||

27anyonyakṛtavairāṇāṃ putrapautraṃ niyacchati
|
putrapautravināśe ca paralokaṃ niyacchati ||27||

28sarveṣāṃ kṛtavairāṇāmaviśvāsaḥ sukhodayaḥ
|
ekāntato na viśvāsaḥ kāryo viśvāsaghātakaiḥ ||28||

29na viśvasedaviśvaste viśvaste nātiviśvaset
|
viśvāsādbhayamutpannamapi mūlaṃ nikṛntati
|
kāmaṃ viśvāsayedanyānpareṣāṃ ca na viśvaset ||29||

30mātā pitā bāndhavānāṃ variṣṭhau bhāryā jarā bījamātraṃ tu putraḥ
|
bhrātā śatruḥ klinnapāṇirvayasya ātmā hyekaḥ sukhaduḥkhasya bhoktā ||30||

31anyonyakṛtavairāṇāṃ na saṃdhirupapadyate
|
sa ca heturatikrānto yadarthamahamāvasam ||31||

32pūjitasyārthamānābhyāṃ jantoḥ pūrvāpakāriṇaḥ
|
mano bhavatyaviśvastaṃ karma trāsayate'balān ||32||

33pūrvaṃ sammānana yatra paścāccaiva vimānanā
|
jahyāttatsattvavānsthānaṃ śatroḥ sammānito'pi san ||33||

34uṣitāsmi tavāgāre dīrghakālaṃ samarcitā
|
tadidaṃ vairamutpannaṃ sukhamāśu vrajāmyaham ||34||

35brahmadatta uvāca |
yaḥ kṛte pratikuryādvai na sa tatrāparānuyāt
|
anṛṇastena bhavati vasa pūjani mā gamaḥ ||35||

36pūjanyuvāca |
na kṛtasya tu kartuśca sakhyaṃ saṃdhīyate punaḥ
|
hṛdayaṃ tatra jānāti kartuścaiva kṛtasya ca ||36||

37brahmadata uvāca |
kṛtasya caiva kartuśca sakhyaṃ saṃdhīyate punaḥ
|
vairasyopaśamo dṛṣṭaḥ pāpaṃ nopāśnute punaḥ ||37||

38pūjanyuvāca |
nāsti vairamatikrāntaṃ sāntvito'smīti nāśvaset
|
viśvāsādbaddhyate loke tasmācchreyo'pyadarśanam ||38||

39tarasā ye na śakyante śastraiḥ suniśitairapi
|
sāmnā te'pi nigṛhyante gajā iva kareṇubhiḥ ||39||

40brahmadatta uvāca |
saṃvāsājjāyate sneho jīvitāntakareṣvapi
|
anyonyasya ca viśvāsaḥ śvapacena śuno pathā ||40||

41anyonkṛtavairāṇāṃ saṃvāsānmṛdutāṃ gatam
|
naiva tiṣṭhati tadvairaṃ puṣkarasthamivodakam ||41||

42pūjanyuvāca|
vairaṃ pañcasamutthānaṃ tacca budhyanti paṇḍitāḥ
|
strīkṛtaṃ vāstujaṃ vājaṃ sasāpatnāparādhajam ||42||

43tatra dātā na hantavyaḥ kṣatriyeṇa viśeṣataḥ
|
prakāśaṃ vā'prakāśaṃ vā budhvā doṣabalābalam ||43||

44kṛtavaire na viśvāsaḥ kāryastviha suhṛdyapi
|
channaṃ saṃtiṣṭhate vairaṃ gūḍho'gniriva dāruṣu ||44||

45na vittena na pāruṣyairna ca sāntvena vā''śritaiḥ
|
kopāgniḥ śāmyate rājastoyāgniriva sāgare ||45||

46na hi vairāgnirudbhūtaḥ karma cāpyaparādhajam
|
śāmyatyadagdhvā nṛpate vinā hyekatarakṣayāt ||46||

47satkṛtasyārthamānābhyāmanupūrvāpakāriṇaḥ
|
nādeyo'mitraviśvāsaḥ karma trāsayate'balān ||47||

48naivāpakārye kasmiṃścidahaṃ tvayi tathā bhavān
|
uṣitāsmi gṛhe'haṃ te nedānīṃ viśvasāmyaham ||48||

49brahmadatta uvāca |
kālena kriyate kāryaṃ tathaiva vividhāḥ kriyāḥ
|
kālenaite pravartante kaḥ kasyehāparādhyati ||49||

50tulyaṃ cobhe pravartete maraṇaṃ janma caiva ha
|
kāryate caiva kālena tannimittaṃ na jīvati ||50||

51vadhyante yugapatkecidekaikasya na cāpare
|
kālo dahati bhūtāni samprāpyāgnirivendhanam ||51||

52nāhaṃ pramāṇaṃ naiva tvamanyonyaṃ kāraṇaṃ śubhe
|
kālo nityamupādatte sukhaṃ duḥkhaṃ ca dehinām ||52||

53evaṃ vaseha sasnehā yathākāmamahiṃsitā
|
yatkṛtaṃ tattu me kṣāntaṃ tvaṃ ca vai kṣama pūjani ||53||

54pūjanyuvāca |
yadi kālaḥ pramāṇaṃ te na vairaṃ kasyacidbhavet
|
kasmāttvapacitiṃ yānti bāndhavā bāndhavairhataiḥ ||54||

55kasmāddevāsurāḥ pūrvamanyonyamabhijaghnire
|
yadi kālena niryāṇaṃ sukhaṃ duḥkhaṃ bhavābhavau ||55||

56bhiṣajo bhaiṣajaṃ kartuṃ kasmādicchanti rogiṇaḥ
|
yadi kālena pacyante bheṣajaiḥ kiṃ prayojanam ||56||

57pralāpaḥ sumahānkasmāt kriyate śokamūrcchitaiḥ
|
yadi kālaḥ pramāṇaṃ te kasmāddharmo'sti kartṛṣu ||57||

58tava putro mamāpatyaṃ hatavānsa hato mayā
|
anantaraṃ tvayā'haṃ ca hantavyā hi narādhipa ||58||

59ahaṃ hi putraśokena kṛtapāpā tavātmaje
|
yathā tvayā prahartavyaṃ tathā tattvaṃ ca me śṛṇu ||59||

60bhakṣyārthaṃ krīḍanārthaṃ ca narā vāñchanti pakṣiṇaḥ
|
tṛtīyo nāsti saṃyogo vadhabandhādṛte kṣamaḥ ||60||

61vadhabandhabhayādete mokṣatantramupāśritāḥ
|
maraṇotpātajaṃ duḥkhaṃ prāhurvedavido janāḥ ||61||

62sarvasya dayitāḥ prāṇāḥ sarvasya dayitāḥ sutāḥ
|
duḥkhādudvijate sarvaḥ sarvasya sukhamīpsitam ||62||

63duḥkhaṃ jarā brahmadatta duḥkhamarthaviparyayaḥ
|
duḥkhaṃ cāniṣṭasaṃvāso duḥkhamiṣṭaviyojanam ||63||

64vadhabandhakṛtaṃ duḥkhaṃ strīkṛtaṃ sahajaṃ tathā
|
duḥkha sutena satataṃ janānviparivartate ||64||

65na duḥkhaṃ paraduḥkhe vai kecidāhurabuddhayaḥ
|
yo duḥkhaṃ nābhijānāti sa jalpati mahājane ||65||

66yastu śocati duḥkhārtaḥ sa kathaṃ vaktumutsahet
|
rasajñaḥ sarvaduḥkhasya yathā''tmani tathā pare ||66||

67yatkṛtaṃ te mayā rājaṃstvayā ca mama yatkṛtam
|
na tadvarṣaśataiḥ śakyaṃ vyapohitumarindama ||67||

68āvayoḥ kṛtamanyonyaṃ punaḥ sandhirna vidyate
|
smṛtvā smṛtvā hi te putraṃ navaṃ vairaṃ bhaviṣyati ||68||

69vairapantikamāsādya yaḥ prītiṃ kartumicchati
|
mṛnmayasyeva bhagnasya yathā saṃdhirna vidyate ||69||

70niścayaḥ svārthaśāstreṣu viśvāsaścāsukhodayaḥ
|
uśanā caiva gāthe dve prahlādāyābravītpurā ||70||

71ye vairiṇaḥ śraddadhate satye satyetare'pi vā
|
vadhyante śraddadhānāstu madhu śuṣkatṛṇairyathā ||71||

72na hi vairāṇi śāmyanti kule duḥkhagatāni ca
|
ākhyātāraśca vidyante kule vai dhriyate pumān ||72||

73upagṛhya tu vairāṇi sāntvayanti narādhipa
|
athainaṃ pratipiṃṣanti pūrṇaṃ ghaṭamivāśmani ||73||

74sadā na viśvasedrājā pāpaṃ kṛtveha kasyacit
|
apakṛtya pareṣāṃ hi viśvāsādduḥkhamaśnute ||74||

75brahmadatta uvāca |
nāviśvāsādvindate'rthānīhate cāpi kiṃcana
|
bhayāttvekatarānnityaṃ mṛtakalpā bhavanti ca ||75||

76pūjanyuvāca|
yasyeha vraṇinī pādau padbhyāṃ ca parisarpati
|
khanyete tasya tau pādau suguptamiha dhāvataḥ ||76||

77netrābhyāṃ sarujābhyāṃ yaḥ prativātamudīkṣate
|
tasya vāyurundā'tyarthaṃ netrayorbhavati dhruvam ||77||

78duṣṭaṃ panthānamāsādya yo mohādupapadyate
|
ātmano balabhajñāya tadantaṃ tasya jīvitam ||78||

79yastu varṣamavijñāya kṣetraṃ karṣati karṣakaḥ
|
hīnaḥ puruṣakāreṇa sasyaṃ naivāśnute tataḥ ||79||

80yastu tiktaṃ kaṣāyaṃ vā svādu vā madhuraṃ hitam
|
āhāraṃ kurute nityaṃ so'mṛtatvāya kalpate ||80||

81pathyaṃ muktvā tu yo mohādduṣṭamaśnāti bhojanam
|
pariṇāmamavijñāya tadantaṃ tasya jīvitam ||81||

82daivaṃ puruṣakāraśca sthitāvanyonyasaṃśrayāt
|
udārāṇāṃ tu satkarma daivaṃ klībā upāsate ||82||

83karma cātmahitaṃ kāryaṃ tīkṣṇaṃ vā yadi vā mṛdu
|
grasyate'karmaśīlastu sadānarthairakiñcanaḥ ||83||

84tasmātsarvaṃ vyapohyārthaṃ kārya eva parākramaḥ
|
sarvasvamapi santyajya kāryamātmahitaṃ naraiḥ ||84||

85vidyā śauryaṃ ca dākṣyaṃ ca balaṃ dhairyaṃ ca pañcamam
|
mitrāṇi sahajānyāhurvartayantīha tairbudhāḥ ||85||

86niveśanaṃ ca kupyaṃ ca kṣetraṃ bhāryā suhṛjjanaḥ
|
etānyupahitānyāhuḥ sarvatra labhate pumān ||86||

87sarvatra ramate prājñaḥ sarvatra ca virājate
|
na vibhīpayate kaścidbhīṣito na bibheti ca ||87||

88nityaṃ buddhimato'pyarthaḥ svalpako'pi vivardhate
|
dākṣyeṇakurvataḥ karma saṃyapātpratitiṣṭhati ||88||

89gṛhasnehāvabaddhānāṃ narāṇāmalpamedhasām
|
kustrī khādati māṃsāni māghamāṃ segavā iva ||89||

90gṛhaṃ kṣetrāṇi mitrāṇi svadeśa iti cāpare
|
ityevamavasīdanti narā buddhiviparyaye ||90||

91utpatetsahajāddeśādvyādhidurbhikṣapīḍitāt
|
anyatra vastuṃ gacchedvā vasedvā nityamānitaḥ ||91||

92tasmādanyatra yāsyāmi vastuṃ nāhamihotsahe
|
kṛtametadanāryaṃ me tava putre ca pārthiva ||92||

93kubhāryāṃ ca kuputraṃ ca kurājānaṃ kusauhṛdam
|
kusambandhaṃ kudeśaṃ ca dūrataḥ parivarjayet ||93||

94kuputre nāsti viśvāsaḥ kubhāryāyāṃ kuto ratiḥ
|
kurājye nirvṛtirnāsti kudeśe nāsti jīvikā ||94||

95kumitre saṃgatirnāsti nityamasthirasauhṛde
|
avamānaḥ kusambandhe bhavatyarthaviparyaye ||95||

96sā bhāryā priyaṃ brūte sa putro yatra nirvṛtiḥ
|
tanmitraṃ yatra viśvāsaḥ sa deśo yatra jīvyate ||96||

97yatra nāsti balātkāraḥ sa rājā tīvraśāsanaḥ
|
bhīreva nāsti sambandho daridraṃ yo bubhūṣate ||97||

98bhāryā deśo'tha mitrāṇi putrasambandhibāndhavāḥ
|
ete sarve guṇavati dharmanetre mahīpatau ||98||

99adharmajñasya vilayaṃ prajā gacchanti nigrahāt
|
rājā mūlaṃ trivargasya svapramatto'nupālayet ||99||

100baliṣaḍbhāgamuddhṛtya baliṃ samupayojayet
|
na rakṣati prajāḥ samyagyaḥ sa pārthivataskaraḥ ||100||

101dattvābhayaṃ yaḥ svayameva rājā na tatpramāṇaṃ kurute'rthalobhāt
|
sa sarvalokādupalabhya pāpaṃ so'dharmabuddhirnirayaṃ prayāti ||101||

102dattvābhayaṃ svayaṃ rājā pramāṇaṃ kurute yadi
|
sa sarvasukhakṛjjñeyaḥ prajā dharmeṇa pālayan ||102||

103mātā pitā gururgoptā vahnirvaiśravaṇo yamaḥ
|
sapta rājño guṇānetānmanurāha prajāpatiḥ ||103||

104pitā hi rājā rāṣṭrasya prajānāṃ yo'nukampanaḥ
|
tasminmithyāvinīto hi tiryaggacchati mānavaḥ ||104||

105sambhāvayati māteva dīnamapyupapadyate
|
dahatyagnirivānaṣṭānyamayannasato yamaḥ ||105||

106iṣṭeṣu visṛjannarthānkubera iva kāmadaḥ
|
gururdharmopadeśena goptā ca paripālayan ||106||

107yastu rañjayate rājā paurajānapadān guṇaiḥ
|
na tasya bhramate rājyaṃ svayaṃ dharmānupālanāt ||107||

108svayaṃ samupajānanhi paurajānapadārcanam
|
sa sukhaṃ prekṣate rājā iha loke paratra ca ||108||

109nityodvignāḥ prajā yasya karabhāraprapīḍitāḥ
|
anarthaivipralupyante sa gacchati parābhavam ||109||

110prajā yasya vivardhante sarasīva mahotpalam
|
sa sarvaphalabhāgrājā svargaloke mahīyate ||110||

111balinā vigraho rājanna kadācit praśasyate
|
balinā vigraho yasya kuto rājyaṃ kutaḥ sukham ||111||

112bhīṣma uvāca|
saivamuktvā śakunikā brahmadattaṃ narādhipam
|
rājānaṃ samanujñāpya jagāmābhīpsitāṃ diśam ||112||

113etatte brahmadattasya pūjanyā saha bhāṣitam
|
mayoktaṃ nṛpatiśreṣṭha kimanyacchrotumicchasi ||113||
