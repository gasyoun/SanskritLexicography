Mahābhārata 12.269 (Śāntiparvan)

1kapila uvāca |
etāvadanupaśyanti yatayo yānti mārgagāḥ
|
naiṣāṃ sarveṣu lokeṣu kaścidasti vyatikramaḥ ||1||

2nirdvandvāṃ nirnamaskārā nirāśīrbadhanā budhāḥ
|
vimuktāḥ sarvapāpebhyaścaranti śucayo'malāḥ ||2||

3apavarge'tha saṃtyāge buddhau ca kṛtaniścayāḥ
|
brahmiṣṭhā brahmabhūtāśca brahmaṇyeva kṛtālayāḥ ||3||

4viśokā naṣṭarajasasteṣāṃ lokāḥ sanātanāḥ
|
teṣāṃ gatiṃ parāṃ prāpya gārhasthye kiṃ prayojanam ||4||

5syūmaraśmiruvāca |
yadyeṣā paramā kāṣṭhā yadyeṣā paramā gatiḥ
|
gṛhasthānavyapāśritya nāśramo'nyaḥ pravartate ||5||

6yathā mātaramāśritya sarve jīvanti jantavaḥ
|
evaṃ gārhasthyamāśritya vartanta itarāśramāḥ ||6||

7gṛhastha eva yajate gṛhasthastapyate tapaḥ
|
gārhasthyamasya dharmasya mūlaṃ yatkicidejate ||7||

8prajanādyabhinirvṛttāḥ sarve prāṇabhṛto janāḥ
|
prajanaṃ cāpyutānyatra na kathaṃcana vidyate ||8||

9yāstu syurbarhiroṣadhyo bahiranyāstathādrijāḥ
|
oṣadhibhyo bahiryasmātprāṇātkaścinna dṛśyate ||9||

10kasyaiṣā vāgbhavetsatyā mokṣo nāsti gṛhāditi
|
aśraddadhānairaprājñaiḥ sūkṣmadarśanavarjitaiḥ ||10||

11nirāsairalasaiḥ śrāntaistapyamānaiḥ svakarmabhiḥ
|
śamasyoparamo dṛṣṭaḥ pravrajyāyāmapaṇḍitaiḥ ||11||

12trailokyasyaiva heturhi maryādā śāśvatī dhruvā
|
brāhmaṇo nāma bhagavān janmaprabhṛti pūjyate ||12||

13prāggarbhādhānānmantrā hi pravartante dvijātiṣu
|
aviśrambheṣu vartante viśrambheṣvapyasaṃśayam ||13||

14dāhe punaḥ saṃśrayaṇe saṃśrite pātrabhojane
|
dāne gavāṃ paśūnāṃ vā piṇḍānāmapsu majjane ||14||

15arciṣmanto barhiṣadaḥ kravyādāḥ pitarastathā
|
mṛtasyāpyanumanyante mantrān mantrāśca kāraṇam ||15||

16evaṃ krośatsu vedeṣu kuto mokṣo'sti kasyacit
|
ṛṇavanto yadā martyāḥ pitṛdevadvijātiṣu ||16||

17śriyā vihīnairalasaiḥ paṇḍitaiḥ sampravartitam
|
vedavādāparijñānaṃ satyābhāsamivānṛtam ||17||

18na vai pāpairhriyate kṛṣyate vā yo brāhmaṇo yajate vedaśāstraiḥ
|
urdhvaṃ yajñaiḥ paśubhiḥ sārdhameti saṃtarpitastarpayate ca kāmaiḥ ||18||

19na vedānāṃ paribhavānna śāṭhyena na māyayā
|
mahatprāpnoti puruṣo brahmaṇi brahma vindati ||19||

20kapila uvāca |
darśaṃ ca paurṇamāsaṃ ca agnihotraṃ ca dhīmataḥ
|
cāturmāsyāni caivāsaṃsteṣu dharmaḥ sanātanaḥ ||20||

21anārambhāḥ sudhṛtayaḥ śucayo brahmasaṃjñitāḥ
|
brahmaṇaiva sma te devāṃstarpayantyamṛtaiṣiṇaḥ ||21||

22sarvabhūtātmabhūtasya sarvabhūtāni paśyataḥ
|
devāpi mārge muhyanti apadasya padaiṣiṇaḥ ||22||

23caturdvāraṃ puruṣaṃ caturmukhaṃ caturdhā cainamupayāti vācā
|
bāhubhyāṃ vāca udarādupasthātteṣāṃ dvāraṃ dvārapālo bubhūṣeta ||23||

24nakṣairdīvyennādadītānyavittaṃ na vā'yonīyasya śṛtaṃ pragṛhṇāt
|
kruddho na caiva prahareta dhīmāṃstathā'sya tatpāṇipādaṃ suguptam ||24||

25nākrośamṛcchenna vṛthā vadecca na paiśunaṃ janavādaṃ ca kuryāt
|
satyavrato mitabhāṣo'pramattastathā'sya vāgdvāramatho suguptam ||25||

26nānāśanaḥ syānna mahāśanaḥ syādalolupaḥ sādhubhirāgataḥ syāt
|
yātrārthamāhāramihādadīta tathā'sya syājjāṭharī dvāraguptiḥ ||26||

27na vīrapatnī vihareta nārīṃ na cāpi nārīmanṛtāvāhvayīta
|
bhāryāvrataṃ hyātmani dhārayīta tathāsyopasthadvāraguptirbhaveta ||27||

28dvārāṇi yasya sarvāṇi suguptāni manīṣiṇaḥ
|
upasthamudaraṃ bāhū vākcaturthī bhaveddvijaḥ ||28||

29modhānyaguptadvārasya sarvāṇyeva bhavantyuta
|
kiṃ tasya tapasā kāryaṃ kiṃ yajñena kimātmanā ||29||

30anuttarīyavasanamanupastīrṇaśāyinam
|
bāhūpadhānaṃ śāmyantaṃ taṃ devā brāhmaṇaṃ viduḥ ||30||

31dvandvārāmeṣu sarveṣu ya eko ramate muniḥ
|
pareṣāmananudhyāyaṃstaṃ devā brāhmaṇaṃ viduḥ ||31||

32yena sarvamidaṃ buddhaṃ prakṛtirvikṛtiśca yā
|
gatijñaḥ sarvabhūtānāṃ taṃ devā brāhmaṇaṃ viduḥ ||32||

33abhayaṃ sarvabhūtebhyaḥ sarveṣāmamayaṃ yataḥ
|
sarvabhūtātmabhūto yastaṃ devā brāhmaṇaṃ viduḥ ||33||

34nāntareṇānujānanti dānayajñakriyāphalam
|
avijñāya ca tatsarvamanyadrocayate phalam ||34||

35svakarmabhiḥ saṃśritānāṃ tapo ghoratvamāgatam
|
taṃ sadācāramāśritya purāṇaṃ śāśvataṃ dhruvam ||35||

36aśaknuvantaścarituṃ kiṃciddharmeṣu sūtritam
|
nirāpaddharma ācāro hyapramādo parābhavaḥ ||36||

37phalanavanti ca karmāṇi vyuṣṭimanti dhruvāṇi ca
|
viguṇāni ca paśyanti tathā'naikāntikāni ca ||37||

38guṇāścātra sudurjñeyā jñātāścātra suduṣkarāḥ
|
anuṣṭhitāścāntavanta iti tvamanupaśyasi ||38||

39syūmaraśmiruvāca |
yathā ca vedaprāmāṇyaṃ tyāgaśca saphalo yathā
|
tau panthānāvubhau vyaktau bhagavaṃstadbravīhi me ||39||

40kapila uvāca |
pratyakṣamiha paśyanti bhavantaḥ satpathe sthitāḥ
|
pratyakṣaṃ tu kimatrāsti yadbhavanta upāsate ||40||

41syūmaraśmiruvāca |
syūmaraśmirahaṃ brahman jijñāsārthamihāgataḥ
|
śreyaskāmaḥ pratyavocamārjavānna vivakṣayā ||41||

42imaṃ ca saṃśayaṃ ghoraṃ bhagavānprabravītu me
|
pratyakṣamiha paśyanto bhavantaḥ satpathe sthitāḥ
|
kimatra pratyakṣatamaṃ bhavanto yadupāsate ||42||

43anyatra tarkaśāstrebhya āgamārthaṃ yathāgamam
|
āgamo vedavādāstu tarkaśāstrāṇi cāgamaḥ ||43||

44yathāśramamupāsīta āgamastatra sidhyati
|
siddhiḥ pratyakṣarūpā ca dṛśyatyāgamaniścayāt ||44||

45naurnāvīva nibaddhā hi srotasā sanibandhanā
|
hriyamāṇā kathaṃ vipra kubuddhīṃstārayiṣyati
|
etadbravītu bhagavānupapanno'smyadhīhi bhoḥ ||45||

46naiva tyāgī na saṃtuṣṭo nāśoko na nirāmayaḥ
|
nānirvidhitso nāvṛtto nāpavṛtto'sti kaścana ||46||

47bhavanto'pi ca hṛṣayanti śocanti ca yathā vayam
|
indriyārthāśca bhavatāṃ samānāḥ sarvajantuṣu ||47||

48evaṃ caturṇāṃ varṇānamāśramāṇāṃ pravṛttiṣu
|
ekamālambamānānāṃ nirṇaye kiṃ nirāmayam ||48||

49kapila uvāca |
yadyadācarate śāstramarthyaṃ sarvapravṛttiṣu
|
yasya yatra hyanuṣṭhānaṃ tatra tatra nirāmayam ||49||

50jñānaṃ plāvayate sarvaṃ yo jñānaṃ hyanuvartate
|
jñānādapetya yā vṛttiḥ sā vināśayati prajāḥ ||50||

51bhavanto jñānino vyaktaṃ sarvataśca nirāmayāḥ
|
aikātmyaṃ nāma kaściddhi kadācidupapadyate ||51||

52śāstraṃ hyabuddhvā tattvena kecidvādabalājjanāḥ
|
kāmadveṣābhibhūtatvādahaṅkāravaśaṃ gatāḥ ||52||

53yāthātathyamavijñāya śāstrāṇāṃ śāstradasyavaḥ
|
brahmastenā nirārambhā dambhamohavaśānugāḥ ||53||

54nairguṇyameva paśyanti na guṇānanuyuñjate
|
teṣāṃ tamaḥśarīrāṇāṃ tama eva parāyaṇam ||54||

55yo yathā prakṛtirjantuḥ prakṛteḥ syādvaśānugaḥ
|
tasya dveṣaśca kāmaśca krodho dambho'nṛtaṃ madaḥ
|
nityamevābhivartante guṇāḥ prakṛtisambhavāḥ ||55||

56evaṃ dhyātvā'nupaśyantaḥ santyajeyuḥ śubhāśubham
|
parāṃ gatimabhīpsanto yatayaḥ saṃyame ratāḥ ||56||

57syūmaraśmiruvāca |
sarvametanmayā brahman śāstrataḥ parikīrtitam
|
na hyavijñāya śāstrārthaṃ pravartante pravṛttayaḥ ||57||

58yaḥ kaścinyāyya ācāraḥ sarvaṃ śāstramiti śrutiḥ
|
yadanyāyyamaśāstraṃ tadityeṣā śrūyate śrutiḥ ||58||

59na pravṛttirṝte śāstrātkācidastīti niścayaḥ
|
yadanyadvedavādebhyastadaśāstramiti śrutiḥ||59||

60śāstrādapetaṃ paśyanti bahavo vyaktamāninaḥ
|
śāstradoṣānna paśyanti śocanti ca yathā vayam
|
indriyārthāśca bhavatāṃ samānāḥ sarvajantuṣu ||60||

61evaṃ caturṇāṃ varṇānāmāśramāṇāṃ pravṛttiṣu
|
ekamālambamānānāṃ nirṇaye sarvatodiśam ||61||

62ānantyaṃ vadamānena śaktenāvarjitātmanā
|
avijñānahataprajñā hīnaprajñāstamovṛtāḥ ||62||

63śakyaṃ tvekena yuktona kṛtakṛtyena sarvaśaḥ
|
piṇḍamānaṃ vyapāśritya carituṃ vijitātmanā ||63||

64vedavādaṃ vyapāśritya mokṣo'stīti prabhāṣitum
|
apetanyāyaśāstreṇa sarvalokavigarhiṇā ||64||

65idaṃ tu duṣkaraṃ karma kuṭumbamabhisaṃśritam
|
dānamadhyayanaṃ yajñaḥ prajāsaṃtānamārjavam ||65||

66yadyetadevaṃ kṛtvāpi na vimokṣo'sti kasyacit
|
dhikkartāraṃ ca kāryaṃ ca śramaścāyaṃ nirarthakaḥ ||66||

67nāstikyamanyathā ca syādvedānāṃ pṛṣṭhataḥ kriyā
|
etasyānantyamicchāmi bhagavan śrotumañjasā ||67||

68tattvaṃ vadasva me brahmannupasanno'smyadhīhi bhoḥ
|
yathā ve vidito mokṣastathecchāmyupaśikṣitum ||68||
