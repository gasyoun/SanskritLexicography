Mahābhārata 7.201 (Droṇaparvan)

1sañjaya uvāca|
tat prabhagnaṃ balaṃ dṛṣṭvā kuntīputro dhanañjayaḥ
|
nyavārayadameyātmā droṇaputrajayepsayā||1||

2tataste sainikā rājannaiva tatrāvatasthire
|
saṃsthāpyamānā yatnena govindenārjunena ca||2||

3eka eva ca bībhatsuḥ somakāvayavaiḥ saha
|
matsyairanyaiśca saṃdhāya kauravānsaṃnyavartata||3||

4tato drutamatikramya siṃhalāṅgūlaketanam
|
savyasācī maheṣvāsamaśvatthāmānamabravīt||4||

5yā śaktiryacca vijñānaṃ yadvīryaṃ yacca pauruṣam
|
dhārtarāṣṭreṣu yā prītirdveṣo'smāsu ca yacca te||5||

6yacca bhūyo'sti tejaste tat sarvaṃ mayi darśaya
|
sa eva droṇahantā te darpaṃ chetsyati pārṣataḥ||6||

7kālānalasamaprakhyaṃ dviṣatāmantakopamam
|
samāsādaya pāñcālyaṃ māṃ cāpi
|
sahakeśavam darpaṃ nāśayitāsmyadya tavodvṛttasya saṃyuge||7||

8dhṛtarāṣṭra uvāca|
ācāryaputro mānārho balavāṃścāpi sañjaya
|
prītirdhanañjaye cāsya priyaścāpi mahātmanaḥ||8||

9na bhūtapūrvaṃ bībhatsorvākyaṃ paruṣamīdṛśam
|
atha kasmātsa kaunteyaḥ sakhāyaṃ rūkṣamuktavān||9||

10sañjaya uvāca|
yuvarāje hate caiva vṛddhakṣatre ca paurave
|
iṣvastravidhisampanne mālave ca sudarśane||10||

11dhṛṣṭadyumne sātyakau ca bhīme cāpi parājite
|
yudhiṣṭhirasya tairvākyairmarmaṇyapi ca ghaṭṭite||11||

12antarbhede ca sañjāte duḥkhaṃ saṃsmṛtya ca prabho
|
abhūtapūrvo bībhatsorduḥkhānmanyurajāyata||12||

13tasmādanarhamaślīlamapriyaṃ drauṇimuktavān
|
mānyamācāryatanayaṃ rūkṣaṃ kāpuruṣaṃ yathā||13||

14evamuktaḥ śvasan krodhānmaheṣvāsatamo nṛpa
|
pārthena paruṣaṃ vākyaṃ sarvamarmabhidā girā||14||

15drauṇiścukopa pārthāya kṛṣṇāya ca viśeṣataḥ
|
sa tu yatto rathe sthitvā vāryupaspṛśya vīryavān||15||

16devairapi sudurdharṣamastramāgneyamādade
|
dṛśyādṛśyānarigaṇānuddiśyācāryanandanaḥ||16||

17so'bhimantrya śaraṃ dīptaṃ vidhūmamiva pāvakam
|
sarvataḥ krodhamāviśya cikṣepa paravīrahā||17||

18tatastumulamākāśe śaravarṣamajāyata
|
pāvakārciḥ parītaṃ tatpārthamevābhipupluve||18||

19ulkāśca gaganātpeturdiśaśca na cakāśire
|
tamaśca sahasā raudraṃ camūmavatatāra tām||19||

20rakṣāṃsi ca piśācāśca vineduratisaṅgatāḥ
|
vavuścāśiśirā vātāḥ sūryo naiva tatāpa ca||20||

21vāyasāścāpi cākrandandikṣu sarvāsu bhairavam
|
rudhiraṃ cāpi varṣaṃto vinedustoyadā divi||21||

22pakṣiṇaḥ paśavo gāvo vineduścāpi suvratāḥ
|
paramaṃ prayatātmāno na śāntimupalebhire||22||

23bhrāntasarvamahābhūtamāvartitadivākaram
|
trailokyamabhisaṃtaptaṃ jvarāviṣṭamivābhavat||23||

24astratejobhisaṃtaptaṃ nāgā bhūmiśayāstathā
|
niḥśvasantaḥ samutpetustejo ghoraṃ mumukṣavaḥ||24||

25jalajāni ca sattvāni dahyamānāni bhārata
|
na śāntimupajagmurhi tapyamānairjalāśayaiḥ||25||

26digbhyaḥ pradigbhyaḥ khādbhūmeḥ sarvataḥ śaravṛṣṭayaḥ
|
uccāvacāni peturvai garuḍānilaraṃhasaiḥ||26||

27taiḥ śarairdroṇaputrasya vajravegaiḥ samāhatāḥ
|
pradagdhā ripavaḥ peturagnidagdhā iva drumāḥ||27||

28dahyamānā mahābhāgāḥ petururvyāṃ samantataḥ
|
nadanto bhairavānnādāñjaladopamaniḥsvanān||28||

29apare pradrutā nāgā bhayatrastā viśāṃpate
|
tresurdiśo yathā pūrvaṃ vane dāvāgnisaṃvṛtāḥ||29||

30drumāṇāṃ śikharāṇīva dāvadagdhāni māriṣa
|
aśvavṛndānyadṛśyanta rathavṛndāni bhārata||30||

31apatanta rathaughāśca tatra tatra sahasraśaḥ
|
tat sainyaṃ bhayasaṃvignaṃ dadāha yudhi bhārata||31||

32yugānte sarvabhūtāni saṃvartaka ivānalaḥ
|
dṛṣṭvā tu pāṇḍavīṃ senāṃ dahyamānāṃ mahāhave||32||

33prahṛṣṭāstāvakā rājansiṃhanādānvinedire
|
tatastūryasahasrāṇi nānāliṅgāni bhārata||33||

34tūrṇamājaghnire hṛṣṭāstāvakā jitakāśinaḥ
|
kṛtsnā hyakṣauhiṇī rājan savyasācī ca pāṇḍavaḥ||34||

35tamasā saṃvṛte loke nādṛśyanta mahāhave
|
naiva nastādṛśaṃ rājandṛṣṭapūrvaṃ na ca śrutam||35||

36yādṛśaṃ droṇaputreṇa sṛṣṭamastramamarṣiṇā
|
arjunastu mahārāja brāhmamastramudairayat||36||

37sarvāstrapratighātārthaṃ vihitaṃ padmayoninā
|
tato muhūrtādiva tattamo vyupaśaśāma ha||37||

38pravavau cānilaḥ śīto diśaśca vimalā babhuḥ
|
tatrādbhutamapaśyāma kṛtsnāmakṣauhiṇīṃ hatām||38||

39anabhijñeyarūpāṃ ca pradagdhāmastratejasā
|
tato vīrau maheṣvāsau vimuktau keśavārjunau||39||

40sahitau pratyadṛśyetāṃ nabhasīva tamonudau
|
tato gāṇḍīvadhanvā ca keśavaścākṣatāvubhau||40||

41sapatākadhvajahayaḥ sānukarṣavarāyudhaḥ
|
prababhau saratho yuktastāvakānāṃ bhayaṃkaraḥ||41||

42tataḥ kilakilāśabdaḥ śaṅkhabherīsvanaiḥ saha
|
pāṇḍavānāṃ prahṛṣṭānāṃ kṣaṇena samajāyata||42||

43hatāviti tayorāsītsenayorubhayormatiḥ
|
tarasā'bhyāgatau dṛṣṭvā sahitau keśavārjunau||43||

44tāvakṣatau pramuditau dadhmaturvārijottamau
|
dṛṣṭvā pramuditānpārthāṃstvadīyā vyathitā bhṛśam||44||

45vimuktau ca mahātmānau dṛṣṭvā drauṇiḥ suduḥkhitaḥ
|
muhūrtaṃ cintayāmāsa kiṃ tvetaditi māriṣa||45||

46cintayitvā tu rājendra dhyānaśokaparāyaṇaḥ
|
niḥśvasan dīrghamuṣṇaṃ ca vimanāścābhavattataḥ||46||

47sañjaya uvāca|
tato drauṇirdhanustyaktvā rathātpraskandya vegitaḥ
|
dhigdhiksarvamidaṃ mithyetyuktvā saṃprādravadraṇāt||47||

48tataḥ snigdhāmbudābhāsaṃ vedāvāsamakalmaṣam
|
vedavyāsaṃ sarasvatyāvāsaṃ vyāsaṃ dadarśa ha||48||

49taṃ drauṇiragrato dṛṣṭvā sthitaṃ kurukulodvaham
|
sannakaṇṭho'bravīdvākyamabhivādya sudīnavat||49||

50bho bho māyā yadṛcchā vā na vidmaḥ kimidaṃ bhavet
|
astraṃ tvidaṃ kathaṃ mithyā mama kaśca vyatikramaḥ||50||

51adharottarametadvā lokānāṃ vā parābhavaḥ
|
yadimau jīvataḥ kṛṣṇau ‘kālo hi duratikramaḥ’||51||

52nāsurā na ca gandharvā na piśācā na rākṣasāḥ
|
na sarpā yakṣapatagā na manuṣyāḥ kathaṃcana||52||

53utsahante'nyathā kartumetadastraṃ mayeritam
|
tadidaṃ kevalaṃ hatvā śāntamakṣauhiṇīṃ jvalat||53||

54sarvaghāti mayā muktamastraṃ paramadāruṇam
|
kenemau martyadharmāṇau nāvadhītkeśavārjunau||54||

55etatprabrūhi bhaganmayā pṛṣṭo yathātatham
|
śrotumicchāmi tattvena sarvametanmahāmune||55||

56vyāsa uvāca|
mahāntamevamarthaṃ māṃ yaṃ tvaṃ pṛcchasi vismayāt
|
taṃ pravakṣyāmi te sarvaṃ samādhāya manaḥ śṛṇu||56||

57yo'sau nārāyaṇo nāma pūrveṣāmapi pūrvajaḥ
|
ajāyata ca kāryārthaṃ putro dharmasya viśvakṛt||57||

58sa tapastīvramātasthe śiśiraṃ girimāsthitaḥ
|
ūrdhvabāhurmahātejā jvalanādityasannibhaḥ||58||

59ṣaṣṭiṃ varṣasahasrāṇi tāvantyeva śatāni ca
|
aśoṣayattadā''tmānaṃ vāyubhakṣo'mbujekṣaṇaḥ||59||

60athāparaṃ tapastaptvā dvistato'nyatpunarmahat
|
dyāvāpṛthivyorvivaraṃ tejasā samapūrayat||60||

61sa tena tapasā tāta brahmabhūto yadā'bhavat
|
tato viśveśvaraṃ yoniṃ viśvasya jagataḥ patim||61||

62dadarśa bhṛśadurdharṣaṃ sarvadevairabhiṣṭutam
|
aṇīyāṃsamaṇubhyaśca bṛhadbhyaśca bṛhattamam||62||

63rudramīśānavṛṣabhaṃ haraṃ śaṃbhuṃ kapardinam
|
cekitānaṃ parāṃ yoniṃ tiṣṭhato gacchataśca ha||63||

64durvāraṇaṃ durdṛśaṃ tigmamanyuṃ mahātmānaṃ sarvaharaṃ pracetasam
|
divyaṃ cāpamiṣudhī cādadānaṃ hiraṇyavarmāṇamanantavīryam||64||

65pinākinaṃ vajriṇaṃ dīptaśūlaṃ paraśvadhiṃ gadinaṃ cāyatāsim
|
śubhraṃ jaṭilaṃ musalinaṃ candramaujiṃ vyāghrājinaṃ parighiṇaṃ daṇḍapāṇim||65||

66śubhāṅgadaṃ nāgayajñopavītaṃ viśvairgaṇaiḥ śobhitaṃ bhūtasaṅghaiḥ
|
ekībhūtaṃ tapasāṃ sannidhānaṃ vayotigaiḥ suṣṭutamiṣṭavāgbhiḥ||66||

67jalaṃ diśaṃ khaṃ kṣitiṃ candrasūryau tathā vāyvagnī pramimāṇaṃ jagacca
|
nālaṃ draṣṭuṃ yaṃ janā bhinnavṛttā brahmadviṣannaghnamamṛtasya yonim||67||

68yaṃ paśyanti brāhmaṇāḥ sādhuvṛttāḥ kṣīṇe pāpe manasā vītaśokāḥ
|
taṃ niṣpatantaṃ tapasā dharmamīḍyaṃ tadbhaktyā vai viśvarūpaṃ dadarśa
|
dṛṣṭvā cainaṃ vāṅmanobuddhidehaiḥ saṃhṛṣṭātmā mumude vāsudevaḥ||68||

69akṣamālāparikṣiptaṃ jyotiṣāṃ paramaṃ nidhim
|
tato nārāyaṇo dṛṣṭvā vavande viśvasaṃbhavam||69||

70varadaṃ pṛthucārvaṅgyā pārvatyā sahitaṃ prabhum
|
krīḍamānaṃ mahātmānaṃ bhūtasaṅghagaṇairvṛtam||70||

71ajamīśānamavyaktaṃ kāraṇātmānamacyutam
|
abhivādyātha rudrāya sadyo'ndhakanipātine
|
padmākṣastaṃ virūpākṣamabhituṣṭāva bhaktimān||71||

72śrīnārāyaṇa uvāca|
tvatsaṃbhūtā bhūtakṛto vareṇya goptāro'sya bhuvanasyādideva
|
āviśyemāṃ dharaṇīṃ ye'bhyarakṣan purā purāṇīṃ tava devasṛṣṭim||72||

73surāsurānnāgarakṣaḥpiśācānnarānsuparṇānatha gandharvayakṣān
|
pṛthagvidhānbhūtasaṅghāṃśca viśvāṃstvatsaṃbhūtānvidma sarvāṃstathaiva
|
aindraṃ yāmyaṃ vāruṇaṃ vaittapālyaṃ paitraṃ tvāṣṭraṃ karma saumyaṃ ca tubhyam||73||

74rūpaṃ jyotiḥ śabda ākāśavāyuḥ sparśaḥ svādyaṃ salilaṃ gandha urvī
|
kālo brahmā brahma ca brāhmaṇāśca tvatsaṃbhūtaṃ sthāsnu cariṣṇu cedam||74||

75adbhyaḥ stokā yānti yathā pṛthaktvaṃ tābhiścaikyaṃ saṃkṣaye yānti bhūyaḥ
|
evaṃ vidvānprabhavaṃ cāpyayaṃ ca matvā bhūtānāṃ tva sāyujyameti||75||

76divyāmṛtau mānasau dvau suparṇau vācā śākhāḥ pippalāḥ sapta gopāḥ
|
daśāpyanye ye puraṃ dhārayanti tvayā sṛṣṭāstvaṃ hi tebhyaḥ paro hi||76||

77bhūtaṃ bhavyaṃ bhavitā cāpyadhṛṣyaṃ tvatsaṃbhūtā bhuvanānīha viśvā
|
bhaktaṃ ca māṃ bhajamānaṃ bhajasva mā rīriṣo māmahitāhitena||77||

78ātmānaṃ tvāmātmano na vyabodhaṃ vidvānevaṃ gacchati brahma śukram
|
astauṣaṃ tvāṃ tava saṃmānamicchan vicinvanvai sadṛśaṃ devavarya
|
sudurlabhāndehi varānmameṣṭānabhiṣṭutaḥ pravikārṣīśca māyām||78||

79vyāsa uvāca|
tasmai varānacintyātmā nīlakaṇṭhaḥ pinākadhṛt
|
arhate devamukhyāya prāyacchadṛṣisaṃstutaḥ||79||

80śrībhagavānuvāca
|
matprasādānmanuṣyeṣu deva-gandharvayoniṣu
|
aprameyabalātmā tvaṃ nārāyaṇa bhaviṣyasi||80||

81na ca tvāṃ prasahiṣyanti devāsuramahoragāḥ
|
na piśācā na gandharvā na yakṣā na ca rākṣasāḥ||81||

82na suparṇāstathā nāgā na ca viśve viyonijāḥ
|
na kaścittvāṃ ca devo'pi samareṣu vijeṣyati||82||

83na śastreṇa na vajreṇa nāgninā na ca vāyunā
|
na cārdreṇa na śuṣkeṇa trasena sthāvareṇa ca||83||

84kaścittava rujāṃ kartā matprasādātkathañcana
|
api vai samaraṃ gatvā bhaviṣyasi mamādhikaḥ||84||

85evamete varā labdhāḥ purastādviddhi śauriṇā
|
sa eṣa devaścarati māyayā mohayañjagat||85||

86tasyaiva tapasā jātaṃ naraṃ nāma mahāmunim
|
tulyametena devena taṃ jānīhyarjunaṃ sadā||86||

87tāvetau pūrvadevānāṃ paramopacitāvṛṣī
|
lokayātrāvidhānārthaṃ sañjayete yuge yuge||87||

88tathaiva karmaṇā kṛtsnaṃ mahatastapaso'pi ca
|
tejo manyuṃ ca bibhraṃstvaṃ jāto raudro mahāmate||88||

89sa bhavāndevavatprājño jñātvā bhavamayaṃ jagat
|
avākarṣastvamātmānaṃ niyamaistatpriyepsayā||89||

90śubhramatra haviḥ kṛtvā mahāpuruṣavigraham
|
ījivāṃstvaṃ japairhomairupahāraiśca mānada||90||

91sa tathā pūjyamānaste pūrvadehe'pyatūtuṣat
|
puṣkalāṃśca varānprādāttava vidvan hṛdi sthitān||91||

92janmakarmatapoyogāstayostava ca puṣkalāḥ
|
tābhyāṃ liṅge'rcito devastvayārcāyāṃ yuge yuge||92||

93sarvarūpaṃ bhavaṃ jñātvā liṅge yo'rcayati prabhum
|
ātmayogāśca tasmin vai śāstrayogāśca śāśvatāḥ||93||

94evaṃ devā yajanto hi siddhāśca paramarṣayaḥ
|
prārthayante paraṃ loke sthāṇumekaṃ sa sarvakṛt||94||

95sa eṣa rudrabhaktaśca keśavo rudrasaṃbhavaḥ
|
kṛṣṇa eva hi yaṣṭavyo yajñaiścaiva sanātanaḥ||95||

96sarvabhūtabhavaṃ jñātvā liṅgamarcati yaḥ prabhoḥ
|
tasminnabhyadhikāṃ prītiṃ karoti vṛṣabhadhvajaḥ||96||

97sañjaya uvāca|
tasya tadvacanaṃ śrutvā droṇaputro mahārathaḥ
|
namaścakāra rudrāya bahu mene ca keśavam||97||

98hṛṣṭaromā ca vaśyātmā so'bhivādya maharṣaye
|
varūthinīmabhiprekṣya hyavahāramakārayat||98||

99tataḥ pratyavahāro'bhūtpāṇḍavānāṃ viśāṃpate
|
kauravāṇāṃ ca dīnānāṃ droṇe yudhi nipātite||99||

100yuddhaṃ kṛtvā dinānpañca droṇo hatvā varūthinīm
|
brahmalokaṃ gato rājan brāhmaṇo veda‌pāragaḥ||100||
