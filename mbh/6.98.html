Mahābhārata 6.98 (Bhīṣmaparvan)

1sañjaya uvāca|
vākśalyaistava putreṇa so'tividdho mahāmanāḥ
|
duḥkhena mahatā''viṣṭo novācāpriyamaṇvapi ||1||

2sa dhyātvā suciraṃ kālaṃ duḥkharoṣasamanvitaḥ
|
śvasamāno yathā nāgaḥ praṇunno vākśalākayā ||2||

3uddhṛtya cakṣuṣī kopānnirdahanniva bhārata
|
sadevāsuragandharvaṃ lokaṃ lokavidāṃ varaḥ ||3||

4abravīt tava putraṃ sa sāmapūrvamidaṃ vacaḥ
|
kiṃ tvaṃ duryodhanaivaṃ māṃ vākśalyairapakṛntasi ||4||

5ghaṭapānaṃ yathāśakti kurvāṇaṃ ca tava priyam
|
juhvānaṃ samare prāṇāṃstava vai priyakāmyayā ||5||

6yadā tu pāṇḍavaḥ śūraḥ khāṇḍave'gnimatarpayat
|
parājitya raṇe śakraṃ paryāptaṃ tannidarśanam ||6||

7yadā ca tvāṃ mahābāho gandharvairhṛtamojasā
|
amocayatpāṇḍusutaḥ paryāptaṃ tannidarśanam ||7||

8dravamāṇeṣu śūreṣu sodareṣu tava prabho
|
sūtaputre ca rādheye paryāptaṃ tannidarśanam ||8||

9yacca naḥ sahitān sarvān virāṭanagare tadā
|
eka eva samudyātaḥ paryāptaṃ tannidarśanam ||9||

10droṇaṃ ca yudhi saṃrabdhaṃ māṃ ca nirjitya saṃyuge
|
vāsāṃsi sa samādatta paryāptaṃ tannidarśanam ||10||

11tathā drauṇiṃ maheṣvāsaṃ śāradvatamathāpi ca
|
gograhe jitavānpūrvaṃ paryāptaṃ tannidarśanam ||11||

12vijitya ca yadā karṇaṃ sadā puruṣamāninam
|
uttarāyai dadau vastraṃ paryāptaṃ tannirśanam ||12||

13nivātakavacān yuddhe vāsavenāpi durjayān
|
jitavān samare pārthaḥ paryāptaṃ tannidarśanam ||13||

14ko hi śakto raṇe jetuṃ pāṇḍavaṃ rabhasaṃ tadā
|
yasya goptā jagadgoptā śaṅkhacakragadādharaḥ ||14||

15vāsudevo'nantaśaktiḥ sṛṣṭisaṃhārakārakaḥ
|
sarveśvaro devadevaḥ paramātmā sanātanaḥ ||15||

16ukto'si bahuśo rājannāradādyairmaharṣibhiḥ
|
tvaṃ tu mohānna jānīṣe vācyāvācyaṃ suyodhana ||16||

17mumūrṣurhi naraḥ sarvān vṛkṣān paśyati kāñcanān
|
tathā tvamapi gāndhāre viparītāni paśyasi ||17||

18svayaṃ vairaṃ mahatkṛtvā pāṇḍavaiḥ saha sṛñjayaiḥ
|
yuddhyasva tānadya raṇe paśyāmaḥ puruṣo bhava ||18||

19ahaṃ tu somakān sarvān pañcālāṃśca samāgatān
|
nihaniṣye naravyāghra varjayitvā śikhaṇḍinam ||19||

20tairvā'haṃ nihataḥ saṃkhye gamiṣye yamasādanam
|
tān vā nihatya samare prītiṃ dāsyāmyahaṃ tava ||20||

21pūrvaṃ hi strī samutpannā śikhaṇḍī rājaveśmani
|
varadānāt pumān jātaḥ saiṣā vai strī śikhaṇḍinī ||21||

22tamahaṃ na haniṣyāmi prāṇatyāge'pi bhārata
|
yā'sau prāṅnirmitā dhātrā saiṣā vai strī śikhaṇḍinī ||22||

23sukhaṃ svapihi gāndhāre śvo'pi kartā mahāraṇam
|
yaṃ janāḥ kathayiṣyanti yāvatsthāsyati medinī ||23||

24evamuktastava suto nirjagāma janeśvara
|
abhivādya guruṃ mūrdhnā prayayau svaṃ niveśanam||24||

25āgamya tu tato rājā visṛjya ca mahājanam
|
praviveśa tatastūrṇaṃ kṣayaṃ śatrukṣayaṅkaraḥ ||25||

26praviṣṭaḥ sa niśāṃ tāṃ ca gamayāmāsa pārthiva
|
prabhātāyāṃ ca śarvaryāṃ prātarutthāya tānnṛpaḥ ||26||

27rājñaḥ samājñāpayata senāṃ yojayateti ha
|
adya bhīṣmo raṇe kruddho nihaniṣyati somakān ||27||

28duryodhanasya tacchrutvā rātrau vilapitaṃ bahu
|
manyamānaḥ sa taṃ rājan pratyādeśamivātmanaḥ ||28||

29nirvedaṃ paramaṃ gatvā vinindya paravaśyatām
|
dīrghaṃ dadhyau śāntanavo yoddhukāmo'rjunaṃ raṇe ||29||

30iṅgitena tu tajjñātvā gāṅgeyena vicintitam
|
duryodhano mahārāja duḥśāsanamacodayat ||30||

31duḥśāsana rathāstūrṇaṃ yujyantāṃ bhīṣmarakṣiṇaḥ
|
dvāviṃśatimanīkāni sarvāṇyevābhicodaya ||31||

32idaṃ hi samanuprāptaṃ varṣapūgābhicintitam
|
pāṇḍavānāṃ sasainyānāṃ vadho rājyasya cāgamaḥ ||32||

33tatra kāryatamaṃ manye bhīṣmasyaivābhirakṣaṇam
|
sa no guptaḥ sahāyaḥ syāddhanyāt pārthāṃśca saṃyuge ||33||

34abravīddhi viśuddhātmā nāhaṃ hanyāṃ śikhaṇḍinam
|
strīpūrvako hyasau rājaṃstasmādvarjyo mayā raṇe ||34||

35lokastadveda yadahaṃ pituḥ priyacikīrṣayā
|
rājyaṃ sphītaṃ mahābāho striyaśca tyaktavān purā ||35||

36naiva cāhaṃ striyaṃ jātu na strīpūrvaṃ kathañcana
|
hanyāṃ yudhi naraśreṣṭha satyametadbravīmi te ||36||

37ayaṃ strīpūrvako rājañchikhaṇḍī yadi te śrutaḥ
|
udyoge kathitaṃ yattattathā jātā śikhaṇḍinī ||37||

38kanyā bhūtvā pumān jātaḥ sa ca māṃ yodhayiṣyati
|
tasyāhaṃ pramukhe bāṇānnamuñceyaṃ kathañcana ||38||

39yuddhe hi kṣatriyāṃstāta pāṇḍavānāṃ jayaiṣiṇaḥ
|
sarvānanyān haniṣyāmi saṃprāptān raṇamūrdhani ||39||

40evaṃ māṃ bharataśreṣṭha gāṅgeyaḥ prāha śāstravit
|
tatra sarvātmanā manye gāṅgeyasyaiva pālanam ||40||

41arakṣyamāṇaṃ hi vṛko hanyāt siṃhaṃ mahāhave
|
mā vṛkeṇeva gāṅgeyaṃ ghātayema śikhaṇḍinā ||41||

42mātulaḥ śakuniḥ śalyaḥ kṛpo droṇo viviṃśatiḥ
|
yattā rakṣantu gāṅgeyaṃ tasmin gupte dhruvo jayaḥ ||42||

43etacchrutvā tu te sarve duryodhanavacastadā
|
sarvato rathavaṃśena gāṅgeyaṃ paryavārayan ||43||

44putrāśca tava gāṅgeyaṃ parivārya yayurmudā
|
kaṃpayanto bhuvaṃ dyāṃ ca kṣobhayantaśca pāṃḍavān ||44||

45te rathaiḥ suprasaṃyuktairdantibhiśca mahārathāḥ
|
parivārya raṇe bhīṣmaṃ daṃśitāḥ samavasthitāḥ ||45||

46yathā devāsure yuddhe tridaśā vajradhāriṇam
|
sarve te sma vyatiṣṭhanta rakṣantastaṃ mahāratham ||46||

47tato duryodhano rājā punarbhrātaramabravīt
|
savyaṃ cakraṃ yudhāmanyuruttamaujāśca dakṣiṇam ||47||

48goptārāvarjunasyaitāvarjuno'pi śikhaṇḍinaḥ
|
rakṣyamāṇaḥ sa pārthena tathā'smābhirvivarjitaḥ ||48||

49yathā bhīṣmaṃ na no hanyādduḥśāsana tathā kuru
|
bhrātustadvacanaṃ śrutvā putro duḥśāsanastava ||49||

50bhīṣmaṃ pramukhataḥ kṛtvā prayayau saha senayā
|
bhīṣmaṃ tu rathavaṃśena dṛṣṭvā samabhisaṃvṛtam ||50||

51arjuno rathināṃ śreṣṭho dhṛṣṭadyumnamuvāca ha
|
śikhaṇḍinaṃ naravyāghraṃ bhīṣmasya pramukhe nṛpa
|
sthāpayasvādya pāñcālya tasya goptāhamityuta ||51||
