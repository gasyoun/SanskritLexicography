Mahābhārata 5.107 (Udyogaparvan)

1nārada uvāca|
evamuktastadā tena viśvāmitreṇa dhīmatā
|
nāste na śete nāhāraṃ kurute gālavastadā ||1||

2tvagasthibhūto hariṇaścintā-śokaparāyaṇaḥ
|
śocamāno'timātraṃ sa dahyamānaśca manyunā
|
gālavo duḥkhito duḥkhādvilalāpa suyodhana ||2||

3kutaḥ puṣṭāni mitrāṇi kuto'rthāḥ sañcayaḥ kutaḥ
|
hayānāṃ candraśubhrāṇāṃ śatānyaṣṭau kuto mama ||3||

4kuto me bhojane śraddhā sukhaśraddhā kutaśca me
|
śraddhā me jīvitasyāpi chinnā kiṃ jīvitena me ||4||

5ahaṃ pāre samudrasya pṛthivyā vā paraṃparāt
|
gatvā''tmānaṃ vimuñcāmi kiṃ phalaṃ jīvitena me ||5||

6adhanasyākṛtārthasya tyaktasya vividhaiḥ phalaiḥ
|
ṛṇaṃ dhārayamāṇasya kutaḥ sukhamanīhayā ||6||

7suhṛdāṃ hi dhanaṃ bhuktvā kṛtvā praṇayamīpsitam
|
pratikartumaśaktasya jīvitānmaraṇaṃ varam ||7||

8pratiśrutya kariṣyeti kartavyaṃ tadakurvataḥ
|
mithyāvacanadagdhasya iṣṭāpūrtaṃ praṇaśyati ||8||

9na rūpamanṛtasyāsti nānṛtasyāsti santatiḥ
|
nānṛtasyādhipatyaṃ ca kuta eva gatiḥ śubhā ||9||

10kutaḥ kṛtaghnasya yaśaḥ kutaḥ sthānaṃ kutaḥ sukham
|
aśraddheyaḥ kṛtaghno hi kṛtaghne nāsti niṣkṛtiḥ ||10||

11na jīvatyadhanaḥ pāpaḥ kutaḥ pāpasya tantraṇam
|
pāpo dhruvamavāpnoti vināśaṃ nāśayan kṛtam ||11||

12so'haṃ pāpaḥ kṛtaghnaśca kṛpaṇaścānṛto'pi ca
|
guroryaḥ kṛtakāryaḥ saṃstatkaromi na bhāṣitam ||12||

13so'haṃ prāṇān vimokṣyāmi kṛtvā yatnamanuttamam
|
arthitā na mayā kācit kṛtapūrvā divaukasām ||13||

14mānayanti ca māṃ sarve tridaśā yajñasaṃstare
|
ahaṃ tu vibudhaśreṣṭhaṃ devaṃ tribhuvaneśvaram
|
viṣṇuṃ gacchāmyahaṃ kṛṣṇaṃ gatiṃ gatimatāṃ varam ||14||

15bhogā yasmāt pratiṣṭhante vyāpya sarvān surāsurān
|
praṇato draṣṭumicchāmi kṛṣṇaṃ yoginamavyayam ||15||

16evamukte sakhā tasya garuḍo vinatātmajaḥ
|
darśayāmāsa taṃ prāha saṃhṛṣṭaḥ priyakāmyayā ||16||

17suhṛdbhavānmama mataḥ suhṛdāṃ ca mataḥ suhṛt
|
īpsitenābhilāṣeṇa yoktavyo vibhave sati ||17||

18vibhavaścāsti me vipra vāsavāvarajo dvija
|
pūrvamuktastvadarthaṃ ca kṛtaḥ kāmaśca tena me ||18||

19sa bhavānetu gacchāva nayiṣye tvāṃ yathāsukham
|
deśaṃ pāraṃ pṛthivyā vā gaccha gālava mā ciram ||19||
