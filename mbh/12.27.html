Mahābhārata 12.27 (Śāntiparvan)

1yudhiṣṭhira uvāca|
abhimanyau hate bāle draupadyāstanayeṣu ca
|
dhṛṣṭadyumne virāṭe ca drupade ca mahīpatau ||1||

2vṛṣasene ca dharmajñe dhṛṣṭaketau tu pārthive
|
tathānyeṣu narendreṣu nānādeśyeṣu saṃyuge ||2||

3na ca muñcati māṃ śoko jñātighātinamāturam
|
rājyakāmukamatyugraṃ svavaṃśocchedakāriṇam ||3||

4yasyāṅke krīḍamānena mayā vai parivartitam
|
sa mayā rājyalubdhena gāṅgeyo yudhi pātitaḥ ||4||

5yadā hyenaṃ vighūrṇantamapaśyaṃ pārthasāyakaiḥ
|
kampamānaṃ yathā vajraiḥ prekṣyamāṇaṃ śikhaṇḍinā ||5||

6jīrṇasiṃhamiva prāṃśuṃ narasiṃhaṃ pitāmaham
|
kīryamāṇaṃ śarairdṛṣṭvā bhṛśaṃ me vyathitaṃ manaḥ ||6||

7prāṅmukhaṃ sīdamānaṃ ca rathe pararathārujam
|
ghūrṇamānaṃ yathā śailaṃ tadā me kaśmalo'bhavat ||7||

8yaḥ sa bāṇadhanuṣpāṇiryodhayāmāsa bhārgavam
|
bahūnyahāni kauravyaḥ kurukṣetre mahāmṛdhe ||8||

9sametaṃ pārthivaṃ kṣatraṃ vārāṇasyāṃ nadīsutaḥ
|
kanyārthamāhvayadvīro rathenaikena saṃyuge ||9||

10yena cogrāyudho rājā cakravartī durāsadaḥ
|
dagdhaścāstrapratāpena sa mayā yudhi ghātitaḥ ||10||

11svayaṃ mṛtyuṃ rakṣamāṇaḥ pāñcālyaṃ yaḥ śikhaṇḍinam
|
na bāṇaiḥ pātayāmāsa so'rjunena nipātitaḥ ||11||

12yadainaṃ patitaṃ bhūmāvapaśyaṃ rudhirokṣitam
|
tadaivāviśadatyugro jvaro māṃ munisattama ||12||

13yena saṃvardhitā bālā yena sma parirakṣitāḥ
|
sa mayā rājyalubdhena pāpena gurughātinā ||13||

14alpakālasya rājyasya kṛte mūḍhena ghātitaḥ
|
ācāryaśca maheṣvāsaḥ sarvapārthivapūjitaḥ
|
abhigamya raṇe mithyā pāpenoktaḥ sutaṃ prati ||14||

15tanme dahati gātrāṇi yanmāṃ gururabhāṣata
|
satyamākhyāhi rājaṃstvaṃ yadi jīvati me sutaḥ ||15||

16satyāmāmarṣayan vipro mayi tatparipṛṣṭavān
|
kuñjaraṃ cāntaraṃ kṛtvā mithyopacaritaṃ mayā ||16||

17subhṛśaṃ rājyalubdhena pāpena gurughātinā
|
satyakañcukamunmucya mayā sa gururāhave ||17||

18aśvatthāmā hata iti niruktaḥ kuñjare hate
|
kā~llokāṃstu gamiṣyāmi kṛtvā karma suduṣkaram ||18||

19aghātayaṃ ca yatkarṇaṃ samareṣvapalāyinam ||19||

20jyeṣṭhabhrātaramatyugraṃ ko mattaḥ pāpakṛttamaḥ
|
abhimanyuṃ ca yadbālaṃ jātaṃ siṃhamivādriṣu ||20||

21prāveśayamahaṃ lubdho vāhinīṃ droṇapālitām
|
tadāprabhṛti bībhatsuṃ na śaknomi nirīkṣitum ||21||

22kṛṣṇaṃ ca puṇḍarīkākṣaṃ kilbiṣī bhrūṇahā yathā
|
draupadīṃ cāpi duḥkhārtāṃ pañcaputrairvinākṛtām ||22||

23śocāmi pṛthivīṃ hīnāṃ pañcabhiḥ parvatairiva
|
so'hamāgaskaraḥ pāpaḥ pṛthivīnāśakārakaḥ ||23||

24āsīna evamevedaṃ śoṣayiṣye kalevaram
|
prāyopaviṣṭaṃ jānīdhvamatha māṃ gurughātinam ||24||

25jātiṣvanyāsvapi yathā na bhaveyaṃ kulāntakṛt
|
na bhokṣye na ca pānīyamupabhokṣye kathañcana ||25||

26śoṣayiṣye priyānprāṇānihastho'haṃ tapodhanāḥ
|
yatheṣṭaṃ gamyatāṃ kāmamanujāne prasādya vaḥ ||26||

27sarve māmanujānīta tyakṣyāmīdaṃ kalevaram
|
vaiśampāyana uvāca|
tamevaṃvādinaṃ pārthaṃ bandhuśokena vihvalam ||27||

28maivamityabravīdvyāso nigṛhya munisattamaḥ ||28||

29vyāsa uvāca |
ativelaṃ mahārāja na śokaṃ kartumarhasi
|
punaruktaṃ tu vakṣyāmi diṣṭametaditi prabho ||29||

30saṃyogā viprayogāntā jātānāṃ prāṇināṃ dhruvam
|
budbudā iva toyeṣu bhavanti na bhavanti ca ||30||

31sarve kṣayāntā nicayāḥ patanāntāḥ samucchrayāḥ
|
saṃyogā viprayogāntā maraṇāntaṃ hi jīvitam ||31||

32sukhaṃ duḥkhāntamālasyaṃ dākṣyaṃ duḥkhaṃ sukhodayam
|
bhūtiḥ śrīrhrīrdhṛtiḥ kīrtirdakṣe vasati nālase ||32||

33nālaṃ sukhāya suhṛdo nālaṃ duḥkhāya śatravaḥ
|
na ca prajālamarthebhyo na sukhebhyo'pyalaṃ dhanam ||33||

34yathā sṛṣṭo'si kaunteya dhātrā karmasu tatkuru
|
ata eva hi siddhiste neśastvaṃ karmaṇāṃ nṛpa ||34||
