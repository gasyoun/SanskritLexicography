Mahābhārata 12.122 (Śāntiparvan)

1bhīṣma uvāca|
atrāpyudāharantīpamitihāsaṃ purātanam
|
aṅgeṣu rājā dyutimānvasuhoma iti śrutaḥ||1||

2sa rājā dharmavinnityaṃ saha patnyā mahātapāḥ
|
muñjapṛṣṭhaṃ jagāmātha pitṛdevarṣipūjitam||2||

3tatra śṛṅge himavato merau kanakaparvate
|
yatra muñjāvaṭe rāmo jaṭāharaṇamādiśat||3||

4tadāprabhṛti rājendra ṛṣibhiḥ saṃśitavrataiḥ
|
mujapṛṣṭha iti proktaḥ sa deśo rudrasevitaḥ||4||

5sa tatra bahubhiryaktastadā śrutimayairguṇaiḥ
|
brāhmaṇānāmanumato devarṣisadṛśo'bhavat||5||

6taṃ kadācidadīnātmā sakhā śakrasya mānitaḥ
|
abhyagacchanmahīpālo māndhātā śatrukarśanaḥ||6||

7so'pasṛtya tu māndhātā vasuhomaṃ narādhipam
|
dṛṣṭvā prakṛṣṭatayasaṃ vineto'gre'bhyatiṣṭhata||7||

8vasuhomo'pi rājño vai pādyamarghyaṃ nyavedayat
|
saptāṅgasya tu rājyasya papraccha kuśalāvyaye||8||

9sadbhirācaritaṃ pūrvaṃ yathāvadanuyāyinam
|
apṛcchadvasuhomastaṃ rājankiṃ karavāṇi te||9||

10so'bravītparamaprīto māndhātā rājasattamam
|
vasuhomaṃ mahāprājñamāsīnaṃ kurunandana||10||

11māndhātovāca
|
bṛhaspatermataṃ rājannadhītaṃ sakalaṃ tvayā
|
tathaivośanasaṃ śāstraṃ vijñātaṃ te narottama||11||

12tadahaṃ jñātumicchāmi daṇḍa utpadyate katham
|
kiṃ cāsya pūrvaṃ jāgarti kiṃ vā paramamucyate||12||

13kathaṃ kṣatriyasaṃsthaśca daṇḍaḥ sampratyavasthitaḥ
|
brūhi me samuhāprājña dadāmyācāryavetanam||13||

14vasuhoma uvāca| śṛṇu rājanyathā daṇḍaḥ sambhūto lokasaṃgrahaḥ
|
prajāvinayarakṣārthaṃ dharmasyātmā sanātanaḥ||14||

15brahmā yiyakṣurbhagavānsarvalokapitāmahaḥ
|
ṛtvijaṃ nātmanastulyaṃ dadarśeti hi naḥ śrutam||15||

16sa garbhaṃ śirasā devo bahuvarṣāṇyadhārayat
|
pūrṇe varṣasahasre tu sa garbhaḥ kṣuvato'patat||16||

17sa kṣupo nāma sambhūtaḥ prajāpatirarindama
|
ṛtvigāsīnmahārāja yajñe tasya mahātmanaḥ||17||

18tasminpravṛtte satre tu brahmaṇaḥ pārthivarṣabha
|
dṛṣṭarūpapradhānatvāddaṇḍaḥ so'ntarhito'bhavat||18||

19tasminnantarhite cāpi prajānāṃ saṃkaro'bhavat
|
naiva kāryaṃ na vākāryaṃ bhojyābhojyaṃ na vidyate||19||

20peyāpeye kutaḥ siddhirhiṃsanti ca parasparam
|
gamyāgamyaṃ tadā nāsīt svaṃ parasvaṃ ca vai samam||20||

21parasparaṃ vilumpanti sārameyā yathāmiṣam
|
abalānbalino ghnanti nirmaryādamavartata||21||

22tataḥ pitāmaho viṣṇuṃ bhagavantaṃ sanātanam
|
sampūjya varadaṃ devaṃ mahādevamathābravīt||22||

23atra tvamanukampāṃ vai kartumarhasi śaṃkara
|
saṃkaro na bhavedatra yathā tadvai vidhīyatām||23||

24tataḥ sa bhagavāndhyātvā ciraṃ śūlavarāyudhaḥ
|
ātmānamātmanā daṇḍaṃ sasṛje devasattamaḥ||24||

25tasmācca dharmacaraṇānnītirdevī sarasvatī
|
sasṛje daṇḍanītiṃ sā triṣu lokeṣu viśrutā||25||

26bhūyaḥ sa bhagavāndhyātvā ciraṃ śūlavarāyudhaḥ
|
tasya tasya nikāyasya cakāraikaikamīśvaram||26||

27devānāmīśvaraṃ cakre devaṃ daśaśatekṣaṇam
|
yamaṃ vaivasvataṃ cāpi pitṝṇāmakarotprabhum||27||

28dhanānāṃ rākṣasānāṃ ca kuberamapi ceśvaram
|
parvatānāṃ patiṃ meruṃ saritāṃ ca mahodadhim||28||

29arpā rājye surāṇāṃ ca vidadhe varuṇaṃ prabhum
|
mṛtyuṃ prāṇeśvaramatho tejasāṃ ca hutāśanam||29||

30rudrāṇāmapi ceśānaṃ goptāraṃ vidadhe prabhum
|
mahātmānaṃ mahādevaṃ viśalākṣaṃ sanātanam||30||

31vasiṣṭhamīśaṃ viprāṇāṃ vasūnāṃ jātavedasam
|
tejasā bhāskaraṃ cakre nakṣatrāṇāṃ niśākaram||31||

32vīrudhāmaṃśumantaṃ ca bhūtānāṃ ca prabhuṃ varam
|
kumāraṃ dvādaśabhujaṃ skandaṃ rājānamādiśat||32||

33kālaṃ sarveśamakarotsaṃhāravinayātmakam
|
mṛtyoścaturvibhāgasya duḥkhasya ca sukhasya ca||33||

34īśvaraḥ sarvadevastu rājarājo narādhipaḥ
|
sarveṣāmeva rudrāṇāṃ śūlapāṇiriti śrutiḥ||34||

35tamenaṃ brahmaṇaḥ putramanujātaṃ kṣupaṃ dadau
|
prajānāmadhipaṃ śreṣṭhaṃ sarvadharmabhṛtāmapi||35||

36mahādevastatastasminvṛtte yajñe yathāvidhi
|
daṇḍaṃ dharmasya goptāraṃ viṣṇave satkṛtaṃ dadau||36||

37viṣṇuraṅgirase prādādaṅgirā munisattamaḥ
|
prādādindramarīcibhyāṃ marīcirbhṛgave dadau||37||

38bhṛgurdadāvṛṣibhyastu daṇḍaṃ dharmasamāhitam
|
ṛṣayo lokapālebhyo lokapālāḥ kṣupāya ca||38||

39kṣupastu manave prādādādityatanayāyaṃ ca
|
putrebhyaḥ śrāddhadevastu sūkṣmadharmārthakāraṇāt||39||

40vibhajya daṇḍaḥ kartavyo dharmeṇa na yadṛcchayā
|
duṣṭānāṃ nigraho daṇḍo hiraṇyaṃ bāhyataḥ kriyā||40||

41vyaṅgatvaṃ ca śarīrasya vadho nālpasya kāraṇāt
|
śarīrapīḍāstāstāśca dehatyāgo vivāsanam||41||

42taṃ dadau sūryaputrastu manurvai rakṣaṇārthakam
|
ānupūrvyācca daṇḍo'yaṃ prajā jāgarti pālayan||42||

43indro jāgarti bhagavānindrādagnirvibhāvasuḥ
|
agnerjāgarti varuṇo varuṇācca prajāpatiḥ||43||

44prajāpatestato dharmo jāgarti vinayātmakaḥ
|
dharmācca brahmaṇaḥ putro vyavasāyaḥ sanātanaḥ||44||

45vyavasāyāttatastejo jāgarti paripālayat
|
oṣadhyastejasastasmādoṣadhībhyaśca parvatāḥ||45||

46parvatebhyaśca jāgarti raso rasaguṇāttathā
|
jāgarti nirṛtirdevī jyotīṃṣi nirṛterapi||46||

47vedāḥ pratiṣṭhāḥ jyotirbhyastato hayaśirāḥ prabhuḥ
|
brahmā pitāmahastasmājjāgarti prabhuravyayaḥ||47||

48pitāmahānmahādevo jāgarti bhagavānśivaḥ
|
viśvedevāḥ śivāccāpi viśvebhyaśca tatharṣayaḥ||48||

49ṛṣibhyo bhagavānsomaḥ somāddevāḥ sanātanāḥ
|
devebhyo brāhmaṇā loke jāgratītyupadhāraya||49||

50brāhmaṇebhyaśca rājanyā lokān rakṣanti dharmataḥ
|
sthāvaraṃ jaṅgamaṃ caiva kṣatriyebhyaḥ sanātanam||50||

51prajā jāgarti loke'smin daṇḍo jāgarti tāsu ca
|
sarvaṃ saṃkṣipate daṇḍaḥ pitāmahasamaprabhaḥ||51||

52jāgarti kālaḥ pūrvaṃ ca madhye cānte ca bhārata
|
īśvaraḥ sarvalokasya mahādevaḥ prajāpatiḥ||52||

53devadevaḥ śivaḥ sarvo jāgarti satataṃ prabhuḥ
|
kapardī śaṅkaro rudraḥ śivaḥ sthāṇurumāpatiḥ||53||

54ityeṣa daṇḍo vikhyāta ādau madhye tathāvare
|
bhūmipālo yathānyāyaṃ vartetānena dharmavit||54||

55bhīṣma uvāca|
itīdaṃ vasuhomasya śṛṇuyādyo mataṃ naraḥ
|
śrutvā samyakpravarteta sarvānkāmānavāpnuyāt||55||

56iti te sarvamākhyātaṃ yo daṇḍo manujarṣabha
|
niyantā sarvalokasya dharmākrāntasya bhārata||56||
