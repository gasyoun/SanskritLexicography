Mahābhārata 3.9 (Āraṇyakaparvan)

1dhṛtarāṣṭra uvāca|
bhagavannāhamapyetadrocaye dyūtasaṃbhavam
|
manye tadvidhinākṛṣya kārito'smīti vai mune||1||

2naitadrocayate bhīṣmo na droṇo viduro na ca
|
gāndhārī necchati dyūtaṃ tatra mohātpravartitam||2||

3parityaktuṃ na śaknomi duryodhanamacetanam
|
putrasnehena bhagavañjānannapi priyavrata||3||

4vyāsa uvāca|
vaicitravīryanṛpate satyamāha yathā bhavān
|
dṛḍhaṃ vidmaḥ paraṃ putraṃ paraṃ putrānna vidyate||4||

5indro'pyaśrunipātena surabhyā pratibodhitaḥ
|
anyaiḥ samṛddhairapyarthairna sutānmanyate param||5||

6atra te vartayiṣyāmi mahadākhyānamuttamam
|
surabhyāścaiva saṃvādamindrasya ca viśāṃpate||6||

7triviṣṭapagatā rājansurabhī prārudatkila
|
gavāṃ mātā purā tāta tāmindro'nvakṛpāyata||7||

8indra uvāca|
kimidaṃ rodiṣi śubhe kaccitkṣemaṃ divaukasām
|
manuṣyeṣvatha nāgeṣu naitadalpaṃ bhaviṣyati||8||

9surabhiruvāca|
vinipāto na te kaściddṛśyate tridaśādhipa
|
ahaṃ tu putraṃ śocāmi tena rodimi kauśika||9||

10paśyainaṃ karṣakaṃ kṣudraṃ durbalaṃ mama putrakam
|
pratodenābhinighnantaṃ lāṅgalena ca pīḍitam||10||

11niṣīdamānaṃ sotkaṇṭhaṃ vadhyamānaṃ surādhipa
|
kṛpāviṣṭāsmi devendra manaścodvijate mama
|
ekastatra balopeto dhuramudvahate'dhikām||11||

12aparopyabalaprāṇaḥ kṛśo dhamanisantataḥ
|
kṛcchrādudvahate bhāraṃ taṃ vai śocāmi vāsava||12||

13vadhyamānaḥ pratodena tudyamānaḥ punaḥ punaḥ
|
naiva śaknoti taṃ bhāramudvoḍhuṃ paśya vāsava||13||

14tato'haṃ tasya śokārtā viraumi bhṛśaduḥkhitā
|
aśrūṇyāvartayantī ca netrābhyāṃ karuṇāyatī||14||

15śakra uvāca|
tava putrasahasreṣu pīḍyamāneṣu śobhane
|
kiṃ kṛpāyitavatyatra putra ekatra hanyati||15||

16surabhiruvāca|
yadi putrasahasrāṇi sarvatra samataiva me
|
dīnasya tu sataḥ śakra putrasyābhyadhikā kṛpā||16||

17vyāsa uvāca|
tadindraḥ surabhīvākyaṃ niśamya bhṛśavismitaḥ
|
jīvitenāpi kauravya mene'bhyadhikamātmajam||17||

18pravavarṣa ca tatraiva sahasā toyamulbaṇam
|
karṣakasyācaranvighnaṃ bhagavānpākaśāsanaḥ||18||

19tadyathā surabhiḥ prāha samavetāstu te tathā
|
suteṣu rājansarveṣu hīneṣvabhyadhikā kṛpā||19||

20yādṛśo me sutaḥ pāṇḍustādṛśo me'si putraka
|
viduraśca mahāprājñaḥ snehādetadbravīmyaham||20||

21cirāya tava putrāṇāṃ śatamekaśca bhārata
|
pāṇḍoḥ pañcaiva lakṣyante te'pi mandāḥ suduḥkhitāḥ||21||

22kathaṃ jīveyuratyantaṃ kathaṃ vardheyurityapi
|
iti dīneṣu pārtheṣu mano me paritapyate||22||

23yadi pārthiva kauravyāñjīvamānānihecchasi
|
duryodhanastava sutaḥ śamaṃ gacchatu pāṇḍavaiḥ||23||
