Mahābhārata 6.30 (Bhīṣmaparvan)

1anāśritaḥ karmaphalaṃ kāryaṃ karma karoti yaḥ
|
sa saṃnyāsī ca yogī ca na niragnirna cākriyaḥ ||1||

2yaṃ saṃnyāsamiti prāhuryogaṃ taṃ viddhi pāṇḍava
|
na hyasaṃnyastasaṅkalpo yogī bhavati kaścana ||2||

3ārurukṣormuneryogaṃ karma kāraṇamucyate
|
yogārūḍhasya tasyaiva śamaḥ kāraṇamucyate ||3||

4yadā hi nendriyārtheṣu na karmasvanuṣajjate
|
sarvasaṅkalpasaṃnyāsī yogārūḍhastadocyate ||4||

5uddharedātmanātmānaṃ nātmānamavasādayet
|
ātmaiva hyātmano bandhurātmaiva ripurātmanaḥ||5||

6bandhurātmātmanastasya yenātmaivātmanā jitaḥ
|
anātmanastu śatrutve vartetātmaiva śatruvat||6||

7jitātmanaḥ praśāntasya paramātmā samāhitaḥ
|
śītoṣṇasukhaduḥkheṣu tathā mānāpamānayoḥ||7||

8jñānavijñānatṛptātmā kūṭastho vijitendriyaḥ
|
yukta ityucyate yogī samaloṣṭāśmakāñcanaḥ||8||

9suhṛnmitrāryudāsīnamadhyasthadveṣyabandhuṣu
|
sādhuṣvapi ca pāpeṣu samabuddhirviśiṣyate||9||

10yogī yuñjīta satatamātmānaṃ rahasi sthitaḥ
|
ekākī yatacittātmā nirāśīraparigrahaḥ||10||

11śucau deśe pratiṣṭhāpya sthiramāsanamātmanaḥ
|
nātyucchritaṃ nātinīcaṃ cailājinakuśottaram||11||

12tatraikāgraṃ manaḥ kṛtvā yatacittendriyakriyaḥ
|
upaviśyāsane yuñjyādyogamātmaviśuddhaye||12||

13samaṃ kāyaśirogrīvaṃ dhārayannacalaṃ sthiraḥ
|
samprekṣya nāsikāgraṃ svaṃ diśaścānavalokayan||13||

14praśāntātmā vigatabhīrbrahmacārivrate sthitaḥ
|
manaḥ saṃyamya maccitto yukta āsīta matparaḥ||14||

15yuñjannevaṃ sadātmānaṃ yogī niyatamānasaḥ
|
śāntiṃ nirvāṇaparamāṃ matsaṃsthāmadhigacchati||15||

16nātyaśnatastu yogo'sti na caikāntamanaśnataḥ
|
na cātisvapnaśīlasya jāgrato naiva cārjuna||16||

17yuktāhāravihārasya yuktaceṣṭasya karmasu
|
yuktasvapnāvabodhasya yogo bhavati duḥkhahā||17||

18yadā viniyataṃ cittamātmanyevāvatiṣṭhate
|
niḥspṛhaḥ sarvakāmebhyo yukta ityucyate tadā||18||

19yadā dīpo nivātastho neṅgate sopamā smṛtā
|
yogino yatacittasya yuñjato yogamātmanaḥ||19||

20yatroparamate cittaṃ niruddhaṃ yogasevayā
|
yatra caivātmanātmānaṃ paśyannātmani tuṣyati||20||

21sukhamātyantikaṃ yattadbuddhigrāhyamatīndriyam
|
vetti yatra na caivāyaṃ sthitaścalati tattvataḥ||21||

22yaṃ labdhvā cāparaṃ lābhaṃ manyate nādhikaṃ tataḥ
|
yasminsthito na duḥkhena guruṇāpi vicālyate||22||

23taṃ vidyādduḥkhasaṃyogaviyogaṃ yogasaṃjñitam
|
sa niścayena yoktavyo yogo'nirviṇṇacetasā||23||

24saṅkalpaprabhavānkāmāṃstyaktvā sarvānaśeṣataḥ
|
manasaivendriyagrāmaṃ viniyamya samantataḥ||24||

25śanaiḥ śanairuparamedbuddhyā dhṛtigṛhītayā
|
ātmasaṃsthaṃ manaḥ kṛtvā na kiñcidapi cintayet||25||

26yato yato niścarati manaścañcalamasthiram
|
tatastato niyamyaitadātmanyeva vaśaṃ nayet||26||

27praśāntamanasaṃ hyenaṃ yoginaṃ sukhamuttamam
|
upaiti śāntarajasaṃ brahmabhūtamakalmaṣam||27||

28yuñjannevaṃ sadātmānaṃ yogī vigatakalmaṣaḥ
|
sukhena brahmasaṃsparśamatyantaṃ sukhamaśnute||28||

29sarvabhūtasthamātmānaṃ sarvabhūtāni cātmani
|
īkṣate yogayuktātmā sarvatra samadarśanaḥ||29||

30yo māṃ paśyati sarvatra sarvaṃ ca mayi paśyati
|
tasyāhaṃ na praṇaśyāmi sa ca me na praṇaśyati||30||

31sarvabhūtasthitaṃ yo māṃ bhajatyekatvamāsthitaḥ
|
sarvathā vartamāno'pi sa yogī mayi vartate||31||

32ātmaupamyena sarvatra samaṃ paśyati yo'rjuna
|
sukhaṃ vā yadi vā duḥkhaṃ sa yogī paramo mataḥ||32||

33arjuna uvāca —
yo'yaṃ yogastvayā proktaḥ sāmyena madhusūdana
|
etasyāhaṃ na paśyāmi cañcalatvātsthitiṃ sthirām ||33||

34cañcalaṃ hi manaḥ kṛṣṇa pramāthi balavaddṛḍham
|
tasyāhaṃ nigrahaṃ manye vāyoriva suduṣkaram||34||

35śrībhagavānuvāca —
asaṃśayaṃ mahābāho mano durnigrahaṃ calam
|
abhyāsena tu kaunteya vairāgyeṇa ca gṛhyate||35||

36asaṃyatātmanā yogo duṣprāpa iti me matiḥ
|
vaśyātmanā tu yatatā śakyo'vāptumupāyataḥ||36||

37arjuna uvāca —
ayatiḥ śraddhayopeto yogāccalitamānasaḥ
|
aprāpya yogasaṃsiddhiṃ kāṃ gatiṃ kṛṣṇa gacchati||37||

38kaccinnobhayavibhraṣṭaśchinnābhramiva naśyati
|
apratiṣṭho mahābāho vimūḍho brahmaṇaḥ pathi||38||

39etanme saṃśayaṃ kṛṣṇa cchettumarhasyaśeṣataḥ
|
tvadanyaḥ saṃśayasyāsya cchettā na hyupapadyate||39||

40śrībhagavānuvāca —
pārtha naiveha nāmutra vināśastasya vidyate
|
na hi kalyāṇakṛtkaściddurgatiṃ tāta gacchati||40||

41prāpya puṇyakṛtāṃ lokānuṣitvā śāśvatīḥ samāḥ
|
śucīnāṃ śrīmatāṃ gehe yogabhraṣṭo'bhijāyate||41||

42athavā yogināmeva kule bhavati dhīmatām
|
etaddhi durlabhataraṃ loke janma yadīdṛśam||42||

43tatra taṃ buddhisaṃyogaṃ labhate paurvadehikam
|
yatate ca tato bhūyaḥ saṃsiddhau kurunandana||43||

44pūrvābhyāsena tenaiva hriyate hyavaśo'pi saḥ
|
jijñāsurapi yogasya śabdabrahmātivartate||44||

45prayatnādyatamānastu yogī saṃśuddhakilbiṣaḥ
|
anekajanmasaṃsiddhastato yāti parāṃ gatim||45||

46tapasvibhyo'dhiko yogī jñānibhyo'pi mato'dhikaḥ
|
karmibhyaścādhiko yogī tasmādyogī bhavārjuna ||46||

47yogināmapi sarveṣāṃ madgatenāntarātmanā
|
śraddhāvānbhajate yo māṃ sa me yuktatamo mataḥ||47||
