Mahābhārata 12.248 (Śāntiparvan)

1vyāsa uvāca |
mano visṛjate bhāvaṃ buddhiradhyavasāyinī
|
hṛdayaṃ priyāpriye veda trividhā karmacodanā ||1||

2indriyebhyaḥ parā hyarthā arthebhyaḥ paramaṃ manaḥ
|
manasastu parā buddhirbuddherātmā paro mataḥ ||2||

3buddhirātmā manuṣyasya buddhirevātmanā''tmani
|
yadā vikurute bhāvaṃ tadā bhavati sā manaḥ ||3||

4indriyāṇāṃ pṛthagbhāvādbuddhirvikriyate hyataḥ
|
śṛṇvatī bhavati śrotraṃ spṛśatī sparśa ucyate ||4||

5paśyatī bhavate dṛṣṭī rasatī rasanaṃ bhavet
|
jighratī bhavati ghrāṇaṃ buddhirvikriyate pṛthak ||5||

6indriyāṇi tu tānyāhusteṣvadṛśyo'dhitiṣṭhati
|
tiṣṭhatī puruṣe buddhistriṣu bhāveṣu vartate ||6||

7kadācillabhate prītiṃ kadācidapi śocati
|
na sukhena na duḥkhena kadācidiha yujyate ||7||

8seyaṃ bhāvātmikā bhāvāṃstrīnetānativartate
|
saritāṃ sāgaro bhartā mahāvelāmivormimān ||8||

9yadā prārthayate kiṃcittadā bhavati sā manaḥ
|
adhiṣṭhānāni vai buddhyāṃ pṛthagetāni saṃsmaret
|
indriyāṇyeva medhyāni vijetavyāni kṛtsnaśaḥ ||9||

10sarvāṇyevānupūrvyeṇa yadyadānuvidhīyate
|
avibhāgagatā buddhirbhāve manasi vartate ||10||

11ye caiva bhāvā vartante sarva eṣveva te triṣu
|
anvarthāḥ sampravartante rathanemimarā iva ||11||

12pradīpārthaṃ manaḥ kuryādindriyairbuddhisattamaiḥ
|
niścaradbhiryathāyogamudāsīnairyadṛcchayā ||12||

13evaṃsvabhāvamevedamiti vidvānna muhyati
|
aśocannaprahṛṣyanhi nityaṃ vigatamatsaraḥ ||13||

14na cātmā śakyate draṣṭumindriyaiḥ kāmagocaraiḥ
|
pravartamānairanaghairduṣkarairakṛtātmabhiḥ ||14||

15teṣāṃ tu manasā raśmīn yadā samyaṅniyacchati
|
tadā prakāśate'syātmā dīpadīptā yathā''kṛtiḥ ||15||

16sarveṣāmeva bhūtānāṃ tamasyapagate yathā
|
prakāśaṃ bhavate sarvaṃ tathedamupadhāryatām ||16||

17yathā vāricaraḥ pakṣī na lipyati jale caran
|
vimuktātmā tathā yogī guṇadoṣairna lipyate ||17||

18evameva kṛtaprajño na doṣairviṣayāṃścaran
|
asajjamānaḥ sarveṣu kathaṃcana na lipyate ||18||

19tyaktvā pūrvakṛtaṃ karma ratiryasya sadātmani
|
sarvabhūtātmabhūtasya guṇavargeṣvasajjataḥ ||19||

20sattvamātmā prasarati guṇānvāpi kadācana
|
na guṇā vidurātmānaṃ guṇānveda sa sarvadā ||20||

21paridraṣṭā guṇānāṃ ca parisraṣṭā yathātatham
|
sattvakṣetrajñayoretadantaraṃ viddhi sūkṣmayoḥ ||21||

22sṛjate'tra guṇāneka eko na sṛjate guṇān
|
pṛthagbhūtau prakṛtyā tau samprayuktau ca sarvadā ||22||

23yathā matsyo'dbhiranyaḥ syātsamprayuktau tathaiva tau
|
maśakodumbarau vā'pi samprayuktau yathā saha ||23||

24iṣīkā vā yathā muñje pṛthak ca saha caiva ca
|
tathaiva sahitāvetāvanyo'nyasminpratiṣṭhitau ||24||
