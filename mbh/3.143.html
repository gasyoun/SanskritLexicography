Mahābhārata 3.143 (Āraṇyakaparvan)

1vaiśaṃpāyana uvāca|
te śūrāstatadhanvānastūṇavantaḥ samārgaṇāḥ
|
baddhagodhāṅgulitrāṇāḥ khaḍgavanto'mitaujasaḥ||1||

2parigṛhya dvijaśreṣṭhāñjyeṣṭhāḥ sarvadhanuṣmatām
|
pāñcālīsahitā rājanprayayurgandhamādanam||2||

3sarāṃsi saritaścaiva parvatāṃśca vanāni ca
|
vṛkṣāṃśca bahulacchāyāndadṛśurgirimūrdhani||3||

4nityapuṣpaphalāndeśāndevarṣigaṇasevitān
|
ātmanyātmānamādhāya vīrā mūlaphalāśinaḥ||4||

5ceruruccāvacākārāndeśānviṣamasaṃkaṭān
|
paśyanto mṛgajātāni bahūni vividhāni ca||5||

6ṛṣisiddhāmarayutaṃ gandharvāpsarasāṃ priyam
|
viviśuste mahātmānaḥ kinnarācaritaṃ girim||6||

7praviśatsvatha vīreṣu parvataṃ gandhamādanam
|
caṇḍavātaṃ mahadvarṣaṃ prādurāsīdviśāṃpate||7||

8tato reṇuḥ samudbhūtaḥ sapatrabahulo mahān
|
pṛthivīṃ cāntarikṣaṃ ca dyāṃ caiva sahasāvṛṇot||8||

9na sma prajñāyate kiṃcidāvṛte vyomni reṇunā
|
na cāpi śekustatkartumanyonyasyābhibhāṣaṇam||9||

10na cāpaśyaṃstato'nyonyaṃ tamasāvṛtacakṣuṣaḥ
|
ākṛṣyamāṇā vātena sāśmacūrṇena bhārata||10||

11drumāṇāṃ vātabhagnānāṃ patatāṃ bhūtale'niśam
|
anyeṣāṃ ca mahījānāṃ śabdaḥ samabhavanmahān||11||

12dyauḥ svitpatati kiṃ bhūmirdīryate parvato nu kim
|
iti te menire sarve pavanenāpi mohitāḥ||12||

13te pathānantarānvṛkṣānvalmīkānviṣamāṇi ca
|
pāṇibhiḥ parimārganto bhītā vāyornililyire||13||

14tataḥ kārmukamādāya bhīmaseno mahābalaḥ
|
kṛṣṇāmādāya saṃgamya tasthāvāśritya pādapam||14||

15dharmarājaśca dhaumyaśca nililyāte mahāvane
|
agnihotrāṇyupādāya sahadevastu parvate||15||

16nakulo brāhmaṇāścānye lomaśaśca mahātapāḥ
|
vṛkṣānāsādya saṃtrastāstatra tatra nililyire||16||

17mandībhūte tu pavane tasminrajasi śāmyati
|
mahadbhirjaladhāraughairvarṣamabhyājagāma ha||17||

18bhṛśaṃ caṭacaṭāśabdo vajrāṇāṃ kṣipyatāmiva
|
tatastāścañcalābhāsaścerurabhreṣu vidyutaḥ||18||

19tato'śmasahitā dhārāḥ saṃvṛṇvantyaḥ samantataḥ
|
prapeturaniśaṃ tatra śīghravātasamīritāḥ||19||

20tatra sāgaragā hyāpaḥ kīryamāṇāḥ samantataḥ
|
prādurāsansakaluṣāḥ phenavantyo viśāṃpate||20||

21vahantyo vāri bahulaṃ phenoḍupapariplutam
|
parisasrurmahāśabdāḥ prakarṣantyo mahīruhān||21||

22tasminnuparate śabde vāte ca samatāṃ gate
|
gate hyambhasi nimnāni prādurbhūte divākare||22||

23nirjagmuste śanaiḥ sarve samājagmuśca bhārata
|
pratasthire punarvīrāḥ parvataṃ gandhamādanam||23||
