Mahābhārata 5.190 (Udyogaparvan)

1bhīṣma uvāca|
evamuktasya dūtena drupadasya tadā nṛpa
|
corasyeva gṛhītasya na prāvartata bhāratī ||1||

2sa yatnamakarottīvraṃ sambandhinyanumānane
|
dūtairmadhurasaṃbhāṣairna tadastīti saṃdiśan ||2||

3sa rājā bhūya evātha jñātvā tattvamathāgamat
|
kanyeti pāñcālasutāṃ tvaramāṇo viniryayau ||3||

4tataḥ saṃpreṣayāmāsa mitrāṇāmamitaujasām
|
duhiturvipralambhaṃ taṃ dhātrīṇāṃ vacanāttadā ||4||

5tataḥ samudayaṃ kṛtvā balānāṃ rājasattamaḥ
|
abhiyāne matiṃ cakre drupadaṃ prati bhārata ||5||

6tataḥ saṃmantrayāmāsa mantribhiḥ sa mahīpatiḥ
|
hiraṇyavarmā rājendra pāñcālyaṃ pārthivaṃ prati ||6||

7tatra vai niścitaṃ teṣāmabhūdrājñāṃ mahātmanām
|
tathyaṃ bhavati cedetat kanyā rājan śikhaṇḍinī ||7||

8baddhvā pañcālarājānamānayiṣyāmahe gṛham
|
anyaṃ rājānamādhāya pañcāleṣu nareśvaram ||8||

9ghātayiṣyāma nṛpatiṃ pāñcālaṃ saśikhaṇḍinam ||9||

10tattadā'nṛtamājñāya punardūtānnarādhipaḥ
|
prāsthāpayat pārṣatāya nihanmīti sthiro bhava ||10||

11bhīṣma uvāca|
sa hi prakṛtyā vai bhītaḥ kilbiṣī ca narādhipaḥ
|
bhayaṃ tīvramanuprāpto drupadaḥ pṛthivīpatiḥ ||11||

12visṛjya dūtān dāśārṇe drupadaḥ śokamūrcchitaḥ
|
sametya bhāryāṃ rahite vākyamāha narādhipaḥ ||12||

13bhayena mahatā''viṣṭo hṛdi śokena cāhataḥ
|
pāñcālarājo dayitāṃ mātaraṃ vai śikhaṇḍinaḥ ||13||

14abhiyāsyati māṃ kopāt sambandhī sumahābalaḥ
|
hiraṇyavarmā nṛpatiḥ karṣamāṇo varūthinīm ||14||

15kimidānīṃ kariṣyāvo mūḍhau kanyāmimāṃ prati
|
śikhaṇḍī kila putraste kanyeti pariśaṅkitaḥ ||15||

16iti sañcintya yatnena samitraḥ sabalānugaḥ
|
vañcito'smīti manvāno māṃ kiloddhartumicchati ||16||

17kimatra tathyaṃ suśroṇi mithyā kiṃ brūhi śobhane
|
śrutvā tvattaḥ śubhaṃ vākyaṃ saṃvidhāsyāmyahaṃ tathā ||17||

18ahaṃ hi saṃśayaṃ prāpto bālā ceyaṃ śikhaṇḍinī
|
tvaṃ ca rājñi mahat kṛcchraṃ saṃprāptā varavarṇini ||18||

19sā tvaṃ sarvavimokṣāya tattvamākhyāhi pṛcchataḥ
|
tathā vidadhyāṃ suśroṇi kṛtyamāśu śucismite ||19||

20śikhaṇḍini ca mā bhaistvaṃ vidhāsye tatra tattvataḥ
|
kṛpayā'haṃ varārohe vañcitaḥ putradharmataḥ ||20||

21mayā dāśārṇako rājā vañcitaḥ sa mahīpatiḥ
|
tadācakṣva mahābhāge vidhāsye tatra yaddhitam ||21||

22jānatā hi narendreṇa khyāpanārthaṃ parasya vai
|
prakāśaṃ coditā devī pratyuvāca mahīpatim ||22||
