Mahābhārata 5.45 (Udyogaparvan)

1sanatsujāta uvāca|
śokaḥ krodhaśca lobhaśca kāmo mānaḥ parāsutā
|
īrṣyā moho vidhitsā cākṛpā'sūyā jugupsutā ||1||

2dvādaśaite mahādoṣā manuṣyaprāṇanāśanāḥ
|
ekaikamete rājendra manuṣyān paryupāsate
|
yaurāviṣṭo naraḥ pāpaṃ mūḍhasaṃjño vyavasyati ||2||

3spṛhayālurugraḥ puruṣo vā vadānyaḥ krodhaṃ bibhran manasā vai vikatthī
|
nṛśaṃsadharmāḥ ṣaḍime janā vai prāpyāpyarthaṃ nota sabhājayante ||3||

4saṃbhogasaṃvidviṣamo'timānī dattvā vikatthī kṛpaṇo durbalaśca
|
bahupraśaṃkasī vanitādviṭ sadaiva saptaivoktāḥ pāpaśīlā nṛśaṃsāḥ ||4||

5dharmaśca satyaṃ ca tapo damaśca amātsaryaṃ hrīstitikṣā'nasūyā
|
dānaṃ śrutaṃ caiva dhṛtiḥ kṣamā ca mahāvratā dvādaśa brāhmaṇasya ||5||

6yo naitebhyaḥ pracyaveddvādaśabhyaḥ sarvāmapīmāṃ pṛthivīṃ sa śiṣyāt
|
tribhirdvābhyāmekato vā'rthito yo nāsya svamastīti ca veditavyam ||6||

7damastyāgo'thāpramāda ityeteṣvamṛtaṃ sthitam
|
etāni brahmamukhyānāṃ brāhmaṇānāṃ manīṣiṇām ||7||

8sadvā'sadvā parīvāde brāhmaṇasya na śasyate
|
narakapratiṣṭhāste vai syurya evaṃ kurute janāḥ ||8||

9mado'ṣṭādaśadoṣaḥ sa syāt purā yo'prakīrtitaḥ
|
lokadveṣyaṃ pratikūlyamabhyasūyā mṛṣā vacaḥ ||9||

10kāmakrodhau pāratantryaṃ parivādo'tha paiśunam
|
arthahānirvivādaśca mātsaryaṃ prāṇipīḍanam ||10||

11īrṣyā modo'tivādaśca saṃjñānāśo'bhyasūyitā
|
tasmāt prājño na mādyeta sadā hyetadvigarhitam ||11||

12sauhṛde vai ṣaḍguṇā veditavyāḥ priye hṛṣyanyapriye ca vyathante
|
syādātmanaḥ suciraṃ yācate yo dadātyayācyamapi deyaṃ khalu syāt
|
iṣṭān putrān vibhavān svāṃśca dārānabhyarthitaścārhati śuddhabhāvaḥ ||12||

13tyaktadravyaḥ saṃvasenneha kāmādbhuṅkte karma svāśiṣaṃ bādhate ca ||13||

14dravyavān guṇavānevaṃ tyāgī bhavati sātvikaḥ
|
pañcabhūtāni pañcabhyo nivartayati tādṛśaḥ ||14||

15etatsamṛddhamapyūrdhvaṃ tapo bhavati kevalam
|
satvāt pracyavamānānāṃ saṅkalpena samāhitam ||15||

16yato yajñaḥ pravardhante satyasyaivāvarodhanāt
|
manasā'nyasya bhavati vācya'nyasyātha karmaṇā||16||

17saṅkalpasiddhaṃ puruṣamasaṅkalpo'dhitiṣṭhati
|
brāhmaṇasya viśeṣeṇa kiñcānyadapi me śrṛṇu ||17||

18adhyāpayenmahadetadyaśasyaṃ vāco vikarāḥ kavayo vadanti
|
asmin yoge sarvamidaṃ pratiṣṭhitaṃ ye tadviduramṛtāste bhavanti||18||

19na karmaṇā sukṛtenaiva rājan satyaṃ jayejjuhuyādvā yajedvā
|
naitena bālo'mṛtyumabhyeti rājan ratiṃ cāsau na labhatyantakāle ||19||

20tṛṣṇīmeka upāsīta ceṣṭeta manasā'pi na
|
tathā saṃstutinindābhyāṃ prītiroṣau vivarjayet ||20||

21atraiva tiṣṭhan kṣatriya brahmāviśati paśyati
|
vedeṣu cānupūrvyeṇa etadvidvan bravīmi te ||21||
