Mahābhārata 6.71 (Bhīṣmaparvan)

1sañjaya uvāca|
dṛṣṭvā bhīṣmeṇa saṃsaktān bhrātṝnanyāṃśca pārthivān
|
samabhyadhāvadgāṅgeyamudyatāstro dhanañjayaḥ ||1||

2pāñcajanyasya nirghoṣaṃ dhanuṣo gāṇḍivasya ca
|
dhvajaṃ ca dṛṣṭvā pārthasya sarvānno bhayamāviśat ||2||

3siṃhalā‌ṅgūlamākāśe jvalantamiva parvatam
|
asajjamānaṃ vṛkṣeṣu dhūmaketumivotthitam ||3||

4bahuvarṇaṃ vicitraṃ ca divyaṃ vānaralakṣaṇam
|
apaśyāma mahārāja dhvajaṃ gāṇḍīvadhanvanaḥ ||4||

5vidyutaṃ meghamadhyasthāṃ bhrājamānāmivāmbare
|
dadṛśurgāṇḍivaṃ yodhā rukmapṛṣṭhaṃ mahāmṛdhe ||5||

6aśuśruma bhṛśaṃ cāsya śakrasyevābhigarjataḥ
|
sughoraṃ talayoḥ śabdaṃ nighnatastava vāhinīm ||6||

7caṇḍavāto yathā meghaḥ savidyutstanayitnumān
|
diśaḥ saṃplāvayansarvāḥ śaravarṣaiḥ samantataḥ ||7||

8samabhyadhāvadgāṅgeyaṃ bhairavāstro dhanañjayaḥ
|
diśaṃ prācīṃ pratīcīṃ ca na jānīmo'stramohitāḥ ||8||

9kāṃdigbhūtāḥ śrantapatrā hatāśvā hatacetasaḥ
|
anyonyamabhisaṃśliṣya yodhāste bharatarṣabha ||9||

10bhīṣmamevābhyalīyanta saha sarvaistavātmajaiḥ
|
teṣāmārtāyanamabhūdbhīṣmaḥ śāntanavo raṇe ||10||

11samutpatanti vitrastā rathebhyo rathinastathā
|
sādinaścāśvapṛṣṭhebhyo bhūmau cāpi padātayaḥ ||11||

12śrutvā gāṇḍīvanirghoṣaṃ visphūrjitamivāśaneḥ
|
sarvasainyāni bhītāni vyavālīyanta bhārata ||12||

13atha kāmbojajairaśvairmahadbhiḥ śīghragāmibhiḥ
|
gopānāṃ bahusāhastrairbālairgopāyanairvṛtaḥ ||13||

14madrasauvīragāndhāraistraigartaiśca viśāṃpate
|
sarvakāliṅgamukhyaiśca kaliṅgādhipatirvṛtaḥ ||14||

15nānānaragaṇaughaiśca duḥśāsanapuraḥsaraḥ
|
jayadrathaśca nṛpatiḥ sahitaḥ sarvarājabhiḥ ||15||

16hayārohavarāścaiva tava putreṇa coditāḥ
|
caturdaśa sahasrāṇi saubalaṃ paryavārayan ||16||

17tataste sahitāḥ sarve vibhaktarathavāhanāḥ
|
arjunaṃ samare jaghnustāvakā bharatarṣabha ||17||

18rathibhirvāraṇairaśvaiḥ pādātaiśca samīritam
|
ghoramāyodhanaṃ cakre mahābhrasadṛśaṃ rajaḥ ||18||

19tomaraprāsanārācagajāśvarathayodhinām
|
balena mahatā bhīṣmaḥ samasajjatkirīṭinā ||19||

20āvantyaḥ kāśirājena bhīmasenena saindhavaḥ
|
ajātaśatrurmadrāṇāmṛṣabheṇa yaśasvinā ||20||

21sahaputraḥ sahāmātyaḥ śalyena samasajjata
|
vikarṇaḥ sahadevena citrasenaḥ śikhaṇḍinā ||21||

22matsyā duryodhanaṃ jagmuḥ śakuniṃ ca viśāṃpate
|
drupada‌ścekitānaśca sātyakiśca mahārathaḥ ||22||

23droṇena samasajjanta saputreṇa mahātmanā
|
kṛpaśca kṛtavarmā ca dhṛṣṭadyumnamabhidrutau ||23||

24evaṃ pravrajitāśvāni bhrāntanāgarathāni ca
|
sainyāni samasajjanta prayuddhāni samantataḥ ||24||

25nirabhre vidyutastīvrā diśaśca rajasā''vṛtāḥ
|
prādurāsan maholkāśca sanirghātā viśāṃpate ||25||

26prādurbhūto mahāvātaḥ pāṃsuvarṣaṃ papāta ca
|
nabhasyantardadhe sūryaḥ sainyena rajasā''vṛtaḥ ||26||

27pramohaḥ sarvasattvānāmatīva samapadyata
|
rajasā cābhibhūtānāmastrajālaiśca tudyatām ||27||

28vīrabāhuvisṛṣṭānāṃ sarvāvaraṇabhedinām
|
saṅghātaḥ śarajālānāṃ tumulaḥ samapadyata ||28||

29prakāśaṃ cakrurākāśamudyatāni bhujottamaiḥ
|
nakṣatravimalābhāni śastrāṇi bharatarṣabha ||29||

30ārṣabhāṇi vicitrāṇi rukmajālāvṛtāni ca
|
saṃpeturdikṣu sarvāsu carmāṇi bharatarṣabha ||30||

31sūryavarṇaiśca nistriṃśaiḥ pātyamānāni sarvaśaḥ
|
dikṣu sarvāsvadṛśyanta śarīrāṇi śirāṃsi ca ||31||

32bhagnacakrākṣanīḍāśca nipātitamahādhvajāḥ
|
hatāśvāḥ pṛthivīṃ jagmustatra tatra mahārathāḥ ||32||

33paripeturhayāścātra kecicchastrakṛtavraṇāḥ
|
rathān viparikarṣānto hateṣu rathayodhiṣu ||33||

34śarāhatā bhinnadehā baddhayoktrā hayottamāḥ
|
yugāni paryakarṣanta tatra tatra sma bhārata ||34||

35adṛśyanta sasūtāśca sāśvāḥ sarathayodhinaḥ
|
ekena balinā rājan vāraṇena vimarditāḥ ||35||

36gandhahastimadasrāvamāghrāya bahavo raṇe
|
sannipāte balaughānāṃ vītamādadire gajāḥ ||36||

37satomarairmahāmātrairnipatadbhirgatāsubhiḥ
|
babhūvāyodhanaṃ channaṃ nārācābhihatairgajaiḥ ||37||

38sannipāte balaughānāṃ preṣitairvaravāraṇaiḥ
|
nipeturyudhi saṃbhagnāḥ sayodhāḥ sadhvajā gajāḥ ||38||

39nāgarājopamairhastairnāgairākṣipya saṃyuge
|
vyadṛśyanta mahārāja saṃbhagnā rathakūbarāḥ ||39||

40viśīrṇarathasaṅghāśca keśeṣvākṣipya dantibhiḥ
|
drumaśākhā ivāvidhya niṣpiṣṭā rathino raṇe ||40||

41ratheṣu ca rathān yuddhe saṃsaktān varavāraṇāḥ
|
vikarṣanto diśaḥ sarvāḥ saṃpetuḥ sarvaśabda‌gāḥ ||41||

42teṣāṃ tathā karṣatāṃ tu gajānāṃ rūpamābabhau
|
saraḥsu nalinīzālaṃ viṣaktamiva karṣatām ||42||

43evaṃ saṃchāditaṃ tatra babhūvāyodhanaṃ mahat
|
sādibhiśca padātaiśca sadhvajaiśca mahārathaiḥ ||43||
