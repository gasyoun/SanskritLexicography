Mahābhārata 13.17 (Anuśāsanaparvan)

1vāsudeva uvāca|
tataḥ sa prayato bhūtvā mama tāta yudhiṣṭhira
|
prāñjaliḥ prāha viprarṣirnāmasaṃgrahamāditaḥ ||1||

2upamanyuruvāca|
brahmaproktairṛṣiproktairvedavedāṅgasambhavaiḥ
|
sarvalokeṣu vikhyātaṃ stutyaṃ stoṣyāmi nāmabhiḥ ||2||

3mahadbhirvihitaiḥ satyaiḥ siddhaiḥ sarvārthasādhakaiḥ
|
ṛṣiṇā taṇḍinā bhaktyā kṛtairvedakṛtātmanā ||3||

4yathoktaiḥ sādhubhiḥ khyātairmunibhistattvada‌rśibhiḥ
|
pravaraṃ prathamaṃ svargyaṃ sarvabhūtahitaṃ śubham ||4||

5śrutaiḥ sarvatra jagati brahmalokāvatāritaiḥ
|
satyaistatparamaṃ brahma brahmaproktaṃ sanātanam ||5||

6vakṣye yadukulaśreṣṭha śṛṇuṣvāvahito mama
|
varayainaṃ bhavaṃ devaṃ bhaktastvaṃ parameśvaram ||6||

7tena te śrāvayiṣyāmi yattadbrahma sanātanam
|
na śakyaṃ vistarātkṛtsnaṃ vaktuṃ sarvasya kenacit ||7||

8yuktenāpi vibhūtīnāmapi varṣaśatairapi
|
yasyādirmadhyamantaṃ ca surairapi na gamyate ||8||

9kastasya śaknuyādvaktuṃ guṇān kārtsnyena mādhava
|
kiṃ tu devasya mahataḥ saṃkṣiptārthapadākṣaram ||9||

10śaktitaścaritaṃ vakṣye prasādāttasya dhīmataḥ
|
aprāpya tu tato'nujñāṃ na śakyaḥ stotumīśvaraḥ ||10||

11yadā tenābhyanujñātaḥ stuto vai sa tadā mayā
|
anādinidhanasyāhaṃ jagadyonermahātmanaḥ ||11||

12nāmnāṃ kañcitsamuddeśaṃ vakṣyāmyavyaktayoninaḥ
|
varasya vareṇyasya viśvarūpasya dhīmataḥ ||12||

13śṛṇu nāmnāṃ ca yaṃ kṛṣṇa yaduktaṃ padmayoninā
|
daśanāmasahastrāṇi yānyāha prapitāmahaḥ ||13||

14tāni nirmathya manasā dadhno ghṛtamivoddhṛtam
|
gireḥ sāraṃ yathā hema puṣpasāraṃ yathā madhu ||14||

15ghṛtātsāraṃ yathā maṇḍastathaitatsāramuddhṛtam
|
sarvapāpāpahamidaṃ caturvedasamanvitam ||15||

16prayatnenādhigantavyaṃ dhāryaṃ ca prayatātmanā
|
māṅgalyaṃ pauṣṭikaṃ caiva rakṣoghnaṃ pāvanaṃ mahat ||16||

17idaṃ bhaktāya dātavyaṃ śraddadhānāstikāya ca
|
nāśraddadhānarūpāya nāstikāyājitātmane||17||

18yaścābhyasūyate devaṃ kāraṇātmānamīśvaram
|
sa kṛṣṇa narakaṃ yāti saha pūrvaiḥ sahātmajaiḥ ||18||

19idaṃ dhyānamidaṃ yogamidaṃ dhyeyamanuttamam
|
idaṃ japyamidaṃ jñānaṃ rahasyamidamuttamam ||19||

20yaṃ jñātvā antakāle'pi gaccheta paramāṃ gatim
|
pavitraṃ maṅgalaṃ medhyaṃ kalyāṇamidamuttamam ||20||

21idaṃ brahmā purā kṛtvā sarvalokapitāmahaḥ
|
sarvastavānāṃ rājatve divyānāṃ samakalpayat ||21||

22tadāprabhṛti caivāyamīśvarasya mahātmanaḥ
|
stavarāja iti khyāto jagatyamarapūjitaḥ ||22||

23brahmalokādayaṃ svarge stavarājo'vatāritaḥ
|
yatastaṇḍiḥ purā prāpa tena taṇḍikṛto'bhavat ||23||

24svargāccaivātra bhūrlokaṃ taṇḍinā hyavatāritaḥ
|
sarvamaṅgalamāṅgalyaṃ sarvapāpapraṇāśanam ||24||

25nigadiṣye mahābāho stavānāmuttamaṃ stavam
|
brahmaṇāmapi yadbrahma parāṇāmapi yatparam ||25||

26tejasāmapi yattejastapasāmapi yattapaḥ
|
śāntānāmapi yaḥ śānto dyutīnāmapi yā dyutiḥ ||26||

27dāntānāmapi yo dānto dhīmatāmapi yā ca dhīḥ
|
devānāmapi yo deva ṛṣīṇāmapi yastvṛṣiḥ ||27||

28yajñānāmapi yo yajñaḥ śivānāmapi yaḥ śivaḥ
|
rudrāṇāmapi yo rudraḥ prabhā prabhavatāmapi ||28||

29yogināmapi yo yogī kāraṇānāṃ ca kāraṇam
|
yato lokāḥ saṃbhavanti na bhavanti yataḥ punaḥ ||29||

30sarvabhūtātmabhūtasya harasyāmitatejasaḥ
|
aṣṭottarasahasraṃ tu nāmnāṃ śarvasya me śṛṇu
|
yacchrutvā manujavyāghra sarvānkāmānavāpsyasi ||30||

154yathā pradhānaṃ bhagavāniti bhaktyā stuto mayā
|
yanna brahmāda‌yo devā vidustattvena narṣayaḥ ||154||

155stotavyamarcyaṃ vandyaṃ ca kaḥ stoṣyati jagatpatim
|
bhaktyā tvevaṃ puraskṛtya mayā yajñapatirvibhuḥ ||155||

156tato'bhyanujñāṃ saṃprāpya stuto matimatāṃ varaḥ
|
śivamebhiḥ stuvan devaṃ nāmabhiḥ puṣṭivardhanaiḥ||156||

157nityayuktaḥ śucirbhaktaḥ prāpnotyātmānamātmanā ||157||

158etaddhi paramaṃ brahma paraṃ brahmādhigacchati
|
ṛṣayaścaiva devāśca stuvantyetena tatparam ||158||

159stūyamāno mahādevastuṣyate niyatātmabhiḥ
|
bhaktānukampī bhagavānātmasaṃsthākaro vibhuḥ ||159||

160tathaiva ca manuṣyeṣu ye manuṣyāḥ pradhānataḥ
|
āstikāḥ śraddadhānāśca vahubhirjanmabhiḥ stavaiḥ ||160||

161bhaktyā hyananyamīśānaṃ paraṃ devaṃ sanātanam
|
karmaṇā manasā vācā bhāvenāmitatejasaḥ ||161||

162śayānā jāgramāṇāśca vrajannupaviśaṃstathā
|
unmiṣannimiṣaṃścaiva cintayantaḥ punaḥ punaḥ ||162||

163śṛṇvantaḥ śrāvayantaśca kathayantaśca te bhavam
|
stuvantaḥ stūyamānāśca tuṣyanti ca ramanti ca ||163||

164janmakoṭisahastreṣu nānāsaṃsārayoniṣu
|
jantorvigatapāpasya bhave bhaktiḥ prajāyate ||164||

165utpannā ca bhave bhaktirananyā sarvabhāvataḥ
|
bhāvinaḥ kāraṇe cāsya sarvayuktasya sarvathā ||165||

166etaddeveṣu duṣprāpaṃ manuṣyeṣu na labhyate
|
nirvighnā niścalā rudre bhaktiravyabhicāriṇī ||166||

167tasyaiva ca prasādena bhaktirutpadyate nṛṇām
|
yena yānti parāṃ siddhiṃ tadbhāgavatacetasaḥ ||167||

168ye sarvabhāvānugatāḥ prapadyante maheśvaram
|
prapannavatsalo devaḥ saṃsārāttānsamuddharet ||168||

169evamanye vikurvanti devāḥ saṃsāramocanam
|
manuṣyāṇāmṛte devaṃ nānyā śaktistapobalam ||169||

170iti tenendrakalpena bhagavānsadasatpatiḥ
|
kṛttivāsāḥ stutaḥ kṛṣṇa taṇḍinā śubhabuddhinā ||170||

171stavametaṃ bhagavato brahmā svayamadhārayat
|
gīyate ca sa buddhyeta brahmā śaṅkarasannidhau ||171||

172idaṃ puṇyaṃ pavitraṃ ca sarvadā pāpanāśanam
|
yogadaṃ mokṣadaṃ caiva svargadaṃ toṣadaṃ tathā ||172||

173evametatpaṭhante ya ekabhaktyā tu śaṅkaram
|
yā gatiḥ sāṃkhyayogānāṃ vrajantyetāṃ gatiṃ tadā ||173||

174stavametaṃ prayatnena sadā rudrasya sannidhau
|
abdamekaṃ caredbhaktaḥ prāpnuyādīpsitaṃ phalam ||174||

175etadrahasyaṃ paramaṃ brahmaṇo hṛdi saṃsthitam
|
brahmā provāca śakrāya śakraḥ provāca mṛtyave ||175||

176mṛtyuḥ provāca rudrebhyo rudrebhyastaṇḍimāgamat
|
mahatā tapasā prāptastaṇḍinā brahmasadmani ||176||

177taṇḍiḥ provāca śukrāya gautamāya ca bhārgavaḥ
|
vaivasvatāya manave gautamaḥ prāha mādhava ||177||

178nārāyaṇāya sādhyāya samādhiṣṭhāya dhīmate
|
yamāya prāha bhagavān sādhyo nārāyaṇo'cyutaḥ ||178||

179nāciketāya bhagavānāha vaivasvato yamaḥ
|
mārkaṇḍeyāya vārṣṇeya nāciketo'bhyabhāṣata ||179||

180mārkaṇḍeyānmayā prāpto niyamena janārdana
|
tavāpyahamamitraghna stavaṃ dadyāṃ hyaviśrutam ||180||

181svargyamārogyamāyuṣyaṃ dhanyaṃ vedena saṃmitam
|
nāsya vighnaṃ vikurvanti dānavā yakṣarākṣasāḥ
|
piśācā yātudhānā vā guhyakā bhujagā api ||181||

182yaḥ paṭheta śuciḥ pārtha brahmacārī jitendriyaḥ
|
abhagnayogo varṣaṃ tu so'śvamedhaphalaṃ labhet ||182||
