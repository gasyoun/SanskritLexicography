Mahābhārata 3.18 (Āraṇyakaparvan)

1vāsudeva uvāca|
śālvabāṇārdite tasminpradyumne balināṃ vare
|
vṛṣṇayo bhagnasaṅkalpā vivyathuḥ pṛtanāgatāḥ||1||

2hāhākṛtamabhūtsārvaṃ vṛṣṇyandhakabalaṃ tataḥ
|
pradyumne mohite rājanpare ca muditā bhṛśam||2||

3taṃ tathā mohitaṃ dṛṣṭvā sārathirjavanairhayaiḥ
|
raṇādapāharattūrṇaṃ śikṣito dārukistadā||3||

4nātidūrāpayāte tu rathe rathavarapraṇut
|
dhanurgṛhītvā yantāraṃ labdhasañjño'bravīdidam||4||

5saute kiṃ te vyavasitaṃ kasmādyāsi parāṅmukhaḥ
|
naiṣa vṛṣṇipravīrāṇāmāhave dharma ucyate||5||

6kaccitsaute na te mohaḥ śālvaṃ dṛṣṭvā mahāhave
|
viṣādo vā raṇaṃ dṛṣṭvā brūhi me tvaṃ yathātatham||6||

7sautiruvāca|
jānārdane na me moho nāpi māṃ bhayamāviśat
|
atibhāraṃ tu te manye śālvaṃ keśavanandana||7||

8so'payāmi śanairvīra balavāneṣa pāpakṛt
|
mohitaśca raṇe śūro rakṣyaḥ sārathinā rathī||8||

9āyuṣmaṃstvaṃ mayā nityaṃ rakṣitavyastvayāpyaham
|
rakṣitavyo rathī nityamiti kṛtvāpayāmyaham||9||

10ekaścāsi mahābāho bahavaścāpi dānavāḥ
|
na samaṃ raukmiṇeyāhaṃ raṇaṃ matvāpayāmi vai||10||

11evaṃ bruvati sūte tu tadā makaraketumān
|
uvāca sūtaṃ kauravya nivartaya rathaṃ punaḥ||11||

12dārukātmaja maivaṃ tvaṃ punaḥ kārṣīḥ kathañcana
|
vyapayānaṃ raṇātsaute jīvato mama karhicit||12||

13na sa vṛṣṇikule jāto yo vai tyajati saṅgaram
|
yo vā nipatitaṃ hanti tavāsmīti ca vādinam||13||

14tathā striyaṃ ca yo hanti bālaṃ vṛddhaṃ tathaiva ca
|
virathaṃ viprakīrṇaṃ ca bhagnaśastrāyudhaṃ tathā||14||

15tvaṃ ca sūtakule jāto vinītaḥ sūtakarmaṇi
|
dharmajñaścāsi vṛṣṇīnāmāhaveṣvapi dāruke||15||

16sa jānaṃścaritaṃ kṛtsnaṃ vṛṣṇīnāṃ pṛtanāmukhe
|
apayānaṃ punaḥ saute maivaṃ kārṣīḥ kathañcana||16||

17apayātaṃ hataṃ pṛṣṭhe bhrāntaṃ raṇapalāyitam
|
gadāgrajo durādharṣaḥ kiṃ māṃ vakṣyati mādhavaḥ||17||

18keśavasyāgrajo vāpi nīlavāsā madotkaṭaḥ
|
kiṃ vakṣyati mahābāhurbaladevaḥ samāgataḥ||18||

19kiṃ vakṣyati śinernaptā narasiṃho mahādhanuḥ
|
apayātaṃ raṇātsūta sāmbaśca samitiñjayaḥ||19||

20cārudeṣṇaśca durdharṣastathaiva gadasāraṇau
|
akrūraśca mahābāhuḥ kiṃ māṃ vakṣyati sārathe||20||

21śūraṃ sambhāvitaṃ śāntaṃ nityaṃ puruṣamāninam
|
striyaśca vṛṣṇivīrāṇāṃ kiṃ māṃ vakṣyanti saṃhatāḥ||21||

22pradyumno'yamupāyāti bhītastyaktvā mahāhavam
|
dhigenamiti vakṣyanti na tu vakṣyanti sādhviti||22||

23dhigvācā parihāso'pi mama vā madvidhasya vā
|
mṛtyunābhyadhikaḥ saute sa tvaṃ mā vyapayāḥ punaḥ||23||

24bhāraṃ hi mayi saṃnyasya yāto madhunihā hariḥ
|
yajñaṃ bharatasiṃhasya na hi śakyodya marṣitum||24||

25kṛtavarmā mayā vīro niryāsyanneva vāritaḥ
|
śālvaṃ nivārayiṣye'haṃ tiṣṭha tvamiti sūtaja||25||

26sa ca sambhāvayanmāṃ vai nivṛtto hṛdikātmajaḥ
|
taṃ sametya raṇaṃ tyaktvā kiṃ vakṣyāmi mahāratham||26||

27upayātaṃ durādharṣaṃ śaṅkhacakragadādharam
|
puruṣaṃ puṇḍarīkākṣaṃ kiṃ vakṣyāmi mahābhujam||27||

28sātyakiṃ baladevaṃ ca ye cānye'ndhakavṛṣṇayaḥ
|
mayā spardhanti satataṃ kiṃ nu vakṣyāmi tānaham||28||

29tyaktvā raṇamimaṃ saute pṛṣṭhato'bhyāhataḥ śaraiḥ
|
tvayāpanīto vivaśo na jīveyaṃ kathañcana||29||

30sa nivarta rathenāśu punardārukanandana
|
na caitadevaṃ kartavyamathāpatsu kathañcana||30||

31na jīvitamahaṃ saute bahu manye kathañcana
|
apayāto raṇādbhītaḥ pṛṣṭhato'bhyāhataḥ śaraiḥ||31||

32kadāpi sūtaputra tvaṃ jānīṣe māṃ bhayārditam
|
apayātaṃ raṇaṃ hitvā yathā kāpuruṣaṃ tathā||32||

33na yuktaṃ bhavatā tyaktuṃ saṅgrāmaṃ dārukātmaja
|
mayi yuddhārthini bhṛśaṃ sa tvaṃ yāhi yato raṇam||33||
