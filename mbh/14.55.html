Mahābhārata 14.55 (Āśvamedhikaparvan)

1uttaṅka uvāca|
abhijānāmi jagataḥ kartāraṃ tvāṃ janārdana
|
nūnaṃ bhavatprasādo'yamiti me nāsti saṃśayaḥ||1||

2cittaṃ ca suprasannaṃ me tvadbhāvagatamacyuta
|
vinivṛttaṃ ca me śāpāditi viddhi parantapa||2||

3yadi tvanugrahaṃ kañcittvatto'rhāmi janārdana
|
draṣṭumicchāmi te rūpamaiśvaraṃ tannidarśaya||3||

4vaiśampāyana uvāca|
tataḥ sa tasmai prītātmā darśayāmāsa tadvapuḥ
|
śāśvataṃ vaiṣṇavaṃ dhīmāndadṛśe yaddhanañjayaḥ||4||

5sa dadarśa mahātmānaṃ viśvarūpaṃ mahābhujam
|
sahasrasūryapratimaṃ dīptimat pāvakopamam||5||

6sarvamākāśamāvṛtya tiṣṭhantaṃ sarvatomukham
|
taddṛṣṭvā paramaṃ rūpaṃ viṣṇorvaiṣṇavamadbhutam
|
vismayaṃ ca yayau viprastaṃ dṛṣṭvā parameśvaram||6||

7uttaṅka uvāca|
viśvakarmannamaste'stu viśvātman viśvasambhava
|
padbhyāṃ te pṛthivī vyāptā śirasā cāvṛtaṃ nabhaḥ||7||

8dyāvāpṛthivyoryanmadhyaṃ jaṭhareṇa tavāvṛtam
|
bhujābhyāmāvṛtāścāśāstvamidaṃ sarvamacyuta||8||

9saṃharasva punardevarūpamakṣayyamuttamam
|
punastvāṃ svena rūpeṇa draṣṭumicchāmi śāśvatam||9||

10vaiśampāyana uvāca|
tamuvāca prasannātmā govindo janamejaya
|
varaṃ vṛṇīṣveti tadā tamuttaṅko'bravīdidam||10||

11paryāpta eṣa evādya parastvatto mahādyute
|
yatte rūpamidaṃ kṛṣṇaṃ paśyāmi puruṣottama||11||

12tamabravītpunaḥ kuṣṇo mā tvamatra vicāraya
|
avaśyametatkartavyamamoghaṃ darśanaṃ mama||12||

13uttaṅka uvāca|
avaśyaṃ karaṇīyaṃ ca yadyetanmanyase vibho
|
toyamicchāmi yatreṣṭaṃ maruṣvetaddhi durlabham||13||

14tataḥ saṃhṛtya tattejaḥ provācottaṅkamīśvaraḥ
|
eṣṭavye sati cintyo'hamityuktvā dvārakāṃ yayau||14||

15tataḥ kadācidbhagavānuttaṅkastoyakāṃkṣayā
|
tṛṣitaḥ paricakrāma marau sasmāra cācyutam||15||

16tato digvāsasaṃ dhīmānmātaṅgaṃ malapaṅkinam
|
apaśyata marau tasmin śvayūthaparivāritam||16||

17bhīṣaṇaṃ baddhanistriṃśaṃ bāṇakārmukadhāriṇam
|
tasyādhaḥ srotaso'paśyadvāri bhūridvijottamaḥ||17||

18smaranneva ca taṃ prāha mātaṅgaḥ prahasanniva
|
ehyuttaṅka pratīcchasva mano vāri bhṛgūdvaha||18||

19kṛpā hi pe sumahatī tvāṃ dṛṣṭvā tṛṭsamāśritam
|
ityuktastena sa munistattoyaṃ nābhyanandata||19||

20cikṣepa ca sa taṃ dhīmānvāgbhiruyābhiracyutam
|
punaḥ punaśca mānaṅgaḥ pibasveti tamabravīt||20||

21na cāpibatsa sakrodhaḥ kṣubhitenānantarātmanā
|
te sa tathā niścayāt tena pratyākhyāto mahātmanā||21||

22śvabhiḥ saha mahārāja tatraivāntaradhīyata
|
uttaṅkastaṃ tathā dṛṣṭvā tato vrīḍitamānasa:||22||

23mene pralabdhamātmānaṃ kṛṣṇenāmitraghātinā
|
atha tenaiva mārgeṇa śaṅkhacakragadādharaḥ||23||

24ājagāma mahābuddhiruttaṅkaścainamabravīt
|
na yuktaṃ tādṛśaṃ dātuṃ tvayā puruṣasattama||24||

25salilaṃ vipramukhyebhyo mātaṅgasrotasā vibho
|
ityuktavacanaṃ taṃ tu mahābuddhirjanārdanaḥ||25||

26uttaṅkaṃ ślakṣṇayā vācā sāntvayannidamabravīt
|
yādṛśeneha rūpeṇa yogyaṃ dātuṃ dhṛtena vai||26||

27tādṛśaṃ khalu te dattaṃ yacca tvaṃ nāvabudhyathāḥ
|
mayā tvadarthamukto vai vajrapāṇiḥ purandaraḥ||27||

28uttaṅkāyāmṛtaṃ dehi toyarūpamiti prabhuḥ
|
sa māmuvāca devendro na mo'martyatāṃ vrajet||28||

29anyamasmai varaṃ dehītyasakṛd bhṛgunandana
|
amṛtaṃ deyamityeva mayoktaḥ sa śacīpatiḥ||29||

30sa māṃ prasādya devendraḥ punarevedamabravīt
|
yadi deyamavaśyaṃ vai mātaṅgo'haṃ mahāmate||30||

31bhūtvāmṛtaṃ pradāsyāmi bhārgavāya mahātmane
|
yadyevaṃ pratigṛhṇāti bhārgavo'mṛtamadya vai||31||

32pradātumeṣa gacchāmi bhārgavo'syāmṛtaṃ vibho
|
pratyākhyātastvahaṃ tena dāsyāmi na kathañcana||32||

33sa tathā samayaṃ kṛtvā tena rūpeṇa vāsavaḥ
|
upasthitastvayā cāpi pratyākhyāto'mṛtaṃ dadat||33||

34cāṇḍālarūpī bhagavānsumahāṃste vyatikramaḥ
|
yattu śakyaṃ mayā kartuṃ bhūya eva tavepsitam||34||

35toyepsā tava durdharṣāṃ kariṣye saphalāmaham
|
yeṣvahaḥsu ca te brahman salilepsā bhaviṣyati||35||

36tadā marau bhaviṣyanti jalapūrṇāḥ payodharāḥ
|
rasavacca pradāsyanti toyaṃ te bhṛgunandana||36||

37uttaṅkameghā ityuktāḥ khyāti yāsyanti cāpi te
|
ityuktaḥ prītimānvipraḥ kṛṣṇena sa babhūva ha
|
adyāpyuttaṅkameghāśca marau varṣanti bhārata||37||
