Mahābhārata 6.90 (Bhīṣmaparvan)

1sañjaya uvāca|
vartamāne tathā raudre rājan vīravarakṣaye
|
śakuniḥ saubalaḥ śrīmānpāṃḍavānsamupādravat ||1||

2tathaiva sātvato rājan hārdikyaḥ paravīrahā
|
abhyadravata saṃgrāme pāṇḍavānāṃ varūthinīm ||2||

3tataḥ kāṃbojamukhyānāṃ nadījānāṃ ca vājinām
|
āraṭṭānāṃ mahījānāṃ sindhujānāṃ ca sarvaśaḥ ||3||

4vanāyujānāṃ śubhrāṇāṃ tathā parvatavāsinām
|
vājināṃ vahubhiḥ saṅkhye samaṃtātparivārayan ||4||

5ye cāpare tittirijā javanā vātaraṃhasaḥ
|
suvarṇālaṅkṛtairetairvarmavadbhiḥ sukalpitaiḥ ||5||

6hayairvātajavairmukhyaiḥ pāṇḍavasya suto balī
|
abhyavartata tat sainyaṃ hṛṣṭarūpaḥ paraṃtapaḥ ||6||

7arjunasya sutaḥ śrīmānirāvānnāma vīryavān
|
snuṣāyāṃ nāgarājasya jātaḥ pārthena dhīmatā ||7||

8airāvatena sā dattā anapatyā mahātmanā
|
patyau hate suparṇena kṛpaṇā dīnacetanā ||8||

9bhāryārthaṃ tāṃ ca jagrāha pārthaḥ kāmavaśānugām
|
evameṣa samutpannaḥ parakṣetre'rjunātmajaḥ ||9||

10sa nāgaloke saṃvṛddho mātrā ca parirakṣitaḥ
|
pitṛvyeṇa parityaktaḥ pārthadveṣāddurātmanā ||10||

11rūpavān balasaṃpanno guṇavān satyavikramaḥ
|
indralokaṃ jagāmāśu śrutvā tatrārjunaṃ gatam ||11||

12so'bhigamya mahābāhuḥ pitaraṃ satyavikramaḥ
|
abhyavādayadavyagro vinayena kṛtāñjaliḥ ||12||

13nyavedayata cātmānamarjunasya mahātmanaḥ
|
irāvānasmi bhadraṃ te putraścāhaṃ tava prabho ||13||

14mātuḥ samāgamo yaśca tatsarvaṃ pratyavedayat
|
tacca sarvaṃ yathāvṛttamanusasmāra pāṇḍavaḥ ||14||

15pariṣvajya sutaṃ cāpi ātmanaḥ sadṛśaṃ guṇaiḥ
|
prītimānanayat pārtho devarājaniveśane ||15||

16so'rjunena samājñapto devaloke tadā nṛpa
|
prītipūrvaṃ mahābāhuḥ svakāryaṃ prati bhārata ||16||

17yuddhakāle tvayā'smākaṃ sāhyaṃ deyamiti prabho
|
bāḍhamityevamuktvā tu yuddhakāla ihāgataḥ ||17||

18kāmavarṇajavairaśvairbahubhiḥ saṃvṛto nṛpa
|
te hayāḥ kāñcanāpīḍā nānāvarṇā manojavāḥ ||18||

19utpetuḥ sahasā rājan haṃsā iva mahodadhau
|
te tvadīyān samāsādya hayasaṃghānmanojavān ||19||

20kroḍaiḥ kroḍānabhighnanto ghoṇābhiśca parasparam
|
nipetuḥ sahasā rājan suvegābhihatā bhuvi ||20||

21nipatadbhistathā taiśca hayasaṅghaiḥ parasparam
|
śuśruve dāruṇaḥ śabdaḥ suparṇapatane yathā ||21||

22tathaiva tāvakā rājan sametyānyo'nyamāhave
|
parasparavadhaṃ ghoraṃ cakruste hayasādinaḥ ||22||

23tasmiṃstathā vartamāne sa‌ṅkule tumule bhṛśam
|
ubhayorapi saṃśāntā hayasaṅghāḥ samantataḥ ||23||

24prakṣīṇasāyakāḥ śūrā nihatāśvāḥ śramāturāḥ
|
vilayaṃ samanuprāptāstakṣamāṇāḥ parasparam ||24||

25tataḥ kṣīṇe hayānīke kiñciccheṣe ca bhārata
|
saubalasyānujāḥ śūrā nirgatā raṇamūrdhani ||25||

26vāyuvegasamasparśān jave vāyusamāṃśca te
|
āruhya balasaṃpannānvayaḥsthāṃsturagottamān ||26||

27gajo gavākṣo vṛṣabhaścarmavānārjavaḥ śukaḥ
|
ṣaḍete balasaṃpannā niryayurmahato balāt ||27||

28vāryamāṇāḥ śakuninā taiśca yodhairmahābalaiḥ
|
sannaddhā yuddhakuśalā raudrarūpā mahābalāḥ ||28||

29tadanīkaṃ mahābāho bhittvā paramadurjayam
|
balena mahatā yuktāḥ svargāya vijayaiṣiṇaḥ||29||

30viviśuste tadā hṛṣṭā gāndhārā yuddhadurmadāḥ
|
tān praviṣṭāṃstadā dṛṣṭvā irāvānapi vīryavān ||30||

31abravītsamare yodhān vicitrāndāruṇāyudhān
|
yathaite dhārtarāṣṭrasya yodhāḥ sānugavāhanāḥ ||31||

32hanyante samare sarve tathā nītirvidhīyatām
|
bāḍhamityevamuktvā te sarve yodhā irāvataḥ ||32||

33jaghnusteṣāṃ balānīkaṃ durjayaṃ samare paraiḥ
|
tadanīkamanīkena samare vīkṣya pātitam ||33||

34amṛṣyamāṇāste sarve subalasyātmajā raṇe
|
irāvantamabhidrutya sarvataḥ paryavārayan ||34||

35tāḍayantaḥ śitaiḥ prāsaiścodayantaḥ parasparam
|
te śūrāḥ paryadhāvanta kurvanto mahadākulam ||35||

36irāvānatha nirbhinnaḥ prāsaistīkṣṇairmahātmabhiḥ
|
sravatā rudhireṇāktastotrairviddha iva dvipaḥ ||36||

37purato'piṃ ca pṛṣṭhe ca pārśvayośca bhṛśāhataḥ
|
eko bahubhiratyarthaṃ dhairyādrājanna vivyathe ||37||

38irāvānapi saṃkruddhaḥ sarvāṃstānniśitaiḥ śaraiḥ
|
mohayāmāsa samare viddhvā parapurañjayaḥ ||38||

39prāsānutkṛṣya tarasā svaśarīrādarindamaḥ
|
taireva tāḍayāmāsa subalasyātmajān raṇe ||39||

40vikṛṣya ca śitaṃ khaḍgaṃ gṛhītvā ca śarāvaram
|
padātirdrutamāgacchajjighāṃsuḥ saubalān yudhi ||40||

41tataḥ pratyāgataprāṇāḥ sarve te subalātmajāḥ
|
bhūyaḥ krodhasamāviṣṭā irāvantamabhidrutāḥ||41||

42irāvānapi khaḍgena darśayan pāṇilāghavam
|
abhyavartata tān sarvān saubalān baladarpitaḥ ||42||

43lāghavenātha carataḥ sarve te subalātmajāḥ
|
antaraṃ nābhyagacchanta carantaḥ śīghragairhayaiḥ ||43||

44bhūmiṣṭhamatha taṃ saṅkhye saṃpradṛśya tataḥ punaḥ
|
parivārya bhṛśaṃ sarve grahītumupacakramuḥ ||44||

45athābhyāśagatānāṃ sa khaḍgenāmitrakarśanaḥ
|
asihastāpahastābhyāṃ teṣāṃ gātrāṇyakṛntata ||45||

46āyudhāni ca sarveṣāṃ bāhūnapi vibhūṣitān
|
apatanta nikṛttāṅgā mṛtā bhūmau gatāsavaḥ ||46||

47vṛṣabhastu mahārāja bahudhā viparikṣataḥ
|
amucyata mahāraudrāttasmādvīrāvakartanāt ||47||

48tānsarvānpatitān dṛṣṭvā bhīto duryodhanastataḥ
|
abhyadhāvata saṃkruddho rākṣasaṃ ghoradarśanam ||48||

49ārṣyaśṛṅgiṃ maheṣvāsaṃ māyāvinamarindamam
|
vairiṇaṃ bhīmasenasya pūrvaṃ bakavadhena vai ||49||

50paśya vīra yathā hyeṣa phālgunasya suto balī
|
māyāvī vipriyaṃ kartumakārṣīnme balakṣayam ||50||

51tvaṃ ca kāmagamastāta māyāstre ca viśāradaḥ
|
kṛtavairaśca pārthena tasmādenaṃ raṇe jahi ||51||

52bāḍhamityevamuktvā tu rākṣaso ghoradarśanaḥ
|
prayayau siṃhanādena yatrārjunasuto yuvā ||52||

53ārūḍhairyuddhakuśalairvimalaprāsayodhibhiḥ
|
vīraiḥ prahāribhiryuktaiḥ svairanīkaiḥ samāvṛtaḥ ||53||

54hataśeṣairmahārāja dvisāhasrairhayottamaiḥ
|
nihantukāmaḥ samare irāvantaṃ mahābalam ||54||

55irāvānapi saṃkuddhastvaramāṇaḥ parākramī
|
hantukāmamamitraghno rākṣasaṃ pratyavārayat ||55||

56tamāpatantaṃ saṃprekṣya rākṣasaḥ sumahābalaḥ
|
tvaramāṇastato māyāṃ prayoktumupacakrame ||56||

57tena māyāmayāḥ sṛṣṭā hayāstāvanta eva hi
|
svārūḍhā rākṣasairghoraiḥ śūlapaṭṭiśadhāribhiḥ ||57||

58te saṃrabdhāḥ samāgamya dvisāhasrāḥ prahāriṇaḥ
|
acirādgamayāmāsuḥ pretalokaṃ parasparam ||58||

59tasmiṃstu nihate sainye tāvubhau yuddhadurmadau
|
saṃgrāme samatiṣṭhetāṃ yathā vai vṛtravāsavau ||59||

60ādravantamabhiprekṣya rākṣasaṃ yuddhadurmadam
|
irāvānatha saṃrabdhaḥ pratyadhāvanmahābalaḥ ||60||

61samabhyāśagatasyājau tasya khaḍgena durmateḥ
|
ciccheda kārmukaṃ dīptaṃ śarāvāpaṃ ca sattvaram ||61||

62sa nikṛttaṃ dhanurdaṣṭvā khaṃ javena samāviśat
|
irāvantamabhikruddhaṃ mohayanniva māyayā ||62||

63tato'ntarikṣamutpatya irāvānapi rākṣasam
|
vimohayitvā māyābhistasya gātrāṇisāyakaiḥ ||63||

64ciccheda sarvamarmajñaḥ kāmarūpo durāsadaḥ
|
tathā sa rākṣasaśreṣṭhaḥ śaraiḥ kṛttaḥ punaḥ punaḥ ||64||

65saṃbabhūva mahārāja samavāpa ca yauvanam
|
māyā hi sahajā teṣāṃ vayo rūpaṃ ca kāmajam ||65||

66evaṃ tadrākṣasasyāṅgaṃ chinnaṃ chinnaṃ babhūva ha
|
irāvānapi saṃkruddho rākṣasaṃ taṃ mahābalam ||66||

67paraśvadhena tīkṣṇena ciccheda ca punaḥ punaḥ
|
sa tena balinā bīraśchidyamāna irāvatā ||67||

68rākṣaso'pyanadaddhoraṃ sa śabdastumulo'bhavat
|
paraśvadhakṣataṃ rakṣaḥ susrāva bahu śoṇitam ||68||

69tataścukrodha balavāṃścakre vegaṃ ca saṃyuge
|
ārṣyaśṛṅgistathā dṛṣṭvā samare śatrumūrjitam ||69||

70kṛtvā ghoraṃ mahadrūpaṃ grahītumupacakrame
|
arjunasya sutaṃ vīramirāvantaṃ yaśasvinam ||70||

71saṃgrāmaśiraso madhye sarveṣāṃ tatra paśyatām
|
tāṃ dṛṣṭvā tādṛśīṃ māyāṃ rākṣasasya durātmanaḥ ||71||

72irāvānapi saṃkruddho māyāṃ sraṣṭuṃ pracakrame
|
tasya krodhābhibhūtasya samareṣvanivartinaḥ ||72||

73yo'nvayo mātṛkastasya sa enamabhipedivān
|
sa nāgairbahubhī rājannirāvān saṃvṛto raṇe ||73||

74dadhāra sumahadrūpamananta iva bhogavān
|
tato bahuvidhairnāgaiśchādayāmāsa rākṣasam ||74||

75chādyamānastu nāgaiḥ sa dhyātvā rākṣasapuṅgavaḥ
|
sauparṇaṃ rūpamāsthāya bhakṣayāmāsa pannagān ||75||

76māyayā bhakṣite tasminnanvaye tasya mātṛke
|
vimohitamirāvantaṃ nyahanadrākṣaso'sinā ||76||

77sakuṇḍalaṃ samukuṭaṃ padmendusadṛśaprabham
|
irāvataḥ śiro rakṣaḥ pātayāmāsa bhūtale ||77||

78tasmiṃstu vihate vīre rākṣasenārjunātmaje
|
viśokāḥ samapadyanta dhārtarāṣṭrāḥ sarājakāḥ ||78||

79tasmin mahati saṃgrāme tādṛśe bhairave punaḥ
|
mahān vyatikaro ghoraḥ senayoḥ samapadyata ||79||

80gajā hayāḥ padātāśca vimiśrā dantibhirhatāḥ
|
rathāśvā dantinaścaiva pattibhistatra sūditāḥ ||80||

81tathā pattirathaughāśca hayāśca bahavo raṇe
|
rathibhirnihatā rājaṃstava teṣāṃ ca saṃkule ||81||

82ajānannarjunaścāpi nihataṃ putramaurasam
|
jaghāna samare śūrān rājñastān bhīṣmarakṣiṇaḥ ||82||

83tathaiva tāvakā rājan sṛñjayāśca sahasraśaḥ
|
juhvataḥ samare prāṇānnijaghnuritaretaram ||83||

84muktakeśā vikavacā virathāśchinnakārmukāḥ
|
bāhubhiḥ samayudhyanta samavetāḥ parasparam ||84||

85tathā marmātigairbhīṣmo nijaghāna mahārathān
|
kampayan samare senāṃ pāṇḍavānāṃ paraṃtapaḥ ||85||

86tena yaudhiṣṭhire sainye bahavo mānavā hatāḥ
|
dantinaḥ sādinaścaiva rathino'tha hayāstathā ||86||

87tatra bhārata bhīṣmasya raṇe dṛṣṭvā parākramam
|
atyadbhutamapaśyāma śakrasyeva parākramam ||87||

88tathaiva bhīmasenasya pārṣatasya ca bhārata
|
raudramāsīdraṇe yuddhaṃ sātyakasya ca dhanvinaḥ ||88||

89dṛṣṭvā droṇasya vikrāntaṃ pāṇḍavān bhayamāviśat
|
eka eva raṇe śakto nihantuṃ sarvasainikān ||89||

90kiṃ punaḥ pṛthivīśūrairyodhavrātaiḥ samāvṛtaḥ
|
ityabruvanmahārāja raṇe droṇena pīḍitāḥ ||90||

91vartamāne tathā raudre saṃgrāme bharatarṣabha
|
ubhayoḥ senayoḥ śūrā nāmṛṣyanta parasparam ||91||

92āviṣṭā iva yudhyante rakṣobhūtā mahābalāḥ
|
tāvakāḥ pāṇḍaveyāśca saṃrabdhāstāta dhanvinaḥ ||92||

93na sma paśyāmahe kañcitprāṇānyaḥ parirakṣati
|
saṃgrāme daityasaṅkāśe tasmin vīravarakṣaye||93||
