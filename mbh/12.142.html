Mahābhārata 12.142 (Śāntiparvan)

1yudhiṣṭhira uvāca|
yadi ghoraṃ samuddiṣṭamaśraddheyamivānṛtam
|
asmi sviddasyumaryādā yāmahaṃ parivarjaye ||1||

2sammuhyāmi viṣīdāmi dharmo me śithilīkṛtaḥ
|
udyamaṃ nādhigacchāmi kadācitparisāntvayan ||2||

3bhīṣma uvāca|
naitacchrutvā''gamādeva tava dharmānuśāsanam
|
prajāsamavahāro'yaṃ kavibhiḥ sambhṛtaṃ madhu ||3||

4bahvyaḥ pratividhātavyāḥ prajñā rājñā tatastataḥ
|
naikaśākhena dharmeṇa yatraiṣā sampravartate ||4||

5buddhisaṃjanano dharma ācāraśca satāṃ sadā
|
jñeyo bhavati kauravya sadā tadviddhi me vacaḥ ||5||

6buddhiśreṣṭhā hi rājānaścaranti vijayaiṣiṇaḥ
|
dharmaḥ pratividhātavyo buddhyā rājñā tatastataḥ ||6||

7naikaśākhena dharmeṇa rājño dharmo vidhīyate
|
durbalasya kutaḥ prajñā purastādanupāhṛtā ||7||

8advaidhajñaḥ pathi dvaidhe saṃśayaṃ prāptumarhati
|
buddhidvaidhaṃ veditavyaṃ purastādeva bhārata ||8||

9pārśvataḥ karaṇaṃ prājño viṣṭaṃbhitvā prakārayet
|
janastaccaritaṃ dharmaṃ vijānātyanyathānyathā ||9||

10amithyājñāninaḥ kecinmithyāvijñāninaḥ pare
|
tadvai yathāyathaṃ buddhvā jñānamādadate satām ||10||

11parimuṣṇanti śāstrāṇi dharmasya paripanthinaḥ
|
vaiṣamyamarthavidyānāṃ nirarthāḥ khyāpayanti te ||11||

12ājijīviṣavo vidyāṃ yaśaḥkāmau samantataḥ
|
te sarve nṛpa pāpiṣṭhā dharmasya paripanthinaḥ ||12||

13apakvamatayo mandā na jānanti yathātatham
|
yathā hyaśāstrakuśalāḥ sarvatrāyuktiniṣṭhitāḥ ||13||

14parimuṣṇanti śāstrāṇi śāstradoṣānudarśinaḥ
|
vijñānamarthaṃ vidyānāṃ na samyagiti vartate ||14||

15nindayā paravidyānāṃ svavidyāṃ khyāpayanti ca
|
vāgastrā vākcharībhūtā dugdhavidyāphalā iva ||15||

16tānvidyāvaṇijo viddhi rākṣasāniva bhārata
|
vyājena sadbhirvihito dharmaste parihāsyati ||16||

17na dharmavacanaṃ vācā naiva buddhyeti naḥ śrutam
|
iti bārhaspataṃ jñānaṃ provāca maghavā svayam ||17||

18na tveva vacanaṃ kiṃcidanimittādihocyate
|
suvinītena śāstreṇa na vyavasyantyathāpare ||18||

19lokayātrāmihaike tu dharmaṃ prāhurmanīṣiṇaḥ
|
samuddiṣṭaṃ satāṃ dharmaṃ svayamūheta paṇḍitaḥ ||19||

20amarṣācchāstrasammohādavijñānācca bhārata
|
śāstraṃ prājñasya vadataḥ samūhe yātyadarśanam ||20||

21āgatāgamayā buddhyā vacanena praśasyate
|
ajñānājjñānahetutvādvacanaṃ sādhu manyate ||21||

22anayā hatamevedamiti śāstramapārthakam
|
daiteyānuśanā prāha saṃśayacchedanaṃ purā ||22||

23jñānamapyapadiśyaṃ hi yathā nāsti tathaiva tat
|
taṃ tathā chinnamūlena sannodayitumarhasi ||23||

24anavyavahitaṃ yo vā nedaṃ vākyamupāśnute
|
ugrāyaiva hi sṛṣṭo'si karmaṇe na tvamīkṣase ||24||

25aṅga māmanvavekṣasva rājanyāya bubhūṣate
|
yathā pramucyate tvanyo yadarthaṃ na pramodate ||25||

26ajo'śvaḥ kṣatramityetat sadṛśaṃ brahmaṇā kṛtam
|
tasmādabhīkṣṇaṃ bhūtānāṃ yātrā kācitprasiddhyati ||26||

27yastvavadhyavadhe doṣaḥ sa vadhyasyāvadhe smṛtaḥ
|
yā caiva khalu maryādā yāmayaṃ parivarjayet ||27||

28tasmāttīkṣṇaḥ prajā rājā svadharme sthāpayettataḥ
|
anyonyaṃ bhakṣayanto hi pracareyurvṛkā iva ||28||

29yasya dasyugaṇā rāṣṭre dhvāṃkṣā matsyān jalādiva
|
viharanti parasvāni sa vai kṣatriyapāṃsanaḥ ||29||

30kulīnān sacivān kṛtvā vedavidyāsamanvitān
|
praśādhi pṛthivīṃ rājan prajā dharmeṇa pālayan ||30||

31vihīnaṃ karmaṇā nyāyaṃ yaḥ pragṛhṇāti bhūmipaḥ
|
upāyasyāviśeṣajñaṃ tadvai kṣatraṃ napuṃsakam ||31||

32naivograṃ naiva cānugraṃ dharmeṇeha praśasyate
|
ubhayaṃ na vyatikrāmedugro bhūtvā mṛdurbhava ||32||

33kaṣṭaḥ kṣatriyadharmo'yaṃ sauhṛdaṃ tvayi me sthitam
|
ugrakarmaṇi sṛṣṭo'si tasmādrājyaṃ praśādhi vai ||33||

34aśiṣṭanigraho nityaṃ śiṣṭasya paripālanam
|
evaṃ śukro'bravīddhīmānāpatsu bharatarṣabha ||34||

35yudhiṣṭhira uvāca|
asti cediha maryādā yāmanyo nābhilaṃghayet
|
pṛcchāmi tvāṃ satāṃ śreṣṭha tanme brūhi pitāmaha ||35||

36bhīṣma uvāca|
brāhmaṇāneva seveta vidyāvṛddhāstapasvinaḥ
|
śrutacāritravṛttāḍhyānpavitraṃ hyetaduttamam ||36||

37yā devatāsu vṛttiste sā'stu vipreṣu nityadā
|
kruddhairhi vipraiḥ karmāṇi kṛtāni bahudhā nṛpa ||37||

38prītyā yaśo bhavenmukhyamaprītyā paramaṃ bhayam
|
prītyā hyamṛtavadviprāḥ kruddhāścaiva viṣaṃ yathā ||38||
