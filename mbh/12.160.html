Mahābhārata 12.160 (Śāntiparvan)

1yudhiṣṭhira uvāca|
svādhyāye kṛtayatnasya narasya ca pitāmaha
|
dharmakāmasya dharmātman kiṃ nu śreya ihocyate ||1||

2bahudhā darśane loke śreyo yadiha manyase
|
asmi~lloke pare caiva tanme brūhi pitāmaha ||2||

3mahānayaṃ dharmapatho bahuśākhaśca bhārata
|
kiṃsvideveha dharmāṇāmanuṣṭheyatamaṃ matam ||3||

4dharmasyo mahato rājan bahuśākhasya tattvataḥ
|
yanmūlaṃ paramaṃ tāta tatsarvaṃ brūhyaśeṣataḥ ||4||

5bhīṣma uvāca|
hanta te kathayiṣyāmi yena śreyo hyavāpsyasi
|
pītvā'mṛtamiva prājño jñānatṛpto bhaviṣyasi ||5||

6dharmasya vidhayo naike ye vai proktā maharṣibhiḥ
|
svaṃ svaṃ vijñānamāśritya damasteṣāṃ parāyaṇam ||6||

7damaṃ niḥśreyasaṃ prāhurvṛddhā niścitadarśinaḥ
|
brāhmaṇasya viśeṣeṇa damo dharmaḥ sanātanaḥ ||7||

8damāttasya kriyāsiddhiryathāvadupalabhyate
|
damo dānaṃ tathā yajñānadhītaṃ cātivartate ||8||

9damastejo vardhayati pavitraṃ ca damaḥ param
|
vipāpmā tejasā yuktaḥ puruṣo vindate mahat ||9||

10damena sadṛśaṃ dharmaṃ nānyaṃ lokeṣu śuśruma
|
damo hi paramo loke praśastaḥ sarvadharmiṇām ||10||

11pretya cātra manuṣyendra paramaṃ vindate sukham
|
damena hi samāyukto mahāntaṃ dharmamaśnute ||11||

12sukhaṃ dāntaḥ prasvapiti sukhaṃ ca pratibudhyate
|
sukhaṃ paryeti lokāṃśca manaścāsya prasīdati ||12||

13adāntaḥ puruṣaḥ kleśamabhīkṣṇaṃ pratipadyate
|
anarthāṃśca bahūnanyān prasṛjatyātmadoṣajān ||13||

14āśrameṣu caturṣbāhurdamamevottamaṃ vratam
|
tasya liṅgāni vakṣyāmi yeṣāṃ samudayo damaḥ ||14||

15kṣamā dhṛtirahiṃsā ca samatā satyamārjavam
|
indriyābhijayo dākṣyaṃ mārdavaṃ hrīracāpalam ||15||

16akārpaṇyamasaṃrambhaḥ saṃtoṣaḥ priyavāditā
|
avihiṃsānasūyā cāpyeṣāṃ samudayo damaḥ ||16||

17gurupūjā ca kauravya dayā bhūteṣu paiśunam
|
janavādaṃ mṛṣāvādaṃ stutinindāvisarjanam ||17||

18kāmaṃ krodhaṃ ca lobhaṃ ca darpaṃ stambhaṃ vikatthanam
|
roṣamīrṣyā̭vamānaṃ ca naiva dānto niṣevate ||18||

19anindito hyakāmātmā nālpeṣvarthyanasūyakaḥ
|
samudrakalpaḥ sa naro na kathaṃcana pūryate ||19||

20ahaṃ tvayi mamatvaṃ ca mayi teṣu tathā'pyaham
|
pūrvasambandhisaṃyogaṃ naitaddānto niṣevate ||20||

21sarvā grāmyāstathā''raṇyā yāśca loke pravṛttayaḥ
|
nindāṃ caiva praśaṃsāṃ ca yo nāśrayati mucyate ||21||

22maitro'tha śīlasampannaḥ prasannātmā''tmavicca yaḥ
|
muktasya vividhaiḥ saṅgaistasya pretya phalaṃ mahat ||22||

23suvṛttaḥ śīlasampannaḥ prasannātmā''tmavidbudhaḥ
|
prāpyeha loke satkāraṃ sugatiṃ pratipadyate ||23||

24karma yacchubhameveha sadbhirācaritaṃ ca yat
|
tadeva jñānayuktasya munervartma na hīyate ||24||

25niṣkramya vanamāsthāya jñānayukto jitendriyaḥ
|
kālākāṅkṣī caratyevaṃ brahmabhūyāya kalpate ||25||

26abhayaṃ yasya bhūtebhyo bhūtānāmabhayaṃ yataḥ
|
tasya dehādvimuktasya bhayaṃ nāsti kutaścana ||26||

27avācinoti karmāṇi na ca sampracinoti ha
|
samaḥ sarveṣu bhūteṣu maitrāyaṇagatiścaret ||27||

28śakunīnāmivākāśe jale vāricarasya ca
|
yathā gatirna dṛśyeta tathā tasya na saṃśayaḥ ||28||

29gṛhānutsṛjya yo rājan mokṣamevābhipadyate
|
lokāstejomayāstasya kalpante śāśvatīḥ samāḥ ||29||

30saṃnyasya sarvakarmāṇi saṃnyasya vidhivattapaḥ
|
saṃnyasya vividhā vidyāḥ sarvaṃ saṃnyasya caiva ha ||30||

31kāme śuciranāvṛttaḥ prasannātmā''tmavicchuciḥ
|
prāpyeha loke satkāraṃ svarga samabhipadyate ||31||

32yacca paitāmahaṃ sthānaṃ brahmarāśisamudbhavam
|
guhāyāṃ pihitaṃ nityaṃ taddamenābhigamyate ||32||

33jñānārāmasya buddhasya sarvabhūtāvirodhinaḥ
|
nāvṛttibhayamastīha paralokabhayaṃ kutaḥ ||33||

34eka eva dame doṣo dvitīyo nopapadyate
|
yadenaṃ kṣamayā yuktamaśaktaṃ manyate janaḥ ||34||

35eko'sya sumahāprājñaḥ doṣaḥ syāt sumahānguṇaḥ
|
kṣamayā vipulā lokāḥ sulabhā hi sahiṣṇutā ||35||

36dāntasya kimaraṇyena tathā'dāntasya bhārata
|
yatraiva nivaseddāntastadaraṇyaṃ sa cāśramaḥ ||36||

37vaiśampāyana uvāca|
etadbhīṣmasya vacanaṃ śrutvā rājā yudhiṣṭhiraḥ
|
amṛteneva saṃtṛptaḥ prahṛṣṭaḥ samapadyata ||37||

38punaśca paripapraccha bhīṣmaṃ dharmabhṛtāṃ varam
|
tapaḥ prītaḥ sa covāca tasmai sarvaṃ kurūdvaha ||38||
