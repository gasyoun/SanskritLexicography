Mahābhārata 4.7 (Virāṭaparvan)

1vaiśampāyana uvāca|tato virāṭaṃ prathamaṃ yudhiṣṭhiro rājā sabhāyāmupaviṣṭamāvrajat
|
vaidūryarūpān pratimucya kāñcanānakṣān sa kakṣe parigṛhya vāsasā ||1||

2narādhipo rāṣṭrapatiṃ yaśasvinaṃ mahāyaśāḥ kauravavaṃśavardhanaḥ
|
mahānubhāvo nararājasatkṛto durāsadastīkṣṇaviṣo yathoragaḥ ||2||

3balena rūpeṇa nararṣabho mahānapūrvarūpeṇa yathāmarastathā
|
mahābhrajālairiva saṃvṛto raviryathā'nalo bhasmavṛtaśca vīryavān ||3||

4tamāpatantaṃ prasamīkṣya pāṇḍavaṃ virāṭarāḍindumivābhrasaṃvṛtam
|
samāgataṃ pūrṇaśaśiprabhānanaṃ mahānubhāvaṃ nacireṇa dṛṣṭavān ||4||

5mantridvijān sūtamukhānviśastathā ye cāpi kecit paritaḥ samāsate
|
papraccha ko'yaṃ prathamaṃ sameyivān nṛpopamo'yaṃ samavekṣate sabhām ||5||

6sa tu dvijo'yaṃ bhavitā narottamaḥ patiḥ pṛthivyā iti me manogatam
|
na cāsya dāso na ratho na kuñjaraḥ samīpato bhrājati cāyamindravat ||6||

7śarīraliṅgairupasūcito hyayaṃ mūrddhābhiṣikta iti me manogatam
|
samīpamāyāti ca me gatavyatho yathā gajastāmarasīṃ madotkaṭaḥ ||7||

8vitarkayantaṃ tu nararṣabhastathā yudhiṣṭhiro'bhyetya virāṭamabravīt
|
samrāḍvijānātviha jīvanārthinaṃ vinaṣṭasarvasvamupāgataṃ dvijam ||8||

9ihāhamicchāmi tavānaghāntike vastuṃ yathā kāmacarastathā vibho
|
tamabravīt svāgatamityanantaraṃ rājā prahṛṣṭaḥ pratisaṃgṛhāṇa ca ||9||

10taṃ rājasiṃhaṃ pratigṛhya rājā prītyā''tmanā cainamidaṃ babhāṣe
|
kāmena tātābhivadāmyahaṃ tvāṃ kasyāsi rājño viṣayādihāgataḥ ||10||

11gotraṃ ca nāmāpi ca śaṃsa tattvataḥ
|
kiṃ cāpi śilpaṃ tava vidyate kṛtam ||11||

12yudhiṣṭhira uvāca|yudhiṣṭhirasyāsamahaṃ purā sakhā vaiyāghrapadyaḥ punarasmi vipraḥ
|
akṣān prayoktuṃ kuśalo'smi devināṃ kaṅketi nāmnā'smi virāṭa viśrutaḥ||12||

13virāṭa uvāca|dadāmi te hanta varaṃ yamicchasi praśādhi matsyān vaśago hyahaṃ tava
|
priyāśca dhūrtā mama devinaḥ sadā bhavāṃśca devopama rājyamarhati ||13||

14yudhiṣṭhira uvāca|prāpto vivādaḥ prathamaṃ viśāmpate na vidyate kaṃcana matsya hīnataḥ
|
na me jitaḥ kaścana dhārayeddhanaṃ varo mamaiṣo'stu tava prasādajaḥ ||14||

15virāṭa uvāca|hanyāmavaśyaṃ yadi te'priyaṃ caret pravrājayeyaṃ viṣayāddvijāṃstathā
|
śṛṇvantu me jānapadāḥ samāgatāḥ kaṅko yayā'haṃ viṣaye prabhustathā ||15||

16samānayāno bhavitā'si me sakhā prabhūtavastro bahupānabhojanaḥ
|
paśyestvamantaśca bahiśca sarvadā kṛtaṃ ca te dvāramapāvṛtaṃ mayā ||16||

17ye tvā'nuvāde'yuravṛttikarśitā brūyāśca teṣāṃ vacanena māṃ sadā
|
dāsyāmi sarvaṃ tadahaṃ na saṃśayo na te bhayaṃ vidyati sannidhau mama ||17||

18vaiśampāyana uvāca|evaṃ sa labdhvā tu varaṃ samāgamaṃ virāṭarājena nararṣabhastadā
|
uvāsa dhīraḥ paramārcitaḥ sukhī na cāpi kaściccaritaṃ bubodha tat ||18||
