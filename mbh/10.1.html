Mahābhārata 10.1 (Sauptikaparvan)

1sañjaya uvāca|
tataste sahitā vīrāḥ prayātā dakṣiṇāmukhāḥ
|
upāstamanavelāyāṃ śibirābhyāśamāgatāḥ||1||

2vimucya vāhāṃstvaritā bhītāḥ samabhavaṃstadā
|
gahanaṃ deśamāsādya pracchannānyaviśanta te||2||

3senāniveśamabhito nātidūramavasthitāḥ
|
nikṛttā niśitaiḥ śastraiḥ samantātkṣatavikṣatāḥ||3||

4dīrghamuṣṇaṃ ca niḥśvasya pāṇḍavāneva cintayan
|
śrutvā ca ninadaṃ ghoraṃ pāṇḍavānāṃ jayaiṣiṇām||4||

5anusārabhayādbhītāḥ prāṅmukhāḥ prādravanpunaḥ
|
te muhūrttāttato gatvā śrāntavāhāḥ pipāsitāḥ||5||

6nāmṛṣyanta maheṣvāsāḥ krodhāmarṣavaśaṃgatāḥ
|
rājño vadhena santaptā muhūrttaṃ samavasthitāḥ||6||

7dhṛtarāṣṭra uvāca|
aśraddheyamidaṃ karma kṛtaṃ bhīmena sañjaya
|
yatsa nāgāyutaprāṇaḥ putro mama nipātitaḥ||7||

8avadhyaḥ sarvabhūtānāṃ vajrasaṃhanano yuvā
|
pāṇḍavaiḥ samare putro nihato mama sañjaya||8||

9na diṣṭamabhyatikrāntuṃ śakyaṃ gāvalgaṇe naraiḥ
|
yatsametya raṇe pārthaḥ putro mama nipātitaḥ||9||

10adrisāramayaṃ nūnaṃ hṛdayaṃ mama sañjaya
|
hataṃ putraśataṃ śrutvā yanna dīrṇaṃ sahasradhā||10||

11kathaṃ hi vṛddhamithunaṃ hataputraṃ bhaviṣyati
|
na hyahaṃ pāṇḍaveyasya viṣaye vastumutsahe||11||

12kathaṃ rājñaḥ pitā bhūtvā svayaṃ rājā ca sañjaya
|
preṣyabhūtaḥ pravarteyaṃ pāṇḍaveyasya śāsanāt||12||

13ājñāpya pṛthivīṃ sarvāṃ sthitvā mūrddhani sañjaya
|
yena putraśataṃ pūrṇamekena nihataṃ mama||13||

14kṛtaṃ satyaṃ vacastasya vidurasya mahātmanaḥ
|
akurvatā vacastena mama putreṇa sañjaya||14||

15kathamasya bhaviṣyāmi preṣyabhūto durantakṛt
|
kathaṃ bhīmasya vākyāni śrotuṃ śakṣyāmi sañjaya||15||

16adharmeṇa hate tāta putre duryodhane mama
|
kṛtavarmā kṛpo drauṇiḥ kimakurvata sañjaya||16||

17sañjaya uvāca|
gatvā tu tāvakā rājannātidūramavasthitāḥ
|
apaśyanta vanaṃ ghoraṃ nānādrumalatāvṛtam||17||

18te muhūrtaṃ tu viśramya labdhatoyairhayottamaiḥ
|
sūryāstamanavelāyāṃ samāsedurmahadvanam||18||

19nānāmṛgagaṇairjuṣṭaṃ nānāpakṣigaṇāvṛtam
|
nānādrumalatācchannaṃ nānāvyālaniṣevitam||19||

20nānātoyaiḥ samākīrṇaṃ nānāpuṣpopaśobhitam
|
padminīśatasañcchannaṃ nīlotpalasamāyutam||20||

21praviśya tadvanaṃ ghoraṃ vīkṣamāṇāḥ samantataḥ
|
śākhāsahasrasañchannaṃ nyagrodhaṃ dadṛśustataḥ||21||

22upetya tu tadā rājan nyagrodhaṃ te mahārathāḥ
|
dadṛśurdvipadāṃ śreṣṭhāḥ śreṣṭhaṃ taṃ vai vanaspatim||22||

23te'vatīrya rathebhyaśca vipramucya ca vājinaḥ
|
upaspṛśya yathānyāyaṃ sandhyāmanvāsata prabho||23||

24tato'staṃ parvataśreṣṭhamanuprāpte divākare
|
sarvasya jagato dhātrī śarvarī samapadyata||24||

25grahanakṣatratārābhiḥ sampūrṇābhiralaṅkṛtam
|
nabhoṃśukamivābhāti prekṣaṇīyaṃ samantataḥ||25||

26icchayā te pravalganti ye sattvā rātricāriṇaḥ
|
divācarāśca ye sattvāste nidrāvaśamāgatāḥ||26||

27rātriñcarāṇāṃ satvānāṃ nirghoṣo'bhūtsudāruṇaḥ
|
kravyādāśca pramuditā ghorā prāptā ca śarvarī||27||

28tasmin rātrimukhe ghore duḥkhaśokasamanvitāḥ
|
kṛtavarmā kṛpo drauṇirupopaviviśuḥ samam||28||

29tatropaviṣṭāḥ śocanto nyagrodhasya samīpataḥ
|
tamevārthamatikrāntaṃ kurupāṇḍavayoḥ kṣayam||29||

30nidrayā ca parītāṅgā niṣedurdharaṇītale
|
śrameṇa sudṛḍhaṃ yuktā vikṣatā vividhaiḥ śaraiḥ||30||

31tato nidrāvaśaṃ prāptau kṛpabhojau mahārathau
|
sukhocitāvaduḥkhārhau niṣaṇṇau dharaṇītale||31||

32tau tu suptau mahārāja śramaśokasamanvitau
|
mahārhaśayanopetau bhūmāveva hyanāthavat||32||

33krodhāmarṣaśataṃ prāpto droṇaputrastu bhārata
|
na vai sma sa jagāmātha nidrāṃ sarpa iva śvasan||33||

34na lebhe sa tu nidrāṃ vai dahyamāno hi manyunā
|
vīkṣāñcakre mahābāhustadvanaṃ ghoradarśanam||34||

35vīkṣamāṇo vanoddeśaṃ nānāsatvairniṣevitam
|
apaśyata mahābāhurnyagrodhaṃ vāyasairyutam||35||

36tatra kākasahasrāṇi tāṃ niśāṃ paryaṇāmayan
|
sukhaṃ svapanti kauravya pṛthakpṛthagupāśrayāḥ||36||

37supteṣu teṣu kākeṣu viśrabdheṣu samantataḥ
|
so'paśyatsahasāyāntamulūkaṃ ghoradarśanam||37||

38mahāsvanaṃ mahākāyaṃ haryakṣaṃ babhrupiṅgalam
|
sudīrghaghoṇānakharaṃ suparṇamiva vegitam||38||

39so'tha śabdaṃ mṛduṃ kṛtvā līyamāna ivāṇḍajaḥ
|
nyagrodhasya tataḥ śākhāṃ prārthayāmāsa bhārata||39||

40sannipatya tu śākhāyāṃ nyagrodhasya vihaṅgamaḥ
|
sutāñjaghāna subahūnvāyasānvāyasāntakaḥ||40||

41keṣāñcidacchinatpakṣān śirāṃsi ca cakarta ha
|
caraṇāṃścaiva keṣāñcidbabhañja caraṇāyudhaḥ||41||

42kṣaṇenāhanadbalavānye'sya dṛṣṭipathe sthitāḥ
|
teṣāṃ śarīrāvayavaiḥ śarīraiśca viśāmpate||42||

43nyagrodhamaṇḍalaṃ sarvaṃ sañchannaṃ sarvato'bhavat
|
tāṃstu hatvā tataḥ kākān kauśiko mudito'bhavat||43||

44pratikṛtya yathākāmaṃ śatrūṇāṃ śatrusūdanaḥ
|
taddṛṣṭvā sopadhaṃ karma kauśikena kṛtaṃ niśi||44||

45tadbhāve kṛtasaṅkalpo drauṇireko'nvacintayat
|
upadeśaḥ kṛto'nena pakṣiṇā mama saṃyuge||45||

46śatrūṇāṃ kṣapaṇe yuktaḥ prāptaḥ kālaśca me mataḥ
|
nādya śakyā mayā hantuṃ pāṇḍavājitakāśinaḥ||46||

47balavantaḥ kṛtotsāhāḥ prāptalakṣāḥ prahāriṇaḥ
|
rājñaḥ sakāśātteṣāṃ tu pratijñāto vadho mayā||47||

48pataṅgāgnisamāṃ vṛttimāsthāyātmavināśinīm
|
nyāyato yudhyamānasya prāṇatyāgo na saṃśayaḥ||48||

49chadmanā ca bhavetsiddhiḥ śatrūṇāṃ ca kṣayo mahān
|
tataḥ saṃśayitādarthādyo'rtho niḥsaṃśayo bhavet||49||

50taṃ janā bahu manyante ye ca śāstraviśāradāḥ
|
yaccāpyatra bhavedvākyaṃ garhitaṃ lokaninditam||50||

51kartavyaṃ tanmanuṣyeṇa kṣatradharmeṇa vartatā
|
ninditāni ca sarvāṇi kutsitāni pade pade||51||

52sopadhāni kṛtānyeva pāṇḍavairakṛtātmabhiḥ
|
asminnarthe purā gītāḥ śrūyante dharmacintakaiḥ||52||

53ślokā nyāyamavekṣadbhistattvārthāstattvadarśibhiḥ
|
pariśrānte vidīrṇe vā bhuñjāne vāpi śatrubhiḥ||53||

54prasthāne vā praveśe vā prahartavyaṃ riporbalam
|
nidrārtamardharātre ca tathā naṣṭapraṇāyakam||54||

55bhinnayodhaṃ balaṃ yacca dvidhā yuktaṃ ca yadbhavet
|
ityevaṃ niścayaṃ cakre suptānāṃ niśi māraṇe||55||

56pāṇḍūnāṃ saha pāñcālairdroṇaputraḥ pratāpavān
|
sa krūrāṃ matimāsthāya viniścitya muhurmuhuḥ||56||

57suptau prābodhayatau tu mātulaṃ bhojameva ca
|
to prabuddhau mahātmānau kṛpabhojau mahābalau||57||

58nottaraṃ pratipadyetāṃ tatra yuktaṃ hriyā vṛttau
|
sa muhūrtamiva dhyātvā vāṣpavihvalamabravīt||58||

59hato duryodhano rājā ekavīro mahābalaḥ
|
yasyārthe vairamasmābhirāsaktaṃ pāṇḍavaiḥ saha||59||

60ekākī bahubhiḥ kṣudrairāhave śuddhavikramaḥ
|
pātito bhīmasenena ekādaśacamūpatiḥ||60||

61vṛkodareṇa kṣudreṇa sunuśaṃsamidaṃ kṛtam
|
mūrdhābhiṣiktasya śiraḥ pādena parimṛdnatā||61||

62vinardanti ca pañcālāḥ kṣvelanti ca hasanti ca
|
dhamanti śaṅkhān śataśo hṛṣṭā ghnanti ca dundubhīn||62||

63vāditraghoṣastumulo vimiśraḥ śaṅkhaniḥsvanaiḥ
|
anilenerito ghoro diśaḥ pūrayatīva ha||63||

64aśvānāṃ heṣamāṇānāṃ gajānāṃ caiva bṛṃhatām
|
siṃhanādaśca śūrāṇāṃ śrūyate sumahānayam||64||

65diśaṃ prācīṃ samāśritya hṛṣṭānāṃ gacchatāṃ bhṛśam
|
rathanemisvanāścaiva śrūyante lomaharṣaṇāḥ||65||

66pāṇḍavairdhārtarāṣṭrāṇāṃ yadidaṃ kadanaṃ kṛtam
|
vayameva trayaḥ śiṣṭā asminmahati vaiśase||66||

67kecinnāgaśataprāṇāḥ kecitsarvāstra kovidāḥ
|
nihatāḥ pāṇḍaveyaiste manye kālasya paryayam||67||

68evametena bhāvyaṃ hi nūnaṃ kāryeṇa tatvataḥ
|
yathā hyasyedṛśī niṣṭhā kṛtakārye'pi duṣkare||68||

69bhavato'stu yadi prajñā na mohādapanīyate
|
vyāpanne'sminmahatyarthe yannaḥ śreyastaducyatām||69||
