Mahābhārata 2.49 (Sabhāparvan)

1vaiśampāyana uvāca|
anubhūya tu rājñastaṃ rājasūyaṃ mahākratum
|
yudhiṣṭhirasya nṛpatergāndhārīputrasaṃyutaḥ||1||

2priyakṛnmatamājñāya pūrvaṃ duryodhanasya tat
|
prajñācakṣuṣamāsīnaṃ śakuniḥ saubalastadā||2||

3duryodhanavacaḥ śrutvā dhṛtarāṣṭraṃ janādhipam
|
upagamya mahāprājñaṃ śakunirvākyamabravīt||3||

4śakuniruvāca| duryodhano mahārāja vivarṇo hariṇaḥ kṛśaḥ
|
dīnaścintāparaścaiva taṃ viddhi manujādhipa||4||

5na vai parīkṣase samyagasahyaṃ śatrusaṃbhavam
|
jyeṣṭhaputrasya hṛcchokaṃ kimarthaṃ nāvabudhyase||5||

6dhṛtarāṣṭra uvāca|
duryodhana kutomūlaṃ bhṛśamārto'si putraka
|
śrotavyaścenmayā so'rtho brūhi me kurunandana||6||

7ayaṃ tvāṃ śakuniḥ prāha vivarṇaṃ hariṇaṃ kṛśam
|
cintayaṃśca na paśyāmi śokasya tava sambhavam||7||

8aiśvaryaṃ hi mahatputra tvayi sarvaṃ pratiṣṭhitam
|
bhrātaraḥ suhṛdaścaiva nācaranti tavāpriyam||8||

9ācchādayasi prāvārānaśnāsi piśitaudanam
|
ājāneyā vahantyaśvāḥ kenāsi hariṇaḥ kṛśaḥ||9||

10śayanāni mahārhāṇi yoṣitaśca manoramāḥ
|
guṇavanti ca veśmāni vihārāśca yathāsukham||10||

11devanāmiva te sarvaṃ vāci baddhaṃ na saṃśayaḥ
|
sa dīna iva durdharṣa kasmācchocasi putraka||11||

12duryodhana uvāca|
aśnāmyācchādaye cāhaṃ yathā kupuruṣastathā
|
amarṣaṃ dhāraye cograṃ ninīṣuḥ kālaparyayam||12||

13amarṣaṇaḥ svāḥ prakṛtīrabhibhūya paraṃ sthitaḥ
|
kleśānmumukṣuḥ parajānsa vai puruṣa ucyate||13||

14santoṣo vai śriyaṃ hanti hyabhimānaṃ ca bhārata
|
anukrośabhaye cobhe yairvṛto nāśnute mahat||14||

15na māṃ prīṇāti madbhuktaṃ śriyaṃ dṛṣṭvā yudhiṣṭhere
|
atijvalantīṃ kaunteye vivarṇakaraṇīṃ mama||15||

16sapatnānṛdhyatotmānaṃ hīyamānaṃ niśamya ca
|
adṛśyāmapi kaunteyaśriyaṃ paśyannivodyatām||16||

17tasmādahaṃ vivarṇaśca dīnaśca hariṇaḥ kṛśaḥ
|
aṣṭāśītisahasrāṇi snātakā gṛhamedhinaḥ||17||

18triṃśaddāsīka ekaiko yānbibharti yudhiṣṭhiraḥ
|
daśānyāni sahasrāṇi nityaṃ tatrānnamuttamam
|
bhuñjate rukmapātrībhiryudhiṣṭhiraniveśane||18||

19kadalīmṛgamokāni kṛṣṇaśyāmāruṇāni ca
|
kāmbhojaḥ prāhiṇottasmai parārdhyānapi kambalān
|
gajayoṣidgavāśvasya śataśo'tha sahasraśaḥ||19||

20triśataṃ coṣṭravāmīnāṃ śatāni vicarantyuta
|
rājanyā balimādāya sametā hi nṛpakṣaye||20||

21pṛthagvidhāni ratnāni pārthivāḥ pṛthivīpate
|
āharankratumukhye'sminkuntīputrāya bhūriśaḥ||21||

22na kvaciddhi mayā tādṛgdṛṣṭapūrvo na ca śrutaḥ
|
yādṛgdhanāgamo yajñe pāṇḍuputrasya dhīmataḥ||22||

23aparyantaṃ dhanaughaṃ taṃ dṛṣṭvā śatrorahaṃ nṛpa
|
śamaṃ naivābhigacchāmi cintayāno viśāmpate||23||

24brahmaṇā vāṭadhānāśca gomantaḥ śatasaṅghaśaḥ
|
trikharvaṃ balimādāya dvāri tiṣṭhanti vāritāḥ||24||

25kamaṇḍalūnupādāya jātarūpamayāñśubhān
|
etaddhanaṃ samādāya praveśaṃ lebhire na ca||25||

26yathaiva madhu śakrāya dhārayantyamarastriyaḥ
|
tadasmai kāṃsyamāhārṣīdvāruṇaṃ kalaśodadhiḥ||26||

27śaṅkhapravaramādāya vāsudevo'bhiṣiktavān
|
śakyaṃ rukmasahasrasya bahuratnavibhūṣitam||27||

28dṛṣṭvā ca mama tatsarvaṃ jvararūpamivābhavat
|
gṛhītvā tattu gacchanti samudrau pūrvadakṣiṇau||28||

29tathaiva paścimaṃ yānti gṛhītvā bharatarṣabha
|
uttaraṃ tu na gacchanti vinā tāta patatriṇaḥ||29||

30tatra gatvā'rjuno daṇḍamājahārāmitaṃ dhanam
|
idaṃ cādbhutamatrāsīttanme nigadataḥ śṛṇu||30||

31pūrṇe śatasahasre tu viprāṇāṃ pariviṣyatām
|
sthāpitā tatra sañjñābhūcchaṅkho dhmāyati nityaśaḥ||31||

32muhurmuhuḥ praṇadatastasya śaṅkhasya bhārata
|
aniśaṃ śabdamaśrauṣaṃ tato romāṇi me'hṛṣan||32||

33pārthivairbahubhiḥ kīrṇamupasthānaṃ didṛkṣubhiḥ
|
aśobhata mahārāja nakṣatrairdyairivāmalā||33||

34sarvaratnānyupādāya pārthivā vai janeśvara
|
yajñe tasya mahārāja pāṇḍuputrasya dhīmataḥ||34||

35vaiśyā iva mahīpālā dvijātipariveṣakāḥ
|
na sā śrīrdevarājasya yamasya varuṇasya ca
|
guhyakādhipatervāpi yā śrī rājanyudhiṣṭhire||35||

36tāṃ dṛṣṭvā pāṇḍuputrasya śriyaṃ paramikrāmaham
|
śāntiṃ na parigacchāmi dahyamānena cetasā||36||

37śakuniruvāca| yāmetāmatulāṃ lakṣmīṃ dṛṣṭavānasi pāṇḍave
|
tasyāḥ prāptāvupāyaṃ me śṛṇu satyaparākrama||37||

38ahamakṣeṣvabhijñātaḥ pṛthivyāmapi bhārata
|
hṛdayajñaḥ paṇajñaśca viśeṣajñaśca devane||38||

39dyūtapriyaśca kaunteyo na ca jānāti devitum
|
āhūtaścaiṣyati vyaktaṃ dyūtādapi raṇādapi||39||

40niyataṃ taṃ vijeṣyāmi kṛtvā tu kapaṭaṃ vibho
|
ānayāmi samṛddhiṃ tāṃ divyāṃ copāhvayasva tam||40||

41vaiśampāyana uvāca|
evamuktaḥ śakuninā rājā duryodhanastataḥ
|
dhṛtarāṣṭramidaṃ vākyamapadāntaramabravīt||41||

42ayamutsahate rājanśriyamāhartumakṣavit
|
dyūtena pāṇḍuputrasya tadanujñātumarhasi||42||

43dhṛtarāṣṭra uvāca|
kṣattā mantrī mahāprājñaḥ sthito yasyāsmi śāsane
|
tena saṅgamya vetsyāmi kāryasyāsya viniścayam||43||

44sa hi dharmaṃ puraskṛtya dīrghadarśī paraṃ hitam
|
ubhayoḥ pakṣayoryuktaṃ vakṣyatyarthaviniścayam||44||

45duryodhana uvāca|
nivartayiṣyati tvā'sau yadi kṣattā sameṣyati
|
nivṛtte tvayi rājendra mariṣye'hamasaṃśayam||45||

46sa tvaṃ mayi mṛte rājanvidureṇa sukhī bhava
|
bhokṣyase pṛthivīṃ kṛtsnāṃ kiṃ mayā tvaṃ kariṣyasi||46||

47śampāyana uvāca|
ārtavākyaṃ tu tattasya praṇayoktaṃ niśamya saḥ
|
dhṛtarāṣṭro'bravītpreṣyānduryodhanamate sthitaḥ||47||

48sthūṇāsahasrairbṛhatīṃ śatadvārāṃ sabhāṃ mama
|
manoramāṃ darśanīyāmāśu kurvantuṃ śilpinaḥ||48||

49tataḥ saṃstīrya ratnaistāṃ takṣṇa ānāyya sarvaśaḥ
|
sukṛtāṃ supraveśāṃ ca nivedayata me śanaiḥ||49||

50dūryodhanasya śāntyarthamiti niścitya bhūmipaḥ
|
dhṛtarāṣṭro mahārāja prāhiṇodvidurāya vai||50||

51apṛṣṭvā viduraṃ svasya nāsītkaścidviniścayaḥ
|
dyūte doṣāṃśca jānansa putrasnehādakṛṣyata||51||

52tacchrutvā viduro dhīmānkalidvāramupasthitam
|
vināśamukhamutpannaṃ dhṛtarāṣṭramupādravat||52||

53so'bhigamya mahātmānaṃ bhrātā bhrātaramagrajam
|
mūrdhnā praṇamya caraṇāvidaṃ vacanamabravīt||53||

54vidura uvāca|
nābhinandāmi te rājanvyavasāyamimaṃ prabho
|
putrairbhedo yathā na sthāddyūtahetostathā kuru||54||

55dhṛtarāṣṭra uvāca|
kṣattaḥ putreṣu putrairme kalaho na bhaviṣyati
|
yadi devāḥ prasādaṃ naḥ kariṣyanti na saṃśayaḥ||55||

56aśubhaṃ vā śubhaṃ vāpi hitaṃ vā yadi vā'hitam
|
pravartatāṃ suhṛddyūtaṃ diṣṭametanna saṃśayaḥ||56||

57mayi sannihite droṇe bhīṣme tvayi ca bhārata
|
anayo daivavihito na kathañcidbhaviṣyati||57||

58gaccha tvaṃ rathamāsthāya hayairvātasamairjave
|
khāṇḍavaprasthamadyaiva samānaya yudhiṣṭhiram||58||

59na vācyo vyavasāyo me viduraitadbravīmi te
|
daivameva paraṃ manye yenaitadupapadyate||59||

60ityukto viduro dhīmānnedamastīti cintayan
|
āpageyaṃ mahāprājñamabhyagacchatsuduḥkhitaḥ||60||
