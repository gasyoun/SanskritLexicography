Mahābhārata 1.204 (Ādiparvan)

1droṇa uvāca|
mantrāya samupānītairdhṛtarāṣṭra hitairnṛpa
|
dharmyamarthyaṃ yaśasyaṃ ca vācyamityanuśuśruma||1||

2mamāpyeṣā matistāta yā bhīṣmasya mahātmanaḥ
|
saṃvibhajyāstu kaunteyā dharma eṣa sanātanaḥ||2||

3preṣyatāṃ drupadāyāśu naṟaḥ kaścitpriyaṃvadaḥ
|
bahulaṃ ratnamādāya teṣāmarthāya bhārata||3||

4mithaḥ kṛtyaṃ ca tasmai sa ādāya vasu gacchatu
|
vṛddhiṃ ca paramāṃ brūyāttatsaṃyogodbhavāṃ tathā||4||

5saṃprīyamāṇaṃ tvāṃ brūyādrājanduryodhanaṃ tathā
|
asakṛddrupade caiva dhṛṣṭadyumne ca bhārata||5||

6ucitatvaṃ priyatvaṃ ca yogasyāpi ca varṇayet
|
punaḥpunaśca kaunyeyānmādrīputrau ca sāntvayan||6||

7hiraṇmayāni śubhrāṇi bahūnyābharaṇāni ca
|
vacanāttava rājendra draupadyāḥ saṃprayacchatu||7||

8tathā drupadaputrāṇāṃ sarveṣāṃ bharatarṣabha
|
pāṇḍavānāṃ ca sarveṣāṃ kuntyā yuktāni yāni ca||8||

9evaṃ sāntvasamāyuktaṃ drupadaṃ pāṇḍavaiḥ saha
|
uktvā so'nantaraṃ brūyātteṣāmāgamanaṃ prati||9||

10anujñāteṣu vīreṣu balaṃ gacchatu śobhanam
|
duḥśāsano vikarṇaścāpyānetuṃ pāṇḍavāniha||10||

11tataste pāṇḍavāḥ śreṣṭhāḥ pūjyamānāḥ sadā tvayā
|
prakṛtīnāmanumate pade sthāsyanti paitṛke||11||

12etattava mahārāja putreṣu teṣu caiva hi
|
vṛttamaupayikaṃ manye bhīṣmeṇa saha bhārata||12||

13karṇa uvāca|
yojitāvarthamānābhyāṃ sarvakāryeṣvanantarau
|
na mantrayetāṃ tvacchreyaḥ kimadbhutataraṃ tataḥ||13||

14duṣṭena manasā yo vai pracchannenāntarātmanā
|
brūyānni:śreyasaṃ nāma kathaṃ kuryātsatāṃ matam||14||

15na mitrāṇyarthakṛcchreṣu śreyase cetarāya vā
|
vidhipūrvaṃ hi sarvasya duḥkhaṃ vā yadi vā sukham||15||

16kṛtaprajño'kṛtaprajño bālo vṛddhaśca mānavaḥ
|
sasahāyo'sahāyaśca sarvaṃ sarvatra vindati||16||

17śrūyate hi purā kaścidambuvīca itīśvaraḥ
|
āsīdrājagṛhe rājā māgadhānāṃ mahīkṣitām||17||

18sa hīnaḥ karaṇaiḥ sarvairucchvāsaparamo nṛpaḥ
|
amātyasaṃsthaḥ sarveṣu kāryeṣvevābhavattadā||18||

19tasyāmātyo mahākarṇirbabhūvaikeśvarastadā
|
sa labdhabalamātmānaṃ manyamānovamanyate||19||

20sa rājña upabhogyāni striyo ratnadhanāni ca
|
ādade sarvaśo mūḍha aiśvaryaṃ ca svayaṃ tadā||20||

21tadādāya ca lubdhasya lobhāllobho'bhyavardhata
|
tathā hi sarvamādāya rājyamasya jihīrṣati||21||

22hīnasya karaṇaiḥ sarvairucchvāsaparamasya ca
|
yatamāno'pi tadrājyaṃ na śaśāketi naḥ śrutam||22||

23kimanyadvihitā nūnaṃ tasya sā puruṣendratā
|
yadi te vihitaṃ rājyaṃ bhaviṣyati viśāṃpate||23||

24miṣataḥ sarvalokasya sthāsyate tvayi taddhruvam
|
ato'nyathā cedvihitaṃ yatamāno na lapsyase||24||

25evaṃ vidvannupādatsva mantriṇāṃ sādhvasādhutām
|
duṣṭānāṃ caiva boddhavyamaduṣṭānāṃ ca bhāṣitam||25||

26droṇa uvāca|
vidma te bhāvadoṣeṇa yadarthamidamucyate
|
duṣṭa pāṇḍavahetostvaṃ doṣamākhyāpayasyuta||26||

27hitaṃ tu paramaṃ karṇa bravīmi kulavardhanam
|
atha tvaṃ manyase duṣṭaṃ brūhi yatparamaṃ hitam||27||

28ato'nyathā cetkriyate yadbravīmi paraṃ hitam
|
kuravo vai vinaṅkṣyanti na cireṇaiva me matiḥ||28||
