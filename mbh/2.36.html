Mahābhārata 2.36 (Sabhāparvan)

1vaiśampāyana uvāca|
tato'bhiṣecanīye'hni brāhmaṇā rājabhiḥ saha
|
antarvedīṃ praviviśuḥ satkārārhā maharṣayaḥ||1||

2nāradapramukhāstasyāmantarvedyāṃ mahātmanaḥ
|
samāsīnāḥ śuśubhire saha rājarṣibhistadā||2||

3sametā brahmabhavane devā devarṣayastathā
|
karmāntaramupāsanto jajalpuramitaujasaḥ||3||

4evametanna cāpyevamevaṃ caitanna cānyathā
|
ityūcurbahavastatra vitaṇḍāṃ vai parasparam||4||

5kṛśānarthāṃstataḥ kecidakṛśāṃstatra kurvate
|
akṛśāṃśca kṛśāṃścakrurhetubhiḥ śāstraniścayaiḥ||5||

6tatra medhāvinaḥ kecidarthamanyairudīritam
|
vicikṣipuryathā śyenā nabhogatamivāmiṣam||6||

7keciddharmārthakuśalāḥ kecittatra mahāvratāḥ
|
remire kathayantaśca sarvabhāṣyavidāṃ varāḥ||7||

8sā vedirvedasampannairdevadvijamaharṣibhiḥ
|
ābabhāse samākīrṇā nakṣatrairdyaurivāyatā||8||

9na tasyāṃ sannidhau śūdraḥ kaścidāsīnna cāvratī
|
antarvedyāṃ tadā rājanyudhiṣṭhiraniveśane||9||

10tāṃ tu lakṣmīvato lakṣmīṃ tadā yajñavidhānajām
|
tutoṣa nāradaḥ paśyandharmarājasya dhīmataḥ||10||

11atha cintāṃ samāpede sa munirmanujādhipa
|
nāradastu tadā paśyansarvakṣatrasamāgamam||11||

12sasmāra ca purāvṛttāṃ kathāṃ tāṃ puruṣarṣabha
|
aṃśāvataraṇe yā'sau brahmaṇo bhavane'bhavat||12||

13devānāṃ saṅgamaṃ taṃ tu vijñāya kurunandana
|
nāradaḥ puṇḍarīkākṣaṃ sasmāra manasā harim||13||

14sākṣātsa vibudhārighnaḥ kṣatre nārāyaṇo vibhuḥ
|
pratijñāṃ pālayaṃścemāṃ jātaḥ parapurañjayaḥ||14||

15sandideśa purā yo'sau vibudhānbhūtakṛtsvayam
|
anyonyamabhinighnantaḥ punarlokānavāpsyatha||15||

16iti nārāyaṇaḥ śambhurbhagavānbhūtabhāvanaḥ
|
ādityavibudhānsarvānajāyata yadukṣaye||16||

17kṣitāvandhakavṛṣṇīnāṃ vaṃśe vaṃśabhṛtāṃ varaḥ
|
parayā śuśubhe lakṣmyā nakṣatrāṇāmivoḍurāṭ||17||

18yasya bāhubalaṃ sendrāḥ surāḥ sarva upāsate
|
soyaṃ mānuṣavannāma harirāste'rimardanaḥ||18||

19aho bata mahadbhūtaṃ svayambhūryadidaṃ svayam
|
ādāsyati punaḥ kṣatramevaṃ balasamanvitam||19||

20ityetāṃ nāradaścintāṃ cintayāmāsa sarvavit
|
hariṃ nārāyaṇaṃ jñātvā yajñairījyantamīśvaram||20||

21tasmindharmavidāṃ śreṣṭho dharmarājasya dhīmataḥ
|
mahādhvare mahābuddhistasthau sa bahumānataḥ||21||

22tato bhīṣmo'bravīdrājandharmarājaṃ yudhiṣṭhiram
|
kriyatāmarhaṇaṃ rājñāṃ yathārhamiti bhārata||22||

23ācāryamṛtvijaṃ caiva saṃyujaṃ ca yudhiṣṭhira
|
snātakaṃ ca priyaṃ prāhuḥ ṣaḍarghyārhānnṛpaṃ tathā||23||

24etānarghyānabhigatānāhuḥ saṃvatsaroṣitān
|
ta ime kālapūgasya mahato'smānupāgatāḥ||24||

25eṣāmekaikaśo rājannarghyamānīyatāmiti
|
atha caiṣāṃ variṣṭhāya samarthāyopanīyatām||25||

26yudhiṣṭhira uvāca|
kasmai bhavānmanyate'rghamekasmai kurunandana
|
upanīyamānaṃ yuktaṃ ca tanme brūhi pitāmaha||26||

27vaiśampāyana uvāca|
tato bhīṣmaḥ śāntanavo buddhyā niścitya vīryavān
|
vārṣṇeyaṃ manyate kṛṣṇamarhaṇīyatamaṃ bhuvi||27||

28eṣa hyeṣāṃ samastānāṃ tejobalaparākramaiḥ
|
madhye tapannivābhāti jyotiṣāmiva bhāskaraḥ||28||

29asūryamiva sūryeṇa nirvātamiva vāyunā
|
bhāsitaṃ hlāditaṃ caiva kṛṣṇenedaṃ sado hi naḥ||29||

30tasmai bhīṣmābhyanujñātaḥ sahadevaḥ pratāpavān
|
upajahre'tha vidhivadvārṣṇeyāyārghyamuttamam||30||

31pratijagrāha tatkṛṣṇaḥ śāstradṛṣṭena karmaṇā
|
śiśupālastu tāṃ pūjāṃ vāsudeve na cakṣame||31||

32sa upālabhya bhīṣmaṃ ca dharmarājaṃ ca saṃsadi
|
apākṣipadvāsudevaṃ cedirājo mahābalaḥ||32||
