Mahābhārata 6.56 (Bhīṣmaparvan)

1sañjaya uvāca|
prabhātāyāṃ ca śarvaryāṃ bhīṣmaḥ śāntanavastadā
|
anīkānyanusaṃyāne vyādideśātha bhārata ||1||

2gāruḍaṃ ca mahāvyūhaṃ cakre śāntanavastadā
|
putrāṇāṃ te jayākāṅkṣī bhīṣmaḥ kurupitāmahaḥ ||2||

3garuḍasya svayaṃ tuṇḍe pitā devavratastava
|
cakṣuṣī ca bharadvājaḥ kṛtavarmā ca sātvataḥ ||3||

4aśvatthāmā kṛpaścaiva śīrṣamāstāṃ yaśasvinau
|
traigartairatha kaikeyairvāṭadhāneśca saṃyuge ||4||

5bhūriśravāḥ śalaḥ śalyo bhagadattaśca māriṣa
|
madrakaḥ sindhusauvīrāstathā pāñcanadāśca ye ||5||

6jayadrathena sahitā grīvāyāṃ sanniveśitāḥ
|
pṛṣṭhe duryodhano rājā sodaryaiḥ sānugervṛtaḥ ||6||

7vindānuvindāvāvantyau kāmbojaśca śakaiḥ saha
|
pucchamāsan mahārāja śūrasenāśca sarvaśaḥ ||7||

8māgadhāśca kaliṅgāśca dāserakagaṇaiḥ saha
|
dakṣiṇaṃ pakṣamāsādya sthinā vyūhasya daṃśitāḥ ||8||

9kārūṣāśca vikuñjāśca muṇḍāḥ kuṇḍīvṛṣāstathā
|
bṛhadbalena sahitā vāmaṃ pārśvamavasthitāḥ ||9||

10vyūḍhaṃ dṛṣṭvā tu tat sainyaṃ savyasācī paraṃtapaḥ
|
dhṛṣṭadyumnena sahitaḥ pratyavyūhata saṃyuge ||10||

11ardhacandreṇa vyūhena vyūhaṃ tamatidāruṇam
|
dakṣiṇaṃ śṛṅgamāsthāya bhīmaseno vyarocata ||11||

12nānāśastraughasampannairnānādeśyairnṛpairvṛtaḥ
|
tadanveva virāṭaśca drupadaśca mahārathaḥ ||12||

13tadanantaramevāsīnnīlo nīlāyudhaiḥ saha
|
nīlādanantaraścaiva dhṛṣṭaketurmahābalaḥ ||13||

14cedikāśikarūṣaiśca pauravairapi saṃvṛtaḥ
|
dhṛṣṭadyumnaḥ śikhaṇḍī ca pañcālāśca prabhadrakāḥ ||14||

15madhye sainyasya mahataḥ sthitā yuddhāya bhārata
|
tatraiva dharmarājo'pi gajānīkena saṃvṛtaḥ ||15||

16tatastu sātyakī rājan draupadyāḥ pañca cātmajāḥ
|
abhimanyustataḥ śūra irāvāṃśca tataḥ param ||16||

17bhaimasenistato rājan kekayāśca mahārathāḥ
|
tato'bhūddvipadāṃ śreṣṭho vāmaṃ pārśvamupāśritaḥ ||17||

18sarvasya jagato goptā goptā yasya janārdanaḥ
|
evametaṃ mahāvyūhaṃ pratyavyūhanta pāṇḍavāḥ ||18||

19vadhārthaṃ tava putrāṇāṃ tatpakṣaṃ ye ca saṅgatāḥ
|
tataḥ pravavṛte yuddhaṃ vyatiṣaktarathadvipam ||19||

20tāvakānāṃ pareṣāṃ ca nighnatāmitaretaram
|
hayaughāśca rayaughāśca tatra tatra viśāṃpate ||20||

21saṃpatanto vyadṛśyanta nighnantaste parasparam
|
dhāvatāṃ ca rathaughānāṃ nighnatāṃ ca pṛthak pṛthak ||21||

22babhūva tumulaḥ śabdo vimiśro dundubhikhanaiḥ
|
divaspṛṅ naravīrāṇāṃ nighnatāmitaretaram || saṃprahāre sutumule tava teṣāṃ ca bhārata ||22||
