Mahābhārata 12.1 (Śāntiparvan)

1vaiśampāyana uvāca|
kṛtodakāste suhṛdāṃ sarveṣāṃ pāṇḍunandanāḥ
|
viduro dhṛtarāṣṭraśca sarvāśca bharatastriyaḥ ||1||

2tatra te sumahātmāno nyavasanpāṇḍunandanāḥ
|
śaucaṃ nivartayiṣyanto māsamātraṃ bahiḥ purāt ||2||

3kṛtodakaṃ tu rājānaṃ dharmaputraṃ yudhiṣṭhiram
|
abhijagmurmahātmānaḥ siddhā brahmarṣisattamāḥ ||3||

4dvaipāyano nāradaśca devalaśca mahānṛṣiḥ
|
devasthānaśca kaṇvaśca teṣāṃ śiṣyāśca sattamāḥ ||4||

5anye ca vedavidvāṃsaḥ kṛtaprajñā dvijātayaḥ
|
gṛhasthāḥ snātakāḥ santo dadṛśuḥ kurusattamam ||5||

6te'bhigamya mahātmānaḥ pūjitāśca yathāvidhi
|
āsaneṣu mahārheṣu viviśuste maharṣayaḥ ||6||

7pratigṛhya tataḥ pūjāṃ tatkālasadṛśīṃ tadā
|
paryupāsanyathānyāyaṃ parivārya yudhiṣṭhiram ||7||

8puṇye bhāgīrathītīre śokavyākulacetasam
|
āśvāsayanto rājānaṃ viprāḥ śatasahasraśaḥ ||8||

9nāradastvabravītkāle dharmaputraṃ yudhiṣṭhiram
|
saṃbhāṣya munibhiḥ sārdhaṃ kṛṣṇadaipāyanādibhiḥ ||9||

10bhavatā bāhuvīryeṇa prasādānmādhavasya ca
|
jiteyamavaniḥ kṛtsnā dharmeṇa ca yudhiṣṭhira ||10||

11diṣṭyā muktastu saṃgrāmādasmāllokabhayaṃkarāt
|
kṣatradharmaratañcāpi kaccinmodasi pāṇḍava ||11||

12kaccicca nihatāmitraḥ prīṇāsi suhṛdo nṛpa
|
kaccicchriyamimāṃ prāpya na tvāṃ śokaḥ prabādhate ||12||

13yudhiṣṭhira uvāca|
vijiteyaṃ mahī kṛtsnā kṛṣṇabāhubalāśrayāt
|
brāhmaṇānāṃ prasādena bhīmārjunabalena ca ||13||

14idaṃ mama mahadduḥkhaṃ vartate hṛdi nityadā
|
kṛtvā jñātikṣayamimaṃ mahāntaṃ lobhakāritam ||14||

15saubhadraṃ draupadeyāṃśca ghātayitvā sutān priyān
|
jayo'yamajayākāro bhagavanpratibhāti me ||15||

16kiṃ nu vakṣyati vārṣṇeyī vadhūrme madhusūdanam
|
dvārakāvāsinī kṛṣṇamitaḥ pratigataṃ harim ||16||

17draupadī hataputreyaṃ kṛpaṇā hatabāndhavā
|
asmatpriyahite yuktā bhūyaḥ pīḍayatīva mām ||17||

18idamanyattu bhagavan yattvāṃ vakṣyāmi nārada
|
mantrasaṃvaraṇenāsmi kuntyā duḥkhena yojitaḥ ||18||

19yaḥ sa nāgāyutabalo loke'pratiratho raṇe
|
siṃhakhelagatirdhīmān ghṛṇī dātā yatavrataḥ ||19||

20āśrayo dhārtarāṣṭrāṇāṃ mānī tīkṣṇaparākramaḥ
|
amarṣī nityasaṃraṃbhī kṣeptā'smākaṃ raṇe raṇe ||20||

21śīghrāstraścitrayodhī ca kṛtī cādbhutavikramaḥ
|
gūḍhotpannaḥ sutaḥ kuntyā bhrātā'smākamasau kila ||21||

22toyakarmaṇi taṃ kuntī kathayāmāsa sūryajam
|
putraṃ sarvaguṇopetamavakīrṇaṃ jale purā ||22||

23mañjūṣāyāṃ samādhāya gaṅgāsrotasyamajjayat
|
yaṃ sutaputraṃ loko'yaṃ rādheyaṃ cābhyamanyata ||23||

24sa jyeṣṭhaputraḥ kuntyā vai bhrātā'smākaṃ ca mātṛjaḥ
|
ajānatā mayā bhrātrā rājyalubdhena ghātitaḥ ||24||

25tanme dahati gātrāṇi tularāśimivānalaḥ
|
na hi taṃ veda pārtho'pi bhrātaraṃ śvetavāhanaḥ ||25||

26nāhaṃ na bhīmo na yamau sa tvasmānveda suvrataḥ
|
gatā kila pṛthā tasya sakāśamiti naḥ śrutam ||26||

27asmākaṃ śamakāmā vai tvaṃ ca putro mametyatha
|
pṛthāyā na kṛtaḥ kāmastena cāpi mahātmanā ||27||

28api paścādida mātaryavocaditi naḥ śrutam
|
na hi śakṣyāmyahaṃ tyaktuṃ nṛpaṃ duryodhanaṃ raṇe ||28||

29anāryatvaṃ nṛśaṃsatvaṃ kṛtaghnatvaṃ ca me bhavet
|
yudhiṣṭhireṇa sandhiṃ hi yadi kuryāṃ mate tava ||29||

30bhīto raṇe śvetavāhāditi māṃ maṃsyate janaḥ
|
so'haṃ nirjitya samare vijayaṃ sahakeśavam ||30||

31sandhāsye dharmaputreṇa paścāditi ca so'bravīt
|
tamuvāca kila pṛthā punaḥ pṛthulavakṣasam ||31||

32caturṇāmabhayaṃ dehi kāmaṃ yudhyasva phālgunam
|
so'bravīnmātaraṃ dhīmān vepamānāṃ kṛtāñjaliḥ ||32||

33prāptān viṣahyāṃścaturo na haniṣyāmi te sutān
|
pañcaiva hi sutā devi bhaviṣyanti tava dhruvāḥ ||33||

34sārjunā vā hate karṇe sakarṇā vā hate'rjune
|
taṃ putragṛddhinī bhūyo mātā putramathābravīt ||34||

35bhrātṝṇāṃ svasti kurvīthā yeṣāṃ svasti cikīrṣasi
|
evamuktvā kila pṛthā visṛjyopayayau gṛhān ||35||

36so'rjunena hato vīro bhrātrā bhrātā sahodaraḥ
|
na caiva vivṛto mantraḥ pṛthāyāstasya vā vibho ||36||

37atha śūro maheṣvāsaḥ pārthenājau nipātitaḥ
|
ahaṃ tvajñāsiṣaṃ paścātsvasodaryaṃ dvijottama ||37||

38pūrvajaṃ bhrātaraṃ karṇaṃ pṛthayā vacanātprabho
|
tena me dūyate tīvraṃ hṛdayaṃ bhrātṛghātinaḥ ||38||

39karṇārjunasahāyo'haṃ jayeyamapi vāsavam
|
sabhāyāṃ kliśyamānasya dhārtarāṣṭrairdurātmabhiḥ ||39||

40sahasotpatitaḥ krodhaḥ karṇaṃ dṛṣṭvā praśāmyati
|
yadā hyasya giro rūkṣāḥ śṛṇomi kaṭukādayāḥ ||40||

41sabhāyāṃ gadato dyūte duryodhanahitaiṣiṇaḥ
|
tadā naśyati me roṣaḥ pādau tasya nirīkṣya ha ||41||

42kuntyā hi sadṛśau pādau karṇasyeti matirmama
|
sādṛśyahetumanvicchan pṛthāyāstasya caiva ha ||42||

43kāraṇaṃ nādhigacchāmi kathañcidapi cintayan
|
kathaṃ nu tasya saṃgrāme pṛthivī cakramagrasat ||43||

44kathaṃ nu śapto bhrātā me tattvaṃ vaktumihārhasi
|
śrotumicchāmi bhagavaṃstattaḥ sarvaṃ yathātatham
|
bhavānhi sarvavidvidvān loke veda kṛtākṛtam||44||
