Mahābhārata 12.174 (Śāntiparvan)

1yudhiṣṭhira uvāca|
dharmāḥ pitāmahenoktā rājadharmāśritāḥ śubhāḥ
|
dharmamāśramiṇāṃ śreṣṭhaṃ vaktumarhasi pārthiva ||1||

2bhīṣma uvāca|
sarvatra vihito dharmaḥ satyapretya tapaḥphalam
|
bahudvārasya dharmasya nehāsti viphalā kriyā ||2||

3yasminyasmiṃstu viṣaye yo yo yāti viniścayam
|
sa tamevābhijānāti nānyaṃ bharatasattama ||3||

4yathā yathā ca paryeti lokatantramasāravat
|
tathā tathā virāgo'tra jāyate nātra saṃśayaḥ ||4||

5evaṃ vyavasite loke bahudoṣe yudhiṣṭhira
|
ātmamokṣanimittaṃ vai yateta matimānnaraḥ ||5||

6yudhiṣṭhira uvāca|
naṣṭe dhane vā dāre vā putre pitari vā mṛte
|
yayā buddhyā nudecchokaṃ tanme brūhi pitāmaha ||6||

7bhīṣma uvāca|
naṣṭe dhane vā dāre vā putre pitari vā mṛte
|
aho duḥkhamiti dhyāyan śokasyāpacitiṃ caret ||7||

8atrāpyudāharantīmamitihāsaṃ purātanam
|
yathā senajitaṃ vipraḥ kaścidetyābravītsuhṛt ||8||

9putraśokābhisaṃtaptaṃ rājānaṃ śokavihvalam
|
viṣaṇṇamanasaṃ dṛṣṭvā vipro vacanamabravīt ||9||

10kiṃ nu muhyasi mūḍhastvaṃ śocyaḥ kimanuśocasi
|
yadā tvāmapi śocantaḥ śocyā yāsyanti tāṃ gatim ||10||

11tvaṃ caivāhaṃ ca ye cānye tvāmupāsanti pārthiva
|
sarve tatra gamiṣyāmo yata evāgatā vayam ||11||

12senajiduvāca |
kā buddhiḥ kiṃ tapo vipra kaḥ samādhistapodhana
|
kiṃ jñānaṃ kiṃ śrutaṃ caiva yatprāpya na viṣīdasi ||12||

13brāhmaṇa uvāca|
paśya bhūtāni duḥkhena vyatiṣiktāni sarvaśaḥ
|
uttamādhamamadhyāni teṣu teṣviha karmasu ||13||

14ātmāpi cāyaṃ na mama sarvā vā pṛthivī mama
|
yathā mama tathā'nyeṣāmiti cintya na me vyathā
|
etāṃ buddhimahaṃ prāpya na prahṛṣye na ca vyathe ||14||

15yathā kāṣṭhaṃ ca kāṣṭhaṃ ca sameyātāṃ mahodadhau
|
sametya ca vyapeyātāṃ tadvadbhūtasamāgamaḥ ||15||

16evaṃ putrāśca pautrāśca jñātayo bāndhavāstathā
|
teṣāṃ sneho na kartavyo viprayogo dhruvo hi taiḥ ||16||

17adarśanādāpatitaḥ punaścādarśanaṃ gataḥ
|
na tvāsau veda na tvaṃ taṃ kaḥ san kimanuśocasi ||17||

18tṛṣṇārtiprabhavaṃ duḥkhaṃ duḥkhārtiprabhavaṃ sukham
|
sukhātsaṃjāyate duḥkhaṃ duḥkhamevaṃ punaḥ punaḥ ||18||

19sukhasyānantaraṃ duḥkhaṃ duḥkhasyānantaraṃ sukham
|
sukhaduḥkhe manuṣyāṇāṃ cakravatparivartataḥ ||19||

20sukhāttvaṃ duḥkhamāpannaḥ punarāpatsyase sukham
|
na nityaṃ labhate duḥkhaṃ na nityaṃ labhate sukham ||20||

21śarīramevāyatanaṃ sukhasya duḥkhasya cāpyāyatanaṃ śarīram
|
yadyaccharīreṇa karoti karma tenaiva dehī samupāśnute tat ||21||

22jīvitaṃ ca śarīreṇa jātyaiva saha jāyate
|
ubhe saha vivartete ubhe saha vinaśyataḥ ||22||

23snehapāśairbahuvidhairāviṣṭaviṣayā janāḥ
|
akṛtārthāśca sīdante jalaiḥ saikatasetavaḥ ||23||

24snehena tilavatsarvaṃ sargacakre nipīḍyate
|
tilapīḍairivākramya kleśairajñānasambhavaiḥ ||24||

25saṃcinotyaśubhaṃ karma kalatrāpekṣayā naraḥ
|
ekaḥ kleśānavāpnoti paratreha ca mānavaḥ ||25||

26putradārakuṭumbeṣu prasaktāḥ sarvamānavāḥ
|
śokapaṅkārṇave magnā jīrṇā vanagajā iva ||26||

27putranāśe vittanāśe jñātisambandhināmapi
|
prāpyate sumahadduḥkhaṃ dāvāgnipratimaṃ vibho
|
daivāyattamidaṃ sarvaṃ sukhaduḥkhe bhavābhavau ||27||

28asṛhṛtsasuhṛccāpi saśatrurmitravānapi
|
saprajñaḥ prajñayā hīno daivena labhate sukham ||28||

29nālaṃ sukhāya suhṛdo nālaṃ duḥkhāya śatravaḥ
|
na ca prajñā'lamarthānāṃ na sukhānāmalaṃ dhanam ||29||

30na buddhirdhanalābhāya na jāḍyamasamṛddhaye
|
lokaparyāyavṛttāntaṃ prājño jānāti netaraḥ ||30||

31buddhimantaṃ ca śūraṃ ca mūḍhaṃ bhīruṃ jaḍaṃ kavim
|
durbalaṃ balavantaṃ ca bhāginaṃ bhajate sukham ||31||

32dhenurvatsasya gopasya svāminastaskarasya ca
|
payaḥ pibati yastasyā dhenustasyeti niścayaḥ ||32||

33ye ca mūḍhatamā loke ye ca buddheḥ paraṃ gatāḥ
|
te narāḥ sukhamedhante kliśyatyantarito janaḥ ||33||

34antyeṣu remire dhīrā na te madhyeṣu remire
|
antyaprāptiṃ sukhāmāhurduḥkhamantaramantyayoḥ ||34||

35ye ca buddhisukhaṃ prāptā dvandvātītā vimatsarāḥ
|
tānnaivārthā na cānarthā vyathayanti kadācana ||35||

36atha ye buddhimaprāptā vyatikrāntāśca mūḍhatām
|
te'tivelaṃ prahṛṣyanti santāpamupayānti ca ||36||

37nityaṃ pramuditā mūḍhā divi devagaṇā iva
|
avalepena mahatā paribhūtyā vicetasaḥ ||37||

38sukhaṃ duḥkhāntamālasyaṃ duḥkhaṃ dākṣyaṃ sukhodayam
|
bhūtistvevaṃ śriyā sārdhaṃ dakṣe vasati nālase ||38||

39sukhaṃ vā yadi vā duḥkhaṃ priyaṃ vā yadi vāpriyam
|
prāptaṃ prāptamupāsīta hṛdayenāparājitaḥ ||39||

40śokasthānasahastrāṇi bhayasthānaśatāni ca
|
divase divase mūḍhamāviśanti na paṇḍitam ||40||

41buddhimantaṃ kṛtaprajñaṃ śuśrūṣumanasūyakam
|
dāntaṃ jitendriyaṃ cāpi śoko na spṛśate naram ||41||

42etāṃ buddhiṃ samāsthāya guptacittaścaredbudhaḥ
|
udayāstamayajñaṃ hi na śokaḥ spaṣṭumarhati ||42||

43yannimittaṃ bhavecchokastāpo vā duḥkhameva ca
|
āyāso vā yato mūlamekāṅgamapi tattyajet ||43||

44kiṃcideva mamatvena yadā bhavati kalpitam
|
tadeva paritāpārthaṃ sarvaṃ sampadyate tathā ||44||

45yadyattyajati kāmānāṃ tatsukhasyābhipūryate
|
kāmānusārī puruṣaḥ kāmānanuvinaśyati ||45||

46yacca kāmasukhaṃ loke yacca divyaṃ mahatsukham
|
tṛṣṇākṣayasukhasyaite nārhataḥ ṣoḍaśīṃ kalām ||46||

47pūrvadehakṛtaṃ karma śubhaṃ vā yadi vā'śubham
|
prājñaṃ mūḍhaṃ tathā śūraṃ bhajate yādṛśaṃ kṛtam ||47||

48evameva kilaitāni priyāṇyevāpriyāṇi ca
|
jīveṣu parivartante duḥkhāni ca sukhāni ca ||48||

49etāṃ buddhiṃ samāsthāya sukhamāste guṇānvitaḥ
|
sarvānkāmān jugupseta kāmānkurvīta pṛṣṭhataḥ ||49||

50vṛtta eṣa hṛṣi prauḍho mṛtyureṣa manobhavaḥ
|
krodho nāma śarīrastho dehināṃ procyate budhaiḥ ||50||

51yadā saṃharate kāmān kūrmo'ṅgānīva sarvaśaḥ
|
tadā''tmajyotirātmā'yamātmanyeva prapaśyati ||51||

52na bibheti yadā cāyaṃ yadā cāsmānna bibhyati
|
yadā necchati na dvaiṣṭi brahma sampadyate tadā ||52||

53ubhe satyānṛte tyaktvā śokānandau bhayābhaye
|
priyāpriye parityajya praśāntātmā bhaviṣyati ||53||

54yadā na kurute dhīraḥ sarvabhūteṣu pāpakam
|
karmaṇā, manasā, vācā brahma sampadyate tadā ||54||

55yā dustyajā durmatibhiryā na jīryati jīryataḥ
|
yo'sau prāṇāntiko rogastāṃ tṛṣṇāṃ tyajataḥ sukham ||55||

56atra piṅgalayā gītā gāthāḥ śrūyanti pārthiva
|
yathā sā kṛcchrakāle'pi lebhe dharmaṃ sanātanam ||56||

57saṃkete piṅgalā veśyā kāntenāsīdvinākṛtā
|
atha kṛcchragatā śāntā buddhimāsthāpayattadā ||57||

58piṅgalovāca
|
unmattā'hamanunmattaṃ kāntamanvavasaṃ ciram
|
antike ramaṇaṃ santaṃ nainamadhyagamaṃ purā ||58||

59ekasthūṇaṃ navadvāramapi dhāsyāmyagārakam
|
kā hi kāntamihāyāntamayaṃ kānteti maṃsyate ||59||

60akāmāṃ kāmarūpeṇa dhūrtā narakarūpiṇaḥ
|
na punarvañcayiṣyanti pratibuddhāsmi jāgṛmi ||60||

61anartho hi bhavedartho daivātpūrvakṛtena vā
|
sambuddhā'haṃ nirākārā nāhamadyājitendriyā ||61||

62sukhaṃ nirāśaḥ svapiti nairāśyaṃ paramaṃ sukham
|
āśāmanāśāṃ kṛtvā hi sukhaṃ svapiti piṅgalā ||62||

63bhīṣma uvāca|
etaiścānyaiśca viprasya hetumadbhiḥ prabhāṣitaiḥ
|
paryavasthāpito rājā senajinmumude sukhī ||63||
