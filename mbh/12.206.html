Mahābhārata 12.206 (Śāntiparvan)

1manuruvāca |
yadā taiḥ pañcabhiḥ pañca yuktāni manasā saha
|
atha taddrakṣyate brahma maṇau sūtramivārpitam ||1||

2tadeva ca yathā sūtraṃ suvarṇe vartate punaḥ
|
muktāsvatha pravāleṣu mṛnmaye rājate tathā ||2||

3tadvadgo'śvamanuṣyeṣu tadvaddhastimṛgādiṣu
|
tadvatkīṭapataṅgeṣu prasaktātmā svakarmabhiḥ ||3||

4yena yena śarīreṇa yadyatkarma karotyayam
|
tena tena śarīreṇa tattatphalamupāśnute ||4||

5yathā hyekarasā bhūmiroṣadhyarthānusāriṇī
|
tathā karmānugā buddhirantarātmānudarśinī ||5||

6jñānapūrvā bhavellipsā lipsāpūrvā'bhisaṃdhitā
|
abhisadhipūrvakaṃ karma karmamūlaṃ tataḥ phalam ||6||

7phalaṃ karmātmakaṃ vidyātkarma jñeyātmakaṃ tathā
|
jñeyaṃ jñānātmakaṃ vidyājjñānaṃ sadasadātmakam ||7||

8jñānānāṃ ca phalānāṃ ca jñeyānāṃ karmaṇāṃ tathā
|
kṣayānte yatphalaṃ vidyājñānaṃ jñeyapratiṣṭhitam ||8||

9mahaddhi paramaṃ bhūtaṃ yatprapaśyanti yoginaḥ
|
abudhāstaṃ na paśyanti hyātmasthaṃ guṇabuddhayaḥ ||9||

10pṛthivīrūpato rūpamapāmiha mahattaram
|
adbhyo mahattaraṃ tejastejasaḥ pavano mahān ||10||

11pavanācca mahadvyoma tasmātparataraṃ manaḥ
|
manaso mahatī buddhirbuddheḥ kālo mahānsmṛtaḥ ||11||

12kālātsa bhagavān viṣṇuryasya sarvamidaṃ jagat
|
nādirna madhyaṃ naivāntastasya devasya vidyate ||12||

13anāditvādamadhyatvādanantatvācca so'vyayaḥ
|
atyeti sarvaduḥkhāni duḥkhaṃ hyantavaducyate ||13||

14tadbrahma paramaṃ proktaṃ taddhāma paramaṃ padam
|
tadgatvā kālaviṣayādvimuktā mokṣamāśritāḥ ||14||

15guṇeṣvete prakāśante nirguṇatvāttataḥ param
|
nivṛttilakṣaṇo dharmastathā''nantyāya kalpate ||15||

16ṛco yajūṃṣi sāmāni śarīrāṇi vyapāśritāḥ
|
jihvāgreṣu pravartante yatnasādhyā vināśinaḥ ||16||

17na caivamiṣyate brahma śarīrāśrayasambhavam
|
na yatnasādhyaṃ tadbrahma nādimadhyaṃ na cāntavat ||17||

18ṛcāmādistathā sāmnāṃ yajuṣāmādirucyate
|
antaścādimatāṃ dṛṣṭo na tvādirbrahmaṇaḥ smṛtaḥ ||18||

19anāditvādanantatvāttadantamathāvyayam
|
avyayatvācca nirduḥkhaṃ dvandvābhāvastataḥ param ||19||

20adṛṣṭato'nupāyācca pratisandheśca karmaṇaḥ
|
na tena mārtyāḥ paśyanti yena gacchanti tatpadam ||20||

21viṣayeṣu ca saṃsargācchāśvatasya ca darśanāt
|
manasā cānyadākāṅkṣan paraṃ na pratipadyate ||21||

22guṇānyadiha paśyanti tadicchantyapare janāḥ
|
paraṃ naivābhikāṅkṣanti nirguṇatvādguṇārthinaḥ ||22||

23guṇairyastvavarairyuktaḥ kathaṃ vidyātparānguṇān
|
anumānāddhi gantavyaṃ guṇairavayavaiḥ param ||23||

24sūkṣmeṇa manasā vidmo vācā vaktuṃ na śaknumaḥ
|
mano hi manasā grāhyaṃ darśanena ca darśanam ||24||

25jñānena nirmalīkṛtya buddhiṃ buddhyā manastathā
|
manasā cendriyagrāmamakṣaraṃ pratipadyate ||25||

26buddhiprahīṇo manasā samṛddho nirāśiṣaṃ nirguṇamabhyupaiti
|
paraṃ tyajantīha viloḍyamānā hutāśanaṃ vāyurivendhanastham ||26||

27guṇādāne viprayoge ca teṣāṃ manaḥ sadā buddhiparāvarābhyām
|
anenaiva vidhinā sampravṛtto guṇāpāye brahmaśarīrameti ||27||

28avyaktātmā puruṣo'vyaktakarmā so'vyaktatvaṃ gacchati hyantakāle
|
tairevāyaṃ cendriyairvardhamānairglāyadbhirvā vartate'kāmarūpaḥ ||28||

29sarvairayaṃ cendriyaiḥ samprayukto dehaṃ prāptaḥ pañcabhūtāśrayaḥ syāt
|
nāsāmarthyādgacchati karmaṇeha hīnastena parameṇāvyayena ||29||

30pṛthvyāṃ naraḥ paśyati nāntamasyā hyantaścāsyā bhavitā ceti viddhi
|
paraṃ nayantīha viloḍyamānaṃ yathā plavaṃ vāyurivārṇavastham ||30||

31divākaro guṇamupalabhya nirguṇo yathā bhavedapagataraśmimaṇḍalaḥ
|
tathā hyasau muniriha nirviśeṣavān sa nirguṇaṃ praviśati brahma cāvyayam ||31||

32anāgataṃ sukṛtavatāṃ parāṃ gatiṃ svayambhuvaṃ prabhavanidhānamavyayam
|
sanātanaṃ yadamṛtamavyayaṃ dhruvaṃ nicāyya tatparamamṛtatvamaśnute ||32||
