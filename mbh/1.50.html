Mahābhārata 1.50 (Ādiparvan)

1mantriṇa ūcuḥ|
tataḥ sa rājā rājendraḥ skandhe tasya bhujaṅgamam
|
muneḥ kṣutkṣāma āsajya svapuraṃ prayayau punaḥ||1||

2ṛṣestasya tu putro'bhūdgati jāto mahāyaśāḥ
|
śṛṅgīnāma
|
mahātejāstigmavīryo'tikopanaḥ||2||

3brahmāṇaṃ samupāgamya muniḥ pūjāṃ cakāra ha
|
so'nujñātastatastatra śṛṅgī śuśrāva taṃ tadā||3||

4sakhyuḥ sakāśātpitaraṃ pitrā te dharṣitaṃ purā
|
mṛtaṃ sarpaṃ samāsaktaṃ sthāṇubhūtasya tasya tam||4||

5vahantaṃ rājaśārdūlaḥ skandhenānapakāriṇam
|
tapasvinamatīvātha taṃ munipravaraṃ nṛpa||5||

6jitendriyaṃ viśuddhaṃ ca sthitaṃ karmaṇyathādbhutam
|
tapasā dyotitātmānaṃ sveṣvaṅgeṣu yataṃ tadā||6||

7śubhācāraṃ śubhakathaṃ susthitaṃ tamalolupam
|
akṣudramanasūyaṃ ca vṛddhaṃ maunavrate sthitam
|
śaraṇyaṃ sarvabhūtānāṃ pitrā vinikṛtaṃ tava||7||

8śaśāpātha mahātejāḥ pitaraṃ te ruṣānvitaḥ
|
ṛṣeḥ putro mahātejā bālo'pi sthaviradyutiḥ||8||

9sa kṣipramudakaṃ spṛṣṭvā roṣādidamuvāca ha
|
pitaraṃ te'bhisandhāya tejasā prajvalanniva||9||

10anāgasi gurau yo me mṛtaṃ sarpamavāsṛjat
|
taṃ nāgastakṣakaḥ kruddhastejasā pradahiṣyati||10||

11āśīviṣastigmatejā madvākyabalacoditaḥ
|
saptarātrāditaḥ pāpaṃ paśya me tapaso balam||11||

12ityuktvā prayayau tatra pitā yatrā'raya so'bhavat
|
dṛṣṭvā ca pitaraṃ tasmai taṃ śāpaṃ pratyavedayat||12||

13sa cāpi muniśārdūlaḥ prerayāmāsa te pituḥ
|
śiṣyaṃ gauramukhaṃ nāma śīlavantaṃ guṇānvitam||13||

14ācakhyau sa ca viśrānto rājñaḥ sarvamaśeṣataḥ
|
śapto'si mama putreṇa yatto bhava mahīpate||14||

15takṣakastvāṃ mahārāja tejasā'sau dahiṣyati
|
śrutvā ca tadvaco ghoraṃ pitā te janamejaya||15||

16yatto'bhavatparitrastastakṣakātpannagottamāt
|
tatastasmiṃstu divase saptame samupasthite||16||

17rājñaḥ samīpaṃ brahmarṣiḥ kāśyapo gantumaicchata
|
taṃ dadarśātha nāgendrastakṣakaḥ kāśyapaṃ tadā||17||

18tamabravītpannagendraḥ kāśyapaṃ tvaritaṃ dvijam
|
kva bhavāṃstvarito yāti kiṃ ca kāryaṃ cikīrṣati||18||

19kāśyapa uvāca|
yatra rājā kuruśreṣṭhaḥ parikṣinnāma vai dvija
|
takṣakeṇa bhujaṃgena dhakṣyate kila so'dya vai||19||

20gacchāmyahaṃ taṃ tvaritaḥ sadyaḥ kartumapajvaram
|
mayā'bhipannaṃ taṃ cāpi na sarpo dharṣayiṣyati||20||

21takṣaka uvāca|
kimarthaṃ taṃ mayā daṣṭaṃ saṃjīvayitumicchasi
|
ahaṃ sa takṣako brahmanpaśya me vīryamadbhutam||21||

22na śaktastvaṃ mayā daṣṭaṃ taṃ saṃjīvayituṃ nṛpam
|
ityuktvā takṣakastatra so'daśadvai vanaspatim||22||

23sa daṣṭamātro nāgena bhasmībhūto'bhavannagaḥ
|
kāśyapaśca tato rājannajīvayata taṃ nagam||23||

24tatastaṃ lobhayāmāsa kāmaṃ brūhīti takṣakaḥ
|
sa evamuktastaṃ prāha kāśyapastakṣakaṃ punaḥ||24||

25dhanalipsurahaṃ tatra yāmītyuktaśca tena saḥ
|
tamuvāca mahātmānaṃ takṣakaḥ ślakṣṇayā girā||25||

26yāvaddhanaṃ prārthayase rājñastasmāttato'dhikam
|
gṛhāṇa matta eva tvaṃ sannivartasva cānagha||26||

27sa evamukto nāgena kāśyapo dvipadāṃ varaḥ
|
labdhvā vittaṃ nivavṛte takṣakādyāvadīpsitam||27||

28tasminpratigate vipre chadmanopetya takṣakaḥ
|
taṃ nṛpaṃ nṛpatiśreṣṭhaṃ pitaraṃ dhārmikaṃ tava||28||

29prāsādasthaṃ yattamapi dagdhavānviṣavahninā
|
tatastvaṃ puruṣavyāghra vijayāyābhiṣecitaḥ||29||

30etaddṛṣṭaṃ śrutaṃ cāpi yathāvannṛpasattama
|
asmābhirnikhilaṃ sarvaṃ kathitaṃ te'tidāruṇam||30||

31śrutvā caitaṃ naraśreṣṭha pārthivasya parābhavam
|
asya carṣerudaṅkasya vidhatsva yadanantaram||31||

32sautiruvāca|
etasminneva kāle tu sa rājā janamejayaḥ
|
uvāca mantriṇaḥ sarvānidaṃ vākyamarindamaḥ||32||

33janamejaya uvāca|
atha tatkathitaṃ kena yadvṛttaṃ tadvanaspatau
|
āścaryabhūtaṃ lokasya bhasmarāśīkṛtaṃ tadā||33||

34yadvṛkṣaṃ jīvayāmāsa kāśyapastakṣakeṇa vai
|
nūnaṃ mantrairhataviṣo na praṇaśyeta kāśyapāt||34||

35cintayāmāsa pāpātmā manasā pannagādhamaḥ
|
daṣṭaṃ yadi mayā vipraḥ pārthivaṃ jīvayiṣyati||35||

36takṣakaḥ saṃhataviṣo loke yāsyati hāsyatām
|
vicintyaivaṃ kṛtā tena dhruvaṃ tuṣṭirdvijasya vai||36||

37bhaviṣyati hyupāyena yasya dāsyāmi yātanām
|
ekaṃ tu śrotumicchāmi tadvṛttaṃ nirjane vane||37||

38saṃvādaṃ pannagendrasya kāśyapasya ca kastadā
|
śrutavāndṛṣṭavāṃścāpi bhavatsu kathamāgatam
|
śrutvā tasya vidhāsye'haṃ pannagāntakarīṃ matim||38||

39mantriṇa ūcuḥ|
śṛṇu rājanyathāsmākaṃ yena tatkathitaṃ purā
|
samāgataṃ dvijendrasya pannagendrasya cādhvani||39||

40tasminvṛkṣe naraḥ kaścidindhanārthāya pārthiva
|
vicinvanpūrvamārūḍhaḥ śuṣkaśākhāvanaspatau||40||

41na budhyetāmubhau tau ca nagasthaṃ pannagadvijau
|
saha tenaiva vṛkṣeṇa bhasmībhūto'bhavannṛpa||41||

42dvijaprabhāvādrājendra vyajīvatsa vanaspatiḥ
|
tenāgamya dvijaśreṣṭhaṃ puṃsā'smāsu niveditam||42||

43yathā vṛttaṃ tu tatsarvaṃ takṣakasya dvijasya ca
|
etatte kathitaṃ rājanyathādṛṣṭaṃ śrutaṃ ca yat
|
śrutvā ca nṛpaśārdūla vidhatsva yadanantaram||43||

44sautiruvāca|
mantriṇāṃ tu vacaḥ śrutvā sa rājā janamejayaḥ
|
paryatapyata duḥkhārtaḥ pratyapiṃṣatkaraṃ kare||44||

45niḥśvāsamuṣṇamasakṛddīrghaṃ rājīvalocanaḥ
|
mumocāśrūṇi ca tadā netrābhyāṃ prarudannṛpaḥ||45||

46uvāca ca mahīpālo duḥkhaśokasamanvitaḥ
|
durdharaṃ bāṣpamutsṛjya spṛṣṭvā cāpo yathāvidhi||46||

47muhūrtamiva ca dhyātvā niścitya manasā nṛpaḥ
|
amarṣī mantriṇaḥ sarvānidaṃ vacanamabravīt||47||

48janamejaya uvāca|
śrutvaitadbhavatāṃ vākyaṃ piturme svargatiṃ prati
|
niściteyaṃ mama matiryā ca tāṃ me nibodhata
|
anantaraṃ ca manye'haṃ takṣakāya durātmane||48||

49pratikartavyamityevaṃ yena me hiṃsitaḥ pitā
|
śṛṅgiṇaṃ hetumātraṃ yaḥ kṛtvā dagdhvā ca pārthivam||49||

50iyaṃ durātmatā tasya kāśyapaṃ yo nyavartayat
|
yadā gacchetsa vai vipro nanu jīvetpitā mama||50||

51parihīyeta kiṃ tasya yadi jīvetsa pārthivaḥ
|
kāśyapasya prasādena mantriṇāṃ vinayena ca||51||

52sa tu vāritavānmohātkāśyapaṃ dvijasattamam
|
saṃjijīvayiṣuṃ prāptaṃ rājānamaparājitam||52||

53mahānatikramo hyeṣa takṣakasya durātmanaḥ
|
dvijasya yo'dadaddravyaṃ mā nṛpaṃ jīvayediti||53||

54uttaṅkasyāpriyaṃ kartumātmanaśca mahatpriyam
|
bhavatāṃ caiva sarveṣāṃ gacchāmyapacitiṃ pituḥ||54||
