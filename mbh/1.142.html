Mahābhārata 1.142 (Ādiparvan)

1vaiśaṃpāyana uvāca|
evaṃ śrutvā tu putrasya prajñācakṣurnarādhipaḥ
|
kaṇikasya ca vākyāni tāni śrutvā sa sarvaśaḥ||1||

2dhṛtarāṣṭro dvidhācittaḥ śokārtaḥ samapadyata
|
duryodhanaśca karṇaśca śakuniḥ saubalastathā||2||

3duḥśāsanacaturthāste mantrayāmāsurekataḥ
|
tato duryodhano rājā dhṛtarāṣṭramabhāṣata||3||

4pāṇḍavebhyo bhayaṃ naḥ syāttānvivāsayatāṃ bhavān
|
nipuṇenābhyupāyena nagaraṃ vāraṇāvatam||4||

5dhṛtarāṣṭrastu putreṇa śrutvā vacanamīritam
|
muhūrtamiva saṃcintya duryodhanamathābravīt||5||

6dhṛtarāṣṭra uvāca|
dharmanityaḥ sadā pāṇḍustathā dharmaparāyaṇaḥ
|
sarveṣu jñātiṣu tathā mayi tvāsīdviśeṣataḥ||6||

7nāsau kiṃcidvijānāti bhojanādi cikīrṣitam
|
nivedayati nityaṃ hi mama rājyaṃ dhṛtavrataḥ||7||

8tasya putro yathā pāṇḍustathā dharmaparāyaṇaḥ
|
guṇavāঁllokavikhyātaḥ pauravāṇāṃ susaṃmataḥ||8||

9sa kathaṃ śakyate'smābhirapākartuṃ balāditaḥ
|
pitṛpaitāmāhādrājyātsa sahāyo viśeṣataḥ||9||

10bhṛtā hi pāṇḍunā'mātyā balaṃ ca satataṃ bhṛtam
|
bhṛtāḥ putrāśca pautrāśca teṣāmapi viśeṣataḥ||10||

11te purā satkṛtāstāta pāṇḍunā nāgarā janāḥ
|
kathaṃ yudhiṣṭhirasyārthe na no hanyuḥ sabāndhavān||11||

12duryodhana uvāca|
evametanmayā tāta bhāvitaṃ doṣamātmani
|
dṛṣṭvā prakṛtayaḥ sarvā arthamānena pūjitāḥ||12||

13dhruvamasmatsahāyāste bhaviṣyanti pradhānataḥ
|
arthavargaḥ sahāmātyo matsaṃstho'dya mahīpate||13||

14sa bhavānpāṇḍavānāśu vivāsayitumarhati
|
mṛdunaivābhyupāyena nagaraṃ vāraṇāvatam||14||

15yadā pratiṣṭhitaṃ rājyaṃ mayi rājanbhaviṣyati
|
tadā kuntī sahāpatyā punareṣyati bhārata||15||

16dhṛtarāṣṭra uvāca|
duryodhana mamāpyetaddhṛdi saṃparivartate
|
abhiprāyasya pāpatvānnaivaṃ tu vivṛṇomyaham||16||

17na ca bhīṣmo na ca droṇo na ca kṣattā na gautamaḥ
|
vivāsyamānānkaunteyānanumaṃsyanti karhicit||17||

18samā hi kauraveyāṇāṃ vayaṃ te caiva putraka
|
naite viṣamamiccheyurdharmayuktā manasvinaḥ||18||

19te vayaṃ kauraveyāṇāmeteṣāṃ ca mahātmanām
|
kathaṃ na vadhyatāṃ tāta gacchāma jagatastathā||19||

20duryodhana uvāca|
madhyasthaḥ satataṃ bhīṣmo droṇaputro mayi sthitaḥ
|
yataḥ putrastato droṇo bhavitā nātra saṃśayaḥ||20||

21kṛpaḥ śāradvataścaiva yata etau tato bhavet
|
droṇaṃ ca bhāgineyaṃ ca na sa tyakṣyati karhicit||21||

22kṣattā'rthabaddhastvasmākaṃ pracchannaṃ saṃyataḥ paraiḥ
|
na caikaḥ sa samartho'smānpāṇḍavārthe'dhibādhitum||22||

23suvisrabdhaḥ pāṇḍuputrānsaha mātrā pravāsaya
|
vāraṇāvatamadyaiva yathā yānti yathā kuru||23||

24vinidrakaraṇaṃ ghoraṃ hṛdi śalyamivārpitam
|
śokapāvakamudbhūtaṃ karmaṇaitena nāśaya||24||
