Mahābhārata 13.158 (Anuśāsanaparvan)

1yudhiṣṭhira uvāca|
brāhmaṇānarcase rājan satataṃ saṃśitavratān
|
kaṃ tu karmodayaṃ dṛṣṭvā tānarcasi janādhipa ||1||

2kāṃ vā brāhmaṇapūjāyāṃ vyuṣṭiṃ dṛṣṭvā mahāvrata
|
tānarcasi mahābāho sarvametadvasva me ||2||

3bhīṣma uvāca|
eṣa te keśavaḥ sarvamākhyāsyati mahāmatiḥ
|
vyuṣṭiṃ brāhmaṇapūjāyāṃ dṛṣṭavyuṣṭirmahāvrata ||3||

4balaṃ śrotre vāṅmanaścakṣuṣī ca jñānaṃ tathā saviśuddhaṃ mamādya
|
dehanyāso nāticirānmato me na cāti tūrṇaṃ savitādya yāti ||4||

5uktā dharmā ye purāṇe mahānto rājan viprāṇāṃ kṣatriyāṇāṃ viśāṃ ca
|
tathā śūdrāṇāṃ dharmamupāsate ca śeṣaṃ kṛṣṇādupaśikṣasva pārtha ||5||

6ahaṃ hyenaṃ vedmi tattvena kṛṣṇaṃ yo'yaṃ hi yaccāsya balaṃ purāṇam
|
ameyātmā keśavaḥ kauravendra so'yaṃ dharmaṃ vakṣyati saṃśayeṣu ||6||

7kṛṣṇaḥ pṛthvīmasṛjat khaṃ divaṃ ca kṛṣṇasya dehānmedinī sambabhūva
|
varāho'yaṃ bhīmabalaḥ purāṇaḥ sa parvatānvyasṛjadvai diśaśca ||7||

8asya cādho'ntarikṣaṃ divaṃ ca diśaścatasro vidiśaścatasraḥ
|
sṛṣṭistathaiveyamanuprasūtāsa nirmame viśvamidaṃ purāṇam ||8||

9asya nābhyāṃ puṣkaraṃ saṃprasūtaṃ yatrotpannaḥ svayamevāmitaujāḥ
|
yenācchinnaṃ tattamaḥ pārtha ghoraṃ yattattiṣṭhatyarṇavaṃ tarjayānam ||9||

10kṛte yuge dharma āsītsamagrastretākāle jñānamanuprapannaḥ
|
balaṃ tvāsīddvāpare pārthaṃ kṛṣṇaḥ kalau tvadharmaḥ kṣitimevājagāma ||10||

11sa eva pūrvaṃ nijaghāna daityān sa pūrvadevaśca babhūva samrāṭ
|
sa bhūtānāṃ bhāvano bhūtabhavyaḥ sa viśvasyāsya jagataścābhigoptā ||11||

12yadā dharmo glāti vaṃśe surāṇāṃ tadā kṛṣṇo jāyate mānuṣeṣu
|
dharme sthitvā sa tu vai bhāvitātmā parāṃśca lokānaparāṃśca pāti ||12||

13tyājyaṃ tyaktvā cāsurāṇāṃ vadhāya kāryākārye kāraṇaṃ caiva pārtha
|
kṛtaṃ kariṣyat kriyate ca devo rāhuṃ somaṃ viddhi ca śakramenam ||13||

14sa viśvakarmā sa hi viśvarūpaḥ sa viśvabhugviśvasṛgviśvajicca
|
sa śūlabhṛcchoṇitabhṛtkarālastaṃ karmabhirviditaṃ vai stuvanti ||14||

15taṃ gandharvāṇāmapsarasāṃ ca nityamupatiṣṭhante vibudhānāṃ śatāni
|
taṃ rākṣasāśca pari saṃvadanti rāyaspoṣaḥ sa vijigīṣurekāḥ ||15||

16tamadhvare śaṃsitāraḥ stuvanti rathantare sāmagāśca stuvanti
|
taṃ brāhmaṇā brahmamantraiḥ stuvanti tasmai haviradhvaryavaḥ kalpayanti ||16||

17sa paurāṇīṃ brahmaguhāṃ praviṣṭo mahīsatraṃ bhāratāgre dadarśa
|
sa caiva gāmuddadhārāgryakarmā vikṣobhya daityānuragāndānavāṃśca ||17||

18taṃ ghoṣārthe gīrbhirindrāḥ stuvantisa cāpīśo bhārataikaḥ paśūnām
|
tasya bhakṣānvividhānvedayanti tamevājau vāhanaṃ vedayanti ||18||

19tasyāntarikṣaṃ pṛthivī divaṃ ca sarvaṃ vaśe tiṣṭhati śāśvatasya
|
sa kumbhe retaḥ sasṛje surāṇāṃ yatrotpannamṛṣimāhurvasiṣṭham ||19||

20sa mātariśvā vibhuraśvavājī sa raśmivān savitā cādidevaḥ
|
tenāsurā vijitāḥ sarva eva tadvikrāntairvijitānīha trīṇi ||20||

21sa devānāṃ mānuṣāṇāṃ pitṝṇāṃ tamevāhuryajñavidāṃ vitānam
|
sa eva kālaṃ vibhajannudeti tasyottaraṃ dakṣiṇaṃ cāyane dve ||21||

22tasyaivordhvaṃ tiryagadhaścaranti gabhastayo medinīṃ bhāsayantaḥ
|
taṃ brāhmaṇā vedavido juṣantitasyādityo bhāmupayujya bhāti ||22||

23sa māsi māsyadhvarakṛdvidhatte tamadhvare vedavidaḥ paṭhanti
|
sa evoktaścakramidaṃ trinābhi saptāśvayuktaṃ vahate vai tridhāma||23||

24mahātejāḥ sarvagaḥ sarvasiṃhaḥ kṛṣṇo lokān dhārayate yathaikaḥ
|
haṃsaṃ tamoghnaṃ ca tameva vīra kṛṣṇaṃ sadā pārtha kartāramehi ||24||

25sa ekadā kakṣagato mahātmā tuṣṭo vibhuḥ khāṇḍave dhūmaketuḥ
|
sa rākṣasānuragāṃścāvajitya sarvatragaḥ sarvamagnau juhoti ||25||

26sa eva pārthāya śvetamaśvaṃ prāyacchatsa evāśvānatha sarvāṃścakāra
|
sa bandhurastasya rathastricakrastrivṛcchirāścaturaśvastrinābhiḥ ||26||

27sa vihāyo vyadadhātpañcanābhiḥ sa nirmame gāṃ divamantarikṣam
|
so'raṇyāni vyasṛjatparvatāṃśca hṛṣīkeśo'mitadīptāgnitejāḥ ||27||

28alaṅghayadvai sarito jighāṃsanśakraṃ vajraṃ praharantaṃ nirāsa
|
sa mahendraḥ stūyate vai mahādhvare vipraireko ṛksahastraiḥ purāṇaiḥ ||28||

29durvāsā vai tena nānyena śakyo gṛhe rājan vāsayituṃ mahaujāḥ
|
tamevāhurṛṣimekaṃ purāṇaṃ sa viśvakṛdvidadhātyātmabhāvān ||29||

30vedāṃśca yo vedayate'dhidevo vidhīṃśca yaścāśrayate purāṇān
|
kāme vede laukike yatphalaṃ ca viṣvaksenaḥ sarvametatpratīhi ||30||

31jyotīṃṣi śuklāni hi sarvaloke trayo lokā lokapālāstrayaśca
|
trayo'gnayo vyāhṛtayaśca tisraḥ sarve devā devakīputra eva ||31||

32sa vatsaraḥ sa ṛtuḥ so'rdhamāsaḥ so'horātraḥ sa kalā vai sa kāṣṭhāḥ
|
mātrā muhūrtāśca lavāḥ kṣaṇāśca viṣvaksenaḥ sarvametatpratīhi ||32||

33candrādityau grahanakṣatratārāḥ sarvāṇi darśānyatha paurṇamāsam
|
nakṣatrayogā ṛtavaśca pārtha viṣvaksenātsarvametatprasūtam ||33||

34rudrādityā vasavo'thāśvinau ca sādhyāśca viśve marutāṃ gaṇāśca
|
prajāpatirdevamātā ditiśca sarve kṛṣṇādṛṣayaścaiva sapta ||34||

35vāyurbhūtvā vikṣipate ca viśvamagnirbhūtvā dahate viśvarūpaḥ
|
āpo bhūtvā majjayate ca sarvaṃ brahmā bhūtvā sṛjate viśvasaṅghān ||35||

36vedyaṃ ca yadvedayate ca vedyaṃ vidhiśca yaśca śrayate vidheyam
|
dharme ca vede ca bale ca sarvaṃ carācaraṃ keśavaṃ tvaṃ pratīhi ||36||

37jyotirbhūtaḥ paramo'sau purastātprakāśate yatprabhayā viśvarūpaḥ
|
apaḥ sṛṣṭvā sarvabhūtātmayoniḥ purā'karotsarvamevātha viśvam ||37||

38ṛtūnutpātānvividhānyadbhutāni meghānvidyutsarvamairāvataṃ ca
|
sarvaṃ kṛṣṇāttasthāvaraṃ jaṅgamaṃ ca viśvātmānaṃ viṣṇumenaṃ pratīhi ||38||

39viśvāvāsaṃ nirguṇaṃ vāsudevaṃ saṅkarṣaṇaṃ jīvabhūtaṃ vadanti
|
tataḥ pradyumnamaniruddhaṃ caturthamājñāpayatyātmayonirmahātmā ||39||

40sa pañcadhā pañcajanopapannaṃ sañcodayan viśvamidaṃ sisṛkṣuḥ
|
tataścakārāvanimārutau ca khaṃ jyotirambhaśca tathaiva pārtha ||40||

41sa sthāvaraṃ jaṅgamaṃ caiva metaccaturvidhaṃ lokamimaṃ ca kṛtvā
|
tato bhūmiṃ vyadadhātpañcabījāṃ dyauḥ pṛthivyāṃ dhāsyati bhūri vāri ||41||

42tena viśvaṃ kṛtametaddhi rājansa jīvayatyātmanaivātmayoniḥ
|
tato devānasurān mānavāṃśca lokānṛṣīṃścāpi pitṝn prajāśca
|
samāsena vidhivatprāṇilokānsarvān sadā bhūtapatiḥ sisṛkṣuḥ||42||

43śubhāśubhaṃ sthāvaraṃ jaṅgamaṃ ca viṣvaksenātsarvametatpratīhi
|
yadvartate yacca bhaviṣyatīha sarvaṃ hyetatkeśavaṃ tvaṃ pratīhi ||43||

44mṛtyuścaiva prāṇināmantakāle sākṣātkṛṣṇaḥ śāśvato dharmavāhaḥ
|
bhūtaṃ ca yacceha na vidma kiñcidviṣvaksenātsarvametatpratīhi ||44||

45yatpraśastaṃ ca lokeṣu puṇyaṃ yacca śubhāśubham
|
tatsarvaṃ keśavo'cintyo viparītamataḥ param ||45||

46etādṛśaḥ keśavo'taśca bhūyo nārāyaṇaḥ paramaścāvyayaśca
|
madhyādyantasya jagatastasthuṣaśca bubhūṣatāṃ prabhavaścāvyayaśca ||46||
