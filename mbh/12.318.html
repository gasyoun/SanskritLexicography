Mahābhārata 12.318 (Śāntiparvan)

1yājñavalkya uvāca |
avyaktasthaṃ paraṃ yat tat pṛṣṭaste'haṃ narādhipa
|
paraṃ guhyamimaṃ praśnaṃ śṛṇuṣvāvahito nṛpa||1||

2yathā''rṣeṇeha vidhinā caratā'vanatena ha
|
mayā''dityādavāptāni yajūṃṣi mithilādhipa||2||

3mahatā tapasā devastapiṣṇuḥ sevito mayā
|
prītena cāhaṃ vibhunā sūryeṇoktastadā'nagha||3||

4varaṃ vṛṇīṣva viprarṣe yadiṣṭaṃ te sudurlabham
|
tatte dāsyāmi prītātmā matprasādo hi durlabhaḥ||4||

5tataḥ praṇamya śirasā mayoktastapatāṃ varaḥ
|
yajūṃṣi nopayuktāni kṣipramicchāmi veditum||5||

6tato māṃ bhagavānāha vitariṣyāmi te dvija
|
sarasvatīha vāgbhūtā śarīraṃ te pravekṣyati||6||

7tato māmāha bhagavānāsyaṃ svaṃ vivṛtaṃ kuru
|
vivṛtaṃ ca tato me''syaṃ praviṣṭā ca sarasvatī||7||

8tato vidahyamāno'haṃ praviṣṭoṃ'bhastadānagha
|
avijñānādamarṣācca bhāskarasya mahātmanaḥ||8||

9tato vidahyamānaṃ māmuvāca bhagavān raviḥ
|
muhūrtaṃ sahyatāṃ dāhastata: śītībhaviṣyati||9||

10śītībhūtaṃ ca māṃ dṛṣṭvā bhagavānāha bhāskaraḥ
|
pratiṣṭhāsyati te vedaḥ sakhilaḥ sottaro dvija||10||

11kṛtsnaṃ śatapathaṃ caiva praṇeṣyasi dvijarṣabha
|
tasyānte cāpunarbhāve buddhistava bhaviṣyati||11||

12prāpsyase ca yadiṣṭaṃ tatsāṃkhyayogepsitaṃ padam
|
etāvaduktvā bhagavānastamevābhyavartata||12||

13tato'nuvyāhṛtaṃ śrutvā gate deve vibhāvasau
|
gṛhamāgatya saṃhṛṣṭo'cintayaṃ vai sarasvatīm||13||

14tataḥ pravṛttātiśubhā svaravyañjanabhūṣitā
|
oṅkāramāditaḥ kṛtvā mama devī sarasvatī||14||

15tato'hamarthyaṃ vidhivatsarasvatyai nyavedayam
|
tapatāṃ ca variṣṭhāya niṣaṇṇastatparāyaṇaḥ||15||

16tataḥ śatapathaṃ kṛtsnaṃ sarahasyaṃ sasaṃgraham
|
cakre sapariśeṣaṃ ca harṣeṇa parameṇa ha||16||

17kṛtvā cādhyayanaṃ teṣāṃ śiṣyāṇāṃ śatamuttamam
|
vipriyārthaṃ saśiṣyasya mātulasya mahātmanaḥ||17||

18tataḥ saśiṣyeṇa mayā sūryeṇeva gabhastibhiḥ
|
vyasto yajño mahārāja pitustava mahātmanaḥ||18||

19miṣato devalasyāpi tato'rdhaṃ kṛtavānaham
|
svavedadakṣiṇāyārthe vimarde mātulena ha||19||

20sumantunā'tha pailena tathā jaimininā ca vai
|
pitrā te munibhiścaiva tato'hamanumānitaḥ||20||

21daśa pañca ca prāptāni yajūṃṣyarkānmayānagha
|
tathaiva romaharṣeṇa purāṇamavadhāritam||21||

22bījametatpuraskṛtya devīṃ caiva sarasvatīm
|
sūryasya cānubhāvena pravṛtto'haṃ narādhipa||22||

23kartuṃ śatapathaṃ cedamapūrvaṃ ca kṛtaṃ mayā
|
yathābhilaṣitaṃ mārgaṃ tathā taccopapāditam||23||

24śiṣyāṇāmakhilaṃ kṛtsnamanujñātaṃ sasaṃgraham
|
sarve ca śiṣyāḥ śucayo gatāḥ paramaharṣitāḥ||24||

25śākhāḥ pañcadaśemāstu vidyā bhāskaradeśitāḥ
|
pratiṣṭhāpya yathākāmaṃ vedyaṃ tadanucintayam||25||

26kimatra brahmaṇyamṛtaṃ kiṃ ca vedyamanuttamam
|
cintayaṃstatra cāgatya gandharvo māmapṛcchata||26||

27viśvāvasustato rājanvedāntajñānakovidaḥ
|
caturviśāṃstato'pṛcchatpraśnānvedasya pārthiva||27||

28pañcaviṃśatimaṃ praśnaṃ papracchānvīkṣikīṃ tadā
|
viśvāviśvaṃ tathāśvāśvaṃ mitraṃ varuṇameva ca||28||

29jñānaṃ jñeyaṃ tathā jño'jñaḥ kastapā atapāstathā
|
sūryādaḥ sūrya iti ca vidyāvidye tathaiva ca||29||

30vedyāvedyaṃ tathā rājannacalaṃ calameva ca
|
apūrvamakṣayaṃ kṣayyametatpraśnamanuttamam||30||

31athoktaśca mahārāja rājā gandharvasattamaḥ
|
pṛṣṭavānanupūrveṇa praśnamarthaviduttamam||31||

32muhūrtamuṣyatāṃ tāvadyāvadevaṃ vicintaye
|
bāḍhamityeva kṛtvā ca tūṣṇīṃ gandharva āsthitaḥ||32||

33tato'nucintayamahaṃ bhūyo devī sarasvatīm
|
manasā sa ca me praśno dadhno ghṛtamivoddhṛtam||33||

34tatropaniṣadaṃ caiva pariśeṣaṃ ca pārthiva
|
mathnāmi manasā tāta dṛṣṭvā cānvīkṣikīṃ parām||34||

35caturthī rājaśārdūla vidyaiṣā sāmparāyikī
|
udīritā mayā tubhyaṃ pañcaviṃśādadhiṣṭhitā||35||

36athoktastu mayā rājan rājā viśvāvasustadā
|
śrūyatāṃ yadbhavānasmānpraśnaṃ saṃpṛṣṭavāniha||36||

37viśvāviśveti yadidaṃ gandharvendrānupṛcchasi
|
viśvāvyaktaṃ paraṃ vidyādbhūtabhavyabhayaṃkaram||37||

38triguṇaṃ guṇakartṛtvādviśvānyo niṣkalastathā
|
aśvaścāśvā ca mithunamevamevānudṛśyate||38||

39avyaktaṃ prakṛtiṃ prāhuḥ puruṣeti ca nirguṇam
|
tathaiva mitraṃ puruṣaṃ varuṇaṃ prakṛtiṃ tathā||39||

40jñānaṃ tu prakṛtiṃ prāhurjñeyaṃ niṣkalameva ca
|
ajñaśca jñaśca puruṣastasmānniṣkala ucyate||40||

41kastapā atapāḥ proktaḥ ko'sau puruṣa ucyate
|
tapāstu prakṛtiṃ prāhuratapā niṣkalaḥ smṛtaḥ||41||

42tathaivāvedyamavyaktaṃ vedyaḥ puruṣa ucyate
|
calācalamiti proktaṃ tvayā tadapi me śṛṇu||42||

43calāṃ tu prakṛtiṃ prāhuḥ kāraṇaṃ kṣayasargayoḥ
|
ākṣepasargayoḥ kartā niścalaḥ puruṣaḥ smṛtaḥ||43||

44tathaiva vedyamavyaktamavedyaḥ puruṣastathā
|
ajñāvubhau dhruvau caiva akṣayau cāpyubhāvapi||44||

45ajau nityāvubhau prāhuradhyātmagatiniścayāḥ||45||

46akṣayatvātprajanane ajamatrāhuravyayam
|
akṣayaṃ puruṣaṃ prāhuḥ kṣayo hyasya ca vidyate||46||

47guṇakṣayatvātprakṛtiḥ kartṛtvādakṣayaṃ budhāḥ
|
eṣā te''nvīkṣikī vidyā caturthī sāṃparāyikī||47||

48vidyopetaṃ dhanaṃ kṛtvā karmaṇā nityakarmaṇi
|
ekāntadarśanā vedāḥ sarve viśvāvaso smṛtāḥ||48||

49jāyante ca mriyante ca yasminnete yataścyutāḥ
|
vedārthaṃ ye na jānanti vedyaṃ gandharvasattama||49||

50sāṅgopāṅgānapi yadi yaśca vedānadhīyate
|
vedavedyaṃ na jānīte vedabhāravaho hi saḥ||50||

51yo ghṛtārthī kharīkṣīraṃ mathedgandharvasattama
|
viṣṭhāṃ tatrānupaśyeta na maṇḍaṃ na ca vai ghṛtam||51||

52tathā vedyamavedyaṃ ca vedavidyo na vindati
|
sa kevalaṃ mūḍhamatirjñānabhāravahaḥ smṛtaḥ||52||

53draṣṭavyau nityamevaitau tatpareṇāntarātmanā
|
tathāsya janmanidhane na bhavetāṃ punaḥ punaḥ||53||

54ajasraṃ janmanidhanaṃ cintayitvā trayīmimām
|
parityajya kṣayamiha akṣayaṃ dharmamāsthitaḥ||54||

55yadānupaśyate'tyantamahanyahani kāśyapa
|
tadā sa kevalībhūtaḥ ṣaḍviṃśamanupaśyati||55||

56anyaśca śāśvato'vyaktastathānyaḥ pañcaviṃśakaḥ
|
tasya dvāvanupaśyetāṃ tamekamiti sādhavaḥ||56||

57tenaitaṃ nābhinandanti pañcaviṃśakamacyutam
|
janmamṛtyubhayādyogāḥ sāṃkhyāśca paramaiṣiṇaḥ||57||

58viśvāvasuruvāca |
pañcaviśaṃ yadetatte proktaṃ brāhmaṇasattama
|
tathā tanna tathā ceti tadbhavānvaktumarhati||58||

59jaigīṣavyasyāsitasya devalasya mayā śrutam
|
parāśarasya viprarvārṣagaṇyasya dhīmataḥ||59||

60bhṛgoḥ pañcaśikhasyāsya kapilasya śukasya ca
|
gautamasyārṣṭiṣeṇasya gargasya ca mahātmanaḥ||60||

61nāradasyāsureścaiva pulastyasya ca dhīmataḥ
|
sanatkumārasya tataḥ śukrasya ca mahātmanaḥ||61||

62kaśyapasya pituścaiva pūrvameva mayā śrutam
|
tadanantaraṃ ca rudrasya viśvarūpasya dhīmataḥ||62||

63daivatebhyaḥ pitṛbhyaśca daiteyebhyastatastataḥ
|
prāptametanmayā kṛtsnaṃ vedyaṃ nityaṃ vadantyuta||63||

64tasmāttadvai bhavadbuddhyā śrotumicchāmi brāhmaṇa
|
bhavānprabarhaḥ śāstrāṇāṃ pragalbhaścātibuddhimān||64||

65na tavāviditaṃ kiñcidbhavān śrutinidhiḥ smṛtaḥ
|
kathyate devaloke ca pitṛloke ca brāhmaṇa||65||

66brahmalokagatāścaiva kathayanti maharṣayaḥ
|
patiśca tapatāṃ śaśvadādityastava bhāṣitā||66||

67sāṃkhyajñānaṃ tvayā brahmannavāptaṃ kṛtsnameva ca
|
tathaiva yogaśāstraṃ ca yājñavalkya viśeṣataḥ||67||

68niḥsaṃdigdhaṃ prabuddhastvaṃ budhyamānaścarācaram
|
śrotumicchāmi tajjñānaṃ ghṛtaṃ maṇḍamayaṃ yathā||68||

69yājñavalkya uvāca |
kṛtsnadhāriṇameva tvāṃ manye gandharvasattama
|
jijñāsase ca māṃ rājaṃstannibodha yathāśrutam||69||

70abudhyamānāṃ prakṛtiṃ budhyate pañcaviṃśakaḥ
|
na tu budhyati gandharva prakṛtiḥ pañcaviṃśakam||70||

71anena pratibodhena pradhānaṃ pravadanti tat
|
sāṃkhyayogāśca tattvajñā yathāśrutinidarśanāt||71||

72paśyaṃstathaiva cāpaśyanpaśyatyanya: sadā'nagha
|
ṣaḍviṃśaṃ pañcaviṃśaṃ ca caturviṃśaṃ ca paśyati||72||

73na tu paśyati paśyaṃstu yaścainamanupaśyati
|
pañcaviṃśo'bhimanyeta nānyo'sti parato mama||73||

74na caturviṃśako grāhyo manujairjñānadarśibhiḥ
|
matsyaścodakamanveti pravarteta pravartanāt||74||

75yathaiva budhyate matsyastathaiṣo'pyanubudhyate
|
sa snehātsahavāsācca sābhimānācca nityaśaḥ||75||

76sa nimajjati kālasya yadaikatvaṃ na budhyate
|
unmajjati hi kālasya samatvenābhisaṃvṛtaḥ||76||

77yadā tu manyate'nyo'hamanya eṣa iti dvijaḥ
|
tadā sa kevalībhūtaḥ ṣaḍviṃśamanupaśyati||77||

78anyaśca rājanyavarastathānyaḥ pañcaviṃśakaḥ
|
tatsthānāccānupaśyanti eka eveti sādhavaḥ||78||

79tenaitannābhinandanti pañcaviṃśakamacyutam
|
janmamṛtyubhayādbhītā yogāḥ sāṃkhyāśca kāśyapa
|
ṣaḍviṃśamanupaśyantaḥ śucayastatparāyaṇāḥ||79||

80yadā sa kevalībhūtaḥ ṣaḍviṃśamanupaśyati
|
tadā sa sarvavidvidvānna punarjanma vindati||80||

81evamapratibuddhaśca budhyamānaśca te'nagha
|
buddhaścokto yathātattvaṃ mayā śrutinidarśanāt||81||

82paśyāpaśyaṃ yo na paśyetkṣemyaṃ tattvaṃ ca kāśyapa
|
kevalākevalaṃ cādyaṃ pañcaviśaṃ paraṃ ca yat||82||

83viśvāvasuruvāca |
tathyaṃ śubhaṃ caitaduktaṃ tvayā vibho samyak kṣemyaṃ daivatādyaṃ yathāvat
|
svastyakṣayaṃ bhavataścāstu nityaṃ buddhyā sadā buddhiyuktaṃ manaste||83||

84yājñavalkya uvāca |
evamuktvā saṃprayāto divaṃ sa vibhrājanvai śrīmatā darśanena
|
dṛṣṭaśca tuṣṭyā parayā'bhinandya pradakṣiṇaṃ mama kṛtvā mahātmā||84||

85brahmādīnāṃ khecarāṇāṃ kṣitau ca ye cādhastātsaṃvasante narendra
|
tatraiva taddarśanaṃ darśayanvai samyak kṣemyaṃ ye pathaṃ saṃśritā vai||85||

86sāṃkhyāḥ sarve sāṃkhyadharme ratāśca tadvadyogā yogadharme ratāśca
|
ye cāpyanye mokṣakāmā manuṣyāsteṣāmetaddarśanaṃ jñānadṛṣṭam||86||

87jñānānmokṣo jāyate rājasiṃha nāstyajñānādevamāhurnarendra
|
tasmājjñānaṃ tattvato'nveṣitavyaṃ yenātmānaṃ mokṣayejjanmamṛtyoḥ||87||

88prāpya jñānaṃ brāhmaṇātkṣatriyādvā vaiśyācchūdrādapi nīcādabhīkṣṇam
|
śraddhātavyaṃ śraddadhānena nityaṃ na śraddhinaṃ janmamṛtyū viśetām||88||

89sarve varṇā brāhmaṇā brahmajāśca sarve nityaṃ vyāharante ca brahma
|
tattvaṃ śāstraṃ brahmabuddhyā bravīmi sarvaṃ viśvaṃ brahma caitatsamastam||89||

90brahmāsyato brāhmaṇāḥ samprasūtā bāhubhyāṃ vai kṣatriyāḥ saṃprasūtāḥ
|
nābhyāṃ vaiśyāḥ pādataścāpi śūdrāḥ sarve varṇā nānyathā veditavyāḥ||90||

91ajñānataḥ karmayoniṃ bhajante tāṃ tāṃ rājaṃste tathā yāntyabhāvam
|
tathā varṇā jñānahīnāḥ patante ghorādajñānātprākṛtaṃ yonijālam||91||

92tasmājjñānaṃ sarvato mārgitavyaṃ sarvatrasthaṃ caitaduktaṃ mayā te
|
tatstho brahmā tasthicāṃścāparoyastasmai nityaṃ mokṣamāhurnarendra||92||

93yatte pṛṣṭaṃ tanmayā copadiṣṭaṃ yāthātathyaṃ tadviśoko bhavasva
|
rājangacchasvaitadarthasya pāraṃ samyak proktaṃ svasti te tvastu nityam||93||

94bhīṣma uvāca|
sa evamanuśāstastu yājñavalkyena dhīmatā
|
prītimānabhavadrājā mithilādhipatistadā||94||

95gate munivare tasminkṛte cāpi pradakṣiṇam
|
daivarātirnarapatirāsīnastatra mokṣavit||95||

96gokoṭiṃ sparśayāmāsa hiraṇyaṃ tu tathaiva ca
|
ratnāñjalimathaikaṃ ca brāhmaṇebhyo dadau tadā||96||

97videharājyaṃ ca tadā pratiṣṭhāpya sutasya vai
|
yatidharmamupāsaṃścāpyavasanmithilādhipaḥ||97||

98sāṃkhyajñānamadhīyāno yogaśāstraṃ ca kṛtsnaśaḥ
|
dharmādharmaṃ ca rājendra prākṛtaṃ parigarhayan||98||

99ananta iti kṛtvā sa nityaṃ kevalameva ca
|
dharmādharmau puṇyapāpe satyāsatye tathaiva ca||99||

100janmamṛtyū ca rājendra prākṛtaṃ tadacintayat
|
vyaktāvyaktasya karmedamiti nityaṃ narādhipa||100||

101paśyanti yogāḥ sāṃkhyāśca svaśāstrakṛtalakṣaṇāḥ
|
iṣṭāniṣṭavimuktaṃ hi tasthau brahma parātparam||101||

102nityaṃ tadāhurvidvāṃsaḥ śuci tasmācchucirbhava
|
dīyate yacca labhate dattaṃ yaccānumanyate||102||

103dadāti ca naraśreṣṭha pratigṛhṇāti yacca ha
|
dadātyavyakta ityetatpratigṛhṇāti yacca vai||103||

104ātmā hyevātmano hyekaḥ ko'nyastasmātparo bhavet
|
evaṃ manyasva satatamanyathā mā vicintaya||104||

105yasyāvyaktaṃ na viditaṃ saguṇaṃ nirguṇaṃ punaḥ
|
tena tīrthāni yajñāśca sevitavyā vipaścitā||105||

106na svādhyāyaistapobhirvā yajñairvā kurunandana
|
labhate'vyaktikaṃ sthānaṃ jñātvā'vyaktaṃ mahīyate||106||

107tathaiva mahataḥ sthānamāhaṅkārikameva ca
|
ahaṅkārātparaṃ cāpi sthānāni samavāpnuyāt||107||

108ye tvavyaktātparaṃ nityaṃ jānate śāstratatparāḥ
|
janmamṛtyuvimuktaṃ ca vimuktaṃ sadasacca yat||108||

109etanmayā''ptaṃ janakātpurastāttenāpi cāptaṃ nṛpa yājñavalkyāt
|
jñānaṃ viśiṣṭaṃ na tathā hi yajñā jñānena durgaṃ tarate na yajñaiḥ||109||

110durgaṃ janma nidhanaṃ cāpi rājan na bhautikaṃ jñānavido vadanti
|
yajñaistapobhirniyamairvataiśca divaṃ samāsādya patanti bhūmau||110||

111tasmādupāsasva paraṃ mahacchuci śivaṃ vimokṣaṃ vimalaṃ pavitram
|
kṣetraṃ jñātvā pārthiva jñānayajñamupāsya vai tattvamṛṣirbhaviṣyasi||111||

112yadupaniṣadamupākarottathāsau janakanṛpasya purā hi yājñavalkyaḥ
|
yadupagaṇitaśāśvatāvyayaṃ tacchubhamamṛtatvamaśokamarcchati||112||
