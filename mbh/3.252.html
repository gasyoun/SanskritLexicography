Mahābhārata 3.252 (Āraṇyakaparvan)

1dānavā ūcuḥ| bhoḥ suyodhana rājendra bharatānāṃ kulodvaha
|
śūraiḥ parivṛto nityaṃ tathaiva ca mahātmabhiḥ||1||

2akārṣīḥ sāhasamidaṃ kasmāt prāyopaveśanam
|
ātmatyāgī hyadho yāti vācyatāṃ cāyaśaskarīm||2||

3na hi kāryaviruddheṣu bahupāpeṣu karmasu
|
mūlaghātiṣu sajjante buddhimanto bhavadvidhāḥ||3||

4niyacchaināṃ matiṃ rājan dharmārthasukhanāśinīm
|
yaśaḥpratāpavīryaghnīṃ śatrūṇāṃ harṣavardhanīm||4||

5śrūyatāṃ tu prabho tattvaṃ divyatāṃ cātmano nṛpa
|
nirmāṇaṃ ca śarīrasya tato dhairyamavāpnuhi||5||

6purā tvaṃ tapasā'smābhirlabdho rājan maheśvarāt
|
pūrvakāyaśca pūrvaste nirmito vajrasañcayaiḥ||6||

7astrairabhedyaḥ śastraiścāpyadhaḥkāyaśca te'nagha
|
kṛtaḥ puṣpamayo devyā rūpataḥ strīmanoharaḥ||7||

8evamīśvarasaṃyuktastava deho nṛpottama
|
devyā ca rājaśārdūla divyastvaṃ hi na mānuṣaḥ||8||

9kṣatriyāśca mahāvīryā bhagadattapurogamāḥ
|
divyāstraviduṣaḥ śūrāḥ kṣapayiṣyanti te ripūn||9||

10tadalaṃ te viṣādena bhayaṃ tava na vidyate
|
sahāyārthaṃ ca te vīrāḥ sambhūtā bhuvi dānavāḥ||10||

11bhīṣmadroṇakṛpādīṃśca pravekṣyantyapare'surāḥ
|
yairāviṣṭā ghṛṇāṃ tyaktvā yotsyante tava vairibhiḥ||11||

12naiva putrānna ca bhrātṝnna pitṝnna ca bāndhavān
|
naiva śiṣyānna ca jñātīnna bālān sthavirānna ca||12||

13yudhi samprahariṣyanto mokṣyanti kurusattama
|
niḥsnehā dānavāviṣṭāḥ samākrānte'ntarātmani||13||

14prahariṣyanti vivaśāḥ snehamutsṛjya dūrataḥ
|
hṛṣṭāḥ puruṣaśārdūlāḥ kaluṣīkṛtamānasāḥ
|
avijñānavimūḍhāśca daivācca vidhinirmitāt||14||

15vyābhāṣamāṇāścānyonyaṃ na me jīvan vimokṣyase
|
sarve śastrāstramokṣeṇa pauruṣe samavasthitāḥ||15||

16ślāghamānāḥ kuruśreṣṭha kariṣyanti janakṣayam
|
te'pi pañca mahātmānaḥ pratiyotsyanti pāṇḍavāḥ||16||

17vadhaṃ caiṣāṃ kariṣyanti daivayuktā mahābalāḥ
|
daityarakṣogaṇāścaiva sambhūtāḥ kṣatrayoniṣu||17||

18yotsyanti yudhi vikramya śatrubhistava pārthiva
|
gadābhirmusalaiḥ śūlaiḥ śastrairuccāvacaistathā||18||

19yacca te'ntargataṃ vīra bhayamarjunasambhavam
|
tatrāpi vihito'smābhirvadhopāyo'rjunasya vai||19||

20hatasya narakasyātmā karṇamūrtimupāśritaḥ
|
tadveraṃ saṃsmaran vīra yotsyate keśavārjunau||20||

21sa te vikramaśoṭīro raṇe pārthaṃ vijeṣyati
|
karṇaḥ praharatāṃ śreṣṭhaḥ sarvāṃścārīn mahārathaḥ||21||

22jñātvaitacchadmanā vajrī rakṣārthaṃ savyasācinaḥ
|
kuṇḍale kavacaṃ caiva karṇasyāpahariṣyati||22||

23tasmādasmābhirapyatra daityāḥ śatasahasraśaḥ
|
niyuktā rākṣasāścaiva ye te saṃśaptakā iti||23||

24prakhyātāste'rjunaṃ vīraṃ haniṣyanti ca mā śucaḥ
|
asapatnā tvayā hīyaṃ bhoktavyā vasudhā nṛpa||24||

25mā viṣādaṃ gamastasmānnaitattvayyupapadyate
|
vinaṣṭe tvayi cāsmākaṃ pakṣo hīyeta kaurava||25||

26gaccha vīra na te buddhiranyā kāryā kathañcana
|
tvamasmākaṃ gatirnityaṃ devatānāṃ ca pāṇḍavāḥ||26||

27vaiśampāyana uvāca|
evamuktvā pariṣvajya daityāstaṃ rājakuñjaram
|
samāśvāsya ca durdharṣaṃ putravaddānavarṣabhāḥ||27||

28sthirāṃ kṛtvā buddhimasya priyāṇyuktvā ca bhārata
|
gamyatāmityanujñāya jayamāpnuhi cetyatha||28||

29taurvasṛṣṭaṃ mahābāhuṃ kṛtyā saivānayat punaḥ
|
tameva deśaṃ yatrāsau tadā prāyamupāviśat||29||

30pratinikṣipya taṃ vīraṃ kṛtyā samabhipūjya ca
|
anujñātā ca rājñā sā tathaivāntaradhīyata||30||

31gatāyāmatha tasyāṃ tu rājā duryodhanastadā
|
svapnabhūtamidaṃ sarvamacintayata bhārata||31||

32vijeṣyāmi raṇe pāṇḍūniti cāsyā'bhavanmatiḥ
|
karṇaṃ saṃśaptakāṃścaiva pārthasyāmitraghātinaḥ||32||

33amanyata vadhe yuktān samarthāṃśca suyodhanaḥ
|
evamāśā dṛḍhā tasya dhārtarāṣṭrasya dumateḥ||33||

34vinirjaye pāṇḍavānāmabhavadbharatarṣabha
|
karṇo'pyāviṣṭacittātmā narakasyāntarātmanā||34||

35arjunasya vadhe krūrāṃ karoti sma tadā matim
|
saṃśaptakāśca te vīrā rākṣasāviṣṭacetasaḥ||35||

36rajastamobhyāmākrāntāḥ phālgunasya vadhaiṣiṇaḥ
|
bhīṣmadroṇakṛpādyāśca dānavākrāntacetasaḥ||36||

37na tathā pāṇḍuputrāṇāṃ snehavanto viśāmpate
|
na cācacakṣe kasmaicidetadrājā suyodhanaḥ||37||

38duryodhanaṃ niśānte ca karṇo vaikartano'bravīt
|
smayannivāñjaliṃ kṛtvā pārthivaṃ hetumadvacaḥ||38||

39na mṛto jayate śatrūn jīvan bhadrāṇi paśyati
|
mṛtasya bhadrāṇi kutaḥ kauraveya kuto jayaḥ||39||

40na kālo'dya viṣādasya bhayasya maraṇasya vā
|
pariṣvajyābravīccainaṃ bhujābhyāṃ sa mahābhujaḥ||40||

41uttiṣṭha rājan kiṃ śeṣe kasmācchocasi śatruhan
|
śatrūn pratāpya vīryeṇa sa kathaṃ mṛtyumicchasi||41||

42athavā te bhayaṃ jātaṃ dṛṣṭvā'rjunaparākramam
|
satyaṃ te pratijānāmi vadhiṣyāmi raṇe'rjunam||42||

43gate trayodaśe varṣe satyenāyudhamālabhe
|
ānayiṣyāmyahaṃ pārthān vaśaṃ tava janādhipa||43||

44evamuktastu karṇena daityānāṃ vacanāt tathā
|
praṇipātena cāpyeṣāmudatiṣṭhat suyodhanaḥ||44||

45daityānāṃ tadvacaḥ śrutvā hṛdi kṛtvā sthirāṃ matim
|
tato manujaśārdūlo yojayāmāsa vāhinīm||45||

46rathanāgāśvakalilāṃ padātijanasaṅkulām
|
gaṅgaughapratimā rājan sā prayātā mahācamūḥ||46||

47śvetacchatraiḥ patākābhiścāmaraiśca supāṇḍuraiḥ
|
rathairnāgaiḥ padātaiśca śuśubhe'tīva saṅkulā||47||

48vyapetābhraghane kāle dyaurivāvyaktaśāradī
|
jayāśīrbhirdvijendraiḥ sa stūyamāno'dhirājavat||48||

49gṛhṇannañjalimālāśca dhārtarāṣṭro janādhipaḥ
|
suyodhano yayāvagre śriyā paramayā jvalan||49||

50karṇena sārdhaṃ rājendra saubalena ca devinā
|
duḥśāsanādayaścāsya bhrātaraḥ sarva eva te||50||

51bhūriśravāḥ somadatto mahārājaśca bālhikaḥ
|
rathairnānāvidhākārairhayairgajavaraistathā||51||

52prayāntaṃ nṛpasiṃhaṃ tamanujagmuḥ kurūdvahāḥ
|
kālenālpena rājendra svapuraṃ viviśustadā||52||
