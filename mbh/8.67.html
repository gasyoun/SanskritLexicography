Mahābhārata 8.67 (Karṇaparvan)

1sañjaya uvāca|
taddharmaśīlasya vaco niśamya rājñaḥ kruddhasyātiratho mahātmā
|
uvāca durdharṣamadīnasattvaṃ yudhiṣṭhiraṃ jiṣṇuranantavīryaḥ ||1||

2arjuna uvāca|
saṃśaptakairyudhyamānasya me'dya senāgrayāyo kurusainyeṣu rājan
|
āśīviṣābhān khagamān pramuñcan drauṇiḥ purastātsahasā'bhyatiṣṭhat ||2||

3dṛṣṭvā rathaṃ megharavaṃ mamaiva samastasenāvaraṇe'bhyatiṣṭhan
|
teṣāmahaṃ pañca śatāni hatvā tato drauṇimagamaṃ pārthivāgrya ||3||

4sa māṃ samāsādya narendra yattaḥ samabhyayātsiṃhamiva dvipendraḥ
|
akārṣīcca rathināmujjihīrṣāṃ mahārāja vadhyatāṃ kauravāṇām ||4||

5tato raṇe bhārata duṣprakampya ācāryaputraḥ pravaraḥ kurūṇām
|
māmardayāmāsa śitaiḥ pṛṣatkairjanārdanaṃ caiva viṣāgnikalpaiḥ ||5||

6aṣṭāgavāmaṣṭaśatāni bāṇān mayā prayuddhasya vahanti tasya
|
tāṃstena muktānahamasya bāṇairvyanāśayaṃ vāyurivābhrajālam ||6||

7tato'parānbāṇasaṅghānanekānākarṇapūrṇāyatavipramuktān
|
sasarja śikṣāstrabalaprayatnaistathā yathā prāvṛṣi kālameghaḥ ||7||

8naivādadānaṃ na ca saṃdadhānaṃ jānīmahe katareṇāsyatīti
|
vāmena vā yadi vā dakṣiṇena sa droṇaputraḥ samare paryavartat ||8||

9tasyātataṃ maṇḍalameva sajyaṃ pradṛśyate kārmukaṃ droṇasūnoḥ
|
so'vidhyanmāṃ pañcabhirdroṇaputraḥ śitaiḥ śaraiḥ pañcabhirvāsudevam ||9||

10ahaṃ hi taṃ triṃśatā vajrakalpaiḥ samārdayaṃ nimiṣasyāntareṇa
|
kṣaṇāt śvāvitsamarūpo babhūva samārdito madvisṛṣṭaiḥ pṛṣatkaiḥ ||10||

11sa vikṣaranrudhiraṃ sarvagātre rathānīkaṃ sūtasūnorviveśa
|
mayā'bhibhūtānsainikānāṃ prabarhānasau prapaśyanrudhirapradigdhān ||11||

12tato'bhibhūtaṃ yudhi vīkṣya sainyaṃ vitrastayodhaṃ drutavājināgam
|
pañcāśatā rathamukhyaiḥ sametya karṇastvaranmāmupāyātpramāthī ||12||

13tānsūdayitvā'hamapāsya karṇaṃ draṣṭuṃ bhavantaṃ tvarayā'bhiyātaḥ
|
sarve pañcālā hyudvijante sma karṇaṃ dṛṣṭvā gāvaḥ kesariṇaṃ yathaiva ||13||

14mṛtyorāsyaṃ vyāttamivābhipadya prabhadrakāḥ karṇamāsādya rājan
|
rathāṃstu tānsaptaśatānnimagnāṃstadā karṇaḥ prāhiṇonmṛtyusadma ||14||

15na cāpyabhūtklāntamanāḥ sa rājan yāvannāsmāndṛṣṭavān sūtaputraḥ
|
śrutvā tu tvāṃ tena dṛṣṭaṃ sametamaśvatthāmnā pūrvataraṃ kṣataṃ ca ||15||

16manye kālamapayānasya rājan krūrātkarṇātte'hamacintyakarman
|
mayā karṇasyāstramidaṃ purastādyuddhe dṛṣṭaṃ pāṇḍava citrarūpam ||16||

17na hyanyayoddhā vidyate sṛñjayānāṃ mahārathaṃ yo'dya saheta karṇam
|
śaineyo me sātyakiścakrarakṣau dhṛṣṭadyumnaścāpi tathaiva rājan ||17||

18yudhāmanyuścottamaujāśca śūrau pṛṣṭhato māṃ rakṣatāṃ rājaputrau
|
rathapravīreṇa mahānubhāva dviṣatsainye vartatā dustareṇa ||18||

19sametyāhaṃ sūtaputreṇa saṃkhye vṛtreṇa vajrīva narendramukhya
|
yotsyāmyahaṃ bhārata sūtaputramasminsaṃgrāme yadi vai dṛśyate'dya ||19||

20āyāhi paśyādya yuyutsamānaṃ māṃ sūtaputrasya raṇe jayāya
|
maharṣabhasyeva mukhaṃ prapannāḥ prabhadrakāḥ karṇamabhidravanti ||20||

21ṣaṭsāhasrā bhārata rājaputrāḥ svargāya lokāya raṇe nimagnāḥ
|
karṇaṃ na cedadya nihanmi rājan sabāndhavaṃ yudhyamānaṃ prasahya ||21||

22pratiśrutyākurvato vai gatiryā kaṣṭā yātā tāmahaṃ rājasiṃha
|
āmantraye tvāṃ brūhi jayaṃ raṇe me purā bhīmaṃ dhārtarāṣṭrā grasante ||22||

23sautiṃ haniṣyāmi narendrasiṃha
|
sainyaṃ tathā śatrugaṇāṃśca sarvān ||23||
