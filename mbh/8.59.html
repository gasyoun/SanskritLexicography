Mahābhārata 8.59 (Karṇaparvan)

1sañjaya uvāca|
tataḥ punaḥ samājagmurabhītāḥ kurusṛñjayāḥ
|
yudhiṣṭhiramukhāḥ pārthāḥ sūtaputramukhā vayam ||1||

2tataḥ pravavṛte bhīmaḥ saṃgrāmo lomaharṣaṇaḥ
|
karṇasya pāṇḍavānāṃ ca yamarāṣṭravivardhanaḥ ||2||

3tasminpravṛtte saṃgrāme tumule śoṇitodake
|
saṃśaptakeṣu śūreṣu kiṃcicchiṣṭeṣu bhārata ||3||

4dhṛṣṭadyumno mahārāja sahitaḥ sarvarājabhiḥ
|
karṇamevābhidudrāva pāṇḍavāśca mahārathāḥ ||4||

5āgacchamānāṃstānsaṃkhye prahṛṣṭānvijayaiṣiṇaḥ
|
dadhāraiko raṇe karṇo jalaughāniva parvataḥ ||5||

6samāsādya tu te karṇaṃ vyaśīryanta mahārathāḥ
|
yathācalaṃ samāsādya vāryoghāḥ sarvatodiśam ||6||

7tayorāsīnmahārāja saṃgrāmo lomaharṣaṇaḥ
|
dhṛṣṭadyumnastu rādheyaṃ śareṇānataparvaṇā ||7||

8tāḍayāmāsa samare tiṣṭha tiṣṭheti cābravīt
|
vijayaṃ ca dhanuḥ śreṣṭhaṃ vidhunvāno mahārathaḥ ||8||

9pārṣatasya dhanuśchittvā śarāṃścāśīviṣopamān
|
tāḍayāmāsa saṃkruddhaḥ pārṣataṃ navabhiḥ śaraiḥ ||9||

10te varma hemavikṛtaṃ bhittvā tasya mahātmanaḥ
|
śoṇitāktā vyarājanta śakragopā ivānagha ||10||

11ta‌dapāsya dhanuśchinnaṃ dhṛṣṭadyumno mahārathaḥ
|
athānyaddhanurādāya śarāṃścāśīviṣopamān ||11||

12karṇaṃ vivyādha saptatyā śaraiḥ sannataparvabhiḥ
|
tathaiva rājankarṇo'pi pārṣataṃ śatrutāpanam ||12||

13chādayāmāsa samare śarairāśīviṣopamaiḥ
|
droṇaśatrurmaheṣvāso vivyādha niśitaiḥ śaraiḥ ||13||

14tasya karṇo mahārāja śaraṃ kanakabhūṣaṇam
|
preṣayāmāsa saṃkruddho mṛtyudaṇḍamivāparam ||14||

15tamāpatantaṃ sahasā ghorarūpaṃ viśāṃpate
|
ciccheda śatadhā rājañśaineyaḥ kṛtahastavat ||15||

16dṛṣṭvā vinihataṃ bāṇaṃ śaraiḥ karṇo viśāṃpate
|
sātyakiṃ śaravarṣeṇa samantātparyavārayat ||16||

17vivyādha cainaṃ samare nārācaistatra saptabhiḥ
|
taṃ pratyavidhyacchaineyaḥ śarairhemapariṣkṛtaiḥ ||17||

18tato yuddhaṃ mahārāja cakṣuḥśrotrabhayānakam
|
āsīddhoraṃ ca citraṃ ca prekṣaṇīyaṃ samantataḥ ||18||

19sarveṣāṃ tatra bhūtānāṃ lomaharṣo'bhyajāyata
|
tad‌ dṛṣṭvā samare karma karṇaśaineyayornṛpa ||19||

20etasminnantare drauṇirabhyayātsumahābalam
|
pārṣataṃ śatrudamanaṃ śatruvīryāsunāśanam ||20||

21abhyabhāṣata saṃkruddho drauṇiḥ parapuraṃjayaḥ
|
tiṣṭha tiṣṭhādya brahmaghna na me jīvanvimokṣyase ||21||

22ityuktvā subhṛśaṃ vīraṃ śīghrakṛnniśitaiḥ śaraiḥ
|
pārṣataṃ chāda‌yāmāsa ghorarūpaiḥ sutejanaiḥ ||22||

23yatamānaṃ paraṃ śaktyā yatamāno mahārathaḥ
|
yathā hi samare droṇaḥ pārṣataṃ vīkṣya māriṣa ||23||

24tathā drauṇiṃ raṇe dṛṣṭvā pārṣataḥ paravīrahā
|
nātihṛṣṭamanā bhūtvā manyate mṛtyumātmanaḥ ||24||

25sa jñātvā samaretmānaṃ śastreṇāvadhyameva tu
|
javenābhyāyayau drauṇiṃ kālaḥ kālamiva kṣaye ||25||

26drauṇistu dṛṣṭvā rājendra dhṛṣṭadyumnamavasthitam
|
krodhena niḥśvasanvīraḥ pāṣataṃ samupādravat ||26||

27tāvanyonyaṃ tu dṛṣṭvaiva saṃraṃbhaṃ jagmatuḥ param
|
athābravīnmahārāja droṇaputraḥ pratāpavān ||27||

28dhṛṣṭadyumnaṃ samīpasthaṃ tvaramāṇo viśāṃpate
|
pañcālāpasadādya tvāṃ preṣayiṣyāmi mṛtyave ||28||

29pāpaṃ hi yattvatyā karma ghnatā droṇaṃ purā kṛtam
|
adya tvāṃ tapsyate tadvai yathā na kuśalaṃ tathā ||29||

30arakṣyamāṇaḥ pārthena yadi tiṣṭhasi saṃyuge
|
nāpakrāmasi vā mūḍha satyametadbravīmi te ||30||

31evamuktaḥ pratyuvāca dhṛṣṭadyumnaḥ pratāpavān
|
prativākyaṃ sa evāsirmāmako dāsyate tava ||31||

32yenaiva te piturdattaṃ yatamānasya saṃyuge
|
yadi tāvanmayā droṇo nihato brāhmaṇabruvaḥ ||32||

33tvāmidānīṃ kathaṃ yuddhe na haniṣyāmi vikramāt
|
evamuktvā mahārāja senāpatiramarṣaṇaḥ ||33||

34niśitenātibāṇena drauṇiṃ vivyādha pārṣataḥ
|
tato drauṇiḥ susaṃkruddhaḥ śaraiḥ saṃnataparvabhiḥ ||34||

35ācchādayaddiśo rājan dhṛṣṭadyumnasya saṃyuge
|
naivāntarikṣaṃ na diśo nāpi yodhāḥ samaṃtataḥ ||35||

36dṛśyante vai mahārāja śaraiśchannāḥ sahasraśaḥ
|
tathaiva pārṣato rājan drauṇimāhavaśobhinam ||36||

37śaraiḥ saṃchādayāmāsa sūtaputrasya paśyataḥ
|
rādheyo'pi mahārāja pañcālānsaha pāṇḍavaiḥ ||37||

38draupadeyānyudhāmanyuṃ sātyakiṃ ca mahāratham
|
ekaḥ saṃvārayāmāsa prekṣaṇīyaḥ samantataḥ ||38||

39dhṛṣṭadyumnastu samare drauṇeściccheda kārmukam
|
tadapāsya dhanardrauṇiranyadādāya kārmukam ||39||

40vegavānsamare ghore śarāṃścāśīviṣopamān
|
sa pārṣatasya rājendra dhanuḥ śaktiṃ gadāṃ dhvajam ||40||

41hayānsūtaṃ rathaṃ caiva nimeṣādvyadhamaccharaiḥ
|
sa cchinnadhanvā viratho hatāśvo hatasārathiḥ ||41||

42khaḍgamādatta vipulaṃ śatacandraṃ ca bhānumat
|
drauṇistadapi rājendra bhallaiḥ kṣipraṃ mahārathaḥ ||42||

43ciccheda samare vīraḥ kṣiprahasto dṛḍhāyudhaḥ
|
rathādanavarūḍhasya tada‌dbhutamivābhavat ||43||

44dhṛṣṭadyumnaṃ hi virathaṃ hatāśvaṃ chinnakārmukam
|
śaraiśca bahudhā viddhamastraiśca śakalīkṛtam ||44||

45nāśakadbharataśreṣṭha yatamāno mahārathaḥ
|
tasyāntamiṣubhī rājan yadā drauṇirna jagmivān ||45||

46atha tyaktvā dhanurvīraḥ pārṣataṃ tvarito'nvagāt
|
āsīdāplavato vegastasya rājanmahātmanaḥ ||46||

47garuḍasyeva patato jighṛkṣoḥ pannagottamam
|
etasminneva kāle tu mādhavo'rjunamabravīt ||47||

48paśya pārtha yathā drauṇiḥ pārṣatasya vadhaṃ prati
|
yatnaṃ karoti vipulaṃ hanyāccainaṃ na saṃśayaḥ ||48||

49taṃ mocaya mahābāho pārṣataṃ śatrukarśana
|
drauṇerāsyamanuprāptaṃ mṛtyorāsyagataṃ yathā ||49||

50evamuktvā mahārāja vāsudevaḥ pratāpavān
|
praiṣayatturagāṃstatra yantra drauṇirvyavasthitaḥ ||50||

51te hayāścandrasaṃkāśāḥ keśavena pracoditāḥ
|
āpibanta iva vyoma jagmurdrauṇirathaṃ prati ||51||

52dṛṣṭvā''yātau mahāvīryāvubhau kṛṣṇadhanañjayau
|
dhṛṣṭadyumnavadhe yatnaṃ cakre rājanmahābalaḥ ||52||

53vikṛṣyamāṇaṃ dṛṣṭvaiva dhṛṣṭadyumnaṃ nareśvara
|
śarāṃścikṣepa vai pārtho drauṇiṃ prati mahābalaḥ||53||

54te śarā hemavikṛtā gāṇḍīvapreṣitā bhṛśam
|
drauṇimāsādya viviśurvalmīkamiva pannagāḥ||54||

55sa viddhastaiḥ śarairghorairdroṇaputraḥ pratāpavān
|
utsṛjya samare rājanpāñcālyamamitaujasam ||55||

56rathamāruruhe vīro dhanañjayaśarārditaḥ
|
pragṛhya ca dhanuḥ śreṣṭhaṃ pārthaṃ vivyādha sāyakaiḥ ||56||

57etasminnantare vīraḥ sahadevo janādhipa
|
apovāha rathenājau pārṣataṃ śatrutāpanam ||57||

58arjuno'pi mahārāja drauṇiṃ vivyādha patribhiḥ
|
taṃ droṇaputraḥ saṃkruddho bāhvorurasi cārpayat ||58||

59krodhitastu raṇe pārtho nārācaṃ kālasaṃmitam
|
droṇaputrāya cikṣepa kāladaṇḍamivāparam ||59||

60brāhmaṇasyāṃsadeśe sa nipapāta mahādyutiḥ
|
sa vihvalo mahārāja śaravegena saṃyuge ||60||

61niṣasāda rathopasthe vaiklavyaṃ ca paraṃ yayau
|
tataḥ karṇo mahārāja vyākṣipadvijayaṃ dhanuḥ ||61||

62arjunaṃ samare kruddhaḥ prekṣamāṇo muhurmuhuḥ
|
dvairathaṃ cāpi pārthena kāmayāno mahāraṇe ||62||

63vihvalaṃ tantu vīkṣyātha droṇaputraṃ ca sārathiḥ
|
apovāha rathenājau tvaramāṇo raṇājirāt ||63||

64athotkruṣṭaṃ mahārāja pañcālairjitakāśibhiḥ
|
mokṣitaṃ pārṣataṃ dṛṣṭvā droṇaputraṃ ca pīḍitam ||64||

65vāditrāṇi ca divyāni prāvādyanta sahasraśaḥ
|
siṃhanādāṃśca cakruste dṛṣṭvā saṃkhye tada‌dbhutam ||65||

66evaṃ kṛtvā'bravītpārtho vāsudevaṃ dhanañjayaḥ
|
yāhi saṃśaptakān kṛṣṇa kāryametatparaṃ mama ||66||

67tataḥ prayāto dāśārhaḥ śrutvā pāṇḍavabhāṣitam
|
rathenātipatākena manomārutaraṃhasā ||67||
