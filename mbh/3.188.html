Mahābhārata 3.188 (Āraṇyakaparvan)

1vaiśampāyana uvāca|
tataḥ sa punarevātha mārkaṇḍeyaṃ yaśasvinam
|
papraccha vinayopeto dharmarājo yudhiṣṭhiraḥ||1||

2naike yugasahasrāntāstvayā dṛṣṭā mahāmune
|
na cāpīha samaḥ kaścidāyuṣmān dṛśyate tava||2||

3varjayitvā mahātmānaṃ brahmāṇaṃ parameṣṭhinam
|
na te'sti sadṛśaḥ kaścidāyuṣā brahmavittama||3||

4anantarikṣe loke'smin devadānavavarjite
|
tvameva pralaye vipra brahmāṇamupatiṣṭhase||4||

5pralaye cāpi nirvṛtte prabuddhe ca pitāmahe
|
tvamekaḥ sṛjyamānāni bhūtānīha prapaśyasi||5||

6caturvidhāni viprarṣe yathāvat parameṣṭhinā
|
vāyubhūtā diśaḥ kṛtvā vikṣipyāpastatastataḥ||6||

7tvayā lokaguruḥ sākṣāt sarvalokapitāmahaḥ
|
ārādhito dvijaśreṣṭha tatpareṇa samādhinā||7||

8svapramāṇamatho vipra tvayā kṛtamanekaśaḥ
|
ghoreṇāviśya tapasā vedhasī nirjitāstvayā||8||

9nārāyaṇāṅkaprakhyastvaṃ sāmparāye'tipaṭhyase
|
bhagavānanekaśaḥ kṛtvā tvayā viṣṇośca viśvakṛt||9||

10karṇikoddharaṇaṃ divyaṃ brahmaṇaḥ kāmarūpiṇaḥ
|
ratnālaṅkārayogābhyāṃ dṛgbhyāṃ dṛṣṭastvayā purā||10||

11tasmāt tavāntako mṛtyurjarā vā dehanāśinī
|
na tvāṃ viśati viprarṣe prasādāt parameṣṭhinaḥ||11||

12yadā naiva ravirnāgnirna vāyurna ca candramāḥ
|
naivāntarikṣaṃ naivorvī śeṣaṃ bhavati kiñcana||12||

13tasminnekārṇave loke naṣṭe sthāvarajaṅgame
|
naṣṭe devāsuragaṇe samutsannamahorage||13||

14śayānamamitātmānaṃ padmotpalaniketanam
|
tvamekaḥ sarvabhūteśaṃ brahmāṇamupatiṣṭhasi||14||

15etat pratyakṣataḥ sarvaṃ pūrvaṃ vṛttaṃ dvijottama
|
tasmādicchāmyahaṃ śrotuṃ sarvahetvātmikāṃ kathām||15||

16anubhūtaṃ hi bahuśastvayaikena dvijottama
|
na te'styaviditaṃ kiñcit sarvalokeṣu nityadā||16||

17mārkaṇḍeya uvāca|
hanta te vartayiṣyāmi namaskṛtvā svayambhuve
|
puruṣāya purāṇāya śāśvatāyāvyayāya ca||17||

18avyaktāya susūkṣmāya nirguṇāya guṇātmane
|
sa eṣa puruṣavyāghra pītavāsā janārdanaḥ||18||

19eṣa kartā vikartā ca bhūtātmā bhūtakṛt prabhuḥ
|
acintyaṃ mahadāścaryaṃ pavitramiti cocyate||19||

20anādinidhanaṃ bhūtaṃ viśvamavyayamakṣayam
|
eṣa kartā na kriyate kāraṇaṃ cāpi pauruṣe||20||

21yadyeṣa puruṣo veda vedā api na taṃ viduḥ
|
sarvamāścaryamevaitannirvṛttaṃ rājasattama||21||

22ādito manujavyāghra kṛtsnasya jagataḥ kṣaye
|
catvāryāhuḥ sahasrāṇi varṣāṇāṃ tat kṛtaṃ yugam||22||

23tasya tāvacchatī sandhyā sandhyāṃśaśca tathāvidhaḥ
|
trīṇi varṣasahasrāṇi tretāyugamihocyate||23||

24tasya tāvacchatī sandhyā sandhyāṃśaśca tataḥ param
|
tathā varṣasahastre dve dvāparaṃ parimāṇataḥ||24||

25tasyāpi dviśatī sandhyā sandhyāṃśaśca tathāvidhaḥ
|
sahastramekaṃ varṣāṇāṃ tataḥ kaliyugaṃ smṛtam||25||

26tasya varṣaśataṃ sandhiḥ sandhyāṃśaśca tataḥ param
|
sandhisandhyāṃśayostulyaṃ pramāṇamupadhāraya||26||

27kṣīṇe kaliyuge caiva pravarttati kṛtaṃ yugam
|
eṣā dvādaśasāhastrī yugākhyā parikīrttitā||27||

28etat sahastraparyantamaho brāhmamudāhṛtam
|
viśvaṃ hi brahma bhavane sarvataḥ parivarttate||28||

29lokānāṃ munajavyāghra pralayaṃ taṃ vidurbudhāḥ
|
alpāvaśiṣṭe tu tadā yugānte bharatarṣabha||29||

30sahasrānte narāḥ sarve prāyaśo'nṛtavādinaḥ
|
yajñapratinidhiḥ pārtha dānapratinidhistathā||30||

31vratapratinidhiścaiva tasmin kāle pravarttate
|
brāhmaṇāḥ śūdrakarmāṇastathā śūdrā dhanārjakāḥ||31||

32kṣatradharmeṇa vāpyatra vartayanti gate yuge
|
nivṛttayajñasvādhyāyā daṇḍājinavivarjitāḥ||32||

33brāhmaṇāḥ sarvabhakṣāśca bhaviṣyanti kalau yuge
|
ajapā brāhmaṇāstāta śudrā japaparāyaṇāḥ||33||

34viparīte tadā loke pūrvarūpaṃ kṣayasya tat
|
bahavo mleccharājānaḥ pṛthivyāṃ manujādhipa||34||

35mṛṣānuśāsinaḥ pāpā mṛṣāvādaparāyaṇāḥ
|
andhrāḥ śakāḥ pulindāśca yavanāśca narādhipāḥ||35||

36kāmbojā bāhlikāḥ śūrāstathā''bhīrā narottama
|
na tadā brāhmaṇaḥ kaścit svadharmamupajīvati||36||

37kṣatriyāścāpi vaiśyāśca vikarmasthā narādhipa
|
alpāyuṣaḥ svalpabalāḥ svalpavīryaparākramāḥ||37||

38alpasārālpadehāśca tathā satyālpabhāṣiṇaḥ
|
bahuśūnyā janapadā mṛgavyālāvṛtā diśaḥ||38||

39yugānte samanuprāpte vṛthā ca brahmavādinaḥ
|
bhovādinastathā śūdrā brāhmaṇāścāryavādinaḥ||39||

40yugāntamanujavyāghra bhavanti bahujantavaḥ
|
na tathā ghrāṇayuktāśca sarvagandhā viśāmpate||40||

41rasāśca manujavyāghra na tathā svāduyoginaḥ
|
bahuprajā hrasvadehāḥ śīlācāravivarjitāḥ
|
mukhebhagāḥ striyo rājan bhaviṣyanti yugakṣaye||41||

42aṭṭaśūlā janapadāḥ śivaśūlāścatuṣpathāḥ
|
keśaśūlāḥ striyo rājan bhaviṣyanti yugakṣaye||42||

43alpakṣīrāstathā gāvo bhaviṣyanti janādhipa
|
alpapuṣpaphalāścāpi pādapā bahuvāyasāḥ||43||

44brahmavadhyānuliptānāṃ tathā mithyā'bhiśaṃsinām
|
nṛpāṇāṃ pṛthivīpāla pratigṛhṇanti vai dvijāḥ||44||

45lobhamohaparītāśca mithyādharmadhvajā''vṛtāḥ
|
bhikṣārthaṃ pṛthivīpāla cañcūryante dvijairdiśaḥ||45||

46karabhārabhayādbhītā gṛhasthāḥ parimoṣakāḥ
|
municchadmākṛticchannā vāṇijyamupajīvanaḥ||46||

47mithyā ca nakharomāṇi dhārayanti tadā dvijāḥ
|
arthalobhānnaravyāghra tathā ca brahmacāriṇaḥ||47||

48āśrameṣu vṛthācārāḥ pānapā gurutalpagāḥ
|
iha laukikamīhante māṃsaśoṇitavardhanam||48||

49bahupāṣaṇḍasaṅkīrṇāḥ parānnaguṇavādinaḥ
|
āśramā manujavyāghra bhaviṣyanti yugakṣaye||49||

50yathartuvarṣī bhagavānna tathā pākaśāsanaḥ
|
na cāpi sarvabījāni samyagrohanti bhārata||50||

51hiṃsābhirāmaśca janastathā sampadyate'śuciḥ
|
adharmaphalamatyarthaṃ tadā bhavati cānagha||51||

52tadāḥ ca pṛthivīpāla yo bhaveddharmasaṃyutaḥ
|
alpāyuḥ sa hi mantavyo na hi dharmo'sti kaścana||52||

53bhūyiṣṭhaṃ kūṭamānaiśca paṇyaṃ vikrīḍate janaḥ
|
vaṇijaśca naravyāghra bahumāyā bhavantyuta||53||

54dharmiṣṭhāḥ parihīyante pāpīyān vardhate janaḥ
|
dharmasya balahāniḥ syādadharmaśca balī tathā||54||

55alpāyuṣo daridrāśca dharmiṣṭhā mānavāstathā
|
dīrghāyuṣaḥ samṛddhāśca vidharmāṇo yugakṣaye||55||

56nagarāṇāṃ vihāreṣu vidharmāṇo yugakṣaye
|
adharmiṣṭhairupāyaiśca prajāvyavaharantyuta||56||

57sañcayena tathā'lpena bhavantyāḍhyamadānvitāḥ
|
dhanaṃ viśvāsato nyastaṃ mitho bhūyiṣṭhaśo narāḥ||57||

58hartuṃ vyavasitā rājan pāpācārasamanvitāḥ
|
naitadastīti manujā vartante nirapatrapāḥ||58||

59puruṣādāni sattvāni pakṣiṇo'tha mṛgāstathā
|
nagarāṇāṃ vihāreṣu caityeṣvapi ca śerate||59||

60saptavarṣāṣṭavarṣāśca striyo garbhadharā nṛpa
|
daśadvādaśavarṣāṇāṃ puṃsāṃ putraḥ prajāyate||60||

61bhavanti ṣoḍaśe varṣe narāḥ palitinastathā
|
āyuḥkṣayo manuṣyāṇāṃ kṣiprameva prapadyate||61||

62kṣīṇāyuṣo mahārāja taruṇā vṛddhaśīlinaḥ
|
taruṇānāṃ ca yacchīlaṃ tadvṛ‌ddheṣu prajāyate||62||

63viparītāstadā nāryo vañcayitvārhataḥ patīn
|
vyuccarantyapi duḥśīlā dāsaiḥ paśubhireva ca||63||

64vīrapatnyastathā nāryaḥ saṃśrayanti narān nṛpa
|
bhartāramapi jīvantamanyān vyabhicarantyuta||64||

65tasmin yugasahastrānte samprāpte cāyuṣaḥ kṣaye
|
anāvṛṣṭirmahārāja jāyate bahuvārṣikī||65||

66tatastānyalpasārāṇi sattvāni kṣudhitāni vai
|
pralayaṃ yānti bhūyiṣṭhaṃ pṛthivyāṃ pṛthivīpate||66||

67tato dinakarairdīptaiḥ saptabhirmanujādhipa
|
pīyate salilaṃ sarvaṃ samudreṣu saritsu ca||67||

68yacca kāṣṭhaṃ tṛṇaṃ cāpi śuṣkaṃ cārdraṃ ca bhārata
|
sarvaṃ tadbhasmasādbhūtaṃ dṛśyate bharatarṣabha||68||

69tataḥ saṃvartako vahnirvāyunā saha bhārata
|
lokamāviśate pūrvamādityairupaśoṣitam||69||

70tataḥ sa pṛthivīṃ bhittvā praviśya ca rasātalam
|
deva-dānava-yakṣāṇāṃ bhayaṃ janayate mahat||70||

71nirdahannāgalokaṃ ca yacca kiñcit kṣitāviha
|
adhastāt pṛthivīpāla sarvaṃ nāśayate kṣaṇāt||71||

72tato yojanaviṃśānāṃ sahasrāṇi śatāni ca
|
nirdahatyaśivo vāyuḥ sa ca saṃvartako'nalaḥ||72||

73sadevāsuragandharvaṃ sayakṣoragarākṣasam
|
tato dahati dīptaḥ sa sarvameva jagadvibhuḥ||73||

74tato gajakulaprakhyāstaḍinmālāvibhūṣitāḥ
|
uttiṣṭhanti mahāmeghā nabhasyadbhutadarśanāḥ||74||

75kecinnīlotpalaśyāmāḥ kecit kumudasannibhāḥ
|
kecitkiñjalkasaṅkāśāḥ kecit pītāḥ payodharāḥ||75||

76keciddhāridrasaṅkāśāḥ kākāṇḍakanibhāstathā
|
kecit kamalapatrābhāḥ keciddhiṅgulasaprabhāḥ||76||

77kecit puravarākārāḥ kecidgajakulopamāḥ
|
kecidañjanasaṅkāśāḥ kecinmakarasannibhāḥ||77||

78vidyunmālāpinaddhāṅgāḥ samuttiṣṭhanti vai ghanāḥ
|
ghorarūpā mahārāja ghorasvananināditāḥ
|
tato jaladharāḥ sarve vyāpnuvanti nabhastalam||78||

79tairiyaṃ pṛthivī sarvā saparvatavanākarā
|
āpūryate mahārāja salilaughapariplutā||79||

80tataste jaladā ghorā rāviṇaḥ puruṣarṣabha
|
sarvataḥ plāvayantyāśu coditāḥ parameṣṭhinā||80||

81varṣamāṇā mahat toyaṃ pūrayanto vasundharām
|
sughoramaśivaṃ raudraṃ nāśayanti ca pāvakam||81||

82tato dvādaśavarṣāṇi payodāsta upaplave
|
dhārābhiḥ pūrayanto vai codyamānā mahātmanā||82||

83tataḥ samudraḥ svāṃ velāmatikrāmati bhārata
|
parvatāśca vidīryante mahī cāpsu nimajjati||83||

84sarvataḥ sahasā bhrāntāste payodā nabhastalam
|
saṃveṣṭayitvā naśyanti vāyuvegaparāhatāḥ||84||

85tatastaṃ mārutaṃ ghoraṃ svayambhūrmanujādhipa
|
ādiḥ padmālayo devaḥ pītvā svapiti bhārata||85||

86tasminnekārṇave ghore naṣṭe sthāvarajaṅgame
|
naṣṭe devāsuragaṇe yakṣarākṣasavarjite||86||

87nirmanuṣye mahīpāla niḥśvāpadamahīruhe
|
anantarikṣe loke'smin bhramāmyeko'hamāhataḥ||87||

88ekārṇave jale ghore vicaran pārthivottama
|
apaśyan sarvabhūtāni vaiklavyamagamaṃ tataḥ||88||

89tataḥ sudīrghaṃ gatvā'haṃ plavamāno narādhipa
|
śrāntaḥ kvacinna śaraṇaṃ labhāmyahamatandritaḥ||89||

90tataḥ kadācit paśyāmi tasmin salilasañcaye
|
nyagrodhaṃ sumahāntaṃ vai viśālaṃ pṛthivīpate||90||

91śākhāyāṃ tasya vṛkṣasya vistīrṇāyāṃ narādhipa
|
paryaṅke pṛthivīpāla divyāstaraṇasaṃstute||91||

92upaviṣṭaṃ mahārāja padmendusadṛśānanam
|
phullapadmaviśālākṣaṃ bālaṃ paśyāmi bhārata||92||

93tato me pṛthivīpāla vismayaḥ sumahānabhūt
|
kathaṃ tvayaṃ śiśuḥ śete loke nāśamupāgate||93||

94tapasā cintayaṃścāpi taṃ śiśuṃ nopalakṣye
|
bhūtaṃ bhavyaṃ bhaviṣyaṃ ca jānannapi narādhipa||94||

95atasīpuṣpavarṇābhaḥ śrīvatsakṛtabhūṣaṇaḥ
|
sākṣāllakṣmyā ivāvāsaḥ sa tadā pratibhāti me||95||

96tato māmabravīdbālaḥ sa padmanibhalocanaḥ
|
śrīvatsadhārī dyutimān vākyaṃ śrutisukhāvaham||96||

97jānāmi tvāṃ pariśrāntaṃ tato viśrāmakāṅkṣiṇam
|
mārkaṇḍeya ihāsva tvaṃ yāvadicchasi bhārgava||97||

98abhyantaraṃ śarīre me praviśya munisattama
|
āsva bho vihito vāsaḥ prasādaste kṛto mayā||98||

99tato bālena tenaivamuktasyāsīt tadā mama
|
nirvedo jīvite dīrdhe manuṣyatve ca bhārata||99||

100tato bālena tenāsyaṃ sahasā vivṛtaṃ kṛtam
|
tasyāhamavaśo vaktre daivayogāt praveśitaḥ||100||

101tataḥ praviṣṭastatkukṣiṃ sahasā manujādhipa
|
sarāṣṭranagarākīrṇāṃ kṛtsnāṃ paśyāmi medinīm||101||

102gaṅgāṃ śatadruṃ sītāṃ ca yamunāmatha kauśikīm
|
carmaṇvatīṃ vetravatīṃ candrabhāgāṃ sarasvatīm||102||

103sindhuṃ caiva vipāśāṃ ca nadīṃ godāvarīmapi
|
vasvokasārāṃ nalinīṃ narmadāṃ caiva bhārata||103||

104nadīṃ tāmrāṃ ca veṇāṃ ca puṇyatoyāṃ śubhāvahām
|
suveṇāṃ kṛṣṇaveṇāṃ ca irāmāṃ ca mahānadīm||104||

105vitastāṃ ca mahārāja kāverīṃ ca mahānadīm
|
śoṇaṃ ca puruṣavyāghra viśalyāṃ kimpunāmapi||105||

106etāścānyāśca nadyo'haṃ pṛthivyāṃ yā narottama
|
parikrāman prapaśyāmi tasya kukṣau mahātmanaḥ||106||

107tataḥ samudraṃ paśyāmi yādogaṇaniṣevitam
|
ratnākaramamitraghna payaso nidhimuttamam||107||

108tatra paśyāmi gaganaṃ candrasūryavirājitam
|
jājvalyamānaṃ tejobhiḥ pāvakārkasamaprabham||108||

109paśyāmi ca mahīṃ rājan kānanairupaśobhitām
|
yajante hi tadā rājan brāhmaṇā bahubhirmakhaiḥ||109||

110kṣatriyāśca pravartante sarvavarṇānurañjanaiḥ
|
vaiśyāḥ kṛṣiṃ yathānyāyaṃ kārayanti narādhipa||110||

111śuśrūṣāyāṃ ca niratā dvijānāṃ vṛṣalāstadā
|
tataḥ paripatan rājaṃstasya kukṣau mahātmanaḥ||111||

112himavantaṃ ca pakṣyāmi hemakūṭaṃ ca parvatam
|
niṣadhaṃ cāpi paśyāmi śvetaṃ ca rajatānvitam||112||

113pakṣyāmi ca mahīpāla parvataṃ gandhamādanam
|
mandaraṃ manujavyāghra nīlaṃ cāpi mahāgirim||113||

114paśyāmi ca mahārāja meruṃ kanakaparvatam
|
mahendraṃ caiva pakṣyāmi vindhyaṃ ca girimuttamam||114||

115malayaṃ cāpi paśyāmi pāriyātraṃ ca parvatam
|
ete cānye ca bahavo yāvantaḥ pṛthivīdharāḥ||115||

116tasyodare mayā dṛṣṭāḥ sarve ratnavibhūṣitāḥ
|
siṃhān vyāghrān varāhāṃśca pakṣyāmi manujādhipa||116||

117pṛthivyāṃ yāni cānyāni sattvāni jagatīpate
|
tāni sarvāṇyahaṃ tatra paśyan paryacaraṃ tadā||117||

118kukṣau tasya naravyāghra praviṣṭaḥ sañcaran diśaḥ
|
śakrādīṃścāpi pakṣyāmi kṛtsnān devagaṇānaham||118||

119sādhyānrudrāṃstathā''dityān guhyakānpitarastathā
|
sarpān‌ nāgān suparṇāṃśca vasūnapyaśvināvapi||119||

120gandharvāpsaraso yakṣānṛṣīṃścaiva mahīpate
|
daityadānavasaddhāṃśca nāgāṃśca manujādhipa||120||

121siṃhikātanayāṃścāpi ye cānye suraśatravaḥ
|
yacca kiñcinmayā loke dṛṣṭaṃ sthāvarajaṅgamam||121||

122sarvaṃ paśyāmyahaṃ rājaṃstasya kukṣau mahātmanaḥ
|
tvaramāṇaḥ phalāhāraḥ kṛtsnaṃ jagadidaṃ vibho||122||

123antaḥśarīre tasyāhaṃ varṣāṇāmadhikaṃ śatam
|
na ca paśyāmi tasyāhaṃ dehasyāntaṃ kadācana ||123||

124satataṃ dhāvamānaśca cintayāno viśāmpate
|
āsādayāmi naivāntaṃ tasya rājan mahātmanaḥ||124||

125tatastameva śaraṇaṃ gato'smi vidhivat tadā
|
vareṇyaṃ varadaṃ devaṃ manasā karmaṇaiva ca||125||

126tato'haṃ sahasā rājan vāyuvegena niḥsṛtaḥ
|
mahātmano mukhāt tasya vivṛtāt puruṣottama||126||

127tatastasyaiva śākhāyāṃ nyagrodhasya viśāmpate
|
āste manujaśārdula kṛtsnamādāya vai jagat ||127||

128tenaiva bālaveṣeṇa śrīvatsakṛtalakṣaṇam
|
