Mahābhārata 7.22 (Droṇaparvan)

1dhṛtarāṣṭra uvāca|
bhāradvājena bhagneṣu pāṇḍaveṣu mahāmṛdhe
|
pañcāleṣu ca sarveṣu kaścidanyo'bhyavartata||1||

2āryāṃ yuddhe matiṃ kṛtvā kṣatriyāṇāṃ yaśaskarīm
|
asevitāṃ kāpuruṣaiḥ sevitāṃ puruṣarṣabhaiḥ||2||

3sa hi vīronnataḥ śūro yo bhagneṣu nivartate
|
aho nāsīt pumān kaścid dṛṣṭvā droṇaṃ vyavasthitam||3||

4jumbhamāṇamiva vyāghraṃ prabhinnamiva kuñjaram
|
tyajantamāhave prāṇān sannaddhaṃ citrayodhinam||4||

5maheṣvāsaṃ naravyāghraṃ dviṣatāṃ bhayavardhanam
|
kṛtajñaṃ satyanirataṃ duryodhanahitaiṣiṇam||5||

6bhāradvājaṃ tathā'nīke dṛṣṭvā śūramavasthitam
|
ke śūrāḥ saṃnyavartanta tanmamācakṣva sañjaya||6||

7sañjaya uvāca|
tān dṛṣṭvā calitān saṃkhye praṇunnān droṇasāyakaiḥ
|
pañcālān pāṇḍavān matsyān sṛñjayāṃścedikekayān||7||

8droṇacāpavimuktena śaraugheṇāśuhāriṇā
|
sindhoriva mahaughena hriyamāṇānyathā plavān||8||

9kauravāḥ siṃhanādena nānāvādyasvanena ca
|
rathadvipanarāṃścaiva sarvataḥ samavārayan||9||

10tān paśyan sainyamadhyastho rājā svajanasaṃvṛtaḥ
|
duryodhano'bravīt karṇaṃ prahṛṣṭaḥ prahasanniva||10||

11duryodhana uvāca|
paśya rādheya pañcālān praṇunnān droṇasāyakaiḥ
|
siṃheneva mṛgān vanyāṃstrāsitān dṛḍhadhanvanā||11||

12naite jātu punaryuddhamīheyuriti me matiḥ
|
yathā tu bhagnā droṇena vāteneva mahādrumāḥ||12||

13ardyamānāḥ śarairete rukmapuṅkhairmahātmanā
|
pathā naikena gacchanti ghūrṇamānāstatastataḥ||13||

14sanniruddhāśca kauravyairdroṇena ca mahātmanā
|
ete'nye maṇḍalībhūtāḥ pāvakeneva kuñjarāḥ||14||

15bhramarairāreva cāviṣṭā droṇasya niśitaiḥ śaraiḥ
|
anyonyaṃ samalīyanta palāyanaparāyaṇāḥ||15||

16eṣa bhīmo mahākrodhī hīnaḥ pāṇḍavasṛñjayaiḥ
|
madīyairāvṛto yodhaiḥ karṇa nandayatīva mām||16||

17vyaktaṃ droṇamayaṃ lokamadya paśyati durmatiḥ
|
nirāśo jīvitānnūnamadya rājyācca pāṇḍavaḥ||17||

18karṇa uvāca|
naiṣa jātu mahābāhurjīvannāhavamutsṛjet
|
na cemān puruṣavyāghra siṃhanādān sahiṣyati||18||

19na cāpi pāṇḍavā yuddhe bhajyeranniti me matiḥ
|
śūrāśca balavantaśca kṛtāstrā yuddhadurmadāḥ||19||

20viṣāgnidyūtasaṃkleśān vanavāsaṃ ca pāṇḍavāḥ
|
smaramāṇā na hāsyanti saṃgrāmamiti me matiḥ||20||

21nivṛtto hi mahābāhuramitaujā vṛkodaraḥ
|
varān varān hi kaunteyo rathodārān haniṣyati||21||

22asinā dhanuṣā śaktyā hayairnāgairnarai rathaiḥ
|
āyasena ca daṇḍena vrātān vrātānhaniṣyati||22||

23tamenamanuvartante sātyakipramukhā rathāḥ
|
pañcālāḥ kekayā matsyāḥ pāṇḍavāśca viśeṣataḥ||23||

24śūrāśca balavantaśca vikrāntāśca mahārathāḥ
|
vinighnantaśca bhīmena saṃrabdhenābhicoditāḥ||24||

25te droṇamabhivartante sarvataḥ kurupuṅgavāḥ
|
vṛkodaraṃ parīpsantaḥ sūryamabhragaṇā iva||25||

26ekāyanagatā hyete pīḍayeyuryatavratam
|
arakṣamāṇaṃ śalabhā yathā dīpaṃ mumūrṣavaḥ||26||

27asaṃśayaṃ kṛtāstrāśca paryāptāścāpi vāraṇe
|
atibhāramahaṃ manye bhāradvāje samāhitam||27||

28śīghramanugamiṣyāmo yatra droṇo vyavasthitaḥ
|
kokā iva mahānāgaṃ mā vai hanyuryatavratam||28||

29sañjaya uvāca|
rādheyasya vacaḥ śrutvā rājā duryodhanastataḥ
|
bhrātṛbhiḥ sahito rājan prāyāddroṇarathaṃ prati||29||

30tatrārāvo mahānāsīdekaṃ droṇaṃ jighāṃsatām
|
pāṇḍavānāṃ nivṛttānāṃ nānāvarṇairhayottamaiḥ||30||
