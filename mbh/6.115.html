Mahābhārata 6.115 (Bhīṣmaparvan)

1dhṛtarāṣṭra uvāca|
kathaṃ śāntanavo bhīṣmo daśame'hani sañjaya
|
ayudhyata mahāvīryaḥ pāṇḍavaiḥ saha sṛñjayaiḥ ||1||

2kuravaśca kathaṃ yuddhe pāṇḍavān pratyavārayan
|
ācakṣva me mahāyuddhaṃ bhīṣmasyāhavaśobhinaḥ ||2||

3sañjaya uvāca|kuravaḥ pāṇḍavaiḥ sārdhaṃ yadayudhyanta bhārata
|
yathā ca tadabhūdyuddhaṃ tat tu vakṣyāmi sāṃpratam ||3||

4gamitāḥ paralokāya paramāstraiḥ kirīṭinā
|
ahanyahani saṃkruddhāstāvakānāṃ mahārathāḥ ||4||

5yathāpratijñaṃ kauravyaḥ sa cāpi samitiñjayaḥ
|
pārthānāmakarodbhīṣmaḥ satataṃ samiti kṣayam ||5||

6kurubhiḥ sahitaṃ bhīṣmaṃ yudhyamānaṃ parantapa
|
arjunaṃ ca sapāñcālyaṃ saṃśayo vijaye'bhavat ||6||

7daśame'hani tasmiṃstu bhīṣmārjunasamāgame
|
avartata mahāraudraḥ satataṃ samiti kṣayaḥ ||7||

8tasminnayutaśo rājan bhūyaśaśca paraṃtapaḥ
|
bhīṣmaḥ śāntanavo yodhāñjaghāna paramāstravit ||8||

9yeṣāmajñātakalpāni nāmagotrāṇi pārthiva
|
te hatāstatra bhīṣmeṇa śūrāḥ sarve'nivartinaḥ ||9||

10daśāhāni tatastaptvā bhīṣmaḥ pāṇḍavavāhinīm
|
niravidyata dharmātmā jīvitena parantapa ||10||

11sa kṣipraṃ vadhamanvicchannātmano'bhimukho raṇe
|
na hanyāṃ mānavaśreṣṭhān saṃgrāme subahūniti ||11||

12cintayitvā mahābāhuḥ pitā devavratastava
|
abhyāśasthaṃ mahārāja pāṇḍavaṃ vākyamabravīt ||12||

13yudhiṣṭhira mahāprājña sarvaśāstraviśārada
|
śṛṇuṣva vacanaṃ tāta dharmyaṃ svargyaṃ ca jalpataḥ ||13||

14nirviṇṇo'smi bhṛśaṃ tāta dehenānena bhārata
|
ghnataśca me gataḥ kālaḥ subahūn prāṇino raṇe ||14||

15tasmāt pārthaṃ purodhāya pañcālān sṛñjayāṃstathā
|
madvadhe kriyatāṃ yatno mama cedicchasi priyam ||15||

16tasya tanmatamājñāya pāṇḍavaḥ satyadarśanaḥ
|
bhīṣmaṃ prati yayau rājā saṃgrāme saha sṛñjayaiḥ||16||

17dhṛṣṭadyumnastato rājan pāṇḍavaśca yudhiṣṭhiraḥ
|
śrutvā bhīṣmasya tāṃ vācaṃ codayāmāsaturbalam ||17||

18abhidravadhvaṃ yuddhyadhvaṃ bhīṣmaṃ jayata saṃyuge
|
rakṣitāḥ satyasandhena jiṣṇunā ripujiṣṇunā ||18||

19ayaṃ cāpi maheṣvāsaḥ pārṣato vāhinīpatiḥ
|
bhīmasenaśca samare pālayiṣyati vo dhruvam ||19||

20mā vo bhīṣmādbhayaṃ kiñcidastvadya yudhi sṛñjayāḥ
|
dhruvaṃ bhīṣmaṃ vijeṣyāmaḥ puraskṛtya śikhaṇḍinam ||20||

21te tathā samayaṃ kṛtvā daśame'hani pāṇḍavāḥ
|
brahmalokaparā bhūtvā sañjagmuḥ krodhamūrcchitāḥ||21||

22śikhaṇḍinaṃ puraskṛtya pāṇḍavaṃ ca dhanañjayam
|
bhīṣmasya pātane yatnaṃ paramaṃ te samāsthitāḥ ||22||

23tatastava sutādiṣṭā nānājanapadeśvarāḥ
|
droṇena saha putreṇa sahasenā mahābalāḥ ||23||

24duḥśāsanaśca balavān saha sarvaiḥ sahodaraiḥ
|
bhīṣmaṃ samaramadhyasthaṃ pālayāñcakrire tadā ||24||

25tatastu tāvakāḥ śūrāḥ puraskṛtya mahāvratam
|
śikhaṇḍipramukhān pārthān yodhayanti sma saṃyuge ||25||

26cedibhistu sapañcālaiḥ sahito vānaradhvajaḥ
|
yayau śāntanavaṃ bhīṣmaṃ puraskṛtya śikhaṇḍinam ||26||

27droṇaputraṃ śinernaptā dhṛṣṭaketustu pauravam
|
abhimanyuḥ sahāmātyaṃ duryodhanamayodhayat ||27||

28virāṭastu sahānīkaḥ sahasenaṃ jayadratham
|
vṛddhakṣatrasya dāyādamāsasāda parantapa ||28||

29madrarājaṃ maheṣvāsaṃ sahasainyaṃ yudhiṣṭhiraḥ
|
bhīmaseno'bhiguptastu nāgānīkamupādravat ||29||

30apradhṛṣyamanāvāryaṃ sarvaśastrabhṛtāṃ varam
|
drauṇiṃ prati yayau yattaḥ pāñcālyaḥ saha sodaraiḥ ||30||

31karṇikāradhvajaṃ caiva siṃhaketurarindamaḥ
|
pratyujjagāma saubhadraṃ rājaputro mahadbalaḥ ||31||

32śikhaṇḍinaṃ ca putrāste pāṇḍavaṃ ca dhanañjayam
|
rājabhiḥ samare pārthamabhipeturjighāṃsavaḥ ||32||

33tasminnatimahābhīme senayorvai parākrame
|
saṃpradhāvatsvanīkeṣu medinī samakampata ||33||

34tānyanīkānyanīkeṣu samasajjanta bhārata
|
tāvakānāṃ pareṣāṃ ca dṛṣṭvā śāntanavaṃ raṇe ||34||

35tatasteṣāṃ prataptānāmanyonyamabhidhāvatām
|
prādurāsīnmahāśabdo dikṣu sarvāsu bhārata ||35||

36śaṅkhadundubhighoṣaśca vāraṇānāṃ ca bṛhitaiḥ
|
siṃhanādaśca sainyānāṃ dāruṇaḥ samapadyata ||36||

37sā ca sarvanarendrāṇāṃ candrārkasadṛśī prabhā
|
vīrāṅgadakirīṭeṣu niṣprabhā samapadyata ||37||

38rajomeghāstu sañjajñuḥ śastravidyudbhirāvṛtāḥ
|
dhanuṣāṃ cāpi nirghoṣo dāruṇaḥ samapadyata ||38||

39bāṇaśaṅkhapraṇādāśca bherīṇāṃ ca mahāsvanāḥ
|
rathaghoṣaśca sañjajñe senayorubhayorapi ||39||

40pāśaśaktyṛṣṭisaṅghaiśca bāṇaughaiśca samākulam
|
niṣprakāśamivākāśaṃ senayoḥ samapadyata ||40||

41anyonyaṃ rathinaḥ peturvājinaśca mahāhave
|
kuñjarān kuñjarā jaghnuḥ pādātāṃśca padātayaḥ ||41||

42tatrāsīta sumahadyuddhaṃ kurūṇāṃ pāṇḍavaiḥ saha
|
bhīṣmahetornaravyāghra śyenayorāmiṣe yathā ||42||

43teṣāṃ samāgamo ghoro babhūva yudhi saṃgataḥ
|
anyonyasya vadhārthāya jigīṣūṇāṃ mahāhave ||43||
