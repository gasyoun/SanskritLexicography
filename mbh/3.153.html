Mahābhārata 3.153 (Āraṇyakaparvan)

1vaiśaṃpāyana uvāca|
sa gatvā nalinīṃ ramyāṃ rākṣasairabhirakṣitām
|
kailāsaśikharābhyāśe dadarśa śubhakānanām||1||

2kuberabhuvanābhyāśe jātāṃ parvatanirjharaiḥ
|
suramyāṃ vipulacchāyāṃ nānādrumalatākulām||2||

3haritāmbujasaṃcchannāṃ divyāṃ kanakapuṣkarām
|
nānāpakṣijanākīrṇāṃ sūpatīrthāmakardamām||3||

4atīva ramyāṃ sujalāṃ jātāṃ parvatasānuṣu
|
vicitrabhūtāṃ lokasya śubhāmadbhutadarśanām||4||

5tatrāmṛtarasaṃ śītaṃ laghu kuntīsutaḥ śubham
|
dadarśa vimalaṃ toyaṃ pibaṃśca bahu pāṇḍavaḥ||5||

6tāṃ tu puṣkariṇīṃ ramyāṃ divyasaugandhikāvṛtām|| jātarūpamayaiḥ padmaiśchannāṃ paramagandhibhiḥ ||6||

7vaidūryavaranālaiśca bahucitrairmanoramaiḥ
|
haṃsakāraṇḍavodbhūtaiḥ sṛjadbhiramalaṃ rajaḥ ||7||

8ākrīḍaṃ rājarājasya kuberasya mahātmanaḥ
|
gandharvairapsarobhiśca devaiśca paramārcitām ||8||

9sevitāmṛṣibhirdivyairyakṣaiḥ kiṃpuruṣaistathā
|
rākṣasaiḥ kinnaraiścāpi guptāṃ vaiśravaṇena ca ||9||

10tāṃ ca dṛṣṭvaiva kaunteyo bhīmaseno mahābalaḥ
|
babhūva paramaprīto divyaṃ saṃprekṣya tatsaraḥ ||10||

11tacca krodhavaśā nāma rākṣasā rājaśāsanāt
|
rakṣanti śatasāhasrāścitrāyudhaparicchadāḥ ||11||

12te tu dṛṣṭvaiva kaunteyamajinaiḥ prativāsitam
|
rukmāṅgadadharaṃ vīraṃ bhīmaṃ bhīmaparākramam ||12||

13sāyudhaṃ baddhanistriṃśamaśaṅkitamariṃdamam
|
puṣkarepsumupāyāntamanyonyamabhicukruśuḥ ||13||

14ayaṃ puruṣaśārdūlaḥ sāyudho'jinasaṃvṛtaḥ
|
yaccikīrṣuriha prāptastatsaṃpraṣṭumihārhatha ||14||

15tataḥ sarve mahābāhuṃ samāsādya vṛkodaram
|
tejoyuktamapṛcchanta kastvamākhyātumarhasi||15||

16muniveṣadharaścaiva sāyudhaścaiva lakṣyase
|
yadarthamabhisaṃprāptastadācakṣva mahāmate ||16||
