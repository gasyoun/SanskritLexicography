Mahābhārata 12.219 (Śāntiparvan)

1bhīṣma uvāca|
janako janadevastu jñāpitaḥ paramarṣiṇā
|
punarevānupapraccha sāmparāye bhavābhuvau ||1||

2janaka uvāca |
bhagavanyadi na pretya saṃjñā bhavati kasyacit
|
evaṃ sati kimajñānaṃ jñānaṃ vā kiṃ kariṣyati ||2||

3sarvamucchedaniṣṭhaṃ syātpaśya caitaddvijottama
|
apramattaḥ pramatto vā kiṃ viśeṣaṃ kariṣyati ||3||

4asaṃsargo hi bhūteṣu saṃsargo vā vināśiṣu
|
kasmai kriyeta kalpyeta niścayaḥ ko'tra tattvataḥ ||4||

5bhīṣma uvāca|
tamasā hi praticchannaṃ vibhrāntamiva cāturam
|
punaḥ praśamayanvākyaiḥ kaviḥ pañcaśikho'bravīt ||5||

6ucchedaniṣṭhā nehāsti bhāvaniṣṭhā na vidyate
|
ayaṃ hyapi samāhāraḥ śarīrendriyacetasām
|
vartate pṛthaganyonyamapyapāśritya karmasu ||6||

7dhātavaḥ pañcabhūteṣu khaṃ vāyuryotiṣo dharā
|
te svabhāvena tiṣṭhanti viyujyante svabhāvataḥ ||7||

8ākāśo vāyurūṣmā ca sneho yaścāpi pārthivaḥ
|
eṣa pañcasamāhāraḥ śarīramapi naikadhā ||8||

9jñānamūṣmā ca vāyuśca trividhaḥ kāryasaṃgrahaḥ
|
indriyāṇīndriyāthāśca svabhāvaścetanā manaḥ
|
prāṇāpānau vikāraśca dhātavaścātra niḥsṛtāḥ ||9||

10śravaṇaṃ, sparśanaṃ, jihvā, dṛṣṭirnāsā tathaiva ca
|
indriyāṇīti pañcaite cittapūrvaṃ gatā guṇāḥ ||10||

11tatra vijñānasaṃyuktā trividhā cetanā dhruvā
|
sukhaduḥkheti yāmāhuraduḥkhāmasukheti ca ||11||

12śabdaḥ, sparśaṃ, ca rūpaṃ, ca raso, gandhaśca mūrtayaḥ
|
ete hyāmaraṇātpañca ṣaḍguṇā jñānasiddhaye ||12||

13teṣu karmavisargaśca sarvatattvārthaniścayaḥ
|
tamāhuḥ paramaṃ śukraṃ buddhirityavyayaṃ mahat ||13||

14imaṃ guṇasamāhāramātmabhāvena paśyataḥ
|
asamyagdarśanairduḥkhamanantaṃ nopaśāmyati ||14||

15anātmeti ca yaddṛṣṭaṃ tenāhaṃ na mametyapi
|
vartate kimadhiṣṭhānātprasaktā duḥkhasaṃtatiḥ ||15||

16atra samyagvadho nāma tyāgaśāstramanuttamam
|
śṛṇu yattava mokṣāya bhāṣyamāṇaṃ bhaviṣyati ||16||

17tyāga eva hi sarveṣāṃ yuktānāmapi karmaṇām
|
nityaṃ mithyāvinītānāṃ kleśo duḥkhavaho mataḥ ||17||

18dravyatyāge tu karmāṇi bhogatyāge vratānyapi
|
sukhatyāge tapoyogaṃ sarvatyāge samāpanā ||18||

19tasya mārgo'yamadvaidhaḥ sarvatyāgasya darśitaḥ
|
viprahāṇāya duḥkhasya durgatistvanyathā bhavet ||19||

20pañcajñānendriyāṇyuktvā manaḥṣaṣṭhāni cetasi
|
balaṣaṣṭhāni vakṣyāmi pañcakarmendriyāṇi tu ||20||

21hastau karmendriyaṃ jñeyamatha pādau gatīndriyam
|
prajanānandayoḥ śepho nisarge pāyurindriyam ||21||

22vākca śabdaviśeṣārthamiti pañcānvitaṃ viduḥ
|
evamekādaśaitāni buddhyā''śu visṛjenmanaḥ ||22||

23karṇau śabdaśca cittaṃ ca trayaḥ śravaṇasaṃgrahe
|
tathā sparśe tathā rūpe tathaiva rasagandhayoḥ ||23||

24evaṃ pañcatrikā hyete guṇāstadupalabdhaye
|
yenāyaṃ trividho bhāvaḥ paryāyātsamupasthitaḥ ||24||

25sāttviko rājasaścāpi tāmasaścāpi te trayaḥ
|
trividhā vedanā yeṣu prasūtāḥ sarvasādhanāḥ ||25||

26praharṣaḥ prītirānandaḥ sukhaṃ saṃśāntacittatā
|
akutaścitkutaścidvā cintitaḥ sāttviko guṇaḥ ||26||

27atuṣṭiḥ paritāpaśca śoko lobhastathā'kṣamā
|
liṅgāni rajasastāni dṛśyante hetvahetutaḥ ||27||

28avivekastathā mohaḥ pramodaḥ svapnatandritā
|
kathañcidapi vartante vividhāstāmasā guṇāḥ ||28||

29atra yatprītisaṃyuktaṃ kāye manasi vā bhavet
|
vartate sāttviko bhāva ityapekṣeta tattathā ||29||

30yattvasantoṣasaṃyuktamaprītikaramātmanaḥ
|
pravṛttaṃ raja ityevaṃ tatastadapi cintayet ||30||

31atha yanmohasaṃyuktaṃ kāye manasi vā bhavet
|
apratarkyamavijñeyaṃ tamastadupadhārayet ||31||

32śrotaṃ vyomāśritaṃ bhūtaṃ śabdaḥ śrotaṃ samāśritaḥ
|
nobhayaṃ śabdavijñāne vijñānasyetarasya vā ||32||

33evaṃ tvakcakṣuṣī jihvā nāsikā ceti pañcamī
|
sparśe rūpe rase gandhe tāni ceto manaśca tat ||33||

34svakarmayugapadbhāvo daśasveteṣu tiṣṭhati
|
cittamekādaśaṃ viddhi buddhirdvādaśamī bhavet ||34||

35teṣāmayugapadbhāva ucchedo nāsti tāmase
|
āsthito yugapadbhāvo vyavahāraḥ sa laukikaḥ ||35||

36indriyāṇyapi sūkṣmāṇi dṛṣṭvā pūrvaśrutāgamāt
|
cintayannānuparyeti tribhirevānvito guṇaiḥ ||36||

37yattamopahataṃ cittamāśu saṃhāramadhruvam
|
karotyuparamaṃ kāye tadāhustāmasaṃ budhāḥ ||37||

38yadyadāgamasaṃyuktaṃ na kṛcchramanupaśyati
|
atha tatrāpyupādatte tamo vyaktamivānṛtam ||38||

39evameṣa prasaṃkhyātaḥ svakarmapratyayo guṇaḥ
|
kathañcidvartate samyakkeṣāṃcidvā nivartate ||39||

40etadāhuḥ samāhāraṃ kṣetramadhyātmacintakāḥ
|
sthito manasi yo bhāvaḥ sa vai kṣetrajña ucyate ||40||

41evaṃ sati ka ucchedaḥ śāśvato vā kathaṃ bhavet
|
svabhāvādvartamāneṣu sarvabhūteṣu hetutaḥ ||41||

42yathārṇavagatā nadyo vyaktīrjahati nāma ca
|
nadāśca tāni yacchanti tādṛśaḥ sattvasaṃkṣayaḥ ||42||

43evaṃ sati kutaḥ saṃjñā pretyabhāve punarbhavet
|
jīve ca pratusaṃyukte gṛhyamāṇe ca sarvataḥ ||43||

44imāṃ ca yo veda vimokṣabuddhimātmānamanvicchati cāpramattaḥ
|
na lipyate karmaphalairaniṣṭaiḥ patraṃ visasyeva jalena siktam ||44||

45dṛḍhairhi pāśairbahubhirvimuktaḥ prajānimittairapi daivataiśca
|
yadā hyasau sukhaduḥkhe jahāti muktastadagryāṃ gatimetyaliṅgaḥ ||45||

46śrutipramāṇāgamamaṅgalaiśca śete jarāmṛtyubhayādabhītaḥ
|
kṣīṇe ca puṇye vigate ca pāpe tato nimitte ca phale vinaṣṭe
|
alepamākāśamaliṅgamevamāsthāya paśyanti mahatyasaktāḥ ||46||

47yathorṇanābhiḥ parivartamānastaṃntukṣaye tiṣṭhati pātyamānaḥ
|
tathā vimuktaḥ prajahāti duḥkhaṃ vidhvaṃsate loṣṭha ivādrimṛcchan ||47||

48yathā ruruḥ śṛṅgamatho purāṇaṃ hitvā tvacaṃ vā'pyurago yathā ca
|
vihāya gacchatyanavekṣamāṇastathā vimukto vijahāti duḥkham ||48||

49drumaṃ yathā vā'pyudake patantamutsṛjya pakṣī nipatatyasaktaḥ
|
tathā hyasau sukhaduḥkhe vihāya muktaḥ parārddhyāṃ gatimetyaliṅgaḥ ||49||

50api ca bhavati maithilena gītaṃ nagaramupāhitamagninā'bhivīkṣya
|
na khalu mama hi dahyate'tra kiṃcitsvayamidamāha kila sma bhūmipālaḥ ||50||

51idamamṛtapadaṃ niśamya rājā svayamiha pañcaśikhena bhāṣyamāṇam
|
nikhilamabhisamīkṣya niścitārthaḥ paramasukhī vijahāra vītaśokaḥ ||51||

52imaṃ hi yaḥ paṭhati mokṣaniścayaṃ mahīpate satatamavekṣate tathā
|
upadravānnānubhavatyaduḥkhitaḥ pramucyate kapilamivaitya maithilaḥ ||52||
