Mahābhārata 3.254 (Āraṇyakaparvan)

1vaiśampāyana uvāca|
tataḥ karṇo maheṣvāso balena mahatā vṛtaḥ
|
drupadasya puraṃ ramyaṃ rurodha bharatarṣabha||1||

2yuddhena mahatā cainaṃ cakre vīraṃ vaśānugam
|
suvarṇaṃ rajataṃ cāpi ratnāni vividhāni ca||2||

3karaṃ ca dāpayāmāsa drupadaṃ nṛpasattama
|
taṃ vinirjitya rājendra rājānastasya ye'nugāḥ||3||

4tān sarvān vaśāgāṃścakre karaṃ cainānadāpayat
|
athottarāṃ diśaṃ gatvā vaśe cakre narādhipān||4||

5bhagadattaṃ ca nirjitya rādheyo girimāruhat
|
himavantaṃ mahāśailaṃ yudhyamānaśca śatrubhiḥ||5||

6prayayau ca diśaḥ sarvān nṛpatīn vaśamānayat
|
sa haimavatikān jitvā karaṃ sarvānadāpayat||6||

7nepālaviṣaye ye ca rājānastānavājayat
|
avatīrya tataḥ śailāt pūrvāṃ diśamabhidrutaḥ||7||

8aṅgān vaṅgān kaliṅgāṃśca śuṇḍikān mithilānatha
|
māgadhān karkakhaṇḍāṃśca niveśya viṣaye''tmanaḥ||8||

9āvaśīrāṃśca yodhyāṃśca ahikṣatraṃ ca nirjayat
|
pūrvā diśaṃ vinirjitya vatsabhūmiṃ tathā'gamat||9||

10vatsabhūmiṃ vinirjitya kevalāṃ mṛttikāvatīm
|
mohanaṃ pattanaṃ caiva tripurīṃ kosalāṃ tathā||10||

11etān sarvān vinirjitya karamādāya sarvaśaḥ
|
dakṣiṇāṃ diśamāsthāya karṇo jitvā mahārathān||11||

12rukmiṇaṃ dākṣiṇātyeṣu yodhayāmāsa sūtajaḥ
|
sa yuddhaṃ tumulaṃ kṛtvā rukmī provāca sūtajam||12||

13prīto'smi tava rājendra vikrameṇa balena ca
|
na te vighnaṃ kariṣyāmi pratijñāṃ samapālayam||13||

14prītyā cāhaṃ prayacchāmi hiraṇyaṃ yāvadicchāsa
|
sametya rukmiṇā karṇaḥ pāṇḍyaṃ śailaṃ ca so'gamat||14||

15sa keralaṃ raṇe caiva nīlaṃ cāpi mahīpatim
|
veṇudārisutaṃ caiva ye cānye nṛpasattamāḥ||15||

16dakṣiṇasyāṃ diśi nṛpān karān sarvānadāpayat
|
śaiśupāliṃ tato gatvā vijigye sutanandanaḥ||16||

17pārśvasthāṃścāpi nṛpatīn vaśe cakre mahābalaḥ
|
āvantyāṃśca vaśe kṛtvā sāmnā ca bharatarṣabha
|
vṛṣṇibhiḥ saha saṅgamya paścimāmapi nirjayat||17||

18vāruṇīṃ diśamāgamya yāvanān barbarāṃstathā
|
nṛpān paścimabhūmisthān dāpayāmāsa vai karān||18||

19vijitya pṛthivīṃ sarvāṃ sa pūrvāparadakṣiṇām
|
samlecchāṭavikān vīraḥ saparvatanivāsinaḥ||19||

20bhadrān rohitakāṃścaiva āgreyān mālavānapi
|
gaṇān sarvān vinirjitya nītikṛt prahasanniva||20||

21śaśakān yavanāṃścaiva vijigye sūtanandanaḥ
|
nagnajitpramukhāṃścaiva gaṇān jitvā mahārathān||21||

22evaṃ sa pṛthivīṃ sarvāṃ vaśe kṛtvā mahārathaḥ
|
vijitya puruṣavyāghro nāgasāhvayamāgamat||22||

23tamāgataṃ maheṣvāsaṃ dhārtarāṣṭro janādhipaḥ
|
pratyudgamya mahārāja sabhrātṛpitṛbāndhavaḥ||23||

24arcayāmāsa vidhinā karṇamāhavaśobhinam
|
āśrāvayacca tat karma prīyamāṇo janeśvaraḥ||24||

25yanna bhīṣmānna ca droṇānna kṛpānna ca bālhikāt
|
prāptavānasmi bhadraṃ te tvattaḥ prāptaṃ mayā hi tat||25||

26bahunā ca kimuktena śṛṇu karṇa vaco mama
|
sanātho'smi mahābāho tvayā nāthena sattama||26||

27na hi te pāṇḍavāḥ sarve kalāmarhanti ṣoḍaśīm
|
anye vā puruṣavyāghra rājāno'bhyuditoditāḥ||27||

28sa bhavān dhṛtarāṣṭraṃ taṃ gāndhārīṃ ca yaśasvinīm
|
paśya karṇa maheṣvāsa aditiṃ vajrabhṛdyathā||28||

29tato halahalāśabdaḥ prādurāsīdviśāṃpate
|
hāhākārāśca bahavo nagare nāgasāhvaye||29||

30kecidenaṃ praśaṃsanti nindanti sma tathā pare
|
tūṣṇīmāsaṃstathā cānye nṛpāstatra janādhipa||30||

31evaṃ vijitya rājendra karṇaḥ śastrabhṛtāṃ varaḥ
|
saparvatavanākāśāṃ sasamudrāṃ saniṣkuṭām||31||

32deśairuccāvacaiḥ pūrṇāṃ pattanairnagarairapi
|
dvīpaiścānūpasampūrṇaiḥ pṛthivīṃ pṛthivīpate||32||

33kālena nātidīrgheṇa vaśe kṛtvā tu pārthivān
|
akṣayaṃ dhanamādāya sūtajo nṛpamabhyayāt||33||

34praviśya ca gṛhaṃ rājannabhyantaramarindama
|
gāndhārīsahitaṃ vīro dhṛtarāṣṭraṃ dadarśa saḥ||34||

35putravacca naravyāghra pādau jagrāha dharmavit
|
dhṛtarāṣṭreṇa cāśliṣya premṇā cāpi visarjitaḥ||35||

36tadāprabhṛti rājā ca śakuniścāpi saubalaḥ
|
jānate nirjitān pārthān karṇena yudhi bhārata||36||
