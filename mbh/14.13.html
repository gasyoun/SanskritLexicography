Mahābhārata 14.13 (Āśvamedhikaparvan)

1vāsudeva uvāca|
na bāhyaṃ dravyamutsṛjya siddhirbhavati bhārata
|
śārīraṃ dravyamutsṛjya siddhirbhavati vā na vā||1||

2bāhyadravyavimuktasya śārīreṣu ca gṛddhyataḥ
|
yo dharmo yatsukhaṃ caiva dviṣatāmastu tat tathā||2||

3dvyakṣarastu bhavenmṛtyustryakṣaraṃ brahma śāśvatam
|
mameti ca bhavenmṛtyurna mameti ca śāśvatam||3||

4brahmamṛtyū tato rājannātmanyeva vyavasthitau
|
adṛśyamānau bhūtāni yodhayetāmasaṃśayam||4||

5avināśo'sya sattvasya niyato yadi bhārata
|
bhittvā śarīraṃ bhūtānāmahiṃsāṃ pratipadyate||5||

6labdhvā hi pṛthivīṃ kṛtsnāṃ sahasthāvarajaṅgamām
|
mamatvaṃ yasya naiva syāt kiṃ tayā sa kariṣyati||6||

7athavā vasataḥ pārtha vane vanyena jīvataḥ
|
mamatā yasya dravyeṣu mṛtyorāsye sa vartate||7||

8bāhyāntarāṇāṃ śatrūṇāṃ svabhāvaṃ paśya bhārata
|
yanna paśyati tadbhūtamucyate sa mahābhayāt||8||

9kāmātmānaṃ na praśaṃsanti loke nehākāmā kācidasti pravṛttiḥ
|
sarve kāmā manaso'ṅgaprabhūtā yānpaṇḍitaḥ saṃharate vicintya||9||

10bhūyo bhūyo janmano'bhyāsayogādyogī yogaṃ sāramārga vicintya
|
dānaṃ ca vedādhyayanaṃ tapaśca kāmyāni karmāṇi ca vaidikāni||10||

11vrataṃ yajñānniyamān dhyānayogān kāmena yo nārabhate viditvā
|
yadyaccāyaṃ kāmayate sa dharmo na yo dharmo niyamastasya mūlam||11||

12atra gāthāḥ kāmagītāḥ kīrtayanti purāvidaḥ
|
śṛṇu saṅkīrtyamānāstā akhilena yudhiṣṭhira
|
nāhaṃ śakyo'nupāyena hantuṃ bhūtena kenacit||12||

13yo māṃ prayatate hantuṃ jñātvā praharaṇe balam
|
tasya tasmin praharaṇe punaḥ prādurbhavāmyaham||13||

14yo māṃ prayatate hantuṃ yajñairvividhadakṣiṇaiḥ
|
jaṅgameṣviva dharmātmā punaḥ prādurbhavāmyaham||14||

15yo māṃ prayatate nityaṃ vedairvedāntasādhanaiḥ
|
sthāvareṣviva bhūtātmā tasya prādurbhavāmyaham||15||

16yo māṃ prayatate hantuṃ dhṛtyā satyaparākramaḥ
|
bhāvo bhavāmi tasyāhaṃ sa ca māṃ nāvabudhyate||16||

17yo māṃ prayatate hantuṃ tapasā saṃśitavrataḥ
|
tatastapasi tasyātha punaḥ prādurbhavāmyaham||17||

18yo māṃ prayatate hantuṃ mokṣamāsthāya paṇḍitaḥ
|
tasya mokṣaratisthasya nṛtyāmi ca hasāmi ca
|
avadhyaḥ sarvabhūtānāmahamekaḥ sanātanaḥ||18||

19tasmāttvamapi taṃ kāmaṃ yajñairvividhadakṣiṇaiḥ
|
dharme kuru mahārāja tatra te sa bhaviṣyati||19||

20yajasva vājimedhena vidhivad dakṣiṇāvatā
|
anyaiśca vividhairyajñaiḥ samṛddharāptadakṣiṇaiḥ||20||

21mā te vyathāstu nihatān bandhūnvīkṣya punaḥ punaḥ
|
na śakyāste punardraṣṭuṃ ye hatā'smin raṇājire||21||

22sa tvamiṣṭvā mahāyajñaiḥ samṛddhairāptadakṣiṇaiḥ
|
kīrtiṃ loke parāṃ prāpya gatimagryāṃ gamiṣyasi||22||
