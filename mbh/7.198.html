Mahābhārata 7.198 (Droṇaparvan)

1dhṛtarāṣṭra uvāca|
sāṅgā vedā yathānyāyaṃ yenādhītā mahātmanā
|
yasminsākṣāddhanurvedo hrīniṣeve pratiṣṭhitaḥ||1||

2yasya prasādātkurvanti karmāṇi puruṣarṣabhāḥ
|
amānuṣāṇi saṃgrāme devairasukarāṇi ca||2||

3tasminnākruśyati droṇe samakṣaṃ pāpakarmaṇā
|
nīcātmanā nṛśaṃsena kṣudreṇa gurughātinā||3||

4nāmarṣaṃ tatra kurvanti dhikkṣātraṃ dhigamarṣitām
|
pārthāḥ sarve ca rājānaḥ pṛthivyāṃ ye dhanurdharāḥ||4||

5śrutvā kimāhuḥ pāñcālyaṃ tanmamācakṣva sañjaya
|
sañjaya uvāca|
śrutvā drupadaputrasya tā vācaḥ krūrakarmaṇaḥ||5||

6tūṣṇīṃ babhūvū rājānaḥ sarva eva viśāṃpate
|
arjunastu kaṭākṣeṇa jihmaṃ viprekṣya pārṣatam||6||

7sa bāṣpamatiniḥśvasya dhigdhigityeva cābravīt
|
yudhiṣṭhiraśca bhīmaśca yamau kṛṣṇastathā'pare||7||

8āsan suvrīḍitā rājan sātyakistvabravīdidam
|
nehāsti puruṣaḥ kaścidya imaṃ pāpapūruṣam||8||

9bhāṣamāṇamakalyāṇaṃ śīghraṃ hanyānnarādhamam
|
ete tvāṃ pāṇḍavāḥ sarve kutsayanti vivitsayā||9||

10karmaṇā tena pāpena śvapākaṃ brāhmaṇā iva
|
etatkṛtvā mahatpāpaṃ ninditaḥ sarvasādhubhiḥ||10||

11na lajjase kathaṃ vaktuṃ samitiṃ prāpya śobhanām
|
kathaṃ ca śatadhā jihvā na te mūrdhā ca dīryate||11||

12gurumākrośataḥ kṣudra na cādharmeṇa pātyase
|
vācyastvamasi pārthaiśca sarvaiścāṃdhakavṛṣṇibhiḥ||12||

13yatkarma kaluṣaṃ kṛtvā ślāghase janasaṃsadi
|
akāryaṃ tādṛśaṃ kṛtvā punareva guruṃ kṣipan||13||

14vadhyastvaṃ na tvayārtho'sti muhūrtamapi jīvatā
|
kastvetadvyavasedāryastvadanyaḥ puruṣādhama||14||

15nigṛhya keśeṣu vadhaṃ gurordharmātmanaḥ sataḥ
|
saptāvare tathā pūrve bāndhavāste nimajjitāḥ||15||

16yaśasā ca parityaktāstvāṃ prāpya kulapāṃsanam
|
uktavāṃścāpi yatpārthe bhīṣmaṃ prati nararṣabha||16||

17tathā'nto vihitastena svayameva mahātmanā
|
tasyāpi tava sodaryo nihantā pāpakṛttamaḥ||17||

18nānyaḥ pāñcālyaputrebhyo vidyate bhuvi pāpakṛt
|
sa cāpi sṛṣṭaḥ pitrā te bhīṣmasyāṃtakaraḥ kila||18||

19śikhaṇḍī rakṣitastena sa ca mṛtyurmahātmanaḥ
|
pañcālāścalitā dharmātkṣudrā mitra-gurudruhaḥ||19||

20tvāṃ prāpya sahasodaryaṃ dhikkṛtaṃ sarvasādhubhiḥ
|
punaścedīdṛśīṃ vācaṃ matsamīpe vadiṣyasi||20||

21śiraste pothayiṣyāmi gadayā vajrakalpayā
|
tvāṃ ca brahmahaṇaṃ dṛṣṭvā janaḥ sūryamavekṣate||21||

22brahmahatyā hi te pāpaṃ prāyaścittārthamātmanaḥ
|
pāñcālaka sudurvṛtta mamaiva gurumagrataḥ||22||

23gurorguruṃ ca bhūyo'pi kṣipannaiva hi lajjase
|
tiṣṭha tiṣṭha sahasvaikaṃ gadāpātamimaṃ mama||23||

24tava cāpi sahiṣye'haṃ gadāpātānanekaśaḥ
|
sāttvatenaivamākṣiptaḥ pārṣataḥ paruṣākṣaram||24||

25saṃrabdhaṃ sātyakiṃ prāha saṃkruddhaḥ prahasanniva
|
dhṛṣṭadyumna uvāca|
śrūyate śrūyate ceti kṣamyate ceti mādhava||25||

26sadā'nāryo'śubhaḥ sādhuṃ puruṣaṃ kṣeptumicchati
|
kṣamā praśasyate loke na tu pāpo'rhati kṣamām||26||

27kṣamāvantaṃ hi pāpātmā jito'yamiti manyate
|
sa tvaṃ kṣudrasamācāro nīcātmā pāpaniścayaḥ||27||

28ākeśāgrānnakhāgrācca vaktavyo vaktumicchasi
|
yaḥ sa bhūriśravāśchinnabhujaḥ prāyagatastvayā||28||

29vāryamāṇena hi hatastataḥ pāpataraṃ nu kim
|
gāhamāno mayā droṇo divyenāstreṇa saṃyuge||29||

30visṛṣṭaśastro nihataḥ kiṃ tatra krūra duṣkṛtam
|
ayudhyamānaṃ yastvājau tathā prāyagataṃ munim||30||

31chinnabāhuṃ parairhanyātsātyake sa kathaṃ vadet
|
nihatya tvāṃ padā bhūmau sa vikarṣati vīryavān||31||

32kiṃ tadā na nihaṃsyenaṃ bhūtvā puruṣasattamaḥ
|
tvayā punaranāryeṇa pūrvaṃ pārthena nirjitaḥ||32||

33yadā tadā hataḥ śūraḥ saumadattiḥ pratāpavān
|
yatra yatra tu pāṇḍūnāṃ droṇo drāvayate camūm||33||

34kirañcharasahasrāṇi tatra tatra prayāmyaham
|
sa tvamevaṃvidhaṃ kṛtvā karmacāṇḍālavatsvayam||34||

35vaktumarhasi vaktavyaḥ kasmāttvaṃ paruṣāṇyatha
|
kartā tvaṃ karmaṇo hyasya nāhaṃ vṛṣṇikulādhama||35||

36pāpānāṃ ca tvamāvāsaḥ karmaṇāṃ mā punarvada
|
joṣamāsva na māṃ bhūyo vaktumarhasyataḥ param||36||

37adharottarametaddhi yanmāṃ tvaṃ vaktumarhasi
|
atha vakṣyasi māṃ maurkhyādbhūyaḥ paruṣamīdṛśam||37||

38gamayiṣyāmi bāṇaistvāṃ yudhi vaivasvatakṣayam
|
na caivaṃ mūrkha dharmeṇa kevalenaiva śakyate||38||

39teṣāmapi hyadharmeṇa ceṣṭitaṃ śṛṇu yādṛśam
|
vañcitaḥ pāṇḍavaḥ pūrvamadharmeṇa yudhiṣṭhiraḥ||39||

40draupadī ca parikliṣṭā tathā'dharmeṇa sātyake
|
pravrājitā vanaṃ sarve pāṇḍavāḥ saha kṛṣṇayā||40||

41sarvasvamapakṛṣṭaṃ ca tathā'dharmeṇa bāliśa
|
adharmeṇāpakṛṣṭaśca madrarājaḥ pareritaḥ||41||

42adharmeṇa tathā bālaḥ saubhadro vinipātitaḥ
|
ito'pyadharmeṇa hato bhīṣmaḥ parapurañjayaḥ||42||

43bhūriśravā hyadharmeṇa tvayā dharmavidā hataḥ
|
evaṃ parairācaritaṃ pāṇḍaveyaiśca saṃyuge||43||

44rakṣamāṇairjayaṃ vīrairdharmajñairapi sātvata
|
durjñeyaḥ sa paro dharmastathā'dharmaśca durvidaḥ||44||

45yudhyasva kauravaiḥ sārdhaṃ mā gāḥ pitṛniveśanam
|
sañjaya uvāca|
evamādīni vākyāni krūrāṇi paruṣāṇi ca||45||

46śrāvitaḥ sātyakiḥ śrīmānākampita ivābhavat
|
tacchrutvā krodhatāmrākṣaḥ sātyakistvādade gadām||46||

47viniḥśvasya yathā sarpaḥ praṇidhāya rathe dhanuḥ
|
tato'bhipatya pāñcālyaṃ saṃraṃbheṇedamabravīt||47||

48na tvāṃ vakṣyāmi paruṣaṃ haniṣye tvāṃ vadhakṣamam
|
tamāpatantaṃ sahasā mahābalamamarṣaṇam||48||

49pāñcālyāyābhisaṃkruddhamantakāyāntakopamam
|
codito vāsudevena bhīmaseno mahābalaḥ||49||

50avaplutya rathāttūrṇaṃ bāhubhyāṃ samavārayat
|
dravamāṇaṃ tathā kruddhaṃ sātyakiṃ pāṇḍavo balī||50||

51praspanda‌mānamādāya jagāma balinaṃ balāt
|
sthitvā viṣṭabhya caraṇau bhīmena śinipuṅgavaḥ||51||

52nigṛhītaḥ pade ṣaṣṭhe balena balināṃ varaḥ
|
avaruhya rathāttūrṇaṃ dhriyamāṇaṃ balīyasā||52||

53uvāca ślakṣṇayā vācā sahadevo viśāṃpate
|
asmākaṃ puruṣavyāghra mitramanyanna vidyate||53||

54paramandhakavṛṣṇibhyaḥ pañcālebhyaśca māriṣa
|
tathaivāndhakavṛṣṇīnāṃ tathaiva ca viśeṣataḥ||54||

55kṛṣṇasya ca tathā'smatto mitramanyanna vidyate
|
pañcālānāṃ ca vārṣṇeya samudrāntāṃ vicinvatām||55||

56nānyadasti paraṃ mitraṃ yathā pāṇḍavavṛṣṇayaḥ
|
sa bhavānīdṛśaṃ mitraṃ manyate ca yathā bhavān||56||

57bhavantaśca yathā'smākaṃ bhavatāṃ ca tathā vayam
|
sa evaṃ sarvadharmajña mitradharmamanusmaran||57||

58niyaccha manyuṃ pāñcālyāt praśāmya śinipuṅgava
|
pārṣatasya kṣama tvaṃ vai kṣamatāṃ pārṣataśca te||58||

59vayaṃ kṣamayitāraśca kimanyatra śamādbhavet
|
praśāmyamāne śaineye sahadevena māriṣa||59||

60pāñcālarājasya sutaḥ prahasannidamabravīt
|
muñca muñca śineḥ pautraṃ bhīma yuddhamadānvitam||60||

61āsādayatu māmeṣa dharādharamivānilaḥ
|
yāvadasya śitairbāṇaiḥ saṃraṃbhaṃ vinayāmyaham||61||

62yuddhaśraddhāṃ ca kaunteya jīvitaṃ cāsya saṃyuge
|
kiṃ nu śakyaṃ mayā kartuṃ kāryaṃ yadidamudyatam||62||

63sumahatpāṇḍuputrāṇāmāyāntyete hi kauravāḥ
|
athavā phālgunaḥ sarvānvārayiṣyati saṃyuge||63||

64ahamapyasya mūrdhānaṃ pātayiṣyāmi sāyakaiḥ
|
manyate cchinnabāhuṃ māṃ bhūriśravasamāhave||64||

65utsṛjainamahaṃ cainameṣa vā māṃ haniṣyati
|
śṛṇvanpāñcālavākyāni sātyakiḥ sarpavacchvasan||65||

66bhīmabāhvantare sakto visphuratyaniśaṃ balī
|
tau vṛṣāviva nardantau balinau bāhuśālinau||66||

67tvarayā vāsudevaśca dharmarājaśca māriṣa
|
yatnena mahatā vīrau vārayāmāsatustataḥ||67||

68nivārya parameṣvāsau kopasaṃraktalocanau
|
yuyutsūnaparānsaṃkhye pratīyuḥ kṣatriyarṣabhāḥ||68||
