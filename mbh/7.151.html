Mahābhārata 7.151 (Droṇaparvan)

1dhṛtarāṣṭra uvāca|
sindhurāje hate tāta samare savyasācinā
|
tathaiva bhūriśravasi kimāsīdvo manastadā||1||

2duryodhanena ca droṇastathoktaḥ kurusaṃsadi
|
kimuktavānparaṃ tasmai tanmamācakṣva sañjaya||2||

3sañjaya uvāca|
niṣṭānako mahānāsītsainyānāṃ tava bhārata
|
saindhavaṃ nihataṃ dṛṣṭvā bhūriśravasameva ca||3||

4mantritaṃ tava putrasya te sarvamavamenire
|
yena mantreṇa nihatāḥ śataśaḥ kṣatriyarṣabhāḥ||4||

5droṇastu tadvacaḥ śrutvā putrasya tava durmanāḥ
|
muhūrtamiva taddhyātvā bhṛśamārto'bhyabhāṣata||5||

6droṇa uvāca|
duryodhana kimevaṃ māṃ vākśarairapi kṛntasi
|
ajayyaṃ satataṃ saṃkhye bruvāṇaṃ savyacācinam||6||

7etenaivārjunaṃ jñātumalaṃ kaurava saṃyuge
|
yacchikhaṇḍyavadhīdbhīṣmaṃ pālyamānaḥ kirīṭinā||7||

8avadhyaṃ nihataṃ dṛṣṭvā saṃyuge devadānavaiḥ
|
tadaivājñāsiṣamahaṃ neyamastīti bhāratī||8||

9yaṃ puṃsāṃ triṣu lokeṣu sarvaśūramamaṃsmahi
|
tasminnipatite śūre kiṃ śeṣaṃ paryupāsmahe||9||

10yāṃ sma tān glahate tāta śakuniḥ kurusaṃsadi
|
akṣānna te'kṣā niśitā bāṇāste śatrutāpanāḥ||10||

11ta ete ghnanti nastāta viśikhāḥ pārthacoditāḥ
|
tāṃstadā''khyāyamānastvaṃ vidureṇa na buddhavān||11||

12yāstā vijayataścāpi vidurasya mahātmanaḥ
|
dhīrasya vāco nāśrauṣīḥ kṣemāya vadataḥ śivāḥ||12||

13tadidaṃ vartate ghoramāgataṃ vaiśasaṃ mahat
|
tasyāvamānādvākyasya duryodhana kṛte tava||13||

14yo'vamanya vacaḥ pathyaṃ suhṛdāmāptakāriṇām
|
svamataṃ kurute mūḍhaḥ sa śocyo na cirādiva||14||

15yacca naḥ prekṣamāṇānāṃ kṛṣṇāmānāyya tatsabhām
|
anarhantīṃ kule jātāṃ sarvadharmānucāriṇīm||15||

16tasyādharmasya gāndhāre phalaṃ prāptamidaṃ mahat
|
nocetpāpaṃ pare loke tvamarcchethāstato'dhikam||16||

17yacca tānpāṇḍavān dyūte viṣameṇa vijitya ha
|
prāvrājayastadāraṇye rauravājinavāsasaḥ||17||

18putrāṇāmiva caiteṣāṃ dharmamācaratāṃ sadā
|
druhyetko nu naro loke madanyo brāhmaṇabruvaḥ||18||

19pāṇḍavānāmayaṃ kopastvayā śakuninā saha
|
āhṛto dhṛtarāṣṭrasya saṃmate kurusaṃsadi||19||

20duḥśāsanena saṃyuktaḥ karṇena parivardhitaḥ
|
kṣatturvākyamanādṛtya tvayā'bhyastaḥ punaḥ punaḥ||20||

21yattāḥ sarve parābhūtāḥ paryavārayatā'rjunam
|
sindhurājānamāśritya sa vo madhye kathaṃ hataḥ||21||

22kathaṃ tvayi ca karṇe ca kṛpe śalye ca jīvati
|
aśvatthāmni ca kauravya nidhanaṃ saindhavo'gamat||22||

23yudhyantaḥ sarvarājānastejastigmamupāsate
|
sindhurājaṃ paritrātuṃ sa vo madhye kathaṃ hataḥ||23||

24mayyeva hi viśeṣeṇa tathā duryodhana tvayi
|
āśaṃsata paritrāṇamarjunātsa mahīpatiḥ||24||

25tatastasminparitrāṇamalabdhavati phālgunāt
|
na kiñcida‌nupaśyāmi jīvitasthānamātmanaḥ||25||

26majjantamiva cātmāna dhṛṣṭadyumnasya kilbiṣe
|
paśyāmyahatvā pañcālānsaha tena śikhaṇḍinā||26||

27tanmāṃ kimabhitapyantaṃ vākśaraireva kṛntasi
|
aśaktaḥ sindhurājasya bhūtvā trāṇāya bhārata||27||

28sauvarṇaṃ satyasandhasya dhvajamakliṣṭakarmaṇaḥ
|
apaśyanyudhi bhīṣmasya kathamāśaṃsase jayam||28||

29madhye mahārathānāṃ ca yatrāhanyata saindhavaḥ
|
hato bhūriśravāścaiva kiṃ śeṣaṃ tatra manyase||29||

30kṛpa eva ca durdharṣo yadi jīvati pārthiva
|
yo nāgātsiṃndhurājasya vartma taṃ pūjayāmyaham||30||

31yatrāpaśyaṃ hataṃ bhīṣmaṃ paśyataste'nujasya vai
|
duḥśāsanasya kauravya kurvāṇaṃ karma duṣkaram||31||

32avadhyakalpaṃ saṃgrāme devairapi savāsavaiḥ
|
na te vasundharā'stīti tadā'haṃ cintaye nṛpa||32||

33imāni pāṇḍavānāṃ ca sṛñjayānāṃ ca bhārata
|
anīkānyādravante māṃ sahitānyadya bhārata||33||

34nāhatvā sarvapañcālānkavacasya vimokṣaṇam
|
kartā'smi samare karma dhārtarāṣṭra hitaṃ tava||34||

35rājan brūyāḥ sutaṃ me tvamaśvatthāmānamāhave
|
na somakāḥ pramoktavyā jīvitaṃ parirakṣatā||35||

36yacca pitrā'nuśiṣṭo'si tadvacaḥ paripālaya
|
ānṛśaṃsye dame satye cājave ca sthiro bhava||36||

37dharmārthakāmakuśalo dharmārthāvapyapīḍayan
|
dharmapradhānakāryāṇi kuryāśceti punaḥ punaḥ||37||

38cakṣurmanobhyāṃ santoṣyā viprāḥ pūjyāśca śaktitaḥ
|
na caiṣāṃ vipriyaṃ kāryaṃ te hi vahniśikhopamāḥ||38||

39eṣa tvahamanīkāni praviśāmyarisūdana
|
raṇāya mahate rājaṃstvayā vākśarapīḍitaḥ||39||

40tvaṃ ca duryodhana balaṃ yadi śakto'si pālaya
|
rātrāvapi ca yotsyante saṃrabdhāḥ kurusṛñjayāḥ||40||

41evamuktvā tataḥ prāyāddroṇaḥ pāṇḍavasṛñjayān
|
muṣṇan kṣatriyatejāṃsi nakṣatrāṇāmivāṃśumān||41||
