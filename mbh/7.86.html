Mahābhārata 7.86 (Droṇaparvan)

1sañjaya uvāca|
hanta te saṃpravakṣyāmi sarvaṃ pratyakṣadarśivān
|
śuśrūṣasva sthiro bhūtvā tava hyapanayo mahān ||1||

2gatodake setubandho yādṛk tādṛgayaṃ tava
|
vilāpo niṣphalo rājanmā śuco bharatarṣabha ||2||

3anatikramaṇīyo'yaṃ kṛtāntasyādbhuto vidhiḥ
|
mā śuco bharataśreṣṭha diṣṭametat purātanam ||3||

4yadi tvaṃ hi purā dyūtāt kuntīputraṃ yudhiṣṭhiram
|
nivartayethāḥ putrāṃśca na tvāṃ vyasanamāvrajet ||4||

5yuddhakāle punaḥ prāpte tadaiva bhavatā yadi
|
nivartitāḥ syuḥ saṃrabdhā na tvāṃ vyasanamāvrajet ||5||

6duryodhanaṃ cāvidheyaṃ badhnīteti purā yadi
|
kurūnacodayiṣyastvaṃ na tvāṃ vyasanamāvrajet ||6||

7na te buddhivyabhīcāramupalapsyanti pāṇḍavāḥ
|
pañcālā vṛṣṇayaḥ sarve ye cānye'pi narādhipāḥ ||7||

8sa kṛtvā pitṛkarma tvaṃ putraṃ saṃsthāpya satpathe
|
vartethā yadi dharmeṇa na tvāṃ vyasanamāvrajet ||8||

9tvaṃ tu prājñatamo loke hitvā dharmaṃ sanātanam
|
duryodhanasya karṇasya śakuneścānvagā matam ||9||

10tatte vilapitaṃ sarvaṃ mayā rājanniśāmitam
|
arthe niviśamānasya viṣamiśraṃ yathā madhu ||10||

11nāmanyata tadā kṛṣṇo rājānaṃ pāṇḍavaṃ purā
|
na bhīṣmaṃ naiva ca droṇaṃ yathā tvāṃ manyate'cyutaḥ ||11||

12ajānāt sa yadā tu tvāṃ rājadharmādadhaścyutam
|
tadāprabhṛti kṛṣṇastvāṃ na tathā bahu manyate ||12||

13paruṣāṇyucyamānāṃśca yathā pārthānupekṣase
|
tasyānubandhaḥ prāptastvāṃ putrāṇāṃ rājyakāmuka ||13||

14pitṛpaitāmahaṃ rājyamapavṛttaṃ tadā'nagha
|
atha pārthairjitāṃ kṛtnāṃ pṛthivīṃ pratyapadyathāḥ ||14||

15pāṇḍunā nirjitaṃ rājyaṃ kauravāṇāṃ yaśastathā
|
tataścāpyadhikaṃ bhūyaḥ pāṇḍavairdharmacāribhiḥ ||15||

16teṣāṃ tattādṛśaṃ karma tvāmāsādya suniṣphalam
|
yatpitryādbhraṃśitā rājyāttvayehāmiṣagṛddhinā ||16||

17yatpunaryuddhakāle tvaṃ putrān garhayase nṛpa
|
bahudhā vyāharan doṣānna tadadyopapadyate ||17||

18na hi rakṣanti rājāno yuddhyanto jīvitaṃ raṇe
|
camūṃ vigāhya pārthānāṃ yudhyante kṣatriyarṣabhāḥ ||18||

19yāṃ tu kṛṣṇārjunau senāṃ yāṃ sātyakivṛkodarau
|
rakṣeran ko nu tāṃ yuddhyeccamūmanyatra kauravaiḥ ||19||

20yeṣāṃ yoddhā guḍākeśo yeṣāṃ mantrī janārdanaḥ
|
yeṣāṃ ca sātyakiryoddhā yeṣāṃ yoddhā vṛkodaraḥ ||20||

21ko hi tān viṣahedyoddhuṃ martyadharmā dhanurdharaḥ
|
anyatra kauraveyebhyo ye vā teṣāṃ padānugāḥ ||21||

22yāvattu śakyate kartumantarajñairjanādhipaiḥ
|
kṣatradharmarataiḥ śūraistāvat kurvanti kauravāḥ ||22||

23yathā tu puruṣavyāghrairyuddhaṃ paramasaṃkaṭam
|
kurūṇāṃ pāṇḍavaiḥ sārdhaṃ tatsarvaṃ śrṛṇu tattvataḥ ||23||
