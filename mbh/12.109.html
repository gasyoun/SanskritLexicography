Mahābhārata 12.109 (Śāntiparvan)

1yudhiṣṭhira uvāca|
kathaṃ dharme sthātumicchannaro varteta bhārata
|
vidvan jijñāsamānāya prabrūhi bharatarṣabha||1||

2satyaṃ cevānṛtaṃ cobhe lokānāvṛtya tiṣṭhataḥ
|
dayoḥ kimācaredrājanpuruṣo dharmaniścitaḥ||2||

3kiṃsvitsatyaṃ kimanṛtaṃ kiṃsviddharmyaṃ sanātanam
|
kasminkāle vadetsatyaṃ kasminkāle'nṛtaṃ vadet||3||

4bhīṣma uvāca|
satyasya vacanaṃ sādhu na satyādvidyate param
|
yattu lokeṣu durjñānaṃ tatpravakṣyāmi bhārata||4||

5bhavetsatyaṃ na vaktavyaṃ vaktavyamanṛtaṃ bhavet
|
yatrānṛtaṃ bhavetsatyaṃ satyaṃ vāpyanṛtaṃ bhavet||5||

6tādṛśo badhyate bālo [vardhate pāpo] yatra satyamaniṣṭhitam
|
satyānṛte viniścitya tato bhavati dharmavit||6||

7apyanāryo kṛtaprajñaḥ puruṣo'pyatidāruṇaḥ
|
sumahatprāpnuyātpuṇyaṃ balāko'ndhavadhādiva||7||

8kimāścaryaṃ ca yanmūḍho dharmakāmo'pyadharmavit
|
sumahatprāpnuyātpuṇyaṃ gaṅgāyāmiva kauśikaḥ||8||

9tādṛśo'yamanupraśno yatra dharmaḥ sudurlabhaḥ
|
duṣkaraḥ pratisaṃkhyātuṃ tatkenātra vyavasyati||9||

10prabhavārthāya bhūtānāṃ dharmapravacanaṃ kṛtam
|
yaḥ syātprabhavasaṃyuktaḥ sa dharma iti niścayaḥ||10||

11dhāraṇāddharmamityāhurdharmeṇa vidhṛtāḥ prajāḥ
|
yaḥ syāddhāraṇasaṃyuktaḥ sa dharma iti niścayaḥ||11||

12ahiṃsārthāya bhūtānāṃ dharmapravacanaṃ kṛtam
|
yaḥ syādahiṃsāsaṃyuktaḥ sa dharma iti niścayaḥ||12||

13śrutidharma iti hyeke netyāhurapare janāḥ
|
na ca tatpratyasūyāmo na hi sarvaṃ vidhīyate||13||

14ye'nyāyena jihīrṣanto dhanamicchanti kasyacit
|
tebhyastu na tadākhyeyaṃ sa dharma iti niścayaḥ||14||

15akūjanena cenmokṣo nāvakūjetkathaṃcana
|
avaśyaṃ kūjitavye vā śaṅkaranvā'pyakūjanāt||15||

16śreyastatrānṛtaṃ vaktuṃ satyāditi vicāritam
|
yaḥ pāpaiḥ saha sambandhānmucyate śapathādapi||16||

17na tebhyo'pi dhanaṃ deyaṃ śakye sati kathaṃcana
|
pāpebhyo hi dhanaṃ dattaṃ dātāramapi pīḍayet||17||

18svaśarīroparodhena dhanamādātumicchataḥ
|
satyasampratipattyarthaṃ yadbrūyuḥ sākṣiṇaḥ kvacit||18||

19anuktvā tatra tadvācyaṃ sarve te'nṛtavādinaḥ
|
prāṇātyaye vivāhe ca vaktavyamanṛtaṃ bhavet||19||

20arthasya rakṣaṇārthāya pareṣāṃ dharmakāraṇāt
|
pareṣāṃ siddhimākāṅkṣannīcaḥ syāddharmabhikṣukaḥ||20||

21pratiśrutya pradātavyaḥ svakāryastu balātkṛtaḥ
|
yaḥ kaściddharmasamayātpracyuto dharmasādhanaḥ||21||

22daṇḍanaiva sa hantavyastaṃ panthānaṃ samāśritaḥ
|
cyutaḥ sadaiva dharmebhyo'mānavaṃ dharmamāsthitaḥ||22||

23śaṭhaḥ svadharmamutsṛjya tamicchedupajīvitum
|
sarvopāyairnihantavyaḥ pāpo nikṛtijīvanaḥ||23||

24dhanamityeva pāpānāṃ sarveṣāmiha niścayaḥ
|
aviṣahyā hyasambhojyā nikṛtyā patanaṃ gatāḥ||24||

25cyutā devamanuṣyebhyo yathā pretāstathaiva ta
|
niryajñāstapasā hīnā mā sma taiḥ saha saṅgamaḥ||25||

26dhananāśādduḥkhataraṃ jīvitādviprayojanam
|
ayaṃ te rocatāṃ dharma iti vācyaḥ prayatnataḥ||26||

27na kaścidasti pāpānāṃ dharma ityeṣa niścayaḥ
|
tathāgataṃ ca yo hanyānnāsau pāpena lipyate||27||

28svakarmaṇā hataṃ hanti hata eva sa hanyate
|
teṣu yaḥ samayaṃ kaścitkurvīta hatabuddhiṣu||28||

29yathā kākāśca gṛdhrāśca tathaivopadhijīvinaḥ
|
ardhvaṃ dehavimokṣānte bhavantyetāsu yoniṣu||29||

30yasmin yathā vartate yo manuṣyastasmiṃstathā vartitavyaṃ sa dharmaḥ
|
māyācāro māyayā bādhitavyaḥ sādhvācāraḥ sādhunā pratyupeyaḥ||30||
