Mahābhārata 4.63 (Virāṭaparvan)

1vaiśampāyana uvāca|tato duryodhanaḥ karṇo duḥśāsanaviviṃśatī
|
droṇaśca saha putreṇa kṛpaścāpi mahārathaḥ ||1||

2punaryayuśca saṃrabdhā dhanañjayajighāṃsavaḥ
|
visphārayantaścāpāni balavanti dṛḍhāni ca ||2||

3tānvikīrṇapatākena rathenādityavarcasā
|
pratyudyayau mahārāja samantādvānaradhvajaḥ ||3||

4tataḥ kṛpaśca karṇaśca droṇaśca rathināṃ varaḥ
|
taṃ mahāstrairmahāvīryaṃ parivārya dhanañjayam ||4||

5śaraughānsamyagasyanto jīmūtā iva vārṣikāḥ
|
vavarṣuḥ śaravarṣāṇi pātayanto dhanañjayam ||5||

6iṣubhirbahubhistūrṇaṃ samare lomavāhibhiḥ
|
adūrāt paryavasthāpya pūrayāmāsurādṛtāḥ ||6||

7tathā tairavakīrṇasya divyairastrai samantataḥ
|
na tasya dvyaṅgulamapi vivṛtaṃ sampradṛśyate ||7||

8tataḥ prahasya bībhatsurdivyamaindraṃ mahārathaḥ
|
astramādityasaṅkāśaṃ gāṇḍīve samayojayat ||8||

9śararaśmirivādityaḥ pratasthe samare balī
|
kirīṭamālī kaunteyaḥ sarvānprācchādayatkurūn ||9||

10yathā balāhake vidyutpāvako vā śiloccaye
|
tathā gāṇḍīvamabhavadindrāyudhamivānatam ||10||

11yathā varṣati parjanye vidyudvibhrājateḥ divi
|
dyotayantī diśaḥ sarvāḥ pṛthivīṃ ca samantataḥ ||11||

12tathā daśa diśaḥ sarvāḥ patadgāṇḍīvamāvṛṇot
|
nāgāśca rathinaḥ sarve mumuhustatra bhārata ||12||

13sarve śāntiparā yodhāḥ svacittāni na lebhire
|
saṅgrāme vimukhāḥ sarve yodhāste hatacetasaḥ ||13||

14evaṃ sarvāṇi sainyāni bhagnāni bharatarṣabha
|
vyadravanta diśaḥ sarvā nirāśāni svajīvite ||14||
