Mahābhārata 12.347 (Śāntiparvan)

1śaunaka uvāca|
śrutaṃ bhagavatastasya māhātmyaṃ paramātmanaḥ
|
janma dharmagṛhe caiva naranārāyaṇātmakam||1||

2mahāvarāhasṛṣṭā ca piṇḍotpattiḥ purātanī
|
pravṛttau ca nivṛttau ca yo yathā parikalpitaḥ||2||

3tathā ca naḥ śruto brahmankathyamānastvayā'nagha
|
havyakavyabhujo viṣṇurudakpūrve mahodadhau||3||

4yacca tatkathitaṃ pūrvaṃ tvayā hayaśiro mahat
|
tacca dṛṣṭaṃ bhagavatā brahmaṇā parameṣṭhinā||4||

5kiṃ tadutpāditaṃ pūrvaṃ hariṇā lokadhāriṇā
|
rūpaṃ prabhāvaṃ mahatāmapūrvaṃ dhīmatāṃ vara||5||

6dṛṣṭvā hi vibudhaśreṣṭhamapūrvamamitaujasam
|
tadaśvaśirasaṃ puṇyaṃ brahmā kimakaronmune||6||

7etannaḥ saṃśayaṃ brahmanpurāṇaṃ jñānasaṃbhavam
|
kathayasvottamamate mahāpuruṣanirmitam||7||

8pāvitāḥ sma tvayā brahmanpuṇyāṃ kathayatā kathām
|
sautiruvāca |
kathayiṣyāmi te sarvaṃ purāṇaṃ vedasaṃmitam||8||

9jagau yadbhagavān vyāso rājñaḥ pārikṣitasya vai
|
śrutvā'śvaśiraso mūrtiṃ devasya harimedhasaḥ||9||

10utpannasaṃśayo rājā etadevamacodayat
|
janamejaya uvāca|
yattaddarśitavānbrahmā devaṃ hayaśirodharam||10||

11kimarthaṃ tatsamabhavattanmamācakṣva sattama
|
vaiśampāyana uvāca|
yatkiñcidiha loke vai dehasattvaṃ viśāṃpate||11||

12sarvaṃ pañcabhirāviṣṭaṃ bhūtairīśvarabuddhibhiḥ
|
īśvaro hi jagatsraṣṭā prabhurnārāyaṇo virāṭ||12||

13bhūtāntarātmā varadaḥ saguṇo nirguṇo'pi ca
|
bhūtapralayamatyantaṃ śṛṇuṣva nṛpasattama||13||

14dharaṇyāmatha līnāyāmapsu caikārṇave purā
|
jyotirbhūte jale cāpi līne jyotiṣi cānile||14||

15vāyau cākāśasaṃlīne ākāśe ca manonuge
|
vyakte manasi saṃlīne vyakte cāvyaktatāṃ gate||15||

16avyakte puruṣaṃ yāte puṃsi sarvagate'pi ca
|
tama evābhavatsarvaṃ na prājñāyata kiṃcana||16||

17tamaso brahma saṃbhūtaṃ tamomūlāmṛtātmakam
|
tadviśvabhāvasaṃjñāntaṃ pauruṣīṃ tanumāśritam||17||

18so'niruddha iti proktastatpradhānaṃ pracakṣate
|
tadavyaktabhiti jñeyaṃ triguṇaṃ nṛpasattama||18||

19vidyāsahāyavāndevo viṣvakseno hariḥ prabhuḥ
|
apsveva śayanaṃ cakre nidrāyogamupāgataḥ||19||

20jagataścintayansṛṣṭiṃ citrāṃ bahuguṇodbhavām
|
tasya cintayataḥ sṛṣṭiṃ mahānātmaguṇaḥ smṛtaḥ||20||

21ahaṃkārastato jāto brahmā sa tu caturmukhaḥ
|
hiraṇyagarbho bhagavānsarvalokapitāmahaḥ||21||

22padme'niruddhātsaṃbhūtastadā padmanibhekṣaṇaḥ
|
sahasrapatre dyutimānupaviṣṭaḥ sanātanaḥ||22||

23dadṛśe'dbhutasaṃkāśo lokānāpomayānprabhuḥ
|
sattvasthaḥ parameṣṭhī sa tato bhūtagaṇānsṛjan||23||

24pūrvameva ca padmasya patre sūryāṃśusaprabhe
|
nārāyaṇakṛtau bindū apāmāstāṃ guṇottarau||24||

25tāvapaśyatsa bhagavānanādinidhano'cyutaḥ
|
ekastatrābhavadvindurmadhvābho ruciraprabhaḥ||25||

26sa tāmaso madhurjātastadā nārāyaṇājñayā
|
kaṭhinastvaparo binduḥ kaiṭabho rājasastu saḥ||26||

27tāvabhyadhāvatāṃ śreṣṭhau tamorajasānvitau
|
balavantau gadāhastau padmanālānusāriṇau||27||

28dadṛśāte'ravindasthaṃ brahmāṇamamitaprabham
|
sṛjantaṃ prathamaṃ vedāṃścaturaścāruvigrahān||28||

29tato vigrahavantau tau vedān dṛṣṭvā surottamau
|
sahasā jagṛhaturvedānbrahmaṇaḥ paśyatastadā||29||

30atha tau dānavaśreṣṭhau vedān gṛhya sanātanān
|
rasāṃ viviśatustūrṇamudakpūrve mahodadhau||30||

31tato hṛteṣu vedeṣu brahmā kaśmalamāviśat
|
tato vacanamīśānaṃ prāha vedairvinākṛtaḥ||31||

32brahmovāca|
vedā me paramaṃ cakṣurvedā me paramaṃ balam
|
vedā me paramaṃ dhāma vedā me brahma cottaram||32||

33mama vedā hṛtāḥ sarve dānavābhyāṃ balāditaḥ
|
andhakārā hi me lokā jātā vedairvinākṛtāḥ||33||

34vedānṛte hi kiṃ kuryāṃ lokānāṃ sṛṣṭimuttamām
|
aho bata mahadduḥkhaṃ vedanāśanajaṃ mama||34||

35prāptaṃ dunoti hṛdayaṃ tīvraṃ śokaparāyaṇam
|
ko hi śokārṇave magnaṃ māmito'dya samuddharet||35||

36vedāṃstāṃścānayennaṣṭānkasya cāhaṃ priyo bhave
|
ityevaṃ bhāṣamāṇasya brahmaṇo nṛpasattama||36||

37hareḥ stotrārthamudbhūtā buddhirbuddhimatāṃ vara
|
tato jagau paraṃ japyaṃ sāñjalipragrahaḥ prabhuḥ||37||

38brahmovāca|
oṃnamaste brahmahṛdaya namaste mama pūrvaja
|
lokādya bhuvanaśreṣṭha sāṃkhyayoganidhe prabho||38||

39vyaktāvyaktakarācintya kṣemaṃ panthānamāsthita
|
viśvabhuksarvabhūtānāmantarātmannayonija
|
ahaṃ prasādajastubhyaṃ lokadhāma svayaṃbhuvaḥ||39||

40tvatto me mānasaṃ janma prathamaṃ dvijapūjitam
|
cākṣuṣaṃ vai dvitīyaṃ me janma cāsītpurātanam||40||

41tvatprasādāttu me janma tṛtīyaṃ vācikaṃ mahat
|
tvattaḥ śravaṇajaṃ cāpi caturthaṃ janma me vibho||41||

42nāsatyaṃ cāpi me janma tvattaḥ paramamucyate
|
aṇḍajaṃ cāpi me janma tvattaḥ ṣaṣṭhaṃ vinirmitam||42||

43idaṃ ca saptamaṃ janma padmajanmeti vai prabho
|
sarge sarge hyahaṃ putrastava triguṇavarjita||43||

44prathamaṃ puṇḍarīkākṣaḥ pradhānaguṇakalpitaḥ
|
tvamīśvaraḥ svabhāvaśca karmabandhaḥ svayaṃbhuvaḥ ||44||

45tvayā vinirmito'haṃ vai vedacakṣurvayotigaḥ
|
te me vedā hatāścakṣurandho jāto'smi jāgṛhi||45||

46dadasva cakṣūṃṣi mama priyo'haṃ te priyo'si me
|
evaṃ stutaḥ sa bhagavānpuruṣaḥ sarvatomukhaḥ||46||

47jahau nidrāmatha tadā vedakāryārthamudyataḥ
|
aiśvaryeṇa prayogeṇa dvitīyāṃ tanumāsthitaḥ||47||

48sunāsikena kāyena bhūtvā candraprabhastadā
|
kṛtvā hayaśiraḥ śubhraṃ vedānāmālayaṃ prabhuḥ||48||

49tasya mūrdhā samabhavad dyauḥ sanakṣatratārakā
|
keśāścāsyābhavandīrghā raveraṃśusamaprabhāḥ||49||

50karṇāvākāśapātāle lalāṭaṃ bhūtadhāriṇī
|
gaṅgā sarasvatī śroṇyau bhruvāvāstāṃ mahodadhī||50||

51cakṣuṣī somasūryo te nāsā saṃdhyā punaḥ smṛtā
|
oṃkārastvatha saṃskāro vidyujjihvā ca nirmitā||51||

52dantāśca pitaro rājansomapā iti viśrutāḥ
|
goloko brahmalokaśca oṣṭhāvāstāṃ mahātmanaḥ||52||

53grīvā cāsyābhavadrājankālarātrirguṇottarā
|
etaddhayaśiraḥ kṛtvā nānāmūrtibhirāvṛtam||53||

54antardadhau sa viśveśo viveśa ca rasāṃ prabhuḥ
|
rasāṃ punaḥ praviṣṭaśca yogaṃ paramamāsthitaḥ||54||

55śaikṣyaṃ svaraṃ samāsthāya udgītaṃ prāsṛjatsvaram
|
sa svaraḥ sānunādī ca sarvaśaḥ snigdha eva ca||55||

56babhūvāntarmahībhūtaḥ sarvabhūtaguṇo hitaḥ
|
tatastāvasurau kṛtvā vedānsamayabandhanān||56||

57rasātale vinikṣipya yataḥ śabdastato drutau
|
etasminnantare rājandevo hayaśirodharaḥ||57||

58jagrāha vedānakhilān rasātalagatānhariḥ
|
prādācca brahmaṇe bhūyastataḥ svāṃ prakṛtiṃ gataḥ||58||

59sthāpayitvā hayaśira udakpūrve mahodadhau
|
vedānāmālayaṃ cāpi babhūvāśvaśirāstataḥ||59||

60atha kiñcidapaśyantau dānavau madhukaiṭabhau
|
punarājagmatustatra vegitau paśyatāṃ ca tau||60||

61yatra vedā vinikṣiptāstatsthānaṃ śūnyameva ca
|
tata uttamamāsthāya vegaṃ balavatāṃ varau||61||

62punaruttasthatuḥ śīghraṃ rasānāmālayāttadā
|
dadṛśāte ca puruṣaṃ tamevādikaraṃ prabhum||62||

63śvetaṃ candraviśuddhābhamaniruddhatanau sthitam
|
bhūyo'pyamitavikrāntaṃ nidrāyogamupāgatam||63||

64ātmapramāṇaracite apāmupari kalpite
|
śayane nāgabhogāḍhye jvālāmālāsamāvṛte||64||

65niṣkalmaṣeṇa sattvena saṃpannaṃ ruciraprabham
|
taṃ dṛṣṭvā dānavendrau tau mahāhāsamamuñcatām||65||

66ūcatuśca samāviṣṭau rajasā tamasā ca tau
|
ayaṃ sa puruṣaḥ śvetaḥ śete nidrāmupāgataḥ||66||

67anena nūnaṃ vedānāṃ kṛtamāharaṇaṃ rasāt
|
kasyaiṣa ko nu khalveṣa kiṃ na svapiti bhogavān||67||

68ityuccāritavākyau tau bodhayāmāsaturharim
|
yuddhārthinau hi vijñāya vibuddhaḥ puruṣottamaḥ||68||

69nirīkṣya cāsurendrau tau tato yuddhe mano dadhe
|
atha yuddhaṃ samabhavattayornārāyaṇasya vai||69||

70rajastamoviṣṭatanū tāvubhau madhukaiṭabhau
|
brahmaṇopacitiṃ kurvan jaghāna madhusūdanaḥ||70||

71tatastayorvadhenāśu vedāpaharaṇena ca
|
śokāpanayanaṃ cakre brahmaṇaḥ puruṣottamaḥ||71||

72tataḥ parivṛto brahmā hariṇā vedasatkṛtaḥ
|
nirmame sa tadā lokānkṛtsnānsthāvarajaṅgamān||72||

73dattvā pitāmahāyāgryāṃ matiṃ lokavisargikīm
|
tatraivāntardadhe devo yata evāgato hariḥ||73||

74tau dānavau harirhatvā kṛtvā hayaśirastanum
|
punaḥ pravṛttidharmārthaṃ tāmeva vidadhe tanum||74||

75evameva mahābhāgo babhūvāśvaśirā hariḥ
|
paurāṇametatprakhyātaṃ rūpaṃ varadamaiśvaram||75||

76yo hyetadbrāhmaṇo nityaṃ śṛṇuyāddhārayīta vā
|
na tasyādhyayanaṃ nāśamupagacchetkadācana||76||

77ārādhya tapasogreṇa devaṃ hayaśirodharam
|
pāñcālena kramaḥ prāpto devena pathi deśite||77||

78etaddhayaśiro rājannākhyānaṃ tava kīrtitam
|
purāṇaṃ vedasamitaṃ yanmāṃ tvaṃ paripṛcchasi||78||

79yāṃ yāmicchettanuṃ devaḥ kartuṃ kāryavidhau kvacit
|
tāṃ tāṃ kuryādvikurvāṇaḥ svayamātmānamātmanā||79||

80eṣa vedanidhiḥ śrīmāneṣa vai tapaso nidhiḥ
|
eṣa yogaśca sāṃkhyaṃ ca brahma cāgryaṃ havirvibhuḥ||80||

81nārāyaṇaparā vedā yajñā nārāyaṇātmakāḥ
|
tapo nārāyaṇaparaṃ nārāyaṇaparā gatiḥ||81||

82nārāyaṇaparaṃ satyamṛtaṃ nārāyaṇātmakam
|
nārāyaṇaparo dharmaḥ punarāvṛttidurlabhaḥ||82||

83pravṛttilakṣaṇaścaiva dharmo nārāyaṇātmakaḥ
|
nārāyaṇātmako gandho bhūmau śreṣṭhatamaḥ smṛtaḥ||83||

84apāṃ cāpi guṇā rājanrasā nārāyaṇātmakāḥ
|
jyotiṣāṃ ca paraṃ rūpaṃ smṛtaṃ nārāyaṇātmakam||84||

85nārāyaṇātmakaścāpi sparśo vāyuguṇaḥ smṛtaḥ
|
nārāyaṇātmakaścaiva śabda ākāśasaṃbhavaḥ||85||

86manaścāpi tato bhūtamavyaktaguṇalakṣaṇam
|
nārāyaṇaparaḥ kālo jyotiṣāmayanaṃ ca yat||86||

87nārāyaṇaparā kīrtiḥ śrīśca lakṣmīśca devatāḥ
|
nārāyaṇaparaṃ sāṃkhyaṃ yogo nārāyaṇātmakaḥ||87||

88kāraṇaṃ puruṣo hyeṣāṃ pradhānaṃ cāpi kāraṇam
|
svabhāvaścaiva karmāṇi daivaṃ yeṣāṃ ca kāraṇam||88||

89adhiṣṭhānaṃ tathā kartā karaṇaṃ ca pṛthagvidham
|
vividhā ca tathā ceṣṭā daivaṃ caivātra pañcamam||89||

90pañcakāraṇasaṃkhyāto niṣṭhā sarvatra vai hariḥ
|
tattvaṃ jijñāsamānānāṃ hetubhiḥ sarvatomukhaiḥ||90||

91tattvameko mahāyogī harirnārāyaṇaḥ prabhuḥ
|
brahmādīnāṃ sa lokānāmṛṣīṇāṃ ca mahātmanām||91||

92sāṃkhyānāṃ yogināṃ cāpi yatīnāmātmavedinām
|
manīṣitaṃ vijānāti keśavo na tu tasya te||92||

93ye kecitsarvalokeṣu daivaṃ pitryaṃ ca kurvate
|
dānāni ca prayacchanti tapyante ca tapo mahat||93||

94sarveṣāmāśrayo viṣṇuraiśvaraṃ vidhimāsthitaḥ
|
sarvabhūtakṛtāvaso vāsudeveti cocyate||94||

95ayaṃ hi nityaḥ paramo maharṣirmahāvibhūtirguṇavarjitākhyaḥ
|
guṇaiśca saṃyogamupaiti śīghraṃ kālo yathartāvṛtusamprayuktaḥ||95||

96naivāsya vindanti gatiṃ mahātmano na cāgatiṃ kaścidihānupaśyati
|
jñānātmakā: santi hi ye maharṣayaḥ paśyanti nityaṃ puruṣaṃ guṇādhikam||96||
