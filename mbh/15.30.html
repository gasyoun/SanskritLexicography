Mahābhārata 15.30 (Āśramavāsikaparvan)

1kuntyuvāca|
bhagavan śvaśuro me'si daivatasyāpi daivatam
|
sa me devātidevastvaṃ śṛṇu satyāṃ giraṃ mama||1||

2tapasvī kopano vipro durvāsā nāma me pituḥ
|
bhikṣāmupāto bhoktuṃ tamahaṃ paryatoṣayam||2||

3śaucena tvāgasastyāgaiḥ śuddhena manasā tathā
|
kopasthāneṣvapi mahatsvakupyanna kadācana||3||

4sa prīto varado me'bhūtkṛtakṛtyo mahāmuniḥ
|
avaśyaṃ te gṛhītavyamiti māṃ so'bravīdvacaḥ||4||

5tataḥ śāpabhayādvipramavocaṃ punareva tam
|
evamastviti ca prāha punareva sa me dvijaḥ||5||

6dharmasya jananī bhadre bhavitrī tvaṃ śubhānane
|
vaśe sthāsyanti te devā yāṃstvamāvāhayiṣyasi||6||

7ityuktvāntarhito viprastato'haṃ vismitābhavam
|
na ca sarvāsvavasthāsu smṛtirme vipraṇaśyati||7||

8atha harmyatalasthāhaṃ ravimudyantamīkṣatī
|
saṃsmṛtya tadṛṣervākyaṃ spṛhayantī divāniśam||8||

9sthitā'haṃ bālabhāvena tatra doṣamabuddhyatī
|
atha devaḥ sahasrāṃśurmatsamīpagato'bhavat||9||

10dvidhā kṛtvā''tmano dehaṃ bhūmau ca gagane'pi ca
|
tatāpa lokānekena dvitīyenāgamatsa mām||10||

11sa māmuvāca vepantīṃ varaṃ matto vṛṇīṣva ha
|
gamyatāmiti taṃ cāhaṃ praṇamya śirasā'vadam||11||

12sa māmuvāca tigmāṃśurvṛthā''hvānaṃ na me kṣamam
|
dhakṣyāmi tvāṃ ca vipraṃ ca yena datto varastava||12||

13tamahaṃ rakṣatī vipraṃ śāpādanapakāriṇam
|
putro me tvatsamo deva bhavediti tato'bravam||13||

14tato māṃ tejasā''viśya mohayitvā ca bhānumān
|
uvāca bhavitā putrastavetyabhyagamaddivam||14||

15tato'hamantarbhavane piturvṛttāntarakṣiṇī
|
gūḍhotpannaṃ sutaṃ bālaṃ jale karṇamavāsṛjam||15||

16nūnaṃ tasyaiva devasya prasādātpunareva tu
|
kanyā'hamabhavaṃ vipra yathā prāha sa māmṛṣiḥ||16||

17sa mayā mūḍhayā putro jñāyamāno'pyupekṣitaḥ
|
tanmāṃ dahati viprarṣe yathā suviditaṃ tava||17||

18yadi pāpamapāpaṃ vā yadetadvivṛtaṃ mayā
|
taṃ draṣṭumicchāmi bhagavanvyapanetuṃ tvamarhasi||18||

19yaccāsya rājño viditaṃ hṛdisthaṃ bhavato'nagha
|
taṃ cāyaṃ labhatāṃ kāmamadyaiva munisattama||19||

20ityuktaḥ pratyuvācedaṃ vyāso vedavidāṃ varaḥ
|
sādhu sarvamidaṃ bhāvyamevametadyathā''tya mām||20||

21aparādhaśca te nāsti kanyābhāvaṃ gatā hyasi
|
devāścaiśvaryavanto vai śarīrāṇyāviśanti vai||21||

22santi devanikāyāśca saṅkalpājjanayanti ye
|
vācā dṛṣṭyā tathā sparśātsaṅgharṣeṇeti pañcadhā||22||

23manuṣyadharmo daivena dharmeṇa hi na duṣyati
|
iti kunti vijānīhi vyetu te mānaso jvaraḥ||23||

24sarvaṃ balavatāṃ pathyaṃ sarvaṃ balavatāṃ śuci
|
sarvaṃ balavatāṃ dharmaḥ sarvaṃ balavatāṃ svakam||24||
