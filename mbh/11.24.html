Mahābhārata 11.24 (Strīparvan)

1gāndhāryuvāca|somadattasutaṃ paśya yuyudhānena pātitam
|
vitudyamānaṃ vihagairbahubhirmādhavāntike||1||

2putraśokābhisantaptaḥ śomadatto janārdana
|
yuyudhānaṃ maheṣvāsaṃ garhayanniva dṛśyate||2||

3asau hi bhūriśravaso mātā śokapariplutā
|
āśvāsayati bhartāraṃ somadattamaninditā||3||

4diṣṭyā nainaṃ mahārāja dāruṇaṃ bharatakṣayam
|
kurusaṅkrandanaṃ ghoraṃ yugāntamanupaśyasi||4||

5diṣṭyā yūpadhvajaṃ putraṃ vīraṃ bhūrisahasradam
|
anekakratuyajvānaṃ nihataṃ nānupaśyasi||5||

6diṣṭyā snuṣāṇāmākrande ghoraṃ vilapitaṃ bahu
|
na śṛṇoṣi mahārāja sārasīnāmivārṇave||6||

7ekavastrārdhasaṃvītāḥ prakīrṇāḥ sitamūrdhajāḥ
|
snuṣāste paridhāvanti hatāpatyā hateśvarāḥ||7||

8śvāpadairbhakṣyamāṇaṃ tvamaho diṣṭyā na paśyasi
|
chinnabāhuṃ naravyāghramarjunena nipātitam||8||

9śalaṃ vinihataṃ saṅkhye bhūriśravasameva ca
|
snuṣāśca vividhāḥ sarvā diṣṭyā nādyeha paśyasi||9||

10diṣṭyā tatkāñcanaṃ chatraṃ yūpaketormahātmanaḥ
|
vinīkīrṇaṃ rathopasthe saumadatterna paśyasi||10||

11amūstu bhūriśravaso bhāryāḥ sātyakinā hatam
|
parivāryānuśocanti bhartāramasitekṣaṇāḥ||11||

12etā vilapya karuṇaṃ bhartṛśokena karśitāḥ
|
patantyabhimukhā bhūmau kṛpaṇaṃ bata keśava||12||

13bībhatsuratibībhatsaṃ karmedamakarotkatham
|
pramattasya yadacchaitsīdbāhuṃ śūrasya yajvanaḥ||13||

14tataḥ pāpataraṃ karma kṛtavānapi sātyakiḥ
|
yasmātprāyopaviṣṭasya prāhārṣītsaṃśitātmanaḥ||14||

15eko dvābhyāṃ hataḥ śete tvamadharmeṇa dhārmika
|
kinnu vakṣyati vai satsu goṣṭhīṣu ca sabhāsu ca||15||

16apuṇyamayaśasyaṃ ca karmedaṃ sātyakiḥ svayam
|
iti yūpadhvajasyaitāḥ striyaḥ krośanti mādhava||16||

17bhāryā yūpadhvajasyaiṣā karasammitamadhyamā
|
kṛtvotsaṅge bhujaṃ bhartuḥ kṛpaṇaṃ paridevati||17||

18ayaṃ sa hantā śūrāṇāṃ mitrāṇāmabhayapradaḥ
|
pradātā gosahasrāṇāṃ kṣatriyāntakaraḥ karaḥ||18||

19ayaṃ sa rasanotkarṣī pīnastanavimardanaḥ
|
nābhyūrujaghanasparśī nīvīvisraṃsanaḥ karaḥ||19||

20vāsudevasya sānnidhye pārthenākliṣṭakarmaṇā
|
yudhyataḥ samare'nyena pramattasya nipātitaḥ||20||

21kinnu vakṣyasi saṃsatsu kathāsu ca janārdana
|
arjunasya mahatkarma svayaṃ vā sa kirīṭabhṛt||21||

22ityevaṃ garhayitvaiṣā tūṣṇīmāste varāṅganā
|
tāmetāmanuśocanti sapatnyaḥ svāmiva snuṣām||22||

23gāndhārarājaḥ śakunirbalavānsatyavikramaḥ
|
nihataḥ sahadevena bhāgineyena mātulaḥ||23||

24yaḥ purā hemadaṇḍābhyāṃ vyajanābhyāṃ sma vījyate
|
sa eṣa pakṣibhiḥ pakṣaiḥ śayāna upavījyate||24||

25yaḥ svarūpāṇi kurute śataśo'tha sahasraśaḥ
|
tasya māyāvino māyā dagdhāḥ pāṇḍavatejasā||25||

26māyayā nikṛtiprajño jitavānyo yudhiṣṭhiram
|
sabhāyāṃ vipulaṃ rājyaṃ sa punarjīvitaṃ jitaḥ||26||

27śakuntāḥ śakuniṃ kṛṣṇa samantātparyupāsate
|
kaitavaṃ mama putrāṇāṃ vināśāyopaśikṣitam||27||

28etenaitanmahadvairaṃ prasaktaṃ pāṇḍavaiḥ saha
|
vadhāya mama putrāṇāmātmanaḥ sagaṇasya ca||28||

29yathaiva mama putrāṇāṃ lokāḥ śastrajitāḥ prabho
|
evamasyāpi durbuddherlokāḥ śastreṇa vai jitāḥ||29||

30kathaṃ ca nāyaṃ tatrāpi putrānme bhrātṛbhiḥ saha
|
virodhayedṛjuprajñānanṛjurmadhusūdana||30||
