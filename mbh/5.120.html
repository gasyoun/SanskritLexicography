Mahābhārata 5.120 (Udyogaparvan)

1nārada uvāca|
sa tu rājā punastasyāḥ kartukāmaḥ svayaṃvaram
|
upagamyāśramapadaṃ gaṅgāyamunasaṅgame ||1||

2gṛhītamālyadāmāṃ tāṃ rathamāropya mādhavīm
|
pūruryaduśca bhaginīmāśrame paryadhāvatām ||2||

3nāgayakṣamanuṣyāṇāṃ gandharvamṛgapakṣiṇām
|
śailadrumavanaukānāmāsīttatra samāgamaḥ ||3||

4nānāpuruṣadeśyānāmīśvaraiśca samākulam
|
ṛṣibhirbrahmakalpaiśca samantādāvṛtaṃ vanam ||4||

5nirdiśyamāneṣu tu sā vareṣu varavarṇinī
|
varānutkramya sarvāṃstān varaṃ vṛtavatī vanam ||5||

6avatīrya rathāt kanyā namaskṛtya ca bandhuṣu
|
upagamya vanaṃ puṇyaṃ tapastepe yayātijā ||6||

7upavāsaiśca vividhairdīkṣābhirniyamaistathā
|
ātmano laghutāṃ kṛtvā babhūva mṛgacāriṇī ||7||

8vaidūryāṃkurakalpāni mṛdūni haritāni ca
|
carantī ślakṣṇaśaṣpāṇi tiktāni madhurāṇi ca ||8||

9sravantīnāṃ ca puṇyānāṃ surasāni śucīni ca
|
pibantī vārimukhyāni śītāni vimalāni ca ||9||

10vaneṣu mṛgarājeṣu vyāghraviproṣiteṣu ca
|
dāvāgniviprayukteṣu śūnyeṣu gahaneṣu ca ||10||

11carantī hariṇaiḥ sārdhaṃ mṛgīva vanacāriṇī
|
cacāra vipulaṃ dharmaṃ brahmacaryeṇa saṃvṛtam ||11||

12yayātirapi pūrveṣāṃ rājñāṃ vṛttamanuṣṭhitaḥ
|
bahuvarṣasahastrāyuryuyuje kāladharmaṇā ||12||

13pūruryaduśca dvau vaṃśe vardhamānau narottamau
|
tābhyāṃ pratiṣṭhito loke paraloke ca nāhuṣaḥ ||13||

14mahīpate narapatiryayātiḥ svargamāsthitaḥ
|
maharṣikalpo nṛpatiḥ svargāgryaphalabhugvibhuḥ ||14||

15bahuvarṣasahastrākhye kāle bahuguṇe gate
|
rājarṣiṣu niṣaṇṇeṣu mahīyassu mahardhiṣu ||15||

16avamene narān sarvān devānṛṣigaṇāṃstathā
|
yayātirmūḍhavijñāno vismayāviṣṭacetanaḥ ||16||

17tatastaṃ bubudhe devaḥ śakro balaniṣūdanaḥ
|
te ca rājarṣayaḥ sarve dhigdhigityevamabruvan ||17||

18vicāraśca samutpanno nirīkṣya nahuṣātmajam
|
konvayaṃ kasya vā rājñaḥ kathaṃ vā svargamāgataḥ ||18||

19karmaṇā kena siddho'yaṃ kva vā'nena tapaścitam
|
kathaṃ vā jñāyate svarge kena vā jñāyate'pyuta ||19||

20evaṃ vicārayantaste rājānaṃ svargavāsinaḥ
|
dṛṣṭvā papracchuranyonyaṃ yayātiṃ nṛpatiṃ prati ||20||

21vimānapālāḥ śataśaḥ svargadvārābhirakṣiṇaḥ
|
pṛṣṭā āsanapālāśca na jānīmetyathābruvan ||21||

22sarve te hyāvṛtajñānā nābhyajānanta taṃ nṛpam
|
sa muhūrtādatha nṛpo hataujāścābhavattadā ||22||
