Mahābhārata 3.162 (Āraṇyakaparvan)

1dhanada uvāca|
yudhiṣṭhira dhṛtirdākṣyaṃ deśakālaparākramāḥ
|
lokatantravidhānānāmeṣa pañcavidho vidhiḥ||1||

2dhṛtimantaśca dakṣāśca sve sve karmaṇi bhārata
|
parākramavidhānajñā narā kṛtayuge'bhavan||2||

3dhṛtimān deśakālajñaḥ sarvadharmavidhānavit
|
kṣatriyaḥ kṣatriyaśreṣṭha praśāsti pṛthivīṃ ciram||3||

4ya evaṃ vartate pārtha puruṣaḥ sarvakarmasu
|
sa loke labhate vīra yaśaḥ pretya ca sadgatim||4||

5deśakālāntaraprepsuḥ kṛtvā śakraḥ parākramam
|
saṃprāptastridive rājyaṃ vṛtrahā vasubhiḥ saha||5||

6yastu kevalasaṃrambhātprapātaṃ na nirīkṣate
|
pāpātmā pāpabuddhiryaḥ pāpamevānuvartate||6||

7karmaṇāmavibhāgajñaḥ pretya ceha vinaśyati
|
akālajñaḥ sudurmedhāḥ kāryāṇāmaviśeṣavit||7||

8vṛthācārasamārambhaḥ pretya ceha vinaśyati
|
sāhase vartamānānāṃ nikṛtīnāṃ durātmanām||8||

9sarvasāmarthyalipsūnāṃ pāpo bhavati niścayaḥ
|
adharmajño'valiptaśca bālabuddhiramarṣaṇaḥ||9||

10nirbhayo bhīmaseno'yaṃ taṃ śādhi puruṣarṣabha
|
ārṣṭiṣeṇasya rājarṣeḥ prāpya bhūyastvamāśramam||10||

11tāmistraṃ prathamaṃ pakṣa vītaśokabhayo vasa
|
alakāḥ saha gandharvairyakṣāśca saha kinnaraiḥ||11||

12manniyuktā manuṣyendra sarve ca girivāsinaḥ
|
rakṣiṣyanti mahābāho sahitaṃ dvijasattamaiḥ||12||

13sāhasādanusaṃprāptaḥ pratibudhya vṛkodaraḥ
|
vāryatāṃ sādhvayaṃ rājaṃstvayā dharmabhṛtāṃ vara||13||

14ataḥ paraṃ ca vo rājan drakṣyanti vanagocarāḥ
|
upasthāsyanti vo rājan rakṣiṣyante ca vaḥ sadā||14||

15tathaiva cānnapānāni svādūni ca bahūni ca
|
āhariṣyanti matpreṣyāḥ sadā vaḥ puruṣarṣabhāḥ||15||

16yathā jiṣṇurmahendrasya yathā vāyorvṛkodaraḥ
|
dharmasya tvaṃ yathā tāta yogotpanno nijaḥ sutaḥ||16||

17ātmajāvātmasaṃpannau yamau cobhau yathāśvinoḥ
|
rakṣyāstadvanmamāpīha yūyaṃ sarve yudhiṣṭhira||17||

18arthatattvavidhānajñaḥ sarvadharmavidhānavit
|
bhīmasenādavarajaḥ phālgunaḥ kuśalī divi||18||

19yāḥ kāścana matā loke svargyāḥ paramasaṃpadaḥ
|
janmaprabhṛti tāḥ sarvāḥ sthitāstāta dhanaṃjaye||19||

20damo dānaṃ balaṃ buddhirhrīrdhṛtisteja uttamam
|
etānyapi mahāsattve sthitānyamitatejasi||20||

21na mohāt kurute jiṣṇuḥ karma pāṇḍava garhitam
|
na pārthasya mṛṣoktāni kathayanti narā nṛṣu||21||

22sa devapitṛgandharvaiḥ kurūṇāṃ kīrtivardhanaḥ
|
mānitaḥ kurute'strāṇi śakrasadmani bhārata||22||

23yo'sau sarvānmahīpālāndharmeṇa vaśamānayat
|
sa śāntanurmahātejāḥ pitustava pitāmahaḥ||23||

24prīyate pārtha pārthena divi gāṇḍīvadhanvanā
|
samyakcāsau mahāvīryaḥ kuladhuryeṇa pārthivaḥ||24||

25pitṝn devānṛṣīnviprān pūjayitvā mahātapāḥ
|
sapta mukhyān mahāmedhānāharadyamunāṃ prati||25||

26adhirājaḥ sa rājaṃstvāṃ śāntanuḥ prapitāmahaḥ
|
svargajicchakralokasthaḥ kuśalaṃ paripṛcchati||26||

27vaiśampāyana uvāca|
etacchrutvā tu vacanaṃ dhanadena prabhāṣitam
|
pāṇḍavāśca tatastena babhūvuḥ saṃpraharṣitāḥ||27||

28tataḥ śaktiṃ gadāṃ khaḍgaṃ dhanuśca bharatarṣabhaḥ
|
prādhvaṃ kṛtvā namaścakre kuberāya vṛkodaraḥ||28||

29tato'bravīddhanādhyakṣaḥ śaraṇyaḥ śaraṇāgatam
|
mānahā bhava śatrūṇāṃ suhṛdāṃ nandivardhanaḥ||29||

30sveṣu veśmasu ramyeṣu vasatāmitratāpanāḥ
|
kāmānna parihāsyanti yakṣā vo bharatarṣabhāḥ||30||

31śīghrameva guḍākeśaḥ kṛtāstraḥ punareṣyati
|
sākṣānmaghavatā sṛṣṭaḥ saṃprāpsyati dhanaṃjayaḥ||31||

32evamuttamakarmāṇamanuśiṣya yudhiṣṭhiram
|
astaṃ girivaraśreṣṭhaṃ prayayau guhyakādhipaḥ||32||

33taṃ paristomasaṃkīrṇairnānāratnavibhūṣitaiḥ
|
yānairanuyayuryakṣā rākṣasāśca sahasraśaḥ||33||

34pakṣiṇāmiva nirghoṣaḥ kuberasadanaṃ prati
|
babhūva paramāśvānāmairāvatapathe yathā||34||

35te jagmustūrṇamākāśaṃ dhanādhipativājinaḥ
|
prakarṣanta ivābhrāṇi pibanta iva mārutam||35||

36tatastāni śarīrāṇi gatasattvāni rakṣasām
|
apākṛpyanta śailāgrāddhanādhipatiśāsanāt||36||

37teṣāṃ hi śāpakālaḥ sa kṛto'gastyena dhīmatā
|
samare nihatāstasmācchāpasyānto'bhavattadā||37||

38pāṇḍavāśca mahātmānasteṣu veśmasu tāḥ kṣapāḥ
|
sukhamūṣurgatodvegāḥ pūjitāḥ sarvarākṣasaiḥ||38||
