Mahābhārata 13.106 (Anuśāsanaparvan)

1yudhiṣṭhira uvāca|
sarveṣāmeva varṇānāṃ mlecchānāṃ ca pitāmaha
|
upavāse matiriyaṃ kāraṇaṃ ca na vidmahe ||1||

2brahmakṣatreṇa niyamāścartavyā iti naḥ śrutam
|
upavāse kathaṃ teṣāṃ kṛtyamasti pitāmaha ||2||

3niyamāṃścopavāsāṃśca sarveṣāṃ brūhi pārthiva
|
āpnoti kāṃ gatiṃ tāta upavāsaparāyaṇaḥ ||3||

4upavāsaḥ paraṃ puṇyamupavāsaḥ parāyaṇam
|
upoṣyeha naraśreṣṭha kiṃ phalaṃ pratipadyate ||4||

5adharmānmucyate kena dharmamāpnoti vā katham
|
svargaṃ puṇyaṃ ca labhate kathaṃ bharatasattama ||5||

6upoṣya cāpi kiṃ tena pradeyaṃ syānnarādhipa
|
dharmeṇa ca sukhānaঁrthāllabhedyena bravīhi tam ||6||

7vaiśampāyana uvāca|
evaṃ bruvāṇaṃ kaunteyaṃ dharmajñaṃ dharmattattvavit
|
dharmaputramidaṃ vākyaṃ bhīṣmaḥ śāṃtanavo'bravīt ||7||

8bhīṣma uvāca|
idaṃ khalu mayā rājan śrutamāsītpurātanam
|
upavāsavidhau śreṣṭhā guṇā ye bharatarṣabha ||8||

9ṛṣimaṅgirasaṃ pūrvaṃ pṛṣṭavānasmi bhārata
|
yathā māṃ tvaṃ tathaivāhaṃ pṛṣṭavāṃstaṃ tapodhanam ||9||

10praśnametaṃ mayā pṛṣṭo bhagavānagnisaṃbhavaḥ
|
upavāsavidhiṃ puṇyamācaṣṭa bharatarṣabha ||10||

11aṅgirā uvāca|
brahmakṣatre trirātraṃ tu vihitaṃ kurunandana
|
dvistrirātramathaikāhaṃ nirdiṣṭaṃ puruṣarṣabha ||11||

12vaiśyāḥ śūdrāśca yanmohādupavāsaṃ pracakrire
|
trirātraṃ vā dvirātraṃ vā tayorvyuṣṭirna vidyate ||12||

13caturthabhaktakṣapaṇaṃ vaiśye śūdre vidhīyate
|
trirātraṃ na tu dharmajñairvihitaṃ dharmadarśibhiḥ ||13||

14pañcamyāṃ vā'pi ṣaṣṭhyāṃ ca paurṇamāsyāṃ ca bhārata
|
upoṣya ekabhaktena niyatā''tmā jitendriyaḥ ||14||

15kṣamāvān rūpasampannaḥ śrutavāṃścaiva jāyate
|
nānapatyo bhavetprājño daridro vā kadācana ||15||

16yajiṣṇuḥ pañcamīṃ ṣaṣṭhīṃ kule bhojayate dvijān
|
aṣṭamīmatha kauravya kṛṣṇapakṣe caturdaśīm ||16||

17upoṣya vyādhirahito vīryavānabhijāyate
|
mārgaśīrṣaṃ tu yo māsamekabhaktena saṃkṣipet ||17||

18bhojayecca dvijān śaktyā sa mucyedvyādhikilviṣaiḥ
|
sarvakalyāṇasaṃpūrṇaḥ sarvauṣadhisamanvitaḥ ||18||

19upoṣya vyādhirahito vīryavānabhijāyate
|
kṛṣibhāgī bahudhano bahudhānyaśca jāyate ||19||

20pauṣamāsaṃ tu kaunteya bhaktenaikena yaḥ kṣipet
|
subhago darśanīyaśca yaśobhāgī ca jāyate ||20||

21māghaṃ tu niyato māsamekabhaktena yaḥ kṣipet
|
śrīmatkule jñātimadhye sa mahattvaṃ prapadyate ||21||

22bhagadevatamāsaṃ tu ekabhaktena yaḥ kṣipet
|
strīṣu vallabhatāṃ yāti vaśyāścāsya bhavanti tāḥ ||22||

23caitraṃ tu niyato māsamekabhaktena yaḥ kṣipet
|
suvarṇamaṇimuktāḍhye kule mahati jāyate ||23||

24nistaredekabhaktena vaiśākhaṃ yo jitendriyaḥ
|
naro vā yadi vā nārī jñātīnāṃ śreṣṭhatāṃ vrajet ||24||

25jyeṣṭhāmūlaṃ tu yo māsamekabhaktena saṃkṣipet
|
aiśvaryamatulaṃ śreṣṭhaṃ pumānstrī vā prapadyate ||25||

26āṣāḍhamekabhaktena sthitvā māsamatandritaḥ
|
bahudhānyo bahudhano bahuputraśca jāyate ||26||

27śrāvaṇaṃ niyato māsamekabhaktena yaḥ kṣipet
|
yatra tatrābhiṣekeṇa yujyate jñātivardhanaḥ ||27||

28prauṣṭhapadaṃ tu yo māsamekāhāro bhavennaraḥ
|
gavāḍhyaṃ sphītamacalamaiśvaryaṃ pratipadyate ||28||

29tathaivāśvayujaṃ māsamekabhaktena yaḥ kṣipet
|
mṛjāvān vāhanāḍhyaśca bahuputraśca jāyate ||29||

30kārtikaṃ tu naro māsaṃ yaḥ kuryādekabhojanam
|
śūraśca bahubhāryaśca kīrtimāṃścaiva jāyate ||30||

31iti māsā nakhyāghra kṣipatāṃ parikīrtitāḥ
|
tithīnāṃ niyamā ye tu śṛṇu tānapi pārthiva ||31||

32pakṣe pakṣe gate yastu bhaktamaśnāti bhārata
|
gavāḍhyo bahuputraśca bahubhāryaḥ sa jāyate ||32||

33māsi māsi trirātrāṇi kṛtvā varṣāṇi dvādaśa
|
gaṇādhipatyaṃ prāpnoti niḥsapatnamanāvilam ||33||

34ete tu niyamāḥ sarve kartavyāḥ śarado daśa
|
dve cānye bharataśreṣṭha pravṛttimanuvartatā ||34||

35yastu prātastathā sāyaṃ bhuñjāno nāntarā pibet
|
ahiṃsānirato nityaṃ juhvāno jātavedasam ||35||

36ṣaḍbhiḥ sa varṣairnṛpate sidhyate nātra saṃśayaḥ
|
agniṣṭomasya yajñasya phalaṃ prāpnoti mānavaḥ ||36||

37adhivāse so'psarasāṃ nṛtyagītavinādite
|
ramate strīsahasrāḍhye sukṛtī virajo naraḥ ||37||

38taptakāñcanavarṇābhaṃ vimānamadhirohati
|
pūrṇaṃ varṣasahasraṃ ca brahmaloke mahīyate ||38||

39tatkṣayādiha cāgamya māhātmyaṃ pratipadyate
|
yastu saṃvatsaraṃ pūrṇamekāhāro bhavennaraḥ ||39||

40atirātrasya yajñasya sa phalaṃ samupāśnute
|
daśavarṣasahasrāṇi svarge ca sa mahīyate ||40||

41tatkṣayādiha cāgamya māhātmyaṃ pratipadyate
|
yastu saṃvatsaraṃ pūrṇaṃ caturthaṃ bhaktamaśnute ||41||

42ahiṃsānirato nityaṃ satyavāgvijitendriyaḥ
|
vājapeyasya yajñasya sa phalaṃ samupāśnute ||42||

43daśavarṣasahasrāṇi svargaloke mahīyate
|
ṣaṣṭhe kāle tu kaunteya naraḥ saṃvatsaraṃ kṣipan ||43||

44aśvamedhasya yajñasya phalaṃ prāpnoti mānavaḥ
|
cakravākaprayuktena vimānena sa gacchati ||44||

45catvāriṃśatsahastrāṇi varṣāṇāṃ divi modate
|
aṣṭamena tu bhaktena jovantaṃvatsaraṃ nṛpa ||45||

46gavāmayasya yajñasya phalaṃ prāpnoti mānavaḥ
|
haṃsasārasayuktena vimānena sa gacchati ||46||

47pañcāśataṃ sahasrāṇi varṣāṇāṃ divi modate
|
pakṣe pakṣe gate rājanyo'śnīyādvarṣameva tu ||47||

48ṣaṇmāsānaśanaṃ tasya bhagavānaṅgirā'bravīt
|
ṣaṣṭirvarṣasahasrāṇi divamāvasate ca saḥ ||48||

49vīṇānāṃ vallakīnāṃ ca veṇūnāṃ ca viśāṃpate
|
sughoṣairmadhuraiḥ śabdaiḥ suptaḥ sa pratibodhyate ||49||

50saṃvatsaramihaikaṃ tu māsi māsi pibedapaḥ
|
phalaṃ viśvajitastāta prāpnoti sa naro nṛpa ||50||

51siṃhavyāghraprayuktena vimānena sa gacchati
|
saptatiṃ ca sahasrāṇi varṣāṇāṃ divi modate ||51||

52māsādūrdhvaṃ naravyāghra nopavāso vidhīyate
|
vidhiṃ tvanaśanasyāhuḥ pārtha dharmavido janāḥ ||52||

53anārto vyādhirahito gacchedanaśanaṃ tu yaḥ
|
pade pade yajñaphalaṃ sa prāpnoti na saṃśayaḥ ||53||

54divaṃ haṃsaprayuktena vimānena sa gacchati
|
śataṃ varṣasahasrāṇāṃ modate sa divi prabho ||54||

55śataṃ cāpsarasaḥ kanyā ramayantyapi taṃ naram
|
ārto vā vyādhito vāpi gacchedanaśanaṃ tu yaḥ ||55||

56śataṃ varṣasahasrāṇāṃ modate sa divi prabho
|
kāñcīnūpuraśabdena suptaścaiva prabodhyate ||56||

57sahastrahaṃsayuktena vimānena tu gacchati
|
sa gatvā strīśatākīrṇe ramate bharatarṣabha ||57||

58kṣīṇasyāpyāyanaṃ dṛṣṭaṃ kṣatasya kṣatarohaṇam
|
vyādhitasyauṣadhagrāmaḥ kruddhasya ca prasādanam ||58||

59duḥkhitasyārthamānābhyāṃ duḥkhānāṃ pratiṣedhanam
|
na caite svargakāmasya rocante sukhamedhasaḥ ||59||

60ataḥ sa kāmasaṃyukte vimāne hemasannibhe
|
ramate strīśatākīrṇe puruṣo'laṅkṛtaḥ śuciḥ ||60||

61svasthaḥ saphalasaṅkalpaḥ sukhī vigatakalmaṣaḥ
|
anaśnandehamutsṛjya phalaṃ prāpnoti mānavaḥ ||61||

62bālasūryapratīkāśe vimāne hemavarcasi
|
vaidūryamuktākhacite vīṇāsurajanādite ||62||

63patākādīpikākīrṇe divyaghaṇṭāninādite
|
strīsahasrānucarite sa naraḥ sukhamedhate ||63||

64yāvanti romakūpāṇi tasya gātreṣu pāṇḍava
|
tāvantyeva sahasrāṇi varṣāṇāṃ divi modate ||64||

65nāsti vedātparaṃ śāstraṃ nāsti mātṛsamo guruḥ
|
na dharmātparamo lābhastapo nānaśanātparam ||65||

66brāhmaṇebhyaḥ paraṃ nāsti pāvanaṃ divi ceha ca
|
upavāsaistathā tulyaṃ tapaḥ karma na vidyate ||66||

67upoṣya vidhivaddevāstridivaṃ pratipedire
|
ṛṣayaśca parāṃ siddhimupavāsairavāpnuvan ||67||

68divyavarṣasahasrāṇi viśvāmitreṇa dhīmatā
|
kṣāntamekena bhaktena tena vipratvamāgataḥ ||68||

69cyavano jamadagniśca vasiṣṭho gautamo bhṛguḥ
|
sarva eva divaṃ prāptāḥ kṣamāvanto maharṣayaḥ ||69||

70idamaṅgirasā pūrvaṃ maharṣibhyaḥ pradarśitam
|
yaḥ pradarśayate nityaṃ na sa duḥkhamavāpnute ||70||

71imaṃ tu kaunteya yathākramaṃ vidhiṃ pravartitaṃ hyaṅgirasā maharṣiṇā
|
paṭhecca yo vai śṛṇuyācca nityadā na vidyate tasya narasya kilbiṣam ||71||

72vimucyate cāpi sa sarvasaṅkarairna cāsya doṣairabhibhūyate manaḥ
|
viyonijānāṃ ca vijānate rutaṃ dhruvāṃ ca kīrtiṃ labhate narottamaḥ ||72||
