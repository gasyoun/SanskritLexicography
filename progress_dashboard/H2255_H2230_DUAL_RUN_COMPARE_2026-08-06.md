## Goal restated (both lanes)

1. New audits always write `wall_clock_minutes` (or explicit null + `wall_clock_source`) and
   `max_total_tokens` when available.
2. Kitchen coverage card is **honest** — do not blend pre-instrumentation historical nulls
   with post-cut rows that should have data.
3. Optional offline backfill for pre-cut ledger rows.

**Fail =:** coverage still conflates historical nulls with new audits; or silent invent of
wall-clock without a source tag; or Grok rubber-stamps the Sonnet diff unread.
