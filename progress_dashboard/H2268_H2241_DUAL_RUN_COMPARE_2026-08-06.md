## Independent Grok diagnosis (before treating #1112 as authority)

| Acceptance item | Independent finding | Evidence |
|---|---|---|
| Yield clean% on timeseries | **Read `kitchen_data.yield_quality.clean_window_pct`** — the kitchen already owns this (K2); progress builder should project, not recompute from ledger | Committed `kitchen_data.json` `yield_quality.clean_window_pct = 0.63` |
| Health last | **Read `kitchen_data.health.last_verdict`** (+ binary for charting) | `health.last_verdict = NO-GO`; numeric 0/1 needed for line chart |
| Review approved | **Already on progress timeseries as `approved`** from store `review_breakdown`; kitchen has no separate human-review section | `index.html` already charts `approved` as "Approved (human signed off)"; inventing `kitchen_review_approved` would duplicate store truth under a kitchen_ prefix |
| Idle hours | **`idle.total_idle_hours` is campaign stock** (monotonic grower); **`current_idle_seconds` is the live gap** operators care about day-to-day | stock 430.54 h vs current ~91 h on snapshot |
| Chart ≥1 kitchen metric | Yield + idle are the obvious continuous series; health binary is chartable once encoded 0/1 | Sonnet charted 2 of 3 continuous-ish series; left health_last_go uncharted |

Conclusion before comparison: the right H2241 design is **project kitchen_data into progress_timeseries**, not re-aggregate the ledger inside `build_progress_data`. Sonnet's field set for yield/health/total-idle is the correct source map. Residual gaps: pure-function pin, chart health GO, and surface **current** idle alongside the stock total.
