- Worklist: `src/pilot/output/verb_batch_worklist.json` → `done_promoted` (48), `dcs_attested` (749), remaining 701.

Algorithm reimplemented inline (same definition as merged helper, written without importing it as authority):

1. Scope rate to roots in `done_promoted` only.
2. For each such root, promotion day = max `generated_at[:10]` among its store cards.
3. Bucket roots by that day → mean count over active days → ETA = remaining / rate.

## Acceptance probes (a)(b)(c)

| Probe | Question | Independent ruling |
|---|---|---|
| (a) Scope | Restrict day-lookup to `done_promoted`, or also credit partial "touched" roots? | **Keep promoted-only.** H2233 acceptance allows either; remaining is `scope − promoted`, so the rate must be promotions/day or the ETA understates the work. Store rows with `provenance.root` outside `done_promoted` are mostly batch labels (`nominal_w1_100small`, `no_pwg_w0N`, …) — 19 "roots", not verb headwords. Including them would dilute/corrupt the rate. |
| (b) Day proxy | Last card day vs first card day vs worklist-increment log | **Keep last-card day.** First-card alternative: 4 active days, rate 12.0, ETA 58.4 — optimistically treats multi-day root completion as a single early burst. No separate worklist "promoted_at" log exists; last card is the best store-grounded proxy for "root finished". |
| (c) Window | 6 active days (Jun 29–Jul 6 cluster) vs `store_speed()`'s 14-day convention | **Keep all-active-days mean on completed promotions.** `store_speed` uses last-14 calendar days of *card* activity for a different telemetry block (untouched by H2233). All six promotion days already sit inside a 14-day span of the max promotion day, so a synthetic 14d filter yields the same 8.0/6/87.6. Caveat (not a code defect): the burst is front-loaded (19+15+11 then 1+1+1) and the lane has been idle since 2026-07-06 — the estimate is historical pace, correctly labelled `estimate`. Rolling 14d *calendar* rate would be ~0 today and useless as a burn-down until the lane resumes. |
