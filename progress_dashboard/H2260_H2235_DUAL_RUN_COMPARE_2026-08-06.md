### Probe A — does a per-row review timestamp exist?

Sonnet #1092 wrote that no timestamp field exists on approval records, so only the
append-only rebuild fallback applies. Independent field census of the store:

| Field | Presence |
|---|---|
| top-level `approved_at` / `reviewed_at` | **absent** |
| `provenance.generated_at` | present on all 11 603 rows — **generation** day, not human sign-off |
| `human_review.reviewed_at` | present on **all 5** human-touched rows (3 `approved` + 2 `needs_review`) |

All five `reviewed_at` values share one second (`2026-07-26T07:08:03`) — consistent with a
single batch sign-off of the G5 batch-1 apply. Transition histogram:

| date | approved | needs_review |
|---|---:|---:|
| 2026-07-26 | 3 | 2 |

**Ruling:** H2235's primary path ("derive daily approved transitions from review timestamps
if present") **is** available for the human-touched subset. The append-only stock series is
still the correct signal for **store depth over rebuild days** (covers the whole 11.6k stock
including `ai_translated` that will never have `reviewed_at` until reviewed). Using
`provenance.generated_at` as a fake review day would mislabel AI generation as human
throughput — rejected.
