## PD derivation and evidence

`PD_EDITIONS.md` identifies PD as Deccan College's *Encyclopaedic Dictionary of
Sanskrit on Historical Principles* and explicitly rules that it is not part of
the Petersburg supplement chain. The extension therefore adds a high-confidence,
source-linked `independent-historical-dictionary` relation with
`collapse_at: null`; it does not invent a Petersburg edge. The machine form is
in `witness_tiers.json` under `extensions.H7299` and is generated by
`witness_independence_reaudit.py`.

PD's **93,462 of 104,962 headwords (89.0%)** are single-dictionary unique in the
16-dictionary inventory. This is an **inventory-only** fact: a unique listing
does not demonstrate independent textual attestation. The consumer contract
rejects an inventory claim that carries an `independent_witnesses` count.

## Consumer contract

`witness_corroboration_contract.json` requires every new cross-dictionary claim
to use one of three evidence classes:

- `inventory` — membership/uniqueness only; no independent-witness count;
- `within-family-replication` — ≥2 dictionaries in exactly one family;
- `cross-family-confirmation` — ≥2 families, with the declared witness count
  equal to the derived family count.

The canaries reject `PWG + PWK + MW = 3 independent witnesses`, accept the same
set as one-family replication, preserve AP as a separate control, accept PD as a
separate family, reject PD uniqueness as attestation, and reject unlabelled
claims. Validation failures use `STOP_CANARY_MISMATCH`.
