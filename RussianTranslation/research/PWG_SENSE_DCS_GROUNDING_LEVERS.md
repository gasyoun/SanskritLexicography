## The headline, and the correction it forces

H1632 measured **52 of 7,746 PWG leaf senses (0.67%)** grounded to a DCS attestation by a shared locus, and concluded that the constraint was data availability — that scaling "did not and could not raise the grounding rate", and that growing it "means adding *texts and locus crosswalks*, not tuning a matcher".

**That conclusion was wrong about its own cause.** The aligner was never data-starved in the way the report supposed. It was *blindfolded*: for each DCS lemma it compared the sense's `<ls>` against only the 3 passages that `dcs_kwic()` had sampled **for the viewer** — 3,435 of the 1,148,630 passages available under the wave-1 frame, or **0.299%**. The exact-verse test was measuring the sample, not the corpus.

Removing the blindfold — the SAME `verse_equal()` predicate, the same tiers, no heuristic added — and widening the frame with the SAME selection query gives:

| | grounded leaf senses | rate (aligner-covered subset) | of which exact-verse |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| H1632 baseline — frozen 500, `--locus-scan kwic` | 52 | 0.67% | 5 (0.06%) |
| A2 only — frozen 500, `--locus-scan full` | 543 | 7.01% | 284 (3.67%) |
| A1 only — wide frame, `--locus-scan kwic` | 1,433 | 2.38% | 357 (0.59%) |
| A1+A2 — wide frame, `--locus-scan full` | 5,456 | 9.06% | 2,479 (4.12%) |
| A1+A2+B — wide frame, full scan, + text map | 7,372 | 12.25% | 5,647 (9.38%) |

**141.8× the grounded senses (52 → 7,372) and 18.2× the rate (0.67% → 12.25%), with no criterion relaxed.**

| lever | what changed | grounded senses | Δ vs baseline |
|---|---|---:|---:|

**The levers are strongly super-additive.** A1 alone adds 1,381 grounded senses and A2 alone adds 491, but together they add 5,404 — far more than the 1,872 a purely additive model predicts. A wider frame cites more addresses, and only the full scan can find them; neither lever pays off without the other.

⚠️ **A1's effect on the *rate* is partly compositional, and must not be read as "grounding is easier further down the dictionary".** The frozen 500 were ranked most-polysemous-first, so they carry 15.5 leaf senses per group against the wide frame's 3.7. A single grounded locus therefore lifts the rate more on a shallow entry than on a deep one. The absolute counts (the column left of it) are the honest comparison.

## Precision — three defects the keyhole was hiding

A 60× jump in exact-verse matches is exactly the kind of number that should be disbelieved until it survives an attack. Removing the sampling limit exposed three real precision defects in the matcher, all of which are FIXED here — and the corrected numbers above are the post-fix ones:

1. **Named books collapsed into one numbering space.** `parse_ref_nums()` keeps only digits, so DCS's `Rām, Bā, 6` and `Rām, Utt, 6` both reduced to `(6, …)`. One PWG tuple "matched" the same sarga/verse in up to seven different Rāmāyaṇa books at once — `vara` sense 1a matched Bālakāṇḍa AND Uttarakāṇḍa simultaneously. Same defect for Suśruta (`Su, Cik., 29`) and for the 18 Bhagavadgītā chapters (`MBh, 6, BhaGī 1`, read as parvan 6 adhyāya 1 — not where the Gītā sits). Now `numeric_address()` **abstains** whenever a post-siglum component is non-numeric.
2. **Chapter-level matches were being reported as exact-verse.** 20.9% of DCS's Ṛgveda and 24.1% of its Atharvaveda sentences carry no `sent_counter`, so their address stops at the hymn. Those matches are real but weaker, and now land in their own `locus-chapter` tier (conf 0.70) — never folded into the exact-verse headline. Both rates are reported separately throughout.
3. **The Ṛgveda was unreachable through a one-character bug.** `PWG_TO_DCS_TEXT` keyed the Ṛgveda as ASCII `"RV"`, but PWG's abbrev is `ṚV`. The most canonically-numbered text in the corpus — **50,972 `<ls>` citations, 6.89% of the dictionary's total, second only to the Mahābhārata** — never matched a single key. It is now the largest single contributor of exact-verse rows.

**Regression evidence, stated precisely.** With defects 1 and 2 fixed, re-running the wave-1 configuration (frozen 500, `--locus-scan kwic`) reproduces all three committed H1455 artifacts — `sense_corpus_concordance.tsv`, `sense_corpus_coverage.tsv`, `sense_review_queue.tsv` — with **zero differing lines** (CRLF normalised; git checks the committed copies out with CRLF). So the matcher is provably unchanged and wave-1's published numbers contained no false positives: its 3-passage sample never happened to land on an unsafe address, and the two guards only bind once the scan is widened.

Defect 3 is different and **does** change wave-1's own output by design — reinstating the Ṛgveda takes the frozen frame's exact-verse links from 5 to 12 (17 differing concordance lines). That is a correction, not a regression: those seven Ṛgveda matches were always there to be found.

One number in H1632 **is** sharpened rather than reproduced: of its 52 grounded senses, only **5 were exact-verse** — the other 47 were adhyāya-level Mahābhārata corroboration via the csl-atlas f8 vulgate crosswalk. The report now carries that split as its own row.

So the residue splits into a **tractable 13.9%** (357 of the 443 are tuple-comparable — they need a verified map entry, not new data) and an **untouchable 49.7%** (Pañcatantra, the kośa literature, Manu in Loiseleur's edition, the Oxford catalogue — texts DCS does not carry).

**A name match is not a crosswalk.** Of nine top candidates inspected, four were rejected on their `pwgbib` entry: `VP` cites Wilson's *translation* by page; `KĀTY. Only the five whose scheme provably corresponds were mapped. Correcting H1632: it named **Kathāsaritsāgara** as a text "DCS does not contain" — DCS carries it (111,298 tokens); the obstacle is the numbering, not the text.

## Lever C — the untagged corpus, kept strictly separate

An untagged corpus (wisdomlib) cannot raise anything on this page. Every number above is a **sense-level** grounding, which requires a sense tag to bind to; an untagged corpus has none, and none can be had without lemmatising and tagging it ourselves. What it can move is the **lemma-level** attestation rate — 43,352 of 109,050 PWG headword groups (39.8%) have a DCS lemma, leaving 65,698 (60.2%) attested nowhere — and that is a different quantity with a different denominator.

**No wisdomlib-derived number is reported here, and none was measured into a sense-level rate.** H1670 deliberately did not consume the scrape: the lemma-level lane belongs to the wisdomlib-scrape handoffs, and folding it in here would invite exactly the category error this programme keeps catching.

## What this does NOT claim

- **Grounding outside the aligner's reach is still UNKNOWN, never 0.** 10,515 of the 109,050 PWG groups remain `R0_grounding_not_computed`.
- No PWG sense is mapped to a WordNet synset or an MW numbered sense.
- The `overlap` gloss-token tier is reported and **excluded** from every headline; it is not a sense identification.
- `locus-mbh` and `locus-chapter` are adhyāya/hymn-level **corroboration**, not exact-verse identity. The exact-verse column is given alongside every combined figure so neither can be quoted without the other.
- Nothing here says the *remaining* residue is tractable: 49.7% of PWG's citation mass points at texts DCS does not carry.

## Reproduce

```sh
# 1 — the PWG side (needs csl-orig); bulk output is gitignored, the generator is the artifact
cd RussianTranslation/research && python export_frame_sense_loci.py --all

# 2 — the wide frame: the SAME selection query, run to exhaustion
python ../../../kosha/scripts/select_sense_pilot.py \
    --input pwg_sense_loci.all.tsv --size 200000 --out frame_wide.tsv

# 3 — the aligner, per cell (kosha)
python scripts/build_sense_corpus_concordance.py --input <loci> --out-dir run1 --locus-scan kwic
python scripts/build_sense_corpus_concordance.py --input <loci> --out-dir run2 --locus-scan full
python scripts/build_sense_corpus_concordance.py --input <loci> --pilot frame_wide.tsv \
    --out-dir run5 --locus-scan full

# 4 — measure each cell, then this synthesis
python pwg_sense_dcs_attestation_pilot.py --frame-mode file --frame frame_wide.tsv \
    --concordance run5/sense_corpus_concordance.tsv --tag widefulltextmap
python build_grounding_levers_report.py
python pwg_sense_dcs_attestation_pilot.py --selftest
```

Deterministic, no LLM in the measurement path, no sampling, no RNG. The aligner lives in [`build_sense_corpus_concordance.py`](https://github.com/gasyoun/kosha/blob/main/scripts/build_sense_corpus_concordance.py); the DCS master is `VisualDCS/src/DCS-data-2026/dcs_full.sqlite` (920,883,200 bytes — the sibling `src/` and repo-root copies are 0-byte decoys and were not read).
