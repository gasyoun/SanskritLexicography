## 4. Precision spot-check (30 flags, by hand)

30 flags drawn with `random.Random(5262).sample` from the 54 flags of the tier-1 snapshot (the 60 lemmas pymorphy3 does not know plus 3 pilot lemmas), judged by hand by Opus 5.5 (claude-opus-5-5) against each card's Russian gloss span and PWG's German meaning (reports/H5262_flag_context.json). Two questions per flag: does the NKRYa measurement describe the word actually written (vs a tokenizer or lemmatizer artifact), and is the word a gloss defect a reviewer should replace. The sample is drawn from the riskiest tier, so its defect rate is an upper bound for the whole store, not an estimate of it.

## 6. Coverage and what remains

345 of the 4901 distinct lemmas have a verdict; 4556 are still unqueried. The NKRYa key is rate-limited per ACCOUNT (about 60 calls/hour sustained, shared by every session using it), and a lemma costs 1–4 calls, so the remainder is a long unattended drain, not a sitting. `query` resumes exactly where it stopped: the ledger records answers only, so an interrupted or rate-limited lemma is retried, never retired (FINDINGS §646).
