# H2844 — 200-citation line-collapse audit

_Created: 17-08-2026 · Last updated: 17-08-2026_

Acceptance evidence for [H2844](https://github.com/gasyoun/Uprava/blob/main/handoffs/H2844-Opus_SanskritLexicography_reglue-citation-linebreak-collapse-compact-card-view_15.08.26.md) — *«a 200-citation random sample drawn from the affected sites — count how many collapses land inside vs outside a citation clause»*, with the fail condition *«any sampled citation separated from its source clause»*.

**Verdict: PASS** — 200 of 200 sampled citations stay with their source clause in both renderings; 0 torn.

## Method

- Population: every inherited wrap in the store's `ru` field whose next line opens a `<ls>` citation — **24,103 sites**, spread over the 4,434 store rows that carry any newline at all.
- Sample: `random.Random(20260817).sample`, n=200. Re-runnable verbatim: `python src/collapse_audit.py --n 200 --seed 20260817`.
- Verdict per site is read off the **rendered** string by [`collapse_audit.verdict`](https://github.com/gasyoun/SanskritLexicography/blob/master/RussianTranslation/src/collapse_audit.py), which never sees `collapse` — its selftest feeds it the pre-H2844 line-break-preserving render and requires `torn`, so a PASS is not vacuous.

## Where the collapse lands (n=200)

| position of the wrap | n | reading |
|---|---|---|
| `after_gloss` | 173 | **inside a citation clause** — joins the citation to the Russian/Sanskrit clause that sources it (the P1 case) |
| `after_cite` | 27 | **inside a citation run** — joins one citation to the previous citation of the same list |

Inside a citation clause or run: **200**. Outside: **0**.

## Negative control

The same 200 sites judged against the **pre-H2844 render** (every store newline kept as a line break): 200 torn. That is the defect P1 removed, and it is what the audit reports when tearing is real.
