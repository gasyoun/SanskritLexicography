# Per-volume publication years for multi-volume dictionaries — MG ruling 15-09-2026

A span like "1855–1875" is a title-page span, NOT per-volume precision. **Each volume of a
multi-volume work has its own single publication year**, and it must always be kept in mind
for **PW, PWG, EWA, KEWA** (and any other multi-volume dictionary).

Working tables (to be pinned per part from the title pages where marked):

- **Great Petersburg dictionary (Böhtlingk–Roth, Cologne `pwg`)**: Bd 1 1855 · Bd 2 1856 ·
  Bd 3 1857 · Bd 4 1858 · Bd 5 1861 · Bd 6 1863 · Bd 7 1872–75 (issued in parts).
- **Kürzere Fassung (Cologne `pw`, MG's «PWK»)**: Pt 1 1879 · Pt 2 1880 · … · Pt 7 **1889**
  (final part carries the closing supplement; parts 2–6 years to be pinned from title pages).
  The `sup_1`…`sup_7` tags in `pw.txt` are THIS work's per-part Nachträge — not the great
  PW's vol 7 (the census/§18 naming trap applies).
- **KEWA (Mayrhofer)**: Bd I 1956 · II 1963 · III 1976 · IV 1980.
- **EWA (Mayrhofer)**: Bd I 1992 · II 1996 · III 2001.

Why it matters: the annexure-draws-on-Nachträge argument turns on which parts were printed
when relative to MW72 (1872) and MW99 (1899). The kāritra locus (kürzere Fassung pt 7,
p. 331 col d) is **1889** — printed 10 years before MW99 and 17 years after MW72: MW99
could have taken it; it did not.
